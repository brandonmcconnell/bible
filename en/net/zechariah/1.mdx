<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:4; Zechariah 7:1; Zechariah 1:7"> In the eighth month of Darius’ second year, the word of the Lord came to the prophet Zechariah, son of Berechiah son of Iddo, as follows:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:6; Lamentations 5:7; Ezekiel 22:31"> The Lord was very angry with your ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Malachi 3:7; Jeremiah 3:22; Jeremiah 4:1"> Therefore say to the people: The Lord who rules over all says, “Turn to me,” says the Lord who rules over all, “and I will turn to you,” says the Lord who rules over all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 78:8; Psalms 106:6–7; Jeremiah 35:15"> “Do not be like your ancestors, to whom the former prophets called out, saying, ‘The Lord who rules over all says, “Turn now from your evil wickedness,”’ but they would by no means obey me,” says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5; Job 14:10–12; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> “As for your ancestors, where are they? And did the prophets live forever?</span>

</div>

## The Interpretation of the First Vision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 4:4–5; Zechariah 5:5; Zechariah 2:3"> Then I asked one nearby, “What are these, sir?” The angelic messenger who replied to me said, “I will show you what these are.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 1:11; Zechariah 6:5–8; Hebrews 1:14"> Then the man standing among the myrtle trees spoke up and said, “These are the ones whom the Lord has sent to walk about on the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 1:10; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 103:20–21"> The riders then agreed with the angel of the Lord, who was standing among the myrtle trees, “We have been walking about on the earth, and now everything is at rest and quiet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 7:5; Jeremiah 29:10; Psalms 102:13"> The angel of the Lord then asked, “Lord who rules over all, how long before you have compassion on Jerusalem and the other cities of Judah which you have been so angry with for these seventy years?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Isaiah 40:1–2; Jeremiah 30:10–22"> The Lord then addressed good, comforting words to the angelic messenger who was speaking to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 2:18; Isaiah 40:1; Zechariah 1:17"> Turning to me, the messenger then said, “Cry out that the Lord who rules over all says, ‘I am very much moved for Jerusalem and for Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:12–17; Jeremiah 51:24; Isaiah 54:8"> But I am greatly displeased with the nations that take my grace for granted. I was a little displeased with them, but they have only made things worse for themselves.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Oracle of Response

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3; Ezra 6:14–15; Zechariah 2:10–11"> “‘Therefore,’ says the Lord, ‘I have become compassionate toward Jerusalem and will rebuild my temple in it,’ says the Lord who rules over all. ‘Once more a surveyor’s measuring line will be stretched out over Jerusalem.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Zechariah 2:12; Zephaniah 3:15–17"> Speak up again with the message of the Lord who rules over all: ‘My cities will once more overflow with prosperity, and once more the Lord will comfort Zion and validate his choice of Jerusalem.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Vision Two: The Four Horns

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 5:13; 2 Kings 24:1–20; Zechariah 5:9"> Once again I looked and this time I saw four horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 1:21; Zechariah 8:14; Zechariah 2:2"> So I asked the angelic messenger who spoke with me, “What are these?” He replied, “These are the horns that have scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 10:3–5; Zechariah 12:2–6; Zechariah 9:12–16"> Next the Lord showed me four blacksmiths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 75:10; Zechariah 1:19; Psalms 75:4–5"> I asked, “What are these going to do?” He answered, “These horns are the ones that have scattered Judah so that there is no one to be seen. But the blacksmiths have come to terrify Judah’s enemies and cut off the horns of the nations that have thrust themselves against the land of Judah in order to scatter its people.”</span>
</div>
</div>
