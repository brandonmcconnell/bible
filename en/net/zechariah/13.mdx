<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:12; Revelation 16:19; Ezekiel 5:2–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will happen in all the land, says the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that two-thirds of the people in it will be cut off and die,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but one-third will be left in it.</span><br /></span>
