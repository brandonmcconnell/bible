<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Zechariah 1:16; Ezekiel 47:4"> I looked again, and there was a man with a measuring line in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:39; Ezekiel 45:6; Revelation 21:15–17"> I asked, “Where are you going?” He replied, “To measure Jerusalem in order to determine its width and its length.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 1:19; Zechariah 1:8–11; Zechariah 4:5"> At this point the angelic messenger who spoke to me went out, and another messenger came to meet him</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 1:17; Jeremiah 33:22; Ezekiel 38:11"> and said to him, “Hurry, speak to this young man as follows: ‘Jerusalem will no longer be enclosed by walls because of the multitude of people and animals there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 4:5; Zechariah 9:8; Psalms 3:3"> But I (the Lord says) will be a wall of fire surrounding Jerusalem and the source of glory in her midst.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Ezekiel 17:21; Isaiah 52:11–12"> “You there! Flee from the northland!” says the Lord, “for like the four winds of heaven I have scattered you,” says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Genesis 19:17; Isaiah 52:11"> “Escape, Zion, you who live among the Babylonians!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 17:8; Deuteronomy 32:10; John 15:21–23"> For the Lord who rules over all says to me that for his own glory he has sent me to the nations that plundered you – for anyone who touches you touches the pupil of his eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 19:16; Isaiah 14:2; Zechariah 4:9"> “I am about to punish them in such a way,” he says, “that they will be looted by their own slaves.” Then you will know that the Lord who rules over all has sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; John 1:14; Zephaniah 3:14–15"> “Sing out and be happy, Zion my daughter! For look, I have come; I will settle in your midst,” says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; 1 Peter 2:9–10; Acts 28:28"> “Many nations will join themselves to the Lord on the day of salvation, and they will also be my people. Indeed, I will settle in the midst of you all.” Then you will know that the Lord who rules over all has sent me to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 1:17; Deuteronomy 32:9; Jeremiah 10:16"> The Lord will take possession of Judah as his portion in the holy land and he will choose Jerusalem once again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:20; Psalms 68:5; Psalms 78:65"> Be silent in the Lord’s presence, all people everywhere, for he is being moved to action in his holy dwelling place.</span>
