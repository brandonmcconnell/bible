<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 2:12–17; Ezekiel 31:17; Isaiah 10:33–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Howl, fir tree,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because the cedar has fallen;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the majestic trees have been destroyed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Howl, oaks of Bashan,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because the impenetrable forest has fallen.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:44; Jeremiah 25:34–36; Zechariah 11:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen to the howling of shepherds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because their magnificence has been destroyed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Listen to the roaring of young lions,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because the thickets of the Jordan have been devastated.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 11:7; Matthew 23:37; Romans 15:8"> The Lord my God says this: “Shepherd the flock set aside for slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:7; Hosea 12:8; 1 Timothy 6:5–10"> Those who buy them slaughter them and are not held guilty; those who sell them say, ‘Blessed be the Lord, for I am rich.’ Their own shepherds have no compassion for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:14; Matthew 22:7; Isaiah 9:19–21"> Indeed, I will no longer have compassion on the people of the land,” says the Lord, “but instead I will turn every last person over to his neighbor and his king. They will devastate the land, and I will not deliver it from them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Zechariah 11:4; Isaiah 11:4"> So I began to shepherd the flock destined for slaughter, the most afflicted of all the flock. Then I took two staffs, calling one “Pleasantness” and the other “Binders,” and I tended the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 49:7; Matthew 23:34–36; John 15:18"> Next I eradicated the three shepherds in one month, for I ran out of patience with them and, indeed, they detested me as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:11; Matthew 13:10–11; Matthew 21:19"> I then said, “I will not shepherd you. What is to die, let it die, and what is to be eradicated, let it be eradicated. As for those who survive, let them eat each other’s flesh!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 11:7; Psalms 89:39; Hebrews 8:8–13"> Then I took my staff “Pleasantness” and cut it in two to annul my covenant that I had made with all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:12; Luke 2:38; Deuteronomy 31:21"> So it was annulled that very day, and then the most afflicted of the flock who kept faith with me knew that that was the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Exodus 21:32; Luke 22:3–6"> Then I said to them, “If it seems good to you, pay me my wages, but if not, forget it.” So they weighed out my payment – thirty pieces of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 27:3–10; Acts 1:18–19; Isaiah 53:2–3"> The Lord then said to me, “Throw to the potter that exorbitant sum at which they valued me!” So I took the thirty pieces of silver and threw them to the potter at the temple of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 9:21; Isaiah 11:13; Acts 23:7–10"> Then I cut the second staff “Binders” in two in order to annul the covenant of brotherhood between Judah and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10–12; Luke 11:40; Jeremiah 2:26–27"> Again the Lord said to me, “Take up once more the equipment of a foolish shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2–6; Jeremiah 23:2; Matthew 23:2–4"> Indeed, I am about to raise up a shepherd in the land who will not take heed to the sheep headed to slaughter, will not seek the scattered, and will not heal the injured. Moreover, he will not nourish the one that is healthy but instead will eat the meat of the fat sheep and tear off their hooves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:1; Ezekiel 13:3; 1 Corinthians 8:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to the worthless shepherd</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who abandons the flock!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May a sword fall on his arm and his right eye!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May his arm wither completely away,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and his right eye become completely blind!”</span></span>
