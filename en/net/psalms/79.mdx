<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 3:12; 2 Chronicles 36:19; Psalms 74:1–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">O God, foreigners have invaded your chosen land;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they have polluted your holy temple</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and turned Jerusalem into a heap of ruins.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Jeremiah 34:20; Jeremiah 16:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have given the corpses of your servants</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to the birds of the sky;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the flesh of your loyal followers</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to the beasts of the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:4; Jeremiah 14:16; Revelation 11:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have made their blood flow like water</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all around Jerusalem, and there is no one to bury them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 80:6; Lamentations 5:1; Psalms 89:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">We have become an object of disdain to our neighbors;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">those who live on our borders taunt and insult us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Psalms 74:1; Psalms 85:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">How long will this go on, O Lord?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Will you stay angry forever?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">How long will your rage burn like fire?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:25; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Psalms 14:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pour out your anger on the nations that do not acknowledge you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on the kingdoms that do not pray to you!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34–35; Zechariah 1:15; Isaiah 64:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they have devoured Jacob</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and destroyed his home.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 64:9; Psalms 142:6; Psalms 116:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not hold us accountable for the sins of earlier generations!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Quickly send your compassion our way,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for we are in serious trouble!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:7; Psalms 25:11; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Help us, O God, our deliverer!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the sake of your glorious reputation, rescue us!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Forgive our sins for the sake of your reputation!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:10; Exodus 7:5; Psalms 115:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why should the nations say, “Where is their God?”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Before our very eyes may the shed blood of your servants</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">be avenged among the nations!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 102:20; Psalms 146:6–7; Isaiah 33:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen to the painful cries of the prisoners!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Use your great strength to set free those condemned to die!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:18; Genesis 4:15; Leviticus 26:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pay back our neighbors in full!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May they be insulted the same way they insulted you, O Lord!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 95:7; Isaiah 43:21; Psalms 74:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then we, your people, the sheep of your pasture,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will continually thank you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">We will tell coming generations of your praiseworthy acts.</span></span>
