<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:2; Acts 24:16; 1 Peter 2:19"> Therefore it is necessary to be in subjection, not only because of the wrath of the authorities but also because of your conscience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:17–21; Mark 12:14–17; Matthew 17:24–27"> For this reason you also pay taxes, for the authorities are God’s servants devoted to governing.</span>

## Exhortation to Love Neighbors

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:10; Matthew 22:39–40; Galatians 5:14"> Owe no one anything, except to love one another, for the one who loves his neighbor has fulfilled the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Matthew 22:39; Luke 10:27"> For the commandments, “Do not commit adultery, do not murder, do not steal, do not covet,” (and if there is any other commandment) are summed up in this, “Love your neighbor as yourself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:39–40; Romans 13:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> Love does no wrong to a neighbor. Therefore love is the fulfillment of the law.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Motivation to Godly Conduct

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Peter 4:7; Ephesians 5:14"> And do this because we know the time, that it is already the hour for us to awake from sleep, for our salvation is now nearer than when we became believers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:31–32; Ephesians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 6:7"> The night has advanced toward dawn; the day is near. So then we must lay aside the works of darkness, and put on the weapons of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:21; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–5"> Let us live decently as in the daytime, not in carousing and drunkenness, not in sexual immorality and sensuality, not in discord and jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Galatians 5:16–17; Galatians 3:27"> Instead, put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the flesh to arouse its desires.</span>
</div>
