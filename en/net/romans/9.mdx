<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:7; Romans 1:9; Galatians 1:20"> I am telling the truth in Christ (I am not lying!), for my conscience assures me in the Holy Spirit –</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:18; Psalms 119:136; Jeremiah 13:17"> I have great sorrow and unceasing anguish in my heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 32:32; Esther 8:6; Romans 11:14"> For I could wish that I myself were accursed – cut off from Christ – for the sake of my people, my fellow countrymen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:12; Psalms 147:19; Romans 9:6"> who are Israelites. To them belong the adoption as sons, the glory, the covenants, the giving of the law, the temple worship, and the promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 10:30; John 1:1–3; Colossians 1:16–19"> To them belong the patriarchs, and from them, by human descent, came the Christ, who is God over all, blessed forever! Amen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 2:28–29; Galatians 6:16; Romans 11:1–2"> It is not as though the word of God had failed. For not all those who are descended from Israel are truly Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 21:12; Hebrews 11:18; John 8:37–39"> nor are all the children Abraham’s true descendants; rather “through Isaac will your descendants be counted.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:13; Romans 4:11–16; Galatians 3:26–29"> This means it is not the children of the flesh who are the children of God; rather, the children of promise are counted as descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 18:10; Genesis 18:14; Genesis 17:21"> For this is what the promise declared: “About a year from now I will return and Sarah will have a son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 25:21–23; Luke 16:26; Romans 5:11"> Not only that, but when Rebekah had conceived children by one man, our ancestor Isaac –</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:4–5; 2 Peter 1:10"> even before they were born or had done anything good or bad (so that God’s purpose in election would stand, not by works but by his calling) –</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 25:22–23; 2 Samuel 8:14; 1 Kings 22:47"> it was said to her, “The older will serve the younger,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 1:2–3; Deuteronomy 21:15; Genesis 29:33"> just as it is written: “Jacob I loved, but Esau I hated.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:7; Psalms 145:17; Deuteronomy 32:4"> What shall we say then? Is there injustice with God? Absolutely not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 33:19; Romans 9:16; Micah 7:18"> For he says to Moses: “I will have mercy on whom I have mercy, and I will have compassion on whom I have compassion.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8; Matthew 11:25–26; 1 Peter 2:9–10"> So then, it does not depend on human desire or exertion, but on God who shows mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Proverbs 16:4; Exodus 14:17–18"> For the scripture says to Pharaoh: “For this very purpose I have raised you up, that I may demonstrate my power in you, and that my name may be proclaimed in all the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 11:20; Exodus 4:21; Romans 9:15–16"> So then, God has mercy on whom he chooses to have mercy, and he hardens whom he chooses to harden.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:35; 2 Chronicles 20:6; 1 Corinthians 15:12"> You will say to me then, “Why does he still find fault? For who has ever resisted his will?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 29:16; Isaiah 45:9–11; Isaiah 64:8"> But who indeed are you – a mere human being – to talk back to God? Does what is molded say to the molder, “Why have you made me like this?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Proverbs 16:4; 2 Timothy 2:20–21"> Has the potter no right to make from the same lump of clay one vessel for special use and another for ordinary use?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; Jude 1:4; Romans 9:21"> But what if God, willing to demonstrate his wrath and to make known his power, has endured with much patience the objects of wrath prepared for destruction?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 2:4; 1 Peter 1:2–5; Colossians 1:27"> And what if he is willing to make known the wealth of his glory on the objects of mercy that he has prepared beforehand for glory –</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Acts 13:47–48; Ephesians 3:6–8"> even us, whom he has called, not only from the Jews but also from the Gentiles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; 1 Peter 2:10; John 16:27"> As he also says in Hosea:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will call those who were not my people, ‘My people,’ and I will call her who was unloved, ‘My beloved.&#42;’”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 1:9–10; 1 John 3:1–3; Galatians 3:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">“And in the very place where it was said to them, ‘You are not my people,&#42;’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there they will be called ‘sons of the living God.’”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 10:20–23; Romans 11:4–6; Isaiah 1:9"> And Isaiah cries out on behalf of Israel, “Though the number of the children of Israel are as the sand of the sea, only the remnant will be saved,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 10:23; Revelation 19:11; Acts 17:31"> for the Lord will execute his sentence on the earth completely and quickly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Isaiah 13:19; Amos 4:11"> Just as Isaiah predicted,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“If the Lord of armies had not left us descendants,&#42;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">we would have become like Sodom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and we would have resembled Gomorrah.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Israel’s Rejection Culpable

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Hebrews 11:7; Galatians 2:16"> What shall we say then? – that the Gentiles who did not pursue righteousness obtained it, that is, a righteousness that is by faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 11:7; Romans 3:20; James 2:10–11"> but Israel even though pursuing a law of righteousness did not attain it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Peter 2:8; Luke 7:23"> Why not? Because they pursued it not by faith but (as if it were possible) by works. They stumbled over the stumbling stone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Isaiah 8:14–15; Romans 10:11"> just as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Look, I am laying in Zion a stone that will cause people to stumble</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and a rock that will make them fall,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">yet the one who believes in him will not be put to shame.”</span></span>
</div>
