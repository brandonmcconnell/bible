<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:22; 1 Samuel 12:22; Philippians 3:5"> So I ask, God has not rejected his people, has he? Absolutely not! For I too am an Israelite, a descendant of Abraham, from the tribe of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:14; Romans 8:29–30; Exodus 32:1"> God has not rejected his people whom he foreknew! Do you not know what the scripture says about Elijah, how he pleads with God against Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10–18; 1 Kings 18:13; Nehemiah 9:26"> “Lord, they have killed your prophets, they have demolished your altars; I alone am left and they are seeking my life!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 19:18; Numbers 25:3; Zephaniah 1:4"> But what was the divine response to him? “I have kept for myself seven thousand people who have not bent the knee to Baal&#42;.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 9:27; Ephesians 1:5–6; Romans 9:11"> So in the same way at the present time there is a remnant chosen by grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Romans 4:4–5; 2 Timothy 1:9"> And if it is by grace, it is no longer by works, otherwise grace would no longer be grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Romans 11:5; 2 Corinthians 4:4"> What then? Israel failed to obtain what it was diligently seeking, but the elect obtained it. The rest were hardened,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:4; Isaiah 29:10; Ezekiel 12:2"> as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“God gave them a spirit of stupor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">eyes that would not see and ears that would not hear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to this very day.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 69:22–23; Luke 12:20; Psalms 28:4"> And David says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Let their table become a snare and trap,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a stumbling block and a retribution for them;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 69:23; Deuteronomy 28:64–68; 2 Peter 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">let their eyes be darkened so that they may not see,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and make their backs bend continually.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 13:46–48; Romans 11:31; Romans 10:19"> I ask then, they did not stumble into an irrevocable fall, did they? Absolutely not! But by their transgression salvation has come to the Gentiles, to make Israel jealous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:25; Colossians 1:27; Romans 11:15"> Now if their transgression means riches for the world and their defeat means riches for the Gentiles, how much more will their full restoration bring?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 2 Timothy 1:11–12; Ephesians 3:8"> Now I am speaking to you Gentiles. Seeing that I am an apostle to the Gentiles, I magnify my ministry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:16; 1 Timothy 4:16; Romans 9:3"> if somehow I could provoke my people to jealousy and save some of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 1:10; Romans 5:10–11; Colossians 1:20–21"> For if their rejection is the reconciliation of the world, what will their acceptance be but life from the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:30; James 1:18; Proverbs 3:9"> If the first portion of the dough offered is holy, then the whole batch is holy, and if the root is holy, so too are the branches.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 2:11–13; Ephesians 3:6; Matthew 21:43"> Now if some of the branches were broken off, and you, a wild olive shoot, were grafted in among them and participated in the richness of the olive root,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:12; Romans 11:20; Ephesians 2:19–20"> do not boast over the branches. But if you boast, remember that you do not support the root, but the root supports you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 11:17; Romans 11:11–12; Romans 11:23–24"> Then you will say, “The branches were broken off so that I could be grafted in.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:16; 2 Corinthians 1:24; 1 Corinthians 10:12"> Granted! They were broken off because of their unbelief, but you stand by faith. Do not be arrogant, but fear!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4–9; Jeremiah 25:29; Jeremiah 49:12"> For if God did not spare the natural branches, perhaps he will not spare you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 3:6; 1 Corinthians 15:2"> Notice therefore the kindness and harshness of God – harshness toward those who have fallen, but God’s kindness toward you, provided you continue in his kindness; otherwise you also will be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:16; Zechariah 12:10; Matthew 23:39"> And even they – if they do not continue in their unbelief – will be grafted in, for God is able to graft them in again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:17–18; Romans 11:30"> For if you were cut off from what is by nature a wild olive tree, and grafted, contrary to nature, into a cultivated olive tree, how much more will these natural branches be grafted back into their own olive tree?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 21:24; 2 Corinthians 3:14–16; Romans 16:25"> For I do not want you to be ignorant of this mystery, brothers and sisters, so that you may not be conceited: A partial hardening has happened to Israel until the full number of the Gentiles has come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 59:20; Jeremiah 31:31–37; Psalms 14:7"> And so all Israel will be saved, as it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The Deliverer will come out of Zion;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he will remove ungodliness from Jacob.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Isaiah 59:21; Ezekiel 36:25–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">And this is my covenant with them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I take away their sins.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:15; Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Matthew 21:43"> In regard to the gospel they are enemies for your sake, but in regard to election they are dearly loved for the sake of the fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 7:21; Numbers 23:19; Malachi 3:6"> For the gifts and the call of God are irrevocable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 3:7; Ephesians 2:1–2; 1 Corinthians 7:25"> Just as you were formerly disobedient to God, but have now received mercy due to their disobedience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 11:15; Romans 10:16; Romans 11:25"> so they too have now been disobedient in order that, by the mercy shown to you, they too may now receive mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 3:9; 1 Timothy 2:4–6"> For God has consigned all people to disobedience so that he may show mercy to them all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 5:9; Job 11:7–9; Psalms 92:5"> Oh, the depth of the riches and wisdom and knowledge of God! How unsearchable are his judgments and how fathomless his ways!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Job 15:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">For who has known the mind of the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or who has been his counselor?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 41:11; Job 35:7; 1 Corinthians 4:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Or who has first given to God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that God needs to repay him?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 2:10"> For from him and through him and to him are all things. To him be glory forever! Amen.</span>
</div>
