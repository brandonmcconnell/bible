<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:20–22; Romans 9:1–3; John 5:34"> Brothers and sisters, my heart’s desire and prayer to God on behalf of my fellow Israelites is for their salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 21:20; Romans 9:31–32; Proverbs 19:2"> For I can testify that they are zealous for God, but their zeal is not in line with the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Luke 16:15; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For ignoring the righteousness that comes from God, and seeking instead to establish their own righteousness, they did not submit to God’s righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 3:24; Romans 8:3–4; Matthew 5:17–18"> For Christ is the end of the law, with the result that there is righteousness for everyone who believes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Nehemiah 9:29"> For Moses writes about the righteousness that is by the law: “The one who does these things will live by them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:11–14; Proverbs 30:4; Ephesians 4:8–10"> But the righteousness that is by faith says: “Do not say in your heart, ‘Who will ascend into heaven?’” (that is, to bring Christ down)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Revelation 1:18; Romans 4:25"> or “Who will descend into the abyss?” (that is, to bring Christ up from the dead).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:14; Romans 1:16–17; 1 Timothy 4:6"> But what does it say? “The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart” (that is, the word of faith that we preach),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 12:8; Acts 16:31; Philippians 2:11"> because if you confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Romans 10:9; Galatians 2:16"> For with the heart one believes and thus has righteousness and with the mouth one confesses and thus has salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Romans 9:33; 1 Peter 2:6"> For the scripture says, “Everyone who believes in him will not be put to shame.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; Revelation 17:14; Galatians 3:28"> For there is no distinction between the Jew and the Greek, for the same Lord is Lord of all, who richly blesses all who call on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:21; Joel 2:32"> For everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 26:17–18; Titus 1:3"> How are they to call on one they have not believed in? And how are they to believe in one they have not heard of? And how are they to hear without someone preaching to them ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Nahum 1:15; Ephesians 2:17"> And how are they to preach unless they are sent? As it is written, “How timely is the arrival of those who proclaim the good news&#42;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 3:3; John 12:38–40"> But not all have obeyed the good news, for Isaiah says, “Lord, who has believed our report?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 3:2; Galatians 3:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:13"> Consequently faith comes from what is heard, and what is heard comes through the preached word of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 19:4; Romans 1:8; Matthew 24:14"> But I ask, have they not heard? Yes, they have: Their voice has gone out to all the earth, and their words to the ends of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Romans 11:11; Romans 11:14"> But again I ask, didn’t Israel understand? First Moses says, “I will make you jealous by those who are not a nation; with a senseless nation I will provoke you to anger.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:1–2; Romans 9:30; Isaiah 55:4–5"> And Isaiah is even bold enough to say, “I was found by those who did not seek me; I became well known to those who did not ask for me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 65:2–5; 1 Peter 2:8; Jeremiah 35:15"> But about Israel he says, “All day long I held out my hands to this disobedient and stubborn people!”</span>
