## Exhortation to Mutual Acceptance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:34; Romans 5:2"> Receive one another, then, just as Christ also received you, to God’s glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Matthew 15:24; John 1:11"> For I tell you that Christ has become a servant of the circumcised on behalf of God’s truth to confirm the promises made to the fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:50; Psalms 18:49; Romans 3:29"> and thus the Gentiles glorify God for his mercy. As it is written, “Because of this I will confess you among the Gentiles, and I will sing praises to your name.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 67:3–4; Deuteronomy 32:43; Isaiah 42:10–12"> And again it says: “Rejoice, O Gentiles, with his people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 117:1"> And again, “Praise the Lord all you Gentiles, and let all the peoples praise him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 11:10; Isaiah 11:1; Matthew 12:21"> And again Isaiah says, “The root of Jesse will come, and the one who rises to rule over the Gentiles, in him will the Gentiles hope.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:16–17; Romans 12:12; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> Now may the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace as you believe in him, so that you may abound in hope by the power of the Holy Spirit.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Motivation for Writing the Letter

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 2 Peter 1:12; 1 John 2:21"> But I myself am fully convinced about you, my brothers and sisters, that you yourselves are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, and able to instruct one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 15:10; Romans 1:5"> But I have written more boldly to you on some points so as to remind you, because of the grace given to me by God</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:13; Romans 1:1; Acts 9:15"> to be a minister of Christ Jesus to the Gentiles. I serve the gospel of God like a priest, so that the Gentiles may become an acceptable offering, sanctified by the Holy Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14–16; 2 Corinthians 12:11–21; Hebrews 5:1"> So I boast in Christ Jesus about the things that pertain to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 15:12; Romans 1:5; Acts 21:19"> For I will not dare to speak of anything except what Christ has accomplished through me in order to bring about the obedience of the Gentiles, by word and deed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:12; Hebrews 2:4; Acts 19:11–12"> in the power of signs and wonders, in the power of the Spirit of God. So from Jerusalem even as far as Illyricum I have fully preached the gospel of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:13–16; 1 Corinthians 3:9–15; Ephesians 2:20–22"> And in this way I desire to preach where Christ has not been named, so as not to build on another person’s foundation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 52:15; Isaiah 65:1"> but as it is written: “Those who were not told about him will see, and those who have not heard will understand.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Intention of Visiting the Romans

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 1:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:17–18"> This is the reason I was often hindered from coming to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 15:32; Romans 1:10–12; Romans 15:29"> But now there is nothing more to keep me in these regions, and I have for many years desired to come to you</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:28; Romans 1:12; Acts 15:3"> when I go to Spain. For I hope to visit you when I pass through and that you will help me on my journey there, after I have enjoyed your company for a while.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Acts 19:21; Acts 20:22"> But now I go to Jerusalem to minister to the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:1–9; Acts 11:27–30; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> For Macedonia and Achaia are pleased to make some contribution for the poor among the saints in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:11; Galatians 6:6; Philemon 1:19"> For they were pleased to do this, and indeed they are indebted to the Jerusalem saints. For if the Gentiles have shared in their spiritual things, they are obligated also to minister to them in material things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 15:24; Philippians 4:17; Colossians 1:6"> Therefore after I have completed this and have safely delivered this bounty to them, I will set out for Spain by way of you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 1:10–12; Psalms 16:11"> and I know that when I come to you I will come in the fullness of Christ’s blessing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 1:11; 2 Corinthians 4:11"> Now I urge you, brothers and sisters, through our Lord Jesus Christ and through the love of the Spirit, to join fervently with me in prayer to God on my behalf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:2; 2 Corinthians 8:4; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> Pray that I may be rescued from those who are disobedient in Judea and that my ministry in Jerusalem may be acceptable to the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:18; Philemon 1:20; Philemon 1:7"> so that by God’s will I may come to you with joy and be refreshed in your company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; Philippians 4:9; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> Now may the God of peace be with all of you. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
