<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 3:18–19; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Galatians 3:13"> There is therefore now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 6:14; 2 Corinthians 3:17; Romans 6:18"> For the law of the life-giving Spirit in Christ Jesus has set you free from the law of sin and death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 13:39; Galatians 4:4–5; Hebrews 7:18–19"> For God achieved what the law could not do because it was weakened through the flesh. By sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh and concerning sin, he condemned sin in the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 5:16; Galatians 5:22–25; Colossians 1:22"> so that the righteous requirement of the law may be fulfilled in us, who do not walk according to the flesh but according to the Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Galatians 5:19–25; John 3:6"> For those who live according to the flesh have their outlook shaped by the things of the flesh, but those who live according to the Spirit have their outlook shaped by the things of the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 6:8; Romans 8:7; Romans 8:13"> For the outlook of the flesh is death, but the outlook of the Spirit is life and peace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 John 2:15–16; James 4:4"> because the outlook of the flesh is hostile to God, for it does not submit to the law of God, nor is it able to do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:5–6; Hebrews 11:5–6; Romans 8:9"> Those who are in the flesh cannot please God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 4:6; 1 Corinthians 3:16; 2 Timothy 1:14"> You, however, are not in the flesh but in the Spirit, if indeed the Spirit of God lives in you. Now if anyone does not have the Spirit of Christ, this person does not belong to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 3:17; Galatians 2:20; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> But if Christ is in you, your body is dead because of sin, but the Spirit is your life because of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:14; Romans 6:4–5; 2 Corinthians 4:11"> Moreover if the Spirit of the one who raised Jesus from the dead lives in you, the one who raised Christ from the dead will also make your mortal bodies alive through his Spirit who lives in you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 6:2–15; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20; 1 Peter 4:2–3"> So then, brothers and sisters, we are under obligation, not to the flesh, to live according to the flesh</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 6:8; 1 Peter 2:11; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> (for if you live according to the flesh, you will die), but if by the Spirit you put to death the deeds of the body you will live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 5:18; John 1:12; Galatians 3:26"> For all who are led by the Spirit of God are the sons of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:5–7; 2 Timothy 1:7; 1 Corinthians 2:12"> For you did not receive the spirit of slavery leading again to fear, but you received the Spirit of adoption, by whom we cry, “Abba, Father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:13; 2 Corinthians 1:22; 2 Corinthians 5:5"> The Spirit himself bears witness to our spirit that we are God’s children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 4:7; Galatians 3:29; Ephesians 3:6"> And if children, then heirs (namely, heirs of God and also fellow heirs with Christ) – if indeed we suffer with him so we may also be glorified with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:17–18; 1 Peter 4:13; 1 Peter 1:5–7"> For I consider that our present sufferings cannot even be compared to the glory that will be revealed to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 3:17–18; Romans 8:23; 1 John 3:2"> For the creation eagerly waits for the revelation of the sons of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–19; Genesis 5:29; Romans 8:22"> For the creation was subjected to futility – not willingly but because of God who subjected it – in hope</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 22:3–5; Acts 3:21; 2 Peter 3:13"> that the creation itself will also be set free from the bondage of decay into the glorious freedom of God’s children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:4; Jeremiah 12:11; John 16:21"> For we know that the whole creation groans and suffers together until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 3:20–21; 2 Corinthians 5:2–5; Hebrews 9:28"> Not only this, but we ourselves also, who have the firstfruits of the Spirit, groan inwardly as we eagerly await our adoption, the redemption of our bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:7; Hebrews 11:1; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> For in hope we were saved. Now hope that is seen is not hope, because who hopes for what he sees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Hebrews 10:36; Psalms 27:14"> But if we hope for what we do not see, we eagerly wait for it with endurance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:20; Ephesians 6:18; Galatians 4:6"> In the same way, the Spirit helps us in our weakness, for we do not know how we should pray, but the Spirit himself intercedes for us with inexpressible groanings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; 1 John 5:14–15; Ephesians 2:18"> And he who searches our hearts knows the mind of the Spirit, because the Spirit intercedes on behalf of the saints according to God’s will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; James 1:12; Genesis 50:20"> And we know that all things work together for good for those who love God, who are called according to his purpose,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4–5; Jeremiah 1:5; Ephesians 1:11"> because those whom he foreknew he also predestined to be conformed to the image of his Son, that his Son would be the firstborn among many brothers and sisters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; Romans 8:28; Ephesians 1:11"> And those he predestined, he also called; and those he called, he also justified; and those he justified, he also glorified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 118:6; Jeremiah 1:19; Psalms 56:11"> What then shall we say about these things? If God is for us, who can be against us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:16; Romans 8:28; Psalms 84:11"> Indeed, he who did not spare his own Son, but gave him up for us all – how will he not also, along with him, freely give us all things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Revelation 12:10–11; Isaiah 50:8–9; Isaiah 54:17"> Who will bring any charge against God’s elect? It is God who justifies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 7:25; Romans 8:27; Hebrews 9:24"> Who is the one who will condemn? Christ is the one who died (and more than that, he was raised), who is at the right hand of God, and who also is interceding for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 10:28; Psalms 103:17; John 16:33"> Who will separate us from the love of Christ? Will trouble, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or danger, or sword?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 44:22; Isaiah 53:7; 1 Corinthians 15:30"> As it is written, “For your sake we encounter death all day long; we were considered as sheep to be slaughtered.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:57; 1 John 4:4; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> No, in all these things we have complete victory through him who loved us!</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 10:28; Romans 14:8; 2 Timothy 1:12"> For I am convinced that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor heavenly rulers, nor things that are present, nor things to come, nor powers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 10:28–30; Romans 8:35; Romans 5:8"> nor height, nor depth, nor anything else in creation will be able to separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
</div>
