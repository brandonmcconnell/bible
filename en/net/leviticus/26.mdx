## The Benefits of Obedience

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; Deuteronomy 11:13–15; Deuteronomy 28:1–14"> “‘If you walk in my statutes and are sure to obey my commandments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 8:12; Psalms 67:6; Leviticus 25:21"> I will give you your rains in their time so that the land will give its yield and the trees of the field will produce their fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 9:13; Joel 2:26; Joel 2:19"> Threshing season will extend for you until the season for harvesting grapes, and the season for harvesting grapes will extend until sowing season, so you will eat your bread until you are satisfied, and you will live securely in your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 35:9; Zephaniah 3:13; Psalms 147:14"> I will grant peace in the land so that you will lie down to sleep without anyone terrifying you. I will remove harmful animals from the land, and no sword of war will pass through your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> You will pursue your enemies and they will fall before you by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 23:10; Deuteronomy 28:7; Deuteronomy 32:30"> Five of you will pursue a hundred, and a hundred of you will pursue ten thousand, and your enemies will fall before you by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 17:6–7; Nehemiah 9:23; Ezekiel 16:62"> I will turn to you, make you fruitful, multiply you, and maintain my covenant with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 25:22; 2 Kings 19:29; Luke 12:17"> You will still be eating stored produce from the previous year and will have to clean out what is stored from the previous year to make room for new.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 25:8; Revelation 21:3; Exodus 29:45"> “‘I will put my tabernacle in your midst and I will not abhor you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; Jeremiah 30:22; Jeremiah 7:23"> I will walk among you, and I will be your God and you will be my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:27; Leviticus 25:38; Psalms 116:16"> I am the Lord your God who brought you out from the land of Egypt, from being their slaves, and I broke the bars of your yoke and caused you to walk upright.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Consequences of Disobedience

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Leviticus 26:18; Jeremiah 17:27"> “‘If, however, you do not obey me and keep all these commandments –</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 17:15; Deuteronomy 31:16; Zechariah 7:11–13"> if you reject my statutes and abhor my regulations so that you do not keep all my commandments and you break my covenant –</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 31:8; Jeremiah 5:17; Micah 6:15"> I for my part will do this to you: I will inflict horror on you, consumption and fever, which diminish eyesight and drain away the vitality of life. You will sow your seed in vain because your enemies will eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 17:10; Proverbs 28:1; Psalms 53:5"> I will set my face against you. You will be struck down before your enemies, those who hate you will rule over you, and you will flee when there is no one pursuing you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 26:21; Leviticus 26:28; Leviticus 26:24"> “‘If, in spite of all these things, you do not obey me, I will discipline you seven times more on account of your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:23; 1 Kings 17:1; Daniel 4:37"> I will break your strong pride and make your sky like iron and your land like bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 127:1; Deuteronomy 11:17; Isaiah 49:4"> Your strength will be used up in vain, your land will not give its yield, and the trees of the land will not produce their fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 26:18; Leviticus 26:27"> “‘If you walk in hostility against me and are not willing to obey me, I will increase your affliction seven times according to your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:15; Deuteronomy 32:24; Judges 5:6"> I will send the wild animals against you and they will bereave you of your children, annihilate your cattle, and diminish your population so that your roads will become deserted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Jeremiah 2:30; Amos 4:6–12"> “‘If in spite of these things you do not allow yourselves to be disciplined and you walk in hostility against me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 18:26; 2 Samuel 22:27; Job 9:4"> I myself will also walk in hostility against you and strike you seven times on account of your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 14:12; Ezekiel 5:17; Jeremiah 29:17–18"> I will bring on you an avenging sword, a covenant vengeance. Although you will gather together into your cities, I will send pestilence among you and you will be given into enemy hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Micah 6:14; Isaiah 3:1; Psalms 105:16"> When I break off your supply of bread, ten women will bake your bread in one oven; they will ration your bread by weight, and you will eat and not be satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 26:24; Leviticus 26:21"> “‘If in spite of this you do not obey me but walk in hostility against me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 59:18; Jeremiah 21:5; Isaiah 66:15"> I will walk in hostile rage against you and I myself will also discipline you seven times on account of your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:10; Lamentations 4:10; Matthew 24:19"> You will eat the flesh of your sons and the flesh of your daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 23:20; Isaiah 27:9; Ezekiel 6:13"> I will destroy your high places and cut down your incense altars, and I will stack your dead bodies on top of the lifeless bodies of your idols. I will abhor you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:3; Lamentations 1:10; Hebrews 10:26"> I will lay your cities waste and make your sanctuaries desolate, and I will refuse to smell your soothing aromas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 19:8; Jeremiah 18:16"> I myself will make the land desolate and your enemies who live in it will be appalled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Zechariah 7:14; Ezekiel 20:23; Deuteronomy 28:64–66"> I will scatter you among the nations and unsheathe the sword after you, so your land will become desolate and your cities will become a waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:21; Leviticus 26:43; Leviticus 25:2–4"> “‘Then the land will make up for its Sabbaths all the days it lies desolate while you are in the land of your enemies; then the land will rest and make up its Sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 24:5–6; Romans 8:22"> All the days of the desolation it will have the rest it did not have on your Sabbaths when you lived on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Isaiah 30:17; Leviticus 26:17"> “‘As for the ones who remain among you, I will bring despair into their hearts in the lands of their enemies. The sound of a blowing leaf will pursue them, and they will flee as one who flees the sword and fall down even though there is no pursuer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 7:12–13; Judges 2:14; Jeremiah 37:10"> They will stumble over each other as those who flee before a sword, though there is no pursuer, and there will be no one to take a stand for you before your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:48; Isaiah 27:13; Jeremiah 42:17–18"> You will perish among the nations; the land of your enemies will consume you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Restoration through Confession and Repentance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:17; Ezekiel 24:23; Ezekiel 33:10"> “‘As for the ones who remain among you, they will rot away because of their iniquity in the lands of your enemies, and they will also rot away because of their ancestors’ iniquities which are with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Nehemiah 9:2–5; 1 John 1:8–10"> However, when they confess their iniquity and their ancestors’ iniquity which they committed by trespassing against me, by which they also walked in hostility against me</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:7; 2 Chronicles 32:26; 2 Chronicles 12:6–7"> (and I myself will walk in hostility against them and bring them into the land of their enemies), and then their uncircumcised hearts become humbled and they make up for their iniquity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Exodus 6:5; Ezekiel 16:60"> I will remember my covenant with Jacob and also my covenant with Isaac and also my covenant with Abraham, and I will remember the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Romans 8:7; Leviticus 26:15; John 7:7"> The land will be abandoned by them in order that it may make up for its Sabbaths while it is made desolate without them, and they will make up for their iniquity because they have rejected my regulations and have abhorred my statutes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Romans 11:2; Nehemiah 9:31; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> In spite of this, however, when they are in the land of their enemies I will not reject them and abhor them to make a complete end of them, to break my covenant with them, for I am the Lord their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 25:38; Ezekiel 20:22; Ezekiel 20:9"> I will remember for them the covenant with their ancestors whom I brought out from the land of Egypt in the sight of the nations to be their God. I am the Lord.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
