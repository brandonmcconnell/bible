<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 1:1; Galatians 4:24–25; Exodus 19:1"> The Lord spoke to Moses at Mount Sinai:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:21; Leviticus 26:34–35; Exodus 23:10"> “Speak to the Israelites and tell them, ‘When you enter the land that I am giving you, the land must observe a Sabbath to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 23:10"> Six years you may sow your field, and six years you may prune your vineyard and gather the produce,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:21; Leviticus 25:20–23; Exodus 23:10–11"> but in the seventh year the land must have a Sabbath of complete rest – a Sabbath to the Lord. You must not sow your field or prune your vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 19:29; Isaiah 37:30"> You must not gather in the aftergrowth of your harvest and you must not pick the grapes of your unpruned vines; the land must have a year of complete rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 2:44; Exodus 23:11; Acts 4:34–35"> You may have the Sabbath produce of the land to eat – you, your male servant, your female servant, your hired worker, the resident foreigner who stays with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> your cattle, and the wild animals that are in your land – all its produce will be for you to eat.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Regulations for the Jubilee Year of Release

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 23:15; Genesis 2:2"> “‘You must count off seven weeks of years, seven times seven years, and the days of the seven weeks of years will amount to forty-nine years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:24; Leviticus 23:27; 1 Thessalonians 1:8"> You must sound loud horn blasts – in the seventh month, on the tenth day of the month, on the Day of Atonement – you must sound the horn in your entire land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:8; Isaiah 63:4; 1 Peter 2:16"> So you must consecrate the fiftieth year, and you must proclaim a release in the land for all its inhabitants. That year will be your jubilee; each one of you must return to his property and each one of you must return to his clan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 27:17; Leviticus 25:4–5"> That fiftieth year will be your jubilee; you must not sow the land, harvest its aftergrowth, or pick the grapes of its unpruned vines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 25:6–7"> Because that year is a jubilee, it will be holy to you – you may eat its produce from the field.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Release of Landed Property

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Leviticus 27:17–24; Numbers 36:4"> “‘In this year of jubilee you must each return to your property.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Leviticus 25:17; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> If you make a sale to your fellow citizen or buy from your fellow citizen, no one is to wrong his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 27:18–23; Philippians 4:5"> You may buy it from your fellow citizen according to the number of years since the last jubilee; he may sell it to you according to the years of produce that are left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 25:27; Leviticus 25:51"> The more years there are, the more you may make its purchase price, and the fewer years there are, the less you must make its purchase price, because he is only selling to you a number of years of produce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 19:14; Leviticus 19:32; Leviticus 25:43"> No one is to oppress his fellow citizen, but you must fear your God, because I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:6; Deuteronomy 12:10; Proverbs 1:33"> You must obey my statutes and my regulations; you must be sure to keep them so that you may live securely in the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:26; Psalms 85:12; Leviticus 26:5"> “‘The land will give its fruit and you may eat until you are satisfied, and you may live securely in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 25:4; Luke 12:29; Numbers 11:4"> If you say, ‘What will we eat in the seventh year if we do not sow and gather our produce?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:8; Proverbs 10:22; 2 Corinthians 9:10"> I will command my blessing for you in the sixth year so that it may yield the produce for three years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 19:29; Leviticus 26:10; Isaiah 37:30"> and you may sow the eighth year and eat from that sixth year’s produce – old produce. Until you bring in the ninth year’s produce, you may eat old produce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Psalms 39:12; Ezekiel 48:14"> The land must not be sold without reclaim because the land belongs to me, for you are foreigners and residents with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Leviticus 25:51–53; Leviticus 25:27"> In all your landed property you must provide for the right of redemption of the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ruth 2:20; Jeremiah 32:7–8; Ruth 3:12"> “‘If your brother becomes impoverished and sells some of his property, his near redeemer is to come to you and redeem what his brother sold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 5:7"> If a man has no redeemer, but he prospers and gains enough for its redemption,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> he is to calculate the value of the years it was sold, refund the balance to the man to whom he had sold it, and return to his property.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 35:9–10; Leviticus 25:10; 1 Peter 1:4–5"> If he has not prospered enough to refund a balance to him, then what he sold will belong to the one who bought it until the jubilee year, but it must revert in the jubilee and the original owner may return to his property.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Release of Houses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> “‘If a man sells a residential house in a walled city, its right of redemption must extend until one full year from its sale; its right of redemption must extend to a full calendar year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> If it is not redeemed before the full calendar year is ended, the house in the walled city will belong without reclaim to the one who bought it throughout his generations; it will not revert in the jubilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–8"> The houses of villages, however, which have no wall surrounding them must be considered as the field of the land; they will have the right of redemption and must revert in the jubilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 21:1–45; Numbers 35:1–8"> As for the cities of the Levites, the houses in the cities which they possess, the Levites must have a perpetual right of redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 18:20–24; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> Whatever someone among the Levites might redeem – the sale of a house which is his property in a city – must revert in the jubilee, because the houses of the cities of the Levites are their property in the midst of the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 25:23; Acts 4:36–37; Numbers 35:2–5"> Moreover, the open field areas of their cities must not be sold, because that is their perpetual possession.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Debt and Slave Regulations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:7–8; Acts 11:29; Leviticus 25:25"> “‘If your brother becomes impoverished and is indebted to you, you must support him; he must live with you like a foreign resident.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Ezekiel 18:8"> Do not take interest or profit from him, but you must fear your God and your brother must live with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> You must not lend him your money at interest and you must not sell him food for profit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 11:45; Numbers 15:41; Exodus 20:2"> I am the Lord your God who brought you out from the land of Egypt to give you the land of Canaan – to be your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 21:2; 1 Kings 9:22; 2 Kings 4:1"> “‘If your brother becomes impoverished with regard to you so that he sells himself to you, you must not subject him to slave service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 21:2–3"> He must be with you as a hired worker, as a resident foreigner; he must serve with you until the year of jubilee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 25:28; Exodus 21:3; John 8:32"> but then he may go free, he and his children with him, and may return to his family and to the property of his ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Leviticus 25:55; Romans 6:22; 1 Corinthians 7:21–23"> Since they are my servants whom I brought out from the land of Egypt, they must not be sold in a slave sale.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Colossians 4:1; Leviticus 25:46; Exodus 1:13–14"> You must not rule over him harshly, but you must fear your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 12:44; Psalms 2:8–9; Isaiah 14:1–2"> “‘As for your male and female slaves who may belong to you – you may buy male and female slaves from the nations all around you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Isaiah 56:3–6"> Also you may buy slaves from the children of the foreigners who reside with you, and from their families that are with you, whom they have fathered in your land, they may become your property.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 14:2; Leviticus 25:43"> You may give them as inheritance to your children after you to possess as property. You may enslave them perpetually. However, as for your brothers the Israelites, no man may rule over his brother harshly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="James 2:5; Leviticus 25:26; 1 Samuel 2:7–8"> “‘If a resident foreigner who is with you prospers and your brother becomes impoverished with regard to him so that he sells himself to a resident foreigner who is with you or to a member of a foreigner’s family,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; Hebrews 2:11–13; Leviticus 25:35"> after he has sold himself he retains a right of redemption. One of his brothers may redeem him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Leviticus 25:26"> or his uncle or his cousin may redeem him, or anyone of the rest of his blood relatives – his family – may redeem him, or if he prospers he may redeem himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Isaiah 16:14; Isaiah 21:16; Leviticus 25:53"> He must calculate with the one who bought him the number of years from the year he sold himself to him until the jubilee year, and the cost of his sale must correspond to the number of years, according to the rate of wages a hired worker would have earned while with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> If there are still many years, in keeping with them he must refund most of the cost of his purchase for his redemption,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> but if only a few years remain until the jubilee, he must calculate for himself in keeping with the remaining years and refund it for his redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Leviticus 25:46; Leviticus 25:43"> He must be with the one who bought him like a yearly hired worker. The one who bought him must not rule over him harshly in your sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 21:2–3; Leviticus 25:40–41; Isaiah 49:9"> If, however, he is not redeemed in these ways, he must go free in the jubilee year, he and his children with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Leviticus 25:42; Isaiah 43:3; Exodus 13:3"> because the Israelites are my own servants; they are my servants whom I brought out from the land of Egypt. I am the Lord your God.</span>
</div>
</div>
