<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:26; 1 Peter 1:14–16; Leviticus 11:47"> as well as to distinguish between the holy and the common, and between the unclean and the clean,</span>

## Perpetual Statutes Moses spoke to Aaron

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 21:22; Numbers 18:9–10; Leviticus 7:9"> Then Moses spoke to Aaron and to Eleazar and Ithamar, his remaining sons, “Take the grain offering which remains from the gifts of the Lord and eat it unleavened beside the altar, for it is most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Leviticus 6:16; Numbers 18:10"> You must eat it in a holy place because it is your allotted portion and the allotted portion of your sons from the gifts of the Lord, for this is what I have been commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:11; Leviticus 7:29–34; Exodus 29:24–28"> Also, the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution offering you must eat in a ceremonially clean place, you and your sons and daughters with you, for they have been given as your allotted portion and the allotted portion of your sons from the peace offering sacrifices of the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 7:34; Genesis 17:13; Leviticus 7:29–30"> The thigh of the contribution offering and the breast of the wave offering they must bring in addition to the gifts of the fat parts to wave them as a wave offering before the Lord, and it will belong to you and your sons with you for a perpetual statute just as the Lord has commanded.”</span>

## The Problem with the Inaugural Sin Offering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 9:3; Leviticus 9:15; Leviticus 6:26"> Later Moses sought diligently for the sin offering male goat, but it had actually been burnt. So he became angry at Eleazar and Ithamar, Aaron’s remaining sons, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 7:6–7; Numbers 18:1; Leviticus 6:24–30"> “Why did you not eat the sin offering in the sanctuary? For it is most holy and he gave it to you to bear the iniquity of the congregation, to make atonement on their behalf before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 6:30; Leviticus 6:26"> See here! Its blood was not brought into the holy place within! You should certainly have eaten it in the sanctuary just as I commanded!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 9:12; Leviticus 9:8; Jeremiah 14:12"> But Aaron spoke to Moses, “See here! Just today they presented their sin offering and their burnt offering before the Lord and such things as these have happened to me! If I had eaten a sin offering today would the Lord have been pleased?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 7:8–9; Matthew 12:20; 2 Chronicles 30:18–20"> When Moses heard this explanation, he was satisfied.</span>
