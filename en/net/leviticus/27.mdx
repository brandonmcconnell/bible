<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord spoke to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 6:2; 1 Samuel 1:28; Judges 11:30–31"> “Speak to the Israelites and tell them, ‘When a man makes a special votive offering based on the conversion value of persons to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 27:25; Numbers 18:16"> the conversion value of the male from twenty years old up to sixty years old is fifty shekels by the standard of the sanctuary shekel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 27:9–10; Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 26:15"> If the person is a female, the conversion value is thirty shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> If the person is from five years old up to twenty years old, the conversion value of the male is twenty shekels, and for the female ten shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 3:40–43; Numbers 18:14–16"> If the person is one month old up to five years old, the conversion value of the male is five shekels of silver, and for the female the conversion value is three shekels of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 90:10"> If the person is from sixty years old and older, if he is a male the conversion value is fifteen shekels, and for the female ten shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 14:7; Leviticus 14:21–22; Luke 21:1–4"> If he is too poor to pay the conversion value, he must stand the person before the priest and the priest will establish his conversion value; according to what the man who made the vow can afford, the priest will establish his conversion value.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Redemption of Vowed Animals

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> “‘If what is vowed is a kind of animal from which an offering may be presented to the Lord, anything which he gives to the Lord from this kind of animal will be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 27:15–33; James 1:8"> He must not replace or exchange it, good for bad or bad for good, and if he does indeed exchange one animal for another animal, then both the original animal and its substitute will be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:18; Malachi 1:14"> If what is vowed is an unclean animal from which an offering must not be presented to the Lord, then he must stand the animal before the priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and the priest will establish its conversion value, whether good or bad. According to the assessed conversion value of the priest, thus it will be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:19; Leviticus 27:15; Leviticus 22:14"> If, however, the person who made the vow redeems the animal, he must add one fifth to its conversion value.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Redemption of Vowed Houses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 25:29–31; Leviticus 27:21"> “‘If a man consecrates his house as holy to the Lord, the priest will establish its conversion value, whether good or bad. Just as the priest establishes its conversion value, thus it will stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 27:13"> If the one who consecrates it redeems his house, he must add to it one fifth of its conversion value in silver, and it will belong to him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Redemption of Vowed Fields

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:11–14; Hosea 3:2; Acts 4:34–37"> “‘If a man consecrates to the Lord some of his own landed property, the conversion value must be calculated in accordance with the amount of seed needed to sow it, a homer of barley seed being priced at fifty shekels of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> If he consecrates his field in the jubilee year, the conversion value will stand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 25:15–16; Leviticus 25:51–52; Leviticus 25:27"> but if he consecrates his field after the jubilee, the priest will calculate the price for him according to the years that are left until the next jubilee year, and it will be deducted from the conversion value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 27:13"> If, however, the one who consecrated the field redeems it, he must add to it one fifth of the conversion price and it will belong to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> If he does not redeem the field, but sells the field to someone else, he may never redeem it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:29; Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 25:31"> When it reverts in the jubilee, the field will be holy to the Lord like a permanently dedicated field; it will become the priest’s property.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Leviticus 25:25"> “‘If he consecrates to the Lord a field he has purchased, which is not part of his own landed property,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 27:18; Leviticus 27:12"> the priest will calculate for him the amount of its conversion value until the jubilee year, and he must pay the conversion value on that jubilee day as something that is holy to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 25:28; Leviticus 27:20"> In the jubilee year the field will return to the one from whom he bought it, the one to whom it belongs as landed property.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Numbers 18:16; Numbers 3:47"> Every conversion value must be calculated by the standard of the sanctuary shekel; twenty gerahs to the shekel.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Redemption of the Firstborn

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12–13; Numbers 18:17"> “‘Surely no man may consecrate a firstborn that already belongs to the Lord as a firstborn among the animals; whether it is an ox or a sheep, it belongs to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 27:11"> If, however, it is among the unclean animals, he may ransom it according to its conversion value and must add one fifth to it, but if it is not redeemed it must be sold according to its conversion value.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Things Permanently Dedicated to the Lord

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 6:17–19; Leviticus 27:21; Exodus 22:20"> “‘Surely anything which a man permanently dedicates to the Lord from all that belongs to him, whether from people, animals, or his landed property, must be neither sold nor redeemed; anything permanently dedicated is most holy to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:18–23; Numbers 21:2–3"> Any human being who is permanently dedicated must not be ransomed; such a person must be put to death.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Redemption of the Tithe

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 23:23; Nehemiah 13:12; Genesis 28:22"> “‘Any tithe of the land, from the grain of the land or from the fruit of the trees, belongs to the Lord; it is holy to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> If a man redeems part of his tithe, however, he must add one fifth to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:13; Ezekiel 20:37; Micah 7:14"> All the tithe of herd or flock, everything which passes under the rod, the tenth one will be holy to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 27:10"> The owner must not examine the animals to distinguish between good and bad, and he must not exchange it. If, however, he does exchange it, both the original animal and its substitute will be holy. It must not be redeemed.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Colophon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 26:46; Numbers 1:1; Hebrews 12:18–25"> These are the commandments which the Lord commanded Moses to tell the Israelites at Mount Sinai.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
