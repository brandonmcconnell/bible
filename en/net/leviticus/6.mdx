<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the Lord spoke to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Acts 5:4; Leviticus 5:15"> “When a person sins and commits a trespass against the Lord by deceiving his fellow citizen in regard to something held in trust, or a pledge, or something stolen, or by extorting something from his fellow citizen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 23:4; Leviticus 19:12; Exodus 22:9–11"> or has found something lost and denies it and swears falsely concerning any one of the things that someone might do to sin –</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:18; Amos 3:10; Leviticus 5:3–4"> when it happens that he sins and he is found guilty, then he must return whatever he had stolen, or whatever he had extorted, or the thing that he had held in trust, or the lost thing that he had found,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 5:16; Luke 19:8; Numbers 5:7–8"> or anything about which he swears falsely. He must restore it in full and add one fifth to it; he must give it to its owner when he is found guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 5:15; Leviticus 5:18; Isaiah 53:10–11"> Then he must bring his guilt offering to the Lord, a flawless ram from the flock, convertible into silver shekels, for a guilt offering to the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 4:26; 1 John 1:9; 1 John 2:1–2"> So the priest will make atonement on his behalf before the Lord and he will be forgiven for whatever he has done to become guilty.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Sacrificial Instructions for the Priests: The Burnt Offering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then the Lord spoke to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 6:12–13; Numbers 28:3; Exodus 29:38–42"> “Command Aaron and his sons, ‘This is the law of the burnt offering. The burnt offering is to remain on the hearth on the altar all night until morning, and the fire of the altar must be kept burning on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 28:39–43; Leviticus 16:4; Leviticus 1:13"> Then the priest must put on his linen robe and must put linen leggings over his bare flesh, and he must take up the fatty ashes of the burnt offering that the fire consumed on the altar, and he must place them beside the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 4:12; Ezekiel 44:19; Hebrews 13:11–13"> Then he must take off his clothes and put on other clothes, and he must bring the fatty ashes outside the camp to a ceremonially clean place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 9:24; Exodus 29:38–42; Nehemiah 13:31"> but the fire which is on the altar must be kept burning on it. It must not be extinguished. So the priest must kindle wood on it morning by morning, and he must arrange the burnt offering on it and offer the fat of the peace offering up in smoke on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> A continual fire must be kept burning on the altar. It must not be extinguished.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Grain Offering of the Common Person

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 15:4; Leviticus 2:1–2; John 6:32"> “‘This is the law of the grain offering. The sons of Aaron are to present it before the Lord in front of the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Leviticus 2:9"> and the priest must take up with his hand some of the choice wheat flour of the grain offering and some of its olive oil, and all of the frankincense that is on the grain offering, and he must offer its memorial portion up in smoke on the altar as a soothing aroma to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:29; Leviticus 2:3; Leviticus 10:12–13"> Aaron and his sons are to eat what is left over from it. It must be eaten unleavened in a holy place; they are to eat it in the courtyard of the Meeting Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Leviticus 2:11; Numbers 18:9–10"> It must not be baked with yeast. I have given it as their portion from my gifts. It is most holy, like the sin offering and the guilt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 6:29; Numbers 18:10; Haggai 2:12–14"> Every male among the sons of Aaron may eat it. It is a perpetual allotted portion throughout your generations from the gifts of the Lord. Anyone who touches these gifts must be holy.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Grain Offering of the Priests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then the Lord spoke to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 29:2; Exodus 16:36; Leviticus 2:1–16"> “This is the offering of Aaron and his sons which they must present to the Lord on the day when he is anointed: a tenth of an ephah of choice wheat flour as a continual grain offering, half of it in the morning and half of it in the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 2:5; Leviticus 7:9; 1 Chronicles 9:31"> It must be made with olive oil on a griddle and you must bring it well soaked, so you must present a grain offering of broken pieces as a soothing aroma to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:22–25; Isaiah 53:10; Leviticus 8:21"> The high priest who succeeds him from among his sons must do it. It is a perpetual statute; it must be offered up in smoke as a whole offering to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 2:10; Leviticus 6:16–17"> Every grain offering of a priest must be a whole offering; it must not be eaten.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sin Offering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Then the Lord spoke to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 4:24; Leviticus 1:11; Leviticus 1:3"> “Tell Aaron and his sons, ‘This is the law of the sin offering. In the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered the sin offering must be slaughtered before the Lord. It is most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 10:17–18; Ezekiel 44:28–29; Hosea 4:8"> The priest who offers it for sin is to eat it. It must be eaten in a holy place, in the court of the Meeting Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 29:37; Haggai 2:12; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> Anyone who touches its meat must be holy, and whoever spatters some of its blood on a garment, you must wash whatever he spatters it on in a holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 11:33; Leviticus 15:12; Hebrews 9:9–10"> Any clay vessel it is boiled in must be broken, and if it was boiled in a bronze vessel, then that vessel must be rubbed out and rinsed in water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 6:25; Leviticus 6:17–18; Numbers 18:10"> Any male among the priests may eat it. It is most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 10:18; Leviticus 16:27–28; Hebrews 13:11"> But any sin offering from which some of its blood is brought into the Meeting Tent to make atonement in the sanctuary must not be eaten. It must be burned up in the fire.</span>
</div>
</div>
