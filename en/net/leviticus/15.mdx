<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Leviticus 13:1; Leviticus 11:1"> The Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; 2 Samuel 3:29; Leviticus 22:4"> “Speak to the Israelites and tell them, ‘When any man has a discharge from his body, his discharge is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:20; Ezekiel 16:26; Leviticus 12:3"> Now this is his uncleanness in regard to his discharge – whether his body secretes his discharge or blocks his discharge, he is unclean. All the days that his body has a discharge or his body blocks his discharge, this is his uncleanness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Ephesians 5:11; 1 Corinthians 15:33"> “‘Any bed the man with a discharge lies on will be unclean, and any furniture he sits on will be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 16:26; Leviticus 17:15"> Anyone who touches his bed must wash his clothes, bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 1:16"> The one who sits on the furniture the man with a discharge sits on must wash his clothes, bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The one who touches the body of the man with a discharge must wash his clothes, bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:4; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Isaiah 1:16"> If the man with a discharge spits on a person who is ceremonially clean, that person must wash his clothes, bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:34"> Any means of riding the man with a discharge rides on will be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:8; Psalms 26:6; Leviticus 15:8"> Anyone who touches anything that was under him will be unclean until evening, and the one who carries those items must wash his clothes, bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Anyone whom the man with the discharge touches without having rinsed his hands in water must wash his clothes, bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 11:32–33; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> A clay vessel which the man with the discharge touches must be broken, and any wooden utensil must be rinsed in water.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Purity Regulations for Male Bodily Discharges

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 14:8; Leviticus 8:33; Leviticus 15:28"> “‘When the man with the discharge becomes clean from his discharge he is to count off for himself seven days for his purification, and he must wash his clothes, bathe in fresh water, and be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 12:8; Leviticus 12:6; Hebrews 10:10"> Then on the eighth day he is to take for himself two turtledoves or two young pigeons, and he is to present himself before the Lord at the entrance of the Meeting Tent and give them to the priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 14:30–31; Leviticus 4:26; Matthew 3:17"> and the priest is to make one of them a sin offering and the other a burnt offering. So the priest is to make atonement for him before the Lord for his discharge.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:10–11; Leviticus 22:4; Leviticus 15:5"> “‘When a man has a seminal emission, he must bathe his whole body in water and be unclean until evening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and he must wash in water any clothing or leather that has semen on it, and it will be unclean until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–5; 1 Corinthians 6:12"> When a man has sexual intercourse with a woman and there is a seminal emission, they must bathe in water and be unclean until evening.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Female Bodily Discharges

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 12:2; Matthew 15:19; Mark 5:25"> “‘When a woman has a discharge and her discharge is blood from her body, she is to be in her menstruation seven days, and anyone who touches her will be unclean until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 15:4–9; Proverbs 2:16–19; Proverbs 6:35"> Anything she lies on during her menstruation will be unclean, and anything she sits on will be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 7:14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Isaiah 22:14"> Anyone who touches her bed must wash his clothes, bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Anyone who touches any furniture she sits on must wash his clothes, bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> If there is something on the bed or on the furniture she sits on, when he touches it he will be unclean until evening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 20:18; 1 Peter 2:11; Leviticus 18:19"> and if a man actually has sexual intercourse with her so that her menstrual impurity touches him, then he will be unclean seven days and any bed he lies on will be unclean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 5:25; Matthew 9:20; Luke 8:43"> “‘When a woman’s discharge of blood flows many days not at the time of her menstruation, or if it flows beyond the time of her menstruation, all the days of her discharge of impurity will be like the days of her menstruation – she is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Any bed she lies on all the days of her discharge will be to her like the bed of her menstruation, any furniture she sits on will be unclean like the impurity of her menstruation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 15:21; Hebrews 10:22; Leviticus 17:15–16"> and anyone who touches them will be unclean, and he must wash his clothes, bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Purity Regulations from Female Bodily Discharges

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 15:13–15; Galatians 3:13; Ephesians 1:6–7"> “‘If she becomes clean from her discharge, then she is to count off for herself seven days, and afterward she will be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Then on the eighth day she must take for herself two turtledoves or two young pigeons and she must bring them to the priest at the entrance of the Meeting Tent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and the priest is to make one a sin offering and the other a burnt offering. So the priest is to make atonement for her before the Lord from her discharge of impurity.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Summary of Purification Regulations for Bodily Discharges

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 19:20; Numbers 19:13; Ezekiel 5:11"> “‘Thus you are to set the Israelites apart from their impurity so that they do not die in their impurity by defiling my tabernacle which is in their midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 11:46; Leviticus 13:59; Numbers 6:13"> This is the law of the one with a discharge: the one who has a seminal emission and becomes unclean by it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 15:19; Leviticus 20:18; Leviticus 15:24–25"> the one who is sick in her menstruation, the one with a discharge, whether male or female, and a man who has sexual intercourse with an unclean woman.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
