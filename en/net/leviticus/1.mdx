## Introduction to the Sacrificial Regulations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 19:3; Exodus 25:22; Exodus 29:42"> Then the Lord called to Moses and spoke to him from the Meeting Tent:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 22:18–19; Romans 12:6; 1 Chronicles 16:29"> “Speak to the Israelites and tell them, ‘When someone among you presents an offering to the Lord, you must present your offering from the domesticated animals, either from the herd or from the flock.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Burnt Offering Regulations: Animal from the Herd

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Hebrews 9:14; Leviticus 22:19–24"> “‘If his offering is a burnt offering from the herd he must present it as a flawless male; he must present it at the entrance of the Meeting Tent for its acceptance before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:10; Exodus 29:15; Leviticus 4:20"> He must lay his hand on the head of the burnt offering, and it will be accepted for him to make atonement on his behalf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 1:11; Leviticus 3:8; Leviticus 3:2"> Then the one presenting the offering must slaughter the bull before the Lord, and the sons of Aaron, the priests, must present the blood and splash the blood against the sides of the altar which is at the entrance of the Meeting Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 7:8; Genesis 3:21"> Next, the one presenting the offering must skin the burnt offering and cut it into parts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 6:12–13; Genesis 22:9; 1 Chronicles 21:26"> and the sons of Aaron, the priest, must put fire on the altar and arrange wood on the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 9:13–14; Leviticus 1:12; 1 Kings 18:33"> Then the sons of Aaron, the priests, must arrange the parts with the head and the suet on the wood that is in the fire on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 8:21; Leviticus 1:13; Ephesians 5:2"> Finally, the one presenting the offering must wash its entrails and its legs in water and the priest must offer all of it up in smoke on the altar – it is a burnt offering, a gift of a soothing aroma to the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Animal from the Flock

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6–7; Leviticus 1:2–3; Genesis 8:20"> “‘If his offering is from the flock for a burnt offering – from the sheep or the goats – he must present a flawless male,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 1:5; Exodus 40:22; Leviticus 7:2"> and must slaughter it on the north side of the altar before the Lord, and the sons of Aaron, the priests, will splash its blood against the altar’s sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Next, the one presenting the offering must cut it into parts, with its head and its suet, and the priest must arrange them on the wood which is in the fire, on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 1:9"> Then the one presenting the offering must wash the entrails and the legs in water, and the priest must present all of it and offer it up in smoke on the altar – it is a burnt offering, a gift of a soothing aroma to the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

## From the Birds

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 5:7; Luke 2:24; Leviticus 12:8"> “‘If his offering to the Lord is a burnt offering from the birds, he must present his offering from the turtledoves or from the young pigeons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 5:8–9; Psalms 22:21; 1 John 2:27"> The priest must present it at the altar, pinch off its head and offer the head up in smoke on the altar, and its blood must be drained out against the side of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 1:2; Luke 1:35; Leviticus 16:27"> Then the priest must remove its entrails by cutting off its tail feathers, and throw them to the east side of the altar into the place of fatty ashes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:10; John 19:30; Leviticus 1:9–10"> and tear it open by its wings without dividing it into two parts. Finally, the priest must offer it up in smoke on the altar on the wood which is in the fire – it is a burnt offering, a gift of a soothing aroma to the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>
