<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord spoke to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44–45; Leviticus 20:7; 1 Peter 1:15–16"> “Speak to the whole congregation of the Israelites and tell them, ‘You must be holy because I, the Lord your God, am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Exodus 20:8; Hebrews 12:9"> Each of you must respect his mother and his father, and you must keep my Sabbaths. I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:1; Exodus 20:23; Psalms 115:4–7"> Do not turn to idols, and you must not make for yourselves gods of cast metal. I am the Lord your God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Eating the Peace Offering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 22:19; Leviticus 22:29; Leviticus 7:16"> “‘When you sacrifice a peace offering sacrifice to the Lord, you must sacrifice it so that it is accepted for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 7:11–17"> It must be eaten on the day of your sacrifice and on the following day, but what is left over until the third day must be burned up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 7:18–21; Leviticus 22:25; Jeremiah 16:18"> If, however, it is eaten on the third day, it is spoiled, it will not be accepted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 22:15; Leviticus 5:1"> and the one who eats it will bear his punishment for iniquity because he has profaned what is holy to the Lord. That person will be cut off from his people.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Leaving the Gleanings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:22; Deuteronomy 24:19–22; Ruth 2:2"> “‘When you gather in the harvest of your land, you must not completely harvest the corner of your field, and you must not gather up the gleanings of your harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:6; Leviticus 25:6; Isaiah 24:13"> You must not pick your vineyard bare, and you must not gather up the fallen grapes of your vineyard. You must leave them for the poor and the foreigner. I am the Lord your God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Dealing Honestly

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 101:7; Ephesians 4:25; Colossians 3:9"> “‘You must not steal, you must not tell lies, and you must not deal falsely with your fellow citizen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Deuteronomy 5:11; Leviticus 18:21"> You must not swear falsely in my name, so that you do not profane the name of your God. I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 5:4; Malachi 3:5; Deuteronomy 24:14–15"> You must not oppress your neighbor or commit robbery against him. You must not withhold the wages of the hired laborer overnight until morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:18; Leviticus 19:32; Leviticus 25:17"> You must not curse a deaf person or put a stumbling block in front of a blind person. You must fear your God; I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Justice, Love, and Propriety

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:17; Proverbs 24:23; Deuteronomy 27:19"> “‘You must not deal unjustly in judgment: you must neither show partiality to the poor nor honor the rich. You must judge your fellow citizen fairly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 23:1; Exodus 23:7; Proverbs 20:19"> You must not go about as a slanderer among your people. You must not stand idly by when your neighbor’s life is at stake. I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 2:9; 1 John 2:11; Luke 17:3"> You must not hate your brother in your heart. You must surely reprove your fellow citizen so that you do not incur sin on account of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 22:39–40; Mark 12:31–34"> You must not take vengeance or bear a grudge against the children of your people, but you must love your neighbor as yourself. I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Deuteronomy 22:9–11; Matthew 9:16–17"> You must keep my statutes. You must not allow two different kinds of your animals to breed, you must not sow your field with two different kinds of seed, and you must not wear a garment made of two different kinds of fabric.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Lying with a Slave Woman

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:23–24; Exodus 21:20–21"> “‘When a man has sexual intercourse with a woman, although she is a slave woman designated for another man and she has not yet been ransomed, or freedom has not been granted to her, there will be an obligation to pay compensation. They must not be put to death, because she was not free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 6:6–7; Leviticus 5:1–6; Leviticus 5:15"> He must bring his guilt offering to the Lord at the entrance of the Meeting Tent, a guilt offering ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:20"> and the priest is to make atonement for him with the ram of the guilt offering before the Lord for his sin that he has committed, and he will be forgiven of his sin that he has committed.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Produce of Fruit Trees

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 22:27; Acts 7:51; Jeremiah 9:25–26"> “‘When you enter the land and plant any fruit tree, you must consider its fruit to be forbidden. Three years it will be forbidden to you; it must not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9; Deuteronomy 18:4; Numbers 18:12–13"> In the fourth year all its fruit will be holy, praise offerings to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Haggai 1:9–11; Malachi 3:8–10; Leviticus 26:3–4"> Then in the fifth year you may eat its fruit to add its produce to your harvest. I am the Lord your God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Blood, Hair, and Body

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:10–14; 2 Kings 17:17; 2 Chronicles 33:6"> “‘You must not eat anything with the blood still in it. You must not practice either divination or soothsaying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Deuteronomy 14:1; Ezekiel 44:20"> You must not round off the corners of the hair on your head or ruin the corners of your beard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Leviticus 21:5; 1 Kings 18:28"> You must not slash your body for a dead person or incise a tattoo on yourself. I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:17–18; Leviticus 21:7; Hosea 4:12–14"> Do not profane your daughter by making her a prostitute, so that the land does not practice prostitution and become full of lewdness.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Purity, Honor, Respect, and Honesty

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 26:2; Leviticus 19:3; Ecclesiastes 5:1"> “‘You must keep my Sabbaths and fear my sanctuary. I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; Leviticus 20:27; Leviticus 20:6–7"> Do not turn to the spirits of the dead and do not seek familiar spirits to become unclean by them. I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:1; Lamentations 5:12; Proverbs 16:31"> You must stand up in the presence of the aged, honor the presence of an elder, and fear your God. I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 23:9; Exodus 22:21; Malachi 3:5"> When a foreigner resides with you in your land, you must not oppress him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:19; Leviticus 19:18; Exodus 12:48–49"> The foreigner who resides with you must be to you like a native citizen among you; so you must love him as yourself, because you were foreigners in the land of Egypt. I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:15; Deuteronomy 25:13; Proverbs 16:11"> You must not do injustice in the regulation of measures, whether of length, weight, or volume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Exodus 20:2; Deuteronomy 25:13–15"> You must have honest balances, honest weights, an honest ephah, and an honest hin. I am the Lord your God who brought you out from the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:1; Leviticus 18:4–5; Deuteronomy 5:1"> You must be sure to obey all my statutes and regulations. I am the Lord.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
