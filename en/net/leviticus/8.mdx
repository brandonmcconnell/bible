## Clothing Aaron

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; Ephesians 5:26; Exodus 29:4"> So Moses brought Aaron and his sons forward and washed them with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Isaiah 61:10; Galatians 3:27"> Then he put the tunic on Aaron, wrapped the sash around him, and clothed him with the robe. Next he put the ephod on him and placed on him the decorated band of the ephod, and fastened the ephod closely to him with the band.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 2:63; 1 Thessalonians 5:8; Isaiah 59:17"> He then set the breastpiece on him and put the Urim and Thummim into the breastpiece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 2:9–11; Zechariah 6:11–14; Exodus 28:36–38"> Finally, he set the turban on his head and attached the gold plate, the holy diadem, to the front of the turban just as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Anointing the Tabernacle and Aaron, and Clothing Aaron’s Sons

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 40:9–11; Leviticus 8:2; Exodus 30:23–29"> Then Moses took the anointing oil and anointed the tabernacle and everything in it, and so consecrated them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Titus 3:6; Exodus 30:29; Exodus 16:19"> Next he sprinkled some of it on the altar seven times and so anointed the altar, all its vessels, and the wash basin and its stand to consecrate them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 30:30; Psalms 133:2; Exodus 29:7"> He then poured some of the anointing oil on the head of Aaron and anointed him to consecrate him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 29:8–9; Revelation 5:10; Revelation 1:6"> Moses also brought forward Aaron’s sons, clothed them with tunics, wrapped sashes around them, and wrapped headbands on them just as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Consecration Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:19; Psalms 66:15; 1 Peter 3:18"> Then he brought near the sin offering bull and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the sin offering bull,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 4:7; Hebrews 9:18–23; Ezekiel 43:19–27"> and he slaughtered it. Moses then took the blood and put it all around on the horns of the altar with his finger and decontaminated the altar, and he poured out the rest of the blood at the base of the altar and so consecrated it to make atonement on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 29:13; Leviticus 3:3–5; Leviticus 4:8–9"> Then he took all the fat on the entrails, the protruding lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys and their fat, and Moses offered it all up in smoke on the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 4:11–12; Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 6:30"> but the rest of the bull – its hide, its flesh, and its dung – he completely burned up outside the camp just as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 8:2; Leviticus 1:4–13; Exodus 29:15–18"> Then he presented the burnt offering ram and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and he slaughtered it. Moses then splashed the blood against the altar’s sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 1:8"> Then he cut the ram into parts, and Moses offered the head, the parts, and the suet up in smoke,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Exodus 29:18; Leviticus 1:17"> but the entrails and the legs he washed with water, and Moses offered the whole ram up in smoke on the altar – it was a burnt offering for a soothing aroma, a gift to the Lord, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 8:2; Leviticus 8:29; Exodus 29:19–31"> Then he presented the second ram, the ram of ordination, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the ram</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 29:20; Leviticus 14:14; Romans 12:1"> and he slaughtered it. Moses then took some of its blood and put it on Aaron’s right earlobe, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 9:18–22"> Next he brought Aaron’s sons forward, and Moses put some of the blood on their right earlobes, on their right thumbs, and on the big toes of their right feet, and Moses splashed the rest of the blood against the altar’s sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 3:9; Leviticus 3:3–5; Isaiah 53:10"> Then he took the fat (the fatty tail, all the fat on the entrails, the protruding lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys and their fat ) and the right thigh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 29:23; 1 Timothy 2:5; Acts 5:12"> and from the basket of unleavened bread that was before the Lord he took one unleavened loaf, one loaf of bread mixed with olive oil, and one wafer, and placed them on the fat parts and on the right thigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:21; Hebrews 9:14; Exodus 29:24–37"> He then put all of them on the palms of Aaron and his sons, who waved them as a wave offering before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 29:25; Psalms 22:13–14; Hebrews 10:14–22"> Moses then took them from their palms and offered them up in smoke on the altar on top of the burnt offering – they were an ordination offering for a soothing aroma; it was a gift to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:31; 1 Peter 4:11; Leviticus 7:30–34"> Finally, Moses took the breast and waved it as a wave offering before the Lord from the ram of ordination. It was Moses’ share just as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Anointing Aaron, his Sons, and their Garments

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 29:21; Numbers 3:3; Exodus 30:30"> Then Moses took some of the anointing oil and some of the blood which was on the altar and sprinkled it on Aaron and his garments, and on his sons and his sons’ garments with him. So he consecrated Aaron, his garments, and his sons and his sons’ garments with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 29:31–32; Galatians 2:20; 1 Samuel 2:13–17"> Then Moses said to Aaron and his sons, “Boil the meat at the entrance of the Meeting Tent, and there you are to eat it and the bread which is in the ordination offering basket, just as I have commanded, saying, ‘Aaron and his sons are to eat it,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 29:34; Exodus 12:10; Hebrews 3:13–14"> but the remainder of the meat and the bread you must burn with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 29:35; Numbers 19:12; Ezekiel 43:25–27"> And you must not go out from the entrance of the Meeting Tent for seven days, until the day when your days of ordination are completed, because you must be ordained over a seven-day period.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 7:27; Hebrews 7:16; Hebrews 10:11–12"> What has been done on this day the Lord has commanded to be done to make atonement for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 3:7; Deuteronomy 11:1; 1 Kings 2:3"> You must reside at the entrance of the Meeting Tent day and night for seven days and keep the charge of the Lord so that you will not die, for this is what I have been commanded.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:2; Exodus 40:16"> So Aaron and his sons did all the things the Lord had commanded through Moses.</span>
</div>
