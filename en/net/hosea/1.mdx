## Symbols of Sin and Judgment: The Prostitute and Her Children

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 2:4–5; Hosea 3:1; Deuteronomy 31:16"> When the Lord first spoke through Hosea, he said to him, “Go marry a prostitute who will bear illegitimate children conceived through prostitution, because the nation continually commits spiritual prostitution by turning away from the Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:1–3"> So Hosea married Gomer, the daughter of Diblaim. Then she conceived and gave birth to a son for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 10:7–8; 2 Kings 10:10–11; Ezekiel 23:10"> Then the Lord said to Hosea, “Name him ‘Jezreel,’ because in a little while I will punish the dynasty of Jehu on account of the bloodshed in the valley of Jezreel, and I will put an end to the kingdom of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 6:33; Joshua 17:16; Hosea 2:18"> At that time, I will destroy the military power of Israel in the valley of Jezreel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; 1 Peter 2:10; Hosea 2:4"> She conceived again and gave birth to a daughter. Then the Lord said to him, “Name her ‘No Pity’ (Lo-Ruhamah) because I will no longer have pity on the nation of Israel. For I will certainly not forgive their guilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6; Isaiah 12:2; 2 Kings 19:35"> But I will have pity on the nation of Judah. I will deliver them by the Lord their God; I will not deliver them by the warrior’s bow, by sword, by military victory, by chariot horses, or by chariots.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> When she had weaned ‘No Pity’ (Lo-Ruhamah) she conceived again and gave birth to another son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:1"> Then the Lord said: “Name him ‘Not My People’ (Lo-Ammi), because you are not my people and I am not your God.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Restoration of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:18; Galatians 4:6–7; John 1:12"> However, in the future the number of the people of Israel will be like the sand of the sea which can be neither measured nor numbered. Although it was said to them, “You are not my people,” it will be said to them, “You are children of the living God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 3:5; Isaiah 11:12–13; Jeremiah 50:4–5"> Then the people of Judah and the people of Israel will be gathered together. They will appoint for themselves one leader, and will flourish in the land. Certainly, the day of Jezreel will be great!</span>
</div>
</div>
