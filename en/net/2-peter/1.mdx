## Salutation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 2:13; Romans 3:21–26; Titus 1:4"> From Simeon Peter, a slave and apostle of Jesus Christ, to those who through the righteousness of our God and Savior, Jesus Christ, have been granted a faith just as precious as ours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 3:18; John 17:3; Philippians 3:8"> May grace and peace be lavished on you as you grow in the rich knowledge of God and of Jesus our Lord!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Believers’ Salvation and the Work of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 8:28–30"> I can pray this because his divine power has bestowed on us everything necessary for life and godliness through the rich knowledge of the one who called us by his own glory and excellence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 4:23–24; Ezekiel 36:25–27; 2 Corinthians 3:18"> Through these things he has bestowed on us his precious and most magnificent promises, so that by means of what was promised you may become partakers of the divine nature, after escaping the worldly corruption that is produced by evil desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:8; Proverbs 4:23; 2 Peter 3:18"> For this very reason, make every effort to add to your faith excellence, to excellence, knowledge;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Romans 5:3–4; Luke 21:19"> to knowledge, self-control; to self-control, perseverance; to perseverance, godliness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:1; Romans 12:10; 1 Thessalonians 3:12"> to godliness, brotherly affection; to brotherly affection, unselfish love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Titus 3:14; John 15:2; Hebrews 6:12"> For if these things are really yours and are continually increasing, they will keep you from becoming ineffective and unproductive in your pursuit of knowing our Lord Jesus Christ more intimately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Peter 2:18–20; Ephesians 5:26; Titus 2:14"> But concerning the one who lacks such things – he is blind. That is to say, he is nearsighted, since he has forgotten about the cleansing of his past sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 3:17; Luke 6:47–49; Hebrews 6:11"> Therefore, brothers and sisters, make every effort to be sure of your calling and election. For by doing this you will never stumble into sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Matthew 25:34; Revelation 3:21"> For thus an entrance into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ, will be richly provided for you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Salvation Based on the Word of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 2:21; Romans 15:14–15; Philippians 3:1"> Therefore, I intend to remind you constantly of these things even though you know them and are well established in the truth that you now have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 3:1; 2 Peter 1:14; Hebrews 13:3"> Indeed, as long as I am in this tabernacle, I consider it right to stir you up by way of a reminder,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6; John 21:18–19; Deuteronomy 31:14"> since I know that my tabernacle will soon be removed, because our Lord Jesus Christ revealed this to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19–29; Psalms 71:18; Joshua 24:24–29"> Indeed, I will also make every effort that, after my departure, you have a testimony of these things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 1:14; 1 Timothy 1:4"> For we did not follow cleverly concocted fables when we made known to you the power and return of our Lord Jesus Christ; no, we were eyewitnesses of his grandeur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 9:7; Matthew 17:5; Matthew 3:17"> For he received honor and glory from God the Father, when that voice was conveyed to him by the Majestic Glory: “This is my dear Son, in whom I am delighted.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 17:6; Joshua 5:15; Exodus 3:5"> When this voice was conveyed from heaven, we ourselves heard it, for we were with him on the holy mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Isaiah 9:2; Psalms 119:105"> Moreover, we possess the prophetic word as an altogether reliable thing. You do well if you pay attention to this as you would to a light shining in a murky place, until the day dawns and the morning star rises in your hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 3:3; Romans 12:6; Romans 6:6"> Above all, you do well if you recognize this: No prophecy of scripture ever comes about by the prophet’s own imagination,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:15–17; 1 Peter 1:11; 2 Samuel 23:2"> for no prophecy was ever borne of human impulse; rather, men carried along by the Holy Spirit spoke from God.</span>
</div>
</div>
