<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 18:1; Judges 17:8; Malachi 2:15"> In those days Israel had no king. There was a Levite living temporarily in the remote region of the Ephraimite hill country. He acquired a concubine from Bethlehem in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:28; Deuteronomy 22:21; Leviticus 21:9"> However, she got angry at him and went home to her father’s house in Bethlehem in Judah. When she had been there four months,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 34:3; Genesis 50:21; Leviticus 19:17"> her husband came after her, hoping he could convince her to return. He brought with him his servant and a pair of donkeys. When she brought him into her father’s house and the girl’s father saw him, he greeted him warmly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> His father-in-law, the girl’s father, persuaded him to stay with him for three days, and they ate and drank together, and spent the night there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 19:8; Genesis 18:5; Psalms 104:15"> On the fourth day they woke up early and the Levite got ready to leave. But the girl’s father said to his son-in-law, “Have a bite to eat for some energy, then you can go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 19:9; Judges 16:25; Ruth 3:7"> So the two of them sat down and had a meal together. Then the girl’s father said to the man, “Why not stay another night and have a good time!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> When the man got ready to leave, his father-in-law convinced him to stay another night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> He woke up early in the morning on the fifth day so he could leave, but the girl’s father said, “Get some energy. Wait until later in the day to leave!” So they ate a meal together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Luke 24:29; James 4:13–14"> When the man got ready to leave with his concubine and his servant, his father-in-law, the girl’s father, said to him, “Look! The day is almost over! Stay another night! Since the day is over, stay another night here and have a good time. You can get up early tomorrow and start your trip home.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 15:63; Joshua 15:8; Judges 1:8"> But the man did not want to stay another night. He left and traveled as far as Jebus (that is, Jerusalem). He had with him a pair of saddled donkeys and his concubine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:6; Judges 1:21; Judges 19:10"> When they got near Jebus, it was getting quite late and the servant said to his master, “Come on, let’s stop at this Jebusite city and spend the night in it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> But his master said to him, “We should not stop at a foreign city where non-Israelites live. We will travel on to Gibeah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:26; Hosea 5:8; Isaiah 10:29"> He said to his servant, “Come on, we will go into one of the other towns and spend the night in Gibeah or Ramah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So they traveled on, and the sun went down when they were near Gibeah in the territory of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 19:18; Matthew 25:35; Matthew 25:43"> They stopped there and decided to spend the night in Gibeah. They came into the city and sat down in the town square, but no one invited them to spend the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 19:1; Psalms 104:23; Genesis 3:19"> But then an old man passed by, returning at the end of the day from his work in the field. The man was from the Ephraimite hill country; he was living temporarily in Gibeah. (The residents of the town were Benjaminites.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 32:17; Genesis 16:8"> When he looked up and saw the traveler in the town square, the old man said, “Where are you heading? Where do you come from?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 18:31; John 15:6; 1 Samuel 1:7"> The Levite said to him, “We are traveling from Bethlehem in Judah to the remote region of the Ephraimite hill country. That’s where I’m from. I had business in Bethlehem in Judah, but now I’m heading home. But no one has invited me into their home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> We have enough straw and grain for our donkeys, and there is enough food and wine for me, your female servant, and the young man who is with your servants. We lack nothing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 6:23; James 2:15–16; Hebrews 13:2"> The old man said, “Everything is just fine! I will take care of all your needs. But don’t spend the night in the town square.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 43:24; Genesis 18:4; Luke 7:44"> So he brought him to his house and fed the donkeys. They washed their feet and had a meal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 19:4–5; 1 Corinthians 6:9; Judges 20:5"> They were having a good time, when suddenly some men of the city, some good-for-nothings, surrounded the house and kept beating on the door. They said to the old man who owned the house, “Send out the man who came to visit you so we can have sex with him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 34:7; 2 Samuel 13:12; Judges 20:6"> The man who owned the house went outside and said to them, “No, my brothers! Don’t do this wicked thing! After all, this man is a guest in my house. Don’t do such a disgraceful thing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 19:8; Deuteronomy 21:14; Genesis 34:2"> Here are my virgin daughter and my guest’s concubine. I will send them out and you can abuse them and do to them whatever you like. But don’t do such a disgraceful thing to this man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 10:9; Hosea 9:9; Hosea 7:4–7"> The men refused to listen to him, so the Levite grabbed his concubine and made her go outside. They raped her and abused her all night long until morning. They let her go at dawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Judges 19:3; Genesis 18:12; 1 Peter 3:6"> The woman arrived back at daybreak and was sprawled out on the doorstep of the house where her master was staying until it became light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> When her master got up in the morning, opened the doors of the house, and went outside to start on his journey, there was the woman, his concubine, sprawled out on the doorstep of the house with her hands on the threshold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 20:5; 1 Kings 18:29"> He said to her, “Get up, let’s leave!” But there was no response. He put her on the donkey and went home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 20:6–7; 1 Samuel 11:7; Romans 10:2"> When he got home, he took a knife, grabbed his concubine, and carved her up into twelve pieces. Then he sent the pieces throughout Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 20:7; Proverbs 13:10; Hosea 9:9"> Everyone who saw the sight said, “Nothing like this has happened or been witnessed during the entire time since the Israelites left the land of Egypt! Take careful note of it! Discuss it and speak!”</span>
