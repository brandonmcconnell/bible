<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:10; Deuteronomy 3:12–17; Proverbs 15:1"> Jephthah assembled all the men of Gilead and they fought with Ephraim. <span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 3:28; Joshua 22:11; Joshua 2:7"> The Gileadites captured the fords of the Jordan River opposite Ephraim. Whenever an Ephraimite fugitive said, “Let me cross over,” the men of Gilead asked him, “Are you an Ephraimite?” If he said, “No,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:2; Psalms 69:15; Mark 14:70"> then they said to him, “Say ‘Shibboleth!’” If he said, “Sibboleth” (and could not pronounce the word correctly), they grabbed him and executed him right there at the fords of the Jordan. On that day forty-two thousand Ephraimites fell dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Jephthah led Israel for six years; then he died and was buried in his city in Gilead.</span>
