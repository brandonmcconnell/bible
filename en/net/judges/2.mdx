## The End of an Era

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 24:28–31; Joshua 22:6"> When Joshua dismissed the people, the Israelites went to their allotted portions of territory, intending to take possession of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; Joshua 24:31; 2 Chronicles 24:2"> The people worshiped the Lord throughout Joshua’s lifetime and as long as the elderly men who outlived him remained alive. These men had witnessed all the great things the Lord had done for Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 24:29–30"> Joshua son of Nun, the Lord’s servant, died at the age of one hundred ten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 19:50; Joshua 24:30"> The people buried him in his allotted land in Timnath Heres in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:12; Titus 1:16; Exodus 5:2"> That entire generation passed away; a new generation grew up that had not personally experienced the Lord’s presence or seen what he had done for Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Monotonous Cycle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 4:1; Judges 6:1; Judges 3:7"> The Israelites did evil before the Lord by worshiping the Baals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 13:5; Deuteronomy 6:14–15"> They abandoned the Lord God of their ancestors who brought them out of the land of Egypt. They followed other gods – the gods of the nations who lived around them. They worshiped them and made the Lord angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 10:6; Psalms 106:36; Judges 3:7"> They abandoned the Lord and worshiped Baal and the Ashtars.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 17:20; Psalms 106:40–42; Deuteronomy 32:30"> The Lord was furious with Israel and handed them over to robbers who plundered them. He turned them over to their enemies who lived around them. They could not withstand their enemies’ attacks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Joshua 23:15–16; Leviticus 26:14–46"> Whenever they went out to fight, the Lord did them harm, just as he had warned and solemnly vowed he would do. They suffered greatly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 13:20; Psalms 106:43–45; 1 Samuel 12:11"> The Lord raised up leaders who delivered them from these robbers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 1:6; Deuteronomy 9:12; Judges 2:7"> But they did not obey their leaders. Instead they prostituted themselves to other gods and worshiped them. They quickly turned aside from the path their ancestors had walked. Their ancestors had obeyed the Lord’s commands, but they did not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; Joshua 1:5; Exodus 2:24"> When the Lord raised up leaders for them, the Lord was with each leader and delivered the people from their enemies while the leader remained alive. The Lord felt sorry for them when they cried out in agony because of what their harsh oppressors did to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 8:33; Judges 4:1; Matthew 23:32"> When a leader died, the next generation would again act more wickedly than the previous one. They would follow after other gods, worshiping them and bowing down to them. They did not give up their practices or their stubborn ways.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Divine Decision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Joshua 23:16; Exodus 32:10–11"> The Lord was furious with Israel. He said, “This nation has violated the terms of the agreement I made with their ancestors by disobeying me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 23:13; Judges 2:3; Judges 3:3"> So I will no longer remove before them any of the nations that Joshua left unconquered when he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; Deuteronomy 8:16; Deuteronomy 13:3"> Joshua left those nations to test Israel. I wanted to see whether or not the people would carefully walk in the path marked out by the Lord, as their ancestors were careful to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> This is why the Lord permitted these nations to remain and did not conquer them immediately; he did not hand them over to Joshua.</span>
</div>
</div>
