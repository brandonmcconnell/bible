## Judah Takes the Lead

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 20:18; Numbers 27:21; Judges 20:28"> After Joshua died, the Israelites asked the Lord, “Who should lead the invasion against the Canaanites and launch the attack?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; Hebrews 7:14; Numbers 2:3"> The Lord said, “The men of Judah should take the lead. Be sure of this! I am handing the land over to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:11; Joshua 19:1; Judges 1:17"> The men of Judah said to their relatives, the men of Simeon, “Invade our allotted land with us and help us attack the Canaanites. Then we will go with you into your allotted land.” So the men of Simeon went with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:8; Exodus 23:28–29; Joshua 11:6–8"> The men of Judah attacked, and the Lord handed the Canaanites and Perizzites over to them. They killed ten thousand men at Bezek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> They met Adoni-Bezek at Bezek and fought him. They defeated the Canaanites and Perizzites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> When Adoni-Bezek ran away, they chased him and captured him. Then they cut off his thumbs and big toes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:33; Revelation 13:10; Luke 6:37–38"> Adoni-Bezek said, “Seventy kings, with thumbs and big toes cut off, used to lick up food scraps under my table. God has repaid me for what I did to them.” They brought him to Jerusalem, where he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 15:63; Judges 1:21"> The men of Judah attacked Jerusalem and captured it. They put the sword to it and set the city on fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 15:13–20; Joshua 11:21; Joshua 10:36"> Later the men of Judah went down to attack the Canaanites living in the hill country, the Negev, and the lowlands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Joshua 14:15; Jeremiah 9:23"> The men of Judah attacked the Canaanites living in Hebron. (Hebron used to be called Kiriath Arba.) They killed Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 15:15; Joshua 10:38–39"> From there they attacked the people of Debir. (Debir used to be called Kiriath Sepher.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:16–17; 1 Samuel 17:25; 1 Samuel 18:23"> Caleb said, “To the man who attacks and captures Kiriath Sepher I will give my daughter Acsah as a wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 3:9"> When Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb’s younger brother, captured it, Caleb gave him his daughter Acsah as a wife.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 15:18–19"> One time Acsah came and charmed her father so she could ask him for some land. When she got down from her donkey, Caleb said to her, “What would you like?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Corinthians 9:5; 1 Peter 3:9"> She answered, “Please give me a special present. Since you have given me land in the Negev, now give me springs of water.” So Caleb gave her both the upper and lower springs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 10:29–32; Judges 4:11; 1 Samuel 15:6"> Now the descendants of the Kenite, Moses’ father-in-law, went up with the people of Judah from the City of Date Palm Trees to Arad in the desert of Judah, located in the Negev. They went and lived with the people of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 21:3; Judges 1:3; Joshua 19:4"> The men of Judah went with their brothers the men of Simeon and defeated the Canaanites living in Zephath. They wiped out Zephath. So people now call the city Hormah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 11:22; Judges 3:3; Joshua 15:45–47"> The men of Judah captured Gaza, Ashkelon, Ekron, and the territory surrounding each of these cities.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Judges 1:2; Psalms 60:12"> The Lord was with the men of Judah. They conquered the hill country, but they could not conquer the people living in the coastal plain, because they had chariots with iron-rimmed wheels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 1:10; Joshua 15:13–14; Numbers 14:24"> Caleb received Hebron, just as Moses had promised. He drove out the three Anakites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 15:63; Judges 19:10–12; Judges 1:8"> The men of Benjamin, however, did not conquer the Jebusites living in Jerusalem. The Jebusites live with the people of Benjamin in Jerusalem to this very day.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Partial Success

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 1:19; 2 Kings 18:7; Numbers 1:32"> When the men of Joseph attacked Bethel, the Lord was with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Genesis 35:6; Genesis 48:3"> When the men of Joseph spied out Bethel (it used to be called Luz),</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 2:12–14; 1 Samuel 30:15"> the spies spotted a man leaving the city. They said to him, “If you show us a secret entrance into the city, we will reward you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 6:22–25"> He showed them a secret entrance into the city, and they put the city to the sword. But they let the man and his extended family leave safely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 7:6; 2 Chronicles 1:17"> He moved to Hittite country and built a city. He named it Luz, and it has kept that name to this very day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 17:11–13; Jeremiah 48:10; Psalms 106:34–35"> The men of Manasseh did not conquer Beth Shan, Taanach, or their surrounding towns. Nor did they conquer the people living in Dor, Ibleam, Megiddo or their surrounding towns. The Canaanites managed to remain in those areas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Whenever Israel was strong militarily, they forced the Canaanites to do hard labor, but they never totally conquered them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; 1 Kings 9:16"> The men of Ephraim did not conquer the Canaanites living in Gezer. The Canaanites lived among them in Gezer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 19:15"> The men of Zebulun did not conquer the people living in Kitron and Nahalol. The Canaanites lived among them and were forced to do hard labor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 19:24–30"> The men of Asher did not conquer the people living in Acco or Sidon, nor did they conquer Ahlab, Aczib, Helbah, Aphek, or Rehob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 106:34–35"> The people of Asher live among the Canaanites residing in the land because they did not conquer them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 19:32–38; Judges 1:30; Psalms 18:24"> The men of Naphtali did not conquer the people living in Beth Shemesh or Beth Anath. They live among the Canaanites residing in the land. The Canaanites living in Beth Shemesh and Beth Anath were forced to do hard labor for them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:1"> The Amorites forced the people of Dan to live in the hill country. They did not allow them to live in the coastal plain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Joshua 19:42; Judges 12:12; Joshua 10:12"> The Amorites managed to remain in Har Heres, Aijalon, and Shaalbim. Whenever the tribe of Joseph was strong militarily, the Amorites were forced to do hard labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 34:4; Joshua 15:2–3"> The border of Amorite territory ran from the Scorpion Ascent to Sela and on up.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
