## Micah Hires a Professional

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:1; Ruth 1:1–2"> There was a young man from Bethlehem in Judah. He was a Levite who had been temporarily residing among the tribe of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:10–11; Judges 17:11; Joshua 24:33"> This man left the town of Bethlehem in Judah to find another place to live. He came to the Ephraimite hill country and made his way to Micah’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Micah said to him, “Where do you come from?” He replied, “I am a Levite from Bethlehem in Judah. I am looking for a new place to live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:19; Judges 18:19–20; 1 Timothy 6:10"> Micah said to him, “Stay with me. Become my adviser and priest. I will give you ten pieces of silver per year, plus clothes and food.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> So the Levite agreed to stay with the man; the young man was like a son to Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 18:30; Numbers 16:8–10; Numbers 16:5"> Micah paid the Levite; the young man became his priest and lived in Micah’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 10:2–3; Proverbs 14:12; John 16:2"> Micah said, “Now I know God will make me rich, because I have this Levite as my priest.”</span>
