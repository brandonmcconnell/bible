<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; Joshua 19:43; 1 John 2:16"> Samson went down to Timnah, where a Philistine girl caught his eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 34:4; Genesis 21:21; Genesis 24:2–3"> When he got home, he told his father and mother, “A Philistine girl in Timnah has caught my eye. Now get her for my wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 15:18; 1 Samuel 17:36; 1 Samuel 14:6"> But his father and mother said to him, “Certainly you can find a wife among your relatives or among all our people! You should not have to go and get a wife from the uncircumcised Philistines.” But Samson said to his father, “Get her for me, because she is the right one for me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:20; Psalms 115:3; Judges 13:1"> Now his father and mother did not realize this was the Lord’s doing, because he was looking for an opportunity to stir up trouble with the Philistines (for at that time the Philistines were ruling Israel).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Samson went down to Timnah. When he approached the vineyards of Timnah, he saw a roaring young lion attacking him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25; 1 Samuel 11:6"> The Lord’s spirit empowered him and he tore the lion in two with his bare hands as easily as one would tear a young goat. But he did not tell his father or mother what he had done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Samson continued on down to Timnah and spoke to the girl. In his opinion, she was just the right one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 1:20; Genesis 29:21"> Some time later, when he went back to marry her, he turned aside to see the lion’s remains. He saw a swarm of bees in the lion’s carcass, as well as some honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:25–30; Proverbs 25:15"> He scooped it up with his hands and ate it as he walked along. When he returned to his father and mother, he offered them some and they ate it. But he did not tell them he had scooped the honey out of the lion’s carcass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 29:22; Revelation 19:9; Esther 1:7–22"> Then Samson’s father accompanied him to Timnah for the marriage. Samson hosted a party there, for this was customary for bridegrooms to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 9:15; 1 Samuel 16:6; 1 Samuel 10:23"> When the Philistines saw he had no attendants, they gave him thirty groomsmen who kept him company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 10:1; Ezekiel 17:2; Genesis 45:22"> Samson said to them, “I will give you a riddle. If you really can solve it during the seven days the party lasts, I will give you thirty linen robes and thirty sets of clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> But if you cannot solve it, you will give me thirty linen robes and thirty sets of clothes.” They said to him, “Let us hear your riddle.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:2–4; 1 Kings 17:6; Romans 8:37"> He said to them,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Out of the one who eats came something to eat;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">out of the strong one came something sweet.”</span><br />They could not solve the riddle for three days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 16:5; Judges 15:6; Proverbs 6:26"> On the fourth day they said to Samson’s bride, “Trick your husband into giving the solution to the riddle. If you refuse, we will burn up you and your father’s family. Did you invite us here to make us poor?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 16:15; Genesis 2:24"> So Samson’s bride cried on his shoulder and said, “You must hate me; you do not love me! You told the young men a riddle, but you have not told me the solution.” He said to her, “Look, I have not even told my father or mother. Do you really expect me to tell you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 16:16; Job 2:9; Judges 16:13"> She cried on his shoulder until the party was almost over. Finally, on the seventh day, he told her because she had nagged him so much. Then she told the young men the solution to the riddle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> On the seventh day, before the sun set, the men of the city said to him,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“What is sweeter than honey?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">What is stronger than a lion?”</span><br />He said to them,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“If you had not plowed with my heifer,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you would not have solved my riddle!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:6; Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25"> The Lord’s spirit empowered him. He went down to Ashkelon and murdered thirty men. He took their clothes and gave them to the men who had solved the riddle. He was furious as he went back home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 15:2; John 3:29; Psalms 55:12–13"> Samson’s bride was then given to his best man.</span>
</div>
