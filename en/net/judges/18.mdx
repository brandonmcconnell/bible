<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 17:6; Judges 19:1; Judges 1:34"> In those days Israel had no king. And in those days the Danite tribe was looking for a place to settle, because at that time they did not yet have a place to call their own among the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 13:25; Joshua 2:1; Judges 18:11"> The Danites sent out from their whole tribe five representatives, capable men from Zorah and Eshtaol, to spy out the land and explore it. They said to them, “Go, explore the land.” They came to the Ephraimite hill country and spent the night at Micah’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 12:6; Genesis 27:22; Matthew 26:73"> As they approached Micah’s house, they recognized the accent of the young Levite. So they stopped there and said to him, “Who brought you here? What are you doing in this place? What is your business here?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 17:10; Ezekiel 13:19; Proverbs 28:21"> He told them what Micah had done for him, saying, “He hired me and I became his priest.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:10; Isaiah 30:1; 1 Kings 22:5"> They said to him, “Seek a divine oracle for us, so we can know if we will be successful on our mission.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 22:6; 1 Kings 22:15; Psalms 33:18"> The priest said to them, “Go with confidence. The Lord will be with you on your mission.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:27–28; 1 Samuel 3:13"> So the five men journeyed on and arrived in Laish. They noticed that the people there were living securely, like the Sidonians do, undisturbed and unsuspecting. No conqueror was troubling them in any way. They lived far from the Sidonians and had no dealings with anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 18:11; Judges 18:2; Judges 13:2"> When the Danites returned to their tribe in Zorah and Eshtaol, their kinsmen asked them, “How did it go?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 2:23–24; Numbers 13:30; Joshua 18:3"> They said, “Come on, let’s attack them, for we saw their land and it is very good. You seem lethargic, but don’t hesitate to invade and conquer the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 18:27; Judges 18:7; Deuteronomy 2:29"> When you invade, you will encounter unsuspecting people. The land is wide! God is handing it over to you – a place that lacks nothing on earth!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 18:16"> So six hundred Danites, fully armed, set out from Zorah and Eshtaol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:25; Joshua 15:60; 2 Chronicles 1:4"> They went up and camped in Kiriath Jearim in Judah. (To this day that place is called Camp of Dan. It is west of Kiriath Jearim.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 24:30; Judges 19:1; Judges 18:2–3"> From there they traveled through the Ephraimite hill country and arrived at Micah’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 17:5; Judges 18:3–4; 1 Samuel 14:28"> The five men who had gone to spy out the land of Laish said to their kinsmen, “Do you realize that inside these houses are an ephod, some personal idols, a carved image, and a metal image? Decide now what you want to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 43:27; 1 Samuel 17:22; John 14:27"> They stopped there, went inside the young Levite’s house (which belonged to Micah), and asked him how he was doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 18:11"> Meanwhile the six hundred Danites, fully armed, stood at the entrance to the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 18:14; Judges 17:4–5; Judges 18:2"> The five men who had gone to spy out the land broke in and stole the carved image, the ephod, the personal idols, and the metal image, while the priest was standing at the entrance to the gate with the six hundred fully armed men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> When these men broke into Micah’s house and stole the carved image, the ephod, the personal idols, and the metal image, the priest said to them, “What are you doing?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 29:9; Job 21:5; Judges 17:10"> They said to him, “Shut up! Put your hand over your mouth and come with us! You can be our adviser and priest. Wouldn’t it be better to be a priest for a whole Israelite tribe than for just one man’s family?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Ezekiel 13:19; Proverbs 30:15"> The priest was happy. He took the ephod, the personal idols, and the carved image and joined the group.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> They turned and went on their way, but they walked behind the children, the cattle, and their possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> After they had gone a good distance from Micah’s house, Micah’s neighbors gathered together and caught up with the Danites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 114:5; 2 Samuel 14:5; 1 Samuel 11:5"> When they called out to the Danites, the Danites turned around and said to Micah, “Why have you gathered together?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18–19; Isaiah 44:18–20; Psalms 115:8"> He said, “You stole my gods that I made, as well as this priest, and then went away. What do I have left? How can you have the audacity to say to me, ‘What do you want?’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 27:2; Job 3:5; 1 Samuel 30:6"> The Danites said to him, “Don’t say another word to us, or some very angry men will attack you, and you and your family will die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The Danites went on their way; when Micah realized they were too strong to resist, he turned around and went home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:7; Judges 18:10"> Now the Danites took what Micah had made, as well as his priest, and came to Laish, where the people were undisturbed and unsuspecting. They struck them down with the sword and burned the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:6; Judges 18:7; Numbers 13:21"> No one came to the rescue because the city was far from Sidon and they had no dealings with anyone. The city was in a valley near Beth Rehob. The Danites rebuilt the city and occupied it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Genesis 14:14; 1 Kings 15:20"> They named it Dan after their ancestor, who was one of Israel’s sons. But the city’s name used to be Laish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:10–11; Judges 13:1; Judges 17:5"> The Danites worshiped the carved image. Jonathan, descendant of Gershom, son of Moses, and his descendants served as priests for the tribe of Dan until the time of the exile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; 1 Samuel 1:3; Judges 19:18"> They worshiped Micah’s carved image the whole time God’s authorized shrine was in Shiloh.</span>
