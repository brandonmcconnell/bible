## The Lord’s Patience Runs Short

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 11:33; 1 Kings 11:7; 2 Kings 23:13"> The Israelites again did evil in the Lord’s sight. They worshiped the Baals and the Ashtars, as well as the gods of Syria, Sidon, Moab, the Ammonites, and the Philistines. They abandoned the Lord and did not worship him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Deuteronomy 32:16–22; 1 Samuel 12:9–10"> The Lord was furious with Israel and turned them over to the Philistines and Ammonites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Isaiah 30:13; Judges 10:5"> They ruthlessly oppressed the Israelites that eighteenth year – that is, all the Israelites living east of the Jordan in Amorite country in Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:1–2; Deuteronomy 28:65; 2 Chronicles 15:5"> The Ammonites crossed the Jordan to fight with Judah, Benjamin, and Ephraim. Israel suffered greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:10; Psalms 107:28; Psalms 107:13"> The Israelites cried out for help to the Lord: “We have sinned against you. We abandoned our God and worshiped the Baals.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 3:31; Numbers 21:21–25; Exodus 14:30"> The Lord said to the Israelites, “Did I not deliver you from Egypt, the Amorites, the Ammonites, the Philistines,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 6:3; 2 Chronicles 26:6–7; Psalms 106:42–43"> the Sidonians, Amalek, and Midian when they oppressed you? You cried out for help to me, and I delivered you from their power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:13; 1 Chronicles 28:9; Judges 2:12"> But since you abandoned me and worshiped other gods, I will not deliver you again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Deuteronomy 32:37–38; 1 Kings 18:27–28"> Go and cry for help to the gods you have chosen! Let them deliver you from trouble!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:18; 2 Samuel 15:26; Proverbs 28:13"> But the Israelites said to the Lord, “We have sinned. You do to us as you see fit, but deliver us today!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 63:9; Psalms 106:44–45; Ezekiel 18:30–32"> They threw away the foreign gods they owned and worshiped the Lord. Finally the Lord grew tired of seeing Israel suffer so much.</span>
</div>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 11:11; Judges 12:7; Judges 1:1"> The leaders of Gilead said to one another, “Who is willing to lead the charge against the Ammonites? He will become the leader of all who live in Gilead!”</span>
