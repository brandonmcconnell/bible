<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 1:19; Psalms 106:42; Judges 3:9"> The Israelites cried out for help to the Lord, because Sisera had nine hundred chariots with iron-rimmed wheels, and he cruelly oppressed the Israelites for twenty years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Luke 2:36; 2 Kings 22:14"> Now Deborah, a prophetess, wife of Lappidoth, was leading Israel at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:19; Jeremiah 31:15; Exodus 18:26"> She would sit under the Date Palm Tree of Deborah between Ramah and Bethel in the Ephraimite hill country. The Israelites would come up to her to have their disputes settled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 13:47; Judges 8:18; Hebrews 11:32"> She summoned Barak son of Abinoam from Kedesh in Naphtali. She said to him, “Is it not true that the Lord God of Israel is commanding you? Go, march to Mount Tabor! Take with you ten thousand men from Naphtali and Zebulun!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 18:40; Ezekiel 38:10–16; Psalms 83:9–10"> I will bring Sisera, the general of Jabin’s army, to you at the Kishon River, along with his chariots and huge army. I will hand him over to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:10–14; Matthew 14:30–31"> Barak said to her, “If you go with me, I will go. But if you do not go with me, I will not go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 2:14; 1 Samuel 2:30; Judges 4:17–22"> She said, “I will indeed go with you. But you will not gain fame on the expedition you are undertaking, for the Lord will turn Sisera over to a woman.” Deborah got up and went with Barak to Kedesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 5:18; Judges 5:15; Judges 4:14"> Barak summoned men from Zebulun and Naphtali to Kedesh. Ten thousand men followed him; Deborah went up with him as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Joshua 19:33; Numbers 10:29"> Now Heber the Kenite had moved away from the Kenites, the descendants of Hobab, Moses’ father-in-law. He lived near the great tree in Zaanannim near Kedesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 89:12; Jeremiah 46:18; Joshua 19:12"> When Sisera heard that Barak son of Abinoam had gone up to Mount Tabor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 4:2–3; Judges 4:7"> he ordered all his chariotry – nine hundred chariots with iron-rimmed wheels – and all the troops he had with him to go from Harosheth-Haggoyim to the River Kishon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:3; 2 Samuel 5:24; Isaiah 52:12"> Deborah said to Barak, “Spring into action, for this is the day the Lord is handing Sisera over to you! Has the Lord not taken the lead?” Barak quickly went down from Mount Tabor with ten thousand men following him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 10:10; Psalms 83:9–10; Judges 5:20–21"> The Lord routed Sisera, all his chariotry, and all his army with the edge of the sword. Sisera jumped out of his chariot and ran away on foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 2:12; Leviticus 26:7–8; James 2:13"> Now Barak chased the chariots and the army all the way to Harosheth Haggoyim. Sisera’s whole army died by the edge of the sword; not even one survived!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 69:22; Isaiah 57:21; Amos 5:19–20"> Now Sisera ran away on foot to the tent of Jael, wife of Heber the Kenite, for King Jabin of Hazor and the family of Heber the Kenite had made a peace treaty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 6:19"> Jael came out to welcome Sisera. She said to him, “Stop and rest, my lord. Stop and rest with me. Don’t be afraid.” So Sisera stopped to rest in her tent, and she put a blanket over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 5:25–26; Genesis 24:43; Isaiah 41:17"> He said to her, “Give me a little water to drink, because I’m thirsty.” She opened a goatskin container of milk and gave him some milk to drink. Then she covered him up again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 2:3–5; 2 Samuel 17:20"> He said to her, “Stand watch at the entrance to the tent. If anyone comes along and asks you, ‘Is there a man here?’ say ‘No.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 5:26–27; 1 Corinthians 1:27; Judges 3:21"> Then Jael wife of Heber took a tent peg in one hand and a hammer in the other. She crept up on him, drove the tent peg through his temple into the ground while he was asleep from exhaustion, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:10–15; 2 Samuel 17:3"> Now Barak was chasing Sisera. Jael went out to welcome him. She said to him, “Come here and I will show you the man you are searching for.” He went with her into the tent, and there he saw Sisera sprawled out dead with the tent peg in his temple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:24; Psalms 18:47; Psalms 18:39"> That day God humiliated King Jabin of Canaan before the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:12"> Israel’s power continued to overwhelm King Jabin of Canaan until they did away with him.</span>
</div>
