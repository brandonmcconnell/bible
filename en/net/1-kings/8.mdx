<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:2–10; 1 Chronicles 15:25; 2 Samuel 6:12–17"> Then Solomon convened in Jerusalem Israel’s elders, all the leaders of the Israelite tribes and families, so they could witness the transferal of the ark of the Lord’s covenant from the city of David (that is, Zion).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:34; 2 Chronicles 7:8–10; Zechariah 14:16–19"> All the men of Israel assembled before King Solomon during the festival in the month Ethanim (the seventh month).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:9; Joshua 3:6; Numbers 4:15"> When all Israel’s elders had arrived, the priests lifted the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 3:4; 2 Chronicles 1:3; Exodus 40:2–33"> The priests and Levites carried the ark of the Lord, the tent of meeting, and all the holy items in the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:13; 1 Chronicles 16:1; 1 Kings 8:62–63"> Now King Solomon and all the Israelites who had assembled with him went on ahead of the ark and sacrificed more sheep and cattle than could be counted or numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 6:19; 2 Samuel 6:17; 1 Kings 6:27"> The priests brought the ark of the Lord’s covenant to its assigned place in the inner sanctuary of the temple, in the most holy place, under the wings of the cherubs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The cherubs’ wings extended over the place where the ark sat; the cherubs overshadowed the ark and its poles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:13–15; Exodus 37:4–5; Joshua 4:9"> The poles were so long their ends were visible from the holy place in front of the inner sanctuary, but they could not be seen from beyond that point. They have remained there to this very day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 25:21; Hebrews 9:4; Exodus 40:20"> There was nothing in the ark except the two stone tablets Moses had placed there in Horeb. It was there that the Lord made an agreement with the Israelites after he brought them out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 40:34–35; 2 Chronicles 7:1–2"> Once the priests left the holy place, a cloud filled the Lord’s temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:4–5; Ezekiel 44:4; 2 Corinthians 3:18"> The priests could not carry out their duties because of the cloud; the Lord’s glory filled his temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; 2 Chronicles 6:1–11; Leviticus 16:2"> Then Solomon said, “The Lord has said that he lives in thick darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:13; Psalms 132:13–14; Hebrews 9:24"> O Lord, truly I have built a lofty temple for you, a place where you can live permanently.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:18; 1 Kings 8:55–56; 2 Chronicles 30:18–20"> Then the king turned around and pronounced a blessing over the whole Israelite assembly as they stood there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 1:68; 1 Chronicles 29:20; 1 Chronicles 29:10"> He said, “The Lord God of Israel is worthy of praise because he has fulfilled what he promised my father David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:1; 1 Kings 8:29; 2 Samuel 7:4–8"> He told David, ‘Since the day I brought my people Israel out of Egypt, I have not chosen a city from all the tribes of Israel to build a temple in which to live. But I have chosen David to lead my people Israel.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2–3; 1 Chronicles 22:7; 1 Chronicles 28:2"> Now my father David had a strong desire to build a temple to honor the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:7–9; 2 Corinthians 8:12"> The Lord told my father David, ‘It is right for you to have a strong desire to build a temple to honor me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:5; 1 Chronicles 22:8–10; 1 Chronicles 28:6"> But you will not build the temple; your very own son will build the temple for my honor.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:5–6; Philippians 1:6; 1 Kings 8:15"> The Lord has kept the promise he made. I have taken my father David’s place and have occupied the throne of Israel, as the Lord promised. I have built this temple for the honor of the Lord God of Israel</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 8:9; Deuteronomy 31:26; 1 Kings 8:5–6"> and set up in it a place for the ark containing the covenant the Lord made with our ancestors when he brought them out of the land of Egypt.”</span>

## Solomon Prays for Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezra 9:5; 1 Kings 8:54; Exodus 9:33"> Solomon stood before the altar of the Lord in front of the entire assembly of Israel and spread out his hands toward the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:22; Deuteronomy 7:9; Daniel 9:4"> He prayed: “O Lord, God of Israel, there is no god like you in heaven above or on earth below! You maintain covenantal loyalty to your servants who obey you with sincerity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:16; 1 Kings 8:15; 2 Samuel 7:12"> You have kept your word to your servant, my father David; this very day you have fulfilled what you promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 2:4; 1 Kings 9:4–6; Jeremiah 33:17–26"> Now, O Lord, God of Israel, keep the promise you made to your servant, my father David, when you said, ‘You will never fail to have a successor ruling before me on the throne of Israel, provided that your descendants watch their step and serve me as you have done.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:25–29; Isaiah 41:17; 1 Samuel 1:17"> Now, O God of Israel, may the promise you made to your servant, my father David, be realized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:6; Isaiah 66:1; Jeremiah 23:24"> “God does not really live on the earth! Look, if the sky and the highest heaven cannot contain you, how much less this temple I have built!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 88:1–2; Psalms 5:1; Luke 18:1"> But respond favorably to your servant’s prayer and his request for help, O Lord my God. Answer the desperate prayer your servant is presenting to you today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:20; 1 Kings 8:16; 2 Chronicles 7:15–16"> Night and day may you watch over this temple, the place where you promised you would live. May you answer your servant’s prayer for this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 8:39; 1 Kings 8:34; 1 Kings 8:36"> Respond to the request of your servant and your people Israel for this place. Hear from inside your heavenly dwelling place and respond favorably.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 22:8–11; 2 Chronicles 6:22–23; Leviticus 5:1"> “When someone is accused of sinning against his neighbor and the latter pronounces a curse on the alleged offender before your altar in this temple, be willing to forgive the accused if the accusation is false.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:1; Isaiah 3:10–11; Romans 2:6–10"> Listen from heaven and make a just decision about your servants’ claims. Condemn the guilty party, declare the other innocent, and give both of them what they deserve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 26:17; Deuteronomy 28:25; Jonah 3:10"> “The time will come when your people Israel are defeated by an enemy because they sinned against you. If they come back to you, renew their allegiance to you, and pray for your help in this temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:4–9; Daniel 9:12; Genesis 13:15"> then listen from heaven, forgive the sin of your people Israel, and bring them back to the land you gave to their ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Deuteronomy 11:17; 1 Kings 8:33"> “The time will come when the skies are shut up tightly and no rain falls because your people sinned against you. When they direct their prayers toward this place, renew their allegiance to you, and turn away from their sin because you punish them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 27:11; 1 Samuel 12:23; Jeremiah 6:16"> then listen from heaven and forgive the sin of your servants, your people Israel. Certainly you will then teach them the right way to live and send rain on your land that you have given your people to possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:21–22; Leviticus 26:16; 2 Chronicles 20:9"> “The time will come when the land suffers from a famine, a plague, blight and disease, or a locust invasion, or when their enemy lays siege to the cities of the land, or when some other type of plague or epidemic occurs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 73:21–22; 1 Kings 8:22; Philippians 4:6"> When all your people Israel pray and ask for help, as they acknowledge their pain and spread out their hands toward this temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Jeremiah 17:10; Acts 1:24"> then listen from your heavenly dwelling place, forgive their sin, and act favorably toward each one based on your evaluation of his motives. (Indeed you are the only one who can correctly evaluate the motives of all people.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 130:4; Revelation 19:5; Deuteronomy 6:2"> Then they will obey you throughout their lifetimes as they live on the land you gave to our ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Kings 10:1–2; Acts 8:27–40; Exodus 18:8–12"> “Foreigners, who do not belong to your people Israel, will come from a distant land because of your reputation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:24; 2 Kings 17:36; Exodus 3:13–16"> When they hear about your great reputation and your ability to accomplish mighty deeds, they will come and direct their prayers toward this temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 102:15; 1 Samuel 17:46; Psalms 22:27"> Then listen from your heavenly dwelling place and answer all the prayers of the foreigners. Then all the nations of the earth will acknowledge your reputation, obey you like your people Israel do, and recognize that this temple I built belongs to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:34; Psalms 78:67–69; 1 Kings 8:16"> “When you direct your people to march out and fight their enemies, and they direct their prayers to the Lord toward his chosen city and this temple I built for your honor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Genesis 18:25; Psalms 9:4; Jeremiah 5:28"> then listen from heaven to their prayers for help and vindicate them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; Proverbs 20:9; 1 John 1:8–10"> “The time will come when your people will sin against you (for there is no one who is sinless!) and you will be angry with them and deliver them over to their enemies, who will take them as prisoners to their own land, whether far away or close by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 106:6; Nehemiah 1:6–11; Nehemiah 9:26–30"> When your people come to their senses in the land where they are held prisoner, they will repent and beg for your mercy in the land of their imprisonment, admitting, ‘We have sinned and gone astray; we have done evil.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Daniel 6:10; Jeremiah 29:12–14; Deuteronomy 4:29"> When they return to you with all their heart and being in the land where they are held prisoner, and direct their prayers to you toward the land you gave to their ancestors, your chosen city, and the temple I built for your honor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="2 Kings 19:19; 1 Kings 8:45; Zechariah 1:15–16"> then listen from your heavenly dwelling place to their prayers for help and vindicate them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 106:46; 2 Chronicles 30:9; Acts 7:10"> Forgive all the rebellious acts of your sinful people and cause their captors to have mercy on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:10; Jeremiah 11:4; Deuteronomy 4:20"> After all, they are your people and your special possession whom you brought out of Egypt, from the middle of the iron-smelting furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; 1 Kings 8:29; Psalms 86:5"> “May you be attentive to your servant’s and your people Israel’s requests for help and may you respond to all their prayers to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Deuteronomy 14:2; Deuteronomy 32:9"> After all, you picked them out of all the nations of the earth to be your special possession, just as you, O sovereign Lord, announced through your servant Moses when you brought our ancestors out of Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:1; 2 Chronicles 6:12–13; Luke 22:45"> When Solomon finished presenting all these prayers and requests to the Lord, he got up from before the altar of the Lord where he had kneeled and spread out his hands toward the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="1 Kings 8:14; 2 Samuel 6:18; Numbers 6:23–26"> When he stood up, he pronounced a blessing over the entire assembly of Israel, saying in a loud voice:</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Joshua 23:14–15; Joshua 21:44–45; Luke 21:33"> “The Lord is worthy of praise because he has made Israel his people secure just as he promised! Not one of all the faithful promises he made through his servant Moses is left unfulfilled!</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Deuteronomy 31:6; Deuteronomy 31:8"> May the Lord our God be with us, as he was with our ancestors. May he not abandon us or leave us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Psalms 119:36; Ezekiel 36:26–27; Jeremiah 31:33"> May he make us submissive, so we can follow all his instructions and obey the commandments, rules, and regulations he commanded our ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; John 17:9; John 17:20–24"> May the Lord our God be constantly aware of these requests of mine I have presented to him, so that he might vindicate his servant and his people Israel as the need arises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:35; Jeremiah 10:10–12; 1 Kings 18:39"> Then all the nations of the earth will recognize that the Lord is the only genuine God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="1 Kings 11:4; 2 Kings 20:3; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> May you demonstrate wholehearted devotion to the Lord our God by following his rules and obeying his commandments, as you are presently doing.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Solomon Dedicates the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:17–19; Ezra 6:16–17; 2 Chronicles 7:4–10"> The king and all Israel with him were presenting sacrifices to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Ezra 6:16–17; 2 Chronicles 15:11; Nehemiah 12:27"> Solomon offered as peace offerings to the Lord 22,000 cattle and 120,000 sheep. Then the king and all the Israelites dedicated the Lord’s temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:1; 2 Chronicles 7:7"> That day the king consecrated the middle of the courtyard that is in front of the Lord’s temple. He offered there burnt sacrifices, grain offerings, and the fat from the peace offerings, because the bronze altar that stood before the Lord was too small to hold all these offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Numbers 34:5; Numbers 34:8; 1 Kings 8:2"> At that time Solomon and all Israel with him celebrated a festival before the Lord our God for two entire weeks. This great assembly included people from all over the land, from Lebo Hamath in the north to the Brook of Egypt in the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Philippians 4:4; Acts 2:46; Zechariah 9:17"> On the fifteenth day after the festival started, he dismissed the people. They asked God to empower the king and then went to their homes, happy and content because of all the good the Lord had done for his servant David and his people Israel.</span>
