<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1–2; Acts 7:47; 1 Chronicles 29:19"> In the four hundred and eightieth year after the Israelites left Egypt, in the fourth year of Solomon’s reign over Israel, during the month Ziv (the second month), he began building the Lord’s temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:1–41; Ezekiel 41:1–15; Revelation 21:16–17"> The temple King Solomon built for the Lord was 90 feet long, 30 feet wide, and 45 feet high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:11; 2 Chronicles 3:3–4; Ezekiel 41:15"> The porch in front of the main hall of the temple was 30 feet long, corresponding to the width of the temple. It was 15 feet wide, extending out from the front of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 41:26; Ezekiel 41:16"> He made framed windows for the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:16; 1 Kings 6:19–21; 1 Kings 6:31"> He built an extension all around the walls of the temple’s main hall and holy place and constructed side rooms in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The bottom floor of the extension was seven and a half feet wide, the middle floor nine feet wide, and the third floor ten and a half feet wide. He made ledges on the temple’s outer walls so the beams would not have to be inserted into the walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:5–6; 1 Peter 2:5; Exodus 20:25"> As the temple was being built, only stones shaped at the quarry were used; the sound of hammers, pickaxes, or any other iron tool was not heard at the temple while it was being built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:6–7"> The entrance to the bottom level of side rooms was on the south side of the temple; stairs went up to the middle floor and then on up to the third floor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 6:14; 1 Kings 6:38"> He finished building the temple and covered it with rafters and boards made of cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He built an extension all around the temple; it was seven and a half feet high and it was attached to the temple by cedar beams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <br />The Lord said to Solomon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:10; Colossians 1:23; 1 Kings 3:14"> “As for this temple you are building, if you follow my rules, observe my regulations, and obey all my commandments, I will fulfill through you the promise I made to your father David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:6; Exodus 25:8; Leviticus 26:11"> I will live among the Israelites and will not abandon my people Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 6:9; 1 Kings 6:38; Acts 7:47–48"> So Solomon finished building the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 7:7"> He constructed the walls inside the temple with cedar planks; he paneled the inside with wood from the floor of the temple to the rafters of the ceiling. He covered the temple floor with boards made from the wood of evergreens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:8; 1 Kings 8:6; Hebrews 9:3"> He built a wall 30 feet in from the rear of the temple as a partition for an inner sanctuary that would be the most holy place. He paneled the wall with cedar planks from the floor to the rafters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The main hall in front of the inner sanctuary was 60 feet long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:24"> The inside of the temple was all cedar and was adorned with carvings of round ornaments and of flowers in bloom. Everything was cedar; no stones were visible.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 8:6–10; Psalms 28:2; 1 Kings 6:5"> He prepared the inner sanctuary inside the temple so that the ark of the covenant of the Lord could be placed there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 6:2–3; 1 Kings 6:22; 1 Kings 7:48"> The inner sanctuary was 30 feet long, 30 feet wide, and 30 feet high. He plated it with gold, as well as the cedar altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:7–9; 2 Chronicles 3:14–16; 1 Kings 6:5"> Solomon plated the inside of the temple with gold. He hung golden chains in front of the inner sanctuary and plated the inner sanctuary with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 30:1; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:7–14"> He plated the entire inside of the temple with gold, as well as the altar inside the inner sanctuary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 37:7–9; Genesis 3:24; 1 Peter 1:12"> In the inner sanctuary he made two cherubs of olive wood; each stood 15 feet high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Each of the first cherub’s wings was seven and a half feet long; its entire wingspan was 15 feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The second cherub also had a wingspan of 15 feet; it was identical to the first in measurements and shape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Each cherub stood 15 feet high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 37:9; Exodus 25:20; 2 Chronicles 5:8"> He put the cherubs in the inner sanctuary of the temple. Their wings were spread out. One of the first cherub’s wings touched one wall and one of the other cherub’s wings touched the opposite wall. The first cherub’s other wing touched the second cherub’s other wing in the middle of the room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> He plated the cherubs with gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 6:32; Psalms 92:12–15; 2 Chronicles 4:2–5"> On all the walls around the temple, inside and out, he carved cherubs, palm trees, and flowers in bloom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 21:18–21; Isaiah 60:17; Isaiah 54:11–12"> He plated the floor of the temple with gold, inside and out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–20; Ephesians 3:18; John 14:6"> He made doors of olive wood at the entrance to the inner sanctuary; the pillar on each doorpost was five-sided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 6:18; 1 Kings 6:29"> On the two doors made of olive wood he carved cherubs, palm trees, and flowers in bloom, and he plated them with gold. He plated the cherubs and the palm trees with hammered gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> In the same way he made doorposts of olive wood for the entrance to the main hall, only with four-sided pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:23–25; 1 Kings 5:8"> He also made two doors out of wood from evergreens; each door had two folding leaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> He carved cherubs, palm trees, and flowers in bloom and plated them with gold, leveled out over the carvings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 7:12; 2 Chronicles 7:7; Exodus 27:9–19"> He built the inner courtyard with three rows of chiseled stones and a row of cedar beams.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Kings 6:1; 2 Chronicles 3:2"> In the month Ziv of the fourth year of Solomon’s reign the foundation was laid for the Lord’s temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezra 6:14–15; 1 Kings 7:1; Zechariah 4:9"> In the eleventh year, in the month Bul (the eighth month) the temple was completed in accordance with all its specifications and blueprints. It took seven years to build.</span>
</div>
