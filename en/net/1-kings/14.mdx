<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; 1 Kings 13:33–34; 1 Samuel 31:2"> At that time Jeroboam’s son Abijah became sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:8; 1 Kings 11:29–38; 2 Samuel 14:2"> Jeroboam told his wife, “Disguise yourself so that people cannot recognize you are Jeroboam’s wife. Then go to Shiloh; Ahijah the prophet, who told me I would rule over this nation, lives there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:7–8; 1 Kings 13:7; 2 Kings 4:42"> Take ten loaves of bread, some small cakes, and a container of honey and visit him. He will tell you what will happen to the boy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 11:29; 1 Samuel 3:2; 1 Samuel 4:15"> Jeroboam’s wife did as she was told. She went to Shiloh and visited Ahijah. Now Ahijah could not see; he had lost his eyesight in his old age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 6:8–12; Proverbs 21:30; 2 Kings 4:27"> But the Lord had told Ahijah, “Look, Jeroboam’s wife is coming to find out from you what will happen to her son, for he is sick. Tell her so-and-so. When she comes, she will be in a disguise.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 5:17–28; Acts 5:9–10; Luke 20:20–23"> When Ahijah heard the sound of her footsteps as she came through the door, he said, “Come on in, wife of Jeroboam! Why are you pretending to be someone else? I have been commissioned to give you bad news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 16:2; 2 Samuel 12:7–8; 1 Samuel 15:16"> Go, tell Jeroboam, ‘This is what the Lord God of Israel says: “I raised you up from among the people and made you ruler over my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 15:5; 1 Kings 11:33–38; 2 Chronicles 28:1"> I tore the kingdom away from the Davidic dynasty and gave it to you. But you are not like my servant David, who kept my commandments and followed me wholeheartedly by doing only what I approve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:35; Nehemiah 9:26; Psalms 50:17"> You have sinned more than all who came before you. You went and angered me by making other gods, formed out of metal; you have completely disregarded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; 1 Kings 21:21; 2 Kings 14:26"> So I am ready to bring disaster on the dynasty of Jeroboam. I will cut off every last male belonging to Jeroboam in Israel, including even the weak and incapacitated. I will burn up the dynasty of Jeroboam, just as one burns manure until it is completely consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 16:4; Jeremiah 15:3; 1 Kings 21:23–24"> Dogs will eat the members of your family who die in the city, and the birds of the sky will eat the ones who die in the country.”’ Indeed, the Lord has announced it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 14:3; 1 Kings 14:16–17; 2 Kings 1:6"> “As for you, get up and go home. When you set foot in the city, the boy will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:3; 2 Chronicles 12:12; Job 19:28"> All Israel will mourn him and bury him. He is the only one in Jeroboam’s family who will receive a decent burial, for he is the only one in whom the Lord God of Israel found anything good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 15:27–29; Ezekiel 7:2–7; Ezekiel 12:22–28"> The Lord will raise up a king over Israel who will cut off Jeroboam’s dynasty. It is ready to happen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 23:15–16; Psalms 52:5; 2 Kings 15:29"> The Lord will attack Israel, making it like a reed that sways in the water. He will remove Israel from this good land he gave to their ancestors and scatter them beyond the Euphrates River, because they angered the Lord by making Asherah poles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 15:34; 1 Kings 16:2; 1 Kings 13:34"> He will hand Israel over to their enemies because of the sins which Jeroboam committed and which he made Israel commit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 15:33; 1 Kings 16:15; 1 Kings 16:23"> So Jeroboam’s wife got up and went back to Tirzah. As she crossed the threshold of the house, the boy died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 14:13"> All Israel buried him and mourned for him, just as the Lord had predicted through his servant the prophet Ahijah.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jeroboam’s Reign Ends

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:2–20; 1 Chronicles 27:24; 1 Kings 16:20"> The rest of the events of Jeroboam’s reign, including the details of his battles and rule, are recorded in the scroll called the Annals of the Kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:12; Psalms 3:5; 1 Kings 11:43"> Jeroboam ruled for twenty-two years; then he passed away. His son Nadab replaced him as king.</span>

## Rehoboam’s Reign over Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:13; 1 Kings 11:36; 1 Kings 14:31"> Now Rehoboam son of Solomon ruled in Judah. He was forty-one years old when he became king and he ruled for seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city the Lord chose from all the tribes of Israel to be his home. His mother was an Ammonite woman named Naamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:1; 1 Corinthians 10:22; Psalms 78:58"> Judah did evil in the sight of the Lord. They made him more jealous by their sins than their ancestors had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:2; Isaiah 57:5; 2 Kings 17:9–10"> They even built for themselves high places, sacred pillars, and Asherah poles on every high hill and under every green tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:17; 1 Corinthians 6:9; 1 Kings 15:12"> There were also male cultic prostitutes in the land. They committed the same horrible sins as the nations that the Lord had driven out from before the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 11:40; 2 Chronicles 12:2–4; 2 Chronicles 12:9"> In King Rehoboam’s fifth year, King Shishak of Egypt attacked Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 15:18; 2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 7:51"> He took away the treasures of the Lord’s temple and of the royal palace; he took everything, including all the golden shields that Solomon had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11; 1 Samuel 22:17; 1 Kings 18:46"> King Rehoboam made bronze shields to replace them and assigned them to the officers of the royal guard who protected the entrance to the royal palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:11"> Whenever the king visited the Lord’s temple, the royal guard carried them and then brought them back to the guardroom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 22:45; 1 Kings 15:23; 1 Kings 14:19"> The rest of the events of Rehoboam’s reign, including his accomplishments, are recorded in the scroll called the Annals of the Kings of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 12:24; 1 Kings 12:21; 1 Kings 15:6–7"> Rehoboam and Jeroboam were continually at war with each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:16; Matthew 1:7; 1 Kings 15:3"> Rehoboam passed away and was buried with his ancestors in the city of David. His mother was an Ammonite named Naamah. His son Abijah replaced him as king.</span>
</div>
