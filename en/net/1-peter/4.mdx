<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; 1 Peter 2:12; Jude 1:10"> So they are astonished when you do not rush with them into the same flood of wickedness, and they vilify you.</span>

## Service, Suffering, and Judgment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 5:8–9; 1 Peter 1:13; 1 Peter 5:8"> For the culmination of all things is near. So be self-controlled and sober-minded for the sake of prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:12; Colossians 3:14; Proverbs 17:9"> Above all keep your love for one another fervent, because love covers a multitude of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Romans 12:13; Philippians 2:14"> Show hospitality to one another without complaining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 4:1–2"> Just as each one has received a gift, use it to serve one another as good stewards of the varied grace of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:31; Ephesians 6:10; 1 Peter 4:10"> Whoever speaks, let it be with God’s words. Whoever serves, do so with the strength that God supplies, so that in everything God will be glorified through Jesus Christ. To him belong the glory and the power forever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:12; 1 Peter 1:6–7; 1 Peter 5:9"> Dear friends, do not be astonished that a trial by fire is occurring among you, as though something strange were happening to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 8:17; James 1:2–3; Romans 5:3"> But rejoice in the degree that you have shared in the sufferings of Christ, so that when his glory is revealed you may also rejoice and be glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; Luke 6:22; Matthew 5:11"> If you are insulted for the name of Christ, you are blessed, because the Spirit of glory, who is the Spirit of God, rests on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:11; 1 Timothy 5:13"> But let none of you suffer as a murderer or thief or criminal or as a troublemaker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17–18; Philippians 1:29; 1 Peter 4:19"> But if you suffer as a Christian, do not be ashamed, but glorify God that you bear such a name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:29; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Romans 2:9"> For it is time for judgment to begin, starting with the house of God. And if it starts with us, what will be the fate of those who are disobedient to the gospel of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:31; Ezekiel 18:24; Luke 23:31"> And if the righteous are barely saved, what will become of the ungodly and sinners?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 37:5; 2 Timothy 1:12; 1 Peter 3:17"> So then let those who suffer according to the will of God entrust their souls to a faithful Creator as they do good.</span>
</div>
