## Suffering for Doing Good

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 12:10; Ephesians 4:31–2; 1 Peter 1:22"> Finally, all of you be harmonious, sympathetic, affectionate, compassionate, and humble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; Romans 12:17; Romans 12:14"> Do not return evil for evil or insult for insult, but instead bless others because you were called to inherit a blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 34:12–16; James 1:26; 1 Peter 2:1"> For<br /><span className="pl pl-1">the one who wants to love life and see good days must keep his tongue from evil and his lips from uttering deceit.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Psalms 34:14; Hebrews 12:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he must turn away from evil and do good;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he must seek peace and pursue it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 34:15–16; John 9:31; Proverbs 15:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the eyes of the Lord are upon the righteous and his ears are open to their prayer.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But the Lord’s face is against those who do evil.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Romans 8:28; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> For who is going to harm you if you are devoted to what is good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; 1 Peter 2:19–20; Matthew 5:10–12"> But in fact, if you happen to suffer for doing what is right, you are blessed. But do not be terrified of them or be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; 2 Timothy 2:25–26; Luke 21:14–15"> But set Christ apart as Lord in your hearts and always be ready to give an answer to anyone who asks about the hope you possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Hebrews 13:18; 1 Peter 2:15"> Yet do it with courtesy and respect, keeping a good conscience, so that those who slander your good conduct in Christ may be put to shame when they accuse you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 4:19; Matthew 26:39"> For it is better to suffer for doing good, if God wills it, than for doing evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Hebrews 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because Christ also suffered once for sins,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the just for the unjust,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to bring you to God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">by being put to death in the flesh</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but by being made alive in the spirit.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 4:6; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 42:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">In it he went and preached to the spirits in prison,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5; Genesis 6:3"> after they were disobedient long ago when God patiently waited in the days of Noah as an ark was being constructed. In the ark a few, that is eight souls, were delivered through water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 16:16; Ezekiel 36:25–26; Acts 2:38"> And this prefigured baptism, which now saves you – not the washing off of physical dirt but the pledge of a good conscience to God – through the resurrection of Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 10:12"> who went into heaven and is at the right hand of God with angels and authorities and powers subject to him.</span>
