<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:3–4; Luke 24:48; Romans 8:17–18"> So as your fellow elder and a witness of Christ’s sufferings and as one who shares in the glory that will be revealed, I urge the elders among you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:7; 1 Timothy 3:8; Titus 3:1"> Give a shepherd’s care to God’s flock among you, exercising oversight not merely as a duty but willingly under God’s direction, not for shameful profit but eagerly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:9; Philippians 3:17; Ezekiel 34:4"> And do not lord it over those entrusted to you, but be examples to the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 1:12; 1 Corinthians 9:25; 1 John 3:2"> Then when the Chief Shepherd appears, you will receive the crown of glory that never fades away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 4:6; Proverbs 3:34; Ephesians 5:21"> In the same way, you who are younger, be subject to the elders. And all of you, clothe yourselves with humility toward one another, because God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:10; Luke 14:11; Proverbs 29:23"> And God will exalt you in due time, if you humble yourselves under his mighty hand</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 55:22; Philippians 4:6; Psalms 37:5"> by casting all your cares on him because he cares for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:11; James 4:7; Ephesians 4:27"> Be sober and alert. Your enemy the devil, like a roaring lion, is on the prowl looking for someone to devour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 4:7; Acts 14:22; Ephesians 6:11–13"> Resist him, strong in your faith, because you know that your brothers and sisters throughout the world are enduring the same kinds of suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Peter 1:6–7"> And, after you have suffered for a little while, the God of all grace who called you to his eternal glory in Christ will himself restore, confirm, strengthen, and establish you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 4:11; Romans 11:36; Revelation 1:6"> To him belongs the power forever. Amen.</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Acts 12:25; Psalms 87:4"> The church in Babylon, chosen together with you, greets you, and so does Mark, my son.</span>
