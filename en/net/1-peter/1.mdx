<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:13; Ephesians 1:4–5; Hebrews 12:24"> according to the foreknowledge of God the Father by being set apart by the Spirit for obedience and for sprinkling with Jesus Christ’s blood. May grace and peace be yours in full measure!</span>

## New Birth to Joy and Holiness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:4; Romans 15:13"> Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! By his great mercy he gave us new birth into a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Hebrews 9:15; 1 Peter 5:4"> that is, into an inheritance imperishable, undefiled, and unfading. It is reserved in heaven for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Ephesians 2:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:13–14"> who by God’s power are protected through faith for a salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 5:10; Romans 12:12"> This brings you great joy, although you may have to suffer for a short time in various trials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 23:10; Isaiah 48:10; 1 Peter 4:12"> Such trials show the proven character of your faith, which is much more valuable than gold – gold that is tested by fire, even though it is passing away – and will bring praise and glory and honor when Jesus Christ is revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 20:29; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Romans 15:13"> You have not seen him, but you love him. You do not see him now but you believe in him, and so you rejoice with an indescribable and glorious joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 6:22; James 1:21; Hebrews 11:13"> because you are attaining the goal of your faith – the salvation of your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:17; Luke 10:24; Luke 24:25–27"> Concerning this salvation, the prophets who predicted the grace that would come to you searched and investigated carefully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 52:13–14; 2 Peter 1:21; Luke 24:25–27"> They probed into what person or time the Spirit of Christ within them was indicating when he testified beforehand about the sufferings appointed for Christ and his subsequent glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 15:26; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Ephesians 3:10"> They were shown that they were serving not themselves but you, in regard to the things now announced to you through those who proclaimed the gospel to you by the Holy Spirit sent from heaven – things angels long to catch a glimpse of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Luke 12:35; 1 Peter 4:7"> Therefore, get your minds ready for action by being fully sober, and set your hope completely on the grace that will be brought to you when Jesus Christ is revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Titus 3:3–5; 1 Peter 4:2–3"> Like obedient children, do not comply with the evil urges you used to follow in your ignorance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:48; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Hebrews 12:14"> but, like the Holy One who called you, become holy yourselves in all of your conduct,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Leviticus 19:2; Leviticus 20:7"> for it is written, “You shall be holy, because I am holy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:34–35; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Matthew 6:9"> And if you address as Father the one who impartially judges according to each one’s work, live out the time of your temporary residence here in reverence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:20; Titus 2:14; Psalms 49:7–8"> You know that from your empty way of life inherited from your ancestors you were ransomed – not by perishable things like silver or gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 1:7; 1 Peter 2:22–24; John 1:29"> but by precious blood like that of an unblemished and spotless lamb, namely Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:9–10; Hebrews 9:26"> He was foreknown before the foundation of the world but was manifested in these last times for your sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Hebrews 2:9; John 12:44"> Through him you now trust in God, who raised him from the dead and gave him glory, so that your faith and hope are in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Hebrews 13:1; Romans 12:9–10"> You have purified your souls by obeying the truth in order to show sincere mutual love. So love one another earnestly from a pure heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; Hebrews 4:12; John 1:13"> You have been born anew, not from perishable but from imperishable seed, through the living and enduring word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; Psalms 103:15; James 4:14"> For<br /><span className="pl pl-1">all flesh is like grass</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and all its glory like the flower of the grass;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the grass withers and the flower falls off,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Matthew 5:18; Psalms 119:89"> <span className="pl pl-1">but the word of the Lord endures forever.</span>And this is the word that was proclaimed to you.</span>
</div>
</div>
