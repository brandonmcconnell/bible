## A Living Stone, a Chosen People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Acts 4:11–12; 1 Peter 2:7"> So as you come to him, a living stone rejected by men but chosen and priceless in God’s sight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Peter 2:9; 1 Corinthians 3:9"> you yourselves, as living stones, are built up as a spiritual house to be a holy priesthood and to offer spiritual sacrifices that are acceptable to God through Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Romans 9:32–33; 1 Peter 2:4"> For it says in scripture, “Look,I lay in Zion a stone, a chosen and priceless cornerstone, and whoever believes in him will never be put to shame&#42;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Mark 12:10–11; Acts 4:11–12"> So you who believe see his value, but for those who do not believe, the stone that the builders rejected has become the cornerstone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; 1 Corinthians 1:23; Romans 9:32–33"> and a stumbling-stone and a rock to trip over&#42;. They stumble because they disobey the word, as they were destined to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Deuteronomy 7:6; Revelation 1:6"> But you are a chosen race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people of his own, so that you may proclaim the virtues of the one who called you out of darkness into his marvelous light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; Hebrews 4:16; Hosea 1:9–10"> You once were not a people, but now you are God’s people. You were shown no mercy, but now you have received mercy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; Galatians 5:24; 1 Peter 4:2"> Dear friends, I urge you as foreigners and exiles to keep away from fleshly desires that do battle against the soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; Matthew 5:16; Titus 2:7–8"> and maintain good conduct among the non-Christians, so that though they now malign you as wrongdoers, they may see your good deeds and glorify God when he appears.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Submission to Authorities

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 3:1; Romans 13:1–7; 1 Timothy 2:1–2"> Be subject to every human institution for the Lord’s sake, whether to a king as supreme</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 13:3–4"> or to governors as those he commissions to punish wrongdoers and praise those who do good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17; Titus 2:8; Matthew 7:26"> For God wants you to silence the ignorance of foolish people by doing good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Romans 6:22; Romans 6:18"> Live as free people, not using your freedom as a pretext for evil, but as God’s slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; Romans 12:10; Romans 13:7"> Honor all people, love the family of believers, fear God, honor the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Titus 2:9–10; Ephesians 6:5–7; Colossians 3:22–25"> Slaves, be subject to your masters with all reverence, not only to those who are good and gentle, but also to those who are perverse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:10–12; 1 Peter 3:14–17; 1 Peter 2:20"> For this finds God’s favor, if because of conscience toward God someone endures hardships in suffering unjustly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:19; 1 Peter 3:17; Matthew 5:10–12"> For what credit is it if you sin and are mistreated and endure it? But if you do good and suffer and so endure, this finds favor with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 16:24; John 13:15"> For to this you were called, since Christ also suffered for you, leaving an example for you to follow in his steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 4:15"> He committed no sin nor was deceit found in his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 Peter 4:19; Hebrews 12:3"> When he was maligned, he did not answer back; when he suffered, he threatened no retaliation, but committed himself to God who judges justly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–6; Matthew 8:17; Romans 6:11"> He himself bore our sins in his body on the tree, that we may cease from sinning and live for righteousness. By his wounds you were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6; Ezekiel 34:6; Luke 15:4–6"> For you were going astray like sheep but now you have turned back to the shepherd and guardian of your souls.</span>
</div>
