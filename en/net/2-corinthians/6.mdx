## Unequal Partners

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 10:21; James 4:4"> Do not become partners with those who do not believe, for what partnership is there between righteousness and lawlessness, or what fellowship does light have with darkness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; 1 Corinthians 10:20–21; 1 John 5:11–13"> And what agreement does Christ have with Beliar? Or what does a believer share in common with an unbeliever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 21:3; Exodus 29:45; Hebrews 8:10"> And what mutual agreement does the temple of God have with idols? For we are the temple of the living God, just as God said, “I will live in them and will walk among them, and I will be their God, and they will be my people: &#42;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Revelation 18:4; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> Therefore “come out from their midst, and be separate,” says the Lord, “and touch no unclean thing, and I will welcome you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:7; John 1:12; 2 Samuel 7:14"> and I will be a father to you, and you will be my sons and daughters,” says the All-Powerful Lord.</span>
