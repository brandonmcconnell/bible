<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 16:9; 2 Corinthians 9:2; 2 Corinthians 9:4"> Now we make known to you, brothers and sisters, the grace of God given to the churches of Macedonia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 2:5; Proverbs 11:25; 2 Corinthians 6:10"> that during a severe ordeal of suffering, their abundant joy and their extreme poverty have overflowed in the wealth of their generosity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:5; Exodus 35:21–22"> For I testify, they gave according to their means and beyond their means. They did so voluntarily,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> begging us with great earnestness for the blessing and fellowship of helping the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 12:1; Romans 14:7–9; 2 Chronicles 30:12"> And they did this not just as we had hoped, but they gave themselves first to the Lord and to us by the will of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 2 Corinthians 8:4"> Thus we urged Titus that, just as he had previously begun this work, so also he should complete this act of kindness for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8; 2 Peter 1:5–8; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> But as you excel in everything – in faith, in speech, in knowledge, and in all eagerness and in the love from us that is in you – make sure that you excel in this act of kindness too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:6; 1 John 3:17–19; James 2:14–16"> I am not saying this as a command, but I am testing the genuineness of your love by comparison with the eagerness of others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Philippians 2:6–8; Matthew 20:28"> For you know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that although he was rich, he became poor for your sakes, so that you by his poverty could become rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18–19; Hebrews 13:16; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> So here is my opinion on this matter: It is to your advantage, since you made a good start last year both in your giving and your desire to give,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:2"> to finish what you started, so that just as you wanted to do it eagerly, you can also complete it according to your means.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 12:42–44; 2 Corinthians 9:7; Luke 21:1–4"> For if the eagerness is present, the gift itself is acceptable according to whatever one has, not according to what he does not have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 4:34; Romans 15:26–27"> For I do not say this so there would be relief for others and suffering for you, but as a matter of equality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:12; Acts 4:34"> At the present time, your abundance will meet their need, so that one day their abundance may also meet your need, and thus there may be equality,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:18; Luke 22:35"> as it is written: “The one who gathered much did not have too much, and the one who gathered little did not have too little.”</span>

## The Mission of Titus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Jeremiah 32:40; 2 Corinthians 2:13–14"> But thanks be to God who put in the heart of Titus the same devotion I have for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 13:22; 2 Corinthians 8:10"> because he not only accepted our request, but since he was very eager, he is coming to you of his own accord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> And we are sending along with him the brother who is praised by all the churches for his work in spreading the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:3–4; 2 Corinthians 4:15; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> In addition, this brother has also been chosen by the churches as our traveling companion as we administer this generous gift to the glory of the Lord himself and to show our readiness to help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:22; Romans 14:16; Ephesians 5:15"> We did this as a precaution so that no one should blame us in regard to this generous gift we are administering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Peter 2:12; Philippians 4:8"> For we areconcerned about what is right not only before the Lord but also before men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 2:20–22"> And we are sending with them our brother whom we have tested many times and found eager in many matters, but who now is much more eager than ever because of the great confidence he has in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Philemon 1:17; 2 Corinthians 8:6"> If there is any question about Titus, he is my partner and fellow worker among you; if there is any question about our brothers, they are messengers of the churches, a glory to Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:14; 2 Corinthians 7:4; 2 Corinthians 9:2–4"> Therefore show them openly before the churches the proof of your love and of our pride in you.</span>
