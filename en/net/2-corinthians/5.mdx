<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 8:23; 1 Corinthians 15:53–54; 2 Corinthians 5:3–4"> For in this earthly house we groan, because we desire to put on our heavenly dwelling,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:7–11; Revelation 16:15; Revelation 3:18"> if indeed, after we have put on our heavenly house, we will not be found naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:53–54; 2 Corinthians 5:2; Isaiah 25:8"> For we groan while we are in this tent, since we are weighed down, because we do not want to be unclothed, but clothed, so that what is mortal may be swallowed up by life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:22; Ephesians 1:13–14; Romans 8:23"> Now the one who prepared us for this very purpose is God, who gave us the Spirit as a down payment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:1; Philippians 3:20–21; Hebrews 11:13"> Therefore we are always full of courage, and we know that as long as we are alive here on earth we are absent from the Lord –</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:1–27; 2 Corinthians 4:18; Romans 8:24–25"> for we live by faith, not by sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:20–24; 2 Corinthians 5:6; John 14:3"> Thus we are full of courage and would prefer to be away from the body and at home with the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; 2 Peter 3:14; Romans 14:18"> So then whether we are alive or away, we make it our ambition to please him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Revelation 22:12; Ephesians 6:8"> For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, so that each one may be paid back according to what he has done while in the body, whether good or evil.</span>

## The Message of Reconciliation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jude 1:23; Hebrews 10:31; 2 Timothy 2:24–26"> Therefore, because we know the fear of the Lord, we try to persuade people, but we are well known to God, and I hope we are well known to your consciences too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:1; 2 Corinthians 1:14; Galatians 6:12–14"> We are not trying to commend ourselves to you again, but are giving you an opportunity to be proud of us, so that you may be able to answer those who take pride in outward appearance and not in what is in the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:16–17; 2 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Corinthians 12:6"> For if we are out of our minds, it is for God; if we are of sound mind, it is for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; Romans 14:7–9; Matthew 10:37–38"> For the love of Christ controls us, since we have concluded this, that Christ died for all; therefore all have died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 14:7–9; 1 Peter 4:2–4; 1 Peter 1:14–15"> And he died for all so that those who live should no longer live for themselves but for him who died for them and was raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Philippians 3:7–8; John 6:63"> So then from now on we acknowledge no one from an outward human point of view. Even though we have known Christ from such a human point of view, now we do not know him in that way any longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26; Ephesians 4:22–24; Isaiah 43:18–19"> So then, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation; what is old has passed away – look, what is new has come!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:20–21; 2 Corinthians 5:19–20; Romans 5:10–11"> And all these things are from God who reconciled us to himself through Christ, and who has given us the ministry of reconciliation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 44:22; 1 John 2:1–2"> In other words, in Christ God was reconciling the world to himself, not counting people’s trespasses against them, and he has given us the message of reconciliation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; Luke 14:23; Malachi 2:7"> Therefore we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God were making His plea through us. We plead with you on Christ’s behalf, “Be reconciled to God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 2:22–24; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 5:19"> God made the one who did not know sin to be sin for us, so that in him we would become the righteousness of God.</span>
