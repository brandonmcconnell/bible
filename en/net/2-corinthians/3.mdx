## The Greater Glory of the Spirit’s Ministry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:9–11; Romans 10:4; Exodus 31:18"> But if the ministry that produced death – carved in letters on stone tablets – came with glory, so that the Israelites could not keep their eyes fixed on the face of Moses because of the glory of his face (a glory which was made ineffective),</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:18; Galatians 3:14"> how much more glorious will the ministry of the Spirit be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 3:21–22; Hebrews 12:18–21; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For if there was glory in the ministry that produced condemnation, how much more does the ministry that produces righteousness excel in glory!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 1:17; Haggai 2:7–9; Acts 26:13"> For indeed, what had been glorious now has no glory because of the tremendously greater glory of what replaced it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 5:20–21; Hebrews 8:13; Hebrews 7:21–25"> For if what was made ineffective came with glory, how much more has what remains come in glory!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:4; Acts 4:13; Philippians 1:20"> Therefore, since we have such a hope, we behave with great boldness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 34:33–35; Hebrews 10:1–9; Ephesians 2:14–15"> and not like Moses who used to put a veil over his face to keep the Israelites from staring at the result of the glory that was made ineffective.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3–4; Acts 26:18; Romans 11:25"> But their minds were closed. For to this very day, the same veil remains when they hear the old covenant read. It has not been removed because only in Christ is it taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 13:27–29"> But until this very day whenever Moses is read, a veil lies over their minds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 34:34; Isaiah 29:18; Isaiah 25:7"> but when one turns to the Lord, the veil is removed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:2; Galatians 5:13; John 8:32"> Now the Lord is the Spirit, and where the Spirit of the Lord is present, there is freedom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:6; Ephesians 4:22–24; Romans 12:2"> And we all, with unveiled faces reflecting the glory of the Lord, are being transformed into the same image from one degree of glory to another, which is from the Lord, who is the Spirit.</span>
</div>
