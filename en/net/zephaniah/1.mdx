## The Lord’s Day of Judgment is Approaching

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 7:13; 2 Kings 22:16–17; Ezekiel 33:27–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I will destroy everything from the face of the earth,” says the Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Ezekiel 7:19; Jeremiah 12:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I will destroy people and animals;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will destroy the birds in the sky</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the fish in the sea.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">(The idolatrous images of these creatures will be destroyed along with evil people.)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will remove humanity from the face of the earth,” says the Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Micah 5:13; 2 Kings 23:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I will attack Judah</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and all who live in Jerusalem.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will remove from this place every trace of Baal worship,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as well as the very memory of the pagan priests.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:13; 1 Kings 11:33; 2 Kings 17:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will remove those who worship the stars in the sky from their rooftops,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">those who swear allegiance to the Lord while taking oaths in the name of their ‘king,’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; Jeremiah 2:13; Jeremiah 15:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">and those who turn their backs on the Lord</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and do not want the Lord’s help or guidance.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:20; Zechariah 2:13; Zephaniah 1:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be silent before the Lord God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the Lord’s day of judgment is almost here.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The Lord has prepared a sacrificial meal;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he has ritually purified his guests.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 24:21; Isaiah 3:18–24; Jeremiah 22:24–30"> <span className="pl pl-1">“On the day of the Lord’s sacrificial meal,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will punish the princes and the king’s sons,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and all who wear foreign styles of clothing.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:5; Amos 3:10; 1 Samuel 2:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">On that day I will punish all who leap over the threshold,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who fill the house of their master with wealth taken by violence and deceit.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:14; Amos 8:3; 2 Kings 22:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">On that day,” says the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“a loud cry will go up from the Fish Gate,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">wailing from the city’s newer district,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and a loud crash from the hills.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 5:1; Jeremiah 25:34; Hosea 12:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wail, you who live in the market district,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for all the merchants will disappear</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and those who count money will be removed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 6:1; Jeremiah 48:11; Ezekiel 8:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">At that time I will search through Jerusalem with lamps.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will punish the people who are entrenched in their sin,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">those who think to themselves,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The Lord neither rewards nor punishes.’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 5:11; Micah 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their wealth will be stolen</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their houses ruined!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will not live in the houses they have built,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor will they drink the wine from the vineyards they have planted.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Ezekiel 30:3; Zephaniah 1:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord’s great day of judgment is almost here;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it is approaching very rapidly!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There will be a bitter sound on the Lord’s day of judgment;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">at that time warriors will cry out in battle.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 2:2; Isaiah 22:5; Revelation 6:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">That day will be a day of God’s anger,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a day of distress and hardship,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a day of devastation and ruin,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a day of darkness and gloom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a day of clouds and dark skies,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Amos 3:6; Isaiah 32:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">a day of trumpet blasts and battle cries.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Judgment will fall on the fortified cities and the high corner towers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Psalms 83:10; Jeremiah 2:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will bring distress on the people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they will stumble like blind men,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for they have sinned against the Lord.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their blood will be poured out like dirt;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their flesh will be scattered like manure.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:8; Ezekiel 7:19; Proverbs 11:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Neither their silver nor their gold will be able to deliver them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the day of the Lord’s angry judgment.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The whole earth will be consumed by his fiery wrath.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Indeed, he will bring terrifying destruction on all who live on the earth.”</span></span>
</div>
