<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:7; 2 Corinthians 4:18; Romans 8:24–25"> Now faith is being sure of what we hope for, being convinced of what we do not see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39; Hebrews 11:4"> For by it the people of old received God’s commendation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 33:6; 2 Peter 3:5; John 1:3"> By faith we understand that the worlds were set in order at God’s command, so that the visible has its origin in the invisible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 12:24; Genesis 4:3–5; 1 John 3:11–12"> By faith Abel offered God a greater sacrifice than Cain, and through his faith he was commended as righteous, because God commended him for his offerings. And through his faith he still speaks, though he is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 5:21–24; Jude 1:14; Hebrews 11:6"> By faith Enoch was taken up so that he did not see death, and he was not to be found because God took him up. For before his removal he had been commended as having pleased God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 8:17; Jeremiah 29:13–14; Matthew 6:33"> Now without faith it is impossible to please him, for the one who approaches God must believe that he exists and that he rewards those who seek him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 6:13–1; 1 Peter 3:20; Ezekiel 14:14"> By faith Noah, when he was warned about things not yet seen, with reverent regard constructed an ark for the deliverance of his family. Through faith he condemned the world and became an heir of the righteousness that comes by faith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 12:1–4; Acts 7:2–4; Matthew 7:24–25"> By faith Abraham obeyed when he was called to go out to a place he would later receive as an inheritance, and he went out without understanding where he was going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 12:8; Genesis 13:18; Genesis 13:3"> By faith he lived as a foreigner in the promised land as though it were a foreign country, living in tents with Isaac and Jacob, who were fellow heirs of the same promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 13:14; Philippians 3:20; Hebrews 12:22"> For he was looking forward to the city with firm foundations, whose architect and builder is God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 4:20–21; Hebrews 10:23; Genesis 18:11–14"> By faith, even though Sarah herself was barren and he was too old, he received the ability to procreate, because he regarded the one who had given the promise to be trustworthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 32:12; Genesis 15:5"> So in fact children were fathered by one man – and this one as good as dead – like the number of stars in the sky and like the innumerable grains of sand on the seashore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 23:4; Psalms 39:12; Hebrews 11:27"> These all died in faith without receiving the things promised, but they saw them in the distance and welcomed them and acknowledged that they were strangers and foreigners on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:18–7; Hebrews 11:16; Hebrews 13:14"> For those who speak in such a way make it clear that they are seeking a homeland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 24:6–8; Genesis 12:10; Genesis 32:9–11"> In fact, if they had been thinking of the land that they had left, they would have had opportunity to return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 13:14; Hebrews 11:10; Philippians 3:20"> But as it is, they aspire to a better land, that is, a heavenly one. Therefore, God is not ashamed to be called their God, for he has prepared a city for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 22:16; Genesis 22:1–12; James 2:21–24"> By faith Abraham, when he was tested, offered up Isaac. He had received the promises, yet he was ready to offer up his only son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 21:12; Romans 9:7; Genesis 17:19"> God had told him, “Through Isaac descendants will carry on your name,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 4:17–21; Matthew 9:28; Ephesians 3:20"> and he reasoned that God could even raise him from the dead, and in a sense he received him back from there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 27:27–40; Genesis 28:2–3"> By faith also Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau concerning the future.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 47:31–1; Genesis 48:5–22"> By faith Jacob, as he was dying, blessed each of the sons of Joseph and worshiped as he leaned on his staff as he leaned on his staff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 13:19; Genesis 50:24–25; Joshua 24:32"> By faith Joseph, at the end of his life, mentioned the exodus of the sons of Israel and gave instructions about his burial.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Exodus 1:16; Hebrews 13:6"> By faith, when Moses was born, his parents hid him for three months, because they saw the child was beautiful and they were not afraid of the king’s edict.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 2:10–11; Acts 7:21–24"> By faith, when he grew up, Moses refused to be called the son of Pharaoh’s daughter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 84:10; 2 Timothy 2:3–10; Job 36:21"> choosing rather to be ill-treated with the people of God than to enjoy sin’s fleeting pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Hebrews 11:6; 1 Peter 4:14"> He regarded abuse suffered for Christ to be greater wealth than the treasures of Egypt, for his eyes were fixed on the reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:18; Hebrews 11:13; Hebrews 11:1"> By faith he left Egypt without fearing the king’s anger, for he persevered as though he could see the one who is invisible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 12:21–30; Hebrews 9:19; Hebrews 12:24"> By faith he kept the Passover and the sprinkling of the blood, so that the one who destroyed the firstborn would not touch them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 14:13–21; Psalms 106:9–11; Psalms 78:13"> By faith they crossed the Red Sea as if on dry ground, but when the Egyptians tried it, they were swallowed up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 6:3–20; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5"> By faith the walls of Jericho fell after the people marched around them for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 2:25; Joshua 6:22–25; Matthew 1:5"> By faith Rahab the prostitute escaped the destruction of the disobedient, because she welcomed the spies in peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:1; 1 Samuel 1:20; 1 Samuel 16:13"> And what more shall I say? For time will fail me if I tell of Gideon, Barak, Samson, Jephthah, of David and Samuel and the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 144:1–2; Hebrews 6:12–15; Hebrews 10:36"> Through faith they conquered kingdoms, administered justice, gained what was promised, shut the mouths of lions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Kings 6:16–18; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; 1 Samuel 17:51–52"> quenched raging fire, escaped the edge of the sword, gained strength in weakness, became mighty in battle, put foreign armies to flight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 4:27–37; 1 Kings 17:22–24; Philippians 3:11"> and women received back their dead raised to life. But others were tortured, not accepting release, to obtain resurrection to a better life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:2; Genesis 39:20; Jeremiah 37:15–21"> And others experienced mocking and flogging, and even chains and imprisonment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:21; Jeremiah 26:23; 1 Kings 19:10"> They were stoned, sawed apart, murdered with the sword; they went about in sheepskins and goatskins; they were destitute, afflicted, ill-treated</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 18:4; 1 Samuel 22:1; 1 Kings 19:9"> (the world was not worthy of them); they wandered in deserts and mountains and caves and openings in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 10:23–24; Hebrews 11:13; Hebrews 11:2"> And these all were commended for their faith, yet they did not receive what was promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 6:11; Hebrews 8:6; Hebrews 10:11–14"> For God had provided something better for us, so that they would be made perfect together with us.</span>
</div>
