<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12–14; John 19:17–18; Acts 7:58"> Therefore, to sanctify the people by his own blood, Jesus also suffered outside the camp.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:13; Galatians 6:10; Romans 12:13"> And do not neglect to do good and to share what you have, for God is pleased with such sacrifices.</span>
