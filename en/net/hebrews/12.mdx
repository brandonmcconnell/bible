<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Revelation 2:3"> Think of him who endured such opposition against himself by sinners, so that you may not grow weary in your souls and give up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Revelation 2:13; Hebrews 10:32–34"> You have not yet resisted to the point of bloodshed in your struggle against sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 3:11–12; Job 5:17–18; Revelation 3:19"> And have you forgotten the exhortation addressed to you as sons?<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“My son, do not scorn the Lord’s discipline</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or give up when he corrects you&#42;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 3:12; Psalms 119:75"> <span className="pl pl-1">“For the Lord disciplines the one he loves and chastises every son he accepts.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 12:6; 1 Peter 5:9–10; Psalms 73:1"> But if you do not experience discipline, something all sons have shared in, then you are illegitimate and are not sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Numbers 27:16; Isaiah 42:5"> Besides, we have experienced discipline from our earthly fathers and we respected them; shall we not submit ourselves all the more to the Father of spirits and receive life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 1:4; Leviticus 11:44–45; Titus 2:14"> For they disciplined us for a little while as seemed good to them, but he does so for our benefit, that we may share his holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 5:3–5; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Hebrews 12:5–6"> Now all discipline seems painful at the time, not joyful. But later it produces the fruit of peace and righteousness for those trained by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; Hebrews 12:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:14"> Therefore, strengthen your listless hands and your weak knees,</span>

## Do Not Reject God’s Warning

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Peter 1:15–16; 1 Thessalonians 4:7"> Pursue peace with everyone, and holiness, for without it no one will see the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:12; Deuteronomy 29:18; Galatians 5:4"> See to it that no one comes short of the grace of God, that no one be like a bitter root springing up and causing trouble, and through him many become defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 25:31–34; 1 Corinthians 6:15–20; Revelation 22:15"> And see to it that no one becomes an immoral or godless person like Esau, who sold his own birthright for a single meal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; Matthew 25:11–12; Matthew 7:23"> For you know that later when he wanted to inherit the blessing, he was rejected, for he found no opportunity for repentance, although he sought the blessing with tears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:11; Exodus 24:17; Exodus 20:18"> For you have not come to something that can be touched, to a burning fire and darkness and gloom and a whirlwind</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:16; 1 Corinthians 15:52; Deuteronomy 4:12"> and the blast of a trumpet and a voice uttering words such that those who heard begged to hear no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 19:12–13; Exodus 19:16; Galatians 3:10"> For they could not bear what was commanded: “If even an animal touches the mountain, it must be stoned.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 19:16; Deuteronomy 9:19; Isaiah 6:3–5"> In fact, the scene was so terrifying that Moses said, “I shudder with fear.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Galatians 4:26; Philippians 3:20; Revelation 14:1"> But you have come to Mount Zion, the city of the living God, the heavenly Jerusalem, and to myriads of angels, to the assembly</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 1:18; Hebrews 11:40; Luke 10:20"> and congregation of the firstborn, who are enrolled in heaven, and to God, the judge of all, and to the spirits of the righteous, who have been made perfect,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 9:15; Hebrews 11:28; Hebrews 11:4"> and to Jesus, the mediator of a new covenant, and to the sprinkled blood that speaks of something better than Abel’s does.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Deuteronomy 30:17; Hebrews 2:1–3"> Take care not to refuse the one who is speaking! For if they did not escape when they refused the one who warned them on earth, how much less shall we, if we reject the one who warns from heaven?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Haggai 2:6–7; Isaiah 13:13; Exodus 19:18"> Then his voice shook the earth, but now he has promised, “I will once more shake not only the earth but heaven too.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:31; Matthew 24:35; 2 Peter 3:10–11"> Now this phrase “once more” indicates the removal of what is shaken, that is, of created things, so that what is unshaken may remain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 12:1–2; Hebrews 13:15; Daniel 2:44"> So since we are receiving an unshakable kingdom, let us give thanks, and through this let us offer worship pleasing to God in devotion and awe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Deuteronomy 9:3; Hebrews 10:27"> For our God is indeed a devouring fire.</span>
</div>
