<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 12:15; Hebrews 4:9; Proverbs 28:14"> Therefore we must be wary that, while the promise of entering his rest remains open, none of you may seem to have come short of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; Romans 10:16–17; Hebrews 11:6"> For we had good news proclaimed to us just as they did. But the message they heard did them no good, since they did not join in with those who heard it in faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Matthew 11:28–29; 1 Peter 1:20"> For we who have believed enter that rest, as he has said, “As I swore in my anger, ‘They will never enter my rest!’” And yet God’s works were accomplished from the foundation of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Exodus 31:17; Genesis 2:1–2"> For he has spoken somewhere about the seventh day in this way: “And God rested on the seventh day from all his works,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Hebrews 4:3; Hebrews 3:11"> but to repeat the text cited earlier: “They will never enter my rest!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Hebrews 3:18–19; Acts 13:46–47"> Therefore it remains for some to enter it, yet those to whom it was previously proclaimed did not enter because of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:7; Hebrews 3:7–8; Hebrews 3:15"> So God again ordains a certain day, “Today,” speaking through David after so long a time, as in the words quoted before, “O, that today you would listen as he speaks! Do not harden your hearts.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:15; Joshua 22:4; Deuteronomy 12:9"> For if Joshua had given them rest, God would not have spoken afterward about another day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10; Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:14–17"> Consequently a Sabbath rest remains for the people of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Hebrews 4:3–4; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> For the one who enters God’s rest has also rested from his works, just as God did from his own works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:28–30; Luke 13:24; Hebrews 3:18–1"> Thus we must make every effort to enter that rest, so that no one may fall by following the same pattern of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:29; Isaiah 55:11; Ephesians 6:17"> For the word of God is living and active and sharper than any double-edged sword, piercing even to the point of dividing soul from spirit, and joints from marrow; it is able to judge the desires and thoughts of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:5; Psalms 33:13–15; Proverbs 15:3"> And no creature is hidden from God, but everything is naked and exposed to the eyes of him to whom we must render an account.</span>

## Jesus Our Compassionate High Priest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 9:12"> Therefore since we have a great high priest who has passed through the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us hold fast to our confession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17–18; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Isaiah 53:4–5"> For we do not have a high priest incapable of sympathizing with our weaknesses, but one who has been tempted in every way just as we are, yet without sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:19–23"> Therefore let us confidently approach the throne of grace to receive mercy and find grace whenever we need help.</span>
