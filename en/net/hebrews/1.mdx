## Introduction: God Has Spoken Fully and Finally in His Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 1:20–21; Joel 2:28; Numbers 12:6–8"> After God spoke long ago in various portions and in various ways to our ancestors through the prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Colossians 1:16–18"> in these last days he has spoken to us in a son, whom he appointed heir of all things, and through whom he created the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; Revelation 4:11; John 14:9–10"> The Son is the radiance of his glory and the representation of his essence, and he sustains all things by his powerful word, and so when he had accomplished cleansing for sins, he sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:21; Philippians 2:9–11; 1 Peter 3:22"> Thus he became so far better than the angels as he has inherited a name superior to theirs.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Son Is Superior to Angels

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; Acts 13:33; Hebrews 5:5"> For to which of the angels did God ever say, “You are my son! Today I have fathered you”? And in another place he says, “I will be his father and he will be my son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:7; Colossians 1:18; Deuteronomy 32:43"> But when he again brings his firstborn into the world, he says, “Let all the angels of God worship him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 104:4; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 6:17"> And he says of the angels, “He makes his angels spirits and his ministers a flame of fire,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 45:6–7; Isaiah 9:6–7; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> but of the Son he says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Your throne, O God, is forever and ever,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and a righteous scepter is the scepter of your kingdom.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 45:7; Psalms 23:5; Isaiah 61:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have loved righteousness and hated lawlessness.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So God, your God, has anointed you over your companions with the oil of rejoicing.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 102:25–27; Genesis 1:1; John 1:1–3"> And,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You founded the earth in the beginning, Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the heavens are the works of your hands.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Mark 13:31; Luke 21:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will perish, but you continue.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And they will all grow old like a garment,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 102:26–27; Hebrews 13:8; John 8:58"> <span className="pl pl-1">and like a robe you will fold them up</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and like a garment they will be changed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but you are the same and your years will never run out.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Mark 12:36; Matthew 22:44"> But to which of the angels has he ever said, “Sit at my right hand until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet”?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 34:7; Psalms 103:20–21; Psalms 91:11–12"> Are they not all ministering spirits, sent out to serve those who will inherit salvation?</span>
</div>
</div>
