## Exposition of Psalm 95: Hearing God’s Word in Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:7–11; Hebrews 3:15; Isaiah 55:3"> Therefore, as the Holy Spirit says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Oh, that today you would listen as he speaks!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; Exodus 17:7; Zechariah 7:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion, in the day of testing in the wilderness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Deuteronomy 4:9; Exodus 19:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">“There your fathers tested me and tried me, and they saw my works for forty years.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 95:10; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Psalms 67:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Therefore, I became provoked at that generation and said, ‘Their hearts are always wandering and they have not known my ways.’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:34–35; Hebrews 4:3; Numbers 32:10–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">“As I swore in my anger, ‘They will never enter my rest!’”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 17:9"> See to it, brothers and sisters, that none of you has an evil, unbelieving heart that forsakes the living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Hebrews 10:24–25; 2 Timothy 4:2"> But exhort one another each day, as long as it is called “Today,” that none of you may become hardened by sin’s deception.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 3:6; Hebrews 6:11; Hebrews 12:10"> For we have become partners with Christ, if in fact we hold our initial confidence firm until the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7–8; Psalms 95:7; Hebrews 10:38"> As it says, “O, that today you would listen as he speaks! Do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 14:30; Numbers 26:65"> For which ones heard and rebelled? Was it not all who came out of Egypt under Moses’ leadership?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:29; Numbers 26:64–65; Numbers 14:32–33"> And against whom was God provoked for forty years? Was it not those who sinned, whose dead bodies fell in the wilderness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Hebrews 4:6; Deuteronomy 1:34–35"> And to whom did he swear they would never enter into his rest, except those who were disobedient?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jude 1:5; John 3:36; Mark 16:16"> So we see that they could not enter because of unbelief.</span>
</div>
