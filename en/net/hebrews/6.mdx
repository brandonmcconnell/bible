<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 5:12–14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Hebrews 9:14"> Therefore we must progress beyond the elementary instructions about Christ and move on to maturity, not laying this foundation again: repentance from dead works and faith in God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:13–57; Acts 17:31–32; Romans 6:3–5"> teaching about baptisms, laying on of hands, resurrection of the dead, and eternal judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:21; 1 Corinthians 16:7; 1 Corinthians 4:19"> And this is what we intend to do, if God permits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–29; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Matthew 5:13"> For it is impossible in the case of those who have once been enlightened, tasted the heavenly gift, become partakers of the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:3; Luke 8:13; Matthew 13:20–21"> tasted the good word of God and the miracles of the coming age,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 2:21; Hebrews 10:29; Hebrews 6:4"> and then have committed apostasy, to renew them again to repentance, since they are crucifying the Son of God for themselves all over again and holding him up to contempt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Isaiah 44:3; Psalms 65:9–13"> For the ground that has soaked up the rain that frequently falls on it and yields useful vegetation for those who tend it receives a blessing from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; John 15:6; Luke 13:7–9"> But if it produces thorns and thistles, it is useless and about to be cursed; its fate is to be burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:39; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Hebrews 6:4–6"> But in your case, dear friends, even though we speak like this, we are convinced of better things relating to salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; Hebrews 13:16; Galatians 6:10"> For God is not unjust so as to forget your work and the love you have demonstrated for his name, in having served and continuing to serve the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–8; Hebrews 3:6; Romans 5:2–5"> But we passionately want each of you to demonstrate the same eagerness for the fulfillment of your hope until the end,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 10:36; 1 John 2:25"> so that you may not be sluggish, but imitators of those who through faith and perseverance inherit the promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 22:15–18; Luke 1:73; Jeremiah 22:5"> Now when God made his promise to Abraham, since he could swear by no one greater, he swore by himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Deuteronomy 1:10; Nehemiah 9:23"> saying, “Surely I will bless you greatly and multiply your descendants abundantly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 12:2–3; Romans 4:17–25; Exodus 1:7"> And so by persevering, Abraham inherited the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:11; Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 31:53"> For people swear by something greater than themselves, and the oath serves as a confirmation to end all dispute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 6:18; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 55:11"> In the same way God wanted to demonstrate more clearly to the heirs of the promise that his purpose was unchangeable, and so he intervened with an oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Titus 1:2; 1 Samuel 15:29"> so that we who have found refuge in him may find strong encouragement to hold fast to the hope set before us through two unchangeable things, since it is impossible for God to lie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 43:5; Romans 5:5–10; Hebrews 9:7"> We have this hope as an anchor for the soul, sure and steadfast, which reaches inside behind the curtain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14; Hebrews 3:1; Hebrews 5:6"> where Jesus our forerunner entered on our behalf, since he became a priest forever in the order of Melchizedek forever in the order of Melchizedek.</span>
