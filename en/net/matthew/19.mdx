## The Rich Young Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 10:17–30; John 3:15; Jude 1:21"> Now someone came up to him and said, “Teacher, what good thing must I do to gain eternal life?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Luke 10:26–28; Galatians 3:11–13"> He said to him, “Why do you ask me about what is good? There is only one who is good. But if you want to enter into life, keep the commandments.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:16–21; Exodus 20:12–17; Mark 10:19"> “Which ones?” he asked. Jesus replied, “Do not murder, do not commit adultery, do not steal, do not give false testimony,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; Exodus 20:12; Matthew 22:39"> honor your father and mother, and love your neighbor as yourself your neighbor as yourself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 3:24; Mark 10:20–21; Philippians 3:6"> The young man said to him, “I have wholeheartedly obeyed all these laws. What do I still lack?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 10:21; Luke 18:22; Luke 12:33"> Jesus said to him, “If you wish to be perfect, go sell your possessions and give the money to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven. Then come, follow me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; Ezekiel 33:31; Matthew 13:22"> But when the young man heard this he went away sorrowful, for he was very rich.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Matthew 13:22; Mark 10:23"> Then Jesus said to his disciples, “I tell you the truth, it will be hard for a rich person to enter the kingdom of heaven!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 5:44; Luke 18:25; Mark 10:24–25"> Again I say, it is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich person to enter into the kingdom of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 10:13; Matthew 24:22; Romans 11:5–7"> The disciples were greatly astonished when they heard this and said, “Then who can be saved?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:27; Luke 1:37; Job 42:2"> Jesus looked at them and replied, “This is impossible for mere humans, but for God all things are possible.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Philippians 3:8; Luke 18:28; Luke 5:11"> Then Peter said to him, “Look, we have left everything to follow you! What then will there be for us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 22:28–30; 1 Corinthians 6:2–3; Matthew 25:31"> Jesus said to them, “I tell you the truth: In the age when all things are renewed, when the Son of Man sits on his glorious throne, you who have followed me will also sit on twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 18:29–30; Mark 10:29–30; Philippians 3:8"> And whoever has left houses or brothers or sisters or father or mother or children or fields for my sake will receive a hundred times as much and will inherit eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 20:16; Luke 13:30; Mark 10:31"> But many who are first will be last, and the last first.</span>
</div>
