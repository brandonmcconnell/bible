## The Baptism of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 1:32–34; Matthew 2:22; Mark 1:9–11"> Then Jesus came from Galilee to John to be baptized by him in the Jordan River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 3:3–7; Acts 1:5–8; John 13:6–8"> But John tried to prevent him, saying, “I need to be baptized by you, and yet you come to me?”</span>
