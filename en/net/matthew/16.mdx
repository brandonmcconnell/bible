## The Yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 8:13–14; Matthew 15:39"> When the disciples went to the other side, they forgot to take bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:1; Galatians 5:9; Matthew 16:12"> “Watch out,” Jesus said to them, “beware of the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 8:16–18; Matthew 15:16–18; Acts 10:14"> So they began to discuss this among themselves, saying, “It is because we brought no bread.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:30; Matthew 14:31; Matthew 8:26"> When Jesus learned of this, he said, “You who have such little faith! Why are you arguing among yourselves about having no bread?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 14:17–21; John 6:9–13; Luke 9:13–17"> Do you still not understand? Don’t you remember the five loaves for the five thousand, and how many baskets you took up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 15:34–38; Mark 8:17–21; Mark 8:5–9"> Or the seven loaves for the four thousand and how many baskets you took up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 16:6; Luke 12:56; Mark 8:21"> How could you not understand that I was not speaking to you about bread? But beware of the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:20; Acts 23:8; Matthew 15:4–9"> Then they understood that he had not told them to be on guard against the yeast in bread, but against the teaching of the Pharisees and Sadducees.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Peter’s Confession

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:18–20; Mark 8:27–29; Daniel 7:13"> When Jesus came to the area of Caesarea Philippi, he asked his disciples, “Who do people say that the Son of Man is?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 6:15; Matthew 14:2; Malachi 4:5"> They answered, “Some say John the Baptist, others Elijah, and others Jeremiah or one of the prophets.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 9:20; Matthew 13:11; Mark 8:29"> He said to them, “But who do you say that I am?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; John 11:27; 1 John 4:15"> Simon Peter answered, “You are the Christ, the Son of the living God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 6:45; Ephesians 1:17–18; Matthew 11:25–27"> And Jesus answered him, “You are blessed, Simon son of Jonah, because flesh and blood did not reveal this to you, but my Father in heaven!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 2:19–22; 1 Corinthians 3:9–11; Isaiah 28:16"> And I tell you that you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church, and the gates of Hades will not overpower it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 18:18; Isaiah 22:22; Revelation 1:18"> I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven. Whatever you bind on earth will have been bound in heaven, and whatever you release on earth will have been released in heaven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 8:30; Luke 9:21; John 1:41"> Then he instructed his disciples not to tell anyone that he was the Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## First Prediction of Jesus’ Death and Resurrection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 24:6–7; Matthew 20:17–19; Matthew 17:22–23"> From that time on Jesus began to show his disciples that he must go to Jerusalem and suffer many things at the hands of the elders, chief priests, and experts in the law, and be killed, and on the third day be raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 8:32; John 13:6–8; Matthew 16:16–17"> So Peter took him aside and began to rebuke him: “God forbid, Lord! This must not happen to you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 8:5–8; Colossians 3:2; Philippians 3:19"> But he turned and said to Peter, “Get behind me, Satan! You are a stumbling block to me, because you are not setting your mind on God’s interests, but on man’s.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 14:27; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> Then Jesus said to his disciples, “If anyone wants to become my follower, he must deny himself, take up his cross, and follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 10:39; Mark 8:35; Revelation 12:11"> For whoever wants to save his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life for my sake will find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 8:36–37; Job 27:8; Luke 9:25"> For what does it benefit a person if he gains the whole world but forfeits his life? Or what can a person give in exchange for his life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Psalms 62:12; Romans 2:6"> For the Son of Man will come with his angels in the glory of his Father, and then he will reward each person according to what he has done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 9:27; Mark 9:1; John 8:52"> I tell you the truth, there are some standing here who will not experience death before they see the Son of Man coming in his kingdom.”</span>
</div>
