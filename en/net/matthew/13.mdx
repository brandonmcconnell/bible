<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:4–10; Matthew 13:1–15; Mark 4:1–12"> On that day after Jesus went out of the house, he sat by the lake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 5:3; Matthew 4:25; Mark 4:1"> And such a large crowd gathered around him that he got into a boat to sit while the whole crowd stood on the shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 4:2–9; Mark 4:13; Psalms 78:2"> He told them many things in parables, saying: “Listen! A sower went out to sow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:18–19"> And as he sowed, some seeds fell along the path, and the birds came and devoured them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Ezekiel 36:26; Zechariah 7:12"> Other seeds fell on rocky ground where they did not have much soil. They sprang up quickly because the soil was not deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 13:21; James 1:11–12; Colossians 1:23"> But when the sun came up, they were scorched, and because they did not have sufficient root, they withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 4:18–19; Jeremiah 4:3–4; Genesis 3:18"> Other seeds fell among the thorns, and they grew up and choked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 13:23; Luke 8:15; Genesis 26:12"> But other seeds fell on good soil and produced grain, some a hundred times as much, some sixty, and some thirty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 2:11; Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:7"> The one who has ears had better listen!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 4:33–34; Mark 4:10"> Then the disciples came to him and said, “Why do you speak to them in parables?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9–10; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 19:11"> He replied, “You have been given the opportunity to know the secrets of the kingdom of heaven, but they have not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:29; Mark 4:24–25; Luke 8:18"> For whoever has will be given more, and will have an abundance. But whoever does not have, even what he has will be taken from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:2; Jeremiah 5:21; Isaiah 42:18–20"> For this reason I speak to them in parables: Although they see they do not see, and although they hear they do not hear nor do they understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Ezekiel 12:2; 2 Corinthians 3:14"> And concerning them the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled that says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘You will listen carefully yet will never understand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you will look closely yet will never comprehend.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Mark 4:12; Zechariah 7:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the heart of this people has become dull;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they are hard of hearing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they have shut their eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so that they would not see with their eyes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and hear with their ears</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and understand with their hearts</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and turn, and I would heal them.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:23–24; Ephesians 1:17–18; Matthew 16:17"> “But your eyes are blessed because they see, and your ears because they hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:56; Hebrews 11:13; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> For I tell you the truth, many prophets and righteous people longed to see what you see but did not see it, and to hear what you hear but did not hear it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:11–12; Luke 8:11–15; Mark 4:13–20"> “So listen to the parable of the sower:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 8:11–15; Matthew 13:38; Hebrews 2:1"> When anyone hears the word about the kingdom and does not understand it, the evil one comes and snatches what was sown in his heart; this is the seed sown along the path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31–32; Psalms 106:12–13; John 5:35"> The seed sown on rocky ground is the person who hears the word and immediately receives it with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 2 Peter 1:8–9; Ephesians 3:17"> But he has no root in himself and does not endure; when trouble or persecution comes because of the word, immediately he falls away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Luke 8:14; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> The seed sown among thorns is the person who hears the word, but worldly cares and the seductiveness of wealth choke the word, so it produces nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 1:11; Matthew 12:33; John 15:16"> But as for the seed sown on good soil, this is the person who hears the word and understands. He bears fruit, yielding a hundred, sixty, or thirty times what was sown.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Weeds

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 25:1; Matthew 13:47; Matthew 20:1"> He presented them with another parable: “The kingdom of heaven is like a person who sowed good seed in his field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; 2 Peter 2:1; Acts 20:30–31"> But while everyone was sleeping, an enemy came and sowed weeds among the wheat and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 4:26–29"> When the plants sprouted and bore grain, then the weeds also appeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 Corinthians 6:1; 2 Corinthians 5:18–20"> So the slaves of the owner came and said to him, ‘Sir, didn’t you sow good seed in your field? Then where did the weeds come from?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:3–7; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; Luke 9:49–54"> He said, ‘An enemy has done this.’ So the slaves replied, ‘Do you want us to go and gather them?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> But he said, ‘No, since in gathering the weeds you may uproot the wheat with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 3:12; 1 Timothy 5:24; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> Let both grow together until the harvest. At harvest time I will tell the reapers, “First collect the weeds and tie them in bundles to be burned, but then gather the wheat into my barn.”’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Mustard Seed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 13:18–19; Matthew 17:20; Mark 4:30–32"> He gave them another parable: “The kingdom of heaven is like a mustard seed that a man took and sowed in his field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:6; Daniel 4:12; Ezekiel 17:23–24"> It is the smallest of all the seeds, but when it has grown it is the greatest garden plant and becomes a tree, so that the wild birds come and nest in its branches.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Explanation for the Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 16:17–20; Matthew 13:11; Matthew 9:28"> Then he left the crowds and went into the house. And his disciples came to him saying, “Explain to us the parable of the weeds in the field.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–7; Matthew 13:41; Luke 10:16"> He answered, “The one who sowed the good seed is the Son of Man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 8:44; Philippians 3:18–19; Acts 13:10"> The field is the world and the good seed are the people of the kingdom. The weeds are the people of the evil one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 13:49; Revelation 14:15–19; Matthew 25:31"> and the enemy who sows them is the devil. The harvest is the end of the age, and the reapers are angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 13:39"> As the weeds are collected and burned with fire, so it will be at the end of the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Matthew 18:7; 2 Peter 2:1–2"> The Son of Man will send his angels, and they will gather from his kingdom everything that causes sin as well as all lawbreakers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 13:50; Luke 16:23–24; Matthew 8:12"> They will throw them into the fiery furnace, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; Matthew 11:15; Luke 12:32"> Then the righteous will shine like the sun in the kingdom of their Father. The one who has ears had better listen!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Parables on the Kingdom of Heaven

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Proverbs 2:2–5; Colossians 2:3; Philippians 3:7–9"> “The kingdom of heaven is like a treasure, hidden in a field, that a person found and hid. Then because of joy he went and sold all that he had and bought that field.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 3:13–18; Matthew 16:26; Matthew 13:24"> “Again, the kingdom of heaven is like a merchant searching for fine pearls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 18:28–30; Proverbs 2:4"> When he found a pearl of great value, he went out and sold everything he had and bought it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 15:6; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Matthew 22:9–10"> “Again, the kingdom of heaven is like a net that was cast into the sea that caught all kinds of fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Matthew 13:40–43; Matthew 3:12"> When it was full, they pulled it ashore, sat down, and put the good fish into containers and threw the bad away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Revelation 20:12–15; Matthew 24:31; Matthew 13:39"> It will be this way at the end of the age. Angels will come and separate the evil from the righteous</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 13:42; Matthew 8:12; Revelation 14:10–11"> and throw them into the fiery furnace, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Matthew 13:11; Mark 4:34"> “Have you understood all these things?” They replied, “Yes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 John 2:7–8; 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Titus 1:9"> Then he said to them, “Therefore every expert in the law who has been trained for the kingdom of heaven is like the owner of a house who brings out of his treasure what is new and old.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Rejection at Nazareth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Mark 4:33–35"> Now when Jesus finished these parables, he moved on from there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; John 7:15–16; Mark 6:1–6"> Then he came to his hometown and began to teach the people in their synagogue. They were astonished and said, “Where did this man get such wisdom and miraculous powers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 6:42; Mark 6:3; Luke 3:23"> Isn’t this the carpenter’s son? Isn’t his mother named Mary? And aren’t his brothers James, Joseph, Simon, and Judas?</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And aren’t all his sisters here with us? Where did he get all this?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 4:44; Matthew 11:6; Luke 4:24"> And so they took offense at him. But Jesus said to them, “A prophet is not without honor except in his hometown and in his own house.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Mark 6:5–6; Romans 11:20; Hebrews 3:12–19"> And he did not do many miracles there because of their unbelief.</span>
