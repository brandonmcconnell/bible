<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Acts 6:11–13; Deuteronomy 19:16–21; Mark 14:55–56"> The chief priests and the whole Sanhedrin were trying to find false testimony against Jesus so that they could put him to death.</span>
