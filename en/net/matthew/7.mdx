## Ask, Seek, Knock

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; 1 John 3:22; John 15:7"> “Ask and it will be given to you; seek and you will find; knock and the door will be opened for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 81:10; Psalms 81:16; Luke 23:42–43"> For everyone who asks receives, and the one who seeks finds, and to the one who knocks, the door will be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13"> Is there anyone among you who, if his son asks for bread, will give him a stone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Or if he asks for a fish, will give him a snake?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13; James 1:17; Romans 8:32"> If you then, although you are evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father in heaven give good gifts to those who ask him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:31; Romans 13:8–10; Galatians 5:13–14"> In everything, treat others as you would want them to treat you, for this fulfills the law and the prophets.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Narrow Gate

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 13:24–25; Proverbs 16:25; John 14:6"> “Enter through the narrow gate, because the gate is wide and the way is spacious that leads to destruction, and there are many who enter through it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 13:23–30; Matthew 22:14; Matthew 16:24–25"> But the gate is narrow and the way is difficult that leads to life, and there are few who find it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Tree and Its Fruit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 4:1; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Matthew 24:11"> “Watch out for false prophets, who come to you in sheep’s clothing but inwardly are voracious wolves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:43–45; Matthew 12:33; James 3:12"> You will recognize them by their fruit. Grapes are not gathered from thorns or figs from thistles, are they?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 12:33–35; Galatians 5:22–24; Ephesians 5:9"> In the same way, every good tree bears good fruit, but the bad tree bears bad fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; 1 John 3:9–10"> A good tree is not able to bear bad fruit, nor a bad tree to bear good fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 3:10; Hebrews 6:8; John 15:2–6"> Every tree that does not bear good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 7:16; Acts 5:38"> So then, you will recognize them by their fruit.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Judgment of Pretenders

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:13; Luke 6:46; James 1:22"> “Not everyone who says to me, ‘Lord, Lord,’ will enter into the kingdom of heaven – only the one who does the will of my Father in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 13:25–27; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Malachi 3:17–18"> On that day, many will say to me, ‘Lord, Lord, didn’t we prophesy in your name, and in your name cast out demons and do many powerful deeds?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Psalms 6:8; Luke 13:25"> Then I will declare to them, ‘I never knew you. Go away from me, you lawbreakers!’</span>
</div>
</div>

## Hearing and Doing

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 6:47–49; John 13:17; 1 Corinthians 3:10–11"> “Everyone who hears these words of mine and does them is like a wise man who built his house on rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 1:12; Psalms 125:1–2; 1 Peter 1:7"> The rain fell, the flood came, and the winds beat against that house, but it did not collapse because it had been founded on rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:9; Luke 6:49; James 2:20"> Everyone who hears these words of mine and does not do them is like a foolish man who built his house on sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 13:19–22; 2 Peter 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> The rain fell, the flood came, and the winds beat against that house, and it collapsed; it was utterly destroyed!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 4:32; Mark 1:22; John 7:46"> When Jesus finished saying these things, the crowds were amazed by his teaching,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12–13; Luke 20:8; Deuteronomy 18:18–19"> because he taught them like one who had authority, not like their experts in the law.</span>
</div>
