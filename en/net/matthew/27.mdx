## Judas’ Suicide

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; Luke 22:47–48; Job 20:15–29"> Now when Judas, who had betrayed him, saw that Jesus had been condemned, he regretted what he had done and returned the thirty silver coins to the chief priests and the elders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:30; Titus 1:16; Acts 18:15–17"> saying, “I have sinned by betraying innocent blood!” But they said, “What is that to us? You take care of it yourself!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 1:18–19; 2 Samuel 17:23; 1 Kings 16:18"> So Judas threw the silver coins into the temple and left. Then he went out and hanged himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:7–9; Deuteronomy 23:18; Isaiah 61:8"> The chief priests took the silver and said, “It is not lawful to put this into the temple treasury, since it is blood money.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> After consulting together they bought the Potter’s Field with it, as a burial place for foreigners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:19; Matthew 28:15; Joshua 4:9"> For this reason that field has been called the “Field of Blood” to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 1:22; Leviticus 27:2–7"> Then what was spoken by Jeremiah the prophet was fulfilled: “They took the thirty silver coins, the price of the one whose price had been set by the people of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 11:13"> and they gave them for the potter’s field, as the Lord commanded me.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus and Pilate

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 23:2–3; Mark 15:2–5; Matthew 26:25"> Then Jesus stood before the governor, and the governor asked him, “Are you the king of the Jews?” Jesus said, “You say so.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:63; Isaiah 53:7; Matthew 27:14"> But when he was accused by the chief priests and the elders, he did not respond.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:62; John 18:35; Acts 22:24"> Then Pilate said to him, “Don’t you hear how many charges they are bringing against you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; Psalms 71:7; 1 Corinthians 4:9"> But he did not answer even one accusation, so that the governor was quite amazed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:38–40; Luke 23:16; John 19:16"> During the feast the governor was accustomed to release one prisoner to the crowd, whomever they wanted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 15:7; Acts 3:14; Luke 23:25"> At that time they had in custody a notorious prisoner named Jesus Barabbas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 19:15; 1 Kings 18:21; Mark 15:9–12"> So after they had assembled, Pilate said to them, “Whom do you want me to release for you, Jesus Barabbas or Jesus who is called the Christ?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Acts 5:17; Mark 15:10"> (For he knew that they had handed him over because of envy.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Proverbs 29:1; Matthew 27:24"> As he was sitting on the judgment seat, his wife sent a message to him: “Have nothing to do with that innocent man; I have suffered greatly as a result of a dream about him today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:14–15; John 19:15–16; John 18:40"> But the chief priests and the elders persuaded the crowds to ask for Barabbas and to have Jesus killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The governor asked them, “Which of the two do you want me to release for you?” And they said, “Barabbas!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–3; Isaiah 49:7; Acts 13:38"> Pilate said to them, “Then what should I do with Jesus who is called the Christ?” They all said, “Crucify him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:18–19; Acts 7:57; Acts 23:12–15"> He asked, “Why? What wrong has he done?” But they shouted more insistently, “Crucify him!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus is Condemned and Mocked

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:4; Psalms 26:6; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> When Pilate saw that he could do nothing, but that instead a riot was starting, he took some water, washed his hands before the crowd and said, “I am innocent of this man’s blood. You take care of it yourselves!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 2:19; Acts 5:28; Acts 7:52"> In reply all the people said, “Let his blood be on us and on our children!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; John 19:1; Mark 15:15"> Then he released Barabbas for them. But after he had Jesus flogged, he handed him over to be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 18:28; John 18:33; Acts 10:1"> Then the governor’s soldiers took Jesus into the governor’s residence and gathered the whole cohort around him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 15:17; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> They stripped him and put a scarlet robe around him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 20:19; Psalms 69:19–20"> and after braiding a crown of thorns, they put it on his head. They put a staff in his right hand, and kneeling down before him, they mocked him: “Hail, king of the Jews!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 53:7"> They spat on him and took the staff and struck him repeatedly on the head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 Kings 21:13; 1 Kings 21:10"> When they had mocked him, they stripped him of the robe and put his own clothes back on him. Then they led him away to crucify him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Crucifixion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:21; Matthew 16:24; Luke 23:26"> As they were going out, they found a man from Cyrene named Simon, whom they forced to carry his cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 19:17–19; Mark 15:22–32; Mark 15:34"> They came to a place called Golgotha (which means “Place of the Skull”)</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Mark 15:23; Matthew 27:48"> and offered Jesus wine mixed with gall to drink. But after tasting it, he would not drink it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 22:18; Mark 15:24–32; Luke 23:34"> When they had crucified him, they divided his clothes by throwing dice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:44; Mark 15:39"> Then they sat down and kept guard over him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 23:38; John 19:19–22; Mark 15:26"> Above his head they put the charge against him, which read: “This is Jesus, the king of the Jews.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43; Isaiah 53:12"> Then two outlaws were crucified with him, one on his right and one on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 109:25; Lamentations 1:12; Psalms 69:7–12"> Those who passed by defamed him, shaking their heads</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 26:61; Matthew 4:6; Matthew 4:3"> and saying, “You who can destroy the temple and rebuild it in three days, save yourself! If you are God’s Son, come down from the cross!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Isaiah 28:22; Psalms 22:12–13"> In the same way even the chief priests – together with the experts in the law and elders – were mocking him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 1:49; Matthew 27:37; Acts 4:14"> “He saved others, but he cannot save himself! He is the king of Israel! If he comes down now from the cross, we will believe in him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 22:8; John 10:36; Isaiah 37:10"> He trusts in God – let God, if he wants to, deliver him now because he said, ‘I am God’s Son’!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:38; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:39–43"> The robbers who were crucified with him also spoke abusively to him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus’ Death

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Amos 8:9; Mark 15:33–34; Luke 23:44–45"> Now from noon until three, darkness came over all the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 53:10"> At about three o’clock Jesus shouted with a loud voice, “Eli, Eli, lema sabachthani? ” that is, “My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Mark 15:35–36; Matthew 11:14"> When some of the bystanders heard it, they said, “This man is calling for Elijah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36"> Immediately one of them ran and got a sponge, filled it with sour wine, put it on a stick, and gave it to him to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> But the rest said, “Leave him alone! Let’s see if Elijah will come to save him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:30; Isaiah 53:9–12; Psalms 22:14–15"> Then Jesus cried out again with a loud voice and gave up his spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Mark 15:38; Hebrews 6:19"> Just then the temple curtain was torn in two, from top to bottom. The earth shook and the rocks were split apart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; 1 Thessalonians 4:14; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> And tombs were opened, and the bodies of many saints who had died were raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Isaiah 48:2; Revelation 21:2"> (They came out of the tombs after his resurrection and went into the holy city and appeared to many people.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:36; Mark 15:39; Matthew 27:43"> Now when the centurion and those with him who were guarding Jesus saw the earthquake and what took place, they were extremely terrified and said, “Truly this one was God’s Son!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Luke 23:48–49; John 19:25–27"> Many women who had followed Jesus from Galilee and given him support were also there, watching from a distance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 19:25; Mark 15:47–1; Matthew 28:1"> Among them were Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James and Joseph, and the mother of the sons of Zebedee.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus’ Burial

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Mark 15:42–47; Luke 23:50–56"> Now when it was evening, there came a rich man from Arimathea, named Joseph, who was also a disciple of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 23:52–53; Mark 15:44–46"> He went to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus. Then Pilate ordered that it be given to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> Joseph took the body, wrapped it in a clean linen cloth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 28:2; Matthew 27:66"> and placed it in his own new tomb that he had cut in the rock. Then he rolled a great stone across the entrance of the tomb and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Matthew 28:1; Matthew 27:56"> (Now Mary Magdalene and the other Mary were sitting there, opposite the tomb.)</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Guard at the Tomb

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 15:42; John 19:14; John 19:42"> The next day (which is after the day of preparation) the chief priests and the Pharisees assembled before Pilate</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 9:22; Matthew 16:21; John 2:19"> and said, “Sir, we remember that while that deceiver was still alive he said, ‘After three days I will rise again.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Matthew 28:13; Matthew 12:45"> So give orders to secure the tomb until the third day. Otherwise his disciples may come and steal his body and say to the people, ‘He has been raised from the dead,’ and the last deception will be worse than the first.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Matthew 27:66; Matthew 28:11–15; Proverbs 21:30"> Pilate said to them, “Take a guard of soldiers. Go and make it as secure as you can.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Daniel 6:17; Matthew 27:60; 2 Timothy 2:19"> So they went with the soldiers of the guard and made the tomb secure by sealing the stone.</span>
</div>
