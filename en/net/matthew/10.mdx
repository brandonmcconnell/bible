<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 3:6; Luke 10:9; Acts 4:30"> Heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse lepers, cast out demons. Freely you received, freely give.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Luke 9:3–5; Luke 10:4–12"> Do not take gold, silver, or copper in your belts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 10:7–12; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:4–14"> no bag for the journey, or an extra tunic, or sandals or staff, for the worker deserves his provisions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 10:7–8; Job 31:32; Judges 19:16–21"> Whenever you enter a town or village, find out who is worthy there and stay with them until you leave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:6; Luke 10:5–6; Acts 10:36"> As you enter the house, give it greetings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 10:6; Psalms 35:13; 2 Corinthians 2:16"> And if the house is worthy, let your peace come on it, but if it is not worthy, let your peace return to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 10:10–11; Acts 18:6; Nehemiah 5:13"> And if anyone will not welcome you or listen to your message, shake the dust off your feet as you leave that house or that town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6; 1 John 4:17; John 15:22–24"> I tell you the truth, it will be more bearable for the region of Sodom and Gomorrah on the day of judgment than for that town!</span>
</div>

## Persecution of Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:3; Philippians 2:15; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> “I am sending you out like sheep surrounded by wolves, so be wise as serpents and innocent as doves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 13:9; Matthew 23:34; Acts 26:11"> Beware of people, because they will hand you over to councils and flog you in their synagogues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:25–27; Matthew 8:4; Psalms 2:1–6"> And you will be brought before governors and kings because of me, as a witness to them and the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 4:12; Mark 13:11–13; 2 Timothy 4:17"> Whenever they hand you over for trial, do not worry about how to speak or what to say, for what you should say will be given to you at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10; Luke 12:12"> For it is not you speaking, but the Spirit of your Father speaking through you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 21:16–17; Micah 7:5–6; Mark 13:12–13"> “Brother will hand over brother to death, and a father his child. Children will rise against parents and have them put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 24:13; James 1:12; Revelation 2:10"> And you will be hated by everyone because of my name. But the one who endures to the end will be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:28; Acts 8:1; Mark 13:26"> Whenever they persecute you in one place, flee to another. I tell you the truth, you will not finish going through all the towns of Israel before the Son of Man comes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 15:20; Luke 6:40; John 13:16"> “A disciple is not greater than his teacher, nor a slave greater than his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:15; Matthew 12:24; Matthew 9:34"> It is enough for the disciple to become like his teacher, and the slave like his master. If they have called the head of the house ‘Beelzebul,’ how much more will they defame the members of his household!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Fear God, Not Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 8:17; Mark 4:22; Jeremiah 1:8"> “Do not be afraid of them, for nothing is hidden that will not be revealed, and nothing is secret that will not be made known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 5:20; John 16:13; John 16:1"> What I say to you in the dark, tell in the light, and what is whispered in your ear, proclaim from the housetops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:4–5; Isaiah 8:12–13; Revelation 2:10"> Do not be afraid of those who kill the body but cannot kill the soul. Instead, fear the one who is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 12:6–7; Psalms 104:27–30"> Aren’t two sparrows sold for a penny? Yet not one of them falls to the ground apart from your Father’s will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 12:7; 1 Samuel 14:45; Acts 27:34"> Even all the hairs on your head are numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 12:24; Psalms 8:5; Matthew 6:26"> So do not be afraid; you are more valuable than many sparrows.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 3:5; Romans 10:9–10; Luke 12:8–9"> “Whoever, then, acknowledges me before people, I will acknowledge before my Father in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:12; Luke 9:26; Luke 12:9"> But whoever denies me before people, I will deny him also before my Father in heaven.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Not Peace, but a Sword

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 12:49–53; Acts 14:4; Acts 13:45–50"> “Do not think that I have come to bring peace to the earth. I have not come to bring peace but a sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Matthew 10:21; Luke 21:16"> For I have come to set a man against his father, a daughter against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Micah 7:6; Jeremiah 12:6; Psalms 41:9"> and a man’s enemies will be the members of his household.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 14:26; Matthew 22:37; Philippians 3:7–9"> “Whoever loves father or mother more than me is not worthy of me, and whoever loves son or daughter more than me is not worthy of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 14:27; Mark 8:34; Matthew 16:24"> And whoever does not take up his cross and follow me is not worthy of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 12:25; Mark 8:35–36; Luke 17:33"> Whoever finds his life will lose it, and whoever loses his life because of me will find it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Rewards

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 13:20; Luke 9:48; Luke 10:16"> “Whoever receives you receives me, and whoever receives me receives the one who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="3 John 1:5–8; Hebrews 6:10; 2 Kings 4:16–17"> Whoever receives a prophet in the name of a prophet will receive a prophet’s reward. Whoever receives a righteous person in the name of a righteous person will receive a righteous person’s reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10; Matthew 25:40; Mark 9:41–42"> And whoever gives only a cup of cold water to one of these little ones in the name of a disciple, I tell you the truth, he will never lose his reward.”</span>
</div>
