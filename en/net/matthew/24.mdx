## Signs of the End of the Age

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 13:3–4; Acts 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–11"> As he was sitting on the Mount of Olives, his disciples came to him privately and said, “Tell us, when will these things happen? And what will be the sign of your coming and of the end of the age?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Jeremiah 29:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:3"> Jesus answered them, “Watch out that no one misleads you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 24:11; Matthew 24:24; Jeremiah 14:14"> For many will come in my name, saying, ‘I am the Christ,’ and they will mislead many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:7–8; Luke 21:9; 1 Peter 3:14–15"> You will hear of wars and rumors of wars. Make sure that you are not alarmed, for this must happen, but the end is still to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 21:11; Revelation 6:8; Isaiah 19:2"> For nation will rise up in arms against nation, and kingdom against kingdom. And there will be famines and earthquakes in various places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Deuteronomy 28:59"> All these things are the beginning of birth pains.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Persecution of Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 16:2; John 15:19–20; Luke 21:16–17"> “Then they will hand you over to be persecuted and will kill you. You will be hated by all the nations because of my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Luke 21:16; Matthew 11:6"> Then many will be led into sin, and they will betray one another and hate one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; Matthew 7:15; Matthew 24:5"> And many false prophets will appear and deceive many,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 2:4–5; James 4:1–4; Revelation 3:15"> and because lawlessness will increase so much, the love of many will grow cold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 10:22; Mark 13:13; Revelation 2:10"> But the person who endures to the end will be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 14:6; Romans 10:18; Romans 16:25–26"> And this gospel of the kingdom will be preached throughout the whole inhabited earth as a testimony to all the nations, and then the end will come.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Abomination of Desolation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 9:27; Daniel 12:11; Mark 13:14"> “So when you see the abomination of desolation – spoken about by Daniel the prophet – standing in the holy place (let the reader understand),</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 19:15–17; Luke 21:21–22; Hebrews 11:7"> then those in Judea must flee to the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 17:31–33; Mark 13:15–16; Matthew 10:27"> The one on the roof must not come down to take anything out of his house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and the one in the field must not turn back to get his cloak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 23:29–30; Deuteronomy 28:53–56; Luke 21:23"> Woe to those who are pregnant and to those who are nursing their babies in those days!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:29; Acts 1:12"> Pray that your flight may not be in winter or on a Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Joel 2:2; Malachi 4:1"> For then there will be great suffering unlike anything that has happened from the beginning of the world until now, or ever will happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 13:20; 2 Timothy 2:10; Matthew 24:31"> And if those days had not been cut short, no one would be saved. But for the sake of the elect those days will be cut short.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Luke 17:23–24; Deuteronomy 13:1–3"> Then if anyone says to you, ‘Look, here is the Christ!’ or ‘There he is!’ do not believe him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Mark 13:22; Revelation 13:13–14"> For false messiahs and false prophets will appear and perform great signs and wonders to deceive, if possible, even the elect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:1; Luke 21:13; Isaiah 48:5–6"> Remember, I have told you ahead of time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 3:2–3; Isaiah 40:3; Matthew 3:1"> So then, if someone says to you, ‘Look, he is in the wilderness,’ do not go out, or ‘Look, he is in the inner rooms,’ do not believe him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 17:24–37; Zechariah 9:14; Job 37:3"> For just like the lightning comes from the east and flashes to the west, so the coming of the Son of Man will be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 17:37; Job 39:27–30; Deuteronomy 28:49"> Wherever the corpse is, there the vultures will gather.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Arrival of the Son of Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Amos 8:9; Joel 3:15; Isaiah 13:10"> “Immediately after the suffering of those days, the sun will be darkened, and the moon will not give its light; the stars will fall from heaven, and the powers of heaven will be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Daniel 7:13; Luke 21:27"> Then the sign of the Son of Man will appear in heaven, and all the tribes of the earth will mourn. They will see the Son of Man arriving on the clouds of heaven with power and great glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16; Mark 13:27; Isaiah 27:13"> And he will send his angels with a loud trumpet blast, and they will gather his elect from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Fig Tree

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 13:28–29; Luke 21:29–30"> “Learn this parable from the fig tree: Whenever its branch becomes tender and puts out its leaves, you know that summer is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 5:9; Hebrews 10:37; Ezekiel 7:2–14"> So also you, when you see all these things, know that he is near, right at the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 13:30–31; Luke 21:32–33; Matthew 16:28"> I tell you the truth, this generation will not pass away until all these things take place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; 1 Peter 1:25; Numbers 23:19"> Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will never pass away.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Be Ready!

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Acts 1:7; Matthew 24:44"> “But as for that day and hour no one knows it – not even the angels in heaven – except the Father alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:1–7; 2 Peter 2:5"> For just like the days of Noah were, so the coming of the Son of Man will be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 17:26–28; Romans 13:13–14; Luke 14:18–20"> For in those days before the flood, people were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day Noah entered the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:20; Matthew 24:37; Acts 13:41"> And they knew nothing until the flood came and took them all away. It will be the same at the coming of the Son of Man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 17:34–37; 2 Peter 2:7–9; 2 Peter 2:5"> Then there will be two men in the field; one will be taken and one left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 11:5; Isaiah 47:2; Luke 17:35"> There will be two women grinding grain with a mill; one will be taken and one left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 21:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:6; Matthew 25:13"> “Therefore stay alert, because you do not know on what day your Lord will come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 12:39; Matthew 24:44; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–6"> But understand this: If the owner of the house had known at what time of night the thief was coming, he would have been alert and would not have let his house be broken into.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 25:10; Matthew 24:42; Matthew 25:13"> Therefore you also must be ready, because the Son of Man will come at an hour when you do not expect him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Faithful and Wise Slave

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Matthew 25:23; 1 Corinthians 4:1–2"> “Who then is the faithful and wise slave, whom the master has put in charge of his household, to give the other slaves their food at the proper time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; Luke 12:43; Luke 12:37"> Blessed is that slave whom the master finds at work when he comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 25:23; Matthew 25:21; Luke 12:37"> I tell you the truth, the master will put him in charge of all his possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 18:32; Isaiah 32:6; Luke 12:45"> But if that evil slave should say to himself, ‘My master is staying away a long time,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Revelation 17:6; Revelation 16:6; Micah 3:5"> and he begins to beat his fellow slaves and to eat and drink with drunkards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Revelation 3:3; Matthew 24:42–44"> then the master of that slave will come on a day when he does not expect him and at an hour he does not foresee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 22:13; Luke 12:46"> and will cut him in two, and assign him a place with the hypocrites, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.</span>
</div>
