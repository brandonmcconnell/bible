## The Parable of the Unforgiving Slave

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:24; Romans 14:12; Matthew 3:2"> “For this reason, the kingdom of heaven is like a king who wanted to settle accounts with his slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 38:4; Psalms 130:3–4; Luke 16:5"> As he began settling his accounts, a man who owed ten thousand talents was brought to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 4:1; Nehemiah 5:5; Luke 7:42"> Because he was not able to repay it, the lord ordered him to be sold, along with his wife, children, and whatever he possessed, and repayment to be made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 18:29; Luke 7:43; Matthew 8:2"> Then the slave threw himself to the ground before him, saying, ‘Be patient with me, and I will repay you everything.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 145:8; Nehemiah 9:17; Psalms 86:15"> The lord had compassion on that slave and released him, and forgave him the debt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 20:2; Ezekiel 45:9; Deuteronomy 15:2"> After he went out, that same slave found one of his fellow slaves who owed him one hundred silver coins. So he grabbed him by the throat and started to choke him, saying, ‘Pay back what you owe me!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 18:26; Matthew 6:12; Philemon 1:18–19"> Then his fellow slave threw himself down and begged him, ‘Be patient with me, and I will repay you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 22:27; 1 Kings 21:27–29"> But he refused. Instead, he went out and threw him in prison until he repaid the debt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Hebrews 13:3; Mark 3:5"> When his fellow slaves saw what had happened, they were very upset and went and told their lord everything that had taken place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 19:22; Matthew 25:26; Romans 3:19"> Then his lord called the first slave and said to him, ‘Evil slave! I forgave you all that debt because you begged me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Colossians 3:13; Matthew 6:12; Ephesians 4:32–2"> Should you not have shown mercy to your fellow slave, just as I showed it to you?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:25–26; James 2:13; Luke 12:58–59"> And in anger his lord turned him over to the prison guards to torture him until he repaid all he owed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; James 2:13; Luke 6:37–38"> So also my heavenly Father will do to you, if each of you does not forgive your brother from your heart.”</span>
