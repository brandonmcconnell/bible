## Healing the Centurion’s Servant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:1–10; Acts 27:43; Acts 10:1–33"> When he entered Capernaum, a centurion came to him asking for help:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 4:1; Matthew 4:24; Acts 9:33"> “Lord, my servant is lying at home paralyzed, in terrible anguish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 9:18–19; Luke 7:6; Mark 5:23–24"> Jesus said to him, “I will come and heal him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Luke 7:6–7; Psalms 33:9"> But the centurion replied, “Lord, I am not worthy to have you come under my roof. Instead, just say the word and my servant will be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 4:35–36; Titus 2:9; Colossians 3:22"> For I too am a man under authority, with soldiers under me. I say to this one, ‘Go’ and he goes, and to another ‘Come’ and he comes, and to my slave ‘Do this’ and he does it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Luke 7:9; Mark 6:6"> When Jesus heard this he was amazed and said to those who followed him, “I tell you the truth, I have not found such faith in anyone in Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 13:28–29; Ephesians 3:6; Galatians 3:28–29"> I tell you, many will come from the east and west to share the banquet with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:30; Luke 13:28; Matthew 21:43"> but the sons of the kingdom will be thrown out into the outer darkness, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:29–30; Mark 9:23; John 4:50"> Then Jesus said to the centurion, “Go; just as you believed, it will be done for you.” And the servant was healed at that hour.</span>
</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 4:42–43; Luke 8:22; Mark 4:35"> Now when Jesus saw a large crowd around him, he gave orders to go to the other side of the lake.</span>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 8:22–25; Mark 4:36–41; Matthew 9:1"> As he got into the boat, his disciples followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 8:23; Psalms 107:23–27; Mark 4:37–38"> And a great storm developed on the sea so that the waves began to swamp the boat. But he was asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jonah 1:6; 2 Chronicles 14:11; Mark 4:38–39"> So they came and woke him up saying, “Lord, save us! We are about to die!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10–14; Psalms 65:7; Psalms 107:28–30"> But he said to them, “Why are you cowardly, you people of little faith?” Then he got up and rebuked the winds and the sea, and it was dead calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 7:37; Mark 6:51; Matthew 14:33"> And the men were amazed and said, “What sort of person is this? Even the winds and the sea obey him!”</span>

## Healing the Gadarene Demoniacs

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 8:26–39; Mark 5:1–20; Acts 10:38"> When he came to the other side, to the region of the Gadarenes, two demon-possessed men coming from the tombs met him. They were extremely violent, so that no one was able to pass by that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 1:24; Luke 4:34; James 2:19"> They cried out, “Son of God, leave us alone! Have you come here to torment us before the time?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:8; Leviticus 11:7; Mark 5:11"> A large herd of pigs was feeding some distance from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 20:1–2; Luke 8:30–33; Mark 5:12"> Then the demons begged him, “If you drive us out, send us into the herd of pigs.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 5:13; Acts 2:23; Revelation 20:7"> And he said, “Go!” So they came out and went into the pigs, and the herd rushed down the steep slope into the lake and drowned in the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:34–36; Mark 5:14–16; Acts 19:15–17"> The herdsmen ran off, went into the town, and told everything that had happened to the demon-possessed men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 16:39; Luke 8:37–39; Luke 5:8"> Then the entire town came out to meet Jesus. And when they saw him, they begged him to leave their region.</span>
