<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:6; Joshua 20:7; 1 Chronicles 12:38"> Rehoboam traveled to Shechem, for all Israel had gathered in Shechem to make Rehoboam king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 11:40; 1 Kings 12:2; 1 Kings 11:28"> When Jeroboam son of Nebat heard the news, he was still in Egypt, where he had fled from King Solomon. Jeroboam returned from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 12:3"> They sent for him and Jeroboam and all Israel came and spoke to Rehoboam, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 12:4; Exodus 2:23; Matthew 11:29–30"> “Your father made us work too hard! Now if you lighten the demands he made and don’t make us work as hard, we will serve you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 12:5; Proverbs 3:28"> He said to them, “Go away for three days, then return to me.” So the people went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 32:7; Proverbs 19:20; Proverbs 27:10"> King Rehoboam consulted with the older advisers who had served his father Solomon when he had been alive. He asked them, “How do you advise me to answer these people?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Genesis 49:21; 1 Kings 12:7"> They said to him, “If you are fair to these people, grant their request, and are cordial to them, they will be your servants from this time forward.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:14; Proverbs 1:25; Ecclesiastes 10:16"> But Rehoboam rejected their advice and consulted the young advisers who served him, with whom he had grown up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 10:6; 2 Samuel 17:5–6; 1 Kings 22:6–8"> He asked them, “How do you advise me to respond to these people who said to me, ‘Lessen the demands your father placed on us’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 29:23; Proverbs 13:16; 2 Samuel 17:7–13"> The young advisers with whom Rehoboam had grown up said to him, “Say this to these people who have said to you, ‘Your father made us work hard, but now lighten our burden’ – say this to them: ‘I am a lot harsher than my father!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; Isaiah 47:6; Revelation 9:10"> My father imposed heavy demands on you; I will make them even heavier. My father punished you with ordinary whips; I will punish you with whips that really sting your flesh.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 12:12–15; 2 Chronicles 10:5"> Jeroboam and all the people reported to Rehoboam on the third day, just as the king had ordered when he said, “Return to me on the third day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 10:8; Proverbs 19:27; 1 Samuel 25:10–11"> The king responded to the people harshly. He rejected the advice of the older men</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 12:5; Ecclesiastes 10:16"> and followed the advice of the younger ones. He said, “My father imposed heavy demands on you; I will make them even heavier. My father punished you with ordinary whips; I will punish you with whips that really sting your flesh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 11:29–39; 1 Samuel 2:25; 2 Chronicles 25:16–20"> The king refused to listen to the people, because God was instigating this turn of events so that he might bring to pass the prophetic announcement he had made through Ahijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam son of Nebat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:1; 2 Chronicles 10:19; Psalms 76:10"> When all Israel saw that the king refused to listen to them, the people answered the king, “We have no portion in David – no share in the son of Jesse! Return to your homes, O Israel! Now, look after your own dynasty, O David!” So all Israel returned to their homes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:1; 1 Kings 12:17; 1 Kings 11:36"> (Rehoboam continued to rule over the Israelites who lived in the cities of Judah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 5:14; 1 Kings 4:6; Acts 7:57–58"> King Rehoboam sent Hadoram, the supervisor of the work crews, out after them, but the Israelites stoned him to death. King Rehoboam managed to jump into his chariot and escape to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 12:19–20; Ezra 9:7; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> So Israel has been in rebellion against the Davidic dynasty to this very day.</span>
