<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 15:24; Ephesians 6:10; Matthew 1:8"> His son Jehoshaphat replaced him as king and solidified his rule over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:8; 2 Chronicles 11:5; 2 Chronicles 11:11–12"> He placed troops in all of Judah’s fortified cities and posted garrisons throughout the land of Judah and in the cities of Ephraim that his father Asa had seized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 22:2; Isaiah 41:10; 2 Timothy 4:22"> The Lord was with Jehoshaphat because he followed in his ancestor David’s footsteps at the beginning of his reign. He did not seek the Baals,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; Luke 1:6; 1 Thessalonians 2:12"> but instead sought the God of his ancestors and obeyed his commands, unlike the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:1; Matthew 6:33; Psalms 127:1"> The Lord made his kingdom secure; all Judah brought tribute to Jehoshaphat, and he became very wealthy and greatly respected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 14:9; 2 Chronicles 15:17; 2 Chronicles 20:33"> He was committed to following the Lord; he even removed the high places and Asherah poles from Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:3; Nehemiah 8:7–8; Ecclesiastes 12:9–10"> In the third year of his reign he sent his officials Ben-Hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah to teach in the cities of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 7:1–6; 2 Chronicles 19:8; Malachi 2:7"> They were accompanied by the Levites Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jehonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah, and Tob-Adonijah, and by the priests Elishama and Jehoram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 5:46; Romans 3:2; Acts 8:40"> They taught throughout Judah, taking with them the scroll of the law of the Lord. They traveled to all the cities of Judah and taught the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:14; Exodus 15:14–16; Proverbs 16:7"> The Lord put fear into all the kingdoms surrounding Judah; they did not make war with Jehoshaphat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:8; 2 Chronicles 9:14; 2 Samuel 8:2"> Some of the Philistines brought Jehoshaphat tribute, including a load of silver. The Arabs brought him 7,700 rams and 7,700 goats from their flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:2–6; 2 Chronicles 14:6–7; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> Jehoshaphat’s power kept increasing. He built fortresses and storage cities throughout Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:25–31; 2 Chronicles 26:10–15"> He had many supplies stored in the cities of Judah and an army of skilled warriors stationed in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:1; Genesis 15:5; Numbers 1:18"> These were their divisions by families: There were a thousand officers from Judah. Adnah the commander led 300,000 skilled warriors,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Jehochanan the commander led 280,000,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 5:9; Judges 5:2; 1 Chronicles 29:9"> and Amasiah son of Zikri, who volunteered to serve the Lord, led 200,000 skilled warriors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:8; 2 Samuel 1:21–22"> From Benjamin, Eliada, a skilled warrior, led 200,000 men who were equipped with bows and shields,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and Jehozabad led 180,000 trained warriors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:2; 2 Chronicles 11:12; 2 Chronicles 11:23"> These were the ones who served the king, besides those whom the king placed in the fortified cities throughout Judah.</span>
