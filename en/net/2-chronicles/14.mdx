## Asa’s Religious and Military Accomplishments

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:11; 2 Chronicles 31:20; 1 Kings 15:14"> Asa did what the Lord his God desired and approved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:5; Exodus 34:13; 2 Kings 23:14"> He removed the pagan altars and the high places, smashed the sacred pillars, and cut down the Asherah poles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:19; Psalms 119:10; Psalms 101:2–8"> He ordered Judah to seek the Lord God of their ancestors and to observe his law and commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:4; 2 Chronicles 34:7"> He removed the high places and the incense altars from all the cities of Judah. The kingdom had rest under his rule.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:15; 1 Chronicles 22:9; Job 34:29"> He built fortified cities throughout Judah, for the land was at rest and there was no war during those years; the Lord gave him peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; 2 Chronicles 14:6; Jeremiah 29:12–14"> He said to the people of Judah: “Let’s build these cities and fortify them with walls, towers, and barred gates. The land remains ours because we have followed the Lord our God and he has made us secure on all sides.” So they built the cities and prospered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:3; 2 Chronicles 17:14–19; 2 Chronicles 11:1"> Asa had an army of 300,000 men from Judah, equipped with large shields and spears. He also had 280,000 men from Benjamin who carried small shields and were adept archers; they were all skilled warriors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:8; 2 Chronicles 12:2–3; 2 Chronicles 11:8"> Zerah the Cushite marched against them with an army of 1,000,000 men and 300 chariots. He arrived at Mareshah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 1:17; Joshua 19:4"> and Asa went out to oppose him. They deployed for battle in the Valley of Zephathah near Mareshah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:19; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Psalms 22:5"> Asa prayed to the Lord his God: “O Lord, there is no one but you who can help the weak when they are vastly outnumbered. Help us, O Lord our God, for we rely on you and have marched on your behalf against this huge army. O Lord our God, don’t let men prevail against you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:15; Psalms 136:17–18; Deuteronomy 28:7"> The Lord struck down the Cushites before Asa and Judah. The Cushites fled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:19; Genesis 26:1; Genesis 20:1"> and Asa and his army chased them as far as Gerar. The Cushites were wiped out; they were shattered before the Lord and his army. The men of Judah carried off a huge amount of plunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:10; Genesis 35:5; 2 Chronicles 20:29"> They defeated all the cities surrounding Gerar, for the Lord caused them to panic. The men of Judah looted all the cities, for they contained a huge amount of goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:30–47; Numbers 31:9; 1 Samuel 30:20"> They also attacked the tents of the herdsmen in charge of the livestock. They carried off many sheep and camels and then returned to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
