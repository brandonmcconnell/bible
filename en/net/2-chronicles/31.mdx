## The People Contribute to the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:1; 1 Chronicles 16:4–6; 2 Chronicles 29:24–26"> Hezekiah appointed the divisions of the priests and Levites to do their assigned tasks – to offer burnt sacrifices and present offerings and to serve, give thanks, and offer praise in the gates of the Lord’s sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:17; 2 Chronicles 35:7; Colossians 2:16–17"> The king contributed some of what he owned for burnt sacrifices, including the morning and evening burnt sacrifices and the burnt sacrifices made on Sabbaths, new moon festivals, and at other appointed times prescribed in the law of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Malachi 2:7; Numbers 18:8–21; Nehemiah 13:10–13"> He ordered the people living in Jerusalem to contribute the portion prescribed for the priests and Levites so they might be obedient to the law of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:12; 2 Chronicles 24:10–11; Nehemiah 10:35–39"> When the edict was issued, the Israelites freely contributed the initial portion of their grain, wine, olive oil, honey, and all the produce of their fields. They brought a tenth of everything, which added up to a huge amount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 27:30; 2 Chronicles 11:16–17; Deuteronomy 14:28"> The Israelites and people of Judah who lived in the cities of Judah also contributed a tenth of their cattle and sheep, as well as a tenth of the holy items consecrated to the Lord their God. They brought them and placed them in many heaps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 23:16–24"> In the third month they began piling their contributions in heaps and finished in the seventh month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 1:3; Genesis 14:19–20; Philippians 4:10"> When Hezekiah and the officials came and saw the heaps, they praised the Lord and pronounced blessings on his people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> When Hezekiah asked the priests and Levites about the heaps,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 3:10; 1 Chronicles 6:8; 2 Corinthians 9:8–11"> Azariah, the head priest from the family of Zadok, said to him, “Since the contributions began arriving in the Lord’s temple, we have had plenty to eat and have a large quantity left over. For the Lord has blessed his people, and this large amount remains.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:38–39; Nehemiah 13:5; Nehemiah 13:12–13"> Hezekiah ordered that storerooms be prepared in the Lord’s temple. When this was done,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:9; 2 Kings 12:15; 1 Chronicles 26:20–26"> they brought in the contributions, tithes, and consecrated items that had been offered. Konaniah, a Levite, was in charge of all this, assisted by his brother Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:5; 2 Chronicles 31:10–11; 1 Chronicles 9:11"> Jehiel, Azaziah, Nahath, Asahel, Jerimoth, Jozabad, Eliel, Ismakiah, Mahath, and Benaiah worked under the supervision of Konaniah and his brother Shimei, as directed by King Hezekiah and Azariah, the supervisor of God’s temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 23:38; 1 Chronicles 26:12; Leviticus 27:28"> Kore son of Imnah, a Levite and the guard on the east side, was in charge of the voluntary offerings made to God and disbursed the contributions made to the Lord and the consecrated items.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 21:9–19; 1 Chronicles 9:22; 2 Chronicles 29:12"> In the cities of the priests, Eden, Miniamin, Jeshua, Shemaiah, Amariah, and Shecaniah faithfully assisted him in making disbursements to their fellow priests according to their divisions, regardless of age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezra 3:4; Leviticus 21:22–23; 1 Chronicles 23:3"> They made disbursements to all the males three years old and up who were listed in the genealogical records – to all who would enter the Lord’s temple to serve on a daily basis and fulfill their duties as assigned to their divisions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; 1 Chronicles 23:27; Numbers 3:15"> They made disbursements to the priests listed in the genealogical records by their families, and to the Levites twenty years old and up, according to their duties as assigned to their divisions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 15:16; Isaiah 5:16; 2 Chronicles 31:15"> and to all the infants, wives, sons, and daughters of the entire assembly listed in the genealogical records, for they faithfully consecrated themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:12–15; Leviticus 25:34; Numbers 35:2–5"> As for the descendants of Aaron, the priests who lived in the outskirts of all their cities, men were assigned to disburse portions to every male among the priests and to every Levite listed in the genealogical records.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 20:3; 2 Kings 22:2; Acts 24:16"> This is what Hezekiah did throughout Judah. He did what the Lord his God considered good and right and faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Jeremiah 29:13; Joshua 1:7–8"> He wholeheartedly and successfully reinstituted service in God’s temple and obedience to the law, in order to follow his God.</span>
</div>
