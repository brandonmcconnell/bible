<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 9:10–28"> After twenty years, during which Solomon built the Lord’s temple and his royal palace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 9:11–18"> Solomon rebuilt the cities that Huram had given him and settled Israelites there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; 1 Chronicles 18:3; 2 Samuel 8:3"> Solomon went to Hamath Zobah and seized it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 9:17–19"> He built up Tadmor in the wilderness and all the storage cities he had built in Hamath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:24; Joshua 16:5; 2 Chronicles 14:7"> He made upper Beth Horon and lower Beth Horon fortified cities with walls and barred gates,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2; 2 Chronicles 8:4; 2 Chronicles 1:14"> and built up Baalath, all the storage cities that belonged to him, and all the cities where chariots and horses were kept. He built whatever he wanted in Jerusalem, Lebanon, and throughout his entire kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 15:18–21; Deuteronomy 7:1; 1 Kings 9:20–22"> Now several non-Israelite peoples were left in the land after the conquest of Joshua, including the Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; 1 Kings 9:21; 1 Kings 4:6"> Their descendants remained in the land (the Israelites were unable to wipe them out). Solomon conscripted them for his work crews and they continue in that role to this very day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11–12; Exodus 19:5–6; Galatians 4:31"> Solomon did not assign Israelites to these work crews; the Israelites served as his soldiers, officers, charioteers, and commanders of his chariot forces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 9:23; 1 Kings 5:16; 2 Chronicles 2:18"> These men worked for Solomon as supervisors; there were a total of 250 of them who were in charge of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 3:1; 1 Kings 7:8; 1 Kings 9:24"> Solomon moved Pharaoh’s daughter up from the City of David to the palace he had built for her, for he said, “My wife must not live in the palace of King David of Israel, for the places where the ark of the Lord has entered are holy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:1; 2 Chronicles 15:8; John 10:23"> Then Solomon offered burnt sacrifices to the Lord on the altar of the Lord which he had built in front of the temple’s porch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 29:38–42; Ezekiel 46:3–15"> He observed the daily requirements for sacrifices that Moses had specified for Sabbaths, new moon festivals, and the three annual celebrations – the Feast of Unleavened Bread, the Feast of Weeks, and the Feast of Temporary Shelters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:17; Ezra 6:18; Nehemiah 12:36"> As his father David had decreed, Solomon appointed the divisions of the priests to do their assigned tasks, the Levitical orders to lead worship and help the priests with their daily tasks, and the divisions of the gatekeepers to serve at their assigned gates. This was what David the man of God had ordered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:12; 1 Chronicles 9:29; 1 Kings 7:51"> They did not neglect any detail of the king’s orders pertaining to the priests, Levites, and treasuries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 5:18; 1 Kings 6:7"> All the work ordered by Solomon was completed, from the day the foundation of the Lord’s temple was laid until it was finished; the Lord’s temple was completed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 14:22; Deuteronomy 2:8; Numbers 33:35"> Then Solomon went to Ezion Geber and to Elat on the coast in the land of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:10; 2 Chronicles 9:13; Ecclesiastes 2:8"> Huram sent him ships and some of his sailors, men who were well acquainted with the sea. They sailed with Solomon’s men to Ophir, and took from there 450 talents of gold, which they brought back to King Solomon.</span>
