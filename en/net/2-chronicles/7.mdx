<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:36; 1 Kings 8:65; Joel 1:14"> On the eighth day they held an assembly, for they had dedicated the altar for seven days and celebrated the festival for seven more days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 8:66; Psalms 100:2; Deuteronomy 12:18"> On the twenty-third day of the seventh month, Solomon sent the people home. They left happy and contented because of the good the Lord had done for David, Solomon, and his people Israel.</span>

## The Lord Gives Solomon a Promise and a Warning

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 9:1–9; Ecclesiastes 2:10–11; 2 Chronicles 2:1"> After Solomon finished building the Lord’s temple and the royal palace, and accomplished all his plans for the Lord’s temple and his royal palace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; 1 Kings 9:2; 2 Chronicles 7:16"> the Lord appeared to Solomon at night and said to him: “I have answered your prayer and chosen this place to be my temple where sacrifices are to be made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:26–28; Deuteronomy 11:17; Revelation 11:6"> When I close up the sky so that it doesn’t rain, or command locusts to devour the land’s vegetation, or send a plague among my people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40–41; James 4:9–10; Isaiah 55:6–7"> if my people, who belong to me, humble themselves, pray, seek to please me, and repudiate their sinful practices, then I will respond from heaven, forgive their sin, and heal their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; 2 Chronicles 6:40; Nehemiah 1:6"> Now I will be attentive and responsive to the prayers offered in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:15; 2 Chronicles 7:12; 2 Chronicles 6:5–6"> Now I have chosen and consecrated this temple by making it my permanent home; I will be constantly present there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 3:7; Ezekiel 36:27; 1 Kings 11:38"> You must serve me as your father David did. Do everything I commanded and obey my rules and regulations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:16; Jeremiah 33:20–21; 2 Samuel 7:13–16"> Then I will establish your dynasty, just as I promised your father David, ‘You will not fail to have a successor ruling over Israel.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 26:14; Deuteronomy 28:15; Leviticus 26:33–46"> “But if you people ever turn away from me, fail to obey the regulations and rules I instructed you to keep, and decide to serve and worship other gods,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 17:20; Deuteronomy 28:37; Jeremiah 24:9"> then I will remove you from my land I have given you, I will abandon this temple I have consecrated with my presence, and I will make you an object of mockery and ridicule among all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; Jeremiah 16:10–12; Jeremiah 19:8"> As for this temple, which was once majestic, everyone who passes by it will be shocked and say, ‘Why did the Lord do this to this land and this temple?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 9:12; Lamentations 2:16–17; Ezekiel 14:23"> Others will then answer, ‘Because they abandoned the Lord God of their ancestors, who led them out of Egypt. They embraced other gods whom they worshiped and served. That is why he brought all this disaster down on them.’”</span>
</div>
