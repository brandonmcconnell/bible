## Battles with the Philistines

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:18–22; 2 Samuel 21:15; Joshua 12:12"> Later there was a battle with the Philistines in Gezer. At that time Sibbekai the Hushathite killed Sippai, one of the descendants of the Rephaim, and the Philistines were subdued.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:19; 1 Samuel 21:9; 1 Samuel 17:7"> There was another battle with the Philistines in which Elhanan son of Jair the Bethlehemite killed the brother of Goliath the Gittite, whose spear had a shaft as big as the crossbeam of a weaver’s loom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:20"> In a battle in Gath there was a large man who had six fingers on each hand and six toes on each foot – twenty-four in all! He too was a descendant of Rapha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13; 1 Samuel 16:9; Isaiah 37:23"> When he taunted Israel, Jonathan son of Shimea, David’s brother, killed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Joshua 14:12; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> These were the descendants of Rapha who lived in Gath; they were killed by the hand of David and his soldiers.</span>
</div>
