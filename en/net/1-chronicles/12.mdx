## Support for David in Hebron

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:3–4; 1 Chronicles 11:10; 1 Samuel 16:1"> The following is a record of the armed warriors who came with their leaders and joined David in Hebron in order to make David king in Saul’s place, in accordance with the Lord’s decree:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> From Judah came 6,800 trained warriors carrying shields and spears.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> From Simeon there were 7,100 warriors.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> From Levi there were 4,600.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 11:9; 2 Kings 25:18; 1 Chronicles 6:49–57"> Jehoiada, the leader of Aaron’s descendants, brought 3,700 men with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; 1 Chronicles 6:8; 1 Chronicles 6:53"> along with Zadok, a young warrior, and twenty-two leaders from his family.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8–9; 1 Chronicles 12:2; Genesis 31:23"> From Benjamin, Saul’s tribe, there were 3,000, most of whom, up to that time, had been loyal to Saul.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 6:4"> From Ephraim there were 20,800 warriors, who had brought fame to their families.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 17:1–18"> From the half tribe of Manasseh there were 18,000 who had been designated by name to come and make David king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ephesians 5:17; Luke 12:56–57; Esther 1:13"> From Issachar there were 200 leaders and all their relatives at their command – they understood the times and knew what Israel should do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; John 1:47"> From Zebulun there were 50,000 warriors who were prepared for battle, equipped with all kinds of weapons, and ready to give their undivided loyalty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> From Naphtali there were 1,000 officers, along with 37,000 men carrying shields and spears.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> From Dan there were 28,600 men prepared for battle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:33; Joel 2:7"> From Asher there were 40,000 warriors prepared for battle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 14:3; Deuteronomy 3:12–16; Joshua 13:7–32"> From the other side of the Jordan, from Reuben, Gad, and the half tribe of Manasseh, there were 120,000 men armed with all kinds of weapons.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 49:8–10; 1 Kings 8:61; Psalms 101:2"> All these men were warriors who were ready to march. They came to Hebron to make David king over all Israel by acclamation; all the rest of the Israelites also were in agreement that David should become king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; 2 Samuel 19:42; Genesis 26:30"> They spent three days feasting there with David, for their relatives had given them provisions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Samuel 16:1; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> Also their neighbors, from as far away as Issachar, Zebulun, and Naphtali, were bringing food on donkeys, camels, mules, and oxen. There were large supplies of flour, fig cakes, raisins, wine, olive oil, beef, and lamb, for Israel was celebrating.</span>
</div>
