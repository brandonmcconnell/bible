<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:10; 1 Chronicles 13:14; 1 Chronicles 25:3"> including Obed-Edom and sixty-eight colleagues. Obed-Edom son of Jeduthun and Hosah were gatekeepers.</span>
