Exiles Who Resettled in Jerusalem The people of Judah were carried away to Babylon because of their unfaithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:73; Ezra 2:58; Ezra 2:70"> The first to resettle on their property and in their cities were some Israelites, priests, Levites, and temple servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:1; 2 Chronicles 11:16; Nehemiah 11:4–9"> Some from the tribes of Judah, Benjamin, and Ephraim and Manasseh settled in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:5; Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:20"> The settlers included: Uthai son of Ammihud, son of Omri, son of Imri, son of Bani, who was a descendant of Perez son of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; Nehemiah 11:5"> From the Shilonites: Asaiah the firstborn and his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; 1 Chronicles 2:6; Genesis 38:30"> From the descendants of Zerah: Jeuel. Their relatives numbered 690.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:20; Nehemiah 11:7; Nehemiah 8:4"> From the descendants of Benjamin: Sallu son of Meshullam, son of Hodaviah, son of Hassenuah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Ibneiah son of Jeroham; Elah son of Uzzi, son of Mikri; and Meshullam son of Shephatiah, son of Reuel, son of Ibnijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:8"> Their relatives, listed in their genealogical records, numbered 956. All these men were leaders of their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:19; Nehemiah 11:10–24"> From the priests: Jedaiah; Jehoiarib; Jakin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:11; 2 Kings 25:18; Numbers 4:33"> Azariah son of Hilkiah, son of Meshullam, son of Zadok, son of Meraioth, son of Ahitub the leader in God’s temple;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:40; Ezra 2:37; Nehemiah 11:12–13"> Adaiah son of Jeroham, son of Pashhur, son of Malkijah; and Maasai son of Adiel, son of Jahzerah, son of Meshullam, son of Meshillemith, son of Immer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:14; 1 Chronicles 26:30; 1 Chronicles 26:32"> Their relatives, who were leaders of their families, numbered 1,760. They were capable men who were assigned to carry out the various tasks of service in God’s temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 26:57; Nehemiah 10:11; 1 Chronicles 6:29"> From the Levites: Shemaiah son of Hasshub, son of Azrikam, son of Hashabiah a descendant of Merari;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:22; Nehemiah 11:17; 1 Chronicles 11:17"> Bakbakkar; Heresh; Galal; Mattaniah son of Mika, son of Zikri, son of Asaph;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:1; 2 Chronicles 35:15; 1 Chronicles 12:25"> Obadiah son of Shemaiah, son of Galal, son of Jeduthun; and Berechiah son of Asa, son of Elkanah, who lived among the settlements of the Netophathites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:1–32; 1 Chronicles 23:5; Nehemiah 11:19"> The gatekeepers were: Shallum, Akkub, Talmon, Ahiman, and their brothers. Shallum was the leader;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:1–2; Ezekiel 44:2–3; 1 Kings 10:5"> he serves to this day at the King’s Gate on the east. These were the gatekeepers from the camp of the descendants of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 26:9–11; Psalms 44:1; 2 Kings 11:15"> Shallum son of Kore, son of Ebiasaph, son of Korah, and his relatives from his family (the Korahites) were assigned to guard the entrance to the sanctuary. Their ancestors had guarded the entrance to the Lord’s dwelling place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 31:6; Numbers 3:32; Numbers 4:33"> Phinehas son of Eleazar had been their leader in earlier times, and the Lord was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:14; 1 Chronicles 26:2"> Zechariah son of Meshelemiah was the guard at the entrance to the meeting tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Chronicles 28:13; 1 Chronicles 23:1–32"> All those selected to be gatekeepers at the entrances numbered 212. Their names were recorded in the genealogical records of their settlements. David and Samuel the prophet had appointed them to their positions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:14; 2 Chronicles 23:19; Ezekiel 44:10–11"> They and their descendants were assigned to guard the gates of the Lord’s sanctuary (that is, the tabernacle).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:14–18"> The gatekeepers were posted on all four sides – east, west, north, and south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 11:5; 2 Chronicles 23:8; 2 Kings 11:7"> Their relatives, who lived in their settlements, came from time to time and served with them for seven-day periods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:5; Nehemiah 10:38–39; 1 Chronicles 26:20–27"> The four head gatekeepers, who were Levites, were assigned to guard the storerooms and treasuries in God’s sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:30–32; Malachi 1:10; 1 Samuel 3:15"> They would spend the night in their posts all around God’s sanctuary, for they were assigned to guard it and would open it with the key every morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:4–5; Nehemiah 12:44; 1 Chronicles 26:22–26"> Some of them were in charge of the articles used by those who served; they counted them when they brought them in and when they brought them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:29; Exodus 30:23–38; Exodus 27:20"> Some of them were in charge of the equipment and articles of the sanctuary, as well as the flour, wine, olive oil, incense, and spices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 30:23–25; Exodus 37:29; Exodus 30:35–38"> (But some of the priests mixed the spices.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:19; 1 Chronicles 9:22; Leviticus 6:21"> Mattithiah, a Levite, the firstborn son of Shallum the Korahite, was in charge of baking the bread for offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–8; 1 Chronicles 6:33–48; Exodus 25:30"> Some of the Kohathites, their relatives, were in charge of preparing the bread that is displayed each Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:31–33; Psalms 134:1–2; 1 Chronicles 16:4–6"> The musicians and Levite family leaders stayed in rooms at the sanctuary and were exempt from other duties, for day and night they had to carry out their assigned tasks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:1–15; 1 Chronicles 9:13"> These were the family leaders of the Levites, as listed in their genealogical records. They lived in Jerusalem.</span>

## Jeiel’s Descendants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:23–24; 1 Chronicles 2:50–52; 1 Chronicles 8:29–40"> Jeiel (the father of Gibeon) lived in Gibeon. His wife was Maacah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:39; 1 Chronicles 8:33"> His firstborn son was Abdon, followed by Zur, Kish, Baal, Ner, Nadab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:31"> Gedor, Ahio, Zechariah, and Mikloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:32"> Mikloth was the father of Shimeam. They also lived near their relatives in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:22; 1 Chronicles 8:33–38; 1 Chronicles 10:2"> Ner was the father of Kish, and Kish was the father of Saul. Saul was the father of Jonathan, Malki-Shua, Abinadab, and Eshbaal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Samuel 4:4; 1 Chronicles 8:34–36"> The son of Jonathan: Meribbaal, who was the father of Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:35"> The sons of Micah: Pithon, Melech, Tahrea, and Ahaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:36"> Ahaz was the father of Jarah, and Jarah was the father of Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri. Zimri was the father of Moza,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:37"> and Moza was the father of Binea. His son was Rephaiah, whose son was Eleasah, whose son was Azel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Azel had six sons: Azrikam his firstborn, followed by Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. These were the sons of Azel.</span>
