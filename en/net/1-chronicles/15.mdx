</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:28; 1 Kings 2:35; 1 Samuel 22:20–23"> David summoned the priests Zadok and Abiathar, along with the Levites Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, and Amminadab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:6; Exodus 19:14–15; 1 Chronicles 15:14"> He told them: “You are the leaders of the Levites’ families. You and your relatives must consecrate yourselves and bring the ark of the Lord God of Israel up to the place I have prepared for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:3; 1 Corinthians 14:40; 1 John 1:8–10"> The first time you did not carry it; that is why the Lord God attacked us, because we did not ask him about the proper way to carry it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:15; Joel 2:16–17; 2 Chronicles 29:34"> The priests and Levites consecrated themselves so they could bring up the ark of the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Numbers 7:9; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> The descendants of Levi carried the ark of God on their shoulders with poles, just as Moses had ordered according to the divine command.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; 2 Chronicles 5:13; 1 Chronicles 16:42"> David told the leaders of the Levites to appoint some of their relatives as musicians; they were to play various instruments, including stringed instruments and cymbals, and to sing loudly and joyfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:39; 1 Chronicles 6:44; 1 Chronicles 6:33"> So the Levites appointed Heman son of Joel; one of his relatives, Asaph son of Berechiah; one of the descendants of Merari, Ethan son of Kushaiah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4; 1 Chronicles 26:8; 1 Chronicles 13:14"> along with some of their relatives who were second in rank, including Zechariah, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-Edom, and Jeiel, the gatekeepers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:6; 1 Chronicles 16:5; 1 Chronicles 13:8"> The musicians Heman, Asaph, and Ethan were to sound the bronze cymbals;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 46:1–11"> Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah were to play the harps according to the alamoth style;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5; 1 Chronicles 25:6–7; Psalms 81:1–2"> Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-Edom, Jeiel, and Azaziah were to play the lyres according to the sheminith style, as led by the director;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:27; 1 Chronicles 15:16; 1 Chronicles 25:7–8"> Kenaniah, the leader of the Levites, was in charge of transport, for he was well-informed on this matter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 84:10; 2 Kings 22:4; 1 Chronicles 9:21–23"> Berechiah and Elkanah were guardians of the ark;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:6; Numbers 10:8; 1 Chronicles 15:28"> Shebaniah, Joshaphat, Nethanel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer the priests were to blow the trumpets before the ark of God; Obed-Edom and Jehiel were also guardians of the ark.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:12–23; Micah 5:2; Philippians 3:3"> So David, the leaders of Israel, and the commanders of units of a thousand went to bring up the ark of the Lord’s covenant from the house of Obed-Edom with celebration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 23:29; 1 Chronicles 29:14; Psalms 66:13–15"> When God helped the Levites who were carrying the ark of the Lord’s covenant, they sacrificed seven bulls and seven rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:18; 2 Samuel 6:14"> David was wrapped in a linen robe, as were all the Levites carrying the ark, the musicians, and Kenaniah the supervisor of transport and the musicians; David also wore a linen ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; 1 Chronicles 15:16; 2 Chronicles 5:12–13"> All Israel brought up the ark of the Lord’s covenant; they were shouting, blowing trumpets, sounding cymbals, and playing stringed instruments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:20–23; 2 Samuel 6:16; Deuteronomy 31:26"> As the ark of the Lord’s covenant entered the City of David, Michal, Saul’s daughter, looked out the window. When she saw King David jumping and celebrating, she despised him.</span>
</div>
