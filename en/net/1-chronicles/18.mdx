<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:6; 2 Samuel 8:5–6; 1 Samuel 14:47"> The Arameans of Damascus came to help King Hadadezer of Zobah, but David killed 22,000 of the Arameans.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 12:9–10"> David took the golden shields which Hadadezer’s servants had carried and brought them to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:8; 2 Chronicles 4:12–18; 1 Kings 7:15–47"> From Tibhath and Kun, Hadadezer’s cities, David took a great deal of bronze. (Solomon used it to make the big bronze basin called “The Sea,” the pillars, and other bronze items.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:9"> When King Tou of Hamath heard that David had defeated the entire army of King Hadadezer of Zobah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:10; Isaiah 39:1; 2 Chronicles 9:1"> he sent his son Hadoram to King David to extend his best wishes and to pronounce a blessing on him for his victory over Hadadezer, for Tou had been at war with Hadadezer. He also sent various items made of gold, silver, and bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:13; 1 Chronicles 26:26–27; 1 Kings 7:51"> King David dedicated these things to the Lord, along with the silver and gold which he had carried off from all the nations, including Edom, Moab, the Ammonites, the Philistines, and Amalek.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:11; 2 Samuel 10:10; 2 Samuel 8:13–14"> Abishai son of Zeruiah killed 18,000 Edomites in the Valley of Salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:6; Genesis 27:37; Psalms 144:10"> He placed garrisons in Edom, and all the Edomites became David’s subjects. The Lord protected David wherever he campaigned.</span>

## David’s Officials

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:15; Psalms 89:14; Psalms 78:71–72"> David reigned over all Israel; he guaranteed justice for all his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:6; 1 Kings 4:3; 2 Samuel 8:16"> Joab son of Zeruiah was commanding general of the army; Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was secretary;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; 2 Samuel 20:25; 1 Kings 4:3"> Zadok son of Ahitub and Abimelech son of Abiathar were priests; Shavsha was scribe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 2 Samuel 15:18; 1 Kings 2:34–35"> Benaiah son of Jehoiada supervised the Kerethites and Pelethites; and David’s sons were the king’s leading officials.</span>
</div>
