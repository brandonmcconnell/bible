<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:1–2; 1 Samuel 23:2; 1 Samuel 14:18"> Let’s move the ark of our God back here, for we did not seek his will throughout Saul’s reign.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:21–1; 1 Kings 8:65; 2 Samuel 6:1"> So David assembled all Israel from the Shihor River in Egypt to Lebo Hamath, to bring the ark of God from Kiriath Jearim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Joshua 15:9; 2 Kings 19:15"> David and all Israel went up to Baalah (that is, Kiriath Jearim) in Judah to bring up from there the ark of God the Lord, who sits enthroned between the cherubim – the ark that is called by his name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; 1 Samuel 6:7; 1 Chronicles 15:2"> They transported the ark on a new cart from the house of Abinadab; Uzzah and Ahio were guiding the cart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 150:3–5; Psalms 68:25–27; Amos 6:5"> while David and all Israel were energetically celebrating before God, singing and playing various stringed instruments, tambourines, cymbals, and trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:6"> When they arrived at the threshing floor of Kidon, Uzzah reached out his hand to take hold of the ark, because the oxen stumbled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:15; 1 Chronicles 15:13; Numbers 4:15"> The Lord was so furious with Uzzah, he killed him, because he reached out his hand and touched the ark. He died right there before God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:7; 2 Samuel 6:9; Genesis 32:32"> David was angry because the Lord attacked Uzzah; so he called that place Perez Uzzah, which remains its name to this very day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; 1 Kings 8:27; Job 25:5–6"> David was afraid of God that day and said, “How will I ever be able to bring the ark of God up here?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4; 1 Chronicles 15:18; 2 Samuel 4:3"> So David did not move the ark to the City of David; he left it in the house of Obed-Edom the Gittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4–5; Proverbs 10:22; Proverbs 3:9–10"> The ark of God remained in Obed-Edom’s house for three months; the Lord blessed Obed-Edom’s family and everything that belonged to him.</span>
