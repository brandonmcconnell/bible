<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 15:24; 1 Chronicles 15:18"> Obed-Edom’s sons: The firstborn Shemaiah, the second Jehozabad, the third Joah, the fourth Sakar, the fifth Nethanel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 128:1"> the sixth Ammiel, the seventh Issachar, and the eighth Peullethai. (Indeed, God blessed Obed-Edom.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:28; 1 Timothy 6:12; Judges 6:12"> His son Shemaiah also had sons, who were leaders of their families, for they were highly respected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The sons of Shemaiah: Othni, Rephael, Obed, and Elzabad. His relatives Elihu and Semakiah were also respected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:6; 1 Peter 4:11; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11"> All these were the descendants of Obed-Edom. They and their sons and relatives were respected men, capable of doing their responsibilities. There were sixty-two of them related to Obed-Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:14; 1 Chronicles 26:1"> Meshelemiah had sons and relatives who were respected – eighteen in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2"> Hosah, one of the descendants of Merari, had sons: The firstborn Shimri (he was not actually the firstborn, but his father gave him that status),</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> the second Hilkiah, the third Tebaliah, and the fourth Zechariah. All of Hosah’s sons and relatives numbered thirteen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8"> These divisions of the gatekeepers, corresponding to their leaders, had assigned responsibilities, like their relatives, as they served in the Lord’s temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8; 1 Chronicles 24:31; 1 Chronicles 24:5"> They cast lots, both young and old, according to their families, to determine which gate they would be responsible for.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The lot for the east gate went to Shelemiah. They then cast lots for his son Zechariah, a wise adviser, and the lot for the north gate went to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:24; 1 Chronicles 26:17"> Obed-Edom was assigned the south gate, and his sons were assigned the storehouses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:24; 1 Chronicles 26:10–12; 1 Kings 10:5"> Shuppim and Hosah were assigned the west gate, along with the Shalleketh gate on the upper road. One guard was adjacent to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 9:24"> Each day there were six Levites posted on the east, four on the north, and four on the south. At the storehouses they were posted in pairs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 23:11"> At the court on the west there were four posted on the road and two at the court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 16:11"> These were the divisions of the gatekeepers who were descendants of Korah and Merari.</span>

## Supervisors of the Storehouses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:22; 1 Chronicles 28:12–19; 1 Chronicles 22:14–16"> Their fellow Levites were in charge of the storehouses in God’s temple and the storehouses containing consecrated items.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:7; 1 Chronicles 6:17; 1 Chronicles 29:8"> The descendants of Ladan, who were descended from Gershon through Ladan and were leaders of the families of Ladan the Gershonite, included Jehieli</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:8; Nehemiah 10:38; 1 Chronicles 26:20"> and the sons of Jehieli, Zetham and his brother Joel. They were in charge of the storehouses in the Lord’s temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 3:27; 1 Chronicles 23:12; Numbers 3:19"> As for the Amramites, Izharites, Hebronites, and Uzzielites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:20; 1 Chronicles 23:15–16"> Shebuel son of Gershom, the son of Moses, was the supervisor of the storehouses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:17–18; 1 Chronicles 23:15; Exodus 18:4"> His relatives through Eliezer included: Rehabiah his son, Jeshaiah his son, Joram his son, Zikri his son, and Shelomith his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:11; 1 Chronicles 29:2–9; 1 Chronicles 22:14"> Shelomith and his relatives were in charge of all the storehouses containing the consecrated items dedicated by King David, the family leaders who led units of a thousand and a hundred, and the army officers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 12:14; Joshua 6:19; Nehemiah 10:32–34"> They had dedicated some of the plunder taken in battles to be used for repairs on the Lord’s temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Samuel 17:55; 2 Samuel 10:9–14"> They were also in charge of everything dedicated by Samuel the prophet, Saul son of Kish, Abner son of Ner, and Joab son of Zeruiah; Shelomith and his relatives were in charge of everything that had been dedicated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:4; Nehemiah 11:16; 2 Chronicles 34:13"> As for the Izharites: Kenaniah and his sons were given responsibilities outside the temple as officers and judges over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:17; 1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> As for the Hebronites: Hashabiah and his relatives, 1,700 respected men, were assigned responsibilities in Israel west of the Jordan; they did the Lord’s work and the king’s service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:19; Joshua 21:39; 1 Chronicles 29:27"> As for the Hebronites: Jeriah was the leader of the Hebronites according to the genealogical records. In the fortieth year of David’s reign, they examined the records and discovered there were highly respected men in Jazer in Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:11; 1 Chronicles 12:37; 1 Chronicles 23:24"> Jeriah had 2,700 relatives who were respected family leaders. King David placed them in charge of the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh; they took care of all matters pertaining to God and the king.</span>
</div>
