<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 4:27; 2 Chronicles 26:11–13; Deuteronomy 1:15"> What follows is a list of Israelite family leaders and commanders of units of a thousand and a hundred, as well as their officers who served the king in various matters. Each division was assigned to serve for one month during the year; each consisted of 24,000 men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:11; 2 Samuel 23:8"> Jashobeam son of Zabdiel was in charge of the first division, which was assigned the first month. His division consisted of 24,000 men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; Numbers 10:14; Genesis 49:8–10"> He was a descendant of Perez; he was in charge of all the army officers for the first month.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:9; 1 Chronicles 11:12"> Dodai the Ahohite was in charge of the division assigned the second month; Mikloth was the next in rank. His division consisted of 24,000 men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:17; 1 Kings 4:4–5"> The third army commander, assigned the third month, was Benaiah son of Jehoiada the priest. He was the leader of his division, which consisted of 24,000 men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:20–23; 1 Chronicles 11:22–25; 2 Samuel 22:20–23"> Benaiah was the leader of the thirty warriors and his division; his son was Ammizabad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:26; 2 Samuel 23:24; 2 Samuel 2:18–23"> The fourth, assigned the fourth month, was Asahel, brother of Joab; his son Zebadiah succeeded him. His division consisted of 24,000 men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:25; 1 Chronicles 26:29"> The fifth, assigned the fifth month, was the commander Shamhuth the Izrahite. His division consisted of 24,000 men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:28; 2 Samuel 23:26"> The sixth, assigned the sixth month, was Ira son of Ikkesh the Tekoite. His division consisted of 24,000 men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:26"> The seventh, assigned the seventh month, was Helez the Pelonite, an Ephraimite. His division consisted of 24,000 men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:18; 1 Chronicles 11:29; Numbers 26:20"> The eighth, assigned the eighth month, was Sibbekai the Hushathite, a Zerahite. His division consisted of 24,000 men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:28; 2 Samuel 23:27"> The ninth, assigned the ninth month, was Abiezer the Anathothite, a Benjaminite. His division consisted of 24,000 men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:30; 2 Samuel 23:28"> The tenth, assigned the tenth month, was Maharai the Netophathite, a Zerahite. His division consisted of 24,000 men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:31; 2 Samuel 23:30"> The eleventh, assigned the eleventh month, was Benaiah the Pirathonite, an Ephraimite. His division consisted of 24,000 men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:29; 1 Chronicles 4:13; Judges 3:9"> The twelfth, assigned the twelfth month, was Heldai the Netophathite, a descendant of Othniel. His division consisted of 24,000 men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The officers of the Israelite tribes:<br />Eliezer son of Zikri was the leader of the Reubenites,<br />Shephatiah son of Maacah led the Simeonites,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:30; 1 Chronicles 24:31; 1 Chronicles 12:27–28"> Hashabiah son of Kemuel led the Levites,<br />Zadok led the descendants of Aaron,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:6; 1 Samuel 17:29; 1 Samuel 17:13"> Elihu, a brother of David, led Judah,<br />Omri son of Michael led Issachar,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Ishmaiah son of Obadiah led Zebulun,<br />Jerimoth son of Azriel led Naphtali,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Hoshea son of Azaziah led the Ephraimites,<br />Joel son of Pedaiah led the half-tribe of Manasseh,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:27; 1 Samuel 14:50–51; 2 Samuel 3:37"> Iddo son of Zechariah led the half-tribe of Manasseh in Gilead,<br />Jaasiel son of Abner led Benjamin,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:1"> Azarel son of Jeroham led Dan.<br />These were the commanders of the Israelite tribes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; 1 Chronicles 21:2–5; Hebrews 11:12"> David did not count the males twenty years old and under, for the Lord had promised to make Israel as numerous as the stars in the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:1–17; 2 Samuel 24:1–15"> Joab son of Zeruiah started to count the men but did not finish. God was angry with Israel because of this, so the number was not recorded in the scroll called The Annals of King David.</span>
</div>

## Royal Officials

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:2; Exodus 1:11; 2 Chronicles 26:10"> Azmaveth son of Adiel was in charge of the king’s storehouses;<br />Jonathan son of Uzziah was in charge of the storehouses in the field, in the cities, in the towns, and in the towers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Ezri son of Kelub was in charge of the field workers who farmed the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Shimei the Ramathite was in charge of the vineyards;<br />Zabdi the Shiphmite was in charge of the wine stored in the vineyards.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 10:27; 2 Chronicles 1:15; 1 Kings 4:7"> Baal-Hanan the Gederite was in charge of the olive and sycamore trees in the lowlands;<br />Joash was in charge of the storehouses of olive oil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:16; Isaiah 65:10"> Shitrai the Sharonite was in charge of the cattle grazing in Sharon;<br />Shaphat son of Adlai was in charge of the cattle in the valleys.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 47:6; Job 1:3"> Obil the Ishmaelite was in charge of the camels;<br />Jehdeiah the Meronothite was in charge of the donkeys.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:10"> Jaziz the Hagrite was in charge of the sheep.<br />All these were the officials in charge of King David’s property.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:21; 2 Samuel 13:3; 1 Chronicles 11:11"> Jonathan, David’s uncle, was a wise adviser and scribe;<br />Jehiel son of Hacmoni cared for the king’s sons.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:12; 2 Samuel 15:37; 2 Samuel 15:32"> Ahithophel was the king’s adviser;<br />Hushai the Arkite was the king’s confidant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:6; 1 Kings 1:7; 1 Chronicles 27:5"> Ahithophel was succeeded by Jehoiada son of Benaiah and by Abiathar.<br />Joab was the commanding general of the king’s army.</span>
</div>
