<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Ecclesiastes 7:1; Numbers 11:15"> So now, Lord, kill me instead, because I would rather die than live!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 20:15; Jonah 4:9; Micah 6:3"> The Lord said, “Are you really so very angry?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 19:9; 1 Kings 19:13; Jeremiah 17:15–16"> Jonah left the city and sat down east of it. He made a shelter for himself there and sat down under it in the shade to see what would happen to the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 103:10–14; Isaiah 39:2; Esther 5:9"> The Lord God appointed a little plant and caused it to grow up over Jonah to be a shade over his head to rescue him from his misery. Now Jonah was very delighted about the little plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joel 1:12; Job 1:21; Psalms 102:10"> So God sent a worm at dawn the next day, and it attacked the little plant so that it dried up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 4:3; Psalms 121:6; Ezekiel 19:12"> When the sun began to shine, God sent a hot east wind. So the sun beat down on Jonah’s head, and he grew faint. So he despaired of life, and said, “I would rather die than live!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 5:2; Job 18:4; Judges 16:16"> God said to Jonah, “Are you really so very angry about the little plant?” And he said, “I am as angry as I could possibly be!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:31; Genesis 17:12"> The Lord said, “You were upset about this little plant, something for which you have not worked nor did you do anything to make it grow. It grew up overnight and died the next day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 18:33; Jonah 1:2; Luke 15:28–32"> Should I not be even more concerned about Nineveh, this enormous city? There are more than one hundred twenty thousand people in it who do not know right from wrong, as well as many animals!”</span>
