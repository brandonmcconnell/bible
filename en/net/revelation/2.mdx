<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:1; 2 Peter 2:1–3; 1 Thessalonians 5:21"> ‘I know your works as well as your labor and steadfast endurance, and that you cannot tolerate evil. <span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Galatians 6:9; James 5:7–11"> I am also aware that you have persisted steadfastly, endured much for the sake of my name, and have not grown weary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 24:12–13; Jeremiah 2:2–5; Hebrews 6:10–11"> But I have this against you: You have departed from your first love!</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 101:3; 2 John 1:9–10; Psalms 26:5"> But you do have this going for you: You hate what the Nicolaitans practice – practices I also hate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 2:17; Revelation 2:11; Matthew 11:15"> The one who has an ear had better hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who conquers, I will permit him to eat from the tree of life that is in the paradise of God.’</span>

## To the Church in Smyrna

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; Revelation 1:8; Revelation 1:11"> “To the angel of the church in Smyrna write the following:<br />“This is the solemn pronouncement of the one who is the first and the last, the one who was dead, but came to life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; 2 Corinthians 8:9; James 2:5–6"> ‘I know the distress you are suffering and your poverty (but you are rich). I also know the slander against you by those who call themselves Jews and really are not, but are a synagogue of Satan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; Matthew 10:22; Revelation 3:10–11"> Do not be afraid of the things you are about to suffer. The devil is about to have some of you thrown into prison so you may be tested, and you will experience suffering for ten days. Remain faithful even to the point of death, and I will give you the crown that is life itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 20:6; Revelation 20:14; Revelation 21:8"> The one who has an ear had better hear what the Spirit says to the churches. The one who conquers will in no way be harmed by the second death.’</span>
</div>
</div>

## To the Church in Pergamum

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 2:16; Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 1:16"> “To the angel of the church in Pergamum write the following:<br />“This is the solemn pronouncement of the one who has the sharp double-edged sword:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 2:9–10; 1 Timothy 5:8; Revelation 3:8–9"> ‘I know where you live – where Satan’s throne is. Yet you continue to cling to my name and you have not denied your faith in me, even in the days of Antipas, my faithful witness, who was killed in your city where Satan lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 31:16; 2 Peter 2:15; Revelation 2:20"> But I have a few things against you: You have some people there who follow the teaching of Balaam, who instructed Balak to put a stumbling block before the people of Israel so they would eat food sacrificed to idols and commit sexual immorality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 2:6"> In the same way, there are also some among you who follow the teaching of the Nicolaitans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:8; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 2:5"> Therefore, repent! If not, I will come against you quickly and make war against those people with the sword of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 62:2; Isaiah 65:15; Revelation 19:12–13"> The one who has an ear had better hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who conquers, I will give him some of the hidden manna, and I will give him a white stone, and on that stone will be written a new name that no one can understand except the one who receives it.’</span>
</div>
</div>

## To the Church in Thyatira

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 1:14–15; Revelation 1:11; John 3:35–36"> “To the angel of the church in Thyatira writethe following:<br />“ This is the solemn pronouncement of the Son of God, the one who has eyes like a fiery flame and whose feet are like polished bronze:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; 2 Peter 1:7; John 15:2"> ‘I know your deeds: your love, faith, service, and steadfast endurance. In fact, your more recent deeds are greater than your earlier ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 2:14; 1 Kings 16:31; 1 Corinthians 10:18–21"> But I have this against you: You tolerate that woman Jezebel, who calls herself a prophetess, and by her teaching deceives my servants to commit sexual immorality and to eat food sacrificed to idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Romans 2:4–5; Revelation 9:20–21"> I have given her time to repent, but she is not willing to repent of her sexual immorality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 17:2; Luke 13:5; Ezekiel 16:37–41"> Look! I am throwing her onto a bed of violent illness, and those who commit adultery with her into terrible suffering, unless they repent of her deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Romans 8:27; Psalms 7:9"> Furthermore, I will strike her followers with a deadly disease, and then all the churches will know that I am the one who searches minds and hearts. I will repay each one of you what your deeds deserve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:11; Revelation 12:9; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> But to the rest of you in Thyatira, all who do not hold to this teaching (who have not learned the so-called “deep secrets of Satan”), to you I say: I do not put any additional burden on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 3:11; Revelation 3:3; Hebrews 10:23"> However, hold on to what you have until I come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 3:21; Daniel 7:27; Revelation 2:7"> And to the one who conquers and who continues in my deeds until the end, I will give him authority over the nations –</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 2:8–9; Revelation 12:5; Revelation 19:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">he will rule them with an iron rod&#42;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and like clay jars he will break them to pieces,</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; 2 Peter 1:19; Luke 1:78–79"> just as I have received the right to rule from my Father – and I will give him the morning star.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 2:7"> The one who has an ear had better hear what the Spirit says to the churches.’</span>
</div>
</div>
