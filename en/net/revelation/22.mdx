<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Colossians 2:18–19; Revelation 14:7"> But he said to me, “Do not do this! I am a fellow servant with you and with your brothers the prophets, and with those who obey the words of this book. Worship God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 8:26; Revelation 1:3; 1 Peter 4:7"> Then he said to me, “Do not seal up the words of the prophecy contained in this book, because the time is near.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; 1 John 5:3; Revelation 22:7"> Blessed are those who wash their robes so they can have access to the tree of life and can enter into the city by the gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; Galatians 5:19–21; Philippians 3:2"> Outside are the dogs and the sorcerers and the sexually immoral, and the murderers, and the idolaters and everyone who loves and practices falsehood!</span>
