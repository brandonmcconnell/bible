<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:9–10; Isaiah 29:11; Revelation 5:7"> Then I saw in the right hand of the one who was seated on the throne a scroll written on the front and back and sealed with seven seals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 29:11–12; Revelation 5:5; Revelation 10:1"> And I saw a powerful angel proclaiming in a loud voice: “Who is worthy to open the scroll and to break its seals?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 5:13; Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34"> But no one in heaven or on earth or under the earth was able to open the scroll or look into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 12:8–9; Revelation 4:1"> So I began weeping bitterly because no one was found who was worthy to open the scroll or to look into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 11:1; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Revelation 22:16"> Then one of the elders said to me, “Stop weeping! Look, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the root of David, has conquered; thus he can open the scroll and its seven seals.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 4:10; John 1:29; Revelation 5:12"> Then I saw standing in the middle of the throne and of the four living creatures, and in the middle of the elders, a Lamb that appeared to have been killed. He had seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven spirits of God sent out into all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 5:1; Revelation 4:2–3"> Then he came and took the scroll from the right hand of the one who was seated on the throne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Revelation 8:3–4; Philippians 2:9–11"> and when he had taken the scroll, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders threw themselves to the ground before the Lamb. Each of them had a harp and golden bowls full of incense (which are the prayers of the saints).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; 1 John 1:7; Matthew 26:28"> They were singing a new song:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are worthy to take the scroll</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and to open its seals</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because you were killed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and at the cost of your own blood you have purchased for God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">persons from every tribe, language, people, and nation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 1:6; Daniel 7:27; Revelation 20:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have appointed them as a kingdom and priests to serve our God, and they will reign on the earth.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 7:10; Hebrews 12:22; Revelation 4:4"> Then I looked and heard the voice of many angels in a circle around the throne, as well as the living creatures and the elders. Their number was ten thousand times ten thousand – thousands times thousands –</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Revelation 19:1; Revelation 4:11"> all of whom were singing in a loud voice:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Worthy is the lamb who was killed</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to receive power and wealth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and wisdom and might</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and honor and glory and praise!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; 1 Chronicles 29:11; Psalms 148:2–13"> Then I heard every creature – in heaven, on earth, under the earth, in the sea, and all that is in them – singing:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“To the one seated on the throne and to the Lamb</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">be praise, honor, glory, and ruling power forever and ever!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 19:4; Revelation 4:9–11; Revelation 5:8"> And the four living creatures were saying “Amen,” and the elders threw themselves to the ground and worshiped.</span>
