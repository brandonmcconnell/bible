<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 20:1; Revelation 17:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> The angel then threw him into the abyss and locked and sealed it so that he could not deceive the nations until the one thousand years were finished. <span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Daniel 7:18; Matthew 19:28"> Then I saw thrones and seated on them were those who had been given authority to judge. These had not worshiped the beast or his image and had refused to receive his mark on their forehead or hand. They came to life and reigned with Christ for a thousand years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:11; Revelation 11:15; Luke 14:14"> (The rest of the dead did not come to life until the thousand years were finished.) This is the first resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 2:11; Revelation 1:6; 2 Timothy 2:12"> Blessed and holy is the one who takes part in the first resurrection. ## Satan’s Final Defeat

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 20:2"> Now when the thousand years are finished, Satan will be released from his prison</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 16:14; Ezekiel 38:1; Revelation 20:10"> and will go out to deceive the nations at the four corners of the earth, Gog and Magog, to bring them together for the battle. They are as numerous as the grains of sand in the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 39:6; Ezekiel 38:9"> They went up on the broad plain of the earth and encircled the camp of the saints and the beloved city, but fire came down from heaven and devoured them completely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Revelation 19:20; Matthew 25:46"> And the devil who deceived them was thrown into the lake of fire and sulfur, where the beast and the false prophet are too, and they will be tormented there day and night forever and ever.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Great White Throne

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 21:1; 2 Peter 3:7; Matthew 25:31"> Then I saw a large white throne and the one who was seated on it; the earth and the heaven fled from his presence, and no place was found for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Matthew 16:27; Daniel 7:10"> And I saw the dead, the great and the small, standing before the throne. Then books were opened, and another book was opened – the book of life. So the dead were judged by what was written in the books, according to their deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 5:28–29; Revelation 6:8; Hosea 13:14"> The sea gave up the dead that were in it, and Death and Hades gave up the dead that were in them, and each one was judged according to his deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:26; Revelation 20:6; Revelation 21:8"> Then Death and Hades were thrown into the lake of fire. This is the second death – the lake of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 3:36; Matthew 13:50; Matthew 25:41"> If anyone’s name was not found written in the book of life, that person was thrown into the lake of fire.</span>
</div>
