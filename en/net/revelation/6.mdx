<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 6:5; Revelation 5:1; Revelation 6:7"> I looked on when the Lamb opened one of the seven seals, and I heard one of the four living creatures saying with a thunderous voice, “Come!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 6:3–8; Zechariah 1:8; Psalms 45:3–5"> So I looked, and here came a white horse! The one who rode it had a bow, and he was given a crown, and as a conqueror he rode out to conquer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 4:7; Revelation 6:1"> Then when the Lamb opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature saying, “Come!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 6:2; Matthew 10:34; Revelation 13:10"> And another horse, fiery red, came out, and the one who rode it was granted permission to take peace from the earth, so that people would butcher one another, and he was given a huge sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 6:2; Ezekiel 4:16; Zechariah 6:6"> Then when the Lamb opened the third seal I heard the third living creature saying, “Come!” So I looked, and here came a black horse! The one who rode it had a balance scale in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 9:4; Revelation 4:6; Revelation 7:3"> Then I heard something like a voice from among the four living creatures saying, “A quart of wheat will cost a day’s pay and three quarts of barley will cost a day’s pay. But do not damage the olive oil and the wine!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 4:7; Revelation 6:3; Revelation 6:1"> Then when the Lamb opened the fourth seal I heard the voice of the fourth living creature saying, “Come!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; Jeremiah 15:2–3; Revelation 1:18"> So I looked and here came a pale green horse! The name of the one who rode it was Death, and Hades followed right behind. They were given authority over a fourth of the earth, to kill its population with the sword, famine, and disease, and by the wild animals of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 20:4; John 16:2; Revelation 1:9"> Now when the Lamb opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of those who had been violently killed because of the word of God and because of the testimony they had given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 19:2; Psalms 9:12; Revelation 16:5–7"> They cried out with a loud voice, “How long, Sovereign Master, holy and true, before you judge those who live on the earth and avenge our blood?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Revelation 7:9; Revelation 3:4–5"> Each of them was given a long white robe and they were told to rest for a little longer, until the full number was reached of both their fellow servants and their brothers who were going to be killed just as they had been.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Joel 2:10; Acts 2:19–20"> Then I looked when the Lamb opened the sixth seal, and a huge earthquake took place; the sun became as black as sackcloth made of hair, and the full moon became blood red;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4; Matthew 24:29; Revelation 9:1"> and the stars in the sky fell to the earth like a fig tree dropping its unripe figs when shaken by a fierce wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4; Revelation 16:20; 2 Peter 3:10"> The sky was split apart like a scroll being rolled up, and every mountain and island was moved from its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19; Isaiah 2:21; Isaiah 24:21–22"> Then the kings of the earth, the very important people, the generals, the rich, the powerful, and everyone, slave and free, hid themselves in the caves and among the rocks of the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 23:30; Hosea 10:8; Revelation 4:2"> They said to the mountains and to the rocks, “Fall on us and hide us from the face of the one who is seated on the throne and from the wrath of the Lamb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 3:2; Joel 2:11; Psalms 76:7"> because the great day of their wrath has come, and who is able to withstand it?”</span>
