<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 12:3; Revelation 17:3; Daniel 7:23–25"> Then I saw a beast coming up out of the sea. It had ten horns and seven heads, and on its horns were ten diadem crowns, and on its heads a blasphemous name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 7:4–7; 1 Peter 5:8; Revelation 17:12"> Now the beast that I saw was like a leopard, but its feet were like a bear’s, and its mouth was like a lion’s mouth. The dragon gave the beast his power, his throne, and great authority to rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 17:8; Revelation 13:14; Revelation 17:17"> One of the beast’s heads appeared to have been killed, but the lethal wound had been healed. And the whole world followed the beast in amazement;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 13:2; Revelation 18:18; Revelation 13:15"> they worshiped the dragon because he had given ruling authority to the beast, and they worshiped the beast too, saying: “Who is like the beast?” and “Who is able to make war against him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 7:8; Daniel 7:25; Daniel 11:36"> The beast was given a mouth speaking proud words and blasphemies, and he was permitted to exercise ruling authority for forty-two months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; Hebrews 9:24; Revelation 12:12"> So the beast opened his mouth to blaspheme against God – to blaspheme both his name and his dwelling place, that is, those who dwell in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 7:21; Revelation 11:7; Daniel 7:25"> The beast was permitted to go to war against the saints and conquer them. He was given ruling authority over every tribe, people, language, and nation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–20; Ephesians 1:4; Matthew 25:34"> and all those who live on the earth will worship the beast, everyone whose name has not been written since the foundation of the world in the book of life belonging to the Lamb who was killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Revelation 2:17; Revelation 2:11"> If anyone has an ear, he had better listen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:2; Isaiah 33:1; Revelation 14:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">If anyone is meant for captivity,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">into captivity he will go.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If anyone is to be killed by the sword,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">then by the sword he must be killed.</span>This requires steadfast endurance and faith from the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 7:8; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; Matthew 7:15"> Then I saw another beast coming up from the earth. He had two horns like a lamb, but was speaking like a dragon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 17:10–11; Revelation 14:11; Revelation 14:9"> He exercised all the ruling authority of the first beast on his behalf, and made the earth and those who inhabit it worship the first beast, the one whose lethal wound had been healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; Revelation 19:20; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–10"> He performed momentous signs, even making fire come down from heaven in front of people</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 12:9; Revelation 19:20; Revelation 13:8"> and, by the signs he was permitted to perform on behalf of the beast, he deceived those who live on the earth. He told those who live on the earth to make an image to the beast who had been wounded by the sword, but still lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 20:4; Revelation 19:20; Daniel 3:3–6"> The second beast was empowered to give life to the image of the first beast so that it could speak, and could cause all those who did not worship the image of the beast to be killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 20:4; Revelation 19:20; Ezekiel 9:4"> He also caused everyone (small and great, rich and poor, free and slave ) to obtain a mark on their right hand or on their forehead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 15:2; Revelation 13:16; Revelation 14:11"> Thus no one was allowed to buy or sell things unless he bore the mark of the beast – that is, his name or his number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 15:2; Revelation 17:9; Revelation 21:17"> This calls for wisdom: Let the one who has insight calculate the beast’s number, for it is man’s number, and his number is 666.</span>
