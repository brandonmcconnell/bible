## The Wedding Celebration of the Lamb

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:1; Revelation 14:2; Psalms 93:1"> Then I heard what sounded like the voice of a vast throng, like the roar of many waters and like loud crashes of thunder. ## The Son of God Goes to War

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 96:13; Isaiah 11:3–5; Psalms 98:9"> Then I saw heaven opened and here came a white horse! The one riding it was called “Faithful” and “True,” and with justice he judges and goes to war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Luke 10:22; Revelation 2:17–18"> His eyes are like a fiery flame and there are many diadem crowns on his head. He has a name written that no one knows except himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 1:1; Isaiah 63:1–6; John 1:14"> He is dressed in clothing dipped in blood, and he is called the Word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jude 1:14; Revelation 7:9; Revelation 19:8"> The armies that are in heaven, dressed in white, clean, fine linen, were following him on white horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 63:2–6; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 2:27"> From his mouth extends a sharp sword, so that with it he can strike the nations. He will rule them with an iron rod, and he stomps the winepress of the furious wrath of God, the All-Powerful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; 1 Timothy 6:15; Daniel 2:47"> He has a name written on his clothing and on his thigh: “King of kings and Lord of lords.”</span>
</div>
