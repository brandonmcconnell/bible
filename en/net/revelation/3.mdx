## To the Church in Philadelphia

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 22:22; Matthew 16:19; Revelation 1:18"> “To the angel of the church in Philadelphia write the following:<br />“This is the solemn pronouncement of the Holy One, the True One, who holds the key of David, who opens doors no one can shut, and shuts doors no one can open:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:9; Colossians 4:3; Revelation 3:7"> ‘I know your deeds. (Look! I have put in front of you an open door that no one can shut.) I know that you have little strength, but you have obeyed my word and have not denied my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:14; Revelation 2:9; Isaiah 49:23"> Listen! I am going to make those people from the synagogue of Satan – who say they are Jews yet are not, but are lying – Look, I will make them come and bow down at your feet and acknowledge that I have loved you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 2:9; Revelation 14:12; Zechariah 13:9"> Because you have kept my admonition to endure steadfastly, I will also keep you from the hour of testing that is about to come on the whole world to test those who live on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 1:12; Revelation 22:12; Revelation 2:10"> I am coming soon. Hold on to what you have so that no one can take away your crown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 22:4; Revelation 21:2; Revelation 14:1"> The one who conquers I will make a pillar in the temple of my God, and he will never depart from it. I will write on him the name of my God and the name of the city of my God (the new Jerusalem that comes down out of heaven from my God), and my new name as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 2:7"> The one who has an ear had better hear what the Spirit says to the churches.’</span>
</div>
</div>

## To the Church in Laodicea

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; Revelation 1:5; 2 Corinthians 1:20"> “To the angel of the church in Laodicea write the following:<br />“This is the solemn pronouncement of the Amen, the faithful and true witness, the originator of God’s creation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:11; Matthew 6:24; 1 Kings 18:21"> ‘I know your deeds, that you are neither cold nor hot. I wish you were either cold or hot!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 2:5; Zechariah 11:8–9; Jeremiah 14:19"> So because you are lukewarm, and neither hot nor cold, I am going to vomit you out of my mouth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 13:7; Hosea 12:8; Romans 7:24"> Because you say, “I am rich and have acquired great wealth, and need nothing,” but do not realize that you are wretched, pitiful, poor, blind, and naked,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; 1 Peter 1:7; Isaiah 55:1"> take my advice and buy gold from me refined by fire so you can become rich! Buy from me white clothing so you can be clothed and your shameful nakedness will not be exposed, and buy eye salve to put on your eyes so you can see!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 3:11–12; 1 Corinthians 11:32; Hebrews 12:5–11"> All those I love, I rebuke and discipline. So be earnest and repent!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 12:36–37; Revelation 19:9; John 14:21–23"> Listen! I am standing at the door and knocking! If anyone hears my voice and opens the door I will come into his home and share a meal with him, and he with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:12; Matthew 19:28; Revelation 2:26–27"> I will grant the one who conquers permission to sit with me on my throne, just as I too conquered and sat down with my Father on his throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 2:17"> The one who has an ear had better hear what the Spirit says to the churches.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
