<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:8; Jeremiah 10:25; Revelation 15:1"> Then I heard a loud voice from the temple declaring to the seven angels: “Go and pour out on the earth the seven bowls containing God’s wrath.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 8:7; Revelation 16:11; Revelation 13:15–18"> So the first angel went and poured out his bowl on the earth. Then ugly and painful sores appeared on the people who had the mark of the beast and who worshiped his image.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 7:17–21; Revelation 8:8–9; Revelation 11:6"> Next, the second angel poured out his bowl on the sea and it turned into blood, like that of a corpse, and every living creature that was in the sea died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 7:17–20; Revelation 8:10–11; Hosea 13:15"> Then the third angel poured out his bowl on the rivers and the springs of water, and they turned into blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 11:17; Revelation 1:4"> Now I heard the angel of the waters saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are just – the one who is and who was,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the Holy One – because you have passed these judgments,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Revelation 11:18; Revelation 6:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">because they poured out the blood of your saints and prophets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so you have given them blood to drink. They got what they deserved!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 19:2; Revelation 6:9; Revelation 15:3–4"> Then I heard the altar reply, “Yes, Lord God, the All-Powerful, your judgments are true and just!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:20; Revelation 14:18; Jonah 4:8"> Then the fourth angel poured out his bowl on the sun, and it was permitted to scorch people with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 2:21; Revelation 16:21; Revelation 11:13"> Thus people were scorched by the terrible heat, yet they blasphemed the name of God, who has ruling authority over these plagues, and they would not repent and give him glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 9:2; Matthew 8:12; Isaiah 8:21–22"> Then the fifth angel poured out his bowl on the throne of the beast so that darkness covered his kingdom, and people began to bite their tongues because of their pain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 16:9; Revelation 16:2; Revelation 16:21"> They blasphemed the God of heaven because of their sufferings and because of their sores, but nevertheless they still refused to repent of their deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 9:14; Jeremiah 51:36; Isaiah 44:27"> Then the sixth angel poured out his bowl on the great river Euphrates and dried up its water to prepare the way for the kings from the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 19:20; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Revelation 20:10"> Then I saw three unclean spirits that looked like frogs coming out of the mouth of the dragon, out of the mouth of the beast, and out of the mouth of the false prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; 1 Timothy 4:1; Revelation 20:8"> For they are the spirits of the demons performing signs who go out to the kings of the earth to bring them together for the battle that will take place on the great day of God, the All-Powerful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Matthew 24:42–43; 2 Peter 3:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">(Look! I will come like a thief!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Blessed is the one who stays alert and does not lose his clothes so that he will not have to walk around naked and his shameful condition be seen.)</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 12:11; Judges 5:19; Joel 3:9–14"> Now the spirits gathered the kings and their armies to the place that is called Armageddon in Hebrew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 19:30; Revelation 21:6; Ephesians 6:12"> Finally the seventh angel poured out his bowl into the air and a loud voice came out of the temple from the throne, saying: “It is done!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Revelation 4:5; Revelation 11:19"> Then there were flashes of lightning, roaring, and crashes of thunder, and there was a tremendous earthquake – an earthquake unequaled since humanity has been on the earth, so tremendous was that earthquake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 18:5; Revelation 17:18; Revelation 18:21"> The great city was split into three parts and the cities of the nations collapsed. So Babylon the great was remembered before God, and was given the cup filled with the wine made of God’s furious wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 6:14; Revelation 20:11; Jeremiah 4:23–25"> Every island fled away and no mountains could be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Joshua 10:11; Revelation 16:9"> And gigantic hailstones, weighing about a hundred pounds each, fell from heaven on people, but they blasphemed God because of the plague of hail, since it was so horrendous.</span>
