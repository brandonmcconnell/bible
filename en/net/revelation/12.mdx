## War in Heaven

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jude 1:9; Daniel 12:1; Ephesians 6:12"> Then war broke out in heaven: Michael and his angels fought against the dragon, and the dragon and his angels fought back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 37:10; Revelation 12:11; Jude 1:6"> But the dragon was not strong enough to prevail, so there was no longer any place left in heaven for him and his angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 10:18; John 12:31; Revelation 20:10"> So that huge dragon – the ancient serpent, the one called the devil and Satan, who deceives the whole world – was thrown down to the earth, and his angels along with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 22:31; Zechariah 3:1–2; Revelation 11:15"> Then I heard a loud voice in heaven saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The salvation and the power</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the kingdom of our God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the ruling authority of his Christ, have now come,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because the accuser of our brothers and sisters,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the one who accuses them day and night before our God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">has been thrown down.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:3–5; John 16:33; 1 John 5:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">But they overcame him</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">by the blood of the Lamb</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and by the word of their testimony,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they did not love their lives so much that they were afraid to die.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Revelation 8:13; Psalms 96:11–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore you heavens rejoice, and all who reside in them!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But woe to the earth and the sea</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because the devil has come down to you!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He is filled with terrible anger,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for he knows that he only has a little time!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 3:15; Revelation 12:3–5; John 16:33"> Now when the dragon realized that he had been thrown down to the earth, he pursued the woman who had given birth to the male child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Isaiah 40:31; Revelation 12:6"> But the woman was given the two wings of a giant eagle so that she could fly out into the wilderness, to the place God prepared for her, where she is taken care of – away from the presence of the serpent – for a time, times, and half a time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7; Isaiah 59:19; Psalms 18:4"> Then the serpent spouted water like a river out of his mouth after the woman in an attempt to sweep her away by a flood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 17:6; Exodus 12:35–36; 2 Kings 8:9"> but the earth came to her rescue; the ground opened up and swallowed the river that the dragon had spewed from his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:12; Genesis 3:15; Revelation 11:7"> So the dragon became enraged at the woman and went away to make war on the rest of her children, those who keep God’s commandments and hold to the testimony about Jesus. And the dragon stood on the sand of the seashore.</span>
