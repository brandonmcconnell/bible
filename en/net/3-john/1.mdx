## Introduction and Thanksgiving

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 3:18; Romans 16:23; 1 Corinthians 1:14"> From the elder, to Gaius my dear brother, whom I love in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:3–9; 2 Peter 3:18; Colossians 1:4–6"> Dear friend, I pray that all may go well with you and that you may be in good health, just as it is well with your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 John 1:4; 2 John 1:2; Psalms 119:11"> For I rejoiced greatly when the brothers came and testified to your truth, just as you are living according to the truth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 4:19; 2 Timothy 1:2; Proverbs 23:24"> I have no greater joy than this: to hear that my children are living according to the truth.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Charge to Gaius

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; Luke 12:42; Luke 16:10–12"> Dear friend, you demonstrate faithfulness by whatever you do for the brothers (even though they are strangers).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; Titus 3:13; Acts 15:3"> They have testified to your love before the church. You will do well to send them on their way in a manner worthy of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:5; Revelation 2:3; 2 Kings 5:15–16"> For they have gone forth on behalf of “The Name,” accepting nothing from the pagans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–9; 1 Corinthians 16:10–11; 2 Corinthians 7:2–3"> Therefore we ought to support such people, so that we become coworkers in cooperation with the truth.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Diotrephes the Troublemaker

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 1:7–16; Luke 22:24–27; Matthew 23:4–8"> I wrote something to the church, but Diotrephes, who loves to be first among them, does not acknowledge us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 66:5; 2 Corinthians 13:2; Luke 6:22"> Therefore, if I come, I will call attention to the deeds he is doing – the bringing of unjustified charges against us with evil words! And not being content with that, he not only refuses to welcome the brothers himself, but hinders the people who want to do so and throws them out of the church!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–17; Psalms 37:27; Ephesians 5:1"> Dear friend, do not imitate what is bad but what is good. The one who does good is of God; the one who does what is bad has not seen God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Worthy Demetrius

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; John 21:24; Acts 22:12"> Demetrius has been testified to by all, even by the truth itself. We also testify to him, and you know that our testimony is true.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Conclusion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 John 1:12"> I have many things to write to you, but I do not wish to write to you with pen and ink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 43:23; Ephesians 6:23; Romans 16:1–16"> But I hope to see you right away, and we will speak face to face. Peace be with you. The friends here greet you. Greet the friends there by name.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
