<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 25:12–13; Ezekiel 34:25; Ezekiel 37:26"> “My covenant with him was designed to bring life and peace. I gave its statutes to him to fill him with awe, and he indeed revered me and stood in awe before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; 2 Timothy 2:15–16; Revelation 14:5"> He taught what was true; sinful words were not found on his lips. He walked with me in peace and integrity, and he turned many people away from sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24–25; Leviticus 10:11; Deuteronomy 21:5"> For the lips of a priest should preserve knowledge of sacred things, and people should seek instruction from him because he is the messenger of the Lord who rules over all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:29; Jeremiah 18:15; Isaiah 30:11"> You, however, have turned from the way. You have caused many to violate the law; you have corrupted the covenant with Levi,” says the Lord who rules over all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:30; Deuteronomy 1:17; Matthew 19:17–18"> “Therefore, I have caused you to be ignored and belittled before all people to the extent to which you are not following after me and are showing partiality in your instruction.”</span>

## The Rebellion of the People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; Malachi 2:11; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> Do we not all have one father? Did not one God create us? Why do we betray one another, in this way making light of the covenant of our ancestors?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–18; Ezra 9:1–2; Nehemiah 13:23–29"> Judah has become disloyal, and unspeakable sins have been committed in Israel and Jerusalem. For Judah has profaned the holy things that the Lord loves and has turned to a foreign god!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:29; Leviticus 20:3; Ezekiel 24:21"> May the Lord cut off from the community of Jacob every last person who does this, as well as the person who presents improper offerings to the Lord who rules over all!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–15; Jeremiah 14:12; Psalms 78:34–37"> You also do this: You cover the altar of the Lord with tears as you weep and groan, because he no longer pays any attention to the offering nor accepts it favorably from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 2:15; Ecclesiastes 9:9; Isaiah 54:6"> Yet you ask, “Why?” The Lord is testifying against you on behalf of the wife you married when you were young, to whom you have become unfaithful even though she is your companion and wife by law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 19:4–6; Malachi 2:14; Mark 10:6–8"> No one who has even a small portion of the Spirit in him does this. What did our ancestor do when seeking a child from God? Be attentive, then, to your own spirit, for one should not be disloyal to the wife he took in his youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 19:3–9; Matthew 5:31–32; Mark 10:2–12"> “I hate divorce,” says the Lord God of Israel, “and the one who is guilty of violence,” says the Lord who rules over all. “Pay attention to your conscience, and do not be unfaithful.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Resistance to the Lord through Self-deceit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:24; 2 Peter 3:3–4; Ecclesiastes 8:11"> You have wearied the Lord with your words. But you say, “How have we wearied him?” Because you say, “Everyone who does evil is good in the Lord’s opinion, and he delights in them,” or “Where is the God of justice?”</span>
