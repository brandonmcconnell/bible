<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; Exodus 15:20; Micah 6:4"> His sister stationed herself at a distance to find out what would happen to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 7:21; Psalms 12:5; Psalms 9:9"> Then the daughter of Pharaoh came down to wash herself by the Nile, while her attendants were walking alongside the river, and she saw the basket among the reeds. She sent one of her attendants, took it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 8:50; 1 Peter 3:8; Acts 7:21"> opened it, and saw the child – a boy, crying! – and she felt compassion for him and said, “This is one of the Hebrews’ children.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 2:4; Numbers 26:59; Numbers 12:1"> Then his sister said to Pharaoh’s daughter, “Shall I go and get a nursing woman for you from the Hebrews, so that she may nurse the child for you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3–4; Psalms 27:10; Exodus 6:20"> Pharaoh’s daughter said to her, “Yes, do so.” So the young girl went and got the child’s mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Pharaoh’s daughter said to her, “Take this child and nurse him for me, and I will pay your wages.” So the woman took the child and nursed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24; Genesis 16:11; 1 Samuel 1:20"> When the child grew older she brought him to Pharaoh’s daughter, and he became her son. She named him Moses, saying, “Because I drew him from the water.”</span>

## The Presumption of the Deliverer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24–26; Acts 7:22–24; Exodus 1:11"> In those days, when Moses had grown up, he went out to his people and observed their hard labor, and he saw an Egyptian man attacking a Hebrew man, one of his own people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 7:24–26"> He looked this way and that and saw that no one was there, and then he attacked the Egyptian and concealed the body in the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:26–28; 1 Corinthians 6:7–8"> When he went out the next day, there were two Hebrew men fighting. So he said to the one who was in the wrong, “Why are you attacking your fellow Hebrew?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 12:14; Matthew 21:23; Genesis 19:9"> The man replied, “Who made you a ruler and a judge over us? Are you planning to kill me like you killed that Egyptian?” Then Moses was afraid, thinking, “Surely what I did has become known.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 7:29; Hebrews 11:27; Genesis 24:11"> When Pharaoh heard about this event, he sought to kill Moses. So Moses fled from Pharaoh and settled in the land of Midian, and he settled by a certain well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 24:11; Exodus 3:1; 1 Samuel 9:11"> Now a priest of Midian had seven daughters, and they came and began to draw water and fill the troughs in order to water their father’s flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 29:10; Genesis 21:25; Genesis 26:15–22"> When some shepherds came and drove them away, Moses came up and defended them and then watered their flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Numbers 10:29; Exodus 4:18"> So when they came home to their father Reuel, he asked, “Why have you come home so early today?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:11; Genesis 29:10"> They said, “An Egyptian man rescued us from the shepherds, and he actually drew water for us and watered the flock!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Genesis 43:25; Hebrews 13:2"> He said to his daughters, “So where is he? Why in the world did you leave the man? Call him, so that he may eat a meal with us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 31:38–40; Hebrews 13:5; Hebrews 11:25"> Moses agreed to stay with the man, and he gave his daughter Zipporah to Moses in marriage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13–14; Acts 7:29; Exodus 22:21"> When she bore a son, Moses named him Gershom, for he said, “I have become a resident foreigner in a foreign land.”</span>
</div>
