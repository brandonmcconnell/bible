## The Construction of the Courtyard

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 27:9–19; Psalms 89:7; 1 Kings 6:36"> He made the courtyard. For the south side the hangings of the courtyard were of fine twisted linen, one hundred fifty feet long,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> with their twenty posts and their twenty bronze bases, with the hooks of the posts and their bands of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For the north side the hangings were one hundred fifty feet, with their twenty posts and their twenty bronze bases, with the hooks of the posts and their bands of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For the west side there were hangings seventy-five feet long, with their ten posts and their ten bases, with the hooks of the posts and their bands of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For the east side, toward the sunrise, it was seventy-five feet wide,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:14"> with hangings on one side of the gate that were twenty-two and a half feet long, with their three posts and their three bases,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and for the second side of the gate of the courtyard, just like the other, the hangings were twenty-two and a half feet long, with their three posts and their three bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> All the hangings around the courtyard were of fine twisted linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The bases for the posts were bronze. The hooks of the posts and their bands were silver, their tops were overlaid with silver, and all the posts of the courtyard had silver bands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:14"> The curtain for the gate of the courtyard was of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen, the work of an embroiderer. It was thirty feet long, and like the hangings in the courtyard, it was seven and a half feet high,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> with four posts and their four bronze bases. Their hooks and their bands were silver, and their tops were overlaid with silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:9; Exodus 27:19; Ephesians 2:21–22"> All the tent pegs of the tabernacle and of the courtyard all around were bronze.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 35:30–3; Exodus 31:1–5; Psalms 119:6"> Now Bezalel son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah, made everything that the Lord had commanded Moses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 35:34; Exodus 31:6"> and with him was Oholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, an artisan, a designer, and an embroiderer in blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine linen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:12"> The silver of those who were numbered of the community was one hundred talents and 1,775 shekels, according to the sanctuary shekel,</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:19; Exodus 26:25; Exodus 26:21"> The one hundred talents of silver were used for casting the bases of the sanctuary and the bases of the special curtain – one hundred bases for one hundred talents, one talent per base.</span>
