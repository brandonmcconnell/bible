## Blessing during Bondage in Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:1–2; Genesis 46:8–26; Genesis 35:23–26"> These are the names of the sons of Israel who entered Egypt – each man with his household entered with Jacob:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 35:22"> Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 28:20; Genesis 35:23"> Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Dan and Naphtali, Gad and Asher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Genesis 46:26–27; Judges 8:30"> All the people who were directly descended from Jacob numbered seventy. But Joseph was already in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 50:26; Genesis 50:24; Acts 7:14–16"> and in time Joseph and his brothers and all that generation died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 46:3; Genesis 1:28; Deuteronomy 26:5"> The Israelites, however, were fruitful, increased greatly, multiplied, and became extremely strong, so that the land was filled with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 7:18; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Ecclesiastes 9:15"> Then a new king, who did not know about Joseph, came to power over Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 105:24–25; Ecclesiastes 4:4; James 3:14–16"> He said to his people, “Look at the Israelite people, more numerous and stronger than we are!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Psalms 83:3–4; Proverbs 1:11"> Come, let’s deal wisely with them. Otherwise they will continue to multiply, and if a war breaks out, they will ally themselves with our enemies and fight against us and leave the country.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 15:13; Genesis 47:11; Exodus 3:7"> So they put foremen over the Israelites to oppress them with hard labor. As a result they built Pithom and Rameses as store cities for Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 105:24; Exodus 1:9; Romans 8:28"> But the more the Egyptians oppressed them, the more they multiplied and spread. As a result the Egyptians loathed the Israelites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:20"> and they made the Israelites serve rigorously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Numbers 20:15; Exodus 2:23"> They made their lives bitter by hard service with mortar and bricks and by all kinds of service in the fields. Every kind of service the Israelites were required to give was rigorous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The king of Egypt said to the Hebrew midwives, one of whom was named Shiphrah and the other Puah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Matthew 21:38; Revelation 12:4"> “When you assist the Hebrew women in childbirth, observe at the delivery: If it is a son, kill him, but if it is a daughter, she may live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Exodus 1:21; Matthew 10:28"> But the midwives feared God and did not do what the king of Egypt had told them; they let the boys live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:4; 2 Samuel 13:28"> Then the king of Egypt summoned the midwives and said to them, “Why have you done this and let the boys live?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 2:4–24; 2 Samuel 17:19–20; 1 Samuel 21:2"> The midwives said to Pharaoh, “Because the Hebrew women are not like the Egyptian women – for the Hebrew women are vigorous; they give birth before the midwife gets to them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10; Ecclesiastes 8:12; Proverbs 11:18"> So God treated the midwives well, and the people multiplied and became very strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 11:38; 1 Samuel 2:35; Psalms 37:3"> And because the midwives feared God, he made households for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Proverbs 1:16; Exodus 1:16"> Then Pharaoh commanded all his people, “All sons that are born you must throw into the river, but all daughters you may let live.”</span>
