## The Building of the Tabernacle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 6:23; Exodus 26:1–37; Ezekiel 1:5–28"> All the skilled among those who were doing the work made the tabernacle with ten curtains of fine twisted linen and blue and purple and scarlet; they were made with cherubim that were the work of an artistic designer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The length of one curtain was forty-two feet, and the width of one curtain was six feet – the same size for each of the curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; Exodus 26:3; Psalms 122:3"> He joined five of the curtains to one another, and the other five curtains he joined to one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:4"> He made loops of blue material along the edge of the end curtain in the first set; he did the same along the edge of the end curtain in the second set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 26:5; Exodus 26:10"> He made fifty loops on the first curtain, and he made fifty loops on the end curtain that was in the second set, with the loops opposite one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 12:20"> He made fifty gold clasps and joined the curtains together to one another with the clasps, so that the tabernacle was a unit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–13"> He made curtains of goats’ hair for a tent over the tabernacle; he made eleven curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The length of one curtain was forty-five feet, and the width of one curtain was six feet – one size for all eleven curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He joined five curtains by themselves and six curtains by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He made fifty loops along the edge of the end curtain in the first set and fifty loops along the edge of the curtain that joined the second set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He made fifty bronze clasps to join the tent together so that it might be a unit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:14"> He made a covering for the tent out of ram skins dyed red and over that a covering of fine leather.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 25:5; Numbers 25:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> He made the frames for the tabernacle of acacia wood as uprights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The length of each frame was fifteen feet, the width of each frame was two and a quarter feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> with two projections per frame parallel one to another. He made all the frames of the tabernacle in this way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> So he made frames for the tabernacle: twenty frames for the south side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He made forty silver bases under the twenty frames – two bases under the first frame for its two projections, and likewise two bases under the next frame for its two projections,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and for the second side of the tabernacle, the north side, he made twenty frames</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and their forty silver bases, two bases under the first frame and two bases under the next frame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:22; Exodus 26:27"> And for the back of the tabernacle on the west he made six frames.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> He made two frames for the corners of the tabernacle on the back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:32; Ephesians 3:18–19; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> At the two corners they were doubled at the lower end and finished together at the top in one ring. So he did for both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:25"> So there were eight frames and their silver bases, sixteen bases, two bases under each frame.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 26:26–29; Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:5"> He made bars of acacia wood, five for the frames on one side of the tabernacle</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 26:26"> and five bars for the frames on the second side of the tabernacle, and five bars for the frames of the tabernacle for the back side on the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> He made the middle bar to reach from end to end in the center of the frames.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> He overlaid the frames with gold and made their rings of gold to provide places for the bars, and he overlaid the bars with gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 10:20; Exodus 40:21"> He made the special curtain of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen; he made it with cherubim, the work of an artistic designer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18"> He made for it four posts of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold, with gold hooks, and he cast for them four silver bases.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 26:36–37; Exodus 40:28"> He made a hanging for the entrance of the tent of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen, the work of an embroiderer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 27:10"> and its five posts and their hooks. He overlaid their tops and their bands with gold, but their five bases were bronze.</span>
</div>
</div>
