<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:10; Exodus 35:34–1; Exodus 28:3"> Moreover, I have also given him Oholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, and I have given ability to all the specially skilled, that they may make everything I have commanded you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 36:8–9; Exodus 25:10–22; Exodus 26:1–37"> the tent of meeting, the ark of the testimony, the atonement lid that is on it, all the furnishings of the tent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 37:10–28; Exodus 30:1–10; Exodus 25:23–40"> the table with its utensils, the pure lampstand with all its utensils, the altar of incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–8; Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 40:11"> the altar for the burnt offering with all its utensils, the large basin with its base,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 4:5–14; Exodus 28:1–43; Exodus 39:1–43"> the woven garments, the holy garments for Aaron the priest and the garments for his sons, to minister as priests,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Exodus 30:23–38"> the anointing oil, and sweet incense for the Holy Place. They will make all these things just as I have commanded you.”</span>

## Sabbath Observance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:12; Ezekiel 20:20; Leviticus 19:30"> “Tell the Israelites, ‘Surely you must keep my Sabbaths, for it is a sign between me and you throughout your generations, that you may know that I am the Lord who sanctifies you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 35:2–3; Numbers 15:32–36; Ezekiel 20:21"> So you must keep the Sabbath, for it is holy for you. Everyone who defiles it must surely be put to death; indeed, if anyone does any work on it, then that person will be cut off from among his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 2:2; Exodus 16:23; Leviticus 23:3"> Six days work may be done, but on the seventh day is a Sabbath of complete rest, holy to the Lord; anyone who does work on the Sabbath day must surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; Genesis 9:13; Genesis 17:11"> The Israelites must keep the Sabbath by observing the Sabbath throughout their generations as a perpetual covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:31; Exodus 31:13; Genesis 2:2–3"> It is a sign between me and the Israelites forever; for in six days the Lord made the heavens and the earth, and on the seventh day he rested and was refreshed.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:13; Exodus 32:15–16; Deuteronomy 5:22"> He gave Moses two tablets of testimony when he had finished speaking with him on Mount Sinai, tablets of stone written by the finger of God.</span>
