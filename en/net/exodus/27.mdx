<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:27; Exodus 29:12; Leviticus 4:7"> You are to make its four horns on its four corners; its horns will be part of it, and you are to overlay it with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 7:45; Numbers 4:14; 1 Chronicles 28:17"> You are to make its pots for the ashes, its shovels, its tossing bowls, its meat hooks, and its fire pans – you are to make all its utensils of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 35:16; Exodus 25:12; Exodus 38:4–5"> You are to make a grating for it, a network of bronze, and you are to make on the network four bronze rings on its four corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 38:4"> You are to put it under the ledge of the altar below, so that the network will come halfway up the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:13–15; Numbers 4:44; Exodus 30:4"> You are to make poles for the altar, poles of acacia wood, and you are to overlay them with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:4; Numbers 4:13–14"> The poles are to be put into the rings so that the poles will be on two sides of the altar when carrying it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 25:9; Hebrews 8:5"> You are to make the altar hollow, out of boards. Just as it was shown you on the mountain, so they must make it.</span>

## The Courtyard

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:9–20; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 42:3"> “You are to make the courtyard of the tabernacle. For the south side there are to be hangings for the courtyard of fine twisted linen, one hundred fifty feet long for one side,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 26:19–21; Jeremiah 52:21; Exodus 36:38"> with twenty posts and their twenty bronze bases, with the hooks of the posts and their bands of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Likewise for its length on the north side, there are to be hangings for one hundred fifty feet, with twenty posts and their twenty bronze bases, with silver hooks and bands on the posts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The width of the court on the west side is to be seventy-five feet with hangings, with their ten posts and their ten bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The width of the court on the east side, toward the sunrise, is to be seventy-five feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Exodus 26:36"> The hangings on one side of the gate are to be twenty-two and a half feet long, with their three posts and their three bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> On the second side there are to be hangings twenty-two and a half feet long, with their three posts and their three bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 26:36; Judges 5:30; Exodus 28:39"> For the gate of the courtyard there is to be a curtain of thirty feet, of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine twined linen, the work of an embroiderer, with four posts and their four bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> All the posts around the courtyard are to have silver bands; their hooks are to be silver, and their bases bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The length of the courtyard is to be one hundred fifty feet and the width seventy-five feet, and the height of the fine twisted linen hangings is to be seven and a half feet, with their bronze bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Isaiah 22:23–25; Numbers 4:32"> All the utensils of the tabernacle used in all its service, all its tent pegs, and all the tent pegs of the courtyard are to be made of bronze.</span>
</div>

## Offering the Oil

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 24:1–4; Zechariah 4:11–14; Psalms 23:5"> “You are to command the Israelites that they bring to you pure oil of pressed olives for the light, so that the lamps will burn regularly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 19:21; Numbers 18:23; 1 Samuel 3:3"> In the tent of meeting outside the curtain that is before the testimony, Aaron and his sons are to arrange it from evening to morning before the Lord. This is to be a lasting ordinance among the Israelites for generations to come.</span>
</div>
