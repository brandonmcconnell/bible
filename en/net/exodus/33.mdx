## The Presence of the Lord

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6–7; Exodus 29:42–43; Proverbs 15:29"> Moses took the tent and pitched it outside the camp, at a good distance from the camp, and he called it the tent of meeting. Anyone seeking the Lord would go out to the tent of meeting that was outside the camp.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 16:27"> And when Moses went out to the tent, all the people would get up and stand at the entrance to their tents and watch Moses until he entered the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 99:7; Exodus 31:18; Exodus 13:21–22"> And whenever Moses entered the tent, the pillar of cloud would descend and stand at the entrance of the tent, and the Lord would speak with Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 8:22; 1 Kings 8:14; Exodus 4:31"> When all the people would see the pillar of cloud standing at the entrance of the tent, all the people, each one at the entrance of his own tent, would rise and worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 12:8; Deuteronomy 34:10; Exodus 24:13"> The Lord would speak to Moses face to face, the way a person speaks to a friend. Then Moses would return to the camp, but his servant, Joshua son of Nun, a young man, did not leave the tent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 33:17; Jeremiah 1:5; John 10:14–15"> Moses said to the Lord, “See, you have been saying to me, ‘Bring this people up,’ but you have not let me know whom you will send with me. But you said, ‘I know you by name, and also you have found favor in my sight.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 119:33; Psalms 25:4; Psalms 27:11"> Now if I have found favor in your sight, show me your way, that I may know you, that I may continue to find favor in your sight. And see that this nation is your people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 21:44; Matthew 11:28; Joshua 1:5"> And the Lord said, “My presence will go with you, and I will give you rest.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:9; Psalms 4:6; Exodus 33:3"> And Moses said to him, “If your presence does not go with us, do not take us up from here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:7; Numbers 14:14; 1 Kings 8:53"> For how will it be known then that I have found favor in your sight, I and your people? Is it not by your going with us, so that we will be distinguished, I and your people, from all the people who are on the face of the earth?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; 1 John 5:14–15; Exodus 33:12"> The Lord said to Moses, “I will do this thing also that you have requested, for you have found favor in my sight, and I know you by name.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:23; Exodus 33:20; Titus 2:13"> And Moses said, “Show me your glory.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 9:15–18; Ephesians 1:6–8; Jeremiah 31:14"> And the Lord said, “I will make all my goodness pass before your face, and I will proclaim the Lord by name before you; I will be gracious to whom I will be gracious, I will show mercy to whom I will show mercy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 32:30; John 1:18; 1 Timothy 6:16"> But he added, “You cannot see my face, for no one can see me and live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:31; Zechariah 3:7; Joshua 20:4"> The Lord said, “Here is a place by me; you will station yourself on a rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 91:1; Isaiah 2:21; Psalms 91:4"> When my glory passes by, I will put you in a cleft in the rock and will cover you with my hand while I pass by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 33:20; John 1:18; 1 Timothy 6:16"> Then I will take away my hand, and you will see my back, but my face must not be seen.”</span>
</div>
