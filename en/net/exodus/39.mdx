## The Ephod

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 28:6–12; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 25:7"> He made the ephod of gold, blue, purple, scarlet, and fine twisted linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 36:8; Exodus 26:1"> They hammered the gold into thin sheets and cut it into narrow strips to weave them into the blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and into the fine linen, the work of an artistic designer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> They made shoulder pieces for it, attached to two of its corners, so it could be joined together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Matthew 28:20; Isaiah 11:5"> The artistically woven waistband of the ephod that was on it was like it, of one piece with it, of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 28:9; Ezekiel 28:13; Job 28:16"> They set the onyx stones in gold filigree settings, engraved as with the engravings of a seal with the names of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 4:7; Exodus 28:12; Exodus 28:29"> He put them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod as stones of memorial for the Israelites, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Breastpiece of Decision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Leviticus 8:8–9; Isaiah 59:17"> He made the breastpiece, the work of an artistic designer, in the same fashion as the ephod, of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet, and fine twisted linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> It was square – they made the breastpiece doubled, nine inches long and nine inches wide when doubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 28:21; Exodus 28:16–17; Revelation 21:19–21"> They set on it four rows of stones: a row with a ruby, a topaz, and a beryl – the first row;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 28:18; Ezekiel 28:13"> and the second row, a turquoise, a sapphire, and an emerald;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and the third row, a jacinth, an agate, and an amethyst;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and the fourth row, a chrysolite, an onyx, and a jasper. They were enclosed in gold filigree settings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:12"> The stones were for the names of the sons of Israel, twelve, corresponding to the number of their names. Each name corresponding to one of the twelve tribes was like the engravings of a seal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 10:28; John 17:12; 1 Peter 1:5"> They made for the breastpiece braided chains like cords of pure gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:12"> and they made two gold filigree settings and two gold rings, and they attached the two rings to the upper two ends of the breastpiece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> They attached the two gold chains to the two rings at the ends of the breastpiece;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 28:14; Song of Solomon 1:10"> the other two ends of the two chains they attached to the two settings, and they attached them to the shoulder pieces of the ephod at the front of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> They made two rings of gold and put them on the other two ends of the breastpiece on its edge, which is on the inner side of the ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 26:3"> They made two more gold rings and attached them to the bottom of the two shoulder pieces on the front of the ephod, close to the juncture above the waistband of the ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; 1 Corinthians 1:25; 1 Corinthians 1:27"> They tied the breastpiece by its rings to the rings of the ephod by blue cord, so that it was above the waistband of the ephod, so that the breastpiece would not be loose from the ephod, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Other Garments

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 28:31–35"> He made the robe of the ephod completely blue, the work of a weaver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> There was an opening in the center of the robe, like the opening of a collar, with an edge all around the opening so that it could not be torn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 28:33; Galatians 5:22"> They made pomegranates of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and twisted linen around the hem of the robe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 28:33–34; Song of Solomon 4:13; Psalms 89:15"> They made bells of pure gold and attached the bells between the pomegranates around the hem of the robe between the pomegranates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 28:34; Song of Solomon 4:13; Song of Solomon 6:7"> There was a bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate, all around the hem of the robe, to be used in ministering, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:18; 1 Peter 1:13; Isaiah 61:10"> They made tunics of fine linen – the work of a weaver, for Aaron and for his sons –</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Ezekiel 44:18; Exodus 28:39"> and the turban of fine linen, the headbands of fine linen, and the undergarments of fine twisted linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 28:39"> The sash was of fine twisted linen and blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, the work of an embroiderer, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Hebrews 7:26; Hebrews 1:3"> They made a plate, the holy diadem, of pure gold and wrote on it an inscription, as on the engravings of a seal, “Holiness to the Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> They attached to it a blue cord, to attach it to the turban above, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 35:11–19; Exodus 31:7–11; Exodus 36:1–38"> They brought the tabernacle to Moses, the tent and all its furnishings, clasps, frames, bars, posts, and bases;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> and the coverings of ram skins dyed red, the covering of fine leather, and the protecting curtain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 25:17; Hebrews 9:8"> the ark of the testimony and its poles, and the atonement lid;</span>

<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Exodus 27:21; Matthew 5:14–16"> the pure lampstand, its lamps, with the lamps set in order, and all its accessories, and oil for the light;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:7; Exodus 37:29; Exodus 30:3"> and the gold altar, and the anointing oil, and the fragrant incense; and the curtain for the entrance to the tent;</span>
