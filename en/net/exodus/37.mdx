<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:14; Ezekiel 40:39–42; Malachi 1:12"> He made the table of acacia wood; its length was three feet, its width one foot six inches, and its height two feet three inches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> He overlaid it with pure gold, and he made a surrounding border of gold for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> He made a surrounding frame for it about three inches wide, and he made a surrounding border of gold for its frame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> He cast four gold rings for it and attached the rings at the four corners where its four legs were.</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Six branches were extending from its sides, three branches of the lampstand from one side of it, and three branches of the lampstand from the other side of it.</span>

## The Making of the Altar of Incense

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> He overlaid it with pure gold – its top, its four walls, and its horns – and he made a surrounding border of gold for it.</span>
