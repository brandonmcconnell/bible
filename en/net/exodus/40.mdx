<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the Lord spoke to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 26:30; Exodus 13:4; Numbers 7:1"> “On the first day of the first month you are to set up the tabernacle, the tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:12; Numbers 4:5; Revelation 11:19"> You are to place the ark of the testimony in it and shield the ark with the special curtain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 24:8; Exodus 40:22–25; Exodus 37:10–24"> You are to bring in the table and set out the things that belong on it; then you are to bring in the lampstand and set up its lamps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Hebrews 9:24; 1 John 2:1–2"> You are to put the gold altar for incense in front of the ark of the testimony and put the curtain at the entrance to the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 40:29; 1 John 4:9–10; Hebrews 13:10"> You are to put the altar for the burnt offering in front of the entrance to the tabernacle, the tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; 1 John 1:7; Psalms 26:6"> You are to put the large basin between the tent of meeting and the altar and put water in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 40:33; Ephesians 4:11–12; Matthew 16:18"> You are to set up the courtyard around it and put the curtain at the gate of the courtyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 8:10; Numbers 7:1; Matthew 3:16"> And take the anointing oil, and anoint the tabernacle and all that is in it, and sanctify it and all its furnishings, and it will be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 29:36–37; Luke 1:35; Isaiah 61:1"> Then you are to anoint the altar for the burnt offering with all its utensils; you are to sanctify the altar, and it will be the most holy altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> You must also anoint the large basin and its pedestal, and you are to sanctify it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 8:1–13; Leviticus 9:1–24; Exodus 29:1–35"> “You are to bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the tent of meeting and wash them with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 28:41; 1 John 2:20; 1 John 2:27"> Then you are to clothe Aaron with the holy garments and anoint him and sanctify him so that he may minister as my priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Romans 8:30; 1 Corinthians 1:30"> You are to bring his sons and clothe them with tunics</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 25:13; Hebrews 5:1–14; Hebrews 8:1–10"> and anoint them just as you anointed their father, so that they may minister as my priests; their anointing will make them a priesthood that will continue throughout their generations.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Exodus 40:17–32; Deuteronomy 12:32"> This is what Moses did, according to all the Lord had commanded him – so he did.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 7:1; Exodus 40:1–2; Numbers 9:1"> So the tabernacle was set up on the first day of the first month, in the second year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27–28; 1 Peter 1:5; Leviticus 26:11"> When Moses set up the tabernacle and put its bases in place, he set up its frames, attached its bars, and set up its posts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:1–14; Exodus 36:8–19"> Then he spread the tent over the tabernacle and put the covering of the tent over it, as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:34; Deuteronomy 10:5; Hebrews 10:19–21"> He took the testimony and put it in the ark, attached the poles to the ark, and then put the atonement lid on the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:33; Exodus 40:3; Exodus 35:12"> And he brought the ark into the tabernacle, hung the protecting curtain, and shielded the ark of the testimony from view, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 26:35; John 6:53–57; Ephesians 3:8"> And he put the table in the tent of meeting, on the north side of the tabernacle, outside the curtain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; Exodus 40:4; Hebrews 9:2"> And he set the bread in order on it before the Lord, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 26:35; Revelation 2:5; John 1:1"> And he put the lampstand in the tent of meeting opposite the table, on the south side of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 40:4; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 4:5"> Then he set up the lamps before the Lord, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Hebrews 7:25; John 11:42"> And he put the gold altar in the tent of meeting in front of the curtain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 30:7"> and he burned fragrant incense on it, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Hebrews 10:19–20; Ephesians 2:18"> Then he put the curtain at the entrance to the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 40:6; Hebrews 13:5–6; Hebrews 13:10"> He also put the altar for the burnt offering by the entrance to the tabernacle, the tent of meeting, and offered on it the burnt offering and the meal offering, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 40:7; Exodus 38:8; Ezekiel 36:25"> Then he put the large basin between the tent of meeting and the altar and put water in it for washing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 30:19–20; Psalms 26:6; John 13:10"> Moses and Aaron and his sons would wash their hands and their feet from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 73:19; Exodus 30:19–20; Exodus 40:19"> Whenever they entered the tent of meeting, and whenever they approached the altar, they would wash, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 40:8; Exodus 27:9–16; John 17:4"> And he set up the courtyard around the tabernacle and the altar, and put the curtain at the gate of the courtyard. So Moses finished the work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 15:8; Numbers 9:15–23; 2 Chronicles 7:2"> Then the cloud covered the tent of meeting, and the glory of the Lord filled the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 8:11; Leviticus 16:2; 2 Chronicles 7:2"> Moses was not able to enter the tent of meeting because the cloud settled on it and the glory of the Lord filled the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:19; Numbers 9:17; Exodus 13:21–22"> But when the cloud was lifted up from the tabernacle, the Israelites would set out on all their journeys;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 9:19–22; Psalms 31:15"> but if the cloud was not lifted up, then they would not journey further until the day it was lifted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 78:14; Numbers 9:15; Exodus 13:21"> For the cloud of the Lord was on the tabernacle by day, but fire would be on it at night, in plain view of all the house of Israel, throughout all their journeys.</span>
</div>
