## Encouragement in Persecution

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 1:28; Acts 14:22; Luke 20:35"> This is evidence of God’s righteous judgment, to make you worthy of the kingdom of God, for which in fact you are suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; Colossians 3:25; Psalms 94:20–23"> For it is right for God to repay with affliction those who afflict you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Matthew 16:27; Jude 1:14–15"> and to you who are being afflicted to give rest together with us when the Lord Jesus is revealed from heaven with his mighty angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17; 2 Peter 3:7; Hebrews 10:27"> With flaming fire he will mete out punishment on those who do not know God and do not obey the gospel of our Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Revelation 14:10–11; Matthew 25:46"> They will undergo the penalty of eternal destruction, away from the presence of the Lord and from the glory of his strength,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 1:18; 1 Corinthians 3:13; John 17:10"> when he comes to be glorified among his saints and admired on that day among all who have believed – and you did in fact believe our testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Colossians 1:9–13; 1 Thessalonians 1:3"> And in this regard we pray for you always, that our God will make you worthy of his calling and fulfill by his power your every desire for goodness and every work of faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:10; 1 Peter 4:14; John 17:10"> that the name of our Lord Jesus may be glorified in you, and you in him, according to the grace of our God and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
