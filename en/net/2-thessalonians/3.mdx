## Response to the Undisciplined

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:10–11; 2 Thessalonians 3:7; Romans 16:17"> But we command you, brothers and sisters, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to keep away from any brother who lives an undisciplined life and not according to the tradition they received from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:6; 2 Thessalonians 3:9; 1 Corinthians 4:16"> For you know yourselves how you must imitate us, because we did not behave without discipline among you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:11; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; Ephesians 4:28"> and we did not eat anyone’s food without paying. Instead, in toil and drudgery we worked night and day in order not to burden any of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7; 1 Thessalonians 2:6; 1 Corinthians 9:4–14"> It was not because we do not have that right, but to give ourselves as an example for you to imitate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 20:4"> For even when we were with you, we used to give you this command: “If anyone is not willing to work, neither should he eat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:13; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Thessalonians 4:11"> For we hear that some among you are living an undisciplined life, not doing their own work but meddling in the work of others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:28; 1 Thessalonians 4:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:8"> Now such people we command and urge in the Lord Jesus Christ to work quietly and so provide their own food to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 6:9–10; Hebrews 12:3; 2 Corinthians 4:1"> But you, brothers and sisters, do not grow weary in doing what is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Corinthians 5:11"> But if anyone does not obey our message through this letter, take note of him and do not associate closely with him, so that he may be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Corinthians 5:5"> Yet do not regard him as an enemy, but admonish him as a brother.</span>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 29:11; John 16:33; Matthew 1:23"> Now may the Lord of peace himself give you peace at all times and in every way. The Lord be with you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:21; 2 Thessalonians 1:5; Colossians 4:18"> I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand, which is how I write in every letter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Romans 16:23"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all.</span>
