<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Galatians 5:17; Romans 7:23"> Where do the conflicts and where do the quarrels among you come from? Is it not from this, from your passions that battle inside you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 16:24; Matthew 7:7–8; James 1:5"> You desire and you do not have; you murder and envy and you cannot obtain; you quarrel and fight. You do not have because you do not ask;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 5:14; 1 John 3:22; James 1:6–7"> you ask and do not receive because you ask wrongly, so you can spend it on your passions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Romans 8:7; John 15:19"> Adulterers, do you not know that friendship with the world means hostility toward God? So whoever decides to be the world’s friend makes himself God’s enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 6:19; Numbers 11:29"> Or do you think the scripture means nothing when it says, “The spirit that God caused to live within us has an envious yearning”?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 3:34; 1 Peter 5:5"> But he gives greater grace. Therefore it says, “God opposes the proud, but he gives grace to the humble.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–9; Ephesians 4:27; Ephesians 6:11–12"> So submit to God. But resist the devil and he will flee from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; Hebrews 10:22; Malachi 3:7"> Draw near to God and he will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners, and make your hearts pure, you double-minded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10–11; Matthew 5:4; Luke 6:25"> Grieve, mourn, and weep. Turn your laughter into mourning and your joy into despair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 147:6; Matthew 23:12; 1 Peter 5:6"> Humble yourselves before the Lord and he will exalt you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 2:1; Romans 2:1; James 5:9"> Do not speak against one another, brothers and sisters. He who speaks against a fellow believer or judges a fellow believer speaks against the law and judges the law. But if you judge the law, you are not a doer of the law but its judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 14:4; Romans 2:1; Matthew 10:28"> But there is only one who is lawgiver and judge – the one who is able to save and destroy. On the other hand, who are you to judge your neighbor?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Luke 12:17–20; Genesis 11:3–4"> Come now, you who say, “Today or tomorrow we will go into this or that town and spend a year there and do business and make a profit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; Psalms 39:5; 1 John 2:17"> You do not know about tomorrow. What is your life like? For you are a puff of smoke that appears for a short time and then vanishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Hebrews 6:3; Lamentations 3:37"> You ought to say instead, “If the Lord is willing, then we will live and do this or that.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; 1 Corinthians 5:6; Psalms 52:7"> But as it is, you boast in your arrogance. All such boasting is evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:41; John 13:17; John 15:22"> So whoever knows what is good to do and does not do it is guilty of sin.</span>
</div>
