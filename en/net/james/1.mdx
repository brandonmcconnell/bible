## Joy in Trials

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 1:6–8; James 1:12; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> My brothers and sisters, consider it nothing but joy when you fall into all sorts of trials,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 5:3–4; Hebrews 10:36; Colossians 1:11"> because you know that the testing of your faith produces endurance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:19; 1 Peter 5:10; Matthew 5:48"> And let endurance have its perfect effect, so that you will be perfect and complete, not deficient in anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 2:3–6; John 15:7; Proverbs 3:5–7"> But if anyone is deficient in wisdom, he should ask God, who gives to all generously and without reprimand, and it will be given to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:21–22; Hebrews 11:6; Mark 11:22–24"> But he must ask in faith without doubting, for the one who doubts is like a wave of the sea, blown and tossed around by the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 4:3; Isaiah 58:3–4; Proverbs 21:27"> For that person must not suppose that he will receive anything from the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; Matthew 6:24; James 4:8"> since he is a double-minded individual, unstable in all his ways.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; James 2:5–6; Proverbs 19:1"> Now the believer of humble means should take pride in his high position.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; 1 John 2:17; Job 14:2"> But the rich person’s pride should be in his humiliation, because he will pass away like a wildflower in the meadow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7–8; Psalms 37:35–36; Psalms 49:6–14"> For the sun rises with its heat and dries up the meadow; the petal of the flower falls off and its beauty is lost forever. So also the rich person in the midst of his pursuits will wither away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 1 Peter 1:6–8; Revelation 2:10"> Happy is the one who endures testing, because when he has proven to be genuine, he will receive the crown of life that God promised to those who love him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:12–13; James 1:12; Romans 9:19–20"> Let no one say when he is tempted, “I am tempted by God,” for God cannot be tempted by evil, and he himself tempts no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 7:21–22; Ephesians 4:22; Matthew 15:18"> But each one is tempted when he is lured and enticed by his own desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Job 15:35; Romans 5:12–21"> Then when desire conceives, it gives birth to sin, and when sin is full grown, it gives birth to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 6:7; James 1:19; Philippians 2:12"> Do not be led astray, my dear brothers and sisters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 3:27; Matthew 7:11; Hebrews 13:8"> All generous giving and every perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of lights, with whom there is no variation or the slightest hint of change.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:29–31; 1 Peter 1:23; John 3:3–5"> By his sovereign plan he gave us birth through the message of truth, that we would be a kind of firstfruits of all he created.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Living Out the Message

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 17:27; Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 14:29"> Understand this, my dear brothers and sisters! Let every person be quick to listen, slow to speak, slow to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24–25; Ephesians 4:26; James 3:17–18"> For human anger does not accomplish God’s righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 4:22; Ephesians 1:13; 1 Peter 2:11"> So put away all filth and evil excess and humbly welcome the message implanted within you, which is able to save your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 2:13; Luke 6:46–48; John 13:17"> But be sure you live out the message and do not merely listen to it and so deceive yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 7:26–27; Ezekiel 33:31–32; James 2:14–26"> For if someone merely listens to the message and does not live it out, he is like someone who gazes at his own face in a mirror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Peter 3:11; Luke 1:66; Luke 7:39"> For he gazes at himself and then goes out and immediately forgets what sort of person he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:28; John 13:17; 2 Corinthians 3:17–18"> But the one who peers into the perfect law of liberty and fixes his attention there, and does not become a forgetful listener but one who lives it out – he will be blessed in what he does.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Psalms 141:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> If someone thinks he is religious yet does not bridle his tongue, and so deceives his heart, his religion is futile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–17; 1 John 3:17–19; Galatians 6:9–10"> Pure and undefiled religion before God the Father is this: to care for orphans and widows in their misfortune and to keep oneself unstained by the world.</span>
</div>
</div>
