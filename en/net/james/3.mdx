<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 32:9; Psalms 39:1; James 1:26"> And if we put bits into the mouths of horses to get them to obey us, then we guide their entire bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 107:25–27; Matthew 8:24; Acts 27:14–38"> Look at ships too: Though they are so large and driven by harsh winds, they are steered by a tiny rudder wherever the pilot’s inclination directs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:21; Jeremiah 9:3–8; Proverbs 12:18"> So too the tongue is a small part of the body, yet it has great pretensions. Think how small a flame sets a huge forest ablaze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 120:2–3; Proverbs 16:27; Mark 7:15"> And the tongue is a fire! The tongue represents the world of wrongdoing among the parts of our bodies. It pollutes the entire body and sets fire to the course of human existence – and is set on fire by hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 5:4"> For every kind of animal, bird, reptile, and sea creature is subdued and has been subdued by humankind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalms 140:3; Psalms 55:21"> But no human being can subdue the tongue; it is a restless evil, full of deadly poison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Genesis 1:26–27; Psalms 62:4"> With it we bless the Lord and Father, and with it we curse people made in God’s image.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:14; 1 Peter 3:9; 1 Corinthians 3:3"> From the same mouth come blessing and cursing. These things should not be so, my brothers and sisters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> A spring does not pour out fresh water and bitter water from the same opening, does it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:43–44; Matthew 12:33; Matthew 7:16–20"> Can a fig tree produce olives, my brothers and sisters, or a vine produce figs? Neither can a salt water spring produce fresh water.</span>

## True Wisdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; James 3:17; Matthew 5:5"> Who is wise and understanding among you? By his good conduct he should show his works done in the gentleness that wisdom brings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 3:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4; Philippians 2:3"> But if you have bitter jealousy and selfishness in your hearts, do not boast and tell lies against the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> Such wisdom does not come from above but is earthly, natural, demonic.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 3:14; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> For where there is jealousy and selfishness, there is disorder and every evil practice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 5:22–23; Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> But the wisdom from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, accommodating, full of mercy and good fruit, impartial, and not hypocritical.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Hosea 10:12; Philippians 1:11"> And the fruit that consists of righteousness is planted in peace among those who make peace.</span>
