## Faith and Works Together

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 7:26–27; Matthew 7:21–23; James 1:22–25"> What good is it, my brothers and sisters, if someone claims to have faith but does not have works? Can this kind of faith save him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 3:11; Matthew 25:35–40; Isaiah 58:7"> If a brother or sister is poorly clothed and lacks daily food,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 3:27–28; 1 John 3:16–18; Matthew 25:42–45"> and one of you says to them, “Go in peace, keep warm and eat well,” but you do not give them what the body needs, what good is it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 2:26; 2 Peter 1:5–9; James 2:19–20"> So also faith, if it does not have works, is dead being by itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 3:13; James 2:14; Galatians 5:6"> But someone will say, “You have faith and I have works.” Show me your faith without works and I will show you faith by my works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 4:34; Mark 1:24; Matthew 8:29"> You believe that God is one; well and good. Even the demons believe that – and tremble with fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 2:17; James 2:26; Proverbs 12:11"> But would you like evidence, you empty fellow, that faith without works is useless?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 22:16–18; Genesis 22:9–12; John 8:39"> Was not Abraham our father justified by works when he offered Isaac his son on the altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:3; Hebrews 11:17–19; Galatians 5:6"> You see that his faith was working together with his works and his faith was perfected by works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Isaiah 41:8; 2 Chronicles 20:7"> And the scripture was fulfilled that says, “Now Abraham believed God and it was counted to him for righteousness,” and he was called God’s friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 2:21–22; James 2:15–18; Psalms 60:12"> You see that a person is justified by works and not by faith alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Joshua 6:22–25; Joshua 2:19–21"> And similarly, was not Rahab the prostitute also justified by works when she welcomed the messengers and sent them out by another way?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 2:20; James 2:17; James 2:14"> For just as the body without the spirit is dead, so also faith without works is dead.</span>
