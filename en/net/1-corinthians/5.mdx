<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:14; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> But now I am writing to you not to associate with anyone who calls himself a Christian who is sexually immoral, or greedy, or an idolater, or verbally abusive, or a drunkard, or a swindler.
