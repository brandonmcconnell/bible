## Different Members in One Body

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 12:4–5; Colossians 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> For just as the body is one and yet has many members, and all the members of the body – though many – are one body, so too is Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5; Colossians 3:11; Galatians 3:28"> For in one Spirit we were all baptized into one body. Whether Jews or Greeks or slaves or free, we were all made to drink of the one Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27–28; 1 Corinthians 12:12; Ephesians 4:25"> For in fact the body is not a single member, but many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; 2 Kings 14:9"> If the foot says, “Since I am not a hand, I am not part of the body,” it does not lose its membership in the body because of that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:22; Philippians 2:3"> And if the ear says, “Since I am not an eye, I am not part of the body,” it does not lose its membership in the body because of that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:21; Psalms 139:13–16; Proverbs 20:12"> If the whole body were an eye, what part would do the hearing? If the whole were an ear, what part would exercise the sense of smell?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:28; Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> But as a matter of fact, God has placed each of the members in the body just as he decided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> If they were all the same member, where would the body be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:14; 1 Corinthians 12:12"> So now there are many members, but one body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 10:1–5; Nehemiah 4:16–21; Job 29:11"> The eye cannot say to the hand, “I do not need you,” nor in turn can the head say to the foot, “I do not need you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 14:28; Ecclesiastes 9:14–15; Ecclesiastes 4:9–12"> On the contrary, those members that seem to be weaker are essential,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 3:21; Genesis 3:7"> and those members we consider less honorable we clothe with greater honor, and our unpresentable members are clothed with dignity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 3:11; Genesis 2:25"> but our presentable members do not need this. Instead, God has blended together the body, giving greater honor to the lesser member,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; 1 Corinthians 1:10–12; John 17:21–26"> so that there may be no division in the body, but the members may have mutual concern for one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Galatians 6:2; Hebrews 13:3"> If one member suffers, everyone suffers with it. If a member is honored, all rejoice with it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 4:12; Colossians 1:24; Ephesians 5:30"> Now you are Christ’s body, and each of you is a member of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–13; Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 12:18"> And God has placed in the church first apostles, second prophets, third teachers, then miracles, gifts of healing, helps, gifts of leadership, different kinds of tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 12:14–20"> Not all are apostles, are they? Not all are prophets, are they? Not all are teachers, are they? Not all perform miracles, do they?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10"> Not all have gifts of healing, do they? Not all speak in tongues, do they? Not all interpret, do they?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:1; 1 Corinthians 14:39; Matthew 5:6"> But you should be eager for the greater gifts.<br />And now I will show you a way that is beyond comparison.</span>
</div>
