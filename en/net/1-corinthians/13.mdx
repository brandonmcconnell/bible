<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; 1 Peter 4:8; Galatians 5:22"> If I speak in the tongues of men and of angels, but I do not have love, I am a noisy gong or a clanging cymbal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:8; 1 Corinthians 13:3; 1 John 4:20–21"> And if I have prophecy, and know all mysteries and all knowledge, and if I have all faith so that I can remove mountains, but do not have love, I am nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 2:14–17; John 15:13; Matthew 23:5"> If I give away everything I own, and if I give over my body in order to boast, but do not have love, I receive no benefit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; Ephesians 4:32; Galatians 5:21–22"> Love is patient, love is kind, it is not envious. Love does not brag, it is not puffed up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Philippians 2:3–5; 1 John 3:16–17"> It is not rude, it is not self-serving, it is not easily angered or resentful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 12:9; 3 John 1:3–4; 2 John 1:4"> It is not glad about injustice, but rejoices in the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4; 2 Timothy 2:24"> It bears all things, believes all things, hopes all things, endures all things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:13; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Galatians 5:6"> Love never ends. But if there are prophecies, they will be set aside; if there are tongues, they will cease; if there is knowledge, it will be set aside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 2:2–3; Ephesians 3:18–19; 1 Corinthians 2:9"> For we know in part, and we prophesy in part,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:12; Isaiah 60:19–20; 2 Corinthians 5:7–8"> but when what is perfect comes, the partial will be set aside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 3:1–2; Galatians 4:1"> When I was a child, I talked like a child, I thought like a child, I reasoned like a child. But when I became an adult, I set aside childish ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 3:2; 2 Corinthians 3:18; 1 Corinthians 13:9–10"> For now we see in a mirror indirectly, but then we will see face to face. Now I know in part, but then I will know fully, just as I have been fully known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:14; Colossians 3:14; 1 John 4:7–18"> And now these three remain: faith, hope, and love. But the greatest of these is love.</span>
