<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Luke 12:42; Luke 16:10–12"> Now what is sought in stewards is that one be found faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 7:24; 1 Corinthians 2:15; 1 Samuel 16:7"> So for me, it is a minor matter that I am judged by you or by any human court. In fact, I do not even judge myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 143:2; 1 John 3:20–21; Psalms 130:3"> For I am not aware of anything against myself, but I am not acquitted because of this. The one who judges me is the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:7; 2 Corinthians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> So then, do not judge anything before the time. Wait until the Lord comes. He will bring to light the hidden things of darkness and reveal the motives of hearts. Then each will receive recognition from God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:6; 2 Corinthians 11:4; 2 Corinthians 12:19"> I have applied these things to myself and Apollos because of you, brothers and sisters, so that through us you may learn “not to go beyond what is written,” so that none of you will be puffed up in favor of the one against the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 3:27; 1 Peter 4:10; James 1:17"> For who concedes you any superiority? What do you have that you did not receive? And if you received it, why do you boast as though you did not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 6:25; Proverbs 13:7; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> Already you are satisfied! Already you are rich! You have become kings without us! I wish you had become kings so that we could reign with you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:33; Romans 8:36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–12"> For, I think, God has exhibited us apostles last of all, as men condemned to die, because we have become a spectacle to the world, both to angels and to people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:3; Acts 26:24; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> We are fools for Christ, but you are wise in Christ! We are weak, but you are strong! You are distinguished, we are dishonored!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:35; 2 Corinthians 11:23–27; Philippians 4:12"> To the present hour we are hungry and thirsty, poorly clothed, brutally treated, and without a roof over our heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Matthew 5:11; 1 Peter 4:12–14"> We do hard work, toiling with our own hands. When we are verbally abused, we respond with a blessing, when persecuted, we endure,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Lamentations 3:45; Acts 22:22"> when people lie about us, we answer in a friendly manner. We are the world’s dirt and scum, even now.</span>
</div>

## A Father’s Warning

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Corinthians 4:15; 3 John 1:4"> I am not writing these things to shame you, but to correct you as my dear children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:19; 1 Corinthians 3:10; 1 Corinthians 9:14"> For though you may have ten thousand guardians in Christ, you do not have many fathers, because I became your father in Christ Jesus through the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Thessalonians 3:9; Philippians 3:17"> I encourage you, then, be imitators of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:10; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> For this reason, I have sent Timothy to you, who is my dear and faithful son in the Lord. He will remind you of my ways in Christ, as I teach them everywhere in every church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:6–8; 1 Corinthians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 4:21"> Some have become arrogant, as if I were not coming to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:1–4; Acts 18:21; Acts 19:21"> But I will come to you soon, if the Lord is willing, and I will find out not only the talk of these arrogant people, but also their power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4; 1 Thessalonians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5"> For the kingdom of God is demonstrated not in idle talk but with power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:2; 2 Corinthians 13:10; 1 Thessalonians 2:7"> What do you want? Shall I come to you with a rod of discipline or with love and a spirit of gentleness?</span>
