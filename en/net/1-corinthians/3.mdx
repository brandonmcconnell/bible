<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 5:13; 1 Corinthians 2:14–15; Ephesians 4:13–14"> So, brothers and sisters, I could not speak to you as spiritual people, but instead as people of the flesh, as infants in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 5:11–14; John 16:12; 1 Peter 2:2"> I fed you milk, not solid food, for you were not yet ready. In fact, you are still not ready,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:16; Galatians 5:15; Titus 3:3"> for you are still influenced by the flesh. For since there is still jealousy and dissension among you, are you not influenced by the flesh and behaving like unregenerate people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 4:6"> For whenever someone says, “I am with Paul,” or “I am with Apollos,” are you not merely human?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:4; 2 Corinthians 6:1; 1 Peter 4:10"> What is Apollos, really? Or what is Paul? Servants through whom you came to believe, and each of us in the ministry the Lord gave us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9–10; Proverbs 11:25; 2 Corinthians 10:14–15"> I planted, Apollos watered, but God caused it to grow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:5; Galatians 6:3; Psalms 115:1"> So neither the one who plants counts for anything, nor the one who waters, but God who causes the growth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 2:6; Matthew 16:27; 2 John 1:8"> The one who plants and the one who waters work as one, but each will receive his reward according to his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> We are coworkers belonging to God. You are God’s field, God’s building.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:11; Ephesians 2:20; Romans 15:20"> According to the grace of God given to me, like a skilled master-builder I laid a foundation, but someone else builds on it. And each one must be careful how he builds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20; Isaiah 28:16; 2 Timothy 2:19"> For no one can lay any foundation other than what is being laid, which is Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3; 2 Timothy 3:7; 2 Timothy 2:20"> If anyone builds on the foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, or straw,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 1:7; 1 Corinthians 3:14–15; Revelation 20:12"> each builder’s work will be plainly seen, for the Day will make it clear, because it will be revealed by fire. And the fire will test what kind of work each has done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:8; Matthew 25:21–23; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> If what someone has built survives, he will receive a reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:12–13; Jude 1:23"> If someone’s work is burned up, he will suffer loss. He himself will be saved, but only as through fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; Ezekiel 36:27; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> Do you not know that you are God’s temple and that God’s Spirit lives in you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:18–20; Ezekiel 43:12; Numbers 19:20"> If someone destroys God’s temple, God will destroy him. For God’s temple is holy, which is what you are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Isaiah 5:21; Galatians 6:3"> Guard against self-deception, each of you. If someone among you thinks he is wise in this age, let him become foolish so that he can become wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 5:13; Psalms 141:10; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> For the wisdom of this age is foolishness with God. As it is written, “He catches the wise in their craftiness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 94:11; Colossians 2:8; Romans 1:21"> And again, “The Lord knows that the thoughts of the wise are futile.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Romans 8:28; Jeremiah 9:23–24"> So then, no more boasting about mere mortals! For everything belongs to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–12; Philippians 1:21; 1 Corinthians 9:19–22"> whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas or the world or life or death or the present or the future. Everything belongs to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; 1 Corinthians 11:3; John 17:9–10"> and you belong to Christ, and Christ belongs to God.</span>
