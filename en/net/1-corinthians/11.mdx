## Women’s Head Coverings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:15; 1 Corinthians 4:17; Proverbs 31:28–31"> I praise you because you remember me in everything and maintain the traditions just as I passed them on to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:16; Colossians 1:18; Colossians 3:18"> But I want you to know that Christ is the head of every man, and the man is the head of a woman, and God is the head of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:30; 1 Corinthians 12:28; 1 Corinthians 12:10"> Any man who prays or prophesies with his head covered disgraces his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:12; Luke 2:36; Acts 2:17"> But any woman who prays or prophesies with her head uncovered disgraces her head, for it is one and the same thing as having a shaved head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:5; Numbers 5:18"> For if a woman will not cover her head, she should cut off her hair. But if it is disgraceful for a woman to have her hair cut off or her head shaved, she should cover her head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3; Genesis 5:1; Psalms 8:6"> For a man should not have his head covered, since he is the image and glory of God. But the woman is the glory of the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:13; Genesis 2:21–23"> For man did not come from woman, but woman from man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 2:18; Genesis 2:23–24; Genesis 2:20"> Neither was man created for the sake of woman, but woman for man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 24:64–65; Hebrews 1:14; Matthew 18:10"> For this reason a woman should have a symbol of authority on her head, because of the angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; 1 Corinthians 7:10–14; 1 Corinthians 12:12–22"> In any case, in the Lord woman is not independent of man, nor is man independent of woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:36; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 1:2–3"> For just as woman came from man, so man comes through woman. But all things come from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:57; John 7:24; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> Judge for yourselves: Is it proper for a woman to pray to God with her head uncovered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:26; 1 Corinthians 14:35"> Does not nature itself teach you that if a man has long hair, it is a disgrace for him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> but if a woman has long hair, it is her glory? For her hair is given to her for a covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Timothy 6:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 2:14"> If anyone intends to quarrel about this, we have no other practice, nor do the churches of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lord’s Supper

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; 1 Corinthians 14:23; 1 Corinthians 11:22"> Now in giving the following instruction I do not praise you, because you come together not for the better but for the worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 1:10–12; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> For in the first place, when you come together as a church I hear there are divisions among you, and in part I believe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 2:19; Titus 3:10; Acts 20:30"> For there must in fact be divisions among you, so that those of you who are approved may be evident.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Now when you come together at the same place, you are not really eating the Lord’s Supper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:13; Jude 1:12; 1 Corinthians 11:23–25"> For when it is time to eat, everyone proceeds with his own supper. One is hungry and another becomes drunk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 20:28; Proverbs 17:5; James 2:5–6"> Do you not have houses so that you can eat and drink? Or are you trying to show contempt for the church of God by shaming those who have nothing? What should I say to you? Should I praise you? I will not praise you for this!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:19–20; Matthew 26:26–28; 1 Corinthians 11:23–25"> For I received from the Lord what I also passed on to you, that the Lord Jesus on the night in which he was betrayed took bread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:53–58; 1 Corinthians 11:27–28; 1 Corinthians 10:3–4"> and after he had given thanks he broke it and said, “This is my body, which is for you. Do this in remembrance of me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 22:20; Hebrews 9:15–20; Hebrews 13:20"> In the same way, he also took the cup after supper, saying, “This cup is the new covenant in my blood. Do this, every time you drink it, in remembrance of me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Revelation 1:7; John 14:3"> For every time you eat this bread and drink the cup, you proclaim the Lord’s death until he comes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 6:51; 1 Corinthians 10:21; Hebrews 10:29"> For this reason, whoever eats the bread or drinks the cup of the Lord in an unworthy manner will be guilty of the body and blood of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 6:4; 2 Corinthians 13:5; Lamentations 3:40"> A person should examine himself first, and in this way let him eat the bread and drink of the cup.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:27; 1 Corinthians 11:30; 1 Corinthians 11:32–34"> For the one who eats and drinks without careful regard for the body eats and drinks judgment against himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Numbers 20:12; Amos 3:2"> That is why many of you are weak and sick, and quite a few are dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 1:9; Psalms 32:3–5; Revelation 2:5"> But if we examined ourselves, we would not be judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5–11; Psalms 118:18; Proverbs 3:11–12"> But when we are judged by the Lord, we are disciplined so that we may not be condemned with the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> So then, my brothers and sisters, when you come together to eat, wait for one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:21–22; 1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Corinthians 4:19"> If anyone is hungry, let him eat at home, so that when you assemble it does not lead to judgment. I will give directions about other matters when I come.</span>
</div>
