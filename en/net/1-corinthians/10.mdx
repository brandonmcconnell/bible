<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:7; 1 Corinthians 10:11; Hebrews 4:11"> These things happened as examples for us, so that we will not crave evil things as they did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 32:6–8; Psalms 106:19–20; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> So do not be idolaters, as some of them were. As it is written, “The people sat down to eat and drink and rose up to play.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 1 Corinthians 6:18; Psalms 106:29"> And let us not be immoral, as some of them were, and twenty-three thousand died in a single day.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 16:41; Numbers 14:37"> And do not complain, as some of them did, and were killed by the destroying angel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 15:4; Romans 13:11; 1 Corinthians 9:10"> These things happened to them as examples and were written for our instruction, on whom the ends of the ages have come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:20; 2 Peter 3:17; Proverbs 16:18"> So let the one who thinks he is standing be careful that he does not fall.</span>

## Avoid Idol Feasts

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; 1 John 5:21; 1 Peter 2:11"> So then, my dear friends, flee from idolatry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 11:13"> I am speaking to thoughtful people. Consider what I say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:26–28; 1 Corinthians 11:23–29; Acts 2:42"> Is not the cup of blessing that we bless a sharing in the blood of Christ? Is not the bread that we break a sharing in the body of Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:5; Colossians 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> Because there is one bread, we who are many are one body, for we all share the one bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 3:3–5; Galatians 6:16; Leviticus 7:6"> Look at the people of Israel. Are not those who eat the sacrifices partners in the altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:4; 1 Corinthians 3:7; 1 Corinthians 13:2"> Am I saying that idols or food sacrificed to them amount to anything?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 9:20; Psalms 106:37–39; Deuteronomy 32:16–17"> No, I mean that what the pagans sacrifice is to demons and not to God. I do not want you to be partners with demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; 1 Kings 18:21; 2 Corinthians 6:15–17"> You cannot drink the cup of the Lord and the cup of demons. You cannot take part in the table of the Lord and the table of demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Deuteronomy 32:16; Hebrews 10:31"> Or are we trying to provoke the Lord to jealousy? Are we really stronger than he is?</span>
</div>
</div>

## Live to Glorify God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:12; 1 Corinthians 8:9; Ephesians 4:29"> “Everything is lawful,” but not everything is beneficial. “Everything is lawful,” but not everything builds others up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:1–2; 1 Corinthians 10:33; Philippians 2:4–5"> Do not seek your own good, but the good of the other person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; 1 Corinthians 8:7; Titus 1:15"> Eat anything that is sold in the marketplace without questions of conscience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 24:1; Exodus 19:5; Psalms 50:12"> for the earth and its abundance are the Lord’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:25; Luke 10:7–8; 1 Corinthians 5:9–11"> If an unbeliever invites you to dinner and you want to go, eat whatever is served without asking questions of conscience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:10–13; 1 Corinthians 8:7; 1 Corinthians 10:26"> But if someone says to you, “This is from a sacrifice,” do not eat, because of the one who told you and because of conscience –</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:19; Romans 14:15–21; 1 Corinthians 8:9–13"> I do not mean yours but the other person’s. For why is my freedom being judged by another’s conscience?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 14:6; 1 Timothy 4:3–4"> If I partake with thankfulness, why am I blamed for the food that I give thanks for?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Colossians 3:23; 1 Peter 4:11"> So whether you eat or drink, or whatever you do, do everything for the glory of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 20:28; 1 Corinthians 10:33; Romans 14:13"> Do not give offense to Jews or Greeks or to the church of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23"> just as I also try to please everyone in all things. I do not seek my own benefit, but the benefit of many, so that they may be saved.</span>
</div>
