## Elim-elek (Elimelech) and Naomi left their homeland and went to a country called Mo-ab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Genesis 26:1; Ezekiel 14:13"> This story comes from a long time ago when the leader for the Israelite people was called a Judge.<br />There was a man named Elim-elek who was from the Ef-rath (Ephrath) clan. And he lived in the town of Bethlehem in the country that belonged to the tribe of Judah.<br />Elim-elek was married to Naomi and they had 2 sons, Mah-lon and Kill-e-on (Kilion).<br />There came a time when there was not much food growing in the country of Israel, and all the people were really hungry. So Elim-elek took his wife and his 2 sons to live for a while in a country called Mo-ab.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:10–11; Psalms 34:19; Hebrews 12:6"> Some time later, Elim-elek died and Naomi was left all alone with her 2 sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 1:5; 1 Kings 11:1–2; Deuteronomy 23:3"> Those 2 young men married women from Mo-ab. One woman's name was Or-pah and the other woman's name was Ruth.<br />They all lived together in Mo-ab for about another 10 years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Psalms 89:30–32; Isaiah 49:21"> but then both Mah-lon and Kill-e-on died. And so Naomi was all alone. She had lost her husband and now she had lost her 2 sons.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Naomi went back to Bethlehem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 132:15; Exodus 3:16; Psalms 111:5"> Later on, someone told Naomi that the Lord had helped his people in Israel and had given them food. So Naomi and her daughters-in-law got ready to leave Mo-ab and go back to Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 8:3; Ruth 1:14; Exodus 18:27"> Naomi, Or-pah and Ruth left their home in Mo-ab, and they started to walk along the track that went back to the land of Judah in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 2:20; Ruth 1:5; Colossians 3:18"> Then Naomi stopped and said to her daughters-in-law, “I want you to go back and stay with your mothers. I will pray and ask the Lord to be kind to you, just like you have been kind to me and my 2 sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ruth 3:1; Acts 20:37; Genesis 29:11"> I will ask the Lord to give each of you a new husband to look after you and to keep you safe.” Then Naomi kissed them and said goodbye to them, and they all started to cry out loud.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 8:23; Psalms 16:3; Psalms 119:63"> Both the young women said, “No, we don't want to do that, we want to go with you and live with you and your people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Genesis 38:11"> But Naomi said, “Why do you want to come with me? I cannot have more sons so that you can marry them. No, go home my daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; 1 Timothy 5:9"> Go back to your families. I might get married today and have 2 more sons straight away, but I am too old for that to happen. And even if I can have 2 more sons straight away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 19:21; Judges 2:15; Psalms 32:4"> you cannot wait for them to grow up. No, you cannot wait that long to have a husband. I think the Lord has turned against me. I am too sad and angry, and I don't want you to feel like that too!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:24; Matthew 16:24; Matthew 10:37"> They all cried out loud again. Then Or-pah kissed her mother-in-law to say goodbye and she started to walk back to her family in Mo-ab. But Ruth grabbed Naomi and held onto her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 11:24; Joshua 24:15; Zephaniah 1:6"> Naomi said to her, “Ruth, Or-pah is going back to her people and to her gods. I want you to go with her.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Ruth made a strong promise to Naomi

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:21; Ruth 2:11–12; 2 Kings 2:2–6"> But Ruth said, “Don't force me to leave you, and don't stop me from following you. This is my strong promise to you. Wherever you go, I will go. Wherever you live, I will live there too. Your people will be my people and your God will be my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:17; 1 Kings 2:23; 2 Kings 6:31"> Where you die, I will die there too. Where you are buried, I want to be buried in the same place. If I leave you before I die, then I ask the Lord to really punish me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:14; Ephesians 6:10; Acts 2:42"> When Naomi saw that Ruth had made up her mind to go with her, she stopped talking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 21:10; Isaiah 23:7; Lamentations 2:15"> Then they walked together until they got to Bethlehem and everyone in the town was very excited to see them. All the women said, “Is that really you Naomi?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 6:4; Hebrews 12:11; Psalms 73:14"> But Naomi said, “Do not call me Naomi. That name means that I am happy, but I am not happy. Call me Mara. That name means that I am not happy, I am like food that has gone sour. God All Powerful has made my life very hard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:7–8; Job 1:21; Job 16:8"> I left this country a happy person, but now the Lord has made me come back here and I have nothing. The Lord All Powerful has sent me too much trouble, so do not call me Naomi!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ruth 2:23; 2 Samuel 21:9; Exodus 9:31–32"> That is how Naomi and Ruth, her daughter-in-law from Mo-ab, came home to Bethlehem.<br />They got there when the people in that country started to go out to their paddocks to cut all the long barley grass. The people grew that barley grass to make food from the seeds.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
