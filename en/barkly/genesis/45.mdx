## Joseph told his brothers who he really was

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:15; Acts 10:41; Genesis 43:30–31"> When Judah finished his story, Joseph could not stop himself from crying, and so he said to his workers, “Please leave me alone with these men.” Then after all his workers had left the room, Joseph told his brothers who he really was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 46:29; Acts 20:37; Numbers 14:1"> He cried, and he made so much noise that his workers heard him and everyone who worked in the government building heard about it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 7:13; Acts 9:5; Zechariah 12:10"> Joseph said to his brothers, “I am Joseph. Is my father still alive?” But his brothers were so frightened, they could not say anything.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Matthew 14:27; Genesis 50:18"> So Joseph said to them, “It's alright, come close to me.” And when they came close, he said to them again, “I am Joseph, your brother. You sold me as a slave to those Ishmaelite men and they led me here to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Acts 7:9–15; Genesis 45:7–8"> But do not be frightened or angry at yourselves for selling me, God sent me here to save the lives of a lot of people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 47:23; Genesis 41:54; Genesis 41:56"> This country has not grown any food for 2 years and no food will grow for another 5 years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 44:4; Acts 7:35; Psalms 18:50"> God sent me here to make a way for your families to stay alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 15:16; Romans 9:16; John 19:11"> So then, you didn't send me here, God did. And he has made me the most important person that works for Pharaoh. I am like a father for Pharaoh. I look after him and I am the boss of the government and I am the boss over the whole country of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 45:19–20; Genesis 45:13"> I want you to go home to my father as fast as you can and say to him, ‘Your son Joseph said, “God has made me the boss over all of Egypt. You have to come to Egypt as fast as you can.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 9:26; Exodus 8:22; Genesis 46:34–6"> I want you to come and live in a place called Goshen. Bring all your children and grandchildren, all your animals and everything you have so that you can all live near me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 47:12; Genesis 47:6; Matthew 15:5–6"> There is going to be another 5 years when no food will grow, but I will look after you so that you and all your family will have all the food you need.” ’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:23; Luke 24:39; John 20:27"> Then Joseph said to his brothers, “You can see for yourselves, and so can Benjamin, that I am your brother Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:14; Revelation 21:23; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> So tell my father how powerful I am here in Egypt. Tell him everything you have seen and heard. Quick, go and bring him here to me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 33:4; Genesis 29:11; Genesis 46:29"> Joseph put his arms around Benjamin and they cried together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 15:20; 1 Samuel 10:1; Psalms 77:4"> Joseph cried and kissed all his brothers, and they all sat down to talk.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 41:33; Esther 1:21; Genesis 20:15"> When Pharaoh the king of Egypt and all his helpers heard that Joseph's brothers were in Egypt they were all happy for Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 42:25–26; Genesis 44:1–2"> The king said to Joseph, “Tell your brothers to put bags of food onto their donkeys and go back to Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 27:28; Numbers 18:12; Genesis 47:6"> Tell them to bring their father and all their families back to me. I will give them the best land that I can find in Egypt and they will have the best food that we can give them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 46:5; Genesis 45:27; Genesis 31:17–18"> Joseph, give them some of our wagons. That way the women and children can ride in them when they come to Egypt. And make sure your father comes with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; Luke 17:31; Deuteronomy 7:16"> Tell them not to worry if they have to leave some of their things behind. We will give them new things, they will be the best things we have here in Egypt.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:19; Ezekiel 23:24; Lamentations 1:18"> So Jacob's sons did what Pharaoh said. And Joseph gave them wagons and food for the trip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 43:34; 2 Kings 5:5; Judges 14:12"> He gave each of his brothers one lot of new clothes, but he gave Benjamin 5 lots of clothes and a really big bag full of silver money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 24:10; Exodus 16:3; Genesis 43:11"> Joseph sent his father some presents too. He sent 10 donkeys loaded with good things from Egypt and 10 girl donkeys with bags of seeds and bread and all the food they would need for the trip back to Canaan. And Joseph sent even more food for when everybody had to travel back to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:13; Psalms 133:1–3; Genesis 42:21–22"> Then before he sent his brothers away, he said, “Do not argue as you go home.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And so, the brothers left Egypt and went home to their father Jacob in Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 44:28; Genesis 37:35; Luke 24:34"> They said to their father, “Joseph is alive, and he is the boss over all of Egypt.” Jacob was shocked and did not believe them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 85:6; Isaiah 57:15; Genesis 45:19"> But when he heard everything that Joseph said, and saw all the wagons that Joseph had sent to carry them back to Egypt, he started to feel better.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:28–30; John 16:21–22; Genesis 46:30"> Then Israel, whose other name was Jacob, said, “I believe you now. My son Joseph is alive and I will go and see him before I die.”</span>
</div>
</div>
