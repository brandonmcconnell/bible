## Abraham married Keturah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:32–33; Genesis 23:1–2; Genesis 28:1"> Some time after Sarah died, Abraham married another woman and her name was Keturah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:32–33; Numbers 25:17–18; Job 2:11"> Abraham and Keturah had 6 sons together and their names were Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Jeremiah 49:8; Ezekiel 27:20"> Later on, Jokshan became the father of Sheba and Dedan, and when Dedan grew up, he had 3 sons. From these 3 sons came the Assyrian, Letush and Leum people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6"> Midian had 5 sons, and they were named Efah, Effer, Hanock, Abida, and Elday-ah. All these people came from Abraham and Keturah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 24:36; Hebrews 1:2; Galatians 3:29"> Before he died, Abraham was very kind to the sons he had with Hagar and Keturah and gave them a lot of presents. But he sent them away to live in country to the East. He did not want them to live close to Isaac. And so, when Abraham died, Isaac got everything that belonged to his father.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Abraham died

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 12:4"> Abraham was 175 years old when he died and he was buried just like his old father's before him. He had lived a long time and had a good life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 50:13; Genesis 35:29; Genesis 49:29–30"> His 2 sons, Isaac and Ishmael took Abraham's body and put it in the same cave where Abraham buried his wife Sarah. It was called Mack-pelah and was near Mamre. Abraham had paid Efron for that land. Efron's father was named Zoar the Hittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 16:14; Genesis 24:62"> Isaac lived near Beer La-hai Roy after Abraham died and it was there that God was good to Isaac.</span>
</div>
</div>

## This is Ishmael's Story

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 83:6; Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:13"> Many years before, Sarah had an Egyptian woman that worked for her. Her name was Hagar. When Sarah found that she could not have a baby, she gave Hagar to be like a wife for Abraham. Sarah did this so that Hagar and Abraham could have a baby together. Hagar did have a son and Abraham named him Ishmael. Later on, Abraham sent Hagar and Ishmael away from his camp. But God had promised Hagar that Ishmael would have a big family.</span>
</div>
</div>

## This is the family that came from Ishmael

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:29–31; Isaiah 60:7; Song of Solomon 1:5"> This is a list of Ishmael's sons. Nebay-oth, was the oldest, then Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam, Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, Tema, Jetur, Nafish, and Kedemah was the youngest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 17:20; Genesis 17:23"> Each of the 12 sons was the leader of his own tribe and they all lived in their own country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Genesis 25:7–8"> Ishmael died when he was 137 years old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 16:12; Genesis 20:1; 2 Kings 23:29"> The tribes that came from Ishmael lived in a big area that went from Havilah to Shur. Shur is near the Egyptian border, on the west side of Assyria. And what the angel said to Hagar years before at the waterhole, came true. Ishmael's mob did fight with each other all the time.</span>
</div>
</div>

## This is Isaac's story

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 1:2; Luke 3:34; Acts 7:8"> This is the story of Abraham's son named Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 24:29; Genesis 24:67; Genesis 22:23"> Isaac was 40 years old when he married Rebekah. She came from Abraham's country and was the daughter of Bethuel who lived in Harran. Her brother's name was Laban and he was called The Aramean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Isaiah 58:9; Ezra 8:23"> Isaac and Rebekah were married for 20 years and they still did not have any children and so Isaac prayed for her. God heard his prayer and later on Rebekah knew she was going to have a baby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Samuel 28:6; 1 Samuel 30:8"> But she was going to have twins and they started to fight each other even before they were born. Rebekah was upset and said to herself, “Why is this happening to me?” Then she prayed to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 9:10–13; Genesis 27:40; Genesis 27:29"> And the Lord said to her, “There are 2 boys inside you. One day, they will be 2 nations. And they will be against each other all the time. One nation will be stronger than the other, and the boy that is born second will be the boss over the boy that is born first.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> On the day the twins were born, the first one was all red and had hair all over his body like a coat, and so they named him Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 27:36; Hosea 12:3; Genesis 38:28–30"> When the second baby was born, he was holding his brother's foot so they named him Jacob. Jacob means Tricky One in their language. Isaac was 60 years old when his sons were born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 11:9; Genesis 21:20; Genesis 10:9"> When the boys grew up, Esau was a very good hunter. He loved being out in the bush, but Jacob was a quiet man who liked to stay around his parent's camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 27:19; Genesis 27:31; Genesis 27:4"> Isaac loved to eat the bush food that Esau took home and so Esau was Isaac's favourite son, but Rebekah loved Jacob the best.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Esau gave up his place in the family

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 13:25; Isaiah 40:30–31; 1 Samuel 14:28"> One day, Esau came home after hunting, and he was really hungry. He found Jacob cooking some stew and said, “I am really hungry, give me some of that red stew.” And that is why people named him Edom. It means Red.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Jacob answered, “I will give you the stew, if you give me your place in the family. That means that I will be like the oldest son and will get more than you, when our father dies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachi 3:14; Job 21:15; Job 22:17"> Esau said, “I am so hungry that I feel like I am going to die. I am not worried about getting my father's things after he dies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 12:16; Genesis 27:36; Hebrews 6:16"> But Jacob said, “First, you have to make a strong promise to me.” And so Esau promised that Jacob would be the leader of the family after Isaac died. It was like Esau sold his place in the family for a bowl of stew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Philippians 3:18–19; Hebrews 12:16–17; 1 Corinthians 15:32"> Jacob gave Esau some bread and stew with vegetables in it. Esau ate and drank and then he went away. In this way, Esau showed that he did not care about being the next leader of the family after Isaac died.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
