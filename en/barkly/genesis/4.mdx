## Cain and Abel took presents to God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 3:12; Genesis 3:15; Genesis 4:25"> Adam slept with his wife Eve and at the right time she had a baby boy and Eve named him Cain. She said, “The Lord helped me to have a son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 3:15; 1 John 3:12; Luke 11:51"> Later on Eve had another son, and she named him Abel.<br />When the boys were grown up, Abel looked after sheep, and Cain got the ground ready to grow food plants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 18:12; Leviticus 2:1–11; 1 Kings 17:7"> One day, Cain wanted to show respect to the Lord, so he took some food from his garden and gave it to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Proverbs 3:9; Exodus 13:12"> Abel wanted to show respect to the Lord too, so he went and took some of the first born sheep. He killed them, and gave the best parts to the Lord.<br />God was happy with Abel and he was happy with what Abel gave him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Job 5:2; Luke 15:28–30"> But God was not happy with Cain, and he was not happy with what Cain gave him. And so, Cain got very angry and his face had an ugly look.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Job 5:2; Luke 15:31–32"> The Lord said “Cain, why are you angry? Why do you look so sad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:15; Romans 2:6–10; Isaiah 3:10–11"> If you do the right thing, I will be happy with you. But, if you don't do the right thing, look out. You are thinking the wrong way. It is like you have a wild animal inside your head that wants you to do all sorts of bad things. You have to be the boss of your thinking.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 23:35; 1 John 3:12–15; Jude 1:11"> But Cain went and found his brother Abel and said to him, “Let's go for a walk.” And while they were walking, Cain hit his brother and killed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; John 8:44; Psalms 9:12"> Later on, the Lord said to Cain, “Where is your brother Abel?”<br />Cain said, “I don't know. It's not my job to look after him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 12:24; Psalms 72:14; Hebrews 11:4"> The Lord said to Cain, “Why have you done this really bad thing? I can see your brother's blood on the ground, and it is like his blood is calling out to me. That blood is telling me that you killed your brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Job 16:18; Genesis 3:14"> You killed your brother and the ground soaked up his blood. And so I am going to put a curse on you. From now on the ground will be your enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 9:17; Leviticus 26:36; Leviticus 26:20"> And if you try to grow plants in the ground, nothing will grow for you. You will always go from place to place, and you will never settle down anywhere.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:22; Revelation 16:9; Revelation 16:11"> Cain said to the Lord, “You are too hard on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 35:19; Psalms 143:7; Job 15:20–24"> You are forcing me away from you and this country and I will not be able to grow food for myself. I will have to walk around from place to place and never settle down. And anyone that finds me will kill me. Yes God, you are being too hard on me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:4; Psalms 79:12; Genesis 4:24"> Then the Lord said, “Cain, I promise you, that if someone kills you, I will punish that person 7 times more than I punished you.” Then the Lord put a special mark on Cain to stop anyone from killing him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The family that came from Cain

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:39; Job 2:7; Genesis 4:14"> Then Cain went away from the Lord and lived in a country called Nod. Nod was on the east side of the Garden of Eden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 49:11; Ecclesiastes 2:4–11; Daniel 4:30"> Cain slept with his wife, and they had a baby son and they named him Enock. Cain built a town, and he called the town, Enock for his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 5:21"> Enock grew up and he had a son named Irad.<br />Irad grew up and he had a son named Methu-jayel.<br />Methu-jayel grew up, and he had a son named Methu-shayel.<br />Methu-shayel grew up and had a son named Larmek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:8; Genesis 2:18"> Larmek grew up and married 2 women. The name of one wife was Adah. The name of the other wife was Zillah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 4:2; Jeremiah 35:9–10; John 8:44"> Larmek's wife Adah had a baby boy named Jabal.<br />Jabal grew up and he was the first person to live in a tent and look after sheep and bullocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 21:12; Isaiah 5:12; Genesis 31:27"> Adah had another son, a brother for Jabal. His name was Jubal.<br />Jubal grew up and he was the first person to play music on an instrument like a guitar and other instruments that had pipes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:25; Deuteronomy 8:9; 2 Chronicles 2:7"> Larmek's other wife Zillah had a son named Tubal-Cain, and a daughter named Naymah.<br />Tubal-Cain grew up and made tools from iron and another brown metal called bronze.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 23:18; Exodus 20:13; Leviticus 19:18"> One day Larmek said to his 2 wives,<br />“Adah and Zillah, listen to me.<br />A long time ago, Cain killed his brother,<br />And God punished him.<br />If anyone killed Cain,<br />God said he would punish that person 7 times more.<br />Well, I killed a man that hurt me.<br />But, if someone kills me,<br />that person must get payback 77 times more.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Adam and Eve had another son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 4:8; Genesis 4:10–11; 1 Chronicles 1:1"> Adam slept with his wife again, and they had another son. Eve named him Seth. She said, “God has given me another son to take Abel's place in the family.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 116:17; 1 Kings 18:24; Joel 2:32"> Seth grew up and had a son and they named him Enosh.<br />It was about this time that people started to pray to the Lord and show him respect.</span>
</div>
