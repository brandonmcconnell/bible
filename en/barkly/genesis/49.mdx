## This is what Jacob said to his 12 sons before he died

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 24:14; Jeremiah 23:20; Daniel 10:14"> Then Jacob sent someone to bring all his sons to him. When they came he said, “My sons, stand around me so that I can tell you what is going to happen to each one of you later on.” This is what he said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 34:11; Proverbs 7:1; Proverbs 23:22"> “Listen carefully, sons of Jacob. Listen to your father Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:17; Psalms 78:51; Psalms 105:36"> Reuben. You were born first when I was young and strong. And you showed everyone that I could be a father. You are the first born son. You are strong and you should be the leader for the family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; 1 Chronicles 5:1; Deuteronomy 27:20"> But you are like the floodwater. You cannot say, ‘No’ to yourself. You went and slept with one of my wives. You shamed me, and so you cannot be the leader of the family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 34:25–31; Genesis 46:10–11; Genesis 29:33–34"> Simeon and Levi used their the wrong way and were violent. They are both like that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Psalms 26:9; Psalms 16:9"> I will not sit down with them when they have their secret meetings. I do not want to know what they are planning to do next. They were so angry that they killed men, and they crippled cattle just for fun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:1–9; 1 Chronicles 4:24–31; 1 Chronicles 6:65"> You are too violent and cruel and so this is my curse on you. I will split up your families and spread them into all the tribes of Jacob. Yes, I will split you up and spread your families into all the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 7:14; Genesis 27:29; 1 Chronicles 5:2"> Judah. All your brothers will say good things about you. And you will beat all your enemies. All your brothers will bow down to the ground in front of you to show respect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Numbers 23:24; Revelation 5:5"> You Judah, my son, are strong like a young lion that has killed an animal. You are like a lion that is lying down to rest, and everyone is too frightened to wake you up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Numbers 24:17; Isaiah 9:6"> Kings will come from Judah's family. Someone from Judah's line will always sit on the king's special chair called a throne until the real king comes, and then all the nations will do what he says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 63:1–3; Joel 3:18; Revelation 7:14"> That king will have a lot of grapevines and will tie his donkey to the best branch and if the donkey eats the leaves, the grapevine will still grow grapes. The king will have a lot of wine, and he will wash his clothes in the best wine that he has.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29"> His eyes will be dark, like the colour of his wine, and his teeth will be white, just like milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:18–19; Genesis 30:20; Joshua 19:10–16"> Zeb-you-lun will live near the sea, and his country will go nearly to a town called Sidon. Ships will be safe when they are tied up next to his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:32; Genesis 30:18; Deuteronomy 33:18"> Iss-a-car is like a big strong donkey that lies down when it has a heavy load.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 81:6; Matthew 23:4; Joshua 14:15"> When Iss-a-car sees that his country is good and that it is a good place to rest, he will want to live there. But he will be forced to work very hard for other people so that he can stay there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 30:6; Deuteronomy 33:22; Judges 18:26–27"> Dan will be the leader of his tribe, just like the other brothers will lead their tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:35; Judges 18:22–31; Judges 14:1–15"> But Dan's tribe will be small and dangerous, just like a small cheeky snake. Those snakes lie next to the road and bite horse's legs so that when the horse jumps up, the rider falls off its back.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:174; Psalms 119:166; Micah 7:7"> Then Jacob called out, “O God, I am waiting for you to save me.” And Jacob kept telling his sons what was going to happen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 30:11; 1 Chronicles 5:11–22; Deuteronomy 33:20–21"> “Bad men will come to Gad and try to steal all his things, but Gad will fight them and beat them. He will force them to go away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:24–25; Genesis 30:13; Genesis 46:17"> Asher's country will grow a lot of food. That food will be so good, kings will want to eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:23; Genesis 30:8; Joshua 19:32–39"> Naftali is like a wild animal that runs fast and is hard to catch. The family that comes from Naftali will be good looking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 41:52; Psalms 128:3; Psalms 1:1–3"> Joseph is like a grapevine that grows a lot of fruit. That strong vine gets its water from a spring, and its branches grow over the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:24; Psalms 64:3; Psalms 118:13"> His enemies were angry with him and wanted to fight him. They shot a lot of arrows at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 132:2; Isaiah 28:16; Psalms 132:5"> But his arms were strong. And he aimed his bow to shoot arrows back at them. God is the Most Powerful one, he is the one who leads Israel. He is the one who keeps Israel safe, he is the one who makes Joseph strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 28:13; Genesis 35:11; Philippians 4:19"> My God is the one that helps you. My God is the Most Powerful one, and he is the one who blesses you. He is the one that does good things for you and makes you strong. He will send good rain to you from the sky, and he will make water come up from under the ground for you. He will give you a lot of children and your animals will grow in number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:15–16; Habakkuk 3:6; Isaiah 66:5"> I am asking God to do good things for you. The country or hills can't give you any help like that. Joseph is the powerful leader over his brothers and so I am asking God to give Joseph all the good things I want him to have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 20:25; Genesis 35:18; Ezekiel 22:27"> Benjamin is dangerous. He is like a wild dog. He and his men are always fighting other people and taking their things. Then at night he splits up what they have taken and gives his fighters their share.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Esther 8:11; 1 Kings 18:31"> Jacob told his 12 sons what would happen to each one of them later on. All the 12 sons had families that were big and they were called The 12 Tribes of Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jacob told his sons what to do with his body after he died

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 47:30; Genesis 50:13; Genesis 15:15"> Jacob said to his sons, “I am about to die. I want you to take my body back to where my father and grandfather are buried in Canaan. My grandfather Abraham paid money for some country that belonged to a man named Efron from the Hittite tribe. Abraham wanted to use the cave in that country as a cemetery, and it is in the paddock called Mack-pelah on the east side of Mamre. Abraham, Sarah, Isaac and Rebekah are all buried in that cave. And I buried my wife Leah there too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 23:17–20"> Remember that money was paid to the Hittite people for that paddock and the cave and so that cemetery belongs to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 49:29; Genesis 25:8; Genesis 35:29"> When Jacob finished talking he lay back down on his bed and died.</span>
</div>
</div>
