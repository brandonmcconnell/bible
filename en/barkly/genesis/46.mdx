## Jacob and his family go to Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 28:13; Genesis 26:22–25; Genesis 28:10"> Joseph's father's name was Jacob, but God changed his name to Israel. Sometimes he was named Jacob and sometimes he was named Israel. And so, Israel and all the other families took everything they owned and started to go south to Egypt. When they got to Beersheba, Jacob burnt an animal to show respect to God, the God of his father Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 15:1; Job 33:14–15; Job 4:13"> That night, God spoke to Israel and said, “Jacob, Jacob.” And Jacob answered, “Here I am. I am listening.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 12:2; Deuteronomy 26:5; Isaiah 43:1–2"> God said, “I am the God of your father Isaac. Do not be frightened to go down to Egypt. Your sons are going to have a lot of children and grandchildren, and I am going to make them into a great nation down there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Genesis 28:15; Genesis 48:21"> I will go down to Egypt with you, and at the right time, I will bring your family out of that country again. And I promise you, that your son Joseph will be with you when you die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 45:21; Genesis 45:19; Genesis 45:27"> Then Jacob and all his family left Beersheba. Jacob's sons put their wives, children and their father into the wagons that the king of Egypt gave them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 24:4; Isaiah 52:4; Acts 7:15"> When they left Canaan they took all their animals and everything they owned with them. And so that is how Jacob took his family to Egypt. He took his sons, grandsons, daughters and granddaughters. Yes, all his family went to Egypt.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jacob's family

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 1:1–5; Deuteronomy 33:6; 1 Chronicles 2:1–55"> This is a list of the names of Israel's sons and the families that went to Egypt. And Israel's other name was Jacob. Reuben was Jacob's first son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Reuben's sons were Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron and Carmi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 6:15; Genesis 29:33; Genesis 28:1"> Simeon's sons were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jakin, Zohar, and Shaul, and Shaul's mother was a Canaanite woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 29:34; 1 Chronicles 6:16; Numbers 8:1–26"> Levi's sons were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:21; Genesis 38:10; Genesis 29:35"> Judah's sons were Er, Onan, Shelah, Perez, and Zerah, but Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan. Perez's sons were Hezron and Hamul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:32; Genesis 35:23; Genesis 30:14–18"> Iss-a-car's sons were Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:18–19; Numbers 26:26–27; Numbers 1:30–31"> Zeb-you-lun's sons were Sered, Elon, and Jah-leel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 29:32–35; Genesis 30:17–21; Genesis 34:1–31"> These sons were born while Jacob worked for Leah's father Laban in Harran. Jacob and Leah had a daughter too. Her name was Dinah. There were 33 people in this part of Jacob's family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 26:15–17; Genesis 30:11; Numbers 1:24–25"> This is a list of the sons Jacob had with Zilpah. She was the working girl that Laban gave to Leah, and later on, Leah gave Zilpah to Jacob to be another wife. Zilpah had 2 sons with Jacob. Their names were Gad and Asher. Gad's sons were Zefon, Haggi, Shuni, Ezbon, Eri, Arodi, and Areli. Asher's sons were Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, and Beriah, and their sister was Serah. Beriah's sons were Heber and Mal-kiel. There were 16 people in this part of the family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 44:27; Genesis 29:18; Genesis 35:24"> Jacob and Rachel had 2 sons, Joseph and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 41:50–52; Genesis 41:45; Deuteronomy 33:13–17"> While Joseph was in Egypt, he and his wife, As-en-ath had 2 sons, Manasseh and Efraim. As-en-ath's father was the Egyptian ceremony man named Potifera. He was an Egyptian ceremony man at a place called On.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:6–12; 1 Chronicles 8:1–7; Numbers 26:38–40"> Benjamin's sons were Bela, Beker, Ashbel, Gera, Naaman, Ehi, Rosh, Muppim, Huppim, and Ard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> There were 14 people in this part of Jacob's family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:38–39; Genesis 49:16–17; Numbers 1:12"> These are the sons that Jacob had with Bilhah. She was the working girl that Laban gave to his daughter Rachel, and later on Rachel gave Bilhah to be another wife for Jacob. Bilhah had 2 sons with Jacob. Their names were Dan and Naftali. Dan had a son named Hushi. And Naftali had 4 sons, named Jar-ziel, Guni, Jezer and Shillem. There were 7 people in this part of Jacob's family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 1:5; Genesis 35:11; Judges 8:30"> And so, there were 66 people in Jacob's family when he travelled to Egypt. But the wives of Jacob's sons were not counted in that number. Joseph and his wife, As-en-ath had 2 sons when Joseph lived in Egypt. There were 70 people living with Jacob when he was in Egypt.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jacob and the families in Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 45:10; Genesis 44:16–34; Genesis 49:8"> Before Jacob and the family got to Goshen, he sent his son Judah to ask Joseph how to get there. When Jacob and all the families arrived in Goshen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 15:20; Genesis 45:14–15; Genesis 33:4"> Joseph told his workers to get his special wagon ready so that he could go and meet his father Jacob. As soon as Joseph saw his father, he put his arms around him and Joseph cried for a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 2:29–30; Genesis 45:28"> Then Israel said to Joseph, “Now that I have seen that you are alive, I can die a happy man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 47:1–3; Hebrews 2:11; Acts 18:3"> Joseph said to all of his brothers and their families, “I am going to tell the king that you are here. I will say, ‘My brothers and all my father's family have left Canaan and have come to stay with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 47:3; 1 Kings 9:27; 1 Kings 18:5–6"> They look after farm animals, and they have led all their animals here to Egypt. They have taken everything they own from Canaan and carried it here.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 46:32; Genesis 47:2–4; Jonah 1:8"> When the king wants you to go and talk to him, he will ask you, ‘What work do you do?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 8:26; Genesis 43:32; Genesis 46:32"> When you answer him, you must show him respect and say, ‘We have always looked after farm animals and our grandfathers from a long time ago did the same.’ If you say that, the king will let you live here in Goshen, away from where the Egyptian people live. You see they do not like to live near people that look after sheep.”</span>
</div>
</div>
