## Sheckem raped Dinah, Jacob and Leah's daughter

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 30:21; Jeremiah 2:36; Genesis 30:13"> Some time later, Jacob and Leah's daughter Dinah went to visit some of the women that lived in that country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 5:28; Job 31:1; Job 31:9"> On the way there, Sheckem saw her. Sheckem was the son of Hamor who was the Hivite leader for that country. When Sheckem saw Dinah, he grabbed her and raped her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 2:14; Isaiah 40:2; 2 Chronicles 30:22"> But then, Sheckem fell in love with Dinah and said sweet words to her. He wanted her to love him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 14:2; 2 Samuel 13:13; Genesis 21:21"> Sheckem went home and said to his father Hamor, “Can you ask Jacob if I can marry Dinah?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:22; Leviticus 10:3; 1 Samuel 10:27"> Dinah went home and told her father what Sheckem did to her. Jacob listened, but he did not say anything until his sons came home from working with their animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> While he was waiting for his sons to come home, Hamor and Sheckem came to talk to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 7:15; Judges 20:6; Deuteronomy 22:21"> They were still talking when Jacob's sons came home from their work. When her brothers heard what Sheckem did to Dinah they were very angry. Sheckem had done the wrong thing and shamed all of Israel's family.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 11:2; Psalms 119:20; Psalms 84:2"> Hamor said to Jacob and his sons, “My son Sheckem really loves Dinah. Will you let him marry her?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 19:14"> And I have an idea. Why don't your daughters and sons marry our sons and daughters?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 47:27; Genesis 42:34"> Why don't you come and live here with us? You can own your own land and buy and sell things just like we do.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 33:15; Genesis 18:3"> Sheckem said to Jacob, “Please be kind to me. I will give you anything you ask for. Ask for as much money or animals as you like, just let me marry your daughter.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jacob's sons trick Sheckem and his family

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 25:27–34; Isaiah 59:13; Job 13:4"> Jacob's sons were thinking about the bad thing that happened to their sister Dinah. So they made up their minds to trick Sheckem and his father Hamor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–39; 2 Samuel 15:7; Genesis 17:11"> Jacob's sons said, “We cannot let you marry our sister. You have not had that young men's ceremony. If we let you marry Dinah, we would all be shamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:12"> There is only one way we will let you marry Dinah, all your men must have that young men's ceremony. Then we will all be the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then you can marry our women and our men can marry your women and it will be like we are one nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But if you will not have that young men's ceremony, we will take Jacob's daughter Dinah and leave this country.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Hamor and Sheckem both thought it was a good idea to have that young men's ceremony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:12; Acts 13:50; 1 Samuel 22:14"> Sheckem loved Dinah that much, he wanted to have that operation straight away. Sheckem was the most important person in Hamor's family and so he and his father went to the town gate and had a meeting with all the leaders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> They said to the leaders, “These people that have come to live here are very friendly. We think we should let them live with us. We have plenty of room and our sons can marry their daughters and their sons can marry our daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But they will only stay here if all our men have their young men's ceremony. If we do that we can be one big mob together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 1:12–13; John 6:26–27; John 2:16"> And, if we agree to do that, they will stay here. All their animals and everything they own will be ours too. Come on, let's do what they ask.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 23:10; 1 Corinthians 7:19; Isaiah 1:10–16"> All the men in the town agreed with Hamor and Sheckem and had the young men's ceremony.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 49:5–7; Genesis 29:33–34; Numbers 31:7"> Simeon and Levi, 2 of Jacob's sons and full brothers for Dinah, waited until the 3rd day after all the men had the operation and the men were still in a lot of pain. Then Simeon and Levi picked up their long knives/swords, and they moved quickly into the town and killed all the men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:26; Deuteronomy 32:42; Isaiah 31:8"> They killed Hamor and Sheckem and then took Dinah from Sheckem's house and went home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 7:13; Joshua 7:21; Exodus 2:14"> Then all of Jacob's sons joined Simeon and Levi and together they took everything they could find from the town where their sister was raped. They took all the women and children as prisoners. And they even took all the animals that were in the paddocks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 105:12; Joshua 7:25; Exodus 5:21"> When they went home, Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, “You have made a lot of trouble for me. All the Canaanite people that live in this country will hate me. What were you thinking? We are only a small family. If the Canaanites and Perizzites gang up and fight us, we will be finished. All my family will die.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 6:34; Genesis 34:13; Genesis 49:7"> But his sons said, “Sheckem should not have treated our sister like a woman that sleeps with men for money.”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
