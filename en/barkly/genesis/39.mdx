## Joseph's life in Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Psalms 105:17; Genesis 37:36"> When the Ishmaelites got to Egypt, they sold Joseph to a man named Potifar. Potifar was an important man in Egypt. He was boss over all the soldiers that guarded the government building. That was where the king worked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:14; Psalms 91:15; Jeremiah 15:20"> God was very good to Joseph and helped him to do everything the right way. Joseph was so good at what he did, he was allowed to live in Potifar's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Genesis 30:27; Matthew 5:16"> Potifar saw that God helped Joseph do everything just right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 39:21–22; Proverbs 27:18; Genesis 19:19"> And so Potifar was happy with Joseph and made him the boss over all the people in his house and over everything he owned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; 2 Samuel 6:11–12; Acts 27:24"> God did even more good things for Potifar after Joseph started to be the boss of Potifar's house. God made all Potifar's animals grow fat and his paddocks grow a lot of food. Joseph was God's man and that is why all these good things happened for Potifar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12; Luke 19:17; Proverbs 31:11"> And so Potifar had no worries, he only had to worry about the food he ate.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Potifar's wife tried to get Joseph to do the wrong thing

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:11; Ezekiel 16:34; 2 Peter 2:14"> Potifar's wife looked at Joseph and saw that he was strong and good looking, and she wanted him. One day she said to him, “Come to bed with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 23:26–28; Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 9:13–18"> Joseph said, “No way. I cannot do that. Your husband trusts me to look after everything he owns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13; Genesis 20:6; Genesis 42:18"> I am the boss of this house, and your husband has not kept anything from me, just you. You are his wife and you belong to him, and so there is no way that I will do this really bad thing that you want me to do. If I did that bad thing, I would be going against God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Genesis 39:8; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> Day after day, she tried to get him to sleep with her, but every day he said, “No.” He would not listen to her or stay near her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; Ephesians 5:3; Malachi 3:5"> But one day, when Joseph went into the house to do his work, Potifar's wife saw her chance to get him. She knew that there weren't any other men in the house and so she grabbed Joseph's coat and said, “Come to bed with me!”Potifar's wife tried to do the wrong thing with Joseph But Joseph got out of his coat and ran outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> When Potifar's wife saw that she still had Joseph's coat in her hand, she got wild and shouted out to the workers, “Look at this. This coat belongs to that Hebrew stranger. My husband has let him come into my house and he has shamed me. He came and tried to sleep with me like I was his wife, but I screamed and he ran away without his coat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:32; Jeremiah 4:22; Jeremiah 9:3–5"> She kept Joseph's coat and when her husband came home she told him the same story. She said, “That Hebrew stranger you let come into our house tried to shame me. He tried to sleep with me like we were married.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But I screamed and he ran away without his coat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 4:5–6; Proverbs 18:17; Proverbs 6:34–35"> Potifar listened to his wife's story and got really angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 40:15; 2 Timothy 2:9; Psalms 105:18–19"> He arrested Joseph and put him in jail where the king's prisoners were kept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 3:21; Isaiah 41:10; 1 Peter 3:17"> But again, God was with Joseph and was kind to him. God made the boss of the jail respect Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 40:3–4; Genesis 39:4; 1 Samuel 2:30"> The jail boss picked Joseph to be the next boss under him. Joseph had to look after everything that happened in the jail. And he was the boss over all the other prisoners too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Genesis 39:2–3; Psalms 1:3"> And so the guard did not have to worry about anything that happened in the jail. The Lord was with Joseph and helped him do everything just right.</span>
</div>
</div>
