## Esau's family line

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; 1 Chronicles 1:35; Deuteronomy 23:7"> This is the story of Esau's family. Esau's other name was Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 36:25; Genesis 28:9; Genesis 9:25"> Esau married 3 women who came from the country called Canaan. Their names were Adah, Ohol-i-bar-mah and Bas-e-math, whose other name was Ma-ha-lath. Adah was the daughter of a Hittite man called Elon, and she gave Esau a son named Eli-faz. Ohol-i-bar-mah was the daughter of a man named Anah. Her grandfather's name was Zibeon who was a Hivite man. Ohol-i-bar-mah gave Esau 3 sons, Jeush, Jalam and Korah. Bas-e-math's father was Ishmael, Isaac's brother. Bas-e-math had a brother who was named Neb-you-cad-nezzaray-oth. Bas-e-math gave Esau a son named Rew-el. All these children were born while Esau and his wives lived in Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 32:3; Genesis 12:5; Genesis 17:8"> Jacob and Esau had too many animals for that country to feed, so Esau moved away from there and lived in a country called Seir. Seir was a long way from Canaan. Esau took his wives, his children, all his workers, all his animals and everything else that he got while he lived in Canaan.</span>
</div>
</div>

## More story about Esau

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 19:37"> This is the story of Esau's sons and their families that lived in the hill country called Seir. Esau was the father of all the people called Edomites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 36:3–4; 1 Chronicles 1:35–54"> This is the list of Esau's sons. Eli-faz. His mother was Adah Rew-el. His mother was Bas-e-math</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 36:15–16; 1 Chronicles 1:35–36"> Adah and Esau's son Eli-faz had 5 sons. Their names were Teman, Omar, Zefo, Gatam and Kenaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 17:8–16; Deuteronomy 25:17–19; 1 Samuel 15:2–9"> Eli-faz and his second wife named Timna had a son and his name was Amalek. So Elifaz's mother, Adah, was grandmother for all of Eli-faz's 6 boys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:37; Genesis 36:17"> Bas-e-math and Esau's son, Rew-el had 4 sons. Their names were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah and Mizzah. And so Bas-e-math was grandmother for these 4 boys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 36:18; 1 Chronicles 1:35"> Ohol-i-bar-mah, whose father was Anah and whose grandfather was Zibeon had 3 sons with Esau. The son's names were Jeush, Jalam and Korah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Obadiah 1:9; Job 2:11; Genesis 36:11–12"> This is the list of the leaders of the tribes that came from Esau. They all lived in Edom. Esau's first born son was named Eli-faz. Eli-faz's sons were named Teman, Omar, Zefo, Kenaz, Korah, Gatam and Amalek. They were leaders of new tribes in the country of Edom. And Esau's wife Adah was their grandmother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 36:13; 1 Chronicles 1:37; Genesis 36:4"> This next list of men were the sons of Esau's son named Rew-el and they were leaders of new tribes in Edom. Their names were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. Esau's wife Bas-e-math was their grandmother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 36:14; 1 Chronicles 1:35; Genesis 36:5"> This next list of men were Esau and Ohol-i-bar-mah's sons. Their names were Jeush, Jalam and Korah. They were all leaders of new tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And so Esau, whose other name was Edom, was the father of all these men and each of them were leaders for their own tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:12; Deuteronomy 2:22; Genesis 14:6"> Esau and his family moved to a country called Seir, but there were other people already living there. Seir was called after a man with that name. He was from the Horite tribe. This is a list of Seir's sons. Their names were Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. These men were leaders of their tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:39"> Lotan had 2 sons. They were named Hori and Homam. Lotan had a sister named Timna.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:40"> Shobal had 5 sons and they were named Alvan, Mana-hath, Ebal, Sheffo and Onam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:10; 2 Samuel 18:9; 1 Kings 4:28"> Zibeon had 2 sons, and they were named Ayah and Anah. Anah was the man who found springs of hot water in the dry country while he was looking after his father's donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 36:18; 1 Chronicles 1:41"> Anah had 2 children. The boy was named Dishon and the daughter was named Ohol-i-bar-mah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:41"> Dishon had 4 sons and they were named Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran and Keran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:42; Genesis 36:21; 1 Chronicles 1:38"> Ezer had 3 sons and they were named Bilhan, Zaavan and Akan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Lamentations 4:21; Job 1:1; Jeremiah 25:20"> Dishan had 2 sons, and they were named Uz and Aran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 36:20; Genesis 36:28; 1 Chronicles 1:41–42"> This is a list of the leaders of the Horite clans that lived in the country called Seir. Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer and Dishan. The clans were called the same name as their leaders.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Kings of Edom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 17:6; 1 Chronicles 1:43–50; Genesis 17:16"> This is a list of the kings of Edom before the Israel people had a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> The king of Edom was named Bela, and his father was named Beor. Bela's town was called Din-ha-bah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:13; Jeremiah 49:22; Amos 1:12"> When Bela died, Jobab was king. Jobab's father was named Zerah and he came from a place called Bozrah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 36:11; Job 2:11; Genesis 36:15"> When Jobab died, Husham was king. He came from a country where the people were called Temanites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> When Husham died, Hadad was king, and his town was called Avith. Hadad's father was named Bedad, and Hadad's army beat the Midianite army in the country called Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:47"> When Hadad died, Samlah was king. He came from a place called Mas-re-kah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:48; Genesis 10:11"> When Samlah died, Shaul was king. He came from a place called Reho-both. It was next to the You-Fray-tees River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> When Shaul died, Baal-Hanan was king. His father was named Acbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 15:15; 1 Chronicles 1:50"> When Baal-Hanan, the son of Acbor, died, Hadad was king. His town was called Pau and his wife's name was Mehet-abel. She was the daughter of Matred, who was the daughter of Me-Zahab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:51–54; Exodus 15:15; Genesis 36:31"> This is the list of the tribes that came from Esau. It is a list of the leaders and where they lived. Timna, Alvah, Jetheh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Ohol-i-ba-mah, Elah, Pinon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Kenaz, Teman, Mibzar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 45:8; 1 Chronicles 4:14; Genesis 25:30"> Magdiel and Iram. These were the tribal leaders in the country called Edom. Later on, Esau was named the father of the Edomite people.</span>
</div>
</div>
