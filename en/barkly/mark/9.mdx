<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 9:27; Hebrews 2:9; Luke 22:30"> Then Jesus said to them, “Listen carefully. Some of you will see a very special thing happen. You will see this thing happen before you die.” Jesus talked to Moses and Elijah on a big hill<br />And you can read this story in Matthew 17:1-9 and in Luke 9:28-36</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 9:28–36; Isaiah 33:17; Matthew 17:1–8"> Six days later, Jesus took Peter, James and John to the top of a big hill, and while they were standing there, Jesus started to look really different.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 28:3; Daniel 7:9; Revelation 7:14"> His clothes were so white they were shining like the sun. Nobody could wash clothes to make them look so white.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 9:30–31; Matthew 17:3–4; Acts 3:21–24"> Then Peter, James and John saw Jesus talking to Moses and Elijah, those men that lived a long time ago.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 1:23; John 14:8–9; Revelation 22:3–4"> Straight away Peter called out, “Teacher, this is great and it is good for us to be here with you. We should put up 3 shade shelters, one for you, one for Moses and one for Elijah.” Peter, James and John were really frightened and that is why Peter called out like that.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Peter 1:17; Matthew 3:17; Mark 1:11"> Then a cloud covered them all, and God talked to them from the cloud and said, “This is my son. I love him very much. You have to listen to what he tells you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 10:16; Acts 8:39–40; Luke 24:31"> When the cloud had gone away, Peter, James and John looked around for those 2 old men, but they were gone. Only Jesus was standing there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 12:40; Matthew 17:9–13; Mark 5:43"> When Jesus, Peter, James and John were walking back down the hill, Jesus said to them, “I am God's special man from heaven and you must not tell anyone what you just saw. I will die and I will come back alive again. When that happens, you can tell people what you saw today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 17:18; Luke 24:7–8; Luke 18:33–34"> So Peter, James and John kept quiet and they did not tell anyone what they had seen up on the hill. But they did talk to each other about the words that Jesus said about him dying and coming back alive again. They did not understand that part.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Malachi 3:1; Matthew 11:14"> And so they asked Jesus, “Why do our law teachers say that Elijah must come before God's special man can come?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 52:14"> Jesus answered, “Those law teachers are right. Elijah did come to make everything right and people did bad things to him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="-13 Jesus was talking about John the Baptist being like Elijah.">a</a></sup>I am God's special man from heaven, and they will do bad things to me too. They will not listen to me, they will turn against me, and they will give me a lot of pain. All this was written down in God's book a long time ago.” Jesus sent a bad spirit out of a boy<br />And you can read this story in Matthew 17:14-21 and in Luke 9:37-43</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 9:37–42; Matthew 17:14–20; Luke 11:53–54"> When they got down the hill, they saw a big crowd of people standing around the other followers. Those followers were arguing with some Jewish lawmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:30; Mark 9:2–3"> When the crowd saw Jesus, they were surprised and happy, so they ran to welcome him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 5:30–32; Mark 8:11"> Jesus said, “What are you arguing about?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 9:25; Luke 11:14; John 4:47"> One of the men said, “Teacher, I have my son with me, and he has a bad spirit inside him that stops him from talking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Mark 11:23; Matthew 17:16"> The bad spirit makes the boy take a fit whenever it wants to, so that the boy falls to the ground. Spit comes out of his mouth, he grinds his teeth together and his body goes hard like a stone. I asked your followers to make that bad spirit go out of the boy, but they can't do it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; John 20:27; Numbers 14:27"> Jesus said, “O, you people! You just cannot believe, can you? How long do I have to stay here and do everything for you? Bring the boy here to me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 1:26; Luke 4:35; John 8:44"> The people took the boy to Jesus, and as soon as that bad spirit saw Jesus, it made the boy have the biggest fit. He fell to the ground and as he rolled around, a lot of spit came out of his mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 9:33; Acts 4:22; Mark 5:25"> Jesus said to the boy's father, “How long has your boy been like this?”<br />The man said, “O, a long time, right from when he was a little boy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:22–28; Mark 5:19; Matthew 20:34"> Lots of times, the bad spirit makes him fall into the fire and into water. That bad spirit is trying to kill him. Please feel sorry for him and help us if you can.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:40; Hebrews 11:6; Mark 11:23"> Jesus answered the man, “You said, ‘If you can’. Don't you know that God can do anything if you believe in him?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 17:5; Hebrews 12:2; Ephesians 2:8"> The boy's father shouted out, “I do believe in God. But please help me believe in him more!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:18; Luke 9:42; Mark 9:15"> Jesus saw that a lot of people were coming to watch. So he growled at the bad spirit and said, “Listen to me you bad spirit that stops people talking and listening. I am telling you to come out of this little boy right now, and you stay out of him. You are not allowed to go back into him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 9:20; Mark 1:26; Mark 9:18"> The bad spirit screamed as it came out of the boy, and made him take another big fit so that he fell down on the ground. The boy looked like he was dead, and a lot of people said, “Poor thing, he is finished.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Acts 3:7; Mark 1:41"> But Jesus held the boy's hand and helped him to stand up, and the boy's father took him home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Mark 7:17; Matthew 15:15"> Then Jesus and his followers went into a house, and his followers asked him quietly, “Why couldn't we make that bad spirit go out of the boy?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 5:15; Daniel 9:3; Matthew 17:20"> Jesus told them, “There is only one way to make that kind of bad spirit go out of a person. You have to pray and ask God to help you do it.” Jesus told his followers that he was going to die<br />And you can read this story in Matthew 17:22-23 and in Luke 9:43-45</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 17:22–23; Luke 9:43–45; Mark 6:31–32"> Later, Jesus and his followers quietly left that place and went across the country of Galilee. Jesus did not want everyone to know where they were going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Mark 9:12; Luke 24:44–46"> He wanted to have time alone with his followers so that he could teach them. He said, “I am God's special man from heaven, but somebody is going to turn against me and help my enemies to arrest me. They will kill me, but I will only be dead for 3 days, and then I will come alive again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 2:50; Luke 9:45; Luke 18:34"> His followers did not understand what he said, but they were too frightened to ask him about it. Jesus' followers argued about who was the most important person in their group<br />And you can read this story in Matthew 18:1-5 and in Luke 9:46-48</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 18:1–5; Hebrews 4:13; Luke 9:46–48"> While they walked along the road to Cap-per-ni-um (Capernaum), Jesus' followers argued with each other about which one of them was the most important person in their group. When they arrived and went into the house where they were going to stay, Jesus asked them, “What were you arguing about on the road?” His followers did not answer him. They were too shamed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:25–28; Mark 10:42–45; Luke 22:26"> Jesus sat down and called his followers to come and listen to him. He said, “A real leader is a person that says to themselves, ‘I am not more important than anybody else.’ So if anyone wants to be a leader, that person has to think that they are not important. They have to work to help everyone else.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 10:16; Matthew 19:14–15; Matthew 18:2"> Jesus found a little boy and put him in the middle of the group. He picked the boy up and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 18:3–5; Luke 10:16; Matthew 10:40–42"> “When a person loves me and is kind and friendly to a little child, then that person is being kind and friendly to me. And if that person is kind and friendly to me, they are being kind and friendly to my father too. And he is the one who sent me.” A man used Jesus' name to send bad spirits out of people<br />And you can read this story in Luke 9:49-50</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 9:49–50; Numbers 11:26–29; Luke 11:19"> John, one of the followers, said to Jesus, “Teacher, we saw a man using your name to send bad spirits out of people. He is not one of our group and so we told him to stop.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:3; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Matthew 13:28–29"> But Jesus said, “No! Don't stop him. If he uses my name to do good and powerful things for people, it means that he respects me. And if he respects me, he will not say bad things about me later on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 12:30; Luke 11:23"> Anybody that is not against us, is really on our side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; Matthew 25:40; 1 Corinthians 15:23"> And if anybody sees that you belong to me and gives you a drink of water, God will do something good for them and no-one can take that good thing away from them.” Jesus told some stories about the right way to live<br />And you can read this story in Matthew 18:6-9 and in Luke 17:1-2</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 18:6; Luke 17:1–2; Matthew 18:10"> Jesus was still holding the child when he said, “Listen, there might be a young child that believes in me. If anyone makes that child turn away from God, I tell you, it would be better if a really heavy stone was tied to the person's neck. And then, that person was pushed into the sea. Drowning in the sea would be better than God punishing them for making that child turn away from God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 5:29–30; Matthew 18:8–9; Matthew 25:41"> And if there is something that makes you go against God then you have to do something about it. If you want to do something bad with your hand, cut it off. It is better for you to live with God forever with only one hand, than to go to hell with 2 hands, where the fire never goes out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 18:8; Matthew 5:22; Mark 9:43"> In the same way, if you want to do something bad with your foot, cut it off. It is better for you to live with God forever with only one foot, than to go to hell with 2 feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 18:9; Mark 9:43; Matthew 5:22"> And it is the same for your eye. If you are looking at something and it makes you do the wrong thing, dig your eye out. It is better for you to be in God's family with only one eye, than to go to hell with 2 eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Isaiah 66:24; Mark 9:43–45; Matthew 25:41"> People that go to hell will feel like they are being eaten by worms that never die. And they will feel like their bodies are being burnt by a fire that never goes out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Leviticus 2:13; Ezekiel 43:24"> You know how you put a little bit of salt on your food, well just like that, God sends little bits of trouble to people. And those troubles are like small fires.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Romans 12:18; Matthew 5:13"> And listen to this other story about salt. You know how you put salt on your food to make your food taste better? I tell you, if salt loses its salty taste, it is useless and you cannot make it good again. Think about this. You have to be like good salt for everybody. Yes, be like that salt so that you can all live quietly together.”</span>
</div>
