## The Jewish Leaders took Jesus to the Roman court

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 27:1–2; Luke 23:1–5; Luke 22:66"> Early in the morning, the Chief Priests (Jewish ceremony leaders), the lawmen, the Jewish elders and all the members of the Jewish council had a meeting. At that meeting they made up their minds to tie Jesus up and take him to the Roman leader in Jerusalem. The Roman leader's name was Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Matthew 27:11–14; 1 Timothy 6:13"> When they got there, Pilate started to ask Jesus some questions. He said, “Are you the King of the Jews?”<br />Jesus said, “You can call me that.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; Mark 15:5; John 18:29–31"> Then the Chief Priests said to Pilate, “This man has done a lot of bad things.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 19:10; Matthew 27:13; Matthew 26:62"> So Pilate said to Jesus, “The Chief Priests are saying lots of bad things about you. Why don't you say something for yourself?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; Zechariah 3:8; Psalms 71:7"> But Jesus said nothing, and Pilate was really amazed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:39–40; Acts 25:9; Acts 24:27"> At that time there was a man in jail called Bar-abbas (Barabbas). He was one of the men that were always fighting against the Romans to try and make them leave the Jewish country. He had killed a man in one of those fights, and that is why he was in jail.<br />Every year at Passover time, Pilate let one Jewish prisoner go free. But he let the Jewish people pick that prisoner.<br />And so just before the Passover ceremony began, a big crowd of people went to see Pilate and they said, “This is the time for you to let one of our people go free.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:14–16; John 19:4–5; John 18:39"> Pilate knew that the Chief Priests were jealous of Jesus and that was why they had arrested him. So Pilate said to the crowd, “Do you want me to let the King of the Jews go free?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 3:14; John 18:40; Hosea 5:1"> But the Chief Priests told the crowd to ask for Bar-abbas, and so the crowd called out, “No! Not him! Give us Bar-abbas.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; Isaiah 9:6–7; Zechariah 9:9"> Pilate said, “So what do you want me to do with this man you call the King of the Jews?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> All the people shouted, “Nail him to a cross.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:41; Luke 23:23–24; Isaiah 53:3"> Pilate said, “Why? What has he done wrong?”<br />But the crowd did not listen. They shouted out even louder, “Nail him to a cross.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 19:1; Matthew 27:26; Isaiah 50:6"> Pilate wanted to make the crowd happy and so he told his soldiers to let Bar-abbas go free from jail. Then Pilate said to his soldiers, “Take Jesus outside and flog him and then take him away and nail him up on a cross to die.” The soldiers made fun of Jesus<br />And you can read this story in Matthew 27:27-31; and in John 19:2-3</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 19:9; Matthew 27:27–31; John 18:28"> The soldiers took Jesus into the yard outside where Pilate lived, and they called out to all the other soldiers to come and watch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 27:28–30; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> First they put a purple coat on Jesus, so that he looked like a king. Then they found some prickly sticks and made them into a headband so that it looked like a king's crown, and they pushed it onto Jesus' head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:42–43; Mark 15:2; Genesis 37:10"> Then they started to tease him. They got down on their knees in front of Jesus and said, “Hey, you King of the Jews. You are so great!” And they kept on hitting Jesus on the head with a stick, and they spat on him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 19:16; Matthew 27:31"> When they finished making fun of Jesus, they took off the long purple coat and put his own clothes back on him. Then they took him out of the town to nail him up on a cross.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:32; Luke 23:26; Romans 16:13"> On the way, they met a man called Simon from Sy-reen (Cyrene). He was the father of Alexander and Rufus, and he was coming back into town from the farm lands outside Jerusalem. The soldiers grabbed Simon and made him carry the cross for Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 27:33–44; John 19:17–27; Luke 23:27–38"> The soldiers took Jesus to a place called Golgotha. In their language it means Skull Place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 27:34; John 19:28–30; Luke 23:36"> And they tried to make Jesus drink some wine that had a drug called Merr (Myrrh) in it. But he would not drink it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 23:34; John 19:23–24; Matthew 27:35–36"> It was 9 o'clock in the morning when the soldiers nailed Jesus' hands and feet to the cross. They wrote The King of the Jews on a piece of wood and nailed it to the cross above his head. Then they gambled for Jesus' clothes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 23:32–33; Matthew 27:38; John 19:18"> And there were 2 men that had broken Roman laws, so the soldiers nailed them onto crosses and put them up next to Jesus. They put one man on Jesus' left-hand side and they put the other man on Jesus' right-hand side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Psalms 109:25; Lamentations 1:12"> There were people walking by and as they looked at Jesus they rubbished him. They shook their heads and said, “You think you can knock down the Temple, and then build it up again in 3 days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Why don't you save yourself? Go on, get down from that cross!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:23–24; Matthew 27:41–43; Luke 23:35–37"> The Chief Priests and the law teachers teased Jesus too. They said to each other, “He saved other people, but he cannot save himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Luke 23:39–43; Zechariah 9:9"> If he really is the man God promised to send and if he really is the King of the Israel nation, he has to prove it. If he comes down from that cross, we will believe in him.”<br />The men on the other crosses joined in and rubbished Jesus too. Jesus died<br />And you can read this story in Matthew 27:45-56, in Luke 23:44-49 and in John 19:28-30</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Luke 23:44–45; Amos 8:9–10"> In the middle of the day, at about 12 o'clock, the sky went dark like it was night time. And it stayed dark for about 3 hours until 3 o'clock in the afternoon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Matthew 27:46; Hebrews 5:7"> At 3 o'clock Jesus called out in their language, and said, “My God, my God, why did you go away and leave me all alone?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:47–49; Mark 9:11–13; Matthew 17:11–13"> Some of the people standing there heard what Jesus said, and they thought that he was calling out for Elijah to come from heaven to help him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Mark 15:23; John 19:28–30"> One man ran and put sour wine on a rag. Then he put the rag on a stick and held it up to Jesus' mouth for him to have a drink. And then the man said, “Ok, leave him alone now. Let's wait and see if Elijah will come and get him down from the cross.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 19:30; Luke 23:46; Matthew 27:50"> Jesus called out again, and then he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–23; Luke 23:45; Hebrews 9:3–12"> Inside the Temple in Jerusalem, there was a big curtain. When Jesus died, that curtain ripped into 2 pieces, from the top of the curtain to the bottom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Luke 23:47–48; Matthew 27:43"> And back at the cross, the leader of those Roman soldiers was standing near Jesus. He saw the way Jesus died, and he said, “It is true. This man really was the son of God.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Some women saw everything that happened

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Luke 8:2–3; Matthew 27:55–56"> While Jesus was on the cross, some women were watching from a long way off. These women had come with Jesus and his followers to Jerusalem. They were Mary Mag-da-lean (Magdalene), Sal-o-me (Salome), and another Mary who was the mother of young James and Joses. And there were some other women too. Before this time, these women had looked after Jesus and went everywhere with him and his followers in Galilee. Joseph buried Jesus<br />And you can read this story in Matthew 27:57-61, in Luke 23:50-56 and in John 19:38-42</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 23:50–56; John 19:38–42; John 19:31"> The day that Jesus died was the day when the Jewish people got everything ready for their Sabbath rest day. That rest day started after the sun went down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 2:38; Luke 2:25; Luke 23:51"> There was a man called Joseph who came from Ari-ma-thee-a (Arimathea). He was an important man in the Jewish council and everyone said that he was a good man. Joseph was waiting for the time when God was going to be the King over everybody in the world.<br />Joseph was not frightened of Pilate and so when it was dark, and nearly time for the Jewish rest day to start, he went to see Pilate. Joseph asked Pilate if it was alright for him to take Jesus' body down from the cross and bury it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 19:31–37"> Pilate was amazed. He said, “Is that man dead already?”<br />He sent somebody to get the leader of the soldiers and said, “Is it true? Is that man really dead?”<br />The leader of the soldiers said, “Yes, it is true.”<br />And so Pilate told Joseph that he was allowed to take Jesus' body down from the cross and bury it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 22:16"> Joseph took Jesus' body down from the cross and wrapped it in a sheet. He put Jesus' body in a cave that somebody had cut inside a stoney hill. Then he rolled a very big stone in front of the cave to block the door.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 15:40; Mark 16:1; Luke 23:55–2"> Mary Mag-da-lean and Mary the mother of Joses, saw where Joseph put Jesus' body.</span>
</div>
</div>
