## Jesus went into Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:1–11; John 12:14–19; Luke 19:29–40"> When Jesus and his followers got near to Jerusalem they stopped on the side of a hill called The Mount of Olives. There were 2 towns there and they were called Bethany and Beth-far-gee (Bethphage). Jesus told 2 of his followers to go into one of those towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 21:2–3; Luke 19:30–31"> He said, “When you get there, you will see a donkey tied to a post. Nobody has ever sat on that donkey before, but I want you to untie it and bring it to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 10:36; Mark 14:15; Psalms 110:3"> If somebody wants to know why you are taking that donkey, you can say, ‘The Lord needs it for a while, but don't worry, later on he will send it back to you.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 26:19; Luke 19:32–34; Matthew 21:6–7"> The 2 men went into the town. They found the donkey tied up outside the door of a house, and they started to untie it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Some people standing there said to them, “What are you doing? Why are you untying that young donkey?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Jesus' followers said, “The Lord needs it for a while, but don't worry, later on he will send it back to you.” So the people let the followers take the donkey away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Luke 19:35–36; Matthew 21:7–8"> The men took the young donkey back to Jesus and put their coats onto the donkey's back. Then Jesus sat on the donkey and started to ride into Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40"> A lot of people put their coats down on the road in front of the donkey, and other people cut leaves from trees and put them on the road too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 118:25–26; Matthew 21:9; Matthew 23:39"> Some of the people walked in front of Jesus, and some people walked behind him.<br />They shouted,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Say good things about the God who will save us. The man that came from God is strong and powerful.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Luke 2:14; Luke 19:38–40"> <span className="pl pl-1">God's nation is coming and it will be strong and powerful again like it was in King David's time.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Yes, say good things about the God who lives in heaven. He will save us.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 8:1–2; Luke 19:41–45; Luke 21:37–38"> Jesus went into Jerusalem and walked into the Jewish ceremony house called the Temple to have a good look at what was going on in there.The Temple in Jerusalem But it was late and the sun was starting to go down, so Jesus left Jerusalem, and went back to Bethany with his 12 followers. Jesus cursed a fruit tree<br />And you can read this story in Matthew 21:18-19</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:18–22; Matthew 4:2; John 19:28"> The next morning, Jesus was hungry. He left Bethany and started to walk back to Jerusalem with his followers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Luke 13:6–9; Isaiah 5:7"> As they walked along, Jesus saw a fruit tree in front of him and so he went to see if there was any fruit. But it was the wrong time of year, and so he did not find any fruit, he just found leaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Matthew 7:19; Matthew 3:10"> His followers heard him say to the fruit tree, “No-one will ever eat any fruit from you again.” Jesus forced some people out of the Temple<br />And you can read this story in Matthew 21:12-17 in Luke 19:45-48 and in John 2:13-22</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 2:13–17; Matthew 21:12–16; Luke 19:45–47"> Jesus and his followers went back to Jerusalem and walked into the Temple yard. In the yard there were people buying and selling animals and birds that were going to be killed and used in the Temple ceremonies. There were other men too that swapped Roman money for Temple money so that people could pay their Temple tax. Those men were called money changers. Jesus forced them out of the yard and pushed over their tables and chairs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He would not let anyone use the Temple yard as a shop.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Jeremiah 7:11; Luke 19:46"> Then Jesus started to teach the people. He said, “A long time ago God told 2 men to write down these words in his book. They wrote,<br />‘I want my house to be a place where people from every tribe can come and pray.’ But you have made my house to be like a place where robbers hide.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Mark 12:12; Mark 14:1–2"> When the Chief Priests (Jewish ceremony leaders) and the law teachers heard what Jesus said, they were very angry. They started to think about how they could kill Jesus but they were frightened of him. They could see how happy and excited the people were when Jesus was teaching them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 21:37; Mark 11:11; John 12:36"> Later that day, when the sun started to go down, Jesus and his followers left Jerusalem and went back to Bethany. The fruit tree died<br />And you can read this story in Matthew 21:20-22</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Mark 11:14; John 15:6"> In the morning, Jesus and his followers walked past the fruit tree again. They saw that it was dead right down to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 3:33; Matthew 23:7; Matthew 25:41"> Peter remembered the words Jesus said to the fruit tree the day before. So he said, “Teacher, that tree you cursed is already dead.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; John 14:1; Mark 9:23"> Jesus said, “You should really trust God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 17:20; John 15:7; Matthew 21:21"> I tell you, someone might ask God to make this big hill jump into the sea. That person must not say, ‘Might be God will do it.’ That person has to truly believe with all their heart that God will do it and God will force that hill to jump into the sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; 1 John 5:14–15; 1 John 3:22"> So if you ask God for anything, you have to believe that God will give it to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; Colossians 3:13; Ephesians 4:32"> And when you are praying, if someone has done something bad to you, you have to forgive them. If you forgive that person, your father in heaven will forgive you for all the bad things you have done.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="-26 Some other Bibles have these words as well. “But if you don't forgive other people, then your Father in heaven will not forgive your sins. He will not forgive you for the bad things you have done.”">a</a></sup>The Jewish leaders asked Jesus a question<br />And you can read this story in Matthew 21:23-27 and in Luke 20:1-8</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 20:1–8; Matthew 21:23–27; Psalms 2:1–5"> When Jesus and his followers got to Jerusalem, they walked into the Temple yard again. Some of the Chief Priests, law teachers and Jewish elders walked up to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; Acts 7:38–39; Numbers 16:3"> They said, “Who told you to force all those people out of this yard? Who said that you are allowed to do that?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 20:3–8; Matthew 21:24; Isaiah 52:13"> Jesus said to them, “First, you answer my question. If you answer my question, then I will tell you who said that I was allowed to force those men out of the yard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 3:1–20; John 3:25–36; Mark 1:1–11"> This is my question. Who told John to do that washing ceremony called baptism in the Jordan River? Did someone here on earth tell John to do that, or did God tell him to do it?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:29; John 1:34; Matthew 21:31–32"> Those Jewish leaders talked together to work out their answer. They said to each other, “If we say that God told John to baptise people, then we will be in trouble. Jesus will say to us, ‘If God told John to baptise people, why didn't you listen to him?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 21:46; Mark 12:12; Matthew 14:5"> And if we say that someone here on earth told John to baptise people, we will still be in trouble.” All the people believed that God sent John and so those Jewish leaders were frightened of them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12"> The Jewish leaders went back to Jesus and said, “We do not know who told John to baptise people in the river.”<br />Then Jesus said, “You don't want to answer me, and so I don't want to answer you. I will not tell you who said that I can do these things.”</span>
</div>
</div>
