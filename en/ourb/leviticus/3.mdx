<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 6:14; Leviticus 1:3; Romans 5:1–2"> “‘If his offering is a sacrifice of peace offerings; if he offers it from the herd, whether male or female, he shall offer it without defect before Adonai.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:13; Leviticus 4:8–9; Exodus 29:22"> He shall offer of the sacrifice of peace offerings an offering made by fire to Adonai; the fat that covers the innards, and all the fat that is on the innards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 3:10; Leviticus 8:25; Exodus 29:22"> and the two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, which is by the loins, and the cover on the liver, with the kidneys, he shall take away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 6:12; Exodus 29:13; 1 Peter 2:5"> Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;’s sons shall burn it on the altar on the burnt offering, which is on the wood that is on the fire: it is an offering made by fire, of a pleasant aroma to Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:11–12; Galatians 3:28; Leviticus 1:2"> “‘If his offering for a sacrifice of peace offerings to Adonai is from the flock; male or female, he shall offer it without defect.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 1:5; Ephesians 3:12; Leviticus 3:13"> and he shall lay his hand on the head of his offering, and kill it before the Tent of Meeting: and Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;’s sons shall sprinkle its blood around on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 29:22; Leviticus 9:19; Leviticus 8:25"> He shall offer from the sacrifice of peace offerings an offering made by fire to Adonai; its fat, the entire tail fat, he shall take away close to the backbone; and the fat that covers the inwards, and all the fat that is on the inwards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 3:4"> and the two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, which is by the loins, and the cover on the liver, with the kidneys, he shall take away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 21:17; Leviticus 21:6; Leviticus 3:16"> The priest shall burn it on the altar: it is the food of the offering made by fire to Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 9:15; Isaiah 53:2; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> “‘If his offering is a goat, then he shall offer it before Adonai:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 3:8; Romans 5:15–21; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> and he shall lay his hand on its head, and kill it before the Tent of Meeting; and the sons of Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; shall sprinkle its blood around on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 26:38; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 22:37; Leviticus 3:9–11"> He shall offer from it as his offering, an offering made by fire to Adonai; the fat that covers the innards, and all the fat that is on the innards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and the two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, which is by the loins, and the cover on the liver, with the kidneys, he shall take away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 7:23–25; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 22:37; Isaiah 53:10"> The priest shall burn them on the altar: it is the food of the offering made by fire, for a pleasant aroma; all the fat is Adonai’s.</span>
