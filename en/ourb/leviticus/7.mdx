<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 6:17; Leviticus 5:14–7; Leviticus 6:25"> “‘This is the Torah ·Teaching· of the fault incurring guilt offering. It is most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 1:5; Leviticus 6:25; Leviticus 1:11"> In the place where they kill the burnt offering, he shall kill the fault incurring guilt offering; and its blood he shall sprinkle around on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:13; Leviticus 3:15–16; Psalms 51:6"> He shall offer all of its fat: the fat tail, and the fat that covers the innards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 3:4"> and the two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, which is by the loins, and the cover on the liver, with the kidneys, shall he take away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 1:13; Galatians 5:24; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> and the priest shall burn them on the altar for an offering made by fire to Adonai: it is a fault incurring guilt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 6:29; Numbers 18:9–10; Leviticus 2:3"> Every male among the priests may eat of it. It shall be eaten in a holy place. It is most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 6:25–26; Leviticus 14:13; 1 Corinthians 9:13"> “‘As is the habitual sin ·miss the mark· offering, so is the fault incurring guilt offering; there is one Torah ·Teaching· for them. The priest who makes atonement with them shall have it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 4:11; Exodus 29:14; Romans 13:14"> The priest who offers any man’s burnt offering, even the priest shall have for himself the skin of the burnt offering which he has offered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 18:9; Ezekiel 44:29; Leviticus 2:10"> Every meal offering that is baked in the oven, and all that is dressed in the pan, and on the griddle, shall be the priest’s who offers it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14; Exodus 16:18"> Every meal offering, mixed with oil or dry, belongs to all the sons of Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;, one as well as another. (3)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 22:18–21; Ezekiel 45:15; Leviticus 3:1–17"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn=". Lev 7:11 (Lev 7:11-21) (#2b.128):  “The Law of the sacrifice of the peace offering” (v11) T. Directive: Among the sacrifices for Thanksgiving (v12-15), one of each gift (sacrifice animal) is to be presented to the Cohen ·Priest· as his possession, the rest of the animals and the matzah ·unleavened bread· associated will be offered up to Adonai on the altar. The meat is to be eaten that same day T. Directive: For sacrifices connected with a Vow Offerings (v16-18), the same directions apply, except the meat may be eaten on the second day also, but not on the third day / T. Directive: For Vow Offerings, any meat eaten on the third day will cause the offering to be not accepted and he will bear the consequences of his wrongdoing T. Directive: The Peace Offering meat may not touch anything unclean, otherwise it must be burned up completely / Directive: Any clean person may eat the Peace Offerings / T. Directive: Any person who is unclean may not offer Peace Offerings, nor may any person who touched something unclean touch the Peace Offerings / Consequence: Such a person will be cut off from his people">a</a></sup>“‘This is the Torah ·Teaching· of the sacrifice of peace offerings, which one shall offer to Adonai .</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 2:4; Numbers 6:15; 1 Peter 2:5"> If he offers it for a thanksgiving, then he shall offer with the sacrifice of thanksgiving<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn=". Quoted in  Heb 13:15">b</a></sup>unleavened cakes mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil, and cakes mixed with oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 4:5; Leviticus 23:17; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:33"> With cakes of leavened bread he shall offer his offering with the sacrifice of his peace offerings for thanksgiving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:19; Exodus 29:27–28; Numbers 18:24–32"> Of it he shall offer one out of each offering for a heave offering to Adonai. It shall be the priest’s who sprinkles the blood of the peace offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 22:29–30"> The flesh of the sacrifice of his peace offerings for thanksgiving shall be eaten on the day of his offering. He shall not leave any of it until the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:5–8; Deuteronomy 12:6; Psalms 116:14"> “‘But if the sacrifice of his offering is a vow, or a free will offering, it shall be eaten on the day that he offers his sacrifice; and on the next day what remains of it shall be eaten:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:10; Hosea 6:2; 1 Corinthians 15:4"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn=". Lev 7:17 (Lev 7:16-18) (#2b.129):  T. To burn the flesh of peace offering sacrifice that remains on the third day / R. To burn the leftover sacrifices">c</a></sup>but what remains of the meat of the sacrifice on the third day shall be burned with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:7–8; Leviticus 11:41; Numbers 18:27"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn=". Lev 7:18 (Lev 7:16-18) (#2b.130):  T. Not to eat the sacrifice flesh after three days / R. Not to eat the abomination of intended delay; it is punished by uncleanness and removal / R. Not to eat from sacrifices offered with improper intentions">d</a></sup>If any of the meat of the sacrifice of his peace offerings is eaten on the third day, it will not be accepted, and it shall not be credited to him who offers it. It will be an abomination, and the soul who eats any of it will bear his depravity (moral evil).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 11:41; Titus 1:15; Acts 10:15–16"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn=". Lev 7:19 (Lev 7:19-20) (#2b.131):  Not to eat consecrated foods that have become unclean">e</a></sup>“‘The meat that touches any unclean thing shall not be eaten.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn=". Lev 7:19 (Lev 7:19-20) (#2b.132):  T. Sacrificial meat that touches something unclean must be burned with fire / R. To burn the flesh of any sacrifice that has become ceremonially unclean">f</a></sup>It shall be burned with fire. As for the meat, everyone who is clean may eat it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 15:2–33; Genesis 17:14; Leviticus 22:3–7"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn=". Lev 7:20 (Lev 7:19-21) (#2b.133):  T. A Cohen ·Priest· who is unclean shall not eat any of the peace offering food / R. A person who is unclean shall not eat consecrated food nor holy things">g</a></sup>but the soul who eats of the meat of the sacrifice of peace offerings, that belongs to Adonai, having his uncleanness on him, that soul shall be cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 12:1–59; Leviticus 11:20; Ezekiel 4:14"> When anyone touches any unclean thing, the uncleanness of man, or an unclean animal, or any unclean abomination, and eats some of the meat of the sacrifice of peace offerings, which belong to Adonai, that soul shall be cut off from his people.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Adonai spoke to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 3:16–17; Leviticus 17:6; 1 Samuel 2:15–17"> “Speak to the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, saying, ‘You shall eat no fat, of bull, or sheep, or goat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 22:31; Leviticus 17:15; Leviticus 22:8"> The fat of that which dies of itself, and the fat of that which is torn of animals, may be used for any other service, but you shall in no way eat of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For whoever eats the fat of the animal, of which men offer an offering made by fire to Adonai, even the soul who eats it shall be cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 9:4; Leviticus 3:17; Acts 15:20"> You shall not eat any blood, whether it is of bird or of animal, in any of your dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 10:29; Leviticus 7:20–21; Leviticus 7:25"> Whoever it is who eats any blood, that soul shall be cut off from his people.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Adonai spoke to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 1:20; 1 John 1:7; Leviticus 3:1–17"> “Speak to the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, saying, ‘He who offers the sacrifice of his peace offerings to Adonai shall bring his offering to Adonai out of the sacrifice of his peace offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 6:20; Leviticus 3:9; Leviticus 3:14"> With his own hands he shall bring the offerings of Adonai made by fire. He shall bring the fat with the breast, that the breast may be waved for a wave offering before Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 3:5; Leviticus 3:11; Leviticus 3:16"> The priest shall burn the fat on the altar, but the breast shall be Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;’s and his sons’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 6:20; Leviticus 7:34; Leviticus 9:21"> The right thigh you shall give to the priest for a heave offering out of the sacrifices of your peace offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 26:1–46; Leviticus 6:1–30; Leviticus 7:3"> He among the sons of Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; who offers the blood of the peace offerings, and the fat, shall have the right thigh for a portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 18:18–19; Leviticus 10:14–15; Exodus 29:9"> For the breast that is waved and the thigh that is raised I have taken from the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; out of the sacrifices of their peace offerings, and have given them to Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; the priest and to his sons as their chok ·portion· forever from the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:20; 1 John 2:27; Numbers 18:7–19"> This is the anointing portion of Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;, and the anointing portion of his sons, out of the offerings of Adonai made by fire, in the day when he presented them to minister to Adonai in the priest’s office;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Leviticus 8:12; Leviticus 8:30; Exodus 40:13–15"> which Adonai enjoined to be given them of the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, in the day that he anointed them, by a regulation forever throughout all their generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 29:1; Leviticus 1:1–6; Leviticus 6:9–18"> This is the Torah ·Teaching· of the burnt offering, of the meal offering, and of the habitual sin ·miss the mark· offering, and of the fault incurring guilt offering, and of the consecration, and of the sacrifice of peace offerings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 1:1–2"> which Adonai enjoined Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; in Mount Sinai &#91;Thorn&#93;, in the day that he enjoined the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; to offer their offerings to Adonai, in the wilderness of Sinai &#91;Thorn&#93;.</span>
