<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Leviticus 13:1; Leviticus 11:1"> Adonai spoke to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; and to Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; 2 Samuel 3:29; Leviticus 22:4"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn=". Lev 15:2-3 (Lev 15:1-12, 15:13-15, 15:31) (#3.221):  T. A man having a discharge from the body, the discharge is unclean / R. Observe the laws of impurity caused by a man’s running issue">a</a></sup>“Speak to the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, and tell them, ‘When any man has a discharge from his body, because of his discharge he is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:20; Ezekiel 16:26; Leviticus 12:3"> This shall be his uncleanness in his discharge: whether his body runs with his discharge, or his body has stopped from his discharge, it is his uncleanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Ephesians 5:11; 1 Corinthians 15:33"> “‘Every bed whereon he who has the discharge lies shall be unclean; and everything he sits on shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 16:26; Leviticus 17:15"> Whoever touches his bed shall wash his clothes, and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 1:16"> He who sits on anything whereon the man who has the discharge sat shall wash his clothes, and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> “‘He who touches the body of him who has the discharge shall wash his clothes, and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:4; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Isaiah 1:16"> “‘If he who has the discharge spits on him who is clean, then he shall wash his clothes, and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:34"> “‘Whatever saddle he who has the discharge rides on shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:8; Psalms 26:6; Leviticus 15:8"> Whoever touches anything that was under him shall be unclean until the evening. He who carries those things shall wash his clothes, and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> “‘Whoever he who has the discharge touches, without having rinsed his hands in water, he shall wash his clothes, and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 11:32–33; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> “‘The earthen vessel, which he who has the discharge touches, shall be broken; and every vessel of wood shall be rinsed in water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 14:8; Leviticus 8:33; Leviticus 15:28"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn=". Lev 15:13-14 (Lev 15:13-15, 15:31) (#3.222):  T. After a person is cleansed from any bodily discharge causing them to be unclean, they will count seven days, then they are to wash their body and clothes in a mikvah ·ritual washing, baptism· and on the eighth day to offer sacrifice / R. A man who had a running issue must bring an offering after he goes to the mikvah ·ritual washing, baptism·">b</a></sup>“‘When he who has a discharge is cleansed of his discharge, then he shall count to himself seven days for his cleansing, and wash his clothes; and he shall bathe his flesh in running water, and shall be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 12:8; Leviticus 12:6; Hebrews 10:10"> “‘On the eighth day he shall take two turtledoves, or two young pigeons, and come before Adonai to the door of the Tent of Meeting, and give them to the priest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 14:30–31; Leviticus 4:26; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 3:17"> and the priest shall offer them, the one for a habitual sin ·miss the mark· offering, and the other for a burnt offering. The priest shall make atonement for him before Adonai for his discharge. (RY:7, LY:6)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:10–11; Leviticus 22:4; Leviticus 15:5"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn=". Lev 15:16 (Lev 15:16-18, 15:31) (#3.223):  Seminal fluid emission causes defilement">c</a></sup>“‘If any man has an emission of semen,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn=". Lev 15:16 (Lev 15:16-18, 15:31) (#3.224):  T. Purification from all kinds of bodily discharge causing uncleanness is to be effected by immersion in the waters of a mikvah ·ritual washing, baptism· / R. Every unclean person must immerse himself in a mikvah ·ritual washing, baptism· to become pure">d</a></sup>then he shall bathe all his flesh in water, and be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Every garment, and every skin, whereon the semen is, shall be washed with water, and be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–5; 1 Corinthians 6:12"> If a man lies with a woman and there is an emission of semen, they shall both bathe themselves in water, and be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 12:2; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 15:19; Mark [Hammer] 5:25"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn=". Lev 15:19 (Lev 15:19:24, 15:31) (#3.225):  T. A woman in niddah ·menstruating· is unclean for seven days and causes defilement for anyone who touches her / R. A menstruating woman is unclean and causes defilement">e</a></sup>“‘If a woman has a discharge, and her discharge in her flesh is blood, she shall be in her impurity seven days: and whoever touches her shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 15:4–9; Proverbs 2:16–19; Proverbs 6:35"> “‘Everything that she lies on in her impurity shall be unclean. Everything also that she sits on shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 7:14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Isaiah 22:14"> Whoever touches her bed shall wash his clothes, and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Whoever touches anything that she sits on shall wash his clothes, and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> If it is on the bed, or on anything whereon she sits, when he touches it, he shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 20:18; 1 Peter 2:11; Leviticus 18:19"> “‘If any man lies with her, and her monthly flow is on him, he shall be unclean seven days; and every bed whereon he lies shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark [Hammer] 5:25; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 9:20; Luke [White-light] 8:43"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn=". Lev 15:25 (Lev 15:25-30, 15:31) (#3.226):  T. A woman having a not-regular flow of blood (whether it is excessively long or at the wrong time) is unclean like niddah ·menstruation· and causes defilement for anyone who touches her / R. Observe the laws of impurity caused by a woman’s running issue">f</a></sup>“‘If a woman has a discharge of her blood many days not in the time of her period, or if she has a discharge beyond the time of her period; all the days of the discharge of her uncleanness shall be as in the days of her period: she is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Every bed whereon she lies all the days of her discharge shall be to her as the bed of her period: and everything whereon she sits shall be unclean, as the uncleanness of her period.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 15:21; Hebrews 10:22; Leviticus 17:15–16"> Whoever touches these things shall be unclean, and shall wash his clothes, and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 15:13–15; Galatians 3:13; Ephesians 1:6–7"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn=". Lev 15:28-29 (Lev 15:28-30, 15:31) (#3.227):  T. After a female person is cleansed from an unclean issue, she is to offer sacrifice / R. A woman who had a running issue must bring an offering after she goes to the mikvah ·ritual washing, baptism·">g</a></sup>“‘But if she is cleansed of her discharge, then she shall count to herself seven days, and after that she shall be clean. (LY:7)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> On the eighth day she shall take two turtledoves, or two young pigeons, and bring them to the priest, to the door of the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> The priest shall offer the one for a habitual sin ·miss the mark· offering, and the other for a burnt offering; and the priest shall make atonement for her before Adonai for the uncleanness of her discharge. (Maftir ·Conclusion·)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 19:20; Numbers 19:13; Ezekiel 5:11"> “‘Thus you shall separate the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; from their uncleanness, so they will not die in their uncleanness, when they defile my tabernacle that is among them.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 11:46; Leviticus 13:59; Numbers 6:13"> This is the Torah ·Teaching· of him who has a discharge, and of him who has an emission of semen, so that he is unclean thereby;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 15:19; Leviticus 20:18; Leviticus 15:24–25"> and of her who has her period, and of a man or woman who has a discharge, and of him who lies with her who is unclean.</span>
