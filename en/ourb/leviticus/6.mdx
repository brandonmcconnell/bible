<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew ch 6 v. 1">e</a></sup>Adonai spoke to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 6:12–13; Numbers 28:3; Exodus 29:38–42"> “Enjoin Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; and his sons, saying, ‘This is the Torah ·Teaching· of the burnt offering: the burnt offering shall be on the hearth on the altar all night until the morning; and the fire of the altar shall be kept burning on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 28:39–43; Leviticus 16:4; Leviticus 1:13"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn=". Lev 6:10 [Heb Bible Lev 6:3] (#2b.119):  Remove the ashes from the fire on the Altar of Burnt Offerings daily">f</a></sup>The priest shall put on his linen garment, and he shall put on his linen breeches upon his body; and he shall remove the ashes from where the fire has consumed the burnt offering on the altar, and he shall put them beside the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 4:12; Ezekiel 44:19; Hebrews 13:11–13"> He shall take off his garments, and put on other garments, and carry the ashes outside the camp to a clean place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 9:24; Exodus 29:38–42; Nehemiah 13:31"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn=". Lev 6:12 (Lev 6:12-13) [Heb Bible Lev 6:5 (Lev 6:5-6)] (#2b.120):  T. To have a fire burning continually on the Altar of Burnt Offerings / R. To light a fire on the altar every day">g</a></sup>The fire on the altar shall be kept burning on it, it shall not go out; and the priest shall burn wood on it every morning: and he shall lay the burnt offering in order upon it, and shall burn on it the fat of the peace offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn=". Lev 6:13 [Heb Bible Lev 6:6] (#2b.121):  Not to allow the fire of the Altar of Burnt Offerings to be extinguish">h</a></sup>Fire shall be kept burning on the altar continually; it shall not go out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 15:4; Leviticus 2:1–2; John [Yah is gracious] 6:32"> “‘This is the Torah ·Teaching· of the meal offering: the sons of Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; shall offer it before Adonai, before the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Leviticus 2:9"> He shall take from there his handful of the fine flour of the meal offering, and of its oil, and all the frankincense which is on the meal offering, and shall burn it on the altar for a pleasant aroma, as its memorial, to Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:29; Leviticus 2:3; Leviticus 10:12–13"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn=". Lev 6:16 (Lev 6:14-19) [Heb Bible Lev 6:9 (Lev 6:7-11)] (#1.8):  Aaron and his sons shall eat the remained of the grain offering">i</a></sup>That which is left of it Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; and his sons shall eat. It shall be eaten without yeast in a holy place. They shall eat it in the court of the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Leviticus 2:11; Numbers 18:9–10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn=". Lev 6:17 (Lev 6:14-18) [Heb Bible Lev 6:10 (Lev 6:7-11)] (#2b.122):  Not to bake the grain offerings with leaven">j</a></sup>It shall not be baked with yeast. I have given it as their portion of my offerings made by fire. It is most holy, as the habitual sin ·miss the mark· offering, and as the fault incurring guilt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 6:29; Numbers 18:10; Haggai 2:12–14"> Every male among the children of Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; shall eat of it, as their chok ·portion· forever throughout all your generations, from the offerings of Adonai made by fire. Whoever touches them shall be holy.’” (2)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Adonai spoke to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 29:2; Exodus 16:36; Leviticus 2:1–16"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn=". Lev 6:20 (Lev 6:19-23) [Heb Bible Lev 6:13 (Lev 6:12-16)] (#2b.123):  The Cohen Gadol ·High Priest· (v22) offers a grain offering daily">k</a></sup>“This is the offering of Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; and of his sons, which they shall offer to Adonai in the day when he is anointed: one tenth of an ephah &#91;an omer; 2.3 q; 2.2 L&#93; of fine flour for a meal offering perpetually, half of it in the morning, and half of it in the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 2:5; Leviticus 7:9; 1 Chronicles 9:31"> It shall be made with oil in a griddle. When it is soaked, you shall bring it in. You shall offer the meal offering in baked pieces for a pleasant aroma to Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:22–25; Isaiah 53:10; Leviticus 8:21"> The anointed priest that will be in his place from among his sons shall offer it. By a statute forever, it shall be wholly burned to Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 2:10; Leviticus 6:16–17"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn=". Lev 6:23 [Heb Bible Lev 6:16] (#2b.124):  T. The twice daily grain offering of the Cohen ·Priest· shall be entirely made burn up in smoke, it must not be eaten / R. Not to eat the meal offering of the Cohen Gadol ·High Priest·">l</a></sup>Every meal offering of a priest shall be wholly burned. It shall not be eaten.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Adonai spoke to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 4:24; Leviticus 1:11; Leviticus 1:3"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn=". Lev 6:25 (Lev 6:24-30) [Heb Bible Lev 6:18 (Lev 6:17-23)] (#2b.125):  The procedure for the sin offering">m</a></sup>“Speak to Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; and to his sons, saying, ‘This is the Torah ·Teaching· of the habitual sin ·miss the mark· offering: in the place where the burnt offering is killed, the habitual sin ·miss the mark· offering shall be killed before Adonai. It is most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 10:17–18; Ezekiel 44:28–29; Hosea 4:8"> The priest who offers it for sin ·err (the standard goal)· shall eat it. It shall be eaten in a holy place, in the court of the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 29:37; Haggai 2:12; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> Whatever shall touch its flesh shall be holy. When there is any of its blood sprinkled on a garment, you shall wash that on which it was sprinkled in a holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 11:33; Leviticus 15:12; Hebrews 9:9–10"> But the earthen vessel in which it is boiled shall be broken; and if it is boiled in a bronze vessel, it shall be scoured, and rinsed in water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 6:25; Leviticus 6:17–18; Numbers 18:10"> Every male among the priests shall eat of it: it is most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 10:18; Leviticus 16:27–28; Hebrews 13:11"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew ch 6 v. 23">n</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn=". Lev 6:30 [Heb Bible Lev 6:23] (#2b.126):  Not to eat the flesh of sin offerings in which the blood was brought inside the Tent of Meeting">o</a></sup>No habitual sin ·miss the mark· offering, of which any of the blood is brought into the Tent of Meeting to make atonement in the Holy Place, shall be eaten: it shall be burned with fire.</span>
