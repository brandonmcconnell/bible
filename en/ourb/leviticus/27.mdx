<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Adonai spoke to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 6:2; 1 Samuel 1:28; Judges 11:30–31"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn=". Lev 27:2 (Lev 27:2-8) (#10.594):  For one who voluntarily vows the value of a person unto Adonai, to pay the amount fixed in the Torah ·Teaching·">a</a></sup>“Speak to the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, and say to them, ‘When a man consecrates a person to Adonai in a vow, according to your valuation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 27:25; Numbers 18:16"> your valuation of a male from twenty years old even to sixty years old shall be fifty shekels &#91;1.25 lb; 0.57 kg&#93; of silver, after the shekel of the sanctuary (the shekel is 0.4 oz; 11.34 g).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 27:9–10; Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 26:15"> If she is a female, then your valuation shall be thirty shekels &#91;12 oz; 3/4 lb; 340.2 g&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> If the person is from five years old even to twenty years old, then your valuation shall be for a male twenty shekels &#91;8 oz; 1/2 lb; 226.8 g&#93;, and for a female ten shekels &#91;4 oz; 113.4 g&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 3:40–43; Numbers 18:14–16"> If the person is from a month old even to five years old, then your valuation shall be for a male five shekels &#91;2 oz; 56.7 g&#93; of silver, and for a female your valuation shall be three shekels of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 90:10"> If the person is from sixty years old and upward; if he is a male, then your valuation shall be fifteen shekels &#91;6 oz; 170.1 g&#93;, and for a female ten shekels &#91;4 oz; 113.4 g&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark [Hammer] 14:7; Leviticus 14:21–22; Luke [White-light] 21:1–4"> But if he is poorer than your valuation, then he shall be set before the priest, and the priest shall assign a value to him. The priest shall assign a value according to his ability to pay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> “‘If it is an animal, of which men offer an offering to Adonai, all that any man gives of such to Adonai becomes holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 27:15–33; James 1:8"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn=". Lev 27:10 (Lev 27:9-10) (#10.595):  Not to substitute a voluntarily vowed animal">b</a></sup>He shall not alter it,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn=". Lev 27:10 (Lev 27:9-10) (#10.596):  When trying to substitute a voluntarily vowed animal, both the new animal and the substituted animal remain dedicated">c</a></sup>nor change it, a good for a bad, or a bad for a good: and if he shall at all change animal for animal, then both it and that for which it is changed shall be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:18; Malachi 1:14"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn=". Lev 27:11-12 (#10.597):  T. For one who voluntarily vows an unclean animal to pay its value according to the Cohen ·Priest· / R. To estimate the value of consecrated animals">d</a></sup>If it is any unclean animal, of which they do not offer as an offering to Adonai, then he shall set the animal before the priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and the priest shall value it, whether it is good or bad. As you the priest values it, so shall it be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:19; Leviticus 27:15; Leviticus 22:14"> But if he will indeed redeem it, then he shall add the fifth part of it to its valuation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 25:29–31; Leviticus 27:21"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn=". Lev 27:14 (#10.598):  T. For one who voluntarily vows the value of his house to give according to the value evaluated by the Cohen ·Priest· / R. To estimate the value of consecrated houses">e</a></sup>“‘When a man dedicates his house to be holy to Adonai, then the priest shall evaluate it, whether it is good or bad: as the priest shall evaluate it, so shall it stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 27:13"> If he who dedicates it will redeem his house, then he shall add the fifth part of the money of your valuation to it, and it shall be his. (RY:7, LY:5)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:11–14; Hosea 3:2; Acts 4:34–37"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn=". Lev 27:16 (Lev 27:16-24, 27:22-23, 27:28) (#10.599):  T. For one who consecrates a portion of his land unto Adonai, he is to give according to its estimated production and the years before the Yovel Year ·Shofar blast Year·, also called the Jubilee Year ·Shofar Year·, as appointed in the (Lev ch 27) portion / R. To estimate the value of consecrated fields">f</a></sup>“‘If a man dedicates to Adonai part of the field of his possession, then your valuation shall be according to the seed for it. The sowing of a homer &#91;6.33 bushels; 59 gal; 220 L&#93; of barley shall be valued at fifty shekels &#91;1.25 lb; 0.57 kg&#93; of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> If he dedicates his field from the Jubilee Year ·Shofar Year·, according to your valuation it shall stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 25:15–16; Leviticus 25:51–52; Leviticus 25:27"> But if he dedicates his field after the Jubilee ·Shofar·, then the priest shall reckon to him the money according to the years that remain to the Jubilee Year ·Shofar Year·; and an abatement shall be made from your valuation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 27:13"> If he who dedicated the field will indeed redeem it, then he shall add the fifth part of the money of your valuation to it, and it shall remain his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> If he will not redeem the field, or if he has sold the field to another man, it shall not be redeemed any more;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:29; Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 25:31"> but the field, when it goes out in the Jubilee ·Shofar·, shall be holy to Adonai, as a devoted field. It shall be owned by the priests. (LY:6)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Leviticus 25:25"> “‘If he dedicates a field to Adonai which he has bought, which is not of the field of his possession,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 27:18; Leviticus 27:12"> then the priest shall reckon to him the worth of your valuation up to the Jubilee Year ·Shofar Year·; and he shall give your valuation on that day, as a holy thing to Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 25:28; Leviticus 27:20"> In the Jubilee Year ·Shofar Year· the field shall teshuvah ·completely return· to him from whom it was bought, even to him to whom the possession of the land belongs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Numbers 18:16; Numbers 3:47"> All your valuations shall be according to the shekel of the sanctuary: the shekel is 0.4 oz; 11.34 g; or called twenty gerahs &#91;20/50 of an oz&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12–13; Numbers 18:17"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn=". Lev 27:26 (Lev 27:26-28) (#10.600):  T. Not to voluntarily consecrate the firstborn animal to Adonai / R. Not to change a consecrated animal from one sacrifice to another">g</a></sup>“‘Only the firstborn among animals, which is made a firstborn to Adonai, no man may dedicate it; whether an ox or sheep. It is Adonai’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 27:11"> If it is an unclean animal, then he shall buy it back according to your valuation, and shall add to it the fifth part of it; or if it is not redeemed, then it shall be sold according to your valuation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 6:17–19; Leviticus 27:21; Exodus 22:20"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn=". Lev 27:28 (Lev 27:28-33) (#10.601):  T. “No devoted thing that a man shall voluntarily vow and devote to Adonai of all that he has shall be sold or redeemed” / R. Carry out the laws of interdicting ·prohibited / forbidden· possessions, in Hebrew this is called cherem ·devoted to destruction / under the ban· T. Note: The Hebrew cherem is commonly translated as “put under the ban” or “devote to destruction,” it can also mean “exterminate,” and infrequently it can mean to “ostracize” or “excommunicate”">h</a></sup>“‘Notwithstanding, no devoted thing, that a man shall devote to Adonai of all that he has, whether of man or animal, or of the field of his possession,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn=". Lev 27:28 (Lev 27:26-29) (#10.602):  T. Not to sell anything unconditionally concentrated to Adonai of all a person may own / R. Not to sell cherem ·devoted to destruction / under the ban· a field that has been concentrated to Adonai">i</a></sup>shall be sold<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn=". Lev 27:28 (Lev 27:26-29) (#10.603):  Not to redeem cherem ·devoted to destruction / under the ban· a field that has been concentrated to Adonai">j</a></sup>or redeemed: every devoted thing is most holy to Adonai. (LY:7)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:18–23; Numbers 21:2–3"> “‘No one devoted, who shall be devoted from among men, shall be ransomed; he shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 23:23; Nehemiah 13:12; Genesis 28:22"> “‘All the tithe of the land, whether of the seed of the land or of the fruit of the trees, is Adonai’s. It is holy to Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> If a man redeems anything of his tithe, he shall add a fifth part to it. (Maftir ·Conclusion·)</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:13; Ezekiel 20:37; Micah 7:14"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn=". Lev 27:32 (Lev 27:32-33) (#8.507):  To set apart a tenth tithe from herd or flock (for the Levites)">k</a></sup>All the tithe of the herds or the flocks, whatever passes under the rod, the tenth shall be holy to Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 27:10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn=". Lev 27:33 (Lev 27:32-33) (#8.508):  The tenth tithe from the flock or herd cannot be redeemed or exchanged">l</a></sup>He shall not search whether it is good or bad, neither shall he change it. If he changes it at all, then both it and that for which it is changed shall be holy. It shall not be redeemed.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 26:46; Numbers 1:1; Hebrews 12:18–25"> These are the mitzvot ·instructions· which Adonai enjoined Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; for the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; on Mount Sinai &#91;Thorn&#93;.</span>
