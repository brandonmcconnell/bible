<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Revelation 8:3–4; Genesis 9:16"> You shall put pure frankincense on each row, that it may be to the bread for a memorial, even an offering made by fire to Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4; Numbers 4:7; 1 Chronicles 9:32"> Every Shabath ·Intermission· day he shall set it in order before Adonai continually. It is on the behalf of the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; an everlasting covenant.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Leviticus 24:15–16; Exodus 3:15"> The son of the Israelite &#91;Descendant of God prevails&#93; woman blasphemed HaShem &#91;The Name&#93;, and cursed; and they brought him to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;. His mother’s name was Shelomith, the daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan &#91;He judged&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 18:15–16; Numbers 15:34; Numbers 27:5"> They put him in custody, until Adonai’s will should be declared to them.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:5–6; Deuteronomy 19:11–12; Deuteronomy 27:24"> “‘He who strikes any man mortally shall surely be put to death.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn=". Paraphrase Quoted in  Matt 5:21">a</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 5:38; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 7:2; Deuteronomy 19:21"> If anyone injures his neighbor; as he has done, so shall it be done to him:</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 12:49; Numbers 15:15–16; Numbers 9:14"> You shall have one kind of judgment for the foreigner as well as the native-born; for I am Adonai your God.’”</span>
