<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Revelation 19:6–9; 2 Corinthians 12:21"> Those servants went out into the highways, and gathered together as many as they found, both bad and good. The wedding was filled with guests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 19:8; Hebrews 4:12–13"> But when the king came in to see the guests, he saw there a man who didn’t have on wedding clothing,</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 8:12; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 25:30; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 24:51"> Then the king said to the servants, ‘Bind him hand and foot, take him away, and throw him into the outer darkness; there is where the weeping and grinding of teeth will be.’</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 20:20–26; Mark [Hammer] 12:13–17; Psalms 2:2"> Then the Pharisees &#91;Separated&#93; went and took counsel how they might entrap him in his talk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark [Hammer] 3:6; Mark [Hammer] 8:15; Mark [Hammer] 12:14"> They sent their disciples to him, along with the Herodians, saying, “Rabbi ·Teacher·, we know that you are honest, and teach the way of God in truth, no matter whom you teach, for you are not partial to anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 2:1; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 17:25"> Tell us what you think: Does Torah ·Teaching· permit paying taxes to Caesar &#91;Ruler&#93;, or not?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John [Yah is gracious] 8:6; Luke [White-light] 5:22; John [Yah is gracious] 2:25"> But Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; perceived their wickedness, and said, “Why do you test me, you hypocrites?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 20:2; Revelation 6:6; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 18:28"> Show me the tax money.” They brought to him a denarius &#91;one day’s wage&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 20:24"> He asked them, “Whose is this image and inscription?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:7; Proverbs 24:21; Acts 4:19"> They said to him, “Caesar’s &#91;Ruler&#93;’s.” Then he said to them, “Give therefore to Caesar &#91;Ruler&#93; the things that are Caesar’s, and to God the things that are God’s.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark [Hammer] 12:12; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 22:46; Luke [White-light] 20:25–26"> When they heard it, they marveled, and left him, and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Mark [Hammer] 12:18–27; Acts 23:6–8"> On that day Sadducees &#91;Morally-upright&#93; (those who say that there is no resurrection) came to him. They asked him hard questions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Luke [White-light] 20:28; Ruth 1:11"> saying, “Rabbi ·Teacher·, Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; said, ‘If a man dies, having no children, his brother shall marry his wife, and raise up offspring for his brother.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn=". <span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> In the same way, the second also, and the third, to the seventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> After them all, the woman died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> In the resurrection therefore, whose wife will she be of the seven? For they all had her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John [Yah is gracious] 20:9; Romans 15:4; Luke [White-light] 1:37"> But Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; answered them, “You are mistaken, not knowing the Scriptures, nor the power of God.</span>

<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 12:3; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 21:16; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 21:42"> But concerning the resurrection of the dead, haven’t you read that which was spoken to you by God, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Mark [Hammer] 12:26–27; Luke [White-light] 20:37–38"> ‘I am the God of Abraham &#91;Father of a multitude&#93;, and the God of Isaac &#91;Laughter&#93;, and the God of Jacob &#91;Supplanter&#93;?’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn=". <span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark [Hammer] 12:29–30; Luke [White-light] 10:27; Deuteronomy 6:5"> Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; said to him, “‘You shall have agapao ·total devotion love· to MarYah &#91;Master Yahweh&#93; your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn=". Quoted from  Deut 6:5">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> This is the greatest and most important mitzvah ·instruction·.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark [Hammer] 12:31; Luke [White-light] 10:27–37; Leviticus 19:18"> A second likewise is this, ‘You shall show agapao ·total devoted love· to your neighbor as yourself.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn=". <span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 7:12; 1 John 4:19–21"> All of the Torah ·Teaching· and the Prophets depend on these two mitzvot ·instructions·.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 20:41–44; Mark [Hammer] 12:35–37; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 22:34"> Now while the Pharisees &#91;Separated&#93; were gathered together, Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; asked them a question,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; John [Yah is gracious] 6:68–69; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 16:13–17"> saying, “What do you think of the Messiah &#91;Anointed one&#93;? Whose son is he?” They said to him, “Of David &#91;Beloved&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:2; Revelation 4:2; Mark [Hammer] 12:36"> He said to them, “How then does David &#91;Beloved&#93; in haRuach &#91;the Spirit, Breath&#93; call him MarYah &#91;Master Yahweh&#93;, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Acts 2:34–35"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Yahweh said to 'adoni ·my Lord·,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">sit at my right hand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">until I put your enemies under your feet’ ?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn=". <span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Revelation 22:16; John [Yah is gracious] 8:58"> “If then David &#91;Beloved&#93; calls him MarYah &#91;Master Yahweh&#93;, in what way can he be his son?”</span>
