<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 3:1; Mark [Hammer] 8:15; Luke [White-light] 9:7–9"> At that time, Herod &#91;Heroic&#93; the tetrarch (one of four co-emperors) heard the report concerning Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 16:14; John [Yah is gracious] 10:41; Mark [Hammer] 8:28"> and said to his servants, “This is John &#91;Yah is gracious&#93; the Immerser. He is risen from the dead. That is why these powers work in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark [Hammer] 6:17; Luke [White-light] 3:19–20; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 4:12"> For Herod &#91;Heroic&#93; had laid hold of John &#91;Yah is gracious&#93;, and bound him, and put him in prison for the sake of Herodias, his brother Philip &#91;Loves horses&#93;’s wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:21; Leviticus 18:16; Mark [Hammer] 6:18"> For John &#91;Yah is gracious&#93; said to him, “It violates the Torah ·Teaching· for you to have her as your wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 21:26; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 11:9; Mark [Hammer] 6:19–20"> When he would have put him to death, he feared the multitude, because they counted him as a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark [Hammer] 6:21–23; Genesis 40:20; Hosea 1:5–6"> But when Herod &#91;Heroic&#93;’s birthday came, the daughter of Herodias danced among them and pleased Herod &#91;Heroic&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 5:3; Esther 5:6; Esther 7:2"> There he promised with an oath to give her whatever she should ask.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark [Hammer] 6:24; Proverbs 29:10; Proverbs 1:16"> She, being prompted by her mother, said, “Give me here on a platter the head of John &#91;Yah is gracious&#93; the Immerser.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John [Yah is gracious] 19:12–16; 1 Samuel 14:28; Mark [Hammer] 6:26"> The king was grieved, but for the sake of his oaths, and of those who sat at the table with him, he commanded it to be given,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 17:12; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 21:35–36; Luke [White-light] 9:9"> and he sent and beheaded John &#91;Yah is gracious&#93; in the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 16:6; Genesis 49:7; Ezekiel 16:3–4"> His head was brought on a platter, and given to the young lady: and she brought it to her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 8:2; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 27:58–61"> His disciples came, and took the body, and buried it; and they went and told Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 9:10–17; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 14:1–2; Mark [Hammer] 6:30–44"> Now when Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; heard this, he withdrew from there in a boat, to a deserted place apart. When the multitudes heard it, they followed him on foot from the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 9:36; Hebrews 2:17"> Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; went out, and he saw a great multitude. He had compassion on them, and healed their sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 15:23; Mark [Hammer] 8:3; Mark [Hammer] 6:35–36"> When evening had come, his disciples came to him, saying, “This place is deserted, and the hour is already late. Send the multitudes away, that they may go into the villages, and buy themselves food.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; Ecclesiastes 11:2"> But Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; said to them, “They don’t need to go away. You give them something to eat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 15:33–34; Mark [Hammer] 6:37–38; Luke [White-light] 9:13"> They told him, “We only have here five loaves and two fish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He said, “Bring them here to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark [Hammer] 8:6–7; Luke [White-light] 24:30; 1 Samuel 9:13"> He commanded the multitudes to sit down on the grass; and he took the five loaves and the two fish, and looking up to heaven, he blessed, broke and gave the loaves to the disciples, and the disciples gave to the multitudes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:12; Mark [Hammer] 6:42–44; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 16:8–10"> They all ate, and were filled. They took up twelve baskets full of that which remained left over from the broken pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 9:8–11; Acts 4:4"> Those who ate were about five thousand men, besides women and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark [Hammer] 6:45–51; John [Yah is gracious] 6:15–21; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 15:39"> Immediately Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; made the disciples get into the boat, and to go ahead of him to the other side, while he sent the multitudes away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 6:12; Mark [Hammer] 6:46; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 26:36"> After he had sent the multitudes away, he went up into the mountain by himself to pray. When evening had come, he was there alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John [Yah is gracious] 6:18; Mark [Hammer] 6:48; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 8:24"> But the boat was now in the middle of the sea, a stadious’ distance &#91;1/8 mi; 660 ft; 201.2 m, Greek race track distance&#93;, distressed by the waves, for the wind was contrary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:8; John [Yah is gracious] 6:19; Mark [Hammer] 6:48"> It was about four o'clock in the morning, Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; came to them, walking on the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark [Hammer] 6:49–50; Revelation 1:17; Luke [White-light] 1:11–12"> When the disciples saw him walking on the sea, they were troubled, saying, “It’s a ghost!” and they cried out for fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Revelation 1:17–18; John [Yah is gracious] 16:33"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn=". (Is 9:6b)">a</a></sup><span className="woj">But immediately Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; spoke to them, saying, “Take heart! Ena Na &#91;I AM (the Living God)&#93;! Don’t be afraid.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John [Yah is gracious] 6:68; Romans 12:3; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 26:33–35"> Peter &#91;Rock&#93; answered him and said, “Lord, if it is you, command me to come to you on the waters.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Mark [Hammer] 9:23; Acts 3:16"> He said, “Come!” Peter &#91;Rock&#93; stepped down from the boat, and walked on the waters to come to Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 3:54–57; 2 Timothy 4:16–17; Mark [Hammer] 14:38"> But when he saw that the wind was strong, he was afraid, and beginning to sink, he cried out, saying, “Lord, save me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:6–8; Psalms 138:7; Genesis 22:14"> Immediately Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; stretched out his hand, took hold of him, and said to him, “You of little trusting faith, why did you doubt?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 107:29–30; Mark [Hammer] 4:41; John [Yah is gracious] 6:21"> When they got up into the boat, the wind ceased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John [Yah is gracious] 11:27; Psalms 2:7; Romans 1:4"> Those who were in the boat came and worshiped him, saying, “You are truly the Ben-Elohim ·Son of Elohim God·!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark [Hammer] 6:53–56; Luke [White-light] 5:1; John [Yah is gracious] 6:24–25"> When they had crossed over, they came to the land of Gennesaret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 4:24–25; Mark [Hammer] 6:55; Mark [Hammer] 1:28–34"> When the people of that place recognized him, they sent into all that surrounding region, and brought to him all who were sick;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 6:19; Mark [Hammer] 3:10; Exodus 28:33–43"> and they begged him that they might just touch the fringe of his garment. As many as touched it were made whole.</span>
