First he will purify the children of Levi &#91;United with&#93; and will clarify, “this one is of priestly descent, and this one is of Levitical descent.” And he will reject those who are not descended of Israel, as it written, “and the Governor said to them that they should not eat the most holy things till there stood up a priest with 'urim and thummin” (Ezra 2:63). From this you learn that the presumption of descent will be confirmed, and those with established descent will be announced by Ruach haKodesh &#91;Spirit of the Holiness&#93;. And he will establish the descent not merely from Israel but between each tribal descendant. (Mishne Torah  &#91;Second Torah&#93; written by RamBam – Hilchot Melachim, c. 12. sect. 3). (Mal 3:3)</li>
