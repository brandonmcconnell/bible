<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John [Yah is gracious] 20:14; Luke [White-light] 24:15–16; Luke [White-light] 24:31"> But when day had already come, Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; stood on the beach, yet the disciples didn’t know that it was Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93;.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John [Yah is gracious] 13:23; John [Yah is gracious] 20:2; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 14:28–29"> That disciple therefore whom Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; felt agapao ·totally devoted love· for said to Peter &#91;Rock&#93;, “It’s the Master!” So when Simeon Peter &#91;Hearing Rock&#93; heard that it was the Master, he wrapped his coat around him (for he was naked), and threw himself into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11"> But the other disciples came in the little boat (for they were not far from the land, but about two hundred cubits away &#91;300 ft; 914.4 m&#93;), dragging the net full of fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John [Yah is gracious] 21:13; 1 Kings 19:5–6; John [Yah is gracious] 21:10"> So when they got out on the land, they saw a fire of coals there, and fish laid on it, and bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; said to them, “Bring some of the fish which you have just caught.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 5:6–8; Acts 2:41"> Simeon Peter &#91;Hearing Rock&#93; went up, and drew the net to land, full of great fish, one hundred fifty-three; and even though there were so many, the net was not torn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:41; Luke [White-light] 9:45; John [Yah is gracious] 16:19"> Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; said to them, “Come and eat breakfast.” None of the disciples dared inquire of him, “Who are you?” knowing that it was the Master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John [Yah is gracious] 21:9; Acts 10:41; Luke [White-light] 24:42–43"> Then Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; came and took the bread, gave it to them, and the fish likewise.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:2; 1 Peter 2:25"> He said to him again a second time, “Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93;, son of Jonah &#91;Dove&#93;, do you have agapao ·totally devoted love· for me?” He said to him, “Yes, Master; you know that I phileo ·affectionately love, have high regard for· you.” He said to him, “Tend my sheep.”</span>

You know that I phileo ·affectionately love, have high regard for· you.” Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; said to him, “Feed my sheep.</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Peter 1:14; John [Yah is gracious] 21:22; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 10:38"> Now he said this, signifying by what kind of death he would glorify God. When he had said this, he said to him, “Follow me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John [Yah is gracious] 21:7; John [Yah is gracious] 20:2; John [Yah is gracious] 21:24"> Then Peter &#91;Rock&#93;, turning around, saw a disciple following. <span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 13:23–24; Acts 1:6–7; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 24:3–4"> Peter &#91;Rock&#93; seeing him, said to Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93;, “Master, what about this man?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 2:25; Revelation 3:11; Mark [Hammer] 9:1"> Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; said to him, “If I desire that he stay until I come, what is that to you? You follow me.”</span>
