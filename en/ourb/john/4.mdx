<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:20; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Romans 7:3"> for you have had five husbands; and he whom you now have is not your husband. This you have said truly.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John [Yah is gracious] 1:49; Daniel 9:24–26; John [Yah is gracious] 1:41–42"> The woman said to him, “I know that Messiah &#91;Anointed one&#93; comes, he who is called Messiah &#91;Anointed one&#93;. When he has come, he will declare to us all things.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John [Yah is gracious] 8:24; Mark [Hammer] 14:61–62; Romans 10:20–21"> Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; said to her, “Ena Na &#91;I AM (the Living God)&#93;, I who is speaking to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 7:39; John [Yah is gracious] 4:8–9"> At this, his disciples came. They marveled that he was speaking with a woman; yet no one said, “What are you looking for?” or, “Why do you speak with her?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John [Yah is gracious] 4:7; Luke [White-light] 24:33; Mark [Hammer] 16:8–10"> So the woman left her water pot, and went away into the city, and said to the people,</span>

<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4–5; Acts 1:8; Acts 8:12"> Many more trusted because of his word.</span>

<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John [Yah is gracious] 20:29; Hebrews 2:4; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; therefore said to him, “Unless you see signs and wonders, you will in no way trust.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark [Hammer] 5:23; Psalms 88:10–12; Psalms 40:17"> The nobleman said to him, “Sir, come down before my child dies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 8:13; Mark [Hammer] 7:29–30; Romans 4:20–21"> Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; said to him, “Go your way. Your son lives.” The man trusted the word that Yeshua &#91;Salvation&#93; spoke to him, and he went his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Kings 17:23; John [Yah is gracious] 4:50; John [Yah is gracious] 4:53"> As he was now going down, his servants met him and reported, saying “Your child lives!”</span>
