<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Genesis 18:22; Genesis 19:1"> He lifted up his eyes and looked, and saw that three men stood opposite him. When he saw them, he ran to meet them from the tent door, and bowed himself to the earth,</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:33; Hebrews 13:2; Acts 16:15"> Abraham &#91;Father of a multitude&#93; hurried into the tent to Sarah &#91;Princess&#93;, and said, “Quickly prepare three seahs &#91;23.1 qt; 21.9 L&#93; of fine meal, knead it, and make cakes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Malachi 1:14; Luke [White-light] 15:27; Luke [White-light] 15:30"> Abraham &#91;Father of a multitude&#93; ran to the herd, and fetched a tender and good calf, and gave it to the servant. He hurried to dress it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:3; Judges 13:15; Revelation 3:20"> He took butter, milk, and the calf which he had dressed, and set it before them. He stood by them under the tree, and they ate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 2:5; Genesis 24:67; Genesis 4:9"> They asked him, “Where is Sarah &#91;Princess&#93;, your wife?” He said, “See, in the tent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 21:2; Genesis 17:21; Romans 9:8–9"> He said, “I will certainly return to you when the season comes round. Behold, Sarah &#91;Princess&#93; your wife will have a son.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn=". <span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; Hebrews 11:11–12; Luke [White-light] 1:36"> Now Abraham &#91;Father of a multitude&#93; and Sarah &#91;Princess&#93; were old, well advanced in age. Sarah &#91;Princess&#93; had passed the age of childbearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:6; Genesis 17:17; Luke [White-light] 1:18–20"> Sarah &#91;Princess&#93; laughed within herself, saying, “After I have grown old will I have pleasure, my lord being old also?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John [Yah is gracious] 2:25"> Adonai said to Abraham &#91;Father of a multitude&#93;, “Why did Sarah &#91;Princess&#93; laugh, saying, ‘Will I really bear a child, yet I am old?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 1:37; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 19:26; Mark [Hammer] 10:27"> Is anything too hard for Adonai? At the set time I will return to you, when the season comes round, and Sarah &#91;Princess&#93; will have a son.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn=". <span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 1:8; Proverbs 28:13; Ephesians 4:23"> Then Sarah &#91;Princess&#93; denied it, saying, “I didn’t laugh,” for she was afraid. He said, “No, but you did laugh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 15:3; Acts 21:5; Acts 20:38"> The men rose up from there, and looked toward Sodom &#91;Burning&#93;. Abraham &#91;Father of a multitude&#93; went with them to see them on their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Psalms 25:14; John [Yah is gracious] 15:15"> Adonai said, “Will I hide from Abraham &#91;Father of a multitude&#93; what I do,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Genesis 26:4; Galatians 3:14"> since Abraham &#91;Father of a multitude&#93; will surely become a great and mighty nation, and all the nations of the earth will be blessed in him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:6–7; Deuteronomy 4:9–10; 1 Timothy 3:4–5"> For I have known him, to the end that he may enjoin his children and his household after him, that they may keep the way of Adonai, to do righteousness and right judgement; to the end that Adonai may bring on Abraham &#91;Father of a multitude&#93; that which he has spoken of him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 19:13; Ezekiel 16:49–50; Isaiah 3:9"> Adonai said, “Because the cry of Sodom &#91;Burning&#93; and Gomorrah &#91;Rebellious people, Tyrants&#93; is great, and because their habitual sin ·missing the mark· is very grievous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Genesis 11:5; Zephaniah 1:12"> I will go down now, and see whether their deeds are as bad as the reports which have come to me. If not, I will know.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 19:1; Acts 7:55; Ezekiel 22:30"> The men turned from there, and went toward Sodom &#91;Burning&#93;, but Abraham &#91;Father of a multitude&#93; stood yet before Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; 2 Samuel 24:17; Genesis 20:4"> Abraham &#91;Father of a multitude&#93; came near, and said, “Will you consume the upright with the wicked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:1; Isaiah 1:9; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 7:13–14"> What if there are fifty upright within the city? Will you consume and not spare the place for the fifty upright who are in it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 8:3; Isaiah 3:10–11; Job 8:20"> Be it far from you to do things like that, to kill the upright with the wicked, so that the upright should be like the wicked. May that be far from you. Should not the Judge of all the earth do right judgement?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:1; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 24:22; Isaiah 65:8"> Adonai said, “If I find in Sodom &#91;Burning&#93; fifty upright within the city, then I will spare the whole place for their sake.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Isaiah 6:5; 1 Corinthians 15:47–48"> Abraham &#91;Father of a multitude&#93; answered, “See now, I have taken it on myself to speak to 'Adonay &#91;Lord&#93;, although I am dust and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 18:29; 1 Kings 20:32–33; Genesis 18:26"> What if there will lack five of the fifty upright? Will you destroy all the city for lack of five?” He said, “I will not destroy it, if I find forty-five there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Hebrews 4:16"> He spoke to him yet again, and said, “What if there are forty found there?” He said, “I will not do it for the forty’s sake.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 89:7; Psalms 9:12; Esther 4:11–16"> He said, “Oh don’t let 'Adonay &#91;Lord&#93; be angry, and I will speak. What if there are thirty found there?” He said, “I will not do it, if I find thirty there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 11:8; Genesis 18:27; Luke [White-light] 18:1"> He said, “See now, I have taken it on myself to speak to 'Adonay &#91;Lord&#93;. What if there are twenty found there?” He said, “I will not destroy it for the twenty’s sake.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="James 5:15–17; Judges 6:39; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 7:7"> He said, “Oh don’t let 'Adonay &#91;Lord&#93; be angry, and I will speak just once more. What if ten are found there?” He said, “I will not destroy it for the ten’s sake.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 18:22; Genesis 32:26; Genesis 31:55"> Adonai went his way, as soon as he had finished communing with Abraham &#91;Father of a multitude&#93;, and Abraham &#91;Father of a multitude&#93; returned to his place.</span>
