<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 31:17; Exodus 20:11; Psalms 33:6"> The heavens, the earth, and all their vast array were finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:4; Exodus 31:17; Deuteronomy 5:14"> On the seventh 'yom ·period of time, day· God finished his work which he had done; and he shavat ·rested·<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn=". Quoted in  Heb 4:4">a</a></sup>on the seventh day from all his work which he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:12; Mark [Hammer] 2:27; Leviticus 23:3"> God blessed the seventh day, and made it set apart holy, because he rested in it from all his work of barah' ·creation from nothingness· which he had done.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Day 7: God rests and appreciates his creation; He spoke and, in obedience, creation came into being (Col 1:12-17). In Hebrew, shavat means ·rested·; this is why the seventh day is called Shabbat ·repose, desist· (Ex. 20:11).">b</a></sup>(A:4, S:2)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 5:1; Revelation 1:8; Genesis 1:4"> This is the history of the generations of the heavens and of the earth when they were created. On the day that Adonai God made earth and heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 3:23; Hebrews 6:7; Job 5:10"> no plant of the field was yet in the earth, and no herb of the field had yet sprung up; for Adonai God had not caused it to rain on the earth. There was no human to till the ground,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="There was no 'adam ·human· to till the 'adamah ·ground·">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> but a mist went up from the earth, and watered the whole surface of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 33:4; 1 Corinthians 15:45"> Adonai God formed and framed a human from the dust of ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breaths of lives; and man became a living soul.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn=". Quoted in  1 Cor 15:45">d</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Day 6b: God yatzar ·formed, framed· the 'adam ·human· from the ground dust. God breathes the nish'mat chayim ·breaths of lives· into the human and the human becomes a nefesh chai ·soul living·. Unique to other creatures with a living soul, humans are given both soul and spirit, that is physical life and spiritual life united.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Genesis 3:24; Genesis 13:10"> Adonai God planted a garden eastward, in Eden &#91;Delight&#93;, and there he put the man whom he had formed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 3:22; Revelation 2:7; Revelation 22:14"> Out of the ground Adonai God made every tree to grow that is pleasant to the sight, and good for food, including the 'etz chai ·tree of life· in the middle of the garden<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn=". Quoted in  Rev 2:7">f</a></sup>and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Day 6a: God creates various plants to populate the earth. In a special place, Gan-'Eden ·Garden Paradise, Garden Delight·, God interacts with the first humans. In this place is the 'etz chai ·tree of life· and the tree of knowledge of tov v' rah ·good and evil·. Humans are given both work and sustenance.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Psalms 46:4"> A river went out of Eden &#91;Delight&#93; to water the garden; and from there it was parted, and became the source of four rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Genesis 10:29; 1 Samuel 15:7"> The name of the first is Pishon: it flows through the whole land of Havilah, where there is gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 28:20; Numbers 11:7; Ezekiel 28:13"> and the gold of that land is good. Bdellium and onyx stone are also there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:6; Isaiah 11:11"> The name of the second river is Gihon. It is the same river that flows through the whole land of Cush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 10:4; Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 1:7"> The name of the third river is Hiddekel. This is the one which flows in front of Assyria &#91;Level plain&#93;. The fourth river is the Euphrates &#91;Fruitful&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Genesis 2:8; Ephesians 4:28"> Adonai God took the man, and put him into the garden of Eden &#91;Delight&#93; to cultivate and keep it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; 1 Timothy 4:4; 1 Timothy 6:17"> Adonai God enjoined the man, saying, “You may freely eat of every tree of the garden;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:23; James 1:15; Genesis 3:19"> but you shall not eat of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil; for in the day<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="This is an example of yom being used as an indefinite day in the future. What type of death was God making reference to?">h</a></sup>that you eat of it, you will surely have death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:9–12; 1 Corinthians 11:7–12; Proverbs 18:22"> Adonai God said, “It is not good for the man to be alone.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="This is the first time God has identified something lo-tov ·not good·, everything else God made was declared tov ·good·, even the tree of knowledge. What does this unique statement “not good” tell us?">i</a></sup>I will make him a help-mate suitable for him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Genesis 1:28; Genesis 9:2"> Out of the ground Adonai God formed every animal of the field, and every bird of the sky, and brought them to the man to see what he would call them. Whatever the man called every living creature became its name.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="God invites Adam to create in a similar fashion as God did; Adam is allowed to declare for each animal a unique name, the name identifies an essential character of each animal.">j</a></sup>(S:3)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:18"> The man gave names to all livestock, and to the birds of the sky, and to every animal of the field; but for man there was not found a helper suitable for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:12; Job 4:13; 1 Samuel 26:12"> Adonai God caused the man to fall into a deep sleep. As the man slept, he took one of his ribs, and closed up the flesh in its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:8–9; 1 Timothy 2:13; Hebrews 13:4"> Adonai God refined and fashioned an 'ishah ·wife woman· from the rib which had taken from the man, and brought her to the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 5:28–30; 1 Corinthians 11:8–9; 2 Samuel 5:1"> The man said, “This is now bone of my bones, and flesh of my flesh. She will be called ‘'ishah ·wife woman·,’ because she was taken out of 'ish ·husband man·.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="With Hebrew inserted: “(v22) Yahweh God fashioned an 'ishah ·wife woman· from the rib which had taken from the 'adam ·man·, and brought her to the 'adam. (v23) The man said, ‘This is now bone of my bones, and flesh of my flesh. She will be called “'ishah ·wife woman·,” because she was taken out of 'ish ·husband man·.’”">k</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Adam [Human, Red earth] makes an astounding statement, repeating what God had said. It takes an 'ishah ·wife woman· to make an 'adam ·male, human· into a 'ish ·husband man·.">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 19:3–9; Ephesians 5:28–31; Mark [Hammer] 10:6–12"> Therefore a husband man will leave his father and his mother, and will dabak ·cling to, worship· with his wife woman, and they will be echad ·one· flesh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn=". Quoted in  Matt 19:5; Mark 10:7-8; 1 Cor 6:16; Eph 5:31">m</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="From this verse, we see and know that “marriage” was established by God.">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 3:7; Genesis 3:10–11; Romans 10:11"> The husband man and his wife woman were both naked, and they were not ashamed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Day 6b: God formed the male out of dust, God banah fashioned the female out of his rib to be a helpmate; she is not above him nor below him, she is equal from his side.
