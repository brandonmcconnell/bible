<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 32:3; Isaiah 40:11; Romans 15:1"> Please let my lord pass over before his servant, and I will lead on gently, according to the pace of the livestock that are before me and according to the pace of the children, until I come to my lord to Seir.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Genesis 47:25; Genesis 34:11"> Esau &#91;Hairy&#93; said, “Let me now leave with you some of the folk who are with me.” He said, “Why? Let me find chen ·grace· in the sight of my lord.”</span>
