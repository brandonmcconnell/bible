<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Adonai spoke to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:40–41"> “Take the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93; instead of all the firstborn among the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, and the livestock of the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93; instead of their livestock; and the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93; shall be mine. I am Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 13:13; Numbers 18:15–16"> For the redemption of the two hundred seventy-three of the firstborn of the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, who exceed the number of the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93;,</span>

<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> and you shall give the money, with which their remainder is redeemed, to Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; and to his sons.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; took the redemption money from those who exceeded the number of those who were redeemed by the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Numbers 3:46–47; 1 Peter 3:18; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 20:28"> from the firstborn of the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; he took the money, one thousand three hundred sixty-five shekels &#91;34.13 lb; 15.48 kg&#93;, after the shekel of the sanctuary &#91;0.4 oz; 11.34 g&#93;:</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 3:48; Numbers 16:15; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> and Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; gave the redemption money to Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; and to his sons, according to Adonai’s word, as Adonai enjoined Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;.</span>
