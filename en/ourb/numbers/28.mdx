<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 9:21; 1 Kings 18:36; Exodus 12:6"> You shall offer the one lamb in the morning, and you shall offer the other lamb at evening;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 16:36; Exodus 29:38–42"> with one tenth of an ephah &#91;an omer; 2.3 q; 2.2 L&#93; of fine flour for a meal offering, mixed with the fourth part of a hin &#91;2.6 qt; 1.2 L&#93; of beaten oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 5:25; Leviticus 6:9; Psalms 50:8"> It is a continual burnt offering, which was ordained in Mount Sinai &#91;Thorn&#93; for a pleasant aroma, an offering made by fire to Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 29:42; Numbers 28:14; Isaiah 57:6"> Its drink offering shall be the fourth part of a hin &#91;2.6 qt; 1.2 L&#93; for the one lamb. You shall pour out a drink offering of strong drink to Adonai in the holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The other lamb you shall offer at evening. As the meal offering of the morning, and as its drink offering, you shall offer it, an offering made by fire, of a pleasant aroma to Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 58:13; Psalms 92:1–4; Revelation 1:10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn=". Num 28:9 (Num 28:9-10) (#4.333):  T. <span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:4–5; Numbers 29:22; Numbers 29:31"> this is the burnt offering of every Shabath ·Intermission·, besides the continual burnt offering, and its drink offering.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="(9-10) Context of Matt 12:5">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; Ezekiel 46:6; Ezra 3:5"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn=". Num 28:11 (Num 28:11-15) (#4.334):  T. To bring additional offerings on the New Month">d</a></sup>“‘In the beginnings of your months, you shall offer a burnt offering to Adonai: two young bulls, and one ram, seven male lambs a year old without defect;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 15:4–12; Ezekiel 46:5–7; Numbers 29:10"> and three tenths of an ephah &#91;three omers; 6.9 q; 6.6 L&#93; of fine flour for a meal offering, mixed with oil, for each bull; and two tenth parts of fine flour for a meal offering, mixed with oil, for the one ram;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and one tenth part of fine flour mixed with oil for a meal offering to every lamb; for a burnt offering of a pleasant aroma, an offering made by fire to Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Their drink offerings shall be half a hin &#91;5.2 qt; 2.4 L&#93; of wine for a bull, and the third part of a hin &#91;2.6 qt; 1.24 L&#93; of for the ram, and the fourth part of a hin &#91;2.6 qt; 1.2 L&#93; for a lamb. This is the burnt offering of every month throughout the months of the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Numbers 28:22; Numbers 15:24"> One male goat for a habitual sin ·miss the mark· offering to Adonai; it shall be offered besides the continual burnt offering, and its drink offering. (6)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–8; Exodus 12:18; Leviticus 23:5–8"> “‘In the first month Nissan ·Miracles (in Aramaic), 1·, on the fourteenth day of the month, is Adonai’s Pesac ·Passover·.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 23:6; Exodus 23:15; Exodus 13:6"> On the fifteenth day of this month shall be a feast. Unleavened bread shall be eaten for seven days.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> and their meal offering, fine flour mixed with oil. You shall offer three tenths for a bull, and two tenths for the ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> You shall offer one tenth for every lamb of the seven lambs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 8:3"> and one male goat for a habitual sin ·miss the mark· offering, to make atonement for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Numbers 28:10"> You shall offer these besides the burnt offering of the morning, which is for a continual burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> In this way you shall offer daily, for seven days, the food of the offering made by fire, of a pleasant aroma to Adonai. It shall be offered in addition to the continual burnt offering, and its drink offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:8; Numbers 28:18; Exodus 13:6"> On the seventh day you shall have a holy convocation. You shall do no regular aboda ·service·.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Exodus 34:22; Leviticus 23:10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn=". <span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 23:18–19; Numbers 28:11; Numbers 28:19"> but you shall offer a burnt offering for a pleasant aroma to Adonai: two young bulls, one ram, seven male lambs a year old;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and their meal offering, fine flour mixed with oil, three tenths for each bull, two tenths for the one ram,</span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 28:15; Numbers 28:22; 1 Peter 2:24"> one male goat, to make atonement for you.</span>
