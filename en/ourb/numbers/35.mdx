<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 22:1; Numbers 31:12; Numbers 33:50"> Adonai spoke to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; in the plains of Moab &#91;From father&#93; by the Jordan &#91;Descender&#93; at Jericho &#91;Fragrant, Moon&#93;, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 14:3–4; Ezekiel 48:8; Joshua 21:2–42"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn=". Num 35:2 (Num 35:7, 35:1-8, ch 35) (#1.30):  To give the Levites cities to dwell in and the surrounding fields">a</a></sup>“Enjoin the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; to give to the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93; of the inheritance of their possession cities to dwell in. You shall give suburbs for the cities around them to the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:14; Joshua 21:11; Ezekiel 45:2"> They shall have the cities to dwell in. Their suburbs shall be for their livestock, and for their possessions, and for all their animals.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> “The suburbs of the cities, which you shall give to the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93;, shall be from the wall of the city and outward one thousand cubits &#91;1/3 mi; 457.2 km&#93; around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> You shall measure outside of the city for the east side two thousand cubits &#91;1/2 mi; 914 km&#93;, and for the south side two thousand cubits &#91;1/2 mi; 914 km&#93;, and for the west side two thousand cubits &#91;1/2 mi; 914 km&#93;, and for the north side two thousand cubits &#91;1/2 mi; 914 km&#93;, the city being in the middle. This shall be the suburbs of their cities.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 21:13; Joshua 21:3; Psalms 9:9"> “The cities which you shall give to the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93;, they shall be the six cities of refuge, which you shall give for the man slayer to flee to. Besides them you shall give forty-two cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:3–42; 1 Chronicles 6:54–81"> All the cities which you shall give to the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93; shall be forty-eight cities together with their suburbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 26:54; Numbers 33:54; Joshua 21:1–42"> Concerning the cities which you shall give of the possession of the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, from the many you shall take many; and from the few you shall take few. Everyone according to his inheritance which he inherits shall give some of his cities to the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93;.” (RY:7, LY:6)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Adonai spoke to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:9; Deuteronomy 19:1–2; Leviticus 14:34"> “Speak to the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, and tell them, ‘When you pass over the Jordan &#91;Descender&#93; into the land of Canaan &#91;Humbled&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:2; Numbers 35:6"> then you shall appoint for yourselves cities to be cities of refuge for you, that the man slayer who kills any person unintentionally by mistake sin ·error· may flee there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 20:3–6; Deuteronomy 19:6; Joshua 20:9"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn=". Num 35:12 (Num 35:9-15) (#6.401):  T. Not to execute one guilty of a capital offense before he has stood trial / R. Not to kill the murderer before he stands trial T. Note: Murder is defined in (Num 35:16-21) as being premeditated with intent or hatred in contrast to accidental killing (Num 35:22-24), killing without enmity nor premeditated intent to inflict harm">b</a></sup>The cities shall be to you for refuge from the avenger, that the man slayer not die, until he stands before the congregation for judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The cities which you shall give shall be for you six cities of refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:8–10; Deuteronomy 4:41–43; Joshua 20:7–9"> You shall give three cities beyond the Jordan &#91;Descender&#93;, and you shall give three cities in the land of Canaan &#91;Humbled&#93;. They shall be cities of refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 15:16; Galatians 3:28; Romans 3:29"> For the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, and for the stranger and for the foreigner living among them, shall these six cities be for refuge; that everyone who kills any person unintentionally by mistake sin ·error· may flee there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17; Genesis 9:5–6; Deuteronomy 19:11–13"> “‘But if he struck him with an instrument of iron, so that he died, he is a murderer. The murderer shall surely be put to death.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Context of Matt 5:21">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 21:18"> If he struck him with a stone in the hand, by which a man may die, and he died, he is a murderer. The murderer shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Or if he struck him with a weapon of wood in the hand, by which a man may die, and he died, he is a murderer. The murderer shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 20:3; Deuteronomy 19:6; Deuteronomy 19:12"> The avenger of blood shall himself put the murderer to death. When he meets him, he shall put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 21:14; Deuteronomy 19:11; 2 Samuel 3:27"> If he shoved him out of hatred, or hurled at him, lying in wait, so that he died,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> or in hostility struck him with his hand, so that he died, he who struck him shall surely be put to death. He is a murderer. The avenger of blood shall put the murderer to death, when he meets him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 35:11; Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:3"> “‘But if he shoved him suddenly without hostility, or hurled on him anything without lying in wait,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> or with any stone, by which a man may die, not seeing him, and cast it on him, so that he died, and he was not his enemy, neither sought his harm;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 20:6; Numbers 35:12"> then the congregation shall judge between the striker and the avenger of blood according to these judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 29:7; Leviticus 4:3; Romans 3:24–26"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn=". Num 35:25 (Num 35:22-29) (#6.402):  To exile in the city of refuge the person who committed an accidental killing or manslaughter, until the Cohen Gadol ·High Priest· dies T. Note: Specificity the Cohen Gadol ·High Priest· of that year, for the High Priest’s role changes annually">d</a></sup>The congregation shall deliver the man slayer out of the hand of the avenger of blood, and the congregation shall restore him to his city of refuge, where he had fled. He shall dwell therein until the death of the high priest, who was anointed with the holy oil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> “‘But if the man slayer shall at any time go beyond the border of his city of refuge, where he flees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 22:2; Deuteronomy 19:10; Deuteronomy 19:6"> and the avenger of blood finds him outside of the border of his city of refuge, and the avenger of blood kills the man slayer; he shall not be guilty of blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 10:26–30; Hebrews 6:4–8"> because he should have remained in his city of refuge until the death of the high priest. But after the death of the high priest, the man slayer shall teshuvah ·completely return· into the land of his possession.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 27:11; Numbers 27:1"> “‘These things shall be for a statute and judgement to you throughout all your generations in all your dwellings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28; Deuteronomy 19:15; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 18:16"> “‘Whoever kills any person, the murderer shall be slain at the mouth of witnesses; but one witness shall not testify against any person that he die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:11–13; Genesis 9:5–6; Psalms 51:14"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn=". Num 35:31 (Num 35:30-34) (#6.403):  T. Not to take a ransom in lieu for the life of a murderer condemned to death / R. Not to accept monetary restitution to atone for the murderer">e</a></sup>“‘Moreover you shall take no ransom for the life of a murderer who is guilty of death; but he shall surely be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 4:12; Galatians 3:10–13; Galatians 2:21"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn=". Num 35:32 (Num 35:30-34) (#6.404):  Not to take ransom from one who killed another accidentally in order to free him from exile in the City of Refuge before the time of his release T. Note: Murder is defined in (Num 35:16-21) as being premeditated with intent or hatred / Directive: The time of release is based on (Num 35:25 OU402) the death of the Cohen Gadol ·High Priest· of his day">f</a></sup>“‘You shall take no ransom for him who is fled to his city of refuge, that he may come again to dwell in the land, until the death of the priest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 11:50–51; Psalms 106:38; Hosea 4:2–3"> “‘So you shall not pollute the land in which you are; for blood pollutes the land. No atonement can be made for the land for the blood that is shed in it, but by the blood of him who shed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 5:3; Psalms 135:21; Revelation 21:27"> You shall not defile the land which you inhabit, in the middle of which I dwell; for I, Adonai, dwell in the middle of the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;.’”</span>
</div>
