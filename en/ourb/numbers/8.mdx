<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Adonai spoke to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 25:37; Exodus 40:25; Leviticus 24:1–2"> “Speak to Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;, and tell him, ‘When you light the menorah ·lamp· candles, the seven menorah ·lamp· candles shall give light in front of the menorah ·lamp·.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; did so. He lit its menorah ·lamp· candles to light the area in front of the menorah ·lamp·, as Adonai enjoined Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–40; Exodus 25:9; Exodus 25:18"> This was the workmanship of the menorah ·lamp·, beaten work of gold. From its base to its flowers, it was beaten work: according to the pattern which Adonai had shown Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, so he made the menorah ·lamp·.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Adonai spoke to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; James 4:8; Exodus 19:15"> “Take the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93; from among the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, and cleanse them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 19:13; Numbers 19:7–10; Leviticus 8:6"> You shall do this to them, to cleanse them: sprinkle the water of habitual sin ·missing the mark· cleanzing on them, let them shave their whole bodies with a razor, and let them wash their clothes, and cleanse themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 29:1; Leviticus 16:3"> Then let them take a young bull, and its meal offering, fine flour mixed with oil; and another young bull you shall take for a habitual sin ·miss the mark· offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 40:12; Leviticus 8:3; Exodus 29:4–37"> You shall present the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93; before the Tent of Meeting. You shall assemble the whole congregation of the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 1:4; Acts 6:6; 1 Timothy 4:14"> You shall present the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93; before Adonai. The children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; shall lay their hands on the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30; Leviticus 8:29; Numbers 1:49–53"> and Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; shall offer the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93; before Adonai for a wave offering, on the behalf of the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, that it may be theirs to do the service of Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–10; Exodus 29:10; Leviticus 1:4"> “The Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93; shall lay their hands on the heads of the bulls, and you shall offer the one for a habitual sin ·miss the mark· offering, and the other for a burnt offering to Adonai, to make atonement for the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 8:21; Numbers 8:11; Romans 15:16"> You shall set the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93; before Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;, and before his sons, and offer them as a wave offering to Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 3:45; Malachi 3:17; Numbers 18:6"> Thus you shall separate the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93; from among the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, and the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93; shall be mine. (2)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 8:11; Numbers 8:13; Numbers 3:23–37"> “After that, the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93; shall go in to do the service of the Tent of Meeting: and you shall cleanse them, and offer them as a wave offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:45; Numbers 3:9"> For they are wholly given to me from among the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;; instead of all who open the womb, even the firstborn of all the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, I have taken them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Luke [White-light] 2:23; Exodus 13:12–15"> For all the firstborn among the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; are mine, both man and animal. On the day that I struck all the firstborn in the land of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;, I sanctified them for myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> I have taken the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93; instead of all the firstborn among the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 16:46; 2 Chronicles 26:16–20"> I have given the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93; as a gift to Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; and to his sons from among the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, to do the service of the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; in the Tent of Meeting, and to make atonement for the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;; that there be no plague among the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, when the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; come near to the sanctuary.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, and Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;, and all the congregation of the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; did so to the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93;. According to all that Adonai enjoined Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; concerning the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93;, so the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; did to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 8:7; Numbers 19:19; Numbers 8:15"> The Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93; cleansed sin ·err (the standard goal)· from themselves from sin, and they washed their clothes; and Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; offered them for a wave offering before Adonai; and Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; made atonement for them to cleanse them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:2; 2 Chronicles 35:8–15; Numbers 8:15"> After that, the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93; went in to do their service in the Tent of Meeting before Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;, and before his sons: as Adonai had enjoined Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; concerning the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93;, so they did to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Adonai spoke to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 4:3; 1 Chronicles 23:3; Numbers 4:23"> “This is that which belongs to the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93;: from twenty-five years old and upward they shall go in to wait on the service in the work of the Tent of Meeting;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 4:23; 2 Timothy 4:7"> and from the age of fifty years they shall cease waiting on the work, and shall serve no more,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:32; 1 Timothy 4:15"> but shall minister with their brothers in the Tent of Meeting, to perform the duty, and shall do no service. You shall do thus to the Levites &#91;Descendants of United with&#93; concerning their duties.”</span>
