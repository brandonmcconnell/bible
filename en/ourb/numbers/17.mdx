<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 7:44; Exodus 38:21; Numbers 18:2"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; laid up the rods before Adonai in the Tent of the Testimony.</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; brought out all the rods from before Adonai to all the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;. They looked, and each man took his rod.<br />(6)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Ephesians 5:6; Isaiah 1:2"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew ch 17 v. 25">c</a></sup>Adonai said to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, “Put back the rod of Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn=". <span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; did so. As Adonai enjoined him, so he did.</span>
