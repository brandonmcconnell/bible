A stranger shall not come near to you.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 15:15–16; John [Yah is gracious] 3:27; Numbers 3:10"> You and your sons with you shall keep your priesthood for everything of the altar, and for that within the veil. <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 7:7; Leviticus 14:13; Leviticus 6:25–26"> This shall be yours of the most holy things from the fire: every offering of theirs, even every meal offering of theirs, and every habitual sin ·miss the mark· offering of theirs, and every fault incurring guilt offering of theirs, which they shall render to me, shall be most holy for you and for your sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 7:6; Leviticus 6:29; Leviticus 6:16"> You shall eat of it like the most holy things. Every male shall eat of it. It shall be holy to you.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:29; Exodus 23:19; Micah 7:1"> The first-ripe fruits of all that is in their land, which they bring to Adonai, shall be yours. Everyone who is clean in your house shall eat of it.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:20; Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12–13"> Everything that opens the womb, of all flesh which they offer to Adonai, both of man and animal shall be yours.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn=". <span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:2–7"> You shall redeem those who are to be redeemed of them from a month old, according to your estimation, for five shekels &#91;2 oz; 56.7 g&#93; of money, after the shekel of the sanctuary, (the shekel is 0.4 oz; 11.34 g; or called twenty gerahs 20/50 of an oz)</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 29:16; Deuteronomy 15:19–22; Leviticus 3:2–5"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn=". <span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 7:31–34; Exodus 29:26–28"> Their meat shall be yours, as the wave offering breast and as the right thigh, it shall be yours.</span>

It is a covenant of salt forever before Adonai to you and to your offspring with you.”</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 22:9; Numbers 1:51–52; Numbers 3:38"> Henceforth the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; shall not come near the Tent of Meeting, lest they bear crime deserving peanlty, sin ·missing the goal·, and die.</span>

It shall be a statute forever throughout all your generations. Among the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, they shall have no inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 18:26; Malachi 3:8–10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn=". Num 18:24 (Lev 27:30-33; Num 18:19-24) (#8.511):  T. Therefore I have said to them, ‘Among the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; they shall have no inheritance.’”</span>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Out of all your gifts, you shall offer every wave offering of Adonai, of all its best, even the holy part of it out of it.’<br />(Maftir ·Conclusion·)</span>

<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:10–14; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; Luke [White-light] 10:7"> You may eat it anywhere, you and your households, for it is your reward in return for your service in the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 19:8; Leviticus 22:2; Malachi 1:7"> You shall bear no crime deserving peanlty, sin ·missing the goal· by reason of it, when you have heaved from it its best. You shall not profane the holy things of the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, that you not die.’”</span>
