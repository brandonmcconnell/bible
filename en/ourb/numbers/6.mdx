<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 1:15; 1 Timothy 5:23; Amos 2:12"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn=". <span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 6:5; Numbers 6:21; Numbers 6:8–9"> All the days of his separation he shall eat nothing that is made of the grapevine, from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn=". <span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 21:11; Leviticus 19:28; Leviticus 21:1–3"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn=". <span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:17–18"> All the days of his separation he is holy to Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 6:18; Acts 18:18; Leviticus 14:9"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn=". Num 6:9 (Num 6:2, 6:9-12) (#1.26):  T. <span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 15:29; Leviticus 1:14; Leviticus 15:14"> On the eighth day he shall bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the priest, to the door of the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 14:30–31; Leviticus 5:8–10"> The priest shall offer one for a habitual sin ·miss the mark· offering, and the other for a burnt offering, and make atonement for him, because he sinned ·deviated (from the standard goal)· by reason of the dead, and shall make his head holy that same day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 5:6; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 24:13; Leviticus 14:24"> He shall separate to Adonai the days of his separation, and shall bring a male lamb a year old for a fault incurring guilt offering; but the former days shall be void, because his separation was defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 21:26"> “‘This is the Torah ·Teaching· of the Nazarite ·Consecrated·: when the days of his separation are fulfilled, he shall be brought to the door of the Tent of Meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 3:6; Leviticus 4:32; Leviticus 4:2–3"> and he shall offer his offering to Adonai, one male lamb a year old without defect for a burnt offering, and one ewe lamb a year old without defect for a habitual sin ·miss the mark· offering, and one ram without defect for peace offerings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 2:4; Exodus 29:2; Numbers 15:10"> and a basket of matzah ·unleavened bread·, cakes of fine flour mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil, and their meal offering, and their drink offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The priest shall present them before Adonai, and shall offer his habitual sin ·miss the mark· offering, and his burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He shall offer the ram for a sacrifice of peace offerings to Adonai, with the basket of matzah ·unleavened bread·. The priest shall offer also its meal offering, and its drink offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:24; Numbers 6:9; Acts 18:18"> The Nazarite ·Consecrated· shall shave the head of his separation at the door of the Tent of Meeting, and shall take the hair of the head of his separation, and put it on the fire which is under the sacrifice of peace offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:15; Leviticus 7:30; Exodus 29:23–28"> The priest shall take the boiled shoulder of the ram, and one unleavened cake out of the basket, and one unleavened wafer, and shall put them on the hands of the Nazarite ·Consecrated·, after he has shaved the head of his separation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Exodus 29:27–28; Numbers 5:25"> and the priest shall wave them for a wave offering before Adonai. <span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Ezra 2:69; Numbers 5:29"> “‘This is the Torah ·Teaching· of the Nazarite ·Consecrated· who vows, and of his offering to Adonai for his separation, besides that which he is able to get. According to his vow which he vows, so he must do after the Torah ·Teaching· of his separation.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Adonai spoke to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; 1 Chronicles 23:13; Genesis 48:20"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn=".
