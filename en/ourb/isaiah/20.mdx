<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; Numbers 14:34; Ezekiel 4:5–6"> Adonai said, “As my servant Isaiah &#91;Salvation of Yah&#93; has walked naked and barefoot three years for a sign and a wonder concerning Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93; and concerning Ethiopia,</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 30:5; Isaiah 30:3; 2 Kings 18:21"> They will be dismayed and confounded, because of Ethiopia their expectation, and of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93; their glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 23:33; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Hebrews 2:3"> The inhabitants of this coast land will say in that day, ‘Behold, this is our expectation, where we fled for help to be delivered from the king of Assyria &#91;Level plain&#93;.
