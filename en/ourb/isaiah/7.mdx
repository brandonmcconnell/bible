Note on textual variations: Why a virgin female as compared to young female? In the Hebrew Masoretic text, the prophecy in Isaiah uses the Hebrew word almah &#91;a woman of marriageable age&#93; whereas the verse in Matthew uses the Greek word parthenos &#91;a pure virgin or woman of marriageable age&#93;.
