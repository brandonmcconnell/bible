<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:12; Revelation 1:7; Joshua 2:11"> The burden of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;: “Behold, Adonai rides on a swift cloud,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn=". Quoted in  Rev 14:14">a</a></sup>and comes to Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;. The idols of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93; will tremble at his presence; and the heart of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93; will melt within it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 1 Samuel 14:20; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 10:36"> I will stir up the Egyptians &#91;people from Abode of slavery&#93; against the Egyptians &#91;people from Abode of slavery&#93;, and they will fight everyone against his brother, and everyone against his neighbor; city against city, and kingdom against kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; Daniel 2:2; 1 Chronicles 10:13"> The spirit of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93; will fail within it. I will destroy its counsel. They will seek the idols, the charmers, those who have familiar spirits, and the wizards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 46:26; Ezekiel 29:19"> I will give over the Egyptians &#91;people from Abode of slavery&#93; into the hand of a cruel lord. A fierce king will rule over them,” says, Adon, Adonai Tzva'ot &#91;Lord, Yahweh Commander of heaven’s armies&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:36; Ezekiel 30:12; Zechariah 14:18"> The waters will fail from the sea, and the river will be wasted and become dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:25; Exodus 7:18; 2 Kings 19:24"> The rivers will become foul. The streams of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93; will be diminished and dried up. The reeds and flags will wither away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 32:20; Joel 1:17–18; Isaiah 23:3"> The meadows by the Nile, by the brink of the Nile, and all the sown fields of the Nile, will become dry, be driven away, and be no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:10; Habakkuk 1:15; Numbers 11:5"> The fishermen will lament, and all those who fish in the Nile will mourn, and those who spread nets on the waters will languish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:7; Proverbs 7:16; 1 Kings 10:28"> Moreover those who work in combed flax, and those who weave white cloth, will be confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:10; Exodus 7:19; Exodus 8:5"> The pillars will be broken in pieces. All those who work for hire will be grieved in soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Acts 7:22; 1 Kings 4:30"> The princes of Zoan are utterly foolish. The counsel of the wisest counselors of Pharaoh has become stupid. How do you say to Pharaoh, “I am the son of the wise, the son of ancient kings?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20; Isaiah 14:24; Judges 9:38"> Where then are your wise men? Let them tell you now; and let them know what Adonai Tzva'ot &#91;Yahweh Commander of heaven’s armies&#93; has purposed concerning Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:13; Jeremiah 2:16; Jeremiah 46:14"> The princes of Zoan have become fools. The princes of Memphis are deceived. They have caused Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93; to go astray, who are the corner-stone of her tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Kings 22:20–23; Ezekiel 14:7–9"> Adonai has mixed a spirit of perverseness in the middle of her; and they have caused Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93; to go astray in all of its works, like a drunken man staggers in his vomit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:14–15; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12; Proverbs 14:23"> Neither shall there be any work for Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;, which head or tail, palm branch or rush, may do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Nahum 3:13; Isaiah 11:15"> In that day the Egyptians &#91;people from Abode of slavery&#93; will be like women. They will tremble and fear because of the shaking of Adonai Tzva'ot &#91;Yahweh Commander of heaven’s armies&#93;’s hand, which he shakes over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 14:24; Daniel 4:35; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> The land of Judah &#91;Praised&#93; will become a terror to Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;. Everyone to whom mention is made of it will be afraid, because of the plans of Adonai Tzva'ot &#91;Yahweh Commander of heaven’s armies&#93;, which he determines against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:9; Zechariah 2:11; Isaiah 19:21"> In that day, there will be five cities in the land of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93; that speak the language of Canaan &#91;Humbled&#93;, and swear to Adonai Tzva'ot &#91;Yahweh Commander of heaven’s armies&#93;. One will be called “The city of destruction.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 28:18; Joshua 22:10; Joshua 22:26"> In that day, there will be an altar to Adonai in the middle of the land of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;, and a pillar to Adonai at its border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Titus 2:13; Isaiah 45:21–22"> It will be for a sign and for a witness to Adonai Tzva'ot &#91;Yahweh Commander of heaven’s armies&#93; in the land of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;; for they will cry to Adonai because of oppressors, and he will send them one to save and a defend them, and he will deliver them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; Malachi 1:11; John [Yah is gracious] 17:3"> Adonai will be known to Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;, and the Egyptians &#91;people from Abode of slavery&#93; will know Adonai in that day. Yes, they will worship with sacrifice and offering, and will vow a vow to Adonai, and will perform it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Hebrews 12:11; Hosea 14:1"> Adonai will strike Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;, striking and healing. They will make teshuvah ·complete return· to Adonai, and he will be entreated by them, and will heal them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 11:16; Ephesians 2:18–22; Isaiah 35:8–10"> In that day there will be a highway out of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93; to Assyria &#91;Level plain&#93;, and the Assyrian shall come into Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;, and the Egyptian &#91;person from Abode of slavery&#93; into Assyria &#91;Level plain&#93;; and the Egyptians &#91;people from Abode of slavery&#93; will worship with the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 65:8; Genesis 12:2; Galatians 3:14"> In that day, Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; will be the third with Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93; and with Assyria &#91;Level plain&#93;, a blessing within the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 29:23; Hosea 2:23; Romans 9:24–25"> because Adonai Tzva'ot &#91;Yahweh Commander of heaven’s armies&#93; has blessed them, saying, “Blessed be Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93; my people, Assyria &#91;Level plain&#93; the work of my hands, and Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; my inheritance.”</span>
