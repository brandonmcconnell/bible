<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 20:12–19; Isaiah 39:1–8; 2 Samuel 10:2"> At that time, Merodach Baladan the son of Baladan, king of Babylon &#91;Confusion&#93;, sent letters and a present to Hezekiah; for he sh'ma ·heard obeyed· that he had been sick, and had recovered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:25; 2 Chronicles 32:31; Job 31:25"> Hezekiah was pleased with them, and showed them the house of his precious things, the silver, and the gold, the spices, and the precious oil, and all the house of his armor, and all that was found in his treasures. There was nothing in his house, nor in all his dominion, that Hezekiah didn’t show them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:49; 2 Chronicles 16:7; 2 Samuel 12:1"> Then Isaiah &#91;Salvation of Yah&#93; the prophet came to king Hezekiah, and asked him, “What did these men say? Where did they come from to you?” Hezekiah said, “They have come from a country far from me, even from Babylon &#91;Confusion&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 31:33; Joshua 7:19; 1 John 1:9"> Then he asked, “What have they seen in your house?” Hezekiah answered, “They have seen all that is in my house. There is nothing among my treasures that I have not shown them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:13–14; 1 Samuel 15:16"> Then Isaiah &#91;Salvation of Yah&#93; said to Hezekiah, “Sh'ma ·Hear obey· the Davar Yahweh Tzva'ot &#91;Word of He sustains breathing Commander of heaven’s armies&#93;:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 25:13–15; 2 Kings 24:13; Jeremiah 20:5"> ‘Behold, the days are coming when all that is in your house, and that which your fathers have stored up until today, will be carried to Babylon &#91;Confusion&#93;. Nothing will be left,’ says Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 1:1–7; 2 Chronicles 36:10; 2 Kings 24:12"> ‘They will take away your sons who will issue from you, whom you shall father, and they will be eunuchs in the king of Babylon &#91;Confusion&#93;’s palace.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:26; 2 Chronicles 34:28; 1 Peter 5:6"> Then Hezekiah said to Isaiah &#91;Salvation of Yah&#93;, “Adonai’s word which you have spoken is good.” He said moreover, “For there will be peace and truth in my days.”</span>
