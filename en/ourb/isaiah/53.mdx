<li id="fn-ac"><a href="#fnref-ac" className="fnb">ac</a> <span className="fnr">53:8</span> b MP:  Messiah is killed / “cut off” as just punishment in place of the people’s transgressions. (Acts 8:33; 2 Cor 5:21; 1 Pet 1:18-19)</li>
<li id="fn-ad"><a href="#fnref-ad" className="fnb">ad</a> <span className="fnr">53:8</span> . (7-8) Quoted in  Acts 8:32-33</li>
<li id="fn-ae"><a href="#fnref-ae" className="fnb">ae</a> <span className="fnr">53:9</span> a MP:  By being killed along with other criminals, Messiah is assigned a grave with the wicked. (Matt 27:38)</li>
<li id="fn-af"><a href="#fnref-af" className="fnb">af</a> <span className="fnr">53:9</span> a MP:  Buried in a rich man’s grave. Evidence: Raymond Brown comments on Roman attitudes to the bodies of the crucified. The Digest of Justinian 48:24 (200 CE) gives two Roman political legal people’s decisions about criminals executed. Ulpian: “The bodies of those who suffer capital punishment are not to be refused to their relatives.” Julius Paulus adds: “Nor to any who seek them for burial.” Ulpian states this might be refused if the criminal was executed for treason. Therefore Yeshua was not convicted of Roman treason, but likely killed for a political motive. (The Burial of Yeshua Mark 15:42-47 by Raymond Brown (1988)). (Matt 27:57-60)</li>
<li id="fn-ag"><a href="#fnref-ag" className="fnb">ag</a> <span className="fnr">53:9</span> b MP:  Messiah is innocent, having done no violence or deceit, persoanlly or politically. (Luke 23:13-15; 1 Pet 2:21-23)</li>
<li id="fn-ah"><a href="#fnref-ah" className="fnb">ah</a> <span className="fnr">53:9</span> b MP:  Messiah’s character is without blame, even when provoked. (Luke 23:14; 1 Pet 2:23)</li>
<li id="fn-ai"><a href="#fnref-ai" className="fnb">ai</a> <span className="fnr">53:9</span> . Quoted in  1 Pet 2:22</li>
<li id="fn-aj"><a href="#fnref-aj" className="fnb">aj</a> <span className="fnr">53:10</span> a MP:  God will afflict the Messiah, in association with bearing sin. (John 12:7-33; Gal 1:4; 1 Pet 2:24)</li>
<li id="fn-ak"><a href="#fnref-ak" className="fnb">ak</a> <span className="fnr">53:10</span> b MP:  Messiah offers himself as a sin offering. (Matt 20:28b; Eph 5:2)</li>
<li id="fn-al"><a href="#fnref-al" className="fnb">al</a> <span className="fnr">53:10</span> b MP:  God’s desire is fulfilled in Messiah being a sin offering. This end goal is God’s purpose. In context, God pleasure is linked to a sin offering being provided. An atonement, a redemption, and a covering for sin was accomplished through that offering for all people who are associated (see Is 53:5-6, 53:11) bringing God pleasure. Being associated with the sin offering is seen in (Lev 4:4, 4:15, 4:24, 4:29, 4:33). (John 17:1-5; Rom 4:25; Eph 1:16-23; Heb 10:9-10; 1 John 2:2)</li>
<li id="fn-am"><a href="#fnref-am" className="fnb">am</a> <span className="fnr">53:10</span> c MP:  Messiah is resurrected from death and has life beyond the grave. “He will prolong / lengthen his days”. (Luke 24:6-7, 24:46; Acts 2:24)</li>
<li id="fn-an"><a href="#fnref-an" className="fnb">an</a> <span className="fnr">53:11</span> a MP:  God is fully satisfied with the suffering of the Messiah. By accepting this, it infers the sin offering is accepted. Acceptance of the sin offering is seen in (Lev 4:20). Read in context of (Lev 4:2-7). (John 10:17-18; Gal 1:4; 1 Pet 2:24)</li>
<li id="fn-ao"><a href="#fnref-ao" className="fnb">ao</a> <span className="fnr">53:11</span> b MP:  Messiah has intimate knowledge of the anguish and suffering of the sin that his atonement is covering. (Heb 2:17-18; 4:15, 9:28)</li>
<li id="fn-ap"><a href="#fnref-ap" className="fnb">ap</a> <span className="fnr">53:11</span> b MP:  Messiah is God’s servant. (John 6:38; Romans 6:19)</li>
<li id="fn-aq"><a href="#fnref-aq" className="fnb">aq</a> <span className="fnr">53:12</span> a MP:  Because of making his atonement offering, Messiah is greatly exalted by God and reaps rewards. (Matt 28:18; Eph 1:20-22; Heb 1:3)</li>
<li id="fn-ar"><a href="#fnref-ar" className="fnb">ar</a> <span className="fnr">53:12</span> a MP:  “He will divide the spoils.” By being an atonement for many, Messiah is the Redeemer and therefore enjoys the benefits / spoils of this conquest; redeeming people from their former master who rules thru sin to the new Master who is Yahweh. (Luke 11:21-23; John 15:15, Rom 6:11-18, 8:1-4, 8:14-17; Gal 4:3-8; Eph 4:8-9)</li>
<li id="fn-as"><a href="#fnref-as" className="fnb">as</a> <span className="fnr">53:12</span> a MP:  Obedient even unto death. (Matt 16:21-23; Phil 2:8b)</li>
<li id="fn-at"><a href="#fnref-at" className="fnb">at</a> <span className="fnr">53:12</span> b MP:  Messiah is grouped with criminals at his death. (Mark 15:27-28; Luke 22:37)</li>
<li id="fn-au"><a href="#fnref-au" className="fnb">au</a> <span className="fnr">53:12</span> . Quoted in  Mark 15:28; Luke 22.37</li>
<li id="fn-av"><a href="#fnref-av" className="fnb">av</a> <span className="fnr">53:12</span> c MP:  Messiah is the atonement that covers the sins of those who transgress God’s Torah &#91;Teaching&#93;. (Romans 2:23-26; Col 2:14; 1 John 2:2)</li>
<li id="fn-aw"><a href="#fnref-aw" className="fnb">aw</a> <span className="fnr">53:12</span> . Quoted in  1 Cor 15:3; Heb 9:28; 1 Pet 2:24</li>
