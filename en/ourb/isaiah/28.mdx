<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:30; Nahum 1:8; Ezekiel 13:11"> Behold, 'Adonay &#91;Lord&#93; has a mighty and strong one. Like a storm of hail, a destroying storm, and like a storm of mighty waters overflowing, he will cast them down to the earth with his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 26:6; Hebrews 10:29; Revelation 11:2"> The crown of pride of the drunkards of Ephraim &#91;Fruit&#93; will be trodden under foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nahum 3:12; Micah 7:1; Isaiah 28:1"> The fading flower of his glorious beauty, which is on the head of the fertile valley, shall be like the first-ripe fig before the summer; which someone picks and eats as soon as he sees it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 5:4; Isaiah 62:3; Isaiah 60:19"> In that day, Adonai Tzva'ot &#91;Yahweh Commander of heaven’s armies&#93; will become a crown of glory, and a diadem ·crown· of beauty, to the residue of his people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 18:32–34; John [Yah is gracious] 5:30; Joshua 1:9"> and a spirit of mishpat ·justice· to him who sits in judgment, and strength to those who turn back the battle at the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 56:10–12; Luke [White-light] 21:34; Isaiah 19:14"> They are lead astray, intoxicated, unintentional sin ·error· with wine, and stagger with strong drink. The priest and the prophet are lead astray, intoxicated, unintentional sin ·error· with strong drink. They are swallowed up by wine. They stagger with strong drink. They are lead astray, intoxicated, unintentional sin ·error· in vision. They stumble in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:26; Proverbs 26:11; Habakkuk 2:15–16"> For all tables are completely full of filthy vomit and filthiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 131:2; Jeremiah 6:10; John [Yah is gracious] 12:38"> Whom will he teach knowledge? To whom will he explain the message? Those who are weaned from the milk, and drawn from the breasts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 28:13; 2 Timothy 3:7; Jeremiah 11:7"> For it is precept on precept, precept on precept; line on line, line on line; here a little, there a little.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:21; Deuteronomy 28:49; Isaiah 33:19"> But he will speak to this nation with stammering lips and in another language;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 11:28–29; Isaiah 30:15; Jeremiah 6:16"> to whom he said, “This is the resting place. Give rest to weary;” and “This is the refreshing;” yet they would not sh'ma ·hear obey·.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn=". (11-12) Quoted in  1 Cor 14:21">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:14; Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Peter 3:16"> Therefore Adonai’s word will be to them precept on precept, precept on precept; line on line, line on line; here a little, there a little; that they may go, fall backward, be broken, be snared, and be taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 28:22; Isaiah 29:20; Isaiah 1:10"> Therefore sh'ma ·hear obey· Adonai’s word, you scoffers, that rule this people in Jerusalem &#91;City of peace&#93;:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Ezekiel 13:22; Amos 2:4"> “Because you have said, ‘We have made a covenant ·binding contract between two or more parties· with death, and with Sheol ·Place of the dead· are we in agreement. When the overflowing scourge passes through, it won’t come to us; for we have made lies our refuge, and we have hidden ourselves under falsehood.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 118:22; Acts 4:11–12; 1 Peter 2:6–8"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="(context 28:15-18) MP:  Messiah is the foundation stone and corner-stone set in opposition to darkness and death. (See also Is 25:8; Ps 118:26). (1 Pet 2:4-6)">b</a></sup><span className="woj">Therefore thus says 'Adonay Adonai &#91;Lord Yahweh&#93;, “Behold, I lay in Zion &#91;Mountain ridge, Marking&#93; for a foundation stone, a tried stone, a precious corner-stone of a sure foundation. He who trusts shall not act hastily.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn=". Quoted in  Rom 9:33, 10:11; 1 Pet 2:6">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Amos 7:7–9; Revelation 16:21"> I will make mishpat ·justice· the measuring line, and righteousness the plumb line. The hail will sweep away the refuge of lies, and the waters will overflow the hiding place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 7:7; Isaiah 8:10; Isaiah 28:15"> Your covenant ·binding contract between two or more parties· with death shall be annulled, and your agreement with Sheol ·Place of the dead· shall not stand. When the overflowing scourge passes through, then you will be trampled down by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Isaiah 36:22; 2 Kings 24:2"> As often as it passes through, it will seize you; for morning by morning it will pass through, by day and by night; and it will be nothing but terror to understand the message.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; 1 Corinthians 1:18–31; Romans 9:30–32"> For the bed is too short to stretch out on, and the blanket is too narrow to wrap oneself in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:11; Joshua 10:12; 2 Samuel 5:20"> For Adonai will rise up as on Mount Perazim. He will be angry as in the valley of Gibeon; that he may do his work, his unusual work, and bring to pass his act, his extraordinary act.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22–23; Isaiah 24:1–23; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 27:44"> Now therefore don’t be scoffers, lest your bonds be made strong; for I have sh'ma ·heard obeyed· a decree of destruction from 'Adonay, Adonai Tzva'ot &#91;Lord, Yahweh Commander of heaven’s armies&#93;, on the whole earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Jeremiah 22:29; Revelation 2:11"> Give ear, and sh'ma ·hear obey· my voice! Listen, and hear my speech!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Hosea 10:11–12"> Does he who plows to sow plow continually? Does he keep turning the soil and breaking the clods?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:9; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 23:23; Exodus 9:31–32"> When he has leveled its surface, does not he plant the dill, and scatter the cumin seed, and put in the wheat in rows, the barley in the appointed place, and the spelt in its place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 31:3–6; Exodus 36:2; Job 39:17"> For his God instructs him in right judgment, and teaches him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15; Isaiah 27:7–8; Jeremiah 46:28"> For the dill are not threshed with a sharp instrument, neither is a cart wheel turned over the cumin; but the dill is beaten out with a stick, and the cumin with a rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:9–10; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 3:12; John [Yah is gracious] 12:24"> Bread flour must be ground; so he will not always be threshing it. Although he drives the wheel of his threshing cart over it, his horses don’t grind it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Jeremiah 32:19; Job 5:9"> This also comes out from Adonai Tzva'ot &#91;Yahweh Commander of heaven’s armies&#93;, who is wonderful in counsel, and excellent in wisdom.</span>
