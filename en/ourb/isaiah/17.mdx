<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:5; Jeremiah 7:33; Numbers 32:34"> The cities of Aroer are forsaken. They will be for flocks, which shall lie down, and no one shall make them afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 9:11; Isaiah 8:4; Isaiah 7:16"> The fortress shall cease from Ephraim &#91;Fruit&#93;, and the kingdom from Damascus &#91;Bucket of blood&#93;, and the remnant of Syria &#91;Elevated&#93;. They will be as the glory of the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;,” says Adonai Tzva'ot &#91;Yahweh Commander of heaven’s armies&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16; Deuteronomy 32:15–27; Isaiah 24:13"> “It will happen in that day that the glory of Jacob &#91;Supplanter&#93; will be made thin, and the fatness of his flesh will become lean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:30; Joel 3:13; Jeremiah 51:33"> It will be like when the harvester gathers the wheat, and his arm reaps the grain. Yes, it will be like when one gleans grain in the valley of Rephaim &#91;Descendants of Terrible one&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 24:13; Deuteronomy 4:27; Romans 9:27"> Yet gleanings will be left there, like the shaking of an olive tree, two or three olives in the top of the uppermost bough, four or five in the outermost branches of a fruitful tree,” says Yahweh, Elohim Isra'el &#91;He sustains breathing, God of God prevails&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 7:7; Hosea 6:1; Hosea 3:5"> In that day, people will look to their Maker, and their eyes will have respect for haKadosh Isra'el &#91;the Holy One of God prevails&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 30:22; Isaiah 27:9; Isaiah 2:8"> They will not look to the altars, the work of their hands; neither shall they respect that which their fingers have made, either the Asherah poles, or the incense altars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 3:11–15; Isaiah 27:10; Isaiah 24:1–12"> In that day, their strong cities will be like the forsaken places in the woods and on the mountain top, which were forsaken from before the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;; and it will be a desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 106:21; Deuteronomy 32:18; Isaiah 26:4"> For you have forgotten the God of your yesha' ·salvation·, and have not remembered the rock of your strength. Therefore you plant pleasant plants, and set out foreign seedlings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 4:8; Hosea 8:7; Galatians 6:7–8"> In the day of your planting, you hedge it in. In the morning, you make your seed blossom, but the harvest flees away in the day of grief and of desperate sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:23; Luke [White-light] 21:25; Psalms 18:4"> Ah, the uproar of many peoples, who roar like the roaring of the seas; and the rushing of nations, that rush like the rushing of mighty waters!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 9:5; Psalms 1:4; Isaiah 41:15–16"> The nations will rush like the rushing of many waters: but he will rebuke them, and they will flee far off, and will be chased like the chaff of the mountains before the wind, and like the whirling dust before the storm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 19:35; Proverbs 22:23; Jeremiah 13:25"> At evening, behold, terror! Before the morning, they are no more. This is the portion of those who plunder us, and the lot of those who rob us.</span>
