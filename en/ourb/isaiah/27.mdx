<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:27; Isaiah 48:10; Isaiah 17:8"> Therefore by this the depravity (moral evil) of Jacob &#91;Supplanter&#93; will be forgiven, and this is all the fruit of taking away his habitual sin ·missing the mark·:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn=". <span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:2; Jeremiah 26:18; Isaiah 32:13–14"> For the fortified city is solitary, a habitation deserted and forsaken, like the wilderness. The calf will feed there, and there he will lie down, and consume its branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Isaiah 1:3; Isaiah 43:1"> When its boughs are withered, they will be broken off. <span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 30:3–4; Isaiah 56:8"> It will happen in that day, that Adonai will thresh from the flowing stream of the Euphrates &#91;Fruitful&#93; to the brook of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;; and you will be gathered one by one, children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 24:31; Leviticus 25:9; Hebrews 12:22"> It will happen in that day that a great shofar ·ram horn·<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn=".
