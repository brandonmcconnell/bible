<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Adonai spoke to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:6; Deuteronomy 2:14; Deuteronomy 2:7"> “You have encircled this mountain long enough. Turn northward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 20:14–21; Exodus 15:15; Deuteronomy 23:7"> Enjoin the people, saying, ‘You are to pass through the border of your cousins the children of Esau &#91;Hairy&#93;, who dwell in Seir; and they will be afraid of you. Therefore be careful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Joshua 24:4; Jeremiah 27:5"> Don’t contend with them; for I will not give you any of their land, no, not so much as for the sole of your foot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn=". <span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 7:12; Numbers 20:19; Romans 12:17"> You shall purchase food from them for money, that you may eat. You shall also buy water from them for money, that you may drink.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 22:35; Nehemiah 9:21; Deuteronomy 8:2–4"> For Adonai your God has blessed you in all the work of your hand. He has known your walking through this great wilderness. These forty years, Adonai your God has been with you. You have lacked nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 9:26; Judges 11:18; 2 Kings 16:6"> So we passed by from our brothers the children of Esau &#91;Hairy&#93;, who dwell in Seir, from the way of the Arabah from Elath and from Ezion Geber. We turned and passed by the way of the wilderness of Moab &#91;From father&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 19:36–37; Numbers 21:15; Numbers 21:28"> Adonai said to me, “Don’t bother Moab &#91;From father&#93;, neither contend with them in battle; for I will not give you any of his land for a possession; because I have given Ar to the children of Lot &#91;Veil, Covering&#93; for a possession.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 14:5; Deuteronomy 2:11; Numbers 13:22"> (The Emim lived therein before, a people great, and many, and tall, as the Anakim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Numbers 13:28; Genesis 14:5"> These also are considered to be Rephaim &#91;Descendants of Terrible one&#93;, as the Anakim; but the Moabites &#91;Descendants of From father&#93; call them Emim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:22; Genesis 14:6; Deuteronomy 2:32–11"> The Horites also lived in Seir before, but the children of Esau &#91;Hairy&#93; succeeded them. They destroyed them from before them, and lived in their place; as Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; did to the land of his possession, which Adonai gave to them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 21:12; Numbers 13:23"> “Now rise up, and cross over the brook Zered.” We went over the brook Zered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; Numbers 14:28–35; Deuteronomy 1:34–35"> The days in which we came from Kadesh Barnea &#91;Consecrated Son-inconstant&#93;, until we had come over the brook Zered, were thirty-eight years; until all the generation of the men of war were consumed from the middle of the camp, as Adonai swore to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 106:26; Isaiah 66:14; Psalms 32:4"> Moreover Adonai’s hand was against them, to destroy them from the middle of the camp, until they were consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So, when all the men of war were consumed and dead from among the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Adonai spoke to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 21:23; Isaiah 15:1; Numbers 21:15"> “You are to pass over Ar, the border of Moab &#91;From father&#93;, today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:9; Judges 11:13–27; 2 Chronicles 20:10"> When you come near the border of the children of Ammon &#91;Tribal people&#93;, don’t bother them, nor contend with them; for I will not give you any of the land of the children of Ammon &#91;Tribal people&#93; for a possession; because I have given it to the children of Lot &#91;Veil, Covering&#93; for a possession.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 14:5"> (That also is considered a land of Rephaim &#91;Descendants of Terrible one&#93;: Rephaim &#91;Descendants of Terrible one&#93; lived there before; but the Ammonites &#91;Tribal people&#93; call them Zamzummim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:7–8; Deuteronomy 1:28; Deuteronomy 2:22"> a great people, many, and tall, as the Anakim; but Adonai destroyed them before them; and they succeeded them, and lived in their place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Genesis 36:20–30; Deuteronomy 2:12"> as he did for the children of Esau &#91;Hairy&#93;, who dwell in Seir, when he destroyed the Horites from before them; and they succeeded them, and lived in their place even to this day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 10:14; Joshua 13:3; Amos 9:7"> and the Avvim, who lived in villages as far as Gaza, the Caphtorim, who came out of Caphtor, destroyed them, and lived in their place.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5; Daniel 4:17; Judges 11:18–21"> “Rise up, take your journey, and pass over the valley of the Arnon: behold, I have given into your hand Sihon the Amorite &#91;Descendants of Talkers&#93;, king of Heshbon, and his land; begin to possess it, and contend with him in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:25; Exodus 15:14–16; Deuteronomy 28:10"> Today I will begin to put the dread of you and the fear of you on the peoples who are under the whole sky, who shall sh'ma ·hear obey· the report of you, and shall tremble, and be in anguish because of you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 10:5–6; Luke [White-light] 10:10–12; Joshua 13:18"> I sent messengers out of the wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon king of Heshbon with words of peace, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 11:19; Deuteronomy 2:6; Numbers 21:21–23"> “Let me pass through your land. I will go along by the highway, I will turn neither to the right hand nor to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 20:19"> You shall sell me food for money, that I may eat; and give me water for money, that I may drink. Just let me pass through on my feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 20:18; Judges 11:17–18; Deuteronomy 23:3–4"> as the children of Esau &#91;Hairy&#93; who dwell in Seir, and the Moabites &#91;Descendants of From father&#93; who dwell in Ar, did to me; until I pass over the Jordan &#91;Descender&#93; into the land which Yahweh Eloheikhem &#91;Yahweh our God&#93; gives us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 21:23; Exodus 4:21; Joshua 11:19–20"> But Sihon king of Heshbon would not let us pass by him; for Adonai your God hardened his spirit, and made his heart obstinate, that he might deliver him into your hand, as it is today. (6)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:8; Deuteronomy 2:24"> Adonai said to me, “Behold, I have begun to deliver up Sihon and his land before you. Begin to possess, that you may inherit his land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 21:23–30; Judges 11:20–23; Psalms 136:19"> Then Sihon came out against us, he and all his people, to battle at Jahaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Joshua 21:44; Deuteronomy 3:2–3"> Yahweh Eloheikhem &#91;Yahweh our God&#93; delivered him up before us; and we struck him, his sons, and all his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; 1 Samuel 15:3; Deuteronomy 3:6"> We took all his cities at that time, and utterly destroyed every inhabited city, with the women and the little ones. We left no one remaining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Joshua 8:27; Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:9–11"> Only the livestock we took for plunder for ourselves, with the plunder of the cities which we had taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 13:9; Deuteronomy 4:48; Deuteronomy 3:12"> From Aroer, which is on the edge of the valley of the Arnon, and the city that is in the valley, even to Gilead, there was not a city too high for us. Yahweh Eloheikhem &#91;Yahweh our God&#93; delivered up all before us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 21:24; Deuteronomy 3:16; Genesis 32:22"> Only to the land of the children of Ammon &#91;Tribal people&#93; you didn’t come near; all the banks of the river Jabbok, and the cities of the hill country, and wherever Yahweh Eloheikhem &#91;Yahweh our God&#93; forbade us by his enjoined words.</span>
