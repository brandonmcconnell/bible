<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 21:6; Numbers 20:11; Jeremiah 2:6"> who led you through the great and terrible wilderness, with fiery serpents and scorpions, and thirsty ground where there was no water; who poured water for you out of the rock of flint;</span>
