You shall not cause the land to sin ·err (from the standard goal)·, which Adonai your God gives you for an inheritance. (6)</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:25; Daniel 4:27; Exodus 22:26–27"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn=".
