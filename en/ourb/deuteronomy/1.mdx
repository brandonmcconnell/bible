<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 33:38; Deuteronomy 4:1–2; Numbers 20:1"> In the fortieth year, in the eleventh month Shevat ·Staff / Rod / Royal scepter·, on the first day of the month, Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; spoke to the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, according to all that Adonai had given Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, words of enjoinings to them;</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:8; Deuteronomy 32:46; Deuteronomy 31:9"> Beyond the Jordan &#91;Descender&#93;, in the land of Moab &#91;From father&#93;, Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; began to declare this Torah ·Teaching·, saying,</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 10:40; Amos 2:9"> Turn, and take your journey, and go to the hill country of the Amorites &#91;Descendants of Talkers&#93;, and to all the places near there, in the Arabah, in the hill country, and in the lowland, and in the South, and by the seashore, the land of the Canaanites &#91;Descendants of Humbled&#93;, and Lebanon, as far as the great river, the river Euphrates &#91;Fruitful&#93;.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Exodus 32:13; Genesis 22:17"> Adonai your God has multiplied you, and behold, you are today as the stars of the sky for multitude.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn=". <span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 115:14; Genesis 22:17; Numbers 6:27"> Adonai, the God of your fathers, make you a thousand times as many as you are, and bless you, as he has promised you! (2)</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> You answered me, and said, “The thing which you have spoken is good to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:18; Exodus 18:25–26; Ephesians 4:11"> So I took the heads of your tribes, wise men, and known, and made them heads over you, captains of thousands, and captains of hundreds, and captains of fifties, and captains of tens, and officers, according to your tribes.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:7–8"> I said to you, “You have come to the hill country of the Amorites &#91;Descendants of Talkers&#93;, which Yahweh Eloheikhem &#91;Yahweh our God&#93; gives to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Psalms 46:1; Numbers 14:8–9"> Behold, Adonai your God has set the land before you. Go up, take possession, as Adonai, the God of your fathers, has spoken to you. Don’t be afraid, neither be dismayed.” (3)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 13:1–20"> You came near to me, everyone of you, and said, “Let us send men before us, that they may search the land for us, and bring us word again of the way by which we must go up, and the cities to which we shall come.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 13:3–33"> The thing pleased me well. I took twelve men of you, one man for every tribe.</span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> They took some of the fruit of the land in their hands, and brought it down to us, and brought us word again, and said, “It is a good land which Yahweh Eloheikhem &#91;Yahweh our God&#93; gives to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 14:1–4; Acts 7:51; Psalms 106:24–25"> Yet you wouldn’t go up, but rebelled against the order of Adonai your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:28; Psalms 106:25; Numbers 14:3"> You murmured in your tents, and said, “Because Adonai hated us, he has brought us out of the land of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;, to deliver us into the hand of the Amorites &#91;Descendants of Talkers&#93;, to destroy us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 13:28–33; Deuteronomy 9:1–2; Joshua 15:14"> Where are we going up? <span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Then I said to you, “Don’t dread, neither be afraid of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Nehemiah 4:20; Romans 8:31"> Adonai your God who goes before you, he will fight for you, according to all that he did for you in Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93; before your eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3–4; Acts 13:18; Hosea 11:3–4"> and in the wilderness, where you have seen how that Adonai your God bore you, as a man does bear his son, in all the way that you went, until you came to this place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 106:24; Jude 1:5; Hebrews 3:18–19"> Yet in this thing you didn’t trust Adonai your God,</span>

<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 14:22–30; Psalms 95:11; Ezekiel 20:15"> Adonai sh'ma ·heard obeyed· the voice of your words, and was angry, and swore, saying,</span>

<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 20:12; Deuteronomy 4:21; Deuteronomy 34:4"> Also Adonai was angry with me for your sakes, saying, “You also shall not go in there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Numbers 14:30; Deuteronomy 31:7–8"> Joshua &#91;Yah Salvation&#93; the son of Nun, who stands before you, shall go in there. Encourage him, for he shall cause Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; to inherit it. (4)</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15–16; Numbers 14:3; Numbers 14:31"> Moreover your little ones, whom you said should be captured or killed, and your children, who today have no knowledge of good or evil, they shall go in there, and I will give it to, and they shall possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 14:25"> But as for you, turn, and take your journey into the wilderness by the way to the Sea of Suf &#91;Reed Sea&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 14:39–45; Proverbs 19:3; Numbers 22:34"> Then you answered and said to me, “We have sinned ·deviated (from the standard goal)· against Adonai, we will go up and fight, according to all that Yahweh Eloheikhem &#91;Yahweh our God&#93; enjoined us.” Every man of you put on his weapons of war, and presumed to go up into the hill country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Numbers 14:41–43; Joshua 7:8–13; Isaiah 59:1–2"> Adonai said to me, “Tell them, ‘Don’t go up, neither fight; for I am not among you; lest you be struck before your enemies.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 14:44; Isaiah 63:10; Acts 7:51"> So I spoke to you, and you didn’t sh'ma ·hear obey·; but you rebelled against the order of Adonai, and were presumptuous, and went up into the hill country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 118:12; Numbers 14:45; Numbers 21:3"> The Amorites &#91;Descendants of Talkers&#93;, who lived in that hill country, came out against you, and chased you, as bees do, and beat you down in Seir, even to Hormah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Psalms 78:34"> You made teshuvah ·complete return· and wept before Adonai; but Adonai didn’t sh'ma ·hear obey· unto your voice, nor turn his ear to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 20:22; Judges 11:16–17"> So you stayed in Kadesh many days, according to the days that you remained.</span>
