<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:34–35; Mark [Hammer] 3:23; Mark [Hammer] 4:11"> He taught them many things in parables, and told them in his teaching,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 2:5; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:26; Mark [Hammer] 4:26–29"> “Listen! Behold, the farmer went out to sow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:19; Mark [Hammer] 4:15; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:4"> and as he sowed, some seed fell by the road, and the birds came and devoured it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26; Luke [White-light] 8:6; Luke [White-light] 8:13"> Others fell on the rocky ground, where it had little soil, and immediately it sprang up, because it had no depth of soil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:11; Jeremiah 17:5–8; Jonah 4:8"> When the sun had risen, it was scorched; and because it had no root, it withered away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Luke [White-light] 8:14; Genesis 3:17–18"> Others fell among the thorns, and the thorns grew up, and choked it, and it yielded no fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John [Yah is gracious] 15:5; Colossians 1:6; Mark [Hammer] 4:20"> Others fell into the good ground, and yielded fruit, growing up and increasing. Some produced thirty times, some sixty times, and some one hundred times as much.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 11:15; Mark [Hammer] 4:23–24; Revelation 3:22"> He said, “Whoever has ears to hear, let him sh'ma ·hear obey·.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:10–17; Mark [Hammer] 4:34; Proverbs 13:20"> When he was alone, those who were around him with the twelve asked him about the parables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 11:25; Luke [White-light] 8:10; 1 John 5:20"> He said to them, “To you is given the mystery of God’s Kingdom, but to those who are outside, all things are done in parables,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Isaiah 44:18; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:14–15"> that ‘they may be always looking but never seeing; always listening but never understanding. Otherwise, they might turn and their sins ·concrete evil deeds that misses the mark and is without share in the goal· be forgiven.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn=". <span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:51–52; Mark [Hammer] 7:17–18"> He said to them, “Don’t you understand this parable? How will you understand all of the parables?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 8:4; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:19; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:37"> The farmer sows the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:19; 2 Corinthians 2:11"> The ones by the road are the ones where the word is sown; and when they have heard, immediately Satan &#91;Adversary&#93; comes, and takes away the word which has been sown in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark [Hammer] 6:20; Luke [White-light] 8:13; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:20–21"> These in the same way are those who are sown on the rocky places, who, when they have heard the word, immediately receive it with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 11:6; 1 Thessalonians 3:3–5; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 12:31"> They have no root in themselves, but are short-lived. When oppression or persecution arises because of the word, immediately they are scandalized ·to entrap, to cause weak knees that waiver, stumbling block that causes falling, distrusting one that should be trusted and obeyed, disapproving of authority, to judge unfavorably causing displeasure, indignant·.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 8:14; Jeremiah 4:3; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:22"> Others are those who are sown among the thorns. These are those who have heard the word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; 1 Timothy 6:17"> and the cares of this age, and the deceitfulness of riches, and the epithumia ·lusts· of other things entering in choke the word, and it becomes unfruitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Luke [White-light] 8:15; John [Yah is gracious] 15:4–5"> Those which were sown on the good ground are those who hear the word, and accept it, and bear fruit, some thirty times, some sixty times, and some one hundred times.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 5:15; Luke [White-light] 11:33; Luke [White-light] 8:16"> He said to them, “Is the lamp brought to be put under a basket or under a bed? Is not it put on a stand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 8:17; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 10:26–27"> For there is nothing hidden, except that it should be made known; neither was anything made secret, but that it should come to light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark [Hammer] 4:9; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 11:15; Revelation 2:29"> If any man has ears to hear, let him sh'ma ·hear obey·.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Luke [White-light] 8:18; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 7:2"> He said to them, “Take heed what you hear. With whatever measure you measure, it will be measured to you, and more will be given to you who hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:12; Luke [White-light] 8:18; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 25:28–29"> For whoever has, to him will more be given, and he who does not have, even that which he has will be taken away from him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:24; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9; Luke [White-light] 8:11"> He said, “God’s Kingdom is as if a man should cast seed on the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:5; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 1 Corinthians 15:37–38"> and should sleep and rise night and day, and the seed should spring up and grow, he does not know how.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark [Hammer] 4:31–32; Hosea 6:3; Philippians 1:9–11"> For the earth bears fruit: first the blade, then the ear, then the full grain in the ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:30; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:40–43"> But when the fruit is ripe, immediately he puts in the sickle, because the harvest has come.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:31–32; Luke [White-light] 13:18–21; Lamentations 2:13"> He said, “How will we liken God’s Kingdom? Or with what parable will we illustrate it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:20; Luke [White-light] 13:18–19; Daniel 2:44–45"> It’s like a grain of mustard seed, which, when it is sown in the earth, though it is less than all the seeds that are on the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 4:20–22; Daniel 4:10–14; Ezekiel 31:3–10"> yet when it is sown, grows up, and becomes greater than all the herbs, and puts out great branches, so that the birds of the sky can lodge under its shadow.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 13:34–35; John [Yah is gracious] 16:12; Hebrews 5:11–14"> With many such parables he spoke the word to them, as they were able to hear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John [Yah is gracious] 16:25; Mark [Hammer] 4:10; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 15:15–20"> Without a parable he didn’t speak to them; but privately to his own disciples he explained everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 8:18; Luke [White-light] 8:22; Luke [White-light] 8:25"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="(35-41) MP: The storm of sea and waves obey Yahweh to rage and to calm. God hears those who call upon him in their distress to save them. (Ps 107:25-30)">b</a></sup>On that day, when evening had come, he said to them, “Let’s go over to the other side.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark [Hammer] 3:9; Mark [Hammer] 4:1; Mark [Hammer] 5:2"> Leaving the multitude, they took him with them, even as he was, in the boat. Other small boats were also with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 8:23–24; Job 1:19; Luke [White-light] 8:22–23"> A big wind storm arose, and the waves beat into the boat, so much that the boat was already filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Hebrews 4:15; Isaiah 54:6–8"> He himself was in the stern, asleep on the cushion, and they woke him up, and told him, “Rabbi ·Teacher·, don’t you care that we are dying?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 107:29; Psalms 89:9; Psalms 65:7"> He awoke, and rebuked the wind, and said to the sea, “Peace! Be still!” The wind ceased, and there was a great calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 8:25; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 8:26; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 14:31"> He said to them, “Why are you so afraid? How is it that you have no trusting faith?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 8:25; Job 38:11; Psalms 89:7"> They were greatly afraid, and said to one another, “Who then is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?”</span>
