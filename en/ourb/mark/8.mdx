For you have in mind not the things of God, but the things of men.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 10:38; Luke [White-light] 14:26–27; John [Yah is gracious] 10:27"> He called the multitude to himself with his disciples, and said to them, “Whoever wants to come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his execution-stake, and follow me.</span>
