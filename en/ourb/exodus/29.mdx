<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 4:3; Malachi 1:13–14; Exodus 12:5"> “This is the thing that you shall do to them to make them holy, to minister to me in the priest’s office: take one young bull and two rams without defect,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 6:15; Leviticus 2:15; Leviticus 7:12"> matzah ·unleavened bread·, unleavened cakes mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil: you shall make them of fine wheat flour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 6:17; Leviticus 8:26; Leviticus 8:2"> You shall put them into one basket, and bring them in the basket, with the bull and the two rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 40:12; Hebrews 10:22; Exodus 26:36"> You shall bring Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; and his sons to the door of the Tent of Meeting, and shall wash them with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 28:2–8; Leviticus 8:7–8"> You shall take the garments, and put on Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; the coat, the robe of the ephod, the ephod, and the breastplate, and clothe him with the skillfully woven band of the ephod;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 8:9; Exodus 28:36–39"> and you shall set the turban on his head, and put the holy crown on the turban.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 35:25; Leviticus 21:10; John [Yah is gracious] 3:34"> Then you shall take the anointing oil, and pour it on his head, and anoint him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 8:13; Exodus 28:40"> You shall bring his sons, and put coats on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 18:7; Leviticus 8:22–28; Deuteronomy 18:5"> You shall clothe them with belts, Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; and his sons, and bind headbands on them: and they shall have the priesthood by a perpetual regulation: and you shall consecrate Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; and his sons.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 1:4; Leviticus 8:14; Exodus 29:15"> “You shall bring the bull before the Tent of Meeting: and Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; and his sons shall lay their hands on the head of the bull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 9:8; Leviticus 9:12; Exodus 29:4"> You shall kill the bull before Adonai, at the door of the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 27:2; Leviticus 9:9; Leviticus 8:15"> You shall take of the blood of the bull, and put it on the horns of the altar with your finger; and you shall pour out all the blood at the base of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 2:16; Leviticus 9:10"> You shall take all the fat that covers the innards, the cover of the liver, the two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, and burn them on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 4:11–12; Leviticus 4:21; Exodus 30:10"> But the meat of the bull, and its skin, and its dung, you shall burn with fire outside of the camp: it is a habitual sin ·miss the mark· offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 29:19; Exodus 29:3; Leviticus 8:18–21"> “You shall also take the one ram; and Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; and his sons shall lay their hands on the head of the ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 29:11–12"> You shall kill the ram, and you shall take its blood, and sprinkle it around on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:14; Leviticus 1:13; Leviticus 1:9"> You shall cut the ram into its pieces, and wash its innards, and its legs, and put them with its pieces, and with its head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 8:21; Philippians 4:18; Genesis 22:7"> You shall burn the whole ram on the altar: it is a burnt offering to Adonai; it is a pleasant aroma,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn=". Quoted in  Phil 4:18">a</a></sup>an offering made by fire to Adonai.<br />(5)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 29:3; Leviticus 8:22–29"> “You shall take the other ram; and Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; and his sons shall lay their hands on the head of the ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 50:5; Hebrews 10:22; Leviticus 14:16"> Then you shall kill the ram, and take some of its blood, and put it on the tip of the right ear of Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;, and on the tip of the right ear of his sons, and on the thumb of their right hand, and on the big toe of their right foot, and sprinkle the blood around on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 9:22; Exodus 29:7; Exodus 29:1"> You shall take of the blood that is on the altar, and of the anointing oil, and sprinkle it on Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;, and on his garments, and on his sons, and on the garments of his sons with him: and he shall be made holy, and his garments, and his sons, and his sons’ garments with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:13; Leviticus 9:21; Leviticus 9:19"> Also you shall take some of the ram’s fat, the fat tail, the fat that covers the innards, the cover of the liver, the two kidneys, the fat that is on them, and the right thigh (for it is a ram of consecration),</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 29:2–3; Leviticus 8:26"> and one loaf of bread, one cake of oiled bread, and one wafer out of the basket of matzah ·unleavened bread· that is before Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30; Leviticus 8:27; Leviticus 9:21"> You shall put all of this in Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;’s hands, and in his sons’ hands, and shall wave them for a wave offering before Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 8:28; Exodus 29:41; Leviticus 3:16"> You shall take them from their hands, and burn them on the altar on the burnt offering, for a pleasant aroma before Adonai: it is an offering made by fire to Adonai.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 8:29; Leviticus 7:31; Leviticus 7:34"> “You shall take the breast of ram of consecration for Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;, and wave it for a wave offering before Adonai: and it shall be your portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 10:15; Numbers 18:11; Deuteronomy 18:3"> You shall sanctify the breast of the wave offering, and the thigh of the wave offering, which is waved, and which is heaved up, of the ram of consecration, even of that which is for Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;, and of that which is for his sons:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 15:19–20; Leviticus 7:11–38; Leviticus 10:14–15"> and it shall be for Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; and his sons as their chok ·portion· forever from the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;; for it is a wave offering: and it shall be a wave offering from the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; of the sacrifices of their peace offerings, even their wave offering to Adonai.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 18:8; Exodus 28:3–4; Exodus 30:30"> “The holy garments of Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; shall be for his sons after him, to be anointed in them, and to be consecrated in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 20:28; Leviticus 13:5; Acts 20:6–7"> Seven days shall the son who is priest in his place put them on, when he comes into the Tent of Meeting to minister in the holy place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 8:31; 1 Samuel 2:13; Ezekiel 46:20–24"> “You shall take the ram of consecration, and boil its meat in a holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 12:4; Exodus 29:23; Exodus 29:2–3"> Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; and his sons shall eat the meat of the ram, and the bread that is in the basket, at the door of the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 18:4; Leviticus 10:13–18; Psalms 22:26"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn=". Ex 29:33 (Ex 29:34) (#1.4):  T. Aaron [Light-bringer] and his sons, who are Cohenim ·Priests·, are to eat the flesh of the atonement offering / R. Aaron and his sons, who are Cohenim ·Priests·, are to eat the flesh of the sin offering in the Temple">b</a></sup>They shall eat those things with which atonement was made, to consecrate and sanctify them:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn=". Ex 29:33 (#1.5):  The non-priest shall not eat the flesh of the most holy sacrifices">c</a></sup>but a stranger shall not eat of it, because they are holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 12:10; Leviticus 8:32; Exodus 29:22"> If anything of the meat of the consecration, or of the bread, remains to the morning, then you shall burn the remainder with fire: it shall not be eaten, because it is holy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 29:30; Leviticus 14:8–11; Leviticus 8:4–36"> “You shall do so to Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;, and to his sons, according to all that I have enjoined you. You shall consecrate them seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 10:11; Hebrews 9:22–23; Exodus 30:28–29"> Every day you shall offer the bull of habitual sin ·miss the mark· offering for atonement: and you shall cleanse sin ·err (the standard goal)· from the altar, when you make atonement for it; and you shall anoint it, to sanctify it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 40:10; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 23:19; Exodus 30:28–29"> Seven days you shall make atonement for the altar, and sanctify it: and the altar shall be most holy; whatever touches the altar shall be holy.<br />(6)</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4; 1 Chronicles 16:40; Numbers 28:3–8"> “Now this is that which you shall offer on the altar: two lambs a year old day by day continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:13–15; 2 Kings 16:15; Psalms 5:3"> The one lamb you shall offer in the morning; and the other lamb you shall offer at evening:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 2:17; Ezekiel 46:14; Joel 2:14"> and with the one lamb a tenth part of an ephah &#91;an omer 2.3 q; 2.2 L&#93; of fine flour mixed with the fourth part of a hin &#91;2.6 qt; 1.2 L&#93; of beaten oil, and the fourth part of a hin &#91;2.6 qt; 1.2 L&#93; of wine for a drink offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:13–15; 1 Kings 18:29; Psalms 141:2"> The other lamb you shall offer at evening, and shall do to it according to the meal offering of the morning, and according to its drink offering, for a pleasant aroma, an offering made by fire to Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 25:22"> It shall be a continual burnt offering throughout all your generations at the door of the Tent of Meeting before Adonai, where I will meet with you, to speak there to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Kings 8:11; 2 Chronicles 7:1–3; Malachi 3:1"> There I will meet with the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;; and the place shall be sanctified by my glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Leviticus 21:15; Leviticus 22:9; Leviticus 22:16"> I will sanctify the Tent of Meeting and the altar: Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; also and his sons I will sanctify, to minister to me in the priest’s office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 26:12; 2 Corinthians 6:16; Exodus 25:8"> I will dwell among the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, and will be their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 20:5"> They shall know that I am Adonai their God, who brought them out of the land of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;, that I might dwell among them: I am Adonai their God.</span>
