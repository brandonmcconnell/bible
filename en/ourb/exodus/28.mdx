<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 1:1–4; Genesis 43:33"> six of their names on the one stone, and the names of the six that remain on the other stone, in the order of their birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:19; Revelation 7:2; Jeremiah 22:24"> With the work of an engraver in stone, like the engravings of a signet, you shall engrave the two stones, according to the names of the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;: you shall make them to be enclosed in settings of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 28:29; Joshua 4:7; Exodus 39:6–7"> You shall put the two stones on the shoulder straps of the ephod, to be stones of memorial for the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;: and Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; shall bear their names before Adonai on his two shoulders for a memorial. (2)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> You shall make settings of gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 39:15; Exodus 39:17–18; 1 Kings 7:17"> and two chains of pure gold; you shall make them like cords of braided work: and you shall put the braided chains on the settings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> It shall be square and folded double; a span &#91;9 in; 22.86 cm&#93; shall be its length, and a span &#91;9 in; 22.86 cm&#93; its width.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:16; Job 28:6; Song of Solomon 5:14"> and the second row a turquoise, a sapphire, and an emerald;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 54:12; Exodus 39:12"> and the third row a jacinth, an agate, and an amethyst;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:16; Revelation 4:3; Daniel 10:6"> and the fourth row a chrysolite, an onyx, and a jasper: they shall be enclosed in gold in their settings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:12; Revelation 7:4–8; 1 Kings 18:31"> The stones shall be according to the names of the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, twelve, according to their names; like the engravings of a signet, everyone according to his name, they shall be for the twelve tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> You shall make on the breastplate chains like cords, of braided work of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 25:11–15"> You shall make on the breastplate two rings of gold, and shall put the two rings on the two ends of the breastplate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> You shall put the two braided chains of gold in the two rings at the ends of the breastplate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 39:15; Exodus 39:4; Exodus 28:14"> The other two ends of the two braided chains you shall put on the two settings, and put them on the shoulder straps of the ephod in its forepart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> You shall make two rings of gold, and you shall put them on the two ends of the breastplate, on its edge, which is toward the side of the ephod inward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> You shall make two rings of gold, and shall put them on the two shoulder straps of the ephod underneath, in its forepart, close by its coupling, above the skillfully woven band of the ephod.</span>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 28:12; Exodus 28:30; Jeremiah 30:21"> Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; shall bear the names of the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; in the breastplate of mishpat ·judgment· on his heart, when he goes in to the holy place, for a memorial before Adonai continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:65; Ezra 2:63; Numbers 27:21"> You shall put in the breastplate of mishpat ·judgment· the 'Urim ·Lights· and the Thummim ·Perfections·; and they shall be on Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;’s heart, when he goes in before Adonai: and Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; shall bear the judgment of the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; on his heart before Adonai continually.<br />(3)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 28:4; Exodus 39:22–26"> “You shall make the robe of the ephod all of blue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:16; John [Yah is gracious] 19:23–24; Exodus 39:28"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn=". Ex 28:32 (#2b.101):  T. <span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12; Leviticus 16:2"> It shall be on Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; to minister: and its sound shall be sh'ma ·heard obeyed· when he goes in to the holy place before Adonai, and when he comes out, that he not die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Zechariah 14:20; Leviticus 8:9; Exodus 39:30"> “You shall make a plate of pure gold, and engrave on it, like the engravings of a signet, ‘Holy To Yahweh.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 29:6; Exodus 28:4; Zechariah 3:5"> You shall put it on a lace of blue, and it shall be on the sash; on the front of the sash it shall be.</span>

<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 45:14"> You shall weave the coat in checker work of fine linen, and you shall make a turban of fine linen, and you shall make a sash, the work of the embroiderer.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 29:7; Leviticus 8:1–36; Exodus 40:15"> You shall put them on Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; your brother, and on his sons with him, and shall anoint them, and consecrate them, and sanctify them, that they may minister to me in the priest’s office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:18; Leviticus 6:10; Leviticus 16:4"> You shall make them linen breeches to cover the flesh of their nakedness; from the waist even to the thighs they shall reach:</span>
