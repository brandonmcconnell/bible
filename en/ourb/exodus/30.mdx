<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:20; Numbers 1:18; Numbers 1:3"> Everyone who passes over to those who are counted, from twenty years old and upward, shall give the offering to Adonai.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:32–33; Numbers 16:40; Exodus 38:25–31"> You shall take the atonement money from the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, and shall appoint it for the service of the Tent of Meeting; that it may be a memorial for the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; before Adonai, to make atonement for your souls.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Adonai spoke to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 40:7; Exodus 38:8; 1 Kings 7:23"> “You shall also make a basin of bronze, and its base of bronze, in which to wash. You shall put it between the Tent of Meeting and the altar, and you shall put water in it.</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 28:43; Exodus 27:21"> So they shall wash their hands and their feet, that they not die: and it shall be a statute forever to them, even to him and to his descendants throughout their generations.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 45:8; Exodus 29:40; Numbers 3:47"> and of cassia cinnamon five hundred shekels &#91;12.5 lb; 5.67 kg&#93;, after the shekel of the sanctuary (the shekel is 0.4 oz; 11.34 g); and a hin &#91;0.98 gal; 3.7 L&#93; of olive oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; 1 Chronicles 9:30; Exodus 40:9"> You shall make it a holy anointing oil, a perfume compounded after the art of the perfumer: it shall be a holy anointing oil.</span>
