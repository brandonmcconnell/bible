<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 145:4–12; Nehemiah 9:9–15; Psalms 107:2"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; told his father-in-law all that Adonai had done to Pharaoh and to the Egyptians &#91;people from Abode of slavery&#93; for Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;’s sake, all the hardships that had come on them on the way, and how Adonai delivered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Romans 12:15; Isaiah 66:10"> Jethro &#91;Abundance&#93; rejoiced for all the goodness which Adonai had done to Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, in that he had delivered them out of the hand of the Egyptians &#91;people from Abode of slavery&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 14:20; 2 Samuel 18:28; Luke [White-light] 1:68"> Jethro &#91;Abundance&#93; said, “Blessed be Adonai, who has delivered you out of the hand of the Egyptians &#91;people from Abode of slavery&#93;, and out of the hand of Pharaoh; who has delivered the people from under the hand of the Egyptians &#91;people from Abode of slavery&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 95:3; Exodus 15:11; 2 Chronicles 2:5"> Now I know that Adonai is greater than all deities because of the thing in which they dealt arrogantly against them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Deuteronomy 12:7; Exodus 24:11"> Jethro &#91;Abundance&#93;, Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;’ father-in-law, took a burnt offering and sacrifices for God. <span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;’ father-in-law saw all that he did to the people, he said, “What is this thing that you do for the people? Why do you sit alone, and all the people stand around you from morning to evening?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 15:34; Numbers 27:5; Numbers 9:8"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; said to his father-in-law, “Because the people come to me to inquire of God.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;’ father-in-law said to him, “The thing that you do is not good.</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 27:5; Deuteronomy 5:5; Exodus 20:19"> Sh'ma ·Hear obey· now to my voice. I will give you counsel, and God be with you. You represent the people before God, and bring the causes to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:1; Psalms 143:8; Deuteronomy 1:18"> You shall teach them the statutes and the torot ·teachings·, and shall show them the way in which they must walk, and the work that they must do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 6:3; Deuteronomy 1:13–17; 2 Chronicles 19:5–10"> Moreover you shall provide out of all the people able men which fear God: men of truth, hating unjust gain; and place such over them, to be rulers of thousands, rulers of hundreds, rulers of fifties, and rulers of tens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Numbers 27:2; Numbers 15:33"> Let them judge the people at all times. <span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:6–7; Exodus 18:18; 1 Samuel 8:22"> If you will do this thing, and God enjoins you to do so, then you will be able to endure, and all these people also will go to their place in peace.”<br />(3)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra 10:2; Exodus 18:19; Exodus 18:2–5"> So Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; sh'ma ·heard obeyed· the voice of his father-in-law, and did all that he had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:15; Exodus 18:21; Acts 6:5"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; chose able men out of all Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, and made them heads over the people, rulers of thousands, rulers of hundreds, rulers of fifties, and rulers of tens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 18:22; Exodus 18:14–15; Deuteronomy 17:8"> They judged the people at all times. They brought the hard causes to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, but every small matter they judged themselves.</span>
