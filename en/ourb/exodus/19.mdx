<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; Genesis 35:2; Leviticus 11:44–45"> Adonai said to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, “Go to the people, and sanctify them today and tomorrow, and let them wash their garments,</span>
