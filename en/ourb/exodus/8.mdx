<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Daniel 4:37; Acts 12:22–23"> and the frogs shall come up both on you, and on your people, and on all your servants.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 7:19"> Adonai said to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, “Tell Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;, ‘Stretch out your hand with your rod over the rivers, over the streams, and over the pools, and cause frogs to come up on the land of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;.’”</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 7:11; Exodus 7:22; Deuteronomy 13:1–3"> The magicians did the same thing with their enchantments, and brought up frogs on the land of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 10:17; Exodus 9:28; Exodus 8:25–28"> Then Pharaoh called for Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; and Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;, and said, “Entreat Adonai, that he take away the frogs from me, and from my people; and I will let the people go, that they may sacrifice to Adonai.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 18:25; Isaiah 10:15; Judges 7:2"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; said to Pharaoh, “I give you the honor of setting the time that I should pray for you, and for your servants, and for your people, that the frogs be destroyed from you and your houses, and remain in the river only.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 46:9; Psalms 86:8; 1 Chronicles 17:20"> He said, “Tomorrow.” He said, “Be it according to your word, that you may know that there is no one like Yahweh Eloheikhem &#91;Yahweh our God&#93;. (5)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 8:3; Exodus 8:9"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew ch 8 v. 7">a</a></sup>The frogs shall depart from you, and from your houses, and from your servants, and from your people. They shall remain in the river only.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 5:16–18; Exodus 8:30; Exodus 10:18"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; and Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; went out from Pharaoh, and Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; cried to Adonai concerning the frogs which he had brought on Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:10–12"> Adonai did according to the word of Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, and the frogs died out of the houses, out of the courts, and out of the fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 8:24; Exodus 7:21; Joel 2:20"> They gathered them together in heaps, and the land stank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; Ecclesiastes 8:11; Hebrews 3:8"> But when Pharaoh saw that there was a respite, he hardened his heart, and didn’t sh'ma ·hear obey· them, as Adonai had spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 8:5; Exodus 8:17"> Adonai said to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, “Tell Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;, ‘Stretch out your rod, and strike the dust of the earth, that it may become lice throughout all the land of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 105:31; Isaiah 23:9; Acts 12:23"> They did so; and Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; stretched out his hand with his rod, and struck the dust of the earth, and there were lice on man, and on animal; all the dust of the earth became lice throughout all the land of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 7:11; Exodus 9:11; Daniel 5:8"> The magicians tried with their enchantments to produce lice, but they couldn’t. There were lice on man, and on animal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke [White-light] 11:20; Psalms 8:3; Exodus 7:5"> Then the magicians said to Pharaoh, “This is God’s finger:”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn=". Quoted in  Luke 11:20">b</a></sup>and Pharaoh’s heart was hardened, and he didn’t sh'ma ·hear obey· them; as Adonai had spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 7:15; Exodus 9:13; Exodus 8:1"> Adonai said to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, “Rise up early in the morning, and stand before Pharaoh; behold, he comes out to the water; and tell him, ‘This is what Adonai says, “Let my people go, that they may abad ·serve· me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 78:45; Isaiah 7:18; Psalms 105:31"> Else, if you will not let my people go, behold, I will send swarms of flies on you, and on your servants, and on your people, and into your houses: and the houses of the Egyptians &#91;people from Abode of slavery&#93; shall be full of swarms of flies, and also the ground whereon they are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 9:4; Malachi 3:18; Exodus 10:23"> I will set apart in that day the land of Goshen &#91;Drawing near&#93;, in which my people dwell, that no swarms of flies shall be there; to the end you may know that I am Adonai on the earth. (6)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew ch 8 v. 19">c</a></sup>I will put a division between my people and your people: by tomorrow shall this sign be.”’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 105:31; Psalms 78:45; Exodus 8:21"> Adonai did so; and there came grievous swarms of flies into the house of Pharaoh, and into his servants’ houses: and in all the land of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93; the land was corrupted by reason of the swarms of flies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Exodus 9:27; Exodus 10:16"> Pharaoh called for Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; and for Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;, and said, “Go, sacrifice to your God in the land!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 43:32; Genesis 46:34; Deuteronomy 7:25–26"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; said, “It is not appropriate to do so; for we shall sacrifice the abomination of the Egyptians &#91;people from Abode of slavery&#93; to Yahweh Eloheikhem &#91;Yahweh our God&#93;. Behold, shall we sacrifice the abomination of the Egyptians &#91;people from Abode of slavery&#93; before their eyes, and won’t they stone us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Exodus 3:12; Exodus 34:11"> We will go three days’ journey into the wilderness, and sacrifice to Yahweh Eloheikhem &#91;Yahweh our God&#93;, as he has ordered us to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Exodus 9:28; 1 Kings 13:6"> Pharaoh said, “I will let you go, that you may sacrifice to Adonai your God in the wilderness, only you shall not go very far away. Pray for me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 8:15; Jeremiah 42:20–21; Exodus 8:8"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; said, “Behold, I go out from you, and I will pray to Adonai that the swarms of flies may depart from Pharaoh, from his servants, and from his people, tomorrow; only don’t let Pharaoh deal deceitfully any more in not letting the people go to sacrifice to Adonai.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 8:12; Exodus 9:33; James 5:16"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; went out from Pharaoh, and prayed to Adonai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Adonai did according to the word of Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, and he removed the swarms of flies from Pharaoh, from his servants, and from his people. There remained not one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 8:15; Exodus 4:21; Romans 2:5"> Pharaoh hardened his heart this time also, and he didn’t let the people go.</span>
