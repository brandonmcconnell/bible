<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 28:3; Exodus 35:30–35; Exodus 31:1–6"> “Bezalel and Oholiab shall work with every wise-hearted man, in whom Adonai has put wisdom and understanding to know how to do all the work for the service of the sanctuary, according to all that Adonai has enjoined.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 4:17; Hebrews 5:4; Exodus 31:6"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; called Bezalel and Oholiab, and every wise-hearted man, in whose heart Adonai had put wisdom, even everyone whose heart stirred him up to come to the work to do it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:27; Exodus 35:5–21"> and they received from Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; all the offering which the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; had brought for the work of the service of the sanctuary, with which to make it. They brought yet to him free will offerings every morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:10; Luke [White-light] 12:42; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 24:45"> All the wise men, who performed all the work of the sanctuary, each came from his work which they did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:6–10; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 2 Chronicles 24:14"> They spoke to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, saying, “The people bring much more than enough for the service of the work which Adonai enjoined to make.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; enjoied the words, and they caused it to be proclaimed throughout the camp, saying, “Let neither man nor woman make anything else for the offering for the sanctuary.” So the people were restrained from bringing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:10"> For the stuff they had was sufficient for all the work to make it, and too much.<br />(LY:4)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 6:23; Exodus 26:1–37; Ezekiel 1:5–28"> All the wise-hearted men among those who did the work made the tabernacle with ten curtains; of fine twined linen, blue, purple, and scarlet, with cherubim, the work of the skillful workman, they made them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The length of each curtain was twenty-eight cubits, and the width of each curtain four cubits &#91;6 ft; 18.29 m&#93;. All the curtains had one measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; Exodus 26:3; Psalms 122:3"> He coupled five curtains to one another, and the other five curtains he coupled to one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:4"> He made loops of blue on the edge of the one curtain from the edge in the coupling. Likewise he made in the edge of the curtain that was outermost in the second coupling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 26:5; Exodus 26:10"> He made fifty loops in the one curtain, and he made fifty loops in the edge of the curtain that was in the second coupling. The loops were opposite to one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 12:20"> He made fifty clasps of gold, and coupled the curtains to one another with the clasps: so the tabernacle was a unit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–13"> He made curtains of goats’ hair for a covering over the tabernacle. He made them eleven curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The length of each curtain was thirty cubits &#91;45 ft; 137.16 m&#93;, and four cubits &#91;6 ft; 18.29 m&#93; the width of each curtain. The eleven curtains had one measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He coupled five curtains by themselves, and six curtains by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He made fifty loops on the edge of the curtain that was outermost in the coupling, and he made fifty loops on the edge of the curtain which was outermost in the second coupling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He made fifty clasps of bronze to couple the tent together, that it might be a unit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:14"> He made a covering for the tent of rams’ skins dyed red, and a covering of sea cow hides above.<br />(LY:5)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 25:5; Numbers 25:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> He made the boards for the tabernacle of acacia wood, standing up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Ten cubits &#91;15 ft; 45.72 m&#93; was the length of a board, and a cubit and a half &#91;27 in; 68.58 cm&#93; the width of each board.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Each board had two tenons, joined to one another. He made all the boards of the tabernacle this way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> He made the boards for the tabernacle: twenty boards for the south side southward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He made forty sockets of silver under the twenty boards; two sockets under one board for its two tenons, and two sockets under another board for its two tenons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For the second side of the tabernacle, on the north side, he made twenty boards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and their forty sockets of silver; two sockets under one board, and two sockets under another board.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:22; Exodus 26:27"> For the far part of the tabernacle westward he made six boards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> He made two boards for the corners of the tabernacle in the far part.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:32; Ephesians 3:18–19; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> They were double beneath, and in the same way they were all the way to its top to one ring. He did this to both of them in the two corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:25"> There were eight boards, and their sockets of silver, sixteen sockets; under every board two sockets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 26:26–29; Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:5"> He made bars of acacia wood; five for the boards of the one side of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 26:26"> and five bars for the boards of the other side of the tabernacle, and five bars for the boards of the tabernacle for the hinder part westward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> He made the middle bar to pass through in the middle of the boards from the one end to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> He overlaid the boards with gold, and made their rings of gold for places for the bars, and overlaid the bars with gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew [Gift of Yah] 27:51; Hebrews 10:20; Exodus 40:21"> He made the veil of blue, purple, scarlet, and fine twined linen: with cherubim. He made it the work of a skillful workman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18"> He made four pillars of acacia for it, and overlaid them with gold. Their hooks were of gold. He cast four sockets of silver for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 26:36–37; Exodus 40:28"> He made a screen for the door of the tent, of blue, purple, scarlet, and fine twined linen, the work of an embroiderer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 27:10"> and the five pillars of it with their hooks. He overlaid their capitals and their fillets with gold, and their five sockets were of bronze.</span>
</div>
