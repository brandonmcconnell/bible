<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:13–21; Genesis 50:24; Genesis 46:4"> and I have said, I will bring you up out of the affliction of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93; to the land of the Canaanite &#91;Descendant of Humbled&#93;, the Hittite &#91;Descendant of Trembling fear&#93;, the Amorite &#91;Descendants of Talkers&#93;, the Perizzite &#91;Descendant of Belonging to village&#93;, the Hivite &#91;Wicked&#93;, and the Jebusite &#91;Descendants of Thresher&#93;, to a land flowing with milk and honey.”’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 9:1; Exodus 7:16; Exodus 10:24–26"> They will sh'ma ·hear obey· your voice, and you shall come, you and the elders of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, to the king of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;, and you shall tell him, ‘Adonai, the God of the Hebrews &#91;Immigrants&#93;, has met with us.
