<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3; Psalms 78:24–25; Deuteronomy 8:2"> Then Adonai said to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, “Behold, I will rain bread from the sky for you, and the people shall go out and gather a day’s portion every day, that I may test them, whether they will walk in my Torah ·Teaching·, or not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 16:22–23; Exodus 35:2–3; Leviticus 25:21–22"> It shall come to pass on the sixth day, that they shall prepare that which they bring in, and it shall be twice as much as they gather daily.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Numbers 16:28; Exodus 16:12–13"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; and Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; said to all the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, “At evening, then you shall know that Adonai has brought you out from the land of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 40:5; Numbers 16:11; John [Yah is gracious] 11:40"> and in the morning, then you shall see the kavod Yahweh ·weighty glory of He sustains breathing·; because he sh'ma ·hears obeys· your murmurings against Adonai. Who are we, that you murmur against us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:2; 1 Samuel 8:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; said, “Now Adonai shall give you meat to eat in the evening, and in the morning bread to satisfy you; because Adonai sh'ma ·hears obeys· your murmurings which you murmur against him. And who are we? Your murmurings are not against us, but against Adonai.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 16:16; Exodus 16:2; Exodus 16:8"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; said to Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;, “Tell all the congregation of the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, ‘Come near before Adonai, for he has heard your murmurings.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 16:7; Numbers 16:19; Numbers 16:42"> As Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; spoke to the whole congregation of the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, they looked toward the wilderness, and behold, the kavod Yahweh ·weighty glory of He sustains breathing· appeared in the cloud. (A:6, S:5)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Adonai spoke to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Ezekiel 39:22; Exodus 6:7"> “I have sh'ma ·heard obeyed· the murmurings of the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;. Speak to them, saying, ‘At evening you shall eat meat, and in the morning you shall be filled with bread: and you shall know that I am Adonai your God.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 11:9; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:27–28"> In the evening, quail came up and covered the camp; and in the morning the dew lay around the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 11:7–9; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:24"> When the dew that lay had gone, behold, on the surface of the wilderness was a small round thing, small as the frost on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:4; John [Yah is gracious] 6:58; Exodus 16:31"> When the children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; saw it, they asked one another, “Man hu? ·What is it·?” For they didn’t know what it was. Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; answered them, “It is the bread which Adonai has given you to eat.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn=". Quoted in  John 6:31, 6:49">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 16:36; Exodus 16:18; Exodus 16:32–33"> This is the thing which Adonai has enjoined: “Gather of it everyone according to his eating; an omer ·sheath of grain· &#91;2.6 qt; 1.2 L&#93; a head, according to the number of your persons, you shall take it, every man for those who are in his tent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; did so, and gathered some more, some less.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> When they measured it with an omer ·sheath of grain· &#91;2.6 qt; 1.2 L&#93;, he who gathered much had nothing extra, and he who gathered little had nothing lacking.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn=". Quoted in  2 Cor 8:15">b</a></sup>They gathered every man according to his eating.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 12:10; Exodus 23:18; Matthew [Gift of Yah] 6:34"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; said to them, “Let no one leave of it until the morning.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark [Hammer] 10:14; Numbers 16:15; Ephesians 4:26"> Notwithstanding they didn’t sh'ma ·hear obey· Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, but some of them left of it until the morning, and it bred worms, and became foul: and Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; was angry with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–11; Ecclesiastes 12:1; 2 Corinthians 6:2"> They gathered it morning by morning, everyone according to his eating. When the sun grew hot, it melted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 16:5; Leviticus 25:22; Exodus 16:16"> On the sixth day, they gathered twice as much bread, two omers for each one, and all the rulers of the congregation came and told Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 23:3; Exodus 31:15; Exodus 20:8–11"> He said to them, “This is that which Adonai has spoken, ‘Tomorrow is a solemn rest, a holy Sabbath ·Intermission· to Adonai. Bake that which you want to bake, and boil that which you want to boil; and all that remains over lay up for yourselves to be kept until the morning.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:20; Exodus 16:33"> They laid it up until the morning, as Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; enjoined, and it didn’t become foul, and there were no worms in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 16:23; Exodus 16:29; Nehemiah 9:14"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; said, “Eat that today, for today is a Shabath ·Intermission· to Adonai. Today you shall not find it in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:13; Exodus 20:9–11; Ezekiel 46:1"> Six days you shall gather it, but on the seventh day is the Shabath ·Intermission·. In it there shall be none.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 20:4"> On the seventh day, some of the people went out to gather, and they found none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 78:10; Psalms 106:13; Numbers 14:11"> Adonai said to Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, “How long do you refuse to keep my mitzvot ·instructions· and my torot ·teachings·?</span>

Let no one go out of his place on the seventh day.” (S:6)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:12–14; Leviticus 23:3; Hebrews 4:9"> So the people rested on the seventh day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:3; Numbers 11:6–9; Exodus 16:15"> The house of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; called its name Manna ·What is it·, and it was like coriander seed, white; and its taste was like wafers with honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 105:5; Luke [White-light] 22:19; Psalms 111:4–5"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; said, “This is the thing which Adonai has enjoined, ‘Let an omer-full of it be kept throughout all your generations, that they may see the bread with which I fed you in the wilderness, when I brought you out of the land of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4"> Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93; said to Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93;, “Take a pot, and put an omer-full of manna ·what is it·<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn=". Quoted in  Heb 9:4">d</a></sup>in it, and lay it up before Adonai, to be kept throughout all your generations.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 27:21; Exodus 25:21; Exodus 25:16"> As Adonai enjoined Moses &#91;Drawn out&#93;, so Aaron &#91;Light-bringer&#93; laid it up before the Testimony, to be kept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:20–21; Joshua 5:12; Deuteronomy 8:2–3"> The children of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; ate the manna ·what is it· forty years,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn=".
