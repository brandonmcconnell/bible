<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the king of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93; gathered together great forces, as the sand which is by the sea shore, and many ships, and sought to make himself master of Alexander’s kingdom by deceit, and to add it to his own kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And he went forth into Syria &#91;Elevated&#93; with words of peace, and they of the cities opened to him, and met him; For king Alexander’s commandment was that they should meet him, because he was his father in law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Now as he entered into the cities of Ptolemais, he set his forces for a garrison in each city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But when he came near to Azotus, they showed him the temple of Dagon burned with fire, and Azotus and the suburbs thereof pulled down, and the bodies cast abroad, and them that had been burned, whom he burned in the war, for they had made heaps of them in his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And they told the king what things Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; had done, that they might cast blame on him: and the king held his peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; met the king with pomp at Joppa, and they saluted one another, and they slept there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; went with the king as far as the river that is called Eleutherus, and returned to Jerusalem &#91;City of peace&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> But king Ptolemy became master of the cities upon the sea coast, to Selucia which is by the sea, and he devised evil devices concerning Alexander.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And he sent ambassadors to king Demetrius, saying, Come, let us make a covenant with one another, and I will give you my daughter whom Alexander has, and you shall reign over your father’s kingdom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> for I have made teshuvah ·complete repentance· that I gave my daughter to him, for he sought to kill me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he cast blame on him, because he coveted his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And taking his daughter from him, he gave her to Demetrius, and was estranged from Alexander, and their enmity was openly seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Ptolemy entered into Antioch, and put on himself the diadem ·crown· of Asia; and he put two diadems ·crowns· upon his head, the diadem ·crown· of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93; and that of Asia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But king Alexander was in Cilicia at that season, because they of those parts were in revolt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Alexander heard of it, and he came against him in war: and Ptolemy led forth his host, and met him with a strong force, and put him to flight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Alexander fled into Arabia, that he might be sheltered there; but king Ptolemy was exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Zabdiel the Arabian took off Alexander’s head, and sent it to Ptolemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And king Ptolemy died the third day after, and they that were in his strongholds were slain by them that were in the strongholds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Demetrius reigned in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="circa B.C.E 146">a</a></sup>the one hundred sixty seventh year.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> In those days Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; gathered together them of Judea &#91;Praise&#93;, to take the fortress that was at Jerusalem &#91;City of peace&#93;: and he made many engines of war against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And certain that hated their own nation, men that transgressed Torah ·Teaching·, went to the king, and reported to him that Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; was besieging the fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And he heard, and was angered; but when he heard it, he set forth immediately, and came to Ptolemais, and wrote to Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93;, that he should not besiege it, and that he should meet him and speak with him at Ptolemais with all speed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> But when Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; heard this, he commanded to besiege it still: and he chose certain of the elders of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; and of the priests, and put himself in peril,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and taking silver and gold and raiment and various presents besides, went to Ptolemais to the king. And he found favor in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And certain men departing from Torah ·Teaching· of them that were of the nation made complaints against him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and the king did to him even as his predecessors had done to him, and exalted him in the sight of all his Friends,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and confirmed to him the role of Cohen Gadol ·High Priest·, and all the other honors that he had before, and gave him preeminence among his Chief Friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; requested of the king, that he would make Judea &#91;Praise&#93; free from tribute, and the three provinces, and the country of Samaria; and promised him three hundred talents &#91;180,000 denarii; 155,250 lb; 7,020 kg&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And the king consented, and wrote letters to Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; concerning all these things after this manner:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> King Demetrius to his brother Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93;, and to the nation of the Jews &#91;Praisers&#93;, greeting:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> The copy of the letter which we wrote to Lasthenes our kinsman concerning you, we have written also to you, that you may see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> King Demetrius to Lasthenes his father, greeting:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> We have determined to do good to the nation of the Jews &#91;Praisers&#93;, who are our friends, and observe what is just toward us, because of their good will toward us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> We have confirmed therefore to them the borders of Judea &#91;Praise&#93;, and also the three governments of Aphaerema and Lydda and Ramathaim (these were added to Judea &#91;Praise&#93; from the country of Samaria), and all things appertaining to them, for all such as do sacrifice in Jerusalem &#91;City of peace&#93;, instead of the king’s dues which the king received of them yearly aforetime from the produce of the earth and the fruits of trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And as for the other things that pertain to us from henceforth, of the tenths and the tolls that pertain to us, and the salt pits, and the crowns that pertain to us, all these we will bestow upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And not one of these things shall be annulled from this time forth and forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Now therefore be careful to make a copy of these things, and let it be given to Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93;, and let it be set up on the holy mount in a meet and conspicuous place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And king Demetrius saw that the land was quiet before him, and that no resistance was made to him, and he sent away all his forces, each man to his own place, except the foreign forces, which he had raised from the isles of the Gentiles: and all the forces of his fathers hated him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Now Tryphon was of those who aforetime had been of Alexander’s part, and he saw that all the forces murmured against Demetrius, and he went to Imalcue the Arabian, who was nourishing up Antiochus the young child of Alexander,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and pressed sore upon him that he should deliver him to him, that he might reign in his father’s stead: and he told him all that Demetrius had done, and the hatred wherewith his forces hated him; and he abode there many days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; sent to king Demetrius, that he should cast out of Jerusalem &#91;City of peace&#93; them of the fortress, and them that were in the strongholds; for they fought against Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And Demetrius sent to Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93;, saying, I will not only do this for you and your nation, but I will greatly honor you and your nation, if I find fair occasion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Now therefore you shall do well, if you send me men who shall fight for me; for all my forces are revolted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; sent him three thousand valiant men to Antioch: and they came to the king, and the king was glad at their coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And they of the city gathered themselves together into the midst of the city, to the number of one hundred and twenty thousand men, and they were minded to kill the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And the king fled into the court of the palace, and they of the city seized the passages of the city, and began to fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> And the king called the Jews &#91;Praisers&#93; to help him, and they were gathered together to him all at once, and they dispersed themselves in the city, and killed that day to the number of one hundred thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And they set the city on fire, and got many spoils that day, and saved the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> And they of the city saw that the Jews &#91;Praisers&#93; had made themselves masters of the city as they would, and they waxed faint in their hearts, and they cried out to the king with supplication, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> Give us your right hand, and let the Jews &#91;Praisers&#93; cease from fighting against us and the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And they cast away their arms, and made peace; and the Jews &#91;Praisers&#93; were glorified in the sight of the king, and before all that were in his kingdom; and they returned to Jerusalem &#91;City of peace&#93;, having many spoils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And king Demetrius sat on the throne of his kingdom, and the land was quiet before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> And he lied in all that he spoke, and estranged himself from Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93;, and recompensed him not according to the benefits with which he had recompensed him, and afflicted him exceedingly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> Now after this Tryphon returned, and with him the young child Antiochus; and he reigned, and put on a diadem ·crown·.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> And there were gathered to him all the forces which Demetrius had sent away with disgrace, and they fought against him, and he fled and was put to the rout.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And Tryphon took the elephants, and became master of Antioch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> And the young Antiochus wrote to Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93;, saying, I confirm to you the role of Cohen Gadol ·High Priest·, and appoint you over the four governments, and to be one of the king’s Friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> And he sent to him golden utensils and furniture for the table of the show bread, and gave him leave to drink in golden utensils, and to be clothed in purple, and to have a golden buckle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> And his brother Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; he made captain from the Ladder of Tyre to the borders of Egypt &#91;Abode of slavery&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> And Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; went forth, and took his journey beyond the river and through the cities; and all the forces of Syria &#91;Elevated&#93; gathered themselves to him for to be his confederates. And he came to Ascalon, and they of the city met him honorably.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> And he departed thence to Gaza, and they of Gaza shut him out; and he laid siege to it, and burned the suburbs thereof with fire, and spoiled them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> And they of Gaza made request to Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93;, and he gave them his right hand, and took the sons of their princes for hostages, and sent them away to Jerusalem &#91;City of peace&#93;; and he passed through the country as far as Damascus &#91;Bucket of blood&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> And Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; heard that Demetrius’ princes were come to Kedesh, which is in Galilee &#91;District, Circuit&#93;, with a great host, purposing to remove him from his office;</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64"> and he went to meet them, but Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; his brother he left in the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65"> And Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; encamped against Bethsura, and fought against it many days, and shut it up:</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66"> and they made request to him that he would give them his right hand, and he gave it to them; and he put them out from thence, and took possession of the city, and set a garrison over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67"> And Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; and his army encamped at the water of Gennesareth, and early in the morning they got them to the plain of Hazor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68"> And, behold, an army of strangers met him in the plain, and they laid an ambush for him in the mountains, but themselves met him face to face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69"> But they that lay in ambush rose out of their places, and joined battle; and all they that were of Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93;’s side fled:</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70"> not one of them was left, except Mattathias &#91;Gift of Yah&#93; the son of Absalom, and Judas &#91;Praised&#93; the son of Chalphi, captains of the forces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71"> And Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; tore his clothes, and put earth upon his head, and prayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72"> And he turned again to them in battle, and put them to the rout, and they fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73"> And they of his side that fled saw it, and returned to him, and pursued with him to Kedesh to their camp, and they encamped there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74"> And there fell of the strangers on that day about three thousand men: and Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; returned to Jerusalem &#91;City of peace&#93;.</span>
</div>
