<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And king Antiochus was journeying through the upper countries; and he heard say, that in Elymais in Persia &#91;Descendants of Loving-kindness&#93; there was a city renowned for riches, for silver and gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and that the temple which was in it was rich exceedingly, and that therein were golden shields, and breastplates, and arms, which Alexander, son of Philip &#91;Loves horses&#93;, the Macedonian king, who reigned first among the Greeks, left behind there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And he came and sought to take the city, and to pillage it; and he was not able, because the thing was known to them of the city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and they rose up against him to battle: and he fled, and removed thence with great heaviness, to return to Babylon &#91;Confusion&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And there came one bringing him tidings into Persia &#91;Descendants of Loving-kindness&#93;, that the armies, which went against the land of Judah &#91;Praised&#93;, had been put to flight;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and that Lysias went first with a strong host, and was put to shame before them; and that they had waxed strong by reason of arms and power, and with store of spoils, which they took from the armies that they had cut off;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and that they had pulled down the abomination which he had built upon the altar (of burnt offerings) that was in Jerusalem &#91;City of peace&#93;; and that they had compassed about the sanctuary with high walls, as before, and Bethsura, his city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And it came to pass, when the king heard these words, he was astonished and moved exceedingly: and he laid him down upon his bed, and fell sick for grief, because it had not befallen him as he looked for.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And he was there many days, because great grief was renewed upon him, and he made account that he should die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And he called for all his Friends, and said to them, Sleep departs from mine eyes, and my heart fails for care.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And I said in my heart, To what tribulation am I come, and how great a flood is it, wherein I now am! for I was gracious and beloved in my power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> But now I remember the evils which I did at Jerusalem &#91;City of peace&#93;, and that I took all the utensils of silver and gold that were therein, and sent forth to destroy the inhabitants of Judah &#91;Praised&#93; without a cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> I perceive that on this account these evils are come upon me, and, behold, I perish through great grief in a strange land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And he called for Philip &#91;Loves horses&#93;, one of his Friends, and set him over all his kingdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and gave him his diadem ·crown·, and his robe, and his signet ring, to the end he should bring Antiochus his son, and nourish him up that he might be king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And king Antiochus died there in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="circa B.C.E 164">a</a></sup>the one hundred forty ninth year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Lysias knew that the king was dead, and he set up Antiochus his son to reign, whom he had nourished up being young, and he called his name Eupator.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And they that were in the fortress shut up Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; round about the sanctuary, and sought always their hurt, and the strengthening of the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Judas &#91;Praised&#93; thought to destroy them, and called all the people together to besiege them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And they were gathered together, and besieged them in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="circa B.C.E 163">b</a></sup>the one hundred fiftieth year, and he made mounds to shoot from, and engines of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And there came forth some of them that were shut up, and there were joined to them certain ungodly men of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And they went to the king, and said, How long will you not execute judgement, and avenge our brethren?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> We were willing to serve your father, and to walk after his words, and to follow his commandments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and for this cause the children of our people besieged the fortress, and were alienated from us; but as many of us as they could light on they killed, and spoiled our inheritances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And not against us only did they stretch out their hand, but also against all their borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And, behold, they are encamped this day against the fortress at Jerusalem &#91;City of peace&#93;, to take it: and the sanctuary and Bethsura have they fortified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And if you are not beforehand with them quickly, they will do greater things than these, and you shall not be able to control them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And when the king heard this, he was angry, and gathered together all his Friends, even the rulers of his host, and them that were over the horse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And there came to him from other kingdoms, and from isles of the sea, bands of hired soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the number of his forces was one hundred thousand footmen, and twenty thousand horsemen, and thirty two elephants trained for war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And they went through Idumaea, and encamped against Bethsura, and fought against it many days, and made engines of war; and they of Bethsura came out, and burned them with fire, and fought valiantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And Judas &#91;Praised&#93; removed from the fortress, and encamped at Bethzacharias, over against the king’s camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And the king rose early in the morning, and removed his army at full speed along the road to Bethzacharias, and his forces made them ready to battle, and sounded with the trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And they showed the elephants the blood of grapes and mulberries, that they might prepare them for the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And they divided the beasts among the heavy infantry armed with spears, and they set by each elephant a thousand men armed with coats of mail, and helmets of bronze on their heads; and for each beast were appointed five hundred chosen horsemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> These were ready beforehand, wherever the beast was; and wherever the beast went, they went with him; they departed not from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And towers of wood were upon them, strong and covered, one upon each beast, girded fast upon him with cunning contrivances; and upon each beast were thirty two valiant men that fought upon them, beside his Indian</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> (and the residue of the horsemen he set on this side and that side at the two parts of the army), striking terror into the enemy, and protected by the heavy infantry armed with spears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Now when the sun shone upon the shields of gold and bronze, the mountains shone therewith, and blazed like torches of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And a part of the king’s army was spread upon the high mountains, and some on the low ground, and they went on firmly and in order.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And all that heard the noise of their multitude, and the marching of the multitude, and the rattling of the arms, did quake: for the army was exceeding great and strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And Judas &#91;Praised&#93; and his army drew near for battle, and there fell of the king’s army six hundred men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And Eleazar &#91;Help of God&#93;, who was called Avaran, saw one of the beasts armed with royal breastplates, and he was higher than all the beasts, and the king seemed to be upon him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and he gave himself to deliver his people, and to get him an everlasting name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> and he ran upon him courageously into the midst of the heavy infantry armed with spears, and killed on the right hand and on the left, and they parted asunder from him on this side and on that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And he crept under the elephant, and thrust him from beneath, and killed him; and the elephant fell to the earth upon him, and he died there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> And they saw the strength of the kingdom, and the fierce onset of the hosts, and turned away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> But they of the king’s army went up to Jerusalem &#91;City of peace&#93; to meet them, and the king encamped toward Judea &#91;Praise&#93;, and toward mount Sion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> And he made peace with them of Bethsura; and he came out of the city, because they had no food there to endure the siege, because it was a Sabbath ·To cease· to the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> And the king took Bethsura, and appointed a garrison there to keep it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And he encamped against the sanctuary many days; and set there mounds to shoot from, and engines of war, and instruments for casting fire and stones, and pieces to cast darts, and slings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And they also made engines against their engines, and fought for many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> But there were no food in the sanctuary, because it was the seventh year, and they that fled for safety into Judea &#91;Praise&#93; from among the Gentiles had eaten up the residue of the store;</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> and there were but a few left in the sanctuary, because the famine prevailed against them, and they were scattered, each man to his own place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> And Lysias heard say, that Philip &#91;Loves horses&#93;, whom Antiochus the king, whiles he was yet alive, appointed to nourish up his son Antiochus, that he might be king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> was returned from Persia &#91;Descendants of Loving-kindness&#93; and Media &#91;Middle-land&#93;, and with him the forces that went with the king, and that he was seeking to take to him the government.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> And he made haste, and gave consent to depart; and he said to the king and the leaders of the host and to the men, We decay daily, and our food is scant, and the place where we encamp is strong, and the affairs of the kingdom lie upon us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> now therefore let us give the right hand to these men, and make peace with them and with all their nation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> and covenant with them, that they shall walk after their own laws, as aforetime: for because of their laws which we abolished they were angered, and did all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> And the saying pleased the king and the princes, and he sent to them to make peace; and they accepted thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> And the king and the princes swore to them: thereupon they came forth from the stronghold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> And the king entered into mount Sion; and he saw the strength of the place, and set at nothing the oath which he had sworn, and gave commandment to pull down the wall round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> And he removed in haste, and returned to Antioch, and found Philip &#91;Loves horses&#93; master of the city; and he fought against him, and took the city by force.</span>
