<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And John &#91;Yah is gracious&#93; went up from Gazara, and told Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; his father what Cendebaeus was doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; called his two oldest sons, Judas &#91;Praised&#93; and John &#91;Yah is gracious&#93;, and said to them, I and my brethren and my father’s house have fought the battles of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; from our youth, even to this day; and things have prospered in our hands, that we should deliver Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; oftentimes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> But now I am old, and you moreover, by his mercy, are of a sufficient age: be you instead of me and my brother, and go forth and fight for our nation; but let the help which is from heaven (God) be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And he chose out of the country twenty thousand men of war and horsemen, and they went against Cendebaeus, and slept at Modin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And rising up in the morning, they went into the plain, and, behold, a great host came to meet them, of footmen and horsemen: and there was a brook between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he encamped over against them, he and his people: and he saw that the people were afraid to pass over the brook, and he passed over first, and the men saw him, and passed over after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And he divided the people, and set the horsemen in the midst of the footmen: but the enemies’ horsemen were exceeding many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And they sounded with the trumpets; and Cendebaeus and his army were put to the rout, and there fell of them many wounded to death, but they that were left fled to the stronghold:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> at that time was Judas &#91;Praised&#93; John &#91;Yah is gracious&#93;’s brother wounded: but John &#91;Yah is gracious&#93; pursued after them, till he came to Kidron, which Cendebaeus had built;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and they fled to the towers that are in the fields of Azotus; and he burned it with fire; and there fell of them about two thousand men. And he returned into Judea &#91;Praise&#93; in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Ptolemy the son of Abubus had been appointed captain for the plain of Jericho &#91;Fragrant, Moon&#93;, and he had much silver and gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> for he was the Cohen Gadol ·High Priest·’s son in law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And his heart was lifted up, and he was minded to make himself master of the country, and he took counsel deceitfully against Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; and his sons, to make away with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Now Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; was visiting the cities that were in the country, and taking care for the good ordering of them; and he went down to Jericho &#91;Fragrant, Moon&#93;, himself and Mattathias &#91;Gift of Yah&#93; and Judas &#91;Praised&#93; his sons, in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="circa B.C.E 136">a</a></sup>the one hundred seventity seventh year, in the eleventh month Shevat ·Staff / Rod / Royal scepter·, the same is the month Sebat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and the son of Abubus received them deceitfully into the little stronghold that is called Dok, which he had built, and made them a great banquet, and hid men there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And when Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; and his sons had drunk freely, Ptolemy and his men rose up, and took their arms, and came in upon Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; into the banqueting place, and killed him, and his two sons, and certain of his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he committed a great iniquity, and recompensed evil for good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And Ptolemy wrote these things, and sent to the king, that he should send him forces to aid him, and should deliver him their country and the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And he sent others to Gazara to make away with John &#91;Yah is gracious&#93;: and to the captains of thousands he sent letters to come to him, that he might give them silver and gold and gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And others he sent to take possession of Jerusalem &#91;City of peace&#93;, and the mount of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And one ran before to Gazara, and told John &#91;Yah is gracious&#93; that his father and brethren were perished, and he has sent to kill you also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And when he heard, he was sore amazed; and he laid hands on the men that came to destroy him, and killed them; for he perceived that they were seeking to destroy him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the rest of the acts of John &#91;Yah is gracious&#93;, and of his wars, and of his valiant deeds which he did, and of the building of the walls which he built, and of his doings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> behold, they are written in the chronicles of his role as Cohen Gadol ·High Priest·, from the time that he was made Cohen Gadol ·High Priest· after his father.</span>
