<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Antiochus son of Demetrius the king sent letters from the isles of the sea to Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; the priest and ethnarch &#91;governor&#93; of the Jews &#91;Praisers&#93;, and to all the nation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and the contents thereof were after this manner: King Antiochus to Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; the chief priest and ethnarch &#91;governor&#93;, and to the nation of the Jews &#91;Praisers&#93;, greeting:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> In as much as certain pestilent fellows have made themselves masters of the kingdom of our fathers, but my purpose is to claim the kingdom, that I may restore it as it was before; and moreover I have raised a multitude of foreign soldiers, and have prepared ships of war;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> moreover I am minded to land in the country, that I may punish them that have destroyed our country, and them that have made many cities in the kingdom desolate:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Now therefore I confirm to you all the exactions which the kings that were before me remitted to you, and whatever gifts besides they remitted to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and I give you leave to coin money for your country with your own stamp,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> but that Jerusalem &#91;City of peace&#93; and the sanctuary should be free: and all the arms that you have prepared, and the strongholds that you have built, which you have in your possession, let them remain to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and everything owing to the king, and the things that shall be owing to the king from henceforth and for evermore, let them be remitted to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> moreover, when we shall have established our kingdom, we will glorify you and your nation and the temple with great glory, so that your glory shall be made manifest in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> In<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="circa B.C.E 139">a</a></sup>the one hundred seventity fourth year went Antiochus forth into the land of his fathers; and all the forces came together to him, so that there were few men with Tryphon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And king Antiochus pursued him; as he fled, he came to Dor, which is by the sea:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> for he knew that troubles were come upon him all at once, and that his forces had forsaken him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Antiochus encamped against Dor, and with him one hundred and twenty thousand men of war, and eight thousand horse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And he compassed the city round about, and the ships joined in the attack from the sea; and he vexed the city by land and sea, and suffered no man to go out or in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Numenius and his company came from Rome &#91;Town of flowing waters&#93;, having letters to the kings and to the countries, wherein were written these things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Lucius, consul of the Romans &#91;People from Town of flowing waters&#93;, to king Ptolemy, greeting:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The Jews &#91;Praisers&#93;’ ambassadors came to us as our friends and confederates, to renew the old friendship and confederacy, being sent from Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; the Cohen Gadol ·High Priest·, and from the people of the Jews &#91;Praisers&#93;:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> moreover they brought a shield of gold of a thousand pound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> It pleased us therefore to write to the kings and to the countries, that they should not seek their hurt, nor fight against them, and their cities, and their country, nor be confederates with such as fight against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Moreover it seemed good to us to receive the shield of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> If therefore any pestilent fellows have fled from their country to you, deliver them to Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; the Cohen Gadol ·High Priest·, that he may take vengeance on them according to their law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the same things wrote he to Demetrius the king, and to Attalus, and to Arathes, and to Arsaces,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and to all the countries, and to Sampsames, and to the Spartans, and to Delos, and to Myndos, and to Sicyon, and to Caria, and to Samos, and to Pamphylia, and to Lycia, and to Halicarnassus, and to Rhodes, and to Phaselis, and to Cos, and to Side, and to Aradus, and Gortyna, and Cnidus, and Cyprus, and Cyrene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> But the copy hereof they wrote to Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; the Cohen Gadol ·High Priest·.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> But Antiochus the king encamped against Dor the second day, bringing his forces up to it continually, and making engines of war, and he shut up Tryphon from going in or out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; sent him two thousand chosen men to fight on his side; and silver, and gold, and instruments of war in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And he would not receive them, but set at nothing all the covenants which he had made with him aforetime, and was estranged from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he sent to him Athenobius, one of his Friends, to commune with him, saying, You hold possession of Joppa and Gazara, and the fortress that is in Jerusalem &#91;City of peace&#93;, cities of my kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The borders thereof you have wasted, and done great hurt in the land, and got the dominion of many places in my kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Now therefore deliver up the cities which you have taken, and the tributes of the places whereof you have gotten dominion without the borders of Judea &#91;Praise&#93;:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> or else give me for them five hundred talents &#91;300,000 denarii; 258,750 lb; 11,700 kg&#93; of silver; and for the harm that you have done, and the tributes of the cities, other five hundred talents &#91;300,000 denarii; 258,750 lb; 11,700 kg&#93;: or else we will come and subdue you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And Athenobius the king’s Friend came to Jerusalem &#91;City of peace&#93;; and he saw the glory of Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93;, and the cupboard of gold and silver vessels, and his great attendance, and he was amazed; and he reported to him the king’s words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; answered, and said to him, We have neither taken other men’s land, nor have we possession of that which appertains to others, but of the inheritance of our fathers; howbeit, it was had in possession of our enemies wrongfully for a certain time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> But we, having opportunity, hold fast the inheritance of our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> But as touching Joppa and Gazara, which you demand, they did great harm among the people throughout our country, we will give one hundred talents &#91;600,000 denarii; 51,750 lb; 2,340 kg&#93; for them. And he answered him not a word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> but returned in a rage to the king, and reported to him these words, and the glory of Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93;, and all that he had seen: and the king was exceeding angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> But Tryphon embarked on board a ship, and fled to Orthosia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And the king appointed Cendebaeus chief captain of the sea coast, and gave him forces of foot and horse:</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> and he commanded him to encamp before Judea &#91;Praise&#93;, and he commanded him to build up Kidron, and to fortify the gates, and that he should fight against the people: but the king pursued Tryphon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And Cendebaeus came to Jamnia, and began to provoke the people, and to invade Judea &#91;Praise&#93;, and to take the people captive, and to kill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And he built Kidron, and set horsemen there, and forces of foot, to the end that issuing out they might make outroads upon the ways of Judea &#91;Praise&#93;, according as the king commanded him.</span>
