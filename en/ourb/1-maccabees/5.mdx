<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And it came to pass, when the Gentiles round about heard that the altar (of burnt offerings) was built, and the sanctuary dedicated as aforetime, they were exceeding angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And they took counsel to destroy the race of Jacob &#91;Supplanter&#93; that was in the midst of them, and they began to kill and destroy among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And Judas &#91;Praised&#93; fought against the children of Esau &#91;Hairy&#93; in Idumaea at Akrabattine, because they besieged Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;: and he struck them with a great slaughter, and brought down their pride, and took their spoils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And he remembered the wickedness of the children of Baean, who were to the people a snare and a stumbling block, lying in wait for them in the ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And they were shut up by him in the towers; and he encamped against them, and destroyed them utterly, and burned with fire the towers of the place, with all that were therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he passed over to the children of Ammon &#91;Tribal people&#93;, and found a mighty band, and much people, with Timotheus for their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And he fought many battles with them, and they were embarrassed before his face; and he struck them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and got possession of Jazer, and the villages thereof, and returned again into Judea &#91;Praise&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And the Gentiles that were in Gilead gathered themselves together against the Israelites that were on their borders, to destroy them. And they fled to the stronghold of Dathema,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and sent letters to Judas &#91;Praised&#93; and his brethren, saying, The Gentiles that are round about us are gathered together against us to destroy us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and they are preparing to come and get possession of the stronghold whereunto we fled for refuge, and Timotheus is the leader of their host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Now therefore come and deliver us from their hand, for many of us are fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And all our brethren that were in the land of Tubias have been put to death; and they have carried into captivity their wives and their children and their stuff; and they destroyed there about a thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> While the letters were yet reading, behold, there came other messengers from Galilee &#91;District, Circuit&#93; with their clothes tore, bringing a report after this wise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> saying, That there were gathered together against them those of Ptolemais, and of Tyre, and of Sidon, and all Galil-of-the-Goyim &#91;District, Circuit of Non-Jews&#93; to consume them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Now when Judas &#91;Praised&#93; and the people heard these words, there assembled together a great congregation, to consult what they should do for their brethren, that were in tribulation, and were assaulted of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Judas &#91;Praised&#93; said to Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; his brother, Choose you out men, and go and deliver your brethren that are in Galilee &#91;District, Circuit&#93;, but I and Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; my brother will go into the land of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he left Joseph &#91;May he add&#93; the son of Zacharias &#91;Remembered by Yah&#93;, and Azarias, as leaders of the people, with the remnant of the host, in Judea &#91;Praise&#93;, for to keep it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And he gave commandment to them, saying, Take you the charge of this people, and fight no battle with the Gentiles until that we come again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And to Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; were divided three thousand men to go into Galilee &#91;District, Circuit&#93;, but to Judas &#91;Praised&#93; eight thousand men to go into the land of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; went into Galilee &#91;District, Circuit&#93;, and fought many battles with the Gentiles, and the Gentiles were embarrassed before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And he pursued them to the gate of Ptolemais; and there fell of the Gentiles about three thousand men, and he took their spoils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And they took to them those that were in Galilee &#91;District, Circuit&#93;, and in Arbatta, with their wives and their children, and all that they had, and brought them into Judea &#91;Praise&#93; with great gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And Judas &#91;Praised&#93; Maccabee &#91;Praised Hammer&#93; and his brother Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; passed over Jordan &#91;Descender&#93;, and went three days’ journey in the wilderness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and they met with the Nabathaeans, and these met them in a peaceful manner, and told them all things that had befallen their brethren in the land of Gilead:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and how that many of them were shut up in Bosora, and Bosor, and Alema, Casphor, Maked, and Carnaim; all these cities are strong and great:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and how that they were shut up in the rest of the cities of the land of Gilead, and that tomorrow they have appointed to encamp against the strongholds, and to take them, and to destroy all these men in one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Judas &#91;Praised&#93; and his army turned suddenly by the way of the wilderness to Bosora; and he took the city, and killed all the males with the edge of the sword, and took all their spoils, and burned the city with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And he removed from thence by night, and went till he came to the stronghold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the morning came, and they lifted up their eyes, and, behold, much people which could not be counted, bearing ladders and engines of war, to take the stronghold; and they were fighting against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Judas &#91;Praised&#93; saw that the battle was begun, and that the cry of the city went up to heaven (God), with trumpets and a great sound,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> and he said to the men of his host, Fight this day for your brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And he went forth behind them in three companies, and they sounded with their trumpets, and cried out in prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And the army of Timotheus perceived that it was Maccabaeus &#91;Hammer&#93;, and they fled from before him: and he struck them with a great slaughter; and there fell of them on that day about eight thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And he turned aside to Mizpeh and fought against it, and took it, and killed all the males thereof, and took the spoils thereof, and burned it with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> From thence he removed, and took Casphor, Maked, Bosor, and the other cities of the land of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Now after these things Timotheus gathered another army, and encamped over against Raphon beyond the brook.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And Judas &#91;Praised&#93; sent men to spy on the army; and they brought him word, saying, All the Gentiles that be round about us are gathered together to them, an exceeding great host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And they have hired Arabians to help them, and are encamping beyond the brook, ready to come against you to battle. And Judas &#91;Praised&#93; went to meet them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And Timotheus said to the captains of his host, when Judas &#91;Praised&#93; and his army drew near to the brook of water, If he pass over first to us, we shall not be able to withstand him; for he will mightily prevail against us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> but if he be afraid, and encamp beyond the river, we will cross over to him, and prevail against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Now when Judas &#91;Praised&#93; came near to the brook of water, he caused the Torah-Teachers of the people to remain by the brook, and gave commandment to them, saying, Suffer no man to encamp, but let all come to the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And he crossed over the first against them, and all the people after him: and all the Gentiles were embarrassed before his face, and cast away their arms, and fled to the temple at Carnaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And they took the city, and burned the temple with fire, together with all that were therein. And Carnaim was subdued, neither could they stand any longer before the face of Judas &#91;Praised&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And Judas &#91;Praised&#93; gathered together all Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, them that were in the land of Gilead, from the least to the greatest, and their wives, and their children, and their stuff, an exceeding great army, that they might come into the land of Judah &#91;Praised&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And they came as far as Ephron &#91;Fawn-like&#93;, and this same city was great, and it was in the way as they should go, exceeding strong: they could not turn aside from it on the right hand or on the left, but must needs pass through the midst of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> And they of the city shut them out, and stopped up the gates with stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And Judas &#91;Praised&#93; sent to them with words of peace, saying, We will pass through your land to go into our own land, and none shall do you any hurt, we will only pass by on our feet. And they would not open to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> And Judas &#91;Praised&#93; commanded proclamation to be made in the army, that each man should encamp in the place where he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> And the men of the host encamped, and fought against the city all that day and all that night, and the city was delivered into his hands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> and he destroyed all the males with the edge of the sword, and razed the city, and took the spoils thereof, and passed through the city over them that were slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And they went over Jordan &#91;Descender&#93; into the great plain over against Bethshan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> And Judas &#91;Praised&#93; gathered together those that lagged behind, and encouraged the people all the way through, until he came into the land of Judah &#91;Praised&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> And they went up to mount Sion with gladness and joy, and offered whole burnt offerings, because not so much as one of them was slain until they returned in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> And in the days when Judas &#91;Praised&#93; and Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; were in the land of Gilead, and Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; his brother in Galilee &#91;District, Circuit&#93; before Ptolemais,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Joseph &#91;May he add&#93; the son of Zacharias &#91;Remembered by Yah&#93;, and Azarias, rulers of the host, heard of their exploits and of the war, what things they had done;</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> and they said, Let us also get us a name, and let us go fight against the Gentiles that are round about us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> And they gave charge to the men of the host that was with them, and went toward Jamnia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> And Gorgias and his men came out of the city to meet them in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> And Joseph &#91;May he add&#93; and Azarias were put to flight, and were pursued to the borders of Judea &#91;Praise&#93;; and there fell on that day of the people of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; about two thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> And there was a great overthrow among the people, because they hearkened not to Judas &#91;Praised&#93; and his brethren, thinking to do some exploit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> But they were not of the seed of those men, by whose hand deliverance was given to Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> And the man Judas &#91;Praised&#93; and his brethren were glorified exceedingly in the sight of all Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;, and of all the Gentiles, wherever their name was heard of;</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64"> and men gathered together to them, acclaiming them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65"> And Judas &#91;Praised&#93; and his brethren went forth, and fought against the children of Esau &#91;Hairy&#93; in the land toward the south; and he struck Hebron and the villages thereof, and pulled down the strongholds thereof, and burned the towers thereof round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66"> And he removed to go into the land of the Philistines &#91;To roll in dust (As an insult)&#93;, and he went through Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67"> In that day certain priests, desiring to do exploits there, were slain in battle, when as he went out to battle unadvisedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68"> And Judas &#91;Praised&#93; turned aside to Azotus, to the land of the Philistines &#91;To roll in dust (As an insult)&#93;, and pulled down their altars, and burned the carved images of their deities with fire, and took the spoil of their cities, and returned into the land of Judah &#91;Praised&#93;.</span>
