<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="circa B.C.E 141">a</a></sup>the one hundred seventity second year king Demetrius gathered his forces together, and went into Media &#91;Middle-land&#93;, to get him help, that he might fight against Tryphon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And Arsaces, the king of Persia &#91;Descendants of Loving-kindness&#93; and Media &#91;Middle-land&#93;, heard that Demetrius was come into his borders, and he sent one of his princes to take him alive:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and he went and struck the army of Demetrius, and took him, and brought him to Arsaces; and he put him in ward.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And the land had rest all the days of Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93;: and he sought the good of his nation; and his authority and his glory was well-pleasing to them all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And amid all his glory he took Joppa for a haven, and made it an entrance for the isles of the sea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and he enlarged the borders of his nation, and got possession of the country;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and he gathered together a great number of captives, and got the dominion of Gazara, and Bethsura, and the fortress, and he took away the multitude of its uncleanness; and there was none that resisted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And they tilled their land in peace, and the land gave her increase, and the trees of the plains their fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The ancient men sat in the streets, they communed all of them together of good things, and the young men put on glorious and warlike apparel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He provided food for the cities, and furnished them with all manner of munition, until the name of his glory was named to the end of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> He made peace in the land, and Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; rejoiced with great joy:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and they sat each man under his vine and his fig tree, and there was none to make them afraid:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and there ceased in the land any that fought against them: and the kings were embarrassed in those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And he strengthened all those of his people that were brought low: Torah ·Teaching· he searched out, and every distant from Torah ·Teaching· and wicked person he took away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He glorified the sanctuary, and the utensils of the temple he multiplied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And it was heard at Rome &#91;Town of flowing waters&#93; that Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; was dead, and even to Sparta, and they were exceeding sorry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But as soon as they heard that his brother Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; was made Cohen Gadol ·High Priest· in his stead, and ruled the country, and the cities therein,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> they wrote to him on tablets of bronze, to renew with him the friendship and the confederacy which they had confirmed with Judas &#91;Praised&#93; and Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; his brethren;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and they were read before the congregation at Jerusalem &#91;City of peace&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And this is the copy of the letters which the Spartans sent:<br />The rulers of the Spartans, and the city, to Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; the Cohen Gadol ·High Priest·, and to the elders, and the priests, and the residue of the people of the Jews &#91;Praisers&#93;, our brethren, greeting:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The ambassadors that were sent to our people made report to us of your glory and honor: and we were glad for their coming,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and we did register the things that were spoken by them in the public records after this manner: Numenius son of Antiochus, and Antipater son of Jason, the Jews &#91;Praisers&#93;’ ambassadors, came to us to renew the friendship they had with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And it pleased the people to entertain the men honorably, and to put the copy of their words in the public records, to the end that the people of the Spartans might have a memorial thereof: moreover they wrote a copy of these things to Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; the Cohen Gadol ·High Priest·.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> After this Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; sent Numenius to Rome &#91;Town of flowing waters&#93; with a great shield of gold of a thousand pound weight, in order to confirm the confederacy with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> But when the people heard these things, they said, What thanks shall we give to Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; and his sons?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> for he and his brethren and the house of his father have made themselves strong, and have chased away in fight the enemies of Israel &#91;God prevails&#93; from them, and confirmed liberty to Israel &#91;God prevails&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And they wrote on tablets of bronze, and set them upon pillars in mount Sion: and this is the copy of the writing:<br />On the eighteenth day of the sixth month of Elul ·Search, 6·, in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="circa B.C.E 141">b</a></sup>the one hundred seventity second year, and this is the third year of Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; the Cohen Gadol ·High Priest·,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> in Asaramel, in a great congregation of priests and people and princes of the nation, and of the elders of the country, was it notified to us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> In as much as oftentimes there have been wars in the country, but Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; the son of Mattathias &#91;Gift of Yah&#93;, the son of the sons of Joarib, and his brethren, put themselves in jeopardy, and withstood the enemies of their nation, that their sanctuary and Torah ·Teaching· might be established, and glorified their nation with great glory:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and Jonathan &#91;Yah has given&#93; assembled their nation together, and became their Cohen Gadol ·High Priest·, and was gathered to his people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> and their enemies purposed to invade their country, that they might destroy their country utterly, and stretch forth their hands against their sanctuary:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> then rose up Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93;, and fought for his nation, and spent much of his own substance, and armed the valiant men of his nation, and gave them wages:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and he fortified the cities of Judea &#91;Praise&#93;, and Bethsura that lies upon the borders of Judea &#91;Praise&#93;, where the arms of the enemies were aforetime, and set there a garrison of Jews &#91;Praisers&#93;:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> and he fortified Joppa which is upon the sea, and Gazara which is upon the borders of Azotus, wherein the enemies lived aforetime, and placed Jews &#91;Praisers&#93; there, and set therein all things convenient for the reparation thereof:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and the people saw the trusting faith in action of Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93;, and the glory which he thought to bring to his nation, and they made him their leader and Cohen Gadol ·High Priest·, because he had done all these things, and for the justice and the trusting faith which he kept to his nation, and for that he sought by all means to exalt his people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> and in his days things prospered in his hands, so that the Gentiles were taken away out of their country, and they also that were in the city of David &#91;Beloved&#93;, they that were in Jerusalem &#91;City of peace&#93;, who had made themselves a fortress, out of which they issued, and polluted all things round about the sanctuary, and did great hurt to its purity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and he placed Jews &#91;Praisers&#93; therein, and fortified it for the safety of the country and the city, and made high the walls of Jerusalem &#91;City of peace&#93;:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and king Demetrius confirmed to him the role of Cohen Gadol ·High Priest· according to these things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> and made him one of his Friends, and honored him with great honor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> for he had heard say, that the Jews &#91;Praisers&#93; had been called by the Romans &#91;People from Town of flowing waters&#93; friends and confederates and brethren, and that they had met the ambassadors of Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; honorably;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> and that the Jews &#91;Praisers&#93; and the priests were well pleased that Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; should be their leader and Cohen Gadol ·High Priest· forever, until there should arise a faithful prophet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> and that he should be captain over them, and should take charge of the sanctuary, to set them over their works, and over the country, and over the arms, and over the strongholds; and that he should take charge of the sanctuary,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> and that he should be obeyed by all, and that all instruments in the country should be written in his name, and that he should be clothed in purple, and wear gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and that it should not be lawful for any of the people or of the priests to set at nothing any of these things, or to gainsay the words that he should speak, or to gather an assembly in the country without him, or to be clothed in purple, or wear a buckle of gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> but whoever should do otherwise, or set at nothing any of these things, he should be liable to punishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> All the people consented to ordain for Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; that he should do according to these words;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> and Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; accepted hereof, and consented to be Cohen Gadol ·High Priest·, and to be captain and governor of the Jews &#91;Praisers&#93; and of the priests, and to be protector of all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And they commanded to put this writing on tablets of bronze, and to set them up within the precinct of the sanctuary in a conspicuous place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> and moreover to put the copies thereof in the treasury, to the end that Simeon &#91;Hearing&#93; and his sons might have them.</span>
</div>
