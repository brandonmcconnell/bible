## About the empty cave

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 20:1–2; Mark 16:1–8; Matthew 28:1–8"> Very early on Sunday morning the women went to the cave carrying the oil and powder. Mary Magdalene, Joanna and another Mary, who was James' mother, and some other women went together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:3–4; Matthew 27:60–66; Mark 15:46–47"> When they reached the cave, they saw that someone had moved the big stone that had shut the opening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:23; John 20:6–7; Matthew 16:5"> So they went in, but they didn't find the body of the Lord Jesus inside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 16:5; John 20:11–12; Acts 1:10"> While they were standing there wondering about it, suddenly two men came and appeared and stood near them. They were wearing shining clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 16:5–6; Matthew 28:3–5; Revelation 1:18"> The women got a big shock and were very frightened and they bowed down to the ground. The men said to them, “Why are you looking for a living person here in the place for the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 17:22–23; Luke 9:22; Mark 8:31"> He is not here. God has made him alive again. Remember what he said to you while he was in Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 16:21"> He said, ‘People will take me, the one who was born in this world, and they will give me to wicked people. Then they will nail me on a cross and kill me and in three days I will come alive again.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 12:16; John 14:26; John 2:19–22"> Then the women remembered Jesus' words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 24:22–24; Mark 16:7–8; Matthew 28:7–8"> and left the cave and went home. They told Jesus' eleven disciples and his other friends everything that had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 9:16; Mark 16:11; Genesis 19:14"> But when the apostles heard what the women said, they thought they were being stupid and they didn't believe them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 20:3–10"> But Peter got up and ran to the cave. When he reached it he bent down and looked inside. He saw the cloth and that was all, and he too went back home, wondering and amazed.</span>
</div>
</div>

## About two of Jesus' disciples going to Emmaus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 24:18; Mark 16:12–13"> On the same day that Jesus came alive again, two of his disciples were going from Jerusalem to a village called Emmaus. It was a good walk but not too far. While they were going along the road,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:45; Deuteronomy 6:7; Malachi 3:6"> they were talking together about everything that had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:20; John 14:18–19; Luke 24:36"> While they were talking, Jesus came near and walked along with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 20:14; John 21:4; Luke 24:31"> They saw him but they didn't recognize him; they couldn't understand that it was Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:20–22; John 16:6; Ezekiel 9:4–6"> He said, “What are you two talking about to each other as you walk along?” They both stood still, feeling miserable, with sad faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 19:25"> One of them named Cleopas said to Jesus, “Have you been staying in Jerusalem and you don't know what has happened? You must be the only one who doesn't know the news!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; Acts 7:22; John 4:19"> “What news?” Jesus asked. “Surely you know they nailed Jesus of Nazareth on a cross and he died the other day,” they said. “That man was a prophet. The great things that he did and said showed us that God was pleased with him. All the people were pleased with him too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:13–15; Acts 4:8–10; Luke 23:13"> But our chief priests and our rulers gave him to Pilate for him to be killed, and they nailed him to a cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:68; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Luke 2:38"> We had thought, ‘Maybe he is the one God will send to set Israel free!’ But no! He has been dead for three days now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 16:9–10; Matthew 28:7–8; John 20:1–2"> “Some of our women went to the cave early this morning, but they could not find his body. They came back to us and said, ‘We saw two angels. They came and appeared to us and told us that Jesus is alive again now.’ And we were amazed to hear that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 24:12; John 20:1–10"> Some men from our group also went to the cave, and they saw that it was empty, just as the women had said, but they didn't see Jesus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 7:18; Mark 9:19; Hebrews 5:11–12"> Jesus said to them, “Don't you know God's words that were written long ago? Do you still not know that they are true? You are very silly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:11; Isaiah 53:1–12; Zechariah 13:7"> You should know that the Messiah, the one God was to send to you, had to suffer before God makes him rule.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Luke 24:44; Deuteronomy 18:15"> Then Jesus explained to them everything that was written about him long ago in God's book. He told them what Moses said and also what the prophets said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 6:48; Genesis 19:2; Genesis 32:26"> Then they came near Emmaus. Jesus acted as if he were going straight ahead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 4:8; Luke 14:23; Genesis 19:3"> but they stopped him and said, “Stay with us. It will soon be night.” So Jesus went inside and stayed there with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; Luke 24:35; Matthew 15:36"> He sat down to eat and took some damper and thanked God for it. Then he broke it and gave it to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 8:59; Luke 4:30"> Then their eyes were opened, and they recognized him, but Jesus disappeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 24:45; Jeremiah 23:29; Hebrews 4:12"> They said to each other, “We felt excited when Jesus spoke to us, when we were coming along the road and he was explaining God's word to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 16:13; John 20:19–26; Acts 1:14"> Straight away they got up and went back to Jerusalem and found the eleven disciples. The disciples had come together with some other friends of Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5; Mark 16:7; Luke 22:54–62"> and they said, “The Lord really has come alive again! He has appeared to Simon!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 16:12–13; Acts 2:42; Luke 24:30–31"> Then Cleopas and the other disciple told them about meeting Jesus on the road, and what he had said about God, and about them recognizing Jesus when he broke the damper.</span>
</div>
</div>

## About Jesus appearing to his disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:16; Isaiah 57:18; Mark 16:14"> While the two disciples were speaking, the Lord Jesus suddenly came and appeared among them and said, “Peace be with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:13; Matthew 14:26–27; Job 4:14–16"> But his disciples got a big shock and were very frightened. They thought he was a ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:14; Hebrews 4:13; Daniel 4:19"> Jesus said to them, “Why are you afraid? Why do you still not believe me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 20:27; 1 John 1:1; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> Look at my hands and my feet. I am real! Feel me so you will know it is really me. You can see my flesh and my bones. A ghost doesn't have flesh and bones, so I cannot be a ghost. I am a real person.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Jesus showed them his hands and his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 21:5; Psalms 126:1–2; Genesis 45:26–28"> Then they were very happy, but they were still wondering because they still couldn't believe. Jesus said to them, “Have you anything for me to eat?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> They gave him some cooked fish,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 10:41"> and they watched him eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Luke 24:26–27; Zechariah 9:9"> Then Jesus said, “Now you have seen that I really am alive again. That is what I told you before, when we were all together. Long ago Moses and the prophets spoke about me and the people who wrote the songs to praise God spoke about me too. Everything they said has come true because God planned it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Acts 16:14; 1 John 5:20; Psalms 119:18"> Then Jesus explained God's words to his disciples so that they would understand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–12; Isaiah 50:6; Luke 24:44"> and he said, “This is what is written: ‘The Messiah must suffer and die and come alive again on the third day.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 5:31; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 2:38"> God's people must tell others about the Messiah in all different places. First they must tell about him in Jerusalem and then in all the world. They must tell people to stop doing wrong and obey God, so that he can forgive them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 2:32; Acts 13:31"> You disciples of mine know everything that has happened, because you have seen with your own eyes. So you are the ones who must tell other people everything you have seen and heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 1:4; Joel 2:28–32"> “My Father has promised that you will receive his Spirit, and I am the one who will send the Spirit to you. But you must stay here in Jerusalem and wait until God's Spirit comes down in power and touches you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## About Jesus going up to heaven to his Father

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Mark 11:1"> When Jesus had finished speaking, he took his disciples out of Jerusalem to Bethany, and he lifted up his hands and blessed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Acts 1:9; Mark 16:19; 2 Kings 2:11"> While Jesus was still speaking, he moved away from them and was taken up into heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 28:9; John 16:22; Psalms 30:11"> The disciples worshipped him and went back to Jerusalem. They were filled with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Mark 16:20; Acts 2:46–47; Acts 5:41–42"> After that they stayed in the temple all the time, praising God.</span>
</div>
</div>
