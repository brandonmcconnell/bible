## Introduction

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:1–3; 2 Peter 1:16–19; John 20:31"> My name is Luke, and today I am writing this letter to you, Theophilus. You know that many people have already written a report about the things Jesus did while he was here with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:27; Acts 26:16; Hebrews 2:3"> But they didn't see Jesus with their own eyes. Many other people saw Jesus from when he started until he finished the work God gave him to do. And they told those people so that they could write down what they had heard, and they told us too about everything they had seen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 1:1; Acts 26:25; Acts 11:4"> So, Master, I have learnt the things Jesus did from when he started until he went back to his Father. I learnt very carefully so I wouldn't forget. Then I decided to write things down too so that I can send my own report to you. I want to tell you everything from the beginning to the end,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 20:31; Acts 18:25; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> so that you don't miss anything and you can easily understand what happened. And also I want to tell you so you will know that what other people have already taught you is true.</span>
</div>
</div>

## About Gabriel speaking to Zechariah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:10; Matthew 2:1; Nehemiah 12:4"> Years ago, while Herod was king of Judea, there were many priests. They were all in groups named after the priests of long ago. Some were from Abijah's family, and there was one man called Zechariah who belonged to that group. His wife was Elizabeth, and her father was also a priest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:1; Genesis 17:1; Philippians 2:15"> Zechariah and Elizabeth were both good people. They obeyed all the laws of the Lord God, and God could see that they were good. They went on obeying God all the time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Genesis 18:11; 1 Samuel 1:2"> And now they were both old. They had no children. They wanted them, but Elizabeth couldn't have any.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 24:19; 2 Chronicles 31:2"> One day Zechariah was working in the temple. All the priests worked there but they took turns. First some of them worked and then others, and now the men from Abijah's family took their turn to work there. They had different kinds of work to do. Every day one of them burnt something like gum so that sweet-smelling smoke would rise from it as it burnt. He did this because long ago God had told the priests to do it for him. When they chose the man for that job, first of all they wrote all their names down on paper in any order. Then they took turns according to the list. And now it was Zechariah's turn and he burnt the gum in the building that only the priests went into and no one else.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:17; Revelation 8:3; Hebrews 4:14"> While Zechariah was burning the gum he was alone inside the building. The people who had come to the temple stood outside praying to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:26–27; Acts 10:3–4; Judges 13:3"> And while he was there alone, an angel of the Lord God came and appeared to him. Zechariah saw the angel standing on the right-hand side of the altar on which he was burning the gum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:22; Judges 6:22; Luke 2:9–10"> He got a fright and was very afraid.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:21; Acts 10:31; Genesis 25:21"> The angel said to him, “Don't be afraid, Zechariah. You have prayed to God and he has heard you. Later on your wife will have a son and you will call him John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:58; Proverbs 23:24; Genesis 21:6"> And you will be very happy because God has given you a son. Many other people will also be happy when he is born.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Numbers 6:2–4; Luke 7:33"> Then he said, “Your son will be very important. The Lord God will say, ‘He is a great man.’ When he grows up he mustn't drink wine or strong drink that makes people drunk. The Holy Spirit will fill him and control him. It will control him when he is born and it will stay with him all the time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Matthew 21:32; Malachi 3:1"> “He will teach people about the Lord their God. And when they hear him many of the people of Israel will stop doing wrong and do what is right. They will obey the Lord their God, just as their ancestors did long ago.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 4:5–6; Matthew 11:14; Luke 1:76"> “Yes,” the angel said to Zechariah, “your son will go ahead of the Lord and he will be very strong. God's Spirit that made Elijah strong long ago will make your son strong too. And when they hear him, fathers will love and care for their children again. And people who haven't obeyed God will change and start thinking straight. Then they will obey God in the same way as other people who think straight obey him. Many people will be waiting to see what the Lord God will do. And they will listen to him because they listened first to your son.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 38:22; Genesis 17:17; Luke 1:7"> Zechariah asked the angel, “How will I know this is true? I am an old man now and my wife is old too. How can this happen to us?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 1:26; Matthew 18:10; Daniel 9:21–23"> The angel answered, “I am Gabriel. I am God's helper and I stand near him all the time. He has sent me to you to tell you this good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:26; Ezekiel 24:27; Luke 1:22"> But because you haven't believed me you won't be able to speak until your son is born. You don't believe me, but what I am telling you today will happen. Your wife really will have a son.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27"> While Zechariah was still inside the building the people were waiting for him outside. They kept on waiting and waiting and said to themselves, “What is stopping him from coming out? He has been inside for a long time.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 1:62; John 13:24; Acts 21:40"> Then at last Zechariah came out, but he couldn't speak. He just moved his hands and the people said to themselves, “Maybe he has seen God in a vision.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; 2 Kings 11:5–7"> After a while the old man finished his work in the temple and he left Jerusalem and went home and stayed there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Five months went by and his wife Elizabeth kept away from people and hid herself in the house. Then she knew that she was pregnant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 30:22–23; Isaiah 54:1–4; Genesis 21:1–2"> and she said, “The Lord God has been kind to me, because I used to be ashamed of having no children. But I am not ashamed any more.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## About Gabriel speaking to Mary

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; Luke 1:19; John 7:41"> After another month the same angel called Gabriel went to Nazareth, a town in Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:16"> God sent him to a young girl called Mary. She was promised to a man called Joseph, but they hadn't been married yet. Joseph was a descendant of King David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 6:12; Daniel 10:19; Luke 1:30"> Gabriel said to Mary, “The Lord God is very pleased with you. He is with you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:17; Luke 1:12; Acts 10:4"> Mary was upset by his words and she said to herself, “What does it mean? Why has he told me that?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Romans 8:31; Luke 12:32"> Gabriel said to her, “Don't be afraid, because God is pleased with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:21; Matthew 1:25"> You will become pregnant and have a son and you will call him Jesus.</span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Daniel 2:44; Hebrews 1:8; Daniel 7:13–14"> He will rule over the descendants of Jacob forever.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Romans 4:20–21; Psalms 119:38; Psalms 116:16"> Mary said to him, “I have heard your message, and I am a servant of the Lord God, so I won't refuse.” Then the angel left her.</span>
</div>
</div>

## About Mary visiting Elizabeth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 1:65; Joshua 15:48–59; Joshua 10:40"> Mary wanted to go straight away to visit Elizabeth. So she left Nazareth and travelled south. She went up to the hill country of Judea and kept on going up until she found the place where Zechariah lived. When she arrived at his house she went in and she and Elizabeth talked together inside the house. Mary said to Elizabeth, “Are you well? May God be with you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 1:67; Acts 4:8; Luke 1:44"> When Elizabeth heard Mary's words, her baby moved inside her. And at the same time the Holy Spirit filled her and controlled her.</span>
</div>

So now you are the most important woman of all. And God will be pleased with the child too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 20:28; John 13:13; Luke 2:11"> You are the mother of my Lord, but you have come here to me today.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> “When I heard you, my child jumped for joy inside me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 20:29; John 11:40; Luke 11:27–28"> Good things will happen to you because you knew God's word was true and you trust him, because everything the Lord God has told you will surely happen.”</span>
</div>
</div>
