## About Jesus being born

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 24:14; Acts 25:21"> At that time in another place called Rome there was an important ruler called Augustus. He was very powerful and he controlled many different countries and ruled over many different people. Augustus decided to count the names of the people in all those countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 4:24; Acts 26:30"> So the rulers who were under him counted them all. One of them was called Quirinius and he governed in Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> All the people went to their own towns to write down their names.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:42; 1 Samuel 16:1"> So Joseph left the town of Nazareth in Galilee and went to his own town with his promised wife Mary, who was pregnant. They went to the town of Bethlehem in Judea. It was Joseph's town because his ancestors belonged there and long ago King David belonged to the same line as Joseph. So Joseph and Mary went from Galilee to Bethlehem to write their names down there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 33:11; Revelation 12:1–5; Micah 5:2"> They went a long way, and when they arrived in Bethlehem Mary was ready to have her baby, but there was nowhere for them to stay in any of the houses. So they stayed in a little building where they kept the animals. And Mary gave birth to her first son, and she wrapped him in some cloth and put him in the box used for the animals' food.</span>
</div>
</div>

## About the angel of the Lord God telling good news to the shepherds

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 31:39–40; Psalms 78:70–71; 1 Samuel 17:34–35"> At that time, in a place near Bethlehem, there were some men looking after their sheep. They stayed with them, taking care of them during the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; 2 Corinthians 3:18; 1 Kings 8:11"> And the Lord God sent an angel down from heaven, who appeared to them. God made the whole place shine with light, and the men were very frightened.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 52:7"> The angel said to them, “Don't be afraid, but listen to this news I am telling you, because I have come here to bring you good news. When you hear it and when others hear it too, you will all be full of joy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Matthew 1:21; Acts 2:36"> “This very day in David's town a little boy has been born. Long ago God told your ancestors that he would send you his Messiah, and now the Messiah has been born. He is the Lord and he will save you all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 53:1–2; Isaiah 7:11"> “You will find the baby wrapped in cloth and lying in a box, in a little building where they keep the animals. When you find him, you will know that this message is true.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 103:20–21; Revelation 5:11; Psalms 148:2"> Then the men saw many angels. They suddenly appeared in the sky and they all sang together, praising God</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 5:1; Luke 19:38; Colossians 1:20"> and saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Let us praise God who lives in heaven.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He is pleased with people who obey him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">So may they go in peace.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 2:1–2; Matthew 2:9–11"> Then they all went away, going back to heaven.<br />The men said to each other, “Let us go to Bethlehem to see the baby the Lord God has told us about.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:32; Luke 2:12; Luke 2:7"> And they hurried off.<br />They went to Bethlehem and found Joseph and Mary, and saw the baby lying in the box in the place where they kept the animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 66:16; Luke 8:39; Psalms 71:17–18"> They told them what the angel had said about the baby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 2:47; Luke 4:36; Luke 1:65–66"> Then they told other people as well, and when they heard it they were all very surprised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 2:51; Luke 1:66; Proverbs 4:4"> And Mary remembered all these things and kept thinking about them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 106:48; Isaiah 29:19; Acts 11:18"> Then the men went back to their sheep, praising God for the wonderful things they had seen and heard. They had found everything just as the angels had told them.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:31; Genesis 17:12; Galatians 4:4–5"> After eight days they circumcised the baby and called him Jesus. It was the same name the angel had called him before his mother became pregnant.</span>
</div>
</div>

## About the baby Jesus being taken to the temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:1–8"> Joseph and Mary and the baby Jesus stayed in Bethlehem for one month. Then Joseph and Mary went to Jerusalem, taking the baby with them. When they arrived, they went to the temple to give their baby to the Lord God, following the law that Moses had given to the people of Israel. First God told Moses and then Moses told the people God's laws. The Lord God said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“All your firstborn baby boys belong to me.”</span><br />He also said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“When a woman gives birth to a child she must take two doves, or if she hasn't any doves, then she must take two pigeons, and go to the temple and give them to the priest. She must say to him, ‘These two birds are for God.’ Then the priest will kill them.”</span><br />So Mary did that in the temple so she would be cleared from the law.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Luke 2:38; Luke 1:6"> In Jerusalem there lived an old man called Simeon. He was a very good man who always listened to God's word. He had been waiting for the Messiah, because the Messiah would set Israel free. God's Holy Spirit was with the old man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:51; Hebrews 11:5; Luke 9:27"> and had said to him before, “You won't die until you have seen the one the Lord God will send here called the Messiah.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 4:1; Luke 2:41; Luke 2:51"> Now the Spirit said to him, “Go to the temple now.” And so the old man went. While he was in the temple, Joseph and Mary arrived and Simeon met them there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 1:64; Luke 2:20; Luke 2:13–14"> The old man took the baby and held him in front of him. And he praised God and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Genesis 15:15; Philippians 1:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Lord, I have done your work now, and it is finished.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">So let me go and rest in peace, because your word is true.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 49:6; Luke 2:10–11; Luke 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let me go because now I have seen the one you sent to save your people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I have seen him with my own eyes.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 62:1–2; Isaiah 42:1–4; Psalms 98:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-2">You sent him so that all people everywhere could see him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–3; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 26:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will be like a light. He will show the people who don't belong to Israel what God wants them to do.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">When they see that light then they will praise your people of Israel.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 1:65–66; Isaiah 8:18; Luke 2:48"> Mary and Joseph were very surprised at the words the old man said about the child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 8:14–15"> Then Simeon prayed to God and asked him to bless Joseph and Mary and Jesus.<br />And Simeon said to Jesus' mother, “God has chosen this baby and sent him into the world so that some of us Israelite people will die but some will be saved. When he grows up he will teach people what God is like. But many people will reject him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Matthew 12:24–35"> At that time those people who reject God will no longer hide what is in their minds. But you will be very sad, just as though a sharp knife was pushed through your chest.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Genesis 30:13; Acts 21:9"> An old woman called Anna lived in the temple. She was always telling people God's word. Her father was Phanuel from the family of Asher. She was now 84 years old. A long time ago she and her husband had lived together for seven years, but her husband had died, so she had been a widow for a long time.<br />The old woman never left the temple. Day and night she praised the Lord God. Sometimes she ate food but sometimes she went without food and just prayed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 2:25; 2 Corinthians 9:15; Mark 15:43"> While Joseph and Mary and Jesus were still in the temple, the old woman came to where they were. When she saw the baby, she praised God for the birth of the child. From then on she talked about him. She kept on speaking about him to all the people who were waiting until God set the people of Jerusalem free.</span>
</div>
</div>

## About Joseph, Mary and Jesus going back to Nazareth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 2:4; Galatians 4:4–5; Deuteronomy 12:32"> Joseph and Mary finished everything they had to do in the temple to obey God's law. Then they returned to their town of Nazareth in Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 2:52; Luke 1:80; Isaiah 11:1–5"> The child Jesus grew and became strong and wise and God was pleased with him and stayed with him all the time.</span>
</div>
</div>

## About the boy Jesus going to the temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Numbers 28:16; Deuteronomy 12:18"> Many Israelite people used to go to Jerusalem every year at the end of the cold season. They went for the Passover ceremony, and Joseph and Mary used to go too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Jesus was twelve years old now, and Joseph and Mary took him with them to Jerusalem for the ceremony. They went with many other Jewish people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:21–23; Exodus 12:15; 2 Chronicles 25:17"> When the Passover ceremony had finished, Joseph and Mary left Jerusalem to go home to Nazareth. Many of their relatives went with them, but Jesus stayed in Jerusalem. His parents didn't know that he had stayed there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 122:1–4; Isaiah 2:3; Psalms 42:4"> They thought he was with some of the other people.<br />They kept on going through the day and then they looked for him, but Jesus wasn't with the others. They asked everyone if they had seen Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> but they said they hadn't seen him.<br />Then Joseph and Mary searched and searched, but they didn't find him. They slept until morning, and then they returned to Jerusalem. When they arrived there it became dark and they slept until morning. Then they began searching in Jerusalem, on the roads and in the buildings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 5:17; Isaiah 49:1–2; Isaiah 50:4"> And then they went back to the temple, and they found him there.<br />Jesus was sitting with the teachers of Moses' law. They were talking together, and Jesus was listening to them and asking them questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Luke 4:32; Psalms 119:99"> They also asked Jesus questions. When they heard his answers all the people there were very surprised because he was so wise and he gave them such good answers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 2:49; Matthew 12:46; Luke 3:23"> Joseph and Mary were very surprised when they saw Jesus sitting with the teachers. His mother said to him, “Son, why didn't you come with us? Your father and I have been very worried about you. We have been searching everywhere for you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 6:38; John 8:29; Malachi 3:1"> Jesus answered, “Why did you go looking everywhere else? You should have come here first. Didn't you know I would be busy here in my Father's house?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Luke 18:34; Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32"> But they didn't understand what Jesus was saying.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Daniel 7:28; Genesis 37:11"> So Jesus got up and left Jerusalem and went home to Nazareth with Joseph and Mary and he always obeyed them.<br />Mary kept on thinking about all the things she had seen and heard and kept them in her mind.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 2:40; Proverbs 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 2:26"> Jesus grew up and he became wise. God was pleased with him and people were pleased with him too.</span>
</div>
</div>
