## About Jonah praying to Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Lamentations 3:53–56; Psalms 130:1–2; Psalms 91:15"> Jonah prayed to Yahweh his Lord from inside the fish. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 18:4–6; Psalms 120:1; Psalms 34:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I was upset because I thought I would drown, and so I called to you to help me. And you heard me. When I was deep down in the sea in the place of dead people, I called to you and you heard me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Lamentations 3:54; Psalms 69:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">You threw me into the deep sea, right down to the bottom. The sea covered me and the waves were far above me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 31:22; Psalms 5:7; 2 Chronicles 6:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">I thought that you had driven me away forever and that I would never see your holy temple again.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Lamentations 3:54; Psalms 40:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sea covered me, it took my breath away, I couldn't breathe. And seaweed wrapped around my head.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 30:3; Psalms 16:10; Isaiah 38:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">I went on, the sea took me down, down into the deep. I kept on going down into the sea. I kept on and on until I was sitting down on the bottom of the sea. That place belongs to the dead. None of the dead can ever return from there. But you, Yahweh my Lord, brought me back alive from deep down in the sea.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; 2 Chronicles 30:27; Psalms 11:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I felt that I was dying, but I called to you. And you listened to me from your holy temple.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:6; 2 Kings 17:15; Jeremiah 2:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who are always thinking about useless idols don't worship you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 3:8; Psalms 50:14; Ecclesiastes 5:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I will sing songs to you and I will praise you. I said I would kill a sheep for you, so I will kill it so that you will know that I am always going to obey you. You alone, Yahweh, can save us.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 8:26–27; Psalms 33:9; Genesis 1:3"> Then Yahweh spoke to the fish and said, “Now go and take Jonah to the edge of the sea and spew him out there.” So the fish went away. It went on and on until it arrived at the edge of the sea where it spewed Jonah out. Then Jonah got up and went ashore on to dry land.</span>
</div>
