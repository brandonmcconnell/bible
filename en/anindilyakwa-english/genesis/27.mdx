## About Isaac giving a special blessing to Jacob

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:2; Genesis 48:10; Genesis 25:23–25"> Isaac was now an old man and he was blind. He called for his older son Esau and said to him, “Son!” “What is it?” he answered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 4:14; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Proverbs 27:1"> Isaac said, “You can see that I am old now. Maybe I will die soon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:27–28; Genesis 10:9; 1 Corinthians 6:12"> Take your spears and go out into the bush and find an animal for me. Kill one and bring it back here</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:20; Genesis 48:9; Genesis 49:28"> and cook it so that it will be tasty for me to eat. Then bring some here for me. Then I will eat it and give you my special blessing. I want to give it to you before I die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Rebecca heard what Isaac said to Esau. So when Esau went out to hunt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 25:28"> she said to Jacob, “I heard your father talking to Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 6:26; 1 Samuel 24:19; Deuteronomy 33:1"> He said, ‘Bring me an animal and cook it for me so I can eat it. Then I will give you my special blessing before I die, and Yahweh himself will hear it.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1; Genesis 27:13; Acts 5:29"> Look, my son,” Rebecca continued, “listen carefully and do everything I tell you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 13:15; 1 Samuel 16:20"> Go and find two of our fat young goats. Bring them here to me, and I will cook them nicely, just as your father likes his meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then you can take some to him to eat, and he will give you his special blessing before he dies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:25"> Jacob said to his mother, “You know my older brother is very hairy. My hands aren't hairy like his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:18; Genesis 25:27; Malachi 1:14"> Maybe if my father touches me he will find out it is me. Then he will know that I am not Esau but I am tricking him. So I won't get a special blessing, I will be cursed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; 1 Samuel 25:24; 2 Samuel 14:9"> His mother said, “If you are cursed, then you won't be in trouble, I will. Do as I say and bring the goats here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 27:9; Proverbs 23:2–3; Genesis 25:28"> So Jacob went and got the goats and brought them to his mother. And she cooked them just as Isaac liked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 27:27; Luke 20:46; Luke 15:22"> She went and got some good clothes of Esau's and gave them to Jacob and he put them on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then she put goat skin from the goat that Jacob had killed on his hands and arms and on the back of his neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then she gave him the meat and damper that she had cooked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Then Jacob went and took the food to his father and said, “Father!” “What is it?” his father said. “Which of my sons are you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:15; Matthew 26:70–74; Zechariah 13:3–4"> Jacob said, “I am your firstborn son Esau. I have done what you told me. Please sit up and eat this meat. I have brought it so that you can give me your special blessing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Job 13:7; Genesis 24:12"> The old man said, “Son! How did you find the animal so quickly?” Jacob said, “Yahweh your God helped me, so I found it quickly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 57:19; Genesis 27:12"> Isaac said, “Come closer and let me touch you. Are you really Esau?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Jacob came close to his father and Isaac touched him. Then he said, “When you speak your voice is like Jacob's, but your hands are like Esau's.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 27:16; Hebrews 11:20; Romans 9:11–12"> Isaac didn't recognize Jacob because his hands were all covered in goat skin. They were like Esau's. He was ready to give his special blessing to Jacob</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Ephesians 4:25; Proverbs 12:22"> but again he asked him, “Are you really Esau?” “Yes,” said Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 27:4"> Isaac said, “Give me the meat that you have brought. I will eat it first and then I will give you my special blessing.” Jacob gave his father the meat, and also brought some wine for him to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then his father said, “Come over here and kiss me, son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 6:7; Psalms 65:10; Hebrews 11:20"> Jacob moved closer to kiss him and his father smelled Esau's clothes that Jacob was wearing. And so he gave him his special blessing. He said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“My son, you smell very good,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">just as the earth that God made smells good.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:28; Deuteronomy 33:13; Psalms 133:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">May God give you dew from heaven,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and then your plants will grow so that you will have plenty of food.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May he give you plenty of seeds for food and plenty of wine.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Numbers 24:9; Isaiah 49:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">May nations work for you as servants.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">May they say, ‘You are a great person,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and bow down to you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May you rule over your family.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May they also bow down to you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If people curse you, may they be cursed too.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">But if they speak good words to you, may good come to them too.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Isaac finished his special blessing and Jacob left him. About Esau wanting his father's special blessing At the same time Jacob's older brother Esau came back bringing an animal that he had killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 27:4"> He cooked the meat so that it was tasty and took it to his father and said, “Father, you asked me for some meat. Here it is now. Please sit up and eat it. Then give me your special blessing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 27:18"> “Who are you?” Isaac asked. Esau answered, “I am your firstborn son, Esau.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 28:3–4; Romans 11:29; Ephesians 1:3"> The old man was very upset and began to shake all over. He said, “Someone has killed an animal and brought me the meat before you. I have eaten it and given him my special blessing already. I can't take back those words. That blessing is his forever. Who was that person?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Proverbs 1:31; 1 Samuel 30:4"> When Esau heard that he was very angry. He cried out loudly and said, “Father, give me a special blessing too.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:6; Job 13:7; Malachi 2:10"> Isaac said, “Your brother came here and tricked me. So I gave him my special blessing. It should have been yours because you are the firstborn, but now it is your brother's.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 25:26; John 1:47; Genesis 32:28"> Esau said, “I am your firstborn son, but he tricked me before, and so now he is like your firstborn son. And now he has done it again. You should have given me that special blessing, but he got it. Oh dear, it is his now! Twice he has tricked me. Truly he is called Jacob. Please, isn't there a blessing for me too?” The name Jacob means “he tricks people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; Genesis 27:28–29; Genesis 25:23"> Isaac said, “I have already made him rule over you. I told him that his relatives would work for him as servants. I told him he would have plenty of seeds for food and plenty of wine. There is no more, my son. The blessing is finished, so there is nothing for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Genesis 27:34; Isaiah 65:14"> Esau kept on asking and asking him. “Isn't there just one more special blessing? Isn't there also a word for me? Please give me a special word too.” And Esau was upset and began to cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 27:28; Hebrews 11:20; Joshua 24:4"> Isaac said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The dew won't fall from heaven to water your land.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The earth will not be good for you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Kings 8:20–22; Genesis 25:23; 2 Chronicles 25:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will be a brave fighter.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For a while you will work for your younger brother.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then later you will escape so that he won't rule over you.”</span></span>
</div>

## About Jacob leaving home

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 John 3:12–15; Genesis 37:4; Genesis 50:3–4"> Then Esau really hated his brother Jacob, because his father had given him his special blessing. He said, “In a short time my father will die. Then I will kill Jacob.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 37:18–20; 1 Samuel 30:5; Genesis 42:21–22"> Rebecca heard what Esau said and called Jacob to her and said, “Look, my son. Your brother wants to pay you back and kill you because of what you have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 27:8; Genesis 11:31; Genesis 27:13"> Listen to me! Go at once to Haran. Go to my brother Laban,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 31:38; Genesis 31:41"> and stay there with him for a while. Later your brother will forget what you have done and he will calm down. Then I will send someone to bring you back. If you don't go away, maybe both of you will die on the one day. And then both my sons will be gone!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Genesis 24:3; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 28:8"> Then she went to her husband Isaac and said, “Those women from here that Esau married don't belong to us; they are Hittites. I am tired of them. If Jacob also takes one of them for a wife, then what good will that do me?”</span>
