## About God promising to give Abraham a son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 14:13; Genesis 48:3; Genesis 13:18"> Abraham stayed by the special trees belonging to Mamre. And Yahweh came and appeared to him again there. In the middle of the day Abraham was sitting at the doorway of his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Genesis 18:22; Genesis 19:1"> He looked up and saw three men standing there. He got up quickly and ran to them and bowed down and put his face to the ground, because people in that place did that in those days when strangers arrived at their homes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 32:5"> Abraham said to them, “Don't go. Stay here for a while and I will look after you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 24:32; Genesis 19:2; Genesis 43:24"> Let me bring some water for you to wash your feet. This is a good shady tree. Come and sit here to rest for a little while.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Judges 13:15; Judges 6:18"> I will bring some food for you to eat to make you strong when you go on your way. I am very pleased that you have come to my place, so let me look after you.” They answered, “Yes, what you are doing for us is good.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 13:33; Hebrews 13:2; Acts 16:15"> Abraham hurried off and ran inside to Sarah and said, “Quickly, take the best flour and make dampers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Malachi 1:14; Luke 15:27; Luke 15:30"> Then he ran outside again and chose a good, fat calf for them to eat, and gave it to his servant, who killed it and cooked it. When the meat was cooked and ready to eat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:3; Judges 13:15; Revelation 3:20"> Abraham took it with some milk and cream and other food, and brought it to the three men. There in the shade of the tree he himself looked after them while they ate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 2:5; Genesis 24:67; Genesis 4:9"> Then they asked Abraham, “Where is your wife Sarah?” He answered, “She is there inside.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 21:2; Genesis 17:21; Romans 9:8–9"> One of the men was Yahweh and he said to Abraham, “In nine months I will come back here, and your wife will have a son.” Sarah was behind him. She was inside the tent, behind the door, listening to the men talking with Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; Hebrews 11:11–12; Luke 1:36"> Abraham and his wife were both very old now and Sarah's monthly periods had stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:6; Genesis 17:17; Luke 1:18–20"> So Sarah laughed to herself and thought, “How can I have a child? Now that I am an old woman, I am weak. How can I do that? My husband is already an old man. How can we have a child?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 2:25"> Yahweh said to Abraham, “Why did your wife laugh? Why did she say, ‘Am I really going to have a child, when I am an old woman?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:37; Matthew 19:26; Mark 10:27"> I am Yahweh and I can do anything. I have already said, ‘In nine months I will come back and Sarah will have a son.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 1:8; Proverbs 28:13; Ephesians 4:23"> Sarah heard what Yahweh said from inside the tent. She was afraid, and she said, “I didn't laugh.” “Yes, you did,” Yahweh answered. “You laughed.”</span>
</div>

## About Abraham asking Yahweh about Sodom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 15:3; Acts 21:5; Acts 20:38"> Then the three men left and Abraham went with them. They went up a hill and looked down from there towards Sodom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Psalms 25:14; John 15:15"> And Yahweh said to himself, “I will tell this man what I am going to do. I won't hide it from Abraham, I will speak about it now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Genesis 26:4; Galatians 3:14"> His descendants will become a great nation. I will bless all the nations in the same way I am blessing him today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:6–7; Deuteronomy 4:9–10; 1 Timothy 3:4–5"> I have chosen Abraham so that he can tell his sons to obey me. Then they in their turn will tell their sons to obey me and do what is right. If they obey me I will bless them as I have promised Abraham.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 19:13; Ezekiel 16:49–50; Isaiah 3:9"> Then Yahweh spoke to Abraham and said, “I have been told that those people of Sodom and Gomorrah are very wicked. They are doing bad things all the time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Genesis 11:5; Zephaniah 1:12"> I must go down and see if the things I have heard are true.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 19:1; Acts 7:55; Ezekiel 22:30"> Then the two men left and went on down the hill and over towards Sodom. But Yahweh stayed there with Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; 2 Samuel 24:17; Genesis 20:4"> Abraham asked Yahweh, “Are you really going to destroy the wicked people and the good people too?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:1; Isaiah 1:9; Matthew 7:13–14"> If there are fifty good people in the city, will you destroy all the people? Won't you leave the city as it is to save the good people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 8:3; Isaiah 3:10–11; Job 8:20"> You won't kill them all, the good and the bad. Surely not! You can't do that! You can't punish good people. You are the one who must judge everyone, and so you must do what is right.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:1; Matthew 24:22; Isaiah 65:8"> Yahweh answered, “If I find fifty good people in Sodom today, I will leave their city alone for their sake.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Isaiah 6:5; 1 Corinthians 15:47–48"> Then Abraham spoke again. He said, “I am bold speaking to you, Lord, but please let me say a few more words. I am only an ordinary man. I am not good enough to speak to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 18:29; 1 Kings 20:32–33; Genesis 18:26"> But maybe there will only be forty-five good people. If there aren't five more, will you destroy the whole city?” Yahweh answered, “No. I will not destroy the city today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Hebrews 4:16"> Then Abraham spoke again. He said, “Maybe there will only be forty good people. Then what will you do?” Yahweh answered, “Then I will not destroy the city today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 89:7; Psalms 9:12; Esther 4:11–16"> Abraham said, “Please don't be angry, Lord, but let me speak again. Maybe there will only be thirty good people. Then what will you do?” Yahweh answered, “Then I will not destroy the city today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 11:8; Genesis 18:27; Luke 18:1"> Abraham said, “I am bold still speaking to you, Lord, but please let me say more. Maybe there will only be twenty good people. Then what will you do?” Yahweh answered, “Then I will not destroy the city today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="James 5:15–17; Judges 6:39; Matthew 7:7"> Abraham said, “Please don't be angry, Lord, and I will speak just once more. Maybe there will only be ten good people. Then what will you do?” Yahweh answered, “If there are ten good people I will not destroy the city.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 18:22; Genesis 32:26; Genesis 31:55"> Then Yahweh finished speaking and left Abraham, and Abraham went home.</span>
</div>
</div>
