They are over in Goshen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:13; Colossians 1:28; Jude 1:24"> But I have brought some of my brothers here with me to meet you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:33–34; 2 Thessalonians 3:10; Genesis 4:2"> The king said to them, “What work do you do?”<br />They answered, “We are shepherds just as our ancestors were.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:34; Genesis 15:13; Deuteronomy 26:5"> We have come to live in this country, because there is very little food in Canaan, and there is no grass for our sheep and cattle. Please give us permission to live in Goshen.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The king said to Joseph, “Now that your father and your brothers have arrived,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Proverbs 22:29; Genesis 47:11"> the land of Egypt is theirs. Let them settle in Goshen, the best part of Egypt. And if there are any good workers among them, let them look after my sheep and cattle.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 47:10; 2 Samuel 8:10; 1 Samuel 2:20"> Then Joseph went back to his father and brought him to the king. Jacob came up to the king and said, “May God bless you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The king asked him, “How old are you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 39:12; Psalms 39:5; Job 14:1"> Jacob answered, “I have been wandering for 130 years until now. But those years have been hard and few. I have not lived like my ancestors. They lived good, long years as they wandered from place to place.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 47:7; Psalms 129:8; Hebrews 7:7"> Before Jacob left the king, he said again, “May God bless you,” and then he went away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 47:6; Exodus 1:11; Exodus 12:37"> Then Joseph gave his father and his brothers the best place to live in, as the king had told him. It was near the city called Rameses. He also gave food to his father, his brothers and to all the rest of his family, from the oldest to the youngest. He helped his family there in Egypt so that they could settle down.</span>
</div>
</div>

## About there being very little food

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:11; 1 Kings 18:5; Lamentations 4:9"> There was no food because those years were so bad. All the people in Egypt and Canaan were hungry and very weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 41:56; Luke 16:10–12; Luke 16:1–2"> They came from everywhere to Joseph to buy food. Joseph gave them the food that the Egyptians had put in the storehouses before, when there was plenty of food in Egypt, and he took their money to the palace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 8:8; Psalms 37:3; Matthew 6:11"> This went on for some time until all the money in Egypt and Canaan was spent. Then the Egyptians came to Joseph and asked for food. “Please give us food,” they said. “Don't let us starve to death. Our money is all gone, so you must help us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; Daniel 6:5–7; 1 Corinthians 10:32"> Joseph said to them, “Bring your horses and sheep and other animals here to me. Because you haven't any money, give them to me so that I can give you food.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 2:4; Exodus 9:3; Isaiah 31:1"> So they brought their animals to him and he gave them food. That year he gave them food and they gave him their horses and sheep and goats and cattle and donkeys.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 6:26; Jeremiah 38:9"> The next year the Egyptians came to Joseph and said, “Our money is all gone, and all our animals are yours now. We can't hide this and we are telling the truth. We have nothing left to give you. There are only our bodies and our land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:2–3; Job 2:4; Lamentations 1:11"> But please help us and don't let us die. And don't let our land lie empty. Buy us and our land and then you can give us food today. We will be the king's workers and all our land will belong to him too. Give us food now so we won't die. And give us seeds so we can grow food, so the land won't stay empty any longer.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So Joseph bought all the land in Egypt for the king. But those years were still bad, and one by one the Egyptian people gave their land to the king, until it all belonged to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 41:48"> Joseph bought all the Egyptian people and they all became the king's workers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezra 7:24; Nehemiah 13:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:10"> The only land he did not buy was the land that belonged to the priests. They did not have to give their land because the king gave them money to buy food, but he didn't give money to anyone else.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 112:5; Psalms 41:1; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> Joseph said to the Egyptians, “Look, I have bought you and your land for the king. Here are seeds for you to plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 41:34; Psalms 41:1; Psalms 112:5"> Later on when the plants have grown and you cut them down you must give some to the king. If you have five bags of food you will give one to him. The rest will be for you and your families to eat and for seed to plant later.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 33:15; Proverbs 11:26–27; Genesis 18:3"> They answered, “You have saved our lives and you have been good to us. So we will be the king's workers.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 47:22; Ezekiel 7:24"> So Joseph made a law for all the people of Egypt about their land. He said, “Every year when you collect the food you have grown, you must give some to the king. If you have five bags of food, you must give him one, and if you have ten bags you must give him two. You must do that every year.” This is still the law today. All the land of Egypt belongs to the king, except the land of the priests.</span>
</div>
</div>

## About Jacob giving his last words to Joseph

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 46:3; Exodus 1:7; Acts 7:17"> The Israelite people lived in Egypt in the part called Goshen. There they became rich with food and animals and other things. And they had many children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 90:10; Genesis 47:8–9; Genesis 37:2"> And Jacob lived in Egypt for 17 years. But he was now an old man, 147 years old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:14; Genesis 24:49; Genesis 24:2"> When it was nearly time for him to die he called for his son Joseph and he came to him.<br />Jacob said to him, “Put your hand between my legs, so that you can make this promise and you won't change it. Tell me that you will not bury me here in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 49:29–32; Genesis 23:19; Genesis 25:9"> I want you to bury me where my father and grandfather are buried. So carry me there to Canaan.”<br />Joseph answered, “Yes, I will do as you say.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 1:47; Hebrews 11:21; Genesis 24:3"> Jacob said, “Promise me and don't change it.”<br />Joseph put his hand between his father's legs and said, “I am making this promise. When you die, I will take you to Canaan and bury you there.”<br />When Jacob heard his son say that, he thanked God, there where he lay on his bed.</span>
</div>
</div>
