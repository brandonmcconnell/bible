<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

Abraham bought the land and the cave so that he could put his dead people in the cave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; Genesis 35:29; Genesis 50:13"> They put Abraham and his wife Sarah in it and later they put Isaac and his wife Rebecca there too. And I buried Leah there.</span>
