## About circumcision being a sign of the covenant that Yahweh made

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Ephesians 3:20; Luke 1:6"> When Abram was 99 years old, Yahweh came and appeared to him and said, “I am the most powerful God, and you must always obey me. You must always do what is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 17:4–6; Genesis 12:2; Psalms 105:8–11"> Let us make a covenant together. I am making this covenant with you so that it will last forever. I will give you many descendants.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 13:20; Genesis 17:17; Numbers 16:45"> Abram bowed down and put his face to the ground to worship God. And God said to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Galatians 3:28–29; Genesis 12:2"> “I am making this covenant with you and I will not change it. I am telling you that you will be the ancestor of many different people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 1:21–23; Nehemiah 9:7; Genesis 17:15"> From today your name will not be Abram but now you will be called Abraham. I am giving you this new name because you will be the ancestor of many nations.” God said that because Abram means “great father,” but Abraham means “father of many people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 17:4; Matthew 1:6–17; Genesis 35:11"> And God said, “I will give you many descendants. Some of them will be kings in their countries. Your descendants will become so many that they will become nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:7–9; Acts 2:39; Genesis 26:24"> “I won't change my covenant that I have made. It will last forever, for you and also for your descendants. I will be your God today and always and I will be the God of your descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 26:12; Psalms 105:11; Exodus 6:7"> You are living as a stranger here today. But I will give this land to you and your descendants. Even though it belongs to other people today, it will be yours later. The whole land of Canaan will belong to your descendants forever. And I will be their God forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 56:4–5; Psalms 25:10; Psalms 103:18"> Then God said to Abraham, “You must keep my covenant that I have made with you and agree to it. You must not change it and your descendants must not change it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 2:11–12; Romans 4:9–11; Acts 7:8"> You must promise to circumcise all your men, and your descendants must do the same and circumcise every male person. You must all agree to do this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 4:11; Acts 7:8; Deuteronomy 10:16"> From today you must circumcise your sons when they are eight days old. Not only your children, but those you have bought to work for you as servants, they too must circumcise their sons. It doesn't matter if they were born in your house or in a different country, they must do the same. Then when you do that, everyone will know that you and I have made this covenant together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:44; Genesis 37:36; Nehemiah 5:8"> “Don't circumcise just some of your men but circumcise them all. Then if you do that, it will be a sign that the covenant you and I have made together won't change but it will be forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 4:24–26; Numbers 15:30–31; Exodus 12:19"> Then the men who haven't been circumcised won't be my people, because they haven't kept my covenant that I have made.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:25; Genesis 32:28; Genesis 17:5"> Then God said to Abraham, “You must no longer call your wife Sarai, but from today you must call her Sarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Genesis 17:6; Isaiah 49:23"> I will bless her. And I will give you a son, and this son of yours will be Sarah's son. I will bless Sarah and she will be the mother of nations. Some of her descendants will become kings and rule in their countries.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 11:16; Genesis 21:6; Genesis 18:12"> Then Abraham bowed down and put his face to the ground to praise God. But he laughed because he thought, “How can I have children when I am already 100 years old? And how can Sarah have a child when she is 90 years old? She is a very old woman already!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 2:39; Psalms 41:12; Psalms 4:6"> He asked God, “Why can't Ishmael have my things when I die?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 1:13–20; Genesis 21:2–3; 2 Kings 4:16–17"> But God said, “No! Your wife Sarah will give birth to a baby boy for you and you will call him Isaac.” God said that because Isaac means “he laughs.” Then he said, “The covenant that I have made with you will be for him too, and it will never change. It will last forever, for him and for his descendants forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:18; Genesis 21:13; Genesis 25:12–18"> I have heard what you asked me about Ishmael, and so I will bless him too, and I will also give him many children and many descendants. His twelve sons will be kings who will rule over their countries. All his many descendants will be one great nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 21:2–3; Genesis 26:2–5; Galatians 3:29"> “As for Isaac, Sarah will give birth to him next year. Then I will keep my covenant, the one that you and I have made, with Isaac in his turn.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 18:33; Genesis 35:9–15; Judges 6:21"> When God finished speaking to Abraham, he left him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; Genesis 18:19; Genesis 17:10–14"> On that same day Abraham obeyed God and circumcised his son Ishmael and all the other men and boys living in his house. He circumcised his servants, those he had bought, and those who were born in his house, and all the children of the servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 4:11; Genesis 17:17; Romans 4:19–20"> Abraham was 99 years old at the time when they circumcised him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and his son Ishmael was thirteen years old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; Genesis 22:3–4; Genesis 12:4"> They circumcised them both on the one day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 18:19"> and also all the servants who stayed with Abraham.</span>
</div>
