## About Judah and Tamar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1; 2 Kings 4:8; Proverbs 13:20"> At that time Judah left his brothers and went to stay with a man called Hirah who lived in a town called Adullam. They were friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3; Genesis 24:3; Genesis 6:2"> There Judah met a Canaanite girl whose father was called Shua, and he married her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> She had a son and his father named him Er.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> Later she became pregnant again and had another son and named him Onan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; Genesis 38:26; Genesis 46:12"> Later she had another son and named him Shelah. But when the baby was born his father was staying in a place called Achzib.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:3; Genesis 21:21; Matthew 1:3"> When Judah's oldest son Er grew up, his father got a wife for him called Tamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3; Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> But Er was not a good man, he kept on doing evil things. Yahweh did not like what he was doing and so he killed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Matthew 22:23–27; Ruth 4:5–11"> Then Judah spoke to Er's younger brother Onan. He said, “Now that your brother has died, go to his widow and sleep with her so that you can have children for him. Then you can bring them up for him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Deuteronomy 25:6; James 3:16"> But Onan said to himself, “If I have children with this widow they won't be mine. They will belong to my dead brother.” So when he went and slept with her he didn't want to have his brother's children, and he let his semen spill on the ground and he didn't make Tamar pregnant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 11:1; 1 Chronicles 21:7"> But Yahweh didn't like what he had done and so he killed Onan too in the same way he had killed his older brother Er.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 22:13; Ruth 1:11–13"> So now Judah had only one son left alive, and he was afraid that Shelah would be killed like his two brothers.<br />So he said to his daughter-in-law Tamar, “Go back to your father's house and stay there as a widow. Then when my youngest son Shelah grows up you can marry him.” So Tamar went back alone to her father's house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; 2 Samuel 13:39; Judges 14:1"> After some time Judah's wife also died and he was very sad and stayed at home. When he was ready to go out again, Judah decided to go to a place called Timnah because his sheep were there. So he went there with his friend Hirah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Someone said to Tamar, “Your father-in-law is coming to Timnah to cut the wool from his sheep.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 38:26; Genesis 38:11–13; Jeremiah 3:2"> Tamar knew that Judah's youngest son Shelah had grown up now. But Judah had still not given him to her as her husband. He had not kept his promise. Tamar was still wearing her widow's clothes. So she took them off and put on other clothes and covered her face with some material. Then she went to a place called Enaim and sat down near the gate. Enaim was on the road that went to Timnah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Later Judah saw her there and thought she was a woman who slept with other men because she had covered her face. Those kinds of women used to cover their faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 26:15; 2 Samuel 13:11"> So Judah didn't know it was his daughter-in-law Tamar.<br />He went over to her at the side of the road and said, “Come on, how much money do you want?”<br />She said, “What will you give me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:33; Genesis 38:20; Proverbs 20:16"> He answered, “I will send you a young goat afterwards.”<br />“Yes,” she said, “but give me something of yours now to keep until you give me the goat, so that you don't forget.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 38:25–26; Hosea 4:11; Jeremiah 22:24"> He asked, “What can I lend you?”<br />She said, “That cord of yours with your name and the walking stick you are carrying.” So he gave them to her and they slept together and he made her pregnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 38:14; 2 Samuel 14:5; 2 Samuel 14:2"> Then Tamar went home and uncovered her face and put on her widow's clothes again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 20:9; Leviticus 19:17; 2 Samuel 13:3"> Judah gave a young goat to his friend Hirah. He said, “Take this to that woman and get that stick and the cord with my name that I gave her.” Hirah went to Enaim but he couldn't find the woman.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He asked some men there, “Where is that bad woman who was sitting at the side of the road?”<br />They answered, “There have never been any women who sleep with other men in this place. And there aren't any here today.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Judah's friend went back to him and said, “I couldn't find her. The men there told me that there are no bad women in that place.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; Proverbs 6:33; Revelation 16:15"> Judah said, “Let her keep the things. We don't want people to laugh at us. I wanted to give her the goat but you couldn't find her.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 21:9; Ezekiel 23:19; Judges 19:2"> Three months later someone said to Judah, “Your daughter-in-law has been sleeping around and now she is pregnant.”<br />Judah said to them, “Take her away and burn her to death.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 37:32; Genesis 38:18; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> They were taking her away to burn her, but on the way she sent the things to her father-in-law. And she sent him a message saying, “I am pregnant today, but just look at these two things. Who do you think they belong to? The man who made me pregnant gave them to me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:17; John 8:9; Titus 2:11–12"> When Judah saw the things he said, “They are mine. I didn't give her my son Shelah. I should have given my son to her and I didn't do it. It is my fault and not hers.” And Judah never slept with Tamar again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 25:24"> The time came for Tamar to give birth, but she had two babies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> While she was in labour one of them put out an arm. The woman who was helping Tamar caught it and tied some red cotton round it so that she would know that it was the first. She said, “This one was born first.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:4; Matthew 1:3; Genesis 46:12"> But the baby pulled his arm back and the other one was born first. The woman said to him, “You have got out by yourself!” So he was named Perez, because Perez means “he got out by himself.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:6; 1 Chronicles 2:4; Matthew 1:3"> Then his younger brother with the red cotton was born and he was named Zerah, because Zerah means “red.”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
