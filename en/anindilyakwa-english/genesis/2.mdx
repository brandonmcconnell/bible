<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:4; Exodus 31:17; Deuteronomy 5:14"> He worked for six days from when he started until he finished it all, and then he stopped working.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:12; Mark 2:27; Leviticus 23:3"> Then he said, “This seventh day I am resting. So this day is different. It will be separate from the other days, because I have rested from my work today. This is my holy day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 5:1; Revelation 1:8; Genesis 1:4"> That is how God made the world. About Eden<br />At first when Yahweh God made the world,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 3:23; Hebrews 6:7; Job 5:10"> there were no plants on the land and no seeds had started to grow. And he hadn't yet made the rain fall on the ground. And there were no people to grow trees and plants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But water kept coming up out of the ground and it watered the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 33:4; 1 Corinthians 15:45"> Then Yahweh God took some soil and made a man from it. He blew his breath into the man's nose so that he could live. And the man came alive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Genesis 3:24; Genesis 13:10"> Then Yahweh God planted trees and other plants in a place called Eden, in the east. And there he put the man he had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 3:22; Revelation 2:7; Revelation 22:14"> It was a beautiful place because Yahweh God had planted all kinds of trees that were lovely to look at, and that provided good fruit. In the middle of all those fruit trees and other plants Yahweh God planted one tree that gives life and one tree that gives knowledge about what is good and what is bad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Psalms 46:4"> A river flowed in Eden and watered the ground. It flowed on further and divided into four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Genesis 10:29; 1 Samuel 15:7"> One of the rivers is called Pishon, the one that flows through the land called Havilah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 28:20; Numbers 11:7; Ezekiel 28:13"> In that place there is beautiful gold under the ground and other precious stones and also sweet-smelling perfume.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:6; Isaiah 11:11"> Another river called Gihon flows through the land of Cush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 10:4; Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 1:7"> Another river called Tigris flows to the east through the land of Assyria and further on. And another river is called Euphrates. They are the four rivers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Genesis 2:8; Ephesians 4:28"> Yahweh God put the man there in Eden so that he could grow food and look after the place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; 1 Timothy 4:4; 1 Timothy 6:17"> He said to him, “You can eat the fruit from any of the trees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:23; James 1:15; Genesis 3:19"> except one. This tree gives knowledge about what is good and what is bad and you mustn't eat its fruit. If you do eat it you will die the same day.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:9–12; 1 Corinthians 11:7–12; Proverbs 18:22"> Then Yahweh God said, “It is not good for this man to live alone. So I will make a helper to be with him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Genesis 1:28; Genesis 9:2"> So Yahweh God took some soil and made animals and birds out of it. He brought the animals and birds to the man so that he could name them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:18"> So the man named the animals and the birds, but none of them was a good helper for him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:12; Job 4:13; 1 Samuel 26:12"> Then Yahweh God made the man go to sleep. He went fast asleep and while he was sleeping Yahweh God took a rib from him and closed up his flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:8–9; 1 Timothy 2:13; Hebrews 13:4"> He made a woman out of the rib and he brought her to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 5:28–30; 1 Corinthians 11:8–9; 2 Samuel 5:1"> The man said, “Ah! Here is someone like me! My bone is in her, and also my flesh. Yahweh God has made this person from a man today, so her name is ‘Woman.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 19:3–9; Ephesians 5:28–31; Mark 10:6–12"> So because of that a man will leave his father and his mother to live with his wife, and then they will become one.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 3:7; Genesis 3:10–11; Romans 10:11"> The man and the woman were both naked, but they weren't ashamed.</span>
</div>
