## About Esau's descendants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; 1 Chronicles 1:35; Deuteronomy 23:7"> These are the names of Esau's descendants. He had two names, Esau and Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 36:25; Genesis 28:9; Genesis 9:25"> Esau married Canaanite women. One wife was Adah, the daughter of Elon the Hittite. Another wife was Oholibamah, the daughter of Anah and the granddaughter of Zibeon the Hivite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 28:9; Genesis 25:13"> Another wife was Basemath. She was Ishmael's daughter and the sister of Nebaioth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:35; Exodus 2:18; Job 2:11"> Adah and Esau had one son Eliphaz. Basemath and Esau had one son Reuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 35:29"> Oholibamah and Esau had three sons called Jeush, Jalam and Korah. These sons of Esau's were born in Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 32:3; Genesis 12:5; Genesis 17:8"> Then Esau went away taking his wives and sons and daughters and the people who were living with him. He also took his cattle and all his belongings that he had got in Canaan. He went away to live separately from Jacob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 13:6; Genesis 17:8; Genesis 28:4"> because the land where he and Jacob were living was not big enough and they had too many cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:3; Malachi 1:3; Ezekiel 35:2–7"> So Esau went to Edom and lived there in the hill country with his family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 19:37"> Esau was the ancestor of the Edomite people, and these are the names of his descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 36:3–4; 1 Chronicles 1:35–54"> His five sons were called Eliphaz, the son of Adah, Reuel the son of Basemath, and Jeush, Jalam and Korah, the three sons of Oholibamah. Oholibamah's father was Anah and her grandfather was Zibeon. The six sons of Eliphaz were called Teman, Omar, Zepho, Gatam, Kenaz and Amalek. Amalek's mother was a woman called Timna. The four sons of Reuel were called Nahath, Zerah, Shammah and Mizzah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Obadiah 1:9; Job 2:11; Genesis 36:11–12"> Esau was the ancestor of many people. His first son Eliphaz was the father of the leaders called Teman, Omar, Zepho, Kenaz,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 15:15"> Gatam and Amalek. Their grandmother was Esau's wife Adah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 36:13; 1 Chronicles 1:37; Genesis 36:4"> Esau's son Reuel was the father of the leaders called Nahath, Zerah, Shammah and Mizzah. Their grandmother was Esau's wife Basemath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 36:14; 1 Chronicles 1:35; Genesis 36:5"> Esau's sons Jeush, Jalam and Korah were also leaders and their mother was Esau's wife Oholibamah, the daughter of Anah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The sons and grandsons of Esau were all leaders of separate tribes.</span>
</div>
</div>

## About Seir's descendants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:12; Deuteronomy 2:22; Genesis 14:6"> Seir was a Horite and his sons lived in the hill country of Edom. Their names were Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Dishon, Ezer and Dishan. These sons of Seir were the leaders of the Horite people who lived in Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:39"> Lotan's two sons were called Hori and Heman. And Lotan had a sister called Timna.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:40"> Shobal's five sons were Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho and Onam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:10; 2 Samuel 18:9; 1 Kings 4:28"> Zibeon's two sons were Aiah and Anah. Anah was the man who found the hot springs in the desert, when he was looking after his father's donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 36:18; 1 Chronicles 1:41"> Anah's son was called Dishon, and his daughter was called Oholibamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:41"> Dishon's four sons were called Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran and Cheran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:42; Genesis 36:21; 1 Chronicles 1:38"> The three sons of Ezer were Bilhan, Zaavan and Akan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Lamentations 4:21; Job 1:1; Jeremiah 25:20"> The two sons of Dishan were Uz and Aran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 36:20; Genesis 36:28; 1 Chronicles 1:41–42"> These men were the leaders of the Horite people, Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer and Dishan. They ruled separately over their people in the country of Edom.</span>
</div>
</div>

## About the kings of Edom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 17:6; 1 Chronicles 1:43–50; Genesis 17:16"> Before there were any kings in Israel, these kings ruled over the Edomite people in turn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> The first was Bela, the son of Beor. His city was Dinhabah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:13; Jeremiah 49:22; Amos 1:12"> After he died, Jobab, the son of Zerah, took his place. He came from Bozrah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 36:11; Job 2:11; Genesis 36:15"> After he died, Husham, a Temanite, took his place and ruled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> After he died, Hadad, the son of Bedad, ruled. His city was called Avith. When Hadad was fighting the Midianite people in Moab, his soldiers drove away the Midianites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:47"> After Hadad died, Samlah from Masrekah ruled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:48; Genesis 10:11"> After he died, Shaul ruled. He came from Rehoboth on the river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> After he died, Baal-Hanan, the son of Achbor, ruled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 15:15; 1 Chronicles 1:50"> After he died, Hadad from Pau ruled. His wife was called Mehetabel, and her father was Matred and her grandfather was Mezahab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:51–54; Exodus 15:15; Genesis 36:31"> Esau was the ancestor of the Edomite people, and these were their rulers, Timna, Alvah, Jetheth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Oholibamah, Elah, Pinon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Kenaz, Teman, Mizbar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 45:8; 1 Chronicles 4:14; Genesis 25:30"> Magdiel and Iram. The country where they lived was named after them.</span>
</div>
</div>
