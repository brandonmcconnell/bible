## About Adam's descendants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Colossians 3:10; Genesis 1:26–27"> When God created people, he made them like himself. He created them male and female. Then he blessed them and said, “You will be called ‘people’.” These are the names of Adam's descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 4:25; Job 25:4; Psalms 51:5"> When Adam was 130 years old, he had a son who was like himself and he called him Seth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:1–3; Luke 3:36–38; Genesis 5:26"> After that Adam lived another 800 years and he had other children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 5:8; Genesis 5:11; Genesis 3:19"> Then when he was 930 years old he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 4:26"> When Seth was 105 years old he had a son called Enosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> After that he lived another 807 years and he had other children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then when he was 912 years old he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 3:37; 1 Chronicles 1:2"> When Enosh was 90 years old he had a son called Kenan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> After that he lived another 815 years and he had other children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then when he was 905 years old he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 3:37"> When Kenan was 70 years old he had a son called Mahalalel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> After that he lived another 840 years and he had other children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Then when he was 910 years old he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:2"> When Mahalalel was 65 years old he had a son called Jared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> After that he lived another 830 years and he had other children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then when he was 895 years old he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 4:17; 1 Chronicles 1:3; Jude 1:14–15"> When Jared was 162 years old he had a son called Enoch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> After that he lived another 800 years and he had other children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then when he was 962 years old he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 3:37"> When Enoch was 65 years old he had a son called Methuselah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 6:9"> Enoch had fellowship with God. And he had other children, and when he was 365 years old Enoch disappeared, because God took him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 4:18"> When Enoch's son Methuselah was 187 years old he had a son called Lamech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> After that he lived another 782 years and he had other children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Then when he was 969 years old he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When Lamech was 182 years old he had a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 2:5; Hebrews 11:7; Matthew 24:37"> He said, “Yahweh cursed the ground and it was spoilt. But when this child grows up he will comfort us and make our hard work easier.” So he named him Noah because Noah means “he will comfort.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> After that, Lamech lived another 595 years and he had other children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Then when he was 777 years old he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 6:10; Genesis 10:21; Genesis 9:18–19"> When Noah was 500 years old he had his family. His first son was Shem, the next was Japheth and the youngest was Ham.</span>
</div>
