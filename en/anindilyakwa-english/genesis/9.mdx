## About God's covenant with Noah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 9:7; Genesis 1:28; Genesis 8:17"> God blessed Noah and his sons and he said to them, “You will have many children, so that your descendants can live all over the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Genesis 1:28; James 3:7"> All the animals, birds and fish will be afraid of you. And you will rule over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Colossians 2:21–22; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> You can eat them now. I gave you plants for food before, but now I am giving you these animals and birds and fish, so that you can eat meat as well as other food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:23; Deuteronomy 15:23; Deuteronomy 12:16"> But you must not eat meat with the blood still in it. You mustn't eat it, because the life is in the blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 21:28–29; Exodus 21:12; Genesis 4:9–10"> “If one of you kills another person, other people will punish you. And if a dangerous animal kills you, then I will pay it back and kill it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17; Matthew 26:52; Revelation 13:10"> I have made you all like myself, and so if you hit another person and he dies, then someone else will kill you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 1:28; Genesis 9:1; Genesis 9:19"> “You must have many children so that your descendants will live all over the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> God also said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 6:18; Isaiah 54:9–10; Romans 1:3"> “Today I am making my covenant with you and with your descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 8:1; Genesis 9:15–16; Psalms 36:5–6"> It is for all of you and also for everything that lives and breathes here today, everything that you brought out of the boat with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 54:9; Genesis 8:21–22; Genesis 7:21–23"> I am telling you these very important words today. Listen! From now on I will never do again what I have done in this world. I will never again send a lot of rain and flood the whole earth and destroy all the people and birds and animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 17:11; Matthew 26:26–28; Exodus 12:13"> “Now I will show you that I will never change my covenant. It will last forever for you and for everything that breathes. I will put my rainbow in the clouds for you. When you see it you will think about my covenant, and later when your children and then their children see it, they will think about it too. That rainbow will be a sign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When I cover the sky and put a rainbow in the clouds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Ezekiel 16:60; Isaiah 54:8–10"> I too will think about my promise that I am telling you today. It is also for the animals and the birds that live in the world now. I am telling you today that they will not drown again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 54:8–10; Jeremiah 32:40; 2 Samuel 23:5"> “So when you see the rainbow and when I see it too, I will think about my covenant. The rainbow will be a sign that it won't change, it will always be there for you today and afterwards for all people and fish, animals and birds who live in this world.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## About Noah and his sons

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 10:1; Genesis 10:6; Genesis 9:25–27"> The three sons of Noah who went into the boat and came out again were called Shem, Ham and Japheth. Ham was the father of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:4–28; Genesis 8:17; Genesis 5:32"> Noah's sons were the ancestors of all other people after the flood ended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:6; Proverbs 10:11; Isaiah 28:24–26"> Noah grew food and he was the first to grow grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Habakkuk 2:15–16; Proverbs 23:31–32"> He made some wine from the grapes and he and his family drank it. One day Noah drank some of the wine he had made and became drunk. He took off his clothes and slept naked in his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 30:17; Galatians 6:1; Matthew 18:15"> Ham, the father of Canaan, came to his father and when he saw him naked he went out and told his two brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 6:1; Exodus 20:12; 1 Timothy 5:17"> Noah's two older sons took a cloth over to him. They held it behind them and walked backwards to where their father was lying. They covered him with the cloth because they didn't want to see their father naked. When they had put the cloth over their father they went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Then Noah became sober again. When he heard what his youngest son had done he was very angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 9:23; Deuteronomy 27:16; Judges 1:28–30"> He cursed Ham and his descendants and said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I am cursing Canaan!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His brothers will rule over him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 144:15; Genesis 12:1–3; Luke 3:23–36"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am praising Yahweh, the God of Shem.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Shem will rule over Canaan.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 11:12; Romans 15:12; Hebrews 11:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">God will give Japheth many children and they will increase.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And his descendants will live with Shem's people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Japheth will also rule over Canaan.”</span>The name Japheth means “they will become many.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> After the flood had ended Noah lived another 350 years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 5:27; Psalms 90:10; Genesis 5:32"> Then when he was 950 years old he died.</span>
</div>
</div>
