## About God calling Abram

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–6"> Yahweh said to Abram, “Leave this country belonging to you and your father and leave your people here, and go to a different country that I will show you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:9; Genesis 18:18; Galatians 3:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘I will give you many descendants,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will become a great nation.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will bless you. I will make you very great,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that many people will know your name.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then you will bless others.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 27:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">Some people will bless you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and so I will bless them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But if anyone curses you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will curse them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will bless all the nations</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">just as I am blessing you today.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Genesis 11:31; Genesis 11:27"> Abram was an old man now, he was 75 years old. He left the city of Haran and went to a different country called Canaan, because Yahweh had told him to go there. And his nephew Lot went with him. Lot's father and Abram were brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 14:14; Genesis 11:31; Hebrews 11:8–9"> Abram took his wife Sarai and his nephew Lot, and all the men and women who lived with him and worked for him. They took all the things and all the money that they had got in Haran, and they set off. They went on and on until they arrived in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 11:9; Deuteronomy 11:30; Genesis 35:4"> At that time people called Canaanites were still living in the land. They had special trees in different places in the country and the people spoke to them. Abram and all his people stayed in Canaan and went from place to place until they reached the hill country. There was a special tree there in a place called Moreh near the town of Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 18:1; Genesis 13:15"> Yahweh came and appeared to Abram at the tree and said, “This is the country I will give to your descendants.” Then Abram took some big stones and stood them up and made an altar so that he could pray to Yahweh, and then he worshipped him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:31; Genesis 4:26; Genesis 21:33"> From there Abram and all his people went south, and they made their camp in the hill country between a town called Bethel on the west and one called Ai on the east. And Abram did the same as before. He took some big stones and stood them up and made an altar so that he could pray to Yahweh, and then he worshipped him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 13:3; Genesis 24:62; Genesis 13:1"> Abram and his people stayed in Canaan, but they moved camp and went from place to place towards the south.</span>
</div>

## About Abram and his wife in Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 43:1; Genesis 26:1–3; 1 Kings 17:1–18"> But there was very little food in Canaan. There was so little food that Abram and his family left Canaan and went down to Egypt to live there for a while.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 29:17; Genesis 12:14"> When they reached Egypt, before they went into the country, Abram said to his wife Sarai, “You are a beautiful woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 20:11; Genesis 26:7; 1 John 1:8–10"> When the Egyptians see you they will think, ‘That is Abram's wife.’ Maybe they will kill me and let you live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 20:12–13; Genesis 20:2"> So tell them, ‘I am Abram's sister,’ and then they will let me live and they will be kind to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 6:2; Matthew 5:28; Genesis 39:7"> When they left Canaan and went into Egypt, the Egyptians saw that Sarai really was beautiful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 20:2; Genesis 41:1; 2 Kings 18:21"> There was a king in Egypt who ruled over the whole country, and some of his servants told him about Abram's beautiful wife. They took Sarai to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 20:14; Genesis 13:2; Genesis 24:35"> and because she was beautiful the king was kind to Abram. He gave him servants and sheep, goats, cattle, donkeys and camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:21; Genesis 20:18; Psalms 105:14–15"> But Yahweh didn't want Sarai to stay with the king in his palace, and he sent very bad sicknesses to him and to the people who lived with him and they became very sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 26:9–11; 1 Samuel 14:43"> Then the king sent for Abram and asked him, “What have you done to me? Why didn't you tell me that Sarai was your wife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Because you said, ‘This woman is my sister,’ I took her for my wife. That was very wrong to trick me. Now take your wife and go! Get right away from me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 105:14–15; Proverbs 21:1; Exodus 18:27"> Then the king told his men to send them away, and they took Abram and put him out of the country with all his family and everything they owned.</span>
</div>
