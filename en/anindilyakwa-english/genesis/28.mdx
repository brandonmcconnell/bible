## About Esau taking another wife

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 27:33; Genesis 28:1"> Esau heard that Isaac had blessed Jacob and had sent him away to Mesopotamia to find a wife there. He also knew what Isaac had told Jacob when he blessed him. He had said, “Do not marry a Canaanite woman.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1; Ephesians 6:3; Colossians 3:20"> And Esau knew that Jacob had obeyed his father and mother and had gone away to Mesopotamia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 24:3; Genesis 26:34–35; 1 Samuel 8:6"> Then Esau realized that his father did not like the Canaanite women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 36:3; Genesis 36:18; Genesis 36:13"> So he went to Ishmael to get a wife from his family. Ishmael was Abraham's son. Then Esau married Ishmael's daughter. She was called Mahalath and her brother was called Nebaioth.</span>
</div>
</div>

## About Jacob having a dream at Bethel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:2; Genesis 11:31; Genesis 32:10"> Jacob left Beersheba and set off to go to Haran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 8:20; Genesis 28:18; Genesis 31:46"> He went on and on until sunset, when he stopped and camped there for the night. He lay down to sleep and made his head comfortable on a stone. Then he slept,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 1:51; Numbers 12:6; Genesis 32:1–2"> and had a dream. This is what he dreamed. He saw a long ladder reaching from the ground high up to heaven and angels were going up and coming down on it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Genesis 48:3; Genesis 35:12"> Yahweh was standing beside Jacob, and he said to him, “I am Yahweh, the God of Abraham and Isaac. I will give to you and to your descendants this land that you are lying on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Acts 3:25; Genesis 18:18"> Your descendants will be many like the dust of the earth. They will own this place and they will make their country bigger on all sides. I will bless all the nations just as I am blessing you now and I will bless your descendants later.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Isaiah 41:10; Jeremiah 1:19"> I will stay near you and keep you safe wherever you are. Then afterwards I will bring you back again to this place. I will not leave you; I will do everything I have promised you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 5:15; Exodus 3:5; Exodus 15:11"> Jacob woke up and said to himself, “Yahweh is here in this place and I didn't know!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:15; Genesis 35:1–13; Ecclesiastes 5:1"> Then he was afraid and said, “This is a very frightening place! It must be the house of God! It must be the gate that opens into heaven!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 35:14; Genesis 31:45; Genesis 31:13"> Jacob got up next morning and took the stone that his head had been resting on and stood it up there so that people would know that Yahweh had come and appeared there. Then he poured olive oil on the stone so that it would belong to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hosea 12:4–5; Genesis 35:1; Genesis 48:3"> He named the place Bethel, because Bethel means “house of God.” At first the town nearby was called Luz.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:8; 2 Samuel 15:8; Genesis 31:13"> Then Jacob made a promise to Yahweh. He said, “If you will stay near me and keep me safe on my way and give me food and clothes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:31; Deuteronomy 26:17; 2 Samuel 19:24"> and if I come back safely to my father's home, then you, Yahweh, will be my God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 35:7; Genesis 14:20; Deuteronomy 14:22–23"> Then he said, “I have stood this stone up here today so that I can remember you, and this will be the place where people will worship you. If you give me ten cattle, I will give one back to you. If you give me ten of anything, I will give you back one. I will always do that.”</span>
</div>
</div>
