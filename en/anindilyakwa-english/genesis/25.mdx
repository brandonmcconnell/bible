## About Abraham's descendants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:32–33; Genesis 23:1–2; Genesis 28:1"> Abraham had another wife whose name was Keturah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:32–33; Numbers 25:17–18; Job 2:11"> These are the names of the children Keturah gave birth to for Abraham. They were called Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak and Shuah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Jeremiah 49:8; Ezekiel 27:20"> Keturah's son Jokshan was the father of Sheba and Dedan. Dedan's descendants became so many that there were three groups. They were called the Asshurim, the Letushim and the Leummim people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6"> Keturah's son Midian had five sons. They were called Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida and Eldaah.<br />Keturah was the ancestor of all those people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 24:36; Hebrews 1:2; Galatians 3:29"> While Abraham was still alive he gave presents to his sons, the sons of Hagar and Keturah. Then he sent all those sons away from his son Isaac. He sent them to the east to live there.<br />Then Abraham said, “When I die, all my cattle, sheep, goats, camels, donkeys and all my silver and gold and other things will all belong to Isaac.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## About Abraham's death

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 12:4"> Abraham lived for 175 years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 35:28–29; Genesis 15:15; 1 Chronicles 29:28"> They were good years. And then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 50:13; Genesis 35:29; Genesis 49:29–30"> His sons Isaac and Ishmael buried him in the cave called Machpelah that was near Mamre's place. First of all the cave had belonged to Ephron, the one whose father was Zohar the Hittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 23:16; Genesis 49:31"> Abraham had bought the cave from the Hittite people and both Abraham and his wife Sarah were buried there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 16:14; Genesis 24:62"> After Abraham died, God blessed his son Isaac. He lived near the waterhole called “The Owner of the Waterhole is Alive and He Sees Me.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## About Ishmael's descendants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 83:6; Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:13"> Ishmael was Abraham's first son. His mother was Hagar, Sarah's Egyptian servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:29–31; Isaiah 60:7; Song of Solomon 1:5"> He had twelve sons. The first was called Nebaioth. The names of the others were Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 21:11; Isaiah 21:16"> Mishma, Dumah, Massa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:19; Job 2:11"> Hadad, Tema, Jetur and Naphish and the last one was Kedemah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 17:20; Genesis 17:23"> Those twelve men were the ancestors of separate tribes and their villages and camping places were called by their names.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Genesis 25:7–8"> Their father Ishmael was a very old man now, and when he was 137 years old he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 16:12; Genesis 20:1; 2 Kings 23:29"> Ishmael's descendants lived in the land between Havilah and Shur. That was their country, and when people went from Egypt over to Assyria they went through there. Ishmael's family lived away from Isaac's family and from Abraham's other descendants.</span>
</div>
</div>

## About Jacob and Esau

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 1:2; Luke 3:34; Acts 7:8"> This is the story of Abraham's son Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 24:29; Genesis 24:67; Genesis 22:23"> Isaac was forty years old when he married Rebecca. Rebecca was the daughter of Bethuel who was an Aramean from Mesopotamia. And Rebecca's brother was Laban.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Isaiah 58:9; Ezra 8:23"> Rebecca had no children. So Isaac prayed to Yahweh for her to have children. Yahweh heard his prayer and Rebecca became pregnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Samuel 28:6; 1 Samuel 30:8"> She knew that she was going to have twins. Before they were born they struggled together inside her.<br />Rebecca said, “Oh dear, what has happened to me?” So she prayed to Yahweh about the children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 9:10–13; Genesis 27:40; Genesis 27:29"> Yahweh said to her,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“There are two people inside you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They will be the ancestors of two nations.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Their people will be against each other and live separately.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">One brother will be stronger than the other,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the older brother will work for the younger one.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Twin boys were born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 27:11; Genesis 27:16; Genesis 27:23"> The first one was reddish and his skin was hairy, so he was named Esau, because Esau means “hairy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 27:36; Hosea 12:3; Genesis 38:28–30"> When the second one was born, he was holding on tightly to the heel of the first. So he was named Jacob, because Jacob means “he is holding the heel.” But when people talk about others and say, “He is holding the heel,” that means, “he is tricking people.” Their father Isaac was sixty years old when they were born.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 11:9; Genesis 21:20; Genesis 10:9"> The two boys grew up. Esau was good at hunting and he loved being out in the bush. But Jacob was a quiet man who liked to stay at home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 27:19; Genesis 27:31; Genesis 27:4"> Isaac loved Esau more, because he liked eating the animals he killed, but Rebecca loved Jacob.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 13:25; Isaiah 40:30–31; 1 Samuel 14:28"> One day Jacob was cooking some bean soup and Esau came back from hunting. He was hungry</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 36:1; Exodus 15:15; Genesis 36:9"> and he said to his younger brother Jacob, “I am very hungry. Give me some of that red soup.” That is why Esau had two names. One was Esau and the other was Edom, because Edom means “red.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> “Yes, I will give you some,” Jacob answered. “But you must tell me that I am the firstborn and the leader now, and not you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachi 3:14; Job 21:15; Job 22:17"> Esau said, “All right! I am dying of hunger, so what good is it to me if I am the firstborn?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 12:16; Genesis 27:36; Hebrews 6:16"> Jacob said, “I want you to make a promise and not change it. You must promise me that I will be the firstborn and the leader now.” So Esau agreed and made the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Philippians 3:18–19; Hebrews 12:16–17; 1 Corinthians 15:32"> Then Jacob gave him some damper and some of the soup he had cooked. Esau ate the damper and drank the soup and he got up and left, saying, “What does it matter? Let him be the firstborn!”</span>
</div>
</div>
