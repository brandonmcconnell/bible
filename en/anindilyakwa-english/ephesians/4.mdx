## About being in one group, like one body

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Philippians 1:27; 1 Thessalonians 2:12"> Listen to what I, who am in jail, am telling you. I am in jail because I belong to the Lord Jesus Christ. God has chosen you to be his own people. So you who belong to him should go straight on his road.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; 1 Corinthians 13:7; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5"> Don't be proud, but go quietly on God's road. Be gentle and patient. And don't get tired of each other but keep loving one another all the time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:10; Colossians 3:13–15; 1 Corinthians 12:12–13"> God has brought us together by his Spirit to make us friends. So you should really want to stay together, to love and help each other in peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Romans 12:4–5; 1 Corinthians 12:4–13"> We are all one people. We are like one body. And God has given his one Spirit to us all. He has chosen us all in the same way so that we can hope in him in the same way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:6; 1 Corinthians 12:13; 1 Peter 3:21"> There is one Lord and we trust in him in the same way, and we have been baptized in the same way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Malachi 2:10; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> There is only one God and one Father of us all. He rules over us all and over all the world. He takes us all to do his work and he is among us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> God's word tells us that Christ has gone back to his Father in heaven. It says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He went first to heaven,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and took many people with him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And they followed him afterwards.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Then he gave good things to his people.”</span><br />You know that first Christ came down from above, and he was born like us and lived like us in the world. Then afterwards the same one who came down went up again, so that he could be everywhere and give us good things. Christ has freely given good things to each one of us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:28–29; Ephesians 2:20"> What kinds of good things has Christ given to us? He has given different kinds of work to us his people and he helps us to do that work. He has chosen some of us to be apostles, and some to be prophets to tell people his word, and some to tell people who don't know him about Jesus Christ so that they may believe in him too. And he has chosen some to look after his people and some to teach them his word.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11–14; Ephesians 4:16; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> Yes, that's right, Christ has chosen us workers to make each other strong. We should help each other to be strong to work for him all the time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:28; John 17:21; Ephesians 4:5"> We should help each other until we all have the same faith in God's Son. And we should help each other until we all know him in the same way, until we become really strong. We should let our minds keep growing until we become wise like Christ. Then we will understand God's word. We shouldn't be like children who don't understand yet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:9; Romans 16:17–18; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> Listen. Some people might tell us lies, first one thing and then another. They might make up different things and then want to teach them to us, but we mustn't listen to things like that. We should be strong and keep on going on God's straight road.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 3:18; Ephesians 4:25; Zechariah 8:16"> We must speak the truth. We mustn't speak angrily to people but quietly, so that they know that we love them. We must try to be like Christ, the one who rules over us. He is like the head and we are like his body,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 2:19; John 15:5; Colossians 2:2"> with its arms and legs. The head controls the arms and the legs. When your legs are doing good work and getting strong, and when your arms are also doing good work and getting strong, then your body will work well and be strong. In that way Christ controls us God's people. And we must each keep on working well and loving each other. Then we will make each other strong in one group.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Start living like new people

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:21; 1 Peter 4:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> I am speaking to you for the Lord Jesus Christ, and so I am warning you. Don't go on living like people who don't know God. Their thoughts are no good. So don't be like them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Ephesians 2:12; Romans 1:21–23"> Those people are hard. They don't want God and they have turned their backs on him. They don't have the real life that God gives us. They walk in the dark. They are blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Romans 1:24–26; 1 Timothy 4:2"> They are no longer ashamed of what they are doing. They have decided to do wrong. They don't think about anything good, only what is bad. They keep on running off with people they shouldn't be with. Those people who don't know God are always doing all kinds of bad things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 2:11–14; John 6:45; Matthew 11:29"> But those who taught you didn't tell you to do those kinds of things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 5:20; Ephesians 1:13; Hebrews 3:7–8"> They taught you the truth about Jesus and you heard their words and so you know about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Hebrews 12:1; James 1:21"> So don't go on doing what you used to do, because those bad things you wanted were spoiling you. Get rid of sin, just like you get rid of old clothes that are no use now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Romans 8:6; Psalms 51:10"> Do what is good with a clean mind, just as though you are putting on good, new clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:10–14; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ephesians 2:10"> Start that good new way like new people, because God has made you new like himself. So walk straight on God's road all the time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Zechariah 8:16; Proverbs 12:22"> So you must not tell lies any more. When God's people are talking together, you must tell the truth, because we are all in one group with Christ like friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 1:19; Psalms 4:4; Psalms 37:8"> When you get angry, don't do wrong. And don't stay angry all day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 4:7; 1 Peter 5:8; Ephesians 6:16"> And don't let Satan get into you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12; Galatians 6:10"> You thieves who have been stealing things must stop stealing and work now. You must do good work to get honest money so you can share with poor people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:11; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> And this is another thing. Don't speak carelessly with unkind words. Only use good words so that you can help other people and encourage them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:19; Isaiah 63:10; Ephesians 1:13–14"> God has chosen you to be his own people. And he has freely given you his Holy Spirit, so that you will know that you are truly his, and that he will set you free. So don't make God's Spirit sad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Colossians 3:8; Ecclesiastes 7:9; Titus 3:2–3"> Get rid of swearing and angry words. Don't keep on shouting. Don't tease other people. And don't keep telling bad things about other people. And don't be always hating each other,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; Matthew 6:14–15; Mark 11:25"> but be kind to one another and love each other. And forgive each other's sins as God forgives you your sins. God forgives you because Christ has died for you.</span>
</div>
</div>
