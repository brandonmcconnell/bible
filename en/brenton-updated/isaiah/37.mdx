<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings IV 19:1–37; Ezra and Nehemiah 9:5; Isaiah 36:22–38"> And it came to pass, when King Hezekiah heard it, that he rent his clothes, and put on sackcloth, and went up to the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; Kings IV 18:18; Isaiah 36:3"> And he sent Eliakim the steward, and Shebna the scribe, and the elders of the priests clothed with sackcloth, to Isaiah the son of Amoz, the prophet. And they said to him, Thus says Hezekiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 66:9; Hosea 13:13; Isaiah 26:17–18"> Today is a day of affliction, and reproach, and rebuke, and anger: for the pangs are come upon the travailing woman, but she has not strength to bring forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Isaiah 36:20; Isaiah 10:22"> May the Lord thy God hear the words of Rabshakeh, which the king of the Assyrians has sent, to reproach the living God, even to reproach with the words which the Lord thy God has heard: therefore thou shalt pray to thy Lord for these that are left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So the servants of King Hezekiah came to Isaiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 7:4; Isaiah 35:4; Mark 5:36"> And Isaiah said to them, Thus shall ye say to your master, Thus saith the Lord, Be not thou afraid at the words which thou hast heard, wherewith the ambassadors of the king of the Assyrians have reproached me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 31:8–9; Chronicles II 32:21; Isaiah 33:10–12"> Behold, I will send a blast upon him, and he shall hear a report, and return to his own country, and he shall fall by the sword in his own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 10:29; Numbers 33:20–21; Kings IV 19:8–9"> So Rabshakeh returned, and found the king of the Assyrians besieging Libnah: for he had heard that he had departed from Lachish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 18:1; Isaiah 20:5; Isaiah 37:7"> And Tirhakah king of the Ethiopians went forth to attack him. And when he heard it, he turned aside, and sent messengers to Hezekiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 36:15; Kings IV 18:5; Matthew 27:43"> Thus shall ye say to Hezekiah king of Judea, Let not thy God, in whom thou trustest, deceive thee, saying, Jerusalem shall not be delivered into the hand of the king of the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 36:18–20; Kings IV 18:33–35; Isaiah 14:17"> Hast thou not heard what the kings of the Assyrians have done, how they have destroyed the whole earth? and shalt thou be delivered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 11:31; Kings IV 17:6; Kings IV 18:11"> Have the gods of the nations which my fathers destroyed delivered them, both Gozan, and Haran, and Rezeph, which are in the land of Theemath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings IV 19:13; Kings IV 17:24; Isaiah 36:19"> Where are the kings of Hamath? and where is the king of Arpad? and where is the king of the city of Sepharvaim, and of Anagugana?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings IV 19:14; Psalms 123:1–4; Joel 2:17–20"> And Hezekiah received the letter from the messengers, and read it, and went up to the house of the Lord, and opened it before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 7:8–9; Daniel 9:3–4; James 5:13"> And Hezekiah prayed to the Lord, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Psalms 99:1; Psalms 86:10"> O Lord of hosts, God of Israel, who sittest upon the cherubs, thou alone art the God of every kingdom of the world: thou hast made heaven and earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles II 6:40; Psalms 17:6; Isaiah 37:4"> Incline thine ear, O Lord, hearken, O Lord; open thine eyes, O Lord, look, O Lord: and behold the words of Sennacherib, which he has sent to reproach the living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Nahum 2:11–12; Kings IV 15:29; Kings IV 17:6"> For of a truth, Lord, the kings of the Assyrians have laid waste the whole world, and the countries thereof,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 44:9–10; Jeremiah 10:11; Hosea 8:6"> and have cast their idols into the fire: for they were no gods, but the work of men’s hands, wood and stone; and they have cast them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 46:10; Kings III 18:36–37; Psalms 83:17–18"> But now, O Lord our God, deliver us from his hands, that every kingdom of the earth may know that thou art God alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Isaiah 38:3–6; Acts 4:31"> And Isaiah the son of Amoz was sent to Hezekiah, and said to him, Thus saith the Lord, the God of Israel, I have heard thy prayer to me concerning Sennacherib king of the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 16:4; Lamentations 2:13; Isaiah 1:8"> This is the word which God has spoken concerning him; The virgin daughter of Zion has despised thee, and mocked thee; the daughter of Jerusalem has shaken her head at thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:7; Daniel 5:20–23; Isaiah 17:7"> Whom hast thou reproached and provoked? and against whom hast thou lifted up thy voice? and hast thou not lifted up thine eyes on high against the Holy One of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 14:8; Isaiah 10:18; Exodus 15:9"> For thou hast reproached the Lord by messengers; for thou hast said, With the multitude of chariots have I ascended to the height of mountains, and to the sides of Lebanon; and I have cropped the height of his cedars and the beauty of his cypresses; and I entered into the height of the forest region:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings III 20:10; Isaiah 36:12; Kings IV 19:23–24"> and I have made a bridge, and dried up the waters, and every pool of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 4:27–28; Acts 2:23; 1 Peter 2:8"> Hast thou not heard of these things which I did of old? I appointed them from ancient times; but now have I manifested my purpose of desolating nations in their strongholds, and them that dwell in strong cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 129:6; Psalms 37:2; 1 Peter 1:24"> I weakened their hands, and they withered; and they became as dry grass on the housetops, and as grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 2:13; Psalms 139:1–11; Jeremiah 23:23–24"> But now I know thy rest, and thy going out, and thy coming in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:4; Isaiah 30:28; Isaiah 37:34"> And thy wrath wherewith thou hast been enraged, and thy rancor has come up to me; therefore I will put a hook in thy nose, and a bit in thy lips, and will turn thee back by the way by which thou camest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings IV 19:29; Leviticus 25:4–5; Kings III 13:3–5"> And this shall be a sign to thee, Eat this year what thou hast sown; and the second year that which is left: and the third year sow, and reap, and plant vineyards, and eat the fruit of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; Kings IV 19:30–31; Psalms 80:9"> And they that are left in Judea shall take root downward, and bear fruit upward:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 9:7; Kings IV 19:31; Isaiah 59:17"> for out of Jerusalem there shall be a remnant, and the saved ones out of Mount Zion: the zeal of the Lord of hosts shall perform this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 17:12; Isaiah 10:32–34; Isaiah 17:14"> Therefore thus saith the Lord concerning the king of the Assyrians, He shall not enter into this city, nor cast a weapon against it, nor bring a shield against it, nor make a rampart round it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Proverbs 21:30"> But by the way by which he came, by it shall he return, and shall not enter into this city: thus saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings IV 20:6; Isaiah 38:6; Isaiah 31:5"> I will protect this city to save it for my own sake, and for my servant David’s sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 10:12; Kings IV 19:35; Isaiah 10:33–34"> And the angel of the Lord went forth, and slew out of the camp of the Assyrians a hundred and eighty-five thousand: and they arose in the morning and found all these bodies dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jonah 1:2; Jonah 3:3; Genesis 10:11–12"> And Sennacherib king of the Assyrians turned and departed, and dwelt in Nineveh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 8:4; Jeremiah 51:27; Ezra and Nehemiah 4:2"> And while he was worshiping Nisroch his country’s god in the house, Adrammelech and Sharezer his sons smote him with swords; and they escaped into Armenia: and Esarhaddon his son reigned in his stead.</span>
