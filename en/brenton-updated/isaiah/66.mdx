<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:48–50; Acts 17:24; Matthew 5:34–35"> Thus saith the Lord, Heaven is my throne, and the earth is my footstool: what kind of a house will ye build me? and of what kind is to be the place of my rest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 57:15; Psalms 34:18; Psalms 51:17"> For all these things are mine, saith the Lord: and to whom will I have respect, but to the humble and meek, and the man that trembles at my words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; Amos 5:21–22; Isaiah 1:11–15"> But the transgressor that sacrifices a calf to me, is as he that kills a dog; and he that offers fine flour, as one that offers swine’s blood; he that gives frankincense for a memorial, is as a blasphemer. Yet they have chosen their own ways, and their soul has delighted in their abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; Jeremiah 7:13; Proverbs 10:24"> I also will choose their mockeries, and will recompense their sins upon them; because I called them, and they did not hearken to me; I spoke, and they heard not: and they did evil before me, and chose the things wherein I delighted not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:18–20; Isaiah 66:2; Matthew 5:10–12"> Hear the words of the Lord, ye that tremble at his word; speak ye, our brethren, to them that hate you and abominate you, that the name of the Lord may be glorified, and may appear their joy; but they shall be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 3:7–16; Amos 1:2; Isaiah 34:8"> A voice of a cry from the city, a voice from the temple, a voice of the Lord rendering recompense to his adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 12:1–5; Isaiah 54:1; Galatians 4:26"> Before she that travailed brought forth, before the travail pain came on, she escaped it and brought forth a male.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Isaiah 49:20–22; Romans 15:18–21"> Who has heard such a thing? and who has seen after this manner? Has the earth travailed in one day? or has even a nation been born at once, that Zion has travailed, and brought forth her children?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 37:3; Genesis 18:14"> But I have raised this expectation, yet thou hast not remembered me, saith the Lord: behold, have not I made the bearing and barren woman? saith thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; Deuteronomy 32:43; Psalms 137:6"> Rejoice, O Jerusalem, and all ye that love her hold in her a general assembly: rejoice greatly with her, all that now mourn over her:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 60:16; Joel 3:18; Isaiah 60:5"> that ye may suck, and be satisfied with the breast of her consolation; that ye may milk out, and delight yourselves with the influx of her glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 48:18; Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 45:14"> For thus saith the Lord, Behold, I turn toward them as a river of peace, and as a torrent bringing upon them in a flood the glory of the Gentiles: their children shall be borne upon the shoulders, and comforted on the knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; Isaiah 51:3; Isaiah 40:1"> As if his mother should comfort one, so will I also comfort you; and ye shall be comforted in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 10:7; Ezra and Nehemiah 8:31; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:9"> And ye shall see, and your heart shall rejoice, and your bones shall thrive like grass: and the hand of the Lord shall be known to them that fear him, and he shall threaten the disobedient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 30:33; 2 Thessalonians 1:6–9; 2 Peter 3:10–12"> For, behold, the Lord will come as fire, and his chariots as a storm, to render his vengeance with wrath, and his rebuke with a flame of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:21–22; Isaiah 30:30; Ezekiel 39:2–10"> For with the fire of the Lord all the earth shall be judged, and all flesh with his sword: many shall be slain by the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 65:3–4; Deuteronomy 14:3–8; Isaiah 1:28–29"> They that sanctify themselves and purify themselves in the gardens, and eat swine’s flesh in the porches, and the abominations, and the mouse, shall be consumed together, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Revelation 11:15; 2 Corinthians 4:4–6"> And I know their works and their imagination. I am going to gather all nations and tongues; and they shall come, and see my glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 52:15; Romans 15:21; Isaiah 55:5"> And I will leave a sign upon them, and I will send forth them that have escaped of them to the nations, to Tarshish, and Pul, and Lud, and Mosoch, and to Tubal, and to Greece, and to the isles afar off, to those who have not heard my name, nor seen my glory; and they shall declare my glory among the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 60:3–14; Isaiah 65:11; Isaiah 11:9"> And they shall bring your brethren out of all nations for a gift to the Lord with horses, and chariots, in litters drawn by mules with awnings, to the holy city Jerusalem, said the Lord, as though the children of Israel should bring their sacrifices to me with psalms into the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Exodus 19:6; Revelation 1:6"> And I will take of them priests and Levites, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; 2 Peter 3:13; Revelation 21:1"> For as the new heaven and the new earth, which I make, remain before me, saith the Lord, so shall your seed and your name continue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:1; Psalms 86:9; Colossians 2:16–17"> And it shall come to pass from month to month, and from Sabbath to Sabbath, that all flesh shall come to worship before me in Jerusalem, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 14:10–11; Isaiah 14:11; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16"> And they shall go forth, and see the carcasses of the men that have transgressed against me: for their worm shall not die, and their fire shall not be quenched; and they shall be a spectacle to all flesh.</span>
