<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:7; Psalms 94:15; Hebrews 12:14"> Hearken to me, ye that follow after righteousness, and seek the Lord: look to the solid rock, which ye have hewn, and to the hole of the pit which ye have dug.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:24; Galatians 3:9–14; Romans 4:1–5"> Look to Abraham your father, and to Sarah that bore you: for he was alone when I called him, and blessed him, and loved him, and multiplied him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Joel 2:3; Isaiah 52:9"> And now I will comfort thee, O Zion: and I have comforted all her desert places; and I will make her desert places as a garden, and her western places as the garden of the Lord; they shall find in her gladness and exultation, thanksgiving and the voice of praise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Isaiah 2:3; 1 Corinthians 9:21"> Hear me, hear me, my people; and ye kings, hearken to me: for a law shall proceed from me, and my judgment shall be for a light of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:17–18; Isaiah 46:13; Isaiah 42:4"> My righteousness speedily draws nigh, and my salvation shall go forth as light, and on mine arm shall the Gentiles trust: the isles shall wait for me, and on mine arm shall they trust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Psalms 102:25–26; Isaiah 34:4"> Lift up your eyes to the sky, and look on the earth beneath: for the sky was darkened like smoke, and the earth shall wax old like a garment, and the inhabitants shall die in like manner: but my righteousness shall not fail.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 5:11; Psalms 37:31; Ezekiel 2:6"> Hear me, ye that know judgment, the people in whose heart is my law: fear not the reproach of men, and be not overcome by their contempt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Isaiah 50:9; Hosea 5:12"> For as a garment will be devoured by time, and as wool will be devoured by a moth, so shall they be consumed; but my righteousness shall be forever, and my salvation for all generations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 74:13–14; Isaiah 52:1; Psalms 89:10"> Awake, awake, O Jerusalem, and put on the strength of thine arm; awake as in the early time, as the ancient generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 43:16; Isaiah 63:11–12; Isaiah 50:2"> Art thou not it that dried the sea, the water, even the abundance of the deep; that made the depths of the sea a way of passage for the delivered and redeemed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17"> for by the help of the Lord they shall return, and come to Zion with joy and everlasting exultation, for praise and joy shall come upon their head: pain, and grief, and groaning, have fled away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 118:6; 1 Peter 1:24; Isaiah 2:22"> I, even I, am he that comforts thee: consider who thou art, that thou wast afraid of mortal man, and of the son of man, who are withered as grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 45:12; Isaiah 40:22; Psalms 104:2"> And thou hast forgotten God who made thee, who made the sky and founded the earth; and thou wert continually afraid because of the wrath of him that afflicted thee: for whereas he counseled to take thee away, yet now where is the wrath of him that afflicted thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Zechariah 9:11; Isaiah 52:2"> For in thy deliverance he shall not halt, nor tarry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35; Isaiah 54:5; Amos 9:5–6"> for I am thy God, that troubles the sea, and causes the waves thereof to roar: the Lord of hosts is my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:21; Isaiah 49:2; John 17:8"> I will put my words into thy mouth, and I will shelter thee under the shadow of my hand, with which I fixed the sky, and founded the earth: and the Lord shall say to Zion, Thou art my people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:1; Revelation 14:10; Psalms 60:3"> Awake, awake, stand up, O Jerusalem, that hast drunk at the hand of the Lord the cup of his fury: for thou hast drunk out and drained the cup of calamity, the cup of wrath:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 49:21; Psalms 88:18; Psalms 142:4"> and there was none to comfort thee of all the children whom thou borest; and there was none to take hold of thy hand, not even of all the children whom thou hast reared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Amos 7:2; Isaiah 47:9; Ezekiel 14:21"> Wherefore these things are against thee; who shall sympathize with thee in thy grief? downfall, and destruction, famine, and sword: who shall comfort thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11–12; Revelation 16:9–11; Ezekiel 39:19"> Thy sons are the perplexed ones, that sleep at the top of every street as a half-boiled beet; they that are full of the anger of the Lord, caused to faint by the Lord God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 29:9; Isaiah 54:11; Isaiah 51:17"> Therefore hear, thou afflicted one, and drunken, but not with wine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 22:23; Isaiah 51:17; Jeremiah 50:34"> thus saith the Lord God that judges his people, Behold, I have taken out of thy hand the cup of calamity, the cup of my wrath; and thou shalt not drink it anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:24; Zechariah 12:2; Isaiah 49:25–26"> And I will give it into the hands of them that injured thee, and them that afflicted thee; who said to thy soul, Bow down, that we may pass over: and thou didst level thy body with the ground to them passing by without.</span>
</div>
</div>
