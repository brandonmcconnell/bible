<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 2:1–3; Jeremiah 48:1–47; Isaiah 11:14"> The word against the land of Moab.<br />By night the land of Moab shall be destroyed; for by night the wall of the land of Moab shall be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:1; Leviticus 21:5; Isaiah 3:24"> Grieve for yourselves; for even Dibon, where your altar is, shall be destroyed: thither shall ye go up to weep, over Nebo of the land of Moab: howl ye: baldness shall be on every head, and all arms shall be wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jonah 3:6–8; Jeremiah 19:13; Deuteronomy 22:8"> Gird yourselves with sackcloth in her streets: and lament upon her roofs, and in her streets, and in her ways; howl all of you with weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:34; Kings III 19:4; Jonah 4:8"> For Heshbon and Elealeh have cried: their voice was heard to Jahaz: therefore the loins of the region of Moab cry aloud; her soul shall know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:5; Jeremiah 4:20; Luke 19:41–44"> The heart of the region of Moab cries within her to Zoar; for it is as a heifer of three years old: and on the ascent of Eglath they shall go up to thee weeping by the way of Horonaim: she cries, Destruction, and trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 1:10–12; Isaiah 19:5–7; Numbers 32:36"> The water of Nimrim shall be desolate, and the grass thereof shall fail: for there shall be no green grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:36; Nahum 2:12–13; Isaiah 10:6"> Shall Moab even thus be delivered? for I will bring the Arabians upon the valley, and they shall take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:10; Isaiah 15:2–5; Jeremiah 48:31–34"> For the cry has reached the border of the region of Moab, even of Eglaim; and her howling has gone as far as the well of Elim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings IV 17:25; Leviticus 26:28; Leviticus 26:24"> And the water of Dimon shall be filled with blood: for I will bring Arabians upon Dimon, and I will take away the seed of Moab, and Ariel, and the remnant of Adama.</span>
