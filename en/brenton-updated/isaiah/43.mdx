<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; Isaiah 43:7; Zechariah 13:9"> And now thus saith the Lord God that made thee, O Jacob, and formed thee, O Israel, Fear not: for I have redeemed thee, I have called thee by thy name; thou art mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Joshua 1:9; Psalms 66:12"> And if thou pass through water, I am with thee; and the rivers shall not overflow thee: and if thou go through fire, thou shalt not be burned; the flame shall not burn thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 45:15; Chronicles II 14:9–14"> For I am the Lord thy God, the Holy One of Israel, that saves thee: I have made Egypt and Ethiopia thy ransom, and given Seba for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6–8; 1 Peter 2:9; Jeremiah 31:3"> Since thou becamest precious in my sight, thou hast become glorious, and I have loved thee: and I will give men for thee, and princes for thy life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:3; Isaiah 49:12; Luke 13:29"> Fear not; for I am with thee: I will bring thy seed from the east, and will gather thee from the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:17–18; Jeremiah 3:14; Jeremiah 3:18–19"> I will say to the north, Bring; and to the south, Keep not back; bring my sons from the land afar off, and my daughters from the ends of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Isaiah 43:21; Ephesians 1:12"> even all who are called by my name: for I have prepared him for my glory, and I have formed him, and have made him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; Ezekiel 12:2; Isaiah 42:18–20"> and I have brought forth the blind people; for their eyes are alike blind, and they that have ears are deaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 46:10; Isaiah 44:7–9; Isaiah 41:21–26"> All the nations are gathered together, and princes shall be gathered out of them: who will declare these things? or who will declare to you things from the beginning? let them bring forth their witnesses, and be justified; and let them hear, and declare the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:4; Isaiah 45:6; Isaiah 46:8–9"> Be ye my witnesses, and I too am a witness, saith the Lord God, and my servant whom I have chosen: that ye may know, and believe, and understand that I am he: before me there was no other God, and after me there shall be none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 13:4; Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 45:21–22"> I am God; and beside me there is no Savior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:12; Psalms 81:9–10; Isaiah 43:10"> I have declared, and have saved; I have reproached, and there was no strange god among you: ye are my witnesses, and I am the Lord God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 14:27; Isaiah 46:10; Psalms 90:2"> even from the beginning; and there is none that can deliver out of my hands: I will work, and who shall turn it back?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 23:13; Jeremiah 51:24"> Thus saith the Lord God that redeems you, the Holy One of Israel; For your sakes I will send to Babylon, and I will stir up all that flee, and the Chaldeans shall be bound in ships.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 43:3; Matthew 25:34; Isaiah 48:17"> I am the Lord God, your Holy One, who have appointed for Israel your king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 51:10; Isaiah 43:2; Psalms 77:19"> Thus saith the Lord, who makes a way in the sea, and a path in the mighty water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:4–9; Isaiah 1:31; Ezekiel 38:8–18"> who brought forth chariots and horse, and a mighty multitude: but they have lain down, and shall not rise: they are extinct, as quenched flax.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; 2 Corinthians 3:10; Chronicles I 16:12"> Remember ye not the former things, and consider not the ancient things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 48:6; Isaiah 42:9; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> Behold, I will do new things, which shall presently spring forth, and ye shall know them: and I will make a way in the wilderness, and rivers in the dry land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:17; Isaiah 48:21; Isaiah 49:10"> The beasts of the field shall bless me, the owls and young ostriches; for I have given water in the wilderness, and rivers in the dry land, to give drink to my chosen race,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 102:18"> even my people whom I have preserved to tell forth my praises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 6:3; Malachi 3:14; Malachi 1:13"> I have not now called thee, O Jacob; neither have I made thee weary, O Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Amos 5:25; Zechariah 7:5–6; Malachi 3:8"> Thou hast not brought me the sheep of thy whole burnt offering; neither hast thou glorified me with thy sacrifices. I have not caused thee to serve with sacrifices, neither have I wearied thee with frankincense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 7:13; Jeremiah 6:20; Isaiah 1:14"> Neither hast thou purchased for me victims for silver, neither have I desired the fat of thy sacrifices: but thou didst stand before me in thy sins, and in thine iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:12; Micah 7:18–19; Hebrews 10:17"> I, even I, am he that blots out thy transgressions for mine own sake, and thy sins; and I will not remember them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Isaiah 43:9; Romans 8:33"> But do thou remember, and let us plead together: do thou first confess thy transgressions, that thou mayest be justified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 28:7; Jeremiah 5:31; Ezekiel 22:25–28"> Your fathers first, and your princes have transgressed against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; Isaiah 65:15; Zechariah 8:13"> And the princes have defiled my sanctuaries: so I gave Jacob to enemies to destroy, and Israel to reproach.</span>
