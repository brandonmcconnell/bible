<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 65:2; Isaiah 1:2; Hosea 7:13"> Woe to the apostate children, saith the Lord: ye have framed counsel, not by me, and covenants not by my Spirit, to add sins to sins:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Numbers 27:21; Deuteronomy 28:68"> even they that proceed to go down into Egypt, but they have not inquired of me, that they might be helped by Pharaoh, and protected by the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 20:5; Romans 10:11; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> For the protection of Pharaoh shall be to you a disgrace, and there shall be a reproach to them that trust in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Isaiah 57:9; Jeremiah 43:7"> For there are princes in Tanis, evil messengers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:36; Isaiah 30:7; Isaiah 30:16"> In vain shall they labor in seeking to a people, which shall not profit them for help, but shall be for a shame and reproach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:15; Isaiah 46:1–2; Jeremiah 11:4"> The vision of the quadrupeds in the desert.<br />In affliction and distress, where are the lion and lion’s whelp, thence come also asps, and the young of flying asps, there shall they be who bore their wealth on asses and camels to a nation which shall not profit them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:22; Isaiah 7:4; Psalms 118:8–9"> The Egyptians shall help you utterly in vain: tell them, This your consolation is vain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:22; Habakkuk 2:2; Isaiah 8:1"> Now then sit down and write these words on a tablet, and in a book; for these things shall be for many long days, and even forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 30:1; Acts 7:51; Zechariah 1:4–6"> For the people is disobedient, false children, who would not hear the law of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:7–10; Romans 16:18; Amos 2:12"> who say to the prophets, Report not to us; and to them that see visions, Speak them not to us, but speak and report to us another error;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:7; Romans 1:30; John 15:23–24"> and turn us aside from this way; remove from us this path, and remove from us the oracle of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 31:1–3; Isaiah 5:24; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> Therefore thus saith the Holy One of Israel, Because ye have refused to obey these words, and have trusted in falsehood; and because thou hast murmured, and been confident in this respect:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 29:5; Ezekiel 13:10–15; Kings III 20:30"> therefore shall this sin be to you as a wall suddenly falling when a strong city has been taken, of which the fall is very near at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 2:9; Jeremiah 19:10–11; 2 Peter 2:4–5"> And the fall thereof shall be as the breaking of an earthen vessel, as small fragments of a pitcher, so that thou shouldest not find among them a sherd, with which thou mightest take up fire, and with which thou shouldest draw a little water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 26:3–4; Isaiah 32:17; Jeremiah 3:22–23"> Thus saith the Lord, the Holy Lord of Israel; When thou shalt turn and mourn, then thou shalt be saved; and thou shalt know where thou wast, when thou didst trust in vanities: then your strength became vain, yet ye would not hearken:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 31:1; Isaiah 31:3; Deuteronomy 28:25"> but ye said, We will flee upon horses; therefore shall ye flee: and, We will be aided by swift riders; therefore shall they that pursue you be swift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:25; Joshua 23:10; Deuteronomy 32:30"> A thousand shall flee because of the voice of one, and many shall flee on account of the voice of five; until ye be left as a signal post upon a mountain, and as one bearing an ensign upon a hill.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; 2 Peter 3:9; Proverbs 16:20"> And the Lord will again wait, that he may pity you, and will therefore be exalted that he may have mercy upon you: because the Lord your God is a judge: blessed are they that stay themselves upon him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 60:20; Psalms 50:15; Isaiah 65:24"> For the holy people shall dwell in Zion: and whereas Jerusalem has wept bitterly, saying, Pity me; he shall pity thee: when he perceived the voice of thy cry, he hearkened to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings III 22:27; Psalms 80:5; Amos 8:11–12"> And though the Lord shall give you the bread of affliction and scant water, yet they that cause thee to err shall no more at all draw nigh to thee; for thine eyes shall see those that cause thee to err,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Proverbs 3:5–6; Isaiah 58:11"> and thine ears shall hear the words of them that went after thee to lead thee astray, who say, This is the way, let us walk in it, whether to the right or to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 17:3–4; Isaiah 31:7; Zechariah 13:2"> And thou shalt pollute the plated idols, and thou shalt grind to powder the gilded ones, and shalt scatter them as the water of a removed woman, and thou shalt thrust them forth as dung.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 8:11–12; Isaiah 32:20; Psalms 65:9–13"> Then shall there be rain to the seed of thy land; and the bread of the fruit of thy land shall be plenteous and rich: and thy cattle shall feed in that day in a fertile and spacious place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 45:6; Deuteronomy 25:4; Kings I 8:12"> Your bulls and your oxen that till the ground, shall eat chaff mixed with winnowed barley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 43:19–20; Isaiah 35:6–7; Isaiah 2:14–15"> And there shall be upon every lofty mountain and upon every high hill, water running in that day, when many shall perish, and when the towers shall fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Deuteronomy 32:39; Revelation 22:5"> And the light of the moon shall be as the light of the sun, and the light of the sun shall be sevenfold in the day when the Lord shall heal the breach of his people, and shall heal the pain of thy wound.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:8; Nahum 1:5–6; Psalms 18:7–9"> Behold, the name of the Lord comes after a long time, burning wrath: the word of his lips is with glory, a word full of anger, and the anger of his wrath shall devour as fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Isaiah 11:4; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> And his breath, as rushing water in a valley, shall reach as far as the neck, and be divided, to confound the nations for their vain error: error also shall pursue them, and overtake them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Psalms 95:1–2; Exodus 15:1–21"> Must ye always rejoice, and go into my holy places continually, as they that keep a feast? and must ye go with a pipe, as those that rejoice, into the mountain of the Lord, to the God of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 18:13–14; Joshua 10:11; Isaiah 29:6"> And the Lord shall make his glorious voice to be heard, and the wrath of his arm, to make a display with wrath and anger and devouring flame: he shall lighten terribly, and his wrath shall be as water and violent hail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 11:4; Isaiah 9:4; Micah 5:5–6"> For by the voice of the Lord the Assyrians shall be overcome, even by the stroke wherewith he shall smite them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 11:15; Isaiah 19:16; Isaiah 2:19"> And it shall happen to him from every side, that they from whom their hope of assistance was, in which he trusted, themselves shall war against him in turn with drums and with harp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Kings IV 23:10; Genesis 19:24; Jeremiah 19:6"> For thou shalt be required before thy time: has it been prepared for thee also to reign? nay, God has prepared for thee a deep trench, wood piled, fire and much wood: the wrath of the Lord shall be as a trench kindled with sulphur.</span>
</div>
</div>
