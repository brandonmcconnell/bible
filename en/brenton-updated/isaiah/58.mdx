<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 2:15; Hosea 8:1; Acts 20:26–27"> Cry aloud, and spare not; lift up thy voice as with a trumpet, and declare to my people their sins, and to the house of Jacob their iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Isaiah 29:13; Matthew 15:7–9"> They seek me day by day, and desire to know my ways, as a people that had done righteousness, and had not forsaken the judgment of their God: they now ask of me righteous judgment, and desire to draw nigh to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 18:9–12; Malachi 3:14; Leviticus 16:29"> saying, Why have we fasted, and thou regardest not? why have we afflicted our souls, and thou didst not know it?<br />Nay, in the days of your fasts ye find your pleasures, and all them that are under your power ye wound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 21:9–13; Isaiah 59:2; Proverbs 21:27"> If ye fast for quarrels and strifes, and smite the lowly with your fists, wherefore do ye fast to me as ye do this day, so that your voice may be heard in crying?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 9:3–19; Zechariah 7:5; Isaiah 49:8"> I have not chosen this fast, nor such a day for a man to afflict his soul; neither though thou shouldest bend down thy neck as a ring, and spread under thee sackcloth and ashes, neither thus shall ye call a fast acceptable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:10–12; Isaiah 58:9; Jeremiah 34:8–11"> I have not chosen such a fast, saith the Lord; but do thou loose every burden of iniquity, do thou untie the knots of hard bargains, set the bruised free, and cancel every unjust account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 25:35–45; Luke 3:11; 1 John 3:17–18"> Break thy bread to the hungry, and lead the unsheltered poor to thy house: if thou seest one naked, clothe him, and thou shalt not disregard the relations of thine own seed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 52:12; Jeremiah 33:6; Psalms 37:6"> Then shall thy light break forth as the morning, and thy health shall speedily spring forth: and thy righteousness shall go before thee, and the glory of God shall compass thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12–13; Psalms 50:15; Isaiah 65:24"> Then shalt thou cry, and God shall hearken to thee; while thou art yet speaking he will say, Behold, I am here. If thou remove from thee the band, and the stretching forth of the hands, and murmuring speech;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; Isaiah 58:7–8; Isaiah 42:16"> and if thou give bread to the hungry from thy heart, and satisfy the afflicted soul; then shall thy light spring up in darkness, and thy darkness shall be as noonday:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 4:14; Jeremiah 31:12; Psalms 32:8"> and thy God shall be with thee continually, and thou shalt be satisfied according as thy soul desires; and thy bones shall be made fat, and shall be as a well-watered garden, and as a fountain from which the water has not failed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 61:4; Ezekiel 36:33; Amos 9:14"> And thy old waste desert places shall be built up, and thy foundations shall last through all generations; and thou shalt be called a repairer of breaches, and thou shalt cause thy paths between to be in peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:21–27; Psalms 92:1–2; Nehemiah 13:15–22"> If thou turn away thy foot from the Sabbath, so as not to do thy pleasure on the holy days, and shalt call the Sabbaths delightful, holy to God; if thou shalt not lift up thy foot to work, nor speak a word in anger out of thy mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Job 22:26; Isaiah 33:16"> then shalt thou trust on the Lord; and he shall bring thee up to the good places of the land, and feed thee with the heritage of Jacob thy father: for the mouth of the Lord has spoken this.</span>
</div>
