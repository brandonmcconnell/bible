<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Isaiah 51:17; Zechariah 3:4"> Awake, awake, Zion; put on thy strength, O Zion; and do thou put on thy glory, Jerusalem the holy city: there shall no more pass through thee the uncircumcised and unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 51:14; Jeremiah 51:50; Luke 4:18"> Shake off the dust and arise; sit down, Jerusalem: put off the band of thy neck, captive daughter of Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18; Isaiah 45:13; Psalms 44:12"> For thus saith the Lord, Ye have been sold for nought; and ye shall not be ransomed with silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:6; Psalms 69:4; Acts 7:14–15"> Thus saith the Lord, My people went down before to Egypt to sojourn there; and were carried away forcibly to the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Psalms 44:16; Psalms 74:10"> And now why are ye here? Thus saith the Lord, Because my people was taken for nothing, wonder ye and howl. Thus saith the Lord, On account of you my name is continually blasphemed among the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 8:10–11; Ezekiel 37:13–14; Ezekiel 20:44"> Therefore shall my people know my name in that day, for I am he that speaks: I am present,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 10:12–15; Nahum 1:15; Isaiah 61:1–3"> as a season of beauty upon the mountains, as the feet of one preaching glad tidings of peace, as one preaching good news: for I will publish thy salvation, saying, O Zion, thy God shall reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 62:6; Ezekiel 33:7; Jeremiah 32:39"> For the voice of them that guard thee is exalted, and with the voice together they shall rejoice: for eyes shall look to eyes, when the Lord shall have mercy upon Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Psalms 98:4; Isaiah 48:20"> Let the waste places of Jerusalem break forth in joy together, because the Lord has had mercy upon her, and has delivered Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 3:6; Isaiah 51:9; Psalms 98:1–3"> And the Lord shall reveal his holy arm in the sight of all the nations; and all the ends of the earth shall see the salvation that comes from our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:17; Zechariah 2:6–7; Isaiah 48:20"> Depart ye, depart, go out from thence, and touch not the unclean thing; go ye out from the midst of her; separate yourselves, ye that bear the vessels of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 58:8; Deuteronomy 20:4; Exodus 14:19–20"> For ye shall not go forth with tumult, neither go by flight: for the Lord shall go first in advance of you; and the God of Israel shall be he that brings up your rear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:7–11; Matthew 28:18; John 3:31"> Behold, my servant shall understand, and be exalted, and glorified exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:2–5; Matthew 26:67"> As many shall be amazed at thee, so shall thy face be without glory from men, and thy glory shall not be honored by the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:20–21; Isaiah 49:7; Isaiah 55:5"> Thus shall many nations wonder at him; and kings shall keep their mouths shut: for they to whom no report was brought concerning him, shall see; and they who have not heard, shall consider.</span>
