<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Isaiah 24:16; Isaiah 10:12"> Woe to them that afflict you; but no one makes you miserable: and he that deals perfidiously with you does not deal perfidiously: they that deal perfidiously shall be taken and given up, and as a moth on a garment, so shall they be spoiled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; Isaiah 59:16; Isaiah 30:18–19"> Lord, have mercy upon us; for we have trusted in thee: the seed of the rebellious is gone to destruction, but our deliverance was in a time of affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:13–14; Isaiah 10:32–34; Isaiah 37:11–18"> By reason of the terrible sound the nations were dismayed for fear of thee, and the heathen were scattered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 33:23; Joel 2:25; Chronicles II 20:25"> And now shall the spoils of your small and great be gathered: as if one should gather locusts, so shall they mock you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 2:17; Ephesians 1:20–21; Isaiah 32:15–18"> The God who dwells on high is holy: Zion is filled with judgment and righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 112:1–3; Proverbs 19:23; 1 Timothy 4:8"> They shall be delivered up to the law: our salvation is our treasure: there are wisdom and knowledge and piety toward the Lord; these are the treasures of righteousness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 18:18; Kings IV 18:37–3; Isaiah 36:22"> Behold now, these shall be terrified with fear of you: those whom ye feared shall cry out because of you: messengers shall be sent, bitterly weeping, entreating for peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 5:6; Isaiah 35:8; Lamentations 1:4"> For the ways of these shall be made desolate: the terror of the nations has been made to cease, and the covenant with these is taken away, and ye shall by no means deem them men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Nahum 1:4; Isaiah 65:10"> The land mourns; Lebanon is ashamed: Sharon is become marshes; Galilee shall be laid bare, and Carmel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 102:13–18; Amos 6:1"> Now will I arise, saith the Lord, now will I be glorified; now will I be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Isaiah 59:4; James 1:15"> Now shall ye see, now shall ye perceive; the strength of your breath shall be vain; fire shall devour you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 27:4; Kings II 23:6–7; Isaiah 10:17"> And the nations shall be burned up; as a thorn in the field cast out and burned up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 48:10; Ephesians 2:11–18; Daniel 3:27–3"> They that are afar off shall hear what I have done; they that draw nigh shall know my strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Matthew 25:46; Isaiah 66:24"> The sinners in Zion have departed; trembling shall seize the ungodly. Who will tell you that a fire is kindled? Who will tell you of the eternal place?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:37; 1 John 3:7; Ephesians 5:11–13"> He that walks in righteousness, speaking rightly, hating transgression and iniquity, and shaking his hands from gifts, stopping his ears that he should not hear the judgment of blood, shutting his eyes that he should not see injustice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 12:29–31; Isaiah 32:18; Proverbs 1:33"> he shall dwell in a high cave of a strong rock: bread shall be given him, and his water shall be sure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 9:17; John 14:21; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> Ye shall see a king with glory: your eyes shall behold a land from afar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20; Psalms 31:7–8; Isaiah 38:9–22"> Your soul shall meditate terror. Where are the scribes? where are the counselors, where is he that numbers them that are growing up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:11; Deuteronomy 28:49–50; Jeremiah 5:15"> even the small and great people? with whom he took not counsel, neither did he understand a people of deep speech, so that a despised people should not hear, and there is no understanding to him that hears.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 46:5; Isaiah 32:18; Isaiah 54:2"> Behold the city Zion, our refuge: thine eyes shall behold Jerusalem, a rich city, tabernacles which shall not be shaken, neither shall the pins of her tabernacle be moved forever, neither shall her cords be at all broken:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 46:4–5; Isaiah 48:18; Isaiah 66:12"> for the name of the Lord is great to you: ye shall have a place, even rivers and wide and spacious channels: thou shalt not go this way, neither a vessel with oars go thereby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 5:31; James 4:12; Zephaniah 3:15–17"> For my God is great: the Lord our judge shall not pass me by: the Lord is our prince, the Lord is our king; the Lord, he shall save us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings IV 7:8; Psalms 68:12; 1 Corinthians 1:27"> Thy cords are broken, for they had no strength: thy mast has given way, it shall not spread the sails, it shall not bear a signal, until it be given up for plunder; therefore shall many lame men take spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:20; 1 John 1:7–9; Revelation 21:4"> And the people dwelling among them shall by no means say, I am in pain: for their sin shall be forgiven them.</span>
</div>
</div>
