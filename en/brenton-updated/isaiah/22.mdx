<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Psalms 125:2; Joel 3:12"> The word of the Valley of Zion. What has happened to thee, that now ye are all gone up to the housetops which help you not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 32:13; Isaiah 23:7; Lamentations 2:20"> The city is filled with shouting men: thy slain are not slain with swords, nor are thy dead those who have died in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings IV 25:4–7; Isaiah 3:1–8; Jeremiah 39:4–7"> All thy princes have fled, and thy captives are tightly bound, and the mighty men in thee have fled far away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:1; Matthew 2:18; Psalms 77:2"> Therefore I said, Let me alone, I will weep bitterly; labor not to comfort me for the breach of the daughter of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 7:4; Isaiah 37:3; Luke 23:30"> For it is a day of trouble, and of destruction, and of treading down, and there is perplexity sent from the Lord of hosts: they wander in the Valley of Zion; they wander from the least to the greatest on the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings IV 16:9; Isaiah 21:2; Amos 1:5"> And the Elamites took their quivers, and there were men mounted on horses, and there was a gathering for battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Isaiah 10:28–32; Jeremiah 39:1–3"> And it shall be that thy choice valleys shall be filled with chariots, and horsemen shall block up thy gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 7:2; Kings III 10:17; Isaiah 36:1–3"> And they shall uncover the gates of Judah, and they shall look in that day on the choice houses of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings IV 20:20; Nehemiah 3:16; Chronicles II 32:1–6"> And they shall uncover the secret places of the houses of the citadel of David: and they saw that they were many, and that one had turned the water of the old pool into the city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and that they had pulled down the houses of Jerusalem, to fortify the wall of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 7:7; Jeremiah 33:2–3; Isaiah 31:1"> And ye procured to yourselves water between the two walls within the ancient pool: but ye looked not to him that made it from the beginning, and regarded not him that created it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Micah 1:16; Joel 1:13"> And the Lord, the Lord of hosts, called in that day for weeping, and lamentation, and baldness, and for girding with sackcloth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 56:12; 1 Corinthians 15:32; Luke 17:26–29"> but they engaged in joy and gladness, slaying calves, and killing sheep, so as to eat flesh, and drink wine; saying, Let us eat and drink; for tomorrow we die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:13; Kings I 3:14; Isaiah 5:9"> And these things are revealed in the ears of the Lord of hosts: for this sin shall not be forgiven you, until ye die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; Kings IV 18:18; Kings IV 19:2"> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, Go into the chamber, to Shebna the treasurer, and say to him, Why art thou here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:60; Chronicles II 16:14; Kings II 18:18"> and what hast thou to do here, that thou hast here hewn thyself a sepulcher, and madest thyself a sepulcher on high, and hast graven for thyself a dwelling in the rock?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3; Job 9:24; Esther 7:8"> Behold now, the Lord of hosts casts forth and will utterly destroy such a man, and will take away thy robe and thy glorious crown,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 17:13; Amos 7:17"> and will cast thee into a great and unmeasured land, and there thou shalt die: and he will bring thy fair chariot to shame, and the house of thy prince to be trodden down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; Psalms 75:6–7; Job 40:11–12"> And thou shalt be removed from thy stewardship, and from thy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; Kings IV 18:18; Isaiah 37:2"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that I will call my servant Eliakim the son of Hilkiah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:8; Isaiah 9:6–7; Genesis 41:42–43"> and I will put on him thy robe, and I will grant him thy crown with power, and I will give thy stewardship into his hands: and he shall be as a father to them that dwell in Jerusalem, and to them that dwell in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:18–19; Revelation 3:7; Job 12:14"> And I will give him the glory of David; and he shall rule, and there shall be none to speak against him: and I will give him the key of the house of David upon his shoulder; and he shall open, and there shall be none to shut; and he shall shut, and there shall be none to open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 9:8; Job 36:7; Zechariah 10:4"> And I will make him a ruler in a sure place, and he shall be for a glorious throne of his father’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 6:1–3; John 20:21–23; Matthew 28:18"> And every one that is glorious in the house of his father shall trust in him, from the least to the greatest; and they shall depend upon him in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 46:11; Esther 9:24–25; Psalms 146:3"> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, The man that is fastened in the sure place shall be removed and be taken away, and shall fall; and the glory that is upon him shall be utterly destroyed: for the Lord has spoken it.</span>
