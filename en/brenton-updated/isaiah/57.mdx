<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings IV 22:20; Psalms 12:1; Chronicles II 34:28"> See how the just man has perished, and no one lays it to heart: and righteous men are taken away, and no one considers: for the righteous has been removed out of the way of injustice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Luke 2:29"> His burial shall be in peace: he has been removed out of the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 3:10; Matthew 16:4; Hosea 1:2"> But draw ye near hither, ye lawless children, the seed of adulterers and the harlot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 2:13; Psalms 35:21; Isaiah 1:4"> Wherein have ye been rioting? and against whom have ye opened your mouth, and against whom have ye loosed your tongue? are ye not children of perdition? a lawless seed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; Kings IV 16:3–4; Ezekiel 16:20"> who call upon idols under the leafy trees, slaying your children in the valleys among the rocks?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:9; Jeremiah 7:18; Habakkuk 2:19"> That is thy portion, this is thy lot: and to them hast thou poured forth drink offerings, and to these hast thou offered meat offerings. Shall I not therefore be angry for these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:16; Ezekiel 23:41; Jeremiah 2:20"> On a lofty and high mountain, there is thy bed, and thither thou carriedst up thy meat offerings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:32; Ezekiel 23:2–20; Ezekiel 16:25–28"> and behind the posts of thy door thou didst place thy memorials. Didst thou think that if thou shouldest depart from me, thou wouldest gain? thou hast loved those that lay with thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:16; Colossians 2:18; Ezekiel 23:40"> and thou hast multiplied thy whoredom with them, and thou hast increased the number of them that are far from thee, and hast sent ambassadors beyond thy borders, and hast been debased even to hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:25; Jeremiah 18:12; Jeremiah 5:3"> Thou hast wearied thyself with thy many ways; yet thou saidst not, I will cease to strengthen myself: for thou hast done these things; therefore thou hast not supplicated me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:12–13; Proverbs 29:25; Psalms 50:21"> Through dread of whom hast thou feared, and lied against me, and hast not remembered, nor considered me, nor regarded me, yea, though when I see thee I pass thee by, yet thou hast not feared me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Micah 3:2–4; Isaiah 1:11–15"> And I will declare thy righteousness, and thy sins, which shall not profit thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 37:9; Psalms 37:3; Isaiah 56:7"> When thou criest out, let them deliver thee in thine affliction: for all these the wind shall take, and the tempest shall carry them away: but they that cleave to me shall possess the land, and shall inherit my holy mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 62:10; Isaiah 35:8; Isaiah 40:3"> And they shall say, Clear the ways before him, and take up the stumbling blocks out of the way of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 34:18; Isaiah 66:1–2; Psalms 138:6"> Thus saith the Most High, who dwells on high forever, Holy in the holies, is his name, the Most High resting in the holies, and giving patience to the faint-hearted, and giving life to the brokenhearted:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Micah 7:18; Jeremiah 10:24; Psalms 103:9–16"> I will not take vengeance on you forever, neither will I be always angry with you: for my Spirit shall go forth from me, and I have created all breath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Jeremiah 6:13; Colossians 3:5"> On account of sin for a little while I grieved him, and smote him, and turned away my face from him; and he was grieved, and he went on sorrowful in his ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:6; Revelation 7:17; Jeremiah 3:22"> I have seen his ways, and healed him, and comforted him, and given him true comfort;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Acts 2:39; Luke 2:14"> peace upon peace to them that are far off, and to them that are nigh: and the Lord has said, I will heal them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 3:11; Proverbs 4:16–17; Job 18:5–14"> But the unrighteous shall be tossed as troubled waves, and shall not be able to rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 48:22; Isaiah 3:11; Kings IV 9:22"> There is no joy to the ungodly, said God.</span>
