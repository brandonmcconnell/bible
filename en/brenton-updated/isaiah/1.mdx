<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings IV 18:1–21; Chronicles II 26:1–33; Isaiah 6:1"> The vision which Isaiah the son of Amoz saw, which he saw against Judah and against Jerusalem, in the reign of Uzziah, and Jotham, and Ahaz, and Hezekiah, who reigned over Judea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Deuteronomy 30:19; Micah 1:2"> Hear, O heaven, and hearken, O earth: for the Lord has spoken, saying, I have begotten and reared up children, but they have rebelled against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Jeremiah 4:22; Isaiah 44:18"> The ox knows his owner, and the ass his master’s crib: but Israel does not know me, and the people has not regarded me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Jeremiah 2:17; Jeremiah 2:13"> Ah sinful nation, a people full of sins, an evil seed, lawless children: ye have forsaken the Lord, and provoked the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 9:8–11; Ezekiel 24:13; Jeremiah 5:3"> Why should ye be smitten anymore, transgressing more and more? the whole head is pained, and the whole heart sad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 33:6; Matthew 9:12"> From the feet to the head, there is no soundness in them; neither wound, nor bruise, nor festering ulcer are healed: it is not possible to apply a plaster, nor oil, nor bandages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Lamentations 5:2; Deuteronomy 28:43; Deuteronomy 28:33"> Your land is desolate, your cities burned with fire: your land, strangers devour it in your presence, and it is made desolate, overthrown by strange nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; John 12:15; Zechariah 2:10"> The daughter of Zion shall be deserted as a tent in a vineyard, and as a storehouse of fruits in a garden of cucumbers, as a besieged city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 9:29; Isaiah 37:31–32; Genesis 19:24"> And if the Lord of Sabaoth had not left us a seed, we should have been as Sodom, and we should have been made like to Gomorrah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 11:8; Ezekiel 16:49; Amos 9:7"> Hear the word of the Lord, ye rulers of Sodom; attend to the law of God, thou people of Gomorrah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 15:22; Amos 5:21; Jeremiah 6:20"> Of what value to me is the abundance of your sacrifices? saith the Lord: I am full of whole burnt offerings of rams; and I delight not in the fat of lambs, and the blood of bulls and goats:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 34:23; Exodus 23:17; Micah 6:8"> neither shall ye come with these to appear before me; for who has required these things at your hands? Ye shall no more tread my court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 66:3; Malachi 1:10; Matthew 15:9"> Though ye bring fine flour, it is vain; incense is an abomination to me; I cannot bear your new moons, and your Sabbaths, and the great day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:21; Isaiah 43:24; Isaiah 7:13"> your fasting, and rest from work, your new moons also, and your feasts my soul hates: ye have become loathsome to me; I will no more pardon your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 3:4; Isaiah 59:2–3; Proverbs 1:28"> When ye stretch forth your hands, I will turn away mine eyes from you: and though ye make many supplications, I will not hearken to you; for your hands are full of blood.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:11; Isaiah 55:6–7; 1 Peter 2:1"> Wash you, be clean; remove your iniquities from your souls before mine eyes; cease from your iniquities;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Jeremiah 22:3; Psalms 82:3–4"> learn to do well; diligently seek judgment, deliver him that is suffering wrong, plead for the orphan, and obtain justice for the widow.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 44:22; Revelation 7:14; Psalms 51:7"> And come, let us reason together, saith the Lord: and though your sins be as purple, I will make them white as snow; and though they be as scarlet, I will make them white as wool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:15–16; Isaiah 3:10; Joel 2:26"> And if ye be willing, and hearken to me, ye shall eat the good of the land:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; Isaiah 58:14; Numbers 23:19"> but if ye be not willing, nor hearken to me, a sword shall devour you: for the mouth of the Lord has spoken this.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:1–23; Zechariah 8:3; Zephaniah 3:1–3"> How has the faithful city Zion, once full of judgment, become a harlot! wherein righteousness lodged, but now murderers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:28–30; Lamentations 4:1–2; Hosea 4:18"> Your silver is worthless, thy wine merchants mix the wine with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Micah 7:3; Exodus 23:8; Zechariah 7:10"> Thy princes are rebellious, companions of thieves, loving bribes, seeking after rewards; not pleading for orphans, and not heeding the cause of widows.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 35:4; Deuteronomy 28:63"> Therefore thus saith the Lord, the Lord of hosts, Woe to the mighty men of Israel; for my wrath shall not cease against mine adversaries, and I will execute judgment on mine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Malachi 3:3; Jeremiah 9:7; Isaiah 4:4"> And I will bring my hand upon thee, and purge thee completely, and I will destroy the rebellious, and will take away from thee all transgressors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:7; Zechariah 8:3; Isaiah 62:1–2"> And I will establish thy judges as before, and thy counselors as at the beginning: and afterward thou shalt be called the city of righteousness, the faithful mother-city Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 62:12; Isaiah 45:21–25; Isaiah 5:16"> For her captives shall be saved with judgment, and with mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:8–9; Psalms 1:6; Isaiah 30:13"> And the transgressors and the sinners shall be crushed together, and they that forsake the Lord shall be utterly consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 65:3; Isaiah 57:5; Isaiah 66:17"> For they shall be ashamed of their idols, which they delighted in, and they are made ashamed of the gardens which they coveted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5–6; Ezekiel 17:9–10; Matthew 21:19"> For they shall be as a turpentine tree that has cast its leaves, and as a garden that has no water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 66:24; Ezekiel 32:21; Malachi 4:1"> And their strength shall be as a thread of tow, and their works as sparks, and the transgressors and the sinners shall be burned up together, and there shall be none to quench them.</span>
</div>
</div>
