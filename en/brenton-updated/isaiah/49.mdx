<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Galatians 1:15; Isaiah 44:2"> Hearken to me, ye islands; and attend, ye Gentiles; after a long time it shall come to pass, saith the Lord: from my mother’s womb he has called my name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 1:16; Isaiah 50:4"> and he has made my mouth as a sharp sword, and he has hidden me under the shadow of his hand; he has made me as a choice shaft, and he has hidden me in his quiver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; 1 Peter 2:9; Isaiah 44:23"> and said to me, Thou art my servant, O Israel, and in thee I will be glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; Galatians 4:11; Isaiah 53:10–12"> Then I said, I have labored in vain, I have given my strength for vanity and for nothing: therefore is my judgment with the Lord, and my labor before my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Matthew 17:5; John 3:35"> And now, thus saith the Lord that formed me from the womb to be his own servant, to gather Jacob to him and Israel. I shall be gathered and glorified before the Lord, and my God shall be my strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 13:47; Isaiah 42:6; Luke 2:32"> And he said to me, It is a great thing for thee to be called my servant, to establish the tribes of Jacob, and to recover the dispersion of Israel: behold, I have given thee for the covenant of a race, for a light of the Gentiles, that thou shouldest be for salvation to the end of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; 1 Peter 2:4; Isaiah 53:3"> Thus saith the Lord that delivered thee, the God of Israel, Sanctify him that despises his life, him that is abhorred by the nations that are the servants of princes: kings shall behold him, and princes shall arise, and shall worship him, for the Lord’s sake: for the Holy One of Israel is faithful, and I have chosen thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:2; Isaiah 42:6; Psalms 69:13"> Thus saith the Lord, In an acceptable time have I heard thee, and in a day of salvation have I succored thee: and I have formed thee, and given thee for a covenant of the nations, to establish the earth, and to cause to inherit the desert heritages:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 42:7; Isaiah 42:16; 1 Peter 2:9"> saying to them that are in bonds, Go forth; and bidding them that are in darkness show themselves. They shall be fed in all the ways, and in all the paths shall be their pasture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 7:16–17; Matthew 5:6; Ezekiel 34:23"> They shall not hunger, neither shall they thirst; neither shall the heat nor the sun smite them; but he that has mercy on them shall comfort them, and by fountains of waters shall he lead them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 11:16; Isaiah 62:10; Isaiah 35:8–10"> And I will make every mountain a way, and every path a pasture to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5–6; Matthew 8:11; Revelation 11:15"> Behold, these shall come from far: and these from the north and the west, and others from the land of the Persians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 44:23; Isaiah 51:3; Psalms 96:11–13"> Rejoice, ye heavens; and let the earth be glad: let the mountains break forth with joy; for the Lord has had mercy on his people, and has comforted the lowly ones of his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:27; Romans 11:1–5; Psalms 22:1"> But Zion said, The Lord has forsaken me, and, The Lord has forgotten me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 44:21; Psalms 103:13; Matthew 7:11"> Will a woman forget her child, so as not to have compassion upon the offspring of her womb? but if a woman should even forget these, yet I will not forget thee, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Haggai 2:23; Isaiah 62:6; Isaiah 60:18"> Behold, I have painted thy walls on my hands, and thou art continually before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 51:13; Isaiah 62:5; Ezekiel 28:24"> And thou shalt soon be built by those by whom thou wert destroyed, and they that made thee desolate shall go forth of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 54:1–3; Isaiah 66:12–13; Isaiah 61:10"> Lift up thine eyes round about, and look on them all; behold, they are gathered together, and are come to thee. As I live, saith the Lord, thou shalt clothe thyself with them all as with an ornament, and put them on as a bride her attire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 10:10; Isaiah 54:1–2; Jeremiah 30:18–19"> For thy desert and marred and ruined places shall now be too narrow by reason of the inhabitants, and they that devoured thee shall be removed far from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hosea 1:10; Joshua 17:14–16; Galatians 4:26–28"> For thy sons whom thou hast lost shall say in thine ears, The place is too narrow for me: make room for me that I may dwell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13; Isaiah 3:26; Lamentations 1:1–3"> And thou shalt say in thy heart, Who has begotten me these? whereas I was childless, and a widow; but who has brought up these for me? and I was left alone; but whence came these to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Luke 13:29; Isaiah 14:2"> Thus saith the Lord, even the Lord, Behold, I lift up my hand to the nations, and I will lift up my signal to the islands: and they shall bring thy sons in their bosom, and shall bear thy daughters on their shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:14; Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 60:3"> And kings shall be thy nursing fathers, and their princesses thy nurses, they shall bow down to thee on the face of the earth, and shall lick the dust of thy feet; and thou shalt know that I am the Lord, and they that wait on me shall not be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 11:21–22; Psalms 124:6–7; Matthew 12:29"> Will anyone take spoils from a giant? and if one should take a man captive unjustly, shall he be delivered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 54:15–17; Isaiah 35:4; Isaiah 41:11–12"> For thus saith the Lord, If one should take a giant captive, he shall take spoils, and he who takes them from a mighty man shall be delivered: for I will plead thy cause, and I will deliver thy children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Isaiah 41:14–20; Revelation 16:6"> And they that afflicted thee shall eat their own flesh; and they shall drink their own blood as new wine, and shall be drunken: and all flesh shall perceive that I am the Lord that delivers thee, and that upholds the strength of Jacob.</span>
