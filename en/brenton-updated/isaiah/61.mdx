<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:18–19; Luke 7:22; Isaiah 42:7"> The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he has anointed me; he has sent me to preach glad tidings to the poor, to heal the broken in heart, to proclaim liberty to the captives, and recovery of sight to the blind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 5:4; Isaiah 34:8; Luke 4:19"> to declare the acceptable year of the Lord, and the day of recompense; to comfort all that mourn;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 30:11; Jeremiah 17:7–8; John 16:20"> that there should be given to them that mourn in Zion glory instead of ashes, the oil of joy to the mourners, the garment of glory for the spirit of heaviness: and they shall be called generations of righteousness, the planting of the Lord for glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 58:12; Ezekiel 36:33–36; Amos 9:14–15"> And they shall build the old waste places, they shall raise up those that were before made desolate, and shall renew the desert cities, even those that had been desolate for many generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 60:10–14; Isaiah 14:1–2; Ephesians 2:12–20"> And strangers shall come and feed thy flocks, and aliens shall be thy plowmen and vinedressers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 19:6; 1 Peter 2:5; 1 Peter 2:9"> But ye shall be called priests of the Lord, the ministers of God: ye shall eat the strength of nations, and shall be admired because of their wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 42:10; Zechariah 9:12; Isaiah 40:2"> Thus shall they inherit the land a second time, and everlasting joy shall be upon their head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 11:7; Jeremiah 9:24; Zechariah 8:16–17"> For I am the Lord who love righteousness, and hate robberies of injustice; and I will give their labor to the just, and will make an everlasting covenant with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Psalms 115:14; Genesis 22:18"> And their seed shall be known among the Gentiles, and their offspring in the midst of peoples: everyone that sees them shall take notice of them, that they are a seed blessed of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–8; Psalms 132:9; Psalms 132:16"> and they shall greatly rejoice in the Lord. Let my soul rejoice in the Lord; for he has clothed me with the robe of salvation, and the garment of joy: he has put a miter on me as on a bridegroom, and adorned me with ornaments as a bride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 4:26–32; Isaiah 58:11; 1 Peter 2:9"> And as the earth putting forth her flowers, and as a garden its seed; so shall the Lord, even the Lord, cause righteousness to spring forth, and exultation before all nations.</span>
