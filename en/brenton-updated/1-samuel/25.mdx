<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings I 28:3; Numbers 20:29; Deuteronomy 34:8"> And Samuel died, and all Israel assembled, and bewailed him, and they bury him in his house in Ramah: and David arose, and went down to the wilderness of Maon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 38:13; Kings I 23:24; Joshua 15:55"> And there was a man in Maon, and his flocks were in Carmel, and he was a very great man; and he had three thousand sheep, and a thousand she-goats: and he happened to be shearing his flock in Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; Proverbs 31:10; Isaiah 32:5–7"> And the man’s name was Nabal, and his wife’s name was Abigail: and his wife was of good understanding and very beautiful in person: but the man was harsh and evil in his doings, and the man was churlish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 38:13; Kings II 13:23"> And David heard in the wilderness, that Nabal the Carmelite was shearing his sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 43:23; Kings I 17:22"> And David sent ten young men, and he said to the young men, Go up to Carmel, and go to Nabal, and ask him in my name how he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 10:5; Chronicles I 12:18; Psalms 122:7"> And thus shall ye say, May thou and thy house seasonably prosper, and all thine be in prosperity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings I 25:21; Kings I 25:15–16; Kings I 22:2"> And now, behold, I have heard that thy shepherds who were with us in the wilderness are shearing thy sheep, and we hindered them not, neither did we demand anything from them all the time they were in Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esther 9:19; Nehemiah 8:10–12; Luke 14:12–14"> Ask thy servants, and they will tell thee. Let then thy servants find grace in thine eyes, for we are come on a good day; give, we pray thee, whatsoever thy hand may find, to thy son David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings IV 2:15; Chronicles II 14:7; Genesis 8:4"> So the servants come and speak these words to Nabal, according to all these words in the name of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 9:28; Isaiah 32:7; Kings I 22:7–8"> And Nabal sprang up, and answered the servants of David, and said, Who is David? and who is the son of Jesse? nowadays there is abundance of servants who depart every one from his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 8:6; Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 6:9"> And shall I take my bread, and my wine, and my beasts that I have slain for my shearers, and shall I give them to men of whom I know not whence they are?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 36:21–22; Kings II 24:13; Hebrews 13:17"> So the servants of David turned back, and returned, and came and reported to David according to these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings I 23:13; Proverbs 25:8; Kings I 24:5–6"> And David said to his men, Gird on every man his sword. And they went up after David, about four hundred men: and two hundred abode with the stuff.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings I 13:10; Mark 15:29"> And one of the servants reported to Abigail the wife of Nabal, saying, Behold, David sent messengers out of the wilderness to salute our lord; but he turned away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 25:7; Kings I 25:21; Philippians 2:15"> And the men were very good to us; they did not hinder us, neither did they demand from us anything all the days that we were with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 1:10; Exodus 14:22; Zechariah 2:5"> And when we were in the field, they were as a wall round about us, both by night and by day, all the days that we were with them feeding the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings I 20:7; Deuteronomy 13:13; Kings I 20:9"> And now do thou consider, and see what thou wilt do; for mischief is determined against our lord and against his house; and he is a vile character, and one cannot speak to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings II 16:1; Matthew 5:25; Proverbs 6:4–5"> And Abigail hastened, and took two hundred loaves, and two vessels of wine, and five sheep ready dressed, and five ephahs of fine flour, and one homer of dried grapes, and two hundred cakes of figs, and put them upon asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 32:20; Genesis 32:16; Proverbs 31:11–12"> And she said to her servants, Go on before me, and behold I come after you: but she told not her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings IV 4:24"> And it came to pass when she had mounted her ass and was going down by the covert of the mountain, behold, David and his men came down to meet her, and she met them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 17:13; Ephesians 4:31; Romans 12:21"> And David said, Perhaps I have kept all his possessions in the wilderness that he should wrong me, and we did not order the taking anything of all his goods; yet he has rewarded me evil for good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings III 14:10; Kings I 20:13; Kings I 3:17"> So God do to David and more also, if I leave one male of all that belong to Nabal until the morning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings I 20:41; Judges 1:14; Joshua 15:18"> And Abigail saw David, and she hastened, and alighted from her ass; and she fell before David on her face, and did obeisance to him, bowing to the ground</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esther 8:3; Kings I 25:28; Kings II 14:9"> even to his feet, and said, On me, my lord, be my wrong: let, I pray thee, thy servant speak in thine ears, and hear thou the words of thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings I 25:17; Malachi 2:2; Kings II 13:33"> Let not my lord, I pray thee, take to heart this pestilent man, for according to his name, so is he; Nabal is his name, and folly is with him: but I thy handmaid saw not the servants of my lord whom thou didst send.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings II 18:32; Romans 12:19–20; Genesis 20:6"> And now, my lord, as the Lord lives, and thy soul lives, as the Lord has kept thee from coming against innocent blood, and from executing vengeance for thyself, now therefore let thine enemies, and those that seek evil against my lord, become as Nabal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 33:11; Kings I 30:26; Kings IV 5:15"> And now accept this token of goodwill, which thy servant has brought to my lord, and thou shalt give it to the servants that wait on my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings I 18:17; Kings II 7:11; Kings I 24:11"> Remove, I pray thee, the trespass of thy servant; for the Lord will surely make for my lord a sure house, for the Lord fights the battles of my lord, and there shall no evil be ever found in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 17:21; Matthew 10:29–30; Malachi 3:17"> And if a man shall rise up persecuting thee and seeking thy life, yet shall the life of my lord be bound up in the bundle of life with the Lord God, and thou shalt whirl the life of thine enemies as in the midst of a sling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings I 13:14; Psalms 89:20; Kings I 23:17"> And it shall be when the Lord shall have wrought for my lord all the good things he has spoken concerning thee, and shall appoint thee to be ruler over Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 40:14; Romans 12:19; Luke 23:42"> then this shall not be an abomination and offense to my lord, to have shed innocent blood without cause, and for my lord to have avenged himself: and so may the Lord do good to my lord, and thou shalt remember thy handmaid to do her good.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 1:68; Exodus 18:10; Psalms 72:18"> And David said to Abigail, Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, who sent thee this very day to meet me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Kings I 25:26; Proverbs 28:23; Proverbs 9:9"> and blessed be thy conduct, and blessed be thou, who hast hindered me this very day from coming to shed blood, and from avenging myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Kings I 11:11; Kings I 25:26; Joshua 10:6"> But surely as the Lord God of Israel lives, who hindered me this day from doing thee harm, if thou hadst not hastened and come to meet me, then I said, There shall surely not be left to Nabal till the morning one male.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 19:21; Kings I 20:42; Kings IV 5:19"> And David took of her hand all that she brought to him, and said to her, Go in peace to thy house: see, I have hearkened to thy voice, and accepted thy petition.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Kings I 25:19; Kings II 13:23; Kings II 13:28"> And Abigail came to Nabal: and, behold, he had a banquet in his house, as the banquet of a king, and the heart of Nabal was merry within him, and he was very drunken: and she told him nothing great or small till the morning light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:28; Kings I 25:34; Kings I 25:22"> And it came to pass in the morning, when Nabal recovered from his wine, his wife told him these words; and his heart died within him, and he became as a stone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Kings I 26:10; Kings IV 15:5; Kings I 6:9"> And it came to pass after about ten days, that the Lord smote Nabal, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Kings I 25:26; Kings I 25:34; Proverbs 22:23"> And David heard it and said, Blessed be the Lord, who has judged the cause of my reproach at the hand of Nabal, and has delivered his servant from the power of evil; and the Lord has returned the mischief of Nabal upon his own head.<br />And David sent and spoke concerning Abigail, to take her to himself for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 24:51; Genesis 24:37–38"> So the servants of David came to Abigail to Carmel, and spoke to her, saying, David has sent us to thee, to take thee to himself for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; John 13:3–5; Ruth 2:10"> And she arose, and did reverence with her face to the earth, and said, Behold, thy servant is for a handmaid to wash the feet of thy servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 24:61–67; Psalms 45:10–11"> And Abigail arose, and mounted her ass, and five damsels followed her: and she went after the servants of David, and became his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Kings I 30:5; Joshua 15:56; Kings I 27:3"> And David took Ahinoam out of Jezreel, and they were both his wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Kings II 3:14; Kings I 18:27; Kings I 18:20"> And Saul gave Michal his daughter, David’s wife, to Palti the son of Laish who was of Romma.</span>
</div>
