</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings I 4:15; Psalms 90:10; Genesis 27:1"> And it came to pass at that time that Eli was sleeping in his place; and his eyes began to fail, and could not see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Leviticus 24:2–4; Exodus 27:20–21"> And the lamp of God was burning before it was trimmed, and Samuel slept in the temple, where was the ark of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 6:8; 1 Corinthians 12:6–11; Exodus 3:4"> And the Lord called, Samuel, Samuel; and he said, Behold, here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he ran to Eli, and said, Here am I, for thou didst call me: and he said, I did not call thee; return, go to sleep; and he returned and went to sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; Kings I 4:16; Kings II 18:22"> And the Lord called again, Samuel, Samuel: and he went to Eli the second time, and said, Behold here am I, for thou didst call me: and he said, I called thee not; return, go to sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:2; Jeremiah 9:24"> And it was before Samuel knew the Lord, and before the word of the Lord was revealed to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 33:14–15; 1 Corinthians 13:11–12"> And the Lord called Samuel again for the third time: and he arose and went to Eli, and said, Behold, I am here, for thou didst call me: and Eli perceived that the Lord had called the child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Isaiah 6:8; Psalms 85:8"> And he said, Return, child, go to sleep; and it shall come to pass if he shall call thee, that thou shalt say, Speak, for thy servant hears: and Samuel went and lay down in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings I 3:8; Kings I 3:4–6"> And the Lord came, and stood, and called him as before: and Samuel said, Speak, for thy servant hears.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; Jeremiah 19:3; Kings IV 21:12"> And the Lord said to Samuel, Behold, I execute my words in Israel; whoever hears them, both his ears shall tingle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings I 2:27–36; Zechariah 1:6; Joshua 23:15"> In that day I will raise up against Eli all things that I have said against his house; I will begin, and I will make an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 19:18; Matthew 10:37; Kings I 2:12"> And I have told him that I will be avenged on his house perpetually for the iniquities of his sons, because his sons spoke evil against God, and he did not admonish them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 22:14; Kings I 2:25; Jeremiah 15:1"> And it shall not go on so; I have sworn to the house of Eli, the iniquity of the house of Eli shall not be atoned for with incense or sacrifices forever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:6–8; Malachi 1:10; Kings I 1:9"> And Samuel slept till morning, and rose early in the morning, and opened the doors of the house of the Lord; and Samuel feared to tell Eli the vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Eli said to Samuel, Samuel, my son; and he said, Behold, here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings II 3:35; Ruth 1:17; Psalms 141:5"> And he said, What was the word that was spoken to thee? I pray thee hide it not from me: may God do these things to thee, and more also, if thou hide from me anything of all the words that were spoken to thee in thine ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 2:10; Isaiah 39:8; Judges 10:15"> And Samuel reported all the words, and hid them not from him. And Eli said, He is the Lord, he shall do that which is good in his sight.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 39:2; Kings I 9:6; Kings III 8:56"> And Samuel grew, and the Lord was with him, and there did not fall one of his words to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Kings II 3:10; 1 Timothy 1:12"> And all Israel knew from Dan even to Beersheba, that Samuel was faithful as a prophet to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings I 3:1; Genesis 12:7; Kings I 3:4"> And the Lord manifested himself again in Shiloh, for the Lord revealed himself to Samuel; and Samuel was accredited to all Israel as a prophet to the Lord from one end of the land to the other: and Eli was very old, and his sons kept advancing in wickedness, and their way was evil before the Lord.</span>
</div>
