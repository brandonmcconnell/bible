<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings I 17:1; Kings I 29:1–3; Kings I 7:7"> And it came to pass in those days that the Philistines gathered themselves together with their armies to go out to fight with Israel; and Achish said to David, Know surely, that thou shalt go forth to battle with me, thou, and thy men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 12:9; Kings II 16:16–19; Kings I 27:10"> And David said to Achish, Thus now thou shalt know what thy servant will do. And Achish said to David, So will I make thee captain of my bodyguard continually.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Kings I 25:1; Leviticus 20:27"> And Samuel died, and all Israel lamented for him, and they bury him in his city, in Ramah. And Saul had removed those who had in them divining spirits, and the wizards, out of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings I 31:1; Joshua 19:18; Kings IV 4:8"> And the Philistines assemble themselves, and come and encamp in Shunem: and Saul gathers all the men of Israel, and they encamp in Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 57:20–21; Daniel 5:6; Isaiah 21:3–4"> And Saul saw the camp of the Philistines, and he was alarmed, and his heart was greatly dismayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 12:6; Kings I 14:37; Lamentations 2:9"> And Saul inquired of the Lord; and the Lord answered him not by dreams, nor by manifestations, nor by prophets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 16:16; Joshua 17:11; Chronicles I 10:13"> Then Saul said to his servants, Seek for me a woman who has in her a divining spirit, and I will go to her, and inquire of her: and his servants said to him, Behold, there is a woman who has in her a divining spirit at En-dor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; Chronicles I 10:13; Kings III 22:30"> And Saul disguised himself, and put on other raiment, and he goes, and two men with him, and they come to the woman by night; and he said to her, Divine to me, I pray thee, by the divining spirit within thee, and bring up to me him whom I shall name to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 28:3; Kings IV 5:7; Kings II 18:13"> And the woman said to him, Behold now, thou knowest what Saul has done, how he has cut off those who had in them divining spirits, and the wizards from the land, and why dost thou spread a snare for my life to destroy it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings II 14:11; Kings I 19:6; Deuteronomy 18:10–12"> And Saul swore to her, and said, As the Lord lives, no injury shall come upon thee on this account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the woman said, Whom shall I bring up to thee? and he said, Bring up to me Samuel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings I 28:3; Kings III 14:5"> And the woman saw Samuel, and cried out with a loud voice: and the woman said to Saul, Why hast thou deceived me? for thou art Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 82:6–7; John 10:34–35; Exodus 22:28"> And the king said to her, Fear not; tell me whom thou hast seen. And the woman said to him, I saw gods ascending out of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings I 15:27; Kings IV 2:8; Kings I 24:8"> And he said to her, What didst thou perceive? and she said to him, An upright man ascending out of the earth, and he was clothed with a mantle. And Saul knew that this was Samuel, and he stooped with his face to the earth, and did obeisance to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 16:23–26; Kings I 28:6; Kings I 18:12"> And Samuel said, Why hast thou troubled me, that I should come up? And Saul said, I am greatly distressed, and the Philistines war against me, and God has departed from me, and no longer hearkens to me either by the hand of prophets or by dreams: and now I have called thee to tell me what I shall do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings IV 6:27; Lamentations 2:5; Psalms 68:1–3"> And Samuel said, Why askest thou me, whereas the Lord has departed from thee, and taken part with thy neighbor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings I 15:27–29; Kings I 16:13; Kings I 13:13–14"> And the Lord has done to thee, as the Lord spoke by me; and the Lord will rend thy kingdom out of thy hand, and will give it to thy neighbor David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings I 15:9; Kings III 20:42; Psalms 50:21–22"> because thou didst not hearken to the voice of the Lord, and didst not execute his fierce anger upon Amalek, therefore the Lord has done this thing to thee this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 5:5; Jeremiah 28:16–17; Kings III 22:20"> And the Lord shall deliver Israel with thee into the hands of the Philistines, and tomorrow thou and thy sons with thee shall fall, and the Lord shall deliver the army of Israel into the hands of the Philistines.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings I 28:5; Job 26:2; Job 15:20–24"> And Saul instantly fell at his full length upon the earth, and was greatly afraid because of the words of Samuel; and there was no longer any strength in him, for he had eaten no bread all that day, and all that night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 12:3; Job 13:14; Kings I 19:5"> And the woman went in to Saul, and saw that he was greatly disquieted, and said to him, Behold now, thy handmaid has hearkened to thy voice, and I have put my life in my hand, and have heard the words which thou hast spoken to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And now hearken, I pray thee, to the voice of thy handmaid, and I will set before thee a morsel of bread, and eat, and thou shalt be strengthened, for thou wilt be going on thy way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings III 21:4; Luke 24:29; Luke 14:23"> But he would not eat; so his servants and the woman constrained him, and he hearkened to their voice, and rose up from the earth, and sat upon a bench.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 15:23; Genesis 18:7–8"> And the woman had a fat heifer in the house; and she hastened and slew it; and she took meal and kneaded it, and baked unleavened cakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And she brought the meat before Saul, and before his servants; and they ate, and rose up, and departed that night.</span>
</div>
