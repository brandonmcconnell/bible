<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings I 10:26; Kings I 13:5; Kings I 14:31"> And Saul chooses for himself three thousand men of the men of Israel: and there were with Saul two thousand who were in Michmash, and in Mount Bethel, and a thousand were with Jonathan in Gibeah of Benjamin: and he sent the rest of the people every man to his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings I 10:5; Judges 3:27; Judges 6:34"> And Jonathan smote Nasib the Philistine that dwelt in the hill; and the Philistines hear of it, and Saul sounds the trumpet through all the land, saying, The servants have despised us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Exodus 5:21; Zechariah 11:8"> And all Israel heard say, Saul has smitten Nasib the Philistine; now Israel had been put to shame before the Philistines; and the children of Israel went up after Saul in Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings I 14:23; Joshua 11:4; Joshua 18:12"> And the Philistines gather together to war with Israel; and then come up against Israel thirty thousand chariots, and six thousand horsemen, and people as the sand by the seashore for multitude: and they come up, and encamp in Michmash, opposite Beth-aven southward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 6:2; Hebrews 11:38; Judges 10:9"> And the men of Israel saw that they were in a strait so that they could not draw nigh, and the people hid themselves in caves, and sheepfolds, and rocks, and ditches, and pits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:8; Leviticus 26:17; Numbers 32:1–5"> And they that went over went over Jordan to the land of Gad and Gilead: and Saul was yet in Gilgal, and all the people followed after him in amazement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings I 10:8"> And he continued seven days for the appointed testimony, as Samuel told him, and Samuel came not to Gilgal, and his people were dispersed from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 3:4; Proverbs 21:27; Proverbs 15:8"> And Saul said, Bring hither victims, that I may offer whole burnt offerings and peace offerings: and he offered the whole burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings I 15:13; Ruth 2:4; Psalms 129:8"> And it came to pass when he had finished offering the whole burnt offering, that Samuel arrived, and Saul went out to meet him, and to bless him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 13:23; Kings I 13:5; Kings I 13:16"> And Samuel said, What hast thou done? and Saul said, Because I saw how the people were scattered from me, and thou wast not present as thou purposedst according to the set time of the days, and the Philistines were gathered to Michmash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 66:3; Amos 8:5; 2 Corinthians 9:7"> Then I said, Now will the Philistines come down to me to Gilgal, and I have not sought the face of the Lord: so I forced myself and offered the whole burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings I 15:22; Chronicles II 16:9; Kings I 15:28"> And Samuel said to Saul, Thou hast done foolishly; for thou hast not kept my command, which the Lord commanded thee, as now the Lord would have confirmed thy kingdom over Israel forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 13:22; Kings I 15:28; Psalms 89:19–37"> But now thy kingdom shall not stand to thee, and the Lord shall seek for himself a man after his own heart; and the Lord shall appoint him to be a ruler over his people, because thou hast not kept all that the Lord commanded thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 14:2; Kings I 13:2; Kings I 13:6–7"> And Samuel arose, and departed from Gilgal, and the remnant of the people went after Saul to meet him after the men of war, when they had come out of Gilgal to Gibeah of Benjamin. And Saul numbered the people that were found with him, about six hundred men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings I 13:3"> And Saul and Jonathan his son, and the people that were found with them, halted in Gibeah of Benjamin; and they wept: and the Philistines had encamped in Michmash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 18:23; Kings I 14:15; Joshua 19:3"> And men came forth to destroy out of the land of the Philistines in three companies; one company turning by the way of Ophrah toward the land of Shaul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:34; Joshua 18:13–14; Joshua 16:3"> and another company turning the way of Beth-horon, and another company turning by the way of Geba that turns aside to Gai of Zeboim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings IV 24:14; Judges 5:8; Jeremiah 24:1"> And there was not found a smith in all the land of Israel, for the Philistines said, Lest the Hebrews make themselves sword or spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And all Israel went down to the land of the Philistines to forge every one his reaping hook and his tool, and every one his axe and his sickle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And it was near the time of vintage: and their tools were valued at three shekels for a plowshare, and there was the same rate for the axe and the sickle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 5:8; Zechariah 4:6; Kings I 17:50"> And it came to pass in the days of the war of Michmash, that there was not a sword or spear found in the hand of all the people, that were with Saul and Jonathan; but with Saul and Jonathan his son was there found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 10:28; Kings I 14:1; Kings I 14:4–5"> And there went out some from the camp of the Philistines to the place beyond Michmash.</span>
