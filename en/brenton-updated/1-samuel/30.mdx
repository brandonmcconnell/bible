<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings I 15:7; Kings I 29:11; Kings I 27:8–10"> And it came to pass when David and his men had entered Ziklag on the third day, that Amalek had made an incursion upon the south, and upon Ziklag, and smitten Ziklag, and burned it with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings I 27:11; Job 38:11; Psalms 76:10"> And as to the women and all things that were in it, great and small, they slew neither man nor woman, but carried them captives, and went on their way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; 1 Peter 1:6–7; Revelation 3:9"> And David and his men came into the city, and, behold, it was burned with fire; and their wives, and their sons, and their daughters, were carried captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings I 11:4; Numbers 14:1; Judges 21:2"> And David and his men lifted up their voice, and wept till there was no longer any power within them to weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings I 25:42–43; Kings II 2:2; Kings I 1:2"> And both the wives of David were carried captive, Ahinoam the Jezreelitess, and Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 56:3–4; Psalms 56:11; Psalms 62:5"> And David was greatly distressed, because the people spoke of stoning him, because the soul of all the people was grieved, each for his sons and his daughters: but David strengthened himself in the Lord his God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings III 2:26; Kings I 22:20–21; Kings I 23:2–9"> And David said to Abiathar the priest the son of Ahimelech, Bring near the ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings I 30:18; Proverbs 3:5–6; Kings I 23:2"> And David inquired of the Lord, saying, Shall I pursue after this troop? shall I overtake them? and he said to him, Pursue, for thou shalt surely overtake them, and thou shalt surely rescue the captives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 27:2"> So David went, he and the six hundred men with him, and they come as far as the brook Besor, and the superfluous ones stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings I 30:9; Kings I 30:21; Kings I 14:31"> And he pursued them with four hundred men; and there remained behind two hundred men, who tarried on the other side of the brook Besor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Deuteronomy 23:7; Matthew 25:35"> And they find an Egyptian in the field, and they take him, and bring him to David; and they gave him bread and he ate, and they caused him to drink water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 15:19; Kings I 14:27; Kings I 30:13"> And they gave him a piece of a cake of figs, and he ate, and his spirit was restored in him; for he had not eaten bread, and had not drunk water three days and three nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 2:13; Proverbs 12:10; Job 31:13–15"> And David said to him, Whose art thou? and whence art thou? and the young man the Egyptian said, I am the servant of an Amalekite; and my master left me, because I was taken ill three days ago.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings III 1:38; Kings II 8:18; Kings III 1:44"> And we made an incursion on the south of the Cherethite, and on the parts of Judea, and on the south of Caleb, and we burned Ziklag with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Joshua 9:19–20; Ezekiel 17:16"> And David said to him, Wilt thou bring me down to this troop? and he said, Swear now to me by God, that thou wilt not kill me, and that thou wilt not deliver me into the hands of my master, and I will bring thee down upon this troop.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 11:10–13; Kings II 13:28; Judges 16:23–30"> So he brought him down thither, and behold, they were scattered abroad upon the surface of the whole land, eating and drinking, and feasting by reason of all the great spoils which they had taken out of the land of the Philistines, and out of the land of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings I 11:11; Psalms 18:42; Kings I 15:3"> And David came upon them, and smote them from the morning till the evening, and on the next day; and not one of them escaped, except four hundred young men, who were mounted on camels, and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 14:16"> And David recovered all that the Amalekites had taken, and he rescued both his wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 91:9–10; Kings I 30:8; Job 1:10"> And nothing was wanting to them of great or small, either of the spoils, or the sons and daughters, or anything that they had taken of theirs; and David recovered all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings I 30:26; Numbers 31:9–12; Chronicles II 20:25"> And he took all the flocks, and the herds, and led them away before the spoils: and it was said of these spoils, These are the spoils of David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 13:1; Kings I 30:10; Judges 8:15"> And David comes to the two hundred men who were left behind that they should not follow after David, and he had caused them to remain by the brook of Besor; and they came forth to meet David, and to meet his people with him: and David drew near to the people, and they asked him how he did.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Deuteronomy 13:13; Kings III 21:10"> Then every ill-disposed and bad man of the soldiers who had gone with David, answered and said, Because they did not pursue together with us, we will not give them of the spoils which we have recovered, only let each one lead away with him his wife and his children, and let them return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:18; Psalms 44:2–7; Acts 7:2"> And David said, Ye shall not do so, after the Lord has delivered the enemy to us, and guarded us, and the Lord has delivered into our hands the troop that came against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 22:8; Numbers 31:27; Psalms 68:12"> And who will hearken to these your words? for they are not inferior to us; for according to the portion of him that went down to the battle, so shall be the portion of him that abides with the baggage; they shall share alike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings I 16:13"> And it came to pass from that day forward, that it became an ordinance and a custom in Israel until this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings I 25:27; Genesis 33:11; Isaiah 32:8"> And David came to Ziklag, and sent of the spoils to the elders of Judah, and to his friends, saying, Behold some of the spoils of the enemies of the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 15:48; Joshua 19:8; Judges 1:22–23"> to those in Bethel, and to those in Ramoth of the south, and to those in Jattir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 13:16; Joshua 15:50; Joshua 21:14"> And to those in Aroer, and to those in Ammadi, and to those in Siphmoth, and to those in Eshtemoa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 13:16; Joshua 15:50; Joshua 21:14"> and to those in Gath, and to those in Cimath, and to those in Saphec, and to those in Themath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Kings I 27:10; Kings I 15:6"> and to those in Carmel, and to those in the cities of Jerahmeel, and to those in the cities of the Kenite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 1:17; Joshua 19:4; Joshua 15:42"> and to those in Jarmuth, and to those in Beersheba, and to those in Nombe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings II 2:1; Kings II 15:10; Kings II 4:1"> and to those in Hebron, and to all the places which David and his men had passed through.</span>
</div>
