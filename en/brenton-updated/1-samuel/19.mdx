<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings I 18:1; Kings I 18:9"> And Saul spoke to Jonathan his son, and to all his servants, to slay David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 17:17; Acts 9:24; Kings I 18:1–3"> And Jonathan, Saul’s son, loved David much: and Jonathan told David, saying, Saul seeks to kill thee: take heed to thyself therefore tomorrow morning, and hide thyself, and dwell in secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings I 20:9; Kings I 20:13"> And I will go forth, and stand near my father in the field where thou shalt be, and I will speak concerning thee to my father; and I will see what his answer may be, and I will tell thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:20; Proverbs 31:8–9; Genesis 42:22"> And Jonathan spoke favorably concerning David to Saul his father, and said to him, Let not the king sin against thy servant David, for he has not sinned against thee, and his deeds are very good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings I 11:13; Chronicles I 11:14; Judges 9:17"> And he put his life in his hand, and smote the Philistine, and the Lord wrought a great deliverance; and all Israel saw, and rejoiced: why then dost thou sin against innocent blood, to slay David without a cause?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–25; Jeremiah 5:2; Psalms 15:4"> And Saul hearkened to the voice of Jonathan; and Saul swore, saying, As the Lord lives, he shall not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings I 18:2; Kings I 16:21; Kings I 18:13"> And Jonathan called David, and told him all these words; and Jonathan brought David in to Saul, and he was before him as in former times.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 27:3; Psalms 18:32–50"> And there was again war against Saul; and David did valiantly, and fought against the Philistines, and smote them with a very great slaughter, and they fled from before him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 16:14; Kings I 18:10–11; Kings I 16:16"> And an evil spirit from God was upon Saul, and he was resting in his house, and a spear was in his hand, and David was playing on the harp with his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings I 20:33; Kings I 18:11; Proverbs 21:30"> And Saul sought to smite David with the spear; and David withdrew suddenly from the presence of Saul; and he drove the spear into the wall; and David retreated and escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 59:1–17; Judges 16:2"> And it came to pass in that night, that Saul sent messengers to the house of David to watch him, in order to slay him in the morning; and Michal David’s wife told him, saying, Unless thou save thy life this night, tomorrow thou shalt be slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 2:15; Acts 9:24–25; Psalms 34:19"> So Michal lets David down by the window, and he departed, and fled, and escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 31:19; Judges 18:17; Judges 18:14"> And Michal took images, and laid them on the bed, and she put the liver of a goat by his head, and covered them with clothes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 2:5; Kings II 16:17–19; Kings II 17:20"> And Saul sent messengers to take David; and they say that he is sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 27:3–4; Psalms 37:12; Job 31:31"> And he sends to David, saying, Bring him to me on the bed, that I may slay him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the messengers come, and, behold, the images were on the bed, and the goat’s liver at his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings II 2:22; Kings I 22:17; Exodus 1:17–19"> And Saul said to Michal, Why hast thou thus deceived me, and suffered my enemy to depart, and he has escaped? and Michal said to Saul, He said, Let me go, and if not, I will slay thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings I 7:17; Psalms 116:11; Kings I 15:34"> So David fled, and escaped, and comes to Samuel to Ramah, and tells him all that Saul had done to him: and Samuel and David went, and dwelt in Naioth in Ramah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings I 23:19; Kings I 22:9–10; Kings I 26:1"> And it was told Saul, saying, Behold, David is in Naioth in Ramah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joel 2:28; John 7:32; Kings I 10:5–6"> And Saul sent messengers to take David, and they saw the assembly of the prophets, and Samuel stood as appointed over them; and the Spirit of God came upon the messengers of Saul, and they prophesy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 27:22; Jeremiah 13:23; Joel 2:28"> And it was told Saul, and he sent other messengers, and they also prophesied: and Saul sent again a third set of messengers, and they also prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Saul was very angry, and went himself also to Ramah, and he comes as far as the well of the threshing floor that is in Secu; and he asked and said, Where are Samuel and David? and they said, Behold, in Naioth in Ramah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 16:9; Kings I 10:10; Kings I 19:20"> And he went thence to Naioth in Ramah: and there came the Spirit of God upon him also, and he went on prophesying till he came to Naioth in Ramah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings II 6:20; Isaiah 20:2; Micah 1:8"> And he took off his clothes, and prophesied before them; and lay down naked all that day and all that night: therefore they said, Is Saul also among the prophets?</span>
</div>
