<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings I 14:51; Acts 13:21; Chronicles I 9:36–39"> And there was a man of the sons of Benjamin, and his name was Kish, the son of Abiel, the son of Zeror, the son of Becorath, the son of Aphiah, the son of a Benjaminite, a man of might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 6:2; Numbers 13:33; Kings I 16:7"> And this man had a son, and his name was Saul, of great stature, a goodly man; and there was not among the sons of Israel a goodlier than he, high above all the people from his shoulders and upward.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 5:10; Kings I 10:2; Judges 10:4"> And the asses of Kish the father of Saul were lost; and Kish said to Saul his son, Take with thee one of the young men, and arise ye, and go and seek the asses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings IV 4:42; Joshua 24:33; John 3:23"> And they went through Mount Ephraim, and they went through the land of Shalishah, and found them not: and they passed through the land of Shaalim, and they were not there: and they passed through the land of Jamin, and found them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings I 10:2; Kings I 1:1; Luke 12:11"> And when they came to Siph, then Saul said to his young man that was with him, Come and let us return, lest my father leave the asses, and take care for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 13:1; Deuteronomy 33:1; 1 Timothy 6:11"> And the young man said to him, Behold now, there is a man of God in this city, and the man is of high repute; all that he shall speak will surely come to pass: now then let us go, that he may tell us our way on which we have set out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings III 14:3; Kings IV 8:8; Kings IV 4:42"> And Saul said to his young man that was with him, Lo, then, we will go; but what shall we bring the man of God? for the loaves are spent out of our vessels, and we have nothing more with us that belongs to us to bring to the man of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the young man answered Saul again, and said, Behold, there is found in my hand a fourth part of a shekel of silver; and thou shalt give it to the man of God, and he shall tell us our way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 30:10; Kings IV 17:13; Amos 7:12"> Now beforetime in Israel everyone in going to inquire of God said, Come and let us go to the seer; for the people beforetime called the prophet, the seer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings IV 5:13–14"> And Saul said to his servant, Well said, come and let us go: and they went to the city where the man of God was.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 24:11; Exodus 2:16; Genesis 24:18–20"> As they went up the ascent to the city, they find damsels come out to draw water, and they say to them, Is the seer here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Kings I 16:2; Kings I 10:5"> And the virgins answered them, and they say to them, He is: behold, he is before you: now he is coming to the city, because of the day, for today there is a sacrifice for the people in Bama.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:11; Matthew 26:26; Luke 24:30"> As soon as ye shall enter into the city, so shall ye find him in the city, before he goes up to Bama to eat; for the people will not eat until he comes in, for he blesses the sacrifice, and afterward the guests eat; now then go up, for ye shall find him because of the holiday.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And they go up to the city; and as they were entering into the midst of the city, behold, Samuel came out to meet them, to go up to Bama.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 13:21; Kings I 15:1; Job 33:16"> And the Lord uncovered the ear of Samuel one day before Saul came to him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings I 10:1; Psalms 106:44; Exodus 3:7–9"> At this time tomorrow I will send to thee a man out of the land of Benjamin, and thou shalt anoint him to be ruler over my people Israel, and he shall save my people out of the hand of the Philistines; for I have looked upon the humiliation of my people, for their cry is come unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 13:11; Kings I 16:6–12; Nehemiah 13:25"> And Samuel looked upon Saul, and the Lord answered him, Behold the man of whom I spoke to thee, This one shall rule over my people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And Saul drew near to Samuel into the midst of the city, and said, Tell me now which is the house of the seer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:25; John 4:29"> And Samuel answered Saul, and said, I am he: go up before me to Bama, and eat with me today, and I will send thee away in the morning, and I will tell thee all that is in thy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings I 8:5; Kings I 12:13; Kings I 9:3"> And concerning thine asses that have been lost now these three days, care not for them, for they are found. And to whom does the excellency of Israel belong? does it not to thee and to thy father’s house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings I 15:17; Judges 20:46–48; Psalms 68:27"> And Saul answered and said, Am not I the son of a Benjaminite, the least tribe of the people of Israel? and of the least family of the whole tribe of Benjamin? and why hast thou spoken to me according to this word?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 43:32; Luke 14:10"> And Samuel took Saul and his servant, and brought them to the inn, and set them there a place among the chief of those that were called, about seventy men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings I 1:5; Genesis 43:34"> And Samuel said to the cook, Give me the portion which I gave thee, which I told thee to set by thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:4; Leviticus 7:32–33; Exodus 29:22"> Now the cook had boiled the shoulder, and he set it before Saul; and Samuel said to Saul, Behold that which is left: set it before thee, and eat; for it is set thee for a testimony in preference to the others; take of it: and Saul ate with Samuel on that day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 10:9; Deuteronomy 22:8; Kings II 11:2"> And he went down from Bama into the city; and they prepared a lodging for Saul on the roof, and he lay down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 44:4; Joshua 7:13; Judges 19:28"> And it came to pass when the morning dawned, that Samuel called Saul on the roof, saying, Rise up, and I will dismiss thee. And Saul arose, and he and Samuel went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 15:14–15; Kings I 20:38–39; Kings IV 9:5–6"> As they went down to a part of the city, Samuel said to Saul, Speak to the young man, and let him pass on before us; and do thou stand as today, and hearken to the word of God.</span>
</div>
</div>
