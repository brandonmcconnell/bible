<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; Chronicles II 28:18; Chronicles I 11:13"> And the Philistines gather their armies to battle, and gather themselves to Socoh of Judea, and encamp between Socoh and Azekah Ephes-dammim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings I 21:9; Kings I 17:19"> And Saul and the men of Israel gather together, and they encamp in the valley, and set the battle in array against the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And the Philistines stand on the mountain on one side, and Israel stands on the mountain on the other side, and the valley was between them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:22; Kings II 21:16–22; Chronicles I 20:4–8"> And there went forth a mighty man out of the army of the Philistines, Goliath by name, out of Gath, his height was four cubits and a span.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he had a helmet upon his head, and he wore a breastplate of chain armor; and the weight of his breastplate was five thousand shekels of brass and iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings I 17:45; Chronicles II 9:15; Kings III 10:16"> And greaves of brass were upon his legs, and a brazen target was between his shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings II 21:19; Kings I 17:41; Chronicles I 11:23"> And the staff of his spear was like a weavers’ beam, and the spear’s head was formed of six hundred shekels of iron; and his armor bearer went before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings I 8:17; Chronicles I 21:3; Kings I 17:26"> And he stood and cried to the army of Israel, and said to them, Why are ye come forth to set yourselves in battle array against us? Am not I a Philistine, and ye Hebrews of Saul? choose for yourselves a man, and let him come down to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 11:1"> And if he shall be able to fight against me, and shall smite me, then will we be your servants: but if I should prevail and smite him, ye shall be our servants, and serve us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings I 17:25–26; Kings I 17:45; Kings I 17:36"> And the Philistine said, Behold, I have defied the armies of Israel this very day: give me a man, and we will both of us fight in single combat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Deuteronomy 31:8; Proverbs 28:1"> And Saul and all Israel heard these words of the Philistine, and they were dismayed, and greatly terrified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Kings I 16:18; Deuteronomy 20:1–3; Psalms 3:6"> And David said to Saul, Let not, I pray thee, the heart of my lord be dejected within him: thy servant will go, and fight with this Philistine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 13:31; Deuteronomy 9:2; Revelation 13:4"> And Saul said to David, Thou wilt not in anywise be able to go against this Philistine to fight with him, for thou art a mere youth, and he a man of war from his youth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And David said to Saul, Thy servant was tending the flock for his father; and when a lion came and a she-bear, and took a sheep out of the flock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Amos 3:12; 2 Timothy 4:17–18; Daniel 6:22"> then I went forth after him, and smote him, and drew the spoil out of his mouth: and as he rose up against me, then I caught hold of his throat, and smote him, and slew him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Kings I 17:10; Acts 5:38–39; Romans 2:28–29"> Thy servant smote both the lion and the bear, and the uncircumcised Philistine shall be as one of them: shall I not go and smite him, and remove this day a reproach from Israel? for who is this uncircumcised one, who has defied the army of the living God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17–18; Psalms 138:7–8; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> The Lord who delivered me out of the paw of the lion and out of the paw of the bear, he will deliver me out of the hand of this uncircumcised Philistine. And Saul said to David, Go, and the Lord shall be with thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And Saul clothed David with a military coat, and put his brazen helmet on his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Hosea 1:7"> And he girded David with his sword over his coat: and he made trial walking with them once and again: and David said to Saul, I shall not be able to go with these, for I have not proved them: so they remove them from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27–29; Matthew 10:10; Judges 15:15–16"> And he took his staff in his hand, and he chose for himself five smooth stones out of the brook, and put them in the shepherd’s scrip which he had for his store, and his sling was in his hand; and he approached the Philistine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Kings I 16:12; Kings I 17:33; Psalms 123:3–4"> And Goliath saw David, and despised him; for he was a lad, and ruddy, with a fair countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Kings II 9:8; Kings II 3:8; Kings I 24:14"> And the Philistine said to David, Am I as a dog, that thou comest against me with a staff and stones? and David said, Nay, but worse than a dog. And the Philistine cursed David by his gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:9–10; Proverbs 18:12; Kings III 20:10–11"> And the Philistine said to David, Come to me, and I will give thy flesh to the birds of the air, and to the beasts of the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; Chronicles II 32:8; 2 Corinthians 3:5"> And David said to the Philistine, Thou comest to me with sword, and with spear, and with shield; but I come to thee in the name of the Lord God of hosts of the army of Israel, which thou hast defied</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 52:10; Joshua 4:24; Kings IV 19:19"> this day. And the Lord shall deliver thee this day into my hand; and I will slay thee, and take away thy head from off thee, and will give thy limbs and the limbs of the army of the Philistines this day to the birds of the sky, and to the wild beasts of the earth; and all the earth shall know that there is a God in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 44:6–7; Zechariah 4:6; Hosea 1:7"> And all this assembly shall know that the Lord delivers not by sword or spear, for the battle is the Lord’s, and the Lord will deliver you into our hands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Psalms 27:1"> And the Philistine arose and went to meet David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27–28; Kings IV 9:24; Kings III 22:34"> And David stretched out his hand to his scrip, and took thence a stone, and slang it, and smote the Philistine on his forehead, and the stone penetrated through the helmet into his forehead, and he fell upon his face to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Hebrews 11:34; Kings I 21:9; Kings II 23:21"> And David ran, and stood upon him, and took his sword, and slew him, and cut off his head: and the Philistines saw that their champion was dead, and they fled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Joshua 15:11; Joshua 15:33–36; Joshua 15:45–46"> And the men of Israel and Judah arose, and shouted and pursued them as far as the entrance to Gath, and as far as the gate of Ashkelon: and the slain men of the Philistines fell in the way of the gates, both to Gath, and to Ekron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; Kings IV 7:7–16; Jeremiah 30:16"> And the men of Israel returned from pursuing after the Philistines, and they destroyed their camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 16:33; Joshua 4:7–8; Kings I 21:9"> And David took the head of the Philistine, and brought it to Jerusalem; but he put his armor in his tent.</span>
</div>
