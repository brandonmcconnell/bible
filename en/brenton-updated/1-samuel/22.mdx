<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; Kings I 30:22–24; Psalms 72:12–14"> And there gathered to him everyone that was in distress, and everyone that was in debt, and everyone that was troubled in mind; and he was a leader over them, and there were with him about four hundred men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings I 3:18; Kings II 15:25–26; Judges 11:29"> And David departed thence to Mizpah of Moab, and said to the king of Moab, Let, I pray thee, my father and my mother be with thee, until I know what God will do to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 11:1; Chronicles I 12:16; Chronicles I 29:29"> And he persuaded the king of Moab, and they dwelt with him continually, while David was in the hold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles II 29:25; Chronicles I 21:9; Chronicles I 29:29"> And Gad the prophet said to David, Dwell not in the hold: go, and thou shalt enter the land of Judah. So David went, and came and dwelt in the city of Hereth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings I 19:9; Kings I 18:10; Kings I 20:33"> And Saul heard that David was discovered, and his men with him: now Saul dwelt in the hill below the field that is in Ramah, and his spear was in his hand, and all his servants stood near him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings III 12:16; Kings I 8:14–15; Kings I 22:13"> And Saul said to his servants that stood by him, Hear now, ye sons of Benjamin, will the son of Jesse indeed give all of you fields and vineyards, and will he make you all captains of hundreds and captains of thousands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings I 18:3; Kings I 23:21; Kings I 20:13–17"> that ye are all conspiring against me, and there is no one that informs me, whereas my son has made a covenant with the son of Jesse, and there is no one of you that is sorry for me, or informs me that my son has stirred up my servant against me for an enemy, as it is this day?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 52:1–9; Kings I 14:3; Ezekiel 22:9"> And Doeg the Syrian who was over the mules of Saul answered and said, I saw the son of Jesse as he came to Nob to Ahimelech son of Ahitub the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 27:21; Kings I 30:8; Kings I 23:4"> And the priest inquired of God for him, and gave him provisions, and gave him the sword of Goliath the Philistine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:15"> And the king sent to call Ahimelech son of Ahitub and all his father’s sons, the priests that were in Nob; and they all came to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings II 9:6; Kings I 22:7; Isaiah 65:1"> And Saul said, Hear now, thou son of Ahitub. And he said, Lo! I am here, speak, my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings I 22:8; Luke 23:2–5; Amos 7:10"> And Saul said to him, Why have thou and the son of Jesse conspired against me, that thou shouldest give him bread and a sword, and shouldest inquire of God for him, to raise him up against me as an enemy, as he is this day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings I 20:32; Kings I 19:4–5; Kings I 22:13"> And he answered the king, and said, And who is there among all thy servants faithful as David, and he is a son-in-law of the king, and he is executor of all thy commands, and is honorable in thy house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 25:36; 1 Peter 3:16–17; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> Have I begun today to inquire of God for him? by no means: let not the king bring a charge against his servant, and against the whole of my father’s house; for thy servant knew not in all these matters anything great or small.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 28:15; Daniel 3:19–20; Kings III 19:2"> And King Saul said, Thou shalt surely die, Ahimelech, thou, and all thy father’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 1:17; Kings I 14:45; Kings IV 1:13–14"> And the king said to the footmen that attended on him, Draw nigh and slay the priests of the Lord, because their hand is with David, and because they knew that he fled, and they did not inform me. But the servants of the king would not lift their hands to fall upon the priests of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:10–11; Hosea 7:3; Kings I 2:18"> And the king said to Doeg, Turn thou, and fall upon the priests: and Doeg the Syrian turned, and slew the priests of the Lord in that day, three hundred and five men, all wearing an ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings I 15:3; Hosea 10:14; Kings I 21:1"> And he smote Nob the city of the priests with the edge of the sword, both man and woman, infant and suckling, and calf, and ox, and sheep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings I 23:6; Kings I 30:7; Kings III 2:26–27"> And one son of Ahimelech son of Ahitub escapes, and his name was Abiathar, and he fled after David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Abiathar told David that Saul had slain all the priests of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings I 21:1–9; Psalms 44:22"> And David said to Abiathar, I knew it in that day, that Doeg the Syrian would surely tell Saul: I am guilty of the death of the house of thy father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings III 2:26; Hebrews 12:1–3; John 10:28–30"> Dwell with me; fear not, for wherever I shall seek a place of safety for my life, I will also seek a place for thy life, for thou art safely guarded while with me.</span>
</div>
