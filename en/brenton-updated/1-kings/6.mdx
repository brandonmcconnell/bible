<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 3:1–2; Acts 7:47; Chronicles I 29:19"> And it came to pass in the four hundred and fortieth year after the departure of the children of Israel out of Egypt, in the fourth year and second month of the reign of King Solomon over Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 3:1–2; Acts 7:47; Chronicles I 29:19"> that the king commanded that they should take great and costly stones for the foundation of the house, and hewn stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 3:1–2; Acts 7:47; Chronicles I 29:19"> And the men of Solomon, and the men of Hiram hewed the stones, and laid them for a foundation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 3:1–2; Acts 7:47; Chronicles I 29:19"> In the fourth year he laid the foundation of the house of the Lord, in the month Ziv, even in the second month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 3:1–2; Acts 7:47; Chronicles I 29:19"> In the eleventh year, in the month Bul, this is the eighth month, the house was completed according to all its plan, and according to all its arrangement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:1–41; Ezekiel 41:1–15; Revelation 21:16–17"> And the house which the king built to the Lord was forty cubits in length, and twenty cubits in breadth, and its height five and twenty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles I 28:11; Chronicles II 3:3–4; Ezekiel 41:15"> And the porch in front of the temple—twenty cubits was its length according to the breadth of the house in front of the house: and he built the house, and finished it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 41:26; Ezekiel 41:16"> And he made to the house secret windows inclining inward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings III 6:16; Kings III 6:19–21; Kings III 6:31"> And against the wall of the house he set chambers round about the temple and the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The under side was five cubits broad, and the middle part six, and the third was seven cubits broad; for he formed an interval to the house round about without the house, that they might not touch the walls of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:5–6; 1 Peter 2:5; Exodus 20:25"> And the house was built in the construction of it with rough hewn stones: and there was not heard in the house in the building of it hammer or axe, or any iron tool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:6–7"> And the porch of the under side was below the right wing of the house, and there was a winding ascent into the middle chamber, and from the middle to the third story.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 6:14; Kings III 6:38"> So he built the house and finished it; and he made the ceiling of the house with cedars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And he made the partitions through all the house, each five cubits high, and enclosed each partition with cedar boards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 7:7"> And he framed the walls of the house within with cedar boards, from the floor of the house and on to the inner walls and to the beams: he lined the parts enclosed with boards within, and compassed the inward parts of the house with planks of fir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles II 3:8; Kings III 8:6; Hebrews 9:3"> And he built the twenty cubits from the top of the wall, one side from the floor to the beams, and he made it from the oracle to the most holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the temple was forty cubits in extent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings III 8:6–10; Psalms 28:2; Kings III 6:5"> in front of the oracle in the midst of the house within, in order to put there the ark of the covenant of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings III 6:2–3; Kings III 6:22; Kings III 7:48"> The length was twenty cubits, and the breadth was twenty cubits, and the height of it was twenty cubits. And he covered it with perfect gold, and he made an altar</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Chronicles II 3:7–9; Chronicles II 3:14–16; Kings III 6:5"> in front of the oracle, and covered it with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 30:1; Exodus 30:3; Chronicles II 3:7–14"> And he covered the whole house with gold, till he had finished gilding the whole house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 37:7–9; Genesis 3:24; 1 Peter 1:12"> And he made in the oracle two cherubs of ten cubits measured size.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And the wing of one cherub was five cubits, and his other wing was five cubits; ten cubits from the tip of one wing to the tip of the other wing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Thus it was with the other cherub, both were alike finished with one measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the height of the one cherub was ten cubits, and so was it with the second cherub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 37:9; Exodus 25:20; Chronicles II 5:8"> And both the cherubs were in the midst of the innermost part of the house; and they spread out their wings, and one wing touched the wall, and the wing of the other cherub touched the other wall; and their wings in the midst of the house touched each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he covered the cherubs with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings III 6:32; Psalms 92:12–15; Chronicles II 4:2–5"> He graved all the walls of the house round about with the graving of cherubs, and he sculptured palm trees within and without the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 21:18–21; Isaiah 60:17; Isaiah 54:11–12"> And he covered the floor of the house within and without with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–20; Ephesians 3:18; John 14:6"> And for the doorway of the oracle he made doors of juniper wood, there were porches in a fourfold way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:23–25; Kings III 5:8"> In both the doors were planks of fir; the one door had two leaves and their hinges, and the other door had two leaves and turned on hinges,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> being carved with cherubs, and there were palm trees and open flower leaves, and it was overlaid with gold gilded upon the engraving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Kings III 7:12; Chronicles II 7:7; Exodus 27:9–19"> And he built the inner court, three rows of hewn stones, and a row of wrought cedar round about,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Kings III 7:12; Chronicles II 7:7; Exodus 27:9–19"> and he made the curtain of the court of the porch of the house that was in front of the temple.</span>
