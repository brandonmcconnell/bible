<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 12:32–33; Kings IV 23:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:15"> And, behold, there came a man of God out of Judah by the word of the Lord to Bethel, and Jeroboam stood at the altar to sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:1; Kings IV 23:15–18; Kings IV 22:1–2"> And he cried against the altar by the word of the Lord, and said, O altar, altar, thus saith the Lord, Behold, a son is to be born to the house of David, Josiah by name; and he shall offer upon thee the priests of the high places, even of them that sacrifice upon thee, and he shall burn men’s bones upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:22; Exodus 4:3–5; Judges 6:17"> And in that day one shall give a sign, saying, This is the word which the Lord has spoken, saying, Behold, the altar is rent, and the fatness upon it shall be poured out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 11:5; Chronicles II 18:25–27; Luke 6:10"> And it came to pass when King Jeroboam heard the words of the man of God who called on the altar that was in Bethel, that the king stretched forth his hand from the altar, saying, Take hold of him. And, behold, his hand, which he stretched forth against him, withered, and he could not draw it back to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings III 13:3; Jeremiah 28:16–17; Deuteronomy 18:22"> And the altar was rent, and the fatness was poured out from the altar, according to the sign which the man of God gave by the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 9:28; Acts 8:24; Exodus 10:17"> And King Jeroboam said to the man of God, Entreat the Lord thy God, and let my hand be restored to me. And the man of God entreated the Lord, and he restored the king’s hand to him, and it became as before.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 5:15; Kings I 9:7–8; Judges 13:15"> And the king said to the man of God, Enter with me into the house, and dine, and I will give thee a gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 24:13; Numbers 22:18; Mark 6:23"> And the man of God said to the king, If thou shouldest give me the half of thy house, I would not go in with thee, neither will I eat bread, neither will I drink water in this place; for thus the Lord charged me by his word, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 13:17; Deuteronomy 13:13–18; Romans 16:17"> Eat no bread, and drink no water, and return not by the way by which thou camest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So he departed by another way, and returned not by the way by which he came to Bethel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings IV 23:18; Kings III 13:20–21; Numbers 24:2"> And there dwelt an old prophet in Bethel; and his sons came and told him all the works that the man of God did on that day in Bethel, and the words which he spoke to the king: and they turned the face of their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And their father spoke to them, saying, Which way went he? and his sons show him the way by which the man of God who came out of Judah went up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 10:4; Kings II 19:26; Judges 5:10"> And he said to his sons, Saddle me the ass: and they saddled him the ass, and he mounted it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 4:12–13; 2 Corinthians 11:27; 1 Corinthians 4:11–12"> and went after the man of God, and found him sitting under an oak: and he said to him, Art thou the man of God that came out of Judah? And he said to him, I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he said to him, Come with me, and eat bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 13:8–9; Matthew 16:23; Numbers 22:19"> And he said, I shall not by any means be able to return with thee, neither will I eat bread, neither will I drink water in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 20:35; 1 Thessalonians 4:15; Kings III 13:1"> For thus the Lord commanded me by word, saying, Eat not bread there, and drink not water, and return not thither by the way by which thou camest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Matthew 7:15; Numbers 22:35"> And he said to him, I also am a prophet as thou art; and an angel spoke to me by the word of the Lord, saying, Bring him back to thee into thy house, and let him eat bread and drink water: but he lied to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:1; Genesis 3:6; Acts 4:19"> And he brought him back, and he ate bread and drank water in his house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 7:22; Numbers 24:4; Numbers 24:16–24"> And it came to pass while they were sitting at the table, that the word of the Lord came to the prophet that brought him back;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings III 13:17; Kings I 13:13–14; Kings I 4:18"> and he spoke to the man of God that came out of Judah, saying, Thus saith the Lord, Because thou hast resisted the word of the Lord, and hast not kept the commandment which the Lord thy God commanded thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 14:18–20; Kings III 14:13; Jeremiah 22:18–19"> but hast returned, and eaten bread and drunk water in the place of which he spoke to thee, saying, Thou shalt not eat bread, and shalt not drink water; therefore thy body shall in nowise enter into the sepulcher of thy fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And it came to pass after he had eaten bread and drunk water, that he saddled the ass for him, and he turned and departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings III 20:36; Kings IV 2:24; Proverbs 26:13"> And a lion found him in the way, and slew him; and his body was cast out in the way, and the ass was standing by it, and the lion also was standing by the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings III 13:11"> And, behold, men were passing by, and saw the carcass cast in the way, and the lion was standing near the carcass: and they went in and spoke of it in the city where the old prophet dwelt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:6; Proverbs 11:31; Psalms 119:120"> And the prophet that turned him back out of the way heard, and said, This is the man of God who rebelled against the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings III 17:6; Psalms 148:7–8; Daniel 6:22–24"> And he went and found the body cast in the way, and the ass and the lion were standing by the body: and the lion had not devoured the body of the man of God, and had not torn the ass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And the prophet took up the body of the man of God, and laid it on his ass; and the prophet brought him back to his city, to bury him in his own tomb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18; Acts 8:2; Kings III 14:13"> and they bewailed him, saying, Alas, brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings IV 23:17–18; Numbers 23:10; Luke 16:22–23"> And it came to pass after he had lamented him, that he spoke to his sons, saying, Whenever I die, bury me in this tomb wherein the man of God is buried; lay me by his bones, that my bones may be preserved with his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Kings III 16:24; Kings III 13:2; Kings III 12:31"> For the word will surely come to pass which he spoke by the word of the Lord against the altar in Bethel, and against the high houses in Samaria.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Chronicles II 11:15; Chronicles II 13:9; Numbers 17:12–13"> And after this Jeroboam turned not from his sin, but he turned and made of part of the people priests of the high places: whoever would, he consecrated him, and he became a priest for the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Kings III 14:10; Kings III 15:29–30; Kings III 12:30"> And this thing became sin to the house of Jeroboam, even to its destruction and its removal from the face of the earth.</span>
</div>
</div>
