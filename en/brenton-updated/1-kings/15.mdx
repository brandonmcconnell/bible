<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 14:31; Chronicles II 13:1–22"> And in the eighteenth year of the reign of Jeroboam son of Nebat, Abijam son of Rehoboam reigns over Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles II 13:2; Chronicles II 11:20–22; Kings III 15:13"> And he reigned three years over Jerusalem: and his mother’s name was Maacah, daughter of Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings III 11:4; Psalms 119:80; Kings III 3:14"> And he walked in the sins of his father which he wrought in his presence, and his heart was not perfect with the Lord his God, as was the heart of his father David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 11:36; Chronicles II 21:7; Isaiah 37:35"> Howbeit for David’s sake the Lord gave him a remnant, that he might establish his children after him, and might establish Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings III 14:8; Kings II 11:15–17; Kings III 15:3"> Forasmuch as David did that which was right in the sight of the Lord: he turned not from anything that he commanded him all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles II 13:2–22; Kings III 14:29"> And the rest of the history of Abijam, and all that he did, behold, are not these written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah? And there was war between Abijam and Jeroboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles II 14:1; Kings III 14:31; Matthew 1:7–8"> And Abijam slept with his fathers in the twenty-fourth year of Jeroboam; and he is buried with his fathers in the city of David: and Asa his son reigns in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> In the four and twentieth year of Jeroboam king of Israel, Asa begins to reign over Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings III 15:2; Kings III 15:13; Chronicles II 13:2"> And he reigned forty-one years in Jerusalem: and his mother’s name was Ana, daughter of Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles II 14:2; Chronicles II 15:17; Kings III 15:3"> And Asa did that which was right in the sight of the Lord, as David his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings III 22:46; Kings III 14:23–24; Chronicles II 14:2–5"> And he removed the sodomites out of the land, and abolished all the practices which his fathers had kept up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 32:20; Deuteronomy 7:5; Zechariah 13:3"> And he removed Ana his mother from being queen, forasmuch as she gathered a meeting in her grove: and Asa cut down her retreats, and burned them with fire in the brook of Kidron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings III 22:43; Kings III 8:61; Kings III 15:3"> But he removed not the high places; nevertheless the heart of Asa was perfect with the Lord all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 7:51; Chronicles II 14:13; Chronicles II 15:18"> And he brought in the pillars of his father, he even brought in his gold and silver pillars into the house of the Lord, and his vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 15:32; Kings III 14:30; Chronicles II 16:1–6"> And there was war between Asa and Baasha king of Israel all their days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 12:27; Chronicles II 16:1–6; Joshua 18:25"> And Baasha king of Israel went up against Judah, and built Ramah, so that no one should go out or come in for Asa king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 14:26; Kings IV 12:18; Kings III 11:23–24"> And Asa took all the silver and the gold that was found in the treasures of the house of the Lord, and in the treasures of the king’s house, and gave them into the hands of his servants; and King Asa sent them out to the son of Hadad, the son of Tabrimmon son of Hezion king of Syria, who dwelt in Damascus, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:31; Chronicles II 16:7; Romans 3:8"> Make a covenant between me and thee, and between my father and thy father: lo! I have sent forth to thee gold and silver for gifts: come, break thy league with Baasha king of Israel, that he may go up from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings IV 15:29; Judges 18:29; Joshua 11:2"> And the son of Hadad hearkened to King Asa, and sent the chiefs of his forces to the cities of Israel; and they smote Ijon, Dan, and Abel of the house of Maacah, and all Chinneroth, as far as the whole land of Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings III 14:17; Kings III 16:15–18; Chronicles II 16:5"> And it came to pass when Baasha heard it, that he left off building Ramah, and returned to Tirzah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 21:17; Joshua 18:24; Joshua 18:26"> And King Asa charged all Judah without exception: and they take up the stones of Ramah and its timbers with which Baasha was building; and King Asa built with them upon the whole hill of Benjamin, and the watchtower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Chronicles II 16:11–14; Kings III 14:29–31; Kings III 15:7–8"> And the rest of the history of Asa, and all his mighty deeds which he wrought, and the cities which he built, behold, are not these written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah? Nevertheless in the time of his old age he was diseased in his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 1:8; Kings III 22:41–43; Chronicles II 17:1–9"> And Asa slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the city of David his father: and Jehoshaphat his son reigns in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings III 14:20; Kings III 14:12"> And Nadab son of Jeroboam reigns over Israel in the second year of Asa king of Judah, and he reigned two years in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings III 14:16; Kings III 15:34; Kings III 15:30"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, and walked in the way of his father, and in his sins wherein he caused Israel to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 19:44; Joshua 21:23; Kings III 14:14"> And Baasha son of Ahijah, who was over the house of Belaan son of Ahijah, conspired against him, and smote him in Gibbethon of the Philistines; for Nadab and all Israel were besieging Gibbethon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35"> And Baasha slew him in the third year of Asa son of Asa king of Judah; and reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings III 14:9–16; Kings IV 19:25; Kings IV 10:10–11"> And it came to pass when he reigned, that he smote the whole house of Jeroboam, and left none that breathed of Jeroboam, until he had destroyed him utterly, according to the word of the Lord which he spoke by his servant Ahijah the Shilonite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings III 15:26; Kings III 14:22; Kings III 14:9–16"> for the sins of Jeroboam, who led Israel into sin, even by his provocation wherewith he provoked the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings III 14:19; Kings III 16:20; Kings III 16:14"> And the rest of the history of Nadab, and all that he did, behold, are not these written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Kings III 16:8"> And in the third year of Asa king of Judah, Baasha the son of Ahijah begins to reign over Israel in Tirzah, twenty and four years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Kings III 15:26; Kings III 14:16; Kings III 12:28–29"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, and walked in the way of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, and in his sins, as he caused Israel to sin.</span>
