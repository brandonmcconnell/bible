<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings III 22:43; Chronicles II 33:17; Deuteronomy 12:2–5"> Nevertheless the people burned incense on the high places, because a house had not yet been built to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; Psalms 31:23; Deuteronomy 30:16"> And Solomon loved the Lord, so as to walk in the ordinances of David his father; only he sacrificed and burned incense on the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Chronicles I 16:39; Chronicles II 1:3; Chronicles II 7:5"> And he arose and went to Gibeon to sacrifice there, for that was the highest place, and great: Solomon offered a whole burnt offering of a thousand victims on the altar in Gibeon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings III 9:2; James 1:5–6; Numbers 12:6"> And the Lord appeared to Solomon in a dream by night, and the Lord said to Solomon, Ask some petition for thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 9:4; Kings III 1:48; 2 Corinthians 9:11"> And Solomon said, Thou hast dealt very mercifully with thy servant David my father according as he walked before thee in truth, and in righteousness, and in uprightness of heart with thee, and thou hast kept for him this great mercy, to set his son upon his throne, as it is this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles I 29:1; Numbers 27:17; Jeremiah 1:6"> And now, O Lord my God, thou hast appointed thy servant in the room of David my father; and I am a little child, and know not my going out and my coming in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; Genesis 13:16; Chronicles I 21:2"> But thy servant is in the midst of thy people, whom thou hast chosen, a great people, which cannot be numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 1:5; Proverbs 16:16; Hebrews 5:14"> Thou shalt give therefore to thy servant a heart to hear and to judge thy people justly, and to discern between good and evil: for who will be able to judge this thy great people?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8"> And it was pleasing before the Lord, that Solomon asked this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:26; James 4:2–3; Proverbs 16:31"> And the Lord said to him, Because thou hast asked this thing of me, and hast not asked for thyself long life, and hast not asked wealth, nor hast asked the lives of thine enemies, but hast asked for thyself understanding to hear judgment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 5:14–15; Ecclesiastes 1:16; Matthew 12:42"> behold, I have done according to thy word: behold, I have given thee an understanding and wise heart: there has not been anyone like thee before thee, and after thee there shall not arise one like thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20; Matthew 6:33; Proverbs 3:16"> And I have given thee what thou hast not asked, wealth and glory, so that there has not been anyone like thee among kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 3:2; Psalms 91:16; Kings III 15:5"> And if thou wilt walk in my way, to keep my commandments and my ordinances, as David thy father walked, then will I multiply thy days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 41:7; Kings III 8:65; Esther 1:3"> And Solomon awoke, and, behold, it was a dream: and he arose and came to Jerusalem, and stood before the altar that was in front of the ark of the covenant of the Lord in Zion: and he offered whole burnt offerings, and sacrificed peace offerings, and made a great banquet for himself and all his servants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 18:16; Judges 11:1; Numbers 27:2"> Then there appeared two harlots before the king, and they stood before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:7; Genesis 43:20"> And the one woman said, Hear me, my lord; I and this woman dwelt in one house, and we were delivered in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And it came to pass on the third day after I was delivered, this woman also was delivered: and we were together; and there was no one with us besides our two selves in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And this woman’s child died in the night; because she overlaid it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 3:20; Matthew 13:25; Psalms 139:11"> And she arose in the middle of the night, and took my son from my arms, and laid him in her bosom, and laid her dead son in my bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 21:7; Kings I 1:23; Lamentations 4:3–4"> And I arose in the morning to suckle my son, and he was dead: and, behold, I considered him in the morning, and, behold, it was not my son whom I bore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings III 3:23–24"> And the other woman said, No, but the living is my son, and the dead is thy son. So they spoke before the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the king said to them, Thou sayest, This is my son, even the living one, and this woman’s son is the dead one: and thou sayest, No, but the living is my son, and the dead is thy son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And the king said, Fetch a sword. And they brought a sword before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 25:8"> And the king said, Divide the live child, the suckling, in two; and give half of it to one, and half of it to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Jeremiah 31:20; 1 John 3:17"> And the woman whose the living child was, answered and spoke to the king (for her bowels yearned over her son), and she said, I pray thee, my lord, give her the child, and in nowise slay it. But the other said, Let it be neither mine nor hers; divide it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Then the king answered and said, Give the child to her that said, Give it to her, and by no means slay it: she is its mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Daniel 2:47; Kings III 3:9–12; Colossians 2:3"> And all Israel heard this judgment which the king judged, and they feared before the king; because they saw that the wisdom of God was in him, to execute judgment.</span>
</div>
