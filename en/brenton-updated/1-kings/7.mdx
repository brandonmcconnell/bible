<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 9:10; Chronicles II 8:1; Ecclesiastes 2:4–5"> And King Solomon sent, and took Hiram out of Tyre,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings III 10:17; Chronicles II 9:16; Kings III 10:21"> the son of a widow woman; and he was of the tribe of Naphtali, and his father was a Tyrian; a worker in brass, and accomplished in art and skill and knowledge to work every work in brass: and he was brought in to King Solomon, and he wrought all the works.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings III 6:5"> And he cast the two pillars for the porch of the house: eighteen cubits was the height of each pillar, and a circumference of fourteen cubits encompassed it, even the thickness of the pillar: the flutings were four fingers wide, and thus was the other pillar formed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:22; Ezekiel 40:16; Kings III 6:4"> And he made two molten chapiters to put on the heads of the pillars: five cubits was the height of one chapiter, and five cubits was the height of the other chapiter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he made two ornaments of network to cover the chapiters of the pillars; even a net for one chapiter, and a net for the other chapiter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 7:12; Ezekiel 41:25–26"> And hanging work, two rows of brazen pomegranates, formed with network, hanging work, row upon row: and thus he framed the ornaments for the second chapiter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 20:8; Psalms 122:5; Isaiah 9:7"> And he set up the pillars of the porch of the temple: and he set up the one pillar, and called its name Jachin: and he set up the second pillar, and called its name Boaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles II 8:11; Kings III 3:1; Kings III 9:24"> And on the heads of the pillars he made lily-work against the porch, of four cubits,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 7:10–11; Kings III 5:17"> and a chamber over both the pillars, and above the sides an addition equal to the chamber in width.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:19–20; 1 Corinthians 3:10–11; Isaiah 54:11"> And he made the sea, ten cubits from one rim to the other, the same was completely circular round about: its height was five cubits, and its circumference thirty-three cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Peter 2:5"> And stays underneath its rim round about compassed it ten cubits round;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings III 6:36; Kings III 7:6; John 10:23"> and its rim was as the work of the rim of a cup, a lily flower, and the thickness of it was a span.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:11; Chronicles II 2:13–14; Kings III 7:40"> And there were twelve oxen under the sea; three looking to the north, and three looking to the west, and three looking to the south, and three looking to the east: and all their hinder parts were inward, and the sea was above upon them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:14; Chronicles II 4:16; Exodus 31:2–6"> And he made ten brazen bases: five cubits was the length of one base, and four cubits the breadth of it, and its height was six cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:21; Chronicles II 3:15–17; Kings III 7:21"> And this work of the bases was formed with a border to them, and there was a border between the ledges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 38:19; Chronicles II 4:12–13; Exodus 38:17"> And upon their borders between the projections were lions, and oxen, and cherubs: and on the projections, even so above, and also below were the places of lions and oxen, hanging work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 28:14; Exodus 28:24–25; Exodus 28:22"> And there were four brazen wheels to one base; and there were brazen bases, and their four sides answering to them, side pieces under the bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 28:14; Exodus 39:15–18; Exodus 28:22"> And there were axles in the wheels under the base. And the height of one wheel was a cubit and a half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings III 7:22; Kings III 6:32–35; Kings III 6:18"> And the work of the wheels was as the work of chariot wheels: their axles, and their felloes, and the rest of their work, were all molten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Chronicles II 3:16; Chronicles II 4:13; Jeremiah 52:22–23"> The four side pieces were at the four corners of each base; its shoulders were formed of the base.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Chronicles II 3:17; Kings III 6:3; Kings III 7:12"> And on the top of the base half a cubit was the size of it, there was a circle on the top of the base, and there was the top of its spaces and its borders: and it was open at the top of its spaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And its borders were cherubs, and lions, and palm trees, upright, each was joined in front and within and round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:2; Kings IV 25:13; Jeremiah 52:17"> According to the same form he made all the ten bases, even one order and one measure to all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings III 6:18; Chronicles II 4:3; Exodus 37:17–22"> And he made ten brazen lavers, each laver containing forty baths, and measuring four cubits, each laver placed on a several base throughout the ten bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:20; Chronicles II 4:4–5; Matthew 28:19"> And he put five bases on the right side of the house, and five on the left side of the house: and the sea was placed on the right side of the house eastward in the direction of the south.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:5; Kings III 7:38; Kings III 6:18"> And Hiram made the caldrons, and the pans, and the bowls; and Hiram finished making all the works that he wrought for King Solomon in the house of the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:14; Kings IV 25:13; Jeremiah 52:17"> two pillars and the wreathen works of the pillars on the heads of the two pillars; and the two networks to cover both the wreathen works of the flutings that were upon the pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The four hundred pomegranates for both the networks, two rows of pomegranates for one network, to cover both the wreathen works of the bases belonging to both pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hosea 5:14; Ezekiel 10:14; Exodus 37:7"> And the ten bases, and the ten lavers upon the bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:10–13; Ezekiel 3:13; Ezekiel 1:15–21"> And one sea, and the twelve oxen under the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the caldrons, and pans, and bowls, and all the furniture, which Hiram made for King Solomon for the house of the Lord: and there were eight and forty pillars of the house of the king and of the house of the Lord: all the works of the king which Hiram made were entirely of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> There was no reckoning of the brass of which he made all these works, from the very great abundance, there was no end of the weight of the brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:18; Ezekiel 1:16"> In the country round about Jordan did he cast them, in the clay land between Succoth and Zarethan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And King Solomon took the furniture which Hiram made for the house of the Lord, the golden altar, and the golden table of showbread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And he put the five candlesticks on the left, and five on the right in front of the oracle, being of pure gold, and the lampstands, and the lamps, and the snuffers of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Kings III 7:29; Kings III 6:29; Ezekiel 41:18–20"> And there were made the porches, and the nails, and the bowls, and the spoons, and the golden censers, of pure gold: and the panels of the doors of the innermost part of the house, even the holy of holies, and the golden doors of the temple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> So the work of the house of the Lord which Solomon wrought was finished; and Solomon brought in the holy things of David his father, and all the holy things of Solomon; he put the silver, and the gold, and the furniture, into the treasures of the house of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 40:11–12; Zechariah 13:1; 1 John 1:7"> And Solomon built a house for himself in thirteen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:10; Chronicles II 4:6"> And he built the house with the wood of Lebanon; its length was a hundred cubits, and its breadth was fifty cubits, and its height was of thirty cubits, and it was made with three rows of cedar pillars, and the pillars had side pieces of cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:11–16; Exodus 24:6; Exodus 39:32–43"> And he formed the house with chambers above on the sides of the pillars, and the number of the pillars was each row forty and five,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Kings III 7:15–22; Chronicles II 4:12"> and there were three chambers, and space against space in three rows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Kings III 7:20"> And all the doors and spaces formed like chambers were square, and from door to door was a correspondence in three rows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And he made the porch of the pillars, they were fifty cubits long and fifty broad, the porch joining them in front; and the other pillars and the thick beam were in front of the house by the porches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Kings III 7:23; Kings III 7:25"> And there was the Porch of seats where he would judge, the porch of judgment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Exodus 27:3; Exodus 38:3; Chronicles II 4:16"> And their house where he would dwell, had one court communicating with these according to this work; and he built the house for the daughter of Pharaoh whom Solomon had taken, according to this porch.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; Chronicles II 4:17; Genesis 33:17"> All these were of costly stones, sculptured at intervals within even from the foundation even to the top, and outward to the great court,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Chronicles I 22:14; Chronicles I 22:3; Chronicles II 4:18"> founded with large costly stones, stones of ten cubits and eight cubits long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Exodus 37:10–16; Chronicles II 4:8; Exodus 39:36"> And above with costly stones, according to the measure of hewn stones, and with cedars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:7; Chronicles II 4:20; Zechariah 4:11–14"> There were three rows of hewn stones round about the great hall, and a row of sculptured cedar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 25:29; Chronicles II 4:21–22; Exodus 27:3"> and Solomon finished all his house.</span>
</div>
</div>
