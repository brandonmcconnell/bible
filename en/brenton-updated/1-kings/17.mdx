<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:25–26; James 5:17; Luke 1:17"> And Elijah the prophet, the Tishbite of Tishbe of Gilead, said to Ahab, As the Lord God of hosts, the God of Israel, lives, before whom I stand, there shall not be these years dew nor rain, except by the word of my mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:1; Jeremiah 7:1; Hosea 1:1–2"> And the word of the Lord came to Elijah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Jeremiah 36:26; Psalms 83:3"> Depart hence eastward, and hide thee by the brook of Cherith, that is before Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 17:9; Psalms 147:9; Job 38:41"> And it shall be that thou shalt drink water of the brook, and I will charge the ravens to feed thee there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings III 19:9; Matthew 16:24; John 15:14"> And Elijah did according to the word of the Lord, and he sat by the brook of Cherith before Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 78:23–24; Matthew 6:31–33; Jeremiah 37:21"> And the ravens brought him loaves in the morning, and flesh in the evening, and he drank water of the brook.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 54:10; Isaiah 40:30–31"> And it came to pass after some time, that the brook was dried up, because there had been no rain upon the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 22:14; Kings III 17:2; Hebrews 13:6"> And the word of the Lord came to Elijah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 4:26; Obadiah 1:20; 2 Corinthians 4:7"> Arise, and go to Zarephath of the Sidonian land: behold, I have there commanded a widow woman to maintain thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:7; Genesis 24:17; Genesis 21:15"> And he arose and went to Zarephath, and came to the gate of the city: and, behold, a widow woman was there gathering sticks; and Elijah cried after her, and said to her, Fetch me, I pray thee, a little water in a vessel, that I may drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 25:35–40; Kings III 18:4; Hebrews 13:2"> And she went to fetch it; and Elijah cried after her, and said, Bring me, I pray thee, a morsel of the bread that is in thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 15:33–34; Kings III 17:1; Genesis 21:16"> And the woman said, As the Lord thy God lives, I have not a cake, but only a handful of meal in the pitcher, and a little oil in a cruse, and, behold, I am going to gather two sticks, and I shall go in and dress it for myself and my children, and we shall eat it and die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Malachi 3:10; Genesis 22:1–2"> And Elijah said to her, Be of good courage, go in and do according to thy word: but make me thereof a little cake, and thou shalt bring it out to me first, and thou shalt make some for thyself and thy children last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:36–38; Matthew 14:17–20; Kings IV 4:42–44"> For thus saith the Lord, The pitcher of meal shall not fail, and the cruse of oil shall not diminish, until the day that the Lord gives rain upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 11:17; Mark 12:43; Matthew 15:28"> And the woman went and did so, and did eat, she, and he, and her children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:28–30; John 4:50–51; Luke 1:37"> And the pitcher of meal failed not, and the cruse of oil was not diminished, according to the word of the Lord which he spoke by the hand of Elijah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 1:7; Daniel 5:23; John 11:14"> And it came to pass afterward, that the son of the woman the mistress of the house was sick; and his sickness was very severe, until there was no breath left in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 5:8; John 2:4; Kings IV 3:13"> And she said to Elijah, What have I to do with thee, O man of God? hast thou come in to me to bring my sins to remembrance, and to slay my son?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings IV 4:32; Kings IV 4:10; Acts 9:37"> And Elijah said to the woman, Give me thy son. And he took him out of her bosom, and took him up to the chamber in which he himself lodged, and laid him on the bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 5:15–18; Matthew 21:22; Joshua 7:8–9"> And Elijah cried aloud, and said, Alas, O Lord, the witness of the widow with whom I sojourn, thou hast wrought evil for her in slaying her son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:10; Acts 9:40; Kings IV 4:33–35"> And he breathed on the child thrice, and called on the Lord, and said, O Lord my God, let, I pray thee, the soul of this child return to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Revelation 11:11; Hebrews 11:35"> And it was so, and the child cried out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings IV 4:36–37; Acts 9:41; Luke 7:15"> and he brought him down from the upper chamber into the house, and gave him to his mother; and Elijah said, See, thy son lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 16:30; John 2:11; John 3:2"> And the woman said to Elijah, Behold, I know that thou art a man of God, and the word of the Lord in thy mouth is true.</span>
</div>
