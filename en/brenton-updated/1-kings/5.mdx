<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles I 14:1; Chronicles II 2:3; Kings III 5:10"> And thus the officers provided King Solomon: and they execute every one in his month all the orders for the table of the king, they omit nothing. And they carried the barley and the straw for the horses and the chariots to the place where the king might be, each according to his charge.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:3"> And these were the requisite supplies for Solomon: in one day thirty measures of fine flour, and sixty measures of fine pounded meal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles I 28:3; Chronicles I 22:8; Psalms 110:1"> and ten choice calves, and twenty pastured oxen, and a hundred sheep, besides stags, and choice fatted does.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Chronicles I 22:9; Kings III 4:24; Acts 9:31"> For he had dominion on this side the river, and he was at peace on all sides round about.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 3:7; Ezekiel 27:17; Acts 12:20"> And the Lord gave understanding to Solomon, and very much wisdom, and enlargement of heart, as the sand on the seashore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Solomon abounded greatly beyond the wisdom of all the ancients, and beyond all the wise men of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:10; Kings III 4:22"> And he was wiser than all other men: and he was wiser than Ethan the Ezrahite, and than Heman, and than Calcol and Darda the sons of Mahol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings III 3:12; Amos 1:9; James 1:5"> And Solomon spoke three thousand proverbs, and his songs were five thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 9:15; Kings III 4:6"> And he spoke of trees, from the cedar in Lebanon even to the hyssop which comes out through the wall: he spoke also of cattle, and of birds, and of reptiles, and of fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings III 4:6–19; Chronicles I 27:1–15"> And all the nations came to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and ambassadors from all the kings of the earth, as many as heard of his wisdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings III 4:6–19; Chronicles I 27:1–15"> And Solomon took to himself the daughter of Pharaoh to wife, and brought her into the city of David until he had finished the house of the Lord, and his own house, and the wall of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings III 4:6–19; Chronicles I 27:1–15"> Then went up Pharaoh the king of Egypt, and took Gezer, and burned it and the Canaanite dwelling in Mergab; and Pharaoh gave them as a dowry to his daughter the wife of Solomon: and Solomon rebuilt Gezer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 9:20–22; Chronicles II 2:17–18; Chronicles II 8:7–9"> And Hiram king of Tyre sent his servants to anoint Solomon in the room of David his father, because Hiram always loved David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 9:23; Chronicles II 2:2"> And Solomon sent to Hiram, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 6:7; Chronicles I 22:2; Isaiah 28:16"> Thou knewest my father David, that he could not build a house to the name of the Lord my God because of the wars that compassed him about, until the Lord put them under the soles of his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 13:5; Ezekiel 27:9; Psalms 83:7"> And now the Lord my God has given me rest round about; there is no one plotting against me, and there is no evil trespass against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And, behold, I intend to build a house to the name of the Lord my God, as the Lord God spoke to my father David, saying, Thy son whom I will set on thy throne in thy place, he shall build a house to my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And now command, and let men cut wood for me out of Lebanon: and, behold, my servants shall be with thy servants, and I will give thee the wages of thy service, according to all that thou shalt say, because thou knowest that we have no one skilled in cutting timber like the Sidonians.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And it came to pass, as soon as Hiram heard the words of Solomon, that he rejoiced greatly, and said, Blessed be God today, who has given to David a wise son over this numerous people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And he sent to Solomon, saying, I have listened concerning all that thou hast sent to me for: I will do all thy will: as for timber of cedar and fir,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> my servants shall bring them down from Lebanon to the sea: I will form them into rafts, and bring them to the place which thou shalt send to me about; and I will land them there, and thou shalt take them up: and thou shalt do my will, in giving bread to my household.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> So Hiram gave to Solomon cedars, and fir trees, and all his desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Solomon gave to Hiram twenty thousand measures of wheat as food for his house, and twenty thousand baths of beaten oil thus Solomon gave to Hiram yearly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the Lord gave wisdom to Solomon as he promised him; and there was peace between Hiram and Solomon, and they made a covenant between them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And the king raised a levy out of all Israel, and the levy was thirty thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he sent them to Lebanon, ten thousand taking turn every month: they were a month in Lebanon and two months at home: and Adoniram was over the levy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And Solomon had seventy thousand bearers of burdens, and eighty thousand hewers of stone in the mountain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> besides the rulers that were appointed over the works of Solomon, there were three thousand six hundred masters who wrought in the works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And they prepared the stones and the timber during three years.</span>
</div>
</div>
