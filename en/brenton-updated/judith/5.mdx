<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then was it declared to Holofernes, the chief captain of the army of Assyria, that the children of Israel had prepared for war, and had shut up the passages of the hill country, and had fortified all the tops of the high hills, and had laid impediments in the champaign countries:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> wherewith he was very angry, and called all the princes of Moab, and the captains of Ammon, and all the governors of the sea coast,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and he said unto them, Tell me now, ye sons of Canaan, who this people is, that dwelleth in the hill country, and what are the cities that they inhabit, and what is the multitude of their army, and wherein is their power and strength, and what king is set over them, or captain of their army;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and why have they determined not to come and meet me, more than all the inhabitants of the west?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then said Achior, the captain of all the sons of Ammon, Let my lord now hear a word from the mouth of thy servant, and I will declare unto thee the truth concerning this people, which dwelleth near thee, and inhabiteth the hill countries: and there shall no lie come out of the mouth of thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> This people are descended of the Chaldeans:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and they sojourned heretofore in Mesopotamia, because they would not follow the gods of their fathers, which were in the land of Chaldea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> For they left the way of their ancestors, and worshiped the God of heaven, the God whom they knew: so they cast them out from the face of their gods, and they fled into Mesopotamia, and sojourned there many days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then their God commanded them to depart from the place where they sojourned, and to go into the land of Canaan: where they dwelt, and were increased with gold and silver, and with very much cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But when a famine covered all the land of Canaan, they went down into Egypt, and sojourned there, while they were nourished, and became there a great multitude, so that one could not number their nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Therefore the king of Egypt rose up against them, and dealt subtly with them, and brought them low with laboring in brick, and made them slaves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then they cried unto their God, and he smote all the land of Egypt with incurable plagues: so the Egyptians cast them out of their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And God dried the Red Sea before them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and brought them to Mount Sinai, and Kadesh-barnea, and cast forth all that dwelt in the wilderness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> So they dwelt in the land of the Amorites, and they destroyed by their strength all them of Heshbon, and passing over Jordan they possessed all the hill country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And they cast forth before them the Canaanite, the Perizzite, the Jebusite, and the Shechemite, and all the Girgashites, and they dwelt in that country many days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And while they sinned not before their God, they prospered, because the God that hateth iniquity was with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But when they departed from the way which he appointed them, they were destroyed in many battles very sore, and were led captives into a land that was not theirs, and the temple of their God was cast to the ground, and their cities were taken by the enemies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But now are they returned to their God, and are come up from the places where they were scattered, and have possessed Jerusalem, where their sanctuary is, and are seated in the hill country; for it was desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Now therefore, my lord and governor, if there be any error in this people, and they sin against their God, let us consider that this shall be their ruin, and let us go up, and we shall overcome them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But if there be no iniquity in their nation, let my lord now pass by, lest their Lord defend them, and their God be for them, and we become a reproach before all the world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And when Achior had finished these sayings, all the people standing round about the tent murmured, and the chief men of Holofernes, and all that dwelt by the sea side, and in Moab, spoke that he should kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For, say they, we will not be afraid of the face of the children of Israel: for, lo, it is a people that have no strength nor power for a strong battle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Now therefore, lord Holofernes, we will go up, and they shall be a prey to be devoured of all thine army.</span>
</div>
</div>
