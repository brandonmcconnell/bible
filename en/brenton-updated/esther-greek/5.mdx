<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And it came to pass on the third day, when she had ceased praying, that she put off her mean dress, and put on her glorious apparel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And being splendidly arrayed, and having called upon God the Overseer and Preserver of all things, she took her two maids, and she leaned upon one, as a delicate female, and the other followed bearing her train.</span>
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And she was blooming in the perfection of her beauty; and her face was cheerful, as it were benevolent, but her heart was straitened for fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And having passed through all the doors, she stood before the king: and he was sitting upon his royal throne, and he had put on all his glorious apparel, covered all over with gold and precious stones, and was very terrible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And having raised his face resplendent with glory, he looked with intense anger: and the queen fell, and changed her color as she fainted; and she bowed herself upon the head of the maid that went before her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> But God changed the spirit of the king to gentleness, and in intense feeling he sprang from off his throne, and took her into his arms, until she recovered: and he comforted her with peaceable words, and said to her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> What is the matter, Esther? I am thy brother; be of good cheer, thou shalt not die, for our command is openly declared to thee, Draw nigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And having raised the golden scepter he laid it upon her neck, and embraced her, and said, Speak to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And she said to him, I saw thee, my lord, as an angel of God, and my heart was troubled for fear of thy glory; for thou, my lord, art to be wondered at, and thy face is full of grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And while she was speaking, she fainted and fell. Then the king was troubled, and all his servants comforted her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And the king said, What wilt thou, Esther? and what is thy request? ask even to the half of my kingdom, and it shall be thine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And Esther said, Today is my great day: if then it seem good to the king, let both him and Haman come to the feast which I will prepare this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the king said, Hasten Haman hither, that we may perform the word of Esther. So they both come to the feast of which Esther had spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And at the banquet the king said to Esther, What is thy request, queen Esther? speak, and thou shalt have all that thou requirest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And she said, My request and my petition are:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> if I have found favor in the sight of the king, let the king and Haman come again tomorrow to the feast which I shall prepare for them, and tomorrow I will do the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> So Haman went out from the king very glad and merry: but when Haman saw Mordecai the Jew in the court, he was greatly enraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And having gone into his own house, he called his friends, and his wife Zeresh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he showed them his wealth, and the glory with which the king had invested him, and how he had caused him to take precedence and bear chief rule in the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And Haman said, The queen has called no one to the feast with the king but me, and I am invited tomorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> But these things please me not, while I see Mordecai the Jew in the court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Zeresh his wife and his friends said to him, Let there be a gallows made for thee of fifty cubits, and in the morning do thou speak to the king, and let Mordecai be hanged on the gallows: but do thou go in to the feast with the king, and be merry. And the saying pleased Haman, and the gallows was prepared.</span>
