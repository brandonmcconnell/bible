<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the king levied a tax upon his kingdom both by land and sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And as for his strength and valor, and the wealth and glory of his kingdom, behold, they are written in the book of the Persians and Medes, for a memorial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And Mordecai was viceroy to King Artaxerxes, and was a great man in the kingdom, and honored by the Jews, and passed his life beloved of all his nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And Mordecai said, These things have been done of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For I remember the dream which I had concerning these matters: for not one particular of them has failed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> There was the little fountain, which became a river, and there was light, and the sun, and much water. The river is Esther, whom the king married, and made queen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And the two serpents are I and Haman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And the nations are those nations that combined to destroy the name of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> But as for my nation, this is Israel, even they that cried to God, and were delivered: for the Lord delivered his people, and the Lord rescued us out of all these calamities; and God wrought such signs and great wonders as have not been done among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Therefore did he ordain two lots, one for the people of God, and one for all the other nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And these two lots came for an appointed season, and for a day of judgment, before God, and for all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And God remembered his people, and vindicated his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And they shall observe these days, in the month Adar, on the fourteenth and on the fifteenth day of the month, with an assembly, and joy and gladness before God, throughout the generations forever among his people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> In the fourth year of the reign of Ptolemy and Cleopatra, Dositheus, who said that he was a priest and a Levite, and Ptolemy his son, brought in the published letter of Purim, which they said existed, and which Lysimachus the son of Ptolemy, who was in Jerusalem, had interpreted.</span>
