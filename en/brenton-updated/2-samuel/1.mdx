<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 25:2; Proverbs 14:15"> And David said to the young man who brought him the tidings, How knowest thou that Saul and Jonathan his son are dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings I 28:4; Kings II 1:21; Chronicles I 10:4–6"> And the young man that brought the tidings, said to him, I happened accidentally to be upon Mount Gilboa; and, behold, Saul was leaning upon his spear, and, behold, the chariots and captains of horse pressed hard upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings I 22:12; Kings II 9:6; Judges 9:54"> And he looked behind him, and saw me, and called me; and I said, Behold, here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings I 30:17; Kings I 30:13; Kings I 30:1"> And he said to me, Who art thou? and I said, I am an Amalekite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And he said to me, Stand, I pray thee, over me, and slay me, for a dreadful darkness has come upon me, for all my life is in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Judges 9:54; Kings IV 11:12"> So I stood over him and slew him, because I knew he would not live after he was fallen; and I took the crown that was upon his head, and the bracelet that was upon his arm, and I have brought them hither to my lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings II 13:31; Genesis 37:29; Kings II 3:31"> And David laid hold of his garments, and rent them; and all the men who were with him rent their garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17; Psalms 35:13–14; Matthew 5:44"> And they lamented, and wept, and fasted till evening, for Saul and for Jonathan his son, and for the people of Judah, and for the house of Israel, because they were smitten with the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings II 1:8"> And David said to the young man who brought the tidings to him, Whence art thou? and he said, I am the son of an Amalekite sojourner.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings I 26:9; Kings I 31:4; 2 Peter 2:10"> And David said to him, How was it thou wast not afraid to lift thy hand to destroy the anointed of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 2:34; Judges 8:20; Kings II 4:10–12"> And David called one of his young men, and said, Go and fall upon him: and he smote him, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Luke 19:22; Leviticus 20:9"> And David said to him, Thy blood be upon thine own head; for thy mouth has testified against thee, saying, I have slain the anointed of the Lord.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Judges 14:18; Lamentations 4:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Saul and Jonathan, the beloved and the beautiful, were not divided:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Comely were they in their life, and in their death they were not divided:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They were swifter than eagles,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And they were stronger than lions.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Jeremiah 2:32; Judges 5:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Daughters of Israel, weep for Saul,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Who clothed you with scarlet together with your adorning,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Who added golden ornaments to your apparel.</span><br /></span>
