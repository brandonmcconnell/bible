<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Luke 1:74–75; Kings III 5:4"> And it came to pass when the king sat in his house, and the Lord had given him an inheritance on every side free from all his enemies round about him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles I 16:1; Kings II 5:11; Haggai 1:4"> that the king said to Nathan the prophet, Behold now, I live in a house of cedar, and the ark of the Lord dwells in the midst of a tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles I 22:7; Kings III 8:17–18; Kings IV 4:27"> And Nathan said to the king, Go and do all that is in thy heart, for the Lord is with thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Chronicles I 17:3; Numbers 12:6; Amos 3:7"> And it came to pass in that night, that the word of the Lord came to Nathan, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles I 23:3–32; Kings III 5:3–4; Kings III 8:16–19"> Go, and say to my servant David, Thus says the Lord, Thou shalt not build me a house for me to dwell in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 8:16; Joshua 18:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> For I have not dwelt in a house from the day that I brought up the children of Israel out of Egypt to this day, but I have been walking in a lodge and in a tent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles I 17:6; Kings II 5:2; Leviticus 26:11–12"> wheresoever I went with all Israel. Have I ever spoken to any of the tribes of Israel, which I commanded to tend my people Israel, saying, Why have ye not built me a house of cedar?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 78:70–71; Kings II 6:21; Kings II 12:7"> And now thus shalt thou say to my servant David, Thus says the Lord Almighty, I took thee from the sheepcote, that thou shouldest be a prince over my people, over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings II 5:10; Chronicles I 17:8; Kings I 18:14"> And I was with thee wheresoever thou wentest, and I destroyed all thine enemies before thee, and I made thee renowned according to the renown of the great ones on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 9:15; Chronicles I 17:9; Jeremiah 24:6"> And I will appoint a place for my people Israel, and will plant them, and they shall dwell by themselves, and shall be no more distressed; and the son of iniquity shall no more afflict them, as he has done from the beginning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings II 7:27; Kings II 7:1; Chronicles I 17:10"> from the days when I appointed judges over my people Israel: and I will give thee rest from all thine enemies, and the Lord will tell thee that thou shalt build a house to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings III 8:20; Acts 2:30; Acts 13:36"> And it shall come to pass when thy days shall have been fulfilled, and thou shalt sleep with thy fathers, that I will raise up thy seed after thee, even thine own issue, and I will establish his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:31–33; Kings III 8:19; Kings II 7:16"> He shall build for me a house to my name, and I will set up his throne even forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 1:5; Revelation 3:19; Chronicles I 17:13"> I will be to him a father, and he shall be to me a son. And when he happens to transgress, then will I chasten him with the rod of men, and with the stripes of the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 15:23; Kings I 15:28; Psalms 89:28"> But my mercy I will not take from him, as I took it from those whom I removed from my presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Psalms 89:36–37; Kings II 7:13"> And his house shall be made sure, and his kingdom forever before me, and his throne shall be set up forever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles I 17:15; Acts 20:27; 1 Corinthians 15:3"> According to all these words, and according to all this vision, so Nathan spoke to David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 8:4; Chronicles I 17:16; Kings I 18:18"> And King David came in, and sat before the Lord, and said, Who am I, O Lord, my Lord, and what is my house, that thou hast loved me hitherto?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 55:8–9; Chronicles I 17:17; Psalms 36:7"> Whereas I was very little before thee, O Lord, my Lord, yet thou spokest concerning the house of thy servant for a long time to come. And is this the law of man, O Lord, my Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 21:17; Kings I 16:7; Psalms 139:1"> And what shall David yet say to thee? and now thou knowest thy servant, O Lord, my Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 115:1; Psalms 138:2; Ephesians 1:9"> And thou hast wrought for thy servant’s sake, and according to thy heart thou hast wrought all this greatness, to make it known to thy servant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:10; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 86:8"> that he may magnify thee, O my Lord; for there is no one like thee, and there is no God but thou among all of whom we have heard with our ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:26; Deuteronomy 10:21; Deuteronomy 15:15"> And what other nation in the earth is as thy people Israel? whereas God was his guide, to redeem for himself a people to make thee a name, to do mightily and nobly, so that thou shouldest cast out nations and their tabernacles from the presence of thy people, whom thou didst redeem for thyself out of Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:18; 1 Peter 2:10; Genesis 17:7"> And thou hast prepared for thyself thy people Israel to be a people forever, and thou, Lord, art become their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 119:49; Ezekiel 36:37; Genesis 32:12"> And now, O my Lord, the Almighty Lord God of Israel, confirm the word forever which thou hast spoken concerning thy servant and his house: and now as thou hast said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Chronicles I 17:23–24; Psalms 72:18–19; Matthew 6:9"> Let thy name be magnified forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Chronicles I 17:25–26; Psalms 10:17; Ruth 4:4"> Almighty Lord God of Israel, thou hast uncovered the ear of thy servant, saying, I will build thee a house: therefore thy servant has found in his heart to pray this prayer to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 17:17; Exodus 34:6; Numbers 23:19"> And now, O Lord my Lord, thou art God; and thy words will be true, and thou hast spoken these good things concerning thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 6:24–26; Chronicles I 17:27; Psalms 115:12–15"> And now begin and bless the house of thy servant, that it may continue forever before thee; for thou, O Lord, my Lord, hast spoken, and the house of thy servant shall be blessed with thy blessing so as to continue forever.</span>
