<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles I 18:1–17; Kings II 7:9; Kings II 21:15–22"> And it came to pass after this, that David smote the Philistines, and put them to flight, and David took the tribute from out of the hand of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; Kings I 10:27; Kings II 8:6"> And David smote Moab, and measured them out with lines, having laid them down on the ground: and there were two lines for slaying, and two lines he kept alive: and Moab became servants to David, yielding tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings I 14:47; Kings II 10:16; Kings II 10:19"> And David smote Hadadezer the son of Rehob king of Zobah, as he went to extend his power to the river Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:6; Joshua 11:9; Chronicles I 18:4"> And David took a thousand of his chariots, and seven thousand horsemen, and twenty thousand footmen: and David houghed all his chariot horses, and he reserved to himself a hundred chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings III 11:23–25; Isaiah 7:8; Job 9:13"> And Syria of Damascus comes to help Hadadezer king of Zobah, and David smote twenty-two thousand men belonging to the Syrian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings II 8:14; Kings II 7:9; Psalms 18:34–46"> And David placed a garrison in Syria near Damascus, and the Syrians became servants and tributaries to David: and the Lord preserved David whithersoever he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles II 9:15–16; Kings III 14:26–27; Kings III 10:16–17"> And David took the golden bracelets which were on the servants of Hadadezer king of Zobah, and brought them to Jerusalem. And Shishak king of Egypt took them, when he went up to Jerusalem in the days of Rehoboam son of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles I 18:8; Chronicles I 22:16; Ezekiel 47:16"> And King David took from Betah, and from the choice cities of Hadadezer, very much brass: with that Solomon made the brazen sea, and the pillars, and the lavers, and all the furniture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 8:65; Chronicles II 8:4; Chronicles I 18:9"> And Toi the king of Hamath heard that David had smitten all the host of Hadadezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings I 13:10; Chronicles I 18:10; Psalms 129:8"> And Toi sent Joram his son to King David, to ask him of his welfare, and to congratulate him on his fighting against Hadadezer and smiting him, for he was an enemy to Hadadezer: and in his hands were vessels of silver, and vessels of gold, and vessels of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings III 7:51; Chronicles I 29:2; Chronicles I 22:14–16"> And these King David consecrated to the Lord, with the silver and with the gold which he consecrated out of all the cities which he conquered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings II 10:14; Kings II 8:2; Chronicles I 18:11"> out of Edom, and out of Moab, and from the children of Ammon, and from the Philistines, and from Amalek, and from the spoils of Hadadezer son of Rehob king of Zobah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings IV 14:7; Kings II 7:9; Chronicles I 18:12"> And David made himself a name: and when he returned he smote Edom in Gebelem to the number of eighteen thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 27:29; Genesis 25:23; Kings II 8:6"> And he set garrisons in Edom, even in all Edom: and all the Edomites were servants to the king. And the Lord preserved David wherever he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 5:15; Psalms 89:14; Psalms 72:2"> And David reigned over all Israel: and David wrought judgment and justice over all his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 4:3; Chronicles I 11:6; Kings II 19:13"> And Joab the son of Zeruiah was over the host; and Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud was keeper of the records.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles I 6:8; Chronicles I 18:16; Chronicles I 24:3–4"> And Zadok the son of Ahitub, and Ahimelech son of Abiathar, were priests; and Seraiah was the scribe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings I 30:14; Kings II 20:7; Kings II 20:23"> and Benaiah son of Jehoiada was counselor, and the Cherethite and the Pelethite, and the sons of David, were princes of the court.</span>
