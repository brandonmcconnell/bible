<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 7:11–12; Genesis 26:1; Genesis 12:10"> And there was a famine in the days of David three years, year after year; and David sought the face of the Lord. And the Lord said, There is guilt upon Saul and his house because of his bloody murder, whereby he slew the Gibeonites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 9:3–21; Kings IV 10:31; Kings I 14:44"> And King David called the Gibeonites, and spoke to them. (Now the Gibeonites are not the children of Israel, but are of the remnant of the Amorite, and the children of Israel had sworn to them: but Saul sought to smite them in his zeal for the children of Israel and Judah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings II 20:19; Hebrews 9:22; Kings I 26:19"> And David said to the Gibeonites, What shall I do to you? and wherewithal shall I make atonement, that ye may bless the inheritance of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 49:6–8; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> And the Gibeonites said to him, We have no question about silver or gold with Saul and with his house; and there is no man for us to put to death in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings II 21:1; Daniel 9:26; Matthew 7:2"> And he said, What say ye? speak, and I will do it for you. And they said to the king, The man who would have made an end of us, and persecuted us, who plotted against us to destroy us, let us utterly destroy him, so that he shall have no standing in all the coasts of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings I 10:24; Numbers 25:4–5; Kings I 11:4"> Let one give us seven men of his sons, and let us hang them up in the sun to the Lord in Gibeon of Saul, as chosen out for the Lord. And the king said, I will give them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings I 20:15; Kings I 18:3; Kings I 23:18"> But the king spared Mephibosheth son of Jonathan the son of Saul, because of the oath of the Lord that was between them, even between David and Jonathan the son of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings I 18:19; Kings II 3:7"> And the king took the two sons of Rizpah the daughter of Aiah, whom she bore to Saul, Armoni and Mephibosheth, and the five sons of Michal daughter of Saul, whom she bore to Adriel son of Barzillai the Meholathite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ruth 1:22; Deuteronomy 21:1–9; Kings II 6:17"> And he gave them into the hand of the Gibeonites, and they hanged them up to the sun in the mountain before the Lord: and they fell, even the seven together: moreover they were put to death in the days of harvest at the commencement, in the beginning of barley harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:23; Kings III 21:27; Joel 2:23"> And Rizpah the daughter of Aiah took sackcloth, and fixed it for herself on the rock in the beginning of barley harvest, until water dropped upon them out of heaven: and she did not suffer the birds of the air to rest upon them by day, nor the beasts of the field by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings II 2:4; Ruth 2:11–12"> And it was told David what Rizpah the daughter of Aiah the concubine of Saul had done, and they were faint, and Dan the son of Joa of the offspring of the giants overtook them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 17:11; Kings I 31:10–13; Kings II 2:5–7"> And David went and took the bones of Saul, and the bones of Jonathan his son, from the men of the sons of Jabesh-gilead, who stole them from the street of Beth-shan; for the Philistines set them there in the day in which the Philistines smote Saul in Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he carried up thence the bones of Saul and the bones of Jonathan his son, and gathered the bones of them that had been hanged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 18:28; Kings II 24:25; Joshua 7:26"> And they buried the bones of Saul and the bones of Jonathan his son, and the bones of them that had been hanged, in the land of Benjamin in the hill, in the sepulcher of Kish his father; and they did all things that the king commanded: and after this God hearkened to the prayers of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 14:10–11; Kings II 5:22; Ecclesiastes 12:3"> And there was yet war between the Philistines and Israel: and David went down and his servants with him, and they fought with the Philistines, and David went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings II 21:18; Kings II 21:20; Kings I 17:4–5"> And Ishbi, who was of the progeny of Rapha, and the head of whose spear was three hundred shekels of brass in weight, who also was girded with a club, even he thought to smite David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 11:36; Kings II 18:3; Psalms 132:17"> And Abishai the son of Zeruiah helped him and smote the Philistine, and slew him. Then the men of David swore, saying, Thou shalt not any longer go out with us to battle, and thou shalt not quench the lamp of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles I 11:29; Kings II 21:20; Kings II 21:16"> And after this there was a battle again with the Philistines in Gath: then Sibbecai the Hushathite slew Saph of the progeny of Rapha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Chronicles I 20:5; Kings I 17:4–11; Chronicles I 11:26"> And there was a battle in Gob with the Philistines; and Elhanan son of Jaare-oregim the Bethlehemite slew Goliath the Gittite; and the staff of his spear was as a weaver’s beam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings II 21:18; Kings II 21:16; Chronicles I 20:6"> And there was yet a battle in Gath: and there was a man of stature, and the fingers of his hands and the toes of his feet were six on each, four and twenty in number: and he also was born to Rapha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings I 17:10; Kings I 17:36; Kings I 16:9"> And he defied Israel, and Jonathan son of Shimei brother of David, smote him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Chronicles I 20:8; Romans 8:31; Jeremiah 9:23"> These four were born descendants of the giants in Gath, the family of Rapha; and they fell by the hand of David, and by the hand of his servants.</span>
