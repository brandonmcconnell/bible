<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 12:16; Chronicles II 10:16; Deuteronomy 13:13"> And there was a transgressor so called there, and his name was Sheba, a Benjaminite, the son of Bichri: and he blew the trumpet, and said, We have no portion in David, neither have we any inheritance in the son of Jesse: to thy tents, O Israel, every one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 6:66–68; Acts 11:23; Kings II 19:15"> And all the men of Israel went up from following David after Sheba the son of Bichri: but the men of Judah adhered to their king, from Jordan even to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings II 16:21–22; Kings II 15:16; Genesis 40:3"> And David went into his house at Jerusalem: and the king took the ten women his concubines, whom he had left to keep the house, and he put them in a place of custody, and maintained them, and went not in to them; and they were kept living as widows, till the day of their death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings II 19:13; Kings II 17:25; Chronicles I 2:17"> And the king said to Amasa, Call to me the men of Judah for three days, and do thou be present here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings II 19:13; Kings I 13:8"> And Amasa went to call Judah, and delayed beyond the time which David appointed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings II 21:17; Kings II 11:11; Kings III 1:33"> And David said to Amasa, Now shall Sheba the son of Bichri do us more harm than Absalom: now then take thou with thee the servants of thy lord, and follow after him, lest he find for himself strong cities, so will he blind our eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings II 8:18; Kings III 1:38; Kings II 20:23"> And there went out after him Amasa and the men of Joab, and the Cherethites, and the Pelethites, and all the mighty men: and they went out from Jerusalem to pursue after Sheba the son of Bichri.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings II 3:30; Kings II 2:13; Kings II 20:4–5"> And they were by the great stone that is in Gibeon: and Amasa went in before them: and Joab had upon him a military cloak over his apparel, and over it he was girded with a dagger fastened upon his loins in its scabbard: and the dagger came out, it even came out and fell.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:47–48; Matthew 26:48–49; Micah 7:2"> And Joab said to Amasa, Art thou in health, my brother? and the right hand of Joab took hold of the beard of Amasa to kiss him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings II 2:23; Kings II 3:27; Judges 3:21"> And Amasa observed not the dagger that was in the hand of Joab: and Joab smote him with it on the loins, and his bowels were shed out upon the ground, and he did not repeat the blow, and he died: and Joab and Abishai his brother pursued after Sheba the son of Bichri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings II 20:13; Kings II 20:21; Kings IV 9:32"> And there stood over him one of the servants of Joab, and said, Who is he that is for Joab, and who is on the side of David following Joab?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21–22; Psalms 9:16; Kings II 17:25"> And Amasa was weltering in blood in the midst of the way. And a man saw that all the people stood still; and he removed Amasa out of the path into a field, and he cast a garment upon him, because he saw everyone that came to him standing still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings I 6:12; Isaiah 7:3; Kings IV 18:17"> And when he was quickly removed from the road, every man of Israel passed after Joab to pursue after Sheba the son of Bichri.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings IV 15:29; Kings III 15:20; Chronicles II 16:4"> And he went through all the tribes of Israel to Abel, and to Beth-maacah; and all in Charri too were assembled, and followed after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings IV 19:32; Jeremiah 32:24; Kings III 15:20"> And they came and besieged him in Abel and Beth-maacah: and they raised a mound against the city and it stood close to the wall; and all the people with Joab proposed to throw down the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings II 14:2; Ecclesiastes 9:14–18; Kings I 25:32–33"> And a wise woman cried from the wall, and said, Hear, hear; say, I pray ye, to Joab, Draw near hither, and I will speak to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings II 14:12; Kings I 25:24"> And he drew nigh to her, and the woman said to him, Art thou Joab? and he said, I am. And she said to him, Hear the words of thy handmaid; and Joab said, I do hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:10–11"> And she spoke, saying, Of old time they said thus, Surely one was asked in Abel, and Dan, whether the faithful in Israel failed in what they purposed; they will surely ask in Abel, even in like manner, whether they have failed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings I 26:19; Kings II 21:3; Kings II 17:16"> I am a peaceable one of the strong ones in Israel; but thou seekest to destroy a city and a mother-city in Israel: why dost thou seek to ruin the inheritance of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:29; Proverbs 28:13; Jeremiah 17:9"> And Joab answered and said, Far be it, far be it from me, that I should ruin or destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings I 26:9; Judges 2:9; Kings II 17:2–3"> Is not the case thus, that a man of Mount Ephraim, Sheba son of Bichri by name, has even lifted up his hand against King David? Give him only to me, and I will depart from the city. And the woman said to Joab, Behold, his head shall be thrown to thee over the wall.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings II 20:1; Ecclesiastes 7:19; Kings II 11:6–21"> And the woman went in to all the people, and she spoke to all the city in her wisdom; and they took off the head of Sheba the son of Bichri; and took it away and threw it to Joab: and he blew the trumpet, and the people separated from the city away from him, every man to his tent: and Joab returned to Jerusalem to the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings II 8:16–18; Chronicles I 18:15–17; Kings III 4:3–6"> And Joab was over all the forces of Israel: and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and over the Pelethites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings III 4:3; Kings III 12:18; Kings III 4:6"> And Adoram was over the tribute: and Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud was recorder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings III 4:4; Kings II 8:17; Chronicles I 18:16"> And Sheva was scribe: and Zadok and Abiathar were priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings II 23:38; Exodus 2:14; Genesis 41:45"> Moreover Ira the son of Jair was priest to David.</span>
</div>
