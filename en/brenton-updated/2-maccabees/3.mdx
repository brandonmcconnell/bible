<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Now when the holy city was inhabited with all peace, and the laws were kept very well, because of the godliness of Onias the high priest, and his hatred of wickedness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> it came to pass that even the kings themselves did honor the place, and magnify the temple with their best gifts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> insomuch that Seleucus king of Asia of his own revenues bare all the costs belonging to the service of the sacrifices.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But one Simon of the tribe of Benjamin, who was made governor of the temple, fell out with the high priest about disorder in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And when he could not overcome Onias, he got him to Apollonius the son of Tharseas, who then was governor of Coelesyria and Phoenicia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and told him that the treasury in Jerusalem was full of infinite sums of money, so that the multitude of their riches, which did not pertain to the account of the sacrifices, was innumerable, and that it was possible to bring all into the king’s hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Now when Apollonius came to the king, and had shown him of the money whereof he was told, the king chose out Heliodorus his treasurer, and sent him with a commandment to bring him the foresaid money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> So forthwith Heliodorus took his journey, under a color of visiting the cities of Coelesyria and Phoenicia, but indeed to fulfill the king’s purpose.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And when he was come to Jerusalem, and had been courteously received of the high priest of the city, he told him what intelligence was given of the money, and declared wherefore he came, and asked if these things were so indeed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then the high priest told him that there was such money laid up for the relief of widows and fatherless children:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and that some of it belonged to Hyrcanus son of Tobias, a man of great dignity, and not as that wicked Simon had misinformed: the sum whereof in all was four hundred talents of silver, and two hundred of gold:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and that it was altogether impossible that such wrongs should be done unto them, that had committed it to the holiness of the place, and to the majesty and inviolable sanctity of the temple, honored over all the world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> But Heliodorus, because of the king’s commandment given him, said, That in anywise it must be brought into the king’s treasury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So at the day which he appointed he entered in to order this matter: wherefore there was no small agony throughout the whole city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> But the priests, prostrating themselves before the altar in their priests’ vestments, called unto heaven upon him that made a law concerning things given to be kept, that they should safely be preserved for such as had committed them to be kept.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then whoso had looked the high priest in the face, it would have wounded his heart: for his countenance and the changing of his color declared the inward agony of his mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For the man was so compassed with fear and horror of the body, that it was manifest to them that looked upon him, what sorrow he had now in his heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Others ran flocking out of their houses to the general supplication, because the place was like to come into contempt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And the women, girded with sackcloth under their breasts, abounded in the streets, and the virgins that were kept in ran, some to the gates, and some to the walls, and others looked out of the windows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And all, holding their hands toward heaven, made supplication.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then it would have pitied a man to see the falling down of the multitude of all sorts, and the fear of the high priest, being in such an agony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> They then called upon the Almighty Lord to keep the things committed of trust safe and sure for those that had committed them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Nevertheless Heliodorus executed that which was decreed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Now as he was there present himself with his guard about the treasury, the Lord of spirits, and the Prince of all power, caused a great apparition, so that all that presumed to come in with him were astonished at the power of God, and fainted, and were sore afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For there appeared unto them a horse with a terrible rider upon him, and adorned with a very fair covering, and he ran fiercely, and smote at Heliodorus with his forefeet, and it seemed that he that sat upon the horse had complete harness of gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Moreover two other young men appeared before him, notable in strength, excellent in beauty, and comely in apparel, who stood by him on either side, and scourged him continually, and gave him many sore stripes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And Heliodorus fell suddenly unto the ground, and was compassed with great darkness: but they that were with him took him up, and put him into a litter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Thus him, that lately came with a great train and with all his guard into the said treasury, they carried out, being unable to help himself with his weapons: and manifestly they acknowledged the power of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> for he by the hand of God was cast down, and lay speechless without all hope of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> But they praised the Lord, that had miraculously honored his own place: for the temple, which a little afore was full of fear and trouble, when the Almighty Lord appeared, was filled with joy and gladness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Then straightaway certain of Heliodorus’ friends prayed Onias, that he would call upon the most High to grant him his life, who lay ready to give up the ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> So the high priest, suspecting lest the king should misconceive that some treachery had been done to Heliodorus by the Jews, offered a sacrifice for the health of the man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Now as the high priest was making an atonement, the same young men in the same clothing appeared and stood beside Heliodorus, saying, Give Onias the high priest great thanks, insomuch as for his sake the Lord hath granted thee life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> and seeing that thou hast been scourged from heaven, declare unto all men the mighty power of God. And when they had spoken these words, they appeared no more.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> So Heliodorus, after he had offered sacrifice unto the Lord, and made great vows unto him that had saved his life, and saluted Onias, returned with his host to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Then testified he to all men the works of the great God, which he had seen with his eyes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And when the king asked Heliodorus, who might be a fit man to be sent yet once again to Jerusalem, he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> If thou hast any enemy or traitor, send him thither, and thou shalt receive him well scourged, if he escape with his life: for in that place, no doubt, there is an especial power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> For he that dwelleth in heaven hath his eye on that place, and defendeth it; and he beateth and destroyeth them that come to hurt it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And the things concerning Heliodorus, and the keeping of the treasury, fell out on this sort.</span>
</div>
</div>
