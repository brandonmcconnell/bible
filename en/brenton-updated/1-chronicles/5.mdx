<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; Genesis 29:32; Genesis 48:15–22"> And the sons of Reuben the firstborn of Israel (for he was the firstborn; but because of his going up to his father’s couch, his father gave his blessing to his son Joseph, even the son of Israel; and he was not reckoned as firstborn;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:6; Micah 5:2; Hebrews 7:14"> for Judah was very mighty even among his brethren, and one was to be a ruler out of him: but the blessing was Joseph’s).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:9; Exodus 6:14; Numbers 26:5–9"> The sons of Reuben the firstborn of Israel; Hanoch, and Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> The sons of Joel; Shemaiah, and Benaiah his son: and the sons of Gog the son of Shemaiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> His son was Micah, his son Reaiah, his son Baal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles I 5:26; Kings IV 15:29; Chronicles II 28:20"> his son Beerah, whom Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria carried away captive: he is the chief of the Reubenites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles I 5:17"> And his brethren in his family, in their distribution according to their generations; the chief, Jeiel, and Zechariah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles I 5:4; Numbers 32:34; Numbers 32:38"> And Bela the son of Azaz, the son of Shema, the son of Joel: he dwelt in Aroer, and even to Nebo, and Baal-meon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 22:8–9"> And he dwelt eastward to the borders of the wilderness, from the river Euphrates: for they had much cattle in the land of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 21:9; Chronicles I 5:18–21; Psalms 83:6"> And in the days of Saul they made war upon the sojourners in the land; and they fell into their hands, all of them dwelling in their tents eastward of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 13:11; Joshua 13:24–28; Deuteronomy 3:10–17"> The sons of Gad dwelt over against them in the land of Bashan even to Salecah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Joel the firstborn, and Shapham the second, and Janai the scribe in Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And their brethren according to the houses of their fathers; Michael, Meshullam, and Sheba, and Jorai, and Jacan, and Zia, and Eber, seven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> These are the sons of Abihail the son of Huri, the son of Idai, the son of Gilead, the son of Michael, the son of Jeshishai, the son of Jahdo, the son of Buz,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> who was the brother of the son of Abdiel, the son of Guni, he was chief of the house of their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles I 27:29; Song of Solomon 2:1; Isaiah 35:2"> They dwelt in Gilead, in Bashan, and in their villages, and in all the country round about Sharon to the border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings IV 14:16; Kings IV 14:28; Kings IV 15:32"> The enumeration of them all took place in the days of Jotham king of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:3; Joshua 4:12–13"> The sons of Reuben and Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, of mighty men, bearing shields and sword, and bending the bow, and skilled in war, were forty and four thousand and seven hundred and sixty, going forth to battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 25:15; Chronicles I 1:31; Chronicles I 5:10"> And they made war with the Hagrites, and Jetur, and Naphish, and Nodab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 22:4–5; Psalms 9:10; Chronicles II 18:31"> and they prevailed against them: and the Hagrites were given into their hands, they and all their tents: for they cried to God in the battle, and he hearkened to them, because they trusted on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 18:13; Numbers 31:35"> And they took captive their store; five thousand camels, and two hundred and fifty thousand sheep, two thousand asses, and a hundred thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Chronicles II 32:8; Romans 8:31; Joshua 23:10"> For many fell slain, because the war was of God. And they dwelt in their place until the captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:8–9; Song of Solomon 4:8; Psalms 133:3"> And the half-tribe of Manasseh dwelt from Bashan to Baal-hermon, and Senir, and to the Mount Hermon: and they increased in Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Chronicles I 4:38"> And these were the heads of the houses of their families; Epher, and Ishi, and Eliel, and Jeremiah, and Hodaviah, and Jahdiel, mighty men of valor, men of renown, heads of the houses of their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 17:5; Psalms 106:34–39; Exodus 34:15"> But they rebelled against the God of their fathers, and went a-whoring after the gods of the nations of the land, whom God cast out from before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings IV 15:19; Kings IV 17:6; Kings IV 15:29"> And the God of Israel stirred up the spirit of Pul king of Assyria, and the spirit of Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria, and carried away Reuben and Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, and brought them to Halah, and Habor, and to the river Gozan, until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> The sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the sons of Kohath; Amram, and Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And the sons of Amram; Aaron, and Moses, and Miriam: and the sons of Aaron; Nadab, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Eleazar begot Phinehas, Phinehas begot Abishua;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Abishua begot Bukki, and Bukki begot Uzzi;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Uzzi begot Zerahiah, Zerahiah begot Meraioth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and Meraioth begot Amariah, and Amariah begot Ahitub;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> and Ahitub begot Zadok, and Zadok begot Ahimaaz;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and Ahimaaz begot Azariah, and Azariah begot Johanan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> and Johanan begot Azariah: he ministered as priest in the house which Solomon built in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And Azariah begot Amariah, and Amariah begot Ahitub;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and Ahitub begot Zadok, and Zadok begot Shallum;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> and Shallum begot Hilkiah, and Hilkiah begot Azariah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and Azariah begot Seraiah, and Seraiah begot Jehozadak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And Jehozadak went into captivity with Judah and Jerusalem under Nebuchadnezzar.</span>
