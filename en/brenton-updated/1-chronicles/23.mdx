<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 24:1; Chronicles II 34:29–30; Chronicles I 13:1"> And he assembled all the chief men of Israel, and the priests, and the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles I 23:24; Numbers 4:2–49"> And the Levites numbered themselves from thirty years old and upward; and their number by their polls amounted to thirty and eight thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Chronicles II 19:8; Deuteronomy 16:18; Chronicles I 26:29–31"> Of the overseers over the works of the house of the Lord there were twenty-four thousand, and there were six thousand scribes and judges;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles II 29:25–26; Chronicles II 8:14; Psalms 87:7"> and four thousand doorkeepers, and four thousand to praise the Lord with instruments which he made to praise the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles II 8:14; Chronicles I 6:1; Chronicles I 6:16"> And David divided them into daily courses, for the sons of Levi, for Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles I 26:21; Exodus 6:17; Chronicles I 6:17–20"> And for the family of Gershon, Ladan, and Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles I 6:33–34; Chronicles I 15:20–21; Chronicles I 15:7"> The sons of Ladan were Jehiel, the chief, and Zetham, and Joel, three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The sons of Shimei; Shelomoth, Haziel, and Haran, three: these were the chiefs of the families of Ladan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And to the sons of Shimei, Jahath, and Zina, and Jeush, and Beriah: these were the four sons of Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Jahath was the chief, and Zizah the second: and Jeush and Beriah did not multiply sons, and they became only one reckoning according to the house of their father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; Numbers 3:27; Numbers 26:58"> The sons of Kohath; Amram, Izhar, Hebron, Uzziel, four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Exodus 6:20; Kings I 2:28"> The sons of Amram; Aaron and Moses: and Aaron was appointed for the consecration of the most holy things, he and his sons forever, to burn incense before the Lord, to minister and bless in his name forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; Psalms 90:1; Chronicles I 26:23–25"> And as for Moses the man of God, his sons were reckoned to the tribe of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 18:3–4; Exodus 2:22; Exodus 4:20"> The sons of Moses; Gershom, and Eliezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles I 26:24; Chronicles I 25:20; Chronicles I 24:20"> The sons of Gershom; Shebuel the chief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles I 26:25"> And the sons of Eliezer were, Rehabiah the chief: and Eliezer had no other sons; but the sons of Rehabiah were very greatly multiplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles I 26:26; Chronicles I 24:22"> The sons of Izhar; Shelomith the chief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Chronicles I 24:23; Chronicles I 15:9; Chronicles I 23:12"> The sons of Hebron; Jeriah the chief, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, Jekameam the fourth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The sons of Uzziel; Micah the chief, and Isshiah the second.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 6:19; Chronicles I 6:20; Chronicles I 24:26–30"> The sons of Merari; Mahli, and Mushi: the sons of Mahli; Eleazar, and Kish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Chronicles I 24:28; Numbers 36:6–8"> And Eleazar died, and he had no sons, but daughters: and the sons of Kish, their brethren, took them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Chronicles I 24:30"> The sons of Mushi; Mahli, and Eder, and Jeremoth, three.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 10:17; Numbers 10:21; Chronicles I 23:3"> These are the sons of Levi according to the houses of their fathers; chiefs of their families according to their numbering, according to the number of their names, according to their polls, doing the works of service of the house of the Lord, from twenty years old and upward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 132:13–14; Chronicles I 22:18; Kings II 7:11"> For David said, The Lord God of Israel has given rest to his people, and has taken up his abode in Jerusalem forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 4:5; Numbers 7:9; Numbers 4:15"> And the Levites bore not the tabernacle, and all the vessels of it for its service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Chronicles I 23:24; Psalms 72:20; Kings II 23:1"> For by the last words of David was the number of the Levites taken from twenty years old and upward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Chronicles II 35:3–6; Chronicles II 31:11; Chronicles II 35:11–14"> For he appointed them to wait on Aaron, to minister in the house of the Lord, over the courts, and over the chambers, and over the purification of all the holy things, and over the works of the service of the house of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 19:35–36; Exodus 25:30; Leviticus 6:20–23"> and for the showbread, and for the fine flour of the meat offering, and for the unleavened cakes, and for the fried cake, and for the dough, and for every measure;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 92:1–3; Ezra and Nehemiah 3:10–11; Chronicles I 9:33"> and to stand in the morning to praise and give thanks to the Lord, and so in the evening;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13–14; Numbers 28:1–29; Numbers 10:10"> and to be over all the whole burnt offerings that were offered up to the Lord on the Sabbaths, and at the new moons, and at the feasts, by number, according to the order given to them, continually before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:38; Numbers 3:6–9"> And they are to keep the charge of the tabernacle of witness, and the charge of the holy place, and the charges of the sons of Aaron their brethren, to minister in the house of the Lord.</span>
</div>
