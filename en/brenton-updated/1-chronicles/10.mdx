<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings I 31:1–13; Chronicles I 10:8; Kings II 1:6"> Now the Philistines warred against Israel; and they fled from before the Philistines, and fell down slain in Mount Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles I 9:39; Exodus 20:5; Kings I 14:6"> And the Philistines pursued after Saul, and after his sons; and the Philistines smote Jonathan, and Abinadab, and Malchi-shua, sons of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 49:23–24; Kings I 31:3–6; Kings II 1:4–10"> And the battle prevailed against Saul, and the archers hit him with bows and arrows, and they were wounded of the bows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings I 31:4; Judges 9:54; Kings I 17:26"> And Saul said to his armor bearer, Draw thy sword, and pierce me through with it, lest these uncircumcised come and mock me. But his armor bearer would not, for he was greatly afraid: so Saul took a sword, and fell upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And his armor bearer saw that Saul was dead, and he also fell upon his sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 13:10–11; Kings I 4:10–11; Kings I 4:18"> So Saul died, and his three sons on that day, and all his family died at the same time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 26:36; Deuteronomy 28:33; Judges 6:2"> And all the men of Israel that were in the valley saw that Israel fled, and that Saul and his sons were dead, and they left their cities, and fled: and the Philistines came and dwelt in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings I 31:8; Kings IV 3:23; Chronicles II 20:25"> And it came to pass on the next day that the Philistines came to strip the slain, and they found Saul and his sons fallen on Mount Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Judges 16:23–24; Matthew 14:11"> And they stripped him, and took his head, and his armor, and sent them into the land of the Philistines round about, to proclaim the glad tidings to their idols, and to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings I 5:2–7; Kings I 31:10"> And they put their armor in the house of their god, and they put his head in the house of Dagon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 31:11–13; Kings I 11:1–11; Kings II 2:4–7"> And all the dwellers in Gilead heard of all that the Philistines had done to Saul and to Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 35:8; Kings II 3:35; Genesis 50:10"> And all the mighty men rose up from Gilead, and they took the body of Saul, and the bodies of his sons, and brought them to Jabesh, and buried their bones under the oak in Jabesh, and fasted seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 20:6; Leviticus 19:31; Kings I 15:23"> So Saul died for his transgressions, wherein he transgressed against God, against the word of the Lord, forasmuch as he kept it not, because Saul inquired of a wizard to seek counsel, and Samuel the prophet answered him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings I 15:28; Kings I 13:14; Kings I 28:17"> and he sought not the Lord: so he slew him, and turned the kingdom to David the son of Jesse.</span>
