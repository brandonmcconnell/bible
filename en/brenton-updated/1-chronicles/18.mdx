<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles I 19:6; Kings II 8:5–6; Kings I 14:47"> And the Syrian came from Damascus to help Hadadezer king of Zobah; and David smote of the Syrian army twenty and two thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles I 17:8; Psalms 121:8; Proverbs 21:31"> And David put a garrison in Syria near Damascus; and they became tributary servants to David: and the Lord delivered David wherever he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles II 9:15–16; Kings III 10:16–17; Chronicles II 12:9–10"> And David took the golden collars that were on the servants of Hadadezer, and brought them to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings II 8:8; Chronicles II 4:12–18; Kings III 7:15–47"> And David took out of Tibhath, and out of the chief cities of Hadadezer very much brass: of this Solomon made the brazen sea, and the pillars, and the brazen vessels.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings II 8:9"> And Tou king of Hamath heard that David had smitten the whole force of Hadadezer king of Zobah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings II 8:10; Isaiah 39:1; Chronicles II 9:1"> And he sent Hadoram his son to King David to ask how he was, and to congratulate him because he had fought against Hadadezer, and smitten him; for Tou was the enemy of Hadadezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 30:13; Chronicles I 26:26–27; Kings III 7:51"> And all the golden and silver and brazen vessels, even these King David consecrated to the Lord, with the silver and the gold which he took from all the nations; from Edom, and Moab, and from the children of Ammon, and from the Philistines, and from Amalek.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles I 19:11; Kings II 10:10; Kings II 8:13–14"> And Abishai son of Zeruiah smote the Edomites in the Valley of Salt, eighteen thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles I 18:6; Genesis 27:37; Psalms 144:10"> And he put garrisons in the valley; and all the Edomites became David’s servants: and the Lord delivered David wherever he went.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:15; Psalms 89:14; Psalms 78:71–72"> So David reigned over all Israel; and he executed judgment and justice to all his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles I 11:6; Kings III 4:3; Kings II 8:16"> And Joab the son of Zeruiah was over the army, and Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud was recorder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings II 8:17; Kings II 20:25; Kings III 4:3"> And Zadok son of Ahitub, and Ahimelech son of Abiathar, were the priests; and Shavsha was the scribe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings II 8:18; Kings II 15:18; Kings III 2:34–35"> and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethite and the Pelethite, and the sons of David were the chief deputies of the king.</span>
