<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles I 22:5; Kings III 3:7; Chronicles II 13:7"> And David the king said to all the congregation, Solomon my son, whom the Lord has chosen, is young and tender, and the work is great; for it is not for man, but for the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles I 22:3–5; 2 Corinthians 8:3; Isaiah 54:11–12"> I have prepared according to all my might for the house of my God gold, silver, brass, iron, wood, onyx stones, and costly and variegated stones for setting, and every precious stone, and much parian marble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles I 21:24; Psalms 27:4; Psalms 26:8"> And still farther, because I took pleasure in the house of my God, I have gold and silver which I have procured for myself, and, behold, I have given them to the house of my God over and above, beyond what I have prepared for the holy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 9:28; Chronicles I 22:14; Job 28:16"> Three thousand talents of gold of Ophir, and seven thousand talents of fine silver, for the overlaying of the walls of the sanctuary:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 7:2–3; Exodus 35:5–9; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:15–16"> for thee to use the gold for things of gold, and the silver for things of silver, and for every work by the hand of the artificers. And who is willing to dedicate himself in work this day for the Lord?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Chronicles I 27:25–1; Isaiah 60:3–10"> Then the heads of families, and the princes of the children of Israel, and the captains of thousands and captains of hundreds, and the overseers of the works, and the king’s builders, offered willingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:70–71; Ezra and Nehemiah 2:69"> And they gave for the works of the house of the Lord five thousand talents of gold, and ten thousand gold pieces, and ten thousand talents of silver, and eighteen thousand talents of brass, and a hundred thousand talents of iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles I 23:8; Chronicles I 26:21–22"> And they who had precious stone, gave it into the treasuries of the house of the Lord by the hand of Jehiel the Gershonite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7–8; 2 Corinthians 8:3; Kings III 8:61"> And the people rejoiced because of the willingness, for they offered willingly to the Lord with a full heart: and King David rejoiced greatly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 1:3; 1 Peter 1:3; Romans 1:7"> And King David blessed the Lord before the congregation, saying,<br />Blessed art thou, O Lord God of Israel, our Father, from everlasting and to everlasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Daniel 4:34–35; 1 Timothy 1:17"> Thine, O Lord, is the greatness, and the power, and the glory, and the victory, and the might: for thou art Lord of all things that are in heaven and upon the earth: before thy face every king and nation is troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 3:16; Chronicles II 20:6; Deuteronomy 8:18"> From thee come wealth and glory: thou, O Lord, rulest over all, the Lord of all dominion, and in thy hand is strength and rule; and thou art almighty with thy hand to increase and establish all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 106:1; 2 Corinthians 9:15; 2 Corinthians 8:16"> And now, Lord, we give thanks to thee, and praise thy glorious name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:17; 2 Corinthians 3:5; Romans 11:36"> But who am I, and what is my people, that we have been able to be thus forward in offering to thee? for all things are thine, and of thine own have we given thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 144:4; Leviticus 25:23; Psalms 102:11"> for we are strangers before thee, and sojourners, as all our fathers were: our days upon the earth are as a shadow, and there is no remaining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles I 29:14; Psalms 24:1; Chronicles II 31:10"> O Lord our God, as for all this abundance which I have prepared that a house should be built to thy holy name, it is of thy hand, and all is thine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Proverbs 11:20; Chronicles I 28:9"> And I know, Lord, that thou art he that searches the hearts, and thou lovest righteousness. I have willingly offered all these things in simplicity of heart; and now I have seen with joy thy people here present, willingly offering to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Jeremiah 10:23; Hebrews 13:21"> O Lord God of Abraham, and Isaac, and Israel, our fathers, preserve these things in the thought of the heart of thy people forever, and direct their hearts to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 72:1; Chronicles I 28:9; James 1:17"> And to Solomon my son give a good heart, to perform thy commandments, and to observe thy testimonies, and thine ordinances, and to accomplish the building of thy house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 95:6; Exodus 4:31; Psalms 135:19–21"> And David said to the whole congregation, Bless ye the Lord our God. And all the congregation blessed the Lord God of their fathers, and they bowed the knee and worshiped the Lord, and did obeisance to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Chronicles II 7:4–9; Numbers 15:7; Ezra and Nehemiah 6:17"> And David sacrificed to the Lord, and offered up whole burnt offerings to the Lord on the morrow after the first day, a thousand calves, a thousand rams, a thousand lambs, and their drink offerings, and sacrifices in abundance for all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings III 2:35; Chronicles I 23:1; Exodus 24:11"> And they ate and drank joyfully that day before the Lord: and they made Solomon the son of David king a second time, and anointed him king before the Lord, and Zadok to the priesthood.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings III 2:12; Chronicles I 28:5; Romans 13:1"> And Solomon sat upon the throne of his father David, and was highly honored; and all Israel obeyed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Chronicles I 2:3–9; Ezekiel 17:18; Genesis 24:2"> The princes, and the mighty men, and all the sons of King David his father, were subject to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Chronicles II 1:12; Chronicles II 1:1; Ecclesiastes 2:9"> And the Lord magnified Solomon over all Israel, and gave him royal glory, such as was not upon any king before him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Chronicles I 18:14; Psalms 78:71–72"> And David the son of Jesse reigned over Israel forty years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings III 2:11; Kings II 5:4–5; Chronicles I 3:4"> seven years in Hebron, and thirty-three years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Acts 13:36; Chronicles I 23:1"> And he died in a good old age, full of days, in wealth, and glory: and Solomon his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings I 9:9; Kings II 12:1–7; Chronicles I 21:9–11"> And the rest of the acts of David, the former and the latter, are written in the history of Samuel the seer, and in the history of Nathan the prophet, and in the history of Gad the seer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Daniel 4:23; Daniel 2:21"> concerning all his reign, and his power, and the times which went over him, and over Israel, and over all the kingdoms of the earth.</span>
</div>
