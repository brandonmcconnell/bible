<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> O Lord Almighty, God of Israel, the soul in anguish, the troubled spirit, crieth unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Hear, O Lord, and have mercy; for thou art merciful: and have pity upon us, because we have sinned before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For thou endurest forever, and we perish utterly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> O Lord Almighty, thou God of Israel, hear now the prayers of the dead Israelites, and of their children, which have sinned before thee, and not hearkened unto the voice of thee their God: for the which cause these plagues cleave unto us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Remember not the iniquities of our forefathers: but think upon thy power and thy name now at this time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For thou art the Lord our God, and thee, O Lord, will we praise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And for this cause thou hast put thy fear in our hearts, to the intent that we should call upon thy name, and praise thee in our captivity: for we have called to mind all the iniquity of our forefathers, that sinned before thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Behold, we are yet this day in our captivity, where thou hast scattered us, for a reproach and a curse, and to be subject to payments, according to all the iniquities of our fathers, which departed from the Lord our God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Hear, Israel, the commandments of life: give ear to understand wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> How happeneth it, Israel, that thou art in thine enemies’ land, that thou art waxen old in a strange country, that thou art defiled with the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> that thou art counted with them that go down into the grave?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Thou hast forsaken the fountain of wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For if thou hadst walked in the way of God, thou shouldest have dwelt in peace forever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Learn where is wisdom, where is strength, where is understanding; that thou mayest know also where is length of days, and life, where is the light of the eyes, and peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Who hath found out her place? or who hath come into her treasures?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Where are the princes of the heathen, and such as ruled the beasts upon the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> they that had their pastime with the fowls of the air, and they that hoarded up silver and gold, wherein men trust, and made no end of their getting?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For they that wrought in silver, and were so careful, and whose works are unsearchable,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> they are vanished and gone down to the grave, and others are come up in their stead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Young men have seen light, and dwelt upon the earth; but the way of knowledge have they not known,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> nor understood the paths thereof, nor laid hold of it: their children were far off from that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> It hath not been heard of in Canaan, neither hath it been seen in Teman.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The Hagarites that seek wisdom upon earth, the merchants of Merran and of Teman, the authors of fables, and searchers out of understanding; none of these have known the way of wisdom, or remembered her paths.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> O Israel, how great is the house of God! and how large is the place of his possession!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Great, and hath none end; high, and unmeasurable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> There were the giants famous from the beginning, that were of so great stature, and so expert in war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Those did not the Lord choose, neither gave he the way of knowledge unto them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> but they were destroyed, because they had no wisdom, and perished through their own foolishness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Who hath gone up into heaven, and taken her, and brought her down from the clouds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Who hath gone over the sea, and found her, and will bring her for pure gold?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> No man knoweth her way, nor thinketh of her path.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> But he that knoweth all things knoweth her, and hath found her out with his understanding: he that prepared the earth for evermore hath filled it with four-footed beasts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> he that sendeth forth light, and it goeth, calleth it again, and it obeyeth him with fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> The stars shined in their watches, and rejoiced: when he calleth them, they say, Here we be; and so with cheerfulness they showed light unto him that made them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> This is our God, and there shall none other be accounted of in comparison of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> He hath found out all the way of knowledge, and hath given it unto Jacob his servant, and to Israel his beloved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Afterward did he show himself upon earth, and conversed with men.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
