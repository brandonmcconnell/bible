<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 31:10; Exodus 35:23; Exodus 35:19"> All the gold that was employed for the works according to all the fabrication of the holy things, was of the gold of the offerings, twenty-nine talents, and seven hundred and twenty shekels according to the holy shekel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 28:6–12; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 25:7"> And the offering of silver from the men that were numbered of the congregation a hundred talents, and a thousand seven hundred and seventy-five shekels, one drachma apiece, even the half shekel, according to the holy shekel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 36:8; Exodus 26:1"> Every one that passed the survey from twenty years old and upward to the number of six hundred thousand, and three thousand five hundred and fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And the hundred talents of silver went to the casting of the hundred chapiters of the tabernacle, and to the chapiters of the veil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Matthew 28:20; Isaiah 11:5"> a hundred chapiters to the hundred talents, a talent to a chapiter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 28:9; Ezekiel 28:13; Job 28:16"> And the thousand seven hundred and seventy-five shekels he formed into hooks for the pillars, and he gilded their chapiters and adorned them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 4:7; Exodus 28:12; Exodus 28:29"> And the brass of the offering was seventy talents, and a thousand five hundred shekels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Leviticus 8:8–9; Isaiah 59:17"> and they made of it the bases of the door of the tabernacle of witness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and the bases of the court round about, and the bases of the gate of the court, and the pins of the tabernacle, and the pins of the court round about;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 28:21; Exodus 28:16–17; Revelation 21:19–21"> and the brazen appendage of the altar, and all the vessels of the altar, and all the instruments of the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 28:18; Ezekiel 28:13"> And the children of Israel did as the Lord commanded Moses, so did they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And of the gold that remained of the offering they made vessels to minister with before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the blue that was left, and the purple, and the scarlet they made into garments of ministry for Aaron, so that he should minister with them in the sanctuary;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:12"> and they brought the garments to Moses, and the tabernacle, and its furniture, its bases and its bars and the posts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 10:28; John 17:12; 1 Peter 1:5"> and the ark of the covenant, and its bearers, and the altar and all its furniture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:12"> And they made the anointing oil, and the incense of composition, and the pure candlestick,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and its lamps, lamps for burning, and oil for the light,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 28:14; Song of Solomon 1:10"> and the table of showbread, and all its furniture, and the showbread upon it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and the garments of the sanctuary which belong to Aaron, and the garments of his sons, for the priestly ministry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 26:3"> and the curtains of the court, and the posts, and the veil of the door of the tabernacle, and the gate of the court,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; 1 Corinthians 1:25; 1 Corinthians 1:27"> and all the vessels of the tabernacle and all its instruments: and the skins, even rams’ skins dyed red, and the blue coverings, and the coverings of the other things, and the pins, and all the instruments for the works of the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 28:31–35"> Whatsoever things the Lord appointed Moses, so did the children of Israel make all the furniture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Moses saw all the works; and they had done them all as the Lord commanded Moses, so had they made them; and Moses blessed them.</span>
