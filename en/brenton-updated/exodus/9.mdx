<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 8:1; Exodus 10:3; Exodus 8:20"> And the Lord said to Moses, Go in to Pharaoh, and thou shalt say to him, These things saith the Lord God of the Hebrews; Send my people away that they may serve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 8:2; Exodus 4:23; Leviticus 26:27–28"> If however thou wilt not send my people away, but yet detainest them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 7:4; Acts 13:11; Kings I 6:9"> behold, the hand of the Lord shall be upon thy cattle in the fields, both on the horses, and on the asses, and on the camels and oxen and sheep, a very great mortality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 8:22; Exodus 10:23; Malachi 3:18"> And I will make a marvelous distinction in that time between the cattle of the Egyptians, and the cattle of the children of Israel: nothing shall die of all that is of the children’s of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 8:23; Exodus 10:4; Jeremiah 28:16–17"> And God fixed a limit, saying, Tomorrow the Lord will do this thing on the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 78:48; Exodus 9:25; Psalms 78:50"> And the Lord did this thing on the next day, and all the cattle of the Egyptians died, but of the cattle of the children of Israel there died not one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 7:14; Exodus 8:32; Isaiah 48:4"> And when Pharaoh saw, that of all the cattle of the children of Israel there died not one, the heart of Pharaoh was hardened, and he did not let the people go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 8:16"> And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying, Take you handfuls of ashes of the furnace, and let Moses scatter it toward heaven before Pharaoh, and before his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 16:2; Deuteronomy 28:27; Job 2:7"> And let it become dust over all the land of Egypt, and there shall be upon men and upon beasts sore blains breaking forth both on men and on beasts, in all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:27"> So he took of the ashes of the furnace before Pharaoh, and Moses scattered it toward heaven, and it became sore blains breaking forth both on men and on beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 7:11–12; Exodus 8:18–19; Revelation 16:2"> And the sorcerers could not stand before Moses because of the sores, for the sores were on the sorcerers, and in all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 7:13–14; Psalms 81:11–12"> And the Lord hardened Pharaoh’s heart, and he hearkened not to them, as the Lord appointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 8:20; Exodus 7:15; Exodus 9:1"> And the Lord said to Moses, Rise up early in the morning, and stand before Pharaoh; and thou shalt say to him, These things saith the Lord God of the Hebrews, Send away my people that they may serve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 8:10; Leviticus 26:28; Chronicles I 17:20"> For at this present time do I send forth all my plagues into thy heart, and the heart of thy servants and of thy people; that thou mayest know that there is not another such as I in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 3:20; Exodus 9:3; Exodus 9:16"> For now I will stretch forth my hand and smite thee and kill thy people, and thou shalt be consumed from off the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 9:17; Proverbs 16:4; Psalms 83:17–18"> And for this purpose hast thou been preserved, that I might display in thee my strength, and that my name might be published in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Isaiah 10:15; Isaiah 37:23–24"> Dost thou then yet exert thyself to hinder my people, so as not to let them go?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 20:6; Psalms 83:15; Exodus 9:22–25"> Behold, tomorrow at this hour I will rain a very great hail, such as has not been in Egypt, from the time it was created until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:2"> Now then hasten to gather thy cattle, and all that thou hast in the fields; for all the men and cattle as many as shall be found in the fields, and shall not enter into a house (but the hail shall fall upon them), shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 13:13; Proverbs 22:3; Hebrews 11:7"> He of the servants of Pharaoh that feared the word of the Lord, gathered his cattle into the houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:4; Job 34:14; Chronicles I 22:19"> And he that did not attend in his mind to the word of the Lord, left the cattle in the fields.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 16:21; Exodus 7:19; Exodus 8:5"> And the Lord said to Moses, Stretch out thy hand to heaven, and there shall be hail on all the land of Egypt, both on the men and on the cattle, and on all the herbage on the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 18:13; Joshua 10:11; Isaiah 30:30"> And Moses stretched forth his hand to heaven, and the Lord sent thunderings and hail; and the fire ran along upon the ground, and the Lord rained hail on all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 9:23; Matthew 24:21; Exodus 10:6"> So there was hail and flaming fire mingled with hail; and the hail was very great, such as was not in Egypt, from the time there was a nation upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 78:47; Psalms 105:32–33"> And the hail smote in all the land of Egypt both man and beast, and the hail smote all the grass in the field, and the hail broke in pieces all the trees in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 11:7; Exodus 9:4; Exodus 10:23"> Only in the land of Goshen where the children of Israel were, the hail was not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Lamentations 1:18; Psalms 129:4; Exodus 10:16"> And Pharaoh sent and called Moses and Aaron, and said to them, I have sinned this time: the Lord is righteous, and I and my people are wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Exodus 10:17; Exodus 8:28"> Pray then for me to the Lord, and let him cause the thunderings of God to cease, and the hail and the fire, and I will send you forth and ye shall remain no longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:26; Kings III 8:38; Isaiah 1:15"> And Moses said to him, When I shall have departed from the city, I will stretch out my hands to the Lord, and the thunderings shall cease, and the hail and the rain shall be no longer, that thou mayest know that the earth is the Lord’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; Proverbs 16:6; Isaiah 63:17"> But as for thee and thy servants, I know that ye have not yet feared the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ruth 1:22; Ruth 2:23; Habakkuk 3:17"> And the flax and the barley were smitten, for the barley was advanced, and the flax was seeding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 10:22"> But the wheat and the rye were not smitten, for they were late.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 9:29; Exodus 8:12; James 5:17–18"> And Moses went forth from Pharaoh out of the city, and stretched out his hands to the Lord, and the thunders ceased and the hail, and the rain did not drop on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 7:14; Chronicles II 36:13; Exodus 4:21"> And when Pharaoh saw that the rain and the hail and the thunders ceased, he continued to sin; and he hardened his heart, and the heart of his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 4:21"> And the heart of Pharaoh was hardened, and he did not send forth the children of Israel, as the Lord said to Moses.</span>
