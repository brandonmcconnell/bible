<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 8:3; Exodus 37:25–28; Exodus 40:5"> And thou shalt make the altar of incense of incorruptible wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 27:2"> And thou shalt make it a cubit in length, and a cubit in breadth: it shall be square; and the height of it shall be of two cubits, its horns shall be of the same piece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; Exodus 25:24"> And thou shalt gild its grate with pure gold, and its sides round about, and its horns; and thou shalt make for it a wreathen border of gold roundabout.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:27; Exodus 26:29; Exodus 27:7"> And thou shalt make under its wreathen border two rings of pure gold; thou shalt make it to the two corners on the two sides, and they shall be bearings for the staffs, so as to bear it with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:13; Exodus 25:27"> And thou shalt make the staffs of incorruptible wood, and shalt gild them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:21–22; Exodus 30:36; Leviticus 16:13"> And thou shalt set it before the veil that is over the ark of the testimonies, wherein I will make myself known to thee from thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings I 2:28; Chronicles I 23:13; Exodus 27:20–21"> And Aaron shall burn upon it fine compound incense every morning; whensoever he trims the lamps he shall burn incense upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; Hebrews 7:25"> And when Aaron lights the lamps in the evening, he shall burn incense upon it; a constant incense offering always before the Lord for their generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1"> And thou shalt not offer strange incense upon it, nor an offering made by fire, nor a sacrifice; and thou shalt not pour a drink offering upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:18; Hebrews 9:22–23; Leviticus 16:29–30"> And once in the year Aaron shall make atonement on its horns, he shall purge it with the blood of purification for their generations: it is most holy to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; Chronicles I 27:24; Exodus 38:25–26"> If thou take account of the children of Israel in the surveying of them, and they shall give every one a ransom for his soul to the Lord, then there shall not be among them a destruction in the visiting of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:25; Numbers 3:47; Ezekiel 45:12"> And this is what they shall give, as many as pass the survey, half a didrachma which is according to the didrachma of the sanctuary: twenty oboli go to the didrachma, but the half of the didrachma is the offering to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:20; Numbers 1:18; Numbers 1:3"> Every one that passes the survey from twenty years old and upward shall give the offering to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; Leviticus 17:11; Exodus 30:12"> The rich shall not give more, and the poor shall not give less than the half didrachma in giving the offering to the Lord, to make atonement for your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:32–33; Numbers 16:40; Exodus 38:25–31"> And thou shalt take the money of the offering from the children of Israel, and shalt give it for the service of the tabernacle of testimony; and it shall be to the children of Israel a memorial before the Lord, to make atonement for your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 40:7; Exodus 38:8; Kings III 7:23"> Make a brazen laver, and a brazen base for it, for washing; and thou shalt put it between the tabernacle of witness and the altar, and thou shalt pour forth water into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Exodus 40:31–32; Psalms 26:6"> And Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and their feet with water from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–3; Hebrews 12:28–29; Chronicles I 13:10"> Whensoever they shall go into the tabernacle of witness, they shall wash themselves with water, so they shall not die, whensoever they advance to the altar to do service and to offer the whole burnt offerings to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 28:43; Exodus 27:21"> They shall wash their hands and feet with water, whensoever they shall go into the tabernacle of witness; they shall wash themselves with water, that they die not; and it shall be for them a perpetual statute, for him and his posterity after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:19; Song of Solomon 4:14; Ezekiel 27:22"> Do thou also take sweet herbs, the flower of choice myrrh five hundred shekels, and the half of this two hundred and fifty shekels of sweet-smelling cinnamon, and two hundred and fifty shekels of sweet-smelling calamus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 45:8; Exodus 29:40; Numbers 3:47"> and of cassia five hundred shekels of the sanctuary, and a hin of olive oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Chronicles I 9:30; Exodus 40:9"> And thou shalt make it a holy anointing oil, a perfumed ointment tempered by the art of the perfumer: it shall be a holy anointing oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 7:1; 1 John 2:20; Isaiah 61:1"> And thou shalt anoint with it the tabernacle of witness, and the ark of the tabernacle of witness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and all its furniture, and the candlestick and all its furniture, and the altar of incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and the altar of whole burnt offerings and all its furniture, and the table and all its furniture, and the laver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Exodus 29:37; Leviticus 6:18"> And thou shalt sanctify them, and they shall be most holy: everyone that touches them shall be hallowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 8:12; Exodus 28:3; Leviticus 8:30"> And thou shalt anoint Aaron and his sons, and sanctify them that they may minister to me as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Leviticus 21:10; Psalms 89:20"> And thou shalt speak to the children of Israel, saying, This shall be to you a holy anointing oil throughout your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 30:25; Exodus 30:37–38; Leviticus 21:10"> On man’s flesh it shall not be poured, and ye shall not make any for yourselves according to this composition: it is holy, and shall be holiness to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 30:38; Genesis 17:14; Exodus 12:15"> Whosoever shall make it in like manner, and whosoever shall give of it to a stranger, shall be destroyed from among his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Exodus 25:6; Exodus 30:23"> And the Lord said to Moses, Take for thyself sweet herbs, stacte, onycha, sweet galbanum, and transparent frankincense; there shall be an equal weight of each.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 2:13; Exodus 30:25; John 12:3"> And they shall make with it perfumed incense, tempered with the art of a perfumer, a pure holy work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Exodus 30:6; Exodus 16:34"> And of these thou shalt beat some small, and thou shalt put it before the testimonies in the tabernacle of testimony, whence I will make myself known to thee: it shall be to you a most holy incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:32–33"> Ye shall not make any for yourselves according to this composition; it shall be to you a holy thing for the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:33"> Whosoever shall make any in like manner, so as to smell it, shall perish from his people.</span>
