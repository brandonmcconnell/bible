<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 101:5; Proverbs 19:5; Deuteronomy 19:16–21"> Thou shalt not receive a vain report: thou shalt not agree with the unjust man to become an unjust witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 1:10–11; Proverbs 1:15; Luke 23:23–24"> Thou shalt not associate with the multitude for evil; thou shalt not join thyself with a multitude to turn aside with the majority so as to shut out judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:17; Leviticus 19:15; Psalms 82:2–3"> And thou shalt not spare a poor man in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:1–4; 1 Thessalonians 5:15; Matthew 5:44"> And if thou meet thine enemy’s ox or his ass going astray, thou shalt turn them back and restore them to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:4"> And if thou see thine enemy’s ass fallen under its burden, thou shalt not pass by it, but shalt help to raise it with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 5:28; Leviticus 19:15"> Thou shalt not wrest the sentence of the poor in his judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Exodus 34:7; Exodus 23:1"> Thou shalt abstain from every unjust thing: thou shalt not slay the innocent and just, and thou shalt not justify the wicked for gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Proverbs 17:23; Isaiah 5:23"> And thou shalt not receive gifts; for gifts blind the eyes of the seeing, and corrupt just words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:19; Exodus 22:21; Deuteronomy 24:14–18"> And ye shall not afflict a stranger, for ye know the heart of a stranger; for ye were yourselves strangers in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 25:3–4"> Six years thou shalt sow thy land, and gather in the fruits of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 26:34–35; Leviticus 25:2–7; Leviticus 25:11–12"> But in the seventh year thou shalt let it rest, and leave it, and the poor of thy nation shall feed; and the wild beasts of the field shall eat that which remains: thus shalt thou do to thy vineyard and to thine olive yard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Exodus 34:21; Exodus 35:3"> Six days shalt thou do thy works, and on the seventh day there shall be rest, that thine ox and thine ass may rest, and that the son of thy maidservant and the stranger may be refreshed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 23:7; Zechariah 13:2; 1 Timothy 4:16"> Observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you; and ye shall make no mention of the name of other gods, neither shall they be heard out of your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 34:22–24; Leviticus 23:34"> Keep ye a feast to me three times in the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 22:7; Exodus 34:20; Numbers 28:16–25"> Take heed to keep the feast of unleavened bread: seven days ye shall eat unleavened bread, as I charged thee at the season of the month of new corn, for in it thou camest out of Egypt: thou shalt not appear before me empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 34:22; Exodus 22:29; Leviticus 23:9–21"> And thou shalt keep the feast of the harvest of firstfruits of thy labors, whatsoever thou shalt have sown in thy field, and the feast of completion at the end of the year in the gathering in of thy fruits out of thy field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 34:23; Exodus 23:14"> Three times in the year shall all thy males appear before the Lord thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 34:25; Leviticus 2:11; Deuteronomy 16:4"> For when I shall have cast out the nations from before thee, and shall have widened thy borders, thou shalt not offer the blood of my sacrifice with leaven, neither must the fat of my feast abide till the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 34:26; Deuteronomy 14:21; Deuteronomy 26:10"> Thou shalt bring the first offerings of the firstfruits of thy land into the house of the Lord thy God. Thou shalt not seethe a lamb in its mother’s milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Psalms 91:11; Exodus 32:34"> And, behold, I send my angel before thy face, that he may keep thee in the way, that he may bring thee into the land which I have prepared for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 78:56; Psalms 78:40; Hebrews 12:25"> Take heed to thyself and hearken to him, and disobey him not; for he will not give way to thee, for my name is on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Deuteronomy 30:7; Zechariah 2:8"> If ye will indeed hear my voice, and if thou wilt do all the things I shall charge thee with, and keep my covenant, ye shall be to me a peculiar people above all nations, for the whole earth is mine; and ye shall be to me a royal priesthood, and a holy nation: these words shall ye speak to the children of Israel, If ye shall indeed hear my voice, and do all the things I shall tell thee, I will be an enemy to thine enemies, and an adversary to thine adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Isaiah 5:13; Genesis 15:19–21"> For my angel shall go as thy leader, and shall bring thee to the Amorite, and Hittite, and Perizzite, and Canaanite, and Girgashite, and Hivite, and Jebusite, and I will destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 12:30–31; Numbers 33:52"> Thou shalt not worship their gods, nor serve them: thou shalt not do according to their works, but shalt utterly destroy them, and break to pieces their pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:15; Exodus 15:26; Joshua 22:5"> And thou shalt serve the Lord thy God, and I will bless thy bread and thy wine and thy water, and I will turn away sickness from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:14; Job 5:26; Deuteronomy 28:4"> There shall not be on thy land one that is impotent or barren. I will surely fulfill the number of thy days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Genesis 35:5; Deuteronomy 11:25"> And I will send terror before thee, and I will strike with amazement all the nations to which thou shalt come, and I will make all thine enemies to flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:20; Joshua 24:11–12"> And I will send hornets before thee, and thou shalt cast out the Amorites and the Hivites, and the Canaanites and the Hittites from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:22; Joshua 17:12–13; Judges 3:1–4"> I will not cast them out in one year, lest the land become desolate, and the beasts of the field multiply against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> By little and little I will cast them out from before thee, until thou shalt be increased and inherit the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 21:44"> And I will set thy borders from the Red Sea, to the Sea of the Philistines, and from the wilderness to the great river Euphrates; and I will give into your hand those that dwell in the land, and will cast them out from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Exodus 34:12; Exodus 34:15"> Thou shalt make no covenant with them and their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 106:36; Deuteronomy 7:16; Exodus 34:12"> And they shall not dwell in thy land, lest they cause thee to sin against me; for if thou shouldest serve their gods, these will be an offense to thee.</span>
