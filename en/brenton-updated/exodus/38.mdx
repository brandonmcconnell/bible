<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 27:4"> And he made the propitiatory over the ark of pure gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:3; Exodus 25:6"> and the two cherubs of gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Corinthians 2:2; 1 Corinthians 1:24"> one cherub on the one end of the propitiatory, and another cherub on the other end of the propitiatory,</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 27:9–19; Psalms 89:7; Kings III 6:36"> And he made the set table of pure gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and cast for it four rings: two on the one side and two on the other side, broad, so that men should lift it with the staffs in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he made the staffs of the ark and of the table, and gilded them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he made the furniture of the table, both the dishes, and the censers, and the cups, and the bowls with which he should offer drink offerings, of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he made the candlestick which gives light, of gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:14"> the stem solid, and the branches from both its sides;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and blossoms proceeding from its branches, three on this side, and three on the other, made equal to each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And as to their lamps, which are on the ends, knops proceeded from them; and sockets proceeding from them, that the lamps might be upon them; and the seventh socket, on the top of the candlestick, on the summit above, entirely of solid gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And on the candlestick seven golden lamps, and its snuffers gold, and its funnels gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles II 3:14"> He overlaid the posts with silver, and cast for each post golden rings, and gilded the bars with gold; and he gilded the posts of the veil with gold, and made the hooks of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> He made also the rings of the tabernacle of gold; and the rings of the court, and the rings for drawing out the veil above of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Chronicles II 3:9; Exodus 27:19; Ephesians 2:21–22"> He cast the silver chapiters of the tabernacle, and the brazen chapiters of the door of the tabernacle, and the gate of the court; and he made silver hooks for the posts, he overlaid them with silver on the posts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 7:44; Numbers 9:15; Chronicles II 24:6"> He made the pins of the tabernacle and the pins of the court of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 35:30–3; Exodus 31:1–5; Psalms 119:6"> He made the brazen altar of the brazen censers, which belonged to the men engaged in sedition with the gathering of Korah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 35:34; Exodus 31:6"> He made all the vessels of the altar and its grate, and its base, and its bowls, and the brazen fleshhooks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 18:16; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:25"> He made an appendage for the altar of network under the grate, beneath it as far as the middle of it; and he fastened to it four brazen rings on the four parts of the appendage of the altar, wide enough for the bars, so as to bear the altar with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:12"> He made the holy anointing oil and the composition of the incense, the pure work of the perfumer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Exodus 30:13; Exodus 12:37"> He made the brazen laver, and the brazen base of it of the mirrors of the women that fasted, who fasted by the doors of the tabernacle of witness, in the day in which he set it up.</span>
