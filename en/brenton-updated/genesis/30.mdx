<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 5:2; Genesis 29:31; Genesis 37:11"> And Rachel having perceived that she bore Jacob no children, was jealous of her sister; and said to Jacob, Give me children; and if not, I shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 16:2; Psalms 127:3; Kings IV 5:7"> And Jacob was angry with Rachel, and said to her, Am I in the place of God, who has deprived thee of the fruit of the womb?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 50:23; Job 3:12; Ruth 4:11"> And Rachel said to Jacob, Behold my handmaid Bilhah, go in to her, and she shall bear upon my knees, and I also shall have children by her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; Genesis 21:10; Kings II 12:11"> And she gave him Bilhah her maid, for a wife to him; and Jacob went in to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Bilhah, Rachel’s maid, conceived, and bore Jacob a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Lamentations 3:59; Psalms 35:24; Psalms 43:1"> And Rachel said, God has given judgment for me, and hearkened to my voice, and has given me a son; therefore she called his name, Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Bilhah, Rachel’s maid, conceived yet again, and bore a second son to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:24–25; Genesis 23:6; Genesis 49:21"> And Rachel said, God has helped me, and I contended with my sister and prevailed; and she called his name, Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 30:4; Genesis 30:17; Genesis 29:35"> And Leah saw that she ceased from bearing, and she took Zilpah her maid, and gave her to Jacob for a wife; and he went in to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Zilpah the maid of Leah conceived, and bore Jacob a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Isaiah 65:11; Genesis 35:26"> And Leah said, It is happily: and she called his name, Gad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And Zilpah the maid of Leah conceived yet again, and bore Jacob a second son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:48; Proverbs 31:28; Song of Solomon 6:9"> And Leah said, I am blessed, for the women will pronounce me blessed; and she called his name, Asher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Song of Solomon 7:13; Genesis 25:30"> And Reuben went in the day of barley harvest, and found apples of mandrakes in the field, and brought them to his mother Leah; and Rachel said to Leah her sister, Give me of thy son’s mandrakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 16:13; 1 Corinthians 4:3; Numbers 16:9–10"> And Leah said, Is it not enough for thee that thou hast taken my husband, wilt thou also take my son’s mandrakes? And Rachel said, Not so: let him lie with thee tonight for thy son’s mandrakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Jacob came in out of the field at even; and Leah went forth to meet him, and said, Thou shalt come in to me this day, for I have hired thee for my son’s mandrakes; and he lay with her that night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 30:6; Genesis 30:22; Exodus 3:7"> And God hearkened to Leah, and she conceived, and bore Jacob a fifth son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:14–15; Deuteronomy 33:18; Genesis 46:13"> And Leah said, God has given me my reward, because I gave my maid to my husband; and she called his name Issachar, which is, Reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Leah conceived again, and bore Jacob a sixth son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:23; Matthew 4:13; Genesis 49:13"> And Leah said, God has given me a good gift in this time; my husband will choose me, for I have born him six sons: and she called his name, Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:1–3; Genesis 34:26; Genesis 46:15"> And after this she bore a daughter; and she called her name, Dinah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 29:31; Psalms 113:9; Psalms 127:3"> And God remembered Rachel, and God hearkened to her, and he opened her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Genesis 29:31; Luke 1:27"> And she conceived, and bore Jacob a son; and Rachel said, God has taken away my reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:22–26; Genesis 35:24; Genesis 37:2"> And she called his name Joseph, saying, Let God add to me another son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 24:54; Genesis 24:56; Genesis 28:15"> And it came to pass when Rachel had born Joseph, Jacob said to Laban, Send me away, that I may go to my place and to my land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 29:30; Hosea 12:12; Genesis 31:26"> Restore my wives and my children, for whom I have served thee, that I may depart, for thou knowest the service wherewith I have served thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Acts 7:10; Isaiah 61:9"> And Laban said to him, If I have found grace in thy sight, I would augur well, for the Lord has blessed me at thy coming in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 29:15; Genesis 29:19"> Appoint me thy wages, and I will give them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 31:6; Genesis 31:38–40; 1 Peter 2:18"> And Jacob said, Thou knowest in what things I have served thee, and how many cattle of thine are with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Deuteronomy 11:10"> For it was little thou hadst before my time, and it is increased to a multitude, and the Lord God has blessed thee since my coming; now then, when shall I set up also my own house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings II 21:4–6; Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 118:8"> And Laban said to him, What shall I give thee? and Jacob said to him, Thou shalt not give me anything; if thou wilt do this thing for me, I will again tend thy flocks and keep them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 31:8; Genesis 31:10; Genesis 30:35"> Let all thy sheep pass by today, and separate thence every gray sheep among the rams, and every one that is speckled and spotted among the goats—this shall be my reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Kings II 22:21; Kings I 26:23"> And my righteousness shall answer for me on the morrow, for it is my reward before thee: whatever shall not be spotted and speckled among the goats, and gray among the rams, shall be stolen with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:7; Numbers 22:29; 1 Corinthians 14:5"> And Laban said to him, Let it be according to thy word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 31:9"> And he separated in that day the spotted and speckled he-goats, and all the spotted and speckled she-goats, and all that was gray among the rams, and every one that was white among them, and he gave them into the hand of his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And he set a distance of a three days’ journey between them and Jacob. And Jacob tended the cattle of Laban that were left behind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 31:9–13; Ezekiel 31:8"> And Jacob took to himself green rods of storax tree and walnut and plane tree; and Jacob peeled in them white stripes; and as he drew off the green, the white stripe which he had made appeared alternate on the rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And he laid the rods which he had peeled, in the hollows of the watering troughs, that whensoever the cattle should come to drink, as they should have come to drink before the rods, the cattle might conceive at the rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 12:35–36; Genesis 31:9–12; Genesis 31:40"> So the cattle conceived at the rods, and the cattle brought forth young speckled, and streaked and spotted with ash-colored spots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And Jacob separated the lambs, and set before the sheep a speckled ram, and every variegated one among the lambs, and he separated flocks for himself alone, and did not mingle them with the sheep of Laban.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And it came to pass in the time wherein the cattle became pregnant, conceiving in the belly, Jacob put the rods before the cattle in the troughs, that they might conceive by the rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> But he did not put them in indiscriminately whenever the cattle happened to bring forth, but the unmarked ones were Laban’s, and the marked ones Jacob’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Genesis 30:30; Genesis 26:13–14"> And the man became very rich, and he had many cattle, and oxen, and servants, and maidservants, and camels, and asses.</span>
