<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 46:4; Deuteronomy 6:7–8; Acts 8:2"> And Joseph fell upon his father’s face, and wept on him, and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 50:26; Chronicles II 16:14; John 19:39–40"> And Joseph commanded his servants the embalmers to embalm his father; and the embalmers embalmed Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 20:29; Deuteronomy 34:8; Genesis 50:10"> And they fulfilled forty days for him, for so are the days of embalming numbered; and Egypt mourned for him seventy days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 18:3; Esther 4:2"> And when the days of mourning were past, Joseph spoke to the princes of Pharaoh, saying, If I have found favor in your sight, speak concerning me in the ears of Pharaoh, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles II 16:14; Isaiah 22:16; Matthew 27:60"> My father adjured me, saying, In the sepulcher which I dug for myself in the land of Canaan, there thou shalt bury me; now then I will go up and bury my father, and return again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 48:21"> And Pharaoh said to Joseph, Go up, bury thy father, as he constrained thee to swear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 14:16"> So Joseph went up to bury his father; and all the servants of Pharaoh went up with him, and the elders of his house, and all the elders of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 32:24–27; Exodus 10:26; Exodus 10:8–9"> And all the household of Joseph, and his brethren, and all the house of his father, and his kindred; and they left behind the sheep and the oxen in the land of Goshen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; Kings IV 18:24; Song of Solomon 1:9"> And there went up with him also chariots and horsemen; and there was a very great company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 2:13; Acts 8:2; Kings II 1:17"> And they came to the threshing floor of Atad, which is beyond Jordan; and they bewailed him with a great and very sore lamentation; and he made a mourning for his father seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 10:15–19; Genesis 24:6; Deuteronomy 11:30"> And the inhabitants of the land of Canaan saw the mourning at the floor of Atad, and said, This is a great mourning to the Egyptians; therefore he called its name, The mourning of Egypt, which is beyond Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 7:16; Genesis 49:29–32; Ephesians 6:1"> And thus his sons did to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 49:29–31; Genesis 23:16–18; Acts 7:16"> So his sons carried him up into the land of Canaan, and buried him in the double cave, which cave Abraham bought for possession of a burying place, of Ephron the Hittite, before Mamre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Joseph returned to Egypt, he and his brethren, and those that had gone up with him to bury his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Genesis 42:21–22; Psalms 53:5"> And when the brethren of Joseph saw that their father was dead, they said, Let us take heed, lest at any time Joseph remember evil against us, and recompense to us all the evils which we have done against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25"> And they came to Joseph, and said, Thy father adjured us before his death, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Matthew 6:14–15; Genesis 45:8"> Thus say ye to Joseph, Forgive them their injustice and their sin, forasmuch as they have done thee evil; and now pardon the injustice of the servants of the God of thy father. And Joseph wept while they spoke to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 37:7–11; Genesis 42:6; Genesis 45:3"> And they came to him and said, We, these persons, are thy servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Genesis 45:5; Genesis 30:2"> And Joseph said to them, Fear not, for I am God’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Psalms 119:71; Genesis 45:5–8"> Ye took counsel against me for evil, but God took counsel for me for good, that the matter might be as it is today, and much people might be fed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 47:12; 1 Peter 3:9; Genesis 45:10–11"> And he said to them, Fear not, I will maintain you, and your families: and he comforted them, and spoke kindly to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Joseph dwelt in Egypt, he and his brethren, and all the family of his father; and Joseph lived a hundred and ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 32:39; Genesis 30:3; Job 42:16"> And Joseph saw the children of Ephraim to the third generation; and the sons of Machir the son of Manasseh were borne on the sides of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 3:16–17; Genesis 35:12; Genesis 26:3"> And Joseph spoke to his brethren, saying, I die, and God will surely visit you, and will bring you out of this land to the land concerning which God swore to our fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 13:19; Joshua 24:32; Genesis 47:29–31"> And Joseph adjured the sons of Israel, saying, At the visitation with which God shall visit you, then ye shall carry up my bones hence with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 50:2–3; Genesis 47:9; Joshua 24:29"> And Joseph died, aged a hundred and ten years; and they prepared his corpse, and put him in a coffin in Egypt.</span>
