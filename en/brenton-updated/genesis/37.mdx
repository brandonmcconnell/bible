<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 23:4; Genesis 36:7"> And Jacob dwelt in the land where his father sojourned, in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 35:25–26; Genesis 35:22; Kings I 2:22–24"> And these are the generations of Jacob. And Joseph was seventeen years old, feeding the sheep of his father with his brethren, being young; with the sons of Bilhah, and with the sons of Zilpah, the wives of his father; and Joseph brought to Israel their father their evil reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 3:35; Genesis 37:32; Kings II 13:18"> And Jacob loved Joseph more than all his sons, because he was to him the son of old age; and he made for him a coat of many colors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 27:41; 1 John 3:12; 1 John 4:20"> And his brethren having seen that his father loved him more than all his sons, hated him, and could not speak anything peaceable to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Genesis 42:9; John 17:14"> And Joseph dreamed a dream, and reported it to his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 44:18; Judges 9:7"> And he said to them, Hear this dream which I have dreamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 44:14; Genesis 42:6; Genesis 43:26"> I thought ye were binding sheaves in the middle of the field, and my sheaf stood up and was erected, and your sheaves turned round, and did obeisance to my sheaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings I 10:27; Acts 7:35; Hebrews 10:29"> And his brethren said to him, Shalt thou indeed reign over us, or shalt thou indeed be lord over us? And they hated him still more for his dreams and for his words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:9–14; Genesis 37:7; Daniel 8:10"> And he dreamed another dream, and related it to his father, and to his brethren, and said, Behold, I have dreamed another dream: as it were the sun, and the moon, and the eleven stars did me reverence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 27:29; Philippians 2:10–11; Isaiah 60:14"> And his father rebuked him, and said to him, What is this dream which thou hast dreamed? shall indeed both I and thy mother and thy brethren come and bow before thee to the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 7:9; Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51"> And his brethren envied him; but his father observed the saying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 37:1; Genesis 33:18; Genesis 34:25–31"> And his brethren went to feed the sheep of their father to Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; Kings I 3:8; Kings I 3:16"> And Israel said to Joseph, Do not thy brethren feed their flock in Shechem? Come, I will send thee to them; and he said to him, Behold, I am here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 35:27; Luke 19:42; Psalms 125:5"> And Israel said to him, Go and see if thy brethren and the sheep are well, and bring me word; and he sent him out of the Valley of Hebron, and he came to Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:27; Judges 4:22; Genesis 21:14"> And a man found him wandering in the field; and the man asked him, saying, What seekest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Song of Solomon 1:7"> And he said, I am seeking my brethren; tell me where they feed their flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings IV 6:13"> And the man said to him, They have departed hence, for I heard them saying, Let us go to Dothan; and Joseph went after his brethren, and found them in Dothan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 23:12; Psalms 37:12; Psalms 37:32"> And they spied him from a distance before he drew nigh to them, and they wickedly took counsel to slay him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Genesis 37:11; Genesis 37:5"> And each said to his brother, Behold, that dreamer comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Proverbs 27:4; Matthew 2:2–16"> Now then come, let us kill him, and cast him into one of the pits; and we will say, An evil wild beast has devoured him; and we shall see what his dreams will be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 42:22; Genesis 35:22; Joshua 10:28"> And Reuben having heard it, rescued him out of their hands, and said, Let us not kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 22:12; Deuteronomy 13:9; Genesis 42:22"> And Reuben said to them, Shed not blood; cast him into one of these pits in the wilderness, but do not lay your hand upon him; that he might rescue him out of their hands, and restore him to his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:3; Matthew 27:28; Genesis 42:21"> And it came to pass, when Joseph came to his brethren, that they stripped Joseph of his many-colored coat that was upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:6; Psalms 130:1–2; Zechariah 9:11"> And they took him and cast him into the pit; and the pit was empty, it had not water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Genesis 43:11; Jeremiah 8:22"> And they sat down to eat bread; and having lifted up their eyes they beheld, and lo, Ishmaelitish travelers came from Gilead, and their camels were heavily loaded with spices, and resin, and myrrh; and they went to bring them to Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Genesis 4:10; Genesis 25:32"> And Judah said to his brethren, What profit is it if we slay our brother, and conceal his blood?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Genesis 42:21; Kings I 18:17"> Come, let us sell him to these Ishmaelites, but let not our hands be upon him, because he is our brother and our flesh; and his brethren hearkened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 7:9; Psalms 105:17; Genesis 45:4–5"> And the men, the merchants of Midian, went by, and they drew and lifted Joseph out of the pit, and sold Joseph to the Ishmaelites for twenty pieces of gold; and they brought Joseph down into Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 1:20; Genesis 37:34; Genesis 44:13"> And Reuben returned to the pit, and sees not Joseph in the pit; and he rent his garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 42:13; Jeremiah 31:15; Genesis 42:32"> And he returned to his brethren and said, The boy is not; and I, whither am I yet to go?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 37:23; Proverbs 28:13; Genesis 37:3"> And having taken the coat of Joseph, they slew a kid of the goats, and stained the coat with the blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 15:30; Genesis 37:3; Genesis 44:20–23"> And they sent the coat of many colors; and they brought it to their father, and said, This have we found; know if it be thy son’s coat or no.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Genesis 44:28; John 13:7"> And he recognized it, and said, It is my son’s coat, an evil wild beast has devoured him; a wild beast has carried off Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 37:29; Kings II 3:31; Psalms 69:11"> And Jacob rent his clothes, and put sackcloth on his loins, and mourned for his son many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 42:38; Job 2:11; Kings II 12:17"> And all his sons and his daughters gathered themselves together, and came to comfort him; but he would not be comforted, saying, I will go down to my son mourning to Hades; and his father wept for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 40:4; Genesis 37:28; Kings IV 25:8"> And the Midianites sold Joseph into Egypt; to Potiphar, the eunuch of Pharaoh, captain of the guard.</span>
</div>
