<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:14; Proverbs 29:21; Ecclesiastes 2:7"> And Abram said, I am grieved since thou hast given me no seed, but my homeborn servant shall succeed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 17:16; Galatians 4:28; Kings II 7:12"> And immediately there was a voice of the Lord to him, saying, This shall not be thine heir; but he that shall come out of thee shall be thine heir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:12; Romans 4:18; Deuteronomy 1:10"> And he brought him out and said to him, Look up now to heaven, and count the stars, if thou shalt be able to number them fully, and he said, Thus shall thy seed be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 2:23; Galatians 3:6–14; Romans 4:9"> And Abram believed God, and it was counted to him for righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 12:1; Acts 7:2–4; Romans 4:13"> And he said to him, I am God that brought thee out of the land of the Chaldeans, so as to give thee this land to inherit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 1:18; Luke 1:34; Isaiah 7:11"> And he said, Master and Lord, how shall I know that I shall inherit it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 2:24; Leviticus 14:30; Leviticus 14:22"> And he said to him, Take for me a heifer in her third year, and a she-goat in her third year, and a ram in his third year, and a dove and a pigeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:18–19; Leviticus 1:17; Genesis 15:17"> So he took to him all these, and divided them in the midst, and set them opposite to each other, but the birds he did not divide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:4; Psalms 119:13; Ezekiel 17:3"> And birds came down upon the bodies, even upon the divided parts of them, and Abram sat down by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 2:21; Job 33:15; Daniel 10:8–9"> And about sunset a trance fell upon Abram, and lo! a great gloomy terror falls upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:6–7; Exodus 1:11; Exodus 12:40–41"> And it was said to Abram, Thou shalt surely know that thy seed shall be a sojourner in a land not their own, and they shall enslave them, and afflict them, and humble them four hundred years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:9–11; Exodus 6:5–6; Exodus 12:32–38"> And the nation whomsoever they shall serve I will judge; and after this, they shall come forth hither with much property.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 5:26; Genesis 25:7–9; Chronicles II 34:28"> But thou shalt depart to thy fathers in peace, nourished in a good old age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 21:26; Daniel 8:23; Exodus 12:40"> And in the fourth generation they shall return hither, for the sins of the Amorites are not yet filled up, even until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:18–19; Judges 6:21; Jeremiah 11:4"> And when the sun was about to set, there was a flame, and behold a smoking furnace and lamps of fire, which passed between these divided pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 1:3–4; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 24:7"> In that day the Lord made a covenant with Abram, saying, To thy seed I will give this land, from the river of Egypt to the great river Euphrates.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 17:5; Genesis 14:5"> and the Hittites, and the Perizzites, and the Rephaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 10:15–19; Exodus 23:23–28; Deuteronomy 7:1"> and the Amorites, and the Canaanites, and the Hivites, and the Girgashites, and the Jebusites.</span>
