<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 12:6; Genesis 18:10; Genesis 18:14"> And the Lord visited Sarah, as he said, and the Lord did to Sarah, as he spoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 4:22; Hebrews 11:11; Genesis 18:10"> And she conceived and bore to Abraham a son in old age, at the set time according as the Lord spoke to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 17:19; Romans 9:7; Genesis 21:6"> And Abraham called the name of his son that was born to him, whom Sarah bore to him, Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:8; Deuteronomy 12:32; John 7:22–23"> And Abraham circumcised Isaac on the eighth day, as God commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; Romans 4:19; Genesis 17:1"> And Abraham was a hundred years old when Isaac his son was born to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 126:2; Isaiah 54:1; Genesis 17:17"> And Sarah said, The Lord has made laughter for me, for whoever shall hear shall rejoice with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 18:11–12; Isaiah 66:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:10"> And she said, Who shall say to Abraham that Sarah suckles a child? for I have born a child in my old age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings I 1:22; Hosea 1:8; Genesis 40:20"> And the child grew and was weaned, and Abraham made a great feast the day that his son Isaac was weaned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 4:29; Genesis 16:15; Galatians 4:22"> And Sarah having seen the son of Hagar the Egyptian who was born to Abraham, sporting with Isaac her son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:35; 1 John 2:19; Galatians 4:22–31"> then she said to Abraham, Cast out this bondwoman and her son, for the son of this bondwoman shall not inherit with my son Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 12:11; Genesis 17:18; Matthew 10:37"> But the word appeared very hard before Abraham concerning his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:18; Romans 9:7–8; Genesis 17:19"> But God said to Abraham, Let it not be hard before thee concerning the child, and concerning the bondwoman; in all things whatsoever Sarah shall say to thee, hear her voice, for in Isaac shall thy seed be called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 16:10; Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:18"> And moreover I will make the son of this bondwoman a great nation, because he is thy seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Psalms 119:60; Genesis 16:7"> And Abraham rose up in the morning and took loaves and a skin of water, and gave them to Hagar, and he put the child on her shoulder, and sent her away, and she having departed wandered in the wilderness near the well of the oath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings IV 3:9; Psalms 63:1; Jeremiah 14:3"> And the water failed out of the skin, and she cast the child under a fir tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Genesis 29:11; Kings I 24:16"> And she departed and sat down opposite him at a distance, as it were a bowshot, for she said, Surely I cannot see the death of my child: and she sat opposite him, and the child cried aloud and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 16:11; Psalms 50:15; Exodus 3:7"> And God heard the voice of the child from the place where he was, and an angel of God called Hagar out of heaven, and said to her, What is it, Hagar? fear not, for God has heard the voice of the child from the place where he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 21:13; Genesis 16:10; Genesis 17:20"> Rise up, and take the child, and hold him in thy hand, for I will make him a great nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 22:31; Kings IV 6:17–20; Isaiah 35:5–6"> And God opened her eyes, and she saw a well of springing water; and she went and filled the skin with water, and gave the child drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Genesis 39:21; Genesis 16:12"> And God was with the child, and he grew and dwelt in the wilderness, and became an archer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 24:3–4; Numbers 12:16"> And he dwelt in the wilderness, and his mother took him a wife out of Paran of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 3:7; Genesis 26:26; Genesis 26:28"> And it came to pass at that time that Abimelech spoke, and Ahuzzath his friend, and Phicol the chief captain of his host, to Abraham, saying, God is with thee in all things, whatsoever thou mayest do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Genesis 20:14; Hebrews 6:16"> Now therefore swear to me by God that thou wilt not injure me, nor my seed, nor my name, but according to the righteousness which I have performed with thee thou shalt deal with me, and with the land in which thou hast sojourned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 6:16; Genesis 14:13; Romans 12:18"> And Abraham said, I will swear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 26:15–22; Genesis 29:8; Proverbs 25:9"> And Abraham reproved Abimelech because of the wells of water, which the servants of Abimelech took away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 13:7; Kings IV 5:20–24"> And Abimelech said to him, I know not who has done this thing to thee, neither didst thou tell it me, neither heard I it but only today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 14:22–23; Isaiah 32:8; Romans 1:31"> And Abraham took sheep and calves, and gave them to Abimelech, and both made a covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Abraham set seven ewe lambs by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings I 15:14; Genesis 33:8; Exodus 12:26"> And Abimelech said to Abraham, What are these seven ewe lambs which thou hast set alone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 31:52; Joshua 24:27; Joshua 22:27–28"> And Abraham said, Thou shalt receive the seven ewe lambs of me, that they may be for me as a witness, that I dug this well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 26:33; Genesis 21:14; Kings III 4:25"> Therefore he named the name of that place, The Well of the Oath, for there they both swore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 13:17; Genesis 21:27; Judges 13:1"> And they made a covenant at the well of the oath. And there rose up Abimelech, Ahuzzath his friend, and Phicol the commander-in-chief of his army, and they returned to the land of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Isaiah 40:28; Psalms 90:2"> And Abraham planted a field at the well of the oath, and called there on the name of the Lord, the everlasting God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Chronicles I 29:15; 1 Peter 2:11; Hebrews 11:9"> And Abraham sojourned in the land of the Philistines many days.</span>
