<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ruth 4:21; Ruth 3:12; Ruth 3:2"> And Naomi had a friend an acquaintance of her husband, and the man was a mighty man of the kindred of Elimelech, and his name was Boaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:22; Leviticus 19:9; Deuteronomy 24:19–21"> And Ruth the Moabitess said to Naomi, Let me go now to the field, and I will glean among the ears behind the man with whomsoever I shall find favor: and she said to her, Go, daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:12; Matthew 10:29; Luke 10:31"> And she went; and came and gleaned in the field behind the reapers; and she happened by chance to come on a portion of the land of Boaz, of the kindred of Elimelech.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 129:7–8; Judges 6:12; Luke 1:28"> And, behold, Boaz came from Bethlehem, and said to the reapers, The Lord be with you: and they said to him, The Lord bless thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ruth 4:21; Chronicles I 2:11–12"> And Boaz said to his servant who was set over the reapers, Whose is this damsel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ruth 1:22; Matthew 24:45; Genesis 24:2"> And his servant who was set over the reapers answered and said, It is the Moabitish damsel who returned with Naomi out of the land of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 18:23; Proverbs 15:33"> And she said, I pray you, let me glean and gather among the sheaves after the reapers: and she came and stood from morning till evening, and rested not even a little in the field.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings IV 5:13; Song of Solomon 1:7–8; Kings I 3:6"> And Boaz said to Ruth, Hast thou not heard, my daughter? go not to glean in another field; and depart not thou hence, join thyself here with my damsels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 5:18; Genesis 20:6; Proverbs 6:29"> Let thine eyes be on the field where my men shall reap, and thou shalt go after them: behold, I have charged the young men not to touch thee: and when thou shalt thirst, then thou shalt go to the vessels, and drink of that which the young men shall have drawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings I 25:23; Ruth 2:13; Luke 1:43"> And she fell upon her face, and did reverence to the ground, and said to him, How is it that I have found grace in thine eyes, that thou shouldest take notice of me, whereas I am a stranger?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 14:33; Luke 18:29–30; Luke 5:11"> And Boaz answered and said to her, It has fully been told me how thou hast dealt with thy mother-in-law after the death of thy husband; and how thou didst leave thy father and thy mother, and the land of thy birth, and camest to a people whom thou knewest not before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 36:7; Psalms 91:4; Psalms 63:7"> The Lord recompense thy work: may a full reward be given thee of the Lord God of Israel, to whom thou hast come to trust under his wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings I 1:18; Genesis 34:3; Genesis 33:8"> And she said, Let me find grace in thy sight, my lord, because thou hast comforted me, and because thou hast spoken kindly to thy handmaid, and behold, I shall be as one of thy servants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ruth 2:18; Kings IV 4:43–44; Matthew 14:20"> And Boaz said to her, Now it is time to eat; come hither, and thou shalt eat of the bread, and thou shalt dip thy morsel in the vinegar: and Ruth sat by the side of the reapers, and Boaz handed her meal, and she ate, and was satisfied, and left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 1:5"> And she rose up to glean; and Boaz charged his young men, saying, Let her even glean among the sheaves, and reproach her not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:13; 1 John 3:17–18; Deuteronomy 24:19–21"> And do ye by all means carry it for her, and ye shall surely let fall for her some of that which is heaped up; and let her eat, and glean, and rebuke her not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:11–12; Proverbs 31:27; Exodus 16:36"> So she gleaned in the field till evening, and beat out that she had gleaned, and it was about an ephah of barley.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 2:14; John 6:12–13; 1 Timothy 5:4"> And she took it up, and went into the city: and her mother-in-law saw what she had gleaned, and Ruth brought forth and gave to her the food which she had left from what she had been satisfied with.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ruth 2:10; Psalms 41:1; Kings III 7:21"> And her mother-in-law said to her, Where hast thou gleaned today, and where hast thou wrought? blessed be he that took notice of thee. And Ruth told her mother-in-law where she had wrought, and said, The name of the man with whom I wrought today is Boaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings II 2:5; Ruth 3:9–10; Leviticus 25:25"> And Naomi said to her daughter-in-law, Blessed is he of the Lord, because he has not failed in his mercy with the living and with the dead: and Naomi said to her, The man is near akin to us, he is one of our relations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:7–8; Ruth 2:7–8; Ruth 2:22"> And Ruth said to her mother-in-law, Yea, he said also to me, Keep close to my damsels, until the men shall have finished all my reaping.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:8; Proverbs 27:10"> And Naomi said to Ruth her daughter-in-law, It is well, daughter, that thou wentest out with his damsels; thus they shall not meet thee in another field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–8; Deuteronomy 16:9; Ephesians 6:1–3"> And Ruth joined herself to the damsels of Boaz to glean until they had finished the barley harvest and the wheat harvest.</span>
</div>
