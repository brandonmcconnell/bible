<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The book of the words of Tobit, the son of Tobiel, the son of Hananiel, the son of Aduel, the son of Gabael, of the seed of Asiel, of the tribe of Naphtali;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> who in the time of Shalmaneser king of the Assyrians was led captive out of Thisbe, which is at the right hand of that city, which is called properly Naphtali in Galilee above Asher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> I Tobit have walked all the days of my life in the way of truth and justice, and I did many almsdeeds to my brethren, and my nation, who came with me to Nineveh, into the land of the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And when I was in mine own country, in the land of Israel, being but young, all the tribe of Naphtali my father fell from the house of Jerusalem, which was chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, that all the tribes should sacrifice there, and, where the temple of the habitation of the most High was consecrated and built for all ages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Now all the tribes which together revolted, and the house of my father Naphtali, sacrificed unto the heifer Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But I alone went often to Jerusalem at the feasts, as it was ordained unto all the people of Israel by an everlasting decree, having the firstfruits and tenths of increase, with that which was first shorn; and them gave I at the altar to the priests the children of Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The first tenth part of all increase I gave to the sons of Aaron, who ministered at Jerusalem: another tenth part I sold away, and went, and spent it every year at Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and the third I gave unto them to whom it was meet, as Deborah my father’s mother had commanded me, because I was left an orphan by my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Furthermore, when I was come to the age of a man, I married Anna of mine own kindred, and of her I begot Tobias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And when we were carried away captives to Nineveh, all my brethren and those that were of my kindred did eat of the bread of the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> But I kept myself from eating;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> because I remembered God with all my heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the most High gave me grace and favor before Shalmaneser, so that I was his purveyor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And I went into Media, and left in trust with Gabael, the brother of Gabri, at Rages a city of Media, ten talents of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Now when Shalmaneser was dead, Sennacherib his son reigned in his stead; whose estate was troubled, that I could not go into Media.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And in the time of Shalmaneser I gave many alms to my brethren, and gave my bread to the hungry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and my clothes to the naked: and if I saw any of my nation dead, or cast about the walls of Nineveh, I buried him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And if the King Sennacherib had slain any, when he was come, and fled from Judea, I buried them privily; for in his wrath he killed many; but the bodies were not found, when they were sought for of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And when one of the Ninevites went and complained of me to the king, that I buried them, and hid myself; understanding that I was sought for to be put to death, I withdrew myself for fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then all my goods were forcibly taken away, neither was there anything left me, beside my wife Anna and my son Tobias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And there passed not fifty days, before two of his sons killed him, and they fled into the mountains of Ararat; and Esar-haddon his son reigned in his stead; who appointed over his father’s accounts, and over all his affairs, Ahikar my brother Hanael’s son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Ahikar entreating for me, I returned to Nineveh. Now Ahikar was cupbearer, and keeper of the signet, and steward, and overseer of the accounts: and Esar-haddon appointed him next unto him: and he was my brother’s son.</span>
