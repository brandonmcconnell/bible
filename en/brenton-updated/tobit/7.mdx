<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And he came to the house of Raguel; and Sarah met him: and saluted him, and he them; and she brought them into the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Then said Raguel to Edna his wife, How like is this young man to Tobit my cousin!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And Raguel asked them, From whence are ye, brethren? To whom they said, We are of the sons of Naphtali, which are captives in Nineveh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then he said to them, Do ye know Tobit our kinsman? And they said, We know him. Then said he, Is he in good health?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And they said, He is both alive, and in good health: and Tobias said, He is my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then Raguel leaped up, and kissed him, and wept,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and blessed him, and said unto him, Thou art the son of an honest and good man. But when he had heard that Tobit was blind, he was sorrowful, and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And likewise Edna his wife and Sarah his daughter wept. Moreover they entertained them cheerfully; and after that they had killed a ram of the flock, they set store of meat on the table. Then said Tobias to Raphael, Brother Azariah, speak of those things of which thou didst talk in the way, and let this business be dispatched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> So he communicated the matter with Raguel: and Raguel said to Tobias, Eat and drink, and make merry:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> for it is meet that thou shouldest marry my daughter: nevertheless I will declare unto thee the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> I have given my daughter in marriage to seven men, who died that night they came in unto her: nevertheless for the present be merry. But Tobias said, I will eat nothing here, till we agree and swear one to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And Raguel said, Then take her from henceforth according to the manner, for thou art her cousin, and she is thine, and the merciful God give you good success in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then he called his daughter Sarah, and she came to her father, and he took her by the hand, and gave her to be wife to Tobias, saying, Behold, take her after the law of Moses, and lead her away to thy father. And he blessed them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and called Edna his wife, and took paper, and did write an instrument of covenants, and sealed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then they began to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> After Raguel called his wife Edna, and said unto her, Sister, prepare another chamber, and bring her in thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Which when she had done as he had bidden her, she brought her thither: and she wept, and she received the tears of her daughter, and said unto her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Be of good comfort, my daughter; the Lord of heaven and earth give thee joy for this thy sorrow: be of good comfort, my daughter.</span>
