<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 1:37; Jeremiah 32:27; Genesis 18:14"> So I took the cup out of the Lord’s hand, and caused the nations to whom the Lord sent me to drink:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Deuteronomy 5:9–10; Exodus 34:7"> Jerusalem, and the cities of Judah, and the kings of Judah, and his princes, to make them a desert place, a desolation, and a hissing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Matthew 16:27; Psalms 62:12"> and Pharaoh king of Egypt, and his servants, and his nobles, and all his people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:10; Kings II 7:23; Exodus 10:2"> and all the mingled people, and all the kings of the Philistines, and Ashkelon, and Gaza, and Ekron, and the remnant of Ashdod,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 26:8; Psalms 105:43"> and Edom, and the land of Moab, and the children of Ammon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Exodus 13:5; Jeremiah 11:5"> and the kings of Tyre, and the kings of Sidon, and the kings in the country beyond the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 9:7; Psalms 78:54–55; Daniel 9:10–14"> and Dedan, and Tema, and Buz, and everyone that is shaved round about the face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:36; Deuteronomy 4:26; Joshua 23:15–16"> and all the mingled people lodging in the wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:24; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 77:19"> and all the kings of Elam, and all the kings of the Persians,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and all the kings from the north, the far and the near, each one with his brother, and all the kingdoms which are on the face of the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Jeremiah 32:17; Isaiah 64:8"> And thou shalt say to them, Thus said the Lord Almighty; Drink ye, be ye drunken; and ye shall vomit, and shall fall, and shall in nowise rise, because of the sword which I send among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:3; Jeremiah 32:24; Jeremiah 19:7–12"> And it shall come to pass, when they refuse to take the cup out of thy hand, to drink it, that thou shalt say, Thus said the Lord; Ye shall surely drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 19:13; Jeremiah 52:13"> For I am beginning to afflict the city whereon my name is called, and ye shall by no means be held guiltless: for I am calling a sword upon all that dwell upon the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:22–26; Deuteronomy 9:7–12; Jeremiah 25:7"> And thou shalt prophesy against them these words, and shalt say, The Lord shall speak from on high, from his sanctuary he will utter his voice; he will pronounce a declaration on his place; and these shall answer like men gathering grapes: and destruction is coming on them that dwell on the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings IV 23:27; Jeremiah 27:10; Kings IV 21:4–7"> even upon the extreme part of the earth; for the Lord has a controversy with the nations, he is pleading with all flesh, and the ungodly are given to the sword, saith the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 9:8; Jeremiah 2:26; Isaiah 1:4–6"> Thus said the Lord; Behold, evils are proceeding from nation to nation, and a great whirlwind goes forth from the end of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:13; Jeremiah 2:27; Ezekiel 8:16"> And the slain of the Lord shall be in the day of the Lord from one end of the earth even to the other end of the earth: they shall not be buried; they shall be as dung on the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:30; Ezekiel 8:5–16; Kings IV 21:4–7"> Howl, ye shepherds, and cry; and lament, ye rams of the flock: for your days have been completed for slaughter, and ye shall fall as the choice rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:31; Chronicles II 33:6; Leviticus 18:21"> And flight shall perish from the shepherds, and safety from the rams of the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:24; Jeremiah 32:3; Jeremiah 32:28"> A voice of the crying of the shepherds, and a moaning of the sheep and the rams: for the Lord has destroyed their pastures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:3; Jeremiah 23:6; Ezekiel 34:12–14"> And the peaceable abodes that remain shall be destroyed before the fierceness of my anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Ezekiel 11:19–20; Hebrews 8:10"> He has forsaken his lair, as a lion: for their land is become desolate before the great sword.</span>
