<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="brenton-updated" data-translation-id="eng_boy" data-translation-label="Updated Brenton" data-book="JER" data-book-slug="jeremiah" data-book-name="Jeremiah" data-chapter="27" data-verses="46">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:3; Jeremiah 28:1; Jeremiah 27:12"> The word of the Lord which he spoke against Babylon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 7:1; Amos 7:4; Jeremiah 27:12"> Proclaim ye among the Gentiles, and cause the tidings to be heard, and suppress them not: say ye, Babylon is taken, Bel is confounded; the fearless, the luxurious Merodach is delivered up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 1:9–3; Ezekiel 25:1–17; Ezekiel 17:15–21"> For a nation has come up against her from the north, he shall utterly ravage her land, and there shall be none to dwell in it, neither man nor beast.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:19; Jeremiah 10:10; Jeremiah 25:27"> In those days, and at that time, the children of Israel shall come, they and the children of Judah together; they shall proceed, weeping as they go, seeking the Lord their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Jeremiah 51:15; Psalms 115:15–16"> They shall ask the way till they come to Zion, for that way shall they set their face; and they shall come and flee for refuge to the Lord their God; for the everlasting covenant shall not be forgotten.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:14; Jeremiah 25:9; Daniel 2:37–38"> My people have been lost sheep: their shepherds thrust them out, they caused them to wander on the mountains: they went from mountain to hill, they forgot their resting place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:31; Revelation 13:5–10; Daniel 5:25–31"> All that found them consumed them: their enemies said, Let us not leave them alone, because they have sinned against the Lord: he that gathered their fathers had a pasture of righteousness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:21; Ezekiel 17:19–21; Jeremiah 38:17–19"> Flee ye out of the midst of Babylon, and from the land of the Chaldeans, and go forth, and be as serpents before sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14; Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 29:8"> For, behold, I stir up against Babylon the gatherings of nations out of the land of the north; and they shall set themselves in array against her: thence shall she be taken, as the dart of an expert warrior shall not return empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:31; Jeremiah 28:16; Jeremiah 23:25"> And Chaldea shall be a spoil: all that spoil her shall be satisfied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:8; Jeremiah 27:2; Jeremiah 38:2"> Because ye rejoiced, and boasted, while plundering my heritage; because ye exulted as calves in the grass, and pushed with the horn as bulls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:1; Jeremiah 38:17; Chronicles II 36:11–13"> Your mother is greatly ashamed; your mother that bore you for prosperity is confounded: she is the last of the nations, desolate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:31; Jeremiah 27:8; Proverbs 8:36"> by reason of the Lord’s anger: it shall not be inhabited, but it shall be all a desolation; and everyone that passes through Babylon shall scowl, and they shall hiss at all her plague.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; Jeremiah 29:8–9"> Set yourselves in array against Babylon round about, all ye that bend the bow; shoot at her, spare not your arrows,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:10; Chronicles II 25:16; Jeremiah 6:13–15"> and prevail against her: her hands are weakened, her bulwarks are fallen, and her wall is broken down: for it is vengeance from God: take vengeance upon her; as she has done, do to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings IV 24:13; Jeremiah 28:3; Daniel 1:2"> Utterly destroy seed out of Babylon, and him that holds a sickle in time of harvest: for fear of the Grecian sword, they shall return every one to his people, and every one shall flee to his own land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:17; Jeremiah 38:23; Jeremiah 7:34"> Israel is a wandering sheep; the lions have driven him out: the king of Assyria first devoured him, and afterward this king of Babylon has gnawed his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings I 7:8; Jeremiah 18:20; Kings I 12:19"> Therefore thus saith the Lord; Behold, I will take vengeance on the king of Babylon, and upon his land, as I took vengeance on the king of Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings IV 25:13; Jeremiah 52:17–23; Kings IV 25:17"> And I will restore Israel to his pasture, and he shall feed on Carmel and on Mount Ephraim and in Gilead, and his soul shall be satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:1; Chronicles II 36:10; Kings IV 24:14–16"> In those days, and at that time, they shall seek for the iniquity of Israel, and there shall be none; and for the sins of Judah, and they shall not be found: for I will be merciful to them that are left</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> on the land, saith the Lord.<br />Go up against it roughly, and against them that dwell on it: avenge, O sword, and destroy utterly, saith the Lord, and do according to all that I command thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:19; Jeremiah 32:5"> A sound of war, and great destruction in the land of the Chaldeans!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> How is the hammer of the whole earth broken and crushed! How is Babylon become a desolation among the nations!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> They shall come upon thee, and thou shalt not know it, Babylon, that thou wilt even be taken captive: thou art found and taken, because thou didst resist the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The Lord has opened his treasury, and brought forth the weapons of his anger: for the Lord God has a work in the land of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> For her times are come: open ye her storehouses: search her as a cave, and utterly destroy her: let there be no remnant of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Dry ye up all her fruits, and let them go down to the slaughter: woe to them! for their day is come, and the time of their retribution.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> A voice of men fleeing and escaping from the land of Babylon, to declare to Zion the vengeance that comes from the Lord our God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Summon many against Babylon, even everyone that bends the bow: camp against her round about; let no one of her people escape: render to her according to her works; according to all that she has done, do to her: for she has resisted the Lord, the Holy God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Therefore shall her young men fall in the streets, and all her warriors shall be cast down, saith the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Behold, I am against thee the haughty one, saith the Lord: for thy day is come, and the time of thy retribution.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And thy pride shall fail, and fall, and there shall be no one to set it up again: and I will kindle a fire in her forest, and it shall devour all things round about her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Thus saith the Lord; The children of Israel and the children of Judah have been oppressed: all they that have taken them captive have oppressed them together; for they would not let them go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> But their Redeemer is strong; the Lord Almighty is his name: he will enter into judgment with his adversaries, that he may destroy the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and he will sharpen a sword against the Chaldeans, and against the inhabitants of Babylon, and upon her nobles and upon her wise men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> a sword upon her warriors, and they shall be weakened: a sword upon their horses, and upon their chariots:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> a sword upon their warriors, and upon the mixed people in the midst of her; and they shall be as women: a sword upon the treasures, and they shall be scattered upon her water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and they shall be ashamed: for it is a land of graven images; and in the islands, where they boasted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Therefore shall idols dwell in the islands, and the young of monsters shall dwell in it: it shall not be inhabited anymore forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> As God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah, and the cities bordering upon them, saith the Lord: no man shall dwell there, and no son of man shall sojourn there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Behold, a people comes from the north, and a great nation, and many kings shall be stirred up from the end of the earth; holding bow and dagger:</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> the people is fierce, and will have no mercy: their voices shall sound as the sea, they shall ride upon horses, prepared for war, like fire, against thee, O daughter of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> The king of Babylon heard the sound of them, and his hands were enfeebled: anguish overcame him, pangs as of a woman in travail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Behold, he shall come up as a lion from Jordan to Gaethan; for I will speedily drive them from her, and I will set all the youths against her: for who is like me? and who will resist me? and who is this shepherd who will stand before me?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> Therefore hear ye the counsel of the Lord, which he has taken against Babylon; and his devices, which he has devised upon the Chaldeans inhabiting it: surely lambs of their flock shall be destroyed: surely pasture shall be cut off from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> For at the sound of the taking of Babylon the earth shall quake, and a cry shall be heard among the nations.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
