<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:2; Chronicles II 36:10; Kings IV 24:12–16"> The Lord showed me two baskets of figs, lying in front of the temple of the Lord, after Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had carried captive Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim king of Judah, and the princes, and the artificers, and the prisoners, and the rich men out of Jerusalem, and had brought them to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:7; Isaiah 5:4; Micah 7:1"> The one basket was full of very good figs, as the early figs; and the other basket was full of very bad figs, which could not be eaten, for their badness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 8:2; Zechariah 5:5–11; Matthew 25:32–33"> And the Lord said to me, What seest thou, Jeremiah? and I said, Figs; the good figs, very good; and the bad, very bad, which cannot be eaten, for their badness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Nahum 1:7; Galatians 4:9"> Thus saith the Lord, the God of Israel; As these good figs, so will I acknowledge the Jews that have been carried away captive, whom I have sent forth out of this place into the land of the Chaldeans for good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:10; Jeremiah 29:10; Jeremiah 33:7"> And I will fix mine eyes upon them for good, and I will restore them into this land for good: and I will build them up, and not pull them down; and I will plant them, and not pluck them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 8:10; Jeremiah 30:22; Ezekiel 36:24–28"> And I will give them a heart to know me, that I am the Lord: and they shall be to me a people, and I will be to them a God: for they shall turn to me with all their heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:1; Jeremiah 39:2–9; Jeremiah 24:2"> And as the bad figs, which cannot be eaten, for their badness; thus saith the Lord, So will I deliver Zedekiah king of Judah, and his nobles, and the remnant of Jerusalem, them that are left in this land, and the dwellers in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:4; Jeremiah 29:18; Deuteronomy 28:37"> And I will cause them to be dispersed into all the kingdoms of the earth, and they shall be for a reproach, and a proverb, and an object of hatred, and a curse, in every place whither I have driven them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 51:19; Ezekiel 5:12–17; Jeremiah 16:4"> And I will send against them famine, and pestilence, and the sword, until they are consumed from off the land which I gave them.</span>
