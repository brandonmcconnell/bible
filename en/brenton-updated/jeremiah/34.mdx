<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 34:22; Jeremiah 37:8–10"> Thus said the Lord; Make to thyself bonds and yokes, and put them about thy neck,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:4; Jeremiah 21:7; Kings IV 25:4–7"> and thou shalt send them to the king of Edom, and to the king of Moab, and to the king of the children of Ammon, and to the king of Tyre, and to the king of Sidon, by the hands of their messengers that come to meet them at Jerusalem to Zedekiah king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And thou shalt commission them to say to their lords, Thus said the Lord God of Israel; Thus shall ye say to your lords;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18; Chronicles II 16:14; Kings IV 22:20"> I have made the earth by my great power, and with my high arm, and I will give it to whomsoever it shall seem good in mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings I 15:16–24; Kings I 3:18; Kings III 21:19"> I gave the earth to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon to serve him, and the wild beasts of the field to labor for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Kings IV 23:2–3; Kings IV 11:17"> And the nation and kingdom, all that shall not put their neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon, with sword and famine will I visit them, saith the Lord, until they are consumed by his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12; Leviticus 25:39–46; Exodus 3:18"> And hearken ye not to your false prophets, nor to them that divine to you, nor to them that foretell events by dreams to you, nor to your auguries, nor your sorcerers, that say, Ye shall by no means work for the king of Babylon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:16; Jeremiah 26:10; Jeremiah 38:4"> for they prophesy lies to you, to remove you far from your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:20–22; Proverbs 26:11; Matthew 12:43–45"> But the nation which shall put its neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon, and serve him, I will even leave it upon its land, and it shall serve him, and dwell in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> I spoke also to Zedekiah king of Judah according to all these words, saying, Put your neck into the yoke, and serve the king of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12; Kings III 9:22; Kings IV 17:13–14"> For they prophesy unrighteous words to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:8; Kings IV 23:3; Nehemiah 10:29"> for I sent them not, saith the Lord; and they prophesy in my name unjustly, that I might destroy you, and ye should perish, and your prophets, who unrighteously prophesy lies to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:12; Exodus 20:7; Jeremiah 34:11"> I spoke to you, and to all this people, and to the priests, saying, Thus said the Lord; Hearken not to the words of the prophets that prophesy to you, saying, Behold, the vessels of the Lord’s house shall return from Babylon: for they prophesy to you unrighteous words. I sent them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 15:10; Genesis 15:17–18; Hosea 6:7"> If they are prophets, and if the word of the Lord is in them, let them meet me, for thus has the Lord said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:10; Zephaniah 3:3–4; Daniel 9:6"> And as for the remaining vessels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Jeremiah 16:4; Jeremiah 19:7"> which the king of Babylon took not, when he carried Jechoniah prisoner out of Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 39:8; Jeremiah 39:1–2"> they shall go into Babylon, saith the Lord.</span>
