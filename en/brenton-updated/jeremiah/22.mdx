<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:2; Luke 3:19–20; Kings II 24:11–12"> Thus saith the Lord; Go thou, and go down to the house of the king of Judah, and thou shalt speak there this word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 1:32; Isaiah 9:7; Jeremiah 22:4"> and thou shalt say, Hear the word of the Lord, O king of Judah, that sittest on the throne of David, thou, and thy house, and thy people, and they that go in at these gates:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:12; Micah 6:8; Jeremiah 22:17"> thus saith the Lord; Execute ye judgment and justice, and rescue the spoiled out of the hand of him that wrongs him: and oppress not the stranger, and orphan, and widow, and sin not, and shed no innocent blood in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:25"> For if ye will indeed perform this word, then shall there enter in by the gates of this house kings sitting upon the throne of David, and riding on chariots and horses, they, and their servants, and their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 22:16"> But if ye will not perform these words, by myself have I sworn, saith the Lord, that this house shall be brought to desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Isaiah 6:11; Isaiah 27:10"> For thus saith the Lord concerning the house of the king of Judah; Thou art Gilead to me, and the head of Lebanon: yet surely I will make thee a desert, even cities that shall not be inhabited:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 37:24; Isaiah 10:33–34; Jeremiah 4:6–7"> and I will bring upon thee a destroying man, and his axe: and they shall cut down thy choice cedars, and cast them into the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 9:8–9; Chronicles II 7:20–22; Deuteronomy 29:23–26"> And nations shall pass through this city, and each shall say to his neighbor, Why has the Lord done thus to this great city?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings IV 22:17; Chronicles II 34:25; Jeremiah 50:7"> And they shall say, Because they forsook the covenant of the Lord their God, and worshiped strange gods, and served them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:2; Jeremiah 22:11; Isaiah 57:1"> Weep not for the dead, nor lament for him: weep bitterly for him that goes away: for he shall return no more, nor see his native land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles I 3:15; Chronicles II 36:1–4; Kings IV 23:34"> For thus saith the Lord concerning Shallum the son of Josiah, who reigns in the place of Josiah his father, who has gone forth out of this place; He shall not return thither anymore:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 23:34; Jeremiah 22:18"> but in that place whither I have carried him captive, there shall he die, and shall see this land no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 5:4; Micah 3:10; Leviticus 19:13"> He that builds his house not with justice, and his upper chambers not with judgment, who works by means of his neighbor for nothing, and will by no means give him his reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings II 7:2; Isaiah 5:8–9; Haggai 1:4"> Thou hast built for thyself a well-proportioned house, airy chambers, fitted with windows, and wainscoted with cedar, and painted with vermilion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings IV 23:25; Isaiah 3:10; Ecclesiastes 10:17"> Shalt thou reign, because thou art provoked with thy father Ahaz? they shall not eat, and they shall not drink: it is better for thee to execute judgment and justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:24; Isaiah 1:17; 1 John 2:3–4"> They understood not, they judged not the cause of the afflicted, nor the cause of the poor: is not this thy not knowing me? saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 2:14; Kings IV 24:4; Jeremiah 22:3"> Behold, thine eyes are not good, nor thy heart, but they go after thy covetousness, and after the innocent blood to shed it, and after acts of injustice and slaughter, to commit them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 13:30; Jeremiah 16:6; Jeremiah 16:4"> Therefore thus saith the Lord concerning Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, even concerning this man; they shall not bewail him, saying, Ah brother! neither shall they at all weep for him, saying, Alas Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:30; Kings III 21:23–24; Chronicles II 36:6"> He shall be buried with the burial of an ass; he shall be dragged roughly along and cast outside the gate of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 27:12; Ezekiel 23:22; Lamentations 1:2"> Go up to Lebanon, and cry; and utter thy voice to Bashan, and cry aloud to the extremity of the sea: for all thy lovers are destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:30; Jeremiah 3:25; Jeremiah 7:22–28"> I spoke to thee on occasion of thy trespass, but thou saidst, I will not hearken. This has been thy way from thy youth, thou hast not hearkened to my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:30–31; Jeremiah 12:10; Jeremiah 23:1–2"> The wind shall tend all thy shepherds, and thy lovers shall go into captivity; for then shalt thou be ashamed and disgraced because of all thy lovers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:24; Jeremiah 4:30–31; Jeremiah 22:6"> O thou that dwellest in Lebanon, making thy nest in the cedars, thou shalt groan heavily, when pangs as of a travailing woman are come upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:1; Song of Solomon 8:6; Chronicles I 3:16"> As I live, saith the Lord, though Coniah son of Jehoiakim king of Judah were indeed the seal upon my right hand, thence would I pluck thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:20–21; Jeremiah 21:7; Kings IV 24:15–16"> and I will deliver thee into the hands of them that seek thy life, before whom thou art afraid, into the hands of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings IV 24:15; Jeremiah 15:2–4; Isaiah 22:17"> And I will cast forth thee, and thy mother that bore thee, into a land where thou wast not born; and there ye shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:14; Kings IV 25:27–30; Psalms 86:4"> But they shall by no means return to the land which they long for in their souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 31:12; Hosea 8:8; Jeremiah 48:38"> Coniah is dishonored as a good-for-nothing vessel; for he is thrown out and cast forth into a land which he knew not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Micah 1:2; Jeremiah 6:19"> Land, land, hear the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:30; Psalms 94:20; Matthew 1:11–16"> Write ye this man an outcast: for there shall none of his seed at all grow up to sit on the throne of David, or as a prince yet in Judah.</span>
