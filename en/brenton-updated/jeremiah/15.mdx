<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:14; Ezekiel 14:20–21; Jeremiah 14:11"> And the Lord said to me, Though Moses and Samuel stood before my face, my soul could not be toward them: dismiss this people, and let them go forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:11; Zechariah 11:9; Revelation 13:10"> And it shall be, if they say to thee, Whither shall we go forth? then thou shalt say to them, Thus saith the Lord; As many as are for death, to death; and as many as are for famine, to famine; and as many as are for the sword, to the sword; and as many as are for captivity, to captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:26; Leviticus 26:22; Leviticus 26:16"> And I will punish them with four kinds of death, saith the Lord, the sword to slay, and the dogs to tear, and the wild beasts of the earth, and the birds of the sky to devour and destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; Kings IV 23:26–27; Kings IV 24:3–4"> And I will deliver them up for distress to all the kingdoms of the earth, because of Manasseh son of Hezekiah king of Judah, for all that he did in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 51:19; Nahum 3:7; Jeremiah 21:7"> Who will spare thee, O Jerusalem? and who will fear for thee? or who will turn back to ask for thy welfare?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Zephaniah 1:4; Isaiah 1:4"> Thou hast turned away from me, saith the Lord, thou wilt go back: therefore I will stretch out my hand, and will destroy thee, and will no more spare them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 3:12; Jeremiah 51:2; Isaiah 41:16"> And I will completely scatter them; in the gates of my people they are bereaved of children: they have destroyed my people because of their iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 3:25–1; Jeremiah 6:4–5; Luke 21:35"> Their widows have been multiplied more than the sand of the sea: I have brought young men against the mother, even distress at noonday: I have suddenly cast upon her trembling and anxiety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 2:5; Isaiah 47:9; Lamentations 1:1"> She that bore seven is spent; her soul has fainted under trouble; her sun is gone down while it is yet noon; she is ashamed and disgraced: I will give the remnant of them to the sword before their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 6:22; Exodus 22:25; Proverbs 26:2"> Woe is me, my mother! thou hast born me as some man of strife, and at variance with the whole earth; I have not helped others, nor has anyone helped me; my strength has failed among them that curse me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Jeremiah 29:11–14; Psalms 106:46"> Be it so, Lord, in their prosperity; surely I stood before thee in the time of their calamities, and in the time of their affliction, for their good against the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 40:9; Jeremiah 21:4–5; Isaiah 45:9"> Will iron be known? whereas thy strength is a brazen covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:3; Psalms 44:12; Isaiah 52:3"> Yea, I will give thy treasures for a spoil as a recompense, because of all thy sins, and that in all thy borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:13; Deuteronomy 32:22; Jeremiah 17:4"> And I will enslave thee to thine enemies round about, in a land which thou hast not known; for a fire has been kindled out of my wrath; it shall burn upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:3; Psalms 69:7–9; Luke 21:17"> O Lord, remember me, and visit me, and vindicate me before them that persecute me; do not bear long with them; know how I have met with reproach for thy sake, from those who set at nought thy words;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 10:9–10; Ezekiel 3:1–3; Psalms 119:101–103"> consume them; and thy word shall be to me for the joy and gladness of my heart: for thy name has been called upon me, O Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; Lamentations 3:28; Psalms 26:4–5"> I have not sat in the assembly of them as they mocked, but I feared because of thy power: I sat alone, for I was filled with bitterness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:15; Job 34:6; Micah 1:9"> Why do they that grieve me prevail against me? my wound is severe; whence shall I be healed? it is indeed become to me as deceitful water, that has no faithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 3:7; Luke 21:15; Leviticus 10:10"> Therefore thus saith the Lord, If thou wilt return, then will I restore thee, and thou shalt stand before my face: and if thou wilt bring forth the precious from the worthless, thou shalt be as my mouth: and they shall return to thee; but thou shalt not return to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18–19; Acts 18:9–10; Ezekiel 3:9"> And I will make thee to this people as a strong brazen wall; and they shall fight against thee, but they shall by no means prevail against thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Isaiah 49:24–25; Jeremiah 50:34"> for I am with thee to save thee, and to deliver thee out of the hand of wicked men; and I will ransom thee out of the hand of pestilent men.</span>
