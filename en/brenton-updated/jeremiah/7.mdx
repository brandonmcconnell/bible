<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:2; Revelation 3:22; Amos 7:16"> Hear ye the word of the Lord, all Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:11; Jeremiah 26:13; Isaiah 1:16–19"> Thus saith the Lord God of Israel, Correct your ways and your devices, and I will cause you to dwell in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Matthew 3:9–10; Zephaniah 3:11"> Trust not in yourselves with lying words, for they shall not profit you at all, saying, It is the temple of the Lord, the temple of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings III 6:12–13; Isaiah 1:19; Jeremiah 7:3"> For if ye thoroughly correct your ways and your practices, and do indeed execute judgment between a man and his neighbor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:21–24; Zechariah 7:9–12; Jeremiah 13:10"> and oppress not the stranger, and the orphan, and the widow, and shed not innocent blood in this place, and go not after strange gods to your hurt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Jeremiah 25:5; Jeremiah 3:18"> then will I cause you to dwell in this place, in the land which I gave to your fathers of old and forever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 28:15; Ezekiel 13:6–16; Jeremiah 23:14–16"> But whereas ye have trusted in lying words, whereby ye shall not be profited;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:6; Exodus 20:3; James 4:1–4"> and ye murder, and commit adultery, and steal, and swear falsely, and burn incense to Baal, and are gone after strange gods whom ye know not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:34; Ezekiel 23:37; Ezekiel 23:39"> so that it is evil with you; yet have ye come, and stood before me in the house, whereon my name is called, and ye have said, We have refrained from doing all these abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:45–46; Isaiah 56:7; Matthew 21:13"> Is my house, whereon my name is called, a den of robbers in your eyes? And, behold, I have seen it, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Jeremiah 26:6; Kings I 4:10–11"> For go ye to my place which is in Shiloh, where I caused my name to dwell before, and see what I did to it because of the wickedness of my people Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; Chronicles II 36:15–16; Jeremiah 7:25"> And now, because ye have done all these deeds, and I spoke to you, but ye hearkened not to me; and I called you, but ye answered not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 78:60; Kings IV 25:9; Jeremiah 7:4"> therefore I also will do to the house whereon my name is called, wherein ye trust, and to the place which I gave to you and to your fathers, as I did to Shiloh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:1; Kings IV 17:23; Jeremiah 52:3"> And I will cast you out of my sight, as I cast away your brethren, all the seed of Ephraim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:14; Jeremiah 14:11–12; Jeremiah 15:1"> Therefore pray not thou for this people, and intercede not for them to be pitied, yea, pray not, and approach me not for them: for I will not hearken unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:6–18; Ezekiel 14:23; Jeremiah 6:27"> Seest thou not what they do in the cities of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:13; Jeremiah 44:17–19; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> Their children gather wood, and their fathers kindle a fire, and their women knead dough, to make cakes to the host of heaven; and they have poured out drink offerings to strange gods, that they might provoke me to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:11; Jeremiah 2:19; Daniel 9:7–8"> Do they provoke me to anger? saith the Lord: do they not provoke themselves, that their faces may be ashamed?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:18; Lamentations 2:3–5; Isaiah 42:25"> Therefore thus saith the Lord; Behold, my anger and wrath shall be poured out upon this place, and upon the men, and upon the cattle, and upon every tree of their field, and upon the fruits of the land; and it shall burn, and not be quenched.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Jeremiah 6:20; Amos 5:21–23"> Thus saith the Lord, Gather your whole burnt offerings with your meat offerings, and eat flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Kings I 15:22; Psalms 51:16–17"> For I spoke not to your fathers, and commanded them not in the day wherein I brought them up out of the land of Egypt, concerning whole burnt offerings and sacrifice:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Deuteronomy 5:33; Exodus 19:5–6"> but I commanded them this thing, saying, Hear ye my voice, and I will be to you a God, and ye shall be to me a people: and walk ye in all my ways, which I shall command you, that it may be well with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 81:11–12; Jeremiah 8:5; Jeremiah 7:26"> But they hearkened not to me, and their ear gave no heed, but they walked in the imaginations of their evil heart, and went backward, and not forward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:15; Jeremiah 25:4; Nehemiah 9:26"> from the day that their fathers went forth out of the land of Egypt, even until this day. And I sent to you all my servants, the prophets, by day and early in the morning: yea, I sent them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; Chronicles II 30:8; Jeremiah 7:24"> but they hearkened not to me, and their ear gave no heed; and they made their neck harder than their fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:2; Jeremiah 1:7; Isaiah 65:12"> Therefore thou shalt speak this word to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 59:14–15; Proverbs 1:7; Isaiah 1:4–5"> This is the nation which has not hearkened to the voice of the Lord, nor received correction: truth has failed from their mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 1:20; Micah 1:16; Jeremiah 6:30"> Cut off thy hair, and cast it away, and take up a lamentation on thy lips; for the Lord has reprobated and rejected the generation that does these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:34; Ezekiel 7:20; Daniel 9:27"> For the children of Judah have wrought evil before me, saith the Lord; they have set their abominations in the house on which my name is called, to defile it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings IV 23:10; Jeremiah 32:35; Leviticus 18:21"> And they have built the altar of Topheth, which is in the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, to burn their sons and their daughters with fire; which I did not command them to do, neither did I design it in my heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:11; Jeremiah 19:6; Kings IV 23:10"> Therefore, behold, the days come, saith the Lord, when they shall no more say, The altar of Topheth, and the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, but, The Valley of the Slain; and they shall bury in Topheth, for want of room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:9; Deuteronomy 28:26; Revelation 19:17–18"> And the dead bodies of this people shall be for food to the birds of the sky, and to the wild beasts of the earth; and there shall be none to drive them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Hosea 2:11; Ezekiel 26:13"> And I will destroy out of the cities of Judah, and the streets of Jerusalem, the voice of them that make merry, and the voice of them that rejoice, the voice of the bridegroom, and the voice of the bride; for the whole land shall become a desolation.</span>
</div>
