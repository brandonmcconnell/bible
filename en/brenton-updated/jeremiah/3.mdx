<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1–4; Ezekiel 16:26; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> If a man put away his wife, and she depart from him, and become another man’s, shall she return to him anymore at all? shall not that woman be utterly defiled? yet thou hast gone a-whoring with many shepherds, and hast returned to me, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:7; Genesis 38:14; Ezekiel 8:4–6"> Lift up thine eyes to look straight forward, and see where thou hast not been utterly defiled. Thou hast sat for them by the wayside as a deserted crow, and hast defiled the land with thy fornications and thy wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremiah 14:4; Deuteronomy 28:23"> And thou didst retain many shepherds for a stumbling block to thyself: thou hadst a whore’s face, thou didst become shameless toward all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:2; Jeremiah 3:19; Psalms 71:17"> Hast thou not called me as it were a home, and the father and guide of thy virgin time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 7:3; Isaiah 57:16; Ezekiel 22:6"> Will God’s anger continue forever, or be preserved to the end? Behold, thou hast spoken and done these bad things, and hadst power to do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings IV 17:7–17; Ezekiel 16:31; Jeremiah 7:24"> And the Lord said to me in the days of Josiah the king, Hast thou seen what things the house of Israel has done to me? they have gone on every high mountain, and under every shady tree, and have committed fornication there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:46; Hosea 6:1–4; Hosea 14:1"> And I said after she had committed all these acts of fornication, Turn again to me. Yet she returned not. And faithless Judah saw her faithlessness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1; Hosea 4:15–17; Isaiah 50:1"> And I saw that (for all the sins of which she was convicted, wherein the house of Israel committed adultery, and I put her away, and gave into her hands a bill of divorcement), yet faithless Judah feared not, but went and herself also committed fornication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:27; Isaiah 57:6; Jeremiah 3:2"> And her fornication was nothing accounted of; and she committed adultery with wood and stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 7:14; Jeremiah 12:2; Chronicles II 34:33–18"> And for all these things faithless Judah turned not to me with all her heart, but falsely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:11; Ezekiel 16:51–52; Hosea 11:7"> And the Lord said to me, Israel has justified himself more than faithless Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Ezekiel 33:11; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> Go and read these words toward the north, and thou shalt say, Return to me, O house of Israel, saith the Lord; and I will not set my face against you: for I am merciful, saith the Lord, and I will not be angry with you forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:25; Jeremiah 3:6; Deuteronomy 12:2"> Nevertheless, know thine iniquity, that thou hast sinned against the Lord thy God, and hast scattered thy ways to strangers under every shady tree, but thou didst not hearken to my voice, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 2:19–20; Jeremiah 2:19; Jeremiah 31:32"> Turn, ye children that have revolted, saith the Lord; for I will rule over you: and I will take you one of a city, and two of a family, and I will bring you in to Zion:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:4; Acts 20:28; Ephesians 4:11–12"> and I will give you shepherds after my heart, and they shall certainly tend you with knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; Amos 9:14–15; Ezekiel 37:26"> And it shall come to pass that when ye are multiplied and increased upon the land, saith the Lord, in those days they shall say no more, The ark of the covenant of the Holy One of Israel: it shall not come to mind; it shall not be named; neither shall it be visited; nor shall this be done anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:7; Micah 4:1–5; Jeremiah 17:12"> In those days and at that time they shall call Jerusalem the throne of the Lord; and all the nations shall be gathered to it: and they shall not walk anymore after the imaginations of their evil heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:8; Hosea 1:11; Amos 9:15"> In those days the house of Judah shall come together to the house of Israel, and they shall come, together, from the land of the north, and from all the countries, to the land, which I caused their fathers to inherit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:4; Jeremiah 31:9; Isaiah 63:16"> And I said, So be it, Lord, for thou saidst, I will set thee among children, and will give thee a choice land, the inheritance of the Almighty God of the Gentiles: and I said, Ye shall call me Father; and ye shall not turn away from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 48:8; Jeremiah 5:11; Hosea 5:7"> But as a wife acts treacherously against her husband, so has the house of Israel dealt treacherously against me, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; Jeremiah 31:9; Jeremiah 2:32"> A voice from the lips was heard, even of weeping and supplication of the children of Israel: for they have dealt unrighteously in their ways, they have forgotten God their Holy One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 14:1; Hosea 14:4; Zechariah 13:9"> Turn, ye children that are given to turning, and I will heal your bruises. Behold, we will be thy servants; for thou art the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 121:1–2; Psalms 3:8; Isaiah 12:2"> Truly the hills and the strength of the mountains were a lying refuge: but by the Lord our God is the salvation of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 9:10; Ezekiel 16:63; Ezekiel 16:61"> But shame has consumed the labors of our fathers from our youth; their sheep and their calves, and their sons and their daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:21; Proverbs 5:13; Isaiah 50:11"> We have lain down in our shame, and our disgrace has covered us: because we and our fathers have sinned before our God, from our youth until this day; and we have not hearkened to the voice of the Lord our God.</span>
