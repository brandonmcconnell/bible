<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:8; Jeremiah 40:6; Psalms 41:9"> The word that came to Jeremiah from the Lord (now Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and all his army, and all the country of his dominion, were warring against Jerusalem, and against all the cities of Judah), saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings IV 25:25; Jeremiah 40:5; Psalms 109:5"> Thus has the Lord said; Go to Zedekiah king of Judah, and thou shalt say to him, Thus has the Lord said, This city shall certainly be delivered into the hands of the king of Babylon, and he shall take it, and shall burn it with fire:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings IV 25:25; Lamentations 1:2; Jeremiah 41:11–12"> and thou shalt not escape out of his hand, but shalt certainly be taken, and shalt be given into his hands; and thine eyes shall see his eyes, and thou shalt enter into Babylon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings I 27:11; Psalms 52:1–2"> But hear the word of the Lord, O Zedekiah king of Judah; Thus saith the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings III 16:24; Deuteronomy 14:1; Joshua 18:1"> Thou shalt die in peace: and as they wept for thy fathers that reigned before thee, they shall weep also for thee, saying, Ah lord! and they shall lament for thee down to the grave: for I have spoken the word, said the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings II 3:16; Jeremiah 50:4; Kings II 1:2–16"> And Jeremiah spoke to King Zedekiah all these words in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Psalms 55:23; Ezekiel 22:27"> And the host of the king of Babylon warred against Jerusalem, and against the cities of Judah, and against Lachish, and against Azekah: for these strong cities were left among the cities of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 2:4; Proverbs 13:8; Philippians 3:7–9"> The word that came from the Lord to Jeremiah, after King Zedekiah had concluded a covenant with the people, to proclaim a release;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 6:2; Kings III 15:17–22; Kings I 13:6"> that every one should set at liberty his servant, and every one his handmaid, the Hebrew man and Hebrew woman, that no man of Judah should be a bondman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:14; Jeremiah 40:7; Nehemiah 2:10"> Then all the nobles, and all the people who had entered into the covenant, engaging to set free every one his manservant, and every one his maid, turned,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:13–16; Jeremiah 40:7–8; Jeremiah 42:1"> and gave them over to be menservants and maidservants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings II 2:13; Genesis 14:14–16; Kings I 30:1–8"> And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Thus saith the Lord; I made a covenant with your fathers in the day wherein I took them out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When six years are accomplished, thou shalt set free thy brother the Hebrew, who shall be sold to thee: for he shall serve thee six years, and then thou shalt let him go free: but they hearkened not to me, and inclined not their ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 21:30; Proverbs 28:17; Kings III 20:20"> And this day they turned to do that which was right in my sight, to proclaim every one the release of his neighbor; and they had concluded a covenant before me, in the house whereon my name is called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:4–7; Jeremiah 42:8; Jeremiah 41:10"> But ye turned and profaned my name, to bring back every one his servant, and every one his handmaid, whom ye had sent forth free and at their own disposal, to be to you menservants and maidservants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings II 19:37–38; Jeremiah 42:14; Jeremiah 43:7"> Therefore thus said the Lord; Ye have not hearkened to me, to proclaim a release every one to his neighbor: behold, I proclaim a release to you, to the sword, and to the pestilence, and to the famine; and I will give you up to dispersion among all the kingdoms of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:5; Luke 12:4–5; Jeremiah 42:16"> And I will give the men that have transgressed my covenant, who have not kept my covenant, which they made before me, the calf which they prepared to sacrifice with it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> the princes of Judah, and the men in power, and the priests, and the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> I will even give them to their enemies, and their carcasses shall be food for the birds of the sky and for the wild beasts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And I will give Zedekiah king of Judea, and their princes, into the hands of their enemies, and the host of the king of Babylon shall come upon them that run away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Behold, I will give command, saith the Lord, and will bring them back to this land; and they shall fight against it, and take it, and burn it with fire, and the cities of Judah; and I will make them desolate without inhabitants.</span>
</div>
