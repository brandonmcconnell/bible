<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:26–27; Jeremiah 51:1–14; Isaiah 13:1–3"> And it came to pass, when Jeremiah ceased speaking to the people all the words of the Lord, for which the Lord had sent him to them, even all these words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 46:1; Isaiah 21:9; Jeremiah 51:44"> that Azariah son of Hoshaiah spoke, and Johanan, the son of Kareah, and all the men who had spoken to Jeremiah, saying, It is false: the Lord has not sent thee to us, saying, Enter not into Egypt to dwell there:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 51:62"> but Baruch the son of Neriah sets thee against us, that thou mayest deliver us into the hands of the Chaldeans, to kill us, and that we should be carried away captives to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31; Hosea 1:11; Hosea 3:5"> So Johanan, and all the leaders of the host, and all the people, refused to hearken to the voice of the Lord, to dwell in the land of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:40; Hebrews 8:6–10; Isaiah 55:3"> And Johanan, and all the leaders of the host, took all the remnant of Judah, who had returned to dwell in the land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6; Matthew 9:36; Ezekiel 34:14"> the mighty men, and the women, and the children that were left, and the daughters of the king, and the souls which Nebuzaradan had left with Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, and Jeremiah the prophet, and Baruch the son of Neriah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:8; Jeremiah 2:3; Zechariah 11:5"> And they came into Egypt: for they hearkened not to the voice of the Lord: and they entered into Tahpanhes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Jeremiah 51:6; Revelation 18:4"> And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah in Tahpanhes, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 15:14; Kings II 1:22"> Take thee great stones, and hide them in the entrance, at the gate of the house of Pharaoh in Tahpanhes, in the sight of the men of Judah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:12; Isaiah 33:23; Isaiah 33:4"> and thou shalt say, Thus has the Lord said; Behold, I will send, and will bring Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he shall place his throne upon these stones which thou hast hidden, and he shall lift up weapons against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 47:6; Ezekiel 25:15–17; Ezekiel 26:2–3"> And he shall enter in, and smite the land of Egypt, delivering some for death to death; and some for captivity to captivity; and some for the sword to the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:43; Jeremiah 49:2; Galatians 4:26"> And he shall kindle a fire in the houses of their gods, and shall burn them, and shall carry them away captives: and shall search the land of Egypt, as a shepherd searches his garment; and he shall go forth in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:16; Jeremiah 49:17; Lamentations 2:15–16"> And he shall break to pieces the pillars of Heliopolis that are in On, and shall burn their houses with fire.</span>
