<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Amos 8:1; Joel 2:25"> Thus has the Lord God shown me; and, behold, a swarm of locusts coming from the east; and, behold, one caterpillar, King Gog.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:13; Isaiah 37:4; Jeremiah 42:2"> And it came to pass when he had finished devouring the grass of the land, that I said, Lord God, be merciful; who shall raise up Jacob? for he is small in number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; Jonah 3:10; Hosea 11:8"> Repent, O Lord, for this. And this shall not be, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 7:1; Isaiah 66:15–16; Hebrews 1:7"> Thus has the Lord shown me; and, behold, the Lord called for judgment by fire, and it devoured the great deep, and devoured the Lord’s portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 85:4; Isaiah 1:9; Jeremiah 30:19"> Then I said, O Lord, cease, I pray thee: who shall raise up Jacob? for he is small in number. Repent, O Lord, for this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 7:3; Judges 2:18; Psalms 90:13"> This also shall not be, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 21:13; Revelation 21:15; Isaiah 28:17"> Thus the Lord showed me; and behold, he stood upon a wall of adamant, and in his hand was an adamant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 8:2; Lamentations 2:8; Ezekiel 7:2–9"> And the Lord said to me, What seest thou, Amos? And I said, An adamant. And the Lord said to me, Behold, I appoint an adamant in the midst of my people Israel: I will not pass by them anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings IV 15:8–10; Genesis 46:1; Amos 8:14"> And the joyful altars shall be abolished, and the sacrifices of Israel shall be set aside; and I will rise up against the house of Jeroboam with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:4; Kings III 12:31–32; Jeremiah 26:8–11"> Then Amaziah the priest of Bethel sent to Jeroboam king of Israel, saying, Amos is forming conspiracies against thee in the midst of the house of Israel: the land will be utterly unable to bear all his words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings IV 17:6; Psalms 56:5; Amos 6:7–8"> For thus says Amos, Jeroboam shall die by the sword, and Israel shall be led away captive from his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 8:34; Kings I 9:9"> And Amaziah said to Amos, Go, seer, remove thou into the land of Judah, and live there, and thou shalt prophesy there:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 12:32; Amos 2:12; Kings III 12:29"> but thou shalt no longer prophesy at Bethel: for it is the king’s sanctuary, and it is the royal house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 1:1; Kings IV 4:38; Kings IV 2:5"> And Amos answered, and said to Amaziah, I was not a prophet, nor the son of a prophet; but I was a herdsman, and a gatherer of sycamore fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings II 7:8; Ezekiel 2:3–4; Jeremiah 1:7"> And the Lord took me from the sheep, and the Lord said to me, Go, and prophesy to my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46; Micah 2:6; Ezekiel 21:2"> And now hear the word of the Lord: Thou sayest, Prophesy not to Israel, and raise not a tumult against the house of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 9:3; Kings IV 17:6; Hosea 4:13–14"> Therefore thus saith the Lord; Thy wife shall be a harlot in the city, and thy sons and thy daughters shall fall by the sword, and thy land shall be measured with the line; and thou shalt die in an unclean land; and Israel shall be led captive out of his land. Thus has the Lord God shown me.</span>
