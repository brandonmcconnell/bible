</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings IV 8:12; Amos 1:9; Isaiah 8:4"> And the Lord said, For three sins of Damascus, and for four, I will not turn away from it; because they sawed with iron saws the women with child of the Gileadites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:27; Kings IV 6:24; Kings IV 13:25"> And I will send a fire on the house of Hazael, and it shall devour the foundations of the son of Hadad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 16:9; Amos 9:7; Jeremiah 51:30"> And I will break to pieces the bars of Damascus, and will destroy the inhabitants out of the plain of Aven, and will cut in pieces a tribe out of the men of Haran: and the famous people of Syria shall be led captive, saith the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 1:11; Chronicles II 28:18; Obadiah 1:11"> Thus saith the Lord; For three sins of Gaza, and for four, I will not turn away from them; because they took prisoners the captivity of Solomon, to shut them up into Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 18:8; Jeremiah 47:1; Zephaniah 2:4"> And I will send forth a fire on the walls of Gaza, and it shall devour its foundations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:16; Isaiah 14:29–31; Jeremiah 47:4–5"> And I will destroy the inhabitants out of Ashdod, and a tribe shall be cut off from Ashkelon, and I will stretch out my hand upon Ekron: and the remnant of the Philistines shall perish, saith the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 9:11–14; Isaiah 23:1–18; Joel 3:4–8"> Thus saith the Lord; For three transgressions of Tyre, and for four, I will not turn away from it; because they shut up the prisoners of Solomon into Edom, and remembered not the covenant of brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 9:4; Amos 1:7; Amos 1:4"> And I will send forth a fire on the walls of Tyre, and it shall devour the foundations of it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:12–14; Chronicles II 28:17; Malachi 1:4"> Thus saith the Lord; For three sins of Edom, and for four, I will not turn away from them; because they pursued their brother with the sword, and destroyed the mother upon the earth, and summoned up his anger for a testimony, and kept up his fury to the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:7; Obadiah 1:9–10; Jeremiah 49:20"> And I will send forth a fire upon Teman, and it shall devour the foundations of her walls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; Ezekiel 25:2–7; Jeremiah 49:1–6"> Thus saith the Lord; For three sins of the children of Ammon, and for four, I will not turn away from him; because they ripped up the women with child of the Gileadites, that they might widen their coasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:2; Amos 2:2; Deuteronomy 3:11"> And I will kindle a fire on the walls of Rabbah, and it shall devour her foundations with shouting in the day of war, and she shall be shaken in the days of her destruction:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:3"> and her kings shall go into captivity, their priests and their rulers together, saith the Lord.</span>
