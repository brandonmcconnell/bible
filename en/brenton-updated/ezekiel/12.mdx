<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 4:12; Jeremiah 5:21; Psalms 78:40"> Son of man, thou dwellest in the midst of the iniquities of those, who have eyes to see, and see not; and have ears to hear, and hear not: because it is a provoking house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:3; 2 Timothy 2:25; Jeremiah 26:3"> Thou therefore, son of man, prepare thyself baggage for going into captivity by day in their sight; and thou shalt be led into captivity from thy place into another place in their sight; that they may see that it is a provoking house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:4; Ezekiel 12:12; Kings IV 25:4"> And thou shalt carry forth thy baggage, baggage for captivity, by day before their eyes: and thou shalt go forth at even, as a captive goes forth, in their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 25:4; Jeremiah 39:2–4"> Dig for thyself into the wall of the house, and thou shalt pass through it in their sight:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:3; Isaiah 8:18; Ezekiel 24:24"> thou shalt be lifted up on men’s shoulders, and shalt go forth in secret: thou shalt cover thy face, and shalt not see the ground: because I have made thee a sign to the house of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:18; Ezekiel 37:10; Ezekiel 37:7"> And I did thus according to all that he commanded me; and I carried forth my baggage for captivity by day, and in the evening I dug through the wall for myself, and went out secretly; I was taken up on men’s shoulders before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the word of the Lord came to me in the morning, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19; Ezekiel 17:12; Ezekiel 20:49"> Son of man, have not the house of Israel, the provoking house, said to thee, What doest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings IV 9:25; Isaiah 13:1; Jeremiah 24:8"> Say to them,<br />Thus saith the Lord God, the Prince and the Ruler in Israel, even to all the house of Israel who are in the midst of them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:15; Jeremiah 15:2; Jeremiah 52:28–30"> say, I am performing signs: as I have done, so shall it be to him: they shall go into banishment and captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 25:4; Jeremiah 39:4; Ezekiel 12:6"> And the prince in the midst of them shall be borne upon shoulders, and shall go forth in secret through the wall, and shall dig so that he may go forth thereby: he shall cover his face, that he may not be seen by any eye, and he himself shall not see the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:7; Hosea 7:12; Ezekiel 17:20"> And I will spread out my net upon him, and he shall be caught in my toils: and I will bring him to Babylon to the land of the Chaldeans; but he shall not see it, though he shall die there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:2; Kings IV 25:4–5; Ezekiel 17:21"> And I will scatter to every wind all his assistants round about him, and all that help him; and I will draw out a sword after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Ezekiel 12:16; Ezekiel 12:20"> And they shall know that I am the Lord, when I have scattered them among the nations; and I will disperse them in the countries.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; Ezekiel 14:22–23; Ezekiel 6:8–10"> And I will leave of them a few men in number spared from the sword, and from famine, and from pestilence; that they may declare all their iniquities among the nations whither they have gone; and they shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 5:9; Psalms 80:5; Job 3:24"> Son of man, eat thy bread with sorrow, and drink thy water with torment and affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 7:14; Ezekiel 6:6–7; Ezekiel 6:14"> And thou shalt say to the people of the land, Thus saith the Lord to the inhabitants of Jerusalem on the land of Israel; They shall eat their bread in scarcity, and shall drink their water in desolation, that the land may be desolate with all that it contains: for all that dwell in it are ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Isaiah 7:23–24; Jeremiah 25:9"> And their inhabited cities shall be laid utterly waste, and the land shall be desolate; and ye shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:2–3; Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 12:27"> Son of man, what is your parable on the land of Israel, that ye say, The days are long, the vision has perished?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joel 2:1; Ezekiel 7:5–7; Zephaniah 1:14"> Therefore say to them,<br />Thus saith the Lord; I will even set aside this parable, and the house of Israel shall no more at all use this parable: for thou shalt say to them, The days are at hand, and the import of every vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:13–16; Ezekiel 13:23; Zechariah 13:2–4"> For there shall no more be any false vision, nor anyone prophesying flatteries in the midst of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; Ezekiel 12:28; Isaiah 55:11"> For I the Lord will speak my words; I will speak and perform them, and will no more delay, for in your days, O provoking house, I will speak the word, and will perform it, saith the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Moreover the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Daniel 10:14; Ezekiel 12:22; 2 Peter 3:4"> Son of man, behold, the provoking house of Israel boldly say, The vision which this man sees is for many days, and he prophesies for times afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:23–25; Mark 13:32–37; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–3"> Therefore say to them,<br />Thus saith the Lord; Henceforth none of my words shall linger, which I shall speak: I will speak and do, saith the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>
