<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 10:1; Psalms 80:8–16; Isaiah 5:1–7"> And thou, son of man—of all the wood of the branches that are among the trees of the forest, what shall be made of the wood of the vine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 14:34–35; Jeremiah 24:8; Mark 9:50"> Will they take wood of it to make it fit for work? will they take of it a peg to hang any vessel upon it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:6; Isaiah 27:11; Psalms 80:16"> It is only given to the fire to be consumed; the fire consumes that which is yearly pruned of it, and it is utterly gone. Will it be useful for any work?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:16"> Not even while it is yet whole will it be useful for any work: if the fire shall have utterly consumed it, will it still be fit for work?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 15:2; Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 24:8–10"> Therefore say, Thus saith the Lord, As the vine tree among the trees of the forest, which I have given up to the fire to be consumed, so have I given up the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:8; Psalms 34:16; Isaiah 24:18"> And I will set my face against them; they shall go forth of the fire, and yet fire shall devour them; and they shall know that I am the Lord, when I have set my face against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:14; Zephaniah 1:18; Isaiah 6:11"> And I will give up the land to ruin, because they have utterly transgressed, saith the Lord.</span>
