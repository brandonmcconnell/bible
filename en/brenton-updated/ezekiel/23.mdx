<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:44–46; Jeremiah 3:7–10"> Son of man, there were two women, daughters of one mother:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 17:7; Ezekiel 16:22; Joshua 24:14"> and they went a-whoring in Egypt in their youth: there their breasts fell, there they lost their virginity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:8; Psalms 76:2; Jeremiah 2:2–3"> And their names were Oholah the elder, and Oholibah her sister: and they were mine, and bore sons and daughters: and as for their names, Samaria was Oholah, and Jerusalem was Oholibah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 5:13; Kings IV 17:3; Kings IV 16:7"> And Oholah went a-whoring from me, and doted on her lovers, on the Assyrians that were her neighbors,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:23"> clothed with purple, princes and captains; they were young men and choice, all horsemen riding on horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 5:3; Hosea 6:10; Ezekiel 22:3–4"> And she bestowed her fornication upon them; all were choice sons of the Assyrians: and on whomsoever she doted herself, with them she defiled herself in all their devices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:19; Ezekiel 23:3; Exodus 32:4"> And she forsook not her fornication with the Egyptians: for in her youth they committed fornication with her, and they deflowered her, and poured out their fornication upon her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 11:5; Kings IV 15:29; Kings IV 17:23"> Therefore I delivered her into the hands of her lovers, into the hands of the children of the Assyrians, on whom she doted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:29; Hosea 2:10; Jeremiah 22:8–9"> They uncovered her shame: they took her sons and daughters, and slew her with the sword: and she became a byword among women; and they wrought vengeance in her for the sake of the daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:8–11; Ezekiel 16:47–51; Ezekiel 23:4"> And her sister Oholibah saw it, and she indulged in her fondness more corruptly than she, and in her fornication more than the fornication of her sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 16:7–15; Ezekiel 23:23; Chronicles II 28:16–23"> She doted upon the sons of the Assyrians, princes and captains, her neighbors, clothed with fine linen, horsemen riding on horses; they were all choice young men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 12:1–2; Kings IV 17:18–19; Ezekiel 23:31"> And I saw that they were defiled, that the two had one way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:10; Jeremiah 22:14; Jeremiah 50:2"> And she increased her fornication, and she saw men painted on the wall, likenesses of the Chaldeans painted with a pencil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 8:18; Kings I 18:4; Isaiah 22:21"> having variegated girdles on their loins, having also richly dyed attire upon their heads; all had a princely appearance, the likeness of the children of the Chaldeans, of their native land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:29; Matthew 5:28; Job 31:1"> And she doted upon them as soon as she saw them, and sent forth messengers to them into the land of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:28; Ezekiel 23:22; Genesis 10:10"> And the sons of Babylon came to her, into the bed of rest, and they defiled her in her fornication, and she was defiled by them, and her soul was alienated from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 78:59; Psalms 106:40; Jeremiah 12:8"> And she exposed her fornication, and exposed her shame: and my soul was alienated from her, even as my soul was alienated from her sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:3; Ezekiel 20:7; Ezekiel 16:51"> And thou didst multiply thy fornication, so as to call to remembrance the days of thy youth, wherein thou didst commit whoredom in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:15; Ezekiel 16:26; Jeremiah 5:8"> and thou didst dote upon the Chaldeans, whose flesh is as the flesh of asses, and their members as the members of horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And thou didst look upon the iniquity of thy youth, the things which thou wroughtest in Egypt in thy lodging, where were the breasts of thy youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:37; Revelation 17:16; Jeremiah 6:22–23"> Therefore, Oholibah, thus saith the Lord; Behold, I will stir up thy lovers against thee, from whom thy soul is alienated, and I will bring them upon thee round about,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:21; Kings IV 24:2; Kings IV 20:14–17"> the children of Babylon, and all the Chaldeans, Pekod, and Shua, and Koa, and all the sons of the Assyrians with them; choice young men, governors and captains, all princes and renowned, riding on horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings II 24:14; Ezekiel 26:10; Ezekiel 23:45"> And they all shall come upon thee from the north, chariots and wheels, with a multitude of nations, shields and targets; and the enemy shall set a watch against thee round about: and I will set judgment before them, and they shall take vengeance on thee with their judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:47; Ezekiel 8:1–18; Ezekiel 15:6–7"> And I will bring upon thee my jealousy, and they shall deal with thee in great wrath: they shall take away thy nose and thine ears; and shall cast down thy remnant with the sword: they shall take thy sons and thy daughters; and thy remnant fire shall devour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:39; Jeremiah 13:22; Ezekiel 23:29"> And they shall strip thee of thy raiment, and take away thine ornaments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:41; Ezekiel 23:3; Ezekiel 23:19"> So I will turn back thine ungodliness from thee, and thy fornication from the land of Egypt: and thou shalt not lift up thine eyes upon them, and shalt no more remember Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:22; Ezekiel 23:17; Jeremiah 34:20"> Wherefore thus saith the Lord God; Behold, I will deliver thee into the hands of those whom thou hatest, from whom thy soul is alienated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:39; Ezekiel 23:25–26; Ezekiel 23:45–47"> And they shall deal with thee in hatred, and shall take all the fruits of thy labors and thy toils, and thou shalt be naked and bare: and the shame of thy fornication shall be exposed: and thy ungodliness and thy fornication</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Psalms 106:35–38; Jeremiah 2:18–20"> brought this upon thee, in that thou wentest a-whoring after the nations, and didst defile thyself with their devices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings IV 21:13; Jeremiah 7:14–15; Ezekiel 16:47–51"> Thou didst walk in the way of thy sister; and I will put her cup into thy hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 51:17; Psalms 60:3; Ezekiel 22:4–5"> Thus saith the Lord; Drink thy sister’s cup, deep and large, and full, to cause complete drunkenness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 51:17; Jeremiah 25:27; Jeremiah 25:15–16"> And thou shalt be thoroughly weakened; and the cup of destruction, the cup of thy sister Samaria,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 75:8; Isaiah 51:17; Revelation 18:7"> drink thou it, and I will take away her feasts and her new moons: for I have spoken it, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 14:9; Isaiah 17:10; Jeremiah 32:33"> Therefore thus saith the Lord; Because thou hast forgotten me, and cast me behind thy back, therefore receive thou the reward of thine ungodliness and thy fornication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:2; Isaiah 58:1; Ezekiel 16:2"> And the Lord said to me; Son of man, wilt thou not judge Oholah and Oholibah? and declare to them their iniquities?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:36; Jeremiah 32:35; Jeremiah 7:31"> For they have committed adultery, and blood was in their hands, they committed adultery with their devices, and they passed through the fire to them their children which they bore to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:13; Kings IV 21:7; Ezekiel 7:20"> So long too as they did these things to me, they defiled my sanctuary, and profaned my Sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Kings IV 21:4; Ezekiel 44:7; Jeremiah 23:11"> And when they sacrificed their children to their idols, they also went into my sanctuary to profane it: and whereas they did thus in the midst of my house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Kings IV 9:30; Jeremiah 4:30; Ezekiel 16:13–16"> and whereas they did thus to the men that came from afar, to whom they sent messengers, and as soon as they came, immediately thou didst wash thyself, and didst paint thine eyes and adorn thyself with ornaments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Esther 1:6; Jeremiah 44:17; Isaiah 65:11"> and satest on a prepared bed, and before it there was a table set out, and as for mine incense and mine oil, they rejoiced in them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:11–12; Revelation 12:3; Exodus 32:18–19"> and they raised a sound of music, and that with men coming from the wilderness out of a multitude of men, and they put bracelets on their hands, and a crown of glory on their heads;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:3; Jeremiah 13:23; Ezra and Nehemiah 9:7"> Therefore I said, Do they not commit adultery with these? and has she also gone a-whoring after the manner of a harlot?</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:9–13; Ezekiel 23:3"> And they went in to her, as men go in to a harlot; so they went in to Oholah and to Oholibah to work iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hosea 6:5; Leviticus 20:10; Ezekiel 23:36–39"> And they are just men, and shall take vengeance on them with the judgment of an adulteress and the judgment of blood: for they are adulteresses, and blood is in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:40; Jeremiah 15:4; Jeremiah 24:9"> Thus saith the Lord God, Bring up a multitude upon them, and send trouble and plunder into the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:40–41; Jeremiah 39:8; Ezekiel 24:21"> And stone them with the stones of a multitude, and pierce them with their swords: they shall slay their sons and their daughters, and shall burn up their houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:6; Ezekiel 23:27; Ezekiel 16:41"> And I will remove ungodliness out of the land, and all the women shall be instructed, and shall not do according to their ungodliness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:10; Ezekiel 7:4; Ezekiel 6:7"> And your ungodliness shall be recompensed upon you, and ye shall bear the guilt of your devices: and ye shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
