<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:1–6; Amos 1:13–15; Ezekiel 6:2"> Son of man, set thy face steadfastly against the children of Ammon, and prophesy against them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:2; Psalms 70:2–3; Proverbs 17:5"> and thou shalt say to the children of Ammon, Hear ye the word of the Lord; thus saith the Lord; Forasmuch as ye have rejoiced against my sanctuary, because it was profaned; and against the land of Israel, because it was laid waste; and against the house of Judah, because they went into captivity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Judges 6:33; Kings III 4:30"> therefore, behold, I will deliver you to the children of Kedem for an inheritance, and they shall lodge in thee with their stuff, and they shall pitch their tents in thee: they shall eat thy fruits, and they shall drink thy milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; Kings II 12:26; Isaiah 17:2"> And I will give up the city of Ammon for camels’ pastures, and the children of Ammon for a pasture of sheep: and ye shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:8; Obadiah 1:12; Zephaniah 2:10"> For thus saith the Lord; Because thou hast clapped thy hands, and stamped with thy foot, and heartily rejoiced against the land of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:4; Ezekiel 25:16; Ezekiel 6:14"> therefore I will stretch out my hand against thee, and I will make thee a spoil to the nations; and I will utterly destroy thee from among the peoples, and I will completely cut thee off from out of the countries: and thou shalt know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 15:1–9; Jeremiah 25:21; Jeremiah 48:1–47"> Thus saith the Lord; Because Moab has said, Behold, are not the house of Israel and Judah like all the other nations?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:23; Joshua 13:17; Joshua 13:19–20"> Therefore, behold, I will weaken the shoulder of Moab from his frontier cities, even the choice land, the house of Beth-jeshimoth above the fountain of the city, by the seaside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:32; Psalms 83:3–6; Ezekiel 25:4"> I have given him the children of Kedem in addition to the children of Ammon for an inheritance, that there may be no remembrance of the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Ezekiel 30:19; Psalms 149:7"> And I will execute vengeance on Moab; and they shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:7–22; Ezekiel 25:8; Psalms 137:7"> Thus saith the Lord; Because of what the Edomites have done in taking vengeance on the house of Judah, and because they have remembered injuries, and have exacted full recompense;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:8; Malachi 1:3–4; Jeremiah 25:23"> therefore thus saith the Lord; I will also stretch out my hand upon Edom, and will utterly destroy out of it man and beast; and will make it desolate; and they that are pursued out of Teman shall fall by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 11:14; Genesis 27:29; Hebrews 10:30–31"> And I will execute my vengeance on Edom by the hand of my people Israel: and they shall deal in Edom according to mine anger and according to my wrath, and they shall know my vengeance, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:6; Isaiah 14:29–31; Ezekiel 25:12"> Therefore thus saith the Lord, Because the Philistines have wrought revengefully, and raised up vengeance rejoicing from their heart to destroy the Israelites to a man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings I 30:14; Zephaniah 2:4–15; Ezekiel 25:13"> therefore thus saith the Lord; Behold, I will stretch out my hand upon the Philistines, and will utterly destroy the Cretans, and will cut off the remnant that dwell by the seacoast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:11; Ezekiel 25:14; Ezekiel 5:15"> And I will execute great vengeance upon them; and they shall know that I am the Lord, when I have brought my vengeance upon them.</span>
