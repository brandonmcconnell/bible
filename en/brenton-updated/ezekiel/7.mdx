<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Moreover the word of the Lord came to me, saying, Also, thou, son of man, say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 8:2; 1 Peter 4:7; Ezekiel 7:3"> Thus saith the Lord;<br />An end is come to the land of Israel, the end is come on the four corners of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Ezekiel 5:13; Ezekiel 33:20"> The end is come</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Ezekiel 11:21; Ezekiel 5:11"> on thee, the inhabitant of the land: the time is come, the day has drawn nigh, not with tumult, nor with pangs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; Kings IV 21:12–13; Ezekiel 5:9"> Now I will pour out mine anger upon thee near at hand, and I will accomplish my wrath on thee: and I will judge thee for thy ways, and recompense upon thee all thine abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Ezekiel 7:3; 2 Peter 2:5"> Mine eye shall not spare, nor will I have any mercy: for I will recompense thy ways upon thee, and thine abominations shall be in the midst of thee; and thou shalt know that I am the Lord that smite thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:28; Ezekiel 7:12; Isaiah 22:5"> Now the end is come to thee, and I will send judgment upon thee: and I will take vengeance on thy ways, and will recompense all thine abominations upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:8; Ezekiel 14:19; Ezekiel 20:8"> Mine eye shall not spare, nor will I have any mercy: for I will recompense thy way upon thee, and thine abominations shall be in the midst of thee; and thou shalt know that I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 20:13; Micah 6:9; Isaiah 9:13"> For thus saith the Lord; Behold, the end is come.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 10:5; James 4:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> Behold, the day of the Lord! although the rod has blossomed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:18; Ezekiel 7:23; Isaiah 59:6–8"> pride has sprung up, and will break the staff of the wicked one, and that not with tumult, nor with haste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13–14; 1 Corinthians 7:29–31; Ezekiel 7:5–7"> The time is come, behold the day: let not the buyer rejoice, and let not the seller mourn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 25:24–28; Leviticus 25:31; Job 15:25"> For the buyer shall never again return to the seller, neither shall a man cleave with the eye of hope to his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:5; Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 51:27"> Sound ye the trumpet, and pass sentence on all together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:18; Ezekiel 5:12; Lamentations 1:20"> There shall be war with the sword without, and famine and pestilence within: he that is in the field shall die by the sword; and famine and pestilence shall destroy them that are in the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:11; Isaiah 38:14; Ezra and Nehemiah 9:15"> But they that escape of them shall be delivered, and shall be upon the mountains: and I will slay all the rest, every one for his iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Hebrews 12:12; Jeremiah 6:24"> All hands shall be completely weakened, and all thighs shall be defiled with moisture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 8:10; Isaiah 15:2–3; Isaiah 3:24"> And they shall gird themselves with sackcloth, and amazement shall cover them; and shame shall be upon them, even upon every face, and baldness upon every head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Zephaniah 1:18; Luke 12:19–20"> Their silver shall be cast forth in the streets, and their gold shall be despised: their souls shall not be satisfied, and their bellies shall not be filled: for it was the punishment of their iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:21; Jeremiah 7:30; Ezekiel 8:15–16"> As for their choice ornaments, they employed them for pride, and they made of them images of their abominations: therefore have I made them uncleanness to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings IV 24:13; Psalms 74:2–8; Chronicles II 36:18–19"> And I will deliver them into the hands of strangers to make them a prey, and to the pests of the earth for a spoil; and they shall profane them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:17; Psalms 74:18–23; Psalms 74:10–11"> And I will turn away my face from them, and they shall defile my charge, and shall go in to them unguardedly, and profane them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:9; Jeremiah 27:2; Ezekiel 11:6"> And they shall work uncleanness: because the land is full of strange nations, and the city is full of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Psalms 106:41; Ezekiel 21:31"> And I will turn back the boasting of their strength; and their holy things shall be defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:15–16; Isaiah 57:21; Lamentations 4:17–18"> And though propitiation shall come, and one shall seek peace, yet there shall be none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; Ezekiel 14:1; Micah 3:6"> There shall be woe upon woe, and there shall be message upon message; and a vision shall be sought from a prophet; but the law shall perish from the priest, and counsel from the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Ezekiel 7:4–8; Ezekiel 26:16"> The prince shall clothe himself with desolation, and the hands of the people of the land shall be made feeble: I will do to them according to their ways, and according to their judgments will I punish them; and they shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
