<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 11:2; Joshua 3:7; Numbers 13:16"> According to the word of Ephraim he adopted ordinances for himself in Israel; and he established them for Baal, and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 44:17–20; Kings III 19:18; Isaiah 46:6"> And now they have sinned increasingly, and have made for themselves a molten image of their silver, according to the fashion of idols, the work of artificers accomplished for them: they say, Sacrifice men, for the calves have come to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 6:4; Psalms 1:4; Psalms 68:2"> Therefore shall they be as a morning cloud, and as the early dew that passes away, as chaff blown away from the threshing floor, and as a vapor from tears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 45:21–22; Hosea 12:9; Isaiah 43:10–13"> But I am the Lord thy God that establishes the heaven, and creates the earth, whose hands have framed the whole host of heaven: but I showed them not to thee that thou shouldest go after them: and I brought thee up out of the land of Egypt, and thou shalt know no God but me; and there is no Savior beside me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:15; Deuteronomy 2:7; Deuteronomy 32:10"> I tended thee as a shepherd in the wilderness, in an uninhabited land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–15; Deuteronomy 8:12–14; Nehemiah 9:35"> According to their pastures, so they were completely filled; and their hearts were exalted; therefore they forgot me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 5:14; Jeremiah 5:6; Lamentations 3:10"> And I will be to them as a panther, and as a leopard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings II 17:8; Isaiah 5:29; Psalms 50:22"> I will meet them by the way of the Assyrians, as a she-bear excited, and I will rend the caul of their heart, and the lions’ whelps of the thicket shall devour them there; the wild beasts of the field shall rend them in pieces.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:17; Jeremiah 2:19; Deuteronomy 33:26"> O Israel, who will aid thee in thy destruction?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 8:4; Kings I 8:5–6; Hosea 10:3"> Where is this thy king? let him even save thee in all thy cities: let him judge thee, of whom thou saidst, Give me a king and a prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 10:19; Kings I 15:22–23; Kings III 14:7–16"> And I gave thee a king in mine anger, and kept him back in my wrath.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:34–35; Job 14:17; Romans 2:5"> Ephraim has framed a conspiracy of unrighteousness, his sin is hidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 37:3; Isaiah 13:8; Psalms 48:6"> Pains as of a woman in travail shall come upon him: he is thy wise son, because he shall not stay in the destruction of thy children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 21:4; Isaiah 26:19"> I will deliver them out of the power of Hades, and will redeem them from death: where is thy penalty, O death? O Hades, where is thy sting? comfort is hidden from mine eyes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:12; Ezekiel 17:10; Genesis 41:52"> Forasmuch as he will cause a division among his brethren, the Lord shall bring upon him an east wind from the desert, and shall dry up his veins and quite drain his fountains: he shall dry up his land, and spoil all his precious vessels.</span>
</div>
