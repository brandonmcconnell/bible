<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 11:12; Hosea 8:7; Ezekiel 17:10"> Ephraim has compassed me with falsehood, and the house of Israel and Judah with ungodliness: but now God knows them, and they shall be called God’s holy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 6:2; Isaiah 10:6; Hosea 4:1"> But Ephraim is an evil spirit, he has chased the east wind all the day: he has multiplied empty and vain things, and made a covenant with the Assyrians, and oil has gone in the way of traffic into Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:26; Genesis 32:24–28; Romans 9:11"> And the Lord has a controversy with Judah, in order to punish Jacob: according to his ways and according to his practices will he recompense him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:15; Malachi 3:1; Genesis 35:9–10"> He took his brother by the heel in the womb, and in his labors he had power with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:15; Isaiah 42:8; Psalms 135:13"> And he prevailed with the angel and was strong: they wept, and entreated me: they found me in the house of Aven, and there a word was spoken to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Hosea 14:1; Micah 7:7"> But the Lord God Almighty shall be his memorial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 6:10–11; Amos 8:5–6; Proverbs 11:1"> Thou therefore shalt return to thy God: keep thou mercy and judgment, and draw nigh to thy God continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Psalms 62:10; Psalms 52:7"> As for Canaan, in his hand is a balance of unrighteousness: he has loved to tyrannize.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40–43; Nehemiah 8:15–17; Hosea 13:4"> And Ephraim said, Nevertheless I am rich, I have found refreshment to myself. None of his labors shall be found available to him, by reason of the sins which he has committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings IV 17:13; Ezekiel 20:49; Jeremiah 19:1"> But I the Lord thy God brought thee up out of the land of Egypt: I will yet cause thee to dwell in tabernacles, according to the days of the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Hosea 6:8; Hosea 8:11"> And I will speak to the prophets, and I have multiplied visions, and by the means of the prophets I was represented.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:41; Genesis 27:43; Genesis 28:1–22"> If Gilead exists not, then the chiefs in Gilead when they sacrificed were false, and their altars were as heaps on the ground of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:50–51; Acts 3:22–23; Psalms 77:20"> And Jacob retreated into the plain of Syria, and Israel served for a wife, and waited for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:13; Daniel 11:18; Kings IV 17:7–18"> And the Lord brought Israel out of the land of Egypt by a prophet, and by a prophet was he preserved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Ephraim was angry and excited, therefore his blood shall be poured out upon him, and the Lord shall recompense to him his reproach.</span>
