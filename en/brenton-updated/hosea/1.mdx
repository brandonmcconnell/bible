<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 2:4–5; Hosea 3:1; Deuteronomy 31:16"> The beginning of the word of the Lord by Hosea. And the Lord said to Hosea, Go, take to thyself a wife of fornication, and children of fornication: for the land will surely go a-whoring in departing from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:1–3"> So he went and took Gomer, daughter of Diblaim; and she conceived, and bore him a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings IV 10:7–8; Kings IV 10:10–11; Ezekiel 23:10"> And the Lord said to him, Call his name Jezreel; for yet a little while, and I will avenge the blood of Jezreel on the house of Judah, and will make to cease the kingdom of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 6:33; Joshua 17:16; Hosea 2:18"> And it shall be, in that day, that I will break the bow of Israel in the Valley of Jezreel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; 1 Peter 2:10; Hosea 2:4"> And she conceived again, and bore a daughter. And he said to him, Call her name, Unpitied: for I will no more have mercy on the house of Israel, but will surely set myself in array against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6; Isaiah 12:2; Kings IV 19:35"> But I will have mercy on the house of Judah, and will save them by the Lord their God, and will not save them with bow, nor with sword, nor by war, nor by horses, nor by horsemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And she weaned Unpitied; and she conceived again, and bore a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:1"> And he said, Call his name, Not my people: for ye are not my people, and I am not your God.</span>
