<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 47:11; Jeremiah 8:17"> For suddenly there shall arise up those that bite him, and they that plot against thee shall awake, and thou shalt be a plunder to them.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Nahum 1:14; Kings IV 9:26; Jeremiah 36:31"> Thou hast devised shame to thy house, thou hast utterly destroyed many nations, and thy soul has sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:40; Joshua 24:27; Job 31:38–40"> For the stone shall cry out of the wall, and the beetle out of the timber shall speak.</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Isaiah 50:11; Proverbs 21:30"> Are not these things of the Lord Almighty? surely many people have been exhausted in the fire, and many nations have fainted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; Psalms 22:27; Psalms 72:19"> For the earth shall be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the Lord; it shall cover them as water.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 47:3; Nahum 3:5–6; Isaiah 51:21–23"> Drink thou also thy fill of disgrace instead of glory: shake, O heart, and quake, the cup of the right hand of the Lord has come round upon thee, and dishonor has gathered upon thy glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:8; Psalms 55:23; Zechariah 11:1"> For the ungodliness of Lebanon shall cover thee, and distress because of wild beasts shall dismay thee, because of the blood of men, and the sins of the land and city, and of all that dwell in it.</span>
</div>
