<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 27:13; Numbers 25:14–18; Revelation 6:10"> Avenge the wrongs of the children of Israel on the Midianites, and afterward thou shalt be added to thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Numbers 25:11; Kings IV 10:30"> And Moses spoke to the people, saying, Arm some of you, and set yourselves in array before the Lord against Midian, to inflict vengeance on Midian from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:8; Kings I 14:6; Judges 7:2"> Send a thousand of each tribe from all the tribes of the children of Israel to set themselves in array.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And they numbered of the thousands of Israel a thousand of each tribe, twelve thousands; these were armed for war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:44; Numbers 10:8–9; Chronicles II 13:12–15"> And Moses sent them away a thousand of every tribe with their forces, and Phinehas the son of Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest: and the holy instruments, and the signal trumpets were in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 21:11; Kings III 11:15–16; Kings I 27:9"> And they set themselves in array against Midian, as the Lord commanded Moses; and they slew every male.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:21–22; Numbers 25:15; Revelation 2:14"> And they slew the kings of Midian together with their slain subjects; even Evi and Rekem, and Zur, and Hur, and Reba, five kings of Midian; and they slew with the sword Balaam the son of Beor with their other slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Chronicles II 28:8–10; Chronicles II 28:5"> And they made a prey of the women of Midian, and their store, and their cattle, and all their possessions: and they spoiled their forces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; Kings I 30:1; Joshua 6:24"> And they burned with fire all their cities in the places of their habitation, and they burned their villages with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 8:2"> And they took all their plunder, and all their spoils, both man and beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:1"> And they brought to Moses and to Eleazar the priest, and to all the children of Israel, the captives, and the spoils, and the plunder, to the camp to Araboth Moab, which is at Jordan by Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; Kings I 30:21; Numbers 31:12"> And Moses and Eleazar the priest and all the rulers of the synagogue went forth out of the camp to meet them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings IV 13:19; Kings I 15:13–14; Numbers 31:48"> And Moses was angry with the captains of the host, the heads of thousands and the heads of hundreds who came from the battle array.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 15:3; Jeremiah 48:10; Deuteronomy 2:34"> And Moses said to them, Why have ye saved every female alive?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 2 Peter 2:15; Revelation 2:14"> For they were the occasion to the children of Israel by the word of Balaam of their revolting and despising the word of the Lord, because of Peor; and there was a plague in the congregation of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 21:11–12; Deuteronomy 20:16–18; Deuteronomy 7:2"> Now then slay every male in all the spoil, slay every woman, who has known the lying with man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:10–14; Chronicles II 28:8–10; Deuteronomy 20:14"> And as for all the captivity of women, who have not known the lying with man, save ye them alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 19:11–22; Numbers 5:2; Chronicles I 22:8"> And ye shall encamp outside the great camp seven days; everyone who has slain and who touches a dead body, shall be purified on the third day, and ye and your captivity shall purify yourselves on the seventh day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 19:22; Genesis 35:2; Exodus 19:10"> And ye shall purify every garment and every leathern utensil, and all furniture of goat skin, and every wooden vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 30:16"> And Eleazar the priest said to the men of the host that came from the battle array, This is the ordinance of the law which the Lord has commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Beside the gold, and the silver, and the brass, and the iron, and lead, and tin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 19:9; Numbers 19:17; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> everything that shall pass through the fire shall so be clean, nevertheless it shall be purified with the water of sanctification; and whatsoever will not pass through the fire shall pass through water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 15:13; Leviticus 14:9"> And on the seventh day ye shall wash your garments, and be clean; and afterward ye shall come into the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Take the sum of the spoils of the captivity both of man and beast, thou and Eleazar the priest, and the heads of the families of the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 22:8; Psalms 68:12; Kings I 30:24–25"> And ye shall divide the spoils between the warriors that went out to battle, and the whole congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 18:26; Numbers 31:30; Numbers 31:47"> And ye shall take a tribute for the Lord from the warriors that went out to battle; one soul out of five hundred, from the men, and from the cattle, even from the oxen, and from the sheep, and from the asses; and ye shall take from their half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 29:27; Deuteronomy 12:19; Deuteronomy 12:12"> And thou shalt give them to Eleazar the priest as the firstfruits of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 3:7–8; Numbers 3:25"> And from the half belonging to the children of Israel thou shalt take one in fifty from the men, and from the oxen, and from the sheep, and from the asses, and from all the cattle; and thou shalt give them to the Levites that keep the charges in the tabernacle of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Moses and Eleazar the priest did as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And that which remained of the spoil which the warriors took, was—of the sheep, six hundred and seventy-five thousand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and oxen, seventy-two thousand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> and asses, sixty-one thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And persons of women who had not known lying with man, all the souls, thirty-two thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And the half, even the portion of them that went out to war, from the number of the sheep, was three hundred and thirty-seven thousand and five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And the tribute to the Lord from the sheep was six hundred and seventy-five.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And the oxen, six and thirty thousand, and the tribute to the Lord, seventy-two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And asses, thirty thousand and five hundred, and the tribute to the Lord, sixty-one:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and the persons, sixteen thousand, and the tribute of them to the Lord, thirty-two souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 18:8; Numbers 18:19; Hebrews 7:9–12"> And Moses gave the tribute to the Lord, the heave offering of God, to Eleazar the priest, as the Lord commanded Moses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> from the half belonging to the children of Israel, whom Moses separated from the men of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And the half taken from the sheep, belonging to the congregation, was three hundred and thirty-seven thousand and five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And the oxen, thirty-six thousand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> asses, thirty thousand and five hundred;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> and persons, sixteen thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 31:30; Luke 10:1–8; Isaiah 56:10–11"> And Moses took of the half belonging to the children of Israel the fiftieth part, of men and of cattle, and he gave them to the Levites who keep the charges of the tabernacle of the Lord, as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And all those who were appointed to be officers of thousands of the host, captains of thousands and captains of hundreds, approached Moses, and said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 18:9; Kings I 30:18–19; Psalms 72:14"> Thy servants have taken the sum of the men of war with us, and not one is missing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 30:12; Exodus 30:15–16; Psalms 116:12"> And we have brought our gift to the Lord, every man who has found an article of gold, whether an armlet, or a chain, or a ring, or a bracelet, or a clasp for hair, to make atonement for us before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 7:2–6"> And Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold from them, even every wrought article.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And all the wrought gold, even the offering that they offered to the Lord, was sixteen thousand and seven hundred and fifty shekels from the captains of thousands and the captains of hundreds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:32"> For the men of war took plunder every one for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 30:16; Zechariah 6:14; Acts 10:4"> And Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold from the captains of thousands and captains of hundreds, and brought the vessels into the tabernacle of witness, a memorial of the children of Israel before the Lord.</span>
