<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 21:32; Numbers 32:3; Genesis 13:10–11"> And the children of Reuben and the children of Gad had a multitude of cattle, very great; and they saw the land of Jazer, and the land of Gilead; and the place was a place for cattle:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and the children of Reuben and the children of Gad came, and spoke to Moses, and to Eleazar the priest, and to the princes of the congregation, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:34; Isaiah 16:8–9; Numbers 32:34–38"> Ataroth, and Dibon, and Jazer, and Nimrah, and Heshbon, and Elealeh, and Sebam, and Nebo, and Beon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 21:34; Deuteronomy 2:24–35; Numbers 21:24"> the land which the Lord has delivered up before the children of Israel, is pasture land, and thy servants have cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ruth 2:10; Joshua 7:7; Jeremiah 31:2"> And they said, If we have found grace in thy sight, let this land be given to thy servants for a possession, and do not cause us to pass over Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:5; Kings II 11:11; Philippians 2:4"> And Moses said to the sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben, Shall your brethren go to war, and shall ye sit here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 13:27–4; Numbers 32:9; Deuteronomy 1:28"> And why do ye pervert the minds of the children of Israel, that they should not cross over into the land, which the Lord gives them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:19–25; Numbers 14:2; Numbers 13:2–26"> Did not your fathers thus, when I sent them from Kadesh-barnea to spy out the land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 13:23–10; Deuteronomy 1:24–28"> and they went up to the Valley of the Cluster, and spied the land, and turned aside the heart of the children of Israel, that they should not go into the land, which the Lord gave them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; Psalms 95:11; Ezekiel 20:15"> And the Lord was very angry in that day, and swore, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 14:28–30; Numbers 14:24; Numbers 26:2"> Surely these men who came up out of Egypt from twenty years old and upward, who know good and evil, shall not see the land which I swore to give to Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, for they have not closely followed after me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:36; Numbers 14:30; Numbers 14:24"> save Caleb the son of Jephunneh, who was set apart, and Joshua the son of Nun, for they closely followed after the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5; Numbers 14:33–35; Hebrews 3:16–19"> And the Lord was very angry with Israel; and for forty years he caused them to wander in the wilderness, until all the generation which did evil in the sight of the Lord was extinct.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 11:48; Psalms 78:57; Matthew 23:31–33"> Behold, ye are risen up in the room of your fathers, a combination of sinful men, to increase yet farther the fierce wrath of the Lord against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:11–12; Chronicles II 15:2; Chronicles II 7:19–22"> For ye will turn away from him to desert him yet once more in the wilderness, and ye will sin against this whole congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 33:17; Numbers 34:22"> And they came to him, and said, We will build here folds for our cattle, and cities for our possessions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 4:12–13; Numbers 32:29–32; Deuteronomy 3:18–20"> and we will arm ourselves and go as an advanced guard before the children of Israel, until we shall have brought them into their place; and our possessions shall remain in walled cities because of the inhabitants of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 22:1–5"> We will not return to our houses till the children of Israel shall have been distributed, each to his own inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 13:8; Joshua 12:1–6; Numbers 34:15"> And we will not any longer inherit with them from the other side of Jordan and onward, because we have our full inheritance on the side beyond Jordan eastward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 4:12–13; Deuteronomy 3:18–20; Joshua 1:13–15"> And Moses said to them, If ye will do according to these words, if ye will arm yourselves before the Lord for battle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and every one of you will pass over Jordan fully armed before the Lord, until his enemy be destroyed from before his face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 22:9; Joshua 22:4; Deuteronomy 3:20"> and the land shall be subdued before the Lord, then afterward ye shall return, and be guiltless before the Lord, and as regards Israel; and this land shall be to you for a possession before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 59:12; Proverbs 13:21; Genesis 44:16"> But if ye will not do so, ye will sin against the Lord; and ye shall know your sin, when afflictions shall come upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 32:16; Numbers 30:2; Numbers 32:34–42"> And ye shall build for yourselves cities for your store, and folds for your cattle; and ye shall do that which proceeds out of your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 1:13–14"> And the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad spoke to Moses, saying, Thy servants will do as our lord commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 1:14"> Our store, and our wives, and all our cattle shall be in the cities of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 4:12; Numbers 32:17; Numbers 12:11"> But thy servants will go over all armed and set in order before the Lord to battle, as our lord says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 1:13"> And Moses appointed to them for judges Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun, and the chiefs of the families of the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And Moses said to them, If the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad will pass over Jordan with you, every one armed for war before the Lord, and ye shall subdue the land before you, then ye shall give to them the land of Gilead for a possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 22:19"> But if they will not pass over armed with you to war before the Lord, then shall ye cause to pass over their possessions and their wives and their cattle before you into the land of Canaan, and they shall inherit with you in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad answered, saying, Whatsoever the Lord says to his servants, that will we do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> We will go over armed before the Lord into the land of Canaan, and ye shall give us our inheritance beyond Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 12:6; Deuteronomy 29:8; Deuteronomy 3:12–17"> And Moses gave to them, even to the sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben, and to the half tribe of Manasseh of the sons of Joseph, the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites, and the kingdom of Og king of Bashan, the land and its cities with its coasts, the cities of the land round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36; Numbers 32:3; Isaiah 17:2"> And the sons of Gad built Dibon, and Ataroth, and Aroer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 32:1; Numbers 32:3"> and Shophan, and Jazer, and they set them up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 32:3"> and Nimrah, and Beth-haran, strong cities, and folds for sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 21:27; Numbers 32:3; Isaiah 15:4"> And the sons of Reuben built Heshbon, and Elealeh, and Kiriathaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 46:1; Numbers 32:3; Joshua 23:7"> and Baal-meon, surrounded with walls, and Sibmah; and they called the names of the cities which they built, after their own names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 50:23; Joshua 17:1; Numbers 26:29"> And a son of Machir the son of Manasseh went to Gilead, and took it, and destroyed the Amorite who dwelt in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Joshua 17:1; Joshua 13:29–31; Deuteronomy 3:13–15"> And Moses gave Gilead to Machir the son of Manasseh, and he dwelt there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:14; Judges 10:4; Joshua 13:30"> And Jair the son of Manasseh went and took their villages, and called them the villages of Jair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 49:11; Kings II 18:18"> And Nobah went and took Kohath and her villages, and called them Nobah after his name.</span>
