<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For in the old time also, when the proud giants perished,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The hope of the world governed by thy hand escaped in a weak vessel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And left to all ages a seed of generation.</span><br /></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <span className="pl pl-1">But that which is made with hands is cursed, as well it, as he that made it:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He, because he made it: and it, because, being corruptible, it was called God.</span><br /></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">For neither were they from the beginning, neither shall they be forever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">For by the vain glory of men they entered into the world,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And therefore shall they come shortly to an end.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whom men could not honor in presence, because they dwelt far off,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They took the counterfeit of his visage from far,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And made an express image of a king whom they honored,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">To the end that by this their forwardness they might flatter him that was absent, as if he were present.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Also the singular diligence of the artificer</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Did help to set forward the ignorant to more superstition.</span><br /></span>
