<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:3–4; Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 3:1–35"> <span className="pl pl-1">A wise son keeps the law:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But he that keeps up debauchery dishonors his father.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 66:18; 2 Timothy 4:3–4; Zechariah 7:11–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that turns away his ear from hearing the law,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Even he has made his prayer abominable.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Proverbs 28:6; Revelation 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that walks justly is assisted:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But he that walks in crooked ways shall be entangled therein.</span><br /></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 16:10–12; Proverbs 13:11; Revelation 2:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">A man worthy of credit shall be much blessed:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But the wicked shall not be unpunished.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; Proverbs 18:5; Ezekiel 13:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that reverences not the persons of the just is not good:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Such a one will sell a man for a morsel of bread.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; 1 Timothy 6:9; Proverbs 28:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">An envious man makes haste to be rich,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And knows not that the merciful man will have the mastery over him.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5–6; Matthew 18:15; Psalms 141:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that reproves a man’s ways</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Shall have more favor than he that flatters with the tongue.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:4–6; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 19:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that casts off father or mother, and thinks he sins not;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The same is partaker with an ungodly man.</span><br /></span>
