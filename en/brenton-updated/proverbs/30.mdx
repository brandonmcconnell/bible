<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 31:1; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> These things says the man to them that trust in God; and I cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; 1 Corinthians 3:18; Isaiah 6:5"> For I am the most simple of all men, and there is not in me the wisdom of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:7; Ephesians 3:18–19; Romans 11:33"> God has taught me wisdom, and I know the knowledge of the holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 3:13; Ephesians 4:8–10; Job 26:8"> Who has gone up to heaven, and come down? who has gathered the winds in his &#42; bosom? who has wrapped up the waters in a garment? who has dominion of all the ends of the earth? what is his name? or what is the name of his children?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 18:30; James 3:17; Psalms 12:6"> For all the words of God are tried in the fire, and he defends those that reverence him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:2; Revelation 22:18–19"> Add not to his words, lest he reprove you, and you be made a liar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:42; Kings IV 2:9; Psalms 27:4"> Two things I ask of you; take not favor from me before I die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–8; Psalms 62:9–10; Psalms 119:37"> Remove far from me vanity and falsehood: and give me not wealth or poverty; but appoint me what is needful and sufficient:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 31:20; Ezekiel 16:49–50"> lest I be filled and become false, and say, Who sees me? or be poor and steal, and swear vainly by the name of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:4; Ecclesiastes 7:21; Deuteronomy 23:15"> Deliver not a servant into the hands of his master, lest he curse you, and you be utterly destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:20; Proverbs 30:17; Exodus 21:17"> A wicked generation curse their father, and do not bless their mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 18:11; Proverbs 16:2; 2 Timothy 3:5"> A wicked generation judge themselves to be just, but do not cleanse their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 6:17; Isaiah 2:11; Psalms 101:5"> A wicked generation have lofty eyes, and exalt themselves with their eyelids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 3:7; Psalms 14:4"> A wicked generation have swords for teeth and jaw teeth as knives, so as to destroy and devour the lowly from the earth, and the poor of them from among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 30:21; Proverbs 30:29; Proverbs 6:16"> The horse-leech had three dearly beloved daughters: and these three did not satisfy her; and the fourth was not contented so as to say, Enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:20; Habakkuk 2:5; Genesis 30:1"> The grave, and the love of a woman, and the earth not filled with water; water also and fire will not say, It is enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:22; Deuteronomy 21:18–21; Proverbs 30:11"> The eye that laughs to scorn a father, and dishonors the old age of a mother, let the ravens of the valleys pick it out, and let the young eagles devour it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 42:3; Psalms 139:6"> Moreover there are three things impossible for me to comprehend, and the fourth I know not:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Job 39:27; Exodus 22:16"> the track of a flying eagle; and the ways of a serpent on a rock; and the paths of a ship passing through the sea; and the ways of a man in youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 5:6; Proverbs 7:13–23; Numbers 5:11–30"> Such is the way of an adulterous woman, who having washed herself from what she has done, says she has done nothing &#42; amiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> By three things the earth is troubled, and the fourth it can’t bear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Ecclesiastes 10:7; Kings I 25:10–11"> if a servant reign; or a fool be filled with food;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 27:15; Proverbs 29:21"> or if a maidservant should cast out her own mistress; and if a hateful woman should marry a good man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 12:7"> And there are four very little things upon the earth, but these are wiser than the wise:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–8"> the ants which are weak, and yet prepare their food in summer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 104:18; Leviticus 11:5"> the rabbits also are a feeble race, who make their houses in the rocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Joel 1:6–7; Joel 2:7–11"> The locusts have no king, and yet march orderly at one command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the eft, which supports itself by its hands, and is easily taken, dwells in the fortresses of kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And there are three things which go well, and a fourth which passes along finely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 23:24; Judges 14:18"> A lion's whelp, stronger than all other beasts, which turns not away, nor fears any beast;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 3:15–18; Proverbs 20:2; Proverbs 16:14"> and a cock walking in boldly among the hens, and the goat leading the herd; and a king publicly speaking before a nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 26:12"> If you abandon yourself to mirth, and stretch forth your hand in a quarrel, you shall be disgraced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 29:22; Proverbs 28:25"> Milk out milk, and there shall be butter, and if you wing one's nostrils there shall come out blood: so if you extort words, there will come forth quarrels and strifes</span>
