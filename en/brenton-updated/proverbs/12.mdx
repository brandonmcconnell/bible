</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 5:6; Proverbs 11:20; Revelation 22:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lying lips are an abomination to the Lord;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But he that deals faithfully is accepted with him.</span><br /></span>
