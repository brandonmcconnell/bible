</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 18:5–17; Proverbs 11:13; Proverbs 20:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whenever thy friend shall reproach thee,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Retreat backward, despise him not;</span><br /></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Proverbs 12:18; James 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">As a club, and a dagger, and a pointed arrow,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So also is a man who bears false witness against his friend.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Ezekiel 29:6–7; Job 6:14–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">The way of the wicked and the foot of the transgressor shall perish in an evil day.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Ecclesiastes 3:4; Isaiah 58:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">As vinegar is bad for a sore,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So trouble befalling the body afflicts the heart.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings II 16:12; Matthew 10:13; 1 Corinthians 15:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">For so doing thou shalt heap coals of fire upon his head,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the Lord shall reward thee with good.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 37:22; Psalms 15:3; Romans 1:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">The north wind raises clouds;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So an impudent face provokes the tongue.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 21:9; Proverbs 27:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is better to dwell on a corner of the roof,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Than with a railing woman in an open house.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 15:30; Romans 10:15; Psalms 63:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">As cold water is agreeable to a thirsting soul,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So is a good message from a land far off.</span><br /></span>
