<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:13–16; Proverbs 16:11; Proverbs 20:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">False balances are an abomination before the Lord:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But a just weight is acceptable unto him.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 5:22; Psalms 9:15–16; Proverbs 1:31–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Righteousness traces out blameless paths:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But ungodliness encounters unjust dealing.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:8; Kings III 2:44; Psalms 7:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The righteousness of upright men delivers them:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But transgressors are caught in their own destruction.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 10:28; Job 8:13–14; Job 11:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">At the death of a just man his hope does not perish:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But the boast of the ungodly perishes.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 6:23–24; Proverbs 21:18; Esther 7:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">A righteous man escapes from a snare,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the ungodly man is delivered up in his place.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 2:10–16; Proverbs 4:5–6; 1 John 2:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the mouth of ungodly men is a snare to citizens:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But the understanding of righteous men is prosperous.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 28:12; Esther 8:15–16; Psalms 58:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the prosperity of righteous men a city prospers:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 14:34; Proverbs 29:8; James 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">But by the mouths of ungodly men it is overthrown.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 10:19; 1 Peter 2:23; Luke 18:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">A man void of understanding sneers at his fellow citizens:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But a sensible man is quiet.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 20:19; Proverbs 25:9; Leviticus 19:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">A double-tongued man discloses the secret counsels of an assembly:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But he that is faithful in spirit conceals matters.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 24:6; Proverbs 15:22; Proverbs 20:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">They that have no guidance fall like leaves:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But in much counsel there is safety.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 6:1–5; Proverbs 22:26–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">A bad man does harm wherever he meets a just man:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And he hates the sound of safety.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Matthew 25:34–40; James 2:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">A merciful man does good to his own soul:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But the merciless destroys his own body.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 6:8–9; Ecclesiastes 10:8; Hosea 10:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">An ungodly man performs unrighteous works:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But the seed of the righteous is a reward of truth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 10:16; Romans 2:8–9; Proverbs 12:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">A righteous son is born for life:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But the persecution of the ungodly ends in death.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 119:1; Proverbs 21:29; Chronicles I 29:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Perverse ways are an abomination to the Lord:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But all they that are blameless in their ways are acceptable to him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 112:1–2; Acts 2:39; Jeremiah 32:39"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that unjustly strikes hands shall not be unpunished:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But he that sows righteousness shall receive a faithful reward.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–4; Proverbs 31:30; Nahum 3:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">As an ornament in a swine’s snout,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So is beauty to an ill-minded woman.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Romans 2:8–9; Matthew 5:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the desire of the righteous is good:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But the hope of the ungodly shall perish.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Luke 6:38; Deuteronomy 15:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">There are some who scatter their own, and make it more:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And there are some also who gather, yet have less.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; 2 Corinthians 9:6–9; Isaiah 58:7–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Every sincere soul is blessed:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But a passionate man is not graceful.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 29:13; Genesis 42:6; Amos 8:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">May he that hoards corn leave it to the nation:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But blessing be on the head of him that gives it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 7:15–16; Proverbs 17:11; Psalms 9:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that devises good counsels seeks good favor:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But as for him that seeks after evil, evil shall overtake him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Jeremiah 17:8; Psalms 52:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that trusts in wealth shall fall;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But he that helps righteous men shall rise.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:16; Proverbs 14:19; Proverbs 15:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that deals not graciously with his own house shall inherit the wind;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the fool shall be servant to the wise man.</span><br /></span>
