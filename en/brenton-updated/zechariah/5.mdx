<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:1–49; Revelation 17:1–18; Jeremiah 3:1–2"> And behold a talent of lead lifted up: and behold a woman sat in the midst of the measure.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:49; Hosea 8:1; Daniel 9:26–27"> And I lifted up mine eyes, and saw, and, behold, two women coming forth, and the wind was in their wings; and they had stork’s wings: and they lifted up the measure between the earth and the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And I said to the angel that spoke with me, Whither do these carry away the measure?</span>
