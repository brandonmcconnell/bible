<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 13:9; Isaiah 2:12; Isaiah 13:6"> Behold, the days of the Lord come, and thy spoils shall be divided in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 21:20–24; Isaiah 13:16; Romans 9:27–29"> And I will gather all the Gentiles to Jerusalem to war, and the city shall be taken, and the houses plundered, and the women ravished; and half of the city shall go forth into captivity, but the rest of my people shall not be utterly cut off from the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 12:9; Zephaniah 3:19; Zechariah 2:8–9"> And the Lord shall go forth, and fight with those Gentiles as when he fought in the day of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 1:3–4; Ezekiel 11:23; Acts 1:11–12"> And his feet shall stand in that day on the mount of Olives, which is before Jerusalem on the east, and the mount of Olives shall cleave asunder, half of it toward the east and the west, a very great division; and half the mountain shall lean to the north, and half of it to the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jude 1:14–15; Amos 1:1; Matthew 25:31"> And the valley of my mountains shall be closed up, and the valley of the mountains shall be joined on to Azal, and shall be blocked up as it was blocked up in the days of the earthquake, in the days of Uzziah king of Judah; and the Lord my God shall come, and all the saints with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:10–11; Psalms 112:4; John 12:46"> And it shall come to pass in that day that there shall be no light,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 22:5; 1 Thessalonians 5:2; Daniel 12:4"> and there shall be for one day cold and frost, and that day shall be known to the Lord, and it shall not be day nor night: but toward evening it shall be light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 22:1–2; John 7:38; John 4:14"> And in that day living water shall come forth out of Jerusalem; half of it toward the former sea, and half of it toward the latter sea: and so shall it be in summer and spring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5–6; Revelation 11:15; Daniel 7:27"> And the Lord shall be king over all the earth: in that day there shall be one Lord, and his name one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Joshua 15:32; Kings III 15:22"> compassing all the earth, and the wilderness from Geba unto Rimmon south of Jerusalem. And Rama shall remain in its place. From the gate of Benjamin to the place of the first gate, to the gate of the corners, and to the tower of Hananel, as far as the king’s winepresses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 22:3; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Amos 9:15"> they shall dwell in the city; and there shall be no more any curse, and Jerusalem shall dwell securely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:17–20; Isaiah 66:15–16; Micah 4:11–13"> And this shall be the overthrow with which the Lord will smite all the nations, as many as have fought against Jerusalem; their flesh shall consume away while they are standing upon their feet, and their eyes shall melt out of their holes, and their tongue shall consume away in their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 12:4; Revelation 17:12–17; Judges 7:22"> And there shall be in that day a great panic from the Lord upon them; and they shall lay hold every man of the hand of his neighbor, and his hand shall be clasped with the hand of his neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 23:18; Zechariah 12:2; Chronicles II 20:25–27"> Judah also shall fight in Jerusalem; and God shall gather the strength of all the nations round about, gold, and silver, and apparel, in great abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 14:12"> And this shall be the overthrow of the horses, and mules, and camels, and asses, and all the beasts that are in those camps, according to this overthrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 14:17–19; Isaiah 66:23; Isaiah 66:18–21"> And it shall come to pass, that whosoever shall be left of all the nations that came against Jerusalem, shall even come up every year to worship the king, the Lord Almighty, and to keep the feast of tabernacles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Amos 4:7–8; Isaiah 60:12; Jeremiah 14:4"> And it shall come to pass, that whosoever of all the families of the earth shall not come up to Jerusalem to worship the king, the Lord Almighty, even these shall be added to the others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 14:15; Deuteronomy 11:10–11; Zechariah 14:12"> And if the family of Egypt shall not go up, nor come; then upon them shall be the overthrow with which the Lord shall smite all the nations, whichever of them shall not come up to keep the feast of tabernacles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 3:19"> This shall be the sin of Egypt, and the sin of all the nations, whosoever shall not come up to keep the feast of tabernacles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16–17; Exodus 39:30; Leviticus 8:9"> In that day there shall be upon the bridle of every horse Holiness to the Lord Almighty; and the caldrons in the house of the Lord shall be as bowls before the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:9; Romans 14:6–7; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> And every pot in Jerusalem and in Judah shall be holy to the Lord Almighty: and all that sacrifice shall come and take of them, and shall seethe meat in them: and in that day there shall be no more the Canaanite in the house of the Lord Almighty.</span>
