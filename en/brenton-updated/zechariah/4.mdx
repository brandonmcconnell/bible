<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 11:4; Zechariah 4:14; Romans 11:17"> and two olive trees above it, one on the right of the bowl, and one on the left.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 4:13; Zechariah 1:9; Daniel 2:30"> And the angel that talked with me answered, and spoke to me, saying, Knowest thou not what these things are? And I said, No, my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles II 32:7–8; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; 1 Corinthians 2:4–5"> And he answered and spoke to me, saying, This is the word of the Lord to Zerubbabel, saying, Not by mighty power, nor by strength, but by my Spirit, saith the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:25; Zechariah 4:9; Micah 4:1"> Who art thou, the great mountain before Zerubbabel, that thou shouldest prosper? whereas I will bring out the stone of the inheritance, the grace of it the equal of my grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 6:14–15; Zechariah 6:12–13; Zechariah 6:15"> The hands of Zerubbabel have laid the foundation of this house, and his hands shall finish it: and thou shalt know that the Lord Almighty has sent me to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 8:7; 1 Corinthians 1:28–29; Chronicles II 16:9"> For who has despised the small days? surely they shall rejoice, and shall see the plummet of tin in the hand of Zerubbabel: these are the seven eyes that look upon all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 4:3; Revelation 11:4"> And I answered, and said to him, What are these two olive trees, which are on the right and left hand of the candlestick?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 11:4; Matthew 20:23; Haggai 1:1"> And I asked the second time, and said to him, What are the two branches of the olive trees that are by the side of the two golden pipes that pour into and communicate with the golden oil funnels?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 4:5; Hebrews 5:11–12"> And he said to me, Knowest thou not what these are? and I said, No, my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 11:4; Isaiah 61:1–3; Daniel 9:24–26"> And he said, These are the two anointed ones that stand by the Lord of the whole earth.</span>
