<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings IV 11:21; Kings IV 8:26; Kings IV 11:4"> In the twenty-third year of Joash son of Ahaziah king of Judah began Jehoahaz the son of Jehu to reign in Samaria, and he reigned seventeen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings III 12:26–33; Kings III 14:16; Kings IV 10:29"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, and walked after the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who led Israel to sin; he departed not from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings III 19:17; Kings IV 13:24–25; Judges 2:14"> And the Lord was very angry with Israel, and delivered them into the hand of Hazael king of Syria, and into the hand of the son of Hadad son of Hazael, all their days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings IV 14:26; Exodus 3:7; Psalms 78:34"> And Jehoahaz besought the Lord, and the Lord hearkened to him, for he saw the affliction of Israel, because the king of Syria afflicted them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:27; Kings IV 14:25; Kings IV 14:27"> And the Lord gave deliverance to Israel, and they escaped from under the hand of Syria: and the children of Israel dwelt in their tents as heretofore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 16:33; Kings IV 13:2; Deuteronomy 7:5"> Only they departed not from the sins of the house of Jeroboam, who led Israel to sin: they walked in them—moreover the grove also remained in Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 1:3; Kings IV 10:32; Kings III 20:15"> Whereas there was not left any army to Jehoahaz, except fifty horsemen, and ten chariots, and ten thousand infantry: for the king of Syria had destroyed them, and they made them as dust for trampling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 11:4; Kings III 14:29; Kings III 14:31"> And the rest of the acts of Jehoahaz, and all that he did, and his mighty acts are not these things written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings IV 10:35; Kings IV 13:13; Kings IV 14:8"> And Jehoahaz slept with his fathers, and they buried him in Samaria: and Joash his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> In the thirty-seventh year of Jehoash king of Judah, Joash the son of Jehoahaz began to reign over Israel in Samaria sixteen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings IV 13:2; Kings IV 10:29; Kings IV 3:3"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord; he departed not from all the sin of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who led Israel to sin: he walked in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 13:14–25; Kings IV 14:8–16; Chronicles II 25:17–24"> And the rest of the acts of Joash, and all that he did, and his mighty acts which he performed together with Amaziah king of Judah, are not these written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 1:21; Kings IV 14:28–29; Kings III 2:10"> And Joash slept with his fathers, and Jeroboam sat upon his throne, and he was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings IV 2:12; Philippians 2:26; Genesis 48:1"> Now Elisha was sick of his sickness, whereof he died: and Joash king of Israel went down to him, and wept over his face, and said, My father, my father, the chariot of Israel, and the horseman thereof!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Elisha said to him, Take bow and arrows. And he took to himself a bow and arrows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings IV 4:34; Genesis 49:24; Psalms 144:1"> And he said to the king, Put thy hand on the bow. And Joash put his hand upon it: and Elisha put his hands upon the king’s hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 20:26; Kings IV 5:10–14; 1 Corinthians 1:18"> And he said, Open the window eastward. And he opened it. And Elisha said, Shoot. And he shot. And Elisha said, The arrow of the Lord’s deliverance, and the arrow of deliverance from Syria; and thou shalt smite the Syrians in Aphek until thou have consumed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings IV 4:6; Exodus 17:11; Isaiah 20:2–4"> And Elisha said to him, Take bow and arrows. And he took them. And he said to the king of Israel, Smite upon the ground. And the king smote three times, and stayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings IV 13:25; Numbers 16:15; Kings IV 4:40"> And the man of God was grieved at him, and said, If thou hadst smitten five or six times, then thou shouldest have smitten Syria till thou hadst consumed them; but now thou shalt smite Syria only thrice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings IV 24:2; Kings IV 3:7; Kings IV 6:23"> And Elisha died, and they buried him. And the bands of the Moabites came into the land, at the beginning of the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:1–10; Acts 19:12; John 5:28–29"> And it came to pass as they were burying a man, that behold, they saw a band of men, and they cast the man into the grave of Elisha: and as soon as he touched the bones of Elisha, he revived and stood up on his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings IV 8:12; Kings IV 13:3–7; Psalms 106:40–42"> And Hazael greatly afflicted Israel all the days of Jehoahaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 105:8; Kings IV 14:27; Micah 7:18–20"> And the Lord had mercy and compassion upon them, and had respect to them because of his covenant with Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob; and the Lord would not destroy them, and did not cast them out from his presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 125:3; Luke 18:7"> And Hazael king of Syria died, and the son of Hadad his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings IV 13:18–19; Kings IV 10:32; Kings IV 14:25"> And Jehoash the son of Jehoahaz returned, and took the cities out of the hand of the son of Hadad the son of Hazael, which he had taken out of the hand of Jehoahaz his father in the war: thrice did Joash smite him, and he recovered the cities of Israel.</span>
