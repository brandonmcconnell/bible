<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 1:1; Jeremiah 25:1; Jeremiah 25:9"> In his days went up Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and Jehoiakim became his servant three years; and then he turned and revolted from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:11; Kings IV 23:27; Kings IV 21:12–14"> And the Lord sent against him the bands of the Chaldeans, and the bands of Syria, and the bands of Moab, and the bands of the children of Ammon, and sent them into the land of Judah to prevail against it, according to the word of the Lord, which he spoke by his servants the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings IV 18:25; Kings IV 21:2–11; Kings IV 23:26–27"> Moreover it was the purpose of the Lord concerning Judah, to remove them from his presence, because of the sins of Manasseh, according to all that he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings IV 21:16; Psalms 106:38; Numbers 35:33"> Moreover he shed innocent blood, and filled Jerusalem with innocent blood, and the Lord would not pardon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:8; Jeremiah 26:1–24; Jeremiah 22:13–17"> And the rest of the acts of Jehoiakim, and all that he did, behold, are not these written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18–19; Chronicles II 36:6; Jeremiah 36:30"> And Jehoiakim slept with his fathers: and Jehoiachin his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:5–7; Jeremiah 46:2; Genesis 15:18"> And the king of Egypt came no more out of his land: for the king of Babylon took away all that belonged to the king of Egypt from the river of Egypt as far as the river Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:9; Chronicles I 3:16; Jeremiah 37:1"> Eighteen years old was Jehoiachin when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem: and his mother’s name was Nehushta, daughter of Elnathan, of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his father did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings IV 25:2; Daniel 1:1–2"> At that time went up Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon to Jerusalem, and the city was besieged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came against the city, and his servants besieged it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:1; Jeremiah 52:28; Kings IV 25:27"> And Jehoiachin king of Judah came forth to the king of Babylon, he and his servants, and his mother, and his princes, and his eunuchs; and the king of Babylon took him in the eighth year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 39:6; Kings IV 20:17; Jeremiah 20:5"> And he brought forth thence all the treasures of the house of the Lord, and the treasures of the king’s house, and he cut up all the golden vessels which Solomon the king of Israel had made in the temple of the Lord, according to the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:28; Kings IV 25:12; Kings IV 24:16"> And he carried away the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and all the captains, and the mighty men, taking captive ten thousand prisoners, and every artificer and smith: and only the poor of the land were left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:10; Jeremiah 22:24–28; Esther 2:6"> And he carried Jehoiachin away to Babylon, and the king’s mother, and the king’s wives, and his eunuchs: and he carried away the mighty men of the land into captivity from Jerusalem to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:28; Kings IV 24:14; Jeremiah 29:2"> And all the men of might, even seven thousand, and one thousand artificers and smiths: all were mighty men fit for war; and the king of Babylon carried them captive to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:1; Chronicles II 36:4; Kings IV 23:34"> And the king of Babylon made Mattaniah his son king in his stead, and called his name Zedekiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings IV 23:31; Jeremiah 52:1–11; Jeremiah 37:1"> Twenty and one years old was Zedekiah when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem: and his mother’s name was Hamutal, daughter of Jeremiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings IV 23:37; Jeremiah 24:8; Ezekiel 21:25"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, according to all that Jehoiachin did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:13; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Jeremiah 38:17–21"> For it was according to the Lord’s anger against Jerusalem and on Judah, until he cast them out of his presence, that Zedekiah revolted against the king of Babylon.</span>
