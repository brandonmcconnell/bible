<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:27; Romans 15:18; Proverbs 21:31"> Now Naaman, the captain of the host of Syria, was a great man before his master, and highly respected, because by him the Lord had given deliverance to Syria, and the man was mighty in strength, but a leper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings IV 6:23; Kings IV 13:20; Kings I 13:17–18"> And the Syrians went forth in small bands, and took captive out of the land of Israel a little maid: and she waited on Naaman’s wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 26:29; Luke 17:12–14; Numbers 11:29"> And she said to her mistress, O that my lord were before the prophet of God in Samaria; then he would recover him from his leprosy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:26–27; John 4:28–29; John 1:42–46"> And she went in and told her lord, and said, Thus and thus spoke the maid from the land of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 14:12; Kings IV 8:8–9; Numbers 22:17–18"> And the king of Syria said to Naaman, Go to, go, and I will send a letter to the king of Israel. And he went, and took in his hand ten talents of silver, and six thousand pieces of gold, and ten changes of raiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he brought the letter to the king of Israel, saying, Now then, as soon as this letter shall reach thee, behold, I have sent to thee my servant Naaman, and thou shalt recover him from his leprosy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings I 2:6; Kings III 20:7; Genesis 30:2"> And it came to pass, when the king of Israel read the letter, that he rent his garments, and said, Am I God, to kill and to make alive, that this man sends to me to recover a man of his leprosy? consider, however, I pray you, and see that this man seeks an occasion against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 18:36–37; Hosea 12:13; Ezekiel 2:5"> And it came to pass, when Elisha heard that the king of Israel had rent his garments, that he sent to the king of Israel, saying, Wherefore hast thou rent thy garments? Let Naaman, I pray thee, come to me, and let him know that there is a prophet in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 16:37–39; Kings IV 6:32; Isaiah 60:14"> So Naaman came with horse and chariot, and stood at the door of the house of Elisha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 9:7; 1 Corinthians 6:11; Leviticus 14:7"> And Elisha sent a messenger to him, saying, Go and wash seven times in Jordan, and thy flesh shall return to thee, and thou shalt be cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 15:27; Luke 14:11; Matthew 8:8"> And Naaman was angry, and departed, and said, Behold, I said, He will by all means come out to me, and stand, and call on the name of his God, and lay his hand upon the place, and recover the leper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 1:9; Kings IV 5:17; Kings IV 2:14"> Are not Abana and Pharpar, rivers of Damascus, better than all the waters of Israel? may I not go and wash in them, and be cleansed? and he turned and went away in a rage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings IV 6:21; Kings IV 13:14; Kings IV 2:12"> And his servants came near and said to him, Suppose the prophet had spoken a great thing to thee, wouldest thou not perform it? yet he has but said to thee, Wash, and be cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 33:25; Luke 4:27; Luke 5:13"> So Naaman went down, and dipped himself seven times in Jordan, according to the word of Elisha: and his flesh returned to him as the flesh of a little child, and he was cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 2:47; Daniel 6:26–27; Joshua 2:9–11"> And he and all his company returned to Elisha, and he came and stood before him, and said, Behold, I know that there is no God in all the earth, save only in Israel: and now receive a blessing of thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings IV 5:26; Kings IV 5:20; Kings IV 3:14"> And Elisha said, As the Lord lives, before whom I stand, I will not take one. And he pressed him to take one: but he would not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 26:18; Kings IV 5:12; Romans 14:1"> And Naaman said, Well then, if not, let there be given to thy servant, I pray thee, the load of a yoke of mules; and thou shalt give me of the red earth: for henceforth thy servant will not offer whole burnt offering or sacrifice to other gods, but only to the Lord by reason of this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings IV 7:2; Kings IV 7:17; Kings IV 17:35"> And let the Lord be propitious to thy servant when my master goes into the house of Rimmon to worship there, and he shall lean on my hand, and I shall bow down in the house of Rimmon when he bows down in the house of Rimmon; even let the Lord, I pray, be merciful to thy servant in this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings I 1:17; Mark 5:34; Exodus 4:18"> And Elisha said to Naaman, Go in peace. And he departed from him a little way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings IV 4:12; Kings IV 4:31; Kings IV 6:31"> And Gehazi the servant of Elisha said, Behold, my Lord has spared this Syrian Naaman, so as not to take of his hand what he has brought: as the Lord lives, I will surely run after him, and take somewhat of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 7:6–7; Kings IV 9:17–22; Kings IV 4:26"> So Gehazi followed after Naaman: and Naaman saw him running after him, and turned back from his chariot to meet him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings IV 5:5; Isaiah 59:3; Acts 5:3–4"> And Gehazi said, All is well: my master has sent me, saying, Behold, now are there come to me two young men of the sons of the prophets from Mount Ephraim; give them, I pray thee, a talent of silver, and two changes of raiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings IV 12:10; Kings III 20:7; Kings IV 2:17"> And Naaman said, Take two talents of silver. And he took two talents of silver in two bags, and two changes of raiment, and put them upon two of his servants, and they bore them before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings III 21:16; Joshua 7:11–12; Joshua 7:1"> And he came to a secret place, and took them from their hands, and laid them up in the house, and dismissed the men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings IV 5:22; Genesis 16:8; Proverbs 30:20"> And he went in himself and stood before his master; and Elisha said to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 2:5; 1 Corinthians 5:3; Kings IV 6:12"> Whence comest thou, Gehazi? and Gehazi said, Thy servant has not been hither or thither. And Elisha said to him, Went not my heart with thee, when the man returned from his chariot to meet thee? and now thou hast received silver, and now thou hast received raiment, and olive yards, and vineyards, and sheep, and oxen, and menservants, and maidservants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 4:6; Numbers 12:10; Kings IV 15:5"> The leprosy also of Naaman shall cleave to thee, and to thy seed forever. And he went out from his presence leprous, like snow.</span>
