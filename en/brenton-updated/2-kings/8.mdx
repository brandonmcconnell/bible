<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Haggai 1:11; Kings IV 4:31–35; Ruth 1:1"> And Elisha spoke to the woman, whose son he had restored to life, saying, Arise, and go thou and thy house, and sojourn wherever thou mayest sojourn: for the Lord has called for a famine upon the land; indeed it is come upon the land for seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; Judges 3:3; Kings I 27:1–3"> And the woman arose, and did according to the word of Elisha, both she and her house; and they sojourned in the land of the Philistines seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:16; Luke 18:3–5; Kings IV 4:13"> And it came to pass after the expiration of the seven years, that the woman returned out of the land of the Philistines to the city; and came to cry to the king for her house and for her lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings IV 5:20–27; Kings IV 2:14; Kings IV 6:32"> And the king spoke to Gehazi the servant of Elisha the man of God, saying, Tell me, I pray thee, all the great things which Elisha has done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 4:35; Matthew 10:29–30; Kings IV 6:26"> And it came to pass, as he was telling the king how he had restored to life the dead son, behold, the woman whose son Elisha restored to life came crying to the king for her house and for her lands. And Gehazi said, My lord, O king, this is the woman, and this is her son, whom Elisha restored to life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Proverbs 21:1; Kings II 9:7"> And the king asked the woman, and she told him: and the king appointed her a eunuch, saying, Restore all that was hers, and all the fruits of the field from the day that she left the land until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings III 11:24; Kings IV 6:24; Kings III 20:1"> And Elisha came to Damascus; and the king of Syria the son of Hadad was ill, and they brought him word, saying, The man of God is come hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 19:15; Kings IV 1:2; Kings I 9:7"> And the king said to Hazael, Take in thy hand a present, and go to meet the man of God, and inquire of the Lord by him, saying, Shall I recover of this my disease?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings IV 5:13; Kings IV 13:14; Kings III 19:15"> And Hazael went to meet him, and he took a present in his hand, and all the good things of Damascus, forty camels’ load, and came and stood before him, and said to Elisha, Thy son the son of Hadad, the king of Syria, has sent me to thee to inquire, saying, Shall I recover of this my disease?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 7:7; Genesis 2:17; Amos 7:1"> And Elisha said, Go, say, Thou shalt certainly live; yet the Lord has shown me that thou shalt surely die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Romans 9:2; Jeremiah 9:18"> And he stood before him, and fixed his countenance till he was ashamed: and the man of God wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; Nahum 3:10; Kings IV 15:16"> And Hazael said, Why does my lord weep? And he said, Because I know all the evil that thou wilt do to the children of Israel: thou wilt utterly destroy their strongholds with fire, and thou wilt slay their choice men with the sword, and thou wilt dash their infants against the ground, and their women with child thou wilt rip up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings I 17:43; Kings III 19:15; Kings II 9:8"> And Hazael said, Who is thy servant? a dead dog, that he should do this thing? And Elisha said, The Lord has shown me thee ruling over Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings IV 8:10; Matthew 26:16; Kings IV 5:25"> And he departed from Elisha, and went in to his lord; and he said to him, What said Elisha to thee? and he said, He said to me, Thou shalt surely live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings IV 8:13; Kings III 15:28; Kings III 11:26–37"> And it came to pass on the next day that he took a thick cloth, and dipped it in water, and put it on his face, and he died: and Hazael reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings IV 1:17; Kings IV 3:1; Chronicles II 21:1–20"> In the fifth year of Joram son of Ahab king of Israel, and while Jehoshaphat was king of Judah, Jehoram the son of Jehoshaphat king of Judah began to reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles II 21:5–10"> Thirty and two years old was he when he began to reign, and he reigned eight years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings IV 8:26; Kings III 11:1–5; Chronicles II 21:13"> And he walked in the way of the kings of Israel, as did the house of Ahab; for the daughter of Ahab was his wife: and he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings III 11:36; Kings II 7:12–13; Luke 1:32–33"> But the Lord would not destroy Judah for David his servant’s sake, as he said he would give a light to him and to his sons continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings III 22:47; Kings IV 3:9; Kings IV 8:22"> In his days Edom revolted from under the hand of Judah, and they made a king over themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings II 18:17"> And Joram went up to Zair, and all the chariots that were with him: and it came to pass after he had arisen, that he smote Edom who compassed him about, and the captains of the chariots; and the people fled to their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings IV 19:8; Joshua 21:13; Genesis 27:40"> Yet Edom revolted from under the hand of Judah till this day. Then Libnah revolted at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings IV 15:36; Kings III 11:41; Chronicles II 21:11–20"> And the rest of the acts of Joram, and all that he did, behold, are not these written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Chronicles II 22:6; Chronicles II 25:23; Chronicles II 21:1"> So Joram slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the city of his father David: and Ahaziah his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings IV 9:29; Kings IV 8:16–17; Chronicles II 21:20–6"> In the twelfth year of Joram son of Ahab king of Israel, Ahaziah son of Jehoram began to reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Chronicles II 22:2; Kings IV 9:21–27; Kings IV 11:1"> Twenty and two years old was Ahaziah when he began to reign, and he reigned one year in Jerusalem: and the name of his mother was Athaliah, daughter of Omri king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Chronicles II 22:3–4; 2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> And he walked in the way of the house of Ahab, and did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, as did the house of Ahab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Chronicles II 22:5; Kings III 4:13; Chronicles II 19:2"> And he went with Joram the son of Ahab to war against Hazael king of the Syrians in Ramoth-gilead; and the Syrians wounded Joram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings IV 9:15–16; Kings IV 8:28; Chronicles II 22:5–7"> And King Joram returned to be healed in Jezreel of the wounds with which they wounded him in Ramah, when he fought with Hazael king of Syria. And Ahaziah son of Jehoram went down to see Joram the son of Ahab in Jezreel, because he was sick.</span>
