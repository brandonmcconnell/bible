<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 3:5; Exodus 16:12; Ezekiel 37:4"> And Elisha said, Hear thou the word of the Lord; Thus saith the Lord, As at this time, tomorrow a measure of fine flour shall be sold for a shekel, and two measures of barley for a shekel, in the gates of Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 18:12–14; Genesis 7:11; Malachi 3:10"> And the officer on whose hand the king rested, answered Elisha, and said, Behold, if the Lord shall make floodgates in heaven, might this thing be? and Elisha said, Behold, thou shalt see with thine eyes, but shalt not eat thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46; Numbers 5:2–4; Kings IV 7:4"> And there were four leprous men by the gate of the city: and one said to his neighbor, Why sit we here until we die?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:18; Luke 15:17–19; Kings II 14:14"> If we should say, Let us go into the city, then there is famine in the city, and we shall die there: and if we sit here, then we shall die. Now then come, and let us fall upon the camp of the Syrians: if they should take us alive, then we shall live; and if they should put us to death, then we shall only die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:7; Deuteronomy 32:25; Kings I 30:17"> And they rose up while it was yet night, to go into the camp of Syria; and they came into a part of the camp of Syria, and behold, there was no man there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings II 5:24; Isaiah 31:1; Kings III 10:29"> For the Lord had made the army of Syria to hear a sound of chariots, and a sound of horses, even the sound of a great host: and each man said to his fellow, Now has the king of Israel hired against us the kings of the Hittites, and the kings of Egypt, to come against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Psalms 48:4–6; Hebrews 6:18"> And they arose and fled while it was yet dark, and left their tents, and their horses, and their asses in the camp, as they were, and fled for their lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Joshua 7:21; Matthew 25:18"> And these lepers entered a little way into the camp, and went into one tent, and ate and drank, and took thence silver, and gold, and raiment; and they went and returned thence, and entered into another tent, and took thence, and went and hid the spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Philippians 2:4; Nahum 1:15"> And one man said to his neighbor, We are not doing well thus: this day is a day of glad tidings, and we hold our peace, and are waiting till the morning light, and shall find mischief: now then come, and let us go into the city, and report to the house of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings IV 7:11; Mark 13:34–35; Psalms 127:1"> So they went and cried toward the gate of the city, and reported to them, saying, We went into the camp of Syria, and, behold, there is not there a man, nor voice of man, only horses tied and asses, and their tents as they were.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the porters cried aloud, and reported to the house of the king within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 6:25–29; Joshua 8:4"> And the king rose up by night, and said to his servants, I will now tell you what the Syrians have done to us. They knew that we are hungry; and they have gone forth from the camp and hidden themselves in the field, saying, They will come out of the city, and we shall catch them alive, and go into the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings IV 7:4; Lamentations 4:9; Kings IV 5:13"> And one of his servants answered and said, Let them now take five of the horses that were left, which were left here; behold, they are the number left to all the multitude of Israel; and we will send thither and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So they took two horsemen; and the king of Israel sent after the king of Syria, saying, Go, and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 22:24; Matthew 24:16–18; Hebrews 12:1"> And they went after them even to Jordan: and, behold, all the way was full of garments and vessels, which the Syrians had cast away in their panic. And the messengers returned, and brought word to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings IV 7:1; Isaiah 33:4; Isaiah 33:23"> And the people went out, and plundered the camp of Syria: and a measure of fine flour was sold for a shekel, according to the word of the Lord, and two measures of barley for a shekel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings IV 7:2; Kings IV 6:32; Judges 20:43"> And the king appointed the officer on whose hand the king leaned to have charge over the gate: and the people trampled on him in the gate, and he died, as the man of God had said, who spoke when the messenger came down to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 18:14; Kings IV 7:1–2; Kings IV 6:32"> So it came to pass as Elisha had spoken to the king, saying, Two measures of barley shall be sold for a shekel, and a measure of fine flour for a shekel; and it shall be as at this time tomorrow in the gate of Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings IV 7:2"> And the officer answered Elisha, and said, Behold, if the Lord makes floodgates in heaven, shall this thing be? and Elisha said, Behold, thou shalt see it with thine eyes, but thou shalt not eat thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5–6; Chronicles II 20:20; Job 20:23"> And it was so: for the people trampled on him in the gate, and he died.</span>
