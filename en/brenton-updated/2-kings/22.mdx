<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:39; Chronicles II 34:1–33; Jeremiah 1:2"> Josiah was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned thirty and one years in Jerusalem: and his mother’s name was Jedidah, daughter of Adaiah of Bozkath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; Joshua 1:7; Kings IV 18:3"> And he did that which was right in the sight of the Lord, and walked in all the way of David his father; he turned not aside to the right hand or to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles II 34:3–33"> And it came to pass in the eighteenth year of King Josiah, in the eighth month, the king sent Shaphan the son of Azaliah the son of Meshullam, the scribe of the house of the Lord, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings IV 12:4; Mark 12:41–42; Chronicles II 34:9–18"> Go up to Hilkiah the high priest, and take account of the money that is brought into the house of the Lord, which they that keep the door have collected of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 12:11–14; Kings IV 12:5; Chronicles II 24:12–13"> And let them give it into the hand of the workmen that are appointed in the house of the Lord. And he gave it to the workmen in the house of the Lord, to repair the breaches of the house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> even to the carpenters, and builders, and masons, and also to purchase timber and hewn stones, to repair the breaches of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 12:15; 1 Corinthians 4:2; Nehemiah 7:2"> Only they did not call them to account for the money that was given to them, because they dealt faithfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:24–26; Chronicles II 34:14–28"> And Hilkiah the high priest said to Shaphan the scribe, I have found the book of the law in the house of the Lord. And Hilkiah gave the book to Shaphan, and he read it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:11; Kings IV 25:22; Kings IV 18:18"> And he went into the house of the Lord to the king, and reported the matter to the king, and said, Thy servants have collected the money that was found in the house of the Lord, and have given it into the hand of the workmen that are appointed in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:21; Jeremiah 13:18; Deuteronomy 17:18–20"> And Shaphan the scribe spoke to the king, saying, Hilkiah the priest has given me a book. And Shaphan read it before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles II 34:19; Joel 2:13; Kings IV 22:19"> And it came to pass, when the king heard the words of the book of the law, that he rent his garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:24; Kings IV 25:22; Kings IV 19:2–3"> And the king commanded Hilkiah the priest, and Ahikam the son of Shaphan, and Achbor the son of Micaiah, and Shaphan the scribe, and Asaiah the king’s servant, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23–28; Deuteronomy 31:17–18; Jeremiah 37:17"> Go, inquire of the Lord for me, and for all the people, and for all Judah, and concerning the words of this book that has been found: for the wrath of the Lord that has been kindled against us is great, because our fathers hearkened not to the words of this book, to do according to all the things written concerning us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 4:4; Chronicles II 34:22; Micah 6:4"> So Hilkiah the priest went, and Ahikam, and Achbor, and Shaphan, and Asaiah, to Huldah the prophetess, the mother of Shallum the son of Tikvah son of Harhas, keeper of the robes; and she dwelt in Jerusalem in Masena; and they spoke to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings IV 1:6; Kings IV 1:16; Jeremiah 23:28"> And she said to them, Thus saith the Lord God of Israel, Say to the man that sent you to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 9:11–14; Joshua 23:15; Deuteronomy 29:27"> Thus saith the Lord, Behold, I bring evil upon this place, and upon them that dwell in it, even all the words of the book which the king of Judah has read:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings IV 21:22; Jeremiah 7:20; Deuteronomy 29:24–28"> because they have forsaken me, and burned incense to other gods, that they might provoke me with the works of their hands: therefore my wrath shall burn forth against this place, and shall not be quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles II 34:26–28; Malachi 3:16–17; Isaiah 3:10"> And to the king of Judah that sent you to inquire of the Lord—thus shall ye say to him, Thus saith the Lord God of Israel, As for the words which thou hast heard;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 51:17; Kings III 21:29; Isaiah 57:15"> because thy heart was softened, and thou wast humbled before me, when thou heardest all that I spoke against this place, and against the inhabitants of it, that it should be utterly destroyed and accursed, and thou didst rend thy garments, and weep before me; I also have heard, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 37:37; Isaiah 57:1–2; Chronicles I 17:11"> It shall not be so therefore: behold, I will add thee to thy fathers, and thou shalt be gathered to thy tomb in peace, and thine eyes shall not see any among all the evils which I bring upon this place.</span>
