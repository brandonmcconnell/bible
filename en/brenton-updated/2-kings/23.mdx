<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 34:29–33; Chronicles II 30:2; Deuteronomy 31:28"> So they reported the word to the king: and the king sent and gathered all the elders of Judah and Jerusalem to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings IV 22:8; Deuteronomy 31:10–13; Revelation 20:12"> And the king went up to the house of the Lord, and every man of Judah and all who dwelt in Jerusalem with him, and the priests, and the prophets, and all the people small and great; and he read in their ears all the words of the book of the covenant that was found in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings IV 11:14; Kings IV 11:17; Deuteronomy 13:4"> And the king stood by a pillar, and made a covenant before the Lord, to walk after the Lord, to keep his commandments and his testimonies and his ordinances with all the heart and with all the soul, to confirm the words of this covenant; even the things written in this book. And all the people stood to the covenant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings IV 21:3; Kings IV 21:7; Chronicles II 33:3"> And the king commanded Hilkiah the high priest, and the priests of the second order, and them that kept the door, to bring out of the temple of the Lord all the vessels that were made for Baal, and for the grove, and all the host of heaven, and he burned them without Jerusalem in the fields of Kidron, and took the ashes of them to Bethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:17–19; Kings IV 21:3–4; Hosea 10:5"> And he burned the idolatrous priests, whom the kings of Judah had appointed (and they burned incense in the high places and in the cities of Judah, and the places round about Jerusalem); and them that burned incense to Baal, and to the sun, and to the moon, and to Mazuroth, and to all the host of heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles II 34:4; Kings IV 23:15; Kings III 14:23"> And he carried out the grove from the house of the Lord to the brook Kidron, and burned it at the brook Kidron, and reduced it to powder, and cast its powder on the sepulchers of the sons of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings III 15:12; Ezekiel 16:16; Kings III 14:24"> And he pulled down the house of the sodomites that were by the house of the Lord, where the women wove tents for the grove.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 15:22; Joshua 21:17; Kings III 19:3"> And he brought up all the priests from the cities of Judah, and defiled the high places where the priests burned incense, from Geba even to Beersheba; and he pulled down the house of the gates that was by the door of the gate of Joshua the ruler of the city, on a man’s left hand at the gate of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:10–14; Kings I 2:36; Malachi 2:8–9"> Only the priests of the high places went not up to the altar of the Lord in Jerusalem, for they only ate leavened bread in the midst of their brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 30:33; Jeremiah 19:6; Leviticus 18:21"> And he defiled Topheth which is in the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, constructed for a man to cause his son or his daughter to pass through fire to Molech.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Deuteronomy 4:19; Chronicles II 14:5"> And he burned the horses which the kings of Judah had given to the sun in the entrance of the house of the Lord, by the treasury of Nathan the king’s eunuch, in the suburbs; and he burned the chariot of the sun with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 21:5; Zephaniah 1:5; Jeremiah 19:13"> And the altars that were on the roof of the upper chamber of Ahaz, which the kings of Judah had made, and the altars which Manasseh had made in the two courts of the house of the Lord, did the king pull down and forcibly remove from thence, and cast their dust into the brook of Kidron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 11:7; Kings III 11:5; Numbers 21:29"> And the king defiled the house that was before Jerusalem, on the right hand of the mount of Mosthath, which Solomon king of Israel built to Ashtoreth the abomination of the Sidonians, and to Chemosh the abomination of Moab, and to Milcom the abomination of the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:5; Exodus 23:24; Kings IV 23:16"> And he broke in pieces the pillars, and utterly destroyed the groves, and filled their places with the bones of men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings IV 23:6; Kings III 14:16; Kings III 21:22"> Also the high altar in Bethel, which Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who made Israel to sin, had made, even that high altar he tore down, and broke in pieces the stones of it, and reduced it to powder, and burned the grove.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 13:1–2; Kings III 13:32; John 10:35"> And Josiah turned aside, and saw the tombs that were there in the city, and sent, and took the bones out of the tombs, and burned them on the altar, and defiled it, according to the word of the Lord which the man of God spoke, when Jeroboam stood by the altar at the feast: and he turned and raised his eyes to the tomb of the man of God that spoke these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 13:1; Kings III 13:30–31"> And he said, What is that mound which I see? And the men of the city said to him, It is the grave of the man of God that came out of Judah, and uttered these imprecations which he imprecated upon the altar of Bethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 13:31; Kings III 13:1–22"> And he said, Let him alone; let no one disturb his bones. So his bones were spared, together with the bones of the prophet that came out of Samaria.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Chronicles II 34:6–7; Kings IV 17:9; Ezekiel 8:17–18"> Moreover Josiah removed all the houses of the high places that were in the cities of Samaria, which the kings of Israel made to provoke the Lord, and did to them all that he did in Bethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings IV 11:18; Chronicles II 34:5; Exodus 22:20"> And he sacrificed all the priests of the high places that were there on the altars, and burned the bones of men upon them, and returned to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–8; Numbers 9:2–5; Chronicles II 35:1–19"> And the king commanded all the people, saying, Keep the Passover to the Lord your God, as it is written in the book of this covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Chronicles II 30:1–3; Chronicles II 30:13–20; Chronicles II 35:3–19"> For a Passover such as this had not been kept from the days of the judges who judged Israel, even all the days of the kings of Israel, and of the kings of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> But in the eighteenth year of King Josiah, was the Passover kept to the Lord in Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Kings IV 21:6; Genesis 31:19"> Moreover Josiah removed the sorcerers, and the wizards, and the teraphim, and the idols, and all the abominations that had been set up in the land of Judah and in Jerusalem, that he might keep the words of the law that were written in the book, which Hilkiah the priest found in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings IV 18:5; Jeremiah 29:13; Deuteronomy 6:5"> There was no king like him before him, who turned to the Lord with all his heart, and with all his soul, and with all his strength, according to all the law of Moses; and after him there rose not one like him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings IV 21:11–13; Kings IV 24:4; Jeremiah 3:7–10"> Nevertheless the Lord turned not from the fierceness of his great anger, wherewith he was wroth in his anger against Judah, because of the provocations, wherewith Manasseh provoked him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings IV 18:11; Kings IV 21:13; Kings IV 21:4"> And the Lord said, I will also remove Judah from my presence, as I removed Israel, and will reject this city which I have chosen, even Jerusalem, and the house of which I said, My name shall be there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings IV 20:20"> And the rest of the acts of Josiah, and all that he did, are not these things written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:2; Chronicles II 35:20–24; Judges 5:19"> And in his days went up Pharaoh Neco king of Egypt against the king of the Assyrians to the river Euphrates: and Josiah went out to meet him: and Neco slew him in Megiddo when he saw him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings IV 9:28; Chronicles II 36:1–4; Kings IV 21:24"> And his servants carried him dead from Megiddo, and brought him to Jerusalem, and buried him in his sepulcher: and the people of the land took Jehoahaz the son of Josiah, and anointed him, and made him king in the room of his father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Chronicles I 3:15; Kings IV 24:18; Jeremiah 22:11"> Twenty and three years old was Jehoahaz when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem: and his mother’s name was Hamutal, daughter of Jeremiah of Libnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Kings IV 21:2–7; Kings IV 21:21–22"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his fathers did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Kings III 8:65; Kings IV 25:6; Jeremiah 39:5–6"> And Pharaoh Neco removed him to Riblah in the land of Hamath, so that he should not reign in Jerusalem; and imposed a tribute on the land, a hundred talents of silver, and a hundred talents of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:3–4; Kings IV 24:17; Chronicles I 3:15"> And Pharaoh Neco made Eliakim son of Josiah king of Judah king over them in the place of his father Josiah, and he changed his name to Jehoiakim, and he took Jehoahaz and brought him to Egypt, and he died there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings IV 23:33; Kings IV 15:19–20"> And Jehoiakim gave the silver and the gold to Pharaoh; but he assessed the land to give the money at the command of Pharaoh: they gave the silver and the gold each man according to his assessment together with the people of the land to give to Pharaoh Neco.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:5; Jeremiah 26:1; Chronicles I 3:15"> Twenty-five years old was Jehoiakim when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem: and his mother’s name was Zebidah, daughter of Pedaiah of Rumah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Chronicles II 33:22–23; Ezekiel 19:5–9; Jeremiah 36:31"> And he did that which was evil in the eyes of the Lord, according to all that his fathers had done.</span>
</div>
</div>
