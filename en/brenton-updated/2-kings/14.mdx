<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings IV 13:10; Chronicles II 25:1–4; Kings IV 14:15"> In the second year of Joash the son of Jehoahaz king of Israel, did Amaziah also the son of Joash the king of Judah begin to reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Twenty and five years old was he when he began to reign, and he reigned twenty and nine years in Jerusalem: and his mother’s name was Jehoaddin of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles II 25:2–3; Zechariah 1:4–6; Kings III 11:4"> And he did that which was right in the sight of the Lord, but not as David his father: he did according to all things that his father Joash did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings IV 12:3; Kings IV 16:4; Kings IV 15:35"> Only he removed not the high places: as yet the people sacrificed and burned incense on the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles II 25:3–4; Kings IV 12:20–21; Numbers 35:33"> And it came to pass when the kingdom was established in his hand, that he slew his servants that had slain the king his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:16; Ezekiel 18:20; Ezekiel 18:4"> But he slew not the sons of those that had slain him; according as it is written in the book of the laws of Moses, as the Lord gave commandment, saying, The fathers shall not be put to death for the children, and the children shall not be put to death for the fathers; but every one shall die for his own sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings II 8:13; Chronicles I 18:12; Joshua 15:38"> He smote of Edom ten thousand in the Valley of Salt, and took the Rock in the war, and called its name Joktheel until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles II 25:17–24; Kings II 2:14–17; Kings IV 14:11"> Then Amaziah sent messengers to Jehoash son of Jehoahaz son of Jehu king of Israel, saying, Come, let us look one another in the face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; Kings III 4:33; Chronicles II 33:11"> And Jehoash the king of Israel sent to Amaziah king of Judah, saying, The thistle that was in Lebanon sent to the cedar that was in Lebanon, saying, Give my daughter to thy son to wife: and the wild beasts of the field that were in Lebanon passed by and trod down the thistle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Chronicles II 26:16; Deuteronomy 8:14; Chronicles II 32:25"> Thou hast smitten and wounded Edom, and thy heart has lifted thee up: stay at home and glorify thyself; for wherefore art thou quarrelsome to thy hurt? So both thou wilt fall and Judah with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 19:38; Chronicles II 25:16; Joshua 15:10"> Nevertheless Amaziah hearkened not: so Jehoash king of Israel went up, and he and Amaziah king of Judah looked one another in the face in Beth-shemesh of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings II 18:17; Kings I 4:10; Kings III 22:36"> And Judah was overthrown before Israel, and every man fled to his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:16; Nehemiah 12:39; Zechariah 14:10"> And Jehoash king of Israel took Amaziah the son of Jehoash the son of Ahaziah, in Beth-shemesh; and he came to Jerusalem, and broke down the wall of Jerusalem, beginning at the gate of Ephraim as far as the gate of the corner, four hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings III 14:26; Kings III 7:51; Kings III 15:18"> And he took the gold, and the silver, and all the vessels that were found in the house of the Lord, and in the treasures of the king’s house, and the hostages, and returned to Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings IV 13:12–13; Kings III 14:19–20; Kings IV 10:34–35"> And the rest of the acts of Jehoash, even all that he did in his might, how he warred with Amaziah king of Judah, are not these things written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings IV 13:9; Kings IV 13:13; Kings III 1:21"> And Jehoash slept with his fathers, and was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel; and Jeroboam his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles II 25:25–28; Kings IV 13:10; Kings IV 14:23"> And Amaziah the son of Jehoash king of Judah lived after the death of Joash son of Jehoahaz king of Israel fifteen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 11:41; Kings III 14:29; Kings IV 13:8"> And the rest of the acts of Amaziah, and all that he did, are not these written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 10:31; Kings IV 15:25; Kings IV 12:20–21"> And they formed a conspiracy against him in Jerusalem, and he fled to Lachish: and they sent after him to Lachish, and slew him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings IV 9:28; Kings IV 12:21; Chronicles II 33:20"> And they brought him upon horses; and he was buried in Jerusalem with his fathers in the city of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings IV 21:24; Matthew 1:8–9; Kings IV 15:13"> And all the people of Judah took Uzziah, and he was sixteen years old, and made him king in the room of his father Amaziah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings IV 16:6; Kings III 9:26; Deuteronomy 2:8"> He built Elath, and restored it to Judah, after the king slept with his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hosea 1:1; Amos 7:9–11; Amos 1:1"> In the fifteenth year of Amaziah son of Joash king of Judah began Jeroboam son of Joash to reign over Israel in Samaria forty and one years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings IV 13:6; Kings IV 13:11; Genesis 38:7"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord: he departed not from all the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who led Israel to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:17; Matthew 12:39–40; Joshua 19:13"> He recovered the coast of Israel from the entering in of Hamath to the Sea of Arabah, according to the word of the Lord God of Israel, which he spoke by his servant Jonah the son of Amittai, the prophet of Gath-hepher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; Exodus 3:7; Kings IV 13:4"> For the Lord saw that the affliction of Israel was very bitter, and that they were few in number, straitened and in want, and destitute, and Israel had no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings IV 13:23; Kings IV 13:5; Romans 11:2–12"> And the Lord said that he would not blot out the seed of Israel from under heaven; so he delivered them by the hand of Jeroboam the son of Joash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings III 11:24; Chronicles I 18:5–6; Kings II 8:6"> And the rest of the acts of Jeroboam, and all that he did, and his mighty deeds, which he achieved in war, and how he recovered Damascus and Hamath to Judah in Israel, are not these things written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings IV 15:8"> And Jeroboam slept with his fathers, even with the kings of Israel; and Zechariah his son reigned in his stead.</span>
