<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 5:24; Hebrews 11:5; Kings III 19:16–21"> And it came to pass, when the Lord was going to take Elijah with a whirlwind as it were into heaven, that Elijah and Elisha went out of Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings IV 2:6; Ruth 1:15–18; Kings IV 4:30"> And Elijah said to Elisha, Stay here, I pray thee; for God has sent me to Bethel. And Elisha said, As the Lord lives and thy soul lives, I will not leave thee; so they came to Bethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings IV 4:1; Kings IV 4:38; Kings IV 9:1"> And the sons of the prophets who were in Bethel came to Elisha, and said to him, Dost thou know, that the Lord this day is going to take thy lord away from thy head? And he said, Yea, I know it; be silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings IV 2:2; Joshua 6:26; Kings III 16:34"> And Elijah said to Elisha, Stay here, I pray thee; for the Lord has sent me to Jericho. And he said, As the Lord lives and thy soul lives, I will not leave thee. And they came to Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 2:3; Joshua 1:1–2; Acts 1:11"> And the sons of the prophets who were in Jericho drew near to Elisha, and said to him, Dost thou know that the Lord is about to take away thy master today from thy head? And he said, Yea, I know it; hold your peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings IV 2:2; Joshua 3:15"> And Elijah said to him, Stay here, I pray thee, for the Lord has sent me to Jordan. And Elisha said, As the Lord lives and thy soul lives, I will not leave thee: and they both went on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings III 18:13; Kings IV 2:17; Kings III 18:4"> And fifty men of the sons of the prophets went also, and they stood opposite afar off: and both stood on the bank of Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 19:19; Exodus 14:21–22; Joshua 3:14–17"> And Elijah took his mantle, and wrapped it together, and smote the water: and the water was divided on this side and on that side, and they both went over on dry ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Deuteronomy 21:17; John 16:7"> And it came to pass while they were crossing over, that Elijah said to Elisha, Ask what I shall do for thee before I am taken up from thee. And Elisha said, Let there be, I pray thee, a double portion of thy spirit upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 11:22–24; Kings IV 2:12; John 16:24"> And Elijah said, Thou hast asked a hard thing: if thou shalt see me when I am taken up from thee, then shall it be so to thee; and if not, it shall not be so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings IV 6:17; Psalms 104:3–4; Hebrews 1:14"> And it came to pass as they were going, they went on talking; and, behold, a chariot of fire, and horses of fire, and it separated between them both; and Elijah was taken up in a whirlwind as it were into heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 13:14; Isaiah 37:21; Mark 16:19"> And Elisha saw, and cried, Father, father, the chariot of Israel, and the horseman thereof! And he saw him no more: and he took hold of his garments, and rent them into two pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 19:19; Kings IV 2:8; Kings III 9:26"> And Elisha took up the mantle of Elijah, which fell from off him upon Elisha; and Elisha returned, and stood upon the brink of Jordan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 14:12; Psalms 42:10; Psalms 115:2"> and he took the mantle of Elijah, which fell from off him, and smote the water, and said, Where is the Lord God of Elijah? and he smote the waters, and they were divided hither and thither; and Elisha went over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14; Acts 1:8; Joshua 3:7"> And the sons of the prophets who were in Jericho on the opposite side saw him, and said, The spirit of Elijah has rested upon Elisha. And they came to meet him, and did obeisance to him to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 18:12; Acts 8:39; 2 Corinthians 12:2–3"> And they said to him, Behold now, there are with thy servants fifty men of strength: let them go now, and seek thy lord: peradventure the Spirit of the Lord has taken him up, and cast him into Jordan, or on one of the mountains, or on one of the hills. And Elisha said, Ye shall not send.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings IV 8:11; Hebrews 11:5; Romans 10:2"> And they pressed him until he was ashamed; and he said, Send. And they sent fifty men, and sought three days, and found him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And they returned to him, for he dwelt in Jericho: and Elisha said, Did I not say to you, Go not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 23:26; Deuteronomy 28:2–4; Deuteronomy 28:11"> And the men of the city said to Elisha, Behold, the situation of the city is good, as our lord sees; but the waters are bad, and the ground barren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 9:45; Zephaniah 2:9; Ezekiel 47:11"> And Elisha said, Bring me a new pitcher, and put salt in it. And they took one, and brought it to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 15:25–26; Kings IV 4:41; Leviticus 2:13"> And Elisha went out to the spring of the waters, and cast salt therein, and says, Thus saith the Lord, I have healed these waters; there shall not be any longer death thence or barren land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the waters were healed until this day, according to the word of Elisha which he spoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:16; Job 19:18; Job 30:1"> And he went up thence to Bethel: and as he was going up by the way there came up also little children from the city, and mocked him, and said to him, Go up, baldhead, go up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 13:9–11; Acts 5:5; Hosea 13:8"> And he turned after them, and saw them, and cursed them in the name of the Lord. And, behold, there came out two bears out of the wood, and they tore forty and two children of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings IV 4:25; Kings III 18:19–20; Kings III 18:42"> And he went thence to Mount Carmel, and returned thence to Samaria.</span>
