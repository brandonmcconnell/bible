<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings IV 2:3; Kings IV 4:1; Joshua 17:14"> And the sons of the prophets said to Elisha, Behold now, the place wherein we dwell before thee is too narrow for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 21:3; Acts 18:3; 1 Thessalonians 2:9"> Let us go, we pray thee, unto Jordan, and take thence every man a beam, and make for ourselves a habitation there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 6:28; Judges 19:6; Kings IV 5:23"> And he said, Go. And one of them said gently, Come with thy servants. And he said, I will go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:5; Deuteronomy 29:11"> And he went with them, and they came to Jordan, and began to cut down wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 37:21; Ecclesiastes 10:10; Kings IV 6:15"> And behold, one was cutting down a beam, and the axe head fell into the water: and he cried out, Alas! master: and it was hidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings IV 2:21; Mark 8:23–25; Kings IV 4:41"> And the man of God said, Where did it fall? and he showed him the place: and he broke off a stick, and threw it in there, and the iron came to the surface.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 4:36; Exodus 4:4; Kings IV 4:7"> And he said, Take it up to thyself. And he stretched out his hand, and took it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings IV 6:24; Proverbs 21:30; Proverbs 20:18"> And the king of Syria was at war with Israel: and he consulted with his servants, saying, I will encamp in such a place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Revelation 1:1; Kings IV 6:12"> And Elisha sent to the king of Israel, saying, Take heed that thou pass not by that place, for the Syrians are hidden there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings IV 13:14; Kings IV 5:14; Acts 27:24"> And the king of Israel sent to the place which Elisha mentioned to him, and saved himself thence not once or twice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 18:7–11; Isaiah 57:20–21; Matthew 2:3–12"> And the mind of the king of Syria was very much disturbed concerning this thing; and he called his servants, and said to them, Will ye not tell me who betrays me to the king of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 2:28–30; Amos 3:7; Kings IV 6:9–10"> And one of his servants said, Nay, my Lord, O king, for Elisha the prophet that is in Israel reports to the king of Israel all the words whatsoever thou mayest say in thy bedchamber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 37:17; John 11:47–53; Kings I 23:22–23"> And he said, Go, see where this man is, and I will send and take him. And they sent word to him, saying, Behold, he is in Dothan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings IV 18:17; Kings I 24:2; Kings I 23:26"> And he sent thither horses, and chariots, and a mighty host: and they came by night, and compassed about the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 8:26; Chronicles II 20:12; Psalms 53:5"> And the servant of Elisha rose up early and went out; and, behold, a host compassed the city, and horses and chariots: and the servant said to him, O master, what shall we do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10–14; Romans 8:31; 1 John 4:4"> And Elisha said, Fear not, for they who are with us are more than they that are with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 68:17; Psalms 34:7; Zechariah 6:1–7"> And Elisha prayed, and said, Lord, open, I pray thee, the eyes of the servant, and let him see. And the Lord opened his eyes, and he saw: and, behold, the mountain was full of horses, and there were chariots of fire round about Elisha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 13:11; John 12:40; Genesis 19:11"> And they came down to him; and he prayed to the Lord, and said, Smite, I pray thee, this people with blindness. And he smote them with blindness, according to the word of Elisha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 24:16; Kings II 16:18–19; Luke 9:23"> And Elisha said to them, This is not the city, and this is not the way: follow me, and I will bring you to the man whom ye seek. And he led them away to Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings IV 6:17; Luke 24:31; Luke 16:23"> And it came to pass when they entered into Samaria, that Elisha said, Open, I pray thee, O Lord, their eyes, and let them see. And the Lord opened their eyes, and they saw; and, behold, they were in the midst of Samaria.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings IV 5:13; Kings IV 8:9; Kings IV 2:12"> And the king of Israel said to Elisha, when he saw them, Shall I not verily smite them, my father?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Genesis 48:22; Hosea 2:18"> And he said, Thou shalt not smite them, unless thou wouldest smite those whom thou hast taken captive with thy sword and with thy bow: set bread and water before them, and let them eat and drink, and depart to their master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings IV 5:2; Kings IV 24:2; Luke 6:35"> And he set before them a great feast, and they ate and drank: and he dismissed them, and they departed to their master. And the bands of Syria came no longer into the land of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings III 20:1; Kings III 22:31; Ecclesiastes 9:14"> And it came to pass after this, that the son of Hadad king of Syria gathered all his army, and went up, and besieged Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings IV 6:28–29; Jeremiah 14:18; Jeremiah 14:13–15"> And there was a great famine in Samaria: and, behold, they besieged it, until an ass’s head was valued at fifty pieces of silver, and the fourth part of a cab of dove’s dung at five pieces of silver.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings II 14:4; Isaiah 10:3; Luke 18:3"> And the king of Israel was passing by on the wall, and a woman cried to him, saying, Help, my lord, O king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 118:8–9; Psalms 124:1–3; Psalms 60:11"> And he said to her, Unless the Lord help thee, whence shall I help thee? from the corn floor, or from the winepress?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings II 14:5; Judges 18:23; Lamentations 4:10"> And the king said to her, What is the matter with thee? And the woman said to him, This woman said to me, Give thy son, and we will eat him today, and we will eat my son tomorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 26:29; Deuteronomy 28:53; Deuteronomy 28:57"> So we boiled my son, and ate him; and I said to her on the second day, Give thy son, and let us eat him: and she has hidden her son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings III 21:27; Kings IV 5:7; Kings IV 19:1"> And it came to pass, when the king of Israel heard the words of the woman, that he rent his garments; and he passed by on the wall, and the people saw sackcloth within upon his flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ruth 1:17; Kings III 19:2; John 11:50"> And he said, God do so to me and more also, if the head of Elisha shall stand upon him this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:1; Kings III 18:4; Ezekiel 8:1"> And Elisha was sitting in his house, and the elders were sitting with him; and the king sent a man before him: before the messenger came to him, he also said to the elders, Do ye see that this son of a murderer has sent to take away my head? See, as soon as the messenger shall have come, shut the door, and forcibly detain him at the door: is not the sound of his master’s feet behind him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 2:9; Isaiah 8:21; Genesis 4:13"> While he was yet speaking with them, behold, a messenger came to him: and he said, Behold, this evil is of the Lord; why should I wait for the Lord any longer?</span>
</div>
