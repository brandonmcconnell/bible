<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:25; Kings IV 2:3; Nehemiah 5:2–5"> And one of the wives of the sons of the prophets cried to Elisha, saying, Thy servant my husband is dead; and thou knowest that thy servant feared the Lord: and the creditor is come to take my two sons to be his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings III 17:12; James 2:5; Kings IV 6:26–27"> And Elisha said, What shall I do for thee? tell me what thou hast in the house. And she said, Thy servant has nothing in the house, except oil wherewith I anoint myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 2:7; Psalms 81:10; John 16:24"> And he said to her, Go, borrow for thyself vessels without of all thy neighbors, even empty vessels; borrow not a few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 8:5–9; Matthew 6:6; Ephesians 3:20"> And thou shalt go in and shut the door upon thee and upon thy sons, and thou shalt pour forth into these vessels, and remove that which is filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 5:11; Hebrews 11:7–8; Kings III 17:15–16"> And she departed from him, and shut the door upon herself and upon her sons: they brought the vessels near to her, and she poured in until the vessels were filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 14:20; Kings IV 13:19; Luke 6:19"> And she said to her sons, Bring me yet a vessel. And they said to her, There is not a vessel more. And the oil stayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–12; Kings III 12:22; Romans 12:17"> And she came and told the man of God: and Elisha said, Go, and sell the oil, and thou shalt pay thy debts, and thou and thy sons shall live of the remaining oil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 19:18; Genesis 19:3; Kings IV 4:11–12"> And a day came, when Elisha passed over to Shunem, and there was a great lady there, and she constrained him to eat bread: and it came to pass as often as he went into the city, that he turned aside to eat there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 17:24; Proverbs 31:10–11; Kings IV 4:7"> And the woman said to her husband, See now, I know that this is a holy man of God who comes over continually to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:13; Matthew 10:41–42; Matthew 25:40"> Let us now make for him an upper chamber, a small place; and let us put there for him a bed, and a table, and a stool, and a candlestick: and it shall come to pass that when he comes in to us, he shall turn in thither.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And a day came, and he went in thither, and turned aside into the upper chamber, and lay there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 8:4–5; Kings IV 3:11; Kings IV 4:29–31"> And he said to Gehazi his servant, Call me this Shunammite. And he called her, and she stood before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:16–18; Philippians 4:18–19; Hebrews 6:10"> And he said to him, Say now to her, Behold, thou hast taken all this trouble for us; what should I do for thee? Hast thou any request to make to the king, or to the captain of the host? And she said, I dwell in the midst of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:7; Genesis 15:2–3; Kings I 1:8"> And he said to Gehazi, What must we do for her? and Gehazi his servant said, Indeed she has no son, and her husband is old.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he called her, and she stood by the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 18:10; Luke 1:13; Kings IV 4:28"> And Elisha said to her, At this time next year, as the season is, thou shalt be alive, and embrace a son. And she said, Nay, my lord, do not lie to thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 113:9; Luke 1:36; Genesis 21:1"> And the woman conceived, and bore a son at the very time, as the season was, being alive, as Elisha said to her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 2:4"> And the child grew: and it came to pass when he went out to his father to the reapers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 14:1–2; Jeremiah 4:19"> that he said to his father, My head, my head. And his father said to a servant, Carry him to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 7:12; John 11:3; John 11:14"> And he carried him to his mother, and he lay upon her knees till noon, and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings IV 4:32; Kings IV 4:10; Kings IV 4:7"> And she carried him up and laid him on the bed of the man of God; and she shut the door upon him, and went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 9:38; Kings IV 4:26; John 11:3"> And she called her husband, and said, Send now for me one of the young men, and one of the asses, and I will ride quickly to the man of God, and return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; Numbers 28:11; Chronicles I 23:31"> And he said, Why art thou going to him today? It is neither new moon, nor the Sabbath. And she said, It is well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 4:20; Kings III 13:13; Kings I 25:19–20"> And she saddled the ass, and said to her servant, Be quick, proceed: spare not on my account to ride, unless I shall tell thee. Go, and thou shalt proceed, and come to the man of God to Mount Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings IV 2:25; Isaiah 35:2; Kings III 18:19"> And she rode and came to the man of God to the mountain: and it came to pass when Elisha saw her coming, that he said to Gehazi his servant, See now, that Shunammite comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 15:36; Kings IV 4:23; Kings I 17:18"> Now run to meet her, and thou shalt say, Is it well with thee? is it well with thy husband? is it well with the child? and she said, It is well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings II 7:3; Genesis 18:17; Amos 3:7"> And she came to Elisha to the mountain, and laid hold of his feet; and Gehazi drew near to thrust her away. And Elisha said, Let her alone, for her soul is much grieved in her, and the Lord has hidden it from me, and has not told it me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 30:1; Kings IV 4:16"> And she said, Did I ask a son of my lord? For did I not say, Do not deal deceitfully with me?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings IV 2:14; Kings III 18:46; Exodus 14:16"> And Elisha said to Gehazi, Gird up thy loins, and take my staff in thy hand, and go: if thou meet any man, thou shalt not salute him, and if a man salute thee thou shalt not answer him: and thou shalt lay my staff on the child’s face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ruth 1:16–18; Kings IV 2:2; Exodus 33:12–16"> And the mother of the child said, As the Lord lives and as thy soul lives, I will not leave thee. And Elisha arose, and went after her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:11; Mark 5:39; Mark 9:19–29"> And Gehazi went on before her, and laid his staff on the child’s face: but there was neither voice nor any hearing. So he returned to meet him, and told him, saying, The child is not awakened.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 8:52–53; John 11:17; Kings III 17:17"> And Elisha went into the house, and, behold, the dead child was laid upon his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; Kings IV 4:4; Acts 9:40"> And Elisha went into the house, and shut the door upon themselves, the two, and prayed to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Kings III 17:21; Acts 20:10"> And he went up, and lay upon the child, and put his mouth upon his mouth, and his eyes upon his eyes, and his hands upon his hands; and bowed himself upon him, and the flesh of the child grew warm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings IV 8:5; Kings IV 13:21; Kings III 17:22"> And he returned, and walked up and down in the house: and he went up, and bowed himself on the child seven times; and the child opened his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 11:35; Kings III 17:23; Luke 7:15"> And Elisha cried out to Gehazi, and said, Call this Shunammite. So he called her, and she came in to him: and Elisha said, Take thy son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Hebrews 11:35; Kings IV 2:15; Kings III 17:24"> And the woman went in, and fell at his feet, and did obeisance bowing to the ground; and she took her son, and went out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Kings IV 8:1; Kings IV 2:3; Kings IV 2:1"> And Elisha returned to Gilgal: and a famine was in the land; and the sons of the prophets sat before him: and Elisha said to his servant, Set on the great pot, and boil pottage for the sons of the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Isaiah 5:4; Matthew 15:13"> And he went out into the field to gather herbs, and found a vine in the field, and gathered of it wild gourds, his garment full; and he cast it into the caldron of pottage, for they knew them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 16:18; Exodus 15:23; Exodus 10:17"> And he poured it out for the men to eat: and it came to pass, when they were eating of the pottage, that lo! they cried out, and said, There is death in the pot, O man of God. And they could not eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Kings IV 2:21; Exodus 15:25; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> And he said, Take meal, and cast it into the pot. And Elisha said to his servant Gehazi, Pour out for the people, and let them eat. And there was no longer there any hurtful thing in the pot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Kings I 9:7; John 6:9; Matthew 14:16–21"> And there came a man over from Baal-shalishah, and brought to the man of God twenty barley loaves and cakes of figs, of the firstfruits. And he said, Give to the people, and let them eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:13; Matthew 14:20; John 6:9"> And his servant said, Why should I set this before a hundred men? and he said, Give to the people, and let them eat; for thus saith the Lord, They shall eat and leave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And they ate and left, according to the word of the Lord.</span>
</div>
