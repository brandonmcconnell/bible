<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:1; Kings IV 25:25; Chronicles II 22:10–12"> And Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah saw that her son was dead, and she destroyed all the seed royal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:26; Jeremiah 35:2; Isaiah 7:6–7"> And Jehosheba daughter of King Joram, sister of Ahaziah, took Joash the son of her brother, and stole him from among the king’s sons that were put to death, secreting him and his nurse in the bedchamber, and hid him from the face of Athaliah, and he was not slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 12:8; Chronicles II 22:12; Malachi 3:15"> And he remained with her hidden in the house of the Lord six years: and Athaliah reigned over the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings IV 11:19; Kings IV 11:9; Kings IV 11:17"> And in the seventh year Jehoiada sent and took the captains of hundreds of the Carites and of the Rhasim, and brought them to him into the house of the Lord, and made a covenant of the Lord with them, and adjured them, and Jehoiada showed them the king’s son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles I 9:25; Ezekiel 44:2–3; Chronicles I 23:32"> And charged them, saying, This is the thing which ye shall do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles II 23:4–5; Chronicles I 26:13–19"> Let a third part of you go in on the Sabbath day, and keep ye the watch of the king’s house in the porch; and another third in the gate of the high way, and a third at the gate behind the footmen; and keep ye the guard of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles II 23:6; Kings IV 11:5"> And there shall be two parties among you, even everyone that goes out on the Sabbath, and they shall keep the guard of the Lord’s house before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 27:17; Chronicles II 23:7; Kings III 2:28–31"> And do ye compass the king about every man with his weapon in his hand, and he that goes into the ranges shall die: and they shall be with the king in his going out and in his coming in.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Chronicles II 23:8; Kings IV 11:4; Chronicles I 26:26"> And the captains of hundreds did all things that the wise Jehoiada commanded; and they took each his men, both those that went in on the Sabbath day, and those that went out on the Sabbath day, and went in to Jehoiada the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings II 8:7; Chronicles I 18:7; Chronicles II 5:1"> And the priest gave to the captains of hundreds the swords and spears of King David that were in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings IV 11:8; Ezekiel 8:16; Kings IV 11:10"> And the footmen stood each with his weapon in his hand from the right corner of the house to the left corner of the house, by the altar and the house round about the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 25:16; Kings I 10:24; Kings III 1:39"> And he brought forth the king’s son, and put upon him the crown and gave him the testimony; and he made him king, and anointed him: and they clapped their hands, and said, Long live the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles II 23:12–15"> And Athaliah heard the sound of the people running, and she went in to the people to the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles II 34:31; Kings IV 23:3; Kings IV 9:23"> And she looked, and, behold, the king stood near a pillar according to the manner; and the singers and the trumpeters were before the king and all the people of the land even rejoicing and sounding with trumpets: and Athaliah rent her garments, and cried, A conspiracy, a conspiracy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:7; Chronicles II 23:14; Chronicles II 23:9"> And Jehoiada the priest commanded the captains of hundreds who were over the host, and said to them, Bring her forth without the ranges, and he that goes in after her shall certainly die by the sword. For the priest said, Let her not however be slain in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; Chronicles II 23:15; Matthew 7:2"> And they laid hands upon her, and went in by the way of the horses’ entrance into the house of the Lord, and she was slain there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings II 5:3; Chronicles II 15:12–14; Chronicles II 34:31"> And Jehoiada made a covenant between the Lord and the king and the people, that they should be the Lord’s people; also between the king and the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:3; Kings IV 10:26; Kings III 18:40"> And all the people of the land went into the house of Baal, and tore it down, and completely broke in pieces his altars and his images, and they slew Mattan the priest of Baal before the altars. And the priest appointed overseers over the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:25; Jeremiah 22:4; Chronicles II 23:19"> And he took the captains of the hundreds, and the Carites, and the Rhasim, and all the people of the land, and brought down the king out of the house of the Lord; and they went in by the way of the gate of the footmen of the king’s house, and seated him there on the throne of the kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 29:2; Kings IV 11:14"> And all the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was at rest: and they slew Athaliah with the sword in the house of the king.</span>
</div>
