<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings IV 1:17; Kings III 22:51; Kings IV 8:16"> And Jehoram the son of Ahab began to reign in Israel in the eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and he reigned twelve years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings IV 10:18; Kings IV 10:26–28; Kings III 21:25"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, only not as his father, nor as his mother: and he removed the pillars of Baal which his father had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings III 14:16; Kings III 15:34; Kings IV 15:18"> Only he adhered to the sin of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who made Israel to sin; he departed not from it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings II 8:2; Genesis 26:13–14; Job 1:3"> And Mesha king of Moab was a sheepmaster, and he rendered to the king of Israel in the beginning of the year, a hundred thousand lambs, and a hundred thousand rams, with the wool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 1:1; Kings IV 8:20; Chronicles II 21:8–10"> And it came to pass, after the death of Ahab, that the king of Moab rebelled against the king of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings II 24:1–25; Kings I 11:8; Kings I 15:4"> And King Jehoram went forth in that day out of Samaria, and numbered Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings III 22:4; Chronicles II 22:10–12; Chronicles II 18:3"> And he went and sent to Jehoshaphat king of Judah, saying, The king of Moab has rebelled against me: wilt thou go with me against Moab to war? And he said, I will go up: thou art as I, I am as thou; as my people, so is thy people, as my horses, so are thy horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Malachi 1:2–3; Numbers 21:4"> And he said, What way shall I go up? and he said, The way of the wilderness of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 22:47; Numbers 20:2; Exodus 17:1"> And the king of Israel went, and the king of Judah, and the king of Edom: and they fetched a compass of seven days’ journey; and there was no water for the army, and for the cattle that went with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 78:34–36; Isaiah 51:20; Isaiah 8:21"> And the king of Israel said, Alas! that the Lord should have called the three kings on their way, to give them into the hand of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings III 22:7; Kings III 19:21; Kings IV 3:3"> And Jehoshaphat said, Is there not here a prophet of the Lord, that we may inquire of the Lord by him? And one of the servants of the king of Israel answered and said, There is here Elisha son of Shaphat, who poured water on the hands of Elijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 5:15; Kings IV 2:21; Isaiah 60:14"> And Jehoshaphat said, He has the word of the Lord. And the king of Israel, and Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and the king of Edom, went down to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 22:6; Kings III 18:19; Kings III 22:10–11"> And Elisha said to the king of Israel, What have I to do with thee? go to the prophets of thy father, and the prophets of thy mother. And the king of Israel said to him, Has the Lord called the three kings to deliver them into the hands of Moab?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings IV 5:16; Kings III 17:1; Chronicles II 19:3–4"> And Elisha said, As the Lord of hosts before whom I stand lives, unless I regarded the presence of Jehoshaphat the king of Judah, I would not have looked on thee, nor seen thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 16:23; Ephesians 5:18–19; Ezekiel 1:3"> And now fetch me a harper. And it came to pass, as the harper harped, that the hand of the Lord came upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 2:16–18; Kings IV 4:3"> And he said, Thus saith the Lord, Make this valley full of trenches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 107:35; Isaiah 41:17–18; Isaiah 48:21"> For thus saith the Lord, Ye shall not see wind, neither shall ye see rain, yet this valley shall be filled with water, and ye, and your flocks, and your cattle shall drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:27; Luke 1:37; Jeremiah 32:17"> And this is a light thing in the eyes of the Lord: I will also deliver Moab into your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings IV 13:17; Deuteronomy 20:19–20; Judges 6:16"> And ye shall smite every strong city, and ye shall cut down every good tree, and ye shall stop all wells of water, and spoil every good piece of land with stones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 29:39–40; Daniel 9:21; Kings III 18:36"> And it came to pass in the morning, when the sacrifice was offered, that, behold! waters came from the way of Edom, and the land was filled with water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 6:14; Kings III 20:11"> And all Moab heard that the three kings were come up to fight against them; and they cried out on every side, even all that were girded with a girdle, and they said, Ho! and stood upon the border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And they rose early in the morning, and the sun rose upon the waters, and Moab saw the waters on the opposite side red as blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Exodus 15:9; Isaiah 10:14"> And they said, This is the blood of the sword; and the kings have fought, and each man has smitten his neighbor; now then to the spoils, Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3–4; Joshua 8:20–22; Judges 20:40–46"> And they entered into the camp of Israel; and Israel arose and smote Moab, and they fled from before them; and they went on and smote Moab as they went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 16:7; Jeremiah 48:36; Jeremiah 48:31"> And they razed the cities, and cast every man his stone on every good piece of land and filled it; and they stopped every well, and cut down every good tree, until they left only the stones of the wall cast down; and the slingers compassed the land, and smote it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings IV 3:9; Amos 2:1"> And the king of Moab saw that the battle prevailed against him; and he took with him seven hundred men that drew sword, to cut through to the king of Edom: and they could not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 6:7; Deuteronomy 12:31; Judges 11:31"> And he took his eldest son whom he had designed to reign in his stead, and offered him up for a whole burnt offering on the walls. And there was a great indignation against Israel; and they departed from him, and returned to their land.</span>
</div>
