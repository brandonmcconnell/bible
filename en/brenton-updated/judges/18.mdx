<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 17:6; Judges 19:1; Judges 1:34"> In those days there was no king in Israel; and in those days the tribe of Dan sought for itself an inheritance to inhabit, because no inheritance had fallen to it until that day in the midst of the tribes of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 13:25; Joshua 2:1; Judges 18:11"> And the sons of Dan sent from their families five men of valor, from Zorah and from Eshtaol, to spy out the land and to search it; and they said to them, Go and search out the land. And they came as far as the mount of Ephraim to the house of Micah and they lodged there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 12:6; Genesis 27:22; Matthew 26:73"> in the house of Micah, and they recognized the voice of the young man the Levite, and turned in thither; and said to him, Who brought thee in hither? and what doest thou in this place? and what hast thou here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 17:10; Ezekiel 13:19; Proverbs 28:21"> And he said to them, Thus and thus did Micah to me, and he hired me, and I became his priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:10; Isaiah 30:1; Kings III 22:5"> And they said to him, Inquire now of God, and we shall know whether our way will prosper, on which we are going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 22:6; Kings III 22:15; Psalms 33:18"> And the priest said to them, Go in peace; your way in which ye go, is before the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:27–28; Kings I 3:13"> And the five men went on, and came to Laish; and they saw the people in the midst of it dwelling securely, at ease as is the manner of the Sidonians, and there is no one perverting or shaming a matter in the land, no heir extorting treasures; and they are far from the Sidonians, and they have no intercourse with anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 18:11; Judges 18:2; Judges 13:2"> And the five men came to their brethren to Zorah and Eshtaol, and said to their brethren, Why sit ye here idle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 2:23–24; Numbers 13:30; Joshua 18:3"> And they said, Arise, and let us go up against them, for we have seen the land, and, behold, it is very good, yet ye are still: delay not to go, and to enter in to possess the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 18:27; Judges 18:7; Deuteronomy 2:29"> And whensoever ye shall go, ye shall come in upon a people secure, and the land is extensive, for God has given it into your hand; a place where there is no want of anything that the earth affords.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 18:16"> And there departed thence of the families of Dan, from Zorah and from Eshtaol, six hundred men, girded with weapons of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:25; Joshua 15:60; Chronicles II 1:4"> And they went up, and encamped in Kiriath-jearim in Judah; therefore it was called in that place the camp of Dan, until this day: behold, it is behind Kiriath-jearim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 24:30; Judges 19:1; Judges 18:2–3"> And they went on thence to the mount of Ephraim, and came to the house of Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 17:5; Judges 18:3–4; Kings I 14:28"> And the five men who went to spy out the land of Laish answered, and said to their brethren, Ye know that there is in this place an ephod, and teraphim, and a graven and a molten image; and now consider what ye shall do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 43:27; Kings I 17:22; John 14:27"> And they turned aside there, and went into the house of the young man, the Levite, even into the house of Micah, and asked him how he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 18:11"> And the six hundred men of the sons of Dan who were girded with their weapons of war stood by the door of the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 18:14; Judges 17:4–5; Judges 18:2"> And the five men who went to spy out the land went up, and entered into the house of Micah, and the priest stood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And they took the graven image, and the ephod, and the teraphim, and the molten image; and the priest said to them, What are ye doing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 29:9; Job 21:5; Judges 17:10"> And they said to him, Be silent, lay thy hand upon thy mouth, and come with us, and be to us a father and a priest: is it better for thee to be the priest of the house of one man, or to be the priest of a tribe and house for a family of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Ezekiel 13:19; Proverbs 30:15"> And the heart of the priest was glad, and he took the ephod, and the teraphim, and the graven image, and the molten image, and went in the midst of the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So they turned and departed, and put their children and their property and their baggage before them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> They went some distance from the house of Micah, and, behold, Micah and the men in the houses near Micah’s house, cried out, and overtook the children of Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 114:5; Kings II 14:5; Kings I 11:5"> And the children of Dan turned their face, and said to Micah, What is the matter with thee that thou hast cried out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18–19; Isaiah 44:18–20; Psalms 115:8"> And Micah said, Because ye have taken my graven image which I made, and my priest, and are gone; and what have I remaining? and what is this that ye say to me, Why criest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 27:2; Job 3:5; Kings I 30:6"> And the children of Dan said to him, Let not thy voice be heard with us, lest angry men run upon thee, and take away thy life, and the lives of thy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the children of Dan went their way; and Micah saw that they were stronger than himself, and he returned to his house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:7; Judges 18:10"> And the children of Dan took what Micah had made, and the priest that he had, and they came to Laish, to a people quiet and secure; and they smote them with the edge of the sword, and burned the city with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings II 10:6; Judges 18:7; Numbers 13:21"> And there was no deliverer, because the city is far from the Sidonians, and they have no intercourse with men, and it is in the valley of the house of Rehob; and they built the city, and dwelt in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Genesis 14:14; Kings III 15:20"> And they called the name of the city Dan, after the name of Dan their father, who was born to Israel; and the name of the city was Laish before.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings I 4:10–11; Judges 13:1; Judges 17:5"> And the children of Dan set up the graven image for themselves; and Jonathan son of Gershom son of Manasseh, he and his sons were priests to the tribe of Dan till the time of the carrying away of the nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Kings I 1:3; Judges 19:18"> And they set up for themselves the graven image which Micah made, all the days that the house of God was in Shiloh; and it was so in those days that there was no king in Israel.</span>
</div>
