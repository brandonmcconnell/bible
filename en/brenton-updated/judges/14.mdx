<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; Joshua 19:43; 1 John 2:16"> And Samson went down to Timnah, and saw a woman in Timnah of the daughters of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 34:4; Genesis 21:21; Genesis 24:2–3"> And he went up and told his father and his mother, and said, I have seen a woman in Timnah of the daughters of the Philistines; and now take her to me for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 15:18; Kings I 17:36; Kings I 14:6"> And his father and his mother said to him, Are there no daughters of thy brethren, and is there not a woman of all my people, that thou goest to take a wife of the uncircumcised Philistines?<br />And Samson said to his father, Take her for me, for she is right in my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:20; Psalms 115:3; Judges 13:1"> And his father and his mother knew not that it was of the Lord, that he sought to be revenged on the Philistines: and at that time the Philistines lorded it over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Samson and his father and his mother went down to Timnah, and he came to the vineyard of Timnah; and behold, a young lion roared in meeting him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25; Kings I 11:6"> And the Spirit of the Lord came powerfully upon him, and he crushed him as he would have crushed a kid of the goats, and there was nothing in his hands: and he told not his father and his mother what he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And they went down and spoke to the woman, and she was pleasing in the eyes of Samson.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 1:20; Genesis 29:21"> And after some time he returned to take her, and he turned aside to see the carcass of the lion; and behold, a swarm of bees, and honey were in the mouth of the lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 14:25–30; Proverbs 25:15"> And he took it into his hands, and went on eating, and he went to his father and his mother, and gave to them, and they did eat; but he told them not that he took the honey out of the mouth of the lion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 29:22; Revelation 19:9; Esther 1:7–22"> And his father went down to the woman, and Samson made there a banquet for seven days, for so the young men are used to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 9:15; Kings I 16:6; Kings I 10:23"> And it came to pass when they saw him, that they took thirty guests, and they were with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings III 10:1; Ezekiel 17:2; Genesis 45:22"> And Samson said to them, I propound you a riddle: if ye will indeed tell it me, and discover it within the seven days of the feast, I will give you thirty sheets and thirty changes of raiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And if ye cannot tell it me, ye shall give me thirty napkins and thirty changes of apparel: and they said to him, Propound thy riddle, and we will hear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:2–4; Kings III 17:6; Romans 8:37"> And he said to them,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Meat came forth of the eater,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And sweetness out of the strong:</span><br />and they could not tell the riddle for three days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 16:5; Judges 15:6; Proverbs 6:26"> And it came to pass on the fourth day, that they said to the wife of Samson, Deceive now thy husband, and let him tell thee the riddle, lest we burn thee and thy father’s house with fire: did ye invite us to do us violence?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 16:15; Genesis 2:24"> And Samson’s wife wept before him, and said, Thou dost but hate me, and lovest me not; for the riddle which thou hast propounded to the children of my people, thou hast not told me: and Samson said to her, If I have not told it to my father and my mother, shall I tell it to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 16:16; Job 2:9; Judges 16:13"> And she wept before him the seven days, during which their banquet lasted: and it came to pass on the seventh day, that he told her, because she troubled him; and she told it to the children of her people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the men of the city said to him on the seventh day, before sunrise,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">What is sweeter than honey?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And what is stronger than a lion?</span><br />and Samson said to them,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">If ye had not plowed with my heifer,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Ye would not have known my riddle.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings I 11:6; Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25"> And the Spirit of the Lord came upon him powerfully, and he went down to Ashkelon, and destroyed of the inhabitants thirty men, and took their garments, and gave the changes of raiment to them that told the riddle; and Samson was very angry, and went up to the house of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 15:2; John 3:29; Psalms 55:12–13"> And the wife of Samson was given to one of his friends, with whom he was on terms of friendship.</span>
