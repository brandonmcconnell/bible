<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 20:18; Numbers 27:21; Judges 20:28"> And it came to pass after the death of Joshua, that the children of Israel inquired of the Lord, saying, Who shall go up for us first against the Canaanites, to fight against them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; Hebrews 7:14; Numbers 2:3"> And the Lord said, Judah shall go up: behold, I have delivered the land into his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings II 10:11; Joshua 19:1; Judges 1:17"> And Judah said to his brother Simeon, Come up with me into my lot, and let us array ourselves against the Canaanites, and I also will go with thee into thy lot: and Simeon went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings I 11:8; Exodus 23:28–29; Joshua 11:6–8"> And Judah went up; and the Lord delivered the Canaanite and the Perizzite into their hands, and they smote them in Bezek to the number of ten thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And they overtook Adoni-bezek in Bezek, and fought against him; and they smote the Canaanite and the Perizzite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Adoni-bezek fled, and they pursued after him, and took him, and cut off his thumbs and his great toes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings I 15:33; Revelation 13:10; Luke 6:37–38"> And Adoni-bezek said, Seventy kings, having their thumbs and their great toes cut off, gathered their food under my table: as I therefore have done, so God has recompensed me: and they brought him to Jerusalem, and he died there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 15:63; Judges 1:21"> And the children of Judah fought against Jerusalem, and took it, and smote it with the edge of the sword, and they burned the city with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 15:13–20; Joshua 11:21; Joshua 10:36"> And afterward the children of Judah went down to fight with the Canaanite dwelling in the hill country, and the south, and the plain country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Joshua 14:15; Jeremiah 9:23"> And Judah went to the Canaanite who dwelt in Hebron; and Hebron came out against him; and the name of Hebron before was Kiriath-arba-sepher: and they smote Sheshai, and Ahiman, and Talmai, children of Anak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 15:15; Joshua 10:38–39"> And they went up thence to the inhabitants of Debir; but the name of Debir was before Kiriath-sepher, the city of Letters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:16–17; Kings I 17:25; Kings I 18:23"> And Caleb said, Whosoever shall smite the city of Letters, and shall first take it, I will give to him Achsah my daughter to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 3:9"> And Othniel the younger son of Kenaz the brother of Caleb took it; and Caleb gave him his daughter Achsah to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 15:18–19"> And it came to pass as she went in, that Othniel urged her to ask a field of her father; and she murmured and cried from off her ass, Thou hast sent me forth into a south land: and Caleb said to her, What is thy request?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 25:18; 2 Corinthians 9:5; 1 Peter 3:9"> And Achsah said to him, Give me, I pray thee, a blessing, for thou hast sent me forth into a south land, and thou shalt give me the ransom of water: and Caleb gave her according to her heart the ransom of the upper springs and the ransom of the low springs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 10:29–32; Judges 4:11; Kings I 15:6"> And the children of Jethro the Kenite the father-in-law of Moses went up from the city of palm trees with the children of Judah, to the wilderness that is in the south of Judah, which is at the descent of Arad, and they dwelt with the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 21:3; Judges 1:3; Joshua 19:4"> And Judah went with Simeon his brother, and smote the Canaanite that inhabited Zephath, and they utterly destroyed them; and they called the name of the city Anathema.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 11:22; Judges 3:3; Joshua 15:45–47"> But Judah did not inherit Gaza nor her coasts, nor Ashkelon nor her coasts, nor Ekron nor her coasts, nor Ashdod nor the lands around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Judges 1:2; Psalms 60:12"> And the Lord was with Judah, and he inherited the mountain; for they were not able to destroy the inhabitants of the valley, for Rechab prevented them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 1:10; Joshua 15:13–14; Numbers 14:24"> And they gave Hebron to Caleb, as Moses said; and thence he inherited the three cities of the children of Anak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 15:63; Judges 19:10–12; Judges 1:8"> But the children of Benjamin did not take the inheritance of the Jebusite who dwelt in Jerusalem; and the Jebusite dwelt with the children of Benjamin in Jerusalem until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 1:19; Kings IV 18:7; Numbers 1:32"> And the sons of Joseph, they also went up to Bethel; and the Lord was with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Genesis 35:6; Genesis 48:3"> And they encamped and surveyed Bethel: and the name of the city before was Luz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 2:12–14; Kings I 30:15"> And the spies looked, and behold, a man went out of the city, and they took him; and they said to him, Show us the way into the city, and we will deal mercifully with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 6:22–25"> And he showed them the way into the city; and they smote the city with the edge of the sword; but they let go the man and his family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings IV 7:6; Chronicles II 1:17"> And the man went into the land of the Hittites, and built there a city, and called the name of it Luz; this is its name until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 17:11–13; Jeremiah 48:10; Psalms 106:34–35"> And Manasseh did not drive out the inhabitants of Beth-shean, which is a city of Scythians, nor her towns, nor her suburbs; nor Taanach, nor her towns; nor the inhabitants of Dor, nor her towns; nor the inhabitant of Balac, nor her suburbs, nor her towns; nor the inhabitants of Megiddo, nor her suburbs, nor her towns; nor the inhabitants of Ibleam, nor her suburbs, nor her towns; and the Canaanite began to dwell in this land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And it came to pass when Israel was strong, that he made the Canaanite tributary, but did not utterly drive them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; Kings III 9:16"> And Ephraim did not drive out the Canaanite that dwelt in Gezer; and the Canaanite dwelt in the midst of him in Gezer, and became tributary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 19:15"> And Zebulun did not drive out the inhabitants of Kitron, nor the inhabitants of Domana: and the Canaanite dwelt in the midst of them, and became tributary to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 19:24–30"> And Asher did not drive out the inhabitants of Acco, and that people became tributary to him, nor the inhabitants of Dor, nor the inhabitants of Sidon, nor the inhabitants of Ahlab, nor Achzib, nor Helbah, nor Nai, nor Rehob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 106:34–35"> And Asher dwelt in the midst of the Canaanite who inhabited the land, for he could not drive him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 19:32–38; Judges 1:30; Psalms 18:24"> And Naphtali did not drive out the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh, nor the inhabitants of Beth-anath; and Naphtali dwelt in the midst of the Canaanite who inhabited the land: but the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh and of Beth-anath became tributary to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:1"> And the Amorite drove out the children of Dan into the mountain, for they did not suffer them to come down into the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Joshua 19:42; Judges 12:12; Joshua 10:12"> And the Amorite began to dwell in the mountain of shells, in which are bears, and foxes, in Myrsinon, and in Shaalbim; and the hand of the house of Joseph was heavy upon the Amorite, and he became tributary to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 34:4; Joshua 15:2–3"> And the border of the Amorite was from the going up of Akrabbim, from the rock and upward.</span>
