<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:12–33"> And Jephthah said to them, I and my people and the children of Ammon were very much engaged in war; and I called for you, and ye did not save me out of their hand.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings I 25:10; Deuteronomy 3:12–17; Proverbs 15:1"> And Jephthah gathered all the men of Gilead, and fought with Ephraim; and the men of Gilead smote Ephraim, because they that were escaped of Ephraim said, Ye are of Gilead in the midst of Ephraim and in the midst of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 3:28; Joshua 22:11; Joshua 2:7"> And Gilead took the fords of Jordan before Ephraim; and they that escaped of Ephraim said to them, Let us go over: and the men of Gilead said, Art thou an Ephraimite? and he said, No.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:2; Psalms 69:15; Mark 14:70"> Then they said to him, Say now Stachys; and he did not rightly pronounce it so: and they took him, and slew him at the fords of Jordan; and there fell at that time of Ephraim two and forty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Jephthah judged Israel six years; and Jephthah the Gileadite died, and was buried in his city Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Kings I 16:1; Matthew 2:1"> And after him Ibzan of Bethlehem judged Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 10:4; Judges 12:14"> And he had thirty sons, and thirty daughters, whom he sent forth; and he brought in thirty daughters for his sons from without; and he judged Israel seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Ibzan died, and was buried in Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And after him Elon of Zebulun judged Israel ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles I 8:13; Chronicles I 6:69; Joshua 19:42"> And Elon of Zebulun died, and was buried in Aijalon in the land of Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And after him Abdon the son of Hillel, the Pirathonite, judged Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 5:10; Judges 10:4"> And he had forty sons, and thirty grandsons, that rode upon seventy colts: and he judged Israel eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 5:14; Judges 3:13; Judges 3:27"> And Abdon the son of Hillel, the Pirathonite, died, and was buried in Pirathon in the land of Ephraim in the mount of Amalek.</span>
