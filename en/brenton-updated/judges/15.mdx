<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 14:20; Judges 14:16; Genesis 38:14"> And her father spoke, saying, I said that thou didst surely hate her, and I gave her to one of thy friends: is not her younger sister better than she? let her be to thee instead of her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 14:15"> And Samson said to them, Even for once am I guiltless with regard to the Philistines, in that I do mischief among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:15; Psalms 63:10; Lamentations 5:18"> And Samson went and caught three hundred foxes, and took torches, and turned tail to tail, and put a torch between two tails, and fastened it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings II 14:30; Exodus 22:6"> And he set fire to the torches, and sent the foxes into the corn of the Philistines; and everything was burned from the threshing floor to the standing corn, and even to the vineyard and olives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 14:15; 1 Thessalonians 4:6; Proverbs 22:8"> And the Philistines said, Who has done these things? and they said, Samson the son-in-law of the Timnite, because he has taken his wife, and given her to one of his friends; and the Philistines went up, and burned her and her father’s house with fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 14:4; Romans 12:19; Judges 14:19"> And Samson said to them, Though ye may have dealt thus with her, verily I will be avenged of you, and afterward I will cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 63:3; Isaiah 63:6; Isaiah 25:10"> And he smote them leg on thigh with a great overthrow; and went down and dwelt in a cave of the rock Etam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 15:17; Judges 15:19; Judges 15:14"> And the Philistines went up, and encamped in Judah, and spread themselves abroad in Lehi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the men of Judah said, Why are ye come up against us? and the Philistines said, We are come up to bind Samson, and to do to him as he has done to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 13:1; Judges 14:4; Deuteronomy 28:47–48"> And the three thousand men of Judah went down to the hole of the rock Etam, and they said to Samson, Knowest thou not that the Philistines rule over us? and what is this that thou hast done to us? and Samson said to them, As they did to me, so have I done to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 8:21; Acts 7:25; Kings III 2:25"> And they said to him, We are come down to bind thee to deliver thee into the hand of the Philistines: and Samson said to them, Swear to me that ye will not fall upon me yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And they spoke to him, saying, Nay, but we will only bind thee fast, and deliver thee into their hand, and will by no means slay thee: and they bound him with two new ropes, and brought him from that rock.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 14:19; Judges 3:10; Psalms 118:11"> And they came to Lehi: and the Philistines shouted, and ran to meet him: and the Spirit of the Lord came mightily upon him, and the ropes that were upon his arms became as tow which is burned with fire; and his bonds were consumed from off his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 23:10; Leviticus 26:8; Judges 3:31"> And he found the jawbone of an ass that had been cast away, and he put forth his hand and took it, and smote with it a thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Samson said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">With the jawbone of an ass I have utterly destroyed them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For with the jawbone of an ass I have smitten a thousand men.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And it came to pass when he ceased speaking, that he cast the jawbone out of his hand; and he called that place the Lifting of the jawbone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7–8; Hebrews 11:32; Judges 8:4"> And he was very thirsty, and wept before the Lord, and said, Thou hast been well pleased to grant this great deliverance by the hand of thy servant, and now shall I die for thirst, and fall into the hand of the uncircumcised?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Genesis 45:27; Psalms 34:6"> And God broke open a hollow place in the jaw, and there came thence water, and he drank; and his spirit returned and he revived: therefore the name of the fountain was called, The well of the invoker, which is in Lehi, until this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
