</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:10–14; Matthew 14:30–31"> And Barak said to her, If thou wilt go with me, I will go; and if thou wilt not go, I will not go; for I know not the day on which the Lord prospers his messenger with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Kings I 2:30; Judges 4:17–22"> And she said, I will surely go with thee; but know that thy honor shall not attend on the expedition on which thou goest, for the Lord shall sell Sisera into the hands of a woman: and Deborah arose, and went with Barak out of Kedesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 5:18; Judges 5:15; Judges 4:14"> And Barak called Zebulun and Naphtali out of Kedesh, and there went up at his feet ten thousand men, and Deborah went up with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Joshua 19:33; Numbers 10:29"> And Heber the Kenite had removed from Caina, from the sons of Jobab the father-in-law of Moses, and pitched his tent by the oak of the covetous ones, which is near Kedesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 89:12; Jeremiah 46:18; Joshua 19:12"> And it was told Sisera that Barak the son of Abinoam was gone up to Mount Tabor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 4:2–3; Judges 4:7"> And Sisera summoned all his chariots, nine hundred chariots of iron and all the people with him, from Harosheth of the Gentiles to the brook of Kishon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:3; Kings II 5:24; Isaiah 52:12"> And Deborah said to Barak, Rise up, for this is the day on which the Lord has delivered Sisera into thy hand, for the Lord shall go forth before thee: and Barak went down from Mount Tabor, and ten thousand men after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 10:10; Psalms 83:9–10; Judges 5:20–21"> And the Lord discomfited Sisera, and all his chariots, and all his army, with the edge of the sword before Barak: and Sisera descended from off his chariot, and fled on his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 2:12; Leviticus 26:7–8; James 2:13"> And Barak pursued after the chariots and after the army, into Harosheth of the Gentiles; and the whole army of Sisera fell by the edge of the sword, there was not one left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 69:22; Isaiah 57:21; Amos 5:19–20"> And Sisera fled on his feet to the tent of Jael the wife of Heber the Kenite his friend: for there was peace between Jabin king of Hazor and the house of Heber the Kenite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings IV 6:19"> And Jael went out to meet Sisera, and said to him, Turn aside, my lord, turn aside to me, fear not: and he turned aside to her into the tent; and she covered him with a mantle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 5:25–26; Genesis 24:43; Isaiah 41:17"> And Sisera said to her, Give me, I pray thee, a little water to drink, for I am thirsty: and she opened a bottle of milk, and gave him to drink, and covered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 2:3–5; Kings II 17:20"> And Sisera said to her, Stand now by the door of the tent, and it shall come to pass if any man come to thee, and ask of thee, and say, Is there any man here? then thou shalt say, There is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 5:26–27; 1 Corinthians 1:27; Judges 3:21"> And Jael the wife of Heber took a pin of the tent, and took a hammer in her hand, and went secretly to him, and fastened the pin in his temple, and it went through to the earth, and he fainted away, and darkness fell upon him and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings II 17:10–15; Kings II 17:3"> And, behold, Barak was pursuing Sisera: and Jael went out to meet him, and she said to him, Come, and I will show thee the man whom thou seekest: and he went in to her; and, behold, Sisera was fallen dead, and the pin was in his temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:24; Psalms 18:47; Psalms 18:39"> So God routed Jabin king of Canaan in that day before the children of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings I 3:12"> And the hand of the children of Israel prevailed more and more against Jabin king of Canaan, until they utterly destroyed Jabin king of Canaan.</span>
</div>
