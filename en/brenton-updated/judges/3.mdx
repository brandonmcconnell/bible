<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 2:21–22; Deuteronomy 7:22; Proverbs 17:3"> And these are the nations which the Lord left to prove Israel with them, all that had not known the wars of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 3:5; Genesis 3:7; 1 Corinthians 9:26–27"> Only for the sake of the generations of Israel, to teach them war, only the men before them knew them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 13:3; Joshua 13:5; Judges 10:7"> The five lordships of the Philistines, and every Canaanite, and the Sidonian, and the Hivite who dwelt in Lebanon from the mount of Hermon to Lebo-hamath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 3:1; Judges 2:22; Exodus 15:25"> And this was done in order to prove Israel by them, to know whether they would obey the commands of the Lord, which he charged their fathers by the hand of Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 106:34–38; Genesis 10:15–18; Exodus 3:8"> And the children of Israel dwelt in the midst of the Canaanite, and the Hittite, and the Amorite, and the Perizzite, and the Hivite, and the Jebusite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3–4; Exodus 34:16; Kings III 11:1–5"> And they took their daughters for wives to themselves, and they gave their daughters to their sons, and served their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 34:13; Kings IV 23:6; Kings III 18:19"> And the children of Israel did evil in the sight of the Lord, and forgot the Lord their God, and served Baalim and the groves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Habakkuk 3:7; Judges 4:9"> And the Lord was very angry with Israel, and sold them into the hand of Cushan-rishathaim king of Syria of the rivers: and the children of Israel served Cushan-rishathaim eight years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 3:15; Judges 1:13; Psalms 22:5"> And the children of Israel cried to the Lord; and the Lord raised up a savior to Israel, and he saved them, Othniel the son of Kenaz, the brother of Caleb younger than himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 13:25; Kings I 11:6; Judges 14:19"> And the Spirit of the Lord came upon him, and he judged Israel; and he went out to war against Cushan-rishathaim: and the Lord delivered into his hand Cushan-rishathaim king of Syria of the rivers, and his hand prevailed against Cushan-rishathaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Judges 8:28; Joshua 11:23"> And the land was quiet forty years; and Othniel the son of Kenaz died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings I 12:9; Judges 2:19; Judges 2:14"> And the children of Israel continued to do evil before the Lord: and the Lord strengthened Eglon king of Moab against Israel, because they had done evil before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Deuteronomy 34:3; Judges 5:14"> And he gathered to himself all the children of Ammon and Amalek, and went and smote Israel, and took possession of the city of Palm trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:47–48; Deuteronomy 28:40; Leviticus 26:23–25"> And the children of Israel served Eglon the king of Moab eighteen years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 3:9; Psalms 78:34; Isaiah 36:16"> And the children of Israel cried to the Lord; and he raised up to them a savior, Ehud the son of Gera a son of Jamin, a man who used both hands alike: and the children of Israel sent gifts by his hand to Eglon king of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Song of Solomon 3:8; Revelation 2:12; Psalms 149:6"> And Ehud made himself a dagger of two edges, of a span long, and he girded it under his cloak upon his right thigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:20; Job 15:27; Jeremiah 50:11"> And he went, and brought the presents to Eglon king of Moab, and Eglon was a very handsome man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And it came to pass when Ehud had made an end of offering his gifts, that he dismissed those that brought the gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 4:20; Judges 3:20; Acts 23:18–19"> And he himself returned from the quarries that are by Gilgal; and Ehud said, I have a secret errand to thee, O king! and Eglon said to him, Be silent: and he sent away from his presence all who waited upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:7; Amos 3:15; Psalms 29:1"> And Ehud went in to him; and he sat in his own upper summer chamber quite alone; and Ehud said, I have a message from God to thee, O king: and Eglon rose up from his throne near him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 13:3; Job 20:25; Kings I 15:33"> And it came to pass as he arose, that Ehud stretched forth his left hand, and took the dagger off his right thigh, and plunged it into his belly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and drove in also the haft after the blade, and the fat closed in upon the blade, for he drew not out the dagger from his belly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Ehud went out to the porch, and passed out by the appointed guards, and shut the doors of the chamber upon him, and locked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings I 24:3"> And he went out: and Eglon’s servants came, and saw, and behold, the doors of the upper chamber were locked; and they said, Does he not uncover his feet in the summer chamber?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And they waited till they were ashamed, and, behold, there was no one that opened the doors of the upper chamber; and they took the key, and opened them; and, behold, their lord was fallen down dead upon the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Ehud escaped while they were in a tumult, and no one paid attention to him; and he passed the quarries, and escaped to Seirah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 6:34; Kings I 13:3; Judges 5:14"> And it came to pass when Ehud came into the land of Israel, that he blew the horn in Mount Ephraim, and the children of Israel came down with him from the mountain, and he was before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 12:5; Judges 7:9; Judges 7:15"> And he said to them, Come down after me, for the Lord God has delivered our enemies, even Moab, into our hand; and they went down after him, and seized on the fords of Jordan before Moab, and he did not suffer a man to pass over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Judges 3:17; Psalms 17:10"> And they smote Moab on that day about ten thousand men, every lusty person and every mighty man; and not a man escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 3:11; Judges 5:31"> So Moab was humbled in that day under the hand of Israel, and the land had rest eighty years; and Ehud judged them till he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 5:6; 1 Corinthians 1:17; Kings I 17:50"> And after him rose up Shamgar the son of Anath, and smote the Philistines to the number of six hundred men with a plowshare such as is drawn by oxen; and he too delivered Israel.</span>
</div>
