<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 18:2; Joshua 17:14–18; Judges 10:1"> And there was a man of Mount Ephraim, and his name was Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:25; Ruth 3:10; Kings I 23:21"> And he said to his mother, The eleven hundred pieces of silver which thou tookest to thyself, and about which thou cursedst me, and spokest in my ears, behold, the silver is with me; I took it: and his mother said, Blessed be my son of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:4; Exodus 20:23; Leviticus 19:4"> And he restored the eleven hundred pieces of silver to his mother; and his mother said, I had wholly consecrated the money to the Lord out of my hand for my son, to make a graven and a molten image, and now I will restore it to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6–7; Jeremiah 10:9–10"> But he returned the silver to his mother, and his mother took two hundred pieces of silver, and gave them to a silversmith, and he made it a graven and a molten image; and it was in the house of Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 18:14; Judges 8:27; Genesis 31:19"> And the house of Micah was to him the house of God, and he made an ephod and teraphim, and he consecrated one of his sons, and he became to him a priest.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:8; Proverbs 14:12; Judges 21:25"> And in those days there was no king in Israel; every man did that which was right in his own eyes.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:1; Ruth 1:1–2"> And there was a young man in Bethlehem of the tribe of Judah, and he was a Levite, and he was sojourning there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:10–11; Judges 17:11; Joshua 24:33"> And the man departed from Bethlehem the city of Judah to sojourn in whatever place he might find; and he came as far as Mount Ephraim, and to the house of Micah to accomplish his journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Micah said to him, Whence comest thou? and he said to him, I am a Levite of Bethlehem Judah, and I go to sojourn in any place I may find.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:19; Judges 18:19–20; 1 Timothy 6:10"> And Micah said to him, Dwell with me, and be to me a father and a priest; and I will give thee ten pieces of silver by the year, and a change of raiment, and thy living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the Levite went and began to dwell with the man; and the young man was to him as one of his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 18:30; Numbers 16:8–10; Numbers 16:5"> And Micah consecrated the Levite, and he became to him a priest, and he was in the house of Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 10:2–3; Proverbs 14:12; John 16:2"> And Micah said, Now I know that the Lord will do me good, because a Levite has become my priest.</span>
