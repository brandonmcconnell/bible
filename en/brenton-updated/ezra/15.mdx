<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the cry of the people and their wives was great against their brethren the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And some said, We are numerous with our sons and our daughters; so we will take corn, and eat, and live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And some said, As to our fields and vineyards and houses, let us pledge them, and we will take corn, and eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And some said, We have borrowed money for the king’s tributes—our fields, and our vineyards, and houses are pledged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And now our flesh is as the flesh of our brethren, our children are as their children: yet, behold, we are reducing our sons and our daughters to slavery, and some of our daughters are enslaved: and there is no power of our hands, for our fields and our vineyards belong to the nobles.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And I was much grieved as I heard their cry and these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And my heart took counsel within me, and I contended against the nobles, and the princes, and I said to them, Should every man demand of his brother what ye demand? And I appointed against them a great assembly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and I said to them, We of our free will have redeemed our brethren the Jews that were sold to the Gentiles; and do ye sell your brethren? and shall they be delivered to us? And they were silent, and found no answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And I said, The thing which ye do is not good; ye will not so walk in the fear of our God because of the reproach of the Gentiles our enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Both my brethren, and my acquaintances, and I, have lent them money and corn: let us now leave off this exaction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Restore to them, I pray, as at this day, their fields, and their vineyards, and their olive yards, and their houses, and bring forth to them corn and wine and oil of the money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And they said, We will restore, and we will not exact of them; we will do thus as thou sayest. Then I called the priests, and bound them by oath to do according to this word.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And I shook out my garment, and said, So may God shake out every man who shall not keep to this word, from his house, and from his labors, he shall be even thus shaken out, as an outcast and empty. And all the congregation said, Amen, and they praised the Lord: and the people did this thing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> From the day that he charged me to be their ruler in the land of Judah, from the twentieth year even to the thirty-second year of Artaxerxes, twelve years, I and my brethren ate not provision extorted from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> But as for the former acts of extortion wherein those who were before me oppressed them, they even took of them their last money, forty didrachmas for bread and wine; and the very outcasts of them exercised authority over the people: but I did not so, because of the fear of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Also in the work of the wall I treated them not with rigor, I bought not land: and all that were gathered together came thither to the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the Jews, to the number of a hundred and fifty men, besides those coming to us from the nations round about, were at my table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And there came to me for one day one calf, and I had six choice sheep and a goat; and every ten days wine in abundance of all sorts: yet with these I required not the bread of extortion, because the bondage was heavy upon this people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Remember me, O God, for good, in all that I have done to this people.</span>
</div>
