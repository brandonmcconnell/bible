<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:1; Chronicles I 6:4–14; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:21"> Now after these things, in the reign of Artaxerxes king of the Persians, came up Ezra the son of Seraiah, the son of Azariah, the son of Hilkiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings III 2:35; Kings II 8:17"> the son of Shallum, the son of Zadok, the son of Ahitub,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> the son of Amariah, the son of Azariah, the son of Meraioth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> the son of Zerahiah, the son of Uzzi, the son of Bukki,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 6:25; Chronicles II 26:20; Chronicles I 6:4"> the son of Abishua, the son of Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the first priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 8:22; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:28; Ezra and Nehemiah 8:31"> This Ezra went up out of Babylon; and he was a ready scribe in the law of Moses, which the Lord God of Israel gave: and the king gave him leave, for the hand of the Lord his God was upon him in all things which he sought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 8:1–20; Chronicles I 6:31–48; Chronicles I 9:17–27"> And some of the children of Israel went up, and some of the priests, and of the Levites, and the singers, and the doorkeepers, and the Nathinim, to Jerusalem, in the seventh year of Artaxerxes the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And they came to Jerusalem in the fifth month, this was the seventh year of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 7:6; Nehemiah 2:8; Nehemiah 2:18"> For in the first day of the first month he began the going up from Babylon, and in the first day of the fifth month they came to Jerusalem, for the good hand of his God was upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 2:7; 2 Timothy 4:2; Matthew 7:24"> For Ezra had determined in his heart to seek the law, and to do and teach the ordinances and judgments in Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 5:6; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:6; Matthew 23:2"> And this is the copy of the order which Artaxerxes gave to Ezra the priest, the scribe of the book of the words of the commandments of the Lord, and of his ordinances to Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 2:37; Ezekiel 26:7; Ezra and Nehemiah 4:17"> Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra, the scribe of the law of the Lord God of heaven, Let the order and the answer be accomplished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 6:1; Psalms 148:6; Chronicles II 30:5"> A decree is made by me, that everyone who is willing in my kingdom of the people of Israel, and of the priests and Levites, to go to Jerusalem, be permitted to go with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 1:14; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:25–26; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:28"> One has been sent from the king and the seven counselors, to visit Judea and Jerusalem, according to the law of their God that is in thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 135:21; Chronicles II 6:2; Ezra and Nehemiah 6:12"> And for the house of the Lord there have been sent silver and gold, which the king and the counselors have freely given to the God of Israel, who dwells in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles I 29:6; Ezra and Nehemiah 1:4; Chronicles I 29:9"> And all the silver and gold, whatsoever thou shalt find in all the land of Babylon, with the freewill offering of the people, and the priests that offer freely for the house of God which is in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5–11; Numbers 15:4–13; Ezra and Nehemiah 6:9–10"> And as for every one that arrives there, speedily order him by this letter to bring calves, rams, lambs, and their meat offerings, and their drink offerings; and thou shalt offer them on the altar of the house of your God which is in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings IV 22:7; Ephesians 5:17; Kings IV 12:15"> And whatever it shall seem good to thee and to thy brethren to do with the rest of the silver and the gold, do as it is pleasing to your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:17; Ezra and Nehemiah 8:33–34; Chronicles II 32:19"> And deliver the vessels that are given thee for the service of the house of God, before God in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 6:4; Ezra and Nehemiah 6:8–18"> And as to the rest of the need of the house of thy God, thou shalt give from the king’s treasure houses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 4:16; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:6; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:10–13"> and from me, whatever it shall seem good to thee to give.<br />I King Artaxerxes have made a decree for all the treasuries that are in the country beyond the river, that whatever Ezra the priest and scribe of the God of heaven may ask you, it shall be done speedily,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 16:6–7; Leviticus 2:13; Ezekiel 45:14"> to the amount of a hundred talents of silver, and a hundred measures of wheat, and a hundred baths of wine, and a hundred baths of oil, and salt without reckoning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:4; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:18; Ezra and Nehemiah 6:10–12"> Let whatever is in the decree of the God of heaven, be done: take heed lest anyone make an attack on the house of the God of heaven, lest at any time there should be wrath against the realm of the king and his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 7:7; Ezra and Nehemiah 4:13; Ezra and Nehemiah 2:36–55"> Also this has been declared to you, with respect to all the priests, and Levites, the singers, porters, Nathinim and ministers of the house of God, let no tribute be paid to thee; thou shalt not have power to oppress them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 7:10; Deuteronomy 16:18; James 1:5"> And thou, Ezra, as the wisdom of God is in thy hand, appoint scribes and judges, that they may judge for all the people beyond the river, all that know the law of the Lord thy God; and ye shall make it known to him that knows not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 6:11; Deuteronomy 13:1–18; Leviticus 20:1–27"> And whosoever shall not do the law of God, and the law of the king readily, judgment shall be taken upon him, whether for death or for chastisement, or for a fine of his property, or casting into prison.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 6:22; Revelation 17:17; Isaiah 60:13"> Blessed be the Lord God of our fathers, who has put it thus into the heart of the king, to glorify the house of the Lord which is in Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17–18; Ezra and Nehemiah 5:5; Genesis 32:28"> and has given me favor in the eyes of the king, and of his counselors, and all the rulers of the king, the exalted ones. And I was strengthened according to the good hand of God upon me, and I gathered chief men of Israel to go up with me.</span>
</div>
</div>
