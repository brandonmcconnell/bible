<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; James 2:25; Matthew 1:5"> And Joshua the son of Nun sent out of Shittim two young men to spy the land, saying, Go up and view the land and Jericho: and the two young men went and entered into Jericho; and they entered into the house of a harlot, whose name was Rahab, and lodged there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 43:13; Daniel 4:35; Proverbs 21:30"> And it was reported to the king of Jericho, saying, Men of the sons of Israel have come in hither to spy the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings II 10:3; Genesis 42:9–12; Acts 12:6"> And the king of Jericho sent and spoke to Rahab, saying, Bring out the men that entered into thy house this night; for they are come to spy out the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings IV 6:19; Kings II 17:19–20; Kings II 16:18–19"> And the woman took the two men and hid them; and she spoke to the messengers, saying, The men came in to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:20; Ezekiel 47:12; Revelation 21:25"> but when the gate was shut in the evening, the men went out; I know not whither they are gone: follow after them, if ye may overtake them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 11:23; James 2:25; Colossians 3:3"> But she had brought them up upon the house, and hid them in the flax stalks that were spread by her on the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 3:28; Joshua 2:5; Judges 12:5"> And the men followed after them in the way to Jordan to the fords; and the gate was shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And it came to pass when the men who pursued after them were gone forth, and before the spies had lain down to sleep, that she came up to them on the top of the house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; Deuteronomy 2:25; Joshua 2:11"> and she said to them, I know that the Lord has given you the land; for the fear of you has fallen upon us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Exodus 15:14–16; Exodus 14:21–31"> For we have heard that the Lord God dried up the Red Sea before you, when ye came out of the land of Egypt, and all that he did to the two kings of the Amorites, who were beyond Jordan, to Sihon and Og, whom ye utterly destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Joshua 5:1; Joshua 7:5"> And when we heard it we were amazed in our heart, and there was no longer any spirit in any of us because of you, for the Lord your God is God in heaven above, and on the earth beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 2:18; Joshua 9:18–20; 2 Timothy 1:16–18"> And now swear to me by the Lord God; since I deal mercifully with you, so do ye also deal mercifully with the house of my father:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and save alive the house of my father, my mother, and my brethren, and all my house, and all that they have, and ye shall rescue my soul from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Judges 1:24–25; Genesis 24:49"> And the men said to her, Our life for yours even to death: and she said, When the Lord shall have delivered the city to you, ye shall deal mercifully and truly with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:25; 2 Corinthians 11:33; Joshua 6:20"> And she let them down by the window;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 2:25; Kings I 23:14; Psalms 11:1"> and she said to them, Depart into the hill country, lest the pursuers meet you, and ye shall be hidden there three days until your pursuers return from after you, and afterward ye shall depart on your way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 30:2; Kings II 21:7; Genesis 24:3–8"> And the men said to her, We are clear of this thy oath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 6:23; Joshua 2:21; Joshua 2:12–13"> Behold, we shall enter into a part of the city, and thou shalt set a sign; thou shalt bind this scarlet cord in the window, by which thou hast let us down, and thou shalt bring in to thyself, into thy house, thy father, and thy mother, and thy brethren, and all the family of thy father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 27:31; Kings III 2:36–42; Exodus 12:13"> And it shall come to pass that whosoever shall go outside the door of thy house, his guilt shall be upon him, and we shall be quit of this thine oath; and we will be responsible for all that shall be found with thee in thy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 11:13"> But if anyone should injure us, or betray these our matters, we shall be quit of this thine oath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 2:5; Joshua 2:18; Matthew 7:24"> And she said to them, Let it be according to your word; and she sent them out, and they departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings I 19:10–12; Kings II 17:20; Psalms 32:6–7"> And they came to the hill country, and remained there three days; and the pursuers searched all the roads, and found them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the two young men returned, and came down out of the mountain; and they went over to Joshua the son of Nun, and told him all things that had happened to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Joshua 1:8; Joshua 2:9–11"> And they said to Joshua, The Lord has delivered all the land into our power, and all the inhabitants of that land tremble because of us.</span>
