<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Deuteronomy 7:18; Deuteronomy 1:21"> And the Lord said to Joshua, Fear not, nor be timorous: take with thee all the men of war, and arise, go up to Ai; behold, I have given into thy hands the king of Ai, and his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 6:21; Chronicles II 20:22"> And thou shalt do to Ai, as thou didst to Jericho and its king; and thou shalt take to thyself the spoil of its cattle; set now for thyself an ambush for the city behind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 24:39; Matthew 25:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:2"> And Joshua and all the men of war rose to go up to Ai; and Joshua chose out thirty thousand mighty men, and he sent them away by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 20:29; Joshua 8:16; Judges 20:36"> And he charged them, saying, Do ye lie in ambush behind the city: do not go far from the city, and ye shall all be ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 7:5; Matthew 10:16; Judges 20:31–33"> And I and all with me will draw near to the city: and it shall come to pass when the inhabitants of Ai shall come forth to meet us, as before, that we will flee from before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 20:32; Exodus 15:9; Ecclesiastes 8:11"> And when they shall come out after us, we will draw them away from the city; and they will say, These men flee from before us, as also before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30–31; Kings IV 5:1; Joshua 8:1"> And ye shall rise up out of the ambuscade, and go into the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings II 13:28; Joshua 1:16; Joshua 1:9"> Ye shall do according to this word, lo! I have commanded you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 7:2; Genesis 12:8; Ezra and Nehemiah 2:28"> And Joshua sent them, and they went to lie in ambush; and they lay between Bethel and Ai, westward of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 6:12; Psalms 119:60; Genesis 22:3"> And Joshua rose up early in the morning, and numbered the people; and he went up, he and the elders before the people to Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And all the men of war went up with him, and they went forward and came over against the city eastward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 8:2–3"> And the ambuscade was on the west side of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 24:39; Judges 20:34–36; Deuteronomy 1:1"> And it came to pass when the king of Ai saw it, he hastened and went out to meet them direct to the battle, he and all the people that were with him: and he knew not that there was an ambuscade formed against him behind the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 18:12; Joshua 16:1; Judges 20:36"> And Joshua and Israel saw, and retreated from before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 20:31; Judges 20:36–39; Joshua 8:5–6"> And they pursued after the children of Israel, and they themselves went to a distance from the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 5:13; Joshua 8:3; Isaiah 19:11–13"> There was no one left in Ai who did not pursue after Israel; and they left the city open, and pursued after Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 8:26; Job 15:25; Kings I 17:41"> And the Lord said to Joshua, Stretch forth thy hand with the spear that is in thy hand toward the city, for I have delivered it into thy hands; and the liers in wait shall rise up quickly out of their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Joshua stretched out his hand and his spear toward the city, and the ambuscade rose up quickly out of their place; and they came forth when he stretched out his hand; and they entered into the city, and took it; and they hastened and burned the city with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 76:5; Job 11:20; Genesis 19:28"> And when the inhabitants of Ai looked round behind them, then they saw the smoke going up out of the city to heaven, and they were no longer able to flee this way or that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Joshua and all Israel saw that the ambuscade had taken the city, and that the smoke of the city went up to heaven; and they turned and smote the men of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Joshua 10:28; Joshua 6:21"> And these came forth out of the city to meet them; and they were in the midst of the army, some being on this side, and some on that; and they smote them until there was not left of them one who survived and escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings I 15:8; Joshua 10:17; Revelation 19:20"> And they took the king of Ai alive, and brought him to Joshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 11:10–14; Joshua 10:30–41; Numbers 21:24"> And when the children of Israel had ceased slaying all that were in Ai, and in the fields, and in the mountain on the descent, from whence they pursued them even to the end, then Joshua returned to Ai, and smote it with the edge of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:16–18"> And they that fell in that day, men and women, were twelve thousand: they slew all the inhabitants of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 8:2; Numbers 31:26; Psalms 50:10"> Beside the spoils that were in the city, all things which the children of Israel took as spoil for themselves according to the command of the Lord, as the Lord commanded Joshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:16; Jeremiah 49:2; Kings IV 19:25"> And Joshua burned the city with fire: he made it an uninhabited heap forever, even to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 7:26; Kings II 18:17; Deuteronomy 21:22–23"> And he hanged the king of Ai on a gallows; and he remained on the tree till evening: and when the sun went down, Joshua gave charge, and they took down his body from the tree, and cast it into a pit, and they set over him a heap of stones until this day.</span>
