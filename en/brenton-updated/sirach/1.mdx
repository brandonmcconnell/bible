<div className="hs">The Prologue to the Wisdom of Jesus the son of Sirach</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Whereas many and great things have been delivered unto us by the law and the prophets, and by others that have followed their steps, for the which things Israel ought to be commended for learning and wisdom: and whereof not only the readers must needs become skillful themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> but also they that desire to learn be able to profit them which are without, both by speaking and by writing: my grandfather Jesus, when he had much given himself to the reading of the law, and the prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> and other books of our fathers, and had gotten therein good judgment, was drawn on also himself to write something pertaining to learning and wisdom; to the intent that those which are desirous to learn, and are addicted to these things, might profit much more in living according to the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Wherefore let me entreat you to read it with favor and attention, and to pardon us, wherein we may seem to come short of some words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> which we have labored to interpret; for the same things uttered in Hebrew, and translated into another tongue, have not the same force in them. And not only these things, but the law itself, and the prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> and the rest of the books, have no small difference, when they are spoken in their own language. For in the eight and thirtieth year coming into Egypt, when Euergetes was king, and continuing there some time, I found a book of no small learning:</span>
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> therefore I thought it most necessary for me to bestow some diligence and travail to interpret it: using great watchfulness and skill in that space to bring the book to an end, and set it forth for them also, which in a strange country are willing to learn,</span>
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> being prepared before in manners to live after the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> <span className="pl pl-1">All wisdom cometh from the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And is with him forever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who can number the sand of the sea, and the drops of rain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the days of eternity?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who can find out the height of heaven, and the breadth of the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the deep, and wisdom?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wisdom hath been created before all things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the understanding of prudence from everlasting.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> <span className="pl pl-1">To whom hath the root of wisdom been revealed?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or who hath known her wise counsels?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">He created her, and saw her, and numbered her,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And poured her out upon all his works.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">She is with all flesh according to his gift,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And he hath given her to them that love him.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The fear of the Lord is honor, and glory,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And gladness, and a crown of rejoicing.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The fear of the Lord maketh a merry heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And giveth joy, and gladness, and long life.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whoso feareth the Lord, it shall go well with him at the last,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And he shall find favor in the day of his death.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">To fear the Lord is the beginning of wisdom:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And it was created with the faithful in the womb.</span><br /></span>
