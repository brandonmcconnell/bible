<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 24:20; Chronicles II 20:14; Numbers 24:2"> And Azariah the son of Oded—upon him came the Spirit of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 4:8; Chronicles II 24:20; Chronicles II 15:4"> and he went out to meet Asa, and all Judah and Benjamin, and said, Hear me, Asa, and all Judah and Benjamin. The Lord is with you, while ye are with him; and if ye seek him out, he will be found of you; but if ye forsake him, he will forsake you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:9; Leviticus 10:11; Chronicles II 17:8–9"> And Israel has been a long time without the true God, and without a priest to expound the truth, and without the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6; Deuteronomy 4:29–30; Chronicles II 15:15"> But he shall turn them to the Lord God of Israel, and he will be found of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 5:6; Psalms 121:8; Kings I 13:6"> And in that time there is no peace to one going out, or to one coming in, for the terror of the Lord is upon all that inhabit the lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 21:22–24; Matthew 24:7; Chronicles II 33:11"> And nation shall fight against nation, and city against city; for God has confounded them with every kind of affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Joshua 1:7; Joshua 1:9"> But be ye strong, and let not your hands be weakened: for there is a reward for your work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles II 8:12; Chronicles II 13:19; Chronicles II 4:1"> And when Asa heard these words, and the prophecy of Oded the prophet, then he strengthened himself, and cast out the abominations from all the land of Judah and Benjamin, and from the cities which Jeroboam possessed, in Mount Ephraim, and he renewed the altar of the Lord, which was before the temple of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Chronicles II 11:16; Acts 9:31; Zechariah 8:21–23"> And he assembled Judah and Benjamin, and the strangers that dwelt with him, of Ephraim, and of Manasseh, and of Simeon: for many of Israel were joined to him, when they saw that the Lord his God was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esther 8:9"> And they assembled at Jerusalem in the third month, in the fifteenth year of the reign of Asa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles II 14:13–15; Numbers 31:28–29; Numbers 31:50"> And he sacrificed to the Lord in that day of the spoils which they brought, seven hundred calves and seven thousand sheep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 24:14; Chronicles II 23:16; Deuteronomy 10:12"> And he entered into a covenant that they should seek the Lord God of their fathers with all their heart and with all their soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:20; Psalms 115:13; Kings III 18:40"> And that whoever should not seek the Lord God of Israel, should die, whether young or old, whether man or woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:13; Psalms 81:1–4; Nehemiah 10:29"> And they swore to the Lord with a loud voice, and with trumpets, and with cornets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles II 14:7; Chronicles II 15:12; Isaiah 26:8"> And all Judah rejoiced concerning the oath: for they swore with all their heart, and they sought him with all their desires; and he was found of them: and the Lord gave them rest round about.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 15:13–24; Kings III 15:10; Kings IV 23:15"> And he removed Maacah his mother from being priestess to Asherah; and he cut down the idol, and burned it in the brook of Kidron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings IV 14:4; Kings IV 12:3; Kings III 11:4"> Nevertheless they removed not the high places: they still existed in Israel: nevertheless the heart of Asa was perfect all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 7:51; Chronicles I 26:20–26; Kings III 15:14–15"> And he brought in the holy things of David his father, and the holy things of the house of God, silver, and gold, and vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings III 15:31; Kings III 15:33; Kings III 15:16–17"> And there was no war waged with him until the thirty-fifth year of the reign of Asa.</span>
</div>
