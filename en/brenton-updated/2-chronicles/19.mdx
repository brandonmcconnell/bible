<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 18:31–32"> And Jehoshaphat king of Judah returned to his house at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 139:21–22; 2 John 1:10–11; James 4:4"> And there went out to meet him Jehu the prophet the son of Hanani, and said to him, King Jehoshaphat, dost thou help a sinner, or act friendly toward one hated of the Lord? Therefore has wrath come upon thee from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles II 12:12; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:10; Kings III 14:13"> Nevertheless some good things have been found in thee, forasmuch as thou didst remove the groves from the land of Judah, and didst direct thy heart to seek after the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings I 7:15–17; Kings I 7:3–4; Chronicles II 29:10–11"> And Jehoshaphat dwelt in Jerusalem: and he again went out among the people from Beersheba to the mount of Ephraim, and turned them back to the Lord God of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:18–20; Chronicles II 19:8; 1 Peter 2:13–14"> And he appointed judges in all the strong cities of Judah, city by city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:17; Leviticus 19:15; Ecclesiastes 5:8"> And he said to the judges, Take good heed what ye do: for ye judge not for man, but for the Lord, and with you are matters of judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Genesis 18:25; Romans 2:11"> And now let the fear of the Lord be upon you, and be wary, and do your duty: for there is no unrighteousness with the Lord our God, neither is it for him to respect persons, nor to take bribes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles II 17:8–9; Exodus 18:19–26; Deuteronomy 17:8–13"> Moreover Jehoshaphat appointed in Jerusalem some of the priests, and Levites, and heads of houses of Israel, for the judgment of the Lord, and to judge the dwellers in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Chronicles II 19:7; Kings II 23:3; Deuteronomy 1:16–17"> And he charged them, saying, Thus shall ye do in the fear of the Lord, in truth and with a perfect heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:8–13; Ezekiel 33:6; Ezekiel 3:18–21"> Whatsoever man of your brethren that dwell in their cities shall bring the cause that comes before you, between blood and blood, and between precept and commandment, and ordinances and judgments, ye shall even decide for them; so they shall not sin against the Lord, and there shall not be wrath upon you, and upon your brethren: thus ye shall do, and ye shall not sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles II 19:8; Psalms 18:25–26; 2 Timothy 2:1"> And, behold, Amariah the priest is head over you in every matter of the Lord; and Zebadiah the son of Ishmael is head over the house of Judah in every matter of the king; and the scribes and Levites are before you: be strong and active, and the Lord shall be with the good.</span>
</div>
