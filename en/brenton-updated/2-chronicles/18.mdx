<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 17:5; Chronicles II 21:6; Chronicles II 19:2"> And Jehoshaphat had yet great wealth and glory, and he connected himself by marriage with the house of Ahab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings IV 9:1; Chronicles II 19:2; Kings III 1:9"> And he went down after a term of years to Ahab to Samaria: and Ahab slew for him sheep and calves, in abundance, and for the people with him, and he much desired him to go up with him to Ramoth of the country of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings III 22:4; Kings IV 3:7; 2 John 1:10–11"> And Ahab king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat king of Judah, Wilt thou go with me to Ramoth of the country of Gilead? And he said to him, As I am, so also art thou, as thy people, so also is my people with thee for the war.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:2; Kings II 5:19; Kings II 2:1"> And Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, Seek, I pray thee, the Lord today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3; Micah 3:11; Jeremiah 28:1–17"> And the king of Israel gathered the prophets, four hundred men, and said to them, Shall I go to Ramoth-gilead to battle, or shall I forbear? And they said, Go up, and God shall deliver it into the hands of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 22:7–9; Kings IV 3:11–13"> And Jehoshaphat said, Is there not here a prophet of the Lord besides, that we may inquire of him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Micah 2:7; Kings III 21:20"> And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, There is yet one man by whom to inquire of the Lord; but I hate him, for he does not prophesy concerning me for good, for all his days are for evil: this is Micaiah the son of Imlah. And Jehoshaphat said, Let not the king say so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 22:9; Isaiah 39:7; Chronicles II 18:25–26"> And the king called a eunuch, and said, Fetch quickly Micaiah the son of Imlah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 14:9; Daniel 7:9; Matthew 19:28"> And the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah were sitting each on his throne, and clothed in their robes, sitting in the open space at the entrance of the gate of Samaria: and all the prophets were prophesying before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:17; Zechariah 1:18–21; Jeremiah 28:2–3"> And Zedekiah son of Chenaanah made for himself iron horns, and said, Thus saith the Lord, With these thou shalt thrust Syria until it be consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 24:24–25; Chronicles II 18:33–34; Jude 1:16"> And all the prophets prophesied so, saying, Go up to Ramoth-gilead, and thou shalt prosper; and the Lord shall deliver it into the hands of the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 2:6; Psalms 10:11; Micah 2:11"> And the messenger that went to call Micaiah spoke to him, saying, Behold, the prophets have spoken favorably concerning the king with one mouth; let now, I pray thee, thy words be as the words of one of them, and do thou speak good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 22:35; Numbers 24:13; Numbers 22:18–20"> And Micaiah said, As the Lord lives, whatever God shall say to me, that will I speak.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings III 22:15; Lamentations 4:21; Amos 4:4–5"> And he came to the king, and the king said to him, Micaiah, shall I go up to Ramoth-gilead to battle, or shall I forbear? And he said, Go up, and thou shalt prosper, and they shall be given into your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 26:63; Kings I 14:24; Kings III 22:16"> And the king said to him, How often shall I solemnly charge thee that thou speak to me nothing but truth in the name of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Ezekiel 34:5–6; Numbers 27:17"> And he said, I saw Israel scattered on the mountains, as sheep without a shepherd: and the Lord said, These have no commander; let each return to his home in peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles II 18:7; Kings III 22:18; Proverbs 29:1"> And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, Said I not to thee, that he would not prophesy concerning me good, but evil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 7:9; Isaiah 6:1–5; Kings III 22:19–23"> But he said, Not so. Hear ye the word of the Lord: I saw the Lord sitting on his throne, and all the host of heaven stood by on his right hand and on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:9; Chronicles II 25:19; Proverbs 11:5"> And the Lord said, Who will deceive Ahab king of Israel, that he may go up, and fall in Ramoth-gilead? And one spoke this way, and another spoke that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 1:6; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> And there came forth a spirit, and stood before the Lord, and said, I will deceive him. And the Lord said, Whereby?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 8:44; Kings III 22:21–22; 1 John 4:6"> And he said, I will go forth, and will be a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets. And the Lord said, Thou shalt deceive him, and shalt prevail: go forth, and do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:14; Ezekiel 14:9; Job 12:16"> And now, behold, the Lord has put a false spirit in the mouth of these thy prophets, and the Lord has spoken evil against thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Jeremiah 20:2; Chronicles II 18:10"> Then Zedekiah the son of Chenaanah drew near, and smote Micaiah on the cheek, and said to him, By what way passed the Spirit of the Lord from me to speak to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:21–22; Isaiah 26:20; Jeremiah 28:16–17"> And Micaiah said, Behold, thou shalt see in that day, when thou shalt go from chamber to chamber to hide thyself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Chronicles II 18:8; Chronicles II 34:8; Jeremiah 38:6–7"> And the king of Israel said, Take Micaiah, and carry him back to Amon the governor of the city, and to Joash the captain, the king’s son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Chronicles II 16:10; Psalms 80:5; Luke 23:2"> and ye shall say, Thus said the king, Put this fellow into the prison house, and let him eat the bread of affliction, and drink the water of affliction, until I return in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 20:45–46; Amos 9:10; Micah 1:2"> And Micaiah said, If thou do at all return in peace, the Lord has not spoken by me. And he said, Hear, all ye people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings III 22:29–33"> So the king of Israel, and Jehoshaphat king of Judah, went up to Ramoth-gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings I 28:8; Jeremiah 23:24; Proverbs 26:25"> And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, Disguise me, and I will enter into the battle: and do thou put on my raiment. So the king of Israel disguised himself, and entered into the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:11; Deuteronomy 1:17; Kings III 20:42"> Now the king of Syria had commanded the captains of the chariots that were with him, saying, Fight neither against small nor great, but only against the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Chronicles II 13:14; Psalms 118:13; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> And it came to pass, when the captains of the chariots saw Jehoshaphat, that they said, It is the king of Israel: and they compassed him about to fight against him: and Jehoshaphat cried out, and the Lord delivered him; and God turned them away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Kings III 22:33"> And it came to pass, when the captains of the chariots saw that it was not the king of Israel, that they turned away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Chronicles II 35:23; Kings II 15:11; Kings III 22:34–35"> And a man drew a bow with a good aim, and smote the king of Israel between the lungs and the breastplate: and he said to the charioteer, Turn thy hand, drive me out of the battle, for I am wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 13:21; Chronicles II 18:27; Proverbs 28:17"> And the battle turned in that day; and the king of Israel remained on the chariot against Syria until evening, and died at sunset.</span>
</div>
