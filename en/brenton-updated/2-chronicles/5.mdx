<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings III 8:2; Chronicles II 7:8–10; Leviticus 23:34–36"> And all Israel were assembled unto the king in the feast, this is the seventh month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 8:3; Joshua 3:6; Joshua 6:6"> And all the elders of Israel came; and all the Levites took up the ark,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings III 8:6; Chronicles II 1:3; Kings III 8:4"> and the tabernacle of witness, and all the holy vessels that were in the tabernacle; and the priests and the Levites brought it up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles I 29:21; Kings II 6:13; Chronicles I 16:1–2"> And King Solomon, and all the elders of Israel, and the religious of them, and they of them that were gathered before the ark, were sacrificing calves and sheep, which could not be numbered or reckoned for multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 132:8; Kings III 6:23–28; Kings III 8:6–7"> And the priests brought in the ark of the covenant of the Lord into its place, into the oracle of the house, even into the holy of holies, under the wings of the cherubs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:12–15; Numbers 4:6; Exodus 37:3–5"> And the cherubs stretched out their wings over the place of the ark, and the cherubs covered the ark, and its staffs above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 8:8–9"> And the staffs projected, and the heads of the staffs were seen from the holy place in front of the oracle, they were not seen without: and there they were to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Chronicles II 6:11; Deuteronomy 10:2–5"> There was nothing in the ark except the two tables which Moses placed there in Horeb, which God gave in covenant with the children of Israel, when they went out of the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles II 35:4; Chronicles II 29:5; Job 1:5"> And it came to pass, when the priests went out of the holy place (for all the priests that were found were sanctified, they were not then arranged according to their daily course),</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles I 15:24; Psalms 68:25; Chronicles I 6:39"> that all the singing Levites assigned to the sons of Asaph, to Heman, to Jeduthun, and to his sons, and to his brethren, of them that were clothed in linen garments, with cymbals and lutes and harps, were standing before the altar, and with them a hundred and twenty priests, blowing trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 3:11; Jeremiah 33:11; Chronicles II 7:3"> And there was one voice in the trumpeting and in the psalm singing, and in the loud utterance with one voice to give thanks and praise the Lord; and when they raised their voice together with trumpets and cymbals, and instruments of music, and said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Give thanks to the Lord, for it is good,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For his mercy endures forever—</span><br />then the house was filled with the cloud of the glory of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 40:35; Kings III 8:11; Isaiah 6:1–4"> And the priests could not stand to minister because of the cloud: for the glory of the Lord filled the house of God.</span>
