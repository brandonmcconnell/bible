<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings IV 18:1–3; Chronicles I 3:13; Chronicles II 26:5"> And Hezekiah began to reign at the age of twenty-five years, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem: and his mother’s name was Abijah, daughter of Zechariah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles II 34:2; Chronicles II 28:1"> And he did that which was right in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his father David had done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles II 28:24; Chronicles II 29:7; Matthew 6:33"> And it came to pass, when he was established over his kingdom, in the first month, he opened the doors of the house of the Lord, and repaired them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:2; Nehemiah 3:29; Chronicles II 32:6"> And he brought in the priests and the Levites, and put them on the east side,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles II 35:6; 1 Corinthians 3:16–17; Chronicles II 34:3–8"> and said to them, Hear, ye Levites: now sanctify yourselves, and sanctify the house of the Lord God of your fathers, and cast out the impurity from the holy places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Jeremiah 2:27; Daniel 9:16"> For our fathers have revolted, and done that which was evil before the Lord our God, and have forsaken him, and have turned away their face from the tabernacle of the Lord, and have turned their back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles II 28:24; Leviticus 24:2–8; Chronicles II 29:3"> And they have shut up the doors of the temple, and put out the lamps, and have not burned incense, and have not offered whole burnt offerings in the holy place to the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 25:18; Jeremiah 29:18"> And the Lord was very angry with Judah and Jerusalem, and made them an astonishment, and a desolation, and a hissing, as ye see with your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Chronicles II 28:17; Chronicles II 28:5–8; Lamentations 5:7"> And, behold, your fathers have been smitten with the sword, and your sons and your daughters and your wives are in captivity in a land not their own, as it is even now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; Chronicles II 23:16; Ezra and Nehemiah 10:3"> Therefore it is now in my heart to make a covenant, a covenant with the Lord God of Israel, that he may turn away his fierce wrath from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Galatians 6:7–8; Numbers 8:6–14"> And now be not wanting to your duty, for the Lord has chosen you to stand before him to minister, and to be ministers and burners of incense to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 3:19–20; Chronicles I 15:5–7; Numbers 4:2–20"> Then the Levites rose up, Mahath the son of Amasai, and Joel the son of Azariah, of the sons of Kohath: and of the sons of Merari, Kish the son of Abdi, and Azariah the son of Jehallelel: and of the sons of Gershon, Joah the son of Zimmah, and Eden: these were the sons of Joah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles I 6:39; Chronicles I 15:8; Chronicles I 15:17"> And of the sons of Elizaphan; Shimri, and Jeuel: and of the sons of Asaph; Zechariah, and Mattaniah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles I 6:33; Chronicles I 25:1; Chronicles I 25:6"> and of the sons of Heman; Jehuel, and Shimei: and of the sons of Jeduthun; Shemaiah, and Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles II 30:12; Chronicles I 23:28; Chronicles II 29:5"> And they gathered their brethren, and they purified themselves according to the king’s command by the order of the Lord, to purify the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles II 15:16; Matthew 21:12–13; Matthew 23:27"> And the priests entered into the house of the Lord, to purify it, and they cast out all the uncleanness that was found in the house of the Lord, even into the court of the house of the Lord: and the Levites received it to cast into the brook of Kidron without.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:2–8; Chronicles II 3:4; Kings III 6:3"> And Hezekiah began on the first day, even on the new moon of the first month, to purify, and on the eighth day of the month they entered into the temple of the Lord: and they purified the house of the Lord in eight days; and on the thirteenth day of the first month they finished the work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:1; Chronicles II 4:7–8; Chronicles II 13:11"> And they went in to King Hezekiah, and said, We have purified all the things in the house of the Lord, the altar of whole burnt offering, and its vessels, and the table of showbread, and its vessels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Chronicles II 28:24"> and all the vessels which King Ahaz polluted in his reign, in his apostasy, we have prepared and purified: behold, they are before the altar of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 6:12; Jeremiah 25:4; Exodus 24:4"> And King Hezekiah rose early in the morning, and gathered the chief men of the city, and went up to the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 4:3–14; Job 42:8; Ezekiel 45:23"> And he brought seven calves, seven rams, seven lambs, seven kids of goats for a sin offering, for the kingdom, and for the holy things, and for Israel: and he told the priests the sons of Aaron to go up to the altar of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:18; Leviticus 8:19; Leviticus 8:24"> And they slew the calves, and the priests received the blood, and poured it on the altar: and they slew the rams, and poured the blood upon the altar: also they slew the lambs, and poured the blood round the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 4:15; Leviticus 4:24; Leviticus 1:4"> And they brought the goats for a sin offering before the king and the congregation; and laid their hands upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 14:20; Ezekiel 45:15; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> And the priests slew them, and offered their blood as a propitiation on the altar; and they made atonement for all Israel: for the king said, The whole burnt offering, and the sin offerings are for all Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Chronicles II 8:14; Chronicles I 23:5; Kings II 24:11"> And he stationed the Levites in the house of the Lord with cymbals, and lutes, and harps, according to the commandment of King David, and of Gad the king’s seer, and Nathan the prophet: for by the commandment of the Lord the order was in the hand of the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Chronicles I 23:5; Chronicles I 15:24; Amos 6:5"> And the Levites stood with the instruments of David, and the priests with the trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Chronicles II 23:18; Psalms 137:3–4; Chronicles II 20:21"> And Hezekiah told them to offer up the whole burnt offering on the altar: and when they began to offer the whole burnt offering, they began to sing to the Lord, and the trumpets accompanied the instruments of David king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 5:8–14; Psalms 89:15; Psalms 68:24–26"> And all the congregation worshiped, and the psalm singers were singing, and the trumpets sounding, until the whole burnt sacrifice had been completely offered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Chronicles II 20:18; Chronicles I 29:20; Philippians 2:10–11"> And when they had done offering it, the king and all that were present bowed, and worshiped.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 4:4; Psalms 95:1–2; Chronicles I 16:7–36"> And King Hezekiah and the princes told the Levites to sing hymns to the Lord in the words of David, and of Asaph the prophet: and they sang hymns with gladness, and fell down and worshiped.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Chronicles II 13:9; Exodus 35:22; Leviticus 7:12"> Then Hezekiah answered and said, Now ye have consecrated yourselves to the Lord, bring near and offer sacrifices of praise in the house of the Lord. And the congregation brought sacrifices and thank offerings into the house of the Lord; and every one who was ready in his heart brought whole burnt offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 6:17; Kings III 3:4; Chronicles I 29:21"> And the number of the whole burnt offerings which the congregation brought, was seventy calves, a hundred rams, two hundred lambs: all these were for a whole burnt offering to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And the consecrated calves were six hundred, and the sheep three thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Chronicles II 35:11; Chronicles II 30:3; Psalms 94:15"> But the priests were few, and could not flay the whole burnt offering, so their brethren the Levites helped them, until the work was finished, and until the priests had purified themselves: for the Levites more zealously purified themselves than the priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 15:5–10; Exodus 29:13; Chronicles I 16:37–42"> And the whole burnt offering was abundant, with the fat of the complete peace offering, and the drink offerings of the whole burnt sacrifice. So the service was established in the house of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 2:41; 1 Thessalonians 3:8–9; Chronicles I 29:9"> And Hezekiah and all the people rejoiced, because God had prepared the people: for the thing was done suddenly.</span>
</div>
</div>
