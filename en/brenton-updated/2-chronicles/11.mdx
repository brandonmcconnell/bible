<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 12:21–24; Psalms 33:10; Psalms 33:16"> And Rehoboam came to Jerusalem; and he assembled Judah and Benjamin, a hundred and eighty thousand young men fit for war, and he waged war with Israel to recover the kingdom to Rehoboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles II 12:15; Chronicles II 12:5–7; Chronicles II 8:14"> And the word of the Lord came to Shemaiah the man of God, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings IV 17:34; Genesis 49:28; Revelation 7:4–8"> Speak to Rehoboam the son of Solomon, and to all Judah and Benjamin, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Kings III 11:29–38; Chronicles II 10:15–16"> Thus saith the Lord, Ye shall not go up, and ye shall not war against your brethren: return every one to his home; for this thing is of me. And they hearkened to the word of the Lord, and returned from going against Jeroboam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles II 8:2–6; Chronicles II 14:6–7; Isaiah 22:8–11"> And Rehoboam dwelt in Jerusalem, and he built walled cities in Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:5; Amos 1:1; Chronicles I 4:32"> And he built Bethlehem, and Etam and Tekoa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; Kings I 22:1; Joshua 15:58"> and Beth-zur, and Soco, and Adullam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 54:1; Joshua 15:24; Kings I 23:19"> and Gath, and Mareshah, and Ziph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 15:39; Joshua 15:35; Joshua 10:5"> and Adoraim, and Lachish, and Azekah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Joshua 15:33; Joshua 14:14"> and Zorah, and Aijalon, and Hebron, which belongs to Judah and Benjamin, walled cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles II 17:19; Isaiah 22:10–11; Chronicles II 11:23"> And he fortified them with walls, and placed in them captains, and stores of provisions, oil and wine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings II 13:22; Chronicles II 26:14–15; Chronicles II 32:5"> shields and spears in every several city, and he fortified them very strongly, and he had on his side Judah and Benjamin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the priests and the Levites who were in all Israel were gathered to him out of all the coasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles II 13:9; Numbers 35:2–5; Kings III 12:28–33"> For the Levites left the tents of their possession, and went to Judah to Jerusalem, because Jeroboam and his sons had ejected them so that they should not minister to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 12:28; Kings III 12:31; Kings III 13:33"> And he made for himself priests of the high places, and for the idols, and for the vanities, and for the calves which Jeroboam made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles II 15:9; Acts 11:23; Psalms 84:5–7"> And he cast out from the tribes of Israel those who set their heart to seek the Lord God of Israel: and they came to Jerusalem, to sacrifice to the Lord God of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles II 12:1; Chronicles II 1:1–12; Chronicles II 7:17–19"> And they strengthened the kingdom of Judah; and Judah strengthened Rehoboam the son of Solomon for three years, for he walked three years in the ways of David and Solomon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings I 16:6; Chronicles I 27:18; Kings I 17:13"> And Rehoboam took to himself for a wife Mahalath daughter of Jerimoth the son of David, and Abigail daughter of Eliab the son of Jesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And she bore him sons; Jeush, and Shemariah, and Zaham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Chronicles II 13:2; Chronicles II 11:21; Chronicles II 12:16"> And afterward he took to himself Maacah the daughter of Absalom; and she bore him Abijah, and Attai, and Ziza, and Shelomith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 8:30; Deuteronomy 17:17; Kings III 11:3"> And Rehoboam loved Maacah the daughter of Absalom more than all his wives and all his concubines: for he had eighteen wives and sixty concubines; and he begot twenty-eight sons, and sixty daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:15–17; Chronicles I 29:1; Chronicles I 5:1–2"> And he made Abijah the son of Maacah chief, even a leader among his brethren, for he intended to make him king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Chronicles II 21:3; Genesis 25:6; Luke 16:8"> And he was exalted beyond all his other sons in all the coasts of Judah and Benjamin, and in the strong cities; and he gave them provisions in great abundance: and he desired many wives.</span>
</div>
