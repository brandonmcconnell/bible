<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:11; Chronicles II 33:25; Chronicles II 26:1"> And the people of the land took Jehoahaz the son of Josiah, and anointed him, and made him king over Jerusalem in the room of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Jehoahaz was twenty-three years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and his mother’s name was Hamutal, daughter of Jeremiah of Libnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his fathers had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And Pharaoh Neco bound him in Riblah in the land of Hamath, that he might not reign in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings IV 23:33"> And the king brought him over to Egypt; and imposed a tribute on the land, a hundred talents of silver and a talent of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:10–12; Kings IV 23:34–35; Ezekiel 19:3–4"> And Pharaoh Neco made Eliakim the son of Josiah king over Judah in the room of his father Josiah, and changed his name to Jehoiakim. And Pharaoh Neco took his brother Jehoahaz and brought him into Egypt, and he died there:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:10–12; Kings IV 23:34–35; Ezekiel 19:3–4"> but he had given the silver and the gold to Pharaoh. At that time the land began to be taxed to give the money at the command of Pharaoh; and every one as he could borrowed the silver and the gold of the people of the land, to give to Pharaoh Neco.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 23:36–37; Jeremiah 22:13–19; Jeremiah 26:1"> Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem: and his mother’s name was Zechora, daughter of Nerias of Rumah. And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his fathers did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 23:36–37; Jeremiah 22:13–19; Jeremiah 26:1"> In his days came Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon into the land, and he served him three years, and then revolted from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 23:36–37; Jeremiah 22:13–19; Jeremiah 26:1"> And the Lord sent against them the Chaldeans, and plundering parties of Syrians, and plundering parties of the Moabites, and of the children of Ammon, and of Samaria; but after this they departed, according to the word of the Lord by the hand of his servants the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 23:36–37; Jeremiah 22:13–19; Jeremiah 26:1"> Nevertheless the wrath of the Lord was upon Judah, so that they should be removed from his presence, because of the sins of Manasseh in all that he did,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 23:36–37; Jeremiah 22:13–19; Jeremiah 26:1"> and for the innocent blood which Jehoiakim shed, for he had filled Jerusalem with innocent blood; yet the Lord would not utterly destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles II 33:11; Kings IV 24:5–6; Ezekiel 19:5–9"> And Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up against him, and bound him with brazen fetters, and carried him away to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 24:13; Daniel 5:2–4; Jeremiah 27:16–18"> And he carried away a part of the vessels of the house of the Lord to Babylon, and put them in his temple in Babylon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings IV 24:5–6; Matthew 1:11–12; Chronicles I 3:16–17"> And the rest of the acts of Jehoiakim, and all that he did, behold, are not these things written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah? And Jehoiakim slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in Ganozae: and Jehoiachin his son reigned in his stead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings IV 24:8–17"> Jehoiachin was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months and ten days in Jerusalem, and did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:1; Chronicles II 36:7; Kings IV 24:10–17"> And at the turn of the year, King Nebuchadnezzar sent, and brought him to Babylon, with the precious vessels of the house of the Lord, and made Zedekiah his father’s brother king over Judah and Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings IV 24:18–20; Jeremiah 52:1–3; Jeremiah 27:1"> Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles II 33:23; Jeremiah 37:2–21; Jeremiah 38:14–28"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord his God: he was not ashamed before the prophet Jeremiah, nor because of the word of the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:2–3; Chronicles II 30:8; Ezekiel 17:11–20"> in that he rebelled against King Nebuchadnezzar, which he adjured him by God not to do: but he stiffened his neck, and hardened his heart, so as not to return to the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:8; Ezekiel 22:6; Micah 3:9–11"> And all the great men of Judah, and the priests, and the people of the land transgressed abundantly in the abominations of the heathen, and polluted the house of the Lord which was in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:15; Jeremiah 7:13; Jeremiah 25:3–4"> And the Lord God of their fathers sent by the hand of his prophets; rising early and sending his messengers, for he spared his people, and his sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12–13; Chronicles II 30:10; Luke 22:63–64"> Nevertheless they sneered at his messengers, and set at nought his words, and mocked his prophets, until the wrath of the Lord rose up against his people, till there was no remedy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 74:20; Ezra and Nehemiah 9:7; Ezekiel 9:5–7"> And he brought against them the king of the Chaldeans, and slew their young men with the sword in the house of his sanctuary, and did not spare Zedekiah, and had no mercy upon their virgins, and they led away their old men: he delivered all things into their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:7; Chronicles II 36:10; Isaiah 39:6"> And all the vessels of the house of God, the great and the small, and the treasures of the house of the Lord, and all the treasures of the king and the great men; he brought all to Babylon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 79:1; Kings III 9:8; Jeremiah 52:13–15"> And he burned the house of the Lord, and broke down the wall of Jerusalem, and burned its palaces with fire, and utterly destroyed every beautiful vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:7; Ezra and Nehemiah 1:1–11; Chronicles II 36:22"> And he carried away the remnant to Babylon; and they were servants to him and to his sons until the establishment of the kingdom of the Medes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Leviticus 26:34–35; Zechariah 1:12"> That the word of the Lord by the mouth of Jeremiah might be fulfilled, until the land should enjoy its Sabbaths in resting and Sabbath keeping all the days of its desolation, till the accomplishment of seventy years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:12; Ezra and Nehemiah 1:1–3; Jeremiah 29:10"> In the first year of Cyrus king of the Persians, after the fulfillment of the word of the Lord by the mouth of Jeremiah, the Lord stirred up the spirit of Cyrus king of the Persians, and told him to make proclamation in writing throughout all his kingdom, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 44:26–28; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:13; Romans 8:31"> Thus says Cyrus king of the Persians to all the kingdoms of the earth, The Lord God of heaven has given me power, and he has commanded me to build a house to him in Jerusalem, in Judea. Who is there of you of all his people? his God shall be with him, and let him go up.</span>
</div>
</div>
