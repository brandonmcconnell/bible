<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 15:9; Chronicles II 15:5; Kings III 12:27"> And in the thirty-eighth year of the reign of Asa, the king of Israel went up against Judah, and built Ramah, so as not to allow egress or ingress to Asa king of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings IV 12:18; Chronicles II 28:21; Kings IV 18:15"> And Asa took silver and gold out of the treasures of the house of the Lord, and of the king’s house, and sent them to the son of Hadad king of Syria, which dwelt in Damascus, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 15:4; Chronicles II 18:3; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> Make a covenant between me and thee, and between my father and thy father: behold, I have sent thee gold and silver: come, and turn away from me Baasha king of Israel, and let him depart from me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Chronicles II 17:12; Kings III 15:20"> And the son of Hadad hearkened to King Asa, and sent the captains of his host against the cities of Israel; and smote Ijon, and Dan, and Abel-maim, and all the country round Naphtali.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And it came to pass when Baasha heard it, he left off building Ramah, and put a stop to his work:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles II 21:17; Joshua 15:38; Isaiah 10:29"> then King Asa took all Judah, and took the stones of Ramah, and its timber, with which Baasha had built; and he built with them Geba and Mizpah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles II 19:2; Kings III 16:1; Psalms 146:3–6"> And at that time came Hanani the prophet to Asa king of Judah, and said to him, Because thou didst trust on the king of Syria, and didst not trust on the Lord thy God, therefore the army of Syria is escaped out of thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles II 12:3; Chronicles II 13:16; Chronicles II 16:7"> Were not the Ethiopians and Libyans a great force, in courage, in horsemen, in great numbers? and did not He deliver them into thy hands, because thou trustedst in the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Proverbs 15:3; Jeremiah 16:17"> For the eyes of the Lord look upon all the earth, to strengthen every heart that is perfect toward him. In this thou hast done foolishly; henceforth there shall be war with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Chronicles II 18:26; Acts 16:23–24; Kings II 12:13"> And Asa was angry with the prophet, and put him in prison, for he was angry at this: and Asa vexed some of the people at that time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles II 12:15; Chronicles II 9:29; Chronicles II 35:27"> And, behold, the acts of Asa, the first and the last, are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Chronicles II 28:22; Chronicles I 10:14"> And Asa was diseased in his feet in the thirty-ninth year of his reign, until he was very ill: but in his disease he sought not to the Lord, but to the physicians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 15:24"> And Asa slept with his fathers, and died in the fortieth year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 50:2; Chronicles II 21:19; Jeremiah 34:5"> And they buried him in the sepulcher which he had dug for himself in the city of David, and they laid him on a bed, and filled it with spices and all kinds of perfumes of the apothecaries; and they made for him a very great funeral.</span>
</div>
