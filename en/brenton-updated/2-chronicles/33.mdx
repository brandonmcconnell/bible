<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings IV 21:1–18; Chronicles I 3:13; Isaiah 3:12"> Manasseh was twelve years old when he began to reign, and he reigned fifty-five years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles II 28:3; Deuteronomy 18:9; Kings IV 17:11"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, according to all the abominations of the heathen, whom the Lord destroyed from before the face of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:21; Deuteronomy 17:3; Chronicles II 31:1"> And he returned and built the high places, which his father Hezekiah had pulled down, and set up images to Baalim, and made groves, and worshiped all the host of heaven, and served them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Chronicles II 7:16; Kings III 8:29; Deuteronomy 12:11"> And he built altars in the house of the Lord, concerning which the Lord said, In Jerusalem shall be my name forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:9; Jeremiah 32:34–35; Ezekiel 8:7–18"> And he built altars to all the host of heaven in the two courts of the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Leviticus 20:6; Chronicles II 28:3"> He also passed his children through the fire in the Valley of Ben-hinnom; and he divined, and used auspices, and sorceries, and appointed those who had divining spirits, and enchanters, and wrought abundant wickedness before the Lord, to provoke him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles II 33:4; Chronicles II 7:16; Kings III 11:13"> And he set the graven image, the molten statue, the idol which he made, in the house of God, of which God had said to David and to Solomon his son, In this house, and Jerusalem, which I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, I will put my name forever;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings II 7:10; Deuteronomy 5:31–1; Leviticus 8:36"> and I will not again remove the foot of Israel from the land which I gave to their fathers, if only they will take heed to do all things which I have commanded them, according to all the law and the ordinances and the judgments given by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings IV 24:3–4; Proverbs 29:12; Chronicles II 33:2"> So Manasseh led astray Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, to do evil beyond all the nations which the Lord cast out from before the children of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:15–16; Jeremiah 44:4–5; Zechariah 1:4"> And the Lord spoke to Manasseh, and to his people: but they hearkened not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:36; Chronicles II 36:6; Nehemiah 9:32"> And the Lord brought upon them the captains of the host of the king of Assyria, and they took Manasseh in bonds, and bound him in fetters, and brought him to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 10:3; Psalms 50:15; Chronicles II 32:26"> And when he was afflicted, he sought the face of the Lord his God, and was greatly humbled before the face of the God of his fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles I 5:20; Daniel 4:25; Matthew 7:7–8"> and he prayed to him: and he hearkened to him, and listened to his cry, and brought him back to Jerusalem to his kingdom: and Manasseh knew that the Lord he is God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles II 27:3; Kings III 1:33; Nehemiah 3:3"> And afterward he built a wall without the city of David, from the southwest southward in the valleys and at the entrance through the Fish Gate, as men go out by the gate round about, even as far as Ophel; and he raised it much, and set captains of the host in all the fortified cities in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles II 33:3–7; Hosea 14:1–3; Kings IV 21:7"> And he removed the strange gods and the graven image out of the house of the Lord, and all the altars which he had built in the mount of the house of the Lord, and in Jerusalem, and without the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles II 14:4; Kings III 18:30; Chronicles II 33:9"> And he repaired the altar of the Lord, and offered upon it a sacrifice of peace offering and thank offering, and he told Judah to serve the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles II 32:12; Kings III 22:43; Kings IV 15:4"> Nevertheless the people still sacrificed on the high places, only to the Lord their God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles II 33:19; Chronicles II 33:12–13; Amos 7:12"> And the rest of the acts of Manasseh, and his prayer to God, and the words of the seers that spoke to him in the name of the God of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 9:11; 1 John 1:9; Daniel 5:22"> behold, they are in the account of his prayer; and God hearkened to him. And all his sins, and his backslidings, and the spots on which he built the high places, and set there groves and graven images, before he repented, behold, they are written in the books of the seers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 1:10; Chronicles II 32:33; Kings IV 21:18–25"> And Manasseh slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the garden of his house: and Amon his son reigned in his stead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 4:13–15; Luke 12:19–20; Chronicles II 33:1"> Amon was twenty and two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned two years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings IV 21:20; Ezekiel 20:18; Kings IV 21:1–11"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, as his father Manasseh did: and Amon sacrificed to all the idols which his father Manasseh had made, and served them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Chronicles II 33:12; Chronicles II 33:19; Jeremiah 7:26"> And he was not humbled before the Lord as his father Manasseh was humbled; for his son Amon abounded in transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:22; Chronicles II 25:27–28; Chronicles II 24:25–26"> And his servants conspired against him, and slew him in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 35:31; Chronicles II 34:1; Chronicles II 36:1"> And the people of the land slew the men who had conspired against King Amon; and the people of the land made Josiah his son king in his stead.</span>
</div>
</div>
