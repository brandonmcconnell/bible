<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 22:1; Matthew 1:8–9; Chronicles II 33:25"> Then all the people of the land took Uzziah, and he was sixteen years old, and they made him king in the room of his father Amaziah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings IV 16:6; Chronicles II 25:28; Chronicles II 25:23"> He built Eloth, he recovered it to Judah, after the king slept with his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings IV 15:2–3; Amos 1:1; Hosea 1:1"> Uzziah began to reign at the age of sixteen years, and he reigned fifty-two years in Jerusalem: and his mother’s name was Jecoliah of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Chronicles II 25:2"> And he did that which was right in the sight of the Lord, according to all that Amaziah his father did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles II 15:2; Chronicles II 24:2; Chronicles I 22:13"> And he sought the Lord in the days of Zechariah, who understood the fear of the Lord; and in his days he sought the Lord, and the Lord prospered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:29; Kings I 5:6; Kings I 5:1"> And he went out and fought against the Philistines, and pulled down the walls of Gath, and the walls of Jabneh, and the walls of Ashdod, and he built cities near Ashdod, and among the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles II 21:16; Chronicles I 5:20; Chronicles II 14:11"> And the Lord strengthened him against the Philistines, and against the Arabians that dwelt on the rock, and against the Meunites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings II 8:2; Chronicles II 17:11; Genesis 19:38"> And the Ammonites gave gifts to Uzziah; and his fame spread as far as the entering in of Egypt, for he strengthened himself exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:13; Chronicles II 25:23; Kings IV 14:13"> And Uzziah built towers in Jerusalem, both at the gate of the corner and at the valley gate, and at the corners, and he fortified them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 26:18–21; Isaiah 29:17; Kings IV 3:4"> And he built towers in the wilderness, and dug many wells, for he had many cattle in the low country and in the plain; and vinedressers in the mountain country and in Carmel: for he was a husbandman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings IV 5:2"> And Uzziah had a host of warriors, and that went out orderly to war, and returned orderly in number; and their number was made by the hand of Jeiel the scribe, and Maaseiah the judge, by the hand of Ananias the king’s deputy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The whole number of the chiefs of families of the mighty men of war was two thousand six hundred;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles II 25:5; Chronicles II 14:8; Chronicles II 11:1"> and with them was a warrior force, three hundred thousand and seven thousand and five hundred: these waged war mightily to help the king against his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 20:16; Kings I 17:49"> And Uzziah prepared for them, even for all the host, shields, and spears, and helmets, and breastplates, and bows, and slings for stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 31:4; Matthew 4:24; Chronicles II 2:7"> And he made in Jerusalem machines invented by a wise contriver, to be upon the towers and upon the corners, to cast darts and great stones: and the fame of their preparation was heard at a distance; for he was wonderfully helped, till he was strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles II 32:25; Chronicles II 25:19; Proverbs 16:18"> And when he was strong, his heart was lifted up to his destruction; and he transgressed against the Lord his God, and went into the temple of the Lord to burn incense on the altar of incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles I 6:10; Chronicles I 26:6; Chronicles I 12:28"> And there went in after him Azariah the priest, and with him eighty priests of the Lord, mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 30:7–8; Numbers 18:7; Jeremiah 13:18"> And they withstood Uzziah the king, and said to him, It is not for thee, Uzziah, to burn incense to the Lord, but only for the priests the sons of Aaron, who are consecrated to sacrifice: go forth of the sanctuary, for thou hast departed from the Lord; and this shall not be for glory to thee from the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 12:10; Chronicles II 25:16; Kings IV 5:25–27"> And Uzziah was angry, and in his hand was the censer to burn incense in the temple: and when he was angry with the priests, then the leprosy rose up in his forehead before the priests in the house of the Lord, over the altar of incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:22; Esther 6:12; Leviticus 14:34"> And Azariah the chief priest, and the other priests, turned to look at him, and, behold, he was leprous in his forehead; and they got him hastily out thence, for he also hastened to go out, because the Lord had rebuked him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 13:46; Numbers 12:15; Kings IV 7:3"> And Uzziah the king was a leper to the day of his death, and he dwelt as a leper in a separate house; for he was cut off from the house of the Lord: and Jotham his son was set over his kingdom, judging the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; Isaiah 6:1; Chronicles II 9:29"> And the rest of the acts of Uzziah, the first and the last, are written by Isaiah the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Chronicles II 28:27; Chronicles II 21:20; Chronicles II 26:18"> And Uzziah slept with his fathers, and they buried him with his fathers in the field of the burial place of the kings, for they said, He is a leper; and Jotham his son reigned in his stead.</span>
