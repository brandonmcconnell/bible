<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 8:64; Ezekiel 43:13–17; Kings III 9:25"> And he made a brazen altar, the length of it twenty cubits, and the breadth twenty cubits, and the height ten cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 3:5; Zechariah 13:1; Exodus 30:18–21"> And he made the molten sea, in diameter ten cubits, entirely round, and the height of it five cubits, and the circumference thirty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:10; Ezekiel 10:14; 1 Corinthians 9:9–10"> And beneath it the likeness of calves, they compass it round about: ten cubits compass the laver round about, they cast the calves two rows in their casting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 28:19–20; Revelation 21:14; Mark 16:15"> wherein they made them twelve calves—three looking northward, and three westward, and three southward, and three eastward: and the sea was upon them above, and their hinder parts were inward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings III 7:26"> And its thickness was a handbreadth, and its brim as the brim of a cup, graven with flowers of lilies, holding three thousand measures: and he finished it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 7:38; Exodus 30:18–21; Kings III 7:40"> And he made ten lavers, and set five on the right hand, and five on the left, to wash in them the instruments of the whole burnt offerings, and to rinse the vessels in them; and the sea was for the priests to wash in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–40; Kings III 7:49; John 8:12"> And he made the ten golden candlesticks according to their pattern, and he put them in the temple, five on the right hand, and five on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 7:48; Malachi 1:12; Exodus 25:23–30"> And he made ten tables, and put them in the temple, five on the right hand, and five on the left: and he made a hundred golden bowls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 6:36; Kings IV 21:5; Kings III 7:12"> Also he made the priests’ court, and the great court, and doors to the court, and their panels were overlaid with brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings III 7:39"> And he set the sea at the corner of the house on the right, as it were fronting the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings III 7:40–51; Kings III 7:14"> And Hiram made the fleshhooks, and the fire pans, and the grate of the altar, and all its instruments: and Hiram finished doing all the work which he wrought for King Solomon in the house of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings III 7:41; Chronicles II 3:15–17"> two pillars, and upon them an embossed work for the chapiters on the heads of the two pillars, and two nets to cover the heads of the chapiters which are on the heads of the pillars;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 7:20; Kings III 7:42; Jeremiah 52:23"> and four hundred golden bells for the two nets, and two rows of pomegranates in each net, to cover the two embossed rims of the chapiters which are upon the pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings III 7:27–43"> And he made the ten bases, and he made the lavers upon the bases;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and the one sea, and the twelve calves under it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:13; Kings III 7:13–14; Chronicles II 4:11"> and the footbaths, and the buckets, and the caldrons, and the fleshhooks, and all their furniture (which Hiram made, and brought to King Solomon in the house of the Lord) of pure brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 7:46"> In the country round about Jordan the king cast them, in the clay ground in the house of Succoth, and between that and Zeredah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 7:47; Jeremiah 52:20; Chronicles I 22:14"> So Solomon made all these vessels in great abundance, for the quantity of brass failed not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 9:13; Daniel 5:2–3; Exodus 25:23–30"> And Solomon made all the vessels of the house of the Lord, and the golden altar, and the tables, and upon them were to be the loaves of showbread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:7; Exodus 25:31–37; Kings III 6:5"> also the candlesticks, and the lamps to give light according to the pattern, and in front of the oracle, of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings III 6:29; Exodus 37:20; Kings III 6:35"> And their snuffers, and their lamps were made, and he made the bowls, and the censers, and the fire pans, of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings IV 12:13; Kings IV 25:14; Jeremiah 52:18"> And there was the inner door of the house opening into the holy of holies, and he made the inner doors of the temple of gold. So all the work which Solomon wrought for the house of the Lord was finished.</span>
