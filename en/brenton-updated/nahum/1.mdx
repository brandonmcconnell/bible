<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:13; Jonah 1:2; Zechariah 9:1"> The burden of Nineveh: the book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 8:2; Exodus 20:5; Psalms 94:1"> God is jealous, and the Lord avenges; the Lord avenges with wrath; the Lord takes vengeance on his adversaries, and he cuts off his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 147:5; Exodus 34:6–7; Nehemiah 9:17"> The Lord is long-suffering, and his power is great, and the Lord will not hold any guiltless: his way is in destruction and in the whirlwind, and the clouds are the dust of his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 33:9; Psalms 106:9; Psalms 114:5"> He threatens the sea, and dries it up, and exhausts all the rivers: the land of Bashan, and Carmel are brought low, and the flourishing trees of Lebanon have come to nought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 1:4; Kings II 22:8; Jeremiah 4:24"> The mountains quake at him, and the hills are shaken, and the earth recoils at his presence, even the world, and all that dwell in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:10; Malachi 3:2; Deuteronomy 32:22–23"> Who shall stand before his anger? and who shall withstand in the anger of his wrath? his wrath brings to nought kingdoms, and the rocks are burst asunder by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:27; 2 Timothy 2:19; Lamentations 3:25"> The Lord is good to them that wait on him in the day of affliction; and he knows them that reverence him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 28:17; Isaiah 8:22; Ezekiel 13:13"> But with an overrunning flood he will make an utter end: darkness shall pursue those that rise up against him and his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 21:11; Proverbs 21:30; Psalms 33:10"> What do ye devise against the Lord? he will make a complete end: he will not take vengeance by affliction twice at the same time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 7:4; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 9:18"> For the enemy shall be laid bare even to the foundation, and shall be devoured as twisted yew, and as stubble fully dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; Chronicles II 13:7; Kings IV 18:13–14"> Out of thee shall proceed a device against the Lord, counseling evil things hostile to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 30:19; Joel 2:19; Isaiah 17:14"> Thus saith the Lord who rules over many waters, Even thus shall they be sent away, and the report of thee shall not be heard anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 10:27; Isaiah 9:4; Jeremiah 2:20"> And now will I break his rod from off thee, and will burst thy bonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 109:13; Isaiah 46:1–2; Micah 5:13"> And the Lord shall give a command concerning thee; there shall no more of thy name be scattered: I will utterly destroy the graven images out of the house of thy god, and the molten images: I will make thy grave; for they are swift.</span>
