<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Psalms 5:6; Proverbs 28:17"> And if one be found slain with the sword in the land, which the Lord thy God gives thee to inherit, having fallen in the field, and they do not know who has smitten him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 13:3–4; Deuteronomy 16:18–19"> thine elders and thy judges shall come forth, and shall measure the distances of the cities round about the slain man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 19:2; Matthew 11:28–30; Jeremiah 31:18"> and it shall be that the city which is nearest to the slain man the elders of that city shall take a heifer of the herd, which has not labored, and which has not borne a yoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:21–24; 1 Peter 3:18"> And the elders of that city shall bring down the heifer into a rough valley, which has not been tilled and is not sown, and they shall slay the heifer in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles I 23:13; Malachi 2:7; Deuteronomy 10:8"> And the priests the Levites shall come, because the Lord God has chosen them to stand by him, and to bless in his name, and by their word shall every controversy and every stroke be decided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 73:13; Psalms 26:6; Psalms 51:7"> And all the elders of that city who draw nigh to the slain man shall wash their hands over the head of the heifer which was slain in the valley;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 21:21–23; Job 21:31–34; Kings II 16:8"> and they shall answer and say, Our hands have not shed this blood, and our eyes have not seen it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 1:14; Kings IV 24:4; Ezekiel 23:24–25"> Be merciful to thy people Israel, whom thou hast redeemed, O Lord, that innocent blood may not be charged on thy people Israel: and the blood shall be atoned for to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:12–13; Kings IV 10:30–31; Deuteronomy 13:18"> And thou shalt take away innocent blood from among you, if thou shouldest do that which is good and pleasing before the Lord thy God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:10–16; Joshua 21:44"> And if when thou goest out to war against thine enemies, the Lord thy God should deliver them into thy hands, and thou shouldest take their spoil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 31:10; Genesis 34:3; Genesis 12:14–15"> and shouldest see among the spoil a woman beautiful in countenance, and shouldest desire her, and take her to thyself for a wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 14:9; Numbers 6:9; Ephesians 4:22"> and shouldest bring her within thy house: then shalt thou shave her head, and pare her nails;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 45:10–11; Luke 14:26–27"> and shalt take away her garments of captivity from off her, and she shall abide in thy house, and shall bewail her father and mother the days of a month; and afterward thou shalt go in to her and dwell with her, and she shall be thy wife.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:29; Genesis 34:2; Deuteronomy 22:19"> And it shall be if thou do not delight in her, thou shalt send her out free; and she shall not by any means be sold for money, thou shalt not treat her contemptuously, because thou hast humbled her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 29:33; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 29:18"> And if a man have two wives, the one loved and the other hated, and both the loved and the hated should have born him children, and the son of the hated should be firstborn;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles I 26:10; Chronicles I 5:2; Chronicles II 21:3"> then it shall be that whensoever he shall divide by inheritance his goods to his sons, he shall not be able to give the right of the firstborn to the son of the loved one, having overlooked the son of the hated, which is the firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 49:3; Genesis 25:31–34; Psalms 105:36"> But he shall acknowledge the firstborn of the hated one to give to him double of all things which shall be found by him, because he is the first of his children, and to him belongs the birthright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 20:12; Amos 4:11–12"> And if any man has a disobedient and contentious son, who hearkens not to the voice of his father and the voice of his mother, and they should correct him, and he should not hearken to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 13:3; Deuteronomy 21:2; Deuteronomy 16:18"> then shall his father and his mother take hold of him, and bring him forth to the elders of his city, and to the gate of the place:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29–35; Proverbs 20:1; Proverbs 23:19–21"> and they shall say to the men of their city, This our son is disobedient and contentious, he hearkens not to our voice, he is a reveler and a drunkard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:19–20; Deuteronomy 13:10–11; Leviticus 24:16"> And the men of his city shall stone him with stones, and he shall die; and thou shalt remove the evil one from yourselves, and the rest shall hear and fear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 19:31–38; Acts 26:31; Luke 23:33"> And if there be sin in anyone, and the judgment of death be upon him, and he be put to death, and ye hang him on a tree:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 3:13; John 19:31; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> his body shall not remain all night upon the tree, but ye shall by all means bury it in that day; for everyone that is hanged on a tree is cursed of God; and ye shall by no means defile the land which the Lord thy God gives thee for an inheritance.</span>
</div>
