<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:10; Exodus 23:10–11; Leviticus 25:2–4"> Every seven years thou shalt make a release.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 7:42; James 2:13; Luke 6:34–38"> And this is the ordinance of the release: thou shalt remit every private debt which thy neighbor owes thee, and thou shalt not ask payment of it from thy brother; for it has been called a release to the Lord thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:20; Matthew 17:25–26; 1 Corinthians 6:6–7"> Of a stranger thou shalt ask again whatsoever he has of thine, but to thy brother thou shalt remit his debt to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 58:10–11; Proverbs 28:27; Proverbs 11:24–25"> For thus there shall not be a poor person in the midst of thee, for the Lord thy God will surely bless thee in the land which the Lord thy God gives thee by inheritance, that thou shouldest inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:1–15; Isaiah 1:19–20; Joshua 1:7"> And if ye shall indeed hearken to the voice of the Lord your God, to keep and do all these commandments, as many as I charge thee this day</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:12–13; Luke 6:35; Nehemiah 9:27"> (for the Lord thy God has blessed thee in the way of which he spoke to thee), then thou shalt lend to many nations, but thou shalt not borrow; and thou shalt rule over many nations, but they shall not rule over thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 3:16–17; Deuteronomy 15:9; Proverbs 21:13"> And if there shall be in the midst of thee a poor man of thy brethren in one of thy cities in the land, which the Lord thy God gives thee, thou shalt not harden thy heart, neither shalt thou by any means close up thy hand from thy brother who is in want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 6:34; Matthew 5:42"> Thou shalt surely open thy hands to him, and shalt lend to him as much as he wants according to his need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:15; Matthew 20:15; Jeremiah 17:10"> Take heed to thyself that there be not a secret thing in thy heart, an iniquity, saying, The seventh year, the year of release, draws nigh; and thine eye shall be evil to thy brother that is in want, and thou shalt not give to him, and he shall cry against thee to the Lord, and there shall be great sin in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 22:9; Hebrews 13:16; Isaiah 58:10"> Thou shalt surely give to him, and thou shalt lend him as much as he wants, according as he is in need; and thou shalt not grudge in thy heart as thou givest to him, because on this account the Lord thy God will bless thee in all thy works, and in all things on which thou shalt lay thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 14:7; Matthew 26:11; John 12:8"> For the poor shall not fail off thy land, therefore I charge thee to do this thing, saying, Thou shalt surely open thy hands to thy poor brother, and to him that is distressed upon thy land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:14; Leviticus 25:39–41; Exodus 21:2–6"> And if thy brother or sister, a Hebrew man or a Hebrew woman, be sold to thee, he shall serve thee six years, and in the seventh year thou shalt send him out free from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 22:13; Proverbs 3:27–28"> And when thou shalt send him out free from thee, thou shalt not send him out empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:2; Proverbs 10:22; Psalms 68:10"> Thou shalt give him provision for the way from thy flock, and from thy corn, and from thy wine; as the Lord thy God has blessed thee, thou shalt give to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:12; Matthew 18:32–33; Deuteronomy 5:14–15"> And thou shalt remember that thou wast a servant in the land of Egypt, and the Lord thy God redeemed thee from thence; therefore I charge thee to do this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 21:5–6; Psalms 40:8; Psalms 40:6"> And if he should say to thee, I will not go out from thee, because he continues to love thee and thy house, because he is well with thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings I 1:22; Leviticus 25:39–42"> then thou shalt take an awl, and bore his ear through to the door, and he shall be thy servant forever; and in like manner shalt thou do to thy maidservant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 17:7–8; Isaiah 16:14; Isaiah 21:16"> It shall not seem hard to thee when they are sent out free from thee, because thy servant has served thee six years according to the annual hire of a hireling; so the Lord thy God shall bless thee in all things whatsoever thou mayest do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12; Leviticus 27:26"> Every firstborn that shall be born among thy kine and thy sheep, thou shalt sanctify the males to the Lord thy God; thou shalt not work with thy firstborn calf, and thou shalt not shear the firstborn of thy sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:17; Deuteronomy 14:23; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> Thou shalt eat it before the Lord year by year in the place which the Lord thy God shall choose, thou and thy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:1; Malachi 1:7–8; Leviticus 22:19–25"> And if there be in it a blemish, if it be lame or blind, an evil blemish, thou shalt not sacrifice it to the Lord thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:21–22; Deuteronomy 12:15–16"> Thou shalt eat it in thy cities; the unclean in thee and the clean shall eat it in like manner, as the doe or the stag.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:16; Leviticus 7:26; Deuteronomy 12:23"> Only ye shall not eat the blood; thou shalt pour it out on the earth as water.</span>
