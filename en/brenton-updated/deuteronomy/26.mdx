<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:14; Deuteronomy 13:9; Deuteronomy 6:1–10"> And it shall be when thou shalt have entered into the land, which the Lord thy God gives thee to inherit it, and thou shalt have inherited it, and thou shalt have dwelt upon it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Exodus 23:16; Ezekiel 44:30"> that thou shalt take of the first of the fruits of thy land, which the Lord thy God gives thee, and thou shalt put them into a basket, and thou shalt go to the place which the Lord thy God shall choose to have his name called there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 105:9–10; Deuteronomy 19:17; Genesis 26:3"> And thou shalt come to the priest who shall be in those days, and thou shalt say to him, I testify this day to the Lord my God, that I am come into the land which the Lord swore to our fathers to give to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Matthew 5:23–24; Hebrews 13:10–12"> And the priest shall take the basket out of thy hands, and shall set it before the altar of the Lord thy God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Genesis 46:27; Genesis 31:20"> and he shall answer and say before the Lord thy God, My father abandoned Syria, and went down into Egypt, and sojourned there with a small number, and became there a mighty nation and a great multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Exodus 1:14; Exodus 5:23"> And the Egyptians afflicted us, and humbled us, and imposed hard tasks on us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 2:23–4; Ephesians 3:20–21; Exodus 3:9"> and we cried to the Lord our God, and the Lord heard our voice, and saw our humiliation, and our labor, and our affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Deuteronomy 5:15; Psalms 78:12–13"> And the Lord brought us out of Egypt himself with his great strength, and his mighty hand, and his high arm, and with great visions, and with signs, and with wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Psalms 105:44; Psalms 107:7–8"> And he brought us into this place, and gave us this land, a land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:2; Deuteronomy 18:4; Revelation 22:9"> And now, behold, I have brought the first of the fruits of the land, which thou gavest me, O Lord, a land flowing with milk and honey: and thou shalt leave it before the Lord thy God, and thou shalt worship before the Lord thy God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:11; Deuteronomy 12:7; Philippians 4:4"> and thou shalt rejoice in all the good things, which the Lord thy God has given thee, thou and thy family, and the Levite, and the stranger that is within thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 27:30; Numbers 18:24; Philippians 4:18–19"> And when thou shalt have completed all the tithings of thy fruits in the third year, thou shalt give the second tenth to the Levite, and stranger, and fatherless, and widow; and they shall eat it in thy cities, and be merry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 119:176; Psalms 119:153; Psalms 119:141"> And thou shalt say before the Lord thy God, I have fully collected the holy things out of my house, and I have given them to the Levite, and the stranger, and the orphan, and the widow, according to all the commands which thou didst command me: I did not transgress thy command, and I did not forget it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 7:20; Leviticus 21:11; Hosea 9:4"> And in my distress I did not eat of them, I have not gathered of them for an unclean purpose, I have not given of them to the dead; I have hearkened to the voice of the Lord our God, I have done as thou hast commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 63:15; Zechariah 2:13; Jeremiah 31:23"> Look down from thy holy house, from heaven, and bless thy people Israel, and the land which thou hast given them, as thou didst swear to our fathers, to give to us a land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:1; John 14:15; 1 John 5:2–3"> On this day the Lord thy God charged thee to keep all the ordinances and judgments; and ye shall observe and do them, with all your heart, and with all your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 24:7; Isaiah 12:2; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20"> Thou hast chosen God this day to be thy God, and to walk in all his ways, and to observe his ordinances and judgments, and to hearken to his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 14:2; Deuteronomy 28:9"> And the Lord has chosen thee this day that thou shouldest be to him a peculiar people, as he said, to keep his commands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 28:1"> and that thou shouldest be above all nations, as he has made thee renowned, and a boast, and glorious, that thou shouldest be a holy people to the Lord thy God, as he has spoken.</span>
