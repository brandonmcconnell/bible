<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Now this one, having endured this praiseworthy death, the third was brought along, and exhorted by many to taste and save his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> But he cried out and said, Know ye not, that the father of those who are dead, begot me also; and that the same mother bare me; and that I was brought up in the same tenets?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> I abjure not the noble relationship of my brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Now then, whatever instrument of vengeance ye have, apply it to my body, for ye are not able to touch, even if ye wish it, my soul.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> But they, highly incensed at his boldness of speech, dislocated his hands and feet with racking engines, and wrenching them from their sockets, dismembered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And they dragged round his fingers, and his arms, and his legs, and his ankles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And not being able by any means to strangle him, they tore off his skin, together with the extreme tips of his fingers, flayed him, and then haled him to the wheel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> around which his vertebral joints were loosened, and he saw his own flesh torn to shreds, and streams of blood flowing from his entrails.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And when about to die, he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> We, O accursed tyrant, suffer this for the sake of Divine education and virtue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> But thou, for thy impiety and bloodshedding, shalt endure indissoluble torments.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And thus having died worthily of his brethren, they dragged forward the fourth, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Do not thou share the madness of thy brethren: but give regard to the king, and save thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But he said to them, You have not a fire so scorching as to make me play the coward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> By the blessed death of my brethren, and the eternal punishment of the tyrant, and the glorious life of the pious, I will not repudiate the noble brotherhood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Invent, O tyrant, tortures; that you may learn, even through them, that I am the brother of those tormented before.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> When he had said this, the bloodthirsty, and murderous, and unhallowed Antiochus ordered his tongue to be cut out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But he said, Even if you take away the organ of speech, yet God hears the silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Behold, my tongue is extended, cut it off; for not for that shalt thou extirpate our reasoning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Gladly do we lose our limbs in behalf of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But God shall speedily find you, since you cut off the tongue, the instrument of divine melody.</span>
</div>
</div>
