<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And what wonder? if the lusts of the soul, after participation with what is beautiful, are frustrated,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> on this ground, therefore, the temperate Joseph is praised in that by reasoning, he subdued, on reflection, the indulgence of sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For, although young, and ripe for sexual intercourse, he abrogated by reasoning the stimulus of his passions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And it is not merely the stimulus of sensual indulgence, but that of every desire, that reasoning is able to master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For instance, the law says, Thou shalt not covet thy neighbor’s wife, nor anything that belongs to thy neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Now, then, since it is the law which has forbidden us to desire, I shall much the more easily persuade you, that reasoning is able to govern our lusts, just as it does the affections which are impediments to justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Since in what way is a solitary eater, and a glutton, and a drunkard reclaimed, unless it be clear that reasoning is lord of the passions?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> A man, therefore, who regulates his course by the law, even if he be a lover of money, straightaway puts force upon his own disposition; lending to the needy without interest, and canceling the debt of the incoming Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And should a man be parsimonious, he is ruled by the law acting through reasoning; so that he does not glean his harvest crops, nor vintage: and in reference to other points we may perceive that it is reasoning that conquers his passions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For the law conquers even affection toward parents, not surrendering virtue on their account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And it prevails over marriage love, condemning it when transgressing law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And it lords it over the love of parents toward their children, for they punish them for vice; and it domineers over the intimacy of friends, reproving them when wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And think it not a strange assertion that reasoning can in behalf of the law conquer even enmity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> It alloweth not to cut down the cultivated herbage of an enemy, but preserveth it from the destroyers, and collecteth their fallen ruins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And reasoning appears to be master of the more violent passions, as love of empire and empty boasting, and arrogance, and loud boasting, and slander.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For the temperate understanding repels all these malignant passions, as it does wrath: for it masters even this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Thus Moses, when angered against Dathan and Abiram, did nothing to them in wrath, but regulated his anger by reasoning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For the temperate mind is able, as I said, to be superior to the passions, and to transfer some, and destroy others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For why, else, does our most wise father Jacob blame Simeon and Levi for having irrationally slain the whole race of the Shechemites, saying, Cursed be their anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For if reasoning did not possess the power of subduing angry affections, he would not have spoken thus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For at the time when God created man, He implanted within him his passions and moral nature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And at that time He enthroned above all the holy leader mind, through the medium of the senses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And He gave a law to this mind, by living according to which it will maintain a temperate, and just, and good, and manly reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> How, then, a man may say, if reasoning be master of the passions, has it no control over forgetfulness and ignorance?</span>
