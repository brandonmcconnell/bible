<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> For, behold, in those days and at that time, when I shall have turned the captivity of Judah and Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> I will also gather all the Gentiles, and bring them down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat, and will plead with them there for my people and my heritage Israel, who have been dispersed among the Gentiles; and these Gentiles have divided my land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and cast lots over my people, and have given their boys to harlots, and sold their girls for wine, and have drunk.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And what have ye to do with me, O Tyre, and Sidon, and all Galilee of the Gentiles? do ye render me a recompense? or do ye bear malice against me? quickly and speedily will I return your recompense on your own heads:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> because ye have taken my silver and my gold, and ye have brought my choice ornaments into your temples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and ye have sold the children of Judah and the children of Jerusalem to the children of the Greeks, that ye might expel them from their coasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Therefore, behold, I will raise them up out of the place whither ye have sold them, and I will return your recompense on your own heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And I will sell your sons and your daughters into the hands of the children of Judah, and they shall sell them into captivity to a far distant nation: for the Lord has spoken it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Proclaim these things among the Gentiles; declare war, arouse the warriors, draw near and go up, all ye men of war.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Beat your plowshares into swords, and your sickles into spears: let the weak say, I am strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Gather yourselves together, and go in, all ye nations round about, and gather yourselves there: let the timid become a warrior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Let them be aroused, let all the nations go up to the Valley of Jehoshaphat: for there will I sit to judge all the Gentiles round about.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Bring forth the sickles, for the vintage is come: go in, tread the grapes, for the press is full: cause the vats to overflow; for their wickedness is multiplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Noises have resounded in the valley of judgment: for the day of the Lord is near in the valley of judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The sun and the moon shall be darkened, and the stars shall withdraw their light.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the Lord shall cry out of Zion, and shall utter his voice from Jerusalem; and the heaven and the earth shall be shaken, but the Lord shall spare his people, and shall strengthen the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And ye shall know that I am the Lord your God, who dwell in Zion my holy mountain: and Jerusalem shall be holy, and strangers shall not pass through her anymore.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And it shall come to pass in that day that the mountains shall drop sweet wine, and the hills shall flow with milk, and all the fountains of Judah shall flow with water, and a fountain shall go forth of the house of the Lord, and water the Valley of Flags.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Egypt shall be a desolation, and Edom shall be a desolate plain, because of the wrongs of the children of Judah, because they have shed righteous blood in their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But Judea shall be inhabited forever, and Jerusalem to all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And I will make inquisition for their blood, and will by no means leave it unavenged: and the Lord shall dwell in Zion.</span>
</div>
</div>
