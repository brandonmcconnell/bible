<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> AND they walked in the midst of the fire, praising God, and blessing the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Then Azarias stood, and prayed on this manner; and opening his mouth in the midst of the fire said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Blessed art thou, O Lord, thou God of our fathers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Another reading is, and thy name is worthy to be praised and glorified.">a</a></sup>and worthy to be praised; and thy name is glorified for evermore:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For thou art righteous in all the things that thou hast done: yea, true are all thy works, and thy ways are right, and all thy judgements truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> In all the things that thou hast brought upon us, and upon the holy city of our fathers, even Jerusalem, thou hast executed true judgements: for according to truth and justice hast thou brought all these things upon us because of our sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For we have sinned and committed iniquity, in departing from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> In all things have we trespassed, and not obeyed thy commandments, nor kept them, neither done as thou hast commanded us, that it might go well with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Wherefore all that thou hast brought upon us, and everything that thou hast done to us, thou hast done in true judgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And thou didst deliver us into the hands of lawless enemies, and most hateful forsakers of God, and to an unjust king, and the most wicked in all the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And now we cannot open our mouth; shame and reproach have befallen thy servants, and them that worship thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Deliver us not up utterly, for thy name’s sake, neither disannul thou thy covenant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and cause not thy mercy to depart from us, for the sake of Abraham that is beloved of thee, and for the sake of Isaac thy servant, and Israel thy holy one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> to whom thou didst promise, that thou wouldest multiply their seed as the stars of heaven, and as the sand that is upon the sea shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For we, O Lord, are become less than any nation, and be kept under this day in all the world because of our sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Neither is there at this time prince, or prophet, or leader, or burnt offering, or sacrifice, or oblation, or incense, or place to offer before thee, and to find mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Nevertheless in a contrite heart and a humble spirit let us be accepted;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> like as in the burnt offerings of rams and bullocks, and like as in ten thousands of fat lambs; so let our sacrifice be in thy sight this day, and grant that we may wholly go after thee, for they shall not be ashamed that put their trust in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And now we follow thee with all our heart, we fear thee, and seek thy face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Put us not to shame: but deal with us after thy kindness, and according to the multitude of thy mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Deliver us also according to thy marvellous works, and give glory to thy name, O Lord: and let all them that do thy servants hurt be confounded;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and let them be ashamed of all their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some MSS. omit power and.">b</a></sup>power and might, and let their strength be broken;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and let them know that thou art the Lord, the only God, and glorious over the whole world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the king’s servants, that put them in, ceased not to make the furnace hot with naphtha, pitch, tow, and small wood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> so that the flame streamed forth above the furnace forty and nine cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And it spread, and burned those Chaldeans whom it found about the furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> But the angel of the Lord came down into the furnace together with Azarias and his fellows, and he smote the flame of the fire out of the furnace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and made the midst of the furnace as it had been a moist whistling wind, so that the fire touched them not at all, neither hurt nor troubled them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Then the three, as out of one mouth, praised, and glorified, and blessed God in the furnace, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Blessed art thou, O Lord, thou God of our fathers: and to be praised and exalted above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And blessed is thy glorious and holy name: and to be praised and exalted above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Blessed art thou in the temple of thine holy glory: and to be praised and glorified above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Blessed art thou that beholdest the depths, and sittest upon the cherubim: and to be praised and exalted above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Blessed art thou on the throne of thy kingdom: and to be praised and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Another reading is, exalted.">c</a></sup>extolled above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Blessed art thou in the firmament of heaven: and to be praised and glorified for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> O all ye works of the Lord, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> O ye heavens, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> O ye angels of the Lord, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> O all ye waters that be above the heaven, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> O all<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Another reading is, the host.">d</a></sup>ye powers of the Lord, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> O ye sun and moon, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> O ye stars of heaven, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> O every shower and dew, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> O all ye winds, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> O ye fire and heat, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Verse 45 is omitted by the best authorities.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Before this verse some authorities insert verse 46, O ye dews and storms of snow, bless ye &amp;c.">f</a></sup>O ye nights and days, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> O ye light and darkness, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. O ye cold and heat, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Before this verse some authorities insert verse 49, O ye ice and cold, bless ye &amp;c.">g</a></sup>O ye frost and snow, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> O ye lightnings and clouds, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> O let the earth bless the Lord: let it praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> O ye mountains and hills, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> O all ye things that grow on the earth, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some authorities transpose verses 56 and 55.">h</a></sup>O sea and rivers, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> O ye fountains, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> O ye whales, and all that move in the waters, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> O all ye fowls of the air, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> O all ye beasts and cattle, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> O ye children of men, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some MSS. read O Israel, bless ye.">i</a></sup>O let Israel bless the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> O ye priests<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Some MSS. omit of the Lord.">j</a></sup>of the Lord, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> O ye servants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Some MSS. omit of the Lord.">k</a></sup>of the Lord, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64"> O ye spirits and souls of the righteous, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65"> O ye that are holy and humble of heart, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66"> O Ananias, Azarias, and Misael, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever: for he hath rescued us from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or, the grave Gr. Hades.">l</a></sup>hell, and saved us from the hand of death: he hath delivered us out of the midst of the furnace and burning flame, even out of the midst of the fire hath he delivered us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67"> O give thanks unto the Lord, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68"> O all ye that worship the Lord, bless the God of gods, praise him, and give him thanks: for his mercy endureth for ever.</span>
</div>
