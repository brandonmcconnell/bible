<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:35; Isaiah 51:3; Isaiah 55:12–13"> The wilderness and the solitary place shall be glad; and the desert shall rejoice, and blossom as the rose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Isaiah 60:13; Amos 9:13–15"> It shall blossom abundantly, and rejoice even with joy and singing; the glory of Lebanon shall be given unto it, the excellency of Carmel and Sharon: they shall see the glory of the LORD, the excellency of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:12; Job 4:3–4; Luke 22:32"> Strengthen ye the weak hands, and confirm the feeble knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 5:7–9; Isaiah 41:10–14; Haggai 2:4"> Say to them that are of a fearful heart, Be strong, fear not: behold, your God will come with vengeance, with the recompence of God; he will come and save you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 42:6–7; Luke 7:20–23; Isaiah 29:18"> Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall be unstopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 5:8–9; John 7:37–39; Luke 11:14"> Then shall the lame man leap as an hart, and the tongue of the dumb shall sing: for in the wilderness shall waters break out, and streams in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 26:18; Isaiah 49:10; John 4:14"> And the glowing sand shall become a pool, and the thirsty ground springs of water: in the habitation of jackals, where they lay, shall be grass with reeds and rushes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 7:13–14; Isaiah 42:16; John 14:6"> And an highway shall be there, and a way, and it shall be called The way of holiness; the unclean shall not pass over it; but it shall be for those: the wayfaring men, yea fools, shall not err therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 62:12; Hosea 2:18; Leviticus 26:6"> No lion shall be there, nor shall any ravenous beast go up thereon, they shall not be found there; but the redeemed shall walk there:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 30:19; Revelation 21:4; Isaiah 25:8"> and the ransomed of the LORD shall return, and come with singing unto Zion; and everlasting joy shall be upon their heads: they shall obtain gladness and joy, and sorrow and sighing shall flee away.</span>
