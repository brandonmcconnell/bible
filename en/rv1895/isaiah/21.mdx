<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 14:23; Isaiah 13:1; Jeremiah 51:42"> The burden of the wilderness of the sea. As whirlwinds in the South sweep through, it cometh from the wilderness, from a terrible land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Jeremiah 49:34; Psalms 60:3"> A grievous vision is declared unto me; the treacherous dealer dealeth treacherously, and the spoiler spoileth. Go up, O Elam; besiege, O Media; all the sighing thereof have I made to cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 13:8; Psalms 48:6; Isaiah 26:17"> Therefore are my loins filled with anguish; pangs have taken hold upon me, as the pangs of a woman in travail: I am pained so that I cannot hear; I am dismayed so that I cannot see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:34–36; 1 Samuel 25:36–38; Daniel 5:1"> My heart panteth, horror hath affrighted me: the twilight that I desired hath been turned into trembling unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 51:57; Jeremiah 51:39"> They prepare the table, they set the watch, they eat, they drink: rise up, ye princes, anoint the shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:2–7; Ezekiel 3:17; Habakkuk 2:1–2"> For thus hath the Lord said unto me, Go, set a watchman; let him declare what he seeth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 21:9; Isaiah 37:24; Hebrews 2:1"> and when he seeth a troop, horsemen in pairs, a troop of asses, a troop of camels, he shall hearken diligently with much heed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:1–2; Isaiah 56:10; 1 Peter 5:8"> And he cried as a lion: O Lord, I stand continually upon the watchtower in the day-time, and am set in my ward whole nights:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 14:8; Jeremiah 51:8"> and, behold, here cometh a troop of men, horsemen in pairs. And he answered and said, Babylon is fallen, is fallen; and all the graven images of her gods are broken unto the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Micah 4:13; 2 Kings 13:7"> O thou my threshing, and the corn of my floor: that which I have heard from the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, have I declared unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:14; Obadiah 1:1–16; Amos 1:6"> The burden of Dumah. One calleth unto me out of Seir, Watchman, what of the night? Watchman, what of the night?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:30–32; Acts 2:37–38; Ezekiel 18:30–32"> The watchman said, The morning cometh, and also the night: if ye will inquire, inquire ye: turn ye, come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:23–24; 1 Chronicles 1:32; Ezekiel 27:15"> The burden upon Arabia. In the forest in Arabia shall ye lodge, O ye travelling companies of Dedanites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 25:15; Job 6:19; Romans 12:20"> Unto him that was thirsty they brought water; the inhabitants of the land of Tema did meet the fugitives with their bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:19–20; Isaiah 13:14"> For they fled away from the swords, from the drawn sword, and from the bent bow, and from the grievousness of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 120:5; Isaiah 60:7; Isaiah 16:14"> For thus hath the Lord said unto me, Within a year, according to the years of an hireling, and all the glory of Kedar shall fail:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 1:6; Numbers 23:19; Matthew 24:35"> and the residue of the number of the archers, the mighty men of the children of Kedar, shall be few: for the LORD, the God of Israel, hath spoken it.</span>
