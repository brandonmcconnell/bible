<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> <span className="pl pl-1">A slothful man is compared to the filth of a dunghill:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Every man that taketh it up will shake out his hand.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> <span className="pl pl-1">A father hath shame in having begotten an uninstructed son;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And a foolish daughter is born to his loss.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> <span className="pl pl-1">A prudent daughter shall inherit a husband of her own;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And she that bringeth shame is the grief of him that begat her.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> <span className="pl pl-1">She that is bold bringeth shame upon father and husband;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And she shall be despised of them both.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Unseasonable discourse is as music in mourning;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But stripes and correction are wisdom at every season.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that teacheth a fool is as one that glueth a potsherd together;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Even as one that waketh a sleeper out of a deep sleep.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that discourseth to a fool is as one discoursing to a man that slumbereth;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And at the end he will say, What is it?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <br /><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="-10 Verses 9 and 10 are omitted by the best authorities.">a</a></sup><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Seven days are the days of mourning for the dead;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But for a fool and an ungodly man, all the days of his life.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Talk not much with a foolish man,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And go not to one that hath no understanding:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Beware of him, lest thou have trouble;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And so thou shalt not be</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, defiled: in his onslaught turn">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">defiled in his onslaught:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Turn aside from him, and thou shalt find rest;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And so thou shalt not be wearied in his madness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">What shall be heavier than lead?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And what is the name thereof, but a fool?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sand, and salt, and a mass of iron, is easier to bear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Than a man without understanding.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Timber girt and bound into a building shall not be loosed with shaking:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So a heart established in due season on well advised counsel shall not be afraid.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">A heart settled upon a thoughtful understanding</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Is as an ornament of plaister on a polished wall.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pales set on a high place will not stand against the wind:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So a fearful heart in the imagination of a fool will not stand against any fear.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that pricketh the eye will make tears to fall;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And he that pricketh the heart maketh it to shew feeling.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whoso casteth a stone at birds frayeth them away;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And he that upbraideth a friend will dissolve friendship.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="pl pl-1">If thou hast drawn a sword against a friend, despair not;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For there may be a returning.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <span className="pl pl-1">If thou hast opened thy mouth against a friend, fear not;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For there may be a reconciling;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Except it be for upbraiding, and arrogance, and disclosing of a secret, and a treacherous blow:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For these things every friend will flee.</span></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Gain trust with thy neighbour in his poverty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That in his prosperity thou mayest have gladness:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Abide stedfast unto him in the time of his affliction,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That thou mayest be heir with him in his inheritance.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The remainder of this verse is omitted by the best authorities.">c</a></sup><br /></span>

</div>
