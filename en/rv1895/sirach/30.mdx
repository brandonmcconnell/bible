<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that loveth his son will continue to lay stripes upon him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That he may have joy of him in the end.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that chastiseth his son shall have profit of him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And shall glory of him among his acquaintance.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that teacheth his son shall provoke his enemy to jealousy;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And before friends he shall rejoice of him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> <span className="pl pl-1">His father dieth, and is as though he had not died;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For he hath left one behind him like himself.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> <span className="pl pl-1">In his life, he saw and rejoiced in him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And when he died, he sorrowed not:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> <span className="pl pl-1">He left behind him an avenger against his enemies,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And one to requite kindness to his friends.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that maketh too much of his son shall bind up his wounds;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And his heart will be troubled at every cry.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <span className="pl pl-1">An unbroken horse becometh stubborn;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And a son left at large becometh headstrong.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cocker thy child, and he shall make thee afraid:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Play with him, and he will grieve thee.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Laugh not with him, lest thou have sorrow with him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And thou shalt gnash thy teeth in the end.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give him no liberty in his youth,</span><br /><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="These three lines are absent from the oldest MSS.">a</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">And wink not at his follies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="These three lines are absent from the oldest MSS.">b</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">Bow down his neck in his youth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And beat him on the sides while he is a child,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Lest he wax stubborn, and be disobedient unto thee;</span><br /><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="These three lines are absent from the oldest MSS.">c</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">And there shall be sorrow to thy soul.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Chastise thy son, and take pains with him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Lest his shameless behaviour be an offence unto thee.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Better is a poor man, being sound and strong of constitution,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Than a rich man that is plagued in his body.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Health and a good constitution are better than all gold;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And a strong body than wealth without measure.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is no riches better than health of body;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And there is no gladness above the joy of the heart.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Death is better than a bitter life,</span><br /><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The oldest MSS. omit And eternal rest.">d</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">And</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. age-long rest.">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">eternal rest than a continual sickness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Good things poured out upon a mouth that is closed</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Are as messes of meat laid upon a grave.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> <span className="pl pl-1">What doth an offering profit an idol?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For neither shall it eat nor smell:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So is he that is afflicted of the Lord,</span><br /></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give not over thy soul to sorrow;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And afflict not thyself in thine own counsel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Gladness of heart is the life of a man;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the joyfulness of a man is length of days.</span><br /></span>
