<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Esdras rising from the court of the temple went to the chamber of Jonas the son of Eliasib,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and lodged there, and did eat no bread nor drink water, mourning for the great iniquities of the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And there was made proclamation in all Jewry and Jerusalem to all them that were of the captivity, that they should be gathered together at Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and that whosoever met not there within two or three days, according as the elders that bare rule appointed, their cattle should be seized to the use of the temple, and himself cast out from the multitude of them that were of the captivity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And in three days were all they of the tribe of Judah and Benjamin gathered together at Jerusalem: this was the ninth month, on the twentieth day of the month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And all the multitude sat together trembling in the broad place before the temple because of the present foul weather.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> So Esdras arose up and said unto them, ye have transgressed the law and married strange wives, thereby to increase the sins of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And now make confession and give glory unto the Lord, the God of our fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and do his will, and separate yourselves from the heathen of the land, and from the strange women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then cried the whole multitude, and said with a loud voice, Like as thou hast spoken, so will we do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> But forasmuch as the multitude is great, and it is foul weather, so that we cannot stand without, and this is not a work of one day or two, seeing our sin in these things is spread far:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> therefore let the rulers of the multitude stay, and let all them of our habitations that have strange wives come at the time appointed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and with them the rulers and judges of every place, till we turn away the wrath of the Lord from us for this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Then Jonathan the son of Azael and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Another reading is, Ezias.">a</a></sup>Ezekias the son of Thocanus accordingly took the matter upon them: and Mosollamus and Levis and Sabbateus were assessors to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And they that were of the captivity did according to all these things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Esdras the priest chose unto him principal men of their families, all by name: and on the new moon of the tenth month they were shut in together to examine the matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> So their cause that held strange wives was brought to an end by the new moon of the first month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And of the priests that were come together, and had strange wives, there were found;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> of the sons of Jesus the son of Josedek, and his brethren;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Maaseiah.">b</a></sup>Mathelas, and Eleazar, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Jarib.">c</a></sup>Joribus, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gedaliah">d</a></sup>Joadanus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And they gave their hands to put away their wives, and to offer rams to make reconcilement for their error.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And of the sons of Emmer; Ananias, and Zabdeus, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Harim">e</a></sup>Manes, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Maaseiah.">f</a></sup>Sameus, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Jehiel.">g</a></sup>Hiereel, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Uzziah.">h</a></sup>Azarias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And of the sons of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Pashhur.">i</a></sup>Phaisur; Elionas, Massias, Ismael, and Nathanael, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Jozabad.">j</a></sup>Ocidelus, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Elasah.">k</a></sup>Saloas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And of the Levites; Jozabdus, and Semeis, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Kelaiah.">l</a></sup>Colius, who was called<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Kelita.">m</a></sup>Calitas, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Pethahiah.">n</a></sup>Patheus, and Judas, and Jonas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Of the holy singers;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Eliashib.">o</a></sup>Eliasibus, Bacchurus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Of the porters: Sallumus, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Telem.">p</a></sup>Tolbanes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Of Israel, of the sons of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Parosh.">q</a></sup>Phoros;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Ramiah">r</a></sup>Hiermas, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Izziah. Another reading is, Iezias">s</a></sup>Ieddias, and Melchias, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Mijamin.">t</a></sup>Maelus, and Eleazar, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Malchijah.">u</a></sup>Asibas, and Banneas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Of the sons of Ela; Matthanias, Zacharias, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Jehiel.">v</a></sup>Jezrielus, and Oabdius, and Hieremoth, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Abdi.">w</a></sup>Aedias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And of the sons of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Zattu.">x</a></sup>Zamoth;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Elioenai.">y</a></sup>Eliadas,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Eliashib.">z</a></sup>Eliasimus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Mattaniah.">aa</a></sup>Othonias, Jarimoth, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Zabad.">ab</a></sup>Sabathus, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Aziza.">ac</a></sup>Zardeus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Of the sons of Bebai; Joannes, and Ananias, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Zabbai.">ad</a></sup>Jozabdus, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Athlai.">ae</a></sup>Ematheis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Of the sons of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Bani.">af</a></sup>Mani;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Meshullam.">ag</a></sup>Olamus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Malluch.">ah</a></sup>Mamuchus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Adaiah.">ai</a></sup>Jedeus, Jasubas, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Sheal.">aj</a></sup>Jasaelus, and Hieremoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And of the sons of Addi; Naathus, and Moossias, Laccunus, and Naidus, and Matthanias, and Sesthel, Balnuus, and Manasseas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And of the sons of Annas; Elionas, and Aseas, and Melchias, and Sabbeus, and Simon Chosameus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And of the sons of Asom;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Mattenai.">ak</a></sup>Maltanneus, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Mattattah.">al</a></sup>Mattathias, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Zabad.">am</a></sup>Sabanneus, Eliphalat, and Manasses, and Semei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And of the sons of Baani; Jeremias, Momdis, Ismaerus, Juel, Mamdai, and Pedias, and Anos, Carabasion, and Enasibus, and Mamnitamenus, Eliasis, Bannus, Eliali, Someis, Selemias, Nathanias: and of the sons of Ezora; Sesis, Ezril, Azaelus, Samatus, Zambri, Josephus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And of the sons of Nooma; Mazitias, Zabadeas, Edos, Juel, Banaias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> All these had taken strange wives, and they put them away with their children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And the priests and Levites, and they that were of Israel, dwelt in Jerusalem, and in the country, on the new moon of the seventh month, and the children of Israel in their habitations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And the whole multitude were gathered together with one accord into the broad place before the porch of the temple toward the east:</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> and they said unto Esdras the priest and reader, Bring the law of Moses, that was given of the Lord, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> So Esdras the chief priest brought the law unto the whole multitude both of men and women, and to all the priests, to hear the law on the new moon of the seventh month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And he read in the broad place before the porch of the temple from morning unto mid-day, before both men and women; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Another reading is, they gave all heed.">an</a></sup>all the multitude gave heed unto the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And Esdras the priest and reader of the law stood up upon the pulpit of wood, which was made for that purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And there stood up by him Mattathias, Sammus, Ananias, Azarias, Urias,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Hilkiah.">ao</a></sup>Ezekias, Baalsamus, upon the right hand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and upon his left hand,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Pedaiah.">ap</a></sup>Phaldeus, Misael, Melchias,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Hashuin.">aq</a></sup>Lothasubus, Nabarias, Zacharias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> Then took Esdras the book of the law before the multitude, and sat honourably in the first place before all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And when he opened the law, they stood all straight up. So Esdras blessed the Lord God Most High, the God of hosts, Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> And all the people answered, Amen; and lifting up their hands they fell to the ground, and worshipped the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> Also Jesus, Annus, Sarabias, Iadinus, Jacubus, Sabateus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Hodiah.">ar</a></sup>Auteas, Maiannas, and Calitas, Azarias, and Jozabdus, and Ananias, Phalias, the Levites, taught the law of the Lord,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Some authorities omit and read...Lord.">as</a></sup>and read to the multitude the law of the Lord, making them withal to understand it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> Then said Attharates unto Esdras the chief priest and reader, and to the Levites that taught the multitude, even to all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> This day is holy unto the Lord; (now they all wept when they heard the law:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> go then, and eat the fat, and drink the sweet, and send portions to them that have nothing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> for the day is holy unto the Lord: and be not sorrowful; for the Lord will bring you to honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> So the Levites published all things to the people, saying, This day is holy; be not sorrowful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> Then went they their way, every one to eat and drink, and make merry, and to give portions to them that had nothing, and to make great cheer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> because they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Or, were inspired by">at</a></sup>understood the words wherein they were instructed, and for the which they had been assembled.</span>
</div>
