<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Now in the second year of the reign of Darius, Aggaeus and Zacharius the son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Iddo. Another reading is, Eddin">a</a></sup>Addo, the prophets, prophesied unto the Jews in Jewry and Jerusalem; in the name of the Lord, the God of Israel, prophesied they unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Then stood up Zorobabel the son of Salathiel, and Jesus the son of Josedek, and began to build the house of the Lord at Jerusalem, the prophets of the Lord being with them, and helping them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> At the same time came unto them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Tattenai.">b</a></sup>Sisinnes the governor of Syria and Phoenicia, with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Shetharbozenai">c</a></sup>Sathrabuzanes and his companions, and said unto them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> By whose appointment do ye build this house and this roof, and perform all the other things? and who are the builders that perform these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Nevertheless the elders of the Jews obtained favour, because the Lord had visited the captivity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and they were not hindered from building, until such time as communication was made unto Darius concerning them, and his answer signified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The copy of the letter which Sisinnes, governor of Syria and Phoenicia, and Sathrabuzanes, with their companions, the rulers in Syria and Phoenicia, wrote and sent unto Darius;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> To king Darius, greeting: Let all things be known unto our lord the king, that being come into the country of Judaea, and entered into the city of Jerusalem, we found in the city of Jerusalem the elders of the Jews that were of the captivity</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> building a house unto the Lord, great and new, of hewn and costly stones, with timber laid in the walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And those works are done with great speed, and the work goeth on prosperously in their hands, and with all glory and diligence it is accomplished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then asked we these elders, saying, By whose commandment build ye this house, and lay the foundations of these works?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Therefore, to the intent that we might give knowledge unto thee by writing who were the chief doers, we questioned them, and we required of them the names in writing of their principal men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> So they gave us this answer, We are the servants of the Lord which made heaven and earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And as for this house, it was builded many years ago by a king of Israel great and strong, and was finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> But when our fathers sinned against the Lord of Israel which is in heaven, and provoked him unto wrath, he gave them over into the hands of Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon, king of the Chaldeans;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and they pulled down the house, and burned it, and carried away the people captives unto Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But in the first year that Cyrus reigned over the country of Babylon, king Cyrus wrote to build up this house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the holy vessels of gold and of silver, that Nabuchodonosor had carried away out of the house at Jerusalem, and had set up in his own temple, those Cyrus the king brought forth again out of the temple in Babylonia, and they were delivered to Zorobabel and to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Another reading is, Sabunassarus.">d</a></sup>Sanabassarus the governor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> with commandment that he should carry away<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Another reading is, the same.">e</a></sup>all these vessels, and put them in the temple at Jerusalem; and that the temple of the Lord should be built in its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then Sanabassarus, being come hither, laid the foundations of the house of the Lord which is in Jerusalem; and from that time to this being still a building, it is not yet fully ended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Now therefore, if it seem good, O king, let search be made among the royal archives of our lord the king that are in Babylon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and if it be found that the building of the house of the Lord which is in Jerusalem hath been done with the consent of king Cyrus, and it seem good unto our lord the king, let him signify unto us thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Then commanded King Darius to seek among the archives that were laid up at Babylon: and so at Ekbatana the palace, which is in the country of Media, there was found a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Another reading is, place.">f</a></sup>roll wherein these things were recorded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> In the first year of the reign of Cyrus king Cyrus commanded to build up the house of the Lord which is in Jerusalem, where they do sacrifice with continual fire:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> whose height shall be sixty cubits, and the breadth sixty cubits, with three rows of hewn stones, and one row of new wood of that country; and the expenses therof to be given out of the house of king Cyrus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and that the holy vessels of the house of the Lord, both gold and silver, that Nabuchodonosor took out of the house at Jerusalem, and carried away to Babylon, should be restored to the house at Jerusalem, and be set in the place where they were before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And also he commanded that Sisinnes the governor of Syria and Phoenicia, and Sathrabuzanes, and their companions, and those which were appointed rulers in Syria and Phoenicia, should be careful not to meddle with the place, but suffer Zorobabel, the servant of the Lord, and governor of Judaea, and the elders of the Jews, to build that house of the Lord in its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And I also do command to have it built up whole again; and that they look diligently to help those that be of the captivity of Judaea, till the house of the Lord be finished:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> and that out of the tribute of Coelesyria and Phoenicia a portion be carefully given these men for the sacrifices of the Lord, that is, to Zorobabel the governor, for bullocks, and rams, and lambs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and also corn, salt, wine and oil, and that continually every year without further question, according as the priests that be in Jerusalem shall signify to be daily spent:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> that drink offerings may be made to the Most High God for the king and for his children, and that they may pray for their lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And that commandment be given that whosoever shall transgress, yea, or neglect anything<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="ft Another reading is, afore spoken or written.">g</a></sup>herein written, out of his own house shall a tree be taken, and he thereon be hanged, and all his goods seized for the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> The Lord therefore, whose name is there called upon, utterly destroy every king and nation, that shall stretch out his hand to hinder or endamage that house of the Lord in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> I Darius the king have ordained that according unto these things it be done with diligence.</span>
</div>
