<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the second, that had spoken of the strength of the king, began to say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> O sirs, do not men excel in strength, that bear rule over the sea and land, and all things in them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> But yet is the king stronger: and he is their lord, and hath dominion over them; and in whatsoever he commandeth them they obey him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> If he bid them make war the one against the other, they do it: and if he send them out against the enemies, they go, and overcome mountains, walls, and towers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> They slay and are slain, and transgress not the king’s commandment: if they get the victory, they bring all to the king, as well the spoil, as all things else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Likewise for those that are no soldiers, and have not to do with wars, but use husbandry, when they have reaped again that which they had sown, they bring it to the king, and compel one another to pay tribute unto the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And he is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, one and alone">a</a></sup>but one man: if he command to kill, they kill; if he command to spare, they spare;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> if he command to smite, they smite; if he command to make desolate, they make desolate; if he command to build, they build;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> if he command to cut down, they cut down; if he command to plant, they plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So all his people and his armies obey him: furthermore he lieth down, he eateth and drinketh, and taketh his rest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and these keep watch round about him, neither may any one depart, and do his own business, neither disobey they him in anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> O sirs, how should not the king be strongest, seeing that in such sort he is obeyed? And he held his peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then the third, who had spoken of women, and of truth, (this was Zorobabel) began to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> O sirs, is not the king great, and men are many, and wine is strong? who is it then that ruleth them, or hath the lordship over them? are they not women?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Women have borne the king and all the people that bear rule by sea and land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Even of them came they: and they nourished them up that planted the vineyards, from whence the wine cometh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> These also make garments for men; these bring glory unto men; and without women cannot men be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Yea, and if men have gathered together gold and silver and any other goodly thing,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Another reading is, do they not love &amp;c.">b</a></sup>and see a woman which is comely in favour and beauty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> they let all those things go, and gape after her, and even with open mouth fix their eyes fast on her; and have all more desire unto her than unto gold or silver, or any goodly thing whatsoever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> A man leaveth his own father that brought him up, and his own country, and cleaveth unto his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And with his wife he endeth his days, and remembereth neither father, nor mother, nor country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> By this also ye must know that women have dominion over you: do ye not labor and toil, and give and bring all to women?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Yea, a man taketh his sword, and goeth forth to make outroads, and to rob and to steal, and to sail upon the sea and upon rivers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and looketh upon a lion, and walketh in the darkness; and when he hath stolen, spoiled, and robbed, he bringeth it to his love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Wherefore a man loveth his wife better than father or mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Yea, many there be that have run out of their wits for women, and become bondmen for their sakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Many also have perished, have stumbled, and sinned, for women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And now do ye not believe me? is not the king great in his power? do not all regions fear to touch him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Yet did I see him and Apame the king’s concubine, the daughter of the illustrious Barticus, sitting at the right hand of the king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and taking the crown from the king’s head, and setting it upon her own head; yea, she struck the king with her left hand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> and therewithal the king gaped and gazed upon her with open mouth: if she laughed upon him, he laughed also: but if she took any displeasure at him, he was fain to flatter, that she might be reconciled to him again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> O sirs, how can it be but women should be strong, seeing they do thus?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Then the king and the nobles looked one upon another: so he began to speak concerning truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> O sirs, are not women strong? great is the earth, high is the heaven, swift is the sun in his course, for he compasseth the heavens round about, and fetcheth his course again to his own place in one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Is he not great that maketh these things? therefore great is truth, and stronger than all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> All the earth calleth upon truth, and the heaven blesseth her: all works shake and tremble, but with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Another reading is, him.">c</a></sup>her is no unrighteous thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Wine is unrighteous, the king is unrighteous, women are unrighteous, all the children of men are unrighteous, and unrighteous are all such their works; and there is no truth in them; in their unrighteousness also they shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> But truth abideth, and is strong forever; she liveth and conquereth forevermore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> With her there is no accepting of persons or rewards; but she doeth the things that are just, and refraineth from all unrighteous and wicked things; and all men do well like of her works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Neither in her judgment is any unrighteousness; and she is the strength, and the kingdom, and the power, and the majesty, of all ages. Blessed be the God of truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And with that he held his tongue. And all the people then shouted, and said, Great is truth, and strong above all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Then said the king unto him, Ask what thou wilt more than is appointed in writing, and we will give it thee, inasmuch as thou art found wisest; and thou shalt sit next me, and shalt be called my cousin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Then said he unto the king, Remember thy vow, which thou didst vow to build Jerusalem, in the day when thou camest to thy kingdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and to send away all the vessels that were taken out of Jerusalem, which Cyrus set apart, when he vowed to destroy Babylon, and vowed to send them again thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> Thou didst also vow to build up the temple, which the Edomites burned when Judaea was made desolate by the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And now, O lord the king, this is that which I require, and which I desire of thee, and this is the princely liberality that shall proceed from thee: I pray therefore that thou make good the vow, the performance whereof thou hast vowed to the King of heaven with thine own mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> Then Darius the king stood up, and kissed him, and wrote letters for him unto all the treasurers and governors and captains and satraps, that they should safely bring on their way both him, and all those that should go up with him to build Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> He wrote letters also unto all the governors that were in Coelesyria and Phoenicia, and unto them in Libanus, that they should bring cedar wood from Libanus unto Jerusalem, and that they should build the city with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> Moreover he wrote for all the Jews that should go out of his realm up into Jewry, concerning their freedom, that no officer, no governor, no satrap, nor treasurer, should forcibly enter into their doors;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> and that all the country which they occupied should be free to them without tribute; and that the Edomites should give over the villages of the Jews which then they held:</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> and that there should be yearly given twenty talents to the building of the temple, until the time that it were built;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> and other ten talents yearly, for burnt offerings to be presented upon the altar every day, as they had a commandment to offer seventeen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> and that all they that should come from Babylonia to build the city should have their freedom, as well they as their posterity, and all the priests that came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> He wrote also to give them their charges, and the priests’ vestments wherein they minister;</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> and for the Levites he wrote that their charges should be given them until the day that the house were finished, and Jerusalem builded up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And he commanded to give to all that kept the city lands and wages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> He sent away also all the vessels from Babylon, that Cyrus had set apart; and all that Cyrus had given in commandment, the same charged he also to be done, and sent unto Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> Now when this young man was gone forth, he lifted up his face to heaven toward Jerusalem, and praised the King of heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> and said, From thee cometh victory, from thee cometh wisdom, and thine is the glory, and I am thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> Blessed art thou, who hast given me wisdom: and to thee I give thanks, O Lord of our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> And so he took the letters, and went out, and came unto Babylon, and told it all his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> And they praised the God of their fathers, because he had given them freedom and liberty</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> to go up, and to build Jerusalem, and the temple which is called by his name: and they feasted with instruments of music and gladness seven days.</span>
</div>
