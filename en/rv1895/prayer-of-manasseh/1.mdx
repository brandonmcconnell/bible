<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> O LORD Almighty, that art in heaven, thou God of our fathers, of Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob, and of their righteous seed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> who hast made heaven and earth, with all the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, order Or, array Gen. ii. 1 (Sept.).">a</a></sup>ornament thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> who hast bound the sea by the word of thy commandment; who hast shut up the deep, and sealed it by thy terrible and glorious name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> whom all things fear, yea, tremble before thy power;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> for the majesty of thy glory cannot be borne, and the anger of thy threatening toward sinners is importable:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> thy merciful promise is unmeasurable and unsearchable;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> for thou art the Lord Most High, of great compassion, longsuffering and abundant in mercy, and repentest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. of the evils of men.">b</a></sup>of bringing evils upon men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Alex. MS. omits Thou... saved.">c</a></sup>Thou, O Lord, according to thy great goodness hast promised repentance and forgiveness to them that have sinned against thee: and of thine infinite mercies hast appointed repentance unto sinners, that they may be saved. Thou therefore, O Lord, that art the God of the just, hast not appointed repentance to the just, to Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob, which have not sinned against thee; but thou hast appointed repentance unto me that am a sinner:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> for I have sinned above the number of the sands of the sea. My transgressions are multiplied,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The Alex. MS. omits O Lord... multiplied.">d</a></sup>O Lord: my transgressions are multiplied, and I am not worthy to behold and see the height of heaven for the multitude of mine iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> I am bowed down with many iron bands, that I cannot lift up mine head<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some authorities omit by reason of my sins.">e</a></sup>by reason of my sins, neither have I any respite: for I have provoked thy wrath, and done that which is evil before thee:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The Alex. MS. omits I did... commandments.">f</a></sup>I did not thy will, neither kept I thy commandments: I have set up abominations, and have multiplied<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or, stumbling-blocks">g</a></sup>detestable things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Now therefore I bow the knee of mine heart, beseeching thee of grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> I have sinned, O Lord, I have sinned, and I acknowledge mine iniquities:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> but, I humbly beseech thee, forgive me, O Lord, forgive me, and destroy me not with mine iniquities. Be not angry with me for ever, by reserving evil for me; neither condemn me into the lower parts of the earth. For thou,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Another reading is, O God.">h</a></sup>O Lord, art the God of them that repent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and in me thou wilt shew all thy goodness: for thou wilt save me, that am unworthy, according to thy great mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And I will praise thee for ever all the days of my life: for all the host of heaven doth sing thy praise, and thine is the glory for ever and ever. Amen.</span>
