<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And in those days Judith heard thereof, the daughter of Merari, the son of Ox, the son of Joseph, the son of Oziel, the son of Elkiah, the son of Ananias, the son of Gideon, the son of Raphaim, the son of Ahitub, the son of Elihu, the son of Eliab, the son of Nathanael, the son of Salamiel, the son of Salasadai, the son of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And her husband was Manasses, of her tribe and of her family, and he died in the days of barley harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For he stood over them that bound sheaves in the field, and the heat came upon his head, and he fell on his bed, and died in his city Bethulia: and they buried him with his fathers in the field which is between Dothaim and Balamon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And Judith was a widow in her house three years and four months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And she made her a tent upon the roof of her house, and put on sackcloth upon her loins; and the garments of her widowhood were upon her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And she fasted all the days of her widowhood, save the eves of the sabbaths, and the sabbaths, and the eves of the new moons, and the new moons, and the feasts and joyful days of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And she was of a goodly countenance, and exceeding beautiful to behold: and her husband Manasses had left her gold, and silver, and menservants, and maidservants, and cattle, and lands; and she remained upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And there was none that gave her an evil word; for she feared God exceedingly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And she heard the evil words of the people against the governor, because they fainted for lack of water; and Judith heard all the words that Ozias spake unto them, how he sware to them that he would deliver the city unto the Assyrians after five days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And she sent her maid, that was over all things that she had, to call Ozias and Chabris and Charmis, the elders of her city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And they came unto her, and she said unto them,<br />Hear me now, O ye rulers of the inhabitants of Bethulia: for your word that ye have spoken before the people this day is not right, and ye have set the oath which ye have pronounced between God and you, and have promised to deliver the city to our enemies, unless within these days the Lord turn to help you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And now who are ye that have tempted God this day, and stand instead of God among the children of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And now try the Lord Almighty, and ye shall never know anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For ye shall not find the depth of the heart of man, and ye shall not perceive the things that he thinketh: and how shall ye search out God, which hath made all these things, and know his mind, and comprehend his purpose? Nay, my brethren, provoke not the Lord our God to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For if he be not minded to help us within these five days, he hath power to defend us in such time as he will, or to destroy us before the face of our enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> But do not ye pledge the counsels of the Lord our God: for God is not as man, that he should be threatened; neither as the son of man, that he should be turned by intreaty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Wherefore let us wait for the salvation that cometh from him, and call upon him to help us, and he will hear our voice, if it please him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For there arose none in our age, neither is there any of us to-day, tribe, or kindred, or family, or city, which worship gods made with hands, as it was in the former days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> for the which cause our fathers were given to the sword, and for a spoil, and fell with a great fall before our enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But we know none other god beside him, wherefore we hope that he will not despise us, nor any of our race.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For if we be taken so, all Judaea shall sit upon the ground, and our sanctuary shall be spoiled; and of our blood shall he require the profanation thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the slaughter of our brethren, and the captivity of the land, and the desolation of our inheritance, shall he turn upon our heads among the Gentiles, wheresoever we shall be in bondage; and we shall be an offence and a reproach before them that take us for a possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For our bondage shall not be ordered to favour: but the Lord our God shall turn it to dishonour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And now, brethren, let us shew an example to our brethren, because their soul hangeth upon us, and the sanctuary and the house and the altar rest upon us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Besides all this let us give thanks to the Lord our God, which trieth us, even as he did our fathers also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Remember all the things which he did to Abraham, and all the things in which he tried Isaac, and all the things which happened to Jacob in Mesopotamia of Syria, when he kept the sheep of Laban his mother’s brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> For he hath not tried us in the fire, as he did them, to search out their hearts, neither hath he taken vengeance on us; but the Lord doth scourge them that come near unto him, to admonish them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Ozias said to her, All that thou hast spoken hast thou spoken with a good heart, and there is none that shall gainsay thy words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> For this is not the first day wherein thy wisdom is manifested; but from the beginning of thy days all the people have known thine understanding, because the disposition of thy heart is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> But the people were exceeding thirsty, and compelled us to do as we spake to them, and to bring an oath upon ourselves, which we will not break.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And now pray thou for us, because thou art a godly woman, and the Lord shall send us rain to fill our cisterns, and we shall faint no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And Judith said unto them, Hear me, and I will do a thing, which shall go down to all generations among the children of our race.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Ye shall stand at the gate this night, and I will go forth with my maid: and, within the days after which ye said that ye would deliver the city to our enemies, the Lord shall visit Israel by my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> But ye shall not inquire of mine act: for I will not declare it unto you, till the things be finished that I do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And Ozias and the rulers said unto her, Go in peace, and the Lord God be before thee, to take vengeance on our enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And they returned from the tent, and went to their stations.</span>
</div>
