<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; 1 Chronicles 22:9; 1 Chronicles 28:5"> The proverbs of Solomon the son of David, king of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 7:4; Deuteronomy 4:5–6; 2 Timothy 3:15–17"> To know wisdom and instruction; to discern the words of understanding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:1–9; Job 22:22; Proverbs 8:10–11"> To receive instruction in wise dealing, in righteousness and judgment and equity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:5; Proverbs 8:17; Psalms 119:9"> To give subtilty to the simple, to the young man knowledge and discretion:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 9:9; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> That the wise man may hear, and increase in learning; and that the man of understanding may attain unto sound counsels:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Mark 4:11; Psalms 49:4"> To understand a proverb, and a figure; the words of the wise, and their dark sayings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 28:28; Psalms 111:10–1; Ecclesiastes 12:13"> The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge: but the foolish despise wisdom and instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:20; Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 5:1–2"> My son, hear the instruction of thy father, and forsake not the law of thy mother:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Proverbs 4:9; Genesis 41:42"> For they shall be a chaplet of grace unto thy head, and chains about thy neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Proverbs 16:29; Psalms 1:1"> My son, if sinners entice thee, consent thou not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26; Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 12:6"> If they say, Come with us, let us lay wait for blood, let us lurk privily for the innocent without cause;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 124:3; Psalms 28:1; Romans 3:13"> Let us swallow them up alive as Sheol, and whole, as those that go down into the pit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 12:15; Job 24:2–3"> We shall find all precious substance, we shall fill our houses with spoil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Thou shalt cast thy lot among us; we will all have one purse:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:101; Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 4:14–15"> My son, walk not thou in the way with them; refrain thy foot from their path:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 6:18; Proverbs 4:16"> For their feet run to evil, and they make haste to shed blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Proverbs 7:23; Jeremiah 8:7"> For in vain is the net spread, in the eyes of any bird:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 9:16; Esther 7:10; Proverbs 5:22–23"> And these lay wait for their own blood, they lurk privily for their own lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 15:27; 2 Peter 2:3; James 5:1–4"> So are the ways of every one that is greedy of gain; it taketh away the life of the owners thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 9:3; Colossians 2:3; Luke 11:49"> Wisdom crieth aloud in the street; she uttereth her voice in the broad places;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 5:20; Proverbs 9:3; Matthew 13:2"> She crieth in the chief place of concourse; at the entering in of the gates, in the city, she uttereth her words:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 1:29; Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 5:12"> How long, ye simple ones, will ye love simplicity? and scorners delight them in scorning, and fools hate knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Ezekiel 33:11; Ezekiel 18:27–30"> Turn you at my reproof: behold, I will pour out my spirit unto you, I will make known my words unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; Romans 10:21; Jeremiah 7:13"> Because I have called, and ye refused; I have stretched out my hand, and no man regarded;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 81:11; Luke 7:30; Proverbs 1:30"> But ye have set at nought all my counsel, and would none of my reproof:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Proverbs 10:24; Psalms 37:13"> I also will laugh in the day of your calamity; I will mock when your fear cometh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 3:25–26; Luke 21:34–35; Psalms 58:9"> When your fear cometh as a storm, and your calamity cometh on as a whirlwind; when distress and anguish come upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Micah 3:4; James 4:3; Jeremiah 11:11"> Then shall they call upon me, but I will not answer; they shall seek me diligently, but they shall not find me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Job 21:14–15; Proverbs 6:23"> For that they hated knowledge, and did not choose the fear of the LORD:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 81:11; Proverbs 1:25; Luke 14:18–20"> They would none of my counsel; they despised all my reproof:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 14:14; Jeremiah 6:19; Proverbs 22:8"> Therefore shall they eat of the fruit of their own way, and be filled with their own devices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 8:36; Jeremiah 2:19; John 3:36"> For the backsliding of the simple shall slay them, and the prosperity of fools shall destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 25:12–13; Psalms 112:7–8; Isaiah 26:3"> But whoso hearkeneth unto me shall dwell securely, and shall be quiet without fear of evil.</span>
