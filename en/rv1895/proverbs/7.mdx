<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 2:16; Proverbs 6:24; Proverbs 5:3"> That they may keep thee from the strange woman, from the stranger which flattereth with her words.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 6:32; Proverbs 1:22; Proverbs 1:32"> And I beheld among the simple ones, I discerned among the youths, a young man void of understanding,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 5:8; Proverbs 7:12; Judges 16:1"> Passing through the street near her corner, and he went the way to her house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 24:13–15; Romans 13:12–14; Ephesians 5:11"> In the twilight, in the evening of the day, in the blackness of night and the darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9; Isaiah 3:16–24; 2 Corinthians 11:2–3"> And, behold, there met him a woman with the attire of an harlot, and wily of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 9:13; Titus 2:5; Proverbs 31:10–31"> She is clamorous, and wilful; her feet abide not in her house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 23:28; Proverbs 9:14; Revelation 18:3"> Now she is in the streets, now in the broad places, and lieth in wait at every corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 39:12; Numbers 25:6–8; Genesis 39:7"> So she caught him, and kissed him, and with an impudent face she said unto him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 21:27; Leviticus 7:11; Proverbs 17:1"> Sacrifices of peace offerings are with me; this day have I paid my vows.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 19:9; Ezekiel 27:7; 1 Kings 10:28"> I have spread my couch with carpets of tapestry, with striped cloths of the yarn of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 45:8; Isaiah 57:7–9; Song of Songs 4:13–14"> I have perfumed my bed with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Come, let us take our fill of love until the morning; let us solace ourselves with loves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 20:11; Luke 12:45–46; Luke 12:39"> For the goodman is not at home, he is gone a long journey:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4"> He hath taken a bag of money with him; he will come home at the full moon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 5:3; Proverbs 7:5; 2 Kings 4:8"> With her much fair speech she causeth him to yield, with the flattering of her lips she forceth him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 13:27; Acts 16:24; Acts 14:13"> He goeth after her straightway, as an ox goeth to the slaughter, or as fetters to the correction of the fool;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:12; Proverbs 9:18; Proverbs 1:17"> Till an arrow strike through his liver; as a bird hasteth to the snare, and knoweth not that it is for his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 5:7; Proverbs 4:1; 1 John 2:1"> Now therefore, my sons, hearken unto me, and attend to the words of my mouth.</span>
