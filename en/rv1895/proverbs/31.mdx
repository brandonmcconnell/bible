<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Leviticus 19:15; Amos 5:11–12"> Open thy mouth, judge righteously, and minister judgment to the poor and needy.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 3:1–7; 2 Kings 4:22–23; 2 Kings 4:9–10"> The heart of her husband trusteth in her, and he shall have no lack of gain.</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Timothy 5:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:10–12"> She seeketh wool and flax, and worketh willingly with her hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 31:24; 1 Kings 9:26–28; Ezekiel 27:3–36"> She is like the merchant-ships; she bringeth her food from afar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:11; Mark 1:35; Luke 12:42"> She riseth also while it is yet night, and giveth meat to her household, and their task to her maidens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Song of Songs 8:12; Joshua 15:18"> She considereth a field, and buyeth it: with the fruit of her hands she planteth a vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 38:3; 1 Peter 1:13; Ephesians 6:10"> She girdeth her loins with strength, and maketh strong her arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; Matthew 25:3–10; Psalms 127:2"> She perceiveth that her merchandise is profitable: her lamp goeth not out by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:25–26"> She layeth her hands to the distaff, and her hands hold the spindle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 4:28; Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 22:9"> She spreadeth out her hand to the poor; yea, she reacheth forth her hands to the needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:22; 2 Samuel 1:24; Proverbs 25:20"> She is not afraid of the snow for her household; for all her household are clothed with scarlet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Proverbs 7:16; Genesis 41:42"> She maketh for herself carpets of tapestry; her clothing is fine linen and purple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 12:4; Ruth 4:1; Deuteronomy 16:18"> Her husband is known in the gates, when he sitteth among the elders of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:16; Luke 16:19; Proverbs 31:19"> She maketh linen garments and selleth them; and delivereth girdles unto the merchant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 40:10; Romans 13:14; Job 29:14"> Strength and dignity are her clothing; and she laugheth at the time to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 10:31"> She openeth her mouth with wisdom; and the law of kindness is on her tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; Titus 2:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> She looketh well to the ways of her household, and eateth not the bread of idleness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:15–17; 2 Timothy 1:5; 1 Kings 2:19"> Her children rise up, and call her blessed; her husband also, and he praiseth her, saying:</span>
