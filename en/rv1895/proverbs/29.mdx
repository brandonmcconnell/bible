<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 6:15; 1 Samuel 2:25; Proverbs 1:24–31"> He that being often reproved hardeneth his neck shall suddenly be broken, and that without remedy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 28:12; Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 28:28"> When the righteous are increased, the people rejoice: but when a wicked man beareth rule, the people sigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:11; Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 15:20"> Whoso loveth wisdom rejoiceth his father: but he that keepeth company with harlots wasteth his substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 29:14; Proverbs 20:8; Daniel 11:20"> The king by judgment establisheth the land: but he that exacteth gifts overthroweth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 5:1; Job 17:5; Psalms 5:9"> A man that flattereth his neighbour spreadeth a net for his steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:12; Exodus 15:1; 2 Timothy 2:26"> In the transgression of an evil man there is a snare: but the righteous doth sing and rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 29:16; Psalms 41:1; Proverbs 21:13"> The righteous taketh knowledge of the cause of the poor: the wicked hath not understanding to know it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 11:11; Jeremiah 15:1; James 5:15–18"> Scornful men set a city in a flame: but wise men turn away wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 26:4; Ecclesiastes 10:13; Matthew 7:6"> If a wise man hath a controversy with a foolish man, whether he be angry or laugh, there will be no rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:18–19; 1 John 3:12–13; Genesis 4:5–8"> The bloodthirsty hate him that is perfect: and as for the upright, they seek his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 19:11; Proverbs 12:16; Micah 7:5"> A fool uttereth all his anger: but a wise man keepeth it back and stilleth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 20:8; 2 Kings 10:6–7; Psalms 52:2–4"> If a ruler hearkeneth to falsehood, all his servants are wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:45; Proverbs 22:2; Psalms 13:3"> The poor man and the oppressor meet together: the LORD lighteneth the eyes of them both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 16:12; Isaiah 11:4; Psalms 72:2–4"> The king that faithfully judgeth the poor, his throne shall be established for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 17:25; Proverbs 29:17"> The rod and reproof give wisdom: but a child left to himself causeth shame to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:36; Psalms 91:8; Psalms 58:10"> When the wicked are increased, transgression increaseth: but the righteous shall look upon their fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 23:13–14"> Correct thy son, and he shall give thee rest; yea, he shall give delight unto thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 1:25; Luke 11:28; John 13:17"> Where there is no vision, the people cast off restraint: but he that keepeth the law, happy is he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 26:3; Job 19:16; Proverbs 30:22"> A servant will not be corrected by words: for though he understand he will not give heed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 26:12; Proverbs 14:29"> Seest thou a man that is hasty in his words? there is more hope of a fool than of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He that delicately bringeth up his servant from a child shall have him become a son at the last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Proverbs 17:19; Proverbs 26:21"> An angry man stirreth up strife, and a wrathful man aboundeth in transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 23:12; Luke 14:11; Proverbs 11:2"> A man’s pride shall bring him low: but he that is of a lowly spirit shall obtain honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Proverbs 1:11–19; Proverbs 8:36"> Whoso is partner with a thief hateth his own soul: he heareth the adjuration and uttereth nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 118:8; Luke 12:4; Proverbs 18:10"> The fear of man bringeth a snare: but whoso putteth his trust in the LORD shall be safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 19:6; Proverbs 19:21; Psalms 20:9"> Many seek the ruler’s favour: but a man’s judgment cometh from the LORD.</span>
