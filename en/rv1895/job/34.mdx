<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Moreover Elihu answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 1:5; 1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> Hear my words, ye wise men; and give ear unto me, ye that have knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 12:11; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Job 33:2"> For the ear trieth words, as the palate tasteth meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Judges 19:30; Romans 12:2"> Let us choose for us that which is right: let us know among ourselves what is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 27:2; Job 33:9; Job 16:17"> For Job hath said, I am righteous, and God hath taken away my right:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 6:4; Job 27:4–6; Job 16:13"> Notwithstanding my right I am accounted a liar; my wound is incurable, though I am without transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 15:16; Deuteronomy 29:19; Proverbs 1:22"> What man is like Job, who drinketh up scorning like water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; 1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 13:20"> Which goeth in company with the workers of iniquity, and walketh with wicked men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:14–16; Job 35:3; Malachi 3:14"> For he hath said, it profiteth a man nothing that he should delight himself with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:14; Deuteronomy 32:4; Job 8:3"> Therefore hearken unto me, ye men of understanding: far be it from God, that he should do wickedness; and from the Almighty, that he should commit iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:6; Revelation 22:12"> For the work of a man shall he render unto him, and cause every man to find according to his ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 8:3; Psalms 11:7; Psalms 145:17"> Yea, of a surety, God will not do wickedly, neither will the Almighty pervert judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:11; Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34–36"> Who gave him a charge over the earth? or who hath disposed the whole world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:29; Job 7:17; Job 9:4"> If he set his heart upon man, if he gather unto himself his spirit and his breath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Isaiah 57:16"> All flesh shall perish together, and man shall turn again unto dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:2–6; Job 12:3"> If now thou hast understanding, hear this: hearken to the voice of my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 40:8; Genesis 18:25; 2 Samuel 23:3"> Shall even one that hateth right govern? and wilt thou condemn him that is just and mighty?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Proverbs 17:26; Romans 13:7"> Is it fit to say to a king, Thou art vile? or to nobles, Ye are wicked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Acts 10:34; James 2:5"> How much less to him that respecteth not the persons of princes, nor regardeth the rich more than the poor? for they all are the work of his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 73:19; 1 Samuel 25:37–39; 1 Samuel 26:10"> In a moment they die, even at midnight; the people are shaken and pass away, and the mighty are taken away without hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Job 31:4; Jeremiah 16:17"> For his eyes are upon the ways of a man, and he seeth all his goings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Amos 9:2–3; Proverbs 10:29"> There is no darkness, nor shadow of death, where the workers of iniquity may hide themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Psalms 119:137; Romans 9:20"> For he needeth not further to consider a man, that he should go before God in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Psalms 94:5; Daniel 2:21"> He breaketh in pieces mighty men in ways past finding out, and setteth others in their stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 7:2; Job 34:20; Amos 8:7"> Therefore he taketh knowledge of their works; and he overturneth them in the night, so that they are destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 58:10–11; Deuteronomy 21:21; Isaiah 66:24"> He striketh them as wicked men in the open sight of others;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 28:5; 1 Samuel 15:11; Isaiah 5:12"> Because they turned aside from following him, and would not have regard to any of his ways:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="James 5:4; Exodus 3:7; Job 35:9"> So that they caused the cry of the poor to come unto him, and he heard the cry of the afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 23:13; John 14:27; Psalms 27:9"> When he giveth quietness, who then can condemn? and when he hideth his face, who then can behold him? whether it be done unto a nation, or unto a man, alike:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2–12; Job 34:21; 2 Kings 21:9"> That the godless man reign not, that there be none to ensnare the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Micah 7:9; Job 33:27; Job 42:6"> For hath any said unto God, I have borne chastisement, I will not offend any more:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Psalms 25:4–5; Psalms 139:23–24"> That which I see not teach thou me: if I have done iniquity, I will do it no more?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 33:32; Isaiah 45:9; Psalms 135:6"> Shall his recompence be as thou wilt, that thou refusest it? for thou must choose, and not I: therefore speak what thou knowest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:15; Job 34:4; Job 34:16"> Men of understanding will say unto me, yea, every wise man that heareth me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 35:16; Job 38:2; Job 15:2"> Job speaketh without knowledge, and his words are without wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Job 21:7; Job 23:16; Job 12:6"> Would that Job were tried unto the end, because of his answering like wicked men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Job 27:23; Isaiah 1:19–20; 1 Samuel 15:23"> For he addeth rebellion unto his sin, he clappeth his hands among us, and multiplieth his words against God.</span>
