<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:3; Job 42:4; Job 23:3–4"> Gird up thy loins now like a man: I will demand of thee, and declare thou unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:4; Isaiah 28:18; Job 32:2"> Wilt thou even disannul my judgment? wilt thou condemn me, that thou mayest be justified?</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 2:10; Esther 7:8; Job 14:13"> Hide them in the dust together; bind their faces in the hidden place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–9; Romans 5:6; Psalms 44:6"> Then wilt I also confess of thee that thine own right hand can save thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 40:20; Psalms 104:14; Genesis 1:24–26"> Behold now behemoth, which I made with thee; he eateth grass as all ox.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 41:23"> He moveth his tail like a cedar: the sinews of his thighs are knit together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 48:4; Job 7:12"> His bones are as tubes of brass; his limbs are like bars of iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 26:13; Isaiah 27:1"> He is the chief of the ways of God: he only that made him can make his sword to approach unto him.</span>
