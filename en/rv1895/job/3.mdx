<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–15; Psalms 106:33; Psalms 39:2–3"> After this opened Job his mouth, and cursed his day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 18:14"> And Job answered and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–18; Jeremiah 15:10; Job 10:18–19"> Let the day perish wherein I was born, and the night which said, There is a man child conceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 27:20; Exodus 10:22–23; Joel 2:2"> Let that day be darkness; let not God regard it from above, neither let the light shine upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Psalms 23:4; Job 28:3"> Let darkness and the shadow of death claim it for their own; let a cloud dwell upon it; let all that maketh black the day terrify it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> As for that night, let thick darkness seize upon it: let it not rejoice among the days of the year; let it not come into the number of the months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 24:8; Jeremiah 7:34; Revelation 18:22–23"> Lo, let that night be barren; let no joyful voice come therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 41:1; Job 41:10; Mark 5:38"> Let them curse it that curse the day, who are ready to rouse up leviathan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 41:18; Jeremiah 13:16; Job 30:26"> Let the stars of the twilight thereof be dark: let it look for light, but have none; neither let it behold the eyelids of the morning:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:17; Ecclesiastes 6:3–5; Genesis 20:18"> Because it shut not up the doors of my mother’s womb, nor hid trouble from mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 10:18–19; Isaiah 46:3; Psalms 71:6"> Why died I not from the womb? why did I not give up the ghost when I came out of the belly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 66:12; Genesis 30:3; Genesis 50:23"> Why did the knees receive me? or why the breasts, that I should suck?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Job 21:13; Job 14:10–12"> For now should I have lain down and been quiet; I should have slept; then had I been at rest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 15:28; Psalms 89:48; Psalms 49:14"> With kings and counsellors of the earth, which built up waste places for themselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:27; Job 22:25; Zephaniah 1:18"> Or with princes that had gold, who filled their houses with silver:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 58:8; Ecclesiastes 6:3; 1 Corinthians 15:8"> Or as an hidden untimely birth I had not been; as infants which never saw light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Job 17:16; Hebrews 4:11"> There the wicked cease from troubling; and there the weary be at rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 39:7; Isaiah 14:3–4; Exodus 5:6–8"> There the prisoners are at ease together; they hear not the voice of the taskmaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:8; Job 30:23; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> The small and great are there; and the servant is free from his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:18; Job 7:15–16; 1 Samuel 1:10"> Wherefore is light given to him that is in misery, and life unto the bitter in soul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 9:6; Proverbs 2:4; 1 Kings 19:4"> Which long for death, but it cometh not; and dig for it more than for hid treasures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Which rejoice exceedingly, and are glad, when they can find the grave?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 19:8; Lamentations 3:7; Isaiah 40:27"> Why is light given to a man whose way is hid, and whom God hath hedged in?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 102:9; Psalms 42:3–4; Psalms 80:5"> For my sighing cometh before I eat, and my roarings are poured out like water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 1:5; Job 30:15; Job 31:23"> For the thing which I fear cometh upon me, and that which I am afraid of cometh unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 7:14; Job 27:9; Psalms 143:11"> I am not at ease, neither am I quiet, neither have I rest; but trouble cometh.</span>
