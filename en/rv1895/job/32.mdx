<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 22:21; Job 40:8; Job 27:2"> Then was kindled the wrath of Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite, of the family of Ram: against Job was his wrath kindled, because he justified himself rather than God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 8:6; Job 24:25; Job 22:5–30"> Also against his three friends was his wrath kindled, because they had found no answer, and yet had condemned Job.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Proverbs 18:13"> Now Elihu had waited to speak unto Job, because they were elder than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 32:2; Exodus 32:19"> And when Elihu saw that there was no answer in the mouth of these three men, his wrath was kindled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 15:10; 1 Timothy 5:1; Titus 2:6"> And Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite answered and said, I am young, and ye are very old; wherefore I held back, and durst not shew you mine opinion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:31; Job 12:12; Hebrews 5:12"> I said, Days should speak, and multitude of years should teach wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 38:36; Job 33:4; 2 Timothy 3:16"> But there is a spirit in man, and the breath of the Almighty giveth them understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; Job 12:20; Ecclesiastes 4:13"> It is not the great that are wise, nor the aged that understand judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:25; 1 Corinthians 7:40"> Therefore I said, Hearken to me; I also will shew mine opinion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 29:21; Job 5:27; Proverbs 28:11"> Behold, I waited for your words, I listened for your reasons, whilst ye searched out what to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:3; 1 Timothy 1:7"> Yea, I attended unto you, and, behold, there was none that convinced Job, or that answered his words, among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> Beware lest ye say, We have found wisdom; God may vanquish him, not man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For he hath not directed his words against me; neither will I answer him with your speeches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:24–25; Matthew 22:46; Job 29:22"> They are amazed, they answer no more: they have not a word to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 1:19; Job 13:5; Amos 5:13"> And shall I wait, because they speak not, because they stand still, and answer no more?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:3–4; Job 33:12; Job 32:10"> I also will answer my part, I also will shew mine opinion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:3; Acts 4:20; 2 Corinthians 5:13–14"> For I am full of words; the spirit within me constraineth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 9:17"> Behold, my belly is as wine which hath no vent; like new bottles it is ready to burst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6–7; Job 21:3; Job 13:13"> I will speak, that I may be refreshed; I will open my lips and answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; Job 13:8; Matthew 22:16"> Let me not, I pray you, respect any man’s person; neither will I give flattering titles unto any man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 12:2–3; 1 Thessalonians 2:5; Proverbs 29:5"> For I know not to give flattering titles; else would my Maker soon take me away.</span>
