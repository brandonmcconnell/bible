<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 21:30; Job 34:22; Matthew 7:13"> Is it not calamity to the unrighteous, and disaster to the workers of iniquity?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 34:21; Proverbs 5:21; 2 Chronicles 16:9"> Doth not he see my ways, and number all my steps?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Psalms 44:20–21; Psalms 12:2"> If I have walked with vanity, and my foot hath hasted to deceit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 7:8–9; Daniel 5:27; Isaiah 26:7"> (Let me be weighed in an even balance, that God may know mine integrity;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 15:39; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Matthew 5:29"> If my step hath turned out of the way, and mine heart walked after mine eyes, and if any spot hath cleaved to mine hands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Leviticus 26:16; Deuteronomy 28:38"> Then let me sow, and let another eat; yea, let the produce of my field be rooted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 11:4; Ecclesiastes 7:26; Judges 16:5"> If mine heart have been enticed unto a woman, and I have laid wait at my neighbour’s door:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:10; 2 Samuel 12:11; Isaiah 47:2"> Then let my wife grind unto another, and let others bow down upon her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Deuteronomy 22:22–24; Job 31:28"> For that were an heinous crime; yea, it were an iniquity to be punished by the judges:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 6:27; Jeremiah 5:7–9; Job 15:30"> For it is a fire that consumeth unto Destruction, and would root out all mine increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:14–15; Exodus 21:26–27; Leviticus 25:46"> If I did despise the cause of my manservant or of my maidservant, when they contended with me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 2:13; Psalms 9:12; Zechariah 2:13"> What then shall I do when God riseth up? and when he visiteth, what shall I answer him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 14:31; Malachi 2:10; Job 34:19"> Did not he that made me in the womb make him? and did not one fashion us in the womb?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Deuteronomy 15:7–10; Deuteronomy 28:32"> If I have withheld the poor from their desire, or have caused the eyes of the widow to fail;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 29:12–16; John 13:29; Job 22:7"> Or have eaten my morsel alone, and the fatherless hath not eaten thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> (Nay, from my youth he grew up with me as with a father, and I have been her guide from my mother’s womb;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 22:6; Luke 3:11; Job 24:4"> If I have seen any perish for want of clothing, or that the needy had no covering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:13; Job 29:11"> If his loins have not blessed me, and if he were not warmed with the fleece of my sheep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 22:9; Job 29:12; Micah 7:3"> If I have lifted up my hand against the fatherless, because I saw my help in the gate:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 137:6; Job 38:15; Job 31:40"> Then let my shoulder fall from the shoulder blade, and mine arm be broken from the bone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 13:11; Psalms 119:120; 2 Corinthians 5:11"> For calamity from God was a terror to me, and by reason of his excellency I could do nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 49:6–7; Deuteronomy 8:12–14; Proverbs 11:28"> If I have made gold my hope, and have said to the fine gold, Thou art my confidence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 62:10; Habakkuk 1:16; Isaiah 10:13–14"> If I rejoiced because my wealth was great, and because mine hand had gotten much;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Deuteronomy 4:19; Deuteronomy 17:3"> If I beheld the sun when it shined, or the moon walking in brightness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:6; Deuteronomy 11:16; Psalms 2:12"> And my heart hath been secretly enticed, and my mouth hath kissed my hand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–7; Job 31:11; Psalms 50:6"> This also were an iniquity to be punished by the judges: for I should have lied to God that is above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17–18; Proverbs 17:5; Psalms 35:13–14"> If I rejoiced at the destruction of him that hated me, or lifted up myself when evil found him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:14; Exodus 23:4–5"> (Yea, I suffered not my mouth to sin by asking his life with a curse;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 1:11–12; Micah 3:2–3"> If the men of my tent said not, Who can find one that hath not been satisfied with his flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Genesis 19:2–3; Judges 19:20–21"> The stranger did not lodge in the street; but I opened my doors to the traveller;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Hosea 6:7; Acts 5:8"> If like Adam I covered my transgressions, by hiding mine iniquity in my bosom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 29:25; Esther 4:11"> Because I feared the great multitude, and the contempt of families terrified me, so that I kept silence, and went not out of the door—</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 13:21–22; Job 40:4–5; Job 17:3"> Oh that I had one to hear me! (lo, here is my signature, let the Almighty answer me;) and that I had the indictment which mine adversary hath written!</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 22:22; Job 29:14; Isaiah 62:3"> Surely I would carry it upon my shoulder; I would bind it unto me as a crown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Job 13:15; Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 4:15–16"> I would declare unto him the number of my steps; as a prince would I go near unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:11; James 5:4; Psalms 65:13"> If my land cry out against me, and the furrows thereof weep together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; James 5:4; Isaiah 26:21"> If I have eaten the fruits thereof without money, or have caused the owners thereof to lose their life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; Malachi 1:3; Psalms 72:20"> Let thistles grow instead of wheat, and cockle instead of barley. The words of Job are ended.</span>
