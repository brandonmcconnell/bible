<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; Job 8:8–10; 1 Corinthians 6:5"> No doubt but ye are the people, and wisdom shall die with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 13:2–5; Proverbs 26:4; 2 Corinthians 11:21–23"> But I have understanding as well as you; I am not inferior to you: yea, who knoweth not such things as these?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 30:1; Job 17:6; Psalms 91:15"> I am as one that is a laughing-stock to his neighbour, a man that called upon God, and he answered him: the just, the perfect man is a laughing-stock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 25:8; Proverbs 13:9; Psalms 17:5"> In the thought of him that is at ease there is contempt for misfortune; it is ready for them whose foot slippeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 9:24; Psalms 73:11–12; Psalms 17:14"> The tents of robbers prosper, and they that provoke God are secure; into whose hand God bringeth abundantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:3; Proverbs 6:6; Jeremiah 8:7"> But ask now the beasts, and they shall teach thee; and the fowls of the air, and they shall tell thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Or speak to the earth, and it shall teach thee; and the fishes of the sea shall declare unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 41:20; Jeremiah 27:5–6; Romans 11:36"> Who knoweth not in all these, that the hand of LORD hath wrought this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 17:28; Job 33:4; Acts 17:25"> In whose hand is the soul of every living thing, and the breath of all mankind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 34:3; Hebrews 5:14; Job 6:30"> Doth not the ear try words, even as the palate tasteth its meat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:7; Job 15:10; Job 8:8"> With aged men is wisdom, and in length of days understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 9:4; Job 36:5; Proverbs 2:6–7"> With him is wisdom and might; he hath counsel and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:7; Isaiah 22:22; Malachi 1:4"> Behold, he breaketh down, and it cannot be built again; he shutteth up a man, and there can be no opening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Amos 5:8; Genesis 6:13"> Behold, he withholdeth the waters, and they dry up; again, he sendeth them out, and they overturn the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:9; Ezekiel 14:9; 1 Kings 22:22–23"> With him is strength and effectual working; the deceived and the deceiver are his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20; 2 Samuel 17:23"> He leadeth counsellors away spoiled, and judges maketh he fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 2:21; Psalms 116:16; 2 Chronicles 33:11–14"> He looseth the bond of kings, and bindeth their loins with a girdle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; Isaiah 37:36–38; Revelation 17:14"> He leadeth priests away spoiled, and overthroweth the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 32:9; Job 17:4; Job 12:24"> He removeth the speech of the trusty, and taketh away the understanding of the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:40; Isaiah 23:9; 2 Kings 9:34–37"> He poureth contempt upon princes, and looseth the belt of the strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Corinthians 2:10"> He discovereth deep things out of darkness, and bringeth out to light the shadow of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 9:3; Isaiah 26:15; Isaiah 60:22"> He increaseth the nations, and destroyeth them: he spreadeth the nations abroad, and bringeth them in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 107:40; Job 12:20; Hosea 7:11"> He taketh away the heart of the chiefs of the people of the earth, and causeth them to wander in a wilderness where there is no way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 5:14; Psalms 107:27; Isaiah 19:14"> They grope in the dark without light, and he maketh them to stagger like a drunken man.</span>
