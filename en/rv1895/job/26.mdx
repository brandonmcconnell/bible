<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3–4; Job 4:3–4; Isaiah 41:5–7"> How hast thou helped him that is without power! how hast thou saved the arm that hath no strength!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 71:15–18; Job 32:11–13; Job 33:3"> How hast thou counselled him that hath no wisdom, and plentifully declared sound knowledge!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 22:23–24; Job 32:18; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> To whom hast thou uttered words? and whose spirit came forth from thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 88:10; Genesis 6:4; Job 41:1–34"> They that are deceased tremble beneath the waters and the inhabitants thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 139:8; Proverbs 15:11; Hebrews 4:13"> Sheol is naked before him, and Abaddon hath no covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 9:8; Psalms 104:2–5; Isaiah 40:22"> He stretcheth out the north over empty space, and hangeth the earth upon nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Jeremiah 10:13; Isaiah 5:6"> He bindeth up the waters in his thick clouds; and the cloud is not rent under them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Job 22:14; Habakkuk 3:3–5"> He closeth in the face of his throne, and spreadeth his cloud upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 8:29; Job 38:8–11; Jeremiah 5:22"> He hath described a boundary upon the face of the waters, unto the confines of light and darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; Psalms 18:7; Job 15:15"> The pillars of heaven tremble and are astonished at his rebuke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 51:15; Jeremiah 31:35; Psalms 89:9–10"> He stirreth up the sea with his power, and by his understanding he smiteth through Rahab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 104:30; Psalms 74:13–14"> By his spirit the heavens are garnished; his hand hath pierced the swift serpent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:26–29; Psalms 145:3; Romans 11:33"> Lo, these are but the outskirts of his ways: and how small a whisper do we hear of him! but the thunder of his power who can understand?</span>
