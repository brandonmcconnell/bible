<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 12:4; Psalms 35:15–16; Psalms 69:12"> But now they that are younger than I have me in derision, whose fathers I disdained to set with the dogs of my flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Yea, the strength of their hands, whereto should it profit me? men in whom ripe age is perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 24:5; Hebrews 11:38; Job 24:13–16"> They are gaunt with want and famine; they gnaw the dry ground; in the gloom of wasteness and desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:16; 2 Kings 4:38–39; Amos 7:14"> They pluck salt-wort by the bushes; and the roots of the broom are their meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Daniel 4:32–33; Psalms 109:10"> They are driven forth from the midst of men; they cry after them as after a thief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1–2; Judges 6:2; Isaiah 2:19"> In the clefts of the valleys must they dwell, in holes of the earth and of the rocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 6:5; Job 11:12; Genesis 16:12"> Among the bushes they bray; under the nettles they are gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 16:22; Job 40:4; Isaiah 32:6"> They are children of fools, yea, children of base men; they were scourged out of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Lamentations 3:63; Job 17:6; Lamentations 3:14"> And now I am become their song, yea, I am a byword unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 50:6; Numbers 12:14"> They abhor me, they stand aloof from me, and spare not to spit in my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:21; Ruth 1:21; Job 12:18"> For he hath loosed his cord, and afflicted me, and they have cast off the bridle before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 19:12; Isaiah 3:5; Job 19:18"> Upon my right hand rise the rabble; they thrust aside my feet, and they cast up against me their ways of destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:12; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 69:26"> They mar my path, they set forward my calamity, even men that have no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Psalms 18:4; Psalms 69:14–15"> As through a wide breach they come: in the midst of the ruin they roll themselves upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 13:3; Job 6:4; Isaiah 44:22"> Terrors are turned upon me, they chase mine honour as the wind; and my welfare is passed away as a cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Psalms 22:14; Isaiah 53:12"> And now my soul is poured out within me; days of affliction have taken hold upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 7:4; Job 33:19–21; Isaiah 38:13"> In the night season my bones are pierced in me, and the pains that gnaw me take no rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:5; Job 19:20; Job 2:7"> By the great force of my disease is my garment disfigured: it bindeth me about as the collar of my coat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:31; Job 42:6; Genesis 18:27"> He hath cast me into the mire, and I am become like dust and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 19:7; Psalms 80:4–5; Psalms 22:2"> I cry unto thee, and thou dost not answer me: I stand up, and thou lookest at me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 10:14–17; Job 16:9–14; Job 19:6–9"> Thou art turned to be cruel to me: with the might of thy hand thou persecutest me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 27:21; Psalms 1:4; Hosea 4:19"> Thou liftest me up to the wind, thou causest me to ride upon it; and thou dissolvest me in the storm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5–7; Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> For I know that thou wilt bring me to death, and to the house appointed for all living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Matthew 27:39–44; Job 19:7"> Surely against a ruinous heap he will not put forth his hand; though it be in his destruction, one may utter a cry because of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalms 35:13–14; Jeremiah 13:17"> Did not I weep for him that was in trouble? was not my soul grieved for the needy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:15; Job 3:25–26; Jeremiah 14:19"> When I looked for good, then evil came; and when I waited for light, there came darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Jeremiah 31:20; Jeremiah 4:19"> My bowels boil, and rest not; days of affliction are come upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 42:9; Psalms 38:6; Psalms 43:2"> I go mourning without the sun: I stand up in the assembly, and cry for help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Micah 1:8; Psalms 102:6; Psalms 44:19"> I am a brother to jackals, and a companion to ostriches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 4:8; Psalms 102:3; Psalms 119:83"> My skin is black, and falleth from me, and my bones are burned with heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 5:15; Isaiah 21:4; Isaiah 22:12"> Therefore is my harp turned to mourning, and my pipe into the voice of them that weep.</span>
