<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He breaketh open a shaft away from where men sojourn; they are forgotten of the foot that passeth by; they hang afar from men, they swing to and fro.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 28:25–29; Psalms 104:14–15; Genesis 1:11–12"> As for the earth, out of it cometh bread: and underneath it is turned up as it were by fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 28:16; Exodus 24:10; Isaiah 54:11"> The stones thereof are the place of sapphires, and it hath dust of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 11:6; Job 28:21–23; Job 38:24"> That path no bird of prey knoweth, neither hath the falcon’s eye seen it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The proud beasts have not trodden it, nor hath the fierce lion passed thereby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nahum 1:4–6"> He putteth forth his hand upon the flinty rock; he overturneth the mountains by the roots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:9; Proverbs 14:23; Proverbs 24:4"> He cutteth out channels among the rocks; and his eye seeth every precious thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:5; Isaiah 45:2–3; Isaiah 44:27"> He bindeth the streams that they trickle not; and the thing that is hid bringeth he forth to light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 2:4–6; Job 28:20; James 1:5"> But where shall wisdom be found? and where is the place of understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 8:11; Proverbs 8:18–19; Proverbs 3:14–15"> Man knoweth not the price thereof; neither is it found in the land of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 11:33–34"> The deep saith, It is not in me: and the sea saith, It is not with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 16:16; Proverbs 8:10–11; Proverbs 8:19"> It cannot be gotten for gold, neither shall silver be weighed for the price thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 13:12; Ezekiel 28:13; Psalms 45:9"> It cannot be valued with the gold of Ophir, with the precious onyx, or the sapphire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Revelation 4:6; Revelation 21:11"> Gold and glass cannot equal it: neither shall the exchange thereof be jewels of fine gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:15; Proverbs 31:10; Lamentations 4:7"> No mention shall be made of coral or of crystal: yea, the price of wisdom is above rubies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 8:19; Exodus 39:10; Ezekiel 28:13"> The topaz of Ethiopia shall not equal it, neither shall it be valued with pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 28:12; James 1:5; James 1:17"> Whence then cometh wisdom? and where is the place of understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 49:3–4; 1 Corinthians 2:7–10; Matthew 11:25"> Seeing it is hid from the eyes of all living, and kept close from the fowls of the air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 83:10–12; Job 26:6; Job 28:14"> Destruction and Death say, We have heard a rumour thereof with our ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 8:22–31; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Jude 1:25"> God understandeth the way thereof, and he knoweth the place thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Zechariah 4:10; 2 Chronicles 16:9"> For he looketh to the ends of the earth, and seeth under the whole heaven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Isaiah 40:12; Job 12:15"> To make a weight for the wind; yea, he meteth out the waters by measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 38:25; Job 37:3; Zechariah 10:1"> When he made a decree for the rain, and a way for the lightning of the thunder:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 19:1; Proverbs 8:22–29"> Then did he see it, and declare it; he established it, yea, and searched it out.</span>
