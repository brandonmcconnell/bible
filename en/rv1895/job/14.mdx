<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 5:7; Ecclesiastes 2:23; Job 25:4"> Man that is born of a woman is of few days, and full of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; 1 Peter 1:24; Job 8:9"> He cometh forth like a flower, and is cut down: he fleeth also as a shadow, and continueth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 144:3; Psalms 143:2; Psalms 8:4"> And dost thou open thine eyes upon such an one, and bringest me into judgment with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 3:6; Job 15:14; Ephesians 2:3"> Who can bring a clean thing out of an unclean? not one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 39:4; Acts 17:26; Hebrews 9:27"> Seeing his days are determined, the number of his months is with thee, and thou hast appointed his bounds that he cannot pass;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 7:19; Psalms 39:13; Job 7:1–2"> Look away from him, that he may rest, till he shall accomplish, as an hireling, his day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; Isaiah 11:1; Job 14:14"> For there is hope of a tree, if it be cut down, that it will sprout again, and that the tender branch thereof will not cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36; Isaiah 26:19; John 12:24"> Though the root thereof wax old in the earth, and the stock thereof die in the ground;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:17–24; Ezekiel 17:3–10; Ezekiel 17:22–24"> Yet through the scent of water it will bud, and put forth boughs like a plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 7:7–10; Job 14:12"> But man dieth, and wasteth away: yea, man giveth up the ghost, and where is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 19:5; Jeremiah 15:18; Job 6:15–18"> As the waters fail from the sea, and the river decayeth and drieth up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Job 3:13; Ecclesiastes 3:19–21"> So man lieth down and riseth not: till the heavens be no more, they shall not awake, nor be roused out of their sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Isaiah 57:1–2; Isaiah 26:20–21"> Oh that thou wouldest hide me in Sheol, that thou wouldest keep me secret, until thy wrath be past, that thou wouldest appoint me a set time, and remember me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Job 7:1; Acts 26:8"> If a man die, shall he live again? all the days of my warfare would I wait, till my release should come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 13:22; Psalms 138:8; Job 10:8"> Thou shouldest call, and I would answer thee: thou wouldest have a desire to the work of thine hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 10:6; Job 31:4; Psalms 139:1–4"> But now thou numberest my steps: dost thou not watch over my sin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:34; Hosea 13:12; Job 21:19"> My transgression is sealed up in a bag, and thou fastenest up mine iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 6:14; Job 18:4; Jeremiah 4:24"> And surely the mountain falling cometh to nought, and the rock is removed out of its place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 7:6; Job 27:8; Job 19:10"> The waters wear the stones; the overflowings thereof wash away the dust of the earth: and thou destroyest the hope of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:14; Job 2:12; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> Thou prevailest for ever against him, and he passeth; thou changest his countenance, and sendest him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Isaiah 63:16"> His sons come to honour, and he knoweth it not; and they are brought low, but he perceiveth it not of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 19:20; Luke 16:23–24"> But his flesh upon him hath pain, and his soul within him mourneth.</span>
