<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 20:1"> Then answered Zophar the Naamathite, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 10:19; James 1:19; Job 18:2"> Should not the multitude of words be answered? and should a man full of talk be justified?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 24:25; Titus 2:8"> Should thy boastings make men hold their peace? and when thou mockest, shall no man make thee ashamed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 6:10; Job 35:2"> For thou sayest, My doctrine is pure, and I am clean in thine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 38:1–2; Job 31:35; Job 33:6–18"> But Oh that God would speak, and open his lips against thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Lamentations 3:22; Daniel 2:28"> And that he would shew thee the secrets of wisdom, that is manifold in effectual working! Know therefore that God exacteth of thee less than thine iniquity deserveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Ecclesiastes 3:11; Psalms 145:3"> Canst thou by searching find out God? canst thou find out the Almighty unto perfection?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 22:12; Psalms 139:6–8; Isaiah 55:9"> It is high as heaven; what canst thou do? deeper than Sheol; what canst thou know?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 139:9–10; Job 28:24–25; Psalms 65:5–8"> The measure thereof is longer than the earth, and broader than the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:12–13; Job 12:14; Revelation 3:7"> If he pass through, and shut up, and call unto judgment, then who can hinder him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 10:14; Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23"> For he knoweth vain men: he seeth iniquity also, even though he consider it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 2:20; Ecclesiastes 3:18; James 3:13–17"> But vain man is void of understanding, yea, man is born as a wild ass’s colt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 143:6; Psalms 88:9; 1 Samuel 7:3"> If thou set thine heart aright, and stretch out thine hands toward him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:23; James 4:8; Job 4:7"> If iniquity be in thine hand, put it far away, and let not unrighteousness dwell in thy tents;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 22:26; 1 John 2:28; 1 John 3:19–22"> Surely then shalt thou lift up thy face without spot; yea, thou shalt be stedfast, and shalt not fear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 65:16; Ecclesiastes 5:20; John 16:21"> For thou shalt forget thy misery; thou shalt remember it as waters that are passed away:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 58:8–10; Psalms 37:6; Proverbs 4:18"> And thy life shall be clearer than the noonday; though there be darkness, it shall be as the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:24–26; Psalms 43:5; Psalms 3:5"> And thou shalt be secure, because there is hope; yea, thou shalt search about thee, and shalt take thy rest in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 45:12; Isaiah 60:14; Job 42:8–9"> Also thou shalt lie down, and none shall make thee afraid; yea, many shall make suit unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:65; Job 31:16; Job 17:5"> But the eyes of the wicked shall fail, and they shall have no way to flee, and their hope shall be the giving up of the ghost.</span>
