<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And it came to pass the second night that I saw a dream, and, behold, there came up from the sea an eagle, which had twelve feathered wings, and three heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And I saw, and, behold, she spread her wings over all the earth, and all the winds of heaven blew on her,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="So the chief Oriental versions. The Latin has only and were gathered together.">a</a></sup>and the clouds were gathered together against her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And I beheld, and out of her wings there grew other wings over against them; and they became little wings and small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But her heads were at rest: the head in the midst was greater than the other heads, yet rested it with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Moreover I beheld, and, lo, the eagle flew with her wings, to reign over the earth, and over them that dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And I beheld how that all things under heaven were subject unto her, and no man spake against her, no, not one creature upon earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And I beheld, and, lo, the eagle rose upon her talons, and uttered her voice to her wings, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Watch not all at once: sleep every one in his own place, and watch by course:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> but let the heads be preserved for the last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And I beheld, and, lo, the voice went not out of her heads, but from the midst of her body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And I numbered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Syriac has her little wings, and, &amp;c.">b</a></sup>her wings that were over against the other, and, behold, there were eight of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And I beheld, and, lo, on the right side there arose one wing, and reigned over all the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and so it was, that when it reigned, the end of it came, and it appeared not, so that the place thereof appeared no more: and the next following rose up, and reigned, and it bare rule a great time;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and it happened, that when it reigned, the end of it came also, so that it appeared no more, like as the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And, lo, there came a voice unto it, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Hear thou that hast borne rule over the earth all this time: this I proclaim unto thee, before thou shalt appear no more,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> There shall none after thee attain unto thy time, neither unto the half thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Then arose the third, and had the rule as the others before, and it also appeared no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> So went it with all the wings one after another, as that every one bare rule, and then appeared no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And I beheld, and, lo, in process of time the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Syriac has little wings.">c</a></sup>wings that followed were set up upon the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The Aethiopic has left.">d</a></sup>right side, that they might rule also; and some of them ruled, but within a while they appeared no more:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> some also of them were set up, but ruled not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> After this I beheld, and, lo, the twelve wings appeared no more, nor two of the little wings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and there was no more left upon the eagle’s body, but the three heads that rested, and six little wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And I beheld, and, lo, two little wings divided themselves from the six, and remained under the head that was upon the right side: but four remained in their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And I beheld, and, lo, these<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The Syriac has little wings.">e</a></sup>under wings thought to set up themselves, and to have the rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And I beheld, and, lo, there was one set up, but within a while it appeared no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> A second also, and it was sooner away than the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And I beheld, and, lo, the two that remained thought also in themselves to reign:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> and while they so thought, behold, there awaked one of the heads that were at rest, namely, it that was in the midst; for that was greater than the two other heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And I beheld how that it joined the two other heads with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And, behold, the head was turned with them that were with it, and did eat up the two<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The Syriac has little wings.">f</a></sup>under wings that thought to have reigned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> But this head held the whole earth in possession, and bare rule over those that dwell therein with much oppression; and it had the governance of the world more than all the wings that had been.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And after this I beheld, and, lo, the head also that was in the midst suddenly appeared no more, like as the wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> But there remained the two heads, which also in like sort reigned over the earth, and over those that dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And I beheld, and, lo, the head upon the right side devoured it that was upon the left side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Then I heard a voice, which said unto me, Look before thee, and consider the thing that thou seest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And I beheld, and, lo, as it were a lion roused out of the wood roaring: and I heard how that he sent out a man’s voice unto the eagle, and spake, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Hear thou, I will talk with thee, and the Most High shall say unto thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Art not thou it that remainest of the four beasts, whom I made to reign in my world, that the end of my times might come through them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And the fourth came, and overcame all the beasts that were past, and held the world in governance with great trembling, and the whole compass of the earth with grievous oppression; and so long time dwelt he upon the earth with deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And thou hast judged the earth, but not with truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> For thou hast afflicted the meek, thou hast hurt the peaceable, thou hast hated them that speak truth, thou hast loved liars, and destroyed the dwellings of them that brought forth fruit, and cast down the walls of such as did thee no harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Therefore is thy insolent dealing come up unto the Most High, and thy pride unto the Mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> The Most High also hath looked upon his times, and, behold, they are ended, and his ages are fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And therefore appear no more, thou eagle, nor thy horrible wings, nor thy evil little wings, nor thy cruel heads, nor thy hurtful talons, nor all thy vain body:</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> that all the earth may be refreshed, and be eased, being delivered from thy violence, and that she may hope for the judgement and mercy of him that made her.</span>
</div>
