<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And when I had made an end of speaking these words, there was sent unto me the angel which had been sent unto me the nights afore:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and he said unto me, Up, Esdras, and hear the words that I am come to tell thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And I said, Speak on, my Lord. Then he said unto me, There is a sea set in a wide place, that it might be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="So the chief Oriental versions. The Latin MSS. have deep.">a</a></sup>broad and vast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But the entrance thereof shall be set in a narrow place so as to be like a river;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> whoso then should desire to go into the sea to look upon it, or to rule it, if he went not through the narrow, how could he come into the broad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Another thing also: There is a city builded and set in a plain country, and full of all good things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> but the entrance thereof is narrow, and is set in a dangerous place to fall, having a fire on the right hand, and on the left a deep water:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and there is one only path between them both, even between the fire and the water, so small that there could but one man go there at once.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> If this city now be given unto a man for an inheritance, if the heir pass not the danger before him, how shall he receive his inheritance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And I said, It is so, Lord. Then said he unto me, Even so also is Israel’s portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Because for their sakes I made the world: and when Adam transgressed my statutes, then was decreed that now is done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then were the entrances of this world made narrow, and sorrowful and toilsome: they are but few and evil, full of perils, and charged with great toils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For the entrances of the greater world are wide and sure, and bring forth fruit of immortality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> If then they that live enter not these strait and vain things, they can never receive those that are laid up for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Now therefore why disquietest thou thyself, seeing thou art but a corruptible man? and why art thou moved, whereas thou art but mortal?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and why hast thou not considered in thy mind that which is to come, rather than that which is present?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then answered I and said, O Lord that bearest rule, lo, thou hast ordained in thy law, that the righteous should inherit these things, but that the ungodly should perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The righteous therefore shall suffer strait things, and hope for wide: but they that have done wickedly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="According to some authorities, have not suffered...and shall not see.">b</a></sup>have suffered the strait things, and yet shall not see the wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And he said unto me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Another reading is, There is no judge...and none that hath understanding.">c</a></sup>Thou art not a judge above God, neither hast thou understanding above the Most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Yea, rather let many that now be perish, than that the law of God which is set before them be despised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For God straitly commanded such as came, even as they came, what they should do to live, and what they should observe to avoid punishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Nevertheless they were not obedient unto him; but spake against him, and imagined for themselves vain things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and framed cunning devices of wickedness; and said moreover of the Most High, that he is not; and knew not his ways:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> but they despised his law, and denied his covenants; they have not been faithful to his statutes, and have not performed his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Therefore, Esdras, for the empty are empty things, and for the full are the full things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> For behold, the time shall come, and it shall be, when these tokens, of which I told thee before, shall come to pass, that the bride shall appear, even the city coming forth, and she shall be seen, that now is withdrawn from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And whosoever is delivered from the foresaid evils, the same shall see my wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> For my son Jesus shall be revealed with those that be with him, and shall rejoice them that remain four hundred years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> After these years shall my son Christ dieof them that, and all that have the breath of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Lat. man">d</a></sup>life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the world shall be turned into the old silence seven days, like as in the first beginning: so that no man shall remain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And after seven days the world, that yet awaketh not, shall be raised up, and that shall die that is corruptible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the earth shall restore those that are asleep in her, and so shall the dust those that dwell therein in silence, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, chambers See ch. iv. 35.">e</a></sup>secret places shall deliver those souls that were committed unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And the Most High shall be revealed upon the seat of judgement,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The Syriac adds and the end shall come.">f</a></sup>and compassion shall pass away, and longsuffering shall be withdrawn:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> but judgement only shall remain, truth shall stand, and faith shall wax strong:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and the work shall follow, and the reward shall be shewed, and good deeds shall awake, and wicked deeds shall not sleep.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The passage from verse [36] to verse [105], formerly missing, has been restored to the text. See Preface, page ix.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="So the chief Oriental versions. The Latin MSS. have place.">h</a></sup>pit of torment shall appear, and over against it shall be the place of rest: and the furnace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Lat. Gehenna.">i</a></sup>hell shall be shewed, and over against it the paradise of delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And then shall the Most High say to the nations that are raised from the dead, See ye and understand whom ye have denied, or whom ye have not served, or whose commandments ye have despised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Look on this side and on that: here is delight and rest, and there fire and torments. Thus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="So the chief Oriental versions. The Latin has shalt thou speak.">j</a></sup>shall he speak unto them in the day of judgement:</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> This is a day that hath neither sun, nor moon, nor stars,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> neither cloud, nor thunder, nor lightning, neither wind, nor water, nor air, neither darkness, nor evening, nor morning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> neither summer, nor spring, nor heat, nor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or, storm">k</a></sup>winter, neither frost, nor cold, nor hail, nor rain, nor dew,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> neither noon, nor night, nor dawn, neither shining, nor brightness, nor light, save only the splendour of the glory of the Most High, whereby all shall see the things that are set before them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> for it shall endure as it were a week of years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> This is my judgement and the ordinance thereof; but to thee only have I shewed these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And I answered, I said even then, O Lord, and I say now: blessed are they that be now alive and keep the statutes ordained of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> But as touching them for whom my prayer was made, what shall I say? for who is there of them that be alive that hath not sinned, and who of the sons of men that hath not transgressed thy covenant?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> And now I see, that the world to come shall bring delight to few, but torments unto many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> For an evil heart hath grown up in us, which hath led us astray from these statutes, and hath brought us into corruption and into the ways of death, hath shewed us the paths of perdition and removed us far from life; and that, not a few only, but well nigh all that have been created.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> And he answered me, and said, Hearken unto me, and I will instruct thee; and I will admonish thee yet again:</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> for this cause the Most High hath not made one world, but two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> For whereas thou hast said that the just are not many, but few, and the ungodly abound, hear the answer thereunto.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> If thou hath choice stones exceeding few,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="The Latin is here corrupt.">l</a></sup>wilt thou set for thee over against them according to their number things of lead and clay?</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> And I said, Lord, how shall this be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> And he said unto me, Not only this, but ask the earth, and she shall tell thee; intreat her, and she shall declare unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> For thou shalt say unto her, Thou bringest forth gold and silver and brass, and iron also and lead and clay:</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> but silver is more abundant than gold, and brass than silver, and iron than brass, lead than iron, and clay than lead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> Judge thou therefore which things are precious and to be desired, whatso is abundant or what is rare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> And I said, O Lord that bearest rule, that which is plentiful is of less worth, for that which is more rare is more precious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> And he answered me, and said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="The Latin here is corrupt.">m</a></sup>Weigh within thyself the things that thou hast thought, for he that hath what is hard to get rejoiceth over him that hath what is plentiful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> So also is the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="The Latin is here corrupt.">n</a></sup>judgement which I have promised: for I will rejoice over the few that shall be saved, inasmuch as these are they that have made my glory now to prevail, and of whom my name is now named.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> And I will not grieve over the multitude of them that perish; for these are they that are now like unto vapour, and are become as flame and smoke; they are set on fire and burn hotly, and are quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> And I answered and said, O thou earth, wherefore hast thou brought forth, if the mind is made out of dust, like as all other created things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> For it were better that the dust itself had been unborn, so that the mind might not have been made therefrom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64"> But now the mind groweth with us, and by reason of this we are tormented, because we perish and know it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65"> Let the race of men lament and the beasts of the field be glad; let all that are born lament, but let the fourfooted beasts and the cattle rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66"> For it is far better with them than with us; for they look not for judgement, neither do they know of torments or of salvation promised unto them after death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67"> For what doth it profit us, that we shall be preserved alive, but yet be afflicted with torment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68"> For all that are born are<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="The Latin is here corrupt.">o</a></sup>defiled with iniquities, and are full of sins and laden with offences:</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69"> and if after death we were not to come into judgement, peradventure it had been better for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70"> And he answered me, and said, When the Most High made the world, and Adam and all them that came of him, he first prepared the Judgement and the things that pertain unto the judgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71"> And now understand from thine own words, for thou hast said that the mind groweth with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72"> They therefore that dwell upon the earth shall be tormented for this reason, that having understanding they have wrought iniquity, and receiving commandments have not kept them, and having obtained a law they dealt unfaithfully with that which they received.</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73"> What then will they have to say in the judgement, or how will they answer in the last times?</span>
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74"> For how great a time hath the Most High been longsuffering with them that inhabit the world, and not for their sakes, but because of the times which he hath foreordained!</span>
<span className="v" id="v75" data-v="75"> And I answered and said, if I have found grace in thy sight, O Lord, shew this also unto thy servant, whether after death, even now when every one of us giveth up his soul, we shall be kept in rest until those times come, in which thou shalt renew the creation, or whether we shall be tormented forthwith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v76" data-v="76"> And he answered me, and said, I will shew thee this also; but join not thyself with them that are scorners, nor number thyself with them that are tormented.</span>
<span className="v" id="v77" data-v="77"> For thou hast a treasure of good works laid up with the Most High, but it shall not be shewed thee until the last times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v78" data-v="78"> For concerning death the teaching is: When the determinate sentence hath gone forth from the Most High that a man should die, as the spirit leaveth the body to return again to him who gave it, it adoreth the glory of the Most High first of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v79" data-v="79"> And if it be one of those that have been scorners and have not kept the way of the Most High, and that have despised his law, and that hate them that fear<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Another reading is, him.">p</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v80" data-v="80"> these spirits shall not enter into habitations, but shall wander and be in torments forthwith, ever grieving and sad, in seven ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v81" data-v="81"> The first way, because they have despised the law of the Most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v82" data-v="82"> The second way, because they cannot now make a good returning that they may live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v83" data-v="83"> The third way, they shall see the reward laid up for them that have believed the covenants of the Most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v84" data-v="84"> The fourth way, they shall consider the torment laid up for themselves in the last days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v85" data-v="85"> The fifth way, they shall see the dwelling places of the others guarded by angels, with great quietness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v86" data-v="86"> The sixth way, they shall see<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="The passage is corrupt.">q</a></sup>how forthwith some of them shall pass into torment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v87" data-v="87"> The seventh way, which is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Lat. greater">r</a></sup>more grievous than all the aforesaid ways, because they shall pine away in confusion and be consumed with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="The Latin is here corrupt.">s</a></sup>shame, and shall be withered up by fears, seeing the glory of the Most High before whom they have sinned whilst living, and before whom they shall be judged in the last times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v88" data-v="88"> Now this is the order of those who have kept the ways of the Most High, when they shall be separated from the corruptible vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v89" data-v="89"> In the time<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="The Latin is here corrupt.">t</a></sup>that they dwelt therein they painfully served the Most High, and were in jeopardy every hour, that they might keep the law of the lawgiver perfectly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v90" data-v="90"> Wherefore this is the teaching concerning them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v91" data-v="91"> First of all they shall see with great joy the glory of him who taketh them up, for they shall have rest in seven orders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v92" data-v="92"> The first order, because they have striven with great labour to overcome the evil thought which was fashioned together with them, that it might not lead them astray from life into death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v93" data-v="93"> The second order, because they see the perplexity in which the souls of the ungodly wander, and the punishment that awaiteth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v94" data-v="94"> The third order, they see the witness which he that fashioned them beareth concerning them, that while they lived they kept the law which was given them in trust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v95" data-v="95"> The fourth order, they understand the rest which, being gathered in their chambers, they now enjoy with great quietness, guarded by angels, and the glory that awaiteth them in the last days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v96" data-v="96"> The fifth order, they rejoice, seeing how they have now escaped from that which is corruptible, and how they shall inherit that which is to come, while they see moreover the straitness and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="The Latin is here corrupt.">u</a></sup>painfulness from which they have been delivered, and the large room which they shall receive with joy and immortality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v97" data-v="97"> The sixth order, when it is shewed unto them how their face shall shine as the sun, and how they shall be made like unto the light of the stars, being henceforth incorruptible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v98" data-v="98"> The seventh order, which is greater than all the aforesaid orders, because they shall rejoice with confidence, and because they shall be bold without confusion, and shall be glad without fear, for they hasten to behold the face of him whom in their lifetime they served, and from whom they shall receive their reward in glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v99" data-v="99"> This is the order of the souls of the just, as from henceforth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="The latin is here corrupt.">v</a></sup>is announcer unto them, and aforesaid are the ways of torture which they that would not give heed shall suffer from henceforth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v100" data-v="100"> And I answered and said, shall time therefore be given unto the souls after they are separated from the bodies, that they may see that whereof thou hast spoken unto me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v101" data-v="101"> And he said, Their freedom shall be for seven days, that for seven days they may see the things whereof thou hast been told, and afterwards they shall be gathered together in their habitations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v102" data-v="102"> And I answered and said, if I have found favour in thy sight, shew further unto me thy servant whether in the day of judgment the just will be able to intercede for the ungodly or to intreat the Most High for them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v103" data-v="103"> whether fathers for children, or children for parents, or brethren for brethren, or kinsfolk for their next of kin, or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="So the Oriental versions.">w</a></sup>friends for them that are most dear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v104" data-v="104"> And he answered me, and said, Since thou hast found favor in my sight, I will shew thee this also: The day of judgement is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="The Latin has a bold day">x</a></sup>a day of decision, and displayeth unto all the seal of truth; even as now a father sendeth not his son, or a son his father, or a master his slave, or a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="So the Oriental versions.">y</a></sup>friend him that is most dear, that in his stead he may<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="the Latin has understand.">z</a></sup>be sick, or sleep, or eat, or be healed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v105" data-v="105"> so never shall anyone pray for another<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="So the Syriac. The Latin omits in that day...another.">aa</a></sup>in that day, neither shall one lay a burden on another, for then shall all bear every one his own righteousness or unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v106" data-v="106"> And I answered and said, How do we now find that first Abraham prayed for the people of Sodom, and Moses for the fathers that sinned in the wilderness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v107" data-v="107"> and Joshua after him for Israel in the days of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="That is, Achan. See Josh. vii. 1.">ab</a></sup>Achar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v108" data-v="108"> and Samuel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="So the Syriac and other versions. The Latin omits in the days of Saul.">ac</a></sup>in the days of Saul; and David for the plague: and Solomon for them that should worship in the sanctuary:</span>
<span className="v" id="v109" data-v="109"> and Elijah for those that received rain; and for the dead, that he might live:</span>
<span className="v" id="v110" data-v="110"> and Hezekiah for the people in the days of Sennacherib: and many for many?</span>
<span className="v" id="v111" data-v="111"> If therefore now, when corruption is grown up, and unrighteousness increased, the righteous have prayed for the ungodly, wherefore shall it not be so then also?</span>
<span className="v" id="v112" data-v="112"> He answered me, and said, This present world is not the end; the full glory abideth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Omitted in the Latin.">ad</a></sup>not therein: therefore have they who were able prayed for the weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v113" data-v="113"> But the day of judgement shall be the end of this time,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Omitted in the Latin.">ae</a></sup>and the beginning of the immortality for to come, wherein corruption is passed away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v114" data-v="114"> intemperance is at an end, infidelity is cut off, but righteousness is grown, and truth is sprung up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v115" data-v="115"> Then shall no man be able to have mercy on him that is cast in judgement, nor to thrust down him that hath gotten the victory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v116" data-v="116"> I answered then and said, this is my first and last saying, that it had been better that the earth had not given thee Adam: or else, when it had given him, to have restrained him from sinning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v117" data-v="117"> For what profit is it for all that are in this present time to live in heaviness, and after death to look for punishment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v118" data-v="118"> O thou Adam, what hast thou done? For though it was thou that sinned, the evil is not fallen on thee alone, but upon all of us that come of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v119" data-v="119"> For what profit is it unto us, if there be promised us an immortal time, whereas we have done the works that bring death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v120" data-v="120"> And that there is promised us an everlasting hope, whereas ourselves most miserably are become vain?</span>
<span className="v" id="v121" data-v="121"> And that there are reserved habitations of health and safety, whereas we have lived wickedly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v122" data-v="122"> And that the glory of the Most High shall defend them which have led a pure life, whereas we have walked in the most wicked ways of all?</span>
<span className="v" id="v123" data-v="123"> And that there shall be shewed a paradise, whose fruit endureth without decay, wherein is abundance and healing, but we shall not enter into it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v124" data-v="124"> for we have walked in unpleasant places?</span>
<span className="v" id="v125" data-v="125"> And that the faces of them which have used abstinence shall shine above the stars, whereas our faces shall be blacker than darkness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v126" data-v="126"> For while we lived and committed iniquity, we considered not what we should have to suffer after death.</span>
