<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the angel that was sent unto me, whose name was Uriel, gave me an answer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and said to me, thy heart hath utterly failed thee in regarding this world, and thinkest thou to comprehend the way of the Most High?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then said I, Yea my Lord. And he answered me, and said, I am sent to shew thee three ways, and to set forth three similitudes before thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> whereof if thou canst declare me one, I also will shew thee the way that thou desirest to see, and I will teach thee wherefore the heart is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, corrupt">a</a></sup>wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And I said, say on, my Lord. Then said he unto me, Go to, weigh me a weight of fire, or measure me a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="After the Oriental versions.">b</a></sup>measure of wind, or call me again the day that is past.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then answered I and said, who of the sons of men is able to do this, that thou shouldest ask me of such things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And he said unto me, If I had asked thee, saying, How many dwellings are there in the heart of the sea? or how many springs are there at the fountain head of the deep? or how many<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="After the Oriental versions.">c</a></sup>ways are above the firmament? or which are the outgoings<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="So the Syriac. The Latin omits of hell? or which are the paths.">d</a></sup>of hell? or which are the paths of paradise?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> peradventure thou wouldest say unto me, I never went down into the deep, nor as yet into hell, neither did I ever climb up into heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Nevertheless now have I asked thee but only of the fire and wind, and of the day, things wherethrough thou hast passed, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Another reading is, from which thou canst not be separated">e</a></sup>without which thou canst not be, and yet hast thou given me no answer of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He said moreover unto me, Thine own things, that are grown up with thee, canst thou not know;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> how then can thy vessel comprehend the way of the Most High? and how can he that is already worn out with the corrupted world understand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Syriac and Aethiopic, the way of the incorruptible.">f</a></sup>incorruption?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="So the Syriac and Aethiopic. The Latin is corrupt.">g</a></sup>And when I heard these things I fell upon my face, and said unto him, It were better that we were not here at all, than that we should come hither and live in the midst of ungodliness, and suffer, and not know wherefore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> He answered me, and said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="So the Oriental versions. The Latin is corrupt. See Judg. ix. 8.">h</a></sup>The woods of the trees of the field went forth, and took counsel together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and said, Come, let us go and make war against the sea, that it may depart away before us, and that we may make us more woods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The waves of the sea also in like manner took counsel together, and said, Come, let us go up and subdue the wood of the plain, that there also we may make us another country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The counsel of the wood was in vain, for the fire came and consumed it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> likewise also the counsel of the waves of the sea, for the sand stood up and stopped them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> If thou wert judge now betwixt these two, whom wouldest thou justify, or whom condemn?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> I answered and said, it is a foolish counsel that they both have taken, for the ground is given unto the wood, and the place of the sea is given to bear his waves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then answered he me, and said, Thou hast given a right judgement, and why judgest thou not in thine own case?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For like as the ground is given unto the wood, and the sea to his waves, even so they that dwell upon the earth may understand nothing but that which is upon the earth: and he only that dwelleth above the heavens may understand the things that are above the height of the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then answered I and said, I beseech thee, O Lord,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="After the Oriental versions.">i</a></sup>wherefore is the power of understanding given unto me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For it was not in my mind to be curious of the ways above, but of such things as pass by us daily; because Israel is given up as a reproach to the heathen, and the people whom thou hast loved is given over unto ungodly nations, and the law of our forefathers is made of none effect, and the written covenants are nowhere regarded,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and we pass away out of the world as grasshoppers, and our life is as a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="So the chief Oriental versions. The Latin is corrupt.">j</a></sup>vapour, neither are we worthy to obtain mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> What will he then do for his name whereby we are called? Of these things have I asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then he answered me, and said, if thou be alive thou shalt see, and if thou livest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="So the Syriac.">k</a></sup>long, thou shalt marvel; for the world hasteth fast to pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> For it is not able to bear the things that are promised to the righteous in the times to come: for this world is full of sadness and infirmities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> For the evil<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="so the Syriac and Aethiopic.">l</a></sup>whereof thou askest me is sown, but the gathering thereof is not yet come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> If therefore that which is sown be not reaped, and if the place where the evil is sown pass not away, there cannot come the field where the good is sown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For a grain of evil seed was sown in the heart of Adam from the beginning, and how much wickedness hath it brought forth unto this time! and how much shall it yet bring forth until the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or, threshing-floor">m</a></sup>time of threshing come!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Ponder now by thyself, how great fruit of wickedness a grain of evil seed hath brought forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> When the ears which are without number shall be sown, how great a floor shall they fill!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Then I answered and said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="So the chief oriental versions.">n</a></sup>How long? and when shall these things come to pass? wherefore are our years few and evil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And he answered me, and said, Thou dost not hasten more than the Most High: for thy haste is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="So the Syriac. The Latin is corrupt.">o</a></sup>for thine own self, but he that is above hasteneth on behalf of many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Did not the souls of the righteous ask question of these things in their chambers, saying, How long<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="So the Syriac. The Latin has shall I hope on this fashion?">p</a></sup>are we here? when cometh the fruit of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or, threshing-floor">q</a></sup>threshing time of our reward?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And unto them Jeremiel the archangel gave answer, and said, Even when the number is fulfilled of them that are like unto you. For he hath weighed the world in the balance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and by measure hath he measured the times, and by number hath he numbered the seasons; and he shall not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Syr. rest.">r</a></sup>move nor stir them, until the said measure be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Then answered I and said, O Lord that bearest rule, yet even we all are full of impiety:</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> and for our sakes peradventure it is that the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or, threshing-floor">s</a></sup>threshing time of the righteous is kept back, because of the sins of them that dwell upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> So he answered me, and said, Go thy way to a woman with child, and ask of her when she hath fulfilled her nine months, if her womb may keep the birth any longer within her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Then said I, no, Lord, that can it not. And he said unto me, in the grave the chambers of souls are like the womb:</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> for like as a woman that travaileth maketh haste to escape the anguish of the travail: even so do these places haste to deliver those things that are committed unto them from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Then shall it be shewed thee concerning those things which thou desirest to see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Then answered I and said, if I have found favour in thy sight, and if it be possible, and if I be meet therefore,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> shew me this also, whether there be more to come than is past, or whether the more part is gone over us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> For what is gone I know, but what is for to come I know not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> And he said unto me, Stand up upon the right side, and I shall expound the similitude unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> so I stood, and saw, and, behold, a hot burning oven passed by before me: and it happened, that when the flame was gone by I looked, and, behold, the smoke remained still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> After this there passed by before me a watery cloud, and sent down much rain with a storm; and when the stormy rain was past, the drops remained therein still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> Then said he unto me, Consider with thyself; as the rain is more than the drops, and the fire is greater than the smoke, so the quantity which is past did more exceed; but the drops and the smoke remained still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> Then I prayed, and said, May I live, thinkest thou, until that time? or who shall be in those days?</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> He answered me, and said, As for the tokens whereof thou askest me, I may tell thee of them in part: but as touching thy life, I am not sent to shew thee; for I do not know it.</span>
</div>
