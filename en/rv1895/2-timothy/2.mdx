<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Ephesians 6:10; 2 Timothy 1:7"> Thou therefore, my child, be strengthened in the grace that is in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13–14; 1 Timothy 4:6; 1 Timothy 6:12"> And the things which thou hast heard from me among many witnesses, the same commit thou to faithful men, who shall be able to teach others also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2–3; James 1:12; 2 Timothy 4:5"> Suffer hardship with me, as a good soldier of Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 8:14; 2 Peter 2:20"> No soldier on service entangleth himself in the affairs of this life; that he may please him who enrolled him as a soldier.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Revelation 3:11; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> And if also a man contend in the games, he is not crowned, except he have contended lawfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; 1 Corinthians 9:7–11; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9"> The husbandman that laboureth must be the first to partake of the fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:5; 1 John 5:20; Luke 24:45"> Consider what I say; for the Lord shall give thee understanding in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Romans 2:16; 1 Corinthians 15:11–20"> Remember Jesus Christ, risen from the dead, of the seed of David, according to my gospel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Acts 28:31; 2 Timothy 1:8"> wherein I suffer hardship unto bonds, as a malefactor; but the word of God is not bound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; Colossians 1:24; 2 Timothy 2:3"> Therefore I endure all things for the elect’s sake, that they also may obtain the salvation which is in Christ Jesus with eternal glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:8; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:5"> Faithful is the saying: For if we died with him, we shall also live with him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Matthew 10:33; Revelation 20:4"> if we endure, we shall also reign with him: if we shall deny him, he also will deny us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 3:3; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> if we are faithless, he abideth faithful; for he cannot deny himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 3:9–11; 2 Timothy 2:23; 2 Timothy 4:1"> Of these things put them in remembrance, charging them in the sight of the Lord, that they strive not about words, to no profit, to the subverting of them that hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; 2 Peter 1:10; 1 Timothy 4:6"> Give diligence to present thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, handling aright the word of truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:14"> But shun profane babblings: for they will proceed further in ungodliness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; Nahum 3:15; James 5:3"> and their word will eat as doth a gangrene: of whom is Hymenaeus and Philetus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:12; 1 Timothy 1:19; Hebrews 3:10"> men who concerning the truth have erred, saying that the resurrection is past already, and overthrow the faith of some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 10:14; 1 Corinthians 8:3; Nahum 1:7"> Howbeit the firm foundation of God standeth, having this seal, The Lord knoweth them that are his: and, Let every one that nameth the name of the Lord depart from unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:21–23; 1 Peter 2:5; 1 Timothy 3:15"> Now in a great house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver, but also of wood and of earth; and some unto honour, and some unto dishonour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:17; Ephesians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> If a man therefore purge himself from these, he shall be a vessel unto honour, sanctified, meet for the master’s use, prepared unto every good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; 1 Peter 3:11; 1 Corinthians 6:18"> But flee youthful lusts, and follow after righteousness, faith, love, peace, with them that call on the Lord out of a pure heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:14; Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 4:7"> But foolish and ignorant questionings refuse, knowing that they gender strifes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Titus 3:2; James 1:19–20; 1 Timothy 3:2–3"> And the Lord’s servant must not strive, but be gentle towards all, apt to teach, forbearing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 11:18; Galatians 6:1; 1 Timothy 2:4"> in meekness correcting them that oppose themselves; if peradventure God may give them repentance unto the knowledge of the truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; Isaiah 49:25–26; Psalms 124:7"> and they may recover themselves out of the snare of the devil, having been taken captive by the Lord’s servant unto the will of God.</span>
