<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 6:37; Romans 2:3; John 8:7–9"> Wherefore thou art without excuse, O man, whosoever thou art that judgest: for wherein thou judgest another, thou condemnest thyself; for thou that judgest dost practise the same things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 9:7–8; Zephaniah 3:5; Psalms 145:17"> And we know that the judgment of God is according to truth against them that practise such things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 16:5; Luke 12:14; Romans 1:32–1"> And reckonest thou this, O man, who judgest them that practise such things, and doest the same, that thou shalt escape the judgment of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Isaiah 30:18; Exodus 34:6"> Or despisest thou the riches of his goodness and forbearance and longsuffering, not knowing that the goodness of God leadeth thee to repentance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Romans 1:18; Romans 2:2–3"> but after thy hardness and impenitent heart treasurest up for thyself wrath in the day of wrath and revelation of the righteous judgment of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 62:12; Jeremiah 32:19; Matthew 16:27"> who will render to every man according to his works:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:7–8; Luke 8:15; 2 Timothy 1:10"> to them that by patience in well-doing seek for glory and honour and incorruption, eternal life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:18–21; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; 1 Timothy 6:3–4"> but unto them that are factious, and obey not the truth, but obey unrighteousness, shall be wrath and indignation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17; Ezekiel 18:4; Romans 1:16"> tribulation and anguish, upon every soul of man that worketh evil, of the Jew first, and also of the Greek;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 32:17; Isaiah 26:12; Numbers 6:26"> but glory and honour and peace to every man that worketh good, to the Jew first, and also to the Greek:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Acts 10:34; 2 Chronicles 19:7"> for there is no respect of persons with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:21; Galatians 3:10; Galatians 2:16–19"> For as many as have sinned without law shall also perish without law: and as many as have sinned under law shall be judged by law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 1:22–25; 1 John 3:7; James 2:21–25"> for not the hearers of a law are just before God, but the doers of a law shall be justified:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 2:12; Romans 1:19–20; Romans 2:27"> for when Gentiles which have no law do by nature the things of the law, these, having no law, are a law unto themselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 1:12; Romans 1:18–19"> in that they shew the work of the law written in their hearts, their conscience bearing witness therewith, and their thoughts one with another accusing or else excusing them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Acts 17:31"> in the day when God shall judge the secrets of men, according to my gospel, by Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 2:23; Micah 3:11; Isaiah 48:1–2"> But if thou bearest the name of a Jew, and restest upon the law, and gloriest in God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Deuteronomy 4:8; James 4:17"> and knowest his will, and approvest the things that are excellent, being instructed out of the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 3:17–18; Matthew 15:14; Isaiah 49:6"> and art confident that thou thyself art a guide of the blind, a light of them that are in darkness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 1:13"> a corrector of the foolish, a teacher of babes, having in the law the form of knowledge and of the truth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:27; Matthew 23:3–28; Luke 12:47"> thou therefore that teachest another, teachest thou not thyself? thou that preachest a man should not steal, dost thou steal?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Malachi 3:8; Matthew 12:39; Mark 11:17"> thou that sayest a man should not commit adultery, dost thou commit adultery? thou that abhorrest idols, dost thou rob temples?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 4:16–17; Romans 2:17; Romans 3:2"> thou who gloriest in the law, through thy transgression of the law dishonourest thou God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:20–23; Isaiah 52:5; 2 Peter 2:2"> For the name of God is blasphemed among the Gentiles because of you, even as it is written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Galatians 5:3–6; Acts 7:51"> For circumcision indeed profiteth, if thou be a doer of the law: but if thou be a transgressor of the law, thy circumcision is become uncircumcision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 2:11; 1 Corinthians 7:18–19; Romans 8:4"> If therefore the uncircumcision keep the ordinances of the law, shall not his uncircumcision be reckoned for circumcision?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 12:41–42; Romans 2:29; 2 Corinthians 3:6"> and shall not the uncircumcision which is by nature, if it fulfill the law, judge thee, who with the letter and circumcision art a transgressor of the law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; Matthew 3:9; Romans 9:6–8"> For he is not a Jew, which is one outwardly; neither is that circumcision, which is outward in the flesh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:6; Philippians 3:3; Deuteronomy 10:16"> but he is a Jew, which is one inwardly; and circumcision is that of the heart, in the spirit, not in the letter; whose praise is not of men, but of God.</span>
