<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Deuteronomy 32:40; Revelation 16:17"> And the angel which I saw standing upon the sea and upon the earth lifted up his right hand to heaven,</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:1–3; Jeremiah 15:16; Ezekiel 2:8"> And I went unto the angel, saying unto him that he should give me the little book. <span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:3; Proverbs 16:24; Psalms 119:103"> And I took the little book out of the angel’s hand, and ate it up; and it was in my mouth sweet as honey: and when I had eaten it, my belly was made bitter.</span>
