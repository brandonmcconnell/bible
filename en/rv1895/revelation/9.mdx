<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 8:10; Romans 10:7; Revelation 9:2"> And the fifth angel sounded, and I saw a star from heaven fallen unto the earth: and there was given to him the key of the pit of the abyss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:10; Genesis 19:28; Joel 2:2"> And he opened the pit of the abyss; and there went up a smoke out of the pit, as the smoke of a great furnace; and the sun and the air were darkened by reason of the smoke of the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Revelation 9:5; Joel 2:25"> And out of the smoke came forth locusts upon the earth; and power was given them, as the scorpions of the earth have power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 7:2–4; Ezekiel 9:4; Revelation 14:1"> And it was said unto them that they should not hurt the grass of the earth, neither any green thing, neither any tree, but only such men as have not the seal of God on their foreheads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 9:10; Job 2:6; Revelation 9:3"> And it was given them that they should not kill them, but that they should be tormented five months: and their torment was as the torment of a scorpion, when it striketh a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 23:30; Jeremiah 8:3; Revelation 6:16"> And in those days men shall seek death, and shall in no wise find it; and they shall desire to die, and death fleeth from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 7:8; Nahum 3:17; Joel 2:4–5"> And the shapes of the locusts were like unto horses prepared for war; and upon their heads as it were crowns like unto gold, and their faces were as men’s faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joel 1:6; 1 Corinthians 11:14–15; 1 Peter 3:3"> And they had hair as the hair of women, and their teeth were as the teeth of lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joel 2:5–8; Nahum 2:4–5; Job 39:25"> And they had breastplates, as it were breastplates of iron; and the sound of their wings was as the sound of chariots, of many horses rushing to war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 9:5; Revelation 9:3; Revelation 9:19"> And they have tails like unto scorpions, and stings; and in their tails is their power to hurt men five months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Revelation 9:1–2; Ephesians 2:2"> They have over them as king the angel of the abyss: his name in Hebrew is Abaddon, and in the Greek tongue he hath the name Apollyon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 11:14; Revelation 8:13–2; Revelation 9:13–21"> The first Woe is past: behold, there come yet two Woes hereafter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 8:3–5; Exodus 30:2–3; Revelation 9:1"> And the sixth angel sounded, and I heard a voice from the horns of the golden altar which is before God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 16:12; Revelation 9:15; 2 Samuel 8:3"> one saying to the sixth angel, which had the trumpet, Loose the four angels which are bound at the great river Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 9:18; Revelation 8:7; Revelation 8:9"> And the four angels were loosed, which had been prepared for the hour and day and month and year, that they should kill the third part of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Psalms 68:17; Daniel 7:10"> And the number of the armies of the horsemen was twice ten thousand times ten thousand: I heard the number of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 9:18; Revelation 14:10; 1 Chronicles 12:8"> And thus I saw the horses in the vision, and them that sat on them, having breastplates as of fire and of hyacinth and of brimstone: and the heads of the horses are as the heads of lions; and out of their mouths proceedeth fire and smoke and brimstone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 9:17; Revelation 9:15"> By these three plagues was the third part of men killed, by the fire and the smoke and the brimstone, which proceeded out of their mouths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 9:15; Revelation 9:10; Ephesians 4:14"> For the power of the horses is in their mouth, and in their tails: for their tails are like unto serpents, and have heads; and with them they do hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Deuteronomy 31:29; 1 Timothy 4:1"> And the rest of mankind, which were not killed with these plagues, repented not of the works of their hands, that they should not worship devils, and the idols of gold, and of silver, and of brass, and of stone, and of wood; which can neither see, nor hear, nor walk:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; 2 Corinthians 12:21; Revelation 17:5"> and they repented not of their murders, nor of their sorceries, nor of their fornication, nor of their thefts.</span>
