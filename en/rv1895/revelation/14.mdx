<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 2:32; Revelation 3:12; Revelation 7:3–17"> And I saw, and behold, the Lamb standing on the mount Zion, and with him a hundred and forty and four thousand, having his name, and the name of his Father, written on their foreheads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:8; Ezekiel 43:2; Revelation 1:15"> And I heard a voice from heaven, as the voice of many waters, and as the voice of a great thunder: and the voice which I heard was as the voice of harpers harping with their harps:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Revelation 14:1; Revelation 15:3"> and they sing as it were a new song before the throne, and before the four living creatures and the elders: and no man could learn the song save the hundred and forty and four thousand, even they that had been purchased out of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 3:4; 2 Corinthians 11:2; James 1:18"> These are they which were not defiled with women; for they are virgins. These are they which follow the Lamb whithersoever he goeth. These were purchased from among men, to be the firstfruits unto God and unto the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:27; Psalms 32:2; Zephaniah 3:13"> And in their mouth was found no lie: they are without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 16:15; Revelation 8:13; Ephesians 3:9–11"> And I saw another angel flying in mid heaven, having an eternal gospel to proclaim unto them that dwell on the earth, and unto every nation and tribe and tongue and people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Revelation 15:4; Nehemiah 9:6"> and he saith with a great voice, Fear God, and give him glory; for the hour of his judgment is come: and worship him that made the heaven and the earth and sea and fountains of waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 21:9; Jeremiah 51:7–8; Revelation 18:2–3"> And another, a second angel, followed, saying, Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great, which hath made all the nations to drink of the wine of the wrath of her fornication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 14:11; Revelation 14:6–8; Revelation 13:3–6"> And another angel, a third, followed them, saying with a great voice, If any man worshippeth the beast and his image, and receiveth a mark on his forehead, or upon his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 51:17; Revelation 16:19; Jeremiah 25:15–17"> he also shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God, which is prepared unmixed in the cup of his anger; and he shall be tormented with fire and brimstone in the presence of the holy angels, and in the presence of the Lamb:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 19:3; Isaiah 34:10; Matthew 25:41"> and the smoke of their torment goeth up for ever and ever; and they have no rest day and night, they that worship the beast and his image, and whoso receiveth the mark of his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 13:10; Revelation 12:17; Revelation 3:10"> Here is the patience of the saints, they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 20:6; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; 1 Thessalonians 5:10"> And I heard a voice from heaven saying, Write, Blessed are the dead which die in the Lord from henceforth: yea, saith the Spirit, that they may rest from their labours; for their works follow with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Isaiah 19:1; Matthew 17:5"> And I saw, and behold, a white cloud; and on the cloud I saw one sitting like unto a son of man, having on his head a golden crown, and in his hand a sharp sickle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Matthew 13:39; Mark 4:29"> And another angel came out from the temple, crying with a great voice to him that sat on the cloud, Send forth thy sickle, and reap: for the hour to reap is come; for the harvest of the earth is over-ripe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Revelation 14:19; Revelation 14:14"> And he that sat on the cloud cast his sickle upon the earth; and the earth was reaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:18; Revelation 14:14–15; Revelation 15:5–6"> And another angel came out from the temple which is in heaven, he also having a sharp sickle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Revelation 16:8; Revelation 14:15–16"> And another angel came out from the altar, he that hath power over fire; and he called with a great voice to him that had the sharp sickle, saying, Send forth thy sharp sickle, and gather the clusters of the vine of the earth; for her grapes are fully ripe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:32–33; Revelation 19:15–21"> And the angel cast his sickle into the earth, and gathered the vintage of the earth, and cast it into the winepress, the great winepress, of the wrath of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 1:15; Revelation 19:14–21; Isaiah 63:1–3"> And the winepress was trodden without the city, and there came out blood from the winepress, even unto the bridles of the horses, as far as a thousand and six hundred furlongs.</span>
