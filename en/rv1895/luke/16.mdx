<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Matthew 25:14–30; James 4:3"> And he said also unto the disciples, There was a certain rich man, which had a steward; and the same was accused unto him that he was wasting his goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:9–10; 1 Peter 4:5; Revelation 20:12"> And he called him, and said unto him, What is this that I hear of thee? render the account of thy stewardship; for thou canst be no longer steward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Isaiah 10:3; Proverbs 18:9"> And the steward said within himself, What shall I do, seeing that my lord taketh away the stewardship from me? I have not strength to dig; to beg I am ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; Jeremiah 4:22; James 3:15"> I am resolved what to do, that, when I am put out of the stewardship, they may receive me into their houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:41–42; Matthew 18:24"> And calling to him each one of his lord’s debtors, he said to the first, How much owest thou unto my lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:10; Luke 16:12; Luke 16:9"> And he said, A hundred measures of oil. And he said unto him, Take thy bond, and sit down quickly and write fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Songs 8:11–12; Luke 20:9; Luke 20:12"> Then said he to another, And how much owest thou? And he said, A hundred measures of wheat. He saith unto him, Take thy bond, and write fourscore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5; Ephesians 5:8; Proverbs 6:6–8"> And his lord commended the unrighteous steward because he had done wisely: for the sons of this world are for their own generation wiser than the sons of the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 19:21; 1 Timothy 6:17–19; Daniel 4:27"> And I say unto you, Make to yourselves friends by means of the mammon of unrighteousness; that, when it shall fail, they may receive you into the eternal tabernacles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 16:11–12; Matthew 25:21; Luke 19:17"> He that is faithful in a very little is faithful also in much: and he that is unrighteous in a very little is unrighteous also in much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 12:33; James 2:5; Proverbs 8:18–19"> If therefore ye have not been faithful in the unrighteous mammon, who will commit to your trust the true riches?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 19:13–26; Matthew 25:14–29; Job 1:21"> And if ye have not been faithful in that which is another’s, who will give you that which is your own?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Matthew 6:24; James 4:4"> No servant can serve two masters: for either he will hate the one, and love the other; or else he will hold to one, and despise the other. Ye cannot serve God and mammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:35; Luke 20:47; Jeremiah 6:13"> And the Pharisees, who were lovers of money, heard all these things; and they scoffed at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Proverbs 21:2; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> And he said unto them, Ye are they that justify yourselves in the sight of men; but God knoweth your hearts: for that which is exalted among men is an abomination in the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 11:9–14; Matthew 21:32; Acts 3:24–25"> The law and the prophets were until John: from that time the gospel of the kingdom of God is preached, and every man entereth violently into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:18; Luke 21:33; 1 Peter 1:25"> But it is easier for heaven and earth to pass away, than for one tittle of the law to fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 19:9; Mark 10:11–12; Matthew 5:31–32"> Every one that putteth away his wife, and marrieth another, committeth adultery: and he that marrieth one that is put away from a husband committeth adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:49; Amos 6:4–6; Luke 18:24–25"> Now there was a certain rich man, and he was clothed in purple and fine linen, faring sumptuously every day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:2; 1 Samuel 2:8; James 2:5"> and a certain beggar named Lazarus was laid at his gate, full of sores,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 15:27; Mark 7:28; John 6:12"> and desiring to be fed with the crumbs that fell from the rich man’s table; yea, even the dogs came and licked his sores.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Psalms 49:16–19; James 1:11"> And it came to pass, that the beggar died, and that he was carried away by the angels into Abraham’s bosom: and the rich man also died, and was buried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Luke 16:28; Matthew 18:9"> And in Hades he lifted up his eyes, being in torments, and seeth Abraham afar off, and Lazarus in his bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 4:10; Revelation 22:1; Revelation 7:16–17"> And he cried and said, Father Abraham, have mercy on me, and send Lazarus, that he may dip the tip of his finger in water, and cool my tongue; for I am in anguish in this flame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 6:24; 1 John 2:15; Psalms 17:14"> But Abraham said, Son, remember that thou in thy lifetime receivedst thy good things, and Lazarus in like manner evil things: but now here he is comforted, and thou art in anguish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 3:36; Psalms 49:14; Psalms 50:22"> And beside all this, between us and you there is a great gulf fixed, that they which would pass from hence to you may not be able, and that none may cross over from thence to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And he said, I pray thee therefore, father, that thou wouldest send him to my father’s house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:40; Psalms 49:12–13; 1 Thessalonians 4:6"> for I have five brethren; that he may testify unto them, lest they also come into this place of torment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 15:21; Luke 24:27; Luke 16:16"> But Abraham saith, They have Moses and the prophets; let them hear them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 16:9–11; Luke 16:24; Luke 13:5"> And he said, Nay, father Abraham: but if one go to them from the dead, they will repent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3; Acts 19:8; John 11:43–53"> And he said unto him, If they hear not Moses and the prophets, neither will they be persuaded, if one rise from the dead.</span>
