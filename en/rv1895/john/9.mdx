<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And as he passed by, he saw a man blind from his birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 13:2; John 9:34; Acts 28:4"> And his disciples asked him, saying, Rabbi, who did sin, this man, or his parents, that he should be born blind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:40; Ecclesiastes 9:1–2"> Jesus answered, Neither did this man sin, nor his parents: but that the works of God should be made manifest in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:35; Ephesians 5:16; John 4:34"> We must work the works of him that sent me, while it is day: the night cometh, when no man can work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; Acts 13:47"> When I am in the world, I am the light of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 8:23; Mark 7:33; Revelation 3:18"> When he had thus spoken, he spat on the ground, and made clay of the spittle, and anointed his eyes with the clay,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5; John 9:11; John 11:37"> and said unto him, Go, wash in the pool of Siloam (which is by interpretation, Sent). He went away therefore, and washed, and came seeing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; 1 Samuel 21:11; Luke 18:35"> The neighbours therefore, and they which saw him aforetime, that he was a beggar, said, Is not this he that sat and begged?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Others said, It is he: others said, No, but he is like him. He said, I am he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 9:26; John 9:21; Ecclesiastes 11:5"> They said therefore unto him, How then were thine eyes opened?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 9:27; Jeremiah 36:17"> He answered, The man that is called Jesus made clay, and anointed mine eyes, and said unto me, Go to Siloam, and wash: so I went away and washed, and I received sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 5:11–13; Exodus 2:18–20; John 7:11"> And they said unto him, Where is he? He saith, I know not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:57; John 11:46–47; John 12:42"> They bring to the Pharisees him that aforetime was blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:9; Luke 13:10–17; Matthew 12:1–14"> Now it was the sabbath on the day when Jesus made the clay, and opened his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:26–27; John 9:10–11"> Again therefore the Pharisees also asked him how he received his sight. And he said unto them, He put clay upon mine eyes, and I washed, and do see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:43; John 10:19; Matthew 12:2"> Some therefore of the Pharisees said, This man is not from God, because he keepeth not the sabbath. But others said, How can a man that is a sinner do such signs? And there was a division among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:19; John 6:14; Acts 3:22–26"> They say therefore unto the blind man again, What sayest thou of him, in that he opened thine eyes? And he said, He is a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 3:15–19; John 5:44; Isaiah 26:11"> The Jews therefore did not believe concerning him, that he had been blind, and had received his sight, until they called the parents of him that had received his sight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:8–9; Acts 4:14; Acts 3:10"> and asked them, saying, Is this your son, who ye say was born blind? how then doth he now see?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> His parents answered and said, We know that this is our son, and that he was born blind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> but how he now seeth, we know not; or who opened his eyes, we know not: ask him; he is of age; he shall speak for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 7:13; John 16:2; Luke 6:22"> These things said his parents, because they feared the Jews: for the Jews had agreed already, that if any man should confess him to be Christ, he should be put out of the synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 9:21"> Therefore said his parents, He is of age; ask him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 7:19; John 9:16; John 8:49"> So they called a second time the man that was blind, and said unto him, Give glory to God: we know that this man is a sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 5:10; John 9:30; John 5:11"> He therefore answered, Whether he be a sinner, I know not: one thing I know, that, whereas I was blind, now I see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> They said therefore unto him, What did he to thee? how opened he thine eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:67; John 9:10–15"> He answered them, I told you even now, and ye did not hear: wherefore would ye hear it again? would ye also become his disciples?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 2:17; John 7:19; Matthew 27:39"> And they reviled him, and said, Thou art his disciple; but we are disciples of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:14; Numbers 12:2–7; Acts 7:35"> We know that God hath spoken unto Moses: but as for this man, we know not whence he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; Psalms 119:18; Isaiah 29:18"> The man answered and said unto them, Why, herein is the marvel, that ye know not whence he is, and yet he opened mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 28:9"> We know that God heareth not sinners: but if any man be a worshipper of God, and do his will, him he heareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Revelation 16:18; Job 20:4"> Since the world began it was never heard that any one opened the eyes of a man born blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 9:16; John 3:2; Acts 5:38–39"> If this man were not from God, he could do nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 9:22; John 9:2; Isaiah 66:5"> They answered and said unto him, Thou wast altogether born in sins, and dost thou teach us? And they cast him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 20:31; 1 John 5:13; John 10:36"> Jesus heard that they had cast him out; and finding him, he said, Dost thou believe on the Son of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Song of Songs 5:9; John 1:38"> He answered and said, And who is he, Lord, that I may believe on him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 4:26; Psalms 25:8–9; John 14:21–23"> Jesus said unto him, Thou hast both seen him, and he it is that speaketh with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; Matthew 28:9; Luke 24:52"> And he said, Lord, I believe. And he worshipped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:19; John 12:46; Acts 26:18"> And Jesus said, For judgment came I into this world, that they which see not may see; and that they which see may become blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Matthew 15:12–14; John 7:47–52"> Those of the Pharisees which were with him heard these things, and said unto him, Are we also blind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; John 15:22–24; 1 John 1:8–10"> Jesus said unto them, If ye were blind, ye would have no sin: but now ye say, We see: your sin remaineth.</span>
