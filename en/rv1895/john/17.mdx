<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 13:31–32; John 17:4–5; Philippians 2:9–11"> These things spake Jesus; and lifting up his eyes to heaven, he said, Father, the hour is come; glorify thy Son, that the Son may glorify thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 10:28–29; John 6:39; Daniel 7:14"> even as thou gavest him authority over all flesh, that whatsoever thou hast given him, to them he should give eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 5:20; 1 John 5:11–12; Jeremiah 9:23–24"> And this is life eternal, that they should know thee the only true God, and him whom thou didst send, even Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 4:34; Acts 20:24; 2 Timothy 4:7"> I glorified thee on the earth, having accomplished the work which thou hast given me to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:1–3; Colossians 1:15–17; John 10:30"> And now, O Father, glorify thou me with thine own self with the glory which I had with thee before the world was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 17:26; John 14:21–24; 1 John 5:20"> I manifested thy name unto the men whom thou gavest me out of the world: thine they were, and thou gavest them to me; and they have kept thy word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 7:16–17; John 16:15; John 14:7–10"> Now they know that all things whatsoever thou hast given me are from thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 17:14; John 15:15; John 16:30"> for the words which thou gavest me I have given unto them; and they received them, and knew of a truth that I came forth from thee, and they believed that thou didst send me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; John 17:2; John 17:6"> I pray for them: I pray not for the world, but for those whom thou hast given me; for they are thine:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 10:30; 1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 5:8–14"> and all things that are mine are thine, and thine are mine: and I am glorified in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 6:9; Proverbs 18:10; John 17:12–18"> And I am no more in the world, and these are in the world, and I come to thee. Holy Father, keep them in thy name which thou hast given me, that they may be one, even as we are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 109:6–19; John 10:27–28; John 18:9"> While I was with them, I kept them in thy name which thou hast given me: and I guarded them, and not one of them perished, but the son of perdition; that the scripture might be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Galatians 5:22; John 15:11"> But now I come to thee; and these things I speak in the world, that they may have my joy fulfilled in themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:16; John 15:18–21; 1 John 5:19–20"> I have given them thy word; and the world hated them, because they are not of the world, even as I am not of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 121:7; 1 John 5:18; Matthew 6:13"> I pray not that thou shouldest take them from the world, but that thou shouldest keep them from the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 17:14"> They are not of the world, even as I am not of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:3; Psalms 119:160; Ephesians 5:26"> Sanctify them in the truth: thy word is truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; John 20:21; John 17:3"> As thou didst send me into the world, even so sent I them into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Titus 2:14; Hebrews 2:11; John 17:17"> And for their sakes I sanctify myself, that they themselves also may be sanctified in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 17:6–11; Acts 2:41; Acts 4:4"> Neither for these only do I pray, but for them also that believe on me through their word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:12; Colossians 3:11–14; John 17:11"> that they may all be one; even as thou, Father, art in me, and I in thee, that they also may be in us: that the world may believe that thou didst send me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:18; John 14:20; Mark 16:17–20"> And the glory which thou hast given me I have given unto them; that they may be one, even as we are one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 17:21; John 16:27; 1 John 3:1"> I in them, and thou in me, that they may be perfected into one; that the world may know that thou didst send me, and lovedst them, even as thou lovedst me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 17:5; John 12:26; John 14:3"> Father, that which thou hast given me, I will that, where I am, they also may be with me; that they may behold my glory, which thou hast given me: for thou lovedst me before the foundation of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 17:11; John 7:29; John 17:8"> O righteous Father, the world knew thee not, but I knew thee; and these knew that thou didst send me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 17:23; John 17:6; John 15:9"> and I made known unto them thy name, and will make it known; that the love wherewith thou lovedst me may be in them, and I in them.</span>
