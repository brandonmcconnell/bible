<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; John 10:8–10; Romans 16:18"> Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that entereth not by the door into the fold of the sheep, but climbeth up some other way, the same is a thief and a robber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 10:14; Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:4"> But he that entereth in by the door is the shepherd of the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:11–16; 2 Timothy 2:19; John 10:4"> To him the porter openeth; and the sheep hear his voice: and he calleth his own sheep by name, and leadeth them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:30; John 10:16; John 12:26"> When he hath put forth all his own, he goeth before them, and the sheep follow him: for they know his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 4:5–6; Colossians 2:6–10; Mark 4:24"> And a stranger will they not follow, but will flee from him: for they know not the voice of strangers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Psalms 82:5; Proverbs 28:5"> This parable spake Jesus unto them: but they understood not what things they were which he spake unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 14:6; Ephesians 2:18; John 10:1"> Jesus therefore said unto them again, Verily, verily, I say unto you, I am the door of the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Jeremiah 23:1; John 10:1"> All that came before me are thieves and robbers: but the sheep did not hear them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 14:6; Ephesians 2:18; John 10:7"> I am the door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved, and shall go in and go out, and shall find pasture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 19:10; John 6:51; John 12:47"> The thief cometh not, but that he may steal, and kill, and destroy: I came that they may have life, and may have it abundantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:14–15; Psalms 23:1; John 15:13"> I am the good shepherd: the good shepherd layeth down his life for the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 20:29; Zechariah 11:16–17; Ezekiel 34:2–6"> He that is a hireling, and not a shepherd, whose own the sheep are not, beholdeth the wolf coming, and leaveth the sheep, and fleeth, and the wolf snatcheth them, and scattereth them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:20; John 12:6; Acts 18:17"> he fleeth because he is a hireling, and careth not for the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 10:27; John 10:11; 2 Timothy 2:19"> I am the good shepherd; and I know mine own, and mine own know me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 1:4; John 10:11; Isaiah 53:10"> even as the Father knoweth me, and I know the Father; and I lay down my life for the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 56:8; John 11:52; 1 Peter 2:25"> And other sheep I have, which are not of this fold: them also I must bring, and they shall hear my voice; and they shall become one flock, one shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 10:18; John 10:11; Hebrews 2:9"> Therefore doth the Father love me, because I lay down my life, that I may take it again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:9; Titus 2:14; John 2:19–21"> No one taketh it away from me, but I lay it down of myself. I have power to lay it down, and I have power to take it again. This commandment received I from my Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:16; 1 Corinthians 3:3; John 7:40–43"> There arose a division again among the Jews because of these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:20; Mark 3:21; John 8:47–48"> And many of them said, He hath a devil, and is mad; why hear ye him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 4:11; Isaiah 35:5–6; Psalms 94:9"> Others said, These are not the sayings of one possessed with a devil. Can a devil open the eyes of the blind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And it was the feast of the dedication at Jerusalem: it was winter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 5:12; Acts 3:11"> and Jesus was walking in the temple in Solomon’s porch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 22:67–70; John 1:19; John 8:25"> The Jews therefore came round about him, and said unto him, How long dost thou hold us in suspense? If thou art the Christ, tell us plainly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 10:38; John 8:58; John 14:11"> Jesus answered them, I told you, and ye believe not: the works that I do in my Father’s name, these bear witness of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:47; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; John 12:37–40"> But ye believe not, because ye are not of my sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 10:16; John 10:14; Revelation 3:20"> My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:39–40; John 6:37; John 3:15–16"> and I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, and no one shall snatch them out of my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:37; John 17:2; John 17:9"> My Father, which hath given them unto me, is greater than all; and no one is able to snatch them out of the Father’s hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:1–2; John 14:9; John 17:21–23"> I and the Father are one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 5:18; John 8:59; 1 Samuel 30:6"> The Jews took up stones again to stone him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 John 3:12; John 5:36; Psalms 35:12"> Jesus answered them, Many good works have I shewed you from the Father; for which of those works do ye stone me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 5:18; Leviticus 24:16; John 10:30"> The Jews answered him, For a good work we stone thee not, but for blasphemy; and because that thou, being a man, makest thyself God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 82:6–7; Exodus 7:1; Psalms 82:1"> Jesus answered them, Is it not written in your law, I said, Ye are gods?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Romans 13:1; Luke 16:17"> If he called them gods, unto whom the word of God came (and the scripture cannot be broken),</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 5:17–18; John 3:17; Jeremiah 1:5"> say ye of him, whom the Father sanctified and sent into the world, Thou blasphemest; because I said, I am the Son of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 15:24; John 10:25; John 12:37–40"> If I do not the works of my Father, believe me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 14:20; John 10:25; John 10:30"> But if I do them, though ye believe not me, believe the works: that ye may know and understand that the Father is in me, and I in the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 7:30; John 8:59; John 7:44"> They sought again to take him: and he went forth out of their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 1:28; John 11:54; John 7:1"> And he went away again beyond Jordan into the place where John was at the first baptizing; and there he abode.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 1:33–34; John 1:29–30; Mark 1:37"> And many came unto him; and they said, John indeed did no sign: but all things whatsoever John spake of this man were true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 4:41; John 2:23; John 7:31"> And many believed on him there.</span>
