<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1; Jeremiah 9:10; Jeremiah 7:29"> Hear ye this word which I take up for a lamentation over you, O house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:17; Lamentations 2:13; Jeremiah 2:27"> The virgin of Israel is fallen; she shall no more rise: she is cast down upon her land; there is none to raise her up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22; Ezekiel 12:16; Isaiah 6:13"> For thus saith the Lord GOD: The city that went forth a thousand shall have an hundred left, and that which went forth an hundred shall have ten left, to the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Zephaniah 2:3; Jeremiah 29:12–13"> For thus saith the LORD unto the house of Israel, Seek ye me, and ye shall live:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Amos 4:4; Amos 8:14"> but seek not Beth-el, nor enter into Gilgal, and pass not to Beer-sheba: for Gilgal shall surely go into captivity, and Beth-el shall come to nought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:11; Isaiah 55:6; Ezekiel 20:47–48"> Seek the LORD, and ye shall live; lest he break out like fire in the house of Joseph, and it devour and there be none to quench it in Beth-el:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 6:12; Amos 5:11–12; Zephaniah 1:6"> Ye who turn judgment to wormwood, and cast down righteousness to the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 4:13; Job 9:9; Amos 9:6"> seek him that maketh the Pleiades and Orion, and turneth the shadow of death into the morning, and maketh the day dark with night; that calleth for the waters of the sea, and poureth them out upon the face of the earth; the LORD is his name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 13:17; Jeremiah 37:10; Micah 5:11"> that bringeth sudden destruction upon the strong, so that destruction cometh upon the fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 29:21; 1 Kings 22:8; John 8:45–47"> They hate him that reproveth in the gate, and they abhor him that speaketh uprightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:30; Micah 6:15; Zephaniah 1:13"> Forasmuch therefore as ye trample upon the poor, and take exactions from him of wheat: ye have built houses of hewn stone, but ye shall not dwell in them; ye have planted pleasant vineyards, but ye shall not drink the wine thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 2:6–7; Amos 5:10; Isaiah 1:23"> For I know how manifold are your transgressions, and how mighty are your sins; ye that afflict the just, that take a bribe, and that turn aside the needy in the gate from their right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:4; 2 Timothy 3:1; Ecclesiastes 3:7"> Therefore he that is prudent shall keep silence in such a time; for it is an evil time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Matthew 6:33; Micah 6:8"> Seek good, and not evil, that ye may live: and so the LORD, the God of hosts, shall be with you, as ye say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:9; 3 John 1:11; Psalms 97:10"> Hate the evil, and love the good, and establish judgment in the gate: it may be that the LORD, the God of hosts, will be gracious unto the remnant of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 1:11; Jeremiah 9:17–20; Jeremiah 9:10"> Therefore thus saith the LORD, the God of hosts, the Lord: Wailing shall be in all the broad ways; and they shall say in all the streets, Alas! alas! and they shall call the husbandman to mourning, and such as are skilful of lamentation to wailing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:12; Jeremiah 48:33; Isaiah 16:10"> And in all vineyards shall be wailing: for I will pass through the midst of thee, saith the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joel 1:15; Joel 2:1–2; Joel 2:31"> Woe unto you that desire the day of the LORD! wherefore would ye have the day of the LORD? it is darkness, and not light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 24:17–18; Jeremiah 15:2–3; Acts 28:4"> As if a man did flee from a lion, and a bear met him; or went into the house and leaned his hand on the wall, and a serpent bit him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Ezekiel 34:12; Zephaniah 1:15"> Shall not the day of the LORD be darkness, and not light? even very dark, and no brightness in it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–16; Proverbs 28:9; Leviticus 26:31"> I hate, I despise your feasts, and I will take no delight in your solemn assemblies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; Amos 4:4–5; Isaiah 66:3"> Yea, though ye offer me your burnt offerings and meal offerings, I will not accept them: neither will I regard the peace offerings of your fat beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Amos 6:5; Amos 8:10; Amos 8:3"> Take thou away from me the noise of thy songs; for I will not hear the melody of thy viols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Jeremiah 22:3; Hosea 6:6"> But let judgment roll down as waters, and righteousness as a mighty stream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 7:42–43; Ezekiel 20:8; Nehemiah 9:18"> Did ye bring unto me sacrifices and offerings in the wilderness forty years, O house of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 23:12–13; 1 Kings 11:33; Leviticus 20:2–5"> Yea, ye have borne Siccuth your king and Chiun your images, the star of your god, which ye made to yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 7:43; 2 Kings 17:6; Amos 4:13"> Therefore will I cause you to go into captivity beyond Damascus, saith the LORD, whose name is the God of hosts.</span>
