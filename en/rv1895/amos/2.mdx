<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:8–9; Ezekiel 25:8–9; Amos 1:3"> Thus saith the LORD: For three transgressions of Moab, yea, for four, I will not turn away the punishment thereof; because he burned the bones of the king of Edom into lime:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:24; Jeremiah 48:41; Amos 1:14"> but I will send a fire upon Moab, and it shall devour the palaces of Kerioth; and Moab shall die with tumult, with shouting, and with the sound of the trumpet:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; Jeremiah 48:7; Jeremiah 48:25"> and I will cut off the judge from the midst thereof, and will slay all the princes thereof with him, saith the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:24; Ezekiel 20:16; Ezekiel 20:13"> Thus saith the LORD: For three transgressions of Judah, yea, for four, I will not turn away the punishment thereof; because they have rejected the law of the LORD, and have not kept his statutes, and their lies have caused them to err, after the which their fathers did walk:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 8:14; Jeremiah 17:27; Jeremiah 21:10"> but I will send a fire upon Judah, and it shall devour the palaces of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 3:3; Micah 3:2–3; Amos 5:11–12"> Thus saith the LORD: For three transgressions of Israel, yea, for four, I will not turn away the punishment thereof; because they have sold the righteous for silver and the needy for a pair of shoes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:12; Isaiah 10:2; Ezekiel 22:11"> that pant after the dust of the earth on the head of the poor, and turn aside the way of the meek: and a man and his father will go unto the same maid, to profane my holy name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:12–17; Exodus 22:26–27; Amos 6:6"> and they lay themselves down beside every altar upon clothes taken in pledge, and in the house of their God they drink the wine of such as have been fined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 18:16; Malachi 4:1; Deuteronomy 3:11"> Yet destroyed I the Amorite before them, whose height was like the height of the cedars, and he was strong as the oaks; yet I destroyed his fruit from above, and his roots from beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:7; Exodus 12:51; Amos 3:1"> Also I brought you up out of the land of Egypt, and led you forty years in the wilderness, to possess the land of the Amorite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:25; Numbers 6:2–3; 1 Kings 20:41"> And I raised up of your sons for prophets, and of your young men for Nazirites. Is it not even thus, O ye children of Israel? saith the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 7:13; Isaiah 30:10; Jeremiah 11:21"> But ye gave the Nazirites wine to drink; and commanded the prophets, saying, Prophesy not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:14; Isaiah 43:24; Ezekiel 16:43"> Behold, I will press you in your place, as a cart presseth that is full of sheaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; Psalms 33:16; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> And flight shall perish from the swift, and the strong shall not strengthen his force, neither shall the mighty deliver himself:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 33:16–17; Ezekiel 39:3"> neither shall he stand that handleth the bow; and he that is swift of foot shall not deliver himself: neither shall he that rideth the horse deliver himself:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:52; Jeremiah 48:41; 2 Kings 7:8–20"> and he that is courageous among the mighty shall flee away naked in that day, saith the LORD.</span>
