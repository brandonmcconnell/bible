<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:1; Acts 18:5"> Paul, and Silvanus, and Timothy, unto the church of the Thessalonians in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ: Grace to you and peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:8–9; Ephesians 1:15–16; Philemon 1:4"> We give thanks to God always for you all, making mention of you in our prayers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:13; Revelation 2:19; Galatians 5:6"> remembering without ceasing your work of faith and labour of love and patience of hope in our Lord Jesus Christ, before our God and Father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:13; Titus 3:4–5; 2 Peter 1:10"> knowing, brethren beloved of God, your election,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–9; Mark 16:20; 1 Corinthians 2:2–5"> how that our gospel came not unto you in word only, but also in power, and in the Holy Ghost, and in much assurance; even as ye know what manner of men we shewed ourselves toward you for your sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:1; 1 Corinthians 4:16; Acts 13:52"> And ye became imitators of us, and of the Lord, having received the word in much affliction, with joy of the Holy Ghost;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Titus 2:7; 1 Peter 5:3; 1 Timothy 4:12"> so that ye became an ensample to all that believe in Macedonia and in Achaia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 1:8; Romans 10:14–18; Isaiah 52:7"> For from you hath sounded forth the word of the Lord, not only in Macedonia and Achaia, but in every place your faith to God-ward is gone forth; so that we need not to speak anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:10; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Galatians 4:8–9"> For they themselves report concerning us what manner of entering in we had unto you; and how ye turned unto God from idols, to serve a living and true God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:9; Isaiah 25:8–9; Romans 5:9–10"> and to wait for his Son from heaven, whom he raised from the dead, even Jesus, which delivereth us from the wrath to come.</span>
