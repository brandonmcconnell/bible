<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Colossians 3:1; Revelation 3:21"> Now in the things which we are saying the chief point is this: We have such a high priest, who sat down on the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 33:7; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Hebrews 9:23–24"> a minister of the sanctuary, and of the true tabernacle, which the Lord pitched, not man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Hebrews 5:1; Hebrews 9:14"> For every high priest is appointed to offer both gifts and sacrifices: wherefore it is necessary that this high priest also have somewhat to offer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11–15; Hebrews 5:1; Numbers 17:12–13"> Now if he were on earth, he would not be a priest at all, seeing there are those who offer the gifts according to the law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Colossians 2:17; Hebrews 10:1"> who serve that which is a copy and shadow of the heavenly things, even as Moses is warned of God when he is about to make the tabernacle: for, See, saith he, that thou make all things according to the pattern that was shewed thee in the mount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 7:22; Galatians 3:16–21; Hebrews 12:24"> But now hath he obtained a ministry the more excellent, by how much also he is the mediator of a better covenant, which hath been enacted upon better promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11; Galatians 3:21; Hebrews 7:18"> For if that first covenant had been faultless, then would no place have been sought for a second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Hebrews 10:16–17; Hebrews 12:24"> For finding fault with them, he saith, Behold, the days come, saith the Lord, That I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; Galatians 4:24; Mark 8:23"> Not according to the covenant that I made with their fathers In the day that I took them by the hand to lead them forth out of the land of Egypt; For they continued not in my covenant, And I regarded them not, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26–28; Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 11:19–20"> For this is the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel After those days, saith the Lord; I will put my laws into their mind, And on their heart also will I write them: And I will be to them a God, And they shall be to me a people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Isaiah 54:13; John 6:45"> And they shall not teach every man his fellow-citizen, And every man his brother, saying, Know the Lord: For all shall know me, From the least to the greatest of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:16–17; Isaiah 43:25; Romans 11:27"> For I will be merciful to their iniquities, And their sins will I remember no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Hebrews 7:18–19; Hebrews 7:11–12"> In that he saith, A new covenant, he hath made the first old. But that which is becoming old and waxeth aged is nigh unto vanishing away.</span>
