<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; 2 Corinthians 1:1; 1 Corinthians 1:1–2"> Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through the will of God, to the saints which are at Ephesus, and the faithful in Christ Jesus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Galatians 1:3; Titus 1:4"> Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; 2 Corinthians 1:3; Ephesians 2:6"> Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who hath blessed us with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places in Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 8:28–30; Ephesians 2:10"> even as he chose us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blemish before him in love:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 8:29–30; Ephesians 1:11; Galatians 4:5–6"> having foreordained us unto adoption as sons through Jesus Christ unto himself, according to the good pleasure of his will,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 3:22–26; Ephesians 1:12; Colossians 1:13"> to the praise of the glory of his grace, which he freely bestowed on us in the Beloved:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Romans 3:24; 1 John 1:7–9"> in whom we have our redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of our trespasses, according to the riches of his grace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 5:12; Ephesians 3:10; Romans 5:15"> which he made to abound toward us in all wisdom and prudence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:26–28; Romans 16:25–27; Ephesians 3:3–9"> having made known unto us the mystery of his will, according to his good pleasure which he purposed in him</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 1:20; Philippians 2:9–10; Galatians 4:4"> unto a dispensation of the fulness of the times, to sum up all things in Christ, the things in the heavens, and the things upon the earth; in him, I say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:28; 1 Peter 1:4; Isaiah 46:10–11"> in whom also we were made a heritage, having been foreordained according to the purpose of him who worketh all things after the counsel of his will;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 1:6; Jeremiah 17:5–7; Ephesians 1:13–14"> to the end that we should be unto the praise of his glory, we who had before hoped in Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:22; Ephesians 4:30; 2 Timothy 2:19"> in whom ye also, having heard the word of the truth, the gospel of your salvation,—in whom, having also believed, ye were sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:22; 2 Corinthians 5:5; Ephesians 4:30"> which is an earnest of our inheritance, unto the redemption of God’s own possession, unto the praise of his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philemon 1:5; Colossians 1:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> For this cause I also, having heard of the faith in the Lord Jesus which is among you, and which ye shew toward all the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 1:8–9; Colossians 1:9; Colossians 1:3"> cease not to give thanks for you, making mention of you in my prayers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; James 3:17–18; Colossians 1:9–10"> that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give unto you a spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:18; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Luke 24:45"> having the eyes of your heart enlightened, that ye may know what is the hope of his calling, what the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Ephesians 3:20; Colossians 2:12"> and what the exceeding greatness of his power to us-ward who believe, according to that working of the strength of his might</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 10:12; Mark 16:19; Psalms 110:1"> which he wrought in Christ, when he raised him from the dead, and made him to sit at his right hand in the heavenly places,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:9–11; Colossians 2:10; Colossians 1:15–16"> far above all rule, and authority, and power, and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this world, but also in that which is to come:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 1:18; Colossians 2:10; 1 Corinthians 15:25–27"> and he put all things in subjection under his feet, and gave him to be head over all things to the church,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:18–19; John 1:16; Colossians 2:9–10"> which is his body, the fulness of him that filleth all in all.</span>
