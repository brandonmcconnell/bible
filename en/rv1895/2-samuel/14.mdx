<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:39; 2 Samuel 2:18; 2 Samuel 18:33"> Now Joab the son of Zeruiah perceived that the king’s heart was toward Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:6; Amos 1:1; Ruth 3:3"> And Joab sent to Tekoa, and fetched thence a wise woman, and said unto her, I pray thee, feign thyself to be a mourner, and put on mourning apparel, I pray thee, and anoint not thyself with oil, but be as a woman that had a long time mourned for the dead:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:19; Exodus 4:15; Isaiah 51:16"> and go in to the king, and speak on this manner unto him. So Joab put the words in her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:2; 2 Kings 6:26–28; 1 Samuel 25:23"> And when the woman of Tekoa spake to the king, she fell on her face to the ground, and did obeisance, and said, Help, O king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; 2 Samuel 12:1–3"> And the king said unto her, What aileth thee? And she answered; Of a truth I am a widow woman, and mine husband is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 4:8; Deuteronomy 22:26–27; Exodus 2:13"> And thy handmaid had two sons, and they two strove together in the field, and there was none to part them, but the one smote the other, and killed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 35:19; Matthew 21:38; 2 Samuel 12:17"> And, behold, the whole family is risen against thine handmaid, and they said, Deliver him that smote his brother, that we may kill him for the life of his brother whom he slew, and so destroy the heir also: thus shall they quench my coal which is left, and shall leave to my husband neither name nor remainder upon the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Isaiah 11:3–4; Job 29:16"> And the king said unto the woman, Go to thine house, and I will give charge concerning thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:24; 1 Kings 2:33; Matthew 27:25"> And the woman of Tekoa said unto the king, My lord, O king, the iniquity be on me, and on my father’s house: and the king and his throne be guiltless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the king said, Whosoever saith aught unto thee, bring him to me, and he shall not touch thee any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:45; Matthew 10:30; Numbers 35:19"> Then said she, I pray thee, let the king remember the LORD thy God, that the avenger of blood destroy not any more, lest they destroy my son. And he said, As the LORD liveth, there shalt not one hair of thy son fall to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 18:27; 1 Samuel 25:24; Acts 26:1"> Then the woman said, Let thine handmaid, I pray thee, speak a word unto my lord the king. And he said, Say on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:37–38; 2 Samuel 12:7; Judges 20:2"> And the woman said, Wherefore then hast thou devised such a thing against the people of God? for in speaking this word the king is as one which is guilty, in that the king doth not fetch home again his banished one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 30:23; Numbers 35:28; Hebrews 9:27"> For we must needs die, and are as water spilt on the ground, which cannot be gathered up again; neither doth God take away life, but deviseth means, that he that is banished be not an outcast from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Now therefore seeing that I am come to speak this word unto my lord the king, it is because the people have made me afraid: and thy handmaid said, I will now speak unto the king; it may be that the king will perform the request of his servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:19"> For the king will hear to deliver his servant out of the hand of the man that would destroy me and my son together out of the inheritance of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:9; 2 Samuel 19:27; 2 Samuel 14:20"> Then thine handmaid said, Let, I pray thee, the word of my lord the king be comfortable: for as an angel of God, so is my lord the king to discern good and bad: and the LORD thy God be with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:25; 1 Samuel 3:17–18; Jeremiah 38:14"> Then the king answered and said unto the woman, Hide not from me, I pray thee, aught that I shall ask thee. And the woman said, Let my lord the king now speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:3; 1 Samuel 1:26; 1 Samuel 20:3"> And the king said, Is the hand of Joab with thee in all this? And the woman answered and said, As thy soul liveth, my lord the king, none can turn to the right hand or to the left from aught that my lord the king hath spoken: for thy servant Joab, he bade me, and he put all these words in the mouth of thine handmaid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:17; 2 Samuel 19:27; Job 32:21–22"> to change the face of the matter hath thy servant Joab done this thing: and my lord is wise, according to the wisdom of an angel of God, to know all things that are in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:11; 1 Samuel 14:39; Mark 6:26"> And the king said unto Joab, Behold now, I have done this thing: go therefore, bring the young man Absalom again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ruth 2:2; Job 31:20; Job 29:11"> And Joab fell to the ground on his face, and did obeisance, and blessed the king: end Joab said, Today thy servant knoweth that I have found grace in thy sight, my lord, O king, in that the king hath performed the request of his servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:3; 2 Samuel 13:37–38"> So Joab arose and went to Geshur, and brought Absalom to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 22:4; Genesis 43:3; 2 Samuel 3:13"> And the king said, Let him turn to his own house, but let him not see my face. So Absalom turned to his own house, and saw not the king’s face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 1:6; Deuteronomy 28:35; Job 2:7"> Now in all Israel there was none to be so much praised as Absalom for his beauty: from the sole of his foot even to the crown of his head there was no blemish in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:20; 1 Corinthians 11:14; 2 Samuel 18:9"> And when he polled his head, (now it was at every year’s end that he polled it: because the hair was heavy on him, therefore he polled it:) he weighed the hair of his head at two hundred shekels, after the king’s weight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:18; 2 Samuel 13:1; Isaiah 14:22"> And unto Absalom there were born three sons, and one daughter, whose name was Tamar: she was a woman of a fair countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Absalom dwelt two full years in Jerusalem; and he saw not the king’s face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:30–31; Esther 1:12; Matthew 22:3"> Then Absalom sent for Joab, to send him to the king; but he would not come to him: and he sent again a second time, but he would not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 15:4–5; 2 Kings 10:6–7; 2 Kings 9:33"> Therefore he said unto his servants, See, Joab’s field is near mine, and he hath barley there; go and set it on fire. And Absalom’s servants set the field on fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Then Joab arose, and came to Absalom unto his house, and said unto him, Wherefore have thy servants set my field on fire?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:8; Exodus 14:12; Proverbs 28:13"> And Absalom answered Joab, Behold, I sent unto thee, saying, Come hither, that I may send thee to the king, to say, Wherefore am I come from Geshur? it were better for me to be there still: now therefore let me see the king’s face; and if there be iniquity in me, let him kill me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 33:4; Luke 15:20; Genesis 27:26"> So Joab came to the king, and told him: and when he had called for Absalom, he came to the king, and bowed himself on his face to the ground before the king: and the king kissed Absalom.</span>
