<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> It is also found in the records, that Jeremiah the prophet commanded them that were carried away to take of the fire, as hath been signified above:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and how that the prophet charged them that were carried away, having given them the law, that they should not forget the statutes of the Lord, neither be led astray in their minds, when they saw images of gold and silver, and the adornment thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And with other such words exhorted he them, that the law should not depart from their heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And it was contained in the writing, that the prophet, being warned of God, commanded that the tabernacle and the ark should follow with him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. and when. The Greek text here is probably corrupt.">a</a></sup>when he went forth into the mountain where Moses went up and beheld the heritage of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Jeremiah came and found<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. a cavernous chamber.">b</a></sup>a chamber in the rock, and there he brought in the tabernacle, and the ark, and the altar of incense; and he made fast the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And some of those that followed with him came there that they might mark the way, and could not find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> But when Jeremiah perceived it, he blamed them, saying, Yea and the place shall be unknown until God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. gather together a gathering of the people.">c</a></sup>gather the people again together, and mercy come:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and then shall the Lord disclose these things, and the glory of the Lord shall be seen, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, cloud, as...temple. As Moses">d</a></sup>cloud. As also it was shewed with Moses; as also Solomon besought that the place might be consecrated greatly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and it was also declared that he, having wisdom, offered a sacrifice of dedication, and of the finishing of the temple; so we would have it now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> As Moses prayed unto the Lord, and fire came down out of heaven and consumed the sacrifice, even so prayed Solomon also, and the fire came down and consumed the burnt offerings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Lev. x. 16 and ix. 24.">e</a></sup>(and Moses said, Because the sin offering had not been eaten, it was consumed in like manner with the rest;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and Solomon kept the eight days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the same things were related<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, also">f</a></sup>both in the public archives and in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or, Nehemiah’s records">g</a></sup>the records that concern Nehemiah; and how he, founding a library, gathered together the books about the kings and prophets, and the books of David, and letters of kings about sacred gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And in like manner Judas also gathered together for us all those writings that had been scattered by reason of the war that befell, and they are still with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> If therefore ye have need thereof, send some to fetch them unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Seeing then that we are about to keep the purification, we write unto you; ye will therefore do well if ye keep the days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Now God, who saved all his people, and restored the heritage to all, and the kingdom, and the priesthood, and the hallowing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> even as he promised through the law,— in God have we hope, that he will quickly have mercy upon us, and gather us together out of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. the earth under heaven.">h</a></sup>all the earth unto the holy place: for he delivered us out of great evils, and purified the place.<br />&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Now the things concerning Judas Maccabaeus and his brethren, and the purification of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. greatest.">i</a></sup>great temple, and the dedication of the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> and further the wars against Antiochus Epiphanes, and Eupator his son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and the manifestations that came from heaven unto those that vied with one another in manful deeds for the religion of the Jews; so that, being but a few, they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. took for a prey.">j</a></sup>rescued the whole country, and chased the barbarous multitudes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and recovered again the temple renowned all the world over, and freed the city, and restored the laws which were like to be overthrown, seeing the Lord became<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. propitious.">k</a></sup>gracious unto them with all forbearance:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> these things, I say, which have been declared by Jason of Cyrene in five books, we will assay to abridge in one work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> For having in view the confused mass of the numbers, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or, weariness">l</a></sup>difficulty which awaiteth them that would enter into the narratives of the history, by reason of the abundance of the matter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> we were careful that they who choose to read may be attracted, and that they who wish well to our cause may find it easy to recall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or, the past">m</a></sup>what we have written, and that all readers may have profit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And although to us, who have taken upon us the painful labour of the abridgement, the task is not easy, but a matter of sweat and watching</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> (even as it is no light thing unto him that prepareth a banquet, and seeketh the benefit of others); yet for the sake of the gratitude of the many we will gladly endure the painful labour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> leaving to the historian the exact handling of every particular, and again<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or, making no effort">n</a></sup>having no strength to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or, enlarge on">o</a></sup>fill in the outlines of our abridgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> For as the masterbuilder of a new house must care for the whole<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Gr. foundation.">p</a></sup>structure, and again he that undertaketh to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Gr. decorate in encaustic.">q</a></sup>decorate and paint it must seek out the things fit for the adorning thereof; even so I think it is also with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> To occupy the ground, and to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or, provide a place for discussions">r</a></sup>indulge in long discussions, and to be curious in particulars, becometh the first author of the history:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> but to strive after brevity of expression, and to avoid a laboured fulness in the treatment, is to be granted to him that would bring a writing into a new form.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Here then let us begin the narration, only adding thus much to that which hath been already<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or, spoken of">s</a></sup>said; for it is a foolish thing to make a long prologue to the history, and to abridge the history itself.</span>
</div>
