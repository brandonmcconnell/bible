<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And it came to pass that seven brethren also with their mother were at the king’s command taken and shamefully handled with scourges and cords, to compel them to taste of the abominable swine’s flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> But one of them made himself the spokesman and said, What wouldest thou ask and learn of us? for we are ready to die rather than transgress the laws of our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And the king fell into a rage, and commanded to heat pans and caldrons:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and when these forthwith were heated, he commanded to cut out the tongue of him that had been their spokesman, and to scalp him, and to cut off his extremities, the rest of his brethren and his mother looking on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And when he was utterly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. useless.">a</a></sup>maimed, the king commanded to bring him to the fire, being yet alive, and to fry him in the pan. And as the vapour of the pan spread far, they and their mother also exhorted one another to die nobly, saying thus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The Lord God beholdeth, and in truth is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, comforted in">b</a></sup>intreated for us, as Moses declared in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Deut. xxxi. 21 and xxxii. 36.">c</a></sup>his song, which witnesseth against the people to their faces, saying, And he shall be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, comforted in">d</a></sup>intreated for his servants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And when the first had died after this manner, they brought the second to the mocking; and they pulled off the skin of his head with the hair and asked him, Wilt thou eat, before thy body be punished in every limb?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> But he answered in the language of his fathers and said to them, No. Wherefore he also underwent the next torture in succession, as the first had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And when he was at the last gasp, he said, Thou, miscreant, dost release us out of this present life, but the King of the world shall raise up us, who have died for his laws, unto an eternal renewal of life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And after him was the third made a mocking-stock. And when he was required, he quickly put out his tongue, and stretched forth his hands courageously,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and nobly said, From heaven I possess these; and for his laws’ sake I contemn these; and from him I hope to receive these back again:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> insomuch that the king himself and they that were with him were astonished at the young man’s soul, for that he nothing regarded the pains.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And when he too was dead, they shamefully handled and tortured the fourth in like manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And being come near unto death he said thus: It is good to die at the hands of men and look for the hopes which are given by God, that we shall be raised up again by him; for as for thee, thou shalt have no resurrection unto life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And next after him they brought the fifth, and shamefully handled him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> But he looked toward<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. him.">e</a></sup>the king and said, Because thou hast authority among men, though thou art thyself corruptible, thou doest what thou wilt; yet think not that our race hath been forsaken of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> but hold thou on thy way, and behold his sovereign majesty, how it will torture thee and thy seed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And after him they brought the sixth. And when he was at the point to die he said, Be not vainly deceived, for we suffer these things for our own doings, as sinning against our own God: marvellous things are come to pass;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> but think not thou that thou shalt be unpunished, having assayed to fight against God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But above all was the mother marvellous and worthy of honourable memory; for when she looked on seven sons perishing within the space of one day, she bare the sight with a good courage for the hopes that she had set on the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And she exhorted each one of them in the language of their fathers, filled with a noble temper and stirring up her womanish thought with manly passion, saying unto them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> I know not how ye came into my womb, neither was it I that bestowed on you your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, breath">f</a></sup>spirit and your life, and it was not I that brought into order the first elements of each one of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Therefore the Creator of the world, who fashioned the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or, first origin">g</a></sup>generation of man and devised the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or, first origin">h</a></sup>generation of all things, in mercy giveth back to you again both your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or, breath">i</a></sup>spirit and your life, as ye now contemn your own selves for his laws’ sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> But Antiochus, thinking himself to be despised, and suspecting the reproachful voice, whilst the youngest was yet alive did not only make his appeal to him by words, but also at the same time promised with oaths that he would enrich him and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. make him one that is counted happy.">j</a></sup>raise him to high estate, if he would turn from the customs of his fathers, and that he would take him for his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="See ch. viii. 9.">k</a></sup>Friend and intrust him with affairs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> But when the young man would in no wise give heed, the king called unto him his mother, and exhorted her that she would counsel the lad to save himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And when he had exhorted her with many words, she undertook to persuade her son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> But bending toward him, laughing the cruel tyrant to scorn, she spoke thus in the language of her fathers: My son, have pity upon me that carried thee nine months in my womb, and gave thee suck three years, and nourished and brought thee up unto this age, and sustained thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> I beseech thee, my child, to lift thine eyes unto the heaven and the earth, and to see all things that are therein, and thus to recognise that God made them not of things that were, and that the race of men in this wise cometh into being.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Fear not this butcher, but, proving thyself worthy of thy brethren, accept thy death, that in the mercy of God I may receive thee again with thy brethren.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> But before she had yet ended speaking, the young man said, Whom wait ye for? I obey not the commandment of the king, but I hearken to the commandment of the law that was given to our fathers through Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> But thou, that hast devised all manner of evil against the Hebrews, shalt in no wise escape the hands of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> For we are suffering because of our own sins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and if for rebuke and chastening our living Lord hath been angered a little while, yet shall he again be reconciled with his own servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> But thou, O unholy man and of all most vile, be not vainly lifted up in thy wild pride with uncertain hopes, raising thy hand against the heavenly children;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> For not yet hast thou escaped the judgement of the Almighty God that seeth all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> For these our brethren, having endured a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. short pain of ever-flowing life.">l</a></sup>short pain that bringeth everlasting life, have now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. fallen. By the omission of one Greek letter the words would signify having endured a short pain, have now drunk of ever-flowing life under God’s covenant.">m</a></sup>died under God’s covenant; But thou, through the judgement of God, shalt receive in just measure the penalties of thine arrogancy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> But I, as my brethren, give up both body and soul for the laws of our fathers, calling upon God that he may speedily become<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. propitious.">n</a></sup>gracious to the nation; and that thou amidst trials and plagues mayest confess that he alone is God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and that in me and my brethren<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Some authorities read may be stayed.">o</a></sup>thou mayest stay the wrath of the Almighty, which hath been justly brought upon our whole race.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> But the king, falling into a rage, handled him worse than all the rest, being exasperated at his mocking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> So he also died pure from pollution, putting his whole trust in the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And last of all after her sons the mother died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Let it then suffice to have said thus much concerning the enforcement of sacrificial feasts and the king’s exceeding barbarities.</span>
</div>
</div>
