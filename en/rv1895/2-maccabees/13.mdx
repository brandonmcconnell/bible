<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> In the hundred forty and ninth year tidings were brought to Judas and his company that Antiochus Eupator was coming with great multitudes against Judaea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and with him Lysias his guardian and chancellor,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Greek text here is corrupt.">a</a></sup>each having a Greek force, a hundred and ten thousand footmen, and five thousand and three hundred horsemen, and two and twenty elephants, and three hundred chariots armed with scythes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And Menelaus also joined himself with them, and with great dissimulation encouraged Antiochus, not for the saving of his country, but because he thought that he would be set over the government.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But the King of kings stirred up the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, spirit">b</a></sup>passion of Antiochus against the wicked wretch; and when Lysias informed him that this man was the cause of all the evils, the king commanded to bring him unto Beroea, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Greek text here is perhaps corrupt.">c</a></sup>to put him to death after the manner of that place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Now there is in that place a tower of fifty cubits high, full of ashes, and it had all round it a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. contrivance or machine.">d</a></sup>gallery<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, sloping steeply">e</a></sup>descending sheer on every side into the ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Here him that is guilty of sacrilege, or hath attained a preeminence in any other evil deeds, they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The Greek text here is probably corrupt.">f</a></sup>all push forward into destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> By such a fate it befell the breaker of the law, Menelaus, to die, without obtaining so much as a grave in the earth, and that right justly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> for inasmuch as he had perpetrated many sins<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. about.">g</a></sup>against the altar, whose fire and whose ashes were holy, in ashes did he receive his death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Now the king,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some authorities read indignant.">h</a></sup>infuriated in spirit, was coming with intent to inflict on the Jews the very worst of the sufferings that had befallen them in his father’s time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But when Judas heard of these things, he gave charge to the multitude to call upon the Lord day and night, beseeching him, if ever at any other time, so now to succour them that were at the point to be deprived of the law and their country and the holy temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and not to suffer the people that had been but now a little while revived to fall into the hands of those profane heathen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> So when they had all done the same thing together,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. and besought.">i</a></sup>beseeching the merciful Lord with weeping and fastings and prostration for three days without ceasing, Judas exhorted them and commanded they should join him for service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And having gone apart with the elders he resolved that, before the king’s army should enter into Judaea and make themselves masters of the city, they should go forth and try the matter in fight by the help of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Some authorities read the Lord.">j</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And committing the decision to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Some authorities read Creator.">k</a></sup>Lord of the world, and exhorting them that were with him to contend nobly even unto death for laws, temple, city, country,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or, mode of life">l</a></sup>commonwealth, he pitched his camp by Modin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And given out to his men the watchword, VICTORY IS GOD’S, with a chosen body of the bravest young men he fell upon the camp by night and penetrated to the king’s<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. court.">m</a></sup>tent, and slew<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="The Greek text here is probably corrupt.">n</a></sup>of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Gr. camp.">o</a></sup>army as many as two thousand men, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="The Greek text here is probably corrupt.">p</a></sup>brought down the chiefest elephant with him that was in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Gr. house.">q</a></sup>tower upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And at last they filled the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Gr. camp.">r</a></sup>army with terror and alarm, and departed with good success.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And this had been accomplished when the day was but now dawning, because of the Lord’s protection that gave<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Gr. him.">s</a></sup>Judas help.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But the king, having had a taste of the exceeding boldness of the Jews, made attempts by stratagem upon their positions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and upon a strong fortress of the Jews at Bethsura; he advanced, was turned back, failed, was defeated,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Judas conveyed such things as were necessary unto them that were within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But Rhodocus, from the Jewish ranks, made known to the enemy the secrets of his countrymen. He was sought out, and taken, and shut up in prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The king treated with them in Bethsura the second time, gave his hand, took theirs, departed, attacked the forces of Judas, was put to the worse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> heard that Philip who had been left as chancellor in Antioch had become reckless, was confounded, made to the Jews an overture of peace, submitted himself and sware to acknowledge all their rights, came to terms with them and offered sacrifice, honoured the sanctuary and the place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> shewed kindness and graciously received Maccabaeus, left Hegemonides governor from Ptolemais even unto the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="The form of this word is uncertain. Compare Girzites (or Gizrites), 1 Sam. xxvii. 8. One manuscript reads Gerarenes.">t</a></sup>Gerrenians,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> came to Ptolemais. The men of Ptolemais were displeased at the treaty, for they had exceeding great indignation against the Jews: they desired to annul the articles of the agreement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Lysias<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Gr. came forward to the tribune or judgement seat.">u</a></sup>came forward to speak, made the best defence that was possible, persuaded, pacified, made them well affected, departed unto Antioch. This was the issue of the inroad and departure of the king.</span>
</div>
