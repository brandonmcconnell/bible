<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Now after a very little time Lysias, the king’s guardian and kinsman and chancellor, being sore displeased for the things that had come to pass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> collected about fourscore thousand footmen and all his horsemen and came against the Jews, thinking to make the city a place for Greeks to dwell in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and to levy tribute on the temple, as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, on all the sacred places of the heathen">a</a></sup>on the other sacred places of the nations, and to put up the high priesthood to sale every year;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> holding in no account the might of God, but puffed up with his ten thousands of footmen, and his thousands of horsemen, and his fourscore elephants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And coming into Judaea and drawing near to Bethsuron, which was a strong place and distant from Jerusalem about<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Greek text here is uncertain.">b</a></sup>five leagues, he pressed it hard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But when Maccabaeus and his men learned that he was besieging the strongholds, they and all the people with lamentations and tears made supplication unto the Lord to send a good angel to save Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Maccabaeus himself took up arms first, and exhorted the others to jeopard themselves together with him and succour their brethren; and they sallied forth with him right willingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And as they were there, close to Jerusalem, there appeared at their head one on horseback in white apparel, brandishing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. a panoply.">c</a></sup>weapons of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And they all together praised the merciful God, and were yet more strengthened in heart: being ready to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. wound.">d</a></sup>assail not men only but the wildest beasts, and walls of iron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> they advanced in array, having him that is in heaven to fight on their side, for the Lord had mercy on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And hurling themselves like lions upon the enemy, they slew of them eleven thousand footmen and sixteen hundred horsemen, and forced all the rest to flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> But the more part of them escaped wounded and naked; and Lysias also himself escaped by shameful flight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> But as he was a man not void of understanding, weighing with himself the defeat which had befallen him, and considering that the Hebrews could not be overcome, because the Almighty God fought on their side, he sent again unto them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and persuaded them to come to terms on condition that all their rights were acknowledged, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The Greek text here is corrupt.">e</a></sup>promised that he would also persuade the king to become their friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Maccabaeus gave consent upon all the conditions which Lysias proposed to him, being careful of the common good; for whatsoever requests Maccabaeus delivered in writing unto Lysias concerning the Jews the king allowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For the letters written unto the Jews from Lysias were to this effect:<br />Lysias unto the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. multitude.">f</a></sup>people of the Jews, greeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> John and Absalom, who were sent from you, having delivered the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or, document">g</a></sup>petition written below, made request concerning the things signified therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> What things soever therefore had need to be brought before the king I declared to him, and what things were possible he allowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> If then ye will preserve your good will toward the state, henceforward also will I endeavour to contribute to your good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or, but as to this.">h</a></sup>And on this behalf I have given order in detail, both to these men and to those that are sent from me, to confer with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Fare ye well. Written in the hundred forty and eighth year, on the four and twentieth day of the month<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="This name is not found elsewhere, and is perhaps corrupt.">i</a></sup>Dioscorinthius.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the king’s letter was in these words:<br />King Antiochus unto his brother Lysias, greeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Seeing that our father passed unto the gods having the wish that the subjects of his kingdom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or, should not be disquieted but">j</a></sup>should be undisturbed and give themselves to the care of their own affairs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> we, having heard that the Jews do not consent to our father’s purpose to turn them unto the customs of the Greeks, but choose rather their own manner of living, and make request that the customs of their law be allowed unto them,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> choosing therefore that this nation also should be free from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or, disquiet">k</a></sup>disturbance, we determine that their temple be restored to them, and that they live according to the customs that were in the days of their ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Thou wilt therefore do well to send messengers unto them and give them the right hand of friendship, that they, knowing our mind, may be of good heart, and gladly occupy themselves with the conduct of their own affairs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And unto the nation the king’s letter was after this manner:<br />King Antiochus to the senate of the Jews and to the other Jews, greeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> If ye fare well, we have our desire: we ourselves also are in good health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Menelaus informed us that your desire was to return home and follow your own business.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> They therefore that depart home up to the thirtieth day of Xanthicus shall have our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. right hand.">l</a></sup>friendship, with full permission</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> that the Jews use their own proper meats and observe their own laws, even as heretofore; and none of them shall be in any way molested for the things that have been ignorantly done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Moreover I have sent Menelaus also, that he may encourage you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Fare ye well. Written in the hundred forty and eighth year, on the fifteenth day of Xanthicus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And the Romans also sent unto them a letter in these words:<br />Quintus Memmius and Titus Manius, ambassadors of the Romans, unto the people of the Jews, greeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> In regard to the things which Lysias the king’s kinsman granted you, we also give consent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> But as for the things which he judged should be referred to the king, send one forthwith, after ye have advised thereof, that we may publish such decrees as befit your case; for we are on our way to Antioch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Wherefore send some with speed, that we also may learn what is your mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. Be in good health.">m</a></sup>Farewell. Written in the hundred forty and eighth year, on the fifteenth day of Xanthicus.</span>
</div>
