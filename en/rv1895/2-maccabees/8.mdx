<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> But Judas, who is also called Maccabaeus, and they that were with him, making their way privily into the villages, called unto them their kinsfolk; and taking unto them such as had continued in the Jews’ religion, gathered together as many as six thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And they called upon the Lord, beseeching him to look upon the people that was oppressed by all; and to have compassion on the sanctuary also that had been profaned by the ungodly men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and to have pity on the city also that was suffering ruin and ready to be made even with the ground; and to hearken to the blood that cried unto him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and to remember also the lawless<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. destruction.">a</a></sup>slaughter of the innocent infants, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. concerning the blasphemies.">b</a></sup>the blasphemies that had been committed against his name; and to shew his hatred of wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And when Maccabaeus had trained his men for service, the heathen at once found him irresistible, for that the wrath of the Lord was turned into pity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Greek text of verses 6 and 7 is uncertain.">c</a></sup>And coming unawares he set fire to cities and villages. And in winning back the most important positions, putting to flight no small number of the enemies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> he specially took advantage of the nights for such assaults. And his courage was loudly talked of everywhere.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> But when Philip saw the man gaining ground by little and little, and increasing more and more in his prosperity, he wrote unto Ptolemy, the governor of Coelesyria and Phoenicia, that he should support the king’s cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Ptolemy quickly appointed Nicanor the son of Patroclus, one of the king’s<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See 1 Macc. x. 65. Compare ch. i. 14; vii. 24; x. 13; xiv. 11; 1 Macc. ii. 18.">d</a></sup>Chief Friends, and sent him, in command of no fewer than twenty thousand of all nations, to destroy the whole race of Judaea; and with him he joined Gorgias also, a captain and one that had experience in matters of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Nicanor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, resolved">e</a></sup>undertook by the sale of the captive Jews to make up for the king the tribute of two thousand talents which he was to pay to the Romans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And immediately he sent unto the cities upon the sea coast, inviting them to buy Jewish<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. bodies.">f</a></sup>slaves, promising to allow fourscore and ten<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. bodies.">g</a></sup>slaves for a talent, not expecting the judgement that was to follow upon him from the Almighty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> But tidings came to Judas concerning the inroad of Nicanor; and when he communicated to them that were with him the presence of the army,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> they that were cowardly and distrustful of the judgement of God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The Greek text here is uncertain.">h</a></sup>ran away and left the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And others sold all that was left over to them, and withal besought the Lord to deliver them that had been sold as slaves by the impious Nicanor or ever he met them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and this, if not for their own sakes, yet for the covenants made with their fathers, and because he had called them by his reverend and glorious name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Maccabaeus gathered his men together, six thousand in number, and exhorted them not to be stricken with dismay at the enemy, nor to fear the great multitude of the heathen who came wrongfully against them; but to contend nobly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> setting before their eyes the outrage that had been lawlessly perpetrated upon the holy place, and the shameful handling of the city that had been turned to mockery, and further the overthrow of the mode of life received from their ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For they, said he, trust to arms, and withal to deeds of daring; but we trust on the almighty God, since he is able at a beck to cast down them that are coming against us, and even the whole world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And moreover he recounted unto them the help given from time to time in the days of their ancestors, both the help given in the days of Sennacherib, how that a hundred fourscore and five thousand perished,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> and the help given in the land of Babylon, even the battle that was fought against the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. Galatians.">i</a></sup>Gauls, how that they came to the engagement eight thousand in all, with four thousand Macedonians, and how that, the Macedonians being hard pressed, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Some authorities read eight.">j</a></sup>six thousand destroyed the hundred and twenty thousand, because of the succour which they had from heaven, and took great booty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And when he had with these words made them of good courage, and ready to die for the laws and their country, he divided his army into four parts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. appointing his brethren also leaders.">k</a></sup>appointing his brethren to be with himself leaders of the several bands, to wit, Simon and Joseph and Jonathan, giving each the command of fifteen hundred men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and moreover Eleazer also: then, having read aloud the sacred book, and having given as watchword, THE HELP OF GOD, leading the first band himself, he joined battle with Nicanor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And, since the Almighty fought on their side, they slew of the enemy above nine thousand, and wounded and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. disabled in their limbs.">l</a></sup>disabled the more part of Nicanor’s army, and compelled all to flee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and they took the money of those that had come there to buy them. And after they had pursued them for some<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or, while">m</a></sup>distance, they returned, being constrained by the time of the day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> for it was the day before the sabbath, and for this cause they made no effort to chase them far.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="The exact meaning of this clause is uncertain.">n</a></sup>And when they had gathered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Gr. their arms...the spoils of the enemy.">o</a></sup>the arms of the enemy together, and had stripped off their spoils, they occupied themselves about the sabbath, blessing and thanking the Lord exceedingly, who had saved them unto this day, for that he had caused a beginning of mercy to distil upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And after the sabbath, when they had given of the spoils to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or, wounded Gr. shamefully handled.">p</a></sup>maimed, and to the widows and orphans, the residue they distributed among themselves and their children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And when they had accomplished these things, and had made a common supplication, they besought the merciful Lord to be wholly reconciled with his servants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And having had an encounter with the forces of Timotheus and Bacchides, they killed above twenty thousand of them, and made themselves masters of strongholds exceeding high, and divided very much plunder, giving the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or, wounded Gr. shamefully handled.">q</a></sup>maimed and orphans and widows, and moreover the aged also, an equal share with themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="The exact meaning of this clause is uncertain.">r</a></sup>And when they had gathered the arms<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Gr. of them.">s</a></sup>of the enemy together, they stored them all up carefully in the most important positions, and the residue of the spoils they carried to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And they killed the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="That is, probably, the captain of an irregular auxiliary force. Some write Phylarches, as a proper name.">t</a></sup>phylarch of Timotheus’s forces, a most unholy man, and one who had done the Jews much hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="The Greek text here is perhaps corrupt.">u</a></sup>And as they kept the feast of victory in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or, country">v</a></sup>city of their fathers, they burned those that had set the sacred<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or, porches">w</a></sup>gates on fire, and among them Callisthenes, who had fled into<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or, a solitary hut">x</a></sup>an outhouse; and so they received the meet reward of their impiety.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And the thrice-accursed Nicanor, who had brought the thousand merchants to buy the Jews for slaves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> being through the help of the Lord humbled by them who in his eyes were held to be of least account, put off his glorious apparel, and passing through the midland,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Gr. having made himself solitary.">y</a></sup>shunning all company like a fugitive slave, arrived at Antioch,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or, having won the greatest possible favour by reason of the destruction of his host">z</a></sup>having, as he thought, had the greatest possible good fortune, though his host was destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And he that had taken upon him to make tribute sure for the Romans by the captivity of the men of Jerusalem published abroad that the Jews had One who fought for them, and that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or, because of this their way of life Gr. because of this manner.">aa</a></sup>because this was so the Jews were invulnerable, because they followed the laws ordained by him.</span>
</div>
</div>
