<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And John went up from Gazara, and told Simon his father what Cendebaeus was doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And Simon called his two oldest sons, Judas and John, and said unto them, I and my brethren and my father’s house have fought the battles of Israel from our youth, even unto this day; and things have prospered in our hands, that we should deliver Israel oftentimes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> But now I am old, and ye moreover, by his mercy, are of a sufficient age: be ye instead of me and my brother, and go forth and fight for our nation; but let the help which is from heaven be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And he chose out of the country twenty thousand men of war and horsemen, and they went against Cendebaeus, and slept at Modin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And rising up in the morning, they went into the plain, and, behold, a great host came to meet them, of footmen and horsemen: and there was a brook betwixt them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he encamped over against them, he and his people: and he saw that the people were afraid to pass over the brook, and he passed over first, and the men saw him, and passed over after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And he divided the people, and set the horsemen in the midst of the footmen: but the enemies’ horsemen were exceeding many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And they sounded with the trumpets; and Cendebaeus and his army were put to the rout, and there fell of them many wounded to death, but they that were left fled to the stronghold:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> at that time was Judas John’s brother wounded: but John pursued after them, till he came unto Kidron, which Cendebaeus had built;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and they fled unto the towers that are in the fields of Azotus; and he burned it with fire; and there fell of them about two thousand men. And he returned into Judaea in peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Ptolemy the son of Abubus had been appointed captain for the plain of Jericho, and he had much silver and gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> for he was the high priest’s son in law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And his heart was lifted up, and he was minded to make himself master of the country, and he took counsel deceitfully against Simon and his sons, to make away with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Now Simon was visiting the cities that were in the country, and taking care for the good ordering of them; and he went down to Jericho, himself and Mattathias and Judas his sons, in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="circa B.C. 136.">a</a></sup>the hundred and seventy and seventh year, in the eleventh month, the same is the month Sebat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and the son of Abubus received them deceitfully into the little stronghold that is called Dok, which he had built, and made them a great banquet, and hid men there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And when Simon and his sons had drunk freely, Ptolemy and his men rose up, and took their arms, and came in upon Simon into the banqueting place, and slew him, and his two sons, and certain of his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he committed a great iniquity, and recompensed evil for good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And Ptolemy wrote these things, and sent to the king, that he should send him forces to aid him, and should deliver him their country and the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And he sent others to Gazara to make away with John: and unto the captains of thousands he sent letters to come unto him, that he might give them silver and gold and gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And others he sent to take possession of Jerusalem, and the mount of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And one ran before to Gazara, and told John that his father and brethren were perished, and he hath sent to slay thee also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And when he heard, he was sore amazed; and he laid hands on the men that came to destroy him, and slew them; for he perceived that they were seeking to destroy him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the rest of the acts of John, and of his wars, and of his valiant deeds which he did, and of the building of the walls which he built, and of his doings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> behold, they are written in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. book of days.">b</a></sup>chronicles of his high priesthood, from the time that he was made high priest after his father.</span>
</div>
