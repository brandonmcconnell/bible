<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> In those days rose up Mattathias the son of John, the son of Simeon, a priest of the sons of Joarib, from Jerusalem; and he dwelt at Modin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And he had five sons,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. Joannes.">a</a></sup>John, who was surnamed Gaddis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Simon, who was called Thassi;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Judas, who was called Maccabaeus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Eleazar, who was called Avaran; Jonathan, who was called Apphus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he saw the blasphemies that were committed in Judah and in Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and he said,<br />Woe is me! wherefore was I born to see the destruction of my people, and the destruction of the holy city, and to dwell there, when it was given into the hand of the enemy, the sanctuary into the hand of aliens?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Her temple is become as a man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some authorities read inglorious.">b</a></sup>that was glorious:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> her vessels of glory are carried away into captivity, her infants are slain in her streets, her young men with the sword of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> What nation hath not inherited her palaces, and gotten possession of her spoils?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> her adorning is all taken away; instead of a free woman she is become a bond woman:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and, behold, our holy things and our beauty and our glory are laid waste, and the Gentiles have profaned them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Wherefore should we live any longer?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Mattathias and his sons rent their clothes, and put on sackcloth, and mourned exceedingly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the king’s officers, that were enforcing the apostasy, came into the city Modin to sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And many of Israel came unto them, and Mattathias and his sons were gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the king’s officers answered and spake to Mattathias, saying, Thou art a ruler and an honourable and great man in this city, and strengthened with sons and brethren:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> now therefore come thou first and do the commandment of the king, as all the nations have done, and the men of Judah, and they that remain in Jerusalem: and thou and thy house shall be in the number of the king’s<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See ch. iii. 38: vi. 10, &amp;c. Compare ch. x. 65; xi. 27; 2 Macc. viii. 9.">c</a></sup>Friends, and thou and thy sons shall be honoured with silver and gold and many gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Mattathias answered and said with a loud voice, If all the nations that are in the house of the king’s dominion hearken unto him, to fall away each one from the worship of his fathers, and have made choice to follow his commandments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> yet will I and my sons and my brethren walk in the covenant of our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. May he be propitious. Compare 2 Sam. xxiii. 17 (Sept.).">d</a></sup>Heaven forbid that we should forsake the law and the ordinances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> We will not hearken to the king’s words, to go aside from our worship, on the right hand, or on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And when he had left speaking these words, there came a Jew in the sight of all to sacrifice on the altar which was at Modin, according to the king’s commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And Mattathias saw it, and his zeal was kindled, and his reins trembled, and he shewed forth his wrath according to judgement, and ran, and slew him upon the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And the king’s officer, who compelled men to sacrifice, he killed at that time, and pulled down the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And he was zealous for the law, even as Phinehas did unto Zimri the son of Salu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And Mattathias cried out in the city with a loud voice, saying, Whosoever is zealous for the law, and maintaineth the covenant, let him come forth after me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he and his sons fled into the mountains, and forsook all that they had in the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Then many that sought after justice and judgement went down into the wilderness, to dwell there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> they, and their sons, and their wives, and their cattle; because evils were multiplied upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And it was told the king’s officers, and the forces that were in Jerusalem, the city of David, that certain men, who had broken the king’s commandment, were gone down into the secret places in the wilderness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> and many pursued after them, and having overtaken them, they encamped against them, and set the battle in array against them on the sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And they said unto them, Thus far. Come forth, and do according to the word of the king, and ye shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And they said, We will not come forth, neither will we do the word of the king, to profane the sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And they hasted to give them battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And they answered them not, neither cast they a stone at them, nor stopped up the secret places,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> saying, Let us die all in our innocency: heaven and earth witness over us, that ye put us to death without trial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And they rose up against them in battle on the sabbath, and they died, they and their wives and their children, and their cattle, to the number of a thousand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. souls of men.">e</a></sup>souls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And Mattathias and his friends knew it, and they mourned over them exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And one said to another, If we all do as our brethren have done, and fight not against the Gentiles for our lives and our ordinances, they will now quickly destroy us from off the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And they took counsel on that day, saying, whosoever shall come against us to battle on the sabbath day, let us fight against him, and we shall in no wise all die, as our brethren died in the secret places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Then were gathered together unto them a company of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="That is, Chasidim.">f</a></sup>Hasidaeans, mighty men of Israel, every one that offered himself willingly for the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And all they that fled from the evils were added to them, and became a stay unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And they mustered a host, and smote sinners in their anger, and lawless men in their wrath: and the rest fled to the Gentiles for safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And Mattathias and his friends went round about, and pulled down the altars;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> and they circumcised by force the children that were uncircumcised, as many as they found in the coasts of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> And they pursued after the sons of pride, and the work prospered in their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And they rescued the law out of the hand of the Gentiles, and out of the hand of the kings, neither<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. gave they a horn to the sinner.">g</a></sup>suffered they the sinner to triumph.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> And the days of Mattathias drew near that he should die, and he said unto his sons,<br />Now have pride and rebuke gotten strength, and a season of overthrow, and wrath of indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> And now, my children, be ye zealous for the law, and give your lives for the covenant of your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And call to remembrance the deeds of our fathers which they did in their generations; and receive great glory and an everlasting name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> Was not Abraham found faithful in temptation, and it was reckoned unto him for righteousness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> Joseph in the time of his distress kept the commandment, and became lord of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> Phinehas our father, for that he was zealous exceedingly, obtained the covenant of an everlasting priesthood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> Joshua for fulfilling the word became a judge in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Caleb for bearing witness in the congregation obtained a heritage in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> David for being merciful inherited the throne of a kingdom for ever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> Elijah, for that he was exceeding zealous for the law, was taken up into heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> Hananiah, Azariah, Mishael, believed, and were saved out of the flame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> Daniel for his innocency was delivered from the mouth of lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> And thus consider ye from generation to generation, that none that put their trust in him shall want for strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> And be not afraid of the words of a sinful man; for his glory shall be dung and worms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> To-day he shall be lifted up, and to-morrow he shall in no wise be found, because he is returned unto his dust, and his thought is perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64"> And ye, my children, be strong, and shew yourselves men in behalf of the law; for therein shall ye obtain glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65"> And, behold, Simon your brother, I know that he is a man of counsel; give ear unto him alway: he shall be a father unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66"> And Judas Maccabaeus, he hath been strong and mighty from his youth: he shall be your captain, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some ancient authorities read ye shall fight.">h</a></sup>shall fight the battle of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67"> And take ye unto you all the doers of the law, and avenge the wrong of your people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68"> Render a recompense to the Gentiles, and take heed to the commandments of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69"> And he blessed them, and was gathered to his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70"> And he died in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="circa B.C. 167.">i</a></sup>the hundred and forty and sixth year, and his sons buried him in the sepulchres of his fathers at Modin, and all Israel made great lamentation for him.</span>
</div>
</div>
