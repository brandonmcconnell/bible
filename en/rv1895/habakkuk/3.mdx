<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 7:1–17; Psalms 86:1–17; Psalms 90:1–17"> A PRAYER of Habakkuk the prophet, set to Shigionoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 85:6; Psalms 90:13–17; Hosea 6:2–3"> O LORD, I have heard the report of thee, and am afraid: O LORD, revive thy work in the midst of the years, in the midst of the years make it known; in wrath remember mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Psalms 4:4; Obadiah 1:9"> God came from Teman, and the Holy One from mount Paran.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Selah</span>His glory covered the heavens, and the earth was full of his praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Matthew 17:2; Job 26:14"> And his brightness was as the light; he had rays coming forth from his hand: and there was the hiding of his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 12:29–30; Numbers 16:46–49; Psalms 78:50–51"> Before him went the pestilence, and fiery bolts went forth at his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:26; Deuteronomy 33:15; Nahum 1:5"> He stood, and measured the earth; he beheld, and drove asunder the nations: and the eternal mountains were scattered, the everlasting hills did bow; his goings were as of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 15:14–16; Genesis 10:6–7; Genesis 25:1–4"> I saw the tents of Cushan in affliction: the curtains of the land of Midian did tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 68:17; Deuteronomy 33:26–27; Exodus 14:21–22"> Was the LORD displeased against the rivers? was thine anger against the rivers, or thy wrath against the sea, that thou didst ride upon thine horses, upon thy chariots of salvation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; Psalms 105:41; Psalms 78:15–16"> Thy bow was made quite bare; the oaths to the tribes were a sure word.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Selah</span>Thou didst cleave the earth with rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:24; Joshua 4:23–24; Psalms 93:3"> The mountains saw thee, and were afraid; the tempest of waters passed by: the deep uttered his voice, and lifted up his hands on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 144:5–6; Psalms 18:12–14; Joshua 10:11–13"> The sun and moon stood still in their habitation; at the light of thine arrows as they went, at the shining of thy glittering spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Micah 4:12–13; Numbers 21:23–35"> Thou didst march through the land in indignation, thou didst thresh the nations in anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 110:6; Psalms 105:15; Psalms 68:19–23"> Thou wentest forth for the salvation of thy people, for the salvation of thine anointed; thou woundedst the head out of the house of the wicked, laying bare the foundation even unto the neck.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Selah</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 9:14; Daniel 11:40; Psalms 118:10–12"> Thou didst pierce with his own staves the head of his warriors: they came as a whirlwind to scatter me: their rejoicing was as to devour the poor secretly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 77:19; Habakkuk 3:8; Exodus 15:8"> Thou didst tread the sea with thine horses, the heap of mighty waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:9; Habakkuk 1:5–11; Habakkuk 3:2"> I heard, and my belly trembled, my lips quivered at the voice; rottenness entered into my bones, and I trembled in my place: that I should rest in the day of trouble, when it cometh up against the people which invadeth him in troops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:17; Joel 1:16–18; Deuteronomy 28:15–18"> For though the fig tree shall not blossom, neither shall fruit be in the vines; the labour of the olive shall fail, and the fields shall yield no meat; the flock shall be cut off from the fold, and there shall be no herd in the stalls:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 13:15; Philippians 4:4; Micah 7:7"> Yet I will rejoice in the LORD, I will joy in the God of my salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:34; Psalms 18:33; Philippians 4:13"> Jehovah, the Lord, is my strength, and he maketh my feet like hinds’ feet, and will make me to walk upon mine high places. For the Chief Musician, on my stringed instruments.</span>
