<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Put off, O Jerusalem, the garment of thy mourning and affliction, and put on the comeliness of the glory that cometh from God for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Cast about thee the robe of the righteousness which cometh from God; set a diadem on thine head of the glory of the Everlasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For God will shew thy brightness unto every region under heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For thy name shall be called of God for ever The peace of righteousness, and The glory of godliness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Arise, O Jerusalem, and stand upon the height, and look about thee toward the east, and behold thy children gathered from the going down of the sun unto the rising thereof at the word of the Holy One, rejoicing that God hath remembered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For they went from thee on foot, being led away of their enemies: but God bringeth them in unto thee borne on high with glory,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Another reading is, as children of the kingdom.">a</a></sup>as on a royal throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For God hath appointed that every high mountain, and the everlasting hills, should be made low, and the valleys filled up, to make plain the ground, that Israel may go safely in the glory of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Moreover the woods and every sweet-smelling tree have overshadowed Israel by the commandment of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For God shall lead Israel with joy in the light of his glory with the mercy and righteousness that cometh from him.</span>
