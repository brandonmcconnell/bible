<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:1–2; 2 Kings 25:1–2; Jeremiah 25:1"> The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD in the tenth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, which was the eighteenth year of Nebuchadrezzar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:21; Nehemiah 3:25; Jeremiah 33:1"> Now at that time the king of Babylon’s army besieged Jerusalem: and Jeremiah the prophet was shut up in the court of the guard, which was in the king of Judah’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:2–3; Jeremiah 21:4–7; Jeremiah 26:8–9"> For Zedekiah king of Judah had shut him up, saying, Wherefore dost thou prophesy, and say, Thus saith the LORD, Behold, I will give this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he shall take it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:4–7; 2 Kings 25:4–7; Jeremiah 37:17"> and Zedekiah king of Judah shall not escape out of the hand of the Chaldeans, but shall surely be delivered into the hand of the king of Babylon, and shall speak with him mouth to mouth, and his eyes shall behold his eyes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:7; Ezekiel 17:9–10; Ezekiel 17:15"> and he shall lead Zedekiah to Babylon, and there shall he be until I visit him, saith the LORD: though ye fight with the Chaldeans, ye shall not prosper?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Jeremiah said, The word of the LORD came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:1; Jeremiah 11:21; Leviticus 25:25"> Behold, Hanamel the son of Shallum thine uncle shall come unto thee, saying, Buy thee my field that is in Anathoth: for the right of redemption is thine to buy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:7; Jeremiah 32:2; Jeremiah 33:1"> So Hanamel mine uncle’s son came to me in the court of the guard according to the word of the LORD, and said unto me, Buy my field, I pray thee, that is in Anathoth, which is in the land of Benjamin: for the right of inheritance is thine, and the redemption is thine; buy it for thyself. Then I knew that this was the word of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Esther 3:9; 1 Kings 20:39"> And I bought the field that was in Anathoth of Hanamel mine uncle’s son, and weighed him the money, even seventeen shekels of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:44; Jeremiah 32:12; Jeremiah 32:25"> And I subscribed the deed, and sealed it, and called witnesses, and weighed him the money in the balances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 2:27; 1 Corinthians 11:16; Acts 26:3"> So I took the deed of the purchase, both that which was sealed, according to the law and custom, and that which was open:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:16; Jeremiah 51:59; Jeremiah 36:4–5"> and I delivered the deed of the purchase unto Baruch the son of Neriah, the son of Mahseiah, in the presence of Hanamel mine uncle’s son, and in the presence of the witnesses that subscribed the deed of the purchase, before all the Jews that sat in the court of the guard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And I charged Baruch before them, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Thus saith the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel: Take these deeds, this deed of the purchase, both that which is sealed, and this deed which is open, and put them in an earthen vessel; that they may continue many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 3:10; Amos 9:14–15; Jeremiah 30:18"> For thus saith the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel: Houses and fields and vineyards shall yet again be bought in this land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:1; Genesis 32:9–12; Philippians 4:6–7"> Now after I had delivered the deed of the purchase unto Baruch the son of Neriah, I prayed unto the LORD, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 1:37; Jeremiah 32:27; Genesis 18:14"> Ah Lord GOD! behold, thou hast made the heaven and the earth by thy great power and by thy stretched out arm; there is nothing too hard for thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Deuteronomy 5:9–10; Exodus 34:7"> which shewest mercy unto thousands, and recompensest the iniquity of the fathers into the bosom of their children after them: the great, the mighty God, the LORD of hosts is his name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Matthew 16:27; Psalms 62:12"> great in counsel, and mighty in work: whose eyes are open upon all the ways of the sons of men; to give every one according to his ways, and according to the fruit of his doings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:10; 2 Samuel 7:23; Exodus 10:2"> which didst set signs and wonders in the land of Egypt, even unto this day, both in Israel and among other men; and madest thee a name, as at this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 26:8; Psalms 105:43"> and didst bring forth thy people Israel out of the land of Egypt with signs, and with wonders, and with a strong hand, and with a stretched out arm, and with great terror;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Exodus 13:5; Jeremiah 11:5"> and gavest them this land, which thou didst swear to their fathers to give them, a land flowing milk and honey;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:7; Psalms 78:54–55; Daniel 9:10–14"> and they came in, and possessed it; but they obeyed not thy voice, neither walked in thy law; they have done nothing of all that thou commandedst them to do: therefore thou hast caused all this evil to come upon them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:36; Deuteronomy 4:26; Joshua 23:15–16"> Behold the mounts, they are come unto the city to take it; and the city is given into the hand of the Chaldeans that fight against it, because of the sword, and of the famine, and of the pestilence: and what thou hast spoken is come to pass; and, behold, thou seest it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:24; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 77:19"> And thou hast said unto me, O Lord GOD, Buy thee the field for money, and call witnesses; whereas the city is given into the hand of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then came the word of the LORD unto Jeremiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Jeremiah 32:17; Isaiah 64:8"> Behold, I am the LORD, the God of all flesh: is there any thing too hard for me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:3; Jeremiah 32:24; Jeremiah 19:7–12"> Therefore thus saith the LORD: Behold, I will give this city into the hand of the Chaldeans, and into the hand of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, and he shall take it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 19:13; Jeremiah 52:13"> and the Chaldeans, that fight against this city, shall come and set this city on fire, and burn it, with the houses, upon whose roofs they have offered incense unto Baal, and poured out drink offerings unto other gods, to provoke me to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:22–26; Deuteronomy 9:7–12; Jeremiah 25:7"> For the children of Israel and the children of Judah have only done that which was evil in my sight from their youth: for the children of Israel have only provoked me to anger with the work of their hands, saith the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 23:27; Jeremiah 27:10; 2 Kings 21:4–7"> For this city hath been to me a provocation of mine anger and of my fury from the day that they built it even unto this day; that I should remove it from before my face:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 9:8; Jeremiah 2:26; Isaiah 1:4–6"> because of all the evil of the children of Israel and of the children of Judah, which they have done to provoke me to anger, they, their kings, their princes, their priests, and their prophets, and the men of Judah, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:13; Jeremiah 2:27; Ezekiel 8:16"> And they have turned unto me the back, and not the face: and though I taught them, rising up early and teaching them, yet they have not hearkened to receive instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:30; Ezekiel 8:5–16; 2 Kings 21:4–7"> But they set their abominations in the house which is called by my name, to defile it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:31; 2 Chronicles 33:6; Leviticus 18:21"> And they built the high places of Baal, which are in the valley of the son of Hinnom, to cause their sons and their daughters to pass through the fire unto Molech; which I commanded them not, neither came it into my mind, that they should do this abomination; to cause Judah to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:24; Jeremiah 32:3; Jeremiah 32:28"> And now therefore thus saith the LORD, the God of Israel, concerning this city, whereof ye say, It is given into the hand of the king of Babylon by the sword, and by the famine, and by the pestilence:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:3; Jeremiah 23:6; Ezekiel 34:12–14"> Behold, I will gather them out of all the countries, whither I have driven them in mine anger, and in my fury, and in great wrath; and I will bring them again unto this place, and I will cause them to dwell safely:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Ezekiel 11:19–20; Hebrews 8:10"> and they shall be my people, and I will be their God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19–20; Acts 4:32; John 17:21"> and I will give them one heart and one way, that they may fear me for ever; for the good of them, and of their children after them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–33; Ezekiel 36:26; Jeremiah 24:7"> and I will make an everlasting covenant with them, that I will not turn away from them, to do them good; and I will put my fear in their hearts, that they shall not depart from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Zephaniah 3:17; Isaiah 62:5"> Yea, I will rejoice over them to do them good, and I will plant them in this land assuredly with my whole heart and with my whole soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Joshua 23:14–15; Jeremiah 33:10–11"> For thus saith the LORD: Like as I have brought all this great evil upon this people, so will I bring upon them all the good that I have promised them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:11–14; Jeremiah 32:15; Jeremiah 32:36"> And fields shall be bought in this land, whereof ye say, It is desolate, without man or beast; it is given into the hand of the Chaldeans;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:26; Jeremiah 33:26; Jeremiah 33:11"> Men shall buy fields for money, and subscribe the deeds, and seal them, and call witnesses, in the land of Benjamin, and in the places about Jerusalem, and in the cities of Judah, and in the cities of the hill country, and cities of the lowland, and in the cities of the South: for I will cause their captivity to return, saith the LORD.</span>
