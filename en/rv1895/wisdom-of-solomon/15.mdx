<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> <span className="pl pl-1">For to be acquainted with thee is</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. entire.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">perfect righteousness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And to know thy dominion is the root of immortality.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> <span className="pl pl-1">For neither were we led astray by any evil device of men’s art,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Nor yet by painters’ fruitless labour,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">A form stained with varied colours;</span><br /></span>

<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <span className="pl pl-1">And also, labouring to an evil end, he mouldeth a vain god out of the same clay,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He who, having but a little before been made of earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">After a short space goeth his way to the earth out of which he was taken,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When he is required to render back the</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, life">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">soul which was lent him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Howbeit he hath anxious care,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Not because his powers must fail,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Nor because his span of life is short;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But he matcheth himself against goldsmiths and</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. silver-founders.">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">silversmiths,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And he imitateth moulders in</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, copper">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">brass,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And esteemeth it glory that he mouldeth counterfeits.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="pl pl-1">But</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some authorities read they accounted.">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he accounted our very life to be a</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or, sport">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">plaything,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And our</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or, way of life">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">lifetime a gainful</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or, keeping of festival">l</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">fair;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For, saith he, one must get gain whence one can, though it be by evil.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">For this man beyond all others knoweth that he sinneth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Out of earthy matter making brittle vessels and graven images.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">But most foolish</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or, are">m</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">were they all, and</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. more wretched than the soul of a babe.">n</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of feebler soul than a babe,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The enemies of thy people, who oppressed them;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because they even accounted all the idols of the nations to be gods;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Which have neither the use of eyes for seeing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Nor nostrils for drawing breath,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Nor ears to hear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Nor fingers for handling,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And their feet are helpless for walking.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">For a man made them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And one whose own spirit is borrowed moulded them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For no one hath power, being a man, to mould a god like unto himself,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But, being mortal, he maketh a dead thing by the work of lawless hands;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For he is better than the objects of his worship,</span><br /><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Most authorities read Of which, he indeed.">o</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">Forasmuch as he indeed had life, but they never.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yea, and the creatures that are most hateful do they worship,</span><br /><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="The Greek text here is perhaps corrupt.">p</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">For, being compared as to want of sense, these are worse than all others;</span><br /></span>
