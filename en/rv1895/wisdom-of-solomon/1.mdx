<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because the spirit of the Lord hath filled</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. the inhabited earth.">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the world,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And that which holdeth all things together hath knowledge of every voice.</span><br /></span>
