<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <span className="pl pl-1">(For honourable old age is not that which standeth in length of time,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Nor is its measure given by number of years:</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="pl pl-1">(For the bewitching of naughtiness bedimmeth the things which are good,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the giddy whirl of desire perverteth an innocent mind.)</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Being made perfect in a little while, he fulfilled long</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. times.">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">years;</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">But a righteous man that is dead shall condemn the ungodly that are living,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And youth that is quickly perfected the many years of an unrighteous man’s old age;</span><br /></span>
