<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> <span className="pl pl-1">For great are thy judgements, and hard to</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, set forth">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">interpret;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Therefore souls undisciplined went astray.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> <span className="pl pl-1">For when lawless men had supposed that they held a holy nation in their power,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They, themselves, prisoners of darkness, and bound in the fetters of a long night,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Close kept beneath their roofs,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Lay exiled from the eternal providence.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> <span className="pl pl-1">For while they thought that they were unseen in their secret sins,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They were</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. scattered by.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">sundered one from another by a dark curtain of forgetfulness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Stricken with terrible awe, and sore troubled by spectral forms.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> <span className="pl pl-1">For neither did</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. the recess.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the dark recesses that held them guard them from fears,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But sounds</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some authorities read troubling them sore.">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">rushing down rang around them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And phantoms appeared, cheerless with unsmiling faces.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> <span className="pl pl-1">And no force of fire prevailed to give them light,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Neither were the brightest flames of the stars strong enough to illumine that gloomy night:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> <span className="pl pl-1">But only there appeared to them the glimmering of a fire self-kindled, full of fear;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And in terror they deemed the things which they saw</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">To be worse than that sight, on which they could not gaze.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some authorities read And the mockeries of magic art lay low, and shameful was the rebuke &amp;c.">e</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">And they lay helpless, made the sport of magic art,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And a shameful rebuke of their vaunts of understanding:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they that promised to drive away terrors and troublings from a sick soul,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">These were themselves sick with a ludicrous fearfulness:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">For even if no troublous thing affrighted them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yet, scared with the creepings of vermin and hissings of serpents,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">they perished</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, trembling, and refusing to">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for very trembling,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Refusing even to look on the air, which could on no side be escaped.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="This is the probable sense: the Greek text is perhaps slightly corrupt.">g</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">For wickedness, condemned by a witness within, is a coward thing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And, being pressed hard by conscience, always</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Most authorities read hath added.">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">forecasteth the worst lot:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="pl pl-1">For fear is nothing else but a surrender of the succours which reason offereth;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">And from within the heart the expectation of them being less</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Maketh of greater account the ignorance of the cause that bringeth the torment.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">But they, all through the night which was powerless indeed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And which came upon them out of the recesses of powerless Hades,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All sleeping the same sleep,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now were haunted by monstrous apparitions,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And now were paralysed by their soul’s surrendering;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For fear sudden and unlooked for</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some authorities read was poured upon them.">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">came upon them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">So then every man, whosoever it might be, sinking down</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. there.">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in his place,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Was kept in ward shut up in that prison which was barred not with iron:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">For whether he were a husbandman, or a shepherd,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or a labourer whose toils were in the wilderness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He was overtaken, and endured that inevitable necessity,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For with one chain of darkness were they all bound.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whether there were a whistling wind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or a melodious noise of birds among the spreading branches,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or a measured fall of water running violently,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Or a harsh crashing of rocks hurled down,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or the swift course of animals bounding along unseen,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or the voice of wild beasts harshly roaring,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or an echo rebounding from</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or, a hollow">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the hollows of the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All these things paralysed them with terror.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the whole world beside was enlightened with clear light,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And was occupied with unhindered works;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="pl pl-1">While over them alone was spread a heavy night,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">An image of the darkness that should afterward receive them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But yet heavier than darkness were they unto themselves.</span></span>
