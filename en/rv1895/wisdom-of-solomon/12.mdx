<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> <span className="pl pl-1">For thine incorruptible spirit is in all things.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wherefore thou convictest by little and little them that</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. fall aside.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">fall from the right way,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And, putting them in remembrance by the very things wherein they sin, dost thou admonish them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That escaping from their wickedness they may believe on thee, O Lord.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> <span className="pl pl-1">For verily the old inhabitants of thy holy land,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hating them because they practised detestable works of enchantments and unholy rites</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The words rendered slaughters and impious in verses 5 and 6 differ but slightly from the readings of the Greek text, which here yield no sense.">b</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">(Merciless slaughters of children,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And sacrificial banquets of men’s flesh and of blood),</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Confederates in an impious fellowship,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And murderers of their own helpless babes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It was thy counsel to destroy by the hands of our fathers;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <span className="pl pl-1">That the land which in thy sight is most precious of all lands</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Might receive a worthy colony of God’s</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, children">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">servants.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Nevertheless even these thou didst spare as being men,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And thou sentest</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, wasps">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">hornets as forerunners of thy host,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">To cause them to perish by little and little;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Not that thou wast unable to subdue the ungodly under the hand of the righteous in battle,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or by terrible beasts or by one stern word to make away with them at once;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">But judging them by little and little thou gavest them a place of repentance,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Not being ignorant that their nature by birth was evil, and their wickedness inborn,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And that their manner of thought would in no wise ever be changed,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they were a seed accursed from the beginning:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Neither was it through fear of any that thou didst leave them then unpunished for their sins.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="pl pl-1">For who shall say, What hast thou done?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or who shall withstand thy judgement?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And who shall accuse thee for the perishing of nations which thou didst make?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or who shall come and stand before thee as an avenger for unrighteous men?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">For neither is there any God beside thee that careth for all,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That thou mightest shew unto him that thou didst not judge unrighteously:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Neither shall king or prince be able to look thee in the face to plead for those whom thou hast punished.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">But being righteous thou rulest all things righteously,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Deeming it a thing alien from thy power</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">To condemn one that doth not himself deserve to be punished.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">For thy strength is the beginning of righteousness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And thy sovereignty over all maketh thee to forbear all.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">For when men believe not that thou art perfect in power, thou shewest thy strength,</span><br /><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The Greek text here is perhaps corrupt.">e</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">And</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, in them">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in dealing with them that know it thou puttest their boldness to confusion.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="pl pl-1">But thou, being sovereign over thy strength, judgest in gentleness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And with great forbearance dost thou govern us;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the power is thine whensoever thou hast the will.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> <span className="pl pl-1">But thou didst teach thy people by such works as these,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">How that the righteous must be a lover of men;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And thou didst make thy sons to be of good hope,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because thou givest repentance when men have sinned.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> <span className="pl pl-1">For if on them that were enemies of thy</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or, children">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">servants and due to death</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Thou didst take vengeance with so great heedfulness and indulgence,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Giving them times and place whereby they might escape from their wickedness;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="pl pl-1">With how great carefulness didst thou judge thy sons,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">To whose fathers thou gavest oaths and covenants of good promises!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <span className="pl pl-1">While therefore thou dost chasten us, thou scourgest our enemies ten thousand times more,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">To the intent that we may ponder thy goodness when we judge,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And when we are judged may look for mercy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wherefore also the unrighteous that lived in folly of life</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Thou didst torment through their own abominations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> <span className="pl pl-1">For verily they went astray very far</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or, even beyond">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the ways of error,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Taking as gods those</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. living creatures: and so elsewhere in this book.">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">animals which even among their enemies were held in dishonour,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Deceived like foolish babes.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore, as unto unreasoning children, thou didst send thy judgement to mock them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> <span className="pl pl-1">But they that would not be admonished</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or, by a correction, which was as children’s play Gr. by child-play of correction.">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">by a mocking correction as of children</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Shall have experience of a judgement worthy of God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> <span className="pl pl-1">For through the sufferings whereat they were indignant,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Being punished in these creatures which they supposed to be gods,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They saw, and recognised as the true God him whom before they</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or, denied that they knew">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">refused to know:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Wherefore also the last end of condemnation came upon them.</span></span>
</div>
