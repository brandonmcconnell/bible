<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> <span className="pl pl-1">For that vessel the hunger for gains devised,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And an artificer, even wisdom, built it;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> <span className="pl pl-1">And thy providence, O Father, guideth it along,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because even in the sea thou gavest a way,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And in the waves a sure path,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Shewing that thou canst save out of every danger,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That so even without art a man may put to sea;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> <span className="pl pl-1">And it is thy will that the works of thy wisdom should be not idle;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Therefore also do men intrust their lives to a little piece of wood,,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And passing through the surge</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. by.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on a raft are brought safe to land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Greek text here is perhaps corrupt.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the old time also, when proud giants were perishing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The hope of the world, taking refuge on a raft,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Left to</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, future time Gr. age.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the race of men a seed of generations to come,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Thy hand guiding the helm.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <span className="pl pl-1">For blessed</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, is">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">hath been wood through which cometh righteousness:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <span className="pl pl-1">But the idol made with hands is accursed, itself and he that made it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because his was the working, and the corruptible thing was named a god:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">For both the ungodly doer and his ungodliness are alike hateful to God;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">For verily the deed shall be punished together with him that committed it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore also</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, upon Gr. in.">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">among the idols of the nations shall there be a visitation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because, though formed of things which God created, they were made an abomination,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And stumblingblocks to the souls of men,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And a snare to the feet of the foolish.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the devising of idols was the beginning of fornication,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the invention of them the corruption of life:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">For neither were they from the beginning, neither shall they be for ever;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">For by the vaingloriousness of men they entered into the world,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And therefore was a speedy end devised for them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">For a father worn with untimely grief,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Making an image of the child quickly taken away,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Now honoured him as a god which was then a dead man,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And delivered to those that were under him mysteries and solemn rites.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Afterward the ungodly custom, in process of time grown strong, was kept as a law,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And by the commandments of princes the graven images received worship.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">And when men could not honour them in presence because they dwelt far off,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Imagining the likeness from afar,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They made a visible image of the king whom they honoured,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That by their zeal they might flatter the absent as if present.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="pl pl-1">But unto a yet higher pitch was worship raised even by them that knew him not,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Urged forward by the ambition of the artificer:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> <span className="pl pl-1">For he, wishing peradventure to please one in authority,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Used his art to force the likeness toward a greater beauty;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the multitude, allured by reason of the grace of his handywork,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Now accounted as an object of devotion him that a little before was honoured as a man.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="pl pl-1">And this became</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. an ambush.">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a hidden danger unto life,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because men, in bondage either to calamity or to tyranny,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Invested stones and stocks with the incommunicable Name.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Afterward it was not enough for them to go astray as touching the knowledge of God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But also, while they live</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or, for">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. a great war of ignorance.">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">sore conflict through ignorance of him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That multitude of evils they call peace.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">For either slaughtering children in solemn rites, or celebrating secret mysteries,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or holding frantic revels of strange ordinances,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> <span className="pl pl-1">No longer do they</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or, keep unstained either life or marriage">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">guard either life or purity of marriage,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But one brings upon another either death by treachery, or anguish by adulterate offspring.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> <span className="pl pl-1">And all things confusedly are filled with blood and murder, theft and deceit,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Corruption, faithlessness, tumult, perjury,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or, troubling of the good, forgetfulness of favours">j</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">turmoil,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Ingratitude for benefits received,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Defiling of souls, confusion of</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or, kind">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">sex,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Disorder in marriage, adultery and wantonness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the worship of</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or, idols that may not be named See Ex. xxiii. 13; Ps. xvi. 4; Hos. ii. 17.">l</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">those</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="See ver. 21.">m</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nameless idols</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Is a beginning and cause and end of every evil.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> <span className="pl pl-1">For their worshippers either make merry unto madness, or prophesy lies,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or live unrighteously, or lightly forswear themselves.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> <span className="pl pl-1">For putting their trust in lifeless idols,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When they have sworn a wicked oath, they expect not to suffer harm.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> <span className="pl pl-1">But for both sins shall the just doom pursue them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because they had evil thoughts of God by giving heed to idols,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And swore unrighteously in deceit through contempt for holiness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> <span className="pl pl-1">For it is not the power of them by whom men swear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But it is</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. the Justice of them that sin.">n</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that Justice which hath regard to them that sin,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That visiteth always the transgression of the unrighteous.</span></span>
