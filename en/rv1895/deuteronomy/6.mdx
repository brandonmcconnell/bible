<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Deuteronomy 12:1; Deuteronomy 4:45"> Now this is the commandment, the statutes, and the judgments, which the LORD your God commanded to teach you, that ye might do them in the land whither ye go over to possess it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 13:4; Exodus 20:20"> that thou mightest fear the LORD thy God, to keep all his statutes and his commandments, which I command thee, thou, and thy son, and thy son’s son, all the days of thy life; and that thy days may be prolonged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Exodus 3:8; Genesis 26:4"> Hear therefore, O Israel, and observe to do it; that it may be well with thee, and that ye may increase mightily, as the LORD, the God of thy fathers, hath promised unto thee, in a land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 12:29–32; Isaiah 44:6; 1 Timothy 2:5"> Hear, O Israel: the LORD our God is one LORD:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 12:30; Matthew 22:37; Luke 10:27"> and thou shalt love the LORD thy God with all thine heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Proverbs 7:3; Jeremiah 31:33"> And these words, which I command thee this day, shall be upon thine heart:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:19; Ephesians 6:4; Psalms 78:4–6"> and thou shalt teach them diligently unto thy children, and shalt talk of them when thou sittest in thine house, and when thou walkest by the way, and when thou liest down, and when thou risest up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:21; Proverbs 3:3; Deuteronomy 11:18"> And thou shalt bind them for a sign upon thine hand, and they shall be for frontlets between thine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:20; Isaiah 30:8; Isaiah 57:8"> And thou shalt write them upon the door posts of thy house, and upon thy gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 24:13; Psalms 105:44; Nehemiah 9:25"> And it shall be, when the LORD thy God shall bring thee into the land which he sware unto thy fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give thee; great and goodly cities, which thou buildedst not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:12–18; Deuteronomy 8:10–20; Ezekiel 16:10–20"> and houses full of all good things, which thou filledst not, and cisterns hewn out, which thou hewedst not, vineyards and olive trees, which thou plantedst not, and thou shalt eat and be full;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> then beware lest thou forget the LORD, which brought thee forth out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:8; Deuteronomy 10:20; Matthew 4:10"> Thou shalt fear the LORD thy God; and him shalt thou serve, and shalt swear by his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:6; 1 John 5:21; Deuteronomy 13:7"> Ye shall not go after other gods, of the gods of the peoples which are round about you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 7:4"> for the LORD thy God in the midst of thee is a jealous God; lest the anger of the LORD thy God be kindled against thee, and he destroy thee from off the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Luke 4:12; Matthew 4:7"> Ye shall not tempt the LORD your God, as ye tempted him in Massah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:22; Psalms 119:4; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Ye shall diligently keep the commandments of the LORD your God, and his testimonies, and his statutes, which he hath commanded thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 12:25; Romans 12:2"> And thou shalt do that which is right and good in the sight of the LORD: that it may be well with thee, and that thou mayest go in and possess the good land which the LORD sware unto thy fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 33:52–53; Exodus 23:28–30; Judges 3:1–4"> to thrust out all thine enemies from before thee, as the LORD hath spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Exodus 12:26; Joshua 4:6–7"> When thy son asketh thee in time to come, saying, What mean the testimonies, and the statutes, and the judgments, which the LORD our God hath commanded you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 6:17–18; Ephesians 2:11–12; Deuteronomy 15:15"> then thou shalt say unto thy son, We were Pharaoh’s bondmen in Egypt; and the LORD brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Psalms 58:10–11; Deuteronomy 7:19"> and the LORD shewed signs and wonders, great and sore, upon Egypt, upon Pharaoh, and upon all his house, before our eyes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:18; Deuteronomy 6:10; Exodus 13:5"> and he brought us out from thence, that he might bring us in, to give us the land which he sware unto our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Psalms 41:2; Deuteronomy 10:12–13"> And the LORD commanded us to do all these statutes, to fear the LORD our God, for our good always, that he might preserve us alive, as at this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:13; Romans 10:3; Leviticus 18:5"> And it shall be righteousness unto us, if we observe to do all this commandment before the LORD our God, as he hath commanded us.</span>
