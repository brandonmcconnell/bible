<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Proverbs 22:1; Ecclesiastes 4:2"> A good name is better than precious ointment; and the day of death than the day of one’s birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 90:12; Deuteronomy 32:29; Hebrews 9:27"> It is better to go to the house of mourning, than to go to the house of feasting: for that is the end of all men; and the living will lay it to his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:9–11; Romans 5:3–4; James 4:8–10"> Sorrow is better than laughter: for by the sadness of the countenance the heart is made glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 8:14–16; 1 Samuel 25:36; Luke 7:12–13"> The heart of the wise is in the house of mourning; but the heart of fools is in the house of mirth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:31–32; Psalms 141:5; Revelation 3:19"> It is better to hear the rebuke of the wise, than for a man to hear the song of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 58:9; Psalms 118:12; Ecclesiastes 2:2"> For as the crackling of thorns under a pot, so is the laughter of the fool: this also is vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 23:8; Proverbs 17:23"> Surely extortion maketh a wise man foolish; and a gift destroyeth the understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Proverbs 14:29; Psalms 126:5–6"> Better is the end of a thing than the beginning thereof: and the patient in spirit is better than the proud in spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 14:17; Ephesians 4:26–27"> Be not hasty in thy spirit to be angry: for anger resteth in the bosom of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 6:13; Psalms 14:2–3; Genesis 6:11–12"> Say not thou, What is the cause that the former days were better than these? for thou dost not inquire wisely concerning this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:7; 1 Timothy 6:17–19; Ecclesiastes 2:13"> Wisdom is as good as an inheritance: yea, more excellent is it for them that see the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 8:35; Proverbs 2:7; Proverbs 3:18"> For wisdom is a defence, even as money is a defence: but the excellency of knowledge is, that wisdom preserveth the life of him that hath it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:15; Job 12:14; Psalms 107:43"> Consider the work of God: for who can make that straight, which he hath made crooked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:4; Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 12:13"> In the day of prosperity be joyful, and in the day of adversity consider: God hath even made the one side by side with the other, to the end that man should not find out any thing that shall be after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:12–14; Ecclesiastes 6:12; Psalms 73:3–13"> All this have I seen in the days of my vanity: there is a righteous man that perisheth in his righteousness, and there is a wicked man that prolongeth his life in his evil-doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:3; Proverbs 25:16; Colossians 2:18"> Be not righteous over much; neither make thyself over wise: why shouldest thou destroy thyself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 10:27; Psalms 55:23; Job 22:16"> Be not over much wicked, neither be thou foolish: why shouldest thou die before thy time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:13; Malachi 4:2; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> It is good that thou shouldest take hold of this; yea, also from that withdraw not thine hand: for he that feareth God shall come forth of them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 21:22; Colossians 1:9–11; Proverbs 24:5"> Wisdom is a strength to the wise man more than ten rulers which are in a city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 3:23; Isaiah 53:6; 1 John 1:8–10"> Surely there is not a righteous man upon earth, that doeth good, and sinneth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 30:10; Isaiah 29:21; 2 Samuel 16:10"> Also take not heed unto all words that are spoken; lest thou hear thy servant curse thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 3:9; Matthew 18:32–35; John 8:7–9"> for oftentimes also thine own heart knoweth that thou thyself likewise hast cursed others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 1:22; Genesis 3:5; 1 Corinthians 1:20"> All this have I proved in wisdom: I said, I will be wise; but it was far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:33; 1 Timothy 6:16; Isaiah 55:8–9"> That which is is far off, and exceeding deep; who can find it out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:13; Joshua 7:13; Ecclesiastes 2:20"> I turned about, and my heart was set to know and to search out, and to seek wisdom and the reason of things, and to know that wickedness is folly, and that foolishness is madness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 5:3–5; Proverbs 2:18–19"> And I find a thing more bitter than death, even the woman whose heart is snares and nets, and her hands as bands: whoso pleaseth God shall escape from her; but the sinner shall be taken by her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:8–10; Ecclesiastes 1:1–2"> Behold, this have I found, saith the Preacher; laying one thing to another, to find out the account:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 33:23; 1 Kings 11:1–3; Psalms 12:1"> which my soul still seeketh, but I have not found: one man among a thousand have I found; but a woman among all those have I not found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Mark 7:8–9; Titus 3:3"> Behold, this only have I found, that God made man upright; but they have sought out many inventions.</span>
