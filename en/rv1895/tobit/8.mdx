<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And when they had finished their supper, they brought Tobias in unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> But as he went, he remembered the words of Raphael, and took the ashes of the incense, and put the heart and the liver of the fish thereupon, and made a smoke therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> But when the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. demon.">a</a></sup>devil smelled the smell, he fled into the uppermost parts of Egypt, and the angel bound him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But after they were both shut in together, Tobias rose up from the bed, and said, Sister, arise, and let us pray that the Lord may have mercy on us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Tobias began to say, Blessed art thou, O God of our fathers, and blessed is thy holy and glorious name for ever; let the heavens bless thee, and all thy creatures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Thou madest Adam, and gavest him Eve his wife for a helper and a stay: of them came the seed of men: thou didst say, it is not good that the man should be alone; let us make him a helper like unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And now, O Lord, I take not this my sister for lust, but in truth: command that I may find mercy and grow old with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And she said with him, Amen. And they slept both that night.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Raguel arose, and went and digged a grave,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> saying, Lest he also should die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Raguel came into his house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and said to Edna his wife, Send one of the maidservants, and let them see whether he be alive: but if not, that we may bury him, and no man know it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> So the maidservant opened the door, and went in, and found them both sleeping,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and came forth, and told them that he was alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Raguel blessed God, saying,<br />Blessed art thou, O God, with all pure and holy blessing; and let thy saints bless thee, and all thy creatures; and let all thine angels and thine elect bless thee for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Blessed art thou, because thou hast made me glad; and it hath not befallen me as I suspected; but thou hast dealt with us according to thy great mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Blessed art thou, because thou hast had mercy on two that were the only begotten children of their parents: shew them mercy, O Lord; accomplish their life in health with gladness and mercy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But he commanded his servants to fill the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And he kept the wedding feast for them fourteen days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And before the days of the wedding feast were finished, Raguel sware unto him, that he should not depart till the fourteen days of the wedding feast were fulfilled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and that then he should take the half of his goods, and go in safety to his father; and the rest, said he, when I and my wife shall die.</span>
</div>
