<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And they came to Ecbatana, and arrived at the house of Raguel. But Sarah met them; and she saluted them, and they her; and she brought them into the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And he said to Edna his wife, How like is the young man to Tobit my cousin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And Raguel asked them, From whence are ye, brethren? And they said unto him, We are of the sons of Naphtali, which are captives in Nineveh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And he said unto them, Know ye Tobit our brother? But they said, We know him. And he said unto them, Is he in good health?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> But they said, He is both alive, and in good health: and Tobias said, He is my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Raguel sprang up, and kissed him, and wept,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and blessed him, and said unto him, Thou art the son of an honest and good man. And when he had heard that Tobit had lost his sight, he was grieved, and wept;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and Edna his wife and Sarah his daughter wept. And they received them gladly; and they killed a ram of the flock, and set store of meat before them.<br />But Tobias said to Raphael, Brother Azarias, speak of those things of which thou didst talk in the way, and let the matter be finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And he communicated the thing to Raguel: and Raguel said to Tobias, Eat and drink, and make merry:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> for it appertaineth unto thee to take my child. Howbeit I will shew thee the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> I have given my child to seven men, and whensoever they came in unto her, they died in the night. But for the present be merry. And Tobias said, I will taste nothing here, until ye make covenant and enter into covenant with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And Raguel said, Take her to thyself from henceforth according to the manner: thou art her brother, and she is thine: but the merciful God shall give all good success to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he called his daughter Sarah, and took her by the hand, and gave her to be wife to Tobias, and said, Behold, take her to thyself after the law of Moses, and lead her away to thy father. And he blessed them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And he called Edna his wife, and took a book, and wrote an instrument, and sealed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And they began to eat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Raguel called his wife Edna, and said unto her, Sister, prepare the other chamber, and bring her in thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And she did as he bade her, and brought her in thither: and she wept, and she received the tears of her daughter, and said unto her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Be of good comfort, my child; the Lord of heaven and earth give thee<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Many ancient authorities read joy.">a</a></sup>favour for this thy sorrow: be of good comfort, my daughter.</span>
