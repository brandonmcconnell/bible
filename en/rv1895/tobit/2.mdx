<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Now when I was come home again, and my wife Anna was restored unto me, and my son Tobias, in the feast of Pentecost, which is the holy feast of the seven weeks, there was a good dinner prepared me, and I sat down to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And I saw abundance of meat, and I said to my son, Go and bring what poor man soever thou shalt find of our brethren, who is mindful of the Lord; and lo, I tarry for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And he came, and said, Father, one of our race is strangled, and is cast out in the marketplace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And before I had tasted aught, I sprang up, and took him up into a chamber until the sun was set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And I returned, and washed myself, and ate my bread in heaviness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and remembered the prophecy of Amos, as he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your feasts shall be turned into mourning,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And all your mirth into lamentation.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And I wept: and when the sun was set, I went and made a grave, and buried him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And my neighbours mocked me, and said, He is no longer afraid to be put to death for this matter: and yet he fled away: and, lo, he burieth the dead again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And the same night I returned from burying him, and slept by the wall of my courtyard, being polluted; and my face was uncovered:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and I knew not that there were sparrows in the wall; and, mine eyes being open, the sparrows muted warm dung into mine eyes, and white films came in mine eyes; and I went to the physicians, and they helped me not: but Achiacharus did nourish me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some authorities read until he went.">a</a></sup>until I went into Elymais.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And my wife Anna did spin in the women’s chambers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and did send the work back to the owners. And they on their part paid her wages, and gave her also besides a kid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> But when it came to my house, it began to cry, and I said unto her, From whence is this kid? is it stolen? render it to the owners; for it is not lawful to eat anything that is stolen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But she said, It hath been given me for a gift more than the wages. And I did not believe her, and I bade her render it to the owners; and I was abashed at her. But she answered and said unto me, Where are thine alms and thy righteous deeds? behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. all things are known with thee.">b</a></sup>thou and all thy works are known.</span>
</div>
