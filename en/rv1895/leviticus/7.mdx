<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 6:17; Leviticus 5:14–7; Leviticus 6:25"> And this is the law of the guilt offering: it is most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 1:5; Leviticus 6:25; Leviticus 1:11"> In the place where they kill the burnt offering shall they kill the guilt offering: and the blood thereof shall he sprinkle upon the altar round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:13; Leviticus 3:15–16; Psalms 51:6"> And he shall offer of it all the fat thereof; the fat tail, and the fat that covereth the inwards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 3:4"> and the two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, which is by the loins, and the caul upon the liver, with the kidneys, shall he take away:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 1:13; Galatians 5:24; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> and the priest shall burn them upon the altar for an offering made by fire unto the LORD: it is a guilt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 6:29; Numbers 18:9–10; Leviticus 2:3"> Every male among the priests shall eat thereof: it shall be eaten in a holy place: it is most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 6:25–26; Leviticus 14:13; 1 Corinthians 9:13"> As is the sin offering, so is the guilt offering: there is one law for them: the priest that maketh atonement therewith, he shall have it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 4:11; Exodus 29:14; Romans 13:14"> And the priest that offereth any man’s burnt offering, even the priest shall have to himself the skin of the burnt offering which he hath offered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 18:9; Ezekiel 44:29; Leviticus 2:10"> And every meal offering that is baken in the oven, and all that is dressed in the frying pan, and on the baking pan, shall be the priest’s that offereth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14; Exodus 16:18"> And every meal offering, mingled with oil, or dry, shall all the sons of Aaron have, one as well as another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 22:18–21; Ezekiel 45:15; Leviticus 3:1–17"> And this is the law of the sacrifice of peace offerings, which one shall offer unto the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 2:4; Numbers 6:15; 1 Peter 2:5"> If he offer it for a thanksgiving, then he shall offer with the sacrifice of thanksgiving unleavened cakes mingled with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil, and cakes mingled with oil, of fine flour soaked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 4:5; Leviticus 23:17; Matthew 13:33"> With cakes of leavened bread he shall offer his oblation with the sacrifice of his peace offerings for thanksgiving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:19; Exodus 29:27–28; Numbers 18:24–32"> And of it he shall offer one out of each oblation for an heave offering unto the LORD; it shall be the priest’s that sprinkleth the blood of the peace offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 22:29–30"> And the flesh of the sacrifice of his peace offerings for thanksgiving shall be eaten on the day of his oblation; he shall not leave any of it until the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:5–8; Deuteronomy 12:6; Psalms 116:14"> But if the sacrifice of his oblation be a vow, or a freewill offering, it shall be eaten on the day that he offereth his sacrifice: and on the morrow that which remaineth of it shall be eaten:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:10; Hosea 6:2; 1 Corinthians 15:4"> but that which remaineth of the flesh of the sacrifice on the third day shall be burnt with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:7–8; Leviticus 11:41; Numbers 18:27"> And if any of the flesh of the sacrifice of his peace offerings be eaten on the third day, it shall not be accepted, neither shall it be imputed unto him that offereth it: it shall be an abomination, and the soul that eateth of it shall bear his iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 11:41; Titus 1:15; Acts 10:15–16"> And the flesh that toucheth any unclean thing shall not be eaten; it shall be burnt with fire. And as for the flesh, every one that is clean shall eat thereof:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 15:2–33; Genesis 17:14; Leviticus 22:3–7"> but the soul that eateth of the flesh of the sacrifice of peace offerings, that pertain unto the LORD, having his uncleanness upon him, that soul shall be cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 12:1–59; Leviticus 11:20; Ezekiel 4:14"> And when any one shall touch any unclean thing, the uncleanness of man, or an unclean beast, or any unclean abomination, and eat of the flesh of the sacrifice of peace offerings, which pertain unto the LORD, that soul shall be cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 3:16–17; Leviticus 17:6; 1 Samuel 2:15–17"> Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, Ye shall eat no fat, of ox, or sheep, or goat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 22:31; Leviticus 17:15; Leviticus 22:8"> And the fat of that which dieth of itself, and the fat of that which is torn of beasts, may be used for any other service: but ye shall in no wise eat of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For whosoever eateth the fat of the beast, of which men offer an offering made by fire unto the LORD, even the soul that eateth it shall be cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 9:4; Leviticus 3:17; Acts 15:20"> And ye shall eat no manner of blood, whether it be of fowl or of beast, in any of your dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 10:29; Leviticus 7:20–21; Leviticus 7:25"> Whosoever it be that eateth any blood, that soul shall be cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 1:20; 1 John 1:7; Leviticus 3:1–17"> Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, He that offereth the sacrifice of his peace offerings unto the LORD shall bring his oblation unto the LORD out of the sacrifice of his peace offerings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 6:20; Leviticus 3:9; Leviticus 3:14"> his own hands shall bring the offerings of the LORD made by fire; the fat with the breast shall he bring, that the breast may be waved for a wave offering before the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 3:5; Leviticus 3:11; Leviticus 3:16"> And the priest shall burn the fat upon the altar: but the breast shall be Aaron’s and his sons’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 6:20; Leviticus 7:34; Leviticus 9:21"> And the right thigh shall ye give unto the priest for an heave offering out of the sacrifices of your peace offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 26:1–46; Leviticus 6:1–30; Leviticus 7:3"> He among the sons of Aaron, that offereth the blood of the peace offerings, and the fat, shall have the right thigh for a portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 18:18–19; Leviticus 10:14–15; Exodus 29:9"> For the wave breast and the heave thigh have I taken of the children of Israel out of the sacrifices of their peace offerings, and have given them unto Aaron the priest and unto his sons as a due for ever from the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:20; 1 John 2:27; Numbers 18:7–19"> This is the anointing-portion of Aaron, and the anointing-portion of his sons, out of the offerings of the LORD made by fire, in the day when he presented them to minister unto the LORD in the priest’s office;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Leviticus 8:12; Leviticus 8:30; Exodus 40:13–15"> which the LORD commanded to be given them of the children of Israel, in the day that he anointed them. It is a due for ever throughout their generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 29:1; Leviticus 1:1–6; Leviticus 6:9–18"> This is the law of the burnt offering, of the meal offering, and of the sin offering, and of the guilt offering, and of the consecration, and of the sacrifice of peace offerings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 1:1–2"> which the LORD commanded Moses in mount Sinai, in the day that he commanded the children of Israel to offer their oblations unto the LORD, in the wilderness of Sinai.</span>
