<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the LORD spake unto Moses and to Aaron, saying unto them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 15:11; Leviticus 11:11; Hebrews 9:10"> Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, These are the living things which ye shall eat among all the beasts that are on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:10; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Deuteronomy 6:6–7"> Whatsoever parteth the hoof, and is clovenfooted, and cheweth the cud, among the beasts, that shall ye eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Nevertheless these shall ye not eat of them that chew the cud, or of them that part the hoof: the camel, because he cheweth the cud but parteth not the hoof, he is unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 104:18; Proverbs 30:26; Matthew 7:26"> And the coney, because he cheweth the cud but parteth not the hoof, he is unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:7"> And the hare, because she cheweth the cud but parteth not the hoof, she is unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 66:17; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> And the swine, because he parteth the hoof, and is clovenfooted, but cheweth not the cud, he is unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Colossians 2:16; Hebrews 9:10"> Of their flesh ye shall not eat, and their carcases ye shall not touch; they are unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; James 2:18; Acts 20:21"> These shall ye eat of all that are in the waters: whatsoever hath fins and scales in the waters, in the seas, and in the rivers, them shall ye eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 29:27; Leviticus 7:18; Psalms 139:21–22"> And all that have not fins and scales in the seas, and in the rivers, of all that move in the waters, and of all the living creatures that are in the waters, they are an abomination unto you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and they shall be an abomination unto you; ye shall not eat of their flesh, and their carcases ye shall have in abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Whatsoever hath no fins nor scales in the waters, that is an abomination unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 39:27–30; Jeremiah 4:22; Titus 3:3"> And these ye shall have in abomination among the fowls; they shall not be eaten, they are an abomination: the eagle, and the gier eagle, and the ospray;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and the kite, and the falcon after its kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 12:24; 1 Kings 17:6; Proverbs 30:17"> every raven after its kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 34:11–15; Ephesians 5:7–11; Ephesians 2:2–3"> and the ostrich, and the night hawk, and the seamew, and the hawk after its kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and the little owl, and the cormorant, and the great owl;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and the horned owl, and the pelican, and the vulture;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and the stork, the heron after its kind, and the hoopoe, and the bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Philippians 3:18–19; Leviticus 11:23"> All winged creeping things that go upon all four are an abomination unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Yet these may ye eat of all winged creeping things that go upon all four, which have legs above their feet, to leap withal upon the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; Mark 1:6; Exodus 10:4–5"> even these of them ye may eat; the locust after its kind, and the bald locust after its kind, and the cricket after its kind, and the grasshopper after its kind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> But all winged creeping things, which have four feet, are an abomination unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Ephesians 2:1–3; 2 Corinthians 6:17"> And by these ye shall become unclean: whosoever toucheth the carcase of them shall be unclean until the even:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Leviticus 14:8; Numbers 31:24"> And whosoever beareth aught of the carcase of them shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Every beast which parteth the hoof, and is not clovenfooted, nor cheweth the cud, is unclean unto you: every one that toucheth them shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 11:23; Leviticus 11:20"> And whatsoever goeth upon its paws, among all beasts that go on all four, they are unclean unto you: whoso toucheth their carcase shall be unclean until the even.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25"> And he that beareth the carcase of them shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even: they are unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 11:41–42; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Psalms 10:3"> And these are they which are unclean unto you among the creeping things that creep upon the earth; the weasel, and the mouse, and the great lizard after its kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and the gecko, and the land-crocodile, and the lizard, and the sand-lizard, and the chameleon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25; Leviticus 11:8"> These are they which are unclean to you among all that creep: whosoever doth touch them, when they are dead, shall be unclean until the even.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Titus 3:5; Titus 2:14"> And upon whatsoever any of them, when they are dead, doth fall, it shall be unclean; whether it be any vessel of wood, or raiment, or skin, or sack, whatsoever vessel it be, wherewith any work is done, it must be put into water, and it shall be unclean until the even; then shall it be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 14:45"> And every earthen vessel, whereinto any of them falleth, whatsoever is in it shall be unclean, and it ye shall break.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Proverbs 21:4; Proverbs 21:27"> All food therein which may be eaten, that on which water cometh, shall be unclean: and all drink that may be drunk in every such vessel shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 11:33"> And every thing whereupon any part of their carcase falleth shall be unclean; whether oven, or range for pots, it shall be broken in pieces: they are unclean, and shall be unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; John 4:14"> Nevertheless a fountain or a pit wherein is a gathering of water shall be clean: but that which toucheth their carcase shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:37; 1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:18"> And if aught of their carcase fall upon any sowing seed which is to be sown, it is clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> But if water be put upon the seed, and aught of their carcase fall thereon, it is unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Leviticus 11:40; Leviticus 11:24; Leviticus 15:7"> And if any beast, of which ye may eat, die; he that toucheth the carcase thereof shall be unclean until the even.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Leviticus 22:8; Ezekiel 44:31; Deuteronomy 14:21"> And he that eateth of the carcase of it shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even: he also that beareth the carcase of it shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 11:29; Leviticus 11:20; Leviticus 11:23"> And every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth is an abomination; it shall not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Titus 1:12; Isaiah 65:25; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> Whatsoever goeth upon the belly, and whatsoever goeth upon all four, or whatsoever hath many feet, even all creeping things that creep upon the earth, them ye shall not eat; for they are an abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 20:25; Leviticus 11:41–42"> Ye shall not make yourselves abominable with any creeping thing that creepeth, neither shall ye make yourselves unclean with them, that ye should be defiled thereby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Leviticus 19:2"> For I am the LORD your God: sanctify yourselves therefore, and be ye holy; for I am holy: neither shall ye defile yourselves with any manner of creeping thing that moveth upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Exodus 20:2; Exodus 6:7"> For I am the LORD that brought you up out of the land of Egypt, to be your God: ye shall therefore be holy, for I am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Leviticus 14:54; Leviticus 15:32; Ezekiel 43:12"> This is the law of the beast, and of the fowl, and of every living creature that moveth in the waters, and of every creature that creepeth upon the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Ezekiel 44:23; Romans 14:13–23"> to make a difference between the unclean and the clean, and between the living thing that may be eaten and the living thing that may not be eaten.</span>
