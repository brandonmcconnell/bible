<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Lamentations 3:30; Job 16:10; Matthew 26:67"> Now shalt thou gather thyself in troops, O daughter of troops: he hath laid siege against us: they shall smite the judge of Israel with a rod upon the cheek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:6; John 7:42; Luke 1:31–33"> But thou, Beth-lehem Ephrathah, which art little to be among the thousands of Judah, out of thee shall one come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel; whose goings forth are from of old, from everlasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:1–2; Romans 9:27–28; Micah 4:7"> Therefore will he give them up, until the time that she which travaileth hath brought forth: then the residue of his brethren shall return unto the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 40:10–11; Micah 7:14; Luke 1:32"> And he shall stand, and shall feed his flock in the strength of the LORD, in the majesty of the name of the LORD his God: and they shall abide; for now shall he be great unto the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 2:14; Zechariah 10:3; Ephesians 2:14–17"> And this man shall be our peace: when the Assyrian shall come into our land, and when he shall tread in our palaces, then shall we raise against him seven shepherds, and eight principal men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 10:8–11; Isaiah 14:25; Isaiah 14:2"> And they shall waste the land of Assyria with the sword, and the land of Nimrod in the entrances thereof: and he shall deliver us from the Assyrian, when he cometh into our land, and when he treadeth within our border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 5:3; Isaiah 44:3; Psalms 72:6"> And the remnant of Jacob shall be in the midst of many peoples as dew from the LORD, as showers upon the grass; that tarrieth not for man, nor waiteth for the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 10:5; Micah 4:13; Zechariah 9:15"> And the remnant of Jacob shall be among the nations, in the midst of many peoples, as a lion among the beasts of the forest, as a young lion among the flocks of sheep: who, if he go through, treadeth down and teareth in pieces, and there is none to deliver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Psalms 21:8; Isaiah 14:2–4"> Let thine hand be lifted up above thine adversaries, and let all thine enemies be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 9:10; Hosea 14:3; Psalms 20:7–8"> And it shall come to pass in that day, saith the LORD, that I will cut off thy horses out of the midst of thee, and will destroy thy chariots:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 2:12–17; Ezekiel 38:11; Hosea 10:14"> and I will cut off the cities of thy land, and will throw down all thy strong holds:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:10–12; Isaiah 8:19–20; Isaiah 2:20"> And I will cut off witchcrafts out of thine hand; and thou shalt have no more soothsayers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Ezekiel 36:25; Hosea 14:8"> and I will cut off thy graven images and thy pillars out of the midst of thee; and thou shalt no more worship the work of thine hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 34:13"> and I will pluck up thine Asherim out of the midst of thee: and I will destroy thy cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 149:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Micah 5:8"> And I will execute vengeance in anger and fury upon the nations which hearkened not.</span>
