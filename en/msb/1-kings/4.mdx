</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:1–15"> Solomon had twelve governors over all Israel to provide food for the king and his household. Each one would arrange provisions for one month of the year,</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 21:16; 1 Samuel 6:20; 1 Samuel 6:12"> Ben-deker in Makaz, in Shaalbim, in Beth-shemesh, and in Elon-beth-hanan;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; Joshua 12:17; Joshua 17:2"> Ben-hesed in Arubboth (Socoh and all the land of Hepher belonged to him);</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 17:11; Judges 5:19; Joshua 3:16"> Baana son of Ahilud in Taanach, in Megiddo, and in all of Beth-shean next to Zarethan below Jezreel, from Beth-shean to Abel-meholah and on past Jokmeam;</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:4; Numbers 32:41; 1 Kings 22:3"> Ben-geber in Ramoth-gilead (the villages of Jair son of Manasseh in Gilead belonged to him, as well as the region of Argob in Bashan with its sixty great cities with walls and bronze bars);</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 19:32–39; 2 Samuel 15:27; 1 Samuel 18:18"> Ahimaaz in Naphtali (he had married Basemath, a daughter of Solomon);</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26–17; Joshua 13:9–12; Numbers 21:21–35"> Geber son of Uri in the land of Gilead, including the territories of Sihon king of the Amorites and of Og king of Bashan. There was also one governor in the land of Judah.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; 1 Kings 3:8; Genesis 32:12"> The people of Judah and Israel were as numerous as the sand on the seashore, and they were eating and drinking and rejoicing.</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Joshua 1:4; Psalm 68:29"> And Solomon reigned over all the kingdoms from the Euphrates to the land of the Philistines, as far as the border of Egypt. These kingdoms offered tribute and served Solomon all the days of his life.</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Solomon’s provisions for a single day were thirty cors of fine flour, sixty cors of meal,</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:17–18"> ten fat oxen, twenty range oxen, and a hundred sheep, as well as deer, gazelles, roebucks, and fattened poultry.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9; Psalm 72:11; Psalm 72:3"> For Solomon had dominion over everything west of the Euphrates—over all the kingdoms from Tiphsah to Gaza—and he had peace on all sides.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Micah 4:4; Zechariah 3:10; Judges 20:1"> Throughout the days of Solomon, Judah and Israel dwelt securely from Dan to Beersheba, each man under his own vine and his own fig tree.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:14; 2 Chronicles 9:25; 2 Samuel 8:4"> Solomon had 4,000 stalls for his chariot horses and 12,000 horses.</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Each month the governors in turn provided food for King Solomon and all who came to his table. They saw to it that nothing was lacking.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Esther 8:10; Esther 8:14; Micah 1:13"> Each one also brought to the required place their quotas of barley and straw for the chariot horses and other horses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12; James 3:17; 1 Kings 4:20"> And God gave Solomon wisdom, exceedingly deep insight, and understanding beyond measure, like the sand on the seashore.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 5:11–12; Genesis 25:6; Daniel 1:20"> Solomon’s wisdom was greater than that of all the men of the East, greater than all the wisdom of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:6; 1 Chronicles 15:19; 1 Kings 3:12"> He was wiser than all men—wiser than Ethan the Ezrahite, and wiser than Heman, Calcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol. And his fame spread throughout the surrounding nations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:1–17; Matthew 13:35; Proverbs 1:1–31"> Solomon composed three thousand proverbs, and his songs numbered a thousand and five.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 19:23; Psalm 51:7; Psalm 92:12"> He spoke of trees, from the cedar in Lebanon to the hyssop growing in the wall, and he taught about animals, birds, reptiles, and fish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
