</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> So they sent for Jeroboam, and he and the whole assembly of Israel came to Rehoboam and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11–18; 1 Kings 4:7; Matthew 11:29–30"> “Your father put a heavy yoke on us. But now you must lighten the burden of your father’s service and the heavy yoke he put on us, and we will serve you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 12:12"> Rehoboam answered, “Go away for three days and then return to me.” So the people departed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 12:12; Job 32:7; 2 Samuel 16:20"> Then King Rehoboam consulted with the elders who had served his father Solomon during his lifetime. “How do you advise me to respond to these people?” he asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; 2 Chronicles 10:6–7; 1 Kings 12:13"> They replied, “If you will be a servant to these people and serve them this day, and if you will respond by speaking kind words to them, they will be your servants forever.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 19:20; Ecclesiastes 10:2–3; 2 Chronicles 10:8"> But Rehoboam rejected the advice of the elders; instead, he consulted the young men who had grown up with him and served him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 22:6–8; 2 Samuel 17:5–6; 2 Chronicles 10:9"> He asked them, “What message do you advise that we send back to these people who have spoken to me, saying, ‘Lighten the yoke your father put on us’?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 10:10–11; Proverbs 10:14; Isaiah 47:6"> The young men who had grown up with him replied, “This is how you should answer these people who said to you, ‘Your father made our yoke heavy, but you must make it lighter.’ This is what you should tell them: ‘My little finger is thicker than my father’s waist!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:11; Ezekiel 2:6; Jeremiah 28:13–14"> Whereas my father burdened you with a heavy yoke, I will add to your yoke. Whereas my father scourged you with whips, I will scourge you with scorpions.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 12:5; 2 Chronicles 10:12–14"> After three days, Jeroboam and all the people returned to Rehoboam, since the king had said, “Come back to me on the third day.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 3:17; Proverbs 13:20; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> And the king answered the people harshly. He rejected the advice of the elders</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 5:16–18; 1 Kings 12:10–11; Exodus 5:5–9"> and spoke to them as the young men had advised, saying, “Whereas my father made your yoke heavy, I will add to your yoke. Whereas my father scourged you with whips, I will scourge you with scorpions.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 12:24; Deuteronomy 2:30; Judges 14:4"> So the king did not listen to the people, and indeed this turn of events was from the LORD, to fulfill the word He had spoken to Jeroboam son of Nebat through Ahijah the Shilonite.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:1; Jeremiah 23:5–6; 2 Chronicles 10:16"> When all Israel saw that the king had refused to listen to them, they answered the king: “What portion do we have in David, and what inheritance in the son of Jesse? To your tents, O Israel! Look now to your own house, O David!” So the Israelites went home,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 11:36; 1 Kings 11:13; 2 Chronicles 11:13–17"> but Rehoboam still reigned over the Israelites living in the cities of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 4:6; 2 Samuel 20:24; 1 Kings 5:14"> Then King Rehoboam sent out Adoram, who was in charge of the forced labor, but all Israel stoned him to death. And King Rehoboam mounted his chariot in haste and escaped to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 17:21; 2 Chronicles 10:19; Joshua 4:9"> So to this day Israel has been in rebellion against the house of David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 11:13; 1 Kings 11:32; Hosea 8:4"> When all Israel heard that Jeroboam had returned, they summoned him to the assembly and made him king over all Israel. Only the tribe of Judah followed the house of David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:1–3; 1 Chronicles 21:5; 2 Chronicles 14:8"> And when Rehoboam arrived in Jerusalem, he mobilized the whole house of Judah and the tribe of Benjamin—180,000 chosen warriors—to fight against the house of Israel and restore the kingdom to Rehoboam son of Solomon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:2; 2 Chronicles 12:5–7; 1 Kings 13:1"> But the word of God came to Shemaiah the man of God:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 12:17"> “Tell Rehoboam son of Solomon king of Judah, all the house of Judah and Benjamin, and the rest of the people</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 12:15; 2 Chronicles 25:7–8; Hosea 8:4"> that this is what the LORD says: ‘You are not to go up and fight against your brothers, the Israelites. Each of you must return home, for this is My doing.’” So they listened to the word of the LORD and turned back according to the word of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 8:17; Judges 8:8; Judges 9:45–49"> Then Jeroboam built Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim and lived there. And from there he went out and built Penuel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:18–21; Isaiah 7:9; Mark 2:6–8"> Jeroboam said in his heart, “Now the kingdom might revert to the house of David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5–7; Proverbs 29:25; Deuteronomy 12:14"> If these people go up to offer sacrifices in the house of the LORD at Jerusalem, their hearts will return to their lord, Rehoboam king of Judah; then they will kill me and return to Rehoboam king of Judah.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 10:29; Exodus 32:8; 2 Kings 17:16"> After seeking advice, the king made two golden calves and said to the people, “Going up to Jerusalem is too much for you. Here, O Israel, are your gods, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Judges 18:27–31; Hosea 4:15"> One calf he set up in Bethel, and the other in Dan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 17:21; 1 Kings 13:34; 2 Kings 10:31"> And this thing became a sin; the people walked as far as Dan to worship before one of the calves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:9; 2 Kings 17:32; 1 Kings 13:32–33"> Jeroboam also built shrines on the high places and appointed from every class of people priests who were not Levites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 8:5; 1 Kings 8:2; Amos 7:10–13"> And Jeroboam ordained a feast on the fifteenth day of the eighth month, like the feast that was in Judah, and he offered sacrifices on the altar; he made this offering in Bethel to sacrifice to the calves he had set up, and he installed priests in Bethel for the high places he had set up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 13:1; Numbers 15:39; Matthew 15:6"> On the fifteenth day of the eighth month, a month of his own choosing, Jeroboam offered sacrifices on the altar he had set up in Bethel. So he ordained a feast for the Israelites, offered sacrifices on the altar, and burned incense.</span>
</div>
