</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:1; 1 Kings 14:31; Matthew 1:7–8"> And Abijam rested with his fathers and was buried in the City of David, and his son Asa reigned in his place.</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:2; 2 Chronicles 15:17; 1 Kings 15:3"> And Asa did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, as his father David had done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 22:46; 1 Kings 14:23–24; 2 Chronicles 14:2–5"> He banished the male shrine prostitutes from the land and removed all the idols that his fathers had made.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 32:20; Deuteronomy 7:5; Zechariah 13:3"> He also removed his grandmother Maacah from her position as queen mother because she had made a detestable Asherah pole. Asa chopped down the pole and burned it in the Kidron Valley.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 22:43; 1 Kings 8:61; 1 Kings 15:3"> The high places were not removed, but Asa’s heart was fully devoted to the LORD all his days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 7:51; 2 Chronicles 14:13; 2 Chronicles 15:18"> And he brought into the house of the LORD the silver and gold and the articles that he and his father had dedicated.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 15:32; 1 Kings 14:30; 2 Chronicles 16:1–6"> Now there was war between Asa and Baasha king of Israel throughout their days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 12:27; 2 Chronicles 16:1–6; Joshua 18:25"> Baasha king of Israel went to war against Judah and fortified Ramah to prevent anyone from leaving or entering the territory of Asa king of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 14:26; 2 Kings 12:18; 1 Kings 11:23–24"> So Asa withdrew all the silver and gold that remained in the treasuries of the house of the LORD and the royal palace. He entrusted it to his servants and sent them with this message to Ben-hadad son of Tabrimmon, the son of Hezion, the king of Aram, who was ruling in Damascus:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:31; 2 Chronicles 16:7; Romans 3:8"> “Let there be a treaty between me and you as there was between my father and your father. See, I have sent you a gift of silver and gold. Now go and break your treaty with Baasha king of Israel, so that he will withdraw from me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 15:29; Judges 18:29; Joshua 11:2"> And Ben-hadad listened to King Asa and sent the commanders of his armies against the cities of Israel, conquering Ijon, Dan, Abel-beth-maacah, and the whole land of Naphtali, including the region of Chinnereth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 14:17; 1 Kings 16:15–18; 2 Chronicles 16:5"> When Baasha learned of this, he stopped fortifying Ramah and withdrew to Tirzah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 21:17; Joshua 18:24; Joshua 18:26"> Then King Asa summoned all the men of Judah, with no exceptions, and they carried away the stones of Ramah and the timbers Baasha had used for building. And with these materials King Asa built up Geba of Benjamin, as well as Mizpah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:11–14; 1 Kings 14:29–31; 1 Kings 15:7–8"> Now the rest of the acts of Asa, along with all his might, all his accomplishments, and the cities he built, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? In his old age, however, he became diseased in his feet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 1:8; 1 Kings 22:41–43; 2 Chronicles 17:1–9"> And Asa rested with his fathers and was buried with them in the city of his father David, and his son Jehoshaphat reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 14:20; 1 Kings 14:12"> In the second year of Asa’s reign over Judah, Nadab son of Jeroboam became king of Israel, and he reigned two years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 14:16; 1 Kings 15:34; 1 Kings 15:30"> And he did evil in the sight of the LORD and walked in the way of his father and in his sin, which he had caused Israel to commit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 19:44; Joshua 21:23; 1 Kings 14:14"> Then Baasha son of Ahijah of the house of Issachar conspired against Nadab, and Baasha struck him down at Gibbethon of the Philistines while Nadab and all Israel were besieging the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35"> In the third year of Asa’s reign over Judah, Baasha killed Nadab and reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 14:9–16; 2 Kings 19:25; 2 Kings 10:10–11"> As soon as Baasha became king, he struck down the entire household of Jeroboam. He did not leave to Jeroboam anyone who breathed, but destroyed them all according to the word that the LORD had spoken through His servant Ahijah the Shilonite,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 15:26; 1 Kings 14:22; 1 Kings 14:9–16"> because of the sins Jeroboam had committed and had caused Israel to commit, and because he had provoked the LORD, the God of Israel, to anger.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 14:19; 1 Kings 16:20; 1 Kings 16:14"> As for the rest of the acts of Nadab, along with all his accomplishments, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 15:16"> And there was war between Asa and Baasha king of Israel throughout their days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 16:8"> In the third year of Asa’s reign over Judah, Baasha son of Ahijah became king of all Israel, and he reigned in Tirzah twenty-four years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 15:26; 1 Kings 14:16; 1 Kings 12:28–29"> And Baasha did evil in the sight of the LORD and walked in the way of Jeroboam and in his sin, which he had caused Israel to commit.</span>
