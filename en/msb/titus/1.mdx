</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 14:23; 1 Timothy 1:3; Ecclesiastes 12:9"> The reason I left you in Crete was that you would set in order what was unfinished and appoint elders in every town, as I directed you.</span>

<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; 2 Peter 2:10; 1 Peter 4:10"> As God’s steward, an overseer must be above reproach—not self-willed, not quick-tempered, not given to drunkenness, not violent, not greedy for money.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; Titus 2:7; 1 Kings 5:7"> Instead, he must be hospitable, a lover of good, self-controlled, upright, holy, and disciplined.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:15; 2 Timothy 1:13; 1 Timothy 6:3"> He must hold firmly to the faithful word as it was taught, so that he can encourage others by sound teaching and refute those who contradict it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:6; 1 John 4:1; Galatians 2:4"> For many are rebellious and full of empty talk and deception, especially those of the circumcision,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:6; 1 Timothy 6:5; Romans 3:19"> who must be silenced. For the sake of dishonorable gain, they undermine entire households and teach things they should not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 16:18; 1 Timothy 4:2; Acts 17:28"> As one of their own prophets has said, “Cretans are always liars, evil beasts, lazy gluttons.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 2:2; 1 Timothy 5:20; Titus 2:15"> This testimony is true. Therefore rebuke them sternly, so that they will be sound in the faith</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:4; Colossians 2:22; Galatians 4:9"> and will pay no attention to Jewish myths or to the commands of men who have rejected the truth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Romans 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:23"> To the pure, all things are pure; but to the defiled and unbelieving, nothing is pure. Indeed, both their minds and their consciences are defiled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 2:4; Isaiah 29:13; Ephesians 5:6"> They profess to know God, but by their actions they deny Him. They are detestable, disobedient, and unfit for any good deed.</span>
