<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:1; Acts 16:12–15; Romans 1:1"> Paul and Timothy, servants of Jesus Christ, To all the saints in Christ Jesus at Philippi, together with the overseers and deacons:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 2 Corinthians 1:2; 1 Peter 1:2"> Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:4; 1 Thessalonians 1:2–3; Philemon 1:4–5"> I thank my God every time I remember you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:2; Romans 1:9; 2 John 1:4"> In every prayer for all of you, I always pray with joy,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:14–15; Philippians 1:7; Hebrews 3:14"> because of your partnership in the gospel from the first day until now,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23–24; Philippians 2:13; Ephesians 4:12"> being confident of this, that He who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion until the day of Christ Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:3; Philippians 1:5; Colossians 4:18"> It is right for me to feel this way about all of you, since I have you in my heart. For in my chains and in my defense and confirmation of the gospel, you are all partners in grace with me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:1; Romans 1:9; 2 Timothy 1:4"> God is my witness how I long for all of you with the affection of Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:12; Colossians 1:9; 2 Peter 1:5–6"> And this is my prayer: that your love may abound more and more in knowledge and depth of insight,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> so that you may be able to test and prove what is best and may be pure and blameless for the day of Christ,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 12:11; John 15:8; John 15:4–5"> filled with the fruits of righteousness that come through Jesus Christ, to the glory and praise of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:9; Romans 8:28; Acts 11:19–21"> Now I want you to know, brothers, that my circumstances have actually served to advance the gospel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:9; Ephesians 3:1; Philippians 1:7"> As a result, it has become clear throughout the whole palace guard and to everyone else that I am in chains for Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 6:19–20; 1 Thessalonians 2:2; Luke 1:74"> And most of the brothers, confident in the Lord by my chains, now dare more greatly to speak the word without fear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:17–18; Philippians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 11:13"> It is true that some preach Christ out of envy and rivalry, but others out of goodwill.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 2 Corinthians 4:1–2; Psalm 69:26"> The former preach Christ out of selfish ambition, not sincerely, supposing that they can add to the distress of my chains.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:11–12; Philippians 2:3; Philippians 1:7"> The latter do so in love, knowing that I am appointed for the defense of the gospel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 9:50; Mark 9:38–40; 1 Corinthians 15:11"> What then is the issue? Just this: that in every way, whether by false motives or true, Christ is preached. And in this I rejoice. Yes, and I will continue to rejoice,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Acts 16:7; 2 Corinthians 1:11"> because I know that through your prayers and the provision of the Spirit of Jesus Christ, my distress will turn out for my deliverance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 4:16; 1 Corinthians 6:20; Isaiah 54:4"> I eagerly expect and hope that I will in no way be ashamed, but will have complete boldness so that now as always Christ will be exalted in my body, whether by life or by death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; 2 Corinthians 5:8; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> For to me, to live is Christ, and to die is gain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; Galatians 2:20; Philippians 1:24"> But if I go on living in the body, this will mean fruitful labor for me. So what shall I choose? I do not know.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:8; Psalm 17:15; Revelation 14:13"> I am torn between the two. I desire to depart and be with Christ, which is far better.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 16:7; Philippians 1:22; Philippians 1:25–26"> But it is more necessary for you that I remain in the body.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 2:24; 2 Corinthians 1:24; Ephesians 4:11–13"> Convinced of this, I know that I will remain and will continue with all of you for your progress and joy in the faith,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:14; 2 Corinthians 5:12; Philippians 3:3"> so that through my coming to you again your exultation in Christ Jesus will resound on account of me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 4:1; 1 Corinthians 16:13–14; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> Nevertheless, conduct yourselves in a manner worthy of the gospel of Christ. Then, whether I come and see you or only hear about you in my absence, I will know that you stand firm in one spirit, contending side by side for the faith of the gospel,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7–8; 2 Thessalonians 1:5–6; Matthew 10:28"> without being frightened in any way by those who oppose you. This is indeed a clear sign of their destruction but of your salvation, and it is from God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 4:13; Acts 5:41"> For it has been granted to you on behalf of Christ not only to believe in Him, but also to suffer for Him,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:2; Acts 16:19–40; 2 Timothy 4:7"> since you are encountering the same struggle you saw I had, and now hear that I still have.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
