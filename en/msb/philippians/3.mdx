<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 5:11; Philippians 4:4; James 1:2"> Finally, my brothers, rejoice in the Lord. It is no trouble for me to write the same things to you again, and it is a safeguard for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:13; Titus 1:16; Revelation 22:15"> Watch out for those dogs, those workers of evil, those mutilators of the flesh!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:11; Deuteronomy 30:6; Romans 7:6"> For it is we who are the circumcision, we who worship by the Spirit of God, who glory in Christ Jesus, and who put no confidence in the flesh—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:18–22"> though I myself could have such confidence. If anyone else thinks he has grounds for confidence in the flesh, I have more:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:22; Romans 11:1; Acts 23:6"> circumcised on the eighth day, of the people of Israel, of the tribe of Benjamin; a Hebrew of Hebrews; as to the law, a Pharisee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 1:13–14; Acts 8:3; Acts 26:9–10"> as to zeal, persecuting the church; as to righteousness in the law, faultless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 14:33; Matthew 16:26; Matthew 13:44–46"> But whatever was gain to me I count as loss for the sake of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; Romans 8:18; Philippians 3:7"> More than that, I count all things as loss compared to the surpassing excellence of knowing Christ Jesus my Lord, for whom I have lost all things. I consider them rubbish, that I may gain Christ</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Galatians 2:16"> and be found in Him, not having my own righteousness from the law, but that which is through faith in Christ, the righteousness from God on the basis of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; Romans 8:17; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> I want to know Christ and the power of His resurrection and the fellowship of His sufferings, being conformed to Him in His death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 20:35–36; Revelation 20:5; Acts 23:6"> and so, somehow, to attain to the resurrection from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:11–12; 2 Peter 1:5–8; Psalm 138:8"> Not that I have already obtained all this, or have already been made perfect, but I press on to take hold of that for which Christ Jesus took hold of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:62; Hebrews 12:1–2; Philippians 3:12"> Brothers, I do not consider myself yet to have taken hold of it. But one thing I do: Forgetting what is behind and straining toward what is ahead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24; 1 Peter 5:10; Revelation 3:21"> I press on toward the goal to win the prize of God’s heavenly calling in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 7:17; James 1:4–5; Proverbs 2:3–6"> All of us who are mature should embrace this point of view. And if you think differently about some issue, God will reveal this to you as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 2:6; Revelation 3:3; Revelation 2:4–5"> Nevertheless, we must live up to the standard we have already attained, being of the same mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Philippians 4:9; 1 Corinthians 4:16; 1 Peter 5:3"> Join one another in following my example, brothers, and carefully observe those who walk according to the pattern we set for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 4:17; Isaiah 8:11; Galatians 6:12"> For as I have often told you before, and now say again even with tears: Many live as enemies of the cross of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 16:18; Romans 8:5–7; 2 Peter 2:3"> Their end is destruction, their god is their belly, and their glory is in their shame. Their minds are set on earthly things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 2:19; Colossians 3:1–3; 2 Corinthians 4:18–1"> But our citizenship is in heaven, and we eagerly await a Savior from there, the Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 3:2; Colossians 3:4; 1 Corinthians 15:42–56"> who, by the power that enables Him to subject all things to Himself, will transform our lowly bodies to be like His glorious body.</span>
