</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 12:15–20; Exodus 34:18; Leviticus 23:8"> For seven days you are to eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day there shall be a feast to the LORD.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:8; Deuteronomy 11:18–19; Numbers 15:39"> It shall be a sign for you on your hand and a reminder on your forehead that the Law of the LORD is to be on your lips. For with a mighty hand the LORD brought you out of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Exodus 12:24–25; Exodus 23:15"> Therefore you shall keep this statute at the appointed time year after year.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 13:5"> And after the LORD brings you into the land of the Canaanites and gives it to you, as He swore to you and your fathers,</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 18:15; Leviticus 27:26; Exodus 34:19"> you are to present to the LORD the firstborn male of every womb. All the firstborn males of your livestock belong to the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 34:20; Numbers 18:15–17; Revelation 14:4"> You must redeem every firstborn donkey with a lamb, and if you do not redeem it, you are to break its neck. And every firstborn of your sons you must redeem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 13:3; Joshua 4:6; Psalm 145:4"> In the future, when your son asks you, ‘What does this mean?’ you are to tell him, ‘With a mighty hand the LORD brought us out of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 12:29"> And when Pharaoh stubbornly refused to let us go, the LORD killed every firstborn in the land of Egypt, both of man and beast. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 13:9; Deuteronomy 11:18; Deuteronomy 6:7–9"> So it shall serve as a sign on your hand and a symbol on your forehead, for with a mighty hand the LORD brought us out of Egypt.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:11–12; Nehemiah 9:17; Numbers 14:1–4"> When Pharaoh let the people go, God did not lead them along the road through the land of the Philistines, though it was shorter. For God said, “If the people face war, they might change their minds and return to Egypt.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 14:2; Deuteronomy 32:10; Numbers 33:6–8"> So God led the people around by the way of the wilderness toward the Red Sea. And the Israelites left the land of Egypt arrayed for battle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:24–25; Joshua 24:32; Acts 7:16"> Moses took the bones of Joseph with him because Joseph had made the sons of Israel swear a solemn oath when he said, “God will surely attend to you, and then you must carry my bones with you from this place.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 33:5–6; Exodus 12:37"> They set out from Succoth and camped at Etham on the edge of the wilderness.</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:1–2; Nehemiah 9:12; Deuteronomy 1:33"> And the LORD went before them in a pillar of cloud to guide their way by day, and in a pillar of fire to give them light by night, so that they could travel by day or night.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 121:5–8; Revelation 10:1"> Neither the pillar of cloud by day nor the pillar of fire by night left its place before the people.</span>
