</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 21:2; Isaiah 50:1; Matthew 18:25"> But if it happens after sunrise, there is guilt for his bloodshed. A thief must make full restitution; if he has nothing, he himself shall be sold for his theft.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 22:7; Exodus 22:1; Exodus 22:9"> If what was stolen is actually found alive in his possession—whether ox or donkey or sheep—he must pay back double.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 22:3; Exodus 22:12; Job 20:18"> If a man grazes his livestock in a field or vineyard and allows them to stray so that they graze in someone else’s field, he must make restitution from the best of his own field or vineyard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:9; Exodus 21:33–34; 2 Samuel 14:30–31"> If a fire breaks out and spreads to thornbushes so that it consumes stacked or standing grain, or the whole field, the one who started the fire must make full restitution.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 22:4; Proverbs 6:30–31; Jeremiah 2:26"> If a man gives his neighbor money or goods for safekeeping and they are stolen from the neighbor’s house, the thief, if caught, must pay back double.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Deuteronomy 16:18; Exodus 21:6"> If the thief is not found, the owner of the house must appear before the judges to determine whether he has taken his neighbor’s property.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:1; Exodus 22:4; Numbers 5:6–7"> In all cases of illegal possession of an ox, a donkey, a sheep, a garment, or any lost item that someone claims, ‘This is mine,’ both parties shall bring their cases before the judges. The one whom the judges find guilty must pay back double to his neighbor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:48; 2 Timothy 1:12; Luke 16:11"> If a man gives a donkey, an ox, a sheep, or any other animal to be cared for by his neighbor, but it dies or is injured or stolen while no one is watching,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:16; Leviticus 5:1; 1 Kings 2:42–43"> an oath before the LORD shall be made between the parties to determine whether or not the man has taken his neighbor’s property. The owner must accept the oath and require no restitution.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:39; Exodus 22:7"> But if the animal was actually stolen from the neighbor, he must make restitution to the owner.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nahum 2:12; Micah 5:8; Ezekiel 4:14"> If the animal was torn to pieces, he shall bring it as evidence; he need not make restitution for the torn carcass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Exodus 21:34; Deuteronomy 15:2"> If a man borrows an animal from his neighbor and it is injured or dies while its owner is not present, he must make full restitution.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 8:10"> If the owner was present, no restitution is required. If the animal was rented, the fee covers the loss.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:28–29; Genesis 34:2–4"> If a man seduces a virgin who is not pledged in marriage and sleeps with her, he must pay the full dowry for her to be his wife.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 34:12; 1 Samuel 18:25; Deuteronomy 22:29"> If her father absolutely refuses to give her to him, the man still must pay an amount comparable to the bridal price of a virgin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 20:27; Deuteronomy 18:10–11; 1 Samuel 28:3"> You must not allow a sorceress to live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Deuteronomy 27:21; Leviticus 20:15–16"> Whoever lies with an animal must surely be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–5; Numbers 25:7–8; Deuteronomy 18:20"> If anyone sacrifices to any god other than the LORD alone, he must be set apart for destruction.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:9; Deuteronomy 10:19; Leviticus 19:33"> You must not exploit or oppress a foreign resident, for you yourselves were foreigners in the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:17; Isaiah 1:17; Isaiah 1:23"> You must not mistreat any widow or orphan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 18:6; Psalm 10:17–18; Luke 18:7"> If you do mistreat them, and they cry out to Me in distress, I will surely hear their cry.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 109:9; Jeremiah 15:8; Psalm 76:7"> My anger will be kindled, and I will kill you with the sword; then your wives will become widows and your children will be fatherless.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 15:5; Leviticus 25:35–37; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> If you lend money to one of My people among you who is poor, you must not act as a creditor to him; you are not to charge him interest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 20:16; Amos 2:8; Deuteronomy 24:6"> If you take your neighbor’s cloak as collateral, return it to him by sunset,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 34:6; 2 Chronicles 30:9; Psalm 136:10–11"> because his cloak is the only covering he has for his body. What else will he sleep in? And if he cries out to Me, I will hear, for I am compassionate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 23:5; Ecclesiastes 10:20; Exodus 22:8–9"> You must not blaspheme God or curse the ruler of your people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Exodus 13:2; James 1:18"> You must not hold back offerings from your granaries or vats. You are to give Me the firstborn of your sons.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 22:27; Deuteronomy 15:19; Exodus 13:12"> You shall do likewise with your cattle and your sheep. Let them stay with their mothers for seven days, but on the eighth day you are to give them to Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:14; Leviticus 19:2; Deuteronomy 14:21"> You are to be My holy people. You must not eat the meat of a mauled animal found in the field; you are to throw it to the dogs.</span>
</div>
