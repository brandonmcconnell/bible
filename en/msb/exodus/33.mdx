</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 99:7; Exodus 31:18; Exodus 13:21–22"> As Moses entered the tent, the pillar of cloud would come down and remain at the entrance, and the LORD would speak with Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 8:22; 1 Kings 8:14; Exodus 4:31"> When all the people saw the pillar of cloud standing at the entrance to the tent, they would stand up and worship, each one at the entrance to his own tent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 12:8; Deuteronomy 34:10; Exodus 24:13"> Thus the LORD would speak to Moses face to face, as a man speaks to his friend. Then Moses would return to the camp, but his young assistant Joshua son of Nun would not leave the tent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 33:17; Jeremiah 1:5; John 10:14–15"> Then Moses said to the LORD, “Look, You have been telling me, ‘Lead this people up,’ but You have not let me know whom You will send with me. Yet You have said, ‘I know you by name, and you have found favor in My sight.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 119:33; Psalm 25:4; Psalm 27:11"> Now if indeed I have found favor in Your sight, please let me know Your ways, that I may know You and find favor in Your sight. Remember that this nation is Your people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 21:44; Matthew 11:28; Joshua 1:5"> And the LORD answered, “My Presence will go with you, and I will give you rest.”</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:9; Psalm 4:6; Exodus 33:3"> “If Your Presence does not go with us,” Moses replied, “do not lead us up from here.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:7; Numbers 14:14; 1 Kings 8:53"> For how then can it be known that Your people and I have found favor in Your sight, unless You go with us? How else will we be distinguished from all the other people on the face of the earth?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; 1 John 5:14–15; Exodus 33:12"> So the LORD said to Moses, “I will do this very thing you have asked, for you have found favor in My sight, and I know you by name.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:23; Exodus 33:20; Titus 2:13"> Then Moses said, “Please show me Your glory.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 9:15–18; Ephesians 1:6–8; Jeremiah 31:14"> “I will cause all My goodness to pass before you,” the LORD replied, “and I will proclaim My name—the LORD—in your presence. I will have mercy on whom I have mercy, and I will have compassion on whom I have compassion.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 32:30; John 1:18; 1 Timothy 6:16"> But He added, “You cannot see My face, for no one can see Me and live.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:31; Zechariah 3:7; Joshua 20:4"> The LORD continued, “There is a place near Me where you are to stand upon a rock,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 91:1; Isaiah 2:21; Psalm 91:4"> and when My glory passes by, I will put you in a cleft of the rock and cover you with My hand until I have passed by.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 33:20; John 1:18; 1 Timothy 6:16"> Then I will take My hand away, and you will see My back; but My face must not be seen.”</span>
