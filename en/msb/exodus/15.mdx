<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 15:3; Exodus 15:21; Psalm 106:12"> Then Moses and the Israelites sang this song to the LORD: “I will sing to the LORD, for He is highly exalted. The horse and rider He has thrown into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalm 118:14; Psalm 18:46"> The LORD is my strength and my song, and He has become my salvation. He is my God, and I will praise Him, my father’s God, and I will exalt Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 24:8; Exodus 14:14; Revelation 19:11–21"> The LORD is a warrior, the LORD is His name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 14:13–28; Exodus 14:6–7"> Pharaoh’s chariots and army He has cast into the sea; the finest of his officers are drowned in the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:11; Exodus 15:10; Exodus 14:28"> The depths have covered them; they sank there like a stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 118:15–16; Isaiah 51:9; Revelation 2:27"> Your right hand, O LORD, is majestic in power; Your right hand, O LORD, has shattered the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 47:14; Malachi 4:1"> You overthrew Your adversaries by Your great majesty. You unleashed Your burning wrath; it consumed them like stubble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 4:9; Psalm 78:13; Exodus 14:21–22"> At the blast of Your nostrils the waters piled up; like a wall the currents stood firm; the depths congealed in the heart of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Exodus 14:5; Isaiah 53:12"> The enemy declared, ‘I will pursue, I will overtake. I will divide the spoils; I will gorge myself on them. I will draw my sword; my hand will destroy them.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Deuteronomy 11:4; Exodus 15:5"> But You blew with Your breath, and the sea covered them. They sank like lead in the mighty waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 8:23; Psalm 89:5–8; 1 Samuel 2:2"> Who among the gods is like You, O LORD? Who is like You—majestic in holiness, revered with praises, performing wonders?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:6"> You stretched out Your right hand, and the earth swallowed them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 77:20; 1 Peter 1:5; Psalm 77:14–15"> With loving devotion You will lead the people You have redeemed; with Your strength You will guide them to Your holy dwelling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Joshua 2:9–10; Psalm 48:6"> The nations will hear and tremble; anguish will grip the dwellers of Philistia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 5:1; Joshua 2:11; Joshua 2:9"> Then the chiefs of Edom will be dismayed; trembling will seize the leaders of Moab; those who dwell in Canaan will melt away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Psalm 74:2; Joshua 2:9"> and terror and dread will fall on them. By the power of Your arm they will be as still as a stone until Your people pass by, O LORD, until the people You have bought pass by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 44:2; Psalm 80:8; Psalm 78:54–55"> You will bring them in and plant them on the mountain of Your inheritance—the place, O LORD, You have prepared for Your dwelling, the sanctuary, O Lord, Your hands have established.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 10:16; Psalm 29:10; Psalm 146:10"> The LORD will reign forever and ever!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:28–29; Exodus 14:22–23; Hebrews 11:29"> For when Pharaoh’s horses, chariots, and horsemen went into the sea, the LORD brought the waters of the sea back over them. But the Israelites walked through the sea on dry ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 150:4; 1 Samuel 18:6; Psalm 68:25"> Then Miriam the prophetess, Aaron’s sister, took a tambourine in her hand, and all the women followed her with tambourines and dancing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Revelation 15:3; Revelation 7:10–12"> And Miriam sang back to them: “Sing to the LORD, for He is highly exalted; the horse and rider He has thrown into the sea.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 16:7; 1 Samuel 15:7; Genesis 25:18"> Then Moses led Israel from the Red Sea, and they went out into the Desert of Shur. For three days they walked in the desert without finding water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ruth 1:20; Numbers 33:8"> And when they came to Marah, they could not drink the water there because it was bitter. (That is why it was named Marah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:2; Exodus 14:11; Numbers 20:2–5"> So the people grumbled against Moses, saying, “What are we to drink?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; Psalm 66:10; Judges 3:1"> And Moses cried out to the LORD, and the LORD showed him a log. And when he cast it into the waters, they were sweetened. There the LORD made for them a statute and an ordinance, and there He tested them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 103:3; Deuteronomy 7:15; Exodus 23:25"> saying, “If you will listen carefully to the voice of the LORD your God, and do what is right in His eyes, and pay attention to His commands, and keep all His statutes, then I will not bring on you any of the diseases I inflicted on the Egyptians. For I am the LORD who heals you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 22:2; Revelation 7:17; Numbers 33:9"> Then they came to Elim, where there were twelve springs of water and seventy palm trees, and they camped there by the waters.</span>
