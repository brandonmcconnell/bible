<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 101:5; Proverbs 19:5; Deuteronomy 19:16–21"> “You shall not spread a false report. Do not join the wicked by being a malicious witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 1:10–11; Proverbs 1:15; Luke 23:23–24"> You shall not follow the crowd in wrongdoing. When you testify in a lawsuit, do not pervert justice by siding with the crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:17; Leviticus 19:15; Psalm 82:2–3"> And do not show favoritism to a poor man in his lawsuit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:1–4; 1 Thessalonians 5:15; Matthew 5:44"> If you encounter your enemy’s stray ox or donkey, you must return it to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:4"> If you see the donkey of one who hates you fallen under its load, do not leave it there; you must help him with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 5:28; Leviticus 19:15"> You shall not deny justice to the poor in their lawsuits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Exodus 34:7; Exodus 23:1"> Stay far away from a false accusation. Do not kill the innocent or the just, for I will not acquit the guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Proverbs 17:23; Isaiah 5:23"> Do not accept a bribe, for a bribe blinds those who see and twists the words of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:19; Exodus 22:21; Deuteronomy 24:14–18"> Do not oppress a foreign resident, since you yourselves know how it feels to be foreigners; for you were foreigners in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 25:3–4"> For six years you are to sow your land and gather its produce,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 26:34–35; Leviticus 25:2–7; Leviticus 25:11–12"> but in the seventh year you must let it rest and lie fallow, so that the poor among your people may eat from the field and the wild animals may consume what they leave. Do the same with your vineyard and olive grove.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Exodus 34:21; Exodus 35:3"> For six days you are to do your work, but on the seventh day you must cease, so that your ox and your donkey may rest and the son of your maidservant may be refreshed, as well as the foreign resident.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 23:7; Zechariah 13:2; 1 Timothy 4:16"> Pay close attention to everything I have said to you. You must not invoke the names of other gods; they must not be heard on your lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 34:22–24; Leviticus 23:34"> Three times a year you are to celebrate a feast to Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 22:7; Exodus 34:20; Numbers 28:16–25"> You are to keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread as I commanded you: At the appointed time in the month of Abib you are to eat unleavened bread for seven days, because that was the month you came out of Egypt. No one may appear before Me empty-handed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 34:22; Exodus 22:29; Leviticus 23:9–21"> You are also to keep the Feast of Harvest with the firstfruits of the produce from what you sow in the field. And keep the Feast of Ingathering at the end of the year, when you gather your produce from the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 34:23; Exodus 23:14"> Three times a year all your males are to appear before the Lord GOD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 34:25; Leviticus 2:11; Deuteronomy 16:4"> You must not offer the blood of My sacrifices with anything leavened, nor may the fat of My feast remain until morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 34:26; Deuteronomy 14:21; Deuteronomy 26:10"> Bring the best of the firstfruits of your soil to the house of the LORD your God. You must not cook a young goat in its mother’s milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Psalm 91:11; Exodus 32:34"> Behold, I am sending an angel before you to protect you along the way and to bring you to the place I have prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 78:56; Psalm 78:40; Hebrews 12:25"> Pay attention to him and listen to his voice; do not defy him, for he will not forgive rebellion, since My Name is in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Deuteronomy 30:7; Zechariah 2:8"> But if you will listen carefully to his voice and do everything I say, I will be an enemy to your enemies and a foe to your foes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Isaiah 5:13; Genesis 15:19–21"> For My angel will go before you and bring you into the land of the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Canaanites, Hivites, and Jebusites, and I will annihilate them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 12:30–31; Numbers 33:52"> You must not bow down to their gods or serve them or follow their practices. Instead, you are to demolish them and smash their sacred stones to pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:15; Exodus 15:26; Joshua 22:5"> So you shall serve the LORD your God, and He will bless your bread and your water. And I will take away sickness from among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:14; Job 5:26; Deuteronomy 28:4"> No woman in your land will miscarry or be barren; I will fulfill the number of your days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Genesis 35:5; Deuteronomy 11:25"> I will send My terror ahead of you and throw into confusion every nation you encounter. I will make all your enemies turn and run.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:20; Joshua 24:11–12"> I will send the hornet before you to drive the Hivites and Canaanites and Hittites out of your way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:22; Joshua 17:12–13; Judges 3:1–4"> I will not drive them out before you in a single year; otherwise the land would become desolate and wild animals would multiply against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Little by little I will drive them out ahead of you, until you become fruitful and possess the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 21:44"> And I will establish your borders from the Red Sea to the Sea of the Philistines, and from the desert to the Euphrates. For I will deliver the inhabitants into your hand, and you will drive them out before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Exodus 34:12; Exodus 34:15"> You shall make no covenant with them or with their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalm 106:36; Deuteronomy 7:16; Exodus 34:12"> They must not remain in your land, lest they cause you to sin against Me. For if you serve their gods, it will surely be a snare to you.”</span>
