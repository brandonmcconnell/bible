<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Nehemiah 11:2; Ezra 2:68"> “Tell the Israelites to bring Me an offering. You are to receive My offering from every man whose heart compels him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:9; Job 28:2"> This is the offering you are to accept from them: gold, silver, and bronze;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Genesis 41:42; Ezekiel 16:10"> blue, purple, and scarlet yarn; fine linen and goat hair;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 26:37–1; Exodus 26:14–15; Exodus 36:20"> ram skins dyed red and fine leather; acacia wood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:37; Exodus 40:24–25"> olive oil for the light; spices for the anointing oil and for the fragrant incense;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Exodus 28:6–30"> and onyx stones and gemstones to be mounted on the ephod and breastpiece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Kings 6:13; Revelation 21:3"> And they are to make a sanctuary for Me, so that I may dwell among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Hebrews 8:5; Acts 7:44"> You must make the tabernacle and design all its furnishings according to the pattern I show you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Exodus 37:1–3; Deuteronomy 10:1–3"> And they are to construct an ark of acacia wood, two and a half cubits long, a cubit and a half wide, and a cubit and a half high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 25:24; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:4"> Overlay it with pure gold both inside and out, and make a gold molding around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 27:7; Exodus 25:26; Exodus 25:15"> Cast four gold rings for it and fasten them to its four feet, two rings on one side and two on the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:15; Numbers 4:11; Exodus 27:6"> And make poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Insert the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark, in order to carry it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:8; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> The poles are to remain in the rings of the ark; they must not be removed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:26; 1 Kings 8:9; Hebrews 9:4"> And place inside the ark the Testimony, which I will give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 37:6; Hebrews 4:16"> And you are to construct a mercy seat of pure gold, two and a half cubits long and a cubit and a half wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:20; Hebrews 9:5; 1 Kings 8:6–7"> Make two cherubim of hammered gold at the ends of the mercy seat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> one cherub on one end and one on the other, all made from one piece of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:18; 1 Kings 8:7; Hebrews 9:5"> And the cherubim are to have wings that spread upward, overshadowing the mercy seat. The cherubim are to face each other, looking toward the mercy seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:34; Romans 10:4; Exodus 25:16–17"> Set the mercy seat atop the ark and put the Testimony that I will give you into the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:42–43; Numbers 7:89; Isaiah 37:16"> And I will meet with you there above the mercy seat, between the two cherubim that are over the ark of the Testimony; I will speak with you about all that I command you regarding the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 9:2; Exodus 37:10–16; 1 Kings 7:48"> You are also to make a table of acacia wood two cubits long, a cubit wide, and a cubit and a half high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; 1 Kings 6:20–22"> Overlay it with pure gold and make a gold molding around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:2; Exodus 30:3"> And make a rim around it a handbreadth wide and put a gold molding on the rim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Make four gold rings for the table and fasten them to the four corners at its four legs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Exodus 25:28"> The rings are to be close to the rim, to serve as holders for the poles used to carry the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Acts 9:15; Numbers 10:17"> Make the poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold, so that the table may be carried with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 4:7; Exodus 37:16; Revelation 3:20"> You are also to make the plates and dishes, as well as the pitchers and bowls for pouring drink offerings. Make them out of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–9; Matthew 12:4; Exodus 39:36"> And place the Bread of the Presence on the table before Me at all times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 7:49; Zechariah 4:2; Exodus 37:17–24"> Then you are to make a lampstand of pure, hammered gold. It shall be made of one piece, including its base and shaft, its cups, and its buds and petals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Six branches are to extend from the sides of the lampstand—three on one side and three on the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 17:4–8; Jeremiah 1:11–12; Zechariah 4:3"> There are to be three cups shaped like almond blossoms on the first branch, each with buds and petals, three on the next branch, and the same for all six branches that extend from the lampstand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And on the lampstand there shall be four cups shaped like almond blossoms with buds and petals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> For the six branches that extend from the lampstand, a bud must be under the first pair of branches, a bud under the second pair, and a bud under the third pair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:18; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 9:15"> The buds and branches are to be all of one piece with the lampstand, hammered out of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 8:2; Revelation 1:20–1; Leviticus 24:2–4"> Make seven lamps and set them up on the lampstand so that they illuminate the area in front of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Kings 12:13; Jeremiah 52:18; 2 Kings 25:14"> The wick trimmers and their trays must be of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> The lampstand and all these utensils shall be made from a talent of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Exodus 26:30; Acts 7:44"> See to it that you make everything according to the pattern shown you on the mountain.</span>
