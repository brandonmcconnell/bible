<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 36:8–19; Exodus 26:36; Exodus 26:31"> “You are to construct the tabernacle itself with ten curtains of finely spun linen, each with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and cherubim skillfully worked into them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Numbers 4:25; 1 Chronicles 17:1"> Each curtain shall be twenty-eight cubits long and four cubits wide—all curtains the same size.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 36:10; Colossians 2:2; Ephesians 2:21–22"> Five of the curtains are to be joined together, and the other five joined as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 36:11–12; Exodus 36:17; Exodus 26:10–11"> Make loops of blue material on the edge of the end curtain in the first set, and do the same for the end curtain in the second set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Make fifty loops on one curtain and fifty loops on the end curtain of the second set, so that the loops line up opposite one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 36:13; Exodus 26:11; Exodus 26:33"> Make fifty gold clasps as well, and join the curtains together with the clasps, so that the tabernacle will be a unit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 26:14; Psalm 45:13; Exodus 26:9"> You are to make curtains of goat hair for the tent over the tabernacle—eleven curtains in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 26:13; Exodus 26:2"> Each of the eleven curtains is to be the same size—thirty cubits long and four cubits wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Join five of the curtains into one set and the other six into another. Then fold the sixth curtain over double at the front of the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Make fifty loops along the edge of the end curtain in the first set, and fifty loops along the edge of the corresponding curtain in the second set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:3; Exodus 26:6"> Make fifty bronze clasps and put them through the loops to join the tent together as a unit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> As for the overlap that remains of the tent curtains, the half curtain that is left over shall hang down over the back of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 26:2; Exodus 26:8"> And the tent curtains will be a cubit longer on either side, and the excess will hang over the sides of the tabernacle to cover it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 36:19; Exodus 25:5; Exodus 35:23"> Also make a covering for the tent out of ram skins dyed red, and over that a covering of fine leather.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 36:20–33; Numbers 4:31–32; Exodus 40:17–18"> You are to construct upright frames of acacia wood for the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Each frame is to be ten cubits long and a cubit and a half wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 36:22; Exodus 26:19; Exodus 36:24"> Two tenons must be connected to each other for each frame. Make all the frames of the tabernacle in this way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Construct twenty frames for the south side of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 38:27; Exodus 38:30–31; Exodus 26:37"> with forty silver bases under the twenty frames—two bases for each frame, one under each tenon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For the second side of the tabernacle, the north side, make twenty frames</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and forty silver bases—two bases under each frame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Make six frames for the rear of the tabernacle, the west side,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and two frames for the two back corners of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 36:29–30; Psalm 133:1–3; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> coupled together from bottom to top and fitted into a single ring. These will serve as the two corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> So there are to be eight frames and sixteen silver bases—two under each frame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 15:1; Numbers 4:31; Galatians 6:1–2"> You are also to make five crossbars of acacia wood for the frames on one side of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> five for those on the other side, and five for those on the rear side of the tabernacle, to the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The central crossbar in the middle of the frames shall extend from one end to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 25:11–12"> Overlay the frames with gold and make gold rings to hold the crossbars. Also overlay the crossbars with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Hebrews 8:5; Exodus 25:9"> So you are to set up the tabernacle according to the pattern shown you on the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; 2 Chronicles 3:14; Exodus 36:35"> Make a veil of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely spun linen, with cherubim skillfully worked into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 26:37; Exodus 36:38; Esther 1:6"> Hang it with gold hooks on four posts of acacia wood, overlaid with gold and standing on four silver bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:2–3; Leviticus 16:2; Exodus 40:21"> And hang the veil from the clasps and place the ark of the Testimony behind the veil. So the veil will separate the Holy Place from the Most Holy Place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 40:20; Exodus 25:21; Hebrews 9:5"> Put the mercy seat on the ark of the Testimony in the Most Holy Place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 40:24; Exodus 40:22; Hebrews 9:2"> And place the table outside the veil on the north side of the tabernacle, and put the lampstand opposite the table, on the south side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 36:37; Numbers 9:15; Exodus 26:31"> For the entrance to the tent, you are to make a curtain embroidered with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely spun linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 36:38"> Make five posts of acacia wood for the curtain, overlay them with gold hooks, and cast five bronze bases for them.</span>
