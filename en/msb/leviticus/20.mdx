<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:21; Leviticus 18:21; Leviticus 20:27"> “Tell the Israelites, ‘Any Israelite or foreigner living in Israel who gives any of his children to Molech must be put to death. The people of the land are to stone him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 23:38–39"> And I will set My face against that man and cut him off from his people, because by giving his offspring to Molech, he has defiled My sanctuary and profaned My holy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–5; Acts 17:30; 1 Kings 20:42"> And if the people of the land ever hide their eyes and fail to put to death the man who gives one of his children to Molech,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 106:39; Hosea 2:13; Hosea 2:5"> then I will set My face against that man and his family and cut off from among their people both him and all who follow him in prostituting themselves with Molech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Isaiah 8:19; Leviticus 20:27"> Whoever turns to mediums or spiritists to prostitute himself with them, I will also set My face against that person and cut him off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Leviticus 19:2; 1 Peter 1:15–16"> Consecrate yourselves, therefore, and be holy, because I am the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 31:13; James 1:22; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> And you shall keep My statutes and practice them. I am the LORD who sanctifies you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 21:17; Mark 7:10; Deuteronomy 27:16"> If anyone curses his father or mother, he must be put to death. He has cursed his father or mother; his blood shall be upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:4–5; Deuteronomy 22:22–24; Deuteronomy 5:18"> If a man commits adultery with another man’s wife—with the wife of his neighbor—both the adulterer and the adulteress must surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:20; Deuteronomy 27:23; Amos 2:7"> If a man lies with his father’s wife, he has uncovered his father’s nakedness. Both must surely be put to death; their blood is upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:15; Leviticus 18:23; Genesis 38:18"> If a man lies with his daughter-in-law, both must surely be put to death. They have acted perversely; their blood is upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9; Leviticus 18:22; Jude 1:7"> If a man lies with a man as with a woman, they have both committed an abomination. They must surely be put to death; their blood is upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 18:17; Deuteronomy 27:23; Joshua 7:15"> If a man marries both a woman and her mother, it is depraved. Both he and they must be burned in the fire, so that there will be no depravity among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Deuteronomy 27:21; Exodus 22:19"> If a man lies carnally with an animal, he must be put to death. And you are also to kill the animal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 21:32; Exodus 19:13; Hebrews 12:20"> If a woman approaches any animal to mate with it, you must kill both the woman and the animal. They must surely be put to death; their blood is upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 18:9; Deuteronomy 27:22; Ezekiel 22:11"> If a man marries his sister, whether the daughter of his father or of his mother, and they have sexual relations, it is a disgrace. They must be cut off in the sight of their people. He has uncovered the nakedness of his sister; he shall bear his iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 15:24; Leviticus 18:19; Ezekiel 18:6"> If a man lies with a menstruating woman and has sexual relations with her, he has exposed the source of her flow, and she has uncovered the source of her blood. Both of them must be cut off from among their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 18:6; Leviticus 18:12–30; Exodus 6:20"> You must not have sexual relations with the sister of your mother or your father, for it is exposing one’s own kin; both shall bear their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 18:14; Job 18:19; Luke 23:29"> If a man lies with his uncle’s wife, he has uncovered the nakedness of his uncle. They will bear their sin; they shall die childless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 18:16; Matthew 14:3–4"> If a man marries his brother’s wife, it is an act of impurity. He has uncovered the nakedness of his brother; they shall be childless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 18:25–28; Ezekiel 36:27; Leviticus 18:4–5"> You are therefore to keep all My statutes and ordinances, so that the land where I am bringing you to live will not vomit you out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:5; Leviticus 18:24; Leviticus 18:30"> You must not follow the statutes of the nations I am driving out before you. Because they did all these things, I abhorred them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:2; Exodus 6:8; Deuteronomy 7:6"> But I have told you that you will inherit their land, since I will give it to you as an inheritance—a land flowing with milk and honey. I am the LORD your God, who has set you apart from the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:3–21; Leviticus 11:1–47; Acts 10:11–15"> You are therefore to distinguish between clean and unclean animals and birds. Do not become contaminated by any animal or bird, or by anything that crawls on the ground; I have set these apart as unclean for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:15–16; Leviticus 20:7; Leviticus 20:24"> You are to be holy to Me because I, the LORD, am holy, and I have set you apart from the nations to be My own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Leviticus 20:6; 1 Samuel 28:7–9"> A man or a woman who is a medium or spiritist must surely be put to death. They shall be stoned; their blood is upon them.’”</span>
