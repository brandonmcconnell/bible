<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Leviticus 13:1; Leviticus 11:1"> And the LORD said to Moses and Aaron,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; 2 Samuel 3:29; Leviticus 22:4"> “Say to the Israelites, ‘When any man has a bodily discharge, the discharge is unclean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:20; Ezekiel 16:26; Leviticus 12:3"> This uncleanness is from his discharge, whether his body allows the discharge to flow or blocks it. So his discharge will bring about uncleanness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Ephesians 5:11; 1 Corinthians 15:33"> Any bed on which the man with the discharge lies will be unclean, and any furniture on which he sits will be unclean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 16:26; Leviticus 17:15"> Anyone who touches his bed must wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be unclean until evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 1:16"> Whoever sits on furniture on which the man with the discharge was sitting must wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be unclean until evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Whoever touches the body of the man with a discharge must wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be unclean until evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:4; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Isaiah 1:16"> If the man with the discharge spits on one who is clean, that person must wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be unclean until evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:34"> Any saddle on which the man with the discharge rides will be unclean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:8; Psalm 26:6; Leviticus 15:8"> Whoever touches anything that was under him will be unclean until evening, and whoever carries such things must wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be unclean until evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> If the man with the discharge touches anyone without first rinsing his hands with water, the one who was touched must wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be unclean until evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 11:32–33; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> Any clay pot that the man with the discharge touches must be broken, and any wooden utensil must be rinsed with water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 14:8; Leviticus 8:33; Leviticus 15:28"> When the man has been cleansed from his discharge, he must count off seven days for his cleansing, wash his clothes, and bathe himself in fresh water, and he shall be clean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 12:8; Leviticus 12:6; Hebrews 10:10"> On the eighth day he is to take two turtledoves or two young pigeons, come before the LORD at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, and give them to the priest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 14:30–31; Leviticus 4:26; Matthew 3:17"> The priest is to sacrifice them, one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering. In this way the priest will make atonement for the man before the LORD because of his discharge.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:10–11; Leviticus 22:4; Leviticus 15:5"> When a man has an emission of semen, he must bathe his whole body with water, and he will be unclean until evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Any clothing or leather on which there is an emission of semen must be washed with water, and it will remain unclean until evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–5; 1 Corinthians 6:12"> If a man lies with a woman and there is an emission of semen, both must bathe with water, and they will remain unclean until evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 12:2; Matthew 15:19; Mark 5:25"> When a woman has a discharge consisting of blood from her body, she will be unclean due to her menstruation for seven days, and anyone who touches her will be unclean until evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 15:4–9; Proverbs 2:16–19; Proverbs 6:35"> Anything on which she lies or sits during her menstruation will be unclean,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 7:14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Isaiah 22:14"> and anyone who touches her bed must wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be unclean until evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Whoever touches any furniture on which she was sitting must wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be unclean until evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And whether it is a bed or furniture on which she was sitting, whoever touches it will be unclean until evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 20:18; 1 Peter 2:11; Leviticus 18:19"> If a man lies with her and her menstrual flow touches him, he will be unclean for seven days, and any bed on which he lies will become unclean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 5:25; Matthew 9:20; Luke 8:43"> When a woman has a discharge of her blood for many days at a time other than her menstrual period, or if it continues beyond her period, she will be unclean all the days of her unclean discharge, just as she is during the days of her menstruation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Any bed on which she lies or any furniture on which she sits during the days of her discharge will be unclean, like her bed during her menstrual period.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 15:21; Hebrews 10:22; Leviticus 17:15–16"> Anyone who touches these things will be unclean; he must wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be unclean until evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 15:13–15; Galatians 3:13; Ephesians 1:6–7"> When a woman is cleansed of her discharge, she must count off seven days, and after that she will be ceremonially clean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> On the eighth day she is to take two turtledoves or two young pigeons and bring them to the priest at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> The priest is to sacrifice one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering. In this way the priest will make atonement for her before the LORD for her unclean discharge.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 19:20; Numbers 19:13; Ezekiel 5:11"> You must keep the children of Israel separate from their uncleanness, so that they do not die by defiling My tabernacle, which is among them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 11:46; Leviticus 13:59; Numbers 6:13"> This is the law of him who has a discharge, of the man who has an emission of semen whereby he is unclean,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 15:19; Leviticus 20:18; Leviticus 15:24–25"> of a woman in her menstrual period, of any male or female who has a discharge, and of a man who lies with an unclean woman.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
