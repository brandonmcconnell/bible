<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 2:22; Leviticus 15:19; Job 25:4"> “Say to the Israelites, ‘A woman who becomes pregnant and gives birth to a son will be unclean for seven days, as she is during the days of her menstruation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 2:21; Luke 1:59; John 7:22–23"> And on the eighth day the flesh of the boy’s foreskin is to be circumcised.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 2:22–23; Leviticus 15:25–28; Haggai 2:13"> The woman shall continue in purification from her bleeding for thirty-three days. She must not touch anything sacred or go into the sanctuary until the days of her purification are complete.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:14–15; Genesis 3:13; Leviticus 12:2"> If, however, she gives birth to a daughter, the woman will be unclean for two weeks as she is during her menstruation. Then she must continue in purification from her bleeding for sixty-six days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 2:22; 2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> When the days of her purification are complete, whether for a son or for a daughter, she is to bring to the priest at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting a year-old lamb for a burnt offering and a young pigeon or a turtledove for a sin offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 15:28–30; Hebrews 9:12–14; Leviticus 4:35"> And the priest will present them before the LORD and make atonement for her; and she shall be ceremonially cleansed from her flow of blood. This is the law for a woman giving birth, whether to a male or to a female.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 5:7; Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 1:14"> But if she cannot afford a lamb, she shall bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons, one for a burnt offering and the other for a sin offering. Then the priest will make atonement for her, and she will be clean.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
