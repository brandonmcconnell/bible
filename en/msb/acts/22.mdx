<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:2; Acts 28:17; Acts 13:26"> “Brothers and fathers, listen now to my defense before you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 21:40"> When they heard him speak to them in Hebrew, they became even more silent. Then Paul declared,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 26:5; Philippians 3:5–6; Acts 9:11"> “I am indeed a Jew, born in Tarsus of Cilicia, but raised in this city. I was educated at the feet of Gamaliel in strict conformity to the law of our fathers. I was just as zealous for God as any of you are today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 22:19–20; 1 Timothy 1:13–15; Acts 26:9–11"> I persecuted this Way even to the death, detaining both men and women and throwing them into prison,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 9:1–2; Luke 22:66; Acts 5:21"> as the high priest and the whole Council can testify about me. I even obtained letters from them to their brothers in Damascus, and I was on my way to apprehend these people and bring them to Jerusalem to be punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 9:3–8; Acts 26:12–18; Revelation 1:16"> About noon as I was approaching Damascus, suddenly a bright light from heaven flashed around me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:9; Matthew 25:45; Genesis 22:11"> I fell to the ground and heard a voice say to me, ‘Saul, Saul, why do you persecute Me?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 26:9; 1 Samuel 8:7; Exodus 16:7–8"> ‘Who are You, Lord?’ I asked. ‘I am Jesus of Nazareth, whom you are persecuting,’ He replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 9:7; Daniel 10:7; Acts 26:13"> My companions saw the light and were terrified, but they could not understand the voice of the One speaking to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 16:30; Acts 22:12–16; Acts 2:37"> Then I asked, ‘What should I do, Lord?’ ‘Get up and go into Damascus,’ He told me. ‘There you will be told all that you have been appointed to do.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 9:8–9; Acts 13:11; Isaiah 42:16"> Because the brilliance of the light had blinded me, my companions led me by the hand into Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:22; Acts 6:3; 3 John 1:12"> There a man named Ananias, a devout observer of the law who was highly regarded by all the Jews living there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 9:17–18; Philemon 1:16"> came and stood beside me. ‘Brother Saul,’ he said, ‘receive your sight.’ And at that moment I could see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 15:8; 1 Corinthians 9:1"> Then he said, ‘The God of our fathers has appointed you to know His will and to see the Righteous One and to hear His voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Acts 4:20; John 15:27"> You will be His witness to everyone of what you have seen and heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 2:21; Titus 3:5"> And now what are you waiting for? Get up, be baptized, and wash your sins away, calling on the name of the Lord.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:1–4; Acts 10:9–10; Acts 9:26–28"> Later, when I had returned to Jerusalem and was praying at the temple, I fell into a trance</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 9:29; Luke 21:21; Ezekiel 3:6–7"> and saw the Lord saying to me, ‘Hurry! Leave Jerusalem quickly, because the people here will not accept your testimony about Me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 22:4; Matthew 10:17; Acts 8:3"> ‘Lord,’ I answered, ‘they know very well that in one synagogue after another I imprisoned and beat those who believed in You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 7:58; Acts 8:1; Romans 1:32"> And when the blood of Your witness Stephen was shed, I stood there giving my approval to his death and watching over the garments of those who killed him.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Timothy 2:7; Ephesians 3:6–8"> Then He said to me, ‘Go! I will send you far away to the Gentiles.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 25:24; Acts 21:36; John 19:15"> The crowd listened to Paul until he made this statement. Then they lifted up their voices and shouted, “Rid the earth of him! He is not fit to live!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:13; Acts 26:11; Ecclesiastes 10:3"> As they were crying out and throwing off their cloaks and tossing dust into the air,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 21:31–32; Acts 21:34; John 19:1"> the commander ordered that Paul be brought into the barracks. He directed that Paul be flogged and interrogated to determine the reason for this outcry against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:37; Acts 25:16; Acts 27:43"> But as he stretched him out to strap him down, Paul said to the centurion standing there, “Is it lawful for you to flog a Roman citizen without a trial?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 23:27; Acts 22:29"> On hearing this, the centurion went and reported it to the commander. “Consider what you are about to do,” he said. “This man is a Roman citizen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> The commander went to Paul and asked, “Tell me, are you a Roman citizen?” “Yes,” he answered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> “I paid a high price for my citizenship,” said the commander. “But I was born a citizen,” Paul replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 21:33; Acts 22:24–26; Acts 16:38–39"> At once those who were about to interrogate Paul stepped back, and the commander himself was alarmed when he realized that he had put a Roman citizen in chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 23:28; Acts 21:33; Acts 21:11"> The next day the commander, wanting to learn the real reason Paul was accused by the Jews, released him from the bonds and ordered the chief priests and their whole Sanhedrin to assemble. Then he brought Paul down and had him stand before them.</span>
