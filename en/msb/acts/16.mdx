<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:5; 2 Timothy 1:2; 1 Corinthians 4:17"> Paul came to Derbe and then to Lystra, where he found a disciple named Timothy, the son of a believing Jewish woman and a Greek father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; 2 Timothy 3:15; Acts 14:21"> The brothers in Lystra and Iconium spoke well of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 9:20; Galatians 5:6"> Paul wanted Timothy to accompany him, so he took him and circumcised him on account of the Jews in that area, for they all knew that his father was a Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 15:28–29; Acts 15:2; Acts 11:30"> As they went from town to town, they delivered the decisions handed down by the apostles and elders in Jerusalem for the people to obey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 9:31; Acts 2:47; Acts 6:7"> So the churches were strengthened in the faith and grew daily in numbers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 18:23; Acts 16:7; Acts 13:2–4"> After the Holy Spirit had prevented them from speaking the word in the province of Asia, they traveled through the region of Phrygia and Galatia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:9; Philippians 1:19; Galatians 4:6"> And when they came to the border of Mysia, they tried to enter Bithynia, but the Spirit would not permit them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12; 2 Timothy 4:13; Acts 16:11"> So they passed by Mysia and went down to Troas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 9:10–12; Acts 19:21; Acts 18:5"> During the night, Paul had a vision of a man of Macedonia standing and pleading with him, “Come over to Macedonia and help us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12–13; Acts 14:7; Acts 16:10–17"> As soon as Paul had seen the vision, we got ready to leave for Macedonia, concluding that the Lord had called us to preach the gospel to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 21:1; Acts 16:8"> Therefore we sailed from Troas straight to Samothrace, and the following day on to Neapolis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:2; Philippians 1:1; Acts 20:6"> From there we went to the Roman colony of Philippi, the leading city of that district of Macedonia. And we stayed there several days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 13:14; Colossians 1:23; Acts 17:2"> On the Sabbath we went outside the city along the river, where it was customary to find a place of prayer. After sitting down, we spoke to the women who had gathered there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; Luke 24:45; Isaiah 50:5"> Among those listening was a woman named Lydia, a dealer in purple cloth from the city of Thyatira, who was a worshiper of God. The Lord opened her heart to respond to Paul’s message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 11:14; Hebrews 13:2; Acts 16:33"> And when she and her household had been baptized, she urged us, “If you consider me a believer in the Lord, come and stay at my house.” And she persuaded us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Deuteronomy 18:9–11; Isaiah 8:19"> One day as we were going to the place of prayer, we were met by a slave girl with a spirit of divination, who earned a large income for her masters by fortune-telling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 14:6; 1 Peter 2:16; Mark 5:7"> This girl followed Paul and the rest of us, shouting, “These men are servants of the Most High God, who are proclaiming to us the way of salvation!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Luke 10:17–19; Acts 3:6"> She continued this for many days. Eventually Paul grew so aggravated that he turned and said to the spirit, “In the name of Jesus Christ I command you to come out of her!” And the spirit left her at that very moment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:6; Acts 21:30; Acts 8:3"> When the girl’s owners saw that their hope of making money was gone, they seized Paul and Silas and dragged them before the authorities in the marketplace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 4:4; John 15:18–20; Acts 28:22"> They brought them to the magistrates and said, “These men are Jews and are throwing our city into turmoil</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esther 3:8; Acts 26:3; Jeremiah 10:3"> by promoting customs that are unlawful for us Romans to adopt or practice.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:2; 2 Corinthians 6:5; 2 Corinthians 11:23–25"> The crowd joined in the attack against Paul and Silas, and the magistrates ordered that they be stripped and beaten with rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 2:10; 2 Timothy 2:9; 1 Samuel 23:22–23"> And after striking them with many blows, they threw them into prison and ordered the jailer to guard them securely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 13:27; Job 33:11; Jeremiah 29:26"> On receiving this order, he placed them in the inner cell and fastened their feet in the stocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:16–18; Psalm 119:62; James 1:2"> About midnight Paul and Silas were praying and singing hymns to God, and the other prisoners were listening to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 4:31; Acts 5:19; Acts 12:10"> Suddenly a strong earthquake shook the foundations of the prison. At once all the doors flew open and everyone’s chains came loose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 12:19; Matthew 27:5; Judges 9:54"> When the jailer woke up and saw the prison doors open, he drew his sword and was about to kill himself, presuming that the prisoners had escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:15; Luke 23:34; Luke 6:27–28"> But Paul called out in a loud voice, “Do not harm yourself! We are all here!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Acts 24:25; Isaiah 60:14"> Calling for lights, the jailer rushed in and fell trembling before Paul and Silas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 2:37; John 6:27–29; Isaiah 58:9"> Then he brought them out and asked, “Sirs, what must I do to be saved?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:12; Acts 2:38–39; John 3:36"> They replied, “Believe in the Lord Jesus Christ and you will be saved, you and your household.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 1:16; Colossians 1:27–28; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> Then Paul and Silas spoke the word of the Lord to him and to everyone in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:16; Acts 16:15; Acts 16:25"> At that hour of the night, the jailer took them and washed their wounds. And without delay, he and all his household were baptized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Acts 2:46; Romans 5:11"> Then he brought them into his home and set a meal before them. So he and all his household rejoiced that they had come to believe in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:22; Psalm 76:10; Acts 5:40"> When daylight came, the magistrates sent their officers with the order: “Release those men.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 15:33; John 14:27; 1 Samuel 1:17"> The jailer informed Paul: “The magistrates have sent orders to release you. Now you may go on your way in peace.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 22:25–29; Psalm 58:1–2; Matthew 10:16"> But Paul said to the officers, “They beat us publicly without a trial and threw us into prison, even though we are Roman citizens. And now do they want to send us away secretly? Absolutely not! Let them come themselves and escort us out!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 22:29; Matthew 21:46; Matthew 14:5"> So the officers relayed this message to the magistrates, who were alarmed to hear that Paul and Silas were Roman citizens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 8:34; Mark 5:17; Daniel 6:23"> They came to appease them and led them out, requesting that they leave the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:2–3; Acts 16:14; Acts 14:22"> After Paul and Silas came out of the prison, they went to Lydia’s house to see the brothers and encourage them. Then they left the city.</span>
