<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Galatians 2:12; Luke 15:2"> and said, “You visited uncircumcised men and ate with them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 1:3; Acts 14:27; Joshua 22:21–31"> But Peter began and explained to them the whole sequence of events:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 10:9–32; Acts 9:10; Acts 22:17"> “I was in the city of Joppa praying, and in a trance I saw a vision of something like a large sheet being let down from heaven by its four corners, and it came right down to me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 4:20; Acts 3:4"> I looked at it closely and saw four-footed animals of the earth, wild beasts, reptiles, and birds of the air.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then I heard a voice saying to me, ‘Get up, Peter, kill and eat.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Hosea 9:3; 1 Corinthians 7:14"> ‘No, Lord,’ I said, ‘for nothing impure or unclean has ever entered my mouth.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 15:9; Hebrews 9:13–14; Acts 10:34–35"> But the voice spoke to me from heaven a second time, ‘Do not call anything impure that God has made clean.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 21:17; Numbers 24:10; 2 Corinthians 12:8"> This happened three times, and everything was drawn back up into heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 4:27; Acts 9:10–12; Exodus 4:14"> Just then three men sent to me from Caesarea stopped at the house where I was staying.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 8:29; Romans 3:22; 2 Thessalonians 2:2"> The Spirit told me to accompany them without hesitation. These six brothers also went with me, and we entered the man’s home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 10:30–32; Acts 10:3–6; Acts 9:43"> He told us how he had seen an angel standing in his house and saying, ‘Send men to Joppa for Simon who is called Peter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 16:15; Acts 10:22; Acts 16:31–34"> He will convey to you a message by which you and all your household will be saved.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 10:34–46; Acts 2:2–12"> As I began to speak, the Holy Spirit fell upon them, just as He had fallen upon us at the beginning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 1:5; Mark 1:8; Proverbs 1:23"> Then I remembered the word of the Lord, how He said, ‘John baptized with water, but you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:47; Romans 9:20–26; Daniel 4:35"> So if God gave them the same gift He gave us who believed in the Lord Jesus Christ, who was I to hinder the work of God?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 10:12–13; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Ezekiel 36:26"> When they heard this, they had no further objections, and they glorified God, saying, “So then, God has granted even the Gentiles repentance unto life.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 21:2; Acts 15:3"> Meanwhile those scattered by the persecution that began with Stephen traveled as far as Phoenicia, Cyprus, and Antioch, speaking the message only to Jews.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 27:32; Acts 13:1; 1 Corinthians 1:23–24"> But some of them, men from Cyprus and Cyrene, went to Antioch and began speaking to the Greeks, proclaiming the good news about the Lord Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:66; Acts 2:47; Acts 5:14"> The hand of the Lord was with them, and a great number of people believed and turned to the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 9:27; Acts 8:14; Acts 13:1–3"> When news of this reached the ears of the church in Jerusalem, they sent out Barnabas, that he should go to Antioch.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Acts 14:22; Acts 13:43"> When he arrived and saw the grace of God, he rejoiced and encouraged them all to abide in the Lord with all their hearts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 11:21; Acts 5:14; Acts 9:31"> Barnabas was a good man, full of the Holy Spirit and faith, and a great number of people were brought to the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 9:11; Acts 9:27; Acts 9:30"> Then Barnabas went to Tarsus to look for Saul,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 4:16; Acts 26:28; 1 Peter 4:14"> and when he found him, he brought him back to Antioch. So for a full year they met together with the church and taught large numbers of people. The disciples were first called Christians at Antioch.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 13:1; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Ephesians 4:11"> In those days some prophets came down from Jerusalem to Antioch.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 21:10; Acts 18:2; Luke 3:1"> One of them named Agabus stood up and predicted through the Spirit that a great famine would sweep across the whole world. (This happened under Claudius Caesar.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12–14; Galatians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 8:2–4"> So the disciples, each according to his ability, decided to send relief to the brothers living in Judea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 14:23; Acts 12:25; Acts 15:4"> This they did, sending their gifts to the elders with Barnabas and Saul.</span>
</div>
</div>
