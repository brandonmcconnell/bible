<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 18:19–21; Matthew 26:61–62; Acts 6:13–14"> Then the high priest asked Stephen, “Are these charges true?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 22:1; Genesis 11:31; Psalm 29:3"> And Stephen declared: “Brothers and fathers, listen to me! The God of glory appeared to our father Abraham while he was still in Mesopotamia, before he lived in Haran,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 12:1; Hebrews 11:8; Joshua 24:3"> and told him, ‘Leave your country and your kindred and go to the land I will show you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 12:4–5; Genesis 11:31–32; Isaiah 41:9"> So Abraham left the land of the Chaldeans and settled in Haran. After his father died, God brought him out of that place and into this land where you are now living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 13:15"> He gave him no inheritance here, not even a foot of ground. But God promised to give possession of the land to Abraham and his descendants, even though he did not yet have a child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 15:13; Exodus 12:40–41; Galatians 3:17"> God told him that his descendants would be foreigners in a strange land, and that they would be enslaved and mistreated four hundred years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 3:12; Psalm 105:27–36; Nehemiah 9:9–11"> ‘But I will punish the nation that enslaves them,’ God said, ‘and afterward they will come forth and worship Me in this place.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 35:23–26; Genesis 17:9–14; Genesis 21:1–4"> Then God gave Abraham the covenant of circumcision, and Abraham became the father of Isaac and circumcised him on the eighth day. And Isaac became the father of Jacob, and Jacob of the twelve patriarchs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 39:2; Psalm 105:17; Isaiah 43:2"> Because the patriarchs were jealous of Joseph, they sold him as a slave into Egypt. But God was with him</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 3:4; 2 Timothy 4:18; Proverbs 2:6"> and rescued him from all his troubles. He granted Joseph favor and wisdom in the sight of Pharaoh king of Egypt, who appointed him ruler over Egypt and all his household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 41:54–57; Genesis 45:11; Genesis 42:5"> Then famine and great suffering swept across the land of Egypt and Canaan, and our fathers could not find food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:1–24; Genesis 43:2"> When Jacob heard that there was grain in Egypt, he sent our fathers on their first visit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 45:1–18; Genesis 46:31–10"> On their second visit, Joseph revealed his identity to his brothers, and his family became known to Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 46:26–27; Deuteronomy 10:22; Exodus 1:5"> Then Joseph sent for his father Jacob and all his relatives, seventy-five in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 49:33; Exodus 1:6; Genesis 46:3–7"> So Jacob went down to Egypt, where he and our fathers died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 24:32; Genesis 23:16; Exodus 13:19"> Their bones were carried back to Shechem and placed in the tomb that Abraham had bought from the sons of Hamor of Shechem for a price he paid in silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 1:7–12; Acts 7:6; Acts 13:17"> As the time drew near for God to fulfill His promise to Abraham, our people in Egypt increased greatly in number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 1:8"> Then another king, who knew nothing of Joseph, arose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 105:25; Exodus 1:9–22; Revelation 12:4–5"> He exploited our people and oppressed our fathers, forcing them to abandon their infants so they would die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 11:23; Exodus 2:2–10; 1 Samuel 16:12"> At that time Moses was born, and he was beautiful in the sight of God. For three months he was nurtured in his father’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24; Exodus 2:2–10; Deuteronomy 32:26"> When he was set outside, Pharaoh’s daughter took him and brought him up as her own son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Daniel 1:17–20; 2 Chronicles 9:22"> So Moses was educated in all the wisdom of the Egyptians and was powerful in speech and action.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 2:11–12; Proverbs 21:1; Hebrews 11:24–26"> When Moses was forty years old, he decided to visit his brothers, the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Acts 7:28; John 18:25–27"> And when he saw one of them being mistreated, Moses went to his defense and avenged him by striking down the Egyptian who was oppressing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 15:7; Mark 9:32; 2 Kings 5:1"> He assumed his brothers would understand that God was using him to deliver them, but they did not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 2:13–15; Proverbs 18:19; 1 John 3:11–15"> The next day he came upon two Israelites who were fighting, and he urged them to make peace, saying, ‘Men, you are brothers. Why are you mistreating each other?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Luke 12:14; Acts 7:54"> But the man who was abusing his neighbor pushed Moses aside and said, ‘Who made you ruler and judge over us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Do you want to kill me as you killed the Egyptian yesterday?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 18:2–4; Exodus 2:14–22; Exodus 4:19–20"> At this remark, Moses fled to the land of Midian, where he lived as a foreigner and had two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 3:1–2; Acts 7:35; Isaiah 43:2"> After forty years had passed, an angel of the Lord appeared to Moses in the flames of a burning bush in the desert near Mount Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 3:3–4"> When Moses saw it, he marveled at the sight. As he approached to look more closely, the voice of the Lord came to him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Matthew 22:32; Job 4:14"> ‘I am the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.’ Moses trembled with fear and did not dare to look.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 3:5; Joshua 5:15; Ecclesiastes 5:1"> Then the Lord said to him, ‘Take off your sandals, for the place where you are standing is holy ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 3:7–10; Exodus 4:31; Psalm 106:44"> I have indeed seen the oppression of My people in Egypt. I have heard their groaning and have come down to deliver them. Now come, I will send you back to Egypt.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalm 118:22–23; Luke 19:14; Psalm 113:7–8"> This Moses, whom they had rejected with the words, ‘Who made you ruler and judge?’ is the one whom God sent to be their ruler and redeemer through the angel who appeared to him in the bush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 12:41; Exodus 14:21; Exodus 33:1"> He led them out and performed wonders and signs in the land of Egypt, at the Red Sea, and for forty years in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–19; Acts 3:22–23; John 18:37"> This is the same Moses who told the Israelites, ‘The Lord our God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among your brothers.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Romans 3:2; Hebrews 5:12; 1 Peter 4:11"> He was in the assembly in the wilderness with the angel who spoke to him on Mount Sinai, and with our fathers. And he received a living word to pass on to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 14:3–4; Numbers 11:5; Nehemiah 9:16–17"> But our fathers refused to obey him. Instead, they rejected him and in their heart turned back to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 32:1; Exodus 32:23"> They said to Aaron, ‘Make us gods who will go before us! As for this Moses who led us out of the land of Egypt, we do not know what has happened to him.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalm 106:19–21; Nehemiah 9:18; Revelation 9:20"> At that time they made a calf and offered a sacrifice to the idol, rejoicing in the works of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Amos 5:25–26; Ezekiel 20:39; Jeremiah 19:13"> But God turned away from them and gave them over to the worship of the host of heaven, as it is written in the book of the prophets: ‘Did you bring Me sacrifices and offerings forty years in the wilderness, O house of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Amos 5:26–27; Exodus 20:4–5; Deuteronomy 5:8–9"> You have taken along the tabernacle of Molech and the star of your god Remphan, the idols you made to worship. Therefore I will send you into exile beyond Babylon.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 38:21; Hebrews 8:5"> Our fathers had the tabernacle of the Testimony with them in the wilderness. It was constructed exactly as God had directed Moses, according to the pattern he had seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalm 44:2; Joshua 18:1; Joshua 23:9"> And our fathers who received it brought it in with Joshua when they dispossessed the nations God drove out before them. It remained until the time of David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalm 132:1–5; Acts 13:22; 2 Samuel 7:1–5"> who found favor in the sight of God and asked to provide a dwelling place for the God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Kings 8:20; 1 Kings 6:37–38; 2 Samuel 7:13"> But it was Solomon who built the house for Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 17:24–25; Isaiah 66:1–2; 1 Kings 8:27"> However, the Most High does not dwell in temples made by human hands. As the prophet says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Isaiah 66:1; Psalm 11:4; John 4:21"> ‘Heaven is My throne and the earth is My footstool. What kind of house will you build for Me, says the Lord, or where will My place of repose be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Psalm 33:6–9; Acts 14:15"> Has not My hand made all these things?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:16; Ephesians 4:30; Isaiah 63:10"> You stiff-necked people with uncircumcised heart and ears! You always resist the Holy Spirit, just as your fathers did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 5:12; 2 Chronicles 36:16; 1 Kings 19:10"> Which of the prophets did your fathers fail to persecute? They even killed those who foretold the coming of the Righteous One. And now you are His betrayers and murderers—</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Hebrews 2:2; Galatians 3:19; Acts 7:38"> you who received the law ordained by angels, yet have not kept it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Acts 5:33; Psalm 35:16; Matthew 25:30"> On hearing this, the members of the Sanhedrin were enraged, and they gnashed their teeth at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Mark 16:19; Hebrews 1:3; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> But Stephen, full of the Holy Spirit, looked intently into heaven and saw the glory of God and Jesus standing at the right hand of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 26:64–65; Acts 10:11; Revelation 11:19"> “Look,” he said, “I see heaven open and the Son of Man standing at the right hand of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Zechariah 7:11; Psalm 58:4; Acts 23:27"> At this they covered their ears, cried out in a loud voice, and rushed together at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Acts 22:20; Acts 8:1; Acts 6:13"> They dragged him out of the city and began to stone him. Meanwhile the witnesses laid their garments at the feet of a young man named Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Luke 23:46; Psalm 31:5; Acts 22:16"> While they were stoning him, Stephen appealed, “Lord Jesus, receive my spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Luke 23:34; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> Falling on his knees, he cried out in a loud voice, “Lord, do not hold this sin against them.” And when he had said this, he fell asleep.</span>
