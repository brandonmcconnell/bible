<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:6; Philippians 2:2; Acts 1:13–15"> When the day of Pentecost came, they were all with one accord in one place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:31; 1 Kings 19:11; Psalm 18:10"> Suddenly a sound like a mighty rushing wind came from heaven and filled the whole house where they were sitting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; John 1:32–33; Jeremiah 23:29"> They saw tongues like flames of fire that separated and came to rest on each of them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Acts 13:52; Acts 4:31"> And they were all filled with the Holy Spirit and began to speak in other tongues as the Spirit enabled them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Acts 8:2; Colossians 1:23"> Now there were dwelling in Jerusalem God-fearing Jews from every nation under heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 2:3; 1 Corinthians 16:9; Acts 2:2"> And when this sound rang out, a crowd came together in bewilderment, because each one heard them speaking his own language.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 2:12; Acts 1:11; John 7:52"> Astounded and amazed, they asked one another, “Are not all these who are speaking Galileans?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> How is it then that each of us hears them in his own native language?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 1:1; Acts 18:2; Revelation 1:4"> Parthians, Medes, and Elamites; residents of Mesopotamia, Judea and Cappadocia, Pontus and Asia,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 16:6; Zechariah 8:23; Zechariah 8:20"> Phrygia and Pamphylia, Egypt and the parts of Libya near Cyrene; visitors from Rome,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10; Exodus 15:11; Psalm 78:4"> both Jews and converts to Judaism; Cretans and Arabs—we hear them declaring the wonders of God in our own tongues!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 15:26; Acts 17:20; Luke 18:36"> Astounded and perplexed, they asked one another, “What does this mean?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:23; Acts 2:15; 1 Samuel 1:14"> But others mocked them and said, “They are drunk on new wine!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 51:7; Isaiah 51:1; Deuteronomy 27:9"> Then Peter stood up with the Eleven, lifted up his voice, and addressed the crowd: “Men of Judea and all who dwell in Jerusalem, let this be known to you, and listen carefully to my words.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Samuel 1:15; Matthew 20:3"> These men are not drunk, as you suppose. It is only the third hour of the day!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32"> No, this is what was spoken by the prophet Joel:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32; Isaiah 44:3; Titus 3:4–6"> ‘In the last days, God says, I will pour out My Spirit on all people. Your sons and daughters will prophesy, your young men will see visions, your old men will dream dreams.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11; 1 Corinthians 7:21–22"> Even on My menservants and maidservants I will pour out My Spirit in those days, and they will prophesy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:30–31; Zephaniah 1:14–18; Malachi 4:1–6"> I will show wonders in the heavens above and signs on the earth below, blood and fire and billows of smoke.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Amos 8:9; Matthew 24:29"> The sun will be turned to darkness, and the moon to blood, before the coming of the great and glorious Day of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 10:12–13; Joel 2:32; Psalm 86:5"> And everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 3:2; John 14:10–11; Matthew 12:28"> Men of Israel, listen to this message: Jesus of Nazareth was a man certified by God to you by miracles, wonders, and signs, which God did among you through Him, as you yourselves know.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:22; Acts 4:28; Acts 3:18"> He was delivered up by God’s set plan and foreknowledge, and you, by the hands of the lawless, took and put Him to death by nailing Him to the cross.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:14; 1 Peter 1:21; Acts 2:32"> But God raised Him from the dead, releasing Him from the agony of death, because it was impossible for death to keep Him in its grip.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 16:8–11; Isaiah 41:13; Psalm 62:6"> David says about Him: ‘I saw the Lord always before me; because He is at my right hand, I will not be shaken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 16:9; Psalm 63:5; Psalm 71:23"> Therefore my heart is glad and my tongue rejoices; my body also will dwell in hope,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalm 16:10; Acts 2:31; Revelation 1:18"> because You will not abandon my soul to Hades, nor will You let Your Holy One see decay.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalm 16:11; Psalm 25:4; Psalm 21:6"> You have made known to me the paths of life; You will fill me with joy in Your presence.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 13:36; 1 Kings 2:10; Acts 7:8–9"> Brothers, I can tell you with confidence that the patriarch David died and was buried, and his tomb is with us to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalm 132:11–18; Psalm 89:3–4; Jeremiah 33:14–15"> But he was a prophet and knew that God had promised him on oath that from his descendants according to the flesh He would raise up the Christ to seat Him on his throne.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 2:27; Psalm 16:10; Acts 13:35"> Foreseeing this, David spoke about the resurrection of the Christ, that His soul was not abandoned to Hades, nor did His body see decay.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 3:15; Acts 1:8; Acts 1:22"> God has raised this Jesus to life, to which we are all witnesses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:4; Acts 5:31; Acts 2:17"> Exalted, then, to the right hand of God, He has received from the Father the promised Holy Spirit and has poured out what you now see and hear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalm 110:1; Matthew 22:42–45; Hebrews 1:13"> For David did not ascend into heaven, but he himself says: ‘The Lord said to my Lord, “Sit at My right hand</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 19:19–3; Romans 16:20; Isaiah 60:14"> until I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet.”’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 5:30–31; Romans 14:8–12; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> Therefore let all Israel know with certainty that God has made this Jesus, whom you crucified, both Lord and Christ!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 3:10; Acts 22:10; Hebrews 4:12–13"> When the people heard this, they were cut to the heart and asked Peter and the other apostles, “Brothers, what shall we do?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 22:16; Mark 16:16; Acts 8:12"> Peter replied, “Repent and be baptized, every one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of sins, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 8:30"> This promise belongs to you and your children and to all who are far off—to all whom the Lord our God will call to Himself.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Deuteronomy 32:5; Matthew 17:17"> With many other words he testified, and he urged them, “Be saved from this corrupt generation.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 4:4; Acts 2:47; Acts 16:31–34"> Those who gladly embraced his message were baptized, and about three thousand were added to the believers that day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; Acts 1:14; 1 John 1:3"> They devoted themselves to the apostles’ teaching and to the fellowship, to the breaking of bread and to prayer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 7:16; John 14:12; Luke 8:37"> A sense of awe came over everyone, and the apostles performed many wonders and signs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 4:32; 1 John 3:16–18; 2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> All the believers were together and had everything in common.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 John 3:17; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; James 2:14–16"> Selling their possessions and goods, they shared with anyone who was in need.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 5:42; Acts 20:7; Acts 2:42"> With one accord they continued to meet daily in the temple courts and to break bread from house to house, sharing their meals with gladness and sincerity of heart,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 16:5; Acts 5:13–14; Acts 2:41"> praising God and enjoying the favor of all the people. And the Lord added to the church daily those who were being saved.</span>
</div>
</div>
