<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 55:17; Acts 10:3; Acts 4:13"> One afternoon Peter and John were going up together to the temple at the hour of prayer, the ninth hour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 14:8; John 9:8; Luke 16:20"> And a man who was lame from birth was being carried to the temple gate called Beautiful, where he was put every day to beg from those entering the temple courts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> When he saw Peter and John about to enter, he asked them for money.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:9–10; John 11:40; Luke 4:20"> Peter looked directly at him, as did John. “Look at us!” said Peter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So the man gave them his attention, expecting to receive something from them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 3:16; Acts 10:38; Acts 4:10"> But Peter said, “Silver or gold I do not have, but what I have I give you: In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, get up and walk!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Mark 9:27; Acts 9:41"> Taking him by the right hand, Peter helped him up, and at once the man’s feet and ankles were made strong.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 14:10; Isaiah 35:6; Psalm 107:20–22"> He sprang to his feet and began to walk. Then he went with them into the temple courts, walking and leaping and praising God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:21; Acts 4:16; Luke 13:17"> When all the people saw him walking and praising God,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 3:2; John 5:20; John 9:3"> they recognized him as the man who used to sit begging at the Beautiful Gate of the temple, and they were filled with wonder and amazement at what had happened to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 5:12; John 10:23; Luke 8:38"> While the lame man who had been healed clung to Peter and John, all the people were astonished and ran to them in the walkway called Solomon’s Colonnade.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; John 3:27–28; Daniel 2:28–30"> And when Peter saw this, he addressed the people: “Men of Israel, why are you surprised by this? Why do you stare at us as if by our own power or godliness we had made this man walk?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 22:32; Acts 7:32; Matthew 27:17–25"> The God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, the God of our fathers, has glorified His servant Jesus. You handed Him over and rejected Him before Pilate, even though he had decided to release Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 1:24; Acts 7:52; 1 Peter 3:18"> You rejected the Holy and Righteous One and asked that a murderer be released to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Revelation 21:6; Acts 2:32"> You killed the Author of life, but God raised Him from the dead, and we are witnesses of this fact.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 21:21–22; Acts 4:10; Acts 14:9"> By faith in the name of Jesus, this man whom you see and know has been made strong. It is Jesus’ name and the faith that comes through Him that has given him this complete healing in your presence.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:34; Acts 13:27; John 16:3"> And now, brothers, I know that you acted in ignorance, as did your leaders.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Acts 26:22–23; Acts 2:23"> But in this way God has fulfilled what He foretold through all His prophets, saying that the Christ would suffer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Isaiah 44:22; Isaiah 43:25"> Repent, then, and turn back, so that your sins may be wiped away,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 24:30–36; Revelation 19:11–16; 2 Thessalonians 2:2"> that times of refreshing may come from the presence of the Lord, and that He may send Jesus, the Christ, who has been appointed for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:21; Isaiah 1:26; Acts 1:11"> Heaven must take Him in until the time comes for the restoration of all things, which God announced long ago through all His holy prophets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–19; Acts 7:37; Galatians 4:4"> For Moses said to the fathers, ‘The Lord your God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among your brothers. You must listen to Him in everything He tells you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:19; John 12:48; Hebrews 10:28–30"> Everyone who does not listen to Him will be completely cut off from among his people.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 3:21; Luke 24:27; 1 Samuel 3:20"> Indeed, all the prophets from Samuel on, as many as have spoken, have proclaimed these days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 22:18; Galatians 3:8; Acts 2:39"> And you are sons of the prophets and of the covenant God made with our fathers when He said to Abraham, ‘Through your offspring all the families of the earth will be blessed.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 1:21; Acts 3:22; Jude 1:24"> When God raised up His servant Jesus, He sent Him first to you to bless you by turning each of you from your wicked ways.”</span>
</div>
</div>
