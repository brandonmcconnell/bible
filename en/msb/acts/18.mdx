</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 16:3–4; 1 Corinthians 16:19; 2 Timothy 4:19"> There he found a Jew named Aquila, a native of Pontus, who had recently come from Italy with his wife Priscilla because Claudius had ordered all the Jews to leave Rome. Paul went to visit them,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:12; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; 2 Corinthians 11:9"> and he stayed and worked with them because they were tentmakers by trade, just as he was.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:11; Acts 17:17; Acts 28:23"> Every Sabbath he reasoned in the synagogue, trying to persuade Jews and Greeks alike.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:14; Acts 18:28; Acts 20:21"> And when Silas and Timothy came down from Macedonia, Paul was compelled by the Spirit, testifying to the Jews that Jesus is the Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:4; 2 Samuel 1:16; Ezekiel 33:8–9"> But when they opposed and insulted him, he shook out his garments and told them, “Your blood be on your own heads! I am innocent of it. From now on I will go to the Gentiles.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 16:14; Colossians 4:11; Acts 10:2"> So Paul left the synagogue and went next door to the house of Justus, a worshiper of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:13–17; Romans 10:14–17; Mark 16:15–16"> Crispus, the synagogue leader, and his whole household believed in the Lord. And many of the Corinthians who heard the message believed and were baptized.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Ephesians 6:19–20; Acts 27:23–25"> One night the Lord spoke to Paul in a vision: “Do not be afraid; keep on speaking; do not be silent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9; Exodus 4:12; Isaiah 41:10"> For I am with you and no one will lay a hand on you, because I have many people in this city.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 14:3; Acts 19:10; Acts 20:31"> So Paul stayed for a year and a half, teaching the word of God among the Corinthians.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 13:50; Acts 18:27; Acts 13:7"> While Gallio was proconsul of Achaia, the Jews coordinated an attack on Paul and brought him before the judgment seat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 25:8; Acts 24:5–6; Acts 6:13"> “This man is persuading the people to worship God in ways contrary to the law,” they said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14–15; Acts 25:11; Mark 9:19"> But just as Paul was about to speak, Gallio told the Jews, “If this matter involved a wrongdoing or vicious crime, O Jews, it would be reasonable for me to hear your complaint.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 23:29; Acts 25:19; 1 Timothy 6:4"> But since it is a dispute about words and names and your own law, settle it yourselves. For I refuse to be a judge of such things.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 12:16; Psalm 76:10; Romans 13:3–4"> And he drove them away from the judgment seat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:1; Amos 6:6; Acts 17:32"> At this, all the Greeks seized Sosthenes the synagogue leader and beat him in front of the judgment seat. But none of this was of concern to Gallio.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:24; Numbers 6:18; Numbers 6:5–9"> Paul remained in Corinth for quite some time before saying goodbye to the brothers. He had his head shaved in Cenchrea to keep a vow he had made, and then he sailed for Syria, accompanied by Priscilla and Aquila.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 1:1; 1 Timothy 1:3; 2 Timothy 1:18"> When he reached Ephesus, Paul left Priscilla and Aquila. He himself went into the synagogue there and reasoned with the Jews.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 1:37–38; 1 Corinthians 16:12; Acts 20:16"> When they asked him to stay with them for a while longer, he declined.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:19; James 4:15; Romans 15:32"> But as he left, he said, “I must by all means keep this coming feast in Jerusalem, but I will come back to you if God is willing.” And he set sail from Ephesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 8:40; Acts 15:4; Acts 23:23"> When Paul had landed at Caesarea, he went up and greeted the church at Jerusalem. Then he went down to Antioch.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 14:22; Acts 16:6; Acts 15:41"> After Paul had spent some time in Antioch, he traveled from place to place throughout the region of Galatia and Phrygia, strengthening all the disciples.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:6; Titus 3:13; 1 Corinthians 3:5–6"> Meanwhile a Jew named Apollos, a native of Alexandria, came to Ephesus. He was an eloquent man, well versed in the Scriptures.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 19:3; Romans 12:11; Hosea 14:9"> He had been instructed in the way of the Lord and was fervent in spirit. He spoke and taught accurately about the Lord, though he knew only the baptism of John.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 18:25; Acts 14:3; 1 Corinthians 12:21"> And he began to speak boldly in the synagogue. When Aquila and Priscilla heard him, they took him aside and explained to him the way of God more accurately.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:13–14; James 1:16–18; 1 Peter 1:2–3"> When Apollos resolved to cross over to Achaia, the brothers encouraged him and wrote to the disciples there to welcome him. On his arrival, he was a great help to those who by grace had believed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 18:5; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4; Acts 9:22"> For he powerfully refuted the Jews in public debate, proving from the Scriptures that Jesus is the Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>
