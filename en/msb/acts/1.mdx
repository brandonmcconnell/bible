<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 1:3; Luke 24:19; Matthew 4:23–24"> In my first book, O Theophilus, I wrote about all that Jesus began to do and to teach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 1:9; John 20:21; Isaiah 59:20–21"> until the day He was taken up to heaven, after giving instructions through the Holy Spirit to the apostles He had chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5–7; Acts 13:31; John 20:26"> After His suffering, He presented Himself to them with many convincing proofs that He was alive. He appeared to them over a span of forty days and spoke about the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 24:49; John 14:16; John 15:26"> And while they were gathered together, He commanded them: “Do not leave Jerusalem, but wait for the gift the Father promised, which you have heard Me discuss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Acts 11:15–16; Luke 3:16"> For John baptized with water, but in a few days you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 19:11; Luke 17:20; Daniel 7:27"> So when they came together, they asked Him, “Lord, will You at this time restore the kingdom to Israel?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–2; Mark 13:32"> Jesus replied, “It is not for you to know times or seasons that the Father has fixed by His own authority.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; Mark 16:15; Romans 15:19"> But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes upon you, and you will be witnesses to Me in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Luke 24:50–51; Mark 16:19"> After He had said this, they watched as He was taken up, and a cloud hid Him from their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 24:4; John 20:12; Revelation 7:14"> They were looking intently into the sky as He was going, when suddenly two men dressed in white stood beside them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; Mark 13:26"> “Men of Galilee,” they said, “why do you stand here looking into the sky? This same Jesus, who has been taken from you into heaven, will come back in the same way you have seen Him go into heaven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:52; Matthew 21:1; Luke 24:50"> Then they returned to Jerusalem from the Mount of Olives, which is near the city, a Sabbath day’s journey away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 20:8; Luke 22:12; Mark 14:15"> When they arrived, they went to the upper room where they were staying: Peter and James, John and Andrew, Philip and Thomas, Bartholomew and Matthew, James son of Alphaeus, Simon the Zealot, and Judas son of James.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 6:4; Ephesians 6:18; Colossians 4:2"> With one accord they all continued in prayer and petition, along with the women and Mary the mother of Jesus, and with His brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:6; Acts 21:20; Revelation 3:4"> In those days Peter stood up among the disciples (a gathering of about a hundred and twenty) and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 41:9; John 13:18; Psalm 55:12–15"> “Brothers, this Scripture had to be fulfilled that the Holy Spirit foretold through the mouth of David concerning Judas, who became a guide for those who arrested Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 1:25; Mark 3:19; John 17:12"> He was one of our number and shared in this ministry.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:3–10; Matthew 26:14–15; Psalm 55:23"> (Now with the reward for his wickedness Judas bought a field; there he fell headlong and burst open in the middle, and all his intestines spilled out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 2:22; Matthew 28:15; Acts 21:40"> This became known to all who lived in Jerusalem, so they called that field in their own language Akeldama, that is, Field of Blood.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 69:25; Psalm 109:8–15; Acts 1:25"> “For it is written in the book of Psalms: ‘May his place be deserted; let there be no one to dwell in it,’ and, ‘May another take his position.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 15:27; Luke 10:1–2; Numbers 27:17"> Therefore it is necessary to choose one of the men who have accompanied us the whole time the Lord Jesus went in and out among us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 1:2; Acts 4:33; Acts 1:8–9"> beginning from John’s baptism until the day Jesus was taken up from us. For one of these must become a witness with us of His resurrection.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 1:26"> So they proposed two men: Joseph called Barsabas (also known as Justus) and Matthias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Acts 15:8; Hebrews 4:13"> And they prayed, “Lord, You know everyone’s heart. Show us which of these two You have chosen</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:2; John 6:70–71; John 17:12"> to receive a portion in this ministry and apostleship that Judas abandoned to go to his rightful place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 16:8; Joshua 18:10; 1 Samuel 14:41–42"> Then they cast lots, and the lot fell to Matthias. So he was added to the eleven apostles.</span>
