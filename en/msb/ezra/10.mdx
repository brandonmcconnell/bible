<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:10; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Hebrews 12:12–13"> Get up, for this matter is your responsibility, and we will support you. Be strong and take action!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:25; Nehemiah 5:12; Proverbs 9:9"> So Ezra got up and made the leading priests, Levites, and all Israel take an oath to do what had been said. And they took the oath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18; Nehemiah 3:1; Nehemiah 12:22"> Then Ezra withdrew from before the house of God and walked to the chamber of Jehohanan son of Eliashib. <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 1:1; 2 Chronicles 30:5"> And a proclamation was issued throughout Judah and Jerusalem that all the exiles should gather at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 7:26; John 9:22; Judges 21:5"> Whoever failed to appear within three days would forfeit all his property, according to the counsel of the leaders and elders, and would himself be expelled from the assembly of the exiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:17–18; Jeremiah 10:13; Ezra 7:8–9"> So within the three days, all the men of Judah and Benjamin assembled in Jerusalem, and on the twentieth day of the ninth month, all the people sat in the square at the house of God, trembling regarding this matter and because of the heavy rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; Numbers 32:14; Matthew 23:32"> Then Ezra the priest stood up and said to them, “You have been unfaithful by marrying foreign women, adding to the guilt of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Ezra 10:3; Isaiah 1:16–18"> Now, therefore, make a confession to the LORD, the God of your fathers, and do His will. Separate yourselves from the people of the land and from your foreign wives.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:23; Psalm 78:57; Ezra 10:3–4"> And the whole assembly responded in a loud voice: “Truly we must do as you say!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 10:18–44; Matthew 7:13–14"> But there are many people here, and it is the rainy season. We are not able to stay out in the open. Nor is this the work of one or two days, for we have transgressed greatly in this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:10; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Numbers 25:4"> Let our leaders represent the whole assembly. Then let everyone in our towns who has married a foreign woman come at an appointed time, together with the elders and judges of each town, until the fierce anger of our God in this matter is turned away from us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:16; Nehemiah 10:20; Nehemiah 12:33"> (Only Jonathan son of Asahel and Jahzeiah son of Tikvah, supported by Meshullam and Shabbethai the Levite, opposed this plan.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 29:16; Deuteronomy 13:14; John 7:51"> So the exiles did as proposed. <span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and by the first day of the first month they had dealt with all the men who had married foreign women.</span>
