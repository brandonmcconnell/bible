<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 7:7; Ezra 2:62; 1 Chronicles 9:1"> These are the family heads and genealogical records of those who returned with me from Babylon during the reign of King Artaxerxes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:22; 1 Chronicles 3:1; 1 Chronicles 6:3–15"> from the descendants of Phinehas, Gershom; from the descendants of Ithamar, Daniel; from the descendants of David, Hattush</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 2:3; Nehemiah 10:14; Nehemiah 7:8"> of the descendants of Shecaniah; from the descendants of Parosh, Zechariah, and with him were registered 150 men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezra 2:6; Nehemiah 10:14; Nehemiah 7:11"> from the descendants of Pahath-Moab, Eliehoenai son of Zerahiah, and with him 200 men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> from the descendants of Zattu, Shecaniah son of Jahaziel, and with him 300 men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 2:15; Nehemiah 10:16; Nehemiah 7:20"> from the descendants of Adin, Ebed son of Jonathan, and with him 50 men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 2:31; Nehemiah 7:34; Ezra 2:7"> from the descendants of Elam, Jeshaiah son of Athaliah, and with him 70 men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:4; Ezra 2:4; Nehemiah 7:9"> from the descendants of Shephatiah, Zebadiah son of Michael, and with him 80 men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:11; Ezra 2:6"> from the descendants of Joab, Obadiah son of Jehiel, and with him 218 men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> from the descendants of Bani, Shelomith son of Josiphiah, and with him 160 men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezra 10:28; Nehemiah 7:16; Ezra 2:11"> from the descendants of Bebai, Zechariah son of Bebai, and with him 28 men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezra 2:12; Nehemiah 7:17"> from the descendants of Azgad, Johanan son of Hakkatan, and with him 110 men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 2:13; Nehemiah 7:18"> from the later descendants of Adonikam, these were their names: Eliphelet, Jeiel, and Shemaiah, and with them 60 men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:12; Ezra 2:14; Nehemiah 7:19"> and from the descendants of Bigvai, both Uthai and Zaccur, and with them 70 men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezra 8:21; Ezra 8:31; Ezra 7:7"> Now I assembled these exiles at the canal that flows to Ahava, and we camped there three days. And when I searched among the people and priests, I found no Levites there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 2:6; Ezra 8:18; Ezra 10:39"> Then I summoned the leaders: Eliezer, Ariel, Shemaiah, Elnathan, Jarib, Elnathan, Nathan, Zechariah, and Meshullam, as well as the teachers Joiarib and Elnathan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezra 2:43; Exodus 4:15; Numbers 8:22–26"> And I sent them to Iddo, the leader at Casiphia, with a message for him and his kinsmen, the temple servants at Casiphia, that they should bring to us ministers for the house of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; Ezra 7:28; Nehemiah 2:8"> And since the gracious hand of our God was upon us, they brought us Sherebiah—a man of insight from the descendants of Mahli son of Levi, the son of Israel—along with his sons and brothers, 18 men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:1; 1 Chronicles 6:16; Nehemiah 10:11"> also Hashabiah, together with Jeshaiah, from the descendants of Merari, and his brothers and their sons, 20 men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezra 2:43; Ezra 7:7; 1 Chronicles 9:2"> They also brought 220 of the temple servants, all designated by name. David and the officials had appointed them to assist the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:3; Joel 1:14; 1 Samuel 7:6"> And there by the Ahava Canal I proclaimed a fast, so that we might humble ourselves before our God and ask Him for a safe journey for us and our children, with all our possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Psalm 33:18–19; 2 Chronicles 16:9"> For I was ashamed to ask the king for an escort of soldiers and horsemen to protect us from our enemies on the road, since we had told him, “The hand of our God is gracious to all who seek Him, but His great anger is against all who forsake Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12–13; Matthew 7:7–8; Ezra 8:31"> So we fasted and petitioned our God about this, and He granted our request.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra 8:18–19"> Then I set apart twelve of the leading priests, together with Sherebiah, Hashabiah, and ten of their brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezra 8:33; Ezra 7:14–16; Ezra 1:8"> and I weighed out to them the contribution of silver and gold and the articles that the king, his counselors, his leaders, and all the Israelites there had offered for the house of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezra 1:9–11"> I weighed out into their hands 650 talents of silver, articles of silver weighing 100 talents, 100 talents of gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Lamentations 4:2"> 20 gold bowls valued at 1,000 darics, and two articles of fine polished bronze, as precious as gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 22:2–3; Leviticus 21:6–8; 1 Chronicles 23:28"> Then I told them, “You are holy to the LORD, and these articles are holy. The silver and gold are a freewill offering to the LORD, the God of your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezra 8:33–34; Mark 13:34–35; 1 Chronicles 26:20–26"> Guard them carefully until you weigh them out in the chambers of the house of the LORD in Jerusalem before the leading priests, Levites, and heads of the Israelite families.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezra 8:22; 1 Chronicles 29:2–3; Isaiah 60:13"> So the priests and Levites took charge of the silver and gold and sacred articles that had been weighed out to be taken to the house of our God in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezra 8:21–22; Ezra 7:9; Ezra 8:15"> On the twelfth day of the first month we set out from the Ahava Canal to go to Jerusalem, and the hand of our God was upon us to protect us from the hands of the enemies and bandits along the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:11; Ezra 7:8–9"> So we arrived at Jerusalem and rested there for three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:4; Nehemiah 3:21; Ezra 8:30"> On the fourth day, in the house of our God, we weighed out the silver and gold and sacred articles into the hand of Meremoth son of Uriah, the priest. Eleazar son of Phinehas was with him, along with the Levites Jozabad son of Jeshua and Noadiah son of Binnui.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Everything was verified by number and weight, and the total weight was recorded at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezra 6:17; 2 Chronicles 29:31–32; Psalm 66:10–15"> Then the exiles who had returned from captivity sacrificed burnt offerings to the God of Israel: 12 bulls for all Israel, 96 rams, 77 lambs, and a sin offering of 12 male goats. All this was a burnt offering to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezra 7:21–24; Ezra 4:7–23; Ezra 6:13"> They also delivered the king’s edicts to the royal satraps and governors of the region west of the Euphrates, who proceeded to assist the people and the house of God.</span>
