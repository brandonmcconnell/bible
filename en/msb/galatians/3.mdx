<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="msb" data-translation-id="eng_msb" data-translation-label="MSB" data-book="GAL" data-book-slug="galatians" data-book-name="Galatians" data-chapter="3" data-verses="29">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 5:7–8; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Galatians 1:6"> O foolish Galatians! Who has bewitched you not to obey the truth? Before your very eyes Jesus Christ was clearly portrayed among you as crucified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:16–17; Galatians 3:14; Acts 15:8"> I would like to learn just one thing from you: Did you receive the Spirit by works of the law, or by hearing with faith?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 5:4–8; Galatians 4:7–10; Hebrews 7:16–19"> Are you so foolish? After starting in the Spirit, are you now finishing in the flesh?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 15:2; Hebrews 10:32–39"> Have you suffered so much for nothing, if it really was for nothing?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 3:2; Acts 14:9–10; Acts 19:11–12"> Does God lavish His Spirit on you and work miracles among you because you practice the law, or because you hear and believe?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Romans 4:21–22; James 2:23"> So also, “Abraham believed God, and it was credited to him as righteousness.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:9; Galatians 3:26–29"> Understand, then, that those who have faith are sons of Abraham.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 28:14"> The Scripture foresaw that God would justify the Gentiles by faith, and foretold the gospel to Abraham: “All nations will be blessed through you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 4:16; Galatians 3:29; Romans 4:24"> So those who have faith are blessed along with Abraham, the man of faith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:26; Romans 8:7; Romans 3:19–20"> All who rely on works of the law are under a curse. For it is written: “Cursed is everyone who does not continue to do everything written in the Book of the Law.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:4; Romans 1:17; Hebrews 10:38"> Now it is clear that no one is justified before God by the law, because, “The righteous will live by faith.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Romans 10:5–6; Nehemiah 9:29"> The law, however, is not based on faith; on the contrary, “The man who does these things will live by them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Deuteronomy 21:23; Hebrews 9:12"> Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us. For it is written: “Cursed is everyone who is hung on a tree.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 3:28–29; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Ezekiel 36:26–27"> He redeemed us in order that the blessing promised to Abraham would come to the Gentiles in Christ Jesus, so that by faith we might receive the promise of the Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 9:17; Romans 6:19; Romans 3:5"> Brothers, let me put this in human terms. Even a human covenant, once it is ratified, cannot be canceled or amended.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 4:13; Luke 1:55"> The promises were spoken to Abraham and to his seed. The Scripture does not say, “and to seeds,” meaning many, but “and to your seed,” meaning One, who is Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39–40; Genesis 15:13; Romans 4:13–14"> What I mean is this: The law that came 430 years later does not revoke the covenant previously established by God in Christ, so as to nullify the promise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12–15; Galatians 2:21; Romans 4:13–16"> For if the inheritance depends on the law, then it no longer depends on a promise; but God freely granted it to Abraham through a promise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:53; Romans 7:7–13; Hebrews 2:2"> Why then was the law given? It was added because of transgressions, until the arrival of the seed to whom the promise referred. It was administered through angels by a mediator.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:5; Deuteronomy 6:4; Job 9:33"> A mediator is unnecessary, however, for only one party; but God is one.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Romans 9:31; Romans 3:20–22"> Is the law, then, opposed to the promises of God? Certainly not! For if a law had been given that could impart life, then righteousness would certainly have come from the law.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 11:32; Hebrews 9:15; Galatians 3:23"> But the Scripture pronounces all things confined by sin, so that by faith in Jesus Christ the promise might be given to those who believe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 5:18; Galatians 3:24–25; Galatians 3:19"> Before this faith came, we were held in custody under the law, locked up until faith should be revealed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 10:4; Acts 13:38–39; Romans 7:24–25"> So the law became our guardian to lead us to Christ, that we might be justified by faith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:4; Hebrews 10:15–18"> Now that faith has come, we are no longer under a guardian.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 6:18; John 1:12–13"> You are all sons of God through faith in Christ Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 13:14; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Romans 6:3–4"> For all of you who were baptized into Christ have clothed yourselves with Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:12–13; Colossians 3:11; Romans 3:29–30"> There is neither Jew nor Greek, slave nor free, male nor female, for you are all one in Christ Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:7"> And if you belong to Christ, then you are Abraham’s seed and heirs according to the promise.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
