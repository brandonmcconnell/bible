<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12–13; 2 Samuel 7:8–9; Psalm 89:20"> These are the last words of David: “The oracle of David son of Jesse, the oracle of the man raised on high, the one anointed by the God of Jacob, and the sweet psalmist of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:21; Matthew 22:43; Mark 12:36"> The Spirit of the LORD spoke through me; His word was on my tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:32; Deuteronomy 32:4; Exodus 18:21"> The God of Israel spoke; the Rock of Israel said to me, ‘He who rules the people with justice, who rules in the fear of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Psalm 72:6; Hosea 6:5"> is like the light of the morning at sunrise of a cloudless dawn, the glistening after the rain on the sprouting grass of the earth.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; 2 Samuel 7:14–16; Isaiah 9:6–7"> Is not my house right with God? For He has established with me an everlasting covenant, ordered and secured in every part. Will He not bring about my full salvation and my every desire?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:6; Isaiah 33:12; Deuteronomy 13:13"> But the worthless are all like thorns raked aside, for they can never be gathered by hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 6:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:8; Isaiah 27:4"> The man who touches them must be armed with iron or with the shaft of a spear. The fire burns them to ashes in the place where they lie.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:2; 1 Chronicles 11:11–47; 1 Chronicles 27:32"> These are the names of David’s mighty men: Josheb-basshebeth the Tahchemonite was chief of the Three. He wielded his spear against eight hundred men, whom he killed at one time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:4; 1 Chronicles 11:12–14; 1 Samuel 17:26"> Next in command was Eleazar son of Dodo the Ahohite. As one of the three mighty men, he went with David to taunt the Philistines who had gathered for battle at Pas-dammim. The men of Israel retreated,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:5; 1 Samuel 11:13; Judges 15:14"> but Eleazar stood his ground and struck the Philistines until his hand grew weary and stuck to his sword. The LORD brought about a great victory that day. Then the troops returned to him, but only to plunder the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:13–14; 1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:33"> And after him was Shammah son of Agee the Hararite. When the Philistines had banded together near a field full of lentils, Israel’s troops fled from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 21:31; 2 Samuel 23:10; Psalm 3:8"> But Shammah took his stand in the middle of the field, defended it, and struck down the Philistines. So the LORD brought about a great victory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1; 2 Samuel 5:18; Joshua 12:15"> At harvest time, three of the thirty chief men went down to David at the cave of Adullam, while a company of Philistines was encamped in the Valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:4–5; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Samuel 10:5"> At that time David was in the stronghold, and the garrison of the Philistines was at Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:14; Psalm 42:1–2; Isaiah 41:17"> David longed for water and said, “Oh, that someone would get me a drink of water from the well near the gate of Bethlehem!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 35:14; 2 Corinthians 5:14; Philippians 2:17"> So the three mighty men broke through the Philistine camp, drew water from the well near the gate of Bethlehem, and brought it back to David. But he refused to drink it; instead, he poured it out to the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 17:10; Mark 14:24; 1 Corinthians 15:30"> saying, “Far be it from me, O LORD, to do this! Is this not the blood of the men who risked their lives?” So he refused to drink it. Such were the exploits of the three mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:10; 2 Samuel 10:14; 1 Chronicles 11:20–21"> Now Abishai, the brother of Joab and son of Zeruiah, was chief of the Three, and he wielded his spear against three hundred men, killed them, and won a name along with the Three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:8; Matthew 13:23; 1 Corinthians 15:41"> Was he not more honored than the Three? And he became their commander, even though he was not included among the Three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; Joshua 15:21; 2 Samuel 20:23"> And Benaiah son of Jehoiada was a man of valor from Kabzeel, a man of many exploits. He struck down two champions of Moab, and on a snowy day he went down into a pit and killed a lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:51; 1 Chronicles 11:23; Colossians 2:15"> He also struck down an Egyptian, a huge man. Although the Egyptian had a spear in his hand, Benaiah went against him with a club, snatched the spear from his hand, and killed the Egyptian with his own spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> These were the exploits of Benaiah son of Jehoiada, who won a name along with the three mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:23; 1 Chronicles 27:6; 1 Samuel 22:14"> He was most honored among the Thirty, but he did not become one of the Three. And David appointed him over his guard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:18; 1 Chronicles 27:7; 1 Chronicles 11:26"> Now these were members of the Thirty: Asahel the brother of Joab, Elhanan son of Dodo of Bethlehem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 7:1; 1 Chronicles 11:27–28"> Shammah the Harodite, Elika the Harodite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:2; 1 Chronicles 11:27–28; 1 Chronicles 27:9–10"> Helez the Paltite, Ira son of Ikkesh the Tekoite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 21:18; 1 Chronicles 11:19; 1 Chronicles 11:28"> Abiezer the Anathothite, Mebunnai the Hushathite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:13; 2 Kings 25:23; 1 Chronicles 11:30"> Zalmon the Ahohite, Maharai the Netophathite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:30–31; Joshua 18:28; 1 Chronicles 27:15"> Heled son of Baanah the Netophathite, Ittai son of Ribai from Gibeah of the Benjamites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 12:15; 1 Chronicles 27:14; Judges 12:13"> Benaiah the Pirathonite, Hiddai from the brooks of Gaash,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:16; 1 Chronicles 11:32–33"> Abi-albon the Arbathite, Azmaveth the Barhumite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:34"> Eliahba the Shaalbonite, the sons of Jashen, Jonathan</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:11; 1 Chronicles 11:35"> son of Shammah the Hararite, Ahiam son of Sharar the Hararite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:3; 2 Samuel 15:12; 2 Samuel 10:6"> Eliphelet son of Ahasbai the Maacathite, Eliam son of Ahithophel the Gilonite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:37; Joshua 15:55"> Hezro the Carmelite, Paarai the Arbite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:3; 1 Chronicles 11:38"> Igal son of Nathan of Zobah, Bani the Gadite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:37; 1 Chronicles 11:39; 2 Samuel 4:2"> Zelek the Ammonite, Naharai the Beerothite, the armor-bearer of Joab son of Zeruiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:53; 2 Samuel 20:26; 1 Chronicles 11:40"> Ira the Ithrite, Gareb the Ithrite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:3; Matthew 1:6; 1 Kings 15:5"> and Uriah the Hittite. There were thirty-seven in all.</span>
