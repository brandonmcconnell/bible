<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:31; 1 Samuel 23:4; Genesis 32:2"> Some time later, David inquired of the LORD, “Should I go up to one of the towns of Judah?” “Go up,” the LORD answered. Then David asked, “Where should I go?” “To Hebron,” replied the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:42–43; 1 Samuel 30:5; Luke 22:28–29"> So David went there with his two wives, Ahinoam of Jezreel and Abigail the widow of Nabal of Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:1; 1 Chronicles 12:1–7; 1 Samuel 27:2–3"> David also took the men who were with him, each with his household, and they settled in the towns near Hebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:5; 1 Samuel 31:11–13; 2 Samuel 5:3"> Then the men of Judah came to Hebron, and there they anointed David king over the house of Judah. And they told David, “It was the men of Jabesh-gilead who buried Saul.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:21; Psalm 115:15; Ruth 2:20"> So David sent messengers to the men of Jabesh-gilead to tell them, “The LORD bless you, because you showed this kindness to Saul your lord when you buried him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 14:22; Exodus 34:6; Matthew 5:44"> Now may the LORD show you loving devotion and faithfulness, and I will also show you the same favor because you have done this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; 2 Samuel 10:12; Genesis 15:1"> Now then, be strong and courageous, for though Saul your lord is dead, the house of Judah has anointed me as their king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:50; Genesis 32:2; 2 Samuel 3:7–8"> Meanwhile, Abner son of Ner, the commander of Saul’s army, took Saul’s son Ish-bosheth, moved him to Mahanaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 19:18; Joshua 13:8–11; Numbers 1:40"> and made him king over Gilead, Asher, Jezreel, Ephraim, and Benjamin—over all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Saul’s son Ish-bosheth was forty years old when he began to reign over Israel, and he reigned for two years. The house of Judah, however, followed David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 2:11; 2 Samuel 5:4–5; 1 Chronicles 3:4"> And the length of time that David was king in Hebron over the house of Judah was seven years and six months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 18:25; Joshua 10:2; Joshua 9:3"> One day Abner son of Ner and the servants of Ish-bosheth son of Saul marched out from Mahanaim to Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16; 1 Chronicles 2:16; 2 Samuel 20:23"> So Joab son of Zeruiah and the servants of David marched out and met them by the pool of Gibeon. And the two groups took up positions on opposite sides of the pool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:17; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 17:14"> Then Abner said to Joab, “Let us have the young men get up and compete before us.” “Let them get up,” Joab replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> So they got up and were counted off—twelve for Benjamin and Ish-bosheth son of Saul, and twelve for David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then each man grabbed his opponent by the head and thrust his sword into his opponent’s side, and they all fell together. So this place, which is in Gibeon, is called Helkath-hazzurim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1"> The battle that day was intense, and Abner and the men of Israel were defeated by the servants of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:8; Habakkuk 3:19; Song of Solomon 8:14"> The three sons of Zeruiah were there: Joab, Abishai, and Asahel. Now Asahel was fleet of foot like a wild gazelle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:21; Joshua 1:7; Proverbs 4:27"> and he chased Abner, not turning to the right or to the left in his pursuit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Abner glanced back and said, “Is that you, Asahel?” “It is,” Asahel replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 14:19"> So Abner told him, “Turn to your right or to your left, seize one of the young men, and take his equipment for yourself.” But Asahel would not stop chasing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:27; Proverbs 29:1; 2 Kings 14:10–12"> Once again, Abner warned Asahel, “Stop chasing me. Why should I strike you to the ground? How could I show my face to your brother Joab?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:27; 2 Samuel 4:6; 2 Samuel 5:6"> But Asahel refused to turn away. So Abner thrust the butt of his spear into his stomach, and it came out his back, and he fell dead on the spot. And every man paused when he came to the place where Asahel had fallen and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> But Joab and Abishai pursued Abner. By sunset, they had gone as far as the hill of Ammah opposite Giah on the way to the wilderness of Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The Benjamites rallied to Abner, formed a single unit, and took their stand atop a hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 46:10; Acts 7:26"> Then Abner called out to Joab: “Must the sword devour forever? Do you not realize that this will only end in bitterness? How long before you tell the troops to stop pursuing their brothers?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; 2 Samuel 2:14; Proverbs 15:1"> “As surely as God lives,” Joab replied, “if you had not spoken up, the troops would have continued pursuing their brothers until morning.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> So Joab blew the ram’s horn, and all the troops stopped; they no longer pursued Israel or continued to fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8; Song of Solomon 2:17"> And all that night Abner and his men marched through the Arabah. They crossed the Jordan, marched all morning, and arrived at Mahanaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> When Joab returned from pursuing Abner, he gathered all the troops. In addition to Asahel, nineteen of David’s servants were missing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1; 1 Kings 20:11"> but they had struck down 360 Benjamites who were with Abner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13–16; 1 Samuel 17:58; 2 Chronicles 21:1"> And they took Asahel and buried him in his father’s tomb in Bethlehem. Then Joab and his men marched all night and reached Hebron at daybreak.</span>
