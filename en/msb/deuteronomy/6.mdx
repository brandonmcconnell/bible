<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Deuteronomy 12:1; Deuteronomy 4:45"> These are the commandments and statutes and ordinances that the LORD your God has instructed me to teach you to follow in the land that you are about to enter and possess,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 13:4; Exodus 20:20"> so that you and your children and grandchildren may fear the LORD your God all the days of your lives by keeping all His statutes and commandments that I give you, and so that your days may be prolonged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Exodus 3:8; Genesis 26:4"> Hear, O Israel, and be careful to observe them, so that you may prosper and multiply greatly in a land flowing with milk and honey, just as the LORD, the God of your fathers, has promised you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 12:29–32; Isaiah 44:6; 1 Timothy 2:5"> Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 12:30; Matthew 22:37; Luke 10:27"> And you shall love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Proverbs 7:3; Jeremiah 31:33"> These words I am commanding you today are to be upon your hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:19; Ephesians 6:4; Psalm 78:4–6"> And you shall teach them diligently to your children and speak of them when you sit at home and when you walk along the road, when you lie down and when you get up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:21; Proverbs 3:3; Deuteronomy 11:18"> Tie them as reminders on your hands and bind them on your foreheads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:20; Isaiah 30:8; Isaiah 57:8"> Write them on the doorposts of your houses and on your gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 24:13; Psalm 105:44; Nehemiah 9:25"> And when the LORD your God brings you into the land He swore to your fathers, to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, that He would give you—a land with great and splendid cities that you did not build,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:12–18; Deuteronomy 8:10–20; Ezekiel 16:10–20"> with houses full of every good thing with which you did not fill them, with wells that you did not dig, and with vineyards and olive groves that you did not plant—and when you eat and are satisfied,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> be careful not to forget the LORD who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:8; Deuteronomy 10:20; Matthew 4:10"> Fear the LORD your God, serve Him only, and take your oaths in His name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:6; 1 John 5:21; Deuteronomy 13:7"> Do not follow other gods, the gods of the peoples around you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 7:4"> For the LORD your God, who is among you, is a jealous God. Otherwise the anger of the LORD your God will be kindled against you, and He will wipe you off the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Luke 4:12; Matthew 4:7"> Do not test the LORD your God as you tested Him at Massah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:22; Psalm 119:4; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> You are to diligently keep the commandments of the LORD your God and the testimonies and statutes He has given you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 12:25; Romans 12:2"> Do what is right and good in the sight of the LORD, so that it may be well with you and that you may enter and possess the good land that the LORD your God swore to give your fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 33:52–53; Exodus 23:28–30; Judges 3:1–4"> driving out all your enemies before you, as the LORD has said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Exodus 12:26; Joshua 4:6–7"> In the future, when your son asks, “What is the meaning of the decrees and statutes and ordinances that the LORD our God has commanded you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 6:17–18; Ephesians 2:11–12; Deuteronomy 15:15"> then you are to tell him, “We were slaves of Pharaoh in Egypt, but the LORD brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Psalm 58:10–11; Deuteronomy 7:19"> Before our eyes the LORD inflicted great and devastating signs and wonders on Egypt, on Pharaoh, and on all his household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:18; Deuteronomy 6:10; Exodus 13:5"> But He brought us out from there to lead us in and give us the land that He had sworn to our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Psalm 41:2; Deuteronomy 10:12–13"> And the LORD commanded us to observe all these statutes and to fear the LORD our God, that we may always be prosperous and preserved, as we are to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:13; Romans 10:3; Leviticus 18:5"> And if we are careful to observe every one of these commandments before the LORD our God, as He has commanded us, then that will be our righteousness.”</span>
