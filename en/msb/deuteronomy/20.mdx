</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Deuteronomy 3:22; Deuteronomy 1:30"> For the LORD your God goes with you to fight for you against your enemies, to give you the victory.”</span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:5; Deuteronomy 28:30; Luke 14:18–20"> Has any man become pledged to a woman and not married her? Let him return home, or he may die in battle and another man marry her.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 7:3; Deuteronomy 1:28; Revelation 21:8"> Then the officers shall speak further to the army, saying, “Is any man afraid or fainthearted? Let him return home, so that the hearts of his brothers will not melt like his own.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> When the officers have finished addressing the army, they are to appoint commanders to lead it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 10:5–6; 2 Corinthians 5:18–1; Ephesians 2:17"> When you approach a city to fight against it, you are to make an offer of peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Joshua 11:19–20; Joshua 16:10"> If they accept your offer of peace and open their gates, all the people there will become forced laborers to serve you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> But if they refuse to make peace with you and wage war against you, lay siege to that city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 2:6–12; 1 Kings 11:15–16; Psalm 110:1"> When the LORD your God has delivered it into your hand, you must put every male to the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 8:2; Joshua 22:8; Numbers 31:9"> But the women, children, livestock, and whatever else is in the city—all its spoil—you may take as plunder, and you shall use the spoil of your enemies that the LORD your God gives you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> This is how you are to treat all the cities that are far away from you and do not belong to the nations nearby.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 11:14; Numbers 21:2–3; Numbers 33:52"> However, in the cities of the nations that the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance, you must not leave alive anything that breathes.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 34:5–6; Jeremiah 50:35–40; Deuteronomy 7:1–2"> For you must devote them to complete destruction—the Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites—as the LORD your God has commanded you,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:30–31; Exodus 23:33; Ephesians 5:11"> so that they cannot teach you to do all the detestable things they do for their gods, and so cause you to sin against the LORD your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
