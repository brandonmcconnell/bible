<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:1; Joshua 22:7; Numbers 33:17–18"> These are the words that Moses spoke to all Israel in the wilderness east of the Jordan—in the Arabah opposite Suph—between Paran and Tophel, Laban, Hazeroth, and Dizahab.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Numbers 13:26; Deuteronomy 9:23"> It is an eleven-day journey from Horeb to Kadesh-barnea by way of Mount Seir.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 33:38; Deuteronomy 4:1–2; Numbers 20:1"> In the fortieth year, on the first day of the eleventh month, Moses proclaimed to the Israelites all that the LORD had commanded him concerning them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Joshua 12:2–6; Joshua 13:10–12"> This was after he had defeated Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, and then at Edrei had defeated Og king of Bashan, who lived in Ashtaroth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:8; Deuteronomy 32:46; Deuteronomy 31:9"> On the east side of the Jordan in the land of Moab, Moses began to explain this law, saying:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Numbers 10:11–13; Exodus 19:1–2"> The LORD our God said to us at Horeb: “You have stayed at this mountain long enough.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 10:40; Amos 2:9"> Resume your journey and go to the hill country of the Amorites; go to all the neighboring peoples in the Arabah, in the hill country, in the foothills, in the Negev, and along the seacoast to the land of the Canaanites and to Lebanon, as far as the great River Euphrates.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 26:3–4"> See, I have placed the land before you. Enter and possess the land that the LORD swore He would give to your fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and to their descendants after them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 18:18; Numbers 11:17; Numbers 11:11–14"> At that time I said to you, “I cannot carry the burden for you alone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Exodus 32:13; Genesis 22:17"> The LORD your God has multiplied you, so that today you are as numerous as the stars in the sky.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 115:14; Genesis 22:17; Numbers 6:27"> May the LORD, the God of your fathers, increase you a thousand times over and bless you as He has promised.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; 1 Kings 3:7–9; 2 Corinthians 2:16"> But how can I bear your troubles, burdens, and disputes all by myself?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Acts 1:21–23; Numbers 11:16–17"> Choose for yourselves wise, understanding, and respected men from each of your tribes, and I will appoint them as your leaders.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And you answered me and said, “What you propose to do is good.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:18; Exodus 18:25–26; Ephesians 4:11"> So I took the leaders of your tribes, wise and respected men, and appointed them as leaders over you—as commanders of thousands, of hundreds, of fifties, and of tens, and as officers for your tribes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:24; Exodus 22:21; 2 Chronicles 19:6–10"> At that time I charged your judges: “Hear the disputes between your brothers, and judge fairly between a man and his brother or a foreign resident.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 18:26; James 2:9"> Show no partiality in judging; hear both small and great alike. Do not be intimidated by anyone, for judgment belongs to God. And bring to me any case too difficult for you, and I will hear it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:5; Acts 20:20"> And at that time I commanded you all the things you were to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:2; Deuteronomy 8:15; Jeremiah 2:6"> And just as the LORD our God had commanded us, we set out from Horeb and went toward the hill country of the Amorites, through all the vast and terrifying wilderness you have seen. When we reached Kadesh-barnea,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:7–8"> I said: “You have reached the hill country of the Amorites, which the LORD our God is giving us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Psalm 46:1; Numbers 14:8–9"> See, the LORD your God has placed the land before you. Go up and take possession of it as the LORD, the God of your fathers, has told you. Do not be afraid or discouraged.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 13:1–20"> Then all of you approached me and said, “Let us send men ahead of us to search out the land and bring us word of what route to follow and which cities to enter.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 13:3–33"> The plan seemed good to me, so I selected twelve men from among you, one from each tribe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 13:21–27; Joshua 2:1–2"> They left and went up into the hill country, and came to the Valley of Eshcol and spied out the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> They took some of the fruit of the land in their hands, carried it down to us, and brought us word: “It is a good land that the LORD our God is giving us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 14:1–4; Acts 7:51; Psalm 106:24–25"> But you were unwilling to go up; you rebelled against the command of the LORD your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:28; Psalm 106:25; Numbers 14:3"> You grumbled in your tents and said, “Because the LORD hates us, He has brought us out of the land of Egypt to deliver us into the hand of the Amorites to be annihilated.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 13:28–33; Deuteronomy 9:1–2; Joshua 15:14"> Where can we go? Our brothers have made our hearts melt, saying: ‘The people are larger and taller than we are; the cities are large, with walls up to the heavens. We even saw the descendants of the Anakim there.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> So I said to you: “Do not be terrified or afraid of them!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Nehemiah 4:20; Romans 8:31"> The LORD your God, who goes before you, will fight for you, just as you saw Him do for you in Egypt</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3–4; Acts 13:18; Hosea 11:3–4"> and in the wilderness, where the LORD your God carried you, as a man carries his son, all the way by which you traveled until you reached this place.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalm 106:24; Jude 1:5; Hebrews 3:18–19"> But in spite of all this, you did not trust the LORD your God,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 10:33; Psalm 78:14; Nehemiah 9:12"> who went before you on the journey, in the fire by night and in the cloud by day, to seek out a place for you to camp and to show you the road to travel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 14:22–30; Psalm 95:11; Ezekiel 20:15"> When the LORD heard your words, He grew angry and swore an oath, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalm 95:11"> “Not one of the men of this evil generation shall see the good land I swore to give your fathers,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Joshua 14:6–14; Numbers 32:12"> except Caleb son of Jephunneh. He will see it, and I will give him and his descendants the land on which he has set foot, because he followed the LORD wholeheartedly.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 20:12; Deuteronomy 4:21; Deuteronomy 34:4"> The LORD was also angry with me on your account, and He said, “Not even you shall enter the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Numbers 14:30; Deuteronomy 31:7–8"> Joshua son of Nun, who stands before you, will enter it. Encourage him, for he will enable Israel to inherit the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15–16; Numbers 14:3; Numbers 14:31"> And the little ones you said would become captives—your children who on that day did not know good from evil—will enter the land that I will give them, and they will possess it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 14:25"> But you are to turn back and head for the wilderness along the route to the Red Sea.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 14:39–45; Proverbs 19:3; Numbers 22:34"> “We have sinned against the LORD,” you replied. “We will go up and fight, as the LORD our God has commanded us.” Then each of you put on his weapons of war, thinking it easy to go up into the hill country.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Numbers 14:41–43; Joshua 7:8–13; Isaiah 59:1–2"> But the LORD said to me, “Tell them not to go up and fight, for I am not with you to keep you from defeat by your enemies.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 14:44; Isaiah 63:10; Acts 7:51"> So I spoke to you, but you would not listen. You rebelled against the command of the LORD and presumptuously went up into the hill country.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalm 118:12; Numbers 14:45; Numbers 21:3"> Then the Amorites who lived in the hills came out against you and chased you like a swarm of bees. They routed you from Seir all the way to Hormah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Psalm 78:34"> And you returned and wept before the LORD, but He would not listen to your voice or give ear to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 20:22; Judges 11:16–17"> For this reason you stayed in Kadesh for a long time—a very long time.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
