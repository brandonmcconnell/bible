<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 14:6; Psalm 90:1; Genesis 27:4"> This is the blessing that Moses the man of God pronounced upon the Israelites before his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jude 1:14; Habakkuk 3:3; Revelation 5:11"> He said: “The LORD came from Sinai and dawned upon us from Seir; He shone forth from Mount Paran and came with myriads of holy ones, with flaming fire at His right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 10:39; Malachi 1:2; Deuteronomy 7:6–8"> Surely You love the people; all the holy ones are in Your hand, and they sit down at Your feet; each receives Your words—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 1:17; Psalm 119:111; John 7:19"> the law that Moses gave us, the possession of the assembly of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Judges 17:6; Numbers 23:21"> So the LORD became King in Jeshurun when the leaders of the people gathered, when the tribes of Israel came together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:8; Numbers 32:31–32; Joshua 22:1–9"> Let Reuben live and not die, nor his men be few.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 49:8–12; Psalm 20:2; 2 Samuel 7:9–12"> And concerning Judah he said: “O LORD, hear the cry of Judah and bring him to his people. With his own hands he defends his cause, but may You be a help against his foes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Exodus 28:30; Numbers 20:13"> Concerning Levi he said: “Give Your Thummim to Levi and Your Urim to Your godly one, whom You tested at Massah and contested at the waters of Meribah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Exodus 32:25–29; Matthew 12:48"> He said of his father and mother, ‘I do not consider them.’ He disregarded his brothers and did not know his own sons, for he kept Your word and maintained Your covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:11; Psalm 51:19; Deuteronomy 17:9–11"> He will teach Your ordinances to Jacob and Your law to Israel; he will set incense before You and whole burnt offerings on Your altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:23; Ezekiel 43:27; Ezekiel 20:40–41"> Bless his substance, O LORD, and accept the work of his hands. Smash the loins of those who rise against him, and of his foes so they can rise no more.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Psalm 91:4; Matthew 23:37"> Concerning Benjamin he said: “May the beloved of the LORD rest secure in Him; God shields him all day long, and upon His shoulders he rests.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 49:22–26; Micah 5:7; Isaiah 18:4"> Concerning Joseph he said: “May his land be blessed by the LORD with the precious dew from heaven above and the deep waters that lie beneath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 104:19; Matthew 5:45; Psalm 8:3"> with the bountiful harvest from the sun and the abundant yield of the seasons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:6; Genesis 49:26; James 5:7"> with the best of the ancient mountains and the bounty of the everlasting hills,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Psalm 24:1; Exodus 3:2–4"> with the choice gifts of the land and everything in it, and with the favor of Him who dwelt in the burning bush. May these rest on the head of Joseph and crown the brow of the prince of his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 22:11; Numbers 23:22; Psalm 44:5"> His majesty is like a firstborn bull, and his horns are like those of a wild ox. With them he will gore the nations, even to the ends of the earth. Such are the myriads of Ephraim, and such are the thousands of Manasseh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:13–15; Joshua 19:11; Judges 5:14"> Concerning Zebulun he said: “Rejoice, Zebulun, in your journeys, and Issachar, in your tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 60:5; Isaiah 2:3; Psalm 4:5"> They will call the peoples to a mountain; there they will offer sacrifices of righteousness. For they will feast on the abundance of the seas and the hidden treasures of the sand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Genesis 9:26–27; 1 Chronicles 5:18–21"> Concerning Gad he said: “Blessed is he who enlarges the domain of Gad! He lies down like a lion and tears off an arm or a head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 32:16–42; Judges 5:11; Judges 5:2"> He chose the best land for himself, because a ruler’s portion was reserved for him there. He came with the leaders of the people; he administered the LORD’s justice and His ordinances for Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 18:27; Joshua 19:47; Judges 15:8"> Concerning Dan he said: “Dan is a lion’s cub, leaping out of Bashan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 49:21; Psalm 36:8; Matthew 4:16"> Concerning Naphtali he said: “Naphtali is abounding with favor, full of the blessing of the LORD; he shall take possession of the sea and the south.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:20; Job 29:6; Romans 14:18"> And concerning Asher he said: “May Asher be the most blessed of sons; may he be the most favored among his brothers and dip his foot in oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalm 138:3; Philippians 4:13"> May the bolts of your gate be iron and bronze, and may your strength match your days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Isaiah 43:11–13; Jeremiah 10:6"> “There is none like the God of Jeshurun, who rides the heavens to your aid, and the clouds in His majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalm 18:2; Psalm 90:1–2; Psalm 46:1"> The eternal God is your dwelling place, and underneath are the everlasting arms. He drives out the enemy before you, giving the command, ‘Destroy him!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:9; Genesis 27:28; Exodus 33:16"> So Israel dwells securely; the fountain of Jacob lives untroubled in a land of grain and new wine, where even the heavens drip with dew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 45:17; Isaiah 12:2; Psalm 115:9–11"> Blessed are you, O Israel! Who is like you, a people saved by the LORD? He is the shield that protects you, the sword in which you boast. Your enemies will cower before you, and you shall trample their high places.”</span>
</div>
