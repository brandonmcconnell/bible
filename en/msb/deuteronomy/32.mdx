<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Deuteronomy 4:26; Jeremiah 22:29"> Give ear, O heavens, and I will speak; hear, O earth, the words of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 55:10–11; Psalm 72:6; Micah 5:7"> Let my teaching fall like rain and my speech settle like dew, like gentle rain on new grass, like showers on tender plants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:6; Psalm 150:2; Ephesians 1:19"> For I will proclaim the name of the LORD. Ascribe greatness to our God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 18:30–31; Psalm 92:15; Isaiah 26:4"> He is the Rock, His work is perfect; all His ways are just. A God of faithfulness without injustice, righteous and upright is He.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; Philippians 2:15; Matthew 17:17"> His people have acted corruptly toward Him; the blemish on them is not that of His children, but of a perverse and crooked generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:16; 1 John 3:1; Isaiah 44:2"> Is this how you repay the LORD, O foolish and senseless people? Is He not your Father and Creator? Has He not made you and established you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 78:3–4; Job 8:8–10; Psalm 44:1"> Remember the days of old; consider the years long past. Ask your father, and he will tell you, your elders, and they will inform you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 17:26; Genesis 11:8–9; Daniel 4:17"> When the Most High gave the nations their inheritance, when He divided the sons of man, He set the boundaries of the peoples according to the number of the sons of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; 1 Peter 2:9–10; Exodus 19:5–6"> But the LORD’s portion is His people, Jacob His allotted inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; Psalm 17:8; Hosea 13:5"> He found him in a desert land, in a barren, howling wilderness; He surrounded him, He instructed him, He guarded him as the apple of His eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Isaiah 46:4; Isaiah 63:9"> As an eagle stirs up its nest and hovers over its young, He spread His wings to catch them and carried them on His pinions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 78:52–53; Isaiah 46:4; Isaiah 44:7–8"> The LORD alone led him, and no foreign god was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 58:14; Job 29:6; Psalm 81:16"> He made him ride on the heights of the land and fed him the produce of the field. He nourished him with honey from the rock and oil from the flinty crag,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 49:11; Psalm 81:16; Psalm 147:14"> with curds from the herd and milk from the flock, with the fat of lambs, with rams from Bashan, and goats, with the choicest grains of wheat. From the juice of the finest grapes you drank the wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:20; Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 33:5"> But Jeshurun grew fat and kicked—becoming fat, bloated, and gorged. He abandoned the God who made him and scorned the Rock of his salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 78:58; 1 Corinthians 10:22; Nahum 1:1–2"> They provoked His jealousy with foreign gods; they enraged Him with abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 5:8; Deuteronomy 28:64; Leviticus 17:7"> They sacrificed to demons, not to God, to gods they had not known, to newly arrived gods, which your fathers did not fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 17:10; Jeremiah 2:32; Psalm 106:21"> You ignored the Rock who brought you forth; you forgot the God who gave you birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 106:40; Psalm 5:4; Isaiah 1:2"> When the LORD saw this, He rejected them, provoked to anger by His sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:17–18; Luke 7:31–32; Deuteronomy 32:5"> He said: “I will hide My face from them; I will see what will be their end. For they are a perverse generation—children of unfaithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 10:19; Romans 9:25; Romans 11:11–14"> They have provoked My jealousy by that which is not God; they have enraged Me with their worthless idols. So I will make them jealous by those who are not a people; I will make them angry by a nation without understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Lamentations 4:11; Jeremiah 15:14; Micah 1:4"> For a fire has been kindled by My anger, and it burns to the depths of Sheol; it consumes the earth and its produce, and scorches the foundations of the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:16; Psalm 7:12–13; Isaiah 24:17–18"> I will heap disasters upon them; I will spend My arrows against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 26:22; Ezekiel 5:17; Isaiah 65:25"> They will be wasted from hunger and ravaged by pestilence and bitter plague; I will send the fangs of wild beasts against them, with the venom of vipers that slither in the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:15; Lamentations 1:20; Isaiah 30:16"> Outside, the sword will take their children, and inside, terror will strike the young man and the young woman, the infant and the gray-haired man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:37"> I would have said that I would cut them to pieces and blot out their memory from mankind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 14:15–16; Exodus 32:12; Zechariah 1:14–15"> if I had not dreaded the taunt of the enemy, lest their adversaries misunderstand and say: ‘Our own hand has prevailed; it was not the LORD who did all this.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Isaiah 27:11; Job 28:28"> Israel is a nation devoid of counsel, with no understanding among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalm 81:13; Deuteronomy 5:29; Hosea 14:9"> If only they were wise, they would understand it; they would comprehend their fate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 26:8; Joshua 23:10; Isaiah 30:17"> How could one man pursue a thousand, or two put ten thousand to flight, unless their Rock had sold them, unless the LORD had given them up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 14:25; 1 Samuel 2:2; Daniel 2:47"> For their rock is not like our Rock, even our enemies concede.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:18; Ezekiel 16:45–51; Isaiah 1:10"> But their vine is from the vine of Sodom and from the fields of Gomorrah. Their grapes are poisonous; their clusters are bitter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalm 58:4; Romans 3:13; Psalm 140:3"> Their wine is the venom of serpents, the deadly poison of cobras.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hosea 13:12; Job 14:17; Jeremiah 2:22"> “Have I not stored up these things, sealed up within My vaults?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Hebrews 10:30; Nahum 1:2"> Vengeance is Mine; I will repay. In due time their foot will slip; for their day of disaster is near, and their doom is coming quickly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalm 135:14; Psalm 106:45; Judges 2:18"> For the LORD will vindicate His people and have compassion on His servants when He sees that their strength is gone and no one remains, slave or free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Judges 10:14; 2 Kings 3:13"> He will say: “Where are their gods, the rock in which they took refuge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:18–19; Psalm 50:13; Judges 10:14"> which ate the fat of their sacrifices and drank the wine of their drink offerings? Let them rise up and help you; let them give you shelter!</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:6; Hosea 6:1; Job 5:18"> See now that I am He; there is no God besides Me. I bring death and I give life; I wound and I heal, and there is no one who can deliver from My hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 6:17–18; Genesis 14:22; Revelation 10:5–6"> For I lift up My hand to heaven and declare: As surely as I live forever,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 66:16; Isaiah 34:5–6; Isaiah 27:1"> when I sharpen My flashing sword, and My hand grasps it in judgment, I will take vengeance on My adversaries and repay those who hate Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Jeremiah 46:10; Ezekiel 38:21–22"> I will make My arrows drunk with blood, while My sword devours flesh—the blood of the slain and captives, the heads of the enemy leaders.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Revelation 19:2; Romans 12:19; Revelation 6:10"> Rejoice, O heavens, with Him, and let all God’s angels worship Him. Rejoice, O nations, with His people; for He will avenge the blood of His children. He will take vengeance on His adversaries and repay those who hate Him; He will cleanse His land and His people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Numbers 13:8; Numbers 13:16; Deuteronomy 31:30"> Then Moses came with Joshua son of Nun and recited all the words of this song in the hearing of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> When Moses had finished reciting all these words to all Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:19; Ezekiel 40:4; Proverbs 3:1–4"> he said to them, “Take to heart all the words I have solemnly declared to you this day, so that you may command your children to carefully follow all the words of this law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Proverbs 4:22; Leviticus 18:5; 2 Peter 1:16"> For they are not idle words to you, because they are your life, and by them you will live long in the land that you are crossing the Jordan to possess.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 27:12–13"> On that same day the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 27:12; Isaiah 33:17; Deuteronomy 34:1–5"> “Go up into the Abarim Range to Mount Nebo, in the land of Moab across from Jericho, and view the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the Israelites as their own possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Numbers 33:38; Numbers 20:24–29"> And there on the mountain that you climb, you will die and be gathered to your people, just as your brother Aaron died on Mount Hor and was gathered to his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 27:14; Numbers 20:11–14; Deuteronomy 3:23–27"> For at the waters of Meribah-kadesh in the Wilderness of Zin, both of you broke faith with Me among the Israelites by failing to treat Me as holy in their presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:1–4; Deuteronomy 3:27; Hebrews 11:39"> Although you shall see from a distance the land that I am giving the Israelites, you shall not enter it.”</span>
