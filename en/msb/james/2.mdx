<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 15:22; Esther 3:10; Esther 8:2"> Suppose a man comes into your meeting wearing a gold ring and fine clothes, and a poor man in shabby clothes also comes in.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:9; James 2:6; Luke 7:44–46"> If you lavish attention on the man in fine clothes and say, “Here is a seat of honor,” but say to the poor man, “You must stand” or “Sit here under my footstool,”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:24; Job 34:19; Matthew 7:1–5"> have you not discriminated among yourselves and become judges with evil thoughts?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 5:3; Luke 6:20; Revelation 2:9"> Listen, my beloved brothers: Has not God chosen the poor of this world to be rich in faith and to inherit the kingdom He promised those who love Him?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 17:5; 1 Corinthians 11:22; Proverbs 14:31"> But you have dishonored the poor. Is it not the rich who oppress you and drag you into court?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:9–11; Revelation 13:5–6; 1 Timothy 1:13"> Are they not the ones who blaspheme the noble name by which you have been called?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; Galatians 5:14; Matthew 22:39"> If you really fulfill the royal law stated in Scripture, “Love your neighbor as yourself,” you are doing well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; James 2:1–4; 1 John 3:4"> But if you show favoritism, you sin and are convicted by the law as transgressors.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Matthew 5:18–19; Deuteronomy 27:26"> Whoever keeps the whole law but stumbles at just one point is guilty of breaking all of it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 20:13–14; Matthew 5:21–28; Deuteronomy 5:17–18"> For He who said, “Do not commit adultery,” also said, “Do not murder.” If you do not commit adultery, but do commit murder, you have become a lawbreaker.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 1:25; James 2:8; 2 Peter 1:4–8"> Speak and act as those who are going to be judged by the law that gives freedom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Matthew 6:15; Proverbs 21:13"> For judgment without mercy will be shown to anyone who has not been merciful. Mercy triumphs over judgment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 7:26–27; Matthew 7:21–23; James 1:22–25"> What good is it, my brothers, if someone claims to have faith, but has no deeds? Can such faith save him?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 3:11; Matthew 25:35–40; Isaiah 58:7"> Suppose a brother or sister is without clothes and daily food.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 3:27–28; 1 John 3:16–18; Matthew 25:42–45"> If one of you tells him, “Go in peace; stay warm and well fed,” but does not provide for his physical needs, what good is that?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 2:26; 2 Peter 1:5–9; James 2:19–20"> So too, faith by itself, if it does not result in action, is dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 3:13; James 2:14; Galatians 5:6"> But someone will say, “You have faith and I have deeds.” Show me your faith apart from your deeds, and I will show you my faith by my deeds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 4:34; Mark 1:24; Matthew 8:29"> You believe that God is one. Good for you! Even the demons believe that—and shudder.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 2:17; James 2:26; Proverbs 12:11"> O foolish man, do you want evidence that faith without deeds is dead?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 22:16–18; Genesis 22:9–12; John 8:39"> Was not our father Abraham justified by what he did when he offered his son Isaac on the altar?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:3; Hebrews 11:17–19; Galatians 5:6"> You see that his faith was working with his actions, and his faith was perfected by what he did.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Isaiah 41:8; 2 Chronicles 20:7"> And the Scripture was fulfilled that says, “Abraham believed God, and it was credited to him as righteousness,” and he was called a friend of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 2:21–22; James 2:15–18; Psalm 60:12"> As you can see, then, a man is justified by his deeds and not by faith alone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Joshua 6:22–25; Joshua 2:19–21"> In the same way, was not even Rahab the prostitute justified by her actions when she welcomed the spies and sent them off on another route?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 2:20; James 2:17; James 2:14"> For as the body without the spirit is dead, so faith without deeds is dead.</span>
</div>
</div>
