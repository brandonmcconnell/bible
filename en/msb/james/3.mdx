<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:47–48; 1 Timothy 1:7; 1 Peter 5:3"> Not many of you should become teachers, my brothers, because you know that we who teach will be judged more strictly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:26; Proverbs 13:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> We all stumble in many ways. If anyone is never at fault in what he says, he is a perfect man, able to control his whole body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 32:9; Psalm 39:1; James 1:26"> Indeed, when we put bits into the mouths of horses to make them obey us, we can guide the whole animal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 107:25–27; Matthew 8:24; Acts 27:14–38"> Consider ships as well. Although they are so large and are driven by strong winds, they are steered by a very small rudder wherever the pilot is inclined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:21; Jeremiah 9:3–8; Proverbs 12:18"> In the same way, the tongue is a small part of the body, but it boasts of great things. Consider how small a spark sets a great forest ablaze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 120:2–3; Proverbs 16:27; Mark 7:15"> The tongue also is a fire, a world of wickedness among the parts of the body. It pollutes the whole person, sets the course of his life on fire, and is itself set on fire by hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 5:4"> All kinds of animals, birds, reptiles, and creatures of the sea are being tamed and have been tamed by man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalm 140:3; Psalm 55:21"> but no man can tame the tongue. It is an unruly evil, full of deadly poison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 10:7; Genesis 1:26–27; Psalm 62:4"> With the tongue we bless our God and Father, and with it we curse men, who have been made in God’s likeness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:14; 1 Peter 3:9; 1 Corinthians 3:3"> Out of the same mouth come blessing and cursing. My brothers, this should not be!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Can both fresh water and salt water flow from the same spring?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:43–44; Matthew 12:33; Matthew 7:16–20"> My brothers, can a fig tree grow olives, or a grapevine bear figs? In the same way, no spring can produce both salt water and fresh water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; James 3:17; Matthew 5:5"> Who is wise and understanding among you? Let him show it by his good conduct, by deeds done in the humility that comes from wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 3:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4; Philippians 2:3"> But if you harbor bitter jealousy and selfish ambition in your hearts, do not boast in it or deny the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> Such wisdom does not come from above, but is earthly, unspiritual, demonic.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 3:14; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> For where jealousy and selfish ambition exist, there will be disorder and every evil practice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 5:22–23; Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> But the wisdom from above is first of all pure, then peace-loving, gentle, accommodating, full of mercy and good fruit, impartial, and sincere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Hosea 10:12; Philippians 1:11"> Peacemakers who sow in peace reap the fruit of righteousness.</span>
