</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 34:10; Exodus 29:45–46; Isaiah 41:13"> This is the promise I made to you when you came out of Egypt. And My Spirit remains among you; do not be afraid.”</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; 1 Kings 8:11; John 7:37–39"> I will shake all the nations, and they will come with all their treasures, and I will fill this house with glory, says the LORD of Hosts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 50:10–12; Psalm 24:1; Isaiah 60:17"> The silver is Mine, and the gold is Mine, declares the LORD of Hosts.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 85:8–9; 2 Corinthians 3:9–10; Colossians 1:19–21"> The latter glory of this house will be greater than the former, says the LORD of Hosts. And in this place I will provide peace, declares the LORD of Hosts.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Haggai 2:20; Haggai 1:1; Haggai 1:15–1"> On the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Haggai the prophet, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10–11; Malachi 2:7; Deuteronomy 33:10"> “This is what the LORD of Hosts says: ‘Ask the priests for a ruling.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 6:27; Matthew 23:19; Leviticus 7:6"> If a man carries consecrated meat in the fold of his garment, and it touches bread, stew, wine, oil, or any other food, does that item become holy?’” “No,” replied the priests.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 5:2–3; Numbers 19:11–22; Leviticus 22:4–6"> So Haggai asked, “If one who is defiled by contact with a corpse touches any of these, does it become defiled?” “Yes, it becomes defiled,” the priests answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 28:9; Titus 1:15; Isaiah 1:11–15"> Then Haggai replied, “So it is with this people and this nation before Me, declares the LORD, and so it is with every work of their hands; whatever they offer there is defiled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Haggai 1:5; Ezra 3:10; Ezra 4:24"> Now consider carefully from this day forward: Before one stone was placed on another in the temple of the LORD,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Haggai 1:6; Malachi 2:2; Proverbs 3:9–10"> from that time, when one came expecting a heap of twenty ephahs of grain, there were but ten. When one came to the winepress to draw out fifty baths, there were but twenty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:22; Haggai 1:11; Haggai 1:9"> I struck you—all the work of your hands—with blight, mildew, and hail, but you did not turn to Me, declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 8:9; Haggai 2:15; Ezra 5:1–2"> Consider carefully from this day forward—from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, the day the foundation of the LORD’s temple was laid—consider carefully:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 3:10; Habakkuk 3:17–18; Proverbs 3:9–10"> Is there still seed in the barn? The vine, the fig, the pomegranate, and the olive tree have not yet yielded fruit. But from this day on, I will bless you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For the second time that day, the twenty-fourth day of the month, the word of the LORD came to Haggai, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6–10; Haggai 2:6–7; Haggai 1:1"> “Tell Zerubbabel governor of Judah that I am about to shake the heavens and the earth:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 5:10; Judges 7:22; Zephaniah 3:8"> I will overturn royal thrones and destroy the power of the kingdoms of the nations. I will overturn chariots and their riders; horses and their riders will fall, each by the sword of his brother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; Isaiah 42:1; Song of Solomon 8:6"> On that day, declares the LORD of Hosts, I will take you, My servant, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, declares the LORD, and I will make you like My signet ring, for I have chosen you, declares the LORD of Hosts.”</span>
</div>
