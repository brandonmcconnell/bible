<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2; Luke 17:24; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> After this I saw another angel descending from heaven with great authority, and the earth was illuminated by his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 14:8; Jeremiah 51:8; Jeremiah 51:37"> And he cried out in a mighty voice: “Fallen is Babylon the great! She has become a lair for demons and a haunt for every unclean spirit and every unclean and detestable bird.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 14:8; Revelation 17:2; Revelation 18:9"> All the nations have drunk the wine of the passion of her immorality. The kings of the earth were immoral with her, and the merchants of the earth have grown wealthy from the extravagance of her luxury.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Jeremiah 51:6"> Then I heard another voice from heaven say: “Come out of her, My people, so that you will not share in her sins or contract any of her plagues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 18:20–21; Ezra 9:6; Revelation 16:19"> For her sins are piled up to heaven, and God has remembered her iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:29; Psalm 137:8; Jeremiah 51:24"> Give back to her as she has done to others; pay her back double for what she has done; mix her a double portion in her own cup.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 47:7–9; Zephaniah 2:15; Lamentations 1:1"> As much as she has glorified herself and lived in luxury, give her the same measure of torment and grief. In her heart she says, ‘I sit as queen; I am not a widow and will never see grief.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:34; Jeremiah 50:31; Revelation 17:16"> Therefore her plagues will come in one day—death and grief and famine—and she will be consumed by fire, for mighty is the Lord God who judges her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:3; Revelation 18:3; Revelation 17:2"> Then the kings of the earth who committed sexual immorality and lived in luxury with her will weep and wail at the sight of the smoke rising from the fire that consumes her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:19; Revelation 18:8; Revelation 18:15–17"> In fear of her torment, they will stand at a distance and cry out: “Woe, woe to the great city, the mighty city of Babylon! For in a single hour your judgment has come.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 18:3; Revelation 18:23; Ezekiel 27:27–36"> And the merchants of the earth will weep and mourn over her, because there is no one left to buy their cargo—</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 17:4; Ezekiel 27:5–25; Proverbs 8:10–11"> cargo of gold, silver, precious stones, and pearls; of fine linen, purple, silk, and scarlet; of all kinds of citron wood and every article of ivory, precious wood, bronze, iron, and marble;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:13; Amos 2:6; 2 Peter 2:3"> of cinnamon, incense, myrrh, and frankincense; of wine, olive oil, fine flour, and wheat; of cattle, sheep, horses, and carriages; of bodies and souls of slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Psalm 106:14; Luke 16:25"> And they will say: “The fruit of your soul’s desire has departed from you; all your luxury and splendor have vanished, never to be seen again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 18:3; Acts 19:24–27; Ezekiel 27:31"> The merchants who sold these things and gained their wealth from her will stand at a distance, in fear of her torment. They will weep and mourn,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:4; Luke 16:19–31; Revelation 18:10–11"> saying: “Woe, woe to the great city, clothed in fine linen and purple and scarlet, adorned with gold and precious stones and pearls!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:27–36; Revelation 17:16; Isaiah 23:14"> For in a single hour such fabulous wealth has been destroyed!” Every shipmaster, passenger, and sailor, and all who make their living from the sea, will stand at a distance</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 13:4; Ezekiel 27:30–32; Revelation 18:9–10"> and cry out at the sight of the smoke rising from the fire that consumes her. “What city was ever like this great city?” they will exclaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 18:10; Joshua 7:6; Ezekiel 27:30"> Then they will throw dust on their heads as they weep and mourn and cry out: “Woe, woe to the great city, where all who had ships on the sea were enriched by her wealth! For in a single hour she has been destroyed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 6:10; Revelation 19:1–3; Luke 18:7–8"> Rejoice over her, O heaven, and you saints and apostles and prophets, because God has pronounced for you His judgment against her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:63–64; Ezekiel 26:21; Revelation 5:2"> Then a mighty angel picked up a stone the size of a great millstone and cast it into the sea, saying: “With such violence the great city of Babylon will be cast down, never to be seen again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:13; Jeremiah 25:10; Jeremiah 7:34"> And the sound of harpists and musicians, of flute players and trumpeters, will never ring out in you again. Nor will any craftsmen of any trade be found in you again, nor the sound of a millstone be heard in you again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 18:3; Isaiah 23:8; Nahum 3:4"> The light of a lamp will never shine in you again, and the voices of a bride and bridegroom will never call out in you again. For your merchants were the great ones of the earth, because all the nations were deceived by your sorcery.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 17:6; Revelation 16:6; Jeremiah 51:49"> And there was found in her the blood of prophets and saints, and of all who had been slain on the earth.</span>
