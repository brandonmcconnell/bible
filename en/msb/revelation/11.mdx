<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 21:15; Ezekiel 42:15–20; Zechariah 2:1–2"> Then I was given a measuring rod like a staff and was told, “Go and measure the temple of God and the altar, and count the number of worshipers there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Daniel 7:25; Luke 21:24"> But exclude the courtyard outside the temple. Do not measure it, because it has been given over to the nations, and they will trample the holy city for 42 months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Revelation 11:2; Deuteronomy 19:15"> And I will empower my two witnesses, and they will prophesy for 1,260 days, clothed in sackcloth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 4:11–14; Zechariah 4:2–3; Jeremiah 11:16"> These witnesses are the two olive trees and the two lampstands that stand before the Lord of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:14; 2 Kings 1:10–12; Hosea 6:5"> If anyone wants to harm them, fire proceeds from their mouths and devours their enemies. In this way, anyone who wants to harm them must be killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Luke 4:25; Psalm 105:26–36"> These witnesses have power to shut the sky so that no rain will fall during the days of their prophecy, and power to turn the waters into blood and to strike the earth with every kind of plague as often as they wish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 13:7; Daniel 8:23–24; Daniel 7:25"> When the two witnesses have finished their testimony, the beast that comes up from the Abyss will wage war with them, and will overpower and kill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Revelation 18:10; Revelation 18:21"> Their bodies will lie in the street of the great city—figuratively called Sodom and Egypt—where their Lord was also crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 79:2–3; Revelation 17:15; Revelation 10:11"> For three and a half days all peoples and tribes and tongues and nations will view their bodies and will not permit them to be laid in a tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 18:17; Revelation 3:10; Acts 5:33"> And those who dwell on the earth will gloat over them, and will celebrate and give one another gifts, because these two prophets had tormented them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Romans 8:11; Romans 8:2"> But after the three and a half days, the breath of life from God entered the two witnesses, and they stood on their feet, and great fear fell upon those who saw them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 4:1; 2 Kings 2:11; Acts 1:9"> And I heard a loud voice from heaven saying, “Come up here.” And they went up to heaven in a cloud as their enemies watched them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:7; Revelation 6:12; Joshua 7:19"> And in that day there was a great earthquake, and a tenth of the city collapsed. Seven thousand were killed in the quake, and the rest were terrified and gave glory to the God of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 8:13; Revelation 9:12; Revelation 15:1"> The second woe has passed. Behold, the third woe is coming shortly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 7:14; Revelation 12:10; Daniel 7:27"> Then the seventh angel sounded his trumpet, and loud voices called out in heaven: “The kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord and of His Christ, and He will reign forever and ever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 4:4; Revelation 4:10; Revelation 7:11"> And the twenty-four elders who sit on their thrones before God fell on their faces and worshiped God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 16:5; Revelation 4:8–9"> saying: “We give thanks to You, O Lord God Almighty, the One who is and who was, because You have taken Your great power and have begun to reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 19:5; Revelation 20:12; Revelation 6:10–11"> The nations were enraged, and Your wrath has come. The time has come to judge the dead and to reward Your servants the prophets, as well as the saints and those who fear Your name, both small and great—and to destroy those who destroy the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 16:21; Revelation 15:5–8; Ezekiel 38:22"> Then the temple of God in heaven was opened, and the ark of the covenant of the Lord appeared in His temple. And there were flashes of lightning, and rumblings, and peals of thunder, and a great hailstorm.</span>
