<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 111:2; Exodus 15:1–18; Psalm 139:14"> and they sang the song of God’s servant Moses and of the Lamb: “Great and wonderful are Your works, O Lord God Almighty! Just and true are Your ways, O King of the nations!</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Numbers 1:50; Matthew 27:51"> After this I looked, and the temple—the tabernacle of the Testimony—was opened in heaven.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 10:6; Revelation 5:8; Revelation 16:2–1"> Then one of the four living creatures gave the seven angels seven golden bowls full of the wrath of God, who lives forever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:4; 1 Kings 8:10–11; Exodus 40:34–35"> And the temple was filled with smoke from the glory of God and from His power; and no one could enter the temple until the seven plagues of the seven angels were completed.</span>
