</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; 2 Timothy 4:1–4; Revelation 16:15"> Wake up and strengthen what remains, which is about to die; for I have found your deeds incomplete in the sight of My God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 2:5; 2 Peter 3:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:2"> Remember, then, what you have received and heard. Keep it and repent. If you do not wake up, I will come upon you like a thief, and you will not know the hour when I will come upon you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 3:5; Revelation 7:9; Ecclesiastes 9:8"> But you do have a few people in Sardis who have not soiled their garments, and because they are worthy, they will walk with Me in white.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 10:32; Luke 12:8; Revelation 21:27"> Like them, he who overcomes will be dressed in white. And I will never blot out his name from the Book of Life, but I will confess his name before My Father and His angels.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 2:7"> He who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 22:22; Matthew 16:19; Revelation 1:18"> To the angel of the church in Philadelphia write: These are the words of the One who is holy and true, who holds the key of David. What He opens no one can shut (except Him who opens) and no one can open.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:9; Colossians 4:3; Revelation 3:7"> I know your deeds. Behold, I have placed before you an open door, which no one can shut. I know that you have only a little strength, yet you have kept My word and have not denied My name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:14; Revelation 2:9; Isaiah 49:23"> As for those who belong to the synagogue of Satan, who claim to be Jews but are liars instead, I will make them come and bow down at your feet, and they will know that I love you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 2:9; Revelation 14:12; Zechariah 13:9"> Because you have kept My command to persevere, I will also keep you from the hour of testing that is about to come upon the whole world, to test those who dwell on the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 1:12; Revelation 22:12; Revelation 2:10"> I am coming soon. Hold fast to what you have, so that no one will take your crown.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 22:4; Revelation 21:2; Revelation 14:1"> The one who overcomes I will make a pillar in the temple of My God, and he will never again leave it. Upon him I will write the name of My God, and the name of the city of My God (the new Jerusalem that comes down out of heaven from My God), and My new name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 2:7"> He who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; Revelation 1:5; 2 Corinthians 1:20"> To the angel of the church in Laodicea write: These are the words of the Amen, the faithful and true Witness, the Originator of God’s creation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:11; Matthew 6:24; 1 Kings 18:21"> I know your deeds; you are neither cold nor hot. How I wish you were one or the other!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 2:5; Zechariah 11:8–9; Jeremiah 14:19"> So because you are lukewarm—neither hot nor cold—I am about to vomit you out of My mouth!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 13:7; Hosea 12:8; Romans 7:24"> You say, ‘I am rich; I have grown wealthy and need nothing.’ But you do not realize that you are wretched, pitiful, poor, blind, and naked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; 1 Peter 1:7; Isaiah 55:1"> I counsel you to buy from Me gold refined by fire so that you may become rich, white garments so that you may be clothed and your shameful nakedness not exposed, and salve to anoint your eyes so that you may see.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 3:11–12; 1 Corinthians 11:32; Hebrews 12:5–11"> Those I love, I rebuke and discipline. Therefore be earnest and repent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 12:36–37; Revelation 19:9; John 14:21–23"> Behold, I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears My voice and opens the door, I will come in and dine with him, and he with Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:12; Matthew 19:28; Revelation 2:26–27"> To the one who overcomes, I will grant the right to sit with Me on My throne, just as I overcame and sat down with My Father on His throne.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 2:17"> He who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches.”</span>
