<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:19–22; Lamentations 2:19; Isaiah 1:21"> How the gold has become tarnished, the pure gold has become dull! The gems of the temple lie scattered on every street corner.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:14; Jeremiah 19:11; Romans 9:21–23"> How the precious sons of Zion, once worth their weight in pure gold, are now esteemed as jars of clay, the work of a potter’s hands!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:10; Luke 23:28–29; Lamentations 2:20"> Even jackals offer their breasts to nurse their young, but the daughter of my people has become cruel, like an ostrich in the wilderness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 22:15; Lamentations 2:11–12; Matthew 7:9–11"> The nursing infant’s tongue clings in thirst to the roof of his mouth. Little children beg for bread, but no one gives them any.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 6:3–7; Luke 16:19; Luke 15:16"> Those who once ate delicacies are destitute in the streets; those brought up in crimson huddle in ash heaps.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 19:25; Luke 12:47; Luke 10:12"> The punishment of the daughter of my people is greater than that of Sodom, which was overthrown in an instant without a hand turned to help her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 51:7; Song of Solomon 5:10; Psalm 144:12"> Her dignitaries were brighter than snow, whiter than milk; their bodies were more ruddy than rubies, their appearance like sapphires.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 5:10; Psalm 102:3–5; Job 30:30"> But now their appearance is blacker than soot; they are not recognized in the streets. Their skin has shriveled on their bones; it has become as dry as a stick.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:23; Leviticus 26:39; Jeremiah 16:4"> Those slain by the sword are better off than those who die of hunger, who waste away, pierced with pain because the fields lack produce.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 2:20; Isaiah 49:15; 2 Kings 6:26–29"> The hands of compassionate women have cooked their own children, who became their food in the destruction of the daughter of my people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:20; Ezekiel 22:31; Jeremiah 17:27"> The LORD has exhausted His wrath; He has poured out His fierce anger; He has kindled a fire in Zion, and it has consumed her foundations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Deuteronomy 29:24–28; Psalm 48:4–6"> The kings of the earth did not believe, nor any people of the world, that an enemy or a foe could enter the gates of Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Jeremiah 6:13; Jeremiah 26:8–9"> But this was for the sins of her prophets and the guilt of her priests, who shed the blood of the righteous in her midst.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:34; Isaiah 56:10; Isaiah 1:15"> They wandered blind in the streets, defiled by this blood, so that no one dared to touch their garments.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46"> “Go away! Unclean!” men shouted at them. “Away, away! Do not touch us!” So they fled and wandered. Among the nations it was said, “They can stay here no longer.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 5:12; Isaiah 9:14–16; Deuteronomy 28:64–65"> The presence of the LORD has scattered them; He regards them no more. The priests are shown no honor; the elders find no favor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:16; Isaiah 20:5; 2 Kings 24:7"> All the while our eyes were failing as we looked in vain for help. We watched from our towers for a nation that could not save us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 8:2; Ezekiel 7:2–12; Lamentations 3:52"> They stalked our every step, so that we could not walk in our streets. Our end drew near, our time ran out, for our end had come!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Jeremiah 4:13; Isaiah 5:26–28"> Those who chased us were swifter than the eagles in the sky; they pursued us over the mountains and ambushed us in the wilderness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:21; Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 39:5"> The LORD’s anointed, the breath of our life, was captured in their pits. We had said of him, “Under his shadow we will live among the nations.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Amos 1:11; Revelation 16:15; Job 1:1"> So rejoice and be glad, O Daughter of Edom, you who dwell in the land of Uz. Yet the cup will pass to you as well; you will get drunk and expose yourself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 40:2; Psalm 137:7; Jeremiah 50:20"> O Daughter of Zion, your punishment is complete; He will not prolong your exile. But He will punish your iniquity, O Daughter of Edom; He will expose your sins.</span>
</div>
</div>
