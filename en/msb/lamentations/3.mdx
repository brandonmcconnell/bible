<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 88:7; Job 19:21; Psalm 88:15–16"> I am the man who has seen affliction under the rod of God’s wrath.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 38:13; Psalm 51:8; Psalm 31:9–10"> He has worn away my flesh and skin; He has shattered my bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 3:19; Jeremiah 23:15; Job 19:8"> He has besieged me and surrounded me with bitterness and hardship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 88:5–6; Psalm 143:3; Psalm 143:7"> He has made me dwell in darkness like those dead for ages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 19:8; Job 3:23; Jeremiah 40:4"> He has walled me in so I cannot escape; He has weighed me down with chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 30:20; Psalm 22:2; Job 19:7"> Even when I cry out and plead for help, He shuts out my prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 63:17; Lamentations 3:11; Isaiah 30:28"> He has barred my ways with cut stones; He has made my paths crooked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 10:9; Amos 5:18–20; Hosea 13:7–8"> He is a bear lying in wait, a lion hiding in ambush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 6:1; Job 16:12–13; Lamentations 1:13"> He forced me off my path and tore me to pieces; He left me without help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 38:2; Job 7:20; Job 6:4"> He bent His bow and set me as the target for His arrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 6:4; Job 41:28; Deuteronomy 32:23"> He pierced my kidneys with His arrows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:7; Lamentations 3:63; Psalm 22:6–7"> I am a laughingstock to all my people; they mock me in song all day long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:15; Jeremiah 23:15; Ruth 1:20"> He has filled me with bitterness; He has intoxicated me with wormwood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 20:17; Jeremiah 6:26; Psalm 3:7"> He has ground my teeth with gravel and trampled me in the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 59:11; Zechariah 8:10; Jeremiah 8:15"> My soul has been deprived of peace; I have forgotten what prosperity is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 17:15; 1 Samuel 27:1; Psalm 116:11"> So I say, “My strength has perished, along with my hope from the LORD.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Lamentations 3:15; Lamentations 3:5; Psalm 89:50"> Remember my affliction and wandering, the wormwood and the gall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 42:11; Psalm 42:5–6; Job 21:6"> Surely my soul remembers and is humbled within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; Psalm 130:7; Psalm 77:7–11"> Yet I call this to mind, and therefore I have hope:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 86:15; Luke 1:50; Nehemiah 9:31"> Because of the loving devotion of the LORD we are not consumed, for His mercies never fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 10:23; Psalm 30:5; Isaiah 33:2"> They are new every morning; great is Your faithfulness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 73:26; Psalm 16:5; Lamentations 3:21"> “The LORD is my portion,” says my soul, “therefore I will hope in Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Micah 7:7–8; Isaiah 40:31; Psalm 130:5–6"> The LORD is good to those who wait for Him, to the soul who seeks Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 37:7; Psalm 37:34; Psalm 130:5"> It is good to wait quietly for the salvation of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:1; Matthew 11:29–30; Psalm 119:71"> It is good for a man to bear the yoke while he is still young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:17; Lamentations 2:10; Psalm 102:7"> Let him sit alone in silence, for the LORD has laid it upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 40:4; Luke 15:18–19; Romans 3:19"> Let him bury his face in the dust—perhaps there is still hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 16:10; Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 26:67"> Let him offer his cheek to the one who would strike him; let him be filled with reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalm 94:14; Psalm 103:8–10; Psalm 77:7"> For the Lord will not cast us off forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalm 78:38; Psalm 30:5; Psalm 103:11"> Even if He causes grief, He will show compassion according to His abundant loving devotion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:11; Ezekiel 18:32; Hebrews 12:9–10"> For He does not willingly afflict or grieve the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11–12; Isaiah 51:22–23; Psalm 102:20"> To crush underfoot all the prisoners of the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> to deny a man justice before the Most High,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 59:15; Habakkuk 1:13; Jeremiah 22:3"> to subvert a man in his lawsuit—of these the Lord does not approve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Psalm 33:9–11; Isaiah 46:10; Proverbs 16:9"> Who has spoken and it came to pass, unless the Lord has ordained it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Amos 3:6; Jeremiah 32:42"> Do not both adversity and good come from the mouth of the Most High?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 19:3; Micah 7:9; Genesis 4:5–7"> Why should any mortal man complain, in view of his sins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:5; Psalm 139:23–24; Joel 2:12–13"> Let us examine and test our ways, and turn back to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalm 25:1; Psalm 28:2; Psalm 86:4"> Let us lift up our hearts and hands to God in heaven:</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 15:18–19; Nehemiah 9:26; 2 Kings 24:4"> “We have sinned and rebelled; You have not forgiven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Lamentations 2:17; Lamentations 2:21; Lamentations 3:66"> You have covered Yourself in anger and pursued us; You have killed without pity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 3:8; Zechariah 7:13; Psalm 97:2"> You have covered Yourself with a cloud that no prayer can pass through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:13; Lamentations 2:15; Lamentations 3:14"> You have made us scum and refuse among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalm 22:6–8; Lamentations 2:16; Psalm 44:13–14"> All our enemies open their mouths against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Isaiah 24:17–18; Isaiah 51:19; Jeremiah 48:43–44"> Panic and pitfall have come upon us—devastation and destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Jeremiah 9:18; Jeremiah 9:1"> Streams of tears flow from my eyes over the destruction of the daughter of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:17; Psalm 77:2; Lamentations 1:16"> My eyes overflow unceasingly, without relief,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Isaiah 63:15; Lamentations 5:1; Daniel 9:16–19"> until the LORD looks down from heaven and sees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:9; Luke 19:41–44; Lamentations 1:18"> My eyes bring grief to my soul because of all the daughters of my city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Psalm 35:19; Psalm 35:7; Jeremiah 37:15–16"> Without cause my enemies hunted me like a bird.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Daniel 6:17; Jeremiah 38:9; Jeremiah 38:6"> They dropped me alive into a pit and cast stones upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Jonah 2:3–5; Ezekiel 37:11; Lamentations 3:18"> The waters flowed over my head, and I thought I was going to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Acts 16:24–28; Psalm 142:3–7; Jonah 2:2–4"> I called on Your name, O LORD, out of the depths of the Pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Isaiah 38:5; Job 34:28; Psalm 6:8–9"> You heard my plea: “Do not ignore my cry for relief.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 41:10; Isaiah 58:9"> You drew near when I called on You; You said, “Do not be afraid.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Psalm 34:22–1; Jeremiah 51:36; Psalm 103:4"> You defend my cause, O Lord; You redeem my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Psalm 43:1; Psalm 9:4; Genesis 31:42"> You have seen, O LORD, the wrong done to me; vindicate my cause!</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:19–20; Psalm 10:14; Lamentations 3:59"> You have seen all their malice, all their plots against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Lamentations 5:1; Psalm 89:50; Zephaniah 2:8"> O LORD, You have heard their insults, all their plots against me—</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:3; Psalm 59:7; Psalm 140:3"> the slander and murmuring of my assailants against me all day long.</span>
