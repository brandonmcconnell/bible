<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 23:29; Jude 1:11; Exodus 27:1–8"> Then Balaam said to Balak, “Build for me seven altars here, and prepare for me seven bulls and seven rams.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:14; Numbers 23:30"> So Balak did as Balaam had instructed, and Balak and Balaam offered a bull and a ram on each altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 23:15; Genesis 8:20; Genesis 22:2"> “Stay here by your burnt offering while I am gone,” Balaam said to Balak. “Perhaps the LORD will meet with me. And whatever He reveals to me, I will tell you.” So Balaam went off to a barren height,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 23:16; Ephesians 2:9; Luke 18:12"> and God met with him. “I have set up seven altars,” Balaam said, “and on each altar I have offered a bull and a ram.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18; Jeremiah 1:9; Isaiah 59:21"> Then the LORD put a message in Balaam’s mouth, saying, “Return to Balak and give him this message.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> So he returned to Balak, who was standing there beside his burnt offering, with all the princes of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:4; Numbers 24:3; Numbers 22:5–6"> And Balaam lifted up an oracle, saying: “Balak brought me from Aram, the king of Moab from the mountains of the east. ‘Come,’ he said, ‘put a curse on Jacob for me; come and denounce Israel!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:23; Isaiah 44:25; Numbers 23:20"> How can I curse those whom God has not cursed? How can I denounce those whom the LORD has not denounced?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:28; Deuteronomy 32:8; Exodus 33:16"> For I see them from atop the rocky cliffs, and I watch them from the hills. Behold, a people dwelling apart, not reckoning themselves among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 37:37; Genesis 13:16; Psalm 116:15"> Who can count the dust of Jacob or number even a fourth of Israel? Let me die the death of the righteous; let my end be like theirs!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:2; Numbers 24:10; Numbers 22:17"> Then Balak said to Balaam, “What have you done to me? I brought you here to curse my enemies, and behold, you have only blessed them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:38; Numbers 23:20; Numbers 23:26"> But Balaam replied, “Should I not speak exactly what the LORD puts in my mouth?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 109:17; James 3:9–10; Numbers 22:41"> Then Balak said to him, “Please come with me to another place where you can see them. You will only see the outskirts of their camp—not all of them. And from there, curse them for me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6; Numbers 23:29; Isaiah 1:10–11"> So Balak took him to the field of Zophim, to the top of Pisgah, where he built seven altars and offered a bull and a ram on each altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 23:3; Numbers 22:8"> Balaam said to Balak, “Stay here beside your burnt offering while I meet the LORD over there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 23:5; Numbers 24:1; Numbers 22:35"> And the LORD met with Balaam and put a message in his mouth, saying, “Return to Balak and speak what I tell you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 23:26; 1 Samuel 3:17; Jeremiah 37:17"> So he returned to Balak, who was standing there by his burnt offering with the princes of Moab. “What did the LORD say?” Balak asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 3:20"> Then Balaam lifted up an oracle, saying: “Arise, O Balak, and listen; give ear to me, O son of Zippor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:29; James 1:17; Malachi 3:6"> God is not a man, that He should lie, or a son of man, that He should change His mind. Does He speak and not act? Does He promise and not fulfill?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:27–29; Numbers 22:12; Genesis 12:2"> I have indeed received a command to bless; He has blessed, and I cannot change it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 23:4; Matthew 1:23; Jeremiah 50:20"> He considers no disaster for Jacob; He sees no trouble for Israel. The LORD their God is with them, and the shout of the King is among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 24:8; Deuteronomy 33:17; Job 39:9–11"> God brought them out of Egypt with strength like a wild ox.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 10:18–19; Joshua 13:22; Galatians 1:23–24"> For there is no spell against Jacob and no divination against Israel. It will now be said of Jacob and Israel, ‘What great things God has done!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 24:17"> Behold, the people rise like a lioness; they rouse themselves like a lion, not resting until they devour their prey and drink the blood of the slain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 2:1–3"> Now Balak said to Balaam, “Then neither curse them at all nor bless them at all!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Numbers 22:18; Acts 4:19–20"> But Balaam replied, “Did I not tell you that whatever the LORD says, I must do?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 11:29; Proverbs 21:30; Numbers 23:19–20"> “Please come,” said Balak, “I will take you to another place. Perhaps it will please God that you curse them for me from there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 21:20; Psalm 106:28"> And Balak took Balaam to the top of Peor, which overlooks the wasteland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 23:1–2"> Then Balaam said, “Build for me seven altars here, and prepare for me seven bulls and seven rams.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> So Balak did as Balaam had instructed, and he offered a bull and a ram on each altar.</span>
