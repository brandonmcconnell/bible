<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 4:17; Ezekiel 19:14; Genesis 49:10"> “Speak to the Israelites and take from them twelve staffs, one from the leader of each tribe. Write each man’s name on his staff,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 6:20; Numbers 18:1; Numbers 3:2–3"> and write Aaron’s name on the staff of Levi, because there must be one staff for the head of each tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 30:36; Exodus 29:42–43; Exodus 25:16–22"> Place the staffs in the Tent of Meeting in front of the Testimony, where I meet with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:5; Numbers 17:8; Numbers 16:11"> The staff belonging to the man I choose will sprout, and I will rid Myself of the constant grumbling of the Israelites against you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> So Moses spoke to the Israelites, and each of their leaders gave him a staff—one for each of the leaders of their tribes, twelve staffs in all. And Aaron’s staff was among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 7:44; Exodus 38:21; Numbers 18:2"> Then Moses placed the staffs before the LORD in the Tent of the Testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; John 15:1–6; Psalm 132:17–18"> The next day Moses entered the Tent of the Testimony and saw that Aaron’s staff, representing the house of Levi, had sprouted, put forth buds, blossomed, and produced almonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then Moses brought out all the staffs from the LORD’s presence to all the Israelites. They saw them, and each man took his own staff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Ephesians 5:6; Isaiah 1:2"> The LORD said to Moses, “Put Aaron’s staff back in front of the Testimony, to be kept as a sign for the rebellious, so that you may put an end to their grumbling against Me, lest they die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> So Moses did as the LORD had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 26:11; Isaiah 57:16; Isaiah 6:5"> Then the Israelites declared to Moses, “Look, we are perishing! We are lost; we are all lost!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; 2 Samuel 6:6–12; Genesis 3:3"> Anyone who comes near the tabernacle of the LORD will die. Are we all going to perish?”</span>
