<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:1; Deuteronomy 3:8; Numbers 21:20"> Then the Israelites traveled on and camped in the plains of Moab near the Jordan, across from Jericho.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:25; Numbers 21:20–35; Numbers 21:3"> Now Balak son of Zippor saw all that Israel had done to the Amorites,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 15:15; Isaiah 23:5; Joshua 9:24"> and Moab was terrified of the people because they were numerous. Indeed, Moab dreaded the Israelites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 25:15–18; Numbers 31:8; Numbers 24:17"> So the Moabites said to the elders of Midian, “This horde will devour everything around us, as an ox licks up the grass of the field.” Since Balak son of Zippor was king of Moab at that time,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:4; Joshua 24:9; Micah 6:5"> he sent messengers to summon Balaam son of Beor at Pethor, which is by the Euphrates in the land of his people. “Behold, a people has come out of Egypt,” said Balak. “They cover the face of the land and have settled next to me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Numbers 23:7–8; Acts 16:16"> So please come now and put a curse on this people, because they are too mighty for me. Perhaps I may be able to defeat them and drive them out of the land; for I know that those you bless are blessed, and those you curse are cursed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:7–8; Micah 3:11; Romans 16:18"> The elders of Moab and Midian departed with the fees for divination in hand. They came to Balaam and relayed to him the words of Balak.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:12; Numbers 22:19–20; Jeremiah 12:2"> “Spend the night here,” Balaam replied, “and I will give you the answer that the LORD speaks to me.” So the princes of Moab stayed with Balaam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 20:3; Numbers 22:20; Genesis 16:8"> Then God came to Balaam and asked, “Who are these men with you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Balaam said to God, “Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, sent me this message:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> ‘Behold, a people has come out of Egypt, and they cover the face of the land. Now come and put a curse on them for me. Perhaps I may be able to fight against them and drive them away.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Ephesians 1:3; Genesis 12:2"> But God said to Balaam, “Do not go with them. You are not to curse this people, for they are blessed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Numbers 22:14"> So Balaam got up the next morning and said to Balak’s princes, “Go back to your homeland, because the LORD has refused to let me go with you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 22:37; Numbers 22:13"> And the princes of Moab arose, returned to Balak, and said, “Balaam refused to come with us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 10:7–8; Numbers 22:7–8"> Then Balak sent other princes, more numerous and more distinguished than the first messengers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> They came to Balaam and said, “This is what Balak son of Zippor says: ‘Please let nothing hinder you from coming to me,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 24:11; Esther 5:11; Matthew 4:8–9"> for I will honor you richly and do whatever you say. So please come and put a curse on this people for me!’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 24:13; 1 Kings 22:14; 2 Chronicles 18:13"> But Balaam replied to the servants of Balak, “If Balak were to give me his house full of silver and gold, I could not do anything small or great to go beyond the command of the LORD my God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jude 1:11; 2 Peter 2:3; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> So now, please stay here overnight as the others did, that I may find out what else the LORD has to tell me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 23:12; Numbers 24:13; Numbers 23:26"> That night God came to Balaam and said, “Since these men have come to summon you, get up and go with them, but you must only do what I tell you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 1:15–16"> So in the morning Balaam got up, saddled his donkey, and went with the princes of Moab.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 4:24; Lamentations 2:4; Numbers 22:32"> Then God’s anger was kindled because Balaam was going along, and the angel of the LORD stood in the road to oppose him. Balaam was riding his donkey, and his two servants were with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jude 1:11; 2 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29"> When the donkey saw the angel of the LORD standing in the road with a drawn sword in his hand, she turned off the path and went into a field. So Balaam beat her to return her to the path.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Then the angel of the LORD stood in a narrow passage between two vineyards, with walls on either side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 47:12; Job 5:13–15"> And the donkey saw the angel of the LORD and pressed herself against the wall, crushing Balaam’s foot against it. So he beat her once again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 2:6; Isaiah 26:11"> And the angel of the LORD moved on ahead and stood in a narrow place where there was no room to turn to the right or left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 14:16; James 1:19; Proverbs 27:3–4"> When the donkey saw the angel of the LORD, she lay down under Balaam, and he became furious and beat her with his staff.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Peter 2:16; Luke 1:37; Exodus 4:11"> Then the LORD opened the donkey’s mouth, and she said to Balaam, “What have I done to you that you have beaten me these three times?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 12:10; Matthew 15:19; Proverbs 12:16"> Balaam answered the donkey, “You have made a fool of me! If I had a sword in my hand, I would kill you right now!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 1:27–28"> But the donkey said to Balaam, “Am I not the donkey you have ridden all your life until today? Have I ever treated you this way before?” “No,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 21:19; Luke 24:31; Acts 26:18"> Then the LORD opened Balaam’s eyes, and he saw the angel of the LORD standing in the road with a drawn sword in his hand. And Balaam bowed low and fell facedown.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Acts 13:10; Numbers 22:22"> The angel of the LORD asked him, “Why have you beaten your donkey these three times? Behold, I have come out to oppose you, because your way is perverse before me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 16:33–35; Numbers 14:37; 1 Kings 13:24–28"> The donkey saw me and turned away from me these three times. If she had not turned away, then by now I would surely have killed you and let her live.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 34:31–32; 2 Samuel 12:13; 1 Samuel 15:24"> “I have sinned,” Balaam said to the angel of the LORD, “for I did not realize that you were standing in the road to confront me. And now, if this is displeasing in your sight, I will go back home.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–12; Isaiah 37:26–29; Numbers 22:20–21"> But the angel of the LORD said to Balaam, “Go with the men, but you are to speak only what I tell you.” So Balaam went with the princes of Balak.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 16:2; Acts 28:15; Jeremiah 48:20"> When Balak heard that Balaam was coming, he went out to meet him at the Moabite city on the Arnon border, at the edge of his territory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 24:11; John 5:44; Matthew 4:8–9"> And he said to Balaam, “Did I not send you an urgent summons? Why did you not come to me? Am I really not able to reward you richly?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 22:18; Proverbs 19:21; Numbers 23:16"> “See, I have come to you,” Balaam replied, “but can I say just anything? I must speak only the word that God puts in my mouth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> So Balaam accompanied Balak, and they came to Kiriath-huzoth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 23:14; Proverbs 1:16; Numbers 23:2"> Balak sacrificed cattle and sheep, and he gave portions to Balaam and the princes who were with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 23:13; Numbers 25:2–3; 2 Chronicles 11:15"> The next morning, Balak took Balaam and brought him up to Bamoth-baal. From there he could see the outskirts of the camp of the people.</span>
</div>
</div>
