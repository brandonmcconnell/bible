<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 21:1; Leviticus 13:46; Numbers 12:14"> “Command the Israelites to send away from the camp anyone with a skin disease, anyone who has a bodily discharge, and anyone who is defiled by a dead body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:14; Leviticus 26:11–12; Psalm 68:18"> You must send away male and female alike; send them outside the camp so they will not defile their camp, where I dwell among them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> So the Israelites did this, sending such people outside the camp. They did just as the LORD had instructed Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1–4; Leviticus 5:14–7"> “Tell the Israelites that when a man or woman acts unfaithfully against the LORD by committing any sin against another, that person is guilty</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 5:5; Leviticus 26:40; Joshua 7:19"> and must confess the sin he has committed. He must make full restitution, add a fifth to its value, and give all this to the one he has wronged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 6:6–7; Leviticus 7:7; Leviticus 25:25–26"> But if the man has no relative to whom restitution can be made for the wrong, the restitution belongs to the LORD and must be given to the priest along with the ram of atonement, by which the atonement is made for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:3–4; Leviticus 7:6–14; Leviticus 6:17–18"> Every sacred contribution the Israelites bring to the priest shall belong to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:13"> Each man’s sacred gifts are his own, but whatever he gives to the priest will belong to the priest.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 5:19–20; Proverbs 2:16–17"> “Speak to the Israelites and tell them that if any man’s wife goes astray and is unfaithful to him</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 18:20; Leviticus 20:10; Proverbs 7:18–19"> by sleeping with another man, and it is concealed from her husband and her impurity is undetected (since there is no witness against her and she was not caught in the act),</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:6; Proverbs 6:34; Zephaniah 3:8"> and if a feeling of jealousy comes over her husband and he suspects his wife who has defiled herself—or if a feeling of jealousy comes over him and he suspects her even though she has not defiled herself—</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:16; 1 Kings 17:18; Leviticus 5:11"> then he is to bring his wife to the priest. He must also bring for her an offering of a tenth of an ephah of barley flour. He is not to pour oil over it or put frankincense on it, because it is a grain offering for jealousy, an offering of memorial as a reminder of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 2:22–23; Jeremiah 17:10; Hebrews 13:4"> The priest is to bring the wife forward and have her stand before the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 30:18; John 8:6; Numbers 19:2–9"> Then he is to take some holy water in a clay jar and put some of the dust from the tabernacle floor into the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 5:4; Numbers 5:22; Revelation 10:9–10"> After the priest has the woman stand before the LORD, he is to let down her hair and place in her hands the grain offering of memorial, which is the grain offering for jealousy. The priest is to hold the bitter water that brings a curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 7:2; Numbers 5:12; Matthew 26:63"> And he is to put the woman under oath and say to her, ‘If no other man has slept with you and you have not gone astray and become defiled while under your husband’s authority, may you be immune to this bitter water that brings a curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 5:12"> But if you have gone astray while under your husband’s authority and have defiled yourself and lain carnally with a man other than your husband’—</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 6:26; Nehemiah 10:29; 1 Samuel 14:24"> and the priest shall have the woman swear under the oath of the curse—‘then may the LORD make you an attested curse among your people by making your thigh shrivel and your belly swell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 109:18; Psalm 89:52; Job 31:39–40"> May this water that brings a curse enter your stomach and cause your belly to swell and your thigh to shrivel.’ Then the woman is to say, ‘Amen, Amen.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Psalm 51:1; Psalm 51:9"> And the priest shall write these curses on a scroll and wash them off into the bitter water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 5:3–4; Malachi 3:5"> He is to have the woman drink the bitter water that brings a curse, and it will enter her and may cause her bitter suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 8:27; Numbers 5:18; Numbers 5:15"> The priest shall take from her hand the grain offering for jealousy, wave it before the LORD, and bring it to the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Leviticus 2:9; Leviticus 5:12"> Then the priest is to take a handful of the grain offering as a memorial portion and burn it on the altar; after that he is to have the woman drink the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:18; Zechariah 8:13; Jeremiah 29:18"> When he has made her drink the water, if she has defiled herself and been unfaithful to her husband, then the water that brings a curse will enter her and cause bitter suffering; her belly will swell, her thigh will shrivel, and she will become accursed among her people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Micah 7:7–10; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Psalm 113:9"> But if the woman has not defiled herself and is clean, she will be unaffected and able to conceive children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 5:19; Numbers 5:12; Numbers 5:15"> This is the law of jealousy when a wife goes astray and defiles herself while under her husband’s authority,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> or when a feeling of jealousy comes over a husband and he suspects his wife. He is to have the woman stand before the LORD, and the priest is to apply to her this entire law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Romans 2:8–9; Ezekiel 18:4"> The husband will be free from guilt, but the woman shall bear her iniquity.”</span>
