<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 6:9; Daniel 11:31; John 1:29"> And tell them that this is the food offering you are to present to the LORD as a regular burnt offering each day: two unblemished year-old male lambs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 16:36; Exodus 29:38–42"> along with a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a grain offering, mixed with a quarter hin of oil from pressed olives.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 5:25; Leviticus 6:9; Psalm 50:8"> This is a regular burnt offering established at Mount Sinai as a pleasing aroma, a food offering to the LORD.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 29:42; Numbers 28:14; Isaiah 57:6"> The drink offering accompanying each lamb shall be a quarter hin. Pour out the offering of fermented drink to the LORD in the sanctuary area.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And offer the second lamb at twilight, with the same grain offering and drink offering as in the morning. It is a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 58:13; Psalm 92:1–4; Revelation 1:10"> On the Sabbath day, present two unblemished year-old male lambs, accompanied by a grain offering of two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil, as well as a drink offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:4–5; Numbers 29:22; Numbers 29:31"> This is the burnt offering for every Sabbath, in addition to the regular burnt offering and its drink offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; Ezekiel 46:6; Ezra 3:5"> At the beginning of every month, you are to present to the LORD a burnt offering of two young bulls, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old, all unblemished,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 15:4–12; Ezekiel 46:5–7; Numbers 29:10"> along with three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering with each bull, two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering with the ram,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering with each lamb. This is a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma, a food offering to the LORD.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Their drink offerings shall be half a hin of wine with each bull, a third of a hin with the ram, and a quarter hin with each lamb. This is the monthly burnt offering to be made at each new moon throughout the year.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Numbers 28:22; Numbers 15:24"> In addition to the regular burnt offering with its drink offering, one male goat is to be presented to the LORD as a sin offering.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 12:16; Leviticus 23:7–8"> On the first day there is to be a sacred assembly; you must not do any regular work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Ezekiel 45:21–25; Numbers 29:8"> Present to the LORD a food offering, a burnt offering of two young bulls, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old, all unblemished.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The grain offering shall consist of fine flour mixed with oil; offer three-tenths of an ephah with each bull, two-tenths of an ephah with the ram,</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and a tenth of an ephah with each of the seven lambs.</span>

</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Offer the same food each day for seven days as a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. It is to be offered with its drink offering and the regular burnt offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:8; Numbers 28:18; Exodus 13:6"> On the seventh day you shall hold a sacred assembly; you must not do any regular work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Exodus 34:22; Leviticus 23:10"> On the day of firstfruits, when you present an offering of new grain to the LORD during the Feast of Weeks, you are to hold a sacred assembly; you must not do any regular work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 23:18–19; Numbers 28:11; Numbers 28:19"> Present a burnt offering of two young bulls, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old as a pleasing aroma to the LORD,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> together with their grain offerings of fine flour mixed with oil—three-tenths of an ephah with each bull, two-tenths of an ephah with the ram,</span>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> and a tenth of an ephah with each of the seven lambs.</span>
