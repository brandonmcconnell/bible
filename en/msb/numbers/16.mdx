<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jude 1:11; Deuteronomy 11:6; Exodus 6:21"> Now Korah son of Izhar, the son of Kohath son of Levi, along with some Reubenites—Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab, and On son of Peleth—conducted</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 26:9; Ezekiel 23:10; Genesis 6:4"> a rebellion against Moses, along with 250 men of Israel renowned as leaders of the congregation and representatives in the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 106:16; Numbers 14:14; Exodus 19:6"> They came together against Moses and Aaron and told them, “You have taken too much upon yourselves! For everyone in the entire congregation is holy, and the LORD is in their midst. Why then do you exalt yourselves above the assembly of the LORD?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 14:5; Numbers 20:6; Joshua 7:6"> When Moses heard this, he fell facedown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 65:4; Numbers 17:5; 2 Timothy 2:19"> Then he said to Korah and all his followers, “Tomorrow morning the LORD will reveal who belongs to Him and who is holy, and He will bring that person near to Himself. The one He chooses He will bring near to Himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1; Numbers 16:46–48; 1 Kings 18:21–23"> You, Korah, and all your followers are to do as follows: Take censers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 16:3; Ephesians 1:4; 1 Kings 18:17–18"> and tomorrow you are to place fire and incense in them in the presence of the LORD. Then the man the LORD chooses will be the one who is holy. It is you sons of Levi who have taken too much upon yourselves!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Moses also said to Korah, “Now listen, you sons of Levi!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 7:13; Deuteronomy 10:8; Ezekiel 44:10–11"> Is it not enough for you that the God of Israel has separated you from the congregation of Israel and brought you near to Himself to perform the work at the LORD’s tabernacle, and to stand before the congregation to minister to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 13:10; 3 John 1:9; Numbers 18:7"> He has brought you near, you and all your fellow Levites, but you are seeking the priesthood as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 16:7–8; Exodus 17:2; Numbers 16:3"> Therefore, it is you and all your followers who have conspired against the LORD! As for Aaron, who is he that you should grumble against him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:13–14; Jude 1:8; Proverbs 29:9"> Then Moses summoned Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab, but they said, “We will not come!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Exodus 2:14; Numbers 11:5"> Is it not enough that you have brought us up out of a land flowing with milk and honey to kill us in the wilderness? Must you also appoint yourself as ruler over us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:24; Numbers 36:8–10; Numbers 20:5"> Moreover, you have not brought us into a land flowing with milk and honey or given us an inheritance of fields and vineyards. Will you gouge out the eyes of these men? No, we will not come!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 4:4–5; 2 Corinthians 7:2; 1 Thessalonians 2:10"> Then Moses became very angry and said to the LORD, “Do not regard their offering. I have not taken one donkey from them or mistreated a single one of them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:3; 1 Samuel 12:7; 2 Timothy 2:14"> And Moses said to Korah, “You and all your followers are to appear before the LORD tomorrow—you and they and Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:7"> Each man is to take his censer, place incense in it, and present it before the LORD—250 censers. You and Aaron are to present your censers as well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> So each man took his censer, put fire and incense in it, and stood with Moses and Aaron at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:10; Numbers 16:42; Exodus 16:7"> When Korah had gathered his whole assembly against them at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, the glory of the LORD appeared to the whole congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the LORD said to Moses and Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 16:45; Exodus 32:10; Psalm 73:19"> “Separate yourselves from this congregation so that I may consume them in an instant.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 27:16; Zechariah 12:1; Job 12:10"> But Moses and Aaron fell facedown and said, “O God, the God of the spirits of all flesh, when one man sins, will You be angry with the whole congregation?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 16:21"> “Tell the congregation to move away from the dwellings of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 11:16–17; Numbers 11:25; Numbers 11:30"> So Moses got up and went to Dathan and Abiram, and the elders of Israel followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Revelation 18:4"> And he warned the congregation, “Move away now from the tents of these wicked men. Do not touch anything that belongs to them, or you will be swept away because of all their sins.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 9:4; Isaiah 28:14; Job 40:10–11"> So they moved away from the dwellings of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram. Meanwhile, Dathan and Abiram had come out and stood at the entrances to their tents with their wives and children and infants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:38; Exodus 3:12; John 14:11"> Then Moses said, “This is how you will know that the LORD has sent me to do all these things, for it was not my own doing:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Lamentations 4:22; 1 Kings 22:28; Exodus 32:34"> If these men die a natural death, or if they suffer the fate of all men, then the LORD has not sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalm 55:15; Numbers 16:33; Job 31:3"> But if the LORD brings about something unprecedented, and the earth opens its mouth and swallows them and all that belongs to them so that they go down alive into Sheol, then you will know that these men have treated the LORD with contempt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalm 106:17–18; Numbers 27:3; Deuteronomy 11:6"> As soon as Moses had finished saying all this, the ground beneath them split open,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalm 106:17; Numbers 26:11; Revelation 12:16"> and the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them and their households—all Korah’s men and all their possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:18; Isaiah 14:9; Psalm 9:15"> They went down alive into Sheol with all they owned. The earth closed over them, and they vanished from the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 14:5; Isaiah 33:3; Revelation 6:15–17"> At their cries, all the people of Israel who were around them fled, saying, “The earth may swallow us too!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 26:10; Leviticus 10:2; Psalm 106:18"> And fire came forth from the LORD and consumed the 250 men who were offering the incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 27:28; Numbers 16:18; Numbers 16:7"> “Tell Eleazar son of Aaron the priest to remove the censers from the flames and to scatter the coals far away, because the censers are holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 26:10; Ezekiel 14:8; 2 Peter 2:6"> As for the censers of those who sinned at the cost of their own lives, hammer them into sheets to overlay the altar, for these were presented before the LORD, and so have become holy. They will serve as a sign to the Israelites.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> So Eleazar the priest took the bronze censers brought by those who had been burned up, and he had them hammered out to overlay the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 3:10; Numbers 18:4–7; 2 Chronicles 26:16–21"> just as the LORD commanded him through Moses. This was to be a reminder to the Israelites that no outsider who is not a descendant of Aaron should approach to offer incense before the LORD, lest he become like Korah and his followers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 16:1–7; Psalm 106:23; Numbers 14:2"> The next day the whole congregation of Israel grumbled against Moses and Aaron, saying, “You have killed the LORD’s people!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Numbers 16:19; Leviticus 9:23; Numbers 20:6"> But when the congregation gathered against them, Moses and Aaron turned toward the Tent of Meeting, and suddenly the cloud covered it and the glory of the LORD appeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Then Moses and Aaron went to the front of the Tent of Meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 16:21–22; Numbers 16:24; Numbers 20:6"> “Get away from this congregation so that I may consume them in an instant.” And Moses and Aaron fell facedown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 8:19; 1 Chronicles 27:24; Leviticus 10:6"> Moses said to Aaron, “Take your censer, place fire from the altar in it, and add incense. Go quickly to the congregation and make atonement for them, because wrath has come out from the LORD; the plague has begun.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Deuteronomy 33:10–11; Psalm 106:29"> So Aaron took the censer as Moses had ordered and ran into the midst of the assembly. And seeing that the plague had begun among the people, he offered the incense and made atonement for the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:25; Hebrews 7:24–25; Numbers 16:18"> He stood between the living and the dead, and the plague was halted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 25:9; Numbers 16:32–35; Hebrews 2:1–3"> But those who died from the plague numbered 14,700, in addition to those who had died on account of Korah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:26–30; Numbers 16:43"> Then Aaron returned to Moses at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, since the plague had been halted.</span>
</div>
