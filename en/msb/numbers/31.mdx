<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 21:11; 1 Kings 11:15–16; 1 Samuel 27:9"> Then they waged war against Midian, as the LORD had commanded Moses, and they killed every male.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:21–22; Numbers 25:15; Revelation 2:14"> Among the slain were Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba—the five kings of Midian. They also killed Balaam son of Beor with the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; 2 Chronicles 28:5"> The Israelites captured the Midianite women and their children, and they plundered all their herds, flocks, and goods.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; 1 Samuel 30:1; Joshua 6:24"> Then they burned all the cities where the Midianites had lived, as well as all their encampments,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 8:2"> and carried away all the plunder and spoils, both people and animals.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:1"> They brought the captives, spoils, and plunder to Moses, to Eleazar the priest, and to the congregation of Israel at the camp on the plains of Moab, by the Jordan across from Jericho.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; 1 Samuel 30:21; Numbers 31:12"> And Moses, Eleazar the priest, and all the leaders of the congregation went to meet them outside the camp.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 13:19; 1 Samuel 15:13–14; Numbers 31:48"> But Moses was angry with the officers of the army—the commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds—who were returning from the battle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Jeremiah 48:10; Deuteronomy 2:34"> “Have you spared all the women?” he asked them.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 2 Peter 2:15; Revelation 2:14"> “Look, these women caused the sons of Israel, through the counsel of Balaam, to turn unfaithfully against the LORD at Peor, so that the plague struck the congregation of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 21:11–12; Deuteronomy 20:16–18; Deuteronomy 7:2"> So now, kill all the boys, as well as every woman who has had relations with a man,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:10–14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; Deuteronomy 20:14"> but spare for yourselves every girl who has never had relations with a man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 19:11–22; Numbers 5:2; 1 Chronicles 22:8"> All of you who have killed a person or touched the dead are to remain outside the camp for seven days. On the third day and the seventh day you are to purify both yourselves and your captives.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 19:22; Genesis 35:2; Exodus 19:10"> And purify every garment and leather good, everything made of goat’s hair, and every article of wood.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 30:16"> Then Eleazar the priest said to the soldiers who had gone into battle, “This is the statute of the law which the LORD has commanded Moses:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Only the gold, silver, bronze, iron, tin, and lead—</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 19:9; Numbers 19:17; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> everything that can withstand the fire—must be put through the fire, and it will be clean. But it must still be purified with the water of purification. And everything that cannot withstand the fire must pass through the water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 15:13; Leviticus 14:9"> On the seventh day you are to wash your clothes, and you will be clean. After that you may enter the camp.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The LORD said to Moses,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> “You and Eleazar the priest and the family heads of the congregation are to take a count of what was captured, both of man and beast.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 22:8; Psalm 68:12; 1 Samuel 30:24–25"> Then divide the captives between the troops who went out to battle and the rest of the congregation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 18:26; Numbers 31:30; Numbers 31:47"> Set aside a tribute for the LORD from what belongs to the soldiers who went into battle: one out of every five hundred, whether persons, cattle, donkeys, or sheep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 29:27; Deuteronomy 12:19; Deuteronomy 12:12"> Take it from their half and give it to Eleazar the priest as an offering to the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 3:7–8; Numbers 3:25"> From the Israelites’ half, take one out of every fifty, whether persons, cattle, donkeys, sheep, or other animals, and give them to the Levites who keep charge of the tabernacle of the LORD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> So Moses and Eleazar the priest did as the LORD had commanded Moses,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> and this plunder remained from the spoils the soldiers had taken: 675,000 sheep,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> 72,000 cattle,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> 61,000 donkeys,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and 32,000 women who had not slept with a man.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> This was the half portion for those who had gone to war: 337,500 sheep,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> including a tribute to the LORD of 675,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> 36,000 cattle, including a tribute to the LORD of 72,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> 30,500 donkeys, including a tribute to the LORD of 61,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and 16,000 people, including a tribute to the LORD of 32.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 18:8; Numbers 18:19; Hebrews 7:9–12"> Moses gave the tribute to Eleazar the priest as an offering for the LORD, as the LORD had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> From the Israelites’ half, which Moses had set apart from the men who had gone to war,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> this half belonged to the congregation: 337,500 sheep,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> 36,000 cattle,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> 30,500 donkeys,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> and 16,000 people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 31:30; Luke 10:1–8; Isaiah 56:10–11"> From the Israelites’ half, Moses took one out of every fifty persons and animals and gave them to the Levites who kept charge of the tabernacle of the LORD, as the LORD had commanded him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> Then the officers who were over the units of the army—the commanders of thousands and of hundreds—approached Moses</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 18:9; 1 Samuel 30:18–19; Psalm 72:14"> and said, “Your servants have counted the soldiers under our command, and not one of us is missing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 30:12; Exodus 30:15–16; Psalm 116:12"> So we have brought to the LORD an offering of the gold articles each man acquired—armlets, bracelets, rings, earrings, and necklaces—to make atonement for ourselves before the LORD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 7:2–6"> So Moses and Eleazar the priest received from them all the articles made out of gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> All the gold that the commanders of thousands and of hundreds presented as an offering to the LORD weighed 16,750 shekels.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:32"> Each of the soldiers had taken plunder for himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 30:16; Zechariah 6:14; Acts 10:4"> And Moses and Eleazar the priest received the gold from the commanders of thousands and of hundreds and brought it into the Tent of Meeting as a memorial for the Israelites before the LORD.</span>
</div>
