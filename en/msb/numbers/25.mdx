<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:8; Numbers 33:49; Joshua 2:1"> While Israel was staying in Shittim, the men began to indulge in sexual immorality with the daughters of Moab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:20; Exodus 20:5; Exodus 34:15–16"> who also invited them to the sacrifices for their gods. And the people ate and bowed down to these gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 22:17; Psalm 106:28–29; Hosea 9:10"> So Israel joined in worshiping Baal of Peor, and the anger of the LORD burned against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:17; Deuteronomy 4:3; Deuteronomy 21:23"> Then the LORD said to Moses, “Take all the leaders of the people and execute them in broad daylight before the LORD, so that His fierce anger may turn away from Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; 1 Kings 18:40; Deuteronomy 13:9"> So Moses told the judges of Israel, “Each of you must kill all of his men who have joined in worshiping Baal of Peor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Numbers 22:4; Jeremiah 36:23"> Just then an Israelite man brought to his family a Midianite woman in the sight of Moses and the whole congregation of Israel while they were weeping at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 6:25; Psalm 106:30; Joshua 22:30–31"> On seeing this, Phinehas son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, got up from the assembly, took a spear in his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 16:46–48; Psalm 106:29–31; 1 Chronicles 21:22"> followed the Israelite into his tent, and drove the spear through both of them—through the Israelite and on through the belly of the woman. So the plague against the Israelites was halted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:8; Deuteronomy 4:3–4; Numbers 25:4–5"> but those who died in the plague numbered 24,000.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Psalm 78:58; Deuteronomy 32:16"> “Phinehas son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, has turned My wrath away from the Israelites; for he was zealous for My sake among them, so that I did not consume the Israelites in My zeal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 2:4–5; Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 54:10"> Declare, therefore, that I am granting him My covenant of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17; Romans 10:2–4; Jeremiah 33:22"> It will be a covenant of permanent priesthood for him and his descendants, because he was zealous for his God and made atonement for the Israelites.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:23; Numbers 26:14; 2 Chronicles 19:7"> The name of the Israelite who was slain with the Midianite woman was Zimri son of Salu, the leader of a Simeonite family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Joshua 13:21; Numbers 25:18"> And the name of the slain Midianite woman was Cozbi, the daughter of Zur, a tribal chief of a Midianite family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 31:1–3; Revelation 18:6"> “Attack the Midianites and strike them dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 2:14; Exodus 32:35; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> For they assailed you deceitfully when they seduced you in the matter of Peor and their sister Cozbi, the daughter of the Midianite leader, the woman who was killed on the day the plague came because of Peor.”</span>
