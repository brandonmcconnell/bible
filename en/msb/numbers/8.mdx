<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And Aaron did so; he set up the lamps facing toward the front of the lampstand, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–40; Exodus 25:9; Exodus 25:18"> This is how the lampstand was constructed: it was made of hammered gold from its base to its blossoms, fashioned according to the pattern the LORD had shown Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Again the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; James 4:8; Exodus 19:15"> “Take the Levites from among the Israelites and make them ceremonially clean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 19:13; Numbers 19:7–10; Leviticus 8:6"> This is what you must do to cleanse them: Sprinkle them with the water of purification. Have them shave their whole bodies and wash their clothes, and so purify themselves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 29:1; Leviticus 16:3"> Then have them take a young bull with its grain offering of fine flour mixed with oil, and you are to take a second young bull for a sin offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 40:12; Leviticus 8:3; Exodus 29:4–37"> Bring the Levites before the Tent of Meeting and assemble the whole congregation of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 1:4; Acts 6:6; 1 Timothy 4:14"> You are to present the Levites before the LORD and have the Israelites lay their hands upon them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30; Leviticus 8:29; Numbers 1:49–53"> Aaron is to present the Levites before the LORD as a wave offering from the sons of Israel, so that they may perform the service of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–10; Exodus 29:10; Leviticus 1:4"> And the Levites are to lay their hands on the heads of the bulls, and offer to the LORD one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering, to make atonement for the Levites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 8:21; Numbers 8:11; Romans 15:16"> You are to have the Levites stand before Aaron and his sons and then present them before the LORD as a wave offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 3:45; Malachi 3:17; Numbers 18:6"> In this way you shall separate the Levites from the rest of the Israelites, and the Levites will belong to Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 8:11; Numbers 8:13; Numbers 3:23–37"> After you have cleansed them and presented them as a wave offering, they may come to serve at the Tent of Meeting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:45; Numbers 3:9"> For the Levites have been wholly given to Me from among the sons of Israel. I have taken them for Myself in place of all who come first from the womb, the firstborn of all the sons of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Luke 2:23; Exodus 13:12–15"> For every firstborn male in Israel is Mine, both man and beast. I set them apart for Myself on the day I struck down all the firstborn in the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But I have taken the Levites in place of all the firstborn among the sons of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 16:46; 2 Chronicles 26:16–20"> And I have given the Levites as a gift to Aaron and his sons from among the Israelites, to perform the service for the Israelites at the Tent of Meeting and to make atonement on their behalf, so that no plague will come against the Israelites when they approach the sanctuary.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So Moses, Aaron, and the whole congregation of Israel did with the Levites everything that the LORD had commanded Moses they should do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 8:7; Numbers 19:19; Numbers 8:15"> The Levites purified themselves and washed their clothes, and Aaron presented them as a wave offering before the LORD. Aaron also made atonement for them to cleanse them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:2; 2 Chronicles 35:8–15; Numbers 8:15"> After that, the Levites came to perform their service at the Tent of Meeting in the presence of Aaron and his sons. Thus they did with the Levites just as the LORD had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the LORD said to Moses,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 4:3; 1 Chronicles 23:3; Numbers 4:23"> “This applies to the Levites: Men twenty-five years of age or older shall enter to perform the service in the work at the Tent of Meeting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 4:23; 2 Timothy 4:7"> But at the age of fifty, they must retire from performing the work and no longer serve.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:32; 1 Timothy 4:15"> After that, they may assist their brothers in fulfilling their duties at the Tent of Meeting, but they themselves are not to do the work. This is how you are to assign responsibilities to the Levites.”</span>
</div>
</div>
