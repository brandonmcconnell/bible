<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:34; Isaiah 16:8–9; Numbers 32:34–38"> “Ataroth, Dibon, Jazer, Nimrah, Heshbon, Elealeh, Sebam, Nebo, and Beon,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 21:34; Deuteronomy 2:24–35; Numbers 21:24"> which the LORD conquered before the congregation of Israel, are suitable for livestock—and your servants have livestock.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ruth 2:10; Joshua 7:7; Jeremiah 31:2"> “If we have found favor in your sight,” they said, “let this land be given to your servants as a possession. Do not make us cross the Jordan.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:5; 2 Samuel 11:11; Philippians 2:4"> But Moses asked the Gadites and Reubenites, “Shall your brothers go to war while you sit here?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 13:27–4; Numbers 32:9; Deuteronomy 1:28"> Why are you discouraging the Israelites from crossing into the land that the LORD has given them?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:19–25; Numbers 14:2; Numbers 13:2–26"> This is what your fathers did when I sent them from Kadesh-barnea to inspect the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 13:23–10; Deuteronomy 1:24–28"> For when your fathers went up to the Valley of Eshcol and saw the land, they discouraged the Israelites from entering the land that the LORD had given them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; Psalm 95:11; Ezekiel 20:15"> So the anger of the LORD was kindled that day, and He swore an oath, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 14:28–30; Numbers 14:24; Numbers 26:2"> ‘Because they did not follow Me wholeheartedly, not one of the men twenty years of age or older who came out of Egypt will see the land that I swore to give Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob—</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:36; Numbers 14:30; Numbers 14:24"> not one except Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite and Joshua son of Nun—because they did follow the LORD wholeheartedly.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5; Numbers 14:33–35; Hebrews 3:16–19"> The anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and He made them wander in the wilderness forty years, until the whole generation who had done evil in His sight was gone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 11:48; Psalm 78:57; Matthew 23:31–33"> Now behold, you, a brood of sinners, have risen up in place of your fathers to further stoke the burning anger of the LORD against Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:11–12; 2 Chronicles 15:2; 2 Chronicles 7:19–22"> For if you turn away from following Him, He will once again leave this people in the wilderness, and you will be the cause of their destruction.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 33:17; Numbers 34:22"> Then the Gadites and Reubenites approached Moses and said, “We want to build sheepfolds here for our livestock and cities for our little ones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 4:12–13; Numbers 32:29–32; Deuteronomy 3:18–20"> But we will arm ourselves and be ready to go ahead of the Israelites until we have brought them into their place. Meanwhile, our little ones will remain in the fortified cities for protection from the inhabitants of the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 22:1–5"> We will not return to our homes until every Israelite has taken possession of his inheritance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 13:8; Joshua 12:1–6; Numbers 34:15"> Yet we will not have an inheritance with them across the Jordan and beyond, because our inheritance has come to us on the east side of the Jordan.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 4:12–13; Deuteronomy 3:18–20; Joshua 1:13–15"> Moses replied, “If you will do this—if you will arm yourselves before the LORD for battle,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and if every one of your armed men crosses the Jordan before the LORD, until He has driven His enemies out before Him,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 22:9; Joshua 22:4; Deuteronomy 3:20"> then when the land is subdued before the LORD, you may return and be free of obligation to the LORD and to Israel. And this land will belong to you as a possession before the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 59:12; Proverbs 13:21; Genesis 44:16"> But if you do not do this, you will certainly sin against the LORD—and be assured that your sin will find you out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 32:16; Numbers 30:2; Numbers 32:34–42"> Build cities for your little ones and folds for your flocks, but do what you have promised.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 1:13–14"> The Gadites and Reubenites said to Moses, “Your servants will do just as our lord commands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 1:14"> Our children, our wives, our livestock, and all our animals will remain here in the cities of Gilead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 4:12; Numbers 32:17; Numbers 12:11"> But your servants are equipped for war, and every man will cross over to the battle before the LORD, just as our lord says.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 1:13"> So Moses gave orders about them to Eleazar the priest, to Joshua son of Nun, and to the family leaders of the tribes of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And Moses said to them, “If the Gadites and Reubenites cross the Jordan with you, with every man armed for battle before the LORD, and the land is subdued before you, then you are to give them the land of Gilead as a possession.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 22:19"> But if they do not arm themselves and go across with you, then they must accept their possession among you in the land of Canaan.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> The Gadites and Reubenites replied, “As the LORD has spoken to your servants, so we will do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> We will cross over into the land of Canaan armed before the LORD, that we may have our inheritance on this side of the Jordan.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 12:6; Deuteronomy 29:8; Deuteronomy 3:12–17"> So Moses gave to the Gadites, to the Reubenites, and to the half-tribe of Manasseh son of Joseph the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites and the kingdom of Og king of Bashan—the land including its cities and the territory surrounding them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36; Numbers 32:3; Isaiah 17:2"> And the Gadites built up Dibon, Ataroth, Aroer,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 32:1; Numbers 32:3"> Atroth-shophan, Jazer, Jogbehah,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 32:3"> Beth-nimrah, and Beth-haran as fortified cities, and they built folds for their flocks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 21:27; Numbers 32:3; Isaiah 15:4"> The Reubenites built up Heshbon, Elealeh, Kiriathaim,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 46:1; Numbers 32:3; Joshua 23:7"> as well as Nebo and Baal-meon (whose names were changed), and Sibmah. And they renamed the cities they rebuilt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 50:23; Joshua 17:1; Numbers 26:29"> The descendants of Machir son of Manasseh went to Gilead, captured it, and drove out the Amorites who were there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Joshua 17:1; Joshua 13:29–31; Deuteronomy 3:13–15"> So Moses gave Gilead to the clan of Machir son of Manasseh, and they settled there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:14; Judges 10:4; Joshua 13:30"> Jair, a descendant of Manasseh, went and captured their villages and called them Havvoth-jair.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalm 49:11; 2 Samuel 18:18"> And Nobah went and captured Kenath and its villages and called it Nobah, after his own name.</span>
</div>
</div>
