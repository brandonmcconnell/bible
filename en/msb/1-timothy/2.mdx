<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:3; Matthew 6:9–10; James 5:16"> First of all, then, I urge that petitions, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgiving be offered for everyone—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9–13; Jeremiah 29:7; Ezra 6:10"> for kings and all those in authority—so that we may lead tranquil and quiet lives in all godliness and dignity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 1 Timothy 1:1; 1 Peter 2:20"> For this is good and pleasing in the sight of God our Savior,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:32; 2 Peter 3:9; Ezekiel 18:23"> who wants everyone to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:6; Galatians 3:20; Hebrews 9:15"> For there is one God, and there is one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 John 2:1–2"> who gave Himself as a ransom for all—the testimony that was given at just the right time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 2 Timothy 1:11; Romans 9:1"> For this reason I was appointed as a preacher, an apostle, and a faithful and true teacher of the Gentiles. I am telling the truth in Christ; I am not lying about anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 134:2; Psalm 63:4; Hebrews 10:22"> Therefore I want the men everywhere to pray, lifting up holy hands, without anger or dissension.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Titus 2:3–5; Psalm 149:4"> Likewise, I want the women to adorn themselves with respectable apparel, with modesty, and with self-control, not with braided hair or gold or pearls or expensive clothes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 3:3–5; Revelation 2:19"> but with good deeds, as is proper for women who profess to worship God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:34–35; Ephesians 5:22–24; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> A woman must learn in quietness and full submissiveness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:34"> I do not permit a woman to teach or to exercise authority over a man; she is to remain quiet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 2:18; 1 Corinthians 11:8–9; Genesis 1:27"> For Adam was formed first, and then Eve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:3; Genesis 3:6; Genesis 3:12–13"> And it was not Adam who was deceived, but the woman who was deceived and fell into transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 2:12; Genesis 3:15–16; 1 Peter 4:7"> Women, however, will be saved through childbearing, if they continue in faith, love, and holiness, with self-control.</span>
