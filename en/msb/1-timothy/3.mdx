<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 1:7; Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:2"> This is a trustworthy saying: If anyone aspires to be an overseer, he desires a noble task.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:6–9; 2 Timothy 2:24; Romans 12:13"> An overseer, then, must be above reproach, the husband of but one wife, temperate, self-controlled, respectable, hospitable, able to teach,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 1:7; Ephesians 5:18; Titus 3:2"> not dependent on wine, not violent, not greedy for money, but gentle, peaceable, and free of the love of money.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Titus 1:6; Genesis 18:19; 1 Timothy 3:12"> An overseer must manage his own household well and keep his children under control, with complete dignity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:13; Ephesians 5:24; Acts 20:28"> For if someone does not know how to manage his own household, how can he care for the church of God?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:4; Hebrews 5:12–13; 1 Peter 2:2"> He must not be a recent convert, or he may become conceited and fall under the same condemnation as the devil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Timothy 2:26; Colossians 4:5"> Furthermore, he must have a good reputation with outsiders, so that he will not fall into disgrace and into the snare of the devil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:1; 1 Timothy 5:23; Acts 6:3–6"> Deacons likewise must be dignified, not double-tongued or given to much wine or greedy for money.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19; 2 John 1:9–10; 1 Timothy 1:5"> They must hold to the mystery of the faith with a clear conscience.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:22; 1 Timothy 3:13; Titus 1:6–7"> Additionally, they must first be tested. Then, if they are above reproach, let them serve as deacons.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Titus 2:3; Titus 3:2; 1 Timothy 3:2"> In the same way, the women must be dignified, not slanderers, but temperate and faithful in all things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; 1 Timothy 3:4–5; Philippians 1:1"> A deacon must be the husband of but one wife, a good manager of his children and of his own household.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Hebrews 6:10; Acts 21:35"> For those who have served well as deacons acquire for themselves a high standing and great confidence in the faith that is in Christ Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:34; Philemon 1:22; 2 Corinthians 1:15–17"> Although I hope to come to you soon, I am writing you these things</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Ephesians 2:21–22; 1 Thessalonians 1:9"> in case I am delayed, so that you will know how each one must conduct himself in God’s household, which is the church of the living God, the pillar and foundation of the truth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:14; Hebrews 1:3; Mark 16:19"> By common confession, the mystery of godliness is great: God appeared in the flesh, was vindicated by the Spirit, was seen by angels, was proclaimed among the nations, was believed in throughout the world, was taken up in glory.</span>
</div>
</div>
