<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–4; Mark 13:34; Song of Solomon 8:11–12"> Then Jesus began to speak to them in parables: “A man planted a vineyard. He put a wall around it, dug a wine vat, and built a watchtower. Then he rented it out to some tenants and went away on a journey.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:1–8; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 44:4"> At harvest time, he sent a servant to the tenants to collect his share of the fruit of the vineyard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15; Nehemiah 9:26; Jeremiah 29:26"> But they seized the servant, beat him, and sent him away empty-handed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then he sent them another servant, and casting stones they struck him over the head and sent him away, treating him shamefully.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:6; Matthew 23:37; Nehemiah 9:30"> He sent still another, and this one they killed. He sent many others; some they beat and others they killed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 1:1–2; Matthew 3:17; John 1:34"> Finally, having his one beloved son, he sent him to them last. ‘They will respect my son,’ he said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Matthew 2:16; Acts 2:23"> But the tenants said to one another, ‘This is the heir. Come, let us kill him, and the inheritance will be ours.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 21:39; Luke 20:15; Matthew 21:33"> So they seized the son, killed him, and threw him out of the vineyard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 10:20–12; Matthew 21:43; Luke 19:27"> What then will the owner of the vineyard do? He will come and kill those tenants, and will give the vineyard to others.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 118:22–23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 28:16"> Have you never read this Scripture: ‘The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 118:23; Acts 3:12–16; Habakkuk 1:5"> This is from the Lord, and it is marvelous in our eyes’?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 22:22; John 7:44"> At this, the leaders sought to arrest Jesus, for they knew that He had spoken this parable against them. But fearing the crowd, they left Him and went away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Luke 11:54; Mark 3:6"> Later, they sent some of the Pharisees and Herodians to catch Jesus in His words.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 20:22; John 7:18"> “Teacher,” they said, “we know that You are honest and seek favor from no one. Indeed, You are impartial and teach the way of God in accordance with the truth. Is it lawful to pay taxes to Caesar or not? Should we pay them or not?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:2; Revelation 2:23; Mark 10:2"> But Jesus saw through their hypocrisy and said, “Why are you testing Me? Bring Me a denarius to inspect.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 20:24–26; Revelation 3:12; Matthew 22:19–22"> So they brought it, and He asked them, “Whose image is this? And whose inscription?” “Caesar’s,” they answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:7; 1 Peter 2:17; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> In response Jesus told them, “Give to Caesar what is Caesar’s, and to God what is God’s.” And they marveled at Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 22:23–33; Acts 4:1–2; Acts 23:6–9"> Then the Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection, came to Jesus and questioned Him:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Ruth 4:5; Ruth 1:11–13"> “Teacher, Moses wrote for us that if a man’s brother dies and leaves a wife but no children, the man is to marry his brother’s widow and raise up offspring for him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 22:25–28; Luke 20:29–33"> Now there were seven brothers. The first one married and died, leaving no children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then the second one married the widow, but he also died and left no children. And the third did likewise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> In this way, none of the seven who had her left any children. And last of all, the woman died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In the resurrection, then, whose wife will she be? For all seven were married to her.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 20:9; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Matthew 22:29"> Jesus answered them, “Aren’t you mistaken, because you do not know the Scriptures or the power of God?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:35–36; Matthew 22:30; 1 John 3:2"> When the dead rise, they will neither marry nor be given in marriage. Instead, they will be like the angels in heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Luke 20:37; Genesis 17:7–8"> But concerning the dead rising, have you not read about the burning bush in the Book of Moses, how God told him, ‘I am the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob’?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 14:9; Romans 4:17; Mark 12:24"> He is not the God of the dead, but the God of the living. Therefore you are badly mistaken!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–40; Luke 10:25–28; Luke 20:39"> Now one of the scribes had come up and heard their debate. Noticing how well Jesus had answered them, he asked Him, “Which commandment is the most important of all?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:4; Luke 10:27; Galatians 3:20"> Jesus replied, “This is the most important of all the commandments: ‘Hear, O Israel, the Lord our God, the Lord is One.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5"> Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind and with all your strength.’ This is the first commandment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 13:8–9; Matthew 22:39; Leviticus 19:18"> The second, like it, is this: ‘Love your neighbor as yourself.’ No other commandment is greater than these.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Deuteronomy 4:35; Isaiah 46:9"> “Right, Teacher,” the scribe replied. “You have stated correctly that God is One and there is no other but Him,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Micah 6:6–8; 1 Samuel 15:22"> and to love Him with all your heart and with all your understanding and with all your soul and with all your strength, and to love your neighbor as yourself. This is more important than all burnt offerings and sacrifices.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 20:40; Matthew 22:46"> When Jesus saw that the man had answered wisely, He said, “You are not far from the kingdom of God.” And no one dared to question Him any further.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Matthew 22:41–45; Matthew 26:55"> While Jesus was teaching in the temple courts, He asked, “How can the scribes say that the Christ is the Son of David?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalm 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Matthew 22:43–45"> Speaking by the Holy Spirit, David himself declared: ‘The Lord said to my Lord, “Sit at My right hand until I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet.”’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Matthew 11:25; Matthew 1:23"> Therefore David himself calls Him ‘Lord.’ So how can He be David’s son?” And the large crowd listened to Him with delight.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–7; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 11:43"> In His teaching Jesus also said to them, “Watch out for the scribes. They like to walk around in long robes, to receive greetings in the marketplaces,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="James 2:2–3"> and to have the chief seats in the synagogues and the places of honor at banquets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Luke 20:47; Matthew 23:33"> They defraud widows of their houses, and for a show make lengthy prayers. These men will receive greater condemnation.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 21:1–4; 2 Kings 12:9; John 8:20"> As Jesus was sitting opposite the treasury, He watched the crowd putting money into it. And many rich people put in large amounts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Then one poor widow came and put in two small copper coins, which amounted to a small fraction of a denarius.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Acts 11:29"> Jesus called His disciples to Him and said, “Truly I tell you, this poor widow has put more than all the others into the treasury.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 1 John 3:17; Ezra 2:68–69"> For they all contributed out of their surplus, but she out of her poverty has put in all she had to live on.”</span>
</div>
</div>
