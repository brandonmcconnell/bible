<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 15:1–20; Mark 3:22; Luke 5:17"> Then the Pharisees and some of the scribes who had come from Jerusalem gathered around Jesus.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Matthew 7:3–5; Romans 14:14"> And seeing some of His disciples eating with hands that were defiled (that is, unwashed), they found fault.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 1:14; Mark 7:13; Colossians 2:8"> Now in holding to the tradition of the elders, the Pharisees and all the Jews do not eat until they wash their hands ceremonially.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 9:10; Matthew 23:25; Luke 11:38–39"> And on returning from the market, they do not eat unless they wash. And there are many other traditions for them to observe, including the washing of cups, pitchers, kettles, and couches for dining.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:6; Acts 21:21; Matthew 15:2"> So the Pharisees and scribes questioned Jesus: “Why do Your disciples not walk according to the tradition of the elders? Instead, they eat with unwashed hands.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 15:7–9"> Jesus answered them, “Isaiah prophesied correctly about you hypocrites, as it is written: ‘These people honor Me with their lips, but their hearts are far from Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Matthew 15:9; James 1:26"> They worship Me in vain; they teach as doctrine the precepts of men.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Isaiah 1:12"> You have disregarded the commandment of God to keep the tradition of men—the washing of pitchers and cups, and many other similar things you do.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16–17; Galatians 2:21; Isaiah 29:13"> He went on to say, “You neatly set aside the command of God to maintain your own tradition.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 21:17; Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 5:16"> For Moses said, ‘Honor your father and your mother’ and ‘Anyone who curses his father or mother must be put to death.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 23:18; 1 Timothy 5:4–8; Matthew 15:5"> But you say that if a man says to his father or mother, ‘Whatever you would have received from me is Corban’ (that is, a gift devoted to God),</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> he is no longer permitted to do anything for his father or mother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 7:9; Titus 1:14; Matthew 5:17–20"> Thus you nullify the word of God by the tradition you have handed down. And you do so in many such matters.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:10; Isaiah 6:9; Proverbs 8:5"> Once again Jesus called the whole crowd to Him and said, “All of you, listen to Me and understand:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 4:23; Leviticus 11:42–47; Titus 1:15"> Nothing that enters a man from the outside can defile him; but the things that come out of a man, these are what defile him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> If anyone has ears to hear, let him hear.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Mark 9:28"> After Jesus had left the crowd and gone into the house, His disciples inquired about the parable.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 15:16–17; Hebrews 5:11; Isaiah 28:9–10"> “Are you still so dull?” He asked. “Do you not understand? Nothing that enters a man from the outside can defile him,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 15:17; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Colossians 2:21–22"> because it does not enter his heart, but it goes into the stomach and then is eliminated, thus purifying all foods.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 3:6; James 1:14–15; James 4:1"> He continued: “What comes out of a man, that is what defiles him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Romans 8:7–8; James 4:1–3"> For from within the hearts of men come evil thoughts, adultery, sexual immorality, murder, theft,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 20:15; Matthew 6:23; 1 Peter 2:15"> greed, wickedness, deceit, debauchery, envy, slander, arrogance, and foolishness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 3:17"> All these evils come from within, and these are what defile a man.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:21–28; Matthew 11:21; Ezekiel 28:2"> Jesus left that place and went to the region of Tyre and Sidon. Not wanting anyone to know He was there, He entered a house, but was unable to escape their notice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Mark 9:17–23; Acts 10:25–26"> For a woman whose little daughter had an unclean spirit heard about Jesus, and she came and fell at His feet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Isaiah 49:12; Galatians 3:28"> Now she was a Greek woman of Syrophoenician origin, and she kept asking Jesus to drive the demon out of her daughter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:23–28; Matthew 7:6; Acts 22:21"> “First let the children have their fill,” Jesus said. “For it is not right to take the children’s bread and toss it to the dogs.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Romans 3:29; Acts 11:17–18"> “Yes, Lord,” she replied, “for even the dogs under the table eat the children’s crumbs.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; Matthew 5:3; 1 John 3:8"> Then Jesus told her, “Because of this answer, you may go. The demon has left your daughter.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 3:8; John 4:50–52"> And she went home and found the demon was gone, and her daughter was lying on the bed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 5:20; Matthew 15:29–31; Matthew 4:25"> Then Jesus left the region of Tyre and Sidon and went toward the Sea of Galilee and into the region of the Decapolis.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:14; Mark 5:23; Matthew 9:32–33"> Some people brought to Him a man who was deaf and hardly able to speak, and they begged Jesus to place His hand on him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:23; John 9:6–7; Mark 5:40"> So Jesus took him aside privately, away from the crowd, and put His fingers into the man’s ears. Then He spit and touched the man’s tongue.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 11:41; Mark 5:41; Mark 6:41"> And looking up to heaven, He sighed deeply and said to him, “Ephphatha!” (which means, “Be opened!”).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5–6; Psalm 33:9; Mark 2:12"> Immediately the man’s ears were opened and his tongue was released, and he began to speak plainly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Mark 8:26; Mark 3:12"> Jesus ordered them not to tell anyone. But the more He ordered them, the more widely they proclaimed it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 6:51; Mark 5:42; Exodus 4:10–11"> The people were utterly astonished and said, “He has done all things well! He makes even the deaf hear and the mute speak!”</span>
</div>
