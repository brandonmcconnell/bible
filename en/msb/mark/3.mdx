<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:9–14; Luke 6:6–11; Mark 1:21"> Once again Jesus entered the synagogue, and a man with a withered hand was there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 37:32; Luke 14:1; Luke 6:7"> In order to accuse Jesus, they were watching to see if He would heal on the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 1:28–30; Isaiah 42:4; Luke 6:8"> Then Jesus said to the man with the withered hand, “Stand up among us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Matthew 12:10–12; Hosea 6:6"> And He asked them, “Which is lawful on the Sabbath: to do good or to do evil, to save life or to destroy it?” But they were silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:10; Ephesians 4:18; Romans 11:25"> Jesus looked around at them with anger and sorrow at their hardness of heart. Then He said to the man, “Stretch out your hand.” So he stretched it out, and it was restored, as sound as the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:16; Mark 12:13; Matthew 12:14"> At this, the Pharisees went out and began plotting with the Herodians how they might kill Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 4:25; Luke 6:17; Matthew 12:15"> So Jesus withdrew with His disciples to the sea, accompanied by a large crowd from Galilee, Judea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Isaiah 34:5; Matthew 11:21"> Jerusalem, Idumea, the region beyond the Jordan, and the vicinity of Tyre and Sidon. The large crowd came to Him when they heard what great things He was doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 5:30; John 6:15"> Jesus asked His disciples to have a boat ready for Him so that the crowd would not crush Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56; Matthew 4:23"> For He had healed so many that all who had diseases were pressing forward to touch Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 1:23–24; Luke 8:28"> And when the unclean spirits saw Him, they fell down before Him and cried out, “You are the Son of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:16; Mark 1:25; Mark 1:34"> But He warned them sternly not to make Him known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Luke 6:12–16; Matthew 10:1–4"> Then Jesus went up on the mountain and called for those He wanted, and they came to Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Luke 9:1–6; John 15:16"> He appointed twelve of them to accompany Him, to be sent out to preach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and to have authority to heal sicknesses and to drive out demons:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:42; Acts 1:13; Matthew 16:16–18"> Simon (whom He named Peter),</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Mark 14:33; Jeremiah 23:29"> James son of Zebedee and his brother John (whom He named Boanerges, meaning “Sons of Thunder”),</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14; Matthew 9:9"> Andrew, Philip, Bartholomew, Matthew, Thomas, James son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus, Simon the Zealot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 6:71; John 13:2; Acts 1:16–25"> and Judas Iscariot, who betrayed Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 6:31; John 4:31–34; Mark 9:28"> Then they went into a house, and once again a crowd gathered, so that they could not even eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 26:24; John 10:20; Mark 3:31"> When His family heard about this, they went out to take custody of Him, saying, “He is out of His mind.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:1; Matthew 10:25; Matthew 9:34"> And the scribes who had come down from Jerusalem were saying, “He is possessed by Beelzebul,” and, “By the prince of the demons He drives out demons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:17–23; Matthew 12:25–30; Matthew 13:34"> So Jesus called them together and began to speak to them in parables: “How can Satan drive out Satan?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Isaiah 19:2–3; 2 Samuel 20:6"> If a kingdom is divided against itself, it cannot stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 3:16; Psalm 133:1; Galatians 5:15"> If a house is divided against itself, it cannot stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And if Satan is divided and rises against himself, he cannot stand; his end has come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–26; Matthew 12:29; Luke 11:21–23"> No one can enter a strong man’s house to steal his possessions unless he first ties up the strong man. Then he can plunder his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; 1 John 5:16"> Truly I tell you, the sons of men will be forgiven all sins and blasphemies, as many as they utter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 12:31–32; Luke 12:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> But whoever blasphemes against the Holy Spirit will never be forgiven; he is subject to eternal judgment.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:20; Mark 3:22"> Jesus made this statement because they were saying, “He has an unclean spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:19–21; Matthew 12:46–50"> Then Jesus’ brothers and mother came and stood outside. They sent someone in to summon Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> and a crowd was sitting around Him. “Look,” He was told, “Your mother and brothers and sisters are outside, asking for You.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 3:21; Deuteronomy 33:9; Mark 6:3"> But Jesus replied, “Who is My mother, or who are My brothers?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 12:49–50; Luke 11:27–28; Romans 8:29"> Looking at those seated in a circle around Him, He said, “Here are My mother and My brothers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:17; Matthew 7:21; 1 John 3:22–23"> For whoever does the will of God is My brother and sister and mother.”</span>
</div>
