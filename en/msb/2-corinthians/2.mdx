<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:23; 2 Corinthians 12:20–21; 2 Corinthians 13:10"> So I made up my mind not to make another painful visit to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:8; 2 Corinthians 1:14; 1 Corinthians 12:26"> For if I grieve you, who is left to cheer me but those whom I have grieved?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 5:10; 2 Corinthians 8:22; Philemon 1:21"> I wrote to you as I did so that on my arrival I would not be grieved by those who ought to make me rejoice. I had confidence in all of you, that you would share my joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:12; Proverbs 27:5–6; Leviticus 19:17–18"> For through many tears I wrote you out of great distress and anguish of heart, not to grieve you but to let you know how much I love you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:12–13; Galatians 4:12; Proverbs 17:25"> Now if anyone has caused grief, he has not grieved me but all of you—to some degree, not to overstate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:4–5; 1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Corinthians 7:11"> The punishment imposed on him by the majority is sufficient for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Galatians 6:1–2; Colossians 3:13"> So instead, you ought to forgive and comfort him, so that he will not be overwhelmed by excessive sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:22–23; Galatians 6:1–2; Galatians 6:10"> Therefore I urge you to reaffirm your love for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:6; Philemon 1:21; Philippians 2:22"> My purpose in writing you was to see if you would stand the test and be obedient in everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 20:23; Matthew 18:18; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> If you forgive anyone, I also forgive him. And if I have forgiven anything, I have forgiven it in the presence of Christ for your sake,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Ephesians 6:11–12; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> in order that Satan should not outwit us. For we are not unaware of his schemes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 16:8; Acts 14:27; Colossians 4:3"> Now when I went to Troas to preach the gospel of Christ and a door stood open for me in the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:5–6; 2 Corinthians 12:18; Titus 1:4"> I had no peace in my spirit, because I did not find my brother Titus there. So I said goodbye to them and went on to Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Romans 8:37; 2 Corinthians 2:15–16"> But thanks be to God, who always leads us triumphantly as captives in Christ and through us spreads everywhere the fragrance of the knowledge of Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 1:18; Ezekiel 20:41"> For we are to God the sweet aroma of Christ among those who are being saved and those who are perishing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5–6; John 9:39; 1 Peter 2:7–8"> To the one we are an odor that brings death, to the other a fragrance that brings life. And who is qualified for such a task?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Corinthians 1:12; 2 Peter 2:1–3"> For we are not like others, who peddle the word of God for profit. On the contrary, in Christ we speak before God with sincerity, as men sent from God.</span>
