<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:2; 2 Chronicles 34:1–33; 2 Peter 1:19"> This is the word of the LORD that came to Zephaniah son of Cushi, the son of Gedaliah, the son of Amariah, the son of Hezekiah, in the days of Josiah son of Amon king of Judah:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 7:13; 2 Kings 22:16–17; Ezekiel 33:27–29"> “I will completely sweep away everything from the face of the earth,” declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Ezekiel 7:19; Jeremiah 12:4"> “I will sweep away man and beast; I will sweep away the birds of the air, and the fish of the sea, and the idols with their wicked worshipers. I will cut off mankind from the face of the earth,” declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Micah 5:13; 2 Kings 23:4–5"> “I will stretch out My hand against Judah and against all who dwell in Jerusalem. I will cut off from this place every remnant of Baal, the names of the idolatrous and pagan priests—</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:13; 1 Kings 11:33; 2 Kings 17:33"> those who bow on the rooftops to worship the host of heaven, those who bow down and swear by the LORD but also swear by Milcom,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; Jeremiah 2:13; Jeremiah 15:6"> and those who turn back from following the LORD, neither seeking the LORD nor inquiring of Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:20; Zechariah 2:13; Zephaniah 1:14"> Be silent in the presence of the Lord GOD, for the Day of the LORD is near. Indeed, the LORD has prepared a sacrifice; He has consecrated His guests.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 24:21; Isaiah 3:18–24; Jeremiah 22:24–30"> “On the Day of the LORD’s sacrifice I will punish the princes, the sons of the king, and all who are dressed in foreign apparel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:5; Amos 3:10; 1 Samuel 2:15–16"> On that day I will punish all who leap over the threshold, who fill the house of their master with violence and deceit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:14; Amos 8:3; 2 Kings 22:14"> On that day,” declares the LORD, “a cry will go up from the Fish Gate, a wail from the Second District, and a loud crashing from the hills.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 5:1; Jeremiah 25:34; Hosea 12:7–8"> Wail, O dwellers of the Hollow, for all your merchants will be silenced; all who weigh out silver will be cut off.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 6:1; Jeremiah 48:11; Ezekiel 8:12"> And at that time I will search Jerusalem with lamps and punish the men settled in complacency, who say to themselves, ‘The LORD will do nothing, either good or bad.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 5:11; Micah 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:30"> Their wealth will be plundered and their houses laid waste. They will build houses but not inhabit them, and plant vineyards but never drink their wine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Ezekiel 30:3; Zephaniah 1:7"> The great Day of the LORD is near—near and coming quickly. Listen, the Day of the LORD! Then the cry of the mighty will be bitter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 2:2; Isaiah 22:5; Revelation 6:17"> That day will be a day of wrath, a day of trouble and distress, a day of destruction and desolation, a day of darkness and gloom, a day of clouds and blackness,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Amos 3:6; Isaiah 32:14"> a day of horn blast and battle cry against the fortified cities, and against the high corner towers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Psalm 83:10; Jeremiah 2:17"> I will bring such distress on mankind that they will walk like the blind, because they have sinned against the LORD. Their blood will be poured out like dust and their flesh like dung.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:8; Ezekiel 7:19; Proverbs 11:4"> Neither their silver nor their gold will be able to deliver them on the Day of the LORD’s wrath. The whole earth will be consumed by the fire of His jealousy.” For indeed, He will make a sudden end of all who dwell on the earth.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
