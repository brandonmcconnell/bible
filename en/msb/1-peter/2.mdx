<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; 1 Corinthians 1:23; Romans 9:32–33"> and, “A stone of stumbling and a rock of offense.” They stumble because they disobey the word—and to this they were appointed.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; Hebrews 4:16; Hosea 1:9–10"> Once you were not a people, but now you are the people of God; once you had not received mercy, but now you have received mercy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; Galatians 5:24; 1 Peter 4:2"> Beloved, I urge you, as foreigners and exiles, to abstain from the desires of the flesh, which war against your soul.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; Matthew 5:16; Titus 2:7–8"> Conduct yourselves with such honor among the Gentiles that, though they slander you as evildoers, they may see your good deeds and glorify God on the day He visits us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 3:1; Romans 13:1–7; 1 Timothy 2:1–2"> Therefore submit yourselves for the Lord’s sake to every human institution, whether to the king as the supreme authority,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 13:3–4"> or to governors as those sent by him to punish those who do wrong and to praise those who do right.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17; Titus 2:8; Matthew 7:26"> For it is God’s will that by doing good you should silence the ignorance of foolish men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Romans 6:22; Romans 6:18"> Live in freedom, but do not use your freedom as a cover-up for evil; live as servants of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; Romans 12:10; Romans 13:7"> Treat everyone with high regard: Love the brotherhood of believers, fear God, honor the king.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Titus 2:9–10; Ephesians 6:5–7; Colossians 3:22–25"> Servants, submit yourselves to your masters with all respect, not only to those who are good and gentle, but even to those who are unreasonable.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:10–12; 1 Peter 3:14–17; 1 Peter 2:20"> For if anyone endures the pain of unjust suffering because he is conscious of God, this is to be commended.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:19; 1 Peter 3:17; Matthew 5:10–12"> How is it to your credit if you are beaten for doing wrong and you endure it? But if you suffer for doing good and you endure it, this is commendable before God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 16:24; John 13:15"> For to this you were called, because Christ also suffered for us, leaving you an example, that you should follow in His footsteps:</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 4:15"> “He committed no sin, and no deceit was found in His mouth.”</span>
