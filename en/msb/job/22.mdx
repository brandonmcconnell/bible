<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Eliphaz the Temanite replied:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:10; Galatians 6:7–8; Job 35:6–8"> “Can a man be of use to God? Can even a wise man benefit Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Psalm 147:10–11"> Does it delight the Almighty that you are righteous? Does He profit if your ways are blameless?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 14:3; Psalm 143:2; Revelation 3:19"> Is it for your reverence that He rebukes you and enters into judgment against you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 40:12; Job 15:5–6; Job 15:31–34"> Is not your wickedness great? Are not your iniquities endless?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:26; Ezekiel 18:12; Ezekiel 18:16"> For you needlessly demanded security from your brothers and deprived the naked of their clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 31:17; Matthew 25:42; Isaiah 58:7"> You gave no water to the weary and withheld food from the famished,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 9:15; Job 29:7–17; Job 13:8"> while the land belonged to a mighty man, and a man of honor lived on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 24:21; Job 24:3; Ezekiel 22:7"> You sent widows away empty-handed, and the strength of the fatherless was crushed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 13:21; Job 6:4"> Therefore snares surround you, and sudden peril terrifies you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 69:1–2; Lamentations 3:54; Jonah 2:3"> it is so dark you cannot see, and a flood of water covers you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 11:8; Isaiah 66:1; Psalm 8:3–4"> Is not God as high as the heavens? Look at the highest stars, how lofty they are!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Psalm 10:11; Ezekiel 9:9"> Yet you say: ‘What does God know? Does He judge through thick darkness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 97:2; Psalm 139:11–12; Psalm 33:14"> Thick clouds veil Him so He does not see us as He traverses the vault of heaven.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:5; Genesis 6:11–13"> Will you stay on the ancient path that wicked men have trod?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 15:32; Genesis 7:17–24; Psalm 55:23"> They were snatched away before their time, and their foundations were swept away by a flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 21:14–15; Romans 1:28; Psalm 4:6"> They said to God, ‘Depart from us. What can the Almighty do to us?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 21:16; Job 12:6; Acts 14:17"> But it was He who filled their houses with good things; so I stay far from the counsel of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 58:10; Psalm 107:42; Psalm 52:6"> The righteous see it and are glad; the innocent mock them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6–7; Job 1:16; Job 20:26"> ‘Surely our foes are destroyed, and fire has consumed their excess.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Isaiah 27:5; Philippians 4:7"> Reconcile now and be at peace with Him; thereby good will come to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 119:11; Job 23:12; Deuteronomy 4:1–2"> Receive instruction from His mouth, and lay up His words in your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 11:13–14; Zechariah 1:3; Job 8:5–6"> If you return to the Almighty, you will be restored. If you remove injustice from your tents</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:12; 1 Kings 9:28; Genesis 10:29"> and consign your gold to the dust and the gold of Ophir to the stones of the ravines,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 33:6; Psalm 16:5–6; Genesis 15:1"> then the Almighty will be your gold and the finest silver for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 37:4; Isaiah 58:14; Job 27:10"> Surely then you will delight in the Almighty and lift up your face to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Psalm 116:1; 1 John 5:14–15"> You will pray to Him, and He will hear you, and you will fulfill your vows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Psalm 90:17; Psalm 20:4"> Your decisions will be carried out, and light will shine on your ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 4:6; Job 5:19–27; Luke 1:52"> When men are brought low and you say, ‘Lift them up!’ then He will save the lowly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 42:7–8; James 5:15–16; Genesis 18:26–32"> He will deliver even one who is not innocent, rescuing him through the cleanness of your hands.”</span>
