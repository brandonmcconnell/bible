<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3–4; Job 4:3–4; Isaiah 41:5–7"> “How you have helped the powerless and saved the arm that is feeble!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 71:15–18; Job 32:11–13; Job 33:3"> How you have counseled the unwise and provided fully sound insight!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 22:23–24; Job 32:18; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> To whom have you uttered these words? And whose spirit spoke through you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 88:10; Genesis 6:4; Job 41:1–34"> The dead tremble—those beneath the waters and those who dwell in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 139:8; Proverbs 15:11; Hebrews 4:13"> Sheol is naked before God, and Abaddon has no covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 9:8; Psalm 104:2–5; Isaiah 40:22"> He stretches out the north over empty space; He hangs the earth upon nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Jeremiah 10:13; Isaiah 5:6"> He wraps up the waters in His clouds, yet the clouds do not burst under their own weight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 97:2; Job 22:14; Habakkuk 3:3–5"> He covers the face of the full moon, spreading over it His cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 8:29; Job 38:8–11; Jeremiah 5:22"> He has inscribed a horizon on the face of the waters at the boundary between light and darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; Psalm 18:7; Job 15:15"> The foundations of heaven quake, astounded at His rebuke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 51:15; Jeremiah 31:35; Psalm 89:9–10"> By His power He stirred the sea; by His understanding He shattered Rahab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 27:1; Psalm 104:30; Psalm 74:13–14"> By His breath the skies were cleared; His hand pierced the fleeing serpent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:26–29; Psalm 145:3; Romans 11:33"> Indeed, these are but the fringes of His ways; how faint is the whisper we hear of Him! Who then can understand the thunder of His power?”</span>
