<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 28:2–4; Jeremiah 5:22; Exodus 19:16"> “At this my heart also pounds and leaps from its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 37:5; Job 36:33; Psalm 104:7"> Listen closely to the thunder of His voice and the rumbling that comes from His mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 38:13; Matthew 24:27; Isaiah 11:12"> He unleashes His lightning beneath the whole sky and sends it to the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 29:3–9; Psalm 68:33; Exodus 15:7–8"> Then there comes a roaring sound; He thunders with His majestic voice. He does not restrain the lightning when His voice resounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 5:9; Romans 11:33; Isaiah 40:28"> God thunders wondrously with His voice; He does great things we cannot comprehend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 38:22; Job 36:27; Ezekiel 13:13"> For He says to the snow, ‘Fall on the earth,’ and to the gentle rain, ‘Pour out a mighty downpour.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 111:2; Psalm 109:27; Isaiah 26:11"> He seals up the hand of every man, so that all men may know His work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 104:22; Job 38:40"> The wild animals enter their lairs; they settle down in their dens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:9; Psalm 104:3; Isaiah 21:1"> The tempest comes from its chamber, and the cold from the driving north winds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 38:29–30; Psalm 147:16–18; Psalm 78:47"> By the breath of God the ice is formed and the watery expanses are frozen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 36:27–30; Matthew 17:5; Isaiah 18:4"> He loads the clouds with moisture; He scatters His lightning through them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 148:8; Joel 2:23; James 5:17–18"> They swirl about, whirling at His direction, accomplishing all that He commands over the face of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 18:45; Job 38:26–27; 1 Samuel 12:18–19"> Whether for punishment or for His land, He accomplishes this in His loving devotion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 111:2; Psalm 145:10–12; Exodus 14:13"> Listen to this, O Job; stand still and consider the wonders of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 36:30–32; Isaiah 40:26; Job 37:11"> Do you know how God dispatches the clouds or makes the lightning flash?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 36:4; Proverbs 3:19–20; Isaiah 40:22"> Do you understand how the clouds float, those wonders of Him who is perfect in knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 147:18; Job 6:17; Job 38:31"> You whose clothes get hot when the land lies hushed under the south wind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Psalm 104:2; Isaiah 40:22"> can you, like Him, spread out the skies, as strong as a mirror of bronze?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 38:2; Psalm 73:22; Job 42:3"> Teach us what we should say to Him; we cannot draw up our case because of our darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 11:7–8; Psalm 139:4; Job 6:3"> Should He be told that I want to speak? Would a man ask to be swallowed up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 36:32; Job 26:9; Job 38:25"> Now no one can gaze at the sun when it is bright in the skies after the wind has swept them clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 104:1; Jude 1:25; Hebrews 12:29"> Out of the north He comes in golden splendor; awesome majesty surrounds Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:16; Romans 11:33; Psalm 99:4"> The Almighty is beyond our reach; He is exalted in power! In His justice and great righteousness He does not oppress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; 1 Corinthians 1:26; Psalm 130:4"> Therefore, men fear Him, for He is not partial to the wise in heart.”</span>
