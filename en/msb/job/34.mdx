<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Elihu continued:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 1:5; 1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> “Hear my words, O wise men; give ear to me, O men of learning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 12:11; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Job 33:2"> For the ear tests words as the mouth tastes food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Judges 19:30; Romans 12:2"> Let us choose for ourselves what is right; let us learn together what is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 27:2; Job 33:9; Job 16:17"> For Job has declared, ‘I am righteous, yet God has deprived me of justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 6:4; Job 27:4–6; Job 16:13"> Would I lie about my case? My wound is incurable, though I am without transgression.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 15:16; Deuteronomy 29:19; Proverbs 1:22"> What man is like Job, who drinks up derision like water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 1:1; 1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 13:20"> He keeps company with evildoers and walks with wicked men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:14–16; Job 35:3; Malachi 3:14"> For he has said, ‘It profits a man nothing that he should delight in God.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:14; Deuteronomy 32:4; Job 8:3"> Therefore listen to me, O men of understanding. Far be it from God to do wrong, and from the Almighty to act unjustly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:6; Revelation 22:12"> For according to a man’s deeds He repays him; according to a man’s ways He brings consequences.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 8:3; Psalm 11:7; Psalm 145:17"> Indeed, it is true that God does not act wickedly, and the Almighty does not pervert justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:11; Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34–36"> Who gave Him charge over the earth? Who appointed Him over the whole world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 104:29; Job 7:17; Job 9:4"> If He were to set His heart to it and withdraw His Spirit and breath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Isaiah 57:16"> all flesh would perish together and mankind would return to the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:2–6; Job 12:3"> If you have understanding, hear this; listen to my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 40:8; Genesis 18:25; 2 Samuel 23:3"> Could one who hates justice govern? Will you condemn the just and mighty One,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Proverbs 17:26; Romans 13:7"> who says to kings, ‘You are worthless!’ and to nobles, ‘You are wicked,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Acts 10:34; James 2:5"> who is not partial to princes and does not favor rich over poor? For they are all the work of His hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 73:19; 1 Samuel 25:37–39; 1 Samuel 26:10"> They die in an instant, in the middle of the night. The people convulse and pass away; the mighty are removed without human hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Job 31:4; Jeremiah 16:17"> For His eyes are on the ways of a man, and He sees his every step.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Amos 9:2–3; Proverbs 10:29"> There is no darkness or deep shadow where the workers of iniquity can hide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Psalm 119:137; Romans 9:20"> For God need not examine a man further or have him approach for judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 113:7–8; Psalm 94:5; Daniel 2:21"> He shatters the mighty without inquiry and sets up others in their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 7:2; Job 34:20; Amos 8:7"> Therefore, He recognizes their deeds; He overthrows them in the night and they are crushed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 58:10–11; Deuteronomy 21:21; Isaiah 66:24"> He strikes them for their wickedness in full view,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalm 28:5; 1 Samuel 15:11; Isaiah 5:12"> because they turned aside from Him and had no regard for any of His ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="James 5:4; Exodus 3:7; Job 35:9"> They caused the cry of the poor to come before Him, and He heard the outcry of the afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 23:13; John 14:27; Psalm 27:9"> But when He remains silent, who can condemn Him? When He hides His face, who can see Him? Yet He watches over both man and nation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2–12; Job 34:21; 2 Kings 21:9"> that godless men should not rule or lay snares for the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Micah 7:9; Job 33:27; Job 42:6"> Suppose someone says to God, ‘I have endured my punishment; I will offend no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalm 32:8; Psalm 25:4–5; Psalm 139:23–24"> Teach me what I cannot see; if I have done wrong, I will not do it again.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 33:32; Isaiah 45:9; Psalm 135:6"> Should God repay you on your own terms when you have disavowed Him? You must choose, not I; so tell me what you know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:15; Job 34:4; Job 34:16"> Men of understanding will declare to me, and the wise men who hear me will say:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 35:16; Job 38:2; Job 15:2"> ‘Job speaks without knowledge; his words lack insight.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Job 21:7; Job 23:16; Job 12:6"> If only Job were tried to the utmost for answering like a wicked man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Job 27:23; Isaiah 1:19–20; 1 Samuel 15:23"> For he adds rebellion to his sin; he claps his hands among us and multiplies his words against God.”</span>
