<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 64:3; Job 18:2; Job 27:2"> “How long will you torment me and crush me with your words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 31:7; Nehemiah 4:12; Job 18:4–21"> Ten times now you have reproached me; you shamelessly mistreat me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Proverbs 9:12; Ezekiel 18:4"> Even if I have truly gone astray, my error concerns me alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 35:26; Psalm 38:16; Psalm 55:12"> If indeed you would exalt yourselves above me and use my disgrace against me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 27:2; Job 16:11–14"> then understand that it is God who has wronged me and drawn His net around me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:2–3; Lamentations 3:8; Job 31:35–36"> Though I cry out, ‘Violence!’ I get no response; though I call for help, there is no justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 3:7; Job 3:23; Proverbs 4:19"> He has blocked my way so I cannot pass; He has veiled my paths with darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 89:44; Job 29:7–14; Psalm 89:39"> He has stripped me of my honor and removed the crown from my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 24:20; Psalm 37:35–36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> He tears me down on every side until I am gone; He uproots my hope like a tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:24; Job 16:9; Job 33:10"> His anger burns against me, and He counts me among His enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 30:12; Job 16:13; Isaiah 51:23"> His troops advance together; they construct a ramp against me and encamp around my tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 88:8; Psalm 88:18; Psalm 69:8"> He has removed my brothers from me; my acquaintances have abandoned me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 38:11; Jeremiah 20:10; 2 Samuel 16:23"> My kinsmen have failed me, and my friends have forgotten me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 31:31–32; Psalm 123:3"> My guests and maidservants count me as a stranger; I am a foreigner in their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 1:15–17; Job 1:19"> I call for my servant, but he does not answer, though I implore him with my own mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 2:9–10; Job 17:1"> My breath is repulsive to my wife, and I am loathsome to my own family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 2:23; Job 30:12; Job 30:1"> Even little boys scorn me; when I appear, they deride me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 41:9; Psalm 109:4–5; Psalm 55:12–14"> All my best friends despise me, and those I love have turned against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 102:5; Lamentations 4:8; Job 33:19–22"> My skin and flesh cling to my bones; I have escaped by the skin of my teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:11; Psalm 38:2; Job 2:5"> Have pity on me, my friends, have pity, for the hand of God has struck me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 69:26; Job 31:31; Job 16:13–14"> Why do you persecute me as God does? Will you never get enough of my flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 30:8; Job 31:35; Isaiah 8:1"> I wish that my words were recorded and inscribed in a book,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:1; Exodus 28:21; Exodus 32:16"> by an iron stylus on lead, or chiseled in stone forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Isaiah 54:5; Psalm 78:35"> But I know that my Redeemer lives, and in the end He will stand upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; 1 John 3:2; Psalm 17:15"> Even after my skin has been destroyed, yet in my flesh I will see God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalm 73:26; Isaiah 26:19; Numbers 24:17"> I will see Him for myself; my eyes will behold Him, and not as a stranger. How my heart yearns within me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 19:22; Psalm 69:26; 1 Kings 14:13"> If you say, ‘Let us persecute him, since the root of the matter lies with him,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Psalm 58:10–11"> then you should fear the sword yourselves, because wrath brings punishment by the sword, so that you may know there is a judgment.”</span>
