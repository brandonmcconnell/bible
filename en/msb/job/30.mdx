<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 12:4; Psalm 35:15–16; Psalm 69:12"> “But now they mock me, men younger than I am, whose fathers I would have refused to put with my sheep dogs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> What use to me was the strength of their hands, since their vigor had left them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 24:5; Hebrews 11:38; Job 24:13–16"> Gaunt from poverty and hunger, they gnawed the dry land, and the desolate wasteland by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:16; 2 Kings 4:38–39; Amos 7:14"> They plucked mallow among the shrubs, and the roots of the broom tree were their food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Daniel 4:32–33; Psalm 109:10"> They were banished from among men, shouted at like thieves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1–2; Judges 6:2; Isaiah 2:19"> so that they lived on the slopes of the wadis, among the rocks and in holes in the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 6:5; Job 11:12; Genesis 16:12"> They cried out among the shrubs and huddled beneath the nettles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 16:22; Job 40:4; Isaiah 32:6"> A senseless and nameless brood, they were driven off the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Lamentations 3:63; Job 17:6; Lamentations 3:14"> And now they mock me in song; I have become a byword among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 50:6; Numbers 12:14"> They abhor me and keep far from me; they do not hesitate to spit in my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:21; Ruth 1:21; Job 12:18"> Because God has unstrung my bow and afflicted me, they have cast off restraint in my presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 19:12; Isaiah 3:5; Job 19:18"> The rabble arises at my right; they lay snares for my feet and build siege ramps against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:12; Zechariah 1:15; Psalm 69:26"> They tear up my path; they profit from my destruction, with no one to restrain them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Psalm 18:4; Psalm 69:14–15"> They advance as through a wide breach; through the ruins they keep rolling in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 13:3; Job 6:4; Isaiah 44:22"> Terrors are turned loose against me; they drive away my dignity as by the wind, and my prosperity has passed like a cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 42:4; Psalm 22:14; Isaiah 53:12"> And now my soul is poured out within me; days of affliction grip me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 7:4; Job 33:19–21; Isaiah 38:13"> Night pierces my bones, and my gnawing pains never rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:5; Job 19:20; Job 2:7"> With great force He grasps my garment; He seizes me by the collar of my tunic.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:31; Job 42:6; Genesis 18:27"> He throws me into the mud, and I have become like dust and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 19:7; Psalm 80:4–5; Psalm 22:2"> I cry out to You for help, but You do not answer; when I stand up, You merely look at me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 10:14–17; Job 16:9–14; Job 19:6–9"> You have ruthlessly turned on me; You oppose me with Your strong hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 27:21; Psalm 1:4; Hosea 4:19"> You snatch me up into the wind and drive me before it; You toss me about in the storm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5–7; Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> Yes, I know that You will bring me down to death, to the place appointed for all the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Matthew 27:39–44; Job 19:7"> Yet no one stretches out his hand against a ruined man when he cries for help in his distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalm 35:13–14; Jeremiah 13:17"> Have I not wept for those in trouble? Has my soul not grieved for the needy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:15; Job 3:25–26; Jeremiah 14:19"> But when I hoped for good, evil came; when I looked for light, darkness fell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Jeremiah 31:20; Jeremiah 4:19"> I am churning within and cannot rest; days of affliction confront me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalm 42:9; Psalm 38:6; Psalm 43:2"> I go about blackened, but not by the sun. I stand up in the assembly and cry for help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Micah 1:8; Psalm 102:6; Psalm 44:19"> I have become a brother of jackals, a companion of ostriches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 4:8; Psalm 102:3; Psalm 119:83"> My skin grows black and peels, and my bones burn with fever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 5:15; Isaiah 21:4; Isaiah 22:12"> My harp is tuned to mourning and my flute to the sound of weeping.</span>
