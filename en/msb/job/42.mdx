<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job replied to the LORD:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Mark 10:27; Genesis 18:14"> “I know that You can do all things and that no plan of Yours can be thwarted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 131:1; Psalm 139:6; Job 38:2"> You asked, ‘Who is this who conceals My counsel without knowledge?’ Surely I spoke of things I did not understand, things too wonderful for me to know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 40:7; Job 38:3; Genesis 18:30–32"> You said, ‘Listen now, and I will speak. I will question you, and you shall inform Me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; Romans 10:17; John 12:45"> My ears had heard of You, but now my eyes have seen You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; 1 Timothy 1:13–16; James 4:7–10"> Therefore I despise myself, and I repent in dust and ashes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 11:5–6; Job 32:2–3; Job 32:5"> After the LORD had spoken these words to Job, He said to Eliphaz the Temanite, “My wrath is kindled against you and your two friends. For you have not spoken about Me accurately, as My servant Job has.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:1; James 5:16; Psalm 103:10"> So now, take seven bulls and seven rams, go to My servant Job, and sacrifice a burnt offering for yourselves. Then My servant Job will pray for you, for I will accept his prayer and not deal with you according to your folly. For you have not spoken accurately about Me, as My servant Job has.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Acts 9:6; Acts 10:33"> So Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite went and did as the LORD had told them; and the LORD accepted Job’s prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:3; Job 8:6–7; Proverbs 22:4"> After Job had prayed for his friends, the LORD restored his prosperity and doubled his former possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 19:13–14; Proverbs 16:7"> All his brothers and sisters and prior acquaintances came and dined with him in his house. They consoled him and comforted him over all the adversity that the LORD had brought upon him. And each one gave him a piece of silver and a gold ring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 8:7; Ecclesiastes 7:8; Proverbs 10:22"> So the LORD blessed Job’s latter days more than his first. He owned 14,000 sheep, 6,000 camels, 1,000 yoke of oxen, and 1,000 female donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 1:2; Psalm 107:41; Psalm 127:3"> And he also had seven sons and three daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He named his first daughter Jemimah, his second Keziah, and his third Keren-happuch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 27:7; Psalm 144:12; Acts 7:20"> No women as beautiful as Job’s daughters could be found in all the land, and their father granted them an inheritance among their brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 17:6; Psalm 128:6; Genesis 50:23"> After this, Job lived 140 years and saw his children and their children to the fourth generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Genesis 15:15; Job 5:26"> And so Job died, old and full of years.</span>
