<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 4:17; Romans 3:20; Job 25:4"> “Yes, I know that it is so, but how can a mortal be righteous before God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 10:2; Job 40:2; Isaiah 57:15–16"> If one wished to contend with God, he could not answer Him one time out of a thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 36:5; Romans 11:33; Ephesians 3:20"> God is wise in heart and mighty in strength. Who has resisted Him and prospered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Luke 21:11; Habakkuk 3:10"> He moves mountains without their knowledge and overturns them in His anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 2:6; Hebrews 12:26; Psalm 75:3"> He shakes the earth from its place, so that its foundations tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Amos 8:9; Luke 21:25–26"> He commands the sun not to shine; He seals off the stars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Isaiah 40:22; Psalm 104:2–3"> He alone stretches out the heavens and treads on the waves of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Genesis 1:16; Job 38:31–41"> He is the Maker of the Bear and Orion, of the Pleiades and the constellations of the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 5:9; Psalm 72:18; Ephesians 3:20"> He does great things beyond searching out, and wonders without number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 23:8–9; Job 35:14; Psalm 77:19"> Were He to pass by me, I would not see Him; were He to move, I would not recognize Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 45:9; Job 11:10; Job 23:13"> If He takes away, who can stop Him? Who dares to ask Him, ‘What are You doing?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 89:10; Job 26:12; Isaiah 30:7"> God does not restrain His anger; the helpers of Rahab cower beneath Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:27; Job 9:3; Job 4:19"> How then can I answer Him or choose my arguments against Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 10:15; Job 8:5; Job 10:2"> For even if I were right, I could not answer. I could only beg my Judge for mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 29:24; Exodus 6:9; Psalm 66:18–20"> If I summoned Him and He answered me, I do not believe He would listen to my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 16:14; Job 2:3; Job 34:6"> For He would crush me with a tempest and multiply my wounds without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:19; Lamentations 3:15; Psalm 88:15–18"> He does not let me catch my breath, but overwhelms me with bitterness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:32–33; Job 9:4; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> If it is a matter of strength, He is indeed mighty! If it is a matter of justice, who can summon Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:5–6; Philippians 3:12–15; Psalm 143:2"> Even if I were righteous, my mouth would condemn me; if I were blameless, it would declare me guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; Psalm 139:23–24; Proverbs 28:26"> Though I am blameless, I have no concern for myself; I despise my own life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:1–3; Luke 13:2–4; Job 10:8"> It is all the same, and so I say, ‘He destroys both the blameless and the wicked.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 24:12; Job 1:13–19; Job 4:7"> When the scourge brings sudden death, He mocks the despair of the innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 10:3; Jeremiah 12:1–2; Psalm 73:3–7"> The earth is given into the hand of the wicked; He blindfolds its judges. If it is not He, then who is it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 7:6–7; James 4:14; Psalm 90:9–10"> My days are swifter than a runner; they flee without seeing good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Isaiah 18:2; Job 39:27–30"> They sweep by like boats of papyrus, like an eagle swooping down on its prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 7:13; Jeremiah 8:18; Job 7:11"> If I were to say, ‘I will forget my complaint and change my expression and smile,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalm 119:120; Psalm 88:15–16; Job 21:6"> I would still dread all my sufferings; I know that You will not acquit me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 21:27; Job 10:14–17"> Since I am already found guilty, why should I labor in vain?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; Psalm 26:6; 1 John 1:8"> If I should wash myself with snow and cleanse my hands with lye,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; Isaiah 59:6; Job 15:6"> then You would plunge me into the pit, and even my own clothes would despise me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 9:20; Ecclesiastes 6:10; Psalm 143:2"> For He is not a man like me, that I can answer Him, that we can take each other to court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:25; 1 John 2:1–2; Job 9:19"> Nor is there a mediator between us, to lay his hand upon us both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalm 39:10; Job 13:11; Job 33:7"> Let Him remove His rod from me, so that His terror will no longer frighten me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Then I would speak without fear of Him. But as it is, I am on my own.</span>
