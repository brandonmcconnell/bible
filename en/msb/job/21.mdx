<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Job 34:2; Job 15:11"> “Listen carefully to my words; let this be your consolation to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 16:10; Job 16:20"> Bear with me while I speak; then, after I have spoken, you may go on mocking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 102:1; Psalm 42:11; Psalm 142:2–3"> Is my complaint against a man? Then why should I not be impatient?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 29:9; Job 40:4; Judges 18:19"> Look at me and be appalled; put your hand over your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Psalm 119:120; Psalm 88:15"> When I remember, terror takes hold, and my body trembles in horror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 73:3–12; Job 12:6; Jeremiah 12:1–3"> Why do the wicked live on, growing old and increasing in power?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 17:6; Job 5:3–4; Job 20:10"> Their descendants are established around them, and their offspring before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:34; Psalm 73:5; Psalm 73:19"> Their homes are safe from fear; no rod of punishment from God is upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 23:26; Luke 16:19; Deuteronomy 7:13–14"> Their bulls breed without fail; their cows bear calves and do not miscarry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 107:41; Psalm 127:3–5"> They send forth their little ones like a flock; their children skip about,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 6:4–6; Isaiah 5:12; Genesis 4:21"> singing to the tambourine and lyre and making merry at the sound of the flute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 36:11; Luke 12:19–20; Psalm 73:4"> They spend their days in prosperity and go down to Sheol in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:17; Romans 8:7; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12"> Yet they say to God: ‘Leave us alone! For we have no desire to know Your ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Job 34:9; Malachi 1:13–14"> Who is the Almighty, that we should serve Him, and what would we gain if we pray to Him?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 1:1; Job 22:18; Genesis 49:6"> Still, their prosperity is not in their own hands, so I stay far from the counsel of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 18:5–6; Proverbs 20:20; Proverbs 13:9"> How often is the lamp of the wicked put out? Does disaster come upon them? Does God, in His anger, apportion destruction?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 1:4; Psalm 83:13; Job 13:25"> Are they like straw before the wind, like chaff swept away by a storm?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Ezekiel 18:19–20; Ezekiel 18:2"> It is said that God lays up one’s punishment for his children. Let God repay the man himself, so he will know it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 14:10; Isaiah 51:17; Psalm 75:8"> Let his eyes see his own destruction; let him drink for himself the wrath of the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 14:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Psalm 55:23"> For what does he care about his household after him, when the number of his months has run out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 11:34; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:13–14"> Can anyone teach knowledge to God, since He judges those on high?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 20:22–23; Luke 12:19–21; Psalm 73:4–5"> One man dies full of vigor, completely secure and at ease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:8; Psalm 17:10; Job 15:27"> His body is well nourished, and his bones are rich with marrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16–17; Job 7:11; Proverbs 14:10"> Yet another man dies in the bitterness of his soul, having never tasted prosperity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:2; Isaiah 14:11; Job 20:11"> But together they lie down in the dust, and worms cover them both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 5:22; Psalm 119:86; Psalm 59:4"> Behold, I know your thoughts full well, the schemes by which you would wrong me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 8:22; Job 20:7; Habakkuk 2:9–11"> For you say, ‘Where now is the nobleman’s house, and where are the tents in which the wicked dwell?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalm 129:8"> Have you never asked those who travel the roads? Do you not accept their reports?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; 2 Peter 2:9–17; 2 Peter 3:7"> Indeed, the evil man is spared from the day of calamity, delivered from the day of wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:11; Deuteronomy 7:10; Romans 12:19"> Who denounces his behavior to his face? Who repays him for what he has done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 16:22; Psalm 49:14; Ezekiel 32:21–32"> He is carried to the grave, and watch is kept over his tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Job 24:24; Job 30:23"> The clods of the valley are sweet to him; everyone follows behind him, and those before him are without number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 16:2; Job 32:3; Job 13:4"> So how can you comfort me with empty words? For your answers remain full of falsehood.”</span>
