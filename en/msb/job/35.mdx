<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Elihu went on to say:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:36–37; Job 19:6–7; Job 16:17"> “Do you think this is just? You say, ‘I am more righteous than God.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 34:9; Malachi 3:14; Job 9:30–31"> For you ask, ‘What does it profit me, and what benefit do I gain apart from sin?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Job 34:8"> I will reply to you and to your friends as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 22:12; Isaiah 55:9; Nahum 1:3"> Look to the heavens and see; gaze at the clouds high above you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:19; Proverbs 8:36; Proverbs 9:12"> If you sin, what do you accomplish against Him? If you multiply your transgressions, what do you do to Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 22:2–3; Psalm 16:2; Luke 17:10"> If you are righteous, what do you give Him, or what does He receive from your hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Job 42:8; Acts 27:24"> Your wickedness affects only a man like yourself, and your righteousness only a son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:23; Job 34:28; Exodus 3:7"> Men cry out under great oppression; they plead for relief from the arm of the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 42:8; Acts 16:25; Psalm 149:5"> But no one asks, ‘Where is God my Maker, who gives us songs in the night,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 94:12; Job 36:22; Job 32:8"> who teaches us more than the beasts of the earth and makes us wiser than the birds of the air?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; John 9:31; Psalm 123:3–4"> There they cry out, but He does not answer, because of the pride of evil men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; James 4:3"> Surely God does not listen to empty pleas, and the Almighty does not take note of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 9:11; Micah 7:7–9; Psalm 37:5–6"> How much less, then, when you say that you do not see Him, that your case is before Him and you must wait for Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 89:32; Job 9:14; Job 13:15"> and further, that in His anger He has not punished or taken much notice of folly!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 38:2; Job 33:8–12; Job 34:35–37"> So Job opens his mouth in vain and multiplies words without knowledge.”</span>
