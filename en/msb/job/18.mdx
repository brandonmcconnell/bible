<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 13:5–6; Proverbs 18:13; Job 3:17"> “How long until you end these speeches? Show some sense, and then we can talk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 73:22; Job 17:10; Job 17:4"> Why are we regarded as cattle, as stupid in your sight?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 16:9; Job 14:18; Job 13:14"> You who tear yourself in anger—should the earth be forsaken on your account, or the rocks be moved from their place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Proverbs 24:20; Proverbs 20:20"> Indeed, the lamp of the wicked is extinguished; the flame of his fire does not glow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Job 21:17; Psalm 18:28"> The light in his tent grows dark, and the lamp beside him goes out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Psalm 33:10; Psalm 18:36"> His vigorous stride is shortened, and his own schemes trip him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 9:15; Job 22:10; Psalm 35:8"> For his own feet lead him into a net, and he wanders into its mesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; Job 1:15; Job 1:17"> A trap seizes his heel; a snare grips him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Romans 11:9; Psalm 11:6"> A noose is hidden in the ground, and a trap lies in his path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 15:21; Jeremiah 6:25; Leviticus 26:36"> Terrors frighten him on every side and harass his every step.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Psalm 34:10"> His strength is depleted, and calamity is ready at his side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 2:6; Zechariah 14:12; Revelation 6:8"> It devours patches of his skin; the firstborn of death devours his limbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 10:28; Psalm 55:4; Job 11:20"> He is torn from the shelter of his tent and is marched off to the king of terrors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 11:6; Deuteronomy 29:23; Isaiah 34:9–10"> Fire resides in his tent; burning sulfur rains down on his dwelling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 9:16; Isaiah 5:24; Job 15:30"> The roots beneath him dry up, and the branches above him wither away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 34:16; Proverbs 10:7; Psalm 109:13"> The memory of him perishes from the earth, and he has no name in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 20:8; Job 10:22; Job 3:20"> He is driven from light into darkness and is chased from the inhabited world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:30; Isaiah 14:21–22; Job 20:26–28"> He has no offspring or posterity among his people, no survivor where he once lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 37:13; Jeremiah 50:27; Ezekiel 21:25"> Those in the west are appalled at his fate, while those in the east tremble in horror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Jeremiah 9:3; Jeremiah 10:25"> Surely such is the dwelling of the wicked and the place of one who does not know God.”</span>
