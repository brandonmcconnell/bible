<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 15:2; 1 Kings 19:11"> “How long will you go on saying such things? The words of your mouth are a blustering wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Genesis 18:25"> Does God pervert justice? Does the Almighty pervert what is right?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 1:5; Job 1:18–19; Job 5:4"> When your children sinned against Him, He gave them over to their rebellion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 4:7–10; Hebrews 3:7–8; Job 11:13"> But if you would earnestly seek God and ask the Almighty for mercy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 51:9; Isaiah 3:10; Proverbs 15:8"> if you are pure and upright, even now He will rouse Himself on your behalf and restore your righteous estate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 42:12–13; Matthew 13:31–32; Proverbs 19:20"> Though your beginnings were modest, your latter days will flourish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7; Deuteronomy 4:32; Job 15:18"> Please inquire of past generations and consider the discoveries of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; Psalm 144:4; Job 14:2"> For we were born yesterday and know nothing; our days on earth are but a shadow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 18:15; Matthew 12:35; Job 12:7–8"> Will they not teach you and tell you, and speak from their understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 2:3; Isaiah 19:5–7"> Does papyrus grow where there is no marsh? Do reeds flourish without water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:6; Psalm 129:6–7; Matthew 13:20"> While the shoots are still uncut, they dry up more quickly than grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 9:17; Job 11:20; Job 13:16"> Such is the destiny of all who forget God; so the hope of the godless will perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:5–6"> His confidence is fragile; his security is in a spider’s web.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 27:18; Luke 6:47–49; Matthew 7:24–27"> He leans on his web, but it gives way; he holds fast, but it does not endure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 80:11; Job 21:7–15; Job 5:3"> He is a well-watered plant in the sunshine, spreading its shoots over the garden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Mark 11:20; Jude 1:12"> His roots wrap around the rock heap; he looks for a home among the stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:10; Psalm 37:36; Job 7:8"> If he is uprooted from his place, it will disown him, saying, ‘I never saw you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 20:5; Psalm 113:7; 1 Samuel 2:8"> Surely this is the joy of his way; yet others will spring from the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 4:7; Psalm 37:37; Psalm 37:24"> Behold, God does not reject the blameless, nor will He strengthen the hand of evildoers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 126:2; Genesis 21:6; Psalm 126:6"> He will yet fill your mouth with laughter, and your lips with a shout of joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 132:18; Psalm 109:29; Psalm 35:26"> Your enemies will be clothed in shame, and the tent of the wicked will be no more.”</span>
