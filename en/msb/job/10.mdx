<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 7:11; 1 Kings 19:4; Numbers 11:15"> “I loathe my own life; I will express my complaint and speak in the bitterness of my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 139:23–24; Job 9:29; Psalm 143:2"> I will say to God: Do not condemn me! Let me know why You prosecute me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Psalm 138:8; Job 14:15"> Does it please You to oppress me, to reject the work of Your hands and favor the schemes of the wicked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Luke 16:15; Job 9:32"> Do You have eyes of flesh? Do You see as man sees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Peter 3:8; Psalm 90:2–4; Psalm 102:24–27"> Are Your days like those of a mortal, or Your years like those of a man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 14:16; Psalm 10:15; Zephaniah 1:12"> that You should seek my iniquity and search out my sin—</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Daniel 3:15; Psalm 139:1–2"> though You know that I am not guilty, and there is no deliverance from Your hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 119:73; Isaiah 43:7; Job 10:3"> Your hands shaped me and altogether formed me. Would You now turn and destroy me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Isaiah 64:8; Genesis 3:19"> Please remember that You molded me like clay. Would You now return me to dust?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 139:14–16"> Did You not pour me out like milk, and curdle me like cheese?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:4–8; Ephesians 4:16; Job 40:17–18"> You clothed me with skin and flesh, and knit me together with bones and sinews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:28; Job 33:4; Acts 17:25"> You have granted me life and loving devotion, and Your care has preserved my spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 23:13; Deuteronomy 32:39; Isaiah 45:15"> Yet You concealed these things in Your heart, and I know that this was in Your mind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 7:21; Psalm 130:3; Exodus 34:7"> If I sinned, You would take note, and would not acquit me of my iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 3:11; Job 9:15; Job 9:20–21"> If I am guilty, woe to me! And even if I am righteous, I cannot lift my head. I am full of shame and aware of my affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 38:13; Lamentations 3:10; Job 5:9"> Should I hold my head high, You would hunt me like a lion, and again display Your power against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ruth 1:21; Job 16:8; Job 16:11–16"> You produce new witnesses against me and multiply Your anger toward me. Hardships assault me in wave after wave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–18; Jeremiah 15:10; Job 11:20"> Why then did You bring me from the womb? Oh, that I had died, and no eye had seen me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 58:8"> If only I had never come to be, but had been carried from the womb to the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:1; Psalm 39:13; Psalm 103:15–16"> Are my days not few? Withdraw from me, that I may have a little comfort,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 3:5; 2 Samuel 12:23; Job 16:22"> before I go—never to return—to a land of darkness and gloom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 88:12; Jeremiah 2:6; Jeremiah 13:16"> to a land of utter darkness, of deep shadow and disorder, where even the light is like darkness.”</span>
