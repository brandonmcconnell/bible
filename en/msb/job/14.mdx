<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 5:7; Ecclesiastes 2:23; Job 25:4"> “Man, who is born of woman, is short of days and full of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; 1 Peter 1:24; Job 8:9"> Like a flower, he comes forth, then withers away; like a fleeting shadow, he does not endure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 144:3; Psalm 143:2; Psalm 8:4"> Do You open Your eyes to one like this? Will You bring him into judgment before You?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 3:6; Job 15:14; Ephesians 2:3"> Who can bring out clean from unclean? No one!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 39:4; Acts 17:26; Hebrews 9:27"> Since his days are determined and the number of his months is with You, and since You have set limits that he cannot exceed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 7:19; Psalm 39:13; Job 7:1–2"> look away from him and let him rest, so he can enjoy his day as a hired hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; Isaiah 11:1; Job 14:14"> For there is hope for a tree: If it is cut down, it will sprout again, and its tender shoots will not fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36; Isaiah 26:19; John 12:24"> If its roots grow old in the ground and its stump dies in the soil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:17–24; Ezekiel 17:3–10; Ezekiel 17:22–24"> at the scent of water it will bud and put forth twigs like a sapling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 7:7–10; Job 14:12"> But a man dies and is laid low; he breathes his last, and where is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 19:5; Jeremiah 15:18; Job 6:15–18"> As water disappears from the sea and a river becomes parched and dry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Job 3:13; Ecclesiastes 3:19–21"> so a man lies down and does not rise. Until the heavens are no more, he will not be awakened or roused from sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Isaiah 57:1–2; Isaiah 26:20–21"> If only You would hide me in Sheol and conceal me until Your anger has passed! If only You would appoint a time for me and then remember me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Job 7:1; Acts 26:8"> When a man dies, will he live again? All the days of my hard service I will wait, until my renewal comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 13:22; Psalm 138:8; Job 10:8"> You will call, and I will answer; You will desire the work of Your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 10:6; Job 31:4; Psalm 139:1–4"> For then You would count my steps, but would not keep track of my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:34; Hosea 13:12; Job 21:19"> My transgression would be sealed in a bag, and You would cover over my iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 6:14; Job 18:4; Jeremiah 4:24"> But as a mountain erodes and crumbles and a rock is dislodged from its place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 7:6; Job 27:8; Job 19:10"> as water wears away the stones and torrents wash away the soil, so You destroy a man’s hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:14; Job 2:12; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> You forever overpower him, and he passes on; You change his countenance and send him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Isaiah 63:16"> If his sons receive honor, he does not know it; if they are brought low, he is unaware.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 19:20; Luke 16:23–24"> He feels only the pain of his own body and mourns only for himself.”</span>
