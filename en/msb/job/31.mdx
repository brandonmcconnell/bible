<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 1:14–15; 1 John 2:16; Matthew 5:28–29"> “I have made a covenant with my eyes. How then could I gaze with desire at a virgin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 20:29; Job 27:13; Hebrews 13:4"> For what is the allotment of God from above, or the heritage from the Almighty on high?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 21:30; Job 34:22; Matthew 7:13"> Does not disaster come to the unjust and calamity to the workers of iniquity?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 34:21; Proverbs 5:21; 2 Chronicles 16:9"> Does He not see my ways and count my every step?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Psalm 44:20–21; Psalm 12:2"> If I have walked in falsehood or my foot has rushed to deceit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 7:8–9; Daniel 5:27; Isaiah 26:7"> let God weigh me with honest scales, that He may know my integrity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 15:39; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Matthew 5:29"> If my steps have turned from the path, if my heart has followed my eyes, or if impurity has stuck to my hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Leviticus 26:16; Deuteronomy 28:38"> then may another eat what I have sown, and may my crops be uprooted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 11:4; Ecclesiastes 7:26; Judges 16:5"> If my heart has been enticed by my neighbor’s wife, or I have lurked at his door,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:10; 2 Samuel 12:11; Isaiah 47:2"> then may my own wife grind grain for another, and may other men sleep with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Deuteronomy 22:22–24; Job 31:28"> For that would be a heinous crime, an iniquity to be judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 6:27; Jeremiah 5:7–9; Job 15:30"> For it is a fire that burns down to Abaddon; it would root out my entire harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:14–15; Exodus 21:26–27; Leviticus 25:46"> If I have rejected the cause of my manservant or maidservant when they made a complaint against me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 2:13; Psalm 9:12; Zechariah 2:13"> what will I do when God rises to judge? How will I answer when called to account?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 14:31; Malachi 2:10; Job 34:19"> Did not He who made me in the womb also make them? Did not the same One form us in the womb?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Deuteronomy 15:7–10; Deuteronomy 28:32"> If I have denied the desires of the poor or allowed the widow’s eyes to fail,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 29:12–16; John 13:29; Job 22:7"> if I have eaten my morsel alone, not sharing it with the fatherless—</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> though from my youth I reared him as would a father, and from my mother’s womb I guided the widow—</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 22:6; Luke 3:11; Job 24:4"> if I have seen one perish for lack of clothing, or a needy man without a cloak,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:13; Job 29:11"> if his heart has not blessed me for warming him with the fleece of my sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 22:9; Job 29:12; Micah 7:3"> if I have lifted up my hand against the fatherless because I saw that I had support in the gate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 137:6; Job 38:15; Job 31:40"> then may my arm fall from my shoulder and be torn from its socket.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 13:11; Psalm 119:120; 2 Corinthians 5:11"> For calamity from God terrifies me, and His splendor I cannot overpower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 49:6–7; Deuteronomy 8:12–14; Proverbs 11:28"> If I have put my trust in gold or called pure gold my security,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 62:10; Habakkuk 1:16; Isaiah 10:13–14"> if I have rejoiced in my great wealth because my hand had gained so much,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Deuteronomy 4:19; Deuteronomy 17:3"> if I have beheld the sun in its radiance or the moon moving in splendor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:6; Deuteronomy 11:16; Psalm 2:12"> so that my heart was secretly enticed and my hand threw a kiss from my mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–7; Job 31:11; Psalm 50:6"> this would also be an iniquity to be judged, for I would have denied God on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17–18; Proverbs 17:5; Psalm 35:13–14"> If I have rejoiced in my enemy’s ruin, or exulted when evil befell him—</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:14; Exodus 23:4–5"> I have not allowed my mouth to sin by asking for his life with a curse—</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 1:11–12; Micah 3:2–3"> if the men of my house have not said, ‘Who is there who has not had his fill?’—</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Genesis 19:2–3; Judges 19:20–21"> but no stranger had to lodge on the street, for my door has been open to the traveler—</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Hosea 6:7; Acts 5:8"> if I have covered my transgressions like Adam by hiding my guilt in my heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 29:25; Esther 4:11"> because I greatly feared the crowds and the contempt of the clans terrified me, so that I kept silent and would not go outside—</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 13:21–22; Job 40:4–5; Job 17:3"> (Oh, that I had one to hear me! Here is my signature. Let the Almighty answer me; let my accuser compose an indictment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 22:22; Job 29:14; Isaiah 62:3"> Surely I would carry it on my shoulder and wear it like a crown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Job 13:15; Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 4:15–16"> I would give account of all my steps; I would approach Him like a prince.)—</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:11; James 5:4; Psalm 65:13"> if my land cries out against me and its furrows weep together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; James 5:4; Isaiah 26:21"> if I have devoured its produce without payment or broken the spirit of its tenants,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; Malachi 1:3; Psalm 72:20"> then let briers grow instead of wheat and stinkweed instead of barley.” Thus conclude the words of Job.</span>
