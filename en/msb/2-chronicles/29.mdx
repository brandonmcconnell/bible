<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 18:1–3; 1 Chronicles 3:13; 2 Chronicles 26:5"> Hezekiah was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem twenty-nine years. His mother’s name was Abijah, the daughter of Zechariah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:2; 2 Chronicles 28:1"> And he did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, just as his father David had done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:24; 2 Chronicles 29:7; Matthew 6:33"> In the first month of the first year of his reign, Hezekiah opened and repaired the doors of the house of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:2; Nehemiah 3:29; 2 Chronicles 32:6"> Then he brought in the priests and Levites and gathered them in the square on the east side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:6; 1 Corinthians 3:16–17; 2 Chronicles 34:3–8"> “Listen to me, O Levites,” he said. “Consecrate yourselves now and consecrate the house of the LORD, the God of your fathers. Remove from the Holy Place every impurity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Jeremiah 2:27; Daniel 9:16"> For our fathers were unfaithful and did evil in the sight of the LORD our God. They abandoned Him, turned their faces away from the dwelling place of the LORD, and turned their backs on Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:24; Leviticus 24:2–8; 2 Chronicles 29:3"> They also shut the doors of the portico and extinguished the lamps. They did not burn incense or present burnt offerings in the Holy Place of the God of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 25:18; Jeremiah 29:18"> Therefore, the wrath of the LORD has fallen upon Judah and Jerusalem, and He has made them an object of terror, horror, and scorn, as you can see with your own eyes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:17; 2 Chronicles 28:5–8; Lamentations 5:7"> For behold, this is why our fathers have fallen by the sword, and our sons and daughters and wives are in captivity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; 2 Chronicles 23:16; Ezra 10:3"> Now it is in my heart to make a covenant with the LORD, the God of Israel, so that His fierce anger will turn away from us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Galatians 6:7–8; Numbers 8:6–14"> Now, my sons, do not be negligent, for the LORD has chosen you to stand before Him, to serve Him, to minister before Him, and to burn incense.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 3:19–20; 1 Chronicles 15:5–7; Numbers 4:2–20"> Then the Levites set to work: Mahath son of Amasai and Joel son of Azariah from the Kohathites; Kish son of Abdi and Azariah son of Jehallelel from the Merarites; Joah son of Zimmah and Eden son of Joah from the Gershonites;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:39; 1 Chronicles 15:8; 1 Chronicles 15:17"> Shimri and Jeuel from the Elizaphanites; Zechariah and Mattaniah from the Asaphites;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:33; 1 Chronicles 25:1; 1 Chronicles 25:6"> Jehiel and Shimei from the Hemanites; and Shemaiah and Uzziel from the Jeduthunites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:12; 1 Chronicles 23:28; 2 Chronicles 29:5"> When they had assembled their brothers and consecrated themselves, they went in to cleanse the house of the LORD, according to the command of the king by the words of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:16; Matthew 21:12–13; Matthew 23:27"> So the priests went inside the house of the LORD to cleanse it, and they brought out to the courtyard all the unclean things that they found in the temple of the LORD. Then the Levites took these things and carried them out to the Kidron Valley.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:2–8; 2 Chronicles 3:4; 1 Kings 6:3"> They began the consecration on the first day of the first month, and on the eighth day of the month they reached the portico of the LORD. For eight more days they consecrated the house of the LORD itself, finishing on the sixteenth day of the first month.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:1; 2 Chronicles 4:7–8; 2 Chronicles 13:11"> Then they went in to King Hezekiah and reported, “We have cleansed the entire house of the LORD, the altar of burnt offering with all its utensils, and the table of the showbread with all its utensils.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:24"> Moreover, we have prepared and consecrated all the articles that King Ahaz in his unfaithfulness cast aside during his reign. They are now in front of the altar of the LORD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 6:12; Jeremiah 25:4; Exodus 24:4"> Early the next morning King Hezekiah gathered the city officials and went up to the house of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 4:3–14; Job 42:8; Ezekiel 45:23"> They brought seven bulls, seven rams, seven lambs, and seven male goats as a sin offering for the kingdom, for the sanctuary, and for Judah. And the king commanded the priests, the descendants of Aaron, to offer them on the altar of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:18; Leviticus 8:19; Leviticus 8:24"> So they slaughtered the bulls, and the priests took the blood and splattered it on the altar. They slaughtered the rams and splattered the blood on the altar. And they slaughtered the lambs and splattered the blood on the altar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 4:15; Leviticus 4:24; Leviticus 1:4"> Then they brought the goats for the sin offering before the king and the assembly, who laid their hands on them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 14:20; Ezekiel 45:15; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> And the priests slaughtered the goats and put their blood on the altar for a sin offering, to make atonement for all Israel, because the king had ordered the burnt offering and the sin offering for all Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 23:5; 2 Samuel 24:11"> Hezekiah stationed the Levites in the house of the LORD with cymbals, harps, and lyres according to the command of David, of Gad the king’s seer, and of Nathan the prophet. For the command had come from the LORD through His prophets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:5; 1 Chronicles 15:24; Amos 6:5"> The Levites stood with the instruments of David, and the priests with the trumpets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:18; Psalm 137:3–4; 2 Chronicles 20:21"> And Hezekiah ordered that the burnt offering be sacrificed on the altar. When the burnt offering began, the song of the LORD and the trumpets began as well, accompanied by the instruments of David king of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 5:8–14; Psalm 89:15; Psalm 68:24–26"> The whole assembly was worshiping, the singers were singing, and the trumpeters were playing. All this continued until the burnt offering was completed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:18; 1 Chronicles 29:20; Philippians 2:10–11"> When the offerings were completed, the king and all those present with him bowed down and worshiped.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 4:4; Psalm 95:1–2; 1 Chronicles 16:7–36"> Then King Hezekiah and his officials ordered the Levites to sing praises to the LORD in the words of David and of Asaph the seer. So they sang praises with gladness and bowed down and worshiped.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:9; Exodus 35:22; Leviticus 7:12"> Then Hezekiah said, “Now that you have consecrated yourselves to the LORD, come near and bring sacrifices and thank offerings to the house of the LORD.” So the assembly brought sacrifices and thank offerings, and all whose hearts were willing brought burnt offerings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezra 6:17; 1 Kings 3:4; 1 Chronicles 29:21"> The number of burnt offerings the assembly brought was seventy bulls, a hundred rams, and two hundred lambs; all these were for a burnt offering to the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And the consecrated offerings were six hundred bulls and three thousand sheep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:11; 2 Chronicles 30:3; Psalm 94:15"> However, since there were not enough priests to skin all the burnt offerings, their Levite brothers helped them until the work was finished and until the priests had consecrated themselves. For the Levites had been more diligent in consecrating themselves than the priests had been.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 15:5–10; Exodus 29:13; 1 Chronicles 16:37–42"> Furthermore, the burnt offerings were abundant, along with the fat of the peace offerings and the drink offerings for the burnt offerings. So the service of the house of the LORD was established.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 2:41; 1 Thessalonians 3:8–9; 1 Chronicles 29:9"> Then Hezekiah and all the people rejoiced at what God had prepared for the people, because everything had been accomplished so quickly.</span>
</div>
</div>
