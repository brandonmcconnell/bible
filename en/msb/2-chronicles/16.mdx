<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:9; 2 Chronicles 15:5; 1 Kings 12:27"> In the thirty-sixth year of Asa’s reign, Baasha king of Israel went up against Judah and fortified Ramah to prevent anyone from leaving or entering the territory of Asa king of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 12:18; 2 Chronicles 28:21; 2 Kings 18:15"> So Asa withdrew the silver and gold from the treasuries of the house of the LORD and the royal palace, and he sent it with this message to Ben-hadad king of Aram, who was ruling in Damascus:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 15:4; 2 Chronicles 18:3; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> “Let there be a treaty between me and you as there was between my father and your father. See, I have sent you silver and gold. Now go and break your treaty with Baasha king of Israel, so that he will withdraw from me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 2 Chronicles 17:12; 1 Kings 15:20"> And Ben-hadad listened to King Asa and sent the commanders of his armies against the cities of Israel, conquering Ijon, Dan, Abel-maim, and all the store cities of Naphtali.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> When Baasha learned of this, he stopped fortifying Ramah and abandoned his work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:17; Joshua 15:38; Isaiah 10:29"> Then King Asa brought all the men of Judah, and they carried away the stones of Ramah and the timbers Baasha had used for building. And with these materials he built up Geba and Mizpah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:2; 1 Kings 16:1; Psalm 146:3–6"> At that time Hanani the seer came to King Asa of Judah and told him, “Because you have relied on the king of Aram and not on the LORD your God, the army of the king of Aram has escaped from your hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:3; 2 Chronicles 13:16; 2 Chronicles 16:7"> Were not the Cushites and Libyans a vast army with many chariots and horsemen? Yet because you relied on the LORD, He delivered them into your hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Proverbs 15:3; Jeremiah 16:17"> For the eyes of the LORD roam to and fro over all the earth, to show Himself strong on behalf of those whose hearts are fully devoted to Him. You have acted foolishly in this matter. From now on, therefore, you will be at war.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:26; Acts 16:23–24; 2 Samuel 12:13"> Asa was angry with the seer and became so enraged over this matter that he put the man in prison. And at the same time Asa oppressed some of the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:15; 2 Chronicles 9:29; 2 Chronicles 35:27"> Now the acts of Asa, from beginning to end, are indeed written in the Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; 2 Chronicles 28:22; 1 Chronicles 10:14"> In the thirty-ninth year of his reign, Asa became diseased in his feet, and his disease became increasingly severe. Yet even in his illness he did not seek the LORD, but only the physicians.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 15:24"> So in the forty-first year of his reign, Asa died and rested with his fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 50:2; 2 Chronicles 21:19; Jeremiah 34:5"> And he was buried in the tomb that he had cut out for himself in the City of David. They laid him on a bier that was full of spices and various blended perfumes; then they made a great fire in his honor.</span>
</div>
</div>
