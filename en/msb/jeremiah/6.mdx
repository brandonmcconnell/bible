<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:14; Judges 1:21; Joshua 18:21–28"> “Run for cover, O sons of Benjamin; flee from Jerusalem! Sound the ram’s horn in Tekoa; send up a signal over Beth-haccherem, for disaster looms from the north, even great destruction.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:31; Isaiah 1:8; Isaiah 3:16–17"> Though she is beautiful and delicate, I will destroy the Daughter of Zion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 19:43; Jeremiah 39:1–3; 2 Kings 24:10–12"> Shepherds and their flocks will come against her; they will pitch their tents all around her, each tending his own portion:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:8; Zephaniah 2:4; Joel 3:9"> ‘Prepare for battle against her; rise up, let us attack at noon. Woe to us, for the daylight is fading; the evening shadows grow long.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:13; Isaiah 32:14; 2 Chronicles 36:19"> Rise up, let us attack by night and destroy her fortresses!’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:19–20; Jeremiah 32:24"> For this is what the LORD of Hosts says: “Cut down the trees and raise a siege ramp against Jerusalem. This city must be punished; there is nothing but oppression in her midst.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:8; Ezekiel 7:23; Ezekiel 7:11"> As a well gushes its water, so she pours out her evil. Violence and destruction resound in her; sickness and wounds are ever before Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:18; Hosea 9:12; Jeremiah 17:23"> Be forewarned, O Jerusalem, or I will turn away from you; I will make you a desolation, a land without inhabitant.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:9; Jeremiah 16:16; Obadiah 1:5–6"> This is what the LORD of Hosts says: “Glean the remnant of Israel as thoroughly as a vine. Pass your hand once more like a grape gatherer over the branches.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Jeremiah 7:26; John 7:7"> To whom can I give this warning? Who will listen to me? Look, their ears are closed, so they cannot hear. See, the word of the LORD has become offensive to them; they find no pleasure in it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:21; Jeremiah 20:9; Micah 3:8"> But I am full of the LORD’s wrath; I am tired of holding it back. “Pour it out on the children in the street, and on the young men gathered together. For both husband and wife will be captured, the old and the very old alike.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:10; Isaiah 5:25; Deuteronomy 28:39–43"> Their houses will be turned over to others, their fields and wives as well, for I will stretch out My hand against the inhabitants of the land,” declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:11; Jeremiah 8:10; Micah 3:11"> “For from the least of them to the greatest, all are greedy for gain; from prophet to priest, all practice deceit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:10; Jeremiah 23:17; Jeremiah 4:10"> They dress the wound of My people with very little care, saying, ‘Peace, peace,’ when there is no peace at all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:12; Matthew 15:14; Jeremiah 3:3"> Are they ashamed of the abomination they have committed? No, they have no shame at all; they do not even know how to blush. So they will fall among the fallen; when I punish them, they will collapse,” says the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 30:21; Deuteronomy 32:7; Jeremiah 18:15"> This is what the LORD says: “Stand at the crossroads and look. Ask for the ancient paths: ‘Where is the good way?’ Then walk in it, and you will find rest for your souls. But they said, ‘We will not walk in it!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:4; Isaiah 58:1; Zechariah 7:11"> I appointed watchmen over you and said, ‘Listen for the sound of the ram’s horn.’ But they answered, ‘We will not listen!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:10; Jeremiah 31:10; Micah 6:5"> Therefore hear, O nations, and learn, O congregations, what will happen to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Jeremiah 8:9; Jeremiah 17:10"> Hear, O earth! I am bringing disaster on this people, the fruit of their own schemes, because they have paid no attention to My word and have rejected My instruction.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11; Amos 5:21–22; Isaiah 60:6"> What use to Me is frankincense from Sheba or sweet cane from a distant land? Your burnt offerings are not acceptable; your sacrifices do not please Me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; Ezekiel 3:20; Jeremiah 13:16"> Therefore this is what the LORD says: “I will lay stumbling blocks before this people; fathers and sons alike will be staggered; friends and neighbors will perish.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:41–43; Jeremiah 10:22; Jeremiah 6:1"> This is what the LORD says: “Behold, an army is coming from the land of the north; a great nation is stirred up from the ends of the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 13:18; Jeremiah 50:42; Luke 21:25–26"> They grasp the bow and spear; they are cruel and merciless. Their voice roars like the sea, and they ride upon horses, lined up like men in formation against you, O Daughter of Zion.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 50:43; Jeremiah 49:24"> We have heard the report; our hands hang limp. Anguish has gripped us, pain like that of a woman in labor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:29; Jeremiah 20:10; Psalm 31:13"> Do not go out to the fields; do not walk the road. For the enemy has a sword; terror is on every side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:8; Zechariah 12:10; Isaiah 32:11"> O daughter of my people, dress yourselves in sackcloth and roll in ashes. Mourn with bitter wailing, as you would for an only son, for suddenly the destroyer will come upon us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18; Jeremiah 15:20; Ezekiel 3:8–10"> “I have appointed you to examine My people like ore, so you may know and try their ways.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:4; Jeremiah 5:23; Jeremiah 18:18"> All are hardened rebels, walking around as slanderers. They are bronze and iron; all of them are corrupt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:13; Jeremiah 9:7; 1 Peter 4:12"> The bellows blow fiercely, blasting away the lead with fire. The refining proceeds in vain, for the wicked are not purged.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hosea 9:17; Psalm 119:119; Isaiah 1:22"> They are called rejected silver, because the LORD has rejected them.”</span>
</div>
</div>
