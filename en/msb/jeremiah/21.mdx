<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:25; Jeremiah 37:3; Jeremiah 52:24"> This is the word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD when King Zedekiah sent to him Pashhur son of Malchijah and the priest Zephaniah son of Maaseiah. They said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:3; Jeremiah 42:4–6; Jeremiah 37:7"> “Please inquire of the LORD on our behalf, since Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon is waging war against us. Perhaps the LORD will perform for us something like all His past wonders, so that Nebuchadnezzar will withdraw from us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> But Jeremiah answered, “You are to tell Zedekiah that</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:5; Jeremiah 37:8–10; Isaiah 5:5"> this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: ‘I will turn against you the weapons of war in your hands, with which you are fighting the king of Babylon and the Chaldeans who besiege you outside the wall, and I will assemble their forces in the center of this city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; Exodus 6:6; Isaiah 5:25"> And I Myself will fight against you with an outstretched hand and a mighty arm, with anger, fury, and great wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Ezekiel 14:13; Jeremiah 12:3–4"> I will strike down the residents of this city, both man and beast. They will die in a terrible plague.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:17; 2 Kings 25:5–7; Habakkuk 1:6–10"> ‘After that,’ declares the LORD, ‘I will hand over Zedekiah king of Judah, his officers, and the people in this city who survive the plague and sword and famine, to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and to their enemies who seek their lives. He will put them to the sword; he will not spare them or show pity or compassion.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:19; Deuteronomy 30:15; Deuteronomy 11:26"> Furthermore, you are to tell this people that this is what the LORD says: ‘Behold, I set before you the way of life and the way of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:2; Jeremiah 45:5; Jeremiah 39:18"> Whoever stays in this city will die by sword and famine and plague, but whoever goes out and surrenders to the Chaldeans who besiege you will live; he will retain his life like a spoil of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:11; Amos 9:4; Jeremiah 44:27"> For I have set My face against this city to bring disaster and not good, declares the LORD. It will be delivered into the hand of the king of Babylon, who will destroy it with fire.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:20; Micah 3:1; Jeremiah 13:18"> Moreover, tell the house of the king of Judah to hear the word of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Proverbs 31:8–9; Jeremiah 4:4"> O house of David, this is what the LORD says: ‘Administer justice every morning, and rescue the victim of robbery from the hand of his oppressor, or My wrath will go forth like fire and burn with no one to extinguish it because of their evil deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:8; Lamentations 4:12; Obadiah 1:3–4"> Behold, I am against you who dwell above the valley, atop the rocky plateau—declares the LORD—you who say, “Who can come against us? Who can enter our dwellings?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10–11; Jeremiah 32:19; Proverbs 1:31"> I will punish you as your deeds deserve, declares the LORD. I will kindle a fire in your forest that will consume everything around you.’”</span>
