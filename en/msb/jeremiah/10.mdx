<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Jeremiah 42:15; Amos 7:16"> Hear the word that the LORD speaks to you, O house of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Leviticus 18:3; Luke 21:25–28"> This is what the LORD says: “Do not learn the ways of the nations or be terrified by the signs in the heavens, though the nations themselves are terrified by them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 44:9–20; Leviticus 18:30; 1 Peter 1:18"> For the customs of the peoples are worthless; they cut down a tree from the forest; it is shaped with a chisel by the hands of a craftsman.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 46:7; Isaiah 40:19–20; Psalm 115:4"> They adorn it with silver and gold and fasten it with hammer and nails, so that it will not totter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 46:7; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Isaiah 41:23–24"> Like scarecrows in a cucumber patch, their idols cannot speak. They must be carried because they cannot walk. Do not fear them, for they can do no harm, and neither can they do any good.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 96:4; Exodus 15:11; Psalm 48:1"> There is none like You, O LORD. You are great, and Your name is mighty in power.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 15:4; Psalm 22:28; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> Who would not fear You, O King of nations? This is Your due. For among all the wise men of the nations, and in all their kingdoms, there is none like You.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 10:2; Jeremiah 10:14; Psalm 115:8"> But they are altogether senseless and foolish, instructed by worthless idols made of wood!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 115:4; Daniel 10:5; 1 Kings 10:22"> Hammered silver is brought from Tarshish, and gold from Uphaz—the work of a craftsman from the hands of a goldsmith. Their clothes are blue and purple, all fashioned by skilled workers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Isaiah 57:15; Nahum 1:6"> But the LORD is the true God; He is the living God and eternal King. The earth quakes at His wrath, and the nations cannot endure His indignation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 96:5; Isaiah 2:18; Lamentations 3:66"> Thus you are to tell them: “These gods, who have made neither the heavens nor the earth, will perish from this earth and from under these heavens.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; Job 9:8; Proverbs 3:19"> The LORD made the earth by His power; He established the world by His wisdom and stretched out the heavens by His understanding.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 135:7; Job 38:22; Job 38:34–35"> When He thunders, the waters in the heavens roar; He causes the clouds to rise from the ends of the earth. He generates the lightning with the rain and brings forth the wind from His storehouses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 135:16–18; Psalm 115:4–8; Isaiah 46:7–8"> Every man is senseless and devoid of knowledge; every goldsmith is put to shame by his idols. For his molten images are a fraud, and there is no breath in them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:22; Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 51:18"> They are worthless, a work to be mocked. In the time of their punishment they will perish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:9; Jeremiah 32:18; Jeremiah 31:35"> The Portion of Jacob is not like these, for He is the Maker of all things, and Israel is the tribe of His inheritance—the LORD of Hosts is His name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:3–12; Jeremiah 6:1; Matthew 24:15"> Gather up your belongings from this land, you who live under siege.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:29; Zechariah 1:6; Deuteronomy 28:63–64"> For this is what the LORD says: “Behold, at this time I will sling out the inhabitants of the land and bring distress upon them so that they may be captured.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 7:9; Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 14:17"> Woe to me because of my brokenness; my wound is grievous! But I said, “This is truly my sickness, and I must bear it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; Jeremiah 31:15; Isaiah 54:2"> My tent is destroyed, and all its ropes are snapped. My sons have departed from me and are no more. I have no one left to pitch my tent or set up my curtains.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2–10; Jeremiah 12:10; Jeremiah 23:1–2"> For the shepherds have become senseless; they do not seek the LORD. Therefore they have not prospered, and all their flock is scattered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Habakkuk 1:6–9; Jeremiah 6:22"> Listen! The sound of a report is coming—a great commotion from the land to the north. It will make the cities of Judah a desolation, a haunt for jackals.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 20:24; Proverbs 16:1; Psalm 37:23"> I know, O LORD, that a man’s way is not his own; no one who walks directs his own steps.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 38:1; Psalm 6:1; Jeremiah 30:11"> Correct me, O LORD, but only with justice—not in Your anger, or You will bring me to nothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 79:6–7; Jeremiah 8:16; Job 18:21"> Pour out Your wrath on the nations that do not acknowledge You, and on the families that do not call on Your name. For they have devoured Jacob; they have consumed him and finished him off; they have devastated his homeland.</span>
</div>
</div>
