</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:8; John 15:14; Proverbs 3:5"> So I bought a loincloth in accordance with the word of the LORD, and I put it around my waist.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:8"> Then the word of the LORD came to me a second time:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 4:10; Psalm 137:1; Jeremiah 51:63–64"> “Take the loincloth that you bought and are wearing, and go at once to Perath and hide it there in a crevice of the rocks.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; Exodus 39:42–43; 2 Timothy 2:3"> So I went and hid it at Perath, as the LORD had commanded me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Many days later the LORD said to me, “Arise, go to Perath, and get the loincloth that I commanded you to hide there.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:12; Isaiah 64:6; Jeremiah 13:10"> So I went to Perath and dug up the loincloth, and I took it from the place where I had hidden it. But now it was ruined—of no use at all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then the word of the LORD came to me:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremiah 13:15–17; Zephaniah 3:11"> “This is what the LORD says: In the same way I will ruin the pride of Judah and the great pride of Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; Jeremiah 9:14; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16"> These evil people, who refuse to listen to My words, who follow the stubbornness of their own hearts, and who go after other gods to serve and worship them, they will be like this loincloth—of no use at all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Jeremiah 33:9; Psalm 81:11"> For just as a loincloth clings to a man’s waist, so I have made the whole house of Israel and the whole house of Judah cling to Me, declares the LORD, so that they might be My people for My renown and praise and glory. But they did not listen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19"> Therefore you are to tell them that this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: ‘Every wineskin shall be filled with wine.’ And when they reply, ‘Don’t we surely know that every wineskin should be filled with wine?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 63:6; Psalm 75:8; Psalm 60:3"> then you are to tell them that this is what the LORD says: ‘I am going to fill with drunkenness all who live in this land—the kings who sit on David’s throne, the priests, the prophets, and all the people of Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Psalm 2:9; Jeremiah 19:9–11"> I will smash them against one another, fathers and sons alike, declares the LORD. I will allow no mercy or pity or compassion to keep Me from destroying them.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 28:14–22; Revelation 2:29; Acts 4:19–20"> Listen and give heed. Do not be arrogant, for the LORD has spoken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:9; Isaiah 8:22; Isaiah 5:30"> Give glory to the LORD your God before He brings darkness, before your feet stumble on the dusky mountains. You wait for light, but He turns it into deep gloom and thick darkness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 2:2; Jeremiah 9:1; Jeremiah 14:17"> But if you do not listen, I will weep in secret because of your pride. My eyes will overflow with tears, because the LORD’s flock has been taken captive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:26; 2 Kings 24:12; 2 Kings 24:15"> Say to the king and to the queen mother: “Take a lowly seat, for your glorious crowns have fallen from your heads.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:27; Jeremiah 39:9; Deuteronomy 28:52"> The cities of the Negev have been shut tight, and no one can open them. All Judah has been carried into exile, wholly taken captive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:22; Jeremiah 1:14; Habakkuk 1:6"> Lift up your eyes and see those coming from the north. Where is the flock entrusted to you, the sheep that were your pride?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 13:8; Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 5:31"> What will you say when He sets over you close allies whom you yourself trained? Will not pangs of anguish grip you, as they do a woman in labor?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nahum 3:5; Jeremiah 16:10–11; Jeremiah 5:19"> And if you ask yourself, “Why has this happened to me?” It is because of the magnitude of your iniquity that your skirts have been stripped off and your body has been exposed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; Jeremiah 9:5; Matthew 19:24–28"> Can the Ethiopian change his skin, or the leopard his spots? Neither are you able to do good—you who are accustomed to doing evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 1:4; Leviticus 26:33; Ezekiel 5:12"> “I will scatter you like chaff driven by the desert wind.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 20:29; Jeremiah 2:32; Psalm 9:17"> This is your lot, the portion I have measured to you,” declares the LORD, “because you have forgotten Me and trusted in falsehood.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Lamentations 1:8; Hosea 2:10; Ezekiel 23:29"> So I will pull your skirts up over your face, that your shame may be seen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hosea 8:5; Ezekiel 6:13; Ezekiel 24:13"> Your adulteries and lustful neighings, your shameless prostitution on the hills and in the fields—I have seen your detestable acts. Woe to you, O Jerusalem! How long will you remain unclean?”</span>
</div>
</div>
