<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:17; Isaiah 22:4; Lamentations 2:11"> Oh, that my head were a spring of water, and my eyes a fountain of tears! I would weep day and night over the slain daughter of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:10; Jeremiah 5:7–8; Hosea 4:2"> If only I had a traveler’s lodge in the wilderness, I would abandon my people and depart from them, for they are all adulterers, a crowd of faithless people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:8; Jeremiah 4:22; Judges 2:10"> “They bend their tongues like bows; lies prevail over truth in the land. For they proceed from evil to evil, and they do not take Me into account,” declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Proverbs 26:24–25; Psalm 12:2–3"> “Let everyone guard against his neighbor; do not trust any brother, for every brother deals craftily, and every friend spreads slander.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 6:12; Habakkuk 2:13; Job 15:5"> Each one betrays his friend; no one tells the truth. They have taught their tongues to lie; they wear themselves out committing iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; Jeremiah 13:10; Jeremiah 8:5"> You dwell in the midst of deception; in their deceit they refuse to know Me,” declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:25; Jeremiah 6:27; Malachi 3:3"> Therefore this is what the LORD of Hosts says: “Behold, I will refine them and test them, for what else can I do because of the daughter of My people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 28:3; Proverbs 26:24–26; Jeremiah 9:3"> Their tongues are deadly arrows; they speak deception. With his mouth a man speaks peace to his neighbor, but in his heart he sets a trap for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:9; Jeremiah 5:29; Isaiah 1:24"> Should I not punish them for these things? declares the LORD. Should I not avenge Myself on such a nation as this?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:4; Hosea 4:3; Jeremiah 12:10"> I will take up a weeping and wailing for the mountains, a dirge over the wilderness pasture, for they have been scorched so no one passes through, and the lowing of cattle is not heard. Both the birds of the air and the beasts have fled; they have gone away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 25:2; Isaiah 13:22; Jeremiah 10:22"> “And I will make Jerusalem a heap of rubble, a haunt for jackals; and I will make the cities of Judah a desolation, without inhabitant.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 14:9; Psalm 107:43; Psalm 107:34"> Who is the man wise enough to understand this? To whom has the mouth of the LORD spoken, that he may explain it? Why is the land destroyed and scorched like a desert, so no one can pass through it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:19; Jeremiah 22:9; Ezra 9:10"> And the LORD answered, “It is because they have forsaken My law, which I set before them; they have not walked in it or obeyed My voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:17; Jeremiah 7:24; 1 Peter 1:18"> Instead, they have followed the stubbornness of their hearts and gone after the Baals, as their fathers taught them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:14; Lamentations 3:15; Psalm 80:5"> Therefore this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: “Behold, I will feed this people wormwood and give them poisoned water to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Ezekiel 5:2; Leviticus 26:33"> I will scatter them among the nations that neither they nor their fathers have known, and I will send a sword after them until I have finished them off.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Amos 5:16–17; Ecclesiastes 12:5; 2 Chronicles 35:25"> This is what the LORD of Hosts says: “Take note, and summon the wailing women; send for the most skillful among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:1; Isaiah 22:4; Jeremiah 14:17"> Let them come quickly and take up a lament over us, that our eyes may overflow with tears, and our eyelids may gush with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Ezekiel 7:16–18; Deuteronomy 28:29"> For the sound of wailing is heard from Zion: ‘How devastated we are! How great is our shame! For we have abandoned the land because our dwellings have been torn down.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 23:27–30; Isaiah 32:9–13; Job 22:22"> Now, O women, hear the word of the LORD. Open your ears to the word of His mouth. Teach your daughters to wail, and one another to lament.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:11; Ezekiel 9:5–6; Jeremiah 15:7"> For death has climbed in through our windows; it has entered our fortresses to cut off the children from the streets, the young men from the town squares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:2; Jeremiah 25:33; Psalm 83:10"> Declare that this is what the LORD says: “The corpses of men will fall like dung upon the open field, like newly cut grain behind the reaper, with no one to gather it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27–29; Isaiah 5:21; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> This is what the LORD says: “Let not the wise man boast in his wisdom, nor the strong man in his strength, nor the wealthy man in his riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:17; Galatians 6:14; 1 Corinthians 1:31"> But let him who boasts boast in this, that he understands and knows Me, that I am the LORD, who exercises loving devotion, justice and righteousness on the earth—for I delight in these things,” declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 2:8–9; Galatians 5:2–6; Romans 2:25–26"> “Behold, the days are coming,” declares the LORD, “when I will punish all who are circumcised only in the flesh—</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Leviticus 26:41; Ezekiel 44:7"> Egypt, Judah, Edom, Ammon, Moab, and all the inhabitants of the desert who clip the hair of their temples. For all these nations are uncircumcised, and the whole house of Israel is uncircumcised in heart.”</span>
