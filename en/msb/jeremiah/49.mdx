<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 1:13–15; Zephaniah 2:8–11; Ezekiel 25:2–10"> Concerning the Ammonites, this is what the LORD says: “Has Israel no sons? Is he without heir? Why then has Milcom taken possession of Gad? Why have his people settled in their cities?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:19; Ezekiel 21:20; Deuteronomy 3:11"> Therefore, behold, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will sound the battle cry against Rabbah of the Ammonites. It will become a heap of ruins, and its villages will be burned. Then Israel will drive out their dispossessors, says the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:7; Jeremiah 48:37; Isaiah 15:2"> Wail, O Heshbon, for Ai has been destroyed; cry out, O daughters of Rabbah! Put on sackcloth and mourn; run back and forth within your walls, for Milcom will go into exile together with his priests and officials.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Jeremiah 9:23; Jeremiah 21:13"> Why do you boast of your valleys—your valleys so fruitful, O faithless daughter? You trust in your riches and say, ‘Who can come against me?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:5; Jeremiah 49:29; Jeremiah 15:8"> Behold, I am about to bring terror upon you, declares the Lord GOD of Hosts, from all those around you. You will each be driven headlong, with no one to regather the fugitives.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:39; Jeremiah 48:47; Ezekiel 16:53"> Yet afterward I will restore the Ammonites from captivity,” declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 1:11–12; Jeremiah 25:21; Genesis 36:15"> Concerning Edom, this is what the LORD of Hosts says: “Is there no longer wisdom in Teman? Has counsel perished from the prudent? Has their wisdom decayed?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:23; Jeremiah 46:21; Jeremiah 49:30"> Turn and run! Lie low, O dwellers of Dedan, for I will bring disaster on Esau at the time I punish him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Obadiah 1:5–6; Isaiah 17:6"> If grape gatherers came to you, would they not leave some gleanings? Were thieves to come in the night, would they not steal only what they wanted?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:14; Malachi 1:3–4; Isaiah 45:3"> But I will strip Esau bare; I will uncover his hiding places, and he will be unable to conceal himself. His descendants will be destroyed along with his relatives and neighbors, and he will be no more.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 68:5; Hosea 14:3; James 1:27"> Abandon your orphans; I will preserve their lives. Let your widows trust in Me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:28–29; Obadiah 1:16; Jeremiah 25:15"> For this is what the LORD says: “If those who do not deserve to drink the cup must drink it, can you possibly remain unpunished? You will not go unpunished, for you must drink it too.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 22:16; Isaiah 34:6; Genesis 36:33"> For by Myself I have sworn, declares the LORD, that Bozrah will become a desolation, a disgrace, a ruin, and a curse, and all her cities will be in ruins forever.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 30:4; Isaiah 37:7; Ezekiel 7:25–26"> I have heard a message from the LORD; an envoy has been sent to the nations: “Assemble yourselves to march against her! Rise up for battle!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 1:51; Obadiah 1:2; Micah 7:10"> “For behold, I will make you small among nations, despised among men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:25; Amos 9:2; Job 39:27"> The terror you cause and the pride of your heart have deceived you, O dwellers in the clefts of the rocks, O occupiers of the mountain summit. Though you elevate your nest like the eagle, even from there I will bring you down,” declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:7; Jeremiah 50:13; Jeremiah 49:13"> “Edom will become an object of horror. All who pass by will be appalled and will scoff at all her wounds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:33; Deuteronomy 29:23; Jeremiah 50:40"> As Sodom and Gomorrah were overthrown along with their neighbors,” says the LORD, “no one will dwell there; no man will abide there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:5; Jeremiah 30:21; Jeremiah 50:44–46"> Behold, one will come up like a lion from the thickets of the Jordan to the watered pasture. For in an instant I will chase Edom from her land. Who is the chosen one I will appoint for this? For who is like Me, and who can challenge Me? What shepherd can stand against Me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:45; Malachi 1:3–4; Jeremiah 49:7"> Therefore hear the plans that the LORD has drawn up against Edom and the strategies He has devised against the people of Teman: Surely the little ones of the flock will be dragged away; certainly their pasture will be made desolate because of them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:46; Ezekiel 32:10; Isaiah 14:4–15"> At the sound of their fall the earth will quake; their cry will resound to the Red Sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:40–41; Hosea 8:1; Isaiah 13:8"> Look! An eagle will soar and swoop down, spreading its wings over Bozrah. In that day the hearts of Edom’s mighty men will be like the heart of a woman in labor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 10:9; 2 Kings 18:34; Genesis 15:2"> Concerning Damascus: “Hamath and Arpad are put to shame, for they have heard a bad report; they are agitated like the sea; their anxiety cannot be calmed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Damascus has become feeble; she has turned to flee. Panic has gripped her; anguish and pain have seized her like a woman in labor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:9; Jeremiah 51:41; Isaiah 1:26"> How is the city of praise not forsaken, the town that brings Me joy?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:30; Amos 4:10; Jeremiah 11:22"> For her young men will fall in the streets, and all her warriors will be silenced in that day,” declares the LORD of Hosts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Amos 1:3–5; 1 Kings 15:18–20; Jeremiah 43:12"> “I will set fire to the walls of Damascus; it will consume the fortresses of Ben-hadad.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 6:3; Genesis 25:13; Isaiah 11:14"> Concerning Kedar and the kingdoms of Hazor, which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon defeated, this is what the LORD says: “Rise up, advance against Kedar, and destroy the people of the east!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:5; Jeremiah 6:25; Habakkuk 3:7"> They will take their tents and flocks, their tent curtains and all their goods. They will take their camels for themselves. They will shout to them: ‘Terror is on every side!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 27:6; Isaiah 10:7"> Run! Escape quickly! Lie low, O residents of Hazor,” declares the LORD, “for Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has drawn up a plan against you; he has devised a strategy against you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:11; Numbers 23:9; Deuteronomy 33:28"> Rise up, advance against a nation at ease, one that dwells securely,” declares the LORD. “They have no gates or bars; they live alone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:26; Jeremiah 25:23; Jeremiah 49:36"> Their camels will become plunder, and their large herds will be spoil. I will scatter to the wind in every direction those who shave their temples; I will bring calamity on them from all sides,” declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:22; Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 51:37"> “Hazor will become a haunt for jackals, a desolation forever. No one will dwell there; no man will abide there.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 10:22; Daniel 8:2; Jeremiah 25:25"> This is the word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet concerning Elam at the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 22:6; Jeremiah 51:56; Psalm 46:9"> This is what the LORD of Hosts says: “Behold, I will shatter Elam’s bow, the mainstay of their might.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:32; Revelation 7:1; Ezekiel 5:10"> I will bring the four winds against Elam from the four corners of the heavens, and I will scatter them to all these winds. There will not be a nation to which Elam’s exiles will not go.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:16; Jeremiah 48:2; Jeremiah 49:22"> So I will shatter Elam before their foes, before those who seek their lives. I will bring disaster upon them, even My fierce anger,” declares the LORD. “I will send out the sword after them until I finish them off.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:10; Daniel 7:9–14"> I will set My throne in Elam, and destroy its king and officials,” declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:47; Hosea 3:5; Micah 4:1"> “Yet in the last days, I will restore Elam from captivity,” declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
</div>
