<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 2:12; Jeremiah 3:22; Jeremiah 3:12"> “If you will return, O Israel, return to Me,” declares the LORD. “If you will remove your detestable idols from My sight and no longer waver,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 65:16; Jeremiah 9:24; Genesis 22:18"> and if you can swear, ‘As surely as the LORD lives,’ in truth, in justice, and in righteousness, then the nations will be blessed by Him, and in Him they will glory.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Luke 8:7; Luke 8:14"> For this is what the LORD says to the men of Judah and Jerusalem: “Break up your unplowed ground, and do not sow among the thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:16; Deuteronomy 30:6; Romans 2:28–29"> Circumcise yourselves to the LORD, and remove the foreskins of your hearts, O men of Judah and people of Jerusalem. Otherwise, My wrath will break out like fire and burn with no one to extinguish it, because of your evil deeds.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:14; Jeremiah 6:1; Joshua 10:20"> Announce in Judah, proclaim in Jerusalem, and say: “Blow the ram’s horn throughout the land. Cry aloud and say, ‘Assemble yourselves and let us flee to the fortified cities.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:2; Jeremiah 1:13–15; Jeremiah 51:27"> Raise a signal flag toward Zion. Seek refuge! Do not delay! For I am bringing disaster from the north, and terrible destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; Jeremiah 5:6; Isaiah 6:11"> A lion has gone up from his thicket, and a destroyer of nations has set out. He has left his lair to lay waste your land. Your cities will be reduced to ruins and lie uninhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 22:12; Jeremiah 6:26; Isaiah 10:4"> So put on sackcloth, mourn and wail, for the fierce anger of the LORD has not turned away from us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:9–16; Isaiah 29:9–10; Isaiah 22:3–5"> “In that day,” declares the LORD, “the king and officials will lose their courage. The priests will tremble in fear, and the prophets will be astounded.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–12; Isaiah 37:35"> Then I said, “Ah, Lord GOD, how completely You have deceived this people and Jerusalem by saying, ‘You will have peace,’ while a sword is at our throats.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 13:15; Ezekiel 17:10; Jeremiah 23:19"> At that time it will be said to this people and to Jerusalem, “A searing wind from the barren heights in the desert blows toward the daughter of My people, but not to winnow or to sift;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:16; Ezekiel 5:8; Ezekiel 6:11–13"> a wind too strong for that comes from Me. Now I also pronounce judgments against them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 19:1; Isaiah 66:15; Habakkuk 1:8"> Behold, he advances like the clouds, his chariots like the whirlwind. His horses are swifter than eagles. Woe to us, for we are ruined!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 55:7; Acts 8:22"> Wash the evil from your heart, O Jerusalem, so that you may be saved. How long will you harbor wicked thoughts within you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:16; Joshua 17:15; Judges 18:29"> For a voice resounds from Dan, proclaiming disaster from the hills of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:15; Ezekiel 21:22; Isaiah 39:3"> Warn the nations now! Proclaim to Jerusalem: “A besieging army comes from a distant land; they raise their voices against the cities of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:23; 2 Kings 25:1–4; Nehemiah 9:30"> They surround her like men guarding a field, because she has rebelled against Me,” declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:19; Psalm 107:17; Proverbs 1:31"> “Your ways and deeds have brought this upon you. This is your punishment; how bitter it is, because it pierces to the heart!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Isaiah 16:11; Isaiah 22:4"> My anguish, my anguish! I writhe in pain! Oh, the pain in my chest! My heart pounds within me; I cannot be silent. For I have heard the sound of the horn, the alarm of battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 42:7; Matthew 10:28; Joel 1:15"> Disaster after disaster is proclaimed, for the whole land is laid waste. My tents are destroyed in an instant, my curtains in a moment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:10; 2 Chronicles 36:3; 2 Chronicles 36:6–7"> How long must I see the signal flag and hear the sound of the horn?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; Romans 16:19; Jeremiah 5:21"> “For My people are fools; they have not known Me. They are foolish children, without understanding. They are skilled in doing evil, but they know not how to do good.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Mark 13:24–25; Isaiah 13:10"> I looked at the earth, and it was formless and void; I looked to the heavens, and they had no light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:20; Isaiah 5:25; Nahum 1:5–6"> I looked at the mountains, and behold, they were quaking; all the hills were swaying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:2–3; Jeremiah 12:4; Jeremiah 9:10"> I looked, and no man was left; all the birds of the air had fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 107:34; Jeremiah 12:4; Jeremiah 14:2–6"> I looked, and the fruitful land was a desert. All its cities were torn down before the LORD, before His fierce anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:28; Jeremiah 5:10; Jeremiah 5:18"> For this is what the LORD says: “The whole land will be desolate, but I will not finish its destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 50:3; Hosea 4:3"> Therefore the earth will mourn and the heavens above will grow dark. I have spoken, I have planned, and I will not relent or turn back.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19–21; Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 52:7"> Every city flees at the sound of the horseman and archer. They enter the thickets and climb among the rocks. Every city is abandoned; no inhabitant is left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 9:30; Lamentations 1:2; Lamentations 1:19"> And you, O devastated one, what will you do, though you dress yourself in scarlet, though you adorn yourself with gold jewelry, though you enlarge your eyes with paint? You adorn yourself in vain; your lovers despise you; they want to take your life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 1:17; Isaiah 1:15; Jeremiah 13:21"> For I hear a cry like a woman in labor, a cry of anguish like one bearing her first child—the cry of the Daughter of Zion gasping for breath, stretching out her hands to say, “Woe is me, for my soul faints before the murderers!”</span>
