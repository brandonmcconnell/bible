<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:5; 2 Kings 23:16; Jeremiah 7:32–34"> “At that time,” declares the LORD, “the bones of the kings of Judah, the bones of the officials, the bones of the priests, the bones of the prophets, and the bones of the people of Jerusalem will be removed from their graves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 23:5; Jeremiah 9:22; Deuteronomy 4:19"> They will be exposed to the sun and moon, and to all the host of heaven which they have loved, served, followed, consulted, and worshiped. Their bones will not be gathered up or buried, but will become like dung lying on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 9:6; Jeremiah 29:14; Jeremiah 23:3"> And wherever I have banished them, the remnant of this evil family will choose death over life,” declares the LORD of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 24:16; Micah 7:8; Hosea 6:1"> So you are to tell them this is what the LORD says: “Do men fall and not get up again? Does one turn away and not return?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:6; Jeremiah 5:3; Zechariah 7:11"> Why then have these people turned away? Why does Jerusalem always turn away? They cling to deceit; they refuse to return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 14:2; Ezekiel 18:28; Luke 15:17–19"> I have listened and heard; they do not speak what is right. No one repents of his wickedness, asking, ‘What have I done?’ Everyone has pursued his own course like a horse charging into battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:3; Jeremiah 5:4–5; Proverbs 6:6–8"> Even the stork in the sky knows her appointed seasons. The turtledove, the swift, and the thrush keep their time of migration, but My people do not know the requirements of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 9:41; Job 5:12–13; Isaiah 10:1–2"> How can you say, ‘We are wise, and the Law of the LORD is with us,’ when in fact the lying pen of the scribes has produced a deception?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:15; Psalm 119:98–100; Job 5:12"> The wise will be put to shame; they will be dismayed and trapped. Since they have rejected the word of the LORD, what wisdom do they really have?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:12–13; Ezekiel 33:31; Isaiah 56:10–12"> Therefore I will give their wives to other men and their fields to new owners. For from the least of them to the greatest, all are greedy for gain; from prophet to priest, all practice deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Ezekiel 13:10–16; Lamentations 2:14"> They dress the wound of the daughter of My people with very little care, saying, ‘Peace, peace,’ when there is no peace at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:3; Isaiah 3:9; Jeremiah 6:15"> Are they ashamed of the abomination they have committed? No, they have no shame at all; they do not even know how to blush. So they will fall among the fallen; when I punish them, they will collapse, says the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Joel 1:7; Jeremiah 17:8"> I will take away their harvest, declares the LORD. There will be no grapes on the vine, nor figs on the tree, and even the leaf will wither. Whatever I have given them will be lost to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:15; Jeremiah 9:15; Lamentations 3:19"> Why are we just sitting here? Gather together, let us flee to the fortified cities and perish there, for the LORD our God has doomed us. He has given us poisoned water to drink, because we have sinned against the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:19; Jeremiah 4:10; Jeremiah 8:11"> We hoped for peace, but no good has come, for a time of healing, but there was only terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 18:29; Judges 5:22; Jeremiah 4:15–16"> The snorting of enemy horses is heard from Dan. At the sound of the neighing of mighty steeds, the whole land quakes. They come to devour the land and everything in it, the city and all who dwell in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:11; Psalm 58:4–5; Deuteronomy 32:24"> “For behold, I will send snakes among you, vipers that cannot be charmed, and they will bite you,” declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 1:16–17; Jeremiah 6:24; Isaiah 22:4"> My sorrow is beyond healing; my heart is faint within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 39:3; Isaiah 13:5; Psalm 135:21"> Listen to the cry of the daughter of my people from a land far away: “Is the LORD no longer in Zion? Is her King no longer there?” “Why have they provoked Me to anger with their carved images, with their worthless foreign idols?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; Matthew 25:1–12; Luke 13:25"> “The harvest has passed, the summer has ended, but we have not been saved.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:17; Joel 2:6; Nahum 2:10"> For the brokenness of the daughter of my people I am crushed. I mourn; horror has gripped me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:11; Genesis 37:25; Jeremiah 51:8"> Is there no balm in Gilead? Is no physician there? Why then has the health of the daughter of my people not been restored?</span>
