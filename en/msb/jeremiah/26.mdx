<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:4–5; Jeremiah 27:1; 2 Kings 23:34–36"> At the beginning of the reign of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah, this word came from the LORD:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:2; Acts 20:27; Matthew 28:20"> “This is what the LORD says: Stand in the courtyard of the house of the LORD and speak all the words I have commanded you to speak to all the cities of Judah who come to worship there. Do not omit a word.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:3; Isaiah 1:16–19; Jeremiah 18:7–10"> Perhaps they will listen and turn—each from his evil way of life—so that I may relent of the disaster I am planning to bring upon them because of the evil of their deeds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 9:6; Jeremiah 44:10; Isaiah 1:20"> And you are to tell them that this is what the LORD says: ‘If you do not listen to Me and walk in My law, which I have set before you,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:7; 2 Kings 9:7; Jeremiah 7:13"> and if you do not listen to the words of My servants the prophets, whom I have sent you again and again even though you did not listen,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; 2 Kings 22:19; Jeremiah 25:18"> then I will make this house like Shiloh, and I will make this city an object of cursing among all the nations of the earth.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Micah 3:11; Acts 5:17"> Now the priests and prophets and all the people heard Jeremiah speaking these words in the house of the LORD,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 4:13–14; Acts 7:52; Jeremiah 18:18"> and as soon as he had finished telling all the people everything the LORD had commanded him to say, the priests and prophets and all the people seized him, shouting, “You must surely die!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:17–19; Acts 19:24–32; Micah 2:6"> How dare you prophesy in the name of the LORD that this house will become like Shiloh and this city will be desolate and deserted!” And all the people assembled against Jeremiah in the house of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:10; Jeremiah 38:4–6; Jeremiah 37:14–16"> When the officials of Judah heard these things, they went up from the king’s palace to the house of the LORD and sat there at the entrance of the New Gate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:4; Matthew 26:66; Deuteronomy 18:20"> Then the priests and prophets said to the officials and all the people, “This man is worthy of death, for he has prophesied against this city, as you have heard with your own ears!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Jeremiah 26:15; Acts 4:19"> But Jeremiah said to all the officials and all the people, “The LORD sent me to prophesy against this house and against this city all the words that you have heard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 3:9; Jonah 4:2; Isaiah 1:19"> So now, correct your ways and deeds, and obey the voice of the LORD your God, so that He might relent of the disaster He has pronounced against you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 9:25; 2 Samuel 15:26; Jeremiah 38:5"> As for me, here I am in your hands; do to me what you think is good and right.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; Proverbs 6:17; Matthew 26:25"> But know for certain that if you put me to death, you will bring innocent blood upon yourselves, upon this city, and upon its residents; for truly the LORD has sent me to speak all these words in your hearing.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 5:34–39; Acts 23:29; Acts 25:25"> Then the officials and all the people told the priests and prophets, “This man is not worthy of death, for he has spoken to us in the name of the LORD our God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:34; Micah 1:1"> Some of the elders of the land stood up and said to the whole assembly of the people,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 1:1; Nehemiah 4:2; Zechariah 8:3"> “Micah the Moreshite prophesied in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah and told all the people of Judah that this is what the LORD of Hosts says: ‘Zion will be plowed like a field, Jerusalem will become a heap of rubble, and the temple mount a wooded ridge.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 32:14; 2 Samuel 24:16; Isaiah 37:15–20"> Did Hezekiah king of Judah or anyone else in Judah put him to death? Did Hezekiah not fear the LORD and seek His favor, and did not the LORD relent of the disaster He had pronounced against them? But we are about to bring great harm on ourselves!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:2; Joshua 9:17; Joshua 15:60"> Now there was another man prophesying in the name of the LORD, Uriah son of Shemaiah from Kiriath-jearim. He prophesied against this city and against this land the same things that Jeremiah did.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Matthew 14:5; Jeremiah 36:26"> King Jehoiakim and all his mighty men and officials heard his words, and the king sought to put him to death. But when Uriah found out about it, he fled in fear and went to Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:12; 2 Kings 22:12; 2 Kings 22:14"> Then King Jehoiakim sent men to Egypt: Elnathan son of Achbor along with some other men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:30; Revelation 11:7; Ezekiel 19:6"> They brought Uriah out of Egypt and took him to King Jehoiakim, who had him put to the sword and his body thrown into the burial place of the common people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:5–7; Jeremiah 39:14; Jeremiah 1:18–19"> Nevertheless, Ahikam son of Shaphan supported Jeremiah, so he was not handed over to the people to be put to death.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
