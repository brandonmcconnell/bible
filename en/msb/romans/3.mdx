<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 2:25–29; Genesis 25:32; Ecclesiastes 6:11"> What, then, is the advantage of being a Jew? Or what is the value of circumcision?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 9:4; Acts 7:38; 1 Corinthians 9:17"> Much in every way. First of all, they have been entrusted with the very words of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:13; Hebrews 4:2; Romans 9:6"> What if some did not have faith? Will their lack of faith nullify God’s faithfulness?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 51:4; Psalm 116:11; Galatians 2:17"> Certainly not! Let God be true and every man a liar. As it is written: “So that You may be proved right when You speak and victorious when You judge.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 6:19; Galatians 3:15; Romans 9:18–20"> But if our unrighteousness highlights the righteousness of God, what shall we say? That God is unjust to inflict His wrath on us? I am speaking in human terms.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:25; Acts 17:31; Job 8:3"> Certainly not! In that case, how could God judge the world?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:4; Romans 9:19–20; Acts 13:27–29"> For if my falsehood accentuates God’s truthfulness, to the increase of His glory, why am I still condemned as a sinner?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 6:1; Romans 6:15; Romans 5:20"> Why not say, as some slanderously claim that we say, “Let us do evil that good may result”? Their condemnation is deserved!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 6:15; Isaiah 65:5"> What then? Are we any better? Not at all. For we have already made the charge that Jews and Greeks alike are all under sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 53:1–3; Psalm 14:1–3; Romans 3:23"> As it is written: “There is no one righteous, not even one.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 14:2–4; Psalm 53:2; Titus 3:3"> There is no one who understands, no one who seeks God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 14:3; Ecclesiastes 7:20; Isaiah 64:6"> All have turned away, they have together become worthless; there is no one who does good, not even one.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 5:9; Psalm 140:3; Matthew 23:27–28"> “Their throats are open graves; their tongues practice deceit.” “The venom of vipers is on their lips.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 10:7; Psalm 109:17–18; Psalm 59:12"> “Their mouths are full of cursing and bitterness.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7–8; Proverbs 1:16; Proverbs 6:18"> “Their feet are swift to shed blood;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> ruin and misery lie in their wake,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 59:8; Romans 5:1; Luke 1:79"> and the way of peace they have not known.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 36:1; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 16:6"> “There is no fear of God before their eyes.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Galatians 3:22–23; Romans 2:12–18"> Now we know that whatever the law says, it says to those who are under the law, so that every mouth may be silenced and the whole world held accountable to God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Acts 13:39; Romans 3:28"> Therefore no one will be justified in His sight by works of the law. For the law merely brings awareness of sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 10:43; Jeremiah 33:16; Philippians 3:9"> But now, apart from the law, the righteousness of God has been revealed, as attested by the Law and the Prophets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Galatians 3:28; Romans 8:1"> And this righteousness from God comes through faith in Jesus Christ to all and upon all who believe. There is no distinction,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; 1 John 1:8–10; Romans 3:9"> for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; Titus 3:5–7; Colossians 1:14"> and are justified freely by His grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 3:23–24; 1 Peter 1:18–20; 1 John 4:10"> God presented Him as an atoning sacrifice in His blood through faith, in order to demonstrate His righteousness, because in His forbearance He had passed over the sins committed beforehand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Romans 4:5; Romans 3:30"> He did this to demonstrate His righteousness at the present time, so as to be just and to justify the one who has faith in Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 4:2; Romans 2:23; 1 Corinthians 1:29–31"> Where, then, is boasting? It is excluded. On what principle? On that of works? No, but on that of faith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Galatians 3:8; Titus 3:7"> Therefore we maintain that a man is justified by faith apart from works of the law.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Ephesians 3:6; Zechariah 2:11"> Is God the God of Jews only? Is He not the God of Gentiles too? Yes, of Gentiles too,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Galatians 3:20; Philippians 3:3"> since there is only one God, who will justify the circumcised by faith and the uncircumcised through that same faith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Matthew 5:17; Romans 13:8–10"> Do we, then, nullify the law by this faith? Certainly not! Instead, we uphold the law.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
