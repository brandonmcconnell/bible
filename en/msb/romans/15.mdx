<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 69:9; Philippians 2:8; John 4:34"> For even Christ did not please Himself, but as it is written: “The insults of those who insult You have fallen on Me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Romans 4:23–24"> For what was written in the past was written for our instruction, so that through endurance and the encouragement of the Scriptures, we might have hope.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 2:4–5; 1 Peter 3:8; Romans 12:16"> Now may the God who gives endurance and encouragement grant you harmony with one another in Christ Jesus,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 10:29–30; Revelation 1:6; Ephesians 1:3"> so that with one mind and one voice you may glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:34; Romans 5:2"> Accept one another, then, just as Christ accepted you, in order to bring glory to God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Matthew 15:24; John 1:11"> Now I tell you that Christ Jesus has become a servant of the circumcised on behalf of God’s truth, to confirm the promises made to the patriarchs,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:50; Psalm 18:49; Romans 3:29"> so that the Gentiles may glorify God for His mercy. As it is written: “Therefore I will praise You among the Gentiles; I will sing hymns to Your name.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 67:3–4; Deuteronomy 32:43; Isaiah 42:10–12"> Again, it says: “Rejoice, O Gentiles, with His people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 117:1"> And again: “Praise the Lord, all you Gentiles, and extol Him, all you peoples.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 11:10; Isaiah 11:1; Matthew 12:21"> And once more, Isaiah says: “The Root of Jesse will appear, One who will arise to rule over the Gentiles; in Him the Gentiles will put their hope.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:16–17; Romans 12:12; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> Now may the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace as you believe in Him, so that you may overflow with hope by the power of the Holy Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 2 Peter 1:12; 1 John 2:21"> I myself am convinced, my brothers, that you yourselves are full of goodness, brimming with knowledge, and able to instruct others.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 15:10; Romans 1:5"> However, brothers, I have written you a bold reminder on some points, because of the grace God has given me</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:13; Romans 1:1; Acts 9:15"> to be a minister of Jesus Christ to the Gentiles in the priestly service of the gospel of God, so that the Gentiles might become an offering acceptable to God, sanctified by the Holy Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14–16; 2 Corinthians 12:11–21; Hebrews 5:1"> Therefore I exult in Christ Jesus in my service to God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 15:12; Romans 1:5; Acts 21:19"> I will not presume to speak of anything except what Christ has accomplished through me in leading the Gentiles to obedience by word and deed,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:12; Hebrews 2:4; Acts 19:11–12"> by the power of signs and wonders, and by the power of the Spirit of God. So from Jerusalem all the way around to Illyricum, I have fully proclaimed the gospel of Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:13–16; 1 Corinthians 3:9–15; Ephesians 2:20–22"> In this way I have aspired to preach the gospel where Christ was not known, so that I would not be building on someone else’s foundation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 52:15; Isaiah 65:1"> Rather, as it is written: “Those who were not told about Him will see, and those who have not heard will understand.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 1:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:17–18"> That is why I have often been hindered from coming to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 15:32; Romans 1:10–12; Romans 15:29"> But now that there are no further opportunities for me in these regions, and since I have longed for many years to visit you,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:28; Romans 1:12; Acts 15:3"> I hope to come to see you on my way to Spain. And after I have enjoyed your company for a while, you can equip me for my journey.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Acts 19:21; Acts 20:22"> Now, however, I am on my way to Jerusalem to serve the saints there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:1–9; Acts 11:27–30; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> For Macedonia and Achaia were pleased to make a contribution for the poor among the saints in Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:11; Galatians 6:6; Philemon 1:19"> They were pleased to do it, and indeed they owe it to them. For if the Gentiles have shared in their spiritual blessings, they are obligated to minister to them with material blessings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 15:24; Philippians 4:17; Colossians 1:6"> So after I have completed this service and have safely delivered this bounty to them, I will set off to Spain by way of you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 1:10–12; Psalm 16:11"> I know that when I come to you, I will come in the fullness of the blessing of the gospel of Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 1:11; 2 Corinthians 4:11"> Now I urge you, brothers, by our Lord Jesus Christ and by the love of the Spirit, to join me in my struggle by praying to God for me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:2; 2 Corinthians 8:4; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> Pray that I may be delivered from the unbelievers in Judea, and that my service in Jerusalem may be acceptable to the saints there,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:18; Philemon 1:20; Philemon 1:7"> so that by God’s will I may come to you with joy and together with you be refreshed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; Philippians 4:9; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> The God of peace be with all of you. Amen.</span>
</div>
