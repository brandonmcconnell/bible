<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Romans 15:16; 1 Corinthians 1:1"> Paul, a servant of Jesus Christ, called to be an apostle, and set apart for the gospel of God—</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:2; Luke 1:70; Romans 3:21"> the gospel He promised beforehand through His prophets in the Holy Scriptures,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 1:1; Galatians 4:4; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> regarding His Son, who was a descendant of David according to the flesh,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 9:14; Hebrews 5:5–6; Ephesians 1:19–23"> and who through the Spirit of holiness was declared with power to be the Son of God by His resurrection from the dead: Jesus Christ our Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Romans 16:26; Acts 6:7"> Through Him and on behalf of His name, we received grace and apostleship to call all those among the Gentiles to the obedience that comes from faith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 17:14; 2 Peter 1:10"> And you also are among those who are called to belong to Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; Jude 1:1–2; Ephesians 1:2"> To all in Rome who are loved by God and called to be saints: Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 16:19; 1 Corinthians 1:4; 1 Thessalonians 1:8–9"> First, I thank my God through Jesus Christ for all of you, because your faith is being proclaimed all over the world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; 2 Timothy 1:3; Ephesians 1:16–19"> God, whom I serve with my spirit in preaching the gospel of His Son, is my witness how constantly I remember you</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 15:30–32; Acts 18:21; Philemon 1:22"> in my prayers at all times, asking that now at last by God’s will I may succeed in coming to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 1:12; Romans 16:25; Romans 15:29"> For I long to see you so that I may impart to you some spiritual gift to strengthen you,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:7–10; 3 John 1:3–4; 2 Corinthians 7:13"> that is, that you and I may be mutually encouraged by each other’s faith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 19:21; Philippians 4:17; 2 Timothy 4:17"> I do not want you to be unaware, brothers, how often I planned to come to you (but have been prevented from visiting until now), in order that I might have a harvest among you, just as I have had among the other Gentiles.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:16–23; Acts 9:15; Colossians 3:11"> I am obligated both to Greeks and non-Greeks, both to the wise and the foolish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:20; Isaiah 6:8; 1 Corinthians 9:17"> That is why I am so eager to preach the gospel also to you who are in Rome.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 8:38; 2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Peter 4:16"> I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ, because it is the power of God for salvation to everyone who believes, first to the Jew, then to the Greek.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:4; Galatians 3:11; Hebrews 10:38"> For the gospel reveals the righteousness of God that comes by faith from start to finish, just as it is written: “The righteous will live by faith.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:6; 1 Timothy 4:1–2; Romans 1:32"> The wrath of God is being revealed from heaven against all the godlessness and wickedness of men who suppress the truth by their wickedness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 14:16–17; Psalm 19:1–6; Romans 1:20"> For what may be known about God is plain to them, because God has made it plain to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:19; Isaiah 40:26; Psalm 19:1–6"> For since the creation of the world God’s invisible qualities, His eternal power and divine nature, have been clearly seen, being understood from His workmanship, so that men are without excuse.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 4:17–18; 2 Kings 17:15; Jeremiah 2:5"> For although they knew God, they neither glorified Him as God nor gave thanks to Him, but they became futile in their thinking and darkened in their foolish hearts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:19–21; Romans 11:25; Proverbs 26:12"> Although they claimed to be wise, they became fools,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 106:20; Jeremiah 2:11; Acts 17:29"> and exchanged the glory of the immortal God for images of mortal man and birds and animals and reptiles.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 81:11–12; Acts 7:42; Acts 14:16"> Therefore God gave them over in the desires of their hearts to impurity for the dishonoring of their bodies with one another.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18; Jeremiah 13:25; 1 John 2:15–16"> They exchanged the truth of God for a lie, and worshiped and served the creature rather than the Creator, who is forever worthy of praise! Amen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jude 1:7; 1 Timothy 1:10; Romans 1:24"> For this reason God gave them over to dishonorable passions. Even their women exchanged natural relations for unnatural ones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 20:13; Leviticus 18:22; Romans 1:23–24"> Likewise, the men abandoned natural relations with women and burned with lust for one another. Men committed indecent acts with other men, and received in themselves the due penalty for their error.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Romans 1:21; Titus 1:16"> Furthermore, since they did not see fit to acknowledge God, He gave them up to a depraved mind, to do what ought not to be done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:20; Proverbs 16:28; Psalm 41:7"> They have become filled with every kind of wickedness, sexual immorality, evil, greed, and depravity. They are full of envy, murder, strife, deceit, and malice. They are gossips,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:2; Jude 1:16; 2 Peter 2:18"> slanderers, God-haters, insolent, arrogant, and boastful. They invent new forms of evil; they disobey their parents.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:3; Proverbs 18:2; Romans 3:11"> They are senseless, faithless, heartless, unforgiving, merciless.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 2:1–5; Romans 1:21; Romans 6:21"> Although they know God’s righteous decree that those who do such things are worthy of death, they not only continue to do these things, but also approve of those who practice them.</span>
</div>
</div>
