<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:20–22; Romans 9:1–3; John 5:34"> Brothers, my heart’s desire and prayer to God for the Israelites is for their salvation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 21:20; Romans 9:31–32; Proverbs 19:2"> For I testify about them that they are zealous for God, but not on the basis of knowledge.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Luke 16:15; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> Because they were ignorant of God’s righteousness and sought to establish their own, they did not submit to God’s righteousness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 3:24; Romans 8:3–4; Matthew 5:17–18"> For Christ is the end of the law, to bring righteousness to everyone who believes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Nehemiah 9:29"> For concerning the righteousness that is by the law, Moses writes: “The man who does these things will live by them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:11–14; Proverbs 30:4; Ephesians 4:8–10"> But the righteousness that is by faith says: “Do not say in your heart, ‘Who will ascend into heaven?’ (that is, to bring Christ down)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Revelation 1:18; Romans 4:25"> or, ‘Who will descend into the Abyss?’ (that is, to bring Christ up from the dead).”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:14; Romans 1:16–17; 1 Timothy 4:6"> But what does it say? “The word is near you; it is in your mouth and in your heart,” that is, the word of faith we are proclaiming:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 12:8; Acts 16:31; Philippians 2:11"> that if you confess with your mouth, “Jesus is Lord,” and believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead, you will be saved.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Romans 10:9; Galatians 2:16"> For with your heart you believe and are justified, and with your mouth you confess and are saved.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Romans 9:33; 1 Peter 2:6"> It is just as the Scripture says: “Anyone who believes in Him will never be put to shame.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; Revelation 17:14; Galatians 3:28"> For there is no difference between Jew and Greek: The same Lord is Lord of all, and gives richly to all who call on Him,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:21; Joel 2:32"> for, “Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 26:17–18; Titus 1:3"> How then will they call on the One in whom they have not believed? And how will they believe in the One of whom they have not heard? And how will they hear without someone to preach?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Nahum 1:15; Ephesians 2:17"> And how will they preach unless they are sent? As it is written: “How beautiful are the feet of those who bring good news of peace, who bring good news of good things!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 3:3; John 12:38–40"> But not all of them welcomed the good news. For Isaiah says, “Lord, who has believed our message?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 3:2; Galatians 3:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:13"> Consequently, faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 19:4; Romans 1:8; Matthew 24:14"> But I ask, did they not hear? Indeed they did: “Their voice has gone out into all the earth, their words to the ends of the world.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Romans 11:11; Romans 11:14"> I ask instead, did Israel not understand? First, Moses says: “I will make you jealous by those who are not a nation; I will make you angry by a nation without understanding.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:1–2; Romans 9:30; Isaiah 55:4–5"> And Isaiah boldly says: “I was found by those who did not seek Me; I revealed Myself to those who did not ask for Me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 65:2–5; 1 Peter 2:8; Jeremiah 35:15"> But as for Israel he says: “All day long I have held out My hands to a disobedient and obstinate people.”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
