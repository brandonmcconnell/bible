<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:2; Luke 20:20; Psalm 62:4"> One Sabbath, Jesus went to eat in the home of a leading Pharisee, and those in attendance were watching Him closely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Right there before Him was a man with dropsy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:10; Mark 3:4; Luke 13:14–16"> So Jesus asked the experts in the law and the Pharisees, “Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Matthew 21:25–27"> But they remained silent. Then Jesus took hold of the man, healed him, and sent him on his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 13:15; Exodus 23:4–5; Matthew 12:11–12"> And He asked them, “Which of you whose son or ox falls into a pit on the Sabbath day will not immediately pull him out?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 20:40; Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10"> And they were unable to answer these questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Luke 11:43; Matthew 23:6"> When Jesus noticed how the guests chose the places of honor, He told them a parable:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7"> “When you are invited to a wedding banquet, do not sit in the place of honor, in case someone more distinguished than you has been invited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 11:2; Proverbs 3:35"> Then the host who invited both of you will come and tell you, ‘Give this man your seat.’ And in humiliation, you will have to take the last place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7; Proverbs 15:33; 1 Samuel 15:17"> But when you are invited, go and sit in the last place, so that your host will come and tell you, ‘Friend, move up to a better place.’ Then you will be honored in front of those at the table with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 18:14; Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 29:23"> For everyone who exalts himself will be humbled, and the one who humbles himself will be exalted.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:46; James 2:1–6; Luke 6:32–36"> Then Jesus said to the man who had invited Him, “When you host a dinner or a banquet, do not invite your friends or brothers or relatives or rich neighbors. Otherwise, they may invite you in return, and you will be repaid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 14:21; Isaiah 58:10; Proverbs 14:31"> But when you host a banquet, invite the poor, the crippled, the lame, and the blind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Acts 24:15; John 5:29"> and you will be blessed. Since they cannot repay you, you will be repaid at the resurrection of the righteous.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; Luke 22:30; Luke 13:29"> When one of those reclining with Him heard this, he said to Jesus, “Blessed is he who will eat at the feast in the kingdom of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 22:2–14; Revelation 22:17; Isaiah 55:1–7"> But Jesus replied, “A certain man prepared a great banquet and invited many guests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:27–29; Matthew 10:1–4; Matthew 22:3–4"> When it was time for the banquet, he sent his servant to tell those who had been invited, ‘Come, for everything is now ready.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 17:26–31; Luke 18:24"> But one after another they all began to make excuses. The first one said, ‘I have bought a field, and I need to go and see it. Please excuse me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Another said, ‘I have bought five yoke of oxen, and I am going to try them out. Please excuse me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:5; 1 Corinthians 7:33; Luke 14:26–28"> Still another said, ‘I have married a wife, so I cannot come.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 14:13; Matthew 11:5; Luke 14:24"> That servant returned and reported all this to his master. Then the owner of the house became angry and said to his servant, ‘Go out quickly into the streets and alleys of the city, and bring in the poor, the crippled, the lame, and the blind.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 14:2; Ephesians 3:8; 1 Timothy 2:5–6"> ‘Sir,’ the servant replied, ‘it has been done as you ordered, and there is still room.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:9–10; Colossians 1:28; Acts 13:47–48"> So the master told his servant, ‘Go out to the highways and hedges and compel them to come in, so that my house will be full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Acts 13:46; John 8:24"> For I tell you, not one of those men who were invited will taste my banquet. For many are called, but few are chosen.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:24–27; Luke 12:1"> Now large crowds were traveling with Jesus, and He turned and said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; John 12:25; Philippians 3:8"> “If anyone comes to Me and does not hate his father and mother and wife and children and brothers and sisters—yes, even his own life—he cannot be My disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 9:23–25; Mark 8:34–37"> And whoever does not carry his cross and follow Me cannot be My disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 24:27; Matthew 10:22; Joshua 24:19–24"> Which of you, wishing to build a tower, does not first sit down and count the cost to see if he has the resources to complete it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Otherwise, if he lays the foundation and is unable to finish the work, everyone who sees it will ridicule him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 6:11; 2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:11–14"> saying, ‘This man could not finish what he started to build.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 25:8; 2 Kings 18:20–22"> Or what king on his way to war with another king does not first sit down and consider whether he can engage with ten thousand men the one coming against him with twenty thousand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 12:20; James 4:6–10; Luke 12:58"> And if he is unable, he will send a delegation while the other king is still far off, to ask for terms of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; 1 John 2:15–16; Luke 5:28"> In the same way, any one of you who does not give up everything he has cannot be My disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:13; Colossians 4:6; Mark 9:49–50"> Salt is good, but if the salt loses its savor, with what will it be seasoned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Luke 9:44; Revelation 2:11"> It is fit neither for the soil nor for the manure pile, and it is thrown out. He who has ears to hear, let him hear.”</span>
