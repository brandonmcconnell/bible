<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 24:14; Acts 25:21"> Now in those days a decree went out from Caesar Augustus that a census should be taken of the whole empire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 4:24; Acts 26:30"> This was the first census to take place while Quirinius was governor of Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And everyone went to his own town to register.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:42; 1 Samuel 16:1"> So Joseph also went up from Nazareth in Galilee to Judea, to the city of David called Bethlehem, since he was from the house and line of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:22–27; Matthew 1:18–19"> He went there to register with Mary, who was pledged to him in marriage and was expecting a child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 33:11; Revelation 12:1–5; Micah 5:2"> While they were there, the time came for her Child to be born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 4:4; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Isaiah 53:2–3"> And she gave birth to her firstborn, a Son. She wrapped Him in swaddling cloths and laid Him in the manger, because there was no room for them in the inn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 31:39–40; Psalm 78:70–71; 1 Samuel 17:34–35"> And there were shepherds residing in the fields nearby, keeping watch over their flocks by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; 2 Corinthians 3:18; 1 Kings 8:11"> And behold, an angel of the Lord stood before them, and the glory of the Lord shone around them, and they were terrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 52:7"> But the angel said to them, “Do not be afraid! For behold, I bring you good news of great joy that will be for all the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Matthew 1:21; Acts 2:36"> Today in the city of David a Savior has been born to you. He is Christ the Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 53:1–2; Isaiah 7:11"> And this will be the sign to you: You will find a baby wrapped in swaddling cloths and lying in a manger.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 103:20–21; Revelation 5:11; Psalm 148:2"> And suddenly there appeared with the angel a great multitude of the heavenly host, praising God and saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 5:1; Luke 19:38; Colossians 1:20"> “Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good will toward men!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 2:1–2; Matthew 2:9–11"> When the angels had left them and gone into heaven, the shepherds said to one another, “Let us go to Bethlehem and see this thing that has happened, which the Lord has made known to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:32; Luke 2:12; Luke 2:7"> So they hurried off and found Mary and Joseph and the Baby, who was lying in the manger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 66:16; Luke 8:39; Psalm 71:17–18"> After they had seen the Child, they spread widely the message they had received about Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 2:47; Luke 4:36; Luke 1:65–66"> And all who heard it were amazed at what the shepherds said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 2:51; Luke 1:66; Proverbs 4:4"> But Mary treasured up all these things and pondered them in her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 106:48; Isaiah 29:19; Acts 11:18"> The shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all they had heard and seen, which was just as the angel had told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:31; Genesis 17:12; Galatians 4:4–5"> When the eight days before His circumcision had passed, He was named Jesus, the name the angel had given Him before He was conceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:1–8"> And when the time of purification according to the Law of Moses was complete, His parents brought Him to Jerusalem to present Him to the Lord</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 22:29; Numbers 3:13"> (as it is written in the Law of the Lord: “Every firstborn male shall be consecrated to the Lord”),</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 12:6–8; Leviticus 12:2; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> and to offer the sacrifice specified in the Law of the Lord: “A pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Luke 2:38; Luke 1:6"> Now there was a man in Jerusalem named Simeon, who was righteous and devout. He was waiting for the consolation of Israel, and the Holy Spirit was upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:51; Hebrews 11:5; Luke 9:27"> The Holy Spirit had revealed to him that he would not see death before he had seen the Lord’s Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 4:1; Luke 2:41; Luke 2:51"> Led by the Spirit, he went into the temple courts. And when the parents brought in the child Jesus to do for Him what was customary under the Law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 1:64; Luke 2:20; Luke 2:13–14"> Simeon took Him in his arms and blessed God, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Genesis 15:15; Philippians 1:23"> “Sovereign Lord, as You have promised, You now dismiss Your servant in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 49:6; Luke 2:10–11; Luke 3:6"> For my eyes have seen Your salvation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 62:1–2; Isaiah 42:1–4; Psalm 98:2–3"> which You have prepared in the sight of all people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–3; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 26:23"> a light for revelation to the Gentiles, and for glory to Your people Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 1:65–66; Isaiah 8:18; Luke 2:48"> And Joseph and the Child’s mother were amazed at what was spoken about Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 8:14–15"> Then Simeon blessed them and said to His mother Mary: “Behold, this Child is appointed to cause the rise and fall of many in Israel, and to be a sign that will be spoken against,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Matthew 12:24–35"> so that the thoughts of many hearts will be revealed—and a sword will pierce your soul as well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Genesis 30:13; Acts 21:9"> There was also a prophetess named Anna, the daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Asher, who was well along in years. She had been married for seven years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:5; Psalm 23:6; Acts 14:23"> and then was a widow to the age of eighty-four. She never left the temple, but worshiped night and day, fasting and praying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 2:25; 2 Corinthians 9:15; Mark 15:43"> Coming forward at that moment, she gave thanks to the Lord and spoke about the Child to all who were waiting for redemption in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 2:4; Galatians 4:4–5; Deuteronomy 12:32"> When Jesus’ parents had done everything required by the Law of the Lord, they returned to Galilee, to their own town of Nazareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 2:52; Luke 1:80; Isaiah 11:1–5"> And the Child grew and became strong in spirit. He was filled with wisdom, and the grace of God was upon Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Numbers 28:16; Deuteronomy 12:18"> Every year His parents went to Jerusalem for the Feast of the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And when He was twelve years old, they went up to Jerusalem according to the custom of the Feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:21–23; Exodus 12:15; 2 Chronicles 25:17"> When those days were over and they were returning home, the boy Jesus remained behind in Jerusalem, but Joseph and His mother were unaware He had stayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalm 122:1–4; Isaiah 2:3; Psalm 42:4"> Assuming He was in their company, they traveled on for a day before they began to look for Him among their relatives and friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> When they could not find Him, they returned to Jerusalem to search for Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 5:17; Isaiah 49:1–2; Isaiah 50:4"> Finally, after three days they found Him in the temple courts, sitting among the teachers, listening to them and asking them questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Luke 4:32; Psalm 119:99"> And all who heard Him were astounded at His understanding and His answers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 2:49; Matthew 12:46; Luke 3:23"> When His parents saw Him, they were astonished. “Child, why have You done this to us?” His mother asked. “Your father and I have been anxiously searching for You.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 6:38; John 8:29; Malachi 3:1"> “Why were you looking for Me?” He asked. “Did you not know that I had to be in My Father’s house?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Luke 18:34; Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32"> But they did not understand the statement He was making to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Daniel 7:28; Genesis 37:11"> Then He went down to Nazareth with them and was obedient to them. But His mother treasured up all these things in her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 2:40; Proverbs 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 2:26"> And Jesus grew in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and man.</span>
