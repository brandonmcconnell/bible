<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 27:1–2; Matthew 27:11–14; Luke 22:66"> Then the whole council rose and led Jesus away to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 19:12; Luke 23:14"> And they began to accuse Him, saying, “We found this man subverting the nation, forbidding payment of taxes to Caesar, and proclaiming Himself to be Christ, a King.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 1:49; 1 Timothy 6:13; Luke 22:70"> So Pilate asked Him, “Are You the King of the Jews?” “You have said so,” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:22"> Then Pilate said to the chief priests and the crowds, “I find no basis for a charge against this man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 1:14; John 1:43; John 2:11"> But they kept insisting, “He stirs up the people all over Judea with His teaching. He began in Galilee and has come all the way here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 5:37; Luke 13:1"> When Pilate heard of Galilee, he asked if the man was a Galilean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Luke 13:31; Matthew 14:1"> And learning that Jesus was under Herod’s jurisdiction, he sent Him to Herod, who himself was in Jerusalem at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:7–9; Matthew 14:1; Mark 6:14"> When Herod saw Jesus, he was greatly pleased. He had wanted to see Him for a long time, because he had heard many things about Him and was hoping to see Him perform a miracle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 27:14; Matthew 7:6; 1 Peter 2:23"> Herod questioned Jesus at great length, but He gave no answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 11:53; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:5"> Meanwhile, the chief priests and scribes stood there, vehemently accusing Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Matthew 27:27–30; Psalm 69:19–20"> And even Herod and his soldiers ridiculed and mocked Him. Dressing Him in a fine robe, they sent Him back to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 4:27; Revelation 17:13–14; Psalm 83:4–6"> That day Pilate and Herod became friends; before this time they had been enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:14; Matthew 27:21–23; John 18:38"> Then Pilate called together the chief priests, the rulers, and the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:4; Hebrews 7:26; Luke 23:1–2"> and said to them, “You brought me this man as one who was inciting the people to rebellion. I have examined Him here in your presence and found Him not guilty of your charges against Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Neither has Herod, for I sent you back to him. As you can see, He has done nothing deserving of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Luke 23:22; Mark 15:15"> Therefore I will punish Him and release Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Now Pilate was obliged to release to the people one prisoner at the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:36; Acts 3:14; Acts 22:22"> But they all cried out in unison: “Away with this man! Release Barabbas to us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Luke 23:5; Luke 23:2"> (Barabbas had been imprisoned for an insurrection in the city, and for murder.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 15:15; Matthew 14:8–9; Matthew 27:19"> Wanting to release Jesus, Pilate addressed them again,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:22–25; Mark 15:13; John 19:15"> but they kept shouting, “Crucify Him! Crucify Him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 23:14; Luke 23:20; 1 Peter 3:18"> A third time he said to them, “What evil has this man done? I have found in Him no offense worthy of death. So after I punish Him, I will release Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 11:8; Psalm 57:4; Psalm 22:12–13"> But they were insistent, demanding with loud voices for Jesus to be crucified. And their clamor and that of the chief priests prevailed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 17:15"> So Pilate sentenced that their demand be met.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Mark 15:6–7; Mark 15:15"> As they had requested, he released the one imprisoned for insurrection and murder, and he handed Jesus over to their will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:32–44; Mark 15:21–32; Acts 6:9"> As the soldiers led Him away, they seized Simon of Cyrene on his way in from the country, and they put the cross on him to carry behind Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 8:52; Luke 8:2; Mark 15:40"> A great number of people followed Him, including women who kept mourning and wailing for Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:4; Song of Solomon 5:8; Song of Solomon 1:5"> But Jesus turned to them and said, “Daughters of Jerusalem, do not weep for Me, but weep for yourselves and for your children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:19; Luke 21:23–24; Mark 13:17–19"> Look, the days are coming when people will say, ‘Blessed are the barren women, the wombs that never bore, and breasts that never nursed!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 6:16; Hosea 10:8; Isaiah 2:19"> At that time ‘they will say to the mountains, “Fall on us!” and to the hills, “Cover us!”’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; John 15:6; Proverbs 11:31"> For if men do these things while the tree is green, what will happen when it is dry?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:27; Isaiah 53:12; Matthew 27:38"> Two others, who were criminals, were also led away to be executed with Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 15:22–23; John 19:17–18; John 18:32"> When they came to the place called The Skull, they crucified Him there, along with the criminals, one on His right and the other on His left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Acts 7:60; 1 Peter 3:9"> Then Jesus said, “Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing.” And they divided up His garments by casting lots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalm 22:17; Isaiah 42:1; 1 Peter 2:4"> The people stood watching, and the rulers with them sneered at Him, saying, “He saved others; let Him save Himself if He is the Christ, the Chosen One of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalm 69:21; Matthew 27:48; Matthew 27:34"> The soldiers also mocked Him and came up to offer Him sour wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> “If You are the King of the Jews,” they said, “save Yourself!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:26; Matthew 27:37; John 19:19–22"> Above Him was posted an inscription written in Greek, Latin, and Hebrew: THIS IS THE KING OF THE JEWS.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:35"> One of the criminals who hung there heaped abuse on Him. “If You are the Christ,” he said, “save Yourself and us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Luke 12:5; Revelation 15:4"> But the other one rebuked him, saying, “Do you not even fear God, since you are under the same judgment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–41; Ezra 9:13; 1 Peter 1:19"> We are punished justly, for we are receiving what our actions deserve. But this man has done nothing wrong.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Romans 10:9–14; Acts 16:31; 1 John 5:11–13"> Then he said to Jesus, “Lord, remember me when You come into Your kingdom!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Psalm 50:15; 1 Timothy 1:15–16"> And Jesus said to him, “Truly I tell you, today you will be with Me in Paradise.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Mark 15:33–34; Amos 8:9"> It was now about the sixth hour, and darkness came over all the land until the ninth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 9:3–8"> The sun was darkened, and the veil of the temple was torn down the middle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalm 31:5; Acts 7:59; John 19:30"> Then Jesus called out in a loud voice, “Father, into Your hands I commit My Spirit.” And when He had said this, He breathed His last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:39; Luke 23:41"> When the centurion saw what had happened, he gave glory to God, saying, “Surely this was a righteous man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 18:13; Acts 2:37; Jeremiah 31:19"> And when all the people who had gathered for this spectacle saw what had happened, they returned home beating their breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 8:2; Psalm 38:11; Luke 23:55"> But all those who knew Jesus, including the women who had followed Him from Galilee, stood at a distance watching these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Acts 11:24; Acts 10:22"> Now there was a Council member named Joseph, a good and righteous man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Luke 2:38; Isaiah 8:12"> who had not consented to their decision or action. He was from the Judean town of Arimathea and himself was waiting for the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 19:38–42"> He went to Pilate to ask for the body of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 53:9; Mark 15:46"> Then he took it down, wrapped it in a linen cloth, and placed it in a tomb cut into the rock, where no one had yet been laid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:31; Mark 15:42"> It was Preparation Day, and the Sabbath was beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:61; Mark 15:47"> The women who had come with Jesus from Galilee followed, and they saw the tomb and how His body was placed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Luke 24:1; Mark 16:1; Deuteronomy 5:14"> Then they returned to prepare spices and perfumes. And they rested on the Sabbath, according to the commandment.</span>
