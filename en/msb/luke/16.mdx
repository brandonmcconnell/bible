<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Matthew 25:14–30; James 4:3"> Jesus also said to His disciples, “There was a rich man whose manager was accused of wasting his possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:9–10; 1 Peter 4:5; Revelation 20:12"> So he called him in to ask, ‘What is this I hear about you? Turn in an account of your management, for you cannot be manager any longer.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Isaiah 10:3; Proverbs 18:9"> The manager said to himself, ‘What shall I do, now that my master is taking away my position? I am too weak to dig and too ashamed to beg.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; Jeremiah 4:22; James 3:15"> I know what I will do so that after my removal from management, people will welcome me into their homes.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:41–42; Matthew 18:24"> And he called in each one of his master’s debtors. ‘How much do you owe my master?’ he asked the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:10; Luke 16:12; Luke 16:9"> ‘A hundred measures of olive oil,’ he answered. ‘Take your bill,’ said the manager, ‘sit down quickly, and write fifty.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:11–12; Luke 20:9; Luke 20:12"> Then he asked another, ‘And how much do you owe?’ ‘A hundred measures of wheat,’ he replied. ‘Take your bill and write eighty,’ he told him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5; Ephesians 5:8; Proverbs 6:6–8"> The master commended the dishonest manager because he had acted shrewdly. For the sons of this age are more shrewd in dealing with their own kind than are the sons of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 19:21; 1 Timothy 6:17–19; Daniel 4:27"> I tell you, use worldly wealth to make friends for yourselves so that when you fail, they will welcome you into eternal dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 16:11–12; Matthew 25:21; Luke 19:17"> Whoever is faithful with very little will also be faithful with much, and whoever is dishonest with very little will also be dishonest with much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 12:33; James 2:5; Proverbs 8:18–19"> So if you have not been faithful with worldly wealth, who will entrust you with true riches?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 19:13–26; Matthew 25:14–29; Job 1:21"> And if you have not been faithful with the belongings of another, who will give you belongings of your own?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Matthew 6:24; James 4:4"> No servant can serve two masters. Either he will hate the one and love the other, or he will be devoted to the one and despise the other. You cannot serve both God and money.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:35; Luke 20:47; Jeremiah 6:13"> The Pharisees, who were lovers of money, heard all of this and were scoffing at Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Proverbs 21:2; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> So He said to them, “You are the ones who justify yourselves before men, but God knows your hearts. For what is prized among men is detestable before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 11:9–14; Matthew 21:32; Acts 3:24–25"> The Law and the Prophets were proclaimed until John. Since that time, the gospel of the kingdom of God is being preached, and everyone is forcing his way into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:18; Luke 21:33; 1 Peter 1:25"> But it is easier for heaven and earth to pass away than for a single stroke of a pen to drop out of the Law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 19:9; Mark 10:11–12; Matthew 5:31–32"> Anyone who divorces his wife and marries another woman commits adultery, and everyone who marries a divorced woman commits adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:49; Amos 6:4–6; Luke 18:24–25"> Now there was a rich man dressed in purple and fine linen, who lived each day in joyous splendor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:2; 1 Samuel 2:8; James 2:5"> And a beggar named Lazarus lay at his gate, covered with sores</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 15:27; Mark 7:28; John 6:12"> and longing to be fed with the crumbs that fell from the rich man’s table. Even the dogs came and licked his sores.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Psalm 49:16–19; James 1:11"> One day the beggar died and was carried by the angels to Abraham’s side. And the rich man also died and was buried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Luke 16:28; Matthew 18:9"> In Hades, where he was in torment, he looked up and saw Abraham from afar, with Lazarus by his side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 4:10; Revelation 22:1; Revelation 7:16–17"> So he cried out, ‘Father Abraham, have mercy on me and send Lazarus to dip the tip of his finger in water and cool my tongue. For I am in agony in this fire.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 6:24; 1 John 2:15; Psalm 17:14"> But Abraham answered, ‘Child, remember that during your lifetime you received your good things, while Lazarus received bad things. But now he is comforted here, while you are in agony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 3:36; Psalm 49:14; Psalm 50:22"> And besides all this, a great chasm has been fixed between us and you, so that even those who wish cannot cross from here to you, nor can anyone cross from there to us.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> ‘Then I beg you, father,’ he said, ‘send Lazarus to my father’s house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:40; Psalm 49:12–13; 1 Thessalonians 4:6"> for I have five brothers. Let him warn them, so that they will not also end up in this place of torment.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 15:21; Luke 24:27; Luke 16:16"> But Abraham replied, ‘They have Moses and the Prophets; let your brothers listen to them.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 16:9–11; Luke 16:24; Luke 13:5"> ‘No, father Abraham,’ he said, ‘but if someone is sent to them from the dead, they will repent.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3; Acts 19:8; John 11:43–53"> Then Abraham said to him, ‘If they do not listen to Moses and the Prophets, they will not be persuaded even if someone rises from the dead.’”</span>
