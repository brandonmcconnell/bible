<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:6–23; Matthew 26:2–5; John 11:55–57"> Now the Feast of Unleavened Bread, called the Passover, was approaching,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 11:47–53; Matthew 12:14; Luke 20:19"> and the chief priests and scribes were looking for a way to put Jesus to death, for they feared the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 13:2; Mark 14:10–11; John 13:26–27"> Then Satan entered Judas Iscariot, who was one of the Twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Acts 5:24; Luke 22:52"> And Judas went to discuss with the chief priests and temple officers how he might betray Jesus to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 26:15–16; Acts 1:18; Matthew 27:3–5"> They were delighted and agreed to give him money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 14:2; Matthew 26:5"> Judas consented, and began to look for an opportunity to betray Jesus to them in the absence of a crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:18; Mark 14:12–16; 1 Corinthians 5:7"> Then came the day of Unleavened Bread on which the Passover lamb was to be sacrificed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 14:13–16; Acts 4:19; Acts 4:13"> Jesus sent Peter and John, saying, “Go and make preparations for us to eat the Passover.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> “Where do You want us to prepare it?” they asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 16:4; Matthew 26:18–19; Acts 8:26–29"> He answered, “When you enter the city, a man carrying a jug of water will meet you. Follow him to the house he enters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:28; Revelation 3:20; Luke 19:34"> and say to the owner of that house, ‘The Teacher asks: Where is the guest room, where I may eat the Passover with My disciples?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 16:14–15; Acts 1:13; John 2:25"> And he will show you a large upper room, already furnished. Make preparations there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 21:33; Luke 19:32; John 2:5"> So they went and found it just as Jesus had told them. And they prepared the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 26:20; Mark 14:17; Deuteronomy 16:6–7"> When the hour had come, Jesus reclined at the table with His twelve apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 12:50; John 4:34; John 17:1"> And He said to them, “I have eagerly desired to eat this Passover with you before My suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 14:15; Revelation 19:9; Luke 22:30"> For I tell you that I will not eat it again until it is fulfilled in the kingdom of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:19; Psalm 116:13; Deuteronomy 8:10"> After taking the cup, He gave thanks and said, “Take this and divide it among yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 26:29; Luke 22:16; Mark 14:25"> For I tell you that I will not drink of the fruit of the vine until the kingdom of God comes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:23–29; 1 Corinthians 10:16; Mark 14:22–24"> And He took the bread, gave thanks and broke it, and gave it to them, saying, “This is My body, given for you; do this in remembrance of Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 26:28; 1 Corinthians 11:25; Zechariah 9:11"> In the same way, after supper He took the cup, saying, “This cup is the new covenant in My blood, which is poured out for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 41:9; John 13:26; John 13:21–22"> Look! The hand of My betrayer is with Mine on the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 2:23; Psalm 109:6–15; Psalm 55:12–15"> Indeed, the Son of Man will go as it has been determined, but woe to that man who betrays Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:22–25; Mark 14:19; Matthew 26:22"> Then they began to question among themselves which of them was going to do this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 9:46; Mark 9:34; Mark 10:37–41"> A dispute also arose among the disciples as to which of them should be considered the greatest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 20:25–28; Mark 10:41–45"> So Jesus declared, “The kings of the Gentiles lord it over them, and those in authority over them call themselves benefactors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Luke 9:48; Matthew 18:3–5"> But you shall not be like them. Instead, the greatest among you should be like the youngest, and the one who leads like the one who serves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Luke 12:37; John 13:5–16"> For who is greater, the one who reclines at the table or the one who serves? Is it not the one who reclines? But I am among you as one who serves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:18; Hebrews 4:15; Acts 1:25"> You are the ones who have stood by Me in My trials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 25:34; 2 Timothy 2:12; James 2:5"> And I bestow on you a kingdom, just as My Father has bestowed one on Me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Matthew 8:11; Revelation 2:26–27"> so that you may eat and drink at My table and sit on thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Zechariah 3:1; Job 1:6–12"> And the Lord said, “Simon, Simon, Satan has asked to sift all of you like wheat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–10; John 21:15–17; John 17:9–11"> But I have prayed for you, Simon, that your faith will not fail. And when you have turned back, strengthen your brothers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 14:31; Matthew 26:33–35; Mark 14:29"> “Lord,” said Peter, “I am ready to go with You even to prison and to death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 13:38; Matthew 26:34; Mark 14:30"> But Jesus replied, “I tell you, Peter, the rooster will certainly not crow today before you have denied three times that you know Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 10:9–10; Luke 10:4; Psalm 23:1"> Then Jesus asked them, “When I sent you out without purse or bag or sandals, did you lack anything?” “Nothing,” they answered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 10:22–25; 1 Thessalonians 3:4; 1 Thessalonians 2:14–15"> “Now, however,” He told them, “the one with a purse should take it, and likewise a bag; and the one without a sword should sell his cloak and buy one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Luke 23:32; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For I tell you that this Scripture must yet be fulfilled in Me: ‘And He was numbered with the transgressors.’ For what is written about Me is reaching its fulfillment.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 22:49; Matthew 26:52–54; John 18:36"> So they said, “Look, Lord, here are two swords.” “That is enough,” He answered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 21:37; John 18:1–2; Matthew 21:1"> Jesus went out as usual to the Mount of Olives, and His disciples followed Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Matthew 26:36–46; Luke 22:46"> When He came to the place, He told them, “Pray that you will not enter into temptation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 26:39; Mark 14:35; Luke 18:11"> And He withdrew about a stone’s throw beyond them, where He knelt down and prayed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:27–28; Matthew 26:39; Matthew 26:42"> “Father, if You are willing, take this cup from Me. Yet not My will, but Yours be done.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 4:11; Matthew 26:53; Psalm 91:11–12"> Then an angel from heaven appeared to Him and strengthened Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 1:12; Hebrews 5:7; Psalm 22:12–21"> And in His anguish, He prayed more earnestly, and His sweat became like drops of blood falling to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 14:40–41; Matthew 26:43; Matthew 26:40"> When Jesus rose from prayer and returned to the disciples, He found them asleep, exhausted from sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 22:40; Jonah 1:6; Luke 21:34–36"> “Why are you sleeping?” He asked. “Get up and pray so that you will not enter into temptation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 14:41–50; John 18:2–11; Luke 22:3–6"> While He was still speaking, a crowd arrived, led by the man called Judas, one of the Twelve. He approached Jesus to kiss Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Mark 14:44–46; 2 Samuel 20:9–10; Proverbs 27:6"> But Jesus asked him, “Judas, are you betraying the Son of Man with a kiss?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 22:38"> Those around Jesus saw what was about to happen and said to Him, “Lord, should we strike with our swords?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Mark 14:47; Romans 12:19"> And one of them struck the servant of the high priest, cutting off his right ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Romans 12:21; 2 Corinthians 10:1; 1 Peter 2:21–23"> But Jesus answered, “No more of this!” And He touched the man’s ear and healed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 22:4; Mark 14:48–49; John 17:12"> Then Jesus said to the chief priests, temple officers, and elders who had come for Him, “Have you come out with swords and clubs as you would against an outlaw?</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 12:27; Ephesians 6:12; Acts 26:18"> Every day I was with you in the temple courts, and you did not lay a hand on Me. But this hour belongs to you and to the power of darkness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Mark 14:53–54; Matthew 26:57–58; John 18:12–17"> Then they seized Jesus, led Him away, and took Him into the house of the high priest. And Peter followed at a distance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Psalm 26:4–5; 2 Corinthians 6:15–17; Mark 14:66–72"> When those present had kindled a fire in the middle of the courtyard and sat down together, Peter sat down among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 18:17; Mark 14:6; Mark 14:17"> A servant girl saw him seated in the firelight and looked intently at him. “This man also was with Him,” she said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="1 John 1:9; John 18:25; Matthew 26:70"> But Peter denied Him. “Woman, I do not know Him,” he said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 18:25; Matthew 26:71–72; Mark 14:69–70"> A short time later, someone else saw him and said, “You also are one of them.” But Peter said, “Man, I am not.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 18:26–27; Mark 14:69–70; Matthew 26:73–74"> About an hour later, another man insisted, “Certainly this man was with Him, for he too is a Galilean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Matthew 26:74–75; Luke 22:34; Mark 14:71–72"> “Man, I do not know what you are talking about,” Peter replied. And immediately, while he was still speaking, the rooster crowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Luke 22:34; Isaiah 57:15–18; Jeremiah 31:18–20"> And the Lord turned and looked at Peter. Then Peter remembered the word that the Lord had spoken to him: “Before the rooster crows, you will deny Me three times.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Matthew 26:75; 1 Corinthians 10:12; Mark 14:72"> And Peter went outside and wept bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="John 18:22; Mark 14:55–65; Job 16:9–10"> The men who were holding Jesus began to mock Him and beat Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Judges 16:25; Judges 16:21"> They blindfolded Him and kept striking Him in the face and demanding, “Prophesy! Who hit You?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; Matthew 27:39"> And they said many other blasphemous things against Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Matthew 27:1; Mark 15:1; Acts 22:5"> At daybreak the council of the elders of the people, both the chief priests and scribes, met together. They led Jesus into their Sanhedrin and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Matthew 11:3–5; John 8:43–45; Matthew 26:63–68"> “If You are the Christ, tell us.” Jesus answered, “If I tell you, you will not believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Luke 20:3–7"> And if I ask you a question, you will not answer Me nor release Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Psalm 110:1; Mark 16:19"> But from now on the Son of Man will be seated at the right hand of the power of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Luke 23:3; Matthew 27:11; Matthew 26:64"> So they all asked, “Are You then the Son of God?” He replied, “You say that I am.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Matthew 26:65–66; Mark 14:63–64"> “Why do we need any more testimony?” they declared. “We have heard it for ourselves from His own lips.”</span>
