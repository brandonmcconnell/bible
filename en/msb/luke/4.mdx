<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:1–11; Acts 10:38; Mark 1:12–13"> Then Jesus, full of the Holy Spirit, returned from the Jordan and was led by the Spirit into the wilderness,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Hebrews 4:15; Deuteronomy 9:9"> where for forty days He was tempted by the devil. He ate nothing during those days, and afterward, when they had ended, He was hungry.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 4:3; Luke 3:22"> The devil said to Him, “If You are the Son of God, tell this stone to become bread.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Matthew 4:4; Matthew 6:31"> But Jesus answered, “It is written: ‘Man shall not live on bread alone, but on every word of God.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; 1 Corinthians 15:52; Matthew 4:8"> Then the devil led Him up to a high mountain and showed Him in an instant all the kingdoms of the world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 12:31; 1 John 5:19; John 14:30"> “I will give You authority over all these kingdoms and all their glory,” he said. “For it has been relinquished to me, and I can give it to anyone I wish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 8:28; Psalm 72:11; Isaiah 45:14"> So if You worship me, it will all be Yours.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Deuteronomy 10:20; Matthew 4:10"> But Jesus answered, “Get behind Me, Satan! It is written: ‘Worship the Lord your God and serve Him only.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 4:5–6; Luke 4:3; Matthew 8:29"> Then the devil led Him to Jerusalem and set Him on the pinnacle of the temple. “If You are the Son of God,” he said, “throw Yourself down from here.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 91:11–12; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Corinthians 11:14"> For it is written: ‘He will command His angels concerning You to guard You carefully,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 91:12"> and they will lift You up in their hands, so that You will not strike Your foot against a stone.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; Hebrews 3:8–9; 1 Corinthians 10:9"> But Jesus answered, “It also says, ‘Do not put the Lord your God to the test.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; James 4:7; Matthew 4:11"> When the devil had finished every temptation, he left Him until an opportune time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 4:23–25; Acts 10:37; Matthew 4:12"> Jesus returned to Galilee in the power of the Spirit, and the news about Him spread throughout the surrounding region.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Matthew 9:8; Matthew 9:35"> He taught in their synagogues and was glorified by everyone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:2; Acts 13:14–16; Matthew 2:23"> Then Jesus came to Nazareth, where He had been brought up. As was His custom, He entered the synagogue on the Sabbath. And when He stood up to read,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1–3; Acts 13:15; Acts 7:42"> the scroll of Isaiah the prophet was handed to Him. Unrolling it, He found the place where it was written:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1; Matthew 11:5; Psalm 146:7–8"> “The Spirit of the Lord is on Me, because He has anointed Me to preach good news to the poor. He has sent Me to heal the brokenhearted, to proclaim liberty to the captives and recovery of sight to the blind, to release the oppressed,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 61:2; Isaiah 63:4; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> to proclaim the year of the Lord’s favor.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 4:17; John 8:2; Luke 19:48"> Then He rolled up the scroll, returned it to the attendant, and sat down. The eyes of everyone in the synagogue were fixed on Him,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 4:25–26; Acts 3:18; Acts 2:16–18"> and He began by saying, “Today this Scripture is fulfilled in your hearing.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 13:54–56; Psalm 45:2; Acts 6:10"> All spoke well of Him and marveled at the gracious words that came from His lips. “Isn’t this the son of Joseph?” they asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 4:46–53; Matthew 11:23–24; Mark 2:1–12"> Jesus said to them, “Surely you will quote this proverb to Me: ‘Physician, heal yourself! Do here in Your hometown what we have heard that You did in Capernaum.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 13:57; John 4:44; Mark 6:4–5"> Then He added, “Truly I tell you, no prophet is accepted in his hometown.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 5:17; 1 Kings 17:1; Matthew 20:15"> But I tell you truthfully that there were many widows in Israel in the time of Elijah, when the sky was shut for three and a half years and great famine swept over all the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 17:9–24; Matthew 11:21; Obadiah 1:20"> Yet Elijah was not sent to any of them, but to the widow of Zarephath in Sidon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 5:1–27; John 17:12; Matthew 12:4"> And there were many lepers in Israel in the time of Elisha the prophet. Yet not one of them was cleansed—only Naaman the Syrian.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 5:33; 2 Chronicles 24:20–21; Jeremiah 38:6"> On hearing this, all the people in the synagogue were enraged.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:59; Acts 21:28–32; Numbers 15:35"> They got up, drove Him out of the town, and led Him to the brow of the hill on which the town was built, in order to throw Him over the cliff.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:39; John 8:59; John 18:6–7"> But Jesus passed through the crowd and went on His way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 1:21–28; Matthew 4:13; Acts 13:50–2"> Then He went down to Capernaum, a town in Galilee, and on the Sabbath He began to teach the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 4:36; Matthew 7:28–29; John 6:63"> They were astonished at His teaching, because His message had authority.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 1:23"> In the synagogue there was a man possessed by the spirit of an unclean demon. He cried out in a loud voice,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 1:24; James 2:19; Matthew 8:29"> “Ha! What do You want with us, Jesus of Nazareth? Have You come to destroy us? I know who You are—the Holy One of God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Luke 4:39; Matthew 8:26"> But Jesus rebuked the demon. “Be silent!” He said. “Come out of him!” At this, the demon threw the man down before them all and came out without harming him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 4:32; 1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 12:22–23"> All the people were overcome with amazement and asked one another, “What is this message? With authority and power He commands the unclean spirits, and they come out!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 1:45; Luke 4:14; Isaiah 52:13"> And the news about Jesus spread throughout the surrounding region.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 8:14–15; Mark 1:29–31; John 11:22"> After Jesus had left the synagogue, He went to the home of Simon, whose mother-in-law was suffering from a high fever. So they appealed to Jesus on her behalf,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Luke 4:35; Luke 8:24"> and He stood over her and rebuked the fever, and it left her. And she got up at once and began to serve them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 1:32–34; Matthew 8:16–17; Mark 5:23"> At sunset, all who were ill with various diseases were brought to Jesus, and laying His hands on each one, He healed them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:34; James 2:19; Luke 4:34–35"> Demons also came out of many people, crying out, “You are the Christ, the Son of God!” But He rebuked the demons and would not allow them to speak, because they knew He was the Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 1:35–38; John 4:34"> At daybreak, Jesus went out to a solitary place, and the crowds were looking for Him. They came to Him and tried to keep Him from leaving.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 1:38–39; 2 Timothy 4:2; John 9:4"> But Jesus told them, “I must preach the good news of the kingdom of God to the other towns as well, because that is why I was sent.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Mark 1:39; Luke 4:15"> And He continued to preach in the synagogues of Galilee.</span>
</div>
</div>
