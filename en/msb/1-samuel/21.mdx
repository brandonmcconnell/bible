<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 2:26; Nehemiah 11:32; Isaiah 10:32"> Then David came to Nob, to Ahimelech the priest. And when Ahimelech met David, he trembled and asked him, “Why are you alone? Why is no one with you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 2:12; Colossians 3:9; 1 Samuel 22:22"> “The king has given me a mission,” David replied. “He told me no one is to know about the mission on which I am sending you. And I have directed my young men to meet me at a certain place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 3:6; Judges 9:29; 1 Samuel 21:4"> Now then, what do you have on hand? Give me five loaves of bread, or whatever can be found.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 19:15; Matthew 12:3–4; Exodus 25:30"> “There is no common bread on hand,” the priest replied, “but there is some consecrated bread—provided that the young men have kept themselves from women.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 24:9; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–4; 2 Timothy 2:20–21"> David answered, “Women have indeed been kept from us, as is usual when I set out. And the bodies of the young men are holy even on common missions. How much more so today!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:3–4; Leviticus 24:5–9; Matthew 12:3–4"> So the priest gave him the consecrated bread, since there was no bread there but the Bread of the Presence, which had been removed from before the LORD and replaced with hot bread on the day it was taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:9; Psalm 52:1–9; 1 Chronicles 27:29"> Now one of Saul’s servants was there that day, detained before the LORD. And his name was Doeg the Edomite, the chief shepherd for Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then David asked Ahimelech, “Is there not a spear or sword on hand here? For I have brought neither my sword nor my weapons with me, because the king’s mission was urgent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:2; 1 Samuel 17:50–54; Exodus 28:6–14"> The priest replied, “The sword of Goliath the Philistine, whom you killed in the Valley of Elah, is here; it is wrapped in a cloth behind the ephod. If you want, you may take it. For there is no other but this one here.” And David said, “There is none like it; give it to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:21; 1 Kings 19:3; 1 Samuel 27:1–2"> That day David fled from Saul and went to Achish king of Gath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:5; 1 Samuel 18:7–8; 1 Samuel 16:1"> But the servants of Achish said to him, “Is this not David, the king of the land? Did they not sing about him in their dances, saying: ‘Saul has slain his thousands, and David his tens of thousands’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Psalm 56:3; Psalm 34:4"> Now David took these words to heart and was very much afraid of Achish the king of Gath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Ecclesiastes 7:7; Psalm 34:1–22"> So he changed his behavior before them and feigned madness in their hands; he scratched on the doors of the gate and let his saliva run down his beard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:7"> Then Achish said to his servants, “Look, you can see that the man is insane! Why have you brought him to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Am I in need of madmen, that you have brought this man to rave in my presence? Must this man come into my house?”</span>
