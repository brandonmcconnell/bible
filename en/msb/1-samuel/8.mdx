<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:18–19; 1 Timothy 5:21; Nehemiah 7:2"> When Samuel grew old, he appointed his sons as judges over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:38; Genesis 22:19; 1 Chronicles 6:28"> The name of his firstborn son was Joel, and the name of his second was Abijah. They were judges in Beersheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Psalm 15:5; 1 Timothy 6:10"> But his sons did not walk in his ways; they turned aside toward dishonest gain, accepting bribes and perverting justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:17; 2 Samuel 5:3; Exodus 3:16"> So all the elders of Israel gathered together and came to Samuel at Ramah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:14–15; Hosea 13:10–11; 1 Samuel 8:6–8"> “Look,” they said, “you are old, and your sons do not walk in your ways. Now appoint a king to judge us like all the other nations.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:11; James 1:5; 1 Samuel 12:17"> But when they said, “Give us a king to judge us,” their demand was displeasing in the sight of Samuel; so he prayed to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:16; 1 Samuel 10:19; Exodus 16:8"> And the LORD said to Samuel, “Listen to the voice of the people in all that they say to you. For it is not you they have rejected, but they have rejected Me as their king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 106:34–40; Exodus 32:1; Judges 13:1"> Just as they have done from the day I brought them up out of Egypt until this day, forsaking Me and serving other gods, so they are doing to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:25; 1 Samuel 8:11–18; Ezekiel 46:18"> Now listen to their voice; but you must solemnly warn them and show them the manner of the king who will reign over them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So Samuel spoke all the words of the LORD to the people who were asking him for a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:52; Deuteronomy 17:14–20; 1 Samuel 10:25"> He said, “This will be the manner of the king who will reign over you: He will take your sons and appoint them to serve his own chariots and horses, and to run in front of his chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:7; 1 Kings 4:7; 1 Chronicles 27:1–22"> He will appoint some for himself as commanders of thousands and of fifties, and others to plow his ground, to reap his harvest, and to make his weapons of war and equipment for his chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he will take your daughters to be perfumers, cooks, and bakers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:18; 1 Kings 21:7; 1 Kings 21:19"> He will take the best of your fields and vineyards and olive groves and give them to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 1:3; Isaiah 39:7; Genesis 37:36"> He will take a tenth of your grain and grape harvest and give it to his officials and servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And he will take your menservants and maidservants and your best cattle and donkeys and put them to his own use.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He will take a tenth of your flocks, and you yourselves will become his slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:4; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 1:25–28"> When that day comes, you will beg for relief from the king you have chosen, but the LORD will not answer you on that day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16; Jeremiah 7:13; Isaiah 66:4"> Nevertheless, the people refused to listen to Samuel. “No!” they said. “We must have a king over us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:5; Psalm 106:35; Deuteronomy 7:6"> Then we will be like all the other nations, with a king to judge us, to go out before us, and to fight our battles.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:11"> Samuel listened to all the words of the people and repeated them in the hearing of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Hosea 13:11"> “Listen to their voice,” the LORD said to Samuel. “Appoint a king for them.” Then Samuel told the men of Israel, “Everyone must go back to his city.”</span>
