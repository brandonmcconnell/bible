<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 2:4; 1 Samuel 10:26; 1 Samuel 15:34"> When the messengers came to Gibeah of Saul and relayed these words in the hearing of the people, they all wept aloud.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 19:19; Judges 18:23; Genesis 21:17"> Just then Saul was returning from the field, behind his oxen. “What troubles the people?” asked Saul. “Why are they weeping?” And they relayed to him the words of the men from Jabesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:10; 1 Samuel 16:13; Judges 6:34"> When Saul heard their words, the Spirit of God rushed upon him, and he burned with great anger.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:29; Judges 20:1; Judges 21:5–11"> He took a pair of oxen, cut them into pieces, and sent them by messengers throughout the land of Israel, proclaiming, “This is what will be done to the oxen of anyone who does not march behind Saul and Samuel.” Then the terror of the LORD fell upon the people, and they came out together as one man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 1:4–5; 2 Samuel 24:9; 2 Chronicles 17:12–19"> And when Saul numbered them at Bezek, there were 300,000 Israelites and 30,000 men of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 18:17"> So they said to the messengers who had come, “Tell the men of Jabesh-gilead: ‘Deliverance will be yours tomorrow by the time the sun is hot.’” And when the messengers relayed this to the men of Jabesh, they rejoiced.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:2–3"> Then the men of Jabesh said to Nahash, “Tomorrow we will come out, and you can do with us whatever seems good to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 7:16; James 2:13; Exodus 14:24"> The next day Saul organized the troops into three divisions, and during the morning watch they invaded the camp of the Ammonites and slaughtered them, until the hottest part of the day. And the survivors were so scattered that no two of them were left together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:27; Luke 19:27; Psalm 21:8"> Then the people said to Samuel, “Who said that Saul should not reign over us? Bring those men here so we can kill them!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:22; 1 Samuel 19:5; Exodus 14:13"> But Saul ordered, “No one shall be put to death this day, for today the LORD has worked salvation in Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:8; 1 Samuel 7:16; 1 Chronicles 12:38–39"> Then Samuel said to the people, “Come, let us go to Gilgal and renew the kingship there.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:8; 1 Samuel 10:17; 1 Chronicles 29:21–24"> So all the people went to Gilgal and confirmed Saul as king in the presence of the LORD. There they sacrificed peace offerings before the LORD, and Saul and all the Israelites rejoiced greatly.</span>
</div>
