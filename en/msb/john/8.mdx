<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:13; Luke 10:25; Ecclesiastes 3:7"> They said this to test Him, in order to have a basis for accusing Him. But Jesus bent down and began to write on the ground with His finger, not taking notice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:1–5; Romans 2:21–25; Romans 2:1–3"> When they continued to question Him, He straightened up and said to them, “Let him who is without sin among you be the first to cast a stone at her.”</span>

<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:11–12"> Then Jesus straightened up and saw no one but the woman. And He asked her, “Where are your accusers? Has no one condemned you?”</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 3:17; John 5:14; Isaiah 1:16–18"> “No one, Lord,” she answered. “Then neither do I condemn you,” Jesus declared. “Go and sin no more.”</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 12:46; John 9:5; John 12:35"> Once again, Jesus spoke to the people and said, “I am the light of the world. Whoever follows Me will never walk in the darkness, but will have the light of life.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 5:31–47"> So the Pharisees said to Him, “You are testifying about Yourself; Your testimony is not valid.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:28; John 13:3; John 8:42"> Jesus replied, “Even if I testify about Myself, My testimony is valid, because I know where I came from and where I am going. But you do not know where I came from or where I am going.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; John 7:24; John 12:47"> You judge according to the flesh; I judge no one.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:31; John 8:29; John 16:32"> But even if I do judge, My judgment is true, because I am not alone; I am with the Father who sent Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:6; Hebrews 10:28"> Even in your own Law it is written that the testimony of two men is valid.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:38; John 8:58; John 8:25"> I am One who testifies about Myself, and the Father, who sent Me, also testifies about Me.”</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:3; John 8:54–55; John 14:6–9"> “Where is Your Father?” they asked Him. “You do not know Me or My Father,” Jesus answered. “If you knew Me, you would know My Father as well.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:30; John 7:8; Mark 12:41"> Jesus spoke these words while teaching in the temple courts, near the treasury. Yet no one seized Him, because His hour had not yet come.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:34; John 8:24; 1 Corinthians 15:17–18"> Again Jesus said to them, “I am going away, and you will look for Me, but you will die in your sin. Where I am going, you cannot come.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 8:52; Psalm 22:6; John 8:48"> So the Jews began to ask, “Will He kill Himself, since He says, ‘Where I am going, you cannot come’?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 3:31; John 17:14; John 17:16"> Then He told them, “You are from below; I am from above. You are of this world; I am not of this world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; Mark 16:16; John 3:18"> That is why I told you that you would die in your sins. For unless you believe that I am He, you will die in your sins.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:17–29; Luke 22:67; John 1:22"> “Who are You?” they asked. “Just what I have been telling you from the beginning,” Jesus replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 15:15; John 12:47–50; John 8:40"> “I have much to say about you and much to judge. But the One who sent Me is truthful, and what I have heard from Him, I tell the world.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; John 8:43; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4"> They did not understand that He was telling them about the Father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 5:19; John 5:30; John 6:38"> So Jesus said, “When you have lifted up the Son of Man, then you will know that I am He, and that I do nothing on My own, but speak exactly what My Father has taught Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:38; John 16:32; John 4:34"> He who sent Me is with Me. The Father has not left Me alone, because I always do what pleases Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 7:31; John 2:23; John 11:45"> As Jesus spoke these things, many believed in Him.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 John 1:9; John 15:4–9; 2 Timothy 3:14"> So He said to the Jews who had believed Him, “If you continue in My word, you are truly My disciples.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 8:2; John 8:36; John 17:17"> Then you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:9; John 8:39; Genesis 15:13"> “We are Abraham’s descendants,” they answered. “We have never been slaves to anyone. How can You say we will be set free?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 6:16; 2 Peter 2:19; Proverbs 5:22"> Jesus replied, “Truly, truly, I tell you, everyone who sins is a slave to sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–7; Luke 15:31; Romans 8:15–17"> A slave does not remain in the house forever, but a son remains forever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Galatians 5:1; Romans 8:2"> So if the Son sets you free, you will be free indeed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 8:45–47; John 7:1"> I know you are Abraham’s descendants, but you are trying to kill Me because My word has no place within you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 5:19; John 8:44; John 8:41"> I speak of what I have seen in the presence of My Father, and you do what you have seen from your father.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; John 8:37; Romans 9:7"> “Abraham is our father,” they replied. “If you were children of Abraham,” said Jesus, “you would do the works of Abraham.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 8:26; Galatians 4:29; Galatians 4:16"> But now you are trying to kill Me, a man who has told you the truth that I heard from God. Abraham never did such a thing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; John 8:38; Isaiah 63:16"> You are doing the works of your father.” “We are not illegitimate children,” they declared. “Our only Father is God Himself.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:49; John 14:10; John 17:8"> Jesus said to them, “If God were your Father, you would love Me, for I have come here from God. I have not come on My own, but He sent Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 12:39–40; Romans 8:7–8; John 7:17"> Why do you not understand what I am saying? It is because you are unable to accept My message.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 13:10; 1 John 3:12; 1 John 3:8–10"> You belong to your father, the devil, and you want to carry out his desires. He was a murderer from the beginning, refusing to stand in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he lies, he speaks his native language, because he is a liar and the father of lies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Galatians 4:16; John 18:37"> But because I speak the truth, you do not believe Me!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; John 16:8; 1 Peter 2:22"> Which of you can prove Me guilty of sin? If I speak the truth, why do you not believe Me?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 10:26–27; 2 John 1:9; John 18:37"> Whoever belongs to God hears the words of God. The reason you do not hear is that you do not belong to God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 7:20; John 8:52; Matthew 10:5"> The Jews answered Him, “Are we not right to say that You are a Samaritan and You have a demon?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Peter 2:23; Isaiah 49:3; John 13:31–32"> “I do not have a demon,” Jesus replied, “but I honor My Father, and you dishonor Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 5:41; John 8:54; John 7:18"> I do not seek My own glory. There is One who seeks it, and He is the Judge.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 5:24; John 3:15–16"> Truly, truly, I tell you, if anyone keeps My word, he will never see death.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 9:24; John 8:48; Hebrews 2:9"> “Now we know that You have a demon!” declared the Jews. “Abraham died, and so did the prophets, yet You say that anyone who keeps Your word will never taste death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 4:12; Romans 9:5; Hebrews 7:1–7"> Are You greater than our father Abraham? He died, as did the prophets. Who do You claim to be?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 8:50; Proverbs 25:27; 2 Peter 1:17"> Jesus answered, “If I glorify Myself, My glory means nothing. The One who glorifies Me is My Father, of whom you say ‘He is our God.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 8:19; John 8:44; John 7:28–29"> You do not know Him, but I know Him. If I said I did not know Him, I would be a liar like you. But I do know Him, and I keep His word.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13; Genesis 22:18; Galatians 3:7–9"> Your father Abraham rejoiced that he would see My day. He saw it and was glad.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> Then the Jews said to Him, “You are not yet fifty years old, and You have seen Abraham?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Exodus 3:14; Colossians 1:17; John 17:5"> “Truly, truly, I tell you,” Jesus declared, “before Abraham was born, I am!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 11:8; John 10:30–33; John 10:39–40"> At this, they picked up stones to throw at Him. But Jesus was hidden and went out of the temple area, going through the midst of them. And so He passed by.</span>
</div>
