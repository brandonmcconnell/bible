<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 56:8; John 11:52; 1 Peter 2:25"> I have other sheep that are not of this fold. I must bring them in as well, and they will listen to My voice. Then there will be one flock and one shepherd.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:20; Mark 3:21; John 8:47–48"> Many of them said, “He is demon-possessed and insane. Why would you listen to Him?”</span>

</div>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 10:38; John 8:58; John 14:11"> “I already told you,” Jesus replied, “but you did not believe. The works I do in My Father’s name testify on My behalf.</span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:47; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; John 12:37–40"> But because you are not My sheep, you refuse to believe, just as I said to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 10:16; John 10:14; Revelation 3:20"> My sheep listen to My voice; I know them, and they follow Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:39–40; John 6:37; John 3:15–16"> I give them eternal life, and they will never perish. No one can snatch them out of My hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:37; John 17:2; John 17:9"> My Father who has given them to Me is greater than all. No one can snatch them out of My Father’s hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:1–2; John 14:9; John 17:21–23"> I and the Father are one.”</span>
</div>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 John 3:12; John 5:36; Psalm 35:12"> But Jesus responded, “I have shown you many good works from My Father. For which of these do you stone Me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 5:18; Leviticus 24:16; John 10:30"> “We are not stoning You for any good work,” said the Jews, “but for blasphemy, because You, who are a man, declare Yourself to be God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalm 82:6–7; Exodus 7:1; Psalm 82:1"> Jesus replied, “Is it not written in your Law: ‘I have said you are gods’?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Romans 13:1; Luke 16:17"> If he called them gods to whom the word of God came—and the Scripture cannot be broken—</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 5:17–18; John 3:17; Jeremiah 1:5"> then what about the One whom the Father sanctified and sent into the world? How then can you accuse Me of blasphemy for stating that I am the Son of God?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 15:24; John 10:25; John 12:37–40"> If I am not doing the works of My Father, then do not believe Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 14:20; John 10:25; John 10:30"> But if I am doing them, even though you do not believe Me, believe the works themselves, so that you may know and believe that the Father is in Me, and I am in Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 7:30; John 8:59; John 7:44"> At this, they tried again to seize Him, but He escaped their grasp.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 1:33–34; John 1:29–30; Mark 1:37"> Many came to Him and said, “Although John never performed a sign, everything he said about this man was true.”</span>
