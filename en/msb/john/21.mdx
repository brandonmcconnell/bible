<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:23; Matthew 28:16"> Later, by the Sea of Tiberias, Jesus again revealed Himself to the disciples. He made Himself known in this way:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:21–22; John 1:45–1; John 4:46"> Simon Peter, Thomas called Didymus, Nathanael from Cana in Galilee, the sons of Zebedee, and two other disciples were together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 5:5; Matthew 4:18–20; 2 Thessalonians 3:7–9"> Simon Peter told them, “I am going fishing.” “We will go with you,” they said. So they went out and immediately got into the boat, but caught nothing that night.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 20:14; Luke 24:15–16; Luke 24:31"> Early in the morning, Jesus stood on the shore, but the disciples did not recognize that it was Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; Philippians 4:11–13; 1 John 2:13"> So He called out to them, “Children, do you have any fish?” “No,” they answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:4–7; Acts 2:41; John 2:5"> He told them, “Cast the net on the right side of the boat, and you will find some.” So they cast it there, and they were unable to haul it in because of the great number of fish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 13:23; John 20:2; Matthew 14:28–29"> Then the disciple whom Jesus loved said to Peter, “It is the Lord!” As soon as Simon Peter heard that it was the Lord, he put on his outer garment (for he had removed it) and jumped into the sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11"> The other disciples came ashore in the boat. They dragged in the net full of fish, for they were not far from land, only about a hundred yards.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 21:13; 1 Kings 19:5–6; John 21:10"> When they landed, they saw a charcoal fire there with fish on it, and some bread.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Jesus told them, “Bring some of the fish you have just caught.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 5:6–8; Acts 2:41"> So Simon Peter went aboard and dragged the net ashore. It was full of large fish, 153, but even with so many, the net was not torn.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:41; Luke 9:45; John 16:19"> “Come, have breakfast,” Jesus said to them. None of the disciples dared to ask Him, “Who are You?” They knew it was the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 21:9; Acts 10:41; Luke 24:42–43"> Jesus then came and took the bread and gave it to them, and He did the same with the fish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 20:26; John 20:19"> This was now the third time that Jesus appeared to His disciples after He was raised from the dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; 1 John 4:19; Romans 15:1"> When they had finished eating, Jesus asked Simon Peter, “Simon son of Jonah, do you love Me more than these?” “Yes, Lord,” he answered, “You know I love You.” Jesus replied, “Feed My lambs.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:2; 1 Peter 2:25"> Jesus asked a second time, “Simon son of Jonah, do you love Me?” “Yes, Lord,” he answered, “You know I love You.” Jesus told him, “Shepherd My sheep.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 13:38; John 16:30; Mark 14:72"> Jesus asked a third time, “Simon son of Jonah, do you love Me?” Peter was deeply hurt that Jesus had asked him a third time, “Do you love Me?” “Lord, You know all things,” he replied. “You know I love You.” Jesus said to him, “Feed My sheep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 13:36; Acts 12:3–4; Acts 21:11"> Truly, truly, I tell you, when you were young, you dressed yourself and walked where you wanted; but when you are old, you will stretch out your hands, and someone else will dress you and lead you where you do not want to go.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Peter 1:14; John 21:22; Matthew 10:38"> Jesus said this to indicate the kind of death by which Peter would glorify God. And after He had said this, He told him, “Follow Me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 21:7; John 20:2; John 21:24"> Peter turned and saw the disciple whom Jesus loved following them. He was the one who had leaned back against Jesus at the supper to ask, “Lord, who is going to betray You?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 13:23–24; Acts 1:6–7; Matthew 24:3–4"> When Peter saw him, he asked, “Lord, what about him?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 2:25; Revelation 3:11; Mark 9:1"> Jesus answered, “If I want him to remain until I return, what is that to you? You follow Me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:29; Job 33:13; Daniel 4:35"> Because of this, the rumor spread among the brothers that this disciple would not die. However, Jesus did not say that he would not die, but only, “If I want him to remain until I return, what is that to you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 19:35; John 15:27; 1 John 5:6"> This is the disciple who testifies to these things and who has written them down. And we know that his testimony is true.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 40:5; John 20:30–31; Psalm 71:15"> There are many more things that Jesus did. If all of them were written down, I suppose that not even the world itself would have space for the books that would be written. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
