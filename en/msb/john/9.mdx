<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Now as Jesus was passing by, He saw a man blind from birth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 13:2; John 9:34; Acts 28:4"> and His disciples asked Him, “Rabbi, who sinned, this man or his parents, that he was born blind?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:40; Ecclesiastes 9:1–2"> Jesus answered, “Neither this man nor his parents sinned, but this happened so that the works of God would be displayed in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:35; Ephesians 5:16; John 4:34"> While it is daytime, I must do the works of Him who sent Me. Night is coming, when no one can work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; Acts 13:47"> While I am in the world, I am the light of the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 8:23; Mark 7:33; Revelation 3:18"> When Jesus had said this, He spit on the ground, made some mud, and applied it to the eyes of the blind man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5; John 9:11; John 11:37"> Then He told him, “Go, wash in the Pool of Siloam” (which means “Sent”). So the man went and washed, and came back seeing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; 1 Samuel 21:11; Luke 18:35"> At this, his neighbors and those who had formerly seen him blind began to ask, “Isn’t this the man who used to sit and beg?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Some claimed that he was, but others said, “He just looks like him.” But the man kept saying, “I am the one.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 9:26; John 9:21; Ecclesiastes 11:5"> “How were your eyes opened?” they asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 9:27; Jeremiah 36:17"> He answered, “The man they call Jesus made some mud and anointed my eyes, and He told me to go to the Pool of Siloam and wash. So I went and washed and received my sight.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 5:11–13; Exodus 2:18–20; John 7:11"> “Where is He?” they asked. “I do not know,” he answered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:57; John 11:46–47; John 12:42"> They brought to the Pharisees the man who had been blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:9; Luke 13:10–17; Matthew 12:1–14"> Now the day on which Jesus had made the mud and opened his eyes was a Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:26–27; John 9:10–11"> So the Pharisees also asked him how he had received his sight. The man answered, “He put mud on my eyes, and I washed, and now I can see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:43; John 10:19; Matthew 12:2"> Because of this, some of the Pharisees said, “This man is not from God, for He does not keep the Sabbath.” But others said, “How can a sinful man perform such signs?” And there was division among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:19; John 6:14; Acts 3:22–26"> So once again they asked the man who had been blind, “What do you say about Him, since it was your eyes He opened?” “He is a prophet,” the man replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 3:15–19; John 5:44; Isaiah 26:11"> The Jews still did not believe that the man had been blind and had received his sight until they summoned his parents</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:8–9; Acts 4:14; Acts 3:10"> and asked, “Is this your son, the one you say was born blind? So how is it that he can now see?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> His parents answered, “We know he is our son, and we know he was born blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But how he can now see or who opened his eyes, we do not know. He is of age. Ask him. He can speak for himself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 7:13; John 16:2; Luke 6:22"> His parents said this because they were afraid of the Jews. For the Jews had already determined that anyone who confessed Jesus as the Christ would be put out of the synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 9:21"> That was why his parents said, “He is old enough. Ask him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 7:19; John 9:16; John 8:49"> So a second time they called for the man who had been blind and said, “Give glory to God! We know that this man is a sinner.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 5:10; John 9:30; John 5:11"> He answered, “Whether He is a sinner I do not know. There is one thing I do know: I was blind, but now I see!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> “What did He do to you?” they asked again. “How did He open your eyes?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:67; John 9:10–15"> He replied, “I already told you, and you did not listen. Why do you want to hear it again? Do you also want to become His disciples?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 2:17; John 7:19; Matthew 27:39"> Then they heaped insults on him and said, “You are His disciple; we are disciples of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:14; Numbers 12:2–7; Acts 7:35"> We know that God spoke to Moses, but we do not know where this man is from.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; Psalm 119:18; Isaiah 29:18"> “That is remarkable indeed!” the man said. “You do not know where He is from, and yet He opened my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 28:9"> We know that God does not listen to sinners, but He does listen to the one who worships Him and does His will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Revelation 16:18; Job 20:4"> Never before has anyone heard of opening the eyes of a man born blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 9:16; John 3:2; Acts 5:38–39"> If this man were not from God, He could do no such thing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 9:22; John 9:2; Isaiah 66:5"> They replied, “You were born in utter sin, and you are instructing us?” And they threw him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 20:31; 1 John 5:13; John 10:36"> When Jesus heard that they had thrown him out, He found the man and said, “Do you believe in the Son of God?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Song of Solomon 5:9; John 1:38"> “Who is He, Sir?” he replied. “Tell me so that I may believe in Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 4:26; Psalm 25:8–9; John 14:21–23"> “You have already seen Him,” Jesus answered. “He is the One speaking with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; Matthew 28:9; Luke 24:52"> “Lord, I believe,” he said. And he worshiped Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:19; John 12:46; Acts 26:18"> Then Jesus declared, “For judgment I have come into this world, so that the blind may see and those who see may become blind.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Matthew 15:12–14; John 7:47–52"> Some of the Pharisees who were with Him heard this, and they asked Him, “Are we blind too?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; John 15:22–24; 1 John 1:8–10"> “If you were blind,” Jesus replied, “you would not be guilty of sin. But since you say, ‘We see,’ your guilt remains.”</span>
