</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 21:37; Luke 22:39; Mark 11:11–12"> Now Judas His betrayer also knew the place, because Jesus had often met there with His disciples.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 18:12; Luke 22:47–53; Psalm 22:12"> So Judas brought a band of soldiers and officers from the chief priests and Pharisees. They arrived at the garden carrying lanterns, torches, and weapons.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 13:1; John 18:7; Psalm 27:3"> Jesus, knowing all that was coming upon Him, stepped forward and asked them, “Whom are you seeking?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; John 19:19; Matthew 2:23"> “Jesus of Nazareth,” they answered. Jesus said, “I am He.” And Judas His betrayer was standing there with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 27:2; Psalm 70:2–3; 2 Kings 1:9–15"> When Jesus said, “I am He,” they drew back and fell to the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 18:4"> So He asked them again, “Whom are you seeking?” “Jesus of Nazareth,” they answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:28; Matthew 26:56; 2 Corinthians 12:9"> “I told you that I am He,” Jesus replied. “So if you are looking for Me, let these men go.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 17:12"> This was to fulfill the word He had spoken: “I have not lost one of those You have given Me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 14:47; Matthew 26:51–54; Luke 22:49–51"> Then Simon Peter drew his sword and struck the servant of the high priest, cutting off his right ear. The servant’s name was Malchus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 20:22; Matthew 26:39; John 20:17"> “Put your sword back in its sheath!” Jesus said to Peter. “Shall I not drink the cup the Father has given Me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:57; John 18:3; Acts 21:37"> Then the band of soldiers, with its commander and the officers of the Jews, arrested Jesus and bound Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 18:24; John 11:51; Matthew 26:3"> They led Him away first to Annas, who was the father-in-law of Caiaphas, the high priest that year.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 11:49–52"> Caiaphas was the one who had advised the Jews that it would be better if one man perished for the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 22:54; Mark 14:54; Matthew 26:58–68"> Now Simon Peter and the other disciple were following Jesus. Since that disciple was known to the high priest, he also went with Jesus into the courtyard of the high priest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:69; Mark 14:66–68; Luke 22:55–57"> But Peter stood outside at the door. Then the disciple who was known to the high priest went out and spoke to the doorkeeper, and brought Peter in.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 26:69–70; John 21:15; Luke 22:56–57"> At this, the servant girl watching the door said to Peter, “Aren’t you also one of this man’s disciples?” “I am not,” he answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 14:54; John 18:25; Mark 14:67"> Because it was cold, the servants and officers were standing around a charcoal fire they had made to keep warm. And Peter was standing with them, warming himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:55–65; Luke 20:20; Luke 22:63–71"> Meanwhile, the high priest questioned Jesus about His disciples and His teaching.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:26; Isaiah 48:16; Matthew 4:23"> “I have spoken openly to the world,” Jesus answered. “I always taught in the synagogues and at the temple, where the Jews always come together. I said nothing in secret.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 26:59–60; Acts 24:12–13; Luke 22:67"> Why are you asking Me? Ask those who heard My message. Surely they know what I said.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 19:3; Mark 14:65; Luke 22:63–64"> When Jesus had said this, one of the officers standing nearby slapped Him in the face and said, “Is this how You answer the high priest?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 2:20–23; 2 Corinthians 10:1; Matthew 5:39"> Jesus replied, “If I said something wrong, testify as to what was wrong. But if I spoke correctly, why did you strike Me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 18:13; Matthew 26:57"> Then Annas sent Him, still bound, to Caiaphas the high priest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 14:67–72; John 18:17–18; Genesis 18:15"> Simon Peter was still standing and warming himself. So they asked him, “Aren’t you also one of His disciples?” He denied it and said, “I am not.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 18:1; John 18:10; Matthew 26:73"> One of the high priest’s servants, a relative of the man whose ear Peter had cut off, asked, “Didn’t I see you with Him in the garden?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 13:38; Mark 14:71–72; Luke 22:60–62"> Peter denied it once more, and immediately a rooster crowed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 18:33; John 19:9; Matthew 27:27"> Then they led Jesus away from Caiaphas into the Praetorium. By now it was early morning, and the Jews did not enter the Praetorium, to avoid being defiled and unable to eat the Passover.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 27:23; Mark 15:2–5; Matthew 27:11–14"> So Pilate went out to them and asked, “What accusation are you bringing against this man?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 3:13; Luke 20:19–26; Mark 10:33"> “If He were not a criminal,” they replied, “we would not have handed Him over to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 19:6–7; John 19:15; Acts 25:18–20"> “You take Him and judge Him by your own law,” Pilate told them. “We are not permitted to execute anyone,” the Jews then replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:14; Matthew 20:19; Matthew 26:2"> This was to fulfill the word that Jesus had spoken to indicate the kind of death He was going to die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 23:3–4; John 18:37; John 19:3"> Pilate went back into the Praetorium, summoned Jesus, and asked Him, “Are You the King of the Jews?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> “Are you saying this on your own,” Jesus asked him, “or did others tell you about Me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 18:14–16; Nehemiah 4:2; Romans 3:1–2"> “Am I a Jew?” Pilate replied. “Your own people and chief priests handed You over to me. What have You done?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 17:20–21; Colossians 1:12–14; Romans 14:17"> Jesus answered, “My kingdom is not of this world; if it were, My servants would fight to prevent My arrest by the Jews. But now My kingdom is not of this realm.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 4:6; John 8:47; John 8:14"> “Then You are a king!” Pilate said. “You say that I am a king,” Jesus answered. “For this reason I was born and have come into the world, to testify to the truth. Everyone who belongs to the truth listens to My voice.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 19:4; Luke 23:4; John 19:6"> “What is truth?” Pilate asked. And having said this, he went out again to the Jews and told them, “I find no basis for a charge against Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:15–18; Luke 23:16–23; Mark 15:6–14"> But it is your custom that I release to you one prisoner at the Passover. So then, do you want me to release to you the King of the Jews?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 23:25; Mark 15:15; Matthew 27:16"> “Not this man,” they all shouted, “but Barabbas!” (Now Barabbas was an insurrectionist.)</span>
</div>
</div>
