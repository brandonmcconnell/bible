<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 14:3–8; John 11:1; Luke 24:50"> Six days before the Passover, Jesus came to Bethany, the hometown of Lazarus who had died, whom He had raised from the dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 22:27; Luke 10:38–42; Revelation 3:20"> So they hosted a dinner for Jesus there. Martha served, and Lazarus was among those reclining at the table with Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; John 11:2; Luke 10:38–39"> Then Mary took about a pint of expensive perfume, made of pure nard, and she anointed Jesus’ feet and wiped them with her hair. And the house was filled with the fragrance of the perfume.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:4; 1 Samuel 17:28–29; John 13:2"> But one of His disciples, Judas Iscariot, son of Simon, who was going to betray Him, asked,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:22; Luke 12:33; Mark 14:4–5"> “Why wasn’t this perfume sold for three hundred denarii and the money given to the poor?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 13:29; Psalm 50:16–20; James 2:6"> Judas did not say this because he cared about the poor, but because he was a thief. As keeper of the money bag, he used to take from what was put into it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:12; Mark 14:6; Matthew 27:57–60"> “Leave her alone,” Jesus replied. “She has kept this perfume in preparation for the day of My burial.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Mark 14:7; Matthew 26:11"> The poor you will always have with you, but you will not always have Me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:14; John 11:43–45; Acts 3:10–11"> Meanwhile a large crowd of Jews learned that Jesus was there. And they came not only because of Him, but also to see Lazarus, whom He had raised from the dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Matthew 2:16; John 11:47–53"> So the chief priests made plans to kill Lazarus as well,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:45; John 12:18; Acts 13:45"> for on account of him many of the Jews were deserting them and believing in Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:7–10; Matthew 21:4–9; Luke 19:35–38"> The next day a great crowd that had come to the feast heard that Jesus was coming to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 118:25–26; Revelation 15:3; Matthew 21:9–11"> They took palm branches and went out to meet Him, crying out: “Hosanna!” “Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord!” “Blessed is the King of Israel!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Mark 11:1–7; Matthew 21:1–7"> Finding a young donkey, Jesus sat on it, as it is written:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Isaiah 62:11; Zephaniah 3:16–17"> “Do not be afraid, O Daughter of Zion. See, your King is coming, seated on the colt of a donkey.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 2:22; John 14:26; John 7:39"> At first His disciples did not understand these things, but after Jesus was glorified they remembered what had been done to Him, and they realized that these very things had also been written about Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 1:2; 1 John 5:9–12; John 19:35"> Meanwhile, many people who had been with Jesus when He called Lazarus from the tomb and raised him from the dead continued to testify.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 12:11"> That is also why the crowd went out to meet Him, because they heard that He had performed this sign.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:6; Psalm 22:27; 1 John 2:2"> Then the Pharisees said to one another, “You can see that this is doing you no good. Look how the whole world has gone after Him!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:35; Isaiah 11:10; Acts 17:4"> Now there were some Greeks among those who went up to worship at the feast.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Luke 19:2–4; John 6:40"> They came to Philip, who was from Bethsaida in Galilee, and requested of him, “Sir, we want to see Jesus.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 10:13–14; Luke 9:49–50; John 1:40–41"> Philip relayed this appeal to Andrew, and both of them went and told Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:31–32; John 17:1–5; Isaiah 60:9"> But Jesus replied, “The hour has come for the Son of Man to be glorified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36–38; Isaiah 53:10–12; Hebrews 2:9–10"> Truly, truly, I tell you, unless a kernel of wheat falls to the ground and dies, it remains only a seed. But if it dies, it bears much fruit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 10:39; Luke 9:23–24; Mark 8:35"> Whoever loves his life will lose it, but whoever hates his life in this world will keep it for eternal life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Samuel 2:30"> If anyone serves Me, he must follow Me; and where I am, My servant will be as well. If anyone serves Me, the Father will honor him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 12:23; Hebrews 5:7; Matthew 26:42"> Now My soul is troubled, and what shall I say? ‘Father, save Me from this hour’? No, it is for this purpose that I have come to this hour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 13:31–32; Matthew 26:42; Revelation 5:9–14"> Father, glorify Your name!” Then a voice came from heaven: “I have glorified it, and I will glorify it again.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:2–5; Revelation 6:1; Revelation 14:2"> The crowd standing there heard it and said that it had thundered. Others said that an angel had spoken to Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:42; John 11:15; John 5:34"> In response, Jesus said, “This voice was not for My benefit, but yours.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 John 5:19; 1 John 3:8"> Now judgment is upon this world; now the prince of this world will be cast out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:14; John 8:28; 1 Peter 2:24"> And I, when I am lifted up from the earth, will draw everyone to Myself.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 18:32; John 21:19"> He said this to indicate the kind of death He was going to die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Isaiah 9:7; Daniel 7:14"> The crowd replied, “We have heard from the Law that the Christ will remain forever. So how can You say that the Son of Man must be lifted up? Who is this Son of Man?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 12:46; John 16:16; Ephesians 5:8"> Then Jesus told them, “For a little while longer, the Light will be with you. Walk while you have the Light, so that darkness will not overtake you. The one who walks in the darkness does not know where he is going.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; John 8:59; Acts 13:47–48"> While you have the Light, believe in the Light, so that you may become sons of light.” After Jesus had spoken these things, He went away and was hidden from them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 16:31; John 1:11; John 15:24"> Although Jesus had performed so many signs in their presence, they still did not believe in Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 10:16; Romans 10:20"> This was to fulfill the word of Isaiah the prophet: “Lord, who has believed our message? And to whom has the arm of the Lord been revealed?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 10:38; John 5:44; 2 Peter 2:14"> For this reason they were unable to believe. For again, Isaiah says:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Matthew 13:13–15; Luke 8:10"> “He has blinded their eyes and hardened their hearts, so that they cannot see with their eyes, and understand with their hearts, and turn, and I would heal them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 6:1–5; Isaiah 6:9–10; John 1:14"> Isaiah said these things when he saw Jesus’ glory and spoke about Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 9:22; John 7:13; Isaiah 51:7"> Nevertheless, many of the leaders believed in Him. But because of the Pharisees they did not confess Him, for fear that they would be put out of the synagogue.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 5:44; Matthew 23:5–7; John 5:41"> For they loved praise from men more than praise from God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:20; John 5:24"> Then Jesus cried out, “Whoever believes in Me does not believe in Me alone, but in the One who sent Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 14:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Colossians 1:15"> And whoever sees Me sees the One who sent Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 8:12; Psalm 36:9; John 9:5"> I have come into the world as a light, so that no one who believes in Me should remain in darkness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:17; John 8:15–16; John 12:48"> As for anyone who hears My words and does not believe, I do not judge him. For I have not come to judge the world, but to save the world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Hebrews 9:27–28; Romans 2:16"> There is a judge for the one who rejects Me and does not receive My words: The word that I have spoken will judge him on the last day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 14:10; John 8:26; John 5:30"> I have not spoken on My own, but the Father who sent Me has commanded Me what to say and how to say it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 17:3; John 6:68; 1 John 5:20"> And I know that His command leads to eternal life. So I speak exactly what the Father has told Me to say.”</span>
</div>
</div>
