<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 8:6; Matthew 6:33; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> Therefore, since you have been raised with Christ, strive for the things above, where Christ is seated at the right hand of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Chronicles 22:19; Colossians 3:1"> Set your minds on things above, not on earthly things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; Romans 6:2; Colossians 2:20"> For you died, and your life is now hidden with Christ in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 11:25; 1 John 3:2; Galatians 2:20"> When Christ, who is our life, appears, then you also will appear with Him in glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 8:13; Galatians 5:19–21; Galatians 5:24"> Put to death, therefore, the components of your earthly nature: sexual immorality, impurity, lust, evil desires, and greed, which is idolatry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 5:6; Romans 1:18; 1 Peter 1:14"> Because of these, the wrath of God is coming on the sons of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:2; 1 Peter 4:3–4; Romans 6:19–20"> When you lived among them, you also used to walk in these ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Ephesians 4:22; James 1:20–21"> But now you must put aside all such things as these: anger, rage, malice, slander, and filthy language from your lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:22; Ephesians 4:25; Leviticus 19:11"> Do not lie to one another, since you have taken off the old self with its practices,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 4:23–24; Romans 12:2; Ephesians 2:10"> and have put on the new self, which is being renewed in knowledge in the image of its Creator.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:28–29; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Romans 10:12"> Here there is no Greek or Jew, circumcised or uncircumcised, barbarian, Scythian, slave, or free, but Christ is all and is in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Ephesians 4:2; 1 John 4:19"> Therefore, as the elect of God, holy and beloved, clothe yourselves with hearts of compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness, and patience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Mark 11:25; James 2:13"> Bear with one another and forgive any complaint you may have against someone else. Forgive as Christ forgave you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; Ephesians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 13:1–13"> And over all these virtues put on love, which is the bond of perfect unity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 4:6–7; John 14:27; Isaiah 26:3"> Let the peace of God rule in your hearts, for to this you were called as members of one body. And be thankful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 119:11; Ephesians 5:19; John 15:7"> Let the word of Christ richly dwell within you as you teach and admonish one another with all wisdom, and as you sing psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs with gratitude in your hearts to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:31; Colossians 3:23; Proverbs 3:6"> And whatever you do, in word or deed, do it all in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God the Father through Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:22–9; Titus 2:4–5; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> Wives, submit to your husbands, as is fitting in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Genesis 2:23–24; Colossians 3:21"> Husbands, love your wives and do not be harsh with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Ephesians 6:1–3; Proverbs 6:20"> Children, obey your parents in everything, for this is pleasing to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1–4; Ephesians 6:4; Psalm 103:13"> Fathers, do not provoke your children, so they will not become discouraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 6:5–7; 1 Timothy 6:1–2; Galatians 1:10"> Slaves, obey your earthly masters in everything, not only to please them while they are watching, but with sincerity of heart and fear of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Ephesians 6:6–7; Romans 14:8"> And whatever you do, work at it with your whole being, as for the Lord and not for men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 6:8; Galatians 1:10; Acts 20:32"> because you know that you will receive an inheritance from the Lord as your reward. For it is the Lord Christ you are serving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 10:34; Romans 2:11; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> But whoever does wrong will be repaid for his wrong, and there is no favoritism.</span>
