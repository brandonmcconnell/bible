<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 1:1; Ephesians 1:1; 2 Corinthians 1:1"> Paul, called to be an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, and our brother Sosthenes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11; 1 Corinthians 1:30"> To the church of God in Corinth, to those sanctified in Christ Jesus and called to be holy, together with all those everywhere who call on the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, their Lord and ours:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 2 Corinthians 1:2; 1 Peter 1:2"> Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:8; John 14:26; 1 Timothy 1:14"> I always thank my God for you because of the grace He has given you in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:11; 2 Corinthians 8:7; Colossians 2:3"> For in Him you have been enriched in every way, in all speech and all knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:2; 2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Timothy 2:6"> because our testimony about Christ was confirmed in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; 2 Peter 3:12; Hebrews 9:28"> Therefore you do not lack any spiritual gift as you eagerly await the revelation of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23–24; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; Colossians 1:22"> He will sustain you to the end, so that you will be blameless on the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; Hebrews 10:23; Deuteronomy 7:9"> God, who has called you into fellowship with His Son Jesus Christ our Lord, is faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:1–4; Ephesians 4:1–7; 1 Corinthians 12:25"> I appeal to you, brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that all of you agree together, so that there may be no divisions among you and that you may be united in mind and conviction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 18:6; 1 Timothy 6:4; 2 Timothy 2:23–25"> My brothers, some from Chloe’s household have informed me that there are quarrels among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:21–23; John 1:42; Matthew 23:9–10"> What I mean is this: Individuals among you are saying, “I follow Paul,” “I follow Apollos,” “I follow Cephas,” or “I follow Christ.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5; 2 Corinthians 11:4; Acts 2:38"> Is Christ divided? Was Paul crucified for you? Were you baptized into the name of Paul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 18:8; Romans 16:23; 1 Corinthians 1:4"> I thank God that I did not baptize any of you except Crispus and Gaius,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:2; John 3:28–29; John 7:18"> so no one can say that I baptized into my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:15; 1 Corinthians 16:17; Acts 16:33"> Yes, I also baptized the household of Stephanas; beyond that I do not remember if I baptized anyone else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:1; 1 Corinthians 2:13; 2 Peter 1:16"> For Christ did not send me to baptize, but to preach the gospel, not with words of wisdom, lest the cross of Christ be emptied of its power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Romans 1:16; 2 Thessalonians 2:10"> For the message of the cross is foolishness to those who are perishing, but to us who are being saved it is the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; Jeremiah 8:9; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> For it is written: “I will destroy the wisdom of the wise; the intelligence of the intelligent I will frustrate.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:22; Job 12:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> Where is the wise man? Where is the scribe? Where is the philosopher of this age? Has not God made foolish the wisdom of this world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 10:21; Romans 1:20–22; Matthew 11:25"> For since in the wisdom of God the world through its wisdom did not know Him, God was pleased through the foolishness of what was preached to save those who believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:1–4; Matthew 12:38–39; Mark 8:11"> Jews demand a sign and Greeks search for wisdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 2:2; 1 Peter 2:8"> but we preach Christ crucified, a stumbling block to Jews and foolishness to Greeks,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Colossians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 1:18"> but to those who are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ the power of God and the wisdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 11:33–36; 1 Corinthians 1:18; Zechariah 4:6–7"> For the foolishness of God is wiser than man’s wisdom, and the weakness of God is stronger than man’s strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 2:5; 1 Corinthians 3:18–20; James 1:9–11"> Brothers, consider the time of your calling: Not many of you were wise by human standards; not many were powerful; not many were of noble birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 2:5; Matthew 11:25; 1 Corinthians 1:20"> But God chose the foolish things of the world to shame the wise; God chose the weak things of the world to shame the strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 34:24; Romans 4:17; Isaiah 2:11"> He chose the lowly and despised things of the world, and the things that are not, to nullify the things that are,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 3:27; Jeremiah 9:23; Ephesians 2:9"> so that no one may boast in His presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Philippians 3:9; Romans 8:1"> It is because of Him that you are in Christ Jesus, who has become for us wisdom from God: our righteousness, holiness, and redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; 2 Corinthians 10:17; Philippians 3:3"> Therefore, as it is written: “Let him who boasts boast in the Lord.”</span>
