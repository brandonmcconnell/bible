<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nahum 2:10; Isaiah 2:19; Isaiah 42:15"> Behold, the LORD lays waste the earth and leaves it in ruins. He will twist its surface and scatter its inhabitants—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 4:9; Lamentations 5:12–14; Ezekiel 7:12–13"> people and priest alike, servant and master, maid and mistress, buyer and seller, lender and borrower, creditor and debtor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 24:1; Isaiah 6:11; Deuteronomy 29:23"> The earth will be utterly laid waste and thoroughly plundered. For the LORD has spoken this word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Isaiah 33:9; Isaiah 3:26"> The earth mourns and withers; the world languishes and fades; the exalted of the earth waste away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Romans 8:20–21; Daniel 7:25"> The earth is defiled by its people; they have transgressed the laws; they have overstepped the decrees and broken the everlasting covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Matthew 7:14; Zechariah 5:3–4"> Therefore a curse has consumed the earth, and its inhabitants must bear the guilt; the earth’s dwellers have been burned, and only a few survive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 16:10; Joel 1:10–12; Isaiah 16:8"> The new wine dries up, the vine withers. All the merrymakers now groan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 2:11; Revelation 18:22; Ezekiel 26:13"> The joyful tambourines have ceased; the noise of revelers has stopped; the joyful harp is silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 5:22; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Isaiah 5:20"> They no longer sing and drink wine; strong drink is bitter to those who consume it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 17:5–6; Isaiah 24:12"> The city of chaos is shattered; every house is closed to entry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 7:14; Isaiah 24:7–9; Jeremiah 48:33"> In the streets they cry out for wine. All joy turns to gloom; rejoicing is exiled from the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Micah 1:12; Lamentations 5:18"> The city is left in ruins; its gate is reduced to rubble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Ezekiel 6:8–11; Ezekiel 11:16–20"> So will it be on the earth and among the nations, like a harvested olive tree, like a gleaning after a grape harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Jeremiah 31:12; Isaiah 54:1"> They raise their voices, they shout for joy; from the west they proclaim the majesty of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 1:11; Isaiah 42:4; Isaiah 60:9"> Therefore glorify the LORD in the east. Extol the name of the LORD, the God of Israel in the islands of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 21:2; Jeremiah 5:11; Isaiah 33:1"> From the ends of the earth we hear singing: “Glory to the Righteous One.” But I said, “I am wasting away! I am wasting away! Woe is me.” The treacherous betray; the treacherous deal in treachery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:43–44; 1 Kings 19:17; Leviticus 26:21–22"> Terror and pit and snare await you, O dweller of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Psalm 18:7; Joshua 10:10–11"> Whoever flees the sound of panic will fall into the pit, and whoever climbs from the pit will be caught in the snare. For the windows of heaven are open, and the foundations of the earth are shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4–10; Deuteronomy 11:6; Revelation 20:11"> The earth is utterly broken apart, the earth is split open, the earth is shaken violently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 19:14; Hosea 4:1–5; Daniel 11:19"> The earth staggers like a drunkard and sways like a shack. Earth’s rebellion weighs it down, and it falls, never to rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 76:12; Isaiah 34:2–17; Zechariah 14:12–19"> In that day the LORD will punish the host of heaven above and the kings of the earth below.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11; Isaiah 42:22; Jeremiah 38:6–13"> They will be gathered together like prisoners in a pit. They will be confined to a dungeon and punished after many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19; Revelation 21:23; Isaiah 13:10"> The moon will be confounded and the sun will be ashamed; for the LORD of Hosts will reign on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, and before His elders with great glory.</span>
