<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 14:23; Isaiah 13:1; Jeremiah 51:42"> This is the burden against the Desert by the Sea: Like whirlwinds sweeping through the Negev, an invader comes from the desert, from a land of terror.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Jeremiah 49:34; Psalm 60:3"> A dire vision is declared to me: “The traitor still betrays, and the destroyer still destroys. Go up, O Elam! Lay siege, O Media! I will put an end to all her groaning.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 13:8; Psalm 48:6; Isaiah 26:17"> Therefore my body is filled with anguish. Pain grips me, like the pains of a woman in labor. I am bewildered to hear, I am dismayed to see.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:34–36; 1 Samuel 25:36–38; Daniel 5:1"> My heart falters; fear makes me tremble. The twilight I desired has turned to horror.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 51:57; Jeremiah 51:39"> They prepare a table, they lay out a carpet, they eat, they drink! Rise up, O princes, oil the shields!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:2–7; Ezekiel 3:17; Habakkuk 2:1–2"> For this is what the Lord says to me: “Go, post a lookout and have him report what he sees.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 21:9; Isaiah 37:24; Hebrews 2:1"> When he sees chariots with teams of horsemen, riders on donkeys, riders on camels, he must be alert, fully alert.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:1–2; Isaiah 56:10; 1 Peter 5:8"> Then the lookout shouted: “Day after day, my lord, I stand on the watchtower; night after night I stay at my post.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 14:8; Jeremiah 51:8"> Look, here come the riders, horsemen in pairs.” And one answered, saying: “Fallen, fallen is Babylon! All the images of her gods lie shattered on the ground!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Micah 4:13; 2 Kings 13:7"> O my people, crushed on the threshing floor, I tell you what I have heard from the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:14; Obadiah 1:1–16; Amos 1:6"> This is the burden against Dumah: One calls to me from Seir, “Watchman, what is left of the night? Watchman, what is left of the night?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:30–32; Acts 2:37–38; Ezekiel 18:30–32"> The watchman replies, “Morning has come, but also the night. If you would inquire, then inquire. Come back yet again.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:23–24; 1 Chronicles 1:32; Ezekiel 27:15"> This is the burden against Arabia: In the thickets of Arabia you must lodge, O caravans of Dedanites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 25:15; Job 6:19; Romans 12:20"> Bring water for the thirsty, O dwellers of Tema; meet the refugees with food.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:19–20; Isaiah 13:14"> For they flee from the sword—the sword that is drawn—from the bow that is bent, and from the stress of battle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 120:5; Isaiah 60:7; Isaiah 16:14"> For this is what the Lord says to me: “Within one year, as a hired worker would count it, all the glory of Kedar will be gone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 1:6; Numbers 23:19; Matthew 24:35"> The remaining archers, the warriors of Kedar, will be few.” For the LORD, the God of Israel, has spoken.</span>
</div>
</div>
