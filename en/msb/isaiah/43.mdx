<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 100:3; Isaiah 43:7; Zechariah 13:9"> But now, this is what the LORD says—He who created you, O Jacob, and He who formed you, O Israel: “Do not fear, for I have redeemed you; I have called you by your name; you are Mine!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Joshua 1:9; Psalm 66:12"> When you pass through the waters, I will be with you; and when you go through the rivers, they will not overwhelm you. When you walk through the fire, you will not be scorched; the flames will not set you ablaze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 45:15; 2 Chronicles 14:9–14"> For I am the LORD your God, the Holy One of Israel, your Savior; I give Egypt for your ransom, Cush and Seba in your place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6–8; 1 Peter 2:9; Jeremiah 31:3"> Because you are precious and honored in My sight, and because I love you, I will give men in exchange for you and nations in place of your life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:3; Isaiah 49:12; Luke 13:29"> Do not be afraid, for I am with you; I will bring your offspring from the east and gather you from the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:17–18; Jeremiah 3:14; Jeremiah 3:18–19"> I will say to the north, ‘Give them up!’ and to the south, ‘Do not hold them back!’ Bring My sons from afar, and My daughters from the ends of the earth—</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Isaiah 43:21; Ephesians 1:12"> everyone called by My name and created for My glory, whom I have indeed formed and made.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; Ezekiel 12:2; Isaiah 42:18–20"> Bring out a people who have eyes but are blind, and who have ears but are deaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 46:10; Isaiah 44:7–9; Isaiah 41:21–26"> All the nations gather together and the peoples assemble. Who among them can declare this, and proclaim to us the former things? Let them present their witnesses to vindicate them, so that others may hear and say, “It is true.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:4; Isaiah 45:6; Isaiah 46:8–9"> “You are My witnesses,” declares the LORD, “and My servant whom I have chosen, so that you may consider and believe Me and understand that I am He. Before Me no god was formed, and after Me none will come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 13:4; Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 45:21–22"> I, yes I, am the LORD, and there is no Savior but Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:12; Psalm 81:9–10; Isaiah 43:10"> I alone decreed and saved and proclaimed—I, and not some foreign god among you. So you are My witnesses,” declares the LORD, “that I am God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 14:27; Isaiah 46:10; Psalm 90:2"> Even from eternity I am He, and none can deliver out of My hand. When I act, who can reverse it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 23:13; Jeremiah 51:24"> Thus says the LORD your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel: “For your sake, I will send to Babylon and bring them all as fugitives, even the Chaldeans, in the ships in which they rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 43:3; Matthew 25:34; Isaiah 48:17"> I am the LORD, your Holy One, the Creator of Israel, and your King.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 51:10; Isaiah 43:2; Psalm 77:19"> Thus says the LORD, who makes a way in the sea and a path through the surging waters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:4–9; Isaiah 1:31; Ezekiel 38:8–18"> who brings out the chariots and horses, the armies and warriors together, to lie down, never to rise again; to be extinguished, snuffed out like a wick:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; 2 Corinthians 3:10; 1 Chronicles 16:12"> “Do not call to mind the former things; pay no attention to the things of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 48:6; Isaiah 42:9; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> Behold, I am about to do something new; even now it is coming. Do you not see it? Indeed, I will make a way in the wilderness and streams in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:17; Isaiah 48:21; Isaiah 49:10"> The beasts of the field will honor Me, the jackals and the ostriches, because I provide water in the wilderness and rivers in the desert, to give drink to My chosen people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Hebrews 13:15; Psalm 102:18"> The people I formed for Myself will declare My praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 6:3; Malachi 3:14; Malachi 1:13"> But you have not called on Me, O Jacob, because you have grown weary of Me, O Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Amos 5:25; Zechariah 7:5–6; Malachi 3:8"> You have not brought Me sheep for burnt offerings, nor honored Me with your sacrifices. I have not burdened you with offerings, nor wearied you with frankincense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 7:13; Jeremiah 6:20; Isaiah 1:14"> You have not bought Me sweet cane with your silver, nor satisfied Me with the fat of your sacrifices. But you have burdened Me with your sins; you have wearied Me with your iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:12; Micah 7:18–19; Hebrews 10:17"> I, yes I, am He who blots out your transgressions for My own sake and remembers your sins no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Isaiah 43:9; Romans 8:33"> Remind Me, let us argue the matter together. State your case, so that you may be vindicated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 28:7; Jeremiah 5:31; Ezekiel 22:25–28"> Your first father sinned, and your spokesmen rebelled against Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; Isaiah 65:15; Zechariah 8:13"> So I will disgrace the princes of your sanctuary, and I will devote Jacob to destruction and Israel to reproach.”</span>
