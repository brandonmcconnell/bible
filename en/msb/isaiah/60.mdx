<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 61:6; Isaiah 23:18; Isaiah 60:11"> Then you will look and be radiant, and your heart will tremble and swell with joy, because the riches of the sea will be brought to you, and the wealth of the nations will come to you.</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Genesis 25:13; Hebrews 13:15–16"> All the flocks of Kedar will be gathered to you; the rams of Nebaioth will serve you and go up on My altar with acceptance; I will adorn My glorious house.</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 61:5; Isaiah 49:23; Revelation 21:26"> Foreigners will rebuild your walls, and their kings will serve you. Although I struck you in anger, yet in favor I will show you mercy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 21:25–26; Isaiah 60:5; Isaiah 60:18"> Your gates will always stand open; they will never be shut, day or night, so that the wealth of the nations may be brought into you, with their kings being led in procession.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 14:12–19; Isaiah 54:15; Psalm 2:12"> For the nation or kingdom that will not serve you will perish; it will be utterly destroyed.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2; Isaiah 35:2; Psalm 132:7"> The glory of Lebanon will come to you—its cypress, elm, and boxwood together—to adorn the place of My sanctuary, and I will glorify the place of My feet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Isaiah 49:23; Hebrews 12:22"> The sons of your oppressors will come and bow down to you; all who reviled you will fall facedown at your feet and call you the City of the LORD, Zion of the Holy One of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 61:7; Jeremiah 30:17; Isaiah 49:14–23"> Whereas you have been forsaken and despised, with no one passing through, I will make you an everlasting pride, a joy from age to age.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Isaiah 66:11–12; Isaiah 66:14"> You will drink the milk of nations and nurse at the breasts of royalty; you will know that I, the LORD, am your Savior and your Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 11:40; Isaiah 30:26; Isaiah 1:26"> Instead of bronze I will bring you gold; I will bring silver in place of iron, bronze instead of wood, and iron instead of stones. I will appoint peace as your governor and righteousness as your ruler.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 26:1; Isaiah 11:9; Zechariah 9:8"> No longer will violence be heard in your land, nor ruin or destruction within your borders. But you will name your walls Salvation and your gates Praise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 21:23; Revelation 22:5; Zechariah 2:5"> No longer will the sun be your light by day, nor the brightness of the moon shine on your night; for the LORD will be your everlasting light, and your God will be your splendor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Psalm 84:11; Isaiah 35:10"> Your sun will no longer set, and your moon will not wane; for the LORD will be your everlasting light, and the days of your sorrow will cease.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Psalm 37:22; Psalm 37:11"> Then all your people will be righteous; they will possess the land forever; they are the branch of My planting, the work of My hands, so that I may be glorified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; Hebrews 10:36; Luke 18:7"> The least of you will become a thousand, and the smallest a mighty nation. I am the LORD; in its time I will accomplish it quickly.</span>
