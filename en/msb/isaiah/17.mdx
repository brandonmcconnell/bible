<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 25:2; Zechariah 9:1; Amos 1:3–5"> This is the burden against Damascus: “Behold, Damascus is no longer a city; it has become a heap of ruins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:5; Jeremiah 7:33; Numbers 32:34"> The cities of Aroer are forsaken; they will be left to the flocks, which will lie down with no one to fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 9:11; Isaiah 8:4; Isaiah 7:16"> The fortress will disappear from Ephraim, and the sovereignty from Damascus. The remnant of Aram will be like the splendor of the Israelites,” declares the LORD of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16; Deuteronomy 32:15–27; Isaiah 24:13"> “In that day the splendor of Jacob will fade, and the fat of his body will waste away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Joel 3:13; Jeremiah 51:33"> as the reaper gathers the standing grain and harvests the ears with his arm, as one gleans heads of grain in the Valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 24:13; Deuteronomy 4:27; Romans 9:27"> Yet gleanings will remain, like an olive tree that has been beaten—two or three berries atop the tree, four or five on its fruitful branches,” declares the LORD, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 7:7; Hosea 6:1; Hosea 3:5"> In that day men will look to their Maker and turn their eyes to the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 30:22; Isaiah 27:9; Isaiah 2:8"> They will not look to the altars they have fashioned with their hands or to the Asherahs and incense altars they have made with their fingers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 3:11–15; Isaiah 27:10; Isaiah 24:1–12"> In that day their strong cities will be like forsaken thickets and summits, abandoned to the Israelites and to utter desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 106:21; Deuteronomy 32:18; Isaiah 26:4"> For you have forgotten the God of your salvation and failed to remember the Rock of your refuge. Therefore, though you cultivate delightful plots and set out cuttings from exotic vines—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 4:8; Hosea 8:7; Galatians 6:7–8"> though on the day you plant you make them grow, and on that morning you help your seed sprout—yet the harvest will vanish on the day of disease and incurable pain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:23; Luke 21:25; Psalm 18:4"> Alas, the tumult of many peoples; they rage like the roaring seas and clamoring nations; they rumble like the crashing of mighty waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 9:5; Psalm 1:4; Isaiah 41:15–16"> The nations rage like the rush of many waters. He rebukes them, and they flee far away, driven before the wind like chaff on the hills, like tumbleweeds before a gale.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 19:35; Proverbs 22:23; Jeremiah 13:25"> In the evening, there is sudden terror! Before morning, they are no more! This is the portion of those who loot us and the lot of those who plunder us.</span>
