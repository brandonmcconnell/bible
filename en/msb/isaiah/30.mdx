<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="msb" data-translation-id="eng_msb" data-translation-label="MSB" data-book="ISA" data-book-slug="isaiah" data-book-name="Isaiah" data-chapter="30" data-verses="33">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 65:2; Isaiah 1:2; Hosea 7:13"> “Woe to the rebellious children,” declares the LORD, “to those who carry out a plan that is not Mine, who form an alliance, but against My will, heaping up sin upon sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Numbers 27:21; Deuteronomy 28:68"> They set out to go down to Egypt without asking My advice, to seek shelter under Pharaoh’s protection and take refuge in Egypt’s shade.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 20:5; Romans 10:11; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> But Pharaoh’s protection will become your shame, and the refuge of Egypt’s shade your disgrace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Isaiah 57:9; Jeremiah 43:7"> For though their princes are at Zoan and their envoys have arrived in Hanes,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:36; Isaiah 30:7; Isaiah 30:16"> everyone will be put to shame because of a people useless to them. They bring neither help nor benefit, but only shame and disgrace.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:15; Isaiah 46:1–2; Jeremiah 11:4"> This is the burden against the beasts of the Negev: Through a land of hardship and distress, of lioness and lion, of viper and flying serpent, they carry their wealth on the backs of donkeys and their treasures on the humps of camels, to a people of no profit to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:22; Isaiah 7:4; Psalm 118:8–9"> Egypt’s help is futile and empty; therefore I have called her Rahab Who Sits Still.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:22; Habakkuk 2:2; Isaiah 8:1"> Go now, write it on a tablet in their presence and inscribe it on a scroll; it will be for the days to come, a witness forever and ever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 30:1; Acts 7:51; Zechariah 1:4–6"> These are rebellious people, deceitful children, children unwilling to obey the LORD’s instruction.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:7–10; Romans 16:18; Amos 2:12"> They say to the seers, “Stop seeing visions!” and to the prophets, “Do not prophesy to us the truth! Speak to us pleasant words; prophesy illusions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:7; Romans 1:30; John 15:23–24"> Get out of the way; turn off the road. Rid us of the Holy One of Israel!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 31:1–3; Isaiah 5:24; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> Therefore this is what the Holy One of Israel says: “Because you have rejected this message, trusting in oppression and relying on deceit,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 29:5; Ezekiel 13:10–15; 1 Kings 20:30"> this iniquity of yours is like a breach about to fail, a bulge in a high wall, whose collapse will come suddenly—in an instant!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 2:9; Jeremiah 19:10–11; 2 Peter 2:4–5"> It will break in pieces like a potter’s jar, shattered so that no fragment can be found. Not a shard will be found in the dust large enough to scoop the coals from a hearth or to skim the water from a cistern.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 26:3–4; Isaiah 32:17; Jeremiah 3:22–23"> For the Lord GOD, the Holy One of Israel, has said: “By repentance and rest you would be saved; your strength would lie in quiet confidence—but you were not willing.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 31:1; Isaiah 31:3; Deuteronomy 28:25"> “No,” you say, “we will flee on horses.” Therefore you will flee! “We will ride swift horses,” but your pursuers will be faster.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:25; Joshua 23:10; Deuteronomy 32:30"> A thousand will flee at the threat of one; at the threat of five you will all flee, until you are left alone like a pole on a mountaintop, like a banner on a hill.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; 2 Peter 3:9; Proverbs 16:20"> Therefore the LORD longs to be gracious to you; therefore He rises to show you compassion, for the LORD is a just God. Blessed are all who wait for Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 60:20; Psalm 50:15; Isaiah 65:24"> O people in Zion who dwell in Jerusalem, you will weep no more. He will surely be gracious when you cry for help; when He hears, He will answer you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 22:27; Psalm 80:5; Amos 8:11–12"> The Lord will give you the bread of adversity and the water of affliction, but your Teacher will no longer hide Himself—with your own eyes you will see Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 32:8; Proverbs 3:5–6; Isaiah 58:11"> And whether you turn to the right or to the left, your ears will hear this command behind you: “This is the way. Walk in it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 17:3–4; Isaiah 31:7; Zechariah 13:2"> So you will desecrate your silver-plated idols and your gold-plated images. You will throw them away like menstrual cloths, saying to them, “Be gone!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 8:11–12; Isaiah 32:20; Psalm 65:9–13"> Then He will send rain for the seed that you have sown in the ground, and the food that comes from your land will be rich and plentiful. On that day your cattle will graze in open pastures.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 45:6; Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Samuel 8:12"> The oxen and donkeys that work the ground will eat salted fodder, winnowed with shovel and pitchfork.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 43:19–20; Isaiah 35:6–7; Isaiah 2:14–15"> And from every high mountain and every raised hill, streams of water will flow in the day of great slaughter, when the towers fall.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Deuteronomy 32:39; Revelation 22:5"> The light of the moon will be as bright as the sun, and the light of the sun will be seven times brighter—like the light of seven days—on the day that the LORD binds up the brokenness of His people and heals the wounds He has inflicted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:8; Nahum 1:5–6; Psalm 18:7–9"> Behold, the Name of the LORD comes from afar, with burning anger and dense smoke. His lips are full of fury, and His tongue is like a consuming fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Isaiah 11:4; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> His breath is like a rushing torrent that rises to the neck. He comes to sift the nations in a sieve of destruction; He bridles the jaws of the peoples to lead them astray.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalm 42:4; Psalm 95:1–2; Exodus 15:1–21"> You will sing as on the night of a holy festival, and your heart will rejoice like one who walks to the music of a flute, going up to the mountain of the LORD, to the Rock of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalm 18:13–14; Joshua 10:11; Isaiah 29:6"> And the LORD will cause His majestic voice to be heard and His mighty arm to be revealed, striking in angry wrath with a flame of consuming fire, and with cloudburst, storm, and hailstones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 11:4; Isaiah 9:4; Micah 5:5–6"> For Assyria will be shattered at the voice of the LORD; He will strike them with His scepter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 11:15; Isaiah 19:16; Isaiah 2:19"> And with every stroke of the rod of punishment that the LORD brings down on them, the tambourines and lyres will sound as He battles with weapons brandished.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 23:10; Genesis 19:24; Jeremiah 19:6"> For Topheth has long been prepared; it has been made ready for the king. Its funeral pyre is deep and wide, with plenty of fire and wood. The breath of the LORD, like a torrent of burning sulfur, sets it ablaze.</span>
</div>
</div>
