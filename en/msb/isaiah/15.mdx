<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 2:1–3; Jeremiah 48:1–47; Isaiah 11:14"> This is the burden against Moab: Ar in Moab is ruined, destroyed in a night! Kir in Moab is devastated, destroyed in a night!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:1; Leviticus 21:5; Isaiah 3:24"> Dibon goes up to its temple to weep at its high places. Moab wails over Nebo, as well as over Medeba. Every head is shaved, every beard is cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jonah 3:6–8; Jeremiah 19:13; Deuteronomy 22:8"> In its streets they wear sackcloth; on the rooftops and in the public squares they all wail, falling down weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:34; 1 Kings 19:4; Jonah 4:8"> Heshbon and Elealeh cry out; their voices are heard as far as Jahaz. Therefore the soldiers of Moab cry out; their souls tremble within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:5; Jeremiah 4:20; Luke 19:41–44"> My heart cries out over Moab; her fugitives flee as far as Zoar, as far as Eglath-shelishiyah. With weeping they ascend the slope of Luhith; they lament their destruction on the road to Horonaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 1:10–12; Isaiah 19:5–7; Numbers 32:36"> The waters of Nimrim are dried up, and the grass is withered; the vegetation is gone, and the greenery is no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:36; Nahum 2:12–13; Isaiah 10:6"> So they carry their wealth and belongings over the Brook of the Willows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:10; Isaiah 15:2–5; Jeremiah 48:31–34"> For their outcry echoes to the border of Moab. Their wailing reaches Eglaim; it is heard in Beer-elim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 17:25; Leviticus 26:28; Leviticus 26:24"> The waters of Dimon are full of blood, but I will bring more upon Dimon—a lion upon the fugitives of Moab and upon the remnant of the land.</span>
