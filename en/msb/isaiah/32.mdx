<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; Jeremiah 33:15; Hebrews 1:8–9"> Behold, a king will reign in righteousness, and princes will rule with justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; Isaiah 44:3; Isaiah 9:6"> Each will be like a shelter from the wind, a refuge from the storm, like streams of water in a dry land, like the shadow of a great rock in an arid land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 29:18; Acts 26:18; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> Then the eyes of those who see will no longer be closed, and the ears of those who hear will listen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 29:24; Luke 21:14–15; Matthew 16:17"> The mind of the rash will know and understand, and the stammering tongue will speak clearly and fluently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:20; Psalm 15:4; Malachi 3:18"> No longer will a fool be called noble, nor a scoundrel be respected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; Matthew 23:13; Isaiah 3:15"> For a fool speaks foolishness; his mind plots iniquity. He practices ungodliness and speaks falsely about the LORD; he leaves the hungry empty and deprives the thirsty of drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26–28; Matthew 26:59–60; Matthew 26:14–16"> The weapons of the scoundrel are destructive; he hatches plots to destroy the poor with lies, even when the plea of the needy is just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:2; Acts 11:29–30; Luke 6:33–35"> But a noble man makes honorable plans; he stands up for worthy causes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 13:9; Isaiah 3:16; Isaiah 28:23"> Stand up, you complacent women; listen to me. Give ear to my word, you overconfident daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 7:23; Jeremiah 8:13; Zephaniah 1:13"> In a little more than a year you will tremble, O secure ones. For the grape harvest will fail and the fruit harvest will not arrive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 3:24; Isaiah 33:14; Isaiah 20:4"> Shudder, you ladies of leisure; tremble, you daughters of complacency. Strip yourselves bare and put sackcloth around your waists.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:15; Ezekiel 20:6; Nahum 2:7"> Beat your breasts for the pleasant fields, for the fruitful vines,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 22:2; Hosea 9:6; Isaiah 7:23"> and for the land of my people, overgrown with thorns and briers—even for every house of merriment in this city of revelry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 24:12; Isaiah 24:10; Isaiah 25:2"> For the palace will be forsaken, the busy city abandoned. The hill and the watchtower will become caves forever—the delight of wild donkeys and a pasture for flocks—</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 29:17; Ezekiel 39:29; Isaiah 44:3"> until the Spirit is poured out upon us from on high. Then the desert will be an orchard, and the orchard will seem like a forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 94:14–15; 1 Peter 2:9–12; Isaiah 60:21"> Then justice will inhabit the wilderness, and righteousness will dwell in the fertile field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 26:3; Psalm 119:165; Philippians 4:6–9"> The work of righteousness will be peace; the service of righteousness will be quiet confidence forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 2:5; Hosea 2:18–23; Jeremiah 33:16"> Then my people will dwell in a peaceful place, in safe and secure places of rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:17; Isaiah 30:30; Isaiah 28:2"> But hail will level the forest, and the city will sink to the depths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:1; Isaiah 30:23–24; Isaiah 55:10–11"> Blessed are those who sow beside abundant waters, who let the ox and donkey range freely.</span>
