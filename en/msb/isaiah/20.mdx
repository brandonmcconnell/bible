<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; Numbers 14:34; Ezekiel 4:5–6"> Then the LORD said, “Just as My servant Isaiah has gone naked and barefoot for three years as a sign and omen against Egypt and Cush,</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 30:5; Isaiah 30:3; 2 Kings 18:21"> Those who made Cush their hope and Egypt their boast will be dismayed and ashamed.</span>
