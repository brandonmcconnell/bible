</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 34:8; Jeremiah 51:6; Isaiah 35:4"> For the day of vengeance was in My heart, and the year of My redemption had come.</span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 14:10; Revelation 16:19; Isaiah 49:26"> I trampled the nations in My anger; in My wrath I made them drunk and poured out their blood on the ground.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4; Psalm 86:5; Psalm 86:15"> I will make known the LORD’s loving devotion and His praiseworthy acts, because of all that the LORD has done for us—the many good things for the house of Israel according to His great compassion and loving devotion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 17:7; Isaiah 12:2; Exodus 6:7"> For He said, “They are surely My people, sons who will not be disloyal.” So He became their Savior.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Exodus 23:20–21; Malachi 3:1"> In all their distress, He too was afflicted, and the Angel of His Presence saved them. In His love and compassion He redeemed them; He lifted them up and carried them all the days of old.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Ephesians 4:30; Psalm 78:56"> But they rebelled and grieved His Holy Spirit. So He turned and became their enemy, and He Himself fought against them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Exodus 14:22; Psalm 77:20"> Then His people remembered the days of old, the days of Moses. Where is He who brought them through the sea with the shepherds of His flock? Where is the One who set His Holy Spirit among them,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:16; Psalm 78:13; Joshua 3:16"> who sent His glorious arm to lead them by the right hand of Moses, who divided the waters before them to gain for Himself everlasting renown,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Psalm 106:9; Habakkuk 3:15"> who led them through the depths like a horse in the wilderness, so that they did not stumble?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 63:12; Hebrews 4:8–11; Joshua 23:1"> Like cattle going down to the valley, the Spirit of the LORD gave them rest. You led Your people this way to make for Yourself a glorious name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:20; Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalm 80:14"> Look down from heaven and see, from Your holy and glorious habitation. Where are Your zeal and might? Your yearning and compassion for me are restrained.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Malachi 2:10; Jeremiah 3:19"> Yet You are our Father, though Abraham does not know us and Israel does not acknowledge us. You, O LORD, are our Father; our Redeemer from Everlasting is Your name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Joshua 11:20; Ezekiel 14:7–9"> Why, O LORD, do You make us stray from Your ways and harden our hearts from fearing You? Return, for the sake of Your servants, the tribes of Your heritage.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 74:3–7; Revelation 11:2; Deuteronomy 7:6"> For a short while Your people possessed Your holy place, but our enemies have trampled Your sanctuary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 135:4; Acts 14:16; Romans 9:4"> We have become like those You never ruled, like those not called by Your name.</span>
