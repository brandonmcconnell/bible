<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 102:13; Zechariah 1:17; Zechariah 8:22–23"> For the LORD will have compassion on Jacob; once again He will choose Israel and settle them in their own land. The foreigner will join them and unite with the house of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 61:5; Isaiah 60:14; Jeremiah 30:16"> The nations will escort Israel and bring it to its homeland. Then the house of Israel will possess the nations as menservants and maidservants in the LORD’s land. They will make captives of their captors and rule over their oppressors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:10; Isaiah 32:18; Ezra 9:8–9"> On the day that the LORD gives you rest from your pain and torment, and from the hard labor into which you were forced,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Habakkuk 1:2–10; Jeremiah 51:34–35"> you will sing this song of contempt against the king of Babylon: How the oppressor has ceased, and how his fury has ended!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 125:3; Isaiah 9:4; Jeremiah 48:15–17"> The LORD has broken the staff of the wicked, the scepter of the rulers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 7:19–21; James 2:13; Revelation 17:16–17"> It struck the peoples in anger with unceasing blows; it subdued the nations in rage with relentless persecution.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 126:1–3; Psalm 98:7–9; Psalm 96:11–13"> All the earth is at peace and at rest; they break out in song.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:16; Isaiah 55:12–13; Zechariah 11:2"> Even the cypresses and cedars of Lebanon exult over you: “Since you have been laid low, no woodcutter comes against us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:21–32; Jeremiah 50:8; Proverbs 15:24"> Sheol beneath is eager to meet you upon your arrival. It stirs the spirits of the dead to greet you—all the rulers of the earth. It makes all the kings of the nations rise from their thrones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 49:6–14; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Psalm 82:6–7"> They will all respond to you, saying, “You too have become weak, as we are; you have become like us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 5:25–30; Job 21:11–15; Isaiah 66:24"> Your pomp has been brought down to Sheol, along with the music of your harps. Maggots are your bed and worms your blanket.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13–17; Revelation 12:7–10; Luke 10:18"> How you have fallen from heaven, O day star, son of the dawn! You have been cut down to the ground, O destroyer of nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 8:10–12; Ezekiel 28:2; Ezekiel 28:12–16"> You said in your heart: “I will ascend to the heavens; I will raise my throne above the stars of God. I will sit on the mount of assembly, in the far reaches of the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 47:8; Genesis 3:5; 2 Thessalonians 2:4"> I will ascend above the tops of the clouds; I will make myself like the Most High.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 11:23; Ezekiel 32:23; Acts 12:22–23"> But you will be brought down to Sheol, to the lowest depths of the Pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:23; Psalm 58:10–11; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> Those who see you will stare; they will ponder your fate: “Is this the man who shook the earth and made the kingdoms tremble,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:3; Isaiah 13:19–22; Isaiah 45:13"> who turned the world into a desert and destroyed its cities, who refused to let the captives return to their homes?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:25; Isaiah 22:16; Ezekiel 32:18–32"> All the kings of the nations lie in state, each in his own tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:9; Jeremiah 41:7; 1 Kings 21:24"> But you are cast out of your grave like a rejected branch, covered by those slain with the sword, and dumped into a rocky pit like a carcass trampled underfoot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 21:10; Job 18:19; Psalm 37:28"> You will not join them in burial, since you have destroyed your land and slaughtered your own people. The offspring of the wicked will never again be mentioned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 23:35; Exodus 20:5; Habakkuk 2:8–12"> Prepare a place to slaughter his sons for the iniquities of their forefathers. They will never rise up to possess a land or cover the earth with their cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:29–35; Proverbs 10:7; 1 Kings 14:10"> “I will rise up against them,” declares the LORD of Hosts. “I will cut off from Babylon her name and her remnant, her offspring and her posterity,” declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:14; Isaiah 13:21–22; 1 Kings 14:10"> “I will make her a place for owls and for swamplands; I will sweep her away with the broom of destruction,” declares the LORD of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Job 23:13; Isaiah 46:10–11"> The LORD of Hosts has sworn: “Surely, as I have planned, so will it be; as I have purposed, so will it stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Nahum 1:13; Isaiah 37:36–38"> I will break Assyria in My land; I will trample him on My mountain. His yoke will be taken off My people, and his burden removed from their shoulders.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 23:9; Zephaniah 3:6–8; Exodus 15:12"> This is the plan devised for the whole earth, and this is the hand stretched out over all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 43:13; Job 23:13; 2 Chronicles 20:6"> The LORD of Hosts has purposed, and who can thwart Him? His hand is outstretched, so who can turn it back?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 16:20; Isaiah 13:1; 2 Chronicles 28:27"> In the year that King Ahaz died, this burden was received:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 30:6; Isaiah 11:8; 2 Chronicles 26:6"> Do not rejoice, all you Philistines, that the rod that struck you is broken. For a viper will spring from the root of the snake, and a flying serpent from its egg.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 7:21–22; Zephaniah 2:4–7; Isaiah 5:17"> Then the firstborn of the poor will find pasture, and the needy will lie down in safety, but I will kill your root by famine, and your remnant will be slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:14; Isaiah 20:1; Isaiah 14:29"> Wail, O gate! Cry out, O city! Melt away, all you Philistines! For a cloud of smoke comes from the north, and there are no stragglers in its ranks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalm 102:16; Psalm 87:5; James 2:5"> What answer will be given to the envoys of that nation? “The LORD has founded Zion, where His afflicted people will find refuge.”</span>
