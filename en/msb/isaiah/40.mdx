</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Mark 1:2–5; John 1:23"> A voice of one calling: “Prepare the way for the LORD in the wilderness; make a straight highway for our God in the desert.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 3:5; Ezekiel 21:26; Luke 18:14"> Every valley shall be lifted up, and every mountain and hill made low; the uneven ground will become smooth, and the rugged land a plain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Joel 2:28; Isaiah 52:10"> And the glory of the LORD will be revealed, and all humanity together will see it.” For the mouth of the LORD has spoken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 103:15–16; 1 Peter 1:24–25; James 1:10–11"> A voice says, “Cry out!” And I asked, “What should I cry out?” “All flesh is like grass, and all its glory like the flowers of the field.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 41:21"> The grass withers and the flowers fall when the breath of the LORD blows on them; indeed, the people are grass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 13:31; 1 Peter 1:25; Matthew 24:35"> The grass withers and the flowers fall, but the word of our God stands forever.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7–8; Isaiah 12:2; Isaiah 25:9"> Go up on a high mountain, O Zion, herald of good news. Raise your voice loudly, O Jerusalem, herald of good news. Lift it up, do not be afraid! Say to the cities of Judah, “Here is your God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:12; Isaiah 62:11; Revelation 17:14"> Behold, the Lord GOD comes with might, and His arm establishes His rule. His reward is with Him, and His recompense accompanies Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 7:17; Micah 5:4; Psalm 23:1–6"> He tends His flock like a shepherd; He gathers the lambs in His arms and carries them close to His heart. He gently leads the nursing ewes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Hebrews 1:10–12; Psalm 102:25–26"> Who has measured the waters in the hollow of his hand, or marked off the heavens with the span of his hand? Who has held the dust of the earth in a basket, or weighed the mountains on a scale and the hills with a balance?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Romans 11:34; Job 21:22"> Who has directed the Spirit of the LORD, or informed Him as His counselor?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 21:22; Colossians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 12:4–6"> Whom did He consult to enlighten Him, and who taught Him the paths of justice? Who imparted knowledge to Him and showed Him the way of understanding?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Job 34:14–15; Jeremiah 10:10"> Surely the nations are like a drop in a bucket; they are considered a speck of dust on the scales; He lifts up the islands like fine dust.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; Psalm 40:6; Psalm 50:9–12"> Lebanon is not sufficient for fuel, nor its animals enough for a burnt offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 62:9; Daniel 4:34–35; Isaiah 29:7"> All the nations are as nothing before Him; He regards them as nothingness and emptiness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 46:5; 1 Samuel 2:2; Isaiah 40:25"> To whom will you liken God? To what image will you compare Him?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:9; Habakkuk 2:18–19; Isaiah 46:6–7"> To an idol that a craftsman casts and a metalworker overlays with gold and fits with silver chains?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:3–4; Isaiah 41:7; Isaiah 46:7"> One lacking such an offering chooses wood that will not rot. He seeks a skilled craftsman to set up an idol that will not topple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 48:13; Psalm 50:6; Acts 14:17"> Do you not know? Have you not heard? Has it not been declared to you from the beginning? Have you not understood since the foundation of the earth?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 8:27; Psalm 104:2; Isaiah 42:5"> He sits enthroned above the circle of the earth; its dwellers are like grasshoppers. He stretches out the heavens like a curtain, and spreads them out like a tent to dwell in.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 12:21; Psalm 107:40; Isaiah 34:12"> He brings the princes to nothing and makes the rulers of the earth meaningless.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 41:16; Isaiah 17:13; Haggai 1:9"> No sooner are they planted, no sooner are they sown, no sooner have their stems taken root in the ground, than He blows on them and they wither, and a whirlwind sweeps them away like stubble.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 40:18; Deuteronomy 4:33; Deuteronomy 4:15–18"> “To whom will you liken Me, or who is My equal?” asks the Holy One.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 1:16–17; Jeremiah 32:17–19; Isaiah 48:13"> Lift up your eyes on high: Who created all these? He leads forth the starry host by number; He calls each one by name. Because of His great power and mighty strength, not one of them is missing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 18:7–8; Isaiah 49:4; Isaiah 49:14"> Why do you say, O Jacob, and why do you assert, O Israel, “My way is hidden from the LORD, and my claim is ignored by my God”?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 11:33–34; Psalm 147:5; Isaiah 55:8–9"> Do you not know? Have you not heard? The LORD is the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth. He will not grow tired or weary; His understanding is beyond searching out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> He gives power to the faint and increases the strength of the weak.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Amos 2:14; Psalm 34:10; Psalm 33:16"> Even youths grow tired and weary, and young men stumble and fall.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Psalm 27:13–14"> But those who wait upon the LORD will renew their strength; they will mount up with wings like eagles; they will run and not grow weary, they will walk and not faint.</span>
</div>
