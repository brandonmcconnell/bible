</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 45:20; Jeremiah 10:5; Isaiah 46:1"> They lift it to their shoulder and carry it along; they set it in its place, and there it stands, not budging from that spot. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:18–21; Psalm 115:8; Ezekiel 18:28"> Remember this and be brave; take it to heart, you transgressors!</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 111:4; Isaiah 42:9; Isaiah 45:21–22"> Remember what happened long ago, for I am God, and there is no other; I am God, and there is none like Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Psalm 33:11; Ephesians 1:9–11"> I declare the end from the beginning, and from ancient times what is still to come. I say, ‘My purpose will stand, and all My good pleasure I will accomplish.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
