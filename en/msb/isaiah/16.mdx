</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 27:8; Judges 11:18; Jeremiah 48:20"> Like fluttering birds pushed out of the nest, so are the daughters of Moab at the fords of the Arnon:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 32:2; 1 Kings 18:4; Isaiah 25:4"> “Give us counsel; render a decision. Shelter us at noonday with shade as dark as night. Hide the refugees; do not betray the one who flees.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 14:4; Isaiah 51:13; Isaiah 9:4"> Let my fugitives stay with you; be a refuge for Moab from the destroyer.” When the oppressor has gone, destruction has ceased, and the oppressors have vanished from the land,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 7:14; Micah 4:7; Isaiah 9:6–7"> in loving devotion a throne will be established in the tent of David. A judge seeking justice and hastening righteousness will sit on it in faithfulness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 2:1; Obadiah 1:3–4; Jeremiah 48:29–30"> We have heard of Moab’s pomposity, his exceeding pride and conceit, his overflowing arrogance. But his boasting is empty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 3:25; 1 Chronicles 16:3; Jeremiah 48:20"> Therefore let Moab wail; let them wail together for Moab. Moan for the raisin cakes of Kir-hareseth, you who are utterly stricken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 15:4; Numbers 32:38; Jeremiah 48:32"> For the fields of Heshbon have withered, along with the grapevines of Sibmah. The rulers of the nations have trampled its choicest vines, which had reached as far as Jazer and spread toward the desert. Their shoots had spread out and passed over the sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:12; Isaiah 15:4–5; Jeremiah 40:10"> So I weep with Jazer for the vines of Sibmah; I drench Heshbon and Elealeh with my tears. Triumphant shouts have fallen silent over your summer fruit and your harvest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:33; Judges 9:27; Amos 5:17"> Joy and gladness are removed from the orchard; no one sings or shouts in the vineyards. No one tramples the grapes in the winepresses; I have put an end to the cheering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 15:5; Jeremiah 48:36; Hosea 11:8"> Therefore my heart laments for Moab like a harp, my inmost being for Kir-heres.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 15:2; 1 Kings 18:29; Jeremiah 48:35"> When Moab appears on the high place, when he wearies himself and enters his sanctuary to pray, it will do him no good.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 44:8"> This is the message that the LORD spoke earlier concerning Moab.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 21:16; Jeremiah 48:46–47; Isaiah 23:9"> And now the LORD says, “In three years, as a hired worker counts the years, Moab’s splendor will become an object of contempt, with all her many people. And those who are left will be few and feeble.”</span>
</div>
