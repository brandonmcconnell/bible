<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:2; Psalm 68:26; Isaiah 65:16"> “Listen to this, O house of Jacob, you who are called by the name of Israel, who have descended from the line of Judah, who swear by the name of the LORD, who invoke the God of Israel—but not in truth or righteousness—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Romans 2:17; Isaiah 52:1"> who indeed call yourselves after the holy city and lean on the God of Israel; the LORD of Hosts is His name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 42:9; Joshua 21:45; Isaiah 41:22"> I foretold the former things long ago; they came out of My mouth and I proclaimed them. Suddenly I acted, and they came to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Exodus 32:9; Zechariah 7:11–12"> For I knew that you are stubborn; your neck is iron and your forehead is bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 48:3; Luke 1:70; Acts 15:18"> Therefore I declared it to you long ago; I announced it before it came to pass, so that you could not claim, ‘My idol has done this; my carved image and molten god has ordained it.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 4:1; Isaiah 42:9; Romans 16:25–26"> You have heard these things; look at them all. Will you not acknowledge them? From now on I will tell you of new things, hidden things unknown to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> They are created now, and not long ago; you have not heard of them before today. So you cannot claim, ‘I already knew them!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 58:3; Hosea 5:7; Jeremiah 6:10"> You have never heard; you have never understood; for a long time your ears have not been open. For I knew how deceitful you are; you have been called a rebel from birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 79:9; Psalm 106:8; Psalm 143:11"> For the sake of My name I will delay My wrath; for the sake of My praise I will restrain it, so that you will not be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12; Proverbs 17:3; Job 23:10"> See, I have refined you, but not as silver; I have tested you in the furnace of affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:8; Ezekiel 20:9; Isaiah 37:35"> For My own sake, My very own sake, I will act; for how can I let Myself be defamed? I will not yield My glory to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:4; Revelation 22:13; Revelation 1:8"> Listen to Me, O Jacob, and Israel, whom I have called: I am He; I am the first, and I am the last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:10–12; Psalm 102:25; Isaiah 40:26"> Surely My own hand founded the earth, and My right hand spread out the heavens; when I summon them, they stand up together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 43:9; Jeremiah 50:21–29; Isaiah 13:17–18"> Come together, all of you, and listen: Which of the idols has foretold these things? The LORD’s chosen ally will carry out His desire against Babylon, and His arm will be against the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 1:8; Isaiah 45:1–2; Psalm 45:4"> I, even I, have spoken; yes, I have called him. I have brought him, and he will succeed in his mission.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 18:20; John 20:21–22; John 3:34"> Come near to Me and listen to this: From the beginning I have not spoken in secret; from the time it happened, I was there.” And now the Lord GOD has sent me, accompanied by His Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 32:8; Psalm 25:12; Job 22:21–22"> Thus says the LORD your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel: “I am the LORD your God, who teaches you for your benefit, who directs you in the way you should go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:29; Isaiah 66:12; Psalm 119:165"> If only you had paid attention to My commandments, your peace would have been like a river, and your righteousness like waves of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22; Genesis 22:17; Romans 9:27"> Your descendants would have been as countless as the sand, and your offspring as numerous as its grains; their name would never be cut off or eliminated from My presence.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:45; Jeremiah 50:8; Isaiah 52:9"> Leave Babylon! Flee from the Chaldeans! Declare it with a shout of joy, proclaim it, let it go out to the ends of the earth, saying, “The LORD has redeemed His servant Jacob!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 17:6; Isaiah 43:19–20; Psalm 105:41"> They did not thirst when He led them through the deserts; He made water flow for them from the rock; He split the rock, and water gushed out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 57:21; Job 15:20–24; Luke 19:42"> “There is no peace,” says the LORD, “for the wicked.”</span>
