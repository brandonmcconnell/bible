<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Isaiah 24:16; Isaiah 10:12"> Woe to you, O destroyer never destroyed, O traitor never betrayed! When you have finished destroying, you will be destroyed. When you have finished betraying, you will be betrayed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; Isaiah 59:16; Isaiah 30:18–19"> O LORD, be gracious to us! We wait for You. Be our strength every morning and our salvation in time of trouble.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:13–14; Isaiah 10:32–34; Isaiah 37:11–18"> The peoples flee the thunder of Your voice; the nations scatter when You rise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 33:23; Joel 2:25; 2 Chronicles 20:25"> Your spoil, O nations, is gathered as by locusts; like a swarm of locusts men sweep over it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 2:17; Ephesians 1:20–21; Isaiah 32:15–18"> The LORD is exalted, for He dwells on high; He has filled Zion with justice and righteousness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 112:1–3; Proverbs 19:23; 1 Timothy 4:8"> He will be the sure foundation for your times, a storehouse of salvation, wisdom, and knowledge. The fear of the LORD is Zion’s treasure.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 18:18; 2 Kings 18:37–3; Isaiah 36:22"> Behold, their valiant ones cry aloud in the streets; the envoys of peace weep bitterly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 5:6; Isaiah 35:8; Lamentations 1:4"> The highways are deserted; travel has ceased. The treaty has been broken, the witnesses are despised, and human life is disregarded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Nahum 1:4; Isaiah 65:10"> The land mourns and languishes; Lebanon is ashamed and decayed. Sharon is like a desert; Bashan and Carmel shake off their leaves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 12:5; Psalm 102:13–18; Amos 6:1"> “Now I will arise,” says the LORD. “Now I will lift Myself up. Now I will be exalted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 7:14; Isaiah 59:4; James 1:15"> You conceive chaff; you give birth to stubble. Your breath is a fire that will consume you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 27:4; 2 Samuel 23:6–7; Isaiah 10:17"> The peoples will be burned to ashes, like thorns cut down and set ablaze.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 48:10; Ephesians 2:11–18; Daniel 3:27–3"> You who are far off, hear what I have done; you who are near, acknowledge My might.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Matthew 25:46; Isaiah 66:24"> The sinners in Zion are afraid; trembling grips the ungodly: “Who of us can dwell with a consuming fire? Who of us can dwell with everlasting flames?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 119:37; 1 John 3:7; Ephesians 5:11–13"> He who walks righteously and speaks with sincerity, who refuses gain from extortion, whose hand never takes a bribe, who stops his ears against murderous plots and shuts his eyes tightly against evil—</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 12:29–31; Isaiah 32:18; Proverbs 1:33"> he will dwell on the heights; the mountain fortress will be his refuge; his food will be provided and his water assured.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 9:17; John 14:21; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> Your eyes will see the King in His beauty and behold a land that stretches afar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20; Psalm 31:7–8; Isaiah 38:9–22"> Your mind will ponder the former terror: “Where is he who tallies? Where is he who weighs? Where is he who counts the towers?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:11; Deuteronomy 28:49–50; Jeremiah 5:15"> You will no longer see the insolent, a people whose speech is unintelligible, who stammer in a language you cannot understand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 46:5; Isaiah 32:18; Isaiah 54:2"> Look upon Zion, the city of our appointed feasts. Your eyes will see Jerusalem, a peaceful pasture, a tent that does not wander; its tent pegs will not be pulled up, nor will any of its cords be broken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 46:4–5; Isaiah 48:18; Isaiah 66:12"> But there the Majestic One, our LORD, will be for us a place of rivers and wide canals, where no galley with oars will row, and no majestic vessel will pass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 5:31; James 4:12; Zephaniah 3:15–17"> For the LORD is our Judge, the LORD is our lawgiver, the LORD is our King. It is He who will save us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 7:8; Psalm 68:12; 1 Corinthians 1:27"> Your ropes are slack; they cannot secure the mast or spread the sail. Then an abundance of spoils will be divided, and even the lame will carry off plunder.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:20; 1 John 1:7–9; Revelation 21:4"> And no resident of Zion will say, “I am sick.” The people who dwell there will be forgiven of iniquity.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
