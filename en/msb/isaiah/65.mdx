<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 10:20; Romans 9:24–26; 1 Peter 2:10"> “I revealed Myself to those who did not ask for Me; I was found by those who did not seek Me. To a nation that did not call My name, I said, ‘Here I am! Here I am!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:21; Proverbs 1:24; Jeremiah 4:14"> All day long I have held out My hands to an obstinate people who walk in the wrong path, who follow their own imaginations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; Deuteronomy 32:21; Isaiah 66:17"> to a people who continually provoke Me to My face, sacrificing in the gardens and burning incense on altars of brick,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 66:17; Leviticus 11:7; Isaiah 66:3"> sitting among the graves, spending nights in secret places, eating the meat of pigs and polluted broth from their bowls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:9–12; Luke 7:39; Proverbs 16:5"> They say, ‘Keep to yourself; do not come near me, for I am holier than you!’ Such people are smoke in My nostrils, a fire that burns all day long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 50:3; Jeremiah 16:18; Psalm 79:12"> Behold, it is written before Me: I will not keep silent, but I will repay; I will pay it back into their laps,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:27–28; Isaiah 57:7; Exodus 20:5"> both for your iniquities and for those of your fathers,” says the LORD. “Because they burned incense on the mountains and scorned Me on the hills, I will measure into their laps full payment for their former deeds.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 9:27–29; Amos 9:8–9; Romans 11:24–26"> This is what the LORD says: “As the new wine is found in a cluster of grapes, and men say, ‘Do not destroy it, for it contains a blessing,’ so I will act on behalf of My servants; I will not destroy them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 9:11–15; Isaiah 27:6; Romans 11:5–7"> And I will bring forth descendants from Jacob, and heirs from Judah; My elect will possess My mountains, and My servants will dwell there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Isaiah 33:9; Hosea 2:15"> Sharon will become a pasture for flocks, and the Valley of Achor a resting place for herds, for My people who seek Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 2:2; Isaiah 1:28; Deuteronomy 32:17"> But you who forsake the LORD, who forget My holy mountain, who set a table for Fortune and fill bowls of mixed wine for Destiny,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:13; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Zechariah 7:11–13"> I will destine you for the sword, and you will all kneel down to be slaughtered, because I called and you did not answer, I spoke and you did not listen; you did evil in My sight and chose that in which I did not delight.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Isaiah 66:14; Isaiah 61:7"> Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: “My servants will eat, but you will go hungry; My servants will drink, but you will go thirsty; My servants will rejoice, but you will be put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Psalm 66:4; Matthew 22:13"> My servants will shout for joy with a glad heart, but you will cry out with a heavy heart and wail with a broken spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 62:2; Romans 9:26; Acts 11:26"> You will leave behind your name as a curse for My chosen ones, and the Lord GOD will slay you; but to His servants He will give another name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 19:18; Jeremiah 4:2; Psalm 31:5"> Whoever invokes a blessing in the land will do so by the God of truth, and whoever takes an oath in the land will swear by the God of truth. For the former troubles will be forgotten and hidden from My sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 3:13; Revelation 21:1–5; Isaiah 66:22"> For behold, I will create new heavens and a new earth. The former things will not be remembered, nor will they come to mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 98:1–9; Revelation 19:1–6; Isaiah 66:10–14"> But be glad and rejoice forever in what I create; for I will create Jerusalem to be a joy and its people to be a delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17; Isaiah 25:8"> I will rejoice in Jerusalem and take delight in My people. The sounds of weeping and crying will no longer be heard in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 5:26; Ecclesiastes 8:12–13; Deuteronomy 4:40"> No longer will a nursing infant live but a few days, or an old man fail to live out his years. For the youth will die at a hundred years, and he who fails to reach a hundred will be considered accursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Amos 9:14; Isaiah 62:8–9; Isaiah 32:18"> They will build houses and dwell in them; they will plant vineyards and eat their fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 92:12–14; Psalm 91:16; Psalm 21:4"> No longer will they build houses for others to inhabit, nor plant for others to eat. For as is the lifetime of a tree, so will be the days of My people, and My chosen ones will fully enjoy the work of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 61:9; Jeremiah 32:38–39; Deuteronomy 28:3–12"> They will not labor in vain or bear children doomed to disaster; for they will be a people blessed by the LORD—they and their descendants with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 10:12; 1 John 5:14–15; Isaiah 58:9"> Even before they call, I will answer, and while they are still speaking, I will hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 11:6–9; Micah 4:3; Acts 9:19–21"> The wolf and the lamb will feed together, and the lion will eat straw like the ox, but the food of the serpent will be dust. They will neither harm nor destroy on all My holy mountain,” says the LORD.</span>
