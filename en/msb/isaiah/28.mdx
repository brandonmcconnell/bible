<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 7:5; Isaiah 28:7; 2 Kings 14:25–27"> Woe to the majestic crown of Ephraim’s drunkards, to the fading flower of his glorious splendor, set on the summit above the fertile valley, the pride of those overcome by wine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:30; Nahum 1:8; Ezekiel 13:11"> Behold, the Lord has one who is strong and mighty. Like a hailstorm or destructive tempest, like a driving rain or flooding downpour, he will smash that crown to the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 26:6; Hebrews 10:29; Revelation 11:2"> The majestic crown of Ephraim’s drunkards will be trampled underfoot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nahum 3:12; Micah 7:1; Isaiah 28:1"> The fading flower of his beautiful splendor, set on the summit above the fertile valley, will be like a ripe fig before the summer harvest: Whoever sees it will take it in his hand and swallow it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 5:4; Isaiah 62:3; Isaiah 60:19"> On that day the LORD of Hosts will be a crown of glory, a diadem of splendor to the remnant of His people,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 18:32–34; John 5:30; Joshua 1:9"> a spirit of justice to him who sits in judgment, and a strength to those who repel the onslaught at the gate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 56:10–12; Luke 21:34; Isaiah 19:14"> These also stagger from wine and stumble from strong drink: Priests and prophets reel from strong drink and are befuddled by wine. They stumble because of strong drink, muddled in their visions and stumbling in their judgments.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:26; Proverbs 26:11; Habakkuk 2:15–16"> For all their tables are covered with vomit; there is not a place without filth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 131:2; Jeremiah 6:10; John 12:38"> Whom is He trying to teach? To whom is He explaining His message? To infants just weaned from milk? To babies removed from the breast?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 28:13; 2 Timothy 3:7; Jeremiah 11:7"> For they hear: “Order on order, order on order, line on line, line on line; a little here, a little there.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:21; Deuteronomy 28:49; Isaiah 33:19"> Indeed, with mocking lips and foreign tongues, He will speak to this people</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 11:28–29; Isaiah 30:15; Jeremiah 6:16"> to whom He has said: “This is the place of rest, let the weary rest; this is the place of repose.” But they would not listen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 13:14; Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Peter 3:16"> Then the word of the LORD to them will become: “Order on order, order on order, line on line, line on line; a little here, a little there,” so that they will go stumbling backward and will be injured, ensnared, and captured.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 28:22; Isaiah 29:20; Isaiah 1:10"> Therefore hear the word of the LORD, O scoffers who rule this people in Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Ezekiel 13:22; Amos 2:4"> For you said, “We have made a covenant with death; we have fashioned an agreement with Sheol. When the overwhelming scourge passes through it will not touch us, because we have made lies our refuge and falsehood our hiding place.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 118:22; Acts 4:11–12; 1 Peter 2:6–8"> So this is what the Lord GOD says: “See, I lay a stone in Zion, a tested stone, a precious cornerstone, a sure foundation; the one who believes will never be shaken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Amos 7:7–9; Revelation 16:21"> I will make justice the measuring line and righteousness the level. Hail will sweep away your refuge of lies, and water will flood your hiding place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 7:7; Isaiah 8:10; Isaiah 28:15"> Your covenant with death will be dissolved, and your agreement with Sheol will not stand. When the overwhelming scourge passes through, you will be trampled by it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Isaiah 36:22; 2 Kings 24:2"> As often as it passes through, it will carry you away; it will sweep through morning after morning, by day and by night.” The understanding of this message will bring sheer terror.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; 1 Corinthians 1:18–31; Romans 9:30–32"> Indeed, the bed is too short to stretch out on, and the blanket too small to wrap around you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:11; Joshua 10:12; 2 Samuel 5:20"> For the LORD will rise up as at Mount Perazim. He will rouse Himself as in the Valley of Gibeon, to do His work, His strange work, and to perform His task, His disturbing task.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22–23; Isaiah 24:1–23; Matthew 27:44"> So now, do not mock, or your shackles will become heavier. Indeed, I have heard from the Lord GOD of Hosts a decree of destruction against the whole land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Jeremiah 22:29; Revelation 2:11"> Listen and hear my voice. Pay attention and hear what I say.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Hosea 10:11–12"> Does the plowman plow for planting every day? Does he continuously loosen and harrow the soil?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:9; Matthew 23:23; Exodus 9:31–32"> When he has leveled its surface, does he not sow caraway and scatter cumin? He plants wheat in rows and barley in plots, and rye within its border.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 31:3–6; Exodus 36:2; Job 39:17"> For his God instructs and teaches him properly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15; Isaiah 27:7–8; Jeremiah 46:28"> Surely caraway is not threshed with a sledge, and the wheel of a cart is not rolled over the cumin. But caraway is beaten out with a stick, and cumin with a rod.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:9–10; Matthew 3:12; John 12:24"> Grain for bread must be ground, but it is not endlessly threshed. Though the wheels of the cart roll over it, the horses do not crush it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Jeremiah 32:19; Job 5:9"> This also comes from the LORD of Hosts, who is wonderful in counsel and excellent in wisdom.</span>
</div>
</div>
