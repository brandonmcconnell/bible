<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:18–21; Luke 3:22; Isaiah 61:1"> “Here is My Servant, whom I uphold, My Chosen One, in whom My soul delights. I will put My Spirit on Him, and He will bring justice to the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:16–20; 2 Timothy 2:24; Matthew 11:29"> He will not cry out or raise His voice, nor make His voice heard in the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Jeremiah 31:25; Psalm 147:3"> A bruised reed He will not break and a smoldering wick He will not extinguish; He will faithfully bring forth justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–12; Genesis 49:10; Isaiah 52:13–15"> He will not grow weak or discouraged before He has established justice on the earth. In His law the islands will put their hope.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Isaiah 45:12; Jeremiah 10:12"> This is what God the LORD says—He who created the heavens and stretched them out, who spread out the earth and its offspring, who gives breath to the people on it and life to those who walk in it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 2:32; John 8:12; Acts 13:47"> “I, the LORD, have called you for a righteous purpose, and I will take hold of your hand. I will keep you and appoint you to be a covenant for the people and a light to the nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1; Ephesians 1:17–18; Isaiah 35:5"> to open the eyes of the blind, to bring prisoners out of the dungeon and those sitting in darkness out from the prison house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 48:11; Isaiah 43:11; Exodus 34:14"> I am the LORD; that is My name! I will not yield My glory to another or My praise to idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 43:19; Isaiah 46:9–10; John 13:19"> Behold, the former things have happened, and now I declare new things. Before they spring forth I proclaim them to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 33:3; Psalm 40:3; Romans 15:9–11"> Sing to the LORD a new song—His praise from the ends of the earth—you who go down to the sea, and all that is in it, you islands, and all who dwell in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 32:16; Isaiah 60:7; Isaiah 41:18–19"> Let the desert and its cities raise their voices; let the villages of Kedar cry aloud. Let the people of Sela sing for joy; let them cry out from the mountaintops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:9–11; Psalm 117:1–2; Revelation 7:9–12"> Let them give glory to the LORD and declare His praise in the islands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 15:1–3; Psalm 78:65; Isaiah 59:16–19"> The LORD goes forth like a mighty one; He stirs up His zeal like a warrior. He shouts; yes, He roars in triumph over His enemies:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 83:1–2; Ecclesiastes 8:11–12; 2 Peter 3:15"> “I have kept silent from ages past; I have remained quiet and restrained. But now I will groan like a woman in labor; I will at once gasp and pant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 50:2; Nahum 1:4–6; Isaiah 44:27"> I will lay waste the mountains and hills and dry up all their vegetation. I will turn the rivers into dry land and drain the marshes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 30:21; Luke 3:5; Isaiah 60:19–20"> I will lead the blind by a way they did not know; I will guide them on unfamiliar paths. I will turn darkness into light before them and rough places into level ground. These things I will do for them, and I will not forsake them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 97:7; Isaiah 1:29; Isaiah 44:11"> But those who trust in idols and say to molten images, ‘You are our gods!’ will be turned back in utter shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 43:8; Proverbs 20:12; Mark 7:34–37"> Listen, you deaf ones; look, you blind ones, that you may see!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; Ezekiel 12:2; Jeremiah 5:21"> Who is blind but My servant, or deaf like the messenger I am sending? Who is blind like My covenant partner, or blind like the servant of the LORD?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:2–4; Romans 2:21; Acts 28:22–27"> Though seeing many things, you do not keep watch. Though your ears are open, you do not hear.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 8:10; Romans 3:31; Isaiah 46:12–13"> The LORD was pleased, for the sake of His righteousness, to magnify His law and make it glorious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 102:20; Jeremiah 50:17; Isaiah 51:23"> But this is a people plundered and looted, all trapped in caves or imprisoned in dungeons. They have become plunder with no one to rescue them, and loot with no one to say, “Send them back!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Isaiah 48:18; Deuteronomy 32:29"> Who among you will pay attention to this? Who will listen and obey hereafter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 47:6; Isaiah 10:5–6; Matthew 22:7"> Who gave Jacob up for spoil, and Israel to the plunderers? Was it not the LORD, against whom we have sinned? They were unwilling to walk in His ways, and they would not obey His law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 7:9; Isaiah 57:11; Malachi 2:2"> So He poured out on them His furious anger and the fierceness of battle. It enveloped them in flames, but they did not understand; it consumed them, but they did not take it to heart.</span>
