<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:12; Revelation 1:7; Joshua 2:11"> This is the burden against Egypt: Behold, the LORD rides on a swift cloud; He is coming to Egypt. The idols of Egypt will tremble before Him, and the hearts of the Egyptians will melt within them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 1 Samuel 14:20; Matthew 10:36"> “So I will incite Egyptian against Egyptian; brother will fight against brother, neighbor against neighbor, city against city, and kingdom against kingdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; Daniel 2:2; 1 Chronicles 10:13"> Then the spirit of the Egyptians will be emptied out from among them, and I will frustrate their plans, so that they will resort to idols and spirits of the dead, to mediums and spiritists.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 46:26; Ezekiel 29:19"> I will deliver the Egyptians into the hands of a harsh master, and a fierce king will rule over them,” declares the Lord GOD of Hosts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:36; Ezekiel 30:12; Zechariah 14:18"> The waters of the Nile will dry up, and the riverbed will be parched and empty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:25; Exodus 7:18; 2 Kings 19:24"> The canals will stink; the streams of Egypt will trickle and dry up; the reeds and rushes will wither.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 32:20; Joel 1:17–18; Isaiah 23:3"> The bulrushes by the Nile, by the mouth of the river, and all the fields sown along the Nile, will wither, blow away, and be no more.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:10; Habakkuk 1:15; Numbers 11:5"> Then the fishermen will mourn, all who cast a hook into the Nile will lament, and those who spread nets on the waters will pine away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:7; Proverbs 7:16; 1 Kings 10:28"> The workers in flax will be dismayed, and the weavers of fine linen will turn pale.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:10; Exodus 7:19; Exodus 8:5"> The workers in cloth will be dejected, and all the hired workers will be sick at heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Acts 7:22; 1 Kings 4:30"> The princes of Zoan are mere fools; Pharaoh’s wise counselors give senseless advice. How can you say to Pharaoh, “I am one of the wise, a son of eastern kings”?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20; Isaiah 14:24; Judges 9:38"> Where are your wise men now? Let them tell you and reveal what the LORD of Hosts has planned against Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:13; Jeremiah 2:16; Jeremiah 46:14"> The princes of Zoan have become fools; the princes of Memphis are deceived. The cornerstones of her tribes have led Egypt astray.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Kings 22:20–23; Ezekiel 14:7–9"> The LORD has poured into her a spirit of confusion. Egypt has been led astray in all she does, as a drunkard staggers through his own vomit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:14–15; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12; Proverbs 14:23"> There is nothing Egypt can do—head or tail, palm or reed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Nahum 3:13; Isaiah 11:15"> In that day the Egyptians will be like women. They will tremble with fear beneath the uplifted hand of the LORD of Hosts, when He brandishes it against them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 14:24; Daniel 4:35; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> The land of Judah will bring terror to Egypt; whenever Judah is mentioned, Egypt will tremble over what the LORD of Hosts has planned against it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:9; Zechariah 2:11; Isaiah 19:21"> In that day five cities in the land of Egypt will speak the language of Canaan and swear allegiance to the LORD of Hosts. One of them will be called the City of the Sun.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 28:18; Joshua 22:10; Joshua 22:26"> In that day there will be an altar to the LORD in the center of the land of Egypt, and a pillar to the LORD near her border.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 50:15; Titus 2:13; Isaiah 45:21–22"> It will be a sign and a witness to the LORD of Hosts in the land of Egypt. When they cry out to the LORD because of their oppressors, He will send them a savior and defender to rescue them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; Malachi 1:11; John 17:3"> The LORD will make Himself known to Egypt, and on that day Egypt will acknowledge the LORD. They will worship with sacrifices and offerings; they will make vows to the LORD and fulfill them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Hebrews 12:11; Hosea 14:1"> And the LORD will strike Egypt with a plague; He will strike them but heal them. They will turn to the LORD, and He will hear their prayers and heal them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 11:16; Ephesians 2:18–22; Isaiah 35:8–10"> In that day there will be a highway from Egypt to Assyria. The Assyrians will go to Egypt, and the Egyptians to Assyria. The Egyptians and Assyrians will worship together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 65:8; Genesis 12:2; Galatians 3:14"> In that day Israel will join a three-party alliance with Egypt and Assyria—a blessing upon the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 29:23; Hosea 2:23; Romans 9:24–25"> The LORD of Hosts will bless them, saying, “Blessed be Egypt My people, Assyria My handiwork, and Israel My inheritance.”</span>
</div>
</div>
