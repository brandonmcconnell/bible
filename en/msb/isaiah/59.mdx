<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Isaiah 50:2; Numbers 11:23"> Surely the arm of the LORD is not too short to save, nor His ear too dull to hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:25; Proverbs 15:29; Micah 3:4"> But your iniquities have built barriers between you and your God, and your sins have hidden His face from you, so that He does not hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; Ezekiel 13:8; Jeremiah 2:34"> For your hands are stained with blood, and your fingers with iniquity; your lips have spoken lies, and your tongue mutters injustice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 15:35; James 1:15; Micah 7:2–5"> No one calls for justice; no one pleads his case honestly. They rely on empty pleas; they tell lies; they conceive mischief and give birth to iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:7; Isaiah 14:29; Proverbs 23:32"> They hatch the eggs of vipers and weave a spider’s web. Whoever eats their eggs will die; crack one open, and a viper is hatched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 57:12; Revelation 3:17–18; Jeremiah 6:7"> Their cobwebs cannot be made into clothing, and they cannot cover themselves with their works. Their deeds are sinful deeds, and acts of violence are in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:15–17; Proverbs 6:17; Mark 7:21–22"> Their feet run to evil; they are swift to shed innocent blood. Their thoughts are sinful thoughts; ruin and destruction lie in their wake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 57:20–21; Romans 3:17; Luke 1:79"> The way of peace they have not known, and there is no justice in their tracks. They have turned them into crooked paths; no one who treads on them will know peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 5:30; Job 30:26; Lamentations 5:16–17"> Therefore justice is far from us, and righteousness does not reach us. We hope for light, but there is darkness; for brightness, but we walk in gloom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:29; Job 5:14; Lamentations 3:6"> Like the blind, we feel our way along the wall, groping like those without eyes. We stumble at midday as in the twilight; among the vigorous we are like the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Ezekiel 7:16; Jeremiah 9:1"> We all growl like bears and moan like doves. We hope for justice, but find none, for salvation, but it is far from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; Hosea 5:5; Jeremiah 14:7"> For our transgressions are multiplied before You, and our sins testify against us. Our transgressions are indeed with us, and we know our iniquities:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 7:21–22; James 3:6; Hebrews 3:12"> rebelling and denying the LORD, turning away from our God, speaking oppression and revolt, conceiving and uttering lies from the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:4; Ecclesiastes 3:16; Jeremiah 5:31"> So justice is turned away, and righteousness stands at a distance. For truth has stumbled in the public square, and honesty cannot enter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 7:2; Jeremiah 7:28; 1 John 3:11–12"> Truth is missing, and whoever turns from evil becomes prey. The LORD looked and was displeased that there was no justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Psalm 106:23; Isaiah 64:7"> He saw that there was no man; He was amazed that there was no one to intercede. So His own arm brought salvation, and His own righteousness sustained Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 6:17; Ephesians 6:14; 1 Thessalonians 5:8"> He put on righteousness like a breastplate, and the helmet of salvation on His head; He put on garments of vengeance and wrapped Himself in a cloak of zeal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Isaiah 63:6; Romans 2:6"> So He will repay according to their deeds: fury to His enemies, retribution to His foes, and recompense to the islands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Psalm 113:3; Zechariah 4:6"> So shall they fear the name of the LORD where the sun sets, and His glory where it rises. For He will come like a raging flood, driven by the breath of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 11:26–27; Titus 2:11–14; Acts 3:19"> “The Redeemer will come to Zion, to those in Jacob who turn from transgression,” declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Hebrews 10:16; Ezekiel 36:25–27"> “As for Me, this is My covenant with them,” says the LORD. “My Spirit will not depart from you, and My words that I have put in your mouth will not depart from your mouth or from the mouths of your children and grandchildren, from now on and forevermore,” says the LORD.</span>
