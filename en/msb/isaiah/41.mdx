</div>
<div className="bp">

He hands nations over to him and subdues kings before him. He turns them to dust with his sword, to windblown chaff with his bow.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 5:24; Isaiah 57:2"> He pursues them, going on safely, hardly touching the path with his feet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 48:12; Revelation 22:13; Revelation 1:17"> Who has performed this and carried it out, calling forth the generations from the beginning? I, the LORD—the first and the last—I am He.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:15–16; Psalm 65:8; Psalm 66:3"> The islands see and fear; the ends of the earth tremble. They approach and come forward.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 44:12; Isaiah 40:19; Daniel 3:1–7"> Each one helps the other and says to his brother, “Be strong!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 40:19–20; Daniel 3:1–7; Judges 18:17–18"> The craftsman encourages the goldsmith, and he who wields the hammer cheers him who strikes the anvil, saying of the welding, “It is good.” He nails it down so it will not be toppled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 2:23; 2 Chronicles 20:7; Deuteronomy 14:2"> “But you, O Israel, My servant, Jacob, whom I have chosen, descendant of Abraham My friend—</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:22; Psalm 94:14; Deuteronomy 7:6–7"> I brought you from the ends of the earth and called you from its farthest corners. I said, ‘You are My servant.’ I have chosen and not rejected you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 43:1–2; Romans 8:31"> Do not fear, for I am with you; do not be afraid, for I am your God. I will strengthen you; I will surely help you; I will uphold you with My righteous right hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 23:22; Isaiah 45:24; Revelation 3:9"> Behold, all who rage against you will be ashamed and disgraced; those who contend with you will be reduced to nothing and will perish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 37:35–36; Job 20:7–9; Isaiah 17:14"> You will seek them but will not find them. Those who wage war against you will come to nothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalm 73:23; Psalm 63:8"> For I am the LORD your God, who takes hold of your right hand and tells you: Do not fear, I will help you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 43:14; Psalm 22:6; Isaiah 54:5"> Do not fear, O Jacob, you worm, O few men of Israel. I will help you,” declares the LORD. “Your Redeemer is the Holy One of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 4:13; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Zechariah 4:7"> Behold, I will make you into a threshing sledge, new and sharp, with many teeth. You will thresh the mountains and crush them, and reduce the hills to chaff.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:2; Isaiah 61:10–11; Isaiah 45:24–25"> You will winnow them, and a wind will carry them away; a gale will scatter them. But you will rejoice in the LORD; you will glory in the Holy One of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 55:1; Isaiah 30:19; Isaiah 44:3"> The poor and needy seek water, but there is none; their tongues are parched with thirst. I, the LORD, will answer them; I, the God of Israel, will not forsake them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 107:35; Isaiah 35:6–7; Isaiah 58:11"> I will open rivers on the barren heights, and fountains in the middle of the valleys. I will turn the desert into a pool of water, and the dry land into flowing springs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 55:13; Isaiah 37:31–32; Isaiah 35:1"> I will plant cedars in the wilderness, acacias, myrtles, and olive trees. I will set cypresses in the desert, elms and boxwood together,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 12:9; Isaiah 44:23; Isaiah 43:21"> so that all may see and know, may consider and understand, that the hand of the LORD has done this and the Holy One of Israel has created it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 23:3–4; Job 38:3; Micah 6:1–2"> “Present your case,” says the LORD. “Submit your arguments,” says the King of Jacob.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 45:21; Isaiah 48:14; Isaiah 44:7"> “Let them come and tell us what will happen. Tell the former things, so that we may reflect on them and know the outcome. Or announce to us what is coming.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:19; Isaiah 42:9; Jeremiah 10:5"> Tell us the things that are to come, so that we may know that you are gods. Yes, do something good or evil, that we may look on together in dismay.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 115:8; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 41:29"> Behold, you are nothing and your work is of no value. Anyone who chooses you is detestable.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:2; 2 Samuel 22:43; Isaiah 10:6"> I have raised up one from the north, and he has come—one from the east who calls on My name. He will march over rulers as if they were mortar, like a potter who treads the clay.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 44:7; Isaiah 41:22; Isaiah 45:21"> Who has declared this from the beginning, so that we may know, and from times past, so that we may say: ‘He was right’? No one announced it, no one foretold it, no one heard your words.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Isaiah 52:7; Isaiah 44:28"> I was the first to tell Zion: ‘Look, here they are!’ And I gave to Jerusalem a herald of good news.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 63:5; Daniel 2:10–11; Isaiah 40:13–14"> When I look, there is no one; there is no counselor among them; when I ask them, they have nothing to say.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:13; Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 10:2–16"> See, they are all a delusion; their works amount to nothing; their images are as empty as the wind.</span>
</div>
