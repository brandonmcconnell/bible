<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:7; Psalm 94:15; Hebrews 12:14"> “Listen to Me, you who pursue righteousness, you who seek the LORD: Look to the rock from which you were cut, and to the quarry from which you were hewn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:24; Galatians 3:9–14; Romans 4:1–5"> Look to Abraham your father, and to Sarah who gave you birth. When I called him, he was but one; then I blessed him and multiplied him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Joel 2:3; Isaiah 52:9"> For the LORD will comfort Zion and will look with compassion on all her ruins; He will make her wilderness like Eden and her desert like the garden of the LORD. Joy and gladness will be found in her, thanksgiving and melodious song.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Isaiah 2:3; 1 Corinthians 9:21"> Pay attention to Me, My people, and listen to Me, My nation; for a law will go out from Me, and My justice will become a light to the nations; I will bring it about quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:17–18; Isaiah 46:13; Isaiah 42:4"> My righteousness draws near, My salvation is on the way, and My arms will bring justice to the nations. The islands will look for Me and wait in hope for My arm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Psalm 102:25–26; Isaiah 34:4"> Lift up your eyes to the heavens, and look at the earth below; for the heavens will vanish like smoke, the earth will wear out like a garment, and its people will die like gnats. But My salvation will last forever, and My righteousness will never fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 5:11; Psalm 37:31; Ezekiel 2:6"> Listen to Me, you who know what is right, you people with My law in your hearts: Do not fear the scorn of men; do not be broken by their insults.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Isaiah 50:9; Hosea 5:12"> For the moth will devour them like a garment, and the worm will eat them like wool. But My righteousness will last forever, My salvation through all generations.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 74:13–14; Isaiah 52:1; Psalm 89:10"> Awake, awake, put on strength, O arm of the LORD. Wake up as in days past, as in generations of old. Was it not You who cut Rahab to pieces, who pierced through the dragon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 43:16; Isaiah 63:11–12; Isaiah 50:2"> Was it not You who dried up the sea, the waters of the great deep, who made a road in the depths of the sea for the redeemed to cross over?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17"> So the redeemed of the LORD will return and enter Zion with singing, crowned with everlasting joy. Gladness and joy will overtake them, and sorrow and sighing will flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 118:6; 1 Peter 1:24; Isaiah 2:22"> “I, even I, am He who comforts you. Why should you be afraid of mortal man, of a son of man who withers like grass?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 45:12; Isaiah 40:22; Psalm 104:2"> But you have forgotten the LORD, your Maker, who stretched out the heavens and laid the foundations of the earth. You live in terror all day long because of the fury of the oppressor who is bent on destruction. But where is the fury of the oppressor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Zechariah 9:11; Isaiah 52:2"> The captive will soon be freed; he will not die in the dungeon, and his bread will not be lacking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35; Isaiah 54:5; Amos 9:5–6"> For I am the LORD your God who stirs up the sea so that its waves roar—the LORD of Hosts is His name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:21; Isaiah 49:2; John 17:8"> I have put My words in your mouth, and covered you with the shadow of My hand, to establish the heavens, to found the earth, and to say to Zion, ‘You are My people.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:1; Revelation 14:10; Psalm 60:3"> Awake, awake! Rise up, O Jerusalem, you who have drunk from the hand of the LORD the cup of His fury; you who have drained the goblet to the dregs—the cup that makes men stagger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 49:21; Psalm 88:18; Psalm 142:4"> Among all the sons she bore, there is no one to guide her; among all the sons she brought up, there is no one to take her hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Amos 7:2; Isaiah 47:9; Ezekiel 14:21"> These pairs have befallen you: devastation and destruction, famine and sword. Who will grieve for you? Who can comfort you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11–12; Revelation 16:9–11; Ezekiel 39:19"> Your sons have fainted; they lie at the head of every street, like an antelope in a net. They are full of the wrath of the LORD, the rebuke of your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 29:9; Isaiah 54:11; Isaiah 51:17"> Therefore now hear this, you afflicted one, drunken, but not with wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 22:23; Isaiah 51:17; Jeremiah 50:34"> Thus says your Lord, the LORD, even your God, who defends His people: “See, I have removed from your hand the cup of staggering. From that goblet, the cup of My fury, you will never drink again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:24; Zechariah 12:2; Isaiah 49:25–26"> I will place it in the hands of your tormentors, who told you: ‘Lie down, so we can walk over you,’ so that you made your back like the ground, like a street to be traversed.”</span>
