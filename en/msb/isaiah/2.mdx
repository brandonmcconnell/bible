<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; Isaiah 13:1; Habakkuk 1:1"> This is the message that was revealed to Isaiah son of Amoz concerning Judah and Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 4:1–3; Isaiah 56:7; Zechariah 8:3"> In the last days the mountain of the house of the LORD will be established as the chief of the mountains; it will be raised above the hills, and all nations will stream to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:4–5; Luke 24:47; Zechariah 8:20–23"> And many peoples will come and say: “Come, let us go up to the mountain of the LORD, to the house of the God of Jacob. He will teach us His ways so that we may walk in His paths.” For the law will go forth from Zion, and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 2:18; Micah 4:3; Isaiah 11:6–9"> Then He will judge between the nations and arbitrate for many peoples. They will beat their swords into plowshares and their spears into pruning hooks. Nation will no longer take up the sword against nation, nor train anymore for war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Luke 1:79; Isaiah 60:19–20"> Come, O house of Jacob, let us walk in the light of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:20; 2 Kings 1:2; Psalm 106:35"> For You have abandoned Your people, the house of Jacob, because they are filled with influences from the east; they are soothsayers like the Philistines; they strike hands with the children of foreigners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16–17; Isaiah 30:16; Isaiah 31:1"> Their land is full of silver and gold, with no limit to their treasures; their land is full of horses, with no limit to their chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 115:4–8; Revelation 9:20; Jeremiah 2:28"> Their land is full of idols; they bow down to the work of their hands, to what their fingers have made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 5:15; Joshua 24:19; Romans 3:23"> So mankind is brought low, and man is humbled—do not forgive them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 6:15–16; Isaiah 2:19–21; Luke 23:30"> Go into the rocks and hide in the dust from the terror of the LORD and the splendor of His majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:15–16; Psalm 18:27; Isaiah 2:17"> The proud look of man will be humbled, and the loftiness of men brought low; the LORD alone will be exalted in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 23:9; Proverbs 16:5"> For the Day of the LORD of Hosts will come against all the proud and lofty, against all that is exalted—it will be humbled—</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 14:8; Zechariah 11:1–2; Ezekiel 31:3–12"> against all the cedars of Lebanon, lofty and lifted up, against all the oaks of Bashan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 30:25; Isaiah 40:4; 2 Corinthians 10:5"> against all the tall mountains, against all the high hills,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 25:12"> against every high tower, against every fortified wall,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 10:22; Isaiah 23:1; Numbers 33:52"> against every ship of Tarshish, and against every stately vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:29–30; Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 13:11"> So the pride of man will be brought low, and the loftiness of men will be humbled; the LORD alone will be exalted in that day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 27:9; Ezekiel 36:25; Isaiah 21:9"> and the idols will vanish completely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 2:10; Luke 23:30; Hosea 10:8"> Men will flee to caves in the rocks and holes in the ground, away from the terror of the LORD and from the splendor of His majesty, when He rises to shake the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 30:22; Isaiah 31:7; Isaiah 46:6"> In that day men will cast away to the moles and bats their idols of silver and gold—the idols they made to worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19; Song of Solomon 2:14; Isaiah 2:10"> They will flee to caverns in the rocks and crevices in the cliffs, away from the terror of the LORD and from the splendor of His majesty, when He rises to shake the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Psalm 146:3; James 4:14"> Put no more trust in man, who has only the breath in his nostrils. Of what account is he?</span>
