<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 12:38; Romans 10:16–17; 1 Corinthians 1:18"> Who has believed our message? And to whom has the arm of the LORD been revealed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:6–7; Isaiah 52:14; John 1:10–14"> He grew up before Him like a tender shoot, and like a root out of dry ground. He had no stately form or majesty to attract us, no beauty that we should desire Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; John 1:10–11; Isaiah 53:10"> He was despised and rejected by men, a man of sorrows, acquainted with grief. Like one from whom men hide their faces, He was despised, and we esteemed Him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Matthew 8:17; Galatians 3:13"> Surely He took up our infirmities and carried our sorrows; yet we considered Him stricken, struck down by God, and afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24–25; 1 Peter 3:18; Matthew 20:28"> But He was pierced for our transgressions, He was crushed for our iniquities; the punishment that brought us peace was upon Him, and by His stripes we are healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:25; 1 Peter 3:18; Psalm 119:176"> We all like sheep have gone astray, each one has turned to his own way; and the LORD has laid upon Him the iniquity of us all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 8:32–33; Matthew 26:63; 1 Peter 2:23"> He was oppressed and afflicted, yet He did not open His mouth. He was led like a lamb to the slaughter, and as a sheep before her shearers is silent, so He did not open His mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 3:18; Acts 8:33; Matthew 26:65–66"> By oppression and judgment He was taken away, and who can recount His descendants? For He was cut off from the land of the living; He was stricken for the transgression of My people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 27:57–60; 1 Peter 2:22; 1 John 3:5"> He was assigned a grave with the wicked, and with a rich man in His death, although He had done no violence, nor was any deceit in His mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:32; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Galatians 3:13"> Yet it was the LORD’s will to crush Him and to cause Him to suffer; and when His soul is made a guilt offering, He will see His offspring, He will prolong His days, and the good pleasure of the LORD will prosper in His hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 5:18–19; 1 Peter 2:24; Isaiah 53:4–6"> After the anguish of His soul, He will see the light of life and be satisfied. By His knowledge My righteous Servant will justify many, and He will bear their iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 22:37; Hebrews 9:28; Luke 23:32–34"> Therefore I will allot Him a portion with the great, and He will divide the spoils with the strong, because He has poured out His life unto death, and He was numbered with the transgressors. Yet He bore the sin of many and made intercession for the transgressors.</span>
