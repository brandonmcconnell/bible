<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 1:3–4; Mark 1:10; Revelation 20:11"> If only You would rend the heavens and come down, so that mountains would quake at Your presence,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 99:1; Jeremiah 33:9; Ezekiel 38:22–23"> as fire kindles the brushwood and causes the water to boil, to make Your name known to Your enemies, so that the nations will tremble at Your presence!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 34:10; Psalm 65:5–6; Psalm 66:3"> When You did awesome works that we did not expect, You came down, and the mountains trembled at Your presence.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9–10; Psalm 31:19; James 5:7"> From ancient times no one has heard, no ear has perceived, no eye has seen any God besides You, who acts on behalf of those who wait for Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:13–15; Hebrews 4:16; Acts 10:35"> You welcome those who gladly do right, who remember Your ways. Surely You were angry, for we sinned. How can we be saved if we remain in our sins?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 7:18; Romans 7:24; Ephesians 2:1–2"> Each of us has become like something unclean, and all our righteous acts are like filthy rags; we all wither like a leaf, and our iniquities carry us away like the wind.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Hosea 7:14; Isaiah 59:16"> No one calls on Your name or strives to take hold of You. For You have hidden Your face from us and delivered us into the hand of our iniquity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Psalm 119:73; Jeremiah 18:2–6"> But now, O LORD, You are our Father; we are the clay, and You are the potter; we are all the work of Your hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 7:18–20; Psalm 74:1–2; Psalm 79:13"> Do not be angry, O LORD, beyond measure; do not remember our iniquity forever. Oh, look upon us, we pray; we are all Your people!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; Luke 21:24; 2 Chronicles 36:19–21"> Your holy cities have become a wilderness. Zion has become a wasteland and Jerusalem a desolation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 25:9; Psalm 74:5–7; Lamentations 1:7"> Our holy and beautiful temple, where our fathers praised You, has been burned with fire, and all that was dear to us lies in ruins.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 42:14; Psalm 74:10–11; Psalm 83:1"> After all this, O LORD, will You restrain Yourself? Will You keep silent and afflict us beyond measure?</span>
</div>
