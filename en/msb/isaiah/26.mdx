<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 60:18; Isaiah 62:11; Ephesians 5:19–20"> In that day this song will be sung in the land of Judah: We have a strong city; salvation is established as its walls and ramparts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Isaiah 60:11; 1 Peter 2:9"> Open the gates so a righteous nation may enter—one that remains faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 4:7; John 16:33; John 14:27"> You will keep in perfect peace the steadfast of mind, because he trusts in You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalm 62:8; Psalm 55:22"> Trust in the LORD forever, because GOD the LORD is the Rock eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:37; Revelation 18:2; Job 40:11–13"> For He has humbled those who dwell on high; He lays the lofty city low. He brings it down to the ground; He casts it into the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Luke 10:19; Revelation 2:26"> Feet trample it down—the feet of the oppressed, the steps of the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Isaiah 42:16; 1 John 3:7"> The path of the righteous is level; You clear a straight path for the upright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 37:3–7; James 5:7–11; Isaiah 33:2"> Yes, we wait for You, O LORD; we walk in the path of Your judgments. Your name and renown are the desire of our souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Psalm 63:1; Psalm 119:62"> My soul longs for You in the night; indeed, my spirit seeks You at dawn. For when Your judgments come upon the earth, the people of the world learn righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 11:7; John 5:37–38; Romans 2:4–5"> Though grace is shown to the wicked man, he does not learn righteousness. In the land of righteousness he acts unjustly and fails to see the majesty of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:27; Micah 5:9; Isaiah 5:24"> O LORD, Your hand is upraised, but they do not see it. They will see Your zeal for Your people and be put to shame. The fire set for Your enemies will consume them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Psalm 57:2; Psalm 29:11"> O LORD, You will establish peace for us. For all that we have accomplished, You have done for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 63:7; Isaiah 2:8; Isaiah 10:11"> O LORD our God, other lords besides You have ruled over us, but Your name alone do we confess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 18:2–3; Proverbs 10:7; Isaiah 51:12–13"> The dead will not live; the departed spirits will not rise. Therefore You have punished and destroyed them; You have wiped out all memory of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:3; Isaiah 33:17; Nehemiah 9:23"> You have enlarged the nation, O LORD; You have enlarged the nation. You have gained glory for Yourself; You have extended all the borders of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Psalm 50:15; Psalm 77:1–2"> O LORD, they sought You in their distress; when You disciplined them, they poured out a quiet prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 21:3; Isaiah 13:8; John 16:21"> As a woman with child about to give birth writhes and cries out in pain, so were we in Your presence, O LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 13:13; Psalm 17:14; 2 Kings 19:3"> We were with child; we writhed in pain; but we gave birth to wind. We have given no salvation to the earth, nor brought any life into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Ezekiel 37:1–14; Matthew 27:52"> Your dead will live; their bodies will rise. Awake and sing, you who dwell in the dust! For your dew is like the dew of the morning, and the earth will bring forth her dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 30:5; Psalm 27:5; Isaiah 54:7–8"> Go, my people, enter your rooms and shut your doors behind you. Hide yourselves a little while until the wrath has passed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 6:9–11; Isaiah 13:11; Jude 1:14–15"> For behold, the LORD is coming out of His dwelling to punish the inhabitants of the earth for their iniquity. The earth will reveal her bloodshed and will no longer conceal her slain.</span>
