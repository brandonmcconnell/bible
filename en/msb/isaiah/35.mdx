<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:35; Isaiah 51:3; Isaiah 55:12–13"> The wilderness and the dry land will be glad; the desert will rejoice and blossom like a rose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Isaiah 60:13; Amos 9:13–15"> It will bloom profusely and rejoice with joy and singing. The glory of Lebanon will be given to it, the splendor of Carmel and Sharon. They will see the glory of the LORD, the splendor of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:12; Job 4:3–4; Luke 22:32"> Strengthen the limp hands and steady the feeble knees!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 5:7–9; Isaiah 41:10–14; Haggai 2:4"> Say to those with anxious hearts: “Be strong, do not fear! Behold, your God will come with vengeance. With divine retribution He will come to save you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 42:6–7; Luke 7:20–23; Isaiah 29:18"> Then the eyes of the blind will be opened and the ears of the deaf unstopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 5:8–9; John 7:37–39; Luke 11:14"> Then the lame will leap like a deer and the mute tongue will shout for joy. For waters will gush forth in the wilderness, and streams in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 26:18; Isaiah 49:10; John 4:14"> The parched ground will become a pool, the thirsty land springs of water. In the haunt where jackals once lay, there will be grass and reeds and papyrus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 7:13–14; Isaiah 42:16; John 14:6"> And there will be a highway called the Way of Holiness. The unclean will not travel it—only those who walk in the Way—and fools will not stray onto it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 62:12; Hosea 2:18; Leviticus 26:6"> No lion will be there, and no vicious beast will go up on it. Such will not be found there, but the redeemed will walk upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 30:19; Revelation 21:4; Isaiah 25:8"> So the redeemed of the LORD will return and enter Zion with singing, crowned with everlasting joy. Gladness and joy will overtake them, and sorrow and sighing will flee.</span>
