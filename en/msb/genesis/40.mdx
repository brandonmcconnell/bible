<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 40:13; Genesis 39:20–23; Esther 6:1"> Some time later, the king’s cupbearer and baker offended their master, the king of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 16:14; Proverbs 19:12; Psalm 76:10"> Pharaoh was angry with his two officers, the chief cupbearer and the chief baker,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 39:20; Genesis 39:23"> and imprisoned them in the house of the captain of the guard, the same prison where Joseph was confined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 39:1; Psalm 37:5; Genesis 37:36"> The captain of the guard assigned them to Joseph, and he became their personal attendant. After they had been in custody for some time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 41:11; Genesis 40:8; Genesis 37:5–10"> both of these men—the Egyptian king’s cupbearer and baker, who were being held in the prison—had a dream on the same night, and each dream had its own meaning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 41:8; Daniel 2:1–3; Genesis 40:8"> When Joseph came to them in the morning, he saw that they were distraught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:2; 1 Samuel 1:8; Luke 24:17"> So he asked the officials of Pharaoh who were in custody with him in his master’s house, “Why are your faces so downcast today?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 2:47; Genesis 41:15–16; Daniel 2:28"> “We both had dreams,” they replied, “but there is no one to interpret them.” Then Joseph said to them, “Don’t interpretations belong to God? Tell me your dreams.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 7:13–15; Genesis 37:5–10; Daniel 4:8"> So the chief cupbearer told Joseph his dream: “In my dream there was a vine before me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and on the vine were three branches. As it budded, its blossoms opened and its clusters ripened into grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11–1; 1 Kings 10:5; 2 Chronicles 9:4"> Pharaoh’s cup was in my hand, and I took the grapes, squeezed them into his cup, and placed the cup in his hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 41:12; Judges 7:14; Genesis 41:25–26"> Joseph replied, “This is the interpretation: The three branches are three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 3:3; Jeremiah 52:31; 2 Kings 25:27"> Within three days Pharaoh will lift up your head and restore your position. You will put Pharaoh’s cup in his hand, just as you did when you were his cupbearer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Luke 23:42; 1 Samuel 20:14–15"> But when it goes well for you, please remember me and show me kindness by mentioning me to Pharaoh, that he might bring me out of this prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17–18; Genesis 39:20; 1 Timothy 1:10"> For I was kidnapped from the land of the Hebrews, and even here I have done nothing for which they should have put me in this dungeon.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 40:1–2"> When the chief baker saw that the interpretation was favorable, he said to Joseph, “I too had a dream: There were three baskets of white bread on my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 49:20; 1 Chronicles 12:20"> In the top basket were all sorts of baked goods for Pharaoh, but the birds were eating them out of the basket on my head.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 40:12; Genesis 41:26; 1 Corinthians 11:24"> Joseph replied, “This is the interpretation: The three baskets are three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 40:22; Acts 20:27; 2 Samuel 21:6"> Within three days Pharaoh will lift off your head and hang you on a tree. Then the birds will eat the flesh of your body.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 40:19; Genesis 40:13; Matthew 14:6"> On the third day, which was Pharaoh’s birthday, he held a feast for all his officials, and in their presence he lifted up the heads of the chief cupbearer and the chief baker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 40:13; Nehemiah 2:1"> Pharaoh restored the chief cupbearer to his position, so that he once again placed the cup in Pharaoh’s hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 40:19; Genesis 41:16; Daniel 2:30"> But Pharaoh hanged the chief baker, just as Joseph had described to them in his interpretation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 31:12; Job 19:14; Psalm 105:19"> The chief cupbearer, however, did not remember Joseph; he forgot all about him.</span>
