<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:14; Song of Solomon 1:15; Matthew 10:16"> Then Noah sent out a dove to see if the waters had receded from the surface of the ground.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:10; Genesis 8:12"> Noah waited seven more days and again sent out the dove from the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:15; Zechariah 4:12–14; Romans 10:15"> And behold, the dove returned to him in the evening with a freshly plucked olive leaf in her beak. So Noah knew that the waters had receded from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Isaiah 30:18; Genesis 8:10"> And Noah waited seven more days and sent out the dove again, but this time she did not return to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 7:11"> In Noah’s six hundred and first year, on the first day of the first month, the waters had dried up from the earth. So Noah removed the covering from the ark and saw that the surface of the ground was dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:13–14; Genesis 7:11"> By the twenty-seventh day of the second month, the earth was fully dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then God said to Noah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 7:13; Psalm 121:8; Psalm 91:11"> “Come out of the ark, you and your wife, along with your sons and their wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:22; Genesis 9:1; Jeremiah 31:27–28"> Bring out all the living creatures that are with you—birds, livestock, and everything that crawls upon the ground—so that they can spread out over the earth and be fruitful and multiply upon it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 121:8"> So Noah came out, along with his sons and his wife and his sons’ wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Every living creature, every creeping thing, and every bird—everything that moves upon the earth—came out of the ark, kind by kind.</span>

And never again will I destroy all living creatures as I have done.</span>
