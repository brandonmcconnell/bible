<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 1:28"> Now when men began to multiply on the face of the earth and daughters were born to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 82:6–7; 1 John 2:16; 2 Peter 2:14"> the sons of God saw that the daughters of men were beautiful, and they took as wives whomever they chose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:30; Isaiah 63:10; Jeremiah 11:11"> So the LORD said, “My Spirit will not contend with man forever, for he is mortal; his days shall be 120 years.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 13:33; 2 Samuel 21:15–22; 1 Samuel 17:4"> The Nephilim were on the earth in those days—and afterward as well—when the sons of God had relations with the daughters of men. And they bore them children who became the mighty men of old, men of renown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; Romans 1:28–31; Mark 7:21–23"> Then the LORD saw that the wickedness of man was great upon the earth, and that every inclination of the thoughts of his heart was altogether evil all the time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; 1 Samuel 15:11; Ephesians 4:30"> And the LORD regretted that He had made man on the earth, and He was grieved in His heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Psalm 37:20; Jeremiah 12:3–4"> So the LORD said, “I will blot out man, whom I have created, from the face of the earth—every man and beast and crawling creature and bird of the air—for I am grieved that I have made them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:19; Hebrews 4:16; Luke 1:30"> Noah, however, found favor in the eyes of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:6; Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5"> This is the account of Noah. Noah was a righteous man, blameless in his generation; Noah walked with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 5:32"> And Noah had three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:17; Hosea 4:1–2; Habakkuk 2:8"> Now the earth was corrupt in the sight of God, and full of violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 53:2–3; Proverbs 15:3; Psalm 14:1–3"> And God looked upon the earth and saw that it was corrupt; for all living creatures on the earth had corrupted their ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:1–2; 1 Peter 4:7; Ezekiel 7:2–6"> Then God said to Noah, “The end of all living creatures has come before Me, because through them the earth is full of violence. Now behold, I will destroy both them and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:20; Luke 17:27; Matthew 24:38"> Make for yourself an ark of gopher wood; make rooms in the ark and coat it with pitch inside and out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11; Genesis 7:20"> And this is how you are to build it: The ark is to be 300 cubits long, 50 cubits wide, and 30 cubits high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 8:6; Genesis 7:16; Ezekiel 41:16"> You are to make a roof for the ark, finish its walls a cubit from the top, place a door in the side of the ark, and build lower, middle, and upper decks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; 2 Peter 2:5; Genesis 7:21–23"> And behold, I will bring floodwaters upon the earth to destroy every creature under the heavens that has the breath of life. Everything on the earth will perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; Genesis 7:7; Genesis 17:7"> But I will establish My covenant with you, and you will enter the ark—you and your sons and your wife and your sons’ wives with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 7:15–16; Genesis 7:8–9; Genesis 8:17"> And you are to bring two of every living creature into the ark—male and female—to keep them alive with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 10:11–12; Genesis 2:19; Genesis 7:8–16"> Two of every kind of bird and animal and crawling creature will come to you to be kept alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 1:29–30; Psalm 145:16; Psalm 136:25"> You are also to take for yourself every kind of food that is eaten and gather it as food for yourselves and for the animals.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; John 15:14; Matthew 7:24–27"> So Noah did everything precisely as God had commanded him.</span>
