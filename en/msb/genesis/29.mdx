<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 6:3; Judges 6:33; Hosea 12:12"> Jacob resumed his journey and came to the land of the people of the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 49:10; John 4:6"> He looked and saw a well in the field, and near it lay three flocks of sheep, because the sheep were watered from this well. And a large stone covered the mouth of the well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> When all the flocks had been gathered there, the shepherds would roll away the stone from the mouth of the well and water the sheep. Then they would return the stone to its place over the mouth of the well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:10; Genesis 27:43; Acts 7:2"> “My brothers,” Jacob asked the shepherds, “where are you from?” “We are from Haran,” they answered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 24:24; Genesis 24:29; Genesis 31:53"> “Do you know Laban the grandson of Nahor?” Jacob asked. “We know him,” they replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:9; Genesis 37:14; Exodus 18:7"> “Is he well?” Jacob inquired. “Yes,” they answered, “and here comes his daughter Rachel with his sheep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:16; Galatians 6:9–10"> “Look,” said Jacob, “it is still broad daylight; it is not yet time to gather the livestock. Water the sheep and take them back to pasture.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 16:3; Luke 24:2; Genesis 34:14"> But they replied, “We cannot, until all the flocks have been gathered and the stone has been rolled away from the mouth of the well. Then we will water the sheep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:21; Song of Solomon 1:7–8; Exodus 2:15–16"> While he was still speaking with them, Rachel arrived with her father’s sheep, for she was a shepherdess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 2:17"> As soon as Jacob saw Rachel, the daughter of his mother’s brother Laban, with Laban’s sheep, he went up and rolled the stone away from the mouth of the well and watered his uncle’s sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 33:4; Genesis 45:14–15; Genesis 45:2"> Then Jacob kissed Rachel and wept aloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 24:28; Genesis 13:8; Genesis 14:14–16"> He told Rachel that he was Rebekah’s son, a relative of her father, and she ran and told her father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 24:29; Acts 20:37; Romans 16:16"> When Laban heard the news about his sister’s son Jacob, he ran out to meet him. He embraced him and kissed him and brought him to his home, where Jacob told him all that had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:12–13; Genesis 2:23; Judges 9:2"> Then Laban declared, “You are indeed my own flesh and blood.” After Jacob had stayed with him a month,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 30:28; Genesis 31:7"> Laban said to him, “Just because you are my relative, should you work for nothing? Tell me what your wages should be.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ruth 4:11; Genesis 49:31; Genesis 31:4"> Now Laban had two daughters; the older was named Leah, and the younger was named Rachel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 12:11; Genesis 46:19–22; Genesis 24:16"> Leah had weak eyes, but Rachel was shapely and beautiful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 12:12; Exodus 22:16–17; Genesis 34:12"> Since Jacob loved Rachel, he answered, “I will serve you seven years for your younger daughter Rachel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 6:11; Isaiah 6:5; Psalm 12:2"> Laban replied, “Better that I give her to you than to another. Stay here with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:7; Ephesians 5:2; Hosea 12:12"> So Jacob served seven years for Rachel, yet it seemed but a few days because of his love for her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 15:1; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 4:1"> Finally Jacob said to Laban, “Grant me my wife, for my time is complete, and I want to sleep with her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; Ruth 4:10–13; Judges 14:10–18"> So Laban invited all the men of that place and prepared a feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Micah 7:5; Genesis 38:14–15; Genesis 24:65"> But when evening came, Laban took his daughter Leah and gave her to Jacob, and he slept with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 16:1; Genesis 30:9–12; Genesis 46:18"> And Laban gave his servant girl Zilpah to his daughter Leah as her maidservant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Proverbs 11:31; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> When morning came, there was Leah! “What have you done to me?” Jacob said to Laban. “Wasn’t it for Rachel that I served you? Why have you deceived me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Laban replied, “It is not our custom here to give the younger daughter in marriage before the older.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 18:18; Judges 14:12; Genesis 2:2–3"> Finish this week’s celebration, and we will give you the younger one in return for another seven years of work.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Jacob did just that. He finished the week’s celebration, and Laban gave him his daughter Rachel as his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; Genesis 29:24; Genesis 30:3–8"> Laban also gave his servant girl Bilhah to his daughter Rachel as her maidservant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 31:41; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 31:15"> Jacob slept with Rachel as well, and indeed, he loved Rachel more than Leah. So he worked for Laban another seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:21; Matthew 10:37; Deuteronomy 21:15"> When the LORD saw that Leah was unloved, He opened her womb; but Rachel was barren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:7; Exodus 4:31; Exodus 3:7"> And Leah conceived and gave birth to a son, and she named him Reuben, for she said, “The LORD has seen my affliction. Surely my husband will love me now.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 34:25; Genesis 49:5–6; Genesis 34:30"> Again she conceived and gave birth to a son, and she said, “Because the LORD has heard that I am unloved, He has given me this son as well.” So she named him Simeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 32:26–29; Genesis 49:5–7; Genesis 46:11"> Once again Leah conceived and gave birth to a son, and she said, “Now at last my husband will become attached to me, because I have borne him three sons.” So he was named Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 1:2; 1 Chronicles 5:2; Genesis 49:8–12"> And once more she conceived and gave birth to a son and said, “This time I will praise the LORD.” So she named him Judah. Then Leah stopped having children.</span>
