<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 5:1; Revelation 1:8; Genesis 1:4"> This is the account of the heavens and the earth when they were created, in the day that the LORD God made them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 3:23; Hebrews 6:7; Job 5:10"> Now no shrub of the field had yet appeared on the earth, nor had any plant of the field sprouted, for the LORD God had not yet sent rain upon the earth, and there was no man to cultivate the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But springs welled up from the earth and watered the whole surface of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 33:4; 1 Corinthians 15:45"> Then the LORD God formed man from the dust of the ground and breathed the breath of life into his nostrils, and the man became a living being.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Genesis 3:24; Genesis 13:10"> And the LORD God planted a garden in Eden, in the east, where He placed the man He had formed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 3:22; Revelation 2:7; Revelation 22:14"> Out of the ground the LORD God gave growth to every tree that is pleasing to the eye and good for food. And in the middle of the garden were the tree of life and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Psalm 46:4"> Now a river flowed out of Eden to water the garden, and from there it branched into four headwaters:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Genesis 10:29; 1 Samuel 15:7"> The name of the first river is the Pishon; it winds through the whole land of Havilah, where there is gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 28:20; Numbers 11:7; Ezekiel 28:13"> And the gold of that land is pure, and bdellium and onyx are found there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:6; Isaiah 11:11"> The name of the second river is the Gihon; it winds through the whole land of Cush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 10:4; Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 1:7"> The name of the third river is the Tigris; it runs along the east side of Assyria. And the fourth river is the Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 128:2; Genesis 2:8; Ephesians 4:28"> Then the LORD God took the man and placed him in the Garden of Eden to cultivate and keep it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; 1 Timothy 4:4; 1 Timothy 6:17"> And the LORD God commanded him, “You may eat freely from every tree of the garden,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:23; James 1:15; Genesis 3:19"> but you must not eat from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil; for in the day that you eat of it, you will surely die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:9–12; 1 Corinthians 11:7–12; Proverbs 18:22"> The LORD God also said, “It is not good for the man to be alone. I will make for him a suitable helper.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 8:4–8; Genesis 1:28; Genesis 9:2"> And out of the ground the LORD God formed every beast of the field and every bird of the air, and He brought them to the man to see what he would name each one. And whatever the man called each living creature, that was its name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:18"> The man gave names to all the livestock, to the birds of the air, and to every beast of the field. But for Adam no suitable helper was found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:12; Job 4:13; 1 Samuel 26:12"> So the LORD God caused the man to fall into a deep sleep, and while he slept, He took one of the man’s ribs and closed up the area with flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:8–9; 1 Timothy 2:13; Hebrews 13:4"> And from the rib that the LORD God had taken from the man, He made a woman and brought her to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 5:28–30; 1 Corinthians 11:8–9; 2 Samuel 5:1"> And the man said: “This is now bone of my bones and flesh of my flesh; she shall be called ‘woman,’ for out of man she was taken.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 19:3–9; Ephesians 5:28–31; Mark 10:6–12"> For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be united to his wife, and they will become one flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 3:7; Genesis 3:10–11; Romans 10:11"> And the man and his wife were both naked, and they were not ashamed.</span>
