<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 1:1–3; Hebrews 11:3; Isaiah 45:18"> In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:23; Psalm 104:30; Isaiah 45:18"> Now the earth was formless and void, and darkness was over the surface of the deep. And the Spirit of God was hovering over the surface of the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:6; John 1:5; Isaiah 45:7"> And God said, “Let there be light,” and there was light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:13; Genesis 1:18; Ecclesiastes 11:7"> And God saw that the light was good, and He separated the light from the darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 74:16; Isaiah 45:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:5"> God called the light “day,” and the darkness He called “night.” And there was evening, and there was morning—the first day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:15; Psalm 33:6; Job 37:18"> And God said, “Let there be an expanse between the waters, to separate the waters from the waters.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 8:28–29; Psalm 148:4; Matthew 8:27"> So God made the expanse and separated the waters beneath it from the waters above. And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:5; Genesis 1:19; Genesis 1:10"> God called the expanse “sky.” And there was evening, and there was morning—the second day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Peter 3:5; Jonah 1:9; Jeremiah 5:22"> And God said, “Let the waters under the sky be gathered into one place, so that the dry land may appear.” And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Psalm 104:31; Genesis 1:4"> God called the dry land “earth,” and the gathering of waters He called “seas.” And God saw that it was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 104:14–17; Hebrews 6:7; Psalm 147:8"> Then God said, “Let the earth bring forth vegetation: seed-bearing plants and fruit trees, each bearing fruit with seed according to its kind.” And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:28; Isaiah 55:10–11; Matthew 13:24–26"> The earth produced vegetation: seed-bearing plants according to their kinds and trees bearing fruit with seed according to their kinds. And God saw that it was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And there was evening, and there was morning—the third day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 104:19–20; Deuteronomy 4:19; Jeremiah 31:35"> And God said, “Let there be lights in the expanse of the sky to distinguish between the day and the night, and let them be signs to mark the seasons and days and years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And let them serve as lights in the expanse of the sky to shine upon the earth.” And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 136:7–9; Deuteronomy 4:19; Psalm 8:3"> God made two great lights: the greater light to rule the day and the lesser light to rule the night. And He made the stars as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 8:3; Psalm 8:1; Job 38:12"> God set these lights in the expanse of the sky to shine upon the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35; Psalm 19:6"> to preside over the day and the night, and to separate the light from the darkness. And God saw that it was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And there was evening, and there was morning—the fourth day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:19; Psalm 104:24–25; 1 Kings 4:33"> And God said, “Let the waters teem with living creatures, and let birds fly above the earth in the open expanse of the sky.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 104:24–26; Genesis 1:25; Genesis 8:19"> So God created the great sea creatures and every living thing that moves, with which the waters teemed according to their kinds, and every winged bird after its kind. And God saw that it was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 40:15; Genesis 8:17; Psalm 107:38"> Then God blessed them and said, “Be fruitful and multiply and fill the waters of the seas, and let birds multiply on the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And there was evening, and there was morning—the fifth day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 50:9–10; Genesis 6:20; Genesis 8:19"> And God said, “Let the earth bring forth living creatures according to their kinds: livestock, land crawlers, and beasts of the earth according to their kinds.” And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5; Job 12:8–10; Genesis 2:19–20"> God made the beasts of the earth according to their kinds, the livestock according to their kinds, and everything that crawls upon the earth according to its kind. And God saw that it was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 3:10; Ephesians 4:24; Psalm 8:4–8"> Then God said, “Let Us make man in Our image, after Our likeness, to rule over the fish of the sea and the birds of the air, over the livestock, and over all the earth itself and every creature that crawls upon it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 19:4; Ephesians 2:10; Genesis 5:1–2"> So God created man in His own image; in the image of God He created him; male and female He created them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 9:1; Genesis 9:7; Leviticus 26:9"> God blessed them and said to them, “Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth and subdue it; rule over the fish of the sea and the birds of the air and every creature that crawls upon the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 9:3; Psalm 104:14–15; Psalm 145:15–16"> Then God said, “Behold, I have given you every seed-bearing plant on the face of all the earth, and every tree whose fruit contains seed. They will be yours for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalm 104:14; Psalm 145:15–16; Psalm 147:9"> And to every beast of the earth and every bird of the air and every creature that crawls upon the earth—everything that has the breath of life in it—I have given every green plant for food.” And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; Psalm 104:24; Psalm 19:1–2"> And God looked upon all that He had made, and indeed, it was very good. And there was evening, and there was morning—the sixth day.</span>
</div>
