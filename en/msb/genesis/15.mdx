<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:14; Proverbs 29:21; Ecclesiastes 2:7"> Abram continued, “Behold, You have given me no offspring, so a servant in my household will be my heir.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 17:16; Galatians 4:28; 2 Samuel 7:12"> Then the word of the LORD came to Abram, saying, “This one will not be your heir, but one who comes from your own body will be your heir.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:12; Romans 4:18; Deuteronomy 1:10"> And the LORD took him outside and said, “Now look to the heavens and count the stars, if you are able.” Then He told him, “So shall your offspring be.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 2:23; Galatians 3:6–14; Romans 4:9"> Abram believed the LORD, and it was credited to him as righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 12:1; Acts 7:2–4; Romans 4:13"> The LORD also told him, “I am the LORD, who brought you out of Ur of the Chaldeans to give you this land to possess.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 1:18; Luke 1:34; Isaiah 7:11"> But Abram replied, “Lord GOD, how can I know that I will possess it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 2:24; Leviticus 14:30; Leviticus 14:22"> And the LORD said to him, “Bring Me a heifer, a goat, and a ram, each three years old, along with a turtledove and a young pigeon.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:18–19; Leviticus 1:17; Genesis 15:17"> So Abram brought all these to Him, split each of them down the middle, and laid the halves opposite each other. The birds, however, he did not cut in half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:4; Psalm 119:13; Ezekiel 17:3"> And the birds of prey descended on the carcasses, but Abram drove them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 2:21; Job 33:15; Daniel 10:8–9"> As the sun was setting, Abram fell into a deep sleep, and suddenly great terror and darkness overwhelmed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:6–7; Exodus 1:11; Exodus 12:40–41"> Then the LORD said to Abram, “Know for certain that your descendants will be strangers in a land that is not their own, and they will be enslaved and mistreated four hundred years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:9–11; Exodus 6:5–6; Exodus 12:32–38"> But I will judge the nation they serve as slaves, and afterward they will depart with many possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 5:26; Genesis 25:7–9; 2 Chronicles 34:28"> You, however, will go to your fathers in peace and be buried at a ripe old age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 21:26; Daniel 8:23; Exodus 12:40"> In the fourth generation your descendants will return here, for the iniquity of the Amorites is not yet complete.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:18–19; Judges 6:21; Jeremiah 11:4"> When the sun had set and darkness had fallen, behold, a smoking firepot and a flaming torch appeared and passed between the halves of the carcasses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 1:3–4; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 24:7"> On that day the LORD made a covenant with Abram, saying, “To your descendants I have given this land—from the river of Egypt to the great River Euphrates—</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 24:21–22"> the land of the Kenites, Kenizzites, Kadmonites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 17:5; Genesis 14:5"> Hittites, Perizzites, Rephaites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 10:15–19; Exodus 23:23–28; Deuteronomy 7:1"> Amorites, Canaanites, Girgashites, and Jebusites.”</span>
