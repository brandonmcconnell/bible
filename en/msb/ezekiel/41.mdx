<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:47"> Then he measured the temple to be a hundred cubits long, and the temple courtyard and the building with its walls were also a hundred cubits long.</span>

The outer sanctuary, the inner sanctuary, and the porticoes facing the court,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

They were paneled from the ground to the windows, and the windows were covered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:36; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:22"> were alternating carved cherubim and palm trees. Each cherub had two faces:</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:1; 1 Kings 6:33; Ezekiel 40:14"> The outer sanctuary had a rectangular doorframe, and the doorframe of the sanctuary was similar.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
