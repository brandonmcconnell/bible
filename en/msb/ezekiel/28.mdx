</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 1:20; Daniel 2:22; Daniel 2:27–28"> Behold, you are wiser than Daniel; no secret is hidden from you!</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 62:10; Psalm 52:7; Ezekiel 28:2"> By your great skill in trading you have increased your wealth, but your heart has grown proud because of it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Exodus 9:17; James 1:11"> Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you regard your heart as the heart of a god,</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:12; Ezekiel 32:12; Ezekiel 30:11"> behold, I will bring foreigners against you, the most ruthless of nations. They will draw their swords against the beauty of your wisdom and will defile your splendor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:26–27; Ezekiel 27:34; Proverbs 1:12"> They will bring you down to the Pit, and you will die a violent death in the heart of the seas.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Isaiah 31:3; Psalm 82:7"> Will you still say, ‘I am a god,’ in the presence of those who slay you? You will be only a man, not a god, in the hands of those who wound you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:18; Ezekiel 32:19; Philippians 3:3"> You will die the death of the uncircumcised at the hands of foreigners. For I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.”</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Again the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1; Ezekiel 26:17; 2 Corinthians 1:22"> “Son of man, take up a lament for the king of Tyre and tell him that this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘You were the seal of perfection, full of wisdom and perfect in beauty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:15; Genesis 2:8; Ezekiel 27:16"> You were in Eden, the garden of God. Your mountings and settings were crafted in gold, prepared on the day of your creation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 14:12–15; Exodus 30:26; Ezekiel 28:13"> You were anointed as a guardian cherub, for I had ordained you. You were on the holy mountain of God; you walked among the fiery stones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Ezekiel 28:17–18; Romans 7:9"> From the day you were created you were blameless in your ways—until wickedness was found in you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Habakkuk 2:8; Habakkuk 2:17"> By the vastness of your trade, you were filled with violence, and you sinned. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:5; Ezekiel 31:10; James 4:6"> Your heart grew proud of your beauty; you corrupted your wisdom because of your splendor; so I cast you to the earth; I made you a spectacle before kings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Revelation 18:8; Amos 1:9–10"> By the multitude of your iniquities and the dishonesty of your trading you have profaned your sanctuaries. So I made fire come from within you, and it consumed you. I reduced you to ashes on the ground in the eyes of all who saw you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:21; Revelation 18:21; Revelation 18:15–19"> All the nations who know you are appalled over you. You have come to a horrible end and will be no more.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Genesis 10:15; Jeremiah 25:22"> “Son of man, set your face against Sidon and prophesy against her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:13; Exodus 14:17; Ezekiel 20:41"> And you are to declare that this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Behold, I am against you, O Sidon, and I will be glorified within you. They will know that I am the LORD when I execute judgments against her and demonstrate My holiness through her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Ezekiel 26:6; Ezekiel 25:11"> I will send a plague against her and shed blood in her streets; the slain will fall within her, while the sword is against her on every side. Then they will know that I am the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 33:55; Joshua 23:13; 2 Corinthians 12:7"> For the people of Israel will no longer face a pricking brier or a painful thorn from all around them who treat them with contempt. Then they will know that I am the Lord GOD.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:37; Ezekiel 20:41; Ezekiel 36:28"> This is what the Lord GOD says: ‘When I gather the house of Israel from the peoples among whom they have been scattered, I will show Myself holy among them in the sight of the nations. Then they will dwell in their own land, which I have given to My servant Jacob.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:24; Ezekiel 28:22; Jeremiah 32:15"> And there they will dwell securely, build houses, and plant vineyards. They will dwell securely when I execute judgments against all those around them who treat them with contempt. Then they will know that I am the LORD their God.’”</span>
</div>
