<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46; Ezekiel 4:7; Ezekiel 25:2"> “Son of man, set your face against Jerusalem and preach against the sanctuaries. Prophesy against the land of Israel</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 9:22; Jeremiah 21:13; Ezekiel 5:8"> and tell her that this is what the LORD says: ‘I am against you, and I will draw My sword from its sheath and cut off from you both the righteous and the wicked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:47; Ezekiel 7:2; Ezekiel 6:11–14"> Because I will cut off both the righteous and the wicked, My sword will be unsheathed against everyone from south to north.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:30; Nahum 1:9; 1 Samuel 3:12"> Then all flesh will know that I, the LORD, have taken My sword from its sheath, not to return it again.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 22:4; Ezekiel 6:11; Jeremiah 19:10"> But you, son of man, groan! Groan before their eyes with a broken heart and bitter grief.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:17; Ezekiel 7:26; Isaiah 13:7"> And when they ask, ‘Why are you groaning?’ you are to say, ‘Because of the news that is coming. Every heart will melt, and every hand will go limp. Every spirit will faint, and every knee will turn to water.’ Yes, it is coming and it will surely happen, declares the Lord GOD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Again the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 34:5–6; Ezekiel 21:28; Ezekiel 21:3"> “Son of man, prophesy and tell them that this is what the Lord says: ‘A sword, a sword, sharpened and polished—</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 110:5–6; Ezekiel 20:47; Ezekiel 21:25–27"> it is sharpened for the slaughter, polished to flash like lightning! Should we rejoice in the scepter of My son? The sword despises every such stick.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:19; Jeremiah 25:33; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> The sword is appointed to be polished, to be grasped in the hand. It is sharpened and polished, to be placed in the hand of the slayer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:6; Joel 1:13; Ezekiel 21:14"> Cry out and wail, O son of man, for the sword is wielded against My people; it is against all the princes of Israel! They are tossed to the sword with My people; therefore strike your thigh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:10; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Ezekiel 21:25"> Surely testing will come! And what if even the scepter, which the sword despises, does not continue?’ declares the Lord GOD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 24:1; Numbers 24:10; Leviticus 26:24"> ‘So then, son of man, prophesy and strike your hands together. Let the sword strike two times, even three. It is a sword that slays, a sword of great slaughter closing in on every side!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:10; Jeremiah 17:27; Ezekiel 21:22"> So that their hearts may melt and many may stumble, I have appointed at all their gates a sword for slaughter. Yes, it is ready to flash like lightning; it is drawn for slaughter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; Ezekiel 21:4; Ezekiel 14:17"> Slash to the right; set your blade to the left—wherever your blade is directed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Ezekiel 21:14; Zechariah 6:8"> I too will strike My hands together, and I will satisfy My wrath.’ I, the LORD, have spoken.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:1–3; Jeremiah 1:10; Ezekiel 5:1–17"> “Now you, son of man, mark out two roads for the sword of the king of Babylon to take, both starting from the same land. And make a signpost where the road branches off to each city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Amos 1:14; Jeremiah 49:2; Ezekiel 25:5"> Mark out one road for the sword to come against Rabbah of the Ammonites, and another against Judah into fortified Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 18:20; Proverbs 16:33; Judges 17:5"> For the king of Babylon stands at the fork in the road, at the junction of the two roads, to seek an omen: He shakes the arrows, he consults the idols, he examines the liver.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:2; Ezekiel 26:9; Jeremiah 51:14"> In his right hand appears the portent for Jerusalem, where he is to set up battering rams, to call for the slaughter, to lift a battle cry, to direct the battering rams against the gates, to build a ramp, and to erect a siege wall.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 5:15; Ezekiel 21:24; Ezekiel 29:16"> It will seem like a false omen to the eyes of those who have sworn allegiance to him, but it will draw attention to their guilt and take them captive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 4:2; Micah 3:10–12; Jeremiah 3:2"> Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Because you have drawn attention to your guilt, exposing your transgressions, so that your sins are revealed in all your deeds—because you have come to remembrance—you shall be taken in hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:5; Ezekiel 21:29; Ezekiel 17:19"> And you, O profane and wicked prince of Israel, the day has come for your final punishment.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; Psalm 75:7; Jeremiah 13:18"> This is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Remove the turban, and take off the crown. Things will not remain as they are: Exalt the lowly and bring low the exalted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Zechariah 9:9"> A ruin, a ruin, I will make it a ruin! And it will not be restored until the arrival of Him to whom it belongs, to whom I have assigned the right of judgment.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; Ezekiel 21:9–10; Zephaniah 2:8–10"> Now prophesy, son of man, and declare that this is what the Lord GOD says concerning the Ammonites and their contempt: ‘A sword! A sword is drawn for slaughter, polished to consume, to flash like lightning—</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:25; Ezekiel 22:28; Psalm 37:13"> while they offer false visions for you and lying divinations about you—to be placed on the necks of the wicked who are slain, whose day has come, the time of their final punishment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:38; Genesis 15:14; Jeremiah 47:6–7"> Return the sword to its sheath! In the place where you were created, in the land of your origin, I will judge you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Jeremiah 6:22–23; Ezekiel 14:19"> I will pour out My anger upon you; I will breathe the fire of My fury against you; I will hand you over to brutal men, skilled in destruction.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:10; Malachi 4:1; Ezekiel 20:47–48"> You will be fuel for the fire. Your blood will stain your own land. You will not be remembered, for I, the LORD, have spoken.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
