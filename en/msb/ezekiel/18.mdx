<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:29–30; Lamentations 5:7; Ezekiel 37:11"> “What do you people mean by quoting this proverb about the land of Israel: ‘The fathers have eaten sour grapes, and the teeth of the children are set on edge’?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:19–20; Ezekiel 33:11–20; Ezekiel 18:30"> As surely as I live, declares the Lord GOD, you will no longer quote this proverb in Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:20; Romans 6:23; Numbers 16:22"> Behold, every soul belongs to Me; both father and son are Mine. The soul who sins is the one who will die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:14; 1 John 3:7; Proverbs 21:3"> Now suppose a man is righteous and does what is just and right:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:15; Deuteronomy 4:19; Ezekiel 6:13"> He does not eat at the mountain or look to the idols of the house of Israel. He does not defile his neighbor’s wife or approach a woman during her period.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:16; Exodus 22:26; Ezekiel 18:12"> He does not oppress another, but restores the pledge to the debtor. He does not commit robbery, but gives his bread to the hungry and covers the naked with clothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Zechariah 8:16; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> He does not engage in usury or take excess interest, but he withholds his hand from iniquity and executes true justice between men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:11; Habakkuk 2:4; Amos 5:4"> He follows My statutes and faithfully keeps My ordinances. That man is righteous; surely he will live, declares the Lord GOD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 21:12; 1 John 3:12; John 18:40"> Now suppose that man has a violent son, who sheds blood or does any of these things,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 22:14; Matthew 7:21–27; 1 Kings 13:8"> though the father has done none of them: Indeed, the son eats at the mountain and defiles his neighbor’s wife.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:17; Ezekiel 8:6; 2 Kings 21:11"> He oppresses the poor and needy; he commits robbery and does not restore a pledge. He lifts his eyes to idols; he commits abominations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Leviticus 20:9; Ezekiel 3:18"> He engages in usury and takes excess interest. Will this son live? He will not! Since he has committed all these abominations, he will surely die; his blood will be on his own head.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:21; Proverbs 23:24; Jeremiah 8:6"> Now suppose this son has a son who sees all the sins his father has committed, considers them, and does not do likewise:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:6–7; Ezekiel 18:11–13"> He does not eat at the mountain or look to the idols of the house of Israel. He does not defile his neighbor’s wife.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:7; Isaiah 58:7–10; Job 22:7"> He does not oppress another, or retain a pledge, or commit robbery. He gives his bread to the hungry and covers the naked with clothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:8–9; Matthew 18:27–35; Ezekiel 18:13"> He withholds his hand from harming the poor and takes no interest or usury. He keeps My ordinances and follows My statutes. Such a man will not die for his father’s iniquity. He will surely live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:18; Ezekiel 18:26; Ezekiel 18:24"> As for his father, he will die for his own iniquity, because he practiced extortion, robbed his brother, and did what was wrong among his people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Zechariah 1:3–6; Deuteronomy 5:9"> Yet you may ask, ‘Why shouldn’t the son bear the iniquity of his father?’ Since the son has done what is just and right, carefully observing all My statutes, he will surely live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:16; 2 Kings 14:6; Ezekiel 18:4"> The soul who sins is the one who will die. A son will not bear the iniquity of his father, and a father will not bear the iniquity of his son. The righteousness of the righteous man will fall upon him, and the wickedness of the wicked man will fall upon him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:27–28; Titus 2:11–14; Ezekiel 33:19"> But if the wicked man turns from all the sins he has committed, keeps all My statutes, and does what is just and right, he will surely live; he will not die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 7:19; Ezekiel 33:16; Isaiah 43:25"> None of the transgressions he has committed will be held against him. Because of the righteousness he has practiced, he will live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:11; 1 Timothy 2:4; 2 Peter 3:9"> Do I take any pleasure in the death of the wicked? declares the Lord GOD. Wouldn’t I prefer that he turn from his ways and live?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:18; Proverbs 21:16; 2 John 1:8"> But if a righteous man turns from his righteousness and practices iniquity, committing the same abominations as the wicked, will he live? None of the righteous acts he did will be remembered. Because of the unfaithfulness and sin he has committed, he will die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:17; Malachi 3:13–15; Malachi 2:17"> Yet you say, ‘The way of the Lord is not just.’ Hear now, O house of Israel: Is it My way that is unjust? Is it not your ways that are unjust?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> If a righteous man turns from his righteousness and practices iniquity, he will die for this. He will die because of the iniquity he has committed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:21; Isaiah 1:18; Isaiah 55:7"> But if a wicked man turns from the wickedness he has committed and does what is just and right, he will save his life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Luke 15:17–18; James 2:10–12"> Because he considered and turned from all the transgressions he had committed, he will surely live; he will not die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:25; Proverbs 19:3; Ezekiel 18:2"> Yet the house of Israel says, ‘The way of the Lord is not just.’ Are My ways unjust, O house of Israel? Is it not your ways that are unjust?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:21; Matthew 16:27; Matthew 3:2"> Therefore, O house of Israel, I will judge you, each according to his ways, declares the Lord GOD. Repent and turn from all your transgressions, so that your iniquity will not become your downfall.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalm 51:10; Ezekiel 36:26; Ezekiel 11:19"> Cast away from yourselves all the transgressions you have committed, and fashion for yourselves a new heart and a new spirit. Why should you die, O house of Israel?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Ezekiel 18:23; Ezekiel 33:11"> For I take no pleasure in anyone’s death, declares the Lord GOD. So repent and live!</span>
</div>
</div>
