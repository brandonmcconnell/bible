<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:21; Ezekiel 1:2–3; Ezekiel 3:14"> In the twenty-fifth year of our exile, at the beginning of the year, on the tenth day of the month—in the fourteenth year after Jerusalem had been struck down—on that very day the hand of the LORD was upon me, and He took me there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:1; Daniel 7:1; Daniel 7:7"> In visions of God He took me to the land of Israel and set me on a very high mountain, on whose southern slope was a structure that resembled a city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:3; Revelation 11:1; Daniel 10:5–6"> So He took me there, and I saw a man whose appearance was like bronze. He was standing in the gateway with a linen cord and a measuring rod in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:5; Matthew 10:27; Ezekiel 2:7–8"> “Son of man,” he said to me, “look with your eyes, hear with your ears, and pay attention to everything I am going to show you, for that is why you have been brought here. Report to the house of Israel everything you see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:20; Isaiah 26:1; Revelation 21:12"> And I saw a wall surrounding the temple area. Now the length of the measuring rod in the man’s hand was six long cubits (each measuring a cubit and a handbreadth), and he measured the wall to be one rod thick and one rod high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:1; Ezekiel 8:16; Ezekiel 11:1"> Then he came to the gate facing east and climbed its steps. He measured the threshold of the gate to be one rod deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:36; Ezekiel 40:33; 1 Chronicles 23:28"> Each gate chamber was one rod long and one rod wide, and there were five cubits between the gate chambers. The inner threshold of the gate by the portico facing inward was one rod deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then he measured the portico of the gateway inside;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:19"> it was eight cubits deep, and its jambs were two cubits thick. And the portico of the gateway faced the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> There were three gate chambers on each side of the east gate, each with the same measurements, and the gateposts on either side also had the same measurements.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he measured the width of the gateway entrance to be ten cubits, and its length was thirteen cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> In front of each gate chamber was a wall one cubit high, and the gate chambers were six cubits square.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then he measured the gateway from the roof of one gate chamber to the roof of the opposite one; the distance was twenty-five cubits from doorway to doorway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Isaiah 62:9; Ezekiel 42:1"> Next he measured the gateposts to be sixty cubits high. The gateway extended around to the gatepost of the courtyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the distance from the entrance of the gateway to the far end of its inner portico was fifty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:5; 1 Kings 6:4; Ezekiel 41:26"> The gate chambers and their side pillars had beveled windows all around the inside of the gateway. The porticos also had windows all around on the inside. Each side pillar was decorated with palm trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Ezekiel 42:1; 1 Chronicles 23:28"> Then he brought me into the outer court, and there were chambers and a pavement laid out all around the court. Thirty chambers faced the pavement,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> which flanked the gateways and corresponded to the length of the gates; this was the lower pavement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:23; Ezekiel 40:27; Ezekiel 46:1–2"> Then he measured the distance from the front of the lower gateway to the outside of the inner court; it was a hundred cubits on the east side as well as on the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:6"> He also measured the length and width of the gateway of the outer court facing north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:29–30; Ezekiel 40:25–26; Ezekiel 40:10–16"> Its three gate chambers on each side, its side pillars, and its portico all had the same measurements as the first gate: fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:31; Ezekiel 40:37; Ezekiel 40:6"> Its windows, portico, and palm trees had the same measurements as those of the gate facing east. Seven steps led up to it, with its portico opposite them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:19; Ezekiel 40:27–28; Exodus 38:9–12"> There was a gate to the inner court facing the north gate, just as there was on the east. He measured the distance from gateway to gateway to be a hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:9; Ezekiel 40:6; Ezekiel 40:20–21"> Then he led me to the south side, and I saw a gateway facing south. He measured its side pillars and portico, and they had the same measurements as the others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:33; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:21–22"> Both the gateway and its portico had windows all around, like the other windows. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:22; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:6"> Seven steps led up to it, and its portico was opposite them; it had palm trees on its side pillars, one on each side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:23; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 40:19"> The inner court also had a gate facing south, and he measured the distance from gateway to gateway toward the south to be a hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:35; Ezekiel 40:32"> Next he brought me into the inner court through the south gate, and he measured the south gate; it had the same measurements as the others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:25; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:7"> Its gate chambers, side pillars, and portico had the same measurements as the others. Both the gateway and its portico had windows all around; it was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:21; Ezekiel 40:36; Ezekiel 40:25"> (The porticoes around the inner court were twenty-five cubits long and five cubits deep.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:26; Ezekiel 40:34; Ezekiel 40:22"> Its portico faced the outer court, and its side pillars were decorated with palm trees. Eight steps led up to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:35; Ezekiel 40:28–31"> And he brought me to the inner court on the east side, and he measured the gateway; it had the same measurements as the others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:21; Ezekiel 40:36; Ezekiel 40:25"> Its gate chambers, side pillars, and portico had the same measurements as the others. Both the gateway and its portico had windows all around. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:22; Ezekiel 40:31; Ezekiel 40:37"> Its portico faced the outer court, and its side pillars were decorated with palm trees on each side. Eight steps led up to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:2; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 44:4"> Then he brought me to the north gate and measured it. It had the same measurements as the others,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:29; Ezekiel 40:21; Ezekiel 40:16"> as did its gate chambers, side pillars, and portico. It also had windows all around. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:34; Ezekiel 40:31"> Its portico faced the outer court, and its side pillars were decorated with palm trees on each side. Eight steps led up to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:6; Ezekiel 40:17; Leviticus 8:21"> There was a chamber with a doorway by the portico in each of the inner gateways. There the burnt offering was to be washed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:2; Leviticus 1:3–17; Leviticus 4:2–3"> Inside the portico of the gateway were two tables on each side, on which the burnt offerings, sin offerings, and guilt offerings were to be slaughtered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Outside, as one goes up to the entrance of the north gateway, there were two tables on one side and two more tables on the other side of the gate’s portico.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:39–40"> So there were four tables inside the gateway and four outside—eight tables in all—on which the sacrifices were to be slaughtered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:39; Exodus 20:25"> There were also four tables of dressed stone for the burnt offering, each a cubit and a half long, a cubit and a half wide, and a cubit high. On these were placed the utensils used to slaughter the burnt offerings and the other sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 1:8; Leviticus 1:6; Leviticus 8:20"> The double-pronged hooks, each a handbreadth long, were fastened all around the inside of the room, and the flesh of the offering was to be placed on the tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:31–32; 1 Chronicles 16:41–43; Ezekiel 40:27"> Outside the inner gate, within the inner court, were two chambers, one beside the north gate and facing south, and another beside the south gate and facing north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:23; Psalm 134:1; Leviticus 8:35"> Then the man said to me: “The chamber that faces south is for the priests who keep charge of the temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:15; Ezekiel 43:19; 1 Kings 2:35"> and the chamber that faces north is for the priests who keep charge of the altar. These are the sons of Zadok, the only Levites who may approach the LORD to minister before Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:19; Ezekiel 40:23; Ezekiel 40:27"> Next he measured the court. It was square, a hundred cubits long and a hundred cubits wide. And the altar was in front of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:4; 1 Kings 6:3"> Then he brought me to the portico of the temple and measured the side pillars of the portico to be five cubits on each side. The width of the gateway was fourteen cubits and its sidewalls were three cubits on either side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Kings 7:15–21; Ezekiel 40:34; 2 Chronicles 3:17"> The portico was twenty cubits wide and twelve cubits deep, and ten steps led up to it. There were columns by the side pillars, one on each side.</span>
