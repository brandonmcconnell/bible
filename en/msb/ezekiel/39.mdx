<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:2–3; Ezekiel 35:3; Nahum 3:5"> “As for you, O son of man, prophesy against Gog and declare that this is what the Lord GOD says: Behold, I am against you, O Gog, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 40:14; Ezekiel 38:15; Isaiah 37:29"> I will turn you around, drive you along, bring you up from the far north, and send you against the mountains of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 76:3; Hosea 1:5; Jeremiah 21:4–5"> Then I will strike the bow from your left hand and dash down the arrows from your right hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:17–20; Ezekiel 33:27; Ezekiel 38:21"> On the mountains of Israel you will fall—you and all your troops and the nations with you. I will give you as food to every kind of ravenous bird and wild beast.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:19; Ezekiel 32:4; Jeremiah 8:2"> You will fall in the open field, for I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:16; Amos 1:4; Ezekiel 30:8"> I will send fire on Magog and on those who dwell securely in the coastlands, and they will know that I am the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 38:23; Exodus 20:7"> So I will make My holy name known among My people Israel and will no longer allow it to be profaned. Then the nations will know that I am the LORD, the Holy One in Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:17; Revelation 16:17; Ezekiel 7:2–10"> Yes, it is coming, and it will surely happen, declares the Lord GOD. This is the day of which I have spoken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 46:9; Isaiah 66:24; Malachi 1:5"> Then those who dwell in the cities of Israel will go out, kindle fires, and burn up the weapons—the bucklers and shields, the bows and arrows, the clubs and spears. For seven years they will use them for fuel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Isaiah 14:2; Micah 5:8"> They will not gather wood from the countryside or cut it from the forests, for they will use the weapons for fuel. They will loot those who looted them and plunder those who plundered them, declares the Lord GOD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:18; Numbers 34:11; Ezekiel 38:2"> And on that day I will give Gog a burial place in Israel, the Valley of the Travelers, east of the Sea. It will block those who travel through, because Gog and all his hordes will be buried there. So it will be called the Valley of Hamon-gog.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:23; Ezekiel 39:14; Ezekiel 39:16"> For seven months the house of Israel will be burying them in order to cleanse the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:22; Zephaniah 3:19–20; Jeremiah 33:9"> All the people of the land will bury them, and it will bring them renown on the day I display My glory, declares the Lord GOD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:12; Numbers 19:11–19"> And men will be employed to continually pass through the land to cleanse it by burying the invaders who remain on the ground. At the end of the seven months they will begin their search.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 11:44"> As they pass through the land, anyone who sees a human bone will set up a pillar next to it, until the gravediggers have buried it in the Valley of Hamon-gog.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> (Even the city will be named Hamonah.) And so they will cleanse the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:7; Revelation 19:17–18; Isaiah 34:6"> And as for you, son of man, this is what the Lord GOD says: Call out to every kind of bird and to every beast of the field: ‘Assemble and come together from all around to the sacrificial feast that I am preparing for you, a great feast on the mountains of Israel. There you will eat flesh and drink blood.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 22:12; Jeremiah 51:40; Revelation 19:17–18"> You will eat the flesh of the mighty and drink the blood of the princes of the earth as though they were rams, lambs, goats, and bulls—all the fattened animals of Bashan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> At the sacrifice I am preparing, you will eat fat until you are gorged and drink blood until you are drunk.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 19:18; Haggai 2:22; Psalm 76:5–6"> And at My table you will eat your fill of horses and riders, of mighty men and warriors of every kind,’ declares the Lord GOD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 38:23; Isaiah 37:20"> I will display My glory among the nations, and all the nations will see the judgment that I execute and the hand that I lay upon them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Ezekiel 28:26; Ezekiel 39:28"> From that day forward the house of Israel will know that I am the LORD their God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; Isaiah 1:15; Isaiah 59:2"> And the nations will know that the house of Israel went into exile for their iniquity, because they were unfaithful to Me. So I hid My face from them and delivered them into the hands of their enemies, so that they all fell by the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:19; Jeremiah 2:17; Jeremiah 4:18"> I dealt with them according to their uncleanness and transgressions, and I hid My face from them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:13; Isaiah 27:12–13; Hosea 1:11"> Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Now I will restore Jacob from captivity and will have compassion on the whole house of Israel, and I will be jealous for My holy name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Micah 4:4; 1 Kings 4:25; Ezekiel 16:63"> They will forget their disgrace and all the treachery they committed against Me, when they dwell securely in their land, with no one to frighten them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 38:23; Ezekiel 36:23–24"> When I bring them back from the peoples and gather them out of the lands of their enemies, I will show My holiness in them in the sight of many nations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:22; Amos 9:9; Isaiah 27:12"> Then they will know that I am the LORD their God, when I regather them to their own land, not leaving any of them behind after their exile among the nations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joel 2:28; 1 John 3:24; Isaiah 32:15"> And I will no longer hide My face from them, for I will pour out My Spirit on the house of Israel, declares the Lord GOD.”</span>
</div>
</div>
