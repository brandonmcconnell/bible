<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 10:1; Psalm 80:8–16; Isaiah 5:1–7"> “Son of man, how does the wood of the vine surpass any other branch among the trees in the forest?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 14:34–35; Jeremiah 24:8; Mark 9:50"> Can wood be taken from it to make something useful? Or can one make from it a peg on which to hang utensils?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:6; Isaiah 27:11; Psalm 80:16"> No, it is cast into the fire for fuel. The fire devours both ends, and the middle is charred. Can it be useful for anything?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:16"> Even when it was whole, it could not be made useful. How much less can it ever be useful when the fire has consumed it and charred it!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 15:2; Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 24:8–10"> Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Like the wood of the vine among the trees of the forest, which I have given to the fire for fuel, so I will give up the people of Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:8; Psalm 34:16; Isaiah 24:18"> And I will set My face against them. Though they may have escaped the fire, yet another fire will consume them. And when I set My face against them, you will know that I am the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:14; Zephaniah 1:18; Isaiah 6:11"> Thus I will make the land desolate, because they have acted unfaithfully,’ declares the Lord GOD.”</span>
</div>
