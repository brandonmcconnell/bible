</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:4; Ezekiel 1:26–27; Daniel 7:9–10"> Then I looked and saw a figure like that of a man. From His waist down His appearance was like fire, and from His waist up He was as bright as the gleam of amber.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16; Ezekiel 11:1; Ezekiel 5:11"> He stretched out what looked like a hand and took me by the hair of my head. Then the Spirit lifted me up between earth and heaven and carried me in visions of God to Jerusalem, to the entrance of the north gate of the inner court, where the idol that provokes jealousy was seated.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:22–23; Exodus 40:34–35; Ezekiel 11:22–23"> And there I saw the glory of the God of Israel, like the vision I had seen in the plain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:3; Jeremiah 3:2; Jeremiah 32:34"> “Son of man,” He said to me, “now lift up your eyes to the north.” So I lifted up my eyes to the north, and in the entrance north of the Altar Gate I saw this idol of jealousy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 10:19; Ezekiel 8:9"> “Son of man,” He said to me, “do you see what they are doing—the great abominations that the house of Israel is committing—to drive Me far from My sanctuary? Yet you will see even greater abominations.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 21:5; 1 Kings 7:12"> Then He brought me to the entrance to the court, and I looked and saw a hole in the wall.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 29:15; Job 34:22; Amos 9:2–3"> “Son of man,” He told me, “dig through the wall.” So I dug through the wall and discovered a doorway.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:8"> Then He said to me, “Go in and see the wicked abominations they are committing here.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 1:23; Exodus 20:4; Jeremiah 16:18"> So I went in and looked, and engraved all around the wall was every kind of crawling creature and detestable beast, along with all the idols of the house of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 16:17; Numbers 16:35; Numbers 11:16"> Before them stood seventy elders of the house of Israel, with Jaazaniah son of Shaphan standing among them. Each had a censer in his hand, and a fragrant cloud of incense was rising.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 29:15; Ezekiel 9:9; Ezekiel 8:15"> “Son of man,” He said to me, “do you see what the elders of the house of Israel are doing in the darkness, each at the shrine of his own idol? For they are saying, ‘The LORD does not see us; the LORD has forsaken the land.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:6; Jeremiah 9:3; Ezekiel 8:15"> Again, He told me, “You will see them committing even greater abominations.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:9; Ezekiel 44:4"> Then He brought me to the entrance of the north gate of the house of the LORD, and I saw women sitting there, weeping for Tammuz.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12–13; Ezekiel 8:9; Ezekiel 8:6"> “Son of man,” He said to me, “do you see this? Yet you will see even greater abominations than these.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:19; Deuteronomy 17:3; Jeremiah 32:33"> So He brought me to the inner court of the house of the LORD, and there at the entrance to the temple of the LORD, between the portico and the altar, were about twenty-five men with their backs to the temple of the LORD and their faces toward the east; and they were bowing to the east in worship of the sun.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:9; Ezekiel 7:23; Jeremiah 6:7"> “Son of man,” He said to me, “do you see this? Is it not enough for the house of Judah to commit the abominations they are practicing here, that they must also fill the land with violence and continually provoke Me to anger? Look, they are even putting the branch to their nose!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:4; Jeremiah 11:11; Isaiah 1:15"> Therefore I will respond with wrath. I will not look on them with pity, nor will I spare them. Although they shout loudly in My ears, I will not listen to them.”</span>
</div>
