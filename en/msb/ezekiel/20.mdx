</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Jeremiah 32:22; Ezekiel 20:15"> On that day I swore to bring them out of the land of Egypt into a land that I had searched out for them, a land flowing with milk and honey, the glory of all lands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 18:3; Ezekiel 20:8; Leviticus 11:44"> And I said to them: ‘Each of you must throw away the abominations before his eyes, and you must not defile yourselves with the idols of Egypt. I am the LORD your God.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:7; Isaiah 63:10; Ezekiel 20:21"> But they rebelled against Me and refused to listen. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:7; Ezekiel 20:22; Exodus 32:12"> But I acted for the sake of My name, that it should not be profaned in the eyes of the nations among whom they were living, in whose sight I had revealed Myself to Israel by bringing them out of the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 15:22; Exodus 13:17–18; Exodus 14:17–22"> So I brought them out of the land of Egypt and led them into the wilderness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Leviticus 18:5; Deuteronomy 4:8"> And I gave them My statutes and made known to them My ordinances—for the man who does these things will live by them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:20; Ezekiel 37:28; Leviticus 21:23"> I also gave them My Sabbaths as a sign between us, so that they would know that I am the LORD who sanctifies them.</span>
</div>

Then I resolved to pour out My wrath upon them and put an end to them in the wilderness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:9; Ezekiel 36:22–23; Ezekiel 20:22"> But I acted for the sake of My name, so that it would not be profaned in the eyes of the nations in whose sight I had brought them out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 95:11; Psalm 106:26; Hebrews 3:18"> Moreover, with an uplifted hand I swore to them in the wilderness that I would not bring them into the land that I had given them—a land flowing with milk and honey, the glory of all lands—</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 15:39; Ezekiel 20:8; Ezekiel 14:3–4"> because they kept rejecting My ordinances, refusing to walk in My statutes, and profaning My Sabbaths; for their hearts continually went after their idols.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:13; Jeremiah 4:27; Jeremiah 5:18"> Yet I looked on them with pity and did not destroy them or bring them to an end in the wilderness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 1:2–4; Psalm 78:6–8; 1 Peter 1:18"> In the wilderness I said to their children: ‘Do not walk in the statutes of your fathers or keep their ordinances or defile yourselves with their idols.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Deuteronomy 5:32–8; Ezekiel 37:24"> I am the LORD your God; walk in My statutes, keep My ordinances, and practice them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 58:13; Jeremiah 17:22; Exodus 31:13–17"> Keep My Sabbaths holy, that they may be a sign between us, so that you may know that I am the LORD your God.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

So I resolved to pour out My wrath upon them and vent My anger against them in the wilderness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:14; Ezekiel 20:9; Jeremiah 14:21"> But I withheld My hand and acted for the sake of My name, so that it would not be profaned in the eyes of the nations in whose sight I had brought them out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:64–68; Deuteronomy 32:26–27"> However, with an uplifted hand I swore to them in the wilderness that I would scatter them among the nations and disperse them throughout the lands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:16; Ezekiel 6:9; Amos 2:4"> For they did not practice My ordinances, but they rejected My statutes and profaned My Sabbaths, fixing their eyes on the idols of their fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 81:12; Isaiah 66:4; Ezekiel 20:39"> I also gave them over to statutes that were not good and ordinances by which they could not live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Leviticus 18:21; Ezekiel 20:31"> And I pronounced them unclean through their gifts—the sacrifice of every firstborn in the fire—so that I might devastate them, in order that they would know that I am the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Ezekiel 2:7; Ezekiel 3:11"> Therefore, son of man, speak to the house of Israel, and tell them that this is what the Lord GOD says: In this way also your fathers blasphemed Me by their unfaithfulness against Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:13; Nehemiah 9:22–26; Jeremiah 3:6"> When I brought them into the land that I swore to give them and they saw any high hill or leafy tree, there they offered their sacrifices, presented offerings that provoked Me, sent up their fragrant incense, and poured out their drink offerings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:24–25; Ezekiel 16:31"> So I asked them: ‘What is this high place to which you go?’ (And to this day it is called Bamah.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; Jeremiah 7:26; Judges 2:19"> Therefore tell the house of Israel that this is what the Lord GOD says: Will you defile yourselves the way your fathers did, prostituting yourselves with their abominations?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:26; Psalm 106:37–39; Jeremiah 7:31"> When you offer your gifts, sacrificing your sons in the fire, you continue to defile yourselves with all your idols to this day. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:5; Jeremiah 44:17; Psalm 139:2"> When you say, ‘Let us be like the nations, like the peoples of the lands, serving wood and stone,’ what you have in mind will never come to pass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:5; Ezekiel 8:18; Jeremiah 44:6"> As surely as I live, declares the Lord GOD, with a strong hand, an outstretched arm, and outpoured wrath I will rule over you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:38; Amos 9:9–10; Ezekiel 34:16"> With a strong hand, an outstretched arm, and outpoured wrath I will bring you out from the peoples and gather you from the lands to which you have been scattered.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hosea 2:14; Jeremiah 2:35; Ezekiel 17:20"> And I will bring you into the wilderness of the nations, where I will enter into judgment with you face to face.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5–10; Numbers 11:1–35; Ezekiel 20:13"> Just as I entered into judgment with your fathers in the wilderness of the land of Egypt, so I will enter into judgment with you, declares the Lord GOD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:13; Leviticus 27:32; Matthew 25:32–33"> I will make you pass under the rod and will bring you into the bond of the covenant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:9; Amos 9:9–10; Psalm 95:11"> And I will purge you of those who rebel and transgress against Me. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Hosea 4:17; Isaiah 1:13–15; Ezekiel 43:7"> And as for you, O house of Israel, this is what the Lord GOD says: Go and serve your idols, every one of you. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Malachi 3:4; Isaiah 60:7"> For on My holy mountain, the high mountain of Israel, declares the Lord GOD, there the whole house of Israel, all of them, will serve Me in the land. There I will accept them and will require your offerings and choice gifts, along with all your holy sacrifices.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:25; Ezekiel 11:17; Ezekiel 38:23"> When I bring you from the peoples and gather you from the lands to which you have been scattered, I will accept you as a pleasing aroma. And I will show My holiness through you in the sight of the nations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:13; Ezekiel 38:23; Ezekiel 36:23–24"> Then you will know that I am the LORD, when I bring you into the land of Israel, the land that I swore to give your fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Hosea 5:15; Jeremiah 31:18"> There you will remember your ways and all the deeds with which you have defiled yourselves, and you will loathe yourselves for all the evils you have done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:24; Psalm 115:1; Ezekiel 20:9"> Then you will know, O house of Israel, that I am the LORD, when I have dealt with you for the sake of My name and not according to your wicked ways and corrupt acts, declares the Lord GOD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> Now the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Amos 7:16; Ezekiel 21:2; Jeremiah 13:19"> “Son of man, set your face toward the south, preach against it, and prophesy against the forest of the Negev.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:14; Isaiah 24:1–6; Ezekiel 17:24"> Say to the forest of the Negev: Hear the word of the LORD! The blazing flame will not be quenched, and by it every face from south to north will be scorched.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:2–3; Jeremiah 7:20; Deuteronomy 29:24–28"> Then all people will see that I, the LORD, have kindled it; it will not be quenched.”</span>

<div className="bp">
