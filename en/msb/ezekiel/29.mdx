<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:17; Ezekiel 26:1; Ezekiel 20:1"> In the tenth year, on the twelfth day of the tenth month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Jeremiah 44:30; Jeremiah 46:2–16"> “Son of man, set your face against Pharaoh king of Egypt and prophesy against him and against all Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:2; Isaiah 27:1; Jeremiah 44:30"> Speak to him and tell him that this is what the Lord GOD says: Behold, I am against you, O Pharaoh king of Egypt, O great monster who lies among his rivers, who says, ‘The Nile is mine; I made it myself.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; Ezekiel 38:4; Amos 4:2"> But I will put hooks in your jaws and cause the fish of your streams to cling to your scales. I will haul you up out of your rivers, and all the fish of your streams will cling to your scales.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:20; Ezekiel 32:4–6; Jeremiah 8:2"> I will leave you in the desert, you and all the fish of your streams. You will fall on the open field and will not be taken away or gathered for burial. I have given you as food to the beasts of the earth and the birds of the air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 18:21; Isaiah 36:6; Exodus 9:14"> Then all the people of Egypt will know that I am the LORD. For you were only a staff of reeds to the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Ezekiel 17:15–17; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> When Israel took hold of you with their hands, you splintered, tearing all their shoulders; when they leaned on you, you broke, and their backs were wrenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:17; Ezekiel 30:10; Ezekiel 29:19–20"> Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I will bring a sword against you and cut off from you man and beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Proverbs 16:18; Ezekiel 29:10–12"> The land of Egypt will become a desolate wasteland. Then they will know that I am the LORD. Because you said, ‘The Nile is mine; I made it,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:12; Ezekiel 30:6–9; Exodus 14:2"> therefore I am against you and against your rivers. I will turn the land of Egypt into a ruin, a desolate wasteland from Migdol to Syene, and as far as the border of Cush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:13; Jeremiah 43:11–12; Ezekiel 30:10–13"> No foot of man or beast will pass through, and it will be uninhabited for forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:19; Ezekiel 30:7; Ezekiel 30:23"> I will make the land of Egypt a desolation among desolate lands, and her cities will lie desolate for forty years among the ruined cities. And I will disperse the Egyptians among the nations and scatter them throughout the countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:26; Isaiah 19:22"> For this is what the Lord GOD says: At the end of forty years I will gather the Egyptians from the nations to which they were scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:14; Isaiah 11:11; Jeremiah 44:1"> I will restore Egypt from captivity and bring them back to the land of Pathros, the land of their origin. There they will be a lowly kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 10:11; Ezekiel 30:13; Ezekiel 17:14"> Egypt will be the lowliest of kingdoms and will never again exalt itself above the nations. For I will diminish Egypt so that it will never again rule over the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Isaiah 20:5; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> Egypt will never again be an object of trust for the house of Israel, but will remind them of their iniquity in turning to the Egyptians. Then they will know that I am the Lord GOD.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:1; Ezekiel 1:2; Ezekiel 24:1"> In the twenty-seventh year, on the first day of the first month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:6; Jeremiah 25:9; Ezekiel 26:7–12"> “Son of man, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon caused his army to labor strenuously against Tyre. Every head was made bald and every shoulder made raw. But he and his army received no wages from Tyre for the labor they expended on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:24–25; Jeremiah 43:10–13; Ezekiel 32:11"> Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I will give the land of Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, who will carry off its wealth, seize its spoil, and remove its plunder. This will be the wages for his army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Isaiah 10:6–7; Isaiah 45:1–3"> I have given him the land of Egypt as the reward for his labor, because it was done for Me, declares the Lord GOD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:10; Luke 21:15; Psalm 132:17"> In that day I will cause a horn to sprout for the house of Israel, and I will open your mouth to speak among them. Then they will know that I am the LORD.”</span>
