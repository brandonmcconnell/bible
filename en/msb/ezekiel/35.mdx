<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:1; Ezekiel 21:1; Ezekiel 34:1"> Moreover, the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Genesis 32:3; Ezekiel 25:8"> “Son of man, set your face against Mount Seir and prophesy against it,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:12; Ezekiel 35:7; Ezekiel 6:14"> and declare that this is what the Lord GOD says: Behold, I am against you, O Mount Seir. I will stretch out My hand against you and make you a desolate waste.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:9; Malachi 1:3–4; Joel 3:19"> I will turn your cities into ruins, and you will become a desolation. Then you will know that I am the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 137:7; Obadiah 1:10–16; Amos 1:11"> Because you harbored an ancient hatred and delivered the Israelites over to the sword in the time of their disaster at the final stage of their punishment,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:2–6; Revelation 18:24; Obadiah 1:15"> therefore as surely as I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will give you over to bloodshed and it will pursue you. Since you did not hate bloodshed, it will pursue you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:11; Ezekiel 35:3; Judges 5:6–7"> I will make Mount Seir a desolate waste and will cut off from it those who come and go.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:12; Ezekiel 32:4–5; Ezekiel 39:4–5"> I will fill its mountains with the slain; those killed by the sword will fall on your hills, in your valleys, and in all your ravines.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Malachi 1:3–4; Ezekiel 6:7"> I will make you a perpetual desolation, and your cities will not be inhabited. Then you will know that I am the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:5; Psalm 83:4–12; Ezekiel 48:35"> Because you have said, ‘These two nations and countries will be ours, and we will possess them,’ even though the LORD was there,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 9:16; Matthew 7:2; Psalm 73:17–18"> therefore as surely as I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will treat you according to the anger and jealousy you showed in your hatred against them, and I will make Myself known among them when I judge you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:2; Psalm 83:12; Ezekiel 6:7"> Then you will know that I, the LORD, have heard every contemptuous word you uttered against the mountains of Israel when you said, ‘They are desolate; they are given to us to devour!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 11:36; Isaiah 36:20; Jeremiah 29:23"> You boasted against Me with your mouth and multiplied your words against Me. I heard it Myself!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:48; Isaiah 14:7–8; Isaiah 65:13–15"> This is what the Lord GOD says: While the whole earth rejoices, I will make you desolate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:3–4; Lamentations 4:21; Isaiah 34:5–6"> As you rejoiced when the inheritance of the house of Israel became desolate, so will I do to you. You will become a desolation, O Mount Seir, and so will all of Edom. Then they will know that I am the LORD.</span>
</div>
</div>
