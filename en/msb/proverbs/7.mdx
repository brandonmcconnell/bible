<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:18–20; Proverbs 3:3; Deuteronomy 6:8–9"> Tie them to your fingers; write them on the tablet of your heart.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 2:16; Proverbs 6:24; Proverbs 5:3"> that they may keep you from the adulteress, from the stranger with seductive words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 26:8; Judges 5:28; 2 Samuel 6:16"> For at the window of my house I looked through the lattice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 6:32; Proverbs 1:22; Proverbs 1:32"> I saw among the simple, I noticed among the youths, a young man lacking judgment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 5:8; Proverbs 7:12; Judges 16:1"> crossing the street near her corner, strolling down the road to her house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 24:13–15; Romans 13:12–14; Ephesians 5:11"> at twilight, as the day was fading into the dark of the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9; Isaiah 3:16–24; 2 Corinthians 11:2–3"> Then a woman came out to meet him, with the attire of a harlot and cunning of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 9:13; Titus 2:5; Proverbs 31:10–31"> She is loud and defiant; her feet do not remain at home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 23:28; Proverbs 9:14; Revelation 18:3"> Now in the street, now in the squares, she lurks at every corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 39:12; Numbers 25:6–8; Genesis 39:7"> She seizes him and kisses him; she brazenly says to him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 21:27; Leviticus 7:11; Proverbs 17:1"> “I have made my peace offerings; today I have paid my vows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> So I came out to meet you; I sought you, and I have found you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 19:9; Ezekiel 27:7; 1 Kings 10:28"> I have decked my bed with coverings, with colored linen from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 45:8; Isaiah 57:7–9; Song of Solomon 4:13–14"> I have perfumed my bed with myrrh, with aloes, and with cinnamon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Come, let us take our fill of love till morning. Let us delight in loving caresses!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 20:11; Luke 12:45–46; Luke 12:39"> For my husband is not at home; he has gone on a long journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4"> He took with him a bag of money and will not return till the moon is full.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 5:3; Proverbs 7:5; 2 Kings 4:8"> With her great persuasion she entices him; with her flattering lips she lures him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 13:27; Acts 16:24; Acts 14:13"> He follows her on impulse, like an ox going to the slaughter, like a deer bounding into a trap,</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 5:7; Proverbs 4:1; 1 John 2:1"> Now, my sons, listen to me, and attend to the words of my mouth.</span>
