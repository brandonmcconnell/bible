</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 31:20; Ezekiel 16:49–50"> Otherwise, I may have too much and deny You, saying, ‘Who is the LORD?’ Or I may become poor and steal, profaning the name of my God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 18:11; Proverbs 16:2; 2 Timothy 3:5"> There is a generation of those who are pure in their own eyes and yet unwashed of their filth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 57:4; Psalm 3:7; Psalm 14:4"> there is a generation whose teeth are swords and whose jaws are knives, devouring the oppressed from the earth and the needy from among men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 30:21; Proverbs 30:29; Proverbs 6:16"> The leech has two daughters: Give and Give. There are three things that are never satisfied, four that never say, ‘Enough!’:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:20; Habakkuk 2:5; Genesis 30:1"> Sheol, the barren womb, land never satisfied with water, and fire that never says, ‘Enough!’</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 42:3; Psalm 139:6"> There are three things too wonderful for me, four that I cannot understand:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Job 39:27; Exodus 22:16"> the way of an eagle in the sky, the way of a snake on a rock, the way of a ship at sea, and the way of a man with a maiden.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 5:6; Proverbs 7:13–23; Numbers 5:11–30"> This is the way of an adulteress: She eats and wipes her mouth and says, ‘I have done nothing wrong.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Under three things the earth trembles, under four it cannot bear up:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Ecclesiastes 10:7; 1 Samuel 25:10–11"> a servant who becomes king, a fool who is filled with food,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 27:15; Proverbs 29:21"> an unloved woman who marries, and a maidservant who supplants her mistress.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 12:7"> Four things on earth are small, yet they are exceedingly wise:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–8"> The ants are creatures of little strength, yet they store up their food in the summer;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 104:18; Leviticus 11:5"> the rock badgers are creatures of little power, yet they make their homes in the rocks;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Joel 1:6–7; Joel 2:7–11"> the locusts have no king, yet they all advance in formation;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and the lizard can be caught in one’s hands, yet it is found in the palaces of kings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> There are three things that are stately in their stride, and four that are impressive in their walk:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 23:24; Judges 14:18"> a lion, mighty among beasts, refusing to retreat before anything;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 3:15–18; Proverbs 20:2; Proverbs 16:14"> a strutting rooster; a he-goat; and a king with his army around him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 26:12"> If you have foolishly exalted yourself or if you have plotted evil, put your hand over your mouth.</span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 29:22; Proverbs 28:25"> For as the churning of milk yields butter, and the twisting of the nose draws blood, so the stirring of anger brings forth strife.”</span>
