<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 10:13; Psalm 32:9; 1 Corinthians 4:21"> A whip for the horse, a bridle for the donkey, and a rod for the backs of fools!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 26:5; 1 Peter 3:9; Jude 1:9"> Do not answer a fool according to his folly, or you yourself will be like him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 12:16; Matthew 16:1–4; Proverbs 28:11"> Answer a fool according to his folly, lest he become wise in his own eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 10:26; Proverbs 25:13; Proverbs 13:17"> Like cutting off one’s own feet or drinking violence is the sending of a message by the hand of a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 26:9; Psalm 64:8; Proverbs 17:7"> Like lame legs hanging limp is a proverb in the mouth of a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:1; Proverbs 30:22; Proverbs 19:10"> Like binding a stone into a sling is the giving of honor to a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 23:35"> Like a thorn that goes into the hand of a drunkard is a proverb in the mouth of a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 2:6; Proverbs 11:31"> Like an archer who wounds at random is he who hires a fool or passerby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:22; Matthew 12:45; Exodus 8:15"> As a dog returns to its vomit, so a fool repeats his folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:20; 1 Corinthians 3:18–19; Proverbs 26:5"> Do you see a man who is wise in his own eyes? There is more hope for a fool than for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 22:13; Proverbs 19:15; Proverbs 15:19"> The slacker says, “A lion is in the road! A fierce lion roams the public square!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; Proverbs 12:27; Proverbs 12:24"> As a door turns on its hinges, so the slacker turns on his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 19:24"> The slacker buries his hand in the dish; it wearies him to bring it back to his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 26:12; 1 Peter 3:15; Proverbs 12:15"> The slacker is wiser in his own eyes than seven men who answer discreetly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:3; 2 Timothy 2:23–24; Luke 12:14"> Like one who grabs a dog by the ears is a passerby who meddles in a quarrel not his own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 25:18; Isaiah 50:11; Proverbs 7:23"> Like a madman shooting firebrands and deadly arrows,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 5:4; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 15:21"> so is the man who deceives his neighbor and says, “I was only joking!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 22:10; James 3:6; Proverbs 16:28"> Without wood, a fire goes out; without gossip, a conflict ceases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Proverbs 29:22; 2 Samuel 20:1"> Like charcoal for embers and wood for fire, so is a quarrelsome man for kindling strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 18:8; Proverbs 20:19; Ezekiel 22:9"> The words of a gossip are like choice morsels that go down into the inmost being.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:47–48; Luke 11:39; Proverbs 10:18"> Like glaze covering an earthen vessel are burning lips and a wicked heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:20; Proverbs 10:18; Psalm 41:6"> A hateful man disguises himself with his speech, but he lays up deceit in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Jeremiah 12:6; Psalm 28:3"> When he speaks graciously, do not believe him, for seven abominations fill his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 55:21–23; 1 Samuel 18:17; 1 Samuel 18:21"> Though his hatred is concealed by deception, his wickedness will be exposed in the assembly.</span>
