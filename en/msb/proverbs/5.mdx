<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 22:17; Proverbs 4:20; Proverbs 2:1"> My son, pay attention to my wisdom; incline your ear to my insight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 16:23; Proverbs 15:7; Malachi 2:6–7"> that you may maintain discretion and your lips may preserve knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:16; Proverbs 6:24; Proverbs 7:5"> Though the lips of the forbidden woman drip honey and her speech is smoother than oil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Psalm 57:4; Psalm 55:21"> in the end she is bitter as wormwood, sharp as a double-edged sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 7:27; Proverbs 2:18–19"> Her feet go down to death; her steps lead straight to Sheol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 4:26; Proverbs 5:21; Psalm 16:11"> She does not consider the path of life; she does not know that her ways are unstable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1; Proverbs 7:24; Proverbs 22:17–21"> So now, my sons, listen to me, and do not turn aside from the words of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 7:25; Ephesians 5:11; Matthew 6:13"> Keep your path far from her; do not go near the door of her house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:26; Hosea 4:13–14; Genesis 38:23–26"> lest you concede your vigor to others, and your years to one who is cruel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 7:9; Proverbs 6:35; Proverbs 31:3"> lest strangers feast on your wealth, and your labors enrich the house of a foreigner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:21; Revelation 22:15; Hebrews 13:4"> At the end of your life you will groan when your flesh and your body are spent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 1:7; Proverbs 15:5"> and you will say, “How I hated discipline, and my heart despised reproof!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:8; Luke 15:18; Hebrews 13:7"> I did not listen to the voice of my teachers or incline my ear to my mentors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; 2 Peter 2:10–18; 1 Corinthians 10:6–8"> I am on the brink of utter ruin in the midst of the whole assembly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:2–5; Hebrews 13:4; Proverbs 5:18–19"> Drink water from your own cistern, and running water from your own well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 128:3; Psalm 127:3; Isaiah 48:21"> Why should your springs flow in the streets, your streams of water in the public squares?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Let them be yours alone, never to be shared with strangers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:9; Malachi 2:14–15; Song of Solomon 4:15"> May your fountain be blessed, and may you rejoice in the wife of your youth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Song of Solomon 7:3; Song of Solomon 8:14; Song of Solomon 4:5"> A loving doe, a graceful fawn—may her breasts satisfy you always; may you be captivated by her love forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 7:5; Proverbs 6:24; Proverbs 23:33"> Why be captivated, my son, by an adulteress, or embrace the bosom of a stranger?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Proverbs 15:3; Job 34:21"> For a man’s ways are before the eyes of the LORD, and the LORD examines all his paths.</span>
