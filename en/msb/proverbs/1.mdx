<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:1–9; Job 22:22; Proverbs 8:10–11"> and for receiving instruction in wise living and in righteousness, justice, and equity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:5; Proverbs 8:17; Psalm 119:9"> To impart prudence to the simple and knowledge and discretion to the young,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 9:9; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> let the wise listen and gain instruction, and the discerning acquire wise counsel</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 78:2; Mark 4:11; Psalm 49:4"> by understanding the proverbs and parables, the sayings and riddles of the wise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 28:28; Psalm 111:10–1; Ecclesiastes 12:13"> The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge, but fools despise wisdom and discipline.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:20; Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 5:1–2"> Listen, my son, to your father’s instruction, and do not forsake the teaching of your mother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Proverbs 4:9; Genesis 41:42"> For they are a garland of grace on your head and a pendant around your neck.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Proverbs 16:29; Psalm 1:1"> My son, if sinners entice you, do not yield to them.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26; Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 12:6"> If they say, “Come along, let us lie in wait for blood, let us ambush the innocent without cause,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 124:3; Psalm 28:1; Romans 3:13"> let us swallow them alive like Sheol, and whole like those descending into the Pit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 12:15; Job 24:2–3"> We will find all manner of precious goods; we will fill our houses with plunder.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Throw in your lot with us; let us all share one purse”—</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 119:101; Psalm 1:1; Proverbs 4:14–15"> my son, do not walk the road with them or set foot upon their path.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 6:18; Proverbs 4:16"> For their feet run to evil, and they are swift to shed blood.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Proverbs 7:23; Jeremiah 8:7"> How futile it is to spread the net where any bird can see it!</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 9:16; Esther 7:10; Proverbs 5:22–23"> But they lie in wait for their own blood; they ambush their own lives.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 15:27; 2 Peter 2:3; James 5:1–4"> Such is the fate of all who are greedy, whose unjust gain takes the lives of its possessors.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 9:3; Colossians 2:3; Luke 11:49"> Wisdom calls out in the street, she lifts her voice in the square;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 5:20; Proverbs 9:3; Matthew 13:2"> in the main concourse she cries aloud, at the city gates she makes her speech:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 1:29; Psalm 1:1; Proverbs 5:12"> “How long, O simple ones, will you love your simple ways? How long will scoffers delight in their scorn and fools hate knowledge?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Ezekiel 33:11; Ezekiel 18:27–30"> If you had repented at my rebuke, then surely I would have poured out my spirit on you; I would have made my words known to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; Romans 10:21; Jeremiah 7:13"> Because you refused my call, and no one took my outstretched hand,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 81:11; Luke 7:30; Proverbs 1:30"> because you neglected all my counsel, and wanted none of my correction,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 2:4; Proverbs 10:24; Psalm 37:13"> in turn I will mock your calamity; I will sneer when terror strikes you,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 3:25–26; Luke 21:34–35; Psalm 58:9"> when your dread comes like a storm, and your destruction like a whirlwind, when distress and anguish overwhelm you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Micah 3:4; James 4:3; Jeremiah 11:11"> Then they will call on me, but I will not answer; they will earnestly seek me, but will not find me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Job 21:14–15; Proverbs 6:23"> For they hated knowledge and chose not to fear the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalm 81:11; Proverbs 1:25; Luke 14:18–20"> They accepted none of my counsel; they despised all my reproof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 14:14; Jeremiah 6:19; Proverbs 22:8"> So they will eat the fruit of their own way, and be filled with their own devices.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 8:36; Jeremiah 2:19; John 3:36"> For the waywardness of the simple will slay them, and the complacency of fools will destroy them.</span>
