<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 6:20; Proverbs 1:8; 2 Timothy 1:5"> These are the words of King Lemuel—the burden that his mother taught him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; 1 Samuel 1:11; 1 Samuel 1:28"> What shall I say, O my son? What, O son of my womb? What, O son of my vows?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:17; Nehemiah 13:26; Proverbs 7:26–27"> Do not spend your strength on women or your vigor on those who ruin kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Ecclesiastes 10:17; Isaiah 5:22"> It is not for kings, O Lemuel, it is not for kings to drink wine, or for rulers to crave strong drink,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:5"> lest they drink and forget what is decreed, depriving all the oppressed of justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:23; Psalm 104:15; 1 Samuel 1:10"> Give strong drink to one who is perishing, and wine to the bitter in soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18"> Let him drink and forget his poverty, and remember his misery no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 82:3–4; Proverbs 24:11–12; Esther 4:13–16"> Open your mouth for those with no voice, for the cause of all the dispossessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Leviticus 19:15; Amos 5:11–12"> Open your mouth, judge righteously, and defend the cause of the poor and needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:14; Proverbs 12:4; Proverbs 18:22"> A wife of noble character, who can find? She is far more precious than rubies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 3:1–7; 2 Kings 4:22–23; 2 Kings 4:9–10"> The heart of her husband trusts in her, and he lacks nothing of value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18–22; 1 Samuel 25:26–27"> She brings him good and not harm all the days of her life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Timothy 5:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:10–12"> She selects wool and flax and works with eager hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 31:24; 1 Kings 9:26–28; Ezekiel 27:3–36"> She is like the merchant ships, bringing her food from afar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:11; Mark 1:35; Luke 12:42"> She rises while it is still night to provide food for her household and portions for her maidservants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Song of Solomon 8:12; Joshua 15:18"> She appraises a field and buys it; from her earnings she plants a vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 38:3; 1 Peter 1:13; Ephesians 6:10"> She girds herself with strength and shows that her arms are strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; Matthew 25:3–10; Psalm 127:2"> She sees that her gain is good, and her lamp is not extinguished at night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:25–26"> She stretches out her hands to the distaff and grasps the spindle with her fingers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 4:28; Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 22:9"> She opens her arms to the poor and reaches out her hands to the needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:22; 2 Samuel 1:24; Proverbs 25:20"> When it snows, she has no fear for her household, for they are all clothed in scarlet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Proverbs 7:16; Genesis 41:42"> She makes coverings for her bed; her clothing is fine linen and purple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 12:4; Ruth 4:1; Deuteronomy 16:18"> Her husband is known at the city gates, where he sits among the elders of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:16; Luke 16:19; Proverbs 31:19"> She makes linen garments and sells them; she delivers sashes to the merchants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 40:10; Romans 13:14; Job 29:14"> Strength and honor are her clothing, and she can laugh at the days to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 10:31"> She opens her mouth with wisdom, and faithful instruction is on her tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; Titus 2:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> She watches over the affairs of her household and does not eat the bread of idleness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:15–17; 2 Timothy 1:5; 1 Kings 2:19"> Her children rise up and call her blessed; her husband praises her as well:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Song of Solomon 6:8–9; Ephesians 5:27"> “Many daughters have done noble things, but you surpass them all!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 1:7; 1 Peter 3:4–5; Proverbs 11:22"> Charm is deceptive and beauty is fleeting, but a woman who fears the LORD is to be praised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalm 128:2; Hebrews 6:10; Matthew 7:16"> Give her the fruit of her hands, and let her works praise her at the gates.</span>
