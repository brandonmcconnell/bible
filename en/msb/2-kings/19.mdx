<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:5–7; Isaiah 66:9; Hosea 13:13"> to tell him, “This is what Hezekiah says: Today is a day of distress, rebuke, and disgrace; for children have come to the point of birth, but there is no strength to deliver them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Romans 9:27; Joshua 14:12"> Perhaps the LORD your God will hear all the words of the Rabshakeh, whom his master the king of Assyria has sent to defy the living God, and He will rebuke him for the words that the LORD your God has heard. Therefore lift up a prayer for the remnant that still survives.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So the servants of King Hezekiah went to Isaiah,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 18:35; 2 Kings 18:17; Isaiah 37:6–38"> who replied, “Tell your master that this is what the LORD says: ‘Do not be afraid of the words you have heard, with which the servants of the king of Assyria have blasphemed Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Obadiah 1:1; 2 Kings 7:6; Psalm 18:14–15"> Behold, I will put a spirit in him so that he will hear a rumor and return to his own land, where I will cause him to fall by the sword.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 18:14; Joshua 10:29; Joshua 12:11"> When the Rabshakeh heard that the king of Assyria had left Lachish, he withdrew and found the king fighting against Libnah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:27; Isaiah 37:9; 2 Kings 18:17"> Now Sennacherib had been warned about Tirhakah king of Cush: “Look, he has set out to fight against you.” So Sennacherib again sent messengers to Hezekiah, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 18:5; 2 Kings 18:29–30; 2 Chronicles 32:15–19"> “Give this message to Hezekiah king of Judah: ‘Do not let your God, in whom you trust, deceive you by saying that Jerusalem will not be delivered into the hand of the king of Assyria.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 10:8–11; 2 Chronicles 32:13–14; 2 Kings 19:17–18"> Surely you have heard what the kings of Assyria have done to all the other countries, devoting them to destruction. Will you then be spared?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 17:6; Genesis 11:31; Ezekiel 27:23"> Did the gods of the nations destroyed by my fathers rescue those nations—the gods of Gozan, Haran, and Rezeph, and of the people of Eden in Telassar?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 18:34; 2 Kings 17:24; Isaiah 11:11"> Where are the kings of Hamath, Arpad, Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah?’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 37:14; Psalm 91:1–2; Psalm 123:1–4"> So Hezekiah received the letter from the messengers, read it, and went up to the house of the LORD and spread it out before the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; 2 Kings 5:15; Daniel 4:34–35"> And Hezekiah prayed before the LORD: “O LORD, God of Israel, enthroned between the cherubim, You alone are God over all the kingdoms of the earth. You made the heavens and the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:40; Psalm 31:2; 2 Kings 19:4"> Incline Your ear, O LORD, and hear; open Your eyes, O LORD, and see. Listen to the words that Sennacherib has sent to defy the living God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; Job 9:2; 1 Corinthians 14:25"> Truly, O LORD, the kings of Assyria have laid waste these nations and their lands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 17:29; Isaiah 44:9–20; Jeremiah 10:3–9"> They have cast their gods into the fire and destroyed them, for they were not gods, but only wood and stone—the work of human hands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 83:18; 1 Kings 8:43; Psalm 67:1–2"> And now, O LORD our God, please save us from his hand, so that all the kingdoms of the earth may know that You alone, O LORD, are God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 20:5; 2 Chronicles 32:20–21; Jeremiah 33:3"> Then Isaiah son of Amoz sent a message to Hezekiah: “This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: I have heard your prayer concerning Sennacherib king of Assyria.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Lamentations 2:13; Job 16:4; Jeremiah 14:17"> This is the word that the LORD has spoken against him: ‘The Virgin Daughter of Zion despises you and mocks you; the Daughter of Jerusalem shakes her head behind you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Psalm 71:22; Isaiah 30:11–12"> Whom have you taunted and blasphemed? Against whom have you raised your voice and lifted your eyes in pride? Against the Holy One of Israel!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Psalm 20:7; Isaiah 10:18"> Through your servants you have taunted the Lord, and you have said: “With my many chariots I have ascended to the heights of the mountains, to the remote peaks of Lebanon. I have cut down its tallest cedars, the finest of its cypresses. I have reached its farthest outposts, the densest of its forests.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 19:6; Exodus 15:9; 2 Samuel 17:13"> I have dug wells and drunk foreign waters. With the soles of my feet I have dried up all the streams of Egypt.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Isaiah 10:5–6; Isaiah 37:26–27"> Have you not heard? Long ago I ordained it; in days of old I planned it. Now I have brought it to pass, that you should crush fortified cities into piles of rubble.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Psalm 102:11; Jeremiah 51:32"> Therefore their inhabitants, devoid of power, are dismayed and ashamed. They are like plants in the field, tender green shoots, grass on the rooftops, scorched before it is grown.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 37:28–29; Jeremiah 23:23–24; Psalm 139:1–11"> But I know your sitting down, your going out and coming in, and your raging against Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:4; Ezekiel 38:4; 2 Kings 19:33"> Because your rage and arrogance against Me have reached My ears, I will put My hook in your nose and My bit in your mouth; I will send you back the way you came.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 25:20–22; 2 Kings 20:8–9; Exodus 3:12"> And this will be a sign to you, O Hezekiah: This year you will eat what grows on its own, and in the second year what springs from the same. But in the third year you will sow and reap; you will plant vineyards and eat their fruit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 19:4; Isaiah 27:6; 2 Chronicles 32:22–23"> And the surviving remnant of the house of Judah will again take root below and bear fruit above.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 9:7; Romans 11:5; Romans 9:27"> For a remnant will go forth from Jerusalem, and survivors from Mount Zion. The zeal of the LORD of Hosts will accomplish this.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–10; 2 Samuel 20:15; Isaiah 37:33–35"> So this is what the LORD says about the king of Assyria: ‘He will not enter this city or shoot an arrow into it. He will not come before it with a shield or build up a siege ramp against it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; 2 Kings 19:36"> He will go back the way he came, and he will not enter this city, declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 11:12–13; 2 Kings 20:6; 1 Kings 15:4"> I will defend this city and save it for My own sake and for the sake of My servant David.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:21–22; 2 Samuel 24:16; Exodus 12:29–30"> And that very night the angel of the LORD went out and struck down 185,000 men in the camp of the Assyrians. When the people got up the next morning, there were all the dead bodies!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jonah 1:2; 2 Kings 19:7; 2 Kings 19:33"> So Sennacherib king of Assyria broke camp and withdrew. He returned to Nineveh and stayed there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Ezra 4:2; Genesis 8:4; 2 Kings 19:7"> One day, while he was worshiping in the temple of his god Nisroch, his sons Adrammelech and Sharezer put him to the sword and escaped to the land of Ararat. And his son Esar-haddon reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
