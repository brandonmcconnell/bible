</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; Judges 3:3; 1 Samuel 27:1–3"> So the woman had proceeded to do as the man of God had instructed. And she and her household lived as foreigners for seven years in the land of the Philistines.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:16; Luke 18:3–5; 2 Kings 4:13"> At the end of seven years, when the woman returned from the land of the Philistines, she went to the king to appeal for her house and her land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 5:20–27; 2 Kings 2:14; 2 Kings 6:32"> Now the king had been speaking to Gehazi, the servant of the man of God, saying, “Please relate to me all the great things Elisha has done.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 4:35; Matthew 10:29–30; 2 Kings 6:26"> And Gehazi was telling the king how Elisha had brought the dead back to life. Just then the woman whose son Elisha had revived came to appeal to the king for her house and her land. So Gehazi said, “My lord the king, this is the woman, and this is the son Elisha restored to life.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Proverbs 21:1; 2 Samuel 9:7"> When the king asked the woman, she confirmed it. So the king appointed for her an officer, saying, “Restore all that was hers, along with all the proceeds of the field from the day that she left the country until now.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 11:24; 2 Kings 6:24; 1 Kings 20:1"> Then Elisha came to Damascus while Ben-hadad king of Aram was sick, and the king was told, “The man of God has come here.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 19:15; 2 Kings 1:2; 1 Samuel 9:7"> So the king said to Hazael, “Take a gift in your hand, go to meet the man of God, and inquire of the LORD through him, ‘Will I recover from this illness?’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 5:13; 2 Kings 13:14; 1 Kings 19:15"> So Hazael went to meet Elisha, taking with him a gift of forty camel loads of every good thing from Damascus. And he went in and stood before him and said, “Your son Ben-hadad king of Aram has sent me to ask, ‘Will I recover from this illness?’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 7:7; Genesis 2:17; Amos 7:1"> Elisha answered, “Go and tell him, ‘You will surely recover.’ But the LORD has shown me that in fact he will die.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Romans 9:2; Jeremiah 9:18"> Elisha fixed his gaze steadily on him until Hazael became uncomfortable. Then the man of God began to weep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; Nahum 3:10; 2 Kings 15:16"> “Why is my lord weeping?” asked Hazael. “Because I know the evil you will do to the Israelites,” Elisha replied. “You will set fire to their fortresses, kill their young men with the sword, dash their little ones to pieces, and rip open their pregnant women.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:43; 1 Kings 19:15; 2 Samuel 9:8"> “But how could your servant, a mere dog, do such a monstrous thing?” said Hazael. And Elisha answered, “The LORD has shown me that you will be king over Aram.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 8:10; Matthew 26:16; 2 Kings 5:25"> So Hazael left Elisha and went to his master, who asked him, “What did Elisha say to you?” And he replied, “He told me that you would surely recover.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 8:13; 1 Kings 15:28; 1 Kings 11:26–37"> But the next day Hazael took a thick cloth, dipped it in water, and spread it over the king’s face. So Ben-hadad died, and Hazael reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 1:17; 2 Kings 3:1; 2 Chronicles 21:1–20"> In the fifth year of the reign of Joram son of Ahab over Israel, Jehoram son of Jehoshaphat succeeded his father as king of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:5–10"> Jehoram was thirty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem eight years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 8:26; 1 Kings 11:1–5; 2 Chronicles 21:13"> And Jehoram walked in the ways of the kings of Israel, just as the house of Ahab had done. For he married a daughter of Ahab and did evil in the sight of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 11:36; 2 Samuel 7:12–13; Luke 1:32–33"> Yet for the sake of His servant David, the LORD was unwilling to destroy Judah, since He had promised to maintain a lamp for David and his descendants forever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 22:47; 2 Kings 3:9; 2 Kings 8:22"> In the days of Jehoram, Edom rebelled against the hand of Judah and appointed their own king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:17"> So Jehoram crossed over to Zair with all his chariots. When the Edomites surrounded him and his chariot commanders, he rose up and attacked by night. His troops, however, fled to their homes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 19:8; Joshua 21:13; Genesis 27:40"> So to this day Edom has been in rebellion against the hand of Judah. Likewise, Libnah rebelled at the same time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 15:36; 1 Kings 11:41; 2 Chronicles 21:11–20"> As for the rest of the acts of Jehoram, along with all his accomplishments, are they not written in the book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:6; 2 Chronicles 25:23; 2 Chronicles 21:1"> And Jehoram rested with his fathers and was buried with them in the City of David. And his son Ahaziah reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 9:29; 2 Kings 8:16–17; 2 Chronicles 21:20–6"> In the twelfth year of the reign of Joram son of Ahab over Israel, Ahaziah son of Jehoram became king of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:2; 2 Kings 9:21–27; 2 Kings 11:1"> Ahaziah was twenty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem one year. His mother’s name was Athaliah, the granddaughter of Omri king of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:3–4; 2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> And Ahaziah walked in the ways of the house of Ahab and did evil in the sight of the LORD like the house of Ahab, for he was a son-in-law of the house of Ahab.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:5; 1 Kings 4:13; 2 Chronicles 19:2"> Then Ahaziah went with Joram son of Ahab to fight against Hazael king of Aram at Ramoth-gilead, and the Arameans wounded Joram.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 9:15–16; 2 Kings 8:28; 2 Chronicles 22:5–7"> So King Joram returned to Jezreel to recover from the wounds that the Arameans had inflicted on him at Ramah when he fought against Hazael king of Aram. Then Ahaziah son of Jehoram king of Judah went down to Jezreel to visit Joram son of Ahab, because Joram had been wounded.</span>
</div>
