<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 25:8; Hebrews 9:10–11; Hebrews 8:2"> Now even the first covenant had regulations for worship and also an earthly sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 40:4; Leviticus 24:5–8; Exodus 26:35"> A tabernacle was prepared. In its first room were the lampstand, the table, and the consecrated bread. This was called the Holy Place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 26:31–33; Exodus 40:3; Matthew 27:51"> Behind the second curtain was a room called the Most Holy Place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 17:10; 2 Chronicles 5:10; Exodus 40:3"> containing the golden altar of incense and the gold-covered ark of the covenant. Inside the ark were the gold jar of manna, Aaron’s staff that had budded, and the stone tablets of the covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 16:2; Numbers 7:89; Exodus 25:17–22"> Above the ark were the cherubim of glory, overshadowing the mercy seat. But we cannot discuss these things in detail now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Exodus 27:21; Luke 1:8–11"> When everything had been prepared in this way, the priests entered regularly into the first room to perform their sacred duties.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 16:34; Leviticus 5:18; Hebrews 10:19–20"> But only the high priest entered the second room, and then only once a year, and never without blood, which he offered for himself and for the sins the people had committed in ignorance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 14:6; Hebrews 3:7; Hebrews 10:19–22"> By this arrangement the Holy Spirit was showing that the way into the Most Holy Place had not yet been disclosed as long as the first tabernacle was still standing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:21; Hebrews 5:1; Romans 5:14"> It is an illustration for the present time, because the gifts and sacrifices being offered were unable to cleanse the conscience of the worshiper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:15; Colossians 2:16; Leviticus 11:2–47"> They consist only in food and drink and special washings and external regulations imposed until the time of reform.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 9:23–24; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Hebrews 8:1–2"> But when Christ came as high priest of the good things to come, He went through the greater and more perfect tabernacle that is not made by hands and is not a part of this creation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Hebrews 9:15"> He did not enter by the blood of goats and calves, but He entered the Most Holy Place once for all by His own blood, thus securing eternal redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 19:2–21; Leviticus 16:14–16; Acts 15:9"> For if the blood of bulls and goats and the ashes of a heifer sprinkled on those who are ceremonially unclean sanctify them so that their bodies are clean,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; 1 Peter 1:19; 1 John 1:7"> how much more will the blood of Christ, who through the eternal Spirit offered Himself unblemished to God, purify your consciences from works of death, so that we may serve the living God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 3:24–26; Romans 8:30; Romans 8:28"> Therefore Christ is the mediator of a new covenant, so that those who are called may receive the promised eternal inheritance, now that He has died to redeem them from the transgressions committed under the first covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 2:10; 1 Corinthians 15:22; Romans 5:12"> In the case of a will, it is necessary to establish the death of the one who made it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 48:21; John 14:27; Galatians 3:15"> because a will does not take effect until the one who made it has died; it cannot be executed while he is still alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 24:3–8; Hebrews 9:14; Hebrews 9:22"> That is why even the first covenant was not put into effect without blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 24:5–11; Numbers 19:6; Hebrews 10:4"> For when Moses had proclaimed every commandment of the law to all the people, he took the blood of calves and goats, along with water, scarlet wool, and hyssop, and sprinkled the scroll and all the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 24:8; Matthew 26:28; Hebrews 13:20"> saying, “This is the blood of the covenant, which God has commanded you to keep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 8:15; Leviticus 8:19; Exodus 29:36"> In the same way, he sprinkled with blood the tabernacle and all the vessels used in worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 17:11; Leviticus 6:7; Leviticus 4:35"> According to the law, in fact, nearly everything must be purified with blood, and without the shedding of blood there is no forgiveness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–21; Hebrews 10:4; Hebrews 9:14"> So it was necessary for the copies of the heavenly things to be purified with these sacrifices, but the heavenly things themselves with better sacrifices than these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 9:11–12; Hebrews 9:23"> For Christ did not enter a man-made copy of the true sanctuary, but He entered heaven itself, now to appear on our behalf in the presence of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:7; Hebrews 9:14"> Nor did He enter heaven to offer Himself again and again, as the high priest enters the Most Holy Place every year with blood that is not his own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12; 1 Peter 1:20; Hebrews 1:2"> Otherwise, Christ would have had to suffer repeatedly since the foundation of the world. But now He has appeared once for all at the end of the ages to do away with sin by the sacrifice of Himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> Just as man is appointed to die once, and after that to face judgment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Titus 2:13; 1 Peter 2:24; Romans 6:10"> so also Christ was offered once to bear the sins of many; and He will appear a second time, not to bear sin, but to bring salvation to those who eagerly await Him.</span>
