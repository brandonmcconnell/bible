<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 1:20–21; Joel 2:28; Numbers 12:6–8"> On many past occasions and in many different ways, God spoke to our fathers through the prophets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Colossians 1:16–18"> But in these last days He has spoken to us by His Son, whom He appointed heir of all things, and through whom He made the universe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; Revelation 4:11; John 14:9–10"> The Son is the radiance of God’s glory and the exact representation of His nature, upholding all things by His powerful word. After He had provided purification for our sins, He sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:21; Philippians 2:9–11; 1 Peter 3:22"> So He became as far superior to the angels as the name He has inherited is excellent beyond theirs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 2:7; Acts 13:33; Hebrews 5:5"> For to which of the angels did God ever say: “You are My Son; today I have become Your Father”? Or again: “I will be His Father, and He will be My Son”?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 97:7; Colossians 1:18; Deuteronomy 32:43"> And again, when God brings His firstborn into the world, He says: “Let all God’s angels worship Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 104:4; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 6:17"> Now about the angels He says: “He makes His angels winds, His servants flames of fire.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 45:6–7; Isaiah 9:6–7; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> But about the Son He says: “Your throne, O God, endures forever and ever, and justice is the scepter of Your kingdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 45:7; Psalm 23:5; Isaiah 61:3"> You have loved righteousness and hated wickedness; therefore God, Your God, has anointed You above Your companions with the oil of joy.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 102:25–27; Genesis 1:1; John 1:1–3"> And: “In the beginning, O Lord, You laid the foundations of the earth, and the heavens are the work of Your hands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Mark 13:31; Luke 21:33"> They will perish, but You remain; they will all wear out like a garment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 102:26–27; Hebrews 13:8; John 8:58"> You will roll them up like a robe; they will be changed; but You remain the same, and Your years will never end.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 110:1; Mark 12:36; Matthew 22:44"> Yet to which of the angels did God ever say: “Sit at My right hand until I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet”?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 34:7; Psalm 103:20–21; Psalm 91:11–12"> Are not the angels ministering spirits sent to serve those who will inherit salvation?</span>
</div>
</div>
