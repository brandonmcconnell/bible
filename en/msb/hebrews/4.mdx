<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 12:15; Hebrews 4:9; Proverbs 28:14"> Therefore, while the promise of entering His rest still stands, let us be careful that none of you be deemed to have fallen short of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; Romans 10:16–17; Hebrews 11:6"> For we also received the good news just as they did; but the message they heard was of no value to them, since they did not share the faith of those who comprehended it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 95:11; Matthew 11:28–29; 1 Peter 1:20"> Now we who have believed enter that rest. As for the others, it is just as God has said: “So I swore on oath in My anger, ‘They shall never enter My rest.’” And yet His works have been finished since the foundation of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Exodus 31:17; Genesis 2:1–2"> For somewhere He has spoken about the seventh day in this manner: “And on the seventh day God rested from all His works.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 95:11; Hebrews 4:3; Hebrews 3:11"> And again, as He says in the passage above: “They shall never enter My rest.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Hebrews 3:18–19; Acts 13:46–47"> Since, then, it remains for some to enter His rest, and since those who formerly heard the good news did not enter because of their disobedience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 95:7; Hebrews 3:7–8; Hebrews 3:15"> God again designated a certain day as “Today,” when a long time later He spoke through David as was just stated: “Today, if you hear His voice, do not harden your hearts.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:15; Joshua 22:4; Deuteronomy 12:9"> For if Joshua had given them rest, God would not have spoken later about another day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10; Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:14–17"> There remains, then, a Sabbath rest for the people of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Hebrews 4:3–4; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> For whoever enters God’s rest also rests from his own work, just as God did from His.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:28–30; Luke 13:24; Hebrews 3:18–1"> Let us, therefore, make every effort to enter that rest, so that no one will fall by following the same pattern of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:29; Isaiah 55:11; Ephesians 6:17"> For the word of God is living and active. Sharper than any double-edged sword, it pierces even to dividing soul and spirit, joints and marrow. It judges the thoughts and intentions of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:5; Psalm 33:13–15; Proverbs 15:3"> Nothing in all creation is hidden from God’s sight; everything is uncovered and exposed before the eyes of Him to whom we must give account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 9:12"> Therefore, since we have a great high priest who has passed through the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us hold firmly to what we profess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17–18; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Isaiah 53:4–5"> For we do not have a high priest who is unable to sympathize with our weaknesses, but we have one who was tempted in every way that we are, yet was without sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:19–23"> Let us then approach the throne of grace with confidence, so that we may receive mercy and find grace to help us in our time of need.</span>
