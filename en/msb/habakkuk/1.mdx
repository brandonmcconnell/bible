<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nahum 1:1; Isaiah 22:1"> This is the burden that Habakkuk the prophet received in a vision:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 22:1–2; Jeremiah 14:9; Psalm 13:1–2"> How long, O LORD, must I call for help but You do not hear, or cry out to You, “Violence!” but You do not save?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 55:9–11; Jeremiah 9:2–6; Jeremiah 20:8"> Why do You make me see iniquity? Why do You tolerate wrongdoing? Destruction and violence are before me. Strife is ongoing, and conflict abounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 119:126; Isaiah 1:21–23; Job 21:7"> Therefore the law is paralyzed, and justice never goes forth. For the wicked hem in the righteous, so that justice is perverted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:40–41; Lamentations 4:12; Isaiah 29:14"> “Look at the nations and observe—be utterly astounded! For I am doing a work in your days that you would never believe even if someone told you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 24:2; Jeremiah 5:15; Isaiah 23:13"> For behold, I am raising up the Chaldeans—that ruthless and impetuous nation which marches through the breadth of the earth to seize dwellings not their own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:5–9; Jeremiah 52:9–11; Deuteronomy 5:27"> They are dreaded and feared; from themselves they derive justice and sovereignty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Zephaniah 3:3; Hosea 8:1"> Their horses are swifter than leopards, fiercer than wolves of the night. Their horsemen charge ahead, and their cavalry comes from afar. They fly like a vulture, swooping down to devour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Hosea 13:15; Hosea 1:10"> All of them come bent on violence; their hordes advance like the east wind; they gather prisoners like sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:6; Jeremiah 32:24; Isaiah 14:16"> They scoff at kings and make rulers an object of scorn. They laugh at every fortress and build up siege ramps to seize it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 5:20; Daniel 4:30–34; Jeremiah 4:11–12"> Then they sweep by like the wind and pass through. They are guilty; their own strength is their god.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Jeremiah 30:11; Jeremiah 46:28"> Are You not from everlasting, O LORD, my God, my Holy One? We will not die. O LORD, You have appointed them to execute judgment; O Rock, You have established them for correction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 5:4–5; Psalm 34:15–16; Jeremiah 12:1–2"> Your eyes are too pure to look upon evil, and You cannot tolerate wrongdoing. So why do You tolerate the faithless? Why are You silent while the wicked swallow up those more righteous than themselves?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 6:7"> You have made men like the fish of the sea, like creeping things that have no ruler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 4:2; Jeremiah 16:16; Psalm 10:9"> The foe pulls all of them up with a hook; he catches them in his dragnet, and gathers them in his fishing net; so he rejoices gladly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:11; Deuteronomy 8:17; Daniel 4:30"> Therefore he sacrifices to his dragnet and burns incense to his fishing net, for by these things his portion is sumptuous and his food is rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:5–8; Ezekiel 25:1–17; Jeremiah 25:9–26"> Will he, therefore, empty his net and continue to slay nations without mercy?</span>
