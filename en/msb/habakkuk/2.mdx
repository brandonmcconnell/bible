</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:17; Hebrews 10:38; Galatians 3:11–12"> Look at the proud one; his soul is not upright—but the righteous will live by faith—</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:11–12; Proverbs 27:20; Proverbs 20:1"> and wealth indeed betrays him. He is an arrogant man never at rest. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 23:7; Micah 2:4; Jeremiah 50:13"> Will not all of these take up a taunt against him, speaking with mockery and derision: ‘Woe to him who amasses what is not his and makes himself rich with many loans! How long will this go on?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 47:11; Jeremiah 8:17"> Will not your creditors suddenly arise and those who disturb you awaken? Then you will become their prey.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Habakkuk 2:17; Jeremiah 27:7"> Because you have plundered many nations, the remnant of the people will plunder you—because of your bloodshed against man and your violence against the land, the city, and all their dwellers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:16; Jeremiah 22:13–19; Isaiah 28:15"> Woe to him who builds his house by unjust gain, to place his nest on high and escape the hand of disaster!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Nahum 1:14; 2 Kings 9:26; Jeremiah 36:31"> You have plotted shame for your house by cutting off many peoples and forfeiting your life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:40; Joshua 24:27; Job 31:38–40"> For the stones will cry out from the wall, and the rafters will echo it from the woodwork.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 3:10; Daniel 4:27–31; Nahum 3:1"> Woe to him who builds a city with bloodshed and establishes a town by iniquity!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Isaiah 50:11; Proverbs 21:30"> Is it not indeed from the LORD of Hosts that the labor of the people only feeds the fire, and the nations weary themselves in vain?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; Psalm 22:27; Psalm 72:19"> For the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD as the waters cover the sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:7; Genesis 9:22; Revelation 18:3"> Woe to him who gives drink to his neighbors, pouring it from the wineskin until they are drunk, in order to gaze at their nakedness!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 47:3; Nahum 3:5–6; Isaiah 51:21–23"> You will be filled with shame instead of glory. You too must drink and expose your uncircumcision! The cup in the LORD’s right hand will come around to you, and utter disgrace will cover your glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:8; Psalm 55:23; Zechariah 11:1"> For your violence against Lebanon will overwhelm you, and the destruction of animals will terrify you, because of your bloodshed against men and your violence against the land, the city, and all their dwellers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:8; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Isaiah 44:9–10"> What use is an idol, that a craftsman should carve it—or an image, a teacher of lies? For its maker trusts in his own creation; he makes idols that cannot speak.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 135:17; Jeremiah 10:4; 1 Kings 18:26–29"> Woe to him who says to wood, ‘Awake!’ or to silent stone, ‘Arise!’ Can it give guidance? Behold, it is overlaid with gold and silver, yet there is no breath in it at all.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 2:13; Zephaniah 1:7; Psalm 46:10"> But the LORD is in His holy temple; let all the earth be silent before Him.</span>
</div>
