<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 7:1–17; Psalm 86:1–17; Psalm 90:1–17"> This is a prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, according to Shigionoth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 85:6; Psalm 90:13–17; Hosea 6:2–3"> O LORD, I have heard the report of You; I stand in awe, O LORD, of Your deeds. Revive them in these years; make them known in these years. In Your wrath, remember mercy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Psalm 4:4; Obadiah 1:9"> God came from Teman, and the Holy One from Mount Paran. Selah His glory covered the heavens, and His praise filled the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Matthew 17:2; Job 26:14"> His radiance was like the sunlight; rays flashed from His hand, where His power is hidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 12:29–30; Numbers 16:46–49; Psalm 78:50–51"> Plague went before Him, and fever followed in His steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:26; Deuteronomy 33:15; Nahum 1:5"> He stood and measured the earth; He looked and startled the nations; the ancient mountains crumbled; the perpetual hills collapsed. His ways are everlasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 15:14–16; Genesis 10:6–7; Genesis 25:1–4"> I saw the tents of Cushan in distress; the curtains of Midian were trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 68:17; Deuteronomy 33:26–27; Exodus 14:21–22"> Were You angry at the rivers, O LORD? Was Your wrath against the streams? Did You rage against the sea when You rode on Your horses, on Your chariots of salvation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 7:12–13; Psalm 105:41; Psalm 78:15–16"> You brandished Your bow; You called for many arrows. Selah You split the earth with rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:24; Joshua 4:23–24; Psalm 93:3"> The mountains saw You and quaked; torrents of water swept by. The deep roared with its voice and lifted its hands on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 144:5–6; Psalm 18:12–14; Joshua 10:11–13"> Sun and moon stood still in their places at the flash of Your flying arrows, at the brightness of Your shining spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Micah 4:12–13; Numbers 21:23–35"> You marched across the earth with fury; You threshed the nations in wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 110:6; Psalm 105:15; Psalm 68:19–23"> You went forth for the salvation of Your people, to save Your anointed. You crushed the head of the house of the wicked and stripped him from head to toe. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 9:14; Daniel 11:40; Psalm 118:10–12"> With his own spear You pierced his head, when his warriors stormed out to scatter us, gloating as though ready to secretly devour the weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 77:19; Habakkuk 3:8; Exodus 15:8"> You trampled the sea with Your horses, churning the great waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:9; Habakkuk 1:5–11; Habakkuk 3:2"> I heard and trembled within; my lips quivered at the sound. Decay entered my bones; I trembled where I stood. Yet I must wait patiently for the day of distress to come upon the people who invade us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:17; Joel 1:16–18; Deuteronomy 28:15–18"> Though the fig tree does not bud and no fruit is on the vines, though the olive crop fails and the fields produce no food, though the sheep are cut off from the fold and no cattle are in the stalls,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 13:15; Philippians 4:4; Micah 7:7"> yet I will exult in the LORD; I will rejoice in the God of my salvation!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:34; Psalm 18:33; Philippians 4:13"> GOD the Lord is my strength; He makes my feet like those of a deer; He makes me walk upon the heights! For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments.</span>
