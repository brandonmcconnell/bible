<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 2:15; Joel 1:15; 1 Peter 4:7"> Blow the ram’s horn in Zion; sound the alarm on My holy mountain! Let all who dwell in the land tremble, for the Day of the LORD is coming; indeed, it is near—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 1:6; Zephaniah 1:14–15; Joel 2:10–11"> a day of darkness and gloom, a day of clouds and blackness. Like the dawn overspreading the mountains a great and strong army appears, such as never was of old, nor will ever be in ages to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Joel 1:19–20; Genesis 2:8"> Before them a fire devours, and behind them a flame scorches. The land before them is like the Garden of Eden, but behind them, it is like a desert wasteland—surely nothing will escape them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 9:7"> Their appearance is like that of horses, and they gallop like swift steeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 9:9; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 30:30"> With a sound like that of chariots they bound over the mountaintops, like the crackling of fire consuming stubble, like a mighty army deployed for battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nahum 2:10; Isaiah 13:8; Jeremiah 30:6"> Nations writhe in horror before them; every face turns pale.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 30:27; 2 Samuel 1:23; 2 Samuel 2:18–19"> They charge like mighty men; they scale the walls like men of war. Each one marches in formation, not swerving from the course.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:10; Job 33:18; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> They do not jostle one another; each proceeds in his path. They burst through the defenses, never breaking ranks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:21; John 10:1; Exodus 10:6"> They storm the city; they run along the wall; they climb into houses, entering through windows like thieves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Isaiah 13:10; Joel 3:15–16"> Before them the earth quakes; the heavens tremble. The sun and moon grow dark, and the stars lose their brightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Malachi 3:2; Nahum 1:6; Jeremiah 30:7"> The LORD raises His voice in the presence of His army. Indeed, His camp is very large, for mighty are those who obey His command. For the Day of the LORD is great and very dreadful. Who can endure it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 4:8–9; Isaiah 55:6–7; 2 Chronicles 7:13–14"> “Yet even now,” declares the LORD, “return to Me with all your heart, with fasting, weeping, and mourning.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 86:15; Psalm 34:18; Exodus 34:6–7"> So rend your hearts and not your garments, and return to the LORD your God. For He is gracious and compassionate, slow to anger, abounding in loving devotion. And He relents from sending disaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jonah 3:9; Haggai 2:19; 2 Kings 19:4"> Who knows? He may turn and relent and leave a blessing behind Him—grain and drink offerings for the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 1:14; Joel 2:1; 1 Kings 21:12"> Blow the ram’s horn in Zion, consecrate a fast, proclaim a sacred assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:5; 2 Chronicles 20:13; 1 Corinthians 7:5"> Gather the people, sanctify the congregation, assemble the aged, gather the children, even those nursing at the breast. Let the bridegroom leave his room, and the bride her chamber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 115:2; Ezekiel 8:16; Psalm 79:10"> Let the priests who minister before the LORD weep between the portico and the altar, saying, “Spare Your people, O LORD, and do not make Your heritage a reproach, an object of scorn among the nations. Why should they say among the peoples, ‘Where is their God?’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 8:2; Psalm 103:13; Zechariah 1:14"> Then the LORD became jealous for His land, and He spared His people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:29; Ezekiel 36:15; Isaiah 62:8–9"> And the LORD answered His people: “Behold, I will send you grain, new wine, and oil, and by them you will be satisfied. I will never again make you a reproach among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 14:8; Amos 4:10; Isaiah 34:3"> The northern army I will drive away from you, banishing it to a barren and desolate land, its front ranks into the Eastern Sea, and its rear guard into the Western Sea. And its stench will rise; its foul odor will ascend. For He has done great things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:16–17; Isaiah 54:4; Isaiah 41:10"> Do not be afraid, O land; rejoice and be glad, for the LORD has done great things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 8:12; Psalm 65:12; Psalm 67:6"> Do not be afraid, O beasts of the field, for the open pastures have turned green, the trees bear their fruit, and the fig tree and vine yield their best.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 5:7–8; Philippians 4:4; Leviticus 26:4"> Be glad, O children of Zion, and rejoice in the LORD your God, for He has given you the autumn rains for your vindication. He sends you showers, both autumn and spring rains, as before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Malachi 3:10; Amos 9:13; Joel 3:18"> The threshing floors will be full of grain, and the vats will overflow with new wine and oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zechariah 10:6; Joel 1:4–7; Joel 2:2–11"> I will repay you for the years eaten by locusts—the swarming locust, the young locust, the destroying locust, and the devouring locust—My great army that I sent against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalm 22:26; Romans 5:5"> You will have plenty to eat, until you are satisfied. You will praise the name of the LORD your God, who has worked wonders for you. My people will never again be put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:26–28; Leviticus 26:11–12; Zephaniah 3:17"> Then you will know that I am present in Israel and that I am the LORD your God, and there is no other. My people will never again be put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:16–21; Ezekiel 39:29; Isaiah 32:15"> And afterward, I will pour out My Spirit on all people. Your sons and daughters will prophesy, your old men will dream dreams, your young men will see visions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:13; Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11"> Even on My menservants and maidservants, I will pour out My Spirit in those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 2:19–20; Luke 21:11; Matthew 24:29"> I will show wonders in the heavens and on the earth, blood and fire and columns of smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 6:12–13; Matthew 24:29; Malachi 4:1"> The sun will be turned to darkness and the moon to blood before the coming of the great and awesome Day of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 10:11–14; Acts 2:20–21; Isaiah 46:13"> And everyone who calls on the name of the LORD will be saved; for on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there will be deliverance, as the LORD has promised, among the remnant called by the LORD.</span>
