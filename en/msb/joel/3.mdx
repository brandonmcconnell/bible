<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:19–20; Jeremiah 30:3; Ezekiel 39:25"> “Yes, in those days and at that time, when I restore Judah and Jerusalem from captivity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:2–4; Isaiah 66:16; Zephaniah 3:8"> I will gather all the nations and bring them down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat. There I will enter into judgment against them concerning My people, My inheritance, Israel, whom they have scattered among the nations as they divided up My land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nahum 3:10; Obadiah 1:11; Amos 2:6"> They cast lots for My people; they bartered a boy for a prostitute and sold a girl for wine to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 1:6–10; Jeremiah 47:4; Isaiah 59:18"> Now what do you have against Me, O Tyre, Sidon, and all the regions of Philistia? Are you rendering against Me a recompense? If you retaliate against Me, I will swiftly and speedily return your recompense upon your heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:16–17; 2 Kings 18:15–16; 2 Kings 12:18"> For you took My silver and gold and carried off My finest treasures to your temples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:13; Joel 3:3; Deuteronomy 28:68"> You sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks, to send them far from their homeland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5–6; Jeremiah 23:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:6"> Behold, I will rouse them from the places to which you sold them; I will return your recompense upon your heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 60:14; Job 1:15; Isaiah 14:1–2"> I will sell your sons and daughters into the hands of the people of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabeans—to a distant nation.” Indeed, the LORD has spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:3–4; Isaiah 8:9–10; Ezekiel 38:7"> Proclaim this among the nations: “Prepare for war; rouse the mighty men; let all the men of war advance and attack!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 12:8; Micah 4:3; 2 Chronicles 25:8"> Beat your plowshares into swords and your pruning hooks into spears. Let the weak say, ‘I am strong!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 13:3; Zephaniah 3:8; Ezekiel 38:9–18"> Come quickly, all you surrounding nations, and gather yourselves. Bring down Your mighty ones, O LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Isaiah 2:4; Psalm 96:13"> Let the nations be roused and advance to the Valley of Jehoshaphat, for there I will sit down to judge all the nations on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:15–20; Mark 4:29; Hosea 6:11"> Swing the sickle, for the harvest is ripe. Come, trample the grapes, for the winepress is full; the wine vats overflow because their wickedness is great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Philippians 3:2; Isaiah 34:2–8"> Multitudes, multitudes in the valley of decision! For the Day of the LORD is near in the valley of decision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 2:10; Matthew 24:29; Joel 2:31"> The sun and moon will grow dark, and the stars will no longer shine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 1:2; Joel 2:10–11; Haggai 2:6"> The LORD will roar from Zion and raise His voice from Jerusalem; heaven and earth will tremble. But the LORD will be a refuge for His people, a stronghold for the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:27; Nahum 1:15; Isaiah 52:1"> Then you will know that I am the LORD your God, who dwells in Zion, My holy mountain. Jerusalem will be holy, never again to be overrun by foreigners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:1–12; Zechariah 14:8; Revelation 22:1–2"> And in that day the mountains will drip with sweet wine, and the hills will flow with milk. All the streams of Judah will run with water, and a spring will flow from the house of the LORD to water the Valley of Acacias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 19:1–15; Jeremiah 51:35; Zechariah 14:18–19"> Egypt will become desolate, and Edom a desert wasteland, because of the violence done to the people of Judah, in whose land they shed innocent blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:25; Amos 9:15; Isaiah 33:20"> But Judah will be inhabited forever, and Jerusalem from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 4:4; Revelation 21:3; Ezekiel 36:25"> For I will avenge their blood, which I have not yet avenged.” For the LORD dwells in Zion.</span>
