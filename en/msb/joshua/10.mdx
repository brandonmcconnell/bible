</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:11; Isaiah 38:8; Isaiah 60:20"> On the day that the LORD gave the Amorites over to the Israelites, Joshua spoke to the LORD in the presence of Israel: “O sun, stand still over Gibeon, O moon, over the Valley of Aijalon.”</span>

</div>

</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:1; Joshua 10:5; Joshua 10:3"> So they brought the five kings out of the cave—the kings of Jerusalem, Hebron, Jarmuth, Lachish, and Eglon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Romans 16:20; Revelation 2:26–27"> When they had brought the kings to Joshua, he summoned all the men of Israel and said to the army commanders who had accompanied him, “Come here and put your feet on the necks of these kings.” So the commanders came forward and put their feet on their necks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:10"> “Do not be afraid or discouraged,” Joshua said. “Be strong and courageous, for the LORD will do this to all the enemies you fight.”</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 6:21; Deuteronomy 7:16; Deuteronomy 20:16–17"> On that day Joshua captured Makkedah and put it to the sword, along with its king. </div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the LORD also delivered that city and its king into the hand of Israel, and Joshua put all the people to the sword, leaving no survivors. And he did to the king of Libnah as he had done to the king of Jericho.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 10:3; Joshua 12:11; Joshua 10:5"> And Joshua and all Israel with him moved on from Libnah to Lachish. They laid siege to it and fought against it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the LORD delivered Lachish into the hand of Israel, and Joshua captured it on the second day. He put all the people to the sword, just as he had done to Libnah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; Judges 1:29; Joshua 16:3"> At that time Horam king of Gezer went to help Lachish, but Joshua struck him down along with his people, leaving no survivors.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 10:3; Joshua 12:12; Joshua 15:39"> So Joshua moved on from Lachish to Eglon, and all Israel with him. They laid siege to it and fought against it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 26:44; Joshua 10:37; Job 19:10"> That day they captured Eglon and put it to the sword, and Joshua devoted to destruction everyone in the city, just as he had done to Lachish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Judges 1:10; Joshua 15:13; Joshua 14:13–14"> Then Joshua and all Israel with him went up from Eglon to Hebron and fought against it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> They captured it and put to the sword its king, all its villages, and all the people. Joshua left no survivors, just as he had done at Eglon; he devoted to destruction Hebron and everyone in it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Joshua 15:15; Joshua 12:13; Joshua 15:49"> Finally Joshua and all Israel with him turned toward Debir and fought against it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Obadiah 1:18; Joshua 11:8; Deuteronomy 3:3"> And they captured Debir, its king, and all its villages. They put them to the sword and devoted to destruction everyone in the city, leaving no survivors. Joshua did to Debir and its king as he had done to Hebron and as he had done to Libnah and its king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2–16; Exodus 23:31–33; Joshua 8:27"> So Joshua conquered the whole region—the hill country, the Negev, the foothills, and the slopes, together with all their kings—leaving no survivors. He devoted to destruction everything that breathed, just as the LORD, the God of Israel, had commanded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Joshua 11:16; Joshua 15:51; Deuteronomy 9:23"> Joshua conquered the area from Kadesh-barnea to Gaza, and the whole region of Goshen as far as Gibeon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Joshua 10:14; Psalm 46:7; Psalm 80:3"> And because the LORD, the God of Israel, fought for Israel, Joshua captured all these kings and their land in one campaign.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 10:15; Joshua 4:19; 1 Samuel 11:14"> Then Joshua returned with all Israel to the camp at Gilgal.</span>
