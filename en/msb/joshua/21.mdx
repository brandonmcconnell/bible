<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Joshua 19:51; Joshua 17:4"> Now the family heads of the Levites approached Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the other tribes of Israel</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Numbers 35:2–8; Matthew 10:10"> at Shiloh in the land of Canaan and said to them, “The LORD commanded through Moses that we be given cities in which to live, together with pasturelands for our livestock.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 49:7; Deuteronomy 33:8–10; 1 Chronicles 6:54–81"> So by the command of the LORD, the Israelites gave the Levites these cities and their pasturelands out of their own inheritance:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:54–60; Joshua 24:33; Joshua 21:8–19"> The first lot came out for the Kohathite clans. The Levites who were descendants of Aaron the priest received thirteen cities by lot from the tribes of Judah, Simeon, and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 21:20–26; Exodus 6:16–25; 1 Chronicles 6:66–70"> The remaining descendants of Kohath received ten cities by lot from the tribes of Ephraim, Dan, and the half-tribe of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 21:27–33; 1 Chronicles 6:71–76; Exodus 6:16–17"> The descendants of Gershon received thirteen cities by lot from the tribes of Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and the half-tribe of Manasseh in Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:34–40; 1 Chronicles 6:77–81; Exodus 6:19"> And the descendants of Merari received twelve cities from the tribes of Reuben, Gad, and Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 21:3; Joshua 18:6; Numbers 33:54"> So the Israelites allotted to the Levites these cities, together with their pasturelands, as the LORD had commanded through Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:65; Joshua 21:13–18"> From the tribes of Judah and Simeon, they designated these cities by name</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 21:4; Numbers 3:27; Exodus 6:20–26"> to the descendants of Aaron from the Kohathite clans of the Levites, because the first lot fell to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:55; Luke 1:39; 2 Samuel 5:1–5"> They gave them Kiriath-arba (that is, Hebron), with its surrounding pasturelands, in the hill country of Judah. (Arba was the father of Anak.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 14:13–15; 1 Chronicles 6:55–57"> But they had given the fields and villages around the city to Caleb son of Jephunneh as his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 15:42; Joshua 10:29; Joshua 15:54"> So to the descendants of Aaron the priest they gave these cities, together with their pasturelands: Hebron, a city of refuge for the manslayer, Libnah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 15:48; Joshua 15:50; 1 Samuel 30:27–28"> Jattir, Eshtemoa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 15:49; Joshua 15:51; Joshua 12:13"> Holon, Debir,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; 1 Chronicles 6:59; Joshua 15:55"> Ain, Juttah, and Beth-shemesh—nine cities from these two tribes, together with their pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 18:24–25; Joshua 9:3; 1 Chronicles 6:60"> And from the tribe of Benjamin they gave them Gibeon, Geba,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 10:30; 1 Chronicles 6:60; Jeremiah 1:1"> Anathoth, and Almon—four cities, together with their pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> In all, thirteen cities, together with their pasturelands, were given to the priests, the descendants of Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:66; Joshua 21:5"> The remaining Kohathite clans of the Levites were allotted these cities: From the tribe of Ephraim</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 20:7; Judges 9:1; 1 Kings 12:1"> they were given Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim (a city of refuge for the manslayer), Gezer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:68; Joshua 16:3; Joshua 18:13–14"> Kibzaim, and Beth-horon—four cities, together with their pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 19:44–45"> From the tribe of Dan they were given Elteke, Gibbethon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 19:42; Joshua 10:12; 1 Chronicles 6:69"> Aijalon, and Gath-rimmon—four cities, together with their pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 17:11; Judges 5:19"> And from the half-tribe of Manasseh they were given Taanach and Gath-rimmon—two cities, together with their pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> In all, ten cities, together with their pasturelands, were given to the rest of the Kohathite clans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:71; Deuteronomy 1:4; Joshua 20:8"> This is what the Levite clans of the Gershonites were given: From the half-tribe of Manasseh they were given Golan in Bashan, a city of refuge for the manslayer, and Beeshterah—two cities, together with their pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 19:12; 1 Chronicles 6:72–73"> From the tribe of Issachar they were given Kishion, Daberath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 10:23; Joshua 12:11; Joshua 10:3"> Jarmuth, and En-gannim—four cities, together with their pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:74–75; Joshua 19:25–28"> From the tribe of Asher they were given Mishal, Abdon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 18:21; 1 Chronicles 6:75; Judges 1:31"> Helkath, and Rehob—four cities, together with their pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:76; Joshua 20:7; Joshua 19:37"> And from the tribe of Naphtali they were given Kedesh in Galilee (a city of refuge for the manslayer), Hammoth-dor, and Kartan—three cities, together with their pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> In all, thirteen cities, together with their pasturelands, were given to the Gershonite clans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:77; Joshua 19:11; Joshua 21:7"> This is what the Merarite clan (the rest of the Levites) were given: From the tribe of Zebulun they were given Jokneam, Kartah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Dimnah, and Nahalal—four cities, together with their pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 20:8; Deuteronomy 4:43; Numbers 21:23"> From the tribe of Reuben they were given Bezer, Jahaz,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Kedemoth, and Mephaath—four cities, together with their pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 32:2; 1 Kings 22:3; 1 Chronicles 6:80"> And from the tribe of Gad they were given Ramoth in Gilead, a city of refuge for the manslayer, Mahanaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 16:8–9; Numbers 32:35; Numbers 32:1"> Heshbon, and Jazer—four cities in all, together with their pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> In all, twelve cities were allotted to the clans of Merari, the remaining Levite clans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 35:1–8; Deuteronomy 33:10; Genesis 49:7"> For the Levites, then, there were forty-eight cities in all, together with their pasturelands, within the territory of the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Each of these cities had its own surrounding pasturelands; this was true for all the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Psalm 106:42–45; Exodus 23:27–31"> Thus the LORD gave Israel all the land He had sworn to give their fathers, and they took possession of it and settled in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Joshua 11:23; Hebrews 4:9; Deuteronomy 31:3–5"> And the LORD gave them rest on every side, just as He had sworn to their fathers. None of their enemies could stand against them, for the LORD delivered all their enemies into their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 8:56; Joshua 23:14–15; Numbers 23:19"> Not one of all the LORD’s good promises to the house of Israel had failed; everything was fulfilled.</span>
