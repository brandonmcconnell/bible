</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:25; Leviticus 26:17; Deuteronomy 32:30"> So about three thousand men went up, but they fled before the men of Ai.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 2:11; Joshua 2:9; Leviticus 26:36"> And the men of Ai struck down about thirty-six of them, chasing them from the gate as far as the quarries and striking them down on the slopes. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 37:29; Genesis 37:34; Revelation 18:19"> Then Joshua tore his clothes and fell facedown before the ark of the LORD until evening, as did the elders of Israel; and they all sprinkled dust on their heads.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5; 2 Kings 3:10; Matthew 17:20"> “O, Lord GOD,” Joshua said, “why did You ever bring this people across the Jordan to deliver us into the hand of the Amorites to be destroyed? If only we had been content to stay on the other side of the Jordan!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:5–6; Ezra 9:10; Habakkuk 2:1"> O Lord, what can I say, now that Israel has turned its back and run from its enemies?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 32:12; Psalm 83:4; Ezekiel 36:22–23"> When the Canaanites and all who live in the land hear about this, they will surround us and wipe out our name from the earth. Then what will You do for Your great name?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; Exodus 14:15; 1 Samuel 16:1"> But the LORD said to Joshua, “Stand up! Why have you fallen on your face?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 5:1–2; Hebrews 4:13; Joshua 6:17–19"> Israel has sinned; they have transgressed My covenant that I commanded them, and they have taken some of what was devoted to destruction. Indeed, they have stolen and lied, and they have put these things with their own possessions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 5:4–5; Isaiah 59:2; Joshua 6:18"> This is why the Israelites cannot stand against their enemies. They will turn their backs and run from their enemies, because they themselves have been set apart for destruction. I will no longer be with you unless you remove from among you whatever is devoted to destruction.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40–41; Joshua 3:5; Matthew 7:5"> Get up and consecrate the people, saying, ‘Consecrate yourselves for tomorrow, for this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: Among you, O Israel, there are things devoted to destruction. You cannot stand against your enemies until you remove them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 16:33; Jonah 1:7; Joshua 7:17–18"> In the morning you must present yourselves tribe by tribe. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 34:7; 1 Samuel 14:38–39; Judges 20:6"> The one who is caught with the things devoted to destruction must be burned, along with all that belongs to him, because he has transgressed the covenant of the LORD and committed an outrage in Israel.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 119:60; Joshua 3:1; Genesis 22:3"> So Joshua arose early the next morning and had Israel come forward tribe by tribe, and the tribe of Judah was selected.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; 1 Chronicles 2:4–7; Genesis 38:30"> He had the clans of Judah come forward, and the clan of the Zerahites was selected. He had the clan of the Zerahites come forward, and the family of Zabdi was selected.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 32:23; Acts 5:1–10; 1 Samuel 14:42"> And he had the family of Zabdi come forward man by man, and Achan son of Carmi, the son of Zabdi, the son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, was selected.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:5; Jeremiah 13:16; John 9:24"> So Joshua said to Achan, “My son, give glory to the LORD, the God of Israel, and make a confession to Him. I urge you to tell me what you have done; do not hide it from me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 38:18; Job 33:27; Genesis 42:21"> “It is true,” Achan replied, “I have sinned against the LORD, the God of Israel. This is what I did:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 1:15; Psalm 119:37; 1 John 2:15–16"> When I saw among the spoils a beautiful cloak from Shinar, two hundred shekels of silver, and a bar of gold weighing fifty shekels, I coveted them and took them. They are hidden in the ground inside my tent, with the silver underneath.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> So Joshua sent messengers who ran to the tent, and there it all was, hidden in his tent, with the silver underneath.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> They took the things from inside the tent, brought them to Joshua and all the Israelites, and spread them out before the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:27; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; Joshua 7:26"> Then Joshua, together with all Israel, took Achan son of Zerah, the silver, the cloak, the bar of gold, his sons and daughters, his oxen and donkeys and sheep, his tent, and everything else he owned, and brought them to the Valley of Achor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 6:18; 1 Chronicles 2:7; Leviticus 24:14"> “Why have you brought this trouble upon us?” said Joshua. “Today the LORD will bring trouble upon you!” And all Israel stoned him to death. Then they stoned the others and burned their bodies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 65:10; Hosea 2:15; Deuteronomy 13:17"> And they heaped over Achan a large pile of rocks that remains to this day. So the LORD turned from His burning anger. Therefore that place is called the Valley of Achor to this day.</span>
</div>
