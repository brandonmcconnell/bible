<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 26:29; Genesis 41:51; Genesis 50:23"> Now this was the allotment for the tribe of Manasseh as Joseph’s firstborn son, namely for Machir the firstborn of Manasseh and father of the Gileadites, who had received Gilead and Bashan because Machir was a man of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:18; Numbers 26:29–32; Judges 6:11"> So this allotment was for the rest of the descendants of Manasseh—the clans of Abiezer, Helek, Asriel, Shechem, Hepher, and Shemida. These are the other male descendants of the clans of Manasseh son of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 27:1; Numbers 26:33; Numbers 36:2–11"> But Zelophehad son of Hepher (the son of Gilead, the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh) had no sons but only daughters. These are the names of his daughters: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Numbers 27:5–7; Galatians 3:28"> They approached Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the leaders, and said, “The LORD commanded Moses to give us an inheritance among our brothers.” So Joshua gave them an inheritance among their father’s brothers, in keeping with the command of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 17:2–3; Numbers 32:30–42; Joshua 13:29–31"> Thus ten shares fell to Manasseh, in addition to the land of Gilead and Bashan beyond the Jordan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> because the daughters of Manasseh received an inheritance among his sons. And the land of Gilead belonged to the rest of the sons of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:21; Joshua 20:7; Joshua 24:32"> Now the border of Manasseh went from Asher to Michmethath near Shechem, then southward to include the inhabitants of En-tappuah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 16:8; Joshua 15:34; Joshua 12:17"> The region of Tappuah belonged to Manasseh, but Tappuah itself, on the border of Manasseh, belonged to Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 16:3; Joshua 19:29; Joshua 16:8–9"> From there the border continued southward to the Brook of Kanah. There were cities belonging to Ephraim among the cities of Manasseh, but the border of Manasseh was on the north side of the brook and ended at the Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Ephraim’s territory was to the south, and Manasseh’s was to the north, having the Sea as its border and adjoining Asher on the north and Issachar on the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:29; 2 Kings 9:27; Judges 5:19"> Within Issachar and Asher, Manasseh was assigned Beth-shean, Ibleam, Dor (that is, Naphath), Endor, Taanach, and Megiddo, each with their surrounding settlements.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 1:27–28; Exodus 23:29–33; Romans 6:12–14"> But the descendants of Manasseh were unable to occupy these cities, because the Canaanites were determined to stay in this land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; Philippians 4:13; Judges 1:35"> However, when the Israelites grew stronger, they put the Canaanites to forced labor; but they failed to drive them out completely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 26:34–37; Genesis 48:19; Genesis 48:22"> Then the sons of Joseph said to Joshua, “Why have you given us only one portion as an inheritance? We have many people, because the LORD has blessed us abundantly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 13:7; Genesis 14:5; Luke 12:48"> Joshua answered them, “If you have so many people that the hill country of Ephraim is too small for you, go to the forest and clear for yourself an area in the land of the Perizzites and the Rephaim.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 4:12; Judges 4:3; Judges 1:19"> “The hill country is not enough for us,” they replied, “and all the Canaanites who live in the valley have iron chariots, both in Beth-shean with its towns and in the Valley of Jezreel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> So Joshua said to the house of Joseph—to Ephraim and Manasseh—“You have many people and great strength. You shall not have just one allotment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Romans 8:31; Numbers 14:6–9"> because the hill country will be yours as well. It is a forest; clear it, and its farthest limits will be yours. Although the Canaanites have iron chariots and although they are strong, you can drive them out.”</span>
