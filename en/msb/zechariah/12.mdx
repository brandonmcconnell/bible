</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:28; Zechariah 12:3; Zechariah 12:6"> On that day, declares the LORD, I will strike every horse with panic, and every rider with madness. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 10:12; Joel 3:16; Psalm 18:32"> Then the leaders of Judah will say in their hearts: ‘The people of Jerusalem are my strength, for the LORD of Hosts is their God.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Obadiah 1:18; Micah 5:5–8; Zechariah 2:4"> On that day I will make the clans of Judah like a firepot in a woodpile, like a flaming torch among the sheaves; they will consume all the peoples around them on the right and on the left, while the people of Jerusalem remain secure there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:18; Romans 3:27; Luke 1:51–53"> The LORD will save the tents of Judah first, so that the glory of the house of David and of the people of Jerusalem may not be greater than that of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 5:2–4; Zechariah 9:8; Joel 3:16–17"> On that day the LORD will defend the people of Jerusalem, so that the weakest among them will be like David, and the house of David will be like God, like the angel of the LORD going before them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 54:17; Zechariah 14:2–3; Haggai 2:22"> So on that day I will set out to destroy all the nations that come against Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Ezekiel 39:29; Joel 2:28–29"> Then I will pour out on the house of David and on the people of Jerusalem a spirit of grace and prayer, and they will look on Me, the One they have pierced. They will mourn for Him as one mourns for an only child, and grieve bitterly for Him as one grieves for a firstborn son.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:24; 2 Kings 23:29"> On that day the wailing in Jerusalem will be as great as the wailing of Hadad-rimmon in the plain of Megiddo.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 7:3; Luke 3:31; 2 Samuel 5:14"> The land will mourn, each clan on its own: the clan of the house of David and their wives, the clan of the house of Nathan and their wives,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:7; 2 Samuel 16:5; 1 Chronicles 23:10"> the clan of the house of Levi and their wives, the clan of Shimei and their wives,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 9:12"> and all the remaining clans and their wives.</span>
