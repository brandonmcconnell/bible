It will remain inside his house and destroy it, down to its timbers and stones.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 1:9; Zechariah 1:14; Zechariah 2:3"> Then the angel who was speaking with me came forward and told me, “Now lift up your eyes and see what is approaching.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:1–49; Revelation 17:1–18; Jeremiah 3:1–2"> And behold, the cover of lead was raised, and there was a woman sitting inside the basket.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:16; Amos 9:1–4; Proverbs 5:22"> “This is Wickedness,” he said. And he shoved her down into the basket, pushing down the lead cover over its opening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:49; Hosea 8:1; Daniel 9:26–27"> Then I lifted up my eyes and saw two women approaching, with the wind in their wings. Their wings were like those of a stork, and they lifted up the basket between heaven and earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> “Where are they taking the basket?” I asked the angel who was speaking with me.</span>
