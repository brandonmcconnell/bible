</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:23; Ezekiel 28:12; Ezekiel 28:2–5"> and also against Hamath, which borders it, as well as Tyre and Sidon, though they are very shrewd.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:33; Job 27:16; Ezekiel 28:4–5"> Tyre has built herself a fortress; she has heaped up silver like dust, and gold like the dirt of the streets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:18; Ezekiel 26:17; Isaiah 23:1–7"> Behold, the Lord will impoverish her and cast her wealth into the sea, and she will be consumed by fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:26; Revelation 18:9–17; Jeremiah 47:1"> Ashkelon will see and fear; Gaza will writhe in agony, as will Ekron, for her hope will wither. There will cease to be a king in Gaza, and Ashkelon will be uninhabited.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 1:8; Zephaniah 2:10; 1 Peter 5:5"> A mixed race will occupy Ashdod, and I will cut off the pride of the Philistines.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 3:12; Ezekiel 16:57–61; Isaiah 60:14–16"> I will remove the blood from their mouths and the abominations from between their teeth. Then they too will become a remnant for our God; they will become like a clan in Judah, and Ekron will be like the Jebusites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 54:14; Isaiah 60:18; Isaiah 52:1"> But I will camp around My house because of an army, because of those who march to and fro, and never again will an oppressor overrun My people, for now I keep watch with My own eyes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 21:4–7; Zephaniah 3:14–15; John 12:13–16"> Rejoice greatly, O Daughter of Zion! Shout in triumph, O Daughter of Jerusalem! See, your King comes to you, righteous and victorious, humble and riding on a donkey, on a colt, the foal of a donkey.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 1:7; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Hosea 2:18"> And I will cut off the chariot from Ephraim and the horse from Jerusalem, and the bow of war will be broken. Then He will proclaim peace to the nations. His dominion will extend from sea to sea, and from the Euphrates to the ends of the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:14; Exodus 24:8; Psalm 102:19–21"> As for you, because of the blood of My covenant, I will release your prisoners from the waterless pit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 42:10; Isaiah 61:7; Lamentations 3:21–22"> Return to your stronghold, O prisoners of hope; even today I declare that I will restore to you double.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; Zechariah 10:3–7; Isaiah 49:2"> For I will bend Judah as My bow and fit it with Ephraim. I will rouse your sons, O Zion, against the sons of Greece. I will make you like the sword of a mighty man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 18:14; Isaiah 66:15; Psalm 144:5–6"> Then the LORD will appear over them, and His arrow will go forth like lightning. The Lord GOD will sound the ram’s horn and advance in the whirlwinds of the south.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Zechariah 10:7; Zechariah 10:5"> The LORD of Hosts will shield them. They will destroy and conquer with slingstones; they will drink and roar as with wine. And they will be filled like sprinkling bowls, drenched like the corners of the altar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 62:3; Luke 12:32; Haggai 2:23"> On that day the LORD their God will save them as the flock of His people; for like jewels in a crown they will sparkle over His land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 33:17; Isaiah 62:8–9; 1 John 4:8–11"> How lovely they will be, and how beautiful! Grain will make the young men flourish, and new wine, the young women.</span>
</div>
</div>
