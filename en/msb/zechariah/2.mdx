<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Zechariah 1:16; Ezekiel 47:4"> Then I lifted up my eyes and saw a man with a measuring line in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:39; Ezekiel 45:6; Revelation 21:15–17"> “Where are you going?” I asked. “To measure Jerusalem,” he replied, “and to determine its width and length.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 1:19; Zechariah 1:8–11; Zechariah 4:5"> Then the angel who was speaking with me went forth, and another angel came forward to meet him</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 1:17; Jeremiah 33:22; Ezekiel 38:11"> and said to him, “Run and tell that young man: ‘Jerusalem will be a city without walls because of the multitude of men and livestock within it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 4:5; Zechariah 9:8; Psalm 3:3"> For I will be a wall of fire around it, declares the LORD, and I will be the glory within it.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Ezekiel 17:21; Isaiah 52:11–12"> “Get up! Get up! Flee from the land of the north,” declares the LORD, “for I have scattered you like the four winds of heaven,” declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Genesis 19:17; Isaiah 52:11"> “Get up, O Zion! Escape, you who dwell with the Daughter of Babylon!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 17:8; Deuteronomy 32:10; John 15:21–23"> For this is what the LORD of Hosts says: “After His Glory has sent Me against the nations that have plundered you—for whoever touches you touches the apple of His eye—</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 19:16; Isaiah 14:2; Zechariah 4:9"> I will surely wave My hand over them, so that they will become plunder for their own servants. Then you will know that the LORD of Hosts has sent Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; John 1:14; Zephaniah 3:14–15"> “Shout for joy and be glad, O Daughter of Zion, for I am coming to dwell among you,” declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; 1 Peter 2:9–10; Acts 28:28"> “On that day many nations will join themselves to the LORD, and they will become My people. I will dwell among you, and you will know that the LORD of Hosts has sent Me to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 1:17; Deuteronomy 32:9; Jeremiah 10:16"> And the LORD will take possession of Judah as His portion in the Holy Land, and He will once again choose Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:20; Psalm 68:5; Psalm 78:65"> Be silent before the LORD, all people, for He has roused Himself from His holy dwelling.”</span>
