I will also remove the prophets and the spirit of impurity from the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:6–11; Deuteronomy 18:20; Ezekiel 14:9"> And if anyone still prophesies, his father and mother who bore him will say to him, ‘You shall not remain alive, because you have spoken falsely in the name of the LORD.’ When he prophesies, his father and mother who bore him will pierce him through.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 3:6–7; Matthew 3:4; Revelation 11:3"> And on that day every prophet who prophesies will be ashamed of his vision, and he will not put on a hairy cloak in order to deceive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 7:14; Acts 19:17–20"> He will say, ‘I am not a prophet; I work the land, for I was purchased as a servant in my youth.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:35; Psalm 22:16; Proverbs 27:5–6"> If someone asks him, ‘What are these wounds on your chest?’ he will answer, ‘These are the wounds I received in the house of my friends.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:27; Matthew 26:31; John 16:32"> Awake, O sword, against My Shepherd, against the man who is My Companion, declares the LORD of Hosts. Strike the Shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered, and I will turn My hand against the little ones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:12; Revelation 16:19; Ezekiel 5:2–4"> And in all the land, declares the LORD, two-thirds will be cut off and perish, but a third will be left in it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 50:15; Jeremiah 29:11–12; Acts 2:21"> This third I will bring through the fire; I will refine them like silver and test them like gold. They will call on My name, and I will answer them. I will say, ‘They are My people,’ and they will say, ‘The LORD is our God.’”</span>
</div>
