<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 20:1–16; Matthew 12:43–45; Luke 14:16"> Once again, Jesus spoke to them in parables:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–9; Matthew 13:24; Ephesians 5:24–32"> “The kingdom of heaven is like a king who prepared a wedding banquet for his son.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; John 5:40; Luke 14:15–17"> He sent his servants to call those he had invited to the banquet, but they refused to come.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:36; John 6:50–57"> Again, he sent other servants and said, ‘Tell those who have been invited that I have prepared my dinner. My oxen and fattened cattle have been killed, and everything is ready. Come to the wedding banquet.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:3; Luke 14:18–20; 1 John 2:15–16"> But they paid no attention and went away, one to his field, another to his business.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:19–20; John 16:2–3; Acts 7:51–57"> The rest seized his servants, mistreated them, and killed them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 19:27; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Luke 21:24"> When he heard this, the king was enraged, and he sent his troops to destroy those murderers and burn their city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; Acts 13:46; Revelation 3:4"> Then he said to his servants, ‘The wedding banquet is ready, but those I invited were not worthy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 14:21–24; Proverbs 1:20–23; Mark 16:15–16"> Go therefore to the crossroads and invite to the banquet as many as you can find.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Revelation 19:6–9; 2 Corinthians 12:21"> So the servants went out into the streets and gathered everyone they could find, both evil and good, and the wedding hall was filled with guests.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 19:8; Hebrews 4:12–13"> But when the king came in to see the guests, he spotted a man who was not dressed in wedding clothes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:9; Job 5:16; Matthew 20:13"> ‘Friend,’ he asked, ‘how did you get in here without wedding clothes?’ But the man was speechless.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 25:30; Matthew 24:51"> Then the king told the servants, ‘Tie him hand and foot, take him away, and throw him into the outer darkness, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Luke 13:23–24; Matthew 7:13–14"> For many are called, but few are chosen.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Mark 12:13–17; Psalm 2:2"> Then the Pharisees went out and conspired to trap Jesus in His words.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 3:6; Mark 8:15; Mark 12:14"> They sent their disciples to Him along with the Herodians. “Teacher,” they said, “we know that You are honest and that You teach the way of God in accordance with the truth. You seek favor from no one, because You pay no attention to external appearance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 2:1; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 17:25"> So tell us what You think: Is it lawful to pay taxes to Caesar or not?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:6; Luke 5:22; John 2:25"> But Jesus knew their evil intent and said, “You hypocrites, why are you testing Me?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 20:2; Revelation 6:6; Matthew 18:28"> Show Me the coin used for the tax.” And they brought Him a denarius.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 20:24"> “Whose image is this,” He asked, “and whose inscription?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:7; Proverbs 24:21; Acts 4:19"> “Caesar’s,” they answered. So Jesus told them, “Give to Caesar what is Caesar’s, and to God what is God’s.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 12:12; Matthew 22:46; Luke 20:25–26"> And when they heard this, they were amazed. So they left Him and went away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Mark 12:18–27; Acts 23:6–8"> That same day the Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection, came to Jesus and questioned Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Luke 20:28; Ruth 1:11"> “Teacher,” they said, “Moses declared that if a man dies without having children, his brother is to marry the widow and raise up offspring for him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:29–33; Mark 12:19–23; Hebrews 9:27"> Now there were seven brothers among us. The first one married and died without having children. So he left his wife to his brother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The same thing happened to the second and third brothers, down to the seventh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And last of all, the woman also died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> In the resurrection, then, whose wife will she be of the seven? For all of them were married to her.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 20:9; Romans 15:4; Luke 1:37"> Jesus answered, “You are mistaken because you do not know the Scriptures or the power of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 20:34–36; Mark 12:24–25; Matthew 24:38"> In the resurrection, people will neither marry nor be given in marriage. Instead, they will be like the angels of God in heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 12:3; Matthew 21:16; Matthew 21:42"> But concerning the resurrection of the dead, have you not read what God said to you:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Mark 12:26–27; Luke 20:37–38"> ‘I am the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob’? He is not the God of the dead, but of the living.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 12:17; Mark 6:2; John 7:46"> When the crowds heard this, they were astonished at His teaching.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 12:28–31; Matthew 25:3–5; Matthew 12:14"> And when the Pharisees heard that Jesus had silenced the Sadducees, they themselves gathered together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 7:30; Luke 10:25–37; Luke 11:52"> One of them, an expert in the law, tested Him with a question:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 12:28–33; Matthew 5:19–20; Luke 11:42"> “Teacher, which commandment is the greatest in the Law?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 12:29–30; Luke 10:27; Deuteronomy 6:5"> Jesus declared, “‘Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> This is the first and greatest commandment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 12:31; Luke 10:27–37; Leviticus 19:18"> And the second is like it: ‘Love your neighbor as yourself.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 7:12; 1 John 4:19–21"> All the Law and the Prophets hang on these two commandments.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 22:34"> While the Pharisees were assembled, Jesus questioned them:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; John 6:68–69; Matthew 16:13–17"> “What do you think about the Christ? Whose son is He?” “David’s,” they answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:2; Revelation 4:2; Mark 12:36"> Jesus said to them, “How then does David in the Spirit call Him ‘Lord’? For he says:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalm 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Acts 2:34–35"> ‘The Lord said to my Lord, “Sit at My right hand until I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet.”’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Revelation 22:16; John 8:58"> So if David calls Him ‘Lord,’ how can He be David’s son?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Mark 12:34; Luke 20:40; Luke 14:6"> No one was able to answer a word, and from that day on no one dared to question Him any further.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
