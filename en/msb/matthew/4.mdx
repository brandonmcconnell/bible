<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:1–5; 1 Thessalonians 3:5; Numbers 11:4–6"> The tempter came to Him and said, “If You are the Son of God, tell these stones to become bread.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Luke 4:4; John 6:63"> But Jesus answered, “It is written: ‘Man shall not live on bread alone, but on every word that comes from the mouth of God.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 4:9; Matthew 27:53; Revelation 11:2"> Then the devil took Him to the holy city and set Him on the pinnacle of the temple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 91:11–12; Hebrews 1:14; Psalm 34:7"> “If You are the Son of God,” he said, “throw Yourself down. For it is written: ‘He will command His angels concerning You, and they will lift You up in their hands, so that You will not strike Your foot against a stone.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; 1 Corinthians 10:9; Psalm 78:18"> Jesus replied, “It is also written: ‘Do not put the Lord your God to the test.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Revelation 11:15; Psalm 49:16–17"> Again, the devil took Him to a very high mountain and showed Him all the kingdoms of the world and their glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; Daniel 5:18–19; John 16:11"> “All this I will give You,” he said, “if You will fall down and worship me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13–14; Deuteronomy 10:20; Joshua 24:14"> “Get behind Me, Satan!” Jesus told him. “For it is written: ‘Worship the Lord your God and serve Him only.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 26:53; Hebrews 1:14; Luke 22:43"> Then the devil left Him, and angels came and ministered to Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 1:14; Luke 3:20; Matthew 14:3"> When Jesus heard that John had been imprisoned, He withdrew to Galilee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 4:46; Mark 1:21; Matthew 11:23"> Leaving Nazareth, He went and lived in Capernaum, which is by the sea in the region of Zebulun and Naphtali,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 9:1–2; Matthew 2:23; Luke 24:44"> to fulfill what was spoken through the prophet Isaiah:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:1; 2 Kings 15:29; Joshua 21:32"> “Land of Zebulun and land of Naphtali, the Way of the Sea, beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles—</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 9:2; Isaiah 42:6–7; Isaiah 60:1–3"> the people living in darkness have seen a great light; on those living in the land of the shadow of death, a light has dawned.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:38; Mark 1:14–15"> From that time on Jesus began to preach, “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is near.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 1:40–42; Matthew 10:2; Luke 6:14"> As Jesus was walking beside the Sea of Galilee, He saw two brothers, Simon called Peter and his brother Andrew. They were casting a net into the sea, for they were fishermen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 1:17–18; John 12:26; Matthew 16:24"> “Come, follow Me,” Jesus said, “and I will make you fishers of men.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 10:28–31; Matthew 19:27; Luke 18:28–30"> And at once they left their nets and followed Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 1:19–20; Matthew 10:2; Mark 3:17"> Going on from there, He saw two other brothers, James son of Zebedee and his brother John. They were in a boat with their father Zebedee, mending their nets. Jesus called them,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Luke 9:59–60; Deuteronomy 33:9–10"> and immediately they left the boat and their father and followed Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 9:35; Mark 1:39; Acts 10:38"> Jesus went throughout Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every disease and sickness among the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 8:6; Matthew 17:15; Luke 5:15"> News about Him spread all over Syria, and people brought to Him all who were ill with various diseases, those suffering acute pain, the demon-possessed, those having seizures, and the paralyzed, and He healed them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 6:17; Mark 5:20; Mark 3:7–8"> Large crowds followed Him, having come from Galilee, the Decapolis, Jerusalem, Judea, and beyond the Jordan.</span>
</div>
</div>
