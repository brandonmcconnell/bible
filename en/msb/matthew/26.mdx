<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 14:9; Matthew 24:14; Psalm 112:6"> Truly I tell you, wherever this gospel is preached in all the world, what she has done will also be told in memory of her.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 22:3–6; Matthew 10:4; Mark 14:10–11"> Then one of the Twelve, the one called Judas Iscariot, went to the chief priests</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Genesis 37:26–28; Exodus 21:32"> and asked, “What are you willing to give me if I hand Him over to you?” And they set out for him thirty pieces of silver.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 14:13–16; John 7:6; John 17:1"> He answered, “Go into the city to a certain man and tell him that the Teacher says, ‘My time is near. I will keep the Passover with My disciples at your house.’”</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 13:21; Hebrews 4:13; John 6:70–71"> And while they were eating, He said to them, “Truly I tell you, one of you will betray Me.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–14; Hebrews 9:14–22; Leviticus 17:11"> This is My blood of the new covenant, which is poured out for many for the forgiveness of sins.</span>

<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 18:36; Mark 14:47; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> At this, one of Jesus’ companions drew his sword and struck the servant of the high priest, cutting off his ear.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Revelation 13:10; Genesis 9:6; Romans 12:19"> “Put your sword back in its place,” Jesus said to him. “For all who draw the sword will perish by the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Kings 6:17; Matthew 4:11; Daniel 7:10"> Are you not aware that I can call on My Father right now, and He will at once put at My disposal more than twelve legions of angels?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Matthew 26:24; Acts 1:16"> But how then would the Scriptures be fulfilled that say it must happen this way?”</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Mark 12:35; John 8:2; Luke 22:52–53"> At that time Jesus said to the crowd, “Have you come out with swords and clubs to arrest Me as you would an outlaw? Every day I sat with you teaching in the temple courts, and you did not arrest Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; John 16:32; Mark 14:50–52"> But this has all happened so that the writings of the prophets would be fulfilled.” Then all the disciples deserted Him and fled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; Mark 14:53–65; Psalm 56:5–6"> Those who had arrested Jesus led Him away to the house of Caiaphas the high priest, where the scribes and elders had gathered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 7:32; John 18:15–16; John 18:25"> But Peter followed Him at a distance, right up to the courtyard of the high priest. And he went in and sat down with the guards to see the outcome.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Acts 6:11–13; Deuteronomy 19:16–21; Mark 14:55–56"> Now the chief priests, the elders, and the whole Sanhedrin were seeking false testimony against Jesus in order to put Him to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Psalm 35:11; Psalm 27:12"> But they did not find any. Even though many false witnesses came forward, they found none. Finally two false witnesses came forward</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="John 2:19–21; Matthew 27:40; Mark 15:29"> and declared, “This man said, ‘I am able to destroy the temple of God and rebuild it in three days.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="John 19:9–11; John 18:19–24; Mark 14:60"> So the high priest stood up and asked Him, “Have You no answer? What are these men testifying against You?”</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Mark 14:62; Psalm 110:1"> “You have said it yourself,” Jesus answered. “But I say to all of you, from now on you will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of Power and coming on the clouds of heaven.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:24; John 10:33; Matthew 9:3"> At this, the high priest tore his clothes and declared, “He has blasphemed! Why do we need any more witnesses? Look, now you have heard His blasphemy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="John 19:7; Acts 7:52; Leviticus 24:11–16"> What do you think?” “He deserves to die,” they answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 27:30"> Then they spit in His face and struck Him. Others slapped Him</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Luke 22:63–65; Mark 14:65; 1 Peter 2:4–8"> and said, “Prophesy to us, Christ! Who hit You?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="1 Kings 19:13; Matthew 26:71; Acts 5:37"> Meanwhile, Peter was sitting out in the courtyard, and a servant girl came up to him. “You also were with Jesus the Galilean,” she said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:12; Revelation 21:8; Matthew 26:40–43"> But he denied it before them all: “I do not know what you are talking about.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Mark 14:68–69; John 18:25–27; Luke 22:58"> When Peter had gone out to the gateway, another servant girl saw him and said to the people there, “This man was also with Jesus of Nazareth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Matthew 26:74; Isaiah 48:1; Zechariah 8:17"> And again he denied it with an oath: “I do not know the man!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73" data-xref="Judges 12:6; John 18:26–27; Nehemiah 13:24"> After a little while, those standing nearby came up to Peter. “Surely you are one of them,” they said, “for your accent gives you away.”</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; 1 Corinthians 16:22; Mark 14:71–72"> At that he began to curse and swear to them, “I do not know the man!” And immediately a rooster crowed.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v75" data-v="75" data-xref="Matthew 26:34; Romans 7:18–20; Luke 22:31–34"> Then Peter remembered the word that Jesus had spoken to him: “Before the rooster crows, you will deny Me three times.” And he went outside and wept bitterly.</span>
