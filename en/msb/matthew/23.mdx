<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:16; Romans 2:19–24"> So observe and practice everything they tell you to observe. But do not do what they do, for they do not practice what they preach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 11:46; Acts 15:10; Galatians 6:13"> They tie up heavy, burdensome loads and lay them on men’s shoulders, but they themselves are not willing to lift a finger to move them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 6:1–16; John 12:43; Philippians 2:3"> All their deeds are done for men to see. They broaden their phylacteries and lengthen the tassels of their garments.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 John 1:9; Luke 11:43–54; Mark 12:38–39"> They love the places of honor at banquets, the chief seats in the synagogues,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 1:38; John 1:49; John 3:26"> the greetings in the marketplaces, and the title of ‘Rabbi, Rabbi’ by which they are addressed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 3:1; 1 Corinthians 1:12–13; 1 Peter 5:3"> But you are not to be called ‘Rabbi,’ for you have one Teacher, the Christ, and you are all brothers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:1; Hebrews 12:9; Malachi 1:6"> And do not call anyone on earth your father, for you have one Father, who is in heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Nor are you to be called instructors, for you have one Instructor, the Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:19; Galatians 5:13; John 13:14–15"> The greatest among you shall be your servant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; James 4:6; Proverbs 29:23"> For whoever exalts himself will be humbled, and whoever humbles himself will be exalted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Matthew 23:29; Matthew 23:23"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You defraud widows of their houses, and for a show make lengthy prayers. Therefore you will receive greater condemnation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You shut the kingdom of heaven in men’s faces. You yourselves do not enter, nor will you let in those who wish to enter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 2:3; Acts 13:10; Acts 13:43"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You traverse land and sea to win a single convert, and when he becomes one, you make him twice as much a son of hell as you are.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:14; Matthew 5:33–35; James 5:12"> Woe to you, blind guides! You say, ‘If anyone swears by the temple, it means nothing; but if anyone swears by the gold of the temple, he is bound by his oath.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Numbers 16:38–39; Psalm 94:8"> You blind fools! Which is greater: the gold, or the temple that makes it sacred?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And you say, ‘If anyone swears by the altar, it means nothing; but if anyone swears by the gift on it, he is bound by his oath.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:29"> You are foolish and blind! Which is greater: the gift, or the altar that makes it sacred?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So then, he who swears by the altar swears by it and by everything on it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 26:8; 1 Kings 8:13; 2 Chronicles 6:2"> And he who swears by the temple swears by it and by the One who dwells in it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 5:34; Psalm 11:4; Isaiah 66:1"> And he who swears by heaven swears by God’s throne and by the One who sits on it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Micah 6:8; Hosea 6:6"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You pay tithes of mint, dill, and cumin. But you have disregarded the weightier matters of the law: justice, mercy, and faithfulness. You should have practiced the latter, without neglecting the former.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 19:24; Matthew 27:6–8; Matthew 15:2–6"> You blind guides! You strain out a gnat but swallow a camel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:39–40; Matthew 15:19–20; Mark 7:4–13"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You clean the outside of the cup and dish, but inside they are full of greed and unrighteousness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; James 4:8; Hebrews 10:22"> Blind Pharisee! First clean the inside of the cup and dish, so that the outside may become clean as well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 11:44; Acts 23:3; Numbers 19:16"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You are like whitewashed tombs, which look beautiful on the outside but on the inside are full of dead men’s bones and every kind of impurity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Psalm 51:6; Matthew 23:5"> In the same way, on the outside you appear to be righteous, but on the inside you are full of hypocrisy and wickedness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 11:47–48; Acts 2:29"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You build tombs for the prophets and decorate the monuments of the righteous.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:30; Matthew 23:34–35; Matthew 21:35–36"> And you say, ‘If we had lived in the days of our fathers, we would not have been partners with them in shedding the blood of the prophets.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 7:51–52; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Joshua 24:22"> So you testify against yourselves that you are the sons of those who murdered the prophets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 15:16; Numbers 32:14; 1 Thessalonians 2:16"> Fill up, then, the measure of the sin of your fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:7; Matthew 12:34; Matthew 5:22"> You snakes! You brood of vipers! How will you escape the sentence of hell?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 10:16–17; Matthew 13:52; Colossians 1:28"> Because of this, I am sending you prophets and wise men and teachers. Some of them you will kill and crucify, and others you will flog in your synagogues and persecute from town to town.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20–22; Zechariah 1:1; Hebrews 11:4"> And so upon you will come all the righteous blood shed on earth, from the blood of righteous Abel to the blood of Zechariah son of Berechiah, whom you murdered between the temple and the altar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Matthew 24:34; Mark 13:30–31"> Truly I tell you, all these things will come upon this generation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 13:34–35; Ruth 2:12; Psalm 91:4"> O Jerusalem, Jerusalem, who kills the prophets and stones those sent to her, how often I have longed to gather your children together, as a hen gathers her chicks under her wings, but you were unwilling!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:5; Luke 13:35; Luke 21:24"> Look, your house is left to you desolate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalm 118:26; Matthew 21:9; Romans 11:25"> For I tell you that you will not see Me again until you say, ‘Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord.’”</span>
</div>
