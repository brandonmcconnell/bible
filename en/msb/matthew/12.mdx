<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:25; Mark 2:23–28; Luke 6:1–5"> At that time Jesus went through the grainfields on the Sabbath. His disciples were hungry and began to pick the heads of grain and eat them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:10; John 7:21–24; Luke 14:3"> When the Pharisees saw this, they said to Him, “Look, Your disciples are doing what is unlawful on the Sabbath.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:3–6; Luke 6:3; Mark 2:25–26"> Jesus replied, “Have you not read what David did when he and his companions were hungry?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–9; Exodus 25:30; Leviticus 8:31"> He entered the house of God, and he and his companions ate the consecrated bread, which was not lawful for them to eat, but only for the priests.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 28:9–10; John 7:22–23; Nehemiah 13:17"> Or haven’t you read in the Law that on the Sabbath the priests in the temple break the Sabbath and yet are innocent?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 12:41–42; 2 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Peter 2:4–5"> But I tell you that One greater than the temple is here.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Matthew 9:13; Micah 6:6–8"> If only you had known the meaning of ‘I desire mercy, not sacrifice,’ you would not have condemned the innocent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 2:28; Luke 6:5; John 5:17–23"> For the Son of Man is Lord of the Sabbath.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 3:1–6; Luke 6:6–11"> Moving on from there, Jesus entered their synagogue,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 13:14; John 9:16; John 8:6"> and a man with a withered hand was there. In order to accuse Jesus, they asked Him, “Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:4; Luke 14:5; Luke 13:15–17"> He replied, “If one of you has a sheep and it falls into a pit on the Sabbath, will he not take hold of it and lift it out?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 6:26; Luke 6:9; Mark 3:4"> How much more valuable is a man than a sheep! Therefore it is lawful to do good on the Sabbath.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 13:13; Acts 3:7–8"> Then Jesus said to the man, “Stretch out your hand.” So he stretched it out, and it was restored to full use, just like the other.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 11:53; John 10:39; Mark 3:6"> But the Pharisees went out and plotted how they might kill Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 3:7–12; Matthew 10:23; Matthew 19:2"> Aware of this, Jesus withdrew from that place. Large crowds followed Him, and He healed them all,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Matthew 17:9"> warning them not to make Him known.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1–4; Isaiah 42:9; Matthew 13:35"> This was to fulfill what was spoken through the prophet Isaiah:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Luke 4:18; Acts 10:38"> “Here is My Servant, whom I have chosen, My beloved, in whom My soul delights. I will put My Spirit on Him, and He will proclaim justice to the nations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; John 18:36–38; 2 Timothy 2:24–25"> He will not quarrel or cry out; no one will hear His voice in the streets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 42:3–4; Ezekiel 34:16; Isaiah 61:1–3"> A bruised reed He will not break, and a smoldering wick He will not extinguish, till He leads justice to victory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 11:10; Romans 15:12–13; Isaiah 42:4"> In His name the nations will put their hope.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 11:14–15; Mark 7:35–37; Isaiah 29:18"> Then a demon-possessed man who was blind and mute was brought to Jesus, and He healed the man so that the blind and mute man could both speak and see.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; John 4:29; Matthew 22:42–43"> The crowds were astounded and asked, “Could this be the Son of David?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Matthew 9:34; Luke 11:15"> But when the Pharisees heard this, they said, “Only by Beelzebul, the prince of demons, does this man drive out demons.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Galatians 5:15; Jeremiah 17:10; Matthew 9:4"> Knowing their thoughts, Jesus said to them, “Every kingdom divided against itself will be laid waste, and every city or household divided against itself will not stand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; Revelation 20:2–3; 1 John 5:19"> If Satan drives out Satan, he is divided against himself. How then can his kingdom stand?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 19:13–16; Mark 9:38–39; Luke 9:49–50"> And if I drive out demons by Beelzebul, by whom do your sons drive them out? So then, they will be your judges.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 11:20; Daniel 7:14; Matthew 6:33"> But if I drive out demons by the Spirit of God, then the kingdom of God has come upon you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24; Mark 3:27; Luke 11:21–22"> Or again, how can anyone enter a strong man’s house and steal his possessions, unless he first ties up the strong man? Then he can plunder his house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 9:40; Luke 11:23; Joshua 24:15"> He who is not with Me is against Me, and he who does not gather with Me scatters.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Mark 3:28–30; Hebrews 10:29"> Therefore I tell you, every sin and blasphemy will be forgiven men, but the blasphemy against the Spirit will not be forgiven men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 3:29; Hebrews 10:26–29; Hebrews 6:4–6"> Whoever speaks a word against the Son of Man will be forgiven, but whoever speaks against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven, either in the present age or in the one to come.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 6:43–44; Matthew 7:16–20; James 4:8"> Make a tree good and its fruit will be good, or make a tree bad and its fruit will be bad, for a tree is known by its fruit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 6:45; Matthew 15:18; Ephesians 4:29"> You brood of vipers, how can you who are evil say anything good? For out of the overflow of the heart the mouth speaks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 15:4; Colossians 4:6; Proverbs 15:28"> The good man brings good things out of his good store of treasure, and the evil man brings evil things out of his evil store of treasure.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Romans 2:16; Revelation 20:12"> But I tell you that men will give an account on the day of judgment for every careless word they have spoken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Proverbs 13:3; James 2:21–25"> For by your words you will be acquitted, and by your words you will be condemned.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:22; Mark 8:11–12; Luke 11:16"> Then some of the scribes and Pharisees said, “Teacher, we want to see a sign from You.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 16:4; Luke 11:29–32; Mark 8:38"> Jesus replied, “A wicked and adulterous generation demands a sign, but none will be given it except the sign of the prophet Jonah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Jonah 1:17; Matthew 27:63–64; Matthew 16:21"> For as Jonah was three days and three nights in the belly of the great fish, so the Son of Man will be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; Hebrews 11:7; Matthew 12:6"> The men of Nineveh will stand at the judgment with this generation and condemn it; for they repented at the preaching of Jonah, and now One greater than Jonah is here.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Kings 10:1–13; 2 Chronicles 9:1–12; Hebrews 1:2–4"> The Queen of the South will rise at the judgment with this generation and condemn it; for she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and now One greater than Solomon is here.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 11:24–26; Luke 8:28–32; Matthew 8:29"> When an unclean spirit comes out of a man, it passes through arid places seeking rest and does not find it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 John 4:4; Revelation 13:8–9; Jude 1:4–5"> Then it says, ‘I will return to the house I left.’ On its return, it finds the house vacant, swept clean, and put in order.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 11:26; Mark 5:9; Hebrews 6:4–8"> Then it goes and brings with it seven other spirits more wicked than itself, and they go in and dwell there. And the final plight of that man is worse than the first. So will it be with this wicked generation.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 13:55; John 2:12; John 7:3"> While Jesus was still speaking to the crowds, His mother and brothers stood outside, wanting to speak to Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> Someone told Him, “Look, Your mother and brothers are standing outside, wanting to speak to You.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Mark 3:32–33; Matthew 10:37; Luke 2:52"> But Jesus replied, “Who is My mother, and who are My brothers?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 3:34; John 17:8–9; John 20:17–20"> Pointing to His disciples, He said, “Here are My mother and My brothers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Luke 8:21; Mark 3:35; John 15:14"> For whoever does the will of My Father in heaven is My brother and sister and mother.”</span>
</div>
