<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:10–17; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 6:30–44"> When Jesus heard about John, He withdrew by boat privately to a solitary place. But the crowds found out about it and followed Him on foot from the towns.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:8; John 6:19; Mark 6:48"> During the fourth watch of the night, Jesus went out to them, walking on the sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 6:49–50; Revelation 1:17; Luke 1:11–12"> When the disciples saw Him walking on the sea, they were terrified. “It’s a ghost!” they said, and cried out in fear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Revelation 1:17–18; John 16:33"> But Jesus spoke up at once: “Take courage! It is I. Do not be afraid.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:68; Romans 12:3; Matthew 26:33–35"> “Lord, if it is You,” Peter replied, “command me to come to You on the water.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Mark 9:23; Acts 3:16"> “Come,” said Jesus. Then Peter got down out of the boat and walked on the water to go toward Jesus.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 3:54–57; 2 Timothy 4:16–17; Mark 14:38"> But when he saw the strength of the wind, he was afraid and, beginning to sink, cried out, “Lord, save me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:6–8; Psalm 138:7; Genesis 22:14"> Immediately Jesus reached out His hand and took hold of Peter. “You of little faith,” He said, “why did you doubt?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalm 107:29–30; Mark 4:41; John 6:21"> And when they had climbed back into the boat, the wind died down.</span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:27; Psalm 2:7; Romans 1:4"> Then those who were in the boat came and worshiped Him, saying, “Truly You are the Son of God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 6:53–56; Luke 5:1; John 6:24–25"> When they had crossed over, they came to the land of Gennesaret.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 4:24–25; Mark 6:55; Mark 1:28–34"> And when the men of that place recognized Jesus, they sent word to all the surrounding region. People brought all the sick to Him</span>
