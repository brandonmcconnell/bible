<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 8:18; Matthew 4:25–1; Luke 5:15"> When Jesus came down from the mountain, large crowds followed Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 15:25; Luke 17:12–19; Matthew 18:26"> Suddenly a leper came and knelt before Him, saying, “Lord, if You are willing, You can make me clean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 5:13; Psalm 33:9; 2 Kings 5:14"> Jesus reached out His hand and touched the man. “I am willing,” He said. “Be clean!” And immediately his leprosy was cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 14:2–32; Luke 5:14; Luke 17:14"> Then Jesus instructed him, “See that you don’t tell anyone. But go, show yourself to the priest and offer the gift prescribed by Moses, as a testimony to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:1–10; Acts 27:43; Acts 10:1–33"> When Jesus had entered Capernaum, a centurion came and pleaded with Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 4:1; Matthew 4:24; Acts 9:33"> “Lord, my servant lies at home, paralyzed and in terrible agony.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 9:18–19; Luke 7:6; Mark 5:23–24"> “I will go and heal him,” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 107:20; Luke 7:6–7; Psalm 33:9"> The centurion answered, “Lord, I am not worthy to have You come under my roof. But just say the word, and my servant will be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 4:35–36; Titus 2:9; Colossians 3:22"> For I myself am a man under authority, with soldiers under me. I tell one to go, and he goes, and another to come, and he comes. I tell my servant to do something, and he does it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Luke 7:9; Mark 6:6"> When Jesus heard this, He marveled and said to those following Him, “Truly I tell you, I have not found even in Israel such great faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 13:28–29; Ephesians 3:6; Galatians 3:28–29"> I say to you that many will come from the east and the west to share the banquet with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:30; Luke 13:28; Matthew 21:43"> But the sons of the kingdom will be thrown into the outer darkness, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:29–30; Mark 9:23; John 4:50"> Then Jesus said to the centurion, “Go! As you have believed, so will it be done for you.” And his servant was healed at that very hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 4:38–41; Mark 1:29–34; 1 Corinthians 9:5"> When Jesus arrived at Peter’s house, He saw Peter’s mother-in-law sick in bed with a fever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 13:21; Luke 4:38–39; Luke 8:54"> So He touched her hand, and the fever left her, and she got up and began to serve Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Matthew 14:14; Mark 1:32–34"> When evening came, many who were demon-possessed were brought to Jesus, and He drove out the spirits with a word and healed all the sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4; 1 Peter 2:24; Matthew 1:22"> This was to fulfill what was spoken through the prophet Isaiah: “He took up our infirmities and carried our diseases.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 4:42–43; Luke 8:22; Mark 4:35"> When Jesus saw large crowds around Him, He gave orders to cross to the other side of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 22:33–34; John 13:36–38; Luke 14:25–27"> And one of the scribes came to Him and said, “Teacher, I will follow You wherever You go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 84:3; Luke 2:7; Isaiah 53:2–3"> Jesus replied, “Foxes have dens and birds of the air have nests, but the Son of Man has no place to lay His head.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 9:59–62; Leviticus 21:11–12; Numbers 6:6–7"> Another of His disciples requested, “Lord, first let me go and bury my father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 2:13; Luke 15:32; Ephesians 5:14"> But Jesus told him, “Follow Me, and let the dead bury their own dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 8:22–25; Mark 4:36–41; Matthew 9:1"> When He got into the boat, His disciples followed Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 8:23; Psalm 107:23–27; Mark 4:37–38"> Suddenly a violent storm came up on the sea, so that the boat was engulfed by the waves. But Jesus was sleeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jonah 1:6; 2 Chronicles 14:11; Mark 4:38–39"> Then the disciples went and woke Him, saying, “Lord, save us! We are perishing!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10–14; Psalm 65:7; Psalm 107:28–30"> “You of little faith,” Jesus replied, “why are you so afraid?” Then He got up and rebuked the winds and the sea, and it was perfectly calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 7:37; Mark 6:51; Matthew 14:33"> The men were amazed and asked, “What kind of man is this? Even the winds and the sea obey Him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 8:26–39; Mark 5:1–20; Acts 10:38"> When Jesus arrived on the other side in the region of the Gergesenes, He was met by two demon-possessed men coming from the tombs. They were so violent that no one could pass that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 1:24; Luke 4:34; James 2:19"> “What do You want with us, Jesus, Son of God?” they shouted. “Have You come here to torture us before the appointed time?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:8; Leviticus 11:7; Mark 5:11"> In the distance a large herd of pigs was feeding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 20:1–2; Luke 8:30–33; Mark 5:12"> So the demons begged Jesus, “If You drive us out, permit us to go off into the herd of pigs.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 5:13; Acts 2:23; Revelation 20:7"> “Go!” He told them. So they came out and went into the herd of pigs, and the whole herd of pigs rushed down the steep bank into the sea and died in the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:34–36; Mark 5:14–16; Acts 19:15–17"> Those tending the pigs ran off into the town and reported all this, including the account of the demon-possessed men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 16:39; Luke 8:37–39; Luke 5:8"> Then the whole town went out to meet Jesus. And when they saw Him, they begged Him to leave their region.</span>
