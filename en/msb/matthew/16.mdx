<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 11:16; Matthew 16:6; Acts 4:1"> Then the Pharisees and Sadducees came and tested Jesus by asking Him to show them a sign from heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:54–56"> But He replied, “When evening comes, you say, ‘The weather will be fair, for the sky is red,’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 12:56; 1 Chronicles 12:32; Matthew 11:5"> and in the morning, ‘Today it will be stormy, for the sky is red and overcast.’ You hypocrites! You know how to interpret the appearance of the sky, but not the signs of the times.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 11:29–30; Matthew 12:39–40; Jonah 1:17"> A wicked and adulterous generation demands a sign, but none will be given it except the sign of the prophet Jonah.” Then He left them and went away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 8:13–14; Matthew 15:39"> When they crossed to the other side, His disciples forgot to take bread.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:1; Galatians 5:9; Matthew 16:12"> “Watch out!” Jesus told them. “Beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 8:16–18; Matthew 15:16–18; Acts 10:14"> They discussed this among themselves and concluded, “It is because we did not bring any bread.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:30; Matthew 14:31; Matthew 8:26"> Aware of their conversation, Jesus said to them, “You of little faith, why are you debating among yourselves about having brought no bread?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 14:17–21; John 6:9–13; Luke 9:13–17"> Do you still not understand? Do you not remember the five loaves for the five thousand, and how many basketfuls you gathered?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 15:34–38; Mark 8:17–21; Mark 8:5–9"> Or the seven loaves for the four thousand, and how many basketfuls you gathered?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 16:6; Luke 12:56; Mark 8:21"> How do you not understand that I was not telling you about bread, but to beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:20; Acts 23:8; Matthew 15:4–9"> Then they understood that He was not telling them to beware of the leaven used in bread, but of the teaching of the Pharisees and Sadducees.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:18–20; Mark 8:27–29; Daniel 7:13"> When Jesus came to the region of Caesarea Philippi, He questioned His disciples: “Who do people pronounce Me, the Son of Man, to be?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 6:15; Matthew 14:2; Malachi 4:5"> They replied, “Some say John the Baptist; others say Elijah; and still others, Jeremiah or one of the prophets.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 9:20; Matthew 13:11; Mark 8:29"> “But what about you?” Jesus asked. “Who do you say I am?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; John 11:27; 1 John 4:15"> Simon Peter answered, “You are the Christ, the Son of the living God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 6:45; Ephesians 1:17–18; Matthew 11:25–27"> Jesus replied, “Blessed are you, Simon son of Jonah! For this was not revealed to you by flesh and blood, but by My Father in heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 2:19–22; 1 Corinthians 3:9–11; Isaiah 28:16"> And I tell you that you are Peter, and on this rock I will build My church, and the gates of Hades will not prevail against it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 18:18; Isaiah 22:22; Revelation 1:18"> I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven. Whatever you bind on earth will be bound in heaven, and whatever you loose on earth will be loosed in heaven.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 8:30; Luke 9:21; John 1:41"> Then He commanded His disciples not to tell anyone that He was Jesus the Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 24:6–7; Matthew 20:17–19; Matthew 17:22–23"> From that time on Jesus began to show His disciples that He must go to Jerusalem and suffer many things at the hands of the elders, chief priests, and scribes, and that He must be killed and on the third day be raised to life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 8:32; John 13:6–8; Matthew 16:16–17"> Peter took Him aside and began to rebuke Him. “Far be it from You, Lord!” he said. “This shall never happen to You!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 8:5–8; Colossians 3:2; Philippians 3:19"> But Jesus turned and said to Peter, “Get behind Me, Satan! You are a stumbling block to Me. For you do not have in mind the things of God, but the things of men.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 14:27; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> Then Jesus told His disciples, “If anyone wants to come after Me, he must deny himself and take up his cross and follow Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 10:39; Mark 8:35; Revelation 12:11"> For whoever wants to save his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life for My sake will find it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 8:36–37; Job 27:8; Luke 9:25"> What does it profit a man if he gains the whole world, yet forfeits his soul? Or what can a man give in exchange for his soul?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Psalm 62:12; Romans 2:6"> For the Son of Man will come in His Father’s glory with His angels, and then He will repay each one according to what he has done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 9:27; Mark 9:1; John 8:52"> Truly I tell you, some who are standing here will not taste death before they see the Son of Man coming in His kingdom.”</span>
</div>
</div>
