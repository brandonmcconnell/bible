<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 2:16; Judges 3:9; Joshua 15:48"> After the time of Abimelech, a man of Issachar, Tola son of Puah, the son of Dodo, rose up to save Israel. He lived in Shamir, in the hill country of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Tola judged Israel twenty-three years, and when he died, he was buried in Shamir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 32:29; Genesis 31:48"> Tola was followed by Jair the Gileadite, who judged Israel twenty-two years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 32:41; Judges 5:10; Deuteronomy 3:14"> He had thirty sons who rode on thirty donkeys. And they had thirty towns in the land of Gilead, which to this day are called Havvoth-jair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> When Jair died, he was buried in Kamon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 11:33; 1 Kings 11:7; 2 Kings 23:13"> And again the Israelites did evil in the sight of the LORD. They served the Baals, the Ashtoreths, the gods of Aram, Sidon, and Moab, and the gods of the Ammonites and Philistines. Thus they forsook the LORD and did not serve Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Deuteronomy 32:16–22; 1 Samuel 12:9–10"> So the anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and He sold them into the hands of the Philistines and Ammonites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Isaiah 30:13; Judges 10:5"> who that very year harassed and oppressed the Israelites, and they did so for eighteen years to all the Israelites on the other side of the Jordan in Gilead, the land of the Amorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:1–2; Deuteronomy 28:65; 2 Chronicles 15:5"> The Ammonites also crossed the Jordan to fight against Judah, Benjamin, and the house of Ephraim, and Israel was in deep distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:10; Psalm 107:28; Psalm 107:13"> Then the Israelites cried out to the LORD, saying, “We have sinned against You, for we have indeed forsaken our God and served the Baals.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 3:31; Numbers 21:21–25; Exodus 14:30"> The LORD replied, “When the Egyptians, Amorites, Ammonites, Philistines,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 6:3; 2 Chronicles 26:6–7; Psalm 106:42–43"> Sidonians, Amalekites, and Maonites oppressed you and you cried out to Me, did I not save you from their hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:13; 1 Chronicles 28:9; Judges 2:12"> But you have forsaken Me and served other gods, so I will no longer save you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Deuteronomy 32:37–38; 1 Kings 18:27–28"> Go and cry out to the gods you have chosen. Let them save you in your time of trouble.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:18; 2 Samuel 15:26; Proverbs 28:13"> “We have sinned,” the Israelites said to the LORD. “Deal with us as You see fit; but please deliver us today!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 63:9; Psalm 106:44–45; Ezekiel 18:30–32"> So they put away the foreign gods from among them and served the LORD, and He could no longer bear the misery of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 11:29; Genesis 31:49; Judges 11:11"> Then the Ammonites were called to arms and camped in Gilead, and the Israelites assembled and camped at Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 11:11; Judges 12:7; Judges 1:1"> And the rulers of Gilead said to one another, “Whoever will launch the attack against the Ammonites will be the head of all who live in Gilead.”</span>
