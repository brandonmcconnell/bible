<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 2:21–22; Deuteronomy 7:22; Proverbs 17:3"> These are the nations that the LORD left to test all the Israelites who had not known any of the wars in Canaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 3:5; Genesis 3:7; 1 Corinthians 9:26–27"> if only to teach warfare to the subsequent generations of Israel, especially to those who had not known it formerly:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 13:3; Joshua 13:5; Judges 10:7"> the five rulers of the Philistines, all the Canaanites, the Sidonians, and the Hivites who lived in the mountains of Lebanon from Mount Baal-hermon to Lebo-hamath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 3:1; Judges 2:22; Exodus 15:25"> These nations were left to test the Israelites, to find out whether they would keep the commandments of the LORD, which He had given their fathers through Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 106:34–38; Genesis 10:15–18; Exodus 3:8"> Thus the Israelites continued to live among the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3–4; Exodus 34:16; 1 Kings 11:1–5"> And they took the daughters of these people in marriage, gave their own daughters to their sons, and served their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 34:13; 2 Kings 23:6; 1 Kings 18:19"> So the Israelites did evil in the sight of the LORD; they forgot the LORD their God and served the Baals and the Asherahs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Habakkuk 3:7; Judges 4:9"> Then the anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and He sold them into the hand of Cushan-rishathaim king of Aram-naharaim, and the Israelites served him eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 3:15; Judges 1:13; Psalm 22:5"> But when the Israelites cried out to the LORD, He raised up Othniel son of Caleb’s younger brother Kenaz as a deliverer to save them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 13:25; 1 Samuel 11:6; Judges 14:19"> The Spirit of the LORD came upon him, and he became Israel’s judge and went out to war. And the LORD delivered Cushan-rishathaim king of Aram into the hand of Othniel, who prevailed against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Judges 8:28; Joshua 11:23"> So the land had rest for forty years, until Othniel son of Kenaz died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:9; Judges 2:19; Judges 2:14"> Once again the Israelites did evil in the sight of the LORD. So He gave Eglon king of Moab power over Israel, because they had done evil in the sight of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Deuteronomy 34:3; Judges 5:14"> After enlisting the Ammonites and Amalekites to join forces with him, Eglon attacked and defeated Israel, taking possession of the City of Palms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:47–48; Deuteronomy 28:40; Leviticus 26:23–25"> The Israelites served Eglon king of Moab eighteen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 3:9; Psalm 78:34; Isaiah 36:16"> And again they cried out to the LORD, and He raised up Ehud son of Gera, a left-handed Benjamite, as their deliverer. So they sent him with tribute to Eglon king of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Song of Solomon 3:8; Revelation 2:12; Psalm 149:6"> Now Ehud had made for himself a double-edged sword a cubit long. He strapped it to his right thigh under his cloak</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:20; Job 15:27; Jeremiah 50:11"> and brought the tribute to Eglon king of Moab, who was an obese man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> After Ehud had finished presenting the tribute, he ushered out those who had carried it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 4:20; Judges 3:20; Acts 23:18–19"> But upon reaching the idols near Gilgal, he himself turned back and said, “I have a secret message for you, O king.” “Silence,” said the king, and all his attendants left him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:7; Amos 3:15; Psalm 29:1"> Then Ehud approached him while he was sitting alone in the coolness of his upper room. “I have a word from God for you,” Ehud said, and the king rose from his seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 13:3; Job 20:25; 1 Samuel 15:33"> And Ehud reached with his left hand, pulled the sword from his right thigh, and plunged it into Eglon’s belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Even the handle sank in after the blade, and Eglon’s fat closed in over it, so that Ehud did not withdraw the sword from his belly. And Eglon’s bowels emptied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Then Ehud went out through the porch, closing and locking the doors of the upper room behind him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:3"> After Ehud was gone, Eglon’s servants came in and found the doors of the upper room locked. “He must be relieving himself in the cool room,” they said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> So they waited until they became worried and saw that he had still not opened the doors of the upper room. Then they took the key and opened the doors—and there was their lord lying dead on the floor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Ehud, however, had escaped while the servants waited. He passed by the idols and escaped to Seirah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 6:34; 1 Samuel 13:3; Judges 5:14"> On arriving in Seirah, he blew the ram’s horn throughout the hill country of Ephraim. The Israelites came down with him from the hills, and he became their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 12:5; Judges 7:9; Judges 7:15"> “Follow me,” he told them, “for the LORD has delivered your enemies the Moabites into your hand.” So they followed him down and seized the fords of the Jordan leading to Moab, and they did not allow anyone to cross over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Judges 3:17; Psalm 17:10"> At that time they struck down about ten thousand Moabites, all robust and valiant men. Not one of them escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 3:11; Judges 5:31"> So Moab was subdued under the hand of Israel that day, and the land had rest for eighty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 5:6; 1 Corinthians 1:17; 1 Samuel 17:50"> After Ehud came Shamgar son of Anath. And he too saved Israel, striking down six hundred Philistines with an oxgoad.</span>
