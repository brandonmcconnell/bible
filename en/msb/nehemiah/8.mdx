<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 7:6; Nehemiah 3:26; 2 Chronicles 34:15"> At that time all the people gathered together in the square before the Water Gate, and they asked Ezra the scribe to bring out the Book of the Law of Moses, which the LORD had commanded for Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:24; Malachi 2:7; Numbers 29:1"> On the first day of the seventh month, Ezra the priest brought the Law before the assembly of men and women and all who could listen and understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:22; Revelation 2:29; Hebrews 2:1–3"> So Ezra read it aloud from daybreak until noon as he faced the square before the Water Gate, in front of the men and women and those who could understand. And all the people listened attentively to the Book of the Law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezra 10:29; Nehemiah 12:13; Nehemiah 10:20"> Ezra the scribe stood on a high wooden platform built for this occasion. At his right side stood Mattithiah, Shema, Anaiah, Uriah, Hilkiah, and Maaseiah, and at his left were Pedaiah, Mishael, Malchijah, Hashum, Hash-baddanah, Zechariah, and Meshullam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 3:20; 1 Kings 8:14; Luke 4:16–17"> Ezra opened the book in full view of all the people, since he was standing above them all, and as he opened it, all the people stood up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:8; Lamentations 3:41; Exodus 4:31"> Then Ezra blessed the LORD, the great God, and with their hands uplifted, all the people said, “Amen, Amen!” Then they bowed down and worshiped the LORD with their faces to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:4; Leviticus 10:11; Nehemiah 3:23"> The Levites—Jeshua, Bani, Sherebiah, Jamin, Akkub, Shabbethai, Hodiah, Maaseiah, Kelita, Azariah, Jozabad, Hanan, and Pelaiah—instructed the people in the Law as they stood in their places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 24:45; Acts 28:23; Luke 24:32"> So they read from the Book of the Law of God, explaining it and giving insight, so that the people could understand what was being read.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:65; Nehemiah 7:70; Deuteronomy 12:12"> Nehemiah the governor, Ezra the priest and scribe, and the Levites who were instructing the people said to all of them, “This day is holy to the LORD your God. Do not mourn or weep.” For all the people were weeping as they heard the words of the Law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 28:7–8; Proverbs 17:22; 2 Corinthians 12:8–9"> Then Nehemiah told them, “Go and eat what is rich, drink what is sweet, and send out portions to those who have nothing prepared, since today is holy to our Lord. Do not grieve, for the joy of the LORD is your strength.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 13:30"> And the Levites calmed all the people, saying, “Be still, since today is holy. Do not grieve.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 119:174; Psalm 119:111; Psalm 119:130"> Then all the people began to eat and drink, to send out portions, and to rejoice greatly, because they understood the words that had been made known to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 2:1–6; Proverbs 8:33–34; Proverbs 12:1"> On the second day of the month, the heads of all the families, along with the priests and Levites, gathered around Ezra the scribe to study the words of the Law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 23:34; Deuteronomy 16:13–15; Leviticus 23:40–43"> And they found written in the Law, which the LORD had commanded through Moses, that the Israelites were to dwell in booths during the feast of the seventh month,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40; Deuteronomy 16:16; Leviticus 23:4"> and that they should proclaim this message and spread it throughout their towns and in Jerusalem, saying, “Go out to the hill country and bring back branches of olive, wild olive, myrtle, palm, and other leafy trees, to make booths, as it is written.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:39; 2 Kings 14:13; Nehemiah 12:37"> And the people went out, brought back branches, and made booths on their own rooftops, in their courtyards, in the courts of the house of God, and in the squares by the Water Gate and by the Gate of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:13; Ezra 3:4; 2 Chronicles 7:8–10"> The whole assembly that had returned from exile made booths and lived in them. From the days of Joshua son of Nun until that day, the Israelites had not celebrated like this. And there was great rejoicing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 29:35; Leviticus 23:36; Deuteronomy 31:10–13"> Day after day, from the first day to the last, Ezra read from the Book of the Law of God. The Israelites kept the feast for seven days, and on the eighth day they held an assembly, according to the ordinance.</span>
