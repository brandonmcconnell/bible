<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; Nehemiah 2:10; Nehemiah 4:1"> When Sanballat, Tobiah, Geshem the Arab, and the rest of our enemies heard that I had rebuilt the wall and not a gap was left—though to that time I had not yet installed the doors in the gates—</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 37:12; 1 Chronicles 8:12; Nehemiah 11:35"> Sanballat and Geshem sent me this message: “Come, let us meet together in one of the villages on the plain of Ono.” But they were planning to harm me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 9:4; Matthew 10:16; 1 Timothy 4:15–16"> So I sent messengers to them, saying, “I am doing a great work and cannot come down. Why should the work stop while I leave it to go down to you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 16:15–20; Luke 18:5; Galatians 2:5"> Four times they sent me the same message, and each time I gave the same reply.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 6:11; 2 Corinthians 2:11; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> The fifth time, Sanballat sent me this same message by his young servant, who had in his hand an unsealed letter</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; Ezra 4:12; Romans 3:8"> that read: “It is reported among the nations—and Geshem agrees—that you and the Jews are plotting to revolt, and this is why you are building the wall. According to these reports, you are to become their king,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–26; 1 Kings 1:7; 1 Kings 1:18"> and you have even appointed prophets in Jerusalem to proclaim on your behalf: ‘There is a king in Judah.’ Soon these rumors will reach the ears of the king. So come, let us confer together.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 52:2; Job 13:4; Matthew 12:34"> Then I sent him this reply: “There is nothing to these rumors you are spreading; you are inventing them in your own mind.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 138:3; 1 Peter 5:10; Ephesians 3:16"> For they were all trying to frighten us, saying, “Their hands will be weakened in the work, and it will never be finished.” But now, my God, strengthen my hands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:5; Matthew 7:15; Psalm 37:12"> Later, I went to the house of Shemaiah son of Delaiah, the son of Mehetabel, who was confined to his house. He said: “Let us meet at the house of God inside the temple. Let us shut the temple doors because they are coming to kill you—by night they are coming to kill you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 11:1–2; Acts 21:13; Proverbs 28:1"> But I replied, “Should a man like me run away? Should one like me go into the temple to save his own life? I will not go!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:22; 1 Timothy 3:3; Ezekiel 13:7"> I realized that God had not sent him, but that he had uttered this prophecy against me because Tobiah and Sanballat had hired him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; 2 Corinthians 11:12; 2 Timothy 1:7"> He had been hired to intimidate me so that I would sin by doing as he suggested, so they could give me a bad name in order to discredit me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:29; 2 Timothy 4:14–15; Isaiah 9:14–15"> O my God, remember Tobiah and Sanballat for what they have done, and also Noadiah the prophetess and the other prophets who tried to intimidate me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 1:3; Daniel 9:25; Nehemiah 4:1–2"> So the wall was completed in fifty-two days, on the twenty-fifth of Elul.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 126:2; Joshua 5:1; Nehemiah 4:7"> When all our enemies heard about this, all the surrounding nations were afraid and disheartened, for they realized that this task had been accomplished by our God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Micah 7:1–6; Matthew 24:10–12; Nehemiah 3:5"> Also in those days, the nobles of Judah sent many letters to Tobiah, and Tobiah’s letters kept coming to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezra 2:5; Nehemiah 7:10; Nehemiah 3:4"> For many in Judah were bound by oath to him, since he was a son-in-law of Shecaniah son of Arah, and his son Jehohanan had married the daughter of Meshullam son of Berechiah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 4:18–21; Nehemiah 6:9; John 7:7"> Moreover, these nobles kept reporting to me Tobiah’s good deeds, and they relayed my words to him. And Tobiah sent letters to intimidate me.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
