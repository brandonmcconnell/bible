<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:3–5; Nehemiah 9:3; Nehemiah 13:23"> At that time the Book of Moses was read aloud in the hearing of the people, and in it they found the passage stating that no Ammonite or Moabite should ever enter the assembly of God,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Joshua 24:9–10; Numbers 23:8–11"> because they had not met the Israelites with food and water, but had hired Balaam to call down a curse against them (although our God had turned the curse into a blessing).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:2; Exodus 12:38; Numbers 11:4"> As soon as the people heard this law, they excluded from Israel all of foreign descent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44; Nehemiah 6:17–18; Nehemiah 13:28"> Now before this, Eliashib the priest, a relative of Tobiah, had been put in charge of the storerooms of the house of our God</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44; 2 Chronicles 34:11; Numbers 18:21–24"> and had prepared for Tobiah a large room where they had previously stored the grain offerings, the frankincense, the temple articles, and the tithes of grain, new wine, and oil prescribed for the Levites, singers, and gatekeepers, along with the contributions for the priests.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:14; Nehemiah 2:5–6; Matthew 13:25"> While all this was happening, I was not in Jerusalem, because I had returned to Artaxerxes king of Babylon in the thirty-second year of his reign. Some time later I obtained leave from the king</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:5; Matthew 21:12–13; Lamentations 1:10"> to return to Jerusalem. Then I discovered the evil that Eliashib had done on behalf of Tobiah by providing him a room in the courts of the house of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 11:15–17; Ezra 10:1; Psalm 69:9"> And I was greatly displeased and threw all of Tobiah’s household goods out of the room.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:5; 2 Chronicles 29:15–19; Nehemiah 12:45"> Then I ordered that the rooms be purified, and I had the articles of the house of God restored to them, along with the grain offerings and frankincense.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:37; Malachi 3:8; Malachi 1:6–14"> I also learned that because the portions for the Levites had not been given to them, all the Levites and singers responsible for performing the service had gone back to their own fields.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:39; Nehemiah 13:17; Nehemiah 13:25"> So I rebuked the officials and asked, “Why has the house of God been neglected?” Then I gathered the Levites and singers together and stationed them at their posts,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:37–39; Malachi 3:10; Nehemiah 12:44"> and all Judah brought a tenth of the grain, new wine, and oil into the storerooms.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 22:7; Nehemiah 7:2; Nehemiah 12:44"> I appointed as treasurers over the storerooms Shelemiah the priest, Zadok the scribe, and Pedaiah of the Levites, with Hanan son of Zaccur, the son of Mattaniah, to assist them, because they were considered trustworthy. They were responsible for distributing the supplies to their fellow Levites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 122:6–9; Nehemiah 13:22; Nehemiah 13:31"> Remember me for this, O my God, and do not blot out my deeds of loving devotion for the house of my God and for its services.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:31; Nehemiah 13:21; Exodus 34:21"> In those days I saw people in Judah treading winepresses on the Sabbath and bringing in grain and loading it on donkeys, along with wine, grapes, and figs. All kinds of goods were being brought into Jerusalem on the Sabbath day. So I warned them against selling food on that day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 23:12; Deuteronomy 5:14"> Additionally, men of Tyre who lived there were importing fish and all kinds of merchandise and selling them on the Sabbath to the people of Judah in Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:11; Nehemiah 13:25; Isaiah 1:10"> Then I rebuked the nobles of Judah and asked, “What is this evil you are doing—profaning the Sabbath day?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:21–23; Jeremiah 44:9; Numbers 32:14"> Did not your forefathers do the same things, so that our God brought all this disaster on us and on this city? And now you are rekindling His wrath against Israel by profaning the Sabbath!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 23:32; Exodus 31:14–17; Jeremiah 17:19–22"> When the evening shadows began to fall on the gates of Jerusalem, just before the Sabbath, I ordered that the gates be shut and not opened until after the Sabbath. I posted some of my servants at the gates so that no load could enter on the Sabbath day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Once or twice, the merchants and those who sell all kinds of goods camped outside Jerusalem,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:3–4; 1 Peter 2:14; Ezra 7:26"> but I warned them, “Why are you camping in front of the wall? If you do it again, I will lay hands on you.” From that time on, they did not return on the Sabbath.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:30; Nehemiah 13:14; Nehemiah 13:31"> Then I instructed the Levites to purify themselves and guard the gates in order to keep the Sabbath day holy. Remember me for this as well, O my God, and show me mercy according to Your abundant loving devotion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:1–2; Ezra 10:10; Nehemiah 10:30"> In those days I also saw Jews who had married women from Ashdod, Ammon, and Moab.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:9"> Half of their children spoke the language of Ashdod or of the other peoples, but could not speak the language of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3; Nehemiah 13:17; Nehemiah 10:29–30"> I rebuked them and called down curses on them. I beat some of these men and pulled out their hair. Then I made them take an oath before God and said, “You must not give your daughters in marriage to their sons or take their daughters as wives for your sons or for yourselves!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 11:1–8; 1 Kings 3:13; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> Did not King Solomon of Israel sin in matters like this? There was not a king like him among many nations, and he was loved by his God, who made him king over all Israel—yet foreign women drew him into sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezra 10:2; 1 Samuel 30:24"> Must we now hear that you too are doing all this terrible evil and acting unfaithfully against our God by marrying foreign women?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; Nehemiah 3:1; Nehemiah 12:22"> Even one of the sons of Jehoiada son of Eliashib the high priest had become a son-in-law to Sanballat the Horonite. Therefore I drove him away from me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:14; Numbers 25:12–13; Malachi 2:4–8"> Remember them, O my God, because they have defiled the priesthood and the covenant of the priesthood and of the Levites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:30; Nehemiah 12:1–26; 1 Chronicles 23:1–26"> Thus I purified the priests and Levites from everything foreign, and I assigned specific duties to each of the priests and Levites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:14; Nehemiah 13:22; Psalm 106:4"> I also arranged for contributions of wood at the appointed times, and for the firstfruits. Remember me, O my God, with favor.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
