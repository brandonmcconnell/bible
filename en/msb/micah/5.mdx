<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Lamentations 3:30; Job 16:10; Matthew 26:67"> Now, O daughter of troops, mobilize your troops; for a siege is laid against us! With a rod they will strike the cheek of the judge of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:6; John 7:42; Luke 1:31–33"> But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, who are small among the clans of Judah, out of you will come forth for Me One to be ruler over Israel—One whose origins are of old, from the days of eternity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:1–2; Romans 9:27–28; Micah 4:7"> Therefore Israel will be abandoned until she who is in labor has given birth; then the rest of His brothers will return to the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 40:10–11; Micah 7:14; Luke 1:32"> He will stand and shepherd His flock in the strength of the LORD, in the majestic name of the LORD His God. And they will dwell securely, for then His greatness will extend to the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 2:14; Zechariah 10:3; Ephesians 2:14–17"> And He will be our peace when Assyria invades our land and tramples our citadels. We will raise against it seven shepherds, even eight leaders of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 10:8–11; Isaiah 14:25; Isaiah 14:2"> And they will rule the land of Assyria with the sword, and the land of Nimrod with the blade drawn. So He will deliver us when Assyria invades our land and marches into our borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 5:3; Isaiah 44:3; Psalm 72:6"> Then the remnant of Jacob will be in the midst of many peoples like dew from the LORD, like showers on the grass, which do not wait for man or linger for mankind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 10:5; Micah 4:13; Zechariah 9:15"> Then the remnant of Jacob will be among the nations, in the midst of many peoples, like a lion among the beasts of the forest, like a young lion among flocks of sheep, which tramples and tears as it passes through, with no one to rescue them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Psalm 21:8; Isaiah 14:2–4"> Your hand will be lifted over your foes, and all your enemies will be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 9:10; Hosea 14:3; Psalm 20:7–8"> “In that day,” declares the LORD, “I will remove your horses from among you and wreck your chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 2:12–17; Ezekiel 38:11; Hosea 10:14"> I will remove the cities of your land and tear down all your strongholds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:10–12; Isaiah 8:19–20; Isaiah 2:20"> I will cut the sorceries from your hand, and you will have no fortune-tellers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Ezekiel 36:25; Hosea 14:8"> I will also cut off the carved images and sacred pillars from among you, so that you will no longer bow down to the work of your own hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 34:13"> I will root out the Asherah poles from your midst and demolish your cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 149:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Micah 5:8"> I will take vengeance in anger and wrath upon the nations that have not obeyed Me.”</span>
