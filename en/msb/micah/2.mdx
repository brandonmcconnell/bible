<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 36:4; Isaiah 32:7; Psalm 7:14–16"> Woe to those who devise iniquity and plot evil on their beds! At morning’s light they accomplish it because the power is in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:8; Jeremiah 22:17; Amos 8:4"> They covet fields and seize them; they take away houses. They deprive a man of his home, a fellow man of his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:3; Amos 5:13; Isaiah 2:11–12"> Therefore this is what the LORD says: “I am planning against this nation a disaster from which you cannot free your necks. Then you will not walk so proudly, for it will be a time of calamity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:6; Jeremiah 9:10; Jeremiah 9:17–21"> In that day they will take up a proverb against you and taunt you with this bitter lamentation: ‘We are utterly ruined! He has changed the portion of my people. How He has removed it from me! He has allotted our fields to traitors.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:8; Joshua 18:4; Joshua 18:10"> Therefore, you will have no one in the assembly of the LORD to divide the land by lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 2:12; Isaiah 30:10; Acts 5:40"> “Do not preach,” they preach. “Do not preach these things; disgrace will not overtake us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 84:11; Psalm 15:2; Jeremiah 15:16"> Should it be said, O house of Jacob, “Is the Spirit of the LORD impatient? Are these the things He does?” Do not My words bring good to him who walks uprightly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 120:6–7; 2 Samuel 20:19; Isaiah 9:21"> But of late My people have risen up like an enemy: You strip off the splendid robe from unsuspecting passersby like men returning from battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Ezekiel 39:21; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> You drive the women of My people from their pleasant homes. You take away My blessing from their children forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:9; Psalm 106:38; 2 Kings 17:6"> Arise and depart, for this is not your place of rest, because its defilement brings destruction—a grievous destruction!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Isaiah 30:10–11; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> If a man of wind were to come and say falsely, “I will preach to you of wine and strong drink,” he would be just the preacher for this people!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 4:6–7; Micah 7:18; Hosea 1:11"> I will surely gather all of you, O Jacob; I will collect the remnant of Israel. I will bring them together like sheep in a pen, like a flock in the midst of its pasture—a noisy throng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 52:12; Zechariah 9:14–15; Isaiah 45:1–2"> One who breaks open the way will go up before them; they will break through the gate, and go out by it. Their King will pass through before them, the LORD as their leader.</span>
