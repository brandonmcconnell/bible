</div>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:4; Psalm 118:8–9; Matthew 10:16"> Do not rely on a friend; do not trust in a companion. Seal the doors of your mouth from her who lies in your arms.</span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; Psalm 37:7; 1 John 5:14–15"> But as for me, I will look to the LORD; I will wait for the God of my salvation. My God will hear me.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:12; Isaiah 9:2; Psalm 27:1"> Do not gloat over me, my enemy! Though I have fallen, I will arise; though I sit in darkness, the LORD will be my light.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 37:6; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Samuel 24:15"> Because I have sinned against Him, I must endure the rage of the LORD, until He argues my case and executes justice for me. He will bring me into the light; I will see His righteousness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 10:5; Jeremiah 50:33–34; Psalm 42:10"> Then my enemy will see and will be covered with shame—she who said to me, “Where is the LORD your God?” My eyes will see her; at that time she will be trampled like mud in the streets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 9:11–15; Nehemiah 2:8; Nehemiah 2:17"> The day for rebuilding your walls will come—the day for extending your boundary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 19:23–25; Isaiah 11:16; Hosea 11:11"> On that day they will come to you from Assyria and the cities of Egypt, even from Egypt to the Euphrates, from sea to sea and mountain to mountain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10–11; Jeremiah 25:11; Micah 6:13"> Then the earth will become desolate because of its inhabitants, as the fruit of their deeds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 23:1–4; Micah 5:4; Psalm 100:3"> Shepherd with Your staff Your people, the flock of Your inheritance. They live alone in a woodland, surrounded by pastures. Let them graze in Bashan and Gilead, as in the days of old.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 78:12–72; Exodus 3:20; Psalm 68:22"> As in the days when you came out of Egypt, I will show My wonders.</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Psalm 72:9; Psalm 18:45"> They will lick the dust like a snake, like reptiles slithering on the ground. They will come trembling from their strongholds in the presence of the LORD our God; they will tremble in fear of You.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 44:22; Psalm 103:8–9"> Who is a God like You, who pardons iniquity and passes over the transgression of the remnant of His inheritance—who does not retain His anger forever, because He delights in loving devotion?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 38:17; Romans 6:14; Isaiah 43:25"> He will again have compassion on us; He will vanquish our iniquities. You will cast out all our sins into the depths of the sea.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–14; Luke 1:72–74; Deuteronomy 7:8"> You will show faithfulness to Jacob and loving devotion to Abraham, as You swore to our fathers from the days of old.</span>
