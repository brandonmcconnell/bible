<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Psalm 86:6; Psalm 61:1"> For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments. A Maskil of David. Listen to my prayer, O God, and do not ignore my plea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Psalm 66:19; Psalm 77:3"> Attend to me and answer me. I am restless in my complaint, and distraught</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:7–8; Psalm 27:12; Psalm 35:11"> at the voice of the enemy, at the pressure of the wicked. For they bring down disaster upon me and resent me in their anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 116:3; Mark 14:33–34; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> My heart pounds within me, and the terrors of death assail me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 119:120; Job 21:6; 2 Samuel 15:14"> Fear and trembling grip me, and horror has overwhelmed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 11:1; Revelation 12:14; Psalm 139:9"> I said, “Oh, that I had wings like a dove! I would fly away and find rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:2; 2 Samuel 17:21–22; Jeremiah 37:12"> How far away I would flee! In the wilderness I would remain. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 4:6; Matthew 7:25–27; Isaiah 17:12–13"> I would hurry to my shelter, far from this raging tempest.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:7; Genesis 11:7–9; Jeremiah 23:14"> O Lord, confuse and confound their speech, for I see violence and strife in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 59:14–15; Isaiah 59:6–15; Ezekiel 9:4"> Day and night they encircle the walls, while malice and trouble lie within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26–27; Psalm 10:7; Psalm 5:9"> Destruction is within; oppression and deceit never leave the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 41:9; John 13:18; Matthew 26:21–23"> For it is not an enemy who insults me; that I could endure. It is not a foe who rises against me; from him I could hide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:12; Micah 7:5; Jeremiah 9:4"> But it is you, a man like myself, my companion and close friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 42:4; Isaiah 2:3; Ezekiel 33:31"> We shared sweet fellowship together; we walked with the crowd into the house of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 59:13; Psalm 64:7; Psalm 9:17"> Let death seize them by surprise; let them go down to Sheol alive, for evil is with them in their homes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 50:15; Psalm 109:4; Psalm 73:28"> But I call to God, and the LORD saves me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; Psalm 141:2"> Morning, noon, and night, I cry out in distress, and He hears my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 57:3; Psalm 3:6–7; 2 Samuel 22:1"> He redeems my soul in peace from the battle waged against me, even though many oppose me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Revelation 6:10–11; Jeremiah 48:11"> God will hear and humiliate them—the One enthroned for the ages—Selah because they do not change and they have no fear of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 12:1; Psalm 89:34; Psalm 7:4"> My companion attacks his friends; he violates his covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 57:4; Psalm 28:3; Proverbs 5:3–4"> His speech is smooth as butter, but war is in his heart. His words are softer than oil, yet they are swords unsheathed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Psalm 37:5; Philippians 4:6–7"> Cast your burden upon the LORD and He will sustain you; He will never let the righteous be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 10:27; Psalm 5:6; Job 15:32"> But You, O God, will bring them down to the Pit of destruction; men of bloodshed and deceit will not live out half their days. But I will trust in You.</span>
