<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 43:1; Psalm 119:154; Jeremiah 51:36"> Of David. Contend with my opponents, O LORD; fight against those who fight against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 91:4; Exodus 15:3; Isaiah 42:13"> Take up Your shield and buckler; arise and come to my aid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalm 27:2; Psalm 51:12"> Draw the spear and javelin against my pursuers; say to my soul: “I am your salvation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 70:2–3; Jeremiah 46:5; Psalm 35:26"> May those who seek my life be disgraced and put to shame; may those who plan to harm me be driven back and confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 21:18; Isaiah 29:5; Isaiah 37:36"> May they be like chaff in the wind, as the angel of the LORD drives them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:12; Psalm 73:18; Jeremiah 13:16"> May their path be dark and slick, as the angel of the LORD pursues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:25; Psalm 140:5; Psalm 9:15"> For without cause they laid their net for me; without reason they dug a pit for my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 141:9–10; Proverbs 5:22; Luke 21:34"> May ruin befall them by surprise; may the net they hid ensnare them; may they fall into the hazard they created.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Psalm 58:10–11; Luke 1:46–47"> Then my soul will rejoice in the LORD and exult in His salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 140:12; Exodus 15:11; Psalm 51:8"> All my bones will exclaim, “Who is like You, O LORD, who delivers the afflicted from the aggressor, the poor and needy from the robber?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 27:12; Matthew 26:59–60; Acts 24:5–6"> Hostile witnesses come forward; they make charges I know nothing about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 10:32; Jeremiah 18:20; Psalm 38:20"> They repay me evil for good, to the bereavement of my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 69:10–11; Job 30:25; Matthew 5:44"> Yet when they were ill, I put on sackcloth; I humbled myself with fasting, but my prayers returned unanswered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:41–42; 2 Samuel 1:11–12; Genesis 24:67"> I paced about as for my friend or brother; I was bowed down with grief, like one mourning for his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Psalm 7:2; Psalm 35:25–26"> But when I stumbled, they assembled in glee; they gathered together against me. Assailants I did not know slandered me without ceasing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Job 16:9; Psalm 37:12"> Like godless jesters at a feast, they gnashed their teeth at me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:13; Psalm 22:20–21; Psalm 57:4"> How long, O Lord, will You look on? Rescue my soul from their ravages, my precious life from these lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:12; Psalm 22:22–25; Psalm 116:18"> Then I will give You thanks in the great assembly; I will praise You among many people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 69:4; John 15:25; Proverbs 6:13"> Let not my enemies gloat over me without cause, nor those who hate me without reason wink in malice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 120:5–7; Matthew 12:19; Acts 23:15"> For they do not speak peace, but they devise deceitful schemes against those who live quietly in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 22:13; Psalm 40:15; Psalm 70:3"> They gape at me and say, “Aha, aha! Our eyes have seen!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 3:7; Psalm 10:1; Psalm 28:1"> O LORD, You have seen it; be not silent. O Lord, be not far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 44:23; Psalm 7:6; Isaiah 51:9"> Awake and rise to my defense, to my cause, my God and my Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 43:1; Psalm 26:1; Psalm 7:8"> Vindicate me by Your righteousness, O LORD my God, and do not let them gloat over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Psalm 124:3; 2 Samuel 20:19"> Let them not say in their hearts, “Aha, just what we wanted!” Let them not say, “We have swallowed him up!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 38:16; Job 19:5; Psalm 132:18"> May those who gloat in my distress be ashamed and confounded; may those who exalt themselves over me be clothed in shame and reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:40–41; Psalm 149:4; Psalm 40:16"> May those who favor my vindication shout for joy and gladness; may they always say, “Exalted be the LORD who delights in His servant’s well-being.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalm 145:21; Psalm 71:24; Psalm 145:5"> Then my tongue will proclaim Your righteousness and Your praises all day long.</span>
</div>
