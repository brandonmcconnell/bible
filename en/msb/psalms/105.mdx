<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:10; Proverbs 21:1; Genesis 40:20–21"> until his prediction came true and the word of the LORD proved him right.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 41:14"> The king sent and released him; the ruler of peoples set him free.</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Genesis 41:33; Genesis 41:38"> to instruct his princes as he pleased and teach his elders wisdom.</span>

<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalm 78:47–48; Exodus 9:18–28; Revelation 8:7"> He gave them hail for rain, with lightning throughout their land.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalm 78:47; Revelation 9:4"> He struck their vines and fig trees and shattered the trees of their country.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 10:12–15; Psalm 78:46; Joel 1:4–7"> He spoke, and the locusts came—young locusts without number.</span>

<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> They devoured every plant in their land and consumed the produce of their soil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 49:3; Exodus 12:29–30; Psalm 78:51"> Then He struck all the firstborn in their land, the firstfruits of all their vigor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 12:35–36; Exodus 3:22; Acts 13:17"> He brought Israel out with silver and gold, and none among His tribes stumbled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 12:33; Exodus 15:16; Genesis 35:5"> Egypt was glad when they departed, for the dread of Israel had fallen on them.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalm 78:18; Numbers 11:31–33; Joshua 5:12"> They asked, and He brought quail and satisfied them with the bread of heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; 1 Corinthians 10:4; Exodus 17:6"> He opened a rock, and water gushed out; it flowed like a river in the desert.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 2:24; Luke 1:72–73; Genesis 12:7"> For He remembered His holy promise to Abraham His servant.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Isaiah 35:10; Acts 7:36; Jeremiah 31:11–12"> He brought forth His people with rejoicing, His chosen with shouts of joy.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:10–11; Joshua 24:13; Psalm 78:55"> He gave them the lands of the nations, that they might inherit the fruit of others’ labor,</span>
