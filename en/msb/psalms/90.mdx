<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Psalm 71:3; 1 John 4:16"> A prayer of Moses the man of God. Lord, You have been our dwelling place through all generations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 13:8; Genesis 1:1; Psalm 102:24–27"> Before the mountains were born or You brought forth the earth and the world, from everlasting to everlasting You are God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Psalm 146:4"> You return man to dust, saying, “Return, O sons of mortals.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 3:8; Psalm 39:5; Matthew 24:43"> For in Your sight a thousand years are but a day that passes, or a watch of the night.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6; Psalm 73:20; Job 22:16"> You sweep them away in their sleep; they are like the new grass of the morning—</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 14:2; Psalm 92:7; James 1:11"> in the morning it springs up new, but by evening it fades and withers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 39:11; Psalm 59:13; Exodus 14:24"> For we are consumed by Your anger and terrified by Your wrath.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:17; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Hebrews 4:12–13"> You have set our iniquities before You, our secret sins in the light of Your presence.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 78:33; Psalm 39:5; Psalm 90:4"> For all our days decline in Your fury; we finish our years with a sigh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalm 78:39; 2 Samuel 19:35"> The length of our days is seventy years—or eighty if we are strong—yet their pride is but labor and sorrow, for they quickly pass, and we fly away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 26:24; Isaiah 33:14; Nahum 1:6"> Who knows the power of Your anger? Your wrath matches the fear You are due.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:16–17; Psalm 39:4; John 9:4"> So teach us to number our days, that we may present a heart of wisdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 106:45; Psalm 135:14; Deuteronomy 32:36"> Return, O LORD! How long will it be? Have compassion on Your servants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 23:6; Psalm 86:4; Psalm 31:7"> Satisfy us in the morning with Your loving devotion, that we may sing for joy and be glad all our days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 126:5–6; Psalm 30:5; John 16:20"> Make us glad for as many days as You have afflicted us, for as many years as we have seen evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:2; Psalm 44:1; Deuteronomy 32:4"> May Your work be shown to Your servants, and Your splendor to their children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 16:3; 1 Corinthians 3:7; Job 22:28"> May the favor of the Lord our God rest upon us; establish for us the work of our hands—yes, establish the work of our hands!</span>
</div>
</div>
