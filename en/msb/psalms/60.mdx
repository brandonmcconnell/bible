<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:12–13; 2 Samuel 8:3; Psalm 44:9"> For the choirmaster. To the tune of “The Lily of the Covenant.” A Miktam of David for instruction. When he fought Aram-naharaim and Aram-zobah, and Joab returned and struck down 12,000 Edomites in the Valley of Salt. You have rejected us, O God; You have broken us; You have been angry; restore us!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; Hosea 6:1; Isaiah 30:26"> You have shaken the land and torn it open. Heal its fractures, for it is quaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 71:20; Isaiah 51:22; Isaiah 51:17"> You have shown Your people hardship; we are staggered from the wine You made us drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 20:5; Isaiah 11:12; Exodus 17:15"> You have raised a banner for those who fear You, that they may flee the bow. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 108:6–13; Psalm 17:7; Isaiah 41:10"> Respond and save us with Your right hand, that Your beloved may be delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 13:27; Psalm 89:35; Genesis 12:6"> God has spoken from His sanctuary: “I will triumph! I will parcel out Shechem and apportion the Valley of Succoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 49:10; Deuteronomy 33:17; Joshua 13:31"> Gilead is Mine, and Manasseh is Mine; Ephraim is My helmet, Judah is My scepter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; 2 Samuel 8:1–2; Psalm 108:9–10"> Moab is My washbasin; upon Edom I toss My sandal; over Philistia I shout in triumph.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:26–31; 2 Samuel 11:1; Judges 1:12"> Who will bring me to the fortified city? Who will lead me to Edom?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 108:11; Joshua 7:12; Psalm 60:1"> Have You not rejected us, O God? Will You no longer march out, O God, with our armies?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 146:3; Psalm 108:12; Psalm 130:8"> Give us aid against the enemy, for the help of man is worthless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 44:5; Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 63:3"> With God we will perform with valor, and He will trample our enemies.</span>
