<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Psalm 118:6; Exodus 15:2"> Of David. The LORD is my light and my salvation—whom shall I fear? The LORD is the stronghold of my life—whom shall I dread?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 118:12; Psalm 14:4; Psalm 9:3"> When the wicked came upon me to devour my flesh, my enemies and foes stumbled and fell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 3:6; 1 Peter 3:14; Romans 8:35–37"> Though an army encamps around me, my heart will not fear; though a war breaks out against me, I will keep my trust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 26:8; Psalm 23:6; Psalm 65:4"> One thing I have asked of the LORD; this is what I desire: to dwell in the house of the LORD all the days of my life, to gaze on the beauty of the LORD and seek Him in His temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalm 31:20; Psalm 138:7"> For in the day of trouble He will hide me in His shelter; He will conceal me under the cover of His tent; He will set me high upon a rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 3:3; Hebrews 13:15; Psalm 107:22"> Then my head will be held high above my enemies around me. At His tabernacle I will offer sacrifices with shouts of joy; I will sing and make music to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 130:2–4; Psalm 13:3; Psalm 5:2"> Hear, O LORD, my voice when I call; be merciful and answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 105:4; Jeremiah 29:12–13; Isaiah 55:6–7"> My heart said, “Seek His face.” Your face, O LORD, I will seek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 69:17; Psalm 143:7; Psalm 102:2"> Hide not Your face from me, nor turn away Your servant in anger. You have been my helper; do not leave me or forsake me, O God of my salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Isaiah 40:11; John 16:32"> Though my father and mother forsake me, the LORD will receive me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 25:4–5; Psalm 5:8; Psalm 86:11"> Teach me Your way, O LORD, and lead me on a level path, because of my oppressors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 31:8; Psalm 41:11; Matthew 26:59–60"> Do not hand me over to the will of my foes, for false witnesses rise up against me, breathing out violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 142:5; 2 Corinthians 4:16; 2 Corinthians 4:8–14"> Still I am certain to see the goodness of the LORD in the land of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Psalm 31:24; Isaiah 30:18"> Wait patiently for the LORD; be strong and courageous. Wait patiently for the LORD!</span>
