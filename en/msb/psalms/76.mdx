<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 46:9; Ezekiel 39:3–4; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> There He shattered the flaming arrows, the shield and sword and weapons of war. Selah</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nahum 3:18; Psalm 13:3; Isaiah 46:12"> The valiant lie plundered; they sleep their last sleep. No men of might could lift a hand.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Psalm 2:12; Psalm 89:7"> You alone are to be feared. When You are angry, who can stand before You?</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:4–6; Psalm 50:14; Psalm 68:29"> Make and fulfill your vows to the LORD your God; let all the neighboring lands bring tribute to Him who is to be feared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:6; 2 Chronicles 32:21; Psalm 68:12"> He breaks the spirits of princes; He is feared by the kings of the earth.</span>
