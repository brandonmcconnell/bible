<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:1–51; Colossians 1:11; 1 John 4:19"> For the choirmaster. Of David the servant of the LORD, who sang this song to the LORD on the day the LORD had delivered him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul. He said: I love You, O LORD, my strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 144:2; Psalm 62:7; Psalm 19:14"> The LORD is my rock, my fortress, and my deliverer. My God is my rock, in whom I take refuge, my shield, and the horn of my salvation, my stronghold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 91:15; Psalm 145:3; Psalm 62:8"> I will call upon the LORD, who is worthy to be praised; so shall I be saved from my enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 116:3; Matthew 27:24–25; Mark 14:33–34"> The cords of death encompassed me; the torrents of chaos overwhelmed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 86:13; Psalm 88:3–8; Acts 2:24"> The cords of Sheol entangled me; the snares of death confronted me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 50:15; 2 Samuel 22:7; Psalm 34:15"> In my distress I called upon the LORD; I cried to my God for help. From His temple He heard my voice, and my cry for His help reached His ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 4:31; Psalm 68:7–8; Acts 16:25–26"> Then the earth shook and quaked, and the foundations of the mountains trembled; they were shaken because He burned with anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 21:9; Psalm 11:6; Nahum 1:5–6"> Smoke rose from His nostrils, and consuming fire came from His mouth; glowing coals blazed forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 144:5–15; Deuteronomy 33:26; 2 Samuel 22:10"> He parted the heavens and came down with dark clouds beneath His feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 104:3; 2 Samuel 22:11–12; Psalm 99:1"> He mounted a cherub and flew; He soared on the wings of the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 97:2; Deuteronomy 4:11; Psalm 91:1"> He made darkness His hiding place, and storm clouds a canopy around Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; Revelation 16:21; Matthew 17:2"> From the brightness of His presence His clouds advanced—hailstones and coals of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 104:7; 1 Samuel 7:10; Psalm 140:10"> The LORD thundered from heaven; the voice of the Most High resounded—hailstones and coals of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 144:6; Habakkuk 3:11; Numbers 24:8"> He shot His arrows and scattered the foes; He hurled lightning and routed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 106:9; Exodus 15:8; Micah 6:2"> The channels of the sea appeared, and the foundations of the world were exposed, at Your rebuke, O LORD, at the blast of the breath of Your nostrils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:17; Psalm 144:7; Psalm 57:3"> He reached down from on high and took hold of me; He drew me out of deep waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14–15; Psalm 35:10; Psalm 142:6"> He rescued me from my powerful enemy, from foes too mighty for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Obadiah 1:10–14; Deuteronomy 32:35; Psalm 46:11"> They confronted me in my day of calamity, but the LORD was my support.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 118:5; Psalm 31:8; Psalm 18:36"> He brought me out into the open; He rescued me because He delighted in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 24:4; Matthew 6:4; Psalm 58:11"> The LORD has rewarded me according to my righteousness; He has repaid me according to the cleanness of my hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 119:102; Psalm 17:4; Psalm 26:1"> For I have kept the ways of the LORD and have not wickedly departed from my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 119:13; Psalm 119:112; Psalm 119:30"> For all His ordinances are before me; I have not disregarded His statutes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 18:8–9; Psalm 11:7; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> And I have been blameless before Him and kept myself from iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:41–42; Ruth 2:12; 1 Samuel 26:23"> So the LORD has repaid me according to my righteousness, according to the cleanness of my hands in His sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Psalm 62:12; Matthew 18:33–35"> To the faithful You show Yourself faithful, to the blameless You show Yourself blameless;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 3:34; Leviticus 26:23–24; James 2:13"> to the pure You show Yourself pure, but to the crooked You show Yourself shrewd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; James 2:5; Isaiah 57:15"> For You save an afflicted people, but You humble those with haughty eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:29; Job 29:3; Isaiah 42:16"> For You, O LORD, light my lamp; my God lights up my darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:30; Ephesians 6:10–13; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> For in You I can charge an army, and with my God I can scale a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Proverbs 30:5; 2 Samuel 22:31"> As for God, His way is perfect; the word of the LORD is flawless. He is a shield to all who take refuge in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:31; Psalm 86:8; 2 Samuel 22:32"> For who is God besides the LORD? And who is the Rock except our God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 45:5; Psalm 28:7; 2 Samuel 22:33"> It is God who arms me with strength and makes my way clear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:19; Deuteronomy 32:13; Deuteronomy 33:29"> He makes my feet like those of a deer and stations me upon the heights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalm 144:1; Psalm 46:9; 2 Samuel 22:36"> He trains my hands for battle; my arms can bend a bow of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:36; James 3:17–18; Psalm 28:7"> You have given me Your shield of salvation; Your right hand upholds me, and Your gentleness exalts me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; 2 Samuel 22:37; Job 36:16"> You broaden the path beneath me so that my ankles do not give way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 53:10–12; Psalm 44:5; Psalm 35:2"> I pursued my enemies and overtook them; I did not turn back until they were consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:1–25; 2 Samuel 22:39; 1 Samuel 23:5"> I crushed them so they could not rise; they have fallen under my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:40–41; Psalm 18:32; 1 Corinthians 15:25–28"> You have armed me with strength for battle; You have subdued my foes beneath me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalm 21:12; Psalm 94:23"> You have made my enemies retreat before me; I destroyed those who hated me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; Job 27:9; Job 35:12–13"> They cried for help, but there was no one to save them—to the LORD, but He did not answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 10:6; Zechariah 10:5; 2 Kings 13:7"> I ground them as dust in the face of the wind; I trampled them like mud in the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Isaiah 55:5; 2 Samuel 3:1; Isaiah 52:15"> You have delivered me from the strife of the people; You have made me the head of nations; a people I had not known shall serve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalm 66:3; Psalm 81:15; Deuteronomy 33:29"> When they hear me, they obey me; foreigners cower before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Micah 7:17; James 1:11; Isaiah 24:4"> Foreigners lose heart and come trembling from their strongholds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:47; Psalm 57:5; Psalm 18:2"> The LORD lives, and blessed be my Rock! And may the God of my salvation be exalted—</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalm 47:3; Romans 12:19; Nahum 1:2"> the God who avenges me and subdues nations beneath me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalm 140:1; Psalm 140:4; Psalm 86:14"> who delivers me from my enemies. You exalt me above my foes; You rescue me from violent men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Romans 15:9; 2 Samuel 22:50–51; Psalm 72:18–19"> Therefore I will praise You, O LORD, among the nations; I will sing praises to Your name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalm 144:10; Psalm 132:10; 2 Samuel 7:13"> Great salvation He brings to His king. He shows loving devotion to His anointed, to David and his descendants forever.</span>
