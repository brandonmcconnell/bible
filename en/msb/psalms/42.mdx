<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 37:7; Hebrews 10:36–37; Psalm 61:2"> Why are you downcast, O my soul? Why the unease within me? Put your hope in God, for I will yet praise Him for the salvation of His presence.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 38:6; Psalm 43:2; Psalm 18:2"> I say to God my Rock, “Why have You forgotten me? Why must I walk in sorrow because of the enemy’s oppression?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 42:3; Joel 2:17; Luke 2:35"> Like the crushing of my bones, my enemies taunt me, while they say to me all day long, “Where is your God?”</span>
