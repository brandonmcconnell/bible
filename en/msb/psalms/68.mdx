</div>

</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; Judges 11:34; Exodus 15:20"> The singers lead the way, the musicians follow after, among the maidens playing tambourines.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 48:1; Deuteronomy 33:28; Psalm 26:12"> Bless God in the great congregation; bless the LORD from the fountain of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:21; Judges 20:35; Judges 21:6–25"> There is Benjamin, the youngest, ruling them, the princes of Judah in their company, the princes of Zebulun and of Naphtali.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalm 138:8; Psalm 71:3; Psalm 42:8"> Summon Your power, O God; show Your strength, O God, which You have exerted on our behalf.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 10:10; Psalm 76:11; Isaiah 60:6–11"> Because of Your temple at Jerusalem kings will bring You gifts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalm 89:10; Psalm 22:12–13; Jeremiah 50:11"> Rebuke the beast in the reeds, the herd of bulls among the calves of the nations, until it submits, bringing bars of silver. Scatter the nations who delight in war.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 45:14; Zephaniah 3:10; Psalm 44:20"> Envoys will arrive from Egypt; Cush will stretch out her hands to God.</span>
</div>
