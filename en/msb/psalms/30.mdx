<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 25:2; Daniel 4:37; Psalm 35:19"> A Psalm. A song for the dedication of the temple. Of David. I will exalt You, O LORD, for You have lifted me up and have not allowed my foes to rejoice over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 103:3–4; 2 Kings 20:5; Psalm 147:3"> O LORD my God, I cried to You for help, and You healed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 86:13; Psalm 16:10; Jonah 2:4–6"> O LORD, You pulled me up from Sheol; You spared me from descending into the Pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 97:12; Revelation 4:8; 1 Chronicles 16:4"> Sing to the LORD, O you His saints, and praise His holy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 54:7–8; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Psalm 126:5–6"> For His anger is fleeting, but His favor lasts a lifetime. Weeping may stay the night, but joy comes in the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:19; 2 Corinthians 12:7; Psalm 119:117"> In prosperity I said, “I will never be shaken.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 104:29; Psalm 143:7; Psalm 30:5"> O LORD, You favored me; You made my mountain stand strong. When You hid Your face, I was dismayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:6–7; Psalm 34:6; Psalm 130:1–2"> To You, O LORD, I called, and I begged my Lord for mercy:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 6:5; Psalm 115:17–18; Isaiah 38:18"> “What gain is there in my bloodshed, in my descent to the Pit? Will the dust praise You? Will it proclaim Your faithfulness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 54:4; Psalm 143:1; Psalm 143:7–9"> Hear me, O LORD, and have mercy; O LORD, be my helper.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:4; Jeremiah 31:13–14; Ecclesiastes 3:4"> You turned my mourning into dancing; You peeled off my sackcloth and clothed me with joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 145:2; Psalm 146:1–2; Psalm 44:8"> that my heart may sing Your praises and not be silent. O LORD my God, I will give thanks forever.</span>
