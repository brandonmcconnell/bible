<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 113:3; Joshua 22:22; Matthew 25:32"> A Psalm of Asaph. The Mighty One, God the LORD, speaks and summons the earth from where the sun rises to where it sets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 48:2; Psalm 80:1; Revelation 21:23"> From Zion, perfect in beauty, God shines forth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 7:10; Numbers 16:35; Leviticus 10:2"> Our God approaches and will not be silent! Consuming fire precedes Him, and a tempest rages around Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Isaiah 1:2; Deuteronomy 31:28"> He summons the heavens above, and the earth, that He may judge His people:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 26:28; Exodus 24:3–8; Hebrews 13:20"> “Gather to Me My saints, who made a covenant with Me by sacrifice.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 97:6; Psalm 75:7; Psalm 89:5"> And the heavens proclaim His righteousness, for God Himself is Judge. Selah</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Psalm 81:8; Jeremiah 2:9"> “Hear, O My people, and I will speak, O Israel, and I will testify against you: I am God, your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–31; Hebrews 10:4–10; Hosea 6:6"> I do not rebuke you for your sacrifices, and your burnt offerings are ever before Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 6:6–8; Hebrews 10:4–6; Psalm 69:31"> I have no need for a bull from your stall or goats from your pens,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5–6; Psalm 8:6–8; Psalm 104:14"> for every beast of the forest is Mine—the cattle on a thousand hills.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–22; Matthew 6:26; Luke 12:24"> I know every bird in the mountains, and the creatures of the field are Mine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Deuteronomy 10:14; Psalm 24:1–2"> If I were hungry, I would not tell you, for the world is Mine, and the fullness thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Do I eat the flesh of bulls, or drink the blood of goats?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalm 56:12; Deuteronomy 23:21"> Sacrifice a thank offering to God, and fulfill your vows to the Most High.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 107:28; Psalm 91:15; Psalm 34:3–4"> Call upon Me in the day of trouble; I will deliver you, and you will honor Me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Psalm 78:36–38; Romans 2:17–24"> To the wicked, however, God says, “What right have you to recite My statutes and to bear My covenant on your lips?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26; Proverbs 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:3–4"> For you hate My instruction and cast My words behind you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 1:32; 1 Timothy 5:22; Hebrews 13:4"> When you see a thief, you befriend him, and throw in your lot with adulterers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 10:7; Psalm 52:2–4; Psalm 55:21"> You unleash your mouth for evil and harness your tongue to deceit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Proverbs 10:18; Leviticus 19:16"> You sit and malign your brother; you slander your own mother’s son.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:4–5; Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 57:11"> You have done these things, and I kept silent; you thought I was just like you. But now I rebuke you and accuse you to your face.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 8:13; Psalm 9:17; Hosea 4:6"> Now consider this, you who forget God, lest I tear you to pieces, with no one to rescue you:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 91:16; James 3:13; Psalm 24:4–5"> He who sacrifices a thank offering honors Me, and to him who rightly orders his way, I will show the salvation of God.”</span>
</div>
</div>
