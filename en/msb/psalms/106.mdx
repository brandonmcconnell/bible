<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:18; Psalm 105:1; 1 Chronicles 16:34"> Hallelujah! Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His loving devotion endures forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Ephesians 1:19; Psalm 145:3–12"> Who can describe the mighty acts of the LORD or fully proclaim His praise?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 15:2; James 1:25; John 15:14"> Blessed are those who uphold justice, who practice righteousness at all times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 119:132; Luke 23:42; Nehemiah 13:14"> Remember me, O LORD, in Your favor to Your people; visit me with Your salvation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:16; Psalm 105:43; Psalm 1:3"> that I may see the prosperity of Your chosen ones, and rejoice in the gladness of Your nation, and give glory with Your inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:16; Psalm 78:8; 1 Kings 8:47"> We have sinned like our fathers; we have done wrong and acted wickedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 14:11–12; Psalm 78:42; Psalm 78:11"> Our fathers in Egypt did not grasp Your wonders or remember Your abundant kindness; but they rebelled by the sea, there at the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Ezekiel 20:9; Ezekiel 20:14"> Yet He saved them for the sake of His name, to make His power known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 18:15; Isaiah 51:10; Isaiah 63:11–14"> He rebuked the Red Sea, and it dried up; He led them through the depths as through a desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 14:30; Psalm 107:2; Micah 6:4"> He saved them from the hand that hated them; He redeemed them from the hand of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 15:5; Psalm 78:53; Exodus 14:13"> The waters covered their foes; not one of them remained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 14:31–21; Luke 8:13; John 8:30–31"> Then they believed His promises and sang His praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 78:11; Exodus 15:24; Isaiah 48:17–18"> Yet they soon forgot His works and failed to wait for His counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Numbers 11:4; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> They craved intensely in the wilderness and tested God in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 11:31–34; Isaiah 10:16; Psalm 78:29–31"> So He granted their request, but sent a wasting disease upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 16:1–50; Leviticus 21:10–12; Leviticus 21:6–8"> In the camp they envied Moses, as well as Aaron, the holy one of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:6; Numbers 16:29–33; Numbers 26:10"> The earth opened up and swallowed Dathan; it covered the assembly of Abiram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Numbers 16:35–40; Numbers 16:46"> Then fire blazed through their company; flames consumed the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 32:4–8; Exodus 32:35; Nehemiah 9:18"> At Horeb they made a calf and worshiped a molten image.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:11; Exodus 20:4–5; Romans 1:22–23"> They exchanged their Glory for the image of a grass-eating ox.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:32; Psalm 78:42–51; Psalm 106:13"> They forgot God their Savior, who did great things in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 105:23; Psalm 78:51; Exodus 14:25–28"> wondrous works in the land of Ham, and awesome deeds by the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:13–14; Deuteronomy 9:25; Ezekiel 22:30"> So He said He would destroy them—had not Moses His chosen one stood before Him in the breach to divert His wrath from destroying them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:32; Ezekiel 20:6; Hebrews 3:18–19"> They despised the pleasant land; they did not believe His promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 14:22; Deuteronomy 1:26–27; Hebrews 3:15"> They grumbled in their tents and did not listen to the voice of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:15; Numbers 14:28–35; Hebrews 3:11"> So He raised His hand and swore to cast them down in the wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalm 44:11; Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:64–65"> to disperse their offspring among the nations and scatter them throughout the lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hosea 9:10; Numbers 25:1–3; Numbers 25:5"> They yoked themselves to Baal of Peor and ate sacrifices offered to lifeless gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16–21; 1 Corinthians 10:8; Romans 1:21–24"> So they provoked the LORD to anger with their deeds, and a plague broke out among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 25:6–8; Joshua 7:12; Numbers 25:14–15"> But Phinehas stood and intervened, and the plague was restrained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 25:11–13; Mark 14:3–9; Deuteronomy 24:13"> It was credited to him as righteousness for endless generations to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 20:2–13; Numbers 20:23–24; Deuteronomy 4:21"> At the waters of Meribah they angered the LORD, and trouble came to Moses because of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 20:10–11; Psalm 107:11; James 3:2"> For they rebelled against His Spirit, and Moses spoke rashly with his lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Deuteronomy 7:16; Judges 1:21"> They did not destroy the peoples as the LORD had commanded them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Judges 3:5–6; 1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> but they mingled with the nations and adopted their customs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:16; Judges 2:3; Exodus 23:33"> They worshiped their idols, which became a snare to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Kings 17:17; 2 Kings 16:3; Deuteronomy 32:17"> They sacrificed their sons and their daughters to demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; Deuteronomy 21:9; Isaiah 26:21"> They shed innocent blood—the blood of their sons and daughters, whom they sacrificed to the idols of Canaan, and the land was polluted with blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:18; Numbers 15:39; Leviticus 17:7"> They defiled themselves by their actions and prostituted themselves by their deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Deuteronomy 9:29; Judges 2:20"> So the anger of the LORD burned against His people, and He abhorred His own inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Nehemiah 9:27–38; Judges 3:8"> He delivered them into the hand of the nations, and those who hated them ruled over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Judges 4:3; Judges 10:12"> Their enemies oppressed them and subdued them under their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalm 81:12; Judges 2:16–18; Psalm 1:1"> Many times He rescued them, but they were bent on rebellion and sank down in their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Judges 3:9; Judges 4:3; 1 Samuel 7:8–12"> Nevertheless He heard their cry; He took note of their distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalm 105:8; Lamentations 3:32; Judges 2:18"> And He remembered His covenant with them, and relented by the abundance of His loving devotion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezra 9:9; Jeremiah 42:12; Jeremiah 15:11"> He made them objects of compassion to all who held them captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:35–36; Revelation 7:10–12; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> Save us, O LORD our God, and gather us from the nations, that we may give thanks to Your holy name, that we may glory in Your praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalm 41:13; Psalm 89:52; Psalm 105:45–1"> Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. Let all the people say, “Amen!” Hallelujah!</span>
