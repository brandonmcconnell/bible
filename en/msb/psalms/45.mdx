<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 49:3; 2 Samuel 23:2; Job 33:3"> For the choirmaster. To the tune of “The Lilies.” A Maskil of the sons of Korah. A love song. My heart is stirred by a noble theme as I recite my verses to the king; my tongue is the pen of a skillful writer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 4:22; John 7:46; Hebrews 7:26"> You are the most handsome of men; grace has anointed your lips; therefore God has blessed you forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 1:16; Isaiah 49:2"> Strap your sword at your side, O mighty warrior; appear in your majesty and splendor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 65:5; Revelation 6:2; Revelation 19:11"> In your splendor ride forth in victory on behalf of truth and humility and justice; may your right hand show your awesome deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 21:12; Numbers 24:8; Psalm 38:2"> Your arrows pierce the hearts of the king’s foes; the nations fall beneath your feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 1:8–9; Psalm 93:2; John 1:1"> Your throne, O God, endures forever and ever, and justice is the scepter of Your kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 1:9; Psalm 21:6; Isaiah 61:1–3"> You have loved righteousness and hated wickedness; therefore God, your God, has anointed you above your companions with the oil of joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:3; 2 Corinthians 2:14–16; John 19:39"> All your garments are fragrant with myrrh and aloes and cassia; from palaces of ivory the harps make you glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 2:19; Revelation 21:9; Ephesians 5:26–27"> The daughters of kings are among your honored women; the queen stands at your right hand, adorned with the gold of Ophir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:10–13; Genesis 12:1; Matthew 10:37"> Listen, O daughter! Consider and incline your ear: Forget your people and your father’s house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 95:6; Isaiah 54:5; Song of Solomon 4:1–5"> and the king will desire your beauty; bow to him, for he is your lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 22:29; Isaiah 60:3; Isaiah 49:23"> The Daughter of Tyre will come with a gift; men of wealth will seek your favor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–8; Isaiah 61:10; Romans 3:22"> All glorious is the princess in her chamber; her gown is embroidered with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:2; Song of Solomon 8:13; Exodus 28:39"> In colorful garments she is led to the king; her virgin companions are brought before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 7:15–17; Jude 1:24; Isaiah 55:12–13"> They are led in with joy and gladness; they enter the palace of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; 1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 1:6"> Your sons will succeed your fathers; you will make them princes throughout the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 62:3; Malachi 1:11; Psalm 72:17–19"> I will commemorate your name through all generations; therefore the nations will praise you forever and ever.</span>
