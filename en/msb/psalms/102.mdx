</div>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 135:13; Lamentations 5:19; Exodus 3:15"> But You, O LORD, sit enthroned forever; Your renown endures to all generations.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:2; Ezra 7:27; Ezra 3:1–3"> For Your servants delight in her stones and take pity on her dust.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:43; Psalm 138:4; Isaiah 60:3–22"> So the nations will fear the name of the LORD, and all the kings of the earth will fear Your glory.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–2; Psalm 147:2; Jeremiah 33:7"> For the LORD will rebuild Zion; He has appeared in His glory.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:6; Psalm 22:24; Jeremiah 29:11–14"> He will turn toward the prayer of the destitute; He will not despise their prayer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 22:30–31; Romans 15:4; Isaiah 43:21"> Let this be written for the generation to come, so that a people not yet created may praise the LORD.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalm 14:2; 1 Kings 8:43"> For He looked down from the heights of His sanctuary; the LORD gazed out from heaven to earth</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 79:11; Psalm 146:7; Exodus 3:7"> to hear a prisoner’s groaning, to release those condemned to death,</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 22:22; Isaiah 51:11; Psalm 79:13"> that they may proclaim the name of the LORD in Zion and praise Him in Jerusalem,</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 8:20–23; Matthew 24:14; Psalm 72:8–11"> when peoples and kingdoms assemble to serve the LORD.</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 89:38–47; 1 Timothy 4:1–3; 2 Thessalonians 2:3–12"> He has broken my strength on the way; He has cut short my days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 39:13; Habakkuk 1:12; Psalm 102:12"> I say: “O my God, do not take me in the midst of my days! Your years go on through all generations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; Exodus 20:11; Hebrews 1:10–12"> In the beginning You laid the foundations of the earth, and the heavens are the work of Your hands.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Matthew 24:35; Isaiah 34:4"> They will perish, but You remain; they will all wear out like a garment. Like clothing You will change them, and they will be passed on.</span>
