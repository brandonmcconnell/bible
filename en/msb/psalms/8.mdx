<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 8:9; Psalm 113:2–4; Psalm 148:13"> For the choirmaster. According to Gittith. A Psalm of David. O LORD, our Lord, how majestic is Your name in all the earth! You have set Your glory above the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 21:16; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Isaiah 40:31"> From the mouths of children and infants You have ordained praise on account of Your adversaries, to silence the enemy and avenger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; Psalm 33:6; Psalm 111:2"> When I behold Your heavens, the work of Your fingers, the moon and the stars, which You have set in place—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 7:17; Psalm 144:3; Hebrews 2:6–9"> what is man that You are mindful of him, or the son of man that You care for him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:7; Hebrews 2:9; Genesis 1:26–27"> You made him a little lower than the angels; You crowned him with glory and honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 1:28; Genesis 1:26; Matthew 28:18"> You made him ruler of the works of Your hands; You have placed everything under his feet:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 2:20"> all sheep and oxen, and even the beasts of the field,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–25; Psalm 148:10; Job 38:39–30"> the birds of the air and the fish of the sea, all that swim the paths of the seas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 8:1; Job 11:7; Psalm 104:24"> O LORD, our Lord, how majestic is Your name in all the earth!</span>
