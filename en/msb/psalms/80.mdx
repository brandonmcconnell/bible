<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 21:23; Psalm 50:2; Psalm 77:20"> For the choirmaster. To the tune of “The Lilies of the Covenant.” A Psalm of Asaph. Hear us, O Shepherd of Israel, who leads Joseph like a flock; You who sit enthroned between the cherubim, shine forth</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 35:23; Numbers 2:18–24; Psalm 44:23–26"> before Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh. Rally Your mighty power and come to save us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 5:21; Numbers 6:25–26; Psalm 85:4"> Restore us, O God, and cause Your face to shine upon us, that we may be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 85:5; Isaiah 58:2–3; Psalm 59:5"> O LORD God of Hosts, how long will Your anger smolder against the prayers of Your people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 42:3; Isaiah 30:20; Psalm 102:9"> You fed them with the bread of tears and made them drink the full measure of their tears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 79:4; Isaiah 36:8; Isaiah 37:23"> You make us contend with our neighbors; our enemies mock us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 51:10; Isaiah 64:5; Jeremiah 4:14"> Restore us, O God of Hosts, and cause Your face to shine upon us, that we may be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Psalm 44:2; Ezekiel 17:6"> You uprooted a vine from Egypt; You drove out the nations and transplanted it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 24:12; Isaiah 27:6; 1 Kings 4:20"> You cleared the ground for it, and it took root and filled the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 104:16"> The mountains were covered by its shade, and the mighty cedars with its branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 72:8; 1 Chronicles 18:3; 1 Kings 4:24"> It sent out its branches to the Sea, and its shoots toward the River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 5:5; Psalm 89:40–41; Isaiah 18:5–6"> Why have You broken down its walls, so that all who pass by pick its fruit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:6; Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 39:1–3"> The boar from the forest ravages it, and the creatures of the field feed upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 63:15; Psalm 90:13; Daniel 9:16–19"> Return, O God of Hosts, we pray! Look down from heaven and see! Attend to this vine—</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 15:1; Psalm 80:8; Jeremiah 2:21"> the root Your right hand has planted, the son You have raised up for Yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 39:11; John 15:6; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> Your vine has been cut down and burned; they perish at the rebuke of Your countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Psalm 89:21; Psalm 110:1"> Let Your hand be upon the man at Your right hand, on the son of man You have raised up for Yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 119:107; Psalm 119:25; Psalm 119:40"> Then we will not turn away from You; revive us, and we will call on Your name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:22–23; Psalm 80:3; Psalm 27:4"> Restore us, O LORD God of Hosts; cause Your face to shine upon us, that we may be saved.</span>
