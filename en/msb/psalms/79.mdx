<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 42:10; Exodus 7:5; Psalm 115:2"> Why should the nations ask, “Where is their God?” Before our eyes, make known among the nations Your vengeance for the bloodshed of Your servants.</span>
</div>
