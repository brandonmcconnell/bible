<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalm 107:8; Psalm 103:2; Psalm 107:21"> Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loving devotion and His wonders to the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalm 22:25; Psalm 22:22; Psalm 99:5"> Let them exalt Him in the assembly of the people and praise Him in the council of the elders.</span>

<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalm 114:8; Isaiah 35:6–7; Isaiah 41:17–19"> He turns a desert into pools of water and a dry land into flowing springs.</span>
