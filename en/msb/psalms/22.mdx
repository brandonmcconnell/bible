<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 15:34; Matthew 27:46; Hebrews 5:7"> For the choirmaster. To the tune of “The Doe of the Dawn.” A Psalm of David. My God, my God, why have You forsaken me? Why are You so far from saving me, so far from my words of groaning?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 42:3; Luke 18:7; Luke 22:41–46"> I cry out by day, O my God, but You do not answer, and by night, but I have no rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 50:23; Deuteronomy 10:21; Isaiah 6:3"> Yet You are holy, enthroned on the praises of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 14:31; Exodus 14:13–14; Hebrews 11:8–32"> In You our fathers trusted; they trusted and You delivered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Isaiah 49:23; Romans 9:33"> They cried out to You and were set free; they trusted in You and were not disappointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Job 25:6; Isaiah 49:7"> But I am a worm and not a man, scorned by men and despised by the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 15:29; Psalm 109:25; Matthew 27:39–40"> All who see me mock me; they sneer and shake their heads:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 27:42–43; Mark 15:30–32; Psalm 91:14"> “He trusts in the LORD, let the LORD deliver him; let the LORD rescue him, since He delights in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 71:6; Isaiah 49:1–2; Psalm 71:17"> Yet You brought me forth from the womb; You made me secure at my mother’s breast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 1:15; Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 49:1"> From birth I was cast upon You; from my mother’s womb You have been my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 71:12; Psalm 72:12; Psalm 10:1"> Be not far from me, for trouble is near and there is no one to help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 4:1–3; Psalm 68:30; Acts 4:27"> Many bulls surround me; strong bulls of Bashan encircle me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Psalm 35:21; Lamentations 3:46"> They open their jaws against me like lions that roar and maul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 31:10; Daniel 5:6; Job 30:16"> I am poured out like water, and all my bones are disjointed. My heart is like wax; it melts away within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 19:28; Psalm 69:3; Psalm 69:21"> My strength is dried up like a potsherd, and my tongue sticks to the roof of my mouth. You lay me in the dust of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 19:37; Zechariah 12:10; Matthew 27:35"> For dogs surround me; a band of evil men encircles me; they have pierced my hands and feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:35; Job 33:21; Matthew 27:39–41"> I can count all my bones; they stare and gloat over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:35; Luke 23:34; Mark 15:24"> They divide my garments among them and cast lots for my clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 22:11; Psalm 40:13; Psalm 40:17"> But You, O LORD, be not far off; O my Strength, come quickly to help me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 35:17; Zechariah 13:7; Psalm 17:13"> Deliver my soul from the sword, my precious life from the power of wild dogs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17; Numbers 23:22; 1 Peter 5:8"> Save me from the mouth of the lion; at the horns of the wild oxen You have answered me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 2:11–12; Romans 8:29; Psalm 40:9–10"> I will proclaim Your name to my brothers; I will praise You in the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 135:19–20; Revelation 15:4; Psalm 107:1–2"> You who fear the LORD, praise Him! All descendants of Jacob, honor Him! All offspring of Israel, revere Him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 50:6–9; Psalm 116:3–6"> For He has not despised or detested the torment of the afflicted. He has not hidden His face from him, but has attended to his cry for help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 40:9–10; Psalm 35:18; Psalm 111:1"> My praise for You resounds in the great assembly; I will fulfill my vows before those who fear You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 69:32; Psalm 40:16; Isaiah 65:13"> The poor will eat and be satisfied; those who seek the LORD will praise Him. May your hearts live forever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalm 86:9; Isaiah 45:22; Psalm 2:8"> All the ends of the earth will remember and turn to the LORD. All the families of the nations will bow down before Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; Psalm 47:7–8; Revelation 11:15"> For dominion belongs to the LORD and He rules over the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; Isaiah 26:19; Psalm 45:12"> All the rich of the earth will feast and worship; all who go down to the dust will kneel before Him—even those unable to preserve their lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 2:13; Psalm 14:5; 1 Peter 2:9"> Posterity will serve Him; they will declare the Lord to a new generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalm 78:6; Psalm 86:9; Romans 1:17"> They will come and proclaim His righteousness to a people yet unborn—all that He has done.</span>
</div>
