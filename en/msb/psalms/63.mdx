<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 143:6; Psalm 84:2; Psalm 42:1–2"> A Psalm of David, when he was in the Wilderness of Judah. O God, You are my God. Earnestly I seek You; my soul thirsts for You. My body yearns for You in a dry and weary land without water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 27:4; Psalm 105:4; Psalm 84:2–11"> So I have seen You in the sanctuary and beheld Your power and glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 69:16; Psalm 51:15; Philippians 1:23"> Because Your loving devotion is better than life, my lips will glorify You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 104:33; Psalm 134:2; Psalm 28:2"> So I will bless You as long as I live; in Your name I will lift my hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 36:7–9; Isaiah 25:6; Ezra 3:11–13"> My soul is satisfied as with the richest of foods; with joyful lips my mouth will praise You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 42:8; Psalm 119:147–148; Psalm 149:5"> When I remember You on my bed, I think of You through the watches of the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Psalm 61:4; Psalm 27:9"> For You are my help; I will sing for joy in the shadow of Your wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalm 18:35; Psalm 73:23"> My soul clings to You; Your right hand upholds me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 55:15; Psalm 9:17; Psalm 40:14"> But those who seek my life to destroy it will go into the depths of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; Ezekiel 35:5; Ezekiel 39:4"> They will fall to the power of the sword; they will become a portion for foxes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Psalm 21:1; Isaiah 65:16"> But the king will rejoice in God; all who swear by Him will exult, for the mouths of liars will be shut.</span>
