<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 8:34; Isaiah 30:18; Psalm 25:10"> Guide me in Your truth and teach me, for You are the God of my salvation; all day long I wait for You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 103:17; Colossians 3:12; Luke 1:50"> Remember, O LORD, Your compassion and loving devotion, for they are from age to age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 38:17; Job 13:26"> Remember not the sins of my youth, nor my rebellious acts; remember me according to Your loving devotion, because of Your goodness, O LORD.</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 37:11; Proverbs 1:33; Proverbs 19:23"> His soul will dwell in prosperity, and his descendants will inherit the land.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 107:6; Psalm 34:19; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> The troubles of my heart increase; free me from my distress.</span>
