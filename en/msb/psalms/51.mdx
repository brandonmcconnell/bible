<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 1:7–9; Psalm 51:7; Hebrews 10:21–22"> Wash me clean of my iniquity and cleanse me from my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Isaiah 59:12; Psalm 38:18"> For I know my transgressions, and my sin is always before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13–14; Luke 15:21; Genesis 20:6"> Against You, You only, have I sinned and done what is evil in Your sight, so that You may be proved right when You speak and blameless when You judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 5:12; Job 14:4; Psalm 58:3"> Surely I was brought forth in iniquity; I was sinful when my mother conceived me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 15:2; 1 Samuel 16:7; 1 Peter 3:4"> Surely You desire truth in the inmost being; You teach me wisdom in the inmost place.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 5:17–18; Hosea 6:1–2; Matthew 5:4"> Let me hear joy and gladness; let the bones You have crushed rejoice.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ephesians 4:22–24"> Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; 2 Kings 13:23; Isaiah 63:10–11"> Cast me not away from Your presence; take not Your Holy Spirit from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalm 13:5; Isaiah 61:10"> Restore to me the joy of Your salvation, and sustain me with a willing spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 22:32; Acts 26:18–20; Acts 3:19"> Then I will teach transgressors Your ways, and sinners will return to You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 35:28; 2 Samuel 12:9; Psalm 26:9"> Deliver me from bloodguilt, O God, the God of my salvation, and my tongue will sing of Your righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 63:3–5; Hebrews 13:15; Psalm 9:14"> O Lord, open my lips, and my mouth will declare Your praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 40:6; Hosea 6:6; Hebrews 10:5–6"> For You do not delight in sacrifice, or I would bring it; You take no pleasure in burnt offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 34:18; Isaiah 66:2; Psalm 147:3"> The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit; a broken and a contrite heart, O God, You will not despise.</span>
