<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalm 25:2; Psalm 71:1–3"> For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. In You, O LORD, I have taken refuge; let me never be put to shame; save me by Your righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 71:2–3; Psalm 102:2; Psalm 86:1"> Incline Your ear to me; come quickly to my rescue. Be my rock of refuge, the stronghold of my deliverance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 143:10–11; Luke 1:79; Psalm 79:9"> For You are my rock and my fortress; lead me and guide me for the sake of Your name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 25:15; Psalm 140:5; Psalm 19:14"> You free me from the net laid out for me, for You are my refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 23:46; Acts 7:59; Deuteronomy 32:4"> Into Your hands I commit my spirit; You have redeemed me, O LORD, God of truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jonah 2:8; Psalm 24:4; Jeremiah 10:15"> I hate those who cling to worthless idols, but in the LORD I trust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Isaiah 63:9; Psalm 119:153"> I will be glad and rejoice in Your loving devotion, for You have seen my affliction; You have known the anguish of my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 36:16; Deuteronomy 32:30; Psalm 18:19"> You have not delivered me to the enemy; You have set my feet in the open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 6:7; Psalm 88:9; Psalm 73:26"> Be merciful to me, O LORD, for I am in distress; my eyes fail from sorrow, my soul and body as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 88:15; Psalm 32:3–4; Psalm 38:3"> For my life is consumed with grief and my years with groaning; my iniquity has drained my strength, and my bones are wasting away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 38:11; Psalm 88:8; Psalm 88:18"> Among all my enemies I am a disgrace, and among my neighbors even more. I am dreaded by my friends—they flee when they see me on the street.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 88:4–5; Psalm 2:9; Romans 9:21–22"> I am forgotten like a dead man, out of mind. I am like a broken vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; Matthew 27:1; Lamentations 2:22"> For I hear the slander of many; there is terror on every side. They conspire against me and plot to take my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 56:3–4; Psalm 18:2; Psalm 43:5"> But I trust in You, O LORD; I say, “You are my God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Ecclesiastes 3:1–8; John 17:1"> My times are in Your hands; deliver me from my enemies and from those who pursue me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 4:6; Psalm 6:4; Daniel 9:9"> Make Your face shine on Your servant; save me by Your loving devotion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 25:2–3; Jeremiah 20:11; Psalm 34:5"> O LORD, let me not be ashamed, for I have called on You. Let the wicked be put to shame; let them lie silent in Sheol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jude 1:15; Psalm 94:4; 1 Samuel 2:3"> May lying lips be silenced—lips that speak with arrogance against the righteous, full of pride and contempt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9; Isaiah 64:4; Lamentations 3:23–25"> How great is Your goodness which You have laid up for those who fear You, which You have bestowed before the sons of men on those who take refuge in You!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 27:5; Psalm 32:7; Psalm 91:1–4"> You hide them in the secret place of Your presence from the schemes of men. You conceal them in Your shelter from accusing tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:7–13; Psalm 17:7; 1 Peter 2:9"> Blessed be the LORD, for He has shown me His loving devotion in a city under siege.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 116:11; Hebrews 5:7; Psalm 88:16"> In my alarm I said, “I am cut off from Your sight!” But You heard my plea for mercy when I called to You for help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 97:10; Psalm 94:2; Psalm 145:20"> Love the LORD, all His saints. The LORD preserves the faithful, but fully repays the arrogant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 27:14; Psalm 146:5; Colossians 1:11"> Be strong and courageous, all you who hope in the LORD.</span>
