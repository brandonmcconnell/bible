<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 79:9–10; Isaiah 48:11; Psalm 96:8"> Not to us, O LORD, not to us, but to Your name be the glory, because of Your loving devotion, because of Your faithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 42:3; Psalm 79:10; Psalm 42:10"> Why should the nations say, “Where is their God?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 135:6; Daniel 4:35; Isaiah 46:10"> Our God is in heaven; He does as He pleases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:28; Isaiah 46:6–7; Jeremiah 10:3–5"> Their idols are silver and gold, made by the hands of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:5"> They have mouths, but cannot speak; they have eyes, but cannot see;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> they have ears, but cannot hear; they have noses, but cannot smell;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> they have hands, but cannot feel; they have feet, but cannot walk; they cannot even clear their throats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 135:18; Habakkuk 2:18–19; Jeremiah 10:8"> Those who make them become like them, as do all who trust in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 62:8; Psalm 37:3; Psalm 118:2–4"> O Israel, trust in the LORD! He is their help and shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 118:3; Numbers 18:7; Exodus 28:1"> O house of Aaron, trust in the LORD! He is their help and shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 14:26; Proverbs 30:5; Acts 10:35"> You who fear the LORD, trust in the LORD! He is their help and shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 136:23; Ephesians 1:3; Isaiah 44:21"> The LORD is mindful of us; He will bless us. He will bless the house of Israel; He will bless the house of Aaron;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 112:1; Luke 1:50; Psalm 128:1"> He will bless those who fear the LORD—small and great alike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:11; Acts 2:39; Acts 3:25"> May the LORD give you increase, both you and your children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 134:3; Revelation 14:7; Genesis 1:1"> May you be blessed by the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 8:6; Psalm 89:11; Genesis 1:28–30"> The highest heavens belong to the LORD, but the earth He has given to mankind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 6:5; Isaiah 38:18–19; Psalm 88:10–12"> It is not the dead who praise the LORD, nor any who descend into silence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 113:2; Daniel 2:20; Revelation 5:13"> But it is we who will bless the LORD, both now and forevermore. Hallelujah!</span>
