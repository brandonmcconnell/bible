<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 18:34; 2 Samuel 22:35; Psalm 18:2"> Of David. Blessed be the LORD, my Rock, who trains my hands for war, my fingers for battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:2–3; Psalm 18:2; Psalm 91:2"> He is my steadfast love and my fortress, my stronghold and my deliverer. He is my shield, in whom I take refuge, who subdues peoples under me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 8:4; Job 7:17; Hebrews 2:6"> O LORD, what is man, that You regard him, the son of man that You think of him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 102:11; Psalm 39:5–6; Psalm 109:23"> Man is like a breath; his days are like a passing shadow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 104:32; Psalm 18:9; Exodus 19:18"> Part Your heavens, O LORD, and come down; touch the mountains, that they may smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Psalm 18:13–14; Deuteronomy 32:42"> Flash forth Your lightning and scatter them; shoot Your arrows and rout them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 69:1–2; Psalm 18:16; Psalm 54:3"> Reach down from on high; set me free and rescue me from the deep waters, from the grasp of foreigners,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 12:2; Psalm 41:6; Psalm 62:4"> whose mouths speak falsehood, whose right hands are deceitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 33:2–3; Psalm 40:3; Psalm 149:1"> I will sing to You a new song, O God; on a harp of ten strings I will make music to You—</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 18:50; Psalm 140:7; Psalm 33:16–18"> to Him who gives victory to kings, who frees His servant David from the deadly sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 144:7–8; Isaiah 44:20; Psalm 12:2"> Set me free and rescue me from the grasp of foreigners, whose mouths speak falsehood, whose right hands are deceitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 128:3; 1 Peter 3:3–6; Isaiah 44:3–5"> Then our sons will be like plants nurtured in their youth, our daughters like corner pillars carved to adorn a palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:13–14; Leviticus 26:10; Malachi 3:10"> Our storehouses will be full, supplying all manner of produce; our flocks will bring forth thousands, tens of thousands in our fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3–5; Deuteronomy 28:7; Isaiah 24:11"> Our oxen will bear great loads. There will be no breach in the walls, no going into captivity, and no cry of lament in our streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 33:12; Ephesians 1:3; Psalm 146:5"> Blessed are the people of whom this is so; blessed are the people whose God is the LORD.</span>
