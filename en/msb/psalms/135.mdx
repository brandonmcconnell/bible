<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 95:3; Psalm 48:1; Psalm 97:9"> For I know that the LORD is great; our Lord is above all gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 4:35; Psalm 115:3; Matthew 28:18"> The LORD does all that pleases Him in the heavens and on the earth, in the seas and in all their depths.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 136:15; Deuteronomy 6:22; Deuteronomy 4:34"> He sent signs and wonders into your midst, O Egypt, against Pharaoh and all his servants.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 78:55; Psalm 136:21–22; Joshua 11:23"> He gave their land as an inheritance, as a heritage to His people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 102:12; Exodus 3:15; Psalm 8:1"> Your name, O LORD, endures forever, Your renown, O LORD, through all generations.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 115:4–8; Habakkuk 2:18–19; Isaiah 37:19"> The idols of the nations are silver and gold, made by the hands of men.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> they have ears, but cannot hear; nor is there breath in their mouths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 115:8; Isaiah 44:18–20; Jeremiah 10:8"> Those who make them become like them, as do all who trust in them.</span>
