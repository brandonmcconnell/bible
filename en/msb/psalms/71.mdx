<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 31:1–3; Psalm 25:2–3; 1 Peter 2:6"> In You, O LORD, I have taken refuge; let me never be put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 31:1; Psalm 17:6; Psalm 116:1–2"> In Your justice, rescue and deliver me; incline Your ear and save me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 18:2; Psalm 91:9; Proverbs 18:10"> Be my rock of refuge, where I can always go. Give the command to save me, for You are my rock and my fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 140:1–4; Psalm 59:1–2; 2 Samuel 17:1–2"> Deliver me, O my God, from the hand of the wicked, from the grasp of the unjust and ruthless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7; Psalm 39:7; Psalm 119:81"> For You are my hope, O Lord GOD, my confidence from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 22:9–10; Jeremiah 1:5; Psalm 139:15–16"> I have leaned on You since birth; You pulled me from my mother’s womb. My praise is always for You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; 1 Corinthians 4:9; Acts 4:13"> I have become a portent to many, but You are my strong refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 35:28; Psalm 146:2; Psalm 104:1"> My mouth is filled with Your praise and with Your splendor all day long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; 2 Timothy 1:12; Psalm 71:18"> Do not discard me in my old age; do not forsake me when my strength fails.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 83:3; Psalm 56:6; Jeremiah 20:10"> For my enemies speak against me, and those who lie in wait for my life conspire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 7:2; Psalm 3:2; Matthew 27:46"> saying, “God has forsaken him; pursue him and seize him, for there is no one to rescue him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 35:22; Psalm 38:21–22; Psalm 70:5"> Be not far from me, O God. Hurry, O my God, to help me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:11; Psalm 71:24; Psalm 35:26"> May the accusers of my soul be ashamed and consumed; may those who seek my harm be covered with scorn and disgrace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 130:7; 1 John 3:3; Psalm 71:6"> But I will always hope and will praise You more and more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 40:5; Psalm 35:28; Psalm 71:8"> My mouth will declare Your righteousness and Your salvation all day long, though I cannot know their full measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Psalm 51:14; Philippians 3:9"> I will come in the strength of the Lord GOD; I will proclaim Your righteousness—Yours alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 71:5; Psalm 119:9; Psalm 119:102"> O God, You have taught me from my youth, and to this day I proclaim Your marvelous deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; Psalm 71:9; Psalm 78:4"> Even when I am old and gray, do not forsake me, O God, until I proclaim Your power to the next generation, Your might to all who are to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 35:10; Psalm 36:5–6; Psalm 57:10"> Your righteousness reaches to the heavens, O God, You who have done great things. Who, O God, is like You?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 138:7; Psalm 60:3; Psalm 86:13"> Though You have shown me many troubles and misfortunes, You will revive me once again. Even from the depths of the earth You will bring me back up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 12:1; 2 Samuel 3:1; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> You will increase my honor and comfort me once again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 92:1–3; 2 Kings 19:22; Psalm 33:2"> So I will praise You with the harp for Your faithfulness, O my God; I will sing praise to You with the lyre, O Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 104:33; Psalm 103:4; Luke 1:46–47"> When I sing praise to You my lips will shout for joy, along with my soul, which You have redeemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 71:13; Psalm 18:37–43; 1 Corinthians 15:25"> My tongue will indeed proclaim Your righteousness all day long, for those who seek my harm are disgraced and confounded.</span>
