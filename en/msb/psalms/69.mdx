<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 69:14–15; Isaiah 43:2; Lamentations 3:54"> For the choirmaster. To the tune of “Lilies.” Of David. Save me, O God, for the waters are up to my neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 40:2; Jeremiah 38:6; Matthew 7:25"> I have sunk into the miry depths, where there is no footing; I have drifted into deep waters, where the flood engulfs me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 6:6; Psalm 119:82; Isaiah 38:14"> I am weary from my crying; my throat is parched. My eyes fail, looking for my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:25; Psalm 35:19; Psalm 109:3–5"> Those who hate me without cause outnumber the hairs of my head; many are those who would destroy me—my enemies for no reason. Though I did not steal, I must repay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 38:9; Psalm 17:3; Jeremiah 16:17"> You know my folly, O God, and my guilt is not hidden from You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 25:3; Psalm 35:26; Luke 24:19–21"> May those who hope in You not be ashamed through me, O Lord GOD of Hosts; may those who seek You not be dishonored through me, O God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 44:22; Jeremiah 15:15; Isaiah 50:6"> For I have endured scorn for Your sake, and shame has covered my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 31:11; John 1:11; Matthew 26:70–74"> I have become a stranger to my brothers and a foreigner to my mother’s sons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 15:3; John 2:14–17; Psalm 119:139"> because zeal for Your house has consumed me, and the insults of those who insult You have fallen on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 35:13; Psalm 102:8–9; Luke 7:33–34"> I wept and fasted, but it brought me reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 9:7; Jeremiah 24:9; Job 17:6"> I made sackcloth my clothing, and I was sport to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 35:15–16; Mark 15:17–19; Daniel 5:23"> Those who sit at the gate mock me, and I am the song of drunkards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:2; Isaiah 49:8; Psalm 55:16–17"> But my prayer to You, O LORD, is for a time of favor. In Your abundant loving devotion, O God, answer me with Your sure salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 144:7; Psalm 109:21; Jeremiah 38:6–13"> Rescue me from the mire and do not let me sink; deliver me from my foes and out of the deep waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 12:15–16; Acts 2:24; Jonah 2:2–7"> Do not let the floods engulf me or the depths swallow me up; let not the Pit close its mouth over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 109:21; Psalm 25:16; Psalm 63:3"> Answer me, O LORD, for Your loving devotion is good; turn to me in keeping with Your great compassion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 27:9; Psalm 102:2; Psalm 70:1"> Hide not Your face from Your servant, for I am in distress. Answer me quickly!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 22:19; Psalm 22:1; Psalm 111:9"> Draw near to my soul and redeem me; ransom me because of my foes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 53:3; 1 Peter 2:23"> You know my reproach, my shame and disgrace. All my adversaries are before You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 63:5; Psalm 142:4; Mark 14:50"> Insults have broken my heart, and I am in despair. I looked for sympathy, but there was none, for comforters, but I found no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36; Mark 15:23"> They poisoned my food with gall and gave me vinegar to quench my thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Romans 11:8–10; 1 Peter 2:8"> May their table become a snare; may it be a retribution and a trap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 5:6; Romans 11:10; Isaiah 6:9–10"> May their eyes be darkened so they cannot see, and their backs be bent forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 79:6; Hosea 5:10; Exodus 15:15"> Pour out Your wrath upon them, and let Your burning anger overtake them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 1:20; Matthew 23:38; Jeremiah 7:12–14"> May their place be deserted; let there be no one to dwell in their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4; Zechariah 1:15; 2 Chronicles 28:9"> For they persecute the one You struck and recount the pain of those You wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; Nehemiah 4:5; 2 Timothy 4:14"> Add iniquity to their iniquity; let them not share in Your righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Philippians 4:3; Exodus 32:32–33; Luke 10:20"> May they be blotted out of the Book of Life and not listed with the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalm 91:14–16; Psalm 40:17; Psalm 20:1"> But I am in pain and distress; let Your salvation protect me, O God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalm 28:7; Psalm 34:3; Psalm 118:28–29"> I will praise God’s name in song and exalt Him with thanksgiving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalm 50:13–14; Psalm 50:23"> And this will please the LORD more than an ox, more than a bull with horns and hooves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalm 22:26; Psalm 34:2; John 16:22"> The humble will see and rejoice. You who seek God, let your hearts be revived!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalm 68:6; Acts 5:18–19; Zechariah 9:11–12"> For the LORD listens to the needy and does not despise His captive people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalm 96:11; Isaiah 49:13; Psalm 150:6"> Let heaven and earth praise Him, the seas and everything that moves in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 44:26; Psalm 51:18; Isaiah 46:13"> For God will save Zion and rebuild the cities of Judah, that they may dwell there and possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalm 102:28; Psalm 37:29; Acts 2:39"> The descendants of His servants will inherit it, and those who love His name will settle in it.</span>
