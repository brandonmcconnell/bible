<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 10:18; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalm 98:3"> their voice has gone out into all the earth, their words to the ends of the world. In the heavens He has pitched a tent for the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 62:5; Philippians 3:13–14; 1 Corinthians 9:24–26"> Like a bridegroom emerging from his chamber, like a champion rejoicing to run his course,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:5; Job 22:14; Colossians 1:23"> it rises at one end of the heavens and runs its circuit to the other; nothing is deprived of its warmth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 119:130; Psalm 111:7; Colossians 3:16"> The Law of the LORD is perfect, reviving the soul; the testimony of the LORD is trustworthy, making wise the simple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 119:105; Psalm 12:6; Jeremiah 15:16"> The precepts of the LORD are right, bringing joy to the heart; the commandments of the LORD are radiant, giving light to the eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:24; Psalm 119:142; Psalm 119:137–138"> The fear of the LORD is pure, enduring forever; the judgments of the LORD are true, being altogether righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 119:103; Proverbs 3:13–15; Psalm 119:72"> They are more precious than gold, than much pure gold; they are sweeter than honey, than honey from the comb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 119:11; 2 John 1:8; Hebrews 11:6–7"> By them indeed Your servant is warned; in keeping them is great reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 90:8; Job 6:24; Psalm 139:23–24"> Who can discern his own errors? Cleanse me from my hidden faults.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 119:133; Romans 6:12–14; 1 Samuel 25:39"> Keep Your servant also from willful sins; may they not rule over me. Then I will be blameless and cleansed of great transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 104:34; Psalm 51:15; Psalm 119:108"> May the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be pleasing in Your sight, O LORD, my Rock and my Redeemer.</span>
