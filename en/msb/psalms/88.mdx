<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalm 22:2; Luke 18:7"> A song. A Psalm of the sons of Korah. For the choirmaster. According to Mahalath Leannoth. A Maskil of Heman the Ezrahite. O LORD, the God of my salvation, day and night I cry out before You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 31:2; Psalm 141:1–2; Lamentations 3:8"> May my prayer come before You; incline Your ear to my cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 107:18; Lamentations 3:15–19; Mark 14:33–34"> For my soul is full of troubles, and my life draws near to Sheol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 28:1; 2 Corinthians 1:9; Psalm 143:7"> I am counted among those descending to the Pit. I am like a man without strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:8; Psalm 31:22; Isaiah 14:9–12"> I am forsaken among the dead, like the slain who lie in the grave, whom You remember no more, who are cut off from Your care.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 69:15; Psalm 86:13; Psalm 143:3"> You have laid me in the lowest Pit, in the darkest of the depths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 42:7; Job 6:4; Romans 2:5–9"> Your wrath weighs heavily upon me; all Your waves have submerged me. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 31:11; Isaiah 49:7; Jeremiah 32:2"> You have removed my friends from me; You have made me repulsive to them; I am confined and cannot escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 11:13; Psalm 38:10; Psalm 86:3"> My eyes grow dim with grief. I call to You daily, O LORD; I spread out my hands to You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 6:5; Isaiah 26:19; Psalm 30:9"> Do You work wonders for the dead? Do departed spirits rise up to praise You? Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Proverbs 15:11; Psalm 73:18"> Can Your loving devotion be proclaimed in the grave, Your faithfulness in Abaddon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Isaiah 8:22"> Will Your wonders be known in the darkness, or Your righteousness in the land of oblivion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 5:3; Psalm 30:2; Mark 1:35"> But to You, O LORD, I cry for help; in the morning my prayer comes before You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 13:24; Psalm 13:1; Psalm 44:24"> Why, O LORD, do You reject me? Why do You hide Your face from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:4; Luke 22:44; Job 7:11–16"> From my youth I was afflicted and near death. I have borne Your terrors; I am in despair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 90:11; Isaiah 53:4–6; Romans 8:32"> Your wrath has swept over me; Your terrors have destroyed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 22:16; Psalm 116:3; Psalm 69:1–2"> All day long they engulf me like water; they enclose me on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 88:8; Psalm 38:11; Job 19:12–15"> You have removed my beloved and my friend; darkness is my closest companion.</span>
