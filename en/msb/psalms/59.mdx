<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:11–24; Psalm 18:48; Psalm 57:1"> For the choirmaster. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” A Miktam of David, when Saul sent men to watch David’s house in order to kill him. Deliver me from my enemies, O my God; protect me from those who rise against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 139:19; Psalm 26:9; Psalm 55:23"> Deliver me from workers of iniquity, and save me from men of bloodshed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 56:6; Psalm 69:4; 1 Samuel 24:11"> See how they lie in wait for me. Fierce men conspire against me for no transgression or sin of my own, O LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 35:23; Isaiah 51:9; Proverbs 1:16"> For no fault of my own, they move swiftly to attack me. Arise to help me, and take notice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 84:8; Psalm 55:15; Psalm 9:15"> O LORD God of Hosts, the God of Israel, rouse Yourself to punish all the nations; show no mercy to the wicked traitors. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 59:14; Psalm 22:16; 1 Samuel 19:11"> They return in the evening, snarling like dogs and prowling around the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 57:4; Proverbs 15:2; Psalm 10:11"> See what they spew from their mouths—sharp words from their lips: “For who can hear us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 1:26; Psalm 37:13; Psalm 2:4"> But You, O LORD, laugh at them; You scoff at all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 9:9; Psalm 62:2; Psalm 27:14"> I will keep watch for You, O my strength, because You, O God, are my fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 54:7; Psalm 59:17; 1 Peter 5:10"> My God of loving devotion will come to meet me; God will let me stare down my foes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 3:3; Revelation 9:6; Job 40:12"> Do not kill them, or my people will forget. Scatter them by Your power, and bring them down, O Lord, our shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 12:13; Proverbs 18:7; Psalm 10:7"> By the sins of their mouths and the words of their lips, let them be trapped in their pride, in the curses and lies they utter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 83:18; Psalm 7:9; Ezekiel 39:7"> Consume them in wrath; consume them till they are no more, so it may be known to the ends of the earth that God rules over Jacob. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 22:16; Psalm 59:6"> They return in the evening, snarling like dogs and prowling around the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 15:23; Psalm 109:10; Job 30:1–7"> They scavenge for food, and growl if they are not satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 21:13; Ephesians 3:20; Romans 15:9"> But I will sing of Your strength and proclaim Your loving devotion in the morning. For You are my fortress, my refuge in times of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 59:9–10; Psalm 46:1; Psalm 18:1"> To You, O my strength, I sing praises, for You, O God, are my fortress, my God of loving devotion.</span>
