<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Jeremiah 12:3; Jeremiah 17:9–10"> For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. O LORD, You have searched me and known me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:4; 2 Kings 19:27; Psalm 94:11"> You know when I sit and when I rise; You understand my thoughts from afar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:24; Job 31:4; Genesis 28:10–17"> You search out my path and my lying down; You are aware of all my ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 19:14; Hebrews 4:12–13; Psalm 50:19–21"> Even before a word is on my tongue, You know all about it, O LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Psalm 34:7; Revelation 1:17"> You hem me in behind and before; You have laid Your hand upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Job 42:3; Job 26:14"> Such knowledge is too wonderful for me, too lofty for me to attain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:23–24; Jonah 1:3; Jonah 1:10"> Where can I go to escape Your Spirit? Where can I flee from Your presence?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 9:2–4; Jonah 2:2; Job 34:21–22"> If I ascend to the heavens, You are there; if I make my bed in Sheol, You are there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 19:6; Psalm 74:16–17; Malachi 4:2"> If I rise on the wings of the dawn, if I settle by the farthest sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 23:3; Isaiah 41:13; Psalm 73:23"> even there Your hand will guide me; Your right hand will hold me fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:22; Jeremiah 23:24; Psalm 94:7"> If I say, “Surely the darkness will hide me, and the light become night around me”—</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; Job 34:22; Hebrews 4:13"> even the darkness is not dark to You, but the night shines like the day, for darkness is as light to You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Psalm 119:73; Isaiah 44:2"> For You formed my inmost being; You knit me together in my mother’s womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 1:26–27; Psalm 111:2; Psalm 104:24"> I praise You, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made. Marvelous are Your works, and I know this very well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:5; Psalm 139:13; Job 10:8–11"> My frame was not hidden from You when I was made in secret, when I was woven together in the depths of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 56:8; Malachi 3:16; Revelation 20:12"> Your eyes saw my unformed body; all my days were written in Your book and ordained for me before one of them came to be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 40:5; Jeremiah 29:11; Isaiah 55:8–9"> How precious to me are Your thoughts, O God, how vast is their sum!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 3:5; Psalm 139:3; Psalm 40:5"> If I were to count them, they would outnumber the grains of sand; and when I awake, I am still with You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 5:6; Psalm 119:115; Isaiah 11:4"> O God, that You would slay the wicked—away from me, you bloodthirsty men—</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jude 1:15; Exodus 20:7; Psalm 74:18"> who speak of You deceitfully; Your enemies take Your name in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 119:158; Psalm 31:6; Psalm 26:5"> Do I not hate those who hate You, O LORD, and detest those who rise against You?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 101:3–8; Luke 14:26"> I hate them with perfect hatred; I count them as my enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 26:2; Job 31:6; 1 Peter 1:7"> Search me, O God, and know my heart; test me and know my concerns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 143:10; Jeremiah 17:9–10; Psalm 25:8–9"> See if there is any offensive way in me; lead me in the way everlasting.</span>
