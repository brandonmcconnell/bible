<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 103:17; Psalm 94:4; Psalm 107:1"> For the choirmaster. A Maskil of David. After Doeg the Edomite went to Saul and told him, “David has gone to the house of Ahimelech.” Why do you boast of evil, O mighty man? The loving devotion of God endures all day long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 18:21; Psalm 57:4; Jeremiah 9:3–4"> Your tongue devises destruction like a sharpened razor, O worker of deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:3–5; 2 Timothy 3:4; John 8:44"> You love evil more than good, falsehood more than speaking truth. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 120:3; James 3:6–9; 1 Samuel 22:18–19"> You love every word that devours, O deceitful tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 2:22; Psalm 27:13; Revelation 21:8"> Surely God will bring you down to everlasting ruin; He will snatch you up and tear you away from your tent; He will uproot you from the land of the living. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 22:19; Psalm 40:3; Psalm 37:34"> The righteous will see and fear; they will mock the evildoer, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Psalm 73:18–20; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> “Look at the man who did not make God his refuge, but trusted in the abundance of his wealth and strengthened himself by destruction.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 92:12–14; Psalm 1:3; Hosea 14:6–8"> But I am like an olive tree flourishing in the house of God; I trust in the loving devotion of God forever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 130:5–6; Lamentations 3:25–26; Psalm 54:6"> I will praise You forever, because You have done it. I will wait on Your name—for it is good—in the presence of Your saints.</span>
