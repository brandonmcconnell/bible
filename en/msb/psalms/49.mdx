<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 78:1; Psalm 33:8; Matthew 11:15"> For the choirmaster. A Psalm of the sons of Korah. Hear this, all you peoples; listen, all inhabitants of the world,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 62:9; Proverbs 22:2; Jeremiah 5:4–5"> both low and high, rich and poor alike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 19:14; Proverbs 22:17; Matthew 12:35"> My mouth will impart wisdom, and the meditation of my heart will bring understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 78:2; Matthew 13:35; Numbers 12:8"> I will incline my ear to a proverb; I will express my riddle with the harp:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 27:24; Romans 8:33–34; Amos 5:13"> Why should I fear in times of trouble, when wicked usurpers surround me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Psalm 52:7; Jeremiah 9:23"> They trust in their wealth and boast in their great riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Timothy 2:6; 1 Peter 1:18"> No man can possibly redeem his brother or pay his ransom to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Job 36:18–19"> For the redemption of his soul is costly, and never can payment suffice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 89:48; Psalm 16:10; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> that he should live on forever and not see decay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Psalm 39:6; Hebrews 9:27"> For it is clear that wise men die, and the foolish and the senseless both perish and leave their wealth to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 4:17; Psalm 5:9; Psalm 64:6"> Their graves are their eternal homes—their dwellings for endless generations—even though their lands were their namesakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18–21; Psalm 49:20; Psalm 82:7"> But a man, despite his wealth, cannot endure; he is like the beasts that perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:20; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Luke 16:27–28"> This is the fate of the foolish and their followers who endorse their sayings. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; 1 Corinthians 6:2; Daniel 7:22"> Like sheep they are destined for Sheol. Death will be their shepherd. The upright will rule them in the morning, and their form will decay in Sheol, far from their lofty abode.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 73:24; Psalm 56:13; Psalm 86:13"> But God will redeem my life from Sheol, for He will surely take me to Himself. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 37:7; Psalm 37:1; Revelation 21:26"> Do not be afraid when a man grows rich, when the splendor of his house increases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:7; Job 27:19; Luke 16:24"> For when he dies, he will carry nothing away; his abundance will not follow him down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 12:19; Deuteronomy 29:19; Psalm 10:3"> Though in his lifetime he blesses his soul—and men praise you when you prosper—</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 33:30; Genesis 15:15; Psalm 56:13"> he will join the generation of his fathers, who will never see the light of day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 49:12; Ecclesiastes 3:18–19; Job 4:21"> A man who has riches without understanding is like the beasts that perish.</span>
