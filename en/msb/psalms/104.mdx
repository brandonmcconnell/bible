<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 103:22; Psalm 145:3; Psalm 96:6"> Bless the LORD, O my soul! O LORD my God, You are very great; You are clothed with splendor and majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Daniel 7:9; Isaiah 45:12"> He wraps Himself in light as with a garment; He stretches out the heavens like a tent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 9:6; 2 Samuel 22:11; Isaiah 19:1"> laying the beams of His chambers in the waters above, making the clouds His chariot, walking on the wings of the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; 2 Kings 2:11; 2 Kings 6:17"> He makes the winds His messengers, flames of fire His servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 26:7; Psalm 24:2; Psalm 33:9"> He set the earth on its foundations, never to be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:19; 2 Peter 3:5; Genesis 1:2–10"> You covered it with the deep like a garment; the waters stood above the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 18:15; Genesis 8:1; Mark 4:39"> At Your rebuke the waters fled; at the sound of Your thunder they hurried away—</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 8:5; Psalm 33:7"> the mountains rose and the valleys sank to the place You assigned for them—</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Genesis 9:11–15; Jeremiah 5:22"> You set a boundary they cannot cross, that they may never again cover the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:18; Psalm 107:35; Isaiah 35:7"> He sends forth springs in the valleys; they flow between the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 145:16; Job 39:5–8; Psalm 104:13"> They give drink to every beast of the field; the wild donkeys quench their thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 50:11; Psalm 148:10; Matthew 8:20"> The birds of the air nest beside the springs; they sing among the branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 147:8; Jeremiah 10:13; Jeremiah 14:22"> He waters the mountains from His chambers; the earth is satisfied by the fruit of His works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 136:25; Job 28:5; Psalm 147:8–9"> He makes the grass grow for the livestock and provides crops for man to cultivate, bringing forth food from the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 23:5; Ecclesiastes 10:19; Ecclesiastes 9:7"> wine that gladdens the heart of man, oil that makes his face to shine, and bread that sustains his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 24:6; Psalm 92:2; Psalm 29:5"> The trees of the LORD have their fill, the cedars of Lebanon that He planted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 104:12; Leviticus 11:19; Daniel 4:21"> where the birds build their nests; the stork makes her home in the cypresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 30:26; Job 39:1; Leviticus 11:5"> The high mountains are for the wild goats, the cliffs a refuge for the rock badgers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalm 136:7–9"> He made the moon to mark the seasons; the sun knows when to set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Psalm 74:16; Amos 1:13"> You bring darkness, and it becomes night, when all the beasts of the forest prowl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 38:39; Joel 1:20; Psalm 34:10"> The young lions roar for their prey and seek their food from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 37:8; Nahum 3:17; John 3:20"> The sun rises, and they withdraw; they lie down in their dens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 5:12; Ephesians 4:28"> Man goes forth to his work and to his labor until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:12; Psalm 40:5; Psalm 50:10–12"> How many are Your works, O LORD! In wisdom You have made them all; the earth is full of Your creatures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 69:34; Psalm 95:4–5; Genesis 1:28"> Here is the sea, vast and wide, teeming with creatures beyond number, living things both great and small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 107:23; Isaiah 27:1; Psalm 74:14"> There the ships pass, and Leviathan, which You formed to frolic there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalm 136:25; Psalm 147:9; Job 38:41"> All creatures look to You to give them their food in due season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalm 145:16"> When You give it to them, they gather it up; when You open Your hand, they are satisfied with good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 34:14–15; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Psalm 146:4"> When You hide Your face, they are terrified; when You take away their breath, they die and return to dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 33:4; Ezekiel 37:9; Revelation 21:5"> When You send Your Spirit, they are created, and You renew the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 1:31; 2 Timothy 4:18; Ephesians 3:21"> May the glory of the LORD endure forever; may the LORD rejoice in His works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalm 144:5; Psalm 97:4–5; Exodus 19:18"> He looks on the earth, and it trembles; He touches the mountains, and they smolder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalm 63:4; Psalm 146:2; Psalm 145:1–2"> I will sing to the LORD all my life; I will sing praise to my God while I have my being.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:17–18; Philippians 4:4; Psalm 1:2"> May my meditation be pleasing to Him, for I rejoice in the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalm 103:22–1; Psalm 37:38; Psalm 106:48"> May sinners vanish from the earth and the wicked be no more. Bless the LORD, O my soul. Hallelujah!</span>
</div>
