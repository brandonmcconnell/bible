<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 32:11; Philippians 4:4; Psalm 147:1"> Rejoice in the LORD, O righteous ones; it is fitting for the upright to praise Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 150:3–6; Psalm 98:4–5; Psalm 144:9"> Praise the LORD with the harp; make music to Him with ten strings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 5:19; Isaiah 42:10; 1 Chronicles 25:7"> Sing to Him a new song; play skillfully with a shout of joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 12:6; Titus 1:2; Deuteronomy 32:4"> For the word of the LORD is upright, and all His work is trustworthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 11:7; Psalm 119:64; Psalm 104:24"> The LORD loves righteousness and justice; the earth is full of His loving devotion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 11:3; 2 Peter 3:5; Job 33:4"> By the word of the LORD the heavens were made, and all the stars by the breath of His mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 15:8; Joshua 3:16; Joshua 3:13"> He piles up the waters of the sea; He puts the depths into storehouses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 14:6–7; Psalm 96:9–10; Psalm 22:27"> Let all the earth fear the LORD; let all the people of the world revere Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 1:3; Psalm 33:6; Psalm 148:5–6"> For He spoke, and it came to be; He commanded, and it stood firm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 21:11; Job 5:12–13; Isaiah 44:25"> The LORD frustrates the plans of the nations; He thwarts the devices of the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 46:10; Job 23:13"> The counsel of the LORD stands forever, the purposes of His heart to all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 144:15; 1 Peter 2:9; John 15:16"> Blessed is the nation whose God is the LORD, the people He has chosen as His inheritance!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 28:24; Psalm 11:4; Psalm 53:2"> The LORD looks down from heaven; He sees all the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:39; 1 Kings 8:27; Isaiah 57:15"> From His dwelling place He gazes on all who inhabit the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:19; Psalm 44:21; Hosea 7:2"> He shapes the hearts of each; He considers all their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 44:3; Jeremiah 9:23; Psalm 44:6–7"> No king is saved by his vast army; no warrior is delivered by his great strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 21:31; Psalm 20:7; Psalm 147:10"> A horse is a vain hope for salvation; even its great strength cannot save.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Psalm 147:11; Job 36:7"> Surely the eyes of the LORD are on those who fear Him, on those whose hope is in His loving devotion</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 37:19; Proverbs 10:3; Job 5:19–22"> to deliver them from death and keep them alive in famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Psalm 27:14; Psalm 130:5–6"> Our soul waits for the LORD; He is our help and our shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 13:5; Zechariah 10:7; Psalm 30:10–12"> For our hearts rejoice in Him, since we trust in His holy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 13:5; Psalm 119:76; Matthew 9:29"> May Your loving devotion rest on us, O LORD, as we put our hope in You.</span>
