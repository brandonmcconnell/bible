<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Psalm 79:13; Psalm 100:3"> A Maskil of Asaph. Why have You rejected us forever, O God? Why does Your anger smolder against the sheep of Your pasture?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Exodus 15:16; Exodus 15:13"> Remember Your congregation, which You purchased long ago and redeemed as the tribe of Your inheritance—Mount Zion, where You dwell.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 1:10; Psalm 79:1; Daniel 9:17"> Turn Your steps to the everlasting ruins, to everything in the sanctuary the enemy has destroyed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Lamentations 2:7; Matthew 24:15; Daniel 6:27"> Your foes have roared within Your meeting place; they have unfurled their banners as signs,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 5:6; 2 Chronicles 2:14; Jeremiah 46:22–23"> like men wielding axes in a thicket of trees</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 6:18; 1 Kings 6:29; 1 Kings 6:35"> and smashing all the carvings with hatchets and picks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 25:9; Psalm 89:39; Ezekiel 24:21"> They have burned Your sanctuary to the ground; they have defiled the dwelling place of Your Name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 83:4; 2 Kings 4:23; Esther 3:8–9"> They said in their hearts, “We will crush them completely.” They burned down every place where God met us in the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 8:11; Ezekiel 7:26; 1 Samuel 3:1"> There are no signs for us to see. There is no longer any prophet. And none of us knows how long this will last.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 89:50–51; Psalm 44:16; Psalm 89:46"> How long, O God, will the enemy taunt You? Will the foe revile Your name forever?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 2:3; Psalm 78:65–66; Psalm 44:23"> Why do You withdraw Your strong right hand? Stretch it out to destroy them!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 44:4; Exodus 19:5–6; Isaiah 33:22"> Yet God is my King from ancient times, working salvation on the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Psalm 78:13; Ezekiel 29:3"> You divided the sea by Your strength; You smashed the heads of the dragons of the sea;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 27:1; Psalm 72:9; Psalm 104:25–26"> You crushed the heads of Leviathan; You fed him to the creatures of the desert.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; Isaiah 48:21; Psalm 105:41"> You broke open the fountain and the flood; You dried up the ever-flowing rivers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 136:7–9; Matthew 5:45; Psalm 104:19"> The day is Yours, and also the night; You established the moon and the sun.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:8; Acts 17:26; Genesis 8:22"> You set all the boundaries of the earth; You made the summer and winter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 39:8; Revelation 16:19; Deuteronomy 32:6"> Remember how the enemy has mocked You, O LORD, how a foolish people has spurned Your name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:14; Matthew 10:16; Psalm 9:18"> Do not deliver the soul of Your dove to beasts; do not forget the lives of Your afflicted forever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 106:45; Genesis 17:7–8; Hebrews 8:10"> Consider Your covenant, for haunts of violence fill the dark places of the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 12:5; Psalm 35:10; Psalm 9:18"> Do not let the oppressed retreat in shame; may the poor and needy praise Your name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 74:18; Psalm 53:1; Isaiah 52:5"> Rise up, O God; defend Your cause! Remember how the fool mocks You all day long.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Psalm 65:7; Jonah 1:2"> Do not disregard the clamor of Your adversaries, the uproar of Your enemies that ascends continually.</span>
</div>
</div>
