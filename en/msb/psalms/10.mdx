<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 59:12; Psalm 36:11; Psalm 7:16"> In pride the wicked pursue the needy; let them be caught in the schemes they devise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 94:4; Luke 12:19; Zechariah 11:5–8"> For the wicked man boasts in the cravings of his heart; he blesses the greedy and reviles the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 14:1–2; Acts 8:22; Romans 1:28"> In his pride the wicked man does not seek Him; in all his schemes there is no God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 92:5–6; Isaiah 26:11; Psalm 12:5"> He is secure in his ways at all times; Your lofty judgments are far from him; he sneers at all his foes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:11; Matthew 24:48; Isaiah 56:12"> He says to himself, “I will not be moved; from age to age I am free of distress.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 7:14; Psalm 140:9; Psalm 140:3"> His mouth is full of cursing, deceit, and violence; trouble and malice are under his tongue.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:14; 1 Samuel 22:18; Jeremiah 22:17"> He lies in wait near the villages; in ambush he slays the innocent; his eyes watch in stealth for the helpless.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 17:12; Psalm 59:3; Micah 7:2"> He lies in wait like a lion in a thicket; he lurks to seize the oppressed; he catches the lowly in his net.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:21–22; 1 Samuel 18:21–26; 2 Samuel 15:5"> They are crushed and beaten down; the helpless fall prey to his strength.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Psalm 94:7; Psalm 73:11"> He says to himself, “God has forgotten; He hides His face and never sees.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 5:9; Psalm 9:12; Psalm 94:2"> Arise, O LORD! Lift up Your hand, O God! Do not forget the helpless.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:9–10; Psalm 74:10; Genesis 9:5"> Why has the wicked man renounced God? He says to himself, “You will never call me to account.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 68:5; Psalm 146:9; Psalm 55:22"> But You have regarded trouble and grief; You see to repay it by Your hand. The victim entrusts himself to You; You are the helper of the fatherless.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:12; Psalm 37:17; Job 38:15"> Break the arm of the wicked and evildoer; call him to account for his wickedness until none is left to be found.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 29:10; Jeremiah 10:10; 1 Timothy 1:17"> The LORD is King forever and ever; the nations perish from His land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 145:19; 2 Chronicles 34:27; Psalm 34:15"> You have heard, O LORD, the desire of the humble; You will strengthen their hearts. You will incline Your ear,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 9:9; Psalm 82:3; Isaiah 11:4"> to vindicate the fatherless and oppressed, that the men of the earth may strike terror no more.</span>
</div>
</div>
