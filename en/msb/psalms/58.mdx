<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 57:1; Deuteronomy 16:18–19; Psalm 72:1–4"> For the choirmaster. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” A Miktam of David. Do you indeed speak justly, O rulers? Do you judge uprightly, O sons of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 94:20; Isaiah 10:1; Ezekiel 22:27"> No, in your hearts you devise injustice; with your hands you mete out violence on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 51:5; Isaiah 48:8; Proverbs 22:15"> The wicked are estranged from the womb; the liars go astray from birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 140:3; James 3:8; Ecclesiastes 10:11"> Their venom is like the venom of a snake, like a cobra that shuts its ears,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:17; Isaiah 19:3; Deuteronomy 18:11"> refusing to hear the tune of the charmer who skillfully weaves his spell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 3:7; Job 4:10–11; Hosea 5:14"> O God, shatter their teeth in their mouths; O LORD, tear out the fangs of the lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 112:10; Joshua 7:5; Psalm 64:3"> May they vanish like water that runs off; when they draw the bow, may their arrows be blunted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 3:16; Ecclesiastes 6:3; Psalm 37:35–36"> Like a slug that dissolves in its slime, like a woman’s stillborn child, may they never see the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 118:12; Proverbs 10:25; Ecclesiastes 7:6"> Before your pots can feel the burning thorns—whether green or dry—He will sweep them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 68:23; Psalm 91:8; Psalm 64:10"> The righteous will rejoice when they see they are avenged; they will wash their feet in the blood of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 67:4; Isaiah 3:10; Psalm 64:9"> Then men will say, “There is surely a reward for the righteous! There is surely a God who judges the earth!”</span>
