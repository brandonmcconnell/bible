<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:21; Psalm 66:3; Revelation 15:3–4"> With awesome deeds of righteousness You answer us, O God of our salvation, the hope of all the ends of the earth and of the farthest seas.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 147:8; 1 Corinthians 3:6–7; Deuteronomy 32:2"> You soak its furrows and level its ridges; You soften it with showers and bless its growth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joel 2:21–26; Psalm 103:4; Psalm 5:12"> You crown the year with Your bounty, and Your paths overflow with plenty.</span>
