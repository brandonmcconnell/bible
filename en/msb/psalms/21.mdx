<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Psalm 62:7; Psalm 28:7"> For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. O LORD, the king rejoices in Your strength. How greatly he exults in Your salvation!</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:30; Ephesians 1:3; Psalm 59:10"> For You welcomed him with rich blessings; You placed on his head a crown of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 91:16; Revelation 1:18; Psalm 119:77"> He asked You for life, and You granted it—length of days, forever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; John 17:22; Revelation 5:8–13"> Great is his glory in Your salvation; You bestow on him splendor and majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 2:28; Psalm 45:7; Psalm 16:11"> For You grant him blessings forever; You cheer him with joy in Your presence.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 37:28; 1 Kings 13:34; Job 18:16–19"> You will wipe their descendants from the earth, and their offspring from the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 10:2; Psalm 2:1; Psalm 31:13"> Though they intend You harm, the schemes they devise will not prevail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 7:12–13; Psalm 18:40; Psalm 64:7"> For You will put them to flight when Your bow is trained upon them.</span>
