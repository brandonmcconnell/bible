<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:11; Psalm 139:17–18; Exodus 15:11"> Many, O LORD my God, are the wonders You have done, and the plans You have for us—none can compare to You—if I proclaim and declare them, they are more than I can count.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5–12; Isaiah 1:11; 1 Samuel 15:22"> Sacrifice and offering You did not desire, but my ears You have opened. Burnt offerings and sin offerings You did not require.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:7–9; Luke 24:44; John 5:39"> Then I said, “Here I am, I have come—it is written about me in the scroll:</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:22; John 4:34; Jeremiah 15:16"> I delight to do Your will, O my God; Your law is within my heart.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 22:25; Mark 16:15–16; Psalm 22:22"> I proclaim righteousness in the great assembly; behold, I do not seal my lips, as You, O LORD, do know.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 1:16–17; Acts 20:20–21; Romans 10:9–10"> I have not covered up Your righteousness in my heart; I have declared Your faithfulness and salvation; I have not concealed Your loving devotion and faithfulness from the great assembly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 23:6; Psalm 43:3; Psalm 57:3"> O LORD, do not withhold Your mercy from me; Your loving devotion and faithfulness will always guard me.</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 25:17–18; Psalm 70:1–5; Psalm 38:22"> Be pleased, O LORD, to deliver me; hurry, O LORD, to help me.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 35:26; Psalm 35:4; Psalm 71:13"> May those who seek my life be ashamed and confounded; may those who wish me harm be repelled and humiliated.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 35:21; Psalm 69:24–25; Psalm 35:25"> May those who say to me, “Aha, aha!” be appalled at their own shame.</span>
