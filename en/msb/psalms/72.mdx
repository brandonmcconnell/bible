<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; 1 Chronicles 22:12–13; Isaiah 11:2"> Of Solomon. Endow the king with Your justice, O God, and the son of the king with Your righteousness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 32:1; Psalm 72:12–14; Revelation 19:11"> May he judge Your people with righteousness and Your afflicted with justice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Psalm 96:11–13; Psalm 72:16"> May the mountains bring peace to the people, and the hills bring righteousness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 11:4; Ezekiel 34:15–16; Psalm 109:31"> May he vindicate the afflicted among the people; may he save the children of the needy and crush the oppressor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 89:36–37; Psalm 72:17; Daniel 7:14"> May they fear him as long as the sun shines, as long as the moon remains, through all generations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; Deuteronomy 32:2; 2 Samuel 23:4"> May he be like rain that falls on freshly cut grass, like spring showers that water the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:4; Malachi 4:2; Psalm 92:12"> May the righteous flourish in his days and prosperity abound until the moon is no more.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Zechariah 9:10; Psalm 80:11"> May he rule from sea to sea, and from the Euphrates to the ends of the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Micah 7:17; Luke 19:27"> May the nomads bow before him, and his enemies lick the dust.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6; Psalm 68:29; Isaiah 49:7"> May the kings of Tarshish and distant shores bring tribute; may the kings of Sheba and Seba offer gifts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 86:9; Isaiah 49:22–23; Revelation 11:15"> May all kings bow down to him and all nations serve him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 29:12; Revelation 3:17–18; Psalm 102:17"> For he will deliver the needy who cry out and the afflicted who have no helper.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Psalm 109:31; Matthew 5:3"> He will take pity on the poor and needy and save the lives of the oppressed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 116:15; Revelation 19:2; Psalm 69:18"> He will redeem them from oppression and violence, for their blood is precious in his sight.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:14; Revelation 5:8–14; Jude 1:25"> Long may he live! May gold from Sheba be given him. May people ever pray for him; may they bless him all day long.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Psalm 92:12–14; Isaiah 30:23"> May there be an abundance of grain in the land; may it sway atop the hills. May its fruit trees flourish like the forests of Lebanon, the people of its cities like the grass of the field.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:23; Psalm 89:36"> May his name endure forever; may his name continue as long as the sun shines. In him may all nations be blessed; may they call him blessed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 41:13; Psalm 106:48; 1 Chronicles 29:10"> Blessed be the LORD God, the God of Israel, who alone does marvelous deeds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:21; Matthew 6:10; Psalm 41:13"> And blessed be His glorious name forever; may all the earth be filled with His glory. Amen and amen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:1; Job 31:40; Jeremiah 51:64"> Thus conclude the prayers of David son of Jesse.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
