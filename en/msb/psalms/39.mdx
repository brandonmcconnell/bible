<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Psalm 141:3; James 1:26"> For the choirmaster. For Jeduthun. A Psalm of David. I said, “I will watch my ways so that I will not sin with my tongue; I will guard my mouth with a muzzle as long as the wicked are present.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:20; Psalm 38:13–14; Job 32:19–20"> I was speechless and still; I remained silent, even from speaking good, and my sorrow was stirred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:32; Jeremiah 20:9; Ezekiel 3:14"> My heart grew hot within me; as I mused, the fire burned. Then I spoke with my tongue:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 90:12; Psalm 103:14; Job 14:13"> “Show me, O LORD, my end and the measure of my days. Let me know how fleeting my life is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 90:9–10; Psalm 144:4; Psalm 89:47"> You, indeed, have made my days as handbreadths, and my lifetime as nothing before You. Truly each man at his best exists as but a breath. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:20–21; James 4:14; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> Surely every man goes about like a phantom; surely he bustles in vain; he heaps up riches not knowing who will haul them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Psalm 38:15; Psalm 119:81"> And now, O Lord, for what do I wait? My hope is in You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 44:13; Psalm 65:3; Joel 2:17"> Deliver me from all my transgressions; do not make me the reproach of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 2:10; 2 Samuel 16:10; Leviticus 10:3"> I have become mute; I do not open my mouth because of what You have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:34; Job 13:21; Psalm 25:16–17"> Remove Your scourge from me; I am perishing by the force of Your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:28; 1 Corinthians 11:30–32; Isaiah 50:9"> You discipline and correct a man for his iniquity, consuming like a moth what he holds dear; surely each man is but a vapor. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Hebrews 11:13; 2 Kings 20:5"> Hear my prayer, O LORD, and give ear to my cry for help; do not be deaf to my weeping. For I am a foreigner dwelling with You, a stranger like all my fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 10:20–21; Job 14:10–12; Job 14:5–6"> Turn Your gaze away from me, that I may again be cheered before I depart and am no more.”</span>
