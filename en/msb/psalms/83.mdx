<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 28:1; Psalm 35:22; Psalm 50:3"> A song. A Psalm of Asaph. O God, be not silent; be not speechless; be not still, O God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 81:15; Matthew 27:24; Judges 8:28"> See how Your enemies rage, how Your foes have reared their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 64:2; Psalm 27:5; Psalm 31:20"> With cunning they scheme against Your people and conspire against those You cherish,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:2; Jeremiah 11:19; Psalm 74:8"> saying, “Come, let us erase them as a nation; may the name of Israel be remembered no more.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 2:2; Revelation 17:13; Isaiah 8:9–10"> For with one mind they plot together; they form an alliance against You—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:10; 2 Chronicles 20:1; Psalm 137:7"> the tents of Edom and the Ishmaelites, of Moab and the Hagrites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 13:5; Ezekiel 27:3; Ezekiel 27:9"> of Gebal, Ammon, and Amalek, of Philistia with the people of Tyre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:9; Genesis 25:3; 2 Kings 15:19"> Even Assyria has joined them, lending strength to the sons of Lot. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 4:15–24; Isaiah 9:4; Judges 5:21"> Do to them as You did to Midian, as to Sisera and Jabin at the River Kishon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:17; Joshua 17:11; 1 Samuel 28:7"> who perished at Endor and became like dung on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 7:25; Judges 8:12–21"> Make their nobles like Oreb and Zeeb, and all their princes like Zebah and Zalmunna,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:11; Psalm 83:4; Psalm 74:7–8"> who said, “Let us possess for ourselves the pastures of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 21:18; Psalm 35:5; Jeremiah 13:24"> Make them like tumbleweed, O my God, like chaff before the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:22; Isaiah 9:18; Malachi 4:1"> As fire consumes a forest, as a flame sets the mountains ablaze,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 9:17; Psalm 50:3; Psalm 58:9"> so pursue them with Your tempest, and terrify them with Your storm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 9:19–20; Psalm 6:10; Psalm 109:29"> Cover their faces with shame, that they may seek Your name, O LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 35:4; Psalm 40:14–15; Psalm 35:26"> May they be ever ashamed and terrified; may they perish in disgrace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 6:3; Psalm 59:13; Jeremiah 16:21"> May they know that You alone, whose name is the LORD, are Most High over all the earth.</span>
