<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 103:22–1; Psalm 103:1; Psalm 104:35"> Hallelujah! Praise the LORD, O my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 63:4; Psalm 104:33; Psalm 71:14–15"> I will praise the LORD all my life; I will sing praises to my God while I have my being.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 118:8–9; Jeremiah 17:5–6; Isaiah 2:22"> Put not your trust in princes, in mortal man, who cannot save.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Psalm 104:29; Genesis 2:7"> When his spirit departs, he returns to the ground; on that very day his plans perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; Psalm 144:15; Psalm 71:5"> Blessed is he whose help is the God of Jacob, whose hope is in the LORD his God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Revelation 14:7; Psalm 136:5–6"> the Maker of heaven and earth, the sea, and everything in them. He remains faithful forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 103:6; Psalm 68:6; Isaiah 61:1"> He executes justice for the oppressed and gives food to the hungry. The LORD sets the prisoners free,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 11:7; Psalm 145:14; Matthew 9:30"> the LORD opens the eyes of the blind, the LORD lifts those who are weighed down, the LORD loves the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 68:5; Psalm 147:6; Deuteronomy 10:18–19"> The LORD protects foreigners; He sustains the fatherless and the widow, but the ways of the wicked He frustrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 10:16; Exodus 15:18; Revelation 11:15"> The LORD reigns forever, your God, O Zion, for all generations. Hallelujah!</span>
