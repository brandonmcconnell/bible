<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 11:23; Job 23:10; Psalm 26:2"> You have tried my heart; You have visited me in the night. You have tested me and found no evil; I have resolved not to sin with my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Psalm 119:9–11; 1 Corinthians 3:3"> As for the deeds of men—by the word of Your lips I have avoided the ways of the violent.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 116:2; Psalm 86:7; Psalm 88:2"> I call on You, O God, for You will answer me. Incline Your ear to me; hear my words.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:3; Psalm 123:4; Psalm 31:18"> They have closed their callous hearts; their mouths speak with arrogance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 37:14; Proverbs 6:13–14; Psalm 10:8–10"> They have tracked us down, and now surround us; their eyes are set to cast us to the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 7:2; 1 Peter 5:8; Psalm 10:9"> like a lion greedy for prey, like a young lion lurking in ambush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 44:26; Psalm 44:23; Psalm 7:6"> Arise, O LORD, confront them! Bring them to their knees; deliver me from the wicked by Your sword,</span>
