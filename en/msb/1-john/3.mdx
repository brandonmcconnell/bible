<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 1:12; Galatians 3:26; 2 Corinthians 6:18"> Behold what manner of love the Father has given to us, that we should be called children of God. The reason the world does not know us is that it did not know Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:21; Colossians 3:4; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> Beloved, we are now children of God, and what we will be has not yet been revealed. We know that when Christ appears, we will be like Him, for we will see Him as He is.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 John 2:6; 2 Peter 3:14"> And everyone who has this hope in Him purifies himself, just as Christ is pure.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 5:17; Romans 3:20; Romans 4:15"> Everyone who practices sin practices lawlessness as well. Indeed, sin is lawlessness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 9:28; 1 Peter 3:18"> But you know that Christ appeared to take away our sins, and in Him there is no sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 John 1:11; 1 John 2:4; 1 John 3:9"> No one who remains in Him keeps on sinning. No one who continues to sin has seen Him or known Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 2:29; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Romans 2:13"> Little children, let no one deceive you: The one who practices righteousness is righteous, just as Christ is righteous.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14; John 8:44; Colossians 2:15"> The one who practices sin is of the devil, because the devil has been sinning from the very start. This is why the Son of God was revealed, to destroy the works of the devil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 5:18; 1 Peter 1:23; John 1:13"> Anyone born of God refuses to practice sin, because God’s seed abides in him; he cannot go on sinning, because he has been born of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 4:8; 3 John 1:11; Luke 6:35"> By this the children of God are distinguished from the children of the devil: Anyone who does not practice righteousness is not of God, nor is anyone who does not love his brother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 15:12; 2 John 1:5; 1 John 4:7"> This is the message you have heard from the beginning: We should love one another.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Genesis 4:4–15; Proverbs 29:27"> Do not be like Cain, who belonged to the evil one and murdered his brother. And why did Cain slay him? Because his own deeds were evil, while those of his brother were righteous.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 17:14; Luke 21:17"> So do not be surprised, my brothers, if the world hates you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 13:35; 1 John 5:2; 1 John 4:20–21"> We know that we have passed from death to life, because we love our brothers. The one who does not love his brother remains in death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:21–22; Proverbs 26:24–26; Revelation 21:8"> Everyone who hates his brother is a murderer, and you know that eternal life does not reside in a murderer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:9–11; Ephesians 5:2; John 13:34"> By this we know what love is: Jesus laid down His life for us, and we ought to lay down our lives for our brothers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Isaiah 58:7–10; Deuteronomy 15:7–11"> If anyone with earthly possessions sees his brother in need, but withholds his compassion from him, how can the love of God abide in him?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 2:15–16; Romans 12:9; 1 Peter 1:22"> My little children, let us love not in word and speech, but in action and truth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; 1 John 3:21; Hebrews 6:10–11"> And by this we know that we belong to the truth, and assure our hearts in His presence:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 139:1–4; Jeremiah 17:10; Hebrews 4:13"> Even if our hearts condemn us, God is greater than our hearts, and He knows all things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:28; Hebrews 4:16; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> Beloved, if our hearts do not condemn us, we have confidence before God,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 15:7; John 9:31; 1 John 5:14"> and we will receive from Him whatever we ask, because we keep His commandments and do what is pleasing in His sight.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 6:29; John 13:34; 1 Peter 1:22"> And this is His commandment: that we should believe in the name of His Son, Jesus Christ, and we should love one another just as He commanded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16; 1 John 4:15–16; 1 John 4:12–13"> Whoever keeps His commandments remains in God, and God in him. And by this we know that He remains in us: by the Spirit He has given us.</span>
</div>
</div>
