</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 2:22; 2 John 1:7; 1 John 2:18"> and every spirit that does not confess that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is not from God. This is the spirit of the antichrist, which you have heard is coming and which is already in the world at this time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:31; 1 John 5:4; Romans 8:37"> You, little children, are from God and have overcome them, because greater is He who is in you than he who is in the world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:23; 2 Peter 2:2–3; John 17:14"> They are of the world. That is why they speak from the world’s perspective, and the world listens to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 14:17; John 10:27; 1 Corinthians 2:12–14"> We are from God. Whoever knows God listens to us; whoever is not from God does not listen to us. That is how we know the Spirit of truth and the spirit of deception.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 4:20–1; 1 John 4:8; 1 Peter 1:22"> Beloved, let us love one another, because love comes from God. Everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 4:16; Psalm 86:15; 2 Corinthians 13:11"> Whoever does not love does not know God, because God is love.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:16; 1 John 5:11; Romans 8:32"> This is how God’s love was revealed among us: God sent His one and only Son into the world, so that we might live through Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–5; Romans 5:8–10; 1 John 4:19"> And love consists in this: not that we loved God, but that He loved us and sent His Son as the atoning sacrifice for our sins.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 15:12–13; Colossians 3:13; John 13:34"> Beloved, if God so loved us, we also ought to love one another.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 1:18; 1 John 2:5; 1 John 4:17–18"> No one has ever seen God; but if we love one another, God remains in us, and His love is perfected in us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 John 3:24; 1 Corinthians 3:16–17"> By this we know that we remain in Him, and He in us: He has given us of His Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:10; John 3:16–17; John 4:42"> And we have seen and testify that the Father has sent His Son to be the Savior of the world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 10:9; 1 John 5:5; Matthew 10:32"> If anyone confesses that Jesus is the Son of God, God abides in him, and he in God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 3:24; Psalm 36:7–9; 1 John 3:1"> And we have come to know and believe the love that God has for us. God is love; whoever abides in love abides in God, and God in him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 4:12; 1 John 2:28; Romans 8:29"> In this way, love has been perfected among us, so that we may have confidence on the day of judgment; for in this world we are just like Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7; Romans 8:15; 1 John 4:12"> There is no fear in love, but perfect love drives out fear, because fear involves punishment. The one who fears has not been perfected in love.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 4:10; John 3:16; John 15:16"> We love Him because He first loved us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 John 3:17; 1 John 2:9; 1 John 4:12"> If anyone says, “I love God,” but hates his brother, he is a liar. For anyone who does not love his brother, whom he has seen, how can he love God, whom he has not seen?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 22:37–39; Leviticus 19:18; 1 John 3:11"> And we have this commandment from Him: Whoever loves God must love his brother as well.</span>
</div>
