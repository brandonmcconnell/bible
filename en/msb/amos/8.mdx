<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 7:1; Amos 7:7; Amos 7:4"> This is what the Lord GOD showed me: I saw a basket of summer fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 7:8; Lamentations 4:18; Jeremiah 24:1–3"> “Amos, what do you see?” He asked. “A basket of summer fruit,” I replied. So the LORD said to me, “The end has come for My people Israel; I will no longer spare them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 5:23; Amos 6:9–10; Jeremiah 9:21–22"> “In that day,” declares the Lord GOD, “the songs of the temple will turn to wailing. Many will be the corpses, strewn in silence everywhere!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:14; Psalm 14:4; Amos 5:11"> Hear this, you who trample the needy, who do away with the poor of the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15–21; Micah 6:10–11; Hosea 12:7"> asking, “When will the New Moon be over, that we may sell grain? When will the Sabbath end, that we may market wheat? Let us reduce the ephah and increase the shekel; let us cheat with dishonest scales.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 2:6; Leviticus 25:39–42; Nehemiah 5:8"> Let us buy the poor with silver and the needy for a pair of sandals, selling even the chaff with the wheat!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Hosea 9:9; Hosea 8:13"> The LORD has sworn by the Pride of Jacob: “I will never forget any of their deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 9:5; Hosea 4:3; Jeremiah 46:8"> Will not the land quake for this, and all its dwellers mourn? All of it will swell like the Nile; it will surge and then subside like the Nile in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 15:33; Matthew 27:45; Jeremiah 15:9"> And in that day, declares the Lord GOD, I will make the sun go down at noon, and I will darken the earth in the daytime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Zechariah 12:10; Job 20:23"> I will turn your feasts into mourning and all your songs into lamentation. I will cause everyone to wear sackcloth and every head to be shaved. I will make it like a time of mourning for an only son, and its outcome like a bitter day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 74:9; 1 Samuel 3:1; 2 Chronicles 15:3"> Behold, the days are coming, declares the Lord GOD, when I will send a famine on the land—not a famine of bread or a thirst for water, but a famine of hearing the words of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:7–10; Matthew 24:23–26; Daniel 12:4"> People will stagger from sea to sea and roam from north to east, seeking the word of the LORD, but they will not find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Lamentations 1:18; Lamentations 2:21"> In that day the lovely young women—the young men as well—will faint from thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:5; 1 Kings 12:28–29; Deuteronomy 9:21"> Those who swear by the guilt of Samaria and say, ‘As surely as your god lives, O Dan,’ or, ‘As surely as the way of Beersheba lives’—they will fall, never to rise again.”</span>
