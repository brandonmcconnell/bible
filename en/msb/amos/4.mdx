<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 2:17; Jeremiah 5:3; Isaiah 9:13"> “I afflicted all your cities with cleanness of teeth and all your towns with lack of bread, yet you did not return to Me,” declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 9:26; Jeremiah 3:3; 2 Chronicles 7:13–14"> “I also withheld the rain from you when the harvest was three months away. I sent rain on one city but withheld it from another. One field received rain; another without rain withered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 4:6; 1 Kings 18:5; Haggai 1:6"> People staggered from city to city for water to drink, but they were not satisfied; yet you did not return to Me,” declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:22; Haggai 2:17; Joel 1:4"> “I struck you with blight and mildew in your growing gardens and vineyards; the locust devoured your fig and olive trees, yet you did not return to Me,” declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:60; Leviticus 26:25; Joel 2:20"> “I sent plagues among you like those of Egypt; I killed your young men with the sword, along with your captured horses. I filled your nostrils with the stench of your camp, yet you did not return to Me,” declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 13:19; Zechariah 3:2; Genesis 19:24–25"> “Some of you I overthrew as I overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah, and you were like a firebrand snatched from a blaze, yet you did not return to Me,” declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–4; James 4:1–10; Amos 9:1–4"> “Therefore, that is what I will do to you, O Israel, and since I will do this to you, prepare to meet your God, O Israel!</span>
</div>
