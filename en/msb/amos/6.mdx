</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nahum 3:8; Genesis 10:10; 2 Kings 18:34"> Cross over to Calneh and see; go from there to the great Hamath; then go down to Gath of the Philistines. Are you better than these kingdoms? Is their territory larger than yours?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 9:10; Isaiah 56:12; Ezekiel 12:27"> You dismiss the day of calamity and bring near a reign of violence.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 3:12; Luke 16:19; 1 Samuel 25:36–38"> You lie on beds inlaid with ivory, and lounge upon your couches. You dine on lambs from the flock and calves from the stall.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:12; Amos 5:23; Amos 8:3"> You improvise songs on the harp like David and invent your own musical instruments.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:22; 1 Corinthians 12:26; Matthew 26:7–9"> You drink wine by the bowlful and anoint yourselves with the finest oils, but you fail to grieve over the ruin of Joseph.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 7:11; Daniel 5:4–6; Amos 5:5"> Therefore, you will now go into exile as the first of the captives, and your feasting and lounging will come to an end.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:14; Amos 4:2; Amos 8:7"> The Lord GOD has sworn by Himself—the LORD, the God of Hosts, has declared: “I abhor Jacob’s pride and detest his citadels, so I will deliver up the city and everything in it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:3; Job 1:2; Psalm 109:13"> And if there are ten men left in one house, they too will die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 8:3; 1 Samuel 31:12; Amos 5:13"> And when the relative who is to burn the bodies picks them up to remove them from the house, he will call to one inside, “Is anyone else with you?” “None,” that person will answer. “Silence,” the relative will retort, “for the name of the LORD must not be invoked.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 3:15; Isaiah 55:11; 2 Kings 25:9"> For the LORD gives a command: “The great house will be smashed to pieces, and the small house to rubble.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 5:7; Hosea 10:4; Amos 5:11–12"> “Do horses gallop on the cliffs? Does one plow the sea with oxen? But you have turned justice into poison and the fruit of righteousness into wormwood—</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 28:14–15; Luke 12:19–20; 2 Kings 14:25"> you who rejoice in Lo-debar and say, ‘Did we not take Karnaim by our own strength?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:65; 2 Kings 14:25; Jeremiah 5:15–17"> For behold, I will raise up a nation against you, O house of Israel,” declares the LORD, the God of Hosts, “and they will oppress you from Lebo-hamath to the Brook of the Arabah.”</span>
</div>
