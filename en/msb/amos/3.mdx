<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 2:10; Jeremiah 8:3; Micah 3:1"> Hear this word that the LORD has spoken against you, O children of Israel, against the whole family that I brought up out of the land of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Exodus 19:5–6; Deuteronomy 10:15"> “Only you have I known from all the families of the earth; therefore I will punish you for all your iniquities.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–16; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 5:22"> Can two walk together without agreeing where to go?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 11:10; Psalm 104:21; Amos 1:2"> Does a lion roar in the forest when he has no prey? Does a young lion growl in his den if he has caught nothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:12; Jeremiah 31:28; Daniel 9:14"> Does a bird land in a snare where no bait has been set? Does a trap spring from the ground when it has nothing to catch?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Zephaniah 1:16; Isaiah 14:24–27"> If a ram’s horn sounds in a city, do the people not tremble? If calamity comes to a city, has not the LORD caused it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:15; Genesis 18:17; Psalm 25:14"> Surely the Lord GOD does nothing without revealing His plan to His servants the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 4:20; Jeremiah 20:9; Amos 3:4"> The lion has roared—who will not fear? The Lord GOD has spoken—who will not prophesy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 4:1; Amos 6:1; Amos 1:8"> Proclaim to the citadels of Ashdod and to the citadels of Egypt: “Assemble on the mountains of Samaria; see the great unrest in the city and the acts of oppression in her midst.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Zephaniah 1:9; Zechariah 5:3–4"> “For they know not how to do right,” declares the LORD. “They store up violence and destruction in their citadels.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 18:9–11; 2 Kings 17:3–6; 2 Kings 15:19"> Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: “An enemy will surround the land; he will pull down your strongholds and plunder your citadels.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:34–37; Romans 11:4–5; Isaiah 31:4"> This is what the LORD says: “As the shepherd snatches from the mouth of the lion two legs or a piece of an ear, so the Israelites dwelling in Samaria will be rescued having just the corner of a bed or the cushion of a couch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:18–19; 2 Chronicles 24:19; Acts 20:21"> Hear and testify against the house of Jacob, declares the Lord GOD, the God of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 23:15; Hosea 10:5–8; Hosea 10:14–15"> On the day I punish Israel for their transgressions, I will visit destruction on the altars of Bethel; the horns of the altar will be cut off, and they will fall to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 22:39; Jeremiah 36:22; Amos 6:11"> I will tear down the winter house along with the summer house; the houses of ivory will also perish, and the great houses will come to an end,” declares the LORD.</span>
