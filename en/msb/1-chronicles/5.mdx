</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 25:15; 1 Chronicles 1:31; 1 Chronicles 5:10"> They waged war against the Hagrites, as well as Jetur, Naphish, and Nodab.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:8; Romans 8:31; Joshua 23:10"> and many others fell slain, because the battle belonged to God. And they occupied the land until the exile.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:8–9; Song of Solomon 4:8; Psalm 133:3"> Now the people of the half-tribe of Manasseh were numerous. They settled in the land from Bashan to Baal-hermon (that is, Senir, also known as Mount Hermon).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:38"> These were the heads of their families: Epher, Ishi, Eliel, Azriel, Jeremiah, Hodaviah, and Jahdiel. They were mighty men of valor, famous men, and heads of their families.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 17:5; Psalm 106:34–39; Exodus 34:15"> But they were unfaithful to the God of their fathers, and they prostituted themselves with the gods of the peoples of the land, whom God had destroyed before them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 15:19; 2 Kings 17:6; 2 Kings 15:29"> So the God of Israel stirred up the spirit of Pul king of Assyria (that is, Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria) to take the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh into exile. And he brought them to Halah, Habor, Hara, and the river of Gozan, where they remain to this day.</span>
