<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:1–13; 1 Chronicles 10:8; 2 Samuel 1:6"> Now the Philistines fought against Israel, and the men of Israel fled before them, and many fell slain on Mount Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:39; Exodus 20:5; 1 Samuel 14:6"> The Philistines followed hard after Saul and his sons, and they killed Saul’s sons Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 49:23–24; 1 Samuel 31:3–6; 2 Samuel 1:4–10"> When the battle intensified against Saul, the archers overtook him and wounded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:4; Judges 9:54; 1 Samuel 17:26"> Then Saul said to his armor-bearer, “Draw your sword and run me through with it, or these uncircumcised men will come and torture me!” But his armor-bearer was terrified and refused to do it. So Saul took his own sword and fell on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> When his armor-bearer saw that Saul was dead, he too fell on his own sword and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 13:10–11; 1 Samuel 4:10–11; 1 Samuel 4:18"> So Saul died together with his three sons and all his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 26:36; Deuteronomy 28:33; Judges 6:2"> When all the Israelites in the valley saw that the army had fled and that Saul and his sons had died, they abandoned their cities and ran away. So the Philistines came and occupied their cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:8; 2 Kings 3:23; 2 Chronicles 20:25"> The next day, when the Philistines came to strip the dead, they found Saul and his sons fallen on Mount Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Judges 16:23–24; Matthew 14:11"> They stripped Saul, cut off his head, took his armor, and sent messengers throughout the land of the Philistines to proclaim the news in the temple of their idols and among their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:2–7; 1 Samuel 31:10"> They put his armor in the temple of their gods and hung his head in the temple of Dagon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:11–13; 1 Samuel 11:1–11; 2 Samuel 2:4–7"> When all the people of Jabesh-gilead heard about everything the Philistines had done to Saul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 35:8; 2 Samuel 3:35; Genesis 50:10"> all their men of valor set out and retrieved the bodies of Saul and his sons and brought them to Jabesh. And they buried their bones under the oak in Jabesh and fasted seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 20:6; Leviticus 19:31; 1 Samuel 15:23"> So Saul died for his unfaithfulness to the LORD, because he did not keep the word of the LORD and even consulted a medium for guidance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:28; 1 Samuel 13:14; 1 Samuel 28:17"> and he failed to inquire of the LORD. So the LORD put him to death and turned the kingdom over to David son of Jesse.</span>
