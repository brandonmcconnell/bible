<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Hosea 9:9; 1 Samuel 15:34"> Blow the ram’s horn in Gibeah, the trumpet in Ramah; raise the battle cry in Beth-aven: Lead on, O Benjamin!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 1:6; Isaiah 46:10; Hosea 9:11–17"> Ephraim will be laid waste on the day of rebuke. Among the tribes of Israel I proclaim what is certain.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Deuteronomy 27:17; Psalm 93:3–4"> The princes of Judah are like those who move boundary stones; I will pour out My fury upon them like water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Micah 6:16; 1 Kings 12:26–33"> Ephraim is oppressed, crushed in judgment, for he is determined to follow worthless idols.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 51:8; Job 13:28; Isaiah 50:9"> So I am like a moth to Ephraim, and like decay to the house of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 7:11; Hosea 12:1; Hosea 10:6"> When Ephraim saw his sickness and Judah his wound, then Ephraim turned to Assyria and sent to the great king. But he cannot cure you or heal your wound.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 7:2; Micah 5:8; Hosea 13:7–8"> For I am like a lion to Ephraim and like a young lion to the house of Judah. I, even I, will tear them to pieces and then go away. I will carry them off where no one can rescue them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 26:16; Jeremiah 29:12–14; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> Then I will return to My place until they admit their guilt and seek My face; in their affliction they will earnestly seek Me.”</span>
