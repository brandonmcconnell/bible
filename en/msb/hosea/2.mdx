<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10; Hosea 2:23; Zechariah 13:9"> “Say of your brothers, ‘My people,’ and of your sisters, ‘My loved one.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 50:1; Ezekiel 16:25; Ezekiel 23:45"> Rebuke your mother, rebuke her, for she is not My wife, and I am not her husband. Let her remove the adultery from her face and the unfaithfulness from between her breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:22; Isaiah 32:13–14; Ezekiel 19:13"> Otherwise, I will strip her naked and expose her like the day of her birth. I will make her like a desert and turn her into a parched land, and I will let her die of thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:14; Hosea 1:6; Ezekiel 8:18"> I will have no compassion on her children, because they are the children of adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:21; Hosea 3:1; Jeremiah 44:17–18"> For their mother has played the harlot and has conceived them in disgrace. For she thought, ‘I will go after my lovers, who give me bread and water, wool and linen, oil and drink.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 19:8; Job 3:23; Lamentations 3:7–9"> Therefore, behold, I will hedge up her path with thorns; I will enclose her with a wall, so she cannot find her way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:2; Hosea 5:13; Ezekiel 23:4"> She will pursue her lovers but not catch them; she will seek them but not find them. Then she will say, ‘I will return to my first husband, for then I was better off than now.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 1:3; Hosea 4:11; Hosea 13:2"> For she does not acknowledge that it was I who gave her grain, new wine, and oil, who lavished on her silver and gold—which they crafted for Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Haggai 1:6–11; Malachi 1:4"> Therefore I will take back My grain in its time and My new wine in its season; I will take away My wool and linen, which were given to cover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:29; Jeremiah 13:26; Hosea 13:7–8"> And then I will expose her lewdness in the sight of her lovers, and no one will deliver her out of My hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13–14; Amos 5:21; Jeremiah 7:34"> I will put an end to all her exultation: her feasts, New Moons, and Sabbaths—all her appointed feasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 7:23; Isaiah 5:5; Hosea 2:5"> I will destroy her vines and fig trees, which she thinks are the wages paid by her lovers. So I will make them into a thicket, and the beasts of the field will devour them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Judges 3:7; Hosea 13:1"> I will punish her for the days of the Baals when she burned incense to them, when she adorned herself with rings and jewelry, and went after her lovers. But Me she forgot,” declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:1–2; Revelation 12:6; Ezekiel 20:35–36"> “Therefore, behold, I will allure her and lead her to the wilderness, and speak to her tenderly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:2; Ezekiel 28:26; Hosea 11:1"> There I will give back her vineyards and make the Valley of Achor into a gateway of hope. There she will respond as she did in the days of her youth, as in the day she came up out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 54:5; Hosea 2:7; Jeremiah 3:14"> In that day,” declares the LORD, “you will call Me ‘my Husband,’ and no longer call Me ‘my Master.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 23:13; Joshua 23:7; Psalm 16:4"> For I will remove from her lips the names of the Baals; no longer will their names be invoked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:25; Ezekiel 39:9–10; Psalm 46:9"> On that day I will make a covenant for them with the beasts of the field and the birds of the air and the creatures that crawl on the ground. And I will abolish bow and sword and battle in the land, and will make them lie down in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:2; Revelation 21:2; Isaiah 54:8–10"> So I will betroth you to Me forever; I will betroth you in righteousness and justice, in loving devotion and compassion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 John 5:20; John 17:3; Jeremiah 24:7"> And I will betroth you in faithfulness, and you will know the LORD.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 8:12; Isaiah 65:24; Romans 8:32"> “On that day I will respond—” declares the LORD—“I will respond to the heavens, and they will respond to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joel 2:19; Hosea 1:4; Hosea 1:11"> And the earth will respond to the grain, to the new wine and oil, and they will respond to Jezreel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 9:25–26; 1 Peter 2:9–10; Zechariah 13:9"> And I will sow her as My own in the land, and I will have compassion on ‘No Compassion.’ I will say to those called ‘Not My People,’ ‘You are My people,’ and they will say, ‘You are my God.’”</span>
