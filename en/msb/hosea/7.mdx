<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 7:13; Micah 7:3–7; Hosea 6:4"> When I heal Israel, the iniquity of Ephraim will be exposed, as well as the crimes of Samaria. For they practice deceit and thieves break in; bandits raid in the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:2; Amos 8:7; Jeremiah 14:10"> But they fail to consider in their hearts that I remember all their evil. Now their deeds are all around them; they are before My face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Micah 7:3; Romans 1:32; Jeremiah 28:1–4"> They delight the king with their evil, and the princes with their lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:2; James 4:4; Jeremiah 5:7–8"> They are all adulterers, like an oven heated by a baker who needs not stoke the fire from the kneading to the rising of the dough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 28:1; Matthew 14:6; Genesis 40:20"> The princes are inflamed with wine on the day of our king; so he joins hands with those who mock him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 7:7; Hosea 7:4; 1 Samuel 19:11–15"> For they prepare their heart like an oven while they lie in wait; all night their anger smolders; in the morning it blazes like a flaming fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 15:25; 2 Kings 15:30; 2 Kings 15:10"> All of them are hot as an oven, and they devour their rulers. All their kings fall; not one of them calls upon Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 106:35; Ezra 9:12; Nehemiah 13:23–25"> Ephraim mixes with the nations; Ephraim is an unturned cake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 8:7; Isaiah 42:22–25; 2 Kings 13:3–7"> Foreigners consume his strength, but he does not notice. Even his hair is streaked with gray, but he does not know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 5:5; Isaiah 9:13; Amos 4:6–13"> Israel’s arrogance testifies against them, yet they do not return to the LORD their God; despite all this, they do not seek Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 12:1; Hosea 5:13; Hosea 11:11"> So Ephraim has become like a silly, senseless dove—calling out to Egypt, then turning to Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 31:16–29"> As they go, I will spread My net over them; I will bring them down like birds of the air. I will chastise them when I hear them flocking together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 9:12; Hosea 11:12; Isaiah 63:8"> Woe to them, for they have strayed from Me! Destruction to them, for they have rebelled against Me! Though I would redeem them, they speak lies against Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 4:3; Job 35:9–10; Jeremiah 3:10"> They do not cry out to Me from their hearts when they wail upon their beds. They slash themselves for grain and new wine, but turn away from Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; Psalm 62:3; Jeremiah 17:9"> Although I trained and strengthened their arms, they plot evil against Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 78:57; Hosea 9:3; Ezekiel 23:32"> They turn, but not to the Most High; they are like a faulty bow. Their leaders will fall by the sword for the cursing of their tongue; for this they will be ridiculed in the land of Egypt.</span>
