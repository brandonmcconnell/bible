<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 2:15; Exodus 4:22; Hosea 13:4"> When Israel was a child, I loved him, and out of Egypt I called My son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:15; Hosea 2:13; Isaiah 65:7"> But the more I called Israel, the farther they departed from Me. They sacrificed to the Baals and burned incense to carved images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:17; Deuteronomy 1:31; Exodus 15:26"> It was I who taught Ephraim to walk, taking them by the arms, but they never realized that it was I who healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:32; Leviticus 26:13; 2 Corinthians 5:14"> I led them with cords of kindness, with ropes of love; I lifted the yoke from their necks and bent down to feed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 7:16; Hosea 8:13; 2 Kings 17:13–14"> Will they not return to the land of Egypt and be ruled by Assyria because they refused to repent?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; Hosea 10:6; Hosea 10:14"> A sword will flash through their cities; it will destroy the bars of their gates and consume them in their own plans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:5; Amos 5:14–15; Hosea 7:16"> My people are bent on turning from Me. Though they call to the Most High, He will by no means exalt them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:20; Matthew 23:37; Hosea 6:4"> How could I give you up, O Ephraim? How could I surrender you, O Israel? How could I make you like Admah? How could I treat you like Zeboiim? My heart is turned within Me; My compassion is stirred!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 55:8–9; Micah 7:18–20"> I will not execute the full fury of My anger; I will not destroy Ephraim again. For I am God and not man—the Holy One among you—and I will not come in wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 31:4; Joel 3:16; Amos 1:2"> They will walk after the LORD; He will roar like a lion. When He roars, His children will come trembling from the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 11:11; Isaiah 60:8; Zechariah 10:10"> They will come trembling like birds from Egypt and like doves from the land of Assyria. Then I will settle them in their homes, declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 12:7; Revelation 3:21; 2 Chronicles 29:1–32"> Ephraim surrounds Me with lies, the house of Israel with deceit; but Judah still walks with God and is faithful to the Holy One.</span>
