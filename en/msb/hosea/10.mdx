<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 8:11; Leviticus 26:1; Philippians 2:21"> Israel was a luxuriant vine, yielding fruit for himself. The more his fruit increased, the more he increased the altars. The better his land produced, the better he made the sacred pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 5:13; 1 Kings 18:21; Zephaniah 1:5"> Their hearts are devious; now they must bear their guilt. The LORD will break down their altars and demolish their sacred pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 10:15; Hosea 10:7; Hosea 13:11"> Surely now they will say, “We have no king, for we do not revere the LORD. What can a king do for us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 5:7; Amos 6:12; Hosea 4:2"> They speak mere words; with false oaths they make covenants. So judgment springs up like poisonous weeds in the furrows of a field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 9:11; Hosea 5:8; 2 Kings 23:5"> The people of Samaria will fear for the calf of Beth-aven. Indeed, its people will mourn over it with its idolatrous priests—those who rejoiced in its glory—for it has been taken from them into exile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 5:13; Isaiah 30:3; Daniel 11:8"> Yes, it will be carried to Assyria as tribute to the great king. Ephraim will be seized with shame; Israel will be ashamed of its wooden idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 10:3; Jude 1:13; 2 Kings 1:3"> Samaria will be carried off with her king like a twig on the surface of the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 23:30; Revelation 6:16; Isaiah 2:19"> The high places of Aven will be destroyed—it is the sin of Israel; thorns and thistles will overgrow their altars. Then they will say to the mountains, “Cover us!” and to the hills, “Fall on us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Judges 20:5; Judges 19:22–30"> Since the days of Gibeah you have sinned, O Israel, and there you have remained. Did not the battle in Gibeah overtake the sons of iniquity?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Hosea 4:9; Jeremiah 16:16"> I will chasten them when I please; nations will be gathered against them to put them in bondage for their double transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 4:16; Jeremiah 50:11; Deuteronomy 25:4"> Ephraim is a well-trained heifer that loves to thresh; but I will place a yoke on her fair neck. I will harness Ephraim, Judah will plow, and Jacob will break the hard ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3–4; James 3:18; Isaiah 45:8"> Sow for yourselves righteousness and reap the fruit of loving devotion; break up your unplowed ground. For it is time to seek the LORD until He comes and sends righteousness upon you like rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 4:8; Galatians 6:7–8; Proverbs 1:31"> You have plowed wickedness and reaped injustice; you have eaten the fruit of lies. Because you have trusted in your own way and in the multitude of your mighty men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; 2 Kings 18:9–10; Amos 3:8"> the roar of battle will rise against your people, so that all your fortresses will be demolished as Shalman devastated Beth-arbel in the day of battle, when mothers were dashed to pieces along with their children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Hosea 10:7; Romans 7:13"> Thus it will be done to you, O Bethel, because of your great wickedness. When the day dawns, the king of Israel will be utterly cut off.</span>
