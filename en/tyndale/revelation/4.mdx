<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 11:12; Exodus 24:12; Revelation 1:19"> After this I loked and beholde a dore was open in heven and the fyrste voyce which I harde was as it were of a trompet talkinge with me which said: come vp hydder and I will shewe the thynges which must be fulfyllyd hereafter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 22:19; Revelation 1:10; Isaiah 6:1"> And immediatly I was in the sprete: and beholde a seate was put in heven and one sate on the seate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Revelation 21:11; Genesis 9:13–16"> And he that sat was to loke apon like vnto a iaspar stone and a sardyne stone: And there was a rayne bowe aboute the seate in syght lyke to an Emeralde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Revelation 11:16; Revelation 4:10"> And aboute the seate were .xxiiii. seates. And upon the seates .xxiiii. elders syttinge clothed in whyte rayment and had on their heddes crounes of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Revelation 8:5; Revelation 1:4"> And out of the seate proceded lightnynges and thundrynges and voyces and there wer vii. lampes of fyre burninge before the seate which are the vii. sprettes of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 15:2; Revelation 5:6; Revelation 14:3"> And before the seate there was a see of glasse lyke vnto cristall and in the myddes of the seate and rounde aboute the seate were iiii. bestes full of eyes before and behynde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Ezekiel 1:10; Ezekiel 10:21"> And the fyrste best was lyke a lion the seconde best lyke a calfe and the thyrde beste had a face as a man and the fourthe beste was like a flyinge egle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:2–13; Revelation 1:8; Hebrews 13:8"> And the iiii. bestes had eche one of them vi. wynges aboute him and they were full of eyes with in. And they had noo reste daye nether nyght sayinge: holy holy holy lorde god almyghty which was and is and is to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 5:13–14; Revelation 7:11–12; Revelation 10:6"> And when those beestes gave glory and honour and thankes to him that sat on the seate which lyveth for ever and ever:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 5:8; Revelation 19:4; Psalms 95:6"> the xxiiii. elders fell doune before him that sat on the trone and worshipped him that lyveth for ever and caste their crounes before the trone sayinge:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Colossians 1:16–17; Revelation 5:12; Isaiah 40:28"> thou arte worthy lorde to receave glory and honoure and power for thou haste created all thinges and for thy wylles sake they are and were created.</span>
