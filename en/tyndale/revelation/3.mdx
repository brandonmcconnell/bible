<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; 2 Timothy 4:1–4; Revelation 16:15"> Be awake and strength the thynges which remayne that are redy to dye. For I have not founde thy workes perfaycte before god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 2:5; 2 Peter 3:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:2"> Remember therfore how thou hast receaved and hearde and hold faste and repet. Yf thou shalt not watche I will come on ye as a thefe and thou shalt not knowe what houre I wyll come apon the</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 3:5; Revelation 7:9; Ecclesiastes 9:8"> Thou haste a feawe names in Sardis which have not defyled their garmentes: and they shall walke with me in whyte for they are worthy</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 10:32; Luke 12:8; Revelation 21:27"> He that overcometh shalbe clothed in whyte araye and I will not put out his name out of the boke of lyfe and I will confesse his name before my father and before his angelles.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:9; Colossians 4:3; Revelation 3:7"> I knowe thy workes. Beholde I have set before the an open doore and no man can shut it for thou haste a lyttell strengthe and haste kept my sayinges: and haste not denyed my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:14; Revelation 2:9; Isaiah 49:23"> Beholde I make them of the congregacion of Sathan which call them selves Iewes and are not but do lye: Beholde: I will make them that they shall come and worshippe before thy fete: and shall knowe that I love the.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 2:9; Revelation 14:12; Zechariah 13:9"> Because thou hast kept the wordes of my paciece therfore I will kepe the from the houre of temptacion which will come upo all the worlde to tempte them that dwell vpon the erth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 1:12; Revelation 22:12; Revelation 2:10"> Beholde I come shortly. Holde that which thou haste that no man take awaye thy croune.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 22:4; Revelation 21:2; Revelation 14:1"> Him that overcometh will I make a pyllar in the temple of my God and he shall goo no more oute. And I will wryt vpo him the name of my God and the name of the cite of my god newe Ierusalem which cometh doune oute of heven from my God and I will wryte vpon him my newe name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 2:7"> Let him that hath eares heare what the sprete sayth vnto the congregacions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; Revelation 1:5; 2 Corinthians 1:20"> And vnto the messenger of the congregacion which is in Laodicia wryte: This sayth (amen) the faythfull and true witnes the begynninge of the creatures of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:11; Matthew 6:24; 1 Kings 18:21"> I knowe thy workes that thou arte nether colde nor hot: I wolde thou were colde or hotte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 2:5; Zechariah 11:8–9; Jeremiah 14:19"> So then because thou arte bitwene bothe and nether colde ner hot I will spew the oute of my mouth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 13:7; Hosea 12:8; Romans 7:24"> because thou sayst thou arte riche and incresyd with goodes and haste nede of nothynge and knowest not howe thou arte wretched and miserable poore blinde and nakyd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; 1 Peter 1:7; Isaiah 55:1"> I counsell the to bye of me golde tryed in the fyre that thou mayste be riche and whyte raymet that thou mayste be clothed that thy fylthy nakednes do not apere: and anoynt thyne eyes with eye salve that thou mayste se.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 3:11–12; 1 Corinthians 11:32; Hebrews 12:5–11"> As many as I love I rebuke and chasten. Be fervent therfore and repent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 12:36–37; Revelation 19:9; John 14:21–23"> Beholde I stode at the doore and knocke. Yf eny man heare my voyce and opon the dore I will come in vnto him and will suppe with him and he with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:12; Matthew 19:28; Revelation 2:26–27"> To him that overcommeth will I graunte to sytt with me in my seate evyn as I overcam and have sytten with my father in his seate.</span>
