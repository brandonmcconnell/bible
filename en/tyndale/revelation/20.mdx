<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; 2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6"> And I sawe an angell come doune from heven havinge the kaye of the bottomlesse pyt and a gret chayne in his honde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Revelation 12:9; Isaiah 27:1"> And he toke the dragon that olde serpet which is the devyll and Satanas and he bounde him a thousand yeares:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 20:1; Revelation 17:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> and cast him into the bottomlesse pit and he bounde him and set a seale on him that he shuld desceaue the people no moare tyll the .M. yeares were fulfilled. And after that he muste be loosed for a litell season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Daniel 7:18; Matthew 19:28"> And I sawe seattes and they sat apon them and iudgement was geven vnto them: and I sawe the soules of them that were behedded for the witnes of Iesu and for the worde of God: which had not worshypped the best nether his ymage nether had taken his marke vpon their forheddes or on their hondes: and they lyved and raygned with Christ a .M. yere:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:11; Revelation 11:15; Luke 14:14"> but the wother of the deed men lyved not agayne vntyll the .M. yere were fynisshed. This is that fyrst resurreccion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 2:11; Revelation 1:6; 2 Timothy 2:12"> Blessed and holy is he that hath parte in the fyrst resurreccion. For on suche shall the seconde deeth have no power for they shalbe the prestes of God and of Christ and shall raygne with him a .M. yere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 20:2"> And when the .M. yeares are experied Satan shalbe lowsed out of his preson</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 16:14; Ezekiel 38:1; Revelation 20:10"> and shall goo oute to deceave the people which are in the foure quarters of the erth Gog and Magog to gadder them to gedder to batayle whose nombre is as the sonde of the see:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 39:6; Ezekiel 38:9"> and they went vp on the playne of the erth and compased the tentes of the saynctes about and the beloved cite. And fyre cam doune from God out of heven and devoured them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Revelation 19:20; Matthew 25:46"> and the devyll that desceaved them was cast into a lake of fyre and brymstone where the beest and the falce prophet were and shalbe tormented daye and nyght for ever more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 21:1; 2 Peter 3:7; Matthew 25:31"> And I sawe a grett whyte seate and him that sate on it from whose face fleed awaye both the erth and heaven and their place was no more founde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Matthew 16:27; Daniel 7:10"> And I sawe the deed both grett and small stonde before God: And the bokes were opened and another boke was opened which is the boke of lyfe and the deed were iudged of thoo thynges which weer wrytten in the bokes accordinge to their dedes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 5:28–29; Revelation 6:8; Hosea 13:14"> and the see gave vp her deed which were in her and deth and hell delyvered vp the deed which were in them: and they were iudged every man accordinge to his dedes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:26; Revelation 20:6; Revelation 21:8"> And deth and hell were cast into the lake of fyre. This is that second deeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 3:36; Matthew 13:50; Matthew 25:41"> And whosoever was not founde written in the boke of lyfe was cast into the lake of fyre.</span>
