<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 9:10; Job 2:6; Revelation 9:3"> and to the was commaunded that they shulde not kyll them but that they shulde be vexed v monethes and their payne was as the payne that cometh of a scorpion when he hath stoge a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 23:30; Jeremiah 8:3; Revelation 6:16"> And in those dayes shall men seke deeth and shall not fynde it and shall desyre to dye and deeth shall flye from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 7:8; Nahum 3:17; Joel 2:4–5"> And the similitude of the locustes was lyke vnto horses prepared vnto battayll and on their heddes were as it were crownes lyke vnto golde: and their faces were as it had bene the faces of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joel 1:6; 1 Corinthians 11:14–15; 1 Peter 3:3"> And they had heare as the heare of wemen. And their tethe were as the tethe of lyons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joel 2:5–8; Nahum 2:4–5; Job 39:25"> And they had habbergions as it were habbergions of yron. And the sounde of their wynges was as the sounde of charettes when many horsses runne to gedder to battayle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 9:5; Revelation 9:3; Revelation 9:19"> And they had tayles lyke vnto scorpions and there were stinges in their tayles. And their power was to hurt men v. monethes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Revelation 9:1–2; Ephesians 2:2"> And they had a kynge over them which is the angell of the bottomlesse pytt whose name in the hebrew tonge is Abadon: but in the greke tonge Apollion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 11:14; Revelation 8:13–2; Revelation 9:13–21"> One woo is past and beholde two wooes come after this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 8:3–5; Exodus 30:2–3; Revelation 9:1"> And the sixte. angell blewe and I herd a voyce from the iiii. corners of the golden aultre which is before god</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 16:12; Revelation 9:15; 2 Samuel 8:3"> saying to the sixte angell which had the trompe: Loose the iiii. angelles which are bounde in the grett ryver Eufrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 9:18; Revelation 8:7; Revelation 8:9"> And the iiii. angelles were loosed which wer prepared for an houre for a daye for a moneth and for a yeare for to slee the thyrde part of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Psalms 68:17; Daniel 7:10"> And the nombre of horsme of warre were twenty tymes xM. And I herde the nobre of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 9:18; Revelation 14:10; 1 Chronicles 12:8"> And thus I sawe the horses in a vision and them that sate on the havynge fyry habbergions of a Iacyncte coloure and brymstony and the heeddes of the horses werre as the heeddes of lyons. And out of their mouthes went forth fyre and smoke and brymstone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 9:17; Revelation 9:15"> And of these iii. was the thyrde parte of men kylled: that is to saye of fyre smoke and brymstone which proceded out of the mouthes of them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 9:15; Revelation 9:10; Ephesians 4:14"> For their power was in their mouthes and in their tayles: for their tayles were lyke vnto serpetes and had heedes and with them they dyd hurt:</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; 2 Corinthians 12:21; Revelation 17:5"> Also they repented not of their murther and of their sorcery nether of their fornacion nether of their thefte.</span>
