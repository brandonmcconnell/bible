<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Matthew 17:2; Revelation 1:15–16"> And I sawe another myghtye angell come doune from heven clothed with a cloude and the rayne bowe apon his heed. And hys face as it were the sunne and his fete as that were pyllars of fyre</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:9–10; Revelation 10:5; Revelation 5:1–5"> and he had in his honde a lytell boke opyn: and he put his ryght fote apon the see and his lyfte fote on the erth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 31:4; Revelation 4:5; Joel 3:16"> And cryed with a lowde voyce as when a lyon roreth. And when he had cryed seven thondres spake their voyces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 12:4; Daniel 8:26; Daniel 12:9"> And when the vii. thondres had spoken their voyces I was aboute to wryte. And I herde a voyce from heven sayinge vnto me seale vp thoo thynges which the vii. thondres spake and write them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Deuteronomy 32:40; Revelation 16:17"> And the angell which I sawe stonde apon the see and apon the erth lyfte vppe his honde to heven</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 4:11; Revelation 4:9; Revelation 16:17"> and swore by him that liveth for ever more which created heven and the thynges that ther in are and the see and the thynges which therin are: that there shulde be no lenger tyme:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 3:3–9; Romans 16:25; Luke 24:44–47"> but in the dayes of the voyce of the seventh angell when he shall begyn to blowe: even the mistery of god shalbe fynisshed as he preached by his servauntes the prophetes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 10:2; Revelation 10:4–5; Isaiah 30:21"> And the voyce which I herde from heven spake vnto me agayne and sayde: goo and take the lytle boke which ys open in the honde of the angell which stondeth apon the see and apon the erth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:1–3; Jeremiah 15:16; Ezekiel 2:8"> And I went vnto the angell and sayde to him: geve me the lytle boke and he sayd vnto me: take it and eate it vp and it shall make thy belly bytter but it shalbe in thy mouth as swete as hony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:3; Proverbs 16:24; Psalms 119:103"> and I toke the lytle boke out of his honde and ate it vp and it was in my mouth as swete as hony and as sone as I had eate it my belly was bytter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:4; Jeremiah 1:9–10; Revelation 17:15"> And he sayde vnto me: thou muste prophesy agayne amonge the people and nacions and tonges and to many kynges.</span>
