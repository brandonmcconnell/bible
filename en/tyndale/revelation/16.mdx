<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:8; Jeremiah 10:25; Revelation 15:1"> And I herde a great voyce out of the temple sayinge to the seven angels: goo youre wayes poure out youre vialles of wrath apon the erth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 8:7; Revelation 16:11; Revelation 13:15–18"> And the fyrst went and poured out his viall apon the erth and there fell anoysom and a sore botche apon the men which had the marke of the best and apon the which worshipped his ymage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 7:17–21; Revelation 8:8–9; Revelation 11:6"> And the seconde angell shed out his viall apon the see and it turned as it were into the bloud of a deed man: and every lyvinge thynge dyed in the see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 7:17–20; Revelation 8:10–11; Hosea 13:15"> And the thyrde angell shed out his vyall apon the ryvers and fountaynes of waters and they turned to bloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 11:17; Revelation 1:4"> And I herde an angell saye: lorde which arte and wast thou arte ryghteous and holy because thou hast geve soche iudgmentes</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Revelation 11:18; Revelation 6:10–11"> for they shed out the bloude of sayntes and prophettes and therfore hast thou geven them bloud to drynke: for they are worthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 19:2; Revelation 6:9; Revelation 15:3–4"> And I herde another out of the aultre saye: even soo lorde god almyghty true and righteous are thy iudgementes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:20; Revelation 14:18; Jonah 4:8"> And the fourth angell poured out his viall on the sunne and power was geve vnto him to vexe men with heate of fyre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 2:21; Revelation 16:21; Revelation 11:13"> And the men raged in gret heate and spake evyll of the name of God which had power over those plages and they repented not to geve him glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 9:2; Matthew 8:12; Isaiah 8:21–22"> And the fifte angell poured out his vyall apon the seate of the beste and his kyngdome wexed derke and they gnewe their tonges for sorowe</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 16:9; Revelation 16:2; Revelation 16:21"> and blasphemed the god of heven for sorowe and payne of their sores and repented not of their dedes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 9:14; Jeremiah 51:36; Isaiah 44:27"> And the sixte angell poured out his vyall apon the gret ryver Euphrates and the water dryed vp that the wayes of the kyngss of the este shulde be prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 19:20; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Revelation 20:10"> And I sawe thre vnclene sprettes lyke frogges come out of the mouthe of the dragon and out of the mouthe of the beeste and out of the mouthe of the falce prophett.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; 1 Timothy 4:1; Revelation 20:8"> For they are the sprettes of devyls workynge myracles to go out vnto the kynges of the erth and of the whole worlde to gaddre them to the battayle of that gret daye of God allmyghty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Matthew 24:42–43; 2 Peter 3:10"> Beholde I come as a thefe. Happy is he that watcheth and kepeth his garmentes Lest he be founde naked and men se his filthynes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 12:11; Judges 5:19; Joel 3:9–14"> And he gaddered them togedder into a place called in the hebrue tonge Armagedon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 19:30; Revelation 21:6; Ephesians 6:12"> And the seventhe angell poured out his viall in to the ayre. And there came a voyce out of heven from the seate sayinge: it is done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Revelation 4:5; Revelation 11:19"> And there folowed voyces thondringes and lightnynges and there was a grett erthquake soche as was not sence men were apon the erth so myghty an erthquake and so grett.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 18:5; Revelation 17:18; Revelation 18:21"> And the greate cite was devyded into thre parties And the cities of nacions fell. And grett Babilon came in remembraunce before God to geve vnto hyr the cuppe of wyne of the fearcenes of his wrathe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 6:14; Revelation 20:11; Jeremiah 4:23–25"> Every yle fled awaye and the mountaynes were not founde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Joshua 10:11; Revelation 16:9"> And ther fell a gret hayle as it had bene talentes out of heven apon the men and the men blasphemed God be cause of the plage of the hayle for it was grett and the plage of it sore.</span>
