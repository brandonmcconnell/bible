<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 21:25; Ephesians 5:32; Luke 21:11"> And ther appered a gret wonder in heven A woman clothed with the sunne and the mone vnder her fete and apon her heed a croune of xii. starres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 16:21; Galatians 4:19; Revelation 12:4"> And she was with chylde and cryed travayllinge in byrth and payned redy to be delyvered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 17:12; Revelation 12:9; Daniel 7:24"> And ther appered another wonder in heven for beholde a gret red dragon havynge .vii. heddes and ten hornes and crounes vpon his heddes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 8:9–12; Revelation 12:9; John 8:44"> and his tayle drue the thyrde parte of the starres and cast them to the erth. And the dragon stode before the woman which was reddy to be delyvred: for to devoure her chylde as sone as it were borne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 19:15; Revelation 2:26–27; Revelation 12:2"> And she brought forth a man chylde which shulde rule all nacions with a rode of yron And her sonne was taken vp vnto God and to his seate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 13:5; Revelation 11:2–3; Revelation 12:14"> And the woman fleed into wyldernes where she had a place prepared of god that they shulde fede her there a M. .ii .C and lx. dayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jude 1:9; Daniel 12:1; Ephesians 6:12"> And ther was grett battayll in heven Michael and his angells fowght with the dragon and the dragon fowght and his angelles</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 37:10; Revelation 12:11; Jude 1:6"> and prevaylled not: nether was their place founde eny more in heven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 10:18; John 12:31; Revelation 20:10"> And the grett dragon that olde serpent called the devyll and Sathanas was cast out. Which desceaveth all the worlde. And he was cast into the erth and his angelles were cast out also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 22:31; Zechariah 3:1–2; Revelation 11:15"> And I harde a lowde voyce sayinge: in heven is nowe made salvacion and strengthe and the kyngdome of oure God and the power of his Christ For he is cast doune which accused them before god daye and nyght.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:3–5; John 16:33; 1 John 5:5"> And they overcame him by the bloude of the lambe and by the worde of their testimony and they loved not their lyves vnto the deeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Revelation 8:13; Psalms 96:11–13"> Therfore reioyce hevens and ye that dwell in them. Woo to the inhabiters of the erth and of the see: for the devyll is come doune vnto you which hath greet wrath because he knoweth that he hath but a short tyme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 3:15; Revelation 12:3–5; John 16:33"> And when the dragon sawe that he was caste vnto the erth he persecuted the woman which brought forth the man chylde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Isaiah 40:31; Revelation 12:6"> And to the woman were geven two wynges of a great egle that she myght flye into the wyldrenes into her place where she is norysshed for a tyme tymes and halffe a tyme from the presence of the sarpent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7; Isaiah 59:19; Psalms 18:4"> And the dragon cast out of his mouth water after the woman as it had bene a ryver because she hulde have bene caught of the floud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 17:6; Exodus 12:35–36; 2 Kings 8:9"> And the erth holpe the woman and the erth opened her mouth and swalowed vp the rever which the dragon cast out of hys mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:12; Genesis 3:15; Revelation 11:7"> And the dragon was wroth with the woman: and went and made warre with the remnaunt of hyr sede which kepe the commaundmentes of god and have the testimony of Iesus Christe. And I stode on the see sonde.</span>
