<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 1:19; Revelation 11:15; Revelation 8:6–1"> And I sawe angelles stondynge before god and to them were geven vii. trompettes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 5:8; Revelation 8:4; Psalms 141:2"> And another angell cam and stode before the aultre havynge a golden senser and moche of odoures was geven vnto him that he shulde offre of the prayers of all saynctes apon the golden aultre which was before the seate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Luke 1:10; Revelation 8:3"> And the smoke of the odoures which came of the prayers of all saynctes ascended vppe before god out of the angelles honde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 4:5; Revelation 11:19; Ezekiel 10:2–7"> And the angell toke the senser and fylled it with fyre of the aultre and caste it into the erth and voyces were made and thondrynges and lightnynges and erthquake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 8:2"> And the .vii. angells which had the .vii. tropettes prepared them selves to blowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Revelation 9:4; Revelation 9:18"> The fyrst angell blewe and there was made hayle and fyre which were myngled with bloud and they were caste into the erth: and the thryd parte of trees was burnt and all grene grasse was brent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:25; Exodus 7:17–21; Mark 11:23"> And the seconde angell blewe: and as it were a gret mountayne: burnynge with fyre was caste in to the see</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 16:3; Revelation 8:7; Revelation 8:12"> and the thyrde parte of the see tourned to bloud and the thyrde parte of the creatures which had lyfe dyed and the thyrde part of shippes were destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 16:4; Revelation 9:1; Isaiah 14:12"> And the thyrde angell blewe and ther fell a grett starre from heven burnynge as it were a lampe and it fell into the thyrde parte of the ryvers and into fountaynes of waters</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:15; Jeremiah 9:15; Deuteronomy 29:18"> and the name of the starre is called wormwod. And the thyrde part was turned to wormwod. And many me dyed of the waters because they were made bytter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Joel 2:31; Joel 2:10"> And the fourth angell blew and the thyrde parte of the sunne was smytten and the thyrde parte of the mone and the thyrde part of starres: so that the thyrde parte of them was darckned. And the daye was smytten that the thyrde part of it shulde not shyne and lyke wyse the nyght.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 9:12; Revelation 11:14; Revelation 14:6"> And I behelde and herd an angell flyinge thorowe the myddes of heven sayinge with a lowde voyce: Woo wo to the inhabiters of the erth because of the voyces to come of the trompe of the .iii. angells which were yet to blowe.</span>
