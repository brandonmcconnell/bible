<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 6:31–44; John 6:23; Luke 5:1"> After these thinges Iesus wet his waye over the see of Galile nye to a cyte called Tiberias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:24–25; Matthew 12:15; Matthew 15:30–31"> And a greate multitude folowed him because they had sene his myracles which he dyd on them that were diseased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 6:15; Luke 9:28; Luke 6:12–13"> And Iesus went vp into a mountayne and there he sate with his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 2:13; John 11:55; John 5:1"> And ester a feast of the Iewes was nye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 15:33; Luke 9:12–13; Matthew 14:14–15"> Then Iesus lifte vp his eyes and sawe a greate company come vnto him and sayde vnto Philip: whence shall we bye breed that these might eate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 22:1; Deuteronomy 13:3; 2 Chronicles 32:31"> This he sayde to prove him: for he him sylfe knewe what he wolde do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 11:21–22; Mark 6:37; John 12:5"> Philip answered him two hondred peny worthe of breed are not sufficient for them that every man have a litell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:40–44; Matthew 4:18"> Then sayde vnto him one of his disciples Andrew Simon Peters brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; Matthew 14:17; Mark 6:38"> There ys a lad here which hath fyve barly loves and two fisshes: but what is that amonge so many?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 9:14–16; Matthew 15:35–36; Mark 6:39–41"> And Iesus sayde. Make the people sit doune: Ther was moche grasse in the place. And the men sate doune in nombre about five thousande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 6:23; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Matthew 15:36"> And Iesus toke the breed and gave thankes and gave to the disciples and his disciples to them that were set doune. And lykwyse of the fysshes as moche as they wolde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 1:53; Nehemiah 9:25; Nehemiah 8:10"> When they had eate ynough he sayd vnto his disciples: gadder vp the broke meate that remayneth: that nothinge be loost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; 2 Kings 4:2–7; 2 Corinthians 9:8–9"> And they gadered it to geder and fylled twelve baskettes with the broken meate of the five barly loves which broken meate remayned vnto the that had eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–18; Matthew 21:11; Acts 7:37"> Then the men when they had sene the myracle that Iesus dyd sayde: This is of a trueth the Prophet that shuld come into the worlde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:36; John 6:15–21; John 7:3–4"> When Iesus perceaved that they wolde come and take him vp to make him kinge he departed agayne into a mountayne him silfe a lone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And when even was come his disciples wet vnto the see</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 6:45; John 6:24–25; John 2:12"> and entred into a shyppe and went over the see vnto Capernaum. And anone it was darcke and Iesus was not come to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 107:25; Psalms 135:7; Matthew 14:24"> And the see arose with a greate winde that blew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 6:47–49; Matthew 14:25–26; Job 9:8"> And when they had rowe aboute a .xxv. or a xxx. furlonges they sawe Iesus walke on the see and drawe nye vnto the shyp and they were afrayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 14:27–31; Isaiah 43:1–2; Isaiah 44:8"> And he sayde vnto them: It is I be not a frayde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 6:51; Matthew 14:32–33; Revelation 3:20"> Then wolde they have receaved him into the shyp and the ship was by and by at the londe whyther they went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 6:45; Matthew 14:22; John 6:2"> The daye folowynge the people which stode on the other syde of the see sawe that ther was none other shyp theare save that one wher in his disciples were entred and that Iesus went not in with his disciples into the ship: but that his disciples were gone awaye alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:11–12"> How be it, ther came other shippes from Tiberias nye vnto the place where they ate breed when the Lorde had blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:17; Luke 8:40; Mark 6:53"> Then when the people sawe that Iesus was not there nether his disciples they also toke shippinge and came to Caparnaum sekinge for Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 1:38–39; Matthew 23:7"> And when they had founde him on the other syde of the see they sayd vnto him: Rabbi when camest thou hidder?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 4:3–4; Philippians 3:19; Philippians 2:21"> Iesus answered them and sayde: verely verely I saye vnto you: ye seke me not because ye sawe the myracles: but because ye ate of the loves and were filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 3:2; Isaiah 55:2; John 6:54"> Laboure not for the meate which perissheth but for the meate that endureth vnto everlastynge lyfe whiche meate the sonne of man shall geve vnto you. For him hath god the father sealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 16:30; Acts 9:6; Matthew 19:16"> Then sayd they vnto him: what shall we do that we myght worke the workes of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 John 3:23; Acts 16:31; John 3:36"> Iesus answered and sayde vnto them. This is the worke of God that ye beleve on him who he hath sent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; John 10:38; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> They sayde vnto him: what signe shewest thou then that we maye se and beleve the? What doest thou worke?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:15; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:24–25"> Oure fathers dyd eate Manna in the desert as that is writte: He gave them breed from heaven to eate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 15:1; John 6:58; Exodus 16:4"> Iesus sayde vnto the: verely verely I saye vnto you: Moses gave you breed from heaven: but my father geveth you the true breed from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 8:42; John 17:8; John 3:13"> For the breed of God is he which cometh doune from heaven and geveth lyfe vnto the worlde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 4:15; John 6:26; Psalms 4:6"> Then sayde they vnto him: Lorde ever moore geve vs this breed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 6:48–58; Revelation 22:17; John 6:41"> And Iesus sayde vnto them: I am that breed of life. He that cometh to me shall not honger: and he that beleveth on me shall never thurst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8–9; Luke 16:31; John 12:37"> But I sayed vnto you: that ye have sene me and yet beleve not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; John 6:39; John 17:24"> All that the father geveth me shall come to me: and him that cometh to me I cast not awaye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 5:30; John 4:34; John 3:31"> For I came doune from heaven: not to do myne awne will but his will which hath sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 17:12; John 6:40; John 18:9"> And this is the fathers will which hath sent me that of all which he hath geven me I shuld loose no thinge: but shuld rayse it vp agayne at the last daye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 6:54; John 5:24; Romans 6:23"> And this is the wyll of him that sent me: that every man which seith the sonne and beleveth on him have everlastinge lyfe. And I will rayse him vp at the last daye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 6:51–52; John 6:48; John 6:33"> The Iewes then murmured at him because he sayde: I am that breed which is come doune from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Mark 6:3; Matthew 13:55–56"> And they sayde: Is not this Iesus the sonne of Ioseph whose father and mother we knowe? How ys that then that he sayeth I came doune from heaven?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; John 6:64; Mark 9:33"> Iesus answered and sayde vnto them. Murmur not betwene youre selves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 6:65; John 6:39–40; Matthew 11:25–27"> No man can come to me except the father which hath sent me, drawe him. And I will rayse him vp at the last daye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Isaiah 54:13; Jeremiah 31:33–34; Hebrews 10:16"> It is written in the Prophetes, that they shall all be taught of God. Every man therfore that hath hearde and hath learned of the father commeth vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 1:18; John 7:29; Matthew 11:27"> Not that eny man hath sene the father save he which is of God: the same hath sene the father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:36; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 5:9–10"> Verely verely I saye vnto you he that beleveth on me hath everlastinge lyfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 6:51; 1 Corinthians 11:24–25; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> I am that breed of lyfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3–5; Jude 1:5; Zechariah 1:5"> Youre fathers dyd eate Mana in the wildernes and are deed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 6:33; John 6:51; Romans 8:10"> This is that breed which cometh from heaven that he which eateth of it shuld also not dye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 22:19; John 6:33; Hebrews 10:5–12"> I am that lyvinge breed which came doune from heaven. Yf eny man eate of this breed he shall live forever. And the breed that I will geve is my flesshe which I will geve for the lyfe of the worlde</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 6:41"> And the Iewes strove amonge them selves sayinge: How can this felowe geve vs his flesshe to eate?</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 John 5:12; Matthew 26:26–28; John 6:55"> Then Iesus sayde vnto them: Verely, verely I saye vnto you except ye eate the flesshe of the sonne of man and drinke his bloude ye shall not have lyfe in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 6:39–40; John 6:27; Philippians 3:7–10"> Whosoever eateth my flesshe and drinketh my bloude hath eternall lyfe: and I will rayse him vp at the last daye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 15:1; John 8:31; John 6:32"> For my flesshe is meate in dede: and my bloude is drynke in dede.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="1 John 3:24; John 15:4–5; John 14:20"> He that eateth my flesshe and drynketh my bloude dwelleth in me and I in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; John 5:26; John 17:21"> As the lyvinge father hath sent me even so lyve I by my father: and he that eateth me shall live by me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 3:36; John 6:47–51; John 6:41"> This is the breed which cam from heaven: not as youre fathers have eaten Manna and are deed. He that eateth of this breed shall live ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 6:24; Proverbs 8:1–3; Luke 4:31"> These thinges sayd he in the synagoge as he taught in Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="John 6:66; John 8:43; John 8:31"> Many of his disciples when they had herde this sayde: this is an herde sayinge: who can abyde the hearinge of it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="John 2:24–25; John 6:64; John 21:17"> Iesus knew in him selfe that his disciples murmured at it and sayde vnto them: Doth this offende you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 16:19; John 3:13; John 17:4–5"> What and yf ye shall se the sonne of man ascede vp where he was before?</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Galatians 5:25; Hebrews 4:12; Psalms 119:93"> It is the sprete that quyckeneth the flesshe proffeteth nothinge. The wordes that I speake vnto you are sprete and lyfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="John 10:26; 2 Timothy 2:19; Hebrews 4:13"> But ther are some of you that beleve not. For Iesus knewe from the begynnynge which they were that beleved not and who shuld betraye him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="John 6:44–45; John 6:37; John 3:27"> And he sayde: Therfore sayde I vnto you: that no man can come vnto me except it were geven vnto him of my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Luke 9:62; 1 John 2:19; Hebrews 10:38"> From that tyme many of his disciples wet backe and walked no moore with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Ruth 1:11–18; John 6:70; Luke 14:25–33"> Then sayde Iesus to the twelve: will ye alsoo goo awaye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Psalms 73:25; John 5:24; 1 John 5:11–13"> Then Simon Peter answered: Master to whom shall we goo? Thou haste the wordes of eternall lyfe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="John 11:27; Luke 9:20; Mark 8:29"> and we beleve and knowe that thou arte Christ the sonne of the lyvinge God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="John 13:27; John 13:2; John 17:12"> Iesus answered them: Have not I chosen you twelve and yet one of you is the devyll?</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Matthew 26:14–16; Jude 1:4; Acts 1:16–20"> He spake it of Iudas Iscariot the sonne of Simon. For he it was that shuld betraye him and was one of the twelve.</span>
