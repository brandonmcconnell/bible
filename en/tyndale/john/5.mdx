<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 23:14–17; Exodus 34:23"> After that ther was a feast of the Iewes and Iesus went vp to Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:39; Nehemiah 3:1; Nehemiah 3:32"> And ther is at Ierusalem by the slaughterhousse a pole called in the Ebrue tonge Bethseda havinge five porches</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 3:1–4; Luke 7:22; Matthew 15:30"> in which laye a greate multitude of sicke folke of blinde halt and wyddered waytinge for the movinge of the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For an angell wet doune at a certayne ceason into the pole and troubled the water. Whosoever then fyrst after the steringe of the water stepped in was made whoale of what soever disease he had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 3:2; Luke 8:43; Mark 9:21"> And a certayne man was theare which had bene diseased .xxxviii. yeares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 18:41; Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 4:13"> When Iesus sawe him lye and knewe that he now longe tyme had bene diseased he sayde vnto him. Wilt thou be made whoale?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 72:12; Deuteronomy 32:36; 1 Corinthians 9:24"> The sicke answered him: Syr I have no man when the water is troubled to put me into the pole. But in the meane tyme whill I am about to come another steppeth doune before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 5:24; Matthew 9:6; Mark 2:11"> And Iesus sayde vnto him: ryse take vp thy beed and walke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 3:7–8; John 9:14; Mark 3:2–4"> And immediatly the man was made whole and toke vp his beed and went. And the same daye was the Saboth daye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:21; Luke 6:2; John 9:16"> The Iewes therfore sayde vnto him that was made whole. It is the Saboth daye it is not laufull for the to cary thy beed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 2:9–11; John 9:16"> He answered them: he that made me whole sayde vnto me: take vp thy beed and get the hence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Judges 6:29; Romans 10:2"> Then axed they him: what man is that which sayde vnto the take vp thy beed and walke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:30; Luke 24:31; John 8:59"> And he that was healed wist not who it was. For Iesus had gotte him selfe awaye be cause that ther was preace of people in the place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 8:11; Leviticus 26:23–24; Psalms 118:18"> And after that Iesus founde him in the temple and sayd vnto him: beholde thou arte made whole synne no moore lest a worsse thinge happen vnto the.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:11–12; John 4:29; John 9:25"> The man departed and tolde the Iewes that that was Iesus whiche had made him whole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:39; John 7:19–20; Mark 3:6"> And therfore the Iewes dyd persecute Iesus and sought the meanes to slee him because he had done these thinges on the Saboth daye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:4; John 14:10; Colossians 1:16"> And Iesus answered them: my father worketh hidder to and I worke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 10:30; John 10:33; John 19:7"> Therfore the Iewes sought the moare to kill him not only because he had broken the Saboth: but sayde also that God was his father and made him selfe equall with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 12:49; John 8:28; John 14:10"> Then answered Iesus and sayde vnto them: verely verely I saye vnto you: the sonne can do no thinge of him selfe but that he seeth the father do. For whatsoever he doeth that doeth the sonne also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 3:35; Matthew 3:17; John 17:26"> For the father loveth the sonne and sheweth him all thinges whatsoever he him selfe doeth. And he will shewe him greter workes then these because the shoulde marvayle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:11; Deuteronomy 32:39; John 11:25"> For lykwyse as the father rayseth vp the deed and quickeneth them even so the sonne quyckeneth whom he will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 5:27; Acts 17:31; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> Nether iudgeth the father eny man: but hath committed all iudgement vnto the sonne</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 2:23; Luke 10:16; Matthew 11:27"> because that all men shuld honoure the sonne even as they honoure the father. He that honoureth not the sonne the same honoureth not the father which hath sent him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; John 6:40; 1 John 5:11–13"> Verely verely I saye vnto you: He that heareth my wordes and beleveth on him that sent me hath everlastinge lyfe and shall not come into damnacion: but is scaped from deth vnto lyfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:28; Ephesians 5:14; Ephesians 2:5"> Verely verely I saye vnto you: the tyme shall come and now is when the deed shall heare the voyce of the sonne of God. And they that heare shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 11:26; John 1:4; Exodus 3:14"> For as the father hath life in him silfe: so lyke wyse hath he geven to the sonne to have lyfe in him silfe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 10:42; John 5:22; Acts 17:31"> and hath geven him power also to iudge in that he is the sonne of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 20:12; 1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Isaiah 26:19"> Marvayle not at this the houre shall come in the which all that are in the graves shall heare his voice</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Romans 2:6–10; Acts 24:15"> and shall come forthe: they that have done good vnto the resurreccion of lyfe: and they that have done evyll vnto the resurreccion of dampnacion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 6:38; John 5:19; John 4:34"> I can of myne awne selfe do nothinge at all. As I heare I iudge and my iudgement is iust because I seke not myne awne will but the will of the father which hath sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; John 8:54; Revelation 3:14"> Yf I beare witnes of my selfe my witnes is not true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 1:33; John 12:28–30; John 5:36–37"> Ther is a nother that beareth witnes of me and I am sure that the witnes whiche he beareth of me is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 1:29–34; John 3:26–36; John 1:15–27"> Ye sent vnto Iohn and he bare witnes vnto the truthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 19:41–42; John 20:31; 1 John 5:9"> But I receave not the recorde of man. Neverthelesse these thinges I saye that ye might be safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Peter 1:19; John 1:7–8; Matthew 11:11"> He was a burninge and a shyninge light and ye wolde for a season have reioysed in his light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 10:25; John 10:37–38; John 15:24"> But I have greater witnes then the witnes of Iohn. For the workes which the father hath geve me to fynisshe: the same workes which I do beare witnes of me that the father sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 8:18; John 1:18; Matthew 3:17"> And the father him silfe which hath sent me beareth witnes of me. Ye have not hearde his voyce at eny tyme nor ye have sene his shape:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 John 2:14; Deuteronomy 6:6–9; John 12:44–48"> therto his wordes have ye not abydinge in you. For whome he hath sent: him ye beleve not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Luke 24:44; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> Searche the scriptures for in them ye thinke ye have eternall lyfe: and they are they which testify of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 3:19; John 1:11; Psalms 81:11"> And yet will ye not come to me that ye might have lyfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:18; John 5:34; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> I receave not prayse of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 3:17; John 2:25"> But I knowe you that ye have not the love of God in you</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 10:25; Matthew 24:5; John 17:4–6"> I am come in my fathers name and ye receave me not. Yf another shall come in his awne name him will ye receave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 12:43; Romans 2:29; Philippians 2:3"> How can ye beleve which receave honoure one of another and seke not the honoure that commeth of God only?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 3:19–20; Galatians 3:10; 2 Corinthians 3:7–11"> Doo not thinke that I wyll accuse you to my father. Ther is one that accuseth you even Moses in whom ye trust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15; Deuteronomy 18:18–19; John 1:45"> For had ye beleved Moses ye wold have beleved me: for he wrote of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Luke 16:29"> But now ye beleve not his writinge: how shall ye beleve my wordes.</span>
