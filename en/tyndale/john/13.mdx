<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 12:23; 1 John 4:19; John 13:34"> Before the feast of ester when Iesus knewe that his houre was come that he shuld departe out of this worlde vnto the father. When he loved his which were in the worlde vnto the ende he loved them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:13–17; Acts 5:3; John 13:26–27"> And when supper was ended after that the devyll had put in the hert of Iudas Iscariot Simons sonne to betraye him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 3:35; Matthew 11:27; Hebrews 1:2"> Iesus knowinge that the father had geve all thinges into his hondes. And that he was come from God and went to God</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 22:27; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Luke 12:37"> he rose from supper and layde a syde his vpper garmentes and toke a towell and gyrd him selfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:44; Psalms 51:2; Genesis 18:4"> After that poured he water into a basyn and beganne to wash his disciples fete and to wype them with the towell wherwith he was gyrde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 3:11–14; John 1:27; Luke 5:8"> Then came he to Simon Peter. And Peter sayde to him: Lorde shalt thou wesshe my fete?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:8; John 12:16; John 14:26"> Iesus answered and sayde vnto him: what I do thou wotest not now but thou shalt knowe herafter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25; Acts 22:16; Titus 3:5"> Peter sayd vnto him: thou shalt not wesshe my fete whill the worlde stondeth. Iesus answered him: yf I wasshe the not thou shalt have no part with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 51:7; Psalms 51:2; 1 Peter 3:21"> Simon Peter sayde vnto him: Lorde not my fete only: but also my handes and my heed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:3; 2 Corinthians 7:1; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> Iesus sayde to him: he that is wesshed nedeth not save to wesshe his fete and is clene every whit. And ye are clene: but not all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 13:26; John 13:2; John 13:21"> For he knewe his betrayer. Therfore sayde he: ye are not all clene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 13:7; John 13:4; Ezekiel 24:24"> After he had wesshed their fete and receaved his clothes and was set doune agayne he sayde vnto them? wot ye what I have done to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 6:46; 1 Corinthians 12:3; John 11:28"> Ye call me master and Lorde and ye saye well for so am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Acts 20:35; Romans 12:16"> If I then youre Lorde and master have wesshed youre fete ye also ought to wesshe one anothers fete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 11:29; 1 Peter 3:17–18"> For I have geven you an ensample that ye shuld do as I have done to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:40; John 15:20; Matthew 10:24–25"> Verely verely I saye vnto you the servaunt is not greater then his master nether the messenger greater then he that sent him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 11:28; James 4:17; James 1:25"> If ye vnderstonde these thinges happy are ye yf ye do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Matthew 26:23; John 17:12"> I speake not of you all I knowe whom I have chosen. But that the scripture be fulfilled: he that eateth breed with me hath lyfte vp his hele agaynste me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; John 14:29; John 16:4"> Now tell I you before it come: that when it is come to passe ye might beleve that I am he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Luke 9:48; Matthew 25:40"> Verely verely I saye vnto you. He that receaveth who soever I sende receaveth me. And he that receaveth me receaveth him that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 13:18; Matthew 26:21; Mark 14:18"> When Iesus had thus sayd he was troubled in the sprete and testified sayinge: verely verely I saye vnto you that one of you shall betraye me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 22:23; Matthew 26:22; Mark 14:19"> And then the disciples loked one on another doutinge of who he spake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 19:26; John 21:7; John 21:20"> Ther was one of his disciples which leaned on Iesus bosome whom Iesus loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Luke 1:22; Acts 13:16"> To him beckened Simon Peter that he shuld axe who it was of whom he spake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 21:20; Genesis 44:4–12; Esther 7:5"> He then as he leaned on Iesus brest sayde vnto him: Lorde who ys it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 26:23; John 13:30; Mark 14:19–20"> Iesus answered he that ys to whom I geve a soppe when I have dept it. And he wet a soppe and gave it to Iudas Iscarioth Simons sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:3; John 13:2; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> And after the soppe Satan entred into him. Then sayd Iesus vnto him: that thou dost do quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> That wist no man at the table for what intent he spake vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 12:5–6; John 13:1; Ephesians 4:28"> Some of the thought because Iudas had the bagge that Iesus had sayd vnto him bye those thinges that we have nede af agaynst the feast: or that he shulde geve some thinge to the poore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Job 24:13–15; Proverbs 4:16"> Assone then as he had receaved the soppe he wet immediatly out. And it was night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 14:13; 1 Peter 4:11; John 7:39"> When he was gone out Iesus sayde: now is the sonne of man glorified. And God is glorified by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Isaiah 53:10–12; John 17:4–6"> Yf God be glorified by him God shall also glorify him in him selfe: and shall strayght waye glorify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 7:33–34; John 8:21–24; John 16:16–22"> Deare chyldren yet a lytell whyle am I with you. Ye shall seke me and as I sayde vnto the Iewes whither I goo thither can ye not come. Also to you saye I nowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Leviticus 19:18; John 15:17"> A newe commaundment geve I vnto you that ye love to gedder as I have loved you that even so ye love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 4:20–21; 1 John 3:10–14; 1 John 2:10"> By this shall all me knowe that ye are my disciples yf ye shall have love one to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Peter 1:14; John 13:33; John 21:21–22"> Simon Peter sayd vnto him: Lorde whither goest thou? Iesus answered him: whither I goo thou canst not folowe me now but thou shalt folowe me afterwardes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 21:13; Acts 20:24; Mark 14:27–31"> Peter sayd vnto him: Lorde why cannot I folowe the now? I will geve my lyfe for thy sake?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 22:34; Matthew 26:34; Mark 14:30"> Iesus answered him: wilt thou geve thy lyfe for my sake? Verely verely I saye vnto the the cocke shall not crowe tyll thou have denyed me thryse.</span>
