<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And as Iesus passed by he sawe a man which was blynde from his birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 13:2; John 9:34; Acts 28:4"> And his disciples axed him sayinge. Master who dyd synne: this man or his father and mother that he was borne blynde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:40; Ecclesiastes 9:1–2"> Iesus answered: Nether hath this man synned nor yet his father and mother: but that the workes of God shuld be shewed on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:35; Ephesians 5:16; John 4:34"> I must worke the workes of him that sent me whyll it is daye. The nyght cometh when no man can worke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; Acts 13:47"> As longe as I am in the worlde I am the lyght of the worlde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 8:23; Mark 7:33; Revelation 3:18"> Assone as he had thus spoken he spate on the grounde and made claye of the spetle and rubbed the claye on the eyes of the blynde</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5; John 9:11; John 11:37"> and sayde vnto him: Goo wesshe the in the pole of Syloe which by interpretacion signifieth sent. He went his waye and wasshed and cam agayne seinge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; 1 Samuel 21:11; Luke 18:35"> The neghboures and they that had sene him before how that he was a begger sayde: is not this he that sate and begged?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Some sayde: this is he. Other sayd: he is lyke him. But he him selfe sayde: I am even he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 9:26; John 9:21; Ecclesiastes 11:5"> They sayde vnto him: How are thyne eyes opened then?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 9:27; Jeremiah 36:17"> He answered and sayde. The man that is called Iesus made claye and anoynted myne eyes and sayd vnto me: Goo to the pole Syloe and wesshe. I went and wesshed and receaved my syght.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 5:11–13; Exodus 2:18–20; John 7:11"> They sayde vnto him: where is he? He sayde: I cannot tell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:57; John 11:46–47; John 12:42"> Then brought they to the pharises him that a lytell before was blynde:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:9; Luke 13:10–17; Matthew 12:1–14"> for it was the Saboth daye when Iesus made the claye and opened his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:26–27; John 9:10–11"> Then agayne the pharises also axed him how he had receaved his syght. He sayde vnto the: He put claye apon myne eyes and I wasshed and do se.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:43; John 10:19; Matthew 12:2"> Then sayde some of the pharises: this man is not of God because he kepeth not the saboth daye. Other sayde: how can a man that is a synner do suche myracles? And ther was stryfe amonge them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:19; John 6:14; Acts 3:22–26"> Then spake they vnto the blynde agayne: What sayst thou of him because he hath openned thyne eyes? And he sayd: He is a Prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 3:15–19; John 5:44; Isaiah 26:11"> But the Iewes dyd not beleve of the felowe how that he was blynde and receaved his syght vntyll they had called the father and mother of him that had receaved his syght.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:8–9; Acts 4:14; Acts 3:10"> And they axed the saying: Is this youre sonne whome ye saye was borne blynde? How doth he now se then?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> His father and mother answered them and sayde: we wote well that this is oure sonne and that he was borne blynde:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> but by what meanes he now seith that can we not tell or who hath opened his eyes can we not tell. He is olde ynough axe him let him answer for him selfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 7:13; John 16:2; Luke 6:22"> Suche wordes spake his father and mother because they feared the Iewes. For the Iewes had conspyred all redy that yf eny man dyd confesse that he was Christ he shuld be excommunicat out of the synagoge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 9:21"> Therfore sayde his father and mother: he is olde ynough axe him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 7:19; John 9:16; John 8:49"> Then agayne called they the man that was blynde and sayd vnto him: Geve God the prayse: we knowe that this man is a synner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 5:10; John 9:30; John 5:11"> He answered and sayde: Whyther he be a synner or noo I cannot tell: One thinge I am sure of that I was blynde and now I se.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then sayde they to him agayne. What dyd he to the? How opened he thyne eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:67; John 9:10–15"> He answered them I tolde you yerwhyle and the dyd not heare. Wherfore wolde ye heare it agayne? Will ye also be his disciples?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 2:17; John 7:19; Matthew 27:39"> Then rated they him and sayde: Thou arte his disciple. We be Moses disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:14; Numbers 12:2–7; Acts 7:35"> We are sure that God spake with Moses. This felowe we knowe not from whence he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; Psalms 119:18; Isaiah 29:18"> The man answered and sayde vnto them: this is a merveleous thinge that ye wote not whence he is seinge he hath opened myne eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 28:9"> For we be sure that God heareth not synners. But yf eny man be a worshipper of God and do his will him heareth he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Revelation 16:18; Job 20:4"> Sence the worlde beganne was it not hearde that eny man opened the eyes of one that was borne blynd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 9:16; John 3:2; Acts 5:38–39"> If this man were not of God he coulde have done no thinge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 9:22; John 9:2; Isaiah 66:5"> They answered and sayd vnto him: thou arte altogeder borne in synne: and dost thou teache vs? And they cast him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 20:31; 1 John 5:13; John 10:36"> Iesus hearde that they had excommunicate him: and assone as he had founde him he sayd vnto him: doest thou beleve on the sonne of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Song of Solomon 5:9; John 1:38"> He answered and sayde: Who is it Lorde that I myght beleve on him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 4:26; Psalms 25:8–9; John 14:21–23"> And Iesus sayde vnto him: Thou hast sene him and he it is that talketh with the.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; Matthew 28:9; Luke 24:52"> And he sayde: Lorde I beleve: and worshipped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:19; John 12:46; Acts 26:18"> Iesus sayde: I am come vnto iudgement into this worlde: that they which se not myght se and they which se myght be made blynde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Matthew 15:12–14; John 7:47–52"> And some of the pharises which were with him hearde these wordes and sayde vnto him: are we then blynde?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; John 15:22–24; 1 John 1:8–10"> Iesus sayde vnto them: yf ye were blynde ye shuld have no synne. But now ye saye we se therfore youre synne remayneth.</span>
