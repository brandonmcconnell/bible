<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 16:9; Matthew 27:60; John 19:25"> The morow after the saboth daye came Mary Magdalene erly when it was yet darcke vnto the sepulcre and sawe the stone taken awaye from the toumbe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:23; John 20:13; John 21:24"> Then she ranne and came to Simon Peter and to the other disciple whome Iesus loved and sayde vnto them. They have taken awaye the Lorde out of the toumbe and we cannot tell where they have layde him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:12"> Peter went forth and that other disciple and came vnto the sepulcre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12; 1 Corinthians 9:24"> They ranne bothe to gether and that other disciple dyd out runne Peter and came fyrst to the sepulcre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:40; John 20:11; John 11:44"> And he stouped doune and sawe the lynnen clothes lyinge yet wet he not in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:17; John 21:7; Matthew 16:15–16"> Then came Simon Peter folowynge him and went into the sepulcre and sawe the lynnen clothes lye</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 11:44"> and the napkyn that was aboute his heed not lyinge with the lynnen clothe but wrapped togeder in a place by it selfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 20:25; John 20:4; John 1:50"> Then went in also that other disciple which came fyrst to the sepulcre and he sawe and beleved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Isaiah 25:8; Isaiah 53:10–12"> For as yet they knew not the scriptures that he shuld ryse agayne from deeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 7:53; John 16:32"> And the disciples wet awaye agayne vnto their awne home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:5"> Mary stode with out at the sepulcre wepynge. And as she wept she bowed her selfe into the sepulcre</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:3–7; Revelation 3:4; Mark 16:5–6"> and sawe two angels in whyte sittyng the one at the heed and the other at the fete where they had layde the body of Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:15; John 2:4; 1 Samuel 1:8"> And they sayde vnto her: woman why wepest thou? She sayde vnto the: For they have taken awaye my lorde and I wote not where they have layde him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 21:4; Matthew 28:9"> When she had thus sayde she turned her selfe backe and sawe Iesus stondynge and knewe not that it was Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 20:13; John 18:7; John 1:38"> Iesus sayde vnto her: woman why wepest thou? Whom sekest thou? She supposynge that he had bene the gardener sayde vnto him. Syr yf thou have borne him hence tell me where thou hast layde him that I maye fet him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:3; John 1:38; Acts 10:3"> Iesus sayde vnto her: Mary. She turned her selfe and sayde vnto him: Rabboni which is to saye master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:28; Ephesians 4:8–10; Galatians 4:6–7"> Iesus sayde vnto her touche me not for I am not yet ascended to my father. But goo to my brethren and saye vnto them I ascende vnto my father and youre father to: my god and youre god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 24:10; Matthew 28:10; John 20:1"> Mary Magdalene came and tolde the disciples that she had sene the lorde and that he had spoken soche thinges vnto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 20:26; John 14:27; John 20:21"> The same daye at nyght which was the morowe after the saboth daye when the dores were shut where the disciples were assembled to geder for feare of the Iewes came Iesus and stode in the myddes and sayd to the: peace be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 16:22; John 16:20; John 20:27"> And when he had so sayde he shewed vnto them his hondes and his syde. Then were the disciples glad when they sawe the Lorde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 17:18–19; John 13:20; John 3:17"> Then sayde Iesus to them agayne: peace be with you. As my father sent me even so sende I you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 33:4; Genesis 2:7; Acts 8:15"> And when he had sayde that he brethed on them and sayde vnto the: Receave the holy goost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 18:18; Matthew 16:19; Mark 2:5–10"> Whosoevers synnes ye remyt they are remitted vnto the. And whosoevers synnes ye retayne they are retayned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 11:16; John 6:66–67; Hebrews 10:25"> But Thomas one of the twelve called Didymus was not with the when Iesus came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:30; Mark 16:11; Hebrews 3:12"> The other disciples sayd vnto him: we have sene the lorde. And he sayde vnto the: except I se in his hondes the prent of the nayles and put my fynger in the holes of the nayles and thrust my honde into his syde I will not beleve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 20:19; Isaiah 54:10; John 20:21"> And after .viii. dayes agayne his disciples were with in and Thomas with them. Then came Iesus when the dores were shut and stode in the myddes and sayde: peace be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 20:25; 1 John 1:1–2; John 20:20"> After that sayde he to Thomas: bringe thy fynger hether and se my hondes and bringe thy honde and thrust it into my syde and be not faythlesse but belevynge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:1; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 7:14"> Thomas answered and sayde vnto him: my Lorde and my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Hebrews 11:1"> Iesus sayde vnto him. Thomas because thou hast sene me therfore thou belevest: Happy are they that have not sene and yet beleve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 21:25; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Romans 15:4"> And many other signes dyd Iesus in the presence of his disciples which are not written in this boke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 10:43; John 3:15–16; 1 John 5:1"> These are written that ye myght beleve that Iesus is Christ the sonne of God and that in belevynge ye myght have lyfe thorowe his name.</span>
