<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 1:7; Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:2"> This is a true sayinge. Yf a man covet the office of a bysshope he desyreth a good worke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:6–9; 2 Timothy 2:24; Romans 12:13"> Ye and a bisshope must be fautlesse the husband of one wyfe sober discrete honestly aparelled harberous apt to teache</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 1:7; Ephesians 5:18; Titus 3:2"> not dronken no fighter not geve to filthy lucre: but gentle abhorrynge fightynge abhorrynge coveteousnes</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Titus 1:6; Genesis 18:19; 1 Timothy 3:12"> and one that rueleth his awne housse honestly havynge chyldren vnder obedience with all honeste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:13; Ephesians 5:24; Acts 20:28"> For yf a man cannot rule his owne housse how shall he care for the congregacion of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:4; Hebrews 5:12–13; 1 Peter 2:2"> He maye not be a yonge skoler lest he swell and faule into the iudgement of the evyll speaker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Timothy 2:26; Colossians 4:5"> He must also be well reported of amonge them which are with out forth lest he fall into rebuke and snare of the evyll speaker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:1; 1 Timothy 5:23; Acts 6:3–6"> Lykwyse must the deacons be honest not double tonged not geve vnto moche drynkinge nether vnto filthy lucre:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19; 2 John 1:9–10; 1 Timothy 1:5"> but havynge the mistery of the fayth in pure conscience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:22; 1 Timothy 3:13; Titus 1:6–7"> And let them fyrst be proved and then let them minister yf they be founde fautlesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Titus 2:3; Titus 3:2; 1 Timothy 3:2"> Even so must their wynes be honest not evyll speakers: but sober and faythfull in all thinges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; 1 Timothy 3:4–5; Philippians 1:1"> Let the deacons be the husbandes of one wyfe and suche as rule their chyldren well and their awne housholdes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Hebrews 6:10; Acts 21:35"> For they that minister well get them selves good degre and greate libertie in the fayth which is in Christ Iesu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:34; Philemon 1:22; 2 Corinthians 1:15–17"> These thinges write I vnto the trustinge to come shortly vnto the:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Ephesians 2:21–22; 1 Thessalonians 1:9"> but and yf I tarie longe that then thou mayst yet have knowledge how thou oughtest to behave thy silfe in the housse of God which is the congregacion of the livinge God the pillar and grounde of trueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:14; Hebrews 1:3; Mark 16:19"> And with out naye great is that mistery of godlines: God was shewed in the flesshe was iustified in the sprete was sene of angels was preached vnto the gentyls was beleved on in erth and receaved vp in glory.</span>
