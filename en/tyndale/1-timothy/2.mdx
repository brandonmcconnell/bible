<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 134:2; Psalms 63:4; Hebrews 10:22"> I wyll therfore that the men praye every where liftynge vp pure hondes without wrath or dowtinge.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 3:3–5; Revelation 2:19"> but with suche as becometh wemen that professe the worshippynge of God thorow good workes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:34–35; Ephesians 5:22–24; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> Let the woman learne in silence with all subieccion.</span>
