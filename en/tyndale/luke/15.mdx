<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:15; Matthew 9:10–13; Luke 5:29–32"> Then resorted vnto him all the publicans and synners for to heare him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:11; Luke 19:7; Luke 7:39"> And the pharises and scribes murmured sayinge: He receaved to his company synners and eateth with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then put he forthe this similitude to the sayinge:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Ezekiel 34:11–12; Matthew 18:12–14"> What man of you havynge an hundred shepe yf he loose one of thee doth not leve nynty and nyne in the wyldernes and goo after that which is loost vntyll he fynde him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Luke 19:9; Isaiah 46:3–4"> And when he hath founde him he putteth him on his shulders with ioye:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 15:10; Luke 15:24; 1 Peter 2:25"> And assone as he cometh home he calleth to gedder his lovers and neghbours sayinge vnto them: reioyse with me for I have founde my shepe which was loost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 5:32; Luke 15:10; Luke 15:32"> I say vnto you that lyke wyse ioye shalbe in heven over one synner that repenteth moore then over nynety and nyne iuste persons whiche nede noo repentaunce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Ezekiel 34:12; John 10:16"> Ether what woman havynge .x. grotes yf she loose one doth not lyght a candell and swepe the housse and seke diligently tyll she fynde it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 15:6–7"> And when she hath founde it she calleth her lovers and her neghbours sayinge: Reioyce with me for I have founde the groate which I had loost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 15:7; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Ezekiel 33:11"> Lykwyse I saye vnto you ioye is made in the presence of the angels of god over one synner that repenteth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 21:23–31"> And he sayde: a certayne man had two sonnes</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:16–17; Mark 12:44; Luke 15:30"> and the yonger of them sayde to his father: father geve me my parte of the goodes that to me belongeth. And he devided vnto them his substaunce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Jeremiah 2:5; Proverbs 21:17"> And not longe after the yonger sonne gaddered all that he had to gedder and toke his iorney into a farre countre and theare he wasted his goodes with royetous lyvinge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11; Amos 8:9–12; Hosea 2:9–14"> And when he had spent all that he had ther rose a greate derth thorow out all that same londe and he began to lacke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:3; 2 Timothy 2:25–26; Isaiah 1:5"> And he went and clave to a citesyn of that same countre which sent him to his felde to kepe his swyne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 6:19–21; Lamentations 4:5; Hosea 12:1"> And he wold fayne have filled his bely with the coddes that the swyne ate: and noo man gave him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:28; Ephesians 2:4–5; Luke 15:18–19"> Then he came to him selfe and sayde: how many hyred servauntes at my fathers have breed ynough and I dye for honger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 John 1:8–10; Luke 15:21"> I will aryse and goo to my father and will saye vnto him: father I have synned agaynst heven and before the</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 42:6; Luke 5:8; Psalms 84:10"> and am no moare worthy to be called thy sonne make me as one of thy hyred servauntes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 103:10–13; Psalms 86:5; Psalms 86:15"> And he arose and went to his father. And when he was yet a greate waye of his father sawe him and had compassion and ran and fell on his necke and kyssed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Luke 15:18–19; Jeremiah 3:13"> And the sonne sayd vnto him: father I have synned agaynst heven and in thy sight and am no moare worthy to be called thy sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 41:42; Esther 8:2; Revelation 6:11"> But his father sayde to his servautes: bringe forth that best garment and put it on him and put a rynge on his honde and showes on his fete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; Matthew 22:2–14; Psalms 63:5"> And bringe hidder that fatted caulfe and kyll him and let vs eate and be mery:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 2:5; Ezekiel 34:16; Luke 19:10"> for this my sonne was deed and is alyve agayne he was loste and is now founde. And they began to be merye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 150:4; Jeremiah 31:4; Luke 15:11–12"> The elder brother was in the felde and when he cam and drewe nye to the housse he herde minstrelcy and daunsynge</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and called one of his servauntes and axed what thoose thinges meate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 9:17; Philemon 1:16; Acts 22:13"> And he sayd vnto him: thy brother is come and thy father had kylled the fatted caulfe because he hath receaved him safe and sounde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jonah 4:9; 1 Samuel 18:8; Jonah 4:1–4"> And he was angry and wolde not goo in. Then came his father out and entreated him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 18:11–12; Zechariah 7:3; Isaiah 65:5"> He answered and sayde to his father: Loo these many yeares have I done the service nether brake at eny tyme thy commaundment and yet gavest thou me never soo moche as a kyd to make mery with my lovers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Luke 15:12–13; Luke 15:32"> but assone as this thy sonne was come which hath devoured thy goodes with harlootes thou haste for his pleasure kylled the fatted caulfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Romans 9:4; Romans 11:1"> And he sayd vnto him: Sonne thou wast ever with me and all that I have is thyne:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–10; Luke 15:24; Isaiah 35:10"> it was mete that we shuld make mery and be glad: for this thy brother was deed and is a lyve agayne: and was loste and is founde.</span>
