<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 23:24; Genesis 49:10; Luke 9:7"> In the fiftenthe yeare of the raygne of Tiberius the Emperoure Pontius Pylate beinge leftenaut of Iurie and Herode beinge Tetrach of Galile and his brother Philip Tetrach in Iturea and in the region of Traconites and Lysanias the Tetrach of Abyline</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:6; John 18:24; Luke 1:80"> when Anna and Cayphas were the hye prestes: the worde of God came vnto Iohn the sonne of zacharias in the wildernes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 1:77; Matthew 3:5–6; Acts 13:24"> And he came in to all the coostes aboute Iordan preachynge the baptyme of repentaunce for the remission of synnes</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; John 1:23; Luke 1:76–79"> at it is written in the boke of the sayinges of Esaias the Prophet which sayeth: The voyce of a cryar in wyldernes prepare the waye of the Lorde make hys pathes strayght.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:4; Isaiah 42:16; Isaiah 45:2"> Every valley shalbe fylled and every moutayne and hyll shalbe brought lowe. And crocked thinges shalbe made streight: and the rough wayes shalbe made smoth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 52:10; Isaiah 40:5; Psalms 98:2–3"> and all flesshe shall se the saveour sent of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 23:33; Matthew 12:34; 1 Thessalonians 1:10"> Then sayde he to the people that were come to be baptysed of him: O generacion of vipers who hath taught you to flye from the wrath to come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:27–31; Acts 26:20; Isaiah 1:16–18"> Bringe forth due frutes of repentaunce and begynne not to saye in youre selves we have Abraham to oure father. For I saye vnto you: God is able of these stones to reyse vp chyldren vnto Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 7:19; John 15:6; Luke 13:9"> Now also ys the axe leyd vnto the rote of the trees: so that every tree which bringeth not forth good frute shalbe hewe doune and caste in to the fyre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:37; Luke 3:14; Acts 16:30"> And the people axed him sayinge: What shall we do then?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 58:7–11; 1 John 3:17; 1 Timothy 6:18"> He answered and sayde vnto them: He that hath two coottes let him parte with him that hath none: and he that hath meate let him do lykewyse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 7:29; Luke 15:1–2; Luke 18:13"> Then came ther Publicans to be baptised and sayde vnto him: Master what shall we do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 19:8; Ephesians 4:28; 1 Corinthians 6:10"> And he sayde vnto the: requyre no more then that which ys appoynted vnto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5–6; Exodus 20:16; Exodus 23:1"> The soudyoures lyke wyse demaunded of hym sayinge: and what shall we do? And he sayde to them: Do violence to noo man: nether trouble eny man wrongfully: but be content with youre wages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 10:24; John 3:28–29; John 1:19–28"> As the people were in a doute and all men disputed in their hertes of Iohn whether he were very Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Acts 1:5; Acts 11:15–16"> Ihon answered and sayde to them all: I baptyse you with water but a stronger then I cometh after me whose shue latchet I am not worthy to vnlouse: he will baptise you with the holy goost and with fyre:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:7; Matthew 13:30; Micah 4:12"> which hath his fanne in his hond and will pourge his floore and will gader the corne into his barne: but the chaffe wyll he bourne with fyre that never shalbe quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:29–36; John 1:34; John 1:29"> And many other thinges in his exhortacion preached he vnto the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 11:2; Mark 6:17–18"> Then Herode the Tetrach (when he was rebuked of him for Herodias his brother Philippes wyfe and for all the evyls which Herod had done)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:17–22; John 3:24; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16"> added this above all and leyd Iohn in preson.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 1:32–34; Matthew 3:13–17; John 12:27–28"> And that fortuned as all the people receaved baptyme (and when Iesus was baptised and dyd praye) that heaven was opened</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; 2 Peter 1:17–18; John 1:32"> and the holy goost came doune in a bodely shape lyke a dove vpo him and a voyce came from heven sayinge: Thou arte my dere sonne in the do I delyte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Matthew 13:55; Numbers 4:3"> And Iesus him silfe was about thirty yere of age when he begane beinge as men supposed the sonne of Ioseph. which Ioseph was the sonne of Heli:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> which was the sonne of Mathat: which was the sonne of Levi: which was the sonne of Melchi: which was the sonne of Ianna: which was the sonne of Ioseph:</span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> which was the sonne of Simeon: which was the sonne of Iuda: which was the sonne of Ioseph: which was the sonne of Ionam: which was the sonne of Heliachim:</span>
