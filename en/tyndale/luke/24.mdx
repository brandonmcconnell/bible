<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 20:1–2; Mark 16:1–8; Matthew 28:1–8"> On the morowe after the saboth erly in the morninge they came vnto the toumbe and brought the odoures which they had prepared and other wemen with them</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:3–4; Matthew 27:60–66; Mark 15:46–47"> And they founde the stone rouled awaye from the sepulcre</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:23; John 20:6–7; Matthew 16:5"> and went in: but founde not the body of the Lorde Iesu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 16:5; John 20:11–12; Acts 1:10"> And it happened as they were amased therat: Beholde two men stode by them in shynynge vestures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 16:5–6; Matthew 28:3–5; Revelation 1:18"> And as they were a frayde and bowed doune their faces to the erth: they sayd to them: why seke ye the lyvinge amonge the deed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 17:22–23; Luke 9:22; Mark 8:31"> He is not here: but is rysen. Remember how he spake vnto you when he was yet with you in Galile</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 16:21"> sayinge: that the sonne of man must be delyvered into the hondes of synfull men and be crucified and the thyrde daye ryse agayne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 12:16; John 14:26; John 2:19–22"> And they remembred his wordes</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 24:22–24; Mark 16:7–8; Matthew 28:7–8"> and returned from the sepulcre and tolde all these thinges vnto the eleven and to all the remanaunt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 27:56; John 20:11–18; Luke 8:2–3"> It was Mary Magdalen and Ioanna and Mary Iacobi and other that were with the which tolde these thinges vnto the Apostles</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 9:16; Mark 16:11; Genesis 19:14"> and their wordes semed vnto them fayned thinges nether beleved they them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 20:3–10"> Then aroose Peter and ran vnto the sepulcre and stouped in and sawe the lynnen cloothes layde by them selfe and departed wondrynge in him selfe at that which had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 24:18; Mark 16:12–13"> And beholde two of them went that same daye to a toune which was from Ierusalem about thre scoore for longes called Emaus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:45; Deuteronomy 6:7; Malachi 3:6"> and they talked togeder of all these thinges that had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:20; John 14:18–19; Luke 24:36"> And it chaunsed as they commened togeder and reasoned that Iesus him selfe drue neare and went with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 20:14; John 21:4; Luke 24:31"> But their eyes were holden that they coulde not knowe him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:20–22; John 16:6; Ezekiel 9:4–6"> And he sayde vnto them: What maner of communicacions are these that ye have one to another as ye walke and are sadde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 19:25"> And the one of them named Cleophas answered and sayd vnto him: arte thou only a straunger in Ierusalem and haste not knowen the thinges which have chaunsed therin in these dayes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; Acts 7:22; John 4:19"> To whom he sayd: what thinges? And they sayd vnto him: of Iesus of Nazareth which was a Prophet myghtie in dede and worde before god and all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:13–15; Acts 4:8–10; Luke 23:13"> And how the hye prestes and oure rulers delyvered him to be condempned to deeth: and have crucified him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:68; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Luke 2:38"> But we trusted that it shuld have bene he that shuld have delyvered Israel. And as touchynge all these thinges to daye is even the thyrd daye that they were done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 16:9–10; Matthew 28:7–8; John 20:1–2"> Ye and certayne wemen also of oure company made vs astonyed which came erly vnto the sepulcre,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and founde not his boddy: and came sayinge that they had sene a vision of angels which sayde that he was alyve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 24:12; John 20:1–10"> And certayne of them which were with vs went their waye to the sepulcre and founde it even so as the wemen had sayde: but him they sawe not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 7:18; Mark 9:19; Hebrews 5:11–12"> And he sayde vnto the: O foles and slowe of herte to beleve all that the prophetes have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:11; Isaiah 53:1–12; Zechariah 13:7"> Ought not Christ to have suffred these thinges and to enter into his glory?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Luke 24:44; Deuteronomy 18:15"> And he began at Moses and at all the prophetes and interpreted vnto them in all scriptures which were wrytten of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 6:48; Genesis 19:2; Genesis 32:26"> And they drue neye vnto the toune wich they went to. And he made as though he wolde have gone further.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 4:8; Luke 14:23; Genesis 19:3"> But they constrayned him sayinge: abyde with vs for it draweth towardes nyght and the daye is farre passed. And he went in to tary with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; Luke 24:35; Matthew 15:36"> And it came to passe as he sate at meate with them he toke breed blessed it brake and gave to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 8:59; Luke 4:30"> And their eyes were openned and they knewe him: and he vnnisshed out of their syght.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 24:45; Jeremiah 23:29; Hebrews 4:12"> And they sayde betwene them selves: dyd not oure hertes burne with in vs whyll he talked with vs by the waye and as he opened to vs the scriptures?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 16:13; John 20:19–26; Acts 1:14"> And they roose vp the same houre and returned agayne to Ierusalem and founde the eleven gadered to geder and them that were with them</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5; Mark 16:7; Luke 22:54–62"> which sayde: the Lorde is rysen in dede and hath apered to Simon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 16:12–13; Acts 2:42; Luke 24:30–31"> And they tolde what thinges was done in the waye and how they knewe him in breakynge of breed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:16; Isaiah 57:18; Mark 16:14"> As they thus spake Iesus him selfe stode in the myddes of them and sayde vnto them: peace be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:13; Matthew 14:26–27; Job 4:14–16"> And they were abasshed and afrayde supposinge that they had sene a sprete</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:14; Hebrews 4:13; Daniel 4:19"> And he sayde vnto them: Why are ye troubled and why do thoughtes aryse in youre hertes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 20:27; 1 John 1:1; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> Beholde my hondes and my fete that it is even my selfe. Handle me and se: for spretes have not flesshe and bones as ye se me have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And when he had thus spoken he shewed them his hondes and his fete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 21:5; Psalms 126:1–2; Genesis 45:26–28"> And whyll they yet beleved not for ioye and wondred he sayde vnto the: Have ye here eny meate?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And they gave him a pece of a broyled fisshe and of an hony combe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 10:41"> And he toke it and ate it before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Luke 24:26–27; Zechariah 9:9"> And he sayde vnto them. These are the wordes which I spake vnto you whyll I was yet with you: that all must be fulfilled which were written of me in the lawe of Moses and in the Prophetes and in the Psalmes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Acts 16:14; 1 John 5:20; Psalms 119:18"> Then openned he their wyttes that they myght vnderstond the scriptures</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–12; Isaiah 50:6; Luke 24:44"> and sayde vnto them. Thus is it written and thus it behoved Christ to suffre and to ryse agayne from deeth the thyrde daye</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 5:31; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 2:38"> and that repentaunce and remission of synnes shuld be preached in his name amonge all nacions and must beginne at Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 2:32; Acts 13:31"> And ye are witnesses of these thinges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 1:4; Joel 2:28–32"> And beholde I will sende the promes of my father apon you. But tary ye in the cite of Ierusalem vntyll ye be endewed with power from an hye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Mark 11:1"> And he ledde the out into Bethany and lyfte vp his hondes and blest them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Acts 1:9; Mark 16:19; 2 Kings 2:11"> And it cam to passe as he blessed the he departed from the and was caryed vp in to heven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 28:9; John 16:22; Psalms 30:11"> And they worshipped him and returned to Ierusalem with greate ioye</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Mark 16:20; Acts 2:46–47; Acts 5:41–42"> and were continually in the temple praysinge and laudinge God. Amen.</span>
