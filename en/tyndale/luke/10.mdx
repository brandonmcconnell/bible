<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 6:7–13; Luke 9:52; Numbers 11:24–26"> After these thinges the Lorde apoynted other seventie also and sent them two and two before him into every citie and place whither he him silfe wolde come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:36–38; John 4:35–38; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9"> And he sayde vnto them the harvest is greate: but the laborers are feawe. Praye therfore the Lorde of the harvest to send forth laborers into his hervest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; Acts 20:29; Ezekiel 2:3–6"> Goo youre wayes: beholde I sende you forthe as lambes amonge wolves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 22:35; 2 Kings 4:29; Luke 10:4–12"> Beare noo wallet nether scryppe nor shues and salute noo man by the waye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:6; Matthew 10:12–13; Isaiah 57:19"> Into whatsoever housse ye enter fyrst saye: Peace be to this housse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:16; 2 Corinthians 2:15–16; Ephesians 5:6"> And yf the sonne of peace be theare youre peace shall rest vpon him: yf not that shall returne to you agayne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:17–18; Matthew 10:10–11; Galatians 6:6"> And in the same housse tary still eatinge and drinkinge soche as they have. For the laborer is worthy of his rewarde. Go not from housse to housse:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:27; Matthew 10:40; Luke 10:10"> and in to whatso ever citye ye enter yf they receave you eate soche thinges as are set before you</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; John 3:5; Luke 10:11"> and heale the sicke that are theare and saye vnto them: the kyngdome of God is come nye vpon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Matthew 10:14; Acts 13:51"> But into whatsoever citie ye shall enter yf they receave you not goo youre wayes out into the stretes of the same and saye:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 10:9; Acts 13:51; Acts 13:26"> even the very dust which cleaveth on vs of your citie we wipe of agaynst you: Not withstondinge marke this that the kyngdome of God was come nie vpon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 10:15; Matthew 11:24; Lamentations 4:6"> Ye and I saye vnto you: that it shalbe easier in that daye for Sodom then for that cytie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 23:1–18; Joel 3:4–8; Ezekiel 26:1–26"> Wo be to the Chorazin: wo be to the Bethsaida. For yf the miracles had bene done in Tyre and Sidon which have bene done in you they had a greate whyle agone repeted sitting in heere and asshes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 15:22–25; Romans 2:1; Luke 12:47–48"> Neverthelesse it shalbe easier for Tyre and Sidon at the iudgement then for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–15; Matthew 4:13; Luke 13:28"> And thou Capernau which art exalted to heaven shalt be thrust doune to hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 13:20; Matthew 10:40; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> He that heareth you heareth me: and he that dispiseth you despiseth me: and he that dispiseth me despiseth him that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Mark 16:17; Luke 9:1"> And the sevetie returned agayne with ioye sayinge: Lorde even the very devyls are subdued to vs thorowe thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 12:7–9; Isaiah 14:12; Revelation 9:1"> And he sayde vnto them: I sawe satan as it had bene lightenyng faule doune from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 91:13; Mark 16:18; Romans 16:20"> Beholde I geve vnto you power to treade on serpetes and scorpions and over all maner power of the enimye and no thinge shall hurte you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 20:15; Revelation 20:12; Hebrews 12:23"> Neverthelesse in this reioyse not that the spretes are vnder youre power: but reioyse be cause youre names are wrytten in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3; Matthew 13:11–16; Matthew 11:25–27"> That same tyme reioysed Iesus in the sprete and sayde: I confesse vnto the father Lorde of heaven and erth that thou hast hyd these thynges from the wyse and prudent and hast opened them to the babes. Even so father for soo pleased it the.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 11:27; John 1:18; John 6:44–46"> All thinges are geven me of my father. And no man knoweth who the sonne is but the father: nether who the father is save the sonne and he to who the sonne wyll shewe him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:16–17"> And he turned to his disciples and sayde secretly: Happy are the eyes which se that ye se.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 8:56; Hebrews 11:13; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> For I tell you that many prophetes and kynges have desired to se those thinges which ye se and have not sene them: and to heare those thinges which ye heare and have not hearde them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–39; Luke 18:18; Matthew 19:16–19"> And beholde a certayne Lawere stode vp and tempted him sayinge: Master what shall I do to inheret eternall life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Isaiah 8:20; Galatians 3:21–22"> He sayd vnto him: What is written in the lawe? How redest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 12:30–31; Matthew 22:37–40; Deuteronomy 6:5"> And he answered and sayde: Loue thy Lorde God with all thy hert and with all thy soule and with all thy stregthe and with all thy mynde: and thy neghbour as thy sylfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:11; Leviticus 18:5; Matthew 19:17"> And he sayde vnto him: Thou hast answered right. This do and thou shalt live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Romans 4:2; Leviticus 19:34"> He willinge to iustifie him silfe sayde vnto Iesus: Who is then my neghbour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 88:4; Luke 18:31; Lamentations 2:12"> Iesus answered and sayde: A certayne man descended from Hierusalem into Hierico and fell in to the hondes of theves which robbed him of his raymet and wounded him and departed levynge him halfe deed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 3:16–18; Psalms 69:20; Jeremiah 5:31"> And by chaunce ther came a certayne preste that same waye and when he sawe him he passed by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 27:10; Acts 18:17; Psalms 109:25"> And lykewyse a Levite when he was come nye to the place wet and loked on him and passed by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 8:50; Jeremiah 38:7–13; Luke 7:13"> Then a certayne Samaritane as he iornyed came nye vnto him and when he sawe him had compassion on him</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 12:20; Psalms 147:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> and went to and bounde vp his woundes and poured in oyle and wyne and put him on his awne beaste and brought him to a comen ynne and made provision for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Luke 14:13; Romans 16:23"> And on the morowe when he departed he toke out two pece and gave them to the host and sayde vnto him. Take cure of him and whatsoever thou spedest moare when I come agayne I will recompence the.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 7:42; Matthew 21:28–31; Matthew 17:25"> Which now of these thre thynkest thou was neighbour vnto him that fell into the theves hondes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 3:16–18; Micah 6:8; 1 Peter 2:21"> And he sayde: he that shewed mercy on him. Then sayde Iesus vnto him. Goo and do thou lyke wyse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:19–20; John 11:1–5; John 12:1–3"> It fortuned as they wet that he entred in to a certayne toune. And a certayne woman named Martha receaved him into her housse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 8:34; Luke 8:35; John 12:3"> And this woman had a sister called Mary which sate at Iesus fete and hearde his preachinge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 6:27; Luke 12:29; Matthew 14:15"> And Martha was combred about moche servinge and stode and sayde: Master doest thou not care that my sister hath leeft me to minister alone? Byd her therfore that she helpe me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; Luke 21:34; Matthew 6:25–34"> And Iesus answered and sayde vnto her: Martha Martha thou carest and arte troubled about many thinges:</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Psalms 16:5–6; John 10:27–28"> verely one is nedfull. Mary hath chosen her that good parte which shall not be taken awaye from her.</span>
