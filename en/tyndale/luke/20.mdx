<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:23–27; Luke 19:47–48; Acts 6:12"> And that fortuned in one of those dayes as he taught the people in the temple and preached the gospell: the hye prestes and the scribes came with the elders</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; Mark 11:28–33; John 2:18"> and spake vnto him sayinge. Tell vs by what auctorite thou doest these thinges? Ether who is he that gave ye this auctorite?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 15:2–3; Luke 22:68; Colossians 4:6"> He answered and saide vnto the: I also will axe you a questio and answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:18; Matthew 11:7–19; Matthew 17:11–12"> The baptyme of Iohn: was it from heaven or of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:34; John 1:15–18; John 1:30"> And they thought with in them selves sayinge: yf we shall saye from heaven: he will saye: why then beleved ye him not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 11:9; John 10:41"> But and yf we shall saye of men all the people will stone vs. For they be persuaded that Ihon is a Prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; 2 Peter 3:3; Isaiah 41:28"> And they answered that they coulde not tell whence it was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:4–5; Matthew 21:27; Mark 11:33"> And Iesus sayde vnto them: nether tell I you by what auctorite I do these thinges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 21:33–46; Mark 12:1–12; Isaiah 5:1–7"> Then begane he to put forthe to the people this similitude. A certayne man planted a vyneyarde and let it forthe to fermers and went him selfe into a straunge countre for a greate season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Nehemiah 9:26; Matthew 21:34–36"> And when the tyme was come he sent a servaut to his tennauntes that they shuld geve him of the frutes of the vyneyarde. And the tennauntes dyd bet him and sent him awaye empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 10:1; Hebrews 11:36–37; 1 Thessalonians 2:2"> And agayne he sent yet another servaunt. And they dyd bet him and foule entreated him also and sent him awaye emptye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> More over he sent the thyrde to and him they wouded and cast out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 3:17; John 3:16–17; Jeremiah 36:7"> Then sayde the lorde of the vyneyarde: what shall I do? I will sende my deare sonne him peradventure they will reverence when they se him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Hebrews 1:2; Acts 2:23"> But when the fermers sawe him they thought in them selves sayinge: this is the heyre come let vs kyll him that the inheritaunce maye be oures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:12; Mark 12:6–9; Matthew 21:37–40"> And they cast him out of the vyneyarde and kylled him. Now what shall the Lorde of the vyneyarde do vnto them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:27; Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:41"> He will come and destroye those fermers and will let out his vyneyarde to other. When they hearde that they sayde: God forbyd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 118:22; Isaiah 28:16; Acts 4:11"> And he behelde them and sayde: what meaneth this then that is wrytten: The stone that the bylders refused the same is made the heed corner stone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; Zechariah 12:3; Daniel 2:44–45"> whosoever stomble at that stone shalbe broken: but on whosoever it faul vpon it wyll grynde him to powder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 19:47–48; Luke 20:14; Matthew 21:45–46"> And the hye Prestes and the Scribes the same howre went about to laye hondes on him but they feared the people. For they perceaved that he had spoken this similitude agaynst them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 11:54; Matthew 22:15–22; Matthew 27:2"> And they watched him and sent forth spies which shuld fayne them selves perfecte to take him in his wordes and to delyvre him vnto the power and auctorite of the debite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4–5; Matthew 26:49–50; Matthew 22:16"> And they axed him sayinge: Master we knowe that thou sayest and teachest ryght nother cosiderest thou eny manes degre but teachest the waye of God truly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:37; Mark 12:14–17; Acts 5:37"> Ys it laufull for vs to geve Cesar tribute or no?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:18; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Luke 5:22"> He perceaved their craftynes and sayde vnto them: why tept ye me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 18:28; Acts 11:28; Acts 26:32"> Shewe me a peny. Whose ymage and superscripcion hath it? They answered and sayde: Cesars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 13:6–7; Mark 12:17; 1 Peter 2:13–17"> And he sayde vnto them: Geve then vnto Cesar that which belongeth vnto Cesar: and to God that which pertayneth to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 20:20; Proverbs 26:4–5; Titus 1:10"> And they coulde not reprove his sayinge before the people. But they marvayled at his answer and helde their peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 4:1–2; 1 Corinthians 15:12; Mark 12:18–27"> Then came to him certayne of the Saduces which denye that ther is eny resurreccion. And they axed him</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Genesis 38:8; Genesis 38:11"> sayinge: Master Moses wrote vnto vs if eny mannes brother dye havinge a wyfe and the same dye with out yssue: that then his brother shuld take his wyfe and reyse vp seede vnto his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20; Jeremiah 22:30"> Ther were seven brethren and the fyrste toke a wyfe and dyed with out children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the seconde toke the wyfe and he dyed chyldlesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the thyrde toke her and in lyke wyse the resydue of the seven and leeft no chyldren be hynde them and dyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:4; Ecclesiastes 9:5; Hebrews 9:27"> Last of all the woman dyed also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 22:24–28; Mark 12:19–23"> Now at the resurreccio whose wyfe of them shall she be? For seven had her to wyfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 16:8; Luke 17:27; Hebrews 13:4"> Iesus answered and sayde vnto them. The chyldre of this worlde mary wyves and are maryed</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; John 5:29; Daniel 12:2–3"> but they which shalbe made worthy to enioye that worlde and the resurreccion from deeth nether mary wyves nether are maryed</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Philippians 3:21; Matthew 22:30"> nor yet can dye eny moare. For they are equall vnto the angels: and are the sonnes of God in as moche as they are the chyldre of the resurreccion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Mark 12:26–27; Acts 7:30–32"> And that the deed shall ryse agayne even Moses signified besydes the busshe when he sayde: the Lorde God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Iacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:10–11"> For he is not the God of the deed but of them which live. For all live in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 23:9; Mark 12:28–34; Matthew 22:34–40"> Then certayne of the Pharises answered and sayde: Master thou hast well sayde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Mark 12:34; Proverbs 26:5"> And after that durst they not axe him eny question at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 1:1; Matthew 22:41–45"> Then sayde he vnto them: how saye they that Christ is Davids sonne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Acts 2:34–35; Hebrews 1:13"> And David him selfe sayth in the boke of the Psalmes: The Lorde sayde vnto my Lorde syt on my right honde</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 19:27; Psalms 110:1; Revelation 19:14–21"> tyll I make thine enemys thy fothe stole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Luke 1:31–35; Matthew 1:23"> Seinge David calleth him Lorde: How is he then his sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 15:10; Mark 12:38–39; Mark 8:34"> Then in the audience of all the people he sayde vnto his disciples</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 11:43; Mark 12:38–39; Luke 14:7"> beware of the Scribes which desyre to goo in longe clothinge: and love gretynges in the markets and the hyest seates in the synagoges and chefe roumes at feastes</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 12:40; James 3:1; Titus 1:16"> which devoure widdowes houses and that vnder a coloure of longe prayinge: the same shall receave greater damnacion.</span>
