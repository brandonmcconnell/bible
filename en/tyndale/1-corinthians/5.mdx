<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; Revelation 2:21; Colossians 3:5"> There goeth a commen sayinge that ther is fornicacion amonge you and soche fornicacion as is not once named amonge the gentyls: that one shuld have his fathers wyfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 2:20–22; 2 Corinthians 7:7–11; 1 Corinthians 5:13"> And ye swell and have not rather sorowed that he which hath done this dede myght be put from amonge you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:17; 2 Corinthians 13:2"> For I verely as absent in body even so present in sprete have determyned all redy (as though I were present) of him that hath done this dede</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 20:23; Colossians 3:17; Matthew 16:19"> in the name of oure Lorde Iesu Christ when ye are gaddered togedder and my sprete with the power of the Lorde Iesus Christ</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:14–15; Galatians 6:1–2"> to deliver him vnto Satan for the destruccion of the flesshe that the sprete maye be saved in the daye of the Lorde Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 5:9; 1 Corinthians 15:33; James 4:16"> Youre reioysinge is not good: knowe ye not that a lytle leve sowreth the whole lompe of dowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–20; Ephesians 4:22; Colossians 3:5–9"> Pourge therfore the olde leven that ye maye be newe dowe as ye are swete breed. For Christ oure esterlambe is offered vp for vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 2:1–2; Deuteronomy 16:3; Exodus 12:15"> Therfore let vs kepe holy daye not with olde leve nether with the leven of maliciousnes and wickednes: but with the swete breed of purenes and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:14; 2 Corinthians 6:14"> I wrote vnto you in a pistle that ye shuld not company with fornicatours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:27; John 17:15–16; Matthew 5:14–16"> And I meant not at all of the fornicatours of this worlde ether of the coveteous or of extorsioners ether of the ydolaters: for then must ye nedes have gone out of the worlde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:14; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> But now I write vnto you that ye company not togedder yf eny that is called a brother be a fornicator or coveteous or a worshipper of ymages ether a raylar ether a dronkard or an extorcionar: with him that is soche se that ye eate not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:11; 1 Timothy 3:7; Luke 12:14"> For what have I to do to iudge them which are with out? Do ye not iudge them that are with in?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:7; Deuteronomy 13:5; Deuteronomy 21:21"> Them that are with out God shall iudge. Put awaye from amonge you that evyll parson.</span>
