<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; 1 Corinthians 11:34; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21"> When I am come whosoever ye shall alowe by youre letters them will I sende to bringe youre liberalite vnto Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:4; 2 Corinthians 8:19; Romans 15:25"> And yf it be mete that I goo they shall go with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 19:21; 1 Corinthians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 1:15–17"> I will come vnto you after I have gone over Macedonia. For I will goo thorowout Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 15:3; Romans 15:24; Acts 20:38"> With you paraveture I wyll abyde awhyle: or els winter that ye maye brynge me on my waye whyther soever I goo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 18:21; 1 Corinthians 4:19; James 4:15"> I will not se you now in my passage: but I trust to abyde a whyle with you yf God shall suffre me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:1; Exodus 23:16; Acts 18:19"> I will tary at Ephesus untyll whitsontyde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12; Acts 14:27; Colossians 4:3"> For a greate dore and a frutefull is opened vnto me: and ther are many adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; Romans 16:21"> If Timotheus come se that he be with out feare with you. For he worketh the worke of the Lorde as I doo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:12; Acts 15:33; Titus 2:15"> Let no man despyse him: but convaye him forthe in peace that he maye come vnto me. For I loke for him with the brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:5; Titus 3:4"> To speake of brother Apollo: I greatly desyred him to come vnto you with the brethren but his mynde was not at all to come at this tyme. How be it he will come when he shall have conveniet tyme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:13–18; Ephesians 6:10"> Watche ye stonde fast in the fayth auyte you lyke men and be stronge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 13:34–35; 1 Peter 4:8; 1 Timothy 1:5"> Let all youre busynes be done in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:16; Romans 16:5; Romans 16:2"> Brethren (ye knowe the housse of Stephana how that they are the fyrst frutes of Achaia and that they have appoynted them selves to minister vnto the saynctes)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:12; 3 John 1:8"> I beseche you that ye be obedient vnto soche and to all that helpe and laboure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; Philippians 2:30; Philemon 1:13"> I am gladde of the comynge of Stephana Fortunatus and Achaicus: for that which was lackinge on youre parte they have supplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philemon 1:7; 2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:12"> They have comforted my sprete and youres. Loke therfore that ye knowe them that are soche.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 18:2; Philemon 1:2; Romans 16:3–5"> The congregacions of Asia salute you. Aquila and Priscilla salute you moche in the Lorde and so doeth the congregacion that is in their housse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 16:16; 1 Thessalonians 5:26; 1 Peter 5:14"> All the brethren grete you. Grete ye one another with an holy kysse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Colossians 4:18; Galatians 6:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:17"> The salutacion of me Paul with myne awne hande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 6:24; John 14:21; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> Yf eny man love not the Lorde Iesus Christ the same be anathema maranatha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Romans 16:23"> The grace of the Lorde Iesus Christ be with you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:16; Revelation 3:19; 2 Corinthians 12:15"> My love be with you all in Christ Iesu. Amen. ‘The epistle vnto the Corinthyans sent from Philippos by Stephana and Fortunatus and Acaichus and Timotheus.’</span>
