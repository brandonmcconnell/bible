<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 14:37; 1 Corinthians 14:1–18"> In spirituall thinges brethren I wolde not have you ignoraunt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 4:3; 1 Thessalonians 1:9; Psalms 115:5"> Ye knowe that ye were gentyls and went youre wayes vnto domme ydoles even as ye were ledde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 4:2–3; Matthew 16:16–17; Romans 10:9"> Wherfore I declare vnto you that no man speakynge in the sprete of god defieth Iesus. Also no man can saye that Iesus is the lorde: but by the holy goost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Romans 12:4–6"> Ther are diversities of gyftes verely yet but one sprete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–12; Romans 12:6–8; Romans 14:8–9"> And ther are differences of administracions and yet but one lorde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:7; 1 Corinthians 12:11; John 5:17"> And ther are divers maners of operacions and yet but one God which worketh all thinges that are wrought in all creatures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10–11; Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> The gyftes of the sprete are geven to every man to proffit ye congregacion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 2:6–10; Psalms 143:10"> To one is geven thorow the spirite the vtteraunce of wisdome? To another is geven the vtteraunce of knowledge by ye same sprete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 5:14–15; 2 Corinthians 4:13; Ephesians 2:8"> To another is geuen fayth by the same sprete. To another the gyftes of healynge by the same sprete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:39; 1 John 4:1; Mark 16:17"> To another power to do myracles. To another prophesie? To another iudgement of spretes. To another divers tonges. To another the interpretacion of toges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4; Hebrews 2:4; 2 Corinthians 10:13"> And these all worketh even the silfe same sprete devydynge to every man severall gyftes even as he will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 12:4–5; Colossians 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> For as the body is one and hath many membres and all the membres of one body though they be many yet are but one body: even so is Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5; Colossians 3:11; Galatians 3:28"> For in one sprete are we all baptysed to make one body whether we be Iewes or getyls whether we be bonde or fre: and have all dronke of one sprete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27–28; 1 Corinthians 12:12; Ephesians 4:25"> For the body is not one member but many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; 2 Kings 14:9"> Yf the fote saye: I am not the honde therfore I am not of the body: is he therfore not of the body:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:22; Philippians 2:3"> And if the eare saye I am not the eye: therfore I am not of the body: is he therfore not of the body?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:21; Psalms 139:13–16; Proverbs 20:12"> If all the body were an eye where were then the eare? If all were hearynge: where were the smellynge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:28; Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> But now hath god disposed the membres every one of them in the body at his awne pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> If they were all one member: where were the body?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:14; 1 Corinthians 12:12"> Now are ther many membres yet but one body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 10:1–5; Nehemiah 4:16–21; Job 29:11"> And the eye can not saye vnto the honde I have no nede of the: nor the heed also to the fete. I have no nede of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 14:28; Ecclesiastes 9:14–15; Ecclesiastes 4:9–12"> Ye rather a greate deale those membres of the body which seme to be most feble are most necessary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 3:21; Genesis 3:7"> And apon those membres of that body which we thinke lest honest put we most honestie on. And oure vngodly parties have most beauty on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 3:11; Genesis 2:25"> For oure honest members nede it not. But God hath so disposed the body ad hath geven most honoure to that parte which laked</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; 1 Corinthians 1:10–12; John 17:21–26"> lest there shuld be eny stryfe in the body: but that the members shuld indifferetly care one for another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Galatians 6:2; Hebrews 13:3"> And yf one member suffer all suffer with him: yf one member be had in honoure all members be glad also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 4:12; Colossians 1:24; Ephesians 5:30"> Ye are the body of Christ and members one of another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–13; Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 12:18"> And God hath also ordeyned in the congregacion fyrst the Apostels secondarely prophetes thyrdly teachers then the that do miracles: after that the gyftes of healynge helpers governers diversite of tonges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 12:14–20"> Are all Apostles? Are all Prophetes? Are all teachers? Are all doars of miracles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10"> Have all the gyftes of healinge? Do all speake with tonges? Do all interprete?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:1; 1 Corinthians 14:39; Matthew 5:6"> Covet after the best giftes. Amd yet shewe I vnto you a moare excellent waye.</span>
