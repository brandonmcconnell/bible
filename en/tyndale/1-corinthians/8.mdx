<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:7; Isaiah 5:21; Romans 14:3"> To speake of thinges dedicate vnto ydols we are sure that we all have knowledge. knowledge maketh a man swell: but love edifieth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:12; 1 Corinthians 3:18; Proverbs 26:12"> If eny man thinke that he knoweth eny thinge he knoweth nothynge yet as he ought to knowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:8–9; Exodus 33:17; Nahum 1:7"> But yf eny man love god the same is knowen of him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:4; Jeremiah 2:28; Galatians 4:8"> And though ther be that are called goddes whether in heven other in erth (as ther be goddes many and lordes many)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:36; John 1:3; Malachi 2:10"> yet vnto vs is there but one god which is the father of whom are all thinges and we in him: and one lorde Iesus Christ by whom are all thinges and we by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 14:14; 1 Corinthians 10:28–29; Romans 14:22–23"> But every man hath not knowledge. For some suppose that ther is an ydoll vntyll this houre and eate as of a thinge offered vnto the ydole and so their consciences beynge yet weake are defyled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 14:17; Hebrews 13:9; 1 Corinthians 6:13"> Meate maketh vs not acceptable to god. Nether yf we eate are we the better. Nether yf we eate not are we the worsse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Romans 14:20–21; 1 Peter 2:16"> But take hede that youre libertie cause not the weake to faule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:28–29; 1 Corinthians 10:20–21; Romans 14:23"> For yf some man se ye which hast knowledge sit at meate in the ydoles temple shall not the conscience of hym which is weake be boldened to eate those thinges which are offered vnto the ydole?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 14:15; Romans 15:1–3; 1 Corinthians 8:13"> And so thorow thy knowledge shall the weake brother perisshe for whom christ dyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 18:6; Matthew 25:45; 1 Corinthians 12:12"> When ye synne so agaynst the brethren and wounde their weake consciences ye synne agaynst Christ.</span>
