<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 1:1; Ephesians 1:1; 2 Corinthians 1:1"> Paul by vocacion an Apostle of Iesus Christ thorow the will of God and brother Sostenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11; 1 Corinthians 1:30"> Vnto the congregacion of God which is at Corinthum. To them that are sanctified in Christ Iesu sainctes by callynge with all that call on the name of oure lorde Iesus Christ in every place both of theirs and of oures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 2 Corinthians 1:2; 1 Peter 1:2"> Grace be with you and peace from God oure father and from the lorde Iesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:8; John 14:26; 1 Timothy 1:14"> I thanke my God all wayes on youre behalfe for the grace of God which is geuen you by Iesus Christ</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:11; 2 Corinthians 8:7; Colossians 2:3"> that in all thinges ye are made riche by him in all lerninge and in all knowledge</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:2; 2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Timothy 2:6"> even as the testimony of Iesus Christ was confermed in you</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; 2 Peter 3:12; Hebrews 9:28"> so that ye are behynde in no gyft and wayte for the apperynge of oure lorde Iesus Christ</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23–24; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; Colossians 1:22"> which shall streght you vnto the ende that ye maye be blamelesse in the daye of oure lorde Iesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; Hebrews 10:23; Deuteronomy 7:9"> For god is faythfull by whom ye are called vnto the fellishyppe of his sonne Iesus Christe oure lorde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:1–4; Ephesians 4:1–7; 1 Corinthians 12:25"> I beseche you brethren in the name of oure lorde Iesus Christ that ye all speake one thynge and that there be no dissencion amonge you: but be ye knyt together in one mynde and in one meaynge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 18:6; 1 Timothy 6:4; 2 Timothy 2:23–25"> It is shewed vnto me (my brethren) of you by them that are of the housse of Cloe that ther is stryfe amonge you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:21–23; John 1:42; Matthew 23:9–10"> And this is it that I meane: how that comelie amonge you one sayeth: I holde of Paul: another I holde of Apollo: the thyrde I holde of Cephas: the four ye I holde of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5; 2 Corinthians 11:4; Acts 2:38"> Ys Christ devided? was Paul crucified for you? ether were ye baptised in the name of Paul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 18:8; Romans 16:23; 1 Corinthians 1:4"> I thanke God that I christened none of you but Crispus and Gayus</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:2; John 3:28–29; John 7:18"> lest eny shulde saye that I had baptised in myne awne name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:15; 1 Corinthians 16:17; Acts 16:33"> I baptised also the housse of Stephana. Forthermore knowe I not whether I baptised eny man or no.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:1; 1 Corinthians 2:13; 2 Peter 1:16"> For Christ sent me not to baptyse but to preache the gospell not with wysdome of wordes lest the crosse of Christ shuld have bene made of none effecte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Romans 1:16; 2 Thessalonians 2:10"> For the preachinge of the crosse is to them that perisshe folishnes: but vnto vs which are saved it is the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; Jeremiah 8:9; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> For it is written: I will destroye the wysdome of the wyse and will cast awaye the vnderstondinge of the prudet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:22; Job 12:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> Where is the wyse? Where is the scrybe? Where is the searcher of this worlde? Hath not God made the wysdome of this worlde folisshnes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 10:21; Romans 1:20–22; Matthew 11:25"> For when the worlde thorow wysdome knew not God in the wysdome of God: it pleased God thorow folisshnes of preachinge to save them that beleve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:1–4; Matthew 12:38–39; Mark 8:11"> For the Iewes requyre a signe and the Grekes seke after wysdome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 2:2; 1 Peter 2:8"> But we preache Christ crucified vnto the Iewes an occasion of fallinge and vnto the Grekes folisshnes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Colossians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 1:18"> but vnto the which are called both of Iewes and Grekes we preache Christ the power of God and the wysdome of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 11:33–36; 1 Corinthians 1:18; Zechariah 4:6–7"> For the folishnes of God is wyser then men: and the weakenes of God is stronger then men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 2:5; 1 Corinthians 3:18–20; James 1:9–11"> Brethren loke on youre callinge how that not many wyse men after the flesshe not many myghty not many of hye degre are called:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 2:5; Matthew 11:25; 1 Corinthians 1:20"> but God hath chosen the folysshe thinges of the worlde to confounde the wyse. And God hath chosyn the weake thinges of the worlde to confounde thinges which are mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 34:24; Romans 4:17; Isaiah 2:11"> And vile thinges of the worlde and thinges which are despysed hath God chosen yee and thinges of no reputacion for to brynge to nought thinges of reputacion</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 3:27; Jeremiah 9:23; Ephesians 2:9"> that no flesshe shulde reioyce in his presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Philippians 3:9; Romans 8:1"> And vnto him partayne ye in Christ Iesu which of God is made vnto vs wysdome and also rightewesnes and saunctifyinge and redempcion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; 2 Corinthians 10:17; Philippians 3:3"> That accordinge as it is written: he which reioyseth shulde reioyce in the Lorde.</span>
