<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 2:14–17; John 15:13; Matthew 23:5"> And though I bestowed all my gooddes to fede the poore and though I gave my body even that I burned and yet had no love it profeteth me nothinge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; Ephesians 4:32; Galatians 5:21–22"> Love suffreth longe and is corteous. Love envieth not. Love doth not frowardly swelleth not</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4; 2 Timothy 2:24"> suffreth all thynge beleveth all thynges hopeth all thynges endureth in all thynges.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:12; Isaiah 60:19–20; 2 Corinthians 5:7–8"> But when that which is parfect is come then that which is vnparfet shall be done awaye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 3:1–2; Galatians 4:1"> When I was a chylde I spake as a chylde I vnderstode as a childe I ymagened as a chylde. But assone as I was a man I put awaye childesshnes.</span>
