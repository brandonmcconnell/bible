<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:11–12; 1 Corinthians 1:1; Romans 1:1"> Paul an Apostle, not of men, nether by man, but by Iesus Christ, and by God the father which raysed him from deeth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:1; Philippians 4:21; Acts 16:5–6"> and all the brethren which are with me. Vnto the congregacions of Galacia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 John 1:3; 2 Corinthians 13:14; Romans 1:7–15"> Grace be with you and peace from God the father, and from oure Lorde Iesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Romans 4:25; Galatians 2:20"> which gave him selfe for oure synnes to deliver vs from this present evyll worlde thorow the will of God oure father</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Jude 1:25; Psalms 72:19"> to whom be prayse for ever and ever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:4; Galatians 5:7–8; Isaiah 29:13"> I marvayle that ye are so sone turned from him that called you in the grace of Christ vnto another gospell:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 15:24; Romans 16:17–18; Galatians 5:10"> which is nothinge els but that ther be some which trouble you and intende to pervert to gospell of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:13–14; Galatians 1:9; Revelation 22:18–19"> Neverthelesse though we oure selves or an angell from heven preache eny other gospell vnto you the that which we have preached vnto you holde him as a cursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 16:17; Proverbs 30:6; Deuteronomy 4:2"> As I sayde before so saye I now agayne yf eny man preache eny other thinge vnto you then that ye have receaved holde him accursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Acts 5:29; Ephesians 6:6"> Preache I mannes doctrine or Godes? Ether go I about to please men? If I stodyed to please men I were not the servaunt of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 1:1; 1 Corinthians 2:9–10; 1 Corinthians 11:23"> I certifie you brethren that the gospell which was preached of me was not after the maner of men</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 1:16; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Galatians 1:1"> nether receaved I it of man nether was I taught it: but receaved it by the revelacion of Iesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Corinthians 15:9; Acts 22:3–5"> For ye have hearde of my conversacion in tyme past in the Iewes wayes how that beyonde measure I persecuted the congregacion of God and spoyled it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Acts 26:5; Philippians 3:4–6"> and prevayled in the Iewes laye above many of my companions which were of myne awne nacion and was a moche more fervet mayntener of the tradicions of the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 49:5; Isaiah 49:1"> But when it pleased God which seperated me from my mothers wombe and called me by his grace</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:17; Ephesians 3:5–10; Ephesians 6:12"> for to declare his sonne by me that I shuld preache him amonge the hethen: immediatly I comened not of the matter with flesshe and bloud</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 9:20–25; 2 Corinthians 11:32–33; Galatians 1:18"> nether returned to Ierusalem to them which were Apostles before me: but wet my wayes into Arabia and came agayne vnto Damasco.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 9:22–23; Acts 9:26–29; Acts 22:17–18"> Then after thre yeare I returned to Ierusalem to se Peter and abode with him .xv. dayes</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:55; Mark 6:3; Matthew 12:46"> no nother of the Apostles sawe I save Iames the Lordes brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:1; 2 Corinthians 11:31; 2 Corinthians 11:10–11"> The thinges which I write beholde God knoweth I lye not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 15:41; Acts 9:30; Acts 6:9"> After that I went into the costes of Siria and Cilicia:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:14; Romans 16:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:1"> and was vnknowen as touchinge my person vnto the congregacions of Iewrye which were in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:8–10; Acts 9:13; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> But they hearde only that he which persecuted vs in tyme past now preacheth the fayth which before he destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Acts 11:18; Luke 15:10"> And they glorified God on my behalffe.</span>
