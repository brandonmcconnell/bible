<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; Acts 15:1; Romans 9:31–32"> Beholde I Paul saye vnto you that yf ye be circumcised Christ shall proffit you nothinge at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Romans 2:25; James 2:10–11"> I testifie agayne to every man which is circumcised that he is bounde to kepe the whole lawe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:31–32; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Romans 11:6"> Ye are gone quyte from Christ as many as are iustified by the lawe and are fallen from grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 130:5; 1 Thessalonians 1:10; Psalms 62:5"> We loke for and hope in the sprite to be iustified thorow fayth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 7:19; James 2:14–26"> For in Iesu Christ nether is circumcision enythinge worth nether yet vncircomcision but faith which by love is mighty in operacion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24; Galatians 3:1; Hebrews 12:1"> Ye did runne well: who was a let vnto you that ye shuld not obey the trueth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 1:6; Romans 8:28"> Even that counsell that is not of him that called you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 5:6–7; Mark 8:15"> A lytell leven doth leven the whole lompe of dowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 5:12; 2 Corinthians 2:3; Galatians 1:7"> I have trust towarde you in the Lorde that ye wyll be none other wyse mynded. He that troubleth you shall beare his iudgement what soever he be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; Galatians 6:12; Galatians 4:29"> Brethren yf I yet preache circucision: why do I then yet suffre persecucion? For then had the offence which the crosse geveth ceased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 5:13; Titus 3:10"> I wolde to God they were seperated from you which trouble you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 9:19; 1 Corinthians 8:9"> Brethren ye were called in to (libertie) only let not youre libertie be an occasion vnto the flesshe but in love serve one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Leviticus 19:18; Galatians 6:2"> For all the lawe is fulfilled in one worde which is this: thou shalt love thyne neghbour as thy selfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:3; James 3:14–3; Galatians 5:26"> Yf ye byte and devoure one another: take hede lest ye be consumed one of another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 5:24–25; Romans 8:12–14; Galatians 6:8"> I saye walke in the sprete and fulfill not the lustes of the flesshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:13; John 3:6; Romans 8:5–8"> For the flesshe lusteth contrary to the sprete and the sprete cotrary to the flesshe. These are contrary one to the other so that ye cannot do that which ye wolde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:14; Romans 6:14–15; Galatians 5:25"> But and yf ye be ledde of the sprete then are ye not vnder the lawe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Colossians 3:5–8; Matthew 15:18–19"> The dedes of the flesshe are manyfest whiche are these advoutrie fornicacion vnclenes wantannes</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Revelation 21:8"> ydolatrye witchecraft hatred variaunce zele wrath stryfe sedicion sectes</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Romans 8:13; Romans 13:13"> envyinge murther dronkenes glottony and soche lyke: of the which I tell you before as I have tolde you in tyme past that they which comit soche thinges shall not inherite the kyngdome of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–17; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7; John 15:5"> But the frute of sprete is loue ioye peace longesufferinge gentlenes goodnes faythfulnes</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:9; Acts 24:25; Titus 2:2"> meknes temperancye. Agaynst suche ther is no lawe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Romans 13:14; Romans 8:13"> They ye are Christis have crucified the flesshe with the appetites and lustes</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Galatians 5:16; Romans 8:4–5; Romans 8:2"> Yf we lyve in the sprete let vs walke in the sprete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 3:14–16; Philippians 2:1–3; James 4:16"> Let vs not be vayne glorious provokinge one another and envyinge one another.</span>
