<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Matthew 11:28–29; 1 Peter 1:20"> But we which have beleved do enter into his rest as contrarywyse he sayde to the other: I have sworne in my wrath they shall not enter into my rest. And that spake he verely longe after that the workes were made and the foundacion of the worlde layde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Exodus 31:17; Genesis 2:1–2"> For he spake in a certayne place of the seventh daye on this wyse: And god did rest the seventh daye from all his workes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Hebrews 4:3; Hebrews 3:11"> And in this place agayne: They shall not come into my rest.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:7; Hebrews 3:7–8; Hebrews 3:15"> Agayne he apoynteth in David a certayne present daye after so longe a tyme sayinge as it is rehearsed: this daye if ye heare his voyce be not harde herted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:15; Joshua 22:4; Deuteronomy 12:9"> For if Iosue had geven them rest then wolde he not afterwarde have spoke of another daye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10; Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:14–17"> There remayneth therfore yet a rest to the people of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Hebrews 4:3–4; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> For he that is is entred into his rest doth cease from his awne workes as god did from his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:28–30; Luke 13:24; Hebrews 3:18–1"> Let vs study therfore to entre into that rest lest eny man faule after the same ensample in to vnbelefe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:29; Isaiah 55:11; Ephesians 6:17"> For the worde of god is quycke and myghty in operacion and sharper then eny two edged swearde: and entreth through even vnto the dividynge asonder of the soule and the sprete and of the ioyntes and the mary: and iudgeth the thoughtes and the intentes of the herte:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:5; Psalms 33:13–15; Proverbs 15:3"> nether is there eny creature invisible in the sight of it. For all thynges are naked and bare vnto the eyes of him of whom we speake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 9:12"> Seynge then that we have a great hye prest whych is entred into heven (I meane Iesus the sonne of God) let vs holde oure profession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17–18; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Isaiah 53:4–5"> For we have not an hye prest which can not have compassion on oure infirmities: but was in all poyntes tempted lyke as we are: but yet with out synne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:19–23"> Let vs therfore goo boldely vnto the seate of grace that we maye receave mercy and fynde grace to helpe in tyme of nede.</span>
