<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 1:20–21; Joel 2:28; Numbers 12:6–8"> God in tyme past diversly and many wayes spake vnto the fathers by Prophetes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Colossians 1:16–18"> but in these last dayes he hath spoken vnto vs by his sonne whom he hath made heyre of all thinges: by who also he made the worlde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; Revelation 4:11; John 14:9–10"> Which sonne beynge the brightnes of his glory and very ymage of his substance bearinge vp all thinges with the worde of his power hath in his awne person pourged oure synnes and is sitten on the right honde of the maiestie an hye</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:21; Philippians 2:9–11; 1 Peter 3:22"> and is more excellent then the angels in as moche as he hath by inheritaunce obteyned an excellenter name then have they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; Acts 13:33; Hebrews 5:5"> For vnto which of the angels sayde he ateny tyme: Thou arte my sonne this daye be gate I the? And agayne: I will be his father and he shalbe my sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:7; Colossians 1:18; Deuteronomy 32:43"> And agayne when he bringeth in the fyrst begotten sonne in to the worlde he sayth: And all the angels of God shall worshippe him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 104:4; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 6:17"> And of the angels he sayth: He maketh his angels spretes and his ministres flammes of fyre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 45:6–7; Isaiah 9:6–7; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> But vnto the sonne he sayth: God thy seate shalbe forever and ever. The cepter of thy kyngdome is a right cepter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 45:7; Psalms 23:5; Isaiah 61:3"> Thou hast loved rightewesnes and hated iniquyte. Wherfore God which is thy God hath anoynted the with the oyle of gladnes above thy felowes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 102:25–27; Genesis 1:1; John 1:1–3"> And thou Lorde in the begynninge hast layde the foundacion of the erth. And the heves are the workes of thy hondes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Mark 13:31; Luke 21:33"> They shall perisshe but thou shalt endure. They all shall wexe olde as doth a garment:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 102:26–27; Hebrews 13:8; John 8:58"> and as a vesture shalt thou chaunge them and they shalbe chaunged. But thou arte all wayes and thy yeres shall not fayle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Mark 12:36; Matthew 22:44"> Vnto which of the angels sayde he at eny tyme? Sit on my ryght honde tyll I make thyne enemyes thy fote stole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 34:7; Psalms 103:20–21; Psalms 91:11–12"> Are they not all mynistrynge spretes sent to minister for their sakes which shalbe heyres of salvacion?</span>
