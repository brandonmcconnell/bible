<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:5; Romans 8:34; John 14:6"> My lytell children these thynges write I vnto you that ye synne not: yf eny man synne yet we have an advocate with the father Iesus Christ which is righteous:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:14; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 John 4:10"> and he it is that obteyneth grace for oure synnes: not for oure synnes only: but also for the synnes of all the worlde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 14:15; John 15:10; Luke 6:46"> And herby we are sure that we knowe him yf we kepe his commaundementes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 1:6; Titus 1:16; James 2:14–16"> He that sayth I knowe him and kepeth not his commaundementes is a lyar and the veritie is not in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 14:23; John 14:21; 1 John 4:12–13"> Whosoever kepeth his (worde) in him is the love of god parfect in dede. And therby knowe we that we are in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:21; John 13:15; 1 Corinthians 11:1"> He that sayth he bydeth in him ought to walke even as he walked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 3:11; Leviticus 19:18; Mark 12:29–34"> Brethren I write no newe commaundement vnto you: but that olde commaundement which ye hearde from the begynnynge. The olde commaundement is the worde which ye hearde from the begynnynge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Romans 13:12; John 13:34"> Agayne a newe commaundement I write vnto you a thynge that is true in him and also in you: for the darknes is past and the true lyght now shyneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 2:11; 1 John 3:13–17"> He that sayth how that he is in the light and yet hateth his brother is in darknes even vntyll this tyme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 3:14; Romans 9:32–33; Romans 14:13"> He that loveth his brother abydeth in the light and ther is none occasion of evyll in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 12:35; Titus 3:3; 1 John 1:6"> He that hateth his brother is in darknes and walketh in darknes: and cannot tell whither he goeth because that darknes hath blynded his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:43; Acts 13:38; Ephesians 1:7"> Babes I write vnto you how that youre synnes are forgeven you for his names sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 14:7; 1 John 2:14; Luke 10:22"> I wryte vnto you fathers how that ye have knowen him that was from the begynnynge. I wryte vnto you yonge men how that ye have overcome the wicked. I wryte vnto you lytell children how that ye have knowne the father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; 1 John 2:13; Colossians 3:16"> I wryte vnto you fathers how that ye have knowe him that was from the begynnynge. I wryte vnto you youge men how that ye are stronge: and the worde of God abydeth in you and ye have overcome that wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 4:4; Romans 12:2; Matthew 6:24"> Se that ye love not the worlde nether the thynges that are in the worlde. Yf eny man love the worlde the love of the father is not in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 13:14; Galatians 5:17; Psalms 119:36–37"> For all that is in the worlde (as the lust of the flesshe the lust of the eyes and the pryde of gooddes) is not of the father: but of the worlde</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Romans 12:2; 1 Corinthians 7:31"> And the worlde vannyssheth awaye and the lust therof: but he that fulfilleth the will of god abydeth ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 4:3; 2 John 1:7; Matthew 24:24"> Lytell children it is the last tyme and as ye have herde how that Antichrist shall come: even now are there many Antichristes come allredy. Wherby we knowe that it is the last tyme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 20:30; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Luke 8:13"> They went oute from vs but they were not of vs. For yf they had bene of vs they wolde no dout have continued with vs. But that fortuned that that myght appere that they were not of vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 14:26; 1 John 2:27; 2 Corinthians 1:21–22"> And ye have an oyntment of the holy gost and ye knowe all thynges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 1:12; Jude 1:5; Proverbs 1:5"> I wrote not vnto you as though ye knewe not the trueth: but as though ye knewe it and knowe also that no lye commeth of trueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 John 1:7; 1 John 4:3; 1 John 2:18"> Who is a lyar: but he that denyeth that Iesus is Christ? The same is the Antichrist that denyeth the father and the sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 4:15; John 8:19; John 5:23"> Whosoever denyeth the sonne the same hath not the father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 14:23; John 15:9–10; 1 John 4:13"> Let therfore abyde in you that same which ye hearde from the begynnynge. Yf that which ye hearde from the begynnynge shall remayne in you ye also shall continewe in the sonne and in the father</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 10:28; Titus 3:7; Jude 1:21"> And this is the promes that he hath promysed vs even eternall lyfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; 2 John 1:7; Acts 20:29–30"> This have I written vnto you concernynge the that disceave you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:26; 1 John 2:20–21; 1 Corinthians 2:13"> And the anoyntynge which ye have receaved of him dwelleth in you. And ye nede not that eny man teache you: but as the annoyntynge teaheth you all thynges and is true and is no lye: and as it taught you even so byde therin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 John 4:17; Mark 8:38; 1 John 3:2"> And nowe babes abyde in him that when he shall appere we maye be bolde and not be made a shamed of him at his commynge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 3:3–5; 1 John 3:7; 1 John 5:1"> Yf ye knowe that he is righteous knowe also that he which foloweth rightewesnes is borne of him.</span>
