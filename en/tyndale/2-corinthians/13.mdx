<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:16; Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:6"> Now come I the thyrd tyme vnto you. In the mouth of two or thre witnesses shall every thinge stonde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:23; 2 Corinthians 12:20–21; 1 Corinthians 5:5"> I tolde you before and tell you before: and as I sayde when I was present with you the seconde tyme so wryte I now beynge absent to them which in tyme past have synned and to all other: that if I come agayne I will not spare</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:20; 2 Corinthians 12:12; Luke 21:15"> seynge that ye seke experience of Christ which speaketh in me which amonge you is not weake but is myghty in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:18; Romans 6:4; 2 Corinthians 10:3–4"> And verely though it came of weaknes that he was crucified yet liveth he thorow the power of God. And we no dout are weake in him: but we shall live with him by the myght of God amonge you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 Corinthians 11:28; Psalms 139:23–24"> Prove youre selves whether ye are in the fayth or not. Examen youre owne selves: knowe ye not youre awne selves how that Iesus Christ is in you excepte ye be castawayes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:20; 2 Corinthians 13:10; 2 Corinthians 13:3–4"> I trust that ye shall knowe that we are not castawayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:12; 1 Timothy 2:2; 2 Corinthians 6:8–9"> I desyre before God that ye do none evyll not that we shuld seme comendable: but that ye shuld do that which is honest: and let vs be counted as leawde persones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; Mark 9:39; Acts 4:28–30"> We can do no thinge agaynst the trueth but for the trueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; 1 Corinthians 4:10; 1 Thessalonians 3:10"> We are glad when we are weake and ye stronge. This also we wisshe for even that ye were perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:8; 2 Corinthians 2:3; Titus 1:13"> Therfore write I these thinges beynge absent lest when I am present I shuld vse sharpenes accordinge to the power which the Lorde hath geven me to edifie and not to destroye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; Romans 12:16; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> Finallye brethren fare ye well be perfect be of good comforte be of one mynde lyve in peace and the God of love and peace shalbe with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 16:16; 1 Peter 5:14; 1 Thessalonians 5:26"> Grete one another in an holy kysse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 16:16; Romans 16:21–23; Hebrews 13:24"> All ye saynctes salute you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 2:22; Romans 5:5; Romans 16:20"> The grace of oure Lorde Iesus Christ and the love of God and the fellishippe of the holy goost be with you all. Amen. ‘The seconde epistle to the Corinthians. Sent from Philippos a citie in Macedonia, by Titus and Lucas.’</span>
