<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 16:9; 2 Corinthians 9:2; 2 Corinthians 9:4"> I do you to wit brethren of the grace of god which is geven in the congregacions of Macedonia</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 2:5; Proverbs 11:25; 2 Corinthians 6:10"> how that the aboundaunce of their reioysing is that they are tried with moche tribulacion. And therto though they were excedinge poore yet haue they geue excedinge richly and that in singlenesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:5; Exodus 35:21–22"> For to their powers (I beare recorde) yee and beyonde their power they were willynge of their owne accorde</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> and prayed vs with great instaunce that we wolde receave their benefite and suffre them to be parttakers with other in ministrynge to the saynctes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 12:1; Romans 14:7–9; 2 Chronicles 30:12"> And this they did not as we loked for: but gave their awne selves fyrst to the lorde and after vnto vs by the will of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 2 Corinthians 8:4"> so that we coulde not but desyre Titus to acomplysshe the same benivolence amonge you also even as he had begonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8; 2 Peter 1:5–8; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> Now therfore as ye are ryche in all parties in fayth in worde in knowledge in all fervetnes and in love which ye have to vs: even so se that ye be plenteons in this benivolence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:6; 1 John 3:17–19; James 2:14–16"> Thys saye I not as commaundynge: but be cause other are so fervent therfore prove I youre love whether it be perfait or no.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Philippians 2:6–8; Matthew 20:28"> Ye knowe the liberalitie of oure lorde Iesus Christ which though he were riche yet for youre sakes be came poore: that ye thorow his povertie myght be made ryche.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18–19; Hebrews 13:16; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> And I geve counsell hereto. For this is expedient for you which beganne not to do only: but also to will a yeare agoo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:2"> Now therfore performe the dede: that as ther was in you a redines to will even so ye maye performe the dede of that which ye have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 12:42–44; 2 Corinthians 9:7; Luke 21:1–4"> For if ther be fyrst a willynge mynde it is accepted accordynge to that a man hath and not accordinge to that he hath not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 4:34; Romans 15:26–27"> It is not my mynde that other be set at ease and ye brought into combraunce:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:12; Acts 4:34"> but that ther be egalnes now at this tyme that youre aboundaunce sucker their lacke: that their aboundaunce maye supplie youre lacke: that ther maye be equalite</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:18; Luke 22:35"> agreynge to that which is written. He that gaddered moche had never the more aboundaunce and he that gaddered lytell had never the lesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Jeremiah 32:40; 2 Corinthians 2:13–14"> Thankes be vnto god which put in the hert of Titus the same good mynde toward you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 13:22; 2 Corinthians 8:10"> For he accepted the request yee rather he was so well willynge that of his awne accorde came vnto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> We haue sent with him that brother whose laude is in the gospell thorow out all the congregacions:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:3–4; 2 Corinthians 4:15; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> and not so only but is also chosen of the congregacions to be a felowe with vs in oure iorney concerninge this benivolence that is ministred by vs vnto the prayse of the lorde and to stere vp youre prompt mynde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:22; Romans 14:16; Ephesians 5:15"> For thys we eschue that eny man shuld rebuke vs in this plenteous distribucion that is ministred by vs</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Peter 2:12; Philippians 4:8"> and therfore make provision for honest thynges not in the sight of god only but also in the sight of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 2:20–22"> We have sent with them a brother of oures whom we have ofte tymes proved diliget in many thynges but now moche more diligent. The great confidence which I have in you: hath caused me this to do:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Philemon 1:17; 2 Corinthians 8:6"> partly for Titus sake which is my felowe and helper as concernynge you partly because of other which are oure brethren and the messengers of the congregacions and the glory of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:14; 2 Corinthians 7:4; 2 Corinthians 9:2–4"> Wherfore shewe vnto them the proffe of youre love and of the reioysynge that we have of you that the congregacions maye se it.</span>
