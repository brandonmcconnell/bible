<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:13; 1 Corinthians 9:12; Matthew 17:27"> Let vs geve no man occasion of evyll that in oure office be founde no faute:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:10; 2 Corinthians 4:8; 2 Corinthians 11:27"> but in all thynges let vs behave oure selves as the ministers of God. In moche pacience in affliccions in necessite in anguysshe</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:23–25; 2 Corinthians 11:27; Acts 19:23–34"> in strypes in presonmet in stryfe in laboure in watchinge in fastyng</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Colossians 1:27; Galatians 5:22"> in purenes in knowledge in longe sufferynge in kyndnes in the holy goost in love vnfayned</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Timothy 2:15; Ephesians 6:11–20"> in the wordes of trueth in the power of God by the armoure of rightewesnes on the right honde and on the lyfte</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 27:63; 1 Peter 4:14; 1 Timothy 4:10"> in honoure and dishonoure in evyll reporte and good reporte as desceauers and yet true</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:36; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> as unknowen and yet knowen: as dyinge and beholde we yet live: as chastened and not killed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 16:22; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Revelation 2:9"> as sorowynge and yet alwaye mery: as poore and yet make many ryche: as havynge nothynge and yet possessynge all thynges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:3–4; Philippians 1:8; Psalms 119:32"> O ye Corinthyans oure mouth is open vnto you. Oure herte is made large:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:2; Micah 2:7; Philippians 1:8"> ye are in no strayte in vs but are in a strayte in youre awne bowelles:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="3 John 1:4; 1 John 3:18; 1 Thessalonians 2:11"> I promyse you lyke rewarde with me as to my childre. Set youreselues therfore at large</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 10:21; James 4:4"> and beare not a straugers yoke wyth the vnbelevers. For what fellishippe hath rightewesnes with vnrightewesnes? What company hath light with darcknes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; 1 Corinthians 10:20–21; 1 John 5:11–13"> What concorde hath Christ with beliall? Ether what parte hath he that beleveth with an infidele?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 21:3; Exodus 29:45; Hebrews 8:10"> how agreeth the temple of god with ymages? And ye are the temple of that lyuynge god as sayde god. I will dwell amonge the and walke amonge the and wilbe their god: and they shalbe my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Revelation 18:4; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> Wherfore come out from amonge the and separate youreselues (sayth the lorde) and touche none vncleane thynge: so wyll I receave you</span>
