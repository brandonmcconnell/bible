<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:8; 2 Corinthians 1:14; 1 Corinthians 12:26"> For yf I make you sorye who is it that shuld make me glad but the same which is made sory by me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 5:10; 2 Corinthians 8:22; Philemon 1:21"> And I wrote this same pistle vnto you lest yf I came I shuld take hevynes of them of whom I ought to reioyce. Certaynly this confidence have I in you all that my ioye is the ioye of you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:12; Proverbs 27:5–6; Leviticus 19:17–18"> For in great affliccion and anguysshe of hert I wrote vnto you with many teares: not to make you sory but that ye myght perceave the love which I have most specially vnto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:12–13; Galatians 4:12; Proverbs 17:25"> If eny man hath caused sorow the same hath not made me sory but partely: lest I shuld greve you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:4–5; 1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Corinthians 7:11"> It is sufficient vnto the same man that he was rebuked of many. So that now contrary wyse ye ought to forgeve him and comforte him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Galatians 6:1–2; Colossians 3:13"> lest that same persone shuld be swalowed vp with over moche hevines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:22–23; Galatians 6:1–2; Galatians 6:10"> Wherfore I exhorte you that love maye have stregth over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:6; Philemon 1:21; Philippians 2:22"> For this cause verely dyd I write that I myght knowe the profe of you whether ye shuld be obediet in all thinges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 20:23; Matthew 18:18; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> To whom ye forgeve enythinge I forgeve also. And verely if I forgeve enythinge to whom I forgave it for youre sakes forgave I it in the roume of Christ</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Ephesians 6:11–12; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> lest Satan shuld prevet vs. For his thoughtes are not vnknowen vnto vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 16:8; Acts 14:27; Colossians 4:3"> When I was come to Troada for Christes gospels sake (and a great dore was openned vnto me of the Lorde)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:5–6; 2 Corinthians 12:18; Titus 1:4"> I had no rest in my sprete because I founde not Titus my brother: but toke my leave of them and went awaye into Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Romans 8:37; 2 Corinthians 2:15–16"> Thankes be vnto God which alwayes geveth vs the victorie in Christ and openeth the saver of his knowledge by vs in every place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 1:18; Ezekiel 20:41"> For we are vnto God the swete savoure of Christ both amonge them that are saved and also amonge them which perisshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5–6; John 9:39; 1 Peter 2:7–8"> To the one parte are we the savoure of deeth vnto deeth. And vnto the other parte are we the savoure of lyfe vnto lyfe. And who is mete vnto these thinges?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Corinthians 1:12; 2 Peter 2:1–3"> For we are not as many are which choppe and chaunge with the worde of God: but even oute of purenes and by the power of God and in the sight of God so speake we in Christ.</span>
