<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:4; 1 Corinthians 1:21; 2 Corinthians 11:19"> Wolde to god ye coulde suffre me a lytell in my folysshnes: yee and I praye you forbeare me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 2:19–20; Ephesians 5:26–27; Isaiah 54:5"> For I am gelous over you with godly gelousy. For I coupled you to one man to make you a chaste virgen to Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Galatians 1:6; Ephesians 4:14"> But I feare lest as the serpent begyled Eve thorow his sutteltie even so youre wittes shuld be corrupte from the singlenes that is in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 1:6–8; 1 Corinthians 3:11; 1 Timothy 2:5"> For if he that commeth preache another Iesus then hym whom we preached: or if ye receave another sprete then that which ye have receaved: other another gospell then that ye have receaved ye myght right wel have bene content.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:11–12; 1 Corinthians 15:10; Galatians 2:6–9"> I suppose that I was not behynde the chefe apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 3:4; 1 Corinthians 1:17; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> Though I be rude in speakynge yet I am not so in knowledge. How be it amonge you we are knowen to the vtmost what we are in all thynges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; 2 Corinthians 10:1"> Did I therin synne be cause I submitted my silfe that ye myght be exalted and because I preached to you the gospell of God fre?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:18; 2 Corinthians 11:9; Philippians 4:14–16"> I robbed other congregacions and toke wages of the to do you service with all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:8–9; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> And when I was present with yov and had nede I was greuous to no man for that which was lackynge vnto me the brethren which came from Macedonia supplied: and in all thynges I kept my silfe that I shuld not be greveous to you: and so will I kepe my silfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:1; Romans 1:9; Acts 18:12"> Yf the trueth of Christ be in me this ieioysynge shall not be taken from me in the regions of Achaia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:15; 2 Corinthians 6:11–12; 2 Corinthians 7:3"> Wherfore? Be cause I love you not? God knoweth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Corinthians 5:6; 2 Corinthians 5:12"> Neverthe lesse what I doo that will I do to cut awaye occasion from them which desyre occasion that they myght be founde lyke vnto vs in that wherin they reioyce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 1:7; Revelation 2:2; 1 John 4:1"> For these falce apostles are disceatefull workers and fassion them selves lyke vnto the apostles of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 1:8; 2 Corinthians 2:11; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> And no marvayle for satan him silfe is chaunged into the fassion of an angell of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Revelation 9:11; Isaiah 9:14–15"> Therfore it is no great thynge though his ministers fassion them selves as though they were the ministers of rightewesnes: whose ende shalbe acordynge to their dedes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Corinthians 12:6; 2 Corinthians 12:11"> I saye agayne lest eny man thynke that I am folishe: or els even now take me as a fole that I maye bost my silfe a lytell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:12; Philippians 3:3–6; 2 Corinthians 9:4"> That I speake I speake it not after the wayes of the lorde: but as it were folysshly whill we are now come to bostynge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:10; 2 Corinthians 11:21–23; 2 Corinthians 10:12–18"> Seynge that many reioyce after the flesshe I will reioyce also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:10; 1 Corinthians 10:15; 1 Corinthians 8:1"> For ye suffre foles gladly be cause that ye youre selves are wyse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 2:4; Galatians 4:3; Galatians 5:1"> For ye suffre even if a man brynge you into bondage: yf a man devoure: yf a man take: yf a man exalt hym silfe: yf a man smyte you on the face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:10; 2 Corinthians 11:17; 2 Corinthians 10:1–2"> I speake as concernynge rebuke as though we had bene weake. How be it wherin soever eny man dare be bolde (I speake folisshly) I dare be bolde also</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 3:5; Romans 11:1; Romans 9:4"> They are Ebrues so am I: They are Israelites even so am I. They are the seede of Abraham even so am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:4–5; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10; 2 Corinthians 6:9"> They are the ministers of Christ (I speake as a fole) I am moare: In labours moare aboundant: In strypes above measure: In preson more plenteously: In deeth ofte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 13:9; Deuteronomy 25:2–3; Matthew 10:17"> Of the Iewes five tymes receaved I every tyme .xl. strypes saue one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 16:37; Hebrews 11:37"> Thryse was I beten with roddes. I was once stoned. I suffered thryse shipwracke. Nyght and daye have I bene in the depe of the see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 14:5; Acts 17:5; Acts 13:50"> In iorneyinge often: In parels of waters: In parels of robbers: In ieoperdies of myne awne nacion: In ieoperdies amonge the hethen. I have bene in parels in cities in parels in wildernes in parels in the see in parels amonge falce brethren</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Philippians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 6:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:9"> in laboure and travayle in watchynge often in honger in thirst in fastynges often in colde and in nakednes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; Romans 15:16; Acts 20:18–35"> And besyde the thynges which outwardly happen vnto me I am combred dayly and do care for all congregacions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:13; 1 Corinthians 9:22; Galatians 4:8–20"> Who is sicke and I am not sicke? Who is hurte in the fayth and my hert burneth not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:5–11; Proverbs 25:27; 1 Corinthians 2:3"> Yf I must nedes reioyce I will reioyce of myne infirmities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 9:5; Ephesians 1:3; John 10:30"> The God and father of oure lorde Iesus Christ which is blessed for evermore knoweth that I lye not</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 9:24–25; 2 Corinthians 11:26"> In the citie of Damascon the governer of the people vnder kynge Aretas layde watche in the citie of the Damasces and wolde have caught me</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 9:25; 1 Samuel 19:12; Joshua 2:18"> and at a wyndowe was I let doune in a basket thorowe the wall and so scaped his hondes.</span>
