<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; Romans 14:1; Galatians 6:1–2"> We which are stronge ought to beare the fraylnes of them which are weake and not to stonde in oure awne cosaytes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 14:19; 1 Corinthians 10:24; Philippians 2:4–5"> Let every man please his neghbour vnto his welth and edyfyinge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:9; Philippians 2:8; John 4:34"> For Christ pleased not him selfe: but as it is written. The rebukes of the which rebuked the fell one me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Romans 4:23–24"> Whatsoever thinges are written afore tyme are written for oure learnynge that we thorow pacience and comforte of the scripture myght have hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 2:4–5; 1 Peter 3:8; Romans 12:16"> The God of pacience and consolacion geve vnto every one of you that ye be lyke mynded one towardes another after the insample of Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 10:29–30; Revelation 1:6; Ephesians 1:3"> that ye all agreynge together maye with one mouth prayse God the father of oure Lorde Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:34; Romans 5:2"> Wherfore receave ye one another as Christ receaved vs to the prayse of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Matthew 15:24; John 1:11"> And I saye that Iesus Christ was a minister of the circumcision for the trueth of god to conferme the promyses made vnto the fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:50; Psalms 18:49; Romans 3:29"> And let the gentyls prayse god for his mercy as it is written: For this cause I will prayse the amonge the gentyls and synge in thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 67:3–4; Deuteronomy 32:43; Isaiah 42:10–12"> And agayne, he sayth: reioyse ye gentyles with his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 117:1"> And agayne, prayse the Lorde all ye gentyls and laude him all nacions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 11:10; Isaiah 11:1; Matthew 12:21"> And in another place Esaias sayth: ther shalbe the rote of Iesse and he that shall ryse to raygne over the gentyls: in him shall the gentyls trust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:16–17; Romans 12:12; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> The God of hope fyll you with all ioye and peace in belevynge: that ye maye be ryche in hope thorowe the power of the holy goost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 2 Peter 1:12; 1 John 2:21"> I my selfe am full certified of you my brethren that ye youre selves are full of goodnes and filled with all knowledge and are able to exhorte one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 15:10; Romans 1:5"> Neverthelesse brethren I have some what boldly written vnto you as one that putteth you in remembraunce thorow the grace that is geven me of God</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:13; Romans 1:1; Acts 9:15"> that I shuld be the minister of Iesu Christ amonge the gentyls and shuld minister the gladde tydynges of God that the gentyls myght be an acceptable offerynge sanctyfied by the holy goost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14–16; 2 Corinthians 12:11–21; Hebrews 5:1"> I have therfore wherof I maye reioyse in Christ Iesu in tho thinges which pertayne to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 15:12; Romans 1:5; Acts 21:19"> For I dare not speake of eny of tho thinges which Christ hath not wrought by me to make the gentyls obedient with worde and dede</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:12; Hebrews 2:4; Acts 19:11–12"> in myghty signes and wonders by the power of the sprete of God: so that from Ierusalem and the costes rounde aboute vnto Illyricum I have fylled all countres with the gladde tydynges of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:13–16; 1 Corinthians 3:9–15; Ephesians 2:20–22"> So have I enforsed my selfe to preache the gospell not where Christ was named lest I shuld have bylt on another mannes foundacion:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 52:15; Isaiah 65:1"> but as it is written: To whom he was not spoken of they shall se: and they that hearde not shall vnderstonde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 1:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:17–18"> For this cause I have bene ofte let to come vnto you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 15:32; Romans 1:10–12; Romans 15:29"> but now seynge I have no moare to do in these countres and also have bene desyrous many yeares to come vnto you</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:28; Romans 1:12; Acts 15:3"> when I shall take my iorney into spayne I will come to you. I trust to se you in my iorney and to be brought on my waye thither warde by you after that I have somwhat enioyed you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Acts 19:21; Acts 20:22"> Now go I vnto Ierusalem and minister vnto the saynctes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:1–9; Acts 11:27–30; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> For it hath pleased them of Macedonia and Achaia to make a certayne distribucion vpo the poore saynctes which are at Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:11; Galatians 6:6; Philemon 1:19"> It hath pleased them verely and their detters are they. For yf the getils be made partetakers of their spirituall thinges their dutie is to minister vnto the in carnall thinges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 15:24; Philippians 4:17; Colossians 1:6"> When I have performed this and have brought them this frute sealed. I will come backe agayne by you into Spayne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 1:10–12; Psalms 16:11"> And I am sure when I come that I shall come with aboundaunce of the blessinge of the gospell of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 1:11; 2 Corinthians 4:11"> I beseche you brethren for oure Lorde Iesus Christes sake and for the love of the sprete that ye helpe me in my busynes with youre prayers to God for me</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:2; 2 Corinthians 8:4; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> that I maye be delyvered from them which beleve not in Iewry and that this my service which I have to Ierusalem maye be accepted of the saynctes</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:18; Philemon 1:20; Philemon 1:7"> that I maye come vnto you with ioye by the will of God and maye with you be refresshed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; Philippians 4:9; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> The God of peace be with you. Amen.</span>
