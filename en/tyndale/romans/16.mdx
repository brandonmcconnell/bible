<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 4:15; Philemon 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:19"> Lyke wyse grete all the company that is in thy housse. Salute my welbeloved Epenetos which is the fyrst frute amonge them of Achaia.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 16:11; Romans 16:21; Philemon 1:23"> Salute Andronicus and Iunia my cosyns which were presoners with me also which are wele taken amonge the Apostles and were in Christ before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:1; 1 John 3:14; Romans 16:5"> Grete Amplias my beloved in the Lorde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 16:2–3; Romans 16:21"> Salute Vrban oure helper in Christ and Stachys my beloved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:18; 1 Peter 1:7; Deuteronomy 8:2"> Salute Appelles approved in Christ. Salute them which are of Aristobolus housholde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 16:21; Romans 16:7"> Salute Herodion my kynsman. Grete them of the housholde of Narcissus which are in the Lorde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Hebrews 6:10–11; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Salute Triphena and Triphosa which wemen dyd labour in the Lorde. Salute the beloved Persis which laboured in the Lorde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:21; 1 Timothy 5:2; 2 John 1:1"> Salute Rufus chosen in the Lorde and his mother and myne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; Romans 8:29; Hebrews 3:1"> Grete Asincritus, Phlegon, Herman, Patrobas, Hermen, and the brethren which are with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:2; Romans 1:7; Romans 16:14"> Salute Philologus and Iulia, Nereus and his sister, and Olimpha and all the saynctes which are with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:26; 1 Corinthians 16:20; 2 Corinthians 13:12"> Salute one another with an holy kysse. The congregacions of Christ salute you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jude 1:19; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Timothy 6:3–5"> I beseche you brethren marke them which cause division and geve occasions of evyll contrary to the doctrine which ye have learned: and avoyde them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 2:4; Matthew 7:15; 1 Timothy 6:5"> For they that are suche serve not the Lorde Iesus Christ: but their awne bellyes and with swete preachinges and flatteringe wordes deceave the hertes of the innocetes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; 1 Corinthians 14:20; Philippians 2:15"> For youre obedience extendeth to all men. I am glad no dout of you. But yet I wolde have you wyse vnto that which is good and to be innocetes concerninge evyll.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:19; 1 John 3:8; Genesis 3:15"> The God of peace treade Satan vnder youre fete shortly. The grace of oure Lorde Iesu Christ be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Acts 17:5; Acts 13:1"> Thimotheus my worke felow and Lucius and Iason and Sopater my kynsmen salute you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Galatians 6:11; 1 Corinthians 16:21; Romans 16:8"> I Tertius salute you which wrote this epistle in the Lorde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:14; Acts 19:22; 2 Timothy 4:20"> Gaius myne hoste and the hoste of all the congregacions saluteth you. Erastus the chamberlayne of the cite saluteth you. And Quartus a brother saluteth you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The grace of oure Lorde Iesu Christ be with you all. Amen</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:7; Romans 2:16; Colossians 1:26–27"> To him that is of power to stablisshe you accordinge to my gospell and preachinge of Iesus Christ in vtteringe of the mistery which was kept secret sence the worlde begane</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 1:5; Galatians 3:8; Romans 15:18"> but now is opened by the scriptures of prophesie at the commaundement of the everlastinge god to stere vp obedience to the faith publisshed amonge all nacions:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Romans 11:36; Revelation 7:10–12"> To the same God which alone is wyse be prayse thorowe Iesus Christ for ever. Amen. ‘To the Romayns. Sent from Chorinthum by Phebe she that was the minister vnto the congregacion at Chenchrea.’</span>
