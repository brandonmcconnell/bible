<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:6; Romans 6:3"> Remember ye not brethren (I speake to them that know the lawe) how that the lawe hath power over a man as longe as it endureth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 2:23–24; 1 Corinthians 7:39; 1 Corinthians 7:4"> For the woman which is in subieccion to a man is bounde by the lawe to the man as longe as he liveth. Yf the man be deed she is lowsed from the lawe of the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; Matthew 5:32; Mark 10:6–12"> So then yf whill the man liveth she couple her selfe with another man she shalbe counted a wedlocke breaker. But yf the man be deed she is fre from the lawe: so that she is no wedlocke breaker though she couple her selfe with another man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Galatians 5:22–23; Isaiah 54:5"> Even so ye my brethren are deed concerninge the lawe by the body of Christ that ye shuld be coupled to another (I meane to him that is rysen agayne from deeth) that we shuld bringe forth frute vnto God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:3; Galatians 5:16–17; Galatians 5:24"> For when we were in the flesshe the lustes of synne which were stered vppe by the lawe raygned in oure membres to bringe forth frute vnto deeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 3:23–25; 2 Corinthians 3:6; Romans 7:4"> But now are we delivered from the lawe and deed from that whervnto we werein bondage that we shuld serve in a newe conversacion of the sprete and not in the olde conversacion of the letter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:20; Romans 4:15; 1 Corinthians 15:56"> What shall we saye then? is the lawe synne? God forbid: but I knewe not what synne meant but by the lawe. For I had not knowne what lust had meant excepte the lawe had sayde thou shalt not lust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:11; 1 Corinthians 15:56; Romans 4:15"> But synne toke an occasion by the meanes of the commaundement and wrought in me all manner of concupiscece. For with out the lawe synne was deed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Romans 8:7; James 2:10–11"> I once lived with out lawe. But when the commaundement came synne revyved and I was deed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:7; Romans 10:5; Leviticus 18:5"> And the very same comaundement which was ordeyned vnto lyfe was founde to be vnto me an occasion of deeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 3:13; Romans 7:8; Hebrews 3:13"> For synne toke occasion by the meanes of the comaundement and so disceaved me and by the selfe commaundement slewe me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:8; Psalms 119:137; Psalms 19:7–12"> Wherfore the lawe is holy and the commaundement holy iust and good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 1:13–15; Galatians 3:21; Romans 8:3"> Was that then which is good made deeth vnto me? God forbyd. Naye synne was deeth vnto me that it myght appere how that synne by the meanes of that which is good had wrought deeth in me: that synne which is vnder the commandement myght be out of measure synfull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 21:25; 2 Kings 17:17; Romans 7:18"> For we knowe that the lawe is spirituall: but I am carnall solde vnder synne</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; Romans 7:19–20; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> because I wote not what I doo. For what I wold that do I not: but what I hate that do I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 7:12; Romans 7:14; 1 Timothy 1:8"> Yf I do now that which I wolde not I graute to the lawe that it is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 7:20; Philippians 3:8–9; Romans 7:18"> So then nowe it is not I that do it but synne that dwelleth in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:6; Psalms 51:5; Galatians 5:17"> For I knowe that in me (that is to saye in my flesshe) dwelleth no good thinge. To will is present with me: but I fynde no meanes to performe that which is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 7:15"> For I doo not that good thinge which I wold: but that evill do I which I wolde not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 7:17"> Finally yf I do that I wolde not then is it not I that doo it but synne that dwelleth in me doeth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:23; Psalms 119:133"> I fynde then by the lawe that when I wolde do good evyll is present with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 1:2; Psalms 40:8; Psalms 119:35"> I delite in the lawe of God concerninge the inner man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; James 4:1; Romans 8:2"> But I se another lawe in my membres rebellinge agaynst the lawe of my mynde and subduynge me vnto the lawe of synne which is in my membres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 6:6; 2 Timothy 4:18; Titus 2:14"> O wretched man that I am: who shall delyver me from this body of deeth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Galatians 5:17–24; Colossians 3:17; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> I thanke God thorow Iesus Christ oure Lorde. So then I my silfe in my mynde serve the lawe of God and in my flesshe the lawe of synne.</span>
