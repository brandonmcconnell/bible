<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 32:17; Romans 15:13; John 16:33"> Because therfore that we are iustified by fayth we are at peace with god thorow oure Lorde Iesue Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Ephesians 3:12; Romans 12:12"> by who we have awaye in thorow fayth vnto this grace wherin we stonde and reioyce in hope of the prayse that shalbe geven of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 1:2–3; James 1:12; 1 Peter 3:14"> Nether do we so only: but also we reioyce in tribulacion. For we know that tribulacion bringeth pacience</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; James 1:12; 2 Corinthians 4:8–12"> pacience bringeth experience experience bringeth hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 4:6; Ephesians 3:16–19; Philippians 1:20"> And hope maketh not ashamed for the love of God is sheed abrod in oure hertes by the holy goost which is geven vnto vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–5; Romans 5:8; Colossians 2:13"> For when we were yet weake accordynge to the tyme: Christ dyed for vs which were vngodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:13; 1 John 3:16; Romans 16:4"> Yet scace will eny man dye for a rightewes man. Paraventure for a good man durst a man dye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 15:13; John 3:16; Romans 5:6"> But God setteth out his love that he hath to vs seinge that whyll we were yet synners Christ dyed for vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:10; Romans 8:1; Ephesians 2:13"> Moche more then now (seynge we are iustifyed in his bloud) shall we be saved from wrath thorow him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:18–19; 2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:32"> For yf when we were enemyes we were reconciled to God by the deeth of his sonne: moche more seinge we are reconciled we shal be preservid by his lyfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:18–19; Romans 5:10; Isaiah 61:10"> Not only so but we also ioye in God by the meanes of oure Lorde Iesus Christ by whom we have receavyd the attonment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:21–22; Romans 5:19; Romans 6:23"> Wherfore as by one man synne entred into the worlde and deeth by the meanes of synne. And so deeth went over all men in somoche that all men synned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 4:15; 1 Corinthians 15:56; 1 John 3:4"> For even vnto the tyme of the lawe was synne in the worlde: but synne was not regarded as longe as ther was no lawe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:45; Romans 5:17; Hebrews 9:27"> neverthelesse deeth rayned from Adam to Moses even over them also that synned not with lyke transgression as dyd Adam: which is the similitude of him that is to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 6:23; Ephesians 2:8; Romans 5:12"> But the gyfte is not lyke as the synne. For yf thorow the synne of one many be deed: moche more plenteous vpon many was the grace of God and gyfte by grace: which grace was geven by one man Iesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 13:38–39; Isaiah 1:18; Isaiah 44:22"> And the gifte is not over one synne as deeth cam thorow one synne of one that synned. For damnacion cam of one synne vnto condemnacion: but the gyft cam to iustify from many synnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:21–22; 1 Corinthians 15:49; Romans 5:12"> For yf by the synne of one deeth raigned by the meanes of one moche more shall they which receave aboundance of grace and of the gyfte of rightewesnes raygne in lyfe by the meanes of one (that is to saye) Iesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:22; Romans 5:12; Romans 5:15"> Lykewyse then as by the synne of one condemnacion cam on all men: even so by the iustifyinge of one cometh the rightewesnes that bringeth lyfe vpo all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 5:18; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Philippians 2:8"> For as by one mannes disobediece many be cam synners: so by the obediece of one shall many be made righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 6:1; Titus 3:3–7; Romans 6:14"> But the lawe in the meane tyme entred in that synne shuld encreace. Neverthelater where aboundaunce of synne was there was more plenteousnes of grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Titus 2:11; Romans 5:14; Romans 8:10"> That as synne had raigned vnto deeth even so might grace raygne thorow rightewesnes vnto eternall lyfe by the helpe of Iesu Christ.</span>
