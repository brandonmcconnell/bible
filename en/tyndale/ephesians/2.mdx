<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 6:12; 1 John 5:19; Ephesians 2:3"> in the which in tyme passed ye walked acordynge to the course of this worlde and after the governer that ruleth in the ayer the sprete that now worketh in the children of vnbelefe</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Ephesians 4:22; Galatians 5:16–24"> amonge which we also had oure conversacion in tyme past in the lustes of oure flesshe and fullfilled the will of the flesshe and of the mynde: and were naturally the children of wrath even as wel as other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; Ephesians 1:7; Psalms 86:15"> But God which is rich in mercy thorow his greate love wherwith he loved vs</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 3:24; Ephesians 2:8; Romans 5:10"> even when we were deed by synne hath quickened vs together in Christ (for by grace are ye saved)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–3; Ephesians 1:3; Romans 6:4–5"> and hath raysed vs vp together and made vs sitte together in hevenly thynges thorow Christ Iesus</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:16–17; Titus 3:4; Ephesians 2:4"> for to shewe in tymes to come the excedynge ryches of his grace in kyndnes to vs warde in Christ Iesu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:22–26; Ephesians 2:5; Romans 4:16"> For by grace are ye made safe thorowe fayth and that not of youre selves. For it is the gyfte of God</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 11:6; Titus 3:3–5"> and commeth not of workes lest eny man shuld bost him silfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Colossians 1:10; Hebrews 13:21"> For we are his worckmanshippe created in Christ Iesu vnto good workes vnto the which god ordeyned vs before that we shuld walke in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Colossians 2:11; Colossians 2:13; Colossians 3:11"> Wherfore remember that ye beynge in tyme passed getyls in the flesshe and were called vncircumcision to the which are called circucision in the flesshe which circucision is made by hondes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Colossians 1:21; Jeremiah 31:31–34"> Remember I saye that ye were at that tyme with oute Christ and were reputed aliantes from the comen welth of Israel and were straugers from the testamentes of promes and had no hope and were with out god in this worlde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:39; Colossians 1:20–22; Colossians 1:13–14"> But now in Christ Iesu ye which a whyle agoo were farre of are made nye by the bloude of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11; Colossians 3:15"> For he is oure peace whych hath made of both one and hath broken doune the wall that was a stoppe bitwene vs</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 2:14; Galatians 3:10; Colossians 1:21–22"> and hath also put awaye thorow his flesshe the cause of hatred (that is to saye the lawe of commaundementes contayned in the lawe written) for to make of twayne one newe man in him silfe so makynge peace:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:20–22; Romans 5:10; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> and to reconcile both vnto god in one body thorow his crosse and slewe hatred therby:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 57:19–21; Acts 10:36; Romans 5:1"> and came and preached peace to you which were afarre of and to them that were nye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:13; Ephesians 3:12; Romans 5:2"> For thorow him we both have an open waye in in one sprete vnto the father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; 1 John 3:1; Galatians 6:10"> Now therfore ye are no moare straugers and foreners: but citesyns with the saynctes and of the housholde of god:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Isaiah 28:16; 1 Peter 2:7–8"> and are bilt apon the foundacion of the apostles and prophetes Iesus Christ beynge the heed corner stone</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16–17; 1 Corinthians 3:9; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> in whom every bildynge coupled togedder groweth vnto an holy temple in the lorde</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; 1 Corinthians 3:16; 1 John 4:16"> in whom ye also are bilt togedder and made an habitacion for god in the sprete.</span>
