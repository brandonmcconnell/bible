<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Philippians 1:27; 1 Thessalonians 2:12"> I therfore which am in bondes for the lordes sake exhorte you that ye walke worthy of the vocacion wherwith ye are called</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; 1 Corinthians 13:7; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5"> in all humblenes of mynde and meknes and longe sufferynge forbearinge one another thorowe love</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:10; Colossians 3:13–15; 1 Corinthians 12:12–13"> and that ye be dyliget to kepe the vnitie of the sprete in the bonde of peace</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Romans 12:4–5; 1 Corinthians 12:4–13"> beynge one body and one sprete even as ye are called in one hope of youre callynge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:6; 1 Corinthians 12:13; 1 Peter 3:21"> Let ther be but one lorde one fayth one baptism:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Malachi 2:10; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> one god and father of all which is above all thorow all and in you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> Vnto every one of vs is geven grace acordinge to the measure of the gyft of christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 68:18; Colossians 2:15; 1 Samuel 30:26"> Wherfore he sayth: He is gone vp an hye and hath ledde captivitie captive and hath geven gyftes vnto men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:13; Matthew 12:40; Proverbs 30:4"> That he ascended: what meaneth it but that he also descended fyrst into the lowest parties of the erth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 7:26; Hebrews 4:14; Ephesians 1:20–23"> He that descended is even the same also that ascended vp even above all hevens to fulfill all thinges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:28–29; Ephesians 2:20"> And the very same made some Apostles some prophetes some Evangelistes some Sheperdes some Teachers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11–14; Ephesians 4:16; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> that the sainctes might have all thinges necessarie to worke and minister with all to the edifyinge of the body of christ</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:28; John 17:21; Ephesians 4:5"> tyll we every one (in the vnitie of fayth and knowledge of the sonne of god) growe vp vnto a parfayte man after the measure of age of the fulnes of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:9; Romans 16:17–18; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> That we hence forth be no moare chyldren wauerynge and caryed with every wynde of doctryne by the wylynes of men and craftynes wherby they laye a wayte for vs to deceave vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 3:18; Ephesians 4:25; Zechariah 8:16"> But let vs folowe the trueth in loue and in all thynges growe in him which is the heed that ys to saye Christ</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 2:19; John 15:5; Colossians 2:2"> in whom all the body ys coupled and knet togedder in every ioynt wherwith one ministreth to another (accordinge to the operacion as every parte hath his measure) and increaseth the body vnto the edyfyinge of it silfe in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:21; 1 Peter 4:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> This I saye therfore and testifie in the lorde that ye hence forth walke not as other gentyls walke in vanitie of their mynde</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Ephesians 2:12; Romans 1:21–23"> blynded in their vnderstondynge beynge straungers from the lyfe which is in god thorow the ignorancy that is in them because of the blyndnes of their hertes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Romans 1:24–26; 1 Timothy 4:2"> which beynge past repentaunce have geven them selves vnto wantannes to worke all manner of vnclennes even with gredynes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 2:11–14; John 6:45; Matthew 11:29"> But ye have not so learned Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 5:20; Ephesians 1:13; Hebrews 3:7–8"> if so be ye have hearde of him and are taught in him even as the trueth is in Iesu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Hebrews 12:1; James 1:21"> So then as concernynge the coversacion in tyme past laye from you that olde man which is corrupte thorow the deceavable lustes</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Romans 8:6; Psalms 51:10"> and be ye renued in the sprete of youre myndes</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:10–14; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ephesians 2:10"> and put on that newe man which after the ymage of God is shapen in ryghtewesnes and true holynes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Zechariah 8:16; Proverbs 12:22"> Wherfore put awaye lyinge and speake every man truth vnto his neghbour for as moche as we are members one of another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 1:19; Psalms 4:4; Psalms 37:8"> Be angrye but synne not let not the sonne go doune apon your wrathe</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 4:7; 1 Peter 5:8; Ephesians 6:16"> nether geue place vnto the backbyter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12; Galatians 6:10"> Let him that stole steale no moare but let him rather laboure with his hondes some good thinge that he maye have to geve vnto him that nedeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:11; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> Let no filthy communicacion procede out of youre mouthes: but that whych is good to edefye with all when nede ys: that it maye have faveour with the hearers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:19; Isaiah 63:10; Ephesians 1:13–14"> And greve not the holy sprete of God by whome ye are sealed vnto the daye of redempcion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Colossians 3:8; Ecclesiastes 7:9; Titus 3:2–3"> Let all bitternes fearsnes and wrath rorynge and cursyd speakynge be put awaye from you with all maliciousnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; Matthew 6:14–15; Mark 11:25"> Be ye courteouse one to another and mercifull forgevynge one another even as god for Christes sake forgave you.</span>
