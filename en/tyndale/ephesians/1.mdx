<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; 2 Corinthians 1:1; 1 Corinthians 1:1–2"> Paul an Apostle of Iesu Christ by the will of God. To the saynctes which are at Ephesus and to them which beleve on Iesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Galatians 1:3; Titus 1:4"> Grace be with you and peace from God oure father and from the Lorde Iesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; 2 Corinthians 1:3; Ephesians 2:6"> Blessed be God the father of oure lorde Iesus Christ which hath blessed vs with all maner of spirituall blessinges in hevely thynges by Chryst</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 8:28–30; Ephesians 2:10"> accordynge as he had chosen vs in him before the foundacion of the worlde was layde that we shuld be saintes and without blame before him thorow loue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 8:29–30; Ephesians 1:11; Galatians 4:5–6"> And ordeyned vs before thorow Iesus Christ to be heyres vnto him silfe accordinge to the pleasure of his will</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 3:22–26; Ephesians 1:12; Colossians 1:13"> to the prayse of the glorie of his grace where with he hath made vs accepted in the beloved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Romans 3:24; 1 John 1:7–9"> By whom we have redemption thorow his bloude euen the forgevenes of synnes accordynge to the riches of his grace</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 5:12; Ephesians 3:10; Romans 5:15"> which grace he shed on vs aboundantly in all wisdome and perceavaunce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:26–28; Romans 16:25–27; Ephesians 3:3–9"> And hath openned vnto vs the mistery of his will accordinge to his pleasure and purposed the same in hym silfe</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 1:20; Philippians 2:9–10; Galatians 4:4"> to have it declared when the tyme were full come that all thynges bothe the thynges which are in heven and also the thynges which are in erthe shuld be gaddered togedder even in Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:28; 1 Peter 1:4; Isaiah 46:10–11"> that is to saye in him in whom we are made heyres and were therto predestinate accordynge to the purpose of him which worketh all thinges after the purpose of his awne will:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 1:6; Jeremiah 17:5–7; Ephesians 1:13–14"> that we which before beleved in Christ shuld be vnto the prayse of his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:22; Ephesians 4:30; 2 Timothy 2:19"> In whom also ye (after that ye hearde the worde of trueth I meane the gospell of youre saluacion wherin ye beleved) were sealed with the holy sprete of promes</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:22; 2 Corinthians 5:5; Ephesians 4:30"> which is the ernest of oure inheritaunce to redeme the purchased possession and that vnto the laude of his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philemon 1:5; Colossians 1:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> Wherfore even I (after that I hearde of the fayth which ye have in the lorde Iesu and love vnto all the saynctes)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 1:8–9; Colossians 1:9; Colossians 1:3"> cease not to geve thankes for you makynge mencion of you in my prayers</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; James 3:17–18; Colossians 1:9–10"> that the God of oure lorde Iesus Christ and the father of glory myght geve vnto you the sprete of wisdome and open to you the knowledge of him silfe</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:18; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Luke 24:45"> and lighten the eyes of youre myndes that ye myght knowe what that hope is where vnto he hath called you and what the riches of his glorious inheritaunce is apon the sainctes</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Ephesians 3:20; Colossians 2:12"> and what is the excedynge greatnes of his power to vs warde which beleve accordynge to the workynge of that his mighty power</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 10:12; Mark 16:19; Psalms 110:1"> which he wrought in Christ when he raysed him from deeth and set him on his right honde in hevenly thynges</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:9–11; Colossians 2:10; Colossians 1:15–16"> above all rule power and myght and dominacion and above all names that are named not in this worlde only but also in the worlde to come:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 1:18; Colossians 2:10; 1 Corinthians 15:25–27"> and hath put all thynges vnder his fete and hath made him aboue all thynges ye heed of the congregacion</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:18–19; John 1:16; Colossians 2:9–10"> which is his body and the fulnes of him that filleth all in all thynges.</span>
