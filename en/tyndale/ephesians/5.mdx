<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 4:11; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Ephesians 4:32"> Be ye folowers of god as dere children</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:34; Galatians 2:20; Colossians 3:14"> and walke in love even as Christ loved vs and gave him silfe for vs an offerynge and a sacrifyce of a swete saver to god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Ephesians 5:5; Galatians 5:19–21"> So that fornicacion and all vnclennes or coveteousnes be not once named amonge you as it be commeth saynctes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:8; Ephesians 4:29; 1 Thessalonians 5:18"> nether filthynes nether folishe talkyng nether gestinge which are not comly: but rather gevynge of thankes</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Ephesians 5:3"> For this ye knowe that no whormonger other vnclene person or coveteous person which is the worshipper of ymages hath eny inheritaunce in the kyngdome of Christ and of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 1:18; Colossians 2:8; Matthew 24:4"> Let no man deceave you with vayne wordes. For thorow soche thinges cometh the wrath of God vpon the chyldre of vnbelefe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Ephesians 5:11; 1 Timothy 5:22"> Be not therfore companions with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:12; 1 John 1:7; Acts 26:18"> Ye were once dercknes but are now light in the Lorde. Walke as chyldren of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:9–10; Galatians 5:22–23; 3 John 1:11"> For the frute of the sprete is in all goodnes rightewesnes and trueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:1–2; Hebrews 12:28; Psalms 19:14"> Accept that which is pleasinge to the Lorde:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 13:12; 1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> and have no fellishippe with the vnfrutfull workes of dercknes: but rather rebuke them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:3; Jeremiah 23:24; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> For it is shame even to name those thinges which are done of them in secrete:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:20–21; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Hosea 2:10"> but all thinges when they are rebuked of the light are manifest. For whatsoever is manifest that same is light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; Romans 13:11–12; Isaiah 26:19"> Wherfore he sayth: awake thou that slepest and stond vp from deeth and Christ shall geve the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; James 3:13; Philippians 1:27"> Take hede therfore that ye walke circuspectly: not as foles: but as wyse</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; Amos 5:13; John 12:35"> redemynge the tyme: for the dayes are evyll.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Colossians 1:9"> Wherfore be ye not vnwyse but vnderstonde what the will of the Lorde is</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Luke 1:15; Romans 13:13"> and be not dronke with wyne wherin is excesse: but be fulfilled with the sprete</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 1 Corinthians 14:26; Psalms 95:2"> speakynge vnto youre selves in psalmes and ymnes and spretuall songes synginge and makinge melodie to the Lorde in youre hertes</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Hebrews 13:15"> gevinge thankes all wayes for all thinges vnto God the father in the name of oure Lorde Iesu Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Ephesians 5:24; Ephesians 5:22"> submittinge youre selves one to another in the feare of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:18–1; 1 Peter 3:1–6; Titus 2:5"> Wemen submit youre selves vnto youre awne husbandes as vnto the Lorde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3–10; Colossians 1:18; Ephesians 1:22–23"> For the husbande is the wyves heed even as Christ is the heed of the congregacion and the same is the saveoure of the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 5:33; Colossians 3:20; Titus 2:7"> Therfore as the congregacion is in subieccion to Christ lykwyse let the wyves be in subieccion to their husbandes in all thinges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Colossians 3:19; Ephesians 5:28"> Husbandes love youre wyves even as Christ loved the congregacion and gave him silfe for it</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; John 15:3; Ezekiel 36:25"> to sanctifie it and clensed it in the fountayne of water thorow the worde</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 1:22; Hebrews 9:14; 1 Peter 1:19"> to make it vnto him selfe a glorious congregacion with oute spot or wrynckle or eny soche thinge: but that it shuld be holy and with out blame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25; Matthew 19:5; Genesis 2:21–24"> So ought men to love their wyves as their awne bodyes. He that loveth his wyfe loveth him sylfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:14–15; Ephesians 5:31; Ecclesiastes 4:5"> For no man ever yet hated his awne flesshe: but norissheth and cherisseth it even as the lorde doth the congregacion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 12:12–27; Romans 12:5"> For we are members of his body of his flesshe and of his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:5; Mark 10:7–8"> For this cause shall a man leave father and mother and shall cotinue with his wyfe and two shalbe made one flesshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 54:5"> This is a great secrete but I speake bitwene Christ and the congregacion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 3:2–7; Esther 1:20; Ephesians 5:25"> Neverthelesse do ye so that every one of you love his wyfe truely even as him silfe. And let the wyfe se that she feare her husbande.</span>
