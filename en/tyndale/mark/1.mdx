<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 5:11–12; Matthew 4:3; Matthew 3:17"> The beginnynge of the Gospell of Iesu Christ the sonne of God</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Matthew 11:10; Luke 1:76"> as that is wrytten in the Prophetes: beholde I sende my messenger before thy face which shall prepared thy waye before the.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Matthew 3:3; Luke 3:4–6"> The voyce of a cryer in the wildernes: prepare ye the waye of the Lorde make his paches streyght.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 19:3–4; Matthew 3:6; Acts 13:24–25"> Iohn dyd baptise in the wyldernes and preche the baptyme of repentaunce for the remission of synnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–42; Acts 19:18; Matthew 3:5–6"> And all the londe of Iurie and they of Ierusalem went out vnto him and were all baptised of him in the ryver Iordan confessynge their synnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 1:8; Leviticus 11:22; Matthew 3:4"> Iohn was clothed with cammylles heer and with a gerdyll of a skyn a bout hys loynes. And he dyd eate locustes and wylde hony</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 13:25; Luke 3:16; John 1:27"> and preached sayinge: a stronger then I commeth after me whose shue latchet I am not worthy to stoupe doune and vnlose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:5; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Acts 19:4–6"> I have baptised you with water: but he shall baptise you with the holy goost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 3:13–17; Matthew 2:23; Luke 3:21–22"> And that came to passe in those dayes that Iesus cam from Nazareth a cyte of Galile: and was baptised of Iohn in Iordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:31–34; Luke 3:22; Isaiah 42:1"> And assone as he was come out of the water Iohn sawe heaven open and the holy goost descendinge vpon him lyke a dove.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Mark 9:7; Matthew 3:17"> And ther came a voyce from heaven: Thou arte my dere sonne in whom I delyte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 4:1–11; Luke 4:1–13"> And immediatly the sprete drave him into wildernes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17–18; Hebrews 4:15; Exodus 34:28"> and he was there in the wildernes xl dayes and was tempted of Satan and was with wilde beestes. And the aungels ministred vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Matthew 4:12; Luke 8:1"> After Iohn was taken Iesus came in to Galile preachinge the gospell of the kyngdome of God</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:36–38; Matthew 4:17"> and sayinge: the tyme is come and the kyngdome of God is at honde repent and beleve the gospell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 4:18–22; John 1:40–42; John 6:8"> As he walked by the see of Galile he sawe Simon and Andrew his brother castinge nettes into the see for they were fysshers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 5:10; Matthew 4:19–20; Acts 2:38–41"> And Iesus sayde vnto them: folowe me and I will make you fisshers of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 18:28–30; Luke 5:11; Mark 10:28–31"> And strayght waye they forsoke their nettes and folowed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 5:37; Acts 1:13; Mark 10:35"> And when he had gone a lytell further thence he sawe Iames the sonne of zebede and Ihon his brother even as they were in the shyppe mendinge their nettes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Luke 14:26; Mark 10:29"> And anonne he called them. And they leeft their father zebede in the shippe with his hyred servauntes and went their waye after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 6:2; Mark 1:39; Matthew 4:23"> And they entred into Capernaum: and streight waye on the Saboth dayes he entred in to the synagoge and taught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54; Luke 4:32"> And they merveled at his learninge. For he taught them as one that had power with him and not as the Scribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 9:25; Mark 7:25; Mark 1:34"> And there was in their synagoge a man vexed with an vnclene spirite that cried</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; James 2:19; Acts 3:14"> sayinge: let be: what have we to do with the thou Iesus of Nazareth? Arte thou come to destroye vs? I knowe the what thou arte euen that holy of god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 1:34; Luke 4:41; Acts 16:17"> And Iesus rebuked him sayinge: hoolde thy peace and come out of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 9:26; Mark 9:20; Luke 11:22"> And the vnclene spirite tare him and cryed with a loude voyce and came out of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:31; Mark 7:37; Luke 9:1"> And they were all amased in so moche that they demaunded one of another amonge them selves saying: what thinge is this? what newe doctryne is this? For he commaundeth the foule spirites with power and they obeye him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 9:31; Luke 4:37; Mark 1:45"> And immediatly his fame spreed abroade throughoute all the region borderinge on Galile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 4:38–39; Matthew 8:14–15; Mark 1:21"> And forth with assone as they were come out of the synagoge they entred into the housse of Symon and Andrew with Iames and Ihon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:5; Mark 5:23; John 11:3"> And Symons mother in lawe lay sicke of a fever. And anone they tolde him of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 9:41; Psalms 116:12; Mark 5:41"> And he came and toke her by the honde and lifte her vp: and the fever forsoke hir by and by: and she ministred vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 8:16; Mark 3:2; Luke 4:40"> And at even when the sunne was downe they brought to him all that were diseased and them that were possessed with devyls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 1:5; Acts 13:44"> And all the cite gaddred to gedder at the dore</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 3:12; Matthew 4:23"> and he healed many that were sicke of divers deseases. And he cast out many devyls and suffred not the devyls to speake because they knewe him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Luke 5:16; Luke 22:39–46"> And in the morninge very erly Iesus arose and went out into a solitary place and there prayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And Simon and they that were with him folowed after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 12:19; Mark 1:5; Zechariah 11:11"> And when they had founde him they sayde vnto him: all men seke for the.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 4:43; John 17:8; Isaiah 61:1–3"> And he sayd vnto them: let vs go into the next tounes that I maye preache there also: for truly I cam out for that purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Mark 1:21; Luke 4:43–44"> And he preached in their synagoges throughout all Galile and cast the devyls out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 5:12–14; Matthew 8:2–4; Leviticus 13:1–14"> And there came a leper to him besechinge him and kneled doune vnto him and sayde to him: yf thou wilt thou canest make me clene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 4:15"> And Iesus had compassion on him and put forth his honde touched him and sayde to him: I will be thou clene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 15:3; Mark 5:29; John 4:50–53"> And assone as he had spoke immediatly the leprosy departed from him and was clensed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 9:30; Mark 7:36; Luke 8:56"> And he charged him and sent him awaye forthwith</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Leviticus 14:1–32; Luke 17:14; Matthew 8:4"> and sayd vnto him: Se thou saye no thinge to any man: but get the hence and shewe thy silfe to the preste and offer for thy clensinge those thinges which Moses commaunded for a testimoniall vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 2:13; Matthew 9:31; John 6:2"> But he (assone as he was departed) beganne to tell many thinges and to publyshe the dede: in so moche that Iesus coulde no more opely entre in to the cite but was with out in desert places. And they came to him from every quarter.</span>
