<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–4; Mark 13:34; Song of Solomon 8:11–12"> And he beganne to speake vnto them in similitudes. A certayne man planted a vineyarde and compased it with an hedge and ordeyned a wyne presse and bylt a toure in that. And let that out to hyre vnto husbandme and went into a straunge countre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:1–8; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 44:4"> And when the tyme was come he sent to the tennauntes a servaunt that he myght receave of the tenauntes of the frute of the vyneyarde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15; Nehemiah 9:26; Jeremiah 29:26"> And they caught him and bet him and sent him agayne emptye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And moreoever he sent vnto them another servaunt and at him they cast stones and brake his heed and sent him agayne all to revyled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:6; Matthew 23:37; Nehemiah 9:30"> And agayne he sent another and him they kylled: and many other beetynge some and kyllinge some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 1:1–2; Matthew 3:17; John 1:34"> Yet had he one sonne whom he loved tenderly him also he sent at the last vnto them sayinge: they wyll feare my sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Matthew 2:16; Acts 2:23"> But the tenauntes sayde amongest them selves: this is the heyre: come let vs kyll hym and the inheritaunce shalbe oures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 21:39; Luke 20:15; Matthew 21:33"> And they toke him and kyllid him and cast him out of the vyneyarde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 10:20–12; Matthew 21:43; Luke 19:27"> What shall then the lorde of the vyneyarde do? He will come and destroye the tenauntes and let out the vyneyarde to other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 28:16"> Have ye not redde this scripture? The stoone which the bylders dyd refuse is made the chefe stoone in the corner:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 118:23; Acts 3:12–16; Habakkuk 1:5"> this was done of the Lorde and is mervelous in oure eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 22:22; John 7:44"> And they went about to take him but they feared the people. For they perceaved that he spake that similitude agaynst them. And they left him and went their waye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Luke 11:54; Mark 3:6"> And they sent vnto him certayne of the Pharises with Herodes servantes to take him in his wordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 20:22; John 7:18"> And assone as they were come they sayd vnto him: master we knowe that thou arte true and carest for no man: for thou consyderest not the degre of men but teachest the waye of God truly: Ys it laufull to paye tribute to Cesar or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:2; Revelation 2:23; Mark 10:2"> Ought we to geve or ought we not to geve? He vnderstode their simulacion and sayde vnto them: Why tepte ye me? Brynge me a peny that I maye se that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 20:24–26; Revelation 3:12; Matthew 22:19–22"> And they brought. And he sayde vnto them: Whose ys thys ymage and superscripcion? And they sayde vnto him Cesars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:7; 1 Peter 2:17; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> And Iesus answered and saide vnto the: Then geve to Cesar that which belongeth to Cesar: and to God that which perteyneth to God. And they mervelled at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 22:23–33; Acts 4:1–2; Acts 23:6–9"> Then came the Saduces vnto him which saye ther is no resurreccion. And they axed hym sayinge:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Ruth 4:5; Ruth 1:11–13"> Master Moses wroote vnto vs yf eny mans brother dye and leve his wyfe behinde him and leve no chyldren: that then hys brother shuld take his wyfe and reyse vp seed vnto his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 22:25–28; Luke 20:29–33"> Ther were seven brethren: and the fyrst toke a wyfe and when he dyed leeft no seed behynde him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the seconde toke hir and dyed: nether leeft eny seed. And the thyrde lyke wyse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And seven had her and leeft no seed behynde them. Last of all the wyfe dyed also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In the resurreccion then when they shall ryse agayne: whose wyfe shall she be of them? For seven had her to wyfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 20:9; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Matthew 22:29"> Iesus answered and sayde vnto them: Are ye not therfore deceaved and vnderstonde not the scryptures nether the power of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:35–36; Matthew 22:30; 1 John 3:2"> For when they shall ryse agayne from deeth they nether mary nor are maryed: but are as the angels which are in heven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Luke 20:37; Genesis 17:7–8"> As touchynge the deed that they shall ryse agayne: have ye not redde in the boke of Moses howe in the busshe God spake vnto him sayinge: I am the God of Abraham and God of Ysaac and the God of Iacob?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 14:9; Romans 4:17; Mark 12:24"> He is not the God of the deed but the God of the livynge. Ye are therfore greatly deceaved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–40; Luke 10:25–28; Luke 20:39"> And ther came one of the scribes that had hearde them disputynge to gedder and perceaved that he had answered them well and axed him: Which is the fyrste of all the commaundemetes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:4; Luke 10:27; Galatians 3:20"> Iesus answered him: the fyrste of all the comaundementes is. Heare Israel: The Lorde God is one Lorde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5"> And thou shalt love the Lorde thy God with all thy hert and with all thy soule and with all thy mynde and with all thy strength. This is the fyrste commaundement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 13:8–9; Matthew 22:39; Leviticus 19:18"> And the seconde is lyke vnto this: Thou shalt love thy neghbour as thy silfe. Ther is none other commaundement greater then these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Deuteronomy 4:35; Isaiah 46:9"> And the Scribe sayde vnto him: well master thou hast sayd the truthe that ther ys one God and that ther is none but he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Micah 6:6–8; 1 Samuel 15:22"> And to love him with all the herte and with all the mynde and with all the soule and with all the stregth: and to love a mans neghbour as him silfe ys a greater thinge then all burntofferings and sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 20:40; Matthew 22:46"> And when Iesus sawe that he answered discretly he sayde vnto him: Thou arte not farre from the kyngdome of God. And no man after that durst axe him eny question.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Matthew 22:41–45; Matthew 26:55"> And Iesus answered and sayde teachynge in the temple: how saye the Scribes that Christ is the sonne of David?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Matthew 22:43–45"> for David him selfe inspyred with the holy goost sayde: The Lorde sayde to my Lorde syt on my right honde tyll I make thyne enemyes thy fote stole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Matthew 11:25; Matthew 1:23"> Then David hym silfe calleth him Lorde: and by what meanes is he then his sonne? And moche people hearde him gladly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–7; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 11:43"> And he sayde vnto them in his doctrine: beware of the Scribes which love to goo in longe clothinge: and love salutacions in the market places</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="James 2:2–3"> and the chefe seates in the synagoges and to syt in the vppermost roumes at feastes</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Luke 20:47; Matthew 23:33"> and devoure widowes houses and that vnder coloure of longe prayinge. These shall receave greater dampnacion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 21:1–4; 2 Kings 12:9; John 8:20"> And Iesus sat over agaynst the treasury and behelde how the people put money in to the treasury. And many that were ryche cast in moch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And ther cam a certayne povre widowe and she threwe in two mytes which make a farthynge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Acts 11:29"> And he called vnto him his disciples and sayde vnto them: Verely I saye vnto you that this pover widowe hath cast moare in then all they which have caste into the treasury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 1 John 3:17; Ezra 2:68–69"> For they all dyd cast in of their superfluyte: but she of her poverte dyd cast in all that she had even all her livynge.</span>
