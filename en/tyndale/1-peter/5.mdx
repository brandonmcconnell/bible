<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:7; 1 Timothy 3:8; Titus 3:1"> se that ye fede Christes flocke which is amonge you takynge the oversyght of them</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:9; Philippians 3:17; Ezekiel 34:4"> not as though ye were compelled therto but willyngly: not for the desyre of filthy lucre but of a good mynde. not as though ye were lordes over the parisshes: but that ye be a insample to the flocke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 1:12; 1 Corinthians 9:25; 1 John 3:2"> And when the chef shepheerde shall appere ye shall receave an incorruptible croune of glorye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 4:6; Proverbs 3:34; Ephesians 5:21"> Lykwyse ye yonger submit youre selves vnto the elder. Submit youre selves every man one to another knet youre selves togedder in lowlines of mynde. For god resisteth the proude and geveth grace to the humble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:10; Luke 14:11; Proverbs 29:23"> Submit youre selves therfore vnder the myghty honde of god that he maye exalt you when the tyme is come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 55:22; Philippians 4:6; Psalms 37:5"> Cast all youre care to him: for he careth for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:11; James 4:7; Ephesians 4:27"> Be sober and watch for youre adversary the devyll as a rorynge lion walketh about sekynge whom he maye devoure:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 4:7; Acts 14:22; Ephesians 6:11–13"> whom resist stedfust in the fayth remebrynge that ye do but fulfill the same affliccions which are apoynted to youre brethren that are in the worlde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Peter 1:6–7"> The God of all grace which called you vnto his eternall glory by Christ Iesus shall his awne silfe after ye have soffred a lytell affliccion make you perfect: shall settle strenght and stablishe you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 4:11; Romans 11:36; Revelation 1:6"> To him be glory and dominion for ever and whill the worlde endureth Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:22; Acts 11:23; 1 Corinthians 15:1"> By Silvanus a faythfull brother vnto you (as I suppose) have I written brefly exhortynge and testifyinge how that this is the true grace of god wherin ye stonde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Acts 12:25; Psalms 87:4"> The companions of youre eleccion that are at Babilon, saluteth you and Marcus my sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 16:16; Ephesians 6:23; Romans 8:1"> Grete ye one another with the kysse of love. Peace be with you all which are in Christ Iesus. Amen.</span>
