<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 9:1–6; Mark 3:13–15; Mark 6:7–13"> And he called his .xii. disciples vnto hym and gave them power over vnclene sprites to cast them oute and to heale all maner of sicknesses and all maner of deseases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Acts 1:13; Luke 5:10"> The names of the .xii. Apostles are these. The fyrst Simon called also Peter: and Andrew his brother. Iames the sonne of zebede and Ihon his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 3:18; Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14"> Philip and Bartlemew. Thomas and Mathew the Publican. Iames the sonne of Alphe and Lebbeus otherwyse called Taddeus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 26:14; Matthew 26:47; John 13:2"> Simon of Cane and Iudas Iscarioth which also betrayed hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 4:9; Acts 22:21–23; 2 Kings 17:24–41"> These .xii. sent Iesus and commaunded them sayinge: Go not in to the wayes that leade to the gentyls and in to the cities of the Samaritans enter ye not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:6; Ezekiel 34:6; Isaiah 53:6"> But go rather to the lost shepe of the housse of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 28:31; Matthew 3:2; Luke 9:60"> Go and preach sayinge: that the kyngdome of heven is at hande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 3:6; Luke 10:9; Acts 4:30"> Heale the sicke clense the lepers rayse the deed caste oute the devils. Frely ye have receved frely geve agayne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Luke 9:3–5; Luke 10:4–12"> Posses not golde nor silver nor brassse yn youre gerdels</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 10:7–12; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:4–14"> nor yet scrip towardes your iorney: nether two cotes nether shues nor yet a staffe. For the workman is worthy to have his meate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 10:7–8; Job 31:32; Judges 19:16–21"> In to whatsoever cite or toune ye shall come enquyre who ys worthy yn it and there abyde till ye goo thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:6; Luke 10:5–6; Acts 10:36"> And when ye come in to an housse salute the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 10:6; Psalms 35:13; 2 Corinthians 2:16"> And yf the housse be worthy youre peace shall come apon it. But yf it be not worthy youre peace shall retourne to you agayne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 10:10–11; Acts 18:6; Nehemiah 5:13"> And whosoever shall not receave you nor will heare youre preachynge: when ye departe oute of that housse or that cite shake of the duste of youre fete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6; 1 John 4:17; John 15:22–24"> Truly I say vnto you: it shalbe easier for the londe of zodoma and Gomorra in the daye of iudgement then for that cite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:3; Philippians 2:15; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> Beholde I sende you forthe as shepe amonge wolves. Be ye therfore wyse as serpetes and innocent as doves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 13:9; Matthew 23:34; Acts 26:11"> Beware of men for they shall deliver you vp to the consels and shall scourge you in their synagoges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:25–27; Matthew 8:4; Psalms 2:1–6"> And ye shall be brought to the heed rulers and kynges for my sake in witnes to them and to the gentyls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 4:12; Mark 13:11–13; 2 Timothy 4:17"> But when they delyver you vp take no thought how or what ye shall speake for that shalbe geve you even in that same houre what ye shall saye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10; Luke 12:12"> For it is not ye that speke but the sprite of your father which speaketh in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 21:16–17; Micah 7:5–6; Mark 13:12–13"> The brother shall betraye the brother to deeth and the father the sonne. And the chyldre shall aryse agaynste their fathers and mothers and shall put them to deethe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 24:13; James 1:12; Revelation 2:10"> and ye shall be hated of all me for my name. But he that endureth to the ende shalbe saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:28; Acts 8:1; Mark 13:26"> When they persecute you in one cite flye in to another. I tell you for a treuth ye shall not fynysshe all that cities of Israel tyll the sonne of man be come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 15:20; Luke 6:40; John 13:16"> The disciple ys not above hys master: nor yet the servaut above his lorde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:15; Matthew 12:24; Matthew 9:34"> It is ynough for the disciple to be as hys master ys and that the servaunt be as his lorde ys. yf they have called the lorde of the housse beelzebub: how moche more shall they call them of his housholde so?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 8:17; Mark 4:22; Jeremiah 1:8"> Feare the not therfore. There is no thinge so close that shall not be openned and no thinge so hyd that shall not be knowen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 5:20; John 16:13; John 16:1"> What I tell you in dercknes that speake ye in lyght. And what ye heare in the eare that preache the on the housse toppes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:4–5; Isaiah 8:12–13; Revelation 2:10"> And feare ye not them which kyll the body and be not able to kyll the soule. But rather feare hym which is able to destroye bothe soule and body into hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 12:6–7; Psalms 104:27–30"> Are not two sparowes solde for a farthinge? And none of them dothe lyght on the grounde with out youre father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 12:7; 1 Samuel 14:45; Acts 27:34"> And now are all the heeris of youre heedis numbred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 12:24; Psalms 8:5; Matthew 6:26"> Feare ye not therfore: ye are of more value then many sparowes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 3:5; Romans 10:9–10; Luke 12:8–9"> Who soever therfore shall knowledge me before men hym will I knowledge also before my father which is in heuen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:12; Luke 9:26; Luke 12:9"> But whoso ever shall denye me before men hym will I also denye before my father which is in heven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 12:49–53; Acts 14:4; Acts 13:45–50"> Thynke not that I am come to sende peace into the erth. I came not to send peace but a swearde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Matthew 10:21; Luke 21:16"> For I am come to set a man at varyaunce ageynst hys father and the doughter ageynst hyr mother and the doughterlawe ageynst her motherlawe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Micah 7:6; Jeremiah 12:6; Psalms 41:9"> And a mannes fooes shalbe they of hys owne housholde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 14:26; Matthew 22:37; Philippians 3:7–9"> He that lovith hys father or mother more then me is not mete for me. And he that loveth his sonne or doughter more then me is not mete for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 14:27; Mark 8:34; Matthew 16:24"> And he that taketh not his crosse and foloweth me ys not mete for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 12:25; Mark 8:35–36; Luke 17:33"> He that fyndeth hys lyfe shall lose it: and he that losith hys lyfe for my sake shall fynde it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 13:20; Luke 9:48; Luke 10:16"> He that receavith you receavith me: and he that receavith me receavith him that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="3 John 1:5–8; Hebrews 6:10; 2 Kings 4:16–17"> He that receavith a prophet in the name of a prophet shall receave a prophetes rewarde. And he that receavith a righteous man in the name of a righteous man shall receave the rewarde of a righteous man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10; Matthew 25:40; Mark 9:41–42"> And whosoever shall geve vnto one of these lytle ones to drincke a cuppe of colde water only in the name of a disciple: I tel you of a trueth he shall not lose his rewarde.</span>
