<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 20:1–16; Matthew 12:43–45; Luke 14:16"> And Iesus answered and spake vnto them agayne in similitudes sayinge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–9; Matthew 13:24; Ephesians 5:24–32"> The kingdome of heven is lyke vnto a certayne kynge which maryed his sonne</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; John 5:40; Luke 14:15–17"> and sent forth his servantes to call them that were byd to the weddinge and they wolde not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:36; John 6:50–57"> Ageyne he sent forth other servauntes sayinge: Tell them which are bydden: beholde I have prepared my dynner myne oxen and my fatlinges are kylled and all thinges are redy come vnto the mariage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:3; Luke 14:18–20; 1 John 2:15–16"> But they made light of it and went their wayes: one to his ferme place another about his marchaundise</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:19–20; John 16:2–3; Acts 7:51–57"> the remnaunt toke his seruantes and intreated them vngodly and slewe them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 19:27; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Luke 21:24"> When the kinge hearde that he was wroth and send forth his warryers and distroyed those murtherers and brent vp their cyte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; Acts 13:46; Revelation 3:4"> Then sayde he to his servauutes: the weddinge was prepared. But they which were bydden were not worthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 14:21–24; Proverbs 1:20–23; Mark 16:15–16"> Go ye therfore out into the hyewayes and as many as ye finde byd them to the mariage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Revelation 19:6–9; 2 Corinthians 12:21"> The seruauntes wet out into the hiewayes and gaddered to gedder as many as they coulde fynde both good and bad and the weddinge was furnysshed with gestes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 19:8; Hebrews 4:12–13"> Then the kynge came in to viset the gestes and spyed there a man which had not on a weddinge garment</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:9; Job 5:16; Matthew 20:13"> and sayde vnto him: frende how fortuned it that thou camest in hither and hast not on a weddyng garment? And he was even spechlesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 25:30; Matthew 24:51"> Then sayde the kynge to his ministers: take and bynde hym hand and fote and caste hym into vtter darcknes there shalbe wepinge and gnasshinge of teth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Luke 13:23–24; Matthew 7:13–14"> For many are called and feawe be chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Mark 12:13–17; Psalms 2:2"> Then went the Pharises and toke counsell how they might tagle him in his wordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 3:6; Mark 8:15; Mark 12:14"> And they sent vnto him their disciples with Herodes servauntes sayinge: Master we knowe that thou are true and teachest the waye of god trulie nether carest for eny man for thou consydrest not menes estate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 2:1; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 17:25"> Tell vs therfore: how thynkest thou? Is it lawfull to geve tribute vnto Cesar or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:6; Luke 5:22; John 2:25"> Iesus perceaved their wikednes and sayde: Why tempte ye me ye ypocrites?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 20:2; Revelation 6:6; Matthew 18:28"> Let me se the tribute money. And they toke hym a peny.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 20:24"> And he sayde vnto them: whose is this ymage and superscripcion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:7; Proverbs 24:21; Acts 4:19"> They sayde vnto him: Cesars. Then sayde he vnto them. Geve therfore to Cesar that which is Cesars: and geve vnto god that which is goddes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 12:12; Matthew 22:46; Luke 20:25–26"> When they hearde that they marveled and lefte hym and went there waye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Mark 12:18–27; Acts 23:6–8"> The same daye the Saduces came to him (which saye that there is no resurreccion) and axed him</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Luke 20:28; Ruth 1:11"> sayinge: Master Moses bade yf a man dye havinge no chyldre that the brother mary his wyfe and reyse vp seed vnto his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:29–33; Mark 12:19–23; Hebrews 9:27"> Ther were with vs seven brethren and the fyrste maried and deceased with oute yssue and lefte his wyfe vnto his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Lyke wise the seconde and the thryd vnto the sevethe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Laste of all the woman dyed also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Now in the resurreccion whose wyfe shall she be of the seven? For all had her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 20:9; Romans 15:4; Luke 1:37"> Iesus answered and sayde vnto the: ye are deceaved and vnderstonde not the scriptures nor yet the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 20:34–36; Mark 12:24–25; Matthew 24:38"> For in the resurreccion they nether mary nor are maryed: but are as the angels of God in heven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 12:3; Matthew 21:16; Matthew 21:42"> As touchynge the resurreccion of the deed: have ye not redde what is sayde vnto you of God which sayeth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Mark 12:26–27; Luke 20:37–38"> I am Abrahams God and Ysaacks God and the God of Iacob? God is not the God of the deed: but of the lyvinge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 12:17; Mark 6:2; John 7:46"> And when the people hearde that they were astonyed at his doctrine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 12:28–31; Matthew 25:3–5; Matthew 12:14"> When the Pharises had hearde how that he had put the Saduces to silence they drewe to gedder</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 7:30; Luke 10:25–37; Luke 11:52"> and one of them which was a doctoure of lawe axed a question temptinge him and sayinge:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 12:28–33; Matthew 5:19–20; Luke 11:42"> Master which is the chefe commaundment in the lawe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 12:29–30; Luke 10:27; Deuteronomy 6:5"> Iesus sayde vnto him: love the Lorde thy God with all thine herte with all thy soule and with all thy mynde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> This is the fyrst and the chefe comaundement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 12:31; Luke 10:27–37; Leviticus 19:18"> And ther is another lyke vnto this. Love thyne neghbour as thy selfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 7:12; 1 John 4:19–21"> In these two commaundemetes hange all the lawe and the Prophetes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 22:34"> Whyll the Pharises were gaddered togeder Iesus axed the sayinge:</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; John 6:68–69; Matthew 16:13–17"> what thinke ye of Christ? Whose sonne is he? They sayde vnto him: the sonne of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:2; Revelation 4:2; Mark 12:36"> He sayde vnto them: how then doeth David in spirite call him Lorde sayinge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Acts 2:34–35"> The Lorde sayde to my Lorde syt on my right honde: tyll I make thyne enemyes thy fote stole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Revelation 22:16; John 8:58"> Yf David call him Lorde: how is he then his sonne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Mark 12:34; Luke 20:40; Luke 14:6"> And none coulde answere him ageyne one worde: nether dueste eny from that daye forth axe him eny moo questions.</span>
