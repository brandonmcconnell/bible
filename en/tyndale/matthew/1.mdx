<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Ruth 1:16–17; James 2:25"> Salmon begat Boos of Rahab: Boos begat Obed of Ruth: Obed begat Iesse:</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:20; Jeremiah 52:28–30; Daniel 1:2"> Iosias begat Iechonias and his brethren aboute the tyme they were caryed awaye to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:17; Ezra 3:2; Luke 3:27"> And after they were brought to Babylon Iechonias begat Salathiel: Salathiel begat zorobabel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> zorobabel begat Abiud: Abiud begat Eliachim: Eliachim begat Azor:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Azor begat Sadoc: Sadoc begat Achin: Achin begat Eliud:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Eliud begat Eleasar: Eleasar begat Matthan: Matthan begat Iacob:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:17; John 4:25; Luke 3:23"> Iacob begat Ioseph the husbande of Mary of which was boren that Iesus that is called Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 1:11–12; 2 Kings 24:14; Matthew 11:2"> All the generacions from Abraham to David are fowretene generacions. <span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Luke 1:27–38; Job 14:4"> The byrthe of Iesus Christ was on thys wyse. When hys mother Mary was betrouthed to Ioseph before they came to dwell to gedder she was foude with chylde by the holy goost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Deuteronomy 24:1–4; John 8:4–5"> The Ioseph her husbande beinge a perfect man and loth to make an ensample of her was mynded to put her awaye secretely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Luke 1:19; Matthew 1:18"> Whill he thus thought behold the angell of the Lorde appered vnto him in a dreame saynge: Ioseph the sonne of David feare not to take vnto ye Mary thy wyfe. For that which is conceaved in her is of the holy goost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:23; Acts 4:12; John 1:29"> She shall brynge forthe a sonne and thou shalt call his name Iesus. For he shall save his peple from their synnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 24:44; Matthew 2:23; 1 Kings 8:15"> All this was done to fulfill that which was spoken of the Lorde by the Prophet saynge:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 9:6–7; Psalms 46:11"> Beholde a mayde shall be with chylde and shall brynge forthe a sonne and they shall call his name Emanuel which is by interpretacion God with vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 15:14; James 2:21–26; John 2:5–8"> And Ioseph assone as he awoke out of slepe did as the angell of the Lorde bade hym and toke hys wyfe vnto hym</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 2:21; Exodus 13:2; Matthew 1:21"> and knewe her not tyll she had brought forth hir fyrst sonne and called hys name Iesus.</span>
