<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Matthew 19:1"> And it came to passe when Iesus had fynisshed all these sayinges he sayd vnto his disciples:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 22:1–2; Mark 14:1–2; John 13:1–2"> Ye knowe that after ii. dayes shalbe ester and the sonne of man shalbe delyvered to be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:47–53; John 18:24; Mark 14:54"> Then assembled togedder the chefe prestes and the scribes and the elders of the people to the palice of the hye preste called Cayphas</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 12:14; Psalms 2:2; Acts 13:10"> and heelde a counsell how they mygt take Iesus by suttelte and kyll him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:24; Matthew 21:26; Matthew 14:5"> But they sayd not on the holy daye lest eny vproure aryse amonge the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Luke 7:37–39; John 12:1–8"> When Iesus was in Bethany in the house of Symon the leper</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 12:2–3; Luke 7:46; Luke 7:37–38"> ther came vnto him a woman which had an alablaster boxe of precious oyntment and powred it on his heed as he sate at the bourde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 12:4–6; Mark 14:4; Exodus 5:17"> When his disciples sawe that they had indignacion sayinge: what neded this wast?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 12:5–6; Mark 14:5; Joshua 7:20–21"> This oyntmet myght have bene well solde and geven to the povre. When Iesus vnderstod that he sayde vnto the:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Titus 3:8; Hebrews 13:21; 1 Timothy 5:10"> why trouble ye the woman? She hath wrought a good worke apon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Mark 14:7; John 12:8"> For ye shall have povre folcke alwayes with you: but me shall ye not have all wayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 14:8; John 12:7; Mark 16:1"> And in that she casted this oyntment on my bodye she dyd it to burye me with all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 14:9; Matthew 24:14; Psalms 112:6"> Verely I saye vnto you wheresoever this gospell shalbe preached throughoute all the worlde there shall also this that she hath done be tolde for a memoriall of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 22:3–6; Matthew 10:4; Mark 14:10–11"> Then one of the twelve called Iudas Iscarioth went vnto the chefe prestes</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Genesis 37:26–28; Exodus 21:32"> and sayd: what will ye geve me and I will deliver him vnto you? And they apoynted vnto him thirty peces of sylver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:11; Luke 22:6"> And from that tyme he sought oportunite to betraye him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:7–13; Mark 14:12–16; Exodus 12:18–20"> The fyrst daye of swete breed the disciples cam to Iesus sayinge vnto him: where wylt thou that we prepare for the to eate the paschall lambe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 14:13–16; John 7:6; John 17:1"> And he sayd: Go into the cite vnto soche a man and saye to him: the master sayeth my tyme is at hande I will kepe myne ester at thy housse with my disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 2:5; John 15:14; Matthew 21:6"> And the disciples did as Iesus had apoynted them and made redy the esterlambe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 14:17–21; John 13:21; Luke 22:14–16"> When the even was come he sate doune with the .xii.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 13:21; Hebrews 4:13; John 6:70–71"> And as they dyd eate he sayde: Verely I saye vnto you that one of you shall betraye me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 13:22–25; Luke 22:23; Mark 14:19–20"> And they were excedinge sorowfull and beganne every one of the to saye vnto him: is it I master?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:18; Psalms 41:9; John 13:26–28"> He answered and sayde: he that deppeth his honde with me in the disshe the same shall betraye me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 26:22–23; Luke 24:46; Matthew 18:7"> The sonne of man goeth as it is written of him: but wo be to that man by whom the sonne of man shalbe betrayed. It had bene good for that man yf he had never bene borne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 26:64; Matthew 27:11; Luke 22:70"> Then Iudas wich betrayed him answered and sayde: is it I master? He sayde vnto him: thou hast sayde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:23–29; Luke 22:18–20; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> As they dyd eate Iesus toke breed and gave thankes brake it and gave it to the disciples and sayde: Take eate this is my body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; 1 Corinthians 10:16; Psalms 116:13"> And he toke the cup and thanked and gave it them sayinge: drinke of it every one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–14; Hebrews 9:14–22; Leviticus 17:11"> For this is my bloude of the new testament that shalbe shedde for many for the remission of synnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Revelation 7:17; Mark 14:25"> I saye vnto you: I will not drinke hence forth of this frute of the vyne tree vntyll that daye when I shall drinke it new with you in my fathers kyngdome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Ephesians 5:19–20; Luke 22:39"> And when they had sayde grace they went out into mounte olyvete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Mark 14:27–28; John 16:32"> Then sayd Iesus vnto them: all ye shall be offended by me this night. For it is wrytten. I will smyte the shepe herde and the shepe of the flocke shalbe scattered abroode.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 28:10; Luke 18:33–34; Matthew 28:16"> But after I am rysen ageyne I will goo before you into Galile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 22:33; John 13:36–38; Proverbs 28:25–26"> Peter answered and sayde vnto him: though all men shulde be offended by the yet wolde I never be offended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 13:38; Luke 22:34; Matthew 26:75"> Iesus sayde vnto him. Verely I saye vnto the that this same night before the cocke crowe thou shalt denye me thryse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Romans 11:20; John 13:37; Proverbs 29:23"> Peter sayde vnto him: Yf I shulde dye with the yet wolde I not denye the Lyke wyse also sayde all the disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 14:32–42; Matthew 26:42; Luke 22:39–46"> Then went Iesus with them into a place which is called Gethsemane and sayde vnto the disciples syt ye here whyll I go and praye yonder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Matthew 17:1; Mark 5:37"> And he toke with him Peter and the two sonnes of zebede and began to wexe sorowfull and to be in an agonye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 12:27; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 Peter 2:24"> Then sayd Iesus vnto them: my soule is hevy even vnto the deeth. Tary ye here and watche with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 6:38; Philippians 2:8; Luke 22:41–42"> And he went a lytell aparte and fell flat on his face and prayed sayinge: O my father yf it be possible let this cuppe passe from me: neverthelesse not as I wyll but as thou wylt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 14:37; Luke 22:45; Matthew 26:43"> And he came vnto the disciples and founde them a slepe and sayde to Peter: what coulde ye not watche with me one houre:</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 14:38; Luke 22:46; Ephesians 6:18"> watche and praye that ye fall not into temptacion. The spirite is willynge but the flesshe is weake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 26:39; Mark 14:39–40; Hebrews 4:15"> He went awaye once moare and prayed sayinge: O my father yf this cuppe can not passe away from me but that I drinke of it thy wyll be fulfylled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:32; Romans 13:1; Proverbs 23:34"> And he came and founde the a slepe agayne. For their eyes were hevy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:8; Luke 18:1; Matthew 6:7"> And he lefte them and went agayne and prayed the thrid tyme sayinge the same wordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 13:1; John 12:27; Mark 14:41–42"> Then came he to his disciples and sayd vnto them: Slepe hence forth and take youre reest. Take hede the houre is at honde and the sonne of man shalbe betrayed into the hondes of synners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 9:51; John 14:31; 1 Samuel 17:48"> Ryse let vs be goinge: beholde he is at honde that shall betraye me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 1:16; Matthew 26:55; Mark 14:43–50"> Whyll he yet spake: lo Iudas one of the xii. came and with him a greate multitude with sweardes and staves sent from the chefe prestes and elders of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 55:20–21; Psalms 28:3; 2 Samuel 3:27"> And he that betrayed him had geven the a token sayinge: whosoever I kysse that same is he ley hondes on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:25; 1 Thessalonians 5:26; Proverbs 27:6"> And forth with all he came to Iesus and sayde: hayle master and kyssed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 20:13; Matthew 22:12; Psalms 41:9"> And Iesus sayde vnto him: frende wherfore arte thou come? Then came they and layed hondes on Iesus and toke him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 18:36; Mark 14:47; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> And beholde one of them which were with Iesus stretched oute his honde and drue his swearde and stroke a servaunt of the hye preste and smote of his eare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Revelation 13:10; Genesis 9:6; Romans 12:19"> Then sayde Iesus vnto him: put vp thy swearde into his sheathe. For all that ley hond on the swearde shall perisshe with the swearde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Kings 6:17; Matthew 4:11; Daniel 7:10"> Ether thinkest thou that I cannot now praye to my father and he shall geve me moo then .xii. legions of angelles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Matthew 26:24; Acts 1:16"> But how then shuld the scriptures be fulfylled: for so must it be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Mark 12:35; John 8:2; Luke 22:52–53"> The same tyme sayd Iesus to the multitude: ye be come out as it were vnto a thefe with sweardes and staves for to take me. I sate daylie teachinge in the temple amonge you and ye toke me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; John 16:32; Mark 14:50–52"> All this was done that the scriptures of the Prophetes myght be fulfilled. Then all the disciples forsoke him and fleed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; Mark 14:53–65; Psalms 56:5–6"> And they toke Iesus and leed him to Cayphas the hye preeste where the Scribes and the Elders where assembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 7:32; John 18:15–16; John 18:25"> And Peter folowed him a farre of vnto the hye prestes place: and went in and sate with the servauntes to se the ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Acts 6:11–13; Deuteronomy 19:16–21; Mark 14:55–56"> The chefe prestes and the elders and all the counsell sought false witnes agenste Iesus for to put him to deeth</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Psalms 35:11; Psalms 27:12"> but founde none: in somoche that when many false witnesses cam yet founde they none. At the last came two false witnesses and sayd:</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="John 2:19–21; Matthew 27:40; Mark 15:29"> This felowe sayde: I can distroye the temple of God and bylde it agayne in .iii. dayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="John 19:9–11; John 18:19–24; Mark 14:60"> And the chefe preste arose and sayde to him: answerest thou nothinge? How is it that these beare witnes ageynst the?</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Matthew 16:16; Isaiah 53:7"> But Iesus helde his peace: And the chefe Preeste answered and sayd to him: I charge the in the name of the lyvinge God that thou tell vs whether thou be Christ the sonne of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Mark 14:62; Psalms 110:1"> Iesus sayd to him: thou haste sayd. Neverthelesse I saye vnto you hereafter shall ye se the sonne of man syttinge on the right honde of power and come in the clowddes of the skye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:24; John 10:33; Matthew 9:3"> Then the hye preste rent his clothes sayinge: He hath blasphemed: what nede we of eny moo witnesses? Behold now ye have hearde his blasphemy:</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="John 19:7; Acts 7:52; Leviticus 24:11–16"> what thinke ye? They answered and sayd: he his worthy to dye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 27:30"> Then spat they in his face and boffeted him with fistes. And other smote him with the palme af their hondes on the face</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Luke 22:63–65; Mark 14:65; 1 Peter 2:4–8"> sayinge: tell vs thou Christ who is he that smote the?</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="1 Kings 19:13; Matthew 26:71; Acts 5:37"> Peter sate with out in the palice. And a damsell came to him sayinge: Thou also waste with Iesus of Galilee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:12; Revelation 21:8; Matthew 26:40–43"> but he denyed before the all sayinge: I woot not what thou sayst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Mark 14:68–69; John 18:25–27; Luke 22:58"> When he was goone out into the poorche another wenche sawe him and sayde vnto them that were there: This felowe was also with Iesus of Nazareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Matthew 26:74; Isaiah 48:1; Zechariah 8:17"> And agayne he denyed with an oothe that he knew the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73" data-xref="Judges 12:6; John 18:26–27; Nehemiah 13:24"> And after a whyle came vnto him they that stode bye and sayde vnto Peter: suerly thou arte even one of the for thy speache bewreyeth the.</span>
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; 1 Corinthians 16:22; Mark 14:71–72"> Then beganne he to course and to sweare that he knewe not the man. And immedyatly the cocke krewe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v75" data-v="75" data-xref="Matthew 26:34; Romans 7:18–20; Luke 22:31–34"> And Peter remembred the wordes of Iesu which sayde vnto him: before the cocke crowe thou shalt deny me thryse: and went out at the dores and wepte bitterly.</span>
