<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 16:1–8; John 20:1–10; Matthew 27:56"> The Sabboth daye at even which dauneth the morowe after the Sabboth Mary Magdalene and the other Mary came to se the sepulcre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 16:26; John 20:12–13; Mark 16:3–5"> And beholde ther was a greate erth quake. For the angell of the lorde descended from heven: and came and rowlled backe the stone from the dore and sate apon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 9:3; Acts 1:10; Mark 16:5"> His countenaunce was lyke lyghtnynge and his raymet whyte as snowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Daniel 10:7; Matthew 27:65–66"> And for feare of him the kepers were astunnyed and became as deed men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; Hebrews 1:14; Isaiah 41:10"> The angell answered and sayde to the wemen feare ye not. I knowe that ye seke Iesus which was crucified:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 16:6; Matthew 12:40; Luke 24:6–8"> he is not here: he is rysen as he sayde. Come and se the place where the lorde was put:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Luke 24:22–24; Matthew 28:10"> and goo quickly and tell his disciples that he is rysen from deeth. And beholde he will go before you into Galile there ye shall se him. Lo I have tolde you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 2:11; Mark 16:8; Luke 24:36–41"> And they departed quickly from the sepulcre with feare and greate Ioye: and did runne to bringe his disciples worde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 28:17; John 20:14–17; Matthew 14:33"> And as they went to tell his disciples: beholde Iesus met them sayinge: All hayle. And they came and held him by the fete and worshipped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:29; John 20:17; Matthew 28:7"> Then sayde Iesus vnto them: be not afrayde. Go and tell my brethren that they goo in to Galile and there shall they se me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 27:65–66; Matthew 28:4"> When they were gone: beholde some of the kepers came into the cyte and shewed vnto the hie prestes all the thinges that were hapened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:3–4; Acts 4:5–22; Matthew 27:1–2"> And they gaddered them to gedder with the elders and toke counsell and gave large money vnto the soudiers</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:64"> sayinge: Saye that his disciples came by nyght and stole him awaye whill ye slept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Acts 12:19"> And if this come to the rulers cares we wyll pease him and save you harmeles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 27:8; Matthew 26:15"> And they toke the money and dyd as they were taught. And this sayinge is noysed amonge the Iewes vnto this daye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Matthew 28:10; Matthew 28:7"> Then the .xi. disciples went awaye into Galile in to a mountayne where Iesus had appoynted them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 45:11; Psalms 2:12; John 5:23"> And when they sawe hym they worshipped him. But some of them douted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:35; 1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 11:27"> And Iesus came and spake vnto them sayinge: All power ys geve vnto me in heven and in erth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 2:38–39; Acts 1:8"> Go therfore and teache all nacions baptysinge them in the name of the father and the sonne and the holy goost:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Joshua 1:5; Acts 18:9–10"> Teachinge them to observe all thynges what soever I comcommaunded you. And lo I am with you all waye even vntyll the ende of the worlde.</span>
