<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 13:47; Matthew 13:24; Song of Solomon 8:11–12"> For the kyngdome of heven ys lyke vnto an houssholder which went out erly in the morninge to hyre labourers into hys vyneyarde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:26; Ecclesiastes 12:1; Matthew 22:19"> And he agreed with the labourers for a peny a daye and sent them into his vyneyarde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:13; Acts 2:15; Matthew 20:6–7"> And he went out about the thyrde houre and sawe other stonding ydell in the market place</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; 1 Peter 4:2–3; Luke 19:7–10"> and sayd vnto them go ye also into my vyneyarde: and whatsoever is right I will geve you. And they went there waye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24–26; Acts 10:3; Mark 15:33–34"> Agayne he wet out about the sixte and nynthe houre and dyd lyke wyse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; John 9:4; Acts 17:21"> And he went out aboute the eleventhe houre and founde other stondynge ydell and sayde vnto them: Why stonde ye here all the daye ydell?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:11–12; Hebrews 6:10; John 9:4"> They sayde vnto hym: because no man hath hyred vs. He sayde to them: goo ye alsoo into my vyneyarde and whatsoever is right that shall ye receave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:15; 1 Peter 4:10; Romans 2:6–10"> When even was come the lorde of the vyneyarde sayde vnto hys steward: call the labourers and geve them their hyre beginnyng at the laste tyll thou come to the fyrste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 20:6–7; Luke 23:40–43; Matthew 20:2"> And they whiche were hyred aboute the eleventhe houre came and receaved every man a peny.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then came the fyrst supposyng that they shuld receave moare: and they likewyse receaved every man a peny.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:16; Luke 19:7; Acts 22:21–22"> And when they had receaved it they murmured agaynst the good man of the housse</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:5–6; 2 Corinthians 11:23–28; Luke 12:55"> saying: These laste have wrought but one houre and thou hast made them equall vnto vs which have born the burthe and heet of the daye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:50; Matthew 22:12; Romans 9:14–15"> He answered to one of the sayinge: frende I do the no wronge: dyddest thou not agre with me for a peny?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:2; Luke 15:31; Matthew 6:6"> Take that which is thy duty and go thy waye. I will geve vnto this last as moche as to the.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 9:15–24; Deuteronomy 15:9; Jonah 4:1–4"> Ys it not lawfull for me to do as me listeth with myne awne? Ys thyne eye evyll because I am good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Mark 10:31; Matthew 22:14"> Soo the laste shalbe fyrste and the fyrste shalbe laste. For many are called and feawe be chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 10:32–34; Luke 18:31–34; Matthew 16:13"> And Iesus ascended to Ierusalem and toke the .xii. disciples a parte in the waye and sayde to the.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Acts 2:23; Matthew 27:1"> Beholde we goo vp to Ierusalem and the sonne of man shalbe betrayed vnto the chefe prestes and vnto the scribes and they shall condemne him to deeth</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Mark 15:16–20; Matthew 27:27–31"> and shall delyvre him to the getils to be mocked to be scourged and to be crucified: and the thyrd daye he shall ryse agayne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Mark 10:35–45; Matthew 8:2"> Then came to hym the mother of zebedes chyldren with her sonnes worshippynge him and desyringe a certayne thinge of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Mark 10:36–37; Esther 5:3"> And he sayd vnto her: what wilt thou have? She sayde vnto him: Gravnte that these my two sonnes may sit the one on thy right hond and the other on the lifte hond in thy kyngdome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 26:42; Luke 22:42; Matthew 26:39"> Iesus answered and sayd: Ye wot not what ye axe. Are ye able to drynke of the cuppe that I shall drynke of and to be baptised with the baptyme that I shalbe baptised with? They answered to him that we are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 1:9; Matthew 25:34; Acts 12:2"> And he sayd vnto the: Ye shall drinke of my cvp and shalbe baptised with the baptyme that I shalbe baptised with. But to syt on my ryght hond and on my lyst hond is not myne to geve: but to them for whom it is prepared of my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Philippians 2:3; Mark 10:41"> And when the ten hearde this they disdayned at the two brethren:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 22:25–27; Matthew 20:25–28; John 13:12–17"> But Iesus called them vnto him and sayde: Ye knowe that the lordes of the gentyls have dominacion over them. And they that are great exercise power over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 10:43; Mark 9:35; Mark 10:45"> It shall not be so amonge you. But whosoever wyll be greate amonge you let him be youre minister:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:26; Mark 9:33–35; Matthew 18:4"> and whosoever wilbe chefe let him be youre servaut</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 22:27; Hebrews 9:28; Titus 2:14"> even as the sonne of man came not to be ministred vnto but to minister and to geve his lyfe for the redempcion of many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 10:46–52; Luke 18:35–43; Matthew 9:27–31"> And as they departed from Hierico moche people folowed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 10:46; Isaiah 42:16; Matthew 21:9"> And beholde two blinde men sittinge by the waysyde when they hearde Iesus passe by cryed sayinge: Thou Lorde the sonne of David have mercy on vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 19:13; Colossians 4:2; Matthew 7:7–8"> And the people rebuked them be cause they shulde holde their peace. But they cryed the moare sayinge: have mercy on vs thou Lorde which arte the sonne of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:37; Philippians 4:6; Matthew 20:21"> Then Iesus stode styll and called the and sayde: what will ye that I shulde do to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 119:18; Ephesians 1:17–19"> They sayd to him: Lorde that oure eyes maye be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15–16; Matthew 9:29; Acts 26:18"> Iesus had compassion on the and touched their eyes. And immediatly their eyes receaved syght. And they folowed him.</span>
