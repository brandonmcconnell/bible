<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 25:25; Acts 25:12; Acts 10:1"> When it was concluded that we shuld sayle into Italy they delivered Paul and certayne other presoners vnto one named Iulius an vnder captayne of Cesars soudiars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 19:29; Philemon 1:24; Colossians 4:10"> And we entred into a ship of Adramicium and lowsed from lond apoynted to sayle by the costes of Asia one Aristarcus out of Macedonia of the contre of Thessalia beinge with vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 24:23; Acts 28:16; Acts 27:43"> And the nexte daye we came to Sidon. And Iulius courteously entreated Paul and gave him liberte to goo vnto his frendes and to refresshe him selfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 15:39; Acts 13:4"> And from thence lanched we and sayled harde by Cypers because the wyndes were contrarye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 6:9; Acts 13:13; Acts 15:41"> Then sayled we over the see of Cilicia and Pamphylia and came to Myra a cite in Lycia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 28:11; Acts 27:1; Acts 6:9"> And there the vnder captayne founde a shippe of Alexander redy to sayle into Italy and put vs therin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 27:21; Titus 1:5; Titus 1:12"> And when we had sayled slowly many dayes and scace were come over agaynst Gnydon (because the wynde with stode vs) we sayled harde by the costes of Candy over agaynste Salmo</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and with moche worke sayled beyonde that and came vnto a place called good porte. Nye whervnto was a citie called Lasea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 29:7; Leviticus 23:27–29; Leviticus 16:29–31"> When moche tyme was spent and saylinge was now ieoperdeous because also that we had overlonge fasted Paul put them in remembraunce</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 2:30; Acts 27:20–26; Acts 27:41–44"> and sayde vnto them Syrs I perceave that this vyage wilbe with hurte and moche domage not of the ladynge and ship only: but also of oure lyves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:4; Hebrews 11:7; Ezekiel 3:17–18"> Neverthelather the vndercaptayne beleved the governer and the master better then tho thinges which were spoken of Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 107:30; Acts 27:8"> And because the haven was not comodius to wynter in many toke counsell to departe thence yf by eny meanes they myght attayne to Phenices and there to wynter which is an haven of Candy and servith to the southwest and northwest wynde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:55; Song of Solomon 4:16; Job 37:17"> When the south wynde blewe they supposynge to obtayne their purpose lowsed vnto Asson and sayled paste all Candy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 4:37; Psalms 107:25–27; Exodus 14:21–27"> But anone after ther arose agaynste their purpose a flawe of wynde out of the northeeste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:4; Acts 27:27"> And when the ship was caught and coulde not resist the wynde we let her goo and drave with the wether.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And we came vnto an yle named Clauda and had moche worke to come by abote</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 27:29; Acts 27:26; Acts 27:41"> which they toke vp and vsed helpe vndergerdynge the shippe fearynge lest we shuld have fallen into Syrtes and we let doune a vessell and so were caryed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jonah 1:5; Acts 27:38; Matthew 16:26"> The nexte daye when we were tossed with an exceadynge tempest they lyghtened the ship</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 2:4; Luke 9:24–25; Mark 8:35–37"> and the thyrde daye we cast out with oure awne hondes the tacklynge of the shippe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Matthew 8:24–25; Jeremiah 2:25"> When at the last nether sunne nor starre in many dayes appered and no small tempest laye apon vs all hope that we shuld be saved was then taken awaye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 42:22; Psalms 107:5–6; Acts 27:7"> Then after longe abstinence Paul stode forth in the myddes of them and sayde: Syrs ye shulde have harkened to me and not have lowsed from Candy nether to have brought vnto vs this harme and losse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 27:25; Acts 27:36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> And nowe I exhorte you to be of good chere. For ther shalbe no losse of eny mans lyfe amonge you save of the ship only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Romans 1:9; 2 Timothy 4:17"> For ther stode by me this nyght the angell of God whose I am and whom I serve</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 23:11; 2 Timothy 4:16–17; Matthew 10:28"> sayinge: feare not Paul for thou must be brought before Cesar. And lo God hath geven vnto the all that sayle with the.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 4:20–21; Luke 1:45; Numbers 23:19"> Wherfore Syrs be of good chere: for I beleve God that so it shalbe even as it was tolde me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 28:1; Acts 27:17; Acts 27:29"> How be it we must be cast into a certayne ylonde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jonah 1:6; Acts 27:30; Revelation 18:17"> But when the fourtethe nyght was come as we were caryed in Adria about mydnyght the shipmen demed that ther appered some countre vnto them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and sounded and founde it .xx. feddoms. And when they had gone a lytell further they sounded agayne and founde .xv. feddoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 27:17; Hebrews 6:19; Deuteronomy 28:67"> Then fearinge lest they shuld have fallen on some Rocke they cast .iiii. ancres out of the sterne and wysshed for the daye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 27:16; Acts 27:32"> As the shipmen were about to fle out of the ship and had let doune the bote into the see vnder a coloure as though they wolde have cast ancres out of the forshippe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:13–14; Psalms 91:11–12; Jeremiah 29:11–13"> Paul sayd vnto the vnder captayne and the soudiers: excepte these abyde in the ship ye cannot be safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–9; Luke 16:8"> Then the soudiers cut of the rope of the bote and let it fall awaye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And in the meane tyme betwixt that and daye Paul besought them all to take meate sayinge: this is the fourtenthe daye that ye have taried and continued fastynge receavinge nothinge at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 10:30; 1 Kings 1:52; Luke 21:18"> Wherfore I praye you to take meate: for this no dout is for youre helth: for ther shall not an heere fall from the heed of eny of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 15:36; 1 Corinthians 10:30–31; 1 Timothy 4:3–4"> And when he had thus spoke he toke breed and gave thankes to God in presence of the all and brake it and begane to eate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4–6; Acts 27:22; Psalms 27:14"> Then were they all of good cheare and they also toke meate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 2:41; Romans 13:1; 1 Peter 3:20"> We were all together in the ship two hundred thre score and sixtene soules.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jonah 1:5; Matthew 6:25; Acts 27:18–19"> And when they had eate ynough they lightened the ship and cast out the wheate into the see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 28:1"> When that was daye they knew not the lande but they spied a certayne haven with a banke into the which they were mynded (yf that were possible) to thrust in the ship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 33:23; Acts 27:29–30"> And when they had taken vp the ancres they comytted them selves vnto the see and lowsed the rudder bondes and hoysed vp the mayne sayle to the wynde and drue to londe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:25–26; Ezekiel 27:26; Acts 27:26–29"> But they chaunsed on a place which had the see on bothe the sydes and thrust in the ship. And the foore parte stucke fast and moved not but the hynder brake with the violence of the waves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Mark 15:15–20; Ecclesiastes 9:3; Psalms 74:20"> The soudears counsell was to kyll the presoners lest eny of them when he had swome out shulde fle awaye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 27:3; Proverbs 16:7; Acts 27:11"> But the vndercaptayne willinge to save Paul kept the from their purpose and commaunded that they that could swyme shulde cast the selves first in to the see and scape to londe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 27:22; Psalms 107:28–30; John 6:39–40"> And the other he commaunded to goo some on bordes and some on broken peces of the ship. And so it came to passe that they came all safe to londe.</span>
