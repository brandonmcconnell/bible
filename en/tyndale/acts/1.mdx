<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 1:3; Luke 24:19; Matthew 4:23–24"> In the former treatise (Deare frende Theophilus) I have written of all that Iesus beganne to do and teache</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 1:9; John 20:21; Isaiah 59:20–21"> vntyll the daye in the which he was taken vp after that he thorowe the holy goost had geven commaundementes vnto the Apostles which he had chosen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5–7; Acts 13:31; John 20:26"> to whom also he shewed him selfe alyve after his passion by many tokens apperynge vnto them fourty dayes and speakynge of the kyngdome of god</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 24:49; John 14:16; John 15:26"> and gaddered them togeder and commaunded the that they shuld not departe from Ierusalem: but to wayte for the promys of the father whereof ye have herde of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Acts 11:15–16; Luke 3:16"> For Iohn baptised with water: but ye shalbe baptised with the holy goost and that with in this feawe dayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 19:11; Luke 17:20; Daniel 7:27"> When they were come togeder they axed of him sayinge: Lorde wilt thou at this tyme restore agayne the kyngdome to Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–2; Mark 13:32"> And he sayde vnto them: It is not for you to knowe the tymes or the seasons which the father hath put in his awne power:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; Mark 16:15; Romans 15:19"> but ye shall receave power of the holy goost which shall come on you. And ye shall be witnesses vnto me in Ierusalem and in all Iewrye and in Samary and even vnto the worldes ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Luke 24:50–51; Mark 16:19"> And when he had spoken these thinges whyll they behelde he was take vp and a cloude receaved him vp out of their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 24:4; John 20:12; Revelation 7:14"> And while they looked stedfastly vp to heaven as he went beholde two men stode by them in white apparell</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; Mark 13:26"> which also sayde: ye men of Galile why stonde ye gasinge vp into heaven? This same Iesus which is taken vp from you in to heaven shall so come even as ye haue sene him goo into heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:52; Matthew 21:1; Luke 24:50"> Then returned they vnto Ierusalem from mount olivete which is nye to Ierusalem coteyninge a Saboth dayes iorney.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 20:8; Luke 22:12; Mark 14:15"> And when they were come in they went vp into a parler where abode both Peter and Iames Iohn and Andrew Philip and Thomas Bartlemew and Mathew Iames the sonne of Alpheus and Simon Zelotes and Iudas Iames sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 6:4; Ephesians 6:18; Colossians 4:2"> These all continued with one acorde in prayer and supplicacion with the wemen and Mary the mother of Iesu and with his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:6; Acts 21:20; Revelation 3:4"> And in those dayes Peter stode vp in the myddes of the disciples and sayde (the noumbre of names that were to gether were aboute an hondred and twenty)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 13:18; Psalms 55:12–15"> Ye men and brethren this scripture must have nedes ben fulfilled which the holy goost thorow the mouth of David spake before of Iudas which was gyde to them that tooke Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 1:25; Mark 3:19; John 17:12"> For he was noubred with vs and had obtayned fellouship in this ministracion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:3–10; Matthew 26:14–15; Psalms 55:23"> And the same hath now possessed a plot of grounde with the rewarde of iniquite and when he was hanged brast a sondre in the myddes and all his bowels gusshed oute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 2:22; Matthew 28:15; Acts 21:40"> And it is knowe vnto all the inhabiters of Ierusalem: in so moche that that felde is called in their mother tonge Acheldama that is to saye the bloud felde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 69:25; Psalms 109:8–15; Acts 1:25"> It is written in the boke of Psalmes: His habitacion be voyde and no man be dwellinge therin: and his bisshoprycke let another take.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 15:27; Luke 10:1–2; Numbers 27:17"> Wherfore of these me which have companyed with vs all the tyme that the Lorde Iesus went in and out amonge vs</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 1:2; Acts 4:33; Acts 1:8–9"> begynninge at the baptyme of Iohn vnto that same daye that he was taken vp from vs must one be ordeyned to be are witnes with vs of his resurreccion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 1:26"> And they appoynted two Ioseph called Barsabas (whose syr name was Iustus) and Mathias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Acts 15:8; Hebrews 4:13"> And they prayed sayinge: thou Lorde which knowest the hertes of all me shewe whether of these two thou hast chosen</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:2; John 6:70–71; John 17:12"> that the one maye take the roume of this ministracion and apostleshippe from the which Iudas by transgression fell that he myght go to his awne place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 16:8; Joshua 18:10; 1 Samuel 14:41–42"> And they gave forthe their lottes and the lot fell on Mathias and he was counted with the eleven Apostles.</span>
