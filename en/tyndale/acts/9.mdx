<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Timothy 1:13; Galatians 1:13"> And Saul yet brethynge oute threatnynges and slaughter agaynst the disciples of the lorde went vnto the hye preste</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 19:9; Acts 19:23; Acts 24:14"> and desyred of him letters to Damasco to the synagoges: that yf he founde eny of this waye whether they were men or wemen he myght bringe them bounde vnto Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 26:12–13; 1 Corinthians 15:8; Acts 22:6"> But as he went on his iorney it fortuned that he drue nye to Damasco and sodenly ther shyned rounde about him a lyght from heven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; Genesis 22:11; Romans 11:22"> And he fell to the erth and hearde a voyce sayinge to him: Saul Saul why persecutest thou me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; 1 Samuel 3:4–10; Job 9:4"> And he sayde what arte thou lorde? And the lorde sayd I am Iesus whom thou persecutest it shalbe harde for the to kycke agaynst the pricke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 22:10; Acts 26:16; 1 Timothy 1:14–16"> And he bothe tremblynge and astonyed sayde: Lorde what wilt thou have me to do? And the Lorde sayde vnto him: aryse and goo into the cite and it shalbe tolde the what thou shalt do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 22:9; Daniel 10:7; Acts 26:13–14"> The men which iornayed with him stode amased for they herde a voyce but sawe no man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 22:11; Acts 9:18; 2 Kings 6:17–20"> And Saul arose from the erth and opened his eyes but sawe no man. Then ledde they him by the honde and brought him into Damasco.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jonah 3:6–8; Esther 4:16; 2 Chronicles 33:12–13"> And he was .iii. dayes with out syght and nether ate nor dranke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 22:1; Acts 10:3; Acts 22:12"> And ther was a certayne disciple at Damasco named Ananias and to him sayde the lorde in a vision: Ananias. And the he sayde: beholde I am here lorde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 21:39; Acts 22:3; Acts 9:30"> And the lorde sayde to him: aryse and goo into the strete which is called strayght and seke in the housse of Iudas after one called Saul of Tharsus. For beholde he prayeth</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 5:23; Acts 9:10; Acts 9:17–18"> and hath sene in a vision a man named Ananias comynge in to him and puttynge his hondes on him that he myght receave his syght.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13–15; Acts 8:3; Acts 22:19–20"> Then Ananias answered: Lorde I have hearde by many of this man how moche evell he hath done to thy sainctes at Ierusalem</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 9:21; Acts 7:59; 2 Timothy 2:22"> and here he hath auctorite of the hye prestes to bynde all that call on thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 1:15–16; Ephesians 3:7–8; 2 Timothy 4:16–17"> The lorde sayde vnto him: Goo thy wayes: for he is a chosen vessell vnto me to beare my name before the gentyls and kynges and the chyldren of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:3; Acts 21:11; 2 Corinthians 6:4"> For I will shewe him how great thinges he must suffre for my names sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 8:17; Acts 6:6; Acts 2:4"> Ananias went his waye and entryd into the housse and put his hondes on him and sayde: brother Saul the lorde that apperyd vnto the in the waye as thou camst hath sent me that thou myghtest receave thy syght and be filled with the holy goost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:14; Acts 22:16; Acts 2:38"> And immediatly ther fell from his eyes as it had bene scales and he receaved syght and arose and was baptised</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 26:20; 1 Samuel 30:12; Galatians 1:17"> and receaved meate and was comforted. Then was Saul a certayne daye with the disciples which were at Damasco.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 20:28; Galatians 2:20; 1 John 4:14–15"> And streyght waye he preached Christ in the synagoges how that he was the sonne of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 9:13–14; Acts 8:3; 2 Thessalonians 1:10"> All that hearde him were amased and sayde: is not this he that spoyled the which called on this name in Ierusalem and came hyther for the entent that he shuld bringe the bounde vnto the hye prestes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 18:5; Isaiah 40:29; Luke 21:15"> But Saul encreased in stregth and confounded the Iewes which dwelte at Damasco affirminge that this was very Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 1:17–18; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Acts 14:19"> And after a good whyle the Iewes toke counsell to gether to kyll him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:32; Acts 25:3; Acts 20:3"> But their layinge awayte was knowen of Saul. And they watched at the gates daye and nyght to kyll him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 2:15; 2 Corinthians 11:33; 1 Samuel 19:11–12"> Then the disciples toke him by night and put him thorow the wall and let him doune in a basket.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 26:20; Galatians 1:17–19; Acts 9:19"> And when Saul was come to Ierusalem he assayde to cople him silfe with the discyples and they were all afrayde of hym and beleued not that he was a disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 4:29; Acts 4:13"> But Bernabas toke hym and brought hym to the apostles and declared to the how he had sene the Lorde in the waye and had spoke wyth hym: and how he had done boldely at damasco in the name of Iesu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 1:21; Numbers 27:16–17; Psalms 121:8"> And he had his conuersacion with them at Ierusalem</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 6:1; Jude 1:9; Acts 11:20"> and quit hym silfe boldly in the name of the lorde Iesu. And he spake and disputed wyth the grekes: and they went aboute to slee hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 11:25; Acts 8:40; Acts 9:11"> But when the brethren knew of that they brought hym to cesarea and sent hym forth to Tharsus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 111:10; John 14:16–18; Isaiah 33:6"> Then had the congregacions rest thorowoute all Iewry and galile and Samary and were edified and walked in the feare of the lorde and multiplied by the comforte of the holy gost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 9:13; Acts 8:25; Proverbs 2:8"> And it chaunsed that as Peter walked throughoute all quarters he came to the saynctes which dwelt at Lydda</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 13:16; John 5:5; Mark 2:3–11"> and there he foude a certayne man namyd Eneas whych had kepte hys bed viii. yere sicke of the palsie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 9:6; Acts 3:12; Acts 3:6"> Then sayde Peter vnto hym: Eneas Iesus Christ make the whole. Aryse and make thy beed. And he arose immedyatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 11:21; Acts 9:42; 1 Chronicles 5:16"> And all that dwelt at lydda and assaron sawe hym and tourned to the lorde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Titus 3:8; Titus 2:7; 2 Chronicles 2:16"> Ther was at Ioppe a certayne woman (whiche was a disciple named Tabitha which by interpretacion is called dorcas) the same was full of good workes and almes dedes which she did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Acts 20:8; John 11:36–37"> And it chaunsed in those dayes that she was sicke and dyed. When they had wesshed her and layd her in a chamber:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 9:36; Acts 11:26; Acts 9:32"> Because Lydda was nye to Ioppa and the disciples had hearde that Peter was there they sent vnto hym desyrynge him that he wolde not be greved to come vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Proverbs 31:30–31; 1 Thessalonians 1:3"> Peter arose and came with them and when he was come they brought him in to the chamber. And all the wydowes stode roude aboute hym wepynge and shewynge the cotes and garmentes which Dorcas made whill she was with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 9:25; Mark 5:40–42; Luke 8:54"> And Peter put the all forth and kneled doune and prayde and turned him to the body and sayde: Tabitha aryse. And she opened her eyes and when she sawe Peter she sat vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Acts 6:1; Genesis 45:26"> And he gave her the honde and lyft her up and called the sainctes and wydowes and shewed her alyve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:44; John 12:11; John 11:45"> And it was knowne throwout all Ioppa and many beleved on the Lorde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 10:6; Acts 10:32"> And it fortuned that he taryed many dayes in Ioppa with one Simon a tanner.</span>
