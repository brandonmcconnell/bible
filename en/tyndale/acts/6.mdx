<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 2:41; Acts 4:35; Acts 2:47"> In those dayes as the nombre of the disciples grewe ther arose a grudge amonge the Grekes agaynst the Ebrues because their wyddowes were despysed in the dayly mynystracion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:9–14; Exodus 18:17–26; 2 Timothy 2:4"> Then the twelve called the multitude of the disciples to gether and sayde: it is not mete that we shuld leave the worde of God and serve at the tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7–15; James 3:17–18; Deuteronomy 1:13"> Wherfore brethren loke ye out amonge you seven men of honest reporte and full of the holy goost and wysdome which we maye apoynte to this nedfull busynes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 1:14; 1 Timothy 4:13–16; Philippians 1:9–11"> But we will geve oure selves cotinually to prayer and to the ministracion of the worde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 11:24; Acts 21:8; Acts 8:5–40"> And the sayinge pleased the whoale multitude. And they chose Steven a man full of fayth and of the holy goost and Philip and Prochorus and Nichanor and Timon and Permenas and Nicholas a converte of Antioche.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; Numbers 8:10; Acts 1:24"> Which they set before the Apostles and they prayed and layde their hondes on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:20; Acts 12:24; Colossians 1:6"> And the worde of God encreased and the noubre of the disciples multiplied in Ierusalem greatly and a great company of the prestes were obedient to the faythe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 4:29–30; Acts 7:55; Acts 6:5"> And Steven full of faythe and power dyd great wondres and myracles amonge the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 15:23; Acts 22:3; Acts 15:41"> Then ther arose certayne of the synagoge which are called Lybertines and Syrenites and of Alexandria and of Cilicia and Asia and disputed with Steven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Matthew 10:19–20; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> And they coulde not resist the wysdome and the sprete with which he spake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 26:59–60; 1 Kings 21:10–13; 1 Timothy 1:13"> Then sent they in men which sayd: we have hearde him speake blasphemous wordes agaynst Moses and agaynst God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Acts 14:2; Matthew 26:57"> And they moved the people and the elders and the scribes: and came apon him and caught him and brought him to the counsell</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 6:11; Acts 21:28; Acts 25:8"> and brought forth falce witnesses which sayde. This man ceasith not to speake blasphemous wordes agaynst this holy place and the lawe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; Hebrews 12:26–28; Acts 21:21"> for we hearde him saye: this Iesus of Nazareth shall destroye this place and shall chaunge the ordinaunces which Moses gave vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Ecclesiastes 8:1; Matthew 17:2"> And all that sate in the counsell loked stedfastly on him and sawe his face as it had bene the face of an angell.</span>
