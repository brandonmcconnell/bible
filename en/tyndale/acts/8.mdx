<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 22:20; Daniel 3:16–18; Acts 5:40"> Saul had pleasure in his deeth. And at that tyme there was a great persecucion agaynst the congregacion which was at Ierusalem and they were all scattered abroade thorowout the regions of Iury and Samaria except the Apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 50:10–11; Genesis 23:2; Jeremiah 22:18"> Then devout men dressed Steven and made great lamentacion over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Galatians 1:13; 1 Corinthians 15:9"> But Saul made havocke of the congregacion entrynge into every housse and drewe out bothe man and woman and thrust the into preson.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Acts 11:19; Acts 8:1"> They that were scattered abroade went every where preachyng the worde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 6:5; Acts 21:8; Acts 8:35–36"> Then came Philip into a cite of Samaria and preached Christ vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 4:41–42; 2 Chronicles 30:12; Matthew 20:15–16"> And the people gave hede vnto those thinges which Philip spake with one acorde in that they hearde and sawe the miracles which he dyd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:1; Acts 14:8–10; Mark 16:17–18"> For vnclene spretes cryinge with loude voyce came out of many that were possessed of them. And manye taken with palsies and many that halted were healed</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:52; Acts 13:48; Acts 8:39"> And ther was great ioye in that cite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 13:6; Acts 5:36; John 7:18"> And ther was a certayne man called Simon which before tyme in the same cite vsed witche crafte and bewitched the people of Samarie sayinge that he was a man that coulde do greate thinges</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; 2 Peter 2:2; Revelation 13:3"> Whom they regarded from the lest to the greatest sayinge: this felow is the great power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 8:9; Galatians 3:1; Isaiah 44:25"> And him they set moche by because of longe tyme with sorcery he had mocked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 1:3; Matthew 28:19"> But assone as they beleved Philippes preachynge of the kyngdome of God and of the name of Iesu Christ they were baptised bothe men and wemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:6–7; John 5:20; Acts 19:11"> Then Simon him selfe beleved also and was baptised and cotinued with Phillip and wondered beholdynge the miracles and signes which were shewed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; Galatians 2:9"> When the Apostles which were at Ierusalem hearde saye that Samaria had receaved the worde of God: they sent vnto them Peter and Iohn</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:38; John 16:23–24; John 14:13–14"> which when they were come prayed for the that they myght receave the holy goost</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 19:2; Matthew 28:19"> For as yet he was come on none of them: But they were baptised only in the name of Christ Iesu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 6:6; 2 Timothy 1:6"> Then layde they their hondes on them and they receaved the holy goost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:5; Matthew 10:8; 2 Kings 8:9"> When Simon sawe that thorowe layinge on of the Apostles hondes on them the holy goost was geven: he offered the money sayinge:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; 3 John 1:9; John 5:44"> Geve me also this power that on whom soever I put the hondes he maye receave the holy goost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 5:17; Matthew 10:8; Acts 2:38"> Then sayde Peter vnto him: thy monye perysh with the because thou wenest that the gifte of God maye be obteyned with money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Psalms 36:1; Matthew 6:22–24"> Thou hast nether parte nor felloushippe in this busines. For thy hert is not ryght in the syght of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:25–26; Daniel 4:27; Hebrews 4:12"> Repent therfore of this thy wickednes and praye God that the thought of thyne hert maye be forgeven the.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 58:6; Proverbs 5:22; John 8:34"> For I perceave that thou arte full of bitter gall and wrapped in iniquite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Numbers 21:7; Job 42:8"> Then answered Simon and sayde: Praye ye to the lorde for me that none of these thinges whiche ye have spoken fall on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Acts 26:22–23; Acts 1:8"> And they when they had testified and preached the worde of the lorde returned toward Ierusalem and preached the gospell in many cities of the Samaritas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 1:3"> Then the angell of the lorde spake vnto Phillip sayinge: aryse and goo towardes mydde daye vnto the waye with goeth doune from Ierusalem vnto Gaza which is in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; Psalms 68:31; Isaiah 56:3–8"> And he arose and wet on. And beholde a man of Ethiopia which was a chaberlayne and of grete auctorite with Candace quene of the Ethiopias and had the rule of all her treasure came to Ierusalem for to praye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Isaiah 1:1; Acts 28:25"> And as he returned home agayne sittynge in his charet he rede Esay the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:19; Acts 11:12; Acts 20:22–23"> Then the sprete sayde vnto Phillip: Goo neare and ioyne thy selfe to yonder charet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 24:44–45; John 5:39; Ephesians 5:17"> And Philip ranne to him and hearde him rede the prophet Esayas and sayde: Vnderstondest thou what thou redest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Psalms 73:16–17; Isaiah 29:18–19"> And he sayd: how can I except I had a gyde? And he desyred Philip that he wold come vp and sit with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7–8; 1 Peter 2:21–24; Jeremiah 51:40"> The tenoure of the scripture which he redde was this. He was ledde as a shepe to be slayne: and lyke a lambe dome before his sherer so opened he not his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Isaiah 53:8; Zechariah 13:7"> Because of his humblenes he was not estemed: who shall declare his generacion? for his lyfe is taken from the erthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Matthew 2:2–4"> The chamberlayne answered Philip and sayde: I praye the of whom speaketh the Prophet this? of him selfe or of some other man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Acts 18:28; Acts 28:23"> And Philip opened his mouth and beganne at the same scripture and preached vnto him Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 10:47; John 3:5; Titus 3:5–6"> And as they went on their waye they came vnto a certayne water and the chamberlayne sayde: Se here is water what shall let me to be baptised?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Philip sayde vnto him: Yf thou beleve with all thyne hert thou mayst. He answered and sayde: I beleve that Iesus Christe is the sonne of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:1; John 3:22–23"> And he commaunded the charet to stonde still. And they went doune bothe into the water: bothe Philip and also the chamberlayne and he baptised him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 18:12; 2 Kings 2:16; Ezekiel 8:3"> And assone as they were come out of the water the sprete of the lorde caught awaye Philip that the chamberlayne sawe him no moore. And he wet on his waye reioysinge:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 21:8; Acts 23:23; Acts 23:33"> but Philip was founde at Azotus. And he walked thorow out the countre preachynge in their cities tyll he came to Cesarea.</span>
