<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–3; 2 Timothy 2:20; Matthew 13:47–48"> A certayne man named Ananias with Saphira his wyfe solde a possession</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:37; 2 Peter 2:14–15; Matthew 23:5"> and kepte awaye parte of the pryce (his wyfe also beynge of counsell) and brought a certayne parte and layde it doune at the Apostles fete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:24; Isaiah 29:15; John 13:2"> Then sayde Peter: Ananias how is it that Satan hath filled thyne hert that thou shuldest lye vnto the holy goost and kepe awaye parte of the pryce of the lyvelod:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 5:3; Joshua 7:25–26; 2 Kings 5:25–27"> Pertayned it not vnto the only and after it was solde was not the pryce in thyne awne power? How is it that thou hast conceaved this thinge in thyne herte? Thou hast not lyed vnto men but vnto God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 5:10–11; Acts 2:43; Acts 5:13"> When Ananias herde these wordes. he fell doune and gave vp the goost. And great feare came on all the that these thinges hearde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 19:40; 2 Samuel 18:17; Deuteronomy 21:23"> And the yonge men roose vp and put him a parte and caryed him out and buryed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And it fortuned as it were aboute the space of .iii. houres after that his wyfe came in ignoraunt of that which was done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 5:2"> And Peter sayde vnto her: Tell me gave ye the londe for so moche? And she sayde: ye for so moche</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Acts 5:3–4; Psalms 95:8–11"> Then Peter sayde vnto her: why have ye agreed to gether to tept the sprete of the Lorde? Beholde the fete of them which have buryed thy husbande are at the dore and shall cary the out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:5"> Then she fell doune strayght waye at his fete and yelded vp the goost. And the yonge men came in and founde her ded and caryed her out and buryed her by her husbande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 5:5; 1 Corinthians 10:11–12; Acts 19:17"> And great feare came on all the congregacion and on as many as hearde it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 19:11; 2 Corinthians 12:12; Hebrews 2:4"> By the hondes of the Apostles were many signes and wondres shewed amonge the people. And they were all together with one acorde in Salomons porche.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:47; Acts 4:21; John 12:42"> And of other durst no man ioyne him selfe to them: neverthelater the people magnyfied them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 2:47; Acts 4:4; Isaiah 55:11–13"> The noumbre of them that beleved in the Lorde bothe of men and wemen grewe moare and moare:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Acts 19:11–12; John 14:12"> in so moche that they brought the sicke into the strettes and layde them on beddes and palettes that at the lest waye the shadowe of Peter when he came by myght shadowe some of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 16:17–18; Mark 2:3–4; Matthew 15:30–31"> There came also a multitude out of the cities roud about vnto Ierusalem bringynge sicke folkes and them which were vexed with vnclene spretes. And they were healed every one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 13:45; Acts 7:9; Acts 17:5"> Then the chefe preste rose vp and all they that were with him (which is the secte of the Saduces) and were full of indignacion</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 4:3; Luke 21:12; 2 Corinthians 11:23"> and layde hondes on the Apostles and put them in the comen preson.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 16:26; Psalms 34:7; Acts 12:7–11"> But the angell of the Lorde by nyght openned the preson dores and brought them forthe and sayde:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 6:63; John 6:68; 1 John 5:11–12"> goo steppe forthe and speake in the temple to the people all the wordes of this lyfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 8:2; Acts 22:15; Acts 12:18–19"> When they hearde that they entred into the temple erly in the morninge and taught. The chefe prest came and they that were with him and called a counsell to gedder and all the elders of the chyldren of Israel and sent to the preson to fet them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> When the ministres came and founde them not in the preson they returned and tolde</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Psalms 33:10; Daniel 6:22–24"> sayinge: the preson founde we shut as sure as was possible and the kepers stondynge with out before the dores. But when we had opened we founde no man with in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Acts 5:26; John 12:19"> When the chefe prest of all and the ruler of the temple and the hye prestes hearde these thinges they douted of them whervnto this wolde growe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Then came one and shewed them: beholde the men that ye put in preson stonde in the temple and teache the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:13; Acts 4:21; Matthew 26:5"> Then went the ruler of the temple with ministers and brought the with out violence. For they feared the people lest they shuld have bene stoned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 5:22; Acts 4:7; Luke 22:66"> And when they had brought them they set them before the counsell. And the chefe preste axed them</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Acts 7:52; Acts 4:10–11"> sayinge: dyd not we straytely commaunde you that ye shuld not teache in this name? And beholde ye have filled Ierusalem with youre doctrine and ye intende to brynge this mans bloud vpon vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:19; 1 Samuel 15:24; Mark 7:7–9"> Peter and the other Apostles answered and sayde: We ought moare to obey God then men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Galatians 3:13; 1 Peter 2:24; Acts 10:39"> The God of oure fathers raysed vp Iesus whom ye slewe and hanged on tre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:47; Acts 2:33; Hebrews 2:10"> Him hath god lifte vp with his right hand to be a ruler and a savioure for to geve repentaunce to Israell and forgevenes of synnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 15:26–27; Hebrews 2:3–4; Acts 1:8"> And we are his recordes concernynge these thinges and also the holy goost whom God hath geve to them that obey him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 7:54; Acts 2:37; Luke 4:28–29"> When they hearde that they clave asunder: and sought meanes to slee them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 22:3; Luke 2:46; Luke 5:17"> Then stode ther vp one in the counsell a Pharisey named Gamaliel a doctoure of lawe had in auctorite amonge all the people and commaunded to put the Apostles a syde a lytell space</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 22:26; Jeremiah 26:19; Matthew 27:19"> and sayde vnto them: Men of Israel take hede to youre selves what ye entende to do as touchinge these men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 8:9; 2 Peter 2:2; Galatians 6:3"> Before these dayes rose vp one Theudas bostinge him selfe to whom resorted a nombre of men about a foure hondred which was slayn and they all which beleved him were scatred a broode and brought to nought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 2:1–2; Psalms 7:14–15; Matthew 26:52"> After this man arose ther vp one Iudas of Galile in the tyme when tribute began and drewe awaye moche people after him. He also perisshed: and all even as many as harkened to him are scattered a brood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Lamentations 3:37; Isaiah 8:9–10"> And now I saye vnto you: refrayne youre selves from these men let them alone. For yf the consell or this worke be of men it will come to nought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; Isaiah 46:10; Matthew 16:18"> But and yf it be of God ye can not destroye it lest haply ye be founde to stryve agaynst God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 10:17; Acts 5:28; Acts 4:17–21"> And to him they agreed and called the Apostles and bet them and commaunded that they shuld not speake in the name of Iesu and let them goo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 4:13–16; Luke 6:22"> And they departed from the counsell reioysynge that they were counted worthy to soffre rebuke for his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Acts 2:46; Acts 20:20; Luke 21:37"> And dayly in the temple and in every housse they ceased not teachinge and preachinge Iesus Christ.</span>
