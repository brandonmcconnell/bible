<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; James 2:9; Leviticus 19:15"> Brethren have not the fayth of oure lorde Iesus Christ the lorde of glory in respecte of persons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 15:22; Esther 3:10; Esther 8:2"> Yf ther come into youre company a man with a golden rynge and in goodly aparell and ther come in also a poore man in vyle rayment</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:9; James 2:6; Luke 7:44–46"> and ye have a respecte to him that weareth the gaye clothynge and saye vnto him. Sit thou here in a good place: and saye vnto the poore stonde thou there or sit here vnder my fotestole:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:24; Job 34:19; Matthew 7:1–5"> are ye not parciall in youre selves and have iudged after evyll thoughtes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 5:3; Luke 6:20; Revelation 2:9"> Harken my deare beloved brethren. Hath not God chosen the poore of this worlde which are ryche in fayth and heyres of the kyngdom which he promysed to them that love him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 17:5; 1 Corinthians 11:22; Proverbs 14:31"> But ye have despised the poore. Are not the rych they which opresse you: and they which drawe you before iudges?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:9–11; Revelation 13:5–6; 1 Timothy 1:13"> Do not they speake evyll of that good name after which ye be named.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; Galatians 5:14; Matthew 22:39"> Yf ye fulfill the royall lawe accordynge to the scripture which sayth. Thou shallt love thyne neghbour as thy silfe ye do well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; James 2:1–4; 1 John 3:4"> But yf ye regarde one person more then another ye commit synne and are rebuked of the lawe as transgressours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Matthew 5:18–19; Deuteronomy 27:26"> Whosoever shall kepe the whole lawe and yet fayle in one poynt he is gyltie in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 20:13–14; Matthew 5:21–28; Deuteronomy 5:17–18"> For he that sayd. Thou shallt not commit adulterie sayed also: thou shallt not kyll. Though thou do none adulterie yet yf thou kill thou arte a transgresser of the lawe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 1:25; James 2:8; 2 Peter 1:4–8"> So speake ye and so do as they that shalbe iudged by the lawe of libertie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Matthew 6:15; Proverbs 21:13"> For ther shalbe iudgement merciles to him that sheweth no mercy and mercy reioyseth agaynst iudgement:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 7:26–27; Matthew 7:21–23; James 1:22–25"> What a vayleth it my brethren though a man saye he hath fayth when he hath no dedes? Can fayth save him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 3:11; Matthew 25:35–40; Isaiah 58:7"> If a brother or a sister be naked or destitute of dayly fode</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 3:27–28; 1 John 3:16–18; Matthew 25:42–45"> and one of you saye vnto them: Departe in peace God sende you warmnes and fode: not withstondinge ye geve the not tho thynges which are nedfull to the body: what helpeth it the?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 2:26; 2 Peter 1:5–9; James 2:19–20"> Even so fayth yf it have no dedes is deed in it selfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 3:13; James 2:14; Galatians 5:6"> Ye and a man myght saye: Thou hast fayth and I have dedes: Shewe me thy fayth by thy dedes: and I will shewe the my fayth by my dedes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 4:34; Mark 1:24; Matthew 8:29"> Belevest thou that ther is one God? Thou doest well. The devyls also beleve and tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 2:17; James 2:26; Proverbs 12:11"> Wilt thou vnderstonde o thou vayne man that fayth with out dedes is deed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 22:16–18; Genesis 22:9–12; John 8:39"> Was not Abraham oure father iustified thorow workes when he offered Isaac his sonne vpo the aultre?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:3; Hebrews 11:17–19; Galatians 5:6"> Thou seist how that fayth wrought with his dedes and through the dedes was the fayth made parfect:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Isaiah 41:8; 2 Chronicles 20:7"> and the scripture was fulfilled which sayth: Abraham beleved God and it was reputed vnto him for rightewesnes: and he was called the frede of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 2:21–22; James 2:15–18; Psalms 60:12"> Ye se then how that of dedes a man is iustified and not of fayth only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Joshua 6:22–25; Joshua 2:19–21"> Lyke wyse also was not Raab the harlot iustifyed thorow workes when she receaved the messengers and sent the out another waye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 2:20; James 2:17; James 2:14"> For as the body with oute the sprete is deed even so fayth with out dedes is deed.</span>
