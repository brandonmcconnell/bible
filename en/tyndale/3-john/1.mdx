<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 John 1:4; 2 John 1:2; Psalms 119:11"> I reioysed greatly when the brethren came and testified of the trueth that is in the how thou walkest in trouthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 4:19; 2 Timothy 1:2; Proverbs 23:24"> I have no greater ioye then for to heare howe that my sonnes walke in veritie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; Luke 12:42; Luke 16:10–12"> Beloved thou doest faythfully what soever thou doest to the brethren and to straungers</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; Titus 3:13; Acts 15:3"> which bare witnes of thy love before all the congregacion. Which brethren when thou bryngest forwardes on their iorney (as it besemeth god) thou shalt do well:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:5; Revelation 2:3; 2 Kings 5:15–16"> because that for his names sake they went forth and toke nothinge of the gentyls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–9; 1 Corinthians 16:10–11; 2 Corinthians 7:2–3"> We therfore ought to receave soche that we also myght be helpers to the trueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 1:7–16; Luke 22:24–27; Matthew 23:4–8"> I wrote vnto the congregacion: but Diotrephes which loveth to have the preeminence amonge them receaveth vs not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 66:5; 2 Corinthians 13:2; Luke 6:22"> Wherfore yf I come I will declare his dedes which he doeth iestinge on vs with malicious wordes nether is therewith content. Not only he him silfe receaveth not the brethren: but also he forbiddeth them that wolde and thrusteth them out of the congregacion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–17; Psalms 37:27; Ephesians 5:1"> Beloved folowe not that which is evyll but that which is good. He that doeth well is of God: but he that doeth evyll seith not God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; John 21:24; Acts 22:12"> Demetrius hath good reporte of all men and of the trueth: ye and we oure selves also beare recorde and ye knowe that oure recorde is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 John 1:12"> I have many thinges to wryte: but I will not with ynke and penne wryte vnto the.</span>
