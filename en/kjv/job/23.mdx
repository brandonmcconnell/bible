<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 32:4; Job 7:11"> Even to day is my complaint bitter: my stroke is heavier than my groaning.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="23.2 stroke: Heb. hand">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 16:21; Jeremiah 14:7; Job 13:3"> Oh that I knew where I might find him! that I might come even to his seat!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 13:18; Daniel 9:18–19; Exodus 32:12–13"> I would order my cause before him, and fill my mouth with arguments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 13:22–23; Job 10:2; 1 Corinthians 4:3–4"> I would know the words which he would answer me, and understand what he would say unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 13:21; Job 9:33–34; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Will he plead against me with his great power? No; but he would put strength in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 13:3; Romans 8:1; Isaiah 1:18"> There the righteous might dispute with him; so should I be delivered for ever from my judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 10:1; Job 9:11; Job 35:14"> Behold, I go forward, but he is not there; and backward, but I cannot perceive him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Isaiah 8:17"> On the left hand, where he doth work, but I cannot behold him: he hideth himself on the right hand, that I cannot see him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; James 1:2–4; 1 Peter 1:7"> But he knoweth the way that I take: when he hath tried me, I shall come forth as gold.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="23.10 that…: Heb. that is with me">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:18; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Job 17:9"> My foot hath held his steps, his way have I kept, and not declined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Psalms 119:103; Psalms 119:11"> Neither have I gone back from the commandment of his lips; I have esteemed the words of his mouth more than my necessary food.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="23.12 esteemed: Heb. hid, or, laid up">c</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="23.12 my…: or, my appointed portion">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 115:3; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 46:10"> But he is in one mind, and who can turn him? and what his soul desireth, even that he doeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:9; Romans 11:33"> For he performeth the thing that is appointed for me: and many such things are with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 77:3; Job 31:23"> Therefore am I troubled at his presence: when I consider, I am afraid of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 22:14; Job 27:2; Psalms 88:16"> For God maketh my heart soft, and the Almighty troubleth me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 19:8; Isaiah 57:1; 2 Kings 22:20"> Because I was not cut off before the darkness, neither hath he covered the darkness from my face.</span>
