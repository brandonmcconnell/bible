<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 13:4–5; Psalms 69:26; Job 11:2–3"> I have heard many such things: miserable comforters are ye all.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="16.2 miserable: or, troublesome">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 15:2; Titus 2:8"> Shall vain words have an end? or what emboldeneth thee that thou answerest?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="16.3 vain…: Heb. words of wind">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 109:25; Psalms 22:7; Lamentations 2:15"> I also could speak as ye do: if your soul were in my soul’s stead, I could heap up words against you, and shake mine head at you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 27:9; Galatians 6:1; Isaiah 35:3–4"> But I would strengthen you with my mouth, and the moving of my lips should asswage your grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 88:15–18; Psalms 77:1–9"> Though I speak, my grief is not asswaged: and though I forbear, what am I eased?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="16.6 what…: Heb. what goeth from me?">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:3; Job 1:15–19; Psalms 6:6–7"> But now he hath made me weary: thou hast made desolate all my company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 10:17; Job 19:20; Ruth 1:21"> And thou hast filled me with wrinkles, which is a witness against me: and my leanness rising up in me beareth witness to my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 35:16; Job 13:24; Lamentations 2:16"> He teareth me in his wrath, who hateth me: he gnasheth upon me with his teeth; mine enemy sharpeneth his eyes upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:15; Psalms 22:13; Isaiah 50:6"> They have gaped upon me with their mouth; they have smitten me upon the cheek reproachfully; they have gathered themselves together against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:12; Psalms 7:14; Psalms 31:8"> God hath delivered me to the ungodly, and turned me over into the hands of the wicked.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="16.11 hath…: Heb. hath shut me up">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 7:20; Lamentations 3:11–12; Lamentations 3:4"> I was at ease, but he hath broken me asunder: he hath also taken me by my neck, and shaken me to pieces, and set me up for his mark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 20:25; Job 6:4; Lamentations 2:11"> His archers compass me round about, he cleaveth my reins asunder, and doth not spare; he poureth out my gall upon the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 15:8; Lamentations 3:3–5; Joel 2:7"> He breaketh me with breach upon breach, he runneth upon me like a giant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 37:34; Psalms 7:5; Psalms 75:5"> I have sewed sackcloth upon my skin, and defiled my horn in the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:34; Psalms 102:3–5; Lamentations 1:16"> My face is foul with weeping, and on my eyelids is the shadow of death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; Psalms 66:18–19; Job 27:6–7"> Not for any injustice in mine hands: also my prayer is pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Ezekiel 24:7; Genesis 4:11"> O earth, cover not thou my blood, and let my cry have no place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 11:31"> Also now, behold, my witness is in heaven, and my record is on high.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="16.19 on high: Heb. in the high places">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 142:2; Job 16:4"> My friends scorn me: but mine eye poureth out tears unto God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="16.20 scorn me: Heb. are my scorners">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:10; Isaiah 45:9; Romans 9:20"> O that one might plead for a man with God, as a man pleadeth for his neighbour!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="16.21 neighbour: or, friend">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 14:10; Job 14:14; Job 14:5"> When a few years are come, then I shall go the way whence I shall not return.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="16.22 a few…: Heb. years of number">h</a></sup></span>
