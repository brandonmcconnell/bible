<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 12:4; Psalms 35:15–16; Psalms 69:12"> But now they that are younger than I have me in derision, whose fathers I would have disdained to have set with the dogs of my flock.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="30.1 younger…: Heb. of fewer days than I">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Yea, whereto might the strength of their hands profit me, in whom old age was perished?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 24:5; Hebrews 11:38; Job 24:13–16"> For want and famine they were solitary; fleeing into the wilderness in former time desolate and waste.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="30.3 solitary: or, dark as the night">b</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="30.3 in…: Heb. yesternight">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:16; 2 Kings 4:38–39; Amos 7:14"> Who cut up mallows by the bushes, and juniper roots for their meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Daniel 4:32–33; Psalms 109:10"> They were driven forth from among men, (they cried after them as after a thief;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1–2; Judges 6:2; Isaiah 2:19"> To dwell in the clifts of the valleys, in caves of the earth, and in the rocks.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="30.6 caves: Heb. holes">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 6:5; Job 11:12; Genesis 16:12"> Among the bushes they brayed; under the nettles they were gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 16:22; Job 40:4; Isaiah 32:6"> They were children of fools, yea, children of base men: they were viler than the earth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="30.8 base…: Heb. men of no name">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Lamentations 3:63; Job 17:6; Lamentations 3:14"> And now am I their song, yea, I am their byword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 50:6; Numbers 12:14"> They abhor me, they flee far from me, and spare not to spit in my face.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="30.10 and…: Heb. and withhold not spittle from">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:21; Ruth 1:21; Job 12:18"> Because he hath loosed my cord, and afflicted me, they have also let loose the bridle before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 19:12; Isaiah 3:5; Job 19:18"> Upon my right hand rise the youth; they push away my feet, and they raise up against me the ways of their destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:12; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 69:26"> They mar my path, they set forward my calamity, they have no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Psalms 18:4; Psalms 69:14–15"> They came upon me as a wide breaking in of waters: in the desolation they rolled themselves upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 13:3; Job 6:4; Isaiah 44:22"> Terrors are turned upon me: they pursue my soul as the wind: and my welfare passeth away as a cloud.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="30.15 my soul: Heb. my principal one">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Psalms 22:14; Isaiah 53:12"> And now my soul is poured out upon me; the days of affliction have taken hold upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 7:4; Job 33:19–21; Isaiah 38:13"> My bones are pierced in me in the night season: and my sinews take no rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:5; Job 19:20; Job 2:7"> By the great force of my disease is my garment changed: it bindeth me about as the collar of my coat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:31; Job 42:6; Genesis 18:27"> He hath cast me into the mire, and I am become like dust and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 19:7; Psalms 80:4–5; Psalms 22:2"> I cry unto thee, and thou dost not hear me: I stand up, and thou regardest me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 10:14–17; Job 16:9–14; Job 19:6–9"> Thou art become cruel to me: with thy strong hand thou opposest thyself against me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="30.21 become…: Heb. turned to be cruel">h</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="30.21 thy…: Heb. the strength of thy hand">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 27:21; Psalms 1:4; Hosea 4:19"> Thou liftest me up to the wind; thou causest me to ride upon it, and dissolvest my substance.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="30.22 substance: or, wisdom">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5–7; Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> For I know that thou wilt bring me to death, and to the house appointed for all living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Matthew 27:39–44; Job 19:7"> Howbeit he will not stretch out his hand to the grave, though they cry in his destruction.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="30.24 grave: Heb. heap">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalms 35:13–14; Jeremiah 13:17"> Did not I weep for him that was in trouble? was not my soul grieved for the poor?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="30.25 in trouble: Heb. hard of day?">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:15; Job 3:25–26; Jeremiah 14:19"> When I looked for good, then evil came unto me: and when I waited for light, there came darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Jeremiah 31:20; Jeremiah 4:19"> My bowels boiled, and rested not: the days of affliction prevented me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 42:9; Psalms 38:6; Psalms 43:2"> I went mourning without the sun: I stood up, and I cried in the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Micah 1:8; Psalms 102:6; Psalms 44:19"> I am a brother to dragons, and a companion to owls.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="30.29 owls: or, ostriches">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 4:8; Psalms 102:3; Psalms 119:83"> My skin is black upon me, and my bones are burned with heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 5:15; Isaiah 21:4; Isaiah 22:12"> My harp also is turned to mourning, and my organ into the voice of them that weep.</span>
