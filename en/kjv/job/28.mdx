<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:5; Isaiah 45:2–3; Isaiah 44:27"> He bindeth the floods from overflowing; and the thing that is hid bringeth he forth to light.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="28.11 overflowing: Heb. weeping">e</a></sup></span>

Fine gold shall not be given for it">f</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Revelation 4:6; Revelation 21:11"> The gold and the crystal cannot equal it: and the exchange of it shall not be for jewels of fine gold.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="28.17 jewels…: or, vessels of">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:15; Proverbs 31:10; Lamentations 4:7"> No mention shall be made of coral, or of pearls: for the price of wisdom is above rubies.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="28.18 coral: or, Ramoth">h</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 28:12; James 1:5; James 1:17"> Whence then cometh wisdom? and where is the place of understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 49:3–4; 1 Corinthians 2:7–10; Matthew 11:25"> Seeing it is hid from the eyes of all living, and kept close from the fowls of the air.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="28.21 air: or, heaven">i</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 8:22–31; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Jude 1:25"> God understandeth the way thereof, and he knoweth the place thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Zechariah 4:10; 2 Chronicles 16:9"> For he looketh to the ends of the earth, and seeth under the whole heaven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Isaiah 40:12; Job 12:15"> To make the weight for the winds; and he weigheth the waters by measure.</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 19:1; Proverbs 8:22–29"> Then did he see it, and declare it; he prepared it, yea, and searched it out.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="28.27 declare…: or, number it">j</a></sup></span>
