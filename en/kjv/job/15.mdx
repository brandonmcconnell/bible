<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 11:34; Jeremiah 23:18; Job 13:5–6"> Hast thou heard the secret of God? and dost thou restrain wisdom to thyself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 11:5; Job 12:3"> What knowest thou, that we know not? what understandest thou, which is not in us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 32:6–7; Job 8:8–10; Job 12:12"> With us are both the grayheaded and very aged men, much elder than thy father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 1:3–5; 1 Kings 22:24"> Are the consolations of God small with thee? is there any secret thing with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Mark 7:21–22"> Why doth thine heart carry thee away? and what do thy eyes wink at,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:25–27; James 3:2–6; Job 9:4"> That thou turnest thy spirit against God, and lettest such words go out of thy mouth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; Job 14:4; Proverbs 20:9"> What is man, that he should be clean? and he which is born of a woman, that he should be righteous?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 25:5; Job 4:18; Job 5:1"> Behold, he putteth no trust in his saints; yea, the heavens are not clean in his sight.</span>

A sound of fears">e</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 109:10; Psalms 59:15; Job 18:12"> He wandereth abroad for bread, saying, Where is it? he knoweth that the day of darkness is ready at his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 24:34; Proverbs 6:11; Job 6:2–4"> Trouble and anguish shall make him afraid; they shall prevail against him, as a king ready to the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Psalms 52:7; Psalms 73:9"> For he stretcheth out his hand against God, and strengtheneth himself against the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 18:40; Job 16:12; 2 Chronicles 32:13–17"> He runneth upon him, even on his neck, upon the thick bosses of his bucklers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 17:10; Jeremiah 5:28; Psalms 73:7"> Because he covereth his face with his fatness, and maketh collops of fat on his flanks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 3:14; Jeremiah 51:37; Micah 7:18"> And he dwelleth in desolate cities, and in houses which no man inhabiteth, which are ready to become heaps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Psalms 49:16–17; James 1:11"> He shall not be rich, neither shall his substance continue, neither shall he prolong the perfection thereof upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 4:9; Job 15:22; Job 18:5–6"> He shall not depart out of darkness; the flame shall dry up his branches, and by the breath of his mouth shall he go away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 59:4; Galatians 6:3; Psalms 62:10"> Let not him that is deceived trust in vanity: for vanity shall be his recompence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:17; Psalms 55:23; Hosea 9:16"> It shall be accomplished before his time, and his branch shall not be green.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="15.32 accomplished: or, cut off">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:39–40; Isaiah 33:9; Revelation 6:13"> He shall shake off his unripe grape as the vine, and shall cast off his flower as the olive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 8:13; Job 27:8; Job 8:22"> For the congregation of hypocrites shall be desolate, and fire shall consume the tabernacles of bribery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Hosea 10:13; Isaiah 59:4–5"> They conceive mischief, and bring forth vanity, and their belly prepareth deceit.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="15.35 vanity: or, iniquity">g</a></sup></span>
