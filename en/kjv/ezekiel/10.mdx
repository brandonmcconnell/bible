<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Ezekiel 9:3; Exodus 40:35"> Then the glory of the LORD went up from the cherub, and stood over the threshold of the house; and the house was filled with the cloud, and the court was full of the brightness of the LORD’s glory.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="10.4 went up: Heb. was lifted up">b</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:16; Psalms 36:6; Psalms 104:24"> And as for their appearances, they four had one likeness, as if a wheel had been in the midst of a wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:17; Ezekiel 10:22; Ezekiel 1:20"> When they went, they went upon their four sides; they turned not as they went, but to the place whither the head looked they followed it; they turned not as they went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:18; Revelation 4:8; Revelation 4:6"> And their whole body, and their backs, and their hands, and their wings, and the wheels, were full of eyes round about, even the wheels that they four had.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="10.12 body: Heb. flesh">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> As for the wheels, it was cried unto them in my hearing, O wheel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="10.13 it was…: or, they were called in my hearing, wheel, or, galgal">e</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:5; Hosea 9:12; Ezekiel 11:22"> And the cherubims were lifted up. This is the living creature that I saw by the river of Chebar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:19–21"> And when the cherubims went, the wheels went by them: and when the cherubims lifted up their wings to mount up from the earth, the same wheels also turned not from beside them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:20–21; Romans 8:2; Ezekiel 1:12"> When they stood, these stood; and when they were lifted up, these lifted up themselves also: for the spirit of the living creature was in them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="10.17 of…: or, of life">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 18:10; Matthew 23:37–39; Psalms 78:60–61"> Then the glory of the LORD departed from off the threshold of the house, and stood over the cherubims.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Ezekiel 1:17–21; Ezekiel 1:26–28"> And the cherubims lifted up their wings, and mounted up from the earth in my sight: when they went out, the wheels also were beside them, and every one stood at the door of the east gate of the LORD’s house; and the glory of the God of Israel was over them above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:1; Ezekiel 10:15; 1 Kings 7:36"> This is the living creature that I saw under the God of Israel by the river of Chebar; and I knew that they were the cherubims.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:6; Ezekiel 41:18–19; Ezekiel 10:14"> Every one had four faces apiece, and every one four wings; and the likeness of the hands of a man was under their wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:11; Ezekiel 1:10; Ezekiel 1:12"> And the likeness of their faces was the same faces which I saw by the river of Chebar, their appearances and themselves: they went every one straight forward.</span>
