<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word of the LORD came again unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:9; 2 Thessalonians 2:4; Isaiah 14:13–14"> Son of man, say unto the prince of Tyrus, Thus saith the Lord GOD; Because thine heart is lifted up, and thou hast said, I am a God, I sit in the seat of God, in the midst of the seas; yet thou art a man, and not God, though thou set thine heart as the heart of God:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="28.2 midst: Heb. heart">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 1:20; Daniel 2:22; Daniel 2:27–28"> Behold, thou art wiser than Daniel; there is no secret that they can hide from thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Proverbs 23:4–5; Proverbs 18:11"> With thy wisdom and with thine understanding thou hast gotten thee riches, and hast gotten gold and silver into thy treasures:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 62:10; Psalms 52:7; Ezekiel 28:2"> By thy great wisdom and by thy traffick hast thou increased thy riches, and thine heart is lifted up because of thy riches:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="28.5 thy great…: Heb. the greatness of thy wisdom">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Exodus 9:17; James 1:11"> Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD; Because thou hast set thine heart as the heart of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:12; Ezekiel 32:12; Ezekiel 30:11"> Behold, therefore I will bring strangers upon thee, the terrible of the nations: and they shall draw their swords against the beauty of thy wisdom, and they shall defile thy brightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:26–27; Ezekiel 27:34; Proverbs 1:12"> They shall bring thee down to the pit, and thou shalt die the deaths of them that are slain in the midst of the seas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Isaiah 31:3; Psalms 82:7"> Wilt thou yet say before him that slayeth thee, I am God? but thou shalt be a man, and no God, in the hand of him that slayeth thee.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="28.9 of him that slayeth: or, of him that woundeth">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:18; Ezekiel 32:19; Philippians 3:3"> Thou shalt die the deaths of the uncircumcised by the hand of strangers: for I have spoken it, saith the Lord GOD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Moreover the word of the LORD came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1; Ezekiel 26:17; 2 Corinthians 1:22"> Son of man, take up a lamentation upon the king of Tyrus, and say unto him, Thus saith the Lord GOD; Thou sealest up the sum, full of wisdom, and perfect in beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:15; Genesis 2:8; Ezekiel 27:16"> Thou hast been in Eden the garden of God; every precious stone was thy covering, the sardius, topaz, and the diamond, the beryl, the onyx, and the jasper, the sapphire, the emerald, and the carbuncle, and gold: the workmanship of thy tabrets and of thy pipes was prepared in thee in the day that thou wast created.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="28.13 sardius: or, ruby">d</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="28.13 beryl: or, chrysolite">e</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="28.13 emerald: or, chrysoprase">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 14:12–15; Exodus 30:26; Ezekiel 28:13"> Thou art the anointed cherub that covereth; and I have set thee so: thou wast upon the holy mountain of God; thou hast walked up and down in the midst of the stones of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Ezekiel 28:17–18; Romans 7:9"> Thou wast perfect in thy ways from the day that thou wast created, till iniquity was found in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Habakkuk 2:8; Habakkuk 2:17"> By the multitude of thy merchandise they have filled the midst of thee with violence, and thou hast sinned: therefore I will cast thee as profane out of the mountain of God: and I will destroy thee, O covering cherub, from the midst of the stones of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:5; Ezekiel 31:10; James 4:6"> Thine heart was lifted up because of thy beauty, thou hast corrupted thy wisdom by reason of thy brightness: I will cast thee to the ground, I will lay thee before kings, that they may behold thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Revelation 18:8; Amos 1:9–10"> Thou hast defiled thy sanctuaries by the multitude of thine iniquities, by the iniquity of thy traffick; therefore will I bring forth a fire from the midst of thee, it shall devour thee, and I will bring thee to ashes upon the earth in the sight of all them that behold thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:21; Revelation 18:21; Revelation 18:15–19"> All they that know thee among the people shall be astonished at thee: thou shalt be a terror, and never shalt thou be any more.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="28.19 a terror: Heb. terrors">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Again the word of the LORD came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Genesis 10:15; Jeremiah 25:22"> Son of man, set thy face against Zidon, and prophesy against it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:13; Exodus 14:17; Ezekiel 20:41"> And say, Thus saith the Lord GOD; Behold, I am against thee, O Zidon; and I will be glorified in the midst of thee: and they shall know that I am the LORD, when I shall have executed judgments in her, and shall be sanctified in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Ezekiel 26:6; Ezekiel 25:11"> For I will send into her pestilence, and blood into her streets; and the wounded shall be judged in the midst of her by the sword upon her on every side; and they shall know that I am the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 33:55; Joshua 23:13; 2 Corinthians 12:7"> And there shall be no more a pricking brier unto the house of Israel, nor any grieving thorn of all that are round about them, that despised them; and they shall know that I am the Lord GOD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:37; Ezekiel 20:41; Ezekiel 36:28"> Thus saith the Lord GOD; When I shall have gathered the house of Israel from the people among whom they are scattered, and shall be sanctified in them in the sight of the heathen, then shall they dwell in their land that I have given to my servant Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:24; Ezekiel 28:22; Jeremiah 32:15"> And they shall dwell safely therein, and shall build houses, and plant vineyards; yea, they shall dwell with confidence, when I have executed judgments upon all those that despise them round about them; and they shall know that I am the LORD their God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="28.26 safely: or, with confidence">h</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="28.26 despise: or, spoil">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
