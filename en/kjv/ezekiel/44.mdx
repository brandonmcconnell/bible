<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:4; Ezekiel 43:10–11; Deuteronomy 12:32"> And the LORD said unto me, Son of man, mark well, and behold with thine eyes, and hear with thine ears all that I say unto thee concerning all the ordinances of the house of the LORD, and all the laws thereof; and mark well the entering in of the house, with every going forth of the sanctuary.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="44.5 mark well: Heb. set thine heart">a</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="44.5 mark well: Heb. set thine heart">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:9; Ezekiel 45:9; 1 Peter 4:3"> And thou shalt say to the rebellious, even to the house of Israel, Thus saith the Lord GOD; O ye house of Israel, let it suffice you of all your abominations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 22:25; Jeremiah 9:26; Ezekiel 44:9"> In that ye have brought into my sanctuary strangers, uncircumcised in heart, and uncircumcised in flesh, to be in my sanctuary, to pollute it, even my house, when ye offer my bread, the fat and the blood, and they have broken my covenant because of all your abominations.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="44.7 strangers: Heb. children of a stranger">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:14; Numbers 18:3–5; Ezekiel 40:45–46"> And ye have not kept the charge of mine holy things: but ye have set keepers of my charge in my sanctuary for yourselves.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="44.8 my charge: or, my ward, or, ordinance">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Ezekiel 44:7; Zechariah 14:21"> Thus saith the Lord GOD; No stranger, uncircumcised in heart, nor uncircumcised in flesh, shall enter into my sanctuary, of any stranger that is among the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:26; Ezekiel 48:11; 2 Kings 23:8–9"> And the Levites that are gone away far from me, when Israel went astray, which went astray away from me after their idols; they shall even bear their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 16:9; 2 Chronicles 29:34; 2 Chronicles 30:17"> Yet they shall be ministers in my sanctuary, having charge at the gates of the house, and ministering to the house: they shall slay the burnt offering and the sacrifice for the people, and they shall stand before them to minister unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 16:10–16; Psalms 106:26; Ezekiel 14:3–4"> Because they ministered unto them before their idols, and caused the house of Israel to fall into iniquity; therefore have I lifted up mine hand against them, saith the Lord GOD, and they shall bear their iniquity.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="44.12 caused…: Heb. were for a stumblingblock of iniquity unto, etc">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 18:3; 2 Kings 23:9; Ezekiel 16:61"> And they shall not come near unto me, to do the office of a priest unto me, nor to come near to any of my holy things, in the most holy place: but they shall bear their shame, and their abominations which they have committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:28–32; Numbers 18:4"> But I will make them keepers of the charge of the house, for all the service thereof, and for all that shall be done therein.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Ezekiel 48:11; Ezekiel 44:7"> But the priests the Levites, the sons of Zadok, that kept the charge of my sanctuary when the children of Israel went astray from me, they shall come near to me to minister unto me, and they shall stand before me to offer unto me the fat and the blood, saith the Lord GOD:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:22; Malachi 1:12; Numbers 18:7–8"> They shall enter into my sanctuary, and they shall come near to my table, to minister unto me, and they shall keep my charge.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Exodus 28:43; Exodus 39:27–29"> And it shall come to pass, that when they enter in at the gates of the inner court, they shall be clothed with linen garments; and no wool shall come upon them, whiles they minister in the gates of the inner court, and within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 39:28; 1 Corinthians 11:4–10; Isaiah 3:20"> They shall have linen bonnets upon their heads, and shall have linen breeches upon their loins; they shall not gird themselves with any thing that causeth sweat.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="44.18 with…: or, in sweating places: Heb. in, or, with sweat">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:20; Leviticus 6:27; Leviticus 6:10–11"> And when they go forth into the utter court, even into the utter court to the people, they shall put off their garments wherein they ministered, and lay them in the holy chambers, and they shall put on other garments; and they shall not sanctify the people with their garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:14; Numbers 6:5; Leviticus 21:5–24"> Neither shall they shave their heads, nor suffer their locks to grow long; they shall only poll their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 10:9; Titus 1:7–8; 1 Timothy 5:23"> Neither shall any priest drink wine, when they enter into the inner court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; Leviticus 21:13–14; 1 Timothy 3:2"> Neither shall they take for their wives a widow, nor her that is put away: but they shall take maidens of the seed of the house of Israel, or a widow that had a priest before.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="44.22 put…: Heb. thrust forth">g</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="44.22 that had…: Heb. from a priest">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:26; Leviticus 10:10–11; 2 Timothy 2:24–25"> And they shall teach my people the difference between the holy and profane, and cause them to discern between the unclean and the clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:4; Ezekiel 22:26; 2 Chronicles 19:8–10"> And in controversy they shall stand in judgment; and they shall judge it according to my judgments: and they shall keep my laws and my statutes in all mine assemblies; and they shall hallow my sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:16; Leviticus 22:4; Matthew 8:21–22"> And they shall come at no dead person to defile themselves: but for father, or for mother, or for son, or for daughter, for brother, or for sister that hath had no husband, they may defile themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 9:13–14; Numbers 19:11–14; Numbers 6:10–21"> And after he is cleansed, they shall reckon unto him seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 6:9–11; Ezekiel 44:17; Leviticus 4:3–35"> And in the day that he goeth into the sanctuary, unto the inner court, to minister in the sanctuary, he shall offer his sin offering, saith the Lord GOD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 13:33; Numbers 18:20; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> And it shall be unto them for an inheritance: I am their inheritance: and ye shall give them no possession in Israel: I am their possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 27:21; Leviticus 27:28"> They shall eat the meat offering, and the sin offering, and the trespass offering; and every dedicated thing in Israel shall be theirs.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="44.29 dedicated: or, devoted">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:35–37; 2 Chronicles 31:10; Deuteronomy 18:4"> And the first of all the firstfruits of all things, and every oblation of all, of every sort of your oblations, shall be the priest’s: ye shall also give unto the priest the first of your dough, that he may cause the blessing to rest in thine house.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="44.30 And the first: or, And the chief">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 22:8; Exodus 22:31; Deuteronomy 14:21"> The priests shall not eat of any thing that is dead of itself, or torn, whether it be fowl or beast.</span>
