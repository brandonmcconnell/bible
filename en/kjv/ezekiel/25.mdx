<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word of the LORD came again unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:1–6; Amos 1:13–15; Ezekiel 6:2"> Son of man, set thy face against the Ammonites, and prophesy against them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:2; Psalms 70:2–3; Proverbs 17:5"> And say unto the Ammonites, Hear the word of the Lord GOD; Thus saith the Lord GOD; Because thou saidst, Aha, against my sanctuary, when it was profaned; and against the land of Israel, when it was desolate; and against the house of Judah, when they went into captivity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Judges 6:33; 1 Kings 4:30"> Behold, therefore I will deliver thee to the men of the east for a possession, and they shall set their palaces in thee, and make their dwellings in thee: they shall eat thy fruit, and they shall drink thy milk.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="25.4 men: Heb. children">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; 2 Samuel 12:26; Isaiah 17:2"> And I will make Rabbah a stable for camels, and the Ammonites a couchingplace for flocks: and ye shall know that I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:8; Obadiah 1:12; Zephaniah 2:10"> For thus saith the Lord GOD; Because thou hast clapped thine hands, and stamped with the feet, and rejoiced in heart with all thy despite against the land of Israel;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="25.6 hands: Heb. hand">b</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="25.6 feet: Heb. foot">c</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="25.6 heart: Heb. soul">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:4; Ezekiel 25:16; Ezekiel 6:14"> Behold, therefore I will stretch out mine hand upon thee, and will deliver thee for a spoil to the heathen; and I will cut thee off from the people, and I will cause thee to perish out of the countries: I will destroy thee; and thou shalt know that I am the LORD.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="25.7 a spoil: or, meat">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 15:1–9; Jeremiah 25:21; Jeremiah 48:1–47"> Thus saith the Lord GOD; Because that Moab and Seir do say, Behold, the house of Judah is like unto all the heathen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:23; Joshua 13:17; Joshua 13:19–20"> Therefore, behold, I will open the side of Moab from the cities, from his cities which are on his frontiers, the glory of the country, Beth-jeshimoth, Baal-meon, and Kiriathaim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="25.9 side…: Heb. shoulder of Moab">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:32; Psalms 83:3–6; Ezekiel 25:4"> Unto the men of the east with the Ammonites, and will give them in possession, that the Ammonites may not be remembered among the nations.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="25.10 with…: or, against the children of Ammon">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Ezekiel 30:19; Psalms 149:7"> And I will execute judgments upon Moab; and they shall know that I am the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:7–22; Ezekiel 25:8; Psalms 137:7"> Thus saith the Lord GOD; Because that Edom hath dealt against the house of Judah by taking vengeance, and hath greatly offended, and revenged himself upon them;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="25.12 by…: Heb. by revenging revengement">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:8; Malachi 1:3–4; Jeremiah 25:23"> Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD; I will also stretch out mine hand upon Edom, and will cut off man and beast from it; and I will make it desolate from Teman; and they of Dedan shall fall by the sword.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="25.13 they…: or, they shall fall by the sword unto Dedan">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 11:14; Genesis 27:29; Hebrews 10:30–31"> And I will lay my vengeance upon Edom by the hand of my people Israel: and they shall do in Edom according to mine anger and according to my fury; and they shall know my vengeance, saith the Lord GOD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:6; Isaiah 14:29–31; Ezekiel 25:12"> Thus saith the Lord GOD; Because the Philistines have dealt by revenge, and have taken vengeance with a despiteful heart, to destroy it for the old hatred;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="25.15 for the…: or, with perpetual hatred">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:14; Zephaniah 2:4–15; Ezekiel 25:13"> Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD; Behold, I will stretch out mine hand upon the Philistines, and I will cut off the Cherethims, and destroy the remnant of the sea coast.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="25.16 sea coast: or, haven of the sea">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:11; Ezekiel 25:14; Ezekiel 5:15"> And I will execute great vengeance upon them with furious rebukes; and they shall know that I am the LORD, when I shall lay my vengeance upon them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="25.17 great vengeance: Heb. great vengeances">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
