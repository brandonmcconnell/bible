<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 18:41; Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 4:13"> When Jesus saw him lie, and knew that he had been now a long time in that case, he saith unto him,<span className="woj">Wilt thou be made whole?</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 5:24; Matthew 9:6; Mark 2:11"> Jesus saith unto him,<span className="woj">Rise, take up thy bed, and walk.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:30; Luke 24:31; John 8:59"> And he that was healed wist not who it was: for Jesus had conveyed himself away, a multitude being in that place.</span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:11; Deuteronomy 32:39; John 11:25"> <span className="woj">For as the Father raiseth up the dead, and quickeneth them; even so the Son quickeneth whom he will.</span></span>
