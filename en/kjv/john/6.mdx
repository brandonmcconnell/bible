<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 6:23; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Matthew 15:36"> And Jesus took the loaves; and when he had given thanks, he distributed to the disciples, and the disciples to them that were set down; and likewise of the fishes as much as they would.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 1:53; Nehemiah 9:25; Nehemiah 8:10"> When they were filled, he said unto his disciples,<span className="woj">Gather up the fragments that remain, that nothing be lost.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 14:27–31; Isaiah 43:1–2; Isaiah 44:8"> But he saith unto them,<span className="woj">It is I; be not afraid.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:11–12"> (Howbeit there came other boats from Tiberias nigh unto the place where they did eat bread, after that the Lord had given thanks:)</span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 4:3–4; Philippians 3:19; Philippians 2:21"> Jesus answered them and said,<span className="woj">Verily, verily, I say unto you, Ye seek me, not because ye saw the miracles, but because ye did eat of the loaves, and were filled.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 3:2; Isaiah 55:2; John 6:54"> <span className="woj">Labour not for the meat which perisheth, but for that meat which endureth unto everlasting life, which the Son of man shall give unto you: for him hath God the Father sealed.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:15; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:24–25"> Our fathers did eat manna in the desert; as it is written, He gave them bread from heaven to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 15:1; John 6:58; Exodus 16:4"> Then Jesus said unto them,<span className="woj">Verily, verily, I say unto you, Moses gave you not that bread from heaven; but my Father giveth you the true bread from heaven.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 8:42; John 17:8; John 3:13"> <span className="woj">For the bread of God is he which cometh down from heaven, and giveth life unto the world.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8–9; Luke 16:31; John 12:37"> <span className="woj">But I said unto you, That ye also have seen me, and believe not.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:36; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 5:9–10"> <span className="woj">Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that believeth on me hath everlasting life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 6:51; 1 Corinthians 11:24–25; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> <span className="woj">I am that bread of life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3–5; Jude 1:5; Zechariah 1:5"> <span className="woj">Your fathers did eat manna in the wilderness, and are dead.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 6:33; John 6:51; Romans 8:10"> <span className="woj">This is the bread which cometh down from heaven, that a man may eat thereof, and not die.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 22:19; John 6:33; Hebrews 10:5–12"> <span className="woj">I am the living bread which came down from heaven: if any man eat of this bread, he shall live for ever: and the bread that I will give is my flesh, which I will give for the life of the world.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="John 2:24–25; John 6:64; John 21:17"> When Jesus knew in himself that his disciples murmured at it, he said unto them,<span className="woj">Doth this offend you?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 16:19; John 3:13; John 17:4–5"> <span className="woj">What and if ye shall see the Son of man ascend up where he was before?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Galatians 5:25; Hebrews 4:12; Psalms 119:93"> <span className="woj">It is the spirit that quickeneth; the flesh profiteth nothing: the words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="John 10:26; 2 Timothy 2:19; Hebrews 4:13"> <span className="woj">But there are some of you that believe not.</span>For Jesus knew from the beginning who they were that believed not, and who should betray him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="John 6:44–45; John 6:37; John 3:27"> And he said,<span className="woj">Therefore said I unto you, that no man can come unto me, except it were given unto him of my Father.</span></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Ruth 1:11–18; John 6:70; Luke 14:25–33"> Then said Jesus unto the twelve,<span className="woj">Will ye also go away?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Psalms 73:25; John 5:24; 1 John 5:11–13"> Then Simon Peter answered him, Lord, to whom shall we go? thou hast the words of eternal life.</span>
