<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 4:34; Acts 20:24; 2 Timothy 4:7"> <span className="woj">I have glorified thee on the earth: I have finished the work which thou gavest me to do.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 7:16–17; John 16:15; John 14:7–10"> <span className="woj">Now they have known that all things whatsoever thou hast given me are of thee.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; John 17:2; John 17:6"> <span className="woj">I pray for them: I pray not for the world, but for them which thou hast given me; for they are thine.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 10:30; 1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 5:8–14"> <span className="woj">And all mine are thine, and thine are mine; and I am glorified in them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 6:9; Proverbs 18:10; John 17:12–18"> <span className="woj">And now I am no more in the world, but these are in the world, and I come to thee. Holy Father, keep through thine own name those whom thou hast given me, that they may be one, as we are.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 109:6–19; John 10:27–28; John 18:9"> <span className="woj">While I was with them in the world, I kept them in thy name: those that thou gavest me I have kept, and none of them is lost, but the son of perdition; that the scripture might be fulfilled.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Galatians 5:22; John 15:11"> <span className="woj">And now come I to thee; and these things I speak in the world, that they might have my joy fulfilled in themselves.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:16; John 15:18–21; 1 John 5:19–20"> <span className="woj">I have given them thy word; and the world hath hated them, because they are not of the world, even as I am not of the world.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 121:7; 1 John 5:18; Matthew 6:13"> <span className="woj">I pray not that thou shouldest take them out of the world, but that thou shouldest keep them from the evil.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:3; Psalms 119:160; Ephesians 5:26"> <span className="woj">Sanctify them through thy truth: thy word is truth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; John 20:21; John 17:3"> <span className="woj">As thou hast sent me into the world, even so have I also sent them into the world.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Titus 2:14; Hebrews 2:11; John 17:17"> <span className="woj">And for their sakes I sanctify myself, that they also might be sanctified through the truth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 17:6–11; Acts 2:41; Acts 4:4"> <span className="woj">Neither pray I for these alone, but for them also which shall believe on me through their word;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:12; Colossians 3:11–14; John 17:11"> <span className="woj">That they all may be one; as thou, Father, art in me, and I in thee, that they also may be one in us: that the world may believe that thou hast sent me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:18; John 14:20; Mark 16:17–20"> <span className="woj">And the glory which thou gavest me I have given them; that they may be one, even as we are one:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 17:21; John 16:27; 1 John 3:1"> <span className="woj">I in them, and thou in me, that they may be made perfect in one; and that the world may know that thou hast sent me, and hast loved them, as thou hast loved me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 17:5; John 12:26; John 14:3"> <span className="woj">Father, I will that they also, whom thou hast given me, be with me where I am; that they may behold my glory, which thou hast given me: for thou lovedst me before the foundation of the world.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 17:11; John 7:29; John 17:8"> <span className="woj">O righteous Father, the world hath not known thee: but I have known thee, and these have known that thou hast sent me.</span></span>
