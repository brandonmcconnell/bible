<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 14:3–8; John 11:1; Luke 24:50"> Then Jesus six days before the passover came to Bethany, where Lazarus was which had been dead, whom he raised from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 22:27; Luke 10:38–42; Revelation 3:20"> There they made him a supper; and Martha served: but Lazarus was one of them that sat at the table with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; John 11:2; Luke 10:38–39"> Then took Mary a pound of ointment of spikenard, very costly, and anointed the feet of Jesus, and wiped his feet with her hair: and the house was filled with the odour of the ointment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:4; 1 Samuel 17:28–29; John 13:2"> Then saith one of his disciples, Judas Iscariot, Simon’s son, which should betray him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:22; Luke 12:33; Mark 14:4–5"> Why was not this ointment sold for three hundred pence, and given to the poor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 13:29; Psalms 50:16–20; James 2:6"> This he said, not that he cared for the poor; but because he was a thief, and had the bag, and bare what was put therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:12; Mark 14:6; Matthew 27:57–60"> Then said Jesus,<span className="woj">Let her alone: against the day of my burying hath she kept this.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Mark 14:7; Matthew 26:11"> <span className="woj">For the poor always ye have with you; but me ye have not always.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:14; John 11:43–45; Acts 3:10–11"> Much people of the Jews therefore knew that he was there: and they came not for Jesus’ sake only, but that they might see Lazarus also, whom he had raised from the dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Matthew 2:16; John 11:47–53"> But the chief priests consulted that they might put Lazarus also to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:45; John 12:18; Acts 13:45"> Because that by reason of him many of the Jews went away, and believed on Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:7–10; Matthew 21:4–9; Luke 19:35–38"> On the next day much people that were come to the feast, when they heard that Jesus was coming to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 118:25–26; Revelation 15:3; Matthew 21:9–11"> Took branches of palm trees, and went forth to meet him, and cried, Hosanna: Blessed is the King of Israel that cometh in the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Mark 11:1–7; Matthew 21:1–7"> And Jesus, when he had found a young ass, sat thereon; as it is written,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Isaiah 62:11; Zephaniah 3:16–17"> Fear not, daughter of Sion: behold, thy King cometh, sitting on an ass’s colt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 2:22; John 14:26; John 7:39"> These things understood not his disciples at the first: but when Jesus was glorified, then remembered they that these things were written of him, and that they had done these things unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 1:2; 1 John 5:9–12; John 19:35"> The people therefore that was with him when he called Lazarus out of his grave, and raised him from the dead, bare record.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 12:11"> For this cause the people also met him, for that they heard that he had done this miracle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:6; Psalms 22:27; 1 John 2:2"> The Pharisees therefore said among themselves, Perceive ye how ye prevail nothing? behold, the world is gone after him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:35; Isaiah 11:10; Acts 17:4"> And there were certain Greeks among them that came up to worship at the feast:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Luke 19:2–4; John 6:40"> The same came therefore to Philip, which was of Bethsaida of Galilee, and desired him, saying, Sir, we would see Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 10:13–14; Luke 9:49–50; John 1:40–41"> Philip cometh and telleth Andrew: and again Andrew and Philip tell Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:31–32; John 17:1–5; Isaiah 60:9"> And Jesus answered them, saying,<span className="woj">The hour is come, that the Son of man should be glorified.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36–38; Isaiah 53:10–12; Hebrews 2:9–10"> <span className="woj">Verily, verily, I say unto you, Except a corn of wheat fall into the ground and die, it abideth alone: but if it die, it bringeth forth much fruit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 10:39; Luke 9:23–24; Mark 8:35"> <span className="woj">He that loveth his life shall lose it; and he that hateth his life in this world shall keep it unto life eternal.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Samuel 2:30"> <span className="woj">If any man serve me, let him follow me; and where I am, there shall also my servant be: if any man serve me, him will my Father honour.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 12:23; Hebrews 5:7; Matthew 26:42"> <span className="woj">Now is my soul troubled; and what shall I say? Father, save me from this hour: but for this cause came I unto this hour.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 13:31–32; Matthew 26:42; Revelation 5:9–14"> <span className="woj">Father, glorify thy name.</span>Then came there a voice from heaven, saying, I have both glorified it, and will glorify it again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:2–5; Revelation 6:1; Revelation 14:2"> The people therefore, that stood by, and heard it, said that it thundered: others said, An angel spake to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:42; John 11:15; John 5:34"> Jesus answered and said,<span className="woj">This voice came not because of me, but for your sakes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 John 5:19; 1 John 3:8"> <span className="woj">Now is the judgment of this world: now shall the prince of this world be cast out.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:14; John 8:28; 1 Peter 2:24"> <span className="woj">And I, if I be lifted up from the earth, will draw all men unto me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 18:32; John 21:19"> This he said, signifying what death he should die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Isaiah 9:7; Daniel 7:14"> The people answered him, We have heard out of the law that Christ abideth for ever: and how sayest thou, The Son of man must be lifted up? who is this Son of man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 12:46; John 16:16; Ephesians 5:8"> Then Jesus said unto them,<span className="woj">Yet a little while is the light with you. Walk while ye have the light, lest darkness come upon you: for he that walketh in darkness knoweth not whither he goeth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; John 8:59; Acts 13:47–48"> <span className="woj">While ye have light, believe in the light, that ye may be the children of light.</span>These things spake Jesus, and departed, and did hide himself from them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 16:31; John 1:11; John 15:24"> But though he had done so many miracles before them, yet they believed not on him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 10:16; Romans 10:20"> That the saying of Esaias the prophet might be fulfilled, which he spake, Lord, who hath believed our report? and to whom hath the arm of the Lord been revealed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 10:38; John 5:44; 2 Peter 2:14"> Therefore they could not believe, because that Esaias said again,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Matthew 13:13–15; Luke 8:10"> He hath blinded their eyes, and hardened their heart; that they should not see with their eyes, nor understand with their heart, and be converted, and I should heal them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 6:1–5; Isaiah 6:9–10; John 1:14"> These things said Esaias, when he saw his glory, and spake of him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 9:22; John 7:13; Isaiah 51:7"> Nevertheless among the chief rulers also many believed on him; but because of the Pharisees they did not confess him, lest they should be put out of the synagogue:</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 5:44; Matthew 23:5–7; John 5:41"> For they loved the praise of men more than the praise of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:20; John 5:24"> Jesus cried and said,<span className="woj">He that believeth on me, believeth not on me, but on him that sent me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 14:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Colossians 1:15"> <span className="woj">And he that seeth me seeth him that sent me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 8:12; Psalms 36:9; John 9:5"> <span className="woj">I am come a light into the world, that whosoever believeth on me should not abide in darkness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:17; John 8:15–16; John 12:48"> <span className="woj">And if any man hear my words, and believe not, I judge him not: for I came not to judge the world, but to save the world.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Hebrews 9:27–28; Romans 2:16"> <span className="woj">He that rejecteth me, and receiveth not my words, hath one that judgeth him: the word that I have spoken, the same shall judge him in the last day.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 14:10; John 8:26; John 5:30"> <span className="woj">For I have not spoken of myself; but the Father which sent me, he gave me a commandment, what I should say, and what I should speak.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 17:3; John 6:68; 1 John 5:20"> <span className="woj">And I know that his commandment is life everlasting: whatsoever I speak therefore, even as the Father said unto me, so I speak.</span></span>
</div>
