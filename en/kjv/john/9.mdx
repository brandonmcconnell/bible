<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And as Jesus passed by, he saw a man which was blind from his birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 13:2; John 9:34; Acts 28:4"> And his disciples asked him, saying, Master, who did sin, this man, or his parents, that he was born blind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:40; Ecclesiastes 9:1–2"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">Neither hath this man sinned, nor his parents: but that the works of God should be made manifest in him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:35; Ephesians 5:16; John 4:34"> <span className="woj">I must work the works of him that sent me, while it is day: the night cometh, when no man can work.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; Acts 13:47"> <span className="woj">As long as I am in the world, I am the light of the world.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 8:23; Mark 7:33; Revelation 3:18"> When he had thus spoken, he spat on the ground, and made clay of the spittle, and he anointed the eyes of the blind man with the clay,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5; John 9:11; John 11:37"> And said unto him,<span className="woj">Go, wash in the pool of Siloam,</span>(which is by interpretation, Sent.) He went his way therefore, and washed, and came seeing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; 1 Samuel 21:11; Luke 18:35"> The neighbours therefore, and they which before had seen him that he was blind, said, Is not this he that sat and begged?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Some said, This is he: others said, He is like him: but he said, I am he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 9:26; John 9:21; Ecclesiastes 11:5"> Therefore said they unto him, How were thine eyes opened?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 9:27; Jeremiah 36:17"> He answered and said, A man that is called Jesus made clay, and anointed mine eyes, and said unto me, Go to the pool of Siloam, and wash: and I went and washed, and I received sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 5:11–13; Exodus 2:18–20; John 7:11"> Then said they unto him, Where is he? He said, I know not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:57; John 11:46–47; John 12:42"> They brought to the Pharisees him that aforetime was blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:9; Luke 13:10–17; Matthew 12:1–14"> And it was the sabbath day when Jesus made the clay, and opened his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:26–27; John 9:10–11"> Then again the Pharisees also asked him how he had received his sight. He said unto them, He put clay upon mine eyes, and I washed, and do see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:43; John 10:19; Matthew 12:2"> Therefore said some of the Pharisees, This man is not of God, because he keepeth not the sabbath day. Others said, How can a man that is a sinner do such miracles? And there was a division among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:19; John 6:14; Acts 3:22–26"> They say unto the blind man again, What sayest thou of him, that he hath opened thine eyes? He said, He is a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 3:15–19; John 5:44; Isaiah 26:11"> But the Jews did not believe concerning him, that he had been blind, and received his sight, until they called the parents of him that had received his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:8–9; Acts 4:14; Acts 3:10"> And they asked them, saying, Is this your son, who ye say was born blind? how then doth he now see?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> His parents answered them and said, We know that this is our son, and that he was born blind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But by what means he now seeth, we know not; or who hath opened his eyes, we know not: he is of age; ask him: he shall speak for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 7:13; John 16:2; Luke 6:22"> These words spake his parents, because they feared the Jews: for the Jews had agreed already, that if any man did confess that he was Christ, he should be put out of the synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 9:21"> Therefore said his parents, He is of age; ask him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 7:19; John 9:16; John 8:49"> Then again called they the man that was blind, and said unto him, Give God the praise: we know that this man is a sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 5:10; John 9:30; John 5:11"> He answered and said, Whether he be a sinner or no, I know not: one thing I know, that, whereas I was blind, now I see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then said they to him again, What did he to thee? how opened he thine eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:67; John 9:10–15"> He answered them, I have told you already, and ye did not hear: wherefore would ye hear it again? will ye also be his disciples?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 2:17; John 7:19; Matthew 27:39"> Then they reviled him, and said, Thou art his disciple; but we are Moses’ disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:14; Numbers 12:2–7; Acts 7:35"> We know that God spake unto Moses: as for this fellow, we know not from whence he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; Psalms 119:18; Isaiah 29:18"> The man answered and said unto them, Why herein is a marvellous thing, that ye know not from whence he is, and yet he hath opened mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 28:9"> Now we know that God heareth not sinners: but if any man be a worshipper of God, and doeth his will, him he heareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Revelation 16:18; Job 20:4"> Since the world began was it not heard that any man opened the eyes of one that was born blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 9:16; John 3:2; Acts 5:38–39"> If this man were not of God, he could do nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 9:22; John 9:2; Isaiah 66:5"> They answered and said unto him, Thou wast altogether born in sins, and dost thou teach us? And they cast him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 20:31; 1 John 5:13; John 10:36"> Jesus heard that they had cast him out; and when he had found him, he said unto him,<span className="woj">Dost thou believe on the Son of God?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Song of Solomon 5:9; John 1:38"> He answered and said, Who is he, Lord, that I might believe on him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 4:26; Psalms 25:8–9; John 14:21–23"> And Jesus said unto him,<span className="woj">Thou hast both seen him, and it is he that talketh with thee.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; Matthew 28:9; Luke 24:52"> And he said, Lord, I believe. And he worshipped him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:19; John 12:46; Acts 26:18"> And Jesus said,<span className="woj">For judgment I am come into this world, that they which see not might see; and that they which see might be made blind.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Matthew 15:12–14; John 7:47–52"> And some of the Pharisees which were with him heard these words, and said unto him, Are we blind also?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; John 15:22–24; 1 John 1:8–10"> Jesus said unto them,<span className="woj">If ye were blind, ye should have no sin: but now ye say, We see; therefore your sin remaineth.</span></span>
</div>
