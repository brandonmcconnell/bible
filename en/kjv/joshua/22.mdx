<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 32:18–33; Deuteronomy 29:7–8"> Then Joshua called the Reubenites, and the Gadites, and the half tribe of Manasseh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 1:12–18; Deuteronomy 3:16–20; Numbers 32:20–29"> And said unto them, Ye have kept all that Moses the servant of the LORD commanded you, and have obeyed my voice in all that I commanded you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 1:23–27"> Ye have not left your brethren these many days unto this day, but have kept the charge of the commandment of the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 13:8; Numbers 32:18; Deuteronomy 3:20"> And now the LORD your God hath given rest unto your brethren, as he promised them: therefore now return ye, and get you unto your tents, and unto the land of your possession, which Moses the servant of the LORD gave you on the other side Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:37; Exodus 20:6; Romans 8:28"> But take diligent heed to do the commandment and the law, which Moses the servant of the LORD charged you, to love the LORD your God, and to walk in all his ways, and to keep his commandments, and to cleave unto him, and to serve him with all your heart and with all your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 14:13; Luke 24:50; Genesis 47:7"> So Joshua blessed them, and sent them away: and they went unto their tents.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 17:1–12; Numbers 32:33; Joshua 13:29–31"> Now to the one half of the tribe of Manasseh Moses had given possession in Bashan: but unto the other half thereof gave Joshua among their brethren on this side Jordan westward. And when Joshua sent them away also unto their tents, then he blessed them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 31:27; Deuteronomy 8:9–14; Proverbs 3:16"> And he spake unto them, saying, Return with much riches unto your tents, and with very much cattle, with silver, and with gold, and with brass, and with iron, and with very much raiment: divide the spoil of your enemies with your brethren.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 32:29; Numbers 32:26; Numbers 32:1"> And the children of Reuben and the children of Gad and the half tribe of Manasseh returned, and departed from the children of Israel out of Shiloh, which is in the land of Canaan, to go unto the country of Gilead, to the land of their possession, whereof they were possessed, according to the word of the LORD by the hand of Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 24:26–27; Genesis 31:46–52; Joshua 4:5–9"> And when they came unto the borders of Jordan, that are in the land of Canaan, the children of Reuben and the children of Gad and the half tribe of Manasseh built there an altar by Jordan, a great altar to see to.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:12–14; Leviticus 17:8–9; Joshua 2:7"> And the children of Israel heard say, Behold, the children of Reuben and the children of Gad and the half tribe of Manasseh have built an altar over against the land of Canaan, in the borders of Jordan, at the passage of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 20:1–11; Deuteronomy 13:15; Galatians 4:17–18"> And when the children of Israel heard of it, the whole congregation of the children of Israel gathered themselves together at Shiloh, to go up to war against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 25:7; Psalms 106:30–31; Exodus 6:25"> And the children of Israel sent unto the children of Reuben, and to the children of Gad, and to the half tribe of Manasseh, into the land of Gilead, Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:4; Exodus 18:25"> And with him ten princes, of each chief house a prince throughout all the tribes of Israel; and each one was an head of the house of their fathers among the thousands of Israel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="22.14 chief…: Heb. house of the father">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:3; 1 Samuel 15:23; Deuteronomy 30:17"> And they came unto the children of Reuben, and to the children of Gad, and to the half tribe of Manasseh, unto the land of Gilead, and they spake with them, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:13–14"> Thus saith the whole congregation of the LORD, What trespass is this that ye have committed against the God of Israel, to turn away this day from following the LORD, in that ye have builded you an altar, that ye might rebel this day against the LORD?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–18; Psalms 106:28–29; Deuteronomy 4:3–4"> Is the iniquity of Peor too little for us, from which we are not cleansed until this day, although there was a plague in the congregation of the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Joshua 22:16; Joshua 7:11"> But that ye must turn away this day from following the LORD? and it will be, seeing ye rebel to day against the LORD, that to morrow he will be wroth with the whole congregation of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; 2 Chronicles 11:16–17; Acts 10:14–15"> Notwithstanding, if the land of your possession be unclean, then pass ye over unto the land of the possession of the LORD, wherein the LORD’s tabernacle dwelleth, and take possession among us: but rebel not against the LORD, nor rebel against us, in building you an altar beside the altar of the LORD our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 7:1; Joshua 7:5; Joshua 7:18"> Did not Achan the son of Zerah commit a trespass in the accursed thing, and wrath fell on all the congregation of Israel? and that man perished not alone in his iniquity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 16:1; 1 Peter 3:15"> Then the children of Reuben and the children of Gad and the half tribe of Manasseh answered, and said unto the heads of the thousands of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 8:39; Psalms 44:21; Deuteronomy 10:17"> The LORD God of gods, the LORD God of gods, he knoweth, and Israel he shall know; if it be in rebellion, or if in transgression against the LORD, (save us not this day,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:16; Deuteronomy 18:19; Ezekiel 3:18"> That we have built us an altar to turn from following the LORD, or if to offer thereon burnt offering or meat offering, or if to offer peace offerings thereon, let the LORD himself require it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 4:6; Deuteronomy 6:20; Genesis 30:33"> And if we have not rather done it for fear of this thing, saying, In time to come your children might speak unto our children, saying, What have ye to do with the LORD God of Israel?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="22.24 In time…: Heb. To morrow">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 12:16; Ezra 4:2–3; 1 Samuel 26:19"> For the LORD hath made Jordan a border between us and you, ye children of Reuben and children of Gad; ye have no part in the LORD: so shall your children make our children cease from fearing the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Therefore we said, Let us now prepare to build us an altar, not for burnt offering, nor for sacrifice:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 24:27; Genesis 31:48; Deuteronomy 12:17–18"> But that it may be a witness between us, and you, and our generations after us, that we might do the service of the LORD before him with our burnt offerings, and with our sacrifices, and with our peace offerings; that your children may not say to our children in time to come, Ye have no part in the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Ezekiel 43:10–11; Hebrews 8:5"> Therefore said we, that it shall be, when they should so say to us or to our generations in time to come, that we may say again, Behold the pattern of the altar of the LORD, which our fathers made, not for burnt offerings, nor for sacrifices; but it is a witness between us and you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 24:16; Deuteronomy 12:13–14; Joshua 22:23"> God forbid that we should rebel against the LORD, and turn this day from following the LORD, to build an altar for burnt offerings, for meat offerings, or for sacrifices, beside the altar of the LORD our God that is before his tabernacle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 22:33; Esther 1:21; Proverbs 15:1"> And when Phinehas the priest, and the princes of the congregation and heads of the thousands of Israel which were with him, heard the words that the children of Reuben and the children of Gad and the children of Manasseh spake, it pleased them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="22.30 pleased…: Heb. was good in their eyes">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Leviticus 26:11–12; Isaiah 12:6"> And Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest said unto the children of Reuben, and to the children of Gad, and to the children of Manasseh, This day we perceive that the LORD is among us, because ye have not committed this trespass against the LORD: now ye have delivered the children of Israel out of the hand of the LORD.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="22.31 now: Heb. then">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 22:12–14; Proverbs 25:13"> And Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest, and the princes, returned from the children of Reuben, and from the children of Gad, out of the land of Gilead, unto the land of Canaan, to the children of Israel, and brought them word again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:20; Luke 2:28; Daniel 2:19"> And the thing pleased the children of Israel; and the children of Israel blessed God, and did not intend to go up against them in battle, to destroy the land wherein the children of Reuben and Gad dwelt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 22:27; Matthew 4:10; Isaiah 43:10"> And the children of Reuben and the children of Gad called the altar Ed: for it shall be a witness between us that the LORD is God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="22.34 Ed: that is, A witness">e</a></sup></span>
