<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 139:7–12; Genesis 4:16; Joshua 19:46"> But Jonah rose up to flee unto Tarshish from the presence of the LORD, and went down to Joppa; and he found a ship going to Tarshish: so he paid the fare thereof, and went down into it, to go with them unto Tarshish from the presence of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 107:23–31; Matthew 8:24–27; Exodus 10:19"> But the LORD sent out a great wind into the sea, and there was a mighty tempest in the sea, so that the ship was like to be broken.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.4 sent out: Heb. cast forth">b</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.4 was like…: Heb. thought to be broken">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Acts 27:18–19; 1 Kings 18:26"> Then the mariners were afraid, and cried every man unto his god, and cast forth the wares that were in the ship into the sea, to lighten it of them. But Jonah was gone down into the sides of the ship; and he lay, and was fast asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jonah 3:9; Psalms 107:28–29; Ephesians 5:14"> So the shipmaster came to him, and said unto him, What meanest thou, O sleeper? arise, call upon thy God, if so be that God will think upon us, that we perish not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:33; Joshua 7:13–18; 1 Samuel 10:20–21"> And they said every one to his fellow, Come, and let us cast lots, that we may know for whose cause this evil is upon us. So they cast lots, and the lot fell upon Jonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 47:3; 1 Samuel 30:13; Joshua 7:19"> Then said they unto him, Tell us, we pray thee, for whose cause this evil is upon us; What is thine occupation? and whence comest thou? what is thy country? and of what people art thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 146:5–6; Psalms 95:5–6; Nehemiah 9:6"> And he said unto them, I am an Hebrew; and I fear the LORD, the God of heaven, which hath made the sea and the dry land.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.9 the LORD: or, JEHOVAH">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jonah 1:3; Job 27:22; Joshua 7:25"> Then were the men exceedingly afraid, and said unto him, Why hast thou done this? For the men knew that he fled from the presence of the LORD, because he had told them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.10 exceedingly…: Heb. with great fear">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:1–6; Micah 6:6–7; 1 Samuel 6:2–3"> Then said they unto him, What shall we do unto thee, that the sea may be calm unto us? for the sea wrought, and was tempestuous.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.11 may…: Heb. may be silent from us">f</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="1.11 wrought…: or, grew more and more tempestuous: Heb. went">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:17; 1 Chronicles 21:17; Joshua 7:12"> And he said unto them, Take me up, and cast me forth into the sea; so shall the sea be calm unto you: for I know that for my sake this great tempest is upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; Job 34:29"> Nevertheless the men rowed hard to bring it to the land; but they could not: for the sea wrought, and was tempestuous against them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="1.13 rowed: Heb. digged">h</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="1.13 wrought…: or, grew more and more tempestuous: Heb. went">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 115:3; Psalms 135:6; Ephesians 1:11"> Wherefore they cried unto the LORD, and said, We beseech thee, O LORD, we beseech thee, let us not perish for this man’s life, and lay not upon us innocent blood: for thou, O LORD, hast done as it pleased thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 8:24; Psalms 93:3–4; Psalms 107:29"> So they took up Jonah, and cast him forth into the sea: and the sea ceased from her raging.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="1.15 ceased: Heb. stood">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 50:14; Genesis 8:20; Daniel 6:26"> Then the men feared the LORD exceedingly, and offered a sacrifice unto the LORD, and made vows.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="1.16 offered…: Heb. sacrifice unto the LORD, and vowed vows">k</a></sup></span>
