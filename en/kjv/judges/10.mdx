</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:13; 1 Chronicles 28:9; Judges 2:12"> Yet ye have forsaken me, and served other gods: wherefore I will deliver you no more.</span>
