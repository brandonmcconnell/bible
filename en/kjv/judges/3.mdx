<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 2:21–22; Deuteronomy 7:22; Proverbs 17:3"> Now these are the nations which the LORD left, to prove Israel by them, even as many of Israel as had not known all the wars of Canaan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 3:5; Genesis 3:7; 1 Corinthians 9:26–27"> Only that the generations of the children of Israel might know, to teach them war, at the least such as before knew nothing thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 13:3; Joshua 13:5; Judges 10:7"> Namely, five lords of the Philistines, and all the Canaanites, and the Sidonians, and the Hivites that dwelt in mount Lebanon, from mount Baal-hermon unto the entering in of Hamath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 3:1; Judges 2:22; Exodus 15:25"> And they were to prove Israel by them, to know whether they would hearken unto the commandments of the LORD, which he commanded their fathers by the hand of Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 106:34–38; Genesis 10:15–18; Exodus 3:8"> And the children of Israel dwelt among the Canaanites, Hittites, and Amorites, and Perizzites, and Hivites, and Jebusites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3–4; Exodus 34:16; 1 Kings 11:1–5"> And they took their daughters to be their wives, and gave their daughters to their sons, and served their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 34:13; 2 Kings 23:6; 1 Kings 18:19"> And the children of Israel did evil in the sight of the LORD, and forgat the LORD their God, and served Baalim and the groves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Habakkuk 3:7; Judges 4:9"> Therefore the anger of the LORD was hot against Israel, and he sold them into the hand of Chushan-rishathaim king of Mesopotamia: and the children of Israel served Chushan-rishathaim eight years.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.8 Mesopotamia: Heb. Aram-naharaim">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 3:15; Judges 1:13; Psalms 22:5"> And when the children of Israel cried unto the LORD, the LORD raised up a deliverer to the children of Israel, who delivered them, even Othniel the son of Kenaz, Caleb’s younger brother.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.9 deliverer: Heb. saviour">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 13:25; 1 Samuel 11:6; Judges 14:19"> And the Spirit of the LORD came upon him, and he judged Israel, and went out to war: and the LORD delivered Chushan-rishathaim king of Mesopotamia into his hand; and his hand prevailed against Chushan-rishathaim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.10 came: Heb. was">c</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.10 Mesopotamia: Heb. Aram">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Judges 8:28; Joshua 11:23"> And the land had rest forty years. And Othniel the son of Kenaz died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:9; Judges 2:19; Judges 2:14"> And the children of Israel did evil again in the sight of the LORD: and the LORD strengthened Eglon the king of Moab against Israel, because they had done evil in the sight of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Deuteronomy 34:3; Judges 5:14"> And he gathered unto him the children of Ammon and Amalek, and went and smote Israel, and possessed the city of palm trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:47–48; Deuteronomy 28:40; Leviticus 26:23–25"> So the children of Israel served Eglon the king of Moab eighteen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 3:9; Psalms 78:34; Isaiah 36:16"> But when the children of Israel cried unto the LORD, the LORD raised them up a deliverer, Ehud the son of Gera, a Benjamite, a man lefthanded: and by him the children of Israel sent a present unto Eglon the king of Moab.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.15 a Benjamite: or, the son of Jemini">e</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="3.15 lefthanded: Heb. shut of his right hand">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Song of Solomon 3:8; Revelation 2:12; Psalms 149:6"> But Ehud made him a dagger which had two edges, of a cubit length; and he did gird it under his raiment upon his right thigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:20; Job 15:27; Jeremiah 50:11"> And he brought the present unto Eglon king of Moab: and Eglon was a very fat man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And when he had made an end to offer the present, he sent away the people that bare the present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 4:20; Judges 3:20; Acts 23:18–19"> But he himself turned again from the quarries that were by Gilgal, and said, I have a secret errand unto thee, O king: who said, Keep silence. And all that stood by him went out from him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="3.19 quarries: or, graven images">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:7; Amos 3:15; Psalms 29:1"> And Ehud came unto him; and he was sitting in a summer parlour, which he had for himself alone. And Ehud said, I have a message from God unto thee. And he arose out of his seat.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="3.20 a summer…: Heb. a parlour of cooling">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 13:3; Job 20:25; 1 Samuel 15:33"> And Ehud put forth his left hand, and took the dagger from his right thigh, and thrust it into his belly:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the haft also went in after the blade; and the fat closed upon the blade, so that he could not draw the dagger out of his belly; and the dirt came out.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="3.22 the dirt…: or, it came out at the buttocks">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Then Ehud went forth through the porch, and shut the doors of the parlour upon him, and locked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:3"> When he was gone out, his servants came; and when they saw that, behold, the doors of the parlour were locked, they said, Surely he covereth his feet in his summer chamber.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="3.24 covereth…: or, doeth his easement">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And they tarried till they were ashamed: and, behold, he opened not the doors of the parlour; therefore they took a key, and opened them: and, behold, their lord was fallen down dead on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Ehud escaped while they tarried, and passed beyond the quarries, and escaped unto Seirath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 6:34; 1 Samuel 13:3; Judges 5:14"> And it came to pass, when he was come, that he blew a trumpet in the mountain of Ephraim, and the children of Israel went down with him from the mount, and he before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 12:5; Judges 7:9; Judges 7:15"> And he said unto them, Follow after me: for the LORD hath delivered your enemies the Moabites into your hand. And they went down after him, and took the fords of Jordan toward Moab, and suffered not a man to pass over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Judges 3:17; Psalms 17:10"> And they slew of Moab at that time about ten thousand men, all lusty, and all men of valour; and there escaped not a man.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="3.29 lusty: Heb. fat">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 3:11; Judges 5:31"> So Moab was subdued that day under the hand of Israel. And the land had rest fourscore years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 5:6; 1 Corinthians 1:17; 1 Samuel 17:50"> And after him was Shamgar the son of Anath, which slew of the Philistines six hundred men with an ox goad: and he also delivered Israel.</span>
</div>
