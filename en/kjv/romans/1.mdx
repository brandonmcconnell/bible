<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Romans 15:16; 1 Corinthians 1:1"> Paul, a servant of Jesus Christ, called to be an apostle, separated unto the gospel of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:2; Luke 1:70; Romans 3:21"> (Which he had promised afore by his prophets in the holy scriptures,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 1:1; Galatians 4:4; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> Concerning his Son Jesus Christ our Lord, which was made of the seed of David according to the flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 9:14; Hebrews 5:5–6; Ephesians 1:19–23"> And declared to be the Son of God with power, according to the spirit of holiness, by the resurrection from the dead:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Romans 16:26; Acts 6:7"> By whom we have received grace and apostleship, for obedience to the faith among all nations, for his name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 17:14; 2 Peter 1:10"> Among whom are ye also the called of Jesus Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; Jude 1:1–2; Ephesians 1:2"> To all that be in Rome, beloved of God, called to be saints: Grace to you and peace from God our Father, and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 16:19; 1 Corinthians 1:4; 1 Thessalonians 1:8–9"> First, I thank my God through Jesus Christ for you all, that your faith is spoken of throughout the whole world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; 2 Timothy 1:3; Ephesians 1:16–19"> For God is my witness, whom I serve with my spirit in the gospel of his Son, that without ceasing I make mention of you always in my prayers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 15:30–32; Acts 18:21; Philemon 1:22"> Making request, if by any means now at length I might have a prosperous journey by the will of God to come unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 1:12; Romans 16:25; Romans 15:29"> For I long to see you, that I may impart unto you some spiritual gift, to the end ye may be established;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:7–10; 3 John 1:3–4; 2 Corinthians 7:13"> That is, that I may be comforted together with you by the mutual faith both of you and me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 19:21; Philippians 4:17; 2 Timothy 4:17"> Now I would not have you ignorant, brethren, that oftentimes I purposed to come unto you, (but was let hitherto,) that I might have some fruit among you also, even as among other Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:16–23; Acts 9:15; Colossians 3:11"> I am debtor both to the Greeks, and to the Barbarians; both to the wise, and to the unwise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:20; Isaiah 6:8; 1 Corinthians 9:17"> So, as much as in me is, I am ready to preach the gospel to you that are at Rome also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 8:38; 2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Peter 4:16"> For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ: for it is the power of God unto salvation to every one that believeth; to the Jew first, and also to the Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:4; Galatians 3:11; Hebrews 10:38"> For therein is the righteousness of God revealed from faith to faith: as it is written, The just shall live by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:6; 1 Timothy 4:1–2; Romans 1:32"> For the wrath of God is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of men, who hold the truth in unrighteousness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 14:16–17; Psalms 19:1–6; Romans 1:20"> Because that which may be known of God is manifest in them; for God hath shewed it unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:19; Isaiah 40:26; Psalms 19:1–6"> For the invisible things of him from the creation of the world are clearly seen, being understood by the things that are made, even his eternal power and Godhead; so that they are without excuse:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 4:17–18; 2 Kings 17:15; Jeremiah 2:5"> Because that, when they knew God, they glorified him not as God, neither were thankful; but became vain in their imaginations, and their foolish heart was darkened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:19–21; Romans 11:25; Proverbs 26:12"> Professing themselves to be wise, they became fools,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 106:20; Jeremiah 2:11; Acts 17:29"> And changed the glory of the uncorruptible God into an image made like to corruptible man, and to birds, and fourfooted beasts, and creeping things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 81:11–12; Acts 7:42; Acts 14:16"> Wherefore God also gave them up to uncleanness through the lusts of their own hearts, to dishonour their own bodies between themselves:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18; Jeremiah 13:25; 1 John 2:15–16"> Who changed the truth of God into a lie, and worshipped and served the creature more than the Creator, who is blessed for ever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jude 1:7; 1 Timothy 1:10; Romans 1:24"> For this cause God gave them up unto vile affections: for even their women did change the natural use into that which is against nature:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 20:13; Leviticus 18:22; Romans 1:23–24"> And likewise also the men, leaving the natural use of the woman, burned in their lust one toward another; men with men working that which is unseemly, and receiving in themselves that recompence of their error which was meet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Romans 1:21; Titus 1:16"> And even as they did not like to retain God in their knowledge, God gave them over to a reprobate mind, to do those things which are not convenient;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:20; Proverbs 16:28; Psalms 41:7"> Being filled with all unrighteousness, fornication, wickedness, covetousness, maliciousness; full of envy, murder, debate, deceit, malignity; whisperers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:2; Jude 1:16; 2 Peter 2:18"> Backbiters, haters of God, despiteful, proud, boasters, inventors of evil things, disobedient to parents,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:3; Proverbs 18:2; Romans 3:11"> Without understanding, covenantbreakers, without natural affection, implacable, unmerciful:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 2:1–5; Romans 1:21; Romans 6:21"> Who knowing the judgment of God, that they which commit such things are worthy of death, not only do the same, but have pleasure in them that do them.</span>
