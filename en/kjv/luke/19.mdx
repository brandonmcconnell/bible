<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 6:1–27; Luke 18:35; 2 Kings 2:18–22"> And Jesus entered and passed through Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 18:24–27; 2 Chronicles 17:5–6"> And, behold, there was a man named Zacchæus, which was the chief among the publicans, and he was rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 23:8; Luke 9:7–9; John 12:21"> And he sought to see Jesus who he was; and could not for the press, because he was little of stature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 9:10; Luke 5:19; 1 Kings 10:27"> And he ran before, and climbed up into a sycomore tree to see him: for he was to pass that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; John 14:23; Hebrews 13:2"> And when Jesus came to the place, he looked up, and saw him, and said unto him,<span className="woj">Zacchæus, make haste, and come down; for to day I must abide at thy house.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:29; Acts 16:34; Acts 16:15"> And he made haste, and came down, and received him joyfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 15:2; Luke 7:39; Luke 7:34"> And when they saw it, they all murmured, saying, That he was gone to be guest with a man that is a sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:6; Numbers 5:7; Luke 18:22–23"> And Zacchæus stood, and said unto the Lord; Behold, Lord, the half of my goods I give to the poor; and if I have taken any thing from any man by false accusation, I restore him fourfold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; Luke 13:16; Romans 4:16"> And Jesus said unto him,<span className="woj">This day is salvation come to this house, forsomuch as he also is a son of Abraham.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Matthew 1:21; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> <span className="woj">For the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 17:20; Acts 1:6; 2 Thessalonians 2:1–3"> And as they heard these things, he added and spake a parable, because he was nigh to Jerusalem, and because they thought that the kingdom of God should immediately appear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–30; Luke 19:12–27; Luke 20:9"> He said therefore,<span className="woj">A certain nobleman went into a far country to receive for himself a kingdom, and to return.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–15; John 12:26; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> <span className="woj">And he called his ten servants, and delivered them ten pounds, and said unto them, Occupy till I come.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Acts 7:51–52; Acts 3:14–15"> <span className="woj">But his citizens hated him, and sent a message after him, saying, We will not have this man to reign over us.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:1–5; Romans 14:10–12; Psalms 2:4–6"> <span className="woj">And it came to pass, that when he was returned, having received the kingdom, then he commanded these servants to be called unto him, to whom he had given the money, that he might know how much every man had gained by trading.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 2:18–26; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> <span className="woj">Then came the first, saying, Lord, thy pound hath gained ten pounds.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 16:10; Matthew 25:21; Revelation 2:26–29"> <span className="woj">And he said unto him, Well, thou good servant: because thou hast been faithful in a very little, have thou authority over ten cities.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 4:20; Matthew 13:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12"> <span className="woj">And the second came, saying, Lord, thy pound hath gained five pounds.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 1 Corinthians 15:41–42; 1 Corinthians 3:8"> <span className="woj">And he said likewise to him, Be thou also over five cities.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:24; Proverbs 26:13–16; James 4:17"> <span className="woj">And another came, saying, Lord, behold, here is thy pound, which I have kept laid up in a napkin:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:24–25; Romans 8:7; 1 John 4:18"> <span className="woj">For I feared thee, because thou art an austere man: thou takest up that thou layedst not down, and reapest that thou didst not sow.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:16; Matthew 12:37; Job 15:5–6"> <span className="woj">And he saith unto him, Out of thine own mouth will I judge thee, thou wicked servant. Thou knewest that I was an austere man, taking up that I laid not down, and reaping that I did not sow:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:25–27; Romans 2:4–5; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> <span className="woj">Wherefore then gavest not thou my money into the bank, that at my coming I might have required mine own with usury?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 16:2; Luke 12:20"> <span className="woj">And he said unto them that stood by, Take from him the pound, and give it to him that hath ten pounds.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 16:2; 2 Samuel 7:19; Isaiah 55:8–9"> <span className="woj">(And they said unto him, Lord, he hath ten pounds.)</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 John 1:8; Luke 8:18; Matthew 21:43"> <span className="woj">For I say unto you, That unto every one which hath shall be given; and from him that hath not, even that he hath shall be taken away from him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Psalms 21:8–9; Matthew 22:7"> <span className="woj">But those mine enemies, which would not that I should reign over them, bring hither, and slay them before me.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 9:51; Hebrews 12:2; 1 Peter 4:1"> And when he had thus spoken, he went before, ascending up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Matthew 21:1–11"> And it came to pass, when he was come nigh to Bethphage and Bethany, at the mount called the mount of Olives, he sent two of his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 22:8–13; Luke 19:32; 1 Samuel 10:2–9"> Saying,<span className="woj">Go ye into the village over against you; in the which at your entering ye shall find a colt tied, whereon yet never man sat: loose him, and bring him hither.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 24:1; Psalms 50:10–12; Mark 11:3–6"> <span className="woj">And if any man ask you, Why do ye loose him? thus shall ye say unto him, Because the Lord hath need of him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 22:13"> And they that were sent went their way, and found even as he had said unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And as they were loosing the colt, the owners thereof said unto them, Why loose ye the colt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; John 10:35; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> And they said, The Lord hath need of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 11:7–8; Matthew 21:7; John 12:14"> And they brought him to Jesus: and they cast their garments upon the colt, and they set Jesus thereon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; Matthew 21:8"> And as he went, they spread their clothes in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 12:12–13; Ezra 3:10–13; Matthew 21:1"> And when he was come nigh, even now at the descent of the mount of Olives, the whole multitude of the disciples began to rejoice and praise God with a loud voice for all the mighty works that they had seen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Luke 13:35; Psalms 118:22–26"> Saying, Blessed be the King that cometh in the name of the Lord: peace in heaven, and glory in the highest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 4:1–2; Matthew 23:13; Acts 4:16–18"> And some of the Pharisees from among the multitude said unto him, Master, rebuke thy disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:11; Isaiah 55:12; Matthew 3:9"> And he answered and said unto them,<span className="woj">I tell you that, if these should hold their peace, the stones would immediately cry out.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 13:34–35; John 11:35; Psalms 119:136"> And when he was come near, he beheld the city, and wept over it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Hebrews 10:26–29; Isaiah 48:18"> Saying,<span className="woj">If thou hadst known, even thou, at least in this thy day, the things which belong unto thy peace! but now they are hid from thine eyes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 21:20–24; Isaiah 29:1–4; Ezekiel 4:2"> <span className="woj">For the days shall come upon thee, that thine enemies shall cast a trench about thee, and compass thee round, and keep thee in on every side,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 21:6; Luke 13:34–35; John 3:18–21"> <span className="woj">And shall lay thee even with the ground, and thy children within thee; and they shall not leave in thee one stone upon another; because thou knewest not the time of thy visitation.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 21:12–13; Mark 11:15–17; John 2:13–17"> And he went into the temple, and began to cast out them that sold therein, and them that bought;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Jeremiah 7:11; Psalms 93:5"> Saying unto them,<span className="woj">It is written, My house is the house of prayer: but ye have made it a den of thieves.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 26:55; Mark 11:27–33"> And he taught daily in the temple. But the chief priests and the scribes and the chief of the people sought to destroy him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:3; Acts 16:14; John 7:46–49"> And could not find what they might do: for all the people were very attentive to hear him.</span>
</div>
