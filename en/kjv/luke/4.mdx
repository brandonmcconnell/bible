<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:1–11; Acts 10:38; Mark 1:12–13"> And Jesus being full of the Holy Ghost returned from Jordan, and was led by the Spirit into the wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Hebrews 4:15; Deuteronomy 9:9"> Being forty days tempted of the devil. And in those days he did eat nothing: and when they were ended, he afterward hungered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 4:3; Luke 3:22"> And the devil said unto him, If thou be the Son of God, command this stone that it be made bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Matthew 4:4; Matthew 6:31"> And Jesus answered him, saying,<span className="woj">It is written, That man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word of God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; 1 Corinthians 15:52; Matthew 4:8"> And the devil, taking him up into an high mountain, shewed unto him all the kingdoms of the world in a moment of time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 12:31; 1 John 5:19; John 14:30"> And the devil said unto him, All this power will I give thee, and the glory of them: for that is delivered unto me; and to whomsoever I will I give it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 8:28; Psalms 72:11; Isaiah 45:14"> If thou therefore wilt worship me, all shall be thine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Deuteronomy 10:20; Matthew 4:10"> And Jesus answered and said unto him,<span className="woj">Get thee behind me, Satan: for it is written, Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 4:5–6; Luke 4:3; Matthew 8:29"> And he brought him to Jerusalem, and set him on a pinnacle of the temple, and said unto him, If thou be the Son of God, cast thyself down from hence:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 91:11–12; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Corinthians 11:14"> For it is written, He shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 91:12"> And in their hands they shall bear thee up, lest at any time thou dash thy foot against a stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; Hebrews 3:8–9; 1 Corinthians 10:9"> And Jesus answering said unto him,<span className="woj">It is said, Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; James 4:7; Matthew 4:11"> And when the devil had ended all the temptation, he departed from him for a season.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 4:23–25; Acts 10:37; Matthew 4:12"> And Jesus returned in the power of the Spirit into Galilee: and there went out a fame of him through all the region round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Matthew 9:8; Matthew 9:35"> And he taught in their synagogues, being glorified of all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:2; Acts 13:14–16; Matthew 2:23"> And he came to Nazareth, where he had been brought up: and, as his custom was, he went into the synagogue on the sabbath day, and stood up for to read.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1–3; Acts 13:15; Acts 7:42"> And there was delivered unto him the book of the prophet Esaias. And when he had opened the book, he found the place where it was written,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1; Matthew 11:5; Psalms 146:7–8"> <span className="woj">The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 61:2; Isaiah 63:4; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> <span className="woj">To preach the acceptable year of the Lord.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 4:17; John 8:2; Luke 19:48"> And he closed the book, and he gave it again to the minister, and sat down. And the eyes of all them that were in the synagogue were fastened on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 4:25–26; Acts 3:18; Acts 2:16–18"> And he began to say unto them,<span className="woj">This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 13:54–56; Psalms 45:2; Acts 6:10"> And all bare him witness, and wondered at the gracious words which proceeded out of his mouth. And they said, Is not this Joseph’s son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 4:46–53; Matthew 11:23–24; Mark 2:1–12"> And he said unto them,<span className="woj">Ye will surely say unto me this proverb, Physician, heal thyself: whatsoever we have heard done in Capernaum, do also here in thy country.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 13:57; John 4:44; Mark 6:4–5"> And he said,<span className="woj">Verily I say unto you, No prophet is accepted in his own country.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 5:17; 1 Kings 17:1; Matthew 20:15"> <span className="woj">But I tell you of a truth, many widows were in Israel in the days of Elias, when the heaven was shut up three years and six months, when great famine was throughout all the land;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 17:9–24; Matthew 11:21; Obadiah 1:20"> <span className="woj">But unto none of them was Elias sent, save unto Sarepta, a city of Sidon, unto a woman that was a widow.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 5:1–27; John 17:12; Matthew 12:4"> <span className="woj">And many lepers were in Israel in the time of Eliseus the prophet; and none of them was cleansed, saving Naaman the Syrian.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 5:33; 2 Chronicles 24:20–21; Jeremiah 38:6"> And all they in the synagogue, when they heard these things, were filled with wrath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:59; Acts 21:28–32; Numbers 15:35"> And rose up, and thrust him out of the city, and led him unto the brow of the hill whereon their city was built, that they might cast him down headlong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:39; John 8:59; John 18:6–7"> But he passing through the midst of them went his way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 1:21–28; Matthew 4:13; Acts 13:50–2"> And came down to Capernaum, a city of Galilee, and taught them on the sabbath days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 4:36; Matthew 7:28–29; John 6:63"> And they were astonished at his doctrine: for his word was with power.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 1:23"> And in the synagogue there was a man, which had a spirit of an unclean devil, and cried out with a loud voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 1:24; James 2:19; Matthew 8:29"> Saying, Let us alone; what have we to do with thee, thou Jesus of Nazareth? art thou come to destroy us? I know thee who thou art; the Holy One of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Luke 4:39; Matthew 8:26"> And Jesus rebuked him, saying,<span className="woj">Hold thy peace, and come out of him.</span>And when the devil had thrown him in the midst, he came out of him, and hurt him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 4:32; 1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 12:22–23"> And they were all amazed, and spake among themselves, saying, What a word is this! for with authority and power he commandeth the unclean spirits, and they come out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 1:45; Luke 4:14; Isaiah 52:13"> And the fame of him went out into every place of the country round about.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 8:14–15; Mark 1:29–31; John 11:22"> And he arose out of the synagogue, and entered into Simon’s house. And Simon’s wife’s mother was taken with a great fever; and they besought him for her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Luke 4:35; Luke 8:24"> And he stood over her, and rebuked the fever; and it left her: and immediately she arose and ministered unto them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 1:32–34; Matthew 8:16–17; Mark 5:23"> Now when the sun was setting, all they that had any sick with divers diseases brought them unto him; and he laid his hands on every one of them, and healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:34; James 2:19; Luke 4:34–35"> And devils also came out of many, crying out, and saying, Thou art Christ the Son of God. And he rebuking them suffered them not to speak: for they knew that he was Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 1:35–38; John 4:34"> And when it was day, he departed and went into a desert place: and the people sought him, and came unto him, and stayed him, that he should not depart from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 1:38–39; 2 Timothy 4:2; John 9:4"> And he said unto them,<span className="woj">I must preach the kingdom of God to other cities also: for therefore am I sent.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Mark 1:39; Luke 4:15"> And he preached in the synagogues of Galilee.</span>
</div>
