<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 4:1–9; Matthew 13:2–9"> And when much people were gathered together, and were come to him out of every city, he spake by a parable:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; James 1:23–24; Mark 4:15"> <span className="woj">A sower went out to sow his seed: and as he sowed, some fell by the way side; and it was trodden down, and the fowls of the air devoured it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7–8; Luke 8:13; Mark 4:5–6"> <span className="woj">And some fell upon a rock; and as soon as it was sprung up, it withered away, because it lacked moisture.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Luke 8:14; Luke 21:34"> <span className="woj">And some fell among thorns; and the thorns sprang up with it, and choked it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 2:7"> <span className="woj">And other fell on good ground, and sprang up, and bare fruit an hundredfold.</span>And when he had said these things, he cried,<span className="woj">He that hath ears to hear, let him hear.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 4:10; Matthew 13:10; Matthew 13:18"> And his disciples asked him, saying, What might this parable be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; Mark 4:11; Matthew 11:25"> And he said,<span className="woj">Unto you it is given to know the mysteries of the kingdom of God: but to others in parables; that seeing they might not see, and hearing they might not understand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Mark 4:14–20; James 1:21"> <span className="woj">Now the parable is this: The seed is the word of God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–14; Revelation 12:9; James 1:23–24"> <span className="woj">Those by the way side are they that hear; then cometh the devil, and taketh away the word out of their hearts, lest they should believe and be saved.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 6:4; John 5:35; Proverbs 12:12"> <span className="woj">They on the rock are they, which, when they hear, receive the word with joy; and these have no root, which for a while believe, and in time of temptation fall away.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; 1 Timothy 6:17"> <span className="woj">And that which fell among thorns are they, which, when they have heard, go forth, and are choked with cares and riches and pleasures of this life, and bring no fruit to perfection.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Galatians 5:22–26; Colossians 1:10"> <span className="woj">But that on the good ground are they, which in an honest and good heart, having heard the word, keep it, and bring forth fruit with patience.</span></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 12:2–3; Matthew 10:26; Mark 4:22"> <span className="woj">For nothing is secret, that shall not be made manifest; neither any thing hid, that shall not be known and come abroad.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:12; Luke 19:26; Mark 4:23–25"> <span className="woj">Take heed therefore how ye hear: for whosoever hath, to him shall be given; and whosoever hath not, from him shall be taken even that which he seemeth to have.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 12:46–50; Mark 3:31–35; Mark 3:21"> Then came to him his mother and his brethren, and could not come at him for the press.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 1:14; Mark 6:3; Matthew 13:55–56"> And it was told him by certain which said, Thy mother and thy brethren stand without, desiring to see thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 11:27–28; James 1:22; John 13:17"> And he answered and said unto them,<span className="woj">My mother and my brethren are these which hear the word of God, and do it.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 8:23–27; Mark 4:35–41; Mark 6:45"> Now it came to pass on a certain day, that he went into a ship with his disciples: and he said unto them,<span className="woj">Let us go over unto the other side of the lake.</span>And they launched forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 93:3–4; Psalms 124:2–4; Psalms 148:8"> But as they sailed he fell asleep: and there came down a storm of wind on the lake; and they were filled with water, and were in jeopardy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 107:25–29; Luke 4:39; Psalms 104:6–9"> And they came to him, and awoke him, saying, Master, master, we perish. Then he arose, and rebuked the wind and the raging of the water: and they ceased, and there was a calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 8:26; Proverbs 30:4; Mark 4:40–41"> And he said unto them,<span className="woj">Where is your faith?</span>And they being afraid wondered, saying one to another, What manner of man is this! for he commandeth even the winds and water, and they obey him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 5:1–20; Matthew 8:28–34"> And they arrived at the country of the Gadarenes, which is over against Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 5:2–5; 1 Samuel 19:24; Numbers 19:16"> And when he went forth to land, there met him out of the city a certain man, which had devils long time, and ware no clothes, neither abode in any house, but in the tombs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; 2 Peter 2:4; Mark 5:6–8"> When he saw Jesus, he cried out, and fell down before him, and with a loud voice said, What have I to do with thee, Jesus, thou Son of God most high? I beseech thee, torment me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 5:3–5; Luke 9:39; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> (For he had commanded the unclean spirit to come out of the man. For oftentimes it had caught him: and he was kept bound with chains and in fetters; and he brake the bands, and was driven of the devil into the wilderness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 26:53; Luke 8:2; Matthew 8:29"> And Jesus asked him, saying,<span className="woj">What is thy name?</span>And he said, Legion: because many devils were entered into him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Philippians 2:10–11; Job 2:5"> And they besought him that he would not command them to go out into the deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3; Revelation 20:7"> And there was there an herd of many swine feeding on the mountain: and they besought him that he would suffer them to enter into them. And he suffered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; John 8:44; Luke 8:22–23"> Then went the devils out of the man, and entered into the swine: and the herd ran violently down a steep place into the lake, and were choked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 5:14; Matthew 8:33; Matthew 28:11"> When they that fed them saw what was done, they fled, and went and told it in the city and in the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 10:39; Acts 22:3; 1 John 3:8"> Then they went out to see what was done; and came to Jesus, and found the man, out of whom the devils were departed, sitting at the feet of Jesus, clothed, and in his right mind: and they were afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 4:24"> They also which saw it told them by what means he that was possessed of the devils was healed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 16:39; Luke 8:28; Luke 10:10–11"> Then the whole multitude of the country of the Gadarenes round about besought him to depart from them; for they were taken with great fear: and he went up into the ship, and returned back again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 9:13–16; Mark 5:18–20; Psalms 145:3–12"> Now the man out of whom the devils were departed besought him that he might be with him: but Jesus sent him away, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 17:15–18; 1 Timothy 5:8; Deuteronomy 10:21"> <span className="woj">Return to thine own house, and shew how great things God hath done unto thee.</span>And he went his way, and published throughout the whole city how great things Jesus had done unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 9:1; Mark 5:21; Mark 6:20"> And it came to pass, that, when Jesus was returned, the people gladly received him: for they were all waiting for him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 5:22–43; Luke 13:14; Acts 18:8"> And, behold, there came a man named Jairus, and he was a ruler of the synagogue: and he fell down at Jesus’ feet, and besought him that he would come into his house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 7:12; Luke 8:45; Job 1:18–19"> For he had one only daughter, about twelve years of age, and she lay a dying. But as he went the people thronged him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 5:25–26; Matthew 9:20–22; Leviticus 15:25–33"> And a woman having an issue of blood twelve years, which had spent all her living upon physicians, neither could be healed of any,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Mark 5:27–28; Deuteronomy 22:12; Acts 19:12"> Came behind him, and touched the border of his garment: and immediately her issue of blood stanched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 5:30–32; Luke 9:13; Luke 5:5"> And Jesus said,<span className="woj">Who touched me?</span>When all denied, Peter and they that were with him said, Master, the multitude throng thee and press thee, and sayest thou,<span className="woj">Who touched me?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 5:17; 1 Peter 2:9"> And Jesus said,<span className="woj">Somebody hath touched me: for I perceive that virtue is gone out of me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 5:33; Luke 17:15–16; 1 Corinthians 2:3"> And when the woman saw that she was not hid, she came trembling, and falling down before him, she declared unto him before all the people for what cause she had touched him, and how she was healed immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Matthew 9:22; Hebrews 4:2"> And he said unto her,<span className="woj">Daughter, be of good comfort: thy faith hath made thee whole; go in peace.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 5:35–43; Isaiah 7:12; Luke 7:6"> While he yet spake, there cometh one from the ruler of the synagogue’s house, saying to him, Thy daughter is dead; trouble not the Master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 11:40; John 11:25; Mark 5:36"> But when Jesus heard it, he answered him, saying,<span className="woj">Fear not: believe only, and she shall be made whole.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Mark 14:33; Matthew 6:5–6; 1 Kings 17:19–23"> And when he came into the house, he suffered no man to go in, save Peter, and James, and John, and the father and the mother of the maiden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 23:27; John 11:4; Zechariah 12:10"> And all wept, and bewailed her: but he said,<span className="woj">Weep not; she is not dead, but sleepeth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Luke 16:14; Isaiah 53:3; John 11:39"> And they laughed him to scorn, knowing that she was dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; John 11:43; Mark 1:31"> And he put them all out, and took her by the hand, and called, saying,<span className="woj">Maid, arise.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 11:44; 1 Kings 17:21–23; Mark 5:43"> And her spirit came again, and she arose straightway: and he commanded to give her meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Luke 5:14"> And her parents were astonished: but he charged them that they should tell no man what was done.</span>
