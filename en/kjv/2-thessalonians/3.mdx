<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:18; 1 Thessalonians 5:24; 1 Corinthians 10:13"> But the Lord is faithful, who shall stablish you, and keep you from evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Philemon 1:21; 1 Thessalonians 4:10"> And we have confidence in the Lord touching you, that ye both do and will do the things which we command you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 119:36; 1 Chronicles 29:18; Deuteronomy 30:6"> And the Lord direct your hearts into the love of God, and into the patient waiting for Christ.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 20:4"> For even when we were with you, this we commanded you, that if any would not work, neither should he eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:13; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Thessalonians 4:11"> For we hear that there are some which walk among you disorderly, working not at all, but are busybodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:28; 1 Thessalonians 4:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:8"> Now them that are such we command and exhort by our Lord Jesus Christ, that with quietness they work, and eat their own bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 6:9–10; Hebrews 12:3; 2 Corinthians 4:1"> But ye, brethren, be not weary in well doing.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Corinthians 5:5"> Yet count him not as an enemy, but admonish him as a brother.</span>
