<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; Exodus 25:24"> And thou shalt overlay it with pure gold, the top thereof, and the sides thereof round about, and the horns thereof; and thou shalt make unto it a crown of gold round about.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="30.3 top: Heb. roof">a</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="30.3 sides: Heb. walls">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:27; Exodus 26:29; Exodus 27:7"> And two golden rings shalt thou make to it under the crown of it, by the two corners thereof, upon the two sides of it shalt thou make it; and they shall be for places for the staves to bear it withal.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="30.4 corners: Heb. ribs">c</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:21–22; Exodus 30:36; Leviticus 16:13"> And thou shalt put it before the vail that is by the ark of the testimony, before the mercy seat that is over the testimony, where I will meet with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:28; 1 Chronicles 23:13; Exodus 27:20–21"> And Aaron shall burn thereon sweet incense every morning: when he dresseth the lamps, he shall burn incense upon it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="30.7 sweet incense: Heb. incense of spices">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; Hebrews 7:25"> And when Aaron lighteth the lamps at even, he shall burn incense upon it, a perpetual incense before the LORD throughout your generations.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="30.8 lighteth: or setteth up: Heb. causeth to ascend">e</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="30.8 at even: Heb. between the two evenings">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1"> Ye shall offer no strange incense thereon, nor burnt sacrifice, nor meat offering; neither shall ye pour drink offering thereon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:18; Hebrews 9:22–23; Leviticus 16:29–30"> And Aaron shall make an atonement upon the horns of it once in a year with the blood of the sin offering of atonements: once in the year shall he make atonement upon it throughout your generations: it is most holy unto the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; 1 Chronicles 27:24; Exodus 38:25–26"> When thou takest the sum of the children of Israel after their number, then shall they give every man a ransom for his soul unto the LORD, when thou numberest them; that there be no plague among them, when thou numberest them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="30.12 their number: Heb. them that are to be numbered">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:25; Numbers 3:47; Ezekiel 45:12"> This they shall give, every one that passeth among them that are numbered, half a shekel after the shekel of the sanctuary: (a shekel is twenty gerahs:) an half shekel shall be the offering of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:20; Numbers 1:18; Numbers 1:3"> Every one that passeth among them that are numbered, from twenty years old and above, shall give an offering unto the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; Leviticus 17:11; Exodus 30:12"> The rich shall not give more, and the poor shall not give less than half a shekel, when they give an offering unto the LORD, to make an atonement for your souls.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="30.15 give more: Heb. multiply">h</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="30.15 give less: Heb. diminish">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:32–33; Numbers 16:40; Exodus 38:25–31"> And thou shalt take the atonement money of the children of Israel, and shalt appoint it for the service of the tabernacle of the congregation; that it may be a memorial unto the children of Israel before the LORD, to make an atonement for your souls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 40:7; Exodus 38:8; 1 Kings 7:23"> Thou shalt also make a laver of brass, and his foot also of brass, to wash withal: and thou shalt put it between the tabernacle of the congregation and the altar, and thou shalt put water therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Exodus 40:31–32; Psalms 26:6"> For Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and their feet thereat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–3; Hebrews 12:28–29; 1 Chronicles 13:10"> When they go into the tabernacle of the congregation, they shall wash with water, that they die not; or when they come near to the altar to minister, to burn offering made by fire unto the LORD:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 28:43; Exodus 27:21"> So they shall wash their hands and their feet, that they die not: and it shall be a statute for ever to them, even to him and to his seed throughout their generations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Moreover the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:19; Song of Solomon 4:14; Ezekiel 27:22"> Take thou also unto thee principal spices, of pure myrrh five hundred shekels, and of sweet cinnamon half so much, even two hundred and fifty shekels, and of sweet calamus two hundred and fifty shekels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 45:8; Exodus 29:40; Numbers 3:47"> And of cassia five hundred shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary, and of oil olive an hin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; 1 Chronicles 9:30; Exodus 40:9"> And thou shalt make it an oil of holy ointment, an ointment compound after the art of the apothecary: it shall be an holy anointing oil.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="30.25 apothecary: or, perfumer">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 7:1; 1 John 2:20; Isaiah 61:1"> And thou shalt anoint the tabernacle of the congregation therewith, and the ark of the testimony,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And the table and all his vessels, and the candlestick and his vessels, and the altar of incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the altar of burnt offering with all his vessels, and the laver and his foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Exodus 29:37; Leviticus 6:18"> And thou shalt sanctify them, that they may be most holy: whatsoever toucheth them shall be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 8:12; Exodus 28:3; Leviticus 8:30"> And thou shalt anoint Aaron and his sons, and consecrate them, that they may minister unto me in the priest’s office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Leviticus 21:10; Psalms 89:20"> And thou shalt speak unto the children of Israel, saying, This shall be an holy anointing oil unto me throughout your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 30:25; Exodus 30:37–38; Leviticus 21:10"> Upon man’s flesh shall it not be poured, neither shall ye make any other like it, after the composition of it: it is holy, and it shall be holy unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 30:38; Genesis 17:14; Exodus 12:15"> Whosoever compoundeth any like it, or whosoever putteth any of it upon a stranger, shall even be cut off from his people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Exodus 25:6; Exodus 30:23"> And the LORD said unto Moses, Take unto thee sweet spices, stacte, and onycha, and galbanum; these sweet spices with pure frankincense: of each shall there be a like weight:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 2:13; Exodus 30:25; John 12:3"> And thou shalt make it a perfume, a confection after the art of the apothecary, tempered together, pure and holy:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="30.35 tempered…: Heb. salted">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Exodus 30:6; Exodus 16:34"> And thou shalt beat some of it very small, and put of it before the testimony in the tabernacle of the congregation, where I will meet with thee: it shall be unto you most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:32–33"> And as for the perfume which thou shalt make, ye shall not make to yourselves according to the composition thereof: it shall be unto thee holy for the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:33"> Whosoever shall make like unto that, to smell thereto, shall even be cut off from his people.</span>
