<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; 1 Chronicles 23:12–14; Exodus 6:16–20"> And there went a man of the house of Levi, and took to wife a daughter of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:23; Acts 7:20; Psalms 112:5"> And the woman conceived, and bare a son: and when she saw him that he was a goodly child, she hid him three months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Acts 7:19; Genesis 6:14"> And when she could not longer hide him, she took for him an ark of bulrushes, and daubed it with slime and with pitch, and put the child therein; and she laid it in the flags by the river’s brink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; Exodus 15:20; Micah 6:4"> And his sister stood afar off, to wit what would be done to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 7:21; Psalms 12:5; Psalms 9:9"> And the daughter of Pharaoh came down to wash herself at the river; and her maidens walked along by the river’s side; and when she saw the ark among the flags, she sent her maid to fetch it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 8:50; 1 Peter 3:8; Acts 7:21"> And when she had opened it, she saw the child: and, behold, the babe wept. And she had compassion on him, and said, This is one of the Hebrews’ children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 2:4; Numbers 26:59; Numbers 12:1"> Then said his sister to Pharaoh’s daughter, Shall I go and call to thee a nurse of the Hebrew women, that she may nurse the child for thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3–4; Psalms 27:10; Exodus 6:20"> And Pharaoh’s daughter said to her, Go. And the maid went and called the child’s mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Pharaoh’s daughter said unto her, Take this child away, and nurse it for me, and I will give thee thy wages. And the woman took the child, and nursed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24; Genesis 16:11; 1 Samuel 1:20"> And the child grew, and she brought him unto Pharaoh’s daughter, and he became her son. And she called his name Moses: and she said, Because I drew him out of the water.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.10 Moses: that is, Drawn out">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24–26; Acts 7:22–24; Exodus 1:11"> And it came to pass in those days, when Moses was grown, that he went out unto his brethren, and looked on their burdens: and he spied an Egyptian smiting an Hebrew, one of his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 7:24–26"> And he looked this way and that way, and when he saw that there was no man, he slew the Egyptian, and hid him in the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:26–28; 1 Corinthians 6:7–8"> And when he went out the second day, behold, two men of the Hebrews strove together: and he said to him that did the wrong, Wherefore smitest thou thy fellow?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 12:14; Matthew 21:23; Genesis 19:9"> And he said, Who made thee a prince and a judge over us? intendest thou to kill me, as thou killedst the Egyptian? And Moses feared, and said, Surely this thing is known.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.14 a prince: Heb. a man, a prince">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 7:29; Hebrews 11:27; Genesis 24:11"> Now when Pharaoh heard this thing, he sought to slay Moses. But Moses fled from the face of Pharaoh, and dwelt in the land of Midian: and he sat down by a well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 24:11; Exodus 3:1; 1 Samuel 9:11"> Now the priest of Midian had seven daughters: and they came and drew water, and filled the troughs to water their father’s flock.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.16 priest: or, prince">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 29:10; Genesis 21:25; Genesis 26:15–22"> And the shepherds came and drove them away: but Moses stood up and helped them, and watered their flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Numbers 10:29; Exodus 4:18"> And when they came to Reuel their father, he said, How is it that ye are come so soon to day?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.18 Reuel: called also Jethro, or, Jether">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:11; Genesis 29:10"> And they said, An Egyptian delivered us out of the hand of the shepherds, and also drew water enough for us, and watered the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Genesis 43:25; Hebrews 13:2"> And he said unto his daughters, And where is he? why is it that ye have left the man? call him, that he may eat bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 31:38–40; Hebrews 13:5; Hebrews 11:25"> And Moses was content to dwell with the man: and he gave Moses Zipporah his daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13–14; Acts 7:29; Exodus 22:21"> And she bare him a son, and he called his name Gershom: for he said, I have been a stranger in a strange land.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2.22 Gershom: that is, A stranger here">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 18:20–21; James 5:4; Exodus 3:7–9"> And it came to pass in process of time, that the king of Egypt died: and the children of Israel sighed by reason of the bondage, and they cried, and their cry came up unto God by reason of the bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 26:3; Psalms 105:42; Exodus 6:5"> And God heard their groaning, and God remembered his covenant with Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; Psalms 55:22; Exodus 3:7–8"> And God looked upon the children of Israel, and God had respect unto them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="2.25 had…: Heb. knew">f</a></sup></span>
