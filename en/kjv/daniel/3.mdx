<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Revelation 17:13; Revelation 13:13–16"> Then the princes, the governors, and captains, the judges, the treasurers, the counsellors, the sheriffs, and all the rulers of the provinces, were gathered together unto the dedication of the image that Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up; and they stood before the image that Nebuchadnezzar had set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 4:1; Daniel 6:25; Micah 6:16"> Then an herald cried aloud, To you it is commanded, O people, nations, and languages,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.4 aloud: Chaldee, with might">a</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.4 it…: Chaldee, they command">b</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 17:8; Revelation 13:3; Revelation 13:8"> Therefore at that time, when all the people heard the sound of the cornet, flute, harp, sackbut, psaltery, and all kinds of musick, all the people, the nations, and the languages, fell down and worshipped the golden image that Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:10; Daniel 6:6; Daniel 6:21"> They spake and said to the king Nebuchadnezzar, O king, live for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 3:4–7; Daniel 6:12; Esther 3:12–14"> Thou, O king, hast made a decree, that every man that shall hear the sound of the cornet, flute, harp, sackbut, psaltery, and dulcimer, and all kinds of musick, shall fall down and worship the golden image:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.10 dulcimer: or, singing: Chaldee, symphony">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And whoso falleth not down and worshippeth, that he should be cast into the midst of a burning fiery furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 6:13; Daniel 2:49; Acts 17:7"> There are certain Jews whom thou hast set over the affairs of the province of Babylon, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego; these men, O king, have not regarded thee: they serve not thy gods, nor worship the golden image which thou hast set up.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.12 have…: Chaldee, have set no regard upon thee">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:2; Daniel 3:1; Isaiah 46:1"> Nebuchadnezzar spake and said unto them, Is it true, O Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, do not ye serve my gods, nor worship the golden image which I have set up?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="3.14 true: or, of purpose">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 5:29; 2 Chronicles 32:15–17; Exodus 5:2"> Now if ye be ready that at what time ye hear the sound of the cornet, flute, harp, sackbut, psaltery, and dulcimer, and all kinds of musick, ye fall down and worship the image which I have made; well: but if ye worship not, ye shall be cast the same hour into the midst of a burning fiery furnace; and who is that God that shall deliver you out of my hands?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="3.15 dulcimer: or, singing: Chaldee, symphony">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 1:7; Daniel 3:12"> Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, answered and said to the king, O Nebuchadnezzar, we are not careful to answer thee in this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Luke 1:37; Hebrews 7:25"> If it be so, our God whom we serve is able to deliver us from the burning fiery furnace, and he will deliver us out of thine hand, O king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:29–32; Matthew 10:39; Revelation 2:10–11"> But if not, be it known unto thee, O king, that we will not serve thy gods, nor worship the golden image which thou hast set up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 3:13; Leviticus 26:24; Proverbs 21:24"> Then was Nebuchadnezzar full of fury, and the form of his visage was changed against Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego: therefore he spake, and commanded that they should heat the furnace one seven times more than it was wont to be heated.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="3.19 full: Chaldee, filled">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 16:23; Acts 16:25; Daniel 3:15"> And he commanded the most mighty men that were in his army to bind Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, and to cast them into the burning fiery furnace.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="3.20 most…: Chaldee, mighty of strength">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then these men were bound in their coats, their hosen, and their hats, and their other garments, and were cast into the midst of the burning fiery furnace.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="3.21 coats: or, mantles">j</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="3.21 hats: or, turbans">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 12:33; Proverbs 21:18; Proverbs 11:8"> Therefore because the king’s commandment was urgent, and the furnace exceeding hot, the flame of the fire slew those men that took up Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="3.22 commandment: Chaldee, word">l</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="3.22 flame: or, spark">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 6:16–17; Jeremiah 38:6; Psalms 34:19"> And these three men, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, fell down bound into the midst of the burning fiery furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 6:7; Daniel 6:22; Daniel 3:9–10"> Then Nebuchadnezzar the king was astonied, and rose up in haste, and spake, and said unto his counsellors, Did not we cast three men bound into the midst of the fire? They answered and said unto the king, True, O king.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="3.24 counsellors: or, governors">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Acts 28:5; Mark 16:18"> He answered and said, Lo, I see four men loose, walking in the midst of the fire, and they have no hurt; and the form of the fourth is like the Son of God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="3.25 they…: Chaldee, there is no hurt in them">o</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 3:17; Isaiah 52:12; Revelation 19:5"> Then Nebuchadnezzar came near to the mouth of the burning fiery furnace, and spake, and said, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, ye servants of the most high God, come forth, and come hither. Then Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, came forth of the midst of the fire.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="3.26 mouth: Chaldee, door">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Hebrews 11:34; Isaiah 26:11"> And the princes, governors, and captains, and the king’s counsellors, being gathered together, saw these men, upon whose bodies the fire had no power, nor was an hair of their head singed, neither were their coats changed, nor the smell of fire had passed on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 14:7–8; Psalms 34:7–8; Psalms 84:11–12"> Then Nebuchadnezzar spake, and said, Blessed be the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, who hath sent his angel, and delivered his servants that trusted in him, and have changed the king’s word, and yielded their bodies, that they might not serve nor worship any god, except their own God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 2:5; Psalms 76:10; Deuteronomy 32:31"> Therefore I make a decree, That every people, nation, and language, which speak any thing amiss against the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, shall be cut in pieces, and their houses shall be made a dunghill: because there is no other God that can deliver after this sort.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="3.29 I make…: Chaldee, a decree is made by me">q</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="3.29 any…: Chaldee, error">r</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="3.29 cut…: Chaldee, made pieces">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 2:49; 1 Samuel 2:30; Daniel 3:12"> Then the king promoted Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, in the province of Babylon.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="3.30 promoted: Chaldee, made to prosper">t</a></sup></span>
</div>
