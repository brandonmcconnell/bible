<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 6:25; 1 Peter 1:2; Daniel 3:4"> Nebuchadnezzar the king, unto all people, nations, and languages, that dwell in all the earth; Peace be multiplied unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 3:26; Psalms 66:16; Psalms 71:18"> I thought it good to shew the signs and wonders that the high God hath wrought toward me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.2 I thought…: Chaldee, It was seemly before me">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 145:13; Isaiah 25:1; Daniel 2:44"> How great are his signs! and how mighty are his wonders! his kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and his dominion is from generation to generation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 47:7–8; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Psalms 30:6–7"> I Nebuchadnezzar was at rest in mine house, and flourishing in my palace:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 2:1; Job 7:13–14; Daniel 2:28–29"> I saw a dream which made me afraid, and the thoughts upon my bed and the visions of my head troubled me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 2:2; Genesis 41:7–8; Isaiah 8:19"> Therefore made I a decree to bring in all the wise men of Babylon before me, that they might make known unto me the interpretation of the dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 44:25; Daniel 2:7; Genesis 41:8"> Then came in the magicians, the astrologers, the Chaldeans, and the soothsayers: and I told the dream before them; but they did not make known unto me the interpretation thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 1:7; Daniel 5:14; Daniel 4:18"> But at the last Daniel came in before me, whose name was Belteshazzar, according to the name of my god, and in whom is the spirit of the holy gods: and before him I told the dream, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 2:48; Daniel 5:11; Daniel 4:8"> O Belteshazzar, master of the magicians, because I know that the spirit of the holy gods is in thee, and no secret troubleth thee, tell me the visions of my dream that I have seen, and the interpretation thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:2; Daniel 4:5; Psalms 37:35–36"> Thus were the visions of mine head in my bed; I saw, and behold a tree in the midst of the earth, and the height thereof was great.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.10 I saw: Chaldee, I was seeing">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 4:21–22; Deuteronomy 9:1; Matthew 11:23"> The tree grew, and was strong, and the height thereof reached unto heaven, and the sight thereof to the end of all the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 13:19; Lamentations 4:20; Ezekiel 31:6–7"> The leaves thereof were fair, and the fruit thereof much, and in it was meat for all: the beasts of the field had shadow under it, and the fowls of the heaven dwelt in the boughs thereof, and all flesh was fed of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Daniel 8:13; Daniel 7:1"> I saw in the visions of my head upon my bed, and, behold, a watcher and an holy one came down from heaven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 3:10; Daniel 4:23; Ezekiel 31:12–13"> He cried aloud, and said thus, Hew down the tree, and cut off his branches, shake off his leaves, and scatter his fruit: let the beasts get away from under it, and the fowls from his branches:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.14 aloud: Chaldee, with might">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 14:7–9; Daniel 4:25–27; Ezekiel 29:14–15"> Nevertheless leave the stump of his roots in the earth, even with a band of iron and brass, in the tender grass of the field; and let it be wet with the dew of heaven, and let his portion be with the beasts in the grass of the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 4:23; Revelation 12:14; Daniel 4:25"> Let his heart be changed from man’s, and let a beast’s heart be given unto him; and let seven times pass over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Daniel 2:21; Psalms 75:6–7"> This matter is by the decree of the watchers, and the demand by the word of the holy ones: to the intent that the living may know that the most High ruleth in the kingdom of men, and giveth it to whomsoever he will, and setteth up over it the basest of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 5:15; Genesis 41:8; Daniel 5:8"> This dream I king Nebuchadnezzar have seen. Now thou, O Belteshazzar, declare the interpretation thereof, forasmuch as all the wise men of my kingdom are not able to make known unto me the interpretation: but thou art able; for the spirit of the holy gods is in thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 8:27; Daniel 7:28; Daniel 4:24"> Then Daniel, whose name was Belteshazzar, was astonied for one hour, and his thoughts troubled him. The king spake, and said, Belteshazzar, let not the dream, or the interpretation thereof, trouble thee. Belteshazzar answered and said, My lord, the dream be to them that hate thee, and the interpretation thereof to thine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 4:10–12; Ezekiel 31:3; Ezekiel 31:16"> The tree that thou sawest, which grew, and was strong, whose height reached unto the heaven, and the sight thereof to all the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Whose leaves were fair, and the fruit thereof much, and in it was meat for all; under which the beasts of the field dwelt, and upon whose branches the fowls of the heaven had their habitation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 2:37–38; 2 Samuel 12:7; Jeremiah 27:6–8"> It is thou, O king, that art grown and become strong: for thy greatness is grown, and reacheth unto heaven, and thy dominion to the end of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 5:21; Daniel 4:13–16"> And whereas the king saw a watcher and an holy one coming down from heaven, and saying, Hew the tree down, and destroy it; yet leave the stump of the roots thereof in the earth, even with a band of iron and brass, in the tender grass of the field; and let it be wet with the dew of heaven, and let his portion be with the beasts of the field, till seven times pass over him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 107:40; Job 40:11–12; Isaiah 46:10–11"> This is the interpretation, O king, and this is the decree of the most High, which is come upon my lord the king:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 4:17; Jeremiah 27:5; Psalms 83:18"> That they shall drive thee from men, and thy dwelling shall be with the beasts of the field, and they shall make thee to eat grass as oxen, and they shall wet thee with the dew of heaven, and seven times shall pass over thee, till thou know that the most High ruleth in the kingdom of men, and giveth it to whomsoever he will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 4:15; Matthew 5:34; Matthew 21:20"> And whereas they commanded to leave the stump of the tree roots; thy kingdom shall be sure unto thee, after that thou shalt have known that the heavens do rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6–7; Jonah 3:9; Acts 8:22"> Wherefore, O king, let my counsel be acceptable unto thee, and break off thy sins by righteousness, and thine iniquities by shewing mercy to the poor; if it may be a lengthening of thy tranquillity.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.27 a lengthening…: or, an healing of thine error">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Zechariah 1:6; Proverbs 10:24"> All this came upon the king Nebuchadnezzar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 2:21; Ecclesiastes 8:11; 1 Peter 3:20"> At the end of twelve months he walked in the palace of the kingdom of Babylon.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="4.29 in: or, upon">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Revelation 18:10; Luke 14:11"> The king spake, and said, Is not this great Babylon, that I have built for the house of the kingdom by the might of my power, and for the honour of my majesty?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 9:3–5; Revelation 16:7; Luke 12:20"> While the word was in the king’s mouth, there fell a voice from heaven, saying, O king Nebuchadnezzar, to thee it is spoken; The kingdom is departed from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5; Daniel 5:21; Proverbs 8:15–16"> And they shall drive thee from men, and thy dwelling shall be with the beasts of the field: they shall make thee to eat grass as oxen, and seven times shall pass over thee, until thou know that the most High ruleth in the kingdom of men, and giveth it to whomsoever he will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Daniel 4:32; Daniel 4:25; Daniel 5:5"> The same hour was the thing fulfilled upon Nebuchadnezzar: and he was driven from men, and did eat grass as oxen, and his body was wet with the dew of heaven, till his hairs were grown like eagles’ feathers, and his nails like birds’ claws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 4:10; Jeremiah 10:10; Daniel 12:7"> And at the end of the days I Nebuchadnezzar lifted up mine eyes unto heaven, and mine understanding returned unto me, and I blessed the most High, and I praised and honoured him that liveth for ever, whose dominion is an everlasting dominion, and his kingdom is from generation to generation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 42:2; Psalms 135:6; Psalms 115:3"> And all the inhabitants of the earth are reputed as nothing: and he doeth according to his will in the army of heaven, and among the inhabitants of the earth: and none can stay his hand, or say unto him, What doest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Daniel 4:34; Proverbs 22:4; Daniel 4:15–16"> At the same time my reason returned unto me; and for the glory of my kingdom, mine honour and brightness returned unto me; and my counsellors and my lords sought unto me; and I was established in my kingdom, and excellent majesty was added unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="James 4:6–7; Psalms 33:4–5; 1 Peter 5:5–6"> Now I Nebuchadnezzar praise and extol and honour the King of heaven, all whose works are truth, and his ways judgment: and those that walk in pride he is able to abase.</span>
</div>
