<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–3; 2 Timothy 2:20; Matthew 13:47–48"> But a certain man named Ananias, with Sapphira his wife, sold a possession,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:37; 2 Peter 2:14–15; Matthew 23:5"> And kept back part of the price, his wife also being privy to it, and brought a certain part, and laid it at the apostles’ feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:24; Isaiah 29:15; John 13:2"> But Peter said, Ananias, why hath Satan filled thine heart to lie to the Holy Ghost, and to keep back part of the price of the land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 5:3; Joshua 7:25–26; 2 Kings 5:25–27"> Whiles it remained, was it not thine own? and after it was sold, was it not in thine own power? why hast thou conceived this thing in thine heart? thou hast not lied unto men, but unto God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 5:10–11; Acts 2:43; Acts 5:13"> And Ananias hearing these words fell down, and gave up the ghost: and great fear came on all them that heard these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 19:40; 2 Samuel 18:17; Deuteronomy 21:23"> And the young men arose, wound him up, and carried him out, and buried him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And it was about the space of three hours after, when his wife, not knowing what was done, came in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 5:2"> And Peter answered unto her, Tell me whether ye sold the land for so much? And she said, Yea, for so much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Acts 5:3–4; Psalms 95:8–11"> Then Peter said unto her, How is it that ye have agreed together to tempt the Spirit of the Lord? behold, the feet of them which have buried thy husband are at the door, and shall carry thee out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:5"> Then fell she down straightway at his feet, and yielded up the ghost: and the young men came in, and found her dead, and, carrying her forth, buried her by her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 5:5; 1 Corinthians 10:11–12; Acts 19:17"> And great fear came upon all the church, and upon as many as heard these things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 19:11; 2 Corinthians 12:12; Hebrews 2:4"> And by the hands of the apostles were many signs and wonders wrought among the people; (and they were all with one accord in Solomon’s porch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:47; Acts 4:21; John 12:42"> And of the rest durst no man join himself to them: but the people magnified them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 2:47; Acts 4:4; Isaiah 55:11–13"> And believers were the more added to the Lord, multitudes both of men and women.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Acts 19:11–12; John 14:12"> Insomuch that they brought forth the sick into the streets, and laid them on beds and couches, that at the least the shadow of Peter passing by might overshadow some of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 16:17–18; Mark 2:3–4; Matthew 15:30–31"> There came also a multitude out of the cities round about unto Jerusalem, bringing sick folks, and them which were vexed with unclean spirits: and they were healed every one.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 13:45; Acts 7:9; Acts 17:5"> Then the high priest rose up, and all they that were with him, (which is the sect of the Sadducees,) and were filled with indignation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 4:3; Luke 21:12; 2 Corinthians 11:23"> And laid their hands on the apostles, and put them in the common prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 16:26; Psalms 34:7; Acts 12:7–11"> But the angel of the Lord by night opened the prison doors, and brought them forth, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 6:63; John 6:68; 1 John 5:11–12"> Go, stand and speak in the temple to the people all the words of this life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 8:2; Acts 22:15; Acts 12:18–19"> And when they heard that, they entered into the temple early in the morning, and taught. But the high priest came, and they that were with him, and called the council together, and all the senate of the children of Israel, and sent to the prison to have them brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But when the officers came, and found them not in the prison, they returned, and told,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Psalms 33:10; Daniel 6:22–24"> Saying, The prison truly found we shut with all safety, and the keepers standing without before the doors: but when we had opened, we found no man within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Acts 5:26; John 12:19"> Now when the high priest and the captain of the temple and the chief priests heard these things, they doubted of them whereunto this would grow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Then came one and told them, saying, Behold, the men whom ye put in prison are standing in the temple, and teaching the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:13; Acts 4:21; Matthew 26:5"> Then went the captain with the officers, and brought them without violence: for they feared the people, lest they should have been stoned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 5:22; Acts 4:7; Luke 22:66"> And when they had brought them, they set them before the council: and the high priest asked them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Acts 7:52; Acts 4:10–11"> Saying, Did not we straitly command you that ye should not teach in this name? and, behold, ye have filled Jerusalem with your doctrine, and intend to bring this man’s blood upon us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:19; 1 Samuel 15:24; Mark 7:7–9"> Then Peter and the other apostles answered and said, We ought to obey God rather than men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Galatians 3:13; 1 Peter 2:24; Acts 10:39"> The God of our fathers raised up Jesus, whom ye slew and hanged on a tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:47; Acts 2:33; Hebrews 2:10"> Him hath God exalted with his right hand to be a Prince and a Saviour, for to give repentance to Israel, and forgiveness of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 15:26–27; Hebrews 2:3–4; Acts 1:8"> And we are his witnesses of these things; and so is also the Holy Ghost, whom God hath given to them that obey him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 7:54; Acts 2:37; Luke 4:28–29"> When they heard that, they were cut to the heart, and took counsel to slay them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 22:3; Luke 2:46; Luke 5:17"> Then stood there up one in the council, a Pharisee, named Gamaliel, a doctor of the law, had in reputation among all the people, and commanded to put the apostles forth a little space;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 22:26; Jeremiah 26:19; Matthew 27:19"> And said unto them, Ye men of Israel, take heed to yourselves what ye intend to do as touching these men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 8:9; 2 Peter 2:2; Galatians 6:3"> For before these days rose up Theudas, boasting himself to be somebody; to whom a number of men, about four hundred, joined themselves: who was slain; and all, as many as obeyed him, were scattered, and brought to nought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 2:1–2; Psalms 7:14–15; Matthew 26:52"> After this man rose up Judas of Galilee in the days of the taxing, and drew away much people after him: he also perished; and all, even as many as obeyed him, were dispersed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Lamentations 3:37; Isaiah 8:9–10"> And now I say unto you, Refrain from these men, and let them alone: for if this counsel or this work be of men, it will come to nought:</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; Isaiah 46:10; Matthew 16:18"> But if it be of God, ye can not overthrow it; lest haply ye be found even to fight against God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 10:17; Acts 5:28; Acts 4:17–21"> And to him they agreed: and when they had called the apostles, and beaten them, they commanded that they should not speak in the name of Jesus, and let them go.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 4:13–16; Luke 6:22"> And they departed from the presence of the council, rejoicing that they were counted worthy to suffer shame for his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Acts 2:46; Acts 20:20; Luke 21:37"> And daily in the temple, and in every house, they ceased not to teach and preach Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>
