<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 21:34–35; Matthew 24:37–39; Luke 17:26–30"> For when they shall say, Peace and safety; then sudden destruction cometh upon them, as travail upon a woman with child; and they shall not escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:8; 2 Peter 3:10; Deuteronomy 19:6"> But ye, brethren, are not in darkness, that that day should overtake you as a thief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Acts 26:18; John 12:36"> Ye are all the children of light, and the children of the day: we are not of the night, nor of darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 1:13; Matthew 24:42; Luke 22:46"> Therefore let us not sleep, as do others; but let us watch and be sober.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 13:13; 1 Corinthians 15:34; 2 Peter 2:13"> For they that sleep sleep in the night; and they that be drunken are drunken in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:12–13; Ephesians 6:11; 1 Peter 1:13"> But let us, who are of the day, be sober, putting on the breastplate of faith and love; and for an helmet, the hope of salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:10; 1 Peter 2:10; 2 Timothy 2:10"> For God hath not appointed us to wrath, but to obtain salvation by our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:8–9; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 8:34"> Who died for us, that, whether we wake or sleep, we should live together with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Hebrews 10:25; Romans 15:2"> Wherefore comfort yourselves together, and edify one another, even as also ye do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; Hebrews 13:7; 1 Timothy 5:17–18"> And we beseech you, brethren, to know them which labour among you, and are over you in the Lord, and admonish you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 9:50; Hebrews 12:14; Galatians 6:6"> And to esteem them very highly in love for their work’s sake. And be at peace among yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Romans 14:1; Acts 20:35"> Now we exhort you, brethren, warn them that are unruly, comfort the feebleminded, support the weak, be patient toward all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Galatians 6:10; Leviticus 19:18"> See that none render evil for evil unto any man; but ever follow that which is good, both among yourselves, and to all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Philippians 4:4; Matthew 5:12"> Rejoice evermore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Colossians 4:2; Ephesians 6:18"> Pray without ceasing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:20; Philippians 4:6; Colossians 3:17"> In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; Acts 7:51; 2 Timothy 1:6"> Quench not the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:3–6; 1 Corinthians 14:1; Numbers 11:25–29"> Despise not prophesyings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Romans 12:2; Ephesians 5:10"> Prove all things; hold fast that which is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; Philippians 4:8; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33"> Abstain from all appearance of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jude 1:24; Hebrews 4:12; 1 Corinthians 1:8–9"> And the very God of peace sanctify you wholly; and I pray God your whole spirit and soul and body be preserved blameless unto the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; Numbers 23:19; 1 Corinthians 1:9"> Faithful is he that calleth you, who also will do it.</span>
