<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; 1 Kings 21:4; Song of Solomon 5:8"> And Amnon was so vexed, that he fell sick for his sister Tamar; for she was a virgin; and Amnon thought it hard for him to do any thing to her.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="13.2 Amnon thought…: Heb. it was marvellous, or, hidden in the eyes of Amnon">a</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Leviticus 18:9; Esther 5:13–14"> And he said unto him, Why art thou, being the king’s son, lean from day to day? wilt thou not tell me? </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:6; Matthew 13:33"> So Amnon lay down, and made himself sick: and when the king was come to see him, Amnon said unto the king, I pray thee, let Tamar my sister come, and make me a couple of cakes in my sight, that I may eat at her hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then David sent home to Tamar, saying, Go now to thy brother Amnon’s house, and dress him meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> So Tamar went to her brother Amnon’s house; and he was laid down. And she took flour, and kneaded it, and made cakes in his sight, and did bake the cakes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="13.8 flour: or, paste">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 45:1; John 3:20; Judges 3:19"> And she took a pan, and poured them out before him; but he refused to eat. And Amnon said, Have out all men from me. And they went out every man from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Amnon said unto Tamar, Bring the meat into the chamber, that I may eat of thine hand. And Tamar took the cakes which she had made, and brought them into the chamber to Amnon her brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 39:11–12"> And when she had brought them unto him to eat, he took hold of her, and said unto her, Come lie with me, my sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 19:23; Leviticus 20:17; Judges 20:6"> And she answered him, Nay, my brother, do not force me; for no such thing ought to be done in Israel: do not thou this folly.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="13.12 force…: Heb. humble me">e</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="13.12 no such…: Heb. it ought not so to be done">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 19:24; Genesis 20:12; Genesis 19:8"> And I, whither shall I cause my shame to go? and as for thee, thou shalt be as one of the fools in Israel. Now therefore, I pray thee, speak unto the king; for he will not withhold me from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:11; Esther 7:8; Judges 20:5"> Howbeit he would not hearken unto her voice: but, being stronger than she, forced her, and lay with her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:17"> Then Amnon hated her exceedingly; so that the hatred wherewith he hated her was greater than the love wherewith he had loved her. And Amnon said unto her, Arise, be gone.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="13.15 exceedingly: Heb. with great hatred greatly">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And she said unto him, There is no cause: this evil in sending me away is greater than the other that thou didst unto me. But he would not hearken unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then he called his servant that ministered unto him, and said, Put now this woman out from me, and bolt the door after her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Genesis 37:3; Psalms 45:13–14"> And she had a garment of divers colours upon her: for with such robes were the king’s daughters that were virgins apparelled. Then his servant brought her out, and bolted the door after her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 7:6; Jeremiah 2:37; 2 Samuel 1:11"> And Tamar put ashes on her head, and rent her garment of divers colours that was on her, and laid her hand on her head, and went on crying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Genesis 46:15; Genesis 34:2"> And Absalom her brother said unto her, Hath Amnon thy brother been with thee? but hold now thy peace, my sister: he is thy brother; regard not this thing. So Tamar remained desolate in her brother Absalom’s house.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="13.20 Amnon: Heb. Aminon">h</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="13.20 regard…: Heb. set not thy heart">i</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="13.20 desolate: Heb. and desolate">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:7; 2 Samuel 3:28–29; 1 Samuel 2:29"> But when king David heard of all these things, he was very wroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 19:17–18; Genesis 31:24; Genesis 24:50"> And Absalom spake unto his brother Amnon neither good nor bad: for Absalom hated Amnon, because he had forced his sister Tamar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 38:12–13; 1 Samuel 25:4; 2 Chronicles 26:10"> And it came to pass after two full years, that Absalom had sheepshearers in Baal-hazor, which is beside Ephraim: and Absalom invited all the king’s sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:8–15; Jeremiah 41:6–7; Psalms 55:21"> And Absalom came to the king, and said, Behold now, thy servant hath sheepshearers; let the king, I beseech thee, and his servants go with thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 14:23; Judges 19:7–10; 2 Samuel 14:22"> And the king said to Absalom, Nay, my son, let us not all now go, lest we be chargeable unto thee. And he pressed him: howbeit he would not go, but blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:9; 2 Samuel 3:27; 2 Samuel 11:13–15"> Then said Absalom, If not, I pray thee, let my brother Amnon go with us. And the king said unto him, Why should he go with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–26"> But Absalom pressed him, that he let Amnon and all the king’s sons go with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 19:6; Judges 19:22; Judges 19:9"> Now Absalom had commanded his servants, saying, Mark ye now when Amnon’s heart is merry with wine, and when I say unto you, Smite Amnon; then kill him, fear not: have not I commanded you? be courageous, and be valiant.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="13.28 have…: or, will you not, since I have commanded you?">k</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="13.28 valiant: Heb. sons of valour">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:9; 1 Kings 1:33; 2 Kings 1:9–12"> And the servants of Absalom did unto Amnon as Absalom had commanded. Then all the king’s sons arose, and every man gat him up upon his mule, and fled.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="13.29 gat…: Heb. rode">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And it came to pass, while they were in the way, that tidings came to David, saying, Absalom hath slain all the king’s sons, and there is not one of them left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:11; 2 Samuel 12:16; Job 1:20"> Then the king arose, and tare his garments, and lay on the earth; and all his servants stood by with their clothes rent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:9; 2 Samuel 13:3; Genesis 27:41"> And Jonadab, the son of Shimeah David’s brother, answered and said, Let not my lord suppose that they have slain all the young men the king’s sons; for Amnon only is dead: for by the appointment of Absalom this hath been determined from the day that he forced his sister Tamar.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="13.32 appointment: Heb. mouth">n</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="13.32 determined: or, settled">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:19"> Now therefore let not my lord the king take the thing to his heart, to think that all the king’s sons are dead: for Amnon only is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 4:8–14; Proverbs 28:17; Amos 5:19"> But Absalom fled. And the young man that kept the watch lifted up his eyes, and looked, and, behold, there came much people by the way of the hill side behind him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And Jonadab said unto the king, Behold, the king’s sons come: as thy servant said, so it is.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="13.35 as thy…: Heb. according to the word of thy servant">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:33; 2 Samuel 12:21; 2 Samuel 13:15"> And it came to pass, as soon as he had made an end of speaking, that, behold, the king’s sons came, and lifted up their voice and wept: and the king also and all his servants wept very sore.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="13.36 very…: Heb. with a great weeping greatly">q</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:3; 2 Samuel 14:32; 2 Samuel 13:34"> But Absalom fled, and went to Talmai, the son of Ammihud, king of Geshur. And David mourned for his son every day.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="13.37 Ammihud: or, Ammihur">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:8; 2 Samuel 14:23; 2 Samuel 14:32"> So Absalom fled, and went to Geshur, and was there three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 24:67; Genesis 38:12; Genesis 37:35"> And the soul of king David longed to go forth unto Absalom: for he was comforted concerning Amnon, seeing he was dead.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="13.39 longed: or, was consumed">s</a></sup></span>
