</div>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:12; Luke 2:38; Deuteronomy 31:21"> And it was broken in that day: and so the poor of the flock that waited upon me knew that it was the word of the LORD.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="11.11 so…: or, the poor of the flock, etc. certainly knew">h</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 9:21; Isaiah 11:13; Acts 23:7–10"> Then I cut asunder mine other staff, even Bands, that I might break the brotherhood between Judah and Israel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="11.14 Bands: or, Binders">j</a></sup></span>
