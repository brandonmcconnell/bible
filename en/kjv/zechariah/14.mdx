<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 12:9; Zephaniah 3:19; Zechariah 2:8–9"> Then shall the LORD go forth, and fight against those nations, as when he fought in the day of battle.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jude 1:14–15; Amos 1:1; Matthew 25:31"> And ye shall flee to the valley of the mountains; for the valley of the mountains shall reach unto Azal: yea, ye shall flee, like as ye fled from before the earthquake in the days of Uzziah king of Judah: and the LORD my God shall come, and all the saints with thee.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="14.5 the mountains: or, my mountains">a</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="14.5 for the…: or, when he shall touch the valley of the mountains to the place he separated">b</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 22:5; 1 Thessalonians 5:2; Daniel 12:4"> But it shall be one day which shall be known to the LORD, not day, nor night: but it shall come to pass, that at evening time it shall be light.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="14.7 it shall be…: or, the day shall be one">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 22:1–2; John 7:38; John 4:14"> And it shall be in that day, that living waters shall go out from Jerusalem; half of them toward the former sea, and half of them toward the hinder sea: in summer and in winter shall it be.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="14.8 former: or, eastern">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5–6; Revelation 11:15; Daniel 7:27"> And the LORD shall be king over all the earth: in that day shall there be one LORD, and his name one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Joshua 15:32; 1 Kings 15:22"> All the land shall be turned as a plain from Geba to Rimmon south of Jerusalem: and it shall be lifted up, and inhabited in her place, from Benjamin’s gate unto the place of the first gate, unto the corner gate, and from the tower of Hananeel unto the king’s winepresses.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="14.10 turned: or, compassed">h</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="14.10 inhabited: or, shall abide">i</a></sup></span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:17–20; Isaiah 66:15–16; Micah 4:11–13"> And this shall be the plague wherewith the LORD will smite all the people that have fought against Jerusalem; Their flesh shall consume away while they stand upon their feet, and their eyes shall consume away in their holes, and their tongue shall consume away in their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 12:4; Revelation 17:12–17; Judges 7:22"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that a great tumult from the LORD shall be among them; and they shall lay hold every one on the hand of his neighbour, and his hand shall rise up against the hand of his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 23:18; Zechariah 12:2; 2 Chronicles 20:25–27"> And Judah also shall fight at Jerusalem; and the wealth of all the heathen round about shall be gathered together, gold, and silver, and apparel, in great abundance.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="14.14 Judah…: or, thou also, O Judah shalt">k</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="14.14 at: or, against">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 14:12"> And so shall be the plague of the horse, of the mule, of the camel, and of the ass, and of all the beasts that shall be in these tents, as this plague.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 14:17–19; Isaiah 66:23; Isaiah 66:18–21"> And it shall come to pass, that every one that is left of all the nations which came against Jerusalem shall even go up from year to year to worship the King, the LORD of hosts, and to keep the feast of tabernacles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Amos 4:7–8; Isaiah 60:12; Jeremiah 14:4"> And it shall be, that whoso will not come up of all the families of the earth unto Jerusalem to worship the King, the LORD of hosts, even upon them shall be no rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 14:15; Deuteronomy 11:10–11; Zechariah 14:12"> And if the family of Egypt go not up, and come not, that have no rain; there shall be the plague, wherewith the LORD will smite the heathen that come not up to keep the feast of tabernacles.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="14.18 that have no: Heb. upon whom there is not">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 3:19"> This shall be the punishment of Egypt, and the punishment of all nations that come not up to keep the feast of tabernacles.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="14.19 punishment: or, sin">n</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16–17; Exodus 39:30; Leviticus 8:9"> In that day shall there be upon the bells of the horses, HOLINESS UNTO THE LORD; and the pots in the LORD’s house shall be like the bowls before the altar.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="14.20 bells: or, bridles">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:9; Romans 14:6–7; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> Yea, every pot in Jerusalem and in Judah shall be holiness unto the LORD of hosts: and all they that sacrifice shall come and take of them, and seethe therein: and in that day there shall be no more the Canaanite in the house of the LORD of hosts.</span>
</div>
