<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 7:7; Ezra 2:62; 1 Chronicles 9:1"> These are now the chief of their fathers, and this is the genealogy of them that went up with me from Babylon, in the reign of Artaxerxes the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:22; 1 Chronicles 3:1; 1 Chronicles 6:3–15"> Of the sons of Phinehas; Gershom: of the sons of Ithamar; Daniel: of the sons of David; Hattush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 2:3; Nehemiah 10:14; Nehemiah 7:8"> Of the sons of Shechaniah, of the sons of Pharosh; Zechariah: and with him were reckoned by genealogy of the males an hundred and fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezra 2:6; Nehemiah 10:14; Nehemiah 7:11"> Of the sons of Pahath-moab; Elihoenai the son of Zerahiah, and with him two hundred males.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Of the sons of Shechaniah; the son of Jahaziel, and with him three hundred males.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 2:15; Nehemiah 10:16; Nehemiah 7:20"> Of the sons also of Adin; Ebed the son of Jonathan, and with him fifty males.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 2:31; Nehemiah 7:34; Ezra 2:7"> And of the sons of Elam; Jeshaiah the son of Athaliah, and with him seventy males.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:4; Ezra 2:4; Nehemiah 7:9"> And of the sons of Shephatiah; Zebadiah the son of Michael, and with him fourscore males.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:11; Ezra 2:6"> Of the sons of Joab; Obadiah the son of Jehiel, and with him two hundred and eighteen males.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And of the sons of Shelomith; the son of Josiphiah, and with him an hundred and threescore males.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezra 10:28; Nehemiah 7:16; Ezra 2:11"> And of the sons of Bebai; Zechariah the son of Bebai, and with him twenty and eight males.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezra 2:12; Nehemiah 7:17"> And of the sons of Azgad; Johanan the son of Hakkatan, and with him an hundred and ten males.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="8.12 the son…: or, the youngest son">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 2:13; Nehemiah 7:18"> And of the last sons of Adonikam, whose names are these, Eliphelet, Jeiel, and Shemaiah, and with them threescore males.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:12; Ezra 2:14; Nehemiah 7:19"> Of the sons also of Bigvai; Uthai, and Zabbud, and with them seventy males.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="8.14 Zabbud: or, Zaccur, as some read">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezra 8:21; Ezra 8:31; Ezra 7:7"> And I gathered them together to the river that runneth to Ahava; and there abode we in tents three days: and I viewed the people, and the priests, and found there none of the sons of Levi.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="8.15 abode: or, pitched">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 2:6; Ezra 8:18; Ezra 10:39"> Then sent I for Eliezer, for Ariel, for Shemaiah, and for Elnathan, and for Jarib, and for Elnathan, and for Nathan, and for Zechariah, and for Meshullam, chief men; also for Joiarib, and for Elnathan, men of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezra 2:43; Exodus 4:15; Numbers 8:22–26"> And I sent them with commandment unto Iddo the chief at the place Casiphia, and I told them what they should say unto Iddo, and to his brethren the Nethinims, at the place Casiphia, that they should bring unto us ministers for the house of our God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="8.17 I told…: Heb. I put words in their mouth">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; Ezra 7:28; Nehemiah 2:8"> And by the good hand of our God upon us they brought us a man of understanding, of the sons of Mahli, the son of Levi, the son of Israel; and Sherebiah, with his sons and his brethren, eighteen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:1; 1 Chronicles 6:16; Nehemiah 10:11"> And Hashabiah, and with him Jeshaiah of the sons of Merari, his brethren and their sons, twenty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezra 2:43; Ezra 7:7; 1 Chronicles 9:2"> Also of the Nethinims, whom David and the princes had appointed for the service of the Levites, two hundred and twenty Nethinims: all of them were expressed by name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:3; Joel 1:14; 1 Samuel 7:6"> Then I proclaimed a fast there, at the river of Ahava, that we might afflict ourselves before our God, to seek of him a right way for us, and for our little ones, and for all our substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Psalms 33:18–19; 2 Chronicles 16:9"> For I was ashamed to require of the king a band of soldiers and horsemen to help us against the enemy in the way: because we had spoken unto the king, saying, The hand of our God is upon all them for good that seek him; but his power and his wrath is against all them that forsake him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12–13; Matthew 7:7–8; Ezra 8:31"> So we fasted and besought our God for this: and he was intreated of us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra 8:18–19"> Then I separated twelve of the chief of the priests, Sherebiah, Hashabiah, and ten of their brethren with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezra 8:33; Ezra 7:14–16; Ezra 1:8"> And weighed unto them the silver, and the gold, and the vessels, even the offering of the house of our God, which the king, and his counsellors, and his lords, and all Israel there present, had offered:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezra 1:9–11"> I even weighed unto their hand six hundred and fifty talents of silver, and silver vessels an hundred talents, and of gold an hundred talents;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Lamentations 4:2"> Also twenty basons of gold, of a thousand drams; and two vessels of fine copper, precious as gold.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="8.27 fine…: Heb. yellow, or, shining brass">e</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="8.27 precious: Heb. desirable">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 22:2–3; Leviticus 21:6–8; 1 Chronicles 23:28"> And I said unto them, Ye are holy unto the LORD; the vessels are holy also; and the silver and the gold are a freewill offering unto the LORD God of your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezra 8:33–34; Mark 13:34–35; 1 Chronicles 26:20–26"> Watch ye, and keep them, until ye weigh them before the chief of the priests and the Levites, and chief of the fathers of Israel, at Jerusalem, in the chambers of the house of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezra 8:22; 1 Chronicles 29:2–3; Isaiah 60:13"> So took the priests and the Levites the weight of the silver, and the gold, and the vessels, to bring them to Jerusalem unto the house of our God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezra 8:21–22; Ezra 7:9; Ezra 8:15"> Then we departed from the river of Ahava on the twelfth day of the first month, to go unto Jerusalem: and the hand of our God was upon us, and he delivered us from the hand of the enemy, and of such as lay in wait by the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:11; Ezra 7:8–9"> And we came to Jerusalem, and abode there three days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:4; Nehemiah 3:21; Ezra 8:30"> Now on the fourth day was the silver and the gold and the vessels weighed in the house of our God by the hand of Meremoth the son of Uriah the priest; and with him was Eleazar the son of Phinehas; and with them was Jozabad the son of Jeshua, and Noadiah the son of Binnui, Levites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> By number and by weight of every one: and all the weight was written at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezra 6:17; 2 Chronicles 29:31–32; Psalms 66:10–15"> Also the children of those that had been carried away, which were come out of the captivity, offered burnt offerings unto the God of Israel, twelve bullocks for all Israel, ninety and six rams, seventy and seven lambs, twelve he goats for a sin offering: all this was a burnt offering unto the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezra 7:21–24; Ezra 4:7–23; Ezra 6:13"> And they delivered the king’s commissions unto the king’s lieutenants, and to the governors on this side the river: and they furthered the people, and the house of God.</span>
</div>
