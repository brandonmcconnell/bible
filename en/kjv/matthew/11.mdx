<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 16:16; Luke 13:24; Ephesians 6:11–13"> <span className="woj">And from the days of John the Baptist until now the kingdom of heaven suffereth violence, and the violent take it by force.</span></span>
