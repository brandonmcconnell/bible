<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Matthew 23:2; Luke 5:21"> Then came to Jesus scribes and Pharisees, which were of Jerusalem, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 11:38; Mark 7:2; Colossians 2:20–23"> Why do thy disciples transgress the tradition of the elders? for they wash not their hands when they eat bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:23; Titus 1:14; Colossians 2:8"> But he answered and said unto them,<span className="woj">Why do ye also transgress the commandment of God by your tradition?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Leviticus 20:9; Exodus 21:17"> <span className="woj">For God commanded, saying, Honour thy father and mother: and, He that curseth father or mother, let him die the death.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 7:10–13; Acts 5:29; Matthew 23:16–18"> <span className="woj">But ye say, Whosoever shall say to his father or his mother, It is a gift, by whatsoever thou mightest be profited by me;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; Jeremiah 8:8; Mark 7:13"> <span className="woj">And honour not his father or his mother, he shall be free. Thus have ye made the commandment of God of none effect by your tradition.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 7:6; Matthew 23:23–29; Acts 28:25–27"> <span className="woj">Ye hypocrites, well did Esaias prophesy of you, saying,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Ezekiel 33:31; Acts 8:21"> <span className="woj">This people draweth nigh unto me with their mouth, and honoureth me with their lips; but their heart is far from me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Mark 7:7; 1 Timothy 1:4"> <span className="woj">But in vain they do worship me, teaching for doctrines the commandments of men.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 7:14–15; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 24:45"> And he called the multitude, and said unto them,<span className="woj">Hear, and understand:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Matthew 15:18–20; James 3:5–8"> <span className="woj">Not that which goeth into the mouth defileth a man; but that which cometh out of the mouth, this defileth a man.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 22:13–14; 1 Corinthians 10:32–33; Galatians 2:5"> Then came his disciples, and said unto him, Knowest thou that the Pharisees were offended, after they heard this saying?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:2; Isaiah 60:21; John 15:6"> But he answered and said,<span className="woj">Every plant, which my heavenly Father hath not planted, shall be rooted up.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:39; Isaiah 56:10; Matthew 23:16–24"> <span className="woj">Let them alone: they be blind leaders of the blind. And if the blind lead the blind, both shall fall into the ditch.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 13:36; Mark 4:34; John 16:29"> Then answered Peter and said unto him, Declare unto us this parable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:9; Mark 7:18; Mark 9:32"> And Jesus said,<span className="woj">Are ye also yet without understanding?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 6:45; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Matthew 7:19–20"> <span className="woj">Do not ye yet understand, that whatsoever entereth in at the mouth goeth into the belly, and is cast out into the draught?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Matthew 12:34; James 3:6–10"> <span className="woj">But those things which proceed out of the mouth come forth from the heart; and they defile the man.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 7:21–23; Galatians 5:19–21; Jeremiah 17:9"> <span className="woj">For out of the heart proceed evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false witness, blasphemies:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Ephesians 5:3–6; 1 Corinthians 6:18–20"> <span className="woj">These are the things which defile a man: but to eat with unwashen hands defileth not a man.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 7:24–30; Matthew 11:21–23; Judges 1:31"> Then Jesus went thence, and departed into the coasts of Tyre and Sidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Mark 7:25–26; Matthew 17:15"> And, behold, a woman of Canaan came out of the same coasts, and cried unto him, saying, Have mercy on me, O Lord, thou Son of David; my daughter is grievously vexed with a devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 14:15; Mark 10:47–48; Lamentations 3:8"> But he answered her not a word. And his disciples came and besought him, saying, Send her away; for she crieth after us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:5–6; Romans 15:8; Jeremiah 50:6–7"> But he answered and said,<span className="woj">I am not sent but unto the lost sheep of the house of Israel.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 32:26; Matthew 20:31; Matthew 14:33"> Then came she and worshipped him, saying, Lord, help me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Matthew 7:6; Philippians 3:2"> But he answered and said,<span className="woj">It is not meet to take the children’s bread, and to cast it to dogs.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 16:21; Job 40:4–5"> And she said, Truth, Lord: yet the dogs eat of the crumbs which fall from their masters’ table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:23–24; Matthew 8:13; Psalms 145:19"> Then Jesus answered and said unto her,<span className="woj">O woman, great is thy faith: be it unto thee even as thou wilt.</span>And her daughter was made whole from that very hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 4:18; John 6:1; Matthew 5:1"> And Jesus departed from thence, and came nigh unto the sea of Galilee; and went up into a mountain, and sat down there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:17–19; Acts 5:15–16; Matthew 14:35–36"> And great multitudes came unto him, having with them those that were lame, blind, dumb, maimed, and many others, and cast them down at Jesus’ feet; and he healed them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 18:43; Luke 17:15–18"> Insomuch that the multitude wondered, when they saw the dumb to speak, the maimed to be whole, the lame to walk, and the blind to see: and they glorified the God of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 27:33; Luke 12:29–30; Matthew 9:36"> Then Jesus called his disciples unto him, and said,<span className="woj">I have compassion on the multitude, because they continue with me now three days, and have nothing to eat: and I will not send them away fasting, lest they faint in the way.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 6:37; Matthew 14:15; Mark 8:4–5"> And his disciples say unto him, Whence should we have so much bread in the wilderness, as to fill so great a multitude?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 16:9–10; Luke 24:41–42; John 21:9–10"> And Jesus saith unto them,<span className="woj">How many loaves have ye?</span>And they said, Seven, and a few little fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 9:14–16; Mark 6:39–40; John 6:10"> And he commanded the multitude to sit down on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 22:19; Romans 14:6; John 6:11"> And he took the seven loaves and the fishes, and gave thanks, and brake them, and gave to his disciples, and the disciples to the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 8:19–21; Matthew 16:9–10; Matthew 15:33"> And they did all eat, and were filled: and they took up of the broken meat that was left seven baskets full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And they that did eat were four thousand men, beside women and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 8:10; Matthew 14:22"> And he sent away the multitude, and took ship, and came into the coasts of Magdala.</span>
</div>
