<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 16:26; John 20:12–13; Mark 16:3–5"> And, behold, there was a great earthquake: for the angel of the Lord descended from heaven, and came and rolled back the stone from the door, and sat upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 9:3; Acts 1:10; Mark 16:5"> His countenance was like lightning, and his raiment white as snow:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Daniel 10:7; Matthew 27:65–66"> And for fear of him the keepers did shake, and became as dead men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; Hebrews 1:14; Isaiah 41:10"> And the angel answered and said unto the women, Fear not ye: for I know that ye seek Jesus, which was crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 16:6; Matthew 12:40; Luke 24:6–8"> He is not here: for he is risen, as he said. Come, see the place where the Lord lay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Luke 24:22–24; Matthew 28:10"> And go quickly, and tell his disciples that he is risen from the dead; and, behold, he goeth before you into Galilee; there shall ye see him: lo, I have told you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 2:11; Mark 16:8; Luke 24:36–41"> And they departed quickly from the sepulchre with fear and great joy; and did run to bring his disciples word.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 28:17; John 20:14–17; Matthew 14:33"> And as they went to tell his disciples, behold, Jesus met them, saying,<span className="woj">All hail.</span>And they came and held him by the feet, and worshipped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:29; John 20:17; Matthew 28:7"> Then said Jesus unto them,<span className="woj">Be not afraid: go tell my brethren that they go into Galilee, and there shall they see me.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 27:65–66; Matthew 28:4"> Now when they were going, behold, some of the watch came into the city, and shewed unto the chief priests all the things that were done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:3–4; Acts 4:5–22; Matthew 27:1–2"> And when they were assembled with the elders, and had taken counsel, they gave large money unto the soldiers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:64"> Saying, Say ye, His disciples came by night, and stole him away while we slept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Acts 12:19"> And if this come to the governor’s ears, we will persuade him, and secure you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 27:8; Matthew 26:15"> So they took the money, and did as they were taught: and this saying is commonly reported among the Jews until this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Matthew 28:10; Matthew 28:7"> Then the eleven disciples went away into Galilee, into a mountain where Jesus had appointed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 45:11; Psalms 2:12; John 5:23"> And when they saw him, they worshipped him: but some doubted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:35; 1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 11:27"> And Jesus came and spake unto them, saying,<span className="woj">All power is given unto me in heaven and in earth.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 2:38–39; Acts 1:8"> <span className="woj">Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Joshua 1:5; Acts 18:9–10"> <span className="woj">Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world.</span>Amen.</span>
