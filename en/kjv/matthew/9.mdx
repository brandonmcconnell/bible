<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:8; Matthew 12:25; Revelation 2:23"> And Jesus knowing their thoughts said,<span className="woj">Wherefore think ye evil in your hearts?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 5:8–14; Mark 2:9–12; Acts 3:16"> <span className="woj">For whether is easier, to say, Thy sins be forgiven thee; or to say, Arise, and walk?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Matthew 9:5; John 20:21–23"> <span className="woj">But that ye may know that the Son of man hath power on earth to forgive sins,</span>(then saith he to the sick of the palsy,)<span className="woj">Arise, take up thy bed, and go unto thine house.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13–16; Luke 5:29–32; Mark 2:15–17"> And it came to pass, as Jesus sat at meat in the house, behold, many publicans and sinners came and sat down with him and his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:19; Luke 5:30; Mark 2:16"> And when the Pharisees saw it, they said unto his disciples, Why eateth your Master with publicans and sinners?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 5:31; Revelation 3:17–18; Mark 2:17"> But when Jesus heard that, he said unto them,<span className="woj">They that be whole need not a physician, but they that are sick.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Luke 19:10; Hosea 6:6"> <span className="woj">But go ye and learn what that meaneth, I will have mercy, and not sacrifice: for I am not come to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:33–39; Mark 2:18–22; Matthew 6:16"> Then came to him the disciples of John, saying, Why do we and the Pharisees fast oft, but thy disciples fast not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; John 3:29; Revelation 21:2"> And Jesus said unto them,<span className="woj">Can the children of the bridechamber mourn, as long as the bridegroom is with them? but the days will come, when the bridegroom shall be taken from them, and then shall they fast.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:1–2; John 16:12; Psalms 125:3"> <span className="woj">No man putteth a piece of new cloth unto an old garment, for that which is put in to fill it up taketh from the garment, and the rent is made worse.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 9:4; Job 32:19; Psalms 119:83"> <span className="woj">Neither do men put new wine into old bottles: else the bottles break, and the wine runneth out, and the bottles perish: but they put new wine into new bottles, and both are preserved.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:41–56; Mark 5:22–43; Matthew 8:2"> While he spake these things unto them, behold, there came a certain ruler, and worshipped him, saying, My daughter is even now dead: but come and lay thy hand upon her, and she shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 10:38; Galatians 6:9–10; Matthew 8:7"> And Jesus arose, and followed him, and so did his disciples.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 8:43–56; Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56"> And, behold, a woman, which was diseased with an issue of blood twelve years, came behind him, and touched the hem of his garment:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 5:26–33; Luke 8:45–47; Acts 19:12"> For she said within herself, If I may but touch his garment, I shall be whole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Matthew 15:28; Matthew 9:2"> But Jesus turned him about, and when he saw her, he said,<span className="woj">Daughter, be of good comfort; thy faith hath made thee whole.</span>And the woman was made whole from that hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:25; Mark 5:35–40; Luke 8:49–51"> And when Jesus came into the ruler’s house, and saw the minstrels and the people making a noise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 20:10; John 11:11–13; Matthew 27:39–43"> He said unto them,<span className="woj">Give place: for the maid is not dead, but sleepeth.</span>And they laughed him to scorn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 9:27; Mark 5:41; Luke 8:54"> But when the people were put forth, he went in, and took her by the hand, and the maid arose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 1:45"> And the fame hereof went abroad into all that land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Matthew 20:30–31; Mark 10:46–48"> And when Jesus departed thence, two blind men followed him, crying, and saying, Thou Son of David, have mercy on us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:23–24; Matthew 13:58; Matthew 8:2"> And when he was come into the house, the blind men came to him: and Jesus saith unto them,<span className="woj">Believe ye that I am able to do this?</span>They said unto him, Yea, Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Mark 10:52; Matthew 8:13"> Then touched he their eyes, saying,<span className="woj">According to your faith be it unto you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Luke 5:14; Matthew 17:9"> And their eyes were opened; and Jesus straitly charged them, saying,<span className="woj">See that no man know it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 7:36; Matthew 9:26; Mark 1:44–45"> But they, when they were departed, spread abroad his fame in all that country.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Matthew 12:22–24; Luke 11:14"> As they went out, behold, they brought to him a dumb man possessed with a devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 35:6; Mark 7:32–37; Exodus 4:11–12"> And when the devil was cast out, the dumb spake: and the multitudes marvelled, saying, It was never so seen in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 11:15; Mark 3:22; Matthew 12:23–24"> But the Pharisees said, He casteth out devils through the prince of the devils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 4:23–24; Acts 10:38; Mark 6:56"> And Jesus went about all the cities and villages, teaching in their synagogues, and preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every sickness and every disease among the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 6:34; Numbers 27:17; Jeremiah 50:6"> But when he saw the multitudes, he was moved with compassion on them, because they fainted, and were scattered abroad, as sheep having no shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 10:2; Mark 16:15; 1 Corinthians 3:9"> Then saith he unto his disciples,<span className="woj">The harvest truly is plenteous, but the labourers are few;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 10:1–2; Jeremiah 3:15; 2 Thessalonians 3:1"> <span className="woj">Pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he will send forth labourers into his harvest.</span></span>
