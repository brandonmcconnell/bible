<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; Luke 22:47–48; Job 20:15–29"> Then Judas, which had betrayed him, when he saw that he was condemned, repented himself, and brought again the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and elders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:30; Titus 1:16; Acts 18:15–17"> Saying, I have sinned in that I have betrayed the innocent blood. And they said, What is that to us? see thou to that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 1:18–19; 2 Samuel 17:23; 1 Kings 16:18"> And he cast down the pieces of silver in the temple, and departed, and went and hanged himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:7–9; Deuteronomy 23:18; Isaiah 61:8"> And the chief priests took the silver pieces, and said, It is not lawful for to put them into the treasury, because it is the price of blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And they took counsel, and bought with them the potter’s field, to bury strangers in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:19; Matthew 28:15; Joshua 4:9"> Wherefore that field was called, The field of blood, unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 1:22; Leviticus 27:2–7"> Then was fulfilled that which was spoken by Jeremy the prophet, saying, And they took the thirty pieces of silver, the price of him that was valued, whom they of the children of Israel did value;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 11:13"> And gave them for the potter’s field, as the Lord appointed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 23:2–3; Mark 15:2–5; Matthew 26:25"> And Jesus stood before the governor: and the governor asked him, saying, Art thou the King of the Jews? And Jesus said unto him,<span className="woj">Thou sayest.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:63; Isaiah 53:7; Matthew 27:14"> And when he was accused of the chief priests and elders, he answered nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:62; John 18:35; Acts 22:24"> Then said Pilate unto him, Hearest thou not how many things they witness against thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; Psalms 71:7; 1 Corinthians 4:9"> And he answered him to never a word; insomuch that the governor marvelled greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:38–40; Luke 23:16; John 19:16"> Now at that feast the governor was wont to release unto the people a prisoner, whom they would.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 15:7; Acts 3:14; Luke 23:25"> And they had then a notable prisoner, called Barabbas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 19:15; 1 Kings 18:21; Mark 15:9–12"> Therefore when they were gathered together, Pilate said unto them, Whom will ye that I release unto you? Barabbas, or Jesus which is called Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Acts 5:17; Mark 15:10"> For he knew that for envy they had delivered him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Proverbs 29:1; Matthew 27:24"> When he was set down on the judgment seat, his wife sent unto him, saying, Have thou nothing to do with that just man: for I have suffered many things this day in a dream because of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:14–15; John 19:15–16; John 18:40"> But the chief priests and elders persuaded the multitude that they should ask Barabbas, and destroy Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The governor answered and said unto them, Whether of the twain will ye that I release unto you? They said, Barabbas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–3; Isaiah 49:7; Acts 13:38"> Pilate saith unto them, What shall I do then with Jesus which is called Christ? They all say unto him, Let him be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:18–19; Acts 7:57; Acts 23:12–15"> And the governor said, Why, what evil hath he done? But they cried out the more, saying, Let him be crucified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:4; Psalms 26:6; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> When Pilate saw that he could prevail nothing, but that rather a tumult was made, he took water, and washed his hands before the multitude, saying, I am innocent of the blood of this just person: see ye to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 2:19; Acts 5:28; Acts 7:52"> Then answered all the people, and said, His blood be on us, and on our children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; John 19:1; Mark 15:15"> Then released he Barabbas unto them: and when he had scourged Jesus, he delivered him to be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 18:28; John 18:33; Acts 10:1"> Then the soldiers of the governor took Jesus into the common hall, and gathered unto him the whole band of soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 15:17; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> And they stripped him, and put on him a scarlet robe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 20:19; Psalms 69:19–20"> And when they had platted a crown of thorns, they put it upon his head, and a reed in his right hand: and they bowed the knee before him, and mocked him, saying, Hail, King of the Jews!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 53:7"> And they spit upon him, and took the reed, and smote him on the head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 Kings 21:13; 1 Kings 21:10"> And after that they had mocked him, they took the robe off from him, and put his own raiment on him, and led him away to crucify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:21; Matthew 16:24; Luke 23:26"> And as they came out, they found a man of Cyrene, Simon by name: him they compelled to bear his cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 19:17–19; Mark 15:22–32; Mark 15:34"> And when they were come unto a place called Golgotha, that is to say, a place of a skull,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Mark 15:23; Matthew 27:48"> They gave him vinegar to drink mingled with gall: and when he had tasted thereof, he would not drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 22:18; Mark 15:24–32; Luke 23:34"> And they crucified him, and parted his garments, casting lots: that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet, They parted my garments among them, and upon my vesture did they cast lots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:44; Mark 15:39"> And sitting down they watched him there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 23:38; John 19:19–22; Mark 15:26"> And set up over his head his accusation written, THIS IS JESUS THE KING OF THE JEWS.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43; Isaiah 53:12"> Then were there two thieves crucified with him, one on the right hand, and another on the left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 109:25; Lamentations 1:12; Psalms 69:7–12"> And they that passed by reviled him, wagging their heads,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 26:61; Matthew 4:6; Matthew 4:3"> And saying, Thou that destroyest the temple, and buildest it in three days, save thyself. If thou be the Son of God, come down from the cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Isaiah 28:22; Psalms 22:12–13"> Likewise also the chief priests mocking him, with the scribes and elders, said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 1:49; Matthew 27:37; Acts 4:14"> He saved others; himself he cannot save. If he be the King of Israel, let him now come down from the cross, and we will believe him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 22:8; John 10:36; Isaiah 37:10"> He trusted in God; let him deliver him now, if he will have him: for he said, I am the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:38; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:39–43"> The thieves also, which were crucified with him, cast the same in his teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Amos 8:9; Mark 15:33–34; Luke 23:44–45"> Now from the sixth hour there was darkness over all the land unto the ninth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 53:10"> And about the ninth hour Jesus cried with a loud voice, saying,<span className="woj">Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani?</span>that is to say, My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Mark 15:35–36; Matthew 11:14"> Some of them that stood there, when they heard that, said, This man calleth for Elias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36"> And straightway one of them ran, and took a spunge, and filled it with vinegar, and put it on a reed, and gave him to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> The rest said, Let be, let us see whether Elias will come to save him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:30; Isaiah 53:9–12; Psalms 22:14–15"> Jesus, when he had cried again with a loud voice, yielded up the ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Mark 15:38; Hebrews 6:19"> And, behold, the veil of the temple was rent in twain from the top to the bottom; and the earth did quake, and the rocks rent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; 1 Thessalonians 4:14; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> And the graves were opened; and many bodies of the saints which slept arose,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Isaiah 48:2; Revelation 21:2"> And came out of the graves after his resurrection, and went into the holy city, and appeared unto many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:36; Mark 15:39; Matthew 27:43"> Now when the centurion, and they that were with him, watching Jesus, saw the earthquake, and those things that were done, they feared greatly, saying, Truly this was the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Luke 23:48–49; John 19:25–27"> And many women were there beholding afar off, which followed Jesus from Galilee, ministering unto him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 19:25; Mark 15:47–1; Matthew 28:1"> Among which was Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James and Joses, and the mother of Zebedee’s children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Mark 15:42–47; Luke 23:50–56"> When the even was come, there came a rich man of Arimathæa, named Joseph, who also himself was Jesus’ disciple:</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 23:52–53; Mark 15:44–46"> He went to Pilate, and begged the body of Jesus. Then Pilate commanded the body to be delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> And when Joseph had taken the body, he wrapped it in a clean linen cloth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 28:2; Matthew 27:66"> And laid it in his own new tomb, which he had hewn out in the rock: and he rolled a great stone to the door of the sepulchre, and departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Matthew 28:1; Matthew 27:56"> And there was Mary Magdalene, and the other Mary, sitting over against the sepulchre.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 15:42; John 19:14; John 19:42"> Now the next day, that followed the day of the preparation, the chief priests and Pharisees came together unto Pilate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 9:22; Matthew 16:21; John 2:19"> Saying, Sir, we remember that that deceiver said, while he was yet alive, After three days I will rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Matthew 28:13; Matthew 12:45"> Command therefore that the sepulchre be made sure until the third day, lest his disciples come by night, and steal him away, and say unto the people, He is risen from the dead: so the last error shall be worse than the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Matthew 27:66; Matthew 28:11–15; Proverbs 21:30"> Pilate said unto them, Ye have a watch: go your way, make it as sure as ye can.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Daniel 6:17; Matthew 27:60; 2 Timothy 2:19"> So they went, and made the sepulchre sure, sealing the stone, and setting a watch.</span>
</div>
