<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:6; Deuteronomy 2:14; Deuteronomy 2:7"> Ye have compassed this mountain long enough: turn you northward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 20:14–21; Exodus 15:15; Deuteronomy 23:7"> And command thou the people, saying, Ye are to pass through the coast of your brethren the children of Esau, which dwell in Seir; and they shall be afraid of you: take ye good heed unto yourselves therefore:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Joshua 24:4; Jeremiah 27:5"> Meddle not with them; for I will not give you of their land, no, not so much as a foot breadth; because I have given mount Seir unto Esau for a possession.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.5 no…: Heb. even to the treading of the sole of the foot">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Numbers 20:19; Romans 12:17"> Ye shall buy meat of them for money, that ye may eat; and ye shall also buy water of them for money, that ye may drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Nehemiah 9:21; Deuteronomy 8:2–4"> For the LORD thy God hath blessed thee in all the works of thy hand: he knoweth thy walking through this great wilderness: these forty years the LORD thy God hath been with thee; thou hast lacked nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 9:26; Judges 11:18; 2 Kings 16:6"> And when we passed by from our brethren the children of Esau, which dwelt in Seir, through the way of the plain from Elath, and from Ezion-gaber, we turned and passed by the way of the wilderness of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 19:36–37; Numbers 21:15; Numbers 21:28"> And the LORD said unto me, Distress not the Moabites, neither contend with them in battle: for I will not give thee of their land for a possession; because I have given Ar unto the children of Lot for a possession.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.9 Distress…: or, Use no hostility against Moab">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 14:5; Deuteronomy 2:11; Numbers 13:22"> The Emims dwelt therein in times past, a people great, and many, and tall, as the Anakims;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Numbers 13:28; Genesis 14:5"> Which also were accounted giants, as the Anakims; but the Moabites call them Emims.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:22; Genesis 14:6; Deuteronomy 2:32–11"> The Horims also dwelt in Seir beforetime; but the children of Esau succeeded them, when they had destroyed them from before them, and dwelt in their stead; as Israel did unto the land of his possession, which the LORD gave unto them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.12 succeeded: Heb. inherited">c</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.12 stead: or, room">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 21:12; Numbers 13:23"> Now rise up, said I, and get you over the brook Zered. And we went over the brook Zered.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2.13 brook: or, valley">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; Numbers 14:28–35; Deuteronomy 1:34–35"> And the space in which we came from Kadesh-barnea, until we were come over the brook Zered, was thirty and eight years; until all the generation of the men of war were wasted out from among the host, as the LORD sware unto them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="2.14 brook: or, valley">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 106:26; Isaiah 66:14; Psalms 32:4"> For indeed the hand of the LORD was against them, to destroy them from among the host, until they were consumed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So it came to pass, when all the men of war were consumed and dead from among the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> That the LORD spake unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 21:23; Isaiah 15:1; Numbers 21:15"> Thou art to pass over through Ar, the coast of Moab, this day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:9; Judges 11:13–27; 2 Chronicles 20:10"> And when thou comest nigh over against the children of Ammon, distress them not, nor meddle with them: for I will not give thee of the land of the children of Ammon any possession; because I have given it unto the children of Lot for a possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 14:5"> (That also was accounted a land of giants: giants dwelt therein in old time; and the Ammonites call them Zamzummims;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="2.20 Zamzummims: also called, Zuzims">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:7–8; Deuteronomy 1:28; Deuteronomy 2:22"> A people great, and many, and tall, as the Anakims; but the LORD destroyed them before them; and they succeeded them, and dwelt in their stead:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Genesis 36:20–30; Deuteronomy 2:12"> As he did to the children of Esau, which dwelt in Seir, when he destroyed the Horims from before them; and they succeeded them, and dwelt in their stead even unto this day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 10:14; Joshua 13:3; Amos 9:7"> And the Avims which dwelt in Hazerim, even unto Azzah, the Caphtorims, which came forth out of Caphtor, destroyed them, and dwelt in their stead.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5; Daniel 4:17; Judges 11:18–21"> Rise ye up, take your journey, and pass over the river Arnon: behold, I have given into thine hand Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land: begin to possess it, and contend with him in battle.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="2.24 begin…: Heb. begin, possess">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:25; Exodus 15:14–16; Deuteronomy 28:10"> This day will I begin to put the dread of thee and the fear of thee upon the nations that are under the whole heaven, who shall hear report of thee, and shall tremble, and be in anguish because of thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 10:5–6; Luke 10:10–12; Joshua 13:18"> And I sent messengers out of the wilderness of Kedemoth unto Sihon king of Heshbon with words of peace, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 11:19; Deuteronomy 2:6; Numbers 21:21–23"> Let me pass through thy land: I will go along by the high way, I will neither turn unto the right hand nor to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 20:19"> Thou shalt sell me meat for money, that I may eat; and give me water for money, that I may drink: only I will pass through on my feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 20:18; Judges 11:17–18; Deuteronomy 23:3–4"> (As the children of Esau which dwell in Seir, and the Moabites which dwell in Ar, did unto me;) until I shall pass over Jordan into the land which the LORD our God giveth us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 21:23; Exodus 4:21; Joshua 11:19–20"> But Sihon king of Heshbon would not let us pass by him: for the LORD thy God hardened his spirit, and made his heart obstinate, that he might deliver him into thy hand, as appeareth this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:8; Deuteronomy 2:24"> And the LORD said unto me, Behold, I have begun to give Sihon and his land before thee: begin to possess, that thou mayest inherit his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 21:23–30; Judges 11:20–23; Psalms 136:19"> Then Sihon came out against us, he and all his people, to fight at Jahaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Joshua 21:44; Deuteronomy 3:2–3"> And the LORD our God delivered him before us; and we smote him, and his sons, and all his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; 1 Samuel 15:3; Deuteronomy 3:6"> And we took all his cities at that time, and utterly destroyed the men, and the women, and the little ones, of every city, we left none to remain:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="2.34 the men…: Heb. every city of men, and women, and little ones">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Joshua 8:27; Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:9–11"> Only the cattle we took for a prey unto ourselves, and the spoil of the cities which we took.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 13:9; Deuteronomy 4:48; Deuteronomy 3:12"> From Aroer, which is by the brink of the river of Arnon, and from the city that is by the river, even unto Gilead, there was not one city too strong for us: the LORD our God delivered all unto us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 21:24; Deuteronomy 3:16; Genesis 32:22"> Only unto the land of the children of Ammon thou camest not, nor unto any place of the river Jabbok, nor unto the cities in the mountains, nor unto whatsoever the LORD our God forbad us.</span>
