<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 8:32; Deuteronomy 26:9; 2 Corinthians 3:2–3"> And thou shalt write upon them all the words of this law, when thou art passed over, that thou mayest go in unto the land which the LORD thy God giveth thee, a land that floweth with milk and honey; as the LORD God of thy fathers hath promised thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:29–30; Joshua 8:30–33"> Therefore it shall be when ye be gone over Jordan, that ye shall set up these stones, which I command you this day, in mount Ebal, and thou shalt plaister them with plaister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:25; Exodus 24:4; Joshua 8:30–31"> And there shalt thou build an altar unto the LORD thy God, an altar of stones: thou shalt not lift up any iron tool upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Leviticus 1:1–17"> Thou shalt build the altar of the LORD thy God of whole stones: and thou shalt offer burnt offerings thereon unto the LORD thy God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 3:1–17; Isaiah 12:3; Psalms 100:1–2"> And thou shalt offer peace offerings, and shalt eat there, and rejoice before the LORD thy God.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:1; 1 Peter 1:14–16; Matthew 5:48"> Thou shalt therefore obey the voice of the LORD thy God, and do his commandments and his statutes, which I command thee this day.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 9:7; Genesis 30:24; Genesis 35:18"> These shall stand upon mount Gerizim to bless the people, when ye are come over Jordan; Simeon, and Levi, and Judah, and Issachar, and Joseph, and Benjamin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:4; Genesis 29:32; Genesis 30:20"> And these shall stand upon mount Ebal to curse; Reuben, Gad, and Asher, and Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="27.13 to curse: Heb. for a cursing">a</a></sup></span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:11; Deuteronomy 33:9–10; Joshua 8:33"> And the Levites shall speak, and say unto all the men of Israel with a loud voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:17; Exodus 20:4; Deuteronomy 5:8"> Cursed be the man that maketh any graven or molten image, an abomination unto the LORD, the work of the hands of the craftsman, and putteth it in a secret place. And all the people shall answer and say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 21:17; Deuteronomy 21:18–21"> Cursed be he that setteth light by his father or his mother. And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Proverbs 22:28; Proverbs 23:10–11"> Cursed be he that removeth his neighbour’s landmark. And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:14; Matthew 15:14; Proverbs 28:10"> Cursed be he that maketh the blind to wander out of the way. And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:18; Deuteronomy 24:17; Proverbs 17:23"> Cursed be he that perverteth the judgment of the stranger, fatherless, and widow. And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:30; Leviticus 18:8; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> Cursed be he that lieth with his father’s wife; because he uncovereth his father’s skirt. And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15"> Cursed be he that lieth with any manner of beast. And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Leviticus 18:9; Ezekiel 22:11"> Cursed be he that lieth with his sister, the daughter of his father, or the daughter of his mother. And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Leviticus 18:17"> Cursed be he that lieth with his mother in law. And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 35:31; Leviticus 24:17; 2 Samuel 11:15–17"> Cursed be he that smiteth his neighbour secretly. And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Exodus 23:7–8; Psalms 15:5"> Cursed be he that taketh reward to slay an innocent person. And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Romans 10:5"> Cursed be he that confirmeth not all the words of this law to do them. And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
