<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:6; Numbers 9:3; 2 Kings 23:21–23"> Moreover Josiah kept a passover unto the LORD in Jerusalem: and they killed the passover on the fourteenth day of the first month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:2; 1 Chronicles 22:19; 2 Chronicles 29:5–11"> And he set the priests in their charges, and encouraged them to the service of the house of the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:26; Malachi 2:7; Deuteronomy 33:10"> And said unto the Levites that taught all Israel, which were holy unto the LORD, Put the holy ark in the house which Solomon the son of David king of Israel did build; it shall not be a burden upon your shoulders: serve now the LORD your God, and his people Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 23:1–26; Nehemiah 11:10–20"> And prepare yourselves by the houses of your fathers, after your courses, according to the writing of David king of Israel, and according to the writing of Solomon his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 134:1; Psalms 135:2; Ezra 6:18"> And stand in the holy place according to the divisions of the families of the fathers of your brethren the people, and after the division of the families of the Levites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="35.5 the families of the fathers: Heb. the house of the fathers">a</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="35.5 the people: Heb. the sons of the people">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:5; Hebrews 9:13–14; 2 Chronicles 29:15"> So kill the passover, and sanctify yourselves, and prepare your brethren, that they may do according to the word of the LORD by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 8:63; 2 Chronicles 30:24; Isaiah 32:8"> And Josiah gave to the people, of the flock, lambs and kids, all for the passover offerings, for all that were present, to the number of thirty thousand, and three thousand bullocks: these were of the king’s substance.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="35.7 gave: Heb. offered">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:31–33; Jeremiah 29:25–26; 1 Chronicles 24:4–5"> And his princes gave willingly unto the people, to the priests, and to the Levites: Hilkiah and Zechariah and Jehiel, rulers of the house of God, gave unto the priests for the passover offerings two thousand and six hundred small cattle, and three hundred oxen.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="35.8 gave willingly: Heb. offered, etc">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:21–28; Jeremiah 3:10; Isaiah 1:10–15"> Conaniah also, and Shemaiah and Nethaneel, his brethren, and Hashabiah and Jeiel and Jozabad, chief of the Levites, gave unto the Levites for passover offerings five thousand small cattle, and five hundred oxen.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="35.9 gave: Heb. offered">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:16; Ezra 6:18; 2 Chronicles 35:4–5"> So the service was prepared, and the priests stood in their place, and the Levites in their courses, according to the king’s commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; Hebrews 9:21–22; 2 Chronicles 29:22–24"> And they killed the passover, and the priests sprinkled the blood from their hands, and the Levites flayed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 3:9–11; Leviticus 3:3; Leviticus 3:5"> And they removed the burnt offerings, that they might give according to the divisions of the families of the people, to offer unto the LORD, as it is written in the book of Moses. And so did they with the oxen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:8–9; 1 Samuel 2:13–15; Deuteronomy 16:7"> And they roasted the passover with fire according to the ordinance: but the other holy offerings sod they in pots, and in caldrons, and in pans, and divided them speedily among all the people.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="35.13 divided…: Heb. made them run">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 6:2–4"> And afterward they made ready for themselves, and for the priests: because the priests the sons of Aaron were busied in offering of burnt offerings and the fat until night; therefore the Levites prepared for themselves, and for the priests the sons of Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:1–7; 1 Chronicles 26:12–19; 1 Chronicles 9:17–19"> And the singers the sons of Asaph were in their place, according to the commandment of David, and Asaph, and Heman, and Jeduthun the king’s seer; and the porters waited at every gate; they might not depart from their service; for their brethren the Levites prepared for them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="35.15 place: Heb. station">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So all the service of the LORD was prepared the same day, to keep the passover, and to offer burnt offerings upon the altar of the LORD, according to the commandment of king Josiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:15–20; 2 Chronicles 30:21–23; Exodus 23:15"> And the children of Israel that were present kept the passover at that time, and the feast of unleavened bread seven days.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="35.17 present: Heb. found">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:5; 2 Kings 23:21–23; 2 Chronicles 30:26–27"> And there was no passover like to that kept in Israel from the days of Samuel the prophet; neither did all the kings of Israel keep such a passover as Josiah kept, and the priests, and the Levites, and all Judah and Israel that were present, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="35.18 present: Heb. found">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> In the eighteenth year of the reign of Josiah was this passover kept.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 10:9; 2 Kings 23:29–30; Jeremiah 46:2–12"> After all this, when Josiah had prepared the temple, Necho king of Egypt came up to fight against Carchemish by Euphrates: and Josiah went out against him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="35.20 temple: Heb. house">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:19; 2 Kings 18:25; Isaiah 36:10"> But he sent ambassadors to him, saying, What have I to do with thee, thou king of Judah? I come not against thee this day, but against the house wherewith I have war: for God commanded me to make haste: forbear thee from meddling with God, who is with me, that he destroy thee not.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="35.21 the house…: Heb. the house of my war">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 5:19; 2 Chronicles 18:29; 1 Kings 22:30"> Nevertheless Josiah would not turn his face from him, but disguised himself, that he might fight with him, and hearkened not unto the words of Necho from the mouth of God, and came to fight in the valley of Megiddo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 22:34; 2 Kings 9:24; Genesis 49:23"> And the archers shot at king Josiah; and the king said to his servants, Have me away; for I am sore wounded.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="35.23 wounded: Heb. made sick">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 12:11; 2 Kings 23:30; Psalms 36:6"> His servants therefore took him out of that chariot, and put him in the second chariot that he had; and they brought him to Jerusalem, and he died, and was buried in one of the sepulchres of his fathers. And all Judah and Jerusalem mourned for Josiah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="35.24 in one…: or, among the sepulchres">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Lamentations 4:20; Jeremiah 22:10; Job 3:8"> And Jeremiah lamented for Josiah: and all the singing men and the singing women spake of Josiah in their lamentations to this day, and made them an ordinance in Israel: and, behold, they are written in the lamentations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:20; 2 Chronicles 32:32"> Now the rest of the acts of Josiah, and his goodness, according to that which was written in the law of the LORD,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="35.26 goodness: Heb. kindnesses">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:22; 2 Chronicles 25:26; 2 Kings 10:34"> And his deeds, first and last, behold, they are written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah.</span>
