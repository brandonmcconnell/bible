<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:5; 2 Chronicles 21:6; 2 Chronicles 19:2"> Now Jehoshaphat had riches and honour in abundance, and joined affinity with Ahab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 9:1; 2 Chronicles 19:2; 1 Kings 1:9"> And after certain years he went down to Ahab to Samaria. And Ahab killed sheep and oxen for him in abundance, and for the people that he had with him, and persuaded him to go up with him to Ramoth-gilead.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="18.2 after…: Heb. at the end of years">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 22:4; 2 Kings 3:7; 2 John 1:10–11"> And Ahab king of Israel said unto Jehoshaphat king of Judah, Wilt thou go with me to Ramoth-gilead? And he answered him, I am as thou art, and my people as thy people; and we will be with thee in the war.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:2; 2 Samuel 5:19; 2 Samuel 2:1"> And Jehoshaphat said unto the king of Israel, Enquire, I pray thee, at the word of the LORD to day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3; Micah 3:11; Jeremiah 28:1–17"> Therefore the king of Israel gathered together of prophets four hundred men, and said unto them, Shall we go to Ramoth-gilead to battle, or shall I forbear? And they said, Go up; for God will deliver it into the king’s hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 22:7–9; 2 Kings 3:11–13"> But Jehoshaphat said, Is there not here a prophet of the LORD besides, that we might enquire of him?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="18.6 besides: Heb. yet, or, more">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Micah 2:7; 1 Kings 21:20"> And the king of Israel said unto Jehoshaphat, There is yet one man, by whom we may enquire of the LORD: but I hate him; for he never prophesied good unto me, but always evil: the same is Micaiah the son of Imla. And Jehoshaphat said, Let not the king say so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 22:9; Isaiah 39:7; 2 Chronicles 18:25–26"> And the king of Israel called for one of his officers, and said, Fetch quickly Micaiah the son of Imla.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="18.8 officers: or, eunuchs">c</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="18.8 Fetch…: Heb. Hasten">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 14:9; Daniel 7:9; Matthew 19:28"> And the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah sat either of them on his throne, clothed in their robes, and they sat in a void place at the entering in of the gate of Samaria; and all the prophets prophesied before them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="18.9 void…: or, floor">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:17; Zechariah 1:18–21; Jeremiah 28:2–3"> And Zedekiah the son of Chenaanah had made him horns of iron, and said, Thus saith the LORD, With these thou shalt push Syria until they be consumed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="18.10 they…: Heb. thou consume them">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 24:24–25; 2 Chronicles 18:33–34; Jude 1:16"> And all the prophets prophesied so, saying, Go up to Ramoth-gilead, and prosper: for the LORD shall deliver it into the hand of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 2:6; Psalms 10:11; Micah 2:11"> And the messenger that went to call Micaiah spake to him, saying, Behold, the words of the prophets declare good to the king with one assent; let thy word therefore, I pray thee, be like one of theirs, and speak thou good.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="18.12 with…: Heb. with one mouth">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 22:35; Numbers 24:13; Numbers 22:18–20"> And Micaiah said, As the LORD liveth, even what my God saith, that will I speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 22:15; Lamentations 4:21; Amos 4:4–5"> And when he was come to the king, the king said unto him, Micaiah, shall we go to Ramoth-gilead to battle, or shall I forbear? And he said, Go ye up, and prosper, and they shall be delivered into your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 26:63; 1 Samuel 14:24; 1 Kings 22:16"> And the king said to him, How many times shall I adjure thee that thou say nothing but the truth to me in the name of the LORD?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Ezekiel 34:5–6; Numbers 27:17"> Then he said, I did see all Israel scattered upon the mountains, as sheep that have no shepherd: and the LORD said, These have no master; let them return therefore every man to his house in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:7; 1 Kings 22:18; Proverbs 29:1"> And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, Did I not tell thee that he would not prophesy good unto me, but evil?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="18.17 but evil: or, but for evil">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 7:9; Isaiah 6:1–5; 1 Kings 22:19–23"> Again he said, Therefore hear the word of the LORD; I saw the LORD sitting upon his throne, and all the host of heaven standing on his right hand and on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:9; 2 Chronicles 25:19; Proverbs 11:5"> And the LORD said, Who shall entice Ahab king of Israel, that he may go up and fall at Ramoth-gilead? And one spake saying after this manner, and another saying after that manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 1:6; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> Then there came out a spirit, and stood before the LORD, and said, I will entice him. And the LORD said unto him, Wherewith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 8:44; 1 Kings 22:21–22; 1 John 4:6"> And he said, I will go out, and be a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets. And the Lord said, Thou shalt entice him, and thou shalt also prevail: go out, and do even so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:14; Ezekiel 14:9; Job 12:16"> Now therefore, behold, the LORD hath put a lying spirit in the mouth of these thy prophets, and the LORD hath spoken evil against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Jeremiah 20:2; 2 Chronicles 18:10"> Then Zedekiah the son of Chenaanah came near, and smote Micaiah upon the cheek, and said, Which way went the Spirit of the LORD from me to speak unto thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:21–22; Isaiah 26:20; Jeremiah 28:16–17"> And Micaiah said, Behold, thou shalt see on that day when thou shalt go into an inner chamber to hide thyself.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="18.24 into…: or, from chamber to chamber: Heb. a chamber in a chamber">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:8; 2 Chronicles 34:8; Jeremiah 38:6–7"> Then the king of Israel said, Take ye Micaiah, and carry him back to Amon the governor of the city, and to Joash the king’s son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:10; Psalms 80:5; Luke 23:2"> And say, Thus saith the king, Put this fellow in the prison, and feed him with bread of affliction and with water of affliction, until I return in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 20:45–46; Amos 9:10; Micah 1:2"> And Micaiah said, If thou certainly return in peace, then hath not the LORD spoken by me. And he said, Hearken, all ye people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 22:29–33"> So the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat the king of Judah went up to Ramoth-gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:8; Jeremiah 23:24; Proverbs 26:25"> And the king of Israel said unto Jehoshaphat, I will disguise myself, and will go to the battle; but put thou on thy robes. So the king of Israel disguised himself; and they went to the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:11; Deuteronomy 1:17; 1 Kings 20:42"> Now the king of Syria had commanded the captains of the chariots that were with him, saying, Fight ye not with small or great, save only with the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:14; Psalms 118:13; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> And it came to pass, when the captains of the chariots saw Jehoshaphat, that they said, It is the king of Israel. Therefore they compassed about him to fight: but Jehoshaphat cried out, and the LORD helped him; and God moved them to depart from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 22:33"> For it came to pass, that, when the captains of the chariots perceived that it was not the king of Israel, they turned back again from pursuing him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="18.32 from…: Heb. from after him">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:23; 2 Samuel 15:11; 1 Kings 22:34–35"> And a certain man drew a bow at a venture, and smote the king of Israel between the joints of the harness: therefore he said to his chariot man, Turn thine hand, that thou mayest carry me out of the host; for I am wounded.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="18.33 at a venture: Heb. in his simplicity">k</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="18.33 between…: Heb. between the joints and between the breastplate">l</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="18.33 wounded: Heb. made sick">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 13:21; 2 Chronicles 18:27; Proverbs 28:17"> And the battle increased that day: howbeit the king of Israel stayed himself up in his chariot against the Syrians until the even: and about the time of the sun going down he died.</span>
