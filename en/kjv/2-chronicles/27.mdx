<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:14; Nehemiah 3:26–27; Jeremiah 20:2"> He built the high gate of the house of the LORD, and on the wall of Ophel he built much.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="27.3 Ophel: or, the tower">a</a></sup></span>
