<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 22:2; Isaiah 41:10; 2 Timothy 4:22"> And the LORD was with Jehoshaphat, because he walked in the first ways of his father David, and sought not unto Baalim;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="17.3 of…: or, of his father, and of David">a</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:1; Matthew 6:33; Psalms 127:1"> Therefore the LORD stablished the kingdom in his hand; and all Judah brought to Jehoshaphat presents; and he had riches and honour in abundance.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="17.5 brought: Heb. gave">b</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 7:1–6; 2 Chronicles 19:8; Malachi 2:7"> And with them he sent Levites, even Shemaiah, and Nethaniah, and Zebadiah, and Asahel, and Shemiramoth, and Jehonathan, and Adonijah, and Tobijah, and Tob-adonijah, Levites; and with them Elishama and Jehoram, priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 5:46; Romans 3:2; Acts 8:40"> And they taught in Judah, and had the book of the law of the LORD with them, and went about throughout all the cities of Judah, and taught the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:14; Exodus 15:14–16; Proverbs 16:7"> And the fear of the LORD fell upon all the kingdoms of the lands that were round about Judah, so that they made no war against Jehoshaphat.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="17.10 fell: Heb. was">d</a></sup></span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:2–6; 2 Chronicles 14:6–7; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> And Jehoshaphat waxed great exceedingly; and he built in Judah castles, and cities of store.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="17.12 castles: or, palaces">e</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And next to him was Jehohanan the captain, and with him two hundred and fourscore thousand.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="17.15 next…: Heb. at his hand">f</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:8; 2 Samuel 1:21–22"> And of Benjamin; Eliada a mighty man of valour, and with him armed men with bow and shield two hundred thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And next him was Jehozabad, and with him an hundred and fourscore thousand ready prepared for the war.</span>
