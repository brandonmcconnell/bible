<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 2:41; Joshua 18:1; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> And this man went up out of his city yearly to worship and to sacrifice unto the LORD of hosts in Shiloh. <span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:2; Genesis 29:30–31; Genesis 45:22"> But unto Hannah he gave a worthy portion; for he loved Hannah: but the LORD had shut up her womb.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.5 worthy: or, double">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 6:14; Job 24:21; Leviticus 18:18"> And her adversary also provoked her sore, for to make her fret, because the LORD had shut up her womb.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.6 provoked: Heb. angered">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:19"> And as he did so year by year, when she went up to the house of the LORD, so she provoked her; therefore she wept, and did not eat.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.7 when…: or, from the time that she, etc: Heb. from her going up">d</a></sup></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:3; Psalms 27:4; Psalms 5:7"> So Hannah rose up after they had eaten in Shiloh, and after they had drunk. Now Eli the priest sat upon a seat by a post of the temple of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 7:11; Luke 22:44; Job 10:1"> And she was in bitterness of soul, and prayed unto the LORD, and wept sore.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.10 in…: Heb. bitter of soul">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 13:5; Numbers 6:5; Genesis 29:32"> And she vowed a vow, and said, O LORD of hosts, if thou wilt indeed look on the affliction of thine handmaid, and remember me, and not forget thine handmaid, but wilt give unto thine handmaid a man child, then I will give him unto the LORD all the days of his life, and there shall no razor come upon his head.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.11 a man…: Heb. seed of men">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:17; James 5:16; Luke 18:1"> And it came to pass, as she continued praying before the LORD, that Eli marked her mouth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="1.12 continued…: Heb. multiplied to pray">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 8:26; Acts 2:13; Genesis 24:42–45"> Now Hannah, she spake in her heart; only her lips moved, but her voice was not heard: therefore Eli thought she had been drunken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Job 8:2; Proverbs 6:9"> And Eli said unto her, How long wilt thou be drunken? put away thy wine from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Lamentations 2:19; Psalms 42:4"> And Hannah answered and said, No, my lord, I am a woman of a sorrowful spirit: I have drunk neither wine nor strong drink, but have poured out my soul before the LORD.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="1.15 of a sorrowful…: Heb. hard of spirit">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:12; Job 6:2–3; Matthew 12:34–35"> Count not thine handmaid for a daughter of Belial: for out of the abundance of my complaint and grief have I spoken hitherto.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="1.16 complaint: or, meditation">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Judges 18:6; Psalms 20:3–5"> Then Eli answered and said, Go in peace: and the God of Israel grant thee thy petition that thou hast asked of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Romans 15:13; Ecclesiastes 9:7"> And she said, Let thine handmaid find grace in thy sight. So the woman went her way, and did eat, and her countenance was no more sad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 41:51–52; Matthew 1:21; Exodus 2:10"> Wherefore it came to pass, when the time was come about after Hannah had conceived, that she bare a son, and called his name Samuel, saying, Because I have asked him of the LORD.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="1.20 when…: Heb. in revolution of days">j</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="1.20 Samuel: that is, Asked of God">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:3; Deuteronomy 12:11; Joshua 24:15"> And the man Elkanah, and all his house, went up to offer unto the LORD the yearly sacrifice, and his vow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:28; 1 Samuel 1:11; Luke 2:22"> But Hannah went not up; for she said unto her husband, I will not go up until the child be weaned, and then I will bring him, that he may appear before the LORD, and there abide for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:25; Luke 11:27; Genesis 21:7–8"> And Elkanah her husband said unto her, Do what seemeth thee good; tarry until thou have weaned him; only the LORD establish his word. So the woman abode, and gave her son suck until she weaned him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 15:9–10; Joshua 18:1; 1 Samuel 4:3–4"> And when she had weaned him, she took him up with her, with three bullocks, and one ephah of flour, and a bottle of wine, and brought him unto the house of the LORD in Shiloh: and the child was young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 18:15–16; Luke 2:22"> And they slew a bullock, and brought the child to Eli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 4:30; 2 Kings 2:4; 2 Kings 2:6"> And she said, Oh my lord, as thy soul liveth, my lord, I am the woman that stood by thee here, praying unto the LORD.</span>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 24:26; Genesis 24:52; 2 Timothy 3:15"> Therefore also I have lent him to the LORD; as long as he liveth he shall be lent to the LORD.
