<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 5:10; 1 Samuel 10:2; Judges 10:4"> And the asses of Kish Saul’s father were lost. And Kish said to Saul his son, Take now one of the servants with thee, and arise, go seek the asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42; Joshua 24:33; John 3:23"> And he passed through mount Ephraim, and passed through the land of Shalisha, but they found them not: then they passed through the land of Shalim, and there they were not: and he passed through the land of the Benjamites, but they found them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:2; 1 Samuel 1:1; Luke 12:11"> And when they were come to the land of Zuph, Saul said to his servant that was with him, Come, and let us return; lest my father leave caring for the asses, and take thought for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 13:1; Deuteronomy 33:1; 1 Timothy 6:11"> And he said unto him, Behold now, there is in this city a man of God, and he is an honourable man; all that he saith cometh surely to pass: now let us go thither; peradventure he can shew us our way that we should go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 14:3; 2 Kings 8:8; 2 Kings 4:42"> Then said Saul to his servant, But, behold, if we go, what shall we bring the man? for the bread is spent in our vessels, and there is not a present to bring to the man of God: what have we?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="9.7 is spent…: Heb. is gone out of, etc">c</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="9.7 have we: Heb. is with us?">d</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 5:13–14"> Then said Saul to his servant, Well said; come, let us go. So they went unto the city where the man of God was.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="9.10 Well said: Heb. Thy word is good">f</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:11; Matthew 26:26; Luke 24:30"> As soon as ye be come into the city, ye shall straightway find him, before he go up to the high place to eat: for the people will not eat until he come, because he doth bless the sacrifice; and afterwards they eat that be bidden. Now therefore get you up; for about this time ye shall find him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="9.13 this time: Heb. to day">i</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 13:11; 1 Samuel 16:6–12; Nehemiah 13:25"> And when Samuel saw Saul, the LORD said unto him, Behold the man whom I spake to thee of! this same shall reign over my people.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="9.17 reign over: Heb. restrain in">k</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:5; 1 Samuel 12:13; 1 Samuel 9:3"> And as for thine asses that were lost three days ago, set not thy mind on them; for they are found. <span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:17; Judges 20:46–48; Psalms 68:27"> And Saul answered and said, Am not I a Benjamite, of the smallest of the tribes of Israel? and my family the least of all the families of the tribe of Benjamin? wherefore then speakest thou so to me?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="9.21 so…: Heb. according to this word">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 43:32; Luke 14:10"> And Samuel took Saul and his servant, and brought them into the parlour, and made them sit in the chiefest place among them that were bidden, which were about thirty persons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:5; Genesis 43:34"> And Samuel said unto the cook, Bring the portion which I gave thee, of which I said unto thee, Set it by thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:4; Leviticus 7:32–33; Exodus 29:22"> And the cook took up the shoulder, and that which was upon it, and set it before Saul.
