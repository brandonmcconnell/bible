<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:12; 2 Corinthians 12:7; 1 John 5:20"> It is not expedient for me doubtless to glory. I will come to visions and revelations of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 22:17; Ezekiel 8:1–3; 1 Thessalonians 4:17"> I knew a man in Christ above fourteen years ago, (whether in the body, I cannot tell; or whether out of the body, I cannot tell: God knoweth;) such an one caught up to the third heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And I knew such a man, (whether in the body, or out of the body, I cannot tell: God knoweth;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 23:43; Revelation 2:7; Ezekiel 31:9"> How that he was caught up into paradise, and heard unspeakable words, which it is not lawful for a man to utter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:30; 1 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Of such an one will I glory: yet of myself I will not glory, but in mine infirmities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:16; 2 Corinthians 11:31; 2 Corinthians 12:11"> For though I would desire to glory, I shall not be a fool; for I will say the truth: but now I forbear, lest any man should think of me above that which he seeth me to be, or that he heareth of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:55; Judges 2:3; 2 Chronicles 32:25–26"> And lest I should be exalted above measure through the abundance of the revelations, there was given to me a thorn in the flesh, the messenger of Satan to buffet me, lest I should be exalted above measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Matthew 26:39–44; 1 Samuel 15:11"> For this thing I besought the Lord thrice, that it might depart from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 12:10; Isaiah 40:29–31"> And he said unto me,<span className="woj">My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness.</span>Most gladly therefore will I rather glory in my infirmities, that the power of Christ may rest upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9; Ephesians 6:10; Romans 5:3"> Therefore I take pleasure in infirmities, in reproaches, in necessities, in persecutions, in distresses for Christ’s sake: for when I am weak, then am I strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:5; 2 Corinthians 11:1; 1 Corinthians 15:8–10"> I am become a fool in glorying; ye have compelled me: for I ought to have been commended of you: for in nothing am I behind the very chiefest apostles, though I be nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:18–19; 2 Corinthians 11:4; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> Truly the signs of an apostle were wrought among you in all patience, in signs, and wonders, and mighty deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Corinthians 9:6; 2 Corinthians 11:7–9"> For what is it wherein ye were inferior to other churches, except it be that I myself was not burdensome to you? forgive me this wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 19:14; Genesis 24:35–36; 1 Corinthians 10:33"> Behold, the third time I am ready to come to you; and I will not be burdensome to you: for I seek not yours, but you: for the children ought not to lay up for the parents, but the parents for the children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:8; 2 Corinthians 1:6; Philippians 2:17"> And I will very gladly spend and be spent for you; though the more abundantly I love you, the less I be loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:5; 2 Corinthians 10:2–3; 2 Corinthians 12:13"> But be it so, I did not burden you: nevertheless, being crafty, I caught you with guile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:5; 1 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Kings 5:20–27"> Did I make a gain of you by any of them whom I sent unto you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; Romans 4:12; Acts 20:33–35"> I desired Titus, and with him I sent a brother. Did Titus make a gain of you? walked we not in the same spirit? walked we not in the same steps?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 9:1; 1 Corinthians 14:26; 2 Corinthians 10:8"> Again, think ye that we excuse ourselves unto you? we speak before God in Christ: but we do all things, dearly beloved, for your edifying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 5:19–21; 1 Peter 2:1; Romans 1:29"> For I fear, lest, when I come, I shall not find you such as I would, and that I shall be found unto you such as ye would not: lest there be debates, envyings, wraths, strifes, backbitings, whisperings, swellings, tumults:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:2; 1 Peter 4:2–3; Colossians 3:5"> And lest, when I come again, my God will humble me among you, and that I shall bewail many which have sinned already, and have not repented of the uncleanness and fornication and lasciviousness which they have committed.</span>
