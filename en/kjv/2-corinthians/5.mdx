<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 19:25–26; 1 John 3:2; 2 Peter 1:13–14"> For we know that if our earthly house of this tabernacle were dissolved, we have a building of God, an house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 8:23; 1 Corinthians 15:53–54; 2 Corinthians 5:3–4"> For in this we groan, earnestly desiring to be clothed upon with our house which is from heaven:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:7–11; Revelation 16:15; Revelation 3:18"> If so be that being clothed we shall not be found naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:53–54; 2 Corinthians 5:2; Isaiah 25:8"> For we that are in this tabernacle do groan, being burdened: not for that we would be unclothed, but clothed upon, that mortality might be swallowed up of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:22; Ephesians 1:13–14; Romans 8:23"> Now he that hath wrought us for the selfsame thing is God, who also hath given unto us the earnest of the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:1; Philippians 3:20–21; Hebrews 11:13"> Therefore we are always confident, knowing that, whilst we are at home in the body, we are absent from the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:1–27; 2 Corinthians 4:18; Romans 8:24–25"> (For we walk by faith, not by sight:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:20–24; 2 Corinthians 5:6; John 14:3"> We are confident, I say, and willing rather to be absent from the body, and to be present with the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; 2 Peter 3:14; Romans 14:18"> Wherefore we labour, that, whether present or absent, we may be accepted of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Revelation 22:12; Ephesians 6:8"> For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ; that every one may receive the things done in his body, according to that he hath done, whether it be good or bad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jude 1:23; Hebrews 10:31; 2 Timothy 2:24–26"> Knowing therefore the terror of the Lord, we persuade men; but we are made manifest unto God; and I trust also are made manifest in your consciences.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:1; 2 Corinthians 1:14; Galatians 6:12–14"> For we commend not ourselves again unto you, but give you occasion to glory on our behalf, that ye may have somewhat to answer them which glory in appearance, and not in heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:16–17; 2 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Corinthians 12:6"> For whether we be beside ourselves, it is to God: or whether we be sober, it is for your cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; Romans 14:7–9; Matthew 10:37–38"> For the love of Christ constraineth us; because we thus judge, that if one died for all, then were all dead:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 14:7–9; 1 Peter 4:2–4; 1 Peter 1:14–15"> And that he died for all, that they which live should not henceforth live unto themselves, but unto him which died for them, and rose again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Philippians 3:7–8; John 6:63"> Wherefore henceforth know we no man after the flesh: yea, though we have known Christ after the flesh, yet now henceforth know we him no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26; Ephesians 4:22–24; Isaiah 43:18–19"> Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:20–21; 2 Corinthians 5:19–20; Romans 5:10–11"> And all things are of God, who hath reconciled us to himself by Jesus Christ, and hath given to us the ministry of reconciliation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 44:22; 1 John 2:1–2"> To wit, that God was in Christ, reconciling the world unto himself, not imputing their trespasses unto them; and hath committed unto us the word of reconciliation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; Luke 14:23; Malachi 2:7"> Now then we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God did beseech you by us: we pray you in Christ’s stead, be ye reconciled to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 2:22–24; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 5:19"> For he hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him.</span>
