<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:8; 2 Corinthians 2:3; Titus 1:13"> Therefore I write these things being absent, lest being present I should use sharpness, according to the power which the Lord hath given me to edification, and not to destruction.</span>
