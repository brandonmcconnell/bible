<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:10; Luke 23:28–29; Lamentations 2:20"> Even the sea monsters draw out the breast, they give suck to their young ones: the daughter of my people is become cruel, like the ostriches in the wilderness.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.3 sea…: or, sea calves">a</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 6:3–7; Luke 16:19; Luke 15:16"> They that did feed delicately are desolate in the streets: they that were brought up in scarlet embrace dunghills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 19:25; Luke 12:47; Luke 10:12"> For the punishment of the iniquity of the daughter of my people is greater than the punishment of the sin of Sodom, that was overthrown as in a moment, and no hands stayed on her.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.6 punishment of the iniquity: or, iniquity">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 51:7; Song of Solomon 5:10; Psalms 144:12"> Her Nazarites were purer than snow, they were whiter than milk, they were more ruddy in body than rubies, their polishing was of sapphire:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 5:10; Psalms 102:3–5; Job 30:30"> Their visage is blacker than a coal; they are not known in the streets: their skin cleaveth to their bones; it is withered, it is become like a stick.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.8 blacker…: Heb. darker than blackness">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:23; Leviticus 26:39; Jeremiah 16:4"> They that be slain with the sword are better than they that be slain with hunger: for these pine away, stricken through for want of the fruits of the field.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.9 pine…: Heb. flow out">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 2:20; Isaiah 49:15; 2 Kings 6:26–29"> The hands of the pitiful women have sodden their own children: they were their meat in the destruction of the daughter of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:20; Ezekiel 22:31; Jeremiah 17:27"> The LORD hath accomplished his fury; he hath poured out his fierce anger, and hath kindled a fire in Zion, and it hath devoured the foundations thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Deuteronomy 29:24–28; Psalms 48:4–6"> The kings of the earth, and all the inhabitants of the world, would not have believed that the adversary and the enemy should have entered into the gates of Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:34; Isaiah 56:10; Isaiah 1:15"> They have wandered as blind men in the streets, they have polluted themselves with blood, so that men could not touch their garments.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="4.14 so…: or, in that they could not but touch">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46"> They cried unto them, Depart ye; it is unclean; depart, depart, touch not: when they fled away and wandered, they said among the heathen, They shall no more sojourn there.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="4.15 it…: or, ye polluted">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 5:12; Isaiah 9:14–16; Deuteronomy 28:64–65"> The anger of the LORD hath divided them; he will no more regard them: they respected not the persons of the priests, they favoured not the elders.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="4.16 anger: or, face">g</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 8:2; Ezekiel 7:2–12; Lamentations 3:52"> They hunt our steps, that we cannot go in our streets: our end is near, our days are fulfilled; for our end is come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Jeremiah 4:13; Isaiah 5:26–28"> Our persecutors are swifter than the eagles of the heaven: they pursued us upon the mountains, they laid wait for us in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:21; Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 39:5"> The breath of our nostrils, the anointed of the LORD, was taken in their pits, of whom we said, Under his shadow we shall live among the heathen.</span>
</div>
