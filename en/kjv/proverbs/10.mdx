<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:1; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 17:25"> The proverbs of Solomon. A wise son maketh a glad father: but a foolish son is the heaviness of his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Proverbs 12:28; Psalms 49:6–10"> Treasures of wickedness profit nothing: but righteousness delivereth from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 37:3; Matthew 6:30–33; Psalms 37:25"> The LORD will not suffer the soul of the righteous to famish: but he casteth away the substance of the wicked.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="10.3 the substance…: or, the wicked for their wickedness">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 21:5; Proverbs 20:4; Proverbs 19:15"> He becometh poor that dealeth with a slack hand: but the hand of the diligent maketh rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 6:8; Proverbs 30:25; Proverbs 17:2"> He that gathereth in summer is a wise son: but he that sleepeth in harvest is a son that causeth shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:2; Proverbs 28:20; Job 29:13"> Blessings are upon the head of the just: but violence covereth the mouth of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 112:6; Luke 1:48; Mark 14:9"> The memory of the just is blessed: but the name of the wicked shall rot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 7:24–25; Proverbs 12:1; James 3:13"> The wise in heart will receive commandments: but a prating fool shall fall.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="10.8 a prating…: Heb. a fool of lips">b</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="10.8 fall: Heb. be beaten">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Matthew 10:26; Psalms 26:11–12"> He that walketh uprightly walketh surely: but he that perverteth his ways shall be known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Proverbs 6:13; 3 John 1:10"> He that winketh with the eye causeth sorrow: but a prating fool shall fall.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="10.10 fall: Heb. be beaten">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:14; Ephesians 4:29; Proverbs 10:6"> The mouth of a righteous man is a well of life: but violence covereth the mouth of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7; Proverbs 17:9"> Hatred stirreth up strifes: but love covereth all sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 26:3; Proverbs 6:32; Isaiah 50:4"> In the lips of him that hath understanding wisdom is found: but a rod is for the back of him that is void of understanding.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="10.13 of understanding: Heb. of heart">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 10:8; Proverbs 13:3"> Wise men lay up knowledge: but the mouth of the foolish is near destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 18:11; Proverbs 19:7; Psalms 52:7"> The rich man’s wealth is his strong city: the destruction of the poor is their poverty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 6:23; Isaiah 3:10–11; Hebrews 6:10"> The labour of the righteous tendeth to life: the fruit of the wicked to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–11; Luke 11:28; Proverbs 6:23"> He is in the way of life that keepeth instruction: but he that refuseth reproof erreth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="10.17 erreth: or, causeth to err">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–26; Psalms 55:21; 2 Samuel 3:27"> He that hideth hatred with lying lips, and he that uttereth a slander, is a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 1:19; James 3:2; Proverbs 17:27–28"> In the multitude of words there wanteth not sin: but he that refraineth his lips is wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Proverbs 12:18; Genesis 6:5"> The tongue of the just is as choice silver: the heart of the wicked is little worth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; Proverbs 17:16; Proverbs 1:29"> The lips of the righteous feed many: but fools die for want of wisdom.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="10.21 of wisdom: Heb. of heart">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 107:38; Genesis 26:12; 1 Samuel 2:7–8"> The blessing of the LORD, it maketh rich, and he addeth no sorrow with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:21; Proverbs 2:14; Proverbs 14:9"> It is as sport to a fool to do mischief: but a man of understanding hath wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; Isaiah 66:4; Matthew 5:6"> The fear of the wicked, it shall come upon him: but the desire of the righteous shall be granted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 15:5; Psalms 58:9; Matthew 7:24–27"> As the whirlwind passeth, so is the wicked no more: but the righteous is an everlasting foundation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 25:13; Proverbs 26:6; Matthew 25:26"> As vinegar to the teeth, and as smoke to the eyes, so is the sluggard to them that send him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 9:11; Proverbs 3:2; Psalms 91:16"> The fear of the LORD prolongeth days: but the years of the wicked shall be shortened.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="10.27 prolongeth: Heb. addeth">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 8:13; Romans 15:13; Psalms 73:24–26"> The hope of the righteous shall be gladness: but the expectation of the wicked shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 21:15; Romans 2:8–9; Philippians 4:13"> The way of the LORD is strength to the upright: but destruction shall be to the workers of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 37:22; Psalms 125:1; Psalms 37:9–10"> The righteous shall never be removed: but the wicked shall not inhabit the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 37:30; Proverbs 10:13; Proverbs 10:20–21"> The mouth of the just bringeth forth wisdom: but the froward tongue shall be cut out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Proverbs 12:6; Proverbs 11:11"> The lips of the righteous know what is acceptable: but the mouth of the wicked speaketh frowardness.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="10.32 frowardness: Heb. frowardnesses">i</a></sup></span>
