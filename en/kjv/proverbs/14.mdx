<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 12:6; James 3:5–6; Revelation 12:11"> In the mouth of the foolish is a rod of pride: but the lips of the wise shall preserve them.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 16:25; Matthew 7:13–14; Proverbs 12:15"> There is a way which seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:2; James 4:9; Ecclesiastes 2:10–11"> Even in laughter the heart is sorrowful; and the end of that mirth is heaviness.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 5:17; Proverbs 14:8; 1 John 4:1"> The simple believeth every word: but the prudent man looketh well to his going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 22:3; Proverbs 3:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> A wise man feareth, and departeth from evil: but the fool rageth, and is confident.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; James 1:19; Ecclesiastes 7:9"> He that is soon angry dealeth foolishly: and a man of wicked devices is hated.</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Proverbs 28:27; Proverbs 11:12"> He that despiseth his neighbour sinneth: but he that hath mercy on the poor, happy is he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 12:2; John 1:17; Psalms 61:7"> Do they not err that devise evil? but mercy and truth shall be to them that devise good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 28:19; Proverbs 12:24; 2 Thessalonians 3:10–12"> In all labour there is profit: but the talk of the lips tendeth only to penury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:11–12; Luke 12:19–20; Luke 16:19–25"> The crown of the wise is their riches: but the foolishness of fools is folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 14:5; Acts 26:16–20; 2 Peter 3:3"> A true witness delivereth souls: but a deceitful witness speaketh lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 19:23; Jeremiah 32:39–40; Psalms 115:13–14"> In the fear of the LORD is strong confidence: and his children shall have a place of refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 21:6; Proverbs 13:14; Proverbs 2:10–18"> The fear of the LORD is a fountain of life, to depart from the snares of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 4:20–21; 2 Kings 13:7; Exodus 1:22"> In the multitude of people is the king’s honour: but in the want of people is the destruction of the prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 1:19; Ecclesiastes 7:9; Proverbs 14:17"> He that is slow to wrath is of great understanding: but he that is hasty of spirit exalteth folly.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="14.29 hasty…: Heb. short of spirit">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 17:22; Proverbs 4:23; Proverbs 3:8"> A sound heart is the life of the flesh: but envy the rottenness of the bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 22:2; Proverbs 17:5; Proverbs 22:16"> He that oppresseth the poor reproacheth his Maker: but he that honoureth him hath mercy on the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:18; 2 Corinthians 5:8; Psalms 23:4"> The wicked is driven away in his wickedness: but the righteous hath hope in his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 12:16; Proverbs 13:16; Proverbs 12:23"> Wisdom resteth in the heart of him that hath understanding: but that which is in the midst of fools is made known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Deuteronomy 28:1–68; Deuteronomy 29:18–28"> Righteousness exalteth a nation: but sin is a reproach to any people.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="14.34 to any…: Heb. to nations">d</a></sup></span>
