<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:29; Romans 11:33–34; Job 11:7–8"> It is the glory of God to conceal a thing: but the honour of kings is to search out a matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 55:9; Psalms 103:11; 1 Kings 4:29"> The heaven for height, and the earth for depth, and the heart of kings is unsearchable.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="25.3 is…: Heb. there is no searching">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:20–21; Ezekiel 22:18; Proverbs 17:3"> Take away the dross from the silver, and there shall come forth a vessel for the finer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 16:12; Proverbs 20:8; Psalms 101:7–8"> Take away the wicked from before the king, and his throne shall be established in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; Proverbs 25:27; Proverbs 16:19"> Put not forth thyself in the presence of the king, and stand not in the place of great men:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="25.6 Put…: Heb. <span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 5:25; Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 18:6"> Go not forth hastily to strive, lest thou know not what to do in the end thereof, when thy neighbour hath put thee to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 18:5–17; Proverbs 11:13; Proverbs 20:19"> Debate thy cause with thy neighbour himself; and discover not a secret to another:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="25.9 a secret…: or, the secret of">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:39"> Lest he that heareth it put thee to shame, and thine infamy turn not away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Isaiah 50:4; Ecclesiastes 12:10"> A word fitly spoken is like apples of gold in pictures of silver.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="25.11 fitly…: Heb. spoken upon his wheels">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 141:5; Proverbs 15:31–32; Proverbs 15:5"> As an earring of gold, and an ornament of fine gold, so is a wise reprover upon an obedient ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 13:17; Proverbs 25:25; Philippians 2:25–30"> As the cold of snow in the time of harvest, so is a faithful messenger to them that send him: for he refresheth the soul of his masters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:6; Luke 14:11; 2 Peter 2:15–19"> Whoso boasteth himself of a false gift is like clouds and wind without rain.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="25.14 of a…: Heb. in a gift of falsehood">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Ecclesiastes 10:4; Proverbs 16:14"> By long forbearing is a prince persuaded, and a soft tongue breaketh the bone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 25:27; Ephesians 5:18; Proverbs 24:13–14"> Hast thou found honey? eat so much as is sufficient for thee, lest thou be filled therewith, and vomit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 19:2–3; Judges 19:18–21; Romans 15:24"> Withdraw thy foot from thy neighbour’s house; lest he be weary of thee, and so hate thee.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="25.17 Withdraw…: or, Let thy foot be seldom in">f</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="25.17 weary…: Heb. full of thee">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Proverbs 12:18; James 3:6"> A man that beareth false witness against his neighbour is a maul, and a sword, and a sharp arrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Ezekiel 29:6–7; Job 6:14–20"> Confidence in an unfaithful man in time of trouble is like a broken tooth, and a foot out of joint.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Ecclesiastes 3:4; Isaiah 58:7"> As he that taketh away a garment in cold weather, and as vinegar upon nitre, so is he that singeth songs to an heavy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Matthew 5:44; Exodus 23:4–5"> If thine enemy be hungry, give him bread to eat; and if he be thirsty, give him water to drink:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Matthew 10:13; 1 Corinthians 15:18"> For thou shalt heap coals of fire upon his head, and the LORD shall reward thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 37:22; Psalms 15:3; Romans 1:30"> The north wind driveth away rain: so doth an angry countenance a backbiting tongue.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="25.23 driveth…: or, bringeth forth rain: so doth a backbiting tongue an angry countenance">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 21:9; Proverbs 27:15–16"> It is better to dwell in the corner of the housetop, than with a brawling woman and in a wide house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 15:30; Romans 10:15; Psalms 63:1"> As cold waters to a thirsty soul, so is good news from a far country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:18–19; 1 Samuel 22:14–18; 1 Thessalonians 2:15"> A righteous man falling down before the wicked is as a troubled fountain, and a corrupt spring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 5:44; Proverbs 27:2; Philippians 2:3"> It is not good to eat much honey: so for men to search their own glory is not glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 16:32; Proverbs 22:24; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> He that hath no rule over his own spirit is like a city that is broken down, and without walls.</span>
