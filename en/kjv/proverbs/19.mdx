<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 21:11; Proverbs 17:10; Proverbs 15:5"> Smite a scorner, and the simple will beware: and reprove one that hath understanding, and he will understand knowledge.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="19.25 will beware: Heb. will be cunning">h</a></sup></span>
