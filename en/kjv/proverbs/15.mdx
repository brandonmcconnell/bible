<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 146:9; Psalms 68:5–6; Proverbs 14:11"> The LORD will destroy the house of the proud: but he will establish the border of the widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Jeremiah 4:14; Proverbs 15:23"> The thoughts of the wicked are an abomination to the LORD: but the words of the pure are pleasant words.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="15.26 pleasant…: Heb. words of pleasantness">g</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15; Proverbs 29:11; Ecclesiastes 5:2"> The heart of the righteous studieth to answer: but the mouth of the wicked poureth out evil things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 9:31; Psalms 145:18–19; Proverbs 15:8"> The LORD is far from the wicked: but he heareth the prayer of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 21:23"> The light of the eyes rejoiceth the heart: and a good report maketh the bones fat.</span>
