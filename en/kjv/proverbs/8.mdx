<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:20–21; Matthew 3:3; John 7:37"> Doth not wisdom cry? and understanding put forth her voice?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 9:14; Proverbs 9:3"> She standeth in the top of high places, by the way in the places of the paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 18:20; Acts 5:20; Job 29:7"> She crieth at the gates, at the entry of the city, at the coming in at the doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; John 3:16; Psalms 50:1"> Unto you, O men, I call; and my voice is to the sons of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Acts 26:18; Revelation 3:17–18"> O ye simple, understand wisdom: and, ye fools, be ye of an understanding heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 1:26; Matthew 7:28–29; Proverbs 23:16"> Hear; for I will speak of excellent things; and the opening of my lips shall be right things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:30; John 8:14; John 17:17"> For my mouth shall speak truth; and wickedness is an abomination to my lips.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="8.7 an…: Heb. the abomination of my lips">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:46; Psalms 12:6; Proverbs 8:13"> All the words of my mouth are in righteousness; there is nothing froward or perverse in them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="8.8 froward: Heb. wreathed">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; James 1:5; John 7:17"> They are all plain to him that understandeth, and right to them that find knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 2:4–5; Psalms 119:127; Psalms 119:72"> Receive my instruction, and not silver; and knowledge rather than choice gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14–15; Proverbs 4:5–7; Proverbs 16:16"> For wisdom is better than rubies; and all the things that may be desired are not to be compared to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 28:26; Exodus 35:30–4; Romans 11:33"> I wisdom dwell with prudence, and find out knowledge of witty inventions.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="8.12 prudence: or, subtilty">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 97:10; Proverbs 16:6; 1 Samuel 2:3"> The fear of the LORD is to hate evil: pride, and arrogancy, and the evil way, and the froward mouth, do I hate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 40:14"> Counsel is mine, and sound wisdom: I am understanding; I have strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 13:1; Daniel 2:21; Matthew 28:18"> By me kings reign, and princes decree justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> By me princes rule, and nobles, even all the judges of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 14:23; John 14:21; Psalms 91:14"> I love them that love me; and those that seek me early shall find me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:16; Matthew 6:33; Luke 16:11–12"> Riches and honour are with me; yea, durable riches and righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14; Proverbs 10:20; Ecclesiastes 7:12"> My fruit is better than gold, yea, than fine gold; and my revenue than choice silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; John 10:27–28; Psalms 32:8"> I lead in the way of righteousness, in the midst of the paths of judgment:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="8.20 lead: or, walk">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; Proverbs 24:4; Matthew 25:46"> That I may cause those that love me to inherit substance; and I will fill their treasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 1:17; John 1:1–2; Proverbs 3:19"> The LORD possessed me in the beginning of his way, before his works of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 17:24; Psalms 2:6; Genesis 1:26"> I was set up from everlasting, from the beginning, or ever the earth was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:20; Genesis 1:2; 1 John 4:9"> When there were no depths, I was brought forth; when there were no fountains abounding with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Psalms 102:25–28; Job 38:4–11"> Before the mountains were settled, before the hills was I brought forth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 1:1–31"> While as yet he had not made the earth, nor the fields, nor the highest part of the dust of the world.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="8.26 fields: or, open places">e</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="8.26 the highest…: or, the chief part">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Job 26:10; Proverbs 3:19"> When he prepared the heavens, I was there: when he set a compass upon the face of the depth:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="8.27 a compass: or, a circle">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When he established the clouds above: when he strengthened the fountains of the deep:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 104:9; Jeremiah 5:22; Genesis 1:9–10"> When he gave to the sea his decree, that the waters should not pass his commandment: when he appointed the foundations of the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:1–3; John 1:18; Isaiah 42:1"> Then I was by him, as one brought up with him: and I was daily his delight, rejoicing always before him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 16:3; John 13:1; Psalms 40:6–8"> Rejoicing in the habitable part of his earth; and my delights were with the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Psalms 119:1–2; Psalms 1:1–4"> Now therefore hearken unto me, O ye children: for blessed are they that keep my ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1; Psalms 81:11–12; Proverbs 5:1"> Hear instruction, and be wise, and refuse it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Matthew 7:24; Proverbs 3:13"> Blessed is the man that heareth me, watching daily at my gates, waiting at the posts of my doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12; Proverbs 12:2"> For whoso findeth me findeth life, and shall obtain favour of the LORD.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="8.35 obtain: Heb. bring forth">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 15:32; Proverbs 1:31; 1 Corinthians 16:22"> But he that sinneth against me wrongeth his own soul: all they that hate me love death.</span>
