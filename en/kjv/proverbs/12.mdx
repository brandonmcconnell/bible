<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 15:10; Proverbs 9:7–8; Psalms 119:97–100"> Whoso loveth instruction loveth knowledge: but he that hateth reproof is brutish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 8:35; Acts 11:24; Psalms 112:5"> A good man obtaineth favour of the LORD: but a man of wicked devices will he condemn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 10:25; Ephesians 3:17; Proverbs 12:12"> A man shall not be established by wickedness: but the root of the righteous shall not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 14:30; Proverbs 31:10–25; Proverbs 14:1"> A virtuous woman is a crown to her husband: but she that maketh ashamed is as rottenness in his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 139:23; Isaiah 55:7; Psalms 140:1–3"> The thoughts of the righteous are right: but the counsels of the wicked are deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 14:3; Jeremiah 5:26; Proverbs 1:11–19"> The words of the wicked are to lie in wait for blood: but the mouth of the upright shall deliver them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:24–27; Proverbs 11:21; Proverbs 10:25"> The wicked are overthrown, and are not: but the house of the righteous shall stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:18; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Corinthians 3:10–15"> A man shall be commended according to his wisdom: but he that is of a perverse heart shall be despised.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="12.8 of a…: Heb. perverse of heart">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 14:11; Proverbs 13:7"> He that is despised, and hath a servant, is better than he that honoureth himself, and lacketh bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; Genesis 33:13–14; Numbers 22:28–32"> A righteous man regardeth the life of his beast: but the tender mercies of the wicked are cruel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="12.10 tender…: or, bowels">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 28:19; Proverbs 14:23; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12"> He that tilleth his land shall be satisfied with bread: but he that followeth vain persons is void of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; John 15:5; Luke 8:13–15"> The wicked desireth the net of evil men: but the root of the righteous yieldeth fruit.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="12.12 the net: or, the fortress">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 11:8; Psalms 64:8"> The wicked is snared by the transgression of his lips: but the just shall come out of trouble.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="12.13 The wicked…: Heb. The snare of the wicked is in the transgression of lips">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:20–21; Isaiah 3:10–11; Proverbs 13:2"> A man shall be satisfied with good by the fruit of his mouth: and the recompence of a man’s hands shall be rendered unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 3:7; Proverbs 26:12; Proverbs 14:16"> The way of a fool is right in his own eyes: but he that hearkeneth unto counsel is wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:11; James 1:19; Proverbs 10:12"> A fool’s wrath is presently known: but a prudent man covereth shame.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="12.16 presently: Heb. in that day">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 14:5; Proverbs 14:25; Proverbs 24:28"> He that speaketh truth sheweth forth righteousness: but a false witness deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 15:4; Psalms 57:4"> There is that speaketh like the piercings of a sword: but the tongue of the wise is health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:9; Psalms 52:4–5; Job 20:5"> The lip of truth shall be established for ever: but a lying tongue is but for a moment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; 1 Peter 3:8–13; Proverbs 12:12"> Deceit is in the heart of them that imagine evil: but to the counsellors of peace is joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 3:13; Psalms 91:10; Romans 8:28"> There shall no evil happen to the just: but the wicked shall be filled with mischief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 5:6; Proverbs 11:20; Revelation 22:15"> Lying lips are abomination to the LORD: but they that deal truly are his delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 13:16; Proverbs 11:13; Proverbs 10:19"> A prudent man concealeth knowledge: but the heart of fools proclaimeth foolishness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:27; Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 21:25–26"> The hand of the diligent shall bear rule: but the slothful shall be under tribute.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="12.24 slothful: or, deceitful">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 15:13; Proverbs 17:22"> Heaviness in the heart of man maketh it stoop: but a good word maketh it glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 John 2:26; Matthew 5:46–48; Proverbs 17:27"> The righteous is more excellent than his neighbour: but the way of the wicked seduceth them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="12.26 excellent: or, abundant">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 26:15; Proverbs 15:16"> The slothful man roasteth not that which he took in hunting: but the substance of a diligent man is precious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="3 John 1:11; Romans 5:21; Ezekiel 18:20–24"> In the way of righteousness is life; and in the pathway thereof there is no death.</span>
