<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 25:21; Acts 7:5; Genesis 12:1–3"> And Abram said, Lord GOD, what wilt thou give me, seeing I go childless, and the steward of my house is this Eliezer of Damascus?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:14; Proverbs 29:21; Ecclesiastes 2:7"> And Abram said, Behold, to me thou hast given no seed: and, lo, one born in my house is mine heir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 17:16; Galatians 4:28; 2 Samuel 7:12"> And, behold, the word of the LORD came unto him, saying, This shall not be thine heir; but he that shall come forth out of thine own bowels shall be thine heir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:12; Romans 4:18; Deuteronomy 1:10"> And he brought him forth abroad, and said, Look now toward heaven, and tell the stars, if thou be able to number them: and he said unto him, So shall thy seed be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 2:23; Galatians 3:6–14; Romans 4:9"> And he believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 12:1; Acts 7:2–4; Romans 4:13"> And he said unto him, I am the LORD that brought thee out of Ur of the Chaldees, to give thee this land to inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 1:18; Luke 1:34; Isaiah 7:11"> And he said, Lord GOD, whereby shall I know that I shall inherit it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 2:24; Leviticus 14:30; Leviticus 14:22"> And he said unto him, Take me an heifer of three years old, and a she goat of three years old, and a ram of three years old, and a turtledove, and a young pigeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:18–19; Leviticus 1:17; Genesis 15:17"> And he took unto him all these, and divided them in the midst, and laid each piece one against another: but the birds divided he not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:4; Psalms 119:13; Ezekiel 17:3"> And when the fowls came down upon the carcases, Abram drove them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 2:21; Job 33:15; Daniel 10:8–9"> And when the sun was going down, a deep sleep fell upon Abram; and, lo, an horror of great darkness fell upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:6–7; Exodus 1:11; Exodus 12:40–41"> And he said unto Abram, Know of a surety that thy seed shall be a stranger in a land that is not theirs, and shall serve them; and they shall afflict them four hundred years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:9–11; Exodus 6:5–6; Exodus 12:32–38"> And also that nation, whom they shall serve, will I judge: and afterward shall they come out with great substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 5:26; Genesis 25:7–9; 2 Chronicles 34:28"> And thou shalt go to thy fathers in peace; thou shalt be buried in a good old age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 21:26; Daniel 8:23; Exodus 12:40"> But in the fourth generation they shall come hither again: for the iniquity of the Amorites is not yet full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:18–19; Judges 6:21; Jeremiah 11:4"> And it came to pass, that, when the sun went down, and it was dark, behold a smoking furnace, and a burning lamp that passed between those pieces.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="15.17 a burning…: Heb. a lamp of fire">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 1:3–4; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 24:7"> In the same day the LORD made a covenant with Abram, saying, Unto thy seed have I given this land, from the river of Egypt unto the great river, the river Euphrates:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 24:21–22"> The Kenites, and the Kenizzites, and the Kadmonites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 17:5; Genesis 14:5"> And the Hittites, and the Perizzites, and the Rephaims,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 10:15–19; Exodus 23:23–28; Deuteronomy 7:1"> And the Amorites, and the Canaanites, and the Girgashites, and the Jebusites.</span>
