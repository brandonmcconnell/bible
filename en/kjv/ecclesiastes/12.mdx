<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 17:13; Jeremiah 9:17–20; Job 30:23"> Also when they shall be afraid of that which is high, and fears shall be in the way, and the almond tree shall flourish, and the grasshopper shall be a burden, and desire shall fail: because man goeth to his long home, and the mourners go about the streets:</span>
