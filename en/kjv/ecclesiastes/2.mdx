<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Ecclesiastes 1:17; Proverbs 31:4–5"> I sought in mine heart to give myself unto wine, yet acquainting mine heart with wisdom; and to lay hold on folly, till I might see what was that good for the sons of men, which they should do under the heaven all the days of their life.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.3 to give…: Heb. to draw my flesh with wine">a</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.3 all…: Heb. the number of the days of their life">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 7:1–12; 2 Chronicles 8:11; 2 Chronicles 26:10"> I made me great works; I builded me houses; I planted me vineyards:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 17:27–29; Song of Solomon 4:12–1; Jeremiah 39:4"> I made me gardens and orchards, and I planted trees in them of all kind of fruits:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:14; Psalms 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8"> I made me pools of water, to water therewith the wood that bringeth forth trees:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 3:4; Genesis 15:3; 1 Kings 4:23"> I got me servants and maidens, and had servants born in my house; also I had great possessions of great and small cattle above all that were in Jerusalem before me:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.7 servants born…: Heb. sons of my house">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 10:10; 1 Kings 9:28; 2 Samuel 19:35"> I gathered me also silver and gold, and the peculiar treasure of kings and of the provinces: I gat me men singers and women singers, and the delights of the sons of men, as musical instruments, and that of all sorts.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.8 musical…: Heb. musical instrument and instruments">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:25; Ecclesiastes 1:16; 1 Kings 10:23"> So I was great, and increased more than all that were before me in Jerusalem: also my wisdom remained with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:9; Ecclesiastes 5:18; Ecclesiastes 3:22"> And whatsoever mine eyes desired I kept not from them, I withheld not my heart from any joy; for my heart rejoiced in all my labour: and this was my portion of all my labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; 1 John 2:16–17; Ecclesiastes 1:3"> Then I looked on all the works that my hands had wrought, and on the labour that I had laboured to do: and, behold, all was vanity and vexation of spirit, and there was no profit under the sun.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:17; Ecclesiastes 7:25; Ecclesiastes 1:9–10"> And I turned myself to behold wisdom, and madness, and folly: for what can the man do that cometh after the king? even that which hath been already done.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2.12 even…: or, in those things which have been already done">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Ecclesiastes 7:11–12; Matthew 6:23"> Then I saw that wisdom excelleth folly, as far as light excelleth darkness.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="2.13 that…: Heb. that there is an excellency in wisdom more than in folly, etc">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 49:10; Proverbs 17:24; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> The wise man’s eyes are in his head; but the fool walketh in darkness: and I myself perceived also that one event happeneth to them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:8; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Ecclesiastes 1:2"> Then said I in my heart, As it happeneth to the fool, so it happeneth even to me; and why was I then more wise? Then I said in my heart, that this also is vanity.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="2.15 happeneth even…: Heb. happeneth to me, even to me">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:11; Ecclesiastes 9:5; Psalms 88:12"> For there is no remembrance of the wise more than of the fool for ever; seeing that which now is in the days to come shall all be forgotten. And how dieth the wise man? as the fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:11; Jonah 4:8; Ecclesiastes 4:2"> Therefore I hated life; because the work that is wrought under the sun is grievous unto me: for all is vanity and vexation of spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:6; Psalms 49:10; Luke 16:27–28"> Yea, I hated all my labour which I had taken under the sun: because I should leave it unto the man that shall be after me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="2.18 taken: Heb. laboured">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 3:17; Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 9:13"> And who knoweth whether he shall be a wise man or a fool? yet shall he have rule over all my labour wherein I have laboured, and wherein I have shewed myself wise under the sun. This is also vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 43:14; Job 17:11–15; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> Therefore I went about to cause my heart to despair of all the labour which I took under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:17–18; 2 Chronicles 31:20–21; 2 Chronicles 34:2"> For there is a man whose labour is in wisdom, and in knowledge, and in equity; yet to a man that hath not laboured therein shall he leave it for his portion. This also is vanity and a great evil.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="2.21 leave: Heb. give">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 127:2; Ecclesiastes 1:3; Philippians 4:6"> For what hath man of all his labour, and of the vexation of his heart, wherein he hath laboured under the sun?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 14:1; Job 5:7; Psalms 6:6–7"> For all his days are sorrows, and his travail grief; yea, his heart taketh not rest in the night. This is also vanity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 8:15; 1 Timothy 6:17"> There is nothing better for a man, than that he should eat and drink, and that he should make his soul enjoy good in his labour. This also I saw, that it was from the hand of God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="2.24 should make…: or, delight his senses">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:1–12; 1 Kings 4:21–24"> For who can eat, or who else can hasten hereunto, more than I?</span>

This also is vanity and vexation of spirit.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="2.26 in his…: Heb. before him">k</a></sup></span>
