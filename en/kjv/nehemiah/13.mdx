<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 23:12; Deuteronomy 5:14"> There dwelt men of Tyre also therein, which brought fish, and all manner of ware, and sold on the sabbath unto the children of Judah, and in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:11; Nehemiah 13:25; Isaiah 1:10"> Then I contended with the nobles of Judah, and said unto them, What evil thing is this that ye do, and profane the sabbath day?</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So the merchants and sellers of all kind of ware lodged without Jerusalem once or twice.</span>

Remember me, O my God, concerning this also, and spare me according to the greatness of thy mercy.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="13.22 greatness: or, multitude">o</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezra 10:2; 1 Samuel 30:24"> Shall we then hearken unto you to do all this great evil, to transgress against our God in marrying strange wives?</span>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:14; Numbers 25:12–13; Malachi 2:4–8"> Remember them, O my God, because they have defiled the priesthood, and the covenant of the priesthood, and of the Levites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="13.29 because…: Heb. for the defilings">t</a></sup></span>
