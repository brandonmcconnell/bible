<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zechariah 2:13; Habakkuk 2:20; Revelation 6:1"> And when he had opened the seventh seal, there was silence in heaven about the space of half an hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 1:19; Revelation 11:15; Revelation 8:6–1"> And I saw the seven angels which stood before God; and to them were given seven trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 5:8; Revelation 8:4; Psalms 141:2"> And another angel came and stood at the altar, having a golden censer; and there was given unto him much incense, that he should offer it with the prayers of all saints upon the golden altar which was before the throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Luke 1:10; Revelation 8:3"> And the smoke of the incense, which came with the prayers of the saints, ascended up before God out of the angel’s hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 4:5; Revelation 11:19; Ezekiel 10:2–7"> And the angel took the censer, and filled it with fire of the altar, and cast it into the earth: and there were voices, and thunderings, and lightnings, and an earthquake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 8:2"> And the seven angels which had the seven trumpets prepared themselves to sound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Revelation 9:4; Revelation 9:18"> The first angel sounded, and there followed hail and fire mingled with blood, and they were cast upon the earth: and the third part of trees was burnt up, and all green grass was burnt up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:25; Exodus 7:17–21; Mark 11:23"> And the second angel sounded, and as it were a great mountain burning with fire was cast into the sea: and the third part of the sea became blood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 16:3; Revelation 8:7; Revelation 8:12"> And the third part of the creatures which were in the sea, and had life, died; and the third part of the ships were destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 16:4; Revelation 9:1; Isaiah 14:12"> And the third angel sounded, and there fell a great star from heaven, burning as it were a lamp, and it fell upon the third part of the rivers, and upon the fountains of waters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:15; Jeremiah 9:15; Deuteronomy 29:18"> And the name of the star is called Wormwood: and the third part of the waters became wormwood; and many men died of the waters, because they were made bitter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Joel 2:31; Joel 2:10"> And the fourth angel sounded, and the third part of the sun was smitten, and the third part of the moon, and the third part of the stars; so as the third part of them was darkened, and the day shone not for a third part of it, and the night likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 9:12; Revelation 11:14; Revelation 14:6"> And I beheld, and heard an angel flying through the midst of heaven, saying with a loud voice, Woe, woe, woe, to the inhabiters of the earth by reason of the other voices of the trumpet of the three angels, which are yet to sound!</span>
