<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 21:15; Ezekiel 42:15–20; Zechariah 2:1–2"> And there was given me a reed like unto a rod: and the angel stood, saying, Rise, and measure the temple of God, and the altar, and them that worship therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Daniel 7:25; Luke 21:24"> But the court which is without the temple leave out, and measure it not; for it is given unto the Gentiles: and the holy city shall they tread under foot forty and two months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Revelation 11:2; Deuteronomy 19:15"> And I will give power unto my two witnesses, and they shall prophesy a thousand two hundred and threescore days, clothed in sackcloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 4:11–14; Zechariah 4:2–3; Jeremiah 11:16"> These are the two olive trees, and the two candlesticks standing before the God of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:14; 2 Kings 1:10–12; Hosea 6:5"> And if any man will hurt them, fire proceedeth out of their mouth, and devoureth their enemies: and if any man will hurt them, he must in this manner be killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Luke 4:25; Psalms 105:26–36"> These have power to shut heaven, that it rain not in the days of their prophecy: and have power over waters to turn them to blood, and to smite the earth with all plagues, as often as they will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 13:7; Daniel 8:23–24; Daniel 7:25"> And when they shall have finished their testimony, the beast that ascendeth out of the bottomless pit shall make war against them, and shall overcome them, and kill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Revelation 18:10; Revelation 18:21"> And their dead bodies shall lie in the street of the great city, which spiritually is called Sodom and Egypt, where also our Lord was crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 79:2–3; Revelation 17:15; Revelation 10:11"> And they of the people and kindreds and tongues and nations shall see their dead bodies three days and an half, and shall not suffer their dead bodies to be put in graves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 18:17; Revelation 3:10; Acts 5:33"> And they that dwell upon the earth shall rejoice over them, and make merry, and shall send gifts one to another; because these two prophets tormented them that dwelt on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Romans 8:11; Romans 8:2"> And after three days and an half the Spirit of life from God entered into them, and they stood upon their feet; and great fear fell upon them which saw them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 4:1; 2 Kings 2:11; Acts 1:9"> And they heard a great voice from heaven saying unto them, Come up hither. And they ascended up to heaven in a cloud; and their enemies beheld them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:7; Revelation 6:12; Joshua 7:19"> And the same hour was there a great earthquake, and the tenth part of the city fell, and in the earthquake were slain of men seven thousand: and the remnant were affrighted, and gave glory to the God of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 8:13; Revelation 9:12; Revelation 15:1"> The second woe is past; and, behold, the third woe cometh quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 7:14; Revelation 12:10; Daniel 7:27"> And the seventh angel sounded; and there were great voices in heaven, saying, The kingdoms of this world are become the kingdoms of our Lord, and of his Christ; and he shall reign for ever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 4:4; Revelation 4:10; Revelation 7:11"> And the four and twenty elders, which sat before God on their seats, fell upon their faces, and worshipped God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 16:5; Revelation 4:8–9"> Saying, We give thee thanks, O Lord God Almighty, which art, and wast, and art to come; because thou hast taken to thee thy great power, and hast reigned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 19:5; Revelation 20:12; Revelation 6:10–11"> And the nations were angry, and thy wrath is come, and the time of the dead, that they should be judged, and that thou shouldest give reward unto thy servants the prophets, and to the saints, and them that fear thy name, small and great; and shouldest destroy them which destroy the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 16:21; Revelation 15:5–8; Ezekiel 38:22"> And the temple of God was opened in heaven, and there was seen in his temple the ark of his testament: and there were lightnings, and voices, and thunderings, and an earthquake, and great hail.</span>
