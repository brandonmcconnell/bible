<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Leviticus 11:44; Ezekiel 20:5"> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, I am the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Exodus 23:24; 1 Peter 4:2–4"> After the doings of the land of Egypt, wherein ye dwelt, shall ye not do: and after the doings of the land of Canaan, whither I bring you, shall ye not do: neither shall ye walk in their ordinances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 18:2; Leviticus 18:26; Ezekiel 20:19"> Ye shall do my judgments, and keep mine ordinances, to walk therein: I am the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Galatians 3:12"> Ye shall therefore keep my statutes, and my judgments: which if a man do, he shall live in them: I am the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17–21; Leviticus 18:7–19; Leviticus 20:11–12"> None of you shall approach to any that is near of kin to him, to uncover their nakedness: I am the LORD.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="18.6 near…: Heb. remainder of his flesh">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; Ezekiel 22:10; Leviticus 18:8–16"> The nakedness of thy father, or the nakedness of thy mother, shalt thou not uncover: she is thy mother; thou shalt not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; 1 Corinthians 5:1; Deuteronomy 27:20"> The nakedness of thy father’s wife shalt thou not uncover: it is thy father’s nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Deuteronomy 27:22; Ezekiel 22:11"> The nakedness of thy sister, the daughter of thy father, or daughter of thy mother, whether she be born at home, or born abroad, even their nakedness thou shalt not uncover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The nakedness of thy son’s daughter, or of thy daughter’s daughter, even their nakedness thou shalt not uncover: for theirs is thine own nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The nakedness of thy father’s wife’s daughter, begotten of thy father, she is thy sister, thou shalt not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 20:19; Exodus 6:20"> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy father’s sister: she is thy father’s near kinswoman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy mother’s sister: for she is thy mother’s near kinswoman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20"> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy father’s brother, thou shalt not approach to his wife: she is thine aunt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 20:12; Genesis 38:26; Ezekiel 22:11"> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy daughter in law: she is thy son’s wife; thou shalt not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Leviticus 20:21; Matthew 22:24"> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy brother’s wife: it is thy brother’s nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Amos 2:7; Deuteronomy 27:23"> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of a woman and her daughter, neither shalt thou take her son’s daughter, or her daughter’s daughter, to uncover her nakedness; for they are her near kinswomen: it is wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachi 2:15; Genesis 30:15; Genesis 29:28"> Neither shalt thou take a wife to her sister, to vex her, to uncover her nakedness, beside the other in her life time.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="18.18 a wife…: or, one wife to another">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 20:18; Leviticus 15:24; Ezekiel 22:10"> Also thou shalt not approach unto a woman to uncover her nakedness, as long as she is put apart for her uncleanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; 1 Corinthians 6:9; Hebrews 13:4"> Moreover thou shalt not lie carnally with thy neighbour’s wife, to defile thyself with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:31; Leviticus 20:2–5; Deuteronomy 18:10"> And thou shalt not let any of thy seed pass through the fire to Molech, neither shalt thou profane the name of thy God: I am the LORD.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="18.21 Molech: Gr. Moloch">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:13; Romans 1:26–27; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> Thou shalt not lie with mankind, as with womankind: it is abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15–16; Leviticus 20:12"> Neither shalt thou lie with any beast to defile thyself therewith: neither shall any woman stand before a beast to lie down thereto: it is confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:12; Leviticus 18:30; Leviticus 20:22–23"> Defile not ye yourselves in any of these things: for in all these the nations are defiled which I cast out before you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 18:28; Leviticus 20:22–23; Jeremiah 2:7"> And the land is defiled: therefore I do visit the iniquity thereof upon it, and the land itself vomiteth out her inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Leviticus 17:10; Leviticus 18:30"> Ye shall therefore keep my statutes and my judgments, and shall not commit any of these abominations; neither any of your own nation, nor any stranger that sojourneth among you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:14; Leviticus 18:24; Deuteronomy 23:18"> (For all these abominations have the men of the land done, which were before you, and the land is defiled;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 3:16; Leviticus 20:22; Leviticus 18:25"> That the land spue not you out also, when ye defile it, as it spued out the nations that were before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:6; Exodus 12:15; Leviticus 17:10"> For whosoever shall commit any of these abominations, even the souls that commit them shall be cut off from among their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Deuteronomy 18:9–12; Leviticus 22:9"> Therefore shall ye keep mine ordinance, that ye commit not any one of these abominable customs, which were committed before you, and that ye defile not yourselves therein: I am the LORD your God.</span>
</div>
