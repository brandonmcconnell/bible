<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 1:1; Galatians 4:24–25; Exodus 19:1"> And the LORD spake unto Moses in mount Sinai, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:21; Leviticus 26:34–35; Exodus 23:10"> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye come into the land which I give you, then shall the land keep a sabbath unto the LORD.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="25.2 keep: Heb. rest">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 23:10"> Six years thou shalt sow thy field, and six years thou shalt prune thy vineyard, and gather in the fruit thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:21; Leviticus 25:20–23; Exodus 23:10–11"> But in the seventh year shall be a sabbath of rest unto the land, a sabbath for the LORD: thou shalt neither sow thy field, nor prune thy vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 19:29; Isaiah 37:30"> That which groweth of its own accord of thy harvest thou shalt not reap, neither gather the grapes of thy vine undressed: for it is a year of rest unto the land.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="25.5 of thy vine…: Heb. of thy separation">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 2:44; Exodus 23:11; Acts 4:34–35"> And the sabbath of the land shall be meat for you; for thee, and for thy servant, and for thy maid, and for thy hired servant, and for thy stranger that sojourneth with thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And for thy cattle, and for the beast that are in thy land, shall all the increase thereof be meat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 23:15; Genesis 2:2"> And thou shalt number seven sabbaths of years unto thee, seven times seven years; and the space of the seven sabbaths of years shall be unto thee forty and nine years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:24; Leviticus 23:27; 1 Thessalonians 1:8"> Then shalt thou cause the trumpet of the jubile to sound on the tenth day of the seventh month, in the day of atonement shall ye make the trumpet sound throughout all your land.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="25.9 of the jubile: Heb. loud of sound">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:8; Isaiah 63:4; 1 Peter 2:16"> And ye shall hallow the fiftieth year, and proclaim liberty throughout all the land unto all the inhabitants thereof: it shall be a jubile unto you; and ye shall return every man unto his possession, and ye shall return every man unto his family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 27:17; Leviticus 25:4–5"> A jubile shall that fiftieth year be unto you: ye shall not sow, neither reap that which groweth of itself in it, nor gather the grapes in it of thy vine undressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 25:6–7"> For it is the jubile; it shall be holy unto you: ye shall eat the increase thereof out of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Leviticus 27:17–24; Numbers 36:4"> In the year of this jubile ye shall return every man unto his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Leviticus 25:17; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> And if thou sell ought unto thy neighbour, or buyest ought of thy neighbour’s hand, ye shall not oppress one another:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 27:18–23; Philippians 4:5"> According to the number of years after the jubile thou shalt buy of thy neighbour, and according unto the number of years of the fruits he shall sell unto thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 25:27; Leviticus 25:51"> According to the multitude of years thou shalt increase the price thereof, and according to the fewness of years thou shalt diminish the price of it: for according to the number of the years of the fruits doth he sell unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 19:14; Leviticus 19:32; Leviticus 25:43"> Ye shall not therefore oppress one another; but thou shalt fear thy God: for I am the LORD your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:6; Deuteronomy 12:10; Proverbs 1:33"> Wherefore ye shall do my statutes, and keep my judgments, and do them; and ye shall dwell in the land in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:26; Psalms 85:12; Leviticus 26:5"> And the land shall yield her fruit, and ye shall eat your fill, and dwell therein in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 25:4; Luke 12:29; Numbers 11:4"> And if ye shall say, What shall we eat the seventh year? behold, we shall not sow, nor gather in our increase:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:8; Proverbs 10:22; 2 Corinthians 9:10"> Then I will command my blessing upon you in the sixth year, and it shall bring forth fruit for three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 19:29; Leviticus 26:10; Isaiah 37:30"> And ye shall sow the eighth year, and eat yet of old fruit until the ninth year; until her fruits come in ye shall eat of the old store.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Psalms 39:12; Ezekiel 48:14"> The land shall not be sold for ever: for the land is mine; for ye are strangers and sojourners with me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="25.23 for ever: or, to be quite cut off: Heb. for cutting off">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Leviticus 25:51–53; Leviticus 25:27"> And in all the land of your possession ye shall grant a redemption for the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ruth 2:20; Jeremiah 32:7–8; Ruth 3:12"> If thy brother be waxen poor, and hath sold away some of his possession, and if any of his kin come to redeem it, then shall he redeem that which his brother sold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 5:7"> And if the man have none to redeem it, and himself be able to redeem it;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="25.26 himself…: Heb. his hand hath attained and found sufficiency">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Then let him count the years of the sale thereof, and restore the overplus unto the man to whom he sold it; that he may return unto his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 35:9–10; Leviticus 25:10; 1 Peter 1:4–5"> But if he be not able to restore it to him, then that which is sold shall remain in the hand of him that hath bought it until the year of jubile: and in the jubile it shall go out, and he shall return unto his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And if a man sell a dwelling house in a walled city, then he may redeem it within a whole year after it is sold; within a full year may he redeem it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And if it be not redeemed within the space of a full year, then the house that is in the walled city shall be established for ever to him that bought it throughout his generations: it shall not go out in the jubile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–8"> But the houses of the villages which have no wall round about them shall be counted as the fields of the country: they may be redeemed, and they shall go out in the jubile.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="25.31 they may…: Heb. redemption belongeth unto it">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 21:1–45; Numbers 35:1–8"> Notwithstanding the cities of the Levites, and the houses of the cities of their possession, may the Levites redeem at any time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 18:20–24; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> And if a man purchase of the Levites, then the house that was sold, and the city of his possession, shall go out in the year of jubile: for the houses of the cities of the Levites are their possession among the children of Israel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="25.33 a man…: or, one of the Levites redeem them">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 25:23; Acts 4:36–37; Numbers 35:2–5"> But the field of the suburbs of their cities may not be sold; for it is their perpetual possession.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:7–8; Acts 11:29; Leviticus 25:25"> And if thy brother be waxen poor, and fallen in decay with thee; then thou shalt relieve him: yea, though he be a stranger, or a sojourner; that he may live with thee.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="25.35 fallen…: Heb. his hand faileth">h</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="25.35 relieve: Heb. strengthen">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Ezekiel 18:8"> Take thou no usury of him, or increase: but fear thy God; that thy brother may live with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Thou shalt not give him thy money upon usury, nor lend him thy victuals for increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 11:45; Numbers 15:41; Exodus 20:2"> I am the LORD your God, which brought you forth out of the land of Egypt, to give you the land of Canaan, and to be your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 21:2; 1 Kings 9:22; 2 Kings 4:1"> And if thy brother that dwelleth by thee be waxen poor, and be sold unto thee; thou shalt not compel him to serve as a bondservant:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="25.39 compel…: Heb. serve thyself with him with the service, etc">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 21:2–3"> But as an hired servant, and as a sojourner, he shall be with thee, and shall serve thee unto the year of jubile:</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 25:28; Exodus 21:3; John 8:32"> And then shall he depart from thee, both he and his children with him, and shall return unto his own family, and unto the possession of his fathers shall he return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Leviticus 25:55; Romans 6:22; 1 Corinthians 7:21–23"> For they are my servants, which I brought forth out of the land of Egypt: they shall not be sold as bondmen.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="25.42 as…: Heb. with the sale of a bondman">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Colossians 4:1; Leviticus 25:46; Exodus 1:13–14"> Thou shalt not rule over him with rigour; but shalt fear thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 12:44; Psalms 2:8–9; Isaiah 14:1–2"> Both thy bondmen, and thy bondmaids, which thou shalt have, shall be of the heathen that are round about you; of them shall ye buy bondmen and bondmaids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Isaiah 56:3–6"> Moreover of the children of the strangers that do sojourn among you, of them shall ye buy, and of their families that are with you, which they begat in your land: and they shall be your possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 14:2; Leviticus 25:43"> And ye shall take them as an inheritance for your children after you, to inherit them for a possession; they shall be your bondmen for ever: but over your brethren the children of Israel, ye shall not rule one over another with rigour.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="25.46 they…: Heb. ye shall serve yourselves with them">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="James 2:5; Leviticus 25:26; 1 Samuel 2:7–8"> And if a sojourner or stranger wax rich by thee, and thy brother that dwelleth by him wax poor, and sell himself unto the stranger or sojourner by thee, or to the stock of the stranger’s family:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="25.47 wax rich…: Heb. his hand obtain, etc">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; Hebrews 2:11–13; Leviticus 25:35"> After that he is sold he may be redeemed again; one of his brethren may redeem him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Leviticus 25:26"> Either his uncle, or his uncle’s son, may redeem him, or any that is nigh of kin unto him of his family may redeem him; or if he be able, he may redeem himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Isaiah 16:14; Isaiah 21:16; Leviticus 25:53"> And he shall reckon with him that bought him from the year that he was sold to him unto the year of jubile: and the price of his sale shall be according unto the number of years, according to the time of an hired servant shall it be with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> If there be yet many years behind, according unto them he shall give again the price of his redemption out of the money that he was bought for.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And if there remain but few years unto the year of jubile, then he shall count with him, and according unto his years shall he give him again the price of his redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Leviticus 25:46; Leviticus 25:43"> And as a yearly hired servant shall he be with him: and the other shall not rule with rigour over him in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 21:2–3; Leviticus 25:40–41; Isaiah 49:9"> And if he be not redeemed in these years, then he shall go out in the year of jubile, both he, and his children with him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="25.54 in these…: or, by these means">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Leviticus 25:42; Isaiah 43:3; Exodus 13:3"> For unto me the children of Israel are servants; they are my servants whom I brought forth out of the land of Egypt: I am the LORD your God.</span>
</div>
