<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the LORD spake unto Moses and to Aaron, saying unto them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 15:11; Leviticus 11:11; Hebrews 9:10"> Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, These are the beasts which ye shall eat among all the beasts that are on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:10; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Deuteronomy 6:6–7"> Whatsoever parteth the hoof, and is clovenfooted, and cheweth the cud, among the beasts, that shall ye eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Nevertheless these shall ye not eat of them that chew the cud, or of them that divide the hoof: as the camel, because he cheweth the cud, but divideth not the hoof; he is unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 104:18; Proverbs 30:26; Matthew 7:26"> And the coney, because he cheweth the cud, but divideth not the hoof; he is unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:7"> And the hare, because he cheweth the cud, but divideth not the hoof; he is unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 66:17; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> And the swine, though he divide the hoof, and be clovenfooted, yet he cheweth not the cud; he is unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Colossians 2:16; Hebrews 9:10"> Of their flesh shall ye not eat, and their carcase shall ye not touch; they are unclean to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; James 2:18; Acts 20:21"> These shall ye eat of all that are in the waters: whatsoever hath fins and scales in the waters, in the seas, and in the rivers, them shall ye eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 29:27; Leviticus 7:18; Psalms 139:21–22"> And all that have not fins and scales in the seas, and in the rivers, of all that move in the waters, and of any living thing which is in the waters, they shall be an abomination unto you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> They shall be even an abomination unto you; ye shall not eat of their flesh, but ye shall have their carcases in abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Whatsoever hath no fins nor scales in the waters, that shall be an abomination unto you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 39:27–30; Jeremiah 4:22; Titus 3:3"> And these are they which ye shall have in abomination among the fowls; they shall not be eaten, they are an abomination: the eagle, and the ossifrage, and the ospray,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the vulture, and the kite after his kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 12:24; 1 Kings 17:6; Proverbs 30:17"> Every raven after his kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 34:11–15; Ephesians 5:7–11; Ephesians 2:2–3"> And the owl, and the night hawk, and the cuckow, and the hawk after his kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the little owl, and the cormorant, and the great owl,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the swan, and the pelican, and the gier eagle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And the stork, the heron after her kind, and the lapwing, and the bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Philippians 3:18–19; Leviticus 11:23"> All fowls that creep, going upon all four, shall be an abomination unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Yet these may ye eat of every flying creeping thing that goeth upon all four, which have legs above their feet, to leap withal upon the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; Mark 1:6; Exodus 10:4–5"> Even these of them ye may eat; the locust after his kind, and the bald locust after his kind, and the beetle after his kind, and the grasshopper after his kind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> But all other flying creeping things, which have four feet, shall be an abomination unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Ephesians 2:1–3; 2 Corinthians 6:17"> And for these ye shall be unclean: whosoever toucheth the carcase of them shall be unclean until the even.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Leviticus 14:8; Numbers 31:24"> And whosoever beareth ought of the carcase of them shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The carcases of every beast which divideth the hoof, and is not clovenfooted, nor cheweth the cud, are unclean unto you: every one that toucheth them shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 11:23; Leviticus 11:20"> And whatsoever goeth upon his paws, among all manner of beasts that go on all four, those are unclean unto you: whoso toucheth their carcase shall be unclean until the even.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25"> And he that beareth the carcase of them shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even: they are unclean unto you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 11:41–42; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Psalms 10:3"> These also shall be unclean unto you among the creeping things that creep upon the earth; the weasel, and the mouse, and the tortoise after his kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the ferret, and the chameleon, and the lizard, and the snail, and the mole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25; Leviticus 11:8"> These are unclean to you among all that creep: whosoever doth touch them, when they be dead, shall be unclean until the even.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Titus 3:5; Titus 2:14"> And upon whatsoever any of them, when they are dead, doth fall, it shall be unclean; whether it be any vessel of wood, or raiment, or skin, or sack, whatsoever vessel it be, wherein any work is done, it must be put into water, and it shall be unclean until the even; so it shall be cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 14:45"> And every earthen vessel, whereinto any of them falleth, whatsoever is in it shall be unclean; and ye shall break it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Proverbs 21:4; Proverbs 21:27"> Of all meat which may be eaten, that on which such water cometh shall be unclean: and all drink that may be drunk in every such vessel shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 11:33"> And every thing whereupon any part of their carcase falleth shall be unclean; whether it be oven, or ranges for pots, they shall be broken down: for they are unclean, and shall be unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; John 4:14"> Nevertheless a fountain or pit, wherein there is plenty of water, shall be clean: but that which toucheth their carcase shall be unclean.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="11.36 wherein…: Heb. a gathering together of waters">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:37; 1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:18"> And if any part of their carcase fall upon any sowing seed which is to be sown, it shall be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> But if any water be put upon the seed, and any part of their carcase fall thereon, it shall be unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Leviticus 11:40; Leviticus 11:24; Leviticus 15:7"> And if any beast, of which ye may eat, die; he that toucheth the carcase thereof shall be unclean until the even.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Leviticus 22:8; Ezekiel 44:31; Deuteronomy 14:21"> And he that eateth of the carcase of it shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even: he also that beareth the carcase of it shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 11:29; Leviticus 11:20; Leviticus 11:23"> And every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth shall be an abomination; it shall not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Titus 1:12; Isaiah 65:25; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> Whatsoever goeth upon the belly, and whatsoever goeth upon all four, or whatsoever hath more feet among all creeping things that creep upon the earth, them ye shall not eat; for they are an abomination.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="11.42 hath…: Heb. doth multiply feet">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 20:25; Leviticus 11:41–42"> Ye shall not make yourselves abominable with any creeping thing that creepeth, neither shall ye make yourselves unclean with them, that ye should be defiled thereby.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="11.43 yourselves abominable: Heb. your souls, etc">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Leviticus 19:2"> For I am the LORD your God: ye shall therefore sanctify yourselves, and ye shall be holy; for I am holy: neither shall ye defile yourselves with any manner of creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Exodus 20:2; Exodus 6:7"> For I am the LORD that bringeth you up out of the land of Egypt, to be your God: ye shall therefore be holy, for I am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Leviticus 14:54; Leviticus 15:32; Ezekiel 43:12"> This is the law of the beasts, and of the fowl, and of every living creature that moveth in the waters, and of every creature that creepeth upon the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Ezekiel 44:23; Romans 14:13–23"> To make a difference between the unclean and the clean, and between the beast that may be eaten and the beast that may not be eaten.</span>
</div>
