<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 26:6; Hebrews 10:29; Revelation 11:2"> The crown of pride, the drunkards of Ephraim, shall be trodden under feet:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="28.3 under…: Heb. with feet">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nahum 3:12; Micah 7:1; Isaiah 28:1"> And the glorious beauty, which is on the head of the fat valley, shall be a fading flower, and as the hasty fruit before the summer; which when he that looketh upon it seeth, while it is yet in his hand he eateth it up.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="28.4 eateth: Heb. swalloweth">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 18:32–34; John 5:30; Joshua 1:9"> And for a spirit of judgment to him that sitteth in judgment, and for strength to them that turn the battle to the gate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 56:10–12; Luke 21:34; Isaiah 19:14"> But they also have erred through wine, and through strong drink are out of the way; the priest and the prophet have erred through strong drink, they are swallowed up of wine, they are out of the way through strong drink; they err in vision, they stumble in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:26; Proverbs 26:11; Habakkuk 2:15–16"> For all tables are full of vomit and filthiness, so that there is no place clean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 131:2; Jeremiah 6:10; John 12:38"> Whom shall he teach knowledge? and whom shall he make to understand doctrine? them that are weaned from the milk, and drawn from the breasts.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="28.9 doctrine: Heb. the hearing?">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 28:13; 2 Timothy 3:7; Jeremiah 11:7"> For precept must be upon precept, precept upon precept; line upon line, line upon line; here a little, and there a little:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="28.10 must be: or, hath been">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:21; Deuteronomy 28:49; Isaiah 33:19"> For with stammering lips and another tongue will he speak to this people.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="28.11 stammering…: Heb. stammerings of lip">f</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="28.11 will…: or, he hath spoken">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 11:28–29; Isaiah 30:15; Jeremiah 6:16"> To whom he said, This is the rest wherewith ye may cause the weary to rest; and this is the refreshing: yet they would not hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 13:14; Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Peter 3:16"> But the word of the LORD was unto them precept upon precept, precept upon precept; line upon line, line upon line; here a little, and there a little; that they might go, and fall backward, and be broken, and snared, and taken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 28:22; Isaiah 29:20; Isaiah 1:10"> Wherefore hear the word of the LORD, ye scornful men, that rule this people which is in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Ezekiel 13:22; Amos 2:4"> Because ye have said, We have made a covenant with death, and with hell are we at agreement; when the overflowing scourge shall pass through, it shall not come unto us: for we have made lies our refuge, and under falsehood have we hid ourselves:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 118:22; Acts 4:11–12; 1 Peter 2:6–8"> Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD, Behold, I lay in Zion for a foundation a stone, a tried stone, a precious corner stone, a sure foundation: he that believeth shall not make haste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Amos 7:7–9; Revelation 16:21"> Judgment also will I lay to the line, and righteousness to the plummet: and the hail shall sweep away the refuge of lies, and the waters shall overflow the hiding place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 7:7; Isaiah 8:10; Isaiah 28:15"> And your covenant with death shall be disannulled, and your agreement with hell shall not stand; when the overflowing scourge shall pass through, then ye shall be trodden down by it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="28.18 trodden…: Heb. a treading down to it">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Isaiah 36:22; 2 Kings 24:2"> From the time that it goeth forth it shall take you: for morning by morning shall it pass over, by day and by night: and it shall be a vexation only to understand the report.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="28.19 to…: or, when he shall make you to understand doctrine">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; 1 Corinthians 1:18–31; Romans 9:30–32"> For the bed is shorter than that a man can stretch himself on it: and the covering narrower than that he can wrap himself in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:11; Joshua 10:12; 2 Samuel 5:20"> For the LORD shall rise up as in mount Perazim, he shall be wroth as in the valley of Gibeon, that he may do his work, his strange work; and bring to pass his act, his strange act.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22–23; Isaiah 24:1–23; Matthew 27:44"> Now therefore be ye not mockers, lest your bands be made strong: for I have heard from the Lord GOD of hosts a consumption, even determined upon the whole earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Jeremiah 22:29; Revelation 2:11"> Give ye ear, and hear my voice; hearken, and hear my speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Hosea 10:11–12"> Doth the plowman plow all day to sow? doth he open and break the clods of his ground?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:9; Matthew 23:23; Exodus 9:31–32"> When he hath made plain the face thereof, doth he not cast abroad the fitches, and scatter the cummin, and cast in the principal wheat and the appointed barley and the rie in their place?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="28.25 the principal…: or, the wheat in the principal place, and barley in the appointed place">j</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="28.25 rie: or, spelt">k</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="28.25 place: Heb. border?">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 31:3–6; Exodus 36:2; Job 39:17"> For his God doth instruct him to discretion, and doth teach him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="28.26 For…: or, And he bindeth it in such sort as his God doth teach him">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15; Isaiah 27:7–8; Jeremiah 46:28"> For the fitches are not threshed with a threshing instrument, neither is a cart wheel turned about upon the cummin; but the fitches are beaten out with a staff, and the cummin with a rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:9–10; Matthew 3:12; John 12:24"> Bread corn is bruised; because he will not ever be threshing it, nor break it with the wheel of his cart, nor bruise it with his horsemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Jeremiah 32:19; Job 5:9"> This also cometh forth from the LORD of hosts, which is wonderful in counsel, and excellent in working.</span>
