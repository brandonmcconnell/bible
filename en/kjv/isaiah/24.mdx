<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 16:10; Joel 1:10–12; Isaiah 16:8"> The new wine mourneth, the vine languisheth, all the merryhearted do sigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 2:11; Revelation 18:22; Ezekiel 26:13"> The mirth of tabrets ceaseth, the noise of them that rejoice endeth, the joy of the harp ceaseth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 5:22; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Isaiah 5:20"> They shall not drink wine with a song; strong drink shall be bitter to them that drink it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 17:5–6; Isaiah 24:12"> The city of confusion is broken down: every house is shut up, that no man may come in.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Jeremiah 31:12; Isaiah 54:1"> They shall lift up their voice, they shall sing for the majesty of the LORD, they shall cry aloud from the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 1:11; Isaiah 42:4; Isaiah 60:9"> Wherefore glorify ye the LORD in the fires, even the name of the LORD God of Israel in the isles of the sea.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="24.15 fires: or, valleys">d</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4–10; Deuteronomy 11:6; Revelation 20:11"> The earth is utterly broken down, the earth is clean dissolved, the earth is moved exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 19:14; Hosea 4:1–5; Daniel 11:19"> The earth shall reel to and fro like a drunkard, and shall be removed like a cottage; and the transgression thereof shall be heavy upon it; and it shall fall, and not rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 76:12; Isaiah 34:2–17; Zechariah 14:12–19"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that the LORD shall punish the host of the high ones that are on high, and the kings of the earth upon the earth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="24.21 punish: Heb. visit upon">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11; Isaiah 42:22; Jeremiah 38:6–13"> And they shall be gathered together, as prisoners are gathered in the pit, and shall be shut up in the prison, and after many days shall they be visited.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="24.22 as prisoners…: Heb. with the gathering of prisoners">h</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="24.22 pit: or, dungeon">i</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="24.22 visited: or, found wanting">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19; Revelation 21:23; Isaiah 13:10"> Then the moon shall be confounded, and the sun ashamed, when the LORD of hosts shall reign in mount Zion, and in Jerusalem, and before his ancients gloriously.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="24.23 before…: or, there shall be glory before his ancients">k</a></sup></span>
