<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 25:4–7; Isaiah 3:1–8; Jeremiah 39:4–7"> All thy rulers are fled together, they are bound by the archers: all that are found in thee are bound together, which have fled from far.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="22.3 by…: Heb. of the bow">a</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 16:9; Isaiah 21:2; Amos 1:5"> And Elam bare the quiver with chariots of men and horsemen, and Kir uncovered the shield.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="22.6 uncovered: Heb. made naked">c</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2; 1 Kings 10:17; Isaiah 36:1–3"> And he discovered the covering of Judah, and thou didst look in that day to the armour of the house of the forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 20:20; Nehemiah 3:16; 2 Chronicles 32:1–6"> Ye have seen also the breaches of the city of David, that they are many: and ye gathered together the waters of the lower pool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And ye have numbered the houses of Jerusalem, and the houses have ye broken down to fortify the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 7:7; Jeremiah 33:2–3; Isaiah 31:1"> Ye made also a ditch between the two walls for the water of the old pool: but ye have not looked unto the maker thereof, neither had respect unto him that fashioned it long ago.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Micah 1:16; Joel 1:13"> And in that day did the Lord GOD of hosts call to weeping, and to mourning, and to baldness, and to girding with sackcloth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 56:12; 1 Corinthians 15:32; Luke 17:26–29"> And behold joy and gladness, slaying oxen, and killing sheep, eating flesh, and drinking wine: let us eat and drink; for to morrow we shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:13; 1 Samuel 3:14; Isaiah 5:9"> And it was revealed in mine ears by the LORD of hosts, Surely this iniquity shall not be purged from you till ye die, saith the Lord GOD of hosts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; 2 Kings 18:18; 2 Kings 19:2"> Thus saith the Lord GOD of hosts, Go, get thee unto this treasurer, even unto Shebna, which is over the house, and say,</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3; Job 9:24; Esther 7:8"> Behold, the LORD will carry thee away with a mighty captivity, and will surely cover thee.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="22.17 will carry…: or, who covered thee with an excellent covering, and clothed thee gorgeously, (next verse) shall surely, etc">g</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="22.17 a mighty…: Heb. the captivity of a man">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 17:13; Amos 7:17"> He will surely violently turn and toss thee like a ball into a large country: there shalt thou die, and there the chariots of thy glory shall be the shame of thy lord’s house.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="22.18 large: Heb. large of spaces">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; Psalms 75:6–7; Job 40:11–12"> And I will drive thee from thy station, and from thy state shall he pull thee down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; 2 Kings 18:18; Isaiah 37:2"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that I will call my servant Eliakim the son of Hilkiah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:8; Isaiah 9:6–7; Genesis 41:42–43"> And I will clothe him with thy robe, and strengthen him with thy girdle, and I will commit thy government into his hand: and he shall be a father to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and to the house of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:18–19; Revelation 3:7; Job 12:14"> And the key of the house of David will I lay upon his shoulder; so he shall open, and none shall shut; and he shall shut, and none shall open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:8; Job 36:7; Zechariah 10:4"> And I will fasten him as a nail in a sure place; and he shall be for a glorious throne to his father’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 6:1–3; John 20:21–23; Matthew 28:18"> And they shall hang upon him all the glory of his father’s house, the offspring and the issue, all vessels of small quantity, from the vessels of cups, even to all the vessels of flagons.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="22.24 vessels of flagons: or, instruments of viols">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 46:11; Esther 9:24–25; Psalms 146:3"> In that day, saith the LORD of hosts, shall the nail that is fastened in the sure place be removed, and be cut down, and fall; and the burden that was upon it shall be cut off: for the LORD hath spoken it.</span>
