<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:2; Psalms 118:28; Numbers 23:19"> O LORD, thou art my God; I will exalt thee, I will praise thy name; for thou hast done wonderful things; thy counsels of old are faithfulness and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 17:1; Isaiah 13:22; Isaiah 25:12"> For thou hast made of a city an heap; of a defenced city a ruin: a palace of strangers to be no city; it shall never be built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; Isaiah 49:23–26; Psalms 46:10–11"> Therefore shall the strong people glorify thee, the city of the terrible nations shall fear thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:12; Isaiah 32:2; James 2:5"> For thou hast been a strength to the poor, a strength to the needy in his distress, a refuge from the storm, a shadow from the heat, when the blast of the terrible ones is as a storm against the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 20:8–9; Isaiah 49:10; Psalms 105:39"> Thou shalt bring down the noise of strangers, as the heat in a dry place; even the heat with the shadow of a cloud: the branch of the terrible ones shall be brought low.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 8:11; Psalms 63:5; Zechariah 9:16–17"> And in this mountain shall the LORD of hosts make unto all people a feast of fat things, a feast of wines on the lees, of fat things full of marrow, of wines on the lees well refined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:18; Isaiah 60:1–3; Matthew 27:51"> And he will destroy in this mountain the face of the covering cast over all people, and the vail that is spread over all nations.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="25.7 destroy: Heb. swallow up">a</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="25.7 cast: Heb. covered">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17; 1 Corinthians 15:54"> He will swallow up death in victory; and the Lord GOD will wipe away tears from off all faces; and the rebuke of his people shall he take away from off all the earth: for the LORD hath spoken it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Genesis 49:18; Micah 7:7"> And it shall be said in that day, Lo, this is our God; we have waited for him, and he will save us: this is the LORD; we have waited for him, we will be glad and rejoice in his salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; Isaiah 18:4; Zephaniah 2:9"> For in this mountain shall the hand of the LORD rest, and Moab shall be trodden down under him, even as straw is trodden down for the dunghill.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="25.10 trodden down under: or, threshed, etc">c</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="25.10 trodden down for…: or, threshed in Madmenah">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 16:14; Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 16:6"> And he shall spread forth his hands in the midst of them, as he that swimmeth spreadeth forth his hands to swim: and he shall bring down their pride together with the spoils of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 26:5; Revelation 18:21; Isaiah 14:23"> And the fortress of the high fort of thy walls shall he bring down, lay low, and bring to the ground, even to the dust.</span>
