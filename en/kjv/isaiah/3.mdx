<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The changeable suits of apparel, and the mantles, and the wimples, and the crisping pins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 38:8; Ruth 3:15; Ezekiel 16:10"> The glasses, and the fine linen, and the hoods, and the vails.</span>
