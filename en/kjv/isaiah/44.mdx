<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Acts 2:17; Isaiah 59:21"> For I will pour water upon him that is thirsty, and floods upon the dry ground: I will pour my spirit upon thy seed, and my blessing upon thine offspring:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; Psalms 1:3; Psalms 92:13–15"> And they shall spring up as among the grass, as willows by the water courses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:5; Zechariah 13:9; 1 Peter 2:9"> One shall say, I am the LORD’s; and another shall call himself by the name of Jacob; and another shall subscribe with his hand unto the LORD, and surname himself by the name of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 41:4; Isaiah 43:10–11; Revelation 1:8"> Thus saith the LORD the King of Israel, and his redeemer the LORD of hosts; I am the first, and I am the last; and beside me there is no God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 41:26; Isaiah 41:22; Isaiah 46:9–10"> And who, as I, shall call, and shall declare it, and set it in order for me, since I appointed the ancient people? and the things that are coming, and shall come, let them shew unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; 1 Samuel 2:2; Deuteronomy 4:35"> Fear ye not, neither be afraid: have not I told thee from that time, and have declared it? ye are even my witnesses. Is there a God beside me? yea, there is no God; I know not any.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="44.8 God; I: Heb. rock, etc">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 10:14–15; Psalms 97:7"> They that make a graven image are all of them vanity; and their delectable things shall not profit; and they are their own witnesses; they see not, nor know; that they may be ashamed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="44.9 delectable: Heb. desirable">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:5; Habakkuk 2:18; Acts 19:26"> Who hath formed a god, or molten a graven image that is profitable for nothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:17; Isaiah 45:16; Psalms 97:7"> Behold, all his fellows shall be ashamed: and the workmen, they are of men: let them all be gathered together, let them stand up; yet they shall fear, and they shall be ashamed together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:6–7; Isaiah 40:19; Isaiah 46:6–7"> The smith with the tongs both worketh in the coals, and fashioneth it with hammers, and worketh it with the strength of his arms: yea, he is hungry, and his strength faileth: he drinketh no water, and is faint.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="44.12 with the tongs: or, with an axe">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 17:4–5; Psalms 115:5–7; Acts 17:29"> The carpenter stretcheth out his rule; he marketh it out with a line; he fitteth it with planes, and he marketh it out with the compass, and maketh it after the figure of a man, according to the beauty of a man; that it may remain in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:20; Jeremiah 10:3–8; Hosea 4:12"> He heweth him down cedars, and taketh the cypress and the oak, which he strengtheneth for himself among the trees of the forest: he planteth an ash, and the rain doth nourish it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="44.14 strengtheneth: or, taketh courage">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:14; Revelation 9:20; Judges 2:19"> Then shall it be for a man to burn: for he will take thereof, and warm himself; yea, he kindleth it, and baketh bread; yea, he maketh a god, and worshippeth it; he maketh it a graven image, and falleth down thereto.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He burneth part thereof in the fire; with part thereof he eateth flesh; he roasteth roast, and is satisfied: yea, he warmeth himself, and saith, Aha, I am warm, I have seen the fire:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 45:20; 1 Kings 18:26; Daniel 6:27"> And the residue thereof he maketh a god, even his graven image: he falleth down unto it, and worshippeth it, and prayeth unto it, and saith, Deliver me; for thou art my god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; John 12:39–40"> They have not known nor understood: for he hath shut their eyes, that they cannot see; and their hearts, that they cannot understand.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="44.18 shut: Heb. daubed">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:15; 1 Kings 11:7; 1 Kings 11:5"> And none considereth in his heart, neither is there knowledge nor understanding to say, I have burned part of it in the fire; yea, also I have baked bread upon the coals thereof; I have roasted flesh, and eaten it: and shall I make the residue thereof an abomination? shall I fall down to the stock of a tree?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="44.19 considereth…: Heb. setteth to his heart">f</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="44.19 the stock…: Heb. that which comes of a tree?">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:28; Job 15:31; Hosea 12:1"> He feedeth on ashes: a deceived heart hath turned him aside, that he cannot deliver his soul, nor say, Is there not a lie in my right hand?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 44:1–2; Isaiah 49:15–16; Isaiah 43:15"> Remember these, O Jacob and Israel; for thou art my servant: I have formed thee; thou art my servant: O Israel, thou shalt not be forgotten of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; 1 Corinthians 6:20; Acts 3:18–19"> I have blotted out, as a thick cloud, thy transgressions, and, as a cloud, thy sins: return unto me; for I have redeemed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 1:6–7; Revelation 5:8–14; Psalms 69:34"> Sing, O ye heavens; for the LORD hath done it: shout, ye lower parts of the earth: break forth into singing, ye mountains, O forest, and every tree therein: for the LORD hath redeemed Jacob, and glorified himself in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 45:12; Isaiah 42:5; Isaiah 54:5"> Thus saith the LORD, thy redeemer, and he that formed thee from the womb, I am the LORD that maketh all things; that stretcheth forth the heavens alone; that spreadeth abroad the earth by myself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Job 5:12–14; Jeremiah 50:36"> That frustrateth the tokens of the liars, and maketh diviners mad; that turneth wise men backward, and maketh their knowledge foolish;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 18:36–38; Isaiah 42:9; Nehemiah 1:3"> That confirmeth the word of his servant, and performeth the counsel of his messengers; that saith to Jerusalem, Thou shalt be inhabited; and to the cities of Judah, Ye shall be built, and I will raise up the decayed places thereof:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="44.26 decayed…: Heb. wastes">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 42:15; Jeremiah 51:36; Jeremiah 50:38"> That saith to the deep, Be dry, and I will dry up thy rivers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:22–3; Isaiah 45:1; Isaiah 45:13"> That saith of Cyrus, He is my shepherd, and shall perform all my pleasure: even saying to Jerusalem, Thou shalt be built; and to the temple, Thy foundation shall be laid.</span>
