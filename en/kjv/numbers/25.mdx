<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 6:25; Psalms 106:30; Joshua 22:30–31"> And when Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, saw it, he rose up from among the congregation, and took a javelin in his hand;</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Psalms 78:58; Deuteronomy 32:16"> Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, hath turned my wrath away from the children of Israel, while he was zealous for my sake among them, that I consumed not the children of Israel in my jealousy.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="25.11 for…: Heb. with my zeal">a</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17; Romans 10:2–4; Jeremiah 33:22"> And he shall have it, and his seed after him, even the covenant of an everlasting priesthood; because he was zealous for his God, and made an atonement for the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:23; Numbers 26:14; 2 Chronicles 19:7"> Now the name of the Israelite that was slain, even that was slain with the Midianitish woman, was Zimri, the son of Salu, a prince of a chief house among the Simeonites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="25.14 chief…: Heb. house of a father">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Joshua 13:21; Numbers 25:18"> And the name of the Midianitish woman that was slain was Cozbi, the daughter of Zur; he was head over a people, and of a chief house in Midian.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 31:1–3; Revelation 18:6"> Vex the Midianites, and smite them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 2:14; Exodus 32:35; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> For they vex you with their wiles, wherewith they have beguiled you in the matter of Peor, and in the matter of Cozbi, the daughter of a prince of Midian, their sister, which was slain in the day of the plague for Peor’s sake.</span>
