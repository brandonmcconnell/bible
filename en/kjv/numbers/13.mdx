<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 78:12; Psalms 78:43; Isaiah 19:11"> And they ascended by the south, and came unto Hebron; where Ahiman, Sheshai, and Talmai, the children of Anak, were. (Now Hebron was built seven years before Zoan in Egypt.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 13:24; Numbers 32:9; Deuteronomy 1:24–25"> And they came unto the brook of Eshcol, and cut down from thence a branch with one cluster of grapes, and they bare it between two upon a staff; and they brought of the pomegranates, and of the figs.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="13.23 brook: or, valley">d</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 13:5"> And they told him, and said, We came unto the land whither thou sentest us, and surely it floweth with milk and honey; and this is the fruit of it.</span>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 14:43; Judges 6:3; Genesis 15:19–21"> The Amalekites dwell in the land of the south: and the Hittites, and the Jebusites, and the Amorites, dwell in the mountains: and the Canaanites dwell by the sea, and by the coast of Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Romans 8:31; Philippians 4:13"> And Caleb stilled the people before Moses, and said, Let us go up at once, and possess it; for we are well able to overcome it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Joshua 14:8; Hebrews 3:19"> But the men that went up with him said, We be not able to go up against the people; for they are stronger than we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Numbers 14:36–37; Ezekiel 36:13–14"> And they brought up an evil report of the land which they had searched unto the children of Israel, saying, The land, through which we have gone to search it, is a land that eateth up the inhabitants thereof; and all the people that we saw in it are men of a great stature.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="13.32 men…: Heb. men of statures">g</a></sup></span>
