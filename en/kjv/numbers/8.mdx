<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 25:37; Exodus 40:25; Leviticus 24:1–2"> Speak unto Aaron, and say unto him, When thou lightest the lamps, the seven lamps shall give light over against the candlestick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And Aaron did so; he lighted the lamps thereof over against the candlestick, as the LORD commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–40; Exodus 25:9; Exodus 25:18"> And this work of the candlestick was of beaten gold, unto the shaft thereof, unto the flowers thereof, was beaten work: according unto the pattern which the LORD had shewed Moses, so he made the candlestick.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; James 4:8; Exodus 19:15"> Take the Levites from among the children of Israel, and cleanse them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 19:13; Numbers 19:7–10; Leviticus 8:6"> And thus shalt thou do unto them, to cleanse them: Sprinkle water of purifying upon them, and let them shave all their flesh, and let them wash their clothes, and so make themselves clean.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="8.7 let them shave…: Heb. let them cause a razor to pass over, etc">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 29:1; Leviticus 16:3"> Then let them take a young bullock with his meat offering, even fine flour mingled with oil, and another young bullock shalt thou take for a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 40:12; Leviticus 8:3; Exodus 29:4–37"> And thou shalt bring the Levites before the tabernacle of the congregation: and thou shalt gather the whole assembly of the children of Israel together:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 1:4; Acts 6:6; 1 Timothy 4:14"> And thou shalt bring the Levites before the LORD: and the children of Israel shall put their hands upon the Levites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30; Leviticus 8:29; Numbers 1:49–53"> And Aaron shall offer the Levites before the LORD for an offering of the children of Israel, that they may execute the service of the LORD.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="8.11 offer: Heb. wave">b</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="8.11 offering: Heb. wave offering">c</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="8.11 they…: Heb. they may be to execute, etc">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–10; Exodus 29:10; Leviticus 1:4"> And the Levites shall lay their hands upon the heads of the bullocks: and thou shalt offer the one for a sin offering, and the other for a burnt offering, unto the LORD, to make an atonement for the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 8:21; Numbers 8:11; Romans 15:16"> And thou shalt set the Levites before Aaron, and before his sons, and offer them for an offering unto the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 3:45; Malachi 3:17; Numbers 18:6"> Thus shalt thou separate the Levites from among the children of Israel: and the Levites shall be mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 8:11; Numbers 8:13; Numbers 3:23–37"> And after that shall the Levites go in to do the service of the tabernacle of the congregation: and thou shalt cleanse them, and offer them for an offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:45; Numbers 3:9"> For they are wholly given unto me from among the children of Israel; instead of such as open every womb, even instead of the firstborn of all the children of Israel, have I taken them unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Luke 2:23; Exodus 13:12–15"> For all the firstborn of the children of Israel are mine, both man and beast: on the day that I smote every firstborn in the land of Egypt I sanctified them for myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And I have taken the Levites for all the firstborn of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 16:46; 2 Chronicles 26:16–20"> And I have given the Levites as a gift to Aaron and to his sons from among the children of Israel, to do the service of the children of Israel in the tabernacle of the congregation, and to make an atonement for the children of Israel: that there be no plague among the children of Israel, when the children of Israel come nigh unto the sanctuary.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="8.19 a gift: Heb. given">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Moses, and Aaron, and all the congregation of the children of Israel, did to the Levites according unto all that the LORD commanded Moses concerning the Levites, so did the children of Israel unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 8:7; Numbers 19:19; Numbers 8:15"> And the Levites were purified, and they washed their clothes; and Aaron offered them as an offering before the LORD; and Aaron made an atonement for them to cleanse them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:2; 2 Chronicles 35:8–15; Numbers 8:15"> And after that went the Levites in to do their service in the tabernacle of the congregation before Aaron, and before his sons: as the LORD had commanded Moses concerning the Levites, so did they unto them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 4:3; 1 Chronicles 23:3; Numbers 4:23"> This is it that belongeth unto the Levites: from twenty and five years old and upward they shall go in to wait upon the service of the tabernacle of the congregation:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="8.24 to…: Heb. to war the warfare of, etc">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 4:23; 2 Timothy 4:7"> And from the age of fifty years they shall cease waiting upon the service thereof, and shall serve no more:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="8.25 cease…: Heb. return from the warfare of the service">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:32; 1 Timothy 4:15"> But shall minister with their brethren in the tabernacle of the congregation, to keep the charge, and shall do no service. Thus shalt thou do unto the Levites touching their charge.</span>
</div>
