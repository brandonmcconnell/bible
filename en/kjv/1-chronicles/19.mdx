<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1–2; 2 Samuel 10:1–19; 1 Samuel 12:12"> Now it came to pass after this, that Nahash the king of the children of Ammon died, and his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:3–6; 1 Samuel 30:26; Genesis 19:37–38"> And David said, I will shew kindness unto Hanun the son of Nahash, because his father shewed kindness to me. And David sent messengers to comfort him concerning his father. So the servants of David came into the land of the children of Ammon to Hanun, to comfort him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 1:23–24; 1 Corinthians 13:5–7; 1 Kings 12:8–11"> But the princes of the children of Ammon said to Hanun, Thinkest thou that David doth honour thy father, that he hath sent comforters unto thee? are not his servants come unto thee for to search, and to overthrow, and to spy out the land?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="19.3 Thinkest…: Heb. In thine eyes doth David, etc">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:4–5; Isaiah 15:2; Mark 12:4"> Wherefore Hanun took David’s servants, and shaved them, and cut off their garments in the midst hard by their buttocks, and sent them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 6:24–26; Matthew 18:31; 1 Kings 16:34"> Then there went certain, and told David how the men were served. And he sent to meet them: for the men were greatly ashamed. And the king said, Tarry at Jericho until your beards be grown, and then return.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:5; Genesis 34:30; 1 Chronicles 18:9"> And when the children of Ammon saw that they had made themselves odious to David, Hanun and the children of Ammon sent a thousand talents of silver to hire them chariots and horsemen out of Mesopotamia, and out of Syria-maachah, and out of Zobah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="19.6 odious: Heb. to stink">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 21:30; Joshua 13:9; Joshua 13:16"> So they hired thirty and two thousand chariots, and the king of Maachah and his people; who came and pitched before Medeba. And the children of Ammon gathered themselves together from their cities, and came to battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:6; 1 Chronicles 11:10–47; 2 Samuel 23:8–39"> And when David heard of it, he sent Joab, and all the host of the mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 20:1; 1 Kings 20:24; Isaiah 28:6"> And the children of Ammon came out, and put the battle in array before the gate of the city: and the kings that were come were by themselves in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:9–14; Judges 20:42–43; Joshua 8:22"> Now when Joab saw that the battle was set against him before and behind, he chose out of all the choice of Israel, and put them in array against the Syrians.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="19.10 the battle…: Heb. the face of the battle was">c</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="19.10 choice: or, young men">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:12; 1 Chronicles 11:20; 1 Chronicles 19:9"> And the rest of the people he delivered unto the hand of Abishai his brother, and they set themselves in array against the children of Ammon.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="19.11 Abishai: Heb. Abshai">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 1:27–28; Nehemiah 4:20; Ecclesiastes 4:9–12"> And he said, If the Syrians be too strong for me, then thou shalt help me: but if the children of Ammon be too strong for thee, then I will help thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:14; Psalms 27:14; 2 Samuel 10:12"> Be of good courage, and let us behave ourselves valiantly for our people, and for the cities of our God: and let the LORD do that which is good in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 20:19–21; 2 Chronicles 13:5–16; Jeremiah 46:15–16"> So Joab and the people that were with him drew nigh before the Syrians unto the battle; and they fled before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Leviticus 26:7"> And when the children of Ammon saw that the Syrians were fled, they likewise fled before Abishai his brother, and entered into the city. Then Joab came to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:16; Zechariah 14:1–3; Micah 4:11–12"> And when the Syrians saw that they were put to the worse before Israel, they sent messengers, and drew forth the Syrians that were beyond the river: and Shophach the captain of the host of Hadarezer went before them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="19.16 river: that is, Euphrates">f</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="19.16 Shophach: also called, Shobach">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 22:6–7; 1 Chronicles 19:9"> And it was told David; and he gathered all Israel, and passed over Jordan, and came upon them, and set the battle in array against them. So when David had put the battle in array against the Syrians, they fought with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:18; Psalms 33:16; Psalms 18:32"> But the Syrians fled before Israel; and David slew of the Syrians seven thousand men which fought in chariots, and forty thousand footmen, and killed Shophach the captain of the host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:19; Joshua 9:9–11; 1 Kings 20:1"> And when the servants of Hadarezer saw that they were put to the worse before Israel, they made peace with David, and became his servants: neither would the Syrians help the children of Ammon any more.</span>
</div>
