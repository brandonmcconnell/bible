<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:33–34; 1 Chronicles 15:20–21; 1 Chronicles 15:7"> The sons of Laadan; the chief was Jehiel, and Zetham, and Joel, three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The sons of Shimei; Shelomith, and Haziel, and Haran, three. These were the chief of the fathers of Laadan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the sons of Shimei were, Jahath, Zina, and Jeush, and Beriah. These four were the sons of Shimei.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="23.10 Zina: or, Zizah">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Jahath was the chief, and Zizah the second: but Jeush and Beriah had not many sons; therefore they were in one reckoning, according to their father’s house.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="23.11 had…: Heb. did not multiply sons">e</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; Psalms 90:1; 1 Chronicles 26:23–25"> Now concerning Moses the man of God, his sons were named of the tribe of Levi.</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:23; 1 Chronicles 15:9; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> Of the sons of Hebron; Jeriah the first, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, and Jekameam the fourth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Of the sons of Uzziel; Michah the first, and Jesiah the second.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:28; Numbers 36:6–8"> And Eleazar died, and had no sons, but daughters: and their brethren the sons of Kish took them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="23.22 brethren: or, kinsmen">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:30"> The sons of Mushi; Mahli, and Eder, and Jeremoth, three.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 10:17; Numbers 10:21; 1 Chronicles 23:3"> These were the sons of Levi after the house of their fathers; even the chief of the fathers, as they were counted by number of names by their polls, that did the work for the service of the house of the LORD, from the age of twenty years and upward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 132:13–14; 1 Chronicles 22:18; 2 Samuel 7:11"> For David said, The LORD God of Israel hath given rest unto his people, that they may dwell in Jerusalem for ever:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="23.25 that…: or, and he dwelleth in Jerusalem, etc">k</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Psalms 72:20; 2 Samuel 23:1"> For by the last words of David the Levites were numbered from twenty years old and above:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="23.27 numbered: Heb. number">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:3–6; 2 Chronicles 31:11; 2 Chronicles 35:11–14"> Because their office was to wait on the sons of Aaron for the service of the house of the LORD, in the courts, and in the chambers, and in the purifying of all holy things, and the work of the service of the house of God;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="23.28 their…: Heb. their station was at the hand of the sons of Aaron">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 19:35–36; Exodus 25:30; Leviticus 6:20–23"> Both for the shewbread, and for the fine flour for meat offering, and for the unleavened cakes, and for that which is baked in the pan, and for that which is fried, and for all manner of measure and size;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="23.29 pan: or, flat plate">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 92:1–3; Ezra 3:10–11; 1 Chronicles 9:33"> And to stand every morning to thank and praise the LORD, and likewise at even;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13–14; Numbers 28:1–29; Numbers 10:10"> And to offer all burnt sacrifices unto the LORD in the sabbaths, in the new moons, and on the set feasts, by number, according to the order commanded unto them, continually before the LORD:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:38; Numbers 3:6–9"> And that they should keep the charge of the tabernacle of the congregation, and the charge of the holy place, and the charge of the sons of Aaron their brethren, in the service of the house of the LORD.</span>
