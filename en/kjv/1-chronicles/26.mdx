<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8"> Among these were the divisions of the porters, even among the chief men, having wards one against another, to minister in the house of the LORD.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 9:24"> Eastward were six Levites, northward four a day, southward four a day, and toward Asuppim two and two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 23:11"> At Parbar westward, four at the causeway, and two at Parbar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 16:11"> These are the divisions of the porters among the sons of Kore, and among the sons of Merari.</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 3:27; 1 Chronicles 23:12; Numbers 3:19"> Of the Amramites, and the Izharites, the Hebronites, and the Uzzielites:</span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:11; 1 Chronicles 29:2–9; 1 Chronicles 22:14"> Which Shelomith and his brethren were over all the treasures of the dedicated things, which David the king, and the chief fathers, the captains over thousands and hundreds, and the captains of the host, had dedicated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 12:14; Joshua 6:19; Nehemiah 10:32–34"> Out of the spoils won in battles did they dedicate to maintain the house of the LORD.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="26.27 spoils…: Heb. battles and spoils">j</a></sup></span>

</div>

In the fortieth year of the reign of David they were sought for, and there were found among them mighty men of valour at Jazer of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:11; 1 Chronicles 12:37; 1 Chronicles 23:24"> And his brethren, men of valour, were two thousand and seven hundred chief fathers, whom king David made rulers over the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half tribe of Manasseh, for every matter pertaining to God, and affairs of the king.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="26.32 affairs: Heb. thing">l</a></sup></span>
