<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nahum 3:6; Malachi 2:9; Job 20:7"> Behold, I will corrupt your seed, and spread dung upon your faces, even the dung of your solemn feasts; and one shall take you away with it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.3 corrupt: or, reprove">a</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.3 spread: Heb. scatter">b</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.3 one…: or, it shall take you away to it">c</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 25:12–13; Ezekiel 34:25; Ezekiel 37:26"> My covenant was with him of life and peace; and I gave them to him for the fear wherewith he feared me, and was afraid before my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; 2 Timothy 2:15–16; Revelation 14:5"> The law of truth was in his mouth, and iniquity was not found in his lips: he walked with me in peace and equity, and did turn many away from iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24–25; Leviticus 10:11; Deuteronomy 21:5"> For the priest’s lips should keep knowledge, and they should seek the law at his mouth: for he is the messenger of the LORD of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:29; Jeremiah 18:15; Isaiah 30:11"> But ye are departed out of the way; ye have caused many to stumble at the law; ye have corrupted the covenant of Levi, saith the LORD of hosts.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.8 stumble at: or, fall in">d</a></sup></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 19:4–6; Malachi 2:14; Mark 10:6–8"> And did not he make one? Yet had he the residue of the spirit. And wherefore one?
