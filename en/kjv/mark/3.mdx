<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Luke 9:1–6; John 15:16"> And he ordained twelve, that they should be with him, and that he might send them forth to preach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And to have power to heal sicknesses, and to cast out devils:</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14; Matthew 9:9"> And Andrew, and Philip, and Bartholomew, and Matthew, and Thomas, and James the son of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus, and Simon the Canaanite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 6:71; John 13:2; Acts 1:16–25"> And Judas Iscariot, which also betrayed him: and they went into an house.</span>
