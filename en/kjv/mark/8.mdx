<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 15:32–39; Mark 6:34–44"> In those days the multitude being very great, and having nothing to eat, Jesus called his disciples unto him, and saith unto them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Micah 7:19; Psalms 103:13"> <span className="woj">I have compassion on the multitude, because they have now been with me three days, and have nothing to eat:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Judges 8:4–6; 1 Samuel 30:10–12"> <span className="woj">And if I send them away fasting to their own houses, they will faint by the way: for divers of them came from far.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; 2 Kings 7:2; Mark 6:52"> And his disciples answered him, From whence can a man satisfy these men with bread here in the wilderness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 14:15–17; Matthew 15:34; Luke 9:13"> And he asked them,<span className="woj">How many loaves have ye?</span>And they said, Seven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Matthew 15:35–36; John 6:10–11"> And he commanded the people to sit down on the ground: and he took the seven loaves, and gave thanks, and brake, and gave to his disciples to set before them; and they did set them before the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; John 21:5; Luke 24:41–42"> And they had a few small fishes: and he blessed, and commanded to set them also before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; Psalms 145:16; Matthew 16:10"> So they did eat, and were filled: and they took up of the broken meat that was left seven baskets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And they that had eaten were about four thousand: and he sent them away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 15:39"> And straightway he entered into a ship with his disciples, and came into the parts of Dalmanutha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Matthew 12:38; Deuteronomy 6:16"> And the Pharisees came forth, and began to question with him, seeking of him a sign from heaven, tempting him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:39–40; Mark 7:34; Matthew 16:4"> And he sighed deeply in his spirit, and saith,<span className="woj">Why doth this generation seek after a sign? verily I say unto you, There shall no sign be given unto this generation.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 12:36; Acts 13:45–46; Psalms 81:12"> And he left them, and entering into the ship again departed to the other side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 16:5"> Now the disciples had forgotten to take bread, neither had they in the ship with them more than one loaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:6–8; Matthew 16:6; Matthew 16:11–12"> And he charged them, saying,<span className="woj">Take heed, beware of the leaven of the Pharisees, and of the leaven of Herod.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 9:46; Luke 20:5; Matthew 16:7–8"> And they reasoned among themselves, saying, It is because we have no bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 6:52; Isaiah 63:17; Mark 2:8"> And when Jesus knew it, he saith unto them,<span className="woj">Why reason ye, because ye have no bread? perceive ye not yet, neither understand? have ye your heart yet hardened?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 28:26–27; Matthew 13:14–15; Jeremiah 5:21"> <span className="woj">Having eyes, see ye not? and having ears, hear ye not? and do ye not remember?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 9:12–17; Matthew 14:17–21; John 6:5–13"> <span className="woj">When I brake the five loaves among five thousand, how many baskets full of fragments took ye up?</span>They say unto him, Twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 15:34–38; Mark 8:1–9"> <span className="woj">And when the seven among four thousand, how many baskets full of fragments took ye up?</span>And they said, Seven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 6:52; Matthew 16:11–12; Mark 8:12"> And he said unto them,<span className="woj">How is it that ye do not understand?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 11:21; Mark 6:45; Luke 10:13"> And he cometh to Bethsaida; and they bring a blind man unto him, and besought him to touch him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 7:33; John 9:6–7; Mark 5:23"> And he took the blind man by the hand, and led him out of the town; and when he had spit on his eyes, and put his hands upon him, he asked him if he saw ought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 32:3; Isaiah 29:18; 1 Corinthians 13:9–12"> And he looked up, and said, I see men as trees, walking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; 2 Peter 3:18; Proverbs 4:18"> After that he put his hands again upon his eyes, and made him look up: and he was restored, and saw every man clearly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Mark 8:23; Mark 5:43"> And he sent him away to his house, saying,<span className="woj">Neither go into the town, nor tell it to any in the town.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:13–20; Luke 9:18–20"> And Jesus went out, and his disciples, into the towns of Cæsarea Philippi: and by the way he asked his disciples, saying unto them,<span className="woj">Whom do men say that I am?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:21; Matthew 14:2; Malachi 4:5"> And they answered, John the Baptist: but some say, Elias; and others, One of the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Matthew 16:15–16; 1 John 5:1"> And he saith unto them,<span className="woj">But whom say ye that I am?</span>And Peter answereth and saith unto him, Thou art the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 9:21; Matthew 16:20; Mark 9:9"> And he charged them that they should tell no man of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 10:33–34; John 2:19; Luke 24:26"> And he began to teach them, that the Son of man must suffer many things, and be rejected of the elders, and of the chief priests, and scribes, and be killed, and after three days rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 16:22; John 16:25; John 16:29"> And he spake that saying openly. And Peter took him, and began to rebuke him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 4:10; Philippians 3:19; 1 John 2:15"> But when he had turned about and looked on his disciples, he rebuked Peter, saying,<span className="woj">Get thee behind me, Satan: for thou savourest not the things that be of God, but the things that be of men.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 14:26–27; John 10:27"> And when he had called the people unto him with his disciples also, he said unto them,<span className="woj">Whosoever will come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:11–13; 1 Corinthians 9:23; Luke 17:33"> <span className="woj">For whosoever will save his life shall lose it; but whosoever shall lose his life for my sake and the gospel’s, the same shall save it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Luke 9:25; Psalms 49:17"> <span className="woj">For what shall it profit a man, if he shall gain the whole world, and lose his own soul?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–8; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> <span className="woj">Or what shall a man give in exchange for his soul?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 12:8–9; Romans 1:16; 1 John 2:23"> <span className="woj">Whosoever therefore shall be ashamed of me and of my words in this adulterous and sinful generation; of him also shall the Son of man be ashamed, when he cometh in the glory of his Father with the holy angels.</span></span>
</div>
