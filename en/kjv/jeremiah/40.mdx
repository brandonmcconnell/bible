So the captain of the guard gave him victuals and a reward, and let him go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:14; Judges 20:1; Judges 21:1"> Then went Jeremiah unto Gedaliah the son of Ahikam to Mizpah; and dwelt with him among the people that were left in the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:10; 2 Kings 25:4; Jeremiah 39:4"> Now when all the captains of the forces which were in the fields, even they and their men, heard that the king of Babylon had made Gedaliah the son of Ahikam governor in the land, and had committed unto him men, and women, and children, and of the poor of the land, of them that were not carried away captive to Babylon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:1; Deuteronomy 3:14; Jeremiah 42:8"> Then they came to Gedaliah to Mizpah, even Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, and Johanan and Jonathan the sons of Kareah, and Seraiah the son of Tanhumeth, and the sons of Ephai the Netophathite, and Jezaniah the son of a Maachathite, they and their men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:11; 2 Kings 25:24; 1 Samuel 20:16–17"> And Gedaliah the son of Ahikam the son of Shaphan sware unto them and to their men, saying, Fear not to serve the Chaldeans: dwell in the land, and serve the king of Babylon, and it shall be well with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:12; Deuteronomy 1:38; Isaiah 16:9"> As for me, behold, I will dwell at Mizpah to serve the Chaldeans, which will come unto us: but ye, gather ye wine, and summer fruits, and oil, and put them in your vessels, and dwell in your cities that ye have taken.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="40.10 to serve: Heb. to stand before">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 16:4; Genesis 36:8; Jeremiah 24:9"> Likewise when all the Jews that were in Moab, and among the Ammonites, and in Edom, and that were in all the countries, heard that the king of Babylon had left a remnant of Judah, and that he had set over them Gedaliah the son of Ahikam the son of Shaphan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:5"> Even all the Jews returned out of all places whither they were driven, and came to the land of Judah, to Gedaliah, unto Mizpah, and gathered wine and summer fruits very much.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:10; 2 Samuel 10:1–6; Jeremiah 25:21"> And said unto him, Dost thou certainly know that Baalis the king of the Ammonites hath sent Ishmael the son of Nethaniah to slay thee? But Gedaliah the son of Ahikam believed them not.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="40.14 to slay…: Heb. to strike thee in soul?">e</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 10:16–17; Romans 3:8; Jeremiah 41:2"> But Gedaliah the son of Ahikam said unto Johanan the son of Kareah, Thou shalt not do this thing: for thou speakest falsely of Ishmael.</span>
