<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word which came to Jeremiah from the LORD, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 1:1; Acts 9:6; Jeremiah 19:1–2"> Arise, and go down to the potter’s house, and there I will cause thee to hear my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 15:14; Jonah 1:3; Acts 26:19"> Then I went down to the potter’s house, and, behold, he wrought a work on the wheels.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="18.3 wheels: or, frames, or, seats">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:20–23; Jeremiah 18:6; Isaiah 45:9"> And the vessel that he made of clay was marred in the hand of the potter: so he made it again another vessel, as seemed good to the potter to make it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="18.4 of clay…: or, was marred, as clay in the hand of the potter">b</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="18.4 made it…: Heb. returned and made, etc">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Romans 9:20–21; Jeremiah 18:4"> O house of Israel, cannot I do with you as this potter? saith the LORD. Behold, as the clay is in the potter’s hand, so are ye in mine hand, O house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 12:14–17; Jeremiah 45:4"> At what instant I shall speak concerning a nation, and concerning a kingdom, to pluck up, and to pull down, and to destroy it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:21; Jeremiah 26:13; Jonah 3:9–10"> If that nation, against whom I have pronounced, turn from their evil, I will repent of the evil that I thought to do unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Amos 9:11–15; Jeremiah 1:10"> And at what instant I shall speak concerning a nation, and concerning a kingdom, to build and to plant it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:18; 1 Samuel 2:30; Ezekiel 18:24"> If it do evil in my sight, that it obey not my voice, then I will repent of the good, wherewith I said I would benefit them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 17:13; Isaiah 55:6–7; Jeremiah 35:15"> Now therefore go to, speak to the men of Judah, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, saying, Thus saith the LORD; Behold, I frame evil against you, and devise a device against you: return ye now every one from his evil way, and make your ways and your doings good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:25; Jeremiah 7:24; Deuteronomy 29:19"> And they said, There is no hope: but we will walk after our own devices, and we will every one do the imagination of his evil heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:30; Hosea 6:10; Jeremiah 23:14"> Therefore thus saith the LORD; Ask ye now among the heathen, who hath heard such things: the virgin of Israel hath done a very horrible thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 6:68"> Will a man leave the snow of Lebanon which cometh from the rock of the field? or shall the cold flowing waters that come from another place be forsaken?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="18.14 the snow…: or, my fields for a rock, or for the snow of Lebanon? shall the running waters be forsaken for the strange cold waters?">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; Jeremiah 2:32; Hosea 11:2"> Because my people hath forgotten me, they have burned incense to vanity, and they have caused them to stumble in their ways from the ancient paths, to walk in paths, in a way not cast up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 22:7; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 19:8"> To make their land desolate, and a perpetual hissing; every one that passeth thereby shall be astonished, and wag his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:24; Jeremiah 2:27; Job 27:21"> I will scatter them as with an east wind before the enemy; I will shew them the back, and not the face, in the day of their calamity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 52:2; Jeremiah 11:19; Malachi 2:7"> Then said they, Come, and let us devise devices against Jeremiah; for the law shall not perish from the priest, nor counsel from the wise, nor the word from the prophet. Come, and let us smite him with the tongue, and let us not give heed to any of his words.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="18.18 with…: or, for the tongue">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:9; Jeremiah 20:12; Psalms 55:16–17"> Give heed to me, O LORD, and hearken to the voice of them that contend with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:7; Psalms 57:6; Psalms 106:23"> Shall evil be recompensed for good? for they have digged a pit for my soul. Remember that I stood before thee to speak good for them, and to turn away thy wrath from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 109:9–20; Lamentations 5:3; Amos 4:10"> Therefore deliver up their children to the famine, and pour out their blood by the force of the sword; and let their wives be bereaved of their children, and be widows; and let their men be put to death; let their young men be slain by the sword in battle.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="18.21 pour…: Heb. pour them out">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Psalms 140:5; Zephaniah 1:10–11"> Let a cry be heard from their houses, when thou shalt bring a troop suddenly upon them: for they have digged a pit to take me, and hid snares for my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 69:22–28; Nehemiah 4:4–5"> Yet, LORD, thou knowest all their counsel against me to slay me: forgive not their iniquity, neither blot out their sin from thy sight, but let them be overthrown before thee; deal thus with them in the time of thine anger.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="18.23 to slay…: Heb. for death">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
