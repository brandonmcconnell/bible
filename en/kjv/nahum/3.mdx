<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:3; Nahum 2:3–4; Job 39:22–25"> The noise of a whip, and the noise of the rattling of the wheels, and of the pransing horses, and of the jumping chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:11; Ezekiel 39:4; Isaiah 34:3"> The horseman lifteth up both the bright sword and the glittering spear: and there is a multitude of slain, and a great number of carcases; and there is none end of their corpses; they stumble upon their corpses:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.3 the bright…: Heb. the flame of the sword, and the lightning of the spear">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 47:9; Isaiah 47:12–13; Revelation 17:1–5"> Because of the multitude of the whoredoms of the wellfavoured harlot, the mistress of witchcrafts, that selleth nations through her whoredoms, and families through her witchcrafts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:26; Ezekiel 23:29; Jeremiah 13:22"> Behold, I am against thee, saith the LORD of hosts; and I will discover thy skirts upon thy face, and I will shew the nations thy nakedness, and the kingdoms thy shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 2:9; Jeremiah 51:37; Job 9:31"> And I will cast abominable filth upon thee, and make thee vile, and will set thee as a gazingstock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:5; Isaiah 51:19; Revelation 18:10"> And it shall come to pass, that all they that look upon thee shall flee from thee, and say, Nineveh is laid waste: who will bemoan her? whence shall I seek comforters for thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 6:2; Ezekiel 30:14–16; Jeremiah 46:25–26"> Art thou better than populous No, that was situate among the rivers, that had the waters round about it, whose rampart was the sea, and her wall was from the sea?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.8 populous…: or, nourishing, etc: Heb. No Amon">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:5; Ezekiel 27:10; Isaiah 20:5"> Ethiopia and Egypt were her strength, and it was infinite; Put and Lubim were thy helpers.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.9 thy helpers: Heb. in thy help">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Joel 3:3; Hosea 13:16"> Yet was she carried away, she went into captivity: her young children also were dashed in pieces at the top of all the streets: and they cast lots for her honourable men, and all her great men were bound in chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Isaiah 2:10; Psalms 75:8"> Thou also shalt be drunken: thou shalt be hid, thou also shalt seek strength because of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 6:13; Isaiah 28:4; Habakkuk 1:10"> All thy strong holds shall be like fig trees with the firstripe figs: if they be shaken, they shall even fall into the mouth of the eater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Isaiah 19:16; Jeremiah 50:37"> Behold, thy people in the midst of thee are women: the gates of thy land shall be set wide open unto thine enemies: the fire shall devour thy bars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:3–4; Nahum 2:1; 2 Chronicles 32:11"> Draw thee waters for the siege, fortify thy strong holds: go into clay, and tread the morter, make strong the brickkiln.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Nahum 2:13; Nahum 3:13"> There shall the fire devour thee; the sword shall cut thee off, it shall eat thee up like the cankerworm: make thyself many as the cankerworm, make thyself many as the locusts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Nehemiah 9:23; Jeremiah 33:22"> Thou hast multiplied thy merchants above the stars of heaven: the cankerworm spoileth, and flieth away.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.16 spoileth: or, spreadeth himself">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:27; Revelation 9:7"> Thy crowned are as the locusts, and thy captains as the great grasshoppers, which camp in the hedges in the cold day, but when the sun ariseth they flee away, and their place is not known where they are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 22:17; Psalms 76:5–6; Jeremiah 50:18"> Thy shepherds slumber, O king of Assyria: thy nobles shall dwell in the dust: thy people is scattered upon the mountains, and no man gathereth them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="3.18 nobles: or, valiant ones">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 1:9; Lamentations 2:15; Job 27:23"> There is no healing of thy bruise; thy wound is grievous: all that hear the bruit of thee shall clap the hands over thee: for upon whom hath not thy wickedness passed continually?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="3.19 healing: Heb. wrinkling">g</a></sup></span>
