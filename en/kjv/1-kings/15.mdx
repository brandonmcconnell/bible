<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 14:17; 1 Kings 16:15–18; 2 Chronicles 16:5"> And it came to pass, when Baasha heard thereof, that he left off building of Ramah, and dwelt in Tirzah.</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 1:8; 1 Kings 22:41–43; 2 Chronicles 17:1–9"> And Asa slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the city of David his father: and Jehoshaphat his son reigned in his stead.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="15.24 Jehoshaphat: Gr.
