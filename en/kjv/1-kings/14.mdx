<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:7–8; 1 Kings 13:7; 2 Kings 4:42"> And take with thee ten loaves, and cracknels, and a cruse of honey, and go to him: he shall tell thee what shall become of the child.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="14.3 with…: Heb. in thine hand">a</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="14.3 cracknels: or, cakes">b</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="14.3 cruse: or, bottle">c</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 5:17–28; Acts 5:9–10; Luke 20:20–23"> And it was so, when Ahijah heard the sound of her feet, as she came in at the door, that he said, Come in, thou wife of Jeroboam; why feignest thou thyself to be another? for I am sent to thee with heavy tidings.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="14.6 heavy: Heb. hard">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 16:2; 2 Samuel 12:7–8; 1 Samuel 15:16"> Go, tell Jeroboam, Thus saith the LORD God of Israel, Forasmuch as I exalted thee from among the people, and made thee prince over my people Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 15:5; 1 Kings 11:33–38; 2 Chronicles 28:1"> And rent the kingdom away from the house of David, and gave it thee: and yet thou hast not been as my servant David, who kept my commandments, and who followed me with all his heart, to do that only which was right in mine eyes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:35; Nehemiah 9:26; Psalms 50:17"> But hast done evil above all that were before thee: for thou hast gone and made thee other gods, and molten images, to provoke me to anger, and hast cast me behind thy back:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; 1 Kings 21:21; 2 Kings 14:26"> Therefore, behold, I will bring evil upon the house of Jeroboam, and will cut off from Jeroboam him that pisseth against the wall, and him that is shut up and left in Israel, and will take away the remnant of the house of Jeroboam, as a man taketh away dung, till it be all gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 16:4; Jeremiah 15:3; 1 Kings 21:23–24"> Him that dieth of Jeroboam in the city shall the dogs eat; and him that dieth in the field shall the fowls of the air eat: for the LORD hath spoken it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 14:3; 1 Kings 14:16–17; 2 Kings 1:6"> Arise thou therefore, get thee to thine own house: and when thy feet enter into the city, the child shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:3; 2 Chronicles 12:12; Job 19:28"> And all Israel shall mourn for him, and bury him: for he only of Jeroboam shall come to the grave, because in him there is found some good thing toward the LORD God of Israel in the house of Jeroboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 15:27–29; Ezekiel 7:2–7; Ezekiel 12:22–28"> Moreover the LORD shall raise him up a king over Israel, who shall cut off the house of Jeroboam that day: but what? even now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 23:15–16; Psalms 52:5; 2 Kings 15:29"> For the LORD shall smite Israel, as a reed is shaken in the water, and he shall root up Israel out of this good land, which he gave to their fathers, and shall scatter them beyond the river, because they have made their groves, provoking the LORD to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 15:34; 1 Kings 16:2; 1 Kings 13:34"> And he shall give Israel up because of the sins of Jeroboam, who did sin, and who made Israel to sin.</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:2–20; 1 Chronicles 27:24; 1 Kings 16:20"> And the rest of the acts of Jeroboam, how he warred, and how he reigned, behold, they are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:12; Psalms 3:5; 1 Kings 11:43"> And the days which Jeroboam reigned were two and twenty years: and he slept with his fathers, and Nadab his son reigned in his stead.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="14.20 slept: Heb. lay down">f</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:1; 1 Corinthians 10:22; Psalms 78:58"> And Judah did evil in the sight of the LORD, and they provoked him to jealousy with their sins which they had committed, above all that their fathers had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:2; Isaiah 57:5; 2 Kings 17:9–10"> For they also built them high places, and images, and groves, on every high hill, and under every green tree.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="14.23 images: or, standing images, or, statues">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:17; 1 Corinthians 6:9; 1 Kings 15:12"> And there were also sodomites in the land: and they did according to all the abominations of the nations which the LORD cast out before the children of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 11:40; 2 Chronicles 12:2–4; 2 Chronicles 12:9"> And it came to pass in the fifth year of king Rehoboam, that Shishak king of Egypt came up against Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 15:18; 2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 7:51"> And he took away the treasures of the house of the LORD, and the treasures of the king’s house; he even took away all: and he took away all the shields of gold which Solomon had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11; 1 Samuel 22:17; 1 Kings 18:46"> And king Rehoboam made in their stead brasen shields, and committed them unto the hands of the chief of the guard, which kept the door of the king’s house.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="14.27 guard: Heb. runners">h</a></sup></span>
