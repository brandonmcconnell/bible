<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; 2 Chronicles 18:4–5; Jeremiah 42:2–6"> And Jehoshaphat said unto the king of Israel, Enquire, I pray thee, at the word of the LORD to day.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 3:11–13; 2 Chronicles 18:6–7"> And Jehoshaphat said, Is there not here a prophet of the LORD besides, that we might enquire of him?</span>

And Jehoshaphat said, Let not the king say so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 9:32; Isaiah 39:7; 1 Kings 22:26–27"> Then the king of Israel called an officer, and said, Hasten hither Micaiah the son of Imlah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="22.9 officer: or, eunuch">b</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 1:18–21; Deuteronomy 33:17; Ezekiel 22:27–28"> And Zedekiah the son of Chenaanah made him horns of iron: and he said, Thus saith the LORD, With these shalt thou push the Syrians, until thou have consumed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:22; 1 Kings 22:6–15; 1 Kings 22:32–36"> And all the prophets prophesied so, saying, Go up to Ramoth-gilead, and prosper: for the LORD shall deliver it into the king’s hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 2:6–7; Psalms 11:1; Isaiah 30:10–11"> And the messenger that was gone to call Micaiah spake unto him, saying, Behold now, the words of the prophets declare good unto the king with one mouth: let thy word, I pray thee, be like the word of one of them, and speak that which is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 24:13; Numbers 22:18; 2 Chronicles 18:12–13"> And Micaiah said, As the LORD liveth, what the LORD saith unto me, that will I speak.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 19:13; Matthew 26:63; Joshua 6:26"> And the king said unto him, How many times shall I adjure thee that thou tell me nothing but that which is true in the name of the LORD?</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 22:8; Proverbs 10:24; Luke 11:45"> And the king of Israel said unto Jehoshaphat, Did I not tell thee that he would prophesy no good concerning me, but evil?</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:9; Job 12:16; Jeremiah 4:10"> And the LORD said, Who shall persuade Ahab, that he may go up and fall at Ramoth-gilead? <span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 20:30; 2 Peter 2:1; Revelation 19:20"> And Micaiah said, Behold, thou shalt see in that day, when thou shalt go into an inner chamber to hide thyself.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="22.25 into…: or, from chamber to chamber: Heb. a chamber in a chamber">e</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:10; 2 Chronicles 18:25–27; Revelation 2:10"> And say, Thus saith the king, Put this fellow in the prison, and feed him with bread of affliction and with water of affliction, until I come in peace.</span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:22; Jeremiah 23:24; 1 Samuel 28:8"> And the king of Israel said unto Jehoshaphat, I will disguise myself, and enter into the battle; but put thou on thy robes. <span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Kings 15:12; 1 Kings 14:24; Deuteronomy 23:17"> And the remnant of the sodomites, which remained in the days of his father Asa, he took out of the land.</span>

<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:1; 1 Kings 22:40; 1 Kings 15:24"> And Jehoshaphat slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the city of David his father: and Jehoram his son reigned in his stead.</span>
