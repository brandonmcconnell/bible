<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 41:8–9; Jeremiah 31:3; Deuteronomy 10:15"> I have loved you, saith Jehovah. Yet ye say, Wherein hast thou loved us? Was not Esau Jacob’s brother? saith Jehovah: yet I loved Jacob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Obadiah 1:10; Ezekiel 35:3–4; Jeremiah 49:16–18"> but Esau I hated, and made his mountains a desolation, and gave his heritage to the jackals of the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:14; Ezekiel 35:9; Ezekiel 11:10"> Whereas Edom saith, We are beaten down, but we will return and build the waste places; thus saith Jehovah of hosts, They shall build, but I will throw down; and men shall call them The border of wickedness, and The people against whom Jehovah hath indignation for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:7; Psalms 58:10–11; Ezekiel 38:16"> And your eyes shall see, and ye shall say, Jehovah be magnified beyond the border of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:46; Isaiah 1:2; John 13:13–17"> A son honoreth his father, and a servant his master: if then I am a father, where is mine honor? and if I am a master, where is my fear? saith Jehovah of hosts unto you, O priests, that despise my name. And ye say, Wherein have we despised thy name?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Malachi 1:12; 1 Corinthians 11:27–32; Malachi 1:8"> Ye offer polluted bread upon mine altar. And ye say, Wherein have we polluted thee? In that ye say, The table of Jehovah is contemptible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Leviticus 22:19–25; Hosea 8:13"> And when ye offer the blind for sacrifice, it is no evil! and when ye offer the lame and sick, it is no evil! Present it now unto thy governor; will he be pleased with thee? or will he accept thy person? saith Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 32:11; Jeremiah 27:18; 1 Peter 1:17"> And now, I pray you, entreat the favor of God, that he may be gracious unto us: this hath been by your means: will he accept any of your persons? saith Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Amos 5:21–24; Jeremiah 6:20"> Oh that there were one among you that would shut the doors, that ye might not kindle fire on mine altar in vain! I have no pleasure in you, saith Jehovah of hosts, neither will I accept an offering at your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:6; Isaiah 54:5; Isaiah 66:19–20"> For from the rising of the sun even unto the going down of the same my name shall be great among the Gentiles; and in every place incense shall be offered unto my name, and a pure offering: for my name shall be great among the Gentiles, saith Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 1:6–8; Malachi 2:8; Amos 2:7"> But ye profane it, in that ye say, The table of Jehovah is polluted, and the fruit thereof, even its food, is contemptible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 6:3; Leviticus 22:19–23; Mark 14:37–38"> Ye say also, Behold, what a weariness is it! and ye have snuffed at it, saith Jehovah of hosts; and ye have brought that which was taken by violence, and the lame, and the sick; thus ye bring the offering: should I accept this at your hand? saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 5:1–10; Ecclesiastes 5:4–5"> But cursed be the deceiver, who hath in his flock a male, and voweth, and sacrificeth unto the Lord a blemished thing; for I am a great King, saith Jehovah of hosts, and my name is terrible among the Gentiles.</span>
</div>
