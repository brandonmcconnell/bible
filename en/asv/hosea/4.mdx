<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Micah 6:2; Isaiah 3:13–14"> Hear the word of Jehovah, ye children of Israel; for Jehovah hath a controversy with the inhabitants of the land, because there is no truth, nor goodness, nor knowledge of God in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 6:9; Jeremiah 6:7; Hosea 7:1"> There is nought but swearing and breaking faith, and killing, and stealing, and committing adultery; they break out, and blood toucheth blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Isaiah 24:4–12; Nahum 1:4"> Therefore shall the land mourn, and every one that dwelleth therein shall languish, with the beasts of the field and the birds of the heavens; yea, the fishes of the sea also shall be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:12; Hosea 4:17; Amos 5:13"> Yet let no man strive, neither let any man reprove; for thy people are as they that strive with the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:8; Hosea 2:2; Galatians 4:26"> And thou shalt stumble in the day, and the prophet also shall stumble with thee in the night; and I will destroy thy mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13; Proverbs 19:2; Hosea 6:6"> My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge: because thou hast rejected knowledge, I will also reject thee, that thou shalt be no priest to me: seeing thou hast forgotten the law of thy God, I also will forget thy children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Philippians 3:19; 1 Samuel 2:30"> As they were multiplied, so they sinned against me: I will change their glory into shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Isaiah 56:11; Titus 1:11"> They feed on the sin of my people, and set their heart on their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 24:2; Jeremiah 5:31; Matthew 15:14"> And it shall be, like people, like priest; and I will punish them for their ways, and will requite them their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 6:14; Leviticus 26:26; Haggai 1:6"> And they shall eat, and not have enough; they shall play the harlot, and shall not increase; because they have left off taking heed to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Luke 21:34; Isaiah 28:7"> Whoredom and wine and new wine take away the understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:27; Hosea 5:4; Habakkuk 2:19"> My people ask counsel at their stock, and their staff declareth unto them; for the spirit of whoredom hath caused them to err, and they have played the harlot, departing from under their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; Jeremiah 3:6; Ezekiel 6:13"> They sacrifice upon the tops of the mountains, and burn incense upon the hills, under oaks and poplars and terebinths, because the shadow thereof is good: therefore your daughters play the harlot, and your brides commit adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; John 8:43; Ephesians 4:18"> I will not punish your daughters when they play the harlot, nor your brides when they commit adultery; for the men themselves go apart with harlots, and they sacrifice with the prostitutes; and the people that doth not understand shall be overthrown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 12:11; Hosea 9:15; Amos 4:4"> Though thou, Israel, play the harlot, yet let not Judah offend; and come not ye unto Gilgal, neither go ye up to Beth-aven, nor swear, As Jehovah liveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 8:5; Jeremiah 3:11"> For Israel hath behaved himself stubbornly, like a stubborn heifer: now will Jehovah feed them as a lamb in a large place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 81:12; Revelation 22:11; Hosea 13:2"> Ephraim is joined to idols; let him alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Amos 5:12; 2 Kings 17:7–17"> Their drink is become sour; they play the harlot continually; her rulers dearly love shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; Jeremiah 4:11–12; Hosea 13:15"> The wind hath wrapped her up in its wings; and they shall be put to shame because of their sacrifices.</span>
