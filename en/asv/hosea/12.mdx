<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 6:2; Isaiah 10:6; Hosea 4:1"> Jehovah hath also a controversy with Judah, and will punish Jacob according to his ways; according to his doings will he recompense him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:26; Genesis 32:24–28; Romans 9:11"> In the womb he took his brother by the heel; and in his manhood he had power with God:</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:15; Isaiah 42:8; Psalms 135:13"> even Jehovah, the God of hosts; Jehovah is his memorial name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Hosea 14:1; Micah 7:7"> Therefore turn thou to thy God: keep kindness and justice, and wait for thy God continually.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Psalms 62:10; Psalms 52:7"> And Ephraim said, Surely I am become rich, I have found me wealth: in all my labors they shall find in me no iniquity that were sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40–43; Nehemiah 8:15–17; Hosea 13:4"> But I am Jehovah thy God from the land of Egypt; I will yet again make thee to dwell in tents, as in the days of the solemn feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 17:13; Ezekiel 20:49; Jeremiah 19:1"> I have also spoken unto the prophets, and I have multiplied visions; and by the ministry of the prophets have I used similitudes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Hosea 6:8; Hosea 8:11"> Is Gilead iniquity? they are altogether false; in Gilgal they sacrifice bullocks; yea, their altars are as heaps in the furrows of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:41; Genesis 27:43; Genesis 28:1–22"> And Jacob fled into the field of Aram, and Israel served for a wife, and for a wife he kept sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:50–51; Acts 3:22–23; Psalms 77:20"> And by a prophet Jehovah brought Israel up out of Egypt, and by a prophet was he preserved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:13; Daniel 11:18; 2 Kings 17:7–18"> Ephraim hath provoked to anger most bitterly: therefore shall his blood be left upon him, and his reproach shall his Lord return unto him.</span>
