<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 9:8; Micah 7:2; Micah 3:9"> Hear this, O ye priests, and hearken, O house of Israel, and give ear, O house of the king; for unto you pertaineth the judgment; for ye have been a snare at Mizpah, and a net spread upon Tabor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 9:15; Isaiah 29:15; Hosea 6:9"> And the revolters are gone deep in making slaughter; but I am a rebuker of them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 3:2; Hebrews 4:13; Hosea 13:1"> I know Ephraim, and Israel is not hid from me; for now, O Ephraim, thou hast played the harlot, Israel is defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:12; 1 John 2:3–4; Hosea 4:1"> Their doings will not suffer them to turn unto their God; for the spirit of whoredom is within them, and they know not Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 7:10; Amos 5:2; Ezekiel 23:31–35"> And the pride of Israel doth testify to his face: therefore Israel and Ephraim shall stumble in their iniquity; Judah also shall stumble with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; John 7:34; Proverbs 1:28"> They shall go with their flocks and with their herds to seek Jehovah; but they shall not find him: he hath withdrawn himself from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 6:7; Isaiah 48:8; Jeremiah 3:20"> They have dealt treacherously against Jehovah; for they have borne strange children: now shall the new moon devour them with their fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Hosea 9:9; 1 Samuel 15:34"> Blow ye the cornet in Gibeah, and the trumpet in Ramah: sound an alarm at Beth-aven; behind thee, O Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 1:6; Isaiah 46:10; Hosea 9:11–17"> Ephraim shall become a desolation in the day of rebuke: among the tribes of Israel have I made known that which shall surely be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Deuteronomy 27:17; Psalms 93:3–4"> The princes of Judah are like them that remove the landmark: I will pour out my wrath upon them like water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Micah 6:16; 1 Kings 12:26–33"> Ephraim is oppressed, he is crushed in judgment; because he was content to walk after man’s command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 51:8; Job 13:28; Isaiah 50:9"> Therefore am I unto Ephraim as a moth, and to the house of Judah as rottenness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 7:11; Hosea 12:1; Hosea 10:6"> When Ephraim saw his sickness, and Judah saw his wound, then went Ephraim to Assyria, and sent to king Jareb: but he is not able to heal you, neither will he cure you of your wound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 7:2; Micah 5:8; Hosea 13:7–8"> For I will be unto Ephraim as a lion, and as a young lion to the house of Judah: I, even I, will tear and go away; I will carry off, and there shall be none to deliver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 26:16; Jeremiah 29:12–14; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> I will go and return to my place, till they acknowledge their offence, and seek my face: in their affliction they will seek me earnestly.</span>
