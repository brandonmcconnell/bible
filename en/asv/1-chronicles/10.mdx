<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:1–13; 1 Chronicles 10:8; 2 Samuel 1:6"> Now the Philistines fought against Israel: and the men of Israel fled from before the Philistines, and fell down slain in mount Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:39; Exodus 20:5; 1 Samuel 14:6"> And the Philistines followed hard after Saul and after his sons; and the Philistines slew Jonathan, and Abinadab, and Malchi-shua, the sons of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 49:23–24; 1 Samuel 31:3–6; 2 Samuel 1:4–10"> And the battle went sore against Saul, and the archers overtook him; and he was distressed by reason of the archers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:4; Judges 9:54; 1 Samuel 17:26"> Then said Saul unto his armor-bearer, Draw thy sword, and thrust me through therewith, lest these uncircumcised come and abuse me. But his armor-bearer would not; for he was sore afraid. Therefore Saul took his sword, and fell upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And when his armor-bearer saw that Saul was dead, he likewise fell upon his sword, and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 13:10–11; 1 Samuel 4:10–11; 1 Samuel 4:18"> So Saul died, and his three sons; and all his house died together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 26:36; Deuteronomy 28:33; Judges 6:2"> And when all the men of Israel that were in the valley saw that they fled, and that Saul and his sons were dead, they forsook their cities, and fled; and the Philistines came and dwelt in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:8; 2 Kings 3:23; 2 Chronicles 20:25"> And it came to pass on the morrow, when the Philistines came to strip the slain, that they found Saul and his sons fallen in mount Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Judges 16:23–24; Matthew 14:11"> And they stripped him, and took his head, and his armor, and sent into the land of the Philistines round about, to carry the tidings unto their idols, and to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:2–7; 1 Samuel 31:10"> And they put his armor in the house of their gods, and fastened his head in the house of Dagon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:11–13; 1 Samuel 11:1–11; 2 Samuel 2:4–7"> And when all Jabesh-gilead heard all that the Philistines had done to Saul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 35:8; 2 Samuel 3:35; Genesis 50:10"> all the valiant men arose, and took away the body of Saul, and the bodies of his sons, and brought them to Jabesh, and buried their bones under the oak in Jabesh, and fasted seven days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 20:6; Leviticus 19:31; 1 Samuel 15:23"> So Saul died for his trespass which he committed against Jehovah, because of the word of Jehovah, which he kept not; and also for that he asked counsel of one that had a familiar spirit, to inquire thereby,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:28; 1 Samuel 13:14; 1 Samuel 28:17"> and inquired not of Jehovah: therefore he slew him, and turned the kingdom unto David the son of Jesse.</span>
