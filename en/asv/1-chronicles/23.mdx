<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:28; 1 Chronicles 28:5; 1 Kings 1:33–39"> Now David was old and full of days; and he made Solomon his son king over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 24:1; 2 Chronicles 34:29–30; 1 Chronicles 13:1"> And he gathered together all the princes of Israel, with the priests and the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Numbers 4:2–49"> And the Levites were numbered from thirty years old and upward: and their number by their polls, man by man, was thirty and eight thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:8; Deuteronomy 16:18; 1 Chronicles 26:29–31"> Of these, twenty and four thousand were to oversee the work of the house of Jehovah; and six thousand were officers and judges;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:25–26; 2 Chronicles 8:14; Psalms 87:7"> and four thousand were doorkeepers; and four thousand praised Jehovah with the instruments which I made, said David, to praise therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 6:1; 1 Chronicles 6:16"> And David divided them into courses according to the sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:21; Exodus 6:17; 1 Chronicles 6:17–20"> Of the Gershonites: Ladan and Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:33–34; 1 Chronicles 15:20–21; 1 Chronicles 15:7"> The sons of Ladan: Jehiel the chief, and Zetham, and Joel, three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The sons of Shimei: Shelomoth, and Haziel, and Haran, three. These were the heads of the fathers’ houses of Ladan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the sons of Shimei: Jahath, Zina, and Jeush, and Beriah. These four were the sons of Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Jahath was the chief, and Zizah the second: but Jeush and Beriah had not many sons; therefore they became a fathers’ house in one reckoning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; Numbers 3:27; Numbers 26:58"> The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel, four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Exodus 6:20; 1 Samuel 2:28"> The sons of Amram: Aaron and Moses; and Aaron was separated, that he should sanctify the most holy things, he and his sons, for ever, to burn incense before Jehovah, to minister unto him, and to bless in his name, for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; Psalms 90:1; 1 Chronicles 26:23–25"> But as for Moses the man of God, his sons were named among the tribe of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 18:3–4; Exodus 2:22; Exodus 4:20"> The sons of Moses: Gershom and Eliezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:24; 1 Chronicles 25:20; 1 Chronicles 24:20"> The sons of Gershom: Shebuel the chief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:25"> And the sons of Eliezer were: Rehabiah the chief; and Eliezer had no other sons; but the sons of Rehabiah were very many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:26; 1 Chronicles 24:22"> The sons of Izhar: Shelomith the chief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:23; 1 Chronicles 15:9; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> The sons of Hebron: Jeriah the chief, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, and Jekameam the fourth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The sons of Uzziel: Micah the chief, and Isshiah the second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 6:19; 1 Chronicles 6:20; 1 Chronicles 24:26–30"> The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Mahli: Eleazar and Kish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:28; Numbers 36:6–8"> And Eleazar died, and had no sons, but daughters only: and their brethren the sons of Kish took them to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:30"> The sons of Mushi: Mahli, and Eder, and Jeremoth, three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 10:17; Numbers 10:21; 1 Chronicles 23:3"> These were the sons of Levi after their fathers’ houses, even the heads of the fathers’ houses of those of them that were counted, in the number of names by their polls, who did the work for the service of the house of Jehovah, from twenty years old and upward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 132:13–14; 1 Chronicles 22:18; 2 Samuel 7:11"> For David said, Jehovah, the God of Israel, hath given rest unto his people; and he dwelleth in Jerusalem for ever:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 4:5; Numbers 7:9; Numbers 4:15"> and also the Levites shall no more have need to carry the tabernacle and all the vessels of it for the service thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Psalms 72:20; 2 Samuel 23:1"> For by the last words of David the sons of Levi were numbered, from twenty years old and upward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:3–6; 2 Chronicles 31:11; 2 Chronicles 35:11–14"> For their office was to wait on the sons of Aaron for the service of the house of Jehovah, in the courts, and in the chambers, and in the purifying of all holy things, even the work of the service of the house of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 19:35–36; Exodus 25:30; Leviticus 6:20–23"> for the showbread also, and for the fine flour for a meal-offering, whether of unleavened wafers, or of that which is baked in the pan, or of that which is soaked, and for all manner of measure and size;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 92:1–3; Ezra 3:10–11; 1 Chronicles 9:33"> and to stand every morning to thank and praise Jehovah, and likewise at even;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13–14; Numbers 28:1–29; Numbers 10:10"> and to offer all burnt-offerings unto Jehovah, on the sabbaths, on the new moons, and on the set feasts, in number according to the ordinance concerning them, continually before Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:38; Numbers 3:6–9"> and that they should keep the charge of the tent of meeting, and the charge of the holy place, and the charge of the sons of Aaron their brethren, for the service of the house of Jehovah.</span>
