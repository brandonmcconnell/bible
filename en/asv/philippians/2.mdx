<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:12; 1 John 4:12; 1 John 4:7–8"> If there is therefore any exhortation in Christ, if any consolation of love, if any fellowship of the Spirit, if any tender mercies and compassions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 12:16; 1 Peter 3:8–9; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> make full my joy, that ye be of the same mind, having the same love, being of one accord, of one mind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 12:10; Galatians 5:26; Ephesians 5:21"> doing nothing through faction or through vainglory, but in lowliness of mind each counting other better than himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Romans 15:1; James 2:8"> not looking each of you to his own things, but each of you also to the things of others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 11:29; 1 Peter 2:21"> Have this mind in you, which was also in Christ Jesus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 5:18; 2 Corinthians 4:4; Hebrews 1:3"> who, existing in the form of God, counted not the being on an equality with God a thing to be grasped,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:9; Matthew 20:28; John 1:14"> but emptied himself, taking the form of a servant, being made in the likeness of men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:18; Hebrews 12:2; Romans 5:19"> and being found in fashion as a man, he humbled himself, becoming obedient even unto death, yea, the death of the cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Matthew 28:18; 1 Peter 3:22"> Wherefore also God highly exalted him, and gave unto him the name which is above every name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 45:23–25; Matthew 28:18; Revelation 5:13–14"> that in the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of things in heaven and things on earth and things under the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Romans 14:11; John 13:13"> and that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–10; Luke 13:23–24; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> So then, my beloved, even as ye have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:21; 2 Corinthians 3:5; 2 Timothy 1:9"> for it is God who worketh in you both to will and to work, for his good pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 4:9; 1 Corinthians 10:10; Philippians 2:3"> Do all things without murmurings and questionings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:14–16; 1 Peter 2:12; Matthew 5:45"> that ye may become blameless and harmless, children of God without blemish in the midst of a crooked and perverse generation, among whom ye are seen as lights in the world,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:26; Galatians 2:2; 1 John 1:1"> holding forth the word of life; that I may have whereof to glory in the day of Christ, that I did not run in vain neither labor in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6; Romans 15:16; Hebrews 13:15–16"> Yea, and if I am offered upon the sacrifice and service of your faith, I joy, and rejoice with you all:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 1:2–4; Philippians 4:4; Philippians 3:1"> and in the same manner do ye also joy, and rejoice with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:17; 1 Thessalonians 3:6–8; 1 Thessalonians 3:2"> But I hope in the Lord Jesus to send Timothy shortly unto you, that I also may be of good comfort, when I know your state.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Philippians 2:2; 1 Corinthians 16:10; Philippians 2:22"> For I have no man likeminded, who will care truly for your state.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Matthew 16:24; Philippians 2:4"> For they all seek their own, not the things of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Corinthians 2:9; 2 Timothy 1:2"> But ye know the proof of him, that, as a child serveth a father, so he served with me in furtherance of the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:3; Philippians 2:19"> Him therefore I hope to send forthwith, so soon as I shall see how it will go with me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="3 John 1:14; Philemon 1:22; Philippians 1:25–26"> but I trust in the Lord that I myself also shall come shortly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 4:18; Philemon 1:1–2; Philippians 4:3"> But I counted it necessary to send to you Epaphroditus, my brother and fellow-worker and fellow-soldier, and your messenger and minister to my need;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Proverbs 12:25; Ephesians 3:13"> since he longed after you all, and was sore troubled, because ye had heard that he was sick:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Philippians 2:30; Psalms 103:3–4"> for indeed he was sick nigh unto death: but God had mercy on him; and not on him only, but on me also, that I might not have sorrow upon sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 16:22; 2 Timothy 1:4; Philippians 2:26–27"> I have sent him therefore the more diligently, that, when ye see him again, ye may rejoice, and that I may be the less sorrowful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12; 1 Corinthians 16:18; 1 Timothy 5:17"> Receive him therefore in the Lord with all joy; and hold such in honor:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:17; Acts 20:24; Philippians 4:10"> because for the work of Christ he came nigh unto death, hazarding his life to supply that which was lacking in your service toward me.</span>
