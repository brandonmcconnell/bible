<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:7–8; 2 Chronicles 11:13; Hosea 5:4"> And Hezekiah sent to all Israel and Judah, and wrote letters also to Ephraim and Manasseh, that they should come to the house of Jehovah at Jerusalem, to keep the passover unto Jehovah, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 9:10–11; 1 Chronicles 13:1–3; 2 Chronicles 30:13"> For the king had taken counsel, and his princes, and all the assembly in Jerusalem, to keep the passover in the second month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; Exodus 12:6; Exodus 12:18"> For they could not keep it at that time, because the priests had not sanctified themselves in sufficient number, neither had the people gathered themselves together to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:4"> And the thing was right in the eyes of the king and of all the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Leviticus 23:2; Esther 8:8–10"> So they established a decree to make proclamation throughout all Israel, from Beer-sheba even to Dan, that they should come to keep the passover unto Jehovah, the God of Israel, at Jerusalem: for they had not kept it in great numbers in such sort as it is written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:31; Esther 8:14; 2 Kings 15:19"> So the posts went with the letters from the king and his princes throughout all Israel and Judah, and according to the commandment of the king, saying, Ye children of Israel, turn again unto Jehovah, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, that he may return to the remnant that are escaped of you out of the hand of the kings of Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:8; Ezekiel 20:13–18; Zechariah 1:3–4"> And be not ye like your fathers, and like your brethren, who trespassed against Jehovah, the God of their fathers, so that he gave them up to desolation, as ye see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 32:9; 2 Chronicles 29:10; Deuteronomy 10:16"> Now be ye not stiffnecked, as your fathers were; but yield yourselves unto Jehovah, and enter into his sanctuary, which he hath sanctified for ever, and serve Jehovah your God, that his fierce anger may turn away from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 55:7; Micah 7:18; Psalms 86:5"> For if ye turn again unto Jehovah, your brethren and your children shall find compassion before them that led them captive, and shall come again into this land: for Jehovah your God is gracious and merciful, and will not turn away his face from you, if ye return unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; 2 Chronicles 30:6; Nehemiah 2:19"> So the posts passed from city to city through the country of Ephraim and Manasseh, even unto Zebulun: but they laughed them to scorn, and mocked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:18; 2 Chronicles 30:21; 2 Chronicles 30:25"> Nevertheless certain men of Asher and Manasseh and of Zebulun humbled themselves, and came to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Jeremiah 24:7; Philippians 2:13"> Also upon Judah came the hand of God to give them one heart, to do the commandment of the king and of the princes by the word of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:2; Psalms 84:7"> And there assembled at Jerusalem much people to keep the feast of unleavened bread in the second month, a very great assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:24; 2 Samuel 15:23; 2 Chronicles 15:16"> And they arose and took away the altars that were in Jerusalem, and all the altars for incense took they away, and cast them into the brook Kidron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; 2 Chronicles 5:11; Exodus 19:22"> Then they killed the passover on the fourteenth day of the second month: and the priests and the Levites were ashamed, and sanctified themselves, and brought burnt-offerings into the house of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; 2 Chronicles 35:15; 2 Kings 11:14"> And they stood in their place after their order, according to the law of Moses the man of God: the priests sprinkled the blood which they received of the hand of the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; 2 Chronicles 35:3–6; Exodus 12:6"> For there were many in the assembly that had not sanctified themselves: therefore the Levites had the charge of killing the passovers for every one that was not clean, to sanctify them unto Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:28; James 5:15–16; Daniel 9:19"> For a multitude of the people, even many of Ephraim and Manasseh, Issachar and Zebulun, had not cleansed themselves, yet did they eat the passover otherwise than it is written. For Hezekiah had prayed for them, saying, The good Jehovah pardon every one</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:3; Proverbs 23:26; Job 11:13"> that setteth his heart to seek God, Jehovah, the God of his fathers, though he be not cleansed according to the purification of the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; James 5:15–16; Psalms 103:3"> And Jehovah hearkened to Hezekiah, and healed the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 12:15; Exodus 13:6; Deuteronomy 12:12"> And the children of Israel that were present at Jerusalem kept the feast of unleavened bread seven days with great gladness; and the Levites and the priests praised Jehovah day by day, singing with loud instruments unto Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:6; Nehemiah 9:3; Isaiah 40:1–2"> And Hezekiah spake comfortably unto all the Levites that had good understanding in the service of Jehovah. So they did eat throughout the feast for the seven days, offering sacrifices of peace-offerings, and making confession to Jehovah, the God of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 8:65; 2 Chronicles 7:9"> And the whole assembly took counsel to keep other seven days; and they kept other seven days with gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; 2 Chronicles 35:7–8; Ephesians 4:8"> For Hezekiah king of Judah did give to the assembly for offerings a thousand bullocks and seven thousand sheep; and the princes gave to the assembly a thousand bullocks and ten thousand sheep: and a great number of priests sanctified themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:11; 2 Chronicles 30:18; 1 Chronicles 16:10–11"> And all the assembly of Judah, with the priests and the Levites, and all the assembly that came out of Israel, and the sojourners that came out of the land of Israel, and that dwelt in Judah, rejoiced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:8–10"> So there was great joy in Jerusalem; for since the time of Solomon the son of David king of Israel there was not the like in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalms 68:5; Numbers 6:23–26"> Then the priests the Levites arose and blessed the people: and their voice was heard, and their prayer came up to his holy habitation, even unto heaven.</span>
