<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 83:5–8; Isaiah 16:6; 2 Chronicles 32:1"> And it came to pass after this, that the children of Moab, and the children of Ammon, and with them some of the Ammonites, came against Jehoshaphat to battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 14:7; 1 Samuel 23:29; Joshua 15:62"> Then there came some that told Jehoshaphat, saying, There cometh a great multitude against thee from beyond the sea from Syria; and, behold, they are in Hazazon-tamar (the same is En-gedi).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 9:3; 2 Chronicles 19:3; 1 Samuel 7:6"> And Jehoshaphat feared, and set himself to seek unto Jehovah; and he proclaimed a fast throughout all Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 60:10–12; Psalms 69:35"> And Judah gathered themselves together, to seek help of Jehovah: even out of all the cities of Judah they came to seek Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 19:15–19; 2 Chronicles 6:12–13; 2 Chronicles 34:31"> And Jehoshaphat stood in the assembly of Judah and Jerusalem, in the house of Jehovah, before the new court;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; 1 Chronicles 29:11–12; Daniel 4:17"> and he said, O Jehovah, the God of our fathers, art not thou God in heaven? and art not thou ruler over all the kingdoms of the nations? and in thy hand is power and might, so that none is able to withstand thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 41:8; James 2:23; Psalms 44:2"> Didst not thou, O our God, drive out the inhabitants of this land before thy people Israel, and give it to the seed of Abraham thy friend for ever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:10; 2 Chronicles 2:4"> And they dwelt therein, and have built thee a sanctuary therein for thy name, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:28–30; 2 Chronicles 6:20; 1 Kings 8:33"> If evil come upon us, the sword, judgment, or pestilence, or famine, we will stand before this house, and before thee (for thy name is in this house), and cry unto thee in our affliction, and thou wilt hear and save.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Deuteronomy 2:4–5; Deuteronomy 2:9"> And now, behold, the children of Ammon and Moab and mount Seir, whom thou wouldest not let Israel invade, when they came out of the land of Egypt, but they turned aside from them, and destroyed them not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 83:3–12; Judges 11:23–24; Jeremiah 18:20"> behold, how they reward us, to come to cast us out of thy possession, which thou hast given us to inherit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 121:1–2; Psalms 25:15; Psalms 141:8"> O our God, wilt thou not judge them? for we have no might against this great company that cometh against us; neither know we what to do: but our eyes are upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 10:1; Acts 21:5; Deuteronomy 29:10"> And all Judah stood before Jehovah, with their little ones, their wives, and their children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:1; 2 Chronicles 24:20; Acts 10:31"> Then upon Jahaziel the son of Zechariah, the son of Benaiah, the son of Jeiel, the son of Mattaniah, the Levite, of the sons of Asaph, came the Spirit of Jehovah in the midst of the assembly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:6; Exodus 14:13–14"> and he said, Hearken ye, all Judah, and ye inhabitants of Jerusalem, and thou king Jehoshaphat: Thus saith Jehovah unto you, Fear not ye, neither be dismayed by reason of this great multitude; for the battle is not yours, but God’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> To-morrow go ye down against them: behold, they come up by the ascent of Ziz; and ye shall find them at the end of the valley, before the wilderness of Jeruel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:13–14; 2 Chronicles 15:2; Isaiah 41:10"> Ye shall not need to fight in this battle: set yourselves, stand ye still, and see the salvation of Jehovah with you, O Judah and Jerusalem; fear not, nor be dismayed: to-morrow go out against them; for Jehovah is with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; 2 Chronicles 7:3; Genesis 24:26"> And Jehoshaphat bowed his head with his face to the ground; and all Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem fell down before Jehovah, worshipping Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:16–22; 1 Chronicles 16:42; 1 Chronicles 16:5"> And the Levites, of the children of the Kohathites and of the children of the Korahites, stood up to praise Jehovah, the God of Israel, with an exceeding loud voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 8:31; John 14:1; John 11:40"> And they rose early in the morning, and went forth into the wilderness of Tekoa: and as they went forth, Jehoshaphat stood and said, Hear me, O Judah, and ye inhabitants of Jerusalem: believe in Jehovah your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:1; Psalms 106:1; 2 Chronicles 5:13"> And when he had taken counsel with the people, he appointed them that should sing unto Jehovah, and give praise in holy array, as they went out before the army, and say, Give thanks unto Jehovah; for his lovingkindness endureth for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:20; Isaiah 19:2; Ezekiel 38:21"> And when they began to sing and to praise, Jehovah set liers-in-wait against the children of Ammon, Moab, and mount Seir, that were come against Judah; and they were smitten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 1 Samuel 14:20; Ezekiel 35:2–3"> For the children of Ammon and Moab stood up against the inhabitants of mount Seir, utterly to slay and destroy them: and when they had made an end of the inhabitants of Seir, every one helped to destroy another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 37:36; Psalms 110:6; 1 Chronicles 5:22"> And when Judah came to the watch-tower of the wilderness, they looked upon the multitude; and, behold, they were dead bodies fallen to the earth, and there were none that escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:19–20; 2 Kings 7:9–16; Numbers 31:15"> And when Jehoshaphat and his people came to take the spoil of them, they found among them in abundance both riches and dead bodies, and precious jewels, which they stripped off for themselves, more than they could carry away: and they were three days in taking the spoil, it was so much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 17:15; Genesis 32:20; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> And on the fourth day they assembled themselves in the valley of Beracah; for there they blessed Jehovah: therefore the name of that place was called The valley of Beracah unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:43; Hebrews 6:20; Psalms 30:1"> Then they returned, every man of Judah and Jerusalem, and Jehoshaphat in the forefront of them, to go again to Jerusalem with joy; for Jehovah had made them to rejoice over their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 14:2–3; Psalms 57:8; Psalms 92:3"> And they came to Jerusalem with psalteries and harps and trumpets unto the house of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:10; 2 Kings 7:6; Exodus 23:27"> And the fear of God was on all the kingdoms of the countries, when they heard that Jehovah fought against the enemies of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:15; 2 Chronicles 14:6–7; 2 Samuel 7:1"> So the realm of Jehoshaphat was quiet; for his God gave him rest round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 22:41–44"> And Jehoshaphat reigned over Judah: he was thirty and five years old when he began to reign; and he reigned twenty and five years in Jerusalem: and his mother’s name was Azubah the daughter of Shilhi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:2–5; 1 Kings 15:11; 2 Chronicles 14:11–13"> And he walked in the way of Asa his father, and turned not aside from it, doing that which was right in the eyes of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:6; 2 Chronicles 19:3; 2 Chronicles 12:14"> Howbeit the high places were not taken away; neither as yet had the people set their hearts unto the God of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 16:1; 2 Chronicles 19:2; 1 Kings 16:7"> Now the rest of the acts of Jehoshaphat, first and last, behold, they are written in the history of Jehu the son of Hanani, which is inserted in the book of the kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 22:48–49; 2 Kings 1:2–16"> And after this did Jehoshaphat king of Judah join himself with Ahaziah king of Israel; the same did very wickedly:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 10:22; 2 Chronicles 9:21; 1 Kings 9:26"> and he joined himself with him to make ships to go to Tarshish; and they made the ships in Ezion-geber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; 2 Chronicles 19:2; 2 Chronicles 9:21"> Then Eliezer the son of Dodavahu of Mareshah prophesied against Jehoshaphat, saying, Because thou hast joined thyself with Ahaziah, Jehovah hath destroyed thy works. And the ships were broken, so that they were not able to go to Tarshish.</span>
