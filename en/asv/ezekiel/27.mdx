<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word of Jehovah came again unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1; Jeremiah 9:10; Ezekiel 28:12"> And thou, son of man, take up a lamentation over Tyre;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:4; Isaiah 23:11; Isaiah 23:2–3"> and say unto Tyre, O thou that dwellest at the entry of the sea, that art the merchant of the peoples unto many isles, thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Thou, O Tyre, hast said, I am perfect in beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:5"> Thy borders are in the heart of the seas; thy builders have perfected thy beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:9; Isaiah 14:8; Song of Solomon 4:8"> They have made all thy planks of fir-trees from Senir; they have taken a cedar from Lebanon to make a mast for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Isaiah 2:13; Zechariah 11:2"> Of the oaks of Bashan have they made thine oars; they have made thy benches of ivory inlaid in boxwood, from the isles of Kittim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:9; Exodus 25:4; Genesis 10:4"> Of fine linen with broidered work from Egypt was thy sail, that it might be to thee for an ensign; blue and purple from the isles of Elishah was thine awning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 10:18; 1 Kings 9:27; Ezekiel 27:11"> The inhabitants of Sidon and Arvad were thy rowers: thy wise men, O Tyre, were in thee, they were thy pilots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 5:18; Joshua 13:5; Psalms 83:7"> The old men of Gebal and the wise men thereof were in thee thy calkers: all the ships of the sea with their mariners were in thee to deal in thy merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:5; Ezekiel 30:5; Isaiah 66:19"> Persia and Lud and Put were in thine army, thy men of war: they hanged the shield and helmet in thee; they set forth thy comeliness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:3–4"> The men of Arvad with thine army were upon thy walls round about, and valorous men were in thy towers; they hanged their shields upon thy walls round about; they have perfected thy beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Isaiah 23:10; Ezekiel 38:13"> Tarshish was thy merchant by reason of the multitude of all kinds of riches; with silver, iron, tin, and lead, they traded for thy wares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:2; Isaiah 66:19; Ezekiel 39:1"> Javan, Tubal, and Meshech, they were thy traffickers; they traded the persons of men and vessels of brass for thy merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 10:3; Ezekiel 38:6; 1 Chronicles 1:6"> They of the house of Togarmah traded for thy wares with horses and war-horses and mules.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Revelation 18:12; 1 Kings 10:22"> The men of Dedan were thy traffickers; many isles were the mart of thy hand: they brought thee in exchange horns of ivory and ebony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 10:6; Ezekiel 28:13; 2 Samuel 10:6"> Syria was thy merchant by reason of the multitude of thy handiworks: they traded for thy wares with emeralds, purple, and broidered work, and fine linen, and coral, and rubies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 11:33; 1 Kings 5:9; Jeremiah 8:22"> Judah, and the land of Israel, they were thy traffickers: they traded for thy merchandise wheat of Minnith, and pannag, and honey, and oil, and balm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 7:8; Genesis 15:2; Ezekiel 47:16–18"> Damascus was thy merchant for the multitude of thy handiworks, by reason of the multitude of all kinds of riches, with the wine of Helbon, and white wool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 30:23–24; Psalms 45:8; Song of Solomon 4:13–14"> Vedan and Javan traded with yarn for thy wares: bright iron, cassia, and calamus, were among thy merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 25:3; Ezekiel 27:15"> Dedan was thy trafficker in precious cloths for riding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 60:7; Genesis 25:13; 1 Kings 10:15"> Arabia, and all the princes of Kedar, they were the merchants of thy hand; in lambs, and rams, and goats, in these were they thy merchants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Isaiah 60:6; Psalms 72:10"> The traffickers of Sheba and Raamah, they were thy traffickers; they traded for thy wares with the chief of all spices, and with all precious stones, and gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 19:12; Isaiah 37:12; Amos 1:5"> Haran and Canneh and Eden, the traffickers of Sheba, Asshur and Chilmad, were thy traffickers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 2:8"> These were thy traffickers in choice wares, in wrappings of blue and broidered work, and in chests of rich apparel, bound with cords and made of cedar, among thy merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 2:16; Psalms 48:7; Isaiah 23:14"> The ships of Tarshish were thy caravans for thy merchandise: and thou wast replenished, and made very glorious in the heart of the seas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 48:7; Ezekiel 26:19; Acts 27:14"> Thy rowers have brought thee into great waters: the east wind hath broken thee in the heart of the seas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Ezekiel 27:24; Ezekiel 27:12"> Thy riches, and thy wares, thy merchandise, thy mariners, and thy pilots, thy calkers, and the dealers in thy merchandise, and all thy men of war, that are in thee, with all thy company which is in the midst of thee, shall fall into the heart of the seas in the day of thy ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:10; Exodus 15:14; Ezekiel 31:16"> At the sound of the cry of thy pilots the suburbs shall shake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:16; Ezekiel 32:10; Revelation 18:17–24"> And all that handle the oar, the mariners, and all the pilots of the sea, shall come down from their ships; they shall stand upon the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; 2 Samuel 1:2; Lamentations 2:10"> and shall cause their voice to be heard over thee, and shall cry bitterly, and shall cast up dust upon their heads; they shall wallow themselves in the ashes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:18; Isaiah 22:12; Isaiah 15:2"> and they shall make themselves bald for thee, and gird them with sackcloth, and they shall weep for thee in bitterness of soul with bitter mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:17; Revelation 18:18; Ezekiel 27:2"> And in their wailing they shall take up a lamentation for thee, and lament over thee, saying, Who is there like Tyre, like her that is brought to silence in the midst of the sea?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Revelation 18:19; Ezekiel 27:3; Ezekiel 28:16"> When thy wares went forth out of the seas, thou filledst many peoples; thou didst enrich the kings of the earth with the multitude of thy riches and of thy merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:26–27; Zechariah 9:3–4; Ezekiel 26:19–21"> In the time that thou wast broken by the seas in the depths of the waters, thy merchandise and all thy company did fall in the midst of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:10; Isaiah 23:6; Ezekiel 26:15–18"> All the inhabitants of the isles are astonished at thee, and their kings are horribly afraid; they are troubled in their countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:16; Psalms 37:10; Zephaniah 2:15"> The merchants among the peoples hiss at thee; thou art become a terror, and thou shalt nevermore have any being.</span>
