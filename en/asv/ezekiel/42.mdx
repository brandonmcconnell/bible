<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:20; Ezekiel 40:17; Ezekiel 42:13"> Then he brought me forth into the outer court, the way toward the north: and he brought me into the chamber that was over against the separate place, and which was over against the building toward the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:13"> Before the length of a hundred cubits was the north door, and the breadth was fifty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:15–16; Ezekiel 41:10; Song of Solomon 7:5"> Over against the twenty cubits which belonged to the inner court, and over against the pavement which belonged to the outer court, was gallery against gallery in the third story.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:19; Luke 13:24; Matthew 7:14"> And before the chambers was a walk of ten cubits’ breadth inward, a way of one cubit; and their doors were toward the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:7"> Now the upper chambers were shorter; for the galleries took away from these, more than from the lower and the middlemost, in the building.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:6; 1 Kings 6:8"> For they were in three stories, and they had not pillars as the pillars of the courts: therefore the uppermost was straitened more than the lowest and the middlemost from the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:12; Ezekiel 42:10"> And the wall that was without by the side of the chambers, toward the outer court before the chambers, the length thereof was fifty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:13–14"> For the length of the chambers that were in the outer court was fifty cubits: and, lo, before the temple were a hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:19; Ezekiel 44:5"> And from under these chambers was the entry on the east side, as one goeth into them from the outer court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:1; Ezekiel 42:13; Ezekiel 41:12–15"> In the thickness of the wall of the court toward the east, before the separate place, and before the building, there were chambers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:4"> And the way before them was like the appearance of the way of the chambers which were toward the north; according to their length so was their breadth: and all their egresses were both according to their fashions, and according to their doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And according to the doors of the chambers that were toward the south was a door at the head of the way, even the way directly before the wall toward the east, as one entereth into them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 10:17; Leviticus 7:6; Ezekiel 40:46"> Then said he unto me, The north chambers and the south chambers, which are before the separate place, they are the holy chambers, where the priests that are near unto Jehovah shall eat the most holy things: there shall they lay the most holy things, and the meal-offering, and the sin-offering, and the trespass-offering; for the place is holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:19; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 29:4–9"> When the priests enter in, then shall they not go out of the holy place into the outer court, but there they shall lay their garments wherein they minister; for they are holy: and they shall put on other garments, and shall approach to that which pertaineth to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:1; Ezekiel 41:15; Ezekiel 40:6–16"> Now when he had made an end of measuring the inner house, he brought me forth by the way of the gate whose prospect is toward the east, and measured it round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Revelation 11:1–2; Zechariah 2:1"> He measured on the east side with the measuring reed five hundred reeds, with the measuring reed round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He measured on the north side five hundred reeds with the measuring reed round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He measured on the south side five hundred reeds with the measuring reed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> He turned about to the west side, and measured five hundred reeds with the measuring reed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:2; Ezekiel 22:26; Ezekiel 40:5"> He measured it on the four sides: it had a wall round about, the length five hundred, and the breadth five hundred, to make a separation between that which was holy and that which was common.</span>
