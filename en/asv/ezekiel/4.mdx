<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 1:2–9; Amos 3:2; Ezekiel 12:3–16"> Thou also, son of man, take thee a tile, and lay it before thee, and portray upon it a city, even Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:22; Jeremiah 6:6; Luke 19:42–44"> and lay siege against it, and build forts against it, and cast up a mound against it; set camps also against it, and plant battering rams against it round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:6; Isaiah 8:18; Isaiah 20:3"> And take thou unto thee an iron pan, and set it for a wall of iron between thee and the city: and set thy face toward it, and it shall be besieged, and thou shalt lay siege against it. This shall be a sign to the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 16:22; Leviticus 10:17; Numbers 18:1"> Moreover lie thou upon thy left side, and lay the iniquity of the house of Israel upon it; according to the number of the days that thou shalt lie upon it, thou shalt bear their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 14:34; Isaiah 53:6"> For I have appointed the years of their iniquity to be unto thee a number of days, even three hundred and ninety days: so shalt thou bear the iniquity of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:34; Daniel 12:11–12; Daniel 9:24–26"> And again, when thou hast accomplished these, thou shalt lie on thy right side, and shalt bear the iniquity of the house of Judah: forty days, each day for a year, have I appointed it unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 52:10; Ezekiel 21:2; Ezekiel 6:2"> And thou shalt set thy face toward the siege of Jerusalem, with thine arm uncovered; and thou shalt prophesy against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:25"> And, behold, I lay bands upon thee, and thou shalt not turn thee from one side to the other, till thou hast accomplished the days of thy siege.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:13; Isaiah 28:25; Ezekiel 4:16"> Take thou also unto thee wheat, and barley, and beans, and lentils, and millet, and spelt, and put them in one vessel, and make thee bread thereof; according to the number of the days that thou shalt lie upon thy side, even three hundred and ninety days, shalt thou eat thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Ezekiel 45:12; Deuteronomy 28:51–68"> And thy food which thou shalt eat shall be by weight, twenty shekels a day: from time to time shalt thou eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13; John 3:34; Ezekiel 4:16"> And thou shalt drink water by measure, the sixth part of a hin: from time to time shalt thou drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 36:12; Genesis 18:6"> And thou shalt eat it as barley cakes, and thou shalt bake it in their sight with dung that cometh out of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 1:8; Hosea 9:3–4"> And Jehovah said, Even thus shall the children of Israel eat their bread unclean, among the nations whither I will drive them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 10:14; Ezekiel 20:49; Ezekiel 9:8"> Then said I, Ah Lord Jehovah! behold, my soul hath not been polluted; for from my youth up even till now have I not eaten of that which dieth of itself, or is torn of beasts; neither came there abominable flesh into my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then he said unto me, See, I have given thee cow’s dung for man’s dung, and thou shalt prepare thy bread thereon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:16; Isaiah 3:1; Leviticus 26:26"> Moreover he said unto me, Son of man, behold, I will break the staff of bread in Jerusalem: and they shall eat bread by weight, and with fearfulness; and they shall drink water by measure, and in dismay:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:23; Leviticus 26:39; Ezekiel 33:10"> that they may want bread and water, and be dismayed one with another, and pine away in their iniquity.</span>
