<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of Jehovah came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:9; Ezekiel 34:14; Ezekiel 20:46"> Son of man, set thy face toward the mountains of Israel, and prophesy unto them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:30; Ezekiel 36:1–4; Jeremiah 3:23"> and say, Ye mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord Jehovah: Thus saith the Lord Jehovah to the mountains and to the hills, to the watercourses and to the valleys: Behold, I, even I, will bring a sword upon you, and I will destroy your high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:5; Leviticus 26:30; 2 Kings 23:14"> And your altars shall become desolate, and your sun-images shall be broken; and I will cast down your slain men before your idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 23:16; Jeremiah 8:1–2; 2 Kings 23:14"> And I will lay the dead bodies of the children of Israel before their idols; and I will scatter your bones round about your altars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:14; Isaiah 6:11; Zechariah 13:2"> In all your dwelling-places the cities shall be laid waste, and the high places shall be desolate; that your altars may be laid waste and made desolate, and your idols may be broken and cease, and your sun-images may be hewn down, and your works may be abolished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:33; Ezekiel 13:23; Ezekiel 35:15"> And the slain shall fall in the midst of you, and ye shall know that I am Jehovah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:22; Jeremiah 44:28; Ezekiel 12:16"> Yet will I leave a remnant, in that ye shall have some that escape the sword among the nations, when ye shall be scattered through the countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:43; Isaiah 7:13; Job 42:6"> And those of you that escape shall remember me among the nations whither they shall be carried captive, how that I have been broken with their lewd heart, which hath departed from me, and with their eyes, which play the harlot after their idols: and they shall loathe themselves in their own sight for the evils which they have committed in all their abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12–14; Daniel 9:12; Jeremiah 44:28"> And they shall know that I am Jehovah: I have not said in vain that I would do this evil unto them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:12; Ezekiel 9:4; Ezekiel 21:14–17"> Thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Smite with thy hand, and stamp with thy foot, and say, Alas! because of all the evil abominations of the house of Israel; for they shall fall by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Daniel 9:7; Lamentations 4:22"> He that is far off shall die of the pestilence; and he that is near shall fall by the sword; and he that remaineth and is besieged shall die by the famine: thus will I accomplish my wrath upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:28; Hosea 4:13; 1 Kings 14:23"> And ye shall know that I am Jehovah, when their slain men shall be among their idols round about their altars, upon every high hill, on all the tops of the mountains, and under every green tree, and under every thick oak, the places where they offered sweet savor to all their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25; Ezekiel 20:33–34; Isaiah 9:12"> And I will stretch out my hand upon them, and make the land desolate and waste, from the wilderness toward Diblah, throughout all their habitations: and they shall know that I am Jehovah.</span>
</div>
