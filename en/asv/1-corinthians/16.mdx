<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 12:13; 2 Corinthians 8:1–9; 2 Corinthians 9:12–15"> Now concerning the collection for the saints, as I gave order to the churches of Galatia, so also do ye.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; 1 Corinthians 11:34; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21"> And when I arrive, whomsoever ye shall approve, them will I send with letters to carry your bounty unto Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:4; 2 Corinthians 8:19; Romans 15:25"> and if it be meet for me to go also, they shall go with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 19:21; 1 Corinthians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 1:15–17"> But I will come unto you, when I shall have passed through Macedonia; for I pass through Macedonia;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 15:3; Romans 15:24; Acts 20:38"> but with you it may be that I shall abide, or even winter, that ye may set me forward on my journey whithersoever I go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 18:21; 1 Corinthians 4:19; James 4:15"> For I do not wish to see you now by the way; for I hope to tarry a while with you, if the Lord permit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:1; Exodus 23:16; Acts 18:19"> But I will tarry at Ephesus until Pentecost;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12; Acts 14:27; Colossians 4:3"> for a great door and effectual is opened unto me, and there are many adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; Romans 16:21"> Now if Timothy come, see that he be with you without fear; for he worketh the work of the Lord, as I also do:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:12; Acts 15:33; Titus 2:15"> let no man therefore despise him. But set him forward on his journey in peace, that he may come unto me: for I expect him with the brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:5; Titus 3:4"> But as touching Apollos the brother, I besought him much to come unto you with the brethren: and it was not at all his will to come now; but he will come when he shall have opportunity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:13–18; Ephesians 6:10"> Watch ye, stand fast in the faith, quit you like men, be strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 13:34–35; 1 Peter 4:8; 1 Timothy 1:5"> Let all that ye do be done in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:16; Romans 16:5; Romans 16:2"> Now I beseech you, brethren (ye know the house of Stephanas, that it is the firstfruits of Achaia, and that they have set themselves to minister unto the saints),</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:12; 3 John 1:8"> that ye also be in subjection unto such, and to every one that helpeth in the work and laboreth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; Philippians 2:30; Philemon 1:13"> And I rejoice at the coming of Stephanas and Fortunatus and Achaicus: for that which was lacking on your part they supplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philemon 1:7; 2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:12"> For they refreshed my spirit and yours: acknowledge ye therefore them that are such.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 18:2; Philemon 1:2; Romans 16:3–5"> The churches of Asia salute you. Aquila and Prisca salute you much in the Lord, with the church that is in their house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 16:16; 1 Thessalonians 5:26; 1 Peter 5:14"> All the brethren salute you. Salute one another with a holy kiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Colossians 4:18; Galatians 6:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:17"> The salutation of me Paul with mine own hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 6:24; John 14:21; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> If any man loveth not the Lord, let him be anathema. Maranatha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Romans 16:23"> The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:16; Revelation 3:19; 2 Corinthians 12:15"> My love be with you all in Christ Jesus. Amen.</span>
