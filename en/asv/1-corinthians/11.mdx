<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Philippians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 1:6; 1 Corinthians 4:16"> Be ye imitators of me, even as I also am of Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:15; 1 Corinthians 4:17; Proverbs 31:28–31"> Now I praise you that ye remember me in all things, and hold fast the traditions, even as I delivered them to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:16; Colossians 1:18; Colossians 3:18"> But I would have you know, that the head of every man is Christ; and the head of the woman is the man; and the head of Christ is God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:30; 1 Corinthians 12:28; 1 Corinthians 12:10"> Every man praying or prophesying, having his head covered, dishonoreth his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:12; Luke 2:36; Acts 2:17"> But every woman praying or prophesying with her head unveiled dishonoreth her head; for it is one and the same thing as if she were shaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:5; Numbers 5:18"> For if a woman is not veiled, let her also be shorn: but if it is a shame to a woman to be shorn or shaven, let her be veiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3; Genesis 5:1; Psalms 8:6"> For a man indeed ought not to have his head veiled, forasmuch as he is the image and glory of God: but the woman is the glory of the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:13; Genesis 2:21–23"> For the man is not of the woman; but the woman of the man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 2:18; Genesis 2:23–24; Genesis 2:20"> for neither was the man created for the woman; but the woman for the man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 24:64–65; Hebrews 1:14; Matthew 18:10"> for this cause ought the woman to have a sign of authority on her head, because of the angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; 1 Corinthians 7:10–14; 1 Corinthians 12:12–22"> Nevertheless, neither is the woman without the man, nor the man without the woman, in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:36; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 1:2–3"> For as the woman is of the man, so is the man also by the woman; but all things are of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:57; John 7:24; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> Judge ye in yourselves: is it seemly that a woman pray unto God unveiled?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:26; 1 Corinthians 14:35"> Doth not even nature itself teach you, that, if a man have long hair, it is a dishonor to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> But if a woman have long hair, it is a glory to her: for her hair is given her for a covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Timothy 6:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 2:14"> But if any man seemeth to be contentious, we have no such custom, neither the churches of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; 1 Corinthians 14:23; 1 Corinthians 11:22"> But in giving you this charge, I praise you not, that ye come together not for the better but for the worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 1:10–12; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> For first of all, when ye come together in the church, I hear that divisions exist among you; and I partly believe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 2:19; Titus 3:10; Acts 20:30"> For there must be also factions among you, that they that are approved may be made manifest among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> When therefore ye assemble yourselves together, it is not possible to eat the Lord’s supper:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:13; Jude 1:12; 1 Corinthians 11:23–25"> for in your eating each one taketh before other his own supper; and one is hungry, and another is drunken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 20:28; Proverbs 17:5; James 2:5–6"> What, have ye not houses to eat and to drink in? or despise ye the church of God, and put them to shame that have not? What shall I say to you? shall I praise you? In this I praise you not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:19–20; Matthew 26:26–28; 1 Corinthians 11:23–25"> For I received of the Lord that which also I delivered unto you, that the Lord Jesus in the night in which he was betrayed took bread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:53–58; 1 Corinthians 11:27–28; 1 Corinthians 10:3–4"> and when he had given thanks, he brake it, and said, This is my body, which is for you: this do in remembrance of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 22:20; Hebrews 9:15–20; Hebrews 13:20"> In like manner also the cup, after supper, saying, This cup is the new covenant in my blood: this do, as often as ye drink it, in remembrance of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Revelation 1:7; John 14:3"> For as often as ye eat this bread, and drink the cup, ye proclaim the Lord’s death till he come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 6:51; 1 Corinthians 10:21; Hebrews 10:29"> Wherefore whosoever shall eat the bread or drink the cup of the Lord in an unworthy manner, shall be guilty of the body and the blood of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 6:4; 2 Corinthians 13:5; Lamentations 3:40"> But let a man prove himself, and so let him eat of the bread, and drink of the cup.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:27; 1 Corinthians 11:30; 1 Corinthians 11:32–34"> For he that eateth and drinketh, eateth and drinketh judgment unto himself, if he discern not the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Numbers 20:12; Amos 3:2"> For this cause many among you are weak and sickly, and not a few sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 1:9; Psalms 32:3–5; Revelation 2:5"> But if we discerned ourselves, we should not be judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5–11; Psalms 118:18; Proverbs 3:11–12"> But when we are judged, we are chastened of the Lord, that we may not be condemned with the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Wherefore, my brethren, when ye come together to eat, wait one for another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:21–22; 1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Corinthians 4:19"> If any man is hungry, let him eat at home; that your coming together be not unto judgment. And the rest will I set in order whensoever I come.</span>
