<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 30:21; Jeremiah 2:36; Genesis 30:13"> And Dinah the daughter of Leah, whom she bare unto Jacob, went out to see the daughters of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 5:28; Job 31:1; Job 31:9"> And Shechem the son of Hamor the Hivite, the prince of the land, saw her; and he took her, and lay with her, and humbled her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 2:14; Isaiah 40:2; 2 Chronicles 30:22"> And his soul clave unto Dinah the daughter of Jacob, and he loved the damsel, and spake kindly unto the damsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 14:2; 2 Samuel 13:13; Genesis 21:21"> And Shechem spake unto his father Hamor, saying, Get me this damsel to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:22; Leviticus 10:3; 1 Samuel 10:27"> Now Jacob heard that he had defiled Dinah his daughter; and his sons were with his cattle in the field: and Jacob held his peace until they came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Hamor the father of Shechem went out unto Jacob to commune with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 7:15; Judges 20:6; Deuteronomy 22:21"> And the sons of Jacob came in from the field when they heard it: and the men were grieved, and they were very wroth, because he had wrought folly in Israel in lying with Jacob’s daughter; which thing ought not to be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 11:2; Psalms 119:20; Psalms 84:2"> And Hamor communed with them, saying, The soul of my son Shechem longeth for your daughter: I pray you, give her unto him to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 19:14"> And make ye marriages with us; give your daughters unto us, and take our daughters unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 47:27; Genesis 42:34"> And ye shall dwell with us: and the land shall be before you; dwell and trade ye therein, and get you possessions therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 33:15; Genesis 18:3"> And Shechem said unto her father and unto her brethren, Let me find favor in your eyes, and what ye shall say unto me I will give.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 22:16–17; Deuteronomy 22:28–29; 1 Samuel 18:25–27"> Ask me never so much dowry and gift, and I will give according as ye shall say unto me: but give me the damsel to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 25:27–34; Isaiah 59:13; Job 13:4"> And the sons of Jacob answered Shechem and Hamor his father with guile, and spake, because he had defiled Dinah their sister,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–39; 2 Samuel 15:7; Genesis 17:11"> and said unto them, We cannot do this thing, to give our sister to one that is uncircumcised; for that were a reproach unto us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:12"> Only on this condition will we consent unto you: if ye will be as we are, that every male of you be circumcised;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> then will we give our daughters unto you, and we will take your daughters to us, and we will dwell with you, and we will become one people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But if ye will not hearken unto us, to be circumcised; then will we take our daughter, and we will be gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And their words pleased Hamor, and Shechem Hamor’s son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:12; Acts 13:50; 1 Samuel 22:14"> And the young man deferred not to do the thing, because he had delight in Jacob’s daughter: and he was honored above all the house of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; Proverbs 31:23; Amos 5:12"> And Hamor and Shechem his son came unto the gate of their city, and communed with the men of their city, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> These men are peaceable with us; therefore let them dwell in the land, and trade therein; for, behold, the land is large enough for them; let us take their daughters to us for wives, and let us give them our daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Only on this condition will the men consent unto us to dwell with us, to become one people, if every male among us be circumcised, as they are circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 1:12–13; John 6:26–27; John 2:16"> Shall not their cattle and their substance and all their beasts be ours? only let us consent unto them, and they will dwell with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 23:10; 1 Corinthians 7:19; Isaiah 1:10–16"> And unto Hamor and unto Shechem his son hearkened all that went out of the gate of his city; and every male was circumcised, all that went out of the gate of his city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 49:5–7; Genesis 29:33–34; Numbers 31:7"> And it came to pass on the third day, when they were sore, that two of the sons of Jacob, Simeon and Levi, Dinah’s brethren, took each man his sword, and came upon the city unawares, and slew all the males.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:26; Deuteronomy 32:42; Isaiah 31:8"> And they slew Hamor and Shechem his son with the edge of the sword, and took Dinah out of Shechem’s house, and went forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 7:13; Joshua 7:21; Exodus 2:14"> The sons of Jacob came upon the slain, and plundered the city, because they had defiled their sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 1:15–16; Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Job 20:5"> They took their flocks and their herds and their asses, and that which was in the city, and that which was in the field;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> and all their wealth, and all their little ones and their wives, took they captive and made a prey, even all that was in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 105:12; Joshua 7:25; Exodus 5:21"> And Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, Ye have troubled me, to make me odious to the inhabitants of the land, among the Canaanites and the Perizzites: and, I being few in number, they will gather themselves together against me and smite me; and I shall be destroyed, I and my house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 6:34; Genesis 34:13; Genesis 49:7"> And they said, Should he deal with our sister as with a harlot?</span>
