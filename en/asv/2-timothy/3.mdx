<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 3:3; 2 Timothy 4:3; 1 Timothy 4:1–3"> But know this, that in the last days grievous times shall come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:21; Romans 1:29–31; Jude 1:16"> For men shall be lovers of self, lovers of money, boastful, haughty, railers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jude 1:18; Romans 1:31; Matthew 10:21"> without natural affection, implacable, slanderers, without self-control, fierce, no lovers of good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 3:18–19; 1 Timothy 3:6; Jude 1:4"> traitors, headstrong, puffed up, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 23:27–28"> holding a form of godliness, but having denied the power thereof: from these also turn away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:4; 2 Peter 3:17; Titus 3:3"> For of these are they that creep into houses, and take captive silly women laden with sins, led away by divers lusts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Ephesians 4:14; 1 Timothy 2:4"> ever learning, and never able to come to the knowledge of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:8–11; Revelation 2:20; 1 Timothy 6:5"> And even as Jannes and Jambres withstood Moses, so do these also withstand the truth; men corrupted in mind, reprobate concerning the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:8; Exodus 7:12; Jeremiah 28:15–17"> But they shall proceed no further: for their folly shall be evident unto all men, as theirs also came to be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:11; Titus 2:7; 2 Peter 1:5–7"> But thou didst follow my teaching, conduct, purpose, faith, longsuffering, love, patience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Acts 14:5–6; 2 Corinthians 11:23–28"> persecutions, sufferings; what things befell me at Antioch, at Iconium, at Lystra; what persecutions I endured: and out of them all the Lord delivered me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 4:12–16; 1 Peter 2:20–21"> Yea, and all that would live godly in Christ Jesus shall suffer persecution.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:1; 2 Peter 2:20; Titus 3:3"> But evil men and impostors shall wax worse and worse, deceiving and being deceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; 2 Timothy 2:2; 2 Timothy 1:13"> But abide thou in the things which thou hast learned and hast been assured of, knowing of whom thou hast learned them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:98–99; Luke 24:45; Proverbs 22:6"> and that from a babe thou hast known the sacred writings which are able to make thee wise unto salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 15:4; Hebrews 4:12; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> Every scripture inspired of God is also profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, for instruction which is in righteousness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:21; Ephesians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> that the man of God may be complete, furnished completely unto every good work.</span>
