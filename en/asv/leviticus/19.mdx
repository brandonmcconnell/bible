<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44–45; Leviticus 20:7; 1 Peter 1:15–16"> Speak unto all the congregation of the children of Israel, and say unto them, Ye shall be holy; for I Jehovah your God am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Exodus 20:8; Hebrews 12:9"> Ye shall fear every man his mother, and his father; and ye shall keep my sabbaths: I am Jehovah your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:1; Exodus 20:23; Psalms 115:4–7"> Turn ye not unto idols, nor make to yourselves molten gods: I am Jehovah your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 22:19; Leviticus 22:29; Leviticus 7:16"> And when ye offer a sacrifice of peace-offerings unto Jehovah, ye shall offer it that ye may be accepted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 7:11–17"> It shall be eaten the same day ye offer it, and on the morrow: and if aught remain until the third day, it shall be burnt with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 7:18–21; Leviticus 22:25; Jeremiah 16:18"> And if it be eaten at all on the third day, it is an abomination; it shall not be accepted:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 22:15; Leviticus 5:1"> but every one that eateth it shall bear his iniquity, because he hath profaned the holy thing of Jehovah: and that soul shall be cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:22; Deuteronomy 24:19–22; Ruth 2:2"> And when ye reap the harvest of your land, thou shalt not wholly reap the corners of thy field, neither shalt thou gather the gleaning of thy harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:6; Leviticus 25:6; Isaiah 24:13"> And thou shalt not glean thy vineyard, neither shalt thou gather the fallen fruit of thy vineyard; thou shalt leave them for the poor and for the sojourner: I am Jehovah your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 101:7; Ephesians 4:25; Colossians 3:9"> Ye shall not steal; neither shall ye deal falsely, nor lie one to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Deuteronomy 5:11; Leviticus 18:21"> And ye shall not swear by my name falsely, and profane the name of thy God: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 5:4; Malachi 3:5; Deuteronomy 24:14–15"> Thou shalt not oppress thy neighbor, nor rob him: the wages of a hired servant shall not abide with thee all night until the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:18; Leviticus 19:32; Leviticus 25:17"> Thou shalt not curse the deaf, nor put a stumblingblock before the blind; but thou shalt fear thy God: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:17; Proverbs 24:23; Deuteronomy 27:19"> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment: thou shalt not respect the person of the poor, nor honor the person of the mighty; but in righteousness shalt thou judge thy neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 23:1; Exodus 23:7; Proverbs 20:19"> Thou shalt not go up and down as a talebearer among thy people: neither shalt thou stand against the blood of thy neighbor: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 2:9; 1 John 2:11; Luke 17:3"> Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thy heart: thou shalt surely rebuke thy neighbor, and not bear sin because of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 22:39–40; Mark 12:31–34"> Thou shalt not take vengeance, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people; but thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Deuteronomy 22:9–11; Matthew 9:16–17"> Ye shall keep my statutes. Thou shalt not let thy cattle gender with a diverse kind: thou shalt not sow thy field with two kinds of seed: neither shall there come upon thee a garment of two kinds of stuff mingled together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:23–24; Exodus 21:20–21"> And whosoever lieth carnally with a woman, that is a bondmaid, betrothed to a husband, and not at all redeemed, nor freedom given her; they shall be punished; they shall not be put to death, because she was not free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 6:6–7; Leviticus 5:1–6; Leviticus 5:15"> And he shall bring his trespass-offering unto Jehovah, unto the door of the tent of meeting, even a ram for a trespass-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:20"> And the priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of the trespass-offering before Jehovah for his sin which he hath sinned: and the sin which he hath sinned shall be forgiven him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 22:27; Acts 7:51; Jeremiah 9:25–26"> And when ye shall come into the land, and shall have planted all manner of trees for food, then ye shall count the fruit thereof as their uncircumcision: three years shall they be as uncircumcised unto you; it shall not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9; Deuteronomy 18:4; Numbers 18:12–13"> But in the fourth year all the fruit thereof shall be holy, for giving praise unto Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Haggai 1:9–11; Malachi 3:8–10; Leviticus 26:3–4"> And in the fifth year shall ye eat of the fruit thereof, that it may yield unto you the increase thereof: I am Jehovah your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:10–14; 2 Kings 17:17; 2 Chronicles 33:6"> Ye shall not eat anything with the blood: neither shall ye use enchantments, nor practise augury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Deuteronomy 14:1; Ezekiel 44:20"> Ye shall not round the corners of your heads, neither shalt thou mar the corners of thy beard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Leviticus 21:5; 1 Kings 18:28"> Ye shall not make any cuttings in your flesh for the dead, nor print any marks upon you: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:17–18; Leviticus 21:7; Hosea 4:12–14"> Profane not thy daughter, to make her a harlot; lest the land fall to whoredom, and the land become full of wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 26:2; Leviticus 19:3; Ecclesiastes 5:1"> Ye shall keep my sabbaths, and reverence my sanctuary: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; Leviticus 20:27; Leviticus 20:6–7"> Turn ye not unto them that have familiar spirits, nor unto the wizards; seek them not out, to be defiled by them: I am Jehovah your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:1; Lamentations 5:12; Proverbs 16:31"> Thou shalt rise up before the hoary head, and honor the face of the old man, and thou shalt fear thy God: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 23:9; Exodus 22:21; Malachi 3:5"> And if a stranger sojourn with thee in your land, ye shall not do him wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:19; Leviticus 19:18; Exodus 12:48–49"> The stranger that sojourneth with you shall be unto you as the home-born among you, and thou shalt love him as thyself; for ye were sojourners in the land of Egypt: I am Jehovah your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:15; Deuteronomy 25:13; Proverbs 16:11"> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment, in measures of length, of weight, or of quantity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Exodus 20:2; Deuteronomy 25:13–15"> Just balances, just weights, a just ephah, and a just hin, shall ye have: I am Jehovah your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:1; Leviticus 18:4–5; Deuteronomy 5:1"> And ye shall observe all my statutes, and all mine ordinances, and do them: I am Jehovah.</span>
