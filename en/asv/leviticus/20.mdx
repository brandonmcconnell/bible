<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:21; Leviticus 18:21; Leviticus 20:27"> Moreover, thou shalt say to the children of Israel, Whosoever he be of the children of Israel, or of the strangers that sojourn in Israel, that giveth of his seed unto Molech; he shall surely be put to death: the people of the land shall stone him with stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 23:38–39"> I also will set my face against that man, and will cut him off from among his people; because he hath given of his seed unto Molech, to defile my sanctuary, and to profane my holy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–5; Acts 17:30; 1 Kings 20:42"> And if the people of the land do at all hide their eyes from that man, when he giveth of his seed unto Molech, and put him not to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 106:39; Hosea 2:13; Hosea 2:5"> then I will set my face against that man, and against his family, and will cut him off, and all that play the harlot after him, to play the harlot with Molech, from among their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Isaiah 8:19; Leviticus 20:27"> And the soul that turneth unto them that have familiar spirits, and unto the wizards, to play the harlot after them, I will even set my face against that soul, and will cut him off from among his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Leviticus 19:2; 1 Peter 1:15–16"> Sanctify yourselves therefore, and be ye holy; for I am Jehovah your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 31:13; James 1:22; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> And ye shall keep my statutes, and do them: I am Jehovah who sanctifieth you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 21:17; Mark 7:10; Deuteronomy 27:16"> For every one that curseth his father or his mother shall surely be put to death: he hath cursed his father or his mother; his blood shall be upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:4–5; Deuteronomy 22:22–24; Deuteronomy 5:18"> And the man that committeth adultery with another man’s wife, even he that committeth adultery with his neighbor’s wife, the adulterer and the adulteress shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:20; Deuteronomy 27:23; Amos 2:7"> And the man that lieth with his father’s wife hath uncovered his father’s nakedness: both of them shall surely be put to death; their blood shall be upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:15; Leviticus 18:23; Genesis 38:18"> And if a man lie with his daughter-in-law, both of them shall surely be put to death: they have wrought confusion; their blood shall be upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9; Leviticus 18:22; Jude 1:7"> And if a man lie with mankind, as with womankind, both of them have committed abomination: they shall surely be put to death; their blood shall be upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 18:17; Deuteronomy 27:23; Joshua 7:15"> And if a man take a wife and her mother, it is wickedness: they shall be burnt with fire, both he and they; that there be no wickedness among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Deuteronomy 27:21; Exodus 22:19"> And if a man lie with a beast, he shall surely be put to death: and ye shall slay the beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 21:32; Exodus 19:13; Hebrews 12:20"> And if a woman approach unto any beast, and lie down thereto, thou shalt kill the woman, and the beast: they shall surely be put to death; their blood shall be upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 18:9; Deuteronomy 27:22; Ezekiel 22:11"> And if a man shall take his sister, his father’s daughter, or his mother’s daughter, and see her nakedness, and she see his nakedness; it is a shameful thing; and they shall be cut off in the sight of the children of their people: he hath uncovered his sister’s nakedness; he shall bear his iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 15:24; Leviticus 18:19; Ezekiel 18:6"> And if a man shall lie with a woman having her sickness, and shall uncover her nakedness; he hath made naked her fountain, and she hath uncovered the fountain of her blood: and both of them shall be cut off from among their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 18:6; Leviticus 18:12–30; Exodus 6:20"> And thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy mother’s sister, nor of thy father’s sister; for he hath made naked his near kin: they shall bear their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 18:14; Job 18:19; Luke 23:29"> And if a man shall lie with his uncle’s wife, he hath uncovered his uncle’s nakedness: they shall bear their sin; they shall die childless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 18:16; Matthew 14:3–4"> And if a man shall take his brother’s wife, it is impurity: he hath uncovered his brother’s nakedness; they shall be childless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 18:25–28; Ezekiel 36:27; Leviticus 18:4–5"> Ye shall therefore keep all my statutes, and all mine ordinances, and do them; that the land, whither I bring you to dwell therein, vomit you not out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:5; Leviticus 18:24; Leviticus 18:30"> And ye shall not walk in the customs of the nation, which I cast out before you: for they did all these things, and therefore I abhorred them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:2; Exodus 6:8; Deuteronomy 7:6"> But I have said unto you, Ye shall inherit their land, and I will give it unto you to possess it, a land flowing with milk and honey: I am Jehovah your God, who hath separated you from the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:3–21; Leviticus 11:1–47; Acts 10:11–15"> Ye shall therefore make a distinction between the clean beast and the unclean, and between the unclean fowl and the clean: and ye shall not make your souls abominable by beast, or by bird, or by anything wherewith the ground teemeth, which I have separated from you as unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:15–16; Leviticus 20:7; Leviticus 20:24"> And ye shall be holy unto me: for I, Jehovah, am holy, and have set you apart from the peoples, that ye should be mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Leviticus 20:6; 1 Samuel 28:7–9"> A man also or a woman that hath a familiar spirit, or that is a wizard, shall surely be put to death: they shall stone them with stones; their blood shall be upon them.</span>
