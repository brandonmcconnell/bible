<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 2:22; Leviticus 15:19; Job 25:4"> Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, If a woman conceive seed, and bear a man-child, then she shall be unclean seven days; as in the days of the impurity of her sickness shall she be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 2:21; Luke 1:59; John 7:22–23"> And in the eighth day the flesh of his foreskin shall be circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 2:22–23; Leviticus 15:25–28; Haggai 2:13"> And she shall continue in the blood of her purifying three and thirty days; she shall touch no hallowed thing, nor come into the sanctuary, until the days of her purifying be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:14–15; Genesis 3:13; Leviticus 12:2"> But if she bear a maid-child, then she shall be unclean two weeks, as in her impurity; and she shall continue in the blood of her purifying threescore and six days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 2:22; 2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> And when the days of her purifying are fulfilled, for a son, or for a daughter, she shall bring a lamb a year old for a burnt-offering, and a young pigeon, or a turtle-dove, for a sin-offering, unto the door of the tent of meeting, unto the priest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 15:28–30; Hebrews 9:12–14; Leviticus 4:35"> and he shall offer it before Jehovah, and make atonement for her; and she shall be cleansed from the fountain of her blood. This is the law for her that beareth, whether a male or a female.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 5:7; Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 1:14"> And if her means suffice not for a lamb, then she shall take two turtle-doves, or two young pigeons; the one for a burnt-offering, and the other for a sin-offering: and the priest shall make atonement for her, and she shall be clean.</span>
