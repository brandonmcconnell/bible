<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 1:1; Galatians 4:24–25; Exodus 19:1"> And Jehovah spake unto Moses in mount Sinai, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:21; Leviticus 26:34–35; Exodus 23:10"> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye come into the land which I give you, then shall the land keep a sabbath unto Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 23:10"> Six years thou shalt sow thy field, and six years thou shalt prune thy vineyard, and gather in the fruits thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:21; Leviticus 25:20–23; Exodus 23:10–11"> but in the seventh year shall be a sabbath of solemn rest for the land, a sabbath unto Jehovah: thou shalt neither sow thy field, nor prune thy vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 19:29; Isaiah 37:30"> That which groweth of itself of thy harvest thou shalt not reap, and the grapes of thy undressed vine thou shalt not gather: it shall be a year of solemn rest for the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 2:44; Exodus 23:11; Acts 4:34–35"> And the sabbath of the land shall be for food for you; for thee, and for thy servant and for thy maid, and for thy hired servant and for thy stranger, who sojourn with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And for thy cattle, and for the beasts that are in thy land, shall all the increase thereof be for food.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 23:15; Genesis 2:2"> And thou shalt number seven sabbaths of years unto thee, seven times seven years; and there shall be unto thee the days of seven sabbaths of years, even forty and nine years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:24; Leviticus 23:27; 1 Thessalonians 1:8"> Then shalt thou send abroad the loud trumpet on the tenth day of the seventh month; in the day of atonement shall ye send abroad the trumpet throughout all your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:8; Isaiah 63:4; 1 Peter 2:16"> And ye shall hallow the fiftieth year, and proclaim liberty throughout the land unto all the inhabitants thereof: it shall be a jubilee unto you; and ye shall return every man unto his possession, and ye shall return every man unto his family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 27:17; Leviticus 25:4–5"> A jubilee shall that fiftieth year be unto you: ye shall not sow, neither reap that which groweth of itself in it, nor gather the grapes in it of the undressed vines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 25:6–7"> For it is a jubilee; it shall be holy unto you: ye shall eat the increase thereof out of the field.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Leviticus 27:17–24; Numbers 36:4"> In this year of jubilee ye shall return every man unto his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Leviticus 25:17; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> And if thou sell aught unto thy neighbor, or buy of thy neighbor’s hand, ye shall not wrong one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 27:18–23; Philippians 4:5"> According to the number of years after the jubilee thou shalt buy of thy neighbor, and according unto the number of years of the crops he shall sell unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 25:27; Leviticus 25:51"> According to the multitude of the years thou shalt increase the price thereof, and according to the fewness of the years thou shalt diminish the price of it; for the number of the crops doth he sell unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 19:14; Leviticus 19:32; Leviticus 25:43"> And ye shall not wrong one another; but thou shalt fear thy God: for I am Jehovah your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:6; Deuteronomy 12:10; Proverbs 1:33"> Wherefore ye shall do my statutes, and keep mine ordinances and do them; and ye shall dwell in the land in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:26; Psalms 85:12; Leviticus 26:5"> And the land shall yield its fruit, and ye shall eat your fill, and dwell therein in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 25:4; Luke 12:29; Numbers 11:4"> And if ye shall say, What shall we eat the seventh year? behold, we shall not sow, nor gather in our increase;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:8; Proverbs 10:22; 2 Corinthians 9:10"> then I will command my blessing upon you in the sixth year, and it shall bring forth fruit for the three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 19:29; Leviticus 26:10; Isaiah 37:30"> And ye shall sow the eighth year, and eat of the fruits, the old store; until the ninth year, until its fruits come in, ye shall eat the old store.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Psalms 39:12; Ezekiel 48:14"> And the land shall not be sold in perpetuity; for the land is mine: for ye are strangers and sojourners with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Leviticus 25:51–53; Leviticus 25:27"> And in all the land of your possession ye shall grant a redemption for the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ruth 2:20; Jeremiah 32:7–8; Ruth 3:12"> If thy brother be waxed poor, and sell some of his possession, then shall his kinsman that is next unto him come, and shall redeem that which his brother hath sold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 5:7"> And if a man have no one to redeem it, and he be waxed rich and find sufficient to redeem it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> then let him reckon the years of the sale thereof, and restore the overplus unto the man to whom he sold it; and he shall return unto his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 35:9–10; Leviticus 25:10; 1 Peter 1:4–5"> But if he be not able to get it back for himself, then that which he hath sold shall remain in the hand of him that hath bought it until the year of jubilee: and in the jubilee it shall go out, and he shall return unto his possession.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And if a man sell a dwelling-house in a walled city, then he may redeem it within a whole year after it is sold; for a full year shall he have the right of redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And if it be not redeemed within the space of a full year, then the house that is in the walled city shall be made sure in perpetuity to him that bought it, throughout his generations: it shall not go out in the jubilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–8"> But the houses of the villages which have no wall round about them shall be reckoned with the fields of the country: they may be redeemed, and they shall go out in the jubilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 21:1–45; Numbers 35:1–8"> Nevertheless the cities of the Levites, the houses of the cities of their possession, may the Levites redeem at any time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 18:20–24; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> And if one of the Levites redeem, then the house that was sold, and the city of his possession, shall go out in the jubilee; for the houses of the cities of the Levites are their possession among the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 25:23; Acts 4:36–37; Numbers 35:2–5"> But the field of the suburbs of their cities may not be sold; for it is their perpetual possession.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:7–8; Acts 11:29; Leviticus 25:25"> And if thy brother be waxed poor, and his hand fail with thee; then thou shalt uphold him: as a stranger and a sojourner shall he live with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Ezekiel 18:8"> Take thou no interest of him or increase, but fear thy God; that thy brother may live with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Thou shalt not give him thy money upon interest, nor give him thy victuals for increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 11:45; Numbers 15:41; Exodus 20:2"> I am Jehovah your God, who brought you forth out of the land of Egypt, to give you the land of Canaan, and to be your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 21:2; 1 Kings 9:22; 2 Kings 4:1"> And if thy brother be waxed poor with thee, and sell himself unto thee; thou shalt not make him to serve as a bondservant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 21:2–3"> As a hired servant, and as a sojourner, he shall be with thee; he shall serve with thee unto the year of jubilee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 25:28; Exodus 21:3; John 8:32"> then shall he go out from thee, he and his children with him, and shall return unto his own family, and unto the possession of his fathers shall he return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Leviticus 25:55; Romans 6:22; 1 Corinthians 7:21–23"> For they are my servants, whom I brought forth out of the land of Egypt: they shall not be sold as bondmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Colossians 4:1; Leviticus 25:46; Exodus 1:13–14"> Thou shalt not rule over him with rigor, but shalt fear thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 12:44; Psalms 2:8–9; Isaiah 14:1–2"> And as for thy bondmen, and thy bondmaids, whom thou shalt have; of the nations that are round about you, of them shall ye buy bondmen and bondmaids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Isaiah 56:3–6"> Moreover of the children of the strangers that sojourn among you, of them shall ye buy, and of their families that are with you, which they have begotten in your land: and they shall be your possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 14:2; Leviticus 25:43"> And ye shall make them an inheritance for your children after you, to hold for a possession; of them shall ye take your bondmen for ever: but over your brethren the children of Israel ye shall not rule, one over another, with rigor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="James 2:5; Leviticus 25:26; 1 Samuel 2:7–8"> And if a stranger or sojourner with thee be waxed rich, and thy brother be waxed poor beside him, and sell himself unto the stranger or sojourner with thee, or to the stock of the stranger’s family;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; Hebrews 2:11–13; Leviticus 25:35"> after that he is sold he may be redeemed: one of his brethren may redeem him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Leviticus 25:26"> or his uncle, or his uncle’s son, may redeem him, or any that is nigh of kin unto him of his family may redeem him; or if he be waxed rich, he may redeem himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Isaiah 16:14; Isaiah 21:16; Leviticus 25:53"> And he shall reckon with him that bought him from the year that he sold himself to him unto the year of jubilee: and the price of his sale shall be according unto the number of years; according to the time of a hired servant shall he be with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> If there be yet many years, according unto them he shall give back the price of his redemption out of the money that he was bought for.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And if there remain but few years unto the year of jubilee, then he shall reckon with him; according unto his years shall he give back the price of his redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Leviticus 25:46; Leviticus 25:43"> As a servant hired year by year shall he be with him: he shall not rule with rigor over him in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 21:2–3; Leviticus 25:40–41; Isaiah 49:9"> And if he be not redeemed by these means, then he shall go out in the year of jubilee, he, and his children with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Leviticus 25:42; Isaiah 43:3; Exodus 13:3"> For unto me the children of Israel are servants; they are my servants whom I brought forth out of the land of Egypt: I am Jehovah your God.</span>
</div>
