<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Deuteronomy 15:19; Leviticus 22:32"> Speak unto Aaron and to his sons, that they separate themselves from the holy things of the children of Israel, which they hallow unto me, and that they profane not my holy name: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 7:20–21; Numbers 19:13; Psalms 16:11"> Say unto them, Whosoever he be of all your seed throughout your generations, that approacheth unto the holy things, which the children of Israel hallow unto Jehovah, having his uncleanness upon him, that soul shall be cut off from before me: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 11:39; Leviticus 15:2–3; Leviticus 15:13–16"> What man soever of the seed of Aaron is a leper, or hath an issue; he shall not eat of the holy things, until he be clean. And whoso toucheth anything that is unclean by the dead, or a man whose seed goeth from him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 11:43–44; Leviticus 15:7; Leviticus 15:19"> or whosoever toucheth any creeping thing, whereby he may be made unclean, or a man of whom he may take uncleanness, whatsoever uncleanness he hath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Hebrews 10:22; Haggai 2:13"> the soul that toucheth any such shall be unclean until the even, and shall not eat of the holy things, unless he bathe his flesh in water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 21:22; Deuteronomy 18:3–4; 1 Corinthians 9:13–14"> And when the sun is down, he shall be clean; and afterward he shall eat of the holy things, because it is his bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 17:15; Exodus 22:31; Ezekiel 44:31"> That which dieth of itself, or is torn of beasts, he shall not eat, to defile himself therewith: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 28:43; Numbers 18:22; Numbers 18:32"> They shall therefore keep my charge, lest they bear sin for it, and die therein, if they profane it: I am Jehovah who sanctifieth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 12:4; 1 Samuel 21:6; Exodus 29:33"> There shall no stranger eat of the holy thing: a sojourner of the priest’s, or a hired servant, shall not eat of the holy thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:13; Exodus 12:44; Numbers 18:11–13"> But if a priest buy any soul, the purchase of his money, he shall eat of it; and such as are born in his house, they shall eat of his bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13; Leviticus 21:3"> And if a priest’s daughter be married unto a stranger, she shall not eat of the heave-offering of the holy things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 10:14; Genesis 38:11; Numbers 18:11–19"> But if a priest’s daughter be a widow, or divorced, and have no child, and be returned unto her father’s house, as in her youth, she shall eat of her father’s bread: but there shall no stranger eat thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 27:15; Leviticus 27:13; Leviticus 5:15–19"> And if a man eat of the holy thing unwittingly, then he shall put the fifth part thereof unto it, and shall give unto the priest the holy thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 18:32; Leviticus 19:8; Ezekiel 22:26"> And they shall not profane the holy things of the children of Israel, which they offer unto Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 22:9; 1 Peter 2:24; Isaiah 53:11–12"> and so cause them to bear the iniquity that bringeth guilt, when they eat their holy things: for I am Jehovah who sanctifieth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Jehovah spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 1:2; Deuteronomy 12:6; Numbers 15:3"> Speak unto Aaron, and to his sons, and unto all the children of Israel, and say unto them, Whosoever he be of the house of Israel, or of the sojourners in Israel, that offereth his oblation, whether it be any of their vows, or any of their freewill-offerings, which they offer unto Jehovah for a burnt-offering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 1:10; Leviticus 1:3; Ephesians 5:27"> that ye may be accepted, ye shall offer a male without blemish, of the bullocks, of the sheep, or of the goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:1; Malachi 1:8; Deuteronomy 15:21"> But whatsoever hath a blemish, that shall ye not offer: for it shall not be acceptable for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 15:8; Numbers 15:3; Leviticus 3:6"> And whosoever offereth a sacrifice of peace-offerings unto Jehovah to accomplish a vow, or for a freewill-offering, of the herd or of the flock, it shall be perfect to be accepted; there shall be no blemish therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 22:20; Malachi 1:8; Leviticus 1:9"> Blind, or broken, or maimed, or having a wen, or scurvy, or scabbed, ye shall not offer these unto Jehovah, nor make an offering by fire of them upon the altar unto Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 21:18"> Either a bullock or a lamb that hath anything superfluous or lacking in his parts, that mayest thou offer for a freewill-offering; but for a vow it shall not be accepted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 22:20; Deuteronomy 23:1; Leviticus 21:20"> That which hath its stones bruised, or crushed, or broken, or cut, ye shall not offer unto Jehovah; neither shall ye do thus in your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 21:8; Ezra 6:8–10; Numbers 15:14–16"> Neither from the hand of a foreigner shall ye offer the bread of your God of any of these; because their corruption is in them, there is a blemish in them: they shall not be accepted for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Jehovah spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 22:30; Leviticus 19:23–24; Leviticus 22:25"> When a bullock, or a sheep, or a goat, is brought forth, then it shall be seven days under the dam; and from the eighth day and thenceforth it shall be accepted for the oblation of an offering made by fire unto Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:6–7; Deuteronomy 14:21; Exodus 34:26"> And whether it be cow or ewe, ye shall not kill it and its young both in one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 116:17; Psalms 107:22; Amos 4:5"> And when ye sacrifice a sacrifice of thanksgiving unto Jehovah, ye shall sacrifice it that ye may be accepted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 7:15–18; Exodus 16:19–20; Leviticus 19:7"> On the same day it shall be eaten; ye shall leave none of it until the morning: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Leviticus 19:37; Numbers 15:40"> Therefore shall ye keep my commandments, and do them: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Luke 11:2; Matthew 6:9"> And ye shall not profane my holy name; but I will be hallowed among the children of Israel: I am Jehovah who halloweth you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 11:45; Exodus 6:7; Leviticus 25:38"> who brought you out of the land of Egypt, to be your God: I am Jehovah.</span>
