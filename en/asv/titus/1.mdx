<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; 1 John 2:25; 2 Timothy 1:9"> in hope of eternal life, which God, who cannot lie, promised before times eternal;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:11; Titus 3:4–6; 1 Timothy 1:1"> but in his own seasons manifested his word in the message, wherewith I was intrusted according to the commandment of God our Saviour;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:2; Jude 1:3; 2 Peter 1:1"> to Titus, my true child after a common faith: Grace and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Saviour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 14:23; 1 Timothy 1:3; Ecclesiastes 12:9"> For this cause left I thee in Crete, that thou shouldest set in order the things that were wanting, and appoint elders in every city, as I gave thee charge;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2–7; 1 Timothy 3:12; Titus 1:6–8"> if any man is blameless, the husband of one wife, having children that believe, who are not accused of riot or unruly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; 2 Peter 2:10; 1 Peter 4:10"> For the bishop must be blameless, as God’s steward; not self-willed, not soon angry, no brawler, no striker, not greedy of filthy lucre;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; Titus 2:7; 1 Kings 5:7"> but given to hospitality, a lover of good, sober-minded, just, holy, self-controlled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:15; 2 Timothy 1:13; 1 Timothy 6:3"> holding to the faithful word which is according to the teaching, that he may be able both to exhort in the sound doctrine, and to convict the gainsayers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:6; 1 John 4:1; Galatians 2:4"> For there are many unruly men, vain talkers and deceivers, specially they of the circumcision,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:6; 1 Timothy 6:5; Romans 3:19"> whose mouths must be stopped; men who overthrow whole houses, teaching things which they ought not, for filthy lucre’s sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 16:18; 1 Timothy 4:2; Acts 17:28"> One of themselves, a prophet of their own, said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Cretans are always liars, evil beasts, idle gluttons.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 2:2; 1 Timothy 5:20; Titus 2:15"> This testimony is true. For which cause reprove them sharply, that they may be sound in the faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:4; Colossians 2:22; Galatians 4:9"> not giving heed to Jewish fables, and commandments of men who turn away from the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Romans 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:23"> To the pure all things are pure: but to them that are defiled and unbelieving nothing is pure; but both their mind and their conscience are defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 2:4; Isaiah 29:13; Ephesians 5:6"> They profess that they know God; but by their works they deny him, being abominable, and disobedient, and unto every good work reprobate.</span>
