<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Hebrews 9:11; Colossians 2:17"> For the law having a shadow of the good things to come, not the very image of the things, can never with the same sacrifices year by year, which they offer continually, make perfect them that draw nigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Micah 7:19; Psalms 103:12"> Else would they not have ceased to be offered? because the worshippers, having been once cleansed, would have had no more consciousness of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 9:7; Exodus 30:10; Leviticus 23:27–28"> But in those sacrifices there is a remembrance made of sins year by year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:11; Isaiah 1:11–15; Micah 6:6–8"> For it is impossible that the blood of bulls and goats should take away sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:6–8; Isaiah 1:11; Isaiah 7:14"> Wherefore when he cometh into the world, he saith,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Sacrifice and offering thou wouldest not,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But a body didst thou prepare for me;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 1:10; Matthew 3:17; Psalms 147:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">In whole burnt offerings and sacrifices for sin thou hadst no pleasure:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 40:7–8; John 4:34; John 6:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then said I, Lo, I am come</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">(In the roll of the book it is written of me)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">To do thy will, O God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5; Mark 12:33"> Saying above, Sacrifices and offerings and whole burnt offerings and sacrifices for sin thou wouldest not, neither hadst pleasure therein (the which are offered according to the law),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 7:18–19; Hebrews 12:27–28; Hebrews 10:7"> then hath he said, Lo, I am come to do thy will. He taketh away the first, that he may establish the second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:14; Hebrews 10:12; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> By which will we have been sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 5:1; Numbers 28:3; Hebrews 10:4"> And every priest indeed standeth day by day ministering and offering oftentimes the same sacrifices, the which can never take away sins:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Colossians 3:1; Romans 8:34"> but he, when he had offered one sacrifice for sins for ever, sat down on the right hand of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:13; Psalms 110:1; Luke 20:43"> henceforth expecting till his enemies be made the footstool of his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:1; Hebrews 9:14"> For by one offering he hath perfected for ever them that are sanctified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 3:6"> And the Holy Spirit also beareth witness to us; for after he hath said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33–34; Hebrews 8:8–12; Romans 11:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is the covenant that I will make with them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">After those days, saith the Lord:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will put my laws on their heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And upon their mind also will I write them;</span>then saith he,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Hebrews 8:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">And their sins and their iniquities will I remember no more.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 10:2; Hebrews 10:14"> Now where remission of these is, there is no more offering for sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 4:16"> Having therefore, brethren, boldness to enter into the holy place by the blood of Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 14:6; John 10:9; Hebrews 9:3"> by the way which he dedicated for us, a new and living way, through the veil, that is to say, his flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 4:14–16; Hebrews 8:1"> and having a great priest over the house of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25; John 3:5; Ephesians 5:26"> let us draw near with a true heart in fulness of faith, having our hearts sprinkled from an evil conscience: and having our body washed with pure water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:24; 1 Corinthians 1:9; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> let us hold fast the confession of our hope that it waver not; for he is faithful that promised:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; 1 John 3:18; Hebrews 13:1"> and let us consider one another to provoke unto love and good works;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 2:42; 1 Thessalonians 5:11; Matthew 18:20"> not forsaking our own assembling together, as the custom of some is, but exhorting one another; and so much the more, as ye see the day drawing nigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 2:20–22; Hebrews 6:4–6; John 9:41"> For if we sin wilfully after that we have received the knowledge of the truth, there remaineth no more a sacrifice for sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Hebrews 2:3; Zephaniah 1:18"> but a certain fearful expectation of judgment, and a fierceness of fire which shall devour the adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:2; Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:2–13"> A man that hath set at nought Moses’ law dieth without compassion on the word of two or three witnesses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 6:6; Ephesians 4:30; Isaiah 63:10"> of how much sorer punishment, think ye, shall he be judged worthy, who hath trodden under foot the Son of God, and hath counted the blood of the covenant wherewith he was sanctified an unholy thing, and hath done despite unto the Spirit of grace?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35–36; Romans 12:19; Nahum 1:2"> For we know him that said, Vengeance belongeth unto me, I will recompense. And again, The Lord shall judge his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; Luke 12:5; Hebrews 12:29"> It is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 6:4; Hebrews 12:4; 2 Timothy 2:3–13"> But call to remembrance the former days, in which, after ye were enlightened, ye endured a great conflict of sufferings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:9; Philippians 4:14; Hebrews 11:36"> partly, being made a gazingstock both by reproaches and afflictions; and partly, becoming partakers with them that were so used.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:16; Matthew 5:11–12"> For ye both had compassion on them that were in bonds, and took joyfully the spoiling of your possessions, knowing that ye have for yourselves a better possession and an abiding one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Galatians 6:8–10; Hebrews 3:6"> Cast not away therefore your boldness, which hath great recompense of reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 12:1; Psalms 37:7; James 5:7–11"> For ye have need of patience, that, having done the will of God, ye may receive the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="James 5:7–9; Habakkuk 2:3–4; Luke 18:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">For yet a very little while,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He that cometh shall come, and shall not tarry.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Galatians 3:11; Habakkuk 2:4; Romans 1:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But my righteous one shall live by faith:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And if he shrink back, my soul hath no pleasure in him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 John 5:5; 1 Peter 1:5; Mark 16:16"> But we are not of them that shrink back unto perdition; but of them that have faith unto the saving of the soul.</span>
