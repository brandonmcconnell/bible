<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Psalms 144:3; Job 7:17–18"> But one hath somewhere testified, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">What is man, that thou art mindful of him?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or the son of man, that thou visitest him?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 8:5; Philippians 2:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou madest him a little lower than the angels;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Thou crownedst him with glory and honor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And didst set him over the works of thy hands:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 13:3; 1 Corinthians 15:27; Psalms 8:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou didst put all things in subjection under his feet.</span><br />For in that he subjected all things unto him, he left nothing that is not subject to him. But now we see not yet all things subjected to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 2:7–9; Revelation 5:9; 1 John 4:9–10"> But we behold him who hath been made a little lower than the angels, even Jesus, because of the suffering of death crowned with glory and honor, that by the grace of God he should taste of death for every man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Acts 5:31; Luke 24:46"> For it became him, for whom are all things, and through whom are all things, in bringing many sons unto glory, to make the author of their salvation perfect through sufferings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:10; Hebrews 13:12; Hebrews 10:14"> For both he that sanctifieth and they that are sanctified are all of one: for which cause he is not ashamed to call them brethren,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:22; Psalms 40:10; John 18:20"> saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will declare thy name unto my brethren,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">In the midst of the congregation will I sing thy praise.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17–18; Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 91:2"> And again, I will put my trust in him. And again, Behold, I and the children whom God hath given me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:10; Romans 8:3; Colossians 2:15"> Since then the children are sharers in flesh and blood, he also himself in like manner partook of the same; that through death he might bring to nought him that had the power of death, that is, the devil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 8:15; 2 Timothy 1:7; Luke 1:74–75"> and might deliver all them who through fear of death were all their lifetime subject to bondage.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Hebrews 4:15–16; Jude 1:24"> For in that he himself hath suffered being tempted, he is able to succor them that are tempted.</span>
