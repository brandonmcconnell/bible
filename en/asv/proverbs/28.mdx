<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:7; Psalms 53:5; Isaiah 26:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wicked flee when no man pursueth;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But the righteous are bold as a lion.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:28; 1 Kings 16:8–29; 2 Kings 15:8–31"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the transgression of a land many are the princes thereof;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But by men of understanding and knowledge the state thereof shall be prolonged.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 18:28–30"> <span className="pl pl-1">A needy man that oppresseth the poor</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Is like a sweeping rain which leaveth no food.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Ephesians 5:11; Matthew 14:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">They that forsake the law praise the wicked;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But such as keep the law contend with them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 92:6; James 1:5; 1 John 2:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Evil men understand not justice;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But they that seek Jehovah understand all things.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 19:1; Proverbs 28:18; Luke 16:19–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Better is the poor that walketh in his integrity,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Than he that is perverse in his ways, though he be rich.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:3–4; Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 3:1–35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whoso keepeth the law is a wise son;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But he that is a companion of gluttons shameth his father.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 13:22; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that augmenteth his substance by interest and increase,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Gathereth it for him that hath pity on the poor.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 66:18; 2 Timothy 4:3–4; Zechariah 7:11–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that turneth away his ear from hearing the law,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Even his prayer is an abomination.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 26:27; Psalms 37:25–26; Galatians 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whoso causeth the upright to go astray in an evil way,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He shall fall himself into his own pit;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But the perfect shall inherit good.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 18:17; Ecclesiastes 9:15–17; Proverbs 26:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The rich man is wise in his own conceit;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But the poor that hath understanding searcheth him out.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 28:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the righteous triumph, there is great glory;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But when the wicked rise, men hide themselves.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 1:8–10; Psalms 32:3–5; Job 31:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that covereth his transgressions shall not prosper;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But whoso confesseth and forsaketh them shall obtain mercy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 23:17; Psalms 2:11; Isaiah 66:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Happy is the man that feareth alway;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But he that hardeneth his heart shall fall into mischief.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 2:16; Proverbs 19:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">As a roaring lion, and a ranging bear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So is a wicked ruler over a poor people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Jeremiah 22:15–17; Ecclesiastes 4:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">The prince that lacketh understanding is also a great oppressor;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But he that hateth covetousness shall prolong his days.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; 1 Kings 21:19; Exodus 21:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">A man that is laden with the blood of any person</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Shall flee unto the pit; let no man stay him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Proverbs 28:6; Revelation 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whoso walketh uprightly shall be delivered;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But he that is perverse in his ways shall fall at once.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Judges 9:4; Proverbs 27:23–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that tilleth his land shall have plenty of bread;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But he that followeth after vain persons shall have poverty enough.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 16:10–12; Proverbs 13:11; Revelation 2:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">A faithful man shall abound with blessings;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But he that maketh haste to be rich shall not be unpunished.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; Proverbs 18:5; Ezekiel 13:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">To have respect of persons is not good;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Neither that a man should transgress for a piece of bread.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; 1 Timothy 6:9; Proverbs 28:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that hath an evil eye hasteth after riches,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And knoweth not that want shall come upon him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5–6; Matthew 18:15; Psalms 141:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that rebuketh a man shall afterward find more favor</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Than he that flattereth with the tongue.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:4–6; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 19:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whoso robbeth his father or his mother, and saith, It is no transgression,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The same is the companion of a destroyer.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 11:25; 1 Timothy 6:6; Proverbs 13:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that is of a greedy spirit stirreth up strife;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But he that putteth his trust in Jehovah shall be made fat.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Jeremiah 17:9; Job 28:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that trusteth in his own heart is a fool;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But whoso walketh wisely, he shall be delivered.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Deuteronomy 15:7; Psalms 41:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that giveth unto the poor shall not lack;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But he that hideth his eyes shall have many a curse.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Job 24:4; Acts 12:23–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the wicked rise, men hide themselves;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But when they perish, the righteous increase.</span></span>
