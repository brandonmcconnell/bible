<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 1:8; Revelation 6:2–6; Zechariah 6:6"> In the first chariot were red horses; and in the second chariot black horses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 6:2; Revelation 6:8; Zechariah 1:8"> and in the third chariot white horses; and in the fourth chariot grizzled strong horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 1:9; Zechariah 5:10; Zechariah 1:19–21"> Then I answered and said unto the angel that talked with me, What are these, my lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 1:14; Hebrews 1:7; Psalms 104:3–4"> And the angel answered and said unto me, These are the four winds of heaven, which go forth from standing before the Lord of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:48; Jeremiah 4:6; Jeremiah 6:1"> The chariot wherein are the black horses goeth forth toward the north country; and the white went forth after them; and the grizzled went forth toward the south country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:9; Zechariah 1:10; Job 1:6–7"> And the strong went forth, and sought to go that they might walk to and fro through the earth: and he said, Get you hence, walk to and fro through the earth. So they walked to and fro through the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Zechariah 1:15; Isaiah 18:3–4"> Then cried he to me, and spake unto me, saying, Behold, they that go toward the north country have quieted my spirit in the north country.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 8:1; Zechariah 7:1; Zechariah 1:1"> And the word of Jehovah came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezra 7:14–16; Ezra 8:26–30; Isaiah 66:20"> Take of them of the captivity, even of Heldai, of Tobijah, and of Jedaiah; and come thou the same day, and go into the house of Josiah the son of Zephaniah, whither they are come from Babylon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 3:1; Psalms 21:3; Song of Solomon 3:11"> yea, take of them silver and gold, and make crowns, and set them upon the head of Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 3:8; Isaiah 4:2; Jeremiah 33:15"> and speak unto him, saying, Thus speaketh Jehovah of hosts, saying, Behold, the man whose name is the Branch: and he shall grow up out of his place; and he shall build the temple of Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Isaiah 49:5–6; Hebrews 3:1"> even he shall build the temple of Jehovah; and he shall bear the glory, and shall sit and rule upon his throne; and he shall be a priest upon his throne; and the counsel of peace shall be between them both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 26:13; Exodus 12:14; Mark 14:9"> And the crowns shall be to Helem, and to Tobijah, and to Jedaiah, and to Hen the son of Zephaniah, for a memorial in the temple of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 60:10; Zechariah 3:7; Isaiah 58:10–14"> And they that are far off shall come and build in the temple of Jehovah; and ye shall know that Jehovah of hosts hath sent me unto you. And this shall come to pass, if ye will diligently obey the voice of Jehovah your God.</span>
