<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:1; 1 Corinthians 15:2; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> For yourselves, brethren, know our entering in unto you, that it hath not been found vain:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 16:22–24; Acts 4:13; Acts 17:2–9"> but having suffered before and been shamefully treated, as ye know, at Philippi, we waxed bold in our God to speak unto you the gospel of God in much conflict.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 2 Corinthians 4:2; 1 Thessalonians 4:7"> For our exhortation is not of error, nor of uncleanness, nor in guile:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 1:10; Romans 8:27; Galatians 2:7"> but even as we have been approved of God to be intrusted with the gospel, so we speak; not as pleasing men, but God who proveth our hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 1:9; 2 Corinthians 12:17; Acts 20:33"> For neither at any time were we found using words of flattery, as ye know, nor a cloak of covetousness, God is witness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 1:10; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; John 5:41"> nor seeking glory of men, neither from you nor from others, when we might have claimed authority as apostles of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 66:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:11; 2 Timothy 2:24–25"> But we were gentle in the midst of you, as when a nurse cherisheth her own children:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 3:16; 2 Corinthians 12:15; Philippians 2:17"> even so, being affectionately desirous of you, we were well pleased to impart unto you, not the gospel of God only, but also our own souls, because ye were become very dear to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–9; 2 Corinthians 11:9; Acts 18:3"> For ye remember, brethren, our labor and travail: working night and day, that we might not burden any of you, we preached unto you the gospel of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; 1 Thessalonians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> Ye are witnesses, and God also, how holily and righteously and unblamably we behaved ourselves toward you that believe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:14–15; Hebrews 13:22; 1 Timothy 5:21"> as ye know how we dealt with each one of you, as a father with his own children, exhorting you, and encouraging you, and testifying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:1; 1 John 2:6; 1 John 1:6–7"> to the end that ye should walk worthily of God, who calleth you into his own kingdom and glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Romans 10:17; 1 Peter 1:23"> And for this cause we also thank God without ceasing, that, when ye received from us the word of the message, even the word of God, ye accepted it not as the word of men, but, as it is in truth, the word of God, which also worketh in you that believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:4; Hebrews 10:33–34; Hebrews 5:7–8"> For ye, brethren, became imitators of the churches of God which are in Judæa in Christ Jesus: for ye also suffered the same things of your own countrymen, even as they did of the Jews;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:12; Luke 11:48–53; Acts 7:52"> who both killed the Lord Jesus and the prophets, and drove out us, and please not God, and are contrary to all men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:13; Acts 14:19; Mark 16:16"> forbidding us to speak to the Gentiles that they may be saved; to fill up their sins always: but the wrath is come upon them to the uttermost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Colossians 2:5; 1 Corinthians 5:3; 1 Thessalonians 3:6"> But we, brethren, being bereaved of you for a short season, in presence not in heart, endeavored the more exceedingly to see your face with great desire:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 15:22; Romans 1:13; Revelation 2:10"> because we would fain have come unto you, I Paul once and again; and Satan hindered us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 4:1; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; Philippians 2:16"> For what is our hope, or joy, or crown of glorying? Are not even ye, before our Lord Jesus at his coming?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:14; 1 Corinthians 11:7; Proverbs 17:6"> For ye are our glory and our joy.</span>
