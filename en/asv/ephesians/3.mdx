<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:24; 2 Timothy 1:8; Philippians 1:13–16"> For this cause I Paul, the prisoner of Christ Jesus in behalf of you Gentiles,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 1:25–27; 1 Timothy 1:11; 2 Timothy 1:11"> if so be that ye have heard of the dispensation of that grace of God which was given me to you-ward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 1:12; Romans 16:25; Ephesians 1:9–11"> how that by revelation was made known unto me the mystery, as I wrote before in few words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 4:3; 1 Corinthians 4:1; 2 Corinthians 11:6"> whereby, when ye read, ye can perceive my understanding in the mystery of Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 1:10–12; 2 Peter 3:2; Titus 1:1–3"> which in other generations was not made known unto the sons of men, as it hath now been revealed unto his holy apostles and prophets in the Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 3:26–29; 1 John 1:3; 1 John 2:25"> to wit, that the Gentiles are fellow-heirs, and fellow-members of the body, and fellow-partakers of the promise in Christ Jesus through the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 1:19; Ephesians 3:20; Ephesians 3:2"> whereof I was made a minister, according to the gift of that grace of God which was given me according to the working of his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 1:27; 1 Corinthians 15:9; Ephesians 2:7"> Unto me, who am less than the least of all saints, was this grace given, to preach unto the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:26; Matthew 13:35; 2 Timothy 1:9"> and to make all men see what is the dispensation of the mystery which for ages hath been hid in God who created all things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 1:12; Ephesians 6:12; 1 Corinthians 2:7"> to the intent that now unto the principalities and the powers in the heavenly places might be made known through the church the manifold wisdom of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:11; Ephesians 1:4"> according to the eternal purpose which he purposed in Christ Jesus our Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Romans 5:2; Hebrews 4:14–16"> in whom we have boldness and access in confidence through our faith in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:24; 2 Thessalonians 3:13; 2 Corinthians 1:6"> Wherefore I ask that ye may not faint at my tribulations for you, which are your glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 95:6; Ephesians 1:16–19; Philippians 2:10"> For this cause I bow my knees unto the Father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 1:10; Revelation 5:8–14; Ephesians 1:21"> from whom every family in heaven and on earth is named,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:11; Philippians 4:13; Isaiah 40:29–31"> that he would grant you, according to the riches of his glory, that ye may be strengthened with power through his Spirit in the inward man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Colossians 2:7; 1 John 4:16; John 14:23"> that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith; to the end that ye, being rooted and grounded in love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 103:11–12; Job 11:7–9; Ephesians 1:18–23"> may be strong to apprehend with all the saints what is the breadth and length and height and depth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 1:23; Colossians 2:9–10; Colossians 1:10"> and to know the love of Christ which passeth knowledge, that ye may be filled unto all the fulness of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8; Romans 4:21; Jeremiah 32:27"> Now unto him that is able to do exceeding abundantly above all that we ask or think, according to the power that worketh in us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jude 1:25; Romans 11:36; 2 Peter 3:18"> unto him be the glory in the church and in Christ Jesus unto all generations for ever and ever. Amen.</span>
