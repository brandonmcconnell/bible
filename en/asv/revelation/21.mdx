<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17–19; 2 Peter 3:13; Isaiah 66:22"> And I saw a new heaven and a new earth: for the first heaven and the first earth are passed away; and the sea is no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:10; Revelation 3:12; Revelation 21:10"> And I saw the holy city, new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God, made ready as a bride adorned for her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27; 2 Corinthians 6:16; John 14:23"> And I heard a great voice out of the throne saying, Behold, the tabernacle of God is with men, and he shall dwell with them, and they shall be his peoples, and God himself shall be with them, and be their God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 7:17; 1 Corinthians 15:26"> and he shall wipe away every tear from their eyes; and death shall be no more; neither shall there be mourning, nor crying, nor pain, any more: the first things are passed away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Isaiah 42:9; Isaiah 43:19"> And he that sitteth on the throne said, Behold, I make all things new. And he saith, Write: for these words are faithful and true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 7:37–38; John 4:10; Revelation 7:17"> And he said unto me, They are come to pass. I am the Alpha and the Omega, the beginning and the end. I will give unto him that is athirst of the fountain of the water of life freely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:18; Hebrews 8:10; Revelation 2:11"> He that overcometh shall inherit these things; and I will be his God, and he shall be my son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Revelation 2:11; Ephesians 5:5–6"> But for the fearful, and unbelieving, and abominable, and murderers, and fornicators, and sorcerers, and idolaters, and all liars, their part shall be in the lake that burneth with fire and brimstone; which is the second death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; Revelation 19:7; Revelation 16:1–17"> And there came one of the seven angels who had the seven bowls, who were laden with the seven last plagues; and he spake with me, saying, Come hither, I will show thee the bride, the wife of the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; Ezekiel 11:24; Ezekiel 40:1–49"> And he carried me away in the Spirit to a mountain great and high, and showed me the holy city Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 4:6; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 22:1"> having the glory of God: her light was like unto a stone most precious, as it were a jasper stone, clear as crystal:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:31–34; Revelation 7:4–8; Revelation 21:17–21"> having a wall great and high; having twelve gates, and at the gates twelve angels; and names written thereon, which are the names of the twelve tribes of the children of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:31–34"> on the east were three gates; and on the north three gates; and on the south three gates; and on the west three gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 11:10; Ephesians 2:20; Isaiah 54:11"> And the wall of the city had twelve foundations, and on them twelve names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 2:1; Revelation 11:1–2; Ezekiel 41:1–5"> And he that spake with me had for a measure a golden reed to measure the city, and the gates thereof, and the wall thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:35; Ezekiel 48:8–20"> And the city lieth foursquare, and the length thereof is as great as the breadth: and he measured the city with the reed, twelve thousand furlongs: the length and the breadth and the height thereof are equal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Deuteronomy 3:11; Revelation 13:18"> And he measured the wall thereof, a hundred and forty and four cubits, according to the measure of a man, that is, of an angel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:11; Revelation 21:21; Revelation 21:19"> And the building of the wall thereof was jasper: and the city was pure gold, like unto pure glass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 54:11–12; Exodus 28:17–21; Job 28:16–19"> The foundations of the wall of the city were adorned with all manner of precious stones. The first foundation was jasper; the second, sapphire; the third, chalcedony; the fourth, emerald;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 4:3"> the fifth, sardonyx; the sixth, sardius; the seventh, chrysolite; the eighth, beryl; the ninth, topaz; the tenth, chrysoprase; the eleventh, jacinth; the twelfth, amethyst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:18; Revelation 17:4; Revelation 22:2"> And the twelve gates were twelve pearls; each one of the several gates was of one pearl: and the street of the city was pure gold, as it were transparent glass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:23; 1 Kings 8:27; 2 Chronicles 2:6"> And I saw no temple therein: for the Lord God the Almighty, and the Lamb, are the temple thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 21:11"> And the city hath no need of the sun, neither of the moon, to shine upon it: for the glory of God did lighten it, and the lamp thereof is the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 21:26; Revelation 22:2; Zechariah 8:22–23"> And the nations shall walk amidst the light thereof: and the kings of the earth bring their glory into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 60:11; Zechariah 14:7; Revelation 22:5"> And the gates thereof shall in no wise be shut by day (for there shall be no night there):</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 21:24"> and they shall bring the glory and the honor of the nations into it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 22:14–15; Isaiah 52:1; Revelation 20:15"> and there shall in no wise enter into it anything unclean, or he that maketh an abomination and a lie: but only they that are written in the Lamb’s book of life.</span>
