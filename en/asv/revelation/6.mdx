<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 6:5; Revelation 5:1; Revelation 6:7"> And I saw when the Lamb opened one of the seven seals, and I heard one of the four living creatures saying as with a voice of thunder, Come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 6:3–8; Zechariah 1:8; Psalms 45:3–5"> And I saw, and behold, a white horse, and he that sat thereon had a bow; and there was given unto him a crown: and he came forth conquering, and to conquer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 4:7; Revelation 6:1"> And when he opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature saying, Come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 6:2; Matthew 10:34; Revelation 13:10"> And another horse came forth, a red horse: and to him that sat thereon it was given to take peace from the earth, and that they should slay one another: and there was given unto him a great sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 6:2; Ezekiel 4:16; Zechariah 6:6"> And when he opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature saying, Come. And I saw, and behold, a black horse; and he that sat thereon had a balance in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 9:4; Revelation 4:6; Revelation 7:3"> And I heard as it were a voice in the midst of the four living creatures saying, A measure of wheat for a shilling, and three measures of barley for a shilling; and the oil and the wine hurt thou not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 4:7; Revelation 6:3; Revelation 6:1"> And when he opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourth living creature saying, Come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; Jeremiah 15:2–3; Revelation 1:18"> And I saw, and behold, a pale horse: and he that sat upon him, his name was Death; and Hades followed with him. And there was given unto them authority over the fourth part of the earth, to kill with sword, and with famine, and with death, and by the wild beasts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 20:4; John 16:2; Revelation 1:9"> And when he opened the fifth seal, I saw underneath the altar the souls of them that had been slain for the word of God, and for the testimony which they held:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 19:2; Psalms 9:12; Revelation 16:5–7"> and they cried with a great voice, saying, How long, O Master, the holy and true, dost thou not judge and avenge our blood on them that dwell on the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Revelation 7:9; Revelation 3:4–5"> And there was given them to each one a white robe; and it was said unto them, that they should rest yet for a little time, until their fellow-servants also and their brethren, who should be killed even as they were, should have fulfilled their course.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Joel 2:10; Acts 2:19–20"> And I saw when he opened the sixth seal, and there was a great earthquake; and the sun became black as sackcloth of hair, and the whole moon became as blood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4; Matthew 24:29; Revelation 9:1"> and the stars of the heaven fell unto the earth, as a fig tree casteth her unripe figs when she is shaken of a great wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4; Revelation 16:20; 2 Peter 3:10"> And the heaven was removed as a scroll when it is rolled up; and every mountain and island were moved out of their places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19; Isaiah 2:21; Isaiah 24:21–22"> And the kings of the earth, and the princes, and the chief captains, and the rich, and the strong, and every bondman and freeman, hid themselves in the caves and in the rocks of the mountains;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 23:30; Hosea 10:8; Revelation 4:2"> and they say to the mountains and to the rocks, Fall on us, and hide us from the face of him that sitteth on the throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 3:2; Joel 2:11; Psalms 76:7"> for the great day of their wrath is come; and who is able to stand?</span>
