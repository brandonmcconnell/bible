<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Colossians 2:18–19; Revelation 14:7"> And he saith unto me, See thou do it not: I am a fellow-servant with thee and with thy brethren the prophets, and with them that keep the words of this book: worship God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 8:26; Revelation 1:3; 1 Peter 4:7"> And he saith unto me, Seal not up the words of the prophecy of this book; for the time is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:10; Proverbs 4:18; Ezekiel 3:27"> He that is unrighteous, let him do unrighteousness still: and he that is filthy, let him be made filthy still: and he that is righteous, let him do righteousness still: and he that is holy, let him be made holy still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Isaiah 62:11; Isaiah 40:10"> Behold, I come quickly; and my reward is with me, to render to each man according as his work is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 21:6; Isaiah 44:6"> I am the Alpha and the Omega, the first and the last, the beginning and the end.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; Galatians 5:19–21; Philippians 3:2"> Without are the dogs, and the sorcerers, and the fornicators, and the murderers, and the idolaters, and every one that loveth and maketh a lie.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:2; Luke 11:52; Revelation 22:2"> and if any man shall take away from the words of the book of this prophecy, God shall take away his part from the tree of life, and out of the holy city, which are written in this book.</span>
