<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:24; Malachi 3:6; Acts 17:24–25"> And God spake unto Moses, and said unto him, I am Jehovah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; Psalms 68:4; Genesis 17:1"> and I appeared unto Abraham, unto Isaac, and unto Jacob, as God Almighty; but by my name Jehovah I was not known to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:4; Genesis 15:18; Genesis 17:7–8"> And I have also established my covenant with them, to give them the land of Canaan, the land of their sojournings, wherein they sojourned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 2:24; Psalms 106:44–45; Isaiah 63:9"> And moreover I have heard the groaning of the children of Israel, whom the Egyptians keep in bondage; and I have remembered my covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:8; 1 Chronicles 17:21; Exodus 7:4"> Wherefore say unto the children of Israel, I am Jehovah, and I will bring you out from under the burdens of the Egyptians, and I will rid you out of their bondage, and I will redeem you with an outstretched arm, and with great judgments:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 29:13; Deuteronomy 4:20"> and I will take you to me for a people, and I will be to you a God; and ye shall know that I am Jehovah your God, who bringeth you out from under the burdens of the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 26:3; Genesis 15:18; Ezekiel 20:42"> And I will bring you in unto the land which I sware to give to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob; and I will give it you for a heritage: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 5:21; Job 21:4; Exodus 14:12"> And Moses spake so unto the children of Israel: but they hearkened not unto Moses for anguish of spirit, and for cruel bondage.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Jehovah spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 6:29; Exodus 5:23; Exodus 3:10"> Go in, speak unto Pharaoh king of Egypt, that he let the children of Israel go out of his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 4:10; Jeremiah 1:6; Exodus 6:30"> And Moses spake before Jehovah, saying, Behold, the children of Israel have not hearkened unto me; how then shall Pharaoh hear me, who am of uncircumcised lips?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 27:23; 1 Timothy 6:13; Psalms 91:11"> And Jehovah spake unto Moses and unto Aaron, and gave them a charge unto the children of Israel, and unto Pharaoh king of Egypt, to bring the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 46:9; 1 Chronicles 5:3; Numbers 26:5–7"> These are the heads of their fathers’ houses. The sons of Reuben the first-born of Israel: Hanoch, and Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi; these are the families of Reuben.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:24; Genesis 46:10; Numbers 26:12–13"> And the sons of Simeon: Jemuel, and Jamin, and Ohad, and Jachin, and Zohar, and Shaul the son of a Canaanitish woman; these are the families of Simeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 3:17; Genesis 46:11; 1 Chronicles 6:1"> And these are the names of the sons of Levi according to their generations: Gershon, and Kohath, and Merari; and the years of the life of Levi were a hundred thirty and seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:17; Numbers 3:18; 1 Chronicles 23:7"> The sons of Gershon: Libni and Shimei, according to their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Chronicles 6:2; Numbers 3:19"> And the sons of Kohath: Amram, and Izhar, and Hebron, and Uzziel; and the years of the life of Kohath were a hundred thirty and three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 3:20; 1 Chronicles 23:21; 1 Chronicles 6:19"> And the sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. These are the families of the Levites according to their generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; Exodus 2:1–2; Exodus 6:18"> And Amram took him Jochebed his father’s sister to wife; and she bare him Aaron and Moses: and the years of the life of Amram were a hundred and thirty and seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 16:1; 1 Chronicles 6:37–38; Numbers 16:32"> And the sons of Izhar: Korah, and Nepheg, and Zichri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 10:4; Numbers 3:30; Nehemiah 3:20"> And the sons of Uzziel: Mishael, and Elzaphan, and Sithri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ruth 4:19–20; Numbers 2:3; Numbers 1:7"> And Aaron took him Elisheba, the daughter of Amminadab, the sister of Nahshon, to wife; and she bare him Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:22–23; Numbers 16:1; 1 Samuel 1:1"> And the sons of Korah: Assir, and Elkanah, and Abiasaph; these are the families of the Korahites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 24:33; Numbers 25:7–13; Joshua 22:31–32"> And Eleazar Aaron’s son took him one of the daughters of Putiel to wife; and she bare him Phinehas. These are the heads of the fathers’ houses of the Levites according to their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 12:51; Exodus 12:17; Exodus 7:4"> These are that Aaron and Moses, to whom Jehovah said, Bring out the children of Israel from the land of Egypt according to their hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 6:26; Exodus 7:10; Psalms 77:20"> These are they that spake to Pharaoh king of Egypt, to bring out the children of Israel from Egypt: these are that Moses and Aaron.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And it came to pass on the day when Jehovah spake unto Moses in the land of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 6:11; Exodus 7:2; Exodus 6:2"> that Jehovah spake unto Moses, saying, I am Jehovah: speak thou unto Pharaoh king of Egypt all that I speak unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 6:12; Exodus 4:10; 1 Corinthians 9:16–17"> And Moses said before Jehovah, Behold, I am of uncircumcised lips, and how shall Pharaoh hearken unto me?</span>
