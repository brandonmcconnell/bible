<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 34:32; Exodus 31:1–11; Romans 2:13"> And Moses assembled all the congregation of the children of Israel, and said unto them, These are the words which Jehovah hath commanded, that ye should do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:3; Luke 13:14–15; Exodus 20:9–10"> Six days shall work be done; but on the seventh day there shall be to you a holy day, a sabbath of solemn rest to Jehovah: whosoever doeth any work therein shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 16:23; Numbers 15:32–36; Exodus 12:16"> Ye shall kindle no fire throughout your habitations upon the sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:1–2"> And Moses spake unto all the congregation of the children of Israel, saying, This is the thing which Jehovah commanded, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Psalms 110:3; Exodus 25:2–7"> Take ye from among you an offering unto Jehovah; whosoever is of a willing heart, let him bring it, Jehovah’s offering: gold, and silver, and brass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–14; Exodus 26:31; Exodus 28:15"> and blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine linen, and goats’ hair,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and rams’ skins dyed red, and sealskins, and acacia wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:1–40; Exodus 30:23"> and oil for the light, and spices for the anointing oil, and for the sweet incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 39:6–14; Exodus 28:9; Exodus 25:5"> and onyx stones, and stones to be set, for the ephod, and for the breastplate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 31:1–6; Exodus 36:1–4"> And let every wise-hearted man among you come, and make all that Jehovah hath commanded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:1–37; Exodus 36:8–34; Exodus 31:7–9"> the tabernacle, its tent, and its covering, its clasps, and its boards, its bars, its pillars, and its sockets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–22; Exodus 26:31–33; Exodus 37:1–9"> the ark, and the staves thereof, the mercy-seat, and the veil of the screen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 25:23–30; Leviticus 24:5–6; Exodus 37:10–16"> the table, and its staves, and all its vessels, and the showbread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Matthew 5:14–15; Exodus 37:17–24"> the candlestick also for the light, and its vessels, and its lamps, and the oil for the light;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 30:1–10; Exodus 30:22–38; Exodus 37:25–28"> and the altar of incense, and its staves, and the anointing oil, and the sweet incense, and the screen for the door, at the door of the tabernacle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 30:18–21; Exodus 38:1–8"> the altar of burnt-offering, with its grating of brass, its staves, and all its vessels, the laver and its base;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Exodus 38:9–20; Exodus 27:9–19"> the hangings of the court, the pillars thereof, and their sockets, and the screen for the gate of the court;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 27:19"> the pins of the tabernacle, and the pins of the court, and their cords;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 31:10; Numbers 4:5–15; Exodus 28:1–43"> the finely wrought garments, for ministering in the holy place, the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons, to minister in the priest’s office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And all the congregation of the children of Israel departed from the presence of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:2; Exodus 35:26; 1 Chronicles 29:14"> And they came, every one whose heart stirred him up, and every one whom his spirit made willing, and brought Jehovah’s offering, for the work of the tent of meeting, and for all the service thereof, and for the holy garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:70–72; Matthew 2:11; Ezra 2:68–69"> And they came, both men and women, as many as were willing-hearted, and brought brooches, and ear-rings, and signet-rings, and armlets, all jewels of gold; even every man that offered an offering of gold unto Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:8; Exodus 25:2–7; Exodus 35:6–10"> And every man, with whom was found blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine linen, and goats’ hair, and rams’ skins dyed red, and sealskins, brought them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12"> Every one that did offer an offering of silver and brass brought Jehovah’s offering; and every man, with whom was found acacia wood for any work of the service, brought it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 16:12; Luke 8:2–3; Proverbs 14:1"> And all the women that were wise-hearted did spin with their hands, and brought that which they had spun, the blue, and the purple, the scarlet, and the fine linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:21; Exodus 36:8; Exodus 35:29"> And all the women whose heart stirred them up in wisdom spun the goats’ hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezra 2:68; 1 Chronicles 29:6; Exodus 35:9"> And the rulers brought the onyx stones, and the stones to be set, for the ephod, and for the breastplate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 30:23–38; Exodus 35:8"> and the spice, and the oil; for the light, and for the anointing oil, and for the sweet incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Exodus 35:21–22; Exodus 35:4"> The children of Israel brought a freewill-offering unto Jehovah; every man and woman, whose heart made them willing to bring for all the work, which Jehovah had commanded to be made by Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 31:1–6; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> And Moses said unto the children of Israel, See, Jehovah hath called by name Bezalel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:17; Isaiah 11:2–5; Isaiah 61:1–3"> and he hath filled him with the Spirit of God, in wisdom, in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all manner of workmanship;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> and to devise skilful works, to work in gold, and in silver, and in brass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and in cutting of stones for setting, and in carving of wood, to work in all manner of skilful workmanship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 31:6; 2 Chronicles 2:14; Ezra 7:27"> And he hath put in his heart that he may teach, both he, and Oholiab, the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 35:31; 1 Kings 7:14; Isaiah 28:26"> Them hath he filled with wisdom of heart, to work all manner of workmanship, of the engraver, and of the skilful workman, and of the embroiderer, in blue, and in purple, in scarlet, and in fine linen, and of the weaver, even of them that do any workmanship, and of those that devise skilful works.</span>
