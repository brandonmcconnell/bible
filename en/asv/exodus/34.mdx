<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 32:19; Exodus 31:18; Psalms 119:89"> And Jehovah said unto Moses, Hew thee two tables of stone like unto the first: and I will write upon the tables the words that were on the first tables, which thou brakest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 19:20; Deuteronomy 9:25; Exodus 19:11"> And be ready by the morning, and come up in the morning unto mount Sinai, and present thyself there to me on the top of the mount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 19:12–13; Exodus 19:21; 1 Timothy 2:5"> And no man shall come up with thee; neither let any man be seen throughout all the mount; neither let the flocks nor herds feed before that mount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And he hewed two tables of stone like unto the first; and Moses rose up early in the morning, and went up unto mount Sinai, as Jehovah had commanded him, and took in his hand two tables of stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 33:19; Numbers 11:25; Luke 9:34–35"> And Jehovah descended in the cloud, and stood with him there, and proclaimed the name of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Joel 2:13; Psalms 103:8–13"> And Jehovah passed by before him, and proclaimed, Jehovah, Jehovah, a God merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and abundant in lovingkindness and truth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:5–6; Psalms 103:3; Deuteronomy 5:9–10"> keeping lovingkindness for thousands, forgiving iniquity and transgression and sin; and that will by no means clear the guilty, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children, and upon the children’s children, upon the third and upon the fourth generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; 2 Chronicles 20:18; Genesis 17:3"> And Moses made haste, and bowed his head toward the earth, and worshipped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 33:12; Deuteronomy 32:9; Psalms 94:14"> And he said, If now I have found favor in thy sight, O Lord, let the Lord, I pray thee, go in the midst of us; for it is a stiffnecked people; and pardon our iniquity and our sin, and take us for thine inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:21; Psalms 77:14; Isaiah 64:3"> And he said, Behold, I make a covenant: before all thy people I will do marvels, such as have not been wrought in all the earth, nor in any nation; and all the people among which thou art shall see the work of Jehovah; for it is a terrible thing that I do with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 33:2; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 3:8"> Observe thou that which I command thee this day: behold, I drive out before thee the Amorite, and the Canaanite, and the Hittite, and the Perizzite, and the Hivite, and the Jebusite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 23:32–33; Joshua 23:12–13; Deuteronomy 7:2"> Take heed to thyself, lest thou make a covenant with the inhabitants of the land whither thou goest, lest it be for a snare in the midst of thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 18:4; Exodus 23:24; Deuteronomy 7:25–26"> but ye shall break down their altars, and dash in pieces their pillars, and ye shall cut down their Asherim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Deuteronomy 32:21; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> (for thou shalt worship no other god: for Jehovah, whose name is Jealous, is a jealous God);</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 2:17; Psalms 106:28; Numbers 25:2"> lest thou make a covenant with the inhabitants of the land, and they play the harlot after their gods, and sacrifice unto their gods, and one call thee and thou eat of his sacrifice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3–4; 1 Kings 11:2–4; Ezra 9:2"> and thou take of their daughters unto thy sons, and their daughters play the harlot after their gods, and make thy sons play the harlot after their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 19:4; Exodus 32:8; Isaiah 46:6–7"> Thou shalt make thee no molten gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 13:4; Leviticus 23:6; Exodus 13:6–7"> The feast of unleavened bread shalt thou keep. Seven days thou shalt eat unleavened bread, as I commanded thee, at the time appointed in the month Abib; for in the month Abib thou camest out from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 22:29; Exodus 13:12"> All that openeth the womb is mine; and all thy cattle that is male, the firstlings of cow and sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:13; Exodus 23:15; Deuteronomy 16:16"> And the firstling of an ass thou shalt redeem with a lamb: and if thou wilt not redeem it, then thou shalt break its neck. All the first-born of thy sons thou shalt redeem. And none shall appear before me empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:12; Deuteronomy 21:4; Luke 13:14"> Six days thou shalt work, but on the seventh day thou shalt rest: in plowing time and in harvest thou shalt rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Deuteronomy 16:10–15; John 7:2"> And thou shalt observe the feast of weeks, even of the first-fruits of wheat harvest, and the feast of ingathering at the year’s end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 23:14; Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 23:17"> Three times in the year shall all thy males appear before the Lord Jehovah, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 78:55; Exodus 33:2; Proverbs 16:7"> For I will cast out nations before thee, and enlarge thy borders: neither shall any man desire thy land, when thou goest up to appear before Jehovah thy God three times in the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 23:18; Exodus 12:10; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8"> Thou shalt not offer the blood of my sacrifice with leavened bread; neither shall the sacrifice of the feast of the passover be left unto the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Deuteronomy 14:21; Deuteronomy 26:2"> The first of the first-fruits of thy ground thou shalt bring unto the house of Jehovah thy God. Thou shalt not boil a kid in its mother’s milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 24:4; Deuteronomy 31:9; Deuteronomy 4:13"> And Jehovah said unto Moses, Write thou these words: for after the tenor of these words I have made a covenant with thee and with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 34:1; Exodus 24:18; Deuteronomy 9:18"> And he was there with Jehovah forty days and forty nights; he did neither eat bread, nor drink water. And he wrote upon the tables the words of the covenant, the ten commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 17:2; Luke 9:29; Exodus 32:15"> And it came to pass, when Moses came down from mount Sinai with the two tables of the testimony in Moses’ hand, when he came down from the mount, that Moses knew not that the skin of his face shone by reason of his speaking with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 9:3; Luke 5:8; Numbers 12:8"> And when Aaron and all the children of Israel saw Moses, behold, the skin of his face shone; and they were afraid to come nigh him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 3:15; Exodus 24:1–3; Genesis 45:3"> And Moses called unto them; and Aaron and all the rulers of the congregation returned unto him: and Moses spake to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 22:14; Exodus 24:3; Matthew 28:20"> And afterward all the children of Israel came nigh: and he gave them in commandment all that Jehovah had spoken with him in mount Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4–6; 2 Corinthians 3:13–18; Romans 10:4"> And when Moses had done speaking with them, he put a veil on his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:16; Hebrews 10:19–22; Hebrews 4:16"> But when Moses went in before Jehovah to speak with him, he took the veil off, until he came out; and he came out, and spake unto the children of Israel that which he was commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 34:29–30; John 5:35; Daniel 12:3"> And the children of Israel saw the face of Moses, that the skin of Moses’ face shone: and Moses put the veil upon his face again, until he went in to speak with him.</span>
