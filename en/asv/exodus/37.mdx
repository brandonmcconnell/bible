<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 30:3"> And he overlaid it with pure gold within and without, and made a crown of gold to it round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And he cast for it four rings of gold, in the four feet thereof; even two rings on the one side of it, and two rings on the other side of it.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Titus 2:14; Romans 3:25"> And he made a mercy-seat of pure gold: two cubits and a half was the length thereof, and a cubit and a half the breadth thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:2; Psalms 104:4; Psalms 80:1"> And he made two cherubim of gold; of beaten work made he them, at the two ends of the mercy-seat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> one cherub at the one end, and one cherub at the other end: of one piece with the mercy-seat made he the cherubim at the two ends thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 1:51; Hebrews 1:14; Genesis 28:12"> And the cherubim spread out their wings on high, covering the mercy-seat with their wings, with their faces one to another; toward the mercy-seat were the faces of the cherubim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:14; Ezekiel 40:39–42; Malachi 1:12"> And he made the table of acacia wood: two cubits was the length thereof, and a cubit the breadth thereof, and a cubit and a half the height thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he overlaid it with pure gold, and made thereto a crown of gold round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he made unto it a border of a handbreadth round about, and made a golden crown to the border thereof round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he cast for it four rings of gold, and put the rings in the four corners that were on the four feet thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Close by the border were the rings, the places for the staves to bear the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he made the staves of acacia wood, and overlaid them with gold, to bear the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:29; 1 Kings 7:50; 2 Kings 12:13"> And he made the vessels which were upon the table, the dishes thereof, and the spoons thereof, and the bowls thereof, and the flagons thereof, wherewith to pour out, of pure gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Hebrews 9:2; 2 Chronicles 13:11"> And he made the candlestick of pure gold: of beaten work made he the candlestick, even its base, and its shaft; its cups, its knops, and its flowers, were of one piece with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And there were six branches going out of the sides thereof; three branches of the candlestick out of the one side thereof, and three branches of the candlestick out of the other side thereof:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> three cups made like almond-blossoms in one branch, a knop and a flower, and three cups made like almond-blossoms in the other branch, a knop and a flower: so for the six branches going out of the candlestick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5; Jeremiah 1:11; Exodus 25:33"> And in the candlestick were four cups made like almond-blossoms, the knops thereof, and the flowers thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:35"> and a knop under two branches of one piece with it, and a knop under two branches of one piece with it, and a knop under two branches of one piece with it, for the six branches going out of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Exodus 25:31; Psalms 51:17"> Their knops and their branches were of one piece with it: the whole of it was one beaten work of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 1:12; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 1:20–1"> And he made the lamps thereof, seven, and the snuffers thereof, and the snuffdishes thereof, of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Of a talent of pure gold made he it, and all the vessels thereof.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And he overlaid it with pure gold, the top thereof, and the sides thereof round about, and the horns of it: and he made unto it a crown of gold round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And he made for it two golden rings under the crown thereof, upon the two ribs thereof, upon the two sides of it, for places for staves wherewith to bear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he made the staves of acacia wood, and overlaid them with gold.</span>
