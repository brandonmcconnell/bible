<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 4:17; Hebrews 5:4; Exodus 31:6"> And Moses called Bezalel and Oholiab, and every wise-hearted man, in whose heart Jehovah had put wisdom, even every one whose heart stirred him up to come unto the work to do it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:27; Exodus 35:5–21"> and they received of Moses all the offering which the children of Israel had brought for the work of the service of the sanctuary, wherewith to make it. And they brought yet unto him freewill-offerings every morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:10; Luke 12:42; Matthew 24:45"> And all the wise men, that wrought all the work of the sanctuary, came every man from his work which they wrought;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:6–10; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 2 Chronicles 24:14"> and they spake unto Moses, saying, The people bring much more than enough for the service of the work which Jehovah commanded to make.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Moses gave commandment, and they caused it to be proclaimed throughout the camp, saying, Let neither man nor woman make any more work for the offering of the sanctuary. So the people were restrained from bringing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:10"> For the stuff they had was sufficient for all the work to make it, and too much.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 6:23; Exodus 26:1–37; Ezekiel 1:5–28"> And all the wise-hearted men among them that wrought the work made the tabernacle with ten curtains; of fine twined linen, and blue, and purple, and scarlet, with cherubim, the work of the skilful workman, Bezalel made them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The length of each curtain was eight and twenty cubits, and the breadth of each curtain four cubits: all the curtains had one measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; Exodus 26:3; Psalms 122:3"> And he coupled five curtains one to another: and the other five curtains he coupled one to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:4"> And he made loops of blue upon the edge of the one curtain from the selvedge in the coupling: likewise he made in the edge of the curtain that was outmost in the second coupling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 26:5; Exodus 26:10"> Fifty loops made he in the one curtain, and fifty loops made he in the edge of the curtain that was in the second coupling: the loops were opposite one to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 12:20"> And he made fifty clasps of gold, and coupled the curtains one to another with the clasps: so the tabernacle was one.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–13"> And he made curtains of goats’ hair for a tent over the tabernacle: eleven curtains he made them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The length of each curtain was thirty cubits, and four cubits the breadth of each curtain: the eleven curtains had one measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And he coupled five curtains by themselves, and six curtains by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he made fifty loops on the edge of the curtain that was outmost in the coupling, and fifty loops made he upon the edge of the curtain which was outmost in the second coupling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he made fifty clasps of brass to couple the tent together, that it might be one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:14"> And he made a covering for the tent of rams’ skins dyed red, and a covering of sealskins above.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 25:5; Numbers 25:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> And he made the boards for the tabernacle, of acacia wood, standing up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Ten cubits was the length of a board, and a cubit and a half the breadth of each board.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Each board had two tenons, joined one to another: thus did he make for all the boards of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And he made the boards for the tabernacle: twenty boards for the south side southward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and he made forty sockets of silver under the twenty boards; two sockets under one board for its two tenons, and two sockets under another board for its two tenons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And for the second side of the tabernacle, on the north side, he made twenty boards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and their forty sockets of silver; two sockets under one board, and two sockets under another board.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:22; Exodus 26:27"> And for the hinder part of the tabernacle westward he made six boards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And two boards made he for the corners of the tabernacle in the hinder part.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:32; Ephesians 3:18–19; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> And they were double beneath; and in like manner they were entire unto the top thereof unto one ring: thus he did to both of them in the two corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:25"> And there were eight boards, and their sockets of silver, sixteen sockets; under every board two sockets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 26:26–29; Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:5"> And he made bars of acacia wood; five for the boards of the one side of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 26:26"> and five bars for the boards of the other side of the tabernacle, and five bars for the boards of the tabernacle for the hinder part westward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And he made the middle bar to pass through in the midst of the boards from the one end to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And he overlaid the boards with gold, and made their rings of gold for places for the bars, and overlaid the bars with gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 10:20; Exodus 40:21"> And he made the veil of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine twined linen: with cherubim, the work of the skilful workman, made he it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18"> And he made thereunto four pillars of acacia, and overlaid them with gold: their hooks were of gold; and he cast for them four sockets of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 26:36–37; Exodus 40:28"> And he made a screen for the door of the Tent, of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine twined linen, the work of the embroiderer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 27:10"> and the five pillars of it with their hooks: and he overlaid their capitals and their fillets with gold; and their five sockets were of brass.</span>
</div>
