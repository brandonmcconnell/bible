<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 18:18; 1 Peter 1:22–23; 1 Timothy 5:9–10"> I commend unto you Phoebe our sister, who is a servant of the church that is at Cenchreæ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:29; Philippians 1:27; Matthew 10:40–42"> that ye receive her in the Lord, worthily of the saints, and that ye assist her in whatsoever matter she may have need of you: for she herself also hath been a helper of many, and of mine own self.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:19; 2 Timothy 4:19; Acts 18:26"> Salute Prisca and Aquila my fellow-workers in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 2:30; 1 John 3:16; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> who for my life laid down their own necks; unto whom not only I give thanks, but also all the churches of the Gentiles:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 4:15; Philemon 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:19"> and salute the church that is in their house. Salute Epænetus my beloved, who is the firstfruits of Asia unto Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 16:12; Matthew 27:55; 1 Timothy 5:10"> Salute Mary, who bestowed much labor on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 16:11; Romans 16:21; Philemon 1:23"> Salute Andronicus and Junias, my kinsmen, and my fellow-prisoners, who are of note among the apostles, who also have been in Christ before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:1; 1 John 3:14; Romans 16:5"> Salute Ampliatus my beloved in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 16:2–3; Romans 16:21"> Salute Urbanus our fellow-worker in Christ, and Stachys my beloved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:18; 1 Peter 1:7; Deuteronomy 8:2"> Salute Apelles the approved in Christ. Salute them that are of the household of Aristobulus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 16:21; Romans 16:7"> Salute Herodion my kinsman. Salute them of the household of Narcissus, that are in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Hebrews 6:10–11; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Salute Tryphæna and Tryphosa, who labor in the Lord. Salute Persis the beloved, who labored much in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:21; 1 Timothy 5:2; 2 John 1:1"> Salute Rufus the chosen in the Lord, and his mother and mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; Romans 8:29; Hebrews 3:1"> Salute Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brethren that are with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:2; Romans 1:7; Romans 16:14"> Salute Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas, and all the saints that are with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:26; 1 Corinthians 16:20; 2 Corinthians 13:12"> Salute one another with a holy kiss. All the churches of Christ salute you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jude 1:19; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Timothy 6:3–5"> Now I beseech you, brethren, mark them that are causing the divisions and occasions of stumbling, contrary to the doctrine which ye learned: and turn away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 2:4; Matthew 7:15; 1 Timothy 6:5"> For they that are such serve not our Lord Christ, but their own belly; and by their smooth and fair speech they beguile the hearts of the innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; 1 Corinthians 14:20; Philippians 2:15"> For your obedience is come abroad unto all men. I rejoice therefore over you: but I would have you wise unto that which is good, and simple unto that which is evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:19; 1 John 3:8; Genesis 3:15"> And the God of peace shall bruise Satan under your feet shortly.<br />The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Acts 17:5; Acts 13:1"> Timothy my fellow-worker saluteth you; and Lucius and Jason and Sosipater, my kinsmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Galatians 6:11; 1 Corinthians 16:21; Romans 16:8"> I Tertius, who write the epistle, salute you in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:14; Acts 19:22; 2 Timothy 4:20"> Gaius my host, and of the whole church, saluteth you. Erastus the treasurer of the city saluteth you, and Quartus the brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some ancient authorities insert here v. 24 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen, and omit the like words in v. 20.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:7; Romans 2:16; Colossians 1:26–27"> Now to him that is able to establish you according to my gospel and the preaching of Jesus Christ, according to the revelation of the mystery which hath been kept in silence through times eternal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 1:5; Galatians 3:8; Romans 15:18"> but now is manifested, and by the scriptures of the prophets, according to the commandment of the eternal God, is made known unto all the nations unto obedience of faith:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Romans 11:36; Revelation 7:10–12"> to the only wise God, through Jesus Christ, to whom be the glory for ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
