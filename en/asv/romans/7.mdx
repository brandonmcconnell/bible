<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:6; Romans 6:3"> Or are ye ignorant, brethren (for I speak to men who know the law), that the law hath dominion over a man for so long time as he liveth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 2:23–24; 1 Corinthians 7:39; 1 Corinthians 7:4"> For the woman that hath a husband is bound by law to the husband while he liveth; but if the husband die, she is discharged from the law of the husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; Matthew 5:32; Mark 10:6–12"> So then if, while the husband liveth, she be joined to another man, she shall be called an adulteress: but if the husband die, she is free from the law, so that she is no adulteress, though she be joined to another man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Galatians 5:22–23; Isaiah 54:5"> Wherefore, my brethren, ye also were made dead to the law through the body of Christ; that ye should be joined to another, even to him who was raised from the dead, that we might bring forth fruit unto God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:3; Galatians 5:16–17; Galatians 5:24"> For when we were in the flesh, the sinful passions, which were through the law, wrought in our members to bring forth fruit unto death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 3:23–25; 2 Corinthians 3:6; Romans 7:4"> But now we have been discharged from the law, having died to that wherein we were held; so that we serve in newness of the spirit, and not in oldness of the letter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:20; Romans 4:15; 1 Corinthians 15:56"> What shall we say then? Is the law sin? God forbid. Howbeit, I had not known sin, except through the law: for I had not known coveting, except the law had said, Thou shalt not covet:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:11; 1 Corinthians 15:56; Romans 4:15"> but sin, finding occasion, wrought in me through the commandment all manner of coveting: for apart from the law sin is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Romans 8:7; James 2:10–11"> And I was alive apart from the law once: but when the commandment came, sin revived, and I died;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:7; Romans 10:5; Leviticus 18:5"> and the commandment, which was unto life, this I found to be unto death:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 3:13; Romans 7:8; Hebrews 3:13"> for sin, finding occasion, through the commandment beguiled me, and through it slew me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:8; Psalms 119:137; Psalms 19:7–12"> So that the law is holy, and the commandment holy, and righteous, and good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 1:13–15; Galatians 3:21; Romans 8:3"> Did then that which is good become death unto me? God forbid. But sin, that it might be shown to be sin, by working death to me through that which is good;—that through the commandment sin might become exceeding sinful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 21:25; 2 Kings 17:17; Romans 7:18"> For we know that the law is spiritual: but I am carnal, sold under sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; Romans 7:19–20; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> For that which I do I know not: for not what I would, that do I practise; but what I hate, that I do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 7:12; Romans 7:14; 1 Timothy 1:8"> But if what I would not, that I do, I consent unto the law that it is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 7:20; Philippians 3:8–9; Romans 7:18"> So now it is no more I that do it, but sin which dwelleth in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:6; Psalms 51:5; Galatians 5:17"> For I know that in me, that is, in my flesh, dwelleth no good thing: for to will is present with me, but to do that which is good is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 7:15"> For the good which I would I do not: but the evil which I would not, that I practise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 7:17"> But if what I would not, that I do, it is no more I that do it, but sin which dwelleth in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:23; Psalms 119:133"> I find then the law, that, to me who would do good, evil is present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 1:2; Psalms 40:8; Psalms 119:35"> For I delight in the law of God after the inward man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; James 4:1; Romans 8:2"> but I see a different law in my members, warring against the law of my mind, and bringing me into captivity under the law of sin which is in my members.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 6:6; 2 Timothy 4:18; Titus 2:14"> Wretched man that I am! who shall deliver me out of the body of this death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Galatians 5:17–24; Colossians 3:17; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> I thank God through Jesus Christ our Lord. So then I of myself with the mind, indeed, serve the law of God; but with the flesh the law of sin.</span>
