<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:6; Isaiah 30:12; Isaiah 59:13"> Woe to her that is rebellious and polluted! to the oppressing city!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Jeremiah 22:21; Jeremiah 7:23–28"> She obeyed not the voice; she received not correction; she trusted not in Jehovah; she drew not near to her God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Ezekiel 22:25–27; Jeremiah 5:6"> Her princes in the midst of her are roaring lions; her judges are evening wolves; they leave nothing till the morrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:26; Hosea 9:7; Malachi 2:8"> Her prophets are light and treacherous persons; her priests have profaned the sanctuary, they have done violence to the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Psalms 145:17; Lamentations 3:23"> Jehovah in the midst of her is righteous; he will not do iniquity; every morning doth he bring his justice to light, he faileth not; but the unjust knoweth no shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Zephaniah 2:5; 1 Corinthians 10:11"> I have cut off nations; their battlements are desolate; I have made their streets waste, so that none passeth by; their cities are destroyed, so that there is no man, so that there is no inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Jeremiah 36:3; Micah 2:1–2"> I said, Only fear thou me; receive correction; so her dwelling shall not be cut off, according to all that I have appointed concerning her: but they rose early and corrupted all their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Zephaniah 1:18; Micah 7:7"> Therefore wait ye for me, saith Jehovah, until the day that I rise up to the prey; for my determination is to gather the nations, that I may assemble the kingdoms, to pour upon them mine indignation, even all my fierce anger; for all the earth shall be devoured with the fire of my jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 11:1; Psalms 22:27; Isaiah 19:18"> For then will I turn to the peoples of a pure language, that they may all call upon the name of Jehovah, to serve him with one consent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 11:11; Psalms 68:31; Isaiah 18:1"> From beyond the rivers of Ethiopia my suppliants, even the daughter of my dispersed, shall bring mine offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 54:4; Joel 2:26–27; Isaiah 11:9"> In that day shalt thou not be put to shame for all thy doings, wherein thou hast transgressed against me; for then I will take away out of the midst of thee thy proudly exulting ones, and thou shalt no more be haughty in my holy mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 14:32; Nahum 1:7; Zechariah 13:8–9"> But I will leave in the midst of thee an afflicted and poor people, and they shall take refuge in the name of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:5; Zephaniah 2:7; Micah 4:7"> The remnant of Israel shall not do iniquity, nor speak lies; neither shall a deceitful tongue be found in their mouth; for they shall feed and lie down, and none shall make them afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 12:6; Zechariah 2:10–11; Micah 4:8"> Sing, O daughter of Zion; shout, O Israel; be glad and rejoice with all the heart, O daughter of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 51:22; Zephaniah 3:5; Isaiah 33:22"> Jehovah hath taken away thy judgments, he hath cast out thine enemy: the King of Israel, even Jehovah, is in the midst of thee; thou shalt not fear evil any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:12; Isaiah 35:3–4; Isaiah 41:10"> In that day it shall be said to Jerusalem, Fear thou not; O Zion, let not thy hands be slack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 149:4; Psalms 147:11; Jeremiah 32:41"> Jehovah thy God is in the midst of thee, a mighty one who will save; he will rejoice over thee with joy; he will rest in his love; he will joy over thee with singing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 42:2–4; Ezekiel 36:24; Lamentations 2:6–7"> I will gather them that sorrow for the solemn assembly, who were of thee; to whom the burden upon her was a reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 49:25–26; Isaiah 60:14; Jeremiah 30:16"> Behold, at that time I will deal with all them that afflict thee; and I will save that which is lame, and gather that which was driven away; and I will make them a praise and a name, whose shame hath been in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14; Malachi 3:12; Isaiah 56:5"> At that time will I bring you in, and at that time will I gather you; for I will make you a name and a praise among all the peoples of the earth, when I bring back your captivity before your eyes, saith Jehovah.</span>
