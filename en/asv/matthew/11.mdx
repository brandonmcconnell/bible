<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 6:17; Matthew 14:3; Matthew 9:14"> Now when John heard in the prison the works of the Christ, he sent by his disciples</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:27; Malachi 3:1; Deuteronomy 18:15–18"> and said unto him, Art thou he that cometh, or look we for another?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And Jesus answered and said unto them, Go and tell John the things which ye hear and see:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4–6; Isaiah 61:1–3; Luke 4:18"> the blind receive their sight, and the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, and the deaf hear, and the dead are raised up, and the poor have good tidings preached to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 26:31"> And blessed is he, whosoever shall find no occasion of stumbling in me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:6; John 5:35; Mark 1:3–5"> And as these went their way, Jesus began to say unto the multitudes concerning John, What went ye out into the wilderness to behold? a reed shaken with the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; 2 Kings 1:8; Zechariah 13:4"> But what went ye out to see? a man clothed in soft raiment? Behold, they that wear soft raiment are in kings’ houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:76; Matthew 14:5; Matthew 21:24–26"> But wherefore went ye out? to see a prophet? Yea, I say unto you, and much more than a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 40:3; Malachi 4:5"> This is he, of whom it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Behold, I send my messenger before thy face,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Who shall prepare thy way before thee.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 7:28; Ephesians 3:8; Matthew 3:11"> Verily I say unto you, Among them that are born of women there hath not arisen a greater than John the Baptist: yet he that is but little in the kingdom of heaven is greater than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 16:16; Luke 13:24; Ephesians 6:11–13"> And from the days of John the Baptist until now the kingdom of heaven suffereth violence, and men of violence take it by force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:17–18; Romans 3:21; Acts 13:27"> For all the prophets and the law prophesied until John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Matthew 17:10–13; Mark 9:11–13"> And if ye are willing to receive it, this is Elijah, that is to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 8:8; Matthew 13:9; Mark 4:23"> He that hath ears to hear, let him hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 7:31–35; Matthew 12:34; Matthew 23:36"> But whereunto shall I liken this generation? It is like unto children sitting in the marketplaces, who call unto their fellows</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 28:9–13; Isaiah 30:29; Jeremiah 9:17–20"> and say, We piped unto you, and ye did not dance; we wailed, and ye did not mourn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Matthew 3:4; John 10:20"> For John came neither eating nor drinking, and they say, He hath a demon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 9:10–11; Luke 15:1–2; Luke 19:7"> The Son of man came eating and drinking, and they say, Behold, a gluttonous man and a winebibber, a friend of publicans and sinners! And wisdom is justified by her works.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:13–15; Jeremiah 8:6; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> Then began he to upbraid the cities wherein most of his mighty works were done, because they repented not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:6–7; Acts 12:20; Luke 9:10"> Woe unto thee, Chorazin! woe unto thee, Bethsaida! for if the mighty works had been done in Tyre and Sidon which were done in you, they would have repented long ago in sackcloth and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 11:24; Matthew 10:15; Hebrews 10:26–31"> But I say unto you, it shall be more tolerable for Tyre and Sidon in the day of judgment, than for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–15; John 4:46–54; Genesis 13:13"> And thou, Capernaum, shalt thou be exalted unto heaven? thou shalt go down unto Hades: for if the mighty works had been done in Sodom which were done in thee, it would have remained until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:15; Matthew 11:22; Mark 6:11"> But I say unto you that it shall be more tolerable for the land of Sodom in the day of judgment, than for thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 21:16; Psalms 8:2; Matthew 16:17"> At that season Jesus answered and said, I thank thee, O Father, Lord of heaven and earth, that thou didst hide these things from the wise and understanding, and didst reveal them unto babes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:11; Job 33:13"> yea, Father, for so it was well-pleasing in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:6–9; Matthew 28:18; Luke 10:22"> All things have been delivered unto me of my Father: and no one knoweth the Son, save the Father; neither doth any know the Father, save the Son, and he to whomsoever the Son willeth to reveal him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; John 7:37; John 6:37"> Come unto me, all ye that labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; 1 John 2:6; Philippians 2:7–8"> Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me; for I am meek and lowly in heart: and ye shall find rest unto your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 5:3; John 16:33; Philippians 4:13"> For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.</span>
</div>
