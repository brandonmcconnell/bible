<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 15:1; John 18:28; Micah 2:1"> Now when morning was come, all the chief priests and the elders of the people took counsel against Jesus to put him to death:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 20:19; Acts 3:13; 1 Timothy 6:13"> and they bound him, and led him away, and delivered him up to Pilate the governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; Luke 22:47–48; Job 20:15–29"> Then Judas, who betrayed him, when he saw that he was condemned, repented himself, and brought back the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and elders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:30; Titus 1:16; Acts 18:15–17"> saying, I have sinned in that I betrayed innocent blood. But they said, What is that to us? see thou to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 1:18–19; 2 Samuel 17:23; 1 Kings 16:18"> And he cast down the pieces of silver into the sanctuary, and departed; and he went away and hanged himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:7–9; Deuteronomy 23:18; Isaiah 61:8"> And the chief priests took the pieces of silver, and said, It is not lawful to put them into the treasury, since it is the price of blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And they took counsel, and bought with them the potter’s field, to bury strangers in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:19; Matthew 28:15; Joshua 4:9"> Wherefore that field was called, The field of blood, unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 1:22; Leviticus 27:2–7"> Then was fulfilled that which was spoken through Jeremiah the prophet, saying, And they took the thirty pieces of silver, the price of him that was priced, whom certain of the children of Israel did price;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 11:13"> and they gave them for the potter’s field, as the Lord appointed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 23:2–3; Mark 15:2–5; Matthew 26:25"> Now Jesus stood before the governor: and the governor asked him, saying, Art thou the King of the Jews? And Jesus said unto him, Thou sayest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:63; Isaiah 53:7; Matthew 27:14"> And when he was accused by the chief priests and elders, he answered nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:62; John 18:35; Acts 22:24"> Then saith Pilate unto him, Hearest thou not how many things they witness against thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; Psalms 71:7; 1 Corinthians 4:9"> And he gave him no answer, not even to one word: insomuch that the governor marvelled greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:38–40; Luke 23:16; John 19:16"> Now at the feast the governor was wont to release unto the multitude one prisoner, whom they would.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 15:7; Acts 3:14; Luke 23:25"> And they had then a notable prisoner, called Barabbas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 19:15; 1 Kings 18:21; Mark 15:9–12"> When therefore they were gathered together, Pilate said unto them, Whom will ye that I release unto you? Barabbas, or Jesus who is called Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Acts 5:17; Mark 15:10"> For he knew that for envy they had delivered him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Proverbs 29:1; Matthew 27:24"> And while he was sitting on the judgment-seat, his wife sent unto him, saying, Have thou nothing to do with that righteous man; for I have suffered many things this day in a dream because of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:14–15; John 19:15–16; John 18:40"> Now the chief priests and the elders persuaded the multitudes that they should ask for Barabbas, and destroy Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But the governor answered and said unto them, Which of the two will ye that I release unto you? And they said, Barabbas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–3; Isaiah 49:7; Acts 13:38"> Pilate saith unto them, What then shall I do unto Jesus who is called Christ? They all say, Let him be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:18–19; Acts 7:57; Acts 23:12–15"> And he said, Why, what evil hath he done? But they cried out exceedingly, saying, Let him be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:4; Psalms 26:6; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> So when Pilate saw that he prevailed nothing, but rather that a tumult was arising, he took water, and washed his hands before the multitude, saying, I am innocent of the blood of this righteous man; see ye to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 2:19; Acts 5:28; Acts 7:52"> And all the people answered and said, His blood be on us, and on our children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; John 19:1; Mark 15:15"> Then released he unto them Barabbas; but Jesus he scourged and delivered to be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 18:28; John 18:33; Acts 10:1"> Then the soldiers of the governor took Jesus into the Prætorium, and gathered unto him the whole band.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 15:17; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> And they stripped him, and put on him a scarlet robe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 20:19; Psalms 69:19–20"> And they platted a crown of thorns and put it upon his head, and a reed in his right hand; and they kneeled down before him, and mocked him, saying, Hail, King of the Jews!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 53:7"> And they spat upon him, and took the reed and smote him on the head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 Kings 21:13; 1 Kings 21:10"> And when they had mocked him, they took off from him the robe, and put on him his garments, and led him away to crucify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:21; Matthew 16:24; Luke 23:26"> And as they came out, they found a man of Cyrene, Simon by name: him they compelled to go with them, that he might bear his cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 19:17–19; Mark 15:22–32; Mark 15:34"> And when they were come unto a place called Golgotha, that is to say, The place of a skull,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Mark 15:23; Matthew 27:48"> they gave him wine to drink mingled with gall: and when he had tasted it, he would not drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 22:18; Mark 15:24–32; Luke 23:34"> And when they had crucified him, they parted his garments among them, casting lots;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:44; Mark 15:39"> and they sat and watched him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 23:38; John 19:19–22; Mark 15:26"> And they set up over his head his accusation written, THIS IS JESUS THE KING OF THE JEWS.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43; Isaiah 53:12"> Then are there crucified with him two robbers, one on the right hand and one on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 109:25; Lamentations 1:12; Psalms 69:7–12"> And they that passed by railed on him, wagging their heads,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 26:61; Matthew 4:6; Matthew 4:3"> and saying, Thou that destroyest the temple, and buildest it in three days, save thyself: if thou art the Son of God, come down from the cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Isaiah 28:22; Psalms 22:12–13"> In like manner also the chief priests mocking him, with the scribes and elders, said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 1:49; Matthew 27:37; Acts 4:14"> He saved others; himself he cannot save. He is the King of Israel; let him now come down from the cross, and we will believe on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 22:8; John 10:36; Isaiah 37:10"> He trusteth on God; let him deliver him now, if he desireth him: for he said, I am the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:38; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:39–43"> And the robbers also that were crucified with him cast upon him the same reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Amos 8:9; Mark 15:33–34; Luke 23:44–45"> Now from the sixth hour there was darkness over all the land until the ninth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 53:10"> And about the ninth hour Jesus cried with a loud voice, saying, Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani? that is, My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Mark 15:35–36; Matthew 11:14"> And some of them that stood there, when they heard it, said, This man calleth Elijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36"> And straightway one of them ran, and took a sponge, and filled it with vinegar, and put it on a reed, and gave him to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> And the rest said, Let be; let us see whether Elijah cometh to save him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:30; Isaiah 53:9–12; Psalms 22:14–15"> And Jesus cried again with a loud voice, and yielded up his spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Mark 15:38; Hebrews 6:19"> And behold, the veil of the temple was rent in two from the top to the bottom; and the earth did quake; and the rocks were rent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; 1 Thessalonians 4:14; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> and the tombs were opened; and many bodies of the saints that had fallen asleep were raised;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Isaiah 48:2; Revelation 21:2"> and coming forth out of the tombs after his resurrection they entered into the holy city and appeared unto many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:36; Mark 15:39; Matthew 27:43"> Now the centurion, and they that were with him watching Jesus, when they saw the earthquake, and the things that were done, feared exceedingly, saying, Truly this was the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Luke 23:48–49; John 19:25–27"> And many women were there beholding from afar, who had followed Jesus from Galilee, ministering unto him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 19:25; Mark 15:47–1; Matthew 28:1"> among whom was Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James and Joses, and the mother of the sons of Zebedee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Mark 15:42–47; Luke 23:50–56"> And when even was come, there came a rich man from Arimathæa, named Joseph, who also himself was Jesus’ disciple:</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 23:52–53; Mark 15:44–46"> this man went to Pilate, and asked for the body of Jesus. Then Pilate commanded it to be given up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> And Joseph took the body, and wrapped it in a clean linen cloth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 28:2; Matthew 27:66"> and laid it in his own new tomb, which he had hewn out in the rock: and he rolled a great stone to the door of the tomb, and departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Matthew 28:1; Matthew 27:56"> And Mary Magdalene was there, and the other Mary, sitting over against the sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 15:42; John 19:14; John 19:42"> Now on the morrow, which is the day after the Preparation, the chief priests and the Pharisees were gathered together unto Pilate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 9:22; Matthew 16:21; John 2:19"> saying, Sir, we remember that that deceiver said while he was yet alive, After three days I rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Matthew 28:13; Matthew 12:45"> Command therefore that the sepulchre be made sure until the third day, lest haply his disciples come and steal him away, and say unto the people, He is risen from the dead: and the last error will be worse than the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Matthew 27:66; Matthew 28:11–15; Proverbs 21:30"> Pilate said unto them, Ye have a guard: go, make it as sure as ye can.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Daniel 6:17; Matthew 27:60; 2 Timothy 2:19"> So they went, and made the sepulchre sure, sealing the stone, the guard being with them.</span>
