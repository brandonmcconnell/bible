<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:1; Luke 20:45–46; Matthew 15:10–20"> Then spake Jesus to the multitudes and to his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 20:46; Mark 12:38; Malachi 2:7"> saying, The scribes and the Pharisees sit on Moses’ seat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:16; Romans 2:19–24"> all things therefore whatsoever they bid you, these do and observe: but do not ye after their works; for they say, and do not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 11:46; Acts 15:10; Galatians 6:13"> Yea, they bind heavy burdens and grievous to be borne, and lay them on men’s shoulders; but they themselves will not move them with their finger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 6:1–16; John 12:43; Philippians 2:3"> But all their works they do to be seen of men: for they make broad their phylacteries, and enlarge the borders of their garments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 John 1:9; Luke 11:43–54; Mark 12:38–39"> and love the chief place at feasts, and the chief seats in the synagogues,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 1:38; John 1:49; John 3:26"> and the salutations in the marketplaces, and to be called of men, Rabbi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 3:1; 1 Corinthians 1:12–13; 1 Peter 5:3"> But be not ye called Rabbi: for one is your teacher, and all ye are brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:1; Hebrews 12:9; Malachi 1:6"> And call no man your father on the earth: for one is your Father, even he who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Neither be ye called masters: for one is your master, even the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:19; Galatians 5:13; John 13:14–15"> But he that is greatest among you shall be your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; James 4:6; Proverbs 29:23"> And whosoever shall exalt himself shall be humbled; and whosoever shall humble himself shall be exalted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Matthew 23:29; Matthew 23:23"> But woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! because ye shut the kingdom of heaven against men: for ye enter not in yourselves, neither suffer ye them that are entering in to enter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some authorities insert here, or after v. 12, v. 14 Woe unto you scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for you devour widows’ houses, even while for a pretence ye make long prayes: therefore ye shall receive greater condemnation. See Mrk 12:40; Luk 20:47.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 2:3; Acts 13:10; Acts 13:43"> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye compass sea and land to make one proselyte; and when he is become so, ye make him twofold more a son of hell than yourselves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:14; Matthew 5:33–35; James 5:12"> Woe unto you, ye blind guides, that say, Whosoever shall swear by the temple, it is nothing; but whosoever shall swear by the gold of the temple, he is a debtor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Numbers 16:38–39; Psalms 94:8"> Ye fools and blind: for which is greater, the gold, or the temple that hath sanctified the gold?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And, Whosoever shall swear by the altar, it is nothing; but whosoever shall swear by the gift that is upon it, he is a debtor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:29"> Ye blind: for which is greater, the gift, or the altar that sanctifieth the gift?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> He therefore that sweareth by the altar, sweareth by it, and by all things thereon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; 1 Kings 8:13; 2 Chronicles 6:2"> And he that sweareth by the temple, sweareth by it, and by him that dwelleth therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 5:34; Psalms 11:4; Isaiah 66:1"> And he that sweareth by the heaven, sweareth by the throne of God, and by him that sitteth thereon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Micah 6:8; Hosea 6:6"> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye tithe mint and anise and cummin, and have left undone the weightier matters of the law, justice, and mercy, and faith: but these ye ought to have done, and not to have left the other undone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 19:24; Matthew 27:6–8; Matthew 15:2–6"> Ye blind guides, that strain out the gnat, and swallow the camel!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:39–40; Matthew 15:19–20; Mark 7:4–13"> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye cleanse the outside of the cup and of the platter, but within they are full from extortion and excess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; James 4:8; Hebrews 10:22"> Thou blind Pharisee, cleanse first the inside of the cup and of the platter, that the outside thereof may become clean also.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 11:44; Acts 23:3; Numbers 19:16"> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye are like unto whited sepulchres, which outwardly appear beautiful, but inwardly are full of dead men’s bones, and of all uncleanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Psalms 51:6; Matthew 23:5"> Even so ye also outwardly appear righteous unto men, but inwardly ye are full of hypocrisy and iniquity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 11:47–48; Acts 2:29"> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye build the sepulchres of the prophets, and garnish the tombs of the righteous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:30; Matthew 23:34–35; Matthew 21:35–36"> and say, If we had been in the days of our fathers, we should not have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 7:51–52; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Joshua 24:22"> Wherefore ye witness to yourselves, that ye are sons of them that slew the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 15:16; Numbers 32:14; 1 Thessalonians 2:16"> Fill ye up then the measure of your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:7; Matthew 12:34; Matthew 5:22"> Ye serpents, ye offspring of vipers, how shall ye escape the judgment of hell?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 10:16–17; Matthew 13:52; Colossians 1:28"> Therefore, behold, I send unto you prophets, and wise men, and scribes: some of them shall ye kill and crucify; and some of them shall ye scourge in your synagogues, and persecute from city to city:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20–22; Zechariah 1:1; Hebrews 11:4"> that upon you may come all the righteous blood shed on the earth, from the blood of Abel the righteous unto the blood of Zachariah son of Barachiah, whom ye slew between the sanctuary and the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Matthew 24:34; Mark 13:30–31"> Verily I say unto you, All these things shall come upon this generation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 13:34–35; Ruth 2:12; Psalms 91:4"> O Jerusalem, Jerusalem, that killeth the prophets, and stoneth them that are sent unto her! how often would I have gathered thy children together, even as a hen gathereth her chickens under her wings, and ye would not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:5; Luke 13:35; Luke 21:24"> Behold, your house is left unto you desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 118:26; Matthew 21:9; Romans 11:25"> For I say unto you, Ye shall not see me henceforth, till ye shall say, Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord.</span>
</div>
