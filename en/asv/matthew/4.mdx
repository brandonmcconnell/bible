<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 2:18; Luke 4:1–13; Mark 1:12–15"> Then was Jesus led up of the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted of the devil.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:1–5; 1 Thessalonians 3:5; Numbers 11:4–6"> And the tempter came and said unto him, If thou art the Son of God, command that these stones become bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Luke 4:4; John 6:63"> But he answered and said, It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 4:9; Matthew 27:53; Revelation 11:2"> Then the devil taketh him into the holy city; and he set him on the pinnacle of the temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 91:11–12; Hebrews 1:14; Psalms 34:7"> and saith unto him, If thou art the Son of God, cast thyself down: for it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">He shall give his angels charge concerning thee:</span>and,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">On their hands they shall bear thee up,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Lest haply thou dash thy foot against a stone.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; 1 Corinthians 10:9; Psalms 78:18"> Jesus said unto him, Again it is written, Thou shalt not make trial of the Lord thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Revelation 11:15; Psalms 49:16–17"> Again, the devil taketh him unto an exceeding high mountain, and showeth him all the kingdoms of the world, and the glory of them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; Daniel 5:18–19; John 16:11"> and he said unto him, All these things will I give thee, if thou wilt fall down and worship me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13–14; Deuteronomy 10:20; Joshua 24:14"> Then saith Jesus unto him, Get thee hence, Satan: for it is written, Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 26:53; Hebrews 1:14; Luke 22:43"> Then the devil leaveth him; and behold, angels came and ministered unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 1:14; Luke 3:20; Matthew 14:3"> Now when he heard that John was delivered up, he withdrew into Galilee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 4:46; Mark 1:21; Matthew 11:23"> and leaving Nazareth, he came and dwelt in Capernaum, which is by the sea, in the borders of Zebulun and Naphtali:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 9:1–2; Matthew 2:23; Luke 24:44"> that it might be fulfilled which was spoken through Isaiah the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:1; 2 Kings 15:29; Joshua 21:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">The land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Toward the sea, beyond the Jordan,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Galilee of the Gentiles,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 9:2; Isaiah 42:6–7; Isaiah 60:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people that sat in darkness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Saw a great light,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And to them that sat in the region and shadow of death,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">To them did light spring up.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:38; Mark 1:14–15"> From that time began Jesus to preach, and to say, Repent ye; for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 1:40–42; Matthew 10:2; Luke 6:14"> And walking by the sea of Galilee, he saw two brethren, Simon who is called Peter, and Andrew his brother, casting a net into the sea; for they were fishers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 1:17–18; John 12:26; Matthew 16:24"> And he saith unto them, Come ye after me, and I will make you fishers of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 10:28–31; Matthew 19:27; Luke 18:28–30"> And they straightway left the nets, and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 1:19–20; Matthew 10:2; Mark 3:17"> And going on from thence he saw two other brethren, James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, in the boat with Zebedee their father, mending their nets; and he called them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Luke 9:59–60; Deuteronomy 33:9–10"> And they straightway left the boat and their father, and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 9:35; Mark 1:39; Acts 10:38"> And Jesus went about in all Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, and preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing all manner of disease and all manner of sickness among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 8:6; Matthew 17:15; Luke 5:15"> And the report of him went forth into all Syria: and they brought unto him all that were sick, holden with divers diseases and torments, possessed with demons, and epileptic, and palsied; and he healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 6:17; Mark 5:20; Mark 3:7–8"> And there followed him great multitudes from Galilee and Decapolis and Jerusalem and Judæa and from beyond the Jordan.</span>
