<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 6:37; Romans 2:1–2; James 4:11–12"> Judge not, that ye be not judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 2:13; Mark 4:24; Luke 6:38"> For with what judgment ye judge, ye shall be judged: and with what measure ye mete, it shall be measured unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 8:7–9; Galatians 6:1; Luke 6:41–42"> And why beholdest thou the mote that is in thy brother’s eye, but considerest not the beam that is in thine own eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Or how wilt thou say to thy brother, Let me cast out the mote out of thine eye; and lo, the beam is in thine own eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:42; Psalms 51:9–13; Matthew 23:13–28"> Thou hypocrite, cast out first the beam out of thine own eye; and then shalt thou see clearly to cast out the mote out of thy brother’s eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 9:7–8; Proverbs 23:9; Matthew 10:14–15"> Give not that which is holy unto the dogs, neither cast your pearls before the swine, lest haply they trample them under their feet, and turn and rend you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; 1 John 3:22; John 15:7"> Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 81:10; Psalms 81:16; Luke 23:42–43"> for every one that asketh receiveth; and he that seeketh findeth; and to him that knocketh it shall be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13"> Or what man is there of you, who, if his son shall ask him for a loaf, will give him a stone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> or if he shall ask for a fish, will give him a serpent?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13; James 1:17; Romans 8:32"> If ye then, being evil, know how to give good gifts unto your children, how much more shall your Father who is in heaven give good things to them that ask him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:31; Romans 13:8–10; Galatians 5:13–14"> All things therefore whatsoever ye would that men should do unto you, even so do ye also unto them: for this is the law and the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 13:24–25; Proverbs 16:25; John 14:6"> Enter ye in by the narrow gate: for wide is the gate, and broad is the way, that leadeth to destruction, and many are they that enter in thereby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 13:23–30; Matthew 22:14; Matthew 16:24–25"> For narrow is the gate, and straitened the way, that leadeth unto life, and few are they that find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 4:1; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Matthew 24:11"> Beware of false prophets, who come to you in sheep’s clothing, but inwardly are ravening wolves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:43–45; Matthew 12:33; James 3:12"> By their fruits ye shall know them. Do men gather grapes of thorns, or figs of thistles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 12:33–35; Galatians 5:22–24; Ephesians 5:9"> Even so every good tree bringeth forth good fruit; but the corrupt tree bringeth forth evil fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; 1 John 3:9–10"> A good tree cannot bring forth evil fruit, neither can a corrupt tree bring forth good fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 3:10; Hebrews 6:8; John 15:2–6"> Every tree that bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down, and cast into the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 7:16; Acts 5:38"> Therefore by their fruits ye shall know them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:13; Luke 6:46; James 1:22"> Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 13:25–27; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Malachi 3:17–18"> Many will say to me in that day, Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy by thy name, and by thy name cast out demons, and by thy name do many mighty works?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Psalms 6:8; Luke 13:25"> And then will I profess unto them, I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 6:47–49; John 13:17; 1 Corinthians 3:10–11"> Every one therefore that heareth these words of mine, and doeth them, shall be likened unto a wise man, who built his house upon the rock:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 1:12; Psalms 125:1–2; 1 Peter 1:7"> and the rain descended, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house; and it fell not: for it was founded upon the rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:9; Luke 6:49; James 2:20"> And every one that heareth these words of mine, and doeth them not, shall be likened unto a foolish man, who built his house upon the sand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 13:19–22; 2 Peter 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> and the rain descended, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and smote upon that house; and it fell: and great was the fall thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 4:32; Mark 1:22; John 7:46"> And it came to pass, when Jesus had finished these words, the multitudes were astonished at his teaching:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12–13; Luke 20:8; Deuteronomy 18:18–19"> for he taught them as one having authority, and not as their scribes.</span>
