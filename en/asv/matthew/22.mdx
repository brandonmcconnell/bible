<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 20:1–16; Matthew 12:43–45; Luke 14:16"> And Jesus answered and spake again in parables unto them, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–9; Matthew 13:24; Ephesians 5:24–32"> The kingdom of heaven is likened unto a certain king, who made a marriage feast for his son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; John 5:40; Luke 14:15–17"> and sent forth his servants to call them that were bidden to the marriage feast: and they would not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:36; John 6:50–57"> Again he sent forth other servants, saying, Tell them that are bidden, Behold, I have made ready my dinner; my oxen and my fatlings are killed, and all things are ready: come to the marriage feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:3; Luke 14:18–20; 1 John 2:15–16"> But they made light of it, and went their ways, one to his own farm, another to his merchandise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:19–20; John 16:2–3; Acts 7:51–57"> and the rest laid hold on his servants, and treated them shamefully, and killed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 19:27; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Luke 21:24"> But the king was wroth; and he sent his armies, and destroyed those murderers, and burned their city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; Acts 13:46; Revelation 3:4"> Then saith he to his servants, The wedding is ready, but they that were bidden were not worthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 14:21–24; Proverbs 1:20–23; Mark 16:15–16"> Go ye therefore unto the partings of the highways, and as many as ye shall find, bid to the marriage feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Revelation 19:6–9; 2 Corinthians 12:21"> And those servants went out into the highways, and gathered together all as many as they found, both bad and good: and the wedding was filled with guests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 19:8; Hebrews 4:12–13"> But when the king came in to behold the guests, he saw there a man who had not on a wedding-garment:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:9; Job 5:16; Matthew 20:13"> and he saith unto him, Friend, how camest thou in hither not having a wedding-garment? And he was speechless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 25:30; Matthew 24:51"> Then the king said to the servants, Bind him hand and foot, and cast him out into the outer darkness; there shall be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Luke 13:23–24; Matthew 7:13–14"> For many are called, but few chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Mark 12:13–17; Psalms 2:2"> Then went the Pharisees, and took counsel how they might ensnare him in his talk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 3:6; Mark 8:15; Mark 12:14"> And they send to him their disciples, with the Herodians, saying, Teacher, we know that thou art true, and teachest the way of God in truth, and carest not for any one: for thou regardest not the person of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 2:1; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 17:25"> Tell us therefore, What thinkest thou? Is it lawful to give tribute unto Cæsar, or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:6; Luke 5:22; John 2:25"> But Jesus perceived their wickedness, and said, Why make ye trial of me, ye hypocrites?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 20:2; Revelation 6:6; Matthew 18:28"> Show me the tribute money. And they brought unto him a denarius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 20:24"> And he saith unto them, Whose is this image and superscription?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:7; Proverbs 24:21; Acts 4:19"> They say unto him, Cæsar’s. Then saith he unto them, Render therefore unto Cæsar the things that are Cæsar’s; and unto God the things that are God’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 12:12; Matthew 22:46; Luke 20:25–26"> And when they heard it, they marvelled, and left him, and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Mark 12:18–27; Acts 23:6–8"> On that day there came to him Sadducees, they that say that there is no resurrection: and they asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Luke 20:28; Ruth 1:11"> saying, Teacher, Moses said, If a man die, having no children, his brother shall marry his wife, and raise up seed unto his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:29–33; Mark 12:19–23; Hebrews 9:27"> Now there were with us seven brethren: and the first married and deceased, and having no seed left his wife unto his brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> in like manner the second also, and the third, unto the seventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And after them all, the woman died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> In the resurrection therefore whose wife shall she be of the seven? for they all had her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 20:9; Romans 15:4; Luke 1:37"> But Jesus answered and said unto them, Ye do err, not knowing the scriptures, nor the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 20:34–36; Mark 12:24–25; Matthew 24:38"> For in the resurrection they neither marry, nor are given in marriage, but are as angels in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 12:3; Matthew 21:16; Matthew 21:42"> But as touching the resurrection of the dead, have ye not read that which was spoken unto you by God, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Mark 12:26–27; Luke 20:37–38"> I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob? God is not the God of the dead, but of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 12:17; Mark 6:2; John 7:46"> And when the multitudes heard it, they were astonished at his teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 12:28–31; Matthew 25:3–5; Matthew 12:14"> But the Pharisees, when they heard that he had put the Sadducees to silence, gathered themselves together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 7:30; Luke 10:25–37; Luke 11:52"> And one of them, a lawyer, asked him a question, trying him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 12:28–33; Matthew 5:19–20; Luke 11:42"> Teacher, which is the great commandment in the law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 12:29–30; Luke 10:27; Deuteronomy 6:5"> And he said unto him, Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> This is the great and first commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 12:31; Luke 10:27–37; Leviticus 19:18"> And a second like unto it is this, Thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 7:12; 1 John 4:19–21"> On these two commandments the whole law hangeth, and the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 22:34"> Now while the Pharisees were gathered together, Jesus asked them a question,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; John 6:68–69; Matthew 16:13–17"> saying, What think ye of the Christ? whose son is he? They say unto him, The son of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:2; Revelation 4:2; Mark 12:36"> He saith unto them, How then doth David in the Spirit call him Lord, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Acts 2:34–35"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord said unto my Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Sit thou on my right hand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Till I put thine enemies underneath thy feet?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Revelation 22:16; John 8:58"> If David then calleth him Lord, how is he his son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Mark 12:34; Luke 20:40; Luke 14:6"> And no one was able to answer him a word, neither durst any man from that day forth ask him any more questions.</span>
