<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:7; Romans 14:13; 1 Corinthians 11:19"> And he said unto his disciples, It is impossible but that occasions of stumbling should come; but woe unto him, through whom they come!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 9:42; Matthew 18:14; Matthew 18:3–6"> It were well for him if a millstone were hanged about his neck, and he were thrown into the sea, rather than that he should cause one of these little ones to stumble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 19:17; Matthew 18:21; Matthew 18:15–17"> Take heed to yourselves: if thy brother sin, rebuke him; and if he repent, forgive him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; Matthew 18:21–22; Romans 12:20"> And if he sin against thee seven times in the day, and seven times turn again to thee, saying, I repent; thou shalt forgive him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Philippians 4:13"> And the apostles said unto the Lord, Increase our faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 17:20; Matthew 21:21; Mark 9:23"> And the Lord said, If ye had faith as a grain of mustard seed, ye would say unto this sycamine tree, Be thou rooted up, and be thou planted in the sea; and it would obey you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:11; Luke 13:15; Luke 14:5"> But who is there of you, having a servant plowing or keeping sheep, that will say unto him, when he is come in from the field, Come straightway and sit down to meat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 12:37; Genesis 43:16; 2 Samuel 12:20"> and will not rather say unto him, Make ready wherewith I may sup, and gird thyself, and serve me, till I have eaten and drunken; and afterward thou shalt eat and drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Doth he thank the servant because he did the things that were commanded?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:16–17; Job 22:2–3; 1 Peter 5:5–6"> Even so ye also, when ye shall have done all the things that are commanded you, say, We are unprofitable servants; we have done that which it was our duty to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 9:51–52; John 4:3–4"> And it came to pass, as they were on the way to Jerusalem, that he was passing along the borders of Samaria and Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46; Luke 5:12; 2 Kings 7:3"> And as he entered into a certain village, there met him ten men that were lepers, who stood afar off:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Matthew 20:30–31; Matthew 9:27"> and they lifted up their voices, saying, Jesus, Master, have mercy on us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:14; Leviticus 13:1–46; 2 Kings 5:14"> And when he saw them, he said unto them, Go and show yourselves unto the priests. And it came to pass, as they went, they were cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 30:11–12; Psalms 103:1–4; Psalms 118:18–19"> And one of them, when he saw that he was healed, turned back, with a loud voice glorifying God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 8:5–25; Matthew 10:5; Revelation 19:10"> and he fell upon his face at his feet, giving him thanks: and he was a Samaritan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:21; Psalms 106:13; Genesis 3:9"> And Jesus answering said, Were not the ten cleansed? but where are the nine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 50:23; Matthew 15:24–28; Psalms 106:13"> Were there none found that returned to give glory to God, save this stranger?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Luke 8:48; Mark 10:52"> And he said unto him, Arise, and go thy way: thy faith hath made thee whole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 18:36; Luke 19:11; Daniel 2:44"> And being asked by the Pharisees, when the kingdom of God cometh, he answered them and said, The kingdom of God cometh not with observation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 12:28; Romans 14:17; Mark 13:21"> neither shall they say, Lo, here! or, There! for lo, the kingdom of God is within you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 5:35; Matthew 9:15; John 8:21–24"> And he said unto the disciples, The days will come, when ye shall desire to see one of the days of the Son of man, and ye shall not see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Matthew 24:23–26; Luke 17:21"> And they shall say to you, Lo, there! Lo, here! go not away, nor follow after them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 24:27; Matthew 24:30; Malachi 4:1–2"> for as the lightning, when it lighteneth out of the one part under the heaven, shineth unto the other part under heaven; so shall the Son of man be in his day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Luke 9:22; Mark 8:31"> But first must he suffer many things and be rejected of this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 24:37–39; Luke 17:24; Genesis 7:7–23"> And as it came to pass in the days of Noah, even so shall it be also in the days of the Son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 12:19–20; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–3; Luke 16:19–23"> They ate, they drank, they married, they were given in marriage, until the day that Noah entered into the ark, and the flood came, and destroyed them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:49–50; Genesis 18:20–21; James 5:1–5"> Likewise even as it came to pass in the days of Lot; they ate, they drank, they bought, they sold, they planted, they builded;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6; Zephaniah 2:9; Jude 1:7"> but in the day that Lot went out from Sodom it rained fire and brimstone from heaven, and destroyed them all:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:7; Revelation 1:7"> after the same manner shall it be in the day that the Son of man is revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 21:21; Mark 13:14–16; Jeremiah 45:5"> In that day, he that shall be on the housetop, and his goods in the house, let him not go down to take them away: and let him that is in the field likewise not return back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 19:26; Hebrews 10:38–39; 2 Peter 2:18–22"> Remember Lot’s wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:35–37; Matthew 10:39; John 12:25"> Whosoever shall seek to gain his life shall lose it: but whosoever shall lose his life shall preserve it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 24:40–41; 2 Peter 2:9; Malachi 3:16–18"> I say unto you, In that night there shall be two men on one bed; the one shall be taken, and the other shall be left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:41; Exodus 11:5; Judges 16:21"> There shall be two women grinding together; the one shall be taken, and the other shall be left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some ancient authorities add v. 36. There shall be two men in the field; the one shall be taken and the other shall be left. Mat 24:40">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 24:28; Job 39:29–30; Revelation 19:17–18"> And they answering say unto him, Where, Lord? And he said unto them, Where the body is, thither will the eagles also be gathered together.</span>
