<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:26; Proverbs 13:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> For in many things we all stumble. If any stumbleth not in word, the same is a perfect man, able to bridle the whole body also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 32:9; Psalms 39:1; James 1:26"> Now if we put the horses’ bridles into their mouths that they may obey us, we turn about their whole body also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 107:25–27; Matthew 8:24; Acts 27:14–38"> Behold, the ships also, though they are so great and are driven by rough winds, are yet turned about by a very small rudder, whither the impulse of the steersman willeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:21; Jeremiah 9:3–8; Proverbs 12:18"> So the tongue also is a little member, and boasteth great things. Behold, how much wood is kindled by how small a fire!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 120:2–3; Proverbs 16:27; Mark 7:15"> And the tongue is a fire: the world of iniquity among our members is the tongue, which defileth the whole body, and setteth on fire the wheel of nature, and is set on fire by hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 5:4"> For every kind of beasts and birds, of creeping things and things in the sea, is tamed, and hath been tamed by mankind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalms 140:3; Psalms 55:21"> but the tongue can no man tame; it is a restless evil, it is full of deadly poison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Genesis 1:26–27; Psalms 62:4"> Therewith bless we the Lord and Father; and therewith curse we men, who are made after the likeness of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:14; 1 Peter 3:9; 1 Corinthians 3:3"> out of the same mouth cometh forth blessing and cursing. My brethren, these things ought not so to be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Doth the fountain send forth from the same opening sweet water and bitter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:43–44; Matthew 12:33; Matthew 7:16–20"> can a fig tree, my brethren, yield olives, or a vine figs? neither can salt water yield sweet.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; James 3:17; Matthew 5:5"> Who is wise and understanding among you? let him show by his good life his works in meekness of wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 3:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4; Philippians 2:3"> But if ye have bitter jealousy and faction in your heart, glory not and lie not against the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> This wisdom is not a wisdom that cometh down from above, but is earthly, sensual, devilish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 3:14; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> For where jealousy and faction are, there is confusion and every vile deed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 5:22–23; Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, easy to be entreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without variance, without hypocrisy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Hosea 10:12; Philippians 1:11"> And the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace for them that make peace.</span>
