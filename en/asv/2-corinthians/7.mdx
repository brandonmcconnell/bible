<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:7; 1 Peter 2:11; Proverbs 8:13"> Having therefore these promises, beloved, let us cleanse ourselves from all defilement of flesh and spirit, perfecting holiness in the fear of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 20:33; 2 Corinthians 6:12–13; 2 Corinthians 11:9"> Open your hearts to us: we wronged no man, we corrupted no man, we took advantage of no man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:11–12; 1 Thessalonians 2:8; 2 Corinthians 11:11"> I say it not to condemn you: for I have said before, that ye are in our hearts to die together and live together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; 2 Corinthians 3:12; James 1:2"> Great is my boldness of speech toward you, great is my glorying on your behalf: I am filled with comfort, I overflow with joy in all our affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:13; Deuteronomy 32:25; Acts 20:1"> For even when we were come into Macedonia our flesh had no relief, but we were afflicted on every side; without were fightings, within were fears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 2:16–17; 2 Corinthians 7:13"> Nevertheless he that comforteth the lowly, even God, comforted us by the coming of Titus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:2; Jude 1:3; 2 Corinthians 8:16"> and not by his coming only, but also by the comfort wherewith he was comforted in you, while he told us your longing, your mourning, your zeal for me; so that I rejoiced yet more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Jeremiah 20:7–9; Matthew 26:21–22"> For though I made you sorry with my epistle, I do not regret it: though I did regret it (for I see that that epistle made you sorry, though but for a season),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 15:7; Jeremiah 31:18–20; 2 Corinthians 7:10–11"> I now rejoice, not that ye were made sorry, but that ye were made sorry unto repentance; for ye were made sorry after a godly sort, that ye might suffer loss by us in nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Acts 11:18; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> For godly sorrow worketh repentance unto salvation, a repentance which bringeth no regret: but the sorrow of the world worketh death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 14:18; 1 Timothy 5:21–22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> For behold, this selfsame thing, that ye were made sorry after a godly sort, what earnest care it wrought in you, yea what clearing of yourselves, yea what indignation, yea what fear, yea what longing, yea what zeal, yea what avenging! In everything ye approved yourselves to be pure in the matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:9; 1 Timothy 3:5; 2 Corinthians 2:17"> So although I wrote unto you, I wrote not for his cause that did the wrong, nor for his cause that suffered the wrong, but that your earnest care for us might be made manifest unto you in the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:6; 1 Corinthians 12:26; 1 Corinthians 16:13"> Therefore we have been comforted: and in our comfort we joyed the more exceedingly for the joy of Titus, because his spirit hath been refreshed by you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:4; 2 Thessalonians 1:4; 2 Corinthians 1:18–20"> For if in anything I have gloried to him on your behalf, I was not put to shame; but as we spake all things to you in truth, so our glorying also which I made before Titus was found to be truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; 2 Corinthians 2:9; 1 John 3:17"> And his affection is more abundantly toward you, while he remembereth the obedience of you all, how with fear and trembling ye received him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:3; Philemon 1:8; Philemon 1:21"> I rejoice that in everything I am of good courage concerning you.</span>
