<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 16:9; 2 Corinthians 9:2; 2 Corinthians 9:4"> Moreover, brethren, we make known to you the grace of God which hath been given in the churches of Macedonia;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 2:5; Proverbs 11:25; 2 Corinthians 6:10"> how that in much proof of affliction the abundance of their joy and their deep poverty abounded unto the riches of their liberality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:5; Exodus 35:21–22"> For according to their power, I bear witness, yea and beyond their power, they gave of their own accord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> beseeching us with much entreaty in regard of this grace and the fellowship in the ministering to the saints:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 12:1; Romans 14:7–9; 2 Chronicles 30:12"> and this, not as we had hoped, but first they gave their own selves to the Lord, and to us through the will of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 2 Corinthians 8:4"> Insomuch that we exhorted Titus, that as he had made a beginning before, so he would also complete in you this grace also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8; 2 Peter 1:5–8; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> But as ye abound in everything, in faith, and utterance, and knowledge, and in all earnestness, and in your love to us, see that ye abound in this grace also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:6; 1 John 3:17–19; James 2:14–16"> I speak not by way of commandment, but as proving through the earnestness of others the sincerity also of your love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Philippians 2:6–8; Matthew 20:28"> For ye know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that, though he was rich, yet for your sakes he became poor, that ye through his poverty might become rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18–19; Hebrews 13:16; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> And herein I give my judgment: for this is expedient for you, who were the first to make a beginning a year ago, not only to do, but also to will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:2"> But now complete the doing also; that as there was the readiness to will, so there may be the completion also out of your ability.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 12:42–44; 2 Corinthians 9:7; Luke 21:1–4"> For if the readiness is there, it is acceptable according as a man hath, not according as he hath not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 4:34; Romans 15:26–27"> For I say not this that others may be eased and ye distressed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:12; Acts 4:34"> but by equality: your abundance being a supply at this present time for their want, that their abundance also may become a supply for your want; that there may be equality:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:18; Luke 22:35"> as it is written, He that gathered much had nothing over; and he that gathered little had no lack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Jeremiah 32:40; 2 Corinthians 2:13–14"> But thanks be to God, who putteth the same earnest care for you into the heart of Titus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 13:22; 2 Corinthians 8:10"> For he accepted indeed our exhortation; but being himself very earnest, he went forth unto you of his own accord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> And we have sent together with him the brother whose praise in the gospel is spread through all the churches;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:3–4; 2 Corinthians 4:15; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> and not only so, but who was also appointed by the churches to travel with us in the matter of this grace, which is ministered by us to the glory of the Lord, and to show our readiness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:22; Romans 14:16; Ephesians 5:15"> avoiding this, that any man should blame us in the matter of this bounty which is ministered by us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Peter 2:12; Philippians 4:8"> for we take thought for things honorable, not only in the sight of the Lord, but also in the sight of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 2:20–22"> And we have sent with them our brother, whom we have many times proved earnest in many things, but now much more earnest, by reason of the great confidence which he hath in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Philemon 1:17; 2 Corinthians 8:6"> Whether any inquire about Titus, he is my partner and my fellow-worker to you-ward; or our brethren, they are the messengers of the churches, they are the glory of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:14; 2 Corinthians 7:4; 2 Corinthians 9:2–4"> Show ye therefore unto them in the face of the churches the proof of your love, and of our glorying on your behalf.</span>
