<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:19; Ecclesiastes 8:15; James 5:5"> I said in my heart, Come now, I will prove thee with mirth; therefore enjoy pleasure: and, behold, this also was vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 14:13; Ecclesiastes 7:2–6; 1 Peter 4:2–4"> I said of laughter, It is mad; and of mirth, What doeth it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Ecclesiastes 1:17; Proverbs 31:4–5"> I searched in my heart how to cheer my flesh with wine, my heart yet guiding me with wisdom, and how to lay hold on folly, till I might see what it was good for the sons of men that they should do under heaven all the days of their life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 7:1–12; 2 Chronicles 8:11; 2 Chronicles 26:10"> I made me great works; I builded me houses; I planted me vineyards;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 17:27–29; Song of Solomon 4:12–1; Jeremiah 39:4"> I made me gardens and parks, and I planted trees in them of all kinds of fruit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:14; Psalms 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8"> I made me pools of water, to water therefrom the forest where trees were reared;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 3:4; Genesis 15:3; 1 Kings 4:23"> I bought men-servants and maid-servants, and had servants born in my house; also I had great possessions of herds and flocks, above all that were before me in Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 10:10; 1 Kings 9:28; 2 Samuel 19:35"> I gathered me also silver and gold, and the treasure of kings and of the provinces; I gat me men-singers and women-singers, and the delights of the sons of men, musical instruments, and that of all sorts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:25; Ecclesiastes 1:16; 1 Kings 10:23"> So I was great, and increased more than all that were before me in Jerusalem: also my wisdom remained with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:9; Ecclesiastes 5:18; Ecclesiastes 3:22"> And whatsoever mine eyes desired I kept not from them; I withheld not my heart from any joy; for my heart rejoiced because of all my labor; and this was my portion from all my labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; 1 John 2:16–17; Ecclesiastes 1:3"> Then I looked on all the works that my hands had wrought, and on the labor that I had labored to do; and, behold, all was vanity and a striving after wind, and there was no profit under the sun.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:17; Ecclesiastes 7:25; Ecclesiastes 1:9–10"> And I turned myself to behold wisdom, and madness, and folly: for what can the man do that cometh after the king? even that which hath been done long ago.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Ecclesiastes 7:11–12; Matthew 6:23"> Then I saw that wisdom excelleth folly, as far as light excelleth darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 49:10; Proverbs 17:24; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> The wise man’s eyes are in his head, and the fool walketh in darkness: and yet I perceived that one event happeneth to them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:8; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Ecclesiastes 1:2"> Then said I in my heart, As it happeneth to the fool, so will it happen even to me; and why was I then more wise? Then said I in my heart, that this also is vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:11; Ecclesiastes 9:5; Psalms 88:12"> For of the wise man, even as of the fool, there is no remembrance for ever; seeing that in the days to come all will have been long forgotten. And how doth the wise man die even as the fool!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:11; Jonah 4:8; Ecclesiastes 4:2"> So I hated life, because the work that is wrought under the sun was grievous unto me; for all is vanity and a striving after wind.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:6; Psalms 49:10; Luke 16:27–28"> And I hated all my labor wherein I labored under the sun, seeing that I must leave it unto the man that shall be after me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 3:17; Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 9:13"> And who knoweth whether he will be a wise man or a fool? yet will he have rule over all my labor wherein I have labored, and wherein I have showed myself wise under the sun. This also is vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 43:14; Job 17:11–15; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> Therefore I turned about to cause my heart to despair concerning all the labor wherein I had labored under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:17–18; 2 Chronicles 31:20–21; 2 Chronicles 34:2"> For there is a man whose labor is with wisdom, and with knowledge, and with skilfulness; yet to a man that hath not labored therein shall he leave it for his portion. This also is vanity and a great evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 127:2; Ecclesiastes 1:3; Philippians 4:6"> For what hath a man of all his labor, and of the striving of his heart, wherein he laboreth under the sun?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 14:1; Job 5:7; Psalms 6:6–7"> For all his days are but sorrows, and his travail is grief; yea, even in the night his heart taketh no rest. This also is vanity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 8:15; 1 Timothy 6:17"> There is nothing better for a man than that he should eat and drink, and make his soul enjoy good in his labor. This also I saw, that it is from the hand of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:1–12; 1 Kings 4:21–24"> For who can eat, or who can have enjoyment, more than I?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 13:22; Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 28:8"> For to the man that pleaseth him God giveth wisdom, and knowledge, and joy; but to the sinner he giveth travail, to gather and to heap up, that he may give to him that pleaseth God. This also is vanity and a striving after wind.</span>
</div>
