<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Jair died, and was buried in Kamon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 11:33; 1 Kings 11:7; 2 Kings 23:13"> And the children of Israel again did that which was evil in the sight of Jehovah, and served the Baalim, and the Ashtaroth, and the gods of Syria, and the gods of Sidon, and the gods of Moab, and the gods of the children of Ammon, and the gods of the Philistines; and they forsook Jehovah, and served him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Deuteronomy 32:16–22; 1 Samuel 12:9–10"> And the anger of Jehovah was kindled against Israel, and he sold them into the hand of the Philistines, and into the hand of the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Isaiah 30:13; Judges 10:5"> And they vexed and oppressed the children of Israel that year: eighteen years oppressed they all the children of Israel that were beyond the Jordan in the land of the Amorites, which is in Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:1–2; Deuteronomy 28:65; 2 Chronicles 15:5"> And the children of Ammon passed over the Jordan to fight also against Judah, and against Benjamin, and against the house of Ephraim; so that Israel was sore distressed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:10; Psalms 107:28; Psalms 107:13"> And the children of Israel cried unto Jehovah, saying, We have sinned against thee, even because we have forsaken our God, and have served the Baalim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 3:31; Numbers 21:21–25; Exodus 14:30"> And Jehovah said unto the children of Israel, Did not I save you from the Egyptians, and from the Amorites, from the children of Ammon, and from the Philistines?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 6:3; 2 Chronicles 26:6–7; Psalms 106:42–43"> The Sidonians also, and the Amalekites, and the Maonites, did oppress you; and ye cried unto me, and I saved you out of their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:13; 1 Chronicles 28:9; Judges 2:12"> Yet ye have forsaken me, and served other gods: wherefore I will save you no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Deuteronomy 32:37–38; 1 Kings 18:27–28"> Go and cry unto the gods which ye have chosen; let them save you in the time of your distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:18; 2 Samuel 15:26; Proverbs 28:13"> And the children of Israel said unto Jehovah, We have sinned: do thou unto us whatsoever seemeth good unto thee; only deliver us, we pray thee, this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 63:9; Psalms 106:44–45; Ezekiel 18:30–32"> And they put away the foreign gods from among them, and served Jehovah; and his soul was grieved for the misery of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 11:29; Genesis 31:49; Judges 11:11"> Then the children of Ammon were gathered together, and encamped in Gilead. And the children of Israel assembled themselves together, and encamped in Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 11:11; Judges 12:7; Judges 1:1"> And the people, the princes of Gilead, said one to another, What man is he that will begin to fight against the children of Ammon? he shall be head over all the inhabitants of Gilead.</span>
</div>
