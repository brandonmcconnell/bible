<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 20:34"> And they continued three years without war between Syria and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:24; 1 Kings 22:1; 1 Kings 22:41"> And it came to pass in the third year, that Jehoshaphat the king of Judah came down to the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:43; 1 Kings 4:13; Joshua 21:38"> And the king of Israel said unto his servants, Know ye that Ramoth-gilead is ours, and we are still, and take it not out of the hand of the king of Syria?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 3:7; Ephesians 5:11; 2 John 1:11"> And he said unto Jehoshaphat, Wilt thou go with me to battle to Ramoth-gilead? And Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, I am as thou art, my people as thy people, my horses as thy horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; 2 Chronicles 18:4–5; Jeremiah 42:2–6"> And Jehoshaphat said unto the king of Israel, Inquire first, I pray thee, for the word of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 18:19; Jeremiah 23:14–17; Ezekiel 13:7–16"> Then the king of Israel gathered the prophets together, about four hundred men, and said unto them, Shall I go against Ramoth-gilead to battle, or shall I forbear? And they said, Go up; for the Lord will deliver it into the hand of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 3:11–13; 2 Chronicles 18:6–7"> But Jehoshaphat said, Is there not here a prophet of Jehovah besides, that we may inquire of him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 5:10; John 3:19–21; John 7:7"> And the king of Israel said unto Jehoshaphat, There is yet one man by whom we may inquire of Jehovah, Micaiah the son of Imlah: but I hate him; for he doth not prophesy good concerning me, but evil. And Jehoshaphat said, Let not the king say so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 9:32; Isaiah 39:7; 1 Kings 22:26–27"> Then the king of Israel called an officer, and said, Fetch quickly Micaiah the son of Imlah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esther 5:1; Esther 6:8–9; 1 Kings 18:29"> Now the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat the king of Judah were sitting each on his throne, arrayed in their robes, in an open place at the entrance of the gate of Samaria; and all the prophets were prophesying before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 1:18–21; Deuteronomy 33:17; Ezekiel 22:27–28"> And Zedekiah the son of Chenaanah made him horns of iron, and said, Thus saith Jehovah, With these shalt thou push the Syrians, until they be consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:22; 1 Kings 22:6–15; 1 Kings 22:32–36"> And all the prophets prophesied so, saying, Go up to Ramoth-gilead, and prosper; for Jehovah will deliver it into the hand of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 2:6–7; Psalms 11:1; Isaiah 30:10–11"> And the messenger that went to call Micaiah spake unto him, saying, Behold now, the words of the prophets declare good unto the king with one mouth: let thy word, I pray thee, be like the word of one of them, and speak thou good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 24:13; Numbers 22:18; 2 Chronicles 18:12–13"> And Micaiah said, As Jehovah liveth, what Jehovah saith unto me, that will I speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 18:27; Judges 10:14; Matthew 26:45"> And when he was come to the king, the king said unto him, Micaiah, shall we go to Ramoth-gilead to battle, or shall we forbear? And he answered him, Go up and prosper; and Jehovah will deliver it into the hand of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 19:13; Matthew 26:63; Joshua 6:26"> And the king said unto him, How many times shall I adjure thee that thou speak unto me nothing but the truth in the name of Jehovah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 27:17; Matthew 9:36; 1 Kings 22:34–36"> And he said, I saw all Israel scattered upon the mountains, as sheep that have no shepherd: and Jehovah said, These have no master; let them return every man to his house in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 22:8; Proverbs 10:24; Luke 11:45"> And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, Did I not tell thee that he would not prophesy good concerning me, but evil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 2:1; Job 1:6; Daniel 7:9–10"> And Micaiah said, Therefore hear thou the word of Jehovah: I saw Jehovah sitting on his throne, and all the host of heaven standing by him on his right hand and on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:9; Job 12:16; Jeremiah 4:10"> And Jehovah said, Who shall entice Ahab, that he may go up and fall at Ramoth-gilead? And one said on this manner; and another said on that manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 2:1; 1 Kings 22:23; Job 1:6–7"> And there came forth a spirit, and stood before Jehovah, and said, I will entice him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 9:23; 1 Samuel 19:9; 1 Samuel 16:14"> And Jehovah said unto him, Wherewith? And he said, I will go forth, and will be a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets. And he said, Thou shalt entice him, and shalt prevail also: go forth, and do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:9; Matthew 24:24–25; Isaiah 44:20"> Now therefore, behold, Jehovah hath put a lying spirit in the mouth of all these thy prophets; and Jehovah hath spoken evil concerning thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 23:2; Lamentations 3:30; Micah 5:1"> Then Zedekiah the son of Chenaanah came near, and smote Micaiah on the cheek, and said, Which way went the Spirit of Jehovah from me to speak unto thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 20:30; 2 Peter 2:1; Revelation 19:20"> And Micaiah said, Behold, thou shalt see on that day, when thou shalt go into an inner chamber to hide thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the king of Israel said, Take Micaiah, and carry him back unto Amon the governor of the city, and to Joash the king’s son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:10; 2 Chronicles 18:25–27; Revelation 2:10"> and say, Thus saith the king, Put this fellow in the prison, and feed him with bread of affliction and with water of affliction, until I come in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 16:29; Micah 1:2; Acts 13:10–11"> And Micaiah said, If thou return at all in peace, Jehovah hath not spoken by me. And he said, Hear, ye peoples, all of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:28; 1 Kings 22:2–9"> So the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat the king of Judah went up to Ramoth-gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:22; Jeremiah 23:24; 1 Samuel 28:8"> And the king of Israel said unto Jehoshaphat, I will disguise myself, and go into the battle; but put thou on thy robes. And the king of Israel disguised himself, and went into the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 20:24; 2 Chronicles 18:30; Jeremiah 16:6"> Now the king of Syria had commanded the thirty and two captains of his chariots, saying, Fight neither with small nor great, save only with the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Jonah 2:1–2; Psalms 130:1–4"> And it came to pass, when the captains of the chariots saw Jehoshaphat, that they said, Surely it is the king of Israel; and they turned aside to fight against him: and Jehoshaphat cried out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 76:10; 1 Kings 22:31"> And it came to pass, when the captains of the chariots saw that it was not the king of Israel, that they turned back from pursuing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:23; Micah 6:13; 1 Samuel 17:49"> And a certain man drew his bow at a venture, and smote the king of Israel between the joints of the armor: wherefore he said unto the driver of his chariot, Turn thy hand, and carry me out of the host; for I am sore wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 22:28; 1 Kings 20:42"> And the battle increased that day: and the king was stayed up in his chariot against the Syrians, and died at even; and the blood ran out of the wound into the bottom of the chariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Kings 14:12; 1 Kings 22:31; Judges 21:24"> And there went a cry throughout the host about the going down of the sun, saying, Every man to his city, and every man to his country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> So the king died, and was brought to Samaria; and they buried the king in Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; Matthew 24:35; Jeremiah 44:21–23"> And they washed the chariot by the pool of Samaria; and the dogs licked up his blood (now the harlots washed themselves there); according unto the word of Jehovah which he spake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Amos 3:15; Ezekiel 27:6; Psalms 45:8"> Now the rest of the acts of Ahab, and all that he did, and the ivory house which he built, and all the cities that he built, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12; 2 Kings 1:17; 2 Kings 1:2"> So Ahab slept with his fathers; and Ahaziah his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:31; 1 Kings 22:2; 2 Chronicles 17:1"> And Jehoshaphat the son of Asa began to reign over Judah in the fourth year of Ahab king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 Kings 1:17; 1 Kings 15:2; 1 Kings 15:10"> Jehoshaphat was thirty and five years old when he began to reign; and he reigned twenty and five years in Jerusalem. And his mother’s name was Azubah the daughter of Shilhi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Kings 15:14; 2 Kings 12:3; Proverbs 4:27"> And he walked in all the way of Asa his father; he turned not aside from it, doing that which was right in the eyes of Jehovah: howbeit the high places were not taken away; the people still sacrificed and burnt incense in the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Kings 22:2; 2 Chronicles 19:2; 2 Kings 8:18"> And Jehoshaphat made peace with the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 14:29; 1 Kings 11:41; 2 Chronicles 20:34"> Now the rest of the acts of Jehoshaphat, and his might that he showed, and how he warred, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Kings 15:12; 1 Kings 14:24; Deuteronomy 23:17"> And the remnant of the sodomites, that remained in the days of his father Asa, he put away out of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; 2 Kings 3:9; 2 Kings 8:20"> And there was no king in Edom: a deputy was king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Kings 9:26; 1 Kings 10:22; 1 Kings 9:28"> Jehoshaphat made ships of Tarshish to go to Ophir for gold: but they went not; for the ships were broken at Ezion-geber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> Then said Ahaziah the son of Ahab unto Jehoshaphat, Let my servants go with thy servants in the ships. But Jehoshaphat would not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:1; 1 Kings 22:40; 1 Kings 15:24"> And Jehoshaphat slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the city of David his father; And Jehoram his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="2 Kings 1:17; 1 Kings 22:40; 1 Kings 15:25"> Ahaziah the son of Ahab began to reign over Israel in Samaria in the seventeenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and he reigned two years over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Kings 15:26; 1 Kings 21:25; Revelation 3:20"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of Jehovah, and walked in the way of his father, and in the way of his mother, and in the way of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, wherein he made Israel to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 Kings 16:30–32; Isaiah 65:3; 2 Kings 3:2"> And he served Baal, and worshipped him, and provoked to anger Jehovah, the God of Israel, according to all that his father had done.</span>
