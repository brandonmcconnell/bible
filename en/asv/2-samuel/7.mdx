<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Luke 1:74–75; 1 Kings 5:4"> And it came to pass, when the king dwelt in his house, and Jehovah had given him rest from all his enemies round about,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:1; 2 Samuel 5:11; Haggai 1:4"> that the king said unto Nathan the prophet, See now, I dwell in a house of cedar, but the ark of God dwelleth within curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:7; 1 Kings 8:17–18; 2 Kings 4:27"> And Nathan said to the king, Go, do all that is in thy heart; for Jehovah is with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:3; Numbers 12:6; Amos 3:7"> And it came to pass the same night, that the word of Jehovah came unto Nathan, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:3–32; 1 Kings 5:3–4; 1 Kings 8:16–19"> Go and tell my servant David, Thus saith Jehovah, Shalt thou build me a house for me to dwell in?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 8:16; Joshua 18:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> for I have not dwelt in a house since the day that I brought up the children of Israel out of Egypt, even to this day, but have walked in a tent and in a tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:6; 2 Samuel 5:2; Leviticus 26:11–12"> In all places wherein I have walked with all the children of Israel, spake I a word with any of the tribes of Israel, whom I commanded to be shepherd of my people Israel, saying, Why have ye not built me a house of cedar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 78:70–71; 2 Samuel 6:21; 2 Samuel 12:7"> Now therefore thus shalt thou say unto my servant David, Thus saith Jehovah of hosts, I took thee from the sheepcote, from following the sheep, that thou shouldest be prince over my people, over Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:10; 1 Chronicles 17:8; 1 Samuel 18:14"> and I have been with thee whithersoever thou wentest, and have cut off all thine enemies from before thee; and I will make thee a great name, like unto the name of the great ones that are in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 9:15; 1 Chronicles 17:9; Jeremiah 24:6"> And I will appoint a place for my people Israel, and will plant them, that they may dwell in their own place, and be moved no more; neither shall the children of wickedness afflict them any more, as at the first,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:27; 2 Samuel 7:1; 1 Chronicles 17:10"> and as from the day that I commanded judges to be over my people Israel; and I will cause thee to rest from all thine enemies. Moreover Jehovah telleth thee that Jehovah will make thee a house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 8:20; Acts 2:30; Acts 13:36"> When thy days are fulfilled, and thou shalt sleep with thy fathers, I will set up thy seed after thee, that shall proceed out of thy bowels, and I will establish his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:31–33; 1 Kings 8:19; 2 Samuel 7:16"> He shall build a house for my name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 1:5; Revelation 3:19; 1 Chronicles 17:13"> I will be his father, and he shall be my son: if he commit iniquity, I will chasten him with the rod of men, and with the stripes of the children of men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:23; 1 Samuel 15:28; Psalms 89:28"> but my lovingkindness shall not depart from him, as I took it from Saul, whom I put away before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Psalms 89:36–37; 2 Samuel 7:13"> And thy house and thy kingdom shall be made sure for ever before thee: thy throne shall be established for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:15; Acts 20:27; 1 Corinthians 15:3"> According to all these words, and according to all this vision, so did Nathan speak unto David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 8:4; 1 Chronicles 17:16; 1 Samuel 18:18"> Then David the king went in, and sat before Jehovah; and he said, Who am I, O Lord Jehovah, and what is my house, that thou hast brought me thus far?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 55:8–9; 1 Chronicles 17:17; Psalms 36:7"> And this was yet a small thing in thine eyes, O Lord Jehovah; but thou hast spoken also of thy servant’s house for a great while to come; and this too after the manner of men, O Lord Jehovah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 21:17; 1 Samuel 16:7; Psalms 139:1"> And what can David say more unto thee? for thou knowest thy servant, O Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 115:1; Psalms 138:2; Ephesians 1:9"> For thy word’s sake, and according to thine own heart, hast thou wrought all this greatness, to make thy servant know it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:10; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 86:8"> Wherefore thou art great, O Jehovah God: for there is none like thee, neither is there any God besides thee, according to all that we have heard with our ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:26; Deuteronomy 10:21; Deuteronomy 15:15"> And what one nation in the earth is like thy people, even like Israel, whom God went to redeem unto himself for a people, and to make him a name, and to do great things for you, and terrible things for thy land, before thy people, whom thou redeemedst to thee out of Egypt, from the nations and their gods?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:18; 1 Peter 2:10; Genesis 17:7"> And thou didst establish to thyself thy people Israel to be a people unto thee for ever; and thou, Jehovah, becamest their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 119:49; Ezekiel 36:37; Genesis 32:12"> And now, O Jehovah God, the word that thou hast spoken concerning thy servant, and concerning his house, confirm thou it for ever, and do as thou hast spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:23–24; Psalms 72:18–19; Matthew 6:9"> And let thy name be magnified for ever, saying, Jehovah of hosts is God over Israel; and the house of thy servant David shall be established before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:25–26; Psalms 10:17; Ruth 4:4"> For thou, O Jehovah of hosts, the God of Israel, hast revealed to thy servant, saying, I will build thee a house: therefore hath thy servant found in his heart to pray this prayer unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 17:17; Exodus 34:6; Numbers 23:19"> And now, O Lord Jehovah, thou art God, and thy words are truth, and thou hast promised this good thing unto thy servant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 6:24–26; 1 Chronicles 17:27; Psalms 115:12–15"> now therefore let it please thee to bless the house of thy servant, that it may continue for ever before thee; for thou, O Lord Jehovah, hast spoken it: and with thy blessing let the house of thy servant be blessed for ever.</span>
