<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Jeremiah 34:20; Jeremiah 16:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">The dead bodies of thy servants have they given to be food unto the birds of the heavens,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The flesh of thy saints unto the beasts of the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:4; Jeremiah 14:16; Revelation 11:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their blood have they shed like water round about Jerusalem;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And there was none to bury them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 80:6; Lamentations 5:1; Psalms 89:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">We are become a reproach to our neighbors,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">A scoffing and derision to them that are round about us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Psalms 74:1; Psalms 85:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">How long, O Jehovah? wilt thou be angry for ever?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Shall thy jealousy burn like fire?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:25; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Psalms 14:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pour out thy wrath upon the nations that know thee not,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And upon the kingdoms that call not upon thy name.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34–35; Zechariah 1:15; Isaiah 64:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they have devoured Jacob,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And laid waste his habitation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 64:9; Psalms 142:6; Psalms 116:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Remember not against us the iniquities of our forefathers:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let thy tender mercies speedily meet us;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For we are brought very low.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:7; Psalms 25:11; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Help us, O God of our salvation, for the glory of thy name;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And deliver us, and forgive our sins, for thy name’s sake.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:10; Exodus 7:5; Psalms 115:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wherefore should the nations say, Where is their God?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let the avenging of the blood of thy servants which is shed</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Be known among the nations in our sight.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 102:20; Psalms 146:6–7; Isaiah 33:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the sighing of the prisoner come before thee:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">According to the greatness of thy power preserve thou those that are appointed to death;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:18; Genesis 4:15; Leviticus 26:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">And render unto our neighbors sevenfold into their bosom</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their reproach, wherewith they have reproached thee, O Lord.</span><br /></span>
