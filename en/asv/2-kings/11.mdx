<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:1; 2 Kings 25:25; 2 Chronicles 22:10–12"> Now when Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah saw that her son was dead, she arose and destroyed all the seed royal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:26; Jeremiah 35:2; Isaiah 7:6–7"> But Jehosheba, the daughter of king Joram, sister of Ahaziah, took Joash the son of Ahaziah, and stole him away from among the king’s sons that were slain, even him and his nurse, and put them in the bedchamber; and they hid him from Athaliah, so that he was not slain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 12:8; 2 Chronicles 22:12; Malachi 3:15"> And he was with her hid in the house of Jehovah six years. And Athaliah reigned over the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 11:19; 2 Kings 11:9; 2 Kings 11:17"> And in the seventh year Jehoiada sent and fetched the captains over hundreds of the Carites and of the guard, and brought them to him into the house of Jehovah; and he made a covenant with them, and took an oath of them in the house of Jehovah, and showed them the king’s son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; Ezekiel 44:2–3; 1 Chronicles 23:32"> And he commanded them, saying, This is the thing that ye shall do: a third part of you, that come in on the sabbath, shall be keepers of the watch of the king’s house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:4–5; 1 Chronicles 26:13–19"> and a third part shall be at the gate Sur; and a third part at the gate behind the guard: so shall ye keep the watch of the house, and be a barrier.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:6; 2 Kings 11:5"> And the two companies of you, even all that go forth on the sabbath, shall keep the watch of the house of Jehovah about the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 27:17; 2 Chronicles 23:7; 1 Kings 2:28–31"> And ye shall compass the king round about, every man with his weapons in his hand; and he that cometh within the ranks, let him be slain: and be ye with the king when he goeth out, and when he cometh in.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:8; 2 Kings 11:4; 1 Chronicles 26:26"> And the captains over hundreds did according to all that Jehoiada the priest commanded; and they took every man his men, those that were to come in on the sabbath, with those that were to go out on the sabbath, and came to Jehoiada the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:7; 1 Chronicles 18:7; 2 Chronicles 5:1"> And the priest delivered to the captains over hundreds the spears and shields that had been king David’s, which were in the house of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 11:8; Ezekiel 8:16; 2 Kings 11:10"> And the guard stood, every man with his weapons in his hand, from the right side of the house to the left side of the house, along by the altar and the house, by the king round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 25:16; 1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Kings 1:39"> Then he brought out the king’s son, and put the crown upon him, and gave him the testimony; and they made him king, and anointed him; and they clapped their hands, and said, Long live the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:12–15"> And when Athaliah heard the noise of the guard and of the people, she came to the people into the house of Jehovah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:31; 2 Kings 23:3; 2 Kings 9:23"> and she looked, and, behold, the king stood by the pillar, as the manner was, and the captains and the trumpets by the king; and all the people of the land rejoiced, and blew trumpets. Then Athaliah rent her clothes, and cried, Treason! treason!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:7; 2 Chronicles 23:14; 2 Chronicles 23:9"> And Jehoiada the priest commanded the captains of hundreds that were set over the host, and said unto them, Have her forth between the ranks; and him that followeth her slay with the sword. For the priest said, Let her not be slain in the house of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; 2 Chronicles 23:15; Matthew 7:2"> So they made way for her; and she went by the way of the horses’ entry to the king’s house: and there was she slain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:3; 2 Chronicles 15:12–14; 2 Chronicles 34:31"> And Jehoiada made a covenant between Jehovah and the king and the people, that they should be Jehovah’s people; between the king also and the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:3; 2 Kings 10:26; 1 Kings 18:40"> And all the people of the land went to the house of Baal, and brake it down; his altars and his images brake they in pieces thoroughly, and slew Mattan the priest of Baal before the altars. And the priest appointed officers over the house of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:25; Jeremiah 22:4; 2 Chronicles 23:19"> And he took the captains over hundreds, and the Carites, and the guard, and all the people of the land; and they brought down the king from the house of Jehovah, and came by the way of the gate of the guard unto the king’s house. And he sat on the throne of the kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 29:2; 2 Kings 11:14"> So all the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet. And Athaliah they had slain with the sword at the king’s house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:1–14; 2 Kings 22:1; 2 Kings 11:4"> Jehoash was seven years old when he began to reign.</span>
</div>
