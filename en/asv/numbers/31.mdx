<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 27:13; Numbers 25:14–18; Revelation 6:10"> Avenge the children of Israel of the Midianites: afterward shalt thou be gathered unto thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Numbers 25:11; 2 Kings 10:30"> And Moses spake unto the people, saying, Arm ye men from among you for the war, that they may go against Midian, to execute Jehovah’s vengeance on Midian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:8; 1 Samuel 14:6; Judges 7:2"> Of every tribe a thousand, throughout all the tribes of Israel, shall ye send to the war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So there were delivered, out of the thousands of Israel, a thousand of every tribe, twelve thousand armed for war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:44; Numbers 10:8–9; 2 Chronicles 13:12–15"> And Moses sent them, a thousand of every tribe, to the war, them and Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest, to the war, with the vessels of the sanctuary and the trumpets for the alarm in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 21:11; 1 Kings 11:15–16; 1 Samuel 27:9"> And they warred against Midian, as Jehovah commanded Moses; and they slew every male.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:21–22; Numbers 25:15; Revelation 2:14"> And they slew the kings of Midian with the rest of their slain: Evi, and Rekem, and Zur, and Hur, and Reba, the five kings of Midian: Balaam also the son of Beor they slew with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; 2 Chronicles 28:5"> And the children of Israel took captive the women of Midian and their little ones; and all their cattle, and all their flocks, and all their goods, they took for a prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; 1 Samuel 30:1; Joshua 6:24"> And all their cities in the places wherein they dwelt, and all their encampments, they burnt with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 8:2"> And they took all the spoil, and all the prey, both of man and of beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:1"> And they brought the captives, and the prey, and the spoil, unto Moses, and unto Eleazar the priest, and unto the congregation of the children of Israel, unto the camp at the plains of Moab, which are by the Jordan at Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; 1 Samuel 30:21; Numbers 31:12"> And Moses, and Eleazar the priest, and all the princes of the congregation, went forth to meet them without the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 13:19; 1 Samuel 15:13–14; Numbers 31:48"> And Moses was wroth with the officers of the host, the captains of thousands and the captains of hundreds, who came from the service of the war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Jeremiah 48:10; Deuteronomy 2:34"> And Moses said unto them, Have ye saved all the women alive?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 2 Peter 2:15; Revelation 2:14"> Behold, these caused the children of Israel, through the counsel of Balaam, to commit trespass against Jehovah in the matter of Peor, and so the plague was among the congregation of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 21:11–12; Deuteronomy 20:16–18; Deuteronomy 7:2"> Now therefore kill every male among the little ones, and kill every woman that hath known man by lying with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:10–14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; Deuteronomy 20:14"> But all the women-children, that have not known man by lying with him, keep alive for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 19:11–22; Numbers 5:2; 1 Chronicles 22:8"> And encamp ye without the camp seven days: whosoever hath killed any person, and whosoever hath touched any slain, purify yourselves on the third day and on the seventh day, ye and your captives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 19:22; Genesis 35:2; Exodus 19:10"> And as to every garment, and all that is made of skin, and all work of goats’ hair, and all things made of wood, ye shall purify yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 30:16"> And Eleazar the priest said unto the men of war that went to the battle, This is the statute of the law which Jehovah hath commanded Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> howbeit the gold, and the silver, the brass, the iron, the tin, and the lead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 19:9; Numbers 19:17; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> everything that may abide the fire, ye shall make to go through the fire, and it shall be clean; nevertheless it shall be purified with the water for impurity: and all that abideth not the fire ye shall make to go through the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 15:13; Leviticus 14:9"> And ye shall wash your clothes on the seventh day, and ye shall be clean; and afterward ye shall come into the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Jehovah spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Take the sum of the prey that was taken, both of man and of beast, thou, and Eleazar the priest, and the heads of the fathers’ houses of the congregation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 22:8; Psalms 68:12; 1 Samuel 30:24–25"> and divide the prey into two parts: between the men skilled in war, that went out to battle, and all the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 18:26; Numbers 31:30; Numbers 31:47"> And levy a tribute unto Jehovah of the men of war that went out to battle: one soul of five hundred, both of the persons, and of the oxen, and of the asses, and of the flocks:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 29:27; Deuteronomy 12:19; Deuteronomy 12:12"> take it of their half, and give it unto Eleazar the priest, for Jehovah’s heave-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 3:7–8; Numbers 3:25"> And of the children of Israel’s half, thou shalt take one drawn out of every fifty, of the persons, of the oxen, of the asses, and of the flocks, even of all the cattle, and give them unto the Levites, that keep the charge of the tabernacle of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Moses and Eleazar the priest did as Jehovah commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Now the prey, over and above the booty which the men of war took, was six hundred thousand and seventy thousand and five thousand sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and threescore and twelve thousand oxen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> and threescore and one thousand asses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and thirty and two thousand persons in all, of the women that had not known man by lying with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And the half, which was the portion of them that went out to war, was in number three hundred thousand and thirty thousand and seven thousand and five hundred sheep:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and Jehovah’s tribute of the sheep was six hundred and threescore and fifteen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And the oxen were thirty and six thousand; of which Jehovah’s tribute was threescore and twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And the asses were thirty thousand and five hundred; of which Jehovah’s tribute was threescore and one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And the persons were sixteen thousand; of whom Jehovah’s tribute was thirty and two persons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 18:8; Numbers 18:19; Hebrews 7:9–12"> And Moses gave the tribute, which was Jehovah’s heave-offering, unto Eleazar the priest, as Jehovah commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And of the children of Israel’s half, which Moses divided off from the men that warred</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> (now the congregation’s half was three hundred thousand and thirty thousand, seven thousand and five hundred sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and thirty and six thousand oxen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> and thirty thousand and five hundred asses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> and sixteen thousand persons),</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 31:30; Luke 10:1–8; Isaiah 56:10–11"> even of the children of Israel’s half, Moses took one drawn out of every fifty, both of man and of beast, and gave them unto the Levites, that kept the charge of the tabernacle of Jehovah; as Jehovah commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And the officers that were over the thousands of the host, the captains of thousands, and the captains of hundreds, came near unto Moses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 18:9; 1 Samuel 30:18–19; Psalms 72:14"> and they said unto Moses, Thy servants have taken the sum of the men of war that are under our charge, and there lacketh not one man of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 30:12; Exodus 30:15–16; Psalms 116:12"> And we have brought Jehovah’s oblation, what every man hath gotten, of jewels of gold, ankle-chains, and bracelets, signet-rings, ear-rings, and armlets, to make atonement for our souls before Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 7:2–6"> And Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold of them, even all wrought jewels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And all the gold of the heave-offering that they offered up to Jehovah, of the captains of thousands, and of the captains of hundreds, was sixteen thousand seven hundred and fifty shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:32"> (For the men of war had taken booty, every man for himself.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 30:16; Zechariah 6:14; Acts 10:4"> And Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold of the captains of thousands and of hundreds, and brought it into the tent of meeting, for a memorial for the children of Israel before Jehovah.</span>
</div>
