<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 1:1; Exodus 40:2"> And Jehovah spake unto Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the first month of the second year after they were come out of the land of Egypt, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–2; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; 2 Chronicles 35:1"> Moreover let the children of Israel keep the passover in its appointed season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 12:6–11; Leviticus 23:5; 2 Chronicles 30:2"> In the fourteenth day of this month, at even, ye shall keep it in its appointed season: according to all the statutes of it, and according to all the ordinances thereof, shall ye keep it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And Moses spake unto the children of Israel, that they should keep the passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:10; Matthew 28:20; Exodus 12:1–13"> And they kept the passover in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, at even, in the wilderness of Sinai: according to all that Jehovah commanded Moses, so did the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 27:2; Exodus 18:15; Numbers 5:2"> And there were certain men, who were unclean by reason of the dead body of a man, so that they could not keep the passover on that day: and they came before Moses and before Aaron on that day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:2; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Exodus 12:27"> and those men said unto him, We are unclean by reason of the dead body of a man: wherefore are we kept back, that we may not offer the oblation of Jehovah in its appointed season among the children of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 85:8; Numbers 27:5; 1 Corinthians 4:4"> And Moses said unto them, Stay ye, that I may hear what Jehovah will command concerning you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Jehovah spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–2; Ephesians 2:12–13; 1 Corinthians 11:28"> Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, If any man of you or of your generations shall be unclean by reason of a dead body, or be on a journey afar off, yet he shall keep the passover unto Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:2–15; Exodus 12:43–49; John 19:36"> In the second month on the fourteenth day at even they shall keep it; they shall eat it with unleavened bread and bitter herbs:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 19:36; Exodus 12:46; Exodus 12:10"> they shall leave none of it unto the morning, nor break a bone thereof: according to all the statute of the passover they shall keep it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 17:14; Exodus 12:15; Numbers 9:7"> But the man that is clean, and is not on a journey, and forbeareth to keep the passover, that soul shall be cut off from his people; because he offered not the oblation of Jehovah in its appointed season, that man shall bear his sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 12:48–49; Leviticus 24:22; Isaiah 56:3–7"> And if a stranger shall sojourn among you, and will keep the passover unto Jehovah; according to the statute of the passover, and according to the ordinance thereof, so shall he do: ye shall have one statute, both for the sojourner, and for him that is born in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Exodus 40:34; Nehemiah 9:19"> And on the day that the tabernacle was reared up the cloud covered the tabernacle, even the tent of the testimony: and at even it was upon the tabernacle as it were the appearance of fire, until morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Exodus 40:38; 1 Corinthians 10:1"> So it was alway: the cloud covered it, and the appearance of fire by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 40:36–38; Psalms 32:8; John 10:9"> And whenever the cloud was taken up from over the Tent, then after that the children of Israel journeyed: and in the place where the cloud abode, there the children of Israel encamped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:1; 2 John 1:6; Exodus 17:1"> At the commandment of Jehovah the children of Israel journeyed, and at the commandment of Jehovah they encamped: as long as the cloud abode upon the tabernacle they remained encamped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 3:7; Numbers 1:52–53; Numbers 3:8"> And when the cloud tarried upon the tabernacle many days, then the children of Israel kept the charge of Jehovah, and journeyed not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And sometimes the cloud was a few days upon the tabernacle; then according to the commandment of Jehovah they remained encamped, and according to the commandment of Jehovah they journeyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:19; Nehemiah 9:12"> And sometimes the cloud was from evening until morning; and when the cloud was taken up in the morning, they journeyed: or if it continued by day and by night, when the cloud was taken up, they journeyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Exodus 40:36–37; Exodus 40:16"> Whether it were two days, or a month, or a year, that the cloud tarried upon the tabernacle, abiding thereon, the children of Israel remained encamped, and journeyed not; but when it was taken up, they journeyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 22:3; Numbers 9:19; Genesis 26:5"> At the commandment of Jehovah they encamped, and at the commandment of Jehovah they journeyed: they kept the charge of Jehovah, at the commandment of Jehovah by Moses.</span>
