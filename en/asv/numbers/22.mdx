<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:1; Deuteronomy 3:8; Numbers 21:20"> And the children of Israel journeyed, and encamped in the plains of Moab beyond the Jordan at Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:25; Numbers 21:20–35; Numbers 21:3"> And Balak the son of Zippor saw all that Israel had done to the Amorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 15:15; Isaiah 23:5; Joshua 9:24"> And Moab was sore afraid of the people, because they were many: and Moab was distressed because of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 25:15–18; Numbers 31:8; Numbers 24:17"> And Moab said unto the elders of Midian, Now will this multitude lick up all that is round about us, as the ox licketh up the grass of the field. And Balak the son of Zippor was king of Moab at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:4; Joshua 24:9; Micah 6:5"> And he sent messengers unto Balaam the son of Beor, to Pethor, which is by the River, to the land of the children of his people, to call him, saying, Behold, there is a people come out from Egypt: behold, they cover the face of the earth, and they abide over against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Numbers 23:7–8; Acts 16:16"> Come now therefore, I pray thee, curse me this people; for they are too mighty for me: peradventure I shall prevail, that we may smite them, and that I may drive them out of the land; for I know that he whom thou blessest is blessed, and he whom thou cursest is cursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:7–8; Micah 3:11; Romans 16:18"> And the elders of Moab and the elders of Midian departed with the rewards of divination in their hand; and they came unto Balaam, and spake unto him the words of Balak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:12; Numbers 22:19–20; Jeremiah 12:2"> And he said unto them, Lodge here this night, and I will bring you word again, as Jehovah shall speak unto me: and the princes of Moab abode with Balaam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 20:3; Numbers 22:20; Genesis 16:8"> And God came unto Balaam, and said, What men are these with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Balaam said unto God, Balak the son of Zippor, king of Moab, hath sent unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Behold, the people that is come out of Egypt, it covereth the face of the earth: now, come curse me them; peradventure I shall be able to fight against them, and shall drive them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Ephesians 1:3; Genesis 12:2"> And God said unto Balaam, Thou shalt not go with them; thou shalt not curse the people; for they are blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Numbers 22:14"> And Balaam rose up in the morning, and said unto the princes of Balak, Get you into your land; for Jehovah refuseth to give me leave to go with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 22:37; Numbers 22:13"> And the princes of Moab rose up, and they went unto Balak, and said, Balaam refuseth to come with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 10:7–8; Numbers 22:7–8"> And Balak sent yet again princes, more, and more honorable than they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And they came to Balaam, and said to him, Thus saith Balak the son of Zippor, Let nothing, I pray thee, hinder thee from coming unto me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 24:11; Esther 5:11; Matthew 4:8–9"> for I will promote thee unto very great honor, and whatsoever thou sayest unto me I will do: come therefore, I pray thee, curse me this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 24:13; 1 Kings 22:14; 2 Chronicles 18:13"> And Balaam answered and said unto the servants of Balak, If Balak would give me his house full of silver and gold, I cannot go beyond the word of Jehovah my God, to do less or more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jude 1:11; 2 Peter 2:3; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> Now therefore, I pray you, tarry ye also here this night, that I may know what Jehovah will speak unto me more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 23:12; Numbers 24:13; Numbers 23:26"> And God came unto Balaam at night, and said unto him, If the men are come to call thee, rise up, go with them; but only the word which I speak unto thee, that shalt thou do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 1:15–16"> And Balaam rose up in the morning, and saddled his ass, and went with the princes of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 4:24; Lamentations 2:4; Numbers 22:32"> And God’s anger was kindled because he went; and the angel of Jehovah placed himself in the way for an adversary against him. Now he was riding upon his ass, and his two servants were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jude 1:11; 2 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29"> And the ass saw the angel of Jehovah standing in the way, with his sword drawn in his hand; and the ass turned aside out of the way, and went into the field: and Balaam smote the ass, to turn her into the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Then the angel of Jehovah stood in a narrow path between the vineyards, a wall being on this side, and a wall on that side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 47:12; Job 5:13–15"> And the ass saw the angel of Jehovah, and she thrust herself unto the wall, and crushed Balaam’s foot against the wall: and he smote her again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 2:6; Isaiah 26:11"> And the angel of Jehovah went further, and stood in a narrow place, where was no way to turn either to the right hand or to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 14:16; James 1:19; Proverbs 27:3–4"> And the ass saw the angel of Jehovah, and she lay down under Balaam: and Balaam’s anger was kindled, and he smote the ass with his staff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Peter 2:16; Luke 1:37; Exodus 4:11"> And Jehovah opened the mouth of the ass, and she said unto Balaam, What have I done unto thee, that thou hast smitten me these three times?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 12:10; Matthew 15:19; Proverbs 12:16"> And Balaam said unto the ass, Because thou hast mocked me: I would there were a sword in my hand, for now I had killed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 1:27–28"> And the ass said unto Balaam, Am not I thine ass, upon which thou hast ridden all thy life long unto this day? was I ever wont to do so unto thee? and he said, Nay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 21:19; Luke 24:31; Acts 26:18"> Then Jehovah opened the eyes of Balaam, and he saw the angel of Jehovah standing in the way, with his sword drawn in his hand; and he bowed his head, and fell on his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Acts 13:10; Numbers 22:22"> And the angel of Jehovah said unto him, Wherefore hast thou smitten thine ass these three times? behold, I am come forth for an adversary, because thy way is perverse before me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 16:33–35; Numbers 14:37; 1 Kings 13:24–28"> and the ass saw me, and turned aside before me these three times: unless she had turned aside from me, surely now I had even slain thee, and saved her alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 34:31–32; 2 Samuel 12:13; 1 Samuel 15:24"> And Balaam said unto the angel of Jehovah, I have sinned; for I knew not that thou stoodest in the way against me: now therefore, if it displease thee, I will get me back again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–12; Isaiah 37:26–29; Numbers 22:20–21"> And the angel of Jehovah said unto Balaam, Go with the men; but only the word that I shall speak unto thee, that thou shalt speak. So Balaam went with the princes of Balak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 16:2; Acts 28:15; Jeremiah 48:20"> And when Balak heard that Balaam was come, he went out to meet him unto the City of Moab, which is on the border of the Arnon, which is in the utmost part of the border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 24:11; John 5:44; Matthew 4:8–9"> And Balak said unto Balaam, Did I not earnestly send unto thee to call thee? wherefore camest thou not unto me? am I not able indeed to promote thee to honor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 22:18; Proverbs 19:21; Numbers 23:16"> And Balaam said unto Balak, Lo, I am come unto thee: have I now any power at all to speak anything? the word that God putteth in my mouth, that shall I speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And Balaam went with Balak, and they came unto Kiriath-huzoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 23:14; Proverbs 1:16; Numbers 23:2"> And Balak sacrificed oxen and sheep, and sent to Balaam, and to the princes that were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 23:13; Numbers 25:2–3; 2 Chronicles 11:15"> And it came to pass in the morning, that Balak took Balaam, and brought him up into the high places of Baal; and he saw from thence the utmost part of the people.</span>
