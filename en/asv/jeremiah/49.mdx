<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 1:13–15; Zephaniah 2:8–11; Ezekiel 25:2–10"> Of the children of Ammon. Thus saith Jehovah: Hath Israel no sons? hath he no heir? why then doth Malcam possess Gad, and his people dwell in the cities thereof?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:19; Ezekiel 21:20; Deuteronomy 3:11"> Therefore, behold, the days come, saith Jehovah, that I will cause an alarm of war to be heard against Rabbah of the children of Ammon; and it shall become a desolate heap, and her daughters shall be burned with fire: then shall Israel possess them that did possess him, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:7; Jeremiah 48:37; Isaiah 15:2"> Wail, O Heshbon, for Ai is laid waste; cry, ye daughters of Rabbah, gird you with sackcloth: lament, and run to and fro among the fences; for Malcam shall go into captivity, his priests and his princes together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Jeremiah 9:23; Jeremiah 21:13"> Wherefore gloriest thou in the valleys, thy flowing valley, O backsliding daughter? that trusted in her treasures, saying, Who shall come unto me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:5; Jeremiah 49:29; Jeremiah 15:8"> Behold, I will bring a fear upon thee, saith the Lord, Jehovah of hosts, from all that are round about thee; and ye shall be driven out every man right forth, and there shall be none to gather together the fugitives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:39; Jeremiah 48:47; Ezekiel 16:53"> But afterward I will bring back the captivity of the children of Ammon, saith Jehovah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 1:11–12; Jeremiah 25:21; Genesis 36:15"> Of Edom. Thus saith Jehovah of hosts: Is wisdom no more in Teman? is counsel perished from the prudent? is their wisdom vanished?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:23; Jeremiah 46:21; Jeremiah 49:30"> Flee ye, turn back, dwell in the depths, O inhabitants of Dedan; for I will bring the calamity of Esau upon him, the time that I shall visit him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Obadiah 1:5–6; Isaiah 17:6"> If grape-gatherers came to thee, would they not leave some gleaning grapes? if thieves by night, would they not destroy till they had enough?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:14; Malachi 1:3–4; Isaiah 45:3"> But I have made Esau bare, I have uncovered his secret places, and he shall not be able to hide himself: his seed is destroyed, and his brethren, and his neighbors; and he is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 68:5; Hosea 14:3; James 1:27"> Leave thy fatherless children, I will preserve them alive; and let thy widows trust in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:28–29; Obadiah 1:16; Jeremiah 25:15"> For thus saith Jehovah: Behold, they to whom it pertained not to drink of the cup shall assuredly drink; and art thou he that shall altogether go unpunished? thou shalt not go unpunished, but thou shalt surely drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 22:16; Isaiah 34:6; Genesis 36:33"> For I have sworn by myself, saith Jehovah, that Bozrah shall become an astonishment, a reproach, a waste, and a curse; and all the cities thereof shall be perpetual wastes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 30:4; Isaiah 37:7; Ezekiel 7:25–26"> I have heard tidings from Jehovah, and an ambassador is sent among the nations, saying, Gather yourselves together, and come against her, and rise up to the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 1:51; Obadiah 1:2; Micah 7:10"> For, behold, I have made thee small among the nations, and despised among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:25; Amos 9:2; Job 39:27"> As for thy terribleness, the pride of thy heart hath deceived thee, O thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock, that holdest the height of the hill: though thou shouldest make thy nest as high as the eagle, I will bring thee down from thence, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:7; Jeremiah 50:13; Jeremiah 49:13"> And Edom shall become an astonishment: every one that passeth by it shall be astonished, and shall hiss at all the plagues thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:33; Deuteronomy 29:23; Jeremiah 50:40"> As in the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah and the neighbor cities thereof, saith Jehovah, no man shall dwell there, neither shall any son of man sojourn therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:5; Jeremiah 30:21; Jeremiah 50:44–46"> Behold, he shall come up like a lion from the pride of the Jordan against the strong habitation: for I will suddenly make them run away from it; and whoso is chosen, him will I appoint over it: for who is like me? and who will appoint me a time? and who is the shepherd that will stand before me?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:45; Malachi 1:3–4; Jeremiah 49:7"> Therefore hear ye the counsel of Jehovah, that he hath taken against Edom; and his purposes, that he hath purposed against the inhabitants of Teman: Surely they shall drag them away, even the little ones of the flock; surely he shall make their habitation desolate over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:46; Ezekiel 32:10; Isaiah 14:4–15"> The earth trembleth at the noise of their fall; there is a cry, the noise whereof is heard in the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:40–41; Hosea 8:1; Isaiah 13:8"> Behold, he shall come up and fly as the eagle, and spread out his wings against Bozrah: and the heart of the mighty men of Edom at that day shall be as the heart of a woman in her pangs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 10:9; 2 Kings 18:34; Genesis 15:2"> Of Damascus. Hamath is confounded, and Arpad; for they have heard evil tidings, they are melted away: there is sorrow on the sea; it cannot be quiet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Damascus is waxed feeble, she turneth herself to flee, and trembling hath seized on her: anguish and sorrows have taken hold of her, as of a woman in travail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:9; Jeremiah 51:41; Isaiah 1:26"> How is the city of praise not forsaken, the city of my joy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:30; Amos 4:10; Jeremiah 11:22"> Therefore her young men shall fall in her streets, and all the men of war shall be brought to silence in that day, saith Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Amos 1:3–5; 1 Kings 15:18–20; Jeremiah 43:12"> And I will kindle a fire in the wall of Damascus, and it shall devour the palaces of Ben-hadad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 6:3; Genesis 25:13; Isaiah 11:14"> Of Kedar, and of the kingdoms of Hazor, which Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon smote.<br />Thus saith Jehovah: Arise ye, go up to Kedar, and destroy the children of the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:5; Jeremiah 6:25; Habakkuk 3:7"> Their tents and their flocks shall they take; they shall carry away for themselves their curtains, and all their vessels, and their camels; and they shall cry unto them, Terror on every side!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 27:6; Isaiah 10:7"> Flee ye, wander far off, dwell in the depths, O ye inhabitants of Hazor, saith Jehovah; for Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon hath taken counsel against you, and hath conceived a purpose against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:11; Numbers 23:9; Deuteronomy 33:28"> Arise, get you up unto a nation that is at ease, that dwelleth without care, saith Jehovah; that have neither gates nor bars, that dwell alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:26; Jeremiah 25:23; Jeremiah 49:36"> And their camels shall be a booty, and the multitude of their cattle a spoil: and I will scatter unto all winds them that have the corners of their hair cut off; and I will bring their calamity from every side of them, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:22; Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 51:37"> And Hazor shall be a dwelling-place of jackals, a desolation for ever: no man shall dwell there, neither shall any son of man sojourn therein.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 10:22; Daniel 8:2; Jeremiah 25:25"> The word of Jehovah that came to Jeremiah the prophet concerning Elam, in the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 22:6; Jeremiah 51:56; Psalms 46:9"> Thus saith Jehovah of hosts: Behold, I will break the bow of Elam, the chief of their might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:32; Revelation 7:1; Ezekiel 5:10"> And upon Elam will I bring the four winds from the four quarters of heaven, and will scatter them toward all those winds; and there shall be no nation whither the outcasts of Elam shall not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:16; Jeremiah 48:2; Jeremiah 49:22"> And I will cause Elam to be dismayed before their enemies, and before them that seek their life; and I will bring evil upon them, even my fierce anger, saith Jehovah; and I will send the sword after them, till I have consumed them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:10; Daniel 7:9–14"> and I will set my throne in Elam, and will destroy from thence king and princes, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:47; Hosea 3:5; Micah 4:1"> But it shall come to pass in the latter days, that I will bring back the captivity of Elam, saith Jehovah.</span>
</div>
</div>
