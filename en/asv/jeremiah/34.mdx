<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:15; Daniel 2:37–38; 2 Kings 25:1–9"> The word which came unto Jeremiah from Jehovah, when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and all his army, and all the kingdoms of the earth that were under his dominion, and all the peoples, were fighting against Jerusalem, and against all the cities thereof, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 34:22; Jeremiah 37:8–10"> Thus saith Jehovah, the God of Israel, Go, and speak to Zedekiah king of Judah, and tell him, Thus saith Jehovah, Behold, I will give this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he shall burn it with fire:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:4; Jeremiah 21:7; 2 Kings 25:4–7"> and thou shalt not escape out of his hand, but shalt surely be taken, and delivered into his hand; and thine eyes shall behold the eyes of the king of Babylon, and he shall speak with thee mouth to mouth, and thou shalt go to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Yet hear the word of Jehovah, O Zedekiah king of Judah: thus saith Jehovah concerning thee, Thou shalt not die by the sword;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18; 2 Chronicles 16:14; 2 Kings 22:20"> thou shalt die in peace; and with the burnings of thy fathers, the former kings that were before thee, so shall they make a burning for thee; and they shall lament thee, saying, Ah Lord! for I have spoken the word, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:16–24; 1 Samuel 3:18; 1 Kings 21:19"> Then Jeremiah the prophet spake all these words unto Zedekiah king of Judah in Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 10:3; Jeremiah 4:5; 2 Kings 18:13–14"> when the king of Babylon’s army was fighting against Jerusalem, and against all the cities of Judah that were left, against Lachish and against Azekah; for these alone remained of the cities of Judah as fortified cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; 2 Kings 23:2–3; 2 Kings 11:17"> The word that came unto Jeremiah from Jehovah, after that the king Zedekiah had made a covenant with all the people that were at Jerusalem, to proclaim liberty unto them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12; Leviticus 25:39–46; Exodus 3:18"> that every man should let his man-servant, and every man his maid-servant, that is a Hebrew or a Hebrewess, go free; that none should make bondmen of them, to wit, of a Jew his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:16; Jeremiah 26:10; Jeremiah 38:4"> And all the princes and all the people obeyed, that had entered into the covenant, that every one should let his man-servant, and every one his maid-servant, go free, that none should make bondmen of them any more; they obeyed, and let them go:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:20–22; Proverbs 26:11; Matthew 12:43–45"> but afterwards they turned, and caused the servants and the handmaids, whom they had let go free, to return, and brought them into subjection for servants and for handmaids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Therefore the word of Jehovah came to Jeremiah from Jehovah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 6:8; Joshua 24:17; Deuteronomy 5:27"> Thus saith Jehovah, the God of Israel: I made a covenant with your fathers in the day that I brought them forth out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12; 1 Kings 9:22; 2 Kings 17:13–14"> At the end of seven years ye shall let go every man his brother that is a Hebrew, that hath been sold unto thee, and hath served thee six years, thou shalt let him go free from thee: but your fathers hearkened not unto me, neither inclined their ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:8; 2 Kings 23:3; Nehemiah 10:29"> And ye were now turned, and had done that which is right in mine eyes, in proclaiming liberty every man to his neighbor; and ye had made a covenant before me in the house which is called by my name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:12; Exodus 20:7; Jeremiah 34:11"> but ye turned and profaned my name, and caused every man his servant, and every man his handmaid, whom ye had let go free at their pleasure, to return; and ye brought them into subjection, to be unto you for servants and for handmaids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Deuteronomy 28:25; Galatians 6:7"> Therefore thus saith Jehovah: Ye have not hearkened unto me, to proclaim liberty, every man to his brother, and every man to his neighbor: behold, I proclaim unto you a liberty, saith Jehovah, to the sword, to the pestilence, and to the famine; and I will make you to be tossed to and fro among all the kingdoms of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 15:10; Genesis 15:17–18; Hosea 6:7"> And I will give the men that have transgressed my covenant, that have not performed the words of the covenant which they made before me, when they cut the calf in twain and passed between the parts thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:10; Zephaniah 3:3–4; Daniel 9:6"> the princes of Judah, and the princes of Jerusalem, the eunuchs, and the priests, and all the people of the land, that passed between the parts of the calf;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Jeremiah 16:4; Jeremiah 19:7"> I will even give them into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of them that seek their life; and their dead bodies shall be for food unto the birds of the heavens, and to the beasts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:24–27; Jeremiah 39:6; Jeremiah 52:10"> And Zedekiah king of Judah and his princes will I give into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of them that seek their life, and into the hand of the king of Babylon’s army, that are gone away from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 39:8; Jeremiah 39:1–2"> Behold, I will command, saith Jehovah, and cause them to return to this city; and they shall fight against it, and take it, and burn it with fire: and I will make the cities of Judah a desolation, without inhabitant.</span>
