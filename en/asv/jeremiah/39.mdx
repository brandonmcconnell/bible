<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:1–2; Jeremiah 52:4–7; 2 Kings 25:1–12"> (in the ninth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, in the tenth month, came Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon and all his army against Jerusalem, and besieged it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:6–7; Micah 2:12–13; 2 Kings 25:3–4"> in the eleventh year of Zedekiah, in the fourth month, the ninth day of the month, a breach was made in the city),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:4; Jeremiah 38:17; Jeremiah 39:13"> that all the princes of the king of Babylon came in, and sat in the middle gate, to wit, Nergal-sharezer, Samgar-nebo, Sarsechim, Rab-saris, Nergal-sharezer, Rab-mag, with all the rest of the princes of the king of Babylon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 2:14; 2 Chronicles 32:5; 2 Kings 25:4–7"> And it came to pass that, when Zedekiah the king of Judah and all the men of war saw them, then they fled, and went forth out of the city by night, by the way of the king’s garden, through the gate betwixt the two walls; and he went out toward the Arabah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33; Joshua 5:10; Jeremiah 52:26–27"> But the army of the Chaldeans pursued after them, and overtook Zedekiah in the plains of Jericho: and when they had taken him, they brought him up to Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon to Riblah in the land of Hamath; and he gave judgment upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:10; Jeremiah 34:19–21; Jeremiah 24:8–10"> Then the king of Babylon slew the sons of Zedekiah in Riblah before his eyes: also the king of Babylon slew all the nobles of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; 2 Kings 25:7; Jeremiah 52:11"> Moreover he put out Zedekiah’s eyes, and bound him in fetters, to carry him to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:3; Jeremiah 38:18; Jeremiah 21:10"> And the Chaldeans burned the king’s house, and the houses of the people, with fire, and brake down the walls of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:1; Genesis 37:36; Jeremiah 10:18"> Then Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard carried away captive into Babylon the residue of the people that remained in the city, the deserters also that fell away to him, and the residue of the people that remained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 25:12; Jeremiah 40:7; Jeremiah 52:16"> But Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard left of the poor of the people, that had nothing, in the land of Judah, and gave them vineyards and fields at the same time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 24:23; Jeremiah 15:21; Job 5:19"> Now Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon gave charge concerning Jeremiah to Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Jeremiah 40:4; Proverbs 21:1"> Take him, and look well to him, and do him no harm; but do unto him even as he shall say unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:3; Jeremiah 39:9"> So Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard sent, and Nebushazban, Rab-saris, and Nergal-sharezer, Rab-mag, and all the chief officers of the king of Babylon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:28; 2 Kings 22:12; Jeremiah 26:24"> they sent, and took Jeremiah out of the court of the guard, and committed him unto Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, that he should carry him home. So he dwelt among the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:9; Jeremiah 32:1–2; Jeremiah 36:1–5"> Now the word of Jehovah came unto Jeremiah, while he was shut up in the court of the guard, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 9:12; Zechariah 1:6; Joshua 23:14–15"> Go, and speak to Ebed-melech the Ethiopian, saying, Thus saith Jehovah of hosts, the God of Israel: Behold, I will bring my words upon this city for evil, and not for good; and they shall be accomplished before thee in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 41:1–2; Psalms 91:14–15; Psalms 50:15"> But I will deliver thee in that day, saith Jehovah; and thou shalt not be given into the hand of the men of whom thou art afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:2; Psalms 34:22; Jeremiah 21:9"> For I will surely save thee, and thou shalt not fall by the sword, but thy life shall be for a prey unto thee; because thou hast put thy trust in me, saith Jehovah.</span>
</div>
