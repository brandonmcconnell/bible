<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1–4; Ezekiel 16:26; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> They say, If a man put away his wife, and she go from him, and become another man’s, will he return unto her again? will not that land be greatly polluted? But thou hast played the harlot with many lovers; yet return again to me, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:7; Genesis 38:14; Ezekiel 8:4–6"> Lift up thine eyes unto the bare heights, and see; where hast thou not been lain with? By the ways hast thou sat for them, as an Arabian in the wilderness; and thou hast polluted the land with thy whoredoms and with thy wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremiah 14:4; Deuteronomy 28:23"> Therefore the showers have been withholden, and there hath been no latter rain; yet thou hadst a harlot’s forehead, thou refusedst to be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:2; Jeremiah 3:19; Psalms 71:17"> Wilt thou not from this time cry unto me, My Father, thou art the guide of my youth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 7:3; Isaiah 57:16; Ezekiel 22:6"> Will he retain his anger for ever? will he keep it to the end? Behold, thou hast spoken and hast done evil things, and hast had thy way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 17:7–17; Ezekiel 16:31; Jeremiah 7:24"> Moreover Jehovah said unto me in the days of Josiah the king, Hast thou seen that which backsliding Israel hath done? she is gone up upon every high mountain and under every green tree, and there hath played the harlot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:46; Hosea 6:1–4; Hosea 14:1"> And I said after she had done all these things, She will return unto me; but she returned not: and her treacherous sister Judah saw it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1; Hosea 4:15–17; Isaiah 50:1"> And I saw, when, for this very cause that backsliding Israel had committed adultery, I had put her away and given her a bill of divorcement, yet treacherous Judah her sister feared not; but she also went and played the harlot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:27; Isaiah 57:6; Jeremiah 3:2"> And it came to pass through the lightness of her whoredom, that the land was polluted, and she committed adultery with stones and with stocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 7:14; Jeremiah 12:2; 2 Chronicles 34:33–18"> And yet for all this her treacherous sister Judah hath not returned unto me with her whole heart, but feignedly, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:11; Ezekiel 16:51–52; Hosea 11:7"> And Jehovah said unto me, Backsliding Israel hath showed herself more righteous than treacherous Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Ezekiel 33:11; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> Go, and proclaim these words toward the north, and say, Return, thou backsliding Israel, saith Jehovah; I will not look in anger upon you; for I am merciful, saith Jehovah, I will not keep anger for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:25; Jeremiah 3:6; Deuteronomy 12:2"> Only acknowledge thine iniquity, that thou hast transgressed against Jehovah thy God, and hast scattered thy ways to the strangers under every green tree, and ye have not obeyed my voice, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 2:19–20; Jeremiah 2:19; Jeremiah 31:32"> Return, O backsliding children, saith Jehovah; for I am a husband unto you: and I will take you one of a city, and two of a family, and I will bring you to Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:4; Acts 20:28; Ephesians 4:11–12"> And I will give you shepherds according to my heart, who shall feed you with knowledge and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; Amos 9:14–15; Ezekiel 37:26"> And it shall come to pass, when ye are multiplied and increased in the land, in those days, saith Jehovah, they shall say no more, The ark of the covenant of Jehovah; neither shall it come to mind; neither shall they remember it; neither shall they miss it; neither shall it be made any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:7; Micah 4:1–5; Jeremiah 17:12"> At that time they shall call Jerusalem the throne of Jehovah; and all the nations shall be gathered unto it, to the name of Jehovah, to Jerusalem: neither shall they walk any more after the stubbornness of their evil heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:8; Hosea 1:11; Amos 9:15"> In those days the house of Judah shall walk with the house of Israel, and they shall come together out of the land of the north to the land that I gave for an inheritance unto your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:4; Jeremiah 31:9; Isaiah 63:16"> But I said, How I will put thee among the children, and give thee a pleasant land, a goodly heritage of the hosts of the nations! and I said, Ye shall call me My Father, and shall not turn away from following me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 48:8; Jeremiah 5:11; Hosea 5:7"> Surely as a wife treacherously departeth from her husband, so have ye dealt treacherously with me, O house of Israel, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; Jeremiah 31:9; Jeremiah 2:32"> A voice is heard upon the bare heights, the weeping and the supplications of the children of Israel; because they have perverted their way, they have forgotten Jehovah their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 14:1; Hosea 14:4; Zechariah 13:9"> Return, ye backsliding children, I will heal your backslidings. Behold, we are come unto thee; for thou art Jehovah our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 121:1–2; Psalms 3:8; Isaiah 12:2"> Truly in vain is the help that is looked for from the hills, the tumult on the mountains: truly in Jehovah our God is the salvation of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 9:10; Ezekiel 16:63; Ezekiel 16:61"> But the shameful thing hath devoured the labor of our fathers from our youth, their flocks and their herds, their sons and their daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:21; Proverbs 5:13; Isaiah 50:11"> Let us lie down in our shame, and let our confusion cover us; for we have sinned against Jehovah our God, we and our fathers, from our youth even unto this day; and we have not obeyed the voice of Jehovah our God.</span>
