<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 2:12; Jeremiah 3:22; Jeremiah 3:12"> If thou wilt return, O Israel, saith Jehovah, if thou wilt return unto me, and if thou wilt put away thine abominations out of my sight; then shalt thou not be removed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 65:16; Jeremiah 9:24; Genesis 22:18"> and thou shalt swear, As Jehovah liveth, in truth, in justice, and in righteousness; and the nations shall bless themselves in him, and in him shall they glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Luke 8:7; Luke 8:14"> For thus saith Jehovah to the men of Judah and to Jerusalem, Break up your fallow ground, and sow not among thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:16; Deuteronomy 30:6; Romans 2:28–29"> Circumcise yourselves to Jehovah, and take away the foreskins of your heart, ye men of Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem; lest my wrath go forth like fire, and burn so that none can quench it, because of the evil of your doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:14; Jeremiah 6:1; Joshua 10:20"> Declare ye in Judah, and publish in Jerusalem; and say, Blow ye the trumpet in the land: cry aloud and say, Assemble yourselves, and let us go into the fortified cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:2; Jeremiah 1:13–15; Jeremiah 51:27"> Set up a standard toward Zion: flee for safety, stay not; for I will bring evil from the north, and a great destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; Jeremiah 5:6; Isaiah 6:11"> A lion is gone up from his thicket, and a destroyer of nations; he is on his way, he is gone forth from his place, to make thy land desolate, that thy cities be laid waste, without inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 22:12; Jeremiah 6:26; Isaiah 10:4"> For this gird you with sackcloth, lament and wail; for the fierce anger of Jehovah is not turned back from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:9–16; Isaiah 29:9–10; Isaiah 22:3–5"> And it shall come to pass at that day, saith Jehovah, that the heart of the king shall perish, and the heart of the princes; and the priests shall be astonished, and the prophets shall wonder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–12; Isaiah 37:35"> Then said I, Ah, Lord Jehovah! surely thou hast greatly deceived this people and Jerusalem, saying, Ye shall have peace; whereas the sword reacheth unto the life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 13:15; Ezekiel 17:10; Jeremiah 23:19"> At that time shall it be said to this people and to Jerusalem, A hot wind from the bare heights in the wilderness toward the daughter of my people, not to winnow, nor to cleanse;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:16; Ezekiel 5:8; Ezekiel 6:11–13"> a full wind from these shall come for me: now will I also utter judgments against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 19:1; Isaiah 66:15; Habakkuk 1:8"> Behold, he shall come up as clouds, and his chariots shall be as the whirlwind: his horses are swifter than eagles. Woe unto us! for we are ruined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 55:7; Acts 8:22"> O Jerusalem, wash thy heart from wickedness, that thou mayest be saved. How long shall thine evil thoughts lodge within thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:16; Joshua 17:15; Judges 18:29"> For a voice declareth from Dan, and publisheth evil from the hills of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:15; Ezekiel 21:22; Isaiah 39:3"> Make ye mention to the nations; behold, publish against Jerusalem, that watchers come from a far country, and give out their voice against the cities of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:23; 2 Kings 25:1–4; Nehemiah 9:30"> As keepers of a field are they against her round about, because she hath been rebellious against me, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:19; Psalms 107:17; Proverbs 1:31"> Thy way and thy doings have procured these things unto thee; this is thy wickedness; for it is bitter, for it reacheth unto thy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Isaiah 16:11; Isaiah 22:4"> My anguish, my anguish! I am pained at my very heart; my heart is disquieted in me; I cannot hold my peace; because thou hast heard, O my soul, the sound of the trumpet, the alarm of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Matthew 10:28; Joel 1:15"> Destruction upon destruction is cried; for the whole land is laid waste: suddenly are my tents destroyed, and my curtains in a moment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:10; 2 Chronicles 36:3; 2 Chronicles 36:6–7"> How long shall I see the standard, and hear the sound of the trumpet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; Romans 16:19; Jeremiah 5:21"> For my people are foolish, they know me not; they are sottish children, and they have no understanding; they are wise to do evil, but to do good they have no knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Mark 13:24–25; Isaiah 13:10"> I beheld the earth, and, lo, it was waste and void; and the heavens, and they had no light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:20; Isaiah 5:25; Nahum 1:5–6"> I beheld the mountains, and, lo, they trembled, and all the hills moved to and fro.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:2–3; Jeremiah 12:4; Jeremiah 9:10"> I beheld, and, lo, there was no man, and all the birds of the heavens were fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Jeremiah 12:4; Jeremiah 14:2–6"> I beheld, and, lo, the fruitful field was a wilderness, and all the cities thereof were broken down at the presence of Jehovah, and before his fierce anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:28; Jeremiah 5:10; Jeremiah 5:18"> For thus saith Jehovah, The whole land shall be a desolation; yet will I not make a full end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 50:3; Hosea 4:3"> For this shall the earth mourn, and the heavens above be black; because I have spoken it, I have purposed it, and I have not repented, neither will I turn back from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19–21; Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 52:7"> Every city fleeth for the noise of the horsemen and bowmen; they go into the thickets, and climb up upon the rocks: every city is forsaken, and not a man dwelleth therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 9:30; Lamentations 1:2; Lamentations 1:19"> And thou, when thou art made desolate, what wilt thou do? Though thou clothest thyself with scarlet, though thou deckest thee with ornaments of gold, though thou enlargest thine eyes with paint, in vain dost thou make thyself fair; thy lovers despise thee, they seek thy life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 1:17; Isaiah 1:15; Jeremiah 13:21"> For I have heard a voice as of a woman in travail, the anguish as of her that bringeth forth her first child, the voice of the daughter of Zion, that gaspeth for breath, that spreadeth her hands, saying, Woe is me now! for my soul fainteth before the murderers.</span>
