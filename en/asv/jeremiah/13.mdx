<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:11; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 27:2"> Thus saith Jehovah unto me, Go, and buy thee a linen girdle, and put it upon thy loins, and put it not in water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:8; John 15:14; Proverbs 3:5"> So I bought a girdle according to the word of Jehovah, and put it upon my loins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:8"> And the word of Jehovah came unto me the second time, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 4:10; Psalms 137:1; Jeremiah 51:63–64"> Take the girdle that thou hast bought, which is upon thy loins, and arise, go to the Euphrates, and hide it there in a cleft of the rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; Exodus 39:42–43; 2 Timothy 2:3"> So I went, and hid it by the Euphrates, as Jehovah commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And it came to pass after many days, that Jehovah said unto me, Arise, go to the Euphrates, and take the girdle from thence, which I commanded thee to hide there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:12; Isaiah 64:6; Jeremiah 13:10"> Then I went to the Euphrates, and digged, and took the girdle from the place where I had hid it; and, behold, the girdle was marred, it was profitable for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then the word of Jehovah came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremiah 13:15–17; Zephaniah 3:11"> Thus saith Jehovah, After this manner will I mar the pride of Judah, and the great pride of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; Jeremiah 9:14; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16"> This evil people, that refuse to hear my words, that walk in the stubbornness of their heart, and are gone after other gods to serve them, and to worship them, shall even be as this girdle, which is profitable for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Jeremiah 33:9; Psalms 81:11"> For as the girdle cleaveth to the loins of a man, so have I caused to cleave unto me the whole house of Israel and the whole house of Judah, saith Jehovah; that they may be unto me for a people, and for a name, and for a praise, and for a glory: but they would not hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19"> Therefore thou shalt speak unto them this word: Thus saith Jehovah, the God of Israel, Every bottle shall be filled with wine: and they shall say unto thee, Do we not certainly know that every bottle shall be filled with wine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 63:6; Psalms 75:8; Psalms 60:3"> Then shalt thou say unto them, Thus saith Jehovah, Behold, I will fill all the inhabitants of this land, even the kings that sit upon David’s throne, and the priests, and the prophets, and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem, with drunkenness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Psalms 2:9; Jeremiah 19:9–11"> And I will dash them one against another, even the fathers and the sons together, saith Jehovah: I will not pity, nor spare, nor have compassion, that I should not destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 28:14–22; Revelation 2:29; Acts 4:19–20"> Hear ye, and give ear; be not proud; for Jehovah hath spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:9; Isaiah 8:22; Isaiah 5:30"> Give glory to Jehovah your God, before he cause darkness, and before your feet stumble upon the dark mountains, and, while ye look for light, he turn it into the shadow of death, and make it gross darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 2:2; Jeremiah 9:1; Jeremiah 14:17"> But if ye will not hear it, my soul shall weep in secret for your pride; and mine eye shall weep sore, and run down with tears, because Jehovah’s flock is taken captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:26; 2 Kings 24:12; 2 Kings 24:15"> Say thou unto the king and to the queen-mother, Humble yourselves, sit down; for your headtires are come down, even the crown of your glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:27; Jeremiah 39:9; Deuteronomy 28:52"> The cities of the South are shut up, and there is none to open them: Judah is carried away captive, all of it; it is wholly carried away captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:22; Jeremiah 1:14; Habakkuk 1:6"> Lift up your eyes, and behold them that come from the north: where is the flock that was given thee, thy beautiful flock?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 13:8; Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 5:31"> What wilt thou say, when he shall set over thee as head those whom thou hast thyself taught to be friends to thee? shall not sorrows take hold of thee, as of a woman in travail?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nahum 3:5; Jeremiah 16:10–11; Jeremiah 5:19"> And if thou say in thy heart, Wherefore are these things come upon me? for the greatness of thine iniquity are thy skirts uncovered, and thy heels suffer violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; Jeremiah 9:5; Matthew 19:24–28"> Can the Ethiopian change his skin, or the leopard his spots? then may ye also do good, that are accustomed to do evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 1:4; Leviticus 26:33; Ezekiel 5:12"> Therefore will I scatter them, as the stubble that passeth away, by the wind of the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 20:29; Jeremiah 2:32; Psalms 9:17"> This is thy lot, the portion measured unto thee from me, saith Jehovah; because thou hast forgotten me, and trusted in falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Lamentations 1:8; Hosea 2:10; Ezekiel 23:29"> Therefore will I also uncover thy skirts upon thy face, and thy shame shall appear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hosea 8:5; Ezekiel 6:13; Ezekiel 24:13"> I have seen thine abominations, even thine adulteries, and thy neighings, the lewdness of thy whoredom, on the hills in the field. Woe unto thee, O Jerusalem! thou wilt not be made clean; how long shall it yet be?</span>
