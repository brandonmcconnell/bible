<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 4:27; Isaiah 62:4; 1 Samuel 2:5"> Sing, O barren, thou that didst not bear; break forth into singing, and cry aloud, thou that didst not travail with child: for more are the children of the desolate than the children of the married wife, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 33:20; Jeremiah 10:20; Isaiah 49:19–20"> Enlarge the place of thy tent, and let them stretch forth the curtains of thy habitations; spare not: lengthen thy cords, and strengthen thy stakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:35–36; Genesis 28:14; Isaiah 43:5–6"> For thou shalt spread abroad on the right hand and on the left; and thy seed shall possess the nations, and make the desolate cities to be inhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; 1 Peter 2:6; Jeremiah 31:19"> Fear not; for thou shalt not be ashamed: neither be thou confounded; for thou shalt not be put to shame: for thou shalt forget the shame of thy youth; and the reproach of thy widowhood shalt thou remember no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25–27; Hosea 2:19–20; 2 Corinthians 11:2–3"> For thy Maker is thy husband; Jehovah of hosts is his name: and the Holy One of Israel is thy Redeemer; the God of the whole earth shall he be called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 62:4; Ecclesiastes 9:9; Hosea 2:14–15"> For Jehovah hath called thee as a wife forsaken and grieved in spirit, even a wife of youth, when she is cast off, saith thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; 2 Peter 3:8; Micah 4:6"> For a small moment have I forsaken thee; but with great mercies will I gather thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:3; Isaiah 60:10; Ezekiel 39:23–24"> In overflowing wrath I hid my face from thee for a moment; but with everlasting lovingkindness will I have mercy on thee, saith Jehovah thy Redeemer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 12:1; Genesis 8:21; Hebrews 6:16–18"> For this is as the waters of Noah unto me; for as I have sworn that the waters of Noah shall no more go over the earth, so have I sworn that I will not be wroth with thee, nor rebuke thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 89:33–34; Psalms 46:2; Ephesians 2:4–5"> For the mountains may depart, and the hills be removed; but my lovingkindness shall not depart from thee, neither shall my covenant of peace be removed, saith Jehovah that hath mercy on thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–6; Acts 14:22; Jeremiah 30:17"> O thou afflicted, tossed with tempest, and not comforted, behold, I will set thy stones in fair colors, and lay thy foundations with sapphires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And I will make thy pinnacles of rubies, and thy gates of carbuncles, and all thy border of precious stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:45; Philippians 4:7; Psalms 119:165"> And all thy children shall be taught of Jehovah; and great shall be the peace of thy children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 3:25–26; Jeremiah 30:10; Zechariah 9:8"> In righteousness shalt thou be established: thou shalt be far from oppression, for thou shalt not fear; and from terror, for it shall not come near thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 41:11–16; Psalms 37:12–13; Zechariah 12:9"> Behold, they may gather together, but not by me: whosoever shall gather together against thee shall fall because of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Proverbs 16:4; Isaiah 46:11"> Behold, I have created the smith that bloweth the fire of coals, and bringeth forth a weapon for his work; and I have created the waster to destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:28–39; Isaiah 54:15; John 10:28–30"> No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper; and every tongue that shall rise against thee in judgment thou shalt condemn. This is the heritage of the servants of Jehovah, and their righteousness which is of me, saith Jehovah.</span>
