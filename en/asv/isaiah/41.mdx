<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Habakkuk 2:20; Zechariah 2:13"> Keep silence before me, O islands; and let the peoples renew their strength: let them come near; then let them speak; let us come near together to judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; 2 Samuel 22:43; Ezra 1:2"> Who hath raised up one from the east, whom he calleth in righteousness to his foot? he giveth nations before him, and maketh him rule over kings; he giveth them as the dust to his sword, as the driven stubble to his bow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 5:24; Isaiah 57:2"> He pursueth them, and passeth on safely, even by a way that he had not gone with his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 48:12; Revelation 22:13; Revelation 1:17"> Who hath wrought and done it, calling the generations from the beginning? I, Jehovah, the first, and with the last, I am he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:15–16; Psalms 65:8; Psalms 66:3"> The isles have seen, and fear; the ends of the earth tremble; they draw near, and come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 44:12; Isaiah 40:19; Daniel 3:1–7"> They help every one his neighbor; and every one saith to his brother, Be of good courage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 40:19–20; Daniel 3:1–7; Judges 18:17–18"> So the carpenter encourageth the goldsmith, and he that smootheth with the hammer him that smiteth the anvil, saying of the soldering, It is good; and he fasteneth it with nails, that it should not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 2:23; 2 Chronicles 20:7; Deuteronomy 14:2"> But thou, Israel, my servant, Jacob whom I have chosen, the seed of Abraham my friend,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:22; Psalms 94:14; Deuteronomy 7:6–7"> thou whom I have taken hold of from the ends of the earth, and called from the corners thereof, and said unto thee, Thou art my servant, I have chosen thee and not cast thee away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 43:1–2; Romans 8:31"> fear thou not, for I am with thee; be not dismayed, for I am thy God; I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee; yea, I will uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 23:22; Isaiah 45:24; Revelation 3:9"> Behold, all they that are incensed against thee shall be put to shame and confounded: they that strive with thee shall be as nothing, and shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:35–36; Job 20:7–9; Isaiah 17:14"> Thou shalt seek them, and shalt not find them, even them that contend with thee: they that war against thee shall be as nothing, and as a thing of nought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 73:23; Psalms 63:8"> For I, Jehovah thy God, will hold thy right hand, saying unto thee, Fear not; I will help thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 43:14; Psalms 22:6; Isaiah 54:5"> Fear not, thou worm Jacob, and ye men of Israel; I will help thee, saith Jehovah, and thy Redeemer is the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 4:13; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Zechariah 4:7"> Behold, I have made thee to be a new sharp threshing instrument having teeth; thou shalt thresh the mountains, and beat them small, and shalt make the hills as chaff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:2; Isaiah 61:10–11; Isaiah 45:24–25"> Thou shalt winnow them, and the wind shall carry them away, and the whirlwind shall scatter them; and thou shalt rejoice in Jehovah, thou shalt glory in the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 55:1; Isaiah 30:19; Isaiah 44:3"> The poor and needy seek water, and there is none, and their tongue faileth for thirst; I, Jehovah, will answer them, I, the God of Israel, will not forsake them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 107:35; Isaiah 35:6–7; Isaiah 58:11"> I will open rivers on the bare heights, and fountains in the midst of the valleys; I will make the wilderness a pool of water, and the dry land springs of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 55:13; Isaiah 37:31–32; Isaiah 35:1"> I will put in the wilderness the cedar, the acacia, and the myrtle, and the oil-tree; I will set in the desert the fir-tree, the pine, and the box-tree together:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 12:9; Isaiah 44:23; Isaiah 43:21"> that they may see, and know, and consider, and understand together, that the hand of Jehovah hath done this, and the Holy One of Israel hath created it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 23:3–4; Job 38:3; Micah 6:1–2"> Produce your cause, saith Jehovah; bring forth your strong reasons, saith the King of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 45:21; Isaiah 48:14; Isaiah 44:7"> Let them bring forth, and declare unto us what shall happen: declare ye the former things, what they are, that we may consider them, and know the latter end of them; or show us things to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:19; Isaiah 42:9; Jeremiah 10:5"> Declare the things that are to come hereafter, that we may know that ye are gods: yea, do good, or do evil, that we may be dismayed, and behold it together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 115:8; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 41:29"> Behold, ye are of nothing, and your work is of nought; an abomination is he that chooseth you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:2; 2 Samuel 22:43; Isaiah 10:6"> I have raised up one from the north, and he is come; from the rising of the sun one that calleth upon my name: and he shall come upon rulers as upon mortar, and as the potter treadeth clay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 44:7; Isaiah 41:22; Isaiah 45:21"> Who hath declared it from the beginning, that we may know? and beforetime, that we may say, He is right? yea, there is none that declareth, yea, there is none that showeth, yea, there is none that heareth your words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Isaiah 52:7; Isaiah 44:28"> I am the first that saith unto Zion, Behold, behold them; and I will give to Jerusalem one that bringeth good tidings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 63:5; Daniel 2:10–11; Isaiah 40:13–14"> And when I look, there is no man: even among them there is no counsellor, that, when I ask of them, can answer a word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:13; Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 10:2–16"> Behold, all of them, their works are vanity and nought; their molten images are wind and confusion.</span>
