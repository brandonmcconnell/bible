<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Micah 3:1–4; Matthew 23:23"> Woe unto them that decree unrighteous decrees, and to the writers that write perverseness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Isaiah 3:14; Isaiah 29:21"> to turn aside the needy from justice, and to rob the poor of my people of their right, that widows may be their spoil, and that they may make the fatherless their prey!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 9:7; Luke 19:44; Job 31:14"> And what will ye do in the day of visitation, and in the desolation which shall come from far? to whom will ye flee for help? and where will ye leave your glory?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25; Isaiah 9:12; Jeremiah 37:10"> They shall only bow down under the prisoners, and shall fall under the slain. For all this his anger is not turned away, but his hand is stretched out still.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:20–24; Isaiah 14:25; Isaiah 30:30"> Ho Assyrian, the rod of mine anger, the staff in whose hand is mine indignation!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 9:17; Jeremiah 25:9; Zechariah 10:5"> I will send him against a profane nation, and against the people of my wrath will I give him a charge, to take the spoil, and to take the prey, and to tread them down like the mire of the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Micah 4:11–12; Acts 2:23"> Howbeit he meaneth not so, neither doth his heart think so; but it is in his heart to destroy, and to cut off nations not a few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 18:24; 2 Kings 19:10; Ezekiel 26:7"> For he saith, Are not my princes all of them kings?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:20; 2 Kings 16:9; Genesis 10:10"> Is not Calno as Carchemish? is not Hamath as Arpad? is not Samaria as Damascus?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 10:14; 2 Kings 19:17–19; 2 Kings 18:33–35"> As my hand hath found the kingdoms of the idols, whose graven images did excel them of Jerusalem and of Samaria;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 37:10–13; Isaiah 2:8; Isaiah 36:19–20"> shall I not, as I have done unto Samaria and her idols, so do to Jerusalem and her idols?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 19:31; Isaiah 65:7; Jeremiah 50:18"> Wherefore it shall come to pass, that, when the Lord hath performed his whole work upon mount Zion and on Jerusalem, I will punish the fruit of the stout heart of the king of Assyria, and the glory of his high looks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:17; Daniel 4:30; Ezekiel 26:2"> For he hath said, By the strength of my hand I have done it, and by my wisdom; for I have understanding: and I have removed the bounds of the peoples, and have robbed their treasures, and like a valiant man I have brought down them that sit on thrones:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 31:25; Proverbs 21:6–7; Nahum 2:9–1"> and my hand hath found as a nest the riches of the peoples; and as one gathereth eggs that are forsaken, have I gathered all the earth: and there was none that moved the wing, or that opened the mouth, or chirped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 9:20–21; Isaiah 45:9; Isaiah 10:5"> Shall the axe boast itself against him that heweth therewith? shall the saw magnify itself against him that wieldeth it? as if a rod should wield them that lift it up, or as if a staff should lift up him that is not wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:21; Acts 12:23; Psalms 106:15"> Therefore will the Lord, Jehovah of hosts, send among his fat ones leanness; and under his glory there shall be kindled a burning like the burning of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 27:4; Numbers 11:1–3; Isaiah 37:23"> And the light of Israel will be for a fire, and his Holy One for a flame; and it will burn and devour his thorns and his briers in one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 19:23; Isaiah 10:33–34; Isaiah 9:18"> And he will consume the glory of his forest, and of his fruitful field, both soul and body: and it shall be as when a standard-bearer fainteth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 21:17; Isaiah 37:36"> And the remnant of the trees of his forest shall be few, so that a child may write them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 37:31–32; Isaiah 50:10; Isaiah 1:9"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that the remnant of Israel, and they that are escaped of the house of Jacob, shall no more again lean upon him that smote them, but shall lean upon Jehovah, the Holy One of Israel, in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; 2 Corinthians 3:14–16; Hosea 7:10"> A remnant shall return, even the remnant of Jacob, unto the mighty God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 9:27–28; Isaiah 6:13; Revelation 20:8"> For though thy people, Israel, be as the sand of the sea, only a remnant of them shall return: a destruction is determined, overflowing with righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 28:22; Isaiah 24:1–23; Romans 9:28"> For a full end, and that determined, will the Lord, Jehovah of hosts, make in the midst of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 12:22–24; Isaiah 37:6; Isaiah 61:3"> Therefore thus saith the Lord, Jehovah of hosts, O my people that dwellest in Zion, be not afraid of the Assyrian, though he smite thee with the rod, and lift up his staff against thee, after the manner of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 11:36; 2 Kings 19:35; Hebrews 10:37"> For yet a very little while, and the indignation against thee shall be accomplished, and mine anger shall be directed to his destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Judges 7:25; 2 Kings 19:35"> And Jehovah of hosts will stir up against him a scourge, as in the slaughter of Midian at the rock of Oreb: and his rod will be over the sea, and he will lift it up after the manner of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Luke 4:18; Isaiah 14:25"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that his burden shall depart from off thy shoulder, and his yoke from off thy neck, and the yoke shall be destroyed by reason of fatness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:2; 1 Samuel 13:2; 1 Samuel 13:5"> He is come to Aiath, he is passed through Migron; at Michmash he layeth up his baggage;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:23; 1 Samuel 7:17; Joshua 21:17"> they are gone over the pass; they have taken up their lodging at Geba; Ramah trembleth; Gibeah of Saul is fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:44; Jeremiah 1:1; Joshua 21:18"> Cry aloud with thy voice, O daughter of Gallim! hearken, O Laishah! O thou poor Anathoth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 15:31"> Madmenah is a fugitive; the inhabitants of Gebim flee for safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:1; Isaiah 37:22; Isaiah 19:16"> This very day shall he halt at Nob: he shaketh his hand at the mount of the daughter of Zion, the hill of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Daniel 4:37; Isaiah 37:38"> Behold, the Lord, Jehovah of hosts, will lop the boughs with terror: and the high of stature shall be hewn down, and the lofty shall be brought low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 37:24; Daniel 4:23; Jeremiah 48:2"> And he will cut down the thickets of the forest with iron, and Lebanon shall fall by a mighty one.</span>
</div>
