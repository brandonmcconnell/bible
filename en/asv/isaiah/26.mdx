<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 60:18; Isaiah 62:11; Ephesians 5:19–20"> In that day shall this song be sung in the land of Judah: We have a strong city; salvation will he appoint for walls and bulwarks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Isaiah 60:11; 1 Peter 2:9"> Open ye the gates, that the righteous nation which keepeth faith may enter in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 4:7; John 16:33; John 14:27"> Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee; because he trusteth in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 62:8; Psalms 55:22"> Trust ye in Jehovah for ever; for in Jehovah, even Jehovah, is an everlasting rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:37; Revelation 18:2; Job 40:11–13"> For he hath brought down them that dwell on high, the lofty city: he layeth it low, he layeth it low even to the ground; he bringeth it even to the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Luke 10:19; Revelation 2:26"> The foot shall tread it down; even the feet of the poor, and the steps of the needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Isaiah 42:16; 1 John 3:7"> The way of the just is uprightness: thou that art upright dost direct the path of the just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 37:3–7; James 5:7–11; Isaiah 33:2"> Yea, in the way of thy judgments, O Jehovah, have we waited for thee; to thy name, even to thy memorial name, is the desire of our soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Psalms 63:1; Psalms 119:62"> With my soul have I desired thee in the night; yea, with my spirit within me will I seek thee earnestly: for when thy judgments are in the earth, the inhabitants of the world learn righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 11:7; John 5:37–38; Romans 2:4–5"> Let favor be showed to the wicked, yet will he not learn righteousness; in the land of uprightness will he deal wrongfully, and will not behold the majesty of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:27; Micah 5:9; Isaiah 5:24"> Jehovah, thy hand is lifted up, yet they see not: but they shall see thy zeal for the people, and be put to shame; yea, fire shall devour thine adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Psalms 57:2; Psalms 29:11"> Jehovah, thou wilt ordain peace for us; for thou hast also wrought all our works for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 63:7; Isaiah 2:8; Isaiah 10:11"> O Jehovah our God, other lords besides thee have had dominion over us; but by thee only will we make mention of thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 18:2–3; Proverbs 10:7; Isaiah 51:12–13"> They are dead, they shall not live; they are deceased, they shall not rise: therefore hast thou visited and destroyed them, and made all remembrance of them to perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:3; Isaiah 33:17; Nehemiah 9:23"> Thou hast increased the nation, O Jehovah, thou hast increased the nation; thou art glorified; thou hast enlarged all the borders of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Psalms 50:15; Psalms 77:1–2"> Jehovah, in trouble have they visited thee; they poured out a prayer when thy chastening was upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 21:3; Isaiah 13:8; John 16:21"> Like as a woman with child, that draweth near the time of her delivery, is in pain and crieth out in her pangs; so we have been before thee, O Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 13:13; Psalms 17:14; 2 Kings 19:3"> We have been with child, we have been in pain, we have as it were brought forth wind; we have not wrought any deliverance in the earth; neither have the inhabitants of the world fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Ezekiel 37:1–14; Matthew 27:52"> Thy dead shall live; my dead bodies shall arise. Awake and sing, ye that dwell in the dust; for thy dew is as the dew of herbs, and the earth shall cast forth the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; Psalms 27:5; Isaiah 54:7–8"> Come, my people, enter thou into thy chambers, and shut thy doors about thee: hide thyself for a little moment, until the indignation be overpast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 6:9–11; Isaiah 13:11; Jude 1:14–15"> For, behold, Jehovah cometh forth out of his place to punish the inhabitants of the earth for their iniquity: the earth also shall disclose her blood, and shall no more cover her slain.</span>
