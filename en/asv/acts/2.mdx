<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 2:3; 1 Corinthians 16:9; Acts 2:2"> And when this sound was heard, the multitude came together, and were confounded, because that every man heard them speaking in his own language.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 2:12; Acts 1:11; John 7:52"> And they were all amazed and marvelled, saying, Behold, are not all these that speak Galilæans?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And how hear we, every man in our own language wherein we were born?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 1:1; Acts 18:2; Revelation 1:4"> Parthians and Medes and Elamites, and the dwellers in Mesopotamia, in Judæa and Cappadocia, in Pontus and Asia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 16:6; Zechariah 8:23; Zechariah 8:20"> in Phrygia and Pamphylia, in Egypt and the parts of Libya about Cyrene, and sojourners from Rome, both Jews and proselytes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 78:4"> Cretans and Arabians, we hear them speaking in our tongues the mighty works of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 15:26; Acts 17:20; Luke 18:36"> And they were all amazed, and were perplexed, saying one to another, What meaneth this?</span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Samuel 1:15; Matthew 20:3"> For these are not drunken, as ye suppose; seeing it is but the third hour of the day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32"> but this is that which hath been spoken through the prophet Joel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32; Isaiah 44:3; Titus 3:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">And it shall be in the last days, saith God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will pour forth of my Spirit upon all flesh:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And your sons and your daughters shall prophesy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And your young men shall see visions,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And your old men shall dream dreams:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11; 1 Corinthians 7:21–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yea and on my servants and on my handmaidens in those days</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Will I pour forth of my Spirit; and they shall prophesy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:30–31; Zephaniah 1:14–18; Malachi 4:1–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will show wonders in the heaven above,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And signs on the earth beneath;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Blood, and fire, and vapor of smoke:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Amos 8:9; Matthew 24:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sun shall be turned into darkness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the moon into blood,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Before the day of the Lord come,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That great and notable day:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 10:12–13; Joel 2:32; Psalms 86:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">And it shall be, that whosoever shall call on the name of the Lord shall be saved.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 3:2; John 14:10–11; Matthew 12:28"> Ye men of Israel, hear these words: Jesus of Nazareth, a man approved of God unto you by mighty works and wonders and signs which God did by him in the midst of you, even as ye yourselves know;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:22; Acts 4:28; Acts 3:18"> him, being delivered up by the determinate counsel and foreknowledge of God, ye by the hand of lawless men did crucify and slay:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:14; 1 Peter 1:21; Acts 2:32"> whom God raised up, having loosed the pangs of death: because it was not possible that he should be holden of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 16:8–11; Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 62:6"> For David saith concerning him,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">I beheld the Lord always before my face;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For he is on my right hand, that I should not be moved:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 16:9; Psalms 63:5; Psalms 71:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore my heart was glad, and my tongue rejoiced;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Moreover my flesh also shall dwell in hope:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Acts 2:31; Revelation 1:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because thou wilt not leave my soul unto Hades,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Neither wilt thou give thy Holy One to see corruption.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 25:4; Psalms 21:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou madest known unto me the ways of life;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Thou shalt make me full of gladness with thy countenance.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 13:36; 1 Kings 2:10; Acts 7:8–9"> Brethren, I may say unto you freely of the patriarch David, that he both died and was buried, and his tomb is with us unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 132:11–18; Psalms 89:3–4; Jeremiah 33:14–15"> Being therefore a prophet, and knowing that God had sworn with an oath to him, that of the fruit of his loins he would set one upon his throne;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 2:27; Psalms 16:10; Acts 13:35"> he foreseeing this spake of the resurrection of the Christ, that neither was he left unto Hades, nor did his flesh see corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 3:15; Acts 1:8; Acts 1:22"> This Jesus did God raise up, whereof we all are witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:4; Acts 5:31; Acts 2:17"> Being therefore by the right hand of God exalted, and having received of the Father the promise of the Holy Spirit, he hath poured forth this, which ye see and hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Matthew 22:42–45; Hebrews 1:13"> For David ascended not into the heavens: but he saith himself,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">The Lord said unto my Lord, Sit thou on my right hand,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 19:19–3; Romans 16:20; Isaiah 60:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Till I make thine enemies the footstool of thy feet.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 5:30–31; Romans 14:8–12; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> Let all the house of Israel therefore know assuredly, that God hath made him both Lord and Christ, this Jesus whom ye crucified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 3:10; Acts 22:10; Hebrews 4:12–13"> Now when they heard this, they were pricked in their heart, and said unto Peter and the rest of the apostles, Brethren, what shall we do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 22:16; Mark 16:16; Acts 8:12"> And Peter said unto them, Repent ye, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ unto the remission of your sins; and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 8:30"> For to you is the promise, and to your children, and to all that are afar off, even as many as the Lord our God shall call unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Deuteronomy 32:5; Matthew 17:17"> And with many other words he testified, and exhorted them, saying, Save yourselves from this crooked generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 4:4; Acts 2:47; Acts 16:31–34"> They then that received his word were baptized: and there were added unto them in that day about three thousand souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; Acts 1:14; 1 John 1:3"> And they continued stedfastly in the apostles’ teaching and fellowship, in the breaking of bread and the prayers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 7:16; John 14:12; Luke 8:37"> And fear came upon every soul: and many wonders and signs were done through the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 4:32; 1 John 3:16–18; 2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> And all that believed were together, and had all things common;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 John 3:17; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; James 2:14–16"> and they sold their possessions and goods, and parted them to all, according as any man had need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 5:42; Acts 20:7; Acts 2:42"> And day by day, continuing stedfastly with one accord in the temple, and breaking bread at home, they took their food with gladness and singleness of heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 16:5; Acts 5:13–14; Acts 2:41"> praising God, and having favor with all the people. And the Lord added to them day by day those that were saved.</span>
</div>
