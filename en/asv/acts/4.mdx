<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 22:4; Acts 6:12; Acts 23:6–9"> And as they spake unto the people, the priests and the captain of the temple and the Sadducees came upon them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 17:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–14; Acts 24:14–15"> being sore troubled because they taught the people, and proclaimed in Jesus the resurrection from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 5:18; Matthew 10:16–17; Acts 16:19–24"> And they laid hands on them, and put them in ward unto the morrow: for it was now eventide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 2:41; 2 Corinthians 2:14–17; 2 Timothy 2:9–10"> But many of them that heard the word believed; and the number of the men came to be about five thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 4:8; Matthew 27:1–2; Luke 20:1"> And it came to pass on the morrow, that their rulers and elders and scribes were gathered together in Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 3:2; John 18:24; Matthew 26:3"> and Annas the high priest was there, and Caiaphas, and John, and Alexander, and as many as were of the kindred of the high priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 11:28; Acts 5:40"> And when they had set them in the midst, they inquired, By what power, or in what name, have ye done this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–20; Acts 2:4"> Then Peter, filled with the Holy Spirit, said unto them, Ye rulers of the people, and elders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15–17; Acts 3:7; John 10:32"> if we this day are examined concerning a good deed done to an impotent man, by what means this man is made whole;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:29–32; Acts 3:6; Acts 2:36"> be it known unto you all, and to all the people of Israel, that in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, whom ye crucified, whom God raised from the dead, even in him doth this man stand here before you whole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Isaiah 28:16; Mark 12:10–12"> He is the stone which was set at nought of you the builders, which was made the head of the corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 14:6; 1 Corinthians 3:11; John 3:36"> And in none other is there salvation: for neither is there any other name under heaven, that is given among men, wherein we must be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27; John 7:15; Matthew 11:25"> Now when they beheld the boldness of Peter and John, and had perceived that they were unlearned and ignorant men, they marvelled; and they took knowledge of them, that they had been with Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 19:36; Acts 3:8–12; Acts 4:21"> And seeing the man that was healed standing with them, they could say nothing against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 26:30–32; Acts 5:34–42; Matthew 5:22"> But when they had commanded them to go aside out of the council, they conferred among themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10; John 12:18"> saying, What shall we do to these men? for that indeed a notable miracle hath been wrought through them, is manifest to all that dwell in Jerusalem; and we cannot deny it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:28; Romans 15:18–22; Acts 5:24"> But that it spread no further among the people, let us threaten them, that they speak henceforth to no man in this name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:40; Luke 24:46–48; Acts 5:20"> And they called them, and charged them not to speak at all nor teach in the name of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Exodus 1:17; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> But Peter and John answered and said unto them, Whether it is right in the sight of God to hearken unto you rather than unto God, judge ye:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 22:15; Jeremiah 20:9; 1 Corinthians 9:16–17"> for we cannot but speak the things which we saw and heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 5:26; Matthew 21:46; Luke 22:2"> And they, when they had further threatened them, let them go, finding nothing how they might punish them, because of the people; for all men glorified God for that which was done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 5:5; Acts 9:33; Luke 13:11"> For the man was more than forty years old, on whom this miracle of healing was wrought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Acts 12:11–12; Psalms 119:63"> And being let go, they came to their own company, and reported all that the chief priests and the elders had said unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Jeremiah 32:17; Nehemiah 9:6"> And they, when they heard it, lifted up their voice to God with one accord, and said, O Lord, thou that didst make the heaven and the earth and the sea, and all that in them is:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–6; Acts 1:16; Acts 2:30"> who by the Holy Spirit, by the mouth of our father David thy servant, didst say,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why did the Gentiles rage,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the peoples imagine vain things?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 2:2; Revelation 17:12–14; Daniel 9:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The kings of the earth set themselves in array,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the rulers were gathered together,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Against the Lord, and against his Anointed:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Acts 4:30; Luke 9:22"> for of a truth in this city against thy holy Servant Jesus, whom thou didst anoint, both Herod and Pontius Pilate, with the Gentiles and the peoples of Israel, were gathered together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:23; Isaiah 46:10; Genesis 50:20"> to do whatsoever thy hand and thy counsel foreordained to come to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:13; Acts 14:3; Philippians 1:14"> And now, Lord, look upon their threatenings: and grant unto thy servants to speak thy word with all boldness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 4:27; Acts 3:6; Acts 5:12"> while thou stretchest forth thy hand to heal; and that signs and wonders may be done through the name of thy holy Servant Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Acts 2:2; Acts 2:4"> And when they had prayed, the place was shaken wherein they were gathered together; and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit, and they spake the word of God with boldness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:10; Ephesians 4:2–6; 1 Peter 3:8"> And the multitude of them that believed were of one heart and soul: and not one of them said that aught of the things which he possessed was his own; but they had all things common.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 1:16; Acts 1:22; Acts 1:8"> And with great power gave the apostles their witness of the resurrection of the Lord Jesus: and great grace was upon them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 2:45; Luke 12:33; Luke 16:9"> For neither was there among them any that lacked: for as many as were possessors of lands or houses sold them, and brought the prices of the things that were sold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 5:2; Acts 2:45; 2 Corinthians 8:20–21"> and laid them at the apostles’ feet: and distribution was made unto each, according as any one had need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Galatians 2:9; Galatians 2:13; Galatians 2:1"> And Joseph, who by the apostles was surnamed Barnabas (which is, being interpreted, Son of exhortation), a Levite, a man of Cyprus by race,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 5:1–2; Acts 4:34–35; Matthew 19:29"> having a field, sold it, and brought the money and laid it at the apostles’ feet.</span>
