<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 27:26; Acts 27:44; Acts 27:39"> And when we were escaped, then we knew that the island was called Melita.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:14; Hebrews 13:2; Colossians 3:11"> And the barbarians showed us no common kindness: for they kindled a fire, and received us all, because of the present rain, and because of the cold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 23:33; Matthew 12:34; Acts 28:4"> But when Paul had gathered a bundle of sticks and laid them on the fire, a viper came out by reason of the heat, and fastened on his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 13:2; Luke 13:4; John 7:24"> And when the barbarians saw the venomous creature hanging from his hand, they said one to another, No doubt this man is a murderer, whom, though he hath escaped from the sea, yet Justice hath not suffered to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 10:19; Mark 16:18; Psalms 91:13"> Howbeit he shook off the creature into the fire, and took no harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 12:22; Acts 14:11–13; Matthew 21:9"> But they expected that he would have swollen, or fallen down dead suddenly: but when they were long in expectation and beheld nothing amiss come to him, they changed their minds, and said that he was a god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:40–41; Luke 19:6–9; Acts 28:2"> Now in the neighborhood of that place were lands belonging to the chief man of the island, named Publius; who received us, and entertained us three days courteously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:40; Mark 6:5; James 5:14–16"> And it was so, that the father of Publius lay sick of fever and dysentery: unto whom Paul entered in, and prayed, and laying his hands on him healed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 5:12; Mark 6:54–56; Acts 5:15"> And when this was done, the rest also that had diseases in the island came, and were cured:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; Philippians 4:11–12; Matthew 6:31–34"> who also honored us with many honors; and when we sailed, they put on board such things as we needed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 27:6; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 45:20"> And after three months we set sail in a ship of Alexandria which had wintered in the island, whose sign was The Twin Brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And touching at Syracuse, we tarried there three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 27:13"> And from thence we made a circuit, and arrived at Rhegium: and after one day a south wind sprang up, and on the second day we came to Puteoli;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 21:4; Acts 20:6; Acts 19:1"> where we found brethren, and were entreated to tarry with them seven days: and so we came to Rome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:3; Romans 15:24; Exodus 4:14"> And from thence the brethren, when they heard of us, came to meet us as far as The Market of Appius and The Three Taverns; whom when Paul saw, he thanked God, and took courage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 27:3; Acts 24:23; Acts 28:30–31"> And when we entered into Rome, Paul was suffered to abide by himself with the soldier that guarded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 25:8; Acts 6:14; Acts 25:10"> And it came to pass, that after three days he called together those that were the chief of the Jews: and when they were come together, he said unto them, I, brethren, though I had done nothing against the people, or the customs of our fathers, yet was delivered prisoner from Jerusalem into the hands of the Romans:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:31–32; Acts 23:29; Acts 22:24–25"> who, when they had examined me, desired to set me at liberty, because there was no cause of death in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 26:32; Acts 25:21; Acts 25:10–12"> But when the Jews spake against it, I was constrained to appeal unto Cæsar; not that I had aught whereof to accuse my nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:9; Acts 26:6–7"> For this cause therefore did I entreat you to see and to speak with me: for because of the hope of Israel I am bound with this chain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 54:17; Isaiah 41:11; Isaiah 50:8"> And they said unto him, We neither received letters from Judæa concerning thee, nor did any of the brethren come hither and report or speak any harm of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Acts 24:14; 1 Peter 3:16"> But we desire to hear of thee what thou thinkest: for as concerning this sect, it is known to us that everywhere it is spoken against.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 17:2–3; Acts 19:8; Acts 18:28"> And when they had appointed him a day, they came to him into his lodging in great number; to whom he expounded the matter, testifying the kingdom of God, and persuading them concerning Jesus, both from the law of Moses and from the prophets, from morning till evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 14:4; Acts 17:4–5; Acts 19:8–9"> And some believed the things which were spoken, and some disbelieved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 15:7; 2 Peter 1:21; Mark 7:6"> And when they agreed not among themselves, they departed after that Paul had spoken one word, Well spake the Holy Spirit through Isaiah the prophet unto your fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Ezekiel 12:2; Isaiah 42:19–20"> saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Go thou unto this people, and say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">By hearing ye shall hear, and shall in no wise understand;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And seeing ye shall see, and shall in no wise perceive:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">For this people’s heart is waxed gross,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And their ears are dull of hearing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And their eyes they have closed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Lest haply they should perceive with their eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And hear with their ears,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And understand with their heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And should turn again,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And I should heal them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:30–32; Acts 26:17–18; Romans 3:29–30"> Be it known therefore unto you, that this salvation of God is sent unto the Gentiles: they will also hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some ancient authorities insert v. 29: And when he had said these words, the Jews departed, having much disputing among themselves.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And he abode two whole years in his own hired dwelling, and received all that went in unto him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 4:29; Ephesians 6:19–20; Matthew 4:23"> preaching the kingdom of God, and teaching the things concerning the Lord Jesus Christ with all boldness, none forbidding him.</span>
