<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Moses went and spake these words unto all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:7; Numbers 27:17; 1 Kings 3:7"> And he said unto them, I am a hundred and twenty years old this day; I can no more go out and come in: and Jehovah hath said unto me, Thou shalt not go over this Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:3; Deuteronomy 31:23; Deuteronomy 3:28"> Jehovah thy God, he will go over before thee; he will destroy these nations from before thee, and thou shalt dispossess them: and Joshua, he shall go over before thee, as Jehovah hath spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 23:28–31; Deuteronomy 2:33; Deuteronomy 3:3–11"> And Jehovah will do unto them as he did to Sihon and to Og, the kings of the Amorites, and unto their land; whom he destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Deuteronomy 20:16–17; Exodus 23:32–33"> And Jehovah will deliver them up before you, and ye shall do unto them according unto all the commandment which I have commanded you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:10; Psalms 27:14"> Be strong and of good courage, fear not, nor be affrighted at them: for Jehovah thy God, he it is that doth go with thee; he will not fail thee, nor forsake thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Deuteronomy 31:6; Deuteronomy 1:38"> And Moses called unto Joshua, and said unto him in the sight of all Israel, Be strong and of good courage: for thou shalt go with this people into the land which Jehovah hath sworn unto their fathers to give them; and thou shalt cause them to inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Deuteronomy 31:6; Joshua 1:9"> And Jehovah, he it is that doth go before thee; he will be with thee, he will not fail thee, neither forsake thee: fear not, neither be dismayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Joshua 3:3; Deuteronomy 17:18"> And Moses wrote this law, and delivered it unto the priests the sons of Levi, that bare the ark of the covenant of Jehovah, and unto all the elders of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 23:34–43; Deuteronomy 15:1–2"> And Moses commanded them, saying, At the end of every seven years, in the set time of the year of release, in the feast of tabernacles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 23:2; Joshua 8:34–35; Deuteronomy 16:16–17"> when all Israel is come to appear before Jehovah thy God in the place which he shall choose, thou shalt read this law before all Israel in their hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:10; Psalms 34:11–14; Deuteronomy 6:6–7"> Assemble the people, the men and the women and the little ones, and thy sojourner that is within thy gates, that they may hear, and that they may learn, and fear Jehovah your God, and observe to do all the words of this law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:2; Deuteronomy 6:7; Proverbs 22:6"> and that their children, who have not known, may hear, and learn to fear Jehovah your God, as long as ye live in the land whither ye go over the Jordan to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 27:13; Deuteronomy 31:23; Job 2:1"> And Jehovah said unto Moses, Behold, thy days approach that thou must die: call Joshua, and present yourselves in the tent of meeting, that I may give him a charge. And Moses and Joshua went, and presented themselves in the tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 40:38; Psalms 99:7; Exodus 33:9–10"> And Jehovah appeared in the Tent in a pillar of cloud: and the pillar of cloud stood over the door of the Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 10:6; Judges 10:13; Judges 2:12"> And Jehovah said unto Moses, Behold, thou shalt sleep with thy fathers; and this people will rise up, and play the harlot after the strange gods of the land, whither they go to be among them, and will forsake me, and break my covenant which I have made with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:42; Isaiah 8:17; Psalms 104:29"> Then my anger shall be kindled against them in that day, and I will forsake them, and I will hide my face from them, and they shall be devoured, and many evils and troubles shall come upon them; so that they will say in that day, Are not these evils come upon us because our God is not among us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:16–17"> And I will surely hide my face in that day for all the evil which they shall have wrought, in that they are turned unto other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Deuteronomy 31:21–22; Deuteronomy 31:26"> Now therefore write ye this song for you, and teach thou it the children of Israel: put it in their mouths, that this song may be a witness for me against the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 6:10–12; Nehemiah 9:25–26"> For when I shall have brought them into the land which I sware unto their fathers, flowing with milk and honey, and they shall have eaten and filled themselves, and waxed fat; then will they turn unto other gods, and serve them, and despise me, and break my covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 5:3; Psalms 139:2; John 2:24–25"> And it shall come to pass, when many evils and troubles are come upon them, that this song shall testify before them as a witness; for it shall not be forgotten out of the mouths of their seed: for I know their imagination which they frame this day, before I have brought them into the land which I sware.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19; Deuteronomy 31:9"> So Moses wrote this song the same day, and taught it the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:14; Acts 7:45"> And he gave Joshua the son of Nun a charge, and said, Be strong and of good courage; for thou shalt bring the children of Israel into the land which I sware unto them: and I will be with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:9; Deuteronomy 17:18"> And it came to pass, when Moses had made an end of writing the words of this law in a book, until they were finished,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:9"> that Moses commanded the Levites, that bare the ark of the covenant of Jehovah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19; 2 Kings 22:8–11; Galatians 2:19"> Take this book of the law, and put it by the side of the ark of the covenant of Jehovah your God, that it may be there for a witness against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:20; Deuteronomy 9:24; Acts 7:51"> For I know thy rebellion, and thy stiff neck: behold, while I am yet alive with you this day, ye have been rebellious against Jehovah; and how much more after my death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Deuteronomy 4:26; Deuteronomy 30:19"> Assemble unto me all the elders of your tribes, and your officers, that I may speak these words in their ears, and call heaven and earth to witness against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 2:19; Deuteronomy 32:5; Luke 21:24"> For I know that after my death ye will utterly corrupt yourselves, and turn aside from the way which I have commanded you; and evil will befall you in the latter days; because ye will do that which is evil in the sight of Jehovah, to provoke him to anger through the work of your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 3:2; Deuteronomy 4:5; John 12:49"> And Moses spake in the ears of all the assembly of Israel the words of this song, until they were finished.</span>
