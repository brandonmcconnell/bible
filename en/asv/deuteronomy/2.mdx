<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 21:4; Deuteronomy 1:40; Numbers 14:25"> Then we turned, and took our journey into the wilderness by the way to the Red Sea, as Jehovah spake unto me; and we compassed mount Seir many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And Jehovah spake unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:6; Deuteronomy 2:14; Deuteronomy 2:7"> Ye have compassed this mountain long enough: turn you northward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 20:14–21; Exodus 15:15; Deuteronomy 23:7"> And command thou the people, saying, Ye are to pass through the border of your brethren the children of Esau, that dwell in Seir; and they will be afraid of you. Take ye good heed unto yourselves therefore;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Joshua 24:4; Jeremiah 27:5"> contend not with them; for I will not give you of their land, no, not so much as for the sole of the foot to tread on; because I have given mount Seir unto Esau for a possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Numbers 20:19; Romans 12:17"> Ye shall purchase food of them for money, that ye may eat; and ye shall also buy water of them for money, that ye may drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Nehemiah 9:21; Deuteronomy 8:2–4"> For Jehovah thy God hath blessed thee in all the work of thy hand; he hath known thy walking through this great wilderness: these forty years Jehovah thy God hath been with thee; thou hast lacked nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 9:26; Judges 11:18; 2 Kings 16:6"> So we passed by from our brethren the children of Esau, that dwell in Seir, from the way of the Arabah from Elath and from Ezion-geber. And we turned and passed by the way of the wilderness of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 19:36–37; Numbers 21:15; Numbers 21:28"> And Jehovah said unto me, Vex not Moab, neither contend with them in battle; for I will not give thee of his land for a possession; because I have given Ar unto the children of Lot for a possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 14:5; Deuteronomy 2:11; Numbers 13:22"> (The Emim dwelt therein aforetime, a people great, and many, and tall, as the Anakim:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Numbers 13:28; Genesis 14:5"> these also are accounted Rephaim, as the Anakim; but the Moabites call them Emim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:22; Genesis 14:6; Deuteronomy 2:32–11"> The Horites also dwelt in Seir aforetime, but the children of Esau succeeded them; and they destroyed them from before them, and dwelt in their stead; as Israel did unto the land of his possession, which Jehovah gave unto them).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 21:12; Numbers 13:23"> Now rise up, and get you over the brook Zered. And we went over the brook Zered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; Numbers 14:28–35; Deuteronomy 1:34–35"> And the days in which we came from Kadesh-barnea, until we were come over the brook Zered, were thirty and eight years; until all the generation of the men of war were consumed from the midst of the camp, as Jehovah sware unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 106:26; Isaiah 66:14; Psalms 32:4"> Moreover the hand of Jehovah was against them, to destroy them from the midst of the camp, until they were consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So it came to pass, when all the men of war were consumed and dead from among the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> that Jehovah spake unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 21:23; Isaiah 15:1; Numbers 21:15"> Thou art this day to pass over Ar, the border of Moab:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:9; Judges 11:13–27; 2 Chronicles 20:10"> and when thou comest nigh over against the children of Ammon, vex them not, nor contend with them; for I will not give thee of the land of the children of Ammon for a possession; because I have given it unto the children of Lot for a possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 14:5"> (That also is accounted a land of Rephaim: Rephaim dwelt therein aforetime; but the Ammonites call them Zamzummim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:7–8; Deuteronomy 1:28; Deuteronomy 2:22"> a people great, and many, and tall, as the Anakim; but Jehovah destroyed them before them; and they succeeded them, and dwelt in their stead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Genesis 36:20–30; Deuteronomy 2:12"> as he did for the children of Esau, that dwell in Seir, when he destroyed the Horites from before them; and they succeeded them, and dwelt in their stead even unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 10:14; Joshua 13:3; Amos 9:7"> And the Avvim, that dwelt in villages as far as Gaza, the Caphtorim, that came forth out of Caphtor, destroyed them, and dwelt in their stead.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5; Daniel 4:17; Judges 11:18–21"> Rise ye up, take your journey, and pass over the valley of the Arnon: behold, I have given into thy hand Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land; begin to possess it, and contend with him in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:25; Exodus 15:14–16; Deuteronomy 28:10"> This day will I begin to put the dread of thee and the fear of thee upon the peoples that are under the whole heaven, who shall hear the report of thee, and shall tremble, and be in anguish because of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 10:5–6; Luke 10:10–12; Joshua 13:18"> And I sent messengers out of the wilderness of Kedemoth unto Sihon king of Heshbon with words of peace, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 11:19; Deuteronomy 2:6; Numbers 21:21–23"> Let me pass through thy land: I will go along by the highway, I will turn neither unto the right hand nor to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 20:19"> Thou shalt sell me food for money, that I may eat; and give me water for money, that I may drink: only let me pass through on my feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 20:18; Judges 11:17–18; Deuteronomy 23:3–4"> as the children of Esau that dwell in Seir, and the Moabites that dwell in Ar, did unto me; until I shall pass over the Jordan into the land which Jehovah our God giveth us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 21:23; Exodus 4:21; Joshua 11:19–20"> But Sihon king of Heshbon would not let us pass by him; for Jehovah thy God hardened his spirit, and made his heart obstinate, that he might deliver him into thy hand, as at this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:8; Deuteronomy 2:24"> And Jehovah said unto me, Behold, I have begun to deliver up Sihon and his land before thee: begin to possess, that thou mayest inherit his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 21:23–30; Judges 11:20–23; Psalms 136:19"> Then Sihon came out against us, he and all his people, unto battle at Jahaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Joshua 21:44; Deuteronomy 3:2–3"> And Jehovah our God delivered him up before us; and we smote him, and his sons, and all his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; 1 Samuel 15:3; Deuteronomy 3:6"> And we took all his cities at that time, and utterly destroyed every inhabited city, with the women and the little ones; we left none remaining:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Joshua 8:27; Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:9–11"> only the cattle we took for a prey unto ourselves, with the spoil of the cities which we had taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 13:9; Deuteronomy 4:48; Deuteronomy 3:12"> From Aroer, which is on the edge of the valley of the Arnon, and from the city that is in the valley, even unto Gilead, there was not a city too high for us; Jehovah our God delivered up all before us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 21:24; Deuteronomy 3:16; Genesis 32:22"> only to the land of the children of Ammon thou camest not near; all the side of the river Jabbok, and the cities of the hill-country, and wheresoever Jehovah our God forbade us.</span>
