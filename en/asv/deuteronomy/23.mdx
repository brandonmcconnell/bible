<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Isaiah 56:3–4; Leviticus 21:17–21"> He that is wounded in the stones, or hath his privy member cut off, shall not enter into the assembly of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 9:6; Isaiah 57:3; Hebrews 12:8"> A bastard shall not enter into the assembly of Jehovah; even to the tenth generation shall none of his enter into the assembly of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:1–2; Nehemiah 13:23; Ruth 4:6"> An Ammonite or a Moabite shall not enter into the assembly of Jehovah; even to the tenth generation shall none belonging to them enter into the assembly of Jehovah for ever:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:2; 2 Peter 2:15; 1 Kings 18:4"> because they met you not with bread and with water in the way, when ye came forth out of Egypt, and because they hired against thee Balaam the son of Beor from Pethor of Mesopotamia, to curse thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Romans 8:31; Ephesians 2:4–5"> Nevertheless Jehovah thy God would not hearken unto Balaam; but Jehovah thy God turned the curse into a blessing unto thee, because Jehovah thy God loved thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:12; 2 Samuel 12:31; Nehemiah 13:23–25"> Thou shalt not seek their peace nor their prosperity all thy days for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:19; Exodus 22:21; Exodus 23:9"> Thou shalt not abhor an Edomite; for he is thy brother: thou shalt not abhor an Egyptian; because thou wast a sojourner in his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 20:5–6; Ephesians 2:12–13; Deuteronomy 23:1–3"> The children of the third generation that are born unto them shall enter into the assembly of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 6:18; Judges 20:26; Revelation 19:11–14"> When thou goest forth in camp against thine enemies, then thou shalt keep thee from every evil thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 15:16; Numbers 5:2–3; 1 Corinthians 5:11–13"> If there be among you any man, that is not clean by reason of that which chanceth him by night, then shall he go abroad out of the camp, he shall not come within the camp:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; Ephesians 5:26–27; Leviticus 14:9"> but it shall be, when evening cometh on, he shall bathe himself in water; and when the sun is down, he shall come within the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Thou shalt have a place also without the camp, whither thou shalt go forth abroad:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:6–8"> and thou shalt have a paddle among thy weapons; and it shall be, when thou sittest down abroad, thou shalt dig therewith, and shalt turn back and cover that which cometh from thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Leviticus 26:12; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> for Jehovah thy God walketh in the midst of thy camp, to deliver thee, and to give up thine enemies before thee; therefore shall thy camp be holy, that he may not see an unclean thing in thee, and turn away from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:15; Obadiah 1:14"> Thou shalt not deliver unto his master a servant that is escaped from his master unto thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:21; Zechariah 7:10; Jeremiah 7:6"> he shall dwell with thee, in the midst of thee, in the place which he shall choose within one of thy gates, where it pleaseth him best: thou shalt not oppress him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 23:7; 1 Kings 14:24; Leviticus 19:29"> There shall be no prostitute of the daughters of Israel, neither shall there be a sodomite of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 3:2; Matthew 7:6; Revelation 22:15"> Thou shalt not bring the hire of a harlot, or the wages of a dog, into the house of Jehovah thy God for any vow: for even both these are an abomination unto Jehovah thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Leviticus 25:35–37; Psalms 15:5"> Thou shalt not lend upon interest to thy brother; interest of money, interest of victuals, interest of anything that is lent upon interest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 15:3; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Unto a foreigner thou mayest lend upon interest; but unto thy brother thou shalt not lend upon interest, that Jehovah thy God may bless thee in all that thou puttest thy hand unto, in the land whither thou goest in to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:4–5; Psalms 66:13–14; Psalms 116:18"> When thou shalt vow a vow unto Jehovah thy God, thou shalt not be slack to pay it: for Jehovah thy God will surely require it of thee; and it would be sin in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But if thou shalt forbear to vow, it shall be no sin in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 20:25; Judges 11:30–31; 1 Samuel 1:11"> That which is gone out of thy lips thou shalt observe and do; according as thou hast vowed unto Jehovah thy God, a freewill-offering, which thou hast promised with thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:26; Romans 12:13; Hebrews 13:5"> When thou comest into thy neighbor’s vineyard, then thou mayest eat of grapes thy fill at thine own pleasure; but thou shalt not put any in thy vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 12:1–2; Mark 2:23; Luke 6:1–2"> When thou comest into thy neighbor’s standing grain, then thou mayest pluck the ears with thy hand; but thou shalt not move a sickle unto thy neighbor’s standing grain.</span>
