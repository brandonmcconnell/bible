<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; Nehemiah 2:10; Nehemiah 4:1"> Now it came to pass, when it was reported to Sanballat and Tobiah, and to Geshem the Arabian, and unto the rest of our enemies, that I had builded the wall, and that there was no breach left therein (though even unto that time I had not set up the doors in the gates),</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:12; 1 Chronicles 8:12; Nehemiah 11:35"> that Sanballat and Geshem sent unto me, saying, Come, let us meet together in one of the villages in the plain of Ono. But they thought to do me mischief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 9:4; Matthew 10:16; 1 Timothy 4:15–16"> And I sent messengers unto them, saying, I am doing a great work, so that I cannot come down: why should the work cease, whilst I leave it, and come down to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 16:15–20; Luke 18:5; Galatians 2:5"> And they sent unto me four times after this sort; and I answered them after the same manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 6:11; 2 Corinthians 2:11; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> Then sent Sanballat his servant unto me in like manner the fifth time with an open letter in his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; Ezra 4:12; Romans 3:8"> wherein was written, It is reported among the nations, and Gashmu saith it, that thou and the Jews think to rebel; for which cause thou art building the wall: and thou wouldest be their king, according to these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–26; 1 Kings 1:7; 1 Kings 1:18"> And thou hast also appointed prophets to preach of thee at Jerusalem, saying, There is a king in Judah: and now shall it be reported to the king according to these words. Come now therefore, and let us take counsel together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 52:2; Job 13:4; Matthew 12:34"> Then I sent unto him, saying, There are no such things done as thou sayest, but thou feignest them out of thine own heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 138:3; 1 Peter 5:10; Ephesians 3:16"> For they all would have made us afraid, saying, Their hands shall be weakened from the work, that it be not done. But now, O God, strengthen thou my hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:5; Matthew 7:15; Psalms 37:12"> And I went unto the house of Shemaiah the son of Delaiah the son of Mehetabel, who was shut up; and he said, Let us meet together in the house of God, within the temple, and let us shut the doors of the temple: for they will come to slay thee; yea, in the night will they come to slay thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 11:1–2; Acts 21:13; Proverbs 28:1"> And I said, Should such a man as I flee? and who is there, that, being such as I, would go into the temple to save his life? I will not go in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:22; 1 Timothy 3:3; Ezekiel 13:7"> And I discerned, and, lo, God had not sent him; but he pronounced this prophecy against me: and Tobiah and Sanballat had hired him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; 2 Corinthians 11:12; 2 Timothy 1:7"> For this cause was he hired, that I should be afraid, and do so, and sin, and that they might have matter for an evil report, that they might reproach me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:29; 2 Timothy 4:14–15; Isaiah 9:14–15"> Remember, O my God, Tobiah and Sanballat according to these their works, and also the prophetess Noadiah, and the rest of the prophets, that would have put me in fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Daniel 9:25; Nehemiah 4:1–2"> So the wall was finished in the twenty and fifth day of the month Elul, in fifty and two days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 126:2; Joshua 5:1; Nehemiah 4:7"> And it came to pass, when all our enemies heard thereof, that all the nations that were about us feared, and were much cast down in their own eyes; for they perceived that this work was wrought of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Micah 7:1–6; Matthew 24:10–12; Nehemiah 3:5"> Moreover in those days the nobles of Judah sent many letters unto Tobiah, and the letters of Tobiah came unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezra 2:5; Nehemiah 7:10; Nehemiah 3:4"> For there were many in Judah sworn unto him, because he was the son-in-law of Shecaniah the son of Arah; and his son Jehohanan had taken the daughter of Meshullam the son of Berechiah to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 4:18–21; Nehemiah 6:9; John 7:7"> Also they spake of his good deeds before me, and reported my words to him. And Tobiah sent letters to put me in fear.</span>
