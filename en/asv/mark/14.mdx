<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 22:1–2; Exodus 12:6–20; John 13:1"> Now after two days was the feast of the passover and the unleavened bread: and the chief priests and the scribes sought how they might take him with subtlety, and kill him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 7:40; Proverbs 21:30; John 12:19"> for they said, Not during the feast, lest haply there shall be a tumult of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 7:37–39; John 12:1–8; John 11:2"> And while he was in Bethany in the house of Simon the leper, as he sat at meat, there came a woman having an alabaster cruse of ointment of pure nard very costly; and she brake the cruse, and poured it over his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:4–5; Matthew 26:8–9; Malachi 1:12–13"> But there were some that had indignation among themselves, saying, To what purpose hath this waste of the ointment been made?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 13:29; Ephesians 4:28; 1 Corinthians 10:10"> For this ointment might have been sold for above three hundred shillings, and given to the poor. And they murmured against her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 3:14; Hebrews 10:24; Hebrews 13:21"> But Jesus said, Let her alone; why trouble ye her? she hath wrought a good work on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Matthew 26:11; James 2:14–16"> For ye have the poor always with you, and whensoever ye will ye can do them good: but me ye have not always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; John 12:7; Mark 15:42–1"> She hath done what she could; she hath anointed my body beforehand for the burying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 26:12–13; Mark 16:15; Matthew 24:14"> And verily I say unto you, Wheresoever the gospel shall be preached throughout the whole world, that also which this woman hath done shall be spoken of for a memorial of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 22:3–6; Matthew 26:14–16; John 13:2"> And Judas Iscariot, he that was one of the twelve, went away unto the chief priests, that he might deliver him unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Hosea 7:3; Jude 1:11"> And they, when they heard it, were glad, and promised to give him money. And he sought how he might conveniently deliver him unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 12:6; Exodus 12:18; Matthew 26:17–19"> And on the first day of unleavened bread, when they sacrificed the passover, his disciples say unto him, Where wilt thou that we go and make ready that thou mayest eat the passover?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 22:10–13; Matthew 26:18–19; Luke 19:30–33"> And he sendeth two of his disciples, and saith unto them, Go into the city, and there shall meet you a man bearing a pitcher of water: follow him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Mark 11:3; John 13:13"> and wheresoever he shall enter in, say to the master of the house, The Teacher saith, Where is my guest-chamber, where I shall eat the passover with my disciples?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Psalms 110:3; Proverbs 21:1–2"> And he will himself show you a large upper room furnished and ready: and there make ready for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 16:4; Luke 22:13; Luke 22:35"> And the disciples went forth, and came into the city, and found as he had said unto them: and they made ready the passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:14; John 13:21–26; Matthew 26:20–24"> And when it was evening he cometh with the twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 6:47; John 6:70"> And as they sat and were eating, Jesus said, Verily I say unto you, One of you shall betray me, even he that eateth with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 22:21–23; Matthew 26:22; John 13:22"> They began to be sorrowful, and to say unto him one by one, Is it I?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 13:26; Matthew 26:23; John 6:71"> And he said unto them, It is one of the twelve, he that dippeth with me in the dish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 26:24–25; Isaiah 53:1–12; John 19:28"> For the Son of man goeth, even as it is written of him: but woe unto that man through whom the Son of man is betrayed! good were it for that man if he had not been born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 26:26–29; Mark 14:24; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> And as they were eating, he took bread, and when he had blessed, he brake it, and gave to them, and said, Take ye: this is my body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 14:22; Matthew 26:27; 1 Corinthians 10:16"> And he took a cup, and when he had given thanks, he gave to them: and they all drank of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11; Exodus 24:8; Revelation 7:9–17"> And he said unto them, This is my blood of the covenant, which is poured out for many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 26:29; Psalms 104:15; Luke 22:16–18"> Verily I say unto you, I shall no more drink of the fruit of the vine, until that day when I drink it new in the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 26:30; Luke 22:39; Acts 16:25"> And when they had sung a hymn, they went out unto the mount of Olives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; John 16:32; Matthew 26:31"> And Jesus saith unto them, All ye shall be offended: for it is written, I will smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 28:16; Mark 16:7; Matthew 28:7"> Howbeit, after I am raised up, I will go before you into Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 26:33–35; Luke 22:33–34; John 13:36–38"> But Peter said unto him, Although all shall be offended, yet will not I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 13:38; Mark 14:66–72; Genesis 1:5"> And Jesus saith unto him, Verily I say unto thee, that thou to-day, even this night, before the cock crow twice, shalt deny me thrice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 19:8; John 13:37; Proverbs 16:18"> But he spake exceeding vehemently, If I must die with thee, I will not deny thee. And in like manner also said they all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 14:36; Mark 14:39; Psalms 18:5–6"> And they come unto a place which was named Gethsemane: and he saith unto his disciples, Sit ye here, while I pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 22:44; Hebrews 5:7; Mark 5:37"> And he taketh with him Peter and James and John, and began to be greatly amazed, and sore troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 12:27; Mark 13:35–37; 1 Peter 5:8"> And he saith unto them, My soul is exceeding sorrowful even unto death: abide ye here, and watch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 14:41; Deuteronomy 9:18; Hebrews 5:7"> And he went forward a little, and fell on the ground, and prayed that, if it were possible, the hour might pass away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Galatians 4:6; Romans 8:15–16; John 5:30"> And he said, Abba, Father, all things are possible unto thee; remove this cup from me: howbeit not what I will, but what thou wilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 26:40; Mark 14:29–31; Hebrews 12:3"> And he cometh, and findeth them sleeping, and saith unto Peter, Simon, sleepest thou? couldest thou not watch one hour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 26:41; Matthew 24:42; Luke 21:36"> Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation: the spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 26:42–44; 2 Corinthians 12:8; Matthew 6:7"> And again he went away, and prayed, saying the same words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 9:33–34; Romans 3:19; Genesis 44:16"> And again he came, and found them sleeping, for their eyes were very heavy; and they knew not what to answer him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 14:35; Mark 9:31; John 17:1"> And he cometh the third time, and saith unto them, Sleep on now, and take your rest: it is enough; the hour is come; behold, the Son of man is betrayed into the hands of sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 26:46; John 18:1–2"> Arise, let us be going: behold, he that betrayeth me is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 18:3–11; Luke 22:47–53; Matthew 26:47–56"> And straightway, while he yet spake, cometh Judas, one of the twelve, and with him a multitude with swords and staves, from the chief priests and the scribes and the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 26:48–50; Proverbs 27:6; Psalms 55:20–21"> Now he that betrayed him had given them a token, saying, Whomsoever I shall kiss, that is he; take him, and lead him away safely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 23:7–10; Malachi 1:6; Mark 12:14"> And when he was come, straightway he came to him, and saith, Rabbi; and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 18:12; Lamentations 4:20; Acts 2:23"> And they laid hands on him, and took him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Matthew 26:51–54; Luke 22:49–51"> But a certain one of them that stood by drew his sword, and smote the servant of the high priest, and struck off his ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 22:52–53; 1 Samuel 26:18; 1 Samuel 24:14–15"> And Jesus answered and said unto them, Are ye come out, as against a robber, with swords and staves to seize me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 18:20; John 8:2; Luke 22:37"> I was daily with you in the temple teaching, and ye took me not: but this is done that the scriptures might be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Mark 14:27; John 16:32; Psalms 88:18"> And they all left him, and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And a certain young man followed with him, having a linen cloth cast about him, over his naked body: and they lay hold on him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Mark 13:14–16; Genesis 39:12; Job 2:4"> but he left the linen cloth, and fled naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 26:57–68; John 18:12–14; Luke 22:54–62"> And they led Jesus away to the high priest: and there come together with him all the chief priests and the elders and the scribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 18:18; Matthew 26:3; John 18:25"> And Peter had followed him afar off, even within, into the court of the high priest; and he was sitting with the officers, and warming himself in the light of the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16–18; Matthew 26:59–60; Matthew 5:22"> Now the chief priests and the whole council sought witness against Jesus to put him to death; and found it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> For many bare false witness against him, and their witness agreed not together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Acts 6:13–14; Matthew 26:60–61; John 2:18–21"> And there stood up certain, and bare false witness against him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 2:19; Hebrews 9:11; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> We heard him say, I will destroy this temple that is made with hands, and in three days I will build another made without hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> And not even so did their witness agree together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="John 19:9–10; Matthew 26:62–63; Mark 15:3–5"> And the high priest stood up in the midst, and asked Jesus, saying, Answerest thou nothing? what is it which these witness against thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Matthew 26:63–64; Mark 15:2; Isaiah 53:7"> But he held his peace, and answered nothing. Again the high priest asked him, and saith unto him, Art thou the Christ, the Son of the Blessed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:3"> And Jesus said, I am: and ye shall see the Son of man sitting at the right hand of Power, and coming with the clouds of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Matthew 26:65; Isaiah 36:22–1; Numbers 14:6"> And the high priest rent his clothes, and saith, What further need have we of witnesses?</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Leviticus 24:16; John 10:31–33; John 19:7"> Ye have heard the blasphemy: what think ye? And they all condemned him to be worthy of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Matthew 26:67–68; Acts 23:2; Isaiah 50:6"> And some began to spit on him, and to cover his face, and to buffet him, and to say unto him, Prophesy: and the officers received him with blows of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Mark 14:54; Matthew 26:69–75; John 18:25–27"> And as Peter was beneath in the court, there cometh one of the maids of the high priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Mark 14:54; Mark 10:47; Matthew 2:23"> and seeing Peter warming himself, she looked upon him, and saith, Thou also wast with the Nazarene, even Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Mark 14:29–31; Matthew 26:71–72; John 13:36–38"> But he denied, saying, I neither know, nor understand what thou sayest: and he went out into the porch; and the cock crew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Luke 22:58; Mark 14:38; Galatians 6:1"> And the maid saw him, and began again to say to them that stood by, This is one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Luke 22:59–60; Matthew 26:73–74; Acts 2:7"> But he again denied it. And after a little while again they that stood by said to Peter, Of a truth thou art one of them; for thou art a Galilæan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; 1 Corinthians 10:12; 2 Kings 10:32"> But he began to curse, and to swear, I know not this man of whom ye speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Mark 14:30; Mark 14:68; Ezekiel 7:16"> And straightway the second time the cock crew. And Peter called to mind the word, how that Jesus said unto him, Before the cock crow twice, thou shalt deny me thrice. And when he thought thereon, he wept.</span>
