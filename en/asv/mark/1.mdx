<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 5:11–12; Matthew 4:3; Matthew 3:17"> The beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Matthew 11:10; Luke 1:76"> Even as it is written in Isaiah the prophet,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Behold, I send my messenger before thy face,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Who shall prepare thy way;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Matthew 3:3; Luke 3:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The voice of one crying in the wilderness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Make ye ready the way of the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Make his paths straight;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 19:3–4; Matthew 3:6; Acts 13:24–25"> John came, who baptized in the wilderness and preached the baptism of repentance unto remission of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–42; Acts 19:18; Matthew 3:5–6"> And there went out unto him all the country of Judæa, and all they of Jerusalem; and they were baptized of him in the river Jordan, confessing their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 1:8; Leviticus 11:22; Matthew 3:4"> And John was clothed with camel’s hair, and had a leathern girdle about his loins, and did eat locusts and wild honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 13:25; Luke 3:16; John 1:27"> And he preached, saying, There cometh after me he that is mightier than I, the latchet of whose shoes I am not worthy to stoop down and unloose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:5; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Acts 19:4–6"> I baptized you in water; but he shall baptize you in the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 3:13–17; Matthew 2:23; Luke 3:21–22"> And it came to pass in those days, that Jesus came from Nazareth of Galilee, and was baptized of John in the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:31–34; Luke 3:22; Isaiah 42:1"> And straightway coming up out of the water, he saw the heavens rent asunder, and the Spirit as a dove descending upon him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Mark 9:7; Matthew 3:17"> and a voice came out of the heavens, Thou art my beloved Son, in thee I am well pleased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 4:1–11; Luke 4:1–13"> And straightway the Spirit driveth him forth into the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17–18; Hebrews 4:15; Exodus 34:28"> And he was in the wilderness forty days tempted of Satan; and he was with the wild beasts; and the angels ministered unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Matthew 4:12; Luke 8:1"> Now after John was delivered up, Jesus came into Galilee, preaching the gospel of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:36–38; Matthew 4:17"> and saying, The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand: repent ye, and believe in the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 4:18–22; John 1:40–42; John 6:8"> And passing along by the sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew the brother of Simon casting a net in the sea; for they were fishers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 5:10; Matthew 4:19–20; Acts 2:38–41"> And Jesus said unto them, Come ye after me, and I will make you to become fishers of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 18:28–30; Luke 5:11; Mark 10:28–31"> And straightway they left the nets, and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 5:37; Acts 1:13; Mark 10:35"> And going on a little further, he saw James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, who also were in the boat mending the nets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Luke 14:26; Mark 10:29"> And straightway he called them: and they left their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired servants, and went after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 6:2; Mark 1:39; Matthew 4:23"> And they go into Capernaum; and straightway on the sabbath day he entered into the synagogue and taught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54; Luke 4:32"> And they were astonished at his teaching: for he taught them as having authority, and not as the scribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 9:25; Mark 7:25; Mark 1:34"> And straightway there was in their synagogue a man with an unclean spirit; and he cried out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; James 2:19; Acts 3:14"> saying, What have we to do with thee, Jesus thou Nazarene? art thou come to destroy us? I know thee who thou art, the Holy One of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 1:34; Luke 4:41; Acts 16:17"> And Jesus rebuked him, saying, Hold thy peace, and come out of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 9:26; Mark 9:20; Luke 11:22"> And the unclean spirit, tearing him and crying with a loud voice, came out of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:31; Mark 7:37; Luke 9:1"> And they were all amazed, insomuch that they questioned among themselves, saying, What is this? a new teaching! with authority he commandeth even the unclean spirits, and they obey him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 9:31; Luke 4:37; Mark 1:45"> And the report of him went out straightway everywhere into all the region of Galilee round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 4:38–39; Matthew 8:14–15; Mark 1:21"> And straightway, when they were come out of the synagogue, they came into the house of Simon and Andrew, with James and John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:5; Mark 5:23; John 11:3"> Now Simon’s wife’s mother lay sick of a fever; and straightway they tell him of her:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 9:41; Psalms 116:12; Mark 5:41"> and he came and took her by the hand, and raised her up; and the fever left her, and she ministered unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 8:16; Mark 3:2; Luke 4:40"> And at even, when the sun did set, they brought unto him all that were sick, and them that were possessed with demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 1:5; Acts 13:44"> And all the city was gathered together at the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 3:12; Matthew 4:23"> And he healed many that were sick with divers diseases, and cast out many demons; and he suffered not the demons to speak, because they knew him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Luke 5:16; Luke 22:39–46"> And in the morning, a great while before day, he rose up and went out, and departed into a desert place, and there prayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And Simon and they that were with him followed after him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 12:19; Mark 1:5; Zechariah 11:11"> and they found him, and say unto him, All are seeking thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 4:43; John 17:8; Isaiah 61:1–3"> And he saith unto them, Let us go elsewhere into the next towns, that I may preach there also; for to this end came I forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Mark 1:21; Luke 4:43–44"> And he went into their synagogues throughout all Galilee, preaching and casting out demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 5:12–14; Matthew 8:2–4; Leviticus 13:1–14"> And there cometh to him a leper, beseeching him, and kneeling down to him, and saying unto him, If thou wilt, thou canst make me clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 4:15"> And being moved with compassion, he stretched forth his hand, and touched him, and saith unto him, I will; be thou made clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 15:3; Mark 5:29; John 4:50–53"> And straightway the leprosy departed from him, and he was made clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 9:30; Mark 7:36; Luke 8:56"> And he strictly charged him, and straightway sent him out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Leviticus 14:1–32; Luke 17:14; Matthew 8:4"> and saith unto him, See thou say nothing to any man: but go show thyself to the priest, and offer for thy cleansing the things which Moses commanded, for a testimony unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 2:13; Matthew 9:31; John 6:2"> But he went out, and began to publish it much, and to spread abroad the matter, insomuch that Jesus could no more openly enter into a city, but was without in desert places: and they came to him from every quarter.</span>
