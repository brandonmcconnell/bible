<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:9–14; Luke 6:6–11; Mark 1:21"> And he entered again into the synagogue; and there was a man there who had his hand withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:32; Luke 14:1; Luke 6:7"> And they watched him, whether he would heal him on the sabbath day; that they might accuse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 1:28–30; Isaiah 42:4; Luke 6:8"> And he saith unto the man that had his hand withered, Stand forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Matthew 12:10–12; Hosea 6:6"> And he saith unto them, Is it lawful on the sabbath day to do good, or to do harm? to save a life, or to kill? But they held their peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:10; Ephesians 4:18; Romans 11:25"> And when he had looked round about on them with anger, being grieved at the hardening of their heart, he saith unto the man, Stretch forth thy hand. And he stretched it forth; and his hand was restored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:16; Mark 12:13; Matthew 12:14"> And the Pharisees went out, and straightway with the Herodians took counsel against him, how they might destroy him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 4:25; Luke 6:17; Matthew 12:15"> And Jesus with his disciples withdrew to the sea: and a great multitude from Galilee followed; and from Judæa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Isaiah 34:5; Matthew 11:21"> and from Jerusalem, and from Idumæa, and beyond the Jordan, and about Tyre and Sidon, a great multitude, hearing what great things he did, came unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 5:30; John 6:15"> And he spake to his disciples, that a little boat should wait on him because of the crowd, lest they should throng him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56; Matthew 4:23"> for he had healed many; insomuch that as many as had plagues pressed upon him that they might touch him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 1:23–24; Luke 8:28"> And the unclean spirits, whensoever they beheld him, fell down before him, and cried, saying, Thou art the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:16; Mark 1:25; Mark 1:34"> And he charged them much that they should not make him known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Luke 6:12–16; Matthew 10:1–4"> And he goeth up into the mountain, and calleth unto him whom he himself would; and they went unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Luke 9:1–6; John 15:16"> And he appointed twelve, that they might be with him, and that he might send them forth to preach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and to have authority to cast out demons:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:42; Acts 1:13; Matthew 16:16–18"> and Simon he surnamed Peter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Mark 14:33; Jeremiah 23:29"> and James the son of Zebedee, and John the brother of James; and them he surnamed Boanerges, which is, Sons of thunder:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14; Matthew 9:9"> and Andrew, and Philip, and Bartholomew, and Matthew, and Thomas, and James the son of Alphæus, and Thaddæus, and Simon the Cananæan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 6:71; John 13:2; Acts 1:16–25"> and Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him. And he cometh into a house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 6:31; John 4:31–34; Mark 9:28"> And the multitude cometh together again, so that they could not so much as eat bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 26:24; John 10:20; Mark 3:31"> And when his friends heard it, they went out to lay hold on him: for they said, He is beside himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:1; Matthew 10:25; Matthew 9:34"> And the scribes that came down from Jerusalem said, He hath Beelzebub, and, By the prince of the demons casteth he out the demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:17–23; Matthew 12:25–30; Matthew 13:34"> And he called them unto him, and said unto them in parables, How can Satan cast out Satan?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Isaiah 19:2–3; 2 Samuel 20:6"> And if a kingdom be divided against itself, that kingdom cannot stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 3:16; Psalms 133:1; Galatians 5:15"> And if a house be divided against itself, that house will not be able to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And if Satan hath risen up against himself, and is divided, he cannot stand, but hath an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–26; Matthew 12:29; Luke 11:21–23"> But no one can enter into the house of the strong man, and spoil his goods, except he first bind the strong man; and then he will spoil his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; 1 John 5:16"> Verily I say unto you, All their sins shall be forgiven unto the sons of men, and their blasphemies wherewith soever they shall blaspheme:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 12:31–32; Luke 12:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> but whosoever shall blaspheme against the Holy Spirit hath never forgiveness, but is guilty of an eternal sin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:20; Mark 3:22"> because they said, He hath an unclean spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:19–21; Matthew 12:46–50"> And there come his mother and his brethren; and, standing without, they sent unto him, calling him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And a multitude was sitting about him; and they say unto him, Behold, thy mother and thy brethren without seek for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 3:21; Deuteronomy 33:9; Mark 6:3"> And he answereth them, and saith, Who is my mother and my brethren?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 12:49–50; Luke 11:27–28; Romans 8:29"> And looking round on them that sat round about him, he saith, Behold, my mother and my brethren!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:17; Matthew 7:21; 1 John 3:22–23"> For whosoever shall do the will of God, the same is my brother, and sister, and mother.</span>
