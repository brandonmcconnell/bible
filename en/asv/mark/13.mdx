</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Mark 4:34; Mark 14:33"> And as he sat on the mount of Olives over against the temple, Peter and James and John and Andrew asked him privately,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 1:6–7; Matthew 24:3; Daniel 12:6"> Tell us, when shall these things be? and what shall be the sign when these things are all about to be accomplished?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Ephesians 5:6; Jeremiah 29:8"> And Jesus began to say unto them, Take heed that no man lead you astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:22; Jeremiah 23:21–25; John 8:24"> Many shall come in my name, saying, I am he; and shall lead many astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 21:9–11; John 14:27; Matthew 24:6–7"> And when ye shall hear of wars and rumors of wars, be not troubled: these things must needs come to pass; but the end is not yet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 24:8; Isaiah 37:3; Psalms 48:6"> For nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom; there shall be earthquakes in divers places; there shall be famines: these things are the beginning of travail.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 10:17–18; John 16:2; Philippians 1:29"> But take ye heed to yourselves: for they shall deliver you up to councils; and in synagogues shall ye be beaten; and before governors and kings shall ye stand for my sake, for a testimony unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:14; Colossians 1:23; Romans 10:18"> And the gospel must first be preached unto all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 21:14–15; Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–21"> And when they lead you to judgment, and deliver you up, be not anxious beforehand what ye shall speak: but whatsoever shall be given you in that hour, that speak ye; for it is not ye that speak, but the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Luke 12:52–53; Luke 21:16"> And brother shall deliver up brother to death, and the father his child; and children shall rise up against parents, and cause them to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:18–19; Matthew 10:22; Matthew 24:13"> And ye shall be hated of all men for my name’s sake: but he that endureth to the end, the same shall be saved.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:27; Daniel 12:11; Daniel 11:31"> But when ye see the abomination of desolation standing where he ought not (let him that readeth understand), then let them that are in Judæa flee unto the mountains:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:31–33; Hebrews 11:7; Philippians 3:7–8"> and let him that is on the housetop not go down, nor enter in, to take anything out of his house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and let him that is in the field not return back to take his cloak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 4:10; Luke 23:29; Hosea 9:14"> But woe unto them that are with child and to them that give suck in those days!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And pray ye that it be not in the winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Joel 2:2; Matthew 24:21"> For those days shall be tribulation, such as there hath not been the like from the beginning of the creation which God created until now, and never shall be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 24:22; Zechariah 13:8–9; Isaiah 65:8–9"> And except the Lord had shortened the days, no flesh would have been saved; but for the elect’s sake, whom he chose, he shortened the days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Matthew 24:23–25; Matthew 24:5"> And then if any man shall say unto you, Lo, here is the Christ; or, Lo, there; believe it not:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; 2 Thessalonians 2:8–14; Mark 13:6"> for there shall arise false Christs and false prophets, and shall show signs and wonders, that they may lead astray, if possible, the elect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 3:17; Mark 13:5; John 14:29"> But take ye heed: behold, I have told you all things beforehand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Acts 2:19–20; Revelation 6:12–14"> But in those days, after that tribulation, the sun shall be darkened, and the moon shall not give her light,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4; Revelation 6:13"> and the stars shall be falling from heaven, and the powers that are in the heavens shall be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Matthew 16:27; Matthew 24:30"> And then shall they see the Son of man coming in clouds with great power and glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zechariah 2:6; Matthew 24:31; Deuteronomy 30:4"> And then shall he send forth the angels, and shall gather together his elect from the four winds, from the uttermost part of the earth to the uttermost part of heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 21:29–31; Matthew 24:32–33"> Now from the fig tree learn her parable: when her branch is now become tender, and putteth forth its leaves, ye know that the summer is nigh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; James 5:9; Ezekiel 12:25–28"> even so ye also, when ye see these things coming to pass, know ye that he is nigh, even at the doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:34; Luke 21:32; Matthew 16:28"> Verily I say unto you, This generation shall not pass away, until all these things be accomplished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Isaiah 51:6; Matthew 24:35"> Heaven and earth shall pass away: but my words shall not pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Matthew 24:36–42; Matthew 25:13"> But of that day or that hour knoweth no one, not even the angels in heaven, neither the Son, but the Father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Luke 12:40; Romans 13:11–12"> Take ye heed, watch and pray: for ye know not when the time is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–30; John 10:3; Luke 12:36–40"> It is as when a man, sojourning in another country, having left his house, and given authority to his servants, to each one his work, commanded also the porter to watch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 13:33; Matthew 24:42; Matthew 24:44"> Watch therefore: for ye know not when the lord of the house cometh, whether at even, or at midnight, or at cockcrowing, or in the morning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6–7; Romans 13:11–14; Matthew 24:48–51"> lest coming suddenly he find you sleeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 13:33; Luke 12:41–46; Mark 13:35"> And what I say unto you I say unto all, Watch.</span>
</div>
