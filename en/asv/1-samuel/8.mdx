<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:11; James 1:5; 1 Samuel 12:17"> But the thing displeased Samuel, when they said, Give us a king to judge us. And Samuel prayed unto Jehovah.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:34–40; Exodus 32:1; Judges 13:1"> According to all the works which they have done since the day that I brought them up out of Egypt even unto this day, in that they have forsaken me, and served other gods, so do they also unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:25; 1 Samuel 8:11–18; Ezekiel 46:18"> Now therefore hearken unto their voice: howbeit thou shalt protest solemnly unto them, and shalt show them the manner of the king that shall reign over them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Samuel told all the words of Jehovah unto the people that asked of him a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:52; Deuteronomy 17:14–20; 1 Samuel 10:25"> And he said, This will be the manner of the king that shall reign over you: he will take your sons, and appoint them unto him, for his chariots, and to be his horsemen; and they shall run before his chariots;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:7; 1 Kings 4:7; 1 Chronicles 27:1–22"> and he will appoint them unto him for captains of thousands, and captains of fifties; and he will set some to plow his ground, and to reap his harvest, and to make his instruments of war, and the instruments of his chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he will take your daughters to be perfumers, and to be cooks, and to be bakers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:18; 1 Kings 21:7; 1 Kings 21:19"> And he will take your fields, and your vineyards, and your oliveyards, even the best of them, and give them to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 1:3; Isaiah 39:7; Genesis 37:36"> And he will take the tenth of your seed, and of your vineyards, and give to his officers, and to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And he will take your men-servants, and your maid-servants, and your goodliest young men, and your asses, and put them to his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He will take the tenth of your flocks: and ye shall be his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:4; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 1:25–28"> And ye shall cry out in that day because of your king whom ye shall have chosen you; and Jehovah will not answer you in that day.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:5; Psalms 106:35; Deuteronomy 7:6"> that we also may be like all the nations, and that our king may judge us, and go out before us, and fight our battles.</span>
