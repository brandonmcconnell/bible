<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:5; Micah 6:6"> Saul was forty years old when he began to reign; and when he had reigned two years over Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:26; 1 Samuel 13:5; 1 Samuel 14:31"> Saul chose him three thousand men of Israel, whereof two thousand were with Saul in Michmash and in the mount of Beth-el, and a thousand were with Jonathan in Gibeah of Benjamin: and the rest of the people he sent every man to his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5; Judges 3:27; Judges 6:34"> And Jonathan smote the garrison of the Philistines that was in Geba; and the Philistines heard of it. And Saul blew the trumpet throughout all the land, saying, Let the Hebrews hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Exodus 5:21; Zechariah 11:8"> And all Israel heard say that Saul had smitten the garrison of the Philistines, and also that Israel was had in abomination with the Philistines. And the people were gathered together after Saul to Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:23; Joshua 11:4; Joshua 18:12"> And the Philistines assembled themselves together to fight with Israel, thirty thousand chariots, and six thousand horsemen, and people as the sand which is on the sea-shore in multitude: and they came up, and encamped in Michmash, eastward of Beth-aven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 6:2; Hebrews 11:38; Judges 10:9"> When the men of Israel saw that they were in a strait (for the people were distressed), then the people did hide themselves in caves, and in thickets, and in rocks, and in coverts, and in pits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:8; Leviticus 26:17; Numbers 32:1–5"> Now some of the Hebrews had gone over the Jordan to the land of Gad and Gilead; but as for Saul, he was yet in Gilgal, and all the people followed him trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:8"> And he tarried seven days, according to the set time that Samuel had appointed: but Samuel came not to Gilgal; and the people were scattered from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 3:4; Proverbs 21:27; Proverbs 15:8"> And Saul said, Bring hither the burnt-offering to me, and the peace-offerings. And he offered the burnt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:13; Ruth 2:4; Psalms 129:8"> And it came to pass that, as soon as he had made an end of offering the burnt-offering, behold, Samuel came; and Saul went out to meet him, that he might salute him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:23; 1 Samuel 13:5; 1 Samuel 13:16"> And Samuel said, What hast thou done? And Saul said, Because I saw that the people were scattered from me, and that thou camest not within the days appointed, and that the Philistines assembled themselves together at Michmash;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 66:3; Amos 8:5; 2 Corinthians 9:7"> therefore said I, Now will the Philistines come down upon me to Gilgal, and I have not entreated the favor of Jehovah: I forced myself therefore, and offered the burnt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; 2 Chronicles 16:9; 1 Samuel 15:28"> And Samuel said to Saul, Thou hast done foolishly; thou hast not kept the commandment of Jehovah thy God, which he commanded thee: for now would Jehovah have established thy kingdom upon Israel for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 13:22; 1 Samuel 15:28; Psalms 89:19–37"> But now thy kingdom shall not continue: Jehovah hath sought him a man after his own heart, and Jehovah hath appointed him to be prince over his people, because thou hast not kept that which Jehovah commanded thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:2; 1 Samuel 13:2; 1 Samuel 13:6–7"> And Samuel arose, and gat him up from Gilgal unto Gibeah of Benjamin. And Saul numbered the people that were present with him, about six hundred men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:3"> And Saul, and Jonathan his son, and the people that were present with them, abode in Geba of Benjamin: but the Philistines encamped in Michmash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 18:23; 1 Samuel 14:15; Joshua 19:3"> And the spoilers came out of the camp of the Philistines in three companies: one company turned unto the way that leadeth to Ophrah, unto the land of Shual;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:34; Joshua 18:13–14; Joshua 16:3"> and another company turned the way to Beth-horon; and another company turned the way of the border that looketh down upon the valley of Zeboim toward the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 24:14; Judges 5:8; Jeremiah 24:1"> Now there was no smith found throughout all the land of Israel; for the Philistines said, Lest the Hebrews make them swords or spears:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> but all the Israelites went down to the Philistines, to sharpen every man his share, and his coulter, and his axe, and his mattock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> yet they had a file for the mattocks, and for the coulters, and for the forks, and for the axes, and to set the goads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 5:8; Zechariah 4:6; 1 Samuel 17:50"> So it came to pass in the day of battle, that there was neither sword nor spear found in the hand of any of the people that were with Saul and Jonathan: but with Saul and with Jonathan his son was there found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 10:28; 1 Samuel 14:1; 1 Samuel 14:4–5"> And the garrison of the Philistines went out unto the pass of Michmash.</span>
