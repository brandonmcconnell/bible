</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:39; Nehemiah 3:1; Nehemiah 3:32"> Now there is in Jerusalem by the sheep gate a pool, which is called in Hebrew Bethesda, having five porches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 3:1–4; Luke 7:22; Matthew 15:30"> In these lay a multitude of them that were sick, blind, halt, withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Many ancient authorities insert, wholly or in part, waiting for the moving of the water: for an angel of the Lord went down at certain seasons into the pool, and troubled the water: whosoever then first after the troubling of the water stepped in was made whole, with whatsoever disease he was holden.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 3:2; Luke 8:43; Mark 9:21"> And a certain man was there, who had been thirty and eight years in his infirmity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 18:41; Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 4:13"> When Jesus saw him lying, and knew that he had been now a long time in that case, he saith unto him, Wouldest thou be made whole?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 72:12; Deuteronomy 32:36; 1 Corinthians 9:24"> The sick man answered him, Sir, I have no man, when the water is troubled, to put me into the pool: but while I am coming, another steppeth down before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 5:24; Matthew 9:6; Mark 2:11"> Jesus saith unto him, Arise, take up thy bed, and walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 3:7–8; John 9:14; Mark 3:2–4"> And straightway the man was made whole, and took up his bed and walked.<br />Now it was the sabbath on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:21; Luke 6:2; John 9:16"> So the Jews said unto him that was cured, It is the sabbath, and it is not lawful for thee to take up thy bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 2:9–11; John 9:16"> But he answered them, He that made me whole, the same said unto me, Take up thy bed, and walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Judges 6:29; Romans 10:2"> They asked him, Who is the man that said unto thee, Take up thy bed, and walk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:30; Luke 24:31; John 8:59"> But he that was healed knew not who it was; for Jesus had conveyed himself away, a multitude being in the place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 8:11; Leviticus 26:23–24; Psalms 118:18"> Afterward Jesus findeth him in the temple, and said unto him, Behold, thou art made whole: sin no more, lest a worse thing befall thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:11–12; John 4:29; John 9:25"> The man went away, and told the Jews that it was Jesus who had made him whole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:39; John 7:19–20; Mark 3:6"> And for this cause the Jews persecuted Jesus, because he did these things on the sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:4; John 14:10; Colossians 1:16"> But Jesus answered them, My Father worketh even until now, and I work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 10:30; John 10:33; John 19:7"> For this cause therefore the Jews sought the more to kill him, because he not only brake the sabbath, but also called God his own Father, making himself equal with God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 12:49; John 8:28; John 14:10"> Jesus therefore answered and said unto them, Verily, verily, I say unto you, The Son can do nothing of himself, but what he seeth the Father doing: for what things soever he doeth, these the Son also doeth in like manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 3:35; Matthew 3:17; John 17:26"> For the Father loveth the Son, and showeth him all things that himself doeth: and greater works than these will he show him, that ye may marvel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:11; Deuteronomy 32:39; John 11:25"> For as the Father raiseth the dead and giveth them life, even so the Son also giveth life to whom he will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 5:27; Acts 17:31; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> For neither doth the Father judge any man, but he hath given all judgment unto the Son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 2:23; Luke 10:16; Matthew 11:27"> that all may honor the Son, even as they honor the Father. He that honoreth not the Son honoreth not the Father that sent him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; John 6:40; 1 John 5:11–13"> Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that heareth my word, and believeth him that sent me, hath eternal life, and cometh not into judgment, but hath passed out of death into life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:28; Ephesians 5:14; Ephesians 2:5"> Verily, verily, I say unto you, The hour cometh, and now is, when the dead shall hear the voice of the Son of God; and they that hear shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 11:26; John 1:4; Exodus 3:14"> For as the Father hath life in himself, even so gave he to the Son also to have life in himself:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 10:42; John 5:22; Acts 17:31"> and he gave him authority to execute judgment, because he is a son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 20:12; 1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Isaiah 26:19"> Marvel not at this: for the hour cometh, in which all that are in the tombs shall hear his voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Romans 2:6–10; Acts 24:15"> and shall come forth; they that have done good, unto the resurrection of life; and they that have done evil, unto the resurrection of judgment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 6:38; John 5:19; John 4:34"> I can of myself do nothing: as I hear, I judge: and my judgment is righteous; because I seek not mine own will, but the will of him that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; John 8:54; Revelation 3:14"> If I bear witness of myself, my witness is not true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 1:33; John 12:28–30; John 5:36–37"> It is another that beareth witness of me; and I know that the witness which he witnesseth of me is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 1:29–34; John 3:26–36; John 1:15–27"> Ye have sent unto John, and he hath borne witness unto the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 19:41–42; John 20:31; 1 John 5:9"> But the witness which I receive is not from man: howbeit I say these things, that ye may be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Peter 1:19; John 1:7–8; Matthew 11:11"> He was the lamp that burneth and shineth; and ye were willing to rejoice for a season in his light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 10:25; John 10:37–38; John 15:24"> But the witness which I have is greater than that of John; for the works which the Father hath given me to accomplish, the very works that I do, bear witness of me, that the Father hath sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 8:18; John 1:18; Matthew 3:17"> And the Father that sent me, he hath borne witness of me. Ye have neither heard his voice at any time, nor seen his form.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 John 2:14; Deuteronomy 6:6–9; John 12:44–48"> And ye have not his word abiding in you: for whom he sent, him ye believe not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Luke 24:44; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> Ye search the scriptures, because ye think that in them ye have eternal life; and these are they which bear witness of me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 3:19; John 1:11; Psalms 81:11"> and ye will not come to me, that ye may have life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:18; John 5:34; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> I receive not glory from men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 3:17; John 2:25"> But I know you, that ye have not the love of God in yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 10:25; Matthew 24:5; John 17:4–6"> I am come in my Father’s name, and ye receive me not: if another shall come in his own name, him ye will receive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 12:43; Romans 2:29; Philippians 2:3"> How can ye believe, who receive glory one of another, and the glory that cometh from the only God ye seek not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 3:19–20; Galatians 3:10; 2 Corinthians 3:7–11"> Think not that I will accuse you to the Father: there is one that accuseth you, even Moses, on whom ye have set your hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15; Deuteronomy 18:18–19; John 1:45"> For if ye believed Moses, ye would believe me; for he wrote of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Luke 16:29"> But if ye believe not his writings, how shall ye believe my words?</span>
</div>
