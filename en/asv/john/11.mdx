<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; John 12:17; 2 Kings 20:1–12"> Now a certain man was sick, Lazarus of Bethany, of the village of Mary and her sister Martha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 12:3; Mark 14:3; Matthew 26:6–7"> And it was that Mary who anointed the Lord with ointment, and wiped his feet with her hair, whose brother Lazarus was sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:5; Revelation 3:19; 2 Timothy 4:20"> The sisters therefore sent unto him, saying, Lord, behold, he whom thou lovest is sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 11:40; John 9:3; 1 Peter 4:14"> But when Jesus heard it, he said, This sickness is not unto death, but for the glory of God, that the Son of God may be glorified thereby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:9–13; John 16:27; John 17:26"> Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 30:18; Isaiah 55:8–9; Genesis 45:1–5"> When therefore he heard that he was sick, he abode at that time two days in the place where he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:40–42; Luke 9:51; Acts 20:22–24"> Then after this he saith to the disciples, Let us go into Judæa again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:31; John 8:59; Acts 21:12–13"> The disciples say unto him, Rabbi, the Jews were but now seeking to stone thee; and goest thou thither again?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 9:4; John 12:35; Jeremiah 31:9"> Jesus answered, Are there not twelve hours in the day? If a man walk in the day, he stumbleth not, because he seeth the light of this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:10–11; Proverbs 4:18–19; Jeremiah 13:16"> But if a man walk in the night, he stumbleth, because the light is not in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 11:13; John 11:43–44"> These things spake he: and after this he saith unto them, Our friend Lazarus is fallen asleep; but I go, that I may awake him out of sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The disciples therefore said unto him, Lord, if he is fallen asleep, he will recover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:24"> Now Jesus had spoken of his death: but they thought that he spake of taking rest in sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:25; John 16:29; John 10:24"> Then Jesus therefore said unto them plainly, Lazarus is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; John 14:10–11; Isaiah 54:15"> And I am glad for your sakes that I was not there, to the intent ye may believe; nevertheless let us go unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 13:37; Matthew 10:3; John 21:2"> Thomas therefore, who is called Didymus, said unto his fellow-disciples, Let us also go, that we may die with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 11:39; Hosea 6:2; Acts 2:27–31"> So when Jesus came, he found that he had been in the tomb four days already.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 14:20; Revelation 21:16; John 6:19"> Now Bethany was nigh unto Jerusalem, about fifteen furlongs off;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 2:11; John 11:31; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> and many of the Jews had come to Martha and Mary, to console them concerning their brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; 1 Thessalonians 4:17; Matthew 25:1"> Martha therefore, when she heard that Jesus was coming, went and met him: but Mary still sat in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:32; 1 Kings 17:18; Matthew 9:18"> Martha therefore said unto Jesus, Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:31; John 11:41–42; Matthew 28:18"> And even now I know that, whatsoever thou shalt ask of God, God will give thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:43–44"> Jesus saith unto her, Thy brother shall rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Acts 24:15; John 5:28–29"> Martha saith unto him, I know that he shall rise again in the resurrection at the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14; John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12"> Jesus said unto her, I am the resurrection, and the life: he that believeth on me, though he die, yet shall he live;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 10:28; John 5:24; Romans 8:13"> and whosoever liveth and believeth on me shall never die. Believest thou this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:16; John 6:69; John 6:14"> She saith unto him, Yea, Lord: I have believed that thou art the Christ, the Son of God, even he that cometh into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 11:20; John 13:13; Mark 10:49"> And when she had said this, she went away, and called Mary her sister secretly, saying, The Teacher is here, and calleth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Psalms 27:8; Song of Solomon 3:1–4"> And she, when she heard it, arose quickly, and went unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:20"> (Now Jesus was not yet come into the village, but was still in the place where Martha met him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:19; Genesis 37:35; 2 Samuel 12:16–18"> The Jews then who were with her in the house, and were consoling her, when they saw Mary, that she rose up quickly and went out, followed her, supposing that she was going unto the tomb to weep there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 11:21; Luke 17:16; Luke 5:8"> Mary therefore, when she came where Jesus was, and saw him, fell down at his feet, saying unto him, Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:38; John 12:27; Mark 3:5"> When Jesus therefore saw her weeping, and the Jews also weeping who came with her, he groaned in the spirit, and was troubled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 15:47; Matthew 28:6; John 1:39"> and said, Where have ye laid him? They say unto him, Lord, come and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Hebrews 4:15; John 11:33"> Jesus wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 11:3; 1 John 3:1; 1 John 4:9–10"> The Jews therefore said, Behold how he loved him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 11:32; Luke 23:39"> But some of them said, Could not this man, who opened the eyes of him that was blind, have caused that this man also should not die?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:33; Luke 24:2; Matthew 27:60"> Jesus therefore again groaning in himself cometh to the tomb. Now it was a cave, and a stone lay against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 11:17; Mark 16:3; Psalms 49:7"> Jesus saith, Take ye away the stone. Martha, the sister of him that was dead, saith unto him, Lord, by this time the body decayeth; for he hath been dead four days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:23–26; Romans 4:17–25"> Jesus saith unto her, Said I not unto thee, that, if thou believedst, thou shouldest see the glory of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; John 17:1; Psalms 123:1"> So they took away the stone. And Jesus lifted up his eyes, and said, Father, I thank thee that thou heardest me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 3:17; John 17:21; 1 John 4:14"> And I knew that thou hearest me always: but because of the multitude that standeth around I said it, that they may believe that thou didst send me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33–36; Acts 9:40; Acts 9:34"> And when he had thus spoken, he cried with a loud voice, Lazarus, come forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 5:25; John 20:7; John 11:25–26"> He that was dead came forth, bound hand and foot with grave-clothes; and his face was bound about with a napkin. Jesus saith unto them, Loose him, and let him go.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 11:19; John 10:41"> Many therefore of the Jews, who came to Mary and beheld that which he did, believed on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 9:13; Acts 5:25; John 12:37"> But some of them went away to the Pharisees, and told them the things which Jesus had done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 12:19; Acts 4:5–6"> The chief priests therefore and the Pharisees gathered a council, and said, What do we? for this man doeth many signs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Matthew 23:35–38; Matthew 27:25"> If we let him thus alone, all men will believe on him: and the Romans will come and take away both our place and our nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 11:51; John 18:13–14"> But a certain one of them, Caiaphas, being high priest that year, said unto them, Ye know nothing at all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:14; Luke 24:46; Romans 3:8"> nor do ye take account that it is expedient for you that one man should die for the people, and that the whole nation perish not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Exodus 28:30"> Now this he said not of himself: but being high priest that year, he prophesied that Jesus should die for the nation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 10:16; Isaiah 49:6; 1 John 2:2"> and not for the nation only, but that he might also gather together into one the children of God that are scattered abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Acts 5:33; Acts 9:23; Mark 14:1"> So from that day forth they took counsel that they might put him to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 7:1; John 7:4; 2 Chronicles 13:19"> Jesus therefore walked no more openly among the Jews, but departed thence into the country near to the wilderness, into a city called Ephraim; and there he tarried with the disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:17–20; Hebrews 9:13–14; John 6:4"> Now the passover of the Jews was at hand: and many went up to Jerusalem out of the country before the passover, to purify themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 7:11; John 11:7–8"> They sought therefore for Jesus, and spake one with another, as they stood in the temple, What think ye? That he will not come to the feast?</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 9:22; John 8:59; John 5:16–18"> Now the chief priests and the Pharisees had given commandment, that, if any man knew where he was, he should show it, that they might take him.</span>
</div>
