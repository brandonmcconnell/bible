<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 20:19"> Then Pilate therefore took Jesus, and scourged him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Luke 23:11; Matthew 27:27–31"> And the soldiers platted a crown of thorns, and put it on his head, and arrayed him in a purple garment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 27:29; Matthew 26:49; Luke 1:28"> and they came unto him, and said, Hail, King of the Jews! and they struck him with their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; John 19:6; Luke 23:4"> And Pilate went out again, and saith unto them, Behold, I bring him out to you, that ye may know that I find no crime in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:2; Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 7:14"> Jesus therefore came out, wearing the crown of thorns and the purple garment. And Pilate saith unto them, Behold, the man!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:31; John 19:4; Acts 2:23"> When therefore the chief priests and the officers saw him, they cried out, saying, Crucify him, crucify him! Pilate saith unto them, Take him yourselves, and crucify him: for I find no crime in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 24:16; John 5:18; Matthew 26:63–66"> The Jews answered him, We have a law, and by that law he ought to die, because he made himself the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 19:13; Acts 14:11–19"> When Pilate therefore heard this saying, he was the more afraid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 18:33; Psalms 38:13–15; Philippians 1:28"> and he entered into the Prætorium again, and saith unto Jesus, Whence art thou? But Jesus gave him no answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 5:19; John 18:39; Daniel 3:14–15"> Pilate therefore saith unto him, Speakest thou not unto me? knowest thou not that I have power to release thee, and have power to crucify thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 13:1; John 3:27; Acts 2:23"> Jesus answered him, Thou wouldest have no power against me, except it were given thee from above: therefore he that delivered me unto thee hath greater sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 18:33–36; Mark 6:16–26"> Upon this Pilate sought to release him: but the Jews cried out, saying, If thou release this man, thou art not Cæsar’s friend: every one that maketh himself a king speaketh against Cæsar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 82:5–7; Matthew 27:19; Amos 4:7"> When Pilate therefore heard these words, he brought Jesus out, and sat down on the judgment-seat at a place called The Pavement, but in Hebrew, Gabbatha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:5; Mark 15:25"> Now it was the Preparation of the passover: it was about the sixth hour. And he saith unto the Jews, Behold, your King!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 23:18; Acts 21:36; John 19:6"> They therefore cried out, Away with him, away with him, crucify him! Pilate saith unto them, Shall I crucify your King? The chief priests answered, We have no king but Cæsar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 23:24–25; Matthew 27:26–31; Mark 15:15–20"> Then therefore he delivered him unto them to be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:33; Luke 14:27; Luke 23:26"> They took Jesus therefore: and he went out, bearing the cross for himself, unto the place called The place of a skull, which is called in Hebrew Golgotha:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Mark 15:24–27; Galatians 3:13"> where they crucified him, and with him two others, on either side one, and Jesus in the midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 27:37; Luke 23:38; Mark 15:26"> And Pilate wrote a title also, and put it on the cross. And there was written, JESUS OF NAZARETH, THE KING OF THE JEWS.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 26:14; Revelation 16:16; Acts 22:2"> This title therefore read many of the Jews, for the place where Jesus was crucified was nigh to the city; and it was written in Hebrew, and in Latin, and in Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:19"> The chief priests of the Jews therefore said to Pilate, Write not, The King of the Jews; but, that he said, I am King of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 76:10; Psalms 65:7; Proverbs 8:29"> Pilate answered, What I have written I have written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 23:34; Mark 15:24; Matthew 27:35"> The soldiers therefore, when they had crucified Jesus, took his garments and made four parts, to every soldier a part; and also the coat: now the coat was without seam, woven from the top throughout.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 22:18; John 19:28; John 19:36–37"> They said therefore one to another, Let us not rend it, but cast lots for it, whose it shall be: that the scripture might be fulfilled, which saith,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">They parted my garments among them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And upon my vesture did they cast lots.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:55–56; John 20:1"> These things therefore the soldiers did. But there were standing by the cross of Jesus his mother, and his mother’s sister, Mary the wife of Clopas, and Mary Magdalene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 21:24; John 20:2; John 2:4"> When Jesus therefore saw his mother, and the disciple standing by whom he loved, he saith unto his mother, Woman, behold, thy son!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 12:48–50; John 16:32; 1 Timothy 5:2–4"> Then saith he to the disciple, Behold, thy mother! And from that hour the disciple took her unto his own home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Psalms 22:15; John 19:30"> After this Jesus, knowing that all things are now finished, that the scripture might be accomplished, saith, I thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 19:18; Luke 23:36; Mark 15:36"> There was set there a vessel full of vinegar: so they put a sponge full of the vinegar upon hyssop, and brought it to his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 17:4; Philippians 2:8; Colossians 2:14–17"> When Jesus therefore had received the vinegar, he said, It is finished: and he bowed his head, and gave up his spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:42; Mark 15:42"> The Jews therefore, because it was the Preparation, that the bodies should not remain on the cross upon the sabbath (for the day of that sabbath was a high day), asked of Pilate that their legs might be broken, and that they might be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43"> The soldiers therefore came, and brake the legs of the first, and of the other that was crucified with him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> but when they came to Jesus, and saw that he was dead already, they brake not his legs:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 John 5:6; Zechariah 12:10; Zechariah 13:1"> howbeit one of the soldiers with a spear pierced his side, and straightway there came out blood and water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 21:24; Hebrews 2:3–4; 1 John 1:1–3"> And he that hath seen hath borne witness, and his witness is true: and he knoweth that he saith true, that ye also may believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 34:20; Numbers 9:12; Exodus 12:46"> For these things came to pass, that the scripture might be fulfilled, A bone of him shall not be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Revelation 1:7; Psalms 22:16–17"> And again another scripture saith, They shall look on him whom they pierced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:42–47; Matthew 27:57–61; Proverbs 29:25"> And after these things Joseph of Arimathæa, being a disciple of Jesus, but secretly for fear of the Jews, asked of Pilate that he might take away the body of Jesus: and Pilate gave him leave. He came therefore, and took away his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 7:50–52; Song of Solomon 4:14; John 3:1–21"> And there came also Nicodemus, he who at the first came to him by night, bringing a mixture of myrrh and aloes, about a hundred pounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:44; John 20:5–7; Acts 5:6"> So they took the body of Jesus, and bound it in linen cloths with the spices, as the custom of the Jews is to bury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 23:53; Matthew 27:60; John 20:15"> Now in the place where he was crucified there was a garden; and in the garden a new tomb wherein was never man yet laid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 19:31; John 19:14; Isaiah 53:9"> There then because of the Jews’ Preparation (for the tomb was nigh at hand) they laid Jesus.</span>
