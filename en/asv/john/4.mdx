<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 3:26; John 3:22; 2 Corinthians 4:5"> When therefore the Lord knew that the Pharisees had heard that Jesus was making and baptizing more disciples than John</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:48; 1 Corinthians 1:13–17"> (although Jesus himself baptized not, but his disciples),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; John 10:40; John 2:11"> he left Judæa, and departed again into Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 17:11; Luke 2:49; Luke 9:51–52"> And he must needs pass through Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 24:32; Genesis 33:19; Genesis 48:22"> So he cometh to a city of Samaria, called Sychar, near to the parcel of ground that Jacob gave to his son Joseph:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 2:17; Matthew 8:24"> and Jacob’s well was there. Jesus therefore, being wearied with his journey, sat thus by the well. It was about the sixth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; 1 Kings 17:10; Genesis 24:43"> There cometh a woman of Samaria to draw water: Jesus saith unto her, Give me to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:13; John 4:39; John 4:5"> For his disciples were gone away into the city to buy food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Matthew 10:5; 2 Kings 17:24–41"> The Samaritan woman therefore saith unto him, How is it that thou, being a Jew, askest drink of me, who am a Samaritan woman? (For Jews have no dealings with Samaritans.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:14; Revelation 7:17; John 7:37–39"> Jesus answered and said unto her, If thou knewest the gift of God, and who it is that saith to thee, Give me to drink; thou wouldest have asked of him, and he would have given thee living water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 3:4"> The woman saith unto him, Sir, thou hast nothing to draw with, and the well is deep: whence then hast thou that living water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 4:6; John 8:53; Matthew 12:42"> Art thou greater than our father Jacob, who gave us the well, and drank thereof himself, and his sons, and his cattle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:27; Isaiah 65:13–14; Luke 16:24"> Jesus answered and said unto her, Every one that drinketh of this water shall thirst again:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:38–39; John 6:35; Isaiah 49:10"> but whosoever drinketh of the water that I shall give him shall never thirst; but the water that I shall give him shall become in him a well of water springing up unto eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 6:34; Romans 8:5; Psalms 4:6"> The woman saith unto him, Sir, give me this water, that I thirst not, neither come all the way hither to draw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23; John 4:18"> Jesus saith unto her, Go, call thy husband, and come hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The woman answered and said unto him, I have no husband. Jesus saith unto her, Thou saidst well, I have no husband:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:20; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Romans 7:3"> for thou hast had five husbands; and he whom thou now hast is not thy husband: this hast thou said truly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 6:14; John 9:17"> The woman saith unto him, Sir, I perceive that thou art a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 12:6–7; Deuteronomy 27:12; Deuteronomy 11:29"> Our fathers worshipped in this mountain; and ye say, that in Jerusalem is the place where men ought to worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:8; Malachi 1:11; John 4:23"> Jesus saith unto her, Woman, believe me, the hour cometh, when neither in this mountain, nor in Jerusalem, shall ye worship the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 17:23; Isaiah 2:3; Romans 9:4–5"> Ye worship that which ye know not: we worship that which we know; for salvation is from the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 3:3; John 5:25; Matthew 15:7–9"> But the hour cometh, and now is, when the true worshippers shall worship the Father in spirit and truth: for such doth the Father seek to be his worshippers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Matthew 15:8–9; Philippians 3:3"> God is a Spirit: and they that worship him must worship in spirit and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 1:49; Daniel 9:24–26; John 1:41–42"> The woman saith unto him, I know that Messiah cometh (he that is called Christ): when he is come, he will declare unto us all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:24; Mark 14:61–62; Romans 10:20–21"> Jesus saith unto her, I that speak unto thee am he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 4:8–9"> And upon this came his disciples; and they marvelled that he was speaking with a woman; yet no man said, What seekest thou? or, Why speakest thou with her?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 4:7; Luke 24:33; Mark 16:8–10"> So the woman left her waterpot, and went away into the city, and saith to the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; 1 Corinthians 14:24–25; John 4:25"> Come, see a man, who told me all things that ever I did: can this be the Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 5:20; Acts 8:5–8; Luke 17:16–18"> They went out of the city, and were coming to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:38; Genesis 24:33; Acts 16:30–34"> In the mean while the disciples prayed him, saying, Rabbi, eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 23:12; Psalms 119:103; John 4:34"> But he said unto them, I have meat to eat that ye know not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 16:6–11; Luke 9:45"> The disciples therefore said one to another, Hath any man brought him aught to eat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 6:38; John 5:30; Job 23:12"> Jesus saith unto them, My meat is to do the will of him that sent me, and to accomplish his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 10:2–3; Matthew 9:37–38; John 4:30"> Say not ye, There are yet four months, and then cometh the harvest? behold, I say unto you, Lift up your eyes, and look on the fields, that they are white already unto harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="James 5:19–20; Romans 1:13; 1 Corinthians 3:5–9"> He that reapeth receiveth wages, and gathereth fruit unto life eternal; that he that soweth and he that reapeth may rejoice together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Job 31:8; Luke 19:21"> For herein is the saying true, One soweth, and another reapeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 1:7; 2 Chronicles 36:15; Acts 10:42–43"> I sent you to reap that whereon ye have not labored: others have labored, and ye are entered into their labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 4:29; John 4:5; John 10:41–42"> And from that city many of the Samaritans believed on him because of the word of the woman, who testified, He told me all things that ever I did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Acts 16:15; Luke 24:29"> So when the Samaritans came unto him, they besought him to abide with them: and he abode there two days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4–5; Acts 1:8; Acts 8:12"> And many more believed because of his word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 1:29; 1 John 5:20"> and they said to the woman, Now we believe, not because of thy speaking: for we have heard for ourselves, and know that this is indeed the Saviour of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 4:13; Romans 15:8; Mark 7:27–28"> And after the two days he went forth from thence into Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 13:57; Luke 4:24; Mark 6:4"> For Jesus himself testified, that a prophet hath no honor in his own country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 2:13–16; Deuteronomy 16:16"> So when he came into Galilee, the Galilæans received him, having seen all the things that he did in Jerusalem at the feast: for they also went unto the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 2:1–11; Luke 7:2; Hosea 5:15"> He came therefore again unto Cana of Galilee, where he made the water wine. And there was a certain nobleman, whose son was sick at Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 4:3; John 11:21; John 11:32"> When he heard that Jesus was come out of Judæa into Galilee, he went unto him, and besought him that he would come down, and heal his son; for he was at the point of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 20:29; Hebrews 2:4; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> Jesus therefore said unto him, Except ye see signs and wonders, ye will in no wise believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 5:23; Psalms 88:10–12; Psalms 40:17"> The nobleman saith unto him, Sir, come down ere my child die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 8:13; Mark 7:29–30; Romans 4:20–21"> Jesus saith unto him, Go thy way; thy son liveth. The man believed the word that Jesus spake unto him, and he went his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Kings 17:23; John 4:50; John 4:53"> And as he was now going down, his servants met him, saying, that his son lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> So he inquired of them the hour when he began to amend. They said therefore unto him, Yesterday at the seventh hour the fever left him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Luke 19:9; Acts 16:34"> So the father knew that it was at that hour in which Jesus said unto him, Thy son liveth: and himself believed, and his whole house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 4:45; John 2:1–11"> This is again the second sign that Jesus did, having come out of Judæa into Galilee.</span>
