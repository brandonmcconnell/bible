<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:15–16; TEHILLIM 82:6; YOCHANAN 10:35–36"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, See, I have made thee as Elohim to Pharaoh: and Aharon thy brother shall be thy navi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:15; SHEMOT 6:29; DEVARIM 4:2"> Thou shalt speak all that I command thee: and Aharon thy brother shall speak unto Pharaoh, that he send the Bnei Yisroel out of his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:21; SHEMOT 11:9; TEHILLIM 105:27–36"> And I will harden the lev Pharaoh, and multiply My Otot and My Mofetim in Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 11:9; SHEMOT 6:6; SHEMOT 12:51"> But Pharaoh shall not pay heed unto you, that I may lay My yad upon Mitzrayim, and bring forth Mine tzve'os, and My people the Bnei Yisroel, out of Eretz Mitzrayim by shefatim gedolim (great judgments).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 14:18; SHEMOT 7:17; SHEMOT 8:22"> And the Egyptians shall know that I am Hashem, when I stretch forth My yad upon Mitzrayim, bringing out Bnei Yisroel from among them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 7:2; BERESHIS 6:22; SHEMOT 7:10"> And Moshe and Aharon did as Hashem commanded them, so did they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 34:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:30; DEVARIM 31:2"> And Moshe was fourscore years old, and Aharon fourscore three years, when they spoke unto Pharaoh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe and Aharon, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:2; YOCHANAN 2:18; YESHAYAH 7:11"> When Pharaoh shall speak unto you, saying, Show a mofet (miracle) for you: then thou shalt say unto Aharon, Take thy matteh, and cast it before Pharaoh, and it shall become a serpent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:3; LUKAS 10:19; AMOS 9:3"> And Moshe and Aharon went in unto Pharaoh, and they did so as Hashem had commanded: and Aharon cast down his matteh before Pharaoh, and his avadim, and it became a serpent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 7:22; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:8; SHEMOT 8:18"> Then Pharaoh also called the chachamim and the mechashefim: now the charetumei Mitzrayim, they also did in like manner with their secret lore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:4; SHEMOT 9:11; SHEMOT 8:18–19"> For they cast down every man his matteh, and they became serpents: but the matteh Aharon swallowed up their mattot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:21; SHEMOT 8:15; ZECHARYAH 7:11–12"> And He hardened lev Pharaoh, that he paid heed not unto them; as Hashem had said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 10:20; SHEMOT 8:15; SHEMOT 10:1"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, the lev Pharaoh is hardened, he refuseth to let the people go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 8:20; SHEMOT 2:5; SHEMOT 7:10"> Get thee unto Pharaoh in the boker; hinei, he goeth out unto the mayim; and thou shalt stand by the bank of the Nile to meet him; and the matteh which was turned to a nachash shalt thou take in thine yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:18; SHEMOT 9:1; SHEMOT 9:13"> And thou shalt say unto him, Hashem Elohei HaIvrim hath sent me unto thee, saying, Let My people go, that they may serve Me in the midbar: and, hinei, hitherto thou wouldest not hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:9; SHEMOT 5:2; SHEMOT 7:5"> Thus saith Hashem, In this thou shalt know that I am Hashem: hinei, I will strike with the matteh that is in Mine yad upon the mayim which are in the Nile, and they shall be turned to dahm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 7:24; SHEMOT 7:21; BAMIDBAR 11:20"> And the dag (fish) that is in the Nile shall die, and the Nile shall stink; and the Egyptians shall loathe to drink of the mayim of the Nile.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 14:21; SHEMOT 10:12; SHEMOT 8:5–6"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, Say unto Aharon, Take thy matteh, and stretch out thine yad upon the mayim of Mitzrayim, upon their streams, upon their rivers, and upon their ponds, and upon all their mikveh of mayim, that they may become dahm; and that there may be dahm throughout kol Eretz Mitzrayim, both in vessels of wood, and in vessels of stone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:44; TEHILLIM 105:29; SHEMOT 7:17–18"> And Moshe and Aharon did so, as Hashem commanded; and he lifted up the matteh, and smote the waters that were in the Nile, in the sight of Pharaoh, and in the sight of his avadim; and all the mayim that were in the Nile were turned to dahm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 7:18; HISGALUS 8:9"> And the dag (fish) that was in the Nile died; and the Nile stank, and the Egyptians could not drink of the mayim of the Nile; and there was dahm throughout kol Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHEMOT 7:11; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:8; YIRMEYAH 27:18"> And the charetumei Mitzrayim did so with their secret lore: and lev Pharaoh was hardened, neither did he pay heed unto them; as Hashem had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 36:24; YECHEZKEL 40:4; MALACHI 2:2"> And Pharaoh turned and went into his bais, neither did he set his mind to this also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And all the Egyptians dug around the Nile for mayim to drink; they could not drink of the mayim of the Nile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHEMOT 10:23; SHEMOT 8:9–10; SHMUEL BAIS 24:13"> And seven days came and went, after Hashem had struck the Nile.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, Go unto Pharaoh, and say unto him, Thus saith Hashem, Let My people go, that they may serve Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And if thou refuse to let them go, hinei, I will strike all thy borders with tzefardaya:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the river shall bring forth tzefardaya abundantly, which shall go up and come into thine bais, and into thy cheder mishkav, and upon thy bed, and into the bais of thy avadim, and upon thy people, and into thine ovens, and into thy kneading bowls:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And the tzefardaya shall come up both on thee, and upon thy people, and upon all thy avadim.</span>
</div>
