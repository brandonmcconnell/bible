<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:1; SHEMOT 12:6; VAYIKRA 23:16–18"> In the chodesh hashelishi (third new moon), after the Bnei Yisroel had their exodus from Eretz Mitzrayim, on that very day they came into the Midbar Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:1; SHEMOT 17:1; SHEMOT 3:12"> For they were departed from Rephidim, and were come to Midbar Sinai, and had encamped in the midbar; and there Yisroel camped before HaHar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:38; SHEMOT 20:21"> And Moshe went up to HaElohim, and Hashem called out unto him from HaHar, saying, Thus shalt thou say to the Bais Ya'akov, and declare unto the Bnei Yisroel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:11–12; YESHAYAH 63:9; YESHAYAH 40:31"> You yourselves have seen what I did unto the Mitzrayim, and how I carried you on eaglesʼ wings, and brought you unto Myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 14:2; DEVARIM 7:6; DEVARIM 26:18"> Now therefore, if ye will obey My voice very carefully, and be shomer over My brit, then ye shall be a segullah (treasured possession) unto Me above all people; for kol ha'aretz is Mine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HISGALUS 5:10; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5"> And ye shall be unto Me a mamlechet kohanim, and a goy kadosh. These are the words which thou shalt recount unto the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:29–30; SHEMOT 3:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:1"> And Moshe came and summoned the Ziknei HaAm, and set authoritatively before their faces all these words just as Hashem commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 24:7; SHEMOT 24:3; YEHOSHUA 24:24"> And kol HaAm answered together, and said, All that Hashem hath spoken we will do. And Moshe brought back the words of HaAm unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:36; SHEMOT 19:16; SHEMOT 24:15–16"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, Hinei, I am coming unto thee in a thick cloud, that HaAm may hear when I speak with thee, and believe thee l'olam. Then Moshe told the words of HaAm unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:22; BERESHIS 35:2; VAYIKRA 11:44–45"> So Hashem said unto Moshe, Go unto HaAm, and set them apart as kodesh today and tomorrow, and let them wash their clothing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:16; SHEMOT 34:5; DEVARIM 33:2"> And be ready by Yom HaShelishi; for Yom HaShelishi Hashem will come down in the sight of kol HaAm upon Mt. Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:20–21; YEHOSHUA 3:4; SHEMOT 19:21"> And thou shalt set boundaries unto HaAm all around, saying, Take heed to yourselves, that ye go not up into HaHar, or touch the edge of it; whosoever toucheth HaHar shall be surely put to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:16; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:20; SHEMOT 19:16"> There shall no yad touch him, but he shall surely be stoned, or surely mortally shot; whether it be behemah or ish, it shall not live; when the yovel soundeth long, they shall come up to HaHar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:10"> So Moshe came down from HaHar unto HaAm, and set apart as kodesh HaAm; and they washed their clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:5; SHMUEL ALEF 21:4–5; SHEMOT 19:11"> Then he said to HaAm, Be ready by Sheloshet Yamim; come not into your isha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:18–19; TEHILLIM 18:11–14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:21"> And it came to pass on the Yom HaShelishi when the boker was breaking, that there were thunderings and lightnings, and a heavy cloud upon HaHar, and the blast of the shofar exceeding loud; so that kol HaAm that was in the machaneh trembled with terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:10; DEVARIM 5:5"> And Moshe led HaAm forth out of the machaneh to encounter HaElohim; and they stood at the foot of HaHar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHOFETIM 5:5; SHEMOT 3:2; TEHILLIM 144:5"> And Mt. Sinai was altogether smoking, because Hashem descended upon it in eish; and the smoke thereof ascended as the smoke of the furnace, and kol HaHar shook violently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="TEHILLIM 81:7; SHEMOT 19:16; NECHEMYAH 9:13"> And when the sound of the shofar was moving &#91;closer&#93;, and grew louder and louder, Moshe spoke, and HaElohim answered him in thunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 9:13; SHEMOT 34:2; SHEMOT 19:11"> And Hashem came down upon Mt. Sinai, onto the top of HaHar; and Hashem summoned Moshe up to the top of HaHar; and Moshe went up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 6:19; SHEMOT 3:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:28–29"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, Go down, warn HaAm, lest they push through toward Hashem to gaze, and many of them fall perishing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHEMOT 24:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 13:9–11"> And let the kohanim also, which come near to Hashem, set themselves apart as kodesh, lest Hashem break forth upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:12; YEHOSHUA 3:4–5"> And Moshe said unto Hashem, HaAm cannot come up to Mt. Sinai; for You Yourself charged us, saying, Establish boundaries for HaHar, and set it apart as kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:16; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:18–25; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:29"> And Hashem said unto him, Away, get thee down, and thou shalt come up, thou, and Aharon with thee; but let not the kohanim and HaAm push through to come up unto Hashem, lest He break forth upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> So Moshe went down unto HaAm and told them.</span>
