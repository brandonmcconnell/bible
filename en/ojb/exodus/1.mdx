## &#91;SHEMOS&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:1–2; BERESHIS 46:8–26; BERESHIS 35:23–26"> Now these are the Shemot Bnei Yisroel, which came into Mitzrayim; every ish and his bais came with Ya'akov:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 35:22"> Reuven, Shim'on, Levi, and Yehudah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:20; BERESHIS 35:23"> Yissakhar, Zevulun, and Binyamin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Dan, and Naphtali, Gad, and Asher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:22; BERESHIS 46:26–27; SHOFETIM 8:30"> And all the nefesh that came out of the loins of Ya'akov were seventy nefesh; for Yosef was in Mitzrayim already.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 50:26; BERESHIS 50:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:14–16"> And Yosef died, and all his achim, and all that generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:3; BERESHIS 1:28; DEVARIM 26:5"> And the Bnei Yisroel were fruitful, and increased abundantly, and multiplied, and became exceeding strong; and the land was filled with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:18; KOHELET 2:18–19; KOHELET 9:15"> Now there arose a melech chadash over Mitzrayim, which had no da'as of Yosef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 105:24–25; KOHELET 4:4; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:14–16"> And he said unto his people, Hinei, the Am Bnei Yisroel are more and stronger than we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:19; TEHILLIM 83:3–4; MISHLE 1:11"> Come, let us outsmart them; lest they multiply, and it come to pass, that, when there falleth out any milchamah, they join also unto our enemies, and fight against us, and so get them up out of the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:13; BERESHIS 47:11; SHEMOT 3:7"> Therefore they did set over them sarei missim (taskmasters) to afflict them with their burdens. And they built for Pharaoh supply cities, Pitom and Ra'meses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="TEHILLIM 105:24; SHEMOT 1:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:28"> But the more they afflicted them, the more they multiplied and spread out. And they were in dread because of the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:20"> And the Egyptians made the Bnei Yisroel to serve with rigor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:19; BAMIDBAR 20:15; SHEMOT 2:23"> And they made their lives bitter with avodah kashah (hard bondage), in mortar, and in brick, and in all manner of avodah in the sadeh; all their service, wherein they made them serve, was with rigor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Melech Mitzrayim spoke to the meyalledot HaIvriyyot (Hebrew midwives), of which the shem of the one was Shiphrah, and of the other Puah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 1:22; MATTITYAHU 21:38; HISGALUS 12:4"> And he said, When ye do the office of a midwife to HaIvriyyot (the Hebrew women) and see them upon the delivery stool; if it be a ben, then ye shall kill him; but if it be a bat, then she shall live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:29; SHEMOT 1:21; MATTITYAHU 10:28"> But the meyalledot feared HaElohim, and did not as Melech Mitzrayim commanded them, but saved the yeladim alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="KOHELET 8:4; SHMUEL BAIS 13:28"> So Melech Mitzrayim called for the meyalledot, and said unto them, Why have ye done this thing, and have saved the yeladim alive?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 2:4–24; SHMUEL BAIS 17:19–20; SHMUEL ALEF 21:2"> And the meyalledot said unto Pharaoh, Because HaIvriyyot are not as the Egyptian women; for they are vigorous, and are delivered before the meyalledot come in unto them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YESHAYAH 3:10; KOHELET 8:12; MISHLE 11:18"> Therefore Elohim dealt well with the meyalledot; and the people multiplied, and grew very strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:38; SHMUEL ALEF 2:35; TEHILLIM 37:3"> And it came to pass, because the meyalledot feared HaElohim, that He established them batim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:19; MISHLE 1:16; SHEMOT 1:16"> And Pharaoh charged all his people, saying, Every ben that is born ye shall cast into the Nile, and every bat ye shall save alive.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
