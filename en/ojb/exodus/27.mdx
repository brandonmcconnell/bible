<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHEMOT 38:1–7; SHEMOT 20:24–26; SHEMOT 40:10"> And thou shalt build the Mizbe'ach of acacia wood, five cubits long, and five cubits wide; the Mizbe'ach shall be foursquare; and the height thereof shall be shalosh cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="TEHILLIM 118:27; SHEMOT 29:12; VAYIKRA 4:7"> And thou shalt make the karnot of it upon the four corners thereof; its karnot shall be of one piece with it; and thou shalt overlay it with nechoshet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:45; BAMIDBAR 4:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:17"> And thou shalt make its pots to receive its ashes, and its shovels, and its basins, and its meat forks, and its firepans; all the vessels thereof thou shalt make of nechoshet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 35:16; SHEMOT 25:12; SHEMOT 38:4–5"> And thou shalt build for it a mikhbar (grate), a strainer reshet (mesh, netting) of nechoshet; and upon the meshwork shalt thou make four taba'ot (rings) of nechoshet in the four corners thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 38:4"> And thou shalt put it under the karkov (&#91;surrounding&#93; border, ledge) of the Mizbe'ach from below, that the meshwork may be half the height of the Mizbe'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 35:13–15; BAMIDBAR 4:44; SHEMOT 30:4"> And thou shalt make badim (carrying poles) designed for the Mizbe'ach, poles of acacia wood, and overlay them with nechoshet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:28; SHEMOT 30:4; BAMIDBAR 4:13–14"> And the badim shall be put into the taba'ot, and the badim shall be upon the two sides of the Mizbe'ach, to carry it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:40; SHEMOT 25:9; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:5"> Hollow, with luchot (planks, boards, panels), shalt thou make as it was showed thee on HaHar, so shall they make it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 38:9–20; YECHEZKEL 40:32; YECHEZKEL 42:3"> And thou shalt make the Khatzer (Courtyard) of the Mishkan; for the south side southward there shall be kela'im (curtains, hangings) for the Khatzer (Courtyard) of twisted linen of a hundred cubits long for one side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:19–21; YIRMEYAH 52:21; SHEMOT 36:38"> And the twenty ammudim thereof and their twenty sockets shall be of nechoshet; the hooks of the ammudim and their bands shall be of kesef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And likewise for the north side in length there shall be kela'im (curtains, hangings) of a hundred cubits long, and its twenty ammudim and their twenty sockets of nechoshet; the hooks of the ammudim and their bands of kesef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And for the width of the Khatzer (Courtyard) on the west side shall be kela'im (curtains, hangings) of fifty cubits; their ammudim ten, and their sockets ten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the width of the Khatzer (Courtyard) on the east side mizrachah (eastward, toward sunrise) shall be fifty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 27:9; SHEMOT 26:36"> The kela'im (curtains, hangings) of one shoulder &#91;of the entrance&#93; shall be fifteen cubits; their ammudim shloshah, and their sockets shloshah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And on the other shoulder &#91;of the entrance&#93; shall be hangings fifteen cubits; their ammudim shloshah, and their sockets shloshah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:36; SHOFETIM 5:30; SHEMOT 28:39"> And for the sha'ar (gate) of the Khatzer (Courtyard) shall be a Masach (Screen) of twenty cubits, of turquoise, and purple, and scarlet, and twisted linen, wrought with ma'aseh rokem (craft or work of embroidering); and their ammudim shall be four, and their sockets four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> All the ammudim around the Khatzer (Courtyard) shall be banded with kesef; their hooks shall be of kesef, and their sockets of nechoshet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The length of the Khatzer (Courtyard) shall be a hundred cubits, and the width fifty by fifty, and the height five cubits of twisted linen, and their sockets of nechoshet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 35:18; YESHAYAH 22:23–25; BAMIDBAR 4:32"> All the vessels of the Mishkan in all the avodah (service) thereof, and all the pegs thereof, and all the pegs of the Khatzer (Courtyard), shall be of nechoshet.</span>
