## &#91;VAYAKHEL&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:32; SHEMOT 31:1–11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:13"> And Moshe assembled kol Adat Bnei Yisroel, and said unto them, These are the Devarim which Hashem hath commanded, that ye should do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:3; LUKAS 13:14–15; SHEMOT 20:9–10"> Sheshet yamim shall work be done, but on the Yom HaShevi'i shall be to you &#91;plural&#93; Kodesh Shabbos Shabbaton to Hashem; whosoever doeth work therein shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:23; BAMIDBAR 15:32–36; SHEMOT 12:16"> Ye shall kindle no eish throughout your habitations upon Yom HaShabbos.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:1–2"> And Moshe spoke unto kol Adat Bnei Yisroel, saying, This is the thing which Hashem commanded, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:7; TEHILLIM 110:3; SHEMOT 25:2–7"> Take ye from among you a terumah (offering) unto Hashem; all of nediv lev of him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., urged by his heart">a</a></sup>let him bring it, a terumat Hashem, zahav, and kesef, and nechoshet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:7–14; SHEMOT 26:31; SHEMOT 28:15"> And turquoise, and purple, and scarlet wool, and fine linen, and goat hair,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And red-dyed ram skins, and tachash skins, and acacia wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 27:20; SHEMOT 25:1–40; SHEMOT 30:23"> And shemen for the light, and spices for shemen hamishchah, and for the aromatic ketoret,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 39:6–14; SHEMOT 28:9; SHEMOT 25:5"> And shoham stones, and gemstones to be set for the Ephod, and for the Choshen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 31:1–6; SHEMOT 36:1–4"> And kol chacham lev among you shall come, and make all that Hashem hath commanded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:1–37; SHEMOT 36:8–34; SHEMOT 31:7–9"> The Mishkan, its Ohel, and its Mikhseh, its hooks, and its kerashim (planks), its crossbars, its ammudim, and its sockets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:10–22; SHEMOT 26:31–33; SHEMOT 37:1–9"> The Aron, and the carrying poles thereof, with the Kapporet, and the Parochet HaMasach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:23–30; VAYIKRA 24:5–6; SHEMOT 37:10–16"> The Shulchan, and its carrying poles, and all its utensils, and the Lechem HaPanim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:31–39; MATTITYAHU 5:14–15; SHEMOT 37:17–24"> The Menorat HaMaohr, and its utensils, and its nerot (lamps), with the Shemen HaMaohr,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 30:1–10; SHEMOT 30:22–38; SHEMOT 37:25–28"> And the Mizbe'ach HaKetoret, and its carrying poles, and the Shemen HaMishchah, and the ketoret spices, and the masach hapetach at the petach of the Mishkan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 27:1–8; SHEMOT 30:18–21; SHEMOT 38:1–8"> The Mizbe'ach HaOlah, with its mikhbar hanechoshet, its carrying poles, and all its utensils, the Kiyor, and its Stand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 7:2; SHEMOT 38:9–20; SHEMOT 27:9–19"> The curtains of the khatzer, its ammudim, and its sockets, and the masach Sha'ar HaKhatzer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 27:19"> The tent pegs of the Mishkan, and the tent pegs of the khatzer, and their cords,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 31:10; BAMIDBAR 4:5–15; SHEMOT 28:1–43"> The Bigdei HaSerad leSharet baKodesh (to do service in HaKodesh), the Bigdei HaKodesh for Aharon the kohen, and the garments of his banim, to minister in the kohenʼs office.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And all the Adat Bnei Yisroel departed from the presence of Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:2; SHEMOT 35:26; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:14"> And they came, every one whose lev moved him, and every one of nedevah ruach of him, and they brought terumat Hashem to the work of the Ohel Mo'ed, and for kol avodat of it, and for the Bigdei HaKodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 7:70–72; MATTITYAHU 2:11; EZRA 2:68–69"> And they came, both anashim and nashim, as many as were nediv lev, and brought bracelets, and face-adornment rings, and rings, and body ornaments, all jewelry of zahav; and every ish that offered offered a tenufat zahav unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:8; SHEMOT 25:2–7; SHEMOT 35:6–10"> And kol ish, with whom was found turquoise, and purple, and scarlet wool, and fine linen, and goat hair, and dyed red rams skin, and tachash skins, brought them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:12"> Every one that did offer a terumat kesef and nechoshet brought the terumat Hashem; and found acacia wood for any melachet haAvodah, brought it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:12; LUKAS 8:2–3; MISHLE 14:1"> And every isha that was chachmat lev spun with her hands, and brought the spun yarn, both of turquoise, and of purple, and of scarlet, and of fine linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHEMOT 35:21; SHEMOT 36:8; SHEMOT 35:29"> And all the nashim whose lev stirred them up in chochmah spun goat hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EZRA 2:68; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:6; SHEMOT 35:9"> And the nesi'im brought shoham stones, and gemstones to be set, for the Ephod, and for the Choshen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHEMOT 30:23–38; SHEMOT 35:8"> And spice, and shemen for the light, and for the shemen hamishchah, and for the ketoret spices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:7; SHEMOT 35:21–22; SHEMOT 35:4"> The Bnei Yisroel brought a nedavah unto Hashem, kol ish and isha, with nadav lev of them brought for kol hamelachah, which Hashem had commanded to be done through Moshe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="SHEMOT 31:1–6; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:11"> And Moshe said unto the Bnei Yisroel, See, Hashem hath called by shem Betzalel ben Uri, ben Chur, of the tribe of Yehudah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:17; YESHAYAH 11:2–5; YESHAYAH 61:1–3"> And He hath filled him with the Ruach Elohim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ac 2:4">b</a></sup>in chochmah, in understanding, and in da'as, and in all manner of workmanship;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And to make machashavot (artistic works), to work in zahav, and in kesef, and in nechoshet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And in the cutting of gems, to set them, and in wood carving, to make every melechet machshevet (work of art).</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="SHEMOT 31:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:14; EZRA 7:27"> And He hath given him the ability to teach, both he, and Oholiav ben Achisamach, of the tribe of Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="SHEMOT 35:31; MELACHIM ALEF 7:14; YESHAYAH 28:26"> Them hath He filled with chochmat lev, to do all manner of artistic work, of the carver, and of the weaver of artistic design work, and of the embroiderer, in turquoise, and in purple, in scarlet wool, and in fine linen, and of the weaver, even of them that do artistic work, and of those that make artistic designs.</span>
</div>
