<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:11; SHEMOT 25:24"> And thou shalt overlay it with zahav tahor, the top thereof, and the sides thereof all around, and the karnenot thereof; and thou shalt make unto it a crown of zahav all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:27; SHEMOT 26:29; SHEMOT 27:7"> And two tabe'ot zahav (golden rings) shalt thou make to it under the crown of it, by the two corners thereof, upon the two sides of it shalt thou make it; and they shall be for places for the carrying poles to bear it withal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:13; SHEMOT 25:27"> And thou shalt make the carrying poles of acacia wood, and overlay them with zahav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:21–22; SHEMOT 30:36; VAYIKRA 16:13"> And thou shalt put it in front of the Parochet that is before the Aron HaEdut, before the Kapporet that is over HaEdut, where I will set My meetings with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 2:28; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:13; SHEMOT 27:20–21"> And Aharon shall burn thereon spice ketoret every boker; when he cleaneth the nerot (lamps), he shall burn it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:25"> And when Aharon lighteth the nerot (lamps) in the afternoon, he shall burn ketoret upon it, ketoret tamid before Hashem throughout your dorot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="VAYIKRA 10:1"> Ye shall offer no ketoret zarah thereon, nor olah, nor minchah; neither shall ye pour nesekh thereon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="VAYIKRA 16:18; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:22–23; VAYIKRA 16:29–30"> And Aharon shall make kapporah upon the karenot of it once in a year with the dahm of the chattat hakippurim; once in the year shall he make kapporah upon it throughout your dorot; it is kodesh kodashim unto Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Lv 16on this">a</a></sup></span>

## &#91;KI TISA&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 31:50; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 27:24; SHEMOT 38:25–26"> When thou takest the census of the Bnei Yisroel after their number, then shall they give every man a kofer nafsho (life price, atoning payment for his nefesh or soul) unto Hashem, when thou numberest them; that there be no negef (plague) among them, when thou numberest them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="VAYIKRA 27:25; BAMIDBAR 3:47; YECHEZKEL 45:12"> This they shall give, every one that passeth among them that are pekudim (numbered, counted), half a shekel after the shekel of HaKodesh. (A shekel is twenty gerahs.) A half shekel shall be the terumah (offering) unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:20; BAMIDBAR 1:18; BAMIDBAR 1:3"> Every one that passeth among them that are numbered, from twenty years old and above, shall give a terumah (offering) unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 31:50; VAYIKRA 17:11; SHEMOT 30:12"> The oisher (rich man) shall not give more, and the poor shall not give less than half a shekel, when they give a terumah (offering) unto Hashem, to make kapporah for your nefashot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 10:32–33; BAMIDBAR 16:40; SHEMOT 38:25–31"> And thou shalt take the kesef hakippurim of the Bnei Yisroel, and shalt appoint it for the Avodat Ohel Mo'ed (Work, Service of the Tent of Appointed Meeting); that it may be a zikaron unto the Bnei Yisroel before Hashem, to make kapporah for your nefashot.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Ps 49; 1K 1:18-19">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 40:7; SHEMOT 38:8; MELACHIM ALEF 7:23"> Thou shalt also make a Kiyor of nechoshet, and its Stand also of nechoshet, to wash withal; and thou shalt place it between the Ohel Mo'ed and the Mizbe'ach, and thou shalt put mayim therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 52:11; SHEMOT 40:31–32; TEHILLIM 26:6"> For Aharon and his banim shall wash their hands and their feet thereat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="VAYIKRA 10:1–3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:28–29; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 13:10"> When they go into the Ohel Mo'ed, they shall wash with mayim, that they die not; or when they come near to the Mizbe'ach to minister, to present offering made by eish unto Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:43; SHEMOT 27:21"> So they shall wash their hands and their feet, that they die not; and it shall be a chok olam to them, even to him and to his zera throughout their dorot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Moreover Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 27:19; SHIR HASHIRIM 4:14; YECHEZKEL 27:22"> Take thou also unto thee choice spices, of pure myrrh five hundred shekel-weights, and of fragrant cinnamon half so much, even two hundred and fifty, and of fragrant cane spice two hundred and fifty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="TEHILLIM 45:8; SHEMOT 29:40; BAMIDBAR 3:47"> And of cassia five hundred shekel-weights, after the shekel of HaKodesh, and of shemen zayit (oil olive) a hin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHEMOT 37:29; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 9:30; SHEMOT 40:9"> And thou shalt make it a shemen mishchat kodesh, an ointment of blended compound, the art of the perfumer; it shall be a shemen mishchat kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 7:1; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20; YESHAYAH 61:1"> And thou shalt anoint the Ohel Mo'ed therewith, and the Aron HaEdut,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And the Shulchan and all its utensils, the Menorah and its utensils, and the Mizbe'ach HaKetoret,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the Mizbe'ach HaOlah with all its utensils, and the Kiyor and its Stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:19; SHEMOT 29:37; VAYIKRA 6:18"> And thou shalt set them apart as kodesh, that they may be kodesh kodashim; whatsoever toucheth them shall be kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="VAYIKRA 8:12; SHEMOT 28:3; VAYIKRA 8:30"> And thou shalt anoint Aharon and his banim, and set them apart as kodesh, that they may minister unto Me in the kohenʼs office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHEMOT 37:29; VAYIKRA 21:10; TEHILLIM 89:20"> And thou shalt speak unto the Bnei Yisroel, saying, This shall be a shemen mishchat kodesh unto Me throughout your dorot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHEMOT 30:25; SHEMOT 30:37–38; VAYIKRA 21:10"> Upon the basar adam shall it not be poured, neither shall ye make any other like it, after the formulation of it; it is kodesh, and it shall be kodesh unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="SHEMOT 30:38; BERESHIS 17:14; SHEMOT 12:15"> Whosoever compoundeth any like it, or whosoever putteth any of it upon a zar, shall even be cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="SHEMOT 37:29; SHEMOT 25:6; SHEMOT 30:23"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, Take unto thee spices, stacte, and onycha, and galbanum; these spices with pure frankincense; of each shall there be an equal weight;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="VAYIKRA 2:13; SHEMOT 30:25; YOCHANAN 12:3"> And thou shalt make it a compound of spice, the art of a perfumer, mixed thoroughly together, tahor and kodesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:22; SHEMOT 30:6; SHEMOT 16:34"> And thou shalt beat some of it very small, and place it in front of HaEdut in the Ohel Mo'ed, where I will set a meeting with thee; it shall be unto you kodesh kodashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="VAYIKRA 2:3; SHEMOT 29:37; SHEMOT 30:32–33"> And as for the ketoret which thou shalt make, ye shall not make to yourselves according to the formulation thereof; it shall be unto thee kodesh for Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="SHEMOT 30:33"> Whosoever shall make its like, to smell it, shall even be cut off from his people.</span>
