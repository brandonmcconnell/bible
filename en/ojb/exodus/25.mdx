## &#91;TERUMAH&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:7; NECHEMYAH 11:2; EZRA 2:68"> Speak unto the Bnei Yisroel, that they bring Me a terumah (offering); of every man that giveth it willingly with his lev ye shall receive My terumah (offering).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEVARIM 8:9; IYOV 28:2"> And this is the terumah (offering) which ye shall receive of them; zahav, and kesef, and nechoshet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:8; BERESHIS 41:42; YECHEZKEL 16:10"> And turquoise, and purple, and scarlet wool, and linen, and goat hair,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:37–1; SHEMOT 26:14–15; SHEMOT 36:20"> And ram skins dyed red, and tachash skins, and acacia wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 27:20; SHEMOT 25:37; SHEMOT 40:24–25"> Shemen for the light, spices for shemen hamishchah (anointing oil) and aromatic ketoret (incense),</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:4; SHEMOT 28:6–30"> Shoham stone, and stones to be set in the Ephod, and in the Choshen (Breastplate).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:16; MELACHIM ALEF 6:13; HISGALUS 21:3"> And let them make Me a Mikdash; that I may dwell among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:40; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:44"> According to all that I show thee, after the tavnit HaMishkan (pattern of the Tabernacle), and the tavnit of all the vessels thereof, even so shall ye make it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:4; SHEMOT 37:1–3; DEVARIM 10:1–3"> And they shall make an Aron (Ark) of acacia wood; two cubits and a half shall be the length thereof, and a cubit and a half the width thereof, and a cubit and a half the height thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:24; SHEMOT 30:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 3:4"> And thou shalt overlay it with zahav tahor, within and without shalt thou overlay it, and shalt make upon it a gold crown all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHEMOT 27:7; SHEMOT 25:26; SHEMOT 25:15"> And thou shalt cast four rings of zahav for it, and put them in the four corners thereof; and two rings shall be in the one side of it, and two rings in the other side of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:15; BAMIDBAR 4:11; SHEMOT 27:6"> And thou shalt make poles of acacia wood, and overlay them with zahav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And thou shalt put the poles into the rings by the sides of the Aron, that the Aron may be carried with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 5:9"> The poles shall be in the rings of the Aron; they shall not be removed from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEVARIM 31:26; MELACHIM ALEF 8:9; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:4"> And thou shalt put into the Aron the Edut (Testimony) &#91;of Tablets&#93; which I shall give thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:5; SHEMOT 37:6; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:16"> And thou shalt make a kapporet of zahav tahor; two cubits and a half shall be the length thereof, and a cubit and a half the width thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 10:20; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:5; MELACHIM ALEF 8:6–7"> And thou shalt make two keruvim of zahav, of beaten work shalt thou make them, in the two ends of the kapporet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And make one keruv on the one end, and the other keruv on the other end, with the kapporet (atonement cover) shall ye make the keruvim on the two ends thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:18; MELACHIM ALEF 8:7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:5"> And the keruvim shall stretch forth their wings upward, covering the kapporet with their wings, and their faces shall look one to another; toward the kapporet shall the faces of the keruvim be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:34; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:4; SHEMOT 25:16–17"> And thou shalt place the kapporet on the Aron from above; and into the Aron thou shalt place the Edut that I shall give thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:42–43; BAMIDBAR 7:89; YESHAYAH 37:16"> And there I will meet with thee, and I will commune with thee from above the kapporet, from between the two keruvim which are upon the Aron HaEdut, of all things which I will give thee in commandment unto the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:2; SHEMOT 37:10–16; MELACHIM ALEF 7:48"> Thou shalt also make a Shulchan of acacia wood; two cubits shall be the length thereof, and a cubit the width thereof, and a cubit and a half the height thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:11; MELACHIM ALEF 6:20–22"> And thou shalt overlay it with zahav tahor, and make thereto a crown of zahav around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHEMOT 37:2; SHEMOT 30:3"> And thou shalt make unto it a border of a handbreadth round about, and thou shalt make a golden crown on the rim thereof round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And thou shalt make for it four rings of zahav, and place the rings in the four corners that are on the four legs thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:14; SHEMOT 25:28"> Over against the rim shall the rings be for holders of the poles to carry the Shulchan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:15; BAMIDBAR 10:17"> And thou shalt make the poles of acacia wood, and overlay them with zahav, that the Shulchan may be carried with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 4:7; SHEMOT 37:16; HISGALUS 3:20"> And thou shalt make the dishes thereof, and spoons thereof, and shelving-tubes thereof, and bowls thereof, with which it shall be used to pour libations; of zahav tahor shalt thou make them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="VAYIKRA 24:5–9; MATTITYAHU 12:4; SHEMOT 39:36"> And thou shalt set upon the Shulchan the Lechem Panim before Me always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:49; ZECHARYAH 4:2; SHEMOT 37:17–24"> And thou shalt make a Menorah of zahav tahor; hammered out shall the Menorah be made; its base, its shaft, and its cups, its knobs, and its blossoms, shall be of the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And six branches shall come out of the sides of it; three branches of the menorah out of the one side, and three branches of the menorah out of the other side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 17:4–8; YIRMEYAH 1:11–12; ZECHARYAH 4:3"> Three cups engraved with almonds on the one branch, a knob and a flower; and three cups made like almonds in the other branch, with a knob and a flower; so for the six branches that come out of the Menorah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And on the Menorah shall be four cups engraved like almonds, its knobs and its flowers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And there shall be a knob under two branches of the same, and a knob under two branches of the same, and a knob under two branches of the same, according to the six branches that proceed out of the Menorah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:18; MELACHIM ALEF 10:16–17; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:15"> Their knobs and their branches shall be of the same; all of it shall be one hammered out work of zahav tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 8:2; HISGALUS 1:20–1; VAYIKRA 24:2–4"> And thou shalt make the nerot shivah thereof; and they shall kindle the nerot thereof, that they may give light toward its face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 12:13; YIRMEYAH 52:18; MELACHIM BAIS 25:14"> And the tongs thereof, and the spoons thereof, shall be of zahav tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Of a talent of zahav tahor shall he make it, with all these vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:5; SHEMOT 26:30; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:44"> And see that thou make them after their tavnit, which was shown thee in HaHar.</span>
