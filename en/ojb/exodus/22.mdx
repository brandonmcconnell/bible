<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 19:8; SHMUEL BAIS 12:6; MISHLE 6:31"> If a ganav be caught breaking in, and be struck down so that he die, there shall be no guilt of bloodshed for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:43; MATTITYAHU 6:19–20; BAMIDBAR 35:27"> If the shemesh be risen upon him, there shall be guilt of bloodshed; the ganav should make full restitution; if he have nothing, then the ganav shall be sold to make restitution for his theft.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 21:2; YESHAYAH 50:1; MATTITYAHU 18:25"> If the theft be certainly found in his yad chayyim, whether it be ox, or donkey, or seh; he shall pay back double.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:7; SHEMOT 22:1; SHEMOT 22:9"> If a man shall allow livestock to graze over a sadeh or kerem (vineyard), or he lets it loose and it graze over the sadeh of another, then from the best of his own sadeh, and of the best of his own kerem (vineyard), shall he make restitution.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:3; SHEMOT 22:12; IYOV 20:18"> If eish break out, and spreads in kotzim (thorns), so that the stacks of grain, or the standing grain, or the sadeh, be consumed therewith; he that kindled the eish shall surely make restitution.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:9; SHEMOT 21:33–34; SHMUEL BAIS 14:30–31"> If an ish shall give unto his re'a kesef or vessels to be shomer over, and it be stolen out of the bais haish; if the ganav be found, let him pay back double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:4; MISHLE 6:30–31; YIRMEYAH 2:26"> If the ganav be not found, then the ba'al habais shall be brought before HaElohim, to see whether he has put his yad on the property of his neighbor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:28; DEVARIM 16:18; SHEMOT 21:6"> For all manner of pesha (trespass, liability), whether it be for ox, for donkey, for seh, for clothing, or for any manner of avedah (lost property, missing thing) which another says, This is it, the case of both shall come before HaElohim; and whom Elohim shall condemn, he shall pay back double unto his re'a.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEVARIM 25:1; SHEMOT 22:4; BAMIDBAR 5:6–7"> If an ish deliver unto his re'a a donkey, or an ox, or a seh, or any behemah, to be shomer over; and it die, or be injured, or carried away, with no eye witness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKAS 12:48; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:12; LUKAS 16:11"> Then shall a shevuat Hashem be between them both, that he hath not laid his yad on the property of his re'a; and the propertyʼs ba'al shall accept thereof, and he shall not make restitution.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:16; VAYIKRA 5:1; MELACHIM ALEF 2:42–43"> And if it be certainly stolen from him, he shall make restitution unto the ba'al thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 31:39; SHEMOT 22:7"> If it be indeed tarof (torn by a wild animal, predator), then let him bring it for ed (witness), and he shall not make restitution for the terefah (torn animal).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="NACHUM 2:12; MICHOH 5:8; YECHEZKEL 4:14"> And if an ish asks to borrow of his re'a, and the animal borrowed be injured, or die, the ba'al thereof being not with it, he shall surely make restitution.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEVARIM 23:19–20; SHEMOT 21:34; DEVARIM 15:2"> But if the ba'al thereof be with it, he shall not make restitution; if it be sakhir (rented, hired), it came for its hire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 8:10"> And if an ish entice a betulah that is not orasah (betrothed, pledged), and lie with her, he shall surely endow her with a marriage contract as his isha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEVARIM 22:28–29; BERESHIS 34:2–4"> If her av utterly refuse to give her unto him, he shall pay kesef according to the mohar habetulot (marriage contract, dowry of the virgins).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 34:12; SHMUEL ALEF 18:25; DEVARIM 22:29"> Thou shalt not allow a mekhashefah (witch, sorceress) to live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:27; DEVARIM 18:10–11; SHMUEL ALEF 28:3"> Kol shochev (every one having sexual relations) with a behemah shall surely be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:23; DEVARIM 27:21; VAYIKRA 20:15–16"> He that sacrificeth unto elohim (the g-ds), other than unto Hashem only, he shall be destroyed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEVARIM 17:2–5; BAMIDBAR 25:7–8; DEVARIM 18:20"> Thou shalt neither mistreat a ger, nor oppress him; for ye were gerim in Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:9; DEVARIM 10:19; VAYIKRA 19:33"> Ye shall not cause pain to any almanah, or yatom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEVARIM 24:17; YESHAYAH 1:17; YESHAYAH 1:23"> If thou cause them pain in any way, and they cry at all unto Me, I will surely hear their cry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="TEHILLIM 18:6; TEHILLIM 10:17–18; LUKAS 18:7"> And My wrath shall be kindled, and I will kill you with the cherev; and your nashim shall be almanot, and your banim shall be yetomim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="TEHILLIM 109:9; YIRMEYAH 15:8; TEHILLIM 76:7"> If thou lend kesef to any of My people that is poor among thee, thou shalt not be to him as a nosheh (a usurer), neither shalt thou lay upon him neshekh (usury, interest).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="TEHILLIM 15:5; VAYIKRA 25:35–37; DEVARIM 23:19–20"> If thou at all take the cloak of thy re'a as security, thou shalt return it unto him by bo hashemesh (sunset);</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MISHLE 20:16; AMOS 2:8; DEVARIM 24:6"> For that is his covering only, it is his cloak for his skin; wherein shall he sleep? And it shall come to pass, when he crieth unto Me, that I will hear; for I am channun (compassionate).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:9; TEHILLIM 136:10–11"> Thou shalt not revile Elohim, nor curse the nasi of thy people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:5; KOHELET 10:20; SHEMOT 22:8–9"> Thou shalt not delay to offer thy fullness offering<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., bikkurim">a</a></sup>or thy kohenʼs heave offering;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e. terumah">b</a></sup>the bechor of thy banim shalt thou present unto Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:16; SHEMOT 13:2; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:18"> Likewise shalt thou do with thine oxen, and with thy tzon; shivat yamim it shall be with its em; on the yom hashemi'ni thou shalt give it Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="VAYIKRA 22:27; DEVARIM 15:19; SHEMOT 13:12"> And ye shall be anshei kodesh unto Me; neither shall ye eat any basar that is terefah (torn of beasts) in the sadeh; ye shall cast it to the kelev (dog).</span>
</div>
