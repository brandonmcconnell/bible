<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKAS 2:23; BAMIDBAR 3:13; DEVARIM 15:19"> Set apart as kodesh unto Me kol bechor, whatsoever openeth the rechem (womb) among the Bnei Yisroel, both of adam and of behemah; it is Mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEVARIM 24:18; DEVARIM 5:15; SHEMOT 20:2"> And Moshe said unto HaAm, Remember this day, in which ye came out from Mitzrayim, out of the bais avadim; for by chozek yad Hashem brought you out from this place; there shall no chametz be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:18; SHEMOT 23:15; SHEMOT 12:2"> This day came ye out in the month Aviv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:8; YEHOSHUA 24:11; SHEMOT 12:25–26"> And it shall be when Hashem shall bring thee into the land of the Kena'ani, and the Chitti, and the Emori, and the Chivi, and the Yevusi, which He swore unto thy Avot to give thee, an eretz flowing with cholov and devash, that thou shalt observe this avodah (service) in this month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:15–20; SHEMOT 34:18; VAYIKRA 23:8"> Shivat yamim thou shalt eat matzot, and in the seventh day shall be a Chag (Feast) to Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:19; MATTITYAHU 16:6"> Matzot shall be eaten shivat hayamim; and there shall no chametz be seen with thee, neither shall there be se'or seen with thee within all thy borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 13:14; SHEMOT 12:26–27; TEHILLIM 44:1"> And thou shalt show thy ben in that day, saying, This is because of what Hashem did for me when I came forth out of Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEVARIM 6:8; DEVARIM 11:18–19; BAMIDBAR 15:39"> And it shall be for an ot (sign) unto thee upon thine yad, and for a zikaron (reminder) between thine eyes, in order that the torat Hashem may be in thy mouth; because with a yad chazakah hath Hashem brought thee out of Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:14; SHEMOT 12:24–25; SHEMOT 23:15"> Thou shalt therefore be shomer over this chukkah in its mo'ed (season, fixed time) perpetually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 13:5"> And it shall be when Hashem shall bring thee into the land of the Kena'ani, as He swore unto thee and to thy avot, and shall give it thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 18:15; VAYIKRA 27:26; SHEMOT 34:19"> That thou shalt set apart unto Hashem all that openeth the rechem (womb), and every firstling that is born of a behemah which thou hast; hazecharim (the males) shall be Hashemʼs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:20; BAMIDBAR 18:15–17; HISGALUS 14:4"> And every firstling of a he-donkey thou shalt redeem with a seh; and if thou wilt not redeem it, then thou shalt break its neck; and kol bechor adam among thy banim shalt thou redeem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 13:3; YEHOSHUA 4:6; TEHILLIM 145:4"> And it shall be when thy ben asketh thee in time to come, saying, Mah zot? (What is this?) that thou shalt say unto him, By chozek yad Hashem brought us out from Mitzrayim, from the bais avadim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:29"> And it came to pass, when Pharaoh stubbornly refused to let us go, that Hashem killed kol bechor of Eretz Mitzrayim, both the bechor adam, and the bechor behemah; therefore Ani zovei'ach laHashem(I sacrifice to Hashem) kol that openeth the rechem (womb), being hazecharim (the males); but kol bechor of my banim I redeem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 13:9; DEVARIM 11:18; DEVARIM 6:7–9"> And it shall be for an ot (sign) upon thine hand, and for totafos (ornaments, frontlets, bands, phylactery) between thine eyes; for by chozek yad Hashem brought us forth out of Mitzrayim.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHEMOT 14:11–12; NECHEMYAH 9:17; BAMIDBAR 14:1–4"> And it came to pass, when Pharaoh had let the people go, that Elohim led them not through the derech of the eretz of the Pelishtim (Philistines), although that was near; for Elohim said, Lest perhaps the people change their mind when they see milchamah, and they return to Mitzrayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 14:2; DEVARIM 32:10; BAMIDBAR 33:6–8"> So Elohim led HaAm about, through the derech of the midbar of the Yam Suf; and the Bnei Yisroel went up out of Eretz Mitzrayim ready for battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BERESHIS 50:24–25; YEHOSHUA 24:32; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:16"> And Moshe took the atzmot Yosef with him; for Yosef had made the Bnei Yisroel swear unconditionally, saying, Elohim will surely visit you; and ye shall carry up my atzmot away from here with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 33:5–6; SHEMOT 12:37"> And they took their journey from Sukkot, and encamped in Etam, at the edge of the midbar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:1–2; NECHEMYAH 9:12; DEVARIM 1:33"> And Hashem went before them by day in an ammud anan, to guide them haderech; and by lailah in an ammud eish, to give them ohr; so they could travel yomam valailah (day or night);</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="TEHILLIM 121:5–8; HISGALUS 10:1"> He took not away the ammud heanan by day, nor the ammud haeish by night, from before HaAm.</span>
