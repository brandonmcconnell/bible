<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:3; SHEMOT 35:30–35; SHEMOT 31:1–6"> Then Betzalel and Oholiav, and kol ish chacham lev, in whom Hashem gave chochmah and tevunah to have da'as to do all manner of work for the Avodas HaKodesh, are to do the work according to all that Hashem had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:4; SHEMOT 31:6"> And Moshe called Betzalel and Oholiav, and kol ish chacham lev, in whose lev Hashem had given chochmah, even every one whose lev stirred him up to come unto the work to do it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 35:29; SHEMOT 35:27; SHEMOT 35:5–21"> And they received of Moshe kol haterumah, which the Bnei Yisroel had brought for the work of the Avodas HaKodesh, to make it withal. And they brought yet unto him nedavah every boker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:10; LUKAS 12:42; MATTITYAHU 24:45"> And all the chachamim, that wrought all the work of HaKodesh, came every man from his work which they were doing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 31:6–10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:2–3; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 24:14"> And they spoke unto Moshe, saying, HaAm bring much more than enough for the Avodah of the work, which Hashem commanded to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Moshe gave commandment, and they caused it to be proclaimed throughout the machaneh, saying, Let neither ish nor isha make any more work for the terumat HaKodesh. So HaAm were restrained from bringing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 31:10"> For the work had been sufficient for all the work to do it v'hoter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 6:23; SHEMOT 26:1–37; YECHEZKEL 1:5–28"> And kol chacham lev among them that wrought the work of the Mishkan made ten curtains of fine linen, twisted with turquoise, and purple, and scarlet wool; with keruvim in a woven artistic design made he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Betzalel">a</a></sup>them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The length of each curtain was twenty and eight cubits, and the width of each curtain four cubits; the curtains were all of the one size.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:27; SHEMOT 26:3; TEHILLIM 122:3"> And he joined five curtains one unto another; and the other five curtains he joined one unto another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:4"> And he made loops of turquoise wool on the edge of one curtain at the end of the first set; likewise he made them along the edge of the end curtains of the second set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:5; SHEMOT 26:10"> Fifty loops made he in one curtain, and fifty loops made he in the end curtain in the second set; the loops were to be opposite one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:4–5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:20–22; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:20"> And he made fifty hooks of zahav, and joined the curtains one unto another with the hooks; so it became Mishkan Echad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:7–13"> And he made curtains of goat hair for the Ohel over the Mishkan; eleven curtains he made them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The length of one curtain was thirty cubits, and four cubits was the width of one curtain; the eleven curtains were of the one size.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And he joined five curtains into a set, and six curtains into a set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he made fifty loops upon the uttermost edge of the curtain at the end of the set, and fifty loops made he upon the edge of the curtain of the other set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he made fifty hooks of nechoshet to attach the Ohel together, that it might be echad (one).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:14"> And he made a Mikhseh (Cover) for the Ohel of dyed red ram skin, and a tachash hide Mikhseh above that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:5; BAMIDBAR 25:1; SHEMOT 40:18–19"> And he made kerashim (planks) for the Mishkan of acacia wood, standing erect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The length of a keresh (plank) was ten cubits, and the width of a keresh one cubit and a half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> One keresh had two yadot (projections, tenons), equally distant one from another; thus did he make for all the kerashim of the Mishkan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And he made kerashim (frames) for the Mishkan; twenty kerashim for the negev (south) side southward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And arba'im sockets of kesef he made under the twenty kerashim; two sockets under one keresh for its two tenons, and two sockets under another keresh for its two tenons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And for the other side of the Mishkan, which is toward the north, he made twenty kerashim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And their arba'im sockets of kesef; two sockets under one keresh, and two sockets under another keresh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:22; SHEMOT 26:27"> And for the sides of the Mishkan westward he made six kerashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And two kerashim made he for the corners of the Mishkan in the back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:32; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:18–19; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:10"> And they were doubled at the bottom, and joined together; at the top thereof, there was one ring; thus he did to both of them in both the corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:25"> And there were eight kerashim; and their sockets were sixteen sockets of kesef, under every keresh two sockets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:26–29; SHEMOT 25:28; SHEMOT 30:5"> And he made crossbars of acacia wood; five for the kerashim of the one side of the Mishkan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:26"> And five crossbars for the kerashim of the other side of the Mishkan, and five crossbars for the kerashim of the Mishkan for the sides westward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And he made the middle crossbar to extend within the kerashim from the one end to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And he overlaid the kerashim with zahav, and made their rings of zahav to be housing for the crossbars, and overlaid the crossbars with zahav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:51; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:20; SHEMOT 40:21"> And he made a Parochet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Mk 15:38">b</a></sup>of turquoise, and purple, and scarlet wool, and twisted fine linen; with keruvim made he it of artistic embroidery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 1:18"> And he made thereunto four ammudim of acacia wood, and overlaid them with zahav; their hooks were of zahav; and he cast for them four sockets of kesef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:36–37; SHEMOT 40:28"> And he made a Masach for the petach of the Ohel of turquoise, and purple, and scarlet wool, and twisted fine linen, the work of artistic embroidery;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="SHEMOT 27:10"> And the five ammudim of it with their hooks; and he overlaid their tops and their bands with zahav; but their five sockets were of nechoshet.</span>
