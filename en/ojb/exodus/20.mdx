<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 1:1–2; BERESHIS 2:2–3; SHEMOT 31:17"> For in sheshet yamim Hashem made Shomayim and Ha'Aretz, the yam, and all that in them is, and rested Yom HaShevi'i; for this reason Hashem blessed Yom HaShabbos, and set it apart as kodesh.</span>
