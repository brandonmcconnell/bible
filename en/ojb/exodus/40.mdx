<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:30; SHEMOT 13:4; BAMIDBAR 7:1"> On the first day of the first month shalt thou set up the Mishkan Ohel Mo'ed.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="VAYIKRA 24:8; SHEMOT 40:22–25; SHEMOT 37:10–24"> And thou shalt bring in the Shulchan, and set in order the things that are to be set in order upon it; and thou shalt bring in the Menorah, and kindle the Nerot thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:19–22; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:24; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1–2"> And thou shalt place the Mizbe'ach Hazahav L'Ketoret before the Aron HaEdut, and set up the Masach HaPetach LaMishkan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 40:29; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:9–10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:10"> And thou shalt set the Mizbe'ach HaOlah before the Petach Mishkan Ohel Mo'ed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 13:1; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7; TEHILLIM 26:6"> And thou shalt set the Kiyor between the Ohel Mo'ed and the Mizbe'ach, and shalt place mayim therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 40:33; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:11–12; MATTITYAHU 16:18"> And thou shalt set up the khatzer (court) all around, and hang up the Masach Sha'ar Hakhatzer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="VAYIKRA 8:10; BAMIDBAR 7:1; MATTITYAHU 3:16"> And thou shalt take the Shemen HaMishchah, and anoint the Mishkan, and all that is therein, and shalt set it apart as kodesh, and all the utensils thereof; and it shall be kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:36–37; LUKAS 1:35; YESHAYAH 61:1"> And thou shalt anoint the Mizbe'ach HaOlah, and all its utensils, and set apart as kodesh the Mizbe'ach: and it shall be Mizbe'ach kodesh kodashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And thou shalt anoint the Kiyor and its Stand, and set it apart as kodesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="VAYIKRA 8:1–13; VAYIKRA 9:1–24; SHEMOT 29:1–35"> And thou shalt bring near Aharon and his banim unto the Petach Ohel Mo'ed, and immerse them with mayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:41; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:27"> And thou shalt dress Aharon with the Bigdei Hakodesh, and anoint him, and set him apart as kodesh; that he may minister unto Me in the kohenʼs office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YESHAYAH 61:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:30; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:30"> And thou shalt bring near his banim, and dress them with kuttanot:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 25:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:1–14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:1–10"> And thou shalt anoint them, as thou didst anoint their av, that they may minister unto Me in the kohenʼs office: for their anointing shall surely be for them a kehunat olam throughout their dorot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:1; SHEMOT 40:17–32; DEVARIM 12:32"> Thus did Moshe; according to all that Hashem commanded him, so did he.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 7:1; SHEMOT 40:1–2; BAMIDBAR 9:1"> And it came to pass in the first month in the second year, on the first day of the month, that the Mishkan was erected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 37:27–28; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5; VAYIKRA 26:11"> And Moshe erected the Mishkan, and fastened its sockets, and set up the kerashim thereof, and put in the crossbars thereof, and set up its ammudim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:1–14; SHEMOT 36:8–19"> And he spread the Ohel over the Mishkan, and put the Mikhseh of the Ohel on it from above; as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:34; DEVARIM 10:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:19–21"> And he took and placed HaEdut (The Testimony),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., the Stone Tablets of the Ten Commandments">a</a></sup>into HaAron, and set the carrying poles on the Aron, and put the Kapporet upon the Aron from above;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:33; SHEMOT 40:3; SHEMOT 35:12"> And he brought the Aron into the Mishkan, and set up the Parochet HaMasach, and shielded with a covering over the Aron HaEdut; as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:35; YOCHANAN 6:53–57; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:8"> And he put the Shulchan in the Ohel Mo'ed, upon the north side of the Mishkan, outside the Parochet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:30; SHEMOT 40:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:2"> And he set the Lechem in order upon it before Hashem; as Hashem had commanded Moshe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:35; HISGALUS 2:5; YOCHANAN 1:1"> And he put the Menorah in the Ohel Mo'ed, opposite the Shulchan, on the south side of the Mishkan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHEMOT 40:4; SHEMOT 25:37; HISGALUS 4:5"> And he set up the nerot (lamps) before Hashem; as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHEMOT 40:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:25; YOCHANAN 11:42"> And he put the Mizbe'ach Hazahav in the Ohel Mo'ed before the Parochet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHEMOT 30:7"> And he burned ketoret spices thereon; as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHEMOT 40:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:19–20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:18"> And he set up the Masach HaPetach LaMishkan (Curtain of the Entrance of the Tabernacle).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHEMOT 40:6; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:5–6; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:10"> And he put the Mizbe'ach HaOlah at the Petach Mishkan Ohel Mo'ed, and offered upon it the olah (burnt offering) and the minchah; as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="SHEMOT 40:7; SHEMOT 38:8; YECHEZKEL 36:25"> And he set the Kiyor between the Ohel Mo'ed and the Mizbe'ach, and put mayim there, to wash withal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHEMOT 30:19–20; TEHILLIM 26:6; YOCHANAN 13:10"> And Moshe and Aharon and his banim washed their hands and their feet thereat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="TEHILLIM 73:19; SHEMOT 30:19–20; SHEMOT 40:19"> When they went into the Ohel Mo'ed, and when they came near unto the Mizbe'ach, they washed; as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="SHEMOT 40:8; SHEMOT 27:9–16; YOCHANAN 17:4"> And he erected the khatzer (courtyard) all around the Mishkan and the Mizbe'ach, and set up the Masach Sha'ar HaKhatzer. So Moshe finished the work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="HISGALUS 15:8; BAMIDBAR 9:15–23; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:2"> Then the Anan covered the Ohel Mo'ed, and the Kavod Hashem filled the Mishkan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:11; VAYIKRA 16:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:2"> And Moshe was not able to enter into the Ohel Mo'ed, because the &#91;Shekinah&#93; cloud abode thereon, and the Kavod Hashem filled the Mishkan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 9:19; BAMIDBAR 9:17; SHEMOT 13:21–22"> And when the Anan was taken up from over the Mishkan, the Bnei Yisroel went onward in all their journeys;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 9:19–22; TEHILLIM 31:15"> But if the Anan were not taken up, then they journeyed not till the day that it was taken up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:14; BAMIDBAR 9:15; SHEMOT 13:21"> For the Anan Hashem was upon the Mishkan by day, and Eish was on it by night, in the sight of all the Bais Yisroel, throughout all their journeys.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Theme of gracious, unmerited Deliverance and Salvation for an Am Kesheh Oref (Obstinate Stiffnecked People) has been presented in the Second Book of Moses.">b</a></sup></span>
