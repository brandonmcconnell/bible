<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:21; SHEMOT 7:13–14; SHEMOT 3:20"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, Go in unto Pharaoh; for I have hardened his lev, and the lev of his avadim, that I might show these My otot before him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOEL 1:3; DEVARIM 4:9; DEVARIM 6:20–22"> And that thou mayest tell in the ears of thy ben, and of thy benʼs ben, what things I have wrought in Mitzrayim, and My otot which I have done among them; that ye may have da'as that I am Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:6; MELACHIM ALEF 21:29; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10"> And Moshe and Aharon came in unto Pharaoh, and said unto him, Thus saith Hashem Elohei HaIvrim, How long wilt thou refuse to humble thyself before Me? Let My people go, that they may serve Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HISGALUS 9:3; YOEL 2:25; SHEMOT 11:4–5"> Else, if thou refuse to let My people go, hineni, tomorrow will I bring the arbeh into thy border;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOEL 1:4; SHEMOT 9:32; YOEL 2:25"> And they shall cover the face of ha'aretz, that one cannot be able to see ha'aretz; and they shall devour the remnant of that which is escaped, which remaineth unto you from the barad, and shall eat kol haetz which groweth for you out of the sadeh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 8:21; SHEMOT 8:3; SHEMOT 11:6"> And they shall fill thy batim (houses), and the batim (houses) of all thy avadim, and the batim (houses) of kol Mitzrayim; which neither thy avot, nor the avot of thy avot have seen, since the day that they were upon ha'aretz unto this day. And he turned himself, and went out from Pharaoh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 18:21; SHEMOT 23:33; YEHOSHUA 23:13"> And Pharaohʼs avadim said unto him, How long shall this man be a mokesh (snare) unto us? Let the anashim go, that they may serve Hashem Eloheihem; knowest thou not yet that Mitzrayim is destroyed?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 10:24; SHEMOT 12:31; SHEMOT 8:8"> And Moshe and Aharon were brought again unto Pharaoh; and he said unto them, Go, serve Hashem Eloheichem; but who are they that shall go?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 5:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:7–8; DEVARIM 31:12–13"> And Moshe said, We will go with ne'areinu and with zekeineinu, with baneinu and with benoteinu, with tzoneinu and with bekareinu (our herds) will we go; for Chag Hashem lanu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 13:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:15; SHEMOT 12:30–31"> And he said unto them, Hashem will indeed be with you, if ever I let you go, and your little ones; look to it, evil &#91;intent&#93; is before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 10:28; TEHILLIM 119:69; SHEMOT 5:4"> Not so; go now ye that are gevarim, and serve Hashem; for that ye did request. And they were driven out from Pharaohʼs presence.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHEMOT 7:19; SHEMOT 10:4–5"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, Stretch out thine yad over Eretz Mitzrayim for the arbeh, that they may come up upon Eretz Mitzrayim, and devour kol esev ha'aretz, even all that the barad hath left as remnant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YONAH 1:4; TEHILLIM 78:26; TEHILLIM 148:8"> And Moshe stretched forth his matteh over Eretz Mitzrayim, and Hashem brought a ruach kadim upon the land all that yom, and all that lailah; and when it was boker, the ruach hakadim brought the arbeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:46; YOEL 1:2–4; TEHILLIM 105:34–35"> And the arbeh went up over kol Eretz Mitzrayim, and rested on the entire border of Mitzrayim: very grievous were they; before them there were no such arbeh as they, neither after them shall be such.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 10:5; TEHILLIM 78:46; YOEL 1:6–7"> For they covered the kol ha'aretz, so that the land was darkened; and they did eat kol esev ha'aretz, and kol p'ri haetz which the barad had left as remnant; and there remained not any yerek baetz, or in the esev hasadeh, through kol Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 9:27; SHMUEL BAIS 19:20; SHMUEL ALEF 15:30"> Then Pharaoh called for Moshe and Aharon in haste; and he said, chatati (I have sinned) against Hashem Eloheichem, and against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHEMOT 8:8; SHEMOT 9:28; MELACHIM ALEF 13:6"> Therefore forgive, now, my sin only this once, and entreat Hashem Eloheichem, that He would only take away from me this mavet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKAS 6:28; SHEMOT 8:28–30; SHEMOT 8:9"> And he went out from Pharaoh, and entreated Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YOEL 2:20; SHEMOT 13:18; SHEMOT 15:4"> And Hashem turned a west ruach chazak me'od, which took away the arbeh, and cast them into the Yam Suf; there remained not one arbeh in all the borders of Mitzrayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:21; SHEMOT 11:10; DEVARIM 2:30"> But Hashem hardened lev Pharaoh, so that he would not let the Bnei Yisroel go.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HISGALUS 16:10–11; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:6; LUKAS 23:44"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, Stretch out thine yad toward Shomayim, that there may be choshech over Eretz Mitzrayim, even choshech which he can feel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="TEHILLIM 105:28; HISGALUS 16:10; SHEMOT 20:21"> And Moshe stretched forth his yad toward Shomayim; and there was a thick choshech in kol Eretz Mitzrayim shloshet yamim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHEMOT 8:22; YESHAYAH 42:16; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9"> They saw not one another, neither rose any from his place for shloshet yamim; but kol Bnei Yisroel had Ohr in their dwellings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHEMOT 10:8–10; SHEMOT 8:28; BERESHIS 34:23"> And Pharaoh called unto Moshe, and said, Go ye, serve Hashem; only let your tzon and your herds remain behind; let your little ones also go with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="VAYIKRA 9:22; VAYIKRA 16:9; SHEMOT 29:1–46"> And Moshe said, Thou must give us also zevakhim and olot, that we may sacrifice unto Hashem Eloheinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YESHAYAH 23:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:44–45; MISHLE 3:9"> Also mikneinu shall go with us; there shall not a hoof be left behind; for thereof must we take to serve Hashem Eloheinu; and we know not with what we must serve Hashem, until we come to there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHEMOT 10:20; SHEMOT 4:21; SHEMOT 14:8"> But Hashem hardened lev Pharaoh, and he would not let them go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 16:10; AMOS 7:13"> And Pharaoh said unto him, Get thee from me, take heed to thyself, see my face no more; for in that day thou seest my face thou shalt die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:27; SHEMOT 12:30–31; SHEMOT 11:4–8"> And Moshe said, Thou hast spoken well, I will see thy face again no more.</span>
</div>
