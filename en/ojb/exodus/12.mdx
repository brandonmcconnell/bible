<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe and Aharon in Eretz Mitzrayim saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 13:4; DEVARIM 16:1; SHEMOT 34:18"> Hachodesh hazeh (this month) shall be unto you the beginning of months; it shall be the first month of the year to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="VAYIKRA 1:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:7; YOCHANAN 1:29"> Speak ye unto kol Adat Yisroel, saying, In the tenth day of this month they shall take to them every man a seh (lamb),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Isa 53:7">a</a></sup>according to the bais avot, a seh for each bais;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And if the household be too small for the seh (lamb),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Isa 53:7">b</a></sup>let him and his neighbor next unto his bais take according to the number of the nefashot; every ish according to what he eats shall make up your count for the seh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:18–19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:13–14; MALACHI 1:14"> Your seh (lamb)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Isa 53:7">c</a></sup>shall be tamim (without blemish), a zachar (male) within its first year; ye shall take it out from the sheep, or from the goats;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:15–18; BAMIDBAR 28:16"> And it will be with you for mishmeret (examination, checking for blemishes) up until the fourteenth day of the same month; and kol Kehal Adat Yisroel shall slaughter (shachat) it in the afternoon &#91;before dark&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:28; SHEMOT 12:22–23; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:22"> And they shall take of the dahm, and strike it upon the two mezuzot (doorposts) and on the mashkof (lintel) of the batim (houses), wherein they shall eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:25; DEVARIM 16:7; SHEMOT 13:3"> And they shall eat the basar in that night, roasted over eish, and matzot; and with maror they shall eat it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Yn 6:53-54where Moshiach alludes to this verse and Isa 53:7.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:8; DEVARIM 16:7; EKHAH 1:13"> Eat not of it raw, nor soaked or boiled in mayim, but roasted over eish; this includes its rosh with its legs, and with the inner parts thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:18; SHEMOT 34:25; SHEMOT 29:34"> And ye shall let nothing of it remain until the boker; and that which remaineth of it until the boker ye shall burn with eish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:7"> And thus shall ye eat it; with your robe girded up,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="i.e., pulled up and tucked in under the belt, for travel">e</a></sup>your sandals on your feet, and your walking staff in your yad; and ye shall eat it with urgent haste; it is Hashemʼs Pesach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 33:4; SHEMOT 12:23; TEHILLIM 82:6"> For I will pass through Eretz Mitzrayim balailah hazeh, and will strike fatally kol bechor Eretz Mitzrayim, both adam and behemah; and against all the elohei Mitzrayim I will execute judgment; I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:28; SHEMOT 12:23; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7"> And the dahm shall be for you, an ot (sign) upon the batim (houses) where ye are; and when I see the dahm, oofasachti (then I will pass over, skip, spare) you &#91;plural&#93;, and the negef (plague, blow, striking, i.e., death of firstborn) shall not be upon you to destroy you, when I strike fatally with a blow against Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 23:21; SHEMOT 12:24; SHEMOT 13:9–10"> And hayom hazeh shall be unto you for zikaron (remembrance, remembering); and ye shall keep it a Chag (Feast) to Hashem throughout your generations; ye shall keep it a Chag (Feast) by a chukkat olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:14; DEVARIM 16:3; SHEMOT 23:15"> Shivat yamim shall ye eat matzot; but the first day ye shall put away se'or (yeast, leaven) out of your batim (houses); for whosoever eateth chametz from the first day until the seventh day, that nefesh shall be cut off from Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:7–8; BAMIDBAR 28:18; SHEMOT 16:23"> And in the yom harishon there shall be a mikra kodesh, and in the yom hashevi'i there shall be a mikra kodesh to you; no manner of melachah shall be done in them, except that which every nefesh must eat,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="i.e., activity necessary for the preparation of food">f</a></sup>that only may be done of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHEMOT 7:5; SHEMOT 13:3; SHEMOT 12:41"> And ye shall be shomer to safeguard the matzot; for on this very day I brought your tzva'os out of Eretz Mitzrayim; therefore shall ye be shomer over this day in your generations by a chukkat olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:5–8; SHEMOT 12:15; SHEMOT 12:1–2"> In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month at erev, ye shall eat matzot, until the one and twentieth day of the month at erev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:15; SHEMOT 23:15; DEVARIM 16:3"> Shivat yamim shall there be no se'or (yeast, leaven) found in your batim (houses); whosoever eateth that which is leavened, even that nefesh shall be cut off from Adat Yisroel, whether he be a ger, or native born in ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Ye shall eat nothing leavened; in all your dwelling-places shall ye eat matzot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:3; LUKAS 22:7–13; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:5–6"> Then Moshe called for all the Ziknei Yisroel, and said unto them, Draw out as separate and take for yourselves a lamb according to your mishpokhot, and slaughter (shachat) the Pesach.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="offering, i.e., Pesach lamb (see Isa 53:7)">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:28; BAMIDBAR 19:18; TEHILLIM 51:7"> And ye shall take a bunch of hyssop, and dip it in the dahm that is in the basin, and strike the mashkof (lintel) and the two mezuzot (door sideposts) with the dahm that is in the basin; and none of you shall go out the door of his bais until boker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="HISGALUS 7:3; HISGALUS 9:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:28"> For Hashem will pass through to strike the Mitzrayim; and when He seeth the dahm upon the mashkof, and on the two mezuzot, Hashem will pasach (pass over, spare, skip) the entrance, and will not permit the Mashkhit (Destroyer),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="i.e., Hashemʼs emissary of judgment, see Num 22:31on the Malach Hashem">h</a></sup>to enter unto your batim to strike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:14; BERESHIS 17:8–10"> And ye shall be shomer over this word as a chok (ordinance) to thee and your children ad olam (forever).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:17; SHEMOT 3:8; DEVARIM 12:8–9"> And it shall come to pass, when ye come to ha'aretz which Hashem will give to you, according as He hath promised, that ye shall be shomer over this avodah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHEMOT 13:14–15; DEVARIM 32:7; TEHILLIM 145:4"> And it shall come to pass, when your banim shall say unto you, Mah haavodah hazot lachem (What is this avodah to you)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:31; SHEMOT 12:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:7"> That ye shall say, It is the zevach of Hashemʼs Pesach, who pasach (passed over, skipped, spared) the batim (households) of the Bnei Yisroel in Mitzrayim, when He struck down the Egyptians, and spared bateinu (our houses). And the people bowed down and worshiped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:28"> And the Bnei Yisroel went away, and did as Hashem had commanded Moshe and Aharon, so did they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:51; SHEMOT 4:23; TEHILLIM 105:36"> And it came to pass, that at midnight Hashem struck down kol bechor in Eretz Mitzrayim, from the bechor of Pharaoh sitting on his throne unto the bechor of the captive that was in the dungeon; and all the bechor behemah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="SHEMOT 11:6; AMOS 5:17; MISHLE 21:13"> And Pharaoh rose up in the lailah, he, and all his avadim, and kol Mitzrayim; and there was a tze'akah gedolah (great wail) in Mitzrayim; for there was not a bais where there was not one dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHEMOT 6:1; SHEMOT 10:9; SHEMOT 3:19–20"> And he called for Moshe and Aharon by night, and said, Rise up, and get you forth, leave from among my people, both ye and the Bnei Yisroel; and go, serve Hashem, as ye have said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHEMOT 10:26; BERESHIS 27:34; BERESHIS 27:38"> Also take your tzon and your herds, as ye have said, and be gone; and bless me also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="TEHILLIM 105:38; SHEMOT 11:1; BERESHIS 20:3"> And the Egyptians were urgent upon the people, that they might send them out of ha'aretz in haste; for they said, Kullanu mesim (We are all dead ones).</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="SHEMOT 8:3"> And the people took their batzek (deaf dough, having no indication of fermentation)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="See 1C 5:7">i</a></sup>before it was leavened, their kneading pans being wrapped up in their clothes &#91;and carried&#93; upon their shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:21–22; SHEMOT 11:2–3; BERESHIS 15:14"> And the Bnei Yisroel did according to the devar Moshe; they requested of the Egyptians k'lei kesef, and k'lei zahav, and garments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:10; SHEMOT 3:21–22"> And Hashem gave the people favor in the sight of the Mitzrayim, so that they granted their request. So they plundered the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:46; SHEMOT 38:26; BAMIDBAR 33:3"> And the Bnei Yisroel journeyed from Ra'meses towards Sukkot, about 600,000 gevarim on foot, not counting women and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 11:4; ZECHARYAH 8:23"> And an erev rav (mixed multitude, a mixed company that was large) went along also with them; and tzon, and herds, even very many domestic animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="SHEMOT 11:1; SHEMOT 6:1; SHEMOT 12:31–33"> And they baked the batzek (deaf dough) which they brought forth out of Mitzrayim, into round flat cakes of matzot; ki lo chametz, because they were thrust out of Mitzrayim, and could not tarry (linger), neither had they prepared for tzeidah (supply of food, provision).</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:17"> Now the moshav (time period of residence) of the Bnei Yisroel dwelling in Mitzrayim was four hundred and thirty shanah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="CHABAKUK 2:3; YOCHANAN 7:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:7"> And it came to pass at the ketz (end) of the four hundred and thirty shanah, even the exact day it came to pass, that kol Tzivos Hashem went out from Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:1–6; SHEMOT 12:14; SHEMOT 13:10"> It is a night of shimmurim (keepings, watchings, vigils) for Hashem to keep watch to bring them out from Eretz Mitzrayim; this is halailah hazeh unto Hashem; shimmurim (watchings, vigils) for kol Bnei Yisroel in their dorot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:48; BAMIDBAR 9:14; SHEMOT 12:11"> And Hashem said unto Moshe and Aharon, This is the Chukkat HaPesach: there shall no ben nekhar eat thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:12–13; BERESHIS 17:23"> But every manʼs eved that is bought for kesef, when thou hast given him bris milah, then shall he eat thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="VAYIKRA 22:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:12"> A toshav and a sachir shall not eat thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 9:12; YOCHANAN 19:36; YOCHANAN 19:33"> In bais echad shall it be eaten; thou shalt not carry forth any of the basar outside the bais; neither shall ye break a bone thereof.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="See Yn 19:36">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:6; BAMIDBAR 9:13; SHEMOT 12:3"> Kol Adat Yisroel shall celebrate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 9:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:28"> And when a ger shall sojourn with thee, and will keep the Pesach unto Hashem, let all his zachar receive bris milah, and then let him come near and keep it; and he shall be as one that is native born in ha'aretz; for no arel (uncircumcised person) shall eat thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 15:15–16; BAMIDBAR 15:29; VAYIKRA 24:22"> Torah echad shall be to him that is native-born, and unto the ger that sojourneth among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:24–25; SHEMOT 6:26; SHEMOT 7:4"> Thus did kol Bnei Yisroel; as Hashem commanded Moshe and Aharon, so did they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:41; SHEMOT 6:26"> And it came to pass the very same day, that Hashem did bring the Bnei Yisroel out of Eretz Mitzrayim by their tzvaos.</span>
