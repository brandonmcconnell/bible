## &#91;MISHPATIM&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:14; DEVARIM 6:20; SHEMOT 24:3–4"> Now these are the mishpatim which thou shalt set before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 15:12–15; SHEMOT 12:44; DEVARIM 15:18"> If thou acquire an eved Ivri, shesh shanim he shall serve; and in the seventh he shall go out lachafeshi (to the freedom) for no charge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEVARIM 15:12–14"> If he came in by himself, he shall go out by himself; if he were a ba'al isha, then his isha shall go out with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> If his adon have given him an isha, and to him she gives birth to banim or banot; the isha and her yeledim shall be her adonʼs, and he shall go out &#91;free&#93; by himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:14–15; DEVARIM 15:16–17; YESHAYAH 26:13"> And if the eved shall plainly say, I love adoni, my isha, and my banim; I will not go out lachafeshi (to the freedom);</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEVARIM 15:17; VAYIKRA 25:40; DEVARIM 19:17–18"> Then his adon shall bring him unto HaElohim; he shall also bring him to the delet (door), or unto the mezuzah; and his adon shall pierce through his ozen (ear) with a piercing-tool; then he shall serve him l'olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 5:5; SHEMOT 21:2–3"> And if an ish sell his bat to be an amah (maidservant), she shall not go out as the avadim &#91;go free&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:8; DEVARIM 20:7; MALACHI 2:11–15"> If she please not her adon, who hath betrothed her to himself, then shall he let her be redeemed &#91;go free&#93;; to sell her unto an am nochri (foreign people) he shall have no power, seeing he hath dealt deceitfully with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And if he have betrothed her unto his ben, he shall deal with her according to the mishpat habanot (rights of &#91;free&#93; daughters).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., those rights of Ex 21:10">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:1–6"> If he take him another, her food, her covering of clothing, and her onah (conjugal rights) shall he not deprive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And if he does not perform these three unto her, then shall she go out free without kesef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 9:6; MATTITYAHU 26:52; VAYIKRA 24:17"> He that strikes down an ish, so that the ish die, shall be surely put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 20:2–9; SHMUEL ALEF 24:18; DEVARIM 4:41–43"> And if he lie not in ambush, but HaElohim deliver him into his yad, then I will appoint thee a makom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., city of refuge">b</a></sup>where he shall flee there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:28–34; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:26; BAMIDBAR 15:30–31"> But if an ish come premeditatedly upon his re'a, to murder him by guile; thou shalt take him from Mine Mizbe'ach for capital punishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEVARIM 21:18–21; MISHLE 30:17; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:9"> And he that striketh down his av, or his em, shall be surely put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEVARIM 24:7; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:10; SHEMOT 22:4"> And he that kidnaps an ish, and selleth him, or if he be found in his power, the kidnapper shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MISHLE 20:20; MARKOS 7:10–11; DEVARIM 27:16"> And he that curseth his av, or his em, shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 21:20; DEVARIM 25:11; SHMUEL BAIS 14:6"> And if anashim quarrel, and one strike another with an even (stone), or with his egrof (fist), and he die not, but is bedfast;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:29; ZECHARYAH 8:4"> If he rise again, and walk around outside upon his staff, then shall he that struck down him be absolved; only shivto yiten (he shall give for his lost time), and shall provide for him to be thoroughly healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MISHLE 29:19; DEVARIM 19:21; BAMIDBAR 35:19"> And if an ish strikes his eved, or his amah, with a shevet (rod), and he die under his yad; he shall be surely avenged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:44–46"> Notwithstanding, if he continue a yom or two, he shall not be avenged; for he is his kesef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEVARIM 22:18–19; SHEMOT 21:30; DEVARIM 16:18"> If men fight, and hurt an isha harah (pregnant woman), so that she gives birth prematurely but not with any injury; he shall be surely punished, according as the ba'al haisha will assess a fine upon him; and he shall pay as the judges determine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEVARIM 19:21; BAMIDBAR 35:31; VAYIKRA 24:19"> And if any ason (harm, fatality) follow, then thou shalt take nefesh for nefesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEVARIM 19:21; MATTITYAHU 5:38–40; MATTITYAHU 7:2"> Ayin for ayin, shen for shen, yad for yad, regel for regel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Burn for burn, wound for wound, chaburah (stripe laceration) for chaburah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHEMOT 21:20; IYOV 31:13–15; TEHILLIM 72:12–14"> And if an ish strike the ayin of his eved, or the ayin of his amah, that it perish; he shall let him go free for his ayinʼs sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And if he strike his evedʼs shen (tooth), or his amahʼs shen; he shall let him go free for his shenʼs sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHEMOT 21:32; VAYIKRA 20:15–16; BERESHIS 9:5–6"> If an ox gore an ish or an isha, that they die; then the ox shall be surely stoned, and his basar shall not be eaten; but the ba'al hashor (owner of the ox) shall be exempt from punishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEVARIM 21:1–9"> But if the shor habitually from mitmol (yesterday) gored, and its ba'al has been warned, and he hath not kept it in the bull pen, and it hath killed an ish or an isha; the shor shall be stoned, and his ba'al also shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="SHEMOT 21:22; SHEMOT 30:12; BAMIDBAR 35:31–33"> If there be laid on him a kofer (atonement payment, ransom), then he shall give for the redemption of his nefesh whatsoever is assessed upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Whether he have gored a ben, or have gored a bat, according to this mishpat shall it be done unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:15; ZECHARYAH 11:12–13; MATTITYAHU 27:3–9"> If the ox shall gore an eved or an amah; he shall give unto their adon sheloshim shekalim kesef, and the ox shall be stoned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MISHLE 28:10; YIRMEYAH 18:20; KOHELET 10:8"> And if an ish shall open a bor (pit), or if an ish shall dig a bor and not cover it, and an ox or a donkey fall therein;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:6; SHEMOT 21:29–30; SHEMOT 22:14"> The ba'al habor shall make restitution, and give kesef unto their ba'alim; and the carcass shall be his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And if the shor ish (ox of a man) hurt the ox of a neighbor, that it die; then they shall sell the shor hachai (live ox), and divide the kesef of it; and the carcass also they shall divide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Or if it be known that the shor hath habitually from mitmol (yesterday) gored, and his ba'al hath not kept it in the bull pen; he shall surely pay ox for ox; and the carcass shall belong to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> If a ganav takes a shor, or a seh, and slaughter it, or sell it; he shall restore five cattle for an ox, and the seh.</span>
