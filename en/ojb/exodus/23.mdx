<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="TEHILLIM 101:5; MISHLE 19:5; DEVARIM 19:16–21"> Thou shalt not spread a false report; put not thine hand with the rashah to be an ed chamas (malicious witness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MISHLE 1:10–11; MISHLE 1:15; LUKAS 23:23–24"> Thou shalt not follow a multitude to do evil; neither shalt thou speak in a riv (cause, lawsuit) to turn aside after many to pervert justice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17; VAYIKRA 19:15; TEHILLIM 82:2–3"> Neither shalt thou favor a poor man in his riv (cause, lawsuit).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEVARIM 22:1–4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:15; MATTITYAHU 5:44"> If thou meet thine enemyʼs shor (ox) or his chamor (donkey) going astray, thou shalt surely bring it back to him again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 22:4"> If thou see the chamor of him that hateth thee lying under his massa, and wouldest refrain from helping him, thou shalt surely help with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MALACHI 3:5; YIRMEYAH 5:28; VAYIKRA 19:15"> Thou shalt not pervert the mishpat of thy poor in his riv (cause, lawsuit).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:25; SHEMOT 34:7; SHEMOT 23:1"> Keep thee far from a devar sheker (false matter); and the naki (innocent) and tzaddik slay thou not; for I will not acquit the rashah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:19; MISHLE 17:23; YESHAYAH 5:23"> And thou shalt take no shochad (bribe, gift); for the shochad blindeth the seeing, and perverteth the words of the tzaddikim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEVARIM 27:19; SHEMOT 22:21; DEVARIM 24:14–18"> Also thou shalt not oppress a ger; for ye know the nefesh of a ger, seeing ye were gerim in Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:3–4"> And shesh shanim thou shalt sow thy land, and shalt gather in the crops thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:34–35; VAYIKRA 25:2–7; VAYIKRA 25:11–12"> But the shevi'it thou shalt leave it shamat (unplowed) and let it lie fallow; that the evyon (poor) of thy people may eat; and what they leave the beasts of the sadeh shall eat. In like manner thou shalt deal with thy kerem (vineyard), and with thy zayit (olive) grove.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKAS 13:14; SHEMOT 34:21; SHEMOT 35:3"> Sheshet yamim thou shalt do thy work, and on the yom hashevi'i thou shalt rest; that thine shor (ox) and thine chamor (donkey) may rest, and the ben of thy amah (handmaid), and the ger, may be refreshed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 23:7; ZECHARYAH 13:2; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:16"> And in all things that I have said unto you be circumspect; and make no mention of the shem elohim acherim, neither let it be heard out of thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:16; SHEMOT 34:22–24; VAYIKRA 23:34"> Three times thou shalt keep a Chag unto Me in the shanah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 22:7; SHEMOT 34:20; BAMIDBAR 28:16–25"> Thou shalt be shomer to keep the Chag HaMatzot; thou shalt eat matzot shivah yamim, as I commanded thee, in the time appointed of the month Aviv; for in it thou camest out from Mitzrayim; and none shall appear before Me empty-handed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:22; SHEMOT 22:29; VAYIKRA 23:9–21"> And the Chag HaKatzir, Bikkurim (Firstfruits)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Ac 2">a</a></sup>of thy labors, which thou hast sown in the sadeh; and the Chag HaAsif (Feast of Ingathering),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Yn 7">b</a></sup>which is in the end of the shanah, when thou hast gathered in thy labors out of the sadeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:16; SHEMOT 34:23; SHEMOT 23:14"> Shalosh pe'amim in the shanah all thy zachar shall appear before HaAdon Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:25; VAYIKRA 2:11; DEVARIM 16:4"> Thou shalt not offer the dahm of My zevach with chametz; neither shall the chelev of My sacrifice remain until the boker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:26; DEVARIM 14:21; DEVARIM 26:10"> The reshit bikkurim of thy land thou shalt bring into the Bais Hashem Eloheicha.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Ac 2">c</a></sup>Thou shalt not cook a kid in his motherʼs cholov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 33:14; TEHILLIM 91:11; SHEMOT 32:34"> Hinei, I send Malach before thee, to be shomer over thee in the derech, and to bring thee into the place which I have prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:56; TEHILLIM 78:40; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:25"> Pay heed to him, and obey his voice, provoke him not; for he will not pardon your peysha'im; for My Shem is in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:3; DEVARIM 30:7; ZECHARYAH 2:8"> But if thou shalt indeed obey his &#91;bat&#93; kol, and do all that I speak; then I will be an enemy unto thine enemies, and an adversary unto thine adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:20; YESHAYAH 5:13; BERESHIS 15:19–21"> For My Malach shall go before thee, and bring thee in unto the Emori, and the Chitti, and the Perizzi, and the Kena'ani, the Chivi, and the Yevusi; I will cut them off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:5; DEVARIM 12:30–31; BAMIDBAR 33:52"> Thou shalt not bow down to their elohim, nor serve them, nor follow after their works; but thou shalt utterly overthrow them, and completely break down their matztzvot (memorial columns dedicated to idols).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:15; SHEMOT 15:26; YEHOSHUA 22:5"> And ye shall serve Hashem Eloheichem, and He shall bless thy lechem, and thy mayim; and I will take machalah (sickness) away from the midst of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:14; IYOV 5:26; DEVARIM 28:4"> There shall nothing miscarry their young, nor be barren, in thy land; the mispar (number) of thy yamim I will fulfill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:25; BERESHIS 35:5; DEVARIM 11:25"> I will send My ehmah (terror) ahead of thee, and will throw into confusion all the people to whom thou shalt come, and I will make all thine enemies turn their backs unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:20; YEHOSHUA 24:11–12"> And I will send the tzirah (hornet) ahead of thee, which shall drive out the Chivi, the Kena'ani, and the Chitti, from before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:22; YEHOSHUA 17:12–13; SHOFETIM 3:1–4"> I will not drive them out from before thee in shanah echat; lest ha'aretz become desolate, and the beast of the sadeh multiply against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Me'at me'at (little by little) I will drive them out from before thee, until thou be increased, and inherit ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:18; DEVARIM 11:24; YEHOSHUA 21:44"> And I will establish thy borders from the Yam Suf even unto the Yam Pelishtim, and from the Midbar unto the &#91;Euphrates&#93; River: for I will deliver the inhabitants of ha'aretz into your hand; and thou shalt drive them out before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:2; SHEMOT 34:12; SHEMOT 34:15"> Thou shalt make no brit (covenant) with them, nor with their elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="TEHILLIM 106:36; DEVARIM 7:16; SHEMOT 34:12"> They shall not dwell in thy land, lest they make thee sin against Me; for if thou serve their elohim, it will surely be a mokesh (snare) unto thee.</span>
