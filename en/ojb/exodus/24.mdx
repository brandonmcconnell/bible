<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 11:16; SHEMOT 6:23; VAYIKRA 10:1–2"> And He said unto Moshe, Come up unto Hashem, thou, and Aharon, Nadav, and Avihu, and shive'im (seventy) of the Ziknei Yisroel; and worship ye afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 24:15; SHEMOT 24:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:21–22"> And Moshe alone shall come near Hashem; but they shall not come near; neither shall HaAm go up with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:8; SHEMOT 24:7; YEHOSHUA 24:22"> And Moshe came and told HaAm kol divrei Hashem, and all the mishpatim; and kol HaAm answered with one voice, and said, All the words which Hashem hath said will we do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:18; DEVARIM 31:9; YEHOSHUA 4:8–9"> And Moshe wrote kol divrei Hashem, and rose up early in the boker, and built a Mizbe'ach at the base of HaHar, and twelve matzevah (stone pillars), according to the twelve Shivtei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 18:12; VAYIKRA 3:1–17; SHEMOT 19:22"> And he sent out na'arei Bnei Yisroel, which offered olot (burnt offerings), and sacrificed shelamim (peace offerings) of bulls unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19; SHEMOT 24:8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:18"> And Moshe took half of the dahm, and put it in bowls; and the other half of the dahm he sprinkled upon the Mizbe'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 31:11–13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:27; SHEMOT 24:3–4"> Then he took the Sefer HaBrit, and read it aloud in the ears of HaAm, and they responded, All that Hashem hath said we will do, and be obedient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:4–5; SHEMOT 24:6; YECHEZKEL 36:25"> And Moshe took the remaining dahm, and sprinkled it on HaAm, and said, Hinei dahm habrit, which Hashem hath cut with you concerning all these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 24:1"> Then went up Moshe, and Aharon, Nadav, and Avihu, and shive'im Ziknei Yisroel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:18; YECHEZKEL 10:1; BERESHIS 32:30"> And they saw the Elohei Yisroel; and there was under His feet the likeness of sapphire stone pavement, and like the very Shomayim in its clearness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 15:23–24; SHEMOT 18:12; BERESHIS 31:54"> And against the leaders of the Bnei Yisroel He laid not His yad; also they saw HaElohim, and did eat and drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEVARIM 5:22; SHEMOT 31:18; SHEMOT 32:15–16"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, Come up to Me into HaHar, and remain there; and I will give thee the Luchot HaEven, and the torah, and the mitzvot which I have written to teach them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 33:11; BAMIDBAR 11:28; SHEMOT 3:1"> And Moshe rose up, and Yehoshua meshareto (the one aiding him, the one ministering to him); and Moshe went up into the Har HaElohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 17:10; SHEMOT 17:12; BERESHIS 22:5"> And he said unto the Zekenim, Tarry ye here for us, until we come again unto you; and, hinei, Aharon and Chur are with you; if any man have a grievance, let him come unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:9; MATTITYAHU 17:5; SHEMOT 19:16"> And Moshe went up into HaHar, and an anan concealed HaHar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:10; BAMIDBAR 16:42; YECHEZKEL 1:28"> And the Kevod Hashem abode upon Mt. Sinai, and the anan concealed it sheshet yamim; and the yom hashevi'i He called unto Moshe out of the midst of the anan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:29; DEVARIM 4:24; SHEMOT 3:2"> And the sight of the Kevod Hashem was like eish ochelet (devouring fire) on the top of HaHar in the eyes of the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEVARIM 9:9; SHEMOT 34:28; DEVARIM 10:10"> And Moshe went into the midst of the anan, going up into HaHar; and Moshe was in HaHar arba'im yom v'arba'im lailah.</span>
