<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:59; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:12–14; SHEMOT 6:16–20"> And there went an ish of the Bais Levi, and took to wife a bat Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:20; TEHILLIM 112:5"> And the isha conceived, and bore a ben; and when she saw him that he was tov, she hid him three months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 1:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:19; BERESHIS 6:14"> And when she could no longer hide him, she took for him a tevah (ark) of papyrus, and daubed it with tar and with pitch, and put the yeled therein; and she set it in the reeds by the bank of the Nile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:59; SHEMOT 15:20; MICHOH 6:4"> And his achot stood afar off, to see what would be done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:21; TEHILLIM 12:5; TEHILLIM 9:9"> And the Bat Pharaoh came down to bathe herself at the Nile; and her na'arot walked along by the Nileʼs bank; and when she saw the tevah (ark) among the reeds, she sent her amah (maid servant) to fetch it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:50; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:21"> And when she had opened it, she saw the yeled; and, hinei, the na'ar was crying. And she had compassion on him, and said, This one is of the yaldei HaIvrim (Hebrew children).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 2:4; BAMIDBAR 26:59; BAMIDBAR 12:1"> Then said his achot to Bat Pharaoh, Shall I go and call to thee a nurse of HaIvriyyot, that she may nurse the yeled for thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YESHAYAH 46:3–4; TEHILLIM 27:10; SHEMOT 6:20"> And Bat Pharaoh said to her, Go. And haalmah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Gn 24:43; Isa 7:14">a</a></sup>went and called the em hayeled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Bat Pharaoh said unto her, Take this yeled away, and nurse it for me, and I will give thee thy wages. And the isha took the yeled, and nursed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:24; BERESHIS 16:11; SHMUEL ALEF 1:20"> And the yeled grew, and she brought him unto Bat Pharaoh, and he became her ben. And she called shmo Moshe: and she said, Because meshitihu (I drew him) out of the mayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:24–26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:22–24; SHEMOT 1:11"> And it came to pass in those days, when Moshe was grown, that he went out unto his achim, and looked on their sevalot: and he spied an Egyptian striking an ish Ivri, one of his achim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:24–26"> And he looked this way and that way, and when he saw that there was no ish, he killed the Egyptian, and hid him in the chol (sand).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:26–28; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:7–8"> And when he went out the second day, hinei, two anashim Ivrim were fighting; he said to him in the wrong, Why strike thou thy re'a?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 12:14; MATTITYAHU 21:23; BERESHIS 19:9"> And he said, Who made thee a sar and a shofet over us? Intendest thou to kill me, as thou killedst the Egyptian? And Moshe feared, and thought, Surely this thing is known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:29; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:27; BERESHIS 24:11"> Now when Pharaoh heard this thing, he sought to slay Moshe. But Moshe fled from the face of Pharaoh, and dwelt in Eretz Midyan; and he sat down by a well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:11; SHEMOT 3:1; SHMUEL ALEF 9:11"> Now the kohen of Midyan had sheva banot; and they came and drew mayim, and filled the troughs to water the tzon of their av.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 29:10; BERESHIS 21:25; BERESHIS 26:15–22"> And the ro'im (shepherds) came and drove them away; but Moshe stood up and rescued them, and watered their tzon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:1; BAMIDBAR 10:29; SHEMOT 4:18"> And when they came to Reuel their av, he said, How is it that ye are come so soon today?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BERESHIS 50:11; BERESHIS 29:10"> And they said, An Egyptian delivered us out of the yad of the ro'im (shepherds), and also drew enough for us, and watered the tzon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 31:54; BERESHIS 43:25; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:2"> And he said unto his banot, And where is he? Why is it that ye have left the ish? Call him, that he may eat lechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 31:38–40; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:25"> And Moshe was content to dwell with the ish; and he gave Moshe Tzipporah his bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:13–14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:29; SHEMOT 22:21"> And she bore him a ben, and he called shmo Gershom; for he said, I have been a ger in a foreign land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:20–21; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:4; SHEMOT 3:7–9"> And it came to pass in process of time, that Melech Mitzrayim died; and the Bnei Yisroel groaned by reason of haAvodah (the Bondage), and they cried out, and their cry came up unto HaElohim by reason of haAvodah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BERESHIS 26:3; TEHILLIM 105:42; SHEMOT 6:5"> And Elohim heard their groaning, and Elohim remembered His brit (covenant) with Avraham, with Yitzchak, and with Ya'akov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:31; TEHILLIM 55:22; SHEMOT 3:7–8"> And Elohim looked upon the Bnei Yisroel, and Elohim had da'as of them.</span>
