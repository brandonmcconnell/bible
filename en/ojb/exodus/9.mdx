<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHEMOT 8:1; SHEMOT 10:3; SHEMOT 8:20"> Then Hashem said unto Moshe, Go in unto Pharaoh, tell him, Thus saith Hashem Elohei HaIvrim, Let My people go, that they may serve Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 8:2; SHEMOT 4:23; VAYIKRA 26:27–28"> For if thou refuse to let them go, and wilt hold them still,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 7:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:11; SHMUEL ALEF 6:9"> Hinei, the Yad Hashem is upon thy mikneh (livestock) which is in the sadeh, upon the susim, upon the donkeys, upon the camels, upon the herd, and upon the tzon; there shall be a very grievous dever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 8:22; SHEMOT 10:23; MALACHI 3:18"> And Hashem shall separate between the mikneh Yisroel and the mikneh Mitzrayim; and there shall nothing die of all that belongs to the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 8:23; SHEMOT 10:4; YIRMEYAH 28:16–17"> And Hashem appointed a mo'ed, saying, Tomorrow Hashem shall do this thing in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:48; SHEMOT 9:25; TEHILLIM 78:50"> And Hashem did that thing on the next day, and all the mikneh of Mitzrayim died; but of the mikneh of the Bnei Yisroel died not one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 7:14; SHEMOT 8:32; YESHAYAH 48:4"> And Pharaoh sent, and, hinei, there was not one of the mikneh Yisroel dead. And the lev Pharaoh was hardened, and he did not let HaAm go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 8:16"> And Hashem said unto Moshe and unto Aharon, Take to you handfuls of piach of the furnace, and let Moshe sprinkle it toward Shomayim in the sight of Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HISGALUS 16:2; DEVARIM 28:27; IYOV 2:7"> And it shall become fine dust in kol Eretz Mitzrayim, and shall be a sh'khin (boil) breaking forth with festers upon adam, and upon behemah, throughout kol Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:27"> And they took piach of the furnace, and stood before Pharaoh; and Moshe sprinkled it up toward Shomayim; and it became a sh'khin breaking forth with festers upon adam, and upon behemah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 7:11–12; SHEMOT 8:18–19; HISGALUS 16:2"> And the charetumim could not stand before Moshe because of the sh'khin; for the sh'khin was upon the charetumim, and upon kol Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:21; SHEMOT 7:13–14; TEHILLIM 81:11–12"> And Hashem hardened the lev Pharaoh, and he paid heed not unto them; as Hashem had spoken unto Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 8:20; SHEMOT 7:15; SHEMOT 9:1"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, Rise up early in the boker, and stand before Pharaoh, and say unto him, Thus saith Hashem Elohei HaIvrim, Let My people go, that they may serve Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 8:10; VAYIKRA 26:28; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:20"> For I will at this time send all My maggefot upon thine lev, and upon thy avadim, and upon thy people; that thou mayest have da'as that there is none like Me in kol ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:20; SHEMOT 9:3; SHEMOT 9:16"> For now I will stretch out My Yad, that I may strike thee and thy people with dever; and thou shalt be cut off from ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:17; MISHLE 16:4; TEHILLIM 83:17–18"> In fact for this cause have I raised thee up, for to show in thee My ko'ach; and that My Shem may be declared throughout kol ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YESHAYAH 37:29; YESHAYAH 10:15; YESHAYAH 37:23–24"> Thou still are exalting thyself against My people, that thou wilt not let them go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 20:6; TEHILLIM 83:15; SHEMOT 9:22–25"> Hinei, tomorrow about this time I will cause it to rain very grievous barad (hail), such as hath not been in Mitzrayim since the foundation thereof even until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="CHABAKUK 3:2"> Send therefore now, and gather thy mikneh, and all that thou hast in the sadeh; for upon every adam (man) and behemah (beast) which shall be found in the sadeh, and shall not be brought into shelter, the barad shall come down upon them, and they shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MISHLE 13:13; MISHLE 22:3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:7"> He that feared the Devar Hashem among the avadim of Pharaoh made his avadim and his mikneh flee into the shelters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 40:4; IYOV 34:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:19"> And he that took not to heart the Devar Hashem left his avadim and his mikneh in the sadeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HISGALUS 16:21; SHEMOT 7:19; SHEMOT 8:5"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, Stretch forth thine yad toward Shomayim, that there may be barad in kol Eretz Mitzrayim, upon adam, and upon behemah, and upon kol esev hasadeh, throughout Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="TEHILLIM 18:13; YEHOSHUA 10:11; YESHAYAH 30:30"> And Moshe stretched forth his matteh toward Shomayim; and Hashem sent thunder and barad, and the eish flashed down upon the ground; and Hashem rained barad upon Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHEMOT 9:23; MATTITYAHU 24:21; SHEMOT 10:6"> So there was barad, and eish mingled with the barad, very grievous, such as there was none like it in kol Eretz Mitzrayim since it became a nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:47; TEHILLIM 105:32–33"> And the barad struck throughout kol Eretz Mitzrayim all that was in the sadeh, both adam and behemah; the barad struck kol esev hasadeh, and beat down kol etz of the sadeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHEMOT 11:7; SHEMOT 9:4; SHEMOT 10:23"> Only in Eretz Goshen, where the Bnei Yisroel were, was there no barad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EKHAH 1:18; TEHILLIM 129:4; SHEMOT 10:16"> And Pharaoh sent, and called for Moshe and Aharon, and said unto them, chatati (I have sinned) this time: Hashem HaTzaddik (is righteous), and I and my people are haresha'im (wicked, guilty, wrong).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHEMOT 8:8; SHEMOT 10:17; SHEMOT 8:28"> Entreat Hashem for it is enough, that there be no more Elohim thunderings and barad; and I will let you go, and ye shall stay no longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:26; MELACHIM ALEF 8:38; YESHAYAH 1:15"> And Moshe said unto him, As soon as I am gone out of the Ir, I will spread out my palms<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="davening">a</a></sup>unto Hashem; and the thunder shall cease, neither shall there be any more barad; that thou mayest have da'as that ha'aretz is Hashemʼs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YESHAYAH 26:10; MISHLE 16:6; YESHAYAH 63:17"> But as for thee and thy avadim, I know that ye will not yet fear Hashem Elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="RUTH 1:22; RUTH 2:23; CHABAKUK 3:17"> And the flax and the barley was struck; for the barley was ripe, and the flax was in bud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHEMOT 10:22"> But the wheat and the spelt were not struck; for they were not yet come up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="SHEMOT 9:29; SHEMOT 8:12; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:17–18"> And Moshe went out of the Ir from Pharaoh, and spread out his palms<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="davening">b</a></sup>unto Hashem; and the thunders and barad ceased, and the rain was not poured upon ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="SHEMOT 7:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:13; SHEMOT 4:21"> And when Pharaoh saw that the rain and the barad and the thunders were ceased, he sinned yet more, and hardened his lev, he and his avadim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:21"> And the lev Pharaoh was hardened, neither would he let the Bnei Yisroel go; as Hashem had spoken by Moshe.</span>
