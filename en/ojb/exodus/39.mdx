</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:6–12; VAYIKRA 8:7; SHEMOT 25:7"> And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Betzalel">a</a></sup>made the Ephod of zahav, turquoise, and purple, and scarlet wool, and twisted fine linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 36:8; SHEMOT 26:1"> And they did hammer the zahav into thin sheets, and cut threads, to work into the turquoise, and into the purple, and into the fine linen, the artistic weaverʼs work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> They made attaching ketefot (shoulder straps) for it, which were attached to its two &#91;upper&#93; ends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:13; MATTITYAHU 28:20; YESHAYAH 11:5"> And the Cheishev (Belt) of his Ephod, which is upon it, was of the same according to the work thereof; of zahav, turquoise, and purple, and scarlet wool, and twisted linen; as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:9; YECHEZKEL 28:13; IYOV 28:16"> And they made the avnei shoham (onyx stones), set in settings of zahav, engraved, like chotam (signet ring) is engraved, according to the Shemot Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 4:7; SHEMOT 28:12; SHEMOT 28:29"> And he fastened them upon the Kitfot HaEphod, that they should be Avnei Zikaron (Memorial Stones) unto the Bnei Yisroel; as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:4; VAYIKRA 8:8–9; YESHAYAH 59:17"> And he made the Choshen of artistic weaverʼs work, like the workmanship of the Ephod; of zahav, turquoise, and purple, and scarlet wool, and fine twisted linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> It was ravu'a (square); they made the Choshen folded double; a span was the length thereof, and a span the width thereof, being doubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:21; SHEMOT 28:16–17; HISGALUS 21:19–21"> And they set in it four rows of gemstones; the first row was an odem, a pitdah, and barekes; this was the first row.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:18; YECHEZKEL 28:13"> And the second row, a nofech, sapphire, and yahalom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the third row, a leshem, shevo, and achlamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the fourth row, a tarshish, shoham, and yashfeh; they were set in zahav in their settings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:12"> And the gemstones were according to the Shemot Bnei Yisroel, Shteym Esreh (Twelve), according to their Shemot, like the engravings of a chotam (signet ring), every one with shmo, according to the Shneym Asar Shevet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:28; YOCHANAN 17:12; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5"> And they made upon the Choshen sharsherot (chains) at the ends braided artistic work of zahav tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:12"> And they made two mishbetzot (filigrees) of zahav, and two rings of zahav; and put the two rings in the two ends of the Choshen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And they fastened the two ropes of zahav into the two rings on the ends of the Choshen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:14; SHIR HASHIRIM 1:10"> And the &#91;other&#93; two ends of the two ropes they fastened in the two filigree settings, and put them on the ketefot HaEphod toward its front.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And they made two rings of zahav, and put them on the two ends of the Choshen, upon the lower border therof, on the inside toward the Ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:3"> And they made two other rings of zahav, and put them on the two kitfot HaEphod on the bottom, toward the front thereof, opposite the seam thereof, above the Cheishev HaEphod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:25; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:27"> And they did bind the Choshen by its rings unto the rings of the Ephod with a turquoise woolen cord, that it might be above the Cheishev HaEphod, and that the Choshen might not be loosed from the Ephod; as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:31–35"> And he made the Me'il HaEphod of artistic weaverʼs work, all of turquoise wool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And there was a head opening in the top of the Me'il, in its center, like the opening of a coat of mail, with a border all around about the head opening, that it should not be torn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:33; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:22"> And they made upon the hems of the Me'il pomegranates of turquoise, and purple, and scarlet wool, and twisted linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:33–34; SHIR HASHIRIM 4:13; TEHILLIM 89:15"> And they made bells of zahav tahor, and put the bells between the pomegranates upon the hem of the Me'il, all around between the pomegranates;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:34; SHIR HASHIRIM 4:13; SHIR HASHIRIM 6:7"> A bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate, all around the hem of the Me'il to minister in; as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 44:18; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:13; YESHAYAH 61:10"> And they made the kuttanot (tunics) of fine linen of artistic weaverʼs work for Aharon, and for his banim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:4; YECHEZKEL 44:18; SHEMOT 28:39"> And a Mitznefet of fine linen, Pa'arei HaMigba'ot of fine linen, mikhnesei bahd,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:39"> And an Avnet of fine linen, and turquoise, and purple, and scarlet wool, of artistic embroidery; as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="HISGALUS 5:10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:26; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:3"> And they made the Tzitz Nezer HaKodesh of zahav tahor, and engraved upon it an inscription, like to the engravings of a chotam, KODESH LAHASHEM.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And they tied unto it a turquoise cord, to fasten it on the Mitznefet above; as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHEMOT 39:42–43; MATTITYAHU 28:20; SHEMOT 25:40"> Thus was all the work of the Mishkan of the Ohel Mo'ed finished; and the Bnei Yisroel did according to all that Hashem commanded Moshe, so did they.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="SHEMOT 35:11–19; SHEMOT 31:7–11; SHEMOT 36:1–38"> And they brought the Mishkan unto Moshe, the Ohel, and all its utensils, its hooks, its kerashim, its crossbars, and its ammudim, and its sockets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And the Mikhseh of red-dyed ram skins, and the Mikhseh of tachash skins, and the Parochet HaMasach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:5; SHEMOT 25:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:8"> The Aron HaEdut, and the carrying poles thereof, and the Kapporet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:30; MELACHIM ALEF 7:48"> The Shulchan, and all the utensils thereof, and the Lechem HaPanim,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:15; SHEMOT 27:21; MATTITYAHU 5:14–16"> The Menorah Hatehorah, with the nerot (lamps) thereof, even with the nerot (lamps) of the order prescribed, and all the utensils thereof, and the Shemen HaMaohr,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="SHEMOT 30:7; SHEMOT 37:29; SHEMOT 30:3"> And the Mizbe'ach Hazahav, and the Shemen HaMishchah, and the aromatic ketoret, and the Masach petach HaOhel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:64; SHEMOT 38:30"> The Mizbe'ach Hanechoshet, and its mikhbar hanechoshet (grate, netting of copper), its carrying poles, and all its utensils, the Kiyor and its Stand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="SHEMOT 27:9–16"> The curtains of the khatzer (courtyard), its ammudim, and its sockets, and the masach Sha'ar HaKhatzer, its cords, and its tent pegs, and all the utensils of the Avodas HaMishkan, for the Ohel Mo'ed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:2; SHEMOT 31:10; SHEMOT 39:1"> The bigdei serad for service in HaKodesh, and the Bigdei Hakodesh for Aharon the kohen, and the garments of his banim, to minister in the kohenʼs office.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="SHEMOT 35:10; SHEMOT 23:21–22; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:15"> According to all that Hashem commanded Moshe, so the Bnei Yisroel did kol haAvodah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 6:23–27; VAYIKRA 9:22–23; SHMUEL BAIS 6:18"> And Moshe did inspect all the work, and, hinei, they had done it as Hashem had commanded, even so had they done it; vayevarech otam Moshe (and Moshe blessed them).</span>
