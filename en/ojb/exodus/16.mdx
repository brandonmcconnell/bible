<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 33:10–12; SHEMOT 15:27; SHEMOT 17:1"> And they took their journey from Elim, and kol Adat Bnei Yisroel came unto the midbar of Sin (pronounced “Seen”), which is between Elim and Sinai, on the fifteenth day of the second month after their departing out of Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:10; SHEMOT 15:24; TEHILLIM 106:25"> And kol Adat Bnei Yisroel murmured against Moshe and Aharon in the midbar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 11:4–5; BAMIDBAR 20:3–5; SHEMOT 17:3"> And the Bnei Yisroel said unto them, If only G-d would have made us die by the yad Hashem in Eretz Mitzrayim, when we sat by the sir habasar (pot of meat), and when we did eat lechem to the full; for ye have brought us forth into this midbar, to kill this kol hakahal with ra'av (hunger).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:3; TEHILLIM 78:24–25; DEVARIM 8:2"> Then said Hashem unto Moshe, Hineni, I will rain lechem from Shomayim upon you; and HaAm shall go out and gather a certain daily provision, that I may test them, whether they will walk in My torah, or no.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:22–23; SHEMOT 35:2–3; VAYIKRA 25:21–22"> And it shall come to pass, that on the yom hashishi (sixth day) they shall prepare that which they bring in; and it shall be twice as much as they gather daily.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 6:7; BAMIDBAR 16:28; SHEMOT 16:12–13"> And Moshe and Aharon said unto kol Bnei Yisroel, At erev, then ye shall know that Hashem hath brought you out from Eretz Mitzrayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:5; BAMIDBAR 16:11; YOCHANAN 11:40"> And in the boker, then ye shall see the kevod Hashem; because He heareth your telunnot (murmurings, grumblings) against Hashem; and who are we, that ye murmur against us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:2; SHMUEL ALEF 8:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:8"> And Moshe said, This shall be, when Hashem shall give you basar in the erev to eat, and in the boker lechem to the full; because Hashem heareth your telunnot which ye murmur against Him; and who are we? Your telunnot are not against us, but against Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 16:16; SHEMOT 16:2; SHEMOT 16:8"> And Moshe spoke unto Aharon, Say unto kol Adat Bnei Yisroel, Come near before Hashem; for He hath heard your telunnot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:7; BAMIDBAR 16:19; BAMIDBAR 16:42"> And it came to pass, as Aharon spoke unto kol Adat Bnei Yisroel, that they looked toward the midbar, and, hinei, the kevod Hashem appeared in the anan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YOEL 3:17; YECHEZKEL 39:22; SHEMOT 6:7"> I have heard the telunnot Bnei Yisroel; speak unto them, saying, At twilight ye shall eat basar, and in the boker ye shall be glutted with lechem; and ye shall know<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="by experience">a</a></sup>that I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 11:9; TEHILLIM 105:40; TEHILLIM 78:27–28"> And it came to pass, that at erev the quails came up, and covered the machaneh; and in the boker there was a layer of tal (dew) about the machaneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 11:7–9; TEHILLIM 105:40; TEHILLIM 78:24"> And when the layer of tal evaporated, hinei, upon the surface of the midbar there lay thin flakes, as thin as a layer of kfor (frost) upon ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:4; YOCHANAN 6:58; SHEMOT 16:31"> And when the Bnei Yisroel saw it, they said one to another, Mahn hu (What is it?) For they knew not mah hu (what &#91;is&#93; this). And Moshe said unto them, This is the lechem which Hashem hath given you to eat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:36; SHEMOT 16:18; SHEMOT 16:32–33"> This is the thing which Hashem hath commanded, Gather of it every man according to his eating &#91;need&#93;, an omer per person, according to the number of your nefashot; take ye every man for them which are in his ohel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the Bnei Yisroel did so, and gathered, some more, some less.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:14–15"> And when they did measure it by the omer, he that gathered much had nothing extra, and he that gathered little had no lack; they gathered every man according to his eating &#91;need&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:10; SHEMOT 23:18; MATTITYAHU 6:34"> And Moshe said, Let no ish leave of it until boker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MARKOS 10:14; BAMIDBAR 16:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:26"> Notwithstanding, they paid heed not unto Moshe; but some left part of it until boker, and &#91;the leftovers&#93; bred tola'im (worms), and stank; and Moshe was angry with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MISHLE 6:6–11; KOHELET 12:1; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:2"> And they gathered it every boker, every man according to his eating &#91;need&#93;; and when the shemesh grew hot, it melted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:5; VAYIKRA 25:22; SHEMOT 16:16"> And it came to pass, that on yom hashishi they gathered twice as much lechem, two omers for one man; and all the nesi'im of the Edah came and told Moshe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:3; SHEMOT 31:15; SHEMOT 20:8–11"> And he said unto them, This is that which Hashem hath said, Tomorrow is a Shabbaton (day of rest), Shabbos Kodesh unto Hashem; bake that which ye will bake today, and boil that ye will boil; and that which remaineth over lay up for you for mishmeret (for keeping) until boker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:20; SHEMOT 16:33"> And they laid it up, saving it until boker, as Moshe commanded; and it did not stink, neither was there any infestation therein.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:23; SHEMOT 16:29; NECHEMYAH 9:14"> And Moshe said, Eat that today; for today is a Shabbos unto Hashem; today ye shall not find it in the sadeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEVARIM 5:13; SHEMOT 20:9–11; YECHEZKEL 46:1"> Sheshet yamim ye shall gather it; but on the yom hashevi'i, which is Shabbos, in it there shall be none.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MISHLE 20:4"> And it came to pass, that there went out some from HaAm on the yom hashevi'i to gather, and they found none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:10; TEHILLIM 106:13; BAMIDBAR 14:11"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, How long refuse ye to be shomer over My mitzvot and My torot?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHEMOT 31:13; YESHAYAH 58:13–14; YECHEZKEL 20:12"> See, that Hashem hath given you the Shabbos, therefore He giveth you on yom hashishi lechem for two days; abide ye every man in his place, let no man go out from his place on yom hashevi'i.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEVARIM 5:12–14; VAYIKRA 23:3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:9"> So HaAm rested on yom hashevi'i.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHIR HASHIRIM 2:3; BAMIDBAR 11:6–9; SHEMOT 16:15"> And Bais Yisroel called the shem thereof Manna; and it was like coriander seed, lavan (white); and the taste of it was like flat cakes fried in devash.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="TEHILLIM 105:5; LUKAS 22:19; TEHILLIM 111:4–5"> And Moshe said, This is the thing which Hashem commandeth, Fill an omer of it to be mishmeret (kept) for your dorot; that they may see the lechem wherewith I have fed you in the midbar, when I brought you forth from Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:4"> And Moshe said unto Aharon, Take a jar, and put an omer full of manna therein, and lay it up before Hashem, to be kept for your dorot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="SHEMOT 27:21; SHEMOT 25:21; SHEMOT 25:16"> As Hashem commanded Moshe, so Aharon laid it up before the Edut (the &#91;Ark&#93; of the Testimony), for mishmeret (to be kept).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 9:20–21; YEHOSHUA 5:12; DEVARIM 8:2–3"> And the Bnei Yisroel did eat manna arba'im shanah, until they came to an eretz noshavet (an inhabited land); they did eat manna, until they came unto the borders of Eretz Kena'an.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:16; SHEMOT 16:32–33"> Now an omer<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., two quarts dry measure">b</a></sup>is the tenth part of an ephah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="T.N. an ephah is about one-half bushel">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
