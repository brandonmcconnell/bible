<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:40; DEVARIM 9:9; SHEMOT 24:18"> And when HaAm saw that Moshe delayed to come down from HaHar, HaAm gathered themselves together unto Aharon, and said unto him, Up, make us elohim (g-ds), which shall go before us; for as for this Moshe, the ish that brought us up out of Eretz Mitzrayim, we know not what is become of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHOFETIM 8:24–27; SHEMOT 12:35–36; YECHEZKEL 16:17"> And Aharon said unto them, Take off the rings of zahav in the ears of your nashim, of your banim, and of your banot, and bring them unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YESHAYAH 46:6; YESHAYAH 40:19–20; YIRMEYAH 10:9"> And kol HaAm removed the rings of zahav which were in their ears, and brought them unto Aharon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 9:18; DEVARIM 9:16; MELACHIM ALEF 12:28"> And he received them at their yad, and fashioned it with a cheret, after he had made it an Egel Masekhah (Molten Calf); and they said, These be thy elohim, O Yisroel, which brought thee up out of Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 10:20; VAYIKRA 23:2; VAYIKRA 23:37"> And when Aharon saw it, he built a Mizbe'ach before it; and Aharon made proclamation, and said, Tomorrow is a chag (feast) to Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:7; BAMIDBAR 25:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:41–42"> And they rose up early the next day, and offered olot, and brought shelamim; and HaAm sat down to eat and to drink, and rose up letzachek (to revel).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 32:11; HOSHEA 9:9; SHOFETIM 2:19"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, Go, get thee down; for thy people, which thou broughtest out of Eretz Mitzrayim, have corrupted themselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:28; SHEMOT 20:23; SHEMOT 20:3–4"> They have turned aside quickly from HaDerech which I commanded them; they have made them an Egel Masekhah, and have bowed down to it, and have sacrificed thereunto, and said, These be thy elohim, O Yisroel, which have brought thee up out of the Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:51; DEVARIM 9:13; YESHAYAH 48:4"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, I have seen this people, and, hinei, it is an Am Kesheh Oref (stiffnecked, obstinate people);</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEVARIM 9:14; BAMIDBAR 14:12; DEVARIM 9:19"> Now therefore let Me alone, that My wrath may burn hot against them, and that I may consume them; I will make of thee a Goy Gadol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEVARIM 9:18–20; DEVARIM 9:26–29; TEHILLIM 106:23"> And Moshe besought Hashem Elohav, and said, Hashem, why doth Thy wrath burn hot against Thy people, which Thou hast brought forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim with ko'ach gadol, and with a yad chazakah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:13–16; DEVARIM 9:28; YEHOSHUA 7:9"> Why should the Egyptians speak, and say, For ra'ah (evil intent) did He bring them out, to slay them in the mountains, and to consume them from the face of ha'adamah? Turn from Thy fierce wrath, and relent of the ra'ah against Thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:13; BERESHIS 12:7; BERESHIS 22:16"> Remember Avraham, Yitzchak, and Yisroel, Thy avadim, to whom Thou swore by Thine own Self, and saidst unto them, I will multiply your zera as the kokhavim of Shomayim, and kol haaretz hazot that I have spoken of will I give unto your zera, and they shall inherit it l'olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YONAH 3:10; TEHILLIM 106:45; YIRMEYAH 26:13"> And Hashem relented of the ra'ah which He thought to do unto His people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEVARIM 9:15; SHEMOT 40:20; DEVARIM 5:22"> And Moshe turned, and went down from HaHar, and the two Luchot HaEdut were in his yad; the Luchot were written on both their sides; on the one side and on the other were they written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 31:18; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:10; DEVARIM 10:1"> And the Luchot were the ma'aseh Elohim, the writing was the Mikhtav Elohim, engraved upon the Luchot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHEMOT 24:13; SHEMOT 17:9; YEHOSHUA 6:20"> And when Yehoshua heard the kol HaAm as they shouted, he said unto Moshe, There is a noise of milchamah in the machaneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 15:1–18; DANIEL 5:4; DANIEL 5:23"> And Moses said, It is not the voice of them that shout for gevurah, neither is it the voice of them that cry of chalushah; but the noise of them that sing that I hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 9:16–17; YIRMEYAH 31:32; EKHAH 5:15"> And it came to pass, as soon as he came nigh unto the machaneh, that he saw the Egel, and the mecholot (dancing); and the anger of Moshe burned hot, and he threw the Luchot out of his hands, and broke them at the foot of HaHar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEVARIM 9:21; MELACHIM BAIS 23:6; MELACHIM BAIS 23:15"> And he took the Egel which they had made, and burned it in the eish, and ground it to powder, and scattered it upon the mayim, and made the Bnei Yisroel drink of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 20:9; YEHOSHUA 7:19–26; MELACHIM ALEF 21:22"> And Moshe said unto Aharon, What did this people unto thee, that thou hast brought so chata'ah gedolah upon them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEVARIM 9:24; SHEMOT 15:24; SHEMOT 16:20"> And Aharon said, Let not the anger of adoni burn hot; thou knowest HaAm, that they are prone to rah (evil).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHEMOT 32:1–4; SHEMOT 32:8"> For they said unto me, Make for us elohim, which shall go before us; for as for this Moshe, the ish that brought us up out of Eretz Mitzrayim, we know not what is become of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHEMOT 32:4; LUKAS 10:29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:10"> And I said unto them, Whosoever hath any zahav, let them remove it. So they gave it me; then I cast it into the eish, and there came out this Egel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YESHAYAH 47:3; MICHOH 1:11; HISGALUS 16:15"> And when Moshe saw that HaAm were exposed; (for Aharon had exposed them to derision among their enemies;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 9:32; MATTITYAHU 12:30; SHMUEL BAIS 20:11"> Then Moshe stood in the sha'ar of the machaneh, and said, Who is on Hashemʼs side? Let him rally unto me. And all the Bnei Levi gathered themselves together unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 25:5; LUKAS 14:26; DEVARIM 33:8–9"> And he said unto them, Thus saith Hashem Elohei Yisroel, Put every man his cherev by his side, and go in and out from sha'ar to sha'ar throughout the machaneh, and slay every man his brother, and every man his companion, and every man his neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEVARIM 33:9; MALACHI 2:4–6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:8"> And the Bnei Levi did according to the word of Moshe; and there fell of HaAm that day about three thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 25:11–13; MATTITYAHU 10:37; ZECHARYAH 13:3"> For Moshe had said, Consecrate yourselves today to Hashem, even every man against his ben, and against his brother; that He may bestow upon you a brocha this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 12:20; BAMIDBAR 25:13; MELACHIM BAIS 17:21"> And it came to pass on the next day, that Moshe said unto HaAm, Ye have sinned a chata'ah gedolah; and now I will go up unto Hashem; perhaps I can make kapporah for your chattat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:23; DANIEL 9:11; DANIEL 9:8"> And Moshe returned unto Hashem, and said, Oh, this people have sinned a chata'ah gedolah, and have made for themselves elohei zahav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="TEHILLIM 69:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:3; HISGALUS 21:27"> Yet now, if Thou wilt forgive their chattat but if not, blot me, now, out of Thy Sefer which Thou hast written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DEVARIM 29:20; YECHEZKEL 18:4; HISGALUS 20:12"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, Whosoever hath sinned against Me, him will I blot out of My Sefer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:20; DEVARIM 32:35; MATTITYAHU 23:35"> Therefore now go, lead HaAm unto the place of which I have spoken unto thee; hinei, Malachi shall go before thee; nevertheless in the yom when I visit I will visit their sin upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:41; SHEMOT 32:25; SHMUEL BAIS 12:9–10"> And Hashem plagued HaAm, because they made the Egel, which Aharon made.</span>
