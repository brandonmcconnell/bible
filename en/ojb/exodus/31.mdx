<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 36:1; SHEMOT 35:30; YOCHANAN 3:27"> See, I have called by shem Betzalel ben Uri, ben Chur, of the tribe of Yehudah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 35:31; YESHAYAH 28:26; MELACHIM ALEF 7:14"> And I have filled him with the Ruach Elohim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Ac 2:4">a</a></sup>in chochmah, and in tevunah, and in da'as, and in kol melachah (all workmanship),</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:1; SHEMOT 25:32–35; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:13–14"> To devise machashavot (artistic designs), to work in zahav, and in kesef, and in nechoshet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:9–21"> And in engraving gemstones, to set them, and in wood-carving, to work in kol melachah (all manner of workmanship).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 35:10; SHEMOT 35:34–1; SHEMOT 28:3"> And I, hinei, I have given with him Oholiav ben Achisamach, of the tribe of Dan; and in the hearts of all chacham lev I have put chochmah, that they may make all that I have commanded thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 36:8–9; SHEMOT 25:10–22; SHEMOT 26:1–37"> The Ohel Mo'ed, and the Aron HaEdut, and the Kapporet that is thereupon, and all the utensils of the Ohel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 37:10–28; SHEMOT 30:1–10; SHEMOT 25:23–40"> And the Shulchan and its utensils, and the Menorah Hatehorah with all its utensils, and the Mizbe'ach HaKetoret,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 38:1–8; SHEMOT 27:1–8; SHEMOT 40:11"> And the Mizbe'ach HaOlah with all its utensils, and the Kiyor and its Stand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 4:5–14; SHEMOT 28:1–43; SHEMOT 39:1–43"> And the Bigdei Haserad (Knit Garments), and the Bigdei HaKodesh for Aharon the kohen, and the garments of his banim, to minister in the kohenʼs office,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 37:29; SHEMOT 30:23–38"> And the Shemen Hamishchah, and Ketoret spices for HaKodesh; according to all that I have commanded thee shall they do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:12; YECHEZKEL 20:20; VAYIKRA 19:30"> Speak thou also unto the Bnei Yisroel, saying, Verily My Shabbatot ye shall be shomer over; for it is an ot (sign) between Me and you throughout your dorot; that ye may have da'as that I am Hashem Who doth set thee apart as kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 35:2–3; BAMIDBAR 15:32–36; YECHEZKEL 20:21"> Ye shall be shomer Shabbos therefore; for it is kodesh unto you; every one that is chillul Shabbos (desecrates Shabbos) shall surely be put to death; for whosoever doeth any work therein, that nefesh shall be cut off from among his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 2:2; SHEMOT 16:23; VAYIKRA 23:3"> Sheshet yamim may work be done; but in the Yom HaShevi'i is Shabbos HaShabbaton, kodesh to Hashem; whosoever doeth any work in the Yom HaShabbos, he shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 50:5; BERESHIS 9:13; BERESHIS 17:11"> Wherefore the Bnei Yisroel shall be shomer Shabbos, to observe Shabbos throughout their dorot, for a brit olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 1:31; SHEMOT 31:13; BERESHIS 2:2–3"> It is an ot between Me and the Bnei Yisroel l'olam; for in sheshet yamim Hashem made Shomayim and HaAretz, and on Yom HaShevi'i He rested (shavat, ceased working), and was refreshed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:13; SHEMOT 32:15–16; DEVARIM 5:22"> And He gave unto Moshe, when He had made an end of speaking with him upon Mt. Sinai, two Luchot HaEdut (Tablets of the Testimony), Luchot Even (Tablets of Stone), written with the Etzba Elohim (Finger of G-d).</span>
