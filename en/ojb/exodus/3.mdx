<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 19:8; SHEMOT 17:6; TEHILLIM 78:70–72"> Now Moshe was ro'eh (shepherding) the tzon of Yitro his khoten (father-in-law), the kohen Midyan; and he led the tzon to the backside of the midbar, and came to the Har HaElohim, even to Chorev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:30–35; YESHAYAH 43:2; MARKOS 12:26"> And the Malach Hashem appeared unto him in a flame of eish out of the midst of a bush; and he looked, and, hinei, the bush burned with eish, and the bush was not consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:31; TEHILLIM 107:8; IYOV 37:14"> And Moshe thought, I will now turn aside, and see this mareh hagadol, why the bush is not burned up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEVARIM 33:16; SHMUEL ALEF 3:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:4"> And when Hashem saw that he turned aside to see, Elohim called out unto him out of the midst of the bush, and said, Moshe, Moshe. And he said, Hineni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 5:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:33; SHEMOT 19:12"> And He said, Draw not nearer here; put off thy na'alayim (sandals) from off thy raglayim, for hamakom whereon thou standest is admat kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKAS 20:37; MATTITYAHU 22:32; SHEMOT 4:5"> Moreover He said, I am Elohei Avicha, Elohei Avraham, Elohei Yitzchak, and Elohei Ya'akov. And Moshe hid his face; for he was afraid to look upon HaElohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 145:19; TEHILLIM 106:44; SHEMOT 2:23–25"> And Hashem said, I have surely seen the oni ami (affliction of My people) which are in Mitzrayim, and have heard their cry by reason of their nogesim (taskmasters); for I know their makhovim (sufferings);</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 50:24; BERESHIS 15:18–21; SHEMOT 3:17"> And I am come down to deliver them out of the yad Mitzrayim, and to bring them up out of that land unto an eretz tovah, a spacious eretz zavat cholov udevash; unto the place of the Kena'ani, and the Chitti, and the Emori, and the Perizzi, and the Chivi, and the Yevusi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MISHLE 22:22–23; SHEMOT 2:23; SHEMOT 1:13–14"> Now therefore, hinei, the cry of the Bnei Yisroel is come unto Me; and I have also seen the oppression wherewith the Egyptians oppress them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MICHOH 6:4; SHMUEL ALEF 12:6; TEHILLIM 105:26"> Come now therefore, and I will send thee unto Pharaoh, that thou mayest bring forth My people the Bnei Yisroel out of Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 18:18; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:5; SHEMOT 6:12"> And Moshe said unto HaElohim, Who am I, that I should go unto Pharaoh, and that I should bring forth the Bnei Yisroel out of Mitzrayim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 1:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:31; YESHAYAH 41:10"> And He said, Certainly Eh-heh-yeh (I will be)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Ex 3:14below">a</a></sup>with thee; and this shall be haOt (the Sign) unto thee, that I have sent thee: when thou hast brought forth HaAm out of Mitzrayim, ye shall serve HaElohim upon this Har.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MISHLE 30:4; YESHAYAH 9:6; YIRMEYAH 23:6"> And Moshe said unto HaElohim, Hinei, when I come unto the Bnei Yisroel, and shall say unto them, Elohei Avoteichem hath sent me unto you; and they shall say to me, What is Shmo? what shall I say unto them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:58; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:8; HISGALUS 1:8"> And Elohim said unto Moshe, Eh-heh-yeh ashair Eh-heh-yeh (I AM WHO I AM); and He said, Thus shalt thou say unto the Bnei Yisroel, EH-HEH-YEH (I AM) hath sent me unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:7–8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:8; MALACHI 3:6"> And Elohim said moreover unto Moshe, Thus shalt thou say unto Bnei Yisroel: Hashem, Elohei Avoteichem, Elohei Avraham, Elohei Yitzchak, and Elohei Ya'akov, hath sent me unto you: this is Shemi l'olam, and this is My remembrance unto all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="TEHILLIM 8:4; SHEMOT 4:31; SHEMOT 4:29"> Go, and gather the Ziknei Yisroel together, and say unto them, Hashem Elohei Avoteichem, Elohei Avraham, Yitzchak, and Ya'akov, appeared unto me, saying, I have surely visited/inspected you, and seen that which is done to you in Mitzrayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:13–21; BERESHIS 50:24; BERESHIS 46:4"> And I have said, I will bring you up out of the Oni Mitzrayim unto the eretz Kena'ani, and the Chitti, and the Emori, and the Perizzi, and the Chivi, and the Yevusi unto eretz zavat cholov udevash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 9:1; SHEMOT 7:16; SHEMOT 10:24–26"> And they shall pay heed to thy voice; and thou shalt go, thou and the Ziknei Yisroel, unto Melech Mitzrayim, and ye shall say unto him, Hashem Elohei HaIvriyim hath met with us; and let us go, now, derech shloshet yamim into the midbar, that we may sacrifice to Hashem Eloheinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 5:2; SHEMOT 6:1; SHEMOT 7:1–14"> And I have da'as that Melech Mitzrayim will not let you go, no, except by a yad chazakah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:36; NECHEMYAH 9:10; SHEMOT 9:15"> And I will stretch out My yad, and strike Mitzrayim with all My nifle'ot which I will do in the midst thereof; and after that he will let you go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:36; TEHILLIM 105:37; SHEMOT 11:3"> And I will give this people chen in the sight of the Egyptians; and it shall come to pass, that, when ye go, ye shall not go empty-handed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 39:10; SHEMOT 11:2; SHEMOT 12:35–36"> But every isha shall borrow of her neighbor, and of her that sojourneth in her bais, jewels of kesef, and jewels of zahav, and clothing; and ye shall put them upon your banim, and upon your banot; so ye shall plunder the Egyptians.</span>
