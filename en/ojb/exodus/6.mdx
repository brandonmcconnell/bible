## &#91;VAEIRA&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 9:24; MALACHI 3:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:24–25"> And Elohim spoke unto Moshe, and said unto him, I am Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TEHILLIM 83:18; TEHILLIM 68:4; BERESHIS 17:1"> And I appeared unto Avraham, unto Yitzchak, and unto Ya'akov, as El Shaddai, but by My Shem Hashem I did not make Myself known to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:4; BERESHIS 15:18; BERESHIS 17:7–8"> And I have also established My brit (covenant) with them, to give them Eretz Kena'an, the land of their sojourning, wherein they sojourned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 2:24; TEHILLIM 106:44–45; YESHAYAH 63:9"> And I have also heard the groaning of the Bnei Yisroel, whom the Egyptians keep in bondage; and I have remembered My brit (covenant).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEVARIM 26:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:21; SHEMOT 7:4"> Therefore say unto the Bnei Yisroel, I am Hashem, and I will bring you out from under the sivlot Mitzrayim, and I will free you out of their bondage, and I will redeem you with a stretched out zero'a, and with mishpatim gedolim:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:6; DEVARIM 29:13; DEVARIM 4:20"> And I will take you to Me for a people, and I will be to you Elohim; and ye shall know that I am Hashem Eloheichem, which bringeth you out from under the sivlot Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 26:3; BERESHIS 15:18; YECHEZKEL 20:42"> And I will bring you in unto HaAretz, concerning the which I did lift up My hand to swear to give it to Avraham, to Yitzchak, and to Ya'akov; and I will give it to you for a morashah (heritage); I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 5:21; IYOV 21:4; SHEMOT 14:12"> And Moshe spoke so unto the Bnei Yisroel; but they did not listen unto Moshe because of their kotzer ruach (shortness of spirit, broken spirit), and for avodah kesheh (cruel bondage).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 6:29; SHEMOT 5:23; SHEMOT 3:10"> Go in, speak unto Pharaoh Melech Mitzrayim, that he let the Bnei Yisroel go out of his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:10; YIRMEYAH 1:6; SHEMOT 6:30"> And Moshe spoke before Hashem, saying, See, the Bnei Yisroel have not paid heed unto me; how then shall Pharaoh hear me, who am of oral sefatayim (uncircumcised lips, stumbling speech, sealed lips)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 27:23; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:13; TEHILLIM 91:11"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe and unto Aharon, and gave them a charge unto the Bnei Yisroel, and unto Pharaoh Melech Mitzrayim, to bring the Bnei Yisroel out of Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:3; BAMIDBAR 26:5–7"> These are the Rashei Bais Avotam: The Bnei Reuven the bechor Yisroel; Chanokh, and Pallu, Chetzron, and Carmi: these are the mishpokhot of Reuven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 4:24; BERESHIS 46:10; BAMIDBAR 26:12–13"> And the Bnei Shim'on; Yemuel, and Yamin, and Ohad, and Yachin, and Tzochar, and Sha'ul ben of HaKena'anit: these are the mishpokhot of Shim'on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:17; BERESHIS 46:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:1"> And these are the shemot Bnei Levi according to their toldot; Gershon, and Kohat, and Merari: and the years of the life of Levi were a hundred thirty and seven shanah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:17; BAMIDBAR 3:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:7"> The Bnei Gershon; Livni, and Shime'i, according to their mishpokhot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:2; BAMIDBAR 3:19"> And the Bnei Kohat; Amram, and Yitzhar, and Chevron, and Uzziel: and the years of the life of Kehat were a hundred thirty and three shanah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:19"> And the Bnei Merari; Machli and Mushi: these are the mishpokhot of Levi according to their toledot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:59; SHEMOT 2:1–2; SHEMOT 6:18"> And Amram took him Yocheved his fatherʼs sister to wife; and she bore him Aharon and Moshe: and the years of the life of Amram were a hundred and thirty and seven shanah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 16:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:37–38; BAMIDBAR 16:32"> And the Bnei Yitzhar; Korach, Nepheg, and Zichri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="VAYIKRA 10:4; BAMIDBAR 3:30; NECHEMYAH 3:20"> And the Bnei Uzziel; Mishael, Eltzaphan, and Sitri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="RUTH 4:19–20; BAMIDBAR 2:3; BAMIDBAR 1:7"> And Aharon took him Elisheva, bat Amminadav, achot Nachshon, to wife; and she bore him Nadav, and Avihu, El'azar and Itamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:22–23; BAMIDBAR 16:1; SHMUEL ALEF 1:1"> And the Bnei Korach; Assir, and Elkanah, and Aviasaph: these are the mishpokhot of the Korchi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 24:33; BAMIDBAR 25:7–13; YEHOSHUA 22:31–32"> And El'azar ben Aharon took him one of the banot Putiel to wife; and she bore him Pinchas: these are the Rashei Avot HaLevi'im according to their mishpokhot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:51; SHEMOT 12:17; SHEMOT 7:4"> These are that Aharon and Moshe, to whom Hashem said, Bring out the Bnei Yisroel from Eretz Mitzrayim according to their tzve'os.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHEMOT 6:26; SHEMOT 7:10; TEHILLIM 77:20"> These are they which spoke to Pharaoh Melech Mitzrayim, to bring out the Bnei Yisroel from Mitzrayim: these are that Moshe and Aharon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And it came to pass on the day when Hashem spoke unto Moshe in Eretz Mitzrayim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHEMOT 6:11; SHEMOT 7:2; SHEMOT 6:2"> That Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying, I am Hashem: speak thou unto Pharaoh Melech Mitzrayim all that I say unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="SHEMOT 6:12; SHEMOT 4:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:16–17"> And Moshe said before Hashem, See, I am of oral sefatayim, and why should Pharaoh pay heed unto me.</span>
