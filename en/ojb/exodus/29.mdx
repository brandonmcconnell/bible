<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:3; MALACHI 1:13–14; SHEMOT 12:5"> And this is the thing that thou shalt do unto them to set them apart as kodesh, to minister unto Me in the kohenʼs office: Take one bull calf, and two rams temimim (without blemish),</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 6:15; VAYIKRA 2:15; VAYIKRA 7:12"> And lechem matzot, and challot matzot mixed with shemen, and wafers matzot smeared with shemen; of fine wheat flour shalt thou make them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 6:17; VAYIKRA 8:26; VAYIKRA 8:2"> Thou shalt put them into sal echad (one basket); bring them near in the sal, with the bull and the two rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 40:12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:22; SHEMOT 26:36"> And Aharon and his banim thou shalt bring unto the petach of the Ohel Mo'ed, and shalt immerse them with mayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:2–8; VAYIKRA 8:7–8"> And thou shalt take the begadim, and put upon Aharon the kesones,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Yn 19:23; Ps 110:4">a</a></sup>and the Me'il HaEphod, and the Ephod, and the Choshen, and gird him with the Cheishev HaEphod;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="VAYIKRA 8:9; SHEMOT 28:36–39"> And thou shalt put the Mitznefet upon his head, and put the Nezer HaKodesh upon the Mitznefet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 35:25; VAYIKRA 21:10; YOCHANAN 3:34"> Then shalt thou take the Shemen HaMishchah, and pour it upon his head, and anoint him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="VAYIKRA 8:13; SHEMOT 28:40"> And thou shalt bring near his banim, and put kuttanot upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 18:7; VAYIKRA 8:22–28; DEVARIM 18:5"> And thou shalt gird them with Avnet, Aharon and his banim, and put the migba'ot on them; and the Kehunnah shall be theirs for a chukkat olam; and thou shalt ordain<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e. fill their hands (invest them with authority as ministers)">b</a></sup>Aharon and his banim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="VAYIKRA 1:4; VAYIKRA 8:14; SHEMOT 29:15"> And thou shalt cause a bull to be brought before the Ohel Mo'ed; Aharon and his banim shall put their hands upon the head of the bull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="VAYIKRA 9:8; VAYIKRA 9:12; SHEMOT 29:4"> And thou shalt slaughter (shachat) the bull before Hashem, by the petach of the Ohel Mo'ed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHEMOT 27:2; VAYIKRA 9:9; VAYIKRA 8:15"> And thou shalt take of the dahm of the bull, and put it upon the karnot of the Mizbe'ach with thy finger, and pour all the dahm on the yesod (base) of the Mizbe'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="VAYIKRA 3:3–4; SHMUEL ALEF 2:16; VAYIKRA 9:10"> And thou shalt take all the chelev that covereth the innards, and the diaphragm with the liver, and the two kidneys, and the chelev that is upon them, and burn them upon the Mizbe'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:11–12; VAYIKRA 4:21; SHEMOT 30:10"> But the basar of the bull, and his hide, and his dung, shalt thou burn with eish outside the machaneh; it is a chattat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:19; SHEMOT 29:3; VAYIKRA 8:18–21"> Thou shalt also take one ram; and Aharon and his banim shall put their hands upon the head of the ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:11–12"> And thou shalt slaughter the ram, and thou shalt take his dahm, and sprinkle it around upon the Mizbe'ach.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Isa 52:15on Moshiachʼs sprinkling of the Goyim">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 4:14; VAYIKRA 1:13; VAYIKRA 1:9"> And thou shalt cut the ayil (ram) into pieces, and wash the innards of him, and his legs, and put them with his pieces, and his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BERESHIS 8:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:18; BERESHIS 22:7"> And thou shalt burn the whole ram upon the Mizbe'ach: it is an olah unto Hashem: it is a re'ach hannichoach,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Pp 4:18">d</a></sup>an offering made by eish unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:3; VAYIKRA 8:22–29"> And thou shalt take the other ayil; and Aharon and his banim shall lay their hands upon the head of the ayil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YESHAYAH 50:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:22; VAYIKRA 14:16"> Then shalt thou slaughter (shachat) the ayil, and take of his dahm, and put it upon the lobe of the right ear of Aharon, and upon the lobe of the right ear of his banim, and upon the thumb of their right yad, and upon the bohen of their right foot, and sprinkle the dahm upon the Mizbe'ach, all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:22; SHEMOT 29:7; SHEMOT 29:1"> And thou shalt take of the dahm that is upon the Mizbe'ach, and of the shemen hamishchah, and sprinkle it upon Aharon, upon his garments, upon his banim, upon the garments of his banim with him; he shall be set apart as kodesh, and his garments, his banim, his banimʼs garments with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:13; VAYIKRA 9:21; VAYIKRA 9:19"> Also thou shalt take of the ram the chelev and the tail, and the fat that covereth the innards, and the diaphragm with the liver, and the two kidneys, and the chelev that is on them, and the right thigh; for it is an ayil millu'im (ram of ordination);</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:2–3; VAYIKRA 8:26"> And one kekar lechem (loaf of bread), and one challah of lechem shemen (oily loaf of bread), and one wafer out of the basket of the matzot that is before Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="VAYIKRA 7:30; VAYIKRA 8:27; VAYIKRA 9:21"> And thou shalt put all in the hands of Aharon, and in the hands of his banim; and shalt wave them for a tenufah before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="VAYIKRA 8:28; SHEMOT 29:41; VAYIKRA 3:16"> And thou shalt take them from their hands, and burn them upon the Mizbe'ach for an olah, for a re'ach hannichoach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Pp 4:18">e</a></sup>before Hashem: it is an offering made by eish unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="VAYIKRA 8:29; VAYIKRA 7:31; VAYIKRA 7:34"> And thou shalt take the breast of the ayil hamillu'im (ram of ordination) for Aharon, and wave it for a tenufah before Hashem; and it shall be thy portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="VAYIKRA 10:15; BAMIDBAR 18:11; DEVARIM 18:3"> And thou shalt set apart as kodesh the breast of the tenufah, and the thigh of the terumah, which is waved, and which is raised up, of the ayil hamillu'im, even of that which is for Aharon, and of that which is for his banim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 15:19–20; VAYIKRA 7:11–38; VAYIKRA 10:14–15"> And it shall be Aharonʼs and his banimʼs by a chok olam from the Bnei Yisroel; for it is a terumah; and it shall be a terumah from the Bnei Yisroel of the zivkhei shelemim of them, even their terumah unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 18:8; SHEMOT 28:3–4; SHEMOT 30:30"> And the Bigdei Hakodesh of Aharon shall be his banimʼs after him, to be anointed therein, and to be ordained in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 20:28; VAYIKRA 13:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:6–7"> And that ben that is kohen in his succession shall put them on shivat yamim, when he cometh into the Ohel Mo'ed to minister in HaKodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="VAYIKRA 8:31; SHMUEL ALEF 2:13; YECHEZKEL 46:20–24"> And thou shalt take the ayil hamillu'im, and cook his basar in the Makom Kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:4; SHEMOT 29:23; SHEMOT 29:2–3"> And Aharon and his banim shall eat the basar of the ayil, and the lechem that is in the sal petach Ohel Mo'ed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 18:4; VAYIKRA 10:13–18; TEHILLIM 22:26"> And they shall eat those things wherewith the kapporah was made, to ordain and to set them apart as kodesh; but a zar shall not eat thereof, because they are kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:10; VAYIKRA 8:32; SHEMOT 29:22"> And if anything remains of the basar of the millu'im, or of the lechem, unto the boker, then thou shalt burn the remainder with eish; it shall not be eaten, because it is kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:30; VAYIKRA 14:8–11; VAYIKRA 8:4–36"> And thus shalt thou do unto Aharon, and to his banim, according to all things which I have commanded thee; shivat yamim shalt thou ordain them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:22–23; SHEMOT 30:28–29"> And thou shalt offer every day the bull of the chattat for kippurim (blood atonements); and thou shalt purify the Mizbe'ach, when thou hast made a kapporah for it, and thou shalt anoint it, to set it apart as kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="SHEMOT 40:10; MATTITYAHU 23:19; SHEMOT 30:28–29"> Shivat yamim thou shalt make a kapporah for the Mizbe'ach, and set it apart as kodesh; and it shall be a Mizbe'ach kodesh kodashim; whatsoever toucheth the Mizbe'ach shall be kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 16:40; BAMIDBAR 28:3–8"> Now this is that which thou shalt offer upon the Mizbe'ach: two kevasim (lambs) of the first year each day tamid (continually).</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 46:13–15; MELACHIM BAIS 16:15; TEHILLIM 5:3"> The one lamb thou shalt offer in the boker; and the other lamb thou shalt offer in the afternoon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:17; YECHEZKEL 46:14; YOEL 2:14"> And with the one lamb a tenth ephah of fine flour mixed with the fourth part of a hin of pressed shemen; and the fourth part of a hin of yayin for a nesekh (drink offering).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Pp 2:17">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 46:13–15; MELACHIM ALEF 18:29; TEHILLIM 141:2"> And the other lamb thou shalt offer in the afternoon, and shalt do thereto according to the minchah of the boker, and according to the nesekh thereof, for a re'ach hannichoach,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See Pp 4:18">g</a></sup>an offering made by eish unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:22"> This shall be an olat tamid throughout your dorot at the petach of the Ohel Mo'ed before Hashem; where I will meet you, to speak there unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:1–3; MALACHI 3:1"> And there I will meet by appointment with the Bnei Yisroel, and it shall be set apart as kodesh by My Kavod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="VAYIKRA 21:15; VAYIKRA 22:9; VAYIKRA 22:16"> And I will set apart as kodesh the Ohel Mo'ed, and the Mizbe'ach; and I will set apart as kodesh also both Aharon and his banim, to minister to Me in the kohenʼs office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:16; SHEMOT 25:8"> And I will dwell among the Bnei Yisroel, and will be their Elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:2; YIRMEYAH 31:33; YECHEZKEL 20:5"> And they shall have da'as that I am Hashem Eloheihem, Who brought them forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim, to rest My<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Shekhinah">h</a></sup>dwelling among them; I am Hashem Eloheihem.</span>
