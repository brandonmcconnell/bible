<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 9:27; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:9; LUKAS 22:30"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying to them, Omein, I say to you, there are some of those standing here who shall not taste mavet (death) until they see that the Malchut Hashem has come in gevurah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKAS 9:28–36; YESHAYAH 33:17; MATTITYAHU 17:1–8"> And after shishah yamim Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach takes Kefa and Ya'akov and Yochanan, and brought them up a high mountain by themselves. And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was transfigured before them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:3; DANIEL 7:9; HISGALUS 7:14"> And his garments became like a dazzlingly brilliant kittel, gantz (exceedingly) white, more white than anyone on earth could bleach them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKAS 9:30–31; MATTITYAHU 17:3–4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:21–24"> And Eliyahu Hanavi appeared to them along with Moshe (Rabbeinu), and they were talking with Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:23; YOCHANAN 14:8–9; HISGALUS 22:3–4"> And in reply, to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Kefa says: Rebbe, it is tov &#91;for&#93; us to be here, and let us make shalosh sukkot, one for you and one for Moshe (Rabbeinu) and one for Eliyahu Hanavi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:17; DANIEL 10:15–19; MARKOS 16:5–8"> Kefa did not know what he was answering, for they were tzufloigen (dazed) with pachad (terror).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:17; MATTITYAHU 3:17; MARKOS 1:11"> And there came an anan (cloud) that was over-shadowing them, and there came a bat kol out of the anan, ZEH BENI AHUVI, ELAV TISHMAUN (This is my beloved Son; listen to him!)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ps 2:7; Ex 24:16">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:39–40; LUKAS 24:31"> And suddenly, when they looked around, they saw no one in their company any more, except only Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:40; MATTITYAHU 17:9–13; MARKOS 5:43"> And &#91;as&#93; they were coming down from the mountain, he gave instructions to them that they should tell no one what they saw, except when the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">b</a></sup>should stand up in his Techiyas HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:18; LUKAS 24:7–8; LUKAS 18:33–34"> And they retained Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiachʼs dvar, discussing among themselves what he might mean by the Techiyas HaMoshiach from HaMesim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MALACHI 4:5; MALACHI 3:1; MATTITYAHU 11:14"> And they were questioning him, saying, Why is it that the Sofrim (Scribes) say it is necessary for Eliyahu Hanavi to come rishonah (first)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:1–12; YESHAYAH 50:6; YESHAYAH 52:14"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Eliyahu Hanavi indeed does come rishonah and brings tikkun (restoration) to all things. And yet how is it written of the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Moshiach">c</a></sup>that he must suffer many things and be NIVZEH VACHADAL<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 53:3">d</a></sup>(despised and rejected)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 14:3–11; MATTITYAHU 17:12–13; MATTITYAHU 11:14"> But I say to you, that Eliyahu Hanavi has indeed come, and they did to him whatever they pleased, just as it has been written concerning him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1Kgs 19:2; 10; Mal 3:23">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 9:37–42; MATTITYAHU 17:14–20; LUKAS 11:53–54"> And when they returned to the talmidim, they saw a great multitude around them and &#91;the&#93; Sofrim (Scribes) arguing with Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiachʼs talmidim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:30; MARKOS 9:2–3"> And ofen ort, as soon as the multitude saw Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, they were surprised and began running up to &#91;him&#93; with Birkat Shalom greetings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 5:30–32; MARKOS 8:11"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach questioned them, About what are you arguing with them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MARKOS 9:25; LUKAS 11:14; YOCHANAN 4:47"> And one of the multitude answered Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach: Rabbi, I brought beni (my son) to you, because he has a shed that makes him illem (mute).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 17:19–20; MARKOS 11:23; MATTITYAHU 17:16"> And whenever it seizes him, it throws him into convulsions and he foams at the mouth, and grinds his teeth, and becomes rigid. And I told your talmidim in order that they might cast it out, and they did not have the koach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:11; YOCHANAN 20:27; BAMIDBAR 14:27"> In reply to them, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says, O dor without emunah, how long will I be with you? How long will I put up with you? Bring the bocher to me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MARKOS 1:26; LUKAS 4:35; YOCHANAN 8:44"> They brought the bocher to him. And when the shed saw Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, it threw the bocher into convulsions, and, falling to the ground, the bocher began rolling around foaming at the mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:33; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:22; MARKOS 5:25"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach questioned the bocherʼs abba, How long has this been happening to him? And he said, From kinder-yoren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:22–28; MARKOS 5:19; MATTITYAHU 20:34"> And also it has often thrown him into the eish and into the mayim that it might destroy him. But if you can do anything, grant rachmei shomayim upon us and help us!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:40; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:6; MARKOS 11:23"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to him, If you can?!! All things are possible to him who has emunah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKAS 17:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:8"> Ofen ort (immediately) the abba of the bocher cried out and began saying, Ani maamin (I believe)! Help my lack of emunah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:18; LUKAS 9:42; MARKOS 9:15"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, having seen that a multitude was gathering, rebuked the ruach hatumah (unclean spirit), saying to it, You cheresh-illem (deaf and mute) ruach hatemeiah, I command you, come out from him and do not ever enter into him again!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MARKOS 9:20; MARKOS 1:26; MARKOS 9:18"> And after shrieking a shrai (scream) and throwing the bocher into violent seizures, the ruach hatemeiah came out. And the bocher was so like a geviyah (corpse) that many exclaimed he was niftar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MARKOS 1:31; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:7; MARKOS 1:41"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, having grasped the hand of the bocher, lifted him up; and the bocher stood up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 17:19–20; MARKOS 7:17; MATTITYAHU 15:15"> And, having entered into a bais, his talmidim began questioning Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach in a yechidus: Why were we not able to perform the gerush shedim (exorcism)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:15; DANIEL 9:3; MATTITYAHU 17:20"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, This kind of gerush shedim is accomplished only by davening with tzomot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 17:22–23; LUKAS 9:43–45; MARKOS 6:31–32"> And from there they went forth and were passing through the Galil, and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach did not want anyone to have daas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:21; MARKOS 9:12; LUKAS 24:44–46"> For Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was teaching his talmidim and was saying to them, The Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">f</a></sup>is to be betrayed into the hands of Bnei Adam, and MOSHIACH YIKARET<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Dan 9:26">g</a></sup>(Moshiach will be violently killed), and when he has been killed and has his histalkus (passing), he will rise in his Techiyas HaMoshiach from HaMesim on Yom HaShelishi (the Third Day).</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LUKAS 2:50; LUKAS 9:45; LUKAS 18:34"> But they did not have binah concerning this dvar, and they were afraid to inquire further.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:1–5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:13; LUKAS 9:46–48"> And they came to K'far-Nachum. And when Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was in the bais, he began questioning them, What were you arguing about baderech (on the road)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MARKOS 9:50; MATTITYAHU 20:21–24; MATTITYAHU 18:1–5"> But they were keeping silent, for baderech they were discussing with one another which of the talmidim was hagadol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:25–28; MARKOS 10:42–45; LUKAS 22:26"> And sitting down, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach called the Shneym Asar and says to them, If anyone wishes to be Rishon (First), he shall be Acharon (Last), and mesharet (minister, servant) of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MARKOS 10:16; MATTITYAHU 19:14–15; MATTITYAHU 18:2"> And having taken a yeled, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach set him in &#91;the&#93; center of them, and, having taken the yeled into his arms, he said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:3–5; LUKAS 10:16; MATTITYAHU 10:40–42"> Whoever receives one yeled such as this biShmi (in my Name) receives me, and whoever receives me, receives not only me but the One who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LUKAS 9:49–50; BAMIDBAR 11:26–29; LUKAS 11:19"> Yochanan said to him, Rebbe, we saw someone performing a gerush shedim in your Name, and we were trying to stop him, because he was not one of your chasidim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Num 11:27-29">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:1–2; MATTITYAHU 13:28–29"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Do not stop him, for no one who will accomplish niflaot (wonders) biShmi (in my Name) will be able soon afterward to speak lashon hora about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:30; LUKAS 11:23"> For the one who is not our mitnagged is for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:42; MATTITYAHU 25:40; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:23"> For whoever gives you to drink a kos of mayim (cup of water) biShmi (in my Name) because you are talmidim of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, omein, I say to you, he shall not lose his sachar (reward).</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:6; LUKAS 17:1–2; MATTITYAHU 18:10"> And whoever causes a michshol (stumbling block) for one of these little ones, the maaminim b'Moshiach who have emunah in me, it would be better for him if a heavy millstone, one turned by a donkey, were hung around his neck and he were cast into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:29–30; MATTITYAHU 18:8–9; MATTITYAHU 25:41"> And if your yad causes you a michshol, cut off your yad; for it is better for you if you enter Chayyei Olam as an amputee, than, having both yadayim, you go away into Gehinnom, into the EISH LO TIKHBEH (fire not &#91;ever&#93; extinguished),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Verses 43, 44, 46 and 48, see Isa 66:24">i</a></sup>&#91;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Where their TOLAAT LO TAMUT (worm does not die) and EISH LO TIKHBEH (the fire not &#91;ever&#93; extinguished).&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:8; MATTITYAHU 5:22; MARKOS 9:43"> And if your regel (foot) causes you a michshol, cut off your regel; it is better for you to enter Chayyei Olam lame, than, having both raglayim, to be cast into Gehinnom, &#91;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> Where their TOLAAT LO TAMUT (worm does not die) and EISH LO TIKHBEH (the fire not &#91;ever&#93; extinguished).&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:9; MARKOS 9:43; MATTITYAHU 5:22"> And if your ayin (eye) causes you a michshol, take it out; for it is better for you to enter the Malchut Hashem one-eyed, than, having both eynayim, to be cast into Gehinnom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="YESHAYAH 66:24; MARKOS 9:43–45; MATTITYAHU 25:41"> Where their TOLAAT LO TAMUT (worm does not die) and EISH LO TIKHBEH (the fire not &#91;ever&#93; extinguished).</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="VAYIKRA 2:13; YECHEZKEL 43:24"> For everyone will have eish as his melach (salt).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Lv 2:13">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:18; MATTITYAHU 5:13"> Melach is tov, but if the melach becomes unsalty, by what &#91;means&#93; will you make it salty again? Have melach in yourselves. And among yourselves let there be shalom.</span>
