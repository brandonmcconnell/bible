<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:11–12; MATTITYAHU 4:3; MATTITYAHU 3:17"> The Beginning of the Besuras HaGeulah (the Good News of Redemption) of Yehoshua, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach &#91;the&#93; Ben HaElohim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MALACHI 3:1; MATTITYAHU 11:10; LUKAS 1:76"> Just as it has been written in Yeshayah HaNavi, HINNENI SHOLEIACH MALACHI (Behold, I send my messenger) before your face, UPINNAH DERECH LEFANAI (and he will prepare the way before me),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ex 23:20; Mal 3:1">a</a></sup>he will prepare your way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:3–5; MATTITYAHU 3:3; LUKAS 3:4–6"> KOL KOREY BAMIDBAR<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="TARGUM HASHIVIM Isa 40:3">b</a></sup>(A voice of one shouting in the wilderness): Prepare the Derech Hashem (the way of the L-rd). Make his paths yashar (straight)!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:3–4; MATTITYAHU 3:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:24–25"> Yochanan came with a mikveh mayim in the midbar, preaching a tevilah of teshuva for the selichat avon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:40–42; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:18; MATTITYAHU 3:5–6"> And all Yehudah and all Yerushalayim were going out to him, and they were submitted to a tevilah using the Yarden River as a mikveh mayim, making vidduy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="to Hashem">c</a></sup>of their averos (sins).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 1:8; VAYIKRA 11:22; MATTITYAHU 3:4"> And Yochanan had camel hair clothing and a leather belt around his waist, and his food was arbe and devash.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2Kgs 1:8; Lv 11:22">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:25; LUKAS 3:16; YOCHANAN 1:27"> And he was preaching, &#91;crying out as a maggid&#93;,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="for the Moshiach">e</a></sup>saying, Hu Habah (He who Comes)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="i.e. Moshiach">f</a></sup>after me has more chozek (strength) than me. I am not worthy to stoop down and remove his sandals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:4–6"> I give you a tevilah in a mikveh mayim, but he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Moshiach">g</a></sup>will give you a tevilah in the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 3:13–17; MATTITYAHU 2:23; LUKAS 3:21–22"> And it was during that period that Yehoshua from Natzeret of the Galil came and submitted to Yochananʼs tevilah in the mikveh mayim of the Yarden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:31–34; LUKAS 3:22; YESHAYAH 42:1"> And ofen ort Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach came up out of the mikveh mayim, and he saw Shomayim being torn open, the Ruach Hakodesh of Hashem descending like a yonah on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 42:1; MARKOS 9:7; MATTITYAHU 3:17"> And there was a bat kol out of Shomayim, ATAH BNI AHUVI ASHER BCHA CHAFATSTI (You are my Son, the beloved, with whom I am well pleased).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:1–11; LUKAS 4:1–13"> Then the Ruach Hakodesh ofen ort thrusts him out into the midbar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:17–18; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:15; SHEMOT 34:28"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was in the midbar arba'im yom varbaim lailah, undergoing nisyonos by HaSatan; and he was with the chayyat hassadeh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Job 5:23">h</a></sup>(wild beasts), and the malachim were the meshartei Hashem ministering to Moshiach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:23; MATTITYAHU 4:12; LUKAS 8:1"> Now after Yochanan was arrested, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach came to the Galil, darshenen (preaching) the Besuras HaGeulah of Hashem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 3:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:36–38; MATTITYAHU 4:17"> And saying, The appointed time has been fulfilled and the Malchut Hashem has come near. Make teshuva, and have emunah in the Besuras HaGeulah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:18–22; YOCHANAN 1:40–42; YOCHANAN 6:8"> And passing along beside Sea of Galilee, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach saw Shim'on and Andrew, the brother of Shim'on, casting a reshet into the sea, for they were daiyagim (fishermen).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKAS 5:10; MATTITYAHU 4:19–20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:38–41"> And he said to them, Come, follow me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="as my talmidim">i</a></sup>and I will make you to become daiyagei adam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKAS 18:28–30; LUKAS 5:11; MARKOS 10:28–31"> And ofen ort, leaving their nets, they followed Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARKOS 5:37; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:13; MARKOS 10:35"> And going on a little, he saw Ya'akov Ben Zavdai and Yochanan his brother, who were also in the sirah (boat) repairing the nets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:37; LUKAS 14:26; MARKOS 10:29"> And ofen ort he summoned them; and, leaving Zavdai their father in the sirah with the sachirim (hired workers), they went away to follow Moshiach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MARKOS 6:2; MARKOS 1:39; MATTITYAHU 4:23"> And they enter into K'far-Nachum, and ofen ort on Shabbos, entering into the shul, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach exercised the ministry of moreh (teacher).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:28–29; MATTITYAHU 13:54; LUKAS 4:32"> And they were astounded at Moshiachʼs torah (teaching), for he was teaching them as one having samchut, and not as the Sofrim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MARKOS 9:25; MARKOS 7:25; MARKOS 1:34"> And, ofen ort, there was in their shul a man with a ruach hatameh, and he shouted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 8:29; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:14"> Saying, Yehoshua of Natzeret, mah lanu vlach? (What to us and to you?) Have you come to bring us churban (destruction)? I have daas<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="of the raz, secret, mystery of">j</a></sup>who you are, HaKadosh of Hashem (the Holy One of G-d).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MARKOS 1:34; LUKAS 4:41; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:17"> But he rebuked the shed, saying, Shekit! Gey zich! And come out of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MARKOS 9:26; MARKOS 9:20; LUKAS 11:22"> And, the ruach hatumah, throwing him into convulsions, let out a shrai and came out of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:31; MARKOS 7:37; LUKAS 9:1"> And all were astounded so that they began to discuss with each other, saying, What is this? Torah Chadasha with samchut? He gives orders to the shedim and they obey him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:31; LUKAS 4:37; MARKOS 1:45"> And the besuroh (news) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach ofen ort (immediately) went forth everywhere into the surrounding region of the Galil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LUKAS 4:38–39; MATTITYAHU 8:14–15; MARKOS 1:21"> And ofen ort (immediately), coming out of shul, they went into the bais (house) of Shim'on and Andrew with Ya'akov and Yochanan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:5; MARKOS 5:23; YOCHANAN 11:3"> And the chamot (shviger, mother-in-law) of Shim'on was bedridden and fever-stricken, and ofen ort (immediately) they tell Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach about her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:41; TEHILLIM 116:12; MARKOS 5:41"> And approaching, holding her hand, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach lifted her up, and the kaddachat<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Dt 28:22">k</a></sup>(fever) left her, and she then began functioning as their mesharetet (servant, keli kodesh, lady minister), waiting on them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 8:16; MARKOS 3:2; LUKAS 4:40"> When erev (evening) came, with the setting of the shemesh (sun), they brought to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach all the cholim (sick persons) and all those possessed by shedim (demons).</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MARKOS 1:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:44"> And the whole shtetl gathered together at the delet (door).</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LUKAS 4:41; MARKOS 3:12; MATTITYAHU 4:23"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach administered refuah (healing) to many cholim (sick persons) suffering from various machlot (illnessess), and he cast out many shedim (demons). And he did not permit the shedim to give utterance, for the shedim knew the raz (mystery, secret) of his identity, that he was the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LUKAS 6:12; LUKAS 5:16; LUKAS 22:39–46"> And very early, while it was still dark, having got up, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach went out and away to a desolate place, and was davening shacharis there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And Shim'on and those with him searched for Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:19; MARKOS 1:5; ZECHARYAH 11:11"> And they found Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and they say to him, Everyone is seeking you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Song 1:4; Hag 2:7">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LUKAS 4:43; YOCHANAN 17:8; YESHAYAH 61:1–3"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, Let us go elsewhere into the neighboring shtetlach, so that also there I may preach. For it was for this &#91;purpose, tachlis&#93; I came.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Isa 61:1">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:23; MARKOS 1:21; LUKAS 4:43–44"> And he came preaching in their shuls (synagogues) and casting out shedim in &#91;the&#93; whole &#91;region of&#93; the Galil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LUKAS 5:12–14; MATTITYAHU 8:2–4; VAYIKRA 13:1–14"> And an ish metzorah (leper) comes to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach begging him and kneeling down and saying, If you are willing, you are able to make me tahor (clean).</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:36; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:15"> And being filled with rachmei Shomayim &#91;and&#93; stretching out his hand, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach touched &#91;the man&#93; and says to him, I am willing. Be made tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:3; MARKOS 5:29; YOCHANAN 4:50–53"> And ofen ort (immediately) the ish metzorah (leper) went away from Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and he was made tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:30; MARKOS 7:36; LUKAS 8:56"> And having sternly warned him, ofen ort (immediately) Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach sent him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="VAYIKRA 14:1–32; LUKAS 17:14; MATTITYAHU 8:4"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to him, See &#91;that&#93; you say nothing to anyone, but rather go show yourself to the kohen and offer &#91;the korban&#93; for the tohorah (purification) of you which Moshe (Rabbeinu) gave mitzvah, for an edut (testimony) to them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Lv 13:49; 14:1-32">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MARKOS 2:13; MATTITYAHU 9:31; YOCHANAN 6:2"> But the one having gone out began to preach many things and to spread the dvar, so that no longer was Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach able to openly enter into a shtetl, but he was outside in desolate places. And they were coming to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach from every direction.</span>
</div>
