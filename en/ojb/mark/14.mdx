<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 22:1–2; SHEMOT 12:6–20; YOCHANAN 13:1"> Now it was two days before Pesach, the Chag HaMatzot. And the Rashei Hakohanim and the Sofrim were seeking how they might by ormah (cunning) do away with Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:40; MISHLE 21:30; YOCHANAN 12:19"> For they were saying, Not at the Chag, for fear that a riot break out among the am haaretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKAS 7:37–39; YOCHANAN 12:1–8; YOCHANAN 11:2"> And he was in Beit-Anyah at the bais of Shim'on the leper, reclining at tish, and an isha (woman) came, having an alabaster flask of costly perfume, pure nard, and having broken open the alabaster flask, she poured &#91;it on&#93; the head of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:4–5; MATTITYAHU 26:8–9; MALACHI 1:12–13"> Now some were there who in ka'as (anger) said to one another, For what reason has this waste of ointment taken place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:28; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:10"> For this was able to be sold for more than three hundred denarii and to be given to the aniyim. And they were reproaching her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:24; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:21"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Leave her alone. Why are you bringing about difficulty for the isha? Has she not performed one of the Gemilut Chasadim on me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 15:11; MATTITYAHU 26:11; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:14–16"> For always the aniyim you have with you, and when you wish you are able to do ma'asim tovim for them, but me you do not always have.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Dt 15:11">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:12; YOCHANAN 12:7; MARKOS 15:42–1"> With what she had she did all she could. She prepared ahead of time to anoint my basar for the kevurah (burial).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 16:9-10; Job 19:25-27; Isa 53:9">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:12–13; MARKOS 16:15; MATTITYAHU 24:14"> And, omein, I say to you, wherever the Besuras HaGeulah is proclaimed in kol haaretz, also what this isha did will be spoken in her memory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKAS 22:3–6; MATTITYAHU 26:14–16; YOCHANAN 13:2"> And Yehudah from K'riot, one of the Shneym Asar, went to the Rashei Hakohanim in order that he might betray him to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:10; HOSHEA 7:3; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:11"> And there was chedvah (joy, gaiety, rejoicing) with the ones having heard and they gave the havtachah (promise) of a matnat kesef (gift of money) to Yehudah. And he was seeking how he might conveniently betray Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:6; SHEMOT 12:18; MATTITYAHU 26:17–19"> And at the rishon of the Chag HaMatzot, when it was customary to prepare the Pesach Korban for mo'ed zevach, his talmidim say to him, Where do you wish us to go and make the preparations for you to conduct the Seder?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ex 12:1-11; Dt 16:1-4">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 22:10–13; MATTITYAHU 26:18–19; LUKAS 19:30–33"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach sends two of his talmidim and he says to them, Go into the city, and you will meet a man carrying a jar of water; follow him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:20; MARKOS 11:3; YOCHANAN 13:13"> And wherever he enters, tell the Baal Bayit that the Rebbe says, Where is my mekom linah where I can conduct the Pesach Seder with my talmidim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:13; TEHILLIM 110:3; MISHLE 21:1–2"> And he will show you a large aliyyah having been furnished and ready. And there prepare for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:4; LUKAS 22:13; LUKAS 22:35"> And the talmidim went out and they came into the city and found things just as Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach told them, and they made preparations for Pesach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKAS 22:14; YOCHANAN 13:21–26; MATTITYAHU 26:20–24"> And erev having come, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach arrives with the Shneym Asar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="TEHILLIM 41:9; YOCHANAN 6:47; YOCHANAN 6:70"> And while they were at tish in a yechidus, reclining and eating, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Omein, I say to you, that one of you will betray me, OKHEL LACHEMI (the one eating my bread).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ps 41:9[10]">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKAS 22:21–23; MATTITYAHU 26:22; YOCHANAN 13:22"> They began to have agmat nefesh (grief), and, one by one, to say to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Surely I am not the one?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:26; MATTITYAHU 26:23; YOCHANAN 6:71"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, It is one of the Shneym Asar, the one dipping with me into the bowl.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ex 12:8; Ps 41:9[10]">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:24–25; YESHAYAH 53:1–12; YOCHANAN 19:28"> For the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:1314">f</a></sup>goes just as it has been written concerning him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 53:3; Dan 9:26; Zech 12:10">g</a></sup>but woe to that man through whom the Bar Enosh is betrayed. &#91;It would have been&#93; better for him if that man had not been born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:26–29; MARKOS 14:24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:16–17"> And at<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Moshiachʼs">h</a></sup>Seudah, while they were eating, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, having taken the matzah, having made the HaMotzi, broke the middle matzah, giving the afikoman to Moshiachʼs talmidim, and said, Take and eat, this is my BASAR.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ex 12:8">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MARKOS 14:22; MATTITYAHU 26:27; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:16"> And having taken the Cup of Redemption &#91;and&#93; having made the bracha, he gave it to Moshiachʼs talmidim, and everyone drank of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 9:11; SHEMOT 24:8; HISGALUS 7:9–17"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, HINEI DAHM HABRIT,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ex 24:8">j</a></sup>the dahm of me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Moshiach, see Isa 53:7">k</a></sup>which is being poured out lama'an RABBIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Isa 53:11">l</a></sup>(for the sake of MANY).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:29; TEHILLIM 104:15; LUKAS 22:16–18"> Omein, I say to you, that no longer will I by any means drink of the p'ri hagefen (fruit of the vine) until Yom HaHu when I drink it chadash (new) in the Malchut Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:30; LUKAS 22:39; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:25"> And having sung the Hallel, they went out to the Mount of Olives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 13:7; YOCHANAN 16:32; MATTITYAHU 26:31"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, All of you will fall away, for it has been written, I will strike down ES HAROEH UTEFUTEN HATZON (the Shepherd and the sheep will be scattered).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Zech 13:7">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:16; MARKOS 16:7; MATTITYAHU 28:7"> But after I am made to stand up alive in the Techiyas HaMoshiach, I will go before you into the Galil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:33–35; LUKAS 22:33–34; YOCHANAN 13:36–38"> But Shim'on Kefa said to him, Even if everyone will fall away, yet I will not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:38; MARKOS 14:66–72; BERESHIS 1:5"> And he says to him, Omein, I say to you, that you today, balailah hazeh (during this night) before the tarnegol crows twice, you will make hakhchashah (denial) of me shalosh pe'amim (three times).</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:8; YOCHANAN 13:37; MISHLE 16:18"> But Kefa kept saying with vehemence, If it is necessary for me to die al kiddush ha-Shem for you, by no means will I make hakhchashah of you. And so also everyone declared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MARKOS 14:36; MARKOS 14:39; TEHILLIM 18:5–6"> And they come to a place of which the name &#91;was&#93; Gat-Shmanim and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to his talmidim, Sit down here while I daven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKAS 22:44; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:7; MARKOS 5:37"> And he takes Kefa and Ya'akov and Yochanan with him and he began to be distressed and to be troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:27; MARKOS 13:35–37; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:8"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, My agmat nefesh (grief) is great, even to the point of mavet (death). Remain here and stay awake and shomer (on guard).</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MARKOS 14:41; DEVARIM 9:18; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:7"> And having gone forth a little, he was falling on the ground and was davening that, if it is possible, this shaah (hour) might pass from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:15–16; YOCHANAN 5:30"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying, Abba, Avi, all things &#91;are&#93; possible for you. Take away this KOS<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Isa 51:17; 53:12">n</a></sup>from me. But not what I will, but what you &#91;will&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:40; MARKOS 14:29–31; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:3"> And he comes and finds them sleeping, and he says to Shim'on Kefa, Are you sleeping? Did you not have chozek (strength) to stay awake one hour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:41; MATTITYAHU 24:42; LUKAS 21:36"> Stay shomer and daven that you may not enter into nisayon (trial, temptation). Indeed the ruach ready but the basar &#91;is&#93; weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:42–44; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:8; MATTITYAHU 6:7"> And again he went away and davened the same devarim (words) as before.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="14:36">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MARKOS 9:33–34; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:19; BERESHIS 44:16"> And once again he came and found them sleeping, for their eyes were falling shut, and they did not have daas of what they might answer him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MARKOS 14:35; MARKOS 9:31; YOCHANAN 17:1"> And he comes the shlishit (third) time and says to them, Sleep &#91;for&#93; the remainder and take your rest. It is enough. The shaah (hour) has come. Hinei, the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Moshiach">p</a></sup>is betrayed into the hands of chote'im (sinners).</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:46; YOCHANAN 18:1–2"> Get up, let us go. Hinei, the one betraying me has drawn near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:3–11; LUKAS 22:47–53; MATTITYAHU 26:47–56"> And, ofen ort, bishas maise (at the same time) Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach is speaking, Yehudah arrives, one of the Shneym Asar, and with him a crowd with swords and clubs with Rashei Hakohanim and the Sofrim and the Zekenim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:48–50; MISHLE 27:6; TEHILLIM 55:20–21"> Now the one betraying Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach had given a signal to them, saying, Whomever I may give the neshikah (kiss), he is &#91;the one&#93;, chap (seize) him! And lead &#91;him&#93; away under guard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:7–10; MALACHI 1:6; MARKOS 12:14"> And when he came, he ofen ort approached Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and says, Rebbe. And he gave him the neshikah (kiss).</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:12; EKHAH 4:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:23"> And they laid their hands on Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and arrested him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:10–11; MATTITYAHU 26:51–54; LUKAS 22:49–51"> But one of those standing by drew his cherev and struck the servant of the Kohen Gadol and cut off his ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="LUKAS 22:52–53; SHMUEL ALEF 26:18; SHMUEL ALEF 24:14–15"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach answered and said to them, Do you come out with swords and clubs as against a shoded (robber) to arrest me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:20; YOCHANAN 8:2; LUKAS 22:37"> Every day I was with you in the Beis HaMikdash teaching Torah and you did not arrest me; but let the Kitvei Hakodesh be fulfilled.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Isa 53:7-12">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="MARKOS 14:27; YOCHANAN 16:32; TEHILLIM 88:18"> And, having left Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, everyone fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And a certain bocher was following along with Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, and the bocher was wearing nothing but a linen garment around his naked body, and they seized the bocher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="MARKOS 13:14–16; BERESHIS 39:12; IYOV 2:4"> But the bocher ran away naked, leaving the linen garment behind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:57–68; YOCHANAN 18:12–14; LUKAS 22:54–62"> And they led away Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach to the Kohen Gadol. And all the Rashei Hakohanim and the Zekenim and the Sofrim were assembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:18; MATTITYAHU 26:3; YOCHANAN 18:25"> And Shim'on Kefa from a distance followed Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach right into the courtyard of the Kohen Gadol. And Kefa was sitting together with the servants and warming himself near the ohr of the hadlakah (bonfire).</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16–18; MATTITYAHU 26:59–60; MATTITYAHU 5:22"> And the Rashei Hakohanim and &#91;the&#93; whole Sanhedrin were seeking edut against Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach in order to have him under the onesh mavet, and they were not finding any.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> For many gave edut sheker against Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, and the eduyot were not in agreement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:13–14; MATTITYAHU 26:60–61; YOCHANAN 2:18–21"> And some, taking the stand, were giving edut sheker (false testimony) against him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="YOCHANAN 2:19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:1"> We heard him saying, I will bring churban to this Beis HaMikdash made with human hands, and, after shlosha yamim, another, not made with hands, I will build.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> So their eduyot were not in agreement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:9–10; MATTITYAHU 26:62–63; MARKOS 15:3–5"> And having stood up in their midst, the Kohen Gadol questioned Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, saying, Do you not answer anything to what these bear solemn edut (testimony) against you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:63–64; MARKOS 15:2; YESHAYAH 53:7"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was silent and did not answer anything.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Isa 53:7-8">r</a></sup>Again the Kohen Gadol was questioning him and says to him, Are you the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, HaBen Hamevorakh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="DANIEL 7:13–14; TEHILLIM 110:1; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:3"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Ani hu, and you will see the BAR ENOSH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Dan 7:13-14">s</a></sup>YOSHEV LIMIN HaGevurah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Ps 110:1">t</a></sup>UVA IM ANENEI HASHOMAYIM (the Bar Enosh &#91;Moshiach&#93; sitting at the right hand of power and coming with the clouds of heaven).</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:65; YESHAYAH 36:22–1; BAMIDBAR 14:6"> And the Kohen Gadol, making the keriah (ritual tearing) of his tunic, says, What further need do we have of edim (witnesses)?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Lv 10:6; 21:10; 24:16; Num 14:6">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="VAYIKRA 24:16; YOCHANAN 10:31–33; YOCHANAN 19:7"> You heard him commit Chillul Hashem gidduf (blasphemy). How does it seem to you? And they all condemned Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach to be deserving of mishpat mavet.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Lv 24:16">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:67–68; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:2; YESHAYAH 50:6"> And some began to spit on him and to cover his face and to strike him and to say to him, Letʼs hear a dvar nevuah (word of prophecy)! And the shomrim (guards) took custody of him by slapping Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="MARKOS 14:54; MATTITYAHU 26:69–75; YOCHANAN 18:25–27"> And Shim'on Kefa being below in the courtyard, one of the maids of the Kohen Gadol comes by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="MARKOS 14:54; MARKOS 10:47; MATTITYAHU 2:23"> And having seen Kefa warming himself, having looked him over, she says, You also were with the one from Natzeret, Yehoshua!</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="MARKOS 14:29–31; MATTITYAHU 26:71–72; YOCHANAN 13:36–38"> But he denied &#91;it&#93;, saying, I do not have daas (knowledge) or binah (understanding) of what you are saying. And he walked away and went outside into the entryway. And a tarnegol crowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="LUKAS 22:58; MARKOS 14:38; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:1"> And the maid, having seen him, began again to say to the ones standing by, This is one of them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="LUKAS 22:59–60; MATTITYAHU 26:73–74; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:7"> But again he made hakhchashah (denial). And after a little &#91;while&#93;, again the ones having stood by were saying to Kefa, You are one of them, for indeed you are a Gelili (inhabitant of the Galil).</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 17:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:12; MELACHIM BAIS 10:32"> And Kefa began to speak a klalah (curse) and to swear, I do not have daas of this man I don't know the one of whom you speak!</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="MARKOS 14:30; MARKOS 14:68; YECHEZKEL 7:16"> And ofen ort for a second time, a tarnegol crowed. And then Kefa remembered the dvar that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach had spoken to him, Before a tarnegol crows twice, shalosh pe'amim (three times) you will make hakhchashah (denial) of me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Mk 14:30">w</a></sup>And having broken down, Kefa was weeping.</span>
