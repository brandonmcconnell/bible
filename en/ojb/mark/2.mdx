<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:47; LUKAS 5:18; LUKAS 18:35–38"> And having entered again into K'far-Nachum, it was heard after<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="many">a</a></sup>some yamim, that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach is at home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MARKOS 2:13; MARKOS 1:45; TEHILLIM 40:9"> And many were gathered together so that there was no longer any room at the delet, and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was speaking the dvar Hashem to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKAS 5:18–26; MATTITYAHU 9:1–8; MATTITYAHU 4:24"> And they come bringing to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach a paralytic being carried along by arba'a anashim (four men).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKAS 5:19; DEVARIM 22:8"> And not being able to bring &#91;the paralytic&#93; to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach on account of the multitude, they removed the roof where Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was, and having made an opening, they lowered the mat upon which the paralytic was lying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:2; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:15; MARKOS 2:9–10"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, having seen the emunah of them, says to the paralytic, Beni, your averos are granted selicha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKAS 5:21–22; MARKOS 8:17; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:5"> And there were some of the Sofrim (Scribes) sitting there and thinking about these things in their levavot (hearts),</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YESHAYAH 43:25; LUKAS 5:21; MICHOH 7:18"> Why is this one speaking thus? He is guilty of Chillul Hashem gidduf (Desecration of the Name, blasphemy). Who is able to grant selicha (forgiveness) to averos (sins) except Hashem?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Isa 43:25">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 139:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:22; LUKAS 5:22"> And, ofen ort (immediately), having had daas in his ruach that in this way they were thinking, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach says to them, Why are you considering such things in your levavot?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 5:22–25; MATTITYAHU 9:5"> Which is easier? To say to the paralytic, Your averos are granted selicha, or to say, Stand and pick up your mat and walk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DANIEL 7:13–14; MATTITYAHU 9:6–8; YOCHANAN 5:20–27"> But in order that you may have daas that the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">c</a></sup>has samchut (authority) on haaretz to grant that averos receive selicha, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to the paralytic,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MARKOS 1:41; YOCHANAN 5:8–10; YOCHANAN 6:63"> To you I say, Stand, pick up your mat, and go to your bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:33; MATTITYAHU 9:8; LUKAS 7:16"> And the man arose, and, ofen ort (immediately), in front of everyone, taking the mat, he went outside. So everyone was astonished and gave kavod to Hashem saying, Never have we seen anything like this!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MARKOS 4:1; MISHLE 1:20–22; LUKAS 19:48"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach went out again beside the lake; and all the multitude was coming to him, and he was teaching them torah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 5:27–32; LUKAS 6:15; MATTITYAHU 9:9–13"> And as he was passing by, he saw Levi Ben-Chalfai sitting in the tax office, and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to him, Follow me. And, getting up, Levi (Mattityahu) followed Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 15:1; LUKAS 5:29–30; MATTITYAHU 9:10–11"> And it came to pass that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach reclined at tish in Leviʼs house, and many mochesim (tax collectors) and chote'im (sinners) were reclining at tish with Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and with his talmidim. For there were many, and they were following him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 19:10; LUKAS 19:7; LUKAS 18:11"> And the Sofrim (scribes) of the Perushim, seeing that Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach eats with chote'im (sinners) and mochesim (tax collectors), were saying to his talmidim, With mochesim (tax collectors) and chote'im (sinners) does he eat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:12–13; LUKAS 15:7; LUKAS 5:31–32"> And having heard this, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, Itʼs not the strong ones that have need of a rofeh (physician) but rather the cholim (sick persons). I did not come to call &#91;the&#93; tzaddikim (righteous ones) but the chote'im (sinners) &#91;to teshuva&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKAS 18:12; MATTITYAHU 6:18; MATTITYAHU 9:14–17"> And the talmidim of Yochanan and the Perushim came with tzomot (fasts). And they come and say to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Why &#91;do&#93; the talmidim of the Perushim and the talmidim of Yochanan come with tzomot, but your talmidim do not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:1–10; BERESHIS 29:22; SHOFETIM 14:10–11"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, said to them, Surely the Bnei HaChuppah of the Choson (Bridegroom) are not able to undergo tzomot as long as they have the Choson with them, nu? As long as they have the Choson with them, they are not able to undergo tzomot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKAS 17:22; HISGALUS 21:9; YESHAYAH 54:5"> But yamim (days) will come when the Choson is taken away from them, and then they will undergo tzomot HaYom HaHu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:13; YESHAYAH 57:16"> No one sews a patch of unshrunk cloth on an old garment; otherwise, the patch will pull away from it, the new from the old, and a worse tear results.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:17; TEHILLIM 119:83; LUKAS 5:37–38"> And no one puts yayin chadash (new wine) into old wineskins; otherwise, the yayin will burst the wineskins and the yayin is lost and so are the wineskins. Instead yayin chadash (new wine) &#91;is put&#93; into new wineskins.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:1–8; DEVARIM 23:24–25; LUKAS 6:1–5"> And it came about &#91;that&#93; on Shabbos, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was passing through the grainfields, and his talmidim began to make &#91;their&#93; way picking the heads of grain.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Dt 23:25">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:3; MATTITYAHU 12:2"> And the Perushim said to him, Look, why are they engaging in what is not mutar (permissible) in the Torah on Shabbos?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 21:3–6; LUKAS 10:26; MATTITYAHU 22:31"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, Have you never read in the Tanakh what Dovid HaMelech did when he was in need and he was hungry, he and the ones with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:17; VAYIKRA 24:5–9; SHMUEL ALEF 21:1"> How he entered into the Beis Hashem during the yamim of Evyatar the Kohen Gadol and the Lechem HaPanim he ate, which to be eaten it is not mutar (permissible) except by the Kohanim, and he gave &#91;some&#93; also to the ones with him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1Chr 24:6; 2Sm 8:17; Lv 24:5,9; 1Sm 21:1-6">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:16; LUKAS 6:9; DEVARIM 5:14"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Shabbos was created for the sake of man and not man for the sake of Shabbos.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ex 23:12; Dt 5:14">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:8; HISGALUS 1:10; YOCHANAN 5:17"> Therefore, the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13, 14">g</a></sup>is even Adon HaShabbos.</span>
</div>
