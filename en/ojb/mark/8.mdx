<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:32–39; MARKOS 6:34–44"> In yamim hahem (those days), again, when there was a great multitude and they had no okhel (food), Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach called his talmidim, and says to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:36; MICHOH 7:19; TEHILLIM 103:13"> I have rachmei shomayim (heavenly mercy, compassion) on the multitude, because they already shlosha yamim (three days) have remained with me and they do not have anything they may eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:31; SHOFETIM 8:4–6; SHMUEL ALEF 30:10–12"> And if I send them away hungry to their batim (houses), they will give out on the way; and some of them have come from far away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:42–44; MELACHIM BAIS 7:2; MARKOS 6:52"> And his talmidim answered him, From where will anyone be able to find enough lechem to feed these ones here in the midbar?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Num 11:21">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 14:15–17; MATTITYAHU 15:34; LUKAS 9:13"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was asking them, How many loaves do you have? And they said, Shevah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:17; MATTITYAHU 15:35–36; YOCHANAN 6:10–11"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach directed the multitude to recline at tish, as it were, on the ground. And having taken the sheva kikrot (loaves), he made a bracha, saying the ha-Motzi, and offered the betziat halechem (the breaking of the bread) and was giving &#91;them&#93; to his talmidim to serve to them, and they served the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 14:19; YOCHANAN 21:5; LUKAS 24:41–42"> And they also had a few small dagim. And after he had made the bracha over them, he directed these to be served as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:42–44; TEHILLIM 145:16; MATTITYAHU 16:10"> And they ate and were satisfied. And there was an abundance of shirayim (Rebbeʼs remainders), sheva baskets &#91;full&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And there were approximately arbaat elafim (four thousand) &#91;people&#93;. And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach sent them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:39"> And ofen ort (immediately), having embarked onto the sirah (boat) with his talmidim, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach came into the region of Dalmanutha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:9; MATTITYAHU 12:38; DEVARIM 6:16"> And the Perushim came out and began to argue with him, seeking from him an ot min HaShomayim (a sign from heaven), testing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:39–40; MARKOS 7:34; MATTITYAHU 16:4"> And sighing deeply in his ruach, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Why does hador hazeh (this generation) seek for an ot? Omein, I say to you, no ot shall be given to hador hazeh (this generation).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:36; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:45–46; TEHILLIM 81:12"> And having left them, Moshiach again embarked and departed to the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:5"> And his talmidim had forgotten to take lechem and did not have anything except one kikar (loaf) in the sirah (boat) with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:6–8; MATTITYAHU 16:6; MATTITYAHU 16:11–12"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was giving orders to them, saying, Take care! Beware of the chametz (leaven) of the Perushim and the chametz of Herod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 9:46; LUKAS 20:5; MATTITYAHU 16:7–8"> And the talmidim were beginning to discuss among themselves that they do not have &#91;any&#93; kikrot (loaves).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MARKOS 6:52; YESHAYAH 63:17; MARKOS 2:8"> And aware of this, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, Why are you discussing that you do not have kikrot? Do you not yet have binah (understanding) nor comprehend? Have your levavot fallen into timtum halev (hardening of the heart), KESHI<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dt 9:27">b</a></sup>(hardness, stubbornness)?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 6:9,10">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:26–27; MATTITYAHU 13:14–15; YIRMEYAH 5:21"> EINAYIM LAKHEM VLO TIRU VOZNAYIM LAKHEM VLO TISHMAU?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Jer 5:21; Ezek 12:2">d</a></sup>(Having eyes, do you not see? And having ears, do you not hear?) And do you not remember,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKAS 9:12–17; MATTITYAHU 14:17–21; YOCHANAN 6:5–13"> When I offered the beziat halechem with the chamesh kikrot (five loaves) for the chameshet elafim (five thousand), how many baskets of shirayim you picked up? And they say to him, Shneym Asar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:34–38; MARKOS 8:1–9"> And when I offered the beziat halechem over the shevah (seven) for the arbaat haalafim (four thousand), how many baskets full of shirayim did you pick up? And they say to him, Shiva.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MARKOS 6:52; MATTITYAHU 16:11–12; MARKOS 8:12"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying to them, Do you not yet have binah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:21; MARKOS 6:45; LUKAS 10:13"> And they come to Beit-Tzaidah. And they bring to him an ivver (blind man), and entreated him to touch him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MARKOS 7:33; YOCHANAN 9:6–7; MARKOS 5:23"> And having grasped the hand of the ivver, he took him outside the shtetl; and, having put spittle on his eyes, &#91;and&#93; having laid hands upon him, he was questioning him, Do you see anything?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YESHAYAH 32:3; YESHAYAH 29:18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:9–12"> And after looking up, the ivver was saying, I see bnei Adam — they look like trees walking!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18; MISHLE 4:18"> Then again Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach placed his hands upon his eyes; and the man looked intently and was restored, and was seeing everything clearly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 8:4; MARKOS 8:23; MARKOS 5:43"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach sent the man to his bais, saying, Do not even enter the shtetl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:13–20; LUKAS 9:18–20"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach went out along with his talmidim, to the shtetlach of Caesarea Philippi; and on the way he was questioning his talmidim, saying to them, Who do Bnei Adam say that I am?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:21; MATTITYAHU 14:2; MALACHI 4:5"> And the talmidim spoke to him, saying, Yochanan of the tevilah of teshuva; and others say, Eliyahu HaNavi; but others, One of the neviim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Mal 3:23">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:15; MATTITYAHU 16:15–16; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:1"> And he continued questioning them, But who do you say that I am? In reply, Kefa says to him, You are the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LUKAS 9:21; MATTITYAHU 16:20; MARKOS 9:9"> And he warned them to tell no one about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MARKOS 10:33–34; YOCHANAN 2:19; LUKAS 24:26"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach began to teach them that it is necessary &#91;for&#93; the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">f</a></sup>to suffer many things and to be rejected by the Zekenim and the Rashei Hakohanim (the Chief Priests) and the Sofrim (Scribes),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 53:3">g</a></sup>and to be killed and after his histalkus (passing) after Shlosha Yamim (Three Days) to undergo the Techiyas HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:22; YOCHANAN 16:25; YOCHANAN 16:29"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was speaking about the matter plainly. And Shim'on Kefa took him aside and began to rebuke him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:19; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15"> But having turned around and having looked at his talmidim, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach rebuked Kefa, and says, Get behind me, HaSatan; for you are not putting your machshavot (thoughts) on the interests of Hashem but on that of Bnei Adam.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="cf. Mk 7:9">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:38; LUKAS 14:26–27; YOCHANAN 10:27"> And having summoned the multitude along with his talmidim, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, If anyone wishes to follow after me, let him make denial of himself, and take up his etz shel mesiros nefesh (tree of self-sacrifice), and follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:11–13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:23; LUKAS 17:33"> For whoever wishes to save his nefesh will lose it; but whoever loses his nefesh for my sake and for the sake of the Besuras HaGeulah will save it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:26; LUKAS 9:25; TEHILLIM 49:17"> For how is the ben Adam (human being) benefited if he gain the kol haOlam, and forfeit his neshamah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="TEHILLIM 49:7–8; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:18–19"> For what shall a ben Adam give in exchange &#91;for&#93; his neshamah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LUKAS 12:8–9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:16; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:23"> For whoever feels bushah (shame) regarding me and my devarim in this dor rah umnaef (evil and adulterous generation), the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Moshiach">i</a></sup>will also be ashamed of him, when Moshiach comes in the kavod of his Av with the malachim hakedoshim.</span>
