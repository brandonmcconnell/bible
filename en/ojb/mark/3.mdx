<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:9–14; LUKAS 6:6–11; MARKOS 1:21"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach entered again into the shul. And there was there a man having a withered hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="TEHILLIM 37:32; LUKAS 14:1; LUKAS 6:7"> And they were watching Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach &#91;to see&#93; if on Shabbos he will heal him, in order that they might accuse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:28–30; YESHAYAH 42:4; LUKAS 6:8"> And he says to the man, the one with the withered hand, Stand up here in the center.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKAS 6:9; MATTITYAHU 12:10–12; HOSHEA 6:6"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, Is it mutar (permissible) on Shabbos to do tov or rah, to restore nefesh or to destroy &#91;it&#93;? But they were silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKAS 6:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:25"> And having looked &#91;at&#93; them with ka'as and having agmat nefesh (grief) at the stubborn hardness of their levavot, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to the man, Stretch out your hand. And the man stretched it out, and his hand was restored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 22:16; MARKOS 12:13; MATTITYAHU 12:14"> And ofen ort (immediately) the Perushim went out and held consultation with the party of Herod against him, as to how they might destroy him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:25; LUKAS 6:17; MATTITYAHU 12:15"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach with his talmidim went away to the lake, and a great multitude from the Galil and also from Yehudah followed Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 35:15; YESHAYAH 34:5; MATTITYAHU 11:21"> And from Yerushalayim and from Idumea and from the other side of the Yarden and from around Tzor-Tzidon area, a great multitude &#91;followed&#93;, hearing everything that he was doing, and they came to Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MARKOS 5:30; YOCHANAN 6:15"> And he said to his talmidim that a sirah (boat) should stand ready for him, lest the multitude might crush him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 14:36; MARKOS 6:56; MATTITYAHU 4:23"> For he had brought refuah to many, with the result that as many as had afflictions pressed in on him in order to touch him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 4:41; MARKOS 1:23–24; LUKAS 8:28"> And the ruchot hatemeiot (unclean spirits), whenever they saw Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, were falling before him and were crying out, saying, You are the Ben HaElohim!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:16; MARKOS 1:25; MARKOS 1:34"> And he sternly rebuked them, lest they should make him known.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 9:1; LUKAS 6:12–16; MATTITYAHU 10:1–4"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach goes up to the mountain and summons those whom he wanted, and they came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:8; LUKAS 9:1–6; YOCHANAN 15:16"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach appointed and gave smichah to Shneym Asar (Twelve), whom also he designated Shlichim, that they might be with him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="be mishtatef in Moshiachʼs chavurah">a</a></sup>and that he might send them out as his Shlichim to preach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and to have samchut (authority) to cast out shedim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:42; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:13; MATTITYAHU 16:16–18"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach appointed the Shneym Asar: Shim'on, to whom he gave the name Kefa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:12; MARKOS 14:33; YIRMEYAH 23:29"> And Ya'akov Ben Zavdai and Yochanan, Ya'akovʼs brother. He gave to them the name Bnei Regesh, (Sons of Thunder).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:13; MARKOS 2:14; MATTITYAHU 9:9"> And Andrew and Philippos and Bar-Talmai, and Mattityahu, and T'oma, and Ya'akov Ben-Chalfai, and Taddai, and Shim'on the Zealot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:71; YOCHANAN 13:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:16–25"> And Yehudah from K'riot, the one who betrayed Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MARKOS 6:31; YOCHANAN 4:31–34; MARKOS 9:28"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach goes to his home shtetl; and again the multitude assembles, with the result that they were not able to have okhel (food).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:24; YOCHANAN 10:20; MARKOS 3:31"> And when his own mishpochah heard of this, they went out to constrain him. For they were saying, He is meshuga.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:1; MATTITYAHU 10:25; MATTITYAHU 9:34"> And the Sofrim (Scribes), the ones coming down from Yerushalayim, were saying, He is possessed by Baal-zibbul and by the Sar HaShedim he casts out shedim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKAS 11:17–23; MATTITYAHU 12:25–30; MATTITYAHU 13:34"> But having called them, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was speaking to them in meshalim: How can HaSatan cast out HaSatan?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 11:14; YESHAYAH 19:2–3; SHMUEL BAIS 20:6"> And if a malchut is divided against itself, it is not possible for that malchut to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16; TEHILLIM 133:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:15"> And if a bais is divided against itself, it will not be possible for that bais to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And if HaSatan has risen up against himself and is divided, it is not possible for him to stand, but his end has arrived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YESHAYAH 49:24–26; MATTITYAHU 12:29; LUKAS 11:21–23"> But no one is able, having entered into the house of the Gibbor to plunder his possessions, unless first, he binds HaGibbor, and then he will plunder the Bayit HaGibbor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKAS 12:10; MATTITYAHU 12:31–32; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:16"> Omein, I say to you that for everything — for the averos (sins) and the Chillul Hashem (Desecration of the Name), whatever blasphemies they may utter — the Bnei Adam will be granted selicha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:31–32; LUKAS 12:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:9"> But whoever commits Chillul Hashem gidduf against the Ruach Hakodesh, can never have selicha, but is guilty of a Chet Olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:20; MARKOS 3:22"> For they were saying, He has a ruach hatumah (an unclean spirit).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LUKAS 8:19–21; MATTITYAHU 12:46–50"> And the Em (Mother) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and his achim come and stand outside and they sent word to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And a multitude was sitting around him, and they are saying to him, Hinei! Your Em (Mother)! And your achim (brothers)! And your achayot (sisters) are outside looking for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MARKOS 3:21; DEVARIM 33:9; MARKOS 6:3"> And in reply to them, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says, Who is immi? And my achim (brothers)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:49–50; LUKAS 11:27–28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:29"> And having looked around at the ones sitting around him, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says, Hinei! Immi and my achim!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:17; MATTITYAHU 7:21; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:22–23"> For whoever does the ratzon Hashem, this one is my brother and my sister and mother.</span>
</div>
