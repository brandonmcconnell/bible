<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 5:1–3; MARKOS 2:13; LUKAS 8:4–10"> And again Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach began to say shiurim (Torah talks) beside the lake. And a huge multitude gathered together to him so that he got into a sirah (boat) on the lake and sat down. The whole multitude was by the lake on the shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:34–35; MARKOS 3:23; MARKOS 4:11"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach was giving them torah with many meshalim (parables) and in the shiurim he said, he was saying to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5; MATTITYAHU 13:26; MARKOS 4:26–29"> Listen. Hinei! The Sower went out to sow &#91;seeds&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:19; MARKOS 4:15; MATTITYAHU 13:4"> And it came about that when he went to sow, that some &#91;seeds&#93; fell beside the road. And the birds came and devoured it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 36:26; LUKAS 8:6; LUKAS 8:13"> And other &#91;seeds&#93; fell upon the rocky places where it did not have much soil, and ofen ort (immediately) it sprang up because it did not have depth of soil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:11; YIRMEYAH 17:5–8; YONAH 4:8"> And when the shemesh rose, it was scorched and, because it did not have a shoresh (root), it withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 4:3; LUKAS 8:14; BERESHIS 3:17–18"> And other seed fell among the thorns, and the thorns grew up and choked them, and it did not yield pri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:6; MARKOS 4:20"> And other seed fell into the adamah tovah (good ground) and it was giving pri (fruit), rising up and growing, thirty, sixty, and one hundredfold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:15; MARKOS 4:23–24; HISGALUS 3:22"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying, He who has ears to hear, let him hear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:10–17; MARKOS 4:34; MISHLE 13:20"> And when he was alone, the Shneym Asar were asking him for a hezber (explanation) of the meshalim (parables).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:25; LUKAS 8:10; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:20"> And he was saying to them, To you the raz (secret) of the Malchut Hashem has been given. But to the outsiders, everything remains in meshalim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 6:9–10; YESHAYAH 44:18; MATTITYAHU 13:14–15"> In order that YIRU they may see and not have daas, VSHIMU they may hear and not have binah, lest they should turn and they should receive refuah and selicha.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Isa 6:9-10, TARGUM HASHIVIM">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:19; MATTITYAHU 13:51–52; MARKOS 7:17–18"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach says to them, Do you not have daas of the meaning of this mashal? Then how will you have binah of all the meshalim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:4; MATTITYAHU 13:19; MATTITYAHU 13:37"> The one sowing sows the dvar Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:8; MATTITYAHU 13:19; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:11"> And these are the ones beside the road where the dvar Hashem is sown. And when they hear it, ofen ort HaSatan comes and takes the dvar that has been sown in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MARKOS 6:20; LUKAS 8:13; MATTITYAHU 13:20–21"> And these are the ones sown upon the rocky places: when they hear the dvar, ofen ort with simcha they receive it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:3–5; MATTITYAHU 12:31"> Yet they do not have a shoresh (root) in themselves but are transitory; then when ES TZARAH comes or redifah (persecution) on account of the dvar, ofen ort they fall away, they become shmad.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Jer 30:7; Mt 24:21-22">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKAS 8:14; YIRMEYAH 4:3; MATTITYAHU 13:22"> And others are the ones sown among thorns. These are the ones having heard the dvar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–17; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:9–10; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:17"> and the worries of the Olam Hazeh and the seduction of ashires (riches) and the desires for other things come in and choke the dvar Hashem and it does not bear pri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:10; LUKAS 8:15; YOCHANAN 15:4–5"> And these are the ones sown upon adamah tovah: they hear the dvar Hashem and are mekabel (receive) the dvar Hashem and bear pri, thirty, sixty and one hundredfold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:15; LUKAS 11:33; LUKAS 8:16"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying to them, Surely a menorah is not brought out in order that it may be put under a measuring bucket or under the couch? Rather, is it not brought out that it may be placed on the shulchan?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKAS 8:17; KOHELET 12:14; MATTITYAHU 10:26–27"> For there is not &#91;anything&#93; nistar except that it may be made nikar nor has it become concealed but that it may come into &#91;the&#93; open.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Jer 16:17">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MARKOS 4:9; MATTITYAHU 11:15; HISGALUS 2:29"> If anyone has ears to hear, let him hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:1; LUKAS 8:18; MATTITYAHU 7:2"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying to them, Pay attention! By what measure you measure, it will be measured to you, and still more will be added to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:12; LUKAS 8:18; MATTITYAHU 25:28–29"> For whoever has, it will be given to him, and whoever does not have, even what he has will be taken away from him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:6–9; LUKAS 8:11"> And he was saying, Thus is the Malchut Hashem: like a man who casts seed upon the soil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="KOHELET 11:5; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:37–38"> And goes to sleep blailah (at night) and arises byom (at day), and the seed sprouts up and grows in such a way that he does not have daas of how it does so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MARKOS 4:31–32; HOSHEA 6:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:9–11"> On its own, the soil bears pri: first the blade, then a head of grain, then full wheat in the head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YOEL 3:13; MATTITYAHU 13:30; MATTITYAHU 13:40–43"> But when the crop is ripe, ofen ort (immediately) he puts forth the MAGGAL, KI VASHAL KATZIR (sickle, for the harvest is ripe).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Yoel 4:13">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:31–32; LUKAS 13:18–21; EKHAH 2:13"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying, To what should we compare the Malchut Hashem or by what mashal (parable) may we present it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:20; LUKAS 13:18–19; DANIEL 2:44–45"> The Malchut Hashem is like a mustard seed, which, when it is sown on the soil, is smaller &#91;than&#93; all the seeds on the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DANIEL 4:20–22; DANIEL 4:10–14; YECHEZKEL 31:3–10"> But when it is sown, it grows up and becomes greater &#91;than&#93; all the plants of the garden and puts forth large branches, so that the OPH HASHOMAYIM can nest under its shade.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:34–35; YOCHANAN 16:12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:11–14"> And with many such meshalim, Rebbe, Melech Hamoshiach was speaking to them the dvar Hashem as they were able to hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:25; MARKOS 4:10; MATTITYAHU 15:15–20"> But apart from meshalim, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was not speaking to them. Only privately, in a yechidus, was Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach explaining everything to his own talmidim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 8:18; LUKAS 8:22; LUKAS 8:25"> And on that day, when erev had come, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach says to them, Let us go over to the other side of the lake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MARKOS 3:9; MARKOS 4:1; MARKOS 5:2"> And leaving the multitude, they took Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, just as he was, in the sirah (boat), and other sirot (boats) were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 8:23–24; IYOV 1:19; LUKAS 8:22–23"> And there arose a fierce gale of wind and the waves were beating against the sirah (boat), so that it was already filling up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:15; YESHAYAH 54:6–8"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was in the stern on the cushion sleeping, and they woke him up, and they say to him, Rebbe, does it not matter to you that we are perishing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="TEHILLIM 107:29; TEHILLIM 89:9; TEHILLIM 65:7"> And having been awakened, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach rebuked the wind, and he spoke to the lake, Sha! Be still! And there was a great calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LUKAS 8:25; MATTITYAHU 8:26; MATTITYAHU 14:31"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Why do you have morech lev (cowardliness)? Do you still not have emunah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LUKAS 8:25; IYOV 38:11; TEHILLIM 89:7"> And they were afraid with a terrible pachad (terror), and they were saying to one another, Who then is this that even the wind and the sea obey him?</span>
</div>
