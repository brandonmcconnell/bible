</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:10–11; LUKAS 11:53–54; YOCHANAN 8:6"> And, having approached, the Perushim, testing him, were asking him, Is it mutar for a man to give a get (divorce) to his wife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:21; LUKAS 10:25"> But in reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, What mitzvah (commandment) did Moshe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Rabbeinu">a</a></sup>give you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEVARIM 24:1–4; MATTITYAHU 5:31–32; YESHAYAH 50:1"> And they said, Moshe Rabbeinu made it mutar (permissible) for a man to write SEFER KERITUT (a get, bill of divorcement) and to send her away (dismiss her, divorce her).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dt 24:1-4">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:8; NECHEMYAH 9:26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:51"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Because of the hardness of your levavot, he wrote you this mitzvah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 95:8">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 1:27; BERESHIS 5:2; BERESHIS 1:1"> But in the beginning of the Beriah (Creation) ZACHAR UNEKEVAH BARA OTAM (&#91;Hashem&#93; made them male and female).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gn 1:27; 5:2">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 2:24; MATTITYAHU 19:5–6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:31"> AL KEN YAAZAV ISH ES AVIV V'ES IMMO V'DAVAK BISHTO (Because of this, a man will leave his father and his mother and he will be joined to his isha &#91;wife&#93;),</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:28; BERESHIS 2:24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:16"> V'HAYU L'VASAR ECHAD, (and the two will be one flesh).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gn 2:24">e</a></sup>For this reason, they are no longer Shnayim but Basar Echad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:1–3; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 7:10–13"> Therefore, what Hashem has joined together, let no ben Adam separate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MARKOS 9:33; MARKOS 9:28; MARKOS 4:10"> And in the bais, the talmidim began to question Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach about this again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:31–32; MATTITYAHU 19:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:10–11"> And he says to them, Whoever gives a get to his isha and marries another, commits niuf (adultery) against his isha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:13"> And if she gets a get (divorce) from her baal (husband) and marries another, she commits niuf (adultery).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Mt 19:9">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:13–15; LUKAS 18:15–17; SHEMOT 10:9–11"> And they brought to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach yeladim that he might lay his hands on them. But the talmidim rebuked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:14; MATTITYAHU 18:10; MATTITYAHU 18:4"> And having seen this, he became displeased and indignant, and said to his talmidim, Permit the yeladim to come to me, and do not hinder them, for of such as these is the Malchut Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:3; LUKAS 18:17; YOCHANAN 3:3–6"> Omein, I say to you, Whoever is not mekabel Malchut Hashem (receives the Kingdom of G-d) as a yeled would be mekabel Malchut Hashem, will by no means enter it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MARKOS 9:36; YESHAYAH 40:11; YOCHANAN 21:15–17"> And having taken the yeladim into his arms, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach laid his hands on them and made a bracha over them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:16–30; LUKAS 18:18–30; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:7"> And as Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was setting out to travel, one running and falling down before him, was asking him, Rabbi haTov, what mitzvah may I do that I might inherit Chayyei Olam?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKAS 18:19; MATTITYAHU 19:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:12"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Why do you call me tov? No one tov except echad, Elohim, nu?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:12–17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:9; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:11"> You have daas of the mitzvot, the Aseres HaDibros (The Decalogue), LO TIRTZACH, LO TINAF, LO TIGNOV, LO TAANEH VREIACHA ED SHAKER, KABEID ES AVICHA VES IMMECHA (Do not murder, do not commit adultery, do not steal, do not bear false witness (do not defraud), honor your father and mother.)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ex 20:12-16; Dt 5:16-20">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 33:31–32; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:6"> And the man was saying to him, Rabbi, from kinder-yoren I have been frum and shomer mitzvot, keeping all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:19–21; HISGALUS 2:4; LUKAS 18:22"> And looking at him and having ahavah for his neshamah, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to him, One thing you lack: go and sell all your possessions and give to the aniyim, and you will have otzar (treasure) in Shomayim; and come, follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–16; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:9–10; LUKAS 12:15"> But at the dvar HaMoshiach, his face turned gloomy and he departed with agmat nefesh (grief), for he had many possessions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:26; LUKAS 18:24; MARKOS 3:5"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, looking around, says to his talmidim, How difficult it will be for the oisher to enter the Malchut Hashem!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ps 52:7; 62:10">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:1–3; MISHLE 11:28; TEHILLIM 52:7"> And the talmidim were amazed at his devarim. But again Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach in reply, says to them, Yeladim, how difficult it is to enter into the Malchut Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:24–25; LUKAS 18:25; MATTITYAHU 23:24"> It is easier &#91;for&#93; a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for an oisher (rich person) to enter the Malchut Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:31; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:9–13"> And they were even more amazed, saying to one another, Then who with the Yeshu'at Eloheinu is able to be saved?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:26; YIRMEYAH 32:27; IYOV 42:2"> Having looked at them, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says, With Bnei Adam &#91;this is&#93; impossible, but not with Hashem. For all things are possible with Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:27–30; LUKAS 18:28–30; LUKAS 14:33"> Shim'on Kefa began to say to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Hinei! We left everything and have followed you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:23; MATTITYAHU 5:10–11; MARKOS 8:35"> Yehoshua said, Omein, I say to you, there is no one who left bais or achim or achayot or em or abba or banim or sadot (fields) for the sake of me and for the sake of the Besuras HaGeulah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LUKAS 18:30; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:10"> But that he shall receive a hundredfold now in the Olam Hazeh, batim (houses) and achim and achayot and imahot and banim and sadot with redifot (persecutions); and in Olam Habah, Chayyei Olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:30; LUKAS 13:30; MATTITYAHU 20:16"> And many Rishonim (First Ones) will be Acharonim (Last Ones); and the Acharonim, will be Rishonim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:17–19; LUKAS 18:31–34; LUKAS 9:51"> And they were on the derech making aliyah leregel (pilgrimage) to Yerushalayim, and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was leading out, walking ahead of them, and they were astounded, and the ones following were afraid. And again Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach took the Shneym Asar aside for a yechidus and began to tell them what was to happen to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:2; MATTITYAHU 20:17–19; MARKOS 8:31"> Saying, Hinei, we are making aliyah leregel to Yerushalayim, and the Bar Enosh will be betrayed to the Rashei Hakohanim and the Sofrim and they will condemn him with onesh mavet (death penalty) and will hand him over to the Goyim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:67; MATTITYAHU 16:21; MARKOS 14:65"> And they will mock him and spit on him and scourge him and will kill &#91;him&#93;, and after his histalkus (passing), on Yom HaShlishi, he in his Techiyas HaMoshiach from HaMesim will stand up alive again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:20–28; SHMUEL BAIS 14:4–11; MELACHIM ALEF 2:20"> And Ya'akov and Yochanan, the banim of Zavdai, approached Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, saying to him, Rebbe, we wish that whatever bakosha we may ask you, you may do for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:7; MELACHIM ALEF 3:5–15; MARKOS 10:51"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, What do you wish me to do for you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:28; MARKOS 16:19; LUKAS 24:26"> And they said to him, Grant to us that one may sit limin (at the right hand) of you and one lismol (at the left hand) in your kavod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LUKAS 12:50; MATTITYAHU 20:21–22; TEHILLIM 75:8"> But he said to them, You do not have daas of what you ask. Are you able to drink the kos which I drink or to undergo my mikveh?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Job 38:2">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:2; HISGALUS 1:9; YOCHANAN 17:14"> And they said to him, We are able. And he said to them, The kos which I drink you shall drink; and you shall have the mikveh in which I am submerged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:23; MATTITYAHU 25:34; YOCHANAN 17:24"> But to sit limin or lismol of me is not mine to grant, but for the ones for whom it has been prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:24; MISHLE 13:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:10"> And hearing this, the Asarah (Ten) became indignant with Ya'akov and Yochanan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:3; LUKAS 22:25; MATTITYAHU 20:25"> And having summoned them, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, You have daas that among the Goyim those whom they recognize as their moshlim (rulers) domineer them and their Gedolim exercise authority over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:8–12; LUKAS 14:11; MARKOS 9:35"> However, it is not so among you. He who wishes to become gadol among you must be your mesharet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And whoever wishes to be first among you must be eved of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:5–8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:9"> For even the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">j</a></sup>did not come to be served but to serve and to give his NEFESH as a kofer (ransom, pedut) LARABBIM (for many, for the Geulah &#91;Redemption&#93; of many).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Isa 53:10-11">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LUKAS 18:35–1; MATTITYAHU 20:29–34; YOCHANAN 9:8"> And they come to Yericho. And as Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was going forth from Yericho with his talmidim and a great multitude, Bar-Timai,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="son of Timai">l</a></sup>an ivver, a poor betler (beggar), was sitting beside the road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MARKOS 1:24; MATTITYAHU 9:27; HISGALUS 22:16"> And when he heard that it was Yehoshua from Natzeret coming, he began to cry out and to shout, Ben Dovid Yehoshua, chaneini!</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 29:13; MARKOS 7:26–29; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:7"> And many were rebuking him that he should shekit, but he kept crying out all the more, Ben Dovid, chaneini!</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="LUKAS 18:40; MATTITYAHU 20:32–34; YOCHANAN 11:28"> Having stopped, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Summon him here. And they called the ivver, saying to him, Chazak! Cheer up! He is calling you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:7–9; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:1"> And having tossed aside his kaftan, and having jumped up, he came to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="LUKAS 18:41–43; MARKOS 10:36; MATTITYAHU 7:7"> And in reply, Yehoshua said, What do you wish that I may do for you? And the ivver said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Rabboni, that I may see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:18; MARKOS 8:25"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Go. Your emunah has brought you tikkun (restored you). And ofen ort (immediately) he regained his sight and he began following Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach baderech (on the road).</span>
