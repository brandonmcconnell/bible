<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YESHAYAH 5:1–4; MARKOS 13:34; SHIR HASHIRIM 8:11–12"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach began to speak to them in meshalim (parables). A man, a Baal Bayit, planted a kerem (vineyard), and he put around &#91;it&#93; a fence and dug a pit for the yekev (winepress) and built a migdal (tower), and he leased it to koremim (vine keepers) and then the man went on a journey.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Isa 5:1-7">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:1–8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:1; YIRMEYAH 44:4"> And the Baal Bayit sent an eved to the tenants, the koremim, in the season &#91;of katzir (harvest)&#93; in order that from the koremim he might receive from the perot (fruit) of the kerem (vineyard).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:15; NECHEMYAH 9:26; YIRMEYAH 29:26"> But they seized the eved and they beat him and sent him away empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And again the Baal Bayit sent to the tenants, the koremim, another eved; and that one they struck on the rosh and insulted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 22:6; MATTITYAHU 23:37; NECHEMYAH 9:30"> And the Baal Bayit sent another; and that one they killed, and many others, some beating, and others killing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:1–2; MATTITYAHU 3:17; YOCHANAN 1:34"> Still he had one BEN AHUV.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="T.N. see 9:7">b</a></sup>Lemaskana (finally), the Baal Bayit sent him to them saying, They will respect BENI.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:20; MATTITYAHU 2:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:23"> But those koremim said to themselves, This one is the Yoresh (Heir) the Bechor. Come, let us kill him, and the nachalah, the bechorah, will be ours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:39; LUKAS 20:15; MATTITYAHU 21:33"> And having seized &#91;him&#93;, they killed him and threw him out of the kerem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:20–12; MATTITYAHU 21:43; LUKAS 19:27"> What then will the Baal Bayit of the kerem do? He will come and destroy the koremim and will give the kerem to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 118:22–23; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:7–8; YESHAYAH 28:16"> Have you not read the Kitvei Hakodesh? EVEN MAASU HABONIM HAYTAH LEROSH PINAH (The stone which the ones building rejected, this one has come to be for &#91;the&#93; chief cornerstone).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TEHILLIM 118:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:12–16; CHABAKUK 1:5"> MEIET HASHEM HAYTAH ZOT, HI NIFLAT BEINEINU (From Adonoi this came to be and it is wonderful in our eyes).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 118:22,23">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MARKOS 11:18; MATTITYAHU 22:22; YOCHANAN 7:44"> And they were seeking to seize him, and they were afraid of the multitude, for they knew that against them he told the mashal. And leaving him, they went away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 20:20–26; LUKAS 11:54; MARKOS 3:6"> And they send to him some of the Perushim (Pharisees) and the Herodians in order that they might catch him in a dvar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:4; LUKAS 20:22; YOCHANAN 7:18"> And having come, they say to him: Rabbi, we have daas that you are an ish Emes and you do not show deference toward anyone. Ki ein masso panim (For there is no partiality) with you but rather on the Derech Hashem you give Divrei Torah in Emes. Is it mutar to give a poll tax to Caesar or not? Should we give or should we not give?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 17:2; HISGALUS 2:23; MARKOS 10:2"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, having seen their tzeviut (hypocrisy), said to them, Why are you testing me? Bring me a denarius that I may look &#91;at it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 20:24–26; HISGALUS 3:12; MATTITYAHU 22:19–22"> And they brought &#91;one&#93;. And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, Whose demut is this and whose inscription? And they said to him, Caesarʼs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:7; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:17; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:14–15"> And he said to them, The things of Caesar, give to Caesar, and the things of Hashem &#91;give&#93; to Hashem. And they were amazed at Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 22:23–33; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:1–2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:6–9"> And &#91;the&#93; Tzedukim come to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, the ones who say that there is not to be a Techiyas HaMesim, and they were questioning him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 25:5–10; RUTH 4:5; RUTH 1:11–13"> Rabbi, Moshe (Rabbeinu) wrote to us that if an ACH of someone should die and leave behind an isha UVEN EIN LO (and there is no son to him) YEVAMAH YAVO ALEIHA ULEKAKHAH LO LISHA VHAYAH HABECHOR ASHER TELED YAKUM AL SHEM ACHIV HAMET<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Dt 25:5">d</a></sup>(the brother of her husband must come to her and he must take her to him as wife and the Bechor she bears shall raise up the name of the dead brother).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 22:25–28; LUKAS 20:29–33"> Now there were shiva achim. And the first took a wife and, dying, did not leave a descendant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the second took her and he died, not having left behind a descendant. And the third likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the seven did not leave a descendant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In the Techiyas HaMesim, when they are made to stand up alive, of which of them will she be &#91;the&#93; isha? For seven had her as wife.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:9; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:15–17; MATTITYAHU 22:29"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, said to them, Is not this the reason you are in error, having daas neither of the Kitvei Hakodesh nor of the gevurat Hashem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKAS 20:35–36; MATTITYAHU 22:30; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:2"> But concerning the Mesim that are made to stand up alive, they do not marry nor are they given in nisuim, but are like the malachim in Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:2–6; LUKAS 20:37; BERESHIS 17:7–8"> But concerning the Mesim, that they are made to stand up alive, have you not read in the sefer Moshe at the bush, how Hashem spoke to him, saying, ANOCHI ELOHEI AVRAHAM ELOHEI YITZCHAK VELOHEI Ya'akov, (Ex 3:6).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:17; MARKOS 12:24"> Hashem is not the G-d of the Mesim (Dead ones) but of the Chayyim (Living ones). You are greatly mistaken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 22:34–40; LUKAS 10:25–28; LUKAS 20:39"> And one of the Sofrim approached and heard them debating, and seeing that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach answered them well, asked him, Which mitzvah is rishonah of all the mitzvot?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEVARIM 6:4; LUKAS 10:27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:20"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach answered, SHEMA YISROEL ADONOI ELOHEINU ADONOI ECHAD is harishonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEVARIM 6:5"> V'AHAVTA ES ADONOI ELOHEICHA B'CHOL LEVAVCHA UVECHOL NAFSHECHA UVECHOL MODECHA.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dt 6:4,5">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:8–9; MATTITYAHU 22:39; VAYIKRA 19:18"> And the shneyah is this, VAHAVTA LREACHA KAMOCHA.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Lv 19:18">f</a></sup>There is not another mitzvah greater than these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:39; DEVARIM 4:35; YESHAYAH 46:9"> And the Baal Torah said to him, Well spoken, Rabbi, beemes you have said that Hashem is ECHAD<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Dt 6:4">g</a></sup>(one), and EIN OD and there is no other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isa 45:18">h</a></sup>except him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Dt 4:35,39; Isa 45:6,14; 46:9">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="HOSHEA 6:6; MICHOH 6:6–8; SHMUEL ALEF 15:22"> and to have ahavah for him B'CHOL LEVAVCHA (with all your heart) and with all your binah UVECHOL MODECHA<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Dt 6:5">j</a></sup>(with all your strength) and L'REACHA KAMOCHA<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Lv 19:18">k</a></sup>(your neighbor as yourself) is greater &#91;than&#93; all of the burnt offerings and sacrifices.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="1Sm 15:22; Hos 6:6; Mic 6:6-8">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:6; LUKAS 20:40; MATTITYAHU 22:46"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, when he saw that this torah teacher answered with chochmah (wisdom), said to him, You are not far from the Malchut Hashem. And no one was daring to put a she'elah (question) in front of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LUKAS 20:41–44; MATTITYAHU 22:41–45; MATTITYAHU 26:55"> While Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying shiurim (Torah talks) in the Beis Hamikdash, he was saying: How is it that the Sofrim say that Moshiach is &#91;merely&#93; ben Dovid?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="TEHILLIM 110:1; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:13; MATTITYAHU 22:43–45"> Dovid himself said by the Ruach Hakodesh, NEUM HASHEM LADONI: SHEV LIMINI, AD ASHIT OYVECHA HADOM LRAGLECHA.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="2Sm 23:2; Ps 110:1">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:3–4; MATTITYAHU 11:25; MATTITYAHU 1:23"> Dovid himself calls Moshiach Adon; how then is Moshiach &#91;merely&#93; ben Dovid? And the large multitude found it a huge oneg (pleasure) to listen to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:1–7; LUKAS 20:45–47; LUKAS 11:43"> And in his torah, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying, Beware of the Sofrim (Scribes, Torah teachers, rabbonim), the ones enamored of strutting about in extremely long kaftans and with equally lengthy Birkat Shalom greetings in the marketplaces,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:2–3"> And places of honor in the shuls and places of honor at the seudot (banquet dinners).</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:13; LUKAS 20:47; MATTITYAHU 23:33"> Beware particularly of the ones devouring the batim (houses) of the almanot (widows) and making a grand show of davening long tefillos. Mishpat (Judgment) will fall more heavily on these.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LUKAS 21:1–4; MELACHIM BAIS 12:9; YOCHANAN 8:20"> And having sat &#91;down&#93; opposite the Beis Hamikdash Otzar (Treasury), Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was observing how the multitude throws copper &#91;coins&#93; into the Otzar. And many ashirim (rich people) were throwing &#91;in&#93; much.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="2Kgs 12:9">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And a poor almanah (widow) came and threw &#91;in&#93; two leptas, which is a kodrantes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="These two coins were next to nothing in value, just a few cents.">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:6–8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:29"> And having summoned his talmidim, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them: Omein, I say to you that this poor almanah has put in more tzedakah (charity contribution) than all those contributing to the Otzar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:2–3; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17; EZRA 2:68–69"> For everyone threw in from their abundance, but this almanah from her need has put in everything she had, all her michyah (subsistence).</span>
</div>
