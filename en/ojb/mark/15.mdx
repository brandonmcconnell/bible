<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:1–2; LUKAS 23:1–5; LUKAS 22:66"> And as soon as it was boker, the Rashei Hakohanim (the Chief Priests) with the Zekenim and Sofrim (Scribes) and &#91;the&#93; whole Sanhedrin, having performed the akedah (binding) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, led &#91;him&#93; away and handed &#91;him&#93; over to Pilate.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gn 22:9">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 2:2; MATTITYAHU 27:11–14; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:13"> And Pilate questioned Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Are you the Melech HaYehudim? And, in reply, he says, You say so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:7; MARKOS 15:5; YOCHANAN 18:29–31"> And the Rashei Hakohanim were accusing Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach &#91;of&#93; many things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:10; MATTITYAHU 27:13; MATTITYAHU 26:62"> And Pilate again questioned him, saying, Do you not answer anything? Look how many things they accuse you &#91;of&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:7; ZECHARYAH 3:8; TEHILLIM 71:7"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach no longer answered anything, so Pilate was astounded.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Isa 53:7">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:39–40; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:27"> Now &#91;at&#93; every Chag he was releasing to them one prisoner for whom they were making bakosha (request).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKAS 23:18–19; MATTITYAHU 27:16; LUKAS 23:25"> Now there was the one being called Bar-Abba, who had been imprisoned with his fellow insurrectionists and who, at the time of the Mered (Revolt, Uprising), had committed retzach (murder).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> So the crowd came and began to ask Pilate to do for them just as in the past was his custom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:14–16; YOCHANAN 19:4–5; YOCHANAN 18:39"> But Pilate answered them, saying, Do you wish &#91;that&#93; I should release to you the Melech HaYehudim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MISHLE 27:4; LETTER TO TITOS 3:3; BERESHIS 4:4–6"> For Pilate knew that because of kinah (envy) the Rashei Hakohanim had handed him over to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:14; YOCHANAN 18:40; HOSHEA 5:1"> But the Rashei Hakohanim incited the crowd that Pilate should instead release Bar-Abba to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 23:5–6; YESHAYAH 9:6–7; ZECHARYAH 9:9"> But Pilate, in reply again, was saying to them, What then do you wish &#91;that&#93; I should do with the one whom you call the Melech HaYehudim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And again they cried out, Let him be talui al HaEtz (being hanged on the Tree)!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Dt 21:23">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 23:41; LUKAS 23:23–24; YESHAYAH 53:3"> But Pilate was saying to them, Why? What ra'ah has he committed? But they all the more cried out, Let him be talui al HaEtz (being hanged on the Tree)!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Dt 21:23">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:1; MATTITYAHU 27:26; YESHAYAH 50:6"> So Pilate, desiring to placate the crowd, released to them Bar-Abba, and handed over Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach to be scourged<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="with the flagellum">e</a></sup>and to be talui al HaEtz<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Dt 21:23">f</a></sup>(to be hanged on the Tree).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:9; MATTITYAHU 27:27–31; YOCHANAN 18:28"> And the chaiyalim (soldiers) led away Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach into the courtyard, which is &#91;the&#93; Praetorium, the governorʼs headquarters, and they called together &#91;the&#93; whole cohort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:28–30; YOCHANAN 19:2–5; LUKAS 23:11"> And they clothe him in &#91;royal&#93; purple and place upon him a keter (crown) of thorns that they had woven together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:42–43; MARKOS 15:2; BERESHIS 37:10"> And they began to greet him, Hail, Melech HaYehudim!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARKOS 14:65; YESHAYAH 53:3–5; LUKAS 23:11"> And they were striking him &#91;on&#93; the rosh with a staff and they were spitting on him and bending their knees and bowing down before him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ps 22:8,17">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:16; MATTITYAHU 27:31"> After mocking him, they stripped him of &#91;royal&#93; purple and clothed him in his garments. And they lead him out that they might make him be talui al HaEtz (being hanged on the Tree).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Dt 21:23">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:32; LUKAS 23:26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:13"> And a certain Shim'on from Cyrene was passing by, who was coming in from &#91;the&#93; countryside. He was the father of Alexander and Rufus. The chaiyalim (soldiers) requisition him in order that he carry Moshiachʼs Etz.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Dt 21:23; Isa 53:4-5">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:33–44; YOCHANAN 19:17–27; LUKAS 23:27–38"> And they bring Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach to a place called Gulgolta, which means, being translated, Place of the Skull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:34; YOCHANAN 19:28–30; LUKAS 23:36"> And they were giving to him yayin having been mixed with myrrh, but this one did not take it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ps 69:21[22]; Prov 31:6">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKAS 23:34; YOCHANAN 19:23–24; MATTITYAHU 27:35–36"> And they hanged Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach on HaEtz and YICHALLEKU VEGADAI (they divide the garments) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, and VAPPILU GORAL (they cast lots) for them, to decide what each might take.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ps 22:18[19]">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:14; MARKOS 15:33; MATTITYAHU 27:45"> Now it was &#91;the&#93; third hour when they nailed and hanged Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach on HAETZ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Dt 21:23">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:37; MATTITYAHU 2:2; YOCHANAN 19:18–22"> And the inscription of the charge against him was inscribed above his rosh, melech hayehudim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKAS 23:32–33; MATTITYAHU 27:38; YOCHANAN 19:18"> And with him they also nailed, each to his own etz, shnei shodedim, one on &#91;the&#93; right, one on &#91;the&#93; left of him. &#91;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the Kitvei Hakodesh was fulfilled, VES POSHEIM NIMNAH (and with lawless persons he was numbered).&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MARKOS 14:58; TEHILLIM 109:25; EKHAH 1:12"> And the ones passing by were reviling him and shaking their heads and saying, Ha! The one bringing churban on the Beis HaMikdash and rebuilding &#91;it&#93; in shlosha yamim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Ps 22:7; 109:25">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Save yourself! Come down from the etz!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:23–24; MATTITYAHU 27:41–43; LUKAS 23:35–37"> Likewise, also, the Rashei Hakohanim with the Sofrim were also mocking him, saying, He saved others, but himself he is not able to save.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Ps 22:7">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:44; LUKAS 23:39–43; ZECHARYAH 9:9"> Let the Moshiach, let Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach of Yisroel, let &#91;him&#93; come down now from the etz, in order that we may see and have emunah! And the ones having been hanged on the etz on either side of him were reproaching him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Mk 8:31">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:45; LUKAS 23:44–45; AMOS 8:9–10"> And when the sixth hour came,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="high noon">p</a></sup>it became choshech over kol haaretz until &#91;the&#93; ninth hour.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Am 8:9">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="TEHILLIM 22:1; MATTITYAHU 27:46; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:7"> And at the ninth hour, he cried out in a kol gadol (loud voice), Eloi, Eloi lamah sabachthani? — which means, being translated, ELI ELI LAMAH AZAVTANI? (My G-d, my G-d, why have you forsaken me?)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Ps 22:1">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:47–49; MARKOS 9:11–13; MATTITYAHU 17:11–13"> And some of the ones having been standing nearby and having heard, were saying, Hinei! He calls for Eliyahu HaNavi!</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="TEHILLIM 69:21; MARKOS 15:23; YOCHANAN 19:28–30"> And someone having run and having filled a sponge with CHOMETZ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Ps 69:21">s</a></sup>(vinegar), and having placed it on a staff, gave a drink to him, saying, Leave him alone. Let us see if Eliyahu Hanavi comes to take him down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:30; LUKAS 23:46; MATTITYAHU 27:50"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, having uttered a kol gadol (loud voice) and having breathed his last, expired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:19–23; LUKAS 23:45; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:3–12"> And the Parochet in the Heikhal was torn in two from top to bottom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:54; LUKAS 23:47–48; MATTITYAHU 27:43"> And the centurion, having stood nearby opposite him, and having seen that he expired this way, said Beemes (actually, in truth), this man was the Ben HaElohim!</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LUKAS 23:49; LUKAS 8:2–3; MATTITYAHU 27:55–56"> And there were also nashim (women) looking on from a distance, among whom &#91;were&#93; Miryam from Magdala, and Miryam the Em of the younger Ya'akov and of Yosi, and Shlomit.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Ps 38:11">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LUKAS 8:2–3; MATTITYAHU 27:55–56"> They were with Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach in the Galil and they had been following him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="as talmidat">u</a></sup>and serving him, and there were many other Jewish women who, with Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach, had made their aliyah leregel (pilgrimage) up to Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="LUKAS 23:50–56; YOCHANAN 19:38–42; YOCHANAN 19:31"> And already, erev (evening) fast approaching, vibahlt (since) it was Preparation Day, which is the day before Shabbos,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LUKAS 2:38; LUKAS 2:25; LUKAS 23:51"> Yosef of Ramatayim, a member of the Sanhedrin and a man of chashivut (prominence) there, a man who was also himself looking forward to the Malchut Hashem, had the bold chozek to go into Pilate and ask for the gufat Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:31–37"> And Pilate was amazed that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was already niftar (deceased), and Pilate summoned the centurion to question him whether Yehoshua was niftar already.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:58; YOCHANAN 19:38; MARKOS 15:39"> And having found out from the centurion, he gave the geviyah (body) to Yosef.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Gn 47:18">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:9; MATTITYAHU 27:59–60; YESHAYAH 22:16"> And having bought linen cloth tachrichim (shrouds) and having taken Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach down, he wrapped him in the tachrichim (shrouds) and placed him in a kever which had been cut from rock, and he rolled a stone against the entrance of the kever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MARKOS 15:40; MARKOS 16:1; LUKAS 23:55–2"> And Miryam of Magdala and Miryam the Em of Yosi were observing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="T.N. as chevra kadisha shomrim eye-witnesses">w</a></sup>of where Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach had been laid.</span>
