<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:1–51; YECHEZKEL 11:22–23; YECHEZKEL 10:19"> And as Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was going out from the Beis HaMikdash, one of his talmidim says to him, Hinei! Rebbe, what large stones and impressive binyanim (buildings)!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKAS 21:6; MATTITYAHU 24:2; MELACHIM ALEF 9:7–8"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Do you see these great binyanim? Not one even (stone) will be left resting on another even (stone). All will be thrown down!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:1; MARKOS 4:34; MARKOS 14:33"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was sitting on the Mount of Olives opposite the Beis HaMikdash having a yechidus (private meeting) with Kefa and Ya'akov and Yochanan and Andrew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:6–7; MATTITYAHU 24:3; DANIEL 12:6"> Tell us when these things will be and what &#91;will be&#93; the ot (sign) when all these things are about to be consummated?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:6; YIRMEYAH 29:8"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach began to say to them, Beware lest anyone deceive you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MARKOS 13:22; YIRMEYAH 23:21–25; YOCHANAN 8:24"> For many will come in my name<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., arrogating my title and authority as Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">a</a></sup>saying I am &#91;here&#93;. And they will deceive many.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Jer 29:8">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKAS 21:9–11; YOCHANAN 14:27; MATTITYAHU 24:6–7"> But when you hear of wars and rumors of wars, do not be troubled. It is necessary for these things to occur, but it is not yet HaKetz (the End).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="of the Olam Hazeh">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:8; YESHAYAH 37:3; TEHILLIM 48:6"> For there will be an intifada of ethnic group against ethnic group and malchut against malchut, there will be earthquakes in place after place, &#91;and&#93; there will be famines. These things are but the beginning of the Chevlei.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Moshiach">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:17–18; YOCHANAN 16:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:29"> But as for yourselves, take heed and be careful. For they will hand you over to the sanhedrin and in the shuls you will be beaten and before moshlim (governors) and melachim (kings) you will take your stand for the sake of me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Moshiach">e</a></sup>as a solemn edut (testimony) to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:18"> And it is necessary that the Besuras HaGeulah first be proclaimed to all nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 21:14–15; LUKAS 12:11–12; MATTITYAHU 10:19–21"> And when they arraign you and hand you over for trial, do not be worried beforehand about what you might say; but whatever is given to you in that hour, this you shall say, for it is not you yourselves who are the ones speaking, but the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:21; LUKAS 12:52–53; LUKAS 21:16"> And ach will hand over ach to mavet, and Av &#91;his&#93; own yeled, and yeladim will rise up against horim (parents) and cause them to be condemned with onesh mavet (the death penalty).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Mic 7:6">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:18–19; MATTITYAHU 10:22; MATTITYAHU 24:13"> And everyone will hate you with sinas chinom (baseless hatred) because of my Name.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Yehoshua, Yeshua">g</a></sup>But the one having endured to HaKetz, this one will receive Yeshu'at Eloheinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DANIEL 9:27; DANIEL 12:11; DANIEL 11:31"> And when you see the SHIKUTS MESHOMEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Dan 9:27; 11:31; 12:11">h</a></sup>(the abomination of desolation, the abomination that causes desolation) standing where it ought not — let the one reading this, take heed — then let the ones in Yehudah flee to the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 17:31–33; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:7–8"> And the one on the roof, let him not come down back inside, and let him not enter his bais to take anything away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the one in the sadeh (field) let him not turn back to get his kaftan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EKHAH 4:10; LUKAS 23:29; HOSHEA 9:14"> And oy to the ones with child and the ones with nursing infants in those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But daven tefillos that it may not occur in khoref (winter).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DANIEL 12:1; YOEL 2:2; MATTITYAHU 24:21"> For in those days will be Tzarah Gedolah (Great Tribulation) of such a kind as has not happened from &#91;the&#93; Reshit Yedei HaBriah (Beginning of the Days of Creation) which Hashem created until now, and never again will be.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Dan 9:26; 12:1; Yoel 2:2">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:22; ZECHARYAH 13:8–9; YESHAYAH 65:8–9"> And unless Hashem makes those yamim shortened, no one would be delivered in the Yeshu'at Eloheinu. But for the sake of the Bechirim, whom Hashem chose, he shortened the yamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKAS 21:8; MATTITYAHU 24:23–25; MATTITYAHU 24:5"> And, then, if someone says to you, Hinei! Here &#91;is&#93; the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach! Hinei! There! do not have emunah in what they say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:24; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:8–14; MARKOS 13:6"> For meshichei sheker (false moshiachs) will arise, and neviei sheker (false prophets), and they will perform otot and moftim so as to deceive and lead astray, if possible, the Bechirim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Dt 13:6,7,11,14">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17; MARKOS 13:5; YOCHANAN 14:29"> But you beware. I have forewarned you concerning all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YESHAYAH 13:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:19–20; HISGALUS 6:12–14"> But after the Tzarah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="13:19">k</a></sup>of yamim hahem, the shemesh will be darkened, and the levanah will not give its ohr,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YESHAYAH 34:4; HISGALUS 6:13"> And the kokhavim (stars) will be falling out of Shomayim, and the kochot (powers), the ones in Shomayim, will be shaken.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Isa 13:10; 34:4; Ezek 32:7-8; Am 8:9; Yoel 3:3f">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:7; MATTITYAHU 16:27; MATTITYAHU 24:30"> And then you will see the BAR ENOSH BA BAANANIM (the Son of Man &#91;Moshiach&#93; coming in clouds)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Dan 7:13-14">m</a></sup>with gevurah rabbah (great power) and kavod (glory).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 2:6; MATTITYAHU 24:31; DEVARIM 30:4"> And then he will send the malachim and he will gather together his Bechirim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="13:20">n</a></sup>from the four winds, from the ends (extremities) of haaretz to the ends (extremities) of Shomayim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Zech 2:6">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKAS 21:29–31; MATTITYAHU 24:32–33"> And from the etz teenah (fig tree) learn the mashal: when by that time its branch has become tender and it puts forth the leaves, you have daas that Kayitz is near;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:17–18; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:9; YECHEZKEL 12:25–28"> So also you, when you see these things happening, have daas that it is near, at the doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:34; LUKAS 21:32; MATTITYAHU 16:28"> Omein, I say to you that by no means HaDor HaZeh passes away until all these things take place.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Mk 13:24; Mt 27:45">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:8; YESHAYAH 51:6; MATTITYAHU 24:35"> Shomayim and haaretz will pass away, but the devarim of me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">q</a></sup>will by no means pass away.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Isa 40:8">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:7; MATTITYAHU 24:36–42; MATTITYAHU 25:13"> But concerning HaYom HaHu or the shaah, no one has daas, neither the malachim in Shomayim nor HaBen, but only HaAv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:18; LUKAS 12:40; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:11–12"> Beware, stay shomer (on guard), for you do not have daas when the time is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:14–30; YOCHANAN 10:3; LUKAS 12:36–40"> It is like a man, when departing on a journey and leaving his bais (house) and, having put his avadim (servants) in charge, each with his assigned avodah (work), gives orders to the gatekeeper to be shomer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MARKOS 13:33; MATTITYAHU 24:42; MATTITYAHU 24:44"> Therefore you be shomer, for you do not have daas when the Baal Bayit comes, either late in the yom or at chatzot halailah or at cockcrow or baboker,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:6–7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:11–14; MATTITYAHU 24:48–51"> Lest having come PITOM (suddenly)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Mal 3:1">s</a></sup>he finds you sleeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MARKOS 13:33; LUKAS 12:41–46; MARKOS 13:35"> And what I say to you, I say to all, be shomer.</span>
