<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 23:56–12; YOCHANAN 20:1–10; MATTITYAHU 28:1–10"> And when Shabbos had ended, Miryam of Magdala and Miryam Em of Ya'akov, and Shlomit bought spices in order that they might go and anoint him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:1; LUKAS 24:1; MATTITYAHU 28:1"> And at Shachrit on Yom Rishon, the first day of the shavua (week), the shemesh (sun) just coming up, they come to the kever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MARKOS 15:46–47; MATTITYAHU 27:60–66"> And they were saying to themselves, Who will roll away the stone for us from the entrance of the kever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:2–4; LUKAS 24:2; YOCHANAN 20:1"> And having looked up they observe that the stone, which was extremely large, had already been rolled aside!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:11–12; LUKAS 24:3–5; YOCHANAN 20:8"> And having entered into the kever, they saw a young man enrobed in white, sitting on the right side, and they were shocked with astonishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:17–18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:3–7; LUKAS 24:46"> But he says to them, Do not be alarmed. You seek Yehoshua/Yeshua from Natzeret, who has been made talui al HaEtz (being hanged on the Tree). He has been made to stand up alive. He is not here. Hinei the place where they laid him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MARKOS 14:28; MATTITYAHU 26:32; MATTITYAHU 28:10"> But go tell his talmidim and Kefa that he goes before you into the Galil. There you will see him, just as he told you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Mt 26:32">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKAS 24:22–24; LUKAS 24:9–11; MARKOS 16:5–6"> And having gone out, they fled from the kever, and trembling and amazement seized them. And they told no one anything, for they were afraid.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:14–19; LUKAS 8:2; YOCHANAN 20:12"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The following verses are an integral portion of the inspired record delivered from early times to the Kehillah of Hashem and should be feared as the canonical, inspired and inerrant Word of G-d.">b</a></sup>And now after Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach stood up alive early on Yom Rishon he appeared rishonah (first) to Miryam of Magdala, from whom he had cast out shiva shedim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:18; LUKAS 24:17; YOCHANAN 16:6"> She went out and announced to the ones who had been with Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach while they were in avelut (mourning) and weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 24:11; MARKOS 16:13–14; MARKOS 9:19"> And those who heard that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach lives and he was seen by her, refused to have emunah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKAS 24:13–32; YOCHANAN 21:14; YOCHANAN 21:1"> And after these things, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach appeared in another form to two of them walking along into the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:25; LUKAS 24:33–35; YOCHANAN 20:8"> And those went and reported to the rest, but the rest did not have emunah either.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:5; LUKAS 24:36–43; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:7–8"> But later Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach appeared to the Achad Asar (The Eleven), themselves reclining at tish, and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach reproached them for their lack of emunah and the KESHI<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Dt 9:27">c</a></sup>(stubbornness, hardness) of their levavot (hearts), because they had not believed those who saw Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach after he had been made to stand up alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:19; MARKOS 13:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:8"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Go into kol haOlam (all the World), and proclaim the Besuras haGeulah to all HaBriah (the Creation).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:25–26; YOCHANAN 3:36; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:9–14"> The one having had emunah (faith) and having submitted to a tevilah of teshuva (immersion of repentance) will be delivered in the Yeshu'at Eloheinu (Salvation of our G-d), but the one not having emunah will come under the gezar din (verdict) of harshaah (condemnation as guilty).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:7"> And these otot (signs) will accompany those that have emunah. BiShmi (In my Name) they will cast out shedim (demons); they will speak with leshonot chadashot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ac 2:4; 10:45-46">d</a></sup>(new tongues);</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKAS 10:19; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:14–15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:3–6"> And with their hands they will pick up nechashim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ac 28:3-5">e</a></sup>(snakes); and, if any deadly poison they drink, it will in no way harm them; upon &#91;the&#93; cholim (sick people) they will lay their hands and they will bring them refuah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:22; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:2"> And then Adoneinu Yehoshua, after speaking to them, was taken up into Shomayim V'YASHAV LIMIN HASHEM (and sat down at the right hand of Hashem).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ps 110:1">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:4–5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:19"> And those having gone forth preached the Hachrazah (Proclamation, Kerygma) everywhere, &#91;while&#93; Adonoi was working with them, confirming the Besuras HaGeulah through the accompanying otot (signs). Omein.</span>
</div>
