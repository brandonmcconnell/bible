<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEVARIM 33:3; YOCHANAN 10:27–30; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5"> All this I took to my lev, explaining it all, that the tzaddikim, and the chachamim, and their works, are in the yad HaElohim; adam does not have da'as of whether it will be ahavah or sinah; all lies before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MALACHI 3:18; KOHELET 2:14–16; MALACHI 3:5"> All things come alike to all; there is one mikreh (fortune) to the tzaddik, and to the rasha; to the tov and to the tahor, and to the tameh; to him that sacrificeth, and to him that sacrificeth not; as is the tov, so is the choteh; and he that sweareth, as he that feareth a shevuah (an oath).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 17:9; KOHELET 8:11; KOHELET 1:17"> This is a rah among all things that are done under the shemesh, that there is one mikreh (fortune) unto all; yea, also the lev of the bnei haAdam is full of rah, and holelot (madness) is in their lev while they live, and after that they go to the mesim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="IYOV 14:7–12; YESHAYAH 38:18; LUKAS 16:26–29"> For to him that is joined to all the living there is bitachon; for a kelev chai (living dog) is better than an aryeh hamet (dead lion).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YESHAYAH 26:14; KOHELET 1:11; TEHILLIM 6:5"> For the living have da'as that they shall die; but the mesim do not have da'as of anything, neither have they any more a sachar, for the memory of them is forgotten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 146:3–4; KOHELET 6:12; IYOV 3:17–18"> Also their ahavah, and their sinah, and their kina, is now perished; neither have they any more a chelek l'olam in any thing that is done under the shemesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="KOHELET 3:12–13; DEVARIM 12:7; KOHELET 10:19"> Go thy way, eat thy lechem with simchah, and drink thy yayin with a lev tov; for HaElohim now accepteth thy ma'asim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:14; HISGALUS 3:4–5; HISGALUS 19:8"> Let thy garments be always white; and let thy head lack no shemen (ointment).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MISHLE 5:18–19; KOHELET 2:10; MALACHI 2:15"> Live joyfully with the isha whom thou lovest all the days of thy chayyei hevel, which he hath given thee under the shemesh, all the days of thy hevel; for that is thy chelek in this life, and in thy amal (toil) which thou laborest under the shemesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:11"> Whatsoever thy hand findeth to do, do it with thy koach; for there is no ma'aseh, nor cheshbon, nor da'as, nor chochmah in Sheol, whither thou goest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 9:23; MISHLE 21:30–31; EKHAH 3:37–38"> I returned, and saw under the shemesh, that the race is not to the swift, nor the battle to the gibborim, neither yet lechem to the chachamim, nor yet osher to the intelligent, nor yet chen (favor) to the experts; but et (time) and pega (chance) happeneth to them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:3; LUKAS 21:34–36; KOHELET 8:5–7"> For haAdam also hath no da'as of his et (time, hour);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="but see Yn 13:1; 8:20; 7:30; 2:4regarding the Moshiachʼs knowledge of his hour">a</a></sup>as the dagim that are caught in a metzodah rah (evil net), and as the birds that are trapped in the pach (snare), so are the Bnei HaAdam snared in an et ra'ah (evil time), when it falleth suddenly upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="KOHELET 8:16; KOHELET 7:15; KOHELET 6:1"> This chochmah have I seen also under the shemesh, and it seemed gedolah (great) unto me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 20:15–22; MELACHIM BAIS 6:24–20"> There was an ir ketanah (little city), and few men within it; and there came a melech gadol against it, and besieged it, and built metzorim gedolim (huge siegeworks) against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="KOHELET 4:13; BERESHIS 40:23; ESTHER 6:2–3"> Now there was found in it a poor chacham, and he by his chochmah delivered the city; yet no man remembered that same poor man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="KOHELET 7:19; MISHLE 24:5; MISHLE 21:22"> Then said I, Chochmah is better than gevurah (strength); nevertheless the poor manʼs chochmah is despised, and his devarim are not heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17–18; MISHLE 28:23; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:20"> Divrei chachamim in quiet are more to be heeded than the shouting of the moshel (one ruling) among kesilim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 7:11–12; KOHELET 9:16; YEHOSHUA 7:5"> Chochmah is better than weapons of war, but one choteh destroyeth much good.</span>
