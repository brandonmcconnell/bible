<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="KOHELET 10:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:8–9; DANIEL 4:35"> Be not hasty to go out of his sight; stand not in a rah (an evil matter); for he executeth whatsoever pleaseth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="IYOV 9:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:1–4; DANIEL 4:35"> For the devar melech is shilton (supreme), and who may say unto him, What doest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:14; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:13–14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:9–10"> Whoso is shomer mitzvah shall meet no harm; and a lev of a chacham discerneth both et (time) and mishpat (judgment).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="KOHELET 3:1; KOHELET 3:17; KOHELET 3:11"> Because every matter has its et (time) and mishpat (judgment), though the ra'at haAdam be great upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="KOHELET 10:14; KOHELET 9:12; MISHLE 24:22"> For he has no da'as of that which shall be; for who can tell him how it will be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 89:48; IYOV 14:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:27"> There is no adam that hath power over the ruach to restrain ruach; neither hath he shilton (power) over the yom hamavet; and there is no discharge in war; neither shall resha deliver its possessor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="KOHELET 5:8; KOHELET 4:7–8; KOHELET 5:13"> All this have I seen, and applied my lev unto every ma'aseh (labor, work) that has been done under the shemesh wherein one adam ruleth over another adam to the otherʼs hurt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="KOHELET 9:5; KOHELET 2:16; MISHLE 10:7"> And so I saw the resha'im buried, who had come and gone out of the mekom kadosh (the holy place), and they were forgotten in the city where they had done such things; this is also hevel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 26:10; TEHILLIM 50:21–22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:4–5"> Because pitgam (sentence) against a ma'aseh hara'ah (an evil work) is not executed speedily, therefore the lev of the bnei haAdam is fully set in them to do rah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="TEHILLIM 37:11; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9; LUKAS 1:50"> Though a choteh do rah an hundred times, and his yamim be prolonged, yet surely I know that it shall be well with them that fear HaElohim, which fear before Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="KOHELET 6:12; YESHAYAH 3:11; IYOV 14:2"> But it shall not be well with the rasha, neither shall he lengthen his yamim like a tzel (shadow); because he feareth not before Elohim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="KOHELET 7:15; KOHELET 2:14; MALACHI 3:15"> There is a hevel which is done upon ha'aretz; that there be tzaddikim, unto whom it happeneth according to the ma'aseh haresha'im; again, there be resha'im, to whom it happeneth according to the ma'aseh hatzaddikim; I say that this also is hevel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="KOHELET 2:24; KOHELET 5:18; KOHELET 3:12–13"> Then I commended simchah, because a man hath no better thing under the shemesh, than to eat, and to drink, and to have simchah; for that shall accompany him in his amal the yamim of his life, which HaElohim giveth him under the shemesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="KOHELET 1:13; TEHILLIM 127:2; KOHELET 2:23"> When I applied mine lev to have da'as of chochmah, and to see the business that is done upon ha'aretz, though oneʼs eynayim see sleep neither yom nor lailah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="KOHELET 3:11; TEHILLIM 73:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:33"> then I beheld kol ma'aseh HaElohim, that haAdam cannot comprehend the ma'aseh that is done under the shemesh; because though haAdam labor to seek it out, yet he shall not comprehend it; moreover, though a chacham claim to have da'as of it, yet shall he not be able to comprehend it.</span>
</div>
