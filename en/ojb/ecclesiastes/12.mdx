<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EKHAH 3:27; KOHELET 11:8; TEHILLIM 71:17–18"> Remember now thy Bo're (Creator) in the yamim of thy bechurot, while the yamei hara'ah come not, nor the shanim draw nigh, when thou shalt say, I have no chefetz (pleasure) in them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YESHAYAH 5:30; SHMUEL ALEF 4:18; SHMUEL ALEF 4:15"> While the shemesh, or the ohr, or the yarei'ach, or the kokhavim, be not darkened, nor the clouds return after the rain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TEHILLIM 90:9–10; TEHILLIM 102:23; SHMUEL BAIS 21:15–17"> In the yom when the shomrei habayit shall tremble, and the strong men shall stoop, and the grinders cease because they are few, and those that look out of the windows grow dim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 25:10; SHMUEL BAIS 19:35; HISGALUS 18:22"> And the delatayim (doors) shall be shut in the street, when the sound of the grinding fades, and he shall arise at the sound of a tzipor (bird), and all their banot hashir (daughters of song) are brought low;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="IYOV 17:13; YIRMEYAH 9:17–20; IYOV 30:23"> Also when they shall be afraid of height, and fears shall be in the derech, and the almond tree shall blossom, and the grasshopper drags himself along, and desire shall fail; because haAdam goeth to his bais olam (eternal home), and the mourners go about the streets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 4:2–3"> Or ever the silver cord be loosed, or the golden bowl be broken, or the pitcher be broken at the fountain, or the wheel broken at the bor (cistern).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DANIEL 12:2; KOHELET 3:20–21; TEHILLIM 146:4"> Then shall the aphar return to ha'aretz as it was; and the ruach shall return unto HaElohim who gave it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="KOHELET 1:2; KOHELET 8:8; KOHELET 1:14"> Hevel havalim, saith Kohelet; all is hevel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 4:32; MISHLE 1:1; MELACHIM ALEF 10:8"> And moreover, because Kohelet was chacham, he taught the people da'as; yea, he applied, and searched out, and set in order many meshalim (proverbs).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:5; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:15; MISHLE 16:21–24"> Kohelet searched to find out divrei chefetz; and that which was written was yosher, even divrei emes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:37; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:12; YECHEZKEL 34:23"> Divrei chachamim are like goads, and like nails firmly fixed are the collected sayings that are given by Ro'eh Echad (One Shepherd).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="KOHELET 1:18; YOCHANAN 21:25; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19–21"> And further, by these, beni, be admonished; of making many sefarim there is no ketz; and much study is a weariness of the basar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:12; MICHOH 6:8; DEVARIM 6:2"> Let us hear the sof (conclusion) of the whole matter; Fear HaElohim, and of His commandments be shomer mitzvot; for this is the whole duty of haAdam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:16; MATTITYAHU 12:36"> For HaElohim shall bring kol ma'aseh (every work) into mishpat (judgment), with every ne'lam (secret thing, concealed thing), whether it be tov (good), or whether it be rah (evil).</span>
</div>
