<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="KOHELET 3:16; KOHELET 5:8; YESHAYAH 5:7"> So I returned, and considered all the oppressions that are done under the shemesh, and, hinei, the tears of such as were oppressed, and they had no menachem (comforter); and on the side of their oppressors there was ko'ach (power); but they had no menachem (comforter).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="IYOV 3:11–26; KOHELET 2:17; KOHELET 9:4–6"> Wherefore I praised the mesim which are already dead more than the living ones which are yet alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKAS 23:29; KOHELET 6:3–5; MATTITYAHU 24:19"> Yea, better is he than both they, which hath not yet been, who hath not seen the ma'aseh harah that is done under the shemesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:12; KOHELET 1:14; KOHELET 2:26"> Again, I considered all amal, and every kishron hama'aseh (skillful achievement), that such is a simple derivative of kinat ish meirei'eihu (the envy of man of his neighbor). This also is hevel, a chasing after ruach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YESHAYAH 9:20; MISHLE 13:4; MISHLE 6:10–11"> The kesil foldeth his hands together, and eateth his own basar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MISHLE 16:8; MISHLE 15:16–17; TEHILLIM 37:16"> Better is a handful with tranquility than both the hands full with amal and chasing after ruach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="KOHELET 4:1; TEHILLIM 78:33; ZECHARYAH 1:6"> Then I returned, and I saw under the shemesh this hevel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:16; MISHLE 27:20; LUKAS 12:20"> There was a man all alone, and there was with him neither ben nor ach (brother); yet there was no ketz (end) to all his amal; neither was his ayin satisfied with osher (riches); neither saith he, For whom do I toil, and deprive my nefesh of tovah? This also is hevel, yea, it is an evil matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MISHLE 27:17; BERESHIS 2:18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:18–21"> Two are better than one because they have a sachar tov for their amal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:18; YESHAYAH 35:3–4"> For if they fall, the echad will lift up his partner, but woe to him that is alone when he falleth; for he hath not another to help him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:1–4"> Also, if two lie down, then they have chom (heat), but how can one be warm alone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DANIEL 3:16–17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:3; SHMUEL BAIS 23:9"> Though echad may be overpowered, shnayim shall withstand him; and a khoot hameshulash (threefold cord) is not quickly broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 16:9–10; MISHLE 19:1"> Better is a poor and a wise yeled than an old and foolish melech, who will no more be admonished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 113:7–8; BERESHIS 41:14; IYOV 5:11"> For out of the bais hasohar he cometh to reign, although he was born a poor man in his malchut (kingdom).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:6"> I considered all the living ones which walk under the shemesh in the throng of the yeled, that is, the second one that shall enter into the place of the former.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="melech">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="KOHELET 1:14; MELACHIM ALEF 1:40; KOHELET 2:17"> There is no ketz of kol haAm, all those at whose head he stands; and yet they who come after shall not rejoice in him. Surely this also is hevel and striving for ruach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Keep thy footing when thou goest to the Bais HaElohim, and be more ready to listen than to give the zevach of kesilim, for they have no da'as that they do rah.</span>
