<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:28–29; MISHLE 15:8"> Be not rash with thy mouth, and let not thine lev be hasty to utter any thing before HaElohim; for HaElohim is in Shomayim, and thou upon ha'aretz; therefore let thy devarim be few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:7; MISHLE 10:19; YESHAYAH 55:9"> As by a multitude of cares cometh a chalom, so by a multitude of devarim cometh the kol kesil (the voice of the fool).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MISHLE 15:2; MISHLE 10:19; KOHELET 10:12–14"> When thou vowest a neder unto Elohim, defer not to pay it; for Hashem hath no pleasure in kesilim; pay that which thou hast vowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 30:2; DEVARIM 23:21–23; TEHILLIM 76:11"> Better it is that thou shouldest not make a neder, than that thou shouldest vow and not fulfill the neder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:4; DEVARIM 23:22; MISHLE 20:25"> Suffer not thy mouth to lead thy basar into chet (sin); neither say thou before the Malach &#91;of G-d&#93;, that it was a mistake. Wherefore should HaElohim be angry at thy voice, and destroy the work of thine hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:26; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:2"> For in the multitude of chalomot and many devarim there are also divers havalim;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="see 1:2">a</a></sup>but fear thou HaElohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="KOHELET 12:13; MATTITYAHU 12:36; KOHELET 8:12"> If thou seest the oppression of the poor, and violent perverting of mishpat and tzedek in a province, marvel not at the matter; for he that is higher watches over him that is high; and there be higher than they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 12:5; KOHELET 4:1; KOHELET 3:16"> Moreover the profit of eretz is for all; Melech himself is served by the sadeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 4:7–23; TEHILLIM 115:16; BERESHIS 1:29–30"> He that loveth kesef shall never have enough kesef; nor shall he that loveth abundance have enough increase; this also is hevel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:10; MATTITYAHU 6:24; LUKAS 12:15"> When hatovah (good things) increase, they are increased that consume them; and what kishron (useful result) is there to the ba'al (owner) thereof, except as an onlooker?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:16; CHABAKUK 2:13; TEHILLIM 119:36–37"> The sleep of the oved (working man) is sweet, whether he eat little or much; but the abundance of the oisher (rich man) will not permit him to sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MISHLE 3:24; TEHILLIM 4:8; YIRMEYAH 31:26"> There is a grievous ra'ah which I have seen under the shemesh; osher (riches) hoarded by the ba'al thereof to his hurt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="KOHELET 6:1–2; LUKAS 18:22–23; BERESHIS 19:14"> The same osher (riches) perish by an evil event and he begetteth a ben; thus this one hath nothing in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:19–20; CHAGGAI 2:16–17; MISHLE 23:5"> As he came forth of the womb of his Em (mother), arom (naked) shall he return to go as he came, and shall take nothing of his amal (labor), which he may carry away in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 49:17; IYOV 1:21; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:7"> And this also is a grievous evil, that in every respect as he came, just so shall he go; and what profit hath he that hath toiled for the ruach (wind)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MISHLE 11:29; KOHELET 1:3; YOCHANAN 6:27"> All his yamim also he eateth in choshech, and he hath much ka'as (grief, vexation) and sickness and anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TEHILLIM 127:2; YECHEZKEL 4:16–17; TEHILLIM 90:7–11"> Hinei I have seen it is tov and yafeh for one to eat and to drink, and to find tovah in all his amal (labor) that he toils under the shemesh all the few days of his life, which HaElohim giveth him; for it is his chelek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="KOHELET 2:24; KOHELET 3:12–13; KOHELET 3:22"> Also kol ha'adam to whom HaElohim hath given osher and possessions, and hath given him power to eat thereof, and to take his chelek, and to rejoice in his amal; this is the gift of Elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 8:18; KOHELET 3:13; KOHELET 6:2"> For he shall not much remember the days of his life; because HaElohim keeps him occupied with simchat libbo (gladness of his heart).</span>
