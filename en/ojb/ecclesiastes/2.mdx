<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 12:19; KOHELET 8:15; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:5"> I said in mine lev, Come now, I will test thee with simchah, therefore enjoy tov; and, hinei, this also is hevel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MISHLE 14:13; KOHELET 7:2–6; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:2–4"> I said of laughter, It is mad; and of simchah, What doeth it accomplish?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MISHLE 20:1; KOHELET 1:17; MISHLE 31:4–5"> I searched in mine lev to draw my basar on with yayin, my mind guiding me with chochmah; and to lay hold on sichlut (folly), till I might see what was tov for bnei haAdam, which they should do under Shomayim all the few days of their life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:1–12; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 26:10"> I undertook me great works; I built me batim (houses); I planted me kramim (vineyards);</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKAS 17:27–29; SHIR HASHIRIM 4:12–1; YIRMEYAH 39:4"> I made me ganot (gardens) and parks, and I planted all kinds of fruit trees in them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 2:14; TEHILLIM 1:3; YIRMEYAH 17:8"> I made me reservoirs of mayim, to water therewith the forest of trees tzomeiach (sprouting up);</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 3:4; BERESHIS 15:3; MELACHIM ALEF 4:23"> I bought me avadim and shfakhot, and had bnei bayit (avadim born in my bais); also I had great possessions of herds and tzon more than all that were in Yerushalayim before me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 10:10; MELACHIM ALEF 9:28; SHMUEL BAIS 19:35"> I amassed for me also kesef and zahav, and the treasure of melachim and of the provinces; I acquired for me men singers and women singers, and the delights of bnei haAdam, musical instruments of all kinds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:25; KOHELET 1:16; MELACHIM ALEF 10:23"> So I was great, and excelled more than all that were before me in Yerushalayim; also my chochmah remained with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="KOHELET 9:9; KOHELET 5:18; KOHELET 3:22"> And whatsoever mine eyes desired I kept not from them, I withheld not my lev from any simchah; for my lev rejoiced in all my amal (labor); and this was my chelek (portion) for all my amal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="KOHELET 1:14; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:16–17; KOHELET 1:3"> Then I looked on kol ma'asim that my hands had wrought, and on the amal that I had labored to do; and, hinei, all was hevel and chasing after ruach, and there was no profit under the shemesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="KOHELET 1:17; KOHELET 7:25; KOHELET 1:9–10"> And I turned myself to consider chochmah, and holelot, and sichlut; for what can the adam do who cometh after HaMelech? Even that which hath been done already.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:8; KOHELET 7:11–12; MATTITYAHU 6:23"> Then I saw that chochmah excelleth sichlut, as far as ohr excelleth choshech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 49:10; MISHLE 17:24; KOHELET 9:11"> The chacham (wise man) hath eyes in his rosh; but the kesil (fool) walketh in choshech; but I myself perceived also that the same mikreh (fortune) happeneth to them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="KOHELET 6:8; KOHELET 1:14; KOHELET 1:2"> Then said I in my lev, As the mikreh befalls the kesil, so also will the same mikreh befall me; and wherein have I then been of more chochmah? Then I said in my lev, This also is hevel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="KOHELET 1:11; KOHELET 9:5; TEHILLIM 88:12"> For no zichron (remembrance) of the chacham — no less the kesil — remains l'olam; seeing that in hayamim haba'im (the days to come) all shall be forgotten. How can the chacham die just like the kesil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="KOHELET 2:11; YONAH 4:8; KOHELET 4:2"> Therefore I hated HaChayyim (Life); because the ma'aseh that is wrought under the shemesh is grievous unto me; for all is hevel and chasing after ruach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="TEHILLIM 39:6; TEHILLIM 49:10; LUKAS 16:27–28"> Yea, I hated all my amal for which I had toiled under the shemesh; because I must leave it unto the adam that shall occupy my place after me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17; KOHELET 3:22; KOHELET 9:13"> And who hath da'as whether he shall be a chacham or a kesil? Nevertheless shall he have shlitah (control, authority) over all my amal for which I have toiled, and have poured out my chochmah under the shemesh. This also is hevel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 43:14; IYOV 17:11–15; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:8–10"> Therefore I turned aside to give up my lev to despair over all the wearisome amal with which I toiled under the shemesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="KOHELET 2:17–18; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 31:20–21; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:2"> For there is an adam whose amal is with chochmah, and da'as, and kishron (skill); yet to an adam that hath not worked for it he must leave it for his chelek. This also is hevel and a ra'ah rabbah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="TEHILLIM 127:2; KOHELET 1:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:6"> For what hath adam for all his amal, and for all the striving of his lev, wherein he hath labored under the shemesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="IYOV 14:1; IYOV 5:7; TEHILLIM 6:6–7"> For all his yamim are machovim (sorrows), and his travail ka'as (grief, vexation); yea, his lev taketh not rest balailah. This also is hevel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="KOHELET 3:22; KOHELET 8:15; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:17"> There is nothing better for adam, than that he should eat and drink, and that he should make his nefesh find satisfaction in his amal. Also this I saw; even this was from the Yad HaElohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="KOHELET 2:1–12; MELACHIM ALEF 4:21–24"> For who can eat, or who can find enjoyment without Hashem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MISHLE 13:22; IYOV 27:16–17; MISHLE 28:8"> For to the adam that is pleasing in His sight, Hashem gives chochmah, and da'as, and simchah; but to the choteh (sinner) Hashem giveth travail; Hashem giveth the work of gathering and storing up, that Hashem may give to him that pleases HaElohim. This also is hevel and chasing after ruach.</span>
</div>
