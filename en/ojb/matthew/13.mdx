<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 8:4–10; MATTITYAHU 13:1–15; MARKOS 4:1–12"> On that day, having gone out of the bais, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach sat beside the lake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKAS 5:3; MATTITYAHU 4:25; MARKOS 4:1"> And many multitudes gathered together to him so that he got into a sirah (boat) to sit down, and the entire multitude stood along the shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MARKOS 4:2–9; MARKOS 4:13; TEHILLIM 78:2"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach told them many things in meshalim (parables), saying Hinei! The Sower went out to sow.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Seeds">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:18–19"> And while he sowed, on the one hand, this<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Seed">b</a></sup>fell along the road, and the birds having come, devoured them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 11:19; YECHEZKEL 36:26; ZECHARYAH 7:12"> And others fell upon the rocky places, where there is not much soil, and immediately it sprouts on account of the lack of the soilʼs depth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:21; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:11–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:23"> And when the shemesh (sun) arose, the zera (seed) was scorched, and because it did not have a root, it withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MARKOS 4:18–19; YIRMEYAH 4:3–4; BERESHIS 3:18"> And others fell among the thorns, and the thorns grew up and choked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:23; LUKAS 8:15; BERESHIS 26:12"> But others fell on the adamah tovah (good ground), and they yielded fruit: the one, one hundred, the other, sixty, the other, thirty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:11; MATTITYAHU 11:15; HISGALUS 2:7"> The one having oznayim (spiritual ears), let him hear!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MARKOS 4:33–34; MARKOS 4:10"> And, approaching, the talmidim said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Why in meshalim (parables) are you speaking to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:9–10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:14; MATTITYAHU 19:11"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach answered them, Because to you it has been granted to have daas of the razim (mysteries) of the Malchut HaShomayim, but to those it has not been granted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:29; MARKOS 4:24–25; LUKAS 8:18"> For whoever has,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="more">c</a></sup>will be given to him, and he will have an abundance. But whoever does not have, even what he has will be taken from him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="see Mt 25:14-29">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 12:2; YIRMEYAH 5:21; YESHAYAH 42:18–20"> For this reason in meshalim I am speaking to them, for while seeing they do not see, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="while">e</a></sup>hearing they do not hear, nor do they have binah (understanding).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YESHAYAH 6:9–10; YECHEZKEL 12:2; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:14"> And in them is fulfilled the nevuah (prophecy) of Yeshayah HaNavi, saying, SHIMU SHAMOA VAL TAVINU UREU RAO VAL TEIDAU (In hearing you will hear and by no means understand, and seeing you will see and by no means perceive).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YESHAYAH 6:10; MARKOS 4:12; ZECHARYAH 7:11"> HASHMEIN LEV HAAM HAZEH VAZNAV HACHBEID VEINAV HASHA, PEN YIREH VEINAV UVEAZNAV YISHMAH ULEVAVO YAVIN, VSHAV NRAFAH LOH.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isa 6:9-10">f</a></sup>(For the heart of this people has been made dull, and &#91;their&#93; ears are hard of hearing, and their eyes are shut, lest they see with the their eyes and with &#91;their&#93; ears they hear, and with the lev (heart) they understand and they turn and I will give them refuah &#91;healing&#93;).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 10:23–24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:17–18; MATTITYAHU 16:17"> But ashrey are your eyes, for they see, and your ears, for they hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:56; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:13; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:10–12"> For truly I say to you that many Neviim and tzaddikim desired to see what you see, and they did not see,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="it">g</a></sup>and to hear what you hear, and they did not hear it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:11–12; LUKAS 8:11–15; MARKOS 4:13–20"> You, therefore, listen to the mashal of the sower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKAS 8:11–15; MATTITYAHU 13:38; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:1"> When anyone hears the Dvar HaMalchut and does not have binah, HaRah (the Evil one) comes and seizes that which was sown in his lev (heart). This is the zera (seed) sown along the path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 33:31–32; TEHILLIM 106:12–13; YOCHANAN 5:35"> And the zera sown upon the rocky places is the person listening to the Dvar Hashem and immediately with simcha receives it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:6; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8–9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:17"> Yet he has no root in himself but is short-lived, and when ES TZARAH comes or persecution on account of the Dvar Hashem, immediately he ceases being a maamin Meshichi (Messianic believer) and becomes meshummad (apostate), falling away and giving up the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="true Orthodox Jewish">h</a></sup>faith.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Jer 30:7">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:17; LUKAS 8:14; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:9–10"> And the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="zera">j</a></sup>sown among the thorns is the one hearing the dvar (word), and the rogez HaOlam Hazeh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Dt 28:65">k</a></sup>(the anxiety of this age) and the mirmah (deceit) of riches, choke the Dvar Hashem and it becomes unfruitful.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Isa 53:9">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:11; MATTITYAHU 12:33; YOCHANAN 15:16"> And the zera sown upon the adamah tovah (the good ground), this is the one who hears the Dvar Hashem and, understanding,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="it">m</a></sup>indeed bears pri and, one produces a hundred, the other sixty, the other thirty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:1; MATTITYAHU 13:47; MATTITYAHU 20:1"> Another mashal Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach placed before them, saying, The Malchut HaShomayim is like a man sowing zera tov (good seed) in his field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:8; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:30–31"> But while men slept, his oyev (enemy) came and oversowed weeds in between the wheat and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MARKOS 4:26–29"> But when the wheat sprouted and produced pri, then the weeds also appeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:17; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:1; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:18–20"> So the servants of the Baal Bayit said to him, Adoneinu, did you not sow zera tov (good seed) in your field? How then does it have weeds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:3–7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:14; LUKAS 9:49–54"> And he said to them, An oyev did this. So the servants say to him, Do you want us to go and pull them all?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> But he says, No, lest gathering the weeds you should uproot the wheat together with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 3:12; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:5"> Permit both to grow together until the Katzir (harvest); and in time of the Katzir, I will say to the kotzerim, Collect first the weeds, and bind them into bundles to burn them. But the wheat gather into my storehouse.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LUKAS 13:18–19; MATTITYAHU 17:20; MARKOS 4:30–32"> Another mashal Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach placed before them, saying, The Malchut HaShomayim is like a mustard seed, which a man took and sowed in his field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 31:6; DANIEL 4:12; YECHEZKEL 17:23–24"> This that is indeed less than all the zeraim (seeds), but when it grows, it is larger than the garden vegetables and it becomes an etz (tree), so that the OPH HASHOMAYIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Job 35:11">n</a></sup>come and dwell in its branches.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:6–7; LUKAS 13:21"> Another mashal Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach spoke to them. The Malchut HaShomayim is like seor (leaven), which having taken, a woman hid in three satas of wheat flour until the whole was leavened.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MARKOS 4:33–34; YOCHANAN 16:25; MATTITYAHU 13:13"> All these things Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach spoke in meshalim to the multitudes. And apart from meshalim Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach did not speak to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:9; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:20–21"> So that might be fulfilled what was spoken through the Navi, saying, EFTCHA VMASHAL PI AVIAH CHIDOT (I will open my mouth with parables, I will utter things having been hidden) from the foundation of the world (Ps 78:2).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:17–20; MATTITYAHU 13:11; MATTITYAHU 9:28"> Then having sent away the multitudes, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach came into the bais (house). And Moshiachʼs talmidim approached him, saying, Explain to us the mashal of the weeds of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:5–7; MATTITYAHU 13:41; LUKAS 10:16"> And answering, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, The Sower of the zera tov is the Bar Enosh (Moshiach).</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:44; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:18–19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:10"> The field is HaOlam Hazeh. And the zera tov, the good seed, these are the Bnei HaMalchut, and the weeds, these are the bnei HaRah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Gn 3:15">o</a></sup>(sons of the Evil one).</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:49; HISGALUS 14:15–19; MATTITYAHU 25:31"> And the Oyev (the Enemy) sowing them is HaSatan. And the Katzir (Harvest), this is HaKetz HaOlam (the end of the age). And the kotzerim (reapers, harvesters) are malachim (angels).</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:39"> As the weeds are pulled up and gathered and are consumed with Eish (Fire), so also it will be at the Ketz HaOlam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:31; MATTITYAHU 18:7; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1–2"> The Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Moshiach">p</a></sup>will send forth his malachim, and they will pull up and gather out of the Moshiachʼs Malchut all the things making meshummad (apostate) and the ones who are without gezetz (law) and antinomian.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="see Ro 6:1-2">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:50; LUKAS 16:23–24; MATTITYAHU 8:12"> And Moshiachʼs malachim will throw them into the furnace of Eish; there will be weeping and grinding of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="DANIEL 12:3; MATTITYAHU 11:15; LUKAS 12:32"> Then the tzaddikim will shine as the shemesh (sun) in the Malchut of their Father. The one having oznayim (spiritual ears), let him hear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MISHLE 2:2–5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:7–9"> The Malchut HaShomayim is like otzar (treasure) hidden in the field, which, having found, a man hid. And from the simcha he experienced, he goes away and sells everything he has and buys that field.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MISHLE 3:13–18; MATTITYAHU 16:26; MATTITYAHU 13:24"> Again, the Malchut HaShomayim is like a merchant searching for fine pearls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:8; LUKAS 18:28–30; MISHLE 2:4"> And having found one precious peninah (pearl), he went away and liquidated everything he had and acquired it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:6; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:2–5; MATTITYAHU 22:9–10"> Again, the Malchut HaShomayim is like a reshet (net) having been cast into the lake, a reshet collecting and gathering dagim (fish) of all descriptions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:30; MATTITYAHU 13:40–43; MATTITYAHU 3:12"> which, when this reshet (net) was filled, they hoisted it upon the shore, sat down, collected the tov (good) into a creel, and the rah (evil), they threw out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="HISGALUS 20:12–15; MATTITYAHU 24:31; MATTITYAHU 13:39"> Thus it will be at HaKetz HaOlam Hazeh (The End of This World). The malachim will go out and they will separate the resha'im from among the tzaddikim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:42; MATTITYAHU 8:12; HISGALUS 14:10–11"> And they will throw the resha'im into the furnace of Eish. In that place there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:19; MATTITYAHU 13:11; MARKOS 4:34"> Did you have binah of all these things? They say to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Ken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:7–8; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:16–17; LETTER TO TITOS 1:9"> So Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Therefore, every sofer (scribe, torah teacher, rabbi) who becomes a talmid of the Malchut HaShomayim is like a man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="who is">r</a></sup>a Baal Bayit, who takes out of his otzar (treasure), chadashot (new things) and also yeshanot (old things).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:28; MARKOS 4:33–35"> And it came about when Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach finished these meshalim, that he went away from there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:28; YOCHANAN 7:15–16; MARKOS 6:1–6"> And having come into Moshiachʼs shtetl, he began ministering as a moreh (teacher) in their shul, so that they were amazed and said, From where did this chochmah come to this one, this chochmah and these moftim (miracles, wonders, omens)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:42; MARKOS 6:3; LUKAS 3:23"> Is this not the ben hanaggar (the carpenterʼs son)? Is not his Em called Miryam? And are not his achim Ya'akov,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="T.N. This is the author of the one of the Igrot Kodesh called Ya'akov.">s</a></sup>Yosef, Shim'on and Yehuda?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="See one of the Igrot Kodesh called Yehuda, of which he is the author.">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And are not his achayot (sisters) with us? From where, therefore, came to this one all these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:44; MATTITYAHU 11:6; LUKAS 4:24"> And they were taking offense at Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach. But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, A Navi (prophet) is not without honor except in his hometown and in his bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="MARKOS 6:5–6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:20; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:12–19"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach did not accomplish in that place many moftim, because of their lack of emunah (faith) and bitachon (trust).</span>
</div>
