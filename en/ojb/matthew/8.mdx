<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 8:18; MATTITYAHU 4:25–1; LUKAS 5:15"> And when Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach came down from the mountain, many multitudes followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:25; LUKAS 17:12–19; MATTITYAHU 18:26"> And an ish metzorah (a leper) there was who approached him and fell down before Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, saying, Adoneinu, if you choose, you can make me tahor (clean).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKAS 5:13; TEHILLIM 33:9; MELACHIM BAIS 5:14"> And, having stretched out his hand, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach touched him, saying, I am willing. Be made tahor. And immediately he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="VAYIKRA 14:2–32; LUKAS 5:14; LUKAS 17:14"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to him, See that you tell no one, but go and show yourself to the kohen and offer the korban about which Moshe gave mitzvah as an edut to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKAS 7:1–10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:43; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:1–33"> Now when Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach entered into K'far-Nachum, a centurion approached him, begging him</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:1; MATTITYAHU 4:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:33"> and saying, Adoneinu, my servant has been bedridden in the house, paralyzed, in terrible tza'ar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:18–19; LUKAS 7:6; MARKOS 5:23–24"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to him, I am coming. I will bring him refuah (healing).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 107:20; LUKAS 7:6–7; TEHILLIM 33:9"> The centurion answered, Adoneinu, I am not worthy that you might come under my roof, but only say the dvar, and my servant will receive the refuah (healing).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 4:35–36; LETTER TO TITOS 2:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:22"> For I also am a man under marut (authority), having soldiers subordinate to myself, and I say to this one, Go! And he goes, and to another, Come! And he comes, and to my servant, Do this! And he does it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:28; LUKAS 7:9; MARKOS 6:6"> And having heard this, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was amazed and said to those who followed him, Omein, I say to you, in no one in Yisroel have I found such great emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 13:28–29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:28–29"> And I say to you, that many from the mizrach (east) and the maarav (west) will come and will sit bimesibba (reclining at tish) with Avraham and Yitzchak and Ya'akov in the Malchut HaShomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:30; LUKAS 13:28; MATTITYAHU 21:43"> But the Bnei HaMalchut will be thrown out into the outer choshech (darkness), where there will be weeping and grinding of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:29–30; MARKOS 9:23; YOCHANAN 4:50"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to the centurion, Go! According to your bitachon (trust), your emunah (faith), let it be for you. And his servant was given refuah (healing) punkt (exactly) at that hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 4:38–41; MARKOS 1:29–34; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:5"> When Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach entered the bais (house) of Kefa, he saw the chamot (mother-in-law, shviger) bedridden and fever stricken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 13:21; LUKAS 4:38–39; LUKAS 8:54"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach touched her hand, and the fever left her. She then began functioning as his mesharetet (servant, lady minister), serving Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 15:26; MATTITYAHU 14:14; MARKOS 1:32–34"> And when erev (evening) had come, they brought to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach many possessed by shedim (evil spirits, demons), and he cast out the shedim with a dvar, and all the cholim (sick persons) he healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:4; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:24; MATTITYAHU 1:22"> Thus was fulfilled what was spoken by Yeshayah the Navi, saying, ACHEN CHOLAYEINU HU NASA UMACHOVEINU SVALAM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Isa 53:4">a</a></sup>(Surely he took up our sicknesses and he carried away<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="T.N. like the Yom Kippur sa'ir l'Azazel scapegoat kapporah">b</a></sup>our sorrows).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKAS 4:42–43; LUKAS 8:22; MARKOS 4:35"> And when he saw the crowd around him, he gave orders to go over to the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKAS 22:33–34; YOCHANAN 13:36–38; LUKAS 14:25–27"> A sofer (scribe, torah teacher) approached Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and said, Rebbe, I will follow you wherever you go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 84:3; LUKAS 2:7; YESHAYAH 53:2–3"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to him, Foxes have holes and the OPH HASHOMAYIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Job 35:11">c</a></sup>(birds of heaven) have nests, but the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">d</a></sup>does not have a place where he may lay his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKAS 9:59–62; VAYIKRA 21:11–12; BAMIDBAR 6:6–7"> And another of Moshiachʼs talmidim said to him, Adoneinu, allow me first to go and bury my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:13; LUKAS 15:32; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:14"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to him, Follow me, and permit the mesim (spiritually dead ones without hitkhadshut, spiritually unregenerate ones) to bury their own mesim (deceased ones, niftarim).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKAS 8:22–25; MARKOS 4:36–41; MATTITYAHU 9:1"> And when he embarked into the sirah, Moshiachʼs talmidim followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKAS 8:23; TEHILLIM 107:23–27; MARKOS 4:37–38"> And hinei! a great storm came up on the lake, so that the sirah (boat) was about to be covered by the waves. But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was sleeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YONAH 1:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:11; MARKOS 4:38–39"> And Moshiachʼs talmidim approached and woke him, saying, Adoneinu, hoshieinu! We are perishing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YESHAYAH 41:10–14; TEHILLIM 65:7; TEHILLIM 107:28–30"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, Why are you fearful, you ones of little emunah? Then, arising, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach rebuked the winds and the sea. And there was a great calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MARKOS 7:37; MARKOS 6:51; MATTITYAHU 14:33"> And the men were astonished, saying, What sort of man is this!? That even the winds and the sea obey him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKAS 8:26–39; MARKOS 5:1–20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:38"> And when Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach came to the other side of the lake, in the land of the people of Gadara, he was met by two men possessed by shedim (demons); these men were coming out of the kevarim (tombs) of the burial caves and were so dangerous no one could pass by on that road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MARKOS 1:24; LUKAS 4:34; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:19"> And they screamed, saying, Mah lanu valach, Ben HaElohim? (What to us and to you, Ben HaElohim?)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="i.e., What business do you have with us, Ben HaElohim">e</a></sup>Have you come here to torture us before the time?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="of the Yom HaDin, the Day of Judgment">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEVARIM 14:8; VAYIKRA 11:7; MARKOS 5:11"> Now there was feeding far away from them a large herd of chazirim (swine).</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="HISGALUS 20:1–2; LUKAS 8:30–33; MARKOS 5:12"> And the shedim begged him, saying, If you cast us out, send us into the herd of chazirim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MARKOS 5:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:23; HISGALUS 20:7"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Go away! So the shedim, coming out, went away into the chazirim. And, hinei, all the herd of chazirim rushed down the bank into the lake, and they perished in the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKAS 8:34–36; MARKOS 5:14–16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:15–17"> And the herdsmen of the chazirim fled, and, having gone away into the town, they told the whole story of what had happened to the men possessed by shedim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:39; LUKAS 8:37–39; LUKAS 5:8"> And hinei! The whole town came out to meet Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach. And seeing him, they begged him to depart from their area.</span>
