<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:47; MATTITYAHU 13:24; SHIR HASHIRIM 8:11–12"> The Malchut HaShomayim is like a man, the Baal Bayit, who went out early in the boker, to hire poalim (workers) for his kerem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 2:26; KOHELET 12:1; MATTITYAHU 22:19"> And having agreed with the poalim to salary them a denarius for their dayʼs pay, the Baal Bayit sent them into his kerem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:15; MATTITYAHU 20:6–7"> And having gone out around the third hour, the Baal Bayit saw others loitering in the marketplace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:11; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:2–3; LUKAS 19:7–10"> and he said to those, You go also into the kerem. And whatever is right, this is what your pay will be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:24–26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:3; MARKOS 15:33–34"> And they left. And again, having gone out around the sixth and the ninth hour, the Baal Bayit did the same thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:12; YOCHANAN 9:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:21"> And around the eleventh hour, having gone out, the Baal Bayit found others standing around, and he says to them, Why have you been standing here all the day not working?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:11–12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:10; YOCHANAN 9:4"> The idle workers say to the Baal Bayit, Because no one hired us. The Baal Bayit says to them, You go also into the kerem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEVARIM 24:15; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:6–10"> And when erev had come, another man, the owner of the kerem, says to his foreman, Call the poalim and give to them the wage, beginning with the acharonim and going to the rishonim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:6–7; LUKAS 23:40–43; MATTITYAHU 20:2"> And the ones that came around the eleventh hour each received a denarius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And when the rishonim came, they were under the impression that they would receive a larger sum; instead, they themselves each received a denarius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:16; LUKAS 19:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:21–22"> And when they received the denarius, they were complaining against the Baal Bayit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:5–6; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:23–28; LUKAS 12:55"> saying, These acharonim worked one hour, and you made them equal to us, the ones having endured the burden and the heat of the whole day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:50; MATTITYAHU 22:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:14–15"> But the Baal Bayit said in reply to one of them, Chaver, I am not cheating you. Did you not agree that I would pay you the usual dayʼs wage, a denarius?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 17:2; LUKAS 15:31; MATTITYAHU 6:6"> Take what belongs to you, your denarius, and go. But it is my ratzon, my good pleasure, to give to this one who is last also what I gave to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:15–24; DEVARIM 15:9; YONAH 4:1–4"> Or is it not allowable for me to do what I wish with the things that are mine? Or do you look with a jealous ayin horo upon my goodness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:30; MARKOS 10:31; MATTITYAHU 22:14"> Thus the Rishonim will be Acharonim, and the Acharonim will be Rishonim. For the invited ones are many, but the nivcharim (chosen ones) are few.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MARKOS 10:32–34; LUKAS 18:31–34; MATTITYAHU 16:13"> And going up to Yerushalayim, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach took the Shneym Asar Talmidim aside in a yechidus and, on the way, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:23; MATTITYAHU 27:1"> Hinei! We are going up to Yerushalayim, and the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Moshiach">a</a></sup>will be handed over to the Rashei Hakohanim and the Sofrim (scribes, torah teachers, or rabbonim), and they will condemn him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:21; MARKOS 15:16–20; MATTITYAHU 27:27–31"> And they will hand Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach over to the Goyim in order to mock and to whip and to hang him up TALUI AL HAETZ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dt 21:23">b</a></sup>(being hanged on the Tree), and after his histalkus (passing), on the Yom HaShlishi (Third Day) there will be Techiyas HaMoshiach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:21; MARKOS 10:35–45; MATTITYAHU 8:2"> Then the Em of Zavdaiʼs banim, along with the sons, approached Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and, prostrating herself before him, she made a bakosha (request) of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:28; MARKOS 10:36–37; ESTHER 5:3"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to her, What do you wish? She says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Say that these, my two banim, may sit, one on your right, the other on your left, in your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="the Moshiachʼs">c</a></sup>Malchut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:42; LUKAS 22:42; MATTITYAHU 26:39"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said in reply, You do not have daas of what you are asking. Are you able to drink the Kos which I am about to drink? They say, We are able.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:9; MATTITYAHU 25:34; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:2"> Moshiach says to them, Indeed, you will drink my Kos, but to sit on the right and on the left of me, this is not mine to grant, but it is for those for whom it has been prepared by my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Moshiachʼs">d</a></sup>Av.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:3; MARKOS 10:41"> And having heard this, HaAsarah (The Ten) became indignant about the two achim (brothers).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKAS 22:25–27; MATTITYAHU 20:25–28; YOCHANAN 13:12–17"> But Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach, having summoned them, said, You have daas that those who have the rule over the Goyim domineer them like tyrants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MARKOS 10:43; MARKOS 9:35; MARKOS 10:45"> But it will not be thus among you. For whoever wishes to be gadol among you will become your mesharet (servant, minister).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKAS 22:26; MARKOS 9:33–35; MATTITYAHU 18:4"> And whoever wishes among you to be rishon (first) will be your eved (servant).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKAS 22:27; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:28; LETTER TO TITOS 2:14"> Just as the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Moshiach">e</a></sup>did not come to be served (to be ministered to), but to serve (to minister), and to give his neshamah (his nefesh), as a kofer (ransom, pedut) LARABBIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isa 53:11">f</a></sup>(for the sake of many, for the Geulah Redemption of many).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MARKOS 10:46–52; LUKAS 18:35–43; MATTITYAHU 9:27–31"> And, as they were going out from Yericho, a great multitude followed Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MARKOS 10:46; YESHAYAH 42:16; MATTITYAHU 21:9"> And hinei! Two ivrim (blind men), sitting beside the road, having heard that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach is passing by, shouted, crying out, Adoneinu, Ben Dovid, chaneinu, yhi chasedcha aleinu<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ps 33:22">g</a></sup>(have mercy on us)!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:2; MATTITYAHU 7:7–8"> However, the crowd rebuked the two ivrim, that they be silent, but they shouted even more, saying Chaneinu, Adoneinu Ben Dovid!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 36:37; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:6; MATTITYAHU 20:21"> And having stopped, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach called to them and said, What do you wish that I should do for you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:17–19"> They say to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach: Adoneinu, that our eyes may be opened!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:15–16; MATTITYAHU 9:29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:18"> And having been filled with rachmei shomayim (heavenly mercy, compassion), he touched their eyes, and immediately their eyes were opened and they followed Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
</div>
