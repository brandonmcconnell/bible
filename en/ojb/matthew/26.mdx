<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:28; MATTITYAHU 19:1"> And it came about when Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach completed all these divrei torah, he said to his talmidim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKAS 22:1–2; MARKOS 14:1–2; YOCHANAN 13:1–2"> You know, that after two days, there is Pesach, and the Bar Enosh is handed over for talui al HaEtz.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Dt 21:23; Dan 7:13">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:47–53; YOCHANAN 18:24; MARKOS 14:54"> Then the Rashei Hakohanim and the Ziknei haAm (Elders of the People) were assembled in the courtyard of the residence of the Kohen Gadol (High Priest), the one being called Caiapha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:14; TEHILLIM 2:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:10"> And they planned to arrest Yehoshua by a trap and kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:24; MATTITYAHU 21:26; MATTITYAHU 14:5"> But they were saying, Not during the Chag (Feast), lest a riot break out among the am haaretz.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="T.N. A careful reading of this book shows that the Moshiach pointed to the One in Ps 110:1; Dan 7:13, Isaiah 53 and Ps 118:22and declared that the same person is being referred to in all these Scriptures: Moshiach Adoneinu.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MARKOS 14:3–9; LUKAS 7:37–39; YOCHANAN 12:1–8"> And, while Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was in Beit-Anyah in the bais of Shim'on the leper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:2–3; LUKAS 7:46; LUKAS 7:37–38"> An isha approached him with an alabaster flask of costly perfume, and she poured it out on Moshiachʼs rosh, as he sat bimesibba (reclining at tish).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="table">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:4–6; MARKOS 14:4; SHEMOT 5:17"> And having seen this, Moshiachʼs talmidim were angry, saying, For what is this waste?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:5–6; MARKOS 14:5; YEHOSHUA 7:20–21"> For it would have been possible to sell this for a generous sum and give to the aniyim (poor ones).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:21; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:10"> Aware of this, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Why are you bringing about difficulty for the isha for the ma'aseh tov (good deed) she does to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEVARIM 15:11; MARKOS 14:7; YOCHANAN 12:8"> For the aniyim you have with you always, but you do not always have me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MARKOS 14:8; YOCHANAN 12:7; MARKOS 16:1"> For this isha (woman) has poured this ointment on my basar (body) in order to prepare me for kevurah (burial).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 53:9Ps 16:9-10">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MARKOS 14:9; MATTITYAHU 24:14; TEHILLIM 112:6"> Omein, I say to you, wherever this Besuras HaGeulah is preached bchol HaOlam (in all the world), in her memory it will be spoken also what was done by her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 22:3–6; MATTITYAHU 10:4; MARKOS 14:10–11"> Then, one of the Shneym Asar (Twelve), Yehudah from K'riot, went to the Rashei Hakohanim</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 11:12–13; BERESHIS 37:26–28; SHEMOT 21:32"> and said, What are you willing to give me? And I will hand him over to you. And they weighed out for him sheloshim shiklei kesef (thirty pieces of silver).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MARKOS 14:11; LUKAS 22:6"> And from then on he was seeking an opportunity that he might hand Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKAS 22:7–13; MARKOS 14:12–16; SHEMOT 12:18–20"> But at the onset of Chag HaMatzot, Moshiachʼs talmidim approached him, saying, Where do you wish we should prepare for you your Seder?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MARKOS 14:13–16; YOCHANAN 7:6; YOCHANAN 17:1"> And he said, Go into the city to such and such person and say to him, Our Rebbe says, My time is at hand. With you I am observing Pesach with my talmidim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YOCHANAN 2:5; YOCHANAN 15:14; MATTITYAHU 21:6"> And the talmidim did as Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach commanded them, and they prepared the Seder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MARKOS 14:17–21; YOCHANAN 13:21; LUKAS 22:14–16"> When erev came, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was sitting bimesibba (reclining) at tish with the Shneym Asar Talmidim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:13; YOCHANAN 6:70–71"> And as they were eating with their Rebbe, he said, Omein, I say to you that one of you will be my betrayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:22–25; LUKAS 22:23; MARKOS 14:19–20"> And much distressed, each one began to say to him, Surely I am not the one, Adoni?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:18; TEHILLIM 41:9; YOCHANAN 13:26–28"> But, in reply, he said, The one having dipped the hand into the bowl<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ex 12:8">e</a></sup>with me, this one is my betrayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:22–23; LUKAS 24:46; MATTITYAHU 18:7"> Indeed the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Moshiach">f</a></sup>goes as it was written concerning him, but oy to that man through whom the Bar Enosh is betrayed. It would have been better for that man if he had not been born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:64; MATTITYAHU 27:11; LUKAS 22:70"> And, in reply, Yehudah the betrayer of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, said, Surely I am not the one, Rabbi? Moshiach says to Yehudah, You have said it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:23–29; LUKAS 22:18–20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:16–17"> And at the Seudah, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, having taken matzah, having made HaMotzi, he broke the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="middle">g</a></sup>matzah, giving the afikoman to the talmidim, and said, Take and eat, this is my basar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YESHAYAH 25:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:16; TEHILLIM 116:13"> And having taken the Cup of Redemption and having made the bracha, he gave it to them, saying, Drink from it, all of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:4–14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:14–22; VAYIKRA 17:11"> For this is my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Moshiachʼs, see Isa 53:7-8">h</a></sup>Dahm HaBrit HaChadasha,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Isa 42:6; Jer 31:31-34">i</a></sup>which is poured out LARABBIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Isa 53:11-12">j</a></sup>(for many) for the selicha (forgiveness) of chattaim (sins).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:2; HISGALUS 7:17; MARKOS 14:25"> And I say to you, I will by no means drink from now on of this pri hagefen (fruit of the vine) until that Day when I drink it with you chadash (new) in the Malchut Avi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:19–20; LUKAS 22:39"> And having sung the Hallel, they went out to the Har HaZeytim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 13:7; MARKOS 14:27–28; YOCHANAN 16:32"> Then Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, All of you will be offended at me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Moshiach">k</a></sup>during this night, for it has been written, HACH ES HAROEH UTEFUTSEN HATSON<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Zech 13:7">l</a></sup>(Strike the Shepherd and the sheep will be scattered).</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:10; LUKAS 18:33–34; MATTITYAHU 28:16"> But after I undergo Techiyas HaMoshiach I will go ahead of you to the Galil.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Mt 28:7">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKAS 22:33; YOCHANAN 13:36–38; MISHLE 28:25–26"> And, in reply, Kefa said to him, If everyone will be offended at you, I never will be offended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:38; LUKAS 22:34; MATTITYAHU 26:75"> He said to him, Omein, I say to you that balailah hazeh (during this night), before a tarnegol (cock) crows, you will make hakhchashah (denial) of me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="as Moshiach">n</a></sup>shalosh pe'amim (three times).</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:20; YOCHANAN 13:37; MISHLE 29:23"> Kefa says to him, Even if it is necessary for me to die al kiddush ha-Shem with you, by no means will I deny you. Likewise all the talmidim spoke also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MARKOS 14:32–42; MATTITYAHU 26:42; LUKAS 22:39–46"> Then Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach comes with them to a place being called Gat-Shmanim, and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to the talmidim, Sit here until I go over there and daven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:21; MATTITYAHU 17:1; MARKOS 5:37"> And having taken Kefa and the two sons of Zavdai, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach began to be sorrowful and distressed with agmat nefesh (grief).</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:27; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:24"> Then he says to them, My nefesh is deadly grieved, even to the point of mavet. Remain here and stay awake with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:38; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:8; LUKAS 22:41–42"> And having gone forward a short distance, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach fell upon his face, davening and saying, Avi, if it is possible, let this Kos pass from me. But not as I will, but as you will.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Mt 6:10; 12:50">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MARKOS 14:37; LUKAS 22:45; MATTITYAHU 26:43"> Then he comes to the talmidim and finds them sleeping, and he says to Kefa, So were you not strong enough to be awake for one hour with me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MARKOS 14:38; LUKAS 22:46; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:18"> Stay awake and offer tefillos, lest you enter lidey nisayon.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Mt 6:13">p</a></sup>Indeed the ruach is willing but the basar is weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:39; MARKOS 14:39–40; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:15"> Again, for a second time, having left, he davened, saying, Avi, if it is not possible for this to pass by except I drink it, let yeaseh rtzonechah (your will be done).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Mt 6:10">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LUKAS 9:32; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:1; MISHLE 23:34"> And having come again, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach found the talmidim sleeping, for their eyes were too heavy to stay open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:8; LUKAS 18:1; MATTITYAHU 6:7"> And having left them again and having gone away, he was davening for the shlishit (third) time, saying the same words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:1; YOCHANAN 12:27; MARKOS 14:41–42"> Then he comes to the talmidim and says to them, Sleep on now and take your rest. Hinei! The hour is at hand, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Dan 7:13">r</a></sup>Bar Enosh is being betrayed into the hands of chote'im (sinners).</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LUKAS 9:51; YOCHANAN 14:31; SHMUEL ALEF 17:48"> Arise, and let us go. Hinei! My betrayer has drawn near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:16; MATTITYAHU 26:55; MARKOS 14:43–50"> And while he was still speaking hinei! Yehudah, one of the Shneym Asar, came and with him came a great multitude with swords and clubs from the Rashei Hakohanim (Chief Priests) and the Ziknei HaAm (the Elders of the People).</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="TEHILLIM 55:20–21; TEHILLIM 28:3; SHMUEL BAIS 3:27"> Now the betrayer had given them a signal, saying, Whomever I may give the neshikah (kiss), he is the one. Chap (grab) him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:25; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:26; MISHLE 27:6"> And immediately, he approached Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, and said, Shalom, Rebbe. And he gave him the neshikah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:13; MATTITYAHU 22:12; TEHILLIM 41:9"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Chaver, do what you came for. Then, having approached, they laid hands on Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and arrested him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:36; MARKOS 14:47; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:4"> And hinei! One of the ones with him stretched out his hand, drew his cherev, and struck the servant of the Kohen Gadol, cutting off his ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="HISGALUS 13:10; BERESHIS 9:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:19"> Then Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to him, Return your cherev into its place; for all who take the sword will die by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 6:17; MATTITYAHU 4:11; DANIEL 7:10"> Or do you think that I am not able to call upon Avi, and He will provide me now more than Shneym Asar legions of malachim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 13:7; MATTITYAHU 26:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:16"> But how then may the Kitvei Hakodesh be fulfilled that say it must happen thus?</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="MARKOS 12:35; YOCHANAN 8:2; LUKAS 22:52–53"> At that moment, he said to the crowd, Do you have the chutzpah (nerve) to come out, as against a revolutionary, with swords and clubs to arrest me? Daily in the Beis Hamikdash I was sitting saying my shiurim and you did not arrest me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 13:7; YOCHANAN 16:32; MARKOS 14:50–52"> But this all happened that the Kitvei Hakodesh of the Neviim might be fulfilled. Then the talmidim deserted Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:3; MARKOS 14:53–65; TEHILLIM 56:5–6"> But the ones having arrested Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach led him away to Caiapha the Kohen Gadol, where the Sofrim and the Zekenim (Elders) were gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:32; YOCHANAN 18:15–16; YOCHANAN 18:25"> And Kefa was following Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach from far away. He followed him as far as the courtyard of the Kohen Gadol and, having gone inside it, Kefa was sitting down with the servants to see the maskana (outcome).</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:11–13; DEVARIM 19:16–21; MARKOS 14:55–56"> And the Rashei Hakohanim and the Sanhedrin all were seeking edut sheker (false testimony of false witnesses) against Yehoshua so that they might put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="DEVARIM 19:15; TEHILLIM 35:11; TEHILLIM 27:12"> And they found none, though many shakranim (liars) came forward. At last, two came forward</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="YOCHANAN 2:19–21; MATTITYAHU 27:40; MARKOS 15:29"> and said, This one said, I am able to bring about a churban (destruction) of the Beis Hamikdash of Hashem and within shloshah yamim to rebuild it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:9–11; YOCHANAN 18:19–24; MARKOS 14:60"> And having got up, the Kohen Gadol said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Do you answer nothing? For what do these men give edut (testimony) against you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="VAYIKRA 5:1; MATTITYAHU 16:16; YESHAYAH 53:7"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was silent. And the Kohen Gadol said to him, I adjure you by Hashem, Elohim Chayyim, to tell us if you are the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Ben HaElohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="DANIEL 7:13; MARKOS 14:62; TEHILLIM 110:1"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to him, You said it. But I say to you, From now on you will see the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Moshiach">s</a></sup>sitting at the right hand of Gevurah (Power) and his Bias (Coming) will be with ANENEI HASHOMAYIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Dan 7:13-14">t</a></sup>(clouds of Heaven).</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 36:24; YOCHANAN 10:33; MATTITYAHU 9:3"> Then the Kohen Gadol made the keriah (rending, tearing of his garments), saying, He has committed Chillul Hashem. What further need do we have of edut? Hinei! Now you have heard the Chillul Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:52; VAYIKRA 24:11–16"> What does it seem to you? And, in reply, they said, He is deserving of mishpat mavet, the death penalty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="YESHAYAH 50:6; YESHAYAH 53:3; MATTITYAHU 27:30"> Then they spat into his face and they struck him and they slapped him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="LUKAS 22:63–65; MARKOS 14:65; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:4–8"> saying, Give us a dvar nevuah, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach. Who is the one who hit you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 19:13; MATTITYAHU 26:71; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:37"> Now Kefa was sitting outside in the courtyard. And one maid approached Kefa, saying, And you were with Yehoshua of the Galil!</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:12; HISGALUS 21:8; MATTITYAHU 26:40–43"> But Kefa denied it before everyone, saying, I do not have daas of what you are saying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="MARKOS 14:68–69; YOCHANAN 18:25–27; LUKAS 22:58"> And having gone out to the gate, another saw Kefa and says to the bystanders, There! This one was with Yehoshua of Natzeret!</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:74; YESHAYAH 48:1; ZECHARYAH 8:17"> And again Kefa denied it with a shevuah (oath), I do not know the man!</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73" data-xref="SHOFETIM 12:6; YOCHANAN 18:26–27; NECHEMYAH 13:24"> And after a little while the bystanders came and said to Kefa, Truly also you are one of them, for even your accent gives you away!</span>
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:19; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:22; MARKOS 14:71–72"> Then Kefa began to curse and swear, I do not know the man! And immediately a tarnegol (cock) crowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v75" data-v="75" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:34; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:18–20; LUKAS 22:31–34"> And Kefa remembered the word which Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach had said: Before a tarnegol (cock) crows, you will deny me shalosh pe'amim (three times). And Kefa went out and wept bitterly.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="T.N.
