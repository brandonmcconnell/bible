<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARKOS 13:1–37; LUKAS 21:5–36; YECHEZKEL 8:6"> And having exited from the Beis Hamikdash, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach departed, and his talmidim approached to show him the buildings of the Beis Hamikdash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKAS 19:44; YIRMEYAH 26:18; MICHOH 3:12"> But he said in reply to them, Do you not see all these things? Omein, I say to you, by no means will be left here one stone upon another, for all will be demolished.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MARKOS 13:3–4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:1–11"> And while Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was sitting upon the Mount of Olives, his talmidim approached him in a yechidus, saying, Tell us ad mosai (how much longer), when will these things be and what about the ot (sign) of the Bias Moshiach, of your Coming, and the Ketz HaOlam Hazeh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1; YIRMEYAH 29:8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:3"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said in reply to them, See to it that someone does not deceive you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:11; MATTITYAHU 24:24; YIRMEYAH 14:14"> For many will come in my Name, saying, I am the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach. And they will deceive many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MARKOS 13:7–8; LUKAS 21:9; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:14–15"> But you are about to hear about milchamot (wars) and rumors of milchamot. See to it that you are not alarmed, for it is necessary for this to happen, but it is not yet HaKetz (the End).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKAS 21:11; HISGALUS 6:8; YESHAYAH 19:2"> For there will be an intifada of ethnic group against ethnic group, and malchut against malchut. There will be famines, pestilences, earthquakes in various places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:3; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:17–18; DEVARIM 28:59"> But all these things are but the beginning of the Chevlei.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Moshiach">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:2; YOCHANAN 15:19–20; LUKAS 21:16–17"> Then they will hand you over to tzoros and they will kill you and you will be hated by all the ethnic groups on account of the Name of me (Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, Yeshua).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MICHOH 7:5–6; LUKAS 21:16; MATTITYAHU 11:6"> And then many will be led into chet (sin), and others they will hand over, and they will hate others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:24; MATTITYAHU 7:15; MATTITYAHU 24:5"> And many neviei sheker (false prophets) will arise and will deceive many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:4–5; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1–4; HISGALUS 3:15"> And because of the increased mufkarut<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Mt 7:23">b</a></sup>(lawlessness, anarchy), the agape (love) of many will become cold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:22; MARKOS 13:13; HISGALUS 2:10"> But the one having endured to HaKetz (the End), this one will receive Yeshu'at Eloheinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HISGALUS 14:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:25–26"> And this Besuras HaGeulah of the Malchut will be announced in kol haOlam (all the world) as an edut to all the Nations, and then will come HaKetz (the End).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DANIEL 9:27; DANIEL 12:11; MARKOS 13:14"> Therefore, when you see the SHIKUTS MESHOMEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Mk 13:14">c</a></sup>(Abomination of Desolation), the thing spoken through Daniel HaNavi, having stood in the Makom Kadosh (Holy Place),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="i.e., the Beis Hamikdash">d</a></sup>— let the reader understand!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 19:15–17; LUKAS 21:21–22; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:7"> — Then the ones in Yehudah, let them flee to the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKAS 17:31–33; MARKOS 13:15–16; MATTITYAHU 10:27"> The one upon the roof, let him not come down to carry away the things from his bais (house).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the one in the field, let him not turn back to get his kaftan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKAS 23:29–30; DEVARIM 28:53–56; LUKAS 21:23"> But oy to the ones with child and the ones with nursing infants in those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:12"> But offer tefillos that your escape may not be in winter or on Shabbos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DANIEL 12:1; YOEL 2:2; MALACHI 4:1"> For then will be Tzarah Gedolah (Great Tribulation) such as has not been from Reshit HaOlam (Beginning of the World) until now nor will it by any means happen again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MARKOS 13:20; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:10; MATTITYAHU 24:31"> And if those days were not cut short, kol basar (all flesh) would not be saved. But, on account of the Kedoshim, the Bechirim (Chosen Ones), those days will be cut short.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKAS 21:8; LUKAS 17:23–24; DEVARIM 13:1–3"> Then, if someone says to you, Hinei, here is the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach! Or, Hinei! Here! Do not have emunah (faith).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="in that">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:9–11; MARKOS 13:22; HISGALUS 13:13–14"> For meshichei sheker (false moshiachs) will arise, and neviei sheker (false prophets), and they will give otot gedolim (great signs) and moftim (wonders), so as to deceive, if possible, even the Bechirim (Chosen ones).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:1; LUKAS 21:13; YESHAYAH 48:5–6"> Hinei! I have told you beforehand!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LUKAS 3:2–3; YESHAYAH 40:3; MATTITYAHU 3:1"> If, therefore, they say to you, Hinei! He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Moshiach">f</a></sup>is in the desert! Do not go out.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="there">g</a></sup>Hinei! &#91;Moshiach&#93; is in the secret cheder (room)! Do not have emunah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="in that">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKAS 17:24–37; ZECHARYAH 9:14; IYOV 37:3"> For as lightning goes out from the mizrach (east) and shines to as far as the maarav (west), thus will be the Bias HaMoshiach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Dan 7:13">i</a></sup>(coming of the Messiah, the Bar Enosh, Son of Man).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKAS 17:37; IYOV 39:27–30; DEVARIM 28:49"> Wherever the place of the Geviya (Corpse) is, there will be gathered together the nesharim (vultures, eagles).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="popularly">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="AMOS 8:9; YOEL 3:15; YESHAYAH 13:10"> Immediately after the Tzarah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Mt 24:21">k</a></sup>(Tribulation) of those days, the shemesh will be darkened, and the levanah will not give its light. And the kokhavim will fall from Shomayim, and the powers of the heavens will be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:7; DANIEL 7:13; LUKAS 21:27"> And then will appear the Ot Bar Enosh (the Sign of the Son of Man) in Shomayim. All the tribes of kol haaretz (all the earth) will see the Bar Enosh Moshiach and his Bias, his Coming, on the ananim of Shomayim (clouds of glory of Heaven) with gevurah (power) and great kavod (glory).</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:16; MARKOS 13:27; YESHAYAH 27:13"> Moshiach will send his malachim with a loud blast of the Shofar, and the malachim will gather together Moshiachʼs Bechirim from the four winds, from one end of Shomayim to the other.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MARKOS 13:28–29; LUKAS 21:29–30"> And from the etz teenah (fig tree), learn its parabolic lesson: when its branch has become tender already and sprouts leaves, you know that Kayitz (Summer) is near;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:9; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:37; YECHEZKEL 7:2–14"> thus also you, when you see all these things, know that it is near, at the very doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MARKOS 13:30–31; LUKAS 21:32–33; MATTITYAHU 16:28"> Omein, I say to you, that this dor (generation) will by no means pass away until all these things come about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:8; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:25; BAMIDBAR 23:19"> Shomayim v'HaAretz will pass away, but my Dvar will by no means pass away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MARKOS 13:32; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:7; MATTITYAHU 24:44"> But concerning that day and hour, no one has daas, not the malachim of Shomayim, but only HaAv (the Father) of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKAS 17:26–27; BERESHIS 6:1–7; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5"> For as in the days of Noach, thus will be the Bias HaMoshiach, the Coming of the Bar Enosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LUKAS 17:26–28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:13–14; LUKAS 14:18–20"> For as they were in those days before HaMabbul (the Flood), eating and drinking, marrying and being given in marriage, until the day when Noach entered the Tevah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:20; MATTITYAHU 24:37; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:41"> and they did not have daas until HaMabbul came and took away everything — thus also will be the Bias HaMoshiach, the Coming of the Bar Enosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LUKAS 17:34–37; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:7–9; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5"> Then two men will be in the field, one is snatched away, and one is left behind (not retained).</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="SHEMOT 11:5; YESHAYAH 47:2; LUKAS 17:35"> Two women are grinding in the mill house, one is snatched away, one is left behind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="LUKAS 21:36; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:6; MATTITYAHU 25:13"> So be shomer, be on your guard, because you do not have daas on which day will be the Bias of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Moshiach">l</a></sup>Adoneichem (your L-rd).</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LUKAS 12:39; MATTITYAHU 24:44; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:2–6"> But understand this: if the Baal Bayit had known in which watch the ganav is coming, he would have stayed awake and would not have allowed his bais to be dug through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:10; MATTITYAHU 24:42; MATTITYAHU 25:13"> For this reason also you be shomer, for in the hour when you do not think, then will be the Bias HaMoshiach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Dan 7:13">m</a></sup>(the Coming of the Bar Enosh).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:21; MATTITYAHU 25:23; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:1–2"> Who then is the faithful and wise eved (servant), whom the Adon appointed over the household servants to give the other servants their okhel at the prescribed time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="HISGALUS 16:15; LUKAS 12:43; LUKAS 12:37"> Ashrey (happy) is that eved whom, when his Adon comes, finds him so doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:23; MATTITYAHU 25:21; LUKAS 12:37"> Omein, I say to you, the Adon will ordain him to oversee all his possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:32; YESHAYAH 32:6; LUKAS 12:45"> But if that wicked slave says in his heart, Adoni (My Master) is dragging his feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="HISGALUS 17:6; HISGALUS 16:6; MICHOH 3:5"> and that slave begins to beat his fellow avadim (slaves), and he eats and drinks with the ones getting drunk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:2–3; HISGALUS 3:3; MATTITYAHU 24:42–44"> the Adon of that slave will come on a day which he does not expect and at an hour of which he does not have daas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 8:12; MATTITYAHU 22:13; LUKAS 12:46"> And the Adon will cut in two that slave, and the slaveʼs portion he will put with the tzevuim (hypocrites). There will be weeping and grinding of teeth.</span>
</div>
