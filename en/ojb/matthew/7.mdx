<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 6:37; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:1–2; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:11–12"> Judge not lest you be judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13; MARKOS 4:24; LUKAS 6:38"> For with what gezar din (verdict) you judge, you will be judged, and with what measure you measure, it will be measured to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:7–9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:1; LUKAS 6:41–42"> And why do you see the speck in the eye of your Ach,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="b'Moshiach">a</a></sup>but you do not consider the beam in your own eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Or how will you say to your Ach,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="b'Moshiach">b</a></sup>Let me take the speck out from your eye! And, hinei! The beam is in your own eye!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKAS 6:42; TEHILLIM 51:9–13; MATTITYAHU 23:13–28"> Tzevua (hypocrite), first take the beam out of your eye, and then you will see clearly enough to take out the speck from the eye of your Ach.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="b'Moshiach">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MISHLE 9:7–8; MISHLE 23:9; MATTITYAHU 10:14–15"> Do not give the kodesh to kelevim nor throw your pearls before chazirim, lest they will trample them with their feet and turn around and tear you into pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:22; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:22; YOCHANAN 15:7"> Keep asking and it shall be given to you; keep searching and you shall find; keep knocking and the delet shall be opened to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 81:10; TEHILLIM 81:16; LUKAS 23:42–43"> For everyone asking receives, and the one searching finds, and to the one knocking the delet will be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 11:11–13"> Or what man is there among you the ben of whom will ask for lechem (bread), and he will give him a stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Or if he asks for a dag (fish), will give him a nachash (snake)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 11:11–13; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:32"> Therefore, if you, being ra'im (evil ones), know to give matanot tovot (good gifts) to your yeladim, how much more does your Av sh'ba'Shomayim give hatov (the good) to the ones asking Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKAS 6:31; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:8–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:13–14"> Therefore, everything that you wish Bnei Adam do for you, thus also you do for them. For this is the Torah and the Neviim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 13:24–25; MISHLE 16:25; YOCHANAN 14:6"> Enter through the derech (way) of the shaar hatzarut (gate of narrowness), for wide is the delet and broad is the rekhov (street) leading to Avaddon (destruction, hell, Abaddon), and rabbim (many) are they who enter through it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 13:23–30; MATTITYAHU 22:14; MATTITYAHU 16:24–25"> But tzar (narrow) is the delet and constricted is the Derech (Way) that leads to Chayyim (life) and few are the ones finding it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1–3; MATTITYAHU 24:11"> Beware of the neviei hasheker (false prophets), who come to you in the malbush (clothing) of kevasim (sheep), but within are ravenous ze'evim (wolves).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 6:43–45; MATTITYAHU 12:33; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:12"> By their perot (fruits) you will have da'as of them. Surely grapes are not gathered from thorns nor figs from thistles, are they?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:33–35; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:22–24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:9"> So every etz tov (good tree) produces pri tov (good fruit), but the etz nishchat (corrupt tree) produces pri rah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:17; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9–10"> An etz tov is not able to produce pri rah nor is an etz nishchat able to produce pri tov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 3:10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:8; YOCHANAN 15:2–6"> Kol etz (every tree) not producing pri tov is cut off and is thrown into HaEish (the Fire).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 66:24; Dan 12:2">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:38"> Therefore, by their perot (fruits) you shall have da'as of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:13; LUKAS 6:46; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:22"> Not all the ones saying to me, Adoneinu, Adoneinu, will enter the Malchut HaShomayim, but the one doing the ratzon Avi sh'ba'Shomayim (the will of my Father in Heaven).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Mt 26:39">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKAS 13:25–27; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:1–2; MALACHI 3:17–18"> Many<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="messianic darshanim">f</a></sup>will say to me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Moshiach">g</a></sup>on that Day,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="the Yom HaDin, the Day of Judgment">h</a></sup>Adoneinu, Adoneinu, did we not speak as neviim in your Name? Did we not cast out shedim (evil spirits, demons) in your Name? Did we not accomplish many niflaot (miracles) in your Name?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:41; TEHILLIM 6:8; LUKAS 13:25"> And then I will tell them to their face, I never had da'as of you. Depart from me, you workers of mufkarut (lawlessness).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ps 6:8[9]">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKAS 6:47–49; YOCHANAN 13:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:10–11"> Therefore, everyone who hears these devarim (words) of mine and does them, is like a chacham (wise man) who built his bais (house) upon the Tzur (Rock).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; TEHILLIM 125:1–2; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7"> And the geshem (rain) came down, the floods arrived, and the winds blew and beat against that bais, and it did not fall, for it had been founded upon the Tzur (Rock).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 8:9; LUKAS 6:49; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20"> And everyone hearing these words of mine<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Divrei Moshiach">j</a></sup>and not doing them is like the shoteh (fool), who built his bais upon the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:19–22; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20–22; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:13"> And the geshem (rain) came down, and the floods arrived, and the winds blew and beat against that bais, and it fell, and gadolah was the mapalah (fall, downfall, defeat) of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKAS 4:32; MARKOS 1:22; YOCHANAN 7:46"> And it came about when Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach finished these divrei torah (words of teaching), the multitudes were filled with fear and wonder at his torah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:12–13; LUKAS 20:8; DEVARIM 18:18–19"> For his ministry of moreh (teacher) was as one having samchut (authority) and not as their Sofrim (scribes, torah teachers, rabbonim).</span>
