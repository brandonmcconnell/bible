<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARKOS 3:13; MATTITYAHU 15:29; MARKOS 4:1"> When Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach saw the multitudes, he ALAH HAHAR<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ex 19:3">a</a></sup>(went up the mountain) and when he sat down, his talmidim came to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:35; LUKAS 6:20–26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:34"> Then Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach began to speak, and gave them this torah (teaching), saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YESHAYAH 66:2; MISHLE 29:23; TEHILLIM 34:18"> Ashrey ANIYEI HARUACH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Isa 66:2">b</a></sup>(Blessed, Happy are the poor in spirit) for theirs is the Malchut HaShomayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:4; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:9–10; YOCHANAN 16:20–22"> Ashrey are the AVELIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 61:2">c</a></sup>(the ones mourning), for they will be comforted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 37:11; YESHAYAH 29:19; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:13"> Ashrey are the ANAVIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 29:19; Zeph 2:3">d</a></sup>(humble), for YIRESHU ARETZ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ps 37:11">e</a></sup>(they will inherit the land).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:14; TEHILLIM 107:9; YOCHANAN 6:27"> Ashrey are the ones hungering and thirsting for TZIDKAT Hashem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Dan 9:24">f</a></sup>(Tzedek Olamim, Everlasting Righteousness), for they will be satisfied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:32–1; MATTITYAHU 6:14–15; SHMUEL BAIS 22:26"> Ashrey are the merciful, for they will be shown rachamim (mercy).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 24:4; TEHILLIM 51:10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:22"> Ashrey are those of LEV TAHOR<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ps 51:10[12]">g</a></sup>(pure heart), for they will see Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:14; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16–18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:18"> Ashrey are the peace-makers, for they will be called bnei haElohim (sons of G-d).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:13–14; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:17"> Ashrey are the ones being persecuted because of TZIDKAT Hashem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Dan 9:24">h</a></sup>(righteousness of Hashem), for theirs is the Malchut HaShomayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:14; LUKAS 21:17; MARKOS 13:13"> Ashrey are you when they reproach you and persecute you and speak all kinds of lashon horah against you, speaking sheker (falsehood, lies) because of me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Moshiach">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKAS 6:23; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:24"> Have simcha (joy) and lev sameach (glad heart), for your sachar (reward) is great in Shomayim, for thus they persecuted the Neviim before you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:6; LUKAS 14:34–35; MARKOS 9:49–50"> You are the melach haaretz (salt of the earth), but if the salt becomes tasteless, in what way will it become salty again? It is no longer good for anything, but is thrown out and trampled by men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:15; YOCHANAN 8:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:8–14"> You are the Ohr HaOlam (the Light of the World). A city lying on a mountaintop cannot be nistar (hidden).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 11:33; MARKOS 4:21; LUKAS 8:16"> No one, after lighting a menorah, places it under a measuring basket, but on the shulchan (table), and it gives ohr (light) to kol anshei habais (all in the house).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:12; LETTER TO TITOS 2:7; YOCHANAN 15:8"> In like manner, let your light so shine before Bnei Adam, that they may see your ma'asim tovim (good works) and give kavod (glory) to your Av sh'ba'Shomayim (Father in Heaven).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:4; LUKAS 16:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:4–5"> Do not think that I came to abolish the Torah or the Neviim. I did not come to abolish but to complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKAS 16:17; YESHAYAH 40:8; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:25"> For, omein, truly I say to you, until Shomayim and haaretz pass away, not one yod, not one tag (ornamental flourish), will pass from the Torah until everything is accomplished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:32; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:10–11; MATTITYAHU 28:20"> Therefore, whoever annuls one of the least of these mitzvot (divine commandments given by Hashemto Moshe Rabbenu) and so teaches Bnei Adam, shall be called katon (least) in the Malchut HaShomayim; but whoever practices and teaches them, this one will be called gadol (great) in the Malchut HaShomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:2–3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:30–32"> For I say unto you that unless the Tzedek (Righteousness) of you exceeds that of the Sofrim and Perushim, you will certainly not enter the Malchut HaShomayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEVARIM 5:17; SHEMOT 20:13; BAMIDBAR 35:30–34"> You have heard that it was said to the ancients, LO TIRTZACH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ex 20:13; Dt 5:17">j</a></sup>(Do not murder), and every rotzeach (murderer) shall be liable before the Bet Din (Court).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:14–15; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:20–21; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9"> But I say to you, that everyone who harbors kaas (anger) against his Ach,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="b'Moshiach">k</a></sup>his chaver, shall be subject to mishpat (judgment); and whoever shall say to his Ach,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="b'Moshiach">l</a></sup>Reyka! (Good for nothing!) will be subject to the Sanhedrin; and whoever shall say Shoteh! (Fool) shall be subject to Eish Gehinnom (Fire of Hell).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MARKOS 11:25; VAYIKRA 6:2–6; MATTITYAHU 5:24"> Therefore, if you bring your korban (sacrifice) to the Mizbeach (altar), and there you remember that your Ach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="b'Moshiach">m</a></sup>has something against you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:17–18; MATTITYAHU 18:15–17"> leave your korban there before the Mizbeach, and go and first be reconciled to your Ach;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="b'Moshiach">n</a></sup>and then come offer your korban.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKAS 12:58–59; MISHLE 25:8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:13"> Come to terms quickly with your ish riv (opponent in a lawsuit), while you are a fellow-traveler on the derech eretz, lest the ish riv might deliver you to the shofet (judge), and the shofet might deliver you to the shoter (law official), and the shoter might deliver you to the beit hasohar (prison house).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:34; LUKAS 12:59; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:9"> For, omein, truly I say to you, you may never come out from there until you repay the last peruta (small coin).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEVARIM 5:18; MISHLE 6:32; SHEMOT 20:14"> You have heard that it was said, LO TINAF<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Ex 20:14">o</a></sup>(Do not commit adultery).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:16; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:14–15; MISHLE 6:25"> But I say to you that everyone looking upon a woman with taavah (lust) for her has already committed niuf (adultery) with her in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:13; MARKOS 9:43–48; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:5"> And if your right eye causes you to commit chet (sin), tear it out and throw it from you, for it is better for you that one of your evarim (members) be lost and not your whole basar (body, flesh) be thrown into Gehinnom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MARKOS 9:43; MATTITYAHU 5:29; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:8"> And if your right hand causes you to commit chet, cut it off and throw it from you, for it is better for you that one of your evarim (members) be lost and not that your whole basar may go into Gehinnom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 3:1; DEVARIM 24:1–4; MATTITYAHU 19:7"> It was also said, Whoever divorces his wife, let him give to her a “get,” a SEFER KERITUT<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Dt 24:1">p</a></sup>(bill of divorce).</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:10–11; LUKAS 16:18"> But I say to you that everyone divorcing his wife, except for the DVAR<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Dt 24:1">q</a></sup>(indecent thing) of zenut (fornication), makes her become a noefet (adulteress), and whoever marries a gerusha (divorcee) commits niuf (adultery).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DEVARIM 23:21; VAYIKRA 19:12; KOHELET 5:4–6"> Again, you have heard that it was said to the ancients, You shall not break your nederim (vows), but you shall repay your vows to Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="See Lv 19:12; Num 30:3; Dt 23:22">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:12; YESHAYAH 66:1; DEVARIM 23:21–23"> But I say to you, Do not swear shevuot (oaths) at all, neither by Shomayim, for it is the kes malchut of Hashem (throne of G-d),</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="TEHILLIM 48:2; MALACHI 1:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 6:6"> nor by haaretz, for it is the hadom (footstool) of his feet, nor by Yerushalayim, for it is the kiryat melech rav<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Ps 48:2[3]">s</a></sup>(city of the great king).</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LUKAS 12:25; MATTITYAHU 6:27; MATTITYAHU 23:16–21"> Neither are you to swear by your rosh, for you are not able to make one hair turn white or black.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:9"> But let your word be ken, ken (yes, yes) or lo, lo (no, no), but anything beyond this is lashon horah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="VAYIKRA 24:19–20; DEVARIM 19:21; SHEMOT 21:22–27"> You have heard that it was said, AYIN TAKHAT AYIN, SHEN TAKHAT SHEN<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Ex 21:24">t</a></sup>(An eye for an eye and a tooth for a tooth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:15; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:17–19"> But I say to you, Do not set yourself against the rasha (evil person), but whoever hits you on your right cheek, turn to him also the other cheek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LUKAS 6:29; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:7"> And the one wishing to sue you and take your tunic, give to him also your kaftan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LUKAS 23:26; MATTITYAHU 27:32; MARKOS 15:21"> And whoever will force you to go one mile, go with him two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MISHLE 19:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:16; LUKAS 6:30–36"> And the one asking you to give and the one wishing to borrow from you, from these do not turn away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:18; TEHILLIM 139:21–22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:13–14"> You have heard that it was said, V'AHAVTA L'REACHA<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Lv 19:18">u</a></sup>(You shall love your neighbor), and you shall hate your oyev (enemy).</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:14; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; LUKAS 6:27–28"> But I say to you, Love your enemies, and offer tefillos (prayers) for the ones bringing redifah (persecution) upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="TEHILLIM 145:9; LUKAS 6:35; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:17"> Do this so that you may become banim of your Av sh'ba'Shomayim, for His shemesh (sun) He makes to rise on the ra'im (evil ones) and the tovim (good ones), and He sends His geshem (rain) upon the tzaddikim (righteous ones) and the resha'im (unrighteous ones).</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LUKAS 6:32–35; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20–23; MATTITYAHU 9:10–11"> For if you have ahavah (love) for the ones who have ahavah for you, what sachar (reward) do you have? Even the mochesim (tax collectors) do that, don't they?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:20; LUKAS 6:32; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20"> And if you give Drishat Shalom (greetings) only to your Achim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="b'Moshiach">v</a></sup>what extraordinary thing are you doing? Don't even the Goyim do the same?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:15–16; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:4; VAYIKRA 19:2"> Therefore, be shlemim (complete), even as is your Av sh'ba'Shomayim.</span>
</div>
