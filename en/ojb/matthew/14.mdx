<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 3:1; MARKOS 8:15; LUKAS 9:7–9"> At that time reports about Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach came to the oznayim (ears) of Herod the Tetrarch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:14; YOCHANAN 10:41; MARKOS 8:28"> And he said to his servants, This one is Yochanan of the tevilah of teshuva. He has had his Techiyah (Resurrection) from HaMesim (the Dead) and for this reason the moftim (wonders) are working in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MARKOS 6:17; LUKAS 3:19–20; MATTITYAHU 4:12"> For Herod had Yochanan arrested and bound him and threw him into the beit hasohar (prison), on account of Herodias, the wife of his brother Philippos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:21; VAYIKRA 18:16; MARKOS 6:18"> For Yochanan kept on saying to him, It is asur for you to have her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:26; MATTITYAHU 11:9; MARKOS 6:19–20"> And &#91;although&#93; Herod was desiring to kill Yochanan, he feared the crowd, because they considered Yochanan a Navi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MARKOS 6:21–23; BERESHIS 40:20; HOSHEA 1:5–6"> Now at the yom huledet celebration of Herod, it came about that the daughter of Herodias danced before them and Herod was pleased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ESTHER 5:3; ESTHER 5:6; ESTHER 7:2"> Herod, therefore, with a shevuah, promised to give to her whatever she might ask.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MARKOS 6:24; MISHLE 29:10; MISHLE 1:16"> So she, prompted by her mother, said, Give to me here upon a serving tray the rosh (head) Yochanan of the tevilah of teshuva.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:12–16; SHMUEL ALEF 14:28; MARKOS 6:26"> And, although grieving, the king commanded &#91;it&#93; to be given, on account of the shevuot (oaths) and the fellow muzmanim (guests).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 17:12; MATTITYAHU 21:35–36; LUKAS 9:9"> And he sent and had Yochanan beheaded in the beit hasohar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HISGALUS 16:6; BERESHIS 49:7; YECHEZKEL 16:3–4"> And the rosh Yochanan of the tevilah of teshuva was brought upon a serving tray, and it was presented to the girl and she presented it to her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:2; MATTITYAHU 27:58–61"> And Yochananʼs talmidim approached and carried away the niftar (deceased person) and buried him. Then they went and reported &#91;it&#93; to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 9:10–17; MATTITYAHU 14:1–2; MARKOS 6:30–44"> And when he heard &#91;this&#93;, he withdrew from there in a sirah (boat) to a desolate place by himself. And when the multitudes heard &#91;this&#93;, they followed Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach by land from the shtetlach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:15; MATTITYAHU 9:36; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:17"> And having gone out, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach saw a great multitude, and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach felt rachmei shomayim (heavenly compassion) for them and brought refuah (healing) to their cholim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:23; MARKOS 8:3; MARKOS 6:35–36"> Now when erev (evening) came, Moshiachʼs talmidim came to him, saying, This place is desolate and the time is late. Dismiss the multitude, so that, having gone into the shtetlach, they may buy for themselves okhel (food).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:42–44; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:2–3; KOHELET 11:2"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, They have no need to go away. You yourselves give them something to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:33–34; MARKOS 6:37–38; LUKAS 9:13"> But Moshiachʼs talmidim say to him, We do not have here anything except five loaves and of dagim (fish), only two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Bring them here to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARKOS 8:6–7; LUKAS 24:30; SHMUEL ALEF 9:13"> And having commanded the multitudes to recline on the grass<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="as at tish">a</a></sup>and having taken the five loaves and the two dagim, and having looked up to Shomayim, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said a bracha over the okhel, and having offered the betziat halechem (the breaking of the bread), Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach gave the lechem to the talmidim, and Moshiachʼs talmidim served the lechem to the multitudes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:12; MARKOS 6:42–44; MATTITYAHU 16:8–10"> And everyone ate and they were satisfied, and they took away shirayim (Rebbeʼs remainders, leftovers), shneym asar (twelve) baskets full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:19; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:8–11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:4"> And the ones eating were about chamesh elafim (five thousand) men, apart from the nashim and yeladim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MARKOS 6:45–51; YOCHANAN 6:15–21; MATTITYAHU 15:39"> And immediately Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach compelled the talmidim to board a sirah (boat) and to go on ahead of him to the other side until he might send away the multitudes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKAS 6:12; MARKOS 6:46; MATTITYAHU 26:36"> And having sent away the multitudes, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach went up to the mountain by himself to daven. Now when erev had come, he was alone there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:18; MARKOS 6:48; MATTITYAHU 8:24"> Now the sirah (boat), being tossed by the waves for the wind was against them, was by this time many stadia distant from the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="IYOV 9:8; YOCHANAN 6:19; MARKOS 6:48"> Now in the fourth watch of the night (between three o'clock and six o'clock in the morning), Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach came to them, walking on the lake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MARKOS 6:49–50; HISGALUS 1:17; LUKAS 1:11–12"> But Moshiachʼs talmidim, having seen him walking on the lake, were terribly shaken, saying, It is a ruach refaim (an apparition)! From pachad they cried out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:11; HISGALUS 1:17–18; YOCHANAN 16:33"> And immediately Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach spoke to them, saying, Chazak! Ani Hu.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ex 3:14">b</a></sup>(I am he) Do not be afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:68; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:3; MATTITYAHU 26:33–35"> And Kefa said in reply to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Adoni, if it is really you, command me to come to you al pnei hamayim (on the surface of the waters).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:13; MARKOS 9:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:16"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Boh! (Come!) And having gone down from the sirah (boat), Kefa walked al pnei hamayim (on the surface of the waters) and came toward him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EKHAH 3:54–57; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:16–17; MARKOS 14:38"> And seeing the strong wind, Kefa was afraid, and having begun to sink, Kefa cried out, saying, Azreini! Adoni, hoshieini! (Help! L-rd, save me!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:6–8; TEHILLIM 138:7; BERESHIS 22:14"> And immediately Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, having stretched out (his) hand, took hold of Kefa and says to him, One of little emunah, why did you doubt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="TEHILLIM 107:29–30; MARKOS 4:41; YOCHANAN 6:21"> And as they were going up into the sirah (boat), the wind became still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:27; TEHILLIM 2:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:4"> And the ones in the sirah (boat) fell down before Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, saying, Beemes (Actually) you are the Ben HaElohim!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MARKOS 6:53–56; LUKAS 5:1; YOCHANAN 6:24–25"> And having crossed over, they came onto the land at Gennesaret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:24–25; MARKOS 6:55; MARKOS 1:28–34"> And having recognized him, the men of that place sent into all the surrounding region around, and they brought to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach all the cholim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LUKAS 6:19; MARKOS 3:10; SHEMOT 28:33–43"> and they were appealing to him that they might touch even the Moshiachʼs tzitzit on his garment, and all who did, received Moshiachʼs refuah (healing).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 53:5">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
