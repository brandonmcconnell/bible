<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEVARIM 23:25; MARKOS 2:23–28; LUKAS 6:1–5"> At that time Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach went on Shabbos through the grainfields. And his talmidim were hungry and began to pick the heads of wheat and to eat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:10; YOCHANAN 7:21–24; LUKAS 14:3"> But the Perushim, seeing this, said to Moshiach: Hinei! Your talmidim are doing what is asur on Shabbos!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 21:3–6; LUKAS 6:3; MARKOS 2:25–26"> But Moshiach said to them, Have you not read what Dovid HaMelech did when he and those with him were hungry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="VAYIKRA 24:5–9; SHEMOT 25:30; VAYIKRA 8:31"> how he entered into the Beis Hashem and ate the Lechem HaPanim (the Bread of the Presence), which was not mutar (permissible) for him to eat nor the ones with him, but only the kohanim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 28:9–10; YOCHANAN 7:22–23; NECHEMYAH 13:17"> Or have you not read in the Torah that on Shabbos the kohanim are mechallel Shabbos in the Beis Hamikdash and yet are blameless?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Num 28:9-10">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:41–42; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 6:18; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:4–5"> But I say to you that something greater than the Beis Hamikdash is here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HOSHEA 6:6; MATTITYAHU 9:13; MICHOH 6:6–8"> But if you'd had daas what this means, CHESED CHAFATZTI VLO ZEVACH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hos 6:6">b</a></sup>(I desire mercy and not sacrifice), you would not have condemned the innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MARKOS 2:28; LUKAS 6:5; YOCHANAN 5:17–23"> For the Bar Enosh (Moshiach) is Adon HaShabbos.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MARKOS 3:1–6; LUKAS 6:6–11"> And having gone from there, he went into their shul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKAS 13:14; YOCHANAN 9:16; YOCHANAN 8:6"> And hinei! There was a man with a withered hand. And they questioned Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, asking, Is it mutar (permissible) to bring refuah (healing) on Shabbos? They did this in order to bring accusation against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEVARIM 22:4; LUKAS 14:5; LUKAS 13:15–17"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, What man will there be among you, who owns a keves (lamb), and if it falls into a pit on Shabbos, will not take hold of it and lift it out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:26; LUKAS 6:9; MARKOS 3:4"> Therefore, how much more valuable is a man than a keves. For this reason, it is mutar (permissible) on Shabbos to do HaTov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 13:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:7–8"> Then Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to the man, Stretch out your hand. And the man stretched out his hand. And it was restored to health, as sound as the other hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:53; YOCHANAN 10:39; MARKOS 3:6"> But the Perushim went out and took counsel against Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach so that they might destroy him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MARKOS 3:7–12; MATTITYAHU 10:23; MATTITYAHU 19:2"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, having da'as of this, departed from there. And many multitudes followed Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, and he brought refuah to them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 8:4; MATTITYAHU 9:30; MATTITYAHU 17:9"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach warned them that they should not make him manifest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YESHAYAH 42:1–4; YESHAYAH 42:9; MATTITYAHU 13:35"> This was to fulfill what was spoken through Yeshayah HaNavi, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YESHAYAH 42:1; LUKAS 4:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:38"> HEN AVDI ETMACH BO, BECHIRI RATZTAH NAFSHI, NATATI RUCHI ALAV, MISHPAT LAGOYIM YOTZI, (Here is my Servant, whom I uphold, my Chosen One in whom my Soul delights, I will put my Spirit on him, he will bring Justice to the Nations),</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:29; YOCHANAN 18:36–38; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:24–25"> LO YITZAK VLO YISSAH VLO YASHMIA BACHUTZ KOLO (He will not shout nor cry out nor raise the sound of his voice in the street.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YESHAYAH 42:3–4; YECHEZKEL 34:16; YESHAYAH 61:1–3"> KANEH RATZUTZ LO YISHBOR UFISHTAH CHEHAH LO YECHABENAH LE-EMET YOTZI MISHPAT (A bruised reed he will not break nor a smoldering wick will he snuff out) until Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach leads mishpat (justice) to victory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YESHAYAH 11:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:12–13; YESHAYAH 42:4"> And in his Name, HAGOYIM will put their tikvah (hope).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 42:1-4; Isa 11:10TARGUM HA-SHIVIM">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKAS 11:14–15; MARKOS 7:35–37; YESHAYAH 29:18"> Then a man, blind and mute, who was possessed by shedim, was brought to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, and he brought refuah to him, so that the mute man was able to speak and to see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:27; YOCHANAN 4:29; MATTITYAHU 22:42–43"> And all the multitudes were amazed and they were saying, Surely this is not the Ben Dovid,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Moshiach">d</a></sup>is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MARKOS 3:22; MATTITYAHU 9:34; LUKAS 11:15"> But, having heard this, the Perushim said, This one does not cast out shedim except by Baal-zibbul, the Sar HaShedim (the Prince of Demons).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:15; YIRMEYAH 17:10; MATTITYAHU 9:4"> But having daas of their thoughts, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Every malchut that is divided against itself is made desolate, and every ir (city) and bais (house) that is divided against itself will not stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:13; HISGALUS 20:2–3; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:19"> And if HaSatan casts out HaSatan, he is divided against himself. How, therefore, will the Malchut HaSatan stand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:13–16; MARKOS 9:38–39; LUKAS 9:49–50"> And if by Baal-zibbul I cast out the shedim, by whom do your banim (sons) cast them out? Therefore, they will be your shofetim (judges).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKAS 11:20; DANIEL 7:14; MATTITYAHU 6:33"> But if by the Ruach Hakodesh I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Moshiach">e</a></sup>cast out the shedim, then upon you the Malchut Hashem has arrived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YESHAYAH 49:24; MARKOS 3:27; LUKAS 11:21–22"> Or how is someone able to enter the Bayit HaGibbor and confiscate his furnishings unless he binds the Gibbor first. Then he will plunder the Bayit HaGibbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MARKOS 9:40; LUKAS 11:23; YEHOSHUA 24:15"> The one not with me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Moshiach">f</a></sup>is against me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="anti-Moshiach">g</a></sup>And the one not gathering with me scatters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LUKAS 12:10; MARKOS 3:28–30; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:29"> Therefore, I say to you, every chet (sin) and gidduf (blasphemy) will be forgiven men, but whoever commits Chillul Hashem against the Ruach Hakodesh will not be forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MARKOS 3:29; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:26–29; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:4–6"> And whoever speaks a word against the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Moshiach">h</a></sup>will be granted selicha (forgiveness), but whoever speaks against the Ruach Hakodesh, selicha will not be granted him either in the Olam Hazeh or in the Olam Habah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKAS 6:43–44; MATTITYAHU 7:16–20; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:8"> Either make the etz (tree) tov (good) and its pri (fruit) will be tov, or make the etz nishchat (corrupt) and its pri will be rah; for by its pri the etz is known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LUKAS 6:45; MATTITYAHU 15:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:29"> You banim of nechashim (sons of snakes), how are you able to speak tovot (good things), for out of the abundance of the lev (heart) the mouth speaks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MISHLE 15:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:6; MISHLE 15:28"> The ish tov (good man) out of the good otzar (treasure) brings forth good; and the ish rah out of the evil otzar brings forth evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="KOHELET 12:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:16; HISGALUS 20:12"> But I say to you, that for every careless lashon horah (evil speech) that men speak, they will be called to account on the Yom HaDin (the Day of Judgment).</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MISHLE 13:3; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:21–25"> For by your devarim you will be pronounced tzodek (righteous), and by your devarim you will be charged with guilt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:22; MARKOS 8:11–12; LUKAS 11:16"> Then some of the Sofrim and Perushim answered Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, saying, Rabbi, we desire to see an ot (miraculous sign) from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:4; LUKAS 11:29–32; MARKOS 8:38"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach answered them, Dor rah umnaef (an evil and adulterous generation) seeks for an ot (miraculous sign), but no ot will be given it except the ot of Yonah HaNavi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="YONAH 1:17; MATTITYAHU 27:63–64; MATTITYAHU 16:21"> For just as Yonah was in the belly of the great fish SHLOSHAH YAMIM USHLOSHAH LAILAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Jon 2:1">i</a></sup>(three days and three nights), so will be the Bar Enosh (Moshiach) in the heart of the earth SHLOSHAH YAMIM USHLOSHAH LAILOT (three days and three nights).</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:42; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:7; MATTITYAHU 12:6"> The men of Nineveh will stand up at the Yom HaDin with this generation and they will condemn it, for the men of Nineveh made teshuva at the darshenen (preaching) of Yonah, and, hinei, something much greater than Yonah is here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 10:1–13; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:1–12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:2–4"> The Queen of the South will be made to stand up alive at the Yom HaDin with this generation and will condemn it, for she came from the ends of the earth to listen to the chochmah of Sh'lomo and, hinei, something much greater than Sh'lomo is here.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LUKAS 11:24–26; LUKAS 8:28–32; MATTITYAHU 8:29"> Now when the ruach hatameh (unclean spirit) goes out from the man, it goes through dry places seeking a manoach (resting place) and it does not find one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:4; HISGALUS 13:8–9; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:4–5"> Then it says, Into my haunt I will return from where I came out. And having come out, it finds the bais standing empty, having been swept and having been put beseder (in order).</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="LUKAS 11:26; MARKOS 5:9; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:4–8"> Then it goes and takes along with itself seven other ruchot more evil than itself and, having entered the residence, it sets up house there, and the last state of that man has become worse than the first. Thus it will be with this evil generation also.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:55; YOCHANAN 2:12; YOCHANAN 7:3"> While he was still speaking to the multitudes, hinei, Moshiachʼs Em and achim stood outside, seeking to speak with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> And someone said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Hinei! Your Em and your achim have stood outside seeking to speak with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="MARKOS 3:32–33; MATTITYAHU 10:37; LUKAS 2:52"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said in reply to the one speaking with him, Who is my Em and who are my achim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="MARKOS 3:34; YOCHANAN 17:8–9; YOCHANAN 20:17–20"> And stretching out his hand upon his talmidim, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Hinei, my Em and my achim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="LUKAS 8:21; MARKOS 3:35; YOCHANAN 15:14"> For whoever does the ratzon (will) of Avi sh'ba'Shomayim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="see Mt 6:10; 26:39">j</a></sup>he is my ach (brother) and achot (sister) and Em (Mother).</span>
</div>
