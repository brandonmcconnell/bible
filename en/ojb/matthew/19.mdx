<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:40; MATTITYAHU 19:1–9; MATTITYAHU 7:28"> And it came to pass when Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach finished these devarim, he departed from the Galil and came into the regions of Yehudah east of the Yarden River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:15; MARKOS 6:55–56; MATTITYAHU 15:30–31"> And a great multitude followed Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, who brought refuah (healing) to them there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MARKOS 10:2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:9; MATTITYAHU 16:1"> And the Perushim approached him with a she'elah to test him, and they asked him, Is it mutar (permissible) for a man to give a get (divorce) to his wife for any and every reason?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 2:23; BERESHIS 1:27; BERESHIS 5:2"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said in reply, Have you not read that HaBoreh (The Creator, Yotzer) bereshis (in the beginning) created them zachar (male) and nekevah (female)?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gn 1:27; 5:2">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BERESHIS 2:21–24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:2"> And he said, AL KEN, YAAZAV ISH ES AVIV V'ES IMMO V'DAVAK B'ISHTO V'HAYU L'VASAR ECHAD (a man will leave his father and his mother and will be joined to his isha (wife), and the two will be one flesh).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gn 2:24">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:4; MARKOS 10:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:10–14"> So they are no longer shnayim (two) but basar echad (one flesh). Therefore, whatever Hashem joined together, let no man divide asunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 24:1–4; MATTITYAHU 5:31; MARKOS 10:4"> They say to him, Why then did Moshe Rabbenu give the mitzvah to give the get, the sefer keritut, and send her away?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MALACHI 2:13–14; BERESHIS 2:24; MARKOS 10:5"> He says to them, Moshe, because of your hardness of heart, permitted you to give the get to your wives; but from bereshis (in the beginning), however, it was not so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:32; LUKAS 16:18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:39"> But I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Moshiach">c</a></sup>say to you that whoever, the case of zenut (fornication) being excepted, gives the get to his wife and marries another, is guilty of niuf (adultery).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:32–35; MISHLE 19:13–14; MISHLE 21:19"> Moshiachʼs talmidim say to him, If thus is the case of the man with the wife, nikhnas l'chuppah (to be wed) is not advantageous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:35; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:7–9"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Not everyone is able to accept this dvar torah, but rather those to whom it has been given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:32–38; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:5; YESHAYAH 56:3–4"> For there are sarisim (eunuchs) who from the womb of their Em were born thus, and there are sarisim who were made sarisim by men, and there are sarisim who make sarisim of themselves for the sake of the Malchut HaShomayim. The one able to receive this, let him receive it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MARKOS 10:13–16; LUKAS 18:15–17; MATTITYAHU 18:2–5"> Then yeladim were brought to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach that he might place his hands on them and say a bracha. However, Moshiachʼs talmidim rebuked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:3; LUKAS 18:16–17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:20"> But he said, Permit the yeladim to come to me, and forbid them not, for of such is the Malchut HaShomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MARKOS 10:16; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:14"> And having placed his hands on the yeladim, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach departed from there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MARKOS 10:17–30; YOCHANAN 3:15; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:21"> And one, having approached Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, said, Rebbe, what mitzvah, what good may I do that I may have Chayyei Olam?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:5; LUKAS 10:26–28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:11–13"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Why do you ask me about the good? There is only One who is good, nu? But if you wish to enter into Chayyim (Life), do not fail to be shomer mitzvot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEVARIM 5:16–21; SHEMOT 20:12–17; MARKOS 10:19"> He says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Which mitzvah? And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, LO TIRTZACH, LO TINAF, LO TIGNOV, LO TAANEH VREIACHAH ED SHAKER, (You shall not murder, commit adultery, steal, bear false witness),</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:18; SHEMOT 20:12; MATTITYAHU 22:39"> KABEID ES AVICHA VES IMMECHA,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ex 20:12-16; Dt 5:16-20">d</a></sup>V'AHAVTA L'REACHA KAMOCHA<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Lv 19:18">e</a></sup>(Honor your father and your mother, and you shall love your neighbor as yourself).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:24; MARKOS 10:20–21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:6"> The bocher (bachelor, young man) says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, I was shomer mitzvot and frum in all these things, so where do I still fall short?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MARKOS 10:21; LUKAS 18:22; LUKAS 12:33"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to him, If you wish to be tamim (perfect), if you wish to have shelemut (perfection, completeness), then go and sell your possessions and give to the aniyim (poor) and you will have otzar (treasure) in Shomayim. Then come and follow me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Moshiach">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:24; YECHEZKEL 33:31; MATTITYAHU 13:22"> But having heard the divrei Moshiach, the young man went away with agmat nefesh (grief), for he was having many possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:9–10; MATTITYAHU 13:22; MARKOS 10:23"> And he said to his talmidim, Omein, I say to you that an oisher with difficulty will enter into the Malchut HaShomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:44; LUKAS 18:25; MARKOS 10:24–25"> And again I say to you, it is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for an oisher to enter into the Malchut HaShomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:13; MATTITYAHU 24:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:5–7"> And when Moshiachʼs talmidim heard this, they were exceedingly astounded, saying, Who then is able to receive the Yeshu'at Eloheinu?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:27; LUKAS 1:37; IYOV 42:2"> And having looked upon them, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, With Bnei Adam it is impossible; but with Hashem all things are possible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:8; LUKAS 18:28; LUKAS 5:11"> Then Kefa said in reply to him, Hinei! We have left everything and followed you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="as Moshiach">g</a></sup>! Where does that leave us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKAS 22:28–30; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:2–3; MATTITYAHU 25:31"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Omein, I say to you, that you, the ones having followed me, when the Briah (Creation) becomes Chadasha (New), when the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Moshiach">h</a></sup>sits upon his Kisei Kavod (Throne of Glory), you also will sit upon the Shneym Asar Kisot (Twelve Thrones) judging the Shneym Asar Shivtei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LUKAS 18:29–30; MARKOS 10:29–30; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:8"> And anyone who left bais or achim or achayot or Abba or Em or banim or sadot (fields) on account of me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Moshiach">i</a></sup>and my Name (Yehoshua, Yeshua), will receive a hundred times over and will inherit Chayyei Olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:16; LUKAS 13:30; MARKOS 10:31"> And many who are Rishonim (first ones) will be Acharonim (last ones), and the Acharonim, Rishonim.</span>
