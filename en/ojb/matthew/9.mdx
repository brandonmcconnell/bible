<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:13; LUKAS 8:37; MATTITYAHU 8:18"> And, having embarked into a sirah (boat), Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach crossed over<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="to the other side of the lake">a</a></sup>and came into his own shtetl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MARKOS 2:1–12; LUKAS 5:18–26; YIRMEYAH 31:33–34"> And hinei! They brought to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach a paralytic lying upon a mat. Having seen their emunah, he said to the paralytic, Chazak! (Be strong!) Selicha (forgiveness) is granted on your averos (sins)!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:65; LUKAS 5:21; VAYIKRA 24:16"> And hinei! Some of the Sofrim said to themselves, This one commits Chillul Hashem!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lv 22:32">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKAS 6:8; MATTITYAHU 12:25; HISGALUS 2:23"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, reading their minds, said, Why are you thinking ra'ah (evil) in your levavot (hearts)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:8–14; MARKOS 2:9–12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:16"> For which is easier to say, Selicha is granted on your averos! Or…Arise and walk!?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YESHAYAH 43:25; MATTITYAHU 9:5; YOCHANAN 20:21–23"> But in order that you may have da'as that the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Moshiach">c</a></sup>has samchut (authority) on earth to pronounce selicha (forgiveness) on chattaim (sins) — Moshiach then declared to the paralytic, Arise, take up your mat and go to your bais (house).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And arising, he went away to his bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKAS 7:16; MATTITYAHU 15:31; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:21"> And having witnessed this, the multitudes experienced yirat Shomayim and gave kavod to Hashem, who had given such samchut to men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 5:27–38; MARKOS 2:14–22; LUKAS 19:2–10"> And going away from there, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach saw a man called Mattityahu sitting in the tax office. And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to him, Follow me. And Mattityahu arose and followed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:13–16; LUKAS 5:29–32; MARKOS 2:15–17"> And it came about that while Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was sitting bimesibba (reclining) at tish (table) in the bais, hinei! Many mochesim (tax collectors) and chote'im (sinners) came and were sitting bimesibba (reclining) at tish (table) with Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and his talmidim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:19; LUKAS 5:30; MARKOS 2:16"> When the Perushim saw this, they were saying to Moshiachʼs talmidim, Why does your Rebbe eat with the mochesim and the chote'im?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKAS 5:31; HISGALUS 3:17–18; MARKOS 2:17"> But when Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach heard this, he said, It is not the bariim (healthy ones) who have need of a rofeh (physician), but the cholim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MARKOS 2:17; LUKAS 19:10; HOSHEA 6:6"> Go and learn what this means: CHESED CHAFATZTI VLO ZEVACH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hos 6:6">d</a></sup>(I desire loving kindness and not sacrifice), for I have not come to call the tzaddikim but the chote'im (sinners).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="to teshuva">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 5:33–39; MARKOS 2:18–22; MATTITYAHU 6:16"> Then Yochananʼs talmidim approached Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, saying, Why do we and the Perushim often undergo tzomot (fasts), but your talmidim do not undergo tzomot?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:9; YOCHANAN 3:29; HISGALUS 21:2"> And Moshiach said to them, Surely the Bnei HaChuppah (wedding invitees) are not able to act as avelim (mourners) as long as the Choson (Bridegroom),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="i.e., Moshiach">f</a></sup>is with them. But the days will come when the Choson is taken away from them, and then they will undergo tzomot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:1–2; YOCHANAN 16:12; TEHILLIM 125:3"> No one sews a patch of unshrunk cloth on an old garment, for the patch pulls away from the garment, and a worse tear results.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 9:4; IYOV 32:19; TEHILLIM 119:83"> Neither do men put yayin chadash (new wine) into old wineskins; otherwise, the wineskins burst, and the wine is spilled, and the wineskins are ruined. But they pour yayin chadash into new wineskins, and both are preserved.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKAS 8:41–56; MARKOS 5:22–43; MATTITYAHU 8:2"> While Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was speaking these things to them one of the nichbadim (dignitaries)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="of the shul">g</a></sup>came and fell down before Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, saying, The bat (daughter) of me has just become niftar (passed away), but come lay your hand upon her and she will live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:38; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:9–10; MATTITYAHU 8:7"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach got up and, with his talmidim, followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKAS 8:43–56; MATTITYAHU 14:36; MARKOS 6:56"> And hinei! An isha (woman) hemorrhaging twelve years approached him from behind and touched the garment of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiachʼs tzitzit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MARKOS 5:26–33; LUKAS 8:45–47; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:12"> For she was saying to herself, If only I might touch his garment, I will receive refuah (healing).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKAS 7:50; MATTITYAHU 15:28; MATTITYAHU 9:2"> Turning and seeing her, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Chazak, bat (daughter) of me! Your emunah has brought you refuah. And the isha was healed from that hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:25; MARKOS 5:35–40; LUKAS 8:49–51"> And having come into the bais (house) of the nichbad (dignitary), and having seen the flute players and the unruly crowd,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:10; YOCHANAN 11:11–13; MATTITYAHU 27:39–43"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying, Go away! For the yaldah is not among the mesim — she sleeps! And they were making leitzonus (mockery, fun) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MARKOS 9:27; MARKOS 5:41; LUKAS 8:54"> But when the crowd had been put outside, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach went in and took the yaldah by the hand and the yaldah was made to stand up alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:24; MATTITYAHU 14:1–2; MARKOS 1:45"> And this report went out into all that region.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:22; MATTITYAHU 20:30–31; MARKOS 10:46–48"> And as Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach went on from there, two ivrim (blind men) followed him, shouting loudly, Chaneinu, Ben Dovid!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MARKOS 9:23–24; MATTITYAHU 13:58; MATTITYAHU 8:2"> And when Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ben Dovid">h</a></sup>entered the bais (house), the ivrim (blind men) approached him, and he says to them, Do you have emunah that I am able to do this? They say to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Ken, Adoneinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:28; MARKOS 10:52; MATTITYAHU 8:13"> Then Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach touched their eyes, saying, According to your emunah, let it be done for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 8:4; LUKAS 5:14; MATTITYAHU 17:9"> And their eyes were opened. Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach sternly warned them, saying, See to it that no one knows!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MARKOS 7:36; MATTITYAHU 9:26; MARKOS 1:44–45"> But having gone out, they made him known in that entire district.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:24; MATTITYAHU 12:22–24; LUKAS 11:14"> After they had departed, the people brought to him a mute man who was possessed by shedim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YESHAYAH 35:6; MARKOS 7:32–37; SHEMOT 4:11–12"> And when the shedim had been cast out by Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, the mute man spoke. And the multitudes marveled, saying, Never was anything like this seen in Eretz Yisroel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LUKAS 11:15; MARKOS 3:22; MATTITYAHU 12:23–24"> But the Perushim said, It was by the Sar HaShedim (the Prince of Demons) that this man casts out shedim!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:23–24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:38; MARKOS 6:56"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach went about all the shtetlach and villages exercising the ministry of moreh (teacher) in their shuls and of darshan (preacher) of the Besuras HaGeulah of the Malchut Hashem and bringing refuah to all the cholim, every machla (disease) and every illness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MARKOS 6:34; BAMIDBAR 27:17; YIRMEYAH 50:6"> And looking out on the multitudes, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach felt rachmei Shomayim (heavenly compassion and mercy) for his people, for they were distressed and weary KATZON ASHER EIN LAHEM ROEH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Num 27:17">i</a></sup>(like sheep without a shepherd).</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKAS 10:2; MARKOS 16:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:9"> Then Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to his talmidim, The katzir (harvest) is indeed great but the kotzerim (harvesters) are few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LUKAS 10:1–2; YIRMEYAH 3:15; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:1"> Therefore, offer tefillos to the Adon HaKatzir (the L-rd of the Harvest) that He may send out Kotzerim into His Katzir.</span>
</div>
