<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARKOS 9:33–37; LUKAS 9:46–48; MATTITYAHU 20:20–28"> At that time, the talmidim approached Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach with this she'elah saying, Who then is greatest in the Malchut HaShomayim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:13–14; MARKOS 9:36–37; MELACHIM ALEF 3:7"> And having called a yeled to himself, he set him before them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MARKOS 10:14–15; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:2; MATTITYAHU 19:14"> and said, Omein, I say to you, unless you change inwardly and have a complete turn around and become like yeladim, you shall never enter the Malchut HaShomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10; LUKAS 14:11; MARKOS 10:43"> Therefore, whoever humbles himself as this yeled, he is the greatest in the Malchut HaShomayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:40; MARKOS 9:37; YOCHANAN 13:20"> And whoever receives one such yeled in my Name receives me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Moshiach">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MARKOS 9:42; LUKAS 17:1–2; MATTITYAHU 18:10"> But whoever causes a michshol (stumbling block) for one of these little ones, who have emunah in me, it is better for him that a heavy millstone be hung around his neck, and that he be drowned in the depth of the sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKAS 17:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:19; MATTITYAHU 26:24"> Oy lOlam (Woe to the World) because of its michsholim (stumbling blocks)! For it is inevitable that michsholim come; but oy to that man through whom the michshol comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:29–30; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:8–9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:12"> And if your hand or your foot causes a michshol for you, cut it off, and throw it from you. It is better for you to enter Chayyim crippled or as one of the pisechim (lame), than, having two hands and two feet, to be cast into the Eish Olam (Eternal Fire).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:22; MATTITYAHU 18:8; MATTITYAHU 5:29"> And if your eye causes a michshol for you, pluck it out and throw it from you. It is better for you to enter Chayyim one-eyed than, having two eyes, to be cast into the Gehinnom HaEish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 91:11; TEHILLIM 34:7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:14"> See to it that you do not look down on one of these little ones, for I say to you that their malachim which are in Shomayim continually behold the face of Avi sh'ba'Shomayim (my Father who is in Heaven).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Moshiach">b</a></sup>came to save that which was lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 34:12; LUKAS 15:4–7; YECHEZKEL 34:16"> What do you think? If any man owns a hundred kevesim (sheep) and one of them has gone astray, does he not leave the ninety-nine on the hillsides and go and search for the one that is straying?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TZEFANYAH 3:17; LUKAS 15:5–10; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13"> And if it turns out that he finds it, beemes I say to you, he has more simcha (joy) over it than over the ninety-nine which have not gone astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 17:12; YOCHANAN 6:39–40; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9"> Thus it is not the ratzon Hashem, the will of your Av sh'ba'Shomayim, that one of these little ones perish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:17; LUKAS 17:3–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:1"> And if your Ach b'Moshiach sins against you, go and reprove him in private, just between the two of you; if he listens to you, you have gained your Ach b'Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEVARIM 19:15; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:1; YOCHANAN 8:17"> But if he does not listen to you, take one or two more Achim b'Moshiach with you, so that by the PI SHNAYIM SHLOSHAH EDIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Dt 19:15">c</a></sup>(by the mouth of two or three witnesses) every word shall be established.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:9–7; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:14–15"> But if he refuses to listen to them, speak to the shtiebel kehillah, and if he even refuses to hear the kehillah, let him be to you as the Goy (heathen, pagan) and the moches (tax-collector).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:19; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:10; YOCHANAN 20:23"> Omein, I say to you, whatever you bind as asur (prohibited) on haaretz will have been bound in Shomayim, and whatever you permit as mutar on haaretz is mutar (permitted) in Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:7; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:14–16; MARKOS 11:24"> Again, omein, I say to you, that if two of you will be in agreement about anything on haaretz that you petition for, it will be done for them by Avi sh'ba'Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:4; HISGALUS 21:3; MATTITYAHU 28:20"> For where two or three are gathered as a Chavurah and are a Kehillah in my name,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Moshiach">d</a></sup>there I am in the midst of them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKAS 17:3–4; MATTITYAHU 18:15"> Then, having approached, Kefa said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach: Adoni, how often will my brother sin against me and I will grant to him selicha (forgiveness)? As many as shevah (seven) times?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:13; MARKOS 11:25; MATTITYAHU 6:14–15"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to Kefa, I do not say to you as many as shevah, but as many as shivim (seventy) times shevah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:12; MATTITYAHU 3:2"> Therefore, the Malchut HaShomayim is like a melech, a king, who wished to settle accounts with his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="TEHILLIM 38:4; TEHILLIM 130:3–4; LUKAS 16:5"> And having begun to settle accounts, a debtor owing ten thousand talents (i.e., 60,000,000 denarii) was brought to the melech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:1; NECHEMYAH 5:5; LUKAS 7:42"> And, as this debtor did not have the means to pay what was owed his adon, the king commanded that he and his wife and children and everything he possessed be sold and the debt repaid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:29; LUKAS 7:43; MATTITYAHU 8:2"> Therefore, crying out for rachamim (mercy) and falling down before him, the servant said, Have savlanut (patience) with me and I will pay back to you everything!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="TEHILLIM 145:8; NECHEMYAH 9:17; TEHILLIM 86:15"> And out of rachamanut (compassion), the adon of that servant pardoned him and forgave the choiv (debt).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:2; YECHEZKEL 45:9; DEVARIM 15:2"> Then the debtor went out and found one of the other fellow servants who happened to owe the debtor one hundred denarii. And seizing and choking the servant, the debtor said, Repay what you owe me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:26; MATTITYAHU 6:12; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:18–19"> And, falling down, the servant said to the debtor, Have savlanut (patience) with me and I will repay you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:27; MELACHIM ALEF 21:27–29"> But the debtor was not willing. Then the debtor went and threw the servant into the beit hasohar (prison) until he should repay the debt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:15; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:3; MARKOS 3:5"> Therefore, when the manʼs fellow servants saw what had taken place, great was their agmat nefesh (grief), and they went and reported to their adon all that had taken place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LUKAS 19:22; MATTITYAHU 25:26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:19"> Then having summoned the debtor, his adon said to him, Wicked servant, all that choiv (debt) I forgave you, because you begged me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:13; MATTITYAHU 6:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:32–2"> Was it not necessary also for you to have rachamim (mercy) upon your fellow servant?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:25–26; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13; LUKAS 12:58–59"> And in charon af (wrath), his adon handed the debtor over to the keepers of the beit hasohar until the debtor should repay the entire choiv (debt).</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:14–15; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13; LUKAS 6:37–38"> Thus also Avi sh'ba'Shomayim will do to you, unless you grant selicha (forgiveness), each one to his brother from your lev (heart).</span>
</div>
