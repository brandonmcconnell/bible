<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARKOS 3:22; MATTITYAHU 23:2; LUKAS 5:21"> Then Perushim and Sofrim (rabbonim) from Yerushalayim approach Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKAS 11:38; MARKOS 7:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:20–23"> Why do your talmidim transgress the Masoret HaZekenim (the Tradition of the Elders)? For they do not do the netilat yadayim before meals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:23; LETTER TO TITOS 1:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:8"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, in reply to them, said, Why &#91;do&#93; also you transgress the mitzvat Hashem for the sake of your own masoros?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:12; VAYIKRA 20:9; SHEMOT 21:17"> For Hashem has said, KABED ES AVICHA VES IMMECHA<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ex 20:12; Dt 5:16">a</a></sup>(honor your father and your mother), and MKALEL AVIV VIMMO MOT YUMAT<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ex 21:17; Lv 20:9">b</a></sup>(the one cursing his father and mother, to die he must die).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MARKOS 7:10–13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:29; MATTITYAHU 23:16–18"> But you say, Whoever says to his abba or his em, whatever support you might have had from me, &#91;it is&#93; a gift.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e., korban, dedicated to G-d">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:8; YIRMEYAH 8:8; MARKOS 7:13"> By no means does &#91;anyone who says this&#93; honor his abba or em. And you nullify the Dvar Hashem on account of your masoros.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MARKOS 7:6; MATTITYAHU 23:23–29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:25–27"> You tzevuim, well did Yeshayah give a dvar hanevuah concerning you, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YESHAYAH 29:13; YECHEZKEL 33:31; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:21"> YAAN KI NIGASH HAAM HAZEH BEFIV UVISHFATAV KI-BDUNI VLIBO RICHAK MIMENI, (This people with their lips honor me, but their heart is far away from me),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YESHAYAH 29:13; MARKOS 7:7; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:4"> VATEHI YIRATAM OTI MITZVAT ANASHIM MELUMADAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 29:13">d</a></sup>(and in vain do they worship me, teaching as doctrines the mitzvot of &#91;mere&#93; men).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MARKOS 7:14–15; LUKAS 20:45–47; LUKAS 24:45"> And having summoned the multitude, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Listen and understand!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MARKOS 7:15; MATTITYAHU 15:18–20; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:5–8"> It is not the thing entering into the mouth that makes the man tameh (unclean), but the thing going out from the mouth, this makes the man tameh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:13–14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:32–33; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:5"> Then Moshiachʼs talmidim approached and say to him, Do you know that the Perushim took offense when they heard &#91;this&#93; dvar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:2; YESHAYAH 60:21; YOCHANAN 15:6"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, in reply, said, Every plant which Avi sh'ba'Shomayim did not plant will be uprooted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 6:39; YESHAYAH 56:10; MATTITYAHU 23:16–24"> Leave them. They are blind morei derech (guides, teachers) of &#91;the&#93; blind. And if the ivver (blind man) leads the ivrim (blind), both will fall into a pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:36; MARKOS 4:34; YOCHANAN 16:29"> And Kefa said in reply to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Explain to us the mashal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:9; MARKOS 7:18; MARKOS 9:32"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Are you still devoid of binah (understanding)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKAS 6:45; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:13; MATTITYAHU 7:19–20"> Do you not have daas that everything which enters into the mouth goes into the stomach and passes into a latrine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MISHLE 15:28; MATTITYAHU 12:34; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:6–10"> But the things coming out from the mouth come out from the lev (heart), which makes tameh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARKOS 7:21–23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:19–21; YIRMEYAH 17:9"> For out of the lev comes evil machshavot (thoughts): retzichot (murders), niufim (adulteries), zenunim (fornications), genevot (thefts), eduyot sheker (false testimonies), giddufim (revilements).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MARKOS 7:3–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:3–6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:18–20"> These are the things making the man tameh, but eating with hands lacking the netilat yadayim (ritual of the washing of the hands),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Mt 27:24">e</a></sup>this does not make the man tameh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MARKOS 7:24–30; MATTITYAHU 11:21–23; SHOFETIM 1:31"> And having gone from that place, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach withdrew into the districts of Tzor and Tzidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:27; MARKOS 7:25–26; MATTITYAHU 17:15"> And a woman from Kena'an came out from those regions, and was shouting, Have mercy on me, Adoni, Ben Dovid! My bat is in torment possessed by shedim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 14:15; MARKOS 10:47–48; EKHAH 3:8"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach did not answer her a word. And Moshiachʼs talmidim having approached, were asking him, saying, Send her away; she shouts after us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:5–6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:8; YIRMEYAH 50:6–7"> But, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said in reply, I was not sent except to the Seh Oveid Beis Yisroel (the Lost Sheep of the House of Israel).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BERESHIS 32:26; MATTITYAHU 20:31; MATTITYAHU 14:33"> But she came and fell down before him, saying, Adoni, azreini (L-rd, help me).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MARKOS 7:27–28; MATTITYAHU 7:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:2"> But, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said in reply, It is not good to take the lechem of the Banim and throw it to the kelevim (dogs).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:8; LUKAS 16:21; IYOV 40:4–5"> But she said, Ken, Adoni, but even the kelevim eat the crumbs falling from the tish (table) of their masters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MARKOS 9:23–24; MATTITYAHU 8:13; TEHILLIM 145:19"> Then, in reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to her, O woman, great &#91;is&#93; your emunah. Let it be done for you as you wish. And the bat (daughter) of her was given refuah (healing) at that very hour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:18; YOCHANAN 6:1; MATTITYAHU 5:1"> And having passed over from that place, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach came beside Lake Kinneret, and having gone up the mountain, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was sitting there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LUKAS 6:17–19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:15–16; MATTITYAHU 14:35–36"> And great multitudes approached Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, having with them pisechim (lame), ivrim (blind), the crippled, the mute, and many others, and they laid them at Moshiachʼs feet, and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach gave the cholim (sick persons) refuah (healing).</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:8; LUKAS 18:43; LUKAS 17:15–18"> The result was that the multitude was astounded, witnessing mute people speaking, cripples made whole, the pisechim (lame) walking, and the ivrim (blind people) seeing, and they gave kavod to Elohei Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:33; LUKAS 12:29–30; MATTITYAHU 9:36"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, having summoned his talmidim, said, I have rachmei shomayim for the multitudes, for already shloshah yamim (three days) they remain with me and they do not have anything they may eat, and I do not want to send them away famished, lest they might faint on the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MARKOS 6:37; MATTITYAHU 14:15; MARKOS 8:4–5"> And Moshiachʼs talmidim say to him, From where bamidbar (in the wilderness) is there enough lechem for us to feed such a vast multitude?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:9–10; LUKAS 24:41–42; YOCHANAN 21:9–10"> And he says to them, How much lechem, how many loaves do you have? And they said, Sheva, and a few dagim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LUKAS 9:14–16; MARKOS 6:39–40; YOCHANAN 6:10"> And when Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach gave the command for the multitude to recline on the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LUKAS 22:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:6; YOCHANAN 6:11"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach took the loaves numbering sheva and the dagim, and, making a bracha, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach offered the betziat halechem (the breaking of the bread) and served them to the talmidim, and Moshiachʼs talmidim served them to the multitudes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MARKOS 8:19–21; MATTITYAHU 16:9–10; MATTITYAHU 15:33"> And everyone ate and they were satisfied, and the Rebbeʼs farbrengen shirayim were numbering sheva baskets full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And the ones eating were arbaat elafim (four thousand) men, not counting nashim and yeladim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MARKOS 8:10; MATTITYAHU 14:22"> And having sent away the multitudes, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach embarked in the sirah (boat), and came to the region of Magadan.</span>
</div>
