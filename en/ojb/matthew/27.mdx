<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARKOS 15:1; YOCHANAN 18:28; MICHOH 2:1"> Now when boker had come, all the Rashei Hakohenim and the Ziknei HaAm took counsel together against Yehoshua to put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:13; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:13"> And having performed the akedah (binding), they led him away, and delivered him up to Pilate the Governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:10; LUKAS 22:47–48; IYOV 20:15–29"> Then when Yehudah, who had betrayed Moshiach, saw that Yehoshua had been condemned, he felt remorse and returned the sheloshim shiklei kesef (thirty pieces of silver) to the Rashei Hakohanim and the Zekenim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:30; LETTER TO TITOS 1:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:15–17"> saying Chatati (I have sinned). I have betrayed dahm naki (innocent blood). But they said, What is that to us? See to that yourself!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:18–19; SHMUEL BAIS 17:23; MELACHIM ALEF 16:18"> And Yehudah threw the shiklei kesef (pieces of silver) into the Beis Hamikdash and departed; and, having gone away, Yehudah hanged himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKAS 6:7–9; DEVARIM 23:18; YESHAYAH 61:8"> And the Rashei Hakohanim took the shiklei kesef and said, It is asur (forbidden) to put these into the Beis Hamikdash otzar (treasury), vi-bahlt (since) it is blood money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And they took counsel together and they bought the Potterʼs Field as a beis hakevoros (cemetery) for zarim (foreigners).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:19; MATTITYAHU 28:15; YEHOSHUA 4:9"> For this reason that field has been called the Sadeh HaDahm (Field of Blood) to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 11:12–13; MATTITYAHU 1:22; VAYIKRA 27:2–7"> Then that which was spoken through Yirmeyah HaNavi was fulfilled, saying, And they took the SHELOSHIM KASEF (thirty pieces of silver), HAYEKAR (the price) of the one whose price had been set by the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 11:13"> And they gave them for the potters field as Hashem directed me (Zech 11:12-13).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 23:2–3; MARKOS 15:2–5; MATTITYAHU 26:25"> Now Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach stood before the Governor, and the Governor questioned Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach saying, Are you the Melech HaYehudim? And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to him, It is as you say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:63; YESHAYAH 53:7; MATTITYAHU 27:14"> And while Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was being accused by the Rashei Hakohanim and the Zekenim, he answered nothing.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Isa 53:7">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:62; YOCHANAN 18:35; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:24"> Then Pilate said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Do you not hear how many things they give edut against you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:18; TEHILLIM 71:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:9"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach did not answer him with even one word, so that the Governor was quite amazed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Isa 53:7">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:38–40; LUKAS 23:16; YOCHANAN 19:16"> Now at the Chag (Festival, Pesach) the Governor was accustomed to release for the multitude any one prisoner they wanted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MARKOS 15:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:14; LUKAS 23:25"> And they were holding at the time a notorious prisoner, called<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Yeshua">c</a></sup>Bar-Abba.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="son of the father">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:15; MELACHIM ALEF 18:21; MARKOS 15:9–12"> When, therefore, they were gathered together, Pilate said to them, Whom do you want me to release for you, Bar-Abba or Yehoshua called Moshiach?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MISHLE 27:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:17; MARKOS 15:10"> For Pilate knew that because of kinah (envy) they had delivered Yehoshua up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 9:9; MISHLE 29:1; MATTITYAHU 27:24"> And while Pilate was sitting on the judgment seat, his wife sent to him, saying, Have nothing to do with that Tzaddik; for last night I suffered greatly in a chalom (dream) because of Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:14–15; YOCHANAN 19:15–16; YOCHANAN 18:40"> However, the Rashei Hakohanim and the Zekenim persuaded the multitudes to ask for Bar-Abba, but Yehoshua they should destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But the Governor answered, saying to them, Which of the two do you want me to release for you? And they said, Bar-Abba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:2–3; YESHAYAH 49:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:38"> In reply, Pilate says to them, What, therefore, may I do with Yehoshua, the one called the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach? Everyone says, Let him be made TALUI AL HAETZ (being hanged on the Tree!) Be hanged on HAETZ!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dt 21:23">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:18–19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:57; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:12–15"> But Pilate said, Why? What rah (evil) has he done? But they kept shouting all the more, saying, Let him be hanged on HAETZ!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:4; TEHILLIM 26:6; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:21"> And when Pilate saw that he was accomplishing nothing, but rather that a riot was starting, he took water and washed his hands in front of the multitude, saying, I am innocent of this manʼs blood; see to that yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 2:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:52"> And, in reply, all the people said, His dahm be on us and on our yeladim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:5; YOCHANAN 19:1; MARKOS 15:15"> Then Pilate released Bar-Abba to them. But after having Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach scourged, he handed him over to be hanged on HAETZ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Dt 21:23">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:28; YOCHANAN 18:33; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:1"> Then the soldiers of the Governor took him into the praetorium and gathered the whole cohort against Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ps 2:1">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MARKOS 15:17; YOCHANAN 19:2–5; LUKAS 23:11"> And they stripped him and put a royal scarlet robe on him, and,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:3; MATTITYAHU 20:19; TEHILLIM 69:19–20"> After weaving a keter of thorns, they placed it on his head and put a reed in his right hand, and they fell down before him and mocked him saying, Hail, Melech HaYehudim!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YESHAYAH 50:6; MATTITYAHU 26:67; YESHAYAH 53:7"> And they spat on Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and took the reed and began to beat him on the head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:7; MELACHIM ALEF 21:13; MELACHIM ALEF 21:10"> And after they had made leitzonus (mockery) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, they took off his robe and put his garments on him, and led him away to hang him on HAETZ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MARKOS 15:21; MATTITYAHU 16:24; LUKAS 23:26"> And as they were coming out, they found a man from Cyrene named Shim'on, whom they pressed into service to bear Moshiachʼs Etz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:17–19; MARKOS 15:22–32; MARKOS 15:34"> And when they had come to a place called Gulgolta, which means place of a skull,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="TEHILLIM 69:21; MARKOS 15:23; MATTITYAHU 27:48"> They gave him wine to drink mingled with gall. And, after tasting it, he was unwilling to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="TEHILLIM 22:18; MARKOS 15:24–32; LUKAS 23:34"> When they had hanged Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach on HAETZ, they divided up Moshiachʼs garments among themselves, casting lots;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:54; MARKOS 15:44; MARKOS 15:39"> And, sitting down, they began to keep watch over him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKAS 23:38; YOCHANAN 19:19–22; MARKOS 15:26"> They put up above his head the charge against him which read, this is yehoshua, melech hayehudim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:18; LUKAS 23:39–43; YESHAYAH 53:12"> At that time, two shodedim were each hanged on his own etz with Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, one on the right and one on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="TEHILLIM 109:25; EKHAH 1:12; TEHILLIM 69:7–12"> And those passing by were hurling insults at Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, wagging their heads,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:61; MATTITYAHU 4:6; MATTITYAHU 4:3"> And saying, You who are going to cause the churban (destruction) of the Beis Hamikdash and rebuild it in shloshah yamim (three days), save yourself! If you are the Ben HaElohim, come down from HAETZ (the Tree).</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="TEHILLIM 35:26; YESHAYAH 28:22; TEHILLIM 22:12–13"> Likewise, also the Rashei Hakohanim along with the Sofrim and Zekenim, were mocking him, and saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:49; MATTITYAHU 27:37; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:14"> He saved others; yet himself he is not able to save. He is Melech Yisroel? Let him now come down from the Etz, and we shall have emunah in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="TEHILLIM 22:8; YOCHANAN 10:36; YESHAYAH 37:10"> He trusts in Hashem; let Hashem be his Moshi'a (Deliverer) and deliver him now, if Hashem takes pleasure in him, for this one said, Ben HaElohim Ani!</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:38; MARKOS 15:32; LUKAS 23:39–43"> And the shodedim (robbers), hanging, each on his etz, with him, were casting similar insults at Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, reproaching him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="AMOS 8:9; MARKOS 15:33–34; LUKAS 23:44–45"> Now, from the sixth hour, choshech fell upon all the land until the ninth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="TEHILLIM 22:1; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:7; YESHAYAH 53:10"> And about the ninth hour, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach cried out with a kol gadol, saying Eli, Eli, lemah sabachthani! (that is, My G-d, my G-d, why hast thou forsaken me? Ps 22:2 &#91;1&#93;).</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MALACHI 4:5; MARKOS 15:35–36; MATTITYAHU 11:14"> And some of those who were standing there, when they heard, began saying, This one calls for Eliyahu HaNavi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="TEHILLIM 69:21; MATTITYAHU 27:34; LUKAS 23:36"> And immediately one of them ran and, taking a sponge, filled it with sour wine and put it on a reed and gave him a drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> But the rest of them said, Wait. We will see whether Eliyahu HaNavi will come to save him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:30; YESHAYAH 53:9–12; TEHILLIM 22:14–15"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach cried out again with a loud voice, and dismissed his ruach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:19–22; MARKOS 15:38; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:19"> And hinei! The parochet of the Beis Hamikdash was torn in two from top to bottom, and the earth shook; and the rocks were split,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="YESHAYAH 26:19; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:20"> And the kevarim (graves) were opened, and many gufot (bodies) of the kedoshim who had fallen asleep were made to stand up alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="HISGALUS 11:2; YESHAYAH 48:2; HISGALUS 21:2"> And coming out of the kevarim after the Techiyas HaMoshiach, they entered the Ir Hakodesh (Holy City) and appeared to many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:36; MARKOS 15:39; MATTITYAHU 27:43"> Now when the centurion, and those who were with him keeping shomer (guard) over Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, saw the earthquake and the things that were happening, they became very frightened and said, Truly this was the Ben HaElohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="LUKAS 8:2–3; LUKAS 23:48–49; YOCHANAN 19:25–27"> And many nashim were there, looking on from a distance, who had followed Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach from the Galil and had ministered to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:25; MARKOS 15:47–1; MATTITYAHU 28:1"> Among them was Miryam of Magdala, Miryam the Em of Ya'akov and Yosef, and the Em of Zavdaiʼs sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:38–42; MARKOS 15:42–47; LUKAS 23:50–56"> And when it was erev, there came an oisher (rich man) from Ramatayim named Yosef who himself had also become a talmid of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="LUKAS 23:52–53; MARKOS 15:44–46"> This man went to Pilate and asked for the gufat Yehoshua.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ps 16:9-10">h</a></sup>Then Pilate ordered it to be given over to Yosef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> And Yosef took the gufat Yehoshua, and wrapped it in the tachrichim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:9; MATTITYAHU 28:2; MATTITYAHU 27:66"> and laid it in Yosefʼs own new kever, which he had hewn out in the rock; and he rolled a large stone against the entrance of the kever (tomb) and went away.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Isa 53:9">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:1; MATTITYAHU 27:56"> And Miryam of Magdala, and the other Miryam, stayed there, sitting opposite the kever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="MARKOS 15:42; YOCHANAN 19:14; YOCHANAN 19:42"> Now on the next day,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="i.e., Motzoei Shabbos">j</a></sup>which is the one after the Preparation, the Rashei Hakohanim and the Perushim gathered together with Pilate</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="LUKAS 9:22; MATTITYAHU 16:21; YOCHANAN 2:19"> and said, Sir, we remember that when he was still alive that mateh (deceiver) said, After shloshah yamim I am to stand up alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:13; MATTITYAHU 12:45"> Therefore, give orders for the kever to be made secure until the Yom HaShlishi (the Third Day), lest his talmidim come and steal him away and say to the people, He has stood up alive from the mesim. And the last deception will be worse than the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:66; MATTITYAHU 28:11–15; MISHLE 21:30"> Pilate said to them, You have a guard, go make the kever as secure as you know how.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="DANIEL 6:17; MATTITYAHU 27:60; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:19"> And they went and made the kever secure, and, along with the guard, they set a seal on the stone.</span>
