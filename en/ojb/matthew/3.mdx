<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:3–6; YOCHANAN 1:6–8; LUKAS 1:76"> Now in those days Yochanan of the tevilah of teshuva appears, darshenen (preaching) as a mevaser (herald)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="for the Moshiach">a</a></sup>in the midbar (wilderness) of Yehudah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:17; MARKOS 1:15; DANIEL 2:44"> saying, Make teshuva, for the Malchut HaShomayim has come near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:3; MALACHI 3:1; YOCHANAN 1:23"> For this<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Yochanan">b</a></sup>is the one spoken of through Yeshayah the Navi, saying, KOL KOREY BAMIDBAR<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 40:3">c</a></sup>(A voice of one shouting in the wilderness): Prepare the Derech Hashem (the way of the L-rd). Make his paths straight!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 1:8; ZECHARYAH 13:4; MARKOS 1:6"> Now Yochanan himself had camel-hair clothing and a leather belt around his waist, and his food was arbe (locusts) and devash (wild honey).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MARKOS 1:5; LUKAS 3:7; YOCHANAN 3:23"> Then all Yerushalayim and all Yehudah and the whole region of the Yarden were going out to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:18; MATTITYAHU 3:13–16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:38–41"> Making vidduy (confession of sin)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="to Hashem">d</a></sup>of their averos (sins), they were submitted to a tevilah (immersion) by him, using the Yarden River as a mikveh mayim (gathering of water).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:34; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:9"> But when he saw many of the Perushim and Tzedukim coming to where he was using the Yarden as a mikveh mayim, Yochanan said to them, You banim (sons) of nachashim (snakes)! Who warned you to flee from the charon af<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hashem">e</a></sup>habah (the coming burning wrath) &#91;of Hashem&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKAS 3:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:20; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:4–8"> Produce pri tov l'teshuva (fruit worthy of repentance)!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 3:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:7–8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:27–29"> Do not presume to say to yourselves, We have the yichus (proud lineage), we have the zechut Avot (merit of the Fathers) of Avraham Avinu (our father Abraham). For I say to you that Hashem is able to raise up from these avanim (stones) banim (sons) of Avraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:6; LUKAS 3:9; MATTITYAHU 7:19"> And already the ax is laid at the shoresh haetzim (the root of the trees); therefore, every etz not producing pri tov (good fruit) is cut down and thrown into the eish (fire).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 4:4; LUKAS 3:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:5"> I give you a tevilah (immersion) in a mikveh mayim for teshuva, but Hu Habah (He who Comes)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="i.e., the Moshiach">f</a></sup>after me has more chozek (strength) than me. I am not worthy to remove his sandals. He will give you a tevilah (immersion) with the Ruach Hakodesh and eish (fire).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MALACHI 4:1; MATTITYAHU 13:30; LUKAS 3:17"> The winnowing fork is in his hand, and he will clear out his threshing floor, and he will gather his wheat into the storehouse; but the chaff he will burn with unquenchable eish (fire).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:32–34; MATTITYAHU 2:22; MARKOS 1:9–11"> Then Yehoshua comes from the Galil to the Yarden to Yochanan, to submit to Yochananʼs tevilah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:3–7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:5–8; YOCHANAN 13:6–8"> But Yochanan would have deterred him, saying, I have need to submit to your tevilah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="T.N. i.e., Moshiachʼs tevilah">g</a></sup>and yet you come to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:34; YOCHANAN 15:10; TEHILLIM 40:7–8"> But answering him, Yehoshua said, Permit it now, for thus it is proper to fulfill all Tzidkat Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Dan 9:24">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YESHAYAH 11:2; MARKOS 1:10; LUKAS 3:21–22"> And having received the tevilah in the Yardenʼs mikveh mayim, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach immediately came up. And, hinei! The Shomayim were opened to him, and he saw the Ruach Hakodesh of Hashem descending like a yonah (dove) and coming upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YESHAYAH 42:1; TEHILLIM 2:7; MATTITYAHU 17:5"> And, hinei, a bat kol (a voice from heaven) came out of Shomayim, saying, ZEH BNI AHUVI ASHER BO CHAFATZTI (This is my Son, the beloved, with whom I am well pleased).</span>
</div>
