<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 11:16; MATTITYAHU 16:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:1"> And the Perushim and Tzedukim approached, to test Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, asking him to present them with an ot (sign) from Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKAS 12:54–56"> But in reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, When it is erev, you say, It will be fair weather, for the sky is fiery red.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKAS 12:56; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:32; MATTITYAHU 11:5"> And in the boker (morning) you say, There will be stormy weather today, for the sky is overcast fiery red and threatening. You have daas to distinguish the signs of the appearance of the sky, but you can't discern the signs of the times?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="T.N. See Ro 1:18">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKAS 11:29–30; MATTITYAHU 12:39–40; YONAH 1:17"> A dor rah umnaef (an evil and adulterous generation) demands an ot (sign), and no ot will be given it except the ot of Yonah HaNavi. And having left them, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MARKOS 8:13–14; MATTITYAHU 15:39"> When the Moshiachʼs talmidim arrived at the other side, they had forgotten to take lechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKAS 12:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:9; MATTITYAHU 16:12"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Take special precaution against the chametz (swelling leaven) of the Perushim and Tzedukim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MARKOS 8:16–18; MATTITYAHU 15:16–18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:14"> But they began reasoning among themselves, saying, We took no lechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:30; MATTITYAHU 14:31; MATTITYAHU 8:26"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, aware of their machshavot (thoughts), said, You men of little emunah, why do you reason among yourselves that you have no lechem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 14:17–21; YOCHANAN 6:9–13; LUKAS 9:13–17"> Do you not yet have binah or remember the five loaves of the chamesh elafim (five thousand), and how many baskets full you took up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:34–38; MARKOS 8:17–21; MARKOS 8:5–9"> Or the shevah loaves of the arbaat elafim (four thousand), and how many large baskets full you took up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:6; LUKAS 12:56; MARKOS 8:21"> How is it that you do not chap (grasp mentally) that I did not speak to you concerning lechem? But beware of the chametz (swelling, all-permeating leaven)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="like evil, like gaavah, pride">b</a></sup>of the Perushim and Tzedukim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:8; MATTITYAHU 15:4–9"> Then Moshiachʼs talmidim understood that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach did not say to beware of the chametz of the lechem, but of the chametz of the teaching of the Perushim and the Tzedukim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 9:18–20; MARKOS 8:27–29; DANIEL 7:13"> Now, having arrived in the district of Caesarea Philippi, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach began asking his talmidim, saying, Who do men say that &#91;I&#93; the Bar Enosh am?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MARKOS 6:15; MATTITYAHU 14:2; MALACHI 4:5"> And Moshiachʼs talmidim said, Some say Yochanan of the tevilah of teshuva, and others say, Eliyahu HaNavi, but still others say, Yirmeyah or one of the Neviim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 9:20; MATTITYAHU 13:11; MARKOS 8:29"> He says to them, But you, who do you consider me to be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 14:33; YOCHANAN 11:27; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:15"> And, Shim'on Kefa said in reply, You are the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, the Ben Elohim Chayyim!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:45; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:17–18; MATTITYAHU 11:25–27"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said in reply to him, Ashrey atah (happy are you), Shim'on Bar-Yonah, because basar vadahm (flesh and blood) did not give you this hisgalus (revelation), but Avi sh'ba'Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:19–22; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:9–11; YESHAYAH 28:16"> And I also say to you that you are Shim'on Kefa<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Petros">c</a></sup>and upon this TSUR I will build my Kehillah, my Chavurah (the Community of Moshiach) and the shaarei Sheol (gates of Sheol) shall not overpower it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:18; YESHAYAH 22:22; HISGALUS 1:18"> I will give you the maftechot Malchut HaShomayim (keys of the Kingdom of Heaven); and whatever you shall bind as asur (prohibited) on haaretz shall be bound as asur (prohibited) in Shomayim, and whatever you shall loose as mutar (permitted) on haaretz shall be loosed as mutar (permitted) in Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MARKOS 8:30; LUKAS 9:21; YOCHANAN 1:41"> Then Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach gave the directive to his talmidim that they should tell no one that he was the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKAS 24:6–7; MATTITYAHU 20:17–19; MATTITYAHU 17:22–23"> From that point he began to explain to his talmidim that it was necessary that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach go to Yerushalayim, and suffer many things from the Zekenim (Elders), and the Rashei Hakohanim (the Chief Priests) and the Sofrim (Scribes) and it was necessary for Moshiach to be killed and have his histalkus (passing), that he would undergo the Techiyas HaMoshiach (Resurrection of Moshiach) on Yom HaShlishi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MARKOS 8:32; YOCHANAN 13:6–8; MATTITYAHU 16:16–17"> And Kefa took him aside and began to rebuke him, saying, Chas vshalom (G-d forbid)! Adoni, this shall never happen to you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:5–8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:19"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach turned and said to Kefa, Get behind me, HaSatan! You are a michshol (stumbling block) to me; for you are not setting your mind on the things of Hashem, but the things of Bnei Adam!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:38; LUKAS 14:27; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1–2"> Then Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to his talmidim, If anyone wishes to come after me, let him turn in hinnazrut (self-denial), and take up his etz shel mesiros nefesh (tree of self-sacrifice), and follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:39; MARKOS 8:35; HISGALUS 12:11"> For whoever wishes to save his nefesh shall lose it; but whoever loses his nefesh on account of me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Moshiach">d</a></sup>shall find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MARKOS 8:36–37; IYOV 27:8; LUKAS 9:25"> For what will a man be benefited if he acquires the whole world and forfeits his neshamah, or what will a man give in exchange for his neshamah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:10; TEHILLIM 62:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:6"> For the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Moshiach">e</a></sup>is about to come in the kavod of his Av with his malachim and will then recompense every man according to his ma'asim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKAS 9:27; MARKOS 9:1; YOCHANAN 8:52"> Omein, I say to you, there are some of those who are standing here who shall not taste death until they see the Bias of the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Moshiach">f</a></sup>coming in his Malchut.</span>
