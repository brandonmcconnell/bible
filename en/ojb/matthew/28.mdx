<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARKOS 16:1–8; YOCHANAN 20:1–10; MATTITYAHU 27:56"> Now after Shabbos, at the time of Shacharis on Yom Rishon (the first day of the week), Miryam of Magdala and the other Miryam came to look at the kever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:26; YOCHANAN 20:12–13; MARKOS 16:3–5"> And hinei! a great earthquake had occurred, for a malach Adonoi (an angel of Hashem) descended from Shomayim and came and rolled away the stone and sat upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MARKOS 9:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:10; MARKOS 16:5"> And his appearance was like lightning and his garment as white as snow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:17; DANIEL 10:7; MATTITYAHU 27:65–66"> And the shomrim (guards) shook for fear of him and became like dead men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:17–18; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:14; YESHAYAH 41:10"> And the malach, in reply, said to the nashim, Do not be afraid, for I know that you are seeking Yehoshua, who has been hanged on HaEtz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MARKOS 16:6; MATTITYAHU 12:40; LUKAS 24:6–8"> He is not here. To him has come the Techiyas HaMesim, just as he said. Come, see the place where HaAdon was lying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:32; LUKAS 24:22–24; MATTITYAHU 28:10"> And go quickly, tell his talmidim about the Techiyas HaMoshiach from HaMesim. And, hinei! He is going ahead of you into the Galil. There you will see him. Hinei! I have told you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 2:11; MARKOS 16:8; LUKAS 24:36–41"> And they departed quickly from the kever with fear and great simcha and ran to report everything to Moshiachʼs talmidim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:17; YOCHANAN 20:14–17; MATTITYAHU 14:33"> And hinei! Moshiach met them, saying Shalom Aleichem. And they came up and took hold of his feet and fell prostrate before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:29; YOCHANAN 20:17; MATTITYAHU 28:7"> Then he said to them, Do not be afraid; go and take word to my Achim that they may go away to the Galil, and there they shall see me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:65–66; MATTITYAHU 28:4"> Now while they were on their way, hinei! Some of the shomrim came into the city and reported to the Rashei Hakohanim all the things that had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:3–4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:5–22; MATTITYAHU 27:1–2"> And when they had assembled with the Zekenim and counseled together, they gave a large sum of money to the soldiers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:64"> And said, You are to say, His talmidim came by night and stole him away while we were asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:19"> And if this should come to the Governorʼs ears, we will win him over and keep you out of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:10; MATTITYAHU 27:8; MATTITYAHU 26:15"> And the ones who took the kesef did as they had been instructed and this story was widely spread among the Yehudim to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:32; MATTITYAHU 28:10; MATTITYAHU 28:7"> But the Achad Asar Talmidim proceeded to the Galil, to the mountain which Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach had designated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TEHILLIM 45:11; TEHILLIM 2:12; YOCHANAN 5:23"> And when they saw him, they prostrated themselves before him, but some were doubtful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:35; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:22; MATTITYAHU 11:27"> And he came up and spoke to them, saying, All samchut (authority) has been given to me in Shomayim and on HaAretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARKOS 16:15–16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:38–39; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:8"> Go, therefore, make talmidim for Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach of all the nations, giving them a tevilah in a mikveh mayim in Hashem, in the Name of HaAv, and HaBen, and HaRuach Hakodesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YESHAYAH 41:10; YEHOSHUA 1:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:9–10"> Teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you. And hinei! I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Moshiach">a</a></sup>am with you always, even unto the Ketz HaOlam Hazeh.</span>
