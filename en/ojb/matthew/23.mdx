<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 12:1; LUKAS 20:45–46; MATTITYAHU 15:10–20"> Then Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach spoke to the multitudes and to his Talmidim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKAS 20:46; MARKOS 12:38; MALACHI 2:7"> Saying, Upon the Kisei Moshe (Chair of Moses) sit the Sofrim and the Perushim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:5; LETTER TO TITOS 1:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:19–24"> Therefore, everything whatever they may tell you, be frum and be shomer, but according to their ma'asim (works) do not be shomer, for they do not practice what they preach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKAS 11:46; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:13"> And they tie up heavy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="extra-Biblical, Mt 4:4; 15:3,6-9; 22:29">a</a></sup>loads, hard to bear, and they place them upon the shoulders of men, but they, with so much as a finger, are not willing to lift the oppressive burdens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:1–16; YOCHANAN 12:43; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:3"> And all their ma'asei hamitzvot (works of the commandments) they do in order to be seen by Bnei Adam, for they broaden their tefillin and lengthen their tzitziyot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9; LUKAS 11:43–54; MARKOS 12:38–39"> and they love the places of honor at the seudot (banquet dinners), and the shuls,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:38; YOCHANAN 1:49; YOCHANAN 3:26"> And the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="obsequious">b</a></sup>Birkat Shalom greetings in the market places, and to be called by Bnei Adam, Rebbe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:12–13; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:3"> But you are not to have pretentious titles like Rebbe, for One is your Rebbe,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach">c</a></sup>and all of you are Achim b'Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:1; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:9; MALACHI 1:6"> And do not refer to anyone in the Olam Hazeh as your Abba, for One is your Av sh'ba'Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Neither be called Moreinu, for One is your Moreh (teacher) the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Moreinu">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:13; YOCHANAN 13:14–15"> And the greatest among you will be your eved mesharet (servant, minister).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:5; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:6; MISHLE 29:23"> But whoever will lift up himself will be humbled, and whoever will humble himself will be lifted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 11:52; MATTITYAHU 23:29; MATTITYAHU 23:23"> But oy to you, Sofrim and Perushim, tzevuim (hypocrites), for in front of Bnei Adam you shut the Malchut HaShomayim. You do not go in yourselves, and the ones entering, you do not permit to enter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Oy to you, Sofrim and Perushim, tzevuim! You devour the batim of almanot and for the sake of chashuve (importance) appearance you make long tefillot; therefore your gezar din (verdict) will be more severe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:43"> Oy to you, Sofrim and Perushim, tzevuim. You travel over the sea and the dry land for the giyyur (proselytization) of one proselyte; then, when he becomes one, you make him twice as much a son of Gehinnom as you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:14; MATTITYAHU 5:33–35; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:12"> Oy to you, morei derech ivrim (blind guides), the, ones saying, Whoever swears by the Beis Hamikdash, it is a worthless shevuah (oath): but whoever swears by the gold of the Beis Hamikdash, he is obligated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:19; BAMIDBAR 16:38–39; TEHILLIM 94:8"> Ivrim! (blind ones), for which is greater, the gold or the Beis Hamikdash which gives the gold its kedushah (holiness, sanctity)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And whoever swears by the Mizbeach (altar), it is worthless; but whoever swears by the korban upon it, he is obligated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:37; SHEMOT 30:29"> Ivrim, blind ones, for which is greater, the korban or Mizbeach which gives the korban its kedushah (holiness, sanctity)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Therefore, the one having sworn by the Mizbeach swears by it and everything upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="TEHILLIM 26:8; MELACHIM ALEF 8:13; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 6:2"> And the one having sworn by the Beis Hamikdash swears by it and by everything indwelling it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:34; TEHILLIM 11:4; YESHAYAH 66:1"> And the one having sworn by Shomayim swears by the kes malchut of Hashem and by the One sitting upon the kes malchut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKAS 11:42; MICHOH 6:8; HOSHEA 6:6"> Oy to you, Sofrim and Perushim, tzevuim, for you give maasros (tithe) of mint and dill and cumin, and you have neglected the matters of the Torah of greater consequence: mishpat, chesed, and emunah. These things it was necessary to do and those<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="others">e</a></sup>not to neglect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:24; MATTITYAHU 27:6–8; MATTITYAHU 15:2–6"> Morei derech ivrim (blind guides), the ones that strain out a gnat and swallow a camel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKAS 11:39–40; MATTITYAHU 15:19–20; MARKOS 7:4–13"> Oy to you, Sofrim and Perushim, tzevuim, you cleanse the outside of the kos (cup) and the dish, but inside they are full of chamdanut (greed) and taavanut (lust).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:1; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:22"> Blind Parush, first cleanse the inside of the kos, that perhaps also the outside may become clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKAS 11:44; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:3; BAMIDBAR 19:16"> Oy to you, Sofrim and Perushim, tzevuim, for you are like kevarim (graves) having been whitewashed, which on the outside indeed appear ois vaist (ostensibly) shein (beautiful), but on the inside are full of the unclean bones of the mesim (dead ones) and every trayfnyak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKAS 16:15; TEHILLIM 51:6; MATTITYAHU 23:5"> Thus on the outside you indeed appear tzaddikim (righteous ones) to Bnei Adam, but on the inside you are full of tzeviut (hypocrisy) and you are lawlessly against the Torah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LUKAS 11:47–48; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:29"> Oy to you, Sofrim and Perushim, tzevuim, for you build kevarim of the Neviim and decorate matsevot (gravestones) of the tzaddikim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 2:30; MATTITYAHU 23:34–35; MATTITYAHU 21:35–36"> And you say, If we were in the yamim (days) of Avoteinu (our Fathers), we would not have been shuttafim (partners) with them in the dahm haNeviim (blood of the Prophets).</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:51–52; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:15–16; YEHOSHUA 24:22"> Therefore, you are edim (witnesses) against yourselves that you are the banim (sons) of the ratzchaniyot (murderers) of the Neviim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:16; BAMIDBAR 32:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:16"> And you fill up the measure of your Avot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 3:7; MATTITYAHU 12:34; MATTITYAHU 5:22"> Snakes, you banim of nachashim, how can you escape the Yom HaDin of Gehinnom?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:16–17; MATTITYAHU 13:52; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:28"> For this reason hinei! I send to you Neviim and Chachamim and Sofrim, some of whom you will kill and some you will make talui al haetz<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Dt 21:23">f</a></sup>(being hanged on the Tree) and some you will subject to the shot (whip) in your shuls, and you will drive them out from city to city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 24:20–22; ZECHARYAH 1:1; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:4"> So that upon you may come all the dahm naki (innocent blood) shed upon the earth from the blood of Hevel to the blood of Zecharyah son of Barachiah, whom you murdered between the Heikhal and the Mizbeach.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="trans. note: cf. Lk 11:51for Mtʼs probable original text here">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:23; MATTITYAHU 24:34; MARKOS 13:30–31"> Omein, I say to you, that all these things will come upon this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKAS 13:34–35; RUTH 2:12; TEHILLIM 91:4"> Yerushalayim, Yerushalayim, the ones that kill the Neviim and stone those having been sent to you! How often have I wanted to gather your yeladim, as a hen gathers her chickens under her wings, but you were not willing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 22:5; LUKAS 13:35; LUKAS 21:24"> Hinei! Look! KI LECHARBAH YIH'YEH HABEIT HAZEH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Jer 22:5">h</a></sup>(for this House will become a ruin).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="(i.e. churban)">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="TEHILLIM 118:26; MATTITYAHU 21:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:25"> For I say to you, by no means will you see me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Moshiach">j</a></sup>from now until you say, BARUCH HABAH B'SHEM ADONOI.</span>
