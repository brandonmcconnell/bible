<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:1–16; MATTITYAHU 12:43–45; LUKAS 14:16"> And, in response, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach again spoke in meshalim (parables) to them, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:7–9; MATTITYAHU 13:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:24–32"> The Malchut HaShomayim is like a man, a Melech (King), who prepared a feast for the chasunoh (wedding) of Bno.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HISGALUS 22:17; YOCHANAN 5:40; LUKAS 14:15–17"> And the Melech sent out his servants to summon the Bnei HaChuppah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Mt 9:15">a</a></sup>(wedding invitees) to the chasunoh, and they did not want to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:46; MATTITYAHU 21:36; YOCHANAN 6:50–57"> Again, the Melech sent out other servants, saying, Tell the Bnei HaChuppah, the wedding invitees, Hinei! My Seudah I have prepared, my oxen and fattened calves have been slaughtered, and everything is ready: Come to the chasunoh!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:3; LUKAS 14:18–20; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–16"> But the Bnei HaChuppah, the chasunoh invitees, having treated it all as a mere trifle, departed, one to his farmerʼs field, one to his business.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:19–20; YOCHANAN 16:2–3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:51–57"> Meanwhile, the others seized the Mesharetim HaMelech (Ministers of the King) and abused them and killed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKAS 19:27; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:17–18; LUKAS 21:24"> So the Melech was angry and, having sent his armies, the King destroyed those ratzchaniyot (murderers), and their city he burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:46; HISGALUS 3:4"> Then the King says to his ministers, The chasunoh is ready, but the invited ones were not worthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 14:21–24; MISHLE 1:20–23; MARKOS 16:15–16"> Therefore, go to the intersections of the streets, and, whoever you find, invite as muzmanim (guests) to the chasunoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:9–11; HISGALUS 19:6–9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:21"> And having gone out to the highways, those ministers congregated everyone they found, rah and tov, and the chasunoh was filled with muzmanim (guests).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:4–5; HISGALUS 19:8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:12–13"> And the Melech, having entered to see the ones sitting bimesibba (reclining at tish), spotted there a man lacking the attire proper for the chasunoh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Yn 3:3,5">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 2:9; IYOV 5:16; MATTITYAHU 20:13"> And the Melech says to him, Chaver, how did you get in here, not having the attire proper for the chasunoh? But the man had nothing to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 8:12; MATTITYAHU 25:30; MATTITYAHU 24:51"> Then the King said to the servants, Bind him feet and hands and expel him into the outer choshech, where there will be weeping and grinding of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:10; LUKAS 13:23–24; MATTITYAHU 7:13–14"> For the invited ones are many, but the nivcharim (chosen ones) are few.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 20:20–26; MARKOS 12:13–17; TEHILLIM 2:2"> Then, having departed, the Perushim took counsel together so that they might entrap Yehoshua in his own words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MARKOS 3:6; MARKOS 8:15; MARKOS 12:14"> And they are sending to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach their talmidim with the Herodians, saying, Rabbi, we know that you are an ehrliche Yid (a good Jew) and of the Derech Hashem you give Divrei Torah in Emes, and you show no deference to flesh, for you are impartial toward Bnei Adam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKAS 2:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:6–7; MATTITYAHU 17:25"> Therefore, tell us what to you seems right: is it mutar (permissible) to pay poll tax to Caesar or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:6; LUKAS 5:22; YOCHANAN 2:25"> But he, having known their rah (evil), said to them, Why do you test me, tzevuim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:2; HISGALUS 6:6; MATTITYAHU 18:28"> Show me the coin of the poll tax. And they brought to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach a denarius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKAS 20:24"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, Whose image is this and whose title?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:7; MISHLE 24:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:19"> They say to him, Caesarʼs. Then Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, Give, therefore, unto Caesar the things of Caesar, and the things of Hashem, give unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MARKOS 12:12; MATTITYAHU 22:46; LUKAS 20:25–26"> And having heard this, they were amazed and, having left Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, they went away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:1; MARKOS 12:18–27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:6–8"> On that day some Tzedukim (Sadducees) who say there is no Techiyas HaMesim approached Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach. And they interrogated Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEVARIM 25:5–10; LUKAS 20:28; RUTH 1:11"> Saying, Rabbi, Moshe Rabbenu said that if someone dies, not having banim, his brother shall marry his isha (wife) to raise up zera (seed) for his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKAS 20:29–33; MARKOS 12:19–23; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:27"> Now there were among us shiva achim (seven brothers), and the first, having married, died. And not having zera (offspring), left his isha to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Likewise, also the second brother, and the third, up to the seventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And last of all, the isha died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> In the Techiyas HaMesim, therefore, she will be the wife of which of the seven? For all had her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:4; LUKAS 1:37"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said in reply to them, You are in error, not having known the Kitvei Hakodesh or the gevurat Hashem (power of G-d).</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LUKAS 20:34–36; MARKOS 12:24–25; MATTITYAHU 24:38"> For in the Techiyas HaMesim they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are like the malachim in Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:3; MATTITYAHU 21:16; MATTITYAHU 21:42"> But concerning the Techiyas HaMesim, have you not read the thing spoken to you by Hashem, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:6; MARKOS 12:26–27; LUKAS 20:37–38"> ANOCHI ELOHEI AVRAHAM ELOHEI YITZCHAK VEELOHEI Ya'akov (I am the G-d of Avraham, the G-d of Yitzchak, and the G-d of Ya'akov)?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ex 3:6">c</a></sup>Hashem is not the G-d of the Mesim (dead ones) but the G-d of the Chayyim (living).</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MARKOS 12:17; MARKOS 6:2; YOCHANAN 7:46"> And having heard this, the multitudes were amazed at Moshiachʼs torah (teaching).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MARKOS 12:28–31; MATTITYAHU 25:3–5; MATTITYAHU 12:14"> But the Perushim, having heard that he silenced the Tzedukim, assembled together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LUKAS 7:30; LUKAS 10:25–37; LUKAS 11:52"> And one of them, a Baal Torah (learned Torah scholar, a Ben Torah), tried to trip up Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach with a she'elah (question):</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MARKOS 12:28–33; MATTITYAHU 5:19–20; LUKAS 11:42"> Rabbi, which mitzvah is gedolah (great) in the Torah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MARKOS 12:29–30; LUKAS 10:27; DEVARIM 6:5"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach gave this teshuva (answer) to the Baal Torah, V'AHAVTAH ES ADONOI ELOHEICHA B'CHOL LEVAVCHA UVECHOL NAFSHECHA UVECHOL MODECHA (And thou shalt love the L-rd thy G-d with all thy heart and with all thy soul and with all thy might).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Dt 6:5">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> This is the gedolah and rishonah mitzvah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MARKOS 12:31; LUKAS 10:27–37; VAYIKRA 19:18"> And the second mitzvah is like it: V'AHAVTA L'REACHA KAMOCHA (And thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Lv 19:18">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:9; MATTITYAHU 7:12; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:19–21"> On these two mitzvot hang the entire Torah and the Neviim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LUKAS 20:41–44; MARKOS 12:35–37; MATTITYAHU 22:34"> And, the Perushim, having been assembled, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach confronted with this she'elah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 23:5–6; YOCHANAN 6:68–69; MATTITYAHU 16:13–17"> Saying, What do you think concerning the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach? Whose Ben is he? The Perushim gave this teshuva (answer) to him: Ben Dovid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 23:2; HISGALUS 4:2; MARKOS 12:36"> Moshiach says to them, How then can Dovid, in the Ruach Hakodesh, call Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adon? …saying</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="TEHILLIM 110:1; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:34–35"> NEUM HASHEM LADONI, SHEV LIMINI AD ASHIT OYVECHA HADOM LERAGLECHA<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ps 110:1">f</a></sup>(Utterance of Hashem to my L-rd, Sit at My right hand until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet).</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:3–4; HISGALUS 22:16; YOCHANAN 8:58"> Therefore, if Dovid calls him Adon,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="i.e., Adoneinu">g</a></sup>how is Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach &#91;merely&#93; Ben Dovid?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="c.f. Mal 3:1; Zech 4:14">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="MARKOS 12:34; LUKAS 20:40; LUKAS 14:6"> And no one was able to give an answer to him, nor did anyone dare to pose another she'elah (question) to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach from that day on.</span>
</div>
