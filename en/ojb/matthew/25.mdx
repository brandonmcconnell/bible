<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 12:35–36; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:15–16; MATTITYAHU 24:42–51"> Then the Malchut HaShomayim will be compared to ten alamot (young unmarried virgins), who, having taken their menorahs, went out to meet the Choson (Bridegroom).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 24:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:1–5; MATTITYAHU 13:19–23"> Now five of them were foolish and five were wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:15–16; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:5; YESHAYAH 48:1–2"> When the foolish took their menorahs, they neglected to take shemen (oil).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:9; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:27; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20"> But the wise took shemen in containers with their menorahs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="CHABAKUK 2:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:6–8; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:4–9"> Now the Choson, being delayed, here is what happened: all the alamot became drowsy and were sleeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:10; MATTITYAHU 24:31; MATTITYAHU 24:44"> And at chatsot halailah (midnight) there was a shout, Hinei! The Choson (Bridegroom)! Go out to meet him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKAS 12:35; HISGALUS 3:19–20; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:14"> Then all the ten alamot awakened and they trimmed their menorahs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MISHLE 13:9; LUKAS 12:35; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:1"> But the foolish said to the wise, Give us from your shemen (oil), for our menorahs are going out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 49:7–9; HISGALUS 3:17–18; YESHAYAH 55:6–7"> But the wise answered, saying, Perhaps there might not be enough for us and for you. Instead, you go to the ones selling and buy for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKAS 13:25; MATTITYAHU 24:44; LUKAS 12:36–37"> And as they were going away to buy, the Bias of the Choson (Bridegroom) occurred! The ones prepared entered with him into the Chasunoh (Wedding) feast and the door was shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:21–23; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:16–17"> And later, here comes also the other alamot, saying, Adoneinu, Adoneinu, open the door for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:3; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:19; YOCHANAN 10:27"> But he, in reply, said, Omein, I say to you, I do not know you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:42–44; HISGALUS 16:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:6"> Be shomer, be on the alert, therefore, for you do not have daas of the Yom or the Shaah (hour, time).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MARKOS 13:34; LUKAS 19:12–27; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:9–11"> For it is as a man going on a journey, who called to his own avadim (slaves) and handed over to them his possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:24; LUKAS 19:13–14; LUKAS 12:48"> And to this one, he gave five talents, and to this one, two, and to this one, one, each according to his own ability. Then the man went on his journey. Immediately</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5–8; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:17–18; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:6"> after the man had gone, the one having received the five talents, went to work with them, and gained five others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MISHLE 3:9–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:16; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10"> Likewise, the one having received the two talents, this one gained two others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8; CHAGGAI 1:2–4; MALACHI 1:10"> But the one, having received one, went out and dug a hole in the ground and buried the gelt (money) of his Adon in a hiding place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:12–15; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:7–12"> And after much time, here comes the Adon of those servants. And he conducts a settling of accounts with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:24; LUKAS 19:16–17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:29"> Then the one who had received five talents came forward, bringing five more talents, saying, Adoni, five talents you gave me. Hinei! Five more talents I gained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:23; LUKAS 16:10; YOCHANAN 12:26"> And his Adon said to him, Shkoyach (well done), eved tov vneeman (good and faithful servant)! A few things you were faithful over, over many things I will ordain you. Enter into the simchah of your Adon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:7–8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:1–3"> And having come forward, the one having received two talents, said, You bestowed upon me two talents. Hinei! Two more talents I gained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:21; MARKOS 14:8–9; MARKOS 12:41–44"> His Adon said to him, Shkoyach (well done), eved tov vneeman! A few things you were faithful over, over many things I will ordain you. Enter into the simcha of your Adon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKAS 19:20–22; LUKAS 15:29; MALACHI 3:14–15"> And also the one who had received one talent stepped forward. He said, Adoni, I knew that you are a hard man, reaping where you did not sow and gathering from that which you did not scatter seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:6–7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:15; HISGALUS 21:8"> And having been afraid and having gone away, I hid your talent by burying it in the ground. Here, see, you have that which belongs to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:32; IYOV 15:5–6"> And, in reply, his Adon said to him, You farbissener (mean) eved rah v'atzel (wicked and lazy slave), so you had daas that I reap where I did not sow and I gather from which I did not scatter seed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEVARIM 23:19–20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:19; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:15"> Then why was it not necessary for you to deposit my gelt with the bankers, and having returned, I would have received back that which was mine with interest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKAS 19:24; LUKAS 10:42"> Take, therefore, from him the talent and give it to the one having the ten talents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LUKAS 8:18; MARKOS 4:25; MATTITYAHU 13:12"> For to every one having, it will be given and he will have abundance; but from the one not having, even what he has will be taken from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 8:12; HISGALUS 3:15–16; MATTITYAHU 13:50"> And as for the useless slave, throw him into the outer choshech, where there will be weeping and grinding of teeth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:7; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:7–8; MATTITYAHU 19:28"> And when the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Dan 7:13">a</a></sup>comes, in his kavod and all his malachim with him, then he will sit upon his Kissei Kavod (Glorious Throne).</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MALACHI 3:18; MATTITYAHU 13:49; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:10"> And there will be assembled all the Goyim, and he will separate them from each other as the Roeh (the Shepherd) separates the Kevasim (Sheep) from the Izzim (Goats).</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="TEHILLIM 100:3; YOCHANAN 10:26–28; YOCHANAN 21:15–17"> And he will put the Kevasim on the right of him, but the Izzim on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19–20; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5; HISGALUS 21:7"> Then he will say to the ones on his right, Come! Baruchei Avi (Blessed of my Father), receive the bechorah (inheritance), the Malchut prepared for you from before Hivvased HaOlam (the establishing of the world).</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16–19; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–16; DEVARIM 15:7–11"> For I hungered and you gave me something to eat. I thirsted and you gave drink to me. I was a sojourner, and you extended hachnosas orchim (hospitality) to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:27; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:16–18; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:14–15"> I was naked and you gave me malbish arumim (clothing the naked). I was ill; with bikkur cholim (visiting the sick) you ministered to me. I was in the beit hasohar (prison); you came to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:14; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:5–6"> Then the tzaddikim will answer him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Moshiach">b</a></sup>Adoneinu, when did we see you hungering and we fed you, or thirsting and we gave you drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And when did we see you a sojourner and we extended hachnosas orchim (hospitality) to you, or naked and we clothed you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And when did we see you ill or in the beit hasohar and we came to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MISHLE 19:17; MISHLE 14:31; MATTITYAHU 10:42"> And in reply, HaMelech<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="HaMoshiach">c</a></sup>will say to them, Omein, I say to you, in as much as you did it to one of the least of these achim of mine, you did it to me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:4; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:6–7; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:9"> Then Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach will say also to the ones on his left, Depart from me, the ones having been cursed, into the Eish Olam having been prepared for HaSatan and his malachim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–24; YOCHANAN 5:23; MATTITYAHU 12:30"> For I hungered and you did not give me something to eat; I thirsted and you did not give drink to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> I was a sojourner and you did not extend hachnosas orchim to me; I was naked and you did not clothe me; ill and in the beit hasohar and you did not visit me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:22; LUKAS 10:29; MATTITYAHU 25:24–27"> Then, also they will answer, saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Moshiach">d</a></sup>Adoneinu, when did we see you hungering or thirsting or a sojourner or naked or sick or in the beit hasohar and we did not minister to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MISHLE 21:13; MISHLE 17:5; MISHLE 14:31"> Then he will answer them, saying, Omein, I say to you, in as much as you did not do it for one of these least ones, neither did you do it for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="DANIEL 12:2; YOCHANAN 3:36; YOCHANAN 5:29"> And these will go away into Onesh Olam (Eternal Punishment), but the tzaddikim into Chayyei Olam (Eternal Life).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Isa 66:24; Dan 12:2">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
