<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:5; YOCHANAN 12:43; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:10"> Be careful that you don't practice your tzedakah (charity giving) before Bnei Adam in order to varf (show off, flaunt) to be seen by Bnei Adam; for then you have no sachar (reward) with your Av sh'ba'Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:5; MATTITYAHU 6:16; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:6–15"> Therefore, whenever you contribute tzedakah, don't blow the shofar before you, as the tzevuim (hypocrites) do in the shuls and in the streets, in order that they may receive kavod (glory) from Bnei Adam. Omein, truly I say to you, they have their sachar (reward).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:4; MATTITYAHU 9:30; MARKOS 1:44"> But when you contribute tzedakah, do not let your left hand have da'as (knowledge) of what your right hand is doing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:18; MATTITYAHU 6:6; YIRMEYAH 17:10"> so that your tzedakah may be nistar (hidden, in secret), and your Av HaRoeh b'seter (Father, the One seeing in secret)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="His">a</a></sup>sachar (reward) will give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKAS 18:10–11; MATTITYAHU 6:2; LUKAS 18:13"> And whenever you daven (pray), do not be as the tzevuim (hypocrites); for they love to stand and daven in the shuls and on the street corners so that they may be seen by an audience. Omein, truly I say to you, they have their sachar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:33; MATTITYAHU 14:23; YESHAYAH 65:24"> But you, whenever you daven, enter into the secret place, your secret cheder, and, having shut your door, offer tefillos to your Av Asher b'Seter (Father Who is in Secret), and your Av haRoeh b'seter (Father, the One seeing in secret)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="His">b</a></sup>sachar will give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="KOHELET 5:2–3; KOHELET 5:7; MELACHIM ALEF 18:26–29"> But when you daven, do not babble on and on maarich (extended, long winded) and vacuously and without kavvanah (heartfelt direction, intention) like the Goyim. For the Goyim think that in their verbosity their techinnah will be heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:32; LUKAS 12:30; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:6"> Therefore, nisht azoi (not so)! Be different, for your Av has daas (knowledge) of what things you have need before you ask Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 11:1–4; HISGALUS 4:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:15"> Therefore, when you offer tefillos, daven like this, in this manner: Avinu sh'ba'Shomayim (Our Father in heaven), yitkadash shmecha (hallowed be your Name).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:36; MATTITYAHU 26:42; MATTITYAHU 12:50"> Tavo malchutechah (Thy Kingdom come) Ye'aseh rtzonechah (Thy will be done) kmoh vaShomayim ken ba'aretz (on earth as it is in heaven).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MISHLE 30:8; LUKAS 11:3; MATTITYAHU 4:4"> Es lechem chukeinu ten lanu hayom (Give us today our daily bread),</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:32; MARKOS 11:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:7"> u-slach lanu es chovoteinu kaasher salachnu (and forgive us our debts as we forgive) gam anachnu lachayaveinu (also our debtors).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:13; HISGALUS 7:14–17; MATTITYAHU 26:41"> V'al tvi'einu lidey nisayon (And lead us not into temptation),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Mt 26:41">c</a></sup>ki im chaltzeinu min harah (but deliver us from evil).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ki l'chah hamamlachah (for thine is the Kingdom) vhagvurah (and the power) vhatiferet (and the glory) l'olmei olamim (forever). Omein">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:32; MARKOS 11:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:13"> For if you give men mechila (pardon, forgiveness) for their chattaim (sins), so also your Av sh'ba'Shomayim will give selicha (forgiveness) to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:35"> But if you do not give men mechila (forgiveness), neither will your chattaim receive selicha from your Av.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YESHAYAH 58:3–5; MATTITYAHU 9:14–15; DANIEL 9:3"> And whenever you undergo a tzom (fast), don't be like the sullen tzevuim (hypocrites), for they disfigure their faces to parade their tzom (fast). Omein, truly I say to you, they have received their sachar (reward).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="RUTH 3:3; KOHELET 9:8; SHMUEL BAIS 12:20"> But when you undergo a tzom, anoint your rosh with shemen and wash your face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:6; MATTITYAHU 6:4; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:18"> so that your tzom is concealed from Bnei Adam but not from your Av Asher b'Seter (Father Who is in Secret). And your Av HaRoeh b'seter (Father the One seeing in secret) will give you sachar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:5; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–16; LUKAS 12:21"> Do not store up for yourselves otzarot (treasures) on haaretz, where moth and rust destroy and where ganavim (thieves) break in and steal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKAS 12:33; MATTITYAHU 19:21; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5"> But zahmlet aich (collect for yourselves) otzarot (treasures) in Shomayim, where neither moth nor rust destroys; neither do ganavim break in and steal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:1–3; LUKAS 12:34; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:18"> For where your otzar is, there also will be your lev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKAS 11:34–36; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:22"> The eye is the menorah of the basar. Therefore, if your eye is unblurred, then your whole basar will be lighted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:11; MATTITYAHU 6:22; YESHAYAH 5:20–21"> But if yours is the ayin horo, your kol basar is choshech; if ohr choshech, great choshech!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–16; LUKAS 16:13; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:4"> No one is able to serve two adonim (masters). For either he will have sinah (hatred) for the one and ahavah (love) for the other, or he will be devoted to the one and despise the other. You cannot serve Hashem and Mammon (Money).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:6; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:32"> Therefore, I say to you, Do not have a LEV ROGEZ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="LEV ROGEZ in verses 25, 27, 31, 34, see Dt 28:65">e</a></sup>(anxious heart) about your life, what you might wear or what you might drink, nor for your basar, what you might put on. Is not life more than okhel (food) and basar more than malbush (clothing)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:29–31; TEHILLIM 147:9; LUKAS 12:24–32"> Look to the OPH HASHOMAYIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Job 35:11">f</a></sup>(birds of heaven), for they do not sow nor reap nor gather into storehouses, and your Av sh'ba'Shomayim feeds them. Are you yourselves not worth more than they?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKAS 12:25–26; KOHELET 3:14; TEHILLIM 39:5–6"> And who among you by a LEV ROGEZ is able to add to his life span one cubit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:6; LUKAS 12:27"> And why have a LEV ROGEZ (anxious heart) about malbush (clothing)? Observe the lilies of the field, how they grow. They do not labor nor spin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:20–22; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:4–6; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:2–5"> But I say to you that not even Sh'lomo HaMelech in all his kavod (glory) was clothed as one of these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 14:31; LUKAS 12:28; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:24"> And if Hashem thus clothes the grass of the field that exists today and tomorrow is thrown into a furnace, how much more will he clothe you, you ones of little emunah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:7; LUKAS 12:29; MATTITYAHU 4:4"> Therefore, do not have a LEV ROGEZ, saying, What might we eat? or What might we drink? or With what might we clothe ourselves?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:8; LUKAS 12:30; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:17"> For all these things the Goyim strive. For your Av sh'ba'Shomayim bavorn (anticipates) that you need all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKAS 12:31; MATTITYAHU 5:6; YOCHANAN 6:27"> But seek first the Malchut Hashem and the Tzidkat Hashem, and all these things will be added to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:25; YOCHANAN 14:27; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:5–6"> Therefore, do not have a LEV ROGEZ for tomorrow, for makhar (tomorrow) will care for itself. Each day has enough tzoros of its own.</span>
