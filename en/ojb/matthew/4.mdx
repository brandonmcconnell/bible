<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:18; LUKAS 4:1–13; MARKOS 1:12–15"> Then Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was led up into the midbar by the Ruach Hakodesh of Hashem to undergo nisyonos (temptations) by HaSatan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 9:18; SHEMOT 34:28; MELACHIM ALEF 19:8"> And, having undergone a tzom (fast) for arba'im yom varbaim lailah (forty days and forty nights), afterward Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was famished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 3:1–5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:5; BAMIDBAR 11:4–6"> And the tempter came to him and said to him, If you are the Ben HaElohim, then speak the command that these avanim (stones) become lechem (bread).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEVARIM 8:3; LUKAS 4:4; YOCHANAN 6:63"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach answered, Katuv (It is written), LO AL HALECHEM LVADOH YCHEYEH HAADAM, KI AL KOL MOTZAH FI HASHEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Dt 8:3">a</a></sup>(Man does not live on bread alone but on every word that comes from the mouth of Hashem).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKAS 4:9; MATTITYAHU 27:53; HISGALUS 11:2"> Then HaSatan takes Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach to the Ir Hakodesh (the Holy City) and sets him atop the pinnacle of the Beis Hamikdash,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 91:11–12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:14; TEHILLIM 34:7"> and says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, If you are the Ben HaElohim, then throw yourself down, for katuv (it is written), MALACHAV YTZAV-VEH LACH (His angels he will command concerning you) and upon their hands they will lift you up lest you strike your foot against a stone (Ps 91:11,12).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 6:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:9; TEHILLIM 78:18"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to HaSatan, Again, it is written, LO TENASSU ES HASHEM ELOHEICHEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dt 6:16">b</a></sup>(Do not test Hashem your G-d).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–16; HISGALUS 11:15; TEHILLIM 49:16–17"> Again, HaSatan takes Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach to a very high mountain and shows him all the mamlechot (kingdoms) of the Olam Hazeh and the kavod (glory) of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:4; DANIEL 5:18–19; YOCHANAN 16:11"> And HaSatan said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, All these things I will give to you, if you will fall down VTISHTACHAVEH (and you will bow down to)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Dt 5:9">c</a></sup>me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEVARIM 6:13–14; DEVARIM 10:20; YEHOSHUA 24:14"> Then Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to him, Depart, HaSatan! For katuv (it is written), ES HASHEM ELOHEICHA TIRA VOTO TAAVOD<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Dt 6:13">d</a></sup>(The L-rd your G-d you shall fear and Him you shall worship).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:53; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:14; LUKAS 22:43"> Then HaSatan leaves him, and, hinei, malachim came and attended to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MARKOS 1:14; LUKAS 3:20; MATTITYAHU 14:3"> Now, having heard that Yochanan was arrested, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach departed into the Galil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:46; MARKOS 1:21; MATTITYAHU 11:23"> And having left behind Natzeret, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach headquartered in K'far-Nachum by the sea, in the environs of Zevulun and Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YESHAYAH 9:1–2; MATTITYAHU 2:23; LUKAS 24:44"> This was to fulfill what was spoken through Yeshayah HaNavi,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YESHAYAH 9:1; MELACHIM BAIS 15:29; YEHOSHUA 21:32"> ARTZAH Zevulun VARTZAH Naphtali (To the land of Zevulun and to the land of Naphtali),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Isa 8:23—9:1">e</a></sup>the way to the sea beyond the Yarden, Galil HaGoyim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YESHAYAH 9:2; YESHAYAH 42:6–7; YESHAYAH 60:1–3"> the people sitting in CHOSHECH RAU OHR GADOL (darkness saw a great light), YOSHVEI V'ERETZ TZALMAVET (the ones living in the land of the shadow of death), a light has dawned among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 3:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:38; MARKOS 1:14–15"> From then Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach began to proclaim, saying, LAlter LiTeshuvah (Immediate Repentance), for the Malchut HaShomayim has come near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:40–42; MATTITYAHU 10:2; LUKAS 6:14"> And walking beside Sea of Galilee (Lake Kinneret), Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach saw two achim, Shim'on, the one being called Kefa (Petros), and his brother Andrew, casting a net into the sea, for they were daiyagim (fishermen).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARKOS 1:17–18; YOCHANAN 12:26; MATTITYAHU 16:24"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says, Come, follow me, and I will make you daiyagei adam (fishers of men).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MARKOS 10:28–31; MATTITYAHU 19:27; LUKAS 18:28–30"> And immediately, leaving their nets, they followed Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MARKOS 1:19–20; MATTITYAHU 10:2; MARKOS 3:17"> And having gone on from there, he saw two other achim, Ya'akov Ben Zavdai and Yochanan his brother, in the sirah (boat) with Zavdai their father, repairing their nets, and he summoned them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:37; LUKAS 9:59–60; DEVARIM 33:9–10"> Immediately, leaving the sirah (boat) and their father, they followed Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Mt 19:29">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:35; MARKOS 1:39; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:38"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach went about in all the Galil, exercising the ministry of moreh (teacher) in their shuls and of darshan (preacher) of the Besuroh Tovoh (Good Tiding of a Joyous Event), the Besuras HaGeulah (the Good News of Redemption) of the Malchut Hashem, and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach went about bringing refuah (healing) to every disease and every illness of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 8:6; MATTITYAHU 17:15; LUKAS 5:15"> And the report went out about Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach throughout all Syria; and they brought to him all the cholim (sick) with various illnesses and machalot, demoniacs, epileptics, and paralytics; and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach brought them refuah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKAS 6:17; MARKOS 5:20; MARKOS 3:7–8"> And from the Galil, the Decapolis, Yerushalayim, Yehudah, and Ever-HaYarden, from all these places great multitudes followed him.</span>
