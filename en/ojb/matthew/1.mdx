<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:17–18; BERESHIS 46:8–27"> Avraham, then Yitzchak, Ya'akov, Yehudah and his brothers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:12; BERESHIS 38:29–30; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 4:1"> then, through Yehudah, Peretz and Zerach by Tamar; then Chetzron, Ram;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:10–12; BAMIDBAR 7:12; BAMIDBAR 2:3"> then Amminadav, Nachshon, Salmon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:31; RUTH 1:16–17; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:25"> then Boaz by Rachav; then Oved by Ruth, then Yishai;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 15:5; SHMUEL ALEF 17:12; SHMUEL BAIS 12:24–25"> then Yishai fathered Dovid HaMelech; then Sh'lomo by the wife of Uriyah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:1–15; MELACHIM ALEF 14:31; MELACHIM ALEF 11:43–24"> then Rechavam, Aviyah, Asa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 26:1–23; MELACHIM BAIS 8:16"> then Yehoshafat, Yoram, Uziyahu;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YESHAYAH 7:1–13; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 26:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 27:1–32"> then Yotam, Achaz, Chizkiyahu,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:33–27; MELACHIM ALEF 13:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:13–15"> then Menasheh, Amon, Yoshiyahu,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 27:20; YIRMEYAH 52:28–30; DANIEL 1:2"> then Ykhanyahu and his achim from the days of the Golus in Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:17; EZRA 3:2; LUKAS 3:27"> After Ykhanyahu, then Shealtiel, then Zerubavel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> then Avichud, then Elyakim, then Azur;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> then Tzadok, then Yakhin, then Elichud;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> then El'azar, then Mattan, then Ya'akov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:17; YOCHANAN 4:25; LUKAS 3:23"> Ya'akov fathered Yosef, the baal (husband) of Miryam, from whom was born Yehoshua, who is called Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Yn 1:49">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 1:11–12; MELACHIM BAIS 24:14; MATTITYAHU 11:2"> So then, there were arba'a asar (fourteen) dorot (generations) from Avraham to Dovid,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="T.N. Dovid = DVD in Hebrew = [d = 4, v = 6, d = 4] = (4 + 6 + 4 = 14)">b</a></sup>and there were arba'a asar dorot from Dovid to the Golus in Babylon, and there were arba'a asar dorot from the Golus in Babylon to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:4–5; LUKAS 1:27–38; IYOV 14:4"> The huledet (birth) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was as follows. When Moshiachʼs Em, Miryam, had been given in erusin to Yosef,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="ben Dovid">c</a></sup>but before they came together, she was found with child through the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 22:21–24; DEVARIM 24:1–4; YOCHANAN 8:4–5"> Her shidduch, Yosef,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="ben Dovid">d</a></sup>being a tzaddik and not wanting to humiliate her publicly, planned to deal with the get (divorce) in a private arrangement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MISHLE 3:5–6; LUKAS 1:19; MATTITYAHU 1:18"> Now just when Yosef<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="ben Dovid">e</a></sup>had thought through to this tachlis (purpose) hinei! A malach Hashem appeared to him in a chalom, and said, Yosef ben Dovid, do not shrink from taking Miryam in nisuim (marriage) as your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="basherte (destined mate) aishes chayil (virtuous woman)">f</a></sup>kallah (bride), because what has been conceived in her is through the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:12; YOCHANAN 1:29"> And she shall bear BEN (Son) and you will call SHMO<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Zech 6:12">g</a></sup>(his name) YEHOSHUA<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Zech 6:11-12">h</a></sup>because he will bring his people yeshu'ah (rescue, salvation, deliverance) from their peyshaim (rebellions).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKAS 24:44; MATTITYAHU 2:23; MELACHIM ALEF 8:15"> Now all this occurred so that which was spoken by Hashem through the Navi might be fulfilled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YESHAYAH 7:14; YESHAYAH 9:6–7; TEHILLIM 46:11"> HINEI, HAALMAH HARAH V'YOLEDET BEN VKARAT SHMO IMMANU-EL (Behold, the Virgin will be with child and will bear Son and will call his name Immanu-El)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Isa 7:14; cf. Orthodox Jewish Bible Introduction">i</a></sup>— which translated means G-d is with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:14; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:21–26; YOCHANAN 2:5–8"> Then Yosef,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="ben Dovid">j</a></sup>rising up from sleep, did as the malach Hashem commanded him and he took his kallah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKAS 2:21; SHEMOT 13:2; MATTITYAHU 1:21"> And Yosef<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="ben Dovid">k</a></sup>did not know her until she bore BEN and Yosef<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Ben Dovid">l</a></sup>called him by name of Yehoshua.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Ben Dovid">m</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="See Baba Bathra 8:6">n</a></sup></span>
