<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MICHOH 5:2; YOCHANAN 7:42; LUKAS 2:4–7"> Now after Yehoshua was born in Beit-Lechem Yehudah, in the days of Herod<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the Great, the Roman client">a</a></sup>king — hinei! — chachamim from eretz Mizrach (the land of the East) arrived in Yerushalayim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 24:17; YIRMEYAH 23:5; HISGALUS 22:16"> saying, Where is he that is born Melech HaYehudim? For we have seen his KOCHAV<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Num 24:17">b</a></sup>(star) in the Mizrach and come to fall down before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:6–7; YOCHANAN 11:47–48; MATTITYAHU 23:37"> Because Herod the king heard this, he was terribly shaken, and all Yerushalayim with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:23; MATTITYAHU 7:29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:12"> And assembling all the Rashei Hakohanim and Sofrim of the people, he inquired of them the place where Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was to be born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:42; RUTH 1:1; SHMUEL ALEF 16:1"> They told him, In Beit-Lechem Yehudah; because so it stands written by the Navi:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 34:23–25; MICHOH 5:2; YESHAYAH 9:6–7"> And YOU, BEIT-LECHEM, (Bethlehem, Mic 5:1&#91;2&#93;) in eretz Yehudah are by no means least among the shtetlach (towns) or ruling<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="alafim">c</a></sup>of Yehudah; because out of you will come a Moshel (Ruler) who will be the Ro'eh Ami Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 1:10; SHMUEL ALEF 18:21; TEHILLIM 55:21"> Then Herod secretly summoned the chachamim and ascertained from them the exact date of the manifestation of the KOCHAV.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:19–20; YIRMEYAH 41:5–7; LUKAS 20:20–21"> Then he sent them to Beit-Lechem, saying, Go and make a thorough search for the yeled (child); as soon as you have found him, report to me, so that I, as you, may come and fall down before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 2:2; MISHLE 8:17; TEHILLIM 25:12"> After they heard the king, they departed, and hinei! The KOCHAV they had seen in the Mizrach (East) was going ahead of them until it came and stood above the place where the yeled was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 105:3; LUKAS 2:20; LUKAS 2:10"> When they saw the KOCHAV, they rejoiced with exceedingly great nachas (joy).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 60:6; TEHILLIM 72:10; LUKAS 2:16"> And, entering the bais (house), they beheld the yeled with Miryam, his Em, and prostrated themselves before him. Then, opening their treasure chests, they offered gifts of gold, frankincense, and myrrh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 2:22; MATTITYAHU 2:19; MATTITYAHU 2:13"> And having been warned in a chalom (dream) not to return to Herod, they took refuge in their own country by another way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:19; MATTITYAHU 1:20"> And when they had gone — hinei! — a malach Hashem appeared to Yosef<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ben Dovid">d</a></sup>in a chalom (dream), saying, Arise, and take the yeled and his Em, and flee to Eretz Mitzrayim (Egypt), and stay there until I tell you, for Herod is about to seek the yeled to destroy him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 2:20–21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:21; MATTITYAHU 1:24"> So, having got up, Yosef<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="ben Dovid">e</a></sup>took the yeled, and his Em by night and departed for Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HOSHEA 11:1; SHEMOT 4:22; BAMIDBAR 24:8"> And Yosef<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="ben Dovid">f</a></sup>was there until the mot Herod. This was to fulfill what had been spoken by Hashem through the Navi, UMIMITZRAYIM KARATI LIVNI<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hos 11:1">g</a></sup>(Out of Egypt have I called my Son).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YESHAYAH 59:7; MISHLE 28:15; SHOFETIM 16:10"> When Herod saw that he had been outwitted by the chachamim, he was greatly enraged, and he ordered the slaughter of all the yeladim in Beit-Lechem and its vicinity who were two years old or under, in accordance with the time that he had learned from the chachamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then came the fulfillment of what was spoken through Yirmeyah HaNavi:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:15; YIRMEYAH 9:17–21; YIRMEYAH 4:31"> KOL BRAMAH NISHMAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Jer 31:14">h</a></sup>(A voice was heard in Ramah),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="15">i</a></sup>a voice of bitter weeping and mourning. Rachel is crying for her yeladim; she refuses to be comforted, because they are no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 1:20; DANIEL 8:25; MATTITYAHU 2:12–13"> When Herod died, hinei, in Eretz Mitzrayim a malach Hashem appeared to Yosef<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="ben Dovid">j</a></sup>in a chalom (dream),</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:19; MELACHIM ALEF 12:1–3; MATTITYAHU 2:13"> saying, Arise, and take the yeled and his Em and go to Eretz Yisroel, for those seeking the life of the yeled are dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:8; BERESHIS 6:22"> And Yosef<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="ben Dovid">k</a></sup>arose and took the yeled and his Em, and entered Eretz Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YESHAYAH 48:17–18; LUKAS 2:39; YOCHANAN 7:52"> And when Yosef<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="ben Dovid">l</a></sup>heard that Archelaus had succeeded his father Herod as king of Yehudah, Yosef<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="ben Dovid">m</a></sup>was afraid to go there. And, having been warned in a chalom, he departed into the districts of the Galil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHOFETIM 13:5; YOCHANAN 1:45–46; MARKOS 1:24"> And Yosef<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="ben Dovid">n</a></sup>made his home in the shtetl (Jewish village) called Natzeret, so that which was spoken by the Neviim might be fulfilled: He will be called a Natzri.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="i.e., Moshiach the Netzer, Tzemach, Isa 11:1; Jer 23:5; Zech 3:8; Isa 53:2; Zech 6:11-12">o</a></sup></span>
</div>
