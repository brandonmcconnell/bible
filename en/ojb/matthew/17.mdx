<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 9:28–36; MATTITYAHU 26:37; MARKOS 5:37"> And after shisha yamim (six days), Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach took Kefa, Ya'akov and Yochanan his brother; he brings them up to a high mountain in yechidus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="TEHILLIM 104:2; MATTITYAHU 28:3; LUKAS 9:29"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was transfigured before them, and Moshiachʼs face shone like the shemesh (sun), and his garments became brilliant like the ohr.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKAS 24:44; MARKOS 9:4; MALACHI 4:5"> And hinei! There appeared before them Moshe Rabbenu and Eliyahu HaNavi conferring with Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MARKOS 9:5–6; LUKAS 9:33; YOCHANAN 14:8–9"> And Kefa said in reply to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Adoni, it is good for us to be here. If you wish, I will make shalosh sukkot here: one for you, and one for Moshe Rabbenu, and one for Eliyahu HaNavi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 3:17; MATTITYAHU 12:18; DEVARIM 18:15"> While he was speaking, hinei! A brightly shining anan (cloud) overshadowed them, and hinei! A bat kol (a voice from heaven) out of the anan was saying, This is My Ben ahuvi (beloved Son) with whom I am well-pleased. Listen to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:14; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:18; DANIEL 8:17"> And having heard this, the talmidim fell on their faces and were filled with pachad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DANIEL 10:18; MATTITYAHU 14:27; DANIEL 8:18"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach came to them, and touched them, and said, Arise, and do not be afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKAS 9:36; MARKOS 9:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:10–11"> And lifting their eyes, they saw no one except only Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach himself, alone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MARKOS 8:30; MARKOS 9:9–13; MATTITYAHU 17:23"> And as Moshiachʼs talmidim were coming down from the mountain, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach gave them this directive saying, Tell the mareh (vision) to no one until the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Moshiach">a</a></sup>has undergone the Techiyas HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MALACHI 4:5–6; MATTITYAHU 11:14; YOCHANAN 1:21"> And Moshiachʼs talmidim asked him, saying, Why then do the Sofrim say that it is necessary for Eliyahu HaNavi to come first?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:21; LUKAS 1:16–17; MALACHI 4:6"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said in reply, Eliyahu HaNavi does indeed have his coming and will restore all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:3–12; MATTITYAHU 16:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:24–28"> But I say to you, that Eliyahu has come already, and they did not have daas of him, but did to him whatever they willed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Mt 6:10">b</a></sup>So also the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Moshiach">c</a></sup>is about to suffer by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:14"> Then the Moshiachʼs talmidim had binah that he spoke to them about Yochanan of the tevilah of teshuva.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MARKOS 9:14–29; MARKOS 1:40; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:25–26"> And when they came to the multitude, a man came up to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, falling down before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:24; IYOV 1:10–19; MARKOS 9:20–22"> And the man said, Adoni, grant rachamim (mercy) on this ben (son) of mine, for he is an epileptic, and suffers horribly; for often he falls into the eish, and often into the mayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 17:19–20; LUKAS 9:40; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:15–16"> And I brought him to your talmidim, and they could not give him refuah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:11; MATTITYAHU 6:30; MARKOS 16:14"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said in reply, O perverted generation without emunah, how long shall I be with you? How long shall I put up with you? Bring him here to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:22; MARKOS 1:34; MARKOS 5:8"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach rebuked it, and the shed came out of him, and the bocher received his refuah that hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARKOS 9:28; MARKOS 4:10"> Then the talmidim came to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach in a yechidus (private meeting with the Rebbe), and said, Why were we not able to cast it out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKAS 17:6; MARKOS 11:23; LUKAS 1:37"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, On account of your little emunah, for, omein, I say to you, if you have emunah as a mustard seed, you shall say to this mountain, Move from here, and it shall be moved; and nothing shall be impossible for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But this kind<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="i.e., kind of mazikim (malicious spirits)">d</a></sup>do not come out except by tefillah and tzom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:21; LUKAS 18:31–34; MATTITYAHU 17:23"> And as they were gathering together in the Galil, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, The Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Moshiach">e</a></sup>is about to be handed over, even betrayed, into the hands of Bnei Adam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:21; YESHAYAH 53:10–12; MARKOS 8:31"> And they will kill him, and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach will have a histalkus (passing) and will undergo the Techiyas HaMoshiach on the Yom HaShlishi. And Moshiachʼs talmidim were deeply moved with agmat nefesh (grief).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHEMOT 38:26; SHEMOT 30:13; MARKOS 9:33"> And when they had come to K'far-Nachum, those who collected the two drachma tax approached Kefa, and said, Does your rebbe not pay the two drachma tax?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 22:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:6–7; MATTITYAHU 22:17"> Kefa said, Ken. And when Kefa came into the bais (house), Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach anticipated Kefa with this question, What think you, Shim'on? From whom do the melechei haaretz (kings of the earth) collect customs or poll tax, from their banim (sons) or from zarim (strangers)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And upon Kefaʼs saying, From zarim, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to Kefa, Veha raya (consequently), the banim are patur (exempt, free).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:19–22"> But, lest we give them offense, go to the yam (sea), and cast in a hook, and take the first dag (fish) that comes up; and when you open its mouth, you will find a stater (coin). Take that and give it to them for you and for me.</span>
</div>
