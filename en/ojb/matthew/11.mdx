<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YESHAYAH 61:1–3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:38; LUKAS 8:1"> And it came about when he finished giving directives to his Shneym Asar Talmidim, he moved on from there, exercising the ministry of moreh (teacher) and darshan (preacher, maggid) in their shtetlach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MARKOS 6:17; MATTITYAHU 14:3; MATTITYAHU 9:14"> Now when Yochanan, in the beit hasohor (prison), heard of the maasei haMoshiach (works of Moshiach), he sent his talmidim to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:27; MALACHI 3:1; DEVARIM 18:15–18"> They asked him, Tell us. Are you Hu Habah (He who comes) or is our chikiah (wait) to be for the Bias (Coming) of another?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach answered them, saying, Go give the report of what you see and hear to Yochanan:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YESHAYAH 35:4–6; YESHAYAH 61:1–3; LUKAS 4:18"> The ivrim (blind people) see, the pisechim (lame people) walk, the metzoraim (lepers) are cleansed, the chereshim (deaf people) hear, the mesim are made to stand up alive, and the aniyim (poor) have the Besuras HaGeulah preached to them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Isa 35:5f; 61:1">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:14–15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:14; MATTITYAHU 26:31"> Ashrey (Happy) is the one who does not find a cause of michshol (falling, stumbling) in me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="as Moshiach">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:6; YOCHANAN 5:35; MARKOS 1:3–5"> As they were leaving, he began to speak to the multitudes about Yochanan, What did you go out into the midbar (wilderness) to see? A reed being shaken by the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 3:4; MELACHIM BAIS 1:8; ZECHARYAH 13:4"> What did you go out to see? Someone shtats (imposing, impressive) in appearance, attired in soft raiment? Hinei, the ones wearing soft raiment are in houses of melachim (kings).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 1:76; MATTITYAHU 14:5; MATTITYAHU 21:24–26"> But what did you go out to see? A Navi? Ken, I tell you. And one greater than a Navi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MALACHI 3:1; YESHAYAH 40:3; MALACHI 4:5"> This is he about whom it has been written, HINNENI SHOLEIACH MALACHI (Behold, I will send my messenger) before your face, UPINNAH DERECH LEFANAI (and he will prepare the way before me).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ex 23:20; Mal 3:1">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 7:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:8; MATTITYAHU 3:11"> Omein, I say to you, of those born of women, there has not appeared one greater than Yochanan of the tevilah of teshuva, yet the person with the least chashivut in the Malchut HaShomayim is greater than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKAS 16:16; LUKAS 13:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:11–13"> From the days of Yochanan of the tevilah of teshuva until now, the Malchut HaShomayim has been under violent attack and the violent seize it with coercion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:17–18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:27"> For all the Neviim and the Torah prophesied until Yochanan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MALACHI 4:5; MATTITYAHU 17:10–13; MARKOS 9:11–13"> And, if you are willing it to be mekabel (accept, receive), he is the “Eliyahu,” who is to come.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Mal 3:23">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 8:8; MATTITYAHU 13:9; MARKOS 4:23"> The one having ears, let him hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 7:31–35; MATTITYAHU 12:34; MATTITYAHU 23:36"> But to what will I compare this generation? It is like yeladim sitting in the marketplaces, calling out to one another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YESHAYAH 28:9–13; YESHAYAH 30:29; YIRMEYAH 9:17–20"> We played the chalil (flute) for you, but you did not dance; we sang a kina (lament, funeral dirge) for you, and you did not mourn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKAS 1:15; MATTITYAHU 3:4; YOCHANAN 10:20"> For Yochanan came neither eating nor drinking, and they say, He has a shed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:10–11; LUKAS 15:1–2; LUKAS 19:7"> The Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Moshiach">e</a></sup>came eating and drinking, and they say, Hinei! A zolel (glutton) and a shikkor (drunkard), a friend of mochesim (tax collectors) and chote'im (sinners). Yet, Chochmah (Wisdom) is vindicated by her ma'asim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKAS 10:13–15; YIRMEYAH 8:6; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:25–26"> Then he began to reproach the shtetlach (villages) in which were accomplished most of his gevurot (mighty deeds), because they did not make teshuva.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 3:6–7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:20; LUKAS 9:10"> Oy l'chah (Woe to you), Korazin! Oy l'chah, Beit-Tzaidah! For if in Tzor and Tzidon were accomplished the gevurot that were accomplished among you, they would have made teshuva long ago in sackcloth and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:24; MATTITYAHU 10:15; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:26–31"> Nevertheless, I say to you, it will be more bearable for Tzor and Tzidon than for you on Yom HaDin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YESHAYAH 14:13–15; YOCHANAN 4:46–54; BERESHIS 13:13"> And you, K'far-Nachum, were you lifted up as far as Shomayim? You shall be brought down as far as Sheol.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isa 14:13,15">f</a></sup>For if in S'dom were accomplished the gevurot that were accomplished among you, S'dom would have a sheerit (remnant) today!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:15; MATTITYAHU 11:22; MARKOS 6:11"> Nevertheless, I say to you, it will be more bearable for the land of S'dom on Yom HaDin than for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:16; TEHILLIM 8:2; MATTITYAHU 16:17"> At that time, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Modeh Ani, Avi, Adon HaShomayim v'HaAretz, (I thank you, Father, L-rd of Heaven and Earth), because you hid these things from the chachamim (wise) and those with seichel, and to them they are nistar (hidden, esoteric), and you made a hisgalus (revelation) of them unto ollelim (infants).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:11; IYOV 33:13"> Ken, Avi, for thus it was pleasing in your sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:6–9; MATTITYAHU 28:18; LUKAS 10:22"> All things were given to me by Avi, and no one has daas (knowledge) of HaBen except HaAv, nor does anyone have daas of HaAv, except HaBen, and anyone to whom HaBen chooses to unveil him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:29; YOCHANAN 7:37; YOCHANAN 6:37"> Come to me, all you who are burden-weary and heavy laden, and I will give you Shabbos menuchah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 6:16; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:7–8"> Take my ol (yoke) upon you and learn from me, for I am anav (humble) and have shiflut (lowliness) in lev (heart), and you will find menuchah (rest, security)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ruth 1:9">g</a></sup>for your nefashot (souls).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Jer 6:16">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:3; YOCHANAN 16:33; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:13"> For my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Moshiachʼs">i</a></sup>ol (yoke) is easy and my burden is light.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="See Mt 23:4">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
