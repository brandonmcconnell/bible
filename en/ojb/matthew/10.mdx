<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 9:1–6; MARKOS 3:13–15; MARKOS 6:7–13"> And summoning his Shneym Asar (Twelve) Talmidim, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach gave to them samchut (authority) over shedim (unclean spirits), so as to cast them out, and samchut (authority) to call forth refuah for every machla and every illness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:13; LUKAS 5:10"> Now of the Shneym Asar Shlichim, the names are these: first, Shim'on, the one being called Kefa, and Andrew his brother, Ya'akov Ben Zavdai and Yochanan his brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MARKOS 3:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:13; MARKOS 2:14"> Philippos and Bar-Talmai, T'oma and Mattityahu the moches, Ya'akov Ben-Chalfai and Taddai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:14; MATTITYAHU 26:47; YOCHANAN 13:2"> Shim'on the Zealot and Yehudah from K'riot, who betrayed Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:21–23; MELACHIM BAIS 17:24–41"> These Shneym Asar (Twelve) did Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach send out with the following directives, saying: In the Derech HaGoyim (Way of the Gentiles, Gentile territory) do not go, and do not enter into any Shomron town,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 50:6; YECHEZKEL 34:6; YESHAYAH 53:6"> but go rather to the Seh Oveid Beis Yisroel (the Lost Sheep of the House of Israel).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:31; MATTITYAHU 3:2; LUKAS 9:60"> And, while going, preach that the Malchut HaShomayim is imminent and miyad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:6; LUKAS 10:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:30"> Heal the cholim, raise the mesim, cleanse the metzoraim (lepers), cast out the shedim; freely you received, freely give.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 22:35; LUKAS 9:3–5; LUKAS 10:4–12"> Take neither gold nor silver nor copper for your money belts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKAS 10:7–12; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:17–18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:4–14"> nor a schnorrerʼs (beggarʼs) bag for the road, nor two bekishe, nor sandals, nor a staff, for the oseh hamelachah (the one doing the work) is worthy of his lechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 10:7–8; IYOV 31:32; SHOFETIM 19:16–21"> And into whichever town or shtetl you enter, inquire who in it is a ben chayil (son of worthiness), and there remain until you leave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:6; LUKAS 10:5–6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:36"> And when you enter into the bais (house), give that household your “Shalom”!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 10:6; TEHILLIM 35:13; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:16"> And if indeed the bais is a bais chayil (a house of worthiness), let your shalom come upon it; but if it is not a bais chayil, let your shalom return to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 10:10–11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:6; NECHEMYAH 5:13"> And whoever neither receives you nor listens to your divrei,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Moshiach">a</a></sup>as you are leaving and as you go outside of that bais (house) or city, then let it be NIER CHATZNO (shake out the fold of the robe),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., wash ones hands of">b</a></sup>shake off the dust of your feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:6; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:17; YOCHANAN 15:22–24"> Omein, I say to you, it will be more tolerable on Yom HaDin (the Day of Judgment) for S'dom and Amora than for that shtetl.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 10:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:20"> Hinei! I send you as kevesim (sheep) in the midst of ze'evim; therefore, have the seichel (wisdom) of nachashim (serpents) and be tamim (faultless) as yonim (doves).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MARKOS 13:9; MATTITYAHU 23:34; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:11"> And beware of Bnei Adam, for they will deliver you up to the sanhedriyot (local councils, bet din courts) and in their shuls they will subject you to the shot (whip).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:25–27; MATTITYAHU 8:4; TEHILLIM 2:1–6"> And before moshelim (governors) and also melachim (kings) you will be led for my sake,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="for the sake of Moshiach">c</a></sup>for an edut (testimony) to them and to the Goyim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:12; MARKOS 13:11–13; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:17"> But when they deliver you up, do not be of a LEV ROGEZ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Dt 28:65">d</a></sup>(anxious heart) worried about how or what you are to say; for it will be given to you in that hour what you are to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKAS 21:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:10; LUKAS 12:12"> For you are not the ones speaking, but the Ruach Avichem (the Spirit of your Father) is the one speaking in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKAS 21:16–17; MICHOH 7:5–6; MARKOS 13:12–13"> And brother will deliver up to death his own brother, even an abba his own yeled. And yeladim will stand up against their horim (parents) and put them to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:13; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; HISGALUS 2:10"> And you will be under the sinas chinom (baseless hatred) of kol Bnei Adam on account of my Name; but the one enduring ad es HaKetz (until the End) will receive the Yeshu'at Eloheinu (the Salvation of our G-d).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:1; MARKOS 13:26"> But when they persecute you in one shtetl, flee to the other; for, omein, I say to you, by no means will you complete the shtetlach of Eretz Yisroel until the Bias HaMoshiach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dan 7:13">e</a></sup>(Coming of the Moshiach, the Bar Enosh).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:20; LUKAS 6:40; YOCHANAN 13:16"> A talmid is not above his Rebbe nor an eved (servant) above his Baal Bayit (master).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKAS 11:15; MATTITYAHU 12:24; MATTITYAHU 9:34"> It is enough for the talmid that he be like his Rebbe, and the eved like his Baal Bayit. If they called the Baal Bayit Baal-zibbul, how much more the anashim (men) in his bais.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LUKAS 8:17; MARKOS 4:22; YIRMEYAH 1:8"> Therefore, do not fear them; for nothing has been veiled which will not be unveiled; and nothing has been nistar (hidden) which will not be made known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:20; YOCHANAN 16:13; YOCHANAN 16:1"> What I say to you in the choshech (darkness), you declare in the ohr (light); and what you hear whispered into your ears, shout, preach, from the rooftops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKAS 12:4–5; YESHAYAH 8:12–13; HISGALUS 2:10"> And do not fear those who kill the basar (flesh), but are unable to kill the nefesh (soul); but rather fear the One who is able to destroy both basar and nefesh in Gehinnom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LUKAS 12:6–7; TEHILLIM 104:27–30"> Are not two sparrows sold for the least valuable copper coin? And yet not one of them will fall to the ground apart from Avichem!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LUKAS 12:7; SHMUEL ALEF 14:45; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:34"> But, as far as you are concerned, even the hairs of your rosh have been inventoried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LUKAS 12:24; TEHILLIM 8:5; MATTITYAHU 6:26"> Therefore, al taarotz (do not be afraid)! You are of more value than many sparrows!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:9–10; LUKAS 12:8–9"> Therefore, everyone who shall declare publicly the Ani Maamin hoda'ah of me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="as Moshiach">f</a></sup>before Bnei HaAdam, I will make hoda'ah (acknowledgment) of him before Avi sh'ba'Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:12; LUKAS 9:26; LUKAS 12:9"> But everyone who makes hakhchashah (denial) of me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="as Moshiach">g</a></sup>I also will make hakhchashah (denial) of him before Avi sh'ba'Shomayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LUKAS 12:49–53; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:45–50"> Do not think that I have come to bring shalom al haaretz (peace on the earth); I have not come to bring shalom but a cherev (sword).</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MICHOH 7:5–6; MATTITYAHU 10:21; LUKAS 21:16"> For I came to divide a man against his Av, and a bat against her Em, and a kallah (bride) against her chamot (mother-in-law, shviger).</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MICHOH 7:6; YIRMEYAH 12:6; TEHILLIM 41:9"> And the OIYVEI ISH ANSHEI VEITO<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Mic 7:6">h</a></sup>(the enemies of a man will be the members of his own household).</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKAS 14:26; MATTITYAHU 22:37; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:7–9"> The one who has more ahavah (love) for a tata (papa) or a mama than for me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Moshiach">i</a></sup>is not worthy of me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Moshiach">j</a></sup>and the one who has more ahavah (love) for a ben or a bat than me is not worthy of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LUKAS 14:27; MARKOS 8:34; MATTITYAHU 16:24"> The one who does not take up his etz shel mesiros nefesh (tree of self-sacrifice) and follow after me, is not worthy of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:25; MARKOS 8:35–36; LUKAS 17:33"> The one who has found his nefesh will lose it; the one who has lost his nefesh for my sake<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="lemaan Moshiach">k</a></sup>will find it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:20; LUKAS 9:48; LUKAS 10:16"> The one who gives the kabbalat panim (welcome) to you gives the Baruch Habah (welcome) to me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="to Moshiach">l</a></sup>and the one who gives the Baruch Habah to me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Moshiach">m</a></sup>gives the Baruch Habah to the One<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Elohim HaAv">n</a></sup>who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="(III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5–8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:10; MELACHIM BAIS 4:16–17"> The one who gives the Baruch Habah to a navi (prophet) in the name of a navi will receive the sachar (reward) of a navi, and the one who gives the Baruch Habah to a tzaddik (righteous man) in the name of a tzaddik will receive the sachar of a tzaddik.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:10; MATTITYAHU 25:40; MARKOS 9:41–42"> And whoever in the name of a talmid<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="of Moshiach">o</a></sup>gives only a cup of cold water to one of these little ones,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="the least of the talmidim of Moshiach">p</a></sup>omein, I say to you, he will by no means lose his sachar.</span>
</div>
