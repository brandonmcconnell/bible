<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:1; MATTITYAHU 23:3; DEVARIM 29:10"> And Moshe called kol Yisroel, and said unto them, Shema, Yisroel, to the chukkim and mishpatim which I speak in your ears today, that ye may learn them, and be shomer to do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:23; SHEMOT 19:5–8; SHEMOT 24:8"> Hashem Eloheinu made a Brit with us in Chorev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEVARIM 29:10–15; BERESHIS 17:21; BERESHIS 17:7"> Hashem made not this Brit with Avoteinu, but with us, even us, who are all of us here alive today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:18–19; DEVARIM 34:10; SHEMOT 19:9"> Hashem talked with you panim b'panim in the har out of the midst of the eish,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:19; SHEMOT 19:16; SHEMOT 20:18–21"> (I stood between Hashem and you at that time, to show you the Devar Hashem; for ye were afraid by reason of the eish, and went not up into the har) saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:2–17; TEHILLIM 81:5–10; VAYIKRA 26:1–2"> &#91;ALEF&#93; I am Hashem Eloheicha, Which brought thee out of Eretz Mitzrayim, from the bais avadim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:3; MATTITYAHU 4:10; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:21"> &#91;BET&#93; Thou shalt have no elohim acharim before Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:4; DEVARIM 4:15–19"> Thou shalt not make thee any pesel, or any temunah of anything that is in Shomayim above, or that is in Ha'Aretz beneath, or that is in the mayim beneath ha'aretz;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:4–6; SHEMOT 34:7; DANIEL 9:4–9"> Lo tishtachaveh (thou shalt not bow down thyself) unto them, nor serve them; for I Hashem Eloheicha am an El Kannah, visiting the iniquity of the avot upon the banim unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate Me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:28; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7; YIRMEYAH 32:18"> And showing chesed unto thousands of them that love Me and are shomer over My mitzvot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:7; VAYIKRA 19:12; DEVARIM 6:13"> &#91;GIMEL&#93; Thou shalt not take the Shem of Hashem Eloheicha in vain; for Hashem will not hold him guiltless that taketh Shmo in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:8–11; YESHAYAH 58:13; YESHAYAH 56:6"> &#91;DALET&#93; Keep shomer Shabbos to set Shabbos apart as kodesh as Hashem Eloheicha commanded thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:12; LUKAS 13:14–16; SHEMOT 23:12"> Sheshet yamim thou shalt labor, and do all thy work:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:4; BERESHIS 2:2; SHEMOT 23:12"> But the Yom HaShevi'i is the Shabbos of Hashem Eloheicha: in it thou shalt not do any work, thou, nor thy ben, nor thy bat, nor thy eved, nor thy maidservant, nor thine ox, nor thine donkey, nor any of thy cattle, nor thy ger that is within thy she'arim; that thy eved and thy amah may rest as well as thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEVARIM 5:6; DEVARIM 15:15; DEVARIM 16:12"> And remember that thou wast an eved in Eretz Mitzrayim, and that Hashem Eloheicha brought thee out thence through a yad chazakah and by an outstretched zero'a; therefore Hashem Eloheicha commanded thee to be shomer Shabbos on Yom HaShabbat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:1–3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:20"> &#91;HEH&#93; Honor thy av and thy em, as Hashem Eloheicha hath commanded thee; that thy yamim may be prolonged, and that it may go well with thee, in ha'adamah which Hashem Eloheicha giveth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:9; MATTITYAHU 5:21–22"> &#91;VAV&#93; Lo tirtzah (thou shalt not murder). 18&#91;ZAYIN&#93; V'lo tinaf (neither shalt thou commit adultery). 19&#91;KHET&#93; V'lo tignov (neither shalt thou steal). 20&#91;TET&#93; V'lo ta'aneh v'reacha ed shav (neither shalt thou bear false witness against thy neighbor).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:14; LUKAS 18:20; VAYIKRA 20:10"> &#91;YOD&#93; V'lo tachmod (neither shalt thou covet, desiring) thy neighborʼs wife, neither shalt thou covet thy neighborʼs bais, his sadeh, or his eved, or his amah, his ox, or his donkey, or any thing that is thy neighborʼs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:28"> These devarim Hashem spoke unto all your Kahal in the mountain out of the midst of the eish, of the anan, and of the thick darkness, with a kol gadol; and He added no more. And He wrote them in two luchot of even (stone), and delivered them unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:16; SHEMOT 23:1; MISHLE 19:9"> And it came to pass, when ye heard the voice out of the midst of the choshech, (for the har did burn with eish,) that ye came near unto me, even all your Rashei Shvatim (Heads of Tribes), and your Zekenim (Elders);</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:9; LUKAS 12:15"> And ye said, Behold, Hashem Eloheinu hath showed us His kavod and His gadol (greatness), and we have heard His voice out of the midst of the eish; we have seen today that Elohim doth talk with man, and he liveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHEMOT 24:12; SHEMOT 31:18; DEVARIM 4:12–15"> Now therefore why should we die? For this great eish will consume us; if we hear the voice of Hashem Eloheinu any more, then we shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:18–19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:18–21"> For who is there of all basar, that hath heard the voice of the Elohim Chayyim speaking out of the midst of the eish, as we have, and lived?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:33; SHEMOT 19:19; SHEMOT 33:20"> Go thou near, and hear all that Hashem Eloheinu shall say; and speak thou unto us all that Hashem Eloheinu shall speak unto thee; and we will hear it, and do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEVARIM 18:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:7–9"> And Hashem heard the voice of your words, when ye spoke unto me; and Hashem said unto me, I have heard the voice of the words of this people, which they have spoken unto thee; they have well said all that they have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:33; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:20; MATTITYAHU 26:63"> O that there were such a lev in them, that they would fear Me, and be shomer mitzvot over all My mitzvot always, that it might be well with them, and with their banim l'olam!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:19"> Go say to them, Get you into your oholim again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEVARIM 18:17; BAMIDBAR 36:5; BAMIDBAR 27:7"> But as for thee, stand thou here by Me, and I will speak unto thee all the mitzvot, and the chukkim, and the mishpatim, which thou shalt teach them, that they may do them in ha'aretz which I give them to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YESHAYAH 48:18; YECHEZKEL 33:31–32; DEVARIM 11:1"> Ye shall be shomer to do therefore as Hashem Eloheicha hath commanded you; ye shall not turn aside to the right or to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Ye shall walk in kol HaDerech which Hashem Eloheicha hath commanded you, that ye may live, and that it may be well with you, and that ye may prolong your yamim in ha'aretz which ye shall possess.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="see Ac 21:21">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
