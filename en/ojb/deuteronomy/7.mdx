<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEVARIM 31:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:19; DEVARIM 4:38"> When Hashem Eloheicha shall bring thee into ha'aretz whither thou goest to possess it, and hath cast out Goyim rabbim before thee, the Chitti, and the Girgashi, and the Emori, and the Kena'ani, and the Perizzi, and the Chivi, and the Yevusi, shivah Goyim greater and mightier than thou;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHOFETIM 2:2; SHEMOT 23:32–33; DEVARIM 20:16–17"> And when Hashem Eloheicha shall deliver them before thee; thou shalt strike them down, and utterly destroy them; thou shalt make no Brit with them, nor show mercy unto them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:2; YEHOSHUA 23:12–13; SHEMOT 34:15–16"> Neither shalt thou make marriages with them; thy bat thou shalt not give unto his ben, nor his bat shalt thou take unto thy ben.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEVARIM 6:15; SHOFETIM 2:11; DEVARIM 4:26"> For they will turn away thy ben from following Me, that they may serve elohim acherim; so will the anger of Hashem be kindled against you, and destroy thee suddenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:24; DEVARIM 12:2–3; SHEMOT 34:13"> But thus shall ye deal with them; ye shall destroy their mizbechot, and break down their matzeivot (pillars), and cut down their Asherah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="sacred trees">a</a></sup>and burn their peselim with eish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEVARIM 14:2; SHEMOT 19:5–6; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9"> For thou art an Am Kadosh unto Hashem Eloheicha; Hashem Eloheicha hath chosen thee to be an Am Segullah (Treasured People) unto Himself, above all people that are upon the face of ha'adamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:11–15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:6"> Hashem did not set His love upon you, nor choose you, because ye were more in number than any people; for ye were the fewest of kol ha'amim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:2; SHEMOT 32:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:4–5"> But because of Ahavat Hashem (because Hashem loved you), and because He would be shomer over haShevu'ah (the Oath) which He had sworn unto Avoteichem, hath Hashem brought you out with a yad chazakah, and redeemed you out of the bais avadim, from the hand of Pharaoh Melech Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:23; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:13; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:3"> Know therefore that Hashem Eloheicha, He is HaElohim, the El HaNe'eman, which is shomer HaBrit and hath chesed with them that love Him and are shomer mitzvot to a thousand generations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YESHAYAH 59:18; NACHUM 1:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:19"> And repayeth them that are the soneh (hater, enemy) of Him to their face, to destroy them; He will not be slack to him that hateth Him, He will repay him to his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:15; DEVARIM 4:1; DEVARIM 5:32"> Thou shalt therefore be shomer mitzvot, and keep the chukkim, and the mishpatim, which I command thee today, to do them.</span>

## &#91;EIKEV&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:1–14; DEVARIM 7:9; VAYIKRA 26:3–13"> Wherefore it shall come to pass, if ye give heed to these mishpatim, and are shomer, and do them, that Hashem Eloheicha shall be shomer over the Brit unto thee and the chesed which He swore unto Avoteicha;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:21; YOCHANAN 15:10; DEVARIM 28:3–5"> And He will love thee, and bless thee, and multiply thee; He will also bless the pri of thy womb, and the pri of thy land, thy grain, and thy wine, and thine oil, the increase of thy cattle, and the flocks of thy sheep, in adamah which He swore unto Avoteicha to give thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:26–33; DEVARIM 28:4; TEHILLIM 127:3"> Baruch shalt thou be more than kol ha'amim; there shall not be male or female barren among you, or among your cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 15:26; TEHILLIM 105:36–37; VAYIKRA 26:3–4"> And Hashem will take away from thee every choli (disease), and will put none of the evil diseases of Mitzrayim, which thou knowest, upon thee; but will lay them upon all them that hate thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:33; SHOFETIM 8:27; DEVARIM 13:8"> And thou shalt consume kol haAm which Hashem Eloheicha shall deliver thee; thine eye shall have no pity upon them; neither shalt thou serve eloheihem (their g-ds); for that will be a snare unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 13:32; BAMIDBAR 33:53; DEVARIM 18:21"> If thou shalt say in thine lev, These Goyim are more than I; how can I dispossess them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="TEHILLIM 105:5; DEVARIM 31:6; DEVARIM 1:29"> Thou shalt not be afraid of them; but shalt well remember what Hashem Eloheicha did unto Pharaoh, and unto all Mitzrayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:34; NECHEMYAH 9:10–11; YIRMEYAH 32:20–21"> The great temptations which thine eyes saw, and the otot, and the mofetim, and the yad chazakah, and the stretched out zero'a, whereby Hashem Eloheicha brought thee out; so shall Hashem Eloheicha do unto kol ha'amim of whom thou art afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 24:12; SHEMOT 23:28–30"> Moreover Hashem Eloheicha will send the hornet swarm among them, until they that are left, and hide themselves from thee, be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 1:5; DEVARIM 10:17; NECHEMYAH 9:32"> Thou shalt not be afraid of them; for Hashem Eloheicha is among you, an El Gadol v'Norah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:29–30; YEHOSHUA 15:63"> And Hashem Eloheicha will put out those Goyim before thee me'at me'at (little by little); thou mayest not consume them at once, lest the beasts of the sadeh increase upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEVARIM 8:20; DEVARIM 2:15; YIRMEYAH 17:18"> But Hashem Eloheicha shall deliver them unto thee, and shall destroy them with a mighty destruction, until they be shmad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 10:8; YEHOSHUA 23:9; YEHOSHUA 1:5"> And He shall deliver their melachim into thine hand, and thou shalt destroy their shem from under Shomayim; there shall no man be able to stand before thee, until thou have destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHEMOT 32:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 14:12; DEVARIM 17:1"> The peselim of eloheihem shall ye burn with eish; thou shalt not desire the kesef or zahav that is on them, nor take it unto thee, lest thou be snared therein; for it is a to'evah (abomination) to Hashem Eloheicha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEVARIM 13:17; HOSHEA 14:8; YECHEZKEL 11:18"> Neither shalt thou bring a to'evah (abomination) into thine bais, lest thou be a cherem like it; but thou shalt utterly detest it, and thou shalt utterly abhor it; for it is a cherem.</span>
