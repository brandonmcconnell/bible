<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 11:24; BAMIDBAR 34:6; SHEMOT 23:31"> And all Naphtali, and Eretz Ephrayim, and Menasheh, and all Eretz Yehudah, unto the Western<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Mediterranean">a</a></sup>Sea,</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:52; BERESHIS 12:7; BERESHIS 28:13"> And Hashem said unto him, This is HaAretz which I swore unto Avraham, unto Yitzchak, and unto Ya'akov, saying, I will give it unto thy zera; I have caused thee to see it with thine eyes, but thou shalt not go over thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:50; YEHOSHUA 1:1–2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:3–6"> So Moshe Eved Hashem died there in Eretz Moav, as Hashem had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9; DEVARIM 3:29"> And He buried him in a valley in Eretz Moav, over against Beit-Pe'or; but no man knoweth of his kever unto this yom.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Isa 53:9on Moshiachʼs kever.">b</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 50:3; BAMIDBAR 20:29; BERESHIS 50:10"> And the Bnei Yisroel wept for Moshe in the plains of Moav shloshim yom; then the yamim of weeping and mourning for Moshe were ended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YESHAYAH 11:2; DANIEL 6:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:6"> And Yehoshua ben Nun was full of the ruach chochmah; for Moshe had laid his hands upon him; and the Bnei Yisroel were heedful unto him, and did as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 33:11; BAMIDBAR 12:6–8; DEVARIM 18:15–18"> And there arose not a navi since in Yisroel like unto Moshe, whom Hashem knew panim el panim (face to face),</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:19; DEVARIM 4:34; TEHILLIM 105:26–38"> In all the otot and the mofetim, which Hashem sent him to do in Eretz Mitzrayim to Pharaoh, and to all his avadim, and to all his land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And in all that yad hachazakah, and in all the morah hagadol (great terror) which Moshe did in the sight of kol Yisroel.</span>
