<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:1; DEVARIM 5:32–3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:1–2"> Kol hamitzvot which I command thee today shall ye be shomer to do, lema'an (in order that) ye may live, and multiply, and go in and possess ha'aretz which Hashem swore unto Avoteichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:3; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7; DEVARIM 8:16"> And thou shalt remember kol haderech which Hashem Eloheicha led thee these arba'im shanah in the midbar, to humble thee, and to prove thee, to know what was in thine lev, whether thou wouldest be shomer over His mitzvot, or no.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:4; LUKAS 4:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:5–6"> And He humbled thee, and suffered thee to hunger, and fed thee with manna, which thou knewest not, neither did Avoteicha know; that He might make thee know that man doth not live by lechem only, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of Hashem doth man live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 9:21; DEVARIM 29:5; MATTITYAHU 26:25–30"> Thy raiment did not increase old upon thee, neither did thy foot swell, these arba'im shanah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:19; MISHLE 3:12; SHMUEL BAIS 7:14"> Thou shalt also consider in thine lev, that, as a man chasteneth bno, so Hashem Eloheicha chasteneth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 6:31; DEVARIM 5:33; SHMUEL ALEF 12:24"> Therefore thou shalt be shomer over the mitzvot of Hashem Eloheicha, to walk in His ways, and to fear Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 11:9–12; SHEMOT 3:8; YECHEZKEL 20:6"> For Hashem Eloheicha bringeth thee into an eretz tovah, a land of brooks of mayim, of fountains and depths that spring out of valleys and hills;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:13; TEHILLIM 147:14; DEVARIM 32:14"> An eretz chittah, and se'orah (barley), and gefen, and te'enah, and pomegranates; an eretz zayit shemen, and devash;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:14; DEVARIM 33:25; YEHOSHUA 22:8"> An eretz wherein thou shalt eat lechem without scarceness, thou shalt not lack anything in it; an eretz whose stones are barzel, and out of whose hills thou mayest dig nechoshet (bronze).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 103:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:6; MISHLE 3:9"> When thou hast eaten and art full, then thou shalt bless Hashem Eloheicha for ha'aretz hatovah which He hath given thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TEHILLIM 106:21; HOSHEA 2:8–9; MISHLE 1:32"> Beware that thou forget not Hashem Eloheicha, in not being shomer over His mitzvot, and His mishpatim, and His chukkot, which I command thee today;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MISHLE 30:9; DEVARIM 28:47; DEVARIM 32:15"> Lest when thou hast eaten and art full, and hast built goodly batim (houses) and dwelt therein;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="IYOV 1:3; BERESHIS 13:1–5; LUKAS 12:13–21"> And when thy herds and thy flocks multiply, and thy kesef and thy zahav is multiplied, and all that thou hast is multiplied;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 106:21; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:7–8; DEVARIM 8:11"> Then thine lev be lifted up, and thou forget Hashem Eloheicha which brought thee forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim, from the bais avadim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:6; BAMIDBAR 20:11; YIRMEYAH 2:6"> Who led thee through that great and terrible midbar, wherein were fiery nachash, and akrav (scorpions), and parched earth, where there was no mayim; Who brought thee forth mayim out of the rock of flint;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEVARIM 8:3; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; SHEMOT 16:15"> Who fed thee in the midbar with manna, which Avoteicha knew not, that He might humble thee, and that He might prove thee, to do thee good at thy acharit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEVARIM 9:4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:7; DANIEL 4:30"> And thou say in thine lev, My ko'ach (power) and the might of mine hand hath gotten me this chayil (wealth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MISHLE 10:22; HOSHEA 2:8; DEVARIM 7:8"> But thou shalt remember Hashem Eloheicha; for it is He that giveth thee power to get wealth, that He may establish His Brit which He swore unto Avoteicha, as it is yom hazeh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:26; SHMUEL ALEF 12:25; TZEFANYAH 1:18"> And it shall be, if thou do at all forget Hashem Eloheicha, and walk after acharei elohim, and serve them, and worship them, I testify against you today that ye shall surely perish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DANIEL 9:11–12; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:16–17"> As the Goyim which Hashem destroyeth before your face, so shall ye perish; because ye would not be obedient unto the voice of Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
</div>
