<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:33–35; DEVARIM 1:4; DEVARIM 29:7"> Then we turned, and went up the way to Bashan; and Og Melech HaBashan came out against us, he and all his people, to engage in milchamah at Edre'i.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:17; BAMIDBAR 21:23–25"> And Hashem said unto me, Fear him not; for I will deliver him, and all his people, and his land, into thy hand; and thou shalt do unto him as thou didst unto Sichon Melech HaEmori which dwelt at Cheshbon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:35; YEHOSHUA 13:12; YEHOSHUA 13:30"> So Hashem Eloheinu delivered into our hands also Og Melech HaBashan, and all his people; and we struck him until no remnant was left to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 4:13; YEHOSHUA 12:4; YEHOSHUA 13:30–31"> And we took all his towns at that time; there was not a kiryah (town, city) which we took not from them, threescore towns, all the region of Argov, the kingdom of Og in Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:28; BAMIDBAR 13:28; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:30"> All these towns were walled with high walls, gates, and bars; beside unwalled towns harbeh me'od (very many).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:34; TEHILLIM 135:10–12; DEVARIM 2:24"> And in cherem we utterly destroyed them, as we did unto Sichon Melech Cheshbon, in cherem utterly destroying the men, nashim, and little ones, of every town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:35; YEHOSHUA 8:27; YEHOSHUA 11:11–14"> But all the behemah (livestock), and the plunder of the towns, we took for booty to ourselves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 13:9–12; YEHOSHUA 12:2–6; BAMIDBAR 32:33–42"> And we took at that time out of the hand of the two melachim of the Emori the land that was on this side of Yarden,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., east of the">a</a></sup>from the Wadi Arnon unto Mt Chermon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:23; TEHILLIM 29:6; YECHEZKEL 27:5"> (Which Chermon the Tzidonim call Siryon; and the Emori call it Senir;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 13:31; YEHOSHUA 13:11–12; DEVARIM 4:49"> All the towns of the plain, and all Gil'ad, and all Bashan, unto Salkhah and Edre'i, towns of the kingdom of Og in Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 14:5; YIRMEYAH 49:2; SHMUEL BAIS 12:26"> For only Og Melech HaBashan remained of the remnant of Repha'im; hinei, his bedstead was a bedstead of barzel. Is it not in Rabbah of the Bnei Ammon? Nine regular cubits was the length thereof, and four cubits the breadth of it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:36; BAMIDBAR 32:32–38; YEHOSHUA 13:8–28"> And ha'aretz hazot, which we possessed at that time, from Aro'er, which is by the Wadi Arnon, and half way up the hill country of Gil'ad, and the towns thereof, gave I unto the Reuveni and Gadi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:39–42; YEHOSHUA 13:29–32; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:23–26"> And the rest of Gil'ad, and all Bashan, being the kingdom of Og, gave I unto the half tribe of Menasheh; all the region of Argov, with all Bashan, which was called Eretz Repha'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:41; YEHOSHUA 13:13; SHMUEL BAIS 3:3"> Yair ben Menasheh took all the region of Argov unto the border of Geshuri and Ma'akhati, and called them after shmo (his own name), HaBashan Havot Yair, unto hayom hazeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:39–40; YEHOSHUA 17:1–3; YEHOSHUA 22:7"> And I gave Gil'ad unto Machir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:24; DEVARIM 2:37; SHMUEL BAIS 24:5"> And unto the Reuveni and unto the Gadi I gave from Gil'ad even unto the Wadi Arnon with the middle of the valley as the border even unto the Wadi Yabbok, which is the border of the Bnei Ammon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 12:3; BERESHIS 14:3; YEHOSHUA 3:16"> The Aravah also, and Yarden, and the coast thereof, from Kinneret even unto the yam of the Aravah, even the Yam HaMelach (the Salt Sea, the Dead Sea), under the slopes of Pisgah to the east.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:20–24; YEHOSHUA 4:12–13; YEHOSHUA 1:12–15"> And I commanded you at that time, saying, Hashem Eloheichem hath given you this land to possess it; ye shall pass over armed before your brethren the Bnei Yisroel, all bnei chayil (valiant men, men fit for military service).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But your nashim, and your little ones, and your livestock (for I know that ye have much livestock,) shall abide in your towns which I have given you;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 22:4; YEHOSHUA 22:8"> Until Hashem have given rest unto your brethren, as well as unto you, and until they also possess ha'aretz which Hashem Eloheichem hath given them beyond Yarden; and then shall ye return every man unto his yerushah (possession, inheritance), which I have given you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 27:18–23; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:10; TEHILLIM 9:10"> And I commanded Yehoshua at that time, saying, Thine eyes have seen all that Hashem Eloheichem hath done unto these two melachim; so shall Hashem do unto all the kingdoms whither thou passest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHEMOT 14:14; DEVARIM 20:4; DEVARIM 1:30"> Ye shall not fear them; for Hashem Eloheichem He shall fight for you.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:8–9"> And I besought Hashem at that time, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHEMOT 15:11; DEVARIM 11:2; TEHILLIM 71:19"> O Adonoi Hashem, Thou hast begun to show Thy eved Thy greatness, and Thy mighty hand; for what El (G-d) is there in Shomayim or on Ha'Aretz, that can do according to Thy works, and according to Thy might?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:21–22; YECHEZKEL 20:6; SHEMOT 3:8"> Ah, let me go over, and see ha'aretz hatovah that is beyond Yarden, that fine hill country, and the Levanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:37; DEVARIM 31:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:7–9"> But Hashem was angry with me for your sakes, and would not hear me; and Hashem said unto me, Enough from thee; speak no more unto Me of this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 27:12; DEVARIM 34:1–4; BERESHIS 13:14–15"> Get thee up into the top of Pisgah, and lift up thine eyes westward, and northward, and southward, and eastward, and see it with thine eyes; for thou shalt not go over this Yarden.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEVARIM 31:3; DEVARIM 31:7; DEVARIM 1:38"> But commission Yehoshua, and encourage him, and strengthen him: for he shall go over before this people, and he shall cause them to inherit ha'aretz which thou shalt see.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEVARIM 34:6; DEVARIM 4:46; BAMIDBAR 25:3"> So we abode in the valley over against Beit-Pe'or.</span>
