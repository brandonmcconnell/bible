<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="VAYIKRA 21:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:26"> Ye are the bnei Hashem Eloheichem; ye shall not cut yourselves, nor make any baldness between your eyes for the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:6; VAYIKRA 20:26; SHEMOT 19:5–6"> For thou art an Am Kadosh unto Hashem Eloheicha, and Hashem hath chosen thee to be an Am Segullah (treasured people) unto Himself, above kol haamim that are upon ha'adamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 4:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:14; VAYIKRA 20:25"> Thou shalt not eat any to'evah (detestable thing).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="VAYIKRA 11:2–45; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:14; MELACHIM ALEF 4:23"> These are the beasts which ye shall eat: the shor, the seh, and the izzim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The deer, and the gazelle, and the yachmur, and the akko, and the antelope, and the wild ox, and the wild goat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MISHLE 18:1; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:17; TEHILLIM 1:1–2"> And every beast that parteth the hoof, and is separated in two hooves, and cheweth the cud among the beasts, that ye shall eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:22–23; LETTER TO TITOS 1:16; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:5"> Nevertheless, these ye shall not eat of them that only chew the cud, or of them that only divide the cloven hoof; as the camel, and the hare, and the coney; for they chew the cud, but divide not the hoof; therefore they are tameh (unclean) unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="VAYIKRA 11:26–27; YESHAYAH 66:3; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:22"> And the chazir, because it divideth the hoof, yet cheweth not the cud, it is tameh (unclean) unto you; ye shall not eat of their basar, nor touch their nevelah (dead carcass).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="VAYIKRA 11:9–12"> These ye shall eat of all that are in the waters: all that have fins and scales shall ye eat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And whatsoever hath not fins and scales ye may not eat; it is tameh (unclean) unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Of all tahorah (clean) birds ye shall eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="VAYIKRA 11:13–19"> But these are they of which ye shall not eat: the nesher, and the vulture, and the bearded vulture,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the kite, and the buzzard after its kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And every raven after its kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="IYOV 30:29"> And the owl, and the night hawk, and the seagull, and the hawk after its kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The little owl, and the great owl, and the horned owl,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the pelican, and the osprey, and the cormorant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the stork, and the heron after its kind, and the hoopoe, and the bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:19; VAYIKRA 11:20–23"> And every creeping thing that flieth is tameh (unclean) unto you; they shall not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But of all tahor (clean) fowls ye may eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:19; SHEMOT 34:26; VAYIKRA 22:8"> Ye shall not eat of anything that dieth of itself; thou shalt give it unto the ger that is in thy she'arim, that he may eat it; or thou mayest sell it unto an alien; for thou art an Am Kadosh unto Hashem Eloheicha. Thou shalt not boil a young goat in its motherʼs cholov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 10:37; DEVARIM 12:17; DEVARIM 26:12–15"> Thou shalt truly give ma'aser (tithe) of all the increase of thy zera, that the sadeh bringeth forth year by year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:5–7; DEVARIM 4:10; DEVARIM 12:17–18"> And thou shalt eat before Hashem Eloheicha, in the place which He shall choose to place Shmo there, the ma'aser (tithe) of thy dagan (grain), of thy tirosh (new wine), and of thine yitzhar (fresh oil), and the bekhorot (firstlings) of thy herds and of thy flocks; that thou mayest learn to stand in awe of Hashem Eloheicha always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:21; DEVARIM 12:5; DEVARIM 11:24"> And if the journey be too long for thee, so that thou art not able to carry it; or if the place be too far from thee, which Hashem Eloheicha shall choose to set Shmo there, when Hashem Eloheicha hath blessed thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Then shalt thou exchange it for kesef, and bind up the money in thine hand, and shalt go unto the place which Hashem Eloheicha shall choose;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="KOHELET 9:7; DEVARIM 12:7; MATTITYAHU 21:12"> And thou shalt spend that kesef for whatsoever thy nefesh desireth after, for cattle, or for sheep, or for yayin, or for shechar (fermented drink), or for whatsoever thy nefesh desireth; and thou shalt eat there before Hashem Eloheicha, and thou shalt rejoice, thou, and thine household,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 18:20; DEVARIM 14:29; DEVARIM 12:12"> And the Levi that is within thy she'arim; thou shalt not forsake him; for he hath no chelek nor nachalah with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEVARIM 14:22; AMOS 4:4; DEVARIM 26:12–15"> At the end of shalosh shanim thou shalt bring forth all the ma'aser (tithe) of thine increase the same year, and shalt store it up within thy she'arim:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEVARIM 15:10; DEVARIM 14:27; MISHLE 19:17"> And the Levi (because he hath no chelek nor nachalah with thee) and the ger, and the yatom, and the almanah, which are within thy she'arim, shall come, and shall eat and be satisfied; that Hashem Eloheicha may bless thee in all the work of thine hand which thou doest.</span>
