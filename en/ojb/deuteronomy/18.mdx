<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 18:8–9; DEVARIM 10:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:13–14"> The kohanim, who are Levi'im, and all the tribe of Levi, shall have no chelek nor nachalah with Yisroel; they shall eat the offerings of Hashem made by eish, even His nachalah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:5–6; TEHILLIM 84:11; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5"> Therefore shall they have no nachalah among their achim; Hashem is their nachalah, as He hath said unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="VAYIKRA 7:30–34; DEVARIM 12:27"> And this shall be the mishpat hakohanim (the right of the kohanim) from the people, from them that offer the zevach, whether it be shor or seh; and they shall give unto the kohen the shoulder, and the two jowls, and the stomach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 12:44–47; BAMIDBAR 18:12–24; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 31:4–10"> The reshit also of thy grain, of thy wine, and of thine oil, and the reshit of the shearing of thy tzon, shalt thou give him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:8; BAMIDBAR 3:10; SHEMOT 28:1–14"> For Hashem Eloheicha hath chosen him out of all thy tribes, to stand to minister in the Shem of Hashem, he and his banim kol hayamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 35:2–3; DEVARIM 12:5; DEVARIM 16:2"> And if a Levi come from any of thy she'arim (gates, i.e., towns) out of kol Yisroel, where he is living, and come with all the earnestness of his nefesh unto the place which Hashem shall choose;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 31:2–4"> Then he shall minister in the Shem of Hashem Elohav, as all his achim the Levi'im do, which stand there before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 12:44; NECHEMYAH 12:47; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 31:4"> They shall have chelek k'chelek (like portions) to eat, beside that which cometh of the sale of his patrimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:29–31; VAYIKRA 18:26–27; VAYIKRA 18:30"> When thou art come into ha'aretz which Hashem Eloheicha giveth thee, thou shalt not learn to imitate the to'avot (abominations) of those Goyim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:31; SHEMOT 22:18; VAYIKRA 19:26"> There shall not be found among you any one that maketh his ben or his bat to pass through the eish<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., be burned as an idolʼs offering">a</a></sup>or kosem kesamim (a diviner of divination) or a m'onen (soothsayer, astrologer), or a m'nachesh (one who interprets omens), or a mekhashshef (witch).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:31; SHMUEL ALEF 28:11–14"> Or one who casts spells, or one who inquires of a ghost or a familiar spirit, or a doresh el hamesim (a consulter of the dead ones, i.e., a necromancer).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:24; DEVARIM 9:4; VAYIKRA 18:27"> For all that do these things are a to'avat Hashem; and because of these to'evot, Hashem Eloheicha is about to drive them out before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:48; BERESHIS 17:1; BERESHIS 6:9"> Thou shalt be tamim (blameless) before Hashem Eloheicha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:16; DEVARIM 18:10; BERESHIS 20:6"> For these Goyim, which thou shalt dispossess, paid heed unto soothsayers, and unto diviners; but as for thee, Hashem Eloheicha hath not suffered thee so to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:22–23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:37; YOCHANAN 1:45"> Hashem Eloheicha will raise up unto thee a Navi from among thee, of thy achim, kamoni (like me);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Moshe, Ex 32:30">b</a></sup>unto him ye must listen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:19; DEVARIM 5:23–28; DEVARIM 9:10"> According to all that thou desiredst of Hashem Eloheicha in Chorev in the Yom HaKahal, saying, Let me not hear again the voice of Hashem Elohav, neither let me see this eish hagedolah any more, that I die not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEVARIM 5:28"> And Hashem said unto me, They have well spoken that which they have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEVARIM 18:15; YOCHANAN 8:28; YOCHANAN 12:49–50"> I will raise them up a Navi from among their achim, like unto thee, and will put My words in his mouth; and he shall speak unto them all that I shall command him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Yn 10:18">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:22–23; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:26"> And it shall come to pass, that whosoever will not listen unto My words which he shall speak Bishmi (in My Name), I will require it of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEVARIM 13:1–5; YIRMEYAH 27:15; YIRMEYAH 23:13–15"> But the navi, which shall presume to speak a word Bishmi (In My Name), which I have not commanded him to speak, or that shall speak in the shem of elohim acharim, even that navi shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1–3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:24; HISGALUS 2:2"> And if thou say in thine lev, How shall we know the word which Hashem hath not spoken?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEVARIM 18:20; MELACHIM BAIS 20:1; YIRMEYAH 28:1–17"> When a navi speaketh b'Shem Hashem, if the thing follow not, nor come to pass, that is the thing which Hashem hath not spoken, but the navi hath spoken it bezadon (in zadon);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="presumption, arrogance">d</a></sup>thou shalt not be afraid of him.</span>
