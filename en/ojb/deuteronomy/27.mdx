<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:2; VAYIKRA 1:1–17"> Thou shalt build the mizbe'ach of Hashem Eloheicha of avanim shleimot (uncut stones); and thou shalt offer olot thereon unto Hashem Eloheicha;</span>

</div>

</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:14; VAYIKRA 18:17"> Arur be he that lieth with his chotenet (mother-in-law).<br />And kol HaAm shall say, Omein.</span>
