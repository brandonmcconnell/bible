## &#91;DEVARIM&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:1; YEHOSHUA 22:7; BAMIDBAR 33:17–18"> These are the devarim (words) which Moshe spoke unto kol Yisroel on the far side of the Yarden in the midbar, in the Aravah opposite Suf between Paran, and Tophel, and Lavan, and Chatzerot, and Di-Zahav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:8; BAMIDBAR 13:26; DEVARIM 9:23"> (There is eleven daysʼ journey from Chorev unto Kadesh-Barnea by way of Mount Se'ir.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 33:38; DEVARIM 4:1–2; BAMIDBAR 20:1"> And it came to pass in the fortieth year, in the eleventh month, on the first day of the month, that Moshe spoke unto the Bnei Yisroel, according unto all that Hashem had given him in commandment unto them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:21–35; YEHOSHUA 12:2–6; YEHOSHUA 13:10–12"> After he had slain Sichon Melech HaEmori, which dwelt in Cheshbon, and Og Melech HaBashan, which dwelt in Ashtarot at Edre'i;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:8; DEVARIM 32:46; DEVARIM 31:9"> On the far side of the Yarden, in Eretz Moav, began Moshe to expound this torah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:1; BAMIDBAR 10:11–13; SHEMOT 19:1–2"> Hashem Eloheinu spoke unto us in Chorev, saying, Ye have dwelt long enough at this mountain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 11:24; YEHOSHUA 10:40; AMOS 2:9"> Turn you, and take your journey, and go to the Har HaEmori (hill country of the Amorites), and unto all the places nigh thereunto, in the Aravah, in the hills, in the Shefelah, and in the Negev, and by the seacoast, to the Eretz HaKena'ani (Land of the Canaanites), and unto Levanon, unto the great river, the river Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:18; BERESHIS 12:7; BERESHIS 26:3–4"> See, I have set ha'aretz (the land) before you; go in and possess ha'aretz which Hashem swore unto Avoteichem, Avraham, Yitzchak, and Ya'akov, to give unto them and to their zera after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 18:18; BAMIDBAR 11:17; BAMIDBAR 11:11–14"> And I spoke unto you at that time, saying, I am not able to bear the burden of you by myself alone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:22; SHEMOT 32:13; BERESHIS 22:17"> Hashem Eloheichem hath multiplied you, and, see, ye are today as numerous as the kokhavei HaShomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TEHILLIM 115:14; BERESHIS 22:17; BAMIDBAR 6:27"> (Hashem Elohei Avoteichem increase you a thousand times over and bless you, as He hath promised you!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:5; MELACHIM ALEF 3:7–9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:16"> How can I myself alone bear your problems, and your massa and your strife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 18:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:21–23; BAMIDBAR 11:16–17"> Take you anashim chachamim, men of wisdom and understanding, who are known among your tribes, and I will set them over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And ye answered me, and said, The thing which thou hast spoken is tov for us to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:18; SHEMOT 18:25–26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:11"> So I took the leaders of your tribes, anashim chachamim, men of understanding, and made them heads over you, over thousands, and over hundreds, and over fifties, and over tens, shoterim among your tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:24; SHEMOT 22:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:6–10"> And I charged your shofetim at that time, saying, Hear the cases between your brethren, and judge righteously between every man and his brother, and the ger that is with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:19; SHEMOT 18:26; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9"> Ye shall not respect persons in mishpat but ye shall hear the katon as well as the gadol; ye shall not be afraid of the face of man; for the mishpat is Elohimʼs: and the case that is too hard for you, bring it unto me, and I will hear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:32; DEVARIM 4:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:20"> And I commanded you at that time all the things which ye should do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:2; DEVARIM 8:15; YIRMEYAH 2:6"> And when we departed from Chorev, we went through all that great and terrible midbar, which ye saw on the way to the Har HaEmori, just as Hashem Eloheinu commanded us; and we came to Kadesh-Barnea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:7–8"> And I said unto you, Ye are come unto the Har HaEmori which Hashem Eloheinu doth give unto us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 1:9; TEHILLIM 46:1; BAMIDBAR 14:8–9"> See, Hashem Eloheicha hath set ha'aretz before thee; go up and possess it, as Hashem Elohei Avoteicha hath said unto thee; fear not, neither be discouraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 13:1–20"> And ye came near unto me every one of you, and said, Let us send anashim ahead of us, and they shall spy out ha'aretz, and bring us word again by what way we must go up, and into what towns we shall come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 13:3–33"> And the proposal pleased me well; and I took Shneym Asar, (Twelve) of you, one of each shevet (tribe);</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 13:21–27; YEHOSHUA 2:1–2"> And they turned and went up into the hill country, and came unto the Eshcol Valley, and explored it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And they took of the pri ha'aretz in their hands, brought it down unto us, brought us word, and said, It is a tovah ha'aretz which Hashem Eloheinu doth give us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:1–4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:51; TEHILLIM 106:24–25"> Notwithstanding ye would not go up, but rebelled against the commandment of Hashem Eloheichem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEVARIM 9:28; TEHILLIM 106:25; BAMIDBAR 14:3"> And ye murmured in your oholim, and said, Because Hashem hated us, He hath brought us forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim, to deliver us into the hand of HaEmori, to destroy us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 13:28–33; DEVARIM 9:1–2; YEHOSHUA 15:14"> Where shall we go up? Our brethren have discouraged levavenu (our hearts), saying, The people is greater and taller than we; the cities are great and walled up to Shomayim; moreover we have seen the Bnei Anakim there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Then I said unto you, Dread not, neither be afraid of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="SHEMOT 14:14; NECHEMYAH 4:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:31"> Hashem Eloheichem which goeth before you, He shall fight for you, according to all that He did for you in Mitzrayim before your eyes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YESHAYAH 46:3–4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:18; HOSHEA 11:3–4"> And in the midbar, where thou hast seen how that Hashem Eloheicha did bear thee, as a man doth carry his ben, in all the way that ye went, until ye came into this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="TEHILLIM 106:24; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:18–19"> Yet in this thing ye were not ma'aminim (believers) in Hashem Eloheichem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 10:33; TEHILLIM 78:14; NECHEMYAH 9:12"> Who went in the way before you, to search you out a place to pitch your tents in, in eish by lailah, to show you by what way ye should go, and in an anan (cloud) by day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:22–30; TEHILLIM 95:11; YECHEZKEL 20:15"> And Hashem heard the sound of your words, and was angry, and on oath, said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="TEHILLIM 95:11"> Surely there shall not one of these men of this dor harah see ha'aretz hatovah, which I swore to give unto Avoteichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:24; YEHOSHUA 14:6–14; BAMIDBAR 32:12"> Except Kalev ben Yephunneh; he shall see it, and to him will I give ha'aretz that he hath trodden upon, and to his children, because he hath wholly followed Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 20:12; DEVARIM 4:21; DEVARIM 34:4"> Also Hashem was angry with me because of you, saying, Thou also shalt not go in there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DEVARIM 3:28; BAMIDBAR 14:30; DEVARIM 31:7–8"> But Yehoshua ben Nun, the one assisting thee, he shall go in there; encourage him, for he shall cause Yisroel to inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="YESHAYAH 7:15–16; BAMIDBAR 14:3; BAMIDBAR 14:31"> Moreover your little ones, which ye said would be taken captive, and your children, which as yet do not have knowledge between tov and rah, they shall go in there, and unto them will I give it, and they shall possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:25"> But as for you, turn, take your journey into the midbar by the way of the Yam Suf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:39–45; MISHLE 19:3; BAMIDBAR 22:34"> Then ye answered and said unto me, We have sinned against Hashem; we will go up and fight, according to all that Hashem Eloheinu did command us. And when ye had girded on every man his weapons of war, ye made light of going up into the hill country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:41–43; YEHOSHUA 7:8–13; YESHAYAH 59:1–2"> And Hashem said unto me, Say unto them. Go not up, neither fight; for I am not in your midst; lest ye be struck down before your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:44; YESHAYAH 63:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:51"> So I spoke unto you; and ye would not hear, but rebelled against the commandment of Hashem, and went presumptuously up into the hill country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="TEHILLIM 118:12; BAMIDBAR 14:45; BAMIDBAR 21:3"> And HaEmori, which dwelt in that hill country, came out against you, and chased you, as bees do, and destroyed you in Se'ir, even unto Chormah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:17; TEHILLIM 78:34"> And ye returned and wept before Hashem; but Hashem would not listen to your voice, nor give ear unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 20:1; BAMIDBAR 20:22; SHOFETIM 11:16–17"> That is why ye abode in Kadesh as long as ye did.</span>
</div>
