<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEVARIM 3:12–13; BAMIDBAR 32:33"> Therefore be shomer over the divrei HaBrit hazot, and do them, lema'an (in order that) ye may prosper in all that ye do.</span>

## &#91;NITZAVIM&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:3; DEVARIM 4:6; LUKAS 11:28"> Ye stand today all of you before Hashem Eloheichem; your rashei shvatim (heads of tribes), your zekenim, and your shoterim, with kol Ish Yisroel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YOEL 2:16–17; HISGALUS 20:12; DEVARIM 31:12–13"> Your little ones, your nashim, and the ger that is in thy machaneh, from the hewer of thy wood unto the drawer of thy mayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:38; YEHOSHUA 9:21–27; SHEMOT 12:48–49"> That thou shouldest enter into Brit with Hashem Eloheicha, and into His alah (oath, imprecation) which Hashem Eloheicha cuts with thee today:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:12–15; MELACHIM BAIS 11:17; DEVARIM 5:2–3"> In order that He may establish thee today for a people unto Himself, and that He may be unto thee Elohim, as He hath said unto thee, and as He hath sworn unto Avoteicha, to Avraham, to Yitzchak, and to Ya'akov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 6:7; BERESHIS 17:7; DEVARIM 28:9"> Neither with you only do I cut this Brit and this alah (oath, imprecation);</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:31–34; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:7–12"> But with him that standeth here with us today before Hashem Eloheinu, and also with him that is not here with us today;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:39; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:14; YIRMEYAH 50:5"> For ye know how we have dwelt in Eretz Mitzrayim; and how we came through the Goyim which ye passed by;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:19; DEVARIM 2:9; DEVARIM 2:4"> And ye have seen their shikkutzim (abominations), and their gillulim (idols), etz va'even, kesef and zahav, which were among them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:36"> Lest there should be among you ish, or isha, or mishpochah, or shevet, whose lev turneth away today from Hashem Eloheinu to go and serve the elohei hagoyim hahem (the g-ds of these Goyim); lest there should be among you a shoresh that beareth bitter poison and wormwood;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="MJ 12:15">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:15; DEVARIM 11:16–17; DEVARIM 13:1–15"> And it come to pass, when he heareth the divrei haalah (words of this oath, imprecation) that he bless himself in his lev, saying, Shalom be with me, though I walk in the stubbornness of mine lev — thus bringing disaster on both the watered and the thirsty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 7:24; BAMIDBAR 15:30; BAMIDBAR 15:39"> Hashem will not spare him, but then the wrath of Hashem and His kina (jealousy) shall burn against that ish, and kol haalah (all the curses) that are written in this sefer shall fall upon him, and Hashem shall blot out shmo from under Shomayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 74:1; TEHILLIM 79:5; DEVARIM 9:14"> And Hashem shall him separate out for disaster from all the Shivtei Yisroel, according to all the curses of HaBrit that are written in Sefer HaTorah Hazeh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:41; YEHOSHUA 7:1–26; YECHEZKEL 13:9"> So that the dor ha'acharon (later generation) to come of your banim that shall rise up after you, and the ger that shall come from an eretz rechokah (a far land), shall say, when they see the makkot of that land, and the diseases which Hashem hath laid upon it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 19:8"> And that the whole land thereof is gofrit (sulfur), and melach (salt), and serefah (burning), that it is not sown, nor beareth, nor any esev groweth therein, like the overthrow of Sodom, and Amora, Admah, and Tzevoyim, which Hashem overthrew in His anger, and in His wrath:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="TZEFANYAH 2:9; YIRMEYAH 17:6; YESHAYAH 34:9"> Even Kol HaGoyim shall say, Why hath Hashem done thus unto this land? What meaneth the heat of this af hagadol (great anger)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 22:8–9; MELACHIM ALEF 9:8–9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:5"> Then men shall say, Because they have forsaken the Brit Hashem Elohei Avotam, which He cut with them when He brought them forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:9; YESHAYAH 47:6; YIRMEYAH 50:7"> For they went and served elohim acherim, and worshiped them, elohim whom they knew not, and whom He had not allotted unto them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:64; SHOFETIM 2:12–13; SHOFETIM 5:8"> And the Af Hashem was kindled against this land, to bring upon it kol hakelalah that are written in this sefer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DANIEL 9:11–14; DEVARIM 28:15–68; DEVARIM 27:15–26"> And Hashem uprooted them out of their land in anger, and in wrath, and in great indignation, and cast them into another land, as it is yom hazeh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:20; MELACHIM ALEF 14:15; TEHILLIM 52:5"> The nistarot (secret things) belong unto Hashem Eloheinu: but the niglot (those things which are revealed) belong unto us and to baneinu ad olam, that we may do all the divrei haTorah hazot.</span>
