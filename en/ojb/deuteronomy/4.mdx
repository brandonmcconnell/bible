<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:5; YECHEZKEL 20:11; VAYIKRA 18:5"> Now therefore shema, Yisroel, unto the chukkim and unto the mishpatim, which I melammed (teach) you, to do them, in order that ye may live, and go in and possess ha'aretz which Hashem Elohei Avoteichem giveth you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:32; MISHLE 30:6; HISGALUS 22:18–19"> Ye shall not add unto the word which I command you, neither shall ye take anything from it, that ye may be shomer mitzvot of the commandments of Hashem Eloheichem which I command you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 25:1–9; TEHILLIM 106:28–29; YEHOSHUA 22:17"> Your eyes have seen what Hashem did because of Ba'al-Pe'or; for all the men that followed Ba'al-Pe'or, Hashem Eloheicha hath destroyed them from among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:20; YEHOSHUA 23:8; YOCHANAN 6:67–69"> But ye that did have deveykus (cleaving) unto Hashem Eloheichem are alive every one of you today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:1–2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:5"> Behold, I have taught you chukkim and mishpatim, even as Hashem Elohai commanded me, that ye should do so in ha'aretz whither ye go to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="IYOV 28:28; TEHILLIM 111:10; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:15"> Be shomer therefore and do them; for this is your chochmah and your binah in the sight of the peoples, which shall hear all these chukkim, and say, Surely HaGoy HaGadol is a wise and understanding people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 145:18; SHMUEL BAIS 7:23; TEHILLIM 34:18"> For what nation is there so great, who hath elohim so near unto them, as Hashem Eloheinu is in all things for which we call upon Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 147:19–20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:12–14; TEHILLIM 119:127–128"> And what nation is there so great, that hath chukkim and mishpatim tzaddikim as all this torah, which I set before you today?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEVARIM 6:7; DEVARIM 11:19; TEHILLIM 78:3–8"> Only be shomer over thyself, and be shomer over thy nefesh diligently, lest thou forget the things which thine eyes have seen, and lest they depart from thy lev kol yamim of thy life; but teach them to thy banim, and the banim of thy banim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:9; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:18–19; SHEMOT 19:16"> Specially the yom that thou stoodest before Hashem Eloheicha in Chorev, when Hashem said unto me, Gather Me the people together, and I will make them hear My words, that they may learn to fear Me kol hayamim that they shall live upon ha'adamah and that they may teach their children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEVARIM 5:23; SHEMOT 19:16–18; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:18–19"> And ye came near and stood at the foot of the mountain; the mountain burned with eish unto the midst of Shomayim, with choshech, and anan, and thick darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:15; DEVARIM 5:4; DEVARIM 5:22"> And Hashem spoke unto you out of the midst of the eish; ye heard the voice of the devarim, but saw no temunah (form, semblance); only ye heard a voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:28; SHEMOT 31:18; DEVARIM 9:9–11"> And He declared unto you His Brit, which He commanded you to do, even Aseres HaDevarim; and He wrote them upon shnei luchot avanim (two tablets of stone).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 105:44–45; YECHEZKEL 21:1–23"> And Hashem commanded me at that time to teach you chukkim and mishpatim, that ye might do them in ha'aretz whither ye go over to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:12; YESHAYAH 40:18; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:4–6"> Be shomer over yourselves, therefore; for ye saw no manner of temunah on the yom that Hashem spoke unto you in Chorev out of the midst of the eish;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:29; DEVARIM 5:8; SHEMOT 20:4–5"> Lest ye corrupt yourselves, and make you a pesel, the temunah of any semel, the likeness of zachar or nekevah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:23"> The likeness of any behemah that is on ha'aretz, the likeness of any winged fowl that flieth in the air,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The likeness of anything that creepeth on the adamah, the likeness of any dag (fish) that is in the waters below;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:25; DEVARIM 17:3; MELACHIM BAIS 21:3"> And lest thou lift up thine eyes unto Shomayim, and when thou seest the shemesh, and the yarei'ach, and the kokhavim, even all the tz'va HaShomayim, shouldest be driven to hishtachaveh (bow down, worship) them, and serve them which Hashem Eloheicha hath divided unto kol Amim under kol HaShomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:51; YIRMEYAH 11:4; SHEMOT 19:5–6"> But Hashem hath taken you, and brought you forth out of the iron furnace, even out of Mitzrayim, to be unto Him an Am Nachalah (a people of inheritance), as ye are yom hazeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:37; BAMIDBAR 20:12; TEHILLIM 106:32–33"> Furthermore Hashem was angry with me for your sakes, and swore that I should not go over Yarden, and that I should not go in unto that ha'aretz hatovah, which Hashem Eloheicha giveth thee for a nachalah (inheritance);</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEVARIM 3:25; DEVARIM 3:27; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:13–15"> But I must die in this land, I must not go over Yarden; but ye shall go over, and possess that ha'aretz hatovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:9; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:12; YEHOSHUA 23:16"> Be shomer over yourselves, lest ye forget the Brit Hashem Eloheichem, which He cut with you, and make you a pesel (idol, image), or the likeness of anything, which Hashem Eloheicha hath forbidden thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:29; NACHUM 1:6; DEVARIM 9:3"> For Hashem Eloheicha is a consuming eish, even an El Kannah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:16; HOSHEA 9:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:22"> When thou shalt father banim, and bnei banim, and ye shall have remained long in Ha'Aretz, and shall corrupt yourselves, and make a pesel, or the likeness of anything, and shall do evil in the sight of Hashem Eloheicha, to provoke Him to anger;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:1; MICHOH 6:2; YIRMEYAH 22:29"> I call Shomayim and HaAretz to witness against you today, that ye shall soon utterly perish from off Ha'Aretz whereunto ye go over Yarden to possess it; ye shall not prolong your yamim upon it, but shall be utterly shmad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:62–64; VAYIKRA 26:33; YECHEZKEL 12:15"> And Hashem shall scatter you among the amim; ye shall be left few in number among the Goyim, whither Hashem shall lead you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:64; YIRMEYAH 16:13; DEVARIM 28:36"> And there ye shall serve elohim, the work of menʼs hands, etz (wood) and even (stone), which neither see, nor hear, nor eat, nor smell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:2; YIRMEYAH 29:12–14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:4"> But if from thence thou shalt seek Hashem Eloheicha, thou shalt find Him, if thou seek Him with all thy lev and with all thy nefesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YOEL 2:12–13; YIRMEYAH 23:20; HOSHEA 3:5"> When thou art in tzar (tribulation), and all these things are come upon thee, even in the acharit hayamim, if thou turn to Hashem Eloheicha and shalt be obedient unto His voice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEVARIM 31:8; TEHILLIM 116:5; NECHEMYAH 9:31"> (For Hashem Eloheicha is an El Rachum;) He will not abandon thee, neither destroy thee, nor forget the Brit Avoteicha which He swore unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="IYOV 8:8; MATTITYAHU 24:31; TEHILLIM 44:1"> For ask now of the yamim that are past, which were before thee, since the yom that Elohim bara man upon Ha'Aretz, and ask from the one side of Shomayim unto the other, whether there hath been any such thing as this great thing is, or hath been heard like it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="SHOFETIM 6:22; DEVARIM 5:24; SHEMOT 33:20"> Did ever people hear the kol Elohim speaking out of the midst of the eish, as thou hast heard, and live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DEVARIM 26:8; DEVARIM 5:15; SHEMOT 6:6"> Or hath Elohim tried to go and take Him a nation from the midst of another nation, by massot (trials), by otot, by mofetim, by milchamah, by a yad chazakah, by an outstretched zero'a, by great terrors, according to all that Hashem Eloheicha did for you in Mitzrayim before your eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="YESHAYAH 45:18; SHMUEL ALEF 2:2; YESHAYAH 45:5"> Unto thee it was showed, that thou mightest know that Hashem He is HaElohim; there is none else beside Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:9; SHEMOT 19:19; DEVARIM 4:33"> Out of Shomayim He made thee to hear His voice, that He might discipline thee; upon Ha'Aretz He showed thee His eish hagedolah; and thou heardest His devarim out of the midst of the eish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:15; YESHAYAH 63:9; SHEMOT 13:9"> And because He loved Avoteicha, therefore He chose their zera after them, and brought thee out in His sight with His ko'ach hagadol out of Mitzrayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:1; DEVARIM 9:1–5; SHEMOT 23:27–28"> To drive out Goyim from before thee greater and mightier than thou art, to bring thee in, to give thee their land for a nachalah, as it is yom hazeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 2:11; DEVARIM 4:35; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:11"> Know therefore today, and consider it in thine lev, that Hashem He is HaElohim in Shomayim above, and upon Ha'Aretz beneath: there is no other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="DEVARIM 6:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:3; DEVARIM 12:28"> Thou shalt be shomer over His chukkim, therefore, and His mitzvot, which I command thee today, that it may go well with thee, and with thy banim after thee, and that thou mayest prolong thy yamim upon adamah, which Hashem Eloheicha giveth thee, kol hayamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 35:6; BAMIDBAR 35:14–15; YEHOSHUA 20:2–9"> Then Moshe separated and set apart three towns on this side of the Yarden toward the rising of the shemesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:18; DEVARIM 19:1–10; BAMIDBAR 35:15–28"> That the rotze'ach might flee thither, which should kill his neighbor unawares, and hated him not in times past; and that fleeing unto one of these towns he might live:</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 20:8; MELACHIM ALEF 4:13; YEHOSHUA 21:27"> Namely, Betzer in the midbar, in the flatland, of the Reuveni; and Ramot in Gil'ad, of the Gadi; and Golan in Bashan, of the Menashi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="DEVARIM 27:3; VAYIKRA 27:34; MALACHI 4:4"> And this is the torah which Moshe set before the Bnei Yisroel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:3; DEVARIM 4:1; TEHILLIM 119:7"> These are the edot, and the chukkim, and the mishpatim, which Moshe spoke unto the Bnei Yisroel, after they came forth out of Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="DEVARIM 3:29; DEVARIM 4:47; DEVARIM 1:4–5"> On this side of the Yarden, in the valley over against Beit-Pe'or, in Eretz Sichon Melech HaEmori, who dwelt at Cheshbon, whom Moshe and the Bnei Yisroel struck down, after they were come forth out of Mitzrayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="DEVARIM 3:1–14; BAMIDBAR 21:33–35; DEVARIM 29:7–8"> And they possessed his land, and Eretz Og Melech HaBashan, two melachim of HaEmori, which were on this side of the Yarden toward the rising of the shemesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="DEVARIM 3:9; DEVARIM 2:36; DEVARIM 3:12"> From Aro'er, which is by the edge of the Wadi Arnon, even unto Mt Siyon, which is Chermon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="DEVARIM 3:17; YEHOSHUA 13:20; DEVARIM 34:1"> And all the Aravah on this side of the Yarden eastward, even unto the Dead Sea, under the slopes of Pisgah.</span>
