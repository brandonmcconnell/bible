<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:7–8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:31; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:4–5"> Because they met you not with lechem and with mayim in the way, when ye came forth out of Mitzrayim; and because they hired against thee Balaam ben Be'or from Petor in Aram Naharayim, to curse thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZRA 9:12; SHMUEL BAIS 12:31; NECHEMYAH 13:23–25"> Nevertheless Hashem Eloheicha would not give heed unto Balaam; but Hashem Eloheicha turned the kelalah into a brocha unto thee, because Hashem Eloheicha loved thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:19; SHEMOT 22:21; SHEMOT 23:9"> Thou shalt not seek their shalom nor their tovat all thy yamim l'olam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:5–6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:12–13; DEVARIM 23:1–3"> Thou shalt not abhor an Edomi; for he is thy brother: thou shalt not abhor a Mitzri (an Egyptian); because thou wast a ger in his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 6:18; SHOFETIM 20:26; HISGALUS 19:11–14"> The banim that are begotten of them shall enter into the Kahal Hashem in their third generation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="VAYIKRA 15:16; BAMIDBAR 5:2–3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:11–13"> When the machaneh (camp) goeth forth against thine enemies, then be shomer over thee against every wicked thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:26–27; VAYIKRA 14:9"> If there be among you any ish, that is not tahor (clean) by reason of a nocturnal occurrence, then shall he go outside the machaneh, he shall not come within the machaneh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> But it shall be, when erev cometh on, he shall wash himself with mayim; and when the shemesh is down, he shall come into the machaneh again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 24:6–8"> Thou shalt have a place also outside the machaneh, whither thou shalt go forth outside;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:1; VAYIKRA 26:12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:16"> And thou shalt have a shovel in addition to thy weapon; and it shall be, when thou wilt relieve thyself outside, thou shalt dig therewith, and shalt turn back and cover that which cometh from thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 30:15; OVADYAH 1:14"> For Hashem Eloheicha walketh in the midst of thy machaneh, to deliver thee, and to give up thine enemies before thee; therefore shall thy machaneh be kadosh; that He see no ervat davar (shameful thing) in thee, and turn away from thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:21; ZECHARYAH 7:10; YIRMEYAH 7:6"> Thou shalt not give up unto his adon the eved which is escaped from his adon unto thee:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See PM">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 23:7; MELACHIM ALEF 14:24; VAYIKRA 19:29"> He shall dwell with thee, even among you, in that place which he shall choose in one of thy she'arim, where it pleases him best; thou shalt not oppress him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:2; MATTITYAHU 7:6; HISGALUS 22:15"> There shall be no kedeshah (ritual prostitute) of the banot Yisroel, nor a kadesh (sodomite ritual prostitute) of the bnei Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:25; VAYIKRA 25:35–37; TEHILLIM 15:5"> Thou shalt not bring the hire of a zonah, or the price of a kelev (male prostitute), into the Bais Hashem Eloheicha for any neder; for even both of these are to'avat Hashem Eloheicha.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEVARIM 15:10; DEVARIM 15:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:58"> Thou shalt not lend upon neshekh (interest) to thy brother; neshekh of kesef, neshekh of okhel, neshekh of any thing that is lent upon neshekh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="KOHELET 5:4–5; TEHILLIM 66:13–14; TEHILLIM 116:18"> Unto a nokhri thou mayest lend upon neshekh; but unto thy brother thou shalt not lend upon neshekh; that Hashem Eloheicha may bless thee in all that thou settest thine hand to in ha'aretz whither thou goest to possess it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> When thou shalt vow a neder unto Hashem Eloheicha, thou shalt not delay to pay it; for Hashem Eloheicha will surely require it of thee; and it would be chet (sin) in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MISHLE 20:25; SHOFETIM 11:30–31; SHMUEL ALEF 1:11"> But if thou shalt abstain from vowing, it shall be no chet in thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:5"> That which is gone out of thy lips thou shalt be shomer over and perform; even a vow thou hast voluntarily vowed unto Hashem Eloheicha, which thou hast promised with thy mouth.</span>
</div>

</div>
