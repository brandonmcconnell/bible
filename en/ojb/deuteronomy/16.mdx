<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:18; SHEMOT 12:2–20; SHEMOT 13:4"> Be shomer of the month of Aviv,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="springtime, i.e., Nisan">a</a></sup>and perform the Pesach offering unto Hashem Eloheicha; for in the month of Aviv Hashem Eloheicha brought thee forth out of Mitzrayim by lailah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:5; DEVARIM 12:26; LUKAS 22:15"> Thou shalt therefore sacrifice the Pesach unto Hashem Eloheicha, of the tzon and the herd, in the place which Hashem shall choose to place Shmo there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:39; SHEMOT 34:18; SHEMOT 12:19–20"> Thou shalt eat no chametz with it; shivat yamim shalt thou eat matzot therewith, even the lechem oni (bread of affliction); for thou camest forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim in haste in order that thou mayest remember the yom when thou camest forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim kol yemei chayyechah (all the days of thy life).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:25; SHEMOT 12:10; SHEMOT 13:7"> And there shall be no se'or (leaven) seen with thee in all thy territory shivat yamim; neither shall there any thing of the basar, which thou sacrificedst ba'Erev baYom HaRishon, remain overnight until the boker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:2; DEVARIM 12:5–6"> Thou mayest not sacrifice the Pesach within any of thy she'arim, which Hashem Eloheicha giveth thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:5; SHEMOT 12:6–9; BAMIDBAR 9:11"> But at the place which Hashem Eloheicha shall choose to place Shmo in, there thou shalt sacrifice the Pesach at erev, at the going down of the shemesh, at the mo'ed (appointed time) that thou camest forth out of Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:13; SHEMOT 12:8–9; YOCHANAN 2:23"> And thou shalt roast and eat it in the place which Hashem Eloheicha shall choose; and thou shalt turn back in the boker, and go unto thy oholim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 13:6–8; VAYIKRA 23:6–8; VAYIKRA 23:36"> Sheshet yamim thou shalt eat matzot; and on the yom hashevi'i shall be an atzeret (assembly) to Hashem Eloheicha; thou shalt do no melachah (work) therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:22; SHEMOT 23:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:1"> Shivah shavu'ot (seven weeks) shalt thou number unto thee; begin to number the shivah shavu'ot from such time as thou begin to put the sickle to the standing crop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:2; VAYIKRA 12:8; MALACHI 3:10–11"> And thou shalt observe Chag Shavuos unto Hashem Eloheicha by giving a terumah (freewill offering) of thine hand, which thou shalt give unto Hashem Eloheicha, according as Hashem Eloheicha hath blessed thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:7; DEVARIM 12:12; DEVARIM 12:18"> And thou shalt rejoice before Hashem Eloheicha, thou, and thy ben, and thy bat, and thy eved, and thy amah, and the Levi that is within thy she'arim, and the ger, and the yatom (orphan), and the almanah, that are among you, in the place which Hashem Eloheicha hath chosen to place Shmo there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEVARIM 15:15; EKHAH 3:19–20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:17–18"> And thou shalt remember that thou wast an eved in Mitzrayim: and thou shalt be shomer and do these chukkim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:16; YOCHANAN 7:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 5:3"> Chag HaSukkot shalt thou observe shivat yamim, after that thou hast gathered in from thy threshing floor and from thy winepress:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 8:9–12; DEVARIM 16:11; YESHAYAH 30:29"> And thou shalt rejoice in thy chag (feast), thou, and thy ben, and thy bat, and thy eved, and thy amah, and the Levi, the ger, and the yatom (orphan), and the almanah, that are within thy she'arim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:36–42; DEVARIM 30:16; DEVARIM 16:10"> Shivat yamim shalt thou observe the chag (feast) unto Hashem Eloheicha in the place which Hashem shall choose, because Hashem Eloheicha shall bless thee in all thine increase, and in all the works of thine hands, so that thou shalt surely rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MISHLE 3:9–10; TEHILLIM 96:8; CHAGGAI 1:9"> Shalosh pe'amim bashanah shall all thy males appear before Hashem Eloheicha in the place which He shall choose; in the Chag Matzot, and in the Chag HaShavu'os, and in the Chag HaSukkot, and they shall not appear before Hashem empty-handed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:6–7; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:12; DEVARIM 16:10"> Every man shall give as he is able, according to the Birkat Hashem Eloheicha which He hath given thee.</span>

## &#91;SHOFTIM&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:4; SHEMOT 18:25–26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:1–6"> Shofetim and shoterim shalt thou appoint thee in all thy she'arim, which Hashem Eloheicha giveth thee, throughout thy shevatim: and they shall judge HaAm with mishpat-tzedek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:15; SHEMOT 23:2; MISHLE 17:23"> Thou shalt not pervert mishpat; thou shalt not show partiality, neither take a shochad: for a bribe doth blind the eyes of the chachamim, and twist the devarim of the tzaddikim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:8; YECHEZKEL 18:9; YECHEZKEL 18:5"> Tzedek tzedek (that which is altogether just) shalt thou follow, that thou mayest live, and inherit ha'aretz which Hashem Eloheicha giveth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:3; MELACHIM BAIS 17:16; SHEMOT 34:13"> Thou shalt not plant any tree as an Asherah near unto the Mizbe'ach of Hashem Eloheicha, which thou shalt make thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:1; SHEMOT 20:4; ZECHARYAH 8:17"> Neither shalt thou set thee up any matzeivah (idolatrous pillar); which Hashem Eloheicha hateth.</span>
