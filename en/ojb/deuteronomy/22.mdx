<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:4–5; YECHEZKEL 34:16; YESHAYAH 58:7"> Thou shalt not see thy brotherʼs shor or his seh go astray, and ignore them; thou shalt in any case bring them back unto thy brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:6"> And if thy brother be not near unto thee, or if thou know him not, then thou shalt bring it unto thine own bais, and it shall be with thee until thy brother seek after it, and thou shalt restore it to him again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> In like manner shalt thou do with his chamor; and so shalt thou do with his raiment; and with all lost things of thy brotherʼs, which he hath lost, and thou hast found, shalt thou do likewise; thou mayest not ignore it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:1–2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:12–13; MATTITYAHU 5:44"> Thou shalt not see thy brotherʼs chamor or his shor fall down by the road, and ignore them; thou shalt surely help him to lift them up again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:4–15; DEVARIM 18:12"> The isha shall not wear that which pertaineth unto a gever (man) neither shall a gever put on simlat isha (garment of a woman); for all that do so are to'avat unto Hashem Eloheicha.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="VAYIKRA 22:28; MISHLE 12:10; BERESHIS 8:17"> If a birdʼs ken (nest) chance to be before thee in the way in any tree, or on the ground, whether they be young ones, or eggs, and the mother is roosting upon the young, or upon the eggs, thou shalt not take the mother with the young;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:40; MISHLE 22:4"> But thou shalt in any wise let the mother go, and take the young to thee; that it may be well with thee, and that thou mayest prolong thy yamim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 11:2; YESHAYAH 22:1; SHEMOT 21:28–36"> When thou buildest a bais chadash, then thou shalt make a parapet for thy roof, that thou bring not dahm (blood guiltiness) upon thine bais, if any fall from thence.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:19; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:3; MATTITYAHU 9:16"> Thou shalt not sow thy kerem with different seeds; lest the fruit of thy zera which thou hast sown, and the fruit of thy kerem, be defiled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:14–16"> Thou shalt not plow with a shor and a chamor together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:19"> Thou shalt not wear a garment of Shaanetz, as of woolen and linen together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:5; BAMIDBAR 15:37–41"> Thou shalt make thee gedilim (fringes, tzitzis, twisted threads) upon the four corners of thy garment, wherewith thou coverest thyself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 29:21; BERESHIS 29:31; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:28–29"> If any ish take an isha, and go in unto her, and hate her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEVARIM 22:19; MISHLE 18:21; MISHLE 18:8"> And give occasions of speech against her, and bring up a shem rah (bad name) upon her, and say, I took this isha, and when I came to her, I found her with no betulim (proofs of virginity);</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then shall the av of the na'arah, and her em, take and bring forth the betulim (proofs of virginity) unto the zekenim of the town in the sha'arah (gate);</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the avi hana'arah shall say unto the zekenim, I gave my bat unto this ish as isha (wife) and he hateth her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And, hinei, he hath given occasions of speech against her, saying, I found not thy bat with betulim; and yet these are my batʼs betulim. And they shall spread the simlah (clothing) before the zekenim of the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 18:21"> And the zekenim of that town shall take that ish and punish him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 22:29; MATTITYAHU 19:8–9; DEVARIM 24:1–4"> And they shall fine him a hundred shekels of kesef, and give them unto the avi hana'arah, because he hath brought a shem rah (bad name) upon a betulat Yisroel; and she shall be his isha; he may not put her away all his yamim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But if this thing be emes, and the betulim be not found for the na'arah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEVARIM 13:5; BERESHIS 34:7; SHOFETIM 20:6"> Then they shall bring out the na'arah to the door of her bais avi, and the anashim of her town shall stone her with avanim that she die; because she hath committed an outrage in Yisroel, to play the whore in her bais avi; so shalt thou put away the rah from among you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:10; YECHEZKEL 23:45–47; YOCHANAN 8:4–5"> If an ish be found lying with an isha married to a husband then they shall both of them die, both the ish that lay with the isha, and the isha; so shalt thou put away the rah from Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 1:18–19; DEVARIM 20:7"> If a na'arah that is a betulah be betrothed unto a husband, and an ish find her in the town, and lie with her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEVARIM 22:21–22; DEVARIM 21:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:13"> Then ye shall bring them both out unto the sha'ar of that town, and ye shall stone them with avanim that they die; the na'arah, because she cried not, being in the town; and the ish, because he hath humbled his neighborʼs isha; so thou shalt put away the rah from among you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 13:14"> But if the ish find a na'arah hame'orasah (betrothed woman) in the sadeh, and the ish overpower her, and lie with her; then the ish only that lay with her shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEVARIM 21:22"> But unto the na'arah thou shalt do nothing; there is in the na'arah no chet mavet (sin worthy of death); for as when an ish riseth against his neighbor, and slayeth him, even so is this matter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:7"> For he found her in the sadeh, and the na'arah hame'orasah cried, and there was no moshia (savior) for her.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See 2C 11:2-3regarding Moshiach and his Kehillah Kallah and Hasatanʼs evil interference with the betrothal and the above three Scriptures T.N.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:16–17"> If an ish find a na'arah that is a betulah, which is not orasah (betrothed), and lay hold on her, and lie with her, and they be found;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEVARIM 22:19; DEVARIM 22:24; DEVARIM 21:14"> Then the ish that lay with her shall give unto the avi hana'arah fifty &#91;shekels&#93; of kesef, and she shall be his isha; because he hath humbled her, he may not put her away all his yamim.</span>
</div>
