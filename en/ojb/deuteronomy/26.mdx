## &#91;KI TAVO&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEVARIM 17:14; DEVARIM 13:9; DEVARIM 6:1–10"> And it shall be, when thou art come in unto ha'aretz which Hashem Eloheicha giveth thee for a nachalah, and possessest it, and dwellest therein,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:19; SHEMOT 23:16; YECHEZKEL 44:30"> That thou shalt take of the reshit kol pri ha'adamah which thou shalt bring of thy land that Hashem Eloheicha giveth thee, and shalt put it in a basket, and shalt go unto the place which Hashem Eloheicha shall choose to place Shmo there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TEHILLIM 105:9–10; DEVARIM 19:17; BERESHIS 26:3"> And thou shalt go unto the kohen that shall be in those yamim, and say unto him, I declare today unto Hashem Eloheicha, that I am come unto the country which Hashem swore unto Avoteinu to give us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:19; MATTITYAHU 5:23–24; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:10–12"> And the kohen shall take the basket out of thine hand, and set it down before the mizbe'ach of Hashem Eloheicha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:22; BERESHIS 46:27; BERESHIS 31:20"> And thou shalt speak and say before Hashem Eloheicha, An Aramean ready to perish was Avi, and he went down into Mitzrayim, and sojourned there with a few, and became there a nation, great, mighty, and populous;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 1:11; SHEMOT 1:14; SHEMOT 5:23"> And the Egyptians mistreated us, and afflicted us, and laid upon us avodah kashah (hard labor);</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 2:23–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:20–21; SHEMOT 3:9"> And when we cried unto Hashem Elohei Avoteinu, Hashem heard our voice, and looked on our affliction, and our labor, and our oppression;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:34; DEVARIM 5:15; TEHILLIM 78:12–13"> And Hashem brought us forth out of Mitzrayim with a yad chazakah, and with an outstretched zero'a, and with great awesomeness, and with otot, and with mofetim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:8; TEHILLIM 105:44; TEHILLIM 107:7–8"> And He hath brought us into this place, and hath given us this land, even an eretz zavat cholov udevash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEVARIM 26:2; DEVARIM 18:4; HISGALUS 22:9"> And now, hinei, I have brought the reshit pri ha'adamah (firstfruits of the fruit of the land), which Thou, Hashem, hast given me. And thou shalt set it before Hashem Eloheicha, and worship before Hashem Eloheicha;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:11; DEVARIM 12:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:4"> And thou shalt rejoice in kol hatov which Hashem Eloheicha hath given unto thee, and unto thine bais, thou, and the Levi, and the ger that is among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="VAYIKRA 27:30; BAMIDBAR 18:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:18–19"> When thou hast made an end of tithing kol ma'aser (all the tithe) of thine tevu'at bashanah hashelishit (increase in the third year), which is the Shnat HaMa'aser (Year of Tithing), and hast given it unto the Levi, the ger, the yatom (orphan), and the almanah, that they may eat within thy she'arim, and be filled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:176; TEHILLIM 119:153; TEHILLIM 119:141"> Then thou shalt say before Hashem Eloheicha, I have brought away HaKodesh (the Holy) out of mine bais, and also have given them unto the Levi, and unto the ger, to the yatom (orphan), and to the almanah, according to all Thy mitzvot which Thou hast commanded me; I have not transgressed Thy mitzvot, neither have I forgotten them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="VAYIKRA 7:20; VAYIKRA 21:11; HOSHEA 9:4"> I have not eaten thereof in my mourning, neither have I taken away whatever thereof while I was tameh, nor given thereof for the dead; but I have given heed to the voice of Hashem Elohai, and have done according to all that Thou hast commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YESHAYAH 63:15; ZECHARYAH 2:13; YIRMEYAH 31:23"> Look down from Thy me'on kadosh (holy dwelling place), from Shomayim, and bless Thy people Yisroel, and ha'adamah which Thou hast given us, as Thou didst swear unto Avoteinu, Eretz zavat cholov udevash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:1; YOCHANAN 14:15; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:2–3"> This day Hashem Eloheicha hath commanded thee to do these chukkim and mishpatim; thou shalt therefore be shomer over and do them with kol levavecha, and with kol nafshecha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHEMOT 24:7; YESHAYAH 12:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:19–20"> Thou hast distinguished Hashem this day to be thy Elohim, and to walk in His ways, and to be shomer over His chukkim, and His mitzvot, and His mishpatim, and to give heed unto His voice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:6; DEVARIM 14:2; DEVARIM 28:9"> And Hashem hath distinguished thee today to be His Am Segullah (treasured people), as He hath promised thee, and that thou shouldest be shomer over all His mitzvot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9; DEVARIM 7:6; DEVARIM 28:1"> And to set thee high above kol HaGoyim which He hath made, in tehillah (praise), and in shem, and in tiferet; and that thou mayest be an Am Kadosh unto Hashem Eloheicha, as He hath spoken.</span>
</div>
