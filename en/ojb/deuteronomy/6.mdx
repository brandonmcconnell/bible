<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:1; DEVARIM 12:1; DEVARIM 4:45"> Now these are the mitzvot, the chukkim, and the mishpatim, which Hashem Eloheichem commanded to teach you, that ye might do them in ha'aretz whither ye go to possess it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:40; DEVARIM 13:4; SHEMOT 20:20"> That thou mightest fear Hashem Eloheicha, to be shomer over all His chukkim and His mitzvot, which I command thee, thou, and thy ben, and thy benʼs ben, all the days of thy life; and that thy days may be prolonged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 22:17; SHEMOT 3:8; BERESHIS 26:4"> Shema therefore, Yisroel, and be shomer to do it; that it may be well with thee, and that ye may increase mightily, as Hashem Elohei Avoteicha hath promised thee, in Eretz Zavat Cholov uDevash.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MARKOS 12:29–32; YESHAYAH 44:6; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:5"> Shema Yisroel Adonoi (Hashem) Eloheinu Adonoi (Hashem) Echad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MARKOS 12:30; MATTITYAHU 22:37; LUKAS 10:27"> And thou shalt love Hashem Eloheicha b'chol levavcha uvechol nafshecha uvechol modecha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:16; MISHLE 7:3; YIRMEYAH 31:33"> And these devarim, which I command thee today, shall be in thine lev;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 11:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:4; TEHILLIM 78:4–6"> And thou shalt teach them diligently unto thy banim, and shalt talk of them when thou sittest in thine bais, and when thou walkest by the way, and when thou liest down, and when thou risest up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MISHLE 6:21; MISHLE 3:3; DEVARIM 11:18"> And thou shalt bind them for an ot upon thine hand, and they shall be as totafos (ornaments)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ex 13:16, Shabbos 57a">a</a></sup>between thine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEVARIM 11:20; YESHAYAH 30:8; YESHAYAH 57:8"> And thou shalt write them upon the mezuzot of thy bais, and on thy she'arim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 24:13; TEHILLIM 105:44; NECHEMYAH 9:25"> And it shall be, when Hashem Eloheicha shall have brought thee into ha'aretz He swore unto Avoteicha, to Avraham, to Yitzchak, and to Ya'akov, to give thee great and goodly cities, which thou buildedst not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:12–18; DEVARIM 8:10–20; YECHEZKEL 16:10–20"> And batim full of all good things, which thou filledst not, and wells digged, which thou diggedst not, kramim (vineyards) and olive trees, which thou plantedst not; when thou shalt have eaten and be full;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then beware lest thou forget Hashem, which brought thee forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim, from the bais avadim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 4:8; DEVARIM 10:20; MATTITYAHU 4:10"> Thou shalt fear Hashem Eloheicha, and serve Him, and shalt swear by Shmo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 25:6; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:21; DEVARIM 13:7"> Ye shall not go after elohim acherim, of elohei ha'amim (the g-ds of the peoples) which are round about you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:24; SHEMOT 20:5; DEVARIM 7:4"> (For Hashem Eloheicha is an El Kannah among you) lest the anger of Hashem Eloheicha be kindled against thee, and destroy thee from off the face of ha'adamah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 17:7; LUKAS 4:12; MATTITYAHU 4:7"> Ye shall not test Hashem Eloheichem, as ye tested Him in Massah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEVARIM 11:22; TEHILLIM 119:4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:58"> Ye diligently shall be shomer mitzvot over the mitzvot Hashem Eloheichem and His edut, and His chukkim, which He hath commanded thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:40; DEVARIM 12:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:2"> And thou shalt do that which is yashar (right, straight) and hatov in the eyes of Hashem; that it may be well with thee, and that thou mayest go in and possess ha'aretz hatovah which Hashem swore unto Avoteicha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 33:52–53; SHEMOT 23:28–30; SHOFETIM 3:1–4"> To cast out all thine enemies from before thee, as Hashem hath spoken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 13:14; SHEMOT 12:26; YEHOSHUA 4:6–7"> And when thy ben asketh thee in time to come, saying, What mean the edut, and the chukkim, and the mishpatim which Hashem Eloheinu hath commanded you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:17–18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:11–12; DEVARIM 15:15"> Then thou shalt say unto thy ben, We were Pharaohʼs avadim in Mitzrayim; and Hashem brought us out of Mitzrayim with a yad chazakah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:34; TEHILLIM 58:10–11; DEVARIM 7:19"> And Hashem showed otot and mofetim, great and terrible, upon Mitzrayim, upon Pharaoh, and upon all his household, before our eyes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEVARIM 6:18; DEVARIM 6:10; SHEMOT 13:5"> And He brought us out from thence, that He might bring us in, to give us ha'aretz which He promised on oath unto Avoteinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:39; TEHILLIM 41:2; DEVARIM 10:12–13"> And Hashem commanded us to do all these chukkim, to fear Hashem Eloheinu, for our good always, that He might preserve us alive, as it is at yom hazeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEVARIM 24:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:3; VAYIKRA 18:5"> And tzedakah it shall be for us, if we are shomer to do all these mitzvot before Hashem Eloheinu, as He hath commanded us.</span>
</div>
