<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHOFETIM 18:2; YEHOSHUA 17:14–18; SHOFETIM 10:1"> And there was an ish from har Ephrayim, whose shem was Mikhay'hu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 13:25; RUTH 3:10; SHMUEL ALEF 23:21"> And he said unto his em, The eleven hundred pieces of kesef that were taken from thee, about which thou did utter a curse, and spoke of also in mine ears, Hinei, the kesef is with me; I took it. And his em said, Baruch beni LaHashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:4; SHEMOT 20:23; VAYIKRA 19:4"> And when he had restored the eleven hundred pieces of kesef to his em, his em said, I had wholly set apart as kodesh the kesef unto Hashem from my hand for beni (my son), to make a pesel (carved image, idol)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Ex 20:4">a</a></sup>and a masekhah (an image cast from a mold);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Ex 32:4">b</a></sup>now therefore I will return it unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YESHAYAH 46:6–7; YIRMEYAH 10:9–10"> So he restored the kesef unto his em; and his em took two hundred pieces of kesef and gave them to the tzoref (goldsmith), who made thereof a pesel and a masekhah; and they were in the bais Mikhay'hu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHOFETIM 18:14; SHOFETIM 8:27; BERESHIS 31:19"> And the ish Michah had a bais elohim, and made an ephod, and teraphim, and ordained one of his banim, who became his kohen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:8; MISHLE 14:12; SHOFETIM 21:25"> In those yamim there was no melech in Yisroel, but every man did that which was right in his own eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MICHOH 5:2; MATTITYAHU 2:1; RUTH 1:1–2"> Meanwhile there was a na'ar from Beit-Lechem Yehudah of the mishpakhat Yehudah, who was a Levi, and he sojourned there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 13:10–11; SHOFETIM 17:11; YEHOSHUA 24:33"> And the ish departed out of the Ir from Beit-Lechem Yehudah to sojourn where he could find a place; and he came to har Ephrayim to the bais Michah, as he made his derech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Michah said unto him, Whence comest thou? And he said unto him, I am a Levi of Beit-Lechem Yehudah, and I go to sojourn where I may find a place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 13:19; SHOFETIM 18:19–20; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:10"> And Michah said unto him, Dwell with me, and be unto me an av and a kohen, and I will give thee ten pieces of kesef a year, and a set of begadim, and mikhyah (maintenance, livelihood). So the Levi went in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the Levi agreed to dwell with the ish; and the na'ar was unto him as one of his banim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHOFETIM 18:30; BAMIDBAR 16:8–10; BAMIDBAR 16:5"> And Michah ordained<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="literally, filled the hands of, consecrated, appointed as his own priest">c</a></sup>the Levi; and the na'ar became his kohen, and was in the bais Mikhay'hu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:2–3; MISHLE 14:12; YOCHANAN 16:2"> Then said Michah, Now I have da'as that Hashem will do me good, seeing I have a Levi as my kohen.</span>
