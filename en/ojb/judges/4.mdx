<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHOFETIM 1:19; TEHILLIM 106:42; SHOFETIM 3:9"> And the Bnei Yisroel cried unto Hashem; for he had nine hundred chariots of barzel (iron); and twenty shanah he oppressed chazakah the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 15:20; LUKAS 2:36; MELACHIM BAIS 22:14"> And isha Devorah, a neviah, the eshet Lapidot, judged Yisroel at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 18:19; YIRMEYAH 31:15; SHEMOT 18:26"> And she held court under the Tomer Devorah between Ramah and Beit-El in har Ephrayim; Bnei Yisroel came up to her for mishpat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:47; SHOFETIM 8:18; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:32"> And she sent and called Barak ben Avinoam out of Kedesh Naphtali, and said unto him, Hath not Hashem Elohei Yisroel commanded, saying, Go and take position at Har Tavor, and take with thee ten thousand ish of the Bnei Naphtali and of the Bnei Zevulun?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:40; YECHEZKEL 38:10–16; TEHILLIM 83:9–10"> And I will lure unto thee to the river Kishon the sar Tze'va Yavin, Sisra, with his chariots and his multitude; and I will deliver him into thine yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:10–14; MATTITYAHU 14:30–31"> And Barak said unto her, If thou wilt go with me, then I will go; but if thou wilt not go with me, then I will not go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHOFETIM 2:14; SHMUEL ALEF 2:30; SHOFETIM 4:17–22"> And she said, I will surely go with thee; notwithstanding the derech of thee shall not take thee to thy teferet (honor); for Hashem shall sell Sisra into the yad of an isha. And Devorah arose, and went with Barak to Kedesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHOFETIM 5:18; SHOFETIM 5:15; SHOFETIM 4:14"> And Barak summoned Zevulun and Naphtali to Kedesh; and he went up with ten thousand ish at his feet; and Devorah went up with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHOFETIM 1:16; YEHOSHUA 19:33; BAMIDBAR 10:29"> Now Chever the Keni, which was of the Bnei Chovav the khoten Moshe, had separated from the Kayin,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="other Keni">a</a></sup>and encamped his ohel unto the oak at Tza'anannim, which is near Kedesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="TEHILLIM 89:12; YIRMEYAH 46:18; YEHOSHUA 19:12"> And they told Sisra that Barak ben Avinoam was gone up to Har Tavor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHOFETIM 4:2–3; SHOFETIM 4:7"> And Sisra gathered together all his chariots, even nine hundred chariots of barzel (iron), and kol haAm that were with him, from Charoshet HaGoyim unto the river Kishon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEVARIM 9:3; SHMUEL BAIS 5:24; YESHAYAH 52:12"> And Devorah said unto Barak, Go! For this is the day in which Hashem hath delivered Sisra into thine yad. Is not Hashem gone out before thee? So Barak went down from Har Tavor, and ten thousand ish after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 10:10; TEHILLIM 83:9–10; SHOFETIM 5:20–21"> And Hashem routed Sisra, and all his chariots, and kol hamachaneh, with the edge of the cherev before Barak; so that Sisra got down from his merkavah (chariot), and fled away on foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:12; VAYIKRA 26:7–8; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13"> But Barak pursued after the chariots, and after the machaneh, as far as Charoshet HaGoyim; and kol machaneh Sisra fell by the edge of the cherev; and there was not a man left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TEHILLIM 69:22; YESHAYAH 57:21; AMOS 5:19–20"> Howbeit Sisra fled away on foot to the ohel Ya'el the isha of Chever the Keni; for there was shalom between Yavin Melech Chatzor and the Bais Chever Keni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 6:19"> And Ya'el went out to meet Sisra, and said unto him, Turn in, adoni, stay with me; fear not. And when he had entered with her into the ohel, she covered him with a covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHOFETIM 5:25–26; BERESHIS 24:43; YESHAYAH 41:17"> And he said unto her, Give me, now, a little mayim to drink; for I am thirsty. And she opened a skin of cholov, and gave him drink, and covered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 2:3–5; SHMUEL BAIS 17:20"> Again he said unto her, Stand at the petach haohel, and it shall be, when any ish doth come and inquire of thee, and say, Is there any ish here? — thou shalt say, No.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHOFETIM 5:26–27; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:27; SHOFETIM 3:21"> Then Ya'el eshet Chever took a yetad haohel (tent peg), and took a makevet (hammer) in her yad, and went softly unto him, and drove the yetad into his temple, and stuck it into the ground; for he was fast asleep exhausted. So he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 17:10–15; SHMUEL BAIS 17:3"> And, hinei, as Barak pursued Sisra, Ya'el came out to meet him, and said unto him, Come, and I will show thee the ish whom thou seekest. And when he came into her ohel, hinei, Sisra lay dead, and the yetad was in his temple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 9:24; TEHILLIM 18:47; TEHILLIM 18:39"> So Elohim subdued on that day Yavin Melech Kena'an before the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 3:12"> And the yad Bnei Yisroel prospered, and prevailed against Yavin Melech Kena'an, until they had destroyed Yavin Melech Kena'an.</span>
</div>
