<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHOFETIM 20:1; MARKOS 6:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:12"> Now the Ish Yisroel had taken an oath in Mitzpah, saying, There shall not any of us give his bat unto Binyamin as isha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHOFETIM 20:26; SHOFETIM 20:18; SHMUEL ALEF 30:4"> And HaAm went to Beit-El, and abode there till erev before HaElohim, and lifted up their voices, and wept gadol;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TEHILLIM 74:1; YEHOSHUA 7:7–9; YESHAYAH 63:17"> And said, Hashem Elohei Yisroel, why is this come to pass in Yisroel, that there should be today shevet echad lacking in Yisroel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 24:25; MELACHIM ALEF 8:64; TEHILLIM 78:34–35"> And it came to pass on the next day, that HaAm rose early, and built there a Mizbe'ach, and offered olot and shelamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHOFETIM 5:23; SHOFETIM 21:18; SHOFETIM 21:1"> And the Bnei Yisroel said, Who is there among kol Shivtei Yisroel that came not up with the Kahal unto Hashem? For they had made the shevuah hagedolah concerning him that came not up before Hashem to Mitzpah, saying, He shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHOFETIM 21:15; SHOFETIM 20:23; LUKAS 19:41–42"> And the Bnei Yisroel had compassion for Binyamin their brother, and said, There is shevet echad cut off from Yisroel this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHOFETIM 21:1; SHOFETIM 21:18; SHMUEL ALEF 14:28–29"> How shall we do for nashim for them that remain, seeing we have taken an oath by Hashem that we will not give them of our banot as nashim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 31:11–13; SHMUEL ALEF 11:1–3; SHMUEL BAIS 2:5–6"> And they said, What one is there of the Shivtei Yisroel that came not up to Mitzpah to Hashem? And, hinei, there came none to the machaneh from Yavesh-Gil'ad to the Kahal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For HaAm were numbered, and, hinei, there were none of the inhabitants of Yavesh-Gil'ad there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHOFETIM 5:23; SHOFETIM 21:5; SHMUEL ALEF 11:7"> And HaEdah sent thither 12,000 ish of the Bnei HeChayil, and commanded them, saying, Go and strike the inhabitants of Yavesh-Gil'ad with the edge of the cherev, with the women and the children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 31:17–18; DEVARIM 2:34"> And this is the thing that ye shall do, Ye shall utterly destroy every zachar, and every isha that hath da'as of lying with man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 18:1; YIRMEYAH 7:12; TEHILLIM 78:60"> And they found among the inhabitants of Yavesh-Gil'ad four hundred na'arah betulah, that had known no man by lying with any zachar; and they brought them unto the machaneh at Shiloh, which is in Eretz Kena'an.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHOFETIM 20:47; DEVARIM 20:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:17"> And the kol HaEdah sent some to speak to the Bnei Binyamin that were at the Rock of Rimmon, and to proclaim shalom unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHOFETIM 21:12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:2; SHOFETIM 20:47"> And Binyamin came again at that time; and they gave them nashim which they had saved alive of the women of Yavesh-Gil'ad; but they sufficed not for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHOFETIM 21:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:13; YESHAYAH 58:12"> And HaAm had compassion for Binyamin, because Hashem had made a breach in the Shivtei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then the Ziknei HaEdah (Elders of the Congregation) said, How shall we do for nashim for them that remain, seeing the isha are destroyed out of Binyamin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:55; BAMIDBAR 36:7"> And they said, There must be yerushah (heir) for them that be survivor of Binyamin, that a shevet be not destroyed out of Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHOFETIM 21:1; SHOFETIM 11:35"> Howbeit we may not give them nashim of our banot; for the Bnei Yisroel have sworn, saying, Cursed be he that giveth an isha to Binyamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:10; DEVARIM 16:1; DEVARIM 16:13"> Then they said, Hinei, there is chag Hashem(feast of Hashem) in Shiloh yearly in a place which is on the north side of Beit-El, on the east side of the road that goeth up from Beit-El to Shechem, and on the south of Levonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Therefore they commanded the Bnei Binyamin, saying, Go and lie in wait in the kramim (vineyards);</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHOFETIM 11:34; SHEMOT 15:20; KOHELET 3:4"> And watch, and, hinei, if the Banot Shiloh come out to join in dances, then come ye out of the kramim (vineyards), and catch you every ish his isha of the Banot Shiloh, and go to Eretz Binyamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHOFETIM 21:1; SHOFETIM 21:18; SHOFETIM 21:14"> And it shall be, when their avot or their achim come unto us for riv (to complain), we<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the Ziknei HaEdah">a</a></sup>will say unto them, Give them to us, for we did not receive every ish his isha through the war; for ye have not given them unto them at this time; now would ye be guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHOFETIM 20:48"> And the Bnei Binyamin did so and took them nashim, according to their number, of them that danced, whom they caught; and they went and returned unto their nachalah, and repaired the towns, and dwelt in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And the Bnei Yisroel departed from there at that time, every ish to his shevet and to his mishpakhah, and they went out from thence every ish to his nachalah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:8; SHOFETIM 17:6; MISHLE 3:5"> In those days there was no melech in Yisroel; every man did that which was yashar (right) in his own eyes.</span>
