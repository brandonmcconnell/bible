<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHOFETIM 20:18; BAMIDBAR 27:21; SHOFETIM 20:28"> Now after the mot Yehoshua it came to pass, that the Bnei Yisroel asked Hashem, saying, Who shall go up for us against the Kena'ani first, to fight against him?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="them">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HISGALUS 5:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:14; BAMIDBAR 2:3"> And Hashem said, Yehudah shall go up; hinei, I have delivered HaAretz into his yad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 10:11; YEHOSHUA 19:1; SHOFETIM 1:17"> And Yehudah said unto Shim'on his brother, Come up with me into my goral (lot), and we will fight against the Kena'ani; and I likewise will go with thee into thy goral. So Shim'on went with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 11:8; SHEMOT 23:28–29; YEHOSHUA 11:6–8"> And Yehudah went up; and Hashem delivered the Kena'ani and the Perizzi into their yad; and they struck them down at Bezek ten thousand ish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And they found Adoni-Bezek at Bezek; and they fought against him, and they struck down the Kena'ani and the Perizzi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But Adoni-Bezek fled; and they pursued after him, and caught him, and cut off his thumbs and his big toes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:33; HISGALUS 13:10; LUKAS 6:37–38"> And Adoni-Bezek said, Threescore and ten melachim, having their thumbs and their big toes cut off, picked up their scraps under my shulchan; as I have done, so Elohim hath requited me. And they brought him to Yerushalayim, and there he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:63; SHOFETIM 1:21"> Now the Bnei Yehudah had fought against Yerushalayim, and had taken it, and put it to the edge of the cherev, and set eish to the Ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:13–20; YEHOSHUA 11:21; YEHOSHUA 10:36"> And afterward the Bnei Yehudah went down to fight against the Kena'ani that dwelt in the har, and in the Negev, and in the western foothills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 13:22; YEHOSHUA 14:15; YIRMEYAH 9:23"> And Yehudah went against the Kena'ani that dwelt in Chevron; (now the shem of Chevron formerly was Kiryat-Arba); and they struck down Sheshai, and Achiman, and Talmai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:15; YEHOSHUA 10:38–39"> And from thence Yehudah went against the inhabitants of Devir; and the shem of Devir formerly was Kiryat-Sepher;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:16–17; SHMUEL ALEF 17:25; SHMUEL ALEF 18:23"> And Kalev said, He that striketh Kiryat-Sepher, and taketh it, to him will I give Achsah my bat as isha.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHOFETIM 3:9"> And Otniel ben Kenaz, Kalevʼs younger brother, took it; and he gave him Achsah his bat as isha.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:18–19"> And it came to pass, when she came to him, that she urged him to ask of her av the sadeh; and she dismounted her donkey; and Kalev said unto her, What wilt thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:18; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:5; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9"> And she said unto him, Give me bracha; since thou hast given me eretz hanegev; give me also gullot mayim. And Kalev gave her the upper gullot and the lower gullot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 10:29–32; SHOFETIM 4:11; SHMUEL ALEF 15:6"> And the Bnei Keni, khoten Moshe, went up out of the Ir HaTemarim with the Bnei Yehudah into the midbar of Yehudah, which lieth in the Negev of Arad; and they went and dwelt among the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:3; SHOFETIM 1:3; YEHOSHUA 19:4"> And Yehudah went with Shim'on his brother, and they struck down the Kena'ani that inhabited Tzephat, and utterly destroyed it. And the shem of the Ir was called Chormah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 11:22; SHOFETIM 3:3; YEHOSHUA 15:45–47"> Also Yehudah took Azah (Gaza) and the territory thereof, and Ashkelon and the territory thereof, and Ekron and the territory thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 41:10; SHOFETIM 1:2; TEHILLIM 60:12"> And Hashem was with Yehudah; and he possessed the inhabitants of the hill country; but could not drive out the inhabitants of the &#91;broad&#93; valley &#91;areas&#93;, because they had chariots of barzel (iron).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHOFETIM 1:10; YEHOSHUA 15:13–14; BAMIDBAR 14:24"> And they gave Chevron unto Kalev, just as Moshe promised; and he expelled from there the three Bnei Anak.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:63; SHOFETIM 19:10–12; SHOFETIM 1:8"> And the Bnei Binyamin did not drive out the Yevusi that inhabited Yerushalayim; and the Yevusi dwell with the Bnei Binyamin in Yerushalayim unto this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHOFETIM 1:19; MELACHIM BAIS 18:7; BAMIDBAR 1:32"> And Bais Yosef, they also went up against Beit-El; and Hashem was with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:19; BERESHIS 35:6; BERESHIS 48:3"> And Bais Yosef sent shomrim (spies) to Beit-El. (Now the shem of the Ir formerly was Luz.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 2:12–14; SHMUEL ALEF 30:15"> And the shomrim saw an ish come forth out of the Ir, and they said unto him, Show us, now, the way to enter into the Ir, and we will show thee chesed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 6:22–25"> And when he showed them the way to enter into the Ir, they struck down the Ir with the edge of the cherev; but they spared the ish and all his mishpakhah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 7:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 1:17"> And the ish went into the eretz HaChittim, and built an Ir, and called the shem thereof Luz; which is the shem thereof unto this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 17:11–13; YIRMEYAH 48:10; TEHILLIM 106:34–35"> Neither did Menasheh drive out the Beit-She'an and her villages, nor Ta'nakh and her villages, nor the inhabitants of Dor and her villages, nor the inhabitants of Yivle'am and her villages, nor the inhabitants of Megiddo and her villages; so the Kena'ani were determined to dwell in that land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And it came to pass, when Yisroel was chazak, that they put the Kena'ani to forced labor, and did not utterly drive them out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 16:10; MELACHIM ALEF 9:16"> Neither did Ephrayim drive out the Kena'ani that dwelt in Gezer; but the Kena'ani dwelt in Gezer among them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:15"> Neither did Zevulun drive out the inhabitants of Kitron, nor the inhabitants of Nahalol; but the Kena'ani dwelt among them, and became subject to forced labor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:24–30"> Neither did Asher drive out the inhabitants of Akko, nor the inhabitants of Tzidon, nor of Achlav, nor of Akhziv, nor of Chelbah, nor of Aphik, nor of Rechov;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="TEHILLIM 106:34–35"> But the Asheri dwelt among the Kena'ani, the inhabitants of HaAretz: for they did not drive them out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:32–38; SHOFETIM 1:30; TEHILLIM 18:24"> Neither did Naphtali drive out the inhabitants of Beit-Shemesh, nor the inhabitants of Beit-Anat; but he dwelt among the Kena'ani, the inhabitants of HaAretz: nevertheless the inhabitants of Beit-Shemesh and of Beit-Anat were unto them subject to forced labor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:47; SHOFETIM 18:1"> And the Emori forced the Bnei Dan into the hill country; for they would not allow them to come down into the &#91;broad&#93; valley &#91;area&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:42; SHOFETIM 12:12; YEHOSHUA 10:12"> But the Emori would dwell in Mt Cheres in Ayalon, and in Sha'albim: yet the yad Bais Yosef prevailed, so that they became subject to forced labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 34:4; YEHOSHUA 15:2–3"> And the territory of the Emori was from the Akrabbim Ascent and the Rock, upward.</span>
</div>
