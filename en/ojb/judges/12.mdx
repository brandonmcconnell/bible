<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHOFETIM 3:28; YEHOSHUA 22:11; YEHOSHUA 2:7"> And the Gilead took the fords of the Yarden before the Ephrayim; and it was so, that when those Ephrayim which were escaped said, Let me cross over; that the anshei Gil'ad said unto him, Art thou an Ephrati? If he said, No,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 69:2; TEHILLIM 69:15; MARKOS 14:70"> Then said they unto him, Say now Shibbolet; and he said Sibbolet, for he could not pronounce it right. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Yiftach judged Yisroel shesh shanim. Then died Yiftach the Gil'adi, and was buried in one of the towns of Gil'ad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MICHOH 5:2; SHMUEL ALEF 16:1; MATTITYAHU 2:1"> And after him Ibtzan of Beit-Lechem judged Yisroel.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then died Ibtzan, and was buried at Beit-Lechem.</span>
</div>

</div>
