<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 38:17; BERESHIS 29:21; LUKAS 15:29"> But it came to pass within a while after, in the time of katzir chittim, that Shimshon visited his isha with a young goat; and he said, I will go in to my isha into the cheder. But her av would not allow him to go in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHOFETIM 14:20; SHOFETIM 14:16; BERESHIS 38:14"> And her av said, I verily thought that thou hadst utterly hated her; therefore I gave her to thy companion; is not her younger achot fairer than she? Take her now, instead of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHOFETIM 14:15"> And Shimshon said concerning them, Now shall I be more blameless than the Pelishtim, though I do them ra'ah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHIR HASHIRIM 2:15; TEHILLIM 63:10; EKHAH 5:18"> And Shimshon went and caught three hundred foxes, and took torches, and turned them tail to tail, and put a torch in the midst between two tails.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 14:30; SHEMOT 22:6"> And when he lit eish to the torches, he let them go into the standing grain of the Pelishtim, and burned up both the harvested grain and also the standing grain, and also olive orchards.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHOFETIM 14:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:6; MISHLE 22:8"> Then the Pelishtim said, Who hath done this? And they answered, Shimshon, the choson of the Timni, because he had taken his isha, and given her to his companion. And the Pelishtim came up, and burned her and her av with eish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHOFETIM 14:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:19; SHOFETIM 14:19"> And Shimshon said unto them, Since ye have done this, yet will I be avenged of you and after that I will cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YESHAYAH 63:3; YESHAYAH 63:6; YESHAYAH 25:10"> And he struck them hip and thigh with a makkah gedolah; and he went down and dwelt in the top of the rock Eitam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHOFETIM 15:17; SHOFETIM 15:19; SHOFETIM 15:14"> Then the Pelishtim went up and encamped in Yehudah, and spread themselves out in Lechi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the ish Yehudah said, Why are ye come up against us? And they answered, To bind Shimshon are we come up, to do to him as he hath done to us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHOFETIM 13:1; SHOFETIM 14:4; DEVARIM 28:47–48"> Then three thousand men of Yehudah went to the top of the rock Eitam and said to Shimshon, Knowest thou not that the Pelishtim are rulers over us? What is this that thou hast done unto us? And he said unto them, As they did unto me, so have I done unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHOFETIM 8:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:25; MELACHIM ALEF 2:25"> And they said unto him, We are come down to bind thee, that we may deliver thee into the yad Pelishtim. And Shimshon said unto them, Swear unto me, that ye will not fall upon me yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And they spoke unto him, saying, No; but we will bind thee fast, and deliver thee into their yad; but surely we will not kill thee. And they bound him with two avotim chadashim (new ropes), and brought him up from the rock.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHOFETIM 14:19; SHOFETIM 3:10; TEHILLIM 118:11"> And when he came unto Lechi, the Pelishtim shouted against him; and the Ruach of Hashem came mightily upon him, and the avotim that were upon his arms became as flax that was burned with eish, and his binding loosed from off his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 23:10; VAYIKRA 26:8; SHOFETIM 3:31"> And he found a new jawbone of a chamor, and put forth his yad and took it and slaughtered a thousand men therewith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Shimshon said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">With the lechi (jawbone) of a chamor</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">heaps upon heaps,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with the jaw of a chamor</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">have I slain a thousand men.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And it came to pass when he had made an end of speaking that he cast away the jawbone out of his yad, and called that place Ramat Lechi (Jawbone Hill).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:7–8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:32; SHOFETIM 8:4"> And he was very thirsty, and called on Hashem, and said, Thou hast given this teshu'ah hagedolah (great victory, rescue, salvation) into the yad of thy eved; and now shall I die for thirst, and fall into the yad of the arelim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 44:3; BERESHIS 45:27; TEHILLIM 34:6"> But Elohim split open the hollow place that is at Lechi, and there came mayim thereout; and when he had drunk, his ruach came again, and he revived; wherefore he called the shem thereof Ein HaKorei (Spring of the Caller), which is in Lechi unto this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHOFETIM 13:1; SHOFETIM 16:31; SHOFETIM 13:5"> And he judged Yisroel in the days of the Pelishtim esrim shanah.</span>
</div>
