<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHOFETIM 18:1; SHOFETIM 17:8; MALACHI 2:15"> And it came to pass in those days, when there was no melech in Yisroel, that there was a certain Levi sojourning in the remote har Ephrayim country, who took to him a pilegesh out of Beit-Lechem Yehudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 16:28; DEVARIM 22:21; VAYIKRA 21:9"> And his pilegesh played the zonah against him, and went away from him unto her bais av to Beit-Lechem Yehudah, and was there four whole months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 34:3; BERESHIS 50:21; VAYIKRA 19:17"> And her ish arose, and went after her, to speak unto her lev, and to bring her back, having his na'ar (servant) with him, and a couple of donkeys; and she brought him into her bais av; and when the avi hana'arah saw him, he had simcha to welcome him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And his khoten (father-in-law), the avi hana'arah, held him fast; and he abode with him shloshet yamim; so they did eat and drink, and lodged there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHOFETIM 19:8; BERESHIS 18:5; TEHILLIM 104:15"> And it came to pass on the fourth day, when they arose early in the boker, that he rose up to depart; the avi hana'arah said unto his choson (son-in-law), Refresh thine lev with a morsel of lechem, and afterward go your way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHOFETIM 19:9; SHOFETIM 16:25; RUTH 3:7"> And they sat down, and did eat and drink both of them together; for the avi hana'arah had said unto the ish, Be pleased, and tarry all night, and let thine lev be content.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And when the ish rose up to depart, his khoten (father-in-law) urged him; therefore he lodged there again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And he arose early in the boker on the yom hachamishi to depart; and the avi hana'arah said, Refresh now thine lev. Tarry till the yom turns,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="past midday">a</a></sup>and they did eat both of them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MISHLE 27:1; LUKAS 24:29; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:13–14"> And when the ish rose up to depart, he, and his pilegesh, and his na'ar, his khoten (father-in-law), the avi hana'arah, said unto him, Hinei, now the yom draweth toward erev, tarry the night now; hinei, the yom groweth to an end, lodge here, that thine lev may be content; and makhar (tomorrow) get you early on your derech, that thou mayest go to your ohel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:63; YEHOSHUA 15:8; SHOFETIM 1:8"> But the ish would not tarry that night, but he rose up and departed, and came to nokhach (straight before, in the direction of) Yevus, which is Yerushalayim; and there were with him two donkeys saddled, his pilegesh also was with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 5:6; SHOFETIM 1:21; SHOFETIM 19:10"> And when they were near Yevus, the yom was far spent; and the na'ar said unto his adon, Come, now, and let us turn in into ir haYevusi, and lodge in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And his adon said unto him, We will not turn aside hither into the ir nochri, that is not of the Bnei Yisroel; we will pass over to Giv'ah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 10:26; HOSHEA 5:8; YESHAYAH 10:29"> And he said unto his na'ar, Come, and let us draw near to one of these places to lodge all night, in Giv'ah, or in Ramah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And they passed on and went their way; and the shemesh went down upon them when they were near Giv'ah, which belongeth to Binyamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHOFETIM 19:18; MATTITYAHU 25:35; MATTITYAHU 25:43"> And they turned aside thither, to go in and to lodge the night in Giv'ah; and when he went in, he sat him down in the rechov of the ir; for there was no ish that took them into his bais to lodge the night.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Job 31:32regarding the sin here, made more shameful in that it was committed against a Levi">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHOFETIM 19:1; TEHILLIM 104:23; BERESHIS 3:19"> And, hinei, there came an ish zaken from his work out of the sadeh at erev, and the ish was also from har Ephrayim; and he sojourned in Giv'ah; but the anashim of the place were Bnei Yemini (Benjamites).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 32:17; BERESHIS 16:8"> And when he had lifted up his eyes, he saw haish haoreach (the wayfaring man, traveling man) in the rechov haIr; and the ish hazaken said, Whither goest thou? And whence comest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHOFETIM 18:31; YOCHANAN 15:6; SHMUEL ALEF 1:7"> And he said unto him, We are passing from Beit-Lechem Yehudah toward the remote har Ephrayim country; from thence am I; and I went to Beit-Lechem Yehudah, but I am now going to the Bais Hashem;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See 18:31">c</a></sup>but there is no ish that receiveth me into the bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Yet there is both straw and fodder for our donkeys; and there is lechem and yayin also for me, and for thy amah (maidservant), and for the na'ar which is with thy avadim; there is no machsor (want, need) of any thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHOFETIM 6:23; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–16; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:2"> And the ish hazaken said, Shalom lach; howsoever let all thy wants lie upon me; only lodge not in the rechov.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 43:24; BERESHIS 18:4; LUKAS 7:44"> So he brought him into his bais, and mixed fodder for the donkeys; and they washed their feet, and did eat and drink.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BERESHIS 19:4–5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:9; SHOFETIM 20:5"> Now as they were making their hearts content, hinei, the anshei haIr, anshei bnei Beliya'al, surrounded the bais, and pounded incessantly on the delet, and shouted to the ish, the Ba'al HaBayit, the zaken, saying, Bring out the ish that came into thine bais, that we may know him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="a thin line drawn between homosexual rape and murder">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BERESHIS 34:7; SHMUEL BAIS 13:12; SHOFETIM 20:6"> And the ish, the Ba'al HaBayit, went out unto them, and said unto them, Nay, my brethren, nay, do not so wickedly; seeing that this ish is come into mine bais, do not this nevalah (outrage).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BERESHIS 19:8; DEVARIM 21:14; BERESHIS 34:2"> Hinei, here is my bat a betulah, and his pilegesh; them I will bring out now, and humble ye them, and do with them what seemeth hatov unto your eyes; but unto this ish do not so devar hanevalah (vile, wicked a thing).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="HOSHEA 10:9; HOSHEA 9:9; HOSHEA 7:4–7"> But the anashim would not pay heed to him; so the ish took his pilegesh, and brought her forth unto them; and they knew her, and abused her kol halailah until the boker; and when the shachar (dawn) came, they let her go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHOFETIM 19:3; BERESHIS 18:12; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:6"> Then came the isha at the break of haboker, and fell down at the petach bais haish where her adon was, ad haohr (until daylight, sunrise).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And her adon rose up in the boker, and opened the daletot habais, and stepped out to go on his derech; and, hinei, the isha, his pilegesh, was fallen at the petach habais! And her hands were upon the sahf (threshold).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHOFETIM 20:5; MELACHIM ALEF 18:29"> And he said unto her, Up, and let us be going. But there was no answer. Then the ish took &#91;the corpse&#93; upon a donkey, and the ish rose up, and set out for his makom (place, home).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHOFETIM 20:6–7; SHMUEL ALEF 11:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:2"> And when he was come into his bais, he took a ma'akhelet (knife), and laid hold on his pilegesh, and divided her, together with her atzmot, into twelve pieces, and sent her into all the territory of Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="SHOFETIM 20:7; MISHLE 13:10; HOSHEA 9:9"> And it was so, that all that saw it said, There was no such deed done nor seen from the yom that the Bnei Yisroel came up out of Eretz Mitzrayim unto this day; give ye heed unto it, make up your minds,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="about the punishment">e</a></sup>and speak.</span>
