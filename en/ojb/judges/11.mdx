<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:32; SHOFETIM 6:12; MELACHIM BAIS 5:1"> Now Yiftach the Gil'adi was a gibbor chayil, and he was the ben of an isha zonah; and Gil'ad fathered Yiftach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:10; MISHLE 2:16; DEVARIM 23:2"> And Gil'adʼs isha bore him banim; and the bnei haisha grew up, and they thrust out Yiftach, and said unto him, Thou shalt not inherit in our bais avi; for thou art the ben isha acheret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHOFETIM 9:4; SHMUEL ALEF 22:2; SHMUEL BAIS 10:8"> Then Yiftach fled from his achim, and dwelt in Eretz Tov; and there were gathered anashim reikim (outlaws) around Yiftach, and went out with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHOFETIM 10:9"> And it came to pass in process of time, that the Bnei Ammon made war against Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 118:22–23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:35–39; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:27–29"> And it was so, that when the Bnei Ammon made war against Yisroel, the ziknei Gil'ad went to fetch Yiftach out of Eretz Tov;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And they said unto Yiftach, Come, and be our katzin (leader), that we may fight with the Bnei Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:9; BERESHIS 45:4–5; YESHAYAH 60:14"> And Yiftach said unto the ziknei Gil'ad, Did not ye hate me, and expel me out of my bais avi? And why are ye come unto me now when ye are in distress?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHOFETIM 10:18; MELACHIM ALEF 13:6; SHEMOT 10:17"> And the ziknei Gil'ad said unto Yiftach, Nevertheless, we turn again to thee now, that thou mayest go with us, and fight against the Bnei Ammon, and be our rosh over all the inhabitants of Gil'ad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:20–29"> And Yiftach said unto the ziknei Gil'ad, If ye bring me home again to fight against the Bnei Ammon, and Hashem deliver them before me, shall I be your rosh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 42:5; BERESHIS 31:50; YIRMEYAH 29:23"> And the ziknei Gil'ad said unto Yiftach, Hashem be witness between us, if we do not so according to thy words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHOFETIM 10:17; SHMUEL ALEF 10:17; SHOFETIM 20:1"> Then Yiftach went with the ziknei Gil'ad, and the people made him rosh and katzin over them; and Yiftach uttered all his words before Hashem in Mitzpah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:26; MATTITYAHU 18:15–16; MISHLE 25:8–9"> And Yiftach sent malachim unto the melech Bnei Ammon, saying, What hast thou to do with me, that thou art come against me to fight in my land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 32:22; BAMIDBAR 21:24–26; DEVARIM 2:37"> And the melech Bnei Ammon answered unto the malachim of Yiftach, Because Yisroel took away my land, when they came up out of Mitzrayim, from Arnon even unto Yabbok, and unto Yarden; now therefore give back those lands again b'shalom (peaceably).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:18; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:14"> And Yiftach sent malachim again unto the melech Bnei Ammon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:19; DEVARIM 2:9; BAMIDBAR 21:27–30"> And said unto him, Thus saith Yiftach, Yisroel took not away Eretz Moav, nor the Eretz Bnei Ammon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:25; DEVARIM 1:40; BAMIDBAR 20:1"> But when Yisroel came up from Mitzrayim, and walked through the midbar until Yam Suf, and came to Kadesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 20:14–21; DEVARIM 2:4–9; BAMIDBAR 20:1"> Then Yisroel sent malachim unto the melech Edom, saying, Let me, now, pass through thy land; but the melech Edom would not pay heed thereto. In like manner they sent unto the melech Moav; but he would not consent; Yisroel abode in Kadesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:1–8; BAMIDBAR 22:36; BAMIDBAR 21:4–13"> Then they went along through the midbar, skirted the Eretz Edom, and Eretz Moav, and came by the east side of Eretz Moav, encamped on the other side of Arnon, but came not within the territory of Moav; for the Arnon was the boundary of Moav.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:21–35; DEVARIM 2:26–36; DEVARIM 3:1–17"> And Yisroel sent malachim unto Sichon melech HaEmori, the melech Cheshbon; and Yisroel said unto him, Let us pass, now, through thy land into my makom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 13:15–32; BAMIDBAR 21:23–25; TEHILLIM 136:17–21"> But Sichon trusted not Yisroel to pass through his territory; but Sichon gathered all his people together, and encamped in Yahtzah, and fought against Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Hashem Elohei Yisroel delivered Sichon and all his people into the yad Yisroel, and they struck them down; so Yisroel possessed all the Eretz HaEmori, the inhabitants of that country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:36"> And they possessed all the territory HaEmori, from Arnon even unto Yabbok, and from the midbar even unto Yarden.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> So now Hashem Elohei Yisroel hath dispossessed HaEmori from before His people Yisroel, and shouldest thou possess it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:29; TEHILLIM 78:55; MICHOH 4:5"> Wilt not thou possess that which Kemosh eloheicha giveth thee to possess? So whatever Hashem Eloheinu shall take possession of before us, that will we possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MICHOH 6:5; YEHOSHUA 24:9–10; DEVARIM 23:3–4"> And now art thou anything better than Balak ben Tzippor, melech Moav? Did he ever strive against Yisroel, or did he ever fight against them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:36; YEHOSHUA 12:5; SHOFETIM 10:8"> While Yisroel dwelt in Cheshbon and her towns, and in Aro'er and her settlements, and in all the towns that be along by the coasts of Arnon, three hundred shanah? Why therefore did ye not recover them within that time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 24:15; BERESHIS 16:5; SHMUEL ALEF 24:12"> Wherefore I have not sinned against thee, but thou doest me wrong to war against me; Hashem HaShofet be judge this day between the Bnei Yisroel and the Bnei Ammon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 14:11; MISHLE 16:18"> Howbeit the melech Bnei Ammon paid heed not unto the words of Yiftach which he sent him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 11:25; SHOFETIM 3:10; SHOFETIM 13:25"> Then the Ruach Hashem came upon Yiftach, and he passed over Gil'ad, and Menasheh, passed over Mitzpeh of Gil'ad, and from Mitzpeh of Gil'ad he passed over unto the Bnei Ammon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:20; SHMUEL ALEF 1:11; KOHELET 5:1–2"> And Yiftach vowed a neder unto Hashem, and said, If thou shalt without fail deliver the Bnei Ammon into mine hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="TEHILLIM 66:13–14; VAYIKRA 27:28–29; SHMUEL ALEF 1:28"> Then it shall be, that whatsoever cometh forth of the delet of my bais to meet me, when I return b'shalom from the Bnei Ammon, shall surely be Hashemʼs, and I will offer it up for an olah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHOFETIM 1:4; SHOFETIM 2:18; SHOFETIM 3:10"> So Yiftach passed over unto the Bnei Ammon to fight against them; and Hashem delivered them into his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 27:17; DEVARIM 2:36"> And he struck them down from Aro'er, even till thou come to Minnit, even 20 towns, unto Avel-Kramim, with a very great slaughter. Thus the Bnei Ammon were subdued before the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:4; SHEMOT 15:20; SHOFETIM 10:17"> And Yiftach came to Mitzpah unto his bais, hinei, his bat came out to meet him with tambourines and with dances; she was his yechidah (only one); beside her he had neither ben nor bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 14:7–9; BERESHIS 37:29–30; BERESHIS 42:36–38"> And it came to pass, when he saw her, that he tore his clothes, and said, Alas, my bat! Thou hast brought me very low, and thou art one of them that trouble me; for I have opened my mouth unto Hashem, and I cannot go back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 18:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:30; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:4"> And she said unto him, Avi, if thou hast opened thy mouth unto Hashem, do to me according to that which hath proceeded out of thy mouth; forasmuch as Hashem hath taken vengeance for thee of thine enemies, even of the Bnei Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKAS 1:25; SHMUEL ALEF 1:6"> She said unto her av, Let this thing be done for me; let me alone two chodashim, that I may go up and down upon the hills, and bewail betulai (my virginity), I and my friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And he said, Go. And he sent her away for two chodashim; and she went with her companions, and bewailed her betulim (virginity) upon the hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="YESHAYAH 66:3; SHOFETIM 11:31; VAYIKRA 27:28–29"> And it came to pass at the end of two chodashim, that she returned unto her av, who did with her according to his neder which he had vowed; and she knew no man. And it was a chok in Yisroel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:25; SHOFETIM 5:11"> That the banot Yisroel went yearly to lament the bat Yiftach the Gil'adi arba'at yamim bashanah.</span>
</div>
</div>
