<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 9:26–29; SHOFETIM 2:19–20; SHOFETIM 2:13–14"> And the Bnei Yisroel did evil in the sight of Hashem; Hashem delivered them into the yad Midyan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Gn 25:2">a</a></sup>sheva shanim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:38; SHMUEL ALEF 13:6; DEVARIM 28:47–48"> And the yad Midyan prevailed against Yisroel; and because of Midyan the Bnei Yisroel made them dens in the mountains, and caves, and strongholds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHOFETIM 6:33; SHOFETIM 3:13; SHOFETIM 7:12"> And so it was, when Yisroel had sown, that Midyan came up, and Amalek, and the Bnei Kedem, even they came up against them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:16; DEVARIM 28:51; MICHOH 6:15"> And they encamped against them, and destroyed the increase of ha'aretz, till thou come unto Azah (Gaza), and left no sustenance for Yisroel, neither seh, nor ox, nor donkey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHOFETIM 7:12; SHOFETIM 8:10; YIRMEYAH 46:23"> For they came up with their cattle and their ohalim, and they came as a swarm of arbeh for multitude; for both they and their camels were without number; and they entered into ha'aretz to destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHOFETIM 3:9; YIRMEYAH 5:17; TEHILLIM 50:15"> And Yisroel was greatly impoverished because of Midyan; and the Bnei Yisroel cried unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And it came to pass, when the Bnei Yisroel cried unto Hashem because of Midyan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:5–32; YESHAYAH 63:9–14; TEHILLIM 136:10–16"> That Hashem sent an ish navi unto the Bnei Yisroel, which said unto them, Thus saith Hashem Elohei Yisroel, I brought you up from Mitzrayim, and brought you forth out of the bais avadim (house of bondage);</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 44:2–3"> And I delivered you out of the yad Mitzrayim, and out of the yad of all that oppressed you, and drove them out from before you, and gave you their land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 10:2; MELACHIM BAIS 17:35–39; YIRMEYAH 9:13"> And I said unto you, I am Hashem Eloheichem; fear not the elohei HaEmori (g-ds of the Amorites), in whose land ye dwell; but ye have not obeyed My voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 17:2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:32; SHOFETIM 13:3"> And there came Malach Hashem, and sat under a terebinth tree which was in Ophrah, that belonged unto Yoash the Aviezri; and bno Gid'on threshed chittim (wheat) in the winepress, to hide it from Midyan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 1:5; LUKAS 1:28; SHOFETIM 2:18"> And the Malach Hashem appeared unto him, and said unto him, Hashem is with thee, thou Gibbor HeChayil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TEHILLIM 77:7–9; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:2; YESHAYAH 59:1–2"> And Gid'on said unto him, Oh Adoni, if Hashem be with us, why then is all this befallen us? And where be all His nifla'ot (miracles) which Avoteinu told us of, saying, Did not Hashem bring us up from Mitzrayim? But now Hashem hath forsaken us, and delivered us into the hands of Midyan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 1:5–9; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:34; SHMUEL ALEF 12:11"> And Hashem turned to him, and said, Go in this thy ko'ach, and thou shalt save Yisroel from the yad Midyan; have not I sent thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 9:21; SHEMOT 3:11; SHEMOT 4:10"> And he said unto Him, O Adonoi, wherewith shall I save Yisroel? Hinei, my eleph is poor in Menasheh, and I am the least in the Bais Avi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 1:5; SHEMOT 3:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:21"> And Hashem said unto him, Surely I will be with thee, and thou shalt strike down Midyan as ish echad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TEHILLIM 86:17; SHEMOT 33:13; YESHAYAH 7:11"> And he said unto Him, If now I have found chen in Thy sight, then show me an ot that Thou speakest with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHOFETIM 13:15; BERESHIS 18:3; BERESHIS 18:5"> Depart not from here, now, until I come unto Thee, and bring forth my minchah, and set it before Thee. And He said, I will tarry until thou come again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:6–8; SHOFETIM 13:15–19; VAYIKRA 2:4"> And Gid'on went in, and made ready a kid, and matzot of an ephah of flour; the basar he put in a basket, and he put the broth in a pot, and brought it out unto Him under the terebinth tree, and presented it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHOFETIM 13:19; MELACHIM ALEF 18:33–34"> And the Malach HaElohim said unto him, Take the basar and the matzot, and lay them upon this rock, and pour out the broth. And he did so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="VAYIKRA 9:24; MELACHIM ALEF 18:38; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:1"> Then the Malach Hashem put forth the tip of the staff that was in his yad, and touched the basar and the matzot; and there rose up eish from the rock, and consumed the basar and the matzot. Then the Malach Hashem departed out of his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BERESHIS 32:30; SHEMOT 33:20; DEVARIM 5:26"> When Gid'on perceived that he was the Malach Hashem, Gid'on said, Alas, Adonoi Hashem! I have seen Malach Hashem face to face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DANIEL 10:19; YOCHANAN 20:26; BERESHIS 43:23"> And Hashem said unto him, Shalom l'chah; fear not; thou shalt not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BERESHIS 22:14; SHEMOT 17:15; SHOFETIM 8:32"> Then Gid'on built a Mizbe'ach there unto Hashem, and called it Hashem Shalom; unto this day it is yet in Ophrah of the Aviezri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:13; SHOFETIM 3:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:29"> And it came to pass the same night, that Hashem said unto him, Take thy fatherʼs young bull, even the second bull of sheva shanim, and tear down the mizbe'ach of Ba'al that thy av hath, and cut down the Asherah that is beside it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:40; SHMUEL BAIS 24:18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:33"> And build a Mizbe'ach unto Hashem Eloheicha upon the top of this rock, in the proper arrangement; take the second bull, offer an olah with the wood of the Asherah pole which thou shalt cut down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:4; YOCHANAN 2:5; DEVARIM 4:1"> Then Gid'on took ten men from his avadim, and did just as Hashem had said unto him; and so it was, because he feared his Bais Avi, and the men of the Ir, that he could not do it yomam (by day), that he did it by lailah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And when the men of the Ir arose early in the boker, hinei, the mizbe'ach of Ba'al was cast down, and the Asherah was cut down that was beside it, and the second bull was offered upon the Mizbe'ach that was built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And they said one to another, Who hath done this thing? And when they inquired and asked, they said, Gid'on ben Yoash hath done this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:6; YIRMEYAH 50:38"> Then the men of the Ir said unto Yoash, Bring out thy ben, that he may die; because he hath cast down the mizbe'ach of Ba'al, and because he hath cut down the Asherah that was beside it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 10:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:11"> And Yoash said unto all that stood against him, Will ye plead for Ba'al? Will ye save him? He that will plead for him, let him be put to death whilst it is yet boker; if he be Elohim, let him plead for himself, because one hath cast down his mizbe'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 12:11; SHOFETIM 7:1; SHMUEL BAIS 11:21"> Therefore on that day he called him Yeruba'al, saying, Let Ba'al contend against him, because he hath torn down his mizbe'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 17:16; SHOFETIM 6:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:35–39"> Then kol Midyan and Amalek and the Bnei Kedem were gathered together, and went over, and encamped in the valley of Yizre'el.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="SHOFETIM 3:27; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 24:20; SHOFETIM 3:10"> But the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hakodesh">b</a></sup>of Hashem came upon Gid'on; he blew a shofar; and Avi'ezer was summoned to follow after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:6–12; SHOFETIM 4:6"> And he sent malachim throughout all Menasheh; who also was gathered after him; and he sent malachim unto Asher, and unto Zevulun, and unto Naphtali; and they went up to meet them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="SHOFETIM 6:14; MATTITYAHU 16:1; SHOFETIM 6:17–20"> And Gid'on said unto HaElohim, If Thou wilt save Yisroel by my yad, as Thou hast said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="HOSHEA 14:5; MATTITYAHU 10:5–6; MATTITYAHU 15:24"> Hinei, I will put a fleece of wool in the goren (threshing floor); and if the tal (dew) be on the fleece only, and it be dry upon all ha'aretz, then shall I know that Thou wilt save Yisroel by my yad, as Thou hast said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YESHAYAH 35:7"> And it was so; for he rose up early on the next day, and squeezed the fleece together, and wrung the tal out of the fleece, a bowl full of mayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:32; YESHAYAH 50:2; TEHILLIM 107:33–35"> And Gid'on said unto HaElohim, Let not Thine anger be hot against me, and I will speak but this once; let me test, now, but this once with the fleece; let it now be dry only upon the fleece, and upon all the ground let there be tal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And Elohim did so that night; for it was dry upon the fleece only, and there was tal on all the ground.</span>
