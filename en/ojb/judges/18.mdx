<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHOFETIM 17:6; SHOFETIM 19:1; SHOFETIM 1:34"> In those days there was no melech in Yisroel; and in those days the shevet (tribe) of the Dani sought them a nachalah to dwell in; for unto that day none had fallen for him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Dan">a</a></sup>among the Shivtei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHOFETIM 13:25; YEHOSHUA 2:1; SHOFETIM 18:11"> And the Bnei Dan sent of their mishpakhat five anashim out of their whole number, anashim, Bnei Chayil, from Tzorah, and from Eshta'ol, to spy out the land, and to explore it; and they said unto them, Go, explore the land; and when they came to har Ephrayim, to the bais Michah, they lodged there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHOFETIM 12:6; BERESHIS 27:22; MATTITYAHU 26:73"> When they were at the bais Michah, they recognized the voice of the na'ar the Levi; so they turned in there, and said unto him, Who brought thee here? And what doth thou in this place? And what is thy business here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHOFETIM 17:10; YECHEZKEL 13:19; MISHLE 28:21"> And he said unto them, Thus and thus dealeth Michah with me, and hath hired me, and I am his kohen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:10; YESHAYAH 30:1; MELACHIM ALEF 22:5"> And they said unto him, Ask counsel of Elohim, that we may have da'as of whether our derech shall succeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:6; MELACHIM ALEF 22:15; TEHILLIM 33:18"> And the kohen said unto them, Go in shalom; your derech wherein ye go is nokhach Hashem(straight in the sight of Hashem).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:47; SHOFETIM 18:27–28; SHMUEL ALEF 3:13"> Then the five anashim departed, and came to Layish, and saw the people that were therein, how they dwelt careless, after the mishpat of the Tzidonim, quiet and unsuspecting; and there was no one in authority in the land, that might put them to shame for anything; but they were far from the Tzidonim, and had no ties with adam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHOFETIM 18:11; SHOFETIM 18:2; SHOFETIM 13:2"> And they returned unto their achim to Tzorah and Eshta'ol; and their achim said unto them, Mah atem (What do you &#91;report&#93;?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 2:23–24; BAMIDBAR 13:30; YEHOSHUA 18:3"> And they said, Arise, that we may go up against them; for we have seen ha'aretz, and, hinei, it is tovah me'od; and will ye do nothing? Be not slothful to go, but enter in to possess ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHOFETIM 18:27; SHOFETIM 18:7; DEVARIM 2:29"> When ye go, ye shall come unto an Am bote'ach (unsuspecting people), and to ha'aretz rachavat; for Elohim hath given it into your hands; a place where there is no lack of any thing that is in ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHOFETIM 18:16"> And there went from thence of the mishpakhat HaDani, out of Tzorah and out of Eshta'ol, 600 men armed with keli milchamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHOFETIM 13:25; YEHOSHUA 15:60; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 1:4"> And they went up, and encamped in Kiryat-Ye'arim, in Yehudah; wherefore they called that place Machaneh Dan unto this day; hinei, it is behind Kiryat-Yearim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 24:30; SHOFETIM 19:1; SHOFETIM 18:2–3"> And they passed thence unto har Ephrayim, and came unto the bais Michah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHOFETIM 17:5; SHOFETIM 18:3–4; SHMUEL ALEF 14:28"> Then answered the five anashim that went to spy out ha'aretz Layish, and said unto their achim, Do ye know that there is in these batim ephod, teraphim, pesel, and maskhah? Now therefore consider what ye have to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 43:27; SHMUEL ALEF 17:22; YOCHANAN 14:27"> And they turned thitherward, and came to the bais hana'ar haLevi, even unto the bais Michah, and gave him a shalom greeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHOFETIM 18:11"> And the 600 men armed with their keli milchamah, which were of the Bnei Dan, stood by the petach hasha'ar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHOFETIM 18:14; SHOFETIM 17:4–5; SHOFETIM 18:2"> And the five anashim that went to spy out ha'aretz went up, and came in thither, and took the pesel, and the ephod, and the teraphim, and the masekhah; and the kohen stood in the petach hasha'ar with the 600 men that were armed with keli milchamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And these went into bais Michah, and fetched the pesel, the ephod, and the teraphim, and the masakhah. Then said the kohen unto them, What are ye doing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="IYOV 29:9; IYOV 21:5; SHOFETIM 17:10"> And they said unto him, Hold thy peace, lay thine yad upon thy mouth, and come with us, and be to us an av and a kohen; is it better for thee to be a kohen unto the bais ish echad, or that thou be a kohen unto a shevet and a mishpakhat in Yisroel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YESHAYAH 56:11; YECHEZKEL 13:19; MISHLE 30:15"> And the lev hakohen was glad, and he took the ephod, and the teraphim, and the pesel, and went in the midst of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So they turned and departed, and put the little ones and the mikneh and the belongings in front of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And when they were a good way from the bais Michah, the men that were in the batim (houses) near to bais Michah were gathered together, and overtook the Bnei Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="TEHILLIM 114:5; SHMUEL BAIS 14:5; SHMUEL ALEF 11:5"> And they cried unto the Bnei Dan. And they turned their faces, and said unto Michah, What aileth thee, that thou comest with such a company?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="CHABAKUK 2:18–19; YESHAYAH 44:18–20; TEHILLIM 115:8"> And he said, Ye have taken away my g-ds which I made, and the kohen, and ye are gone away; and what have I more? And what is this that ye say unto me, What aileth thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="IYOV 27:2; IYOV 3:5; SHMUEL ALEF 30:6"> And the Bnei Dan said unto him, Let not thy voice be heard among us, lest angry anashim run upon thee, and thou lose thy nefesh, with the nefesh of thy household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the Bnei Dan went their way; and when Michah saw that they were chazakim, too strong for him, he turned and went back unto his bais.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="By now we are seeing the point of the story, which is how Dan became infected with apostate religion; see their omission Rv 7">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:47; SHOFETIM 18:7; SHOFETIM 18:10"> And they took the things which Michah had made, and the kohen which he had, and came unto Layish unto a people that were peaceful and unsuspecting and they struck them with the edge of the cherev, and burnt the ir with eish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 10:6; SHOFETIM 18:7; BAMIDBAR 13:21"> And there was no matzil (deliverer, rescuer), because it was far from Tzidon, and they had no tie with adam (any man); and it was in the valley that lieth near Beit-Rechov. And they built an ir, and dwelt therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:47; BERESHIS 14:14; MELACHIM ALEF 15:20"> And they called the shem of the Ir Dan, after the shem of Dan their av, who was born unto Yisroel; howbeit the shem of the Ir was Layish previously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 4:10–11; SHOFETIM 13:1; SHOFETIM 17:5"> And the Bnei Dan set up the pesel; and Yehonatan ben Gershom ben Menasheh, he and his banim were kohanim to the shevet haDani until the yom Golus HaAretz.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="722 B.C.E.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 18:1; SHMUEL ALEF 1:3; SHOFETIM 19:18"> During all the time that the Bais HaElohim was in Shiloh they set up for them pesel Michah, which he made.</span>
