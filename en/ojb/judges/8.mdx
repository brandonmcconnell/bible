<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 19:41; SHOFETIM 12:1–6; IYOV 5:2"> And the Ish Ephrayim said unto him, Why hast thou served us thus that thou calledst us not, when thou wentest to fight with Midyan? And they did criticize him sharply.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:4–7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:2–3; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:13–18"> And he said unto them, What have I done now in comparison with you? Is not the gleaning of the grapes of Ephrayim tov (better) than the vintage of Avi'ezer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MISHLE 15:1; SHOFETIM 7:24–25; YOCHANAN 4:37"> Elohim hath delivered into your hands the sarim of Midyan, Orev and Ze'ev; and what was I able to do in comparison with you? Then their anger was abated toward him, when he had said that.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:16; SHMUEL ALEF 14:28–29"> And Gid'on came to the Yarden, and passed over, he, and the three hundred haIsh that were with him, exhausted yet pursuing them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BERESHIS 33:17; TEHILLIM 60:6; SHMUEL ALEF 25:18"> And he said unto the Anshei Sukkot, Give, now, loaves of lechem unto the people that follow me; for they be exhausted, and I am pursuing after Zevach and Tzalmunna, melachim of Midyan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 20:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:21; MISHLE 18:23"> And the sarei Sukkot said, Are the hands of Zevach and Tzalmunna now in thine yad, that we should give lechem unto thine tz'va (army)?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHOFETIM 7:15"> And Gid'on said, Therefore when Hashem hath delivered Zevach and Tzalmunna into mine yad, then I will tear your basar with the kotz (thorns) of the midbar and with briers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 32:30–31; MELACHIM ALEF 12:25"> And he went up from there to Penuel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Peniel">a</a></sup>and spoke unto them likewise; and the Anshei Penuel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Peniel">b</a></sup>answered him as the men of Sukkot had answered him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHOFETIM 8:17; MELACHIM ALEF 22:27–28"> And he spoke also unto the Anshei Penuel, saying, When I come again in shalom, I will break down this migdal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHOFETIM 7:12; SHOFETIM 20:25; SHOFETIM 20:2"> Now Zevach and Tzalmunna were in Karkor, and their machaneh with them, about 15,000, all that were left of all the machaneh of the Bnei Kedem; for there fell a 120,000 Ish that drew the cherev.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:35; BAMIDBAR 32:42; SHOFETIM 18:27"> And Gid'on went up by the derech of them that dwelt in ohalim on the east of Novach and Yogbehah, and struck down the machaneh; for the machaneh was unsuspecting, thinking themselves secure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="TEHILLIM 83:11; AMOS 2:14; IYOV 12:16–21"> And when Zevach and Tzalmunna fled, he pursued after them, and took the two melachim of Midyan, Zevach and Tzalmunna, and did rout all the machaneh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Gid'on ben Yoash returned from milchamah by the Cheres Pass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHOFETIM 1:24–25; SHMUEL ALEF 30:11–15"> And caught a na'ar of the Anshei Sukkot, and inquired of him; and he wrote down for him the sarim of Sukkot, and the zekenim thereof, even threescore and seventeen ish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHOFETIM 8:6–7"> And he came unto the Anshei Sukkot, and said, Hinei, Zevach and Tzalmunna, with whom ye did upbraid me, saying, Are the hands of Zevach and Tzalmunna now in thine yad, that we should give lechem unto thy anashim that are exhausted?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHOFETIM 8:7; EZRA 2:6; MISHLE 10:13"> And he took the zekenim of the Ir, kotz of the midbar and briers, and with them he taught the Anashim of Sukkot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHOFETIM 8:9; MELACHIM ALEF 12:25"> And he pulled down the migdal of Penuel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Peniel">c</a></sup>and put to death the Anshei HaIr.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHOFETIM 4:6; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:16; TEHILLIM 89:12"> Then said he unto Zevach and Tzalmunna, What manner of men were they whom ye slaughtered at Tavor? And they answered, As thou art, so were they; each one with the bearing of Bnei HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And he said, They were my brethren, even the Bnei Immi; as Hashem liveth, if ye had saved them alive, I should not have put you to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 149:9; SHMUEL ALEF 15:33; YEHOSHUA 10:24"> And he said unto Yeter his bechor, Up, and slay them. But the na'ar (youth) drew not his cherev; for he feared, because he was yet a na'ar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="TEHILLIM 83:11; SHOFETIM 8:26; YESHAYAH 3:18"> Then Zevach and Tzalmunna said, Rise thou, and fall upon us; for as the ish is, so is his gevurah. And Gid'on arose, and put to death Zevach and Tzalmunna, and took away the ornaments that were on their camelsʼ necks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:15; SHOFETIM 9:8–15; SHMUEL ALEF 12:12"> Then the Ish Yisroel said unto Gid'on, Rule thou over us, both thou, and thy ben, and the ben of thy ben also; for thou hast delivered us from the yad Midyan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YESHAYAH 33:22; SHMUEL ALEF 12:12; SHMUEL ALEF 10:19"> And Gid'on said unto them, I will not rule over you, neither shall beni rule over you; Hashem shall rule over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:13; BERESHIS 37:25; BERESHIS 37:28"> And Gid'on said unto them, I would desire a request of you, that ye would give me every ish the nezem (earring) of his booty — for they had nizmei zahav (gold earrings), because they were Yishm'elim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="descendants of Ishmael">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And they answered, We will willingly give them. And they spread out a simlah (upper garment), and did cast therein every ish the nezem of his booty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="HISGALUS 18:16; YOCHANAN 19:2; HISGALUS 18:12"> And the weight of the nizmei hazahav that he requested was 1,700 shekels of zahav; beside ornaments, and pendants, and purple raiment that was on the melachim of Midyan, and apart from the chains that were about their camelsʼ necks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHOFETIM 17:5; SHOFETIM 18:14; TEHILLIM 106:39"> And Gid'on made an ephod thereof, and put it in his Ir, even in Ophrah; and kol Yisroel went there a-whoring after it; which thing became a mokesh (snare) unto Gid'on, and to his Bais.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHOFETIM 5:31; SHOFETIM 3:11; SHOFETIM 3:30"> Thus was Midyan subdued before the Bnei Yisroel, so that they lifted up their heads no more. And HaAretz was in quietness arba'im shanah in the days of Gid'on.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHOFETIM 6:32; SHOFETIM 7:1; SHMUEL ALEF 12:11"> And Yeruba'al ben Yoash went and dwelt in his own bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="SHOFETIM 9:5; SHOFETIM 9:2; MELACHIM ALEF 11:3"> And Gid'on had threescore and ten banim of his own; for he had many nashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHOFETIM 9:18; BERESHIS 22:24; BERESHIS 16:15"> And his pilegesh (concubine) that was in Shechem, she also bore him a ben, whose shem he called Avimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:8; SHOFETIM 6:24; IYOV 5:26"> And Gid'on ben Yoash died in a good old age, and was buried in the kever of Yoash his av, in Ophrah of the Aviezri.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="SHOFETIM 9:46; SHOFETIM 2:19; SHOFETIM 9:4"> And it came to pass, as soon as Gid'on was dead, that the Bnei Yisroel turned again, and went a-whoring after Ba'alim, and made Ba'al-Brit their elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:42; TEHILLIM 106:21; TEHILLIM 78:11"> And the Bnei Yisroel remembered not Hashem Eloheihem, who had delivered them out of the hands of all their enemies on every side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="KOHELET 9:14–15; SHOFETIM 9:5; SHOFETIM 9:16–19"> Neither showed they chesed to the Bais Yeruba'al, namely, Gid'on, according to all the tovah which he had showed unto Yisroel.</span>
</div>
