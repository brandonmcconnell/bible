<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Davar that came to Yirmeyah from Hashem saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 11:6; SHEMOT 19:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:10"> Hear ye the Divrei HaBrit Hazot, and speak unto the Ish Yehudah, and to the inhabitants of Yerushalayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEVARIM 27:26; DEVARIM 28:15–68; DEVARIM 29:19–20"> And say thou unto them, Thus saith Hashem Elohei Yisroel; Arur (cursed) be the ish that obeyeth not the Divrei HaBrit Hazot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:12; YIRMEYAH 7:22–23; DEVARIM 4:20"> Which I commanded Avoteichem in the day that I brought them forth out from Eretz Mitzrayim, from the kur habarzel (the furnace of iron), saying, Obey My voice, and do all which I command you; so shall ye be My people, and I will be your Elohim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:12–13; TEHILLIM 105:9–11; YIRMEYAH 28:6"> That I may perform the shevuah (oath) which I have sworn unto Avoteichem to give them an Eretz zavat cholov udevash as it is this day. Then answered I, and said, Omein, Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:13; YIRMEYAH 7:2"> Then Hashem said unto me, Proclaim all these Devarim in the towns of Yehudah, and in the streets of Yerushalayim, saying, Hear ye the Divrei HaBrit Hazot, and do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:17; SHMUEL ALEF 8:9"> For I earnestly warned Avoteichem in the day that I brought them up out of Eretz Mitzrayim, even unto this day, rising early and warning, saying, Obey My voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 7:24; YIRMEYAH 7:26; YIRMEYAH 35:15"> Yet they obeyed not, nor inclined their ozen (ear), but walked every one in the sherirut (stubbornness) of their lev harah; therefore I brought upon them all the Divrei HaBrit Hazot, which I commanded them to do; but they did them not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HOSHEA 6:9; MATTITYAHU 26:3–4; MICHOH 3:11"> And Hashem said unto me: A kesher (conspiracy) is found among the Ish Yehudah, and among the inhabitants of Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEVARIM 31:16; YECHEZKEL 16:59; TEHILLIM 78:8–10"> They are turned back to the avonot Avotam HaRishonim, which refused to hear My words; and they went after elohim acherim to serve them; Bais Yisroel and Bais Yehudah have broken My Brit which I cut with Avotam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 8:18; MISHLE 1:28; YESHAYAH 1:15"> Therefore thus saith Hashem, Look! I will bring ra'ah (evil, disaster) upon them, which they shall not be able to escape; and though they shall cry out unto Me, I will not pay heed unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 2:28; DEVARIM 32:37; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:22"> Then shall the towns of Yehudah and inhabitants of Yerushalayim go, and cry out unto the elohim unto whom they offer ketoret; but they shall not save them at all in the time of their ra'ah (disaster).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 2:28; MELACHIM BAIS 23:13; YESHAYAH 2:8"> For as many as the mispar (number) of thy towns were thy elohim, O Yehudah; and according to the mispar (number) of the streets of Yerushalayim have ye set up mizbechot to that boshet (shameful ),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="idol">a</a></sup>even mizbechot to burn ketoret unto Ba'al.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 7:16; YIRMEYAH 11:11; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:16"> Therefore pray not thou for HaAm Hazot, neither lift up a rinnah (entreaty) or tefillah on their behalf; for I will not hear them in the time that they cry unto Me for their ra'ah (disaster).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MISHLE 2:14; LETTER TO TITOS 1:15; YIRMEYAH 3:1–2"> What hath My yadid (beloved)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See 12:7">b</a></sup>to do in Mine Bais,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="HaMikdash">c</a></sup>seeing she hath with rabbim (many) wrought clever &#91;ritual&#93; schemes — can basar kodesh turn away from thee thy disaster, that thou can rejoice?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:17–24; YESHAYAH 27:11; TEHILLIM 52:8"> Hashem called thy shem, A green zayit (olive tree), yafeh, and of goodly pri (fruit); with the noise of a great tumult He hath kindled eish upon it, and the branches of it are broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YESHAYAH 5:2; YIRMEYAH 2:21; YIRMEYAH 12:2"> For Hashem Tzva'os, that planted thee, hath pronounced ra'ah against thee, for the evil of Bais Yisroel and of Bais Yehudah, which they have done against themselves to provoke Me to anger in offering ketoret (incense) unto Ba'al.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 23:11–12; MELACHIM BAIS 6:9–10; YECHEZKEL 8:6–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">And Hashem hath given me da'as (knowledge) of it, and I know it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">then Thou opened my eyes to their deeds.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:7–8; YIRMEYAH 20:10; TEHILLIM 83:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I was like an innocent keves (lamb)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that is brought to the slaughter;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I knew not that</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they had devised machashavot (plots) against me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">saying: Let us destroy the etz (tree) with the fruit thereof,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and let us cut him off from Eretz Chayyim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that shmo may be no more remembered.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 20:12; YIRMEYAH 17:10; SHMUEL ALEF 16:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">But, Hashem Tzva'os, Shofet Tzedek,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that testeth the kelayot (kidneys) and the lev,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">let me see Thy vengeance on them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for unto Thee have I committed my cause.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 20:10; AMOS 2:12; LUKAS 13:33–34"> Therefore thus saith Hashem of the anshei Anatot, that seek thy life, saying, Prophesy not B'Shem Hashem, that thou die not by our yad;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 18:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:17; YIRMEYAH 9:21"> Therefore thus saith Hashem Tzva'os, Look! I will punish them; the bochurim shall die by the cherev; their banim and their banot shall die by ra'av (famine);</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 23:12; MICHOH 7:4; HOSHEA 9:7"> And there shall be no she'erit (remnant) of them; for I will bring ra'ah (evil) upon the Anshei Anatot, even the year of their visitation.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="i.e., divine visitation for punishment">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
