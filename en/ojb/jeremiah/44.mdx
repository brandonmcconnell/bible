<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 46:14; YESHAYAH 11:11; SHEMOT 14:2"> The davar that came to Yirmeyah concerning all the Yehudim which dwell in Eretz Mitzrayim, which dwell at Migdol, and at Tachpanches, and at Noph, and in the country of Patros, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YESHAYAH 6:11; YIRMEYAH 9:11; YIRMEYAH 34:22"> Thus saith Hashem Tzva'os Elohei Yisroel; Ye have seen all the ra'ah that I have brought upon Yerushalayim, and upon all the towns of Yehudah; and, behold, this day they are a ruin, and no one dwelleth therein,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEVARIM 29:26; DEVARIM 13:6; DEVARIM 32:17"> Because of their ra'ah (evil) which they have committed to provoke Me to anger, in that they went to burn ketoret, and to serve elohim acherim, whom they knew not, neither they, ye, nor avoteichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 26:5; YIRMEYAH 7:25; YIRMEYAH 7:13"> Howbeit I sent unto you all My avadim the Nevi'im, rising early and sending them, saying, Oh, do not this to'evah (abominable thing) that I hate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 11:10; YIRMEYAH 19:13; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:16"> But they paid heed not, nor inclined their ozen to turn from their ra'ah (wickedness), to burn no ketoret unto elohim acherim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 7:20; YIRMEYAH 21:5; YESHAYAH 51:17"> Wherefore My wrath and Mine anger was poured forth, and was kindled in the towns of Yehudah and in the streets of Yerushalayim; and they are become a desolate ruin, as at this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="CHABAKUK 2:10; YIRMEYAH 51:22; YECHEZKEL 33:11"> Therefore now thus saith Hashem Elohei Tzva'os Elohei Yisroel: Why commit ye this ra'ah gedolah against your nefashot, to cut off from you ish and isha, olel (child) and yonek (infant), out of Yehudah, to leave you no she'erit (remnant);</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 42:18; YIRMEYAH 25:6–7; YIRMEYAH 44:3"> In that ye provoke Me unto wrath with the works of your hands, burning ketoret unto elohim acherim in Eretz Mitzrayim, where ye be gone to dwell, that ye might cut yourselves off, and that ye might be a kelalah (curse) and a cherpah (reproach) among kol HaGoyim of ha'aretz?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 7:17–18; YEHOSHUA 22:17–20; YIRMEYAH 44:15–19"> Have ye forgotten the ra'ot (wickednesses) of avoteichem, and the ra'ot of the melachim of Yehudah, and the ra'ot of their nashim, and your own ra'ot, and the ra'ot of your nashim, which they have committed in Eretz Yehudah, and in the streets of Yerushalayim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MISHLE 28:14; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:6; HISGALUS 15:4"> They are not contrite even unto this day, neither have they feared, nor walked in My torah, nor in My chukkot, that I set before you and before avoteichem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 21:10; VAYIKRA 26:17; VAYIKRA 20:5–6"> Therefore thus saith Hashem Tzva'os Elohei Yisroel: Hineni, I will set My face against you for ra'ah, and to cut off kol Yehudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 42:15–18; YIRMEYAH 42:22; YESHAYAH 65:15"> And I will take the she'erit (remnant) of Yehudah, that have set their faces to go into Eretz Mitzrayim to sojourn there, and they shall all be consumed, and fall in Eretz Mitzrayim; they shall even be consumed by the cherev and by the ra'av (famine); they shall die, from the katon even unto the gadol, by the cherev and by the ra'av; and they shall be an object of cursing, and a horror, and a kelalah, and a cherpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 11:22; YIRMEYAH 43:11; YIRMEYAH 44:27–28"> For I will punish them that dwell in Eretz Mitzrayim, just as I have punished Yerushalayim, by the cherev, by the ra'av, and by the dever;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:27; YIRMEYAH 22:26–27; YIRMEYAH 42:17"> So that none of the she'erit (remnant) of Yehudah, which are gone into Eretz Mitzrayim to sojourn there, shall escape or survive, that they should return into Eretz Yehudah, to the which they lift up &#91;in desire&#93; their nefesh to return to dwell there; for none shall return but such as shall escape.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MISHLE 11:21; YIRMEYAH 5:1–5; BERESHIS 19:4"> Then all the anashim which knew that their nashim had burned ketoret unto elohim acherim, and all the nashim that stood by, a kahal gadol (great multitude), even kol HaAm that dwelt in Eretz Mitzrayim, in Patros, answered Yirmeyah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="IYOV 21:14–15; LUKAS 19:27; YIRMEYAH 16:15–17"> As for the davar that thou hast spoken unto us b'Shem Hashem, we will not pay heed unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 7:18; NECHEMYAH 9:34; YIRMEYAH 44:21"> But we will certainly do whatsoever thing goeth forth out of our own mouth, to burn ketoret unto the Malkat HaShomayim (the Queen of Heaven), and to pour out nesakhim (drink offerings) unto her, as we have done, we, and Avoteinu, our Melachim, and our Sarim, in the towns of Yehudah, and in the streets of Yerushalayim; for then we had plenty of lechem, and were tovim (well off), and saw no ra'ah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MALACHI 3:13–15; BAMIDBAR 11:5–6; IYOV 21:14–15"> But since we left off burning ketoret to the Malkat HaShomayim, and to pour out nesakhim unto her, we have lacked all things, and have been consumed by the cherev and by the ra'av.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 7:18; YIRMEYAH 44:15; MELACHIM ALEF 21:25"> And when we burned ketoret to the Malkat HaShomayim, and poured out nesakhim unto her, did we make for her in her image cakes, and pour out nesakhim unto her, without our anashim?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then Yirmeyah said unto kol HaAm, to the gevarim, and to the nashim, and to kol HaAm which had given him that answer, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 44:17; YIRMEYAH 44:9; YIRMEYAH 11:13"> The ketoret that ye burned in the towns of Yehudah, and in the streets of Yerushalayim, ye, and avoteichem, your melachim, and your sarim, and the am ha'aretz, did not Hashem remember them, and came it not into His mind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 25:18; YESHAYAH 7:13; MALACHI 2:17"> So that Hashem could no longer bear, because of the wickedness of your doings, and because of the to'evot (abominations) which ye have committed; therefore is your land a desolation, and a ruin, and a kelalah, without an inhabitant, as at this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:9; DANIEL 9:11–12; NECHEMYAH 13:18"> Because ye have burned ketoret, and because ye have sinned against Hashem, and have not obeyed the voice of Hashem, nor walked in His torah, nor in His chukkot, nor in His edot; therefore this ra'ah has happened unto you, as at this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 43:7; YIRMEYAH 44:15–16; YIRMEYAH 42:15"> Moreover Yirmeyah said unto kol HaAm, and to all the nashim, Hear the Devar Hashem, all Yehudah that are in the Eretz Mitzrayim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 14:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:12–15; YESHAYAH 28:15"> Thus saith Hashem Tzva'os Elohei Yisroel, saying: Ye and your nashim have both spoken with your mouths, and fulfilled with your yad, saying, We will surely perform nedareinu (our vows) that we have vowed, to burn ketoret to the Malkat HaShomayim, and to pour out nesakhim unto her; ye will surely accomplish your nedarim (vows), and surely perform your nedarim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="TEHILLIM 50:16; YECHEZKEL 20:39; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:13"> Therefore hear ye the Devar Hashem, all Yehudah that dwell in Eretz Mitzrayim: Hineni, I have sworn biShmi HaGadol (by My great Name), saith Hashem, that Shmi shall no more be named in the mouth of any Ish Yehudah in all Eretz Mitzrayim, saying, Adonoi Hashem liveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:28; MELACHIM BAIS 21:14; YIRMEYAH 1:10"> Hineni, I will watch over them for ra'ah, and not for tovah; and Kol Ish Yehudah that are in Eretz Mitzrayim shall be consumed by the cherev and by ra'av, until there be an end of them. 1</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 44:14; YESHAYAH 27:12–13; YESHAYAH 10:19"> Yet a small number that escape the cherev shall return out of Eretz Mitzrayim into Eretz Yehudah, and all the she'erit (remnant) Yehudah, that are gone into Eretz Mitzrayim to sojourn there, shall know whose davar shall stand, Mine, or theirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MISHLE 19:21; MATTITYAHU 24:15–16; YIRMEYAH 44:30"> And this shall be the ot (sign) unto you, saith Hashem, that I will punish you in this makom, that ye may know that My Davar shall surely stand against you for ra'ah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 25:4–7; YIRMEYAH 43:9–13; YIRMEYAH 34:21"> Thus saith Hashem; Hineni, I will give Pharaoh Chophra Melech Mitzrayim into the yad of his oyevim, and into the yad of them that seek his nefesh; just as I gave Tzidkiyah Melech Yehudah into the yad of Nevukhadretzar Melech Bavel, his oyev, and that sought his nefesh (life).</span>
</div>
