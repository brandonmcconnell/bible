<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:19; YIRMEYAH 14:4; DEVARIM 28:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore the revivim (showers) have been withheld,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and there hath been no malkosh (latter rain, spring rain);</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and thou hadst a metzach isha zonah (whoreʼs forehead),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and thou refusedst to blush with shame.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 2:2; YIRMEYAH 3:19; TEHILLIM 71:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wilt thou not from this time cry unto Me:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Avi, Thou art the Alluf (Guide, Friend) of my neurim (youth)?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MICHOH 7:3; YESHAYAH 57:16; YECHEZKEL 22:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will He reserve His anger l'olam?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Will He keep it lanetzach?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Hinei, this is what you preach,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but you practice all hara'ot (the evils) you can.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 17:7–17; YECHEZKEL 16:31; YIRMEYAH 7:24"> Hashem said also unto me in the days of Yoshiyah HaMelech, Hast thou seen that which meshuvah Yisroel (backsliding Yisroel) hath done? She is gone up upon every high hill and under every green tree, and there hath played the zonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 16:46; HOSHEA 6:1–4; HOSHEA 14:1"> And I said after she had done all these things, Turn thou unto Me. But she returned not. And her treacherous achot Yehudah saw it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., saw Israelʼs refusal to renounce fertility cult idolatry">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEVARIM 24:1; HOSHEA 4:15–17; YESHAYAH 50:1"> And I saw, when for all the causes whereby meshuvah Yisroel (backsliding Yisroel) committed adultery, I had put her away, and given her a sefer keritut (bill of divorce); yet her treacherous achot Yehudah feared not, but went and played the zonah also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 2:27; YESHAYAH 57:6; YIRMEYAH 3:2"> And it came to pass through the lightness with which she<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Yisroel">b</a></sup>took her zenut (whoredom), that she defiled HaAretz, and committed adultery with HaEven (the Stone)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="of the fertility cult of Ba'al worship">c</a></sup>and with HaEtz (the Wood).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="of the fertility cult of Ba'al worship">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HOSHEA 7:14; YIRMEYAH 12:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:33–18"> And yet for all this her treacherous achot Yehudah hath not returned unto Me with her kol lev, but in sheker (falsity, pretense, an insincere religious spirit), saith Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 23:11; YECHEZKEL 16:51–52; HOSHEA 11:7"> And Hashem said unto me, Meshuvah Yisroel hath come nearer to justifying herself than treacherous Yehudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="TEHILLIM 86:15; YECHEZKEL 33:11; DEVARIM 4:29–31"> Go and proclaim these devarim toward the north, and say:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Return, thou meshuvah Yisroel,</span>saith Hashem;<br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will not cast down Mine anger to fall upon you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for I am chasid (displaying chesed, mercy),</span>saith Hashem,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will not keep anger l'olam.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 2:25; YIRMEYAH 3:6; DEVARIM 12:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Only acknowledge thine avon (iniquity, guilt of wrongdoing),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that thou hast rebelled against Hashem your G-d,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and hast scattered thy favors to the zarim</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">under every green tree,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and ye have not obeyed My voice,</span>saith Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HOSHEA 2:19–20; YIRMEYAH 2:19; YIRMEYAH 31:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Shuvu (Turn), O banim shovaviv (backsliding children),</span>saith Hashem;<br /><span className="pl pl-1">for I am married unto you:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will take you one from a town,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and two from a mishpakhah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will bring you to Tziyon:</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 23:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:11–12"> And I will give you ro'im according to Mine lev, which shall feed you with da'as and seichel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YESHAYAH 65:17; AMOS 9:14–15; YECHEZKEL 37:26"> And it shall come to pass, when ye be multiplied and increased in HaAretz, in those days, saith Hashem, they shall say no more, The Aron Brit Hashem: neither shall it come to mind: neither shall they remember it; neither shall they ask for it; neither shall another be fashioned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 43:7; MICHOH 4:1–5; YIRMEYAH 17:12"> At that time they shall call Yerushalayim the Kisse Hashem; and all the Goyim shall be gathered unto it, to the Shem Hashem, to Yerushalayim; neither shall they walk any more after the sherirut libam harah (the stubbornness of their evil heart).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:8; HOSHEA 1:11; AMOS 9:15"> In those days Bais Yehudah shall walk with Bais Yisroel, and they shall come together out of the eretz tzafon to HaAretz that I have given for a nachalah unto Avoteichem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 3:4; YIRMEYAH 31:9; YESHAYAH 63:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I said:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">How can I set thee among the banim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and give thee an eretz chemdah (a land of desire),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the most beautiful nachalah of the Goyim?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And I said: Thou shalt call Me, Avi;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and shalt not turn back from following Me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YESHAYAH 48:8; YIRMEYAH 5:11; HOSHEA 5:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Surely as an isha treacherously departeth from her husband,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so have ye dealt treacherously with Me, O Bais Yisroel,</span>saith Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:10; YIRMEYAH 31:9; YIRMEYAH 2:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">A voice was heard upon the high places,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">weeping and tachanunei Bnei Yisroel (supplications of the Children of Israel);</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for they have perverted their derech,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they have forgotten Hashem Eloheihem.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HOSHEA 14:1; HOSHEA 14:4; ZECHARYAH 13:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Shuvu (return), ye banim shovavim (backsliding children),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will heal your meshuvot (backslidings).</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Behold, we come unto Thee;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for Thou art Hashem Eloheinu.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="TEHILLIM 121:1–2; TEHILLIM 3:8; YESHAYAH 12:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Truly in sheker is Salvation hoped for from the hills,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and from the multitude of mountains;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">truly in Hashem Eloheinu</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">is the Teshu'at Yisroel (Salvation of Israel).</span></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="HOSHEA 9:10; YECHEZKEL 16:63; YECHEZKEL 16:61"> For HaBoshet (the Shame),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="i.e. the Ba'al fertility cult false religion">e</a></sup>hath devoured the labor of Avoteinu from ne'ureinu (our youth): their tzon and their bakar, their banim and their banot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 22:21; MISHLE 5:13; YESHAYAH 50:11"> We lie down in our Boshet (Shame), and kelimateinu (our humiliation) covereth us; for we have sinned against Hashem Eloheinu, we and Avoteinu, from our youth even unto this day, and have not obeyed the voice of Hashem Eloheinu.</span>
