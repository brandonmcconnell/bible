<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then the Devar Hashem came to me, saying,</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 1:10; YIRMEYAH 12:14–17; YIRMEYAH 45:4"> At what instant I shall speak concerning a Goy (nation), and concerning a mamlachah (kingdom), to uproot, and to pull down, and to destroy it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:21; YIRMEYAH 26:13; YONAH 3:9–10"> If that Goy, against whom I have pronounced, turn from their ra'ah, I will relent of the ra'ah that I planned to do unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:28; AMOS 9:11–15; YIRMEYAH 1:10"> And at what instant I shall speak concerning a Goy, and concerning a mamlachah, to build and to plant it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 33:18; SHMUEL ALEF 2:30; YECHEZKEL 18:24"> If it do ra'ah in My sight, that it obey not My voice, then I will relent of the tov, wherewith I said I would benefit them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 17:13; YESHAYAH 55:6–7; YIRMEYAH 35:15"> Now therefore go to, speak to the Ish Yehudah, and to the inhabitants of Yerushalayim, saying, Thus saith Hashem; Hinei, I yotzer (mold as a potter) ra'ah against you, and devise a plan against you; return ye now every ish from his derech ra'ah, and make your ways and your doings good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 2:25; YIRMEYAH 7:24; DEVARIM 29:19"> And they said, It is useless; but we will walk according to our own plans, and we will every ish do the sherirut (stubbornness) of his lev ra'ah.</span>
