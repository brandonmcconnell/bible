<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 40:8; YIRMEYAH 40:6; TEHILLIM 41:9"> Now it came to pass in the seventh month, that Yishmael ben Netanyah ben Elishama, who was zera hameluchah, and the officers of HaMelech, even ten anashim with him, came unto Gedalyahu ben Achikam to Mitzpah; and there they did eat lechem together in Mitzpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 25:25; YIRMEYAH 40:5; TEHILLIM 109:5"> Then arose Yishmael ben Netanyah, and the ten anashim that were with him, and struck down Gedalyahu ben Achikam ben Shaphan with the cherev, and killed him, whom Melech Bavel had made governor over ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 25:25; EKHAH 1:2; YIRMEYAH 41:11–12"> Yishmael also killed kol haYehudim that were with him, even with Gedalyahu, at Mitzpah, and the Kasdim (Chaldeans) that were found there, and the anshei hamilchamah (men of war).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 27:11; TEHILLIM 52:1–2"> It came to pass on the second day after he had slain Gedalyahu and no man knew it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 16:24; DEVARIM 14:1; YEHOSHUA 18:1"> That there came certain men from Shechem, from Shiloh, and from Shomron, even 80 men, their beards shaven, their clothes torn, having cut themselves, with minchah and incense in their hand, to bring them to the Beis Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:16; YIRMEYAH 50:4; SHMUEL BAIS 1:2–16"> And Yishmael ben Netanyah went forth from Mitzpah to meet them, weeping all along as he went; and it came to pass, as he met them, he said unto them, Come to Gedalyahu ben Achikam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YESHAYAH 59:7; TEHILLIM 55:23; YECHEZKEL 22:27"> And it was so, when they came into the midst of the Ir, that Yishmael ben Netanyah slaughtered them, and cast them into the midst of the cistern, he, and the anashim that were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="IYOV 2:4; MISHLE 13:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:7–9"> But ten anashim were found among them that said unto Yishmael, Slay us not, for we have hidden in the sadeh, chittim, se'orim, shemen, devash. So he forbore, killed them not among the others.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHOFETIM 6:2; MELACHIM ALEF 15:17–22; SHMUEL ALEF 13:6"> Now the cistern wherein Yishmael had cast all the pigrei ha'anashim (dead bodies of the men), whom he had slain because of Gedalyahu, was the same cistern which Asa HaMelech had made for defense against Ba'asha Melech Yisroel: and Yishmael ben Netanyah filled it with them that were slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 40:14; YIRMEYAH 40:7; NECHEMYAH 2:10"> Then Yishmael carried away captive all the she'erit of the people that were in Mitzpah, even the banot hamelech, and kol HaAm that remained in Mitzpah, whom Nevuzaradan the captain of the imperial guard had committed to Gedalyahu ben Achikam; and Yishmael ben Netanyah carried them away captive, and departed to go over to the Bnei Ammon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 40:13–16; YIRMEYAH 40:7–8; YIRMEYAH 42:1"> When Yochanan ben Kareach with all the officers of the forces that were with him, heard of all the evil Yishmael ben Netanyah had done,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 2:13; BERESHIS 14:14–16; SHMUEL ALEF 30:1–8"> Then they took kol ha'anashim, and went to fight with Yishmael ben Netanyah, and found him by the mayim rabbim that are in Giv'on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Now it came to pass, that when kol haAm which were with Yishmael saw Yochanan ben Kareach, and all the officers of the forces that were with him, then they were glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So kol haAm that Yishmael had carried away captive from Mitzpah turned and went over to Yochanan ben Kareach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="IYOV 21:30; MISHLE 28:17; MELACHIM ALEF 20:20"> But Yishmael ben Netanyah escaped from Yochanan with eight anashim, and went over to the Bnei Ammon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 43:4–7; YIRMEYAH 42:8; YIRMEYAH 41:10"> Then took Yochanan ben Kareach, and all the officers of the forces that were with him, all the she'erit haAm whom he had recovered from Yishmael ben Netanyah, from Mitzpah, after that he had slain Gedalyah ben Achikam, even gevarim anshei hamilchamah, and the nashim, and the children, and the sarisim, whom he had brought from Giv'on;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 19:37–38; YIRMEYAH 42:14; YIRMEYAH 43:7"> And they departed, and dwelt in the Gerut Kimham, which is near Beit-Lechem, intending to go to Mitzrayim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 40:5; LUKAS 12:4–5; YIRMEYAH 42:16"> Because of the Kasdim (Chaldeans); for they were afraid of them, because Yishmael ben Netanyah had slain Gedalyahu ben Achikam, whom Melech Bavel made governor in ha'aretz.</span>
</div>
