<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 29:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:10; MELACHIM BAIS 24:12–16"> Hashem showed me, and, hinei, two baskets of figs were set in front of the Heikhal Hashem; after that Nevukhadretzar Melech Bavel had carried away into the Golus Melech Yehudah Yechanyah ben Y'hoyakim, and the sarim (princes) of Yehudah, with the craftsmen and artisans, from Yerushalayim, and had brought them to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YESHAYAH 5:7; YESHAYAH 5:4; MICHOH 7:1"> One basket had figs tovot me'od, even like the figs that are early ripening; and the other basket had figs ra'ot me'od, which could not be eaten, they were so bad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="AMOS 8:2; ZECHARYAH 5:5–11; MATTITYAHU 25:32–33"> Then said Hashem unto me, What seest thou, Yirmeyah? And I answered, Figs; the good figs, very good; and the bad, very bad, that cannot be eaten they are so bad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Again the Devar Hashem came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 13:9; NACHUM 1:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:9"> Thus saith Hashem Elohei Yisroel: Like these good figs, so will I acknowledge them that of Yehudah are carried away unto the Golus, whom I have sent out of this place into the Eretz Kasdim (Chaldeans) for their good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 42:10; YIRMEYAH 29:10; YIRMEYAH 33:7"> For I will set mine eyes over them for tovah, and I will bring them again to ha'aretz hazot; and I will build them up, and not tear them down; and I will plant them, and not uproot them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:10; YIRMEYAH 30:22; YECHEZKEL 36:24–28"> And I will give them a lev to have da'as of me, that I am Hashem; and they shall be My people, and I will be their Elohim; for they shall return unto Me with all their lev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 43:1; YIRMEYAH 39:2–9; YIRMEYAH 24:2"> And as the bad figs, which cannot be eaten, they are so bad, surely thus saith Hashem: So will I give Tzidkiyah Melech Yehudah, and his sarim (princes), and the she'erit Yerushalayim, that remain in ha'aretz hazot, and them that dwell in Eretz Mitzrayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 15:4; YIRMEYAH 29:18; DEVARIM 28:37"> And I will deliver them for abhorrence, for an offense to kol mamlechot ha'aretz to be a reproach and a byword, a taunt and a kelalah (curse) in all places where I shall drive them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YESHAYAH 51:19; YECHEZKEL 5:12–17; YIRMEYAH 16:4"> And I will send the cherev, the ra'av (famine), and the dever (pestilence), among them, until they are consumed from off ha'aretz that I gave unto them and to their avot.</span>
