<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 29:25; YIRMEYAH 37:3; YIRMEYAH 52:24"> The Davar which came unto Yirmeyah from Hashem, when HaMelech Tzidkiyah sent unto him Pashchur Ben Malkiyah, and Tzephanyah Ben Ma'aseiyah the kohen, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 37:3; YIRMEYAH 42:4–6; YIRMEYAH 37:7"> Inquire, now, of Hashem for us; for Nevukhadretzar Melech Bavel maketh war against us; if it be so that Hashem will deal with us according to all His nifle'ot (wondrous deeds), that he may withdraw from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then said Yirmeyah unto them, Thus shall ye say to Tzidkiyah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:5; YIRMEYAH 37:8–10; YESHAYAH 5:5"> Thus saith Hashem Elohei Yisroel; Hineni, I will turn back against you the weapons of milchamah (war) that are in your hands, wherewith ye fight against Melech Bavel, and against the Kasdim (Chaldeans), which besiege you outside the chomah (wall), and I will assemble them inside this city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YESHAYAH 63:10; SHEMOT 6:6; YESHAYAH 5:25"> And I Myself will fight against you with an outstretched yad (hand) and with a zero'a chazakah (strong arm), even in anger, and in chemah, and in ketzef gadol (great wrath).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TZEFANYAH 1:3; YECHEZKEL 14:13; YIRMEYAH 12:3–4"> And I will strike down the inhabitants of this city, both ha'adam and habehemah; they shall die of dever gadol (great plague).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 37:17; MELACHIM BAIS 25:5–7; CHABAKUK 1:6–10"> And afterward, saith Hashem, I will deliver Tzidkiyah Melech Yehudah, and his avadim, and HaAm, and such as are survivors in this city from dever, from the cherev, and from the ra'av (famine), into the yad Nevukhadretzar Melech Bavel, and into the yad of their oyevim (enemies), and into the yad of those that seek their nefesh; and he shall strike them down with the edge of the cherev; he shall not spare them, neither have pity, nor have rachamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEVARIM 30:19; DEVARIM 30:15; DEVARIM 11:26"> And unto this people thou shalt say, Thus saith Hashem; Hineni, I set before you the Derech HaChayyim and the Derech HaMavet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 38:2; YIRMEYAH 45:5; YIRMEYAH 39:18"> He that abideth in this city shall die by cherev, and by ra'av (famine), and by dever (plague); but he that goeth out, and falleth to the Kasdim (Chaldeans) that besiege you, he shall live, and his nefesh shall be unto him as plunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 44:11; AMOS 9:4; YIRMEYAH 44:27"> For I have set my face against this city for ra'ah, and not for tovah, saith Hashem; it shall be given into the yad Melech Bavel, and he shall set eish to it to burn it down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 17:20; MICHOH 3:1; YIRMEYAH 13:18"> And touching the Bais Melech Yehudah, say, Hear ye the Devar Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 1:17; MISHLE 31:8–9; YIRMEYAH 4:4"> O Bais Dovid, thus saith Hashem:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Execute mishpat in the boker,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and save him that is robbed</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">out of the yad oshek (hand of the oppressor),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">lest My chemah (fury) go out like eish,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and burn that none can quench it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because of the wickedness of your deeds.</span></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 13:8; EKHAH 4:12; OVADYAH 1:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hineni, I am against thee, O inhabitant of the emek (valley),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and tzur (rock) of the plain,</span>saith Hashem;<br /><span className="pl pl-1">which say, Who shall come down against us?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or who shall enter into our me'onot (dwelling places, homes)?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YESHAYAH 3:10–11; YIRMEYAH 32:19; MISHLE 1:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I will visit you in punishment according to the p'ri (fruit) of your deeds,</span>saith Hashem;<br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will kindle an eish in the ya'ar (forest) thereof,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and it shall devour all things round about it.</span></span>
