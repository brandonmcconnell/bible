<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 41:11; YIRMEYAH 40:13; YIRMEYAH 6:13"> Then all the army officers, and Yochanan Ben Kareach, and Yezanyah Ben Hoshayah, and kol HaAm from the katon even unto the gadol, came near,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 36:7; VAYIKRA 26:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:24"> And said unto Yirmeyah HaNavi, Let, now, techinnatenu (our supplication) be accepted before thee, and pray on our behalf unto Hashem Eloheicha, even on behalf of all this she'erit (remnant) — for we are left but a few of many, as thine eyes canst see —</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TEHILLIM 86:11; MISHLE 3:6; TEHILLIM 25:4–5"> That Hashem Eloheicha may show us the derech wherein we should walk, and the thing that we should do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 12:23; SHMUEL ALEF 3:17–18; SHEMOT 8:29"> Then Yirmeyah HaNavi said unto them, I have heard you; hineni, I will pray unto Hashem Eloheichem according to your devarim; and it shall come to pass, that whatsoever thing Hashem shall answer you, I will declare it unto you; I will keep nothing back from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BERESHIS 31:50; SHOFETIM 11:10; MALACHI 3:5"> Then they said to Yirmeyah, Hashem be an ed emes v'ne'eman (true and faithful witness) against us, if we do not act even according to all things for the which Hashem Eloheicha shall send thee to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 7:23; DEVARIM 5:29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:7"> Whether it be tov, or whether it be rah, we will obey the voice of Hashem Eloheinu, to Whom we send thee; that it may be well with us, when we obey the voice of Hashem Eloheinu.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 27:14; YESHAYAH 28:16; CHABAKUK 2:3"> And it came to pass after aseret yamim (ten days), that the Devar Hashem came unto Yirmeyah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 42:1; YIRMEYAH 41:11–16; YIRMEYAH 43:2–5"> Then he called Yochanan Ben Kareach, and all the army officers which were with him, and kol HaAm from the katon even to the gadol,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 19:4; YIRMEYAH 42:2; MELACHIM BAIS 19:6"> And said unto them, Thus saith Hashem Elohei Yisroel, unto Whom ye sent me to present your techinnah before Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:28; YECHEZKEL 36:36; YIRMEYAH 24:6"> If ye will still abide in this land, then will I build you up, and not pull you down, and I will plant you, and not uproot you; for I relent Me of the ra'ah that I have brought upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:31; YIRMEYAH 1:19; YIRMEYAH 1:8"> Be not afraid of Melech Bavel, of whom ye are afraid; be not afraid of him, saith Hashem; for I am with you to save you, and to deliver you from his yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 1:11; TEHILLIM 106:45–46; MISHLE 16:7"> And I will show rachamim unto you, that he will have compassion upon you, and cause you to return to your own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 44:16; SHEMOT 5:2; YIRMEYAH 42:10"> But if ye say, We will not dwell in this land, disobeying the voice of Hashem Eloheichem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 41:17; YIRMEYAH 4:19; YESHAYAH 31:1"> Saying, No; but we will go into Eretz Mitzrayim, where we shall see no milchamah, nor hear the sound of the shofar, nor have hunger for lechem; and there will we dwell;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEVARIM 17:16; YIRMEYAH 44:12–14; YIRMEYAH 42:17"> And now therefore hear the Devar Hashem, ye She'erit Yehudah (Remnant of Judah). Thus saith Hashem Tzva'os Elohei Yisroel: If ye wholly set your faces to go to Mitzrayim, and go to sojourn there, 1</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 44:27; YECHEZKEL 11:8; AMOS 9:1–4"> Then it shall come to pass, that the cherev, which ye feared, shall overtake you there in Eretz Mitzrayim, and the ra'av (famine), whereof ye were afraid, shall follow close after you there in Mitzrayim; and there ye shall die. 0</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 42:22; YIRMEYAH 44:28; YIRMEYAH 44:13–14"> So shall it be with all the anashim that set their faces to go into Mitzrayim to sojourn there; they shall die by the cherev, by the ra'av (famine), and by the dever; and none of them shall remain or escape from the ra'ah (disaster) that I will bring upon them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 7:20; YIRMEYAH 18:16; YIRMEYAH 29:18"> For thus saith Hashem Tzva'os Elohei Yisroel: As Mine anger and My wrath hath been poured forth upon the inhabitants of Yerushalayim, so shall My wrath be poured forth upon you, when ye shall enter into Mitzrayim; and ye shall be an oath, and a horror, and a kelalah (curse), and a cherpah; and ye shall see this makom no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 17:16; YESHAYAH 30:1–7; NECHEMYAH 9:26"> Hashem hath said concerning you, O ye she'erit Yehudah; Go ye not into Mitzrayim; know certainly that I have warned you, this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 14:3–4; YECHEZKEL 33:31; MATTITYAHU 22:15–18"> For ye acted deceitfully, erring in your nafshot, when ye sent me unto Hashem Eloheichem, saying, Pray on our behalf unto Hashem Eloheinu; and according unto all that Hashem Eloheinu shall say, so declare unto us, and we will do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 2:7; ZECHARYAH 7:11–12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:26–27"> And now I have this day declared it to you; but ye have not obeyed the voice of Hashem Eloheichem, nor anything for the which He hath sent me unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 42:17; YECHEZKEL 6:11; HOSHEA 9:6"> Now therefore know certainly that ye shall die by the cherev, by the ra'av (famine), and by the dever, in the makom where ye desire to go and to sojourn.</span>
</div>
