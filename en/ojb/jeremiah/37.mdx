<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 22:24; MELACHIM BAIS 24:17; MELACHIM BAIS 24:12"> And Melech Tzidkiyah Ben Yoshiyah reigned instead of Konyahu<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Yehoyakin">a</a></sup>Ben Y'hoyakim, whom Nevukhadretzar Melech Bavel put on the throne in Eretz Yehudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 24:19–20; MISHLE 29:12; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:12–16"> But neither he, nor his avadim, nor the Am HaAretz, did pay heed unto the Divrei Hashem, which He spoke by Yirmeyah HaNavi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 21:1–2; YIRMEYAH 29:25; YIRMEYAH 42:20"> And HaMelech Tzidkiyah sent Yehukhal Ben Shelemyah and Tzephanyah Ben Ma'aseiyah the Kohen to Yirmeyah HaNavi, saying, Hitpalel na (pray now) unto Hashem Eloheinu for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 37:15; YIRMEYAH 32:2–3"> Now Yirmeyah came in and went out among HaAm; for they had not put him into bais hakeleh (prison).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 17:15; YIRMEYAH 37:11; YIRMEYAH 34:21"> Then the army of Pharaoh was come forth out of Mitzrayim; and when the Kasdim (Chaldeans) that besieged Yerushalayim heard news of them, they withdrew from Yerushalayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then came the Devar Hashem unto Yirmeyah HaNavi saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EKHAH 4:17; MELACHIM BAIS 22:18; YECHEZKEL 17:17"> Thus saith Hashem Elohei Yisroel; Thus shall ye say to Melech Yehudah, that sent you unto Me to enquire of Me; Hinei, the army of Pharaoh, which is come forth l'ezrah (to help, support) you, shall return to Mitzrayim into their own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 39:2–8; YIRMEYAH 38:23; YIRMEYAH 34:21–22"> And the Kasdim (Chaldeans) shall return, and fight against HaIr hazot (this City),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., Jerusalem">b</a></sup>and capture it, and burn it down with eish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="OVADYAH 1:3; YIRMEYAH 29:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:3"> Thus saith Hashem; Deceive not nafshoteichem, saying, The Kasdim (Chaldeans) shall surely depart from us; for they shall not depart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 50:45; YESHAYAH 30:17; YOEL 2:11"> For though ye had struck down kol chayil Kasdim (the whole army of the Chaldeans) that fight against you, and there remained but anashim medukarim (wounded men) among them, yet should they rise up every ish in his ohel, and with eish burn down HaIr hazot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 37:5"> And it came to pass, that when the army of the Kasdim (Chaldeans) was withdrawn from Yerushalayim because of the army of Pharaoh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 1:1; NECHEMYAH 6:11; MELACHIM ALEF 19:3"> Then Yirmeyah went forth out of Yerushalayim to go into the Eretz Binyamin, to get &#91;his&#93; chelek from there among HaAm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 14:10; YIRMEYAH 38:7; LUKAS 23:2"> And when he was in the Sha'ar Binyamin, a ba'al pekidut (captain of the guard) was there, shmo Yiriyah Ben Shelemyah Ben Chananyah; and he arrested Yirmeyah HaNavi, saying, Thou fallest away as a deserter to the Kasdim (Chaldeans).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 52:1–2; YIRMEYAH 40:4–6; TEHILLIM 27:12"> Then said Yirmeyah, Sheker; I fall not away as a deserter to the Kasdim (Chaldeans). But he paid heed not to him; so Yiriyah arrested Yirmeyah, and brought him to the sarim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 38:26; YIRMEYAH 20:1–3; YIRMEYAH 26:16"> Therefore the sarim were enraged with Yirmeyah, and had him beaten, and put him in house arrest in the Bais Yehonatan the Sofer; for they had made that the bais hakeleh (prison).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 38:6; YIRMEYAH 38:10–13; EKHAH 3:53"> When Yirmeyah was entered into the bais habor (dungeon), and into the cells, and Yirmeyah had remained there yamim rabbim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 21:7; YIRMEYAH 24:8; MELACHIM ALEF 14:1–4"> Then HaMelech Tzidkiyah sent, and brought him out; and HaMelech asked him baseter (secretly) in his Bais (Palace), and said, Is there Devar from Hashem? And Yirmeyah said, There is; for, said he, thou shalt be delivered into the yad Melech Bavel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:8; YOCHANAN 10:32; DANIEL 6:22"> Moreover Yirmeyah said unto HaMelech Tzidkiyah, What have I offended against thee, or against thy avadim, or against Am Hazeh, that ye have put me in bais hakeleh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 2:28; YIRMEYAH 28:10–17; YIRMEYAH 6:14"> Where are now your nevi'im which prophesied unto you, saying, Melech Bavel shall not come against you, nor against HaAretz hazot?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 36:7; YIRMEYAH 38:6–9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:18–19"> Therefore hear now, O adoni HaMelech; let now my techinnah be accepted before thee; that thou cause me not to return to the Bais Yehonatan HaSofer, lest I die there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 38:13; YIRMEYAH 38:28; YIRMEYAH 38:9"> Then HaMelech Tzidkiyah commanded that they should commit Yirmeyah into the Khatzer (courtyard) of the Guard, and that they should give him daily kikar lechem (loaf of bread) out of the street of ha'ofim (the bakers), until all the lechem in the Ir was gone. Thus Yirmeyah remained in the Khatzer (courtyard) of the Guard.</span>
</div>
