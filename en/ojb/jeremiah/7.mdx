<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Devar that came to Yirmeyah from Hashem, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 26:2; HISGALUS 3:22; AMOS 7:16"> Stand at the Sha'ar Beis Hashem, and preach there this word, and say, Hear the Devar Hashem, all ye of Yehudah, that enter in at these She'arim (Gates) l'hishta'chavot (to worship, to prostrate before)Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 18:11; YIRMEYAH 26:13; YESHAYAH 1:16–19"> Thus saith Hashem Tzva'os, Elohei Yisroel, Amend your ways and your deeds, and I will cause you to dwell in makom hazeh (this place).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MICHOH 3:11; MATTITYAHU 3:9–10; TZEFANYAH 3:11"> Trust ye not in divrei hasheker (lying words, words of falsehood), saying, This is the Heikhal Hashem, the Heikhal Hashem, the Heikhal Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 6:12–13; YESHAYAH 1:19; YIRMEYAH 7:3"> For if ye throughly amend your ways and your deeds; if ye throughly dispense mishpat between an ish and his re'a (neighbor);</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:21–24; ZECHARYAH 7:9–12; YIRMEYAH 13:10"> If ye oppress not the ger, the yatom, and the almanah, and shed not dahm naki (innocent blood) in this place, neither walk after elohim acherim to your hurt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:40; YIRMEYAH 25:5; YIRMEYAH 3:18"> Then will I cause you to dwell in this place, in HaAretz that I gave to Avoteichem, l'min olam v'ad olam (from forever to forever).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YESHAYAH 28:15; YECHEZKEL 13:6–16; YIRMEYAH 23:14–16"> Hinei, ye trust in divrei sheker (lying words), that are worthless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 7:6; SHEMOT 20:3; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1–4"> Will ye steal, murder, and commit adultery, and swear l'sheker (falsely), and burn ketoret unto Ba'al, and walk after elohim acherim whom ye know not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:34; YECHEZKEL 23:37; YECHEZKEL 23:39"> And come and stand before Me in HaBeis Hazeh, which is called by Shmi, and say, We are delivered l'ma'an (in order to) do all these to'evot (abominations)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 19:45–46; YESHAYAH 56:7; MATTITYAHU 21:13"> Is HaBeis Hazeh, which is called by Shmi, become a me'arah paritzim (a cave, a den of robbers, criminals) in your eyes? Hinei, even I have been watching, saith Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 18:1; YIRMEYAH 26:6; SHMUEL ALEF 4:10–11"> But go ye now unto My Makom (Place) which was in Shiloh, where I made dwell Shmi barishonah (at the first), and see what I did to it because of the ra'at Ami Yisroel (the wickedness of My people Israel).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YESHAYAH 65:12; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:15–16; YIRMEYAH 7:25"> And now, because ye have done all these ma'asim, saith Hashem, and I spoke unto you, rising up early and speaking, but ye paid not heed; and I called you, but ye answered not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:60; MELACHIM BAIS 25:9; YIRMEYAH 7:4"> Therefore will I do unto this Beis, which is called by Shmi, wherein ye trust, and unto the place which I gave to you and to Avoteichem, as I have done to Shiloh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 15:1; MELACHIM BAIS 17:23; YIRMEYAH 52:3"> And I will cast you out of My sight, as I have cast out all your achim, even the whole zera Ephrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 11:14; YIRMEYAH 14:11–12; YIRMEYAH 15:1"> Therefore pray not thou on behalf of HaAm Hazeh, neither lift up plea nor tefillah for them, neither make intercession to Me; for I will not hear thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 8:6–18; YECHEZKEL 14:23; YIRMEYAH 6:27"> Seest thou not what they do in the towns of Yehudah and in the chutzot (streets of) Yerushalayim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 19:13; YIRMEYAH 44:17–19; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:22"> The banim gather wood, and the avot kindle the eish, and the nashim knead their batzek (dough), to make cakes to the Malkat HaShomayim (Queen of Heaven), and to pour out nesakhim (drink offerings) unto elohim acherim, that they may provoke Me to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 20:11; YIRMEYAH 2:19; DANIEL 9:7–8"> Do they provoke Me to anger? saith Hashem; are they not provoking themselves, even to boshet (shame) on their own faces?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 42:18; EKHAH 2:3–5; YESHAYAH 42:25"> Therefore thus saith Adonoi Hashem; Hinei, Mine anger and My fury shall be poured out upon Hamakom Hazeh (this place), upon haadam, and upon behemah, and upon the etz hasadeh, and upon the pri ha'adamah; and it shall burn, and shall not be quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HOSHEA 8:13; YIRMEYAH 6:20; AMOS 5:21–23"> Thus saith Hashem Tzva'os, Elohei Yisroel; Add your olot unto your zevakhim, and eat basar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HOSHEA 6:6; SHMUEL ALEF 15:22; TEHILLIM 51:16–17"> For I spoke not unto Avoteichem, nor commanded them in the day that I brought them out of Eretz Mitzrayim, concerning olah or zevach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHEMOT 15:26; DEVARIM 5:33; SHEMOT 19:5–6"> But this thing I commanded them, saying, Obey My voice, and I will be for you Elohim (your G-d), and ye shall be My people; and walk ye in kol HaDerech that I have commanded you, l'ma'an (in order that) it may go well with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="TEHILLIM 81:11–12; YIRMEYAH 8:5; YIRMEYAH 7:26"> But they paid heed not, nor inclined their ear, but walked in the inclinations of the stubbornness of their lev harah (evil heart), and went backward, and not forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:15; YIRMEYAH 25:4; NECHEMYAH 9:26"> Since the day that Avoteichem came forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim unto this day I have even sent unto you all My servants the Nevi'im, daily rising up early and sending them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 16:12; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:8; YIRMEYAH 7:24"> Yet they paid heed not unto Me, nor inclined their ear, but stiffened stubbornly their neck; they did more evil than their Avot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 26:2; YIRMEYAH 1:7; YESHAYAH 65:12"> Therefore thou shalt speak all these devarim unto them; but they will not pay heed to thee; thou shalt also call unto them; but they will not answer thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YESHAYAH 59:14–15; MISHLE 1:7; YESHAYAH 1:4–5"> But thou shalt say unto them: This is a Goy (Nation) that obeyeth not the voice of Hashem Elohav, nor respondeth to musar; haemunah (the faith) is lost, and is cut off from their mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="IYOV 1:20; MICHOH 1:16; YIRMEYAH 6:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cut off thine hair, O Yerushalayim, and throw it away,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and take up a kinah (lamentation) on barren heights;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for Hashem hath rejected and abandoned</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the dor evrato (the generation of His wrath).</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:34; YECHEZKEL 7:20; DANIEL 9:27"> For the Bnei Yehudah have done rah in My sight, saith Hashem; they have set their shikkutzim (abominations) in the Beis which is called by Shmi, to make it tameh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 23:10; YIRMEYAH 32:35; VAYIKRA 18:21"> And they have built the high places of Tophet, which is in the Gey Ben Hinnom, to burn their banim and their banot in the eish; which I commanded them not, neither entered it into My mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 19:11; YIRMEYAH 19:6; MELACHIM BAIS 23:10"> Therefore, hinei, the yamim are coming, saith Hashem, that it shall no more be called Tophet, nor the Gey Ben Hinnom, but the Gey Haharegah (Valley of Slaughter); for they shall bury in Tophet, till there be no room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 12:9; DEVARIM 28:26; HISGALUS 19:17–18"> And the nivlat HaAm Hazeh (carcass of this people) shall be food for the oph HaShomayim, and for the behemat ha'aretz; and none shall frighten them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="HISGALUS 18:23; HOSHEA 2:11; YECHEZKEL 26:13"> Then will I cause to cease from the towns of Yehudah, and from the chutzot Yerushalayim, the sound of joy, and the sound of simchah, the sound of the Choson (Bridegroom) and the sound of the Kallah (Bride); for HaAretz shall be desolate.</span>
</div>
