<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 39:1–2; MELACHIM BAIS 25:1–2; YIRMEYAH 25:1"> The Davar that came to Yirmeyah from Hashem in the tenth year of Tzidkiyah Melech Yehudah, which was the eighteenth year of Nevukhadretzar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 37:21; NECHEMYAH 3:25; YIRMEYAH 33:1"> For then the army of Melech Bavel besieged Yerushalayim; and Yirmeyah HaNavi was imprisoned in the Khatzer (courtyard) of the Guard, which was in Bais Melech Yehudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 34:2–3; YIRMEYAH 21:4–7; YIRMEYAH 26:8–9"> For Tzidkiyah Melech Yehudah had imprisoned him, saying, Why dost thou prophesy, and say, Thus saith Hashem, Hineni, I will give this city into the yad Melech Bavel, and he shall capture it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 39:4–7; MELACHIM BAIS 25:4–7; YIRMEYAH 37:17"> And Tzidkiyah Melech Yehudah shall not escape out of the yad of the Kasdim (Chaldeans), but shall surely be delivered into the yad Melech Bavel, and shall speak with him mouth to mouth, and his eynayim shall behold his eynayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 39:7; YECHEZKEL 17:9–10; YECHEZKEL 17:15"> And he shall lead Tzidkiyah to Bavel, and there shall he be until I visit him, saith Hashem; though ye fight against the Kasdim (Chaldeans), ye shall not succeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Yirmeyah said, The Devar Hashem came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 1:1; YIRMEYAH 11:21; VAYIKRA 25:25"> Hinei, Chanam'el Ben Shallum thine dod (uncle) shall come unto thee saying, Buy thee my sadeh that is in Anatot: for the mishpat haGeulah (right of Redemption) is thine to buy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:7; YIRMEYAH 32:2; YIRMEYAH 33:1"> So Chanam'el ben dodi came to me in the Khatzer (court) of the Guard according to the Devar Hashem, and said unto me, Buy my sadeh, now, that is in Anatot, which is in Eretz Binyamin; for the mishpat hayerushah (right of possession) is thine, and the geulah (redemption) is thine; buy it for thyself. Then I knew that this was the Devar Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 11:12–13; ESTHER 3:9; MELACHIM ALEF 20:39"> And I bought the sadeh of Chanam'el ben dodi, that was in Anatot, and weighed him out the kesef, even seventeen shekels of kesef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:44; YIRMEYAH 32:12; YIRMEYAH 32:25"> And I signed the sefer (deed), and sealed it, and took edim (witnesses), and weighed him out the kesef on the scales.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 2:27; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:3"> So I took the sefer hamikneh (deed of purchase), both that which was sealed according to the mitzvah and chukkim, and that which was the unsealed copy:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:16; YIRMEYAH 51:59; YIRMEYAH 36:4–5"> And I gave the sefer hamikneh unto Baruch Ben Neriyah Ben Machseiyah, in the sight of Chanam'el my cousin, and in the presence of the edim that signed the sefer hamikneh, before all the Yehudim that sat in the khatzer (courtyard) of the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And I charged Baruch before their eyes, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Thus saith Hashem Tzva'os Elohei Yisroel: Take these sefarim, this sefer hamikneh, both which is sealed, and this sefer which is unsealed; and put them in an earthen vessel, that they may last yamim rabbim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 3:10; AMOS 9:14–15; YIRMEYAH 30:18"> For thus saith Hashem Tzva'os Elohei Yisroel: Batim (houses) and sadot (fields) and kramim (vineyards) shall be bought again in this land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 12:1; BERESHIS 32:9–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:6–7"> Now when I had delivered the sefer hamikneh unto Baruch Ben Neriyah, I davened unto Hashem, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKAS 1:37; YIRMEYAH 32:27; BERESHIS 18:14"> Ah Adonoi Hashem! Hinei, Thou hast made the Shomayim and the Eretz by Thy koach hagadol and Thy outstretched zero'a, and there is nothing too difficult for Thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 10:16; DEVARIM 5:9–10; SHEMOT 34:7"> Thou showest chesed unto alafim, and recompensest avon avot into the kheyk of their banim after them; HaEl HaGadol HaGibbor Hashem Tzva'os Shmo,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 17:10; MATTITYAHU 16:27; TEHILLIM 62:12"> Gadol in etzah (counsel), and mighty in deed; for Thine eynayim are open upon all the drakhim of Bnei Adam; to give everyone according to his drakhim, and according to the fruit of his doings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 9:10; SHMUEL BAIS 7:23; SHEMOT 10:2"> Which hast set otot and mofetim in Eretz Mitzrayim, even unto this day, and in Yisroel, and among adam (mankind); and hast made Thee a Shem, as at this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 6:6; DEVARIM 26:8; TEHILLIM 105:43"> And hast brought forth Thy people Yisroel out of Eretz Mitzrayim with otot, and with mofetim, and with a yad chazakah, and with an outstretched zero'a, and with great terror;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:8; SHEMOT 13:5; YIRMEYAH 11:5"> And hast given them this land, which Thou didst swear to their avot to give them, eretz zavat cholov udevash;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZRA 9:7; TEHILLIM 78:54–55; DANIEL 9:10–14"> And they came in, and possessed it; but they obeyed not Thy voice, neither walked in Thy torah; they have done nothing of all that Thou commandedst them to do; therefore Thou hast caused all this ra'ah to come upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:36; DEVARIM 4:26; YEHOSHUA 23:15–16"> Hinei, the siege ramps, they are come unto the Ir to take it; and the Ir is given into the yad of the Kasdim (Chaldeans), that fight against it, because of the cherev, and of the ra'av, and of the dever; and what Thou hast spoken is come to pass; and, behold, Thou seest it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:24; TEHILLIM 97:2; TEHILLIM 77:19"> And Thou hast said unto me, O Adonoi Hashem, Buy thee the sadeh for kesef, and take edim (witnesses); for the Ir is given into the yad of the Kasdim (Chaldeans).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then came the Devar Hashem unto Yirmeyah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:26; YIRMEYAH 32:17; YESHAYAH 64:8"> Hinei, I am Hashem Elohei Kol Basar; is there any thing too difficult for Me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:3; YIRMEYAH 32:24; YIRMEYAH 19:7–12"> Therefore thus saith Hashem: Hineni, I will give this city into the yad of the Kasdim (Chaldeans), and into the yad of Nevukhadretzar Melech Bavel, and he shall capture it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 21:10; YIRMEYAH 19:13; YIRMEYAH 52:13"> And the Kasdim (Chaldeans), that fight against this city, shall come and set eish to this city, and burn it with the batim (houses), upon whose gagot (roofs) they have offered ketoret unto Ba'al, and poured out nesakhim (drink offerings) unto elohim acherim, to provoke Me to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 7:22–26; DEVARIM 9:7–12; YIRMEYAH 25:7"> For the Bnei Yisroel and the Bnei Yehudah have only done rah (evil) before Me from their youth; for the Bnei Yisroel have only provoked Me to anger with the work of their hands, saith Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 23:27; YIRMEYAH 27:10; MELACHIM BAIS 21:4–7"> For this city hath been to Me as a provocation of Mine anger and of My fury from the day that they built it even unto this day; that I should remove it from before My face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DANIEL 9:8; YIRMEYAH 2:26; YESHAYAH 1:4–6"> Because of all the ra'ah of the Bnei Yisroel and of the Bnei Yehudah, which they have done to provoke Me to anger, they, their melachim, their sarim, their kohanim, and their nevi'im, and the Ish Yehudah, and the inhabitants of Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 7:13; YIRMEYAH 2:27; YECHEZKEL 8:16"> And they have turned unto Me the oref (back), and not the panim; though I taught them, rising up early and teaching them, yet they have not paid heed to receive musar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 7:30; YECHEZKEL 8:5–16; MELACHIM BAIS 21:4–7"> But they set their shikkutzim (abominations) in the Beis,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hamikdash">a</a></sup>which is called by Shmi, to make it tameh (unclean).</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 7:31; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:6; VAYIKRA 18:21"> And they built the high places of Ba'al, which are in the Gey Ben Hinnom, to cause their banim and their banot to pass through the eish unto Molech; which I commanded them not, neither came it into My lev, that they should do this toe'vah (abomination) to cause Yehudah to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:24; YIRMEYAH 32:3; YIRMEYAH 32:28"> And now therefore thus saith Hashem Elohei Yisroel, concerning this city, whereof ye say, It shall be delivered into the yad Melech Bavel by the cherev, and by the ra'av and by the dever;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 23:3; YIRMEYAH 23:6; YECHEZKEL 34:12–14"> Hineni, I will gather them out of all countries, where I have driven them in Mine anger, and in My fury, and in great wrath; and I will bring them back unto this makom, and I will cause them to dwell securely;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 24:7; YECHEZKEL 11:19–20; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:10"> And they shall be My people, and I will be their Elohim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 11:19–20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:32; YOCHANAN 17:21"> And I will give them lev echad (one heart), and derech echad (one way), that they may fear Me kol hayamim, for the tov of them, and of their banim after them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:31–33; YECHEZKEL 36:26; YIRMEYAH 24:7"> And I will make a Brit Olam with them, that I will not turn away from doing them good; but I will put My fear in their hearts, that they shall not depart from Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 24:6; TZEFANYAH 3:17; YESHAYAH 62:5"> Indeed, I will rejoice over them to do them good, and I will plant them in this land b'emes with all My lev and with all My nefesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:28; YEHOSHUA 23:14–15; YIRMEYAH 33:10–11"> For thus saith Hashem; Just as I have brought all this ra'ah hagedolah upon this people, so will I bring upon them kol hatovah that I have promised them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 37:11–14; YIRMEYAH 32:15; YIRMEYAH 32:36"> And sadeh shall be bought in this land, whereof ye say, It is desolate without adam or behemah; it is given into the yad of the Kasdim (Chaldeans).</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 17:26; YIRMEYAH 33:26; YIRMEYAH 33:11"> Sadot (fields) will be bought for kesef, and the sefer (deed) will be signed and sealed and witnessed in Eretz Binyamin, and in the places around Yerushalayim, and in the towns of Yehudah, and the towns of the hill country, and in the towns of the Shephelah, and in the towns of the Negev; for I will cause their captivity to return, saith Hashem.</span>
