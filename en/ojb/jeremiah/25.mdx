<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 25:3; YIRMEYAH 26:5; YIRMEYAH 13:10–11"> And Hashem hath sent unto you all His avadim the nevi'im, rising early and sending them; but ye have not paid heed, nor inclined your ozen to hear.</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 1:10; YIRMEYAH 1:5; DANIEL 5:31"> And I will bring upon that land all My Devarim which I have pronounced against it, even all that is written in this sefer, which Yirmeyah hath prophesied against kol HaGoyim. 0</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 75:8; YESHAYAH 51:17; HISGALUS 14:10"> For thus saith Hashem Elohei Yisroel unto me: Take this kos hayayin haChemah (wine cup of wrath)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Lk 22:42">b</a></sup>from My yad, and cause kol HaGoyim, to whom I send thee, to drink it.</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 46:13–26; NACHUM 3:8–10; YIRMEYAH 46:2"> Pharaoh Melech Mitzrayim, and his avadim, and his sarim and all his people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="IYOV 1:1; YECHEZKEL 30:5; YESHAYAH 20:1"> And all the mixed multitude rabble, and kol melachim of Eretz Utz, and kol melachim of Eretz Pelishtim, and Ashkelon, and Azah (Gaza), and Ekron, and the she'erit (remnant) of Ashdod,</span>
