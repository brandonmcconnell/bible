<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 24:1–2; YIRMEYAH 52:4–7; MELACHIM BAIS 25:1–12"> In the ninth year of Tzidkiyah Melech Yehudah, in the tenth month, came Nevukhadretzar Melech Bavel and all his army against Yerushalayim, and they besieged her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 52:6–7; MICHOH 2:12–13; MELACHIM BAIS 25:3–4"> And in the eleventh year of Tzidkiyah, in the fourth month, the ninth yom of the month, the wall was broken through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 21:4; YIRMEYAH 38:17; YIRMEYAH 39:13"> And all the sarim (princes) of Melech Bavel came in, and sat in the Middle Gate, even Nergal Sar-Etzer, Samgar-Nevo, Sar-Sechim, Rav Saris, Nergal Sar-Etzer, Rav-Mag, with all the remaining of the sarim of Melech Bavel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="AMOS 2:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:5; MELACHIM BAIS 25:4–7"> And it came to pass, that when Tzidkiyah Melech Yehudah saw them, and all the anshei hamilchamah, then they fled, and went forth out of the Ir by lailah, by the derech of the Gan HaMelech, by the sha'ar between the two walls; and he headed toward the Aravah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 23:33; YEHOSHUA 5:10; YIRMEYAH 52:26–27"> But the army of the Kasdim pursued after them, and overtook Tzidkiyah in the plains of Yericho; and when they had captured him, they brought him up to Nevukhadretzar Melech Bavel to Rivlah in Eretz Chamat, where he pronounced mishpatim upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 52:10; YIRMEYAH 34:19–21; YIRMEYAH 24:8–10"> Then Melech Bavel slaughtered the Bnei Tzidkiyah in Rivlah before his eyes; also Melech Bavel slaughtered all the nobles of Yehudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 12:13; MELACHIM BAIS 25:7; YIRMEYAH 52:11"> Moreover he put out Tzidkiyahʼs eyes, and bound him with bronze chains, to carry him to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 1:3; YIRMEYAH 38:18; YIRMEYAH 21:10"> And the Kasdim burned the Bais HaMelech, and the bais haAm, with eish, and broke down the chomot Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 40:1; BERESHIS 37:36; YIRMEYAH 10:18"> Then Nevuzaradan the captain of the imperial guard carried away into the Golus of Babylon the remnant of the people that remained in the city, and those that defected, that went over to him, with the rest of the people that remained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 25:12; YIRMEYAH 40:7; YIRMEYAH 52:16"> But Nevuzaradan the captain of the imperial guard left of the poor of the people, which had nothing, in Eretz Yehudah, and gave them kramim (vineyards) and fields at the same time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:23; YIRMEYAH 15:21; IYOV 5:19"> Now Nevukhadretzar Melech Bavel gave charge concerning Yirmeyah to Nevuzaradan the captain of the imperial guard, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MISHLE 16:7; YIRMEYAH 40:4; MISHLE 21:1"> Take him, and look well to him, and do him no harm; but do unto him even as he shall say unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 39:3; YIRMEYAH 39:9"> So Nevuzaradan the captain of the imperial guard sent, and Nevushazban, Rav-Saris, and Nergal Sar-Etzer, Rav-Mag, and all the officers of Melech Bavel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 38:28; MELACHIM BAIS 22:12; YIRMEYAH 26:24"> Even they sent, and took Yirmeyah out of the Khatzer (courtyard) of the Guard, and committed him unto Gedalyahu ben Achikam ben Shaphan, that he should carry him home; so he remained and dwelt among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:9; YIRMEYAH 32:1–2; YIRMEYAH 36:1–5"> Now the Devar Hashem came unto Yirmeyah, while he was confined in the Khatzer (court) of the Guard, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DANIEL 9:12; ZECHARYAH 1:6; YEHOSHUA 23:14–15"> Go and tell Eved-Melech the Ethiopian (the one from Cush), saying, Thus saith Hashem Tzva'os Elohei Yisroel: Hineni, I am bringing My words upon this city for ra'ah, and not for tovah; and they shall be accomplished in yom hahu before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TEHILLIM 41:1–2; TEHILLIM 91:14–15; TEHILLIM 50:15"> But I will deliver thee in yom hahu, saith Hashem; and thou shalt not be given into the yad of the anashim of whom thou art afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 38:2; TEHILLIM 34:22; YIRMEYAH 21:9"> For I will surely deliver thee, and thou shalt not fall by the cherev, but thy nefesh shall be unto thee for plunder; because thou hast put thy trust in Me, saith Hashem.</span>
