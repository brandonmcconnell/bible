<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 7:9; EZRA 2:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:6–10"> Then Shephatyah Ben Mattan, and Gedalyahu Ben Pashchur, and Yukhal Ben Shelemyah, and Pashchur Ben Malkiyah, heard the Devarim that Yirmeyah had spoken unto kol HaAm, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 39:18; YIRMEYAH 34:17; YIRMEYAH 45:5"> Thus saith Hashem, He that remaineth in this city shall die by the cherev, by the ra'av, and by the dever; but he that goeth forth to the Kasdim (Chaldeans) shall live; for he shall have his nefesh for plunder, and shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 21:10; YIRMEYAH 32:3–5"> Thus saith Hashem, This city shall surely be given into the yad of the army of Melech Bavel, which shall capture it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 26:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:20; NECHEMYAH 6:9"> Therefore the sarim said unto HaMelech, Let now this ish be put to death; for thus he weakeneth the hands of the anshei hamilchamah that are left in this city, and the hands of all the people, in speaking such devarim unto them; for this ish seeketh not the shalom of this people, but the hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:12–16; SHMUEL BAIS 3:39; SHMUEL BAIS 19:22"> Then Tzidkiyah HaMelech said, Hinei, he is in your yad (hand, power); for HaMelech is not he that can do anything against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 37:21; YIRMEYAH 38:22; YIRMEYAH 37:16"> Then took they Yirmeyah, and cast him into the bor (pit, dungeon, cistern) of Malkiyah Ben Hammelech, that was in the Khatzer (courtyard) of the Guard; and they lowered Yirmeyah with ropes. And in the bor (pit) there was no mayim, but mud; so Yirmeyah sank in the mud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 21:19; AMOS 5:10; YIRMEYAH 29:2"> Now when Eved-melech HaCushi (the Ethiopian), an ish saris (official) in the Bais HaMelech, heard that they had put Yirmeyah in the bor; HaMelech was then sitting in the Sha'ar Binyamin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Eved-melech went forth out of the Bais HaMelech, and spoke to HaMelech saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 37:21–6; ESTHER 7:4–6; MISHLE 31:8–9"> Adoni HaMelech, these anashim have done ra'ah in all that they have done to Yirmeyah HaNavi, whom they have cast into the bor; and he is likely to die from hunger in the place where he is; for there is no more lechem in the Ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MISHLE 21:1; ESTHER 8:7; ESTHER 5:2"> Then HaMelech commanded Eved-melech HaCushi (the Ethiopian), saying, Take from here shloshim anashim with thee, and lift up Yirmeyah HaNavi out of the bor (dungeon), before he dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> So Eved-melech took the anashim with him, and went into the Bais HaMelech under the Otzar (Treasury), and took from there old rags and old worn out clothes, and let them down by ropes into the bor to Yirmeyah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:32; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:15"> And Eved-melech HaCushi said unto Yirmeyah, Put now these old rags and worn out clothes under thine armpits under the ropes. And Yirmeyah did so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 37:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:35"> So they drew up Yirmeyah with ropes, and lifted him up out of the bor; and Yirmeyah remained in the Khatzer (courtyard) of the Guard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 37:17; MELACHIM ALEF 22:16; YIRMEYAH 21:1–2"> Then Tzidkiyah HaMelech sent, and took Yirmeyah HaNavi unto him into the third entrance that is in the Bais Hashem; and HaMelech said unto Yirmeyah, I will ask thee a thing; hide nothing from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 22:67–68"> Then Yirmeyah said unto Tzidkiyah, If I declare it unto thee, wilt thou not surely put me to death? And if I give thee etzah, wilt thou not refuse to pay heed unto me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 37:17; YIRMEYAH 38:1–6; YESHAYAH 57:16"> So Tzidkiyah HaMelech swore secretly unto Yirmeyah, saying, As Hashem liveth, that made our very nefesh, I will not put thee to death, neither will I give thee into the yad of these anashim that seek thy nefesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 27:12; YIRMEYAH 27:17; YIRMEYAH 38:2"> Then said Yirmeyah unto Tzidkiyah, Thus saith Hashem Elohei Tzva'os Elohei Yisroel: If thou wilt assuredly surrender unto the sarim of Melech Bavel, then thy nefesh shall live, and this city shall not be burned down with eish; and thou shalt live, and thine Bais;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 38:3; YIRMEYAH 24:8–10; MELACHIM BAIS 25:4–10"> But if thou wilt not surrender unto the sarim of Melech Bavel, then shall this city be given into the yad of the Kasdim, and they shall burn it down with eish, and thou shalt not escape out of their yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:42; YIRMEYAH 38:22; YOCHANAN 19:12–13"> And Tzidkiyah HaMelech said unto Yirmeyah, I am afraid of the Yehudim that are fallen as defectors to the Kasdim (Chaldeans), lest they deliver me into their yad, and they abuse me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DANIEL 4:27; YESHAYAH 55:3; YIRMEYAH 26:13"> But Yirmeyah said, They shall not deliver thee. Now obey the voice of Hashem, which I speak unto thee; so it shall be well unto thee, and thy nefesh shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 2:7; YECHEZKEL 2:4–5; YIRMEYAH 26:15"> But if thou refuse to go forth, this is the davar that Hashem hath showed me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 43:6; EKHAH 1:2; EKHAH 5:11"> And, hinei, all the nashim that are left in the Bais Melech Yehudah shall be brought forth to the sarim of Melech Bavel, and those nashim shall say,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Thy close friends misled thee,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and have prevailed against thee:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">thy feet are sunk in the mud,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they desert thee.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 41:10; YIRMEYAH 39:6; YIRMEYAH 38:18"> So they shall surrender all thy nashim and thy banim to the Kasdim (Chaldeans); and thou shalt not escape out of their yad, but shalt be captured by the yad Melech Bavel; and thou shalt cause this city to be burned down with eish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Then said Tzidkiyah unto Yirmeyah, Let no ish know of these devarim, and thou shalt not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 38:27; YIRMEYAH 38:4–6"> But if the sarim hear that I have talked with thee, and they come unto thee, and say unto thee, Declare unto us now what thou hast said unto HaMelech, hide it not from us, and we will not put thee to death; also what HaMelech said unto thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 37:20; YIRMEYAH 37:15; ESTHER 4:8"> Then thou shalt say unto them, I presented my techinnah (supplication) before HaMelech, that he would not cause me to return to Bais Yehonatan, to die there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 10:15–16; MELACHIM BAIS 6:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:6"> Then came all the sarim unto Yirmeyah, and questioned him; and he told them according to all these devarim that HaMelech had commanded. So they left off speaking with him; for the matter had not been overheard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 37:21; YIRMEYAH 39:14; TEHILLIM 23:4"> So Yirmeyah abode in the Khatzer (courtyard) of the Guard until the day that Yerushalayim was captured; and he was there when Yerushalayim was taken.</span>
