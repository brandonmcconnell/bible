<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 23:35; YIRMEYAH 1:3; YIRMEYAH 25:1"> The Davar which came unto Yirmeyah from Hashem in the days of Y'hoyakim Ben Yoshiyah Melech Yehudah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:55; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 9:26; MELACHIM ALEF 6:5–6"> Go unto the Bais of the Rechavim, and speak unto them, and bring them into the Beis Hashem, into one of the leshakhot (side rooms), and give them yayin to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then I took Ya'azanyah Ben Yirmeyah Ben Chavatzinyah, and his brethren, and all his banim, and kol Bais Rechavim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEVARIM 33:1; MELACHIM BAIS 12:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:14"> And I brought them into the Beis Hashem, into the lishkah (room) of the Bnei Chanan Ben Yigdalyahu, an Ish HaElohim, which was by the lishkah of the sarim, which was above the lishkah of Ma'aseiyah Ben Shallum, the Shomer HaSaf (Gatekeeper);</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="KOHELET 9:7; AMOS 2:12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:9"> And I set before the Bnei Bais Rechavim bowls full of yayin, and kosot (cups), and I said unto them, Drink ye yayin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKAS 1:15; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:55; MELACHIM BAIS 10:15"> But they said, We will drink no yayin; for Yehonadav Ben Rechav avinu commanded us, saying, Ye shall drink no yayin, neither ye, nor your banim ad olam;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 35:10; BERESHIS 25:27; NECHEMYAH 8:14–16"> Neither shall ye build bais, nor sow zera, nor plant kerem (vineyard), nor have any; but all your days ye shall dwell in ohalim; that ye may live yamim rabbim in the adamah where ye are sojourners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:20; MISHLE 4:10; MISHLE 1:8–9"> Thus have we obeyed the voice of Yehonadav Ben Rechav avinu in all that he hath charged us, to drink no yayin kol yameinu (all our days), we, our nashim, our banim, nor our banot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:6; YIRMEYAH 35:7; TEHILLIM 37:16"> Nor to build batim for us to dwell in; neither have we kerem, nor sadeh, nor zera;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But we have dwelt in ohalim, and have obeyed, and done according to all that Yehonadav avinu commanded us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 8:14; YIRMEYAH 4:5–7; DANIEL 1:1–2"> But it came to pass, when Nevukhadretzar Melech Bavel invaded ha'aretz, that we said, Come, and let us go to Yerushalayim because of the army of the Kasdim (Chaldeans), and because of the army of Aram (the Syrians); so we dwell in Yerushalayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then came the Devar Hashem unto Yirmeyah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:33; YIRMEYAH 5:3; YIRMEYAH 6:8–10"> Thus saith Hashem Tzva'os Elohei Yisroel: Go and tell the Ish Yehudah and the inhabitants of Yerushalayim, Will ye not learn musar to pay heed to My devarim? saith Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 25:3–4; YIRMEYAH 7:24–26; MISHLE 1:20–33"> The devarim of Yehonadav Ben Rechav, that he commanded his banim not to drink yayin, has been carried out; for unto this day they drink none, but obey the mitzvat avihem; notwithstanding, I have spoken unto you, rising early and speaking; but ye paid heed not unto Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 18:11; YECHEZKEL 18:30–32; YIRMEYAH 25:5–6"> I have sent also unto you all My avadim the Nevi'im, rising up early and sending them, saying, Turn ye now every ish from his derech hara'ah, and amend your doings, and go not after elohim acherim to serve them, and ye shall dwell in ha'adamah which I have given to you and to avoteichem; but ye have not inclined your ozen (ear), nor paid heed unto Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MALACHI 1:6; YIRMEYAH 35:14; LUKAS 15:11–13"> Indeed the Bnei Yehonadav Ben Rechav have carried out the mitzvat avihem, which he commanded them; but this people hath not paid heed unto Me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YESHAYAH 66:4; YESHAYAH 65:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:21"> Therefore, thus saith Hashem Elohei Tzva'os Elohei Yisroel: Hineni, I will bring upon Yehudah and upon all the inhabitants of Yerushalayim all the ra'ah that I have pronounced against them; because I have spoken unto them, but they have not paid heed; and I have called unto them, but they have not answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEVARIM 5:16; SHEMOT 20:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:1–3"> And Yirmeyah said unto the Bais Rechavim, Thus saith Hashem Tzva'os Elohei Yisroel; Because ye have obeyed the mitzvat Yehonadav avichem, and have been shomer over all his mitzvot, and done according unto all that he hath commanded you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 15:19; LUKAS 21:36; YIRMEYAH 33:17–18"> Therefore thus saith Hashem Tzva'os Elohei Yisroel: Yehonadav Ben Rechav shall not lack an ish to stand before Me kol hayamim.</span>
</div>
