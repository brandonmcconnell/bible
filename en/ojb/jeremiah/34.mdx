<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 1:15; DANIEL 2:37–38; MELACHIM BAIS 25:1–9"> The Davar which came unto Yirmeyah from Hashem, when Nevukhadretzar Melech Bavel, and all his army, and all the mamlechot eretz (kingdoms of the earth) of his dominion, and all the peoples fought against Yerushalayim, and against all the towns thereof, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 21:10; YIRMEYAH 34:22; YIRMEYAH 37:8–10"> Thus saith Hashem Elohei Yisroel; Go and speak to Tzidkiyah Melech Yehudah, and tell him, Thus saith Hashem: Hineni, I will give this city into the yad Melech Bavel, and he shall burn it with eish,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:4; YIRMEYAH 21:7; MELACHIM BAIS 25:4–7"> And thou shalt not escape out of his yad, but shalt surely be captured, and delivered into his yad; and thine eynayim shall behold the eynayim of Melech Bavel, and he shall speak with thee mouth to mouth, and thou shalt go to Bavel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Yet hear the Devar Hashem, O Tzidkiyah Melech Yehudah; Thus saith Hashem of thee, Thou shalt not die by the cherev;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 22:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 16:14; MELACHIM BAIS 22:20"> But thou shalt die in shalom; and with the &#91;royal funeral spice&#93; burnings of thy avot, the former melachim which were before thee, so shall they burn for thee; and they will lament thee, saying, Hoy Adon! For I have pronounced the davar, saith Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:16–24; SHMUEL ALEF 3:18; MELACHIM ALEF 21:19"> Then Yirmeyah HaNavi spoke all these devarim unto Tzidkiyah Melech Yehudah in Yerushalayim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 10:3; YIRMEYAH 4:5; MELACHIM BAIS 18:13–14"> When the army of Melech Bavel fought against Yerushalayim, and against all the towns of Yehudah that were left, against Lakhish, and against Azekah; for these fortified cities remained of the cities of Yehudah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:10; MELACHIM BAIS 23:2–3; MELACHIM BAIS 11:17"> This is the Davar that came unto Yirmeyah from Hashem, after that Tzidkiyah HaMelech had cut a Brit with kol HaAm which were at Yerushalayim, to proclaim deror (liberty) unto them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEVARIM 15:12; VAYIKRA 25:39–46; SHEMOT 3:18"> That every ish should set free his eved, and every ish his shifchah, being Ivri (Hebrew man) or Ivriyah (Hebrew woman); that none should hold another brother Yehudi in bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 26:16; YIRMEYAH 26:10; YIRMEYAH 38:4"> Now when all the sarim, and kol HaAm, which had entered into the brit (covenant), heard that every one should set free his eved, and every one his shifchah, that none should keep them in bondage, then they obeyed, and let them go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20–22; MISHLE 26:11; MATTITYAHU 12:43–45"> But afterward they changed their minds, and caused avadim and the shfakhot, whom they had set free, to return, and brought them into subjection for avadim and for shfakhot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Therefore the Devar Hashem came to Yirmeyah from Hashem, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHOFETIM 6:8; YEHOSHUA 24:17; DEVARIM 5:27"> Thus saith Hashem Elohei Yisroel; I cut a Brit with avoteichem in the day that I brought them forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim, out of the bais avadim, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEVARIM 15:12; MELACHIM ALEF 9:22; MELACHIM BAIS 17:13–14"> At the end of sheva shanim let ye go free every ish his brother HaIvri, which hath been sold unto thee; and when he hath served thee shesh shanim, thou shalt let him go chofshi (free) from thee; but avoteichem paid heed not unto Me, neither inclined their ozen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 34:8; MELACHIM BAIS 23:3; NECHEMYAH 10:29"> And recently ye turned, and did hayashar (the right) in My sight, in proclaiming deror (liberty) every ish to his neighbor; and ye cut a Brit (covenant) before Me in the Beis which is called by Shmi;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:12; SHEMOT 20:7; YIRMEYAH 34:11"> But ye turned around and profaned Shmi, and caused every ish his eved, and every ish his shifchah, whom he had set at liberty at their desire, to return, and brought them into subjection, to be unto you for avadim and for shfakhot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:2; DEVARIM 28:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:7"> Therefore thus saith Hashem: Ye have not paid heed unto Me, in proclaiming deror (freedom), everyone to his brother, and every ish to his neighbor; hineni, I proclaim deror (freedom) for you, saith Hashem, to the cherev, to the dever, and to the ra'av; and I will make you to be a horror to all the mamlechot ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:10; BERESHIS 15:17–18; HOSHEA 6:7"> And I will give the anashim that have transgressed My Brit, which have not performed the Devarim of the Brit (covenant) which they had cut before Me, when they cut the egel (calf) in two, and passed between the parts thereof,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 34:10; TZEFANYAH 3:3–4; DANIEL 9:6"> The sarim of Yehudah, and the sarim of Yerushalayim, the sarisim, and the kohanim, and kol Am HaAretz, which passed between the parts of the egel (calf);</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 7:33; YIRMEYAH 16:4; YIRMEYAH 19:7"> I will even give them into the yad of their oyevim, and into the yad of them that seek their nefesh: and their nevilah (dead body) shall be for food unto the oph HaShomayim, and to the behemat ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 52:24–27; YIRMEYAH 39:6; YIRMEYAH 52:10"> And Tzidkiyah Melech Yehudah and his sarim will I give into the yad of their oyevim, and into the yad of them that seek their nefesh, and into the yad of Melech Bavelʼs army, which hast withdrawn from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 9:11; YIRMEYAH 39:8; YIRMEYAH 39:1–2"> Hineni, I will command, saith Hashem, and cause them to return to this city; and they shall fight against it, and capture it, and burn it with eish; and I will make the towns of Yehudah a desolation without an inhabitant.</span>
</div>
