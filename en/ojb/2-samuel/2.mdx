<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 30:31; SHMUEL ALEF 23:4; BERESHIS 32:2"> And it came to pass after this, that Dovid inquired of Hashem, saying, Shall I go up into any of the towns of Yehudah? And Hashem said unto him, Go up. And Dovid said, To where shall I go up? And he said, Unto Chevron.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:42–43; SHMUEL ALEF 30:5; LUKAS 22:28–29"> So Dovid went up there, and his two nashim also, Achino'am the Yizre'elit, and Avigal eshet Naval the Karmeli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 30:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:1–7; SHMUEL ALEF 27:2–3"> And his anashim that were with him did Dovid bring up, every ish with his household; and they dwelt in the towns of Chevron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 5:5; SHMUEL ALEF 31:11–13; SHMUEL BAIS 5:3"> And anshei Yehudah came, and there they anointed Dovid Melech over Bais Yehudah.<br />And they told Dovid, saying, The Anshei Yavesh-Gil'ad were they that buried Sha'ul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 23:21; TEHILLIM 115:15; RUTH 2:20"> And Dovid sent malachim unto the Anshei Yavesh-Gil'ad, and said unto them, Berukhim atem of Hashem, that ye have showed this chesed unto adoneichem, even unto Sha'ul, burying him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MISHLE 14:22; SHEMOT 34:6; MATTITYAHU 5:44"> And now Hashem show chesed and emes unto you; and I also will repay you this tovah, because ye have done this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:10; SHMUEL BAIS 10:12; BERESHIS 15:1"> Therefore now let your hands be strengthened, be ye bnei chayil; for adoneichem Sha'ul is dead, and also the Bais Yehudah have anointed me Melech over them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 14:50; BERESHIS 32:2; SHMUEL BAIS 3:7–8"> But Avner ben Ner, Sar Tzava of Sha'ul, took Ish-Boshet Ben Sha'ul, and brought him over to Machanayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:18; YEHOSHUA 13:8–11; BAMIDBAR 1:40"> And made him melech over Gil'ad, and over the Ashuri, and over Yizre'el, and over Ephrayim, and over Binyamin, and over all Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Ish-Boshet ben Sha'ul was forty shanah when he began to reign over Yisroel, and reigned two shanim. But Bais Yehudah followed Dovid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:11; SHMUEL BAIS 5:4–5; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:4"> And the time that Dovid was Melech in Chevron over Bais Yehudah was shivah shanah and shishah chodashim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 18:25; YEHOSHUA 10:2; YEHOSHUA 9:3"> And Avner ben Ner, and the avadim of Ish-Boshet Ben Sha'ul, went out from Machanayim to Giv'on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:16; SHMUEL BAIS 20:23"> And Yoav Ben Tzeruyah, and the avadim of Dovid, went out, and met together by the pool of Giv'on; and they sat down, the one on the one side of the pool, and the other on the other side of the pool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 2:17; MISHLE 10:23; MISHLE 17:14"> And Avner said to Yoav, Let the young men now arise for a contest before us. And Yoav said, Let them arise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then there arose and went over by mispar twelve of Binyamin, which pertained to Ish-Boshet ben Sha'ul, and twelve of the avadim of Dovid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And they caught every one his re'a by the rosh, and thrust his cherev in his re'aʼs side; so they fell down together; therefore that makom was called Chelkat-Hatzurim, which is in Giv'on.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:1"> And there was a very fierce milchamah that day; and Avner was defeated, and the Anshei Yisroel, before the Avadim of Dovid.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:8; CHABAKUK 3:19; SHIR HASHIRIM 8:14"> And there were three Bnei Tzeruyah there: Yoav, Avishai, Asahel; Asahel was as swift of foot as a gazelle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 2:21; YEHOSHUA 1:7; MISHLE 4:27"> Asahel pursued after Avner; and in going he turned not to the right nor to the left from following Avner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then Avner looked behind him, and said, Art thou Asahel? And he answered, I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHOFETIM 14:19"> And Avner said to him, Turn thee aside to thy right or to thy left, and lay thee hold on one of the ne'arim, and capture thee his armor. But Asahel would not turn aside from following of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:27; MISHLE 29:1; MELACHIM BAIS 14:10–12"> And Avner said again to Asahel, Turn thee aside from following me; why should I strike thee to the ground? How then should I hold up my face to Yoav achicha?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:27; SHMUEL BAIS 4:6; SHMUEL BAIS 5:6"> Howbeit he refused to turn aside; therefore Avner with the butt end of the khanit struck him under the fifth rib, that the khanit came out his back; and he fell down there, and died in the same place; and it came to pass, that as many as came to the makom where Asahel fell down and died, stopped.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Yoav also and Avishai pursued after Avner; and the shemesh went down when they were come to the hill of Ammah, that lieth before Giach by the derech midbar Giv'on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And the Bnei Binyamin gathered themselves together after Avner, and became aguddah echat (one unit), and stood on the top of one hill.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 46:14; YIRMEYAH 46:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:26"> Then Avner called to Yoav, and said, Shall the cherev devour lanetzach (forever)? Knowest thou not that it will be marah in the latter end? Ad mosai shall it be then, until thou bid the people return from pursuing their brethren?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MISHLE 17:14; SHMUEL BAIS 2:14; MISHLE 15:1"> And Yoav said, As HaElohim liveth, if thou hadst not spoken, HaAm would have continued every one following his brother, not stopping until haboker.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> So Yoav blew a shofar, and kol haAm stood still, and pursued after Yisroel no more, neither fought they any more.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 2:8; SHIR HASHIRIM 2:17"> And Avner and his anashim marched kol halailah through the Aravah, and passed over Yarden, and went through all Bitron, and they came to Machanayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Yoav returned from following Avner; and when he had gathered kol HaAm together, there lacked of Dovidʼs avadim nineteen ish and Asahel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:1; MELACHIM ALEF 20:11"> But the avadim of Dovid had struck down of Binyamin, and of anshei Avner, so that three hundred and threescore ish died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:13–16; SHMUEL ALEF 17:58; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 21:1"> And they took up Asahel, and buried him in the kever of aviv, which was in Beit-Lechem. And Yoav and his men marched kol halailah, and they came to Chevron at dawn.</span>
</div>
</div>
