<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MISHLE 16:7; LUKAS 1:74–75; MELACHIM ALEF 5:4"> And it came to pass, when HaMelech sat in his Bais, and Hashem had given him rest round about from all his oyevim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 16:1; SHMUEL BAIS 5:11; CHAGGAI 1:4"> That HaMelech said unto Natan HaNavi, See now, I dwell in a Bais of cedar, but the Aron HaElohim dwelleth within the curtain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:7; MELACHIM ALEF 8:17–18; MELACHIM BAIS 4:27"> And Natan said to HaMelech, Go, do all that is in thine lev; for Hashem is with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:3; BAMIDBAR 12:6; AMOS 3:7"> And it came to pass that lailah, that the Devar Hashem came unto Natan, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:3–32; MELACHIM ALEF 5:3–4; MELACHIM ALEF 8:16–19"> Go and tell Avdi Dovid, Thus saith Hashem, Shalt thou build Me a Bais for Me to dwell in?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:16; YEHOSHUA 18:1; SHEMOT 40:18–19"> Whereas I have not dwelt in any Bais since the time that I brought up the Bnei Yisroel out of Mitzrayim, even to this day, but have walked in an Ohel and in a Mishkan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:6; SHMUEL BAIS 5:2; VAYIKRA 26:11–12"> In all the places wherein I have walked with kol Bnei Yisroel spoke I a word with any of the Shivtei Yisroel, whom I commanded, to shepherd My people Yisroel, saying, Why build ye not Me a Bais of cedar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:70–71; SHMUEL BAIS 6:21; SHMUEL BAIS 12:7"> Now therefore so shalt thou say unto Avdi Dovid, Thus saith Hashem Tzva'os, I took thee from the sheepfold, from following the tzon, to be Nagid over My people, over Yisroel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 5:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:8; SHMUEL ALEF 18:14"> And I was with thee whithersoever thou didst go, and have cut off all thine oyevim out of thy sight, and have made thee a shem gadol, like unto the shem of the Gedolim that are on ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="AMOS 9:15; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:9; YIRMEYAH 24:6"> Moreover I will appoint makom (place, home) for My people Yisroel, and will plant them, that they may dwell in a place of their own, and be disturbed no more; neither shall the bnei avlah (children of wickedness) afflict them any more, as in former times,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 7:27; SHMUEL BAIS 7:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:10"> Even since the time that I commanded Shofetim to be over My people Yisroel, and have caused thee to rest from all thine oyevim. Also Hashem declareth to thee that He will make thee a Bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:30; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:36"> And when thy yamim be fulfilled, and thou shalt sleep with thy Avot, I will raise up thy Zera after thee, which shall proceed out of thy loins, and I will establish his Mamlachah (Kingdom).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 1:31–33; MELACHIM ALEF 8:19; SHMUEL BAIS 7:16"> He shall build a Bais for My Shem, and I will establish the kisse (throne) of his Mamlachah ad olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:5; HISGALUS 3:19; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:13"> I will be his Av, and he shall be My Ben.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="HaElohim">a</a></sup>If he commit iniquity, I will chasten him with the shevet anashim, and with the floggings of bnei adam;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:23; SHMUEL ALEF 15:28; TEHILLIM 89:28"> But My chesed shall not be taken away from him, as I took it from Sha'ul, whom I removed from before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 1:32–33; TEHILLIM 89:36–37; SHMUEL BAIS 7:13"> And thine Bais and thy Mamlachah shall be established ad olam before thee; thy kisse (throne) shall be established ad olam (forever).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Ps 89:36-39">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:27; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:3"> According to all these devarim, and according to all this chizzayon (revelation), so did Natan speak unto Dovid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="TEHILLIM 8:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:16; SHMUEL ALEF 18:18"> Then went HaMelech Dovid in, and sat before Hashem, and he said, Who am I, Adonoi Hashem? And what is my Bais, that Thou hast brought me this far?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 55:8–9; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:17; TEHILLIM 36:7"> And as if this were a small thing in Thy sight, Adonoi Hashem, Thou hast spoken also the future of Bais Avdecha. And is this fitting for haAdam,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="such as me">c</a></sup>Adonoi Hashem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YOCHANAN 21:17; SHMUEL ALEF 16:7; TEHILLIM 139:1"> And what can Dovid say more unto Thee? For Thou, Adonoi Hashem, knowest Avdecha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="TEHILLIM 115:1; TEHILLIM 138:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:9"> For the sake of Thy word, and according to Thine own lev, hast Thou done all this gedullah (great thing), and made it known to Avdecha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="TEHILLIM 86:10; SHEMOT 15:11; TEHILLIM 86:8"> Wherefore Thou art great, Adonoi Hashem; for there is none like Thee, neither is there any Elohim but Thee, according to all that we have heard with our ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEVARIM 9:26; DEVARIM 10:21; DEVARIM 15:15"> And what Goy echad in ha'aretz is like Thy people, even like Yisroel, whom Elohim went to redeem for an Am for Himself, and to make Him a Shem, and to do for Thee hagedullah (the great thing) and nora'ot, for Thy land, before Thy people, which Thou redeemedst to Thee, from Mitzrayim, from the Goyim and their g-ds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEVARIM 26:18; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:10; BERESHIS 17:7"> For Thou hast confirmed to Thyself Thy people Yisroel to be an Am unto Thee ad olam; and Thou, Hashem, art become their Elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:49; YECHEZKEL 36:37; BERESHIS 32:12"> And now, Hashem Elohim, the davar (word, promise) that Thou hast spoken concerning Avdecha, and concerning his Bais, establish it ad olam, and do just as Thou hast said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:23–24; TEHILLIM 72:18–19; MATTITYAHU 6:9"> And let Thy Shem be magnified ad olam, saying, Hashem Tzivos is Elohim over Yisroel; and let the Bais of Avdecha Dovid be established before Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:25–26; TEHILLIM 10:17; RUTH 4:4"> For Thou, Hashem Tzva'os Elohei Yisroel, hast revealed to ozen Avdecha, saying, I will build thee a Bais; therefore hath Avdecha found in his lev to pray this tefillah unto Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YOCHANAN 17:17; SHEMOT 34:6; BAMIDBAR 23:19"> And now, Adonoi Hashem Atah hu HaElohim, and Thy devarim are emes, and Thou hast promised hatovah hazot unto Avdecha;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 6:24–26; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:27; TEHILLIM 115:12–15"> Therefore now let it please Thee to bless the Bais Avdecha, that it may continue l'olam before Thee; for Thou, Adonoi Hashem, hast spoken it; and with Thy brocha let the Bais Avdecha be blessed l'olam.</span>
