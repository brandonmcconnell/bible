<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 18:5; SHMUEL BAIS 18:14; MISHLE 17:25"> And HaMelech was much moved, and went up to the aliyyat hasha'ar (upper room over the gate) and wept; and as he went, thus he said, O beni (my son) Avshalom, beni, beni Avshalom! If only I had died tachteicha (in place of, instead of) thee, O Avshalom, beni, beni!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MISHLE 19:12; MISHLE 16:15"> And it was told Yoav, Hinei, HaMelech weepeth and mourneth for Avshalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 31:27; SHMUEL BAIS 19:32; SHMUEL BAIS 17:24"> And the teshu'ah (salvation, deliverance) on that day was turned into evel (mourning) unto kol HaAm; for the people heard say that day how HaMelech was grieved for bno Avshalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 18:33; SHMUEL BAIS 15:30"> And the people stole that day into the Ir<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Machanayim">a</a></sup>as people being ashamed steal in when they flee in milchamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 18:47–48; TEHILLIM 3:8; NECHEMYAH 9:27"> But HaMelech covered his face, and HaMelech cried with a kol gadol, O beni Avshalom, O Avshalom, beni, beni!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:5; SHMUEL BAIS 3:24–25; MISHLE 19:9–10"> And Yoav came into the bais to HaMelech, and said, Thou hast disgraced this day the faces of all thy avadim, which this day have saved thy nefesh, and the nefesh of thy banim and of thy banot, and the nefesh of thy nashim, and the nefesh of thy pilagshim (concubines);</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MISHLE 14:28; BERESHIS 34:3; MISHLE 19:15"> In that thou lovest thine enemies, and hatest thy friends. For thou hast declared this day, that thou hast no regard for sarim nor avadim; for this day I perceive, that if Avshalom had lived, and this day all we were mesim (dead ones), then it would have been yashar in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 18:24; SHMUEL BAIS 18:4; SHMUEL BAIS 19:3"> Now therefore arise, go forth, and speak to the lev unto thy avadim; for I swear by Hashem, if thou go not forth, there will not tarry one ish with thee halailah (this night); and that will be worse unto thee than kol hara'ah that befell thee from thy youth until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:14; BERESHIS 3:12–13; SHMUEL BAIS 8:1–14"> Then HaMelech arose, and sat in the sha'ar. And they told unto kol haAm, saying, Hinei, HaMelech doth sit in the sha'ar. And kol haAm came before HaMelech: for Yisroel had fled every ish to his ohel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 18:14; SHOFETIM 18:9; HOSHEA 8:4"> And kol haAm were having dissension throughout kol Shivtei Yisroel saying, HaMelech saved us out of the palm of oyveinu (our enemies) and he delivered us out of the palm of the Pelishtim; and now he is fled from HaAretz because of Avshalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:29; MELACHIM ALEF 2:35; SHMUEL BAIS 15:35–36"> And Avshalom, whom mashachnu (we anointed) over us, is dead in milchamah. Now therefore why are ye not speaking of bringing back HaMelech?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 5:1; BERESHIS 2:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:30"> And Dovid HaMelech sent to Tzadok and to Evyatar the kohanim, saying, Speak unto the Ziknei Yehudah, saying, Why are ye the last ones to bring HaMelech back to his Bais? Seeing the talk of kol Yisroel is come to HaMelech, even to his Bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 17:25; MELACHIM ALEF 19:2; RUTH 1:17"> Ye are my brethren, ye are my atzmot and my basar; why then are ye the last ones to bring back HaMelech?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHOFETIM 20:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:32; TEHILLIM 110:2–3"> And say ye to Amasa, Art thou not of my etzem, and of my basar? Elohim do so to me, and more also, if thou be not sar tzava before me continually in place of Yoav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 5:9; SHMUEL ALEF 11:14–15"> And he swayed the levav kol Ish Yehudah, even as the lev of one man; so that they sent this word unto HaMelech, Return thou, and all thy avadim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:8; SHMUEL BAIS 16:5–13; MELACHIM ALEF 2:36–46"> So HaMelech returned, and came to Yarden. And Yehudah came to Gilgal, to go to meet HaMelech, to conduct HaMelech over Yarden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 16:1–4; SHMUEL BAIS 9:2; SHMUEL BAIS 9:10"> And Shime'i Ben Gera, a Ben-HaYemini, which was of Bachurim, hasted and came down with the Ish Yehudah to meet Dovid HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="TEHILLIM 81:15; HISGALUS 3:9; TEHILLIM 66:3"> And there were an elef ish of Binyamin with him, and Tziva the na'ar of the Bais Sha'ul, and his fifteen banim and his twenty avadim with him; and they went over Yarden before HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 22:15; SHMUEL BAIS 13:20; SHMUEL BAIS 13:33"> And there went over a ferry to carry over the Bais HaMelech, and to do what he thought good. And Shime'i Ben Gera fell down before HaMelech, as he was come over Yarden;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:25; TEHILLIM 78:34–37; BERESHIS 48:14"> And said unto HaMelech, Let not adoni impute avon (iniquity) unto me, neither do thou remember that which thy eved did perversely the day that adoni HaMelech went out of Yerushalayim, that HaMelech should take it to his lev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:28; SHMUEL ALEF 26:9; MELACHIM ALEF 21:10–11"> For thy eved doth know that I have sinned; therefore, hinei, I am come rishom (first) this day of all the Bais Yosef to go down to meet adoni HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 11:13; SHMUEL BAIS 16:10; SHMUEL BAIS 3:39"> But Avishai Ben Tzeruyah answered and said, Shall not Shime'i be put to death for this, because he cursed Hashemʼs Moshiach?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:46; MELACHIM ALEF 2:8–9; MELACHIM ALEF 2:37"> And Dovid said, What have I to do with you, ye Bnei Tzeruyah, that ye should this day be as satan unto me? Shall there be ish put to death this day in Yisroel? For do not I know that I am this day Melech al Yisroel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 41:5; MATTITYAHU 6:16; SHMUEL BAIS 15:30"> Therefore HaMelech said unto Shime'i, Thou shalt not die. And HaMelech swore a shevu'a unto him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 16:17"> And Mephivoshet Ben Sha'ul came down to meet HaMelech, and had neither bathed his regal, nor trimmed his safam (mustache), nor washed his clothes, from the day HaMelech departed until the day he came again in shalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 16:2–3; SHMUEL BAIS 4:4; SHMUEL BAIS 9:3"> And it came to pass, when he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Mephivoshet">b</a></sup>was come to Yerushalayim to meet HaMelech, that HaMelech said unto him, why wentest not thou with me, Mephivoshet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 14:17; SHMUEL BAIS 14:20; SHMUEL ALEF 29:9"> And he answered, Adoni, O Melech, avdi deceived me: for thy eved said, I will saddle me a chamor, that I may ride thereon, and go to HaMelech; because thy eved is pise'ach (lame).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 9:13; SHMUEL BAIS 9:10; SHMUEL BAIS 21:6–9"> And he hath slandered thy eved unto adoni HaMelech; but adoni HaMelech is like malach HaElohim; do therefore what is tov in thine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:15; TEHILLIM 101:5; TEHILLIM 82:2"> For all of the bais avi were but anshei mavet before adoni HaMelech; yet didst thou set thy eved among them that did eat at thine own shulchan. What tzedakah (right) therefore have I yet to appeal any more unto HaMelech?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:24; SHMUEL BAIS 1:26"> And HaMelech said unto him, Why speakest thou any more of thy devarim? I have said, Thou and Tziva divide the sadeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:7; NECHEMYAH 7:63; EZRA 2:61"> And Mephivoshet said unto HaMelech, Indeed, let him take all, forasmuch as adoni HaMelech is come again in shalom unto his own bais.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:2; IYOV 1:3; MISHLE 16:31"> And Barzillai the Gil'adi came down from Roglim, and went over Yarden with HaMelech, to conduct him over Yarden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:34–40; LUKAS 22:28–30; SHMUEL BAIS 9:11"> Now Barzillai was zaken me'od, even fourscore shanah; and he had been the support of HaMelech while he dwelt at Machanayim; for he was an ish gadol me'od.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:29; BERESHIS 47:8–9"> HaMelech said unto Barzillai, Come thou over with me, and with me I will be your support in Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:33; EZRA 2:65; TEHILLIM 90:10"> And Barzillai said unto HaMelech, How long have I to live, that I should go up with HaMelech to Yerushalayim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LUKAS 6:38"> I am this day fourscore shanah old; and can I discern between tov and rah? Can thy eved taste what I eat or what I drink? Can I hear any more the voice of sharim (singing men) and sharot (singing women)? Why then should thy eved be yet a massa (burden) unto adoni HaMelech?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:7; YIRMEYAH 41:17; SHMUEL BAIS 19:40"> Thy eved will go a little way over Yarden with HaMelech; and why should HaMelech reward me with such a reward?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Let thy eved, now, turn back again, that I may die in mine own city, and be buried near the kever of avi and of immi. But hinei thy eved Kimham;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e., one of his sons, 1Kgs 2:7">c</a></sup>let him go over with adoni HaMelech; and do to him what shall seem tov unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="BERESHIS 31:55; RUTH 1:14; BERESHIS 47:7"> And HaMelech answered, Kimham shall cross over with me, and I will do for him that which shall seem tov unto thee; and whatsoever thou shalt request of me, that will I do for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:9; BERESHIS 49:10; SHMUEL BAIS 19:11–15"> And kol haAm crossed over Yarden. And when HaMelech was come over, HaMelech kissed Barzillai, and put a bracha on him; and he returned unto his own makom (place, home).</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="SHOFETIM 8:1; SHOFETIM 12:1; YOCHANAN 7:5–6"> Then HaMelech went on to Gilgal, and Kimhan crossed over with him; and kol Am Yehudah conducted HaMelech, and also half Am Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 19:12; SHMUEL BAIS 5:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:3–17"> And, hinei, kol Ish Yisroel came to HaMelech, and said unto HaMelech, Why have acheinu Ish Yehudah stolen thee away, and have brought HaMelech across, and his Bais (household), and kol Anshei Dovid with him, over Yarden?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:20; SHMUEL BAIS 5:1; MELACHIM ALEF 11:30–31"> And kol Ish Yehudah answered Ish Yisroel, Because HaMelech is near of kin to us; why then be ye angry for this matter? Have we eaten at all from HaMelech? Or hath he given us anything?</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And Ish Yisroel answered the Ish Yehudah, and said, We have ten shares as much in HaMelech, and we have also more in Dovid than ye; why then did ye slight us? Were we not the first to give the davar in bringing back my melech? And the devar Ish Yehudah were harsher than the devar Ish Yisroel.</span>
</div>
