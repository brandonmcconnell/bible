<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 27:6; SHMUEL ALEF 31:6; SHMUEL ALEF 30:17–26"> Now it came to pass after the mot Sha'ul, when Dovid was returned from the slaughter of Amalek, and Dovid had abode two yamim in Tziklag;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 4:12; SHMUEL BAIS 4:10; YEHOSHUA 7:6"> It came even to pass on Yom HaShlishi, that, hinei, an ish came out of the machaneh from Sha'ul with his clothes torn, and adamah upon his rosh; and so it was, when he came to Dovid, that he fell to the ground, and prostrated himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="IYOV 1:15–19; MELACHIM BAIS 5:25"> And Dovid said unto him, From where comest thou? And he said unto him, Out of the machaneh Yisroel am I escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 4:16; SHMUEL ALEF 31:1–6; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 10:1–6"> And Dovid said unto him, How went the matter? Tell me now. And he answered, That the people are fled from the milchamah, and many of the people also are fallen and dead; and Sha'ul and Yonatan bno are dead also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MISHLE 25:2; MISHLE 14:15"> And Dovid said unto the na'ar that told him, How knowest thou that Sha'ul and Yonatan bno are dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 28:4; SHMUEL BAIS 1:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 10:4–6"> And the na'ar that told him said, As I happened to be upon Mt Gilboa, hinei, Sha'ul leaned upon his khanit; and, hinei, the chariots and ba'alei haparashim followed hard after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 22:12; SHMUEL BAIS 9:6; SHOFETIM 9:54"> And when he looked behind him, he saw me, and called unto me. And I answered, Hineini.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 30:17; SHMUEL ALEF 30:13; SHMUEL ALEF 30:1"> And he said unto me, Who art thou? And I answered him, I am an Amaleki.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He said unto me again, Stand, now, over me, and slay me; for shavatz (death through agony) is come upon me, because my nefesh is yet still in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:2; SHOFETIM 9:54; MELACHIM BAIS 11:12"> So I stood over him, and slaughtered him, because I was sure that he could not live after that he was fallen; and I took the nezer (crown, diadem) that was upon his rosh, and the etzadah (bracelet, band) that was on his zero'a, and have brought them here unto adoni.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 13:31; BERESHIS 37:29; SHMUEL BAIS 3:31"> Then Dovid took hold on his clothes, and made the kri'ah on them; and likewise all the anashim that were with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MISHLE 24:17; TEHILLIM 35:13–14; MATTITYAHU 5:44"> And they mourned, and wept, and did a tzom until the erev, for Sha'ul, and for Yonatan bno, and for the Am Hashem, and for Bais Yisroel; because they were fallen by the cherev.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 1:8"> And Dovid said unto the na'ar that told him, From where art thou? And he answered, I am ben ish ger, an Amaleki.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 26:9; SHMUEL ALEF 31:4; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:10"> And Dovid said unto him, How wast thou not afraid to stretch forth thine yad to destroy Hashemʼs moshiach?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:34; SHOFETIM 8:20; SHMUEL BAIS 4:10–12"> And Dovid called one of the ne'arim, and said, Go near, and fall upon him. And he struck him so that he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:25; LUKAS 19:22; VAYIKRA 20:9"> And Dovid said unto him, Thy dahm be upon thy rosh; for thine own peh hath testified against thee, saying, I have slain Hashemʼs moshiach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:25; SHMUEL BAIS 1:19; BERESHIS 50:11"> And Dovid lamented with this kinah (lamentation) over Sha'ul and over Yonatan bno;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 10:13; BERESHIS 49:8; DEVARIM 4:10"> Also he bade them teach the Bnei Yehudah: The Keshet. Hinei, it is written in the Sefer HaYasher:</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 18:4; YESHAYAH 34:6–7; SHMUEL ALEF 14:6–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">From the dahm of the slain, from the chelev of the Gibborim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the keshet Yonatan turned not back,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the cherev Sha'ul returned not empty.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 4:13; SHOFETIM 14:18; EKHAH 4:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sha'ul and Yonatan</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">were beloved and gracious in their lives,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and in their mot they were not parted;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they were swifter than nesharim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they were stronger than arayot.</span></span>
</div>
