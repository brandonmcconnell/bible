<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 13:1–4; MELACHIM ALEF 8:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 13:6–14"> Again, Dovid gathered together kol bachur b'Yisroel, shloshim elef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 4:4; SHMUEL ALEF 7:1; TEHILLIM 80:1"> And Dovid arose, and went with kol HaAm that were with him from Ba'ale-Yehudah, to bring up from there the Aron HaElohim which is called by the Shem; the Shem Hashem Tzva'os that is enthroned on the keruvim is upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 6:7; BAMIDBAR 7:4–9; BAMIDBAR 4:5–12"> And they set the Aron HaElohim upon an agalah chadashah (new wagon, cart), and brought it out of the bais of Avinadav that was in Giv'ah; and Uzza and Achyo, the Bnei Avinadav, guided the agalah chadashah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 13:7; SHMUEL ALEF 7:1–2"> And they brought it out of the bais Avinadav which was at Giv'ah, with the Aron HaElohim; and Achyo walked before the Aron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 13:8; TEHILLIM 150:3–5; AMOS 5:23"> And Dovid and all Bais Yisroel rejoiced before Hashem on all manner of instruments made of cypress, even on kinnorot, on nevalim (lyres), on tambourines, on castanets, and on cymbals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 13:9; BAMIDBAR 4:15; BAMIDBAR 4:19–20"> And when they came to goren Nakhon, Uzza reached forth to the Aron HaElohim, and took hold of it; for the ox tilted it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 6:19; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:13; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 13:10"> And the Af Hashem was kindled against Uzzah; and HaElohim struck him down there for the shal (error); and there he died by the Aron HaElohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YONAH 4:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 13:11–12; YONAH 4:9"> And Dovid burned with anger, because Hashem had made a peretz (outburst), breaking out upon Uzzah; and he called the shem of the makom (place) Peretz-Uzzah, which is what it is called to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:120; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 13:11–12; BAMIDBAR 17:12–13"> And Dovid was afraid of Hashem that day, and said, How can the Aron Hashem come to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:4–8; SHMUEL BAIS 15:19; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:18"> So Dovid would not move the Aron Hashem unto him in Ir Dovid; but Dovid carried it aside into the bais Oved-Edom the Gitti.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 39:5; BERESHIS 30:27; MISHLE 3:9–10"> And the Aron Hashem remained in the bais Oved-Edom the Gitti three months; and Hashem blessed Oved-Edom, and all his bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:25–3; MELACHIM ALEF 8:1; MATTITYAHU 10:42"> And it was told Dovid HaMelech, saying, Hashem hath blessed the bais Oved-Edom, and all that pertaineth unto him, because of the Aron HaElohim. So Dovid went and brought up the Aron HaElohim from the bais Oved-Edom into Ir Dovid with simchah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:15; BAMIDBAR 4:15"> And it was so, that when they that nosei Aron Hashem(carried the Aron HaShem) had gone six steps, he sacrificed ox and fattened calf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 2:18; SHEMOT 15:20; TEHILLIM 150:4"> And Dovid danced before Hashem with kol oz; and Dovid was girded with an ephod bad (linen ephod).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 68:24–27; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:28; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:25"> So Dovid and kol Bais Yisroel brought up the Aron Hashem with ter'uah (shout) and with the sound of the shofar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:29; TEHILLIM 69:7"> And as the Aron Hashem came into Ir Dovid, Michal Bat Sha'ul looked through a chalon (window), and saw Dovid HaMelech leaping and dancing before Hashem; and she despised him in her lev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 1:4; MELACHIM ALEF 8:5"> And they brought in the Aron Hashem, and set it in its makom, inside the ohel that Dovid had pitched for it; and Dovid offered olot (burnt offerings) and shelamim (peace offerings) before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:55; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 16:2; BERESHIS 14:19"> And as soon as Dovid had made an end of offering the olah (burnt offering) and the shelamim (peace offering), he made brocha on HaAm b'Shem Hashem Tzva'os.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 8:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:7–8; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 16:3"> And he apportioned among kol HaAm, even among kol hamon Yisroel, to isha as well as ish, to every one challat, lechem, a portion of meat, and a raisin cake. So kol HaAm departed, each to his bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 6:16; SHMUEL BAIS 6:14; SHOFETIM 9:4"> Then Dovid returned to make brocha on his bais. And Michal Bat Sha'ul came out to meet Dovid, and said, How distinquished was the Melech Yisroel today, who disrobed today in the eyes of the amehot (handmaids) of his avadim, as one of the rekim (vain, empty fellows, indecent boors) shamelessly uncovereth himself!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:22; TEHILLIM 89:19–20; SHMUEL ALEF 13:14"> And Dovid said unto Michal, It was before Hashem, which chose me rather than thy av, and rather than all his bais, to appoint me Nagid over Am Hashem, over Yisroel: therefore will I rejoice before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:2; YESHAYAH 51:7"> And I will yet be more undignified than this, and will be shafal (lowly) in mine own eyes; and of the amahot (maidservants) which thou hast spoken of, of them shall I be held in honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 1:25; YESHAYAH 22:14; YESHAYAH 4:1"> Therefore Michal Bat Sha'ul had no yeled unto her yom mot.</span>
