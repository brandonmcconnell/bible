<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 10:16; DEVARIM 13:13"> And there happened to be there an ish Beliyaal,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="rebel">a</a></sup>shmo Sheva Ben Bikhri an ish from Binyamin; and he blew a shofar, and said, We have no chelek in Dovid, neither have we nachalah in Ben Yishai; every ish to his ohel, O Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:66–68; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:23; SHMUEL BAIS 19:15"> So kol Ish Yisroel went up from after Dovid, and followed Sheva Ben Bikhri; but the Ish Yehudah had deveykus unto their Melech, from Yarden even to Yerushalayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 16:21–22; SHMUEL BAIS 15:16; BERESHIS 40:3"> And Dovid came to his Bais (palace) at Yerushalayim; and HaMelech took the ten nashim, his pilagshim, whom he had left to be shomer over the Bais, and put them in Bais Mishmeret (seclusion), and provided for them, but went not in unto them. So they were shut up unto their yom mot, living in almenut (widowhood).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 19:13; SHMUEL BAIS 17:25; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:17"> Then said HaMelech to Amasa, Assemble the Ish Yehudah within shloshet yamim; be thou here present.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 19:13; SHMUEL ALEF 13:8"> So Amasa went to assemble Yehudah; but he tarried longer than the mo'ed which he had appointed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 21:17; SHMUEL BAIS 11:11; MELACHIM ALEF 1:33"> And Dovid said to Avishai, Now shall Sheva Ben Bikhri do us more harm than did Avshalom; take thou the avadim of adoneicha, pursue after him, lest he get him fortified cities, and escape us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:18; MELACHIM ALEF 1:38; SHMUEL BAIS 20:23"> And there went out after him anshei Yoav, and the Kereti, and the Peleti, and kol Gibborim; and they went out of Yerushalayim, to pursue after Sheva Ben Bikhri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:30; SHMUEL BAIS 2:13; SHMUEL BAIS 20:4–5"> When they were at the Even Hagedolah (Great Stone) which is in Giv'on, Amasa went before them. And Yoavʼs military garment that he had put on was girded unto him, and upon it a khagor with a cherev fastened upon his waist in the sheath thereof; and as he went forth it fell out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 22:47–48; MATTITYAHU 26:48–49; MICHOH 7:2"> Yoav said to Amasa, Art thou in shalom, my brother? And Yoav took Amasa by the zaken (beard) with the right hand to kiss him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 2:23; SHMUEL BAIS 3:27; SHOFETIM 3:21"> But Amasa took no heed to the cherev that was in the yad Yoav; so he stabbed him therewith in the fifth rib, and shed out his bowels to the ground, and struck him not again; and he died. So Yoav and Avishai achiv pursued after Sheva Ben Bikhri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 20:13; SHMUEL BAIS 20:21; MELACHIM BAIS 9:32"> And one of the nearim of Yoav stood by him, and said, He that favoreth Yoav, and he that is for Dovid, let him go after Yoav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MISHLE 24:21–22; TEHILLIM 9:16; SHMUEL BAIS 17:25"> And Amasa wallowed in dahm in the midst of the mesilah (road). And when the ish saw that kol haAm halted, he removed Amasa out of the mesilah into the sadeh, and threw a beged (garment) over him, when he saw that every one that came by him halted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 6:12; YESHAYAH 7:3; MELACHIM BAIS 18:17"> When he was removed from the mesilah, kol haAm went on after Yoav, to pursue after Sheva Ben Bikhri.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 15:29; MELACHIM ALEF 15:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 16:4"> And he passed through all the Shivtei Yisroel unto Abel, and to Beit-Ma'achah, and all the Berim; and they were gathered together, and went also after him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., Sheva">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 19:32; YIRMEYAH 32:24; MELACHIM ALEF 15:20"> And they came and besieged him in Abel of Beit-Ma'achah, and they cast up a siege ramp against the Ir, until it stood by the rampart; and kol haAm that were with Yoav battered the chomah (wall), to throw it down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 14:2; KOHELET 9:14–18; SHMUEL ALEF 25:32–33"> Then cried an isha chachamah (wise woman) from the Ir, Hear, hear; say, now unto Yoav, Come here, that I may speak with thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 14:12; SHMUEL ALEF 25:24"> And when he was come near unto her, the isha said, Art thou Yoav? And he answered, I am he. Then she said unto him, Hear the devarim of thine amah. And he answered, I do hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEVARIM 20:10–11"> Then she spoke, saying, They used to speak in rishonah (old times), saying, They shall surely ask counsel at Abel; and so they settled the matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 26:19; SHMUEL BAIS 21:3; SHMUEL BAIS 17:16"> I am one of them that are of shalom and of emunah in Yisroel; thou seekest to destroy an Ir and an em b'Yisroel; why wilt thou swallow up the nachalat Hashem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKAS 10:29; MISHLE 28:13; YIRMEYAH 17:9"> And Yoav answered and said, Chalilah (far be it), chalilah from me, that I should swallow up or destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 26:9; SHOFETIM 2:9; SHMUEL BAIS 17:2–3"> The matter is not so; but an ish of har Ephrayim, Sheva Ben Bikhri shmo, hath lifted up his yad against HaMelech, even against Dovid; hand over him only, and I will depart from the Ir. And the woman said unto Yoav, Hinei, his rosh shall be thrown to thee from the chomah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 20:1; KOHELET 7:19; SHMUEL BAIS 11:6–21"> Then the isha went unto kol haAm in her chochmah. And they cut off the rosh of Sheva Ben Bikhri, and threw it out to Yoav. And he blew a shofar, and they retired from the city, every ish to his ohel. And Yoav returned to Yerushalayim unto HaMelech.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:16–18; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 18:15–17; MELACHIM ALEF 4:3–6"> Now Yoav was over kol HaTzava Yisroel; and Benayah Ben Yehoyada was over the Kereti and over the Peleti;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 4:3; MELACHIM ALEF 12:18; MELACHIM ALEF 4:6"> And Adoram was over the forced labor; and Yehoshaphat Ben Achilud was mazkir (secretary);</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 4:4; SHMUEL BAIS 8:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 18:16"> And Sheva was sofer; and Tzadok and Evyatar (Abiathar) were the kohanim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 23:38; SHEMOT 2:14; BERESHIS 41:45"> And also Ira the Yairi was kohen to Dovid.</span>
</div>
