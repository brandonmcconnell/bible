<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:18; SHMUEL BAIS 15:32; SHMUEL BAIS 9:2–13"> And when Dovid was a little past the top of the hill, hinei, Tziva the na'ar of Mephivoshet met him, with a couple of chamorim saddled, and upon them 200 lechem, and 100 bunches of tzimmukim, and 100 kayitz, and a skin of yayin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 17:29; SHOFETIM 10:4; SHOFETIM 5:10"> And HaMelech said unto Tziva, What meanest thou by these? And Tziva said, The chamorim are for the Bais HaMelech to ride on; and the lechem and kayitz for the ne'arim to eat; and the yayin, that such as be faint in the midbar may drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 9:9–10; TEHILLIM 15:3; SHMUEL BAIS 19:24–30"> And HaMelech said, And where is the ben adoneicha?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., Sha'ulʼs grandson Mephivoshet">a</a></sup>And Tziva said unto HaMelech, Hinei, he abideth at Yerushalayim; for he said, Today shall Bais Yisroel restore to me the mamlechut avi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MISHLE 18:13; MISHLE 18:17; MISHLE 19:2"> Then said HaMelech to Tziva, Hinei, thine are all that pertained unto Mephivoshet. Tziva said, I humbly prostrate myself. May I find chen in thy sight, adoni, O Melech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:16; SHMUEL ALEF 17:43; SHEMOT 22:28"> When Dovid HaMelech came to Bachurim, hinei, from there came out an ish of the mishpakhat Bais Sha'ul; shmo Shime'i Ben Gera; and he, cursing continuously, came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he cast avanim (stones) at Dovid, and at all the avadim of Dovid HaMelech; and at kol haAm and at all the Gibborim that were on his right and on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 13:13; SHMUEL ALEF 25:17; MELACHIM ALEF 21:10"> And thus said Shime'i when he cursed, Come out, come out, thou ish hadamim and thou ish habeliyya'al;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 1:16; SHMUEL BAIS 3:28–29; MELACHIM ALEF 2:32–33"> Hashem hath returned upon thee all the dahm of the Bais Sha'ul, in whose place thou hast reigned; and Hashem hath delivered the meluchah (kingdom) into the yad Avshalom binecha; and, behold, thou art taken in thy ra'ah, because thou art an ish damim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:28; SHMUEL BAIS 9:8; SHMUEL ALEF 24:14"> Then said Avishai Ben Tzeruyah unto HaMelech, Why should this kelev hamet (dead dog) curse adoni HaMelech? Let me go over, now and cut off his rosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 19:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:20; YOCHANAN 18:11"> And HaMelech said, What have I to do with you, ye Bnei Tzeruyah? So let him curse, because Hashem hath said unto him, Curse Dovid. Who shall then say, why hast thou done so?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 17:1–4; BERESHIS 45:5; SHMUEL BAIS 12:11–12"> And Dovid said to Avishai, and to all his avadim, Hinei, beni, which came forth from within me, seeketh my nefesh; how much more now may this Ben-HaYemini (Benjamite) do it? Let him alone, and let him curse; for Hashem hath bidden him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEVARIM 23:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:10; TEHILLIM 25:18"> It may be that Hashem will look on mine affliction, that Hashem will repay me tovah for his cursing this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:23; SHMUEL BAIS 16:5–6"> And as Dovid and his anashim went by the derech, Shime'i went along on the hillside opposite him, cursed as he went, threw avanim at him, and kicked up aphar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And HaMelech, and kol HaAm that were with him, arrived weary, and refreshed themselves there.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="at the Yarden">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:37; SHMUEL BAIS 15:12"> And Avshalom, and kol HaAm Ish Yisroel, came to Yerushalayim, and Achitophel with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 11:12; SHMUEL ALEF 10:24; MELACHIM ALEF 1:25"> And it came to pass, when Chushai the Arki, re'eh Dovid, was come unto Avshalom, that Chushai said unto Avshalom, Long live HaMelech, Long live HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 19:25; SHMUEL BAIS 15:32–37; DEVARIM 32:6"> And Avshalom said to Chushai, Is this thy chesed (loyal loving-kindness) to thy re'a (friend)? Why wentest thou not with thy re'a (friend)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:13; SHMUEL BAIS 5:1–3"> Chushai said unto Avshalom, Lo; but whom Hashem, this people, and kol Ish Yisroel, choose, his will I be, and with him will I abide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:34; TEHILLIM 55:21; SHMUEL ALEF 28:2"> And again, whom should I serve? Should I not serve in the presence of bno? As I have served in the presence of Avicha, so will I be in thy presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:10; TEHILLIM 37:12–13; YESHAYAH 29:15"> Then said Avshalom to Achitophel, Give etza (counsel, advice, wisdom) among you what we shall do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 20:3; SHMUEL BAIS 15:16; ZECHARYAH 8:13"> And Achitophel said unto Avshalom, Go in unto the pilagshim of Avicha, which he hath left to be shomer over the Bais (palace); and kol Yisroel shall hear that thou art a stench to Avicha; then shall the hands of all that are with thee be chazak (strong).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 12:11–12; SHMUEL BAIS 15:16; SHMUEL BAIS 20:3"> So they spread Avshalom an ohel upon the gag (roof); and Avshalom went in unto the pilagshim of Aviv in the sight of kol Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:12; SHMUEL BAIS 17:14; SHMUEL BAIS 17:23"> And the etza (counsel) of Achitophel, which he counselled in those days, was as if an ish had inquired at the davar HaElohim; so was all the etza (counsel) of Achitophel both with Dovid and with Avshalom.</span>
