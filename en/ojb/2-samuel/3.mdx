<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 14:30; MISHLE 4:18–19; IYOV 8:7"> Now there was long milchamah between the Bais Sha'ul and the Bais Dovid; but Dovid grew stronger and stronger, and the Bais Sha'ul grew weaker and weaker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:1–4; BERESHIS 49:3–4; SHMUEL BAIS 13:1–29"> And unto Dovid were banim born in Chevron; and his bechor was Amnon, of Achino'am the Yizre'elit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 27:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:1; DEVARIM 3:14"> And his second, Kile'av, of Avigal eshet Naval the Karmeli; and the third, Avshalom ben Ma'akhah bat Talmai Melech Geshur;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:13–25; MELACHIM ALEF 1:5–18"> And the fourth, Adoniyah Ben Chaggit; and the fifth, Shephatyah Ben Avital;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the sixth, Yitre'am, by Eglah eshet Dovid. These were born to Dovid in Chevron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 2:8–9; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:8; YESHAYAH 8:9–10"> And it came to pass, while there was milchamah between the Bais Sha'ul and the Bais Dovid, that Avner was strengthening his grip on the Bais Sha'ul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 21:8–11; MELACHIM ALEF 2:21–22; SHMUEL BAIS 12:8"> And Sha'ul had a pilegesh (concubine), whose shem was Ritzpah Bat Ayah; and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ish-Boshet">a</a></sup>said to Avner, why hast thou gone in unto pilegesh avi?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 9:8; SHMUEL BAIS 16:9; MARKOS 6:18–19"> Then was Avner very angry at the devarim of Ish-Boshet, and said, Am I Yehudahʼs rosh kelev? Today do I show chesed unto the Bais Sha'ul avicha, to his brethren, and to his friends, and have not delivered thee into the yad Dovid, and then thou chargest me today with an avon concerning this isha?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:28; MELACHIM ALEF 19:2; RUTH 1:17"> So do Elohim to Avner, and more also! As Hashem hath sworn to Dovid, even so I do for him;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dovid">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHOFETIM 20:1; SHMUEL ALEF 3:20; MELACHIM ALEF 4:25"> To transfer the Mamlachah from the Bais Sha'ul, and to set up the Kisse Dovid over Yisroel and over Yehudah, from Dan even to Be'er-Sheva.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he could not answer Avner a davar again, because he feared him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:38–40; SHMUEL BAIS 3:21; LUKAS 16:5–8"> And Avner sent malachim to Dovid on his behalf, saying, Whose is the eretz? Saying also, Cut thy brit with me, and, hinei, my yad shall be with thee, to bring about kol Yisroel unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 43:3; SHMUEL ALEF 19:11–17; BERESHIS 44:23"> And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Dovid">c</a></sup>said, Tov; I will make a brit with thee; but one thing I require of thee, that is, Thou shalt not see my face, except thou first bring Michal Bat Sha'ul, when thou comest to see my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 18:27; SHMUEL ALEF 18:25; SHMUEL BAIS 2:10"> And Dovid sent malachim to Ish-Boshet Ben Sha'ul, saying, Deliver ishti Michal, which I took in erusin to me for a hundred arlot (foreskins) of the Pelishtim (Philistines).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:44"> And Ish-Boshet sent, and took her from her ish, even from Palti'el Ben Layish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 16:5; SHMUEL BAIS 19:16; MELACHIM ALEF 2:8"> And her ish went with her going along, weeping behind her to Bachurim. Then said Avner unto him, Go, return. And he returned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Avner had communication with the Ziknei Yisroel, saying, Ye sought for Dovid in times past to be melech over you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:28; TEHILLIM 89:19–23; SHMUEL ALEF 9:16"> Now then do it; for Hashem hath spoken of Dovid, saying, By the yad of Avdi Dovid I will save My people Yisroel out of the yad Pelishtim, and out of the yad of all their oyevim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:29; SHMUEL ALEF 10:20–21; TEHILLIM 68:27"> And Avner also spoke in the oznayim of Binyamin; and Avner went also to speak in the oznayim of Dovid in Chevron all that seemed tov to Yisroel, and to the kol Bais Binyamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 31:54; BERESHIS 26:30; ESTHER 1:3"> So Avner came to Dovid to Chevron, and esrim anashim with him. And Dovid made Avner and the anashim that were with him a mishteh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:12; MELACHIM ALEF 11:37; SHMUEL BAIS 3:10"> And Avner said unto Dovid, I will arise and go, and will gather kol Yisroel unto adoni HaMelech, that they may cut a Brit with thee, and that thou mayest reign over all that thine lev desireth. And Dovid sent Avner away; and he went in shalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And, hinei, the avadim of Dovid and Yoav came from a raid, bringing in shalal rav (great plunder) with them; but Avner was not with Dovid in Chevron; he had sent him away, he was gone in shalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> When Yoav and all the tzava that was with him were come, they told Yoav, saying, Avner Ben Ner came to HaMelech, and he hath sent him away, and he is gone in shalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:8; SHMUEL BAIS 19:5–7; YOCHANAN 18:35"> Then Yoav came to HaMelech, and said, What hast thou done? Hinei, Avner came unto thee; why is it that thou hast sent him away, and he is quite gone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:6; YESHAYAH 37:28; BAMIDBAR 27:17"> Thou knowest Avner Ben Ner, that he came to deceive thee, and to have da'as of thy going out and thy coming in, and to have da'as of all that thou doest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MISHLE 26:23–26; MISHLE 27:4–6"> And when Yoav was come out from Dovid, he sent malachim after Avner, which brought him back from the well of Sirah; but Dovid knew it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:5; SHMUEL BAIS 20:9–10; MELACHIM ALEF 2:32"> And when Avner was returned to Chevron, Yoav took him aside in the sha'ar to speak with him privately, and struck him there under the fifth rib, that he died, for the dahm of Asahel his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 35:33; SHEMOT 21:12; BERESHIS 9:6"> And afterward when Dovid heard it, he said, I and my mamlachah are naki (innocent) before Hashem ad olam from the dahm of Avner Ben Ner;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 1:16; TEHILLIM 109:8–19; VAYIKRA 13:44–46"> Let it rest on the rosh Yoav, and on kol Bais Aviv; and let there never fail from the Bais Yoav one that hath a discharge, or that is a metzora (leper), or that leaneth on a pelech (stick, crutch), or that falleth on the cherev, or that lacketh lechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MISHLE 28:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:4; SHMUEL BAIS 2:19–23"> So Yoav, and Avishai his brother slaughtered Avner, because he had slain their brother Asahel at Giv'on in the milchamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:34; SHMUEL BAIS 1:11; SHMUEL BAIS 1:2"> And Dovid said to Yoav, and to kol HaAm that were with him, Rend your clothes, and gird you with sackcloth, and mourn before Avner. And Dovid HaMelech himself followed the mittah (bier, frame or bed holding corpse).</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MISHLE 24:17; IYOV 31:28; SHMUEL ALEF 30:4"> And they buried Avner in Chevron; and HaMelech lifted up his voice and wept at the kever of Avner; and kol HaAm wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 1:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:25; LUKAS 12:19–20"> And in a dirge HaMelech lamented over Avner, and said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Died Avner as a naval (fool) dieth?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="SHOFETIM 16:21; SHMUEL BAIS 1:12; IYOV 24:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thy hands were not bound,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor thy raglayim put into fetters;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as a man falleth before wicked men, so fellest thou.</span><br />And kol HaAm wept again over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 12:17; SHMUEL BAIS 1:12; RUTH 1:17"> And when kol HaAm came to cause Dovid to eat lechem while it was yet day, Dovid swore a shevua (oath), saying, So do Elohim to me, and more also, if I taste lechem, or anything else, till the shemesh go down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MARKOS 7:37; TEHILLIM 62:9; MARKOS 15:11–13"> And kol HaAm took notice of it, and it pleased them; whatsoever HaMelech did pleased kol HaAm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> For kol HaAm and kol Yisroel had da'as that day that it was not of HaMelech to murder Avner Ben Ner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="IYOV 32:9; SHMUEL BAIS 2:8; SHMUEL BAIS 3:12"> And HaMelech said unto his avadim, Know ye not that there is sar and gadol fallen this day in Yisroel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:33–34; TEHILLIM 101:8; TEHILLIM 28:4"> And I am this day weak, though moshuach melech (being anointed king); and these anashim the Bnei Tzeruyah are too harsh for me; Hashem shall reward the doer of evil according to his ra'ah (evil).</span>
