<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YESHAYAH 59:7–8; TEHILLIM 109:2–4; TEHILLIM 3:3–5"> Moreover Achitophel said unto Avshalom, Let me now choose out shneym asar elef ish, and I will arise and pursue after Dovid halailah (this night);</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 16:14; MELACHIM ALEF 22:31; ZECHARYAH 13:7"> And I will come upon him while he is weary and weak of hands, and will make him tremble with fear; and kol haAm that are with him shall flee; and I will strike HaMelech only;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:3; YESHAYAH 48:22; YESHAYAH 57:21"> When I return kol HaAm to you, all except the ish whom thou seekest, it is as if &#91;kol HaAm&#93; returned; so kol haAm shall be in shalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 18:20–21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:32; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:4"> And the saying pleased Avshalom well, and kol Ziknei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:32–37; SHMUEL BAIS 16:16–19"> Then said Avshalom, Call now Chushai the Arki also, and let us hear likewise from his peh (mouth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And when Chushai was come to Avshalom, Avshalom spoke unto him, saying, Achitophel hath spoken after this manner; shall we do after his saying? If not; speak thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MISHLE 31:8"> And Chushai said unto Avshalom, The etza (counsel) that Achitophel hath given is not tovah at this time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="HOSHEA 13:8; SHMUEL ALEF 16:18; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:32–34"> For, said Chushai, thou knowest Avicha and his anashim, that they be Gibborim, and they be enraged in their nefesh, as a dov (bear) robbed of her cubs in the sadeh; and Avicha is ish milchamah, and will not spend the night with HaAm (the troops).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 24:3; SHMUEL ALEF 14:14–15; SHOFETIM 20:32–33"> Hinei, he is hid now in one of the pits, or in some other makom; and it will come to pass, when some of them be overthrown at the outset, that whosoever heareth it will say, There is a magefah (slaughter) among the people that follow Avshalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 2:9–11; SHEMOT 15:15; BERESHIS 49:9"> And he also that is chayil (valiant), whose lev is as the lev ha'aryeh (heart of a lion), shall utterly melt; for kol Yisroel knoweth that Avicha is a Gibbor, and they that be with him are Bnei Chayil (valiant men).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 22:17; SHOFETIM 20:1; TEHILLIM 9:16"> Therefore, I counsel that kol Yisroel be fully gathered unto thee, from Dan even to Be'er-Sheva, as the chol (sand) that is by the yam for multitude; and that thou thyself go into battle in person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 23:23; OVADYAH 1:3; MELACHIM ALEF 20:10"> So shall we come upon him in some makom where he shall be found, and we will light upon him as the tal (dew) falleth on the adamah; and of him and of kol ha'anashim that are with him there shall not be left so much as one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:2; MICHOH 1:6"> Moreover, if he has withdrawn into a city, then shall kol Yisroel bring chavalim (ropes) to that city, and we will draw it into the ravine, until there be not one small stone found there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:34; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:19–20; MISHLE 19:21"> And Avshalom and kol Ish Yisroel said, The etza (counsel) of Chushai the Arki is better than the etza of Achitophel. For Hashem had resolved to annul the etza of Achitophel, to the intent that Hashem might bring ra'ah upon Avshalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:35–36"> Then said Chushai unto Tzadok and to Evyatar (Abiathar) the kohanim, Thus and thus did Achitophel counsel Avshalom and the Ziknei Yisroel; and thus and thus have I counselled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:28; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:54; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:4"> Now therefore send quickly, and tell Dovid, saying, Spend not halailah (this night) in the plains of the midbar, but speedily cross over; lest HaMelech be swallowed up, and kol haAm that are with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:36; YEHOSHUA 18:16; SHMUEL BAIS 15:27"> Now Yehonatan and Achima'atz stayed by Ein-Rogel; for they dared not be seen to come into the Ir; and a shifchah went and told them; and they went and told Dovid HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:16; SHMUEL BAIS 16:5; SHMUEL BAIS 19:16"> Nevertheless a na'ar saw them, and told Avshalom; but they went both of them away quickly, and came to a bais ish in Bachurim, which had a be'er (well) in his khatzer; to where they went down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 1:19; YEHOSHUA 2:4–24"> And the isha took and spread a masakh (covering, curtain) over the mouth of the be'er, and spread hulled grain thereon; and the thing was not known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 1:19; YEHOSHUA 2:22–23; SHMUEL ALEF 19:12–17"> And when the avadim of Avshalom came to the bais to the isha, they said, Where is Achima'atz and Yehonatan? And the isha said unto them, They are gone over the brook of mayim. And when they had sought and could not find them, they returned to Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 17:15–16"> And it came to pass, after they were departed, that they climbed up out of the be'er (well), and went and told Dovid HaMelech, and said unto Dovid, Arise, and cross quickly over the mayim; for thus hath Achitophel counselled against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:16; YOCHANAN 18:9; MISHLE 27:12"> Then Dovid arose, and kol haAm that were with him, and they crossed over Yarden; by the ohr haboker there lacked not one of them that was not gone over Yarden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:5; MELACHIM BAIS 20:1; SHMUEL BAIS 15:12"> And when Achitophel saw that his etza (counsel, advice) was not followed, he saddled his chamor, and arose, and went home to his bais, to his town, and put his bais (household) in order, vayyekhanak (and he strangled, hanged himself), and died, and was buried in the kever aviv (tomb of his father).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BERESHIS 32:2; SHMUEL BAIS 2:8; YEHOSHUA 13:26"> Then Dovid came to Machanayim. And Avshalom passed over Yarden, he and all the Ish Yisroel with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:13; SHMUEL BAIS 19:13; SHMUEL BAIS 20:9–12"> And Avshalom appointed Amasa over the tzava (army) instead of Yoav; now Amasa was ben ish shmo Yitra a Yisroeli, that went in to Avigal Bat Nachash Achot Tzeruyah Em Yoav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 17:1; DEVARIM 3:15; BAMIDBAR 32:1–42"> So Yisroel and Avshalom encamped in Eretz Gil'ad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:7; SHMUEL BAIS 10:1–2; SHMUEL BAIS 9:4"> And it came to pass, when Dovid was come to Machanayim, that Shovi Ben Nachash of Rabbah of the Bnei Ammon, and Machir Ben Ammi'el of Lo-Devar, and Barzillai the Gil'adi of Roglim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:18; OVADYAH 1:3; SHMUEL BAIS 16:1–2"> Brought mishkav (bedding) and sappot (bowls) and earthen vessels, and chittim (wheat), and se'orim (barley), and kemakh (flour), and roasted grain, and pol (beans), and adashim (lentils), and roasted seeds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 16:2; YESHAYAH 21:14; SHMUEL BAIS 16:14"> And devash, and khemah (curds), and tzon, and cheeses of the herd, for Dovid, and for HaAm that were with him, to eat; for they said, HaAm are hungry, and weary, and tzameh (thirsty), in the midbar.</span>
