<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:9; SHMUEL BAIS 3:2–3; SHMUEL BAIS 13:15"> And it came to pass after this, that Avshalom Ben Dovid had an achot yafeh (beautiful sister), whose shem was Tamar; and Amnon Ben Dovid loved her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:10; MELACHIM ALEF 21:4; SHIR HASHIRIM 5:8"> And Amnon was so distressed, that he fell ill for his achot Tamar; for she was betulah; but Amnon thought it impossible for him to do anything to her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:19; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:15"> But Amnon had a re'a, shmo Yonadav Ben Shime'ah Achi Dovid; and Yonadav was an ish chacham (shrewd man).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:17; VAYIKRA 18:9; ESTHER 5:13–14"> And he said unto him, Why art thou, being Ben HaMelech, becoming dal (weak, wretched) from day to day? Wilt thou not tell me? And Amnon said unto him, Ani ohev (I love) Tamar achot Avshalom achi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MARKOS 6:24–25; MISHLE 19:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:15"> And Yehonadav said unto him, Lay thee down on thy mishkav, and make thyself sick; and when avicha cometh to see thee, say unto him, now, let my achot Tamar come, and cause me to eat lechem, and prepare the briyah (food) in my sight, that I may see it, and eat it from her yad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:6; MATTITYAHU 13:33"> So Amnon lay down, and made himself sick; and when HaMelech was come to see him, Amnon said unto HaMelech, Now, let Tamar my achot come, and make me a couple of &#91;heart-shaped&#93; cakes in my sight, that I may eat from her yad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then Dovid sent to Tamar to the palace, saying, Go now to the bais of thy brother Amnon, and prepare for him briyah (food).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> So Tamar went to the bais of her brother Amnon; and he was lying down. And she took batzek (dough), and kneaded it, and made cakes in his sight, and did bake the &#91;heart-shaped&#93; cakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 45:1; YOCHANAN 3:20; SHOFETIM 3:19"> And she took a masret (pan), and poured them out before him; but he refused to eat. And Amnon said, Have kol ish go out from me. And they went out kol ish from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Amnon said unto Tamar, Bring the briyah into the cheder (&#91;bed&#93; room), that I may eat from thine yad. And Tamar took the &#91;heart-shaped&#93; cakes which she had prepared, and brought them into the bedroom to Amnon her brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 39:11–12"> And when she had brought them unto him to eat, he took hold of her, and said unto her, Come lie with me, my achot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHOFETIM 19:23; VAYIKRA 20:17; SHOFETIM 20:6"> And she answered him, No, achi, do not violate me; for no such thing ought to be done in Yisroel; do not thou this nevalah (foul deed, shameful thing).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHOFETIM 19:24; BERESHIS 20:12; BERESHIS 19:8"> And I, to where shall I cause my cherpah (shame, disgrace) to go? And as for thee, thou shalt be as one of the nevalim (wicked fools) in Yisroel. Now therefore, speak unto HaMelech; for he will not withhold me from thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 12:11; ESTHER 7:8; SHOFETIM 20:5"> Howbeit he would not pay heed unto her voice; but, being stronger than she, violated her and raped her, and lay with her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 23:17"> Then Amnon hated her exceedingly; so that the sinah (hatred) wherewith he hated her was gedolah me'od than the ahavah wherewith he had loved her. And Amnon said unto her, Get up, get out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And she said unto him, No, do not this ra'ah in sending me away which is gedolah than the other that thou didst unto me. But he would not pay heed unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then he called his na'ar that ministered unto him, and said, Put now this outside from me, and bolt the delet after her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHOFETIM 5:30; BERESHIS 37:3; TEHILLIM 45:13–14"> She had a kesones of various colors upon her; for with such were the banot HaMelech that were betulot apparelled. Then his mesharet (servant) brought her outside, and bolted the delet after her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 7:6; YIRMEYAH 2:37; SHMUEL BAIS 1:11"> And Tamar put efer (ash) on her rosh (head) and made the kri'ah (tearing, rending) on her kesones of various colors that was on her, and laid her yad on her rosh, and went on weeping as she went.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:19; BERESHIS 46:15; BERESHIS 34:2"> And Avshalom her brother said unto her, Hath Amnon thy brother been with thee? But hold now thy peace, my achot; he is thy brother; this thing take not to heart. So Tamar remained shomemah (desolate) in the bais of her brother Avshalom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 34:7; SHMUEL BAIS 3:28–29; SHMUEL ALEF 2:29"> But when Dovid HaMelech heard of all these things, he was very angry.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:17–18; BERESHIS 31:24; BERESHIS 24:50"> And Avshalom spoke unto his brother Amnon not a davar neither rah nor tov; for Avshalom hated Amnon, because he had violated and raped his achot Tamar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BERESHIS 38:12–13; SHMUEL ALEF 25:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 26:10"> And it came to pass after two full shanim, that Avshalom had gozezim (sheepshearers) in Ba'al-Chatzor, which is in Ephrayim; and Avshalom invited all the Bnei HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 11:8–15; YIRMEYAH 41:6–7; TEHILLIM 55:21"> And Avshalom came to HaMelech, and said, Hinei now, thy eved hath gozezim; let HaMelech, now, and his avadim go with thy eved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKAS 14:23; SHOFETIM 19:7–10; SHMUEL BAIS 14:22"> And HaMelech said to Avshalom, No, beni (my son), let us not all now go, lest we be a burden unto thee. And he urged him; howbeit he would not go, but put a bracha on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 20:9; SHMUEL BAIS 3:27; SHMUEL BAIS 11:13–15"> Then said Avshalom, If not, now, let achi Amnon go with us. And HaMelech said unto him, Why should he go with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MISHLE 26:24–26"> But Avshalom urged him, that he let Amnon and kol Bnei HaMelech go with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHOFETIM 19:6; SHOFETIM 19:22; SHOFETIM 19:9"> Now Avshalom had commanded his avadim, saying, Mark ye now when Amnonʼs lev is high with yayin, and when I say unto you, Strike Amnon; then kill him, fear not; have not I commanded you? Be chazak (courageous), and be bnei chayil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 18:9; MELACHIM ALEF 1:33; MELACHIM BAIS 1:9–12"> And the na'arei Avshalom did unto Amnon as Avshalom had commanded. Then all the Bnei HaMelech arose, and every man mounted him up upon his pered (mule), and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And it came to pass, while they were baderech (on the way), that report came to Dovid, saying, Avshalom hath slain all the Bnei HaMelech, and there is not one of them left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 1:11; SHMUEL BAIS 12:16; IYOV 1:20"> Then HaMelech arose, and made the kri'ah on his garments, and lay on the earth; and all his avadim stood by with their clothes torn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:9; SHMUEL BAIS 13:3; BERESHIS 27:41"> And Yonadav Ben Shime'ah achi Dovid, answered and said, Let not adoni suppose that they have slain all the young men, the Bnei HaMelech; for Amnon only is dead; for by the command of Avshalom this hath been determined from the day that he violated and raped Tamar his achot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 19:19"> Now therefore let not adoni HaMelech take the thing to his lev, to think that all the Bnei HaMelech are dead; for Amnon only is dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="BERESHIS 4:8–14; MISHLE 28:17; AMOS 5:19"> But Avshalom fled.<br />And the na'ar tzofeh (watchman) lifted up his eyes, and looked, and, hinei, there came am rav (much people) by the derech of the hillside behind him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And Yonadav said unto HaMelech, Hinei, the Bnei HaMelech come; as the davar avdecha (word of thy servant), so it is.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 18:33; SHMUEL BAIS 12:21; SHMUEL BAIS 13:15"> And it came to pass, as soon as he had made an end of speaking, that, hinei, the Bnei HaMelech came, and lifted up their voice and wailed; and HaMelech also and all his avadim wept gadol me'od.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:3; SHMUEL BAIS 14:32; SHMUEL BAIS 13:34"> But Avshalom fled, and went to Talmai Ben Ammihud Melech Geshur. And Dovid mourned for bno kol hayamim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:8; SHMUEL BAIS 14:23; SHMUEL BAIS 14:32"> So Avshalom fled, and went to Geshur, and was there shalosh shanim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:67; BERESHIS 38:12; BERESHIS 37:35"> And the nefesh of Dovid HaMelech longed to go forth unto Avshalom; for he was consoled concerning Amnon that died.</span>
</div>
</div>
