And HaMelech said unto HaAm, I will surely go forth with you myself also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 21:17; SHEMOT 17:10–12; MELACHIM ALEF 22:31"> But HaAm answered, Thou shalt not go forth; for if we flee away, they will not set their lev on us; neither if half of us die, will they set their lev on us; but now thou art worth ten thousand of us; therefore, now it is better that thou support us from the Ir.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., Machanayim">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 18:24; SHMUEL BAIS 18:1; SHMUEL ALEF 29:2"> And HaMelech said unto them, What seemeth you best I will do. And HaMelech stood by the side of the sha'ar, and kol haAm came out by hundreds and by thousands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 21:18–21; SHMUEL BAIS 16:11; TEHILLIM 103:13"> And HaMelech commanded Yoav and Avishai and Ittai, saying, Deal gently for my sake with the na'ar, even with Avshalom. And kol HaAm heard when HaMelech gave all the sarim charge concerning Avshalom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 17:18; YEHOSHUA 17:15; SHOFETIM 12:4–6"> HaAm went into the sadeh against Yisroel; the milchamah was in ya'ar (forest) of Ephrayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MISHLE 24:21; SHMUEL BAIS 2:31; SHMUEL BAIS 2:26"> Where Am Yisroel were slain before the avadim of Dovid, and there was there a magefah gedolah that day of esrim elef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 15:10; YEHOSHUA 10:11; TEHILLIM 43:1"> For the milchamah was there scattered over the face of kol ha'aretz; and the ya'ar devoured more people that day than the cherev devoured.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 14:26; MISHLE 30:17; IYOV 31:3"> And Avshalom met the avadim of Dovid. And Avshalom rode upon a pered (mule), and the pered went under the thick boughs of a great oak, and his rosh (head) caught hold of elah (tree, oak), and he was suspended between HaShomayim and ha'aretz; the pered under him went on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And a certain ish saw it, told Yoav, and said, Hinei, I saw Avshalom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="ben Dovid">b</a></sup>talui (hanged) b'elah (on a tree, oak).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Zech 12:10">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Yoav said unto the ish that told him, And, hinei, thou sawest him, and why didst thou not strike him down to the ground there? And I would have given thee ten shekels of kesef, and a khagorah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 18:5"> And the ish said unto Yoav, Though I should receive elef kesef in mine palm, yet would I not put forth mine yad against the ben HaMelech; for in ozneinu (our hearing) HaMelech charged thee and Avishai and Ittai, saying, Beware that none touch the na'ar Avshalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 14:19–20; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:13; SHMUEL BAIS 4:10–12"> Otherwise I should have dealt sheker against mine own nefesh; for there is no matter hid from HaMelech, and thou thyself wouldest have set thyself against me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 45:5; MATTITYAHU 12:40; SHMUEL BAIS 14:30"> Then said Yoav, I will not tarry thus with thee. And he took three shevatim in his yad, and thrust them through the lev Avshalom, while he was yet alive in the midst of the oak tree.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Yn 19:34">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And ten ne'arim that bore the armor of Yoav surrounded Avshalom, struck and slaughtered him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 2:28; SHMUEL BAIS 20:22; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:8"> Then Yoav blew the shofar, and HaAm returned from pursuing after Yisroel; for Yoav held back HaAm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 7:26; YEHOSHUA 8:29; SHMUEL BAIS 19:8"> And they took Avshalom, and cast him into a great pit in the forest, and laid a very great heap of avanim upon him; and all Yisroel fled, every ish to his ohel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BERESHIS 14:17; SHMUEL BAIS 14:27; SHMUEL ALEF 15:12"> Now Avshalom in his lifetime had taken and erected for himself a matzevet (monument), which is in the Emek HaMelech; for he said, I have no ben to keep my shem in remembrance; and he called the matzevet after shmo; and it is called unto this day, Avshalomʼs Monument.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:36; SHMUEL BAIS 18:31; TEHILLIM 9:4"> Then said Achima'atz Ben Tzadok, Let me now run, and take news to HaMelech, how that Hashem hath in justice vindicated him of his oyevim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 18:5; SHMUEL BAIS 18:33; SHMUEL BAIS 17:16–21"> And Yoav said unto him, Thou shalt not be ish besorah (bearer of news) this day, but thou shalt bear tidings another day; but this day thou shalt bear no tidings, because the ben HaMelech is dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then said Yoav to Cushi, Go tell HaMelech what thou hast seen. And Cushi prostrated himself unto Yoav, and ran.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:4"> Then said Achima'atz Ben Tzadok yet again to Yoav, But be what may, let me, now, also run after Cushi. And Yoav said, why wilt thou run, beni, seeing that thou hast no besorah to provide?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:4"> But howsoever, said he, let me run. And he said unto him, Rutz! (run!). Then Achima'atz ran by the derech of the plain, outran Cushi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 19:8; SHMUEL BAIS 13:34; YECHEZKEL 33:2–7"> And Dovid sat between the two she'arim (gates); and the tzofeh (watchman) went up to the gag (roof) over the sha'ar (gate) unto the chomah (wall), and lifted up his eyes, and looked, and hinei an ish running alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And the tzofeh (watchman) cried, and told HaMelech. And HaMelech said, If he be alone, there is besorah (news) in his peh (mouth). And he came rapidly, and drew near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the tzofeh (watchman) saw another man running; and the tzofeh called unto the sho'er (gatekeeper), and said, Hinei, another man running alone. And HaMelech said, This is mevaser (one bringing good news, evangelist).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 9:20; MELACHIM ALEF 1:42; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:15"> And the tzofeh (watchman) said, I see the running of the foremost is like the running of Achima'atz Ben Tzadok. And HaMelech said, He is an ish tov, and cometh with besorah tovah (good news).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See 2Sm 18:10">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 14:4; BERESHIS 14:20; BERESHIS 24:27"> And Achima'atz called, and said unto HaMelech, Shalom. And he fell down to ha'aretz upon his face before HaMelech, and said, Baruch Hashem Eloheicha, which hath delivered up the anashim that lifted up their yad against adoni HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 18:22; SHMUEL BAIS 20:9; SHMUEL BAIS 18:19–20"> And HaMelech said, Is the na'ar Avshalom, shalom? And Achima'atz answered, When Yoav sent eved HaMelech, avdecha, I saw a great tumult, but I knew not what it was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And HaMelech said unto him, Turn aside, and stand here. And he turned aside, and stood still.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 18:19; SHMUEL BAIS 18:28; LUKAS 18:7–8"> And, hinei, Cushi came; and Cushi said, Yitbaser (be informed of news), adoni HaMelech; for Hashem hath in justice vindicated thee this day from the yad of all them that rose up against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHOFETIM 5:31; SHMUEL ALEF 25:26; DANIEL 4:19"> And HaMelech said unto Cushi, Is the na'ar Avshalom shalom? And Cushi answered, The enemies of adoni HaMelech, and all that rise against thee to do thee harm, be as that na'ar is.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="T.N.
