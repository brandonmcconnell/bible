</div>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:23–25; YESHAYAH 7:8; IYOV 9:13"> And when the Syrians of Damascus came to help Hadadezer Melech Tzovah, Dovid struck down the Syrians two and twenty elef ish.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 18:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:16; YECHEZKEL 47:16"> And from Betach,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Tivchat, 1Chr 18:8">a</a></sup>and from Berotai,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Kun, 1Chr 18:8">b</a></sup>cities of Hadadezer, Dovid HaMelech took exceeding much nechoshet.</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 13:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 18:10; TEHILLIM 129:8"> Then To'i sent Yoram bno unto Dovid HaMelech, to give him shalom greetings, and to put a berachah on him, because he had fought against Hadadezer, and defeated him; for Hadadezer had been at war with To'u. And Yoram brought with him vessels of kesef, and vessels of zahav, and vessels of nechoshet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:51; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:14–16"> Which also Dovid HaMelech did dedicate as kodesh unto Hashem, with the kesef and zahav that he had dedicated as kodesh of kol HaGoyim which he subdued;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 10:14; SHMUEL BAIS 8:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 18:11"> Of Syria, and of Moav, and of the Bnei Ammon, and of the Pelishtim, and of Amalek, and of the plunder of Hadadezer Ben Rechov Melech Tzovah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 14:7; SHMUEL BAIS 7:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 18:12"> And Dovid made himself a shem when he returned from striking down Aram in the Gey Melach, being eighteen elef men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:29; BERESHIS 25:23; SHMUEL BAIS 8:6"> And he put garrisons in Edom; throughout all Edom put he garrisons, and all they of Edom became avadim to Dovid. And Hashem saved Dovid wherever he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="AMOS 5:15; TEHILLIM 89:14; TEHILLIM 72:2"> And Dovid reigned over kol Yisroel; and Dovid executed mishpat and tzedakah unto all his people.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 30:14; SHMUEL BAIS 20:7; SHMUEL BAIS 20:23"> And Benayah Ben Yehoyada was over both the Kereti and the Peleti; and Benei Dovid were kohanim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Ps 110:4">c</a></sup></span>
