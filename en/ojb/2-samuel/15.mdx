<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:5; SHMUEL BAIS 12:11; SHMUEL ALEF 8:11"> And it came to pass after this, that Avshalom prepared him merkavah and susim, and fifty ish to run before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 18:14; RUTH 4:1; IYOV 24:14"> And Avshalom rose up early, and stood beside the derech hasha'ar; and it was so, that when any ish that had a riv (lawsuit, complaint) came to HaMelech for mishpat, then Avshalom called unto him, and said, Of what town art thou? And he said, Thy eved is of one of the Shivtei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:15; YECHEZKEL 22:7; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:17"> And Avshalom said unto him, See, thy case is tovim and right; but there is no man deputy of HaMelech to hear thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHOFETIM 9:29; SHOFETIM 9:1–5; MISHLE 27:2"> Avshalom said moreover, Oh that I were made Shofet in the land, that every ish which hath any riv or mishpat might come unto me, and I would bring him justice!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 14:33; TEHILLIM 55:21; MISHLE 26:25"> And it was so, that when any ish came near to him to prostrate himself before him, he put forth his yad, and took him, and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:18; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:3; MISHLE 11:9"> And on this manner did Avshalom to kol Yisroel that came to HaMelech for mishpat; so Avshalom stole the lev anshei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YESHAYAH 58:4; SHMUEL ALEF 16:13; SHMUEL ALEF 16:1–2"> And it came to pass after arba'im shanah, that Avshalom said unto HaMelech, Now let me go and fulfill my neder, which I have vowed unto Hashem, in Chevron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:20–21; SHMUEL BAIS 13:37–38; SHMUEL ALEF 1:11"> For thy eved vowed a neder (vow) while I abode at Geshur in Syria, saying, If Hashem shall bring me again indeed to Yerushalayim, then I will serve Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And HaMelech said unto him, Go in shalom. So he arose, and went to Chevron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:38; SHMUEL BAIS 5:5; SHMUEL BAIS 3:2–3"> But Avshalom sent secret messengers throughout kol Shivtei Yisroel, saying, As soon as ye hear the sound of the shofar, then ye shall say, Avshalom reigneth in Chevron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 9:13; SHMUEL ALEF 22:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:18–19"> And with Avshalom went two hundred ish out of Yerushalayim, that were invited; and they went innocently, and they had da'as of not anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="TEHILLIM 41:9; SHMUEL BAIS 15:31; YEHOSHUA 15:51"> And Avshalom sent for Achitophel the Giloni, Dovidʼs yo'etz (counsellor), from his town, even from Giloh, while he offered zivakhim. And the kesher (conspiracy) was strong; for the people increased continually with Avshalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHOFETIM 9:3; MATTITYAHU 27:22; SHMUEL BAIS 15:6"> And there came a maggid to Dovid, saying, Lev Ish Yisroel are with Avshalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 19:9; YECHEZKEL 46:18; TEHILLIM 55:3–11"> And Dovid said unto all his avadim that were with him at Yerushalayim, Arise, and let us flee; or else we shall not escape from Avshalom; make speed to depart, lest he overtake us suddenly, and bring ra'ah (disaster) upon us, and strike the Ir with the edge of the cherev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:66–69; MISHLE 18:24; LUKAS 22:28–29"> And the avadim of HaMelech said unto HaMelech, Hinei, thy avadim are ready to do whatsoever adoni HaMelech commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 16:21–22; SHMUEL BAIS 20:3; TEHILLIM 3:1"> And HaMelech went forth, and all his Bais after him. And HaMelech left ten nashim, which were pilagshim (concubines), to be shomer over the Bais (palace).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TEHILLIM 3:1–2; KOHELET 10:7; TEHILLIM 66:12"> And HaMelech went forth, and kol haAm after him, and tarried in a place that was far off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:18; SHMUEL ALEF 30:14; SHMUEL BAIS 20:7"> And all his avadim passed on by his yad; and all the Kereti, and all the Peleti, and all the Gittim, six hundred men which came after him from Gat, passed on before HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 18:2; RUTH 1:11–13"> Then said HaMelech to Ittai the Gitti, Why goest thou also with us? Return to thy place, and abide with HaMelech; for thou art a nokhri (foreigner), and also an exile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 23:13; SHMUEL BAIS 2:6; YOCHANAN 1:17"> Whereas thou camest but yesterday, should I this day make thee wander about with us? Seeing I go to where I may, shuv (return thou), and take back thy brethren; chesed and emes be with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="RUTH 1:16–17; MISHLE 17:17; MELACHIM BAIS 4:30"> And Ittai answered HaMelech, and said, As Hashem liveth, and as adoni HaMelech liveth, surely in what makom adoni HaMelech shall be, whether in mavet or chayyim, even there also will thy eved be.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ruth 1:17">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Dovid said to Ittai, Go and pass over. And Ittai the Gitti passed over, and all his anashim, and all the little ones that were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 16:2; MELACHIM ALEF 2:37; YOCHANAN 18:1"> And kol ha'aretz wept with a kol gadol (loud voice), and kol haAm crossed over; HaMelech also himself crossed over the brook Kidron, and kol haAm crossed over, toward the derech of the midbar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 4:15; SHMUEL BAIS 8:17; SHMUEL BAIS 20:25"> And hinei Tzadok also, and all the Levi'im were with him, bearing the Aron Brit HaElohim; and they set down the Aron HaElohim; and Evyatar (Abiathar) went up, until kol HaAm had done passing out of the Ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 25:30; TEHILLIM 26:8; TEHILLIM 43:3–4"> And HaMelech said unto Tzadok, Carry back the Aron HaElohim into the Ir; if I shall find chen (grace) in the eyes of Hashem, He will bring me again, and show me both Him, and His habitation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 3:18; SHMUEL BAIS 22:20; MELACHIM ALEF 10:9"> But if He thus say, I have no delight in thee; hinei, here am I, let Him do to me as seemeth tov unto Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 9:9; SHMUEL BAIS 17:17; SHMUEL BAIS 24:11"> HaMelech said also unto Tzadok HaKohen, Art not thou the Ro'eh (seer)? Return into the Ir in shalom, and your two banim with you, Achima'atz binecha, and Yehonatan Ben Evyatar (Abiathar).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 17:16; SHMUEL BAIS 15:23; SHMUEL BAIS 17:1"> See, I will tarry in the fords of the wilderness, until there come davar from you to inform me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Tzadok therefore and Evyatar (Abiathar) carried the Aron HaElohim back to Yerushalayim; and they tarried there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="ESTHER 6:12; SHMUEL BAIS 19:4; LUKAS 19:41"> And Dovid went up by the ascent of Ma'aleh HaZeitim (Mount of Olives), and wept as he went up, and had his rosh covered, and he went barefoot; and kol haAm with him covered every ish his rosh, and they went up, weeping as they went up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 17:14; SHMUEL BAIS 17:23; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:15"> And one told Dovid, saying, Achitophel is among the conspirators with Avshalom. And Dovid said, Hashem, turn now the etza (counsel) of Achitophel into foolishness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 16:2; SHMUEL BAIS 1:2; TEHILLIM 3:3–5"> And it came to pass, that when Dovid was come to the top of the mount, where he worshiped Elohim, hinei, Chushai the Arki came to meet him with his kesones torn, and adamah upon his rosh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 19:35"> Unto whom Dovid said, If thou passest on with me, then thou shalt be a massa (burden) unto me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 8:2; SHMUEL BAIS 16:16–19; MATTITYAHU 10:16"> But if thou return to the Ir, and say unto Avshalom, I will be thy eved, O melech; as I have been eved avicha hitherto, so will I now also be thy eved; then mayest thou for me bring to nought the etza (counsel) of Achitophel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 17:15–16"> And hast thou not there with thee Tzadok and Evyatar (Abiathar) the kohanim? Therefore it shall be, that what thing soever thou shalt hear out of the Bais HaMelech, thou shalt tell it to Tzadok and Evyatar (Abiathar) the kohanim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 17:17; SHMUEL BAIS 15:27; SHMUEL BAIS 18:19–33"> Hinei, they have there with them their two banim, Achima'atz son of Tzadok, and Yehonatan of Evyatar; by them ye shall send unto me everything that ye can hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 16:15–16; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 27:33"> So Chushai the re'eh Dovid came into the Ir, and Avshalom came into Yerushalayim.</span>
