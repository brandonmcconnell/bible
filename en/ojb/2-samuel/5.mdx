<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 2:6; MICHOH 5:4; YECHEZKEL 34:23"> Also in time past, when Sha'ul was melech over us, thou wast he that didst lead out and brought in Yisroel; and Hashem said to thee, Thou shalt feed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="like a shepherd">a</a></sup>My people Yisroel, and thou shalt become Nagid over Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 2:4; SHMUEL ALEF 16:13; SHMUEL ALEF 23:18"> So all the zekenim of Yisroel came to HaMelech at Chevron; and Dovid HaMelech cut a Brit with them in Chevron before Hashem; v'yimshechu (and they anointed) Dovid Melech Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:27; LUKAS 3:23; MELACHIM ALEF 2:11"> Dovid was in age shloshim shanah when he began to reign, and he reigned arba'im shanim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 2:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:4; MELACHIM ALEF 2:11"> In Chevron he reigned over Yehudah sheva shanim and shishah chodashim; and in Yerushalayim he reigned shloshim v'shalosh shanah over kol Yisroel and Yehudah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHOFETIM 1:8; YIRMEYAH 37:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:4–9"> And HaMelech and his anashim went to Yerushalayim unto the Yevusi, the inhabitants of the land; which spoke unto Dovid, saying, Thou shalt not come in here; but the ivrim (blind ones) and the pisechim (lame ones) shall repel thee; thinking, Dovid cannot come in here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:10; SHMUEL BAIS 6:16; SHMUEL BAIS 6:12"> Nevertheless Dovid captured the Metzudat Tziyon (Fortress of Zion); the same is Ir Dovid (City of Dovid).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:16–17; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:6–9; SHMUEL ALEF 17:25"> And Dovid said on that day, Anyone that striketh the Yevusi will have to reach the water shaft, the pisechim and the ivrim whom the nefesh Dovid hates; thus the saying… The ivver and the piseach are here, he will not enter the bais.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:15; MELACHIM ALEF 9:24; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:5"> So Dovid dwelt in the Metzudah, and called it Ir Dovid. And Dovid built &#91;Ir Dovid&#93; all around from Millo and inward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:31; TEHILLIM 46:11"> And Dovid went on, and grew gadol, and Hashem Elohei Tzva'os was with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 14:1; MELACHIM ALEF 5:1–2; MELACHIM ALEF 5:18"> And Chiram Melech Tzor sent malachim to Dovid, and cedar trees, and charashim of etz (carpenters) and charashim of even (stone masons) kir (wall, i.e., masons of wall stones); and they built Dovid a Bais (Palace).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 7:16; MELACHIM ALEF 10:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 14:2"> And Dovid had da'as that Hashem had established him Melech Yisroel, and that he had exalted his mamlachah for the sake of His people Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:9; DEVARIM 17:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 13:21"> And Dovid took him more pilagshim and nashim out of Yerushalayim after he was come from Chevron; and there were more banim and banot born to Dovid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:5–9; LUKAS 2:31; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 14:4"> And these are the shmot of those that were born unto him in Yerushalayim: Shammua, and Shovav, and Natan, and Sh'lomo,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 14:5"> Yivchar also, and Elishua, and Nepheg, and Yaphia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 14:7"> And Elishama, and Elyada, and Eliphelet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 23:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 14:8–9; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:16"> But when the Pelishtim heard that they had mashechu (anointed) Dovid Melech over Yisroel, all the Pelishtim came up to seek Dovid; but Dovid heard of it, and went down to the metzudah (stronghold).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:8; SHMUEL BAIS 23:13; BERESHIS 14:5"> The Pelishtim also came and spread themselves in the Emek Repha'im.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 2:1; SHMUEL ALEF 23:2; SHMUEL BAIS 5:23"> And Dovid inquired of Hashem, saying, Shall I go up against the Pelishtim? Wilt Thou deliver them into mine yad? And Hashem said unto Dovid, Go up; for I will doubtless deliver the Pelishtim into thine yad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YESHAYAH 28:21"> So Dovid came to Ba'al-Peratzim, and Dovid struck them there, and said, Hashem hath paratz (broken out) upon mine enemies before me, as of peretz mayim. Therefore he called the shem of that place Ba'al-Peratzim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 14:11–12; DEVARIM 7:5; SHMUEL ALEF 5:2–6"> And there they left their atzabim, and Dovid and his anashim burned them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 14:13; SHMUEL BAIS 5:18; MELACHIM ALEF 20:22"> And the Pelishtim came up yet again, and spread themselves out in the Emek Repha'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 14:14; MATTITYAHU 9:29–30; MATTITYAHU 8:23–25"> And when Dovid inquired of Hashem, He said, Thou shalt not go up; but circle around behind them, and come upon them opposite the mulberry trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 7:6; SHOFETIM 4:14; SHOFETIM 7:15"> And let it be, when thou hearest the sound of marching in the tops of the mulberry trees, that then thou shalt bestir thyself; for then shall Hashem go out before thee, to strike the machaneh of the Pelishtim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 14:16; YEHOSHUA 16:10; YESHAYAH 28:21"> And Dovid did so, as Hashem had commanded him; and struck the Pelishtim from Geva until thou come to Gezer.</span>
</div>
