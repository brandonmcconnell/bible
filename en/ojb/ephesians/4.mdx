<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:27; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:12"> Therefore I impart chizzuk (strengthening) to you, I, the prisoner of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu, and I admonish you to walk and fier zich (comport oneself) in your derech in a manner worthy of the kri'ah by which you were called,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:12–13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:4–5"> With all anavah (humility) of mind and meekness, with savlanut (longsuffering), showing forbearance to one another in ahavah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:13–15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:12–13"> Being eager to keep the achdus (unity) of the Ruach Hakodesh in the uniting bond of shalom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:4–5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:4–13"> As there is one Guf (Body)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gn 47:18; Ps 16:9-10; Job 19:25-27; Isa 53:11">a</a></sup>and one Ruach Hakodesh, as also you were called in one Tikvah (hope) of your kri'ah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:13; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:21"> Adon echad, Emunah achat, Tevilah achat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:36; MALACHI 2:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:6"> One Elokim and Avi khol (Father of all), who is over all and through all and in all.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dt 6:4; Zech 14:9">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:7–11"> But to each of us was given the Chen v'Chesed Hashem according to the measure of the Matnat HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 68:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:15; SHMUEL ALEF 30:26"> Therefore it says, ALITA LAMMAROM SHAVITA SHEVI LAKACHTA MATANOT<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 68:18[19]">c</a></sup>(You ascended to the height, you led captive, you received gifts), He has led captive a host of captives, He had brought matanot (gifts) to Bnei Adam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:13; MATTITYAHU 12:40; MISHLE 30:4"> Now if He “ASCENDED”, what can it mean except that also He descended into the lower parts of ha'aretz?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:26; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:20–23"> The one who descended is Himself also the one having ascended far above all the Shomayim, that He might fill all things.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Prov 30:1-4">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 3:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:28–29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:20"> And He gave some to be shlichim, and some nevi'im, and some gifted to be used in Kiruv efforts for Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, and some supervising mashgichim ruchaniyim (spiritual overseers) who are ro'im and morim in the Kehillah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:11–14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:27"> For the equipping of the Kedoshim for the work of avodas kodesh ministry, to the building up of HaGuf HaMoshiach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:28; YOCHANAN 17:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:5"> Until we all attain to the achdus (unity) of the emunah and to the da'as (knowledge) of the Zun fun der Oybershter, to the in Bnei Chayil maturity, to the measure of the stature of the melo (plentitude) of Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:17–18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:20"> In this way, we are no longer olalim (infants) tossed by waves and carried around by every wind of limmud (instruction), by the cunning of Bnei Adam, with craftiness leading to the scheming of remiyah (deceit) and madduchei shav (delusion);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Isa 57:20">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:25; ZECHARYAH 8:16"> But saying HaEmes in ahavah, let us grow up in every respect unto Him who is the Rosh, Moshiach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:19; YOCHANAN 15:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:2"> From whom all HaGuf HaMoshiach being fitly joined together and being united in an agudah binding by that which every joint contributes according as each partʼs proper working process promotes the growth of HaGuf HaMoshiach in building itself up in ahavah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:21; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:3–4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:1–2"> This therefore I say and give solemn edut in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu: no longer are you to fier zich (comport oneself) in your derech as also the Goyim walk, in the futility of their machshavot (thoughts),</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:21–23"> Their binah (understanding) being in choshech, having been alienated from the life of Hashem because of the ignorance existing in them, because of the KESHI<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Dt 9:27">f</a></sup>(stubbornness, hardness) of their levavot,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Dt 29:4">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:24–26; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:2"> Who, having put away remorse, gave themselves over to zimah (lewdness) for the practice of every kind of tumah (uncleanness) with chamdanut (covetousness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 2:11–14; YOCHANAN 6:45; MATTITYAHU 11:29"> But you did not so have lernen of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:7–8"> If indeed you heard about Him and took shiurim in Him, as HaEmes is in Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:6; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:1; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:21"> For the torah you received was that you must disrobe from your former derech of your old self, the one being corrupted according to the ta'avot of AKOV<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Jer 17:9">h</a></sup>(deceitful) remiyah (guile),</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:6; TEHILLIM 51:10"> And to be renewed by the Ruach Hakodesh controlling your machshavot (thoughts),</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:10–14; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:10"> And to enrobe yourself in the Adam HeChadash (the new Man, humanity), having been created according to the demut Hashem in tzidkat Elokeinu and in the kedushah of HaEmes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:9; ZECHARYAH 8:16; MISHLE 12:22"> Therefore, disrobed from Sheker (Falsehood, Lying), let us say HaEmes,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Zech 8:16">i</a></sup>each one with his re'a (neighbor), because we are evarim (members) one of another.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ps 15:2; Lv 19:11">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19; TEHILLIM 4:4; TEHILLIM 37:8"> Have ka'as and do not sin; do not let the shemesh go down on your anger.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ps 4:4">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:7; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:16"> Do not leave an open door for HaSatan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:11–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:10"> Let the one stealing no longer be a ganav, but rather let the ganav get a parnasah (livelihood) and toil with his own hands at something beneficial, that he may have something to share with the one who is nitzrach (needy).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:11; KOHELET 10:12"> Let no lashon hora proceed out of your peh, but only a dvar that is tov, for edification in accordance with the need, that it may mediate Chen v'Chesed Hashem to the ones hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:19; YESHAYAH 63:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:13–14"> And do not grieve the Ruach Hakodesh of Hashem, by whom you were sealed for the Yom HaGeulah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Isa 63:10">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:8; KOHELET 7:9; LETTER TO TITOS 3:2–3"> Let all merirut lev (bitterness) and ka'as and wrath and clamor and lashon hora be removed from you, with all resha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:12–13; MATTITYAHU 6:14–15; MARKOS 11:25"> And have Chen v'Chesed graciousness with one another. Be kind, tender-hearted, forgiving each other, as also Hashem in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach forgave you.</span>
