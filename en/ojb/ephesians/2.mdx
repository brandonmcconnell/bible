<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:12; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:3"> In which you amol (formerly) fier zich (comported oneself), according to the course of the Olam Hazeh, according to the Sar Memshelet HaAvir (Prince of Power of Air),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="the Satan, HaSatan, teivel, the devil">b</a></sup>the ruach now working in the bnei haMeri (sons of Rebellion).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:16–24"> Among them we too all formerly conducted ourselves in the ta'avot of our basar, following the lusts of the flesh and of the machshavot (thoughts), and we were by nature Bnei Za'am (Sons of Wrath) of Hashem, as also the rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:7; TEHILLIM 86:15"> But Hashem, being rich in rachamim (mercy), because of His ahavah harabba (great love) with which He loved us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:10"> Even when we were dead in our peysha'im, He made us alive together with Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach — by the unmerited Chen v'Chesed Hashem you have been delivered in your personal Geulah and the Yeshu'as Elokeinu<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 103:12">c</a></sup>—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:1–3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:4–5"> And raised us up with Moshiach and seated us with Him in Shomayim in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua/Yeshua,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:16–17; LETTER TO TITOS 3:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:4"> In order that, in the Olam HaBah, He might display the surpassing osher of the Chen v'Chesed Hashem of Him in chesed toward us in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua/Yeshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:22–26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:16"> For by unmerited Chen v'Chesed Hashem you have been delivered from Hashemʼs Mishpat (Judgment) and granted a share in the Geulah (Redemption), through emunah; and this is not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="an einfal (intuitive idea)">d</a></sup>of yourselves, it is a matnat Hashem(gift of G-d);</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:6; LETTER TO TITOS 3:3–5"> Not the result<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="of the zokheh (merit)">e</a></sup>of doing Gezetz ma'asim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ac 15:1; Ga 5:34">f</a></sup>so that before Hashem no man should be a ravrevan (boaster, braggart).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Dt 9:5">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:21"> For we are His masterpiece, having been created in Moshiach Yehoshua for ma'asim tovim, which Hashem prepared beforehand, that the derech of our halakhah should be in them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isa 29:23; 42:7; 60:21;">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:11"> Therefore, have zikaron (remembrance) that formerly you, the non-Jews in the flesh, who are called “arelim” (uncircumcised ones) by the ones being called “nimolim” (circumcised ones) which is a Milah performed in the flesh by human hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:21; YIRMEYAH 31:31–34"> Have zikaron (remembrance) that you were at that time bazunder (unrelated and separate) from Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, having been alienated from the torat haEzrakhut (the citizenship) in the Am Brit, from Yisroel, being zarim (strangers) to the Beritot HaHavtacha, farloiren (lost) and having no tikvah (hope) and without G-d in the Olam Hazeh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Isa 14:1; 65:1">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:39; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:20–22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:13–14"> But now in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, you, who formerly where in the outermost courts, have been brought near by the kapporah of the dahm of Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:15"> For Moshiach himself is our shalom, who made the Shnayim into Echad, having broken down in the basar of Moshiach the barrier of the Mechitzah (the dividing partition), the Soreg (barrier),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="of the holy precinct in the Beis HaMikdash between Jews and non-Jews">j</a></sup>the Eyvah (Enmity),</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:21–22"> By annulling the chok (decree, law) of mishpatim in ordinances that the Shnayim He might create in Himself into Adam Chadash Echad (One New Regenerated Humanity), arbitrating shalom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:20–22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:18–21"> And that Moshiach might bring the ritztzuy (reconciliation, cessation of enmity), reconciling to Hashem the Shnayim into one guf (body)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gn 47:18; Ps 16:9-10; Job 19:25-27; Isa 53:11">k</a></sup>through the Moshiachʼs Etz, having put to death the Eyvah by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YESHAYAH 57:19–21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:36; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:1"> And having come, Moshiach preached shalom to you, the ones in the outermost courts, and shalom to the ones near;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Ps 148:14; Isa 57:19">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:2"> Because through Moshiach we both have HaSha'ar laHashem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Ps 118:20">m</a></sup>(gate to approach G-dʼs presence, access of the tzaddikim) by one Ruach Hakodesh to Elokim HaAv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:20; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:10"> Therefore, then, no longer are you zarim and aliens, but you are fellow citizens of the Kedoshim and bnei bayit members of the household of G-d,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:4–5; YESHAYAH 28:16; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:7–8"> Having been built upon the yesod (foundation) of the Shlichim and Nevi'im, Moshiach Yehoshua himself being the ROSH PINAH,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Ps 118:22">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:16–17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:16"> In whom all the binyan (edifice), being fitly joined together, grows into a Heikhal Kadosh b'Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:4–5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:16; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:16"> In whom also you are being built together into a Mishkan of Hashem in the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
