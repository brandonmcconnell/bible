<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:34; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:14"> And fier zich (comport oneself) in your derech in ahavah, as also Moshiach had ahavah for us and gave Himself up on behalf of us as a korban and zevach to Hashem for a RE'ACH HANNICHOACH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gn 8:21">a</a></sup>(a pleasant aroma).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:19–21"> But do not let zenut (fornication) or anything of tumah (uncleanness) or chamdanut (covetousness) even be named among you, as is proper for Kedoshim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:29; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:18"> And also let there be nothing that is an ERVAT DAVAR (indecent thing) or the tipshus (foolery) or foolish talking of letzim (coarse jesting) or shtiklech (misconduct), which are not fitting, but rather hodayah (thanksgiving).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:21; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:9–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:3"> For of this you have da'as vada (with certainty), that every zoneh (fornicator) or impure person or covetous person — chamdanut is avodah zarah, avodah elilim — does not have a nachalah in the Malchut of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:8; MATTITYAHU 24:4"> Let no one deceive you with empty devarim. For, because of these things the Charon Af Hashem comes upon the Bnei HaMeri (sons of Rebellion).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HISGALUS 18:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:11; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:22"> Therefore, do not be shuttafim (partners) with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:12; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:18"> For you were once Choshech, but now you are Ohr (Light) in Hashem. Fier zich (comport oneself) in your derech as yeladim of HaOhr.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:22–23; (III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:11"> For the p'ri HaOhr is in doing chesed and tzedek and emes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:1–2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:28; TEHILLIM 19:14"> Proving that which is well-pleasing be'einei Hashem(in the eyes of G-d).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:12; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:20; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:6"> And do not involve yourself in the ma'asei haChoshech (works of darkness), those works without p'ri; but rather even expose them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:3; YIRMEYAH 23:24; KOHELET 12:14"> For as to the things being done by them under cover of secrecy, it brings bushah (shame) and is a bizayon (disgrace) even to speak of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:20–21; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:5; HOSHEA 2:10"> But everything being exposed by HaOhr (Light) becomes visible,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YESHAYAH 60:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:11–12; YESHAYAH 26:19"> For everything becoming visible is HaOhr (Light); therefore here is the meaning of these words, KUMI<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Isa 51:17; 60:1">b</a></sup>(Rise up), sleeper, and arise from the MESIM,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 26:19">c</a></sup>AND Moshiach WILL SHINE ON YOU.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 60:1-2; Mal 3:20">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:5; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:27"> Therefore, fier zich (comport oneself) in your derech with a careful walk, not as kesilim (fools) but as chachamim (wise ones),</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:5; AMOS 5:13; YOCHANAN 12:35"> Redeeming the time, because the yamim are ra'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:9"> Therefore, do not be foolish, but have binah of what the ratzon Hashem is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MISHLE 20:1; LUKAS 1:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:13"> And do not become a shikker with schnapps, in which is dissipation, but be filled with the Ruach Hakodesh,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Lv 10:9; Prov 20:1; Isa 28:7">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:26; TEHILLIM 95:2"> Speaking to one another in Tehillim and shirim and hymns and neshamah niggunim mi Ruach Hakodesh (soul melodies from the Ruach Hakodesh), singing and making melody in your levavot to Adonoi,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ps 27:6">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:18; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:15"> Giving todot always for everything to Eloheinu, even Avinu b'Shem Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Job 1:21; Ps 34:1">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:22"> Being submissive to one another in the yirat HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:18–1; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:1–6; LETTER TO TITOS 2:5"> Nashim (wives), make yourselves accountable to your ba'alim (husbands) as to Adoneinu,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gn 3:16">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:3–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:22–23"> Because a ba'al (husband) is rosh of the isha as also Moshiach is rosh of the Brit Chadasha Kehillah, being Himself the Go'el (Savior) of HaGuf (the Body of Moshiach).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gn 47:18; Ps 16:9-10; Job 19:25-27; Isa 53:11">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:33; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:20; LETTER TO TITOS 2:7"> But as the Brit Chadasha Kehillah is accountable to Moshiach, so also the nashim should be to their ba'alim in everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:28"> Ba'alim, have ahavah for your nashim, as also Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach has ahavah for the Brit Chadasha Kehillah and gave Himself up on behalf of her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:22; YOCHANAN 15:3; YECHEZKEL 36:25"> That He might bring her to kedushah, having given her tohorah (purification) by the tevilah of the mikveh mayim of the Dvar Hashem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:22; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:14; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19"> That he &#91;as a Choson&#93; might present to Himself &#91;as His Kallah&#93; the Brit Chadasha Kehillah in all her kavod TAMIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ex 12:5; Lv 22:20; Isa 53:7-9SHIR HASHIRIM">j</a></sup>(unblemished) and without wrinkle or any such things, but that she may be kedoshah and without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:25; MATTITYAHU 19:5; BERESHIS 2:21–24"> So also the ba'alim ought to show ahavah for their own nashim as their own gufim;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gn 2:23-24">k</a></sup>the one with ahavah for his own isha has ahavah for himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 34:14–15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:31; KOHELET 4:5"> For no one ever had sin'as chinom for his own basar but nourishes and cherishes it, just as Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach also does the Brit Chadasha Kehillah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:12–27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:5"> Because we are evarim of HaGuf HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="BERESHIS 2:24; MATTITYAHU 19:5; MARKOS 10:7–8"> AL KEN YAAZAV ISH ES AVIV V'ES IMMO V'DAVAK B'ISHTO V'HAYU L'VASAR ECHAD (Because of this a man will leave his father and his mother, and will be joined to the wife of him, and the two will be one basar).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gn 2:24">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:2; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:2; YESHAYAH 54:5"> This is a sod hagadol, but I am referring to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and the Brit Chadasha Kehillah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Song 3:6—4:12; Gn 2:23,24; 24:1-7; 41:45; 47:18; Ex 2:21; Ruth 1:16-17; 3:9">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:2–7; ESTHER 1:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:25"> Each of you, however, should show ahavah for his isha as himself, and an isha should reverence her ba'al (husband).</span>
