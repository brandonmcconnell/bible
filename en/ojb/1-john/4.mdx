<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:21; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:1"> Chaverim, do not believe every ruach.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Jer 29:8">a</a></sup>But test the ruchot (spirits), if they be of Hashem, because many nevi'ei sheker have gone out into the Olam Hazeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:3; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:16"> By this we have da'as of the Ruach Hakodesh of Hashem: every ruach which makes hoda'ah (confession) of Yehoshua, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach as having come in the basar is of Hashem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:22; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:18"> And every ruach which does not make hoda'ah of Yehoshua is not of Hashem. And this is the ruach of the Anti-Moshiach, which you have heard that it is coming; and now it already is in the Olam Hazeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:31; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:37"> Yeladim, you are of Hashem, and you have overcome them, because greater is the One in you than the one in the Olam Hazeh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2Kgs 6:16">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:23; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:2–3; YOCHANAN 17:14"> They are of the Olam Hazeh; therefore, of the Olam Hazeh they speak, and the Olam Hazeh pays heed to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:17; YOCHANAN 10:27; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:12–14"> We are of Hashem; the one having da'as of Hashem pays heed to us; he who is not of Hashem does not pay heed to us. From this we have da'as of the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hakodesh">c</a></sup>of Emes and the ruach tatu'im (spirit of delusion, error).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:20–1; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:8; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:22"> Chaverim, let us have ahavah one for another, because ahavah is of Hashem, and everyone having ahavah has been born of Hashem and has da'as of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:16; TEHILLIM 86:15; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:11"> The one not having ahavah did not have da'as of Hashem, because Hashem is ahavah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:16; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:32"> By this was made hisgalus of the ahavah of Hashem among us, because Hashem has sent His Ben Yechid into the Olam Hazeh that we may live through Him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Yn 3:16">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:4–5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:8–10; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:19"> In this is ahavah, not that we have had ahavah for Hashem, but that Hashem had ahavah for us and gave His Zun fun der Oybershter as a kapporah for chattoteinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:12–13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:13; YOCHANAN 13:34"> Chaverim, if Hashem so had ahavah for us, also we ought to have ahavah one for another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:18; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:17–18"> No one has ever beheld Hashem. If we have ahavah one for another, G-d makes His ma'on in us and the Ahavas Hashem in us has been made shleimah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:20–22; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:16–17"> By this we have da'as that we have our ma'on in Him and He in us, because of the Ruach Hakodesh of Him He has given us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10; YOCHANAN 3:16–17; YOCHANAN 4:42"> And we<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Shlichim">e</a></sup>have beheld and we give solemn hoda'ah that HaAv has sent HaBen as Moshi'a HaOlam (Go'el, Oisleizer).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:9; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:5; MATTITYAHU 10:32"> Whoever makes hoda'ah (confession) that Yehoshua (Yeshua) is the Zun fun der Oybershter, Hashem makes His ma'on in him and he in Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:24; TEHILLIM 36:7–9; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:1"> And we have had da'as and emunah in the ahavah which Hashem has for us. Hashem is ahavah. The one making his ma'on in ahavah makes his ma'on in Hashem and Hashem in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:12; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:29"> By this has the ahavah become shleimah with us, that we may have bitachon in the Yom HaDin, because as that One is, so we are also in the Olam Hazeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:15; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:12"> Pachad (fear) is not in ahavah, but the ahavah hashleimah casts out pachad, because pachad has to do with pachad of the onesh (punishment) and the one with pachad has not been perfected in ahavah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10; YOCHANAN 3:16; YOCHANAN 15:16"> We have ahavah because rishonah He had ahavah for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:12"> If anyone says I have ahavah for Hashem and the Ach b'Moshiach he hates, he is a shakran (liar). For the one not having ahavah for the Ach b'Moshiach of him whom he has seen, how can he have ahavah for the Elohim whom he has not seen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 22:37–39; VAYIKRA 19:18; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:11"> And this mitzvah we have from Him that the one having ahavah for Hashem should have ahavah also for the Ach b'Moshiach of him.</span>
