<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:26; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:18"> See what kind of ahavah HaAv has given to us, that yeladim of Elohim we should be called. And so we are. Therefore, the Olam Hazeh does not have da'as of us, because it did not have da'as of Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:12"> Chaverim, now yeladim of Elohim we are, and not yet it is niglah (is revealed) what we will be. We have da'as that when He is niglah, like Him we will be, because we will see Him as He is.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ps 17:15">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:1; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:6; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:14"> And everyone having this tikvah (hope) in Him keeps himself in tohorah (purification, keeps himself pure), even as that One is tahor.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 18:26">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:15"> Everyone practicing chet also does averah, and chet is averah al HaTorah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:28; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18"> And you have da'as that that One niglah (is revealed) that chata'einu (our sins) He might take away,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 53:4,12">c</a></sup>and chet in Him is not.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 53:9">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:11; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:4; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9"> Everyone making his maon in Him does not go on sinning; everyone sinning has not seen Him nor has had da'as of Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:29; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:15–16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:13"> Yeladim, let no<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="navi sheker, 1Y 4:1">e</a></sup>deceive you; the one practicing tzedek is tzodek, even as that One is Tzaddik.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:14; YOCHANAN 8:44; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:15"> The one practicing averos is of HaSatan, because from the beginning HaSatan sins. For this tachlis was made hisgalus of the Ben HaElohim, that he might destroy the pe'ulot HaSatan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:18; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:23; YOCHANAN 1:13"> Everyone having been born of Hashem is not practicing chet, because His Zera makes maon in him, and he cannot abide sinning, because he is born of Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ps 119:3">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:8; (III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:11; LUKAS 6:35"> By this is made hisgalus of the yeladim of Hashem and the yeladim of HaSatan: everyone not practicing tzedek is not of Hashem; also the one not having ahavah for his Ach b'Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:12; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:7"> Because this is the divrei Torah which we heard from the beginning, that we have ahavah one for another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:4; BERESHIS 4:4–15; MISHLE 29:27"> Not as Kayin, who was of the evil one, and slaughtered the ach of him; and for what cause did he slaughter him? Because the ma'asim of him were ra'im and the ma'asim of the ach of him were ma'asei tzedek.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gn 4:8; Ps 38:20; Prov 29:10">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:18–19; YOCHANAN 17:14; LUKAS 21:17"> And, Achim b'Moshiach, do not marvel if the Olam Hazeh hates you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:35; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:2; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:20–21"> We have da'as that we have made our histalkus (passing) out of Mavet into Chayyim, because we have ahavah for the Achim b'Moshiach; the one not having ahavah makes his ma'on in death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:21–22; MISHLE 26:24–26; HISGALUS 21:8"> Everyone hating his Ach b'Moshiach is a rotzeach (murderer). And you have da'as that every rotzeach does not have Chayyei Olam making a ma'on in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:9–11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:2; YOCHANAN 13:34"> By this we have had da'as of ahavah, because that One on behalf of us laid down his nefesh;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isa 53:10">h</a></sup>and we ought, on behalf of the Achim b'Moshiach, to lay down our nefashot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:16; YESHAYAH 58:7–10; DEVARIM 15:7–11"> Now whoever has vital possessions of the Olam Hazeh and sees the Ach b'Moshiach of him being nitzrach (needy) and has no rachamim (mercy) on him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Dt 15:7,8">i</a></sup>and refuses gemilut Chesed, how does the ahavas Hashem make ma'on in him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:9; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:22"> Yeladim, let us not have ahavah in dvar or in lashon but in ma'aseh and in Emes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ezek 33:31">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:22; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:10–11"> And by this we will have da'as that we are of HaEmes, and before Him we will persuade our levavot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 139:1–4; YIRMEYAH 17:10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:13"> That if our levavot condemn us, Hashem is greater than our levavot, and He has da'as of all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:28; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:16; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:12"> Chaverim, if our levavot do not bring us under the gezar din (verdict) of ashem (guilty), we have bitachon before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:7; YOCHANAN 9:31; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:14"> And whatever we request, we receive from Him, because we are shomer of His mitzvot and the things pleasing before Him we practice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:29; YOCHANAN 13:34; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:22"> And this is the mitzvah of Him, that we have emunah b'Shem of the Ben HaElohim, Yehoshua, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, and that we have ahavah one for another, as He gave mitzvah to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:16; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:15–16; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:12–13"> And the one being shomer of His mitzvot makes his ma'on in Him and He makes His ma'on in him; and by this we have da'as that He abides in us, by the Ruach Hakodesh whom He gave to us.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Yn 14:21,23; Ro 8:9,14,16">k</a></sup></span>
