<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:34–35; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:22–24"> By this we have da'as that we have ahavah for the yeladim of Hashem, when we have ahavas Hashem and we are shomer over His mitzvot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:15; YOCHANAN 15:10; YOCHANAN 14:21–24"> For this is the ahavas Hashem, that we are shomer over His mitzvot, and His mitzvot are not burdensome.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Dt 30:11">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:57; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:4; YOCHANAN 16:33"> Because whatever<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="refers to every believer">b</a></sup>that has been born of Hashem obtains nitzachon (victory) over the Olam Hazeh. And this is the nitzachon (victory), the nitzachon that overcomes the Olam Hazeh, even<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="what makes us a believer">c</a></sup>our emunah (faith).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:15; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:1"> And who is the one overcoming the Olam Hazeh except the one with emunah that Yehoshua is the Zun fun der Oybershter?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:26; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:14; YOCHANAN 16:13"> This One is the One having come by mayim and dahm, Yehoshua, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach; not by the mayim only, but by the mayim and by the dahm; and the Ruach Hakodesh is the one giving solemn eidus, because the Ruach Hakodesh is HaEmes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:19; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:32"> Because there are shloshah giving solemn eidus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:6–7; YOCHANAN 15:26; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:21"> the Ruach Hakodesh and the mayim and the dahm, and the shloshah are for solemn eidus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 3:16–17; MATTITYAHU 17:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:31"> If the solemn eidus of Bnei Adam we receive, the solemn eidus of Hashem is greater, because this is the eidus of Hashem that He has testified concerning the Son of the Oybeshter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:6; YOCHANAN 3:33"> The one with emunah in the Zun fun der Oybershter has the eidus, even the eidus (witness), in himself; the one without emunah in Hashem has made Hashem a shakran (liar), because he has not had emunah in the eidus which Hashem has testified concerning His Zun fun der Oybershter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:23; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:25; YOCHANAN 3:36"> And this is the eidus, that Hashem gave Chayyei Olam to us, and this Chayyim is in His Zun fun der Oybershter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:36; YOCHANAN 5:24; YOCHANAN 1:12"> The one having HaBen has HaChayyim; the one not having the Zun fun der Oybershter does not have Chayyim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:31; YOCHANAN 1:12; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:10–11"> These things I wrote to you who have emunah b'Shem the Zun fun der Oybershter, that you vada (with certainty) may have da'as that you have Chayyei Olam.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Yn 20:31">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:13; YIRMEYAH 33:3; YIRMEYAH 29:12–13"> And this is the bitachon which we have with Him, that if we daven and request anything according to His ratzon (will), He hears us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 11:9–10; MARKOS 11:24; MISHLE 15:29"> And if we have da'as that He hears us, whatever we ask, we have da'as that we have the requests which we asked from Him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1Kgs 3:12">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:4–6; SHMUEL ALEF 2:25; YIRMEYAH 11:14"> If anyone sees his Ach b'Moshiach sinning an averah not leading to mavet, he will ask and Hashem will give to him Chayyim, to the ones sinning a chet not leading to mavet. There is an averah leading to mavet. I do not say we should make request concerning that averah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:4; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:16; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:7–10"> Kol avon is chet, and there is chet not leading to mavet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:4; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:1"> We have da'as that everyone having been born of Hashem does not continually sin, but the One having been born of G-d (Zun fun der Oybershter Moshiach) is shomer over him and HaSatan does not touch him.</span>
</div>

</div>
