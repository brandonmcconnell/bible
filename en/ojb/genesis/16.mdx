<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BERESHIS 31:53; SHEMOT 5:21; TEHILLIM 7:8"> And Sarai said unto Avram, My wrong be upon thee; I have given my shifchah unto thy kheyk; and when she saw that she had conceived, I was despised in her eyes; Hashem judge between me and thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 38:5; MISHLE 14:29; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:7"> But Avram said unto Sarai, Hinei, thy shifchah is in thy hand; do to her as it pleaseth thee. And when Sarai dealt harshly with her, she fled from her face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:18; SHEMOT 15:22; BERESHIS 21:17"> And the Malach Hashem found her by a spring of mayim in the midbar, near the spring on the road to Shur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 3:9; BERESHIS 4:10; KOHELET 10:4"> And he said, Hagar, Saraiʼs shifchah, from where camest thou? And where wilt thou go? And she said, I flee from the face of my gevirah Sarai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:5–6; KOHELET 10:4; LETTER TO TITOS 2:9"> And the Malach Hashem said unto her, Return to thy gevirah, and submit thyself under her hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:20; TEHILLIM 83:6–7; BERESHIS 25:12–18"> And the Malach Hashem said unto her, I will multiply thy zera exceedingly, that it shall not be numbered for multitude.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:18; BERESHIS 21:20; IYOV 39:5–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he will be a pere adam;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">his yad will be against kol,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and kol yad against him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he shall dwell in the presence of all his brethren.</span></span>
