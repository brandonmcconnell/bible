<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:51; BAMIDBAR 12:6; BERESHIS 32:1–2"> And he had a chalom, and hinei a sullam (ladder, stairway) set up on the ground, and the top of it reached to Shomayim: and hinei the malachim of Elohim ascending and descending on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 13:15; BERESHIS 48:3; BERESHIS 35:12"> And, hinei, Hashem stood above it, and said, I am Hashem Elohei Avraham thy av, and Elohei Yitzchak; ha'aretz whereon thou liest, to thee will I give it, and to thy zera;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:25; BERESHIS 18:18"> And thy zera shall be as the dust of ha'aretz, and thou shalt spread abroad to the west, and to the east, and to the north, and to the south; and in thee and in thy zera shall kol hamishpochot haadamah be blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 1:5; YESHAYAH 41:10; YIRMEYAH 1:19"> And, hinei, I am with thee, and will be shomer over thee in all places where thou goest, and will bring thee back into haadamah hazot; for I will not leave thee, until I have done that which I have promised thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 5:15; SHEMOT 3:5; SHEMOT 15:11"> And Ya'akov awoke out of his sleep, and he said, Surely Hashem is in this place! And I had no da'as of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:15; BERESHIS 35:1–13; KOHELET 5:1"> And he was afraid, and said, How nora (awesome) is this place! This is none other than the Beis Elohim, and this is the Sha'ar HaShomayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BERESHIS 35:14; BERESHIS 31:45; BERESHIS 31:13"> And Ya'akov rose up early in the boker, and took haeven (the stone) that he had put for his pillow, and set it up for a matzevah (pillar), and poured shemen upon the top of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="HOSHEA 12:4–5; BERESHIS 35:1; BERESHIS 48:3"> And he called the shem of that place Beit-El; but the shem of that ir was called Luz at the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:8; SHMUEL BAIS 15:8; BERESHIS 31:13"> And Ya'akov vowed a neder (vow), saying, If Elohim will be with me, and will be shomer over me in this derech (way, journey) that I go, and will give me lechem to eat, and beged (clothes) to put on,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHOFETIM 11:31; DEVARIM 26:17; SHMUEL BAIS 19:24"> So that I return to bais avi in shalom; then shall Hashem be for me Elohim (my G-d).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BERESHIS 35:7; BERESHIS 14:20; DEVARIM 14:22–23"> And this even (stone), which I have set for a matzevah (pillar), shall be Beis Elohim: and of all that Thou shalt give me I will surely give the aser (tenth) unto Thee.</span>
