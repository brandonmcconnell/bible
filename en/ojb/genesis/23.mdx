## &#91;CHAYYE SARAH&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:17"> And Sarah was an hundred and seven and twenty shanah old: these were the shnei chayyai Sarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 23:19; BERESHIS 13:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:57"> And Sarah died in Kiryat-Arba; the same is Chevron in eretz Kena'an: and Avraham came to mourn for Sarah, and to weep for her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 10:15; BERESHIS 23:7; SHMUEL ALEF 26:6"> And Avraham stood up from before his dead, and spoke unto the bnei Chet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:15; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:9; BERESHIS 17:8"> I am a ger and a sojourner with you: give me an achuzzah (possession) of a kever with you, that I may bury my dead out of my sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the bnei Chet answered Avraham, saying unto him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:35; BERESHIS 14:14; SHEMOT 32:22"> Hear us, adoni: thou art a nasi Elohim among us; in the choice of kevareinu bury thy dead; none of us shall withhold from thee his kever, that thou mayest bury thy dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:14; BERESHIS 19:1; MISHLE 18:24"> And Avraham stood up, and bowed himself to the am ha'aretz, even to the bnei Chet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:9; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1–2; MELACHIM ALEF 2:17"> And he spoke to them, saying, If it be your nefesh (will) that I should bury my dead out of my sight; hear me, and intercede for me to Ephron ben Tzochar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:17"> That he may give me the cave of Machpelah, which he hath, which is in the end of his sadeh; for the kesef maleh (full price) he shall give it me for an achuzzah (possession) of a kever amongst you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 34:20; BERESHIS 34:24; BERESHIS 23:18"> And Ephron dwelt among the bnei Chet: and Ephron the Chitti answered Avraham in the hearing of the bnei Chet, even of all that went in at the sha'ar of his city, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 19:24; YESHAYAH 32:8; DEVARIM 19:15"> Lo, adoni, hear me; the sadeh give I thee, and the cave that is therein, I give it thee; in the presence of the bnei ammi give I it thee; bury thy dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 23:7; BERESHIS 18:2; BERESHIS 19:1"> And Avraham bowed down himself before the am ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:8; SHMUEL BAIS 24:24; BERESHIS 14:22–23"> And he spoke unto Ephron in the hearing of the am ha'aretz, saying, But if thou wilt give it, now, hear me; I will give thee kesef for the sadeh; accept it from me, and I will bury my dead there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Ephron answered Avraham, saying unto him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 45:12; SHEMOT 30:13; SHEMOT 30:15"> Pay heed, adoni, unto me; the land is worth four hundred shekels of kesef; what is that between me and thee? bury therefore thy dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 11:12; YIRMEYAH 32:9; SHEMOT 30:13"> And Avraham paid heed unto Ephron; and Avraham weighed to Ephron the kesef, which he had named in the hearing of the bnei Chet four hundred shekels of kesef, according to current merchant value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:9; BERESHIS 50:13; BERESHIS 23:20"> And the sadeh of Ephron which was in Machpelah, which was near Mamre, the sadeh, and the cave which was therein, and all the trees that were in the sadeh, that were in all the borders round about, were deeded</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="RUTH 4:1; YIRMEYAH 32:12; BERESHIS 34:20"> Unto Avraham for a property in the presence of the bnei Chet, before all that went in at the sha'ar of his city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BERESHIS 50:13; KOHELET 12:7; KOHELET 12:5"> And after this, Avraham buried Sarah his isha in the cave of the sadeh of Machpelah near Mamre; the same is Chevron in the eretz Kena'an.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:10–14; RUTH 4:7–10; BERESHIS 49:31–32"> And the sadeh, and the cave that is therein, were deeded unto Avraham for an achuzzah of a kever by the bnei Chet.</span>
</div>
