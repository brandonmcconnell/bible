<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 47:29; BERESHIS 39:4–6; BERESHIS 15:2"> And Avraham said unto his eved zekan of his bais, hamoshel (that ruled) over all that he had, Put, now, thy yad under my thigh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:14–17; BERESHIS 26:34–35; BERESHIS 28:8"> And I will make thee swear by Hashem Elohei HaShomayim and Elohei Ha'Aretz, that thou shalt not take an isha unto beni of the Banot HaKena'ani, among whom I dwell:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:2; BERESHIS 22:20–23; BERESHIS 12:7"> But thou shalt go unto my eretz, and to my moledet, and take an isha unto beni Yitzchak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 4:2; SHEMOT 9:2; SHEMOT 20:7"> And the eved said unto him, What if the isha will not be willing to follow me unto HaAretz Hazot: must I needs bring binecha back unto ha'aretz from where thou camest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:1; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20–22; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:39"> And Avraham said unto him, Beware thou that thou bring not beni to there!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 33:2; BERESHIS 13:15; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:14"> Hashem Elohei HaShomayim, which took me from bais avi, and from the eretz of my moledet, and which spoke unto me, and that swore unto me, saying, Unto thy zera will I give HaAretz Hazot; He shall send His Malach before thee, and thou shalt take an isha unto beni from there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 2:17–20; BAMIDBAR 30:5; BAMIDBAR 30:8"> But if the isha will not be willing to follow thee, then thou shalt be released from this my shevu'ah (oath): only do not bring back beni there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:2"> And the eved put his yad under yerech Avraham adonav, and swore to him concerning this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 11:31; DEVARIM 23:4; BERESHIS 27:43"> And the eved took asarah gemalim of the gemalei adonav, and departed; for all the goods of adonav were in his yad: and he arose, and went to Aram Naharayim (Mesopotamia), unto the Ir Nachor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 9:11; SHEMOT 2:16; YOCHANAN 4:7"> And he made his gemalim to kneel down outside the Ir near a be'er hamayim at the time of erev, at the time that women go out to draw mayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:27; SHEMOT 3:6; BERESHIS 26:24"> And he said Hashem Elohei adoni Avraham, now, send me success this yom, and show chesed unto adoni Avraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:43; TEHILLIM 37:5; SHEMOT 2:16"> Hinei, I stand here by the ayin hamayim; and the banot anshei haIr come out to draw mayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHOFETIM 6:37; SHOFETIM 6:17; BERESHIS 15:8"> And let it come to pass, that the na'arah to whom I shall say, Let down thy jug, now, that I may drink; and she shall say, Drink, and I will give thy gemalim drink also; let the same be she that Thou hast appointed for Thy eved Yitzchak; and thereby shall I know that Thou hast showed chesed unto adoni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 11:29; BERESHIS 24:45; YESHAYAH 65:24"> And it came to pass, before he had done speaking, that, hinei, Rivkah came out, who was born to Betuel Ben Milcah, eshet Nachor, achi Avraham, with her jug upon her shoulder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 26:7; BAMIDBAR 31:17–18; BERESHIS 39:6"> And the na'arah was tovat mareh me'od, a betulah, neither had any ish known her: and she went down to haayenah (the spring), and filled her jug, and came up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:7; YESHAYAH 21:14; BERESHIS 26:1–35"> And the eved hurried to meet her, and said, Let me, now, drink a little mayim of thy jug.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MISHLE 31:26; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:8; BERESHIS 24:14"> And she said, Drink, adoni: and she hasted, and let down her jug upon her yad, and gave him drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:14; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:9; BERESHIS 24:45–46"> And when she had done giving him drink, she said, I will draw for thy gemalim also, until they have done drinking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And she hasted, and emptied her jug into the trough, and ran back unto the be'er to draw, and drew for all his gemalim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="TEHILLIM 107:43; BERESHIS 24:12; SHMUEL BAIS 7:18–20"> And the ish gazed at her and held his peace, so as to have da'as whether Hashem had made his derech prosperous or not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 16:11–12; SHEMOT 32:2–3; BERESHIS 23:15–16"> And it came to pass, just as the gemalim had done drinking, that the ish took a nezem zahav (gold ring) by weight a beka, and two tzemidim (bracelets) by weight ten &#91;shekels of&#93; gold for her wrists;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And said, Whose bat art thou? Tell me, now: is there makom in the bais of thy av for us to spend the night?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:15; BERESHIS 22:23; BERESHIS 22:20"> And she said unto him, I am Bat Betuel Ben Milcah, the ben which she bore unto Nachor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YESHAYAH 32:8; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:9; SHOFETIM 19:19–21"> She said moreover unto him, We have both teven (straw) and mispo (fodder) enough, and makom (room) to spend the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:48; SHEMOT 4:31; BERESHIS 24:52"> And the ish bowed his head, worshiped Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:48; BERESHIS 24:12; TEHILLIM 98:3"> And he said, Baruch Hashem Elohei adoni Avraham, who hath not left destitute of His chesed and His emes; I being on the derech, Hashem led me to the bais achei adoni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:67; BERESHIS 24:55; BERESHIS 31:33"> And the na'arah ran, and told them of her bais em these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BERESHIS 29:5; BERESHIS 29:13; BERESHIS 24:55"> And Rivkah had an ach, shmo Lavan; Lavan ran out unto the ish, unto the ayin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And it came to pass, when he saw the nezem and tzemidim upon the wrists of his achot, and when he heard the words of Rivkah his achot, saying, Thus spoke the ish unto me; that he came unto the ish; and, hinei, he stood by the gemalim at the ayin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="RUTH 3:10; BERESHIS 26:29; TEHILLIM 115:15"> And he said, Come in, Gebentshter fun Hashem; why standest thou outside? for I have prepared the bais, and makom for the gemalim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHOFETIM 19:21; BERESHIS 43:24; BERESHIS 18:4"> And the ish came into the bais; and he unloaded his gemalim, and gave teven (straw) and mispo (fodder) for the camels, and mayim to wash his raglayim, and the raglei haanashim that were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="TEHILLIM 132:3–5; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:5–8"> And there was set ochel before him to eat; but he said, I will not eat, until I have stated my business. And he said, Speak on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And he said, I am eved Avraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:1; BERESHIS 13:2; MISHLE 10:22"> And Hashem hath blessed adoni me'od; and he is become prospered: and He hath given him tzon, and bakar, and kesef, and zahav, and avadim, and shefachot, and gemalim, and chamorim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:5; BERESHIS 21:10; BERESHIS 21:1–7"> And Sarah eshet adoni bore ben to adoni when she was old: and unto him hath he given all that he hath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:46; EZRA 9:1–3; BERESHIS 24:2–9"> And adoni made me swear, saying, Thou shalt not take an isha for beni of the Banot HaKena'ani, in whose land I dwell;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:4; BERESHIS 31:19; BERESHIS 12:1"> But thou shalt go unto bais avi, to my mishpokhot, and take an isha unto beni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:5"> And I said unto adoni, What if the isha will not follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:20; TEHILLIM 16:8; BERESHIS 5:24"> And he said unto me, Hashem, before Whom I walk, will send His Malach with thee, and prosper thy derech; and thou shalt take an isha for beni of my mishpokhot, and of my bais avi;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:8; DEVARIM 29:12"> Then shalt thou be released from this my oath, when thou comest to my mishpokhot; and if they give not thee one, thou shalt be released from my oath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 1:11; BERESHIS 24:12–14; EZRA 8:21"> And I came this day unto the ayin, and said, Hashem Elohei adoni Avraham, if now Thou do prosper my derech which I go;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:13–14"> Hinei, I stand by the ayin hamayim; and it shall come to pass, that when haAlmah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="haAlmah used here as synonym for na'arah-betulah, Gn 24:16; see Isa 7:14, Introduction">a</a></sup>cometh forth to draw mayim, and I say to her, Give me, now, a little mayim of thy jar to drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:14; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:8; BERESHIS 2:22"> And she say to me, Both drink thou, and I will also draw for thy gemalim; let the same be the isha whom Hashem hath appointed for ben adoni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:12–17; BERESHIS 24:15–20"> And before I had done davening in mine lev, hinei, Rivkah came forth with her jar on her shoulder; and she went down unto ha'ayenah, and drew: and I said unto her, Let me drink, now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:18–19"> And she made haste, and let down her jar from her shoulder, and said, Drink, and I will give thy gemalim drink also: so I drank, and she made the gemalim drink also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="TEHILLIM 45:13–14; BERESHIS 24:22–24; YESHAYAH 62:3–5"> And I asked her, and said, Whose bat art thou? And she said, Bat Betuel Ben Nachor, the ben whom Milcah bore unto him: and I put the nezem upon her face, and the tzemidim upon her wrists.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="MISHLE 3:5–6; BERESHIS 24:26–27; TEHILLIM 48:14"> And I bowed down my head, and worshiped Hashem, and I said a brocha to Hashem Elohei adoni Avraham, which had led me on the derech emes to take bat achi adoni for bno.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="BERESHIS 47:29; YEHOSHUA 2:14; MISHLE 3:3"> And now if ye will do chesed and emes to adoni, tell me; and if not, tell me; that I may turn to yamin, or to smol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="TEHILLIM 118:23; BERESHIS 31:24; BERESHIS 31:29"> Then Lavan and Betuel answered and said, The thing proceedeth from Hashem; we cannot speak unto thee rah or tov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="BERESHIS 20:15; BERESHIS 24:15; SHMUEL BAIS 16:10"> Hinei, Rivkah is before thee, take her, and go, and let her be isha for ben adoneicha, just as Hashem hath spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:26; BERESHIS 24:48; TEHILLIM 116:1–2"> And it came to pass, that, when eved Avraham heard their words, he worshiped Hashem, bowing himself to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:22; BERESHIS 24:10; SHEMOT 12:35"> And the eved brought forth kelei kesef, and kelei zahav, and begadim, and gave them to Rivkah; he gave also to her ach and to her em migdanot (costly gifts).</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:56; BERESHIS 24:59; BERESHIS 28:5–6"> And they did eat and drink, he and the anashim that were with him, and tarried all night; and they rose up in the boker, and he said, Send me away unto adoni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="BERESHIS 4:3; VAYIKRA 25:29; SHOFETIM 14:8"> And her ach and her em said, Let the na'arah abide with us ten days or so; after that she shall go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="MISHLE 25:25; YEHOSHUA 1:8; YESHAYAH 48:15"> And he said unto them, Hinder me not, seeing Hashem hath prospered my derech; send me away that I may go to adoni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> And they said, We will call the na'arah, and inquire at her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="LUKAS 1:38; TEHILLIM 45:10–11"> And they called Rivkah, and said unto her, Wilt thou go with this ish? And she said, I will go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="BERESHIS 35:8; BAMIDBAR 11:12; BERESHIS 24:50"> And they sent away Rivkah their achot, and her nurse, and eved Avraham, and his anashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="BERESHIS 22:17; BERESHIS 17:16; RUTH 4:11–12"> And they said a brocha over Rivkah, and said unto her, Thou art achoteinu, may thou be increased to thousands upon thousands and may thy zera possess the sha'ar of those which hate them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="TEHILLIM 45:10; BERESHIS 2:24; ESTHER 8:14"> And Rivkah arose, and her na'arot, and they rode upon the gemalim, and followed the ish: and the eved took Rivkah, and went his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:11; BERESHIS 16:14; BERESHIS 12:9"> And Yitzchak had come from Be'er Lachai Roi; for he dwelt in the eretz hanegev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:15; TEHILLIM 1:2; TEHILLIM 104:34"> And Yitzchak went out to meditate in the sadeh at erev: and he lifted up his eyes, and saw, and, hinei, the gemalim (camels) were coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="SHOFETIM 1:14; YEHOSHUA 15:18"> And Rivkah lifted up her eyes, and when she saw Yitzchak, she got down from the gamal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:5–6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:10"> For she had said unto the eved, Who is that ish that walketh in the sadeh to meet us? And the eved had said, He is adoni: therefore she took a veil, and covered herself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="MARKOS 6:30"> And the eved told Yitzchak all things that he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="SHIR HASHIRIM 8:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:22–33; BERESHIS 23:1–2"> And Yitzchak brought her into the ohel of Sarah immo, and took Rivkah, and she became his isha; and he loved her: and Yitzchak was comforted after the &#91;mot&#93; immo.</span>
