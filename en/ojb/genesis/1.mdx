## &#91;PARASHAS BERESHIS&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:1–3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:3; YESHAYAH 45:18"> In the beginning Elohim bara hashomayim (the heavens) and haaretz (the earth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 4:23; TEHILLIM 104:30; YESHAYAH 45:18"> And the earth was tohu vavohu (without form, and void); and darkness was upon the face of the deep. And the Ruach Elohim was hovering upon the face of the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:6; YOCHANAN 1:5; YESHAYAH 45:7"> And Elohim said, Let there be light: and there was light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="KOHELET 2:13; BERESHIS 1:18; KOHELET 11:7"> And Elohim saw the light, that it was tov (good); and Elohim divided the ohr (light) from the choshech (darkness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 74:16; YESHAYAH 45:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:5"> And Elohim called the light Yom (Day), and the darkness He called Lailah (Night). And the erev (evening) and the boker (morning) were Yom Echad(Day One, the First Day).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 51:15; TEHILLIM 33:6; IYOV 37:18"> And Elohim said, Let there be a raki'a (expanse, dome, firmament) in the midst of the mayim (waters), and let it divide the mayim from the mayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MISHLE 8:28–29; TEHILLIM 148:4; MATTITYAHU 8:27"> And Elohim made the raki'a, and divided the waters under the raki'a from the waters which were above the raki'a; and it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 1:5; BERESHIS 1:19; BERESHIS 1:10"> And Elohim called the raki'a Shomayim (Heaven). And the erev and the boker were Yom Sheni (Day Two, the Second Day).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:5; YONAH 1:9; YIRMEYAH 5:22"> And Elohim said, Let the waters under the heaven be gathered together unto one place, and let the yabashah (dry land) appear; and it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:4; TEHILLIM 104:31; BERESHIS 1:4"> And Elohim called the yabashah Eretz (Earth); and the mikveh (gathering together of the waters) called He Seas; and Elohim saw that it was tov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TEHILLIM 104:14–17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:7; TEHILLIM 147:8"> And Elohim said, Let the earth bring forth vegetation, the herb yielding zera (seed), and the fruit tree yielding pri (fruit) after its kind, whose seed is in itself, upon the earth; and it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MARKOS 4:28; YESHAYAH 55:10–11; MATTITYAHU 13:24–26"> And the earth brought forth vegetation, and herb yielding zera (seed) after its kind, and the tree yielding fruit, whose seed was in itself, after its kind; and Elohim saw that it was tov (good).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the erev and the boker were Yom Shlishi (Day Three, the Third Day).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ex 19:11,15-16; Josh 1:11; Num 19:11-16; Jon 2:1; Hos 6:2; 2Kgs 20:5,8; Ezra 6:15; 1C 15:4,20; Notice on Yom HaShlishi (The Third Day, Gn 1:13) is Yom HaBikkurim of the Bria HaOlam, the Firstfruits of the Creation of the World; just as Hos 6:2indicates Yom HaShlishi is the time marker of the Techiyas HaMesim (Resurrection of the Dead), so Yom HaBikkurim fell on Nisan 16, and became a time marker delineating the countdown from the Resurrection of Moshiach Nisan 16, 3793 (33 C.E.) to Shavuos, 3793 and the Matan HaTevilah BeRuach Hakodesh. Lv 23:11.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 104:19–20; DEVARIM 4:19; YIRMEYAH 31:35"> And Elohim said, Let there be lights in the raki'a of the heaven to divide the day from the night; and let them be for otot (signs), and for mo'adim (seasons), and for yamim (days), and shanim (years);</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And let them be for lights in the raki'a of the heaven to give light upon the earth; and it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="TEHILLIM 136:7–9; DEVARIM 4:19; TEHILLIM 8:3"> And Elohim made two great lights; the greater light to rule the day, and the lesser light to rule the night; He made the kokhavim (stars) also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TEHILLIM 8:3; TEHILLIM 8:1; IYOV 38:12"> And Elohim set them in the raki'a of the heaven to give light upon the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:35; TEHILLIM 19:6"> And to rule over the day and over the night, and to divide the light from the darkness; and Elohim saw that it was tov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And the erev and the boker were Yom Revi'i (Day Four, the Fourth Day).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 2:19; TEHILLIM 104:24–25; MELACHIM ALEF 4:33"> And Elohim said, Let the waters bring forth an abundance of living creatures, and fowl that may fly above the earth in the open raki'a of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="TEHILLIM 104:24–26; BERESHIS 1:25; BERESHIS 8:19"> And Elohim created great sea creatures, and every living creature that moveth, which the waters brought forth in abundance, after their kind, and every winged fowl after its kind; and Elohim saw that it was tov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="IYOV 40:15; BERESHIS 8:17; TEHILLIM 107:38"> And Elohim blessed them, saying, Be fruitful, and multiply, and fill the waters in the seas, and let fowl multiply in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the erev and the boker were Yom Chamishi (Day Five, the Fifth Day).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="TEHILLIM 50:9–10; BERESHIS 6:20; BERESHIS 8:19"> And G-d said, Let the earth bring forth the living creature after its kind, cattle, and creeping thing, and beast of the earth after its kind; and it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 27:5; IYOV 12:8–10; BERESHIS 2:19–20"> And G-d made the beast of the earth after its kind, and cattle after their kind, and every thing that creepeth upon the earth after its kind; and G-d saw that it was tov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:24; TEHILLIM 8:4–8"> And G-d said, Let Us make man in Our tzelem, after Our demut: and let them have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over the cattle, and over all the earth, and over every creeping thing that creepeth upon ha'aretz (the earth).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:10; BERESHIS 5:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">So G-d created humankind in His own tzelem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the tzelem Elohim (image of G-d) created He him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">zachar (male) and nekevah (female) created He them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="BERESHIS 9:1; BERESHIS 9:7; VAYIKRA 26:9"> And G-d blessed them, and G-d said unto them, Be fruitful, and multiply, and fill the earth, and subdue it: and have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over every living thing that moveth upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BERESHIS 9:3; TEHILLIM 104:14–15; TEHILLIM 145:15–16"> And G-d said, Hinei, I have given you every herb bearing seed, which is upon the face of kol ha'aretz (all the earth), and every etz (tree), in the which is the fruit of a tree yielding seed; to you it shall be for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="TEHILLIM 104:14; TEHILLIM 145:15–16; TEHILLIM 147:9"> And to every beast of the earth, and to every fowl of the air, and to every thing that creepeth upon the earth, wherein there is life, I have given every green herb for food; and it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:4; TEHILLIM 104:24; TEHILLIM 19:1–2"> And G-d saw every thing that He had made, and, behold, it was tov me'od (very good). And the erev and the boker were Yom Shishi (Day Six, the Sixth Day).</span>
