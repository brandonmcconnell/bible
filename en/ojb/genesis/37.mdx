## &#91;VAYESHEV&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:8; BERESHIS 23:4; BERESHIS 36:7"> And Ya'akov dwelt in the eretz megurei Aviv (in the land wherein his father was a ger) in Eretz Kena'an.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 35:25–26; BERESHIS 35:22; SHMUEL ALEF 2:22–24"> These are the toldot Ya'akov. Yosef, being seventeen years old, was ro'eh with his achim to the tzon; now the na'ar was with the bnei Bilhah, and with the bnei Zilpah, the ne'shei Aviv (the wives of his father); and Yosef brought unto Avihem (their father) dibbatam ra'ah (a bad, evil report of them).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:35; BERESHIS 37:32; SHMUEL BAIS 13:18"> Now Yisroel loved Yosef more than all his children, because he was the ben zekunim (the son of his old age); and he made him a kesones passim (ketonet &#91;tunic&#93; reaching to palms and soles).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Messianic significance Yn 19:23">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:41; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:12; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:20"> And when his achim saw that Avihem loved him more than all his achim, they hated him, and could not speak with shalom unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:12; BERESHIS 42:9; YOCHANAN 17:14"> And Yosef dreamed a chalom (dream),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Mt 2:22">b</a></sup>and he told it to his achim; and they hated him yet the more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 44:18; SHOFETIM 9:7"> And he said unto them, Hear, now, this chalom which I have dreamed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 44:14; BERESHIS 42:6; BERESHIS 43:26"> For, hinei, we were binding alummim (sheaves of wheat) out in the sadeh, and, hinei, my sheaf arose, and also stood upright; and, hinei, your alummot (sheaves of wheat) gathered around it, and bowed down to my sheaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 10:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:35; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:29"> And his achim said to him, Shalt thou indeed reign over us? Or shalt thou indeed have dominion over us? And they hated him yet the more for his chalomot (dreams), and for his devarim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:9–14; BERESHIS 37:7; DANIEL 8:10"> And he dreamed yet another chalom, and told it his achim, and said, Hinei, I have dreamed a chalom more; and, hinei, the shemesh and the yarei'ach and the eleven kokhavim bowed down to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:10–11; YESHAYAH 60:14"> And he told it to Aviv, and to his achim: and Aviv rebuked him, and said unto him, What is this chalom that thou hast dreamed? Shall I and Immecha and Achecha indeed come to bow down ourselves to the ground before thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:9; LUKAS 2:19; LUKAS 2:51"> And his achim had kina (jealousy, envy) toward him; but Aviv was shomer over the saying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:1; BERESHIS 33:18; BERESHIS 34:25–31"> And his achim went for the purpose to be ro'eh tzon Avihem in the vicinity of Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:1–3; SHMUEL ALEF 3:8; SHMUEL ALEF 3:16"> And Yisroel said unto Yosef, Do not thy achim ro'im in Shechem? Come and I will send thee unto them. And he said to him, Hineni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 35:27; LUKAS 19:42; TEHILLIM 125:5"> And he said to him, Go, now, see about the shalom achecha, and the shalom hatzon; and bring me devar. So he sent him out of the Emek Chevron, and he went to Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:27; SHOFETIM 4:22; BERESHIS 21:14"> And a certain ish found him, and, hinei, he was wandering in the sadeh; and the ish asked him, saying, What seekest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 19:10; SHIR HASHIRIM 1:7"> And he said, I seek my achim; tell me, now, where they ro'im (feed their flocks).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 6:13"> And the ish said, They are departed from here; for I heard them say, Let us go to Dotan. And Yosef went after his achim, and found them in Dotan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:12; TEHILLIM 37:12; TEHILLIM 37:32"> And when they saw him merachok (afar off, in the distance), even before he came near unto them, they conspired against him to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:12; BERESHIS 37:11; BERESHIS 37:5"> And they said one to another, Hinei, this ba'al hachalomot cometh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:3; MISHLE 27:4; MATTITYAHU 2:2–16"> Come now therefore, and let us kill him, and throw him into one of the borot (pits), and we will say, Some chayyah ra'ah (evil wild beast) hath devoured him; and we shall see what will become of his chalomot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:22; BERESHIS 35:22; YEHOSHUA 10:28"> And Reuven heard it, and he saved him out of their yadayim; and said, Let us not take his nefesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BERESHIS 22:12; DEVARIM 13:9; BERESHIS 42:22"> And Reuven said unto them, Do no shefach dahm (shedding of blood), but throw him into this bor that is in the midbar, and lay no yad upon him; that he might rescue him out of their yadayim, to take him back to Aviv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:3; MATTITYAHU 27:28; BERESHIS 42:21"> And it came to pass, when Yosef was come unto his achim, that they stripped Yosef of his kesones,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Yn 19:23">c</a></sup>his kesones hapassim that was on him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 38:6; TEHILLIM 130:1–2; ZECHARYAH 9:11"> And they took him, and threw him into the borah (pit); and the bor was empty, there was no mayim in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:28; BERESHIS 43:11; YIRMEYAH 8:22"> And they sat down to eat lechem; and they lifted up their eyes and looked, and, hinei, a caravan of Yishm'elim was coming from Gil'ad with their gemalim bearing spices and balm and myrrh, going to carry it down to Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:20; BERESHIS 4:10; BERESHIS 25:32"> And Yehudah said unto his achim, What betza (profit, gain) is it if we kill achinu (our brother), and conceal his dahm?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:26; BERESHIS 42:21; SHMUEL ALEF 18:17"> Come, and let us sell him to the Yishm'elim, and let not yadeinu (our hands) be upon him; for he is achinu (our brother) and besareinu (our flesh). And his achim agreed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:9; TEHILLIM 105:17; BERESHIS 45:4–5"> Then there passed by anashim Midyanim socharim (men of Midyan, traders); and they drew and lifted up Yosef out of the bor, and sold Yosef to the Yishm'elim for esrim kesef; and they took Yosef to Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="IYOV 1:20; BERESHIS 37:34; BERESHIS 44:13"> And Reuven returned unto the bor; and, hinei, Yosef was not in the bor; and he made the keriah of his clothes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="compare Mk 14:63">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:13; YIRMEYAH 31:15; BERESHIS 42:32"> And he returned unto his achim, and said, The yeled is not; and I, where shall I turn?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:23; MISHLE 28:13; BERESHIS 37:3"> And they took the kesones Yosef, and did shachat the se'ir izzim (slaughtered the kid of the goats), and dipped the kesones in the dahm;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LUKAS 15:30; BERESHIS 37:3; BERESHIS 44:20–23"> And they sent the kesones hapassim, and they brought it to Avihem; and said, This have we found; examine for identification purposes now whether it be the kesones Binecha or no.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:20; BERESHIS 44:28; YOCHANAN 13:7"> And he recognized it, and said, It is the kesones beni; a chayyah ra'ah hath devoured him; Yosef is without doubt torn in pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:29; SHMUEL BAIS 3:31; TEHILLIM 69:11"> And Ya'akov made the keriah of his clothes, and put sackcloth on his loins, and mourned for beno yamim rabbim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:38; IYOV 2:11; SHMUEL BAIS 12:17"> And all his banim and all his banot rose up to comfort him; but he refused to be comforted; and he said, For I will go down to beni mourning to Sheol. Thus Aviv wept for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="BERESHIS 40:4; BERESHIS 37:28; MELACHIM BAIS 25:8"> And the Midyanim sold him into Mitzrayim unto Potiphar, a seris Pharaoh (courtier of Pharaoh), a sar hatabbachim (a captain of the slaughterers, executioners, i.e., captain of the guard).</span>
</div>
