<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:37–39; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5–9; BERESHIS 6:9"> And Hashem said unto Noach, Come thou and all thy bais into the tevah (ark); for thee have I found tzaddik before Me in dor hazeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 8:20; VAYIKRA 10:10; VAYIKRA 11:1–47"> Of every behemah hatehorah thou shalt take to thee by sevens; the male and his mate; and of behemah that are not tehorah by two, the male and his mate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Of also Oph HaShomayim by sevens, the zachar and the nekevah; to keep zera alive upon the face of kol ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 6:13; BERESHIS 7:12; BERESHIS 7:17"> For shivah yamim from now, and I will cause it to rain upon ha'aretz arba'im yom and arba'im lailah; and every living creature that I have made will I wipe out from off the p'nei ha'adamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BERESHIS 6:22; LUKAS 8:21; SHEMOT 39:32"> And Noach did according unto all that Hashem commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 5:32; BERESHIS 8:13"> And Noach was shesh me'ot shanah when the mabbul waters were upon ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKAS 17:27; BERESHIS 6:18; MATTITYAHU 24:38"> And Noach entered in, and his banim, and his isha, and nashim of his banim with him, into the tevah (ark), because of the waters of the mabbul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Of behemah hatehorah, and of behemah that are not tehorah, and of haoph, and of every thing that creepeth upon ha'adamah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 7:16; BERESHIS 2:19; YIRMEYAH 8:7"> There went in shnayim shnayim unto Noach into the tevah (ark), the zachar and the nekevah, as Elohim had commanded Noach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 6:17; BERESHIS 7:4; LUKAS 17:27"> And it came to pass after shivat hayamim, that the waters of the mabbul were upon ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 8:2; MALACHI 3:10; MISHLE 8:28–29"> In the six hundredth year of Noachʼs life, in the second month, the seventeenth day of the month, the same day were all the fountains of the great deep burst open, and the floodgates of Shomayim were opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 7:4; MATTITYAHU 4:2; BERESHIS 7:17"> And the geshem was upon ha'aretz arba'im yom and arba'im lailah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 6:18; BERESHIS 7:1; BERESHIS 6:10"> On that very yom entered Noach, and Shem, and Cham, and Yephet, the bnei Noach, and Noachʼs isha, and the three nashim of his banim with them, into the tevah (ark);</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 7:8–9; BERESHIS 7:2–3"> They, and every beast after its kind, and kol habehemah after their kind, and every remes that crawls upon ha'aretz after its kind, and kol haoph after its kind, every bird of every sort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 6:19–20; YESHAYAH 11:6"> And they went in unto Noach into the tevah (ark), shnayim shnayim of kol habasar, wherein is the ruach chayyim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:10; LUKAS 13:25; TEHILLIM 91:1–10"> And they that went in, went in zachar and nekevah of kol basar, as Elohim had commanded him; then Hashem shut him in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 7:4; BERESHIS 7:12"> And the mabbul was arba'im yom upon ha'aretz; and the waters increased, and lifted up the tevah (ark), and it rose above ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 14:28; TEHILLIM 69:15; IYOV 22:16"> And the waters rose, and were increased greatly upon ha'aretz; and the tevah (ark) floated upon the face of the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="TEHILLIM 104:6–9; TEHILLIM 46:2–3; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:6"> And the waters rose exceedingly upon ha'aretz; and all the high hills, that were under kol HaShomayim, were covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 3:23; TEHILLIM 104:6"> Fifteen cubits upward did the waters rise; and the harim were covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 6:13; BERESHIS 7:4; BERESHIS 6:17"> And kol basar perished that moved upon ha'aretz, both of Oph, and of Behemah, and of Beast, and of every Swarming Thing that swarms upon ha'aretz, and kol haadam;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BERESHIS 2:7; BERESHIS 6:17"> All in whose nostrils was the ruach chayyim, of all that was in the dry land, perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:20; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:7"> And every living creature was wiped out which was upon the p'nei haadamah, both Adam, and Behemah, and the Remes, and the Oph HaShomayim; and they were wiped out from ha'aretz: and Noach only remained alive, and they that were with him in the tevah (ark).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BERESHIS 8:3–4"> And the waters flooded upon ha'aretz a hundred and fifty days.</span>
