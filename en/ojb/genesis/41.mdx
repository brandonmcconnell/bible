## &#91;MIKETZ&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 20:3; BERESHIS 29:14; SHEMOT 4:9"> And it came to pass at the end of two full years, that Pharaoh dreamed a chalom: and, hinei, he stood by haye'or (the River, i.e., the Nile).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YESHAYAH 19:6"> And, hinei, there came up out of haye'or (the River, i.e., the Nile) seven cows, fine in appearance and fat in basar; and they grazed on the riverbank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:20–21; BERESHIS 41:4"> And, hinei, seven other cows came up after them out of the Nile, ra'ot mareh (ugly in appearance) and dakot basar (gaunt ones in flesh, lean-fleshed); and stood by the other cows upon the bank of the Nile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 3:15"> And the cows that were ra'ot hamareh and that were dakot habasar did devour the seven cows that were fine in appearance and fat. Then Pharaoh awoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:14"> And he slept and dreamed a chalom the second time; and, hinei, sheva heads of grain came up upon one stalk, beri'ot (fat ones) and tovot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 17:10; HOSHEA 13:15; YECHEZKEL 19:12"> And, hinei, seven thin heads of grain scorched by the east wind tzomechot (sprung up) after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:5; BERESHIS 20:3"> And the seven thin heads of grain devoured the seven fat and mele'ot (full) heads of grain. And Pharaoh awoke; and, hinei, it was a chalom!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DANIEL 4:7; SHEMOT 7:22; MATTITYAHU 2:1"> And it came to pass in the boker that his ruach was troubled; and he sent and called for all the chartummei Mitzrayim (magicians of Egypt), and all the chachamim thereof; and Pharaoh told them his chalom; but there was no poter (interpreter) for Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 40:14; BERESHIS 40:23; BERESHIS 40:1–3"> Then the sar hamashkim spoke unto Pharaoh, saying, I do remember my faults this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 39:20; BERESHIS 40:2–3; BERESHIS 37:36"> Pharaoh was in wrath with his avadim, and put me b'mishmar (in custody) in the bais sar hatabbachim, both me and the sar ha'ofim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 40:5–8"> And we dreamed a chalom in the same lailah, I and he; and each chalom we dreamed had its own pitron (interpretation).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 40:12–19; BERESHIS 37:36; BERESHIS 39:1"> And there was there with us a na'ar Ivri, eved to the sar hatabbachim; and we told him, and he interpreted to us chalomoteinu (our dreams); to each according to his chalom he did interpret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 43:3; BERESHIS 40:20–22; BERESHIS 40:12"> And it was, just as he interpreted to us, so it came to pass; me he restored unto mine ken (post), and him he hanged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DANIEL 2:25; TEHILLIM 113:7–8; TEHILLIM 105:19–22"> Then Pharaoh sent and summoned Yosef, and they brought him hastily out of the bor (dungeon); and he shaved, and changed his clothes, and came in unto Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DANIEL 5:16; DANIEL 5:12; TEHILLIM 25:14"> And Pharaoh said unto Yosef, I have dreamed a chalom, and there is no poter (interpreter) for it; and I have heard it said of thee, that thou canst understand a chalom to interpret it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:5; BERESHIS 40:8; DANIEL 2:47"> And Yosef answered Pharaoh, saying, It is not in me; Elohim shall give Pharaoh an answer of shalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Pharaoh said unto Yosef, In my chalom, hineni, I stood upon the bank of the Nile;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 24:8; YIRMEYAH 24:1–3; YIRMEYAH 24:5"> And, hinei, there came up out of the Nile seven cows beri'ot basar (fat in body) and sleek of form; and they grazed by the riverbank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And, hinei, seven other cows came up after them, dalot (poor, scrawny, thin) and ra'ot to'ar me'od (very ugly in appearance) and lean-fleshed, such as I never saw in kol Eretz Mitzrayim, unacceptably bad;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the lean and the ra'ot cows did eat up the first seven fat cows;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 3:3; TEHILLIM 37:19; HISGALUS 10:9–10"> And when they had eaten them up, it could not be known that they had eaten them; but they were still rah in appearance, as at the beginning. So I awoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And I saw in my chalom, and, hinei, seven heads of grain came up on one stalk, mele'ot (full) and tovot (good);</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="TEHILLIM 129:6–7; HOSHEA 13:15; MELACHIM BAIS 19:26"> And, hinei, seven heads of grain, withered, thin, and scorched with the east wind, tzomechot (sprung up) after them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DANIEL 4:7; BERESHIS 41:8; SHEMOT 8:19"> And the thin heads of grain devoured the seven tov heads of grain; and I told this unto the chartummim (magicians); but there was none that could explain it to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DANIEL 2:45; HISGALUS 4:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:17"> And Yosef said unto Pharaoh, The chalom of Pharaoh is echad: HaElohim hath revealed to Pharaoh what He is about to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:47; BERESHIS 41:2; BERESHIS 41:53"> The seven good cows are seven shanim; and the seven good heads of grain are seven shanim. The chalom is echad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 8:1; SHMUEL BAIS 24:19"> And the seven lean and ugly cows that came up after them are sheva shanim; and the seven empty heads of grain scorched by the east wind are sheva shnei ra'av (seven years of famine).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:25; BERESHIS 41:16"> This is the thing which I have spoken unto Pharaoh; What HaElohim is about to do He showeth unto Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:26; BERESHIS 41:46–47; BERESHIS 41:49"> Hinei, there come sheva shanim of sava gadol (great fullness, plenty, abundance) throughout kol Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="BERESHIS 47:13; BERESHIS 41:54; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:17"> And there shall arise after them sheva shnei ra'av (seven years of famine) and kol hasava (all abundance) shall be forgotten in Eretz Mitzrayim; and the ra'av (famine) shall consume the land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YESHAYAH 24:20; SHMUEL ALEF 5:6"> And the sava shall not be known in the land by reason of that ra'av (famine) following; for it shall be kaved me'od (very grievous).</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 23:19; YESHAYAH 46:10–11; YESHAYAH 14:24–27"> And for that the chalom was doubled unto Pharaoh; it is because the thing is &#91;decidedly&#93; established by HaElohim, and HaElohim will shortly bring it to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:39; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:3; DEVARIM 1:13"> Now therefore let Pharaoh select an ish navon and chochom (a man discerning and wise) and set him over Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MISHLE 22:3; LUKAS 16:5; MISHLE 6:6–8"> Let Pharaoh do this, and let him appoint pekidim (officers, overseers, commissioners) over the land, and take up the fifth part &#91;of the harvest&#93; of Eretz Mitzrayim in the sheva shnei hasava.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="BERESHIS 45:6–7; BERESHIS 41:56; SHEMOT 4:13"> And let them gather kol ochel of those shanim hatovot that come, and store up grain under the yad Pharaoh, and let them be shomer over ochel in the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="BERESHIS 47:13–25"> And that ochel shall be for reserves for the land against the sheva shnei hara'av, which shall be in Eretz Mitzrayim; that the land perish not through the ra'av.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:10; MELACHIM ALEF 21:2; SHMUEL BAIS 3:36"> And the thing was good in the eyes of Pharaoh, and in the eyes of all his avadim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="IYOV 32:8; DANIEL 5:14; BAMIDBAR 27:18"> And Pharaoh said unto his avadim, Can we find such an ish as this is, an ish in whom is the Ruach Elohim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:33; BERESHIS 41:28; BERESHIS 41:25"> And Pharaoh said unto Yosef, Forasmuch as Elohim hath showed thee all this, there is none so navon (discerning) and chochom (wise) as thou art;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MISHLE 22:29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:10; TEHILLIM 105:21–22"> Thou shalt be over my bais (palace), and according unto thy mouth shall all my people order themselves; only with respect to the kisse will I be greater than thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:9–11; BERESHIS 42:6"> And Pharaoh said unto Yosef, See, I have set thee over kol Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="DANIEL 5:29; DANIEL 5:7; ESTHER 3:10"> And Pharaoh took off his taba'at (signet ring) from his yad, and put it upon yad Yosef, and arrayed him in garments of fine linen, and put a chain of zahav around his neck;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:10; BERESHIS 45:8; BERESHIS 45:26"> And he made him to ride in the second merkavah which he had; and they cried before him, Avrech! And he put him over kol Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="TEHILLIM 105:22; SHEMOT 11:7"> And Pharaoh said unto Yosef, I am Pharaoh, and without thee shall no man lift up his yad or regel in kol Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:20; YECHEZKEL 30:17; SHEMOT 2:16"> And Pharaoh called shem Yosef Tzaphnat Pa'neach; and he gave him as isha Asenat Bat Poti Phera kohen of On. And Yosef went out over all Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="DANIEL 1:19; MELACHIM ALEF 12:6; SHMUEL ALEF 16:21"> And Yosef was thirty years old when he stood before Pharaoh Melech Mitzrayim. And Yosef went out from the presence of Pharaoh, and went throughout kol Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="BERESHIS 26:12; TEHILLIM 72:16"> And in the sheva shnei hasava the land brought forth by handfuls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:34–36; BERESHIS 47:21"> And he gathered up kol ochel of the sheva shanim, which were in Eretz Mitzrayim, and laid up the ochel in the cities; the ochel of the sadeh, which surrounded every city, laid he up in the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:27; SHOFETIM 6:5; BERESHIS 22:17"> And Yosef gathered grain as the chol (sand) of the yam, very much, until he stopped counting; for it was without mispar (number).</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:20; BERESHIS 48:5; SHMUEL BAIS 8:18"> And unto Yosef were born two banim before the shnat ra'av came, which Asenat Bat Poti Phera kohen of On bore unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="TEHILLIM 30:5; MISHLE 31:7; DEVARIM 33:17"> And Yosef called the shem of the bechor Menasheh: For Elohim, said he nashani, (hath made me forget) all my amal, and kol bais Avi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="BERESHIS 49:22; BERESHIS 50:23; BERESHIS 17:6"> And the shem of the second called he Ephrayim: For Elohim hifrani (hath caused me to be fruitful) in the eretz of my oni (suffering).</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:29–31; TEHILLIM 73:20; LUKAS 16:25"> And the sheva shnei hasava that was in Eretz Mitzrayim were ended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="BERESHIS 45:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:11; TEHILLIM 105:16"> And the sheva shnei hara'av began to come, just as Yosef had said; and the ra'av was in all lands; but in kol Eretz Mitzrayim there was lechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 17:5; MATTITYAHU 3:17; YIRMEYAH 14:1–6"> And when kol Eretz Mitzrayim was famished, the people cried to Pharaoh for lechem; and Pharaoh said unto kol Mitzrayim, Go unto Yosef; what he saith to you, do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:6; ZECHARYAH 5:3; LUKAS 21:35"> And the ra'av (famine) was over kol pnei ha'aretz; and Yosef opened all that was in them, and sold unto the Mitzrayim; and the ra'av (famine) became severe in Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:54; BERESHIS 42:5; BERESHIS 41:56"> And kol HaAretz (all the world) came into Mitzrayim to Yosef to buy grain; because that the ra'av (famine) was so chazak in kol HaAretz.</span>
