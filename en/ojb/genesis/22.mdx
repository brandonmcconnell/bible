<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:17; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:21; DEVARIM 8:2"> And it came to pass after these things, that G-d did test Avraham, and said unto him, Avraham: and he said, Hineini (Behold, here I am).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:32; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:17"> And He said, Take now thy son, thine ben yachid (only son) Yitzchak, whom thou lovest, and get thee into eretz Moriyah; and offer him there for a burnt offering upon one of the mountains which I will tell thee of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:60; MATTITYAHU 10:37; LUKAS 14:26"> And Avraham rose up early in the morning, and saddled his donkey, and took two of his servants with him, and Yitzchak his son, and cut the wood for the burnt offering, and rose up, and went unto the place of which G-d had told him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKAS 13:32; MATTITYAHU 17:23; HOSHEA 6:2"> Then on Yom HaShlishi Avraham lifted up his eyes, and saw the place afar off.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1C 15:3">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:1"> And Avraham said unto his servants, Abide ye here with the donkey; and I and the young man will go over there and nishtachaveh (we will worship) and we will come back again to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:17; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:24; YESHAYAH 53:6"> And Avraham took atzei haolah (the wood of the burnt offering), and laid it upon Yitzchak his son; and he took the eish (fire) in his hand, and a knife; and they went both of them together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:15; MATTITYAHU 26:39; BERESHIS 8:20"> And Yitzchak spoke unto Avraham his father, and said, Avi (My father): and he said, Hineini, beni (Here am I, my son). And he said, Hinei, the eish (fire) and the wood: but where is the seh (lamb) for a burnt offering?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Isa 53:7">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:36; YOCHANAN 1:29; HISGALUS 5:12"> And Avraham said, My son, G-d will provide Himself a seh (lamb) for a burnt offering: so they went both of them together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:17–19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:7–8"> And they came to the place which G-d had told him of; and Avraham built a mizbe'ach there, and laid the wood in order, and made the akedah (binding) of Yitzchak his son, and laid him on the mizbe'ach upon the wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:21–23; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:17–19; YESHAYAH 53:6–12"> And Avraham stretched forth his hand, and took the knife to slay his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 21:17; SHEMOT 3:4; BERESHIS 16:9–10"> And the Malach Hashem called unto him out of Shomayim, and said, Avraham, Avraham: and he said, Hineini.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:21–22; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:18; SHMUEL ALEF 15:22"> And he said, Lay not thine hand upon the young man, neither do thou any thing unto him: for now I know that thou fearest G-d, seeing thou hast not withheld thy son, thine ben yachid from Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:13; TEHILLIM 40:6–8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:9–10"> And Avraham lifted up his eyes, and looked, and hinei behind him a ram caught in a thicket by his horns: and Avraham went and took the ram, and offered him up for a burnt offering TAKHAT (instead of) his son.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 53:8">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 22:13; SHEMOT 17:15; BERESHIS 22:8"> And Avraham called the name of that place Hashem Yireh: as it is said to this day, In the mount of Hashem it shall be provided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the Malach Hashem called unto Avraham out of Shomayim the second time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:13–14; LUKAS 1:73; TEHILLIM 105:9"> And said, By Myself have I sworn, saith Hashem, for because thou hast done this thing, and hast not withheld thy son, thine ben yachid:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:5; BERESHIS 24:60; YIRMEYAH 33:22"> That in blessing I will bless thee, and in multiplying I will multiply thy zera as the stars of the skies, and as the sand which is upon the sea shore; and thy zera shall possess the gate of his enemies;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:25; BERESHIS 12:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:16"> And in thy zera shall kol goyei ha'aretz be blessed; because thou hast obeyed My voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BERESHIS 21:31; SHOFETIM 20:1; YEHOSHUA 15:28"> So Avraham returned unto his servants, and they rose up and went together to Be'er-Sheva; and Avraham dwelt at Be'er-Sheva.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 11:29; BERESHIS 24:24; MISHLE 25:25"> And it came to pass after these things, that it was told Avraham, saying, Hinei, Milcah, she hath also born children unto thy brother Nachor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="IYOV 1:1; IYOV 32:2; TEHILLIM 60:1–12"> Utz his bechor (firstborn), and Buz his brother, and Kemuel the father of Aram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Kesed, and Chazo, and Pildash, and Yidlaph, and Betuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:15; BERESHIS 28:5; BERESHIS 24:51"> And Betuel fathered Rivkah: these eight Milcah did bear to Nachor, Avrahamʼs brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BERESHIS 16:3; BERESHIS 25:6; MISHLE 15:25"> And his pilegesh, whose name was Reumah, she bare also Tevach, and Gacham, and Tachash, and Maachah.</span>
