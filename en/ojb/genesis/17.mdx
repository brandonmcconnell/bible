<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:20; LUKAS 1:6"> And when Avram was ninety and nine shanah, Hashem appeared to Avram, and said unto him, I am El Shaddai; walk before Me, and be thou tamim (blameless).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:4–6; BERESHIS 12:2; TEHILLIM 105:8–11"> And I will confirm My brit (covenant) between Me and thee, and will multiply thee exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHOFETIM 13:20; BERESHIS 17:17; BAMIDBAR 16:45"> And Avram fell on his face; and Elohim talked with him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 35:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:28–29; BERESHIS 12:2"> As for Me, hinei, My brit (covenant) is with thee, and thou shalt be an Av of many Goyim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See18:18">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 1:21–23; NECHEMYAH 9:7; BERESHIS 17:15"> Neither shall thy shem any more be called Avram, but thy shem shall be Avraham; for Av hamon Goyim (Father of a multitude of Goyim) have I made thee.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ga 3:29says, “And if you belong to Moshiach (Isa 53:10), then you are of the ZERAH of Avraham Avinu, you are yoreshim (heirs) according to the havtachah (promise).”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:4; MATTITYAHU 1:6–17; BERESHIS 35:11"> And I will make thee exceeding fruitful, and I will make Goyim of thee, and Melechim shall come out of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:7–9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:39; BERESHIS 26:24"> And I will establish My Brit (covenant) between Me and thee and thy zera after thee in their dorot for a Brit Olam, to be Elohim unto thee, and to thy zera after thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:12; TEHILLIM 105:11; SHEMOT 6:7"> And I will give unto thee, and to thy zera after thee, the Eretz wherein thou art now a ger, kol Eretz Kena'an, for an Achuzzah (Possession) Olam (Everlasting); and I will be their Elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YESHAYAH 56:4–5; TEHILLIM 25:10; TEHILLIM 103:18"> And Elohim said unto Avraham, Thou shalt be shomer over My Brit therefore, thou, and thy zera after thee in the dorot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:11–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:9–11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:8"> This is My Brit (covenant), which ye shall be shomer over, between Me and you and thy zera after thee; every zachar among you shall be circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:8; DEVARIM 10:16"> And ye shall circumcise the basar of your arelah; and it shall be an ot brit (sign of the covenant) between Me and you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="VAYIKRA 12:3; LUKAS 2:21; SHEMOT 12:48–49"> And he that is eight days old shall be circumcised among you, every zachar in your dorot, he that is born in the bais, or bought with kesef of any foreigner, which is not of thy zera.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:44; BERESHIS 37:36; NECHEMYAH 5:8"> He that is born in thy bais, and he that is bought with thy kesef, must be circumcised; and My Brit shall be in your basar for a Brit Olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:24–26; BAMIDBAR 15:30–31; SHEMOT 12:19"> And the arel (uncircumcised) zachar whose basar of his arelah (foreskin) is not circumcised, that nefesh shall be cut off from his people; My Brit hefer (he broke).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 12:25; BERESHIS 32:28; BERESHIS 17:5"> And Elohim said unto Avraham, As for Sarai thy isha, thou shalt not call her shem Sarai, but Sarah (Princess) shall her shem be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 35:11; BERESHIS 17:6; YESHAYAH 49:23"> And I will bless her, and give thee ben also of her; yes, will bless her, and she shall give rise to Goyim; melechim of peoples shall be from her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HISGALUS 11:16; BERESHIS 21:6; BERESHIS 18:12"> Then Avraham fell upon his face, and yitzchak (laughed), and said in his lev, Shall a child be born unto him that is a hundred shanah? And shall Sarah, that is ninety shanah, bear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:39; TEHILLIM 41:12; TEHILLIM 4:6"> And Avraham said unto HaElohim, O that Yishmael might live before Thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKAS 1:13–20; BERESHIS 21:2–3; MELACHIM BAIS 4:16–17"> And Elohim said, Sarah thy isha shall bear thee ben indeed; and thou shalt call shmo Yitzchak; and I will establish My Brit (covenant) with him for a Brit Olam, and with his zera after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 21:18; BERESHIS 21:13; BERESHIS 25:12–18"> And as for Yishmael, I have heard thee; hinei, I have blessed him, and will make him fruitful, and will multiply him exceedingly; twelve nasi'im (princes, rulers) shall he father, and I will make him a goy gadol (great nation).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 21:2–3; BERESHIS 26:2–5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:29"> But My Brit (covenant) will I establish with Yitzchak, which Sarah shall bear unto thee at mo'ed hazeh (this set time) next year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:33; BERESHIS 35:9–15; SHOFETIM 6:21"> And He left off speaking with him, and Elohim went up from Avraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:60; BERESHIS 18:19; BERESHIS 17:10–14"> And Avraham took Yishmael bno, and all that were born in his bais, and all that were bought with his kesef, every zachar among the men of Avrahamʼs bais; and circumcised the basar of their arelah in the very same day, as Elohim had said unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:11; BERESHIS 17:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:19–20"> And Avraham was ninety shanah and nine, when he was circumcised in the basar of his arelah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Yishmael bno was thirteen shanah, when he was circumcised in the basar of his arelah (foreskin).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:60; BERESHIS 22:3–4; BERESHIS 12:4"> In the very same day was Avraham circumcised, and Yishmael bno.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:19"> And all the men of his bais, born in the bais, and bought with kesef of the foreigner, were circumcised with him.</span>
