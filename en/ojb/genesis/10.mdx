<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 27:14; YIRMEYAH 51:27"> And the Bnei Gomer: Ashkenaz, and Riphat, and Togarmah.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TZEFANYAH 2:11; YESHAYAH 42:4; YIRMEYAH 2:10"> By these were the coastlands of the Goyim divided in their lands; every one after his leshon, after their mishpechot, in their Goyim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 1:8–16; YECHEZKEL 27:10; TEHILLIM 105:23"> And the Bnei Cham: Cush, and Mitzrayim, and Put, and Kena'an.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 27:22; YECHEZKEL 27:15; YESHAYAH 21:13"> And the Bnei Cush: Seva, and Chavilah, and Savtah, and Ra'amah, and Savtecha; and the Bnei Raamah: Sheva, and Dedan.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And Resen between Nineveh and Kelach; the same is haIr Hagedolah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 1:11–12; YIRMEYAH 46:9; YECHEZKEL 30:5"> And Mitzrayim fathered Ludim, and Anamim, and Lehavim, and Naphtuchim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 47:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 1:12; DEVARIM 2:23"> And Patrusim, and Casluchim, out of whom came Pelishtim (Philistines), and Caphtorim.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 34:2"> And the Chivi, and the Arki, and the Sini,</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 10:6; BERESHIS 11:1–9"> These are the Bnei Cham, after their mishpechot, after their leshonot, in their territories, and in their Goyim.</span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 1:19; BERESHIS 10:32; DEVARIM 32:8"> And unto Ever were born two banim: the shem of one was Peleg; for in his days was ha'aretz divided; and his brotherʼs shem was Yoktan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 1:20–28"> And Yoktan fathered Almodad, and Sheleph, and Chatzarmavet, and Yerach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 1:20–28"> And Hadoram, and Uzal, and Diklah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 1:20–28; MELACHIM ALEF 10:1; BERESHIS 25:3"> And Oval, and Avimael, and Sheva,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:28; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 9:13; BERESHIS 2:11"> And Ophir, and Chavilah, and Yovav; all these were the Bnei Yoktan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 23:7"> And their moshav (dwelling) was from Mesha, as thou goest unto Sephar har hakedem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="BERESHIS 10:5; BERESHIS 10:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:26"> These are the Bnei Shem, after their mishpechot, according to their leshonot, in their territories, after their Goyim.</span>
