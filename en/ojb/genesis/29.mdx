<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHOFETIM 6:3; SHOFETIM 6:33; HOSHEA 12:12"> Then Ya'akov went on his journey, and came into the eretz of the bnei kedem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:14; YESHAYAH 49:10; YOCHANAN 4:6"> And he looked, and hinei a be'er in the sadeh, and, hinei, there were shloshah edrei tzon lying by it; for out of that be'er they watered the edarim: and a great even (stone) was upon the mouth of the be'er.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And to there were all the edarim gathered: and they rolled the even (stone) from the mouth of the be'er, and watered the tzon, and put the even (stone) again upon the mouth of the be'er in its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:10; BERESHIS 27:43; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:2"> And Ya'akov said unto them, My brethren, where are you from? And they said, Of Charan are we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:24; BERESHIS 24:29; BERESHIS 31:53"> And he said unto them, Know ye Lavan ben Nachor? And they said, We know him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 20:9; BERESHIS 37:14; SHEMOT 18:7"> And he said unto them, Is the shalom with him? And they said, Shalom; and, hinei, Rachel his bat cometh with the tzon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:9–10"> And he said, Look, the sun is still high, neither is it time that the flock should be gathered together; water ye the tzon, and go and feed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MARKOS 16:3; LUKAS 24:2; BERESHIS 34:14"> And they said, We cannot, until all the edarim be gathered together, and till they roll the even (stone) from the mouth of the be'er; then we water the tzon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 2:21; SHIR HASHIRIM 1:7–8; SHEMOT 2:15–16"> And while he yet spoke with them, Rachel came with the tzon of her av; for she was a ro'ah (shepherdess).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 2:17"> And it came to pass, when Ya'akov saw Rachel bat Lavan achi immo, and the tzon of Lavan achi immo, that Ya'akov went near, and rolled the even (stone) from the mouth of the be'er, and watered the tzon of Lavan achi immo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 33:4; BERESHIS 45:14–15; BERESHIS 45:2"> And Ya'akov kissed Rachel, and lifted up his voice, and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:28; BERESHIS 13:8; BERESHIS 14:14–16"> And Ya'akov told Rachel that he was the relative of her av, and that he was ben Rivkah; and she ran and told her av.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:37; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:16"> And it came to pass, when Lavan heard the news of Ya'akov ben achoto, that he ran to meet him, and embraced him, and kissed him, and brought him to his bais. And he told Lavan all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 19:12–13; BERESHIS 2:23; SHOFETIM 9:2"> And Lavan said to him, Surely thou art my etzem (bone) and my basar. And he abode with him the space of a chodesh (month).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 30:28; BERESHIS 31:7"> And Lavan said unto Ya'akov, Because thou art my relative, shouldest thou therefore serve me for nothing? Tell me, what shall thy maskoret (wages) be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="RUTH 4:11; BERESHIS 49:31; BERESHIS 31:4"> And Lavan had two banot: the shem of the elder was Leah, and the shem of the younger was Rachel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:11; BERESHIS 46:19–22; BERESHIS 24:16"> Leah had weak eyes; but Rachel was yafeh and lovely in form.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HOSHEA 12:12; SHEMOT 22:16–17; BERESHIS 34:12"> And Ya'akov loved Rachel; and said, I will serve thee sheva shanim for Rachel thy bat haketannah (younger daughter).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 6:11; YESHAYAH 6:5; TEHILLIM 12:2"> And Lavan said, It is better that I give her to thee, than that I should give her to another ish; abide with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:2; HOSHEA 12:12"> And Ya'akov served sheva shanim for Rachel; and they seemed unto him but a few yamim, because of the ahavah he had for her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHOFETIM 15:1; BERESHIS 29:20; BERESHIS 4:1"> And Ya'akov said unto Lavan, Give me my isha, for my yamim are completed, that I may go in unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:9; RUTH 4:10–13; SHOFETIM 14:10–18"> And Lavan gathered together all the anshei hamakom, and made a mishteh (feast).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MICHOH 7:5; BERESHIS 38:14–15; BERESHIS 24:65"> And it came to pass in the erev, that he took Leah his bat, and brought her to him; and he went in unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BERESHIS 16:1; BERESHIS 30:9–12; BERESHIS 46:18"> And Lavan gave unto his daughter Leah Zilpah shifchato (his maid) for a shifchah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:2; MISHLE 11:31; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:13"> And it came to pass, that in the boker, hinei, it was Leah! And he said to Lavan, What is this thou hast done unto me? Did not I serve with thee for Rachel? So why then hast thou deceived me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Lavan said, It must not be so done in our land, to give the younger before the bechirah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:18; SHOFETIM 14:12; BERESHIS 2:2–3"> Finish her shvu'a (week), and we will give thee this also for the avodah (service, work) which thou shalt do for me yet sheva shanim acherot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Ya'akov did so, and fulfilled her shvu'a; and he gave him Rachel his bat to be his isha also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BERESHIS 35:22; BERESHIS 29:24; BERESHIS 30:3–8"> And Lavan gave to his bat Rachel Bilhah his shifchah to be her shifchah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="BERESHIS 31:41; BERESHIS 29:20; BERESHIS 31:15"> And he went in also unto Rachel, and he loved also Rachel more than Leah, and served him yet sheva shanim acherot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 2:21; MATTITYAHU 10:37; DEVARIM 21:15"> And when Hashem saw that Leah was hated, He opened her womb but Rachel was barren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEVARIM 26:7; SHEMOT 4:31; SHEMOT 3:7"> And Leah conceived, and bore a ben, and she called shmo Reuven: for she said, Surely Hashem hath looked upon my misery; now therefore my ish will love me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="BERESHIS 34:25; BERESHIS 49:5–6; BERESHIS 34:30"> And she conceived again, and bore ben; and said, Because Hashem hath heard I was hated, He hath therefore given me this also; and she called shmo Shim'on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="SHEMOT 32:26–29; BERESHIS 49:5–7; BERESHIS 46:11"> And she conceived again, and bore ben; and said, Now this time will my ish yillaveh (become attached) unto me, because I have born him shloshah banim: therefore was shmo called Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 1:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:2; BERESHIS 49:8–12"> And she conceived again, and bore ben; and she said, Now will odeh (I praise)Hashem; therefore she called shmo Yehudah; and stopped giving birth.</span>
