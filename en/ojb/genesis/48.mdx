<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:8; BERESHIS 35:11; BERESHIS 26:4"> And said unto me, Hineni, I will make thee fruitful, and multiply thee, and I will make of thee a kehal of ammim; and will give HaAretz Hazot to thy zera after thee for an achuzzat olam (everlasting possession).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:50–52; YEHOSHUA 14:4; BERESHIS 46:20"> And now thy two banim, Ephrayim and Menasheh, which were born unto thee in Eretz Mitzrayim before I came unto thee into Mitzrayim, are mine; like Reuven and Shim'on, they shall be mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 14:4"> And thy moledet, which are born to thee after them, shall be thine, and shall be called after the shem of their achim in their nachalah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 35:16–19; MATTITYAHU 2:18; BERESHIS 35:9"> And as for me, when I came from Paddan, Rachel died by me in Eretz Kena'an in the way, when there was yet but a space of land to go unto Ephratah; and I buried her there by the derech Ephrat; the same is Beit-Lechem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Yisroel beheld Bnei Yosef, and said, Who are these?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:4; BERESHIS 33:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:21"> And Yosef said unto aviv, They are my banim, whom Elohim hath given me in this place. And he said, Bring them, now, unto me, and I will make upon them a brocha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:1; BERESHIS 27:27; SHMUEL ALEF 3:2"> Now the eyes of Yisroel were dim from age, so that he could not see. And he brought them near unto him; and he kissed them, and embraced them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:20; BERESHIS 45:26; BERESHIS 42:36"> And Yisroel said unto Yosef, I never expected to see thy face; and, hinei, Elohim hath showed me also thy zera.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:6; VAYIKRA 19:32; BERESHIS 19:1"> And Yosef brought them out from between his knees, and he bowed himself with his face to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Yosef took them both, Ephrayim in his yamin toward smol Yisroel, and Menasheh on his smol toward yamin Yisroel, and brought them near unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:51; TEHILLIM 118:16; MATTITYAHU 19:15"> And Yisroel stretched out his yamin, and laid it upon rosh Ephrayim, who was the younger, and his smol upon rosh Menasheh, guiding his hands intentionally; for Menasheh was the bechor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:6; YESHAYAH 33:16"> And he made on Yosef a brocha, and said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">HaElohim, before whom my Avot Avraham and Yitzchak did walk,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">HaElohim Who was Roeh to me all my life long unto this day,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="TEHILLIM 34:22; BERESHIS 49:22; BERESHIS 48:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Malach which redeemed me from kol rah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">may he make a brocha on the ne'arim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and let my shem be named on them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the shem of my Avot Avraham and Yitzchak;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and let them grow into a multitude in the midst of ha'aretz.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 48:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:7–8; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:7"> And when Yosef saw that aviv laid his yamin upon the rosh Ephrayim, it displeased him; and he took hold of yad aviv, to remove it from rosh Ephrayim unto rosh Menasheh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BERESHIS 19:18; BERESHIS 29:26; BERESHIS 43:33"> And Yosef said unto aviv, Not so, Avi; for this is the bechor; put thy yamin upon his rosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 33:17; BAMIDBAR 1:33–35; BERESHIS 25:28"> And aviv refused, and said, I know it, beni (my son), I know it; he also shall become a people, and he also shall be great; but truly achiv hakaton shall be greater than he, and his zera shall become a multitude of Goyim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:60; RUTH 4:11–12; BERESHIS 28:3"> And he made a brocha on them that day, saying, In thee shall Yisroel bless, saying, Elohim make thee like Ephrayim and like Menasheh; and he set Ephrayim before Menasheh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 50:24; BERESHIS 46:4; BERESHIS 28:15"> And Yisroel said unto Yosef, Hinei I die; but Elohim shall be with you, and bring you back unto the Eretz Avoteichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:5; YEHOSHUA 24:32; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:2"> Moreover, I have given to thee one portion above thy achim, which I took out of the yad HaEmori with my cherev and with my keshet.</span>
</div>
