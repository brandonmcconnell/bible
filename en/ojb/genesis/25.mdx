<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 50:13; BERESHIS 35:29; BERESHIS 49:29–30"> And his banim Yitzchak and Yishmael buried him in the cave of Machpelah, in the sadeh of Ephron ben Tzochar the Chitti, which is near Mamre;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 23:16; BERESHIS 49:31"> The sadeh which Avraham purchased of the bnei Chet, there was Avraham buried, and Sarah his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 22:17; BERESHIS 16:14; BERESHIS 24:62"> And it came to pass after the mot Avraham, that Elohim blessed Yitzchak bno; and Yitzchak dwelt near Be'er Lachai Roi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="TEHILLIM 83:6; BERESHIS 17:20; BERESHIS 21:13"> Now these are the toldot Yishmael ben Avraham, whom Hagar the Egyptian, shifchat Sarah, bore unto Avraham:</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:20; BERESHIS 17:23"> These are the bnei Yishmael, and these are their shemot, by their settlement, and by their camps; twelve nasiim (princes, rulers) according to their tribes.</span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 1:2; LUKAS 3:34; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:8"> And these are the toldot of Yitzchak ben Avraham: Avraham fathered Yitzchak;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:29; BERESHIS 24:67; BERESHIS 22:23"> And Yitzchak was arba'im shanah when he took Rivkah as his wife, the bat Betuel HaArami of Paddan-Aram, the achot Lavan HaArami.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKAS 1:13; YESHAYAH 58:9; EZRA 8:23"> And Yitzchak davened to Hashem on behalf of his isha, because she was barren; and Hashem was entreated of him, and Rivkah his isha conceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 9:9; SHMUEL ALEF 28:6; SHMUEL ALEF 30:8"> And the banim struggled jostling within her; and she said, If it be well, why am I thus? And she went to inquire of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:10–13; BERESHIS 27:40; BERESHIS 27:29"> And Hashem said unto her,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Two goyim (nations) are in thy womb,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and two peoples shall be separated from within thee;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the one people shall be stronger than the other people;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the older shall serve the younger.</span></span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And when her days were fulfilled to be delivered, hinei, there were twins in her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:11; BERESHIS 27:16; BERESHIS 27:23"> And the first came out admoni (red) all over like a hairy garment; and they called shmo Esav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:36; HOSHEA 12:3; BERESHIS 38:28–30"> And after that came out his brother, and his yad was grasping on akev Esav; and shmo was called Ya'akov; and Yitzchak was threescore shanah when she bore them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:9; BERESHIS 21:20; BERESHIS 10:9"> And the nearim grew: and Esav was a skilled hunter, an ish sadeh; and Ya'akov was an ish tam (quiet man), dwelling in ohalim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:19; BERESHIS 27:31; BERESHIS 27:4"> And Yitzchak loved Esav, because he did eat of his wild game; but Rivkah loved Ya'akov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MISHLE 13:25; YESHAYAH 40:30–31; SHMUEL ALEF 14:28"> And Ya'akov cooked stew: and Esav came from the sadeh, and he was famished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="BERESHIS 36:1; SHEMOT 15:15; BERESHIS 36:9"> And Esav said to Ya'akov, Let me eat now some of the adom (red stew); for I am famished; therefore was shmo called Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Ya'akov said, First sell me today thy bechorah (birthright, right of the firstborn).</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MALACHI 3:14; IYOV 21:15; IYOV 22:17"> And Esav said, Hinei, I am at the point of death; and what profit shall this bechorah do to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:16; BERESHIS 27:36; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:16"> And Ya'akov said, Swear to me this day; and he swore unto him; and he sold his bechorah unto Ya'akov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:18–19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:16–17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:32"> Then Ya'akov gave Esav lechem and adashim (lentils) stew; and he did eat and drink, and rose up, and went his way; thus Esav despised his bechorah.</span>
