<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 26:1; BERESHIS 26:6; BERESHIS 18:1"> And Avraham journeyed from there toward the Negev region, and dwelled between Kadesh and Shur, and sojourned in Gerar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:15; BERESHIS 26:7; BERESHIS 12:11–13"> And Avraham said of Sarah his isha, She is my achot; and Avimelech Melech Gerar sent, and took Sarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 20:7; MATTITYAHU 27:19; TEHILLIM 105:14"> But Elohim came to Avimelech in a chalom halailah, and said to him, See, thou art met (dead), for the isha which thou hast taken; for she is be'ulat ba'al (married of a husband).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:23–25; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:17; SHMUEL BAIS 4:11"> But Avimelech had not come near her; and he said, Adonoi, wilt Thou slay also a goy tzaddik?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 7:8; MELACHIM ALEF 9:4; DANIEL 6:22"> Said he not unto me, She is my achot? and she, even she herself said, He is achi: in the tohm (integrity, guilelessness) of my lev and with clean hands of my innocency have I done this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:34; MISHLE 21:1; SHMUEL ALEF 25:26"> And HaElohim said unto him in a chalom, Yea, I have da'as that thou didst this in the tohm (integrity, guilelessness) of thy lev; for I also withheld thee from sinning against Me; therefore allowed I thee not to touch her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="IYOV 42:8; SHMUEL ALEF 7:5; SHMUEL BAIS 24:17"> Now therefore restore the ish his isha; for he is a navi, and he shall pray for thee, and thou shalt live; and if thou restore her not, know thou that thou shalt surely die, thou, and all that are thine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Therefore Avimelech rose early in the boker, and called all his avadim, and told all these things in their ears; and the anashim were very afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:18; BERESHIS 26:10; BERESHIS 39:9"> Then Avimelech called Avraham, and said unto him, What hast thou done unto us? and how have I offended thee, that thou hast brought on me and on my mamlechah (kingdom) a chata'ah gedolah (great sin)? thou hast done deeds unto me that ought not to be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Avimelech said unto Avraham, What sawest thou, that thou hast done this thing?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 26:7; BERESHIS 12:12; IYOV 28:28"> And Avraham said, Because I thought, Surely the yirat Elohim is not in this place; and they will slay me on account of my isha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 11:29; BERESHIS 12:13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:22"> And yet indeed she is my achot; she is the bat avi, but not the bat immi; and she became my isha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:1; BERESHIS 12:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:3–5"> And it came to pass, when Elohim caused me to wander from my bais avi, that I said unto her, This is thy chesed which thou shalt show unto me; at every place where we shall come, say of me, He is achi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:16; BERESHIS 20:2; BERESHIS 20:7"> And Avimelech took tzon, and oxen, and avadim, and shfachot, and gave them unto Avraham, and restored to him Sarah his isha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 34:10; BERESHIS 13:9; BERESHIS 47:6"> And Avimelech said, Hinei, my land is before thee; dwell where it pleaseth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MISHLE 27:5; BERESHIS 20:5; BERESHIS 24:65"> And unto Sarah he said, Hinei, I have given thy brother a thousand pieces of kesef; hinei, it is to thee a kesut einayim (covering of the eyes, exoneration), unto all that are with thee, and with all other; thus she was vindicated.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:16; MATTITYAHU 21:22; IYOV 42:9–10"> So Avraham davened unto Elohim: and Elohim healed Avimelech, and his isha, and his maidservants so that they bore children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:17; BERESHIS 30:2; BERESHIS 16:2"> For Hashem had fast closed up kol rechem (every womb) of the bais Avimelech, on account of Sarah Avrahamʼs isha.</span>
</div>
</div>
