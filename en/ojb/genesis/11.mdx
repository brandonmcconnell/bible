<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:6; TZEFANYAH 3:9; YESHAYAH 19:18"> And the kol HaAretz was of one language and of devarim achadim (common words).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 10:10; DANIEL 1:2; BERESHIS 14:1"> And it came to pass, as they migrated from the east, that they found a plain in Eretz Shinar; and they dwelt there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 14:10; SHEMOT 1:14; SHEMOT 2:3"> And they said one to another, Come, let us make brick, and bake them thoroughly. And they had brick for even (stone), and bitumen had they for mortar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:28; YOCHANAN 5:44; LUKAS 1:51"> And they said, Come, let us build us an Ir and a Migdal, whose rosh (top) may reach unto Shomayim; and let us make us a shem, otherwise we shall be scattered abroad upon the face of kol HaAretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:21; TEHILLIM 33:13–14; YOCHANAN 3:13"> And Hashem came down to see the Ir and the Migdal, which Bnei HaAdam built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 2:1–4; BERESHIS 3:22; BERESHIS 11:1"> And Hashem said, See, the Am is echad and they have all one language; and this they begin to do; and now nothing will be impossible for them, which they have proposed to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 33:10; TEHILLIM 55:9; IYOV 5:12–13"> Come, let Us go down, and there confuse their sefat, that they may not understand one anotherʼs language.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKAS 1:51; DEVARIM 32:8; BERESHIS 10:25"> So Hashem scattered them abroad from there upon the face of kol Ha'Aretz; and they left off building the Ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 10:10; BERESHIS 10:5; BERESHIS 10:20"> Therefore is the shem of it called Bavel; because Hashem did there confuse the language of kol Ha'Aretz; and from there did Hashem scatter them abroad upon the face of kol Ha'Aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 1:17–27; BERESHIS 10:21–22; LUKAS 3:34–36"> These are the toldot of Shem: Shem was a hundred years old, and fathered Arpachshad two years after the mabbul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 9:7; TEHILLIM 144:12; BERESHIS 1:28"> And Shem lived after he fathered Arpachshad five hundred shanah, and fathered banim and banot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKAS 3:36"> And Arpachshad lived five and thirty shanah, and fathered Shelach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Arpachshad lived after he fathered Shelach four hundred and three shanim, and fathered banim and banot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Shelach lived thirty shanah, and fathered Ever;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Shelach lived after he fathered Ever four hundred and three shanah, and fathered banim and banot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 10:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 1:19; BAMIDBAR 24:24"> And Ever lived four and thirty shanah, and fathered Peleg;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Ever lived after he fathered Peleg four hundred and thirty shanah, and fathered banim and banot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKAS 3:35"> And Peleg lived thirty shanah, and fathered Reu;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Peleg lived after he fathered Reu two hundred and nine shanim, and fathered banim and banot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKAS 3:35"> And Reu lived two and thirty shanah, and fathered Serug;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Reu lived after he fathered Serug two hundred and seven shanim, and fathered banim and banot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 24:2"> And Serug lived thirty shanah, and fathered Nachor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Serug lived after he fathered Nachor two hundred shanah, and fathered banim and banot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKAS 3:34; YEHOSHUA 24:2"> And Nachor lived nine and twenty shanah, and fathered Terach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Nachor lived after he fathered Terach a hundred and nineteen shanah, and fathered banim and banot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 24:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 1:26–27; BERESHIS 22:20–24"> And Terach lived seventy shanah, and fathered Avram, Nachor, and Haran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:4; BERESHIS 14:12; BERESHIS 11:31"> Now these are the toldot Terach: Terach fathered Avram, Nachor, and Haran; and Haran fathered Lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:7; NECHEMYAH 9:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:2–4"> And Haran died before his av Terach in the land of his birth, in Ur Kasdim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:15; BERESHIS 22:20; BERESHIS 24:15"> And Avram and Nachor took them nashim: the shem eshet Avram was Sarai; and the shem eshet Nachor was Milcah, the bat Haran Avi Milcah, and Avi Yiskah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="BERESHIS 29:31; BERESHIS 21:1–2; LUKAS 1:7"> But Sarai was barren; she had no child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 9:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:2–4; BERESHIS 24:10"> And Terach took Avram bno, and ben bno Lot ben Haran, and Sarai his shnur, eshet Avram bno; and they went forth with them from Ur Kasdim, to go into Eretz Kena'an; and they came unto Charan (Haran), and dwelt there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the days of Terach were two hundred and five shanim; and Terach died in Charan (Haran).</span>
</div>
