<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:4; DEVARIM 6:7–8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:2"> And Yosef fell upon the face of Aviv, and wept over him, and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 50:26; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 16:14; YOCHANAN 19:39–40"> And Yosef commanded his avadim the rofe'im (physicians) to embalm Aviv; and the rofe'im embalmed Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 20:29; DEVARIM 34:8; BERESHIS 50:10"> And arba'im yom were fulfilled for him; for so are fulfilled the days of those which are embalmed; and the Mitzrayim mourned for him shivim yom (seventy days).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:3; ESTHER 4:2"> And when the days of his mourning were past, Yosef spoke unto the Bais Pharaoh, saying, If now I have found chen in your eyes, speak, now, in the oznayim of Pharaoh, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 16:14; YESHAYAH 22:16; MATTITYAHU 27:60"> Avi made me swear, saying, Hinei, I die; in my kever which I dug for myself in Eretz Kena'an, there shalt thou bury me. Therefore let me go up, now, and bury Avi, and I will return.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 48:21"> And Pharaoh said, Go up, and bury Avicha, according as he made thee swear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 14:16"> And Yosef went up to bury Aviv; and with him went up all the avadim of Pharaoh, the zekenim of his Bais, and all the zekenim of Eretz Mitzrayim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:24–27; SHEMOT 10:26; SHEMOT 10:8–9"> And kol Bais Yosef, and his achim, and Bais Aviv; only their little ones, and their tzon, and their bakar (cattle), they left in Eretz Goshen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 14:28; MELACHIM BAIS 18:24; SHIR HASHIRIM 1:9"> And there went up with him both merkavot and parashim; and it was a very great machaneh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="IYOV 2:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:2; SHMUEL BAIS 1:17"> And they came to the goren (threshing floor) of Atad, which is beyond the Yarden, and there they mourned with a gadol and very bitter lamentation; and he made evel (mourning) for Aviv shivat yamim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 10:15–19; BERESHIS 24:6; DEVARIM 11:30"> And when the inhabitants of HaAretz, the Kena'ani, saw the evel (mourning) in the goren Atad, they said, This is a grievous evel to the Mitzrayim; therefore the shem of it was called Avel-Mitzrayim. It is beyond the Yarden.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:16; BERESHIS 49:29–32; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:1"> And his &#91;Ya'akovʼs&#93; banim did unto him according as he commanded them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 49:29–31; BERESHIS 23:16–18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:16"> For his banim carried him into Eretz Kena'an, and buried him in the me'arah (cave) of the sadeh of Machpelah, near Mamre, the sadeh which Avraham had bought for an achuzzat kever (burial estate) and made the purchase from Ephron the Chitti.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And, after burying Aviv, Yosef returned into Mitzrayim, he, and his achim, and all that went up with him to bury Aviv.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MISHLE 28:1; BERESHIS 42:21–22; TEHILLIM 53:5"> And when the Achei Yosef (the brothers of Yosef) saw that Avihem was dead, they said, What if Yosef will hate us, and will certainly requite us all the ra'ah which we did unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MISHLE 29:25"> And they sent word unto Yosef, saying, Avicha did command before he died, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:32; MATTITYAHU 6:14–15; BERESHIS 45:8"> So shall ye say unto Yosef, Forgive, now, the peysha of thy achim, and their chattat; for they did unto thee ra'ah; and now, forgive the peysha of the Avdei Elohei Avicha. And Yosef wept when they spoke unto him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:7–11; BERESHIS 42:6; BERESHIS 45:3"> And his achim also went and fell down before him; and they said, Behold us, we are thy avadim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:19; BERESHIS 45:5; BERESHIS 30:2"> And Yosef said unto them, Fear not; for am I in the place of Elohim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:28; TEHILLIM 119:71; BERESHIS 45:5–8"> But as for you, ye intended ra'ah against me; but Elohim intended it for tovah, to bring to pass, as it is this day, to save Am Rav (much people) alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 47:12; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; BERESHIS 45:10–11"> Now therefore fear ye not; I will provide for you, and your little ones. And he comforted them, and spoke kindly unto their hearts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Yosef dwelt in Mitzrayim, he, and Bais Aviv; and Yosef lived one hundred and ten shanim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:39; BERESHIS 30:3; IYOV 42:16"> And Yosef saw Ephrayimʼs bnei shileshim (children to the third generation); also the Bnei Machir Ben Menasheh were brought up upon the birkei Yosef (knees of Yosef).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:16–17; BERESHIS 35:12; BERESHIS 26:3"> And Yosef said unto his achim, I die; and Elohim will surely visit you, and bring you out of this land unto HaAretz which He promised by oath to Avraham, to Yitzchak, and to Ya'akov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHEMOT 13:19; YEHOSHUA 24:32; BERESHIS 47:29–31"> And Yosef made the Bnei Yisroel take an oath, saying, Elohim will surely visit you, and ye shall carry up my atzmot from hence.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="BERESHIS 50:2–3; BERESHIS 47:9; YEHOSHUA 24:29"> So Yosef died, being in age one hundred and ten shanim; and they embalmed him, and he was placed in an aron in Mitzrayim.</span>
</div>
