<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:41; BERESHIS 43:30–31"> Then Yosef could not control himself before all them that stood by him; and he cried, Cause kol ish to go out from me. And there stood no ish with him, while Yosef made himself known unto his achim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:37; BAMIDBAR 14:1"> And he wept aloud; and the Mitzrayim and the Bais Pharaoh heard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:5; ZECHARYAH 12:10"> And Yosef said unto his achim (brethren), I am Yosef; doth Avi yet live? And his achim could not answer him; for they were shocked with dismay at his presence.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:28; MATTITYAHU 14:27; BERESHIS 50:18"> And Yosef said unto his achim, Come near to me, now. And they came near. And he said, I am Yosef achichem (your brother), whom ye sold into Mitzrayim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Ac 9:4">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BERESHIS 50:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:9–15; BERESHIS 45:7–8"> Now therefore be not grieved, nor angry with yourselves, that ye sold me here; for Elohim did send me before you to save life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 47:23; BERESHIS 41:54; BERESHIS 41:56"> For these two years hath the ra'av (famine) been in the land; and yet there are chamesh shanim (five years), in the which there shall neither be plowing nor harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 44:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:35; TEHILLIM 18:50"> And Elohim sent me before you to save you a she'erit (remnant) in the earth, and to save life for you by a peletah gedolah (great deliverance, escape).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:16; YOCHANAN 19:11"> So now it was not you that sent me here, but HaElohim; and He hath made me as an Av to Pharaoh, and adon of all his Bais, and Moshel throughout kol Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 45:19–20; BERESHIS 45:13"> Haste ye, and go up to Avi, and say unto him, Thus saith thy ben, Yosef, Elohim hath made me as adon kol Mitzrayim; come down unto me, tarry not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 9:26; SHEMOT 8:22; BERESHIS 46:34–6"> And thou shalt dwell in Eretz Goshen, and thou shalt be karov (near) unto me, thou, and thy banim, and thy bnei banecha, and thy tzon, and thy bakar, and all that thou hast:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Yn 14:3">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 47:12; BERESHIS 47:6; MATTITYAHU 15:5–6"> And there will I nourish thee; for yet there are chamesh shanim of ra'av (famine); lest thou, and thy bais, and all that thou hast, come to destitution.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:23; LUKAS 24:39; YOCHANAN 20:27"> And, hinei, your eyes see, and the eyes of achi Binyamin, that it is my mouth that speaketh unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:14; HISGALUS 21:23; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:10–12"> And ye shall tell Avi of kol my kavod b'Mitzrayim, and of all that ye have seen; and ye shall haste and bring down Avi here.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 33:4; BERESHIS 29:11; BERESHIS 46:29"> And he fell upon the neck of Binyamin achiv, and wept; and Binyamin wept upon his neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 15:20; SHMUEL ALEF 10:1; TEHILLIM 77:4"> Moreover he kissed all his achim, and wept upon them; and after that his achim talked with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:33; ESTHER 1:21; BERESHIS 20:15"> And the news thereof was heard in Bais Pharaoh saying, Achei Yosef are come; and it pleased Pharaoh well, and his avadim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:25–26; BERESHIS 44:1–2"> And Pharaoh said unto Yosef, Say unto thy achim, This do ye; load your beasts, and go, get you unto Eretz Kena'an;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:28; BAMIDBAR 18:12; BERESHIS 47:6"> And take Avichem and your households, and come unto me; and I will give you the best of Eretz Mitzrayim, ye shall eat the chelev haaretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:5; BERESHIS 45:27; BERESHIS 31:17–18"> Now thou art commanded, this do ye; take you agalot (wagons) out of Eretz Mitzrayim for your little ones, and for your nashim, and bring Avichem, and come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YESHAYAH 1:19; LUKAS 17:31; DEVARIM 7:16"> Also regard not your kelim; for the best of kol Eretz Mitzrayim is yours.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 45:19; YECHEZKEL 23:24; EKHAH 1:18"> And the Bnei Yisroel did so; and Yosef gave them agalot, according to the commandment of Pharaoh, and gave them provision for the derech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BERESHIS 43:34; MELACHIM BAIS 5:5; SHOFETIM 14:12"> To all of them he gave each man chalifot semalot; but to Binyamin he gave three hundred pieces of kesef, and chamesh chalifot semalot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:10; SHEMOT 16:3; BERESHIS 43:11"> And to Aviv he sent after this manner; ten chamorim loaded with the best of Mitzrayim, and ten female donkeys loaded with grain and lechem and provision for Aviv for the derech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:13; TEHILLIM 133:1–3; BERESHIS 42:21–22"> So he sent his achim away, and they departed; and he said unto them, See that ye not quarrel by the derech.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And they went up out of Mitzrayim, and came into Eretz Kena'an unto Ya'akov avihem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="BERESHIS 44:28; BERESHIS 37:35; LUKAS 24:34"> And told him, saying, Yosef chai!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Lk 24:6">c</a></sup>Yosef is yet alive, and he is Moshel kol Eretz Mitzrayim. And Ya'akovʼs lev fainted, for he believed them not.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Lk 24:10-11">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="TEHILLIM 85:6; YESHAYAH 57:15; BERESHIS 45:19"> And they told him kol divrei Yosef, which he had said unto them; and when he saw the agalot which Yosef had sent to carry him, the ruach Ya'akov avihem revived;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKAS 2:28–30; YOCHANAN 16:21–22; BERESHIS 46:30"> And Yisroel said, It is enough; Yosef Beni chai! Yosef Beni is yet alive; I will go and see him before I die.</span>
</div>
