<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:9–20; YEHOSHUA 10:40; SHMUEL BAIS 24:7"> And Avram went up out of Mitzrayim, he, and his isha, and all that he had, and Lot with him, into the Negev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:35; TEHILLIM 112:1–3; BERESHIS 26:12–13"> And Avram was very rich in cattle, in kesef, and in zahav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:8–9; BERESHIS 12:6"> And he went on his journeys from the Negev even to Beit-El, unto the makom where his ohel had been at the beginning, between Beit-El and Ai;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 29:12; BERESHIS 12:7–8; YESHAYAH 58:9"> Unto the makom of the Mizbe'ach, which he had built there at the first; and there Avram called on the Shem of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 49:29; BERESHIS 25:27; BERESHIS 4:20"> And Lot also, which walked with Avram, had tzon, and herds, and ohalim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 36:6–7; KOHELET 5:10–11; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:9"> And ha'aretz was not able to bear them, that they might dwell together; for their rechush was rav, so that they could not dwell together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 26:20; BERESHIS 12:6; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16"> And there was a riv between the ro'im of the herd of Avram and the ro'im of the herd of Lot; and the Kena'ani and the Perizzi dwelt then in ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:6–7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:26; TEHILLIM 133:1"> And Avram said unto Lot, Let there be no merivah now between me and thee, and between my ro'im and thy ro'im; for we are achim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 20:15; BERESHIS 34:10; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:8–12"> Is not the kol ha'aretz before thee? Separate thyself, now, from me; if thou wilt take the left hand, then I will go to the right; or if thou depart to the right hand, then I will go to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEVARIM 34:3; YESHAYAH 51:3; BERESHIS 14:8"> And Lot lifted up his eyes, and beheld all the plain of Yarden, that it was well watered everywhere, before Hashem destroyed Sodom and Amora, even as the Gan Hashem, like Eretz Mitzrayim, as you go to Tzoar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 13:9; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:25"> Then Lot chose for him all the plain of Yarden; and Lot journeyed mikedem (east); and they separated themselves the one from his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 19:29; BERESHIS 14:12; BERESHIS 19:1"> Avram dwelled in Eretz Kena'an, and Lot dwelled in the cities of the plain, and pitched his ohel toward Sodom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:10; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:6–8; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7"> But the men of Sodom were ra'im and chatta'im before Hashem exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YESHAYAH 49:18; DEVARIM 3:27; BERESHIS 28:14"> And Hashem said unto Avram, after that Lot was separated from him, Lift up now thine eyes, and look from the makom where thou art northward, and southward and eastward, and westward:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:7; BERESHIS 35:12; BERESHIS 15:18"> For kol ha'aretz which thou seest, to thee will I give it, and to thy zera ad olam (forever).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:14; BAMIDBAR 23:10; BERESHIS 32:12"> And I will make thy zera as the dust of ha'aretz; so that if a man can number the aphar of ha'aretz, then shall thy zera also be numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 13:15; BAMIDBAR 13:17–24"> Arise, walk through ha'aretz in the length of it and in the breadth of it; for I will give it unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:1; BERESHIS 8:20; BERESHIS 35:27"> Then Avram moved his ohel, and came and dwelt in Elonei Mamre, which is in Chevron, and built there a Mizbe'ach unto Hashem.</span>
