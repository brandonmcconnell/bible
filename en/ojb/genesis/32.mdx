## &#91;VAYISHLACH&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:6; LUKAS 14:11; BERESHIS 33:8"> And Ya'akov sent malachim before him to Esav achiv unto Eretz Se'ir, the country of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BERESHIS 33:8; BERESHIS 33:15; BERESHIS 33:11"> And he commanded them, saying, Thus shall ye speak unto adoni Esav; Thy eved Ya'akov saith thus, I have sojourned with Lavan, and stayed there until now;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 33:1; BERESHIS 32:8; BERESHIS 32:11"> And I have shor (oxen), and chamor, tzon, and eved, and shifchah; and I have sent to tell adoni, that I may find chen (grace) in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 35:3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:8–10; SHEMOT 14:10"> And the malachim returned to Ya'akov, saying, We came to Esav achicha, and also he cometh to meet thee, and arba me'ot ish with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:16; BERESHIS 33:1–3"> Then Ya'akov was greatly afraid and distressed; and he divided HaAm that was with him, and the tzon, and bakar, and the gemalim, into two machanot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 31:42; TEHILLIM 34:4–6; BERESHIS 31:3"> And said, If Esav come to the one machaneh, and attack it, then the other machaneh which is left shall escape.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 18:35; BERESHIS 24:27; SHMUEL BAIS 7:18"> And Ya'akov said, O Elohei Avi Avraham, and Elohei Avi Yitzchak, Hashem which saidst unto me, Shuv l'aretzecha and to thy moledet (kindred), and I will deal well with thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TEHILLIM 59:1–2; MISHLE 18:19; MATTITYAHU 6:13"> I am not worthy of the least of all the chasadim, and of all the emes, which Thou hast showed unto Thy eved; for with my makal (rod, staff) I passed over this Yarden; and now I am become two machanot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:13–15; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:17; LETTER TO TITOS 1:2"> Save me, now, from the yad achi, from the yad Esav; for I fear him, lest he will come and attack me, em with banim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MISHLE 18:16; BERESHIS 43:11; MISHLE 19:6"> But Thou saidst, I will surely do thee good, and make thy zera as the chol of the yam, which cannot be numbered for multitude.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="IYOV 1:3; DEVARIM 8:18; IYOV 42:12"> And he spent there that same night; and took of that which came to his yad a minchah for Esav achiv;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Two hundred female goats, and twenty male goats, two hundred rechelim (ewes), and twenty eilim (rams),</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 33:8–9; BERESHIS 32:20; MISHLE 2:11"> Thirty nursing gemalim with their colts, forty parot (cows), and ten parim (bulls), twenty female donkeys, and ten male donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 33:3"> And he delivered them into the yad of his avadim, every herd by itself; and said unto his avadim, Pass over before me, and keep a space between herd and herd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BERESHIS 32:4–5"> And he commanded the rishon, saying, When Esav achi meeteth thee, and asketh thee, saying, Whose art thou? And to where goest thou? And whose are these &#91;animals&#93; before thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then thou shalt say, They are of thy eved Ya'akov; it is a minchah sent unto adoni Esav; and, hinei, also he is behind us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="IYOV 42:8–9; MISHLE 21:14; BERESHIS 43:11"> And so commanded he the second, and the third, and all that followed the adarim (herds), saying, On this manner shall ye speak unto Esav, when ye find him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And say ye moreover, Hinei, thy eved Ya'akov is behind us. For he said, Akhapperah (I will appease, cover) his face with the minchah that goeth ahead of me, and afterward I will see his face; perhaps he will accept me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEVARIM 3:16; DEVARIM 2:37; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:8"> So went the minchah (present) over ahead of him; but he himself stayed balailah in the machaneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And he rose up that night, and took his two nashim and his two shifchot, and his eleven yeladim, and passed over the ma'avar (ford) Yabbok.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="HOSHEA 12:3–5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:12; LUKAS 13:24"> And he took them, and sent them over the stream, and sent over &#91;all&#93; that he had.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:7–9; HOSHEA 12:3–4; MATTITYAHU 26:41"> And Ya'akov was left by himself; and there wrestled an ish with him until the shachar (dawn, sunup).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="HOSHEA 12:4; TEHILLIM 115:12–13; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:8–9"> And when he saw that he prevailed not against him, he struck his hip socket; so Ya'akovʼs hip socket dislocated while he wrestled with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And he said, Let me go, for shachar breaketh. And he said, I will not let thee go, unless thou make a berakhah upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:17; BERESHIS 35:10; YESHAYAH 62:2–4"> And he said unto him, What is shemecha? And he said, Ya'akov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YESHAYAH 9:6; SHOFETIM 13:16–18; IYOV 11:7"> And he said, Shimcha shall be called no more Ya'akov, but Yisroel: for sarita im Elohim (&#91;yisrah=to prevail + El=G-d = Yisroel&#93; ye have striven with Elohim) and with anashim, and hast overcome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="SHEMOT 24:10–11; BAMIDBAR 12:8; YESHAYAH 6:5"> And Ya'akov asked him, and said, Tell me, now, shemecha. And he said, Why is it that thou dost ask after shmi? And he made a brocha upon him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:7; BERESHIS 19:15; SHOFETIM 8:8"> And Ya'akov called the shem of the makom (place) Peniel (Face of G-d): for I have seen Elohim panim el panim, and my nefesh is saved.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 5:5"> And as he passed over Penuel the shemesh rose upon him, and he limped upon his hip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Therefore the Bnei Yisroel eat not of the sinew of the thigh vein (gid hanasheh, sciatic nerve) which is upon the hip socket, unto this day; because he touched the hip socket of Ya'akov in the sinew of the thigh vein.</span>
</div>
