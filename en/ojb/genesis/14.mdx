<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 10:10; BERESHIS 10:22; YESHAYAH 37:12"> And it came to pass in the yamim of Amraphel Melech Shinar,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., Babylon">a</a></sup>Aryoch Melech Ellasar, Kedorlaomer Melech Eilam, and Tidal Melech Goyim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 13:10; DEVARIM 29:23; BERESHIS 10:19"> That these made milchamah with Bera Melech Sodom, and with Birsha Melech Amora (Gomorrah), Shinav Melech Admah, and Shemever Melech Tzevoyim, and the Melech of Bela, which is Tzoar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 3:16; DEVARIM 3:17; BAMIDBAR 34:12"> All these were joined together in the Valley of Siddim which is the Yam HaMelach.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., Dead Sea">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 9:25–26; YECHEZKEL 17:15"> Twelve shanah they served Kedorlaomer, and in the thirteenth year they rebelled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 3:11; BERESHIS 15:20; DEVARIM 1:4"> And in the fourteenth year came Kedorlaomer, and the melachim that were with him, and they defeated the Repha'im in Ashterot Karnayim, and the Zuzim in Ham, and the Emim in Shaveh-Kiryatayim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:22; DEVARIM 2:12; BERESHIS 21:21"> And the Chori in their Mt Se'ir, as far as Eil-Paran, which is near the midbar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:2; BERESHIS 16:14; BERESHIS 20:1"> And they turned, and came to Ein Mishpat, which is Kadesh, and conquered all the country of the Amaleki, and also the Emori, that dwelt in Chatzetzon-Tamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 19:22; BERESHIS 13:10; BERESHIS 19:20"> And there went out the Melech Sodom, and the Melech Amora (Gomorrah), and the Melech Admah, and the Melech Tzevoyim, and the Melech Bela (that is, Tzoar); and they joined in milchamah with them in the Valley of Siddim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> With Kedorlaomer Melech Eilam, and with Tidal Melech Goyim, and Amraphel Melech Shinar, and Aryoch Melech Ellasar; four melachim against five.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 19:17; BERESHIS 19:30; YESHAYAH 24:18"> And the Valley of Siddim was full of tar pits; and the Melech Sodom and Amora (Gomorrah) they fled, and fell there; and they that remained fled to the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 14:21; BERESHIS 14:16; DEVARIM 28:31"> And they took all the possessions of Sodom and Amora (Gomorrah), and all their ochel, and went their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:5; BERESHIS 11:27; HISGALUS 3:19"> And they took Lot, Avramʼs brotherʼs son, who dwelt in Sodom, and his possessions, and departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 13:18; BERESHIS 14:24; BERESHIS 40:15"> And there came one that had escaped, and told Avram HaIvri; for he dwelt in Elonei Mamre the Emori, brother of Eshcol, and brother of Aner; and these were Ba'alei Brit Avram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHOFETIM 18:29; BERESHIS 15:3; BERESHIS 17:27"> And when Avram heard that his brother was taken captive, he armed his trained men, born in his own bais, three hundred and eighteen, and pursued them as far as Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:2; TEHILLIM 112:5; MELACHIM ALEF 15:18"> And he divided himself against them, he and his avadim, by lailah, and routed them, and pursued them as far as Chovah, which is on the left hand (north) of Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 30:18–19; SHMUEL ALEF 30:8; BERESHIS 12:2"> And he recovered all the possessions, and also brought again his brother Lot, and his possessions, and the nashim also, and the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 18:18; MISHLE 19:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:1"> And the Melech Sodom went out to meet him after his return from the defeat of Kedorlaomer, and of the melachim that were with him, at the Valley of Shaveh, which is the Valley of the King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:20–3; TEHILLIM 110:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:6"> And Malki-Tzedek Melech Shalem brought forth lechem and yayin and he was the kohen of El Elyon.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 110:4">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:6–7; BERESHIS 14:22; TEHILLIM 24:1"> And he blessed him, and said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Baruch Avram by El Elyon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Possessor of Shomayim va'Aretz;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:22; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:4–9; MALACHI 3:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">And baruch El Elyon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Who hath delivered thine enemies into thy hand.</span><br />And he gave him ma'aser (tithe) of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the Melech Sodom said unto Avram, Give me the nefesh and keep the possessions for thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DANIEL 12:7; DANIEL 4:34; DEVARIM 32:40"> And Avram said to the Melech Sodom, I have lifted up mine hand unto Hashem, El Elyon Possessor of Shomayim va'Aretz,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 5:16; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:9–12; ESTHER 9:15–16"> That I will not take from a thread even to the thong of a sandal, and that I will not take any thing that is thine, lest thou shouldest say, I have made Avram rich;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BERESHIS 14:13; MISHLE 3:27; MATTITYAHU 7:12"> Save only that which the young men have eaten, and the chelek of the anashim which went with me, Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre; let them take their chelek.</span>
