</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BERESHIS 3:23; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:7; IYOV 5:10"> And every plant of the sadeh was not yet in ha'aretz, and every herb of the sadeh had not yet yitzmach (sprung up);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Moshiach is Tzemach, Zech 3:8">a</a></sup>for Hashem Elohim had not caused it to rain upon ha'aretz, and there was not an adam (man) la'avod (to till, to work) haadamah (the ground).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But there went up a mist from ha'aretz, and watered the whole face of the adamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="KOHELET 12:7; IYOV 33:4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:45"> And Hashem Elohim formed the adam of the aphar min haadamah, and breathed into his nostrils the nishmat chayyim; and the adam became a nefesh chayyah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 28:13; BERESHIS 3:24; BERESHIS 13:10"> And Hashem Elohim planted a gan (garden) eastward in Eden; and there He put the adam whom He had formed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 3:22; HISGALUS 2:7; HISGALUS 22:14"> And out of the adamah made Hashem Elohim to spring up<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Gn 2:5">b</a></sup>kol etz (every tree) that is pleasing to the sight, and tov for food; the Etz HaChayyim (Tree of Life) also in the midst of the gan (garden), and the Etz HaDa'as Tov v'Rah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Gn 3:22, i.e., representing moral autonomy; contrast Ex 9:20-21where the Dvar Hashem is the guide of life, even for Gentiles">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HISGALUS 22:1; TEHILLIM 46:4"> And a nahar (river) flowed out of Eden to water the gan (garden); and from there it divided, and became four headwaters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:18; BERESHIS 10:29; SHMUEL ALEF 15:7"> The shem of the first is Pishon; that is it which winds through the kol eretz Chavilah, where there is zahav;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:20; BAMIDBAR 11:7; YECHEZKEL 28:13"> And the zahav of that land is tov; there is &#91;the gemstone&#93; bedolach and the even (gemstone) hashoham.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="this onyx gemstone is used in the construction of the Kohen Gadolʼs Bigdei HaKodesh">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 10:6; YESHAYAH 11:11"> And the shem of the second nahar is Gichon; the same is it that winds through kol eretz Kush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DANIEL 10:4; BERESHIS 15:18; DEVARIM 1:7"> And the shem of the third nahar is Chiddekel (Tigris):<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Dan 10:4">e</a></sup>that is it which goeth toward the east of Assyria. And the fourth nahar is Euphrates.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 128:2; BERESHIS 2:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:28"> And Hashem Elohim took the adam and put him in the Gan Eden la'avod (to till, to work) it and to be shomer over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:22; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:4; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:17"> And Hashem Elohim commanded the adam, saying, Of every etz of the gan thou mayest freely eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:23; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:15; BERESHIS 3:19"> But of the Etz HaDa'as Tov v'Rah, thou shalt not eat of it; for in the yom that thou eatest thereof thou shalt surely die.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1C 15:22; Ro 7:10">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="KOHELET 4:9–12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:7–12; MISHLE 18:22"> And Hashem Elohim said, It is not tov that the adam should be alone; I will make him an ezer (a helper) suitable for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="TEHILLIM 8:4–8; BERESHIS 1:28; BERESHIS 9:2"> And out of the adamah Hashem Elohim formed every beast of the sadeh, and every oph HaShomayim; and brought them unto the adam to see what he would name them; and whatsoever the adam named kol nefesh chayyah, that was shmo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 2:18"> And the adam gave shemot to all behemah, and to the oph HaShomayim, and to every beast of the sadeh; but for Adam there was not found an ezer for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:12; IYOV 4:13; SHMUEL ALEF 26:12"> And Hashem Elohim caused a tardemah (deep sleep) to fall upon the adam, and he slept; and He took from one of his tzalelot (sides, ribs), and closed up the basar in the place thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:8–9; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:4"> And the tzela (rib), which Hashem Elohim had taken from the adam, made He an isha, and brought her unto the adam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:28–30; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:8–9; SHMUEL BAIS 5:1"> And the adam said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">This is now etzem of my etzem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and basar of my basar;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">she shall be called Isha,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because she was taken out of Ish.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:3–9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:28–31; MARKOS 10:6–12"> Therefore shall an ish leave his av and his em, and shall cleave unto his isha: and they shall be basar echad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BERESHIS 3:7; BERESHIS 3:10–11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:11"> And they were both arummim (naked ones), the adam and his isha, and were not ashamed.</span>
