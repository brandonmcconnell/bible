<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YESHAYAH 41:10; TEHILLIM 119:114; TEHILLIM 84:11"> After these things the Devar Hashem came unto Avram in a makhazeh, saying, Fear not, Avram; I am thy mogen, and thy exceeding great sachar (reward).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:5; BERESHIS 12:1–3"> And Avram said, Adonoi Hashem, what wilt Thou give me, since I go childless, and the Ben Meshek of my Bais is this Eli'ezer of Damesek (Damascus)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 14:14; MISHLE 29:21; KOHELET 2:7"> And Avram said, See, to me Thou hast given no zera; and, hinei, one born in my bais is my yoresh (heir).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:28; SHMUEL BAIS 7:12"> And, hinei, the Devar Hashem came unto him saying, This shall not be thy yoresh; but he that shall come forth out of thine own body shall be thy yoresh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:18; DEVARIM 1:10"> And He brought him forth outside, and said, Look now toward Shomayim, and count the kokhavim, if thou be able to number them; and He said unto him, So shall thy zera be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:6–14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:9"> And he believed in Hashem; and He credited &#91;emunah (faith)&#93; to him as tzedakah (righteousness).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:2–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:13"> And He said unto him, I am Hashem Who brought thee out of Ur Kasdim, to give thee ha'aretz hazot to be the yoresh of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKAS 1:18; LUKAS 1:34; YESHAYAH 7:11"> And he said, Adonoi Hashem, how can I have da'as that I will be its yoresh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 2:24; VAYIKRA 14:30; VAYIKRA 14:22"> And He said unto him, Bring Me a heifer meshuleshet, and a she goat meshuleshet, and a ram meshulash, and a dove, and a pigeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 34:18–19; VAYIKRA 1:17; BERESHIS 15:17"> And he brought unto Him all these, and divided them in two, and laid each half one opposite another; but the birds divided he not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:4; TEHILLIM 119:13; YECHEZKEL 17:3"> And when the birds of prey came down upon the carcasses, Avram drove them away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 2:21; IYOV 33:15; DANIEL 10:8–9"> And when the shemesh was going down, a tardemah fell upon Avram; and, hinei, a horror of great darkness fell upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:6–7; SHEMOT 1:11; SHEMOT 12:40–41"> And Hashem said unto Avram, Know of a surety that thy zera shall be a ger in a land not their own, and will serve them; and they will mistreat them arba me'ot shanah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 9:9–11; SHEMOT 6:5–6; SHEMOT 12:32–38"> And also that nation, whom they shall serve, will I judge; and afterward shall they come out with rechush gadol (great substance).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="IYOV 5:26; BERESHIS 25:7–9; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:28"> And thou shalt go to thy avot in shalom; thou shalt be buried at a good old age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 21:26; DANIEL 8:23; SHEMOT 12:40"> But in the fourth generation they shall come back here again; for the avon (iniquity) of the Emori (Amorite) is not yet full.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 34:18–19; SHOFETIM 6:21; YIRMEYAH 11:4"> And it came to pass, that, when the shemesh went down, and it was dark, hinei a smoking fire-pot, and a blazing torch passed between those pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 1:3–4; BERESHIS 12:7; BERESHIS 24:7"> On that day Hashem cut a brit (covenant) with Avram, saying, Unto thy zera have I given ha'aretz hazot, from the Wadi of Mitzrayim unto the great river, the river Euphrates:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 24:21–22"> &#91;the land of&#93; the Keni, and the Kenizzi, and the Kadmoni,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YESHAYAH 17:5; BERESHIS 14:5"> And the Chitti, and the Perizzi, and the Repha'im,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 10:15–19; SHEMOT 23:23–28; DEVARIM 7:1"> And the Emori, and the Kena'ani, and the Girgashi, and the Yevusi.</span>
