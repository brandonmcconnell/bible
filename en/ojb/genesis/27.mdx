<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:14; KOHELET 9:10; MISHLE 27:1"> And he said, Hinei now, I am old, I know not my yom mot:</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:20; BERESHIS 48:9; BERESHIS 49:28"> And make me matamim (savory meat, tasty food), such as I love, and bring it to me, that I may eat; that my nefesh may make a brocha upon thee before I die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Rivkah heard when Yitzchak spoke to Esav bno. And Esav went to the sadeh to hunt for wild game, and to bring it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:28"> And Rivkah spoke unto Ya'akov her ben, saying, Hinei, I heard avicha speak unto Esav achicha, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 6:26; SHMUEL ALEF 24:19; DEVARIM 33:1"> Bring me wild game, and make me matamim, that I may eat, and make a brocha upon thee before Hashem before my mot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:1; BERESHIS 27:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:29"> Now therefore, beni (my son), obey my kol (voice) according to that which I command thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHOFETIM 13:15; SHMUEL ALEF 16:20"> Go now to the tzon, and bring me from there two gedayei izzim tovim (good kids of goats); and I will make them matamim for avicha, such as he loveth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And thou shalt bring it to avicha, that he may eat, and that he may make a brocha upon thee before his mot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:25"> And Ya'akov said to Rivkah immo, Look, Esav achi is an ish sa'ir (hairy man), and I am an ish chalak (smooth man).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEVARIM 27:18; BERESHIS 25:27; MALACHI 1:14"> What if avi will touch me, and I shall seem to him as a meta'te'a (mocker); and I shall bring a kelalah upon me, and not a brocha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:25; SHMUEL ALEF 25:24; SHMUEL BAIS 14:9"> And immo said unto him, Upon me be thy kelalah, beni; only obey my kol (voice), and go bring me them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:9; MISHLE 23:2–3; BERESHIS 25:28"> And he went, and got, and brought them to immo: and immo made matamim, such as aviv loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:27; LUKAS 20:46; LUKAS 15:22"> And Rivkah took begadim of Esav her ben hagadol, the chamudot (best ones) which were with her in the bais, and dressed Ya'akov her ben hakatan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And with orot gedayei haizzim (skins of the kids of the goats) she dressed and covered his hands, and also upon the smooth of his tzavar (neck);</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And she gave the matamim and the lechem, which she had prepared, into the yad Ya'akov her ben.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he came unto aviv, and said, Avi; and he said, Hineni; who art thou, beni?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 28:15; MATTITYAHU 26:70–74; ZECHARYAH 13:3–4"> And Ya'akov said unto aviv, I am Esav thy bechor; I have done according as thou told me; arise, now, sit up and eat of my wild game, that thy nefesh may make a brocha upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:7; IYOV 13:7; BERESHIS 24:12"> And Yitzchak said unto bno, How is it that thou hast found it so quickly, beni? And he said, Hashem Eloheicha worked it out for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:8; YESHAYAH 57:19; BERESHIS 27:12"> And Yitzchak said unto Ya'akov, Come near, now, that I may touch thee, beni, indeed, whether thou be beni Esav or not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Ya'akov went near unto Yitzchak aviv; and he touched him, and said, The kol is kol Ya'akov, but the yadayim are the yedei Esav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:16; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:11–12"> And he discerned him not, for his hands were se'irot, as yedei Esav achiv; so he made a brocha upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:25; MISHLE 12:22"> And he said, Art thou indeed beni Esav? And he said, I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:4"> And he said, Bring it near to me, and I will eat of the wild game of beni, that my nefesh may make a brocha upon thee. And he brought it near to him, and he did eat; and he brought him yayin and he drank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And aviv Yitzchak said unto him, Come near now, and kiss me beni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:7; TEHILLIM 65:10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:20"> And he came near, and kissed him: and he smelled the reiach (smell) of his begadim, and made a brocha upon him, and said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">See, the reiach of beni</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">is as the reiach of a sadeh</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">upon which Hashem hath made a brocha;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEVARIM 33:28; DEVARIM 33:13; TEHILLIM 133:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore HaElohim give thee of the tal haShomayim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the fatness of ha'aretz,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and plenty of dagan and tirosh;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:3; BAMIDBAR 24:9; YESHAYAH 49:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let people serve thee,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and amim bow down to thee:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">be gevir over thy achim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and let bnei immecha bow down to thee:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">arur be every one that curseth thee,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and baruch be he that blesseth thee.</span></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And as soon as Yitzchak had completed making on Ya'akov a brocha, and Ya'akov had hardly gone out from the presence of Yitzchak aviv, that Esav achiv came in from his hunt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:4"> And he also had prepared matamim, and brought it unto aviv and said unto aviv, Let avi arise, and eat of the wild game of bno, that thy nefesh may make a brocha upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:18"> And Yitzchak aviv said unto him, Mi atah? (Who art thou?) And he said, I am binecha, thy bechor Esav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:3–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:3"> And Yitzchak trembled with charadah gedolah ad me'od (exceedingly great trembling), and said, Who? Where is he that hath hunted wild game, and brought it me, and I have eaten of all of it before thou camest, and have made a brocha upon him? And indeed he shall be baruch (blessed)!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:17; MISHLE 1:31; SHMUEL ALEF 30:4"> And when Esav heard the words of aviv, he cried with tzeakah gedolah umarah ad me'od (a great and exceeding bitter cry), and said unto aviv, Make a brocha on me, even me also, O avi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:6; IYOV 13:7; MALACHI 2:10"> And he said, Achicha came with mirmah (deceit), and hath taken away thy brocha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:26; YOCHANAN 1:47; BERESHIS 32:28"> And he said, Is not he rightly called shmo Ya'akov? for vaya'keveni (now he deceived/outwitted me) these two times he took away my bechorah (birthright); and, hinei, now he hath taken away my brocha (blessing). And he asked, Hast thou not reserved a brocha for me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:14; BERESHIS 27:28–29; BERESHIS 25:23"> And Yitzchak answered and said unto Esav, See, I have made him gevir over you, and all his achim have I given to him for avadim; and with dagan and tirosh have I sustained him; and what shall I do now unto thee, beni?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:17; BERESHIS 27:34; YESHAYAH 65:14"> And Esav said unto aviv, Hast thou but one brocha, avi? Make a brocha on me, even me also, O avi. And Esav lifted up his kol (voice), and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:28; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:20; YEHOSHUA 24:4"> And Yitzchak aviv answered and said unto him,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Hinei, thy moshav (dwelling) shall be the fatness of ha'aretz,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and of the tal HaShomayim from above;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 8:20–22; BERESHIS 25:23; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">And by thy cherev shalt thou live,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and shalt serve achicha;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and it shall come to pass when thou shalt become restless,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that thou shalt break his ol (yoke) from off thy tzavar (neck).</span></span>
</div>
