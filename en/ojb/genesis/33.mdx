<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 32:6–7; BERESHIS 27:41–42; BERESHIS 32:16"> And Ya'akov lifted up his eyes, and looked, and, hinei, Esav came, and with him arba me'ot ish (four hundred men). And he divided the yeladim unto Leah, and unto Rachel, and unto the two shefachot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 29:30; BERESHIS 30:22–24; BERESHIS 37:3"> And he put the shefachot and their yeladim rishonah, and Leah and her yeladim acharonim, and Rachel and Yosef acharonim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:6; LUKAS 14:11; BERESHIS 18:2"> And he passed over before them, and bowed himself to the ground sheva pe'amim, until he came near to achiv (his brother).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 45:14–15; TEHILLIM 34:4; LUKAS 15:20"> And Esav ran to meet him, and embraced him, and fell on his tzavar (neck), and kissed him; and they wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 127:3; YESHAYAH 8:18; BERESHIS 48:9"> And he lifted up his eyes, and saw the nashim and the yeladim; and said, Who are those with thee? And he said, The yeladim which Elohim hath graciously given thy eved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then the shefachot came near, they and their yeladim, and they bowed themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Leah also with her yeladim came near, and bowed themselves; and after came Yosef near and Rachel, and they bowed themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 32:5; BERESHIS 39:5; BERESHIS 32:13–20"> And he said, What meanest thou by all this machaneh which I met? And he said, These are to find chen (grace) in the eyes of adoni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:39; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:7; BERESHIS 27:41"> And Esav said, I have enough, achi (my brother); keep that thou hast unto thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="IYOV 33:26; HISGALUS 22:4; BERESHIS 32:30"> And Ya'akov said, No, now, if I have found chen (grace) in thy sight, then receive my minchah at my yad inasmuch as I have seen thy face, as though I had seen the p'nei Elohim (the face of G-d) vatirtzeini (and thou wast pleased to accept me, thou wast appeased by me).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:18; BERESHIS 30:43"> Accept, now, my brocha that is brought to thee; because Elohim hath dealt graciously with me, and because yesh li khol (there is to me all, my needs are met). And he urged him, and he accepted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he said, Nise'ah (let us take our journey), and let us go, I will go next to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:11; YECHEZKEL 34:15–16; YECHEZKEL 34:23–25"> And he said unto him, Adoni knoweth that the yeladim are tender and the nursing tzon and bakar are upon me; and if men should overdrive them yom echad, all the tzon will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 32:3; YESHAYAH 40:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:1"> Let now adoni, pass over before his eved; and I will lead on slowly, according to the pace of the drove that goeth before me and the pace the yeladim are able to endure, until I come unto adoni at Se'ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="RUTH 2:13; BERESHIS 47:25; BERESHIS 34:11"> And Esav said, Let me now leave with thee some of HaAm that are with me. And he said, What needeth it? Let me find chen in the sight of adoni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So Esav returned that day on his derech unto Se'ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TEHILLIM 60:6; YEHOSHUA 13:27; SHOFETIM 8:5"> And Ya'akov journeyed to Sukkot, and built him a bais, and made sukkot for his mikneh; therefore the shem of the makom is called Sukkot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHOFETIM 9:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:16; YEHOSHUA 24:1"> And Ya'akov came shalem to Ir Shechem, which is in eretz Kena'an, when he came from Paddan-Aram; and encamped before the Ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:16; YOCHANAN 4:5; YEHOSHUA 24:32"> And he bought a chelkat hasadeh (piece of land), where he had pitched there his ohel, from the yad Bnei Chamor Avi Shechem, for a hundred pieces of kesitah (money).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 35:7; BERESHIS 21:33; BERESHIS 32:28"> And he erected there a Mizbe'ach, and called it El Elohei Yisroel.</span>
