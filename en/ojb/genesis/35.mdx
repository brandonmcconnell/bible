<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 31:13; HOSHEA 12:4; TEHILLIM 91:15"> And Elohim said unto Ya'akov, Arise, go up to Beit-El, and settle there; and make there a Mizbe'ach unto El (G-d) that appeared unto thee when thou didst flee from the face of Esav achicha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:14; SHEMOT 19:10; YECHEZKEL 18:31"> Then Ya'akov said unto his Bais, and to all that were with him, Put away the elohei hanekhar that are among you, and be tahor, and change your simlah (garments);</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YESHAYAH 43:2; BERESHIS 32:7; BERESHIS 31:3"> And let us arise, and go up to Beit-El; and I will build there a Mizbe'ach unto El (G-d) Who answered me in my yom tzoros, and was with me in the derech in which I went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHOFETIM 9:6; DEVARIM 7:25; SHEMOT 32:20"> And they gave unto Ya'akov kol elohei hanekhar which were in their yad, and all their nezamim which were in their oznayim; and Ya'akov buried them under the elah (terebinth) which was at Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:27; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 17:10; YEHOSHUA 5:1"> And they journeyed; and the chittat Elohim (terror of G-d) was upon the cities that were around them, and they did not pursue after the Bnei Ya'akov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:19; BERESHIS 28:22; BERESHIS 12:8"> So Ya'akov came to Luz, which is in Eretz Kena'an, that is, Beit-El, he and kol haAm that were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 35:3; BERESHIS 35:1; BERESHIS 28:13"> And he built there a Mizbe'ach, and called the makom (place) El Beit-El; because there HaElohim appeared unto him, when he fled from the face of achiv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:59; SHMUEL ALEF 31:13; SHOFETIM 2:5"> But Devorah meineket Rivkah died, and she was buried under an alon (oak) below Beit-El; and shmo was called Alon Bachut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 35:1; BERESHIS 18:1; BERESHIS 26:2"> And Elohim appeared unto Ya'akov again, when he returned from Paddan-Aram, and made a brocha upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:5; BERESHIS 32:27–28; BERESHIS 17:15"> And Elohim said unto him, Shimcha is Ya'akov; shimcha shall not be called any more Ya'akov, but Yisroel shall be shemecha; and He called shmo Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:1; BERESHIS 9:1; BERESHIS 22:17"> And Elohim said unto him, I am El Shaddai; be fruitful and multiply; a Goy (nation) and a Kehal Goyim shall be from thee, and Melechim shall come out of thy loins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:7; BERESHIS 28:13; BERESHIS 26:3–4"> And HaAretz which I gave Avraham and Yitzchak, to thee I will give it, and to thy zera after thee will I give HaAretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:22; BERESHIS 18:33; SHOFETIM 6:21"> And Elohim went up from him in the makom where He talked with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:18–19; SHMUEL ALEF 7:12; SHEMOT 17:15"> And Ya'akov set up a matzevah (pillar, monument) in the makom where He talked with him, even a matzevat even (pillar of stone); and he poured a nesech (drink offering) thereon, and he poured shemen (oil) thereon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:19"> And Ya'akov called the shem of the makom where Elohim spoke with him, Beit-El.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MICHOH 5:2; BERESHIS 48:7; MELACHIM BAIS 5:19"> And they journeyed from Beit-El; and there was still a space of ha'aretz to get to Ephratah; and Rachel travailed, and she had hard labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 30:24; SHMUEL ALEF 4:19–21"> And it came to pass, when she was in hard labor, that the meyaledet (midwife) said unto her, Fear not; thou shalt have this ben also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKAS 12:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:59; LUKAS 23:46"> And it came to pass, as her nefesh was in departing, (for she died) that she called shmo Ben-Oni (Son of Affliction); but aviv called him Binyamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MICHOH 5:2; BERESHIS 48:7; RUTH 4:11"> And Rachel died, and was buried on the derech to Ephratah, which is Beit-Lechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 10:2; BERESHIS 35:14; SHMUEL BAIS 18:17–18"> And Ya'akov set up a matzevah upon her kever; that is matzevet kevurat Rachel to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MICHOH 4:8; LUKAS 2:8"> And Yisroel journeyed, and pitched his ohel beyond Migdal-Eder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:1; VAYIKRA 18:8; SHMUEL BAIS 20:3"> And it came to pass, when Yisroel dwelt in that land, that Reuven went and lay with Bilhah pilegesh aviv and Yisroel heard it. Now the Bnei Ya'akov were Sheneym Asar (Twelve);</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BERESHIS 30:18–20; BERESHIS 46:8–15; BERESHIS 29:32–35"> The Bnei Leah: Reuven bechor Ya'akov, and Shim'on, and Levi, and Yehudah, and Yissakhar, and Zevulun;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BERESHIS 30:22–24; BERESHIS 46:19–22; BERESHIS 35:16–18"> The Bnei Rachel: Yosef, and Binyamin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:23–25; BERESHIS 37:2; BERESHIS 30:4–8"> And the Bnei Bilhah shifchat Rachel: Dan, and Naphtali;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="BERESHIS 30:9–13; BERESHIS 46:16–18; BERESHIS 25:20"> And the Bnei Zilpah shifchat Leah: Gad, and Asher; these are the Bnei Ya'akov, which were born to him in Paddan-Aram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="BERESHIS 13:18; BERESHIS 23:19; BERESHIS 18:1"> And Ya'akov came unto Yitzchak Aviv unto Mamre, unto Kiriat-HaArba, which is Chevron, where Avraham and Yitzchak sojourned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:7; BERESHIS 50:26; BERESHIS 47:28"> And the days of Yitzchak were me'at shanah u'shemonim shanah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:15; BERESHIS 49:33; BERESHIS 49:31"> And Yitzchak expired, and died, and was gathered unto his people, being zaken (old) and full of yamim; and his banim Esav and Ya'akov buried him.</span>
