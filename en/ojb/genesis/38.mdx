<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 22:1; MELACHIM BAIS 4:8; MISHLE 13:20"> And it came to pass at that time, that Yehudah went down from his achim, and turned in to an ish Adullami shmo Chirah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:3; BERESHIS 24:3; BERESHIS 6:2"> And Yehudah saw there a bat ish Kena'ani shmo Shua; and he took her, and went in unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:12; BAMIDBAR 26:19"> She conceived, and bore ben; and he called shmo Er.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:12; BAMIDBAR 26:19"> And she conceived again, and bore ben; and she called shmo Onan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:20; BERESHIS 38:26; BERESHIS 46:12"> And she yet again conceived, and bore ben; and called shmo Shelah; at Keziv, when she bore him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:3; BERESHIS 21:21; MATTITYAHU 1:3"> And Yehudah took a wife for Er his bechor, whose shem was Tamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:3; BERESHIS 46:12; BAMIDBAR 26:19"> And Er, bechor Yehudah, was rah in the eyes of Hashem; and Hashem slaughtered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEVARIM 25:5–10; MATTITYAHU 22:23–27; RUTH 4:5–11"> And Yehudah said unto Onan, Go in unto eshet achicha, and enter into levirate marriage with her, and raise up zera to achicha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:3; DEVARIM 25:6; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16"> And Onan knew that the zera should not be his; and it came to pass, when he went in unto eshet achiv, that he spilled it on the ground, lest he should give zera to achiv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:12; BAMIDBAR 11:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:7"> And the thing which he did was rah in the eyes of Hashem; therefore He slaughtered him also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="VAYIKRA 22:13; RUTH 1:11–13"> Then said Yehudah to Tamar his shnur, Remain an almanah at thy bais avi, till Shelah beni is grown; for he said, Lest he die also, as his achim did. And Tamar went and dwelt in her bais avi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:10; SHMUEL BAIS 13:39; SHOFETIM 14:1"> And in process of time the bat Shua, eshet Yehudah, died; and Yehudah was consoled, and went up unto his sheepshearers to Timnah, he and his re'a Chirah the Adullami.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And it was told Tamar, saying, Hinei, thy shver (father-in-law) goeth up to Timnah to shear his tzon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 38:26; BERESHIS 38:11–13; YIRMEYAH 3:2"> And she put off her widowʼs garments from her, and covered her with a tze'if (veil, shleier), and disguised herself, and sat at the entrance of Einayim which is on the derech to Timnah; for she saw that Shelah was grown, and she was not given unto him as isha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> When Yehudah saw her, he thought her to be a zonah; because she had covered her face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:10; MATTITYAHU 26:15; SHMUEL BAIS 13:11"> And he turned unto her by the derech, and said, Come now, let me come in unto thee; (for he knew not that she was his shnur &#91;daughter-in-law&#93;). And she said, What wilt thou give me, that thou mayest come in unto me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 16:33; BERESHIS 38:20; MISHLE 20:16"> And he said, I will send thee a gedi izzim (kid of the goats). And she said, Wilt thou give me an eravon (pledge, mashkn), till thou send it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BERESHIS 38:25–26; HOSHEA 4:11; YIRMEYAH 22:24"> And he said, What eravon shall I give thee? And she said, Thy chotam (signet), and thy &#91;signet neck&#93; cord, and thy staff that is in thine yad. And he gave it her, and came in unto her, and she conceived by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BERESHIS 38:14; SHMUEL BAIS 14:5; SHMUEL BAIS 14:2"> And she arose, and went away, and took off her tze'if (veil, shleier) from her, and put on the garments of her widowhood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 20:9; VAYIKRA 19:17; SHMUEL BAIS 13:3"> And Yehudah sent the gedi izzim by the yad of his friend the Adullami, to get back his eravon (pledge, mashkn) from the yad haisha; but he found her not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then he asked the men of that place, saying, Where is the kedesha (cult prostitute) that was on the crossroads by the derech? And they said, There was no kedesha in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And he returned to Yehudah, and said, I cannot find her; and also the men of the place said that there was no kedesha in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:12; MISHLE 6:33; HISGALUS 16:15"> And Yehudah said, Let her take for herself, lest we become buz (laughingstock, shpot); hinei, I sent this gedi, and thou hast not found her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="VAYIKRA 21:9; YECHEZKEL 23:19; SHOFETIM 19:2"> And it came to pass about shlosh chadashim (three months) later, that it was told Yehudah saying, Tamar thy shnur hath played the zonah; and also, hinei, she is with child by zenunim (whoredom). And Yehudah said, Bring her forth, and let her be burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:32; BERESHIS 38:18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:5"> When she was brought forth, she sent to her shver (father-in-law), saying, By the man, whose these are, am I with child; and she said, Discern, now, whose are these, the chotam (signet), and the &#91;signet neck&#93; cord, and the staff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 24:17; YOCHANAN 8:9; LETTER TO TITOS 2:11–12"> And Yehudah acknowledged them, and said, She hath been more tzadekah than I; because that I gave her not to Shelah beni. And he knew her again no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:24"> And it came to pass in the time of her travail, that, hinei te'omim (twin boys) were in her beten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And it came to pass, when she travailed in labor, that the one put out his yad; and the midwife took and bound upon his yad a scarlet thread, saying, This came out rishonah (first).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:4; MATTITYAHU 1:3; BERESHIS 46:12"> And it came to pass, as he drew back his yad, that, hinei, his brother came out; and she said, How hast paratzta (thou broken out)? This paretz (breach, breaking) be upon thee; therefore shmo was called Peretz &#91;Breaking out&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 9:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:4; MATTITYAHU 1:3"> And afterward came out his brother, that had the scarlet thread upon his yad; and shmo was called Zerach.</span>
