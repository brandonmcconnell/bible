<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:12; BERESHIS 3:15; BERESHIS 4:25"> And HaAdam knew Chavah (Eve) his isha; and she conceived, and bore Kayin (Cain), and said, Kaniti (I have acquired) ish with Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:15; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:12; LUKAS 11:51"> And again she bore his ach Hevel (Abel).<br />And Hevel was a ro'eh tzon, but Kayin was an oved adamah (tiller of soil).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 18:12; VAYIKRA 2:1–11; MELACHIM ALEF 17:7"> And in ketz yamim it came to pass, that Kayin brought of the p'ri haadamah a minchah unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:4; MISHLE 3:9; SHEMOT 13:12"> And Hevel, he also brought of the bechorot of his tzon and of the chelev thereof. And Hashem regarded &#91;with favor, respect&#93; unto Hevel and to his minchah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:4; IYOV 5:2; LUKAS 15:28–30"> But unto Kayin and to his minchah He had not regard. And Kayin was very angry, and his countenance fell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YESHAYAH 1:18; IYOV 5:2; LUKAS 15:31–32"> And Hashem said unto Kayin, Why art thou angry? And why is thy countenance fallen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:6–10; YESHAYAH 3:10–11"> If thou doest right, shalt thou not be accepted? And if thou doest not right, chattat is lurking at the petach. And unto thee shall be its teshukah (desire), and thou must rule over it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:35; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:12–15; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:11"> And Kayin talked with Hevel his brother; and it came to pass, when they were in the sadeh, that Kayin rose up against Hevel his brother, and killed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MISHLE 28:13; YOCHANAN 8:44; TEHILLIM 9:12"> And Hashem said unto Kayin, Where is Hevel thy brother? And he said, I know not; am I shomer achi (my brotherʼs keeper)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:24; TEHILLIM 72:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:4"> And He said, What hast thou done? The voice of thy brotherʼs dahm crieth unto Me from haadamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:10; IYOV 16:18; BERESHIS 3:14"> And now art thou arur (cursed) from haadamah, which hath opened her mouth to receive thy brotherʼs dahm from thy hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HOSHEA 9:17; VAYIKRA 26:36; VAYIKRA 26:20"> When thou tillest haadamah, it shall not henceforth yield unto thee her ko'ach (strength); na (restless fugitive) vanad (and a wanderer, nomad) shalt thou be in ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="IYOV 15:22; HISGALUS 16:9; HISGALUS 16:11"> And Kayin said unto Hashem, My avon (iniquity, punishment for guilt) is greater than I can bear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 35:19; TEHILLIM 143:7; IYOV 15:20–24"> See, Thou hast driven me out this day from the face of the adamah; and from Thy face shall I be hidden and I shall be a na (restless fugitive) vanad (and a wanderer, nomad) in ha'aretz; and it shall come to pass, that whoever findeth me shall kill me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 9:4; TEHILLIM 79:12; BERESHIS 4:24"> And Hashem said unto him, Therefore whoever killeth Kayin, vengeance shall be taken on him sevenfold. And Hashem placed an ot (mark) upon Kayin, so that none finding him should kill him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 23:39; IYOV 2:7; BERESHIS 4:14"> And Kayin went out from the presence of Hashem, and dwelt in Eretz Nod, east of Eden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TEHILLIM 49:11; KOHELET 2:4–11; DANIEL 4:30"> And Kayin knew his isha; and she conceived, and bore Chanokh (Enoch); and he built an ir (city), and called the shem of the ir, after the shem beno, Chanokh (Enoch).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BERESHIS 36:2; BERESHIS 5:21"> And unto Chanokh (Enoch) was born Irad; and Irad fathered Mechuyael; and Mechuyael fathered Metushael; and Metushael fathered Lamech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BERESHIS 2:24; MATTITYAHU 19:8; BERESHIS 2:18"> And Lamech took unto him two nashim; the shem of the one was Adah, and the shem of the second Tzilah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 4:2; YIRMEYAH 35:9–10; YOCHANAN 8:44"> And Adah bore Yaval; he was the av of such as dwell in ohel, and have mikneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="IYOV 21:12; YESHAYAH 5:12; BERESHIS 31:27"> And his brotherʼs shem was Yuval; he was the av of all such as play kinnor (harp) and ugav (flute).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEVARIM 33:25; DEVARIM 8:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:7"> And Tzilah, she also bore Tuval-Kayin, a forger of every cutting tool of nechoshet and barzel; and the achot of Tuval-Kayin was Na'amah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 23:18; SHEMOT 20:13; VAYIKRA 19:18"> And Lamech said unto his wives,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Adah and Tzilah, Hear my voice;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">ye wives of Lamech, give heed to my speech;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for I have slain an ish for my wounding,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and a yeled for my chabburah.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Ga 6:17">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:22; BERESHIS 4:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">If Kayin (Cain) shall be avenged sevenfold,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">truly Lamech seventy and seven times over.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BERESHIS 4:8; BERESHIS 4:10–11; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 1:1"> And Adam knew his isha again; and she bore a ben, and called shmo Shet; for Elohim, said she, hath appointed me another zera in place of Hevel, whom Kayin killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="TEHILLIM 116:17; MELACHIM ALEF 18:24; YOEL 2:32"> And to Shet, to him also there was born a ben; and he called shmo Enosh; then began men to call upon the Shem of Hashem.</span>
</div>
