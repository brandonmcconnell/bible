<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BERESHIS 30:27; VAYIKRA 19:26; MELACHIM BAIS 21:6"> Is not this it in which adoni drinketh, and whereby indeed he divineth? Ye have done ra'ah in so doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he overtook them, and he spoke unto them these same devarim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 38:16–18; BERESHIS 37:18–32; MELACHIM BAIS 8:13"> And they said unto him, Wherefore saith adoni these words? Chalilah (far be it) that thy avadim should do according to this thing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 43:12; BERESHIS 42:35; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:9"> Hinei, the kesef, which we found in the mouth of our sacks, we brought back unto thee out of Eretz Kena'an; how then should we be ganavim stealing kesef or zahav out of the bais adoneicha?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 31:32; TEHILLIM 7:3–5; IYOV 31:38–40"> With whomsoever of thy avadim it be found, both let him die, and we also will be avadim of adoni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:3; BERESHIS 44:17; BERESHIS 44:33"> And he said, Now also let it be according unto your devarim; he with whom it is found shall be to me eved; and ye shall be nekiyim (blameless).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then they speedily took down every ish his sack to the ground, and opened every ish his sack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 44:26–32; BERESHIS 42:36–38; BERESHIS 43:14"> And he searched, and began at the gadol (eldest), and ended at the katon (youngest); and the gevi'a (cup, goblet) was found in the sack of Binyamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 1:11; BERESHIS 37:29–34; SHMUEL BAIS 1:2"> Then they tore their garments, and loaded every ish his chamor, and returned to the ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 50:18; BERESHIS 43:25; BERESHIS 43:16"> And Yehudah and his achim came to Bais Yosef; for he was yet there; and they fell before him on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 4:10; BERESHIS 21:26; BERESHIS 3:13"> And Yosef said unto them, What ma'aseh (deed) is this that ye have done? Know ye not that an ish like me can find things out as a menachesh (diviner)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 44:9; DANIEL 9:7; BAMIDBAR 32:23"> And Yehudah said, What shall we say unto adoni? What shall we speak? Or how shall we clear ourselves? HaElohim hath found out the avon (iniquity) of thy avadim; look at us, we are now avadim la'adoni, both we, and he also with whom the gevi'a (cup) is found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MISHLE 17:15; SHMUEL BAIS 23:3; BERESHIS 18:25"> And he &#91;Yosef&#93; said, Chalilah that I should do so; but the ish in whose yad the gevi'a is found, he shall be to me eved; and as for you, go back in shalom unto avichem.</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:15; BERESHIS 42:20; AMOS 9:4"> And thou saidst unto thy avadim, Bring him down unto me, that I may set mine eyes upon him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:19; BERESHIS 35:16–18; BERESHIS 30:22–25"> And thy eved Avi said unto us, Ye know that my wife bore me two banim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:33; BERESHIS 37:13–14; BERESHIS 42:36"> And the one went out from me, and I said, Surely he is torn to pieces; and I saw him not ad-hennah (until now);</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:38; BERESHIS 43:14; DEVARIM 31:17"> And if ye take this also from me, and ason (evil, harm) befall him, ye shall bring down my gray hair in ra'ah (misery) to Sheol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 18:1; SHMUEL BAIS 18:33; BERESHIS 44:31"> Now therefore when I come to thy eved Avi, and the na'ar be not with us; seeing that his nefesh is bound up in the nefesh of the na'ar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="BERESHIS 44:29; BERESHIS 37:35; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:10"> It shall come to pass, when he seeth that the na'ar is not with us, that he will die; and thy avadim shall bring down the gray hair of thy eved avinu with yagon (sorrow) to Sheol,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="BERESHIS 43:8–9; BERESHIS 43:16"> For thy eved became surety for the na'ar unto Avi, saying, If I bring him not unto thee, then I shall bear the blame before Avi kol hayamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16; SHEMOT 32:32; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:22"> Therefore, let thy eved, now, abide instead of the na'ar, an eved to adoni; and let the na'ar go up with his achim.</span>
