## &#91;VAYERA&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 14:13; BERESHIS 48:3; BERESHIS 13:18"> And Hashem appeared unto him in the terebinth trees of Mamre; and he sat in the entrance of the ohel (tent) in the heat of the day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:2; BERESHIS 18:22; BERESHIS 19:1"> And he lifted up his eyes and looked, and, hinei, Shloshah Anashim (Three Men)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Isa 6:3">a</a></sup>stood by him; and when he saw them, he ran to meet them from the entrance of the ohel, and prostrated himself on the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 32:5"> And said, Adonoi,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hashem, see Gn 18:13">b</a></sup>if now I have found chen (favor) in Thy sight, pass not on by, please, from Thy servant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:32; BERESHIS 19:2; BERESHIS 43:24"> Let a little water, please, be brought, and wash Thy feet, and rest under haetz (the tree);</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 104:15; SHOFETIM 13:15; SHOFETIM 6:18"> And I will bring a morsel of bread, and refresh Ye Your hearts; after that Ye shall pass on; because for this purpose are Ye come to Your servant. And they said, So do, as thou hast said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:33; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:15"> And Avraham hastened into the ohel unto Sarah, and said, Make ready quickly shlosh measures of fine meal, knead it, and make cakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MALACHI 1:14; LUKAS 15:27; LUKAS 15:30"> And Avraham ran unto the herd, and brought a calf tender and tov, and gave it unto a servant; and he hasted to prepare it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 19:3; SHOFETIM 13:15; HISGALUS 3:20"> And he took curds, and cholov (milk), and the calf which he had prepared, and set it before them; and he stood by them under haetz, and they did eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 2:5; BERESHIS 24:67; BERESHIS 4:9"> And they said unto him, Where is Sarah thy wife? And he said, Hinei, in the ohel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 21:2; BERESHIS 17:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:8–9"> And He said, I will certainly return unto thee at this time next year; and, hinei, Sarah thy wife shall have ben (son). And Sarah heard it in the entrance of the ohel, which was behind him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:11–12; LUKAS 1:36"> Now Avraham and Sarah were zekenim (old ones) and well stricken in age; and &#91;in childbearing&#93; it ceased to be with Sarah after the manner of women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:6; BERESHIS 17:17; LUKAS 1:18–20"> Therefore Sarah laughed &#91;titzchak, sounds like Yitzchak&#93; within herself, saying, After I am grown old shall I have pleasure, adoni being old also?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 2:25"> And Hashem said unto Avraham, Why did Sarah laugh, saying, Shall I which am old of a surety bear a child?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 1:37; MATTITYAHU 19:26; MARKOS 10:27"> Is any thing too hard for Hashem? At the time appointed I will return unto thee, in due season, and Sarah shall have ben (son).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8; MISHLE 28:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:23"> Then Sarah denied, saying, I laughed not; for she was afraid. And He said, Oh yes but thou didst laugh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:38"> And the Anashim rose up from thence, and peered toward Sodom; and Avraham went with them to send them on the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="AMOS 3:7; TEHILLIM 25:14; YOCHANAN 15:15"> And Hashem said, Shall I hide from Avraham that thing which I do;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:8; BERESHIS 26:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:14"> Seeing that Avraham shall surely become a great and mighty nation, and kol goyei ha'aretz (all the nations of the earth) shall be blessed in him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 6:6–7; DEVARIM 4:9–10; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:4–5"> For I know him, that he will command his children and his household after him, and they shall be shomer over the Derech Hashem, to do tzedakah and mishpat; that Hashem may bring upon Avraham that which He hath spoken &#91;promised&#93; of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 19:13; YECHEZKEL 16:49–50; YESHAYAH 3:9"> And Hashem said, Because the outcry of Sodom and Amora is great, and because their sin is very grievous;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:8; BERESHIS 11:5; TZEFANYAH 1:12"> I will go down now, and see whether they have done altogether according to the crying out &#91;for punishment&#93; of it, which is come unto Me; and if not, I will have da'as.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BERESHIS 19:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:55; YECHEZKEL 22:30"> And the Men turned their faces from thence, and went toward Sodom; but Avraham stood yet before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 16:22; SHMUEL BAIS 24:17; BERESHIS 20:4"> And Avraham drew near, and said, Wilt Thou also destroy the tzaddik with the rasha?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 5:1; YESHAYAH 1:9; MATTITYAHU 7:13–14"> Suppose there be fifty tzaddikim within the city; wilt Thou also destroy and not nasa (spare)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="take away and bear the guilt of, Isa 53:12">c</a></sup>the place lema'an (for the sake of) the fifty tzaddikim that are therein?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1K 3:18">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="IYOV 8:3; YESHAYAH 3:10–11; IYOV 8:20"> That be far from Thee to do after this manner, to slay the tzaddik with the rasha; and that the tzaddik should be as the rasha, that be far from Thee: Shall not HaShofet Kol Ha'Aretz (the Judge of All the Earth) do mishpat (right)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 5:1; MATTITYAHU 24:22; YESHAYAH 65:8"> And Hashem said, If I find in Sodom fifty tzaddikim within the city, then I will spare all the place for their sakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="BERESHIS 3:19; YESHAYAH 6:5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:47–48"> And Avraham answered and said, Hinei now, I, which am but aphar and ashes, have taken upon me to speak unto Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:29; MELACHIM ALEF 20:32–33; BERESHIS 18:26"> Suppose there shall lack five of the fifty tzaddikim; wilt Thou destroy all the city for lack of five? And He said, If I find there forty and five, I will not destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:18; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:16"> And he spoke unto Him yet again, and said, Suppose there shall be forty found there. And He said, I will not do it for fortyʼs sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="TEHILLIM 89:7; TEHILLIM 9:12; ESTHER 4:11–16"> And he said unto Him, Oh let not Hashem be angry, and I will speak; suppose there shall thirty be found there. And He said, I will not do it, if I find thirty there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LUKAS 11:8; BERESHIS 18:27; LUKAS 18:1"> And he said, Hinei now, I have taken upon me to speak unto Hashem: suppose there shall be twenty found there. And He said, I will not destroy it for twentyʼs sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:15–17; SHOFETIM 6:39; MATTITYAHU 7:7"> And he said, Oh let not Hashem be angry, and I will speak yet but this once: suppose asarah (ten, i.e., a minyan) shall be found there. And He said, I will not destroy it for the sake of haasarah (the ten).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:22; BERESHIS 32:26; BERESHIS 31:55"> And Hashem went His way, as soon as He had left communing with Avraham; and Avraham returned unto his makom.</span>
</div>
