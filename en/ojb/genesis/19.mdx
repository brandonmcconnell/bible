<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:22; BERESHIS 18:1–5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:2"> And there came two malachim to Sodom at erev; and Lot sat in the sha'ar Sodom; and Lot seeing them rose up to meet them; and he bowed himself with his face toward the ground;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:4; LUKAS 24:28–29; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:2"> And he said, Hinei, adonai (my masters), turn in, now, into the bais avdechem, and tarry all night, and wash your feet, and ye shall rise up early, and go on your ways. And they said, Lo; but we will abide the night in the rekhov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:6–8; SHOFETIM 6:19; SHMUEL ALEF 28:24"> And he pressed upon them greatly; and they turned in unto him, and entered into his bais; and he made them a mishteh (meal, feast), and did bake matzot, and they did eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 13:13; MISHLE 4:16; SHEMOT 23:2"> But before they lay down, the men of the Ir, even the anshei Sodom, surrounded the bais, from na'ar to zeken, all the people from every quarter &#91;of the city&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHOFETIM 19:22; YESHAYAH 3:9; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7"> And they called unto Lot, and said unto him, Where are the anashim which came in to thee halailah (this night)? Bring them out unto us, that we may know them &#91;carnally&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHOFETIM 19:23"> And Lot went outside unto them, and shut the delet after him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:9–11; SHOFETIM 19:23; VAYIKRA 20:13"> And said, Now, achai (my brethren), do not so wickedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHOFETIM 19:24; BERESHIS 19:31–38; SHOFETIM 9:15"> Hinei now, I have two banot which have not known man; now let me bring them out unto you, and do ye to them as is good in your eyes; only unto these anashim do nothing; for therefore came they under the tzel (shadow) of my korah (beam, roof).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 2:14; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:7–8; BERESHIS 13:12"> And they said, Stand back. And they said again, This one fellow came in to sojourn as a ger (alien), and he will now play the shofet; now will we deal worse with thee, than with them. And they pressed strongly upon the man, even Lot, and came near to break down the delet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But the anashim put forth their yad, and pulled Lot into the bais to them, and shut the delet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 6:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:11; DEVARIM 28:28–29"> And they struck the men that were at the petach of the bais with blindness, both katan and gadol; so that they wearied themselves to find the petach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 7:1; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:7; HISGALUS 18:4"> And the anashim said unto Lot, Hast thou here any one else? Choson (Son-in-law), and thy banim, and thy banot, and whatsoever thou hast in the Ir, bring them out of this makom (place);</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:20; MATTITYAHU 13:49–50; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7"> For we are the mashchitim (ones destroying) this makom (place), because the cry of them is so gedolah before the face of Hashem; and Hashem hath sent us to destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 16:21; YIRMEYAH 51:6; BAMIDBAR 16:45"> And Lot went out, and spoke unto his chosonim, which were pledged to marry his banot, and said, Up, get you out of makom hazeh; for Hashem is mashchit (destroying) the Ir (City). But he seemed as one that metzachek (joked) unto his chosonim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 16:24–27; HISGALUS 18:4; BERESHIS 19:22"> And when the shachar came, then the malachim urged Lot, saying, Arise, take thy isha, and thy two banot, which are here or thou be swept away in the avon haIr.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:15–16; LETTER TO TITOS 3:5; YOCHANAN 6:44"> And while he lingered hesitating, the anashim laid hold upon his yad, and upon the yad of his isha, and upon the yad of his two banot; Hashem being merciful unto him; and they brought him out and set him outside the Ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:16–18; LUKAS 9:62; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:13–14"> And it came to pass, when they had brought them forth outside, that he said, Flee and escape for thy nefesh; look not back behind thee, neither stop thou anywhere in all the plain; flee and escape to the mountain, lest thou be swept away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:6–8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:14; BERESHIS 32:26"> And Lot said unto them, Oh, not so, Adonoi (My L-rd):</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 27:1; TEHILLIM 103:1–22; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:14–16"> Hinei now, thy eved hath found chen in Thy sight, and Thou hast magnified Thy chesed, which Thou hast showed unto me in sparing my nefesh; and I cannot flee to escape to the mountain, lest some ra'ah overtake me, and I die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:175; MISHLE 3:5–7; BERESHIS 19:30"> Hinei now, this Ir is near to run unto, and it is a little one; Oh, let me flee to escape there, (is it not mitzar &#91;small&#93;?), and my nefesh shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="TEHILLIM 145:19; TEHILLIM 34:15; TEHILLIM 102:17"> And He said unto him, Hinei, I have accepted thee concerning this thing also, that I will not overthrow the Ir, for the which thou hast spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BERESHIS 13:10; BERESHIS 14:2; BERESHIS 32:25–28"> Haste thee, flee to escape there; for I cannot do anything till thou arrive there. Therefore the shem of the Ir was called Tzoar.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="sounds like mitzar">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The shemesh rose over ha'aretz when Lot entered Tzoar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEVARIM 29:23; YESHAYAH 13:19; AMOS 4:11"> Then Hashem rained upon Sodom and upon Amora gofrit and eish from Hashem out of Shomayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="TEHILLIM 107:34; BERESHIS 14:3; BERESHIS 13:10"> And He overthrew those cities, and all the plain, and all the inhabitants of the cities, and tzemach (what grew) upon haadamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LUKAS 17:31–32; BERESHIS 19:17; MISHLE 14:14"> But his isha, from behind him, looked back, and she became a netziv melach (pillar of salt).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="CHABAKUK 2:1; TEHILLIM 5:3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:1"> And Avraham got up early in the boker to the makom where he stood there before Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="HISGALUS 18:9; HISGALUS 9:2; HISGALUS 21:8"> And he looked down toward Sodom and Amora, and toward all the eretz of the plain, and hinei, he saw kitor (smoke, steam) of the eretz go up like kitor of a furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BERESHIS 8:1; DEVARIM 9:5; BERESHIS 18:23–33"> And it came to pass, when Elohim destroyed the cities of the plain, that zikron Avraham came to Elohim, and He sent Lot out of the midst of the hafekhah (upheaval, destruction, overthrow), when He overthrew the cities in the which Lot dwelt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="BERESHIS 19:19; YESHAYAH 15:5; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8"> And Lot left Tzoar, and dwelt in the har, and his two banot with him; for he feared to dwell in Tzoar: and he dwelt in a cave, he and his two banot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEVARIM 25:5; YESHAYAH 4:1; BERESHIS 16:2"> And the bechirah said unto the younger, Avinu is old, and there is not an ish in ha'aretz to come in unto us after the derech of kol ha'aretz:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:6–7; BERESHIS 11:3; BERESHIS 9:21"> Come, let us make avinu drink yayin, and we will lie with him, that we may preserve zera by avinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="CHABAKUK 2:15–16; MISHLE 23:29–35; MISHLE 20:1"> And they made their av drink yayin balailah: and the bechirah went in, and lay with her av; and he had da'as not when she lay down, nor when she arose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 8:12; YIRMEYAH 6:15; YESHAYAH 3:9"> And it came to pass on the next day, that the bechirah said unto the younger, Hinei, I lay yesternight with avi. Let us make him drink yayin halailah also; and go thou in, and lie with him, that we may preserve zera by avinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MISHLE 24:16; TEHILLIM 8:4; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:7"> And they made their av drink yayin balailah also; and the younger arose, and lay with him; and he had da'as not when she lay down, nor when she arose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="CHABAKUK 2:15; SHOFETIM 1:7; MATTITYAHU 7:2"> Thus were both the banot of Lot with child by their av.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:9; MELACHIM BAIS 3:1–27; BAMIDBAR 21:29"> And the bechirah bore ben, and called shmo Moav; the same is the Avi Moav unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:19; SHOFETIM 10:6–40; TEHILLIM 83:4–8"> And the younger, she also bore ben, and called shmo Ben-Ammi; the same is the Avi Bnei Ammon unto this day.</span>
