<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:20; BERESHIS 4:10; TEHILLIM 50:21"> And He said, Who told thee that thou wast eirom (naked)? Hast thou eaten of HaEtz, which I commanded thee that thou not eat thereof?</span>
