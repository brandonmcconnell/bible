<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 45:10; BERESHIS 46:31; BERESHIS 46:28"> Then Yosef came and told Pharaoh, and said, Avi and my achim, and their tzon, and their bakar, and all that they have, are come out of Eretz Kena'an; and they are now in Eretz Goshen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:28; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:24"> And he took some of his achim, even five anashim, and presented them unto Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:33–34; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:10; BERESHIS 4:2"> And Pharaoh said unto his achim, What is your occupation? And they said unto Pharaoh, Thy avadim are roeh tzon, both we, and also Avoteinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:34; BERESHIS 15:13; DEVARIM 26:5"> They said moreover unto Pharaoh, For to sojourn in the land are we come; for thy avadim have no mireh (pasture) for their tzon; for the ra'av (famine) is heavy in Eretz Kena'an; now therefore, let now thy avadim dwell in Eretz Goshen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Pharaoh spoke unto Yosef, saying, Avicha and acheicha are come unto thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 18:21; MISHLE 22:29; BERESHIS 47:11"> Eretz Mitzrayim is before thee; in the best of the land make avicha and acheicha to dwell; in Eretz Goshen let them dwell; and if thou knowest any anshei chayil among them, then make them sarei mikneh (royal overseers of livestock) of mine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 47:10; SHMUEL BAIS 8:10; SHMUEL ALEF 2:20"> And Yosef brought in Ya'akov aviv, and set him before Pharaoh; and Ya'akov made on Pharaoh a brocha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Pharaoh said unto Ya'akov, How old art thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 39:12; TEHILLIM 39:5; IYOV 14:1"> And Ya'akov said unto Pharaoh, The days of the shanim of my pilgrimage are a hundred and thirty shanah; few and bad have the days of the years of my life been, and have not attained unto the days of the years of the life of my avot in the days of their pilgrimage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 47:7; TEHILLIM 129:8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:7"> And Ya'akov made a brocha on Pharaoh, and went out from the presence of Pharaoh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 47:6; SHEMOT 1:11; SHEMOT 12:37"> And Yosef settled aviv and his achim, and gave them an achuzzah (possession) in Eretz Mitzrayim, in the best of the land, in Eretz Ra'meses, just as Pharaoh had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 45:11; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:8; BERESHIS 47:1"> And Yosef provided for aviv, and his achim, and kol bais aviv, with lechem, according to the children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:11; MELACHIM ALEF 18:5; EKHAH 4:9"> And there was no lechem in kol ha'aretz; for the ra'av was very heavy, so that Eretz Mitzrayim and kol Eretz Kena'an fainted by reason of the ra'av (famine).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:56; LUKAS 16:10–12; LUKAS 16:1–2"> And Yosef gathered up all the kesef that was found in the Eretz Mitzrayim, and in Eretz Kena'an, for the shever (grain) which they bought; and Yosef brought the kesef into Bais Pharaoh (Pharaohʼs palace).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHOFETIM 8:8; TEHILLIM 37:3; MATTITYAHU 6:11"> And when kesef failed in Eretz Mitzrayim, and in Eretz Kena'an, kol Mitzrayim came unto Yosef, and said, Give us lechem; for why should we die in thy presence? For the kesef is gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:5; DANIEL 6:5–7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:32"> And Yosef said, Give your mikneh; and I will give you for your mikneh, if kesef is gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="IYOV 2:4; SHEMOT 9:3; YESHAYAH 31:1"> And they brought their mikneh unto Yosef; and Yosef gave them lechem in exchange for susim, and for the tzon, and for the mikneh of the bakar, and for the chamorim; and he fed them lechem for all their mikneh for that year.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 6:26; YIRMEYAH 38:9"> When that year was ended, they came unto him the shanah hashenit, and said unto him, We will not hide it from adoni, how that our kesef is spent; adoni also hath our mikneh of behemah; there is not ought left in the sight of adoni, but geviyyatenu (our bodies), and adematenu (our lands);</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 5:2–3; IYOV 2:4; EKHAH 1:11"> Why shall we die before thine eyes, both we and adematenu? Buy us and adematenu for lechem, and we and adematenu will be avadim unto Pharaoh; and give us zera, that we may live, and not die, that the adamah be not desolate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Yosef bought kol Admat Mitzrayim for Pharaoh; for the Mitzrayim sold every ish his sadeh, because the ra'av (famine) was chazak over them; so the land became Pharaohʼs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:48"> And as for the people, he removed them to cities from one end of the borders of Mitzrayim even to the other end thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZRA 7:24; NECHEMYAH 13:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:10"> Only the admat hakohanim bought he not; for the kohanim had a chok assigned them of Pharaoh, and did eat their chok which Pharaoh gave them; therefore they sold not their lands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="TEHILLIM 112:5; TEHILLIM 41:1; KOHELET 11:6"> Then Yosef said unto the people, See, I have bought you this day and your land for Pharaoh: lo, here is zera for you, and ye shall sow the adamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:34; TEHILLIM 41:1; TEHILLIM 112:5"> And it shall come to pass in the tevu'ot, that ye shall give the fifth part unto Pharaoh, and four parts shall be your own, for zera of the sadeh, and for your ochel, and for them of your batim, and for food for your children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BERESHIS 33:15; MISHLE 11:26–27; BERESHIS 18:3"> And they said, Thou hast saved our lives; let us find chen (grace, favor) in the sight of adoni, and we will be avadim to Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="BERESHIS 47:22; YECHEZKEL 7:24"> And Yosef made it a chok over Eretz Mitzrayim unto this day, that Pharaoh should have the fifth part, except the admat hakohanim only, which became not Pharaohʼs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:3; SHEMOT 1:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:17"> And Yisroel settled in Eretz Mitzrayim, in Eretz Goshen; and they acquired property therein, and grew, and multiplied exceedingly.</span>
</div>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEVARIM 31:14; BERESHIS 24:49; BERESHIS 24:2"> And the time drew near that Yisroel must die; and he called bno Yosef, and said unto him, If now I have found chen in thy sight, put, now, thy yad under my thigh, and do chesed and emes with me; bury me not, now, in Mitzrayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="BERESHIS 49:29–32; BERESHIS 23:19; BERESHIS 25:9"> But I will lie with my avot, and thou shalt carry me out of Mitzrayim, and bury me in their kever. And he said, I will do as thou hast said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:47; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:21; BERESHIS 24:3"> And he said, Swear unto me. And he swore unto him. And Yisroel prostrated toward the rosh hamittah.</span>
