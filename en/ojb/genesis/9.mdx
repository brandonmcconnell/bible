<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 9:7; BERESHIS 1:28; BERESHIS 8:17"> Vayevarech Elohim et Noach and his banim, and said unto them, Be fruitful, and multiply, and replenish ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="TEHILLIM 8:4–8; BERESHIS 1:28; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:7"> And the fear of you and the dread of you shall be upon every beast of ha'aretz, and upon every bird of the air, upon all that moveth upon ha'adamah, and upon all the dagim of the yam; into your yad are they delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:21–22; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:31"> Every remes that liveth shall be food for you; even as the yarok esev (green plant) have I given you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:23; DEVARIM 15:23; DEVARIM 12:16"> But basar with the nefesh thereof, which is the dahm thereof, shall ye not eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 21:28–29; SHEMOT 21:12; BERESHIS 4:9–10"> And surely your dahm of your nefeshot will I require; at the hand of every beast will I require it, and at the yad haAdam; at the hand of every manʼs brother will I require the nefesh haAdam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="VAYIKRA 24:17; MATTITYAHU 26:52; HISGALUS 13:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Shofech dahm (one who sheddeth manʼs blood),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">by man shall his dahm be shed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for in the tzelem Elohim</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">made He man.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 1:28; BERESHIS 9:1; BERESHIS 9:19"> And you, be ye fruitful, and multiply; bring forth abundantly on ha'aretz, and multiply therein.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Elohim spoke unto Noach, and to his banim with him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 6:18; YESHAYAH 54:9–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:3"> And I, hineni, I establish My brit (covenant) with you, and with your zera after you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 8:1; BERESHIS 9:15–16; TEHILLIM 36:5–6"> And with kol nefesh that is with you, of the bird, of the behemah, and of every animal of ha'aretz with you; from kol yotzei hatevah (all that go out of the ark), to every creature of ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 54:9; BERESHIS 8:21–22; BERESHIS 7:21–23"> And I will establish My brit (covenant) with you, neither shall kol basar yikaret (be cut off, violently killed) any more by the waters of the mabbul; neither shall there be any more mabbul l'shacheit (to destroy) ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:11; MATTITYAHU 26:26–28; SHEMOT 12:13"> And Elohim said, This is the ot (sign) of the brit (covenant) which I make between Me and you and every nefesh chayyah (living creature) that is with you, for dorot olam (perpetual generations);</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 1:28; HISGALUS 4:3; HISGALUS 10:1"> I do set My keshet (bow) in the anan (cloud), and it shall be for an ot brit between Me and ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And it shall come to pass, when I bring an anan over ha'aretz, that the keshet be'anan (rainbow) shall be seen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:9; YECHEZKEL 16:60; YESHAYAH 54:8–10"> And I will remember My brit, which is between Me and you and kol nefesh chayyah of kol basar; and the waters shall no more become a mabbul (flood) l'shacheit (to destroy) kol basar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YESHAYAH 54:8–10; YIRMEYAH 32:40; SHMUEL BAIS 23:5"> And the keshet shall be in the anan; and I will look upon it, that I may remember the brit olam between Elohim and kol nefesh chayyah of kol basar that is upon ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Elohim said unto Noach, This is the ot habrit, which I have established between Me and kol basar that is upon ha'aretz.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BERESHIS 10:1; BERESHIS 10:6; BERESHIS 9:25–27"> And the bnei Noach, that went forth of the tevah, were Shem, and Cham, and Yephet; and Cham is the av of Kena'an (Canaan).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 1:4–28; BERESHIS 8:17; BERESHIS 5:32"> Shloshah (Three) are the bnei Noach; and of them was the kol ha'aretz populated.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEVARIM 20:6; MISHLE 10:11; YESHAYAH 28:24–26"> And Noach began to be an ish ha'adamah (a man of the soil), and he planted a kerem (vineyard);</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MISHLE 20:1; CHABAKUK 2:15–16; MISHLE 23:31–32"> And he drank of the yayin, and became shikker (intoxicated); and he lay uncovered inside his ohel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MISHLE 30:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:1; MATTITYAHU 18:15"> And Cham, the av of Kena'an, saw the erom (nakedness) of his av, and told his two achim on the outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:1; SHEMOT 20:12; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:17"> And Shem and Yephet took a garment, and laid it upon both their shoulders, and walked backward, and covered the erom of their av; and their faces were turned the other way, and they saw not the erom of their av.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And Noach awoke from his yayin, and had da'as of what his ben katan had done unto him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 9:23; DEVARIM 27:16; SHOFETIM 1:28–30"> And he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Arur (cursed) be Kena'an;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">an eved of avadim shall he be unto his achim.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="TEHILLIM 144:15; BERESHIS 12:1–3; LUKAS 3:23–36"> And he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Baruch Hashem Elohei Shem;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Kena'an shall be his eved.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Elohim shall enlarge Yephet,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he shall dwell in the tents of Shem;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Kena'an shall be his eved.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Noach lived after the mabbul three hundred and fifty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BERESHIS 5:27; TEHILLIM 90:10; BERESHIS 5:32"> And all the days of Noach were nine hundred and fifty years; and he died.</span>
