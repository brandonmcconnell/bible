<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="TEHILLIM 12:6; BERESHIS 18:10; BERESHIS 18:14"> And Hashem visited Sarah as He had said, and Hashem did unto Sarah just as He had spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:22; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:11; BERESHIS 18:10"> For Sarah conceived, and bore Avraham ben in his old age, at the mo'ed (set time) of which Elohim had given promise unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:7; BERESHIS 21:6"> And Avraham called the shem of bno that was born unto him, whom Sarah bore to him, Yitzchak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:8; DEVARIM 12:32; YOCHANAN 7:22–23"> And Avraham circumcised bno Yitzchak at age shemonat yamim, as Elohim had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:19; BERESHIS 17:1"> And Avraham was a hundred years old, when bno Yitzchak was born unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 126:2; YESHAYAH 54:1; BERESHIS 17:17"> And she said, Elohim hath brought me tzechok (laughter), so that all that hear yitzchak (will laugh) with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:11–12; YESHAYAH 66:8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:10"> And she said, Who would have said unto Avraham, that Sarah should nurse banim? for I have borne him ben in his old age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 1:22; HOSHEA 1:8; BERESHIS 40:20"> And the yeled grew, and was weaned; and Avraham made a mishteh gadol the same day that Yitzchak was weaned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:29; BERESHIS 16:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:22"> And Sarah saw the ben of Hagar the Egyptian, which she had borne unto Avraham, metzachek (mocking, scoffing).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:35; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:22–31"> Therefore she said unto Avraham, Cast out this bondwoman and her ben: for the ben of this bondwoman shall not be heir with beni (my son), even with Yitzchak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:11; BERESHIS 17:18; MATTITYAHU 10:37"> And the thing was very grievous in the eyes of Avraham because of bno.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:7–8; BERESHIS 17:19"> And Elohim said unto Avraham, Let it not be grievous in thy sight because of the na'ar, and because of thy bondwoman; in all that Sarah hath said unto thee, shema (pay heed) unto her voice; for in Yitzchak shall thy zera be called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 16:10; BERESHIS 17:20; BERESHIS 21:18"> And also of the ben haamah will I make a nation, because he is thy zera.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="KOHELET 9:10; TEHILLIM 119:60; BERESHIS 16:7"> And Avraham rose up early in the boker, and took lechem, and a skin of mayim, and gave it unto Hagar, putting it on her shoulder, and the yeled, and sent her away; and she departed, and wandered in the midbar of Be'er-Sheva.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 3:9; TEHILLIM 63:1; YIRMEYAH 14:3"> And the mayim in the skin was done, and she cast the yeled under one of the bushes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YESHAYAH 49:15; BERESHIS 29:11; SHMUEL ALEF 24:16"> And she went off, and sat down about a bowshot away: for she thought, Let me not see the mot hayeled. And she sat there nearby, and lifted up her voice, and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 16:11; TEHILLIM 50:15; SHEMOT 3:7"> And Elohim heard the voice of the na'ar; and the Malach Elohim called to Hagar out of Shomayim, and said unto her, Mah lach, Hagar? fear not; for Elohim hath heard the voice of the na'ar where he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BERESHIS 21:13; BERESHIS 16:10; BERESHIS 17:20"> Arise, lift up the na'ar, and hold him in thine yad; for I will make him a goy gadol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 22:31; MELACHIM BAIS 6:17–20; YESHAYAH 35:5–6"> And Elohim opened her eyes, and she saw a be'er of mayim; and she went, and filled the skin with mayim, and gave the na'ar drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:15; BERESHIS 39:21; BERESHIS 16:12"> And Elohim was with the na'ar; and he grew, and dwelt in the midbar, and became a roveh keshet (an archer).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 26:34–35; BERESHIS 24:3–4; BAMIDBAR 12:16"> And he dwelt in the midbar of Paran: and his em got him an isha out of Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 3:7; BERESHIS 26:26; BERESHIS 26:28"> And it came to pass at that time, that Avimelech and Phichol the sar tz'va of his spoke unto Avraham, saying, Elohim is with thee in all that thou doest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 2:12; BERESHIS 20:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:16"> Now therefore swear unto me here before Elohim that thou wilt not deal falsely with me, nor with my child, nor with my descendant: but according to the chesed that I have showed thee, thou shalt do unto me, and to the eretz wherein thou hast sojourned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:16; BERESHIS 14:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:18"> And Avraham said, I will swear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BERESHIS 26:15–22; BERESHIS 29:8; MISHLE 25:9"> And Avraham reproved Avimelech because of a be'er hamayim, which Avimelechʼs avadim had violently seized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="BERESHIS 13:7; MELACHIM BAIS 5:20–24"> And Avimelech said, I know not who hath done this thing; neither didst thou tell me, neither yet heard I of it, but today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="BERESHIS 14:22–23; YESHAYAH 32:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:31"> And Avraham took tzon and oxen, and gave them unto Avimelech; and both of them made a brit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Avraham set apart seven ewe lambs of the tzon by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:14; BERESHIS 33:8; SHEMOT 12:26"> And Avimelech said unto Avraham, What mean these seven ewe lambs which thou hast set apart by themselves?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="BERESHIS 31:52; YEHOSHUA 24:27; YEHOSHUA 22:27–28"> And he said, For these sheva ewe lambs shalt thou accept of my hand, that they may be a witness unto me, that I have dug this well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="BERESHIS 26:33; BERESHIS 21:14; MELACHIM ALEF 4:25"> Therefore he called that place Be'er-Sheva; because there they swore an oath both of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHEMOT 13:17; BERESHIS 21:27; SHOFETIM 13:1"> Thus they made a brit at Be'er-Sheva: then Avimelech rose up, and Phichol the sar tzeva of his, and they returned into eretz Pelishtim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:17; YESHAYAH 40:28; TEHILLIM 90:2"> And Avraham planted an eshel (tamarisk tree) in Be'er-Sheva, and called there on the Shem of Hashem El Olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:15; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:9"> And Avraham sojourned in eretz Pelishtim yamim rabbim.</span>
