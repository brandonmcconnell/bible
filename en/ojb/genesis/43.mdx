<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:6–8; MISHLE 16:18; MISHLE 15:16"> And it came to pass, when they had eaten up the shever (grain) which they had brought out of Mitzrayim, then Avihem said unto them, Shuvu (return)! Buy for us a little ochel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 44:23; BERESHIS 43:5; SHMUEL BAIS 3:13"> And Yehudah spoke unto him, saying, The ish did solemnly warn us, saying, Ye shall not see my face, unless achichem be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> If thou wilt send achinu (our brother) with us, we will go down and buy thee ochel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:12; BERESHIS 44:26; BERESHIS 42:38"> But if thou wilt not send him, we will not go down; for the ish said unto us, Ye shall not see my face, unless achichem be with you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Yisroel said, Why dealt ye so ill with me, as to tell the ish whether ye had yet another ach (brother)?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 43:3; BERESHIS 42:13; BERESHIS 43:27"> And they said, The ish asked in detail about us and about our moledet (kindred), saying, Is Avichem yet alive? Have ye another ach? And we declared to him according to these words of &#91;his inquiry&#93;; could we certainly know that he would say, Bring down achichem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:2; DEVARIM 33:6; BERESHIS 45:19"> And Yehudah said unto Yisroel Aviv, Send na'ar with me, and we will arise and go; that we may live, and not die, both we, and thou, and also our little ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:37; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:18–19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:22"> I will be surety for him; of my yad shalt thou require an accounting for him; if I bring him not back unto thee, and set him before thee, then let me bear the blame kol hayamim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 19:16"> For if we had not delayed, surely now we had returned zeh pa'amayim (this second time).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:25; YIRMEYAH 8:22; YECHEZKEL 27:17"> And Yisroel Avihem said unto them, If it must be so now, do this; take of mizimerat HaAretz (the best fruits of the land) in your kelim (vessels), and carry down to the ish a minchah (present), a little balm, and a little devash (honey), nekhot (spices), loht (myrrh), batenim (pistachio nuts), and shekedim (almonds):</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:25; BERESHIS 42:35; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:6"> And take kesef mishneh (double money) in your yad; and the kesef that was returned in the mouth of your sacks, carry it back in your yad; perhaps it was mishgeh (mistake);</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:38"> Take also achichem, and arise, shuvu (return) unto the ish;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ESTHER 4:16; BERESHIS 17:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:14"> And El Shaddai give you rachamim before the ish, that he may send away achichem acher (your other brother), and Binyamin. If I be bereaved of my children, I am bereaved.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the anashim took that minchah, and they took mishneh kesef (double money) in their yad and Binyamin; and rose up, and went down to Mitzrayim, and stood before Yosef.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 44:1; BERESHIS 43:19; BERESHIS 24:2–10"> And when Yosef saw Binyamin with them, he said to the ish over Bais Yosef, Bring these anashim home, and slaughter the tevach, and make ready; for these anashim shall dine with me at tzahorayim (noon).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the ish did as Yosef said; and the ish brought the anashim to the house of Yosef.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:35; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:8; BERESHIS 42:21"> And the anashim were afraid, because they were brought into Bais Yosef; and they said, Because of the kesef that was returned in our sacks at the first time are we brought in; that he may seek occasion against us, and fall upon us, and seize us for avadim, and chamoreinu (our donkeys).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And they came near to the ish over Bais Yosef, and they spoke with him at the petach habayit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:3; BERESHIS 42:10; BERESHIS 42:27"> And said, O adoni, we came indeed down at the first time to buy ochel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 43:12; BERESHIS 42:27–35; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:8"> And it came to pass, when we came to the malon, that we opened our sacks, and, hinei, kesef ish (every manʼs money) was in the mouth of his sack, our kesef in full weight; and we have brought it back again in our yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And kesef acher (other money) have we brought down in our hands to buy ochel; we cannot tell who put our kesef in our sacks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:26; BERESHIS 42:24; YOCHANAN 14:27"> And he said, Shalom lachem, fear not; Eloheichem, and Elohei Avichem, hath given you matmon (treasure) in your sacks; I had your kesef. And he brought Shim'on out unto them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:4; BERESHIS 24:32; LUKAS 7:44"> And the ish brought the anashim into the house of Yosef, and gave them mayim, and they washed their raglayim; and he gave their chamorim mispo (fodder).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BERESHIS 43:11; BERESHIS 43:16"> And they made ready the minchah for bo Yosef (coming of Yosef) at tzahorayim; for they heard that they would eat lechem there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:10–11; BERESHIS 27:29; BERESHIS 42:6"> And when Yosef came home, they presented him the minchah which was in their yad in the house, and prostrated themselves to the ground before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:13; SHMUEL ALEF 25:5; SHMUEL ALEF 17:22"> And he asked them l'shalom (about their welfare), and said, Is there shalom with avichem, the zaken of whom ye spoke? Is he still alive?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:7; BERESHIS 43:26; SHMUEL BAIS 14:4"> And they answered, There is shalom with thy eved avinu; he is still alive. And they bowed their heads, and prostrated themselves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:13; BAMIDBAR 6:25; BERESHIS 30:22–24"> And he lifted up his eyes, and saw achiv Binyamin, ben immo, and said, Is this achichem hakaton (your younger brother), of whom ye spoke unto me? And he said, Elohim be gracious unto thee, beni.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:24; YIRMEYAH 31:20; MELACHIM ALEF 3:26"> And Yosef made haste; for his compassion was stirred upon achiv (his brother); and he sought where to weep; and he entered into his cheder, and wept there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="BERESHIS 45:1; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:10; YESHAYAH 42:14"> And he washed his face, and went out, and controlled himself, and said, Serve lechem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:34; SHEMOT 8:26; BERESHIS 43:16"> And they served him by himself, and them by themselves, and the Mitzrim (Egyptians), which did eat with him, by themselves; because Mitzrim could not eat lechem with the Ivrim; for that is a to'evah (abomination) unto the Mitzrim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And they sat before him, the bechor according to his bechorah (birthright), and the youngest according to his youth; and the anashim marvelled one with another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="BERESHIS 45:22; SHMUEL BAIS 11:8; SHMUEL ALEF 1:5"> And he took and sent masot (portions) unto them from before him; but masat Binyamin was five times as much as any of theirs. And they drank, and feasted with him.</span>
</div>
