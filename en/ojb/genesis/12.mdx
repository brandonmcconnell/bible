## &#91;LEKH LEKHA&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:8; NECHEMYAH 9:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:2–6"> Now Hashem had said unto Avram, Get thee out of thy country, and from thy kindred, and from the bais avicha, unto ha'aretz that I will show thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 7:9; BERESHIS 18:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:14"> So that I will make of thee a goy gadol (great nation), so that I will bless thee, and make thy shem great; and so thou shalt be a brocha (blessing);</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:8; BERESHIS 22:18; BERESHIS 27:29"> And I will bless the one blessing you, and curse him that curses you; and kol mishpochot haadamah shall be blessed through you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="There is a brocha in this verse that many do not know but that every true follower of Moshiach should know.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:8; BERESHIS 11:31; BERESHIS 11:27"> So Avram departed as Hashem had told him; and Lot went with him; and Avram was seventy and five years old when he departed out of Charan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BERESHIS 14:14; BERESHIS 11:31; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:8–9"> And Avram took Sarai his isha, and Lot his brotherʼs son, and all their possessions that they had amassed, and the nefesh that they had gotten in Charan; and they went forth to go into the land of Kena'an; and they arrived in the land of Kena'an.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:9; DEVARIM 11:30; BERESHIS 35:4"> And Avram passed through the land unto the makom Shechem, unto Elon Moreh. And the Kena'ani was then in ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:8; BERESHIS 18:1; BERESHIS 13:15"> And Hashem appeared unto Avram, and said, Unto thy zera will I give ha'aretz hazot: and there built he a mizbe'ach unto Hashem, Who appeared unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 11:31; BERESHIS 4:26; BERESHIS 21:33"> And he removed from there unto the harah mikedem Beit-El, and pitched his tent, having Beit-El on the west, and Ai on the east; and there he built a Mizbe'ach unto Hashem, and called upon the Shem of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 13:3; BERESHIS 24:62; BERESHIS 13:1"> And Avram journeyed, going on still toward the Negev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 43:1; BERESHIS 26:1–3; MELACHIM ALEF 17:1–18"> And there was a ra'av in the land; and Avram went down into Mitzrayim to sojourn there; for the ra'av was severe in ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 26:7; BERESHIS 29:17; BERESHIS 12:14"> And it came to pass, when he was about to enter into Mitzrayim, that he said unto Sarai his isha, Hinei now, I know that thou art an isha yafeh to look upon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 20:11; BERESHIS 26:7; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8–10"> Therefore it shall come to pass, when the Egyptians shall see thee, that they shall say, This is his isha; and they will kill me, but they will save thee alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 26:7; BERESHIS 20:12–13; BERESHIS 20:2"> Say, now, thou art my achot; that it may be well with me for thy sake; and my nefesh shall live on account of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 6:2; MATTITYAHU 5:28; BERESHIS 39:7"> And it came to pass, that, when Avram was come into Mitzrayim, the Egyptians beheld the isha that she was yafeh me'od.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 20:2; BERESHIS 41:1; MELACHIM BAIS 18:21"> The sarim of Pharaoh also saw her, and commended her before Pharaoh; and the isha was taken into Bais Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 20:14; BERESHIS 13:2; BERESHIS 24:35"> And he treated Avram well for her sake: and he acquired sheep and oxen, and male donkeys, and avadim, and shfachot, and female donkeys, and camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 16:21; BERESHIS 20:18; TEHILLIM 105:14–15"> And Hashem plagued Pharaoh and his Bais with nega'im gedolim because of Sarai eshet Avram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BERESHIS 20:9–10; BERESHIS 26:9–11; SHMUEL ALEF 14:43"> And Pharaoh summoned Avram and said, What is this that thou hast done unto me? Why didst thou not tell me that she was thy isha?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Why saidst thou, She is my achot? So I might have taken her to me to wife; now therefore, hinei, thy wife, take her, and go!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 105:14–15; MISHLE 21:1; SHEMOT 18:27"> And Pharaoh commanded his anashim concerning him; and they sent him away, and his isha, and all that he had.</span>
