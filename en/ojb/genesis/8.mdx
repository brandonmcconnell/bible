<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 30:22; BERESHIS 19:29; TEHILLIM 136:23"> And Elohim remembered Noach, and every living thing, and all the behemah that was with him in the tevah (ark); and Elohim made a ruach to pass over ha'aretz, and the waters subsided;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 7:11; MATTITYAHU 8:26–27; MISHLE 8:28"> The ma'ayanot (springs) also of the tehom and the floodgates of Shomayim were stopped, and the geshem from Shomayim was restrained;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 7:24; BERESHIS 7:11"> And the waters receded from on ha'aretz continually; and after the end of the hundred and fifty days the waters were abated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 19:37; YIRMEYAH 51:27; YESHAYAH 37:38"> And the tevah (ark) rested in the seventh month, on the seventeenth day of the month, upon the mountains of Ararat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BERESHIS 7:11"> And the waters decreased continually until the tenth month; in the tenth month, on the first day of the month, were the rashei heharim seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 6:16; DANIEL 6:10"> And it came to pass at the end of arba'im yom, that Noach opened the chalon (window) of the tevah (ark) which he had made;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 147:9; MELACHIM ALEF 17:6; MELACHIM ALEF 17:4"> And he sent forth the orev (raven), which went forth to and fro, until the waters were dried up from off ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHIR HASHIRIM 2:14; SHIR HASHIRIM 1:15; MATTITYAHU 10:16"> Also he sent forth a yonah (dove) from him, to see if the waters were abated from off the face of the adamah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:28; TEHILLIM 116:7; DEVARIM 28:65"> But the yonah found no manoach (place of rest) for the sole of her foot, and she returned unto him into the tevah, for the waters were on the p'nei kol ha'aretz; then he reached forth his yad, and took her, and pulled her in unto him into the tevah (ark).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 7:4; BERESHIS 7:10; BERESHIS 8:12"> And he waited yet another shivat yamim; and again he sent forth the yonah out of the tevah (ark);</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 8:15; ZECHARYAH 4:12–14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:15"> And the yonah returned to him in the erev; and, hinei, in her beak was a zayit (olive) leaf freshly plucked; so Noach had da'as that the mayim were abated from off ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 25:9; YESHAYAH 30:18; BERESHIS 8:10"> And he waited yet another shivat yamim; and sent forth the yonah; which returned not unto him again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 7:11"> And it came to pass in the six hundredth and first year, in the first month, the first day of the month, the mayim were dried up from off ha'aretz; and Noach removed the mikhseh (covering) of the tevah (ark), and looked, and, hinei, the surface of the adamah was dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 7:13–14; BERESHIS 7:11"> And in the second month, on the seven and twentieth day of the month, was ha'aretz dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Elohim spoke unto Noach, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 7:13; TEHILLIM 121:8; TEHILLIM 91:11"> Go forth of the tevah (ark), thou, and thy isha, and thy banim, and the nashim of thy banim with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 1:22; BERESHIS 9:1; YIRMEYAH 31:27–28"> Bring forth with thee every living thing that is with thee, of kol basar, both of bird, and of behemah, and of every creeping thing that creepeth upon ha'aretz; that they may multiply abundantly on ha'aretz, and be fruitful, and increase upon ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="TEHILLIM 121:8"> And Noach went forth, and his banim, and his isha, and the nashim of his banim with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Every animal, every remes, and every bird, and whatsoever creepeth upon ha'aretz, after their kinds, went forth out of the tevah (ark).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:15–16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:1; BERESHIS 22:9"> And Noach built a Mizbe'ach unto Hashem; and took of every behemah hatehorah, and of kol haoph hatahor, and offered olot (burnt offerings) on the Mizbe'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:2; YESHAYAH 54:9–10"> And Hashem smelled a re'ach hannichoach; and Hashem said in His lev, I will not again curse the adamah any more ba'avur (because of) haAdam; for the yetzer of the lev haAdam is rah from his ne'urim (youth, childhood); neither will I again strike any more every thing living, as I have done.</span>
