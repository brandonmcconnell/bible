<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–17; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:1"> Place your machshavot (thoughts) above (in Shomayim), not on the things of the Olam Hazeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:20"> For you died and the Chayyim of you has become nistar<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Co 2:3">b</a></sup>(hidden) with Moshiach in Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:25; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:20"> When Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach who is Chayyeinu (our Life) is made manifest in hisgalus (revelation), then also you with Him will be made manifest in hisgalus in kavod.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:19–21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:24"> Therefore, put to death your evarim (members) in the Olam Hazeh: zenut (fornication), debauchery, sensuality, zimmah (licentiousness), and chamdanut (covetousness), which is avodah zarah (idolatry).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:18; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:14"> Because of such things as these the charon af Hashem habah (the coming burning wrath of Hashem) is on the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:2; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:3–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:19–20"> Such was your derech to fier zich (comport oneself), the derech resha'im formerly,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 1:6">c</a></sup>when you were living in these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:22; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:20–21"> But now you also put away all these things: ka'as (anger), rage, kavvanah ra'ah (malice), and chillul Hashem. Put away loshon hora from your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:25; VAYIKRA 19:11"> Do not speak sheker to one another, having disrobed from the Adam hakadmoni (old<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="unregenerated">d</a></sup>humanity) with his ma'asim (works),</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:23–24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:10"> And instead enrobe with the Adam HaChadash, the one being renewed in da'as in accordance with the demut<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1:15; Gn 1:26-27; Pp 2:6">e</a></sup>of the One having created him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:28–29; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:12"> Here there is neither Yevani nor Yehudi, there is neither those who are or are not of the Bris Milah, there is no uncultured non-Greek speaker, there is no Scythian, eved, or Ben Chorin, but rather Moshiach is all in all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:32; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:2; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:19"> Therefore, as Bechirim (Chosen ones) of Hashem, Kedoshim and ahuvim, enrobe yourselves in tender feelings of rachmei Shomayim (heavenly compassion, mercy), chesed (lovingkindness), anavah (humility), shiflut (lowliness), and savlanut (longsuffering),</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:32; MARKOS 11:25; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13"> Being soivel (bearing with) one another and extending selicha (forgiveness) to each other, if it should be that one is murmuring his complaint against another; just as Adoneinu extended selicha to you, so also you should extend selicha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:1–13"> And to all these things add ahavah, which is the agudah of tamimim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:6–7; YOCHANAN 14:27; YESHAYAH 26:3"> And let the shalom of Moshiach arbitrate in your levavot. You were called to this shalom in one GUF.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Dt 21:23; cf geviyah, Gn 47:18; cf basar, Ps 16:9-10; Job 19:25-27; Isa 53:11">f</a></sup>Let there be todah in your levavot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:19; YOCHANAN 15:7"> Let the dvar of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach make its mishkan in you richly, in all chochmah and in all Moshiachʼs pnimiyus haTorah (innermost Torah teaching) and words of chizzuk, ministering to one another with Tehillim, shirim, and neshamah niggunim miRuach Hakodesh (soul melodies from the Ruach Hakodesh) with gratitude, singing in your levavot to Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ps 47:7">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:31; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:23; MISHLE 3:6"> And whatever thing you do, whether in dvar or in ma'aseh, do all things b'Shem Adoneinu Yehoshua, giving todot to Elohim HaAv through him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Moshiach">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:22–9; LETTER TO TITOS 2:4–5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:3"> Nashim (wives), make yourselves accountable to your ba'alim (husbands), as is proper in Adoneinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:7; BERESHIS 2:23–24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:21"> Ba'alim, have ahavah for your nashim and do not be bitter against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:1–3; MISHLE 6:20"> Yeladim, obey your horim (parents) in all things, for this is well pleasing in Adoneinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MISHLE 4:1–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:4; TEHILLIM 103:13"> Avot, do not provoke your yeladim, lest they be disheartened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:5–7; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:1–2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:10"> Avadim (servants), each of you obey in all things your adon according to the basar, not with eye service as pleasers of Bnei Adam, but with tom lev, with yirat Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:6–7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:8"> Whatever you do, be po'alim (workers) who with their neshamah work facing Adoneinu and not Bnei Adam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:32"> Having da'as that from Adoneinu you will receive the sachar of the nachalah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Co 1:12; Ps 16:5-6">i</a></sup>(alloted inheritance). Serve Adoneinu Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:34; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:10"> For the one doing wrong will be repaid for his wrong, ki ein masso panim im Hashem (for there is no respect of persons with G-d, no partiality).</span>
