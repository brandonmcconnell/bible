<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:22"> That their levavot may be given chozek (strength), having been made an aguda of ahavah and all osher (wealth) of the full assurance of binah, resulting in the da'as of the raz of Hashem, namely Moshiach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:30; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:33"> In whom is nistar (hidden) all the otzarot (treasures) of chochmah and da'as.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Isa 11:2; Jer 23:5">a</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:3–4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:13"> For, though absent in basar, but present with you in the Ruach Hakodesh, with lev same'ach (glad heart) I am seeing your order and the firmness of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Orthodox Jewish">b</a></sup>emunah in Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:10; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:1"> Therefore, as you became mekabel Moshiach and received Moshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua, so let your halichah be in Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:17; YIRMEYAH 17:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:20–22"> Having been rooted and built up in Moshiach and being firmly founded in the emunah as you were given Messianic<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="orthodox">c</a></sup>lernen (study), abounding in hodayah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:6; MATTITYAHU 7:15"> Beware lest there be anyone of you taken captive through philosophical tevunah (wisdom) and empty deceit according to the kabbalah (oral tradition) of mere Bnei Adam, according to the ikkarim (basic principles) of the Olam Hazeh and not according to Moshiach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:19; YOCHANAN 10:30; YOCHANAN 1:14"> Because in Moshiach kol melo Elohim (all the plentitude of G-d) finds its bodily maon laShechinah (dwelling place for the Shekhinah glory, in the hitgashmut).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:16–18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:20–23"> And you have been granted melo (plentitude) in Moshiach, who is the Rosh of all rule and authority.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:6"> In Moshiach also you were circumcised with a Bris Milah not made with human hands, a Bris Milah of the surgically removing of the body of the basar (old fallen and unregenerate nature) in the Bris Milah of Moshiach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:3–5; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:27"> Having been buried in the Ohel of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiachʼs tevilah with him, with whom also you were made to stand up spiritually in the Techiyah together through your emunah and bitachon in the ma'aseh of Hashem who made Moshiach stand up from the Mesim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:5–6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:1"> And you being dead in your peysha'im<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 53:8">d</a></sup>and your orlat basar (uncircumcision of flesh) he made alive together, you together with Moshiach, having granted you selicha for all your peysha'im;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YESHAYAH 44:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:14–16; YESHAYAH 43:25"> Having erased the hand-signed sefer of guilt choiv (debt), the heavenly indictment against us in the maleh chukat haTorah (full statute requirement of the Torah), which was against us. Moshiach has done away with this opposing record, having nailed it to Moshiachʼs Etz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:8; MATTITYAHU 12:29"> Having disarmed the rulers and the authorities, He made a public spectacle of them, having triumphed over them by Moshiachʼs Etz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:5–6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:10"> Therefore, let no one judge you &#91;Goyim&#93; in eating and in drinking or in respect to a yom tov (yontev) or a rosh chodesh or Shabbos;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:1; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:5; YOCHANAN 1:17"> Which are a shadow of the things to come in the Olam HaBah; but the reality, the substance, is Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:8; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1–2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:23"> Let no one deprive you of the prize by delighting in his mystical asceticism and his veneration of malachim and delving into his chazonot (visions) and being vainly puffed up by the machshavot (thoughts) of his basar (old nature unrenewed and unregenerated by the Ruach Hakodesh),</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:15–16; YOCHANAN 15:4–6; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18"> And not holding on to the Rosh, out of whom all the Moshiachʼs Guf, being fully supplied and being be'ichud (united) together through the joints and ligaments, grows with the growth of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:3"> Als (Since) you died with Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach to the ikkarim (basic principles) of the Olam Hazeh, why, as though you still belonged to the keyam (existence) of the Olam Hazeh, do you (Goyim) chain yourself to chumra legalisms?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:3; BERESHIS 3:3; YESHAYAH 52:11"> “Do not touch; do not taste; do not handle.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YESHAYAH 29:13; LETTER TO TITOS 1:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:13"> The things referred to are all destined to deterioration with use, according to the humanly contrived mitzvot and the humanly contrived chukim (laws) of mere Bnei Adam,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="i.e., non-Biblical teachings and humanly contrived religious tradition — Isa 29:13">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:8"> Which things have a superficial appearance of chochmah in self-imposed religion and mystical asceticism and severe physical mortification, but are of no value against the indulgence of the yetzer (the so-called basar unrenewed and unregenerated by the Ruach Hakodesh).</span>
