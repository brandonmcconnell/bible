<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And it came to pass in the middle of the night, that the man was afraid, and turned himself; and, hinei, an isha lay at his feet!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 16:8; RUTH 2:20; RUTH 3:12"> And he said, Who art thou? And she answered, I am Ruth thine handmaid; spread therefore thy robe over thine amah (handmaid); for thou art a Go'el (Redeemer).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="RUTH 2:20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:4–5; RUTH 1:8"> And he said, Berukhah at laHashem, my daughter; for thou hast showed more chesed in the latter end than at the beginning, inasmuch as thou followedst not the bochurim, whether poor or rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MISHLE 31:29–31; MISHLE 31:10; MISHLE 12:4"> And now, my daughter, fear not; I will do to thee all that thou requirest; for kol sha'ar ami doth have da'as that thou art an aishes chayil<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Prov 31">a</a></sup>(virtuous woman).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="RUTH 4:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:6; MATTITYAHU 7:12"> And now it is true that I am thy Go'el; howbeit there is a Go'el karov mimeini (Redeemer nearer than I).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="RUTH 4:5; YIRMEYAH 4:2; RUTH 2:20"> Tarry this night, and it shall be in the boker, that if he will perform unto thee the part of a Go'el, well; let him do the Go'elʼs part; let him redeem you. But if he will not do the part of a Go'el to thee, then will I do the part of a Go'el to thee, as Hashem liveth. Lie down until HaBoker.</span>
