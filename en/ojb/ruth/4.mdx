Turn aside, sit down here. And he turned aside, and sat down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MISHLE 31:23; MELACHIM ALEF 21:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:12"> And he took a minyan of the zekenim of the town, and said, Sit ye down here. And they sat down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MISHLE 13:10; TEHILLIM 112:5"> And he said unto the Go'el, Naomi, that is come again out of the sadeh of Moav, selleth a share of sadeh, which was achinu (our brother) Elimelechʼs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:25–29; YIRMEYAH 32:7–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:17"> And I thought to advise thee, saying, Buy it before the inhabitants, and before the zekenim of my people. If thou wilt redeem it, redeem it; but if thou wilt not redeem it, then tell me, that I may know; for there is none to redeem it beside thee; and I am after thee. And he said, I will redeem it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BERESHIS 38:8; DEVARIM 25:5–6; MATTITYAHU 22:24"> Then said Boaz, What day thou buyest the sadeh of the hand of Naomi, thou must acquire Ruth the Moabitess, the eshet hamet (wife of the dead man), lahakim (to raise up, resurrect) the shem hamet upon his nachalah (inheritance).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:25; RUTH 3:13"> And the Go'el said, I cannot redeem it for myself, lest I mar mine own nachalah; redeem thou my right to thyself; for I cannot redeem it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 25:7–10"> Now this was the manner in former time in Yisroel concerning the geulah (redemption) and concerning the temurah (the substitute, exchange), for to confirm all things; a man plucked off his sandal, and gave it to his neighbor; and this was an attestation in Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Therefore the Go'el said unto Boaz, Buy it for thee. So he drew off his sandal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:10–12; BERESHIS 23:16–18"> And Boaz said unto the zekenim, and unto kol haAm, Ye are witnesses this day, that I have bought all that was Elimelechʼs, and all that was Kilyonʼs and Machlonʼs, of the hand of Naomi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEVARIM 25:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:25; BERESHIS 29:27"> Moreover Ruth the Moabitess, the wife of Machlon, have I purchased to be my wife, lahakim (to raise up, resurrect) the shem hamet (name of the dead man) upon his nachalah, that the shem of the dead man be not cut off from among his brethren, and from the sha'ar of his place; ye are edim (witnesses) HaYom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 29:32–24; MICHOH 5:2; MATTITYAHU 2:6"> And kol HaAm that were in the sha'ar, and the zekenim, said, We are edim. Hashem make the isha haba'ah (the woman coming),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Gn 3:15; Isa 7:14">a</a></sup>into thine bais<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e. Bais Dovid">b</a></sup>like Rachel and like Leah, which two did build the Bais Yisroel; and do thou worthily in Ephrat, and be famous in Beit-Lechem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 38:29; SHMUEL ALEF 2:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:4"> And let thy bais be like the bais of Peretz, whom Tamar bore unto Yehudah, of the zera<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gn 22:18; Ga 3:16">c</a></sup>which Hashem shall give thee of this na'arah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 33:5; BERESHIS 29:31; RUTH 3:11"> So Boaz took Ruth, and she was his isha; and when he went in unto her, Hashem gave her conception, and she bore ben.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Isa 7:14">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 1:5–20; LUKAS 1:58; BERESHIS 12:2"> And the nashim said unto Naomi, Baruch Hashem, which hath not left thee this yom without a Go'el. May shmo be famous in Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 55:22; YESHAYAH 46:4; BERESHIS 45:11"> And may he restore your nefesh and be a nourisher of thine old age; for thy kallah, which loveth thee, which is better to thee than shiva banim, hath born him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Naomi took the yeled, and laid him in her kheyk (bosom), and became omenet (nurse) unto it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKAS 1:58–63"> And the women her shchenim gave him a name, saying, There is a ben born to Naomi; and they called his name Oved; he is the father of Yishai, the father of Dovid.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 1:3–6; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:4–8; LUKAS 3:33"> Now these are the toldot of Peretz; Peretz fathered Chetzron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKAS 3:33; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:9–10; MATTITYAHU 1:4"> And Chetzron fathered Ram, and Ram fathered Amminadav,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:7; LUKAS 3:32; MATTITYAHU 1:4"> And Amminadav fathered Nachshon, and Nachshon fathered Salmon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKAS 3:32; MATTITYAHU 1:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:11–12"> And Salmon fathered Boaz, and Boaz fathered Oved,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:15; YESHAYAH 11:1; MATTITYAHU 1:6"> And Oved fathered Yishai, and Yishai fathered Dovid.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="T.
