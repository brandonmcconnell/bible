<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:10; BERESHIS 26:1; YECHEZKEL 14:13"> Now it came to pass in the yamim when HaShofetim (the Judges) ruled, that there was a ra'av (famine) in the land. And a certain ish (man) of Beit-Lechem Yehudah went to sojourn in the sadei Moav, he, and his isha (wife), and his two banim (sons).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 35:19; MICHOH 5:2; SHMUEL ALEF 17:21"> And the shem (name) of the ish (man) was Elimelech, and the shem (name) of his isha (wife) Naomi, and the shem of his two banim Machlon and Kilyon, Ephrathites of Beit-Lechem Yehudah. And they came into the sadei Moav, and continued there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:10–11; TEHILLIM 34:19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:6"> And Elimelech, Naomiʼs husband, died; and she was left (alive) and her two banim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 1:5; MELACHIM ALEF 11:1–2; DEVARIM 23:3"> And they took them wives of the nashim (women) of Moav; the shem (name) of the one was Orpah, and the shem (name) of the other Ruth: and they dwelled there about eser shanim (ten years).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:39; TEHILLIM 89:30–32; YESHAYAH 49:21"> And Machlon and Kilyon died also both of them; and the isha was left (alive), surviving with neither her yeladim nor her ish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 132:15; SHEMOT 3:16; TEHILLIM 111:5"> Then she arose with her kallot, that she might make teshuvah (return) from the sadei Moav: for in the sadeh of Moav she had heard how that Hashem had visited His people in giving them lechem (bread).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 8:3; RUTH 1:14; SHEMOT 18:27"> Wherefore she went forth out of the place where she was, and her two kallot with her; and they set on the derech (way, road) to make teshuvah (return) unto Eretz Yehudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="RUTH 2:20; RUTH 1:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:18"> And Naomi said unto her two kallot, Go, go back each to her beis em (motherʼs house): may Hashem show chesed (lovingkindness) to you, as ye have dealt with hamesim (the dead ones), and with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="RUTH 3:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:37; BERESHIS 29:11"> Hashem grant you that ye may find menuchah (resting place), each of you in the bais of her ish. Then she kissed them; and they lifted up their voice, and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 8:23; TEHILLIM 16:3; TEHILLIM 119:63"> And they said unto her, Surely we will make teshuvah (return) with thee unto thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEVARIM 25:5; BERESHIS 38:11"> And Naomi said, Turn back, my banot; why will ye go with me? Are there yet any more banim in my womb, that they may be your husbands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:17; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:9"> Turn back, my banot, go your way; for I am too old to have an ish. If I should say, I have tikvah, if I should have an ish halailah (tonight), and should also bear banim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="IYOV 19:21; SHOFETIM 2:15; TEHILLIM 32:4"> Would ye tarry for them till they were grown? Would ye stay from having ba'alim (husbands) for them? Nay, my banot; for it is more mar (bitter) for me than you, for the Yad Hashem has gone out against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MISHLE 18:24; MATTITYAHU 16:24; MATTITYAHU 10:37"> And they lifted up their voice, and wept again; and Orpah kissed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="cf Mt 26:49">a</a></sup>her chamot; but Ruth clung unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHOFETIM 11:24; YEHOSHUA 24:15; TZEFANYAH 1:6"> And she said, Hinei, thy sister-in-law is gone back unto her people, and unto her g-ds; return thou after thy sister-in-law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:21; RUTH 2:11–12; MELACHIM BAIS 2:2–6"> And Ruth said, Entreat me not to leave thee, or to return from following after thee; for whither thou goest, I will go; and where thou lodgest, I will lodge; thy people shall be my people, and thy G-d shall be Elohai;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 3:17; MELACHIM ALEF 2:23; MELACHIM BAIS 6:31"> Where thou diest, will I die, and there will I be buried. Hashem do so to me, and more also, if anything but HaMavet part thee and me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:42"> When she saw that she was steadfastly minded to go with her, then she left urging her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:10; YESHAYAH 23:7; EKHAH 2:15"> So they two went until they came to Beit-Lechem. And it came to pass, when they were come to Beit-Lechem, that kol ha'ir (all the town) was moved about them, and they said, Is this Naomi?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="IYOV 6:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:11; TEHILLIM 73:14"> And she said unto them, Call me not Naomi,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Pleasant">b</a></sup>call me Mara;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Bitter">c</a></sup>for Shaddai hath dealt very bitterly with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 2:7–8; IYOV 1:21; IYOV 16:8"> I went away full and Hashem hath brought me back empty; why then call me Naomi, seeing Hashem hath testified against me, and Shaddai hath made me very bitter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="RUTH 2:23; SHMUEL BAIS 21:9; SHEMOT 9:31–32"> So Naomi returned, and Ruth the Moabitess, her kallah, with her, which returned out of the sadei Moav; and they came to Beit-Lechem in the beginning of katzir seorim (barley harvest).</span>
