<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 15:37; MELACHIM BAIS 16:1; YESHAYAH 1:1"> And it came to pass in the yamim of Achaz ben Yotam ben Uziyahu, Melech Yehudah, that Retzin Melech Aram (Syria), and Pekach ben Remalyahu Melech Yisroel, went up toward Yerushalayim to war against her, but could not prevail over her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:12; YESHAYAH 7:13; YESHAYAH 9:9"> And it was told the Bais Dovid, saying, Aram (Syria) is allied with Ephrayim. And his lev was shaken, and the lev of his people, like the trees of the forest are shaken with the wind.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:17; YESHAYAH 36:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:27"> Then said Hashem unto Yeshayah, Go forth now to meet Achaz, thou, and Shear-Yashuv (A Remnant Will Return) thy ben, at the end of the aqueduct of the Upper Pool on the road to the Fullerʼs Field;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YESHAYAH 35:4; EKHAH 3:26; DEVARIM 20:3"> And say unto him, Take heed, and stay calm; fear not, neither be fainthearted because of the two stubs of these smoking firebrands, because of the fierce anger of Retzin and Aram (Syria), and of the ben Remalyahu;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 83:3–4; ZECHARYAH 1:15; TEHILLIM 2:2"> Or because Aram (Syria), Ephrayim, and ben Remalyahu, have plotted your ruin, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Let us go up against Yehudah, and trouble it, and let us divide it for ourselves, and set a melech in the midst of it, even ben Tav'el;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:10; EKHAH 3:37; YESHAYAH 46:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thus saith Adonoi Hashem:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It shall not stand, neither shall it come to pass.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YESHAYAH 17:1–3; BERESHIS 14:15; SHMUEL BAIS 8:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the rosh of Aram (Syria) is Damascus,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the rosh of Damascus is Retzin;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and within threescore and five shanah</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shall Ephrayim be broken, that it be not a people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the rosh of Ephrayim is Shomron,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the rosh of Shomron is ben Remalyahu.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If ye will not stand in emunah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">then you shall not stand at all.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:5; YESHAYAH 1:5; YESHAYAH 10:20"> Moreover, Hashem spoke again unto Achaz, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 37:30; YESHAYAH 38:7–8; YESHAYAH 38:22"> Ask thee an ot (sign) of Hashem Eloheicha; ask it either in the deepest depths or in the highest heights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 33:31; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:22; MALACHI 3:15"> But Achaz said, I will not ask, neither will I tempt Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YESHAYAH 43:24; YESHAYAH 63:10; YECHEZKEL 34:18"> And he (Yeshayah) said, Hear ye now, O Bais Dovid; Is it a small thing for you to weary anashim, but will ye weary my G-d also?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 1:23; YESHAYAH 9:6; LUKAS 1:31"> Therefore Adonoi Himself shall give you an ot (sign); Hinei, HaAlmah (the unmarried young virgin) shall conceive, and bear Ben, and shall call Shmo Immanu El (G-d is with us).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See extensive commentary in The Translator To The Reader">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YESHAYAH 7:22; AMOS 5:15; MATTITYAHU 3:4"> Curds and honey shall he eat at the time that he knows to refuse the rah, and choose the tov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:4; DEVARIM 1:39; YESHAYAH 9:11"> For before the na'ar (boy) shall understand to refuse the rah, and choose the tov, the adamah (land) will be desolate, of whose two melachim thou art afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:7–8; YESHAYAH 10:5–6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:19–21"> Hashem shall bring upon thee, and upon thy people, and upon the Bais of thy father, yamim such as have not come since the yom when Ephrayim departed from Yehudah:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2Kgs 15:29; 16:9">b</a></sup>even Melech Ashur (Assyria).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YESHAYAH 5:26; YEHOSHUA 24:12; DEVARIM 1:44"> And it shall come to pass in that yom, that Hashem shall whistle for the fly that is in the uttermost part of the Nile of Mitzrayim, and for the bee that is in Eretz Ashur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 2:19; YIRMEYAH 16:16; YESHAYAH 2:21"> And they shall come, and shall settle all of them on the steep ravines, and in the crevices of the rocks, and upon all thorns, and upon all water holes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:7; YECHEZKEL 29:18; YESHAYAH 10:15"> In the same yom shall Adonoi shave with a razor that is hired, namely, by them beyond the river, by Melech Ashur, the head, and the hair of the legs; and it shall also consume the beard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YESHAYAH 5:17; YIRMEYAH 39:10; YESHAYAH 37:30"> And it shall come to pass in that yom, that a man shall keep alive a young cow, and two sheep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YESHAYAH 7:15; MATTITYAHU 3:4; SHMUEL BAIS 17:29"> And it shall come to pass, for the abundance of cholov that they shall give, he shall eat cream; for thickened milk and devash shall every one eat that is left in the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YESHAYAH 5:6; SHIR HASHIRIM 8:11–12; MATTITYAHU 21:33"> And it shall come to pass in that yom, that every place shall be, where there were a thousand gefen worth a thousand kesef, it shall even be for briers and thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:3"> With arrows and with bows shall men come there; because kol ha'aretz shall become briers and thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YESHAYAH 7:21–22; YESHAYAH 13:20–22; YESHAYAH 5:17"> And on all hills that were hoed with a hoe, thou shall not come there for fear of briers and thorns; but it shall be for pasturing of cattle, and for the treading place of seh.</span>
</div>
