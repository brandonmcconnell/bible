<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:1; MELACHIM BAIS 18:13; MELACHIM BAIS 18:17"> Now it came to pass in the fourteenth shanah of Melech Chizkiyah, that Sancheriv Melech Ashur came up against all the fortified cities of Yehudah, and captured them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:9–23; YESHAYAH 7:3; MELACHIM BAIS 18:17–37"> And Melech Ashur sent Rav Shakeh from Lakhish to Yerushalayim unto Melech Chizkiyah with a large army. And he stood by the te'alat haberekhah haelyonah (aqueduct of the Upper Pool) on the highway of the sadeh of the launderer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:16–17; YESHAYAH 22:15–21; SHMUEL BAIS 20:24–25"> Then came forth unto him Elyakim Ben Chilkiyah, who was over the Bais (palace), and Shevna the Sofer, and Yoach Ben Asaph, the Mazkir (secretary).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:19–37; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:16; MISHLE 16:18"> And Rav Shakeh said unto them, Say ye now to Chizkiyah, Thus saith the HaMelech Hagadol, Melech Ashur, What bitachon (confidence) is this wherein thou trustest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:7; YIRMEYAH 52:3; MISHLE 21:30–31"> I say, Your etzah (counsel) and gevurah (strength) for milchamah (war) are only devar sefatayim (words of the lips);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="words of the lips empty words">a</a></sup>now on whom dost thou trust, that thou rebellest against me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 29:6–7; YIRMEYAH 37:5–8; MELACHIM BAIS 18:21"> Hinei, thou trustest in the mishenet (staff) of this broken reed, on Mitzrayim; whereon if an ish lean, it will go into his palm, and pierce it; so is Pharaoh Melech Mitzrayim to all that trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:4–5; TEHILLIM 42:10–11; TEHILLIM 42:5"> But if thou say to me, We trust in Hashem Eloheinu; is it not He, whose high places and whose mizbechot Chizkiyah hath taken away, and said to Yehudah and to Yerushalayim, Ye shall worship before this Mizbe'ach?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:40–43; MELACHIM ALEF 20:10; NECHEMYAH 4:2–5"> Therefore give pledges, now, to adoni HaMelech Ashur, and I will give thee two thousand susim, if thou be able on thy part to set riders upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YESHAYAH 10:8; YESHAYAH 30:16–17; YESHAYAH 36:6"> How then wilt thou turn away the face of one officer of the least of the avadim of adoni, and put thy trust on Mitzrayim for chariots and for parashim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:25; YESHAYAH 10:5–7; MELACHIM ALEF 13:18"> And am I now come up without Hashem against this land to destroy it? Hashem said unto me, Go up against this land, and destroy it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZRA 4:7; DANIEL 2:4; MELACHIM BAIS 18:26–27"> Then said Elyakim and Shevna and Yoach unto Rav Shakeh, Speak now, thee, unto thy avadim in Aramit (Aramaic); for we understand it; and speak not to us in Yehudit (Hebrew), in the ears of the people that are on the chomah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:27; VAYIKRA 26:29; EKHAH 4:9–10"> But Rav Shakeh said, Is it to adoneicha and to you that adoni has sent me to speak these words? Hath he not sent me to the anashim that sit upon the chomah, that they may eat their own dung, and drink their own urine with you?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:18; TEHILLIM 73:8–9; MELACHIM BAIS 18:28–32"> Then Rav Shakeh stood, and cried with a kol gadol in Yehudit, and said, Hear ye the words of HaMelech Hagadol, the Melech Ashur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:4; HISGALUS 13:5–6; DANIEL 6:20"> Thus saith HaMelech, Let not Chizkiyah deceive you; for he shall not be able to save you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YESHAYAH 37:23–24; MATTITYAHU 27:43; TEHILLIM 71:9–11"> Neither let Chizkiyah make you trust in Hashem, saying, Hashem will surely save us; this city shall not be given into the Melech Ashur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 4:25; ZECHARYAH 3:10; MICHOH 4:4"> Pay heed not to Chizkiyah; for thus saith HaMelech Ashur, Make a brocha with me, and come out to me; and eat ye every one of his gefen, and every one of his te'enah and drink ye every one the waters of his own bor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:32; DEVARIM 8:7–9; IYOV 20:17"> Until I come and take you away to an eretz like your own eretz, an eretz dagan and tirosh, an eretz lechem and kramim (vineyards).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YESHAYAH 37:17–18; YESHAYAH 36:15; YESHAYAH 37:10"> Beware lest Chizkiyah mislead you, saying, Hashem will save us. Hath any of the elohei HaGoyim delivered his land out of the yad Melech Ashur?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 10:9–11; YIRMEYAH 49:23; SHMUEL BAIS 8:9"> Where are the elohei Chamat and Arpad? Where are the elohei Sepharvayim? And have they saved Shomron out of my yad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 50:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:19; YESHAYAH 37:23–29"> Who are they among all elohim of these lands, that have saved their land out of my yad, that Hashem should save Yerushalayim out of my yad?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MISHLE 26:4; TEHILLIM 38:13–15; MATTITYAHU 7:6"> But they held their peace, and answered him not a davar; for the mitzvat HaMelech was saying, Answer him not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YESHAYAH 36:3; YESHAYAH 33:7; MATTITYAHU 26:65"> Then came Elyakim Ben Chilkiyah, that was over the Bais, and Shevna the Sofer, and Yoach Ben Asaph, the Mazkir, to Chizkiyah with their begadim torn, and told him the words of Rav Shakeh.</span>
</div>
