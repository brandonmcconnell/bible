<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:17; YIRMEYAH 25:29–30; AMOS 1:8"> In the year<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="711 B.C.E.">a</a></sup>that Tartan came unto Ashdod, (when Sargon Melech Ashur sent him,) and fought against Ashdod, and took it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MICHOH 1:8; MATTITYAHU 3:4; SHMUEL ALEF 19:24"> At the same time spoke Hashem by Yeshayah Ben Amotz, saying, Go and remove the sackcloth from off thy loins, and put off thy sandal from thy regel. And he did so, going arom (naked, stripped) and barefoot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:18; BAMIDBAR 14:34; YECHEZKEL 4:5–6"> And Hashem said, Just as Avdi Yeshayah hath walked arom (naked) and barefoot shalosh shanim for an ot and mofet against Mitzrayim and Kush;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YESHAYAH 19:4; YIRMEYAH 13:26; YIRMEYAH 13:22"> So shall Melech Ashur (Assyria) lead away captive Mitzrayim and Golus-exiled Kush, ne'arim and zekenim, aron (naked) and barefoot, even with their buttocks uncovered, to the shame of Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YESHAYAH 30:5; YESHAYAH 30:3; MELACHIM BAIS 18:21"> And they shall be afraid and ashamed of Kush their expectation, and of Mitzrayim their tiferet (glory, boast).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:33; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:3"> And the inhabitants of this coast shall say in that day, Hinei, such is our expectation, where we fled for ezrah (help) to be delivered from Melech Ashur; and how shall we escape?</span>
</div>
