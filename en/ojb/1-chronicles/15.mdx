<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:27; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 25:7–8"> And Kenanyahu, sar HaLevi'im, was for singing; he directed the song, because of his understanding.</span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 2:18; SHMUEL BAIS 6:14"> And Dovid was clothed with a me'il butz (fine linen robe), and all the Levi'im that bore the Aron, and the meshorerim (singers); and Kenanyah the master of the song with the meshorerim; Dovid also had upon him a linen ephod.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Ps 110:4">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 13:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 5:12–13"> Thus kol Yisroel brought up the Aron Brit Hashem with shouting, and with sound of the shofar, and with trumpets, and with cymbals, sounding nevalim (lyres) and kinnorot (harps).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 6:20–23; SHMUEL BAIS 6:16; DEVARIM 31:26"> And it came to pass, as the Aron Brit Hashem came to Ir Dovid, that Michal Bat Sha'ul, looking out at the chalon, saw Dovid HaMelech dancing and whirling. And she despised him in her lev.</span>
