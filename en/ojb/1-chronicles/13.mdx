<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 150:3–5; TEHILLIM 68:25–27; AMOS 6:5"> And Dovid and Kol Yisroel rejoiced before HaElohim with kol oz and with shirim (songs), and with kinnorot (harps), and with nevalim (lyres), and with tambourines, and with cymbals, and with khatzotzerot (trumpets).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
