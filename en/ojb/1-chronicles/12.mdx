<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHOFETIM 3:15; SHOFETIM 20:16; SHMUEL ALEF 17:49"> They were armed with keshet (bow), and could use both the right hand and the left in hurling stones and shooting khitzim (arrows) from a keshet; they were kinsmen of Sha'ul, of Binyamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:28; SHMUEL BAIS 21:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:33"> The head was Achi'ezer, then Yoash, the Bnei Shema'ah the Giveati; Yeziel, Pelet, the Bnei Azmavet; Berakhah, and Yehu the Anetoti.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 9:3; YEHOSHUA 15:36; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:15"> And Yishma'yah the Giv'oni, a gibbor among the Shloshim (Thirty), and over the Shloshim; and</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Yirmeyah, Yachaziel, Yochanan, and Yozavad the Gederati,</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:58; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 4:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 4:39"> Elkanah, Yishiyahu, Azare'el, Yo'ezer, and Yashov'am, the Korchi,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 2:18; SHMUEL BAIS 17:10; SHMUEL BAIS 23:20"> And Yo'elah, and Zevadyah, the Bnei Yerocham of Gedor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And of the Gadi there separated themselves unto Dovid into the stronghold in the midbar gibborei hachayil (men of valor), and anshei tzavah (army men) for milchamah, that could handle shield and spear, whose faces were like the faces of aryeh, and were as swift as the gazelles upon the harim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Ezer the head, Ovadyah the second, Eliav the third,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Mishmannah the fourth, Yirmeyah the fifth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Attai the sixth, Eliel the seventh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Yochanan the eighth, Elzavad the ninth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:30; VAYIKRA 26:8"> Yirmeyah the tenth, Makhbanai the eleventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 3:15; YEHOSHUA 4:18; YIRMEYAH 49:19"> These were of the Bnei Gad, commanders of the tzava (army). One of the least was equal to a hundred, and the greatest to an elef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> These are they that went over Yarden in the first month, when it was overflowing all its banks; they put to flight all them of the valleys, both toward the east, and toward the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:23; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:11"> And there came of the Bnei Binyamin and Yehudah to the stronghold unto Dovid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHOFETIM 6:34; SHOFETIM 3:10; SHMUEL BAIS 17:25"> And Dovid went out before them, and answered and said unto them, If ye be come in shalom unto me to help me, mine lev shall be united unto you, but if ye be come to betray me to mine enemies, seeing there is no chamas in mine hands, Elohei Avoteinu look thereon, and pass<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="His">a</a></sup>judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 29:2–9"> Then the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="HaKodesh">b</a></sup>came upon Amasai, who was head of the Thirty, and he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Thine are we, Dovid,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and on thy side, thou Ben Yishai.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Shalom, shalom be unto thee,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and shalom be to thine helpers;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for Eloheicha helpeth thee.</span><br />Then Dovid received them, and made them heads of the gedud (raiding band, troop).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEVARIM 33:17; DEVARIM 1:15; SHMUEL ALEF 29:11"> And there defected some of Menasheh to Dovid, when he went with the Pelishtim (Philistines) against Sha'ul for milchamah, but they helped them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Pelishtim">c</a></sup>not; for the rulers of the Pelishtim after etzah (counsel) sent him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Dovid">d</a></sup>away, saying, He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dovid">e</a></sup>will defect to his adon Sha'ul to the jeopardy of our heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 30:1–17; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:24"> As he went to Tziklag, there defected to him those of Menasheh, Adnach, and Yozavad, and Yedi'ael, and Micha'el, and Yozavad, and Elihu, and Tzilletai, heads of the alafim that were of Menasheh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:1; IYOV 17:9; BERESHIS 32:2"> And they helped Dovid against the gedud, for they were all gibborei chayil, and were sarim in the tzava (army).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 2:3–4; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:10; SHMUEL ALEF 16:1"> For at that time day by day there came men to Dovid to help him, until it was a machaneh gadol, like the Machaneh Elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And these are the numbers of the leaders that were ready-armed for the army, and came to Dovid to Chevron, to turn over the Malchut Sha'ul to him, according to the word of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The Bnei Yehudah that bore shield and romach (spear) were six alafim and eight hundred, ready-armed for tzva.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Of the Bnei Shim'on, gibborei chayil for tzava, shivat alafim and one hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 11:9; MELACHIM BAIS 25:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:49–57"> Of the Bnei Levi four alafim and six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:53"> And Yehoyada was the nagid of those of Aharon, and with him were three alafim and sheva hundred;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 2:8–9; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:2; BERESHIS 31:23"> And Tzadok, a na'ar gibbor chayil, and of bais aviv twenty and two sarim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="BERESHIS 6:4"> And of the Bnei Binyamin, the kindred of Sha'ul, three alafim; for up until then the greatest part of them had kept shomrim mishmeret Bais Sha'ul (guarded loyalty to the House of Sha'ul).</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 17:1–18"> And of the Bnei Ephrayim twenty elef and eight hundred, gibborei chayil, famous throughout the bais of their avot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:17; LUKAS 12:56–57; ESTHER 1:13"> And of the half tribe of Menasheh eighteen elef, which were designated by shmot, to come and make Dovid Melech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="TEHILLIM 12:2; YOCHANAN 1:47"> And of the Bnei Yissakhar, which were men that had binah (understanding) of the times, to know what Yisroel ought to do; the heads of them were two hundred; and all their brethren were under their commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Of Zevulun, such as went forth to tzava (military service), expert in milchamah, with all instruments of milchamah, fifty elef, which could keep rank; they were not of double heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And of Naphtali elef sarim, and with them with shield and khanit (spear) thirty and shivah elef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:33; YOEL 2:7"> And of the Dani expert in milchamah twenty and eight elef and six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 14:3; DEVARIM 3:12–16; YEHOSHUA 13:7–32"> And of Asher, such as went forth to tzava (military service), expert in milchamah, forty elef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="BERESHIS 49:8–10; MELACHIM ALEF 8:61; TEHILLIM 101:2"> And on the other side of Yarden, of the Reuveni, and the Gadi, and of the half shevet (tribe) of Menasheh, with all manner of instruments of tzava for the milchamah, a hundred and twenty elef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="BERESHIS 31:54; SHMUEL BAIS 19:42; BERESHIS 26:30"> All these anshei milchamah, that could keep rank, came with a levav shalem to Chevron, to make Dovid Melech over kol Yisroel; and all the rest also of Yisroel were of lev echad to make Dovid Melech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:18; SHMUEL BAIS 16:1; YIRMEYAH 23:5–6"> And there they were with Dovid yamim shloshah, eating and drinking, for their brethren had prepared for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Moreover they that were kerovim (near ones, neighbors) to them, even unto Yissakhar and Zevulun and Naphtali, brought lechem on chamorim, and on gemalim (camels), and on peradim, and on bakar, and food of kemach (flour), cakes of figs, and raisin cakes, and yayin, and shemen, and bakar, and tzon abundantly; for there was simchah in Yisroel.</span>
