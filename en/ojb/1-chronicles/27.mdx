<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 4:27; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 26:11–13; DEVARIM 1:15"> Now the Bnei Yisroel after their number, namely, the rashei ha'avot and sarim of alafim and me'ot (hundreds), and their shoterim that served HaMelech in any matter of the divisions, which came in and went out chodesh b'chodesh (month by month) throughout all the months of the shanah, each division consisting of twenty and four elef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:11; SHMUEL BAIS 23:8"> Over the first division for the chodesh harishon was Yashov'am Ben Zavdiel; and in his division were twenty and four elef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:20; BAMIDBAR 10:14; BERESHIS 49:8–10"> Of the Bnei Peretz was the rosh of all the sarim of the tzava'os (armies) for the first month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 23:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:12"> And over the division of the second month was Dodai the Achochi, and of his division was Miklot also the nagid; in his division likewise were twenty and four elef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 18:17; MELACHIM ALEF 4:4–5"> The third sar (commander) of the tzava (army) for the third month was Benayahu Ben Yehoyada, the Kohen Rosh; and in his division were twenty and four elef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 23:20–23; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:22–25; SHMUEL BAIS 22:20–23"> This is that Benayahu, who was gibbor among the Shloshim (Thirty), and over the Shloshim; and in his division was Ammizavad bno.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:26; SHMUEL BAIS 23:24; SHMUEL BAIS 2:18–23"> The fourth for the fourth month was Asahel the brother of Yoav, and Zevadyah bno after him; and in his division were twenty and four elef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:27; SHMUEL BAIS 23:25; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:29"> The fifth sar for the fifth month was Shamhut the Yizrach; and in his division were twenty and four elef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:28; SHMUEL BAIS 23:26"> The sixth for the sixth month was Ira Ben Ikkesh the Tekoi; and in his division were twenty and four elef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:27; SHMUEL BAIS 23:26"> The seventh for the seventh month was Cheletz the Peloni, of the Bnei Ephrayim; and in his division were 24,000.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 21:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:29; BAMIDBAR 26:20"> The eighth for the eighth month was Sibbekhai the Chushati, of the Zarchi; and in his division were 24,000.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:28; SHMUEL BAIS 23:27"> The ninth for the ninth month was Avi'ezer the Anetoti, of Binyamin; and in his division were 24,000.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:30; SHMUEL BAIS 23:28"> The tenth for the tenth month was Maharai the Netophati, of the Zarchi; and in his division were 24,000.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:31; SHMUEL BAIS 23:30"> The eleventh for the eleventh month was Benayah the Pir'athoni, of the Bnei Ephrayim; and in his division were 24,000.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 23:29; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 4:13; SHOFETIM 3:9"> The twelfth for the twelfth month was Cheldai the Netophati, of Otniel; and in his division were 24,000.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Furthermore over the Shivtei Yisroel; the nagid of the Reuveni was Eli'ezer Ben Zichri; of the Shim'oni, Shephatyahu Ben Ma'achah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:30; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 24:31; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:27–28"> Of the Levi'im, Chashavyah Ben Kemu'el; of Aharon, Tzadok;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:6; SHMUEL ALEF 17:29; SHMUEL ALEF 17:13"> Of Yehudah, Elihu, one of the brethren of Dovid; of Yissakhar, Omri Ben Micha'el;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Of Zevulun, Yishma'yahu Ben Ovadyah; of Naphtali, Yerimot Ben Azri'el;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Of the Bnei Ephrayim, Hoshea Ben Azazyahu; of the half tribe of Menasheh, Yoel Ben Pedayahu;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:27; SHMUEL ALEF 14:50–51; SHMUEL BAIS 3:37"> Of the half tribe of Menasheh in Gil'ad, Yiddo Ben Zecharyahu; of Binyamin, Ya'asiel Ben Avner;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:1"> Of Dan, Azare'el Ben Yerocham. These were the sarim of the Shivtei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:2–5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:12"> But Dovid took not the number of them from twenty shanim and under; because Hashem had said he would increase Yisroel like to the kokhavim of HaShomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:1–17; SHMUEL BAIS 24:1–15"> Yoav Ben Tzeruyah began to number, but he finished not, because there fell ketzef (wrath) on account of this against Yisroel; neither was the number put in the Divrei HaYamim of Melech Dovid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 16:2; SHEMOT 1:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 26:10"> And over the otzarot HaMelech was Azmavet Ben Adiel; and over the storehouses in the sadot, in the towns, and in the villages, and in the migdalot was Yehonatan Ben Uziyahu;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And over them that did the work of the sadeh to till the adamah was Ezri Ben Keluv;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And over the kramim was Shime'i the Ramati; over the increase of the kramim for the wine vats was Zavdi the Shiphmi;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 10:27; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 1:15; MELACHIM ALEF 4:7"> And over the zeitim and the sycomore trees that were in the Shefelah was Ba'al-Chanan the Gederi; over the shemen storehouses, Yoash;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:16; YESHAYAH 65:10"> And over the bakar (herd) that fed in Sharon was Shitrai the Sharoni; over the bakar that were in the valleys was Shaphat Ben Adlai;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="BERESHIS 47:6; IYOV 1:3"> Over the gemalim (camels) also was Ovil the Yishme'eli; and over the donkeys was Yechdeyahu the Meronoti;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:10"> And over the tzon (flock) was Yaziz the Hagri. All these were the sarim of the property which pertained to Melech Dovid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 21:21; SHMUEL BAIS 13:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:11"> Also Yehonatan Dovidʼs dod (uncle) was a yoetz (counsellor), an ish meivin, and a sofer; and Yechiel Ben Chachmoni was with the Bnei HaMelech;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:12; SHMUEL BAIS 15:37; SHMUEL BAIS 15:32"> And Achitophel was the yoetz l'Melech; and Chushai the Arki was the re'a HaMelech;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:6; MELACHIM ALEF 1:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 27:5"> And after Achitophel was Yehoyada Ben Benayahu, and Evyatar (Abiathar); and the sar tzava (commander of the army) of HaMelech was Yoav.</span>
