<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:3; SHMUEL BAIS 5:11–16; EZRA 3:7"> Now Churam Melech Tzor sent malachim to Dovid, and timber of cedars, with masons and carpenters, to build him a bais (palace).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 24:7; TEHILLIM 89:20–37; SHMUEL BAIS 7:16"> And Dovid perceived that Hashem had established him Melech over Yisroel, for his malchut was lifted up on high, for the sake of His people Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MISHLE 5:18–19; SHMUEL BAIS 5:13; KOHELET 7:26–29"> And Dovid took more nashim at Yerushalayim; and Dovid fathered more banim and banot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:5–9; SHMUEL BAIS 5:14; LUKAS 3:31"> Now these are the shmot of his banim which were born in Yerushalayim: Shammua, and Shovav, Natan, and Sh'lomo,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 5:15; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:6"> And Yivchar, and Elishua, and Elpelet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Nogah, and Nepheg, and Yaphia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:8; SHMUEL BAIS 5:16"> And Elishama, and Be'elyada, and Eliphalet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 21:11; HISGALUS 11:15–18; TEHILLIM 2:1–6"> And when the Pelishtim (Philistines) heard that Dovid nimshach (was anointed) Melech over kol Yisroel, all the Pelishtim went up to search for Dovid. Dovid heard of it, and went out against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:15; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 14:13; SHMUEL BAIS 23:13"> And the Pelishtim (Philistines) came and spread out in the Emek Repha'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MISHLE 3:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 14:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 13:3"> And Dovid inquired of Elohim, saying, Shall I go up against the Pelishtim? And wilt Thou deliver them into mine yad? And Hashem said unto him, Go up; for I will deliver them into thine yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 28:21; SHEMOT 14:28; SHMUEL BAIS 5:20"> So they came up to Ba'al-Peratzim; and Dovid defeated them there. Then Dovid said, Elohim hath broken out upon mine oyevim by mine yad like the peretz mayim (breaking forth of waters); therefore they called the shem of that makom Ba'al-Peratzim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHEMOT 32:20; SHMUEL ALEF 5:2–6; MELACHIM BAIS 19:18"> And when they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the Pelishtim">a</a></sup>had abandoned their elohim there, Dovid gave a commandment, and they were burned with eish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 14:9; MELACHIM ALEF 20:22; SHMUEL BAIS 5:22–25"> And the Pelishtim (Philistines) yet again spread out in the emek (valley).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:6–7; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 14:10; TEHILLIM 27:4"> Therefore Dovid inquired again of Elohim, and Elohim said unto him, Go not up after them; circle around upon them, and come upon them opposite the mulberry trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YESHAYAH 13:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:12–13"> And it shall be, when thou shalt hear a sound of marching in the tops of the mulberry trees, that then thou shalt go out to milchamah; for HaElohim is gone forth before thee to strike the machaneh Pelishtim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:14; YOCHANAN 13:17; BERESHIS 6:22"> Dovid therefore did as Elohim commanded him, and they struck down the machaneh Pelishtim from Giv'on even to Gezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:25; YEHOSHUA 6:27; SHEMOT 15:14–16"> And the shem (fame) of Dovid went out into all lands; and Hashem brought the pachad (terror) of him upon kol HaGoyim.</span>
