<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 9:21"> And the banim of Meshelemyahu were Zecharyahu the bechor, Yedi'ael the second, Zevadyahu the third, Yatniel the fourth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Eilam the fifth, Yehochanan the sixth, Elyeho'einai the seventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 16:38; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:24; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:18"> Moreover the banim of Oved-Edom were Shema'yah the bechor, Yehozavad the second, Yoach the third, and Sachar the fourth, and Netan'el the fifth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 128:1"> Ammi'el the sixth, Yissakhar the seventh, Peultai the eighth; for Elohim blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:28; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:12; SHOFETIM 6:12"> Also unto Shema'yah bno were banim born, that ruled throughout the bais avihem, for they were gibborei chayil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The Bnei Shema'yah: Otni, and Repha'el, and Oved, Elzavad, whose brethren were bnei chayil, Elihu, and Semakhyahu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:6; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:4–11"> All these of the Bnei Oved-Edom; they and their banim and their brethren, ish chayil for ko'ach for the avodah, were threescore and two of Oved-Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:1"> And Meshelemyahu had banim and brethren, bnei chayil, eighteen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 16:38; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:1–2"> Also Chosah, of the Bnei Merari, had banim; Shimri the rosh, (for though he was not the bechor, yet avihu appointed him rosh);</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Chilkiyahu the second, Tevalyahu the third, Zecharyahu the fourth; all the banim and brethren of Chosah were thirteen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 25:8"> Among these were the divisions of the sho'arim, even among the rashei hagibborim, having mishmeret (duties), just as their brethren, to minister service in the Beis Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 25:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 24:31; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 24:5"> And they cast goralot, the katon as well as the gadol, to their bais avot, for every sha'ar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the goral for the east fell to Shelemyahu. Then for Zecharyahu bno, a yo'etz (counselor) with seichel (wisdom), they cast goralot; and his goral fell out northward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:24; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:17"> To Oved-Edom southward; and to his banim the Asuppim Bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 12:24; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:10–12; MELACHIM ALEF 10:5"> To Shuppim and Chosah the goral came forth westward, with the Sha'ar Shallechet, by the upper road, mishmar (guard) corresponded to mishmar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 9:24"> Eastward were six Levi'im, northward four a day, southward four a day, and toward Asuppim shnayim shnayim (two and two).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 23:11"> At Parbar westward, four at the road, and two at Parbar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 16:11"> These are the divisions of the sho'arim among the Bnei Korchi, and among the Bnei Merari.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:22; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:12–19; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:14–16"> And of the Levi'im, Achiyah was over the otzarot of the Beis HaElohim, and over the otzarot of the kodashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:8"> As concerning the Bnei La'dan; the Bnei HaGershuni through La'dan, rashei ha'avot, even of La'dan the Gershoni, were Yechieli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:8; NECHEMYAH 10:38; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:20"> The Bnei Yechieli: Zetam, and Yoel his brother, which were over the otzarot of the Beis Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:27; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:12; BAMIDBAR 3:19"> Of the Amrami, and the Yitzhari, the Chevroni, and the Uzzieli;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 24:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:15–16"> And Shevuel Ben Gershom Ben Moshe, was nagid of the otzarot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:17–18; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:15; SHEMOT 18:4"> And his brethren by Eli'ezer: Rechavyahu bno, and Yeshayahu bno, and Yoram bno, and Zichri bno, and Shelomit bno.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:2–9; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:14"> Which Shelomot and his brethren were over all the otzarot of the kodashim, which Dovid HaMelech, and the rashei ha'avot, the sarim over alafim and me'ot, and the sarim of the tzava (army), had dedicated as kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 12:14; YEHOSHUA 6:19; NECHEMYAH 10:32–34"> Out of the plunder won in battles did they dedicate as kadosh to maintain the Beis Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 9:9; SHMUEL ALEF 17:55; SHMUEL BAIS 10:9–14"> And all that Shmuel the ro'eh (seer), and Sha'ul Ben Kish, and Avner Ben Ner, and Yoav Ben Tzeruyah, had dedicated as kodesh; and whosoever had dedicated any thing, it was under the yad of Shelomit, and of his brethren.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:4; NECHEMYAH 11:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:13"> Of the Yitzhari, Kenanyahu and his banim were for the outside labor of Yisroel, for shoterim (officials) and shofetim (judges).</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 27:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:19; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:12"> And of the Chevroni, Chashavyahu and his brethren, bnei chayil, an elef and shivah me'ot (hundred), were in charge of Yisroel on this side of the Yarden westward in all the work of Hashem, and in the avodas HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:19; YEHOSHUA 21:39; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:27"> Among the Chevroni was Yeriyah the rosh, even among the Chevroni, according to the toldot of his avot. In the fortieth year of the reign of Dovid they were sought, and there were found among them gibborei chayil at Yazer of Gil'ad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:37; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:24"> And his brethren, bnei chayil, were two thousand and shivah hundred rashei ha'avot, whom Dovid HaMelech put over the Reuveni, the Gadi, and the half tribe of Menasheh, for every matter pertaining to Elohim, and the affairs of HaMelech.</span>
