<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 35:22; BERESHIS 29:32; BERESHIS 48:15–22"> Now the Bnei Reuven the bechor Yisroel, for he was the bechor; but forasmuch as he defiled the bed of aviv, his bechorah (firstborn birthright) was given unto the Bnei Yosef Ben Yisroel, though &#91;the latter was&#93; not listed in the family registers as the bechorah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 2:6; MICHOH 5:2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:14"> For Yehudah prevailed above his brethren, and of him came the nagid; but the bechorah (portion of the firstborn) was Yosefʼs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:9; SHEMOT 6:14; BAMIDBAR 26:5–9"> The Bnei Reuven bechor Yisroel were Chanokh, and Pallu, Chetzron, and Carmi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> The Bnei Yoel: Shema'yah bno, Gog bno, Shime'i bno,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Michah bno, Reayah bno, Ba'al bno,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:26; MELACHIM BAIS 15:29; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:20"> Be'erah bno, whom Tilgat Pilneser Melech Ashur carried away into the Golus; he was nasi of the Reuveni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:17"> And his relatives by their mishpekhot, when the genealogy of their toldot was reckoned, were the rosh, Ye'iel, and Zecharyah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:4; BAMIDBAR 32:34; BAMIDBAR 32:38"> And Bela Ben Azaz Ben Shema Ben Yoel who dwelt in Aro'er, even unto Nevo and Ba'al-Me'on;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 22:8–9"> And eastward he inhabited as far as the beginning of the midbar from the river Euphrates, because their mikneh (livestock) were multiplied in Eretz Gil'ad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 21:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:18–21; TEHILLIM 83:6"> And in the days of Sha'ul they made milchamah with the Hagari'im, who fell by their yad; and they dwelt in their ohalim (tents) throughout all the area east of Gil'ad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 13:11; YEHOSHUA 13:24–28; DEVARIM 3:10–17"> And the Bnei Gad dwelt next to them, in the Eretz Bashan as far as Salkhah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Yoel the rosh, and Shapham the mishneh (second), and Ya'nai, and Shaphat in Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And their achim of the bais avoteihem were Micha'el, and Meshullam, and Sheva, and Yorai, and Yakan, and Zia, and Ever, shivah (seven) in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> These are the Bnei Avichayil Ben Churi Ben Yaroach Ben Gil'ad Ben Micha'el Ben Yeshishai Ben Yachdo Ben Buz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Achi Ben Avdi'el Ben Guni was rosh of the bais avoteihem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 27:29; SHIR HASHIRIM 2:1; YESHAYAH 35:2"> And they dwelt in Gil'ad in Bashan, and in her towns, and in all the migrashim (open lands) of Sharon, within their borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 14:16; MELACHIM BAIS 14:28; MELACHIM BAIS 15:32"> All these were reckoned by genealogies in the days of Yotam Melech Yehudah, and in the days of Yarov'am Melech Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:3; YEHOSHUA 4:12–13"> The Bnei Reuven, and the Gadi, and half the shevet (tribe) of Menasheh, of bnei chayil, anashim able to bear mogen and cherev, and to shoot with keshet, and trained for milchamah, were four and forty elef seven hundred and threescore, that were ready for military service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:15; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 1:31; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:10"> And they waged milchamah against the Hagari'im, with Yetur, and Naphish, and Nodav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 22:4–5; TEHILLIM 9:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 18:31"> And they were helped against them, and the Hagari'im were delivered into their yad, and all that were with them, for they cried to Elohim in the milchamah, and He was entreated of them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="T.N. These genealogies establish the massively eye-witnessed veracity of the death and resurrection of Israel in and from the Golus.">a</a></sup>because they put their trust in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HISGALUS 18:13; BAMIDBAR 31:35"> And they took away their mikneh: of their gemalim fifty elef, and of tzon two hundred and fifty elef, and of chamorim two thousand, and of nefesh adam a hundred elef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:31; YEHOSHUA 23:10"> For there fell down many slain, because the milchamah was Elohimʼs. And they took up residence in their place until the Golus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEVARIM 3:8–9; SHIR HASHIRIM 4:8; TEHILLIM 133:3"> And Bnei chatzi (half) shevet Menasheh dwelt in the land. They increased from Bashan unto Ba'al-Chermon and Senir, and unto Har Chermon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 4:38"> And these were the heads of the bais avotam, even Epher, Yishi, Eliel, Azriel, Yirmeyah, and Hodavyah, and Yachdiel, anashim gibborei chayil, famous men, and heads of the bais avotam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="HISGALUS 17:5; TEHILLIM 106:34–39; SHEMOT 34:15"> And they transgressed against Elohei Avoteihem, and went a-whoring after the elohim of the peoples of the land, whom Elohim made shmad before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 15:19; MELACHIM BAIS 17:6; MELACHIM BAIS 15:29"> And Elohei Yisroel stirred up the ruach of Pul Melech Ashur, and the ruach of Tilgat Pilneser Melech Ashur, and he carried them away into Golus, even the Reuveni, and the Gadi, and the half tribe of Menasheh, and brought them unto Chalach, and Chavor, and Hara, and to the river Gozan, unto this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> The Bnei Levi: Gershon, Kehat, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the Bnei Kehat: Amram, Yitzhar, and Chevron, and Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And the Bnei Amram: Aharon, and Moshe, and Miryam. The bnei Aharon; Nadav, and Avihu, El'azar, and Itamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> El'azar fathered Pinchas, Pinchas fathered Avishua,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Avishua fathered Bukki, and Bukki fathered Uzzi,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And Uzzi fathered Zerachyah, and Zerachyah fathered Merayot,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Merayot fathered Amaryah, and Amaryah fathered Achituv,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And Achituv fathered Tzadok, and Tzadok fathered Achima'atz,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And Achima'atz fathered Azaryah, and Azaryah fathered Yochanan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And Yochanan fathered Azaryah (he it is that executed the kohenʼs office in the Beis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hamikdash">b</a></sup>that Sh'lomo built in Yerushalayim),</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And Azaryah fathered Amaryah, and Amaryah fathered Achituv,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And Achituv fathered Tzadok, and Tzadok fathered Shallum,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And Shallum fathered Chilkiyah, and Chilkiyah fathered Azaryah,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And Azaryah fathered Serayah, and Serayah fathered Yehotzadak,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And Yehotzadak went into the Golus, when Hashem carried away Yehudah and Yerushalayim by the yad of Nevukhadnetzar.</span>
</div>
