<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 31:1–13; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 10:8; SHMUEL BAIS 1:6"> Now the Pelishtim (Philistines) fought against Yisroel; and the men of Yisroel fled from before the Pelishtim, and fell down chalalim (slain ones) on Mt Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 9:39; SHEMOT 20:5; SHMUEL ALEF 14:6"> And the Pelishtim followed hard after Sha'ul, and after his banim; and the Pelishtim slaughtered Yonatan, and Avinadav, and Malki-Shu'a, the Bnei Sha'ul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 49:23–24; SHMUEL ALEF 31:3–6; SHMUEL BAIS 1:4–10"> And the milchamah became intense against Sha'ul, and the archers overtook him, and he was wounded by the archers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 31:4; SHOFETIM 9:54; SHMUEL ALEF 17:26"> Then said Sha'ul to his noseh keli (armor bearer), Draw thy cherev, and thrust me through therewith; lest these arelim (uncircumcised ones) come and abuse me. But his noseh keli would not; for he was very afraid. So Sha'ul took a cherev, and fell upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And when his noseh keli saw that Sha'ul was dead, he fell likewise on the cherev, and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HOSHEA 13:10–11; SHMUEL ALEF 4:10–11; SHMUEL ALEF 4:18"> So Sha'ul died, and his shloshet banim, and all his Bais died together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:36; DEVARIM 28:33; SHOFETIM 6:2"> And when kol Ish Yisroel that were in the emek (valley) saw that they fled, and that Sha'ul and his banim were dead, then they abandoned their towns, and fled; and the Pelishtim came and took occupation of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 31:8; MELACHIM BAIS 3:23; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:25"> And it came to pass on the next day, when the Pelishtim came to strip the chalalim, that they found Sha'ul and his banim fallen on Mt Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DANIEL 5:23; SHOFETIM 16:23–24; MATTITYAHU 14:11"> And when they had stripped him, they took his rosh (head), and his keli (armor), and sent into eretz Pelishtim all around, to carry the news unto their atzabim, and to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 5:2–7; SHMUEL ALEF 31:10"> And they put his keli in the Bais eloheihem, and fastened his head in the Bais Dagon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 31:11–13; SHMUEL ALEF 11:1–11; SHMUEL BAIS 2:4–7"> And when kol Yavesh-Gil'ad heard all that the Pelishtim had done to Sha'ul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 35:8; SHMUEL BAIS 3:35; BERESHIS 50:10"> They arose, kol ish chayil, and took the gufat Sha'ul (body of Sha'ul), and the gufot of his banim, and brought them to Yavesh, and buried their atzmot under the elah (oak tree) in Yavesh, and underwent a tzom shivat yamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:6; VAYIKRA 19:31; SHMUEL ALEF 15:23"> So Sha'ul died for his ma'al (unfaithfulness) which he committed against Hashem, even against the Devar Hashem, over which he was not shomer, and also for consulting an ov (one with a familiar spirit) to seek guidance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:28; SHMUEL ALEF 13:14; SHMUEL ALEF 28:17"> And inquired not of Hashem; therefore He slaughtered him, and turned over the kingdom unto Dovid Ben Yishai.</span>
