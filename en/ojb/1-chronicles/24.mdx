<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:6; SHEMOT 6:23; BAMIDBAR 26:60"> Now these are the divisions of the Bnei Aharon; the Bnei Aharon; Nadav, and Avihu, El'azar, and Itamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="VAYIKRA 10:2; BAMIDBAR 3:4; BAMIDBAR 26:61"> But Nadav and Avihu died before avihem (their father), and had no banim; therefore El'azar and Itamar became Kohanim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 24:31; SHMUEL ALEF 21:1"> And Dovid with both Tzadok of the Bnei El'azar, and Achimelech of the Bnei Itamar, divided them according to their pekudim (offices, assignments) in their avodah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:24; BAMIDBAR 25:11–13; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:6–12"> And there were more Rashei HaGevarim found of the Bnei El'azar than of the Bnei Itamar, and thus were they divided. Among the Bnei El'azar there were sixteen Rashim l'Bais Avot, and eight among the Bnei Itamar according to the Bais of their Avot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 24:31; YEHOSHUA 18:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:1"> Thus were they divided by goralot (lots) with one another; for the Sarei Kodesh and Sarei HaElohim, were of the Bnei El'azar, and of the Bnei Itamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 18:16; MATTITYAHU 8:19; NECHEMYAH 8:1"> And Shema'yah ben Netan'el the Sofer, one of the Levi'im, wrote them before HaMelech, and the Sarim, and Tzadok HaKohen, and Achimelech ben Evyatar (Abiathar), and before the Rashei HaAvot of the Kohanim and Levi'im, Bais Avechad being chosen for El'azar, and one chosen for Itamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZRA 2:36; NECHEMYAH 11:10; NECHEMYAH 12:19"> Now the first goral (lot) came forth to Yehoyariv, the second to Yedayah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZRA 2:39; NECHEMYAH 7:35; NECHEMYAH 12:15"> The third to Charim, the fourth to Seorim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 12:17"> The fifth to Malkiyah, the sixth to Miyamin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKAS 1:5; NECHEMYAH 12:4; NECHEMYAH 12:17"> The seventh to Hakotz, the eighth to Aviyah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZRA 2:36; NECHEMYAH 12:10; NECHEMYAH 7:39"> The ninth to Yeshua, the tenth to Shekanyahu,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 12:10"> The eleventh to Elyashiv, the twelfth to Yakim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The thirteenth to Chuppah, the fourteenth to Yeshevav,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 7:40; EZRA 2:37; EZRA 10:20"> The fifteenth to Bilgah, the sixteenth to Immer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The seventeenth to Chezir, the eighteenth to Happitzetz,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The nineteenth to Petachyah, the twentieth to Yechezkel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The one and twentieth to Yachin, the two and twentieth to Gamul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The three and twentieth to Delayahu, the four and twentieth to Ma'azyahu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 9:25; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:40; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:11"> These were the pekudim (offices, assigments) of them in their avodah to come into the Bais Hashem, according to their mishpatim (regulations), under Aharon Avihem just as Hashem Elohei Yisroel had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:24; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:18"> And the rest of the Bnei Levi were these: Of the Bnei Amram: Shuva'el; of the Bnei Shuva'el: Yechdeyahu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:25"> Concerning Rechavyahu, of the Bnei Rechavyahu, the first was Yishiyah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:18; BAMIDBAR 3:27; BAMIDBAR 3:19"> Of the Yitzhari: Shelomot; of the Bnei Shelomot: Yachat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:19; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:31"> And &#91;the Bnei Chevron&#93;: Yeriyahu the first, Amaryahu the second, Yachaziel the third, Yekam'am the fourth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:20"> Of the Bnei Uzziel: Michah; of the Bnei Michah: Shamir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:20"> The brother of Michah was Yishiyah; of the Bnei Yishiyah: Zecharyah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:21"> The Bnei Merari were Machli and Mushi; the Bnei Ya'aziyahu, Beno.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:19"> The Bnei Merari: by Ya'aziyahu: Beno, and Shoham, and Zakkur, and Ivri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:22"> Of Machli came El'azar, who had no banim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Concerning Kish: the ben Kish was Yerachme'el.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:23; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:47"> The Bnei Mushi: Machli, and Eder, and Yerimot. These were the Bnei HaLevi'im after the Bais Avoteihem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 24:5–6; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:13; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 25:8"> These likewise cast goralot (lots) corresponding to their brethren the Bnei Aharon in the presence of Dovid HaMelech, and Tzadok, and Achimelech, and the Rashei HaAvot LaKohanim and Levi'im, even the Avot HaRosh as well as Achiv HaKatan.</span>
