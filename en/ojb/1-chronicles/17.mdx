<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:9; ZECHARYAH 8:23"> Then Natan said to Dovid, Do all that is in thine levav; for HaElohim is with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="AMOS 3:7; BAMIDBAR 12:6; YESHAYAH 30:21"> And it came to pass the same lailah, that the Devar Elohim came to Natan, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:2–3; YESHAYAH 55:8–9; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 6:8–9"> Go and tell Dovid Avdi, Thus saith Hashem, Thou shalt not build Me HaBayit to dwell in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 7:6; SHEMOT 40:2–3; MELACHIM ALEF 8:16"> For I have not dwelt in a Bayit since the day that I brought up Yisroel until this day, but have gone from ohel to ohel, and from one mishkan to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 7:7; BAMIDBAR 10:33–36; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:16"> Wheresoever I have walked with kol Yisroel, spoke I a word to any of the Shofetim of Yisroel, whom I commanded to shepherd My people, saying, Why have ye not built me a Bais Arazim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 6:21; SHEMOT 3:1–10; SHMUEL ALEF 17:15"> Now therefore thus shalt thou say unto Avdi Dovid, Thus saith Hashem Tzva'os, I took thee from the naveh, even from following the tzon, that thou shouldest be Nagid over My people Yisroel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 18:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:2; TEHILLIM 113:7–8"> And I have been with thee whithersoever thou hast walked, and have cut off all thine oyevim from before thee, and have made thee a shem like the shem of the gedolim that are in ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 2:23; YECHEZKEL 28:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:6"> Also I will ordain a makom for My people Yisroel, and will plant them, and they shall dwell in their place, and shall be moved no more; neither shall the bnei avel oppress them any more, as they did at the beginning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 7:11; SHOFETIM 2:14–18; SHEMOT 1:21"> And since the time that I commanded Shofetim to be over My people Yisroel. Moreover I will subdue all thine oyevim. Furthermore I tell thee that Hashem will build thee a Bais (House, Dynasty).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TEHILLIM 132:11; MELACHIM ALEF 1:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:5"> And it shall come to pass, when thy yamim are expired that thou must go to be with Avoteicha, that I will raise up thy zera after thee, which shall be of thy banim; and I will establish his Malchut (&#91;Messianic&#93; Kingdom).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 5:5; DANIEL 2:44; YESHAYAH 9:7"> He shall build for Me a Bais, and I will establish his kisse ad olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:5; YESHAYAH 55:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:12"> I will be his Av, and he shall be My Ben;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="HaElohim">a</a></sup>and I will not take My chesed away from him, as I took it from him that was before thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 89:36; LUKAS 1:32–33; TEHILLIM 72:17"> But I will set him in Mine Bais and in My Malchut ad olam, and his kisse shall be established ad olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 23:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:27; SHMUEL BAIS 7:17"> According to all these devarim, and according to all this chazon (revelation), so did Natan speak unto Dovid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 7:18; BERESHIS 32:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:8"> And Dovid HaMelech came and sat before Hashem, and said, Who am I, Hashem Elohim, and what is mine bais, that Thou hast brought me hitherto?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:11–15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:8–11; YESHAYAH 49:6"> And as if this were not sufficient in Thine eyes, O Elohim, Thou hast also spoken of Bais Avdecha in the future afar off, and hast regarded me according to the estate of ha'adam hama'alah (the man of high degree), O Hashem Elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YOCHANAN 21:17; SHMUEL ALEF 2:30; SHMUEL BAIS 7:20–24"> What more can Dovid speak to Thee regarding the kavod of Thy eved? For Thou hast da'as of Thy eved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 37:35; SHMUEL BAIS 7:21; DANIEL 9:17"> Hashem, for the sake of Thy eved, and according to Thine own lev (heart, will), hast Thou done all this gedullah (greatness), in making known kol hagedulot (all great things).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YESHAYAH 44:6; YIRMEYAH 10:6–7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:20"> Hashem, there is none like Thee, neither is there any Elohim zulah (besides) Thee, according to all that we have heard with our ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:9–10; YESHAYAH 63:9; YESHAYAH 48:9"> And what one goy (nation) in ha'aretz is like Thy people Yisroel, whom HaElohim went to redeem to be His own people, to make for Thee Shem Gedullot and Nora'ot, by driving out Goyim from before Thy people whom Thou hast redeemed out of Mitzrayim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:5–6; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:1–12"> For Thy people Yisroel didst Thou make Thine own people ad olam; Thou Hashem, became their Elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:49; YIRMEYAH 11:5; LUKAS 1:38"> Therefore now, Hashem, let the davar that Thou hast spoken concerning Thy eved and concerning his Bais be established ad olam, and do as Thou hast said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:13; YIRMEYAH 31:1; HISGALUS 21:3"> Let it even be established, that Thy Shem may be magnified ad olam, saying, Hashem Tzva'os is Elohei Yisroel, even Elohim to Yisroel; let Bais Dovid Avdecha be established before Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 9:15; YECHEZKEL 36:37; TEHILLIM 10:17"> For Thou, O Elohai, hast revealed in the ozen of Thy eved that Thou wilt build him a Bais; therefore Thy eved hath found to daven in prayer before Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:6–7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:18; LETTER TO TITOS 1:2"> Now, Hashem, Thou art HaElohim, and hast promised this tovah unto Thy eved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:29; TEHILLIM 72:17"> Now therefore let it please Thee to make brocha upon the Bais of Thy eved, that it may be before Thee l'olam; for Thou makest brocha Hashem, and it shall be mevorech l'olam (blessed forever).</span>
