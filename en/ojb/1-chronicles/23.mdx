<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The Bnei Shime'i; Shelomit, and Chaziel, and Haran, three. These were the rashei avot of La'dan.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEVARIM 33:1; TEHILLIM 90:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:23–25"> Now concerning Moshe the Ish HaElohim, his banim were reckoned of the Shevet Levi.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:25"> And the Bnei Eli'ezer were Rechavyah the rosh. And Eli'ezer had no banim acharim; but the Bnei Rechavyah were very many.</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 24:28; BAMIDBAR 36:6–8"> And El'azar died, and had no banim, only banot, and their brethren the Bnei Kish took them as wives.</span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="TEHILLIM 132:13–14; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:18; SHMUEL BAIS 7:11"> For Dovid said, Hashem Elohei Yisroel hath given rest unto His people, that they may dwell in Yerushalayim ad l'olam;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 4:5; BAMIDBAR 7:9; BAMIDBAR 4:15"> And also unto the Levi'im; they shall no more carry the Mishkan, nor any kelim (vessels) of it for the avodah thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:24; TEHILLIM 72:20; SHMUEL BAIS 23:1"> According to the last instructions of Dovid the Bnei Levi were numbered from those twenty shanah and above;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:3–6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 31:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:11–14"> Because their ma'amad (position) was to wait on the Bnei Aharon for the avodas Beis Hashem, in the khatzerot, and in the rooms, and in the tahorat (cleansing) of kol kodesh, and the work of the avodas Beis HaElohim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:35–36; SHEMOT 25:30; VAYIKRA 6:20–23"> Both for the Lechem HaMa'arechet (showbread), and for the solet (flour) for minchah, and for the cakes of matzot, and for that which is baked in the pan, and for that which is scalded, and for all manner of measure and size;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="TEHILLIM 92:1–3; EZRA 3:10–11; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 9:33"> And to stand baboker baboker to thank and praise Hashem, and likewise at erev (evening);</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YESHAYAH 1:13–14; BAMIDBAR 28:1–29; BAMIDBAR 10:10"> And to offer all olot (burnt offerings) unto Hashem on Shabbatot, at Rosh Chodesh, and at mo'adim, according to their number ordained for them, tamid before Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:53; BAMIDBAR 3:38; BAMIDBAR 3:6–9"> And that they should be shomer to keep the mishmeret (watch, guard responsibility) of the Ohel Mo'ed and the mishmeret of the Kodesh, and the mishmeret of the Bnei Aharon their brethren, in the avodas Beis Hashem.</span>
