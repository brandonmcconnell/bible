<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TEHILLIM 60:1; SHMUEL BAIS 8:3; BERESHIS 15:18"> And Dovid struck Hadadezer Melech Tzovah unto Chamat, as he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hadadezer">a</a></sup>went to establish his dominion over the river Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:4; MELACHIM ALEF 10:26; MELACHIM ALEF 4:2"> And Dovid captured from him elef chariots, and 7,000 parashim, and 20,000 foot soldiers. Dovid also hamstrung all the chariot horses, but reserved of them 100 chariot horses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 19:6; SHMUEL BAIS 8:5–6; SHMUEL ALEF 14:47"> And when Aram Darmesek came to help Hadadezer Melech Tzovah, Dovid slaughtered of the Syrians 22,000 ish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:8; TEHILLIM 121:8; MISHLE 21:31"> Then Dovid put garrisons in Aram Darmesek; and the Syrians became Dovidʼs avadim (subjects), bearing minchah (tribute). Thus Hashem saved Dovid wherever he went.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:15–16; MELACHIM ALEF 10:16–17; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:9–10"> And Dovid took the shields of zahav that were on avadim of Hadadezer, and brought them to Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:12–18; MELACHIM ALEF 7:15–47"> Likewise from Tivchat, and from Kun, towns of Hadadezer, Dovid took very much nechoshet, wherewith Sh'lomo made the Yam HaNechoshet, and the Ammudim, and the kelei hanechoshet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:9"> Now when To'u Melech Chamat heard how Dovid had struck down the whole army of Hadadezer Melech Tzovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:10; YESHAYAH 39:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:1"> He sent Hadoram bno to HaMelech Dovid, to give him a shalom greeting, and to congratulate him, because he had fought against Hadadezer, and struck him down (for Hadadezer was an ish milchamah often against To'u); and he sent all manner kelim zahav, kesef and nechoshet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 30:13; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:26–27; MELACHIM ALEF 7:51"> Them also Dovid HaMelech dedicated as kodesh unto Hashem, with the kesef and the zahav that he brought from all these Goyim; from Edom, and from Moav, and from the Bnei Ammon, and from the Pelishtim, and from Amalek.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 19:11; SHMUEL BAIS 10:10; SHMUEL BAIS 8:13–14"> Moreover Avishai Ben Tzeruyah slaughtered of Edom in the Gey HaMelach eighteen elef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 18:6; BERESHIS 27:37; TEHILLIM 144:10"> And he put garrisons in Edom, and kol Edom became Dovidʼs avadim. Thus Hashem saved Dovid wherever he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 33:15; TEHILLIM 89:14; TEHILLIM 78:71–72"> So Dovid reigned over kol Yisroel, and executed mishpat and tzedakah among all his people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:6; MELACHIM ALEF 4:3; SHMUEL BAIS 8:16"> And Yoav Ben Tzeruyah was over the tzava (army), and Yehoshaphat Ben Achilud was mazkir (recorder, secretary).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:17; SHMUEL BAIS 20:25; MELACHIM ALEF 4:3"> And Tzadok Ben Achituv, and Avimelech Ben Evyatar (Abiathar), were the Kohanim, and Shavsha was sofer (scribe),</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:18; SHMUEL BAIS 15:18; MELACHIM ALEF 2:34–35"> And Benayahu Ben Yehoyada was over the Kereti and the Peleti; and the Bnei Dovid were the rishonim (first ones) at the side of HaMelech.</span>
</div>
