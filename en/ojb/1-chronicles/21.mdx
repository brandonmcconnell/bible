<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 24:1–25; ZECHARYAH 3:1; IYOV 2:4–6"> And Satan stood up against Yisroel, and provoked Dovid to number Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 27:23–24; SHMUEL BAIS 3:10; SHMUEL BAIS 24:15"> And Dovid said to Yoav and to the sarei HaAm, Go, number Yisroel from Be'er-Sheva even to Dan; and bring the mispar (number) of them to me, that I may know it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:11; YESHAYAH 26:15; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 19:13"> But Yoav answered, Hashem make His people a hundred times so many more as they be, but, adoni HaMelech, are not they all the avadim of adoni? Why then doth adoni require this thing? Why will he be a cause of ashmah to Yisroel?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:29; SHEMOT 1:17; DANIEL 3:18"> Nevertheless the davar HaMelech prevailed against Yoav. Therefore, Yoav departed, and went throughout kol Yisroel, and came to Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 24:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 27:23"> And Yoav gave up the sum of the number of HaAm unto Dovid. And kol Yisroel were 1,100,000 ish that drew cherev; and Yehudah was 470,000 ish that drew cherev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 27:24; BAMIDBAR 1:47–49; SHMUEL BAIS 20:9–10"> But Levi and Binyamin counted he not among them, for the davar HaMelech was abominable to Yoav.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 15:5; YEHOSHUA 7:5; SHMUEL BAIS 21:14"> And HaElohim was displeased with this thing; therefore He struck Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 12:13; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9; BERESHIS 34:7"> And Dovid said unto HaElohim, I have sinned greatly, because I have done this thing, but now, take away the avon of Thy eved, for I have done very foolishly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 9:9; SHMUEL BAIS 24:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:29"> And Hashem spoke unto Gad, Chozeh Dovid (seer of David) saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 13:21–22; HISGALUS 3:19; MISHLE 1:29–31"> Go and tell Dovid, saying, Thus saith Hashem, I offer thee shalosh (three); choose thee one of them, that I may do it unto thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MISHLE 19:20"> So Gad came to Dovid, and said unto him, Thus saith Hashem, Choose for thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:23; YECHEZKEL 14:19–21"> Either shalosh shanim ra'av (famine); or shloshah chodashim to be subdued before thy foes, while that the cherev of thine oyevim overtaketh thee; or else sheloshet yamim cherev Hashem, even the dever, in HaAretz, and the Malach Hashem destroying throughout all the borders of Yisroel. Now therefore advise thyself what davar I shalll bring to Him that sent me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TEHILLIM 130:7; YESHAYAH 55:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:23"> And Dovid said unto Gad, I am in a great distress; let me fall now into the Yad Hashem; for rabbim me'od (very great) are His rachamim but let me not fall into the yad adam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:30; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 27:24; MELACHIM BAIS 19:35"> So Hashem sent dever upon Yisroel, and there fell of Yisroel shiv'im elef ish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 6:6; SHEMOT 32:14; YIRMEYAH 26:18"> And HaElohim sent a Malach unto Yerushalayim to destroy her, and as he was destroying, Hashem beheld, and He relented Him of the ra'ah, and said to the Malach that destroyed, It is enough, stay now thine yad. And the Malach Hashem stood by the goren (threshing floor) of Ornan the Yevusi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:5; MELACHIM ALEF 21:27; MELACHIM BAIS 6:17"> And Dovid lifted up his eyes, and saw the Malach Hashem stand between Ha'Aretz and HaShomayim, having a drawn cherev in his yad stretched out over Yerushalayim. Then Dovid and the Zekenim, who were clothed in sackcloth, fell upon their faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 16:63; SHMUEL BAIS 24:17; YOCHANAN 10:11–12"> And Dovid said unto HaElohim, Is it not I that commanded the people to be numbered? Even I it is that have sinned and done harah indeed; but as for these tzon, what have they done? Let Thine Yad, Hashem Elohai, be on me, and on the Bais Avi, but not on Thy people, not with magefah (plague).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Isa 53:8for fulfillment">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 3:1; SHMUEL BAIS 24:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:11"> Then the Malach Hashem commanded Gad to say to Dovid, that Dovid should go up, and set up a Mizbe'ach unto Hashem in the goren (threshing floor) of Ornan the Yevusi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:6; YOCHANAN 2:5; MELACHIM BAIS 5:10–14"> And Dovid went up at the davar of Gad, which he spoke B'Shem Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHOFETIM 6:11"> And Ornan turned back, and saw the Malach, and his four banim with him hid themselves. Now Ornan was threshing chittim (wheat).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:23; SHMUEL BAIS 24:18–20"> And as Dovid came to Ornan, Ornan looked and saw Dovid, and went out from the goren (threshing floor), and prostated himself to Dovid with his face to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 24:21; MELACHIM ALEF 21:2; BAMIDBAR 25:8"> Then Dovid said to Ornan, Give me the makom of this goren (threshing floor), that I may build a Mizbe'ach therein unto Hashem; thou shalt give it me for the kesef male (full price), that the magefah (plague) may be withdrawn from HaAm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 24:22–23; SHMUEL ALEF 6:14; BERESHIS 23:4–6"> And Ornan said unto Dovid, Take it to thee, and let adoni HaMelech do that which is tov in his eyes; see, I give thee the bakar also for olot (burnt offerings), and the threshing tools for wood, and the chittim (wheat) for the minchah (grain offering, meal offering); I give it all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:16–17; BERESHIS 14:23; BERESHIS 23:13"> And HaMelech Dovid said to Ornan, Lo (no), but I will verily buy it for the kesef male (full price), for I will not take that which is thine for Hashem, nor offer olah (burnt offerings) without cost.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 24:24–25"> So Dovid gave to Ornan for the makom (place, site) six hundred shekels of zahav by weight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="VAYIKRA 9:24; SHOFETIM 6:21; MISHLE 15:8"> And Dovid built there a Mizbe'ach unto Hashem, and offered olot (burnt offerings) and shelamim (peace offerings), and called upon Hashem, and He answered him from Shomayim by eish upon the Mizbe'ach HaOlah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:11; TEHILLIM 103:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:15–16"> And Hashem commanded the Malach, and He put back his cherev again into the nedan (sheath) thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> At that time when Dovid saw that Hashem had answered him on the goren (threshing floor) of Ornan the Yevusi, then he sacrificed there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 16:39; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 1:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 1:13"> For the Mishkan Hashem, which Moshe made in the midbar, and the Mizbe'ach HaOlah, were at that time in the bamah (high place) at Giv'on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:28–29; HISGALUS 15:4"> But Dovid could not go before it to inquire of Elohim, for he was afraid because of the cherev Malach Hashem.</span>
</div>
</div>
