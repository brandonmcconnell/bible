</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:17; MELACHIM ALEF 9:20–21; MELACHIM ALEF 5:17–18"> And Dovid commanded to gather together the gerim (aliens) that were in Eretz Yisroel; and he set masons to carve hewn stones to build the Beis HaElohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:2; MELACHIM ALEF 7:47"> And Dovid prepared barzel (iron) in abundance for the masmerim (nails) for the daletot (doors) of the she'arim, and for the joints; and nechoshet unweighable in abundance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 5:6–10; EZRA 3:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:3"> Also cedar timber in abundance, for the Tzidonim and the Tzorim brought much cedar lumber to Dovid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 3:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:1; YOCHANAN 3:30"> And Dovid said, Sh'lomo beni (my son) is na'ar and tender, and the Bayit that is to be built for Hashem must be exceeding magnificent, of fame and of tiferet before all nations. I will therefore now make preparation for it. So Dovid prepared abundantly before his mot (death).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 27:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:25–31; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:2"> Then he called for Sh'lomo bno, and charged him to build Bayit (House) for Hashem Elohei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 132:5; MELACHIM ALEF 8:16–20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 6:7–9"> And Dovid said to Sh'lomo, Beni (my son), as for me, it was in my mind to build Bayit (House) for the Shem of Hashem Elohai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 5:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:4–10"> But the Devar Hashem came to me, saying, Thou hast done much shefach dahm, and hast made milchamot gedolot. Thou shalt not build Bayit (House) unto Shemi (My Name), because thou hast done much shefach dahm upon ha'aretz in My sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 4:25; MELACHIM ALEF 5:4; MELACHIM ALEF 4:20"> Hinei, Ben shall be born to thee, who shall be an ish menuchah (man of rest); and I will give him rest from all his oyevim all around; for shmo shall be Sh'lomo, and I will grant shalom and sheket (quiet) unto Yisroel in his yamim (days).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:5; SHMUEL BAIS 7:13–14; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:12–14"> He shall build Bayit (House) for Shmi (My Name); and he shall be to Me as Ben and I will be to him as Av (Father); and I will establish the kisse of his Malchut over Yisroel ad olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:20; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:22"> Now, beni (my son), Hashem be with thee; and prosper thou, to succeed building the Beis Hashem Eloheicha, just as He hath said about thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5; MISHLE 14:8; MELACHIM ALEF 3:9–12"> Only Hashem give thee seichel and binah, so that when He give you command over Yisroel, that thou mayest be shomer over the torat Hashem Eloheicha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 1:6–9; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:7"> Then shalt thou prosper, if thou art shomer to observe the chukkim and mishpatim which Hashem charged Moshe with concerning Yisroel. Chazak (be strong)! And be of good courage! Dread nothing in fear, nor be dismayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:3; MELACHIM BAIS 25:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:4–7"> Now, hinei, in my oni (poor strivings) I have prepared for the Beis Hashem a hundred elef talents of zahav, and an elef alafim talents of kesef; and of nechoshet and barzel (iron) an unweighable abundance; timber also and even (stone) have I provided; and thou mayest add thereto.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:6; SHEMOT 35:32; MELACHIM ALEF 7:14"> Moreover there are workmen with thee in abundance, hewers and carvers of even (stone) and etz (wood), and all manner of artisans chacham in every task.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:14"> Of the zahav, the kesef, and the nechoshet, and the barzel, there is no number. Arise therefore, and begin and work, and Hashem be with thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:1–6; (III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:6"> Dovid also commanded all the sarim of Yisroel to help Sh'lomo bno, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:25; SHMUEL BAIS 7:1"> Is not Hashem Eloheichem with you? And hath He not given you rest on every side? For He hath given the inhabitants of ha'aretz into mine yad, and ha'aretz is subdued before Hashem, and before His people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:9; DEVARIM 4:29; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 16:11"> Now set your levav and your nefesh to seek Hashem Eloheichem; arise therefore, and build ye the Mikdash Hashem HaElohim, to bring Aron Brit Hashem, and the K'lei Kodesh HaElohim (Holy Vessels of G-d), into the Bayit that is to be built to the Shem of Hashem.</span>
</div>
