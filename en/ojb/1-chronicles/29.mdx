<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:5; MELACHIM ALEF 3:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 13:7"> Furthermore Dovid HaMelech said unto kol HaKahal, Sh'lomo beni (my son), the one Elohim hath chosen, is yet na'ar and tender, and the melachah (work, task) is gedolah; for the palace is not for adam, but for Hashem Elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:3–5; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:3; YESHAYAH 54:11–12"> Now I have provided with all my ko'ach for the Bais Elohai the zahav for things to be made of zahav, and the kesef for things of kesef, and the nechoshet for things of nechoshet, the barzel (iron) for things of barzel (iron), and etzim for things of etzim; onyx stones to be set, turquoise, and stones of various colors, and all manner of precious stones, and marble, all in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:24; TEHILLIM 27:4; TEHILLIM 26:8"> Moreover, because I have set my affection on the Bais Elohai, I have of mine own segullah (treasure) zahav and kesef, which I have given to the Bais Elohai, over and above all that I have prepared for the Beis HaKodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:28; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:14; IYOV 28:16"> Even shloshet alafim talents of zahav, of the zahav of Ophir, and shivat alafim talents of refined kesef, to overlay the walls of the buildings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 7:2–3; SHEMOT 35:5–9; EZRA 7:15–16"> The zahav for things of zahav, and the kesef for things of kesef, and for all manner of melachah (work) to be made by the hands of charashim (artisans). And who then is willing to consecrate his yad today unto Hashem?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 27:25–1; YESHAYAH 60:3–10"> Then the sarei ha'avot and the sarei shivtei Yisroel and the sarei ha'alafim and of me'ot, with the sarei melechet hamelech (officials over the work of the King), made freewill offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 7:70–71; EZRA 2:69"> And gave for the Avodas Beis HaElohim five thousand talents and ten thousand darkons of zahav, and ten thousand talents of kesef, and eighteen thousand talents of nechoshet, and one hundred thousand talents of barzel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:21–22"> And they with whom precious stones were found gave them to the Otzer Bais Hashem, by the yad of Yechiel the Gershoni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:7–8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:3; MELACHIM ALEF 8:61"> Then the people rejoiced, for theirs were freewill offerings, because with lev shalem they offered willingly to Hashem; and Dovid HaMelech also rejoiced with simchah gedolah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:3; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:7"> Wherefore Dovid blessed Hashem before the eyes of kol HaKahal; and Dovid said, Baruch atah Adonoi Elohei Yisroel Avinu meOlam v'ad olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:3; DANIEL 4:34–35; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:17"> Thine, Hashem is HaGedulah, and HaGevurah, and HaTiferet and HaNetzach and HaHod; for all that is in the Shomayim and in Ha'aretz is Thine; Thine is the Mamlachah, O Hashem, and Thou art exalted as Rosh above all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:6; DEVARIM 8:18"> Both osher and kavod come of Thee, and Thou art Moshel over all; and in Thine yad is ko'ach and gevurah; and in Thine yad it is to make great, and to give strength unto all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TEHILLIM 106:1; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:15; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:16"> Now therefore, Eloheinu, we thank Thee, and praise Thy Shem Tiferet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:17; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:36"> But who am I, and what is my people, that we should have ko'ach to make this freewill offering? For all things come of Thee, and of Thine own have we given Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 144:4; VAYIKRA 25:23; TEHILLIM 102:11"> For we are gerim before Thee, and toshavim (resident aliens), as were kol Avoteinu; our yamim on ha'aretz are as a tzel (shadow), and there is no mikveh (hope).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:14; TEHILLIM 24:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 31:10"> Hashem Eloheinu, all this great abundance that we have provided to build Thee a Bais for Shem Kadshecha cometh of Thine yad, and is all Thine own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 17:10; MISHLE 11:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:9"> I know also, Elohai, that Thou doth test the levav, and hast pleasure in meisharim (integrities, uprightness, equity). As for me, in the yosher levavi I have made freewill offerings of all these things, and now have I seen with simchah Thy people, which are present here, willingly offer freewill offerings unto Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:39; YIRMEYAH 10:23; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:21"> Hashem Elohei Avraham, Yitzchak, and of Yisroel, Avoteinu, be shomer over this l'olam in the yetzer (inclination) of the machshevot (thoughts) of the levav of Thy people, and set their lev unto Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="TEHILLIM 72:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:9; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:17"> And give unto Sh'lomo beni a levav shalem, to be shomer over Thy mitzvot, Thy edot, and Thy chukkot, and to do all these things, and to build the Bayit, for the which I have made provision.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 95:6; SHEMOT 4:31; TEHILLIM 135:19–21"> And Dovid said to kol HaKahal, Barchu nah es Hashem Eloheichem. And kol HaKahal blessed Hashem Elohei Avoteihem, and bowed down, and fell prostrate before Hashem and before HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:4–9; BAMIDBAR 15:7; EZRA 6:17"> And they sacrificed zevakhim unto Hashem, and offered olot unto Hashem, on the next day after that yom, even a thousand parim (bulls), a thousand eilim (rams), and a thousand kevasim, with their nesakhim, and zevakhim in abundance for kol Yisroel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:35; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:1; SHEMOT 24:11"> And did eat and drink before Hashem on that day with simchah gedolah. And they crowned Sh'lomo Ben Dovid Melech the second time, and anointed him unto Hashem to be Nagid (ruler), and Tzadok to be Kohen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:12; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:1"> Then Sh'lomo sat on the kisse Hashem as Melech instead of Dovid Aviv, and prospered, and kol Yisroel obeyed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:3–9; YECHEZKEL 17:18; BERESHIS 24:2"> And all the sarim, and the gibborim, and all the Bnei HaMelech Dovid submitted themselves unto Sh'lomo HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 1:12; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 1:1; KOHELET 2:9"> And Hashem magnified Sh'lomo exceedingly in the eyes of all Yisroel, and bestowed upon him such hod malchut (royal majesty) as had not been on any melech before him in Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 18:14; TEHILLIM 78:71–72"> Thus Dovid Ben Yishai reigned over kol Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:11; SHMUEL BAIS 5:4–5; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:4"> And the time that he reigned over Yisroel was arba'im shanah; sheva shanim reigned he in Chevron, and thirty and three reigned he in Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:36; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:1"> And he died in a good old age, full of yamim, oisher, and kavod; and Sh'lomo bno reigned in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 9:9; SHMUEL BAIS 12:1–7; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:9–11"> Now the acts of Dovid HaMelech, first and last, behold, they are written in the Divrei Shmuel HaRo'eh, and in the Divrei Natan HaNavi, and in the Divrei Gad the chozeh (seer),</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DANIEL 4:25; DANIEL 4:23; DANIEL 2:21"> With all his malchut and his gevurah, and the times that passed around him, and around Yisroel, and over kol mamlechot ha'aratzot (kingdoms of the lands).</span>
</div>
