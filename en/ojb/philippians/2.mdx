<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:16; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:8–9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:10"> Then make my simcha (joy) shleimah (complete) by having the same lev, the same ahavah, being an agudah association with one neshamah, thinking the same machshavot (thoughts),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:21"> Doing nothing according to anochiyut<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Pp 1:17">a</a></sup>(selfishness) nor according to empty ga'avah (conceit, haughtiness, arrogance), but in anavah (humility), fergin (graciously grant) each other esteem above yourselves.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ro 12:10">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:1; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:8"> Let each of you talmidim look after not only your own interests, but also the best interests of others.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2:21; 1C 10:24,33">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:6; MATTITYAHU 11:29; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:21"> Let this mind be in you which was also in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:18; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:3"> Who, though existing in the demut of the mode of being of Elohim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="His etzem or essential nature, Yn 1:1-2; 17:5">d</a></sup>nevertheless Moshiach did not regard being equal with G-d as a thing to be seized,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gn 3:5">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:9; MATTITYAHU 20:28; YOCHANAN 1:14"> But poured out and emptied himself,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="2C 8:9">f</a></sup>taking the demut of the mode of being of an eved,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 52:13—53:12T.N. see the AVDI TZADDIK TZEMACH DOVID MOSHIACH Jer 23:5; Zech 3:8">g</a></sup>and was born in the likeness of Bnei Adam,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Yn 1:14; Ro 8:3; MJ 2:14-17">h</a></sup>and having been found in appearance as an Adam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:18; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:19"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach humbled himself and took the path of shiflut (lowliness), unto mishma'at<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Pp 2:12">i</a></sup>(obedience)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="cf. Gn 3:17">j</a></sup>even unto death,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Yn 10:17; MJ 5:8; 12:2">k</a></sup>and that, a death on HaEtz.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="the Tree, Dt 21:23; 27:26; Ga 3:13; Pp 3:18">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:16; MATTITYAHU 28:18; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:22"> Therefore, also Hashem exalted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Isa 52:13; 53:12; Dan 9:26; 7:14; Ac 2:33; MJ 1:3">m</a></sup>Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, and gave to him haShem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Ep 1:21; MJ 1:4">n</a></sup>above every name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YESHAYAH 45:23–25; MATTITYAHU 28:18; HISGALUS 5:13–14"> That at haShem of Yehoshua, KOL BERECH (every knee) will bow,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Isa 45:23">o</a></sup>of beings b'Shomayim and ba'Aretz and mitachat la'Aretz (in the world below),</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:11; YOCHANAN 13:13"> And KOL LASHON<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Isa 45:23">p</a></sup>(every tongue) shall make hoda'ah (confession) with an Ani Ma'amin that is an open and public admission that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua (Yeshua) is Adoneinu, to the kavod of Elohim Avinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5–10; LUKAS 13:23–24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:58"> Therefore, Chaverim, just as you have always had mishma'at<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Pp 2:8">q</a></sup>(obedience) concerning me, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your own Yeshu'ah (Salvation)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="1:19[28]">r</a></sup>B'YIR'AH (with fear) and BIRA'DAH “with trembling”.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Ps 2:11">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:21; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:5; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:9"> For Hashem is the one working in you, both to will and to work according to His chafetz (good pleasure of His will).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Ezra 1:5">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:3"> Do all things without murmurings and madon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:14–16; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:12; MATTITYAHU 5:45"> That you may be innocent and without michshol of blame,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="1:10">u</a></sup>bnei haElohim TAMIM U'MUM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Ex 12:5; Lv 22:20; Isa 53:7-9">v</a></sup>(unblemished and unspotted) in the midst of a DOR IKKESH UFETALTOL (warped and crooked generation),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Dt 32:5">w</a></sup>among whom you shine as the ZOHAR<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Dan 12:3">x</a></sup>in the Olam Hazeh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:2; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:1"> Holding fast the Dvar HaChayyim. This is so that I will have reason for glorying on the Yom HaMoshiach,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Pp 1:6,10">y</a></sup>that I neither ran nor labored L'TOHU (in vain).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Isa 49:4; 65:23">z</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:16; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:15–16"> And, indeed, if my neshamah is to be poured out as a nesekh (libation wine) offering upon the korban (sacrifice) of the avodas kodesh (kohenʼs service, ministry) of your emunah, I have simcha, rejoicing together with you all.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="cf. Ro 15:16; 2Ti 4:6">aa</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:2–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:1"> And in the same way also you have simcha, rejoicing together with me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="1:4,18; 1:25; 2:2,17">ab</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:6–8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:2"> I have tikvah b'Adoneinu Yehoshua to send Timotiyos to you soon, that I also may be cheered up in the da'as of the things concerning you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:22"> For I have no one like-minded who will emesdik (genuinely) care for your spiritual welfare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:24; MATTITYAHU 16:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:4"> All of them are seeking their own interest,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="2:4">ac</a></sup>not those of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:17; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:9; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:2"> But of the proven mamashus (real worth) of Timotiyos you have da'as, because as a ben with his abba, Timotiyos served with me in the Besuras HaGeulah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 22:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:19"> Therefore, I have tikvah to send him, as soon as I see how things will go with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:14; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:25–26"> But I have bitachon in Hashem that indeed I will come quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:18; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:1–2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:3"> But I considered it necessary to send to you Epaphroditus, the Ach b'Moshiach and fellow po'el (worker) and fellow chaiyal (soldier) of mine, but your shliach and keli kodesh (minister) of my need.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="4:18">ad</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:15; MISHLE 12:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:13"> I am sending him to you, because he was yearning after you all and was under zeiyar (extremely great) stress, because you heard that he was ill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="TEHILLIM 34:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:30; TEHILLIM 103:3–4"> For indeed he was ill, coming near to death, but Hashem had mercy on him not on him only but also on me, lest I should have agmat nefesh upon agmat nefesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:22; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:26–27"> Therefore, I am all the more eager to dispatch him to you, in order that when you have seen him again, you may have simcha and I may have less agmat nefesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:18; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:17"> Therefore, receive him in Adoneinu with all simcha and hold esteem for such klei kodesh (ministers),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="1C 16:8,16; 1Ti 5:17">ae</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:10"> Because he came near to death on account of the avodas kodesh of Moshiach, performing an act of Messianic mesirat nefesh (whole-hearted devotion)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="to the cause of Moshiach, even at risk of life">af</a></sup>having risked his life, that he might make up for the ministry to me that you could not give.</span>
