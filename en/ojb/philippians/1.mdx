<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:7; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:2; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:2"> Chen v'chesed Hashem and shalom Hashem from Elohim Avinu and Adoneinu Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:2–3; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:4–5"> Modeh Ani (I thank) my G-d upon every zikaron (remembrance) of you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:9; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:4"> Always in every tefillah of mine on behalf of all of you, davening with simcha, making techinnah (supplication)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:14–15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:14"> In view of your deveykus Hashem, your chavrusa partnership in the Besuras HaGeulah from day one until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:23–24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:12"> Being convinced of this: that He who began a pe'ulah hatovah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Pp 2:13">a</a></sup>(good work) in you, will bring that mitzvoh to completion<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 138:8">b</a></sup>by Yom Hashem, the Yom Adoneinu, Moshiach Yehoshua.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Pp 1:10; 2:16">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:18"> It is in fact b'tzedek for me to have hitbonenut (profound contemplation) about you all because I hold you in my lev with hartzige (heartfelt) sincerity, both in regard to my sharsherot (prison chains) and in the apologetic hitstaddekut (defense) and vindicatory work for the Besuras HaGeulah, you being all deveykus (attachment to G-d) participators in the Chen v'Chesed Hashem given to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:9; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:4"> For der Oybershter is mine eidus (G-d is my witness) how I yearn for all of you with the ahavas Moshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:9; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5–6"> And my tefillah (prayer) is this, that your ahavah (love)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="in Moshiach">d</a></sup>may yet increase more and more in profound da'as and binah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:23"> To help you approve the feste (excellent) things of musar (moral discipline) that matter, that you may be sincere and without michshol (stumbling block) of blame<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2:15">e</a></sup>in the Yom HaMoshiach,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1:6; 2:16">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:11; YOCHANAN 15:8; YOCHANAN 15:4–5"> Having been filled with the p'ri Tzedek (fruit of righteousness) through Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua to the kavod Hashem and His tiferet (splendor).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:19–21"> I want you to have da'as, Achim b'Moshiach, that the things concerning me have fallen out to an even greater advancement<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Pp 1:25">g</a></sup>of the Besuras HaGeulah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="2Ti 2:9">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:7"> So that my sharsherot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Pp 1:7">i</a></sup>(chains) have become a hisgalus (revelation) in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach! In all the imperial guard and the public at large!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:19–20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:2; LUKAS 1:74"> And most of the Achim b'Adoneinu, having been strengthened in bitachon by my sharsherot (chains), are more readily willing to dare to fearlessly proclaim the Dvar Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:17–18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:13"> Indeed some darshanim (preachers) preach their derashot (sermons) out of a ruach that is mekanne (jealous, envious) and one of madon (strife); but some have derashot proclaiming Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, and these darshanim have kavvanah tovah (good intention).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:17; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:1–2; TEHILLIM 69:26"> These latter proclaim Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach out of a ruach of ahavah, having da'as that the purpose of my divinely destined appointment here is for the hitstaddekut (apologetic defense) of the Besuras HaGeulah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:11–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:7"> But those other ones preach Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach out of anochiyut<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Pp 2:3">j</a></sup>(selfishness), not with a lev tahor (pure in heart) motive, lu yetzuuyar (assuming) by means of my sharsherot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Pp 1:13-14">k</a></sup>to stir up tzoros.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKAS 9:50; MARKOS 9:38–40; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:11"> Nu? Whether the maggidim are or are not perfect in their intent, the significant thing is that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach is preached! And in this I have simcha. And I will go right on having simcha!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:7; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:11"> For, I have da'as that through your tefillos for me and through the ezrah (aid) given by the Ruach Moshiach, this will turn out for my Yeshu'ah (rescue, salvation).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Pp 1:28; 2:12; Ro 8:28">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:20; YESHAYAH 54:4"> It is my confident tikvah that in nothing I will be put to bushah (shame), but with all ometz lev<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Josh 1:7">m</a></sup>(courage, boldness) as always, even now, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach will be magnified in my basar,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Pp 1:13">n</a></sup>whether through Chayyim (Life) or through Mavet (Death).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:20; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:1"> For to me to live is Moshiach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Ga 2:20">o</a></sup>and to die is revach (gain, profit, hanaa).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:24"> But if it means I am to go on living in the basar, this for me is p'ri for Hashem in the work of avodas kodesh ministry. What will I choose? I do not have da'as,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:8; TEHILLIM 17:15; HISGALUS 14:13"> I am kleir (deliberating). I am pulled two drakhim (ways) by a happy dilemma, on the one tzad (side) having the desire to depart and be with Moshiach, for this is much better.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="2C 5:8">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:25–26"> But on the other tzad (side) to remain in the basar is more necessary for your sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:24; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:11–13"> And having been convinced of this fact, I have da'as that I blaib (remain, continue unchanged) and will continue with all of you, for your advancement<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Pp 1:12">q</a></sup>and simcha of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Orthodox Jewish">r</a></sup>Emunah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="cf. 1:27; 1Ti 3:9; 4:1,6; 5:8; 6:10,21; Yd 3">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:14; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:3"> With the ultimate aim that by coming to you again your glorying may abound in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:13–14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:10"> Only one thing: conduct in practice your torat haEzrakhut<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="see Pp 3:20">t</a></sup>(citizenship) worthily of the Besuras HaGeulah of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach. Then, whether I come and see you or am absent, I may hear concerning you that you are standing like a ma'oz (fortress, bastion) in one ruach, with one neshamah, contending for the Emunah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Yd 3; Pp 4:3">u</a></sup>of the Besuras HaGeulah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:7–8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:5–6; MATTITYAHU 10:28"> And, I might add, not having pachad (fear, terror) in reaction to anything as far as the mitnaggedim (the ones opposing) are concerned, which is a proof to them of their churban (destruction), but of your Yeshu'ah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Pp 1:19; 2:12">v</a></sup>(salvation ), and this from Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:2; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:41"> For to you it was given on behalf of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, not only to have emunah in him but also on behalf of him to be laden with the burden of Messianic yissurim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Pp 3:10">w</a></sup>(sufferings),</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:19–40; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:7"> With you having the same struggle, which you saw in me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Ac 16:22">x</a></sup>and now hear to be in me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Pp 1:13">y</a></sup></span>
