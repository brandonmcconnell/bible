<div className="bp poetry">

</div>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MICHOH 3:11; YESHAYAH 56:11; LETTER TO TITOS 1:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">They</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., the kohanim">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">feed on the chattat (sin) of Ami,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they relish their avon (iniquity).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YESHAYAH 24:2; YIRMEYAH 5:31; MATTITYAHU 15:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">And there shall be, like Am (People), like kohen;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will visit them with punishment for their ways,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and repay them for their doings.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MICHOH 6:14; VAYIKRA 26:26; CHAGGAI 1:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they shall eat, and not have enough;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they shall commit whoredom, and shall not increase;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they have abandoned being shomer to heed Hashem.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MISHLE 20:1; LUKAS 21:34; YESHAYAH 28:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Zenut (prostitution, whoredom) and yayin and tirosh</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">take away the lev (understanding).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 2:27; HOSHEA 5:4; CHABAKUK 2:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ami (My people) consult a piece of wood,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their staff declareth unto them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the ruach zenunim hath caused them to err,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they have played the zonah, forsaking Eloheihem.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YESHAYAH 1:29; YIRMEYAH 3:6; YECHEZKEL 6:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">They sacrifice upon the tops of the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and burn incense upon the hills,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">under oak and poplar and terebinth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because the shade thereof is tov;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">therefore your daughters shall commit whoredom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and your daughters-in-law shall commit ni'uf (adultery).</span><br /></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HOSHEA 12:11; HOSHEA 9:15; AMOS 4:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Though thou, Yisroel, play the zonah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">yet let not Yehudah become guilty;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and come not ye unto Gilgal,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">neither go ye up to Beit-Aven (House of Iniquity),</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="pejorative name for Bethel">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor swear, Chai Hashem (As the L-rd lives).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 7:24; YIRMEYAH 8:5; YIRMEYAH 3:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">For Yisroel slideth back</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="i.e., is stubborn, rebellious, unmanageable">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like a backsliding parah (stubborn, rebellious cow);</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">can Hashem now feed them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like a lamb in a wide field?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TEHILLIM 81:12; HISGALUS 22:11; HOSHEA 13:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ephrayim</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="i.e., the Northern Kingdom, Israel">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">is joined to atzabim (idols);</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">leave him alone.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MICHOH 3:11; AMOS 5:12; MELACHIM BAIS 17:7–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">When their drink is sour,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="i.e., when intoxicated">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they have committed whoredom;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">her mogenim (shields, rulers) deeply love kalon (ignominy, shame).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 1:29; YIRMEYAH 4:11–12; HOSHEA 13:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wind hath bound her up in her wings,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their zevakhot (sacrifices) shall bring them bushah (shame).</span></span>
