<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:50; MELACHIM BAIS 8:16–17; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:16"> Now Yehoshaphat slept with his avot, and was buried with his avot in Ir Dovid. And Yehoram bno reigned in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 23:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 24:5"> And he had achim, the Bnei Yehoshaphat, Azaryah, and Yechiel, and Zecharyahu, and Azaryahu, and Micha'el, and Shephatyahu. All these were the Bnei Yehoshaphat Melech Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:23; DEVARIM 21:15–17; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:5"> And their av have given them mattanot rabbot of kesef, and of zahav, and of precious things, with fortified cities in Yehudah, but the Mamlachah (Kingdom) he gave to Yehoram; because he was the bechor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHOFETIM 9:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 21:17; BERESHIS 4:8"> Now when Yehoram was risen up to the Mamlachah of his av, he strengthened himself, and slaughtered all his achim with the cherev, and also a number of the sarim (leading political figures) of Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 8:17–22"> Yehoram was thirty and two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned shemoneh shanim in Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 18:1; MELACHIM ALEF 12:28–30; MELACHIM BAIS 8:18"> And he walked in the derech of the melachim of Yisroel, as did the Bais Ach'av, for he had the Bat Ach'av to wife, and he wrought that which was rah in the eyes of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:36; TEHILLIM 132:17–18; SHMUEL BAIS 7:12–17"> However, Hashem was not willing to destroy the Bais Dovid, for the sake of HaBrit that He had cut with Dovid, and since He promised to give a ner (lamp) to him and to his banim kol hayamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 21:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:22–23; BERESHIS 27:40"> In the days of Yehoram, Edom rebelled from under the yad Yehudah, and made themselves a melech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then Yehoram went forth with his sarim (princes), and all his chariots with him, and he rose up by lailah, and struck down Edom which had surrounded him, and the commanders of the chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:2; YIRMEYAH 2:13; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 13:10"> So the Edomites rebelled from under the yad Yehudah unto this day. At the same time also did Livnah rebel from under his yad, because he had forsaken Hashem Elohei Avotav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:5; MELACHIM ALEF 11:7; VAYIKRA 17:7"> Moreover he built high places<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="unlawful altars">a</a></sup>in the mountains of Yehudah and caused the inhabitants of Yerushalayim to commit fornication, and led Yehudah astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:2–5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 17:3–4; MELACHIM BAIS 2:1"> And there came a miktav (letter) to him from Eliyahu HaNavi, saying, Thus saith Hashem Elohei Dovid Avicha, Because thou hast not walked in the darkhei Yehoshaphat Avicha, nor in the darkhei Asa Melech Yehudah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 21:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 21:11; MELACHIM ALEF 16:30–33"> But hast walked in the derech of the melachim of Yisroel, and hast made Yehudah and the inhabitants of Yerushalayim to go a-whoring, like the whoredoms of the Bais Ach'av, and also hast murdered thy achim of Bais Avicha, which were better than thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:21; SHEMOT 20:5; HOSHEA 5:11"> Hinei, with a maggefah gedolah (great plague) will Hashem strike thy people, and thy banim, and thy nashim, and all thy possessions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 21:18–19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:18; DEVARIM 28:67"> And thou shalt have cholayim rabbim with machla (disease) of thy bowels, until thy bowels come out by reason of the choli yamim al yamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 17:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:11; MELACHIM ALEF 11:14"> Moreover Hashem stirred up against Yehoram the ruach HaPelishtim, and of the Arabs, who lived near the Cushim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 22:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 22:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:23"> And they came up against Yehudah, and invaded, and carried away all the possessions that were found in the Bais HaMelech, and also his banim, and his nashim; so that there was left him not a ben except Yehoachaz (Achazyahu, Ahaziah), the youngest of his banim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 21:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:23; MELACHIM BAIS 9:29"> And after all this Hashem struck him in his bowels with a choli for which there was no marpeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 16:14; YIRMEYAH 34:5"> And it came to pass that in process of time, after the end of two years, his bowels protruded out by reason of his choli; so he died in great pain. And his people kindled no pyre in his honor, as they had done for his avot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 22:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:27; YIRMEYAH 22:28"> Thirty and two years old was he when he began to reign, and he reigned in Yerushalayim shemoneh shanim, and departed without chemdah (desire &#91;for him&#93;). Howbeit they buried him in the Ir Dovid, but not in the kevarot hamelachim.</span>
