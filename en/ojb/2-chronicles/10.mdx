<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:6; YEHOSHUA 20:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:38"> And Rechav'am went to Shechem, for to Shechem were kol Yisroel come to make him melech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:40; MELACHIM ALEF 12:2; MELACHIM ALEF 11:28"> And it came to pass, when Yarov'am ben Nevat, who was in Mitzrayim, where he fled from the presence of Sh'lomo HaMelech heard it, that Yarov'am returned out of Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:3"> And they sent and called him. So Yarov'am and kol Yisroel came and spoke to Rechav'am, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:4; SHEMOT 2:23; MATTITYAHU 11:29–30"> Avicha made our ol (yoke) kasheh (difficult, harsh); now therefore ease thou somewhat the grievous avodas Avicha, and his heavy ol (yoke)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., taxes, see verse 18">a</a></sup>that he put upon us, and we will serve thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:5; MISHLE 3:28"> And he said unto them, Shuvu (come back, return) unto me after shloshet yamin. And HaAm departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="IYOV 32:7; MISHLE 19:20; MISHLE 27:10"> And HaMelech Rechav'am took counsel with the Zekenim that had stood before Sh'lomo Avi while he yet lived, saying, What counsel give ye me to return answer to HaAm HaZeh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MISHLE 15:1; BERESHIS 49:21; MELACHIM ALEF 12:7"> And they spoke unto him, saying, If thou be kind to HaAm HaZeh, and please them, and speak devarim tovim to them, they will be thy avadim kol hayamim (all the days).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 17:14; MISHLE 1:25; KOHELET 10:16"> But he forsook the Atzat HaZekenim they gave him, and took counsel with the yeladim that were brought up with him, that stood before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 10:6; SHMUEL BAIS 17:5–6; MELACHIM ALEF 22:6–8"> And he said unto them, What advice give ye that we may return answer to HaAm HaZeh, which have spoken to me, saying, Ease somewhat haOl that Avicha did put upon us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MISHLE 29:23; MISHLE 13:16; SHMUEL BAIS 17:7–13"> And the yeladim that grew up with him spoke unto him, saying, Thus shalt thou answer HaAm that spoke unto thee, saying, Avicha made our ol (yoke) heavy, but make thou it somewhat lighter for us; thus shalt thou say unto them, My little finger is thicker than the loins of Avi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:29; YESHAYAH 47:6; HISGALUS 9:10"> For whereas Avi put an ol kaved (a heavy yoke) upon you, I will add more to your ol (yoke). Avi chastised you with shotim (whips), but I will chastise you with akrabim (scorpions).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:12–15; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 10:5"> So Yarov'am and kol HaAm came to Rechav'am on the Yom HaShelishi, as HaMelech said, Shuva (return) to me on the Yom HaShelishi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 10:8; MISHLE 19:27; SHMUEL ALEF 25:10–11"> And HaMelech answered them kashah (harshly); and HaMelech Rechav'am forsook the Atzat HaZekenim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MISHLE 17:14; MISHLE 12:5; KOHELET 10:16"> And answered them after the Atzat HaYeladim, saying, Avi made your ol (yoke) heavy, but I will add thereto; Avi chastised you with shotim (whips), but I will chastise you with akrabim (scorpions).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:29–39; SHMUEL ALEF 2:25; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:16–20"> So HaMelech paid heed not unto HaAm, for the nesibah (turn of events, circumstance) was of HaElohim, that Hashem might perform His Devar, which He spoke by the yad Achiyah HaShiloni to Yarov'am ben Nevat.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1Kgs 11:29-39">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 20:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 10:19; TEHILLIM 76:10"> And when kol Yisroel saw that HaMelech would not pay heed unto them, HaAm answered HaMelech, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">What chelek have we in Dovid?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">We have none nachalah in Ben Yishai.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Every ish to your ohalim, O Yisroel!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and now, Dovid, see to thine own Bais!</span><br />So kol Yisroel went to their ohalim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:1; MELACHIM ALEF 12:17; MELACHIM ALEF 11:36"> But as for the Bnei Yisroel that dwelt in the towns of Yehudah, Rechav'am reigned over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 5:14; MELACHIM ALEF 4:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:57–58"> Then HaMelech Rechav'am sent Hadoram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1Kgs 12:18“Adoram”">c</a></sup>who was over the mas (tax, tribute); and the Bnei Yisroel stoned him with stones, that he died. But HaMelech Rechav'am made speed to get up into his merkavah, to escape to Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:19–20; EZRA 9:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 5:9"> And Yisroel rebelled against the Bais Dovid unto Hayom Hazeh.</span>
