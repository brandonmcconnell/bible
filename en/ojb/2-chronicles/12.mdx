<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:47–48; YESHAYAH 26:13; YIRMEYAH 10:24"> Nevertheless they shall be his avadim; that they may differentiate My avodah, and the avodat mamlechut of the lands.</span>
