<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 14:1–6"> Amatzyah was 25 old when he began to reign, and he reigned 29 shanah in Yerushalayim. And shem immo was Yeho'addin of Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:14; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:21"> And he did that which was yashar in the eyes of Hashem, but not with a levav shalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 24:25–26; MELACHIM BAIS 14:5–22; BERESHIS 9:5–6"> Now it came to pass, when the mamlachah was established under him, that he slaughtered his avadim that had murdered HaMelech Aviv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEVARIM 24:16; YECHEZKEL 18:20; MELACHIM BAIS 14:5–6"> But he slaughtered not their banim, but did as it is written in the Torah in the Sefer Moshe, where Hashem commanded, saying, The avot shall not die for the banim, neither shall the banim die for the avot, but every ish shall die for his own chet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 17:14–18; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:1"> Moreover Amatzyahu gathered Yehudah together, and set them in order in Bais Avot by officers of thousands and officers of hundreds, throughout all Yehudah and Binyamin; and he mustered them from esrim shanah and above, and found them three hundred elef bachur, battle-ready choice men, that could handle spear and shield.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He hired also a hundred elef gibbor chayil out of Yisroel for a hundred talents of kesef.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:2; MELACHIM ALEF 12:28; HOSHEA 9:13"> But there came an Ish HaElohim to him, saying, O HaMelech, let not the Tzeva Yisroel go with thee; for Hashem is not with Yisroel, not with kol Bnei Ephrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:19"> But if thou wilt go, do it; chazak (be strong) for the milchamah; HaElohim shall make thee fall before the enemy, for Elohim hath ko'ach to help, and to cast down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MISHLE 10:22; DEVARIM 8:18"> And Amatzyahu said to the Ish HaElohim, But what shall we do for the hundred talents which I have paid for the army of Yisroel? And the Ish HaElohim answered, Hashem is able to give thee much more than this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:24; SHMUEL BAIS 19:43; MISHLE 29:22"> Then Amatzyahu separated them, the army that had come to him from Ephrayim, to go home again; therefore their anger was greatly kindled against Yehudah, and they returned home in chari af (great anger).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 14:7; TEHILLIM 60:1; SHMUEL BAIS 8:13"> And Amatzyahu strengthened himself, led forth his army, went to the Gey HaMelach, struck down 10,000 of the Bnei Se'ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 20:3; SHMUEL BAIS 12:31"> And another 10,000 left chayyim (alive) did the Bnei Yehudah carry away captive, and brought them unto the top of the cliff, and cast them down from the top of the cliff, that they all were dashed to pieces.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:5; MELACHIM ALEF 16:29; MELACHIM ALEF 9:17"> But the soldiers of the army which Amatzyahu sent back, that they should not go with him to milchamah, fell upon the cities of Yehudah, from Shomron even unto Beit-Choron, and struck down three thousand of them, and took much plunder.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:23; DEVARIM 7:25; DEVARIM 7:5"> Now it came to pass, after that Amatzyahu returned from the slaughter of the people of Edom, that he brought the elohei Bnei Se'ir, and set them up for him to be elohim, and bowed down himself before them, and burned ketoret unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 96:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:11–12; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:7"> Therefore the Af Hashem was kindled against Amatzyahu, and he sent unto him a navi, which said unto him, Why hast thou sought after the elohei haAm, which could not deliver their own people out of thine yad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 18:20–21; YIRMEYAH 29:26; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 18:25"> And it came to pass, as he talked with him, that HaMelech said unto him, Art thou appointed the adviser to HaMelech? Forbear. Why shouldest thou be struck down? Then the navi forbore, and said, I know that Elohim hath determined to destroy thee, because thou hast done this, and hast not paid heed unto my etza (counsel).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 14:8–14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:13; MISHLE 20:3"> Then Amatzyahu Melech Yehudah consulted, and sent to Yoash ben Yehoachaz ben Yehu, Melech Yisroel, saying, Come, let us meet face to face.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHOFETIM 9:8–15; MELACHIM ALEF 4:33; TEHILLIM 80:13"> And Yoash Melech Yisroel sent to Amatzyahu Melech Yehudah, saying, The thistle that was in the Levanon sent to the cedar that was in the Levanon, saying, Give thy bat to beni (my son) as isha; then there passed by a chayyat hasadeh (wild beast) that was in the Levanon, and trode down the thistle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 26:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:25; MISHLE 18:6"> Thou sayest, Hinei, thou hast struck down the people of Edom; and thine lev lifteth thee up to be proud. Abide now at home. Why shouldest thou meddle to thine hurt, that thou shouldest fall, even thou, and Yehudah with thee?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 22:7; MELACHIM ALEF 12:15; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:8"> But Amatzyah would not hear, for it came from HaElohim, that He might deliver them into the yad of their enemies, because they sought after the elohei Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 6:19–20; SHMUEL ALEF 6:9; YEHOSHUA 21:16"> So Yoash Melech Yisroel went up and they met face to face, both he and Amatzyahu Melech Yehudah, at Beit-Shemesh, which belongeth to Yehudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 4:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:5–6; MELACHIM ALEF 22:36"> And Yehudah was routed before Yisroel, and they fled every ish to his ohel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 21:17; NECHEMYAH 8:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 26:9"> And Yoash Melech Yisroel captured Amatzyahu Melech Yehudah ben Yoash ben Yehoachaz, at Beit-Shemesh, and brought him to Yerushalayim, and broke down the Chomat Yerushalayim from the Sha'ar Ephrayim to the Sha'ar HaPinnah, a section the length of which was four hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:15; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:9; MELACHIM BAIS 14:14"> And he took all the zahav and the kesef, and all the vessels that were found in the Beis HaElohim with Oved-Edom, and the otzerot Bais HaMelech, the hostages also, and returned to Shomron.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 14:17–22"> And Amatzyahu ben Yoash Melech Yehudah lived after the mot Yoash ben Yehoachaz Melech Yisroel fifteen years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:34; MELACHIM BAIS 14:15"> Now the rest of the acts of Amatzyahu, harishonim and ha'acharonim, hinei, are they not written in the Sefer of the Melachim of Yehudah and Yisroel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 24:25; YEHOSHUA 10:31; MELACHIM BAIS 14:19"> Now after the time that Amatzyah did turn away from following Hashem they made a kesher against him in Yerushalayim; and he fled to Lakhish, but they sent to Lakhish after him, and slaughtered him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 14:20"> And they brought him upon susim, and buried him with Avotav in Ir Yehudah.</span>
</div>
</div>
