<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:16; MELACHIM ALEF 15:1–8"> Now in the eighteenth year of Melech Yarov'am began Aviyah to reign over Yehudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:20; MELACHIM ALEF 15:6–7; SHOFETIM 19:16"> He reigned 3 shanim in Yerushalayim. And the shem immo was Michayah bat Uriel of Giv'ah. And there was milchamah between Aviyah and Yarov'am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 26:12–13; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:5; SHMUEL ALEF 17:1–3"> And Aviyah led the milchamah with an army of gibborei milchamah, even 400 elef ish bachur. Yarov'am also drew up for milchamah against him with 800 elef ish bachur, gibbor chayil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 18:22; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:2; BERESHIS 10:18"> And Aviyah stood up upon Mt Tzemarayim, which is in the hill country of Ephrayim, and said, Hear me, thou Yarov'am, and kol Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 18:19; VAYIKRA 2:13; MARKOS 9:49–50"> Ought ye not to know that Hashem Elohei Yisroel gave the Mamlachah over Yisroel to Dovid l'olam (forever), even to him and to his banim by a Brit Melach?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Num 18:19">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:26; MELACHIM ALEF 12:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 10:19"> Yet Yarov'am ben Nevat eved Sh'lomo ben Dovid is risen up, and hath rebelled against Adonav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHOFETIM 9:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:5"> And there are gathered unto him anashim rekim, the Bnei Beliyaal, and have strengthened themselves against Rechav'am ben Sh'lomo, when Rechav'am was na'ar and rakh levav (faint-hearted), and could not withstand them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:28; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:15; LUKAS 19:14"> And now ye think to withstand the Mamlechet Hashem in the yad Bnei Dovid; and ye be a great multitude, and there are with you eglei zahav, which Yarov'am made you for elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:14–15; YIRMEYAH 2:11; SHEMOT 29:35"> Have ye not cast out the Kohanim of Hashem, the Bnei Aharon, and the Levi'im, and have made you kohanim after the manner of the peoples of other lands? So whosoever cometh to consecrate himself with a young bull and 7 rams, the same may be a kohen of them that are no elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:5–6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:16–17; ZECHARYAH 13:9"> But as for us, Hashem is Eloheinu, and we have not forsaken Him, and the Kohanim, mesharetim unto Hashem, are the Bnei Aharon, and the Levi'im do their work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:4; VAYIKRA 24:2–9; SHEMOT 29:38"> And they burn unto Hashem baboker baboker and baerev baerev olot and ketoret sammim; the array of lechem is also set in order upon the shulchan hatahor; and the menorah hazahav with the nerot thereof is lit erev ba'erev, for we are the Shomrim of the Mishmeret Hashem Eloheinu; but ye have forsaken Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:39; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:31; BAMIDBAR 10:8–9"> And, hinei, HaElohim Himself is with us as Rosh, and His Kohanim have their chatzotzerot to sound the alarm against you. O Bnei Yisroel, fight ye not against Hashem Elohei Avoteichem; for ye shall not prosper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 8:4; YEHOSHUA 8:9; MISHLE 21:30"> But Yarov'am caused a ma'arav to come about behind them so that they were before Yehudah, and the ma'arav was behind them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:11; SHEMOT 14:10; YEHOSHUA 8:20"> And when Yehudah turned, hinei, the milchamah was before and behind, and they cried unto Hashem, and the Kohanim sounded the battle trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:12; YEHOSHUA 11:8; TEHILLIM 118:4–7"> Then the Ish Yehudah gave a shout, and as the Ish Yehudah shouted, it came to pass, that HaElohim routed Yarov'am and kol Yisroel before Aviyah and Yehudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 16:8; SHOFETIM 11:21; YEHOSHUA 10:12"> And the Bnei Yisroel fled before Yehudah, and Elohim delivered them into their yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 13:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 13:12"> And Aviyah and his army slaughtered them with a makkah rabbah (great slaughter); so there fell down slain of Yisroel five hundred thousand ish bachur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:11; NACHUM 1:7"> Thus the Bnei Yisroel were subdued at that time, and the Bnei Yehudah prevailed, because they relied upon Hashem Elohei Avoteihem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:8; YEHOSHUA 15:9; YEHOSHUA 10:39"> And Aviyah pursued after Yarov'am, and took cities from him: Beit-El with the towns thereof, and Yeshanah with the villages thereof, and Ephrayim with the villages thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:38; MELACHIM ALEF 14:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:23"> Neither did Yarov'am recover ko'ach again in the days of Aviyah; and Hashem struck him, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHOFETIM 9:5; SHMUEL BAIS 5:12–13; SHOFETIM 10:4"> But Aviyah grew strong, and married fourteen nashim, and fathered twenty and two banim, and sixteen banot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:29; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:15; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 24:27"> And the rest of the acts of Aviyah, and his deeds, and his words, are written in the Midrash HaNavi Iddo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> So Aviyah slept with Avotav, and they buried him in Ir Dovid, and Asa bno reigned in his place. In his days HaAretz was at peace eser shanim.</span>
