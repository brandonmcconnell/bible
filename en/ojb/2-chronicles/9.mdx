<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:42; LUKAS 11:31; MELACHIM ALEF 10:1–13"> And when the Malkat Sheva (Queen of Sheba) heard of the fame of Sh'lomo, she came to test Sh'lomo with chidot (hard questions, riddles) at Yerushalayim, with a very great caravan, and camels that carried spices, and zahav in abundance, and precious stones; and when she was come to Sh'lomo, she communed with him of all that was in her levav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:15; MELACHIM ALEF 3:12; MARKOS 4:34"> And Sh'lomo gave answer to all her questions; and there was nothing hid from Sh'lomo which he explained not to her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 3:1–4; MELACHIM ALEF 10:3; MELACHIM ALEF 6:1–7"> And when the Malkat Sheva had seen the Chochmat Sh'lomo, and HaBais (the Palace) that he had built,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKAS 12:37; MELACHIM ALEF 4:22–23; TEHILLIM 143:7"> And the ma'akhal (food) of his shulchan, and the moshav of his avadim, and the attendance of his ministers, and their apparel; his cupbearers also, and their apparel; and his aliyah (ascent) by which he went up to the Beis Hashem; there was no more ruach in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 10:6"> And she said to HaMelech, Emes was the davar (report) which I heard in mine own country about thine acts, and of thy chochmah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 10:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:5; MELACHIM ALEF 4:34"> Howbeit I believed not their words, until I came, and mine eyes had seen it; and, hinei, the one half of the greatness of thy chochmah was not told me, for thou exceedest the report that I heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 27:4; MISHLE 3:14; MISHLE 10:21"> Ashrei are thy anashim, and ashrei are these thy avadim, which stand continually before thee, and hear thy chochmah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:23; YESHAYAH 32:1–2"> Baruch Hashem Eloheicha Who delighted in thee to set thee on His kisse, to be Melech for Hashem Eloheicha, because Eloheicha loved Yisroel, to establish them l'olam, therefore He made thee Melech over them, to maintain Mishpat and Tzedakah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:1; TEHILLIM 72:10; TEHILLIM 72:15"> And she gave HaMelech a hundred and twenty talents of zahav, and spices in great abundance, and precious stones; neither was there ever any such spice as the Malkat Sheva gave Sh'lomo HaMelech.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:18; MELACHIM ALEF 10:11; MELACHIM ALEF 10:22"> And the avadim also of Churam, and the avadim of Sh'lomo, which brought zahav from Ophir, brought atzei algummim (algum trees) and precious stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 10:12; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:5; TEHILLIM 92:1–3"> And HaMelech made of the atzei algummim steps for the Beis Hashem, and for the Beis HaMelech, and kinnorot and nevalim for the musicians; and there were none such seen before in the Eretz Yehudah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 10:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:20; TEHILLIM 20:4"> And Sh'lomo HaMelech gave to Malkat Sheva all her chefetz, whatsoever she asked, beside such that she had brought unto HaMelech. So she turned, and went away to her own land, she and her avadim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TEHILLIM 72:15; TEHILLIM 68:29; MELACHIM ALEF 10:14–28"> Now the weight of zahav that came to Sh'lomo in one year was six hundred and threescore and six talents of zahav;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 68:29; TEHILLIM 72:10"> Besides that which traders and merchants brought. And all the melachim of Arabia and governors of the country brought zahav and kesef to Sh'lomo.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 10:16–17; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:9–10"> And Melech Sh'lomo made 200 shields of hammered zahav; 600 measures of hammered zahav went to one shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:2"> And 300 moginnim he made of hammered zahav; 300 measures of zahav went to one mogen. And HaMelech put them in Beis Ya'ar HaLevanon (House of the Forest of Levanon).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 10:18–20; TEHILLIM 45:8; HISGALUS 20:11"> Moreover HaMelech made a kisse shen gadol (great ivory throne) and overlaid it with zahav tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 24:9; HISGALUS 5:5; BAMIDBAR 23:24"> And there were six steps to the kisse, with a kevesh (ramp) of zahav, which was attached to the kisse, and arms on each side of the seat, and two aroyot (lions) standing by the armrests;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:28; HISGALUS 21:12"> And 12 aroyot stood there on the one side and on the other upon 6 steps. There was nothing like it made for any other mamlechah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 10:21; ESTHER 1:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:27"> And all the kelei mashkeh (drinking vessels) of Sh'lomo HaMelech were of zahav, and all the kelei Beis Ya'ar HaLevanon were of pure zahav; none were of kesef; it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="silver">a</a></sup>was not accounted as anything in the days of Sh'lomo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:48; IYOV 39:13; MELACHIM ALEF 10:22"> HaMelech had a fleet of oniyyot that went to Tarshish with the avadim of Churam. Once every three years came the oniyyot Tarshish bringing zahav, and kesef, ivory, and monkeys, and peacocks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 1:12; TEHILLIM 89:27; MELACHIM ALEF 4:30–31"> And Sh'lomo HaMelech surpassed all the Malkhei HaAretz in riches and chochmah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17; MISHLE 2:6"> And all the Malkhei Ha'Aretz sought the presence of Sh'lomo, to hear his chochmah, that HaElohim had put in his lev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 10:25; IYOV 42:11; TEHILLIM 72:10"> And they brought every ish his minchah (present), kelei kesef, kelei zahav, and clothing, weapons, and spices, susim, and peradim (mules), at a set rate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 4:26; MELACHIM ALEF 10:26; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 1:14"> And Sh'lomo had four thousand stalls for susim and markovot (chariots), and twelve thousand parashim whom he kept in the chariot cities, and with HaMelech at Yerushalayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 4:21; BERESHIS 15:18; MELACHIM ALEF 4:24"> And he reigned over all the melachim from the River<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., the Euphrates">b</a></sup>even unto the Eretz Pelishtim, and to the border of Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 1:15–17; YESHAYAH 9:10; MELACHIM ALEF 10:27–29"> And HaMelech made kesef in Yerushalayim as common as stones, and cedar trees he made like sycamore trees that are in the Shefelah in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 1:16; MELACHIM ALEF 10:28; YESHAYAH 31:1"> And they imported unto Sh'lomo susim out of Mitzrayim, and from all the lands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:29; MELACHIM ALEF 11:41–43; MELACHIM ALEF 11:29"> Now the rest of the Divrei Sh'lomo, harishonim and ha'acharonim, are they not written in the Divrei Natan HaNavi, and in the Nevu'at Achiyah the Shiloni, and in the chazot Yadai HaChozer against Yarov'am ben Nevat?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:42–43"> And Sh'lomo reigned in Yerushalayim over kol Yisroel arba'im shanah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:10; SHMUEL BAIS 7:12; MELACHIM ALEF 1:21"> And Sh'lomo slept with Avotav, and he was buried in Ir Dovid Aviv. And Rechav'am Bno reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
</div>
