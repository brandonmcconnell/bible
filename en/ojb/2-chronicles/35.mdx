<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:6; BAMIDBAR 9:3; MELACHIM BAIS 23:21–23"> Moreover Yoshiyahu kept Pesach unto Hashem in Yerushalayim, and they slaughtered the Pesach on the fourteenth day of the first month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 31:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:19; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:5–11"> And he set up the Kohanim in their mishmerot, and encouraged them to the Avodas Beis Hashem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:26; MALACHI 2:7; DEVARIM 33:10"> And said unto the Levi'im that taught kol Yisroel, which were hakedoshim unto Hashem, Put the Aron HaKodesh in the Beis which Sh'lomo Ben Dovid Melech Yisroel did build; it shall not be a massa (burden, something carried) upon your ketefayim (shoulders); serve now Hashem Eloheichem and His people Yisroel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:1–26; NECHEMYAH 11:10–20"> And prepare yourselves by Bais Avoteichem, after your machlekot, according to the writing of Dovid Melech Yisroel, and according to the mikhtav of Sh'lomo bno.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 134:1; TEHILLIM 135:2; EZRA 6:18"> And stand in HaKodesh according to the pelugot Bais HaAvot (groupings of families) of your achim the Bnei HaAm, and after the chalukat Bais Av (family subdivisions) of the Levi'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:13–14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:15"> So slaughter the Pesach, and set yourselves apart as kodesh, and prepare your achim, that they may do according to the Devar Hashem by the yad Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:63; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:24; YESHAYAH 32:8"> And Yoshiyahu gave to HaAm, of the tzon, kevasim and goats, all for the Pesachim, for all that were present, to the number of 30,000 plus 3,000 bakar; these were from the property of HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:31–33; YIRMEYAH 29:25–26; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 24:4–5"> And his sarim gave linedavah (voluntarily) unto HaAm, to the Kohanim, to the Levi'im. Chilkiyah, Zecharyahu, Yechiel, negidei Beis HaElohim, gave unto the Kohanim for the Pesachim 2,600 and 300 bakar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 7:21–28; YIRMEYAH 3:10; YESHAYAH 1:10–15"> Konanyah also, and Shema'yahu and Netanel, his achim, and Chashavyahu and Ye'iel and Yozavad, Sarei HaLevi'im, gave unto the Levi'im for Pesachim 5,000 and 500 bakar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:16; EZRA 6:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:4–5"> So the Avodah (Service) was prepared, and the Kohanim stood in their place, and the Levi'im in their machlekot, according to the mitzvat HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:34; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:21–22; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:22–24"> And they slaughtered the Pesach, and the Kohanim sprinkled the dahm from their hands, and the Levi'im skinned them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="VAYIKRA 3:9–11; VAYIKRA 3:3; VAYIKRA 3:5"> And they removed the olah, that they might give according to the pelugot l'Bais Avot l'Bnei HaAm, to offer unto Hashem, as it is written in the Sefer Moshe. And so did they with the bakar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:8–9; SHMUEL ALEF 2:13–15; DEVARIM 16:7"> And they roasted the Pesach with eish according to the mishpat; but the other kadashim they boiled in pots, and in caldrons, and in pans, and distributed them in haste among kol Bnei HaAm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:2–4"> And afterward they made ready for themselves, and for the Kohanim; because the Kohanim the Bnei Aharon were busied in offering olah and the chalavim (fats) until lailah; therefore the Levi'im prepared for themselves, and for the Kohanim, the Bnei Aharon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 25:1–7; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:12–19; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 9:17–19"> And the meshorarim (singers), the Bnei Asaph, were in their place, according to the mitzvat Dovid, and Asaph, and Heman, and Yedutun, Chozeh HaMelech; and the sho'arim (gatekeepers) were at every sha'ar; neither did they need to depart from their Avodah, for their achim the Levi'im prepared for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So all the Avodas Hashem was prepared that same day, to keep the Pesach, and to offer olot upon the Mizbe'ach Hashem, according to the mitzvat HaMelech Yoshiyahu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:15–20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:21–23; SHEMOT 23:15"> And the Bnei Yisroel that were present kept the Pesach at that time, and the Chag HaMatzot seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:5; MELACHIM BAIS 23:21–23; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:26–27"> And there was no Pesach like it in Yisroel from the days of Shmuel HaNavi; neither did all the Melachim of Yisroel keep such a Pesach as Yoshiyahu kept, and the Kohanim, and the Levi'im, and kol Yehudah and Yisroel that were present, and the inhabitants of Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> In the eighteenth shanah of the reign of Yoshiyahu was this Pesach kept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YESHAYAH 10:9; MELACHIM BAIS 23:29–30; YIRMEYAH 46:2–12"> After all this, when Yoshiyahu had set in order the Beis,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="HaMikdash">a</a></sup>Nekho Melech Mitzrayim went up to fight at Karkemish on the Euphrates, and Yoshiyahu went up against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:19; MELACHIM BAIS 18:25; YESHAYAH 36:10"> But he sent malachim to him, saying, Mah li valach (What have I to do with thee), thou Melech Yehudah? I come not against thee this day, but against the Bais wherewith I have war,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., Babylon">b</a></sup>for Elohim commanded me to make haste; forbear thee from meddling with Elohim, Who is with me, that He destroy thee not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHOFETIM 5:19; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 18:29; MELACHIM ALEF 22:30"> Nevertheless Yoshiyahu would not turn his face from him, but disguised himself, that he might fight with him, and paid heed not unto the words of Nekho from the mouth of Elohim, and went to fight in the Valley of Megiddo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:34; MELACHIM BAIS 9:24; BERESHIS 49:23"> And the archers shot at Melech Yoshiyahu; and HaMelech said to his avadim, Take me away; for I am badly wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 12:11; MELACHIM BAIS 23:30; TEHILLIM 36:6"> His avadim therefore took him out of that merkavah, and put him in the mishneh merkavah that he had; and they brought him to Yerushalayim, and he died, and was buried in the kivrot Avotav. And all Yehudah and Yerushalayim mourned for Yoshiyahu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EKHAH 4:20; YIRMEYAH 22:10; IYOV 3:8"> And Yirmeyahu lamented for Yoshiyahu; and all the sharim (singing men) and the sharot (singing women) commemorate Yoshiyahu in their laments to this day, and made them as chok<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="set custom">c</a></sup>in Yisroel; and, hinei, they are written in HaKinot (the Laments).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 31:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:32"> Now the rest of the acts of Yoshiyahu, and his acts of chesed, according to what is written in the Torat Hashem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 26:22; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:26; MELACHIM BAIS 10:34"> And his deeds, harishonim and ha'acharonim, hinei, they are written in the Sefer of the Melachim of Yisroel and Yehudah.</span>
