<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 24:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:14; BAMIDBAR 24:2"> And the Ruach Elohim came upon Azaryahu ben Oded;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 24:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:4"> And he went out to meet Asa, and said unto him, Hear ye me, Asa, and kol Yehudah and Binyamin. Hashem is with you, while ye be with Him; if ye seek Him, He will be found of you; if ye forsake Him, He will forsake you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 8:9; VAYIKRA 10:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 17:8–9"> Now for yamim rabbim Yisroel hath been without the Elohei Emes, and without a Kohen Moreh, and without Torah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YESHAYAH 55:6; DEVARIM 4:29–30; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:15"> But when they in their tzoros did turn unto Hashem Elohei Yisroel, and sought Him, He was found by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHOFETIM 5:6; TEHILLIM 121:8; SHMUEL ALEF 13:6"> And in those times there was no shalom to him that went out, nor to him that came in, but mehumot rabbot (great turmoil) was upon all the inhabitants of the lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKAS 21:22–24; MATTITYAHU 24:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:11"> And nation was destroyed of nation, and city of city, for Elohim did trouble them with every tzuris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:58; YEHOSHUA 1:7; YEHOSHUA 1:9"> Be ye strong therefore, and let not your hands be weak, for there is sachar (reward) for your pe'ulah (work).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:12; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 13:19; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:1"> And when Asa heard these words, and the nevu'ah of Oded HaNavi, he took courage, and put away the shikkutzim (abominable idols) from kol Eretz Yehudah and Binyamin, and from the cities which he had captured from Mt Ephrayim, and renewed the Mizbe'ach Hashem, that was in front of the Ulam Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:31; ZECHARYAH 8:21–23"> And he gathered all Yehudah and Binyamin, and the gerim with them from Ephrayim and Menasheh, and from Shim'on, for they fell to him from Yisroel in abundance, when they saw that Hashem Elohav was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ESTHER 8:9"> So they gathered themselves together at Yerushalayim in the chodesh hashelishi, in the fifteenth year of the reign of Asa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:13–15; BAMIDBAR 31:28–29; BAMIDBAR 31:50"> And they offered unto Hashem on that day, of the plunder which they had brought back, 700 bakar and 7,000 tzon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 23:16; DEVARIM 10:12"> And they entered into the Brit to seek Hashem Elohei Avoteihem with all their lev and with all their nefesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:20; TEHILLIM 115:13; MELACHIM ALEF 18:40"> That whosoever would not seek Hashem Elohei Yisroel should be put to death, whether katan or gadol, whether ish or isha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 5:13; TEHILLIM 81:1–4; NECHEMYAH 10:29"> And they swore unto Hashem with a kol gadol, and with teruah, and with trumpets, and with shoferot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:12; YESHAYAH 26:8"> And all Yehudah rejoiced at the shevu'ah, for they had sworn with all their lev, and sought Him with their whole ratzon; and He was found by them; and Hashem gave them rest all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 15:13–24; MELACHIM ALEF 15:10; MELACHIM BAIS 23:15"> And also concerning Ma'achah em Asa HaMelech, he deposed her from being gevirah, because she had made to Asherah a mifletzet, and Asa cut down her mifletzet, and ground it up, and burned it at the Wadi Kidron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 14:4; MELACHIM BAIS 12:3; MELACHIM ALEF 11:4"> But the high places were not removed from Yisroel; nevertheless the levav Asa was shalem all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:51; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:20–26; MELACHIM ALEF 15:14–15"> And he brought into the Beis HaElohim the things that Aviv had dedicated as kodesh, and that he himself had dedicated as kodesh, kesef, and zahav, and kelim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 15:31; MELACHIM ALEF 15:33; MELACHIM ALEF 15:16–17"> And there was no more milchamah unto the five and thirtieth year of the reign of Asa.</span>
