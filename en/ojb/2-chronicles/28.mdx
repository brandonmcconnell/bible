<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:13; YESHAYAH 1:1; MELACHIM BAIS 16:1–20"> Achaz was 20 when he began to reign, and he reigned 16 years in Yerushalayim; but he did not that which was yashar in the eyes of Hashem, like Dovid Aviv,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:17; SHOFETIM 2:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 22:3–4"> For he walked in the ways of the melachim of Yisroel, and made also massekhot for Ba'alim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:6; VAYIKRA 18:21"> Moreover he burned ketoret in the Gey Ben Hinnom, and burned his banim in the eish, after the to'avot of the Goyim whom Hashem had driven out before the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 16:4; DEVARIM 12:2–3; VAYIKRA 26:30"> He also sacrificed and burned ketoret on the high places, and on the hilltops, and under every green tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YESHAYAH 7:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 24:24; MELACHIM BAIS 16:5–6"> Therefore Hashem Elohav delivered him into the yad Melech Aram; and they defeated him, and carried off a great multitude of prisoners, and brought them to Damascus. And he was also delivered into the yad Melech Yisroel, who inflicted on him makkah gedolah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 15:27; YEHOSHUA 24:20; YIRMEYAH 15:6"> For Pekach ben Remalyahu slaughtered in Yehudah 120 elef in yom echad, which were all valiant men; because they had forsaken Hashem Elohei Avotam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 43:15; BERESHIS 41:43; ESTHER 10:3"> And Zichri, a gibbor of Ephrayim, slaughtered Ma'aseiyahu ben HaMelech, and Azrikam the Nagid HaBais, and Elkanah Mishneh HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:4; DEVARIM 28:25; DEVARIM 28:41"> And the Bnei Yisroel carried away captive of their achim 200 elef nashim, banim, and banot, and took also away much plunder from them, and carried back the plunder to Shomron.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YESHAYAH 47:6; EZRA 9:6; HISGALUS 18:5"> But a navi of Hashem was there shmo Oded, and he went out before the faces of the tzava returning to Shomron, and said unto them, Hinei, because Hashem Elohei Avoteichem was angry with Yehudah, He hath delivered them into your yad, and ye have slain them in a rage that reacheth up unto Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:39–46; MATTITYAHU 7:2–4; YIRMEYAH 25:29"> And now ye intend to keep under the Bnei Yehudah and Yerushalayim for avadim and shefachot unto you, but are there not with you, even with you, sins against Hashem Eloheichem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13; EZRA 10:14; MATTITYAHU 5:7"> Now hear me therefore, and send back the prisoners, which ye have taken captive of your achim, for the Charon Af Hashem is upon you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:1; YIRMEYAH 26:6"> Then certain anashim of the Rashei Bnei Ephrayim, Azaryahu ben Yehochanan, Berechyahu ben Meshillemot, Yechizkiyahu ben Shallum, and Amasa ben Chadlai, stood up against them that came from the tzava,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:14; MATTITYAHU 23:32; YEHOSHUA 22:17–18"> And said unto them, Ye shall not bring in the prisoners here; for whereas we have offended against Hashem already, ye intend to add more to chattotenu and to ashmatenu, for our guilt is great, and there is Charon Af against Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So the soldiers released the prisoners and the plunder before the sarim and kol HaKahal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MISHLE 25:21–22; DEVARIM 34:3; SHOFETIM 1:16"> And the men which were designated by shemot rose up, and took the prisoners, and with the plunder clothed all that were naked among them, and arrayed them, and shod them, and gave them to eat and to drink, and put balm on them, and carried all the weak of them upon donkeys, and brought them to Yericho, the Ir Temarim, to their achim. Then they returned to Shomron.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 16:5–7; YESHAYAH 7:17; YESHAYAH 7:1–9"> At that time did HaMelech Achaz send unto the melachim of Assyria to help him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="OVADYAH 1:10; OVADYAH 1:13–14; VAYIKRA 26:18"> For again the people of Edom had come and attacked Yehudah, and carried away prisoners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 16:57; YECHEZKEL 16:27; SHMUEL ALEF 6:9"> The Pelishtim also had invaded the towns of the Shefelah, and of the Negev of Yehudah, and had taken Beit-Shemesh, Ayalon, Gederot, and Sokhoh with the villages thereof, and Timnah with the villages thereof, Gimzo also and the villages thereof, and they dwelt there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 21:2; IYOV 40:12; DEVARIM 28:43"> For Hashem brought Yehudah low because of Achaz Melech Yisroel, for he brought disgrace to Yehudah, and acted treacherously against Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 15:29; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:26; YESHAYAH 30:16"> And Tilgat Pilneser Melech Ashur came unto him, and oppressed him, but helped him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 16:8–9; MELACHIM BAIS 18:15–16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:9"> For Achaz took away a portion out of the Beis Hashem, and out of the Bais HaMelech, and of the sarim, and gave it unto Melech Ashur, but he helped him not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YESHAYAH 1:5; YECHEZKEL 21:13; YIRMEYAH 5:3"> And in the time of his distress he did act even more unfaithfully against Hashem. This is that Melech Achaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:14; YIRMEYAH 44:20–28; CHABAKUK 1:11"> For he sacrificed unto the elohei Damascus, which defeated him, and he said, Because the elohim of the melachim of Syria help them, therefore will I sacrifice to them, that they may help me. But they were the ruin of him, and of kol Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:7; MELACHIM BAIS 16:17–18; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:14"> And Achaz gathered together the vessels of the Beis HaElohim, and cut up the vessels of the Beis HaElohim and shut up the daletot Beis Hashem, and he set up for him mizbechot in every corner of Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:3"> And in every town in Yehudah he made high places to burn ketoret unto elohim acherim, and provoked to anger Hashem Elohei Avotav.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 16:19–20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:34; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 27:7–9"> Now the rest of his acts and of all his ways, the rishonim and the acharonim, hinei, they are written in the Sefer of the Melachim of Yehudah and Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 21:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 24:25; MISHLE 10:7"> And Achaz slept with Avotav, and they buried him in the city, even in Yerushalayim, but they brought him not into the keverim of the Melachim of Yisroel. And Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) bno reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
