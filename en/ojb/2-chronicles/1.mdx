<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 27:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:2"> Then Sh'lomo spoke unto kol Yisroel, to the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and to the shofetim, and to every Nasi in kol Yisroel, the Rashei HaAvot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:29; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 16:39; VAYIKRA 1:1"> So Sh'lomo, and Kol HaKahal with him, went to the high place that was at Giv'on, for there was the Ohel Mo'ed HaElohim, which Moshe eved Hashem had made in the midbar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 6:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:25–28; SHMUEL BAIS 6:2"> But the Aron HaElohim Dovid had brought up from Kiryat-Ye'arim to the place which Dovid had prepared for it; for he had pitched an Ohel for it at Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 31:2; SHEMOT 38:1–7; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:19–20"> Moreover the Mizbe'ach Hanechoshet, that Betzalel ben Uri ben Chur had made, he put in front of the Mishkan Hashem; and Sh'lomo and HaKahal inquired of Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 3:4; MELACHIM ALEF 8:63; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:21"> And Sh'lomo went up there to the Mizbe'ach Hanechoshet before Hashem, which was at the Ohel Mo'ed, and offered a thousand olot upon it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:7–8; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:14–15; YOCHANAN 16:23"> In that lailah did Elohim appear unto Sh'lomo, and said unto him, Ask what I shall give thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:5; YESHAYAH 55:3; SHMUEL BAIS 22:51–1"> And Sh'lomo said unto Elohim, Thou hast showed chesed gadol unto Dovid Avi, and hast made me to reign in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 7:12–16; BERESHIS 13:16; TEHILLIM 132:11–12"> Now, Hashem Elohim, let Thy Davar unto Dovid Avi be established, for Thou hast made me melech over Am Rav like the aphar ha'aretz in multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 3:9; MISHLE 4:7; MISHLE 2:2–6"> Give me now chochmah and intelligence, that I may go out and come in before HaAm Hazeh, for who can judge this Thy people, that is so great?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17; MISHLE 23:7; SHMUEL ALEF 16:7"> And Elohim said to Sh'lomo, Because this was in thine levav, and thou hast not asked osher, riches, or kavod, nor the nefesh of thine enemies, neither yet hast asked yamim rabbim (many days, long life), but hast asked chochmah and intelligence for thyself, that thou mayest judge My people, over whom I have made thee melech,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:22; MATTITYAHU 6:33"> Chochmah and intelligence is granted unto thee; and I will give thee osher, and riches, and kavod, such as none of the melachim have had that be before or after thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 1:3; MELACHIM ALEF 4:24–25"> Then Sh'lomo came from his journey to the high place that was at Giv'on, from before the Ohel Mo'ed, to Yerushalayim, and reigned over Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 4:26; MELACHIM ALEF 10:26–29; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:25"> And Sh'lomo gathered chariots and parashim (horsemen); and he had a thousand and four hundred chariots, and twelve thousand parashim, which he placed in the chariot cities, and with HaMelech at Yerushalayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:27; YESHAYAH 9:10; MELACHIM ALEF 10:27–29"> And HaMelech made kesef and zahav at Yerushalayim as plenteous as avanim, and cedar trees he made as abundant as the sycomore fig trees that are in the Shefelah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:28; MELACHIM ALEF 10:28–29"> And Sh'lomo had susim imported from Mitzrayim and from Keve; the royal buyers purchased them from Keve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 10:29"> And they imported from Mitzrayim, and then exported a merkavah (chariot) for six hundred shekels of kesef, and a sus (horse) for a hundred and fifty; and so they exported susim to all the melachim of the Chittim, and to the melachim of Aram (Syria).</span>
</div>
