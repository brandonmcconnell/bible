<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:64; YECHEZKEL 43:13–17; MELACHIM ALEF 9:25"> Moreover he made a Mizbe'ach nechoshet, twenty cubits the length thereof, and twenty cubits the width thereof, and ten cubits the height thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:5; ZECHARYAH 13:1; SHEMOT 30:18–21"> Also he made a Yam Mutzak (Molten “Sea”) of ten cubits from brim to brim. It was round, and five cubits the height thereof, and a line of thirty cubits did completely encircle it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 1:10; YECHEZKEL 10:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:9–10"> And under it was the demut (likeness) of bulls, all around, ten per cubit, surrounding the Yam. Two rows of bulls were cast, when it was cast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:19–20; HISGALUS 21:14; MARKOS 16:15"> It stood upon shneym asar bakar (twelve bulls), three looking toward the north, and three looking toward the west, and three looking toward the south, and three looking toward the east; and the Yam was set above upon them, and all their hindquarters were toward the inside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:26"> And the thickness of it was a handbreadth, and the brim of it like the work of the brim of a kos, like the flower of a lily; and it received and held the capacity of shloshet alafim baths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:38; SHEMOT 30:18–21; MELACHIM ALEF 7:40"> He made also ten kiyyorim (lavers, basins for washing), and put five on the right and five on the left, to wash in them; such things as they offered for the olah (burnt offering) they washed in them; but the Yam was for the Kohanim to wash in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:31–40; MELACHIM ALEF 7:49; YOCHANAN 8:12"> And he made ten Menorot HaZahav as prescribed, and set them in the Heikhal, five on the right, and five on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:48; MALACHI 1:12; SHEMOT 25:23–30"> He made also ten Shulchanot, and placed them in the Heikhal, five on the right, and five on the left. And he made a hundred mizrekei zahav (golden basins).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 6:36; MELACHIM BAIS 21:5; MELACHIM ALEF 7:12"> Furthermore he made the Khatzer HaKohanim, and the Azarah HaGedolal (the Great Temple Courtyard area), and delatot laAzarah, and overlaid the daletot of them with nechoshet (bronze).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:39"> And he set the Yam on the right, eastward, opposite the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:40–51; MELACHIM ALEF 7:14"> And Churam made the sirot, and the shovels, and the mizrakot. And Churam finished the melachah that he was to make for Melech Sh'lomo for the Beis HaElohim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:41; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 3:15–17"> The two Ammudim, and the bases, and the capitals which were on the top of the two Ammudim, and the two nettings to cover the two bases of the capitals which were on the top of the Ammudim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:20; MELACHIM ALEF 7:42; YIRMEYAH 52:23"> And four hundred pomegranates on the two nettings; two rows of pomegranates on each netting, to cover the two bases of the capitals which were upon the Ammudim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:27–43"> He made also stands, and kiyyorot made he upon the stands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> One Yam, and twelve bakar under it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:13; MELACHIM ALEF 7:13–14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:11"> The sirot also, and the shovels, and the mizlagot, and all their instruments, did Churam-Avi make for Melech Sh'lomo for the Bais Hashem of nechoshet maruk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:46"> In the plain of Yarden did HaMelech cast them, in the avi ha'adamah between Sukkot and Tzeredah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:47; YIRMEYAH 52:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:14"> Thus Sh'lomo made all these vessels in great abundance, for the weight of the nechoshet could not be found out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="HISGALUS 9:13; DANIEL 5:2–3; SHEMOT 25:23–30"> And Sh'lomo made all the vessels that were for the Beis HaElohim, the Mizbe'ach HaZahav also, and the Shulchanot whereon the Lechem HaPanim was set;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:7; SHEMOT 25:31–37; MELACHIM ALEF 6:5"> Moreover the Menorot with their nerot (lamps), that they should burn as prescribed before the Devir, of zahav sagur;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 6:29; SHEMOT 37:20; MELACHIM ALEF 6:35"> And the flowers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Ex 25:31">a</a></sup>and the nerot (lamps), and the tongs, he made of zahav, and that perfect zahav;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 12:13; MELACHIM BAIS 25:14; YIRMEYAH 52:18"> And the snuffers, and the basins, and the ladles, and the firepans, of zahav sagur; and the Petach (Doorway) the Beis, the Daltot HaPenimiyyot (Inner Doors) thereof for the Kodesh HaKadashim, and the Daltot HaBeis laHeikhal, were of zahav.</span>
