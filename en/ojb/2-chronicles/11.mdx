<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:21–24; TEHILLIM 33:10; TEHILLIM 33:16"> And when Rechav'am was come to Yerushalayim, he gathered of the Bais Yehudah and Binyamin a hundred and fourscore thousand bachur oseh milchamah (chosen men, which were warriors), to make war against Yisroel, that he might restore the mamlachah to Rechav'am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:15; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:5–7; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:14"> But the Devar Hashem came to Shema'yah the Ish HaElohim, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 17:34; BERESHIS 49:28; HISGALUS 7:4–8"> Speak unto Rechav'am ben Sh'lomo Melech Yehudah, and to kol Yisroel in Yehudah and Binyamin, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 50:20; MELACHIM ALEF 11:29–38; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 10:15–16"> Thus saith Hashem, Ye shall not go up, nor make war against your achim. Shuvu (return) every ish to his bais, for this thing is done from Me. And they obeyed the divrei Hashem, and returned back from going against Yarov'am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:2–6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:6–7; YESHAYAH 22:8–11"> And Rechav'am dwelt in Yerushalayim, and built arim lematzor (cities for defense) in Yehudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 3:5; AMOS 1:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 4:32"> He built even Beit-Lechem, and Eitam, and Tekoa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:35; SHMUEL ALEF 22:1; YEHOSHUA 15:58"> Beit-Tzur, Sokhoh, Adullam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 54:1; YEHOSHUA 15:24; SHMUEL ALEF 23:19"> Gat, Mareshah, Ziph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:39; YEHOSHUA 15:35; YEHOSHUA 10:5"> And Adorayim, and Lakhish, and Azekah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 13:22; YEHOSHUA 15:33; YEHOSHUA 14:14"> And Tzorah, and Ayalon, and Chevron, which are in Yehudah and in Binyamin arei metzurot (fortified cities).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 17:19; YESHAYAH 22:10–11; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:23"> And he fortified the metzurot (fortifications), and put negidim (rulers) in them, and otzerot ma'achal (storage places of food), and of shemen and yayin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 13:22; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 26:14–15; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:5"> And in all the cities he put shields and spears, and made them exceeding strong, having Yehudah and Binyamin on his side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the Kohanim and the Levi'im that were in kol Yisroel availed themselves to him from all their territories.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 13:9; BAMIDBAR 35:2–5; MELACHIM ALEF 12:28–33"> For the Levi'im abandoned their common-lands and their achuzzah (possession, estate) and came to Yehudah and Yerushalayim; for Yarov'am and his banim had cast them off from executing the office of Kohen unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:28; MELACHIM ALEF 12:31; MELACHIM ALEF 13:33"> And he ordained him kohanim for the high places, and for the se'irim (goat idols, demons) and for the agalim (calf idols) which he had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:23; TEHILLIM 84:5–7"> And after them from all the Shivtei Yisroel such as set their hearts to seek Hashem Elohei Yisroel came to Yerushalayim, to sacrifice unto Hashem Elohei Avoteihem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 1:1–12; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:17–19"> So they strengthened the Malchut Yehudah, and made Rechav'am ben Sh'lomo strong, shanim shalosh. For shanim shalosh they walked in the Derech Dovid u'Sh'lomo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 27:18; SHMUEL ALEF 17:13"> And Rechav'am took him Machalat bat Yerimot Ben Dovid as isha, and Avihayil bat Eliav ben Yishai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Which bore him banim: Ye'ush, and Shemaryah, and Zaham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 13:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:16"> And after her he took Ma'achah bat Avshalom, who bore him Aviyah, and Attai, and Ziza, and Shlomit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHOFETIM 8:30; DEVARIM 17:17; MELACHIM ALEF 11:3"> And Rechav'am loved Ma'achah bat Avshalom more than any of his nashim and his pilagshim; (for he took eighteen nashim, and threescore pilagshim, and fathered twenty and eight banim, and threescore banot.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEVARIM 21:15–17; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:1–2"> And Rechav'am made Aviyah ben Ma'achah the rosh, to be nagid among his achim, for he intended to make him melech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 21:3; BERESHIS 25:6; LUKAS 16:8"> And he acted wisely, and dispersed kol banim of him throughout all the lands of Yehudah and Binyamin, unto all of the arei hametzurot (fortified cities); and he gave them mazon (provision, food) in abundance. And he took for them many wives.</span>
