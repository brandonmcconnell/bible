<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:38; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:26; VAYIKRA 9:23–24"> Now when Sh'lomo had made an end of davening, the eish came down from Shomayim, and consumed the olah and the zevakhim; and the kavod Hashem filled HaBeis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HISGALUS 15:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 5:14; SHEMOT 24:17"> And the Kohanim could not enter into the Beis Hashem, because the kavod Hashem had filled the Beis Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 5:13; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:21; LUKAS 1:50"> And when all the Bnei Yisroel saw how the eish came down, and the kavod Hashem upon HaBeis, they bowed themselves with their faces to the ground upon the ritzpah (floor, pavement) and worshiped, and gave thanks to Hashem, saying, Ki tov ki l'olam chasdo (For He is good; for His mercy endureth forever).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:62–63"> Then HaMelech and kol HaAm offered zevach before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:22; YECHEZKEL 45:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:24"> And HaMelech Sh'lomo offered zevach of 22,000 bakar, and 120,000 tzon; so HaMelech and kol HaAm dedicated the Beis HaElohim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 5:12; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:16–21; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:3"> And the Kohanim stood at their mishmerot (watches); the Levi'im also with kelei shir Hashem, (musical instruments of Hashem) which Dovid HaMelech had made to give thanks to Hashem — ki l'oham chasdo (for His mercy endureth forever) — whenever Dovid praised by their means; and the Kohanim sounded khatzotzrot (trumpets) opposite them, and kol Yisroel stood.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:64–66; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:10–12; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:14"> Moreover Sh'lomo set apart as kodesh the toch hekhatzer (center of the courtyard) that was in front of the Beis Hashem; for there he offered HaOlot and the chelvei hashelamim (fat of peace offerings), because the Mizbe'ach hanechoshet which Sh'lomo had made was not able to receive the Olot, and the Mincha, and HaChalavim (the fats).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:18; MELACHIM ALEF 8:65; NECHEMYAH 8:13–18"> Also at the same time Sh'lomo kept the Chag<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See v. 9">a</a></sup>shivat yamim, and kol Yisroel with him, a Kahal Gadol Me'od, from the Levo (Approach) Chamat unto the Wadi Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:36; MELACHIM ALEF 8:65; YOEL 1:14"> And on the Yom HaShemini they held an Atzeret (solemn assembly); for they kept the chanukat HaMizbe'ach (dedication of the altar),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., the Beis Hamikdash">b</a></sup>shivat yamim, and the Chag<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Sukkot">c</a></sup>shivat yamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:66; TEHILLIM 100:2; DEVARIM 12:18"> And on the three and twentieth day of the seventh month he sent HaAm away into their ohalim joyful and tovei lev for the goodness that Hashem had showed unto Dovid, and to Sh'lomo, and to Yisroel His Am.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:1–9; KOHELET 2:10–11; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:1"> Thus Sh'lomo finished the Beis Hashem, and the Beis HaMelech; and all that came into the lev Sh'lomo to do in the Beis Hashem, and in his own palace, he prosperously effected.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:1; MELACHIM ALEF 9:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:16"> And Hashem appeared to Sh'lomo balailah, and said unto him, I have heard thy tefillah, and have chosen this makom for Myself for a Beis Zevach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 6:26–28; DEVARIM 11:17; HISGALUS 11:6"> When I shut up Shomayim that there be no matar, or when I command the locusts to devour HaAretz, or when I send dever among Ami (My People);</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EKHAH 3:40–41; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:9–10; YESHAYAH 55:6–7"> If Ami, which are called by Shmi, shall humble themselves, and daven, and seek my face, and turn from their derakhim hara'im (wicked ways), then will I hear from Shomayim, and will forgive their chattat, and will heal their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:12; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 6:40; NECHEMYAH 1:6"> Now Mine eyes shall be open, and Mine ears attentive unto the tefillah that is made in Makom HaZeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:15; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:12; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 6:5–6"> For now have I chosen and set apart as kodesh HaBeis HaZeh, that Shmi may be there ad olam, and Mine eyes and Mine lev shall be there kol hayamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 3:7; YECHEZKEL 36:27; MELACHIM ALEF 11:38"> And as for thee, if thou wilt walk before Me, as Dovid Avicha walked, and do according to all that I have commanded thee, and shalt observe My chukkot and My mishpatim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 6:16; YIRMEYAH 33:20–21; SHMUEL BAIS 7:13–16"> Then will I establish the kisse of thy Malchut, just as I have covenanted with Dovid Avicha, saying, Lo yikaret lecha ish moshel b'Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:14; DEVARIM 28:15; VAYIKRA 26:33–46"> But if ye turn away, and forsake My chukkot and My mitzvot, which I have set before you, and shall go and serve elohim acherim, and worship them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 17:20; DEVARIM 28:37; YIRMEYAH 24:9"> Then will I uproot them out of My Adamah which I have given them; and HaBeis HaZeh, which I have set apart as kodesh for Shmi, will I cast out of My sight, and will make it to be a Mashal (byword) and an object of ridicule among Kol haAmim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 22:8–9; YIRMEYAH 16:10–12; YIRMEYAH 19:8"> And HaBeis HaZeh, which is elyon, shall be an astonishment to every one that passeth by it; so that he shall say, Why hath Hashem done thus unto HaAretz HaZot, and unto HaBeis Hazeh?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DANIEL 9:12; EKHAH 2:16–17; YECHEZKEL 14:23"> And it shall be answered, Because they forsook Hashem Elohei Avoteihem, Who brought them forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim, and they laid hold on elohim acherim, and worshiped them, and served them; therefore hath He brought all this ra'ah upon them.</span>
</div>
