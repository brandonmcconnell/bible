<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:7–8; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:13; HOSHEA 5:4"> And Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) sent to kol Yisroel and Yehudah, and wrote iggerot also to Ephrayim and Menasheh, that they should come to the Beis Hashem at Yerushalayim, to keep the Pesach Hashem Elohei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 9:10–11; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 13:1–3; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:13"> For HaMelech had conferred and agreed with his sarim, and kol HaKahal in Yerushalayim, to keep the Pesach in the second month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:34; SHEMOT 12:6; SHEMOT 12:18"> For they could not keep it at its time, because the Kohanim had not set themselves apart as kodesh in sufficient numbers, neither had HaAm gathered themselves together to Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 13:4"> And the thing pleased HaMelech and kol HaKahal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHOFETIM 20:1; VAYIKRA 23:2; ESTHER 8:8–10"> So they established a decree to make proclamation throughout kol Yisroel, from Be'er-Sheva even to Dan, that they should come to keep Pesach unto Hashem Elohei Yisroel in Yerushalayim, for not many had kept Pesach as prescribed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 51:31; ESTHER 8:14; MELACHIM BAIS 15:19"> So the runners went with the iggerot from HaMelech and his sarim throughout kol Yisroel and Yehudah, and according to the commandment of HaMelech, saying, Ye Bnei Yisroel, shuvu (turn, return) unto Hashem Elohei Avraham, Yitzchak, and Yisroel, and He will return to the remnant of you, that are escaped out of the yad of the Kings of Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:8; YECHEZKEL 20:13–18; ZECHARYAH 1:3–4"> And be not ye like Avoteichem, and like your achim, which were unfaithful against Hashem Elohei Avoteihem, Who therefore gave them up to a desolation, as ye see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 32:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:10; DEVARIM 10:16"> Now be ye not stiffnecked, as Avoteichem were, but yield yourselves unto Hashem, and come to His Mikdash, which He hath set apart as kodesh l'olam; and serve Hashem Eloheichem, that the fierceness of His wrath may turn away from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YESHAYAH 55:7; MICHOH 7:18; TEHILLIM 86:5"> For if ye turn again unto Hashem, your achim and your banim shall find rachamim before them that lead them captive, so that they shall come back into HaAretz HaZot, for Hashem Eloheichem is channun (gracious) and rachum (compassionate), and will not turn away His face from you, if ye return unto Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:6; NECHEMYAH 2:19"> So the runners passed from town to town through Eretz Ephrayim and Menasheh even unto Zevulun, but they laughed them to scorn, and mocked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:25"> Nevertheless anashim of Asher and Menasheh and of Zevulun humbled themselves, and went to Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:39; YIRMEYAH 24:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:13"> Also in Yehudah the Yad HaElohim was to give them lev echad to carry out the mitzvat HaMelech and of the sarim, by the word of Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:2; TEHILLIM 84:7"> And there assembled at Yerushalayim Am Rav (Much People) to keep Chag HaMatzot in the second month, a kahal larov me'od (a very great congregation).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:24; SHMUEL BAIS 15:23; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:16"> And they arose and removed the mizbechot that were in Yerushalayim, and all the incense altars they took away, and threw them into the Wadi Kidron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:34; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 5:11; SHEMOT 19:22"> Then they slaughtered the Pesach on the fourteenth day of the second month; and the Kohanim and the Levi'im were ashamed, and they set themselves apart as kodesh, and brought olot to the Beis Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEVARIM 33:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:15; MELACHIM BAIS 11:14"> And they stood in their place after their prescribed manner, according to the Torat Moshe Ish HaElohim; the Kohanim sprinkled the dahm, which they received of the yad of the Levi'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:34; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:3–6; SHEMOT 12:6"> For there were rabbim in HaKahal that were not set apart as kodesh; therefore the Levi'im had the charge of the shechitah (ritual slaughter) of the Pesachim for every one that was not tahor and therefore could not set them apart as kodesh unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:28; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:15–16; DANIEL 9:19"> For most of HaAm, even many of Ephrayim, and Menasheh, Yissakhar, and Zevulun, had not made themselves tahor, yet did they eat the Pesach contrary to what was written. But Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) davened for them, saying, May Hashem Hatov grant kapporah to</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:3; MISHLE 23:26; IYOV 11:13"> Everyone that prepareth his lev to seek HaElohim, Hashem Elohei Avotav, though he be not tahor according to the taharat HaKodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 15:26; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:15–16; TEHILLIM 103:3"> And Hashem paid heed to Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu), and healed HaAm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:15; SHEMOT 13:6; DEVARIM 12:12"> And the Bnei Yisroel that were present at Yerushalayim kept the Chag HaMatzot shivat yamim with simchah gedolah; and the Levi'im and the Kohanim praised Hashem yom b'yom, singing with loud instruments unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:6; NECHEMYAH 9:3; YESHAYAH 40:1–2"> And Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) spoke words of comfort unto all the Levi'im who taught the seichel tov of Hashem, and they did eat the mo'ed seven days, offering zivkhei shelamim, and making confession to Hashem Elohei Avoteihem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:65; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:9"> And the kol HaKahal conferred and agreed to celebrate still another shivat yamim; and they kept another shivat yamim with simcha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:34; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:7–8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:8"> For Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) Melech Yehudah did give to the Kahal a thousand bulls and seven thousand sheep; and the sarim gave to the Kahal a thousand bulls and ten thousand sheep; and a great number of Kohanim set themselves apart as kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 16:10–11"> And kol Kehal Yehudah, with the Kohanim and the Levi'im, and kol HaKahal that came out of Yisroel, and the gerim that came out of Eretz Yisroel, and that dwelt in Yehudah, rejoiced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:8–10"> So there was simchah gedolah in Yerushalayim; for since the days of Sh'lomo Ben Dovid Melech Yisroel there was not the like in Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEVARIM 26:15; TEHILLIM 68:5; BAMIDBAR 6:23–26"> Then the Kohanim and the Levi'im arose and blessed HaAm, and their voice was heard, and their tefillah came up to His Ma'on Kodesh, even unto Shomayim.</span>
</div>
