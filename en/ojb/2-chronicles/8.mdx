<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:10–28"> And it came to pass at the end of esrim shanah, wherein Sh'lomo had built the Beis Hashem, and his own bais (palace),</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:11–18"> That the cities which Churam had restored to Sh'lomo, Sh'lomo rebuilt them, and caused the Bnei Yisroel to dwell there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 13:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 18:3; SHMUEL BAIS 8:3"> And Sh'lomo went to Chamat Tzovah, and captured it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:17–19"> And he built Tadmor in the midbar, and all the storage towns, which he built in Chamat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 7:24; YEHOSHUA 16:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:7"> Also he rebuilt Upper Beit-Choron, and Lower Beit-Choron, fortified cities, with walls, gates, and bars,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 1:14"> And Ba'alat, and all the storage towns that Sh'lomo had, and all the towns for the chariots, and the towns for the parashim (horsemen, cavalry) all the cheshek Sh'lomo (desire of Solomon) that he desired to build in Yerushalayim, and in the Levanon, and throughout all the eretz of his Memshalet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:18–21; DEVARIM 7:1; MELACHIM ALEF 9:20–22"> As for kol haAm that were left of the Chitti, and HaEmori, and the Perizzi, and the Chivi, and the Yevusi, who were not of Yisroel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 16:10; MELACHIM ALEF 9:21; MELACHIM ALEF 4:6"> From their banim, who were left after them in HaAretz, whom the Bnei Yisroel consumed not, them did Sh'lomo conscript for forced labor until yom hazeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 8:11–12; SHEMOT 19:5–6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:31"> But of the Bnei Yisroel did Sh'lomo make no avadim for his melachah; for they were anshei milchamah, and commanders of his officers, and commanders of his chariots and parash.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:23; MELACHIM ALEF 5:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:18"> And these were the chief of the officials of Sh'lomo HaMelech, even two hundred and fifty, that exercised authority over HaAm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 3:1; MELACHIM ALEF 7:8; MELACHIM ALEF 9:24"> And Sh'lomo brought up the Bat Pharaoh out of Ir Dovid unto the palace that he had built for her, for he said, Isha li (wife for me) shall not dwell in the Bais Dovid Melech Yisroel, because the places whereunto the Aron Hashem hath come are Kodesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:8; YOCHANAN 10:23"> Then Sh'lomo offered up Olot unto Hashem on the Mizbe'ach Hashem, which he had built before the Ulam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:16; SHEMOT 29:38–42; YECHEZKEL 46:3–15"> Even according to the requirement of each day on its day, offering according to the mitzvat Moshe, on the Shabbatot, and on Rosh Chodesh, and on the Mo'adot, three times in the year, even in the Chag HaMatzot, and in the Chag HaShavuot, and in the Chag HaSukkot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 9:17; EZRA 6:18; NECHEMYAH 12:36"> And he appointed, according to the mishpat Dovid Aviv, the machlekot (divisions) of the Kohanim to their avodah service, and the Levi'im to their mishmerot (watches), to praise and minister before the Kohanim, as the requirement of every yom required; the Sho'arim (Gatekeepers) also by their machlekot (divisions) at every sha'ar; for thus was the mitzvot Dovid Ish HaElohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:12; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 9:29; MELACHIM ALEF 7:51"> And they deviated not from the mitzvat HaMelech unto the Kohanim and Levi'im concerning any matter, or concerning the otzarot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 5:18; MELACHIM ALEF 6:7"> Now all the work of Sh'lomo was carried out from the yom musad (day of the foundation) of the Beis Hashem until its completion. So the Beis Hashem was perfected.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 14:22; DEVARIM 2:8; BAMIDBAR 33:35"> Then went Sh'lomo to Etzyon-Gever, and to Eilot, on the seacoast of Eretz Edom.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="T.N. See Ep 2:20in connection with the musad (foundation in 2Chr 8:16) where the OJBC speaks about Shluchim and Nevi'im and Moshiach as the Rosh Pinah">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:13; KOHELET 2:8"> And Churam sent him by the hands of his avadim oniyot, and avadim that were experienced seamen; and they went with the avadim of Sh'lomo to Ophir, and took from there four hundred and fifty talents of zahav, and brought them to Sh'lomo HaMelech.</span>
</div>
