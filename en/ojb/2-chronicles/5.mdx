<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZRA 3:11; YIRMEYAH 33:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:3"> It came even to pass, as the trumpeters and meshorarim were as one, making kol echad (one voice, one sound) to be heard in praising and thanking Hashem; and when they lifted up their voice with the trumpets and cymbals and musical instruments, and praised Hashem, saying, Ki tov ki l'olam chasdo (For He is good; for His mercy endureth forever), that then the Beis was filled with an anan, even the Beis Hashem,</span>
