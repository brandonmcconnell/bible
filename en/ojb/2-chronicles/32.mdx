<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:13–37; MELACHIM BAIS 17:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:1–2"> After these things, and haemes thereof, Sancheriv (Sennacherib) Melech Assyria came, and invaded Yehudah, and laid siege against the fortified cities, and thought to conquer them for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKAS 9:53; LUKAS 9:51; MELACHIM BAIS 12:17"> And when Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) saw that Sancheriv was come, and that he was purposed to make milchamah against Yerushalayim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MISHLE 24:6; MISHLE 20:18; MISHLE 15:22"> He took counsel with his sarim and his gibborim to cover over the waters of the springs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Spring of Gihon">a</a></sup>which were outside the Ir; and they did support him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:30; MELACHIM BAIS 18:9; MELACHIM ALEF 19:21"> So there was gathered Am Rav together, who covered the springs, and the wadi that flowed through the midst of HaAretz, saying, Why should the melachim of Assyria come, and find mayim rabbim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:23; MELACHIM ALEF 9:24; MELACHIM BAIS 25:4"> Also he strengthened himself, and repaired all the chomah that was broken down, and raised it on the migdalot, and another chomah outside of it, and repaired Millo<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="terraces">b</a></sup>in Ir Dovid, and made weapons and shields in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:22; NECHEMYAH 8:16"> And he appointed sarim milchamot over HaAm, and gathered them together before him in the rechov of the Sha'ar HaIr, and spoke encouragingly to them, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 6:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:31; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:10"> Chizku (be strong) and courageous, be not afraid nor dismayed because of the melech Assyria, nor because of all the vast army that is with him, for there be more with us than with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 17:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:17; YESHAYAH 41:10"> With him is zero'a basar, but with us is Hashem Eloheinu to help us, and to fight milchamoteinu (our battles). And HaAm took heart upon the words of Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) Melech Yehudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:17; YEHOSHUA 10:31; YESHAYAH 36:2"> After this did Sancheriv melech Assyria send his avadim to Yerushalayim, (but he himself laid siege against Lakhish, with all his forces with him,) unto Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) Melech Yehudah, and unto kol Yehudah that were in Yerushalayim, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:19; YESHAYAH 36:4"> Thus saith Sancheriv melech Assyria, Whereon do ye base confidence, that ye abide in the siege in Yerushalayim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:43; TEHILLIM 11:1–3; YESHAYAH 36:18"> Doth not Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) mislead you to give over yourselves to die by ra'av and by tzama, saying, Hashem Eloheinu shall deliver us out of the yad Melech Assyria?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 31:1; DEVARIM 12:13–14; DEVARIM 12:26–27"> Is not this the same Chizkiyah (Yechizkiyahu) that has removed His high places and His mizbechot, and commanded Yehudah and Yerushalayim, saying, Ye shall worship before Mizbe'ach echad, and burn ketoret upon it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:33–35; YESHAYAH 44:8–10; DANIEL 4:30"> Know ye not what I and avotai have done unto all the people of other lands? Were the elohei goyei ha'aratzot able to deliver their land out of mine yad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YESHAYAH 10:11–12; SHEMOT 15:9–11; YESHAYAH 42:8"> Who was there among kol elohei haGoyim that avotai utterly destroyed, that could save his people out of mine yad, that Eloheichem should be able to save you out of mine yad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 5:2; DANIEL 3:15; YESHAYAH 36:18"> Now therefore let not Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) deceive you, nor mislead you on this manner, neither yet believe him, for no g-d of any goy or mamlachah was able to deliver his people out of mine yad, and out of the yad of avotai. How much less shall Eloheichem deliver you out of mine yad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="TEHILLIM 73:9; YOCHANAN 15:21; IYOV 15:25–26"> And his avadim spoke yet more against Hashem HaElohim, and against His eved Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YESHAYAH 37:14; MELACHIM BAIS 19:12; YESHAYAH 37:28–29"> He wrote also sefarim to blaspheme Hashem Elohei Yisroel, and to speak against Him, saying, As the elohei goyei ha'aratzot have not saved their people out of mine yad, so shall not the Elohei Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) save his people out of mine yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:10; MELACHIM BAIS 18:26–28; SHMUEL ALEF 17:26"> Then they cried with a kol gadol in the language of the Yehudim unto the Am Yerushalayim that were on the chomah, to frighten them, and to terrorize them, that they might capture the Ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 19:18; YESHAYAH 2:8; YIRMEYAH 32:30"> And they spoke against the Elohei Yerushalayim, as against the g-ds of the peoples of the land, which were the work of the hands of ha'adam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 91:14–15; YESHAYAH 37:14–20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:11"> And for this cause Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) HaMelech, and Yeshayahu ben Amotz, HaNavi, davened and cried out to Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YESHAYAH 37:36–38; TEHILLIM 76:5; TEHILLIM 18:50"> And Hashem sent a malach, which destroyed all the fighting gibborim, and the nagid and sar in the machaneh of the melech Assyria. So he withdrew in boshet panim (shamefaced disgrace) to his own land. And when he was entered the bais elohav, they that came forth of his own loins<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e., his sons">c</a></sup>cut him down there with the cherev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YESHAYAH 31:4–5; TEHILLIM 18:48–50; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:5"> Thus Hashem saved Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) and the inhabitants of Yerushalayim from the yad Sancheriv Melech Ashur, and from the yad of all other, and guided them on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 17:5; MATTITYAHU 2:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 1:1"> And many brought minchah unto Hashem to Yerushalayim, and migdanot (expensive gifts) to Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) Melech Yehudah, so that he was magnified in the sight of Kol HaGoyim from thenceforth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YESHAYAH 38:1–8; MELACHIM BAIS 20:1–11; YESHAYAH 38:21–22"> In those yamim Chizkiyah (Yechizkiyahu) became ill, to the point of mot, and davened unto Hashem, and He answered him, and He gave him a mofet.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See 2Kgs 20:1-11">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 24:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 26:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:31"> But Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) did not render back according to the benefit done unto him; for his lev was lifted up in pride; therefore there was ketzef (wrath) upon him, and upon Yehudah and Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 26:18–19; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:27–28; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:12"> Notwithstanding Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) humbled himself for the pride of his lev, both he and the inhabitants of Yerushalayim, so that the ketzef Hashem came not upon them in the days of Chizkiyah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 27:25–31; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 1:12; MISHLE 10:22"> And Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) had exceeding much osher and kavod, and he made himself otzarot for kesef, and for zahav, and for precious stones, and for spices, and for moginim, and for all manner of valuable things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 7:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 26:10; MELACHIM ALEF 4:26"> Silos also for the increase of dagan, and tirosh and yitzhar (pure olive oil); and stalls for the livestock, and pens for all the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:12; MISHLE 10:22; SHMUEL ALEF 2:7"> Moreover he built him towns, and possessions of tzon and bakar in abundance, for Elohim had given him wealth very much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:33; YESHAYAH 22:9–11; TEHILLIM 1:1–3"> This same Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) also covered over the upper source of the Gichon Spring and tunneled it straight down to the west side of Ir Dovid. Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) prospered in all his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEVARIM 8:2; MISHLE 17:3; BERESHIS 22:1"> Howbeit in the business of the ambassadors of the sarim of Babylon, who sent unto him to inquire of the mofet that was done in HaAretz, HaElohim left him, to test him, that He might know all that was in his lev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YESHAYAH 36:1–22; MELACHIM BAIS 18:1–20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 31:20–21"> Now the rest of the acts of Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu), and his acts of chesed, hinei, they are written in the Chazon Yeshayahu ben Amotz HaNavi, and in the Sefer Melachim of Yehudah and Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MISHLE 10:7; MELACHIM ALEF 1:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 16:14"> And Chizkiyahu (Yechizkiyahu) slept with Avotav, and they buried him on the ascent in kivrei Bnei Dovid; and all Yehudah and the inhabitants of Yerushalayim did him kavod at his mot. And Menasheh bno reigned in his place.</span>
