<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:18–20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:8; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13–14"> And he appointed Shofetim in HaAretz throughout all the fortified cities of Yehudah, city by city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:17; VAYIKRA 19:15; KOHELET 5:8"> And said to the Shofetim, Take heed what ye do, for ye judge not for adam, but for Hashem, Who is with you in the devar mishpat (verdict).</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:7; SHMUEL BAIS 23:3; DEVARIM 1:16–17"> And he charged them, saying, Thus shall ye do in the yirat Hashem, be'emunah and with a levav shalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEVARIM 17:8–13; YECHEZKEL 33:6; YECHEZKEL 3:18–21"> And what riv soever shall come before you of your achim that dwell in your cities, between dahm and dahm, between torah and mitzvah, chukkot and mishpatim, ye shall even warn them that they trespass not against Hashem, and so ketzef (wrath) come upon you, and upon your achim; this do, and ye shall not trespass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:8; TEHILLIM 18:25–26; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:1"> And, hinei, Amaryahu Kohen HaRosh is over you in all matters of Hashem; and Zevadyahu ben Yishmael, the Nagid of the Bais Yehudah, for all the matters of HaMelech; also the Levi'im shall be shoterim (officers) before you. Deal courageously, and Hashem be with hatov.</span>
