</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 12:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:21; MELACHIM BAIS 18:15"> Then Asa brought out kesef and zahav out of the otzarot of the Beis Hashem and of the Bais HaMelech, and sent to Ben-Hadad Melech Aram (Syria), the one ruling in Damascus, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TEHILLIM 15:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 18:3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:16"> There is a brit between me and thee, as there was between Avi and Avicha. Hinei, I have sent thee kesef and zahav; come, break thy brit with Ba'asha Melech Yisroel, that he may withdraw from me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHOFETIM 20:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 17:12; MELACHIM ALEF 15:20"> And Ben-Hadad paid heed unto HaMelech Asa, and sent the officers of his forces against the cities of Yisroel, and they struck down Iyon, and Dan, and Abel-Mayim, and all the storage cities of Naphtali.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And it came to pass, when Ba'asha heard it, that he left off building of Ramah, and let his work cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 21:17; YEHOSHUA 15:38; YESHAYAH 10:29"> Then Asa HaMelech took all Yehudah, and they carried away the stones of Ramah, and the timber thereof, wherewith Ba'asha was building; and he built therewith Geva and Mitzpah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:2; MELACHIM ALEF 16:1; TEHILLIM 146:3–6"> And at that time Chanani HaRo'eh came to Asa Melech Yehudah, and said unto him, Because thou hast relied on Melech Aram, and not relied on Hashem Eloheicha, therefore is the army of Melech Aram escaped out of thine yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 13:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 16:7"> Were not the Cushim and the Luvim a huge army, with very many chariots and parashim? Yet, because thou didst rely on Hashem, He delivered them into thine yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:12; MISHLE 15:3; YIRMEYAH 16:17"> For the eyes of Hashem run to and fro kol HaAretz, to strengthen them whose lev is shalem (wholehearted) toward Him. Herein thou hast done foolishly; therefore from henceforth thou shalt have milchamot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 18:26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:23–24; SHMUEL BAIS 12:13"> Then Asa was wroth with the ro'eh, put him in a prison house; for he was in a rage with him because of this thing. And Asa oppressed some of HaAm at the same time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:15; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:29; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:27"> And, hinei, the acts of Asa, harishonim and ha'acharonim, see, they are written in the Sefer HaMelachim Yehudah and Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 17:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:22; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 10:14"> And Asa in the thirty and ninth year of his malchut (reign) became diseased in his raglayim, until his disease was exceedingly great. Yet even in his disease he sought not Hashem, but only the rofe'im.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 15:24"> And Asa slept with his Avotav, and died in the one and fortieth year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 50:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 21:19; YIRMEYAH 34:5"> And they buried him in his own kever (sepulchre, burial cave), which he had cut for himself in Ir Dovid, and laid him on a mishkav (bed) which was filled with sweet spices and perfumes prepared by the apothecaries art; and they burned for him a very large serefah gedolah (great fire, pyre).</span>
</div>
