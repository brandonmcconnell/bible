<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="TEHILLIM 83:5–8; YESHAYAH 16:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:1"> It came to pass after this also, that the Bnei Moav, and the Bnei Ammon, and with them some Ammonim, came against Yehoshaphat to make milchamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 14:7; SHMUEL ALEF 23:29; YEHOSHUA 15:62"> Then there came some that told Yehoshaphat, saying, There cometh a great multitude against thee from beyond the Yam (Dead Sea), from Aram; and, hinei, they are in Chatzetzon-Tamar, which is Ein-Gedi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DANIEL 9:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:3; SHMUEL ALEF 7:6"> And Yehoshaphat feared, and set his face to inquire of Hashem, and proclaimed a tzom throughout all Yehudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="TEHILLIM 50:15; TEHILLIM 60:10–12; TEHILLIM 69:35"> And Yehudah gathered themselves together, to ask help of Hashem, even from all the towns of Yehudah they came to seek help of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 19:15–19; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 6:12–13; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:31"> And Yehoshaphat stood in the Kehal Yehudah and Yerushalayim, in the Beis Hashem, before the khatzer hachadashah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:39; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:11–12; DANIEL 4:17"> And he said, Hashem Elohei Avoteinu, art not Thou Elohim in Shomayim? And moshel (rulest) not Thou over kol mamlechot haGoyim? And in Thine yad is there not ko'ach and gevurah, so that none is able to withstand thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YESHAYAH 41:8; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:23; TEHILLIM 44:2"> Art not Thou Eloheinu, Who didst drive out the inhabitants of this land before Thy people Yisroel, and gavest it to the Zera Avraham ohav'cha l'olam (Thy friend forever)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 6:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:4"> And they dwelt therein, and have built Thee a Mikdash therein for Thy Shem, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 6:28–30; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 6:20; MELACHIM ALEF 8:33"> If, when ra'ah cometh upon us, as the cherev, shefot, or dever, or ra'av (famine), we stand before HaBeis HaZeh, and in Thy presence, (for Thy Shem is in HaBeis HaZeh,) and cry unto Thee in tzarateinu (our affliction), then Thou wilt hear and save.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:19; DEVARIM 2:4–5; DEVARIM 2:9"> And now, hinei, the Bnei Ammon and Moav and Mt Se'ir, whom Thou wouldest not let Yisroel invade, when they came out of Eretz Mitzrayim, but they turned from them, and destroyed them not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TEHILLIM 83:3–12; SHOFETIM 11:23–24; YIRMEYAH 18:20"> Hinei, what gomelim (ones repaying) they are to us, to come to drive us out of Thy yerushah (inheritance), which Thou hast given us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="TEHILLIM 121:1–2; TEHILLIM 25:15; TEHILLIM 141:8"> O Eloheinu, wilt Thou not judge them? For we have no ko'ach against this great multitude that cometh against us; neither know we what to do, but our eyes are upon Thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZRA 10:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:5; DEVARIM 29:10"> And all Yehudah stood before Hashem, with their little ones, their nashim, and their banim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 24:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:31"> Then upon Yachaziel ben Zecharyahu ben Benayah ben Ye'iel ben Matanyah, a Levi of the Bnei Asaph, came the Ruach Hashem in the midst of the Kahal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:7–8; DEVARIM 31:6; SHEMOT 14:13–14"> And he said, Pay heed ye, all Yehudah, and ye inhabitants of Yerushalayim, and thou Melech Yehoshaphat. Thus saith Hashem unto you: Be not afraid nor dismayed by reason of this great multitude; for the milchamah is not yours, but that of Elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Makhar (tomorrow) go ye down against them; hinei, they come up by the Ascent of Tzitz; and ye shall find them at the end of the wadi, before the midbar of Yeruel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHEMOT 14:13–14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:2; YESHAYAH 41:10"> Ye shall not need to fight in this; take up your battle positions, stand ye firm, and see the Yeshu'at Hashem with you, O Yehudah and Yerushalayim. Fear not, nor be dismayed; makhar (tomorrow) go out against them, for Hashem will be with you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:31; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:3; BERESHIS 24:26"> And Yehoshaphat bowed his head with his face to the ground; and all Yehudah and the inhabitants of Yerushalayim fell down before Hashem, worshiping Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:16–22; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 16:42; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 16:5"> And the Levi'im, of the Bnei HaKehatim, and of the Bnei HaKorchim, stood up to praise Hashem Elohei Yisroel with an exceeding kol gadol.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:31; YOCHANAN 14:1; YOCHANAN 11:40"> And they rose early in the boker, and went forth into the Midbar Tekoa; and as they went forth, Yehoshaphat stood and said, Hear me, O Yehudah, and ye inhabitants of Yerushalayim. Ha'aminu BaHashem Eloheichem (Believe in Hashem Eloheichem), so shall ye be established; have faith in His Nevi'im, so shall ye be victorious.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="TEHILLIM 107:1; TEHILLIM 106:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 5:13"> And when he had consulted with HaAm, he appointed meshorarim (singers) unto Hashem, and that should praise the hadrat kodesh, as they went out before the army, and to say, Hodu L'Adonoi ki l'olam chasdo (Give thanks to Hashem, for His mercy endureth forever).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 14:20; YESHAYAH 19:2; YECHEZKEL 38:21"> And when they began with rinnah and tehillah, Hashem set me'arevim (ambushers) against the Bnei Ammon, Moav, and Mt Se'ir, which were come against Yehudah, and they were struck down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHOFETIM 7:22; SHMUEL ALEF 14:20; YECHEZKEL 35:2–3"> For the Bnei Ammon and Moav stood up against the inhabitants of Mt Se'ir, utterly to slay and destroy them. And when they had made an end of the inhabitants of Se'ir, every one helped to destroy another.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YESHAYAH 37:36; TEHILLIM 110:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:22"> And when Yehudah came toward the mitzpeh (guard tower) in the midbar, they looked toward the multitude, and, hinei, they were pegarim (corpses) fallen on the ground, and there was no escapee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 30:19–20; MELACHIM BAIS 7:9–16; BAMIDBAR 31:15"> And when Yehoshaphat and his people came to carry off the plunder of them, they found among them in abundance both property with the pegarim, and articles of value, which they stripped off for themselves, more than they could carry away, and they were yamim shelosha in gathering of the plunder, it was so much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHEMOT 17:15; BERESHIS 32:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 5:9"> And on the fourth day they assembled themselves in the Berakhah Valley, for there they blessed Hashem; therefore the shem of that place was called, the Berakhah Valley unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 12:43; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:20; TEHILLIM 30:1"> Then they returned, kol ish Yehudah and Yerushalayim, and Yehoshaphat in the forefront of them, to go again to Yerushalayim with simcha; for Hashem had made them to rejoice over their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="HISGALUS 14:2–3; TEHILLIM 57:8; TEHILLIM 92:3"> And they came to Yerushalayim with nevalim and kinnorot and khatzotzerot unto the Beis Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 17:10; MELACHIM BAIS 7:6; SHEMOT 23:27"> And the Pachad Elohim was on all the kingdoms of those lands, when they had heard that Hashem fought against the oyevei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:15; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:6–7; SHMUEL BAIS 7:1"> So the Malchut Yehoshaphat was at peace, for Elohav gave him rest on every side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:41–44"> And Yehoshaphat reigned over Yehudah. He was thirty and five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned twenty and five years in Yerushalayim. And shem immo was Azuvah bat Shilchi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:2–5; MELACHIM ALEF 15:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:11–13"> And he walked in the derech Aviv Asa, and departed not from it, doing that which was yashar in the sight of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 17:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:14"> Howbeit the high places were not removed; for HaAm as yet had not set their hearts unto Elohei Avoteihem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 16:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:2; MELACHIM ALEF 16:7"> Now the rest of the acts of Yehoshaphat, harishonim and ha'acharonim, hinei, they are written in the Annals of Yehu ben Chanani, who is recorded in the Sefer of the Melachim of Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:48–49; MELACHIM BAIS 1:2–16"> And after this did Yehoshaphat Melech Yehudah join himself in alliance with Achazyah Melech Yisroel, who did very wickedly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 10:22; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:21; MELACHIM ALEF 9:26"> And he joined himself with him to make oniyot (ships) to go to Tarshish, and they made the oniyot in Etzyon-Gever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MISHLE 13:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:21"> Then Eli'ezer ben Dodavahu of Mareshah prophesied against Yehoshaphat, saying, Because thou hast joined thyself in alliance with Achazyahu, Hashem hath wrecked thy ma'asim. And the oniyot were wrecked, that they were not able to sail to Tarshish.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="T.N. Yehoshaphat had a weakness for entangling himself with non-believers and suffering the consequences.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
