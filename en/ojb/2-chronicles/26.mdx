<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 22:1; MATTITYAHU 1:8–9; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:25"> Then kol HaAm Yehudah took Uziyahu, who was sixteen years old, and made him Melech in the place of Aviv Amatzyahu.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 16:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:28; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:23"> He built up Eilot, and restored it to Yehudah, after that HaMelech<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Amatzyahu">a</a></sup>slept with Avotav.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 15:2–3; AMOS 1:1; HOSHEA 1:1"> Sixteen years old was Uziyahu when he began to reign, and he reigned fifty and two shanah in Yerushalayim. Shem immo also was Yecholyah of Yerushalayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:2"> And he did that which was yashar in the eyes of Hashem, according to all that Aviv Amatzyahu did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 24:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:13"> And he sought Elohim in the days of Zecharyahu, who had understanding in the visions of HaElohim; and as long as he sought Hashem, HaElohim made him to have success.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YESHAYAH 14:29; SHMUEL ALEF 5:6; SHMUEL ALEF 5:1"> And he went forth and warred against the Pelishtim (Philistines), and broke down the chomat Gat, and the chomat Yavneh, and the chomat Ashdod, and built towns near Ashdod, and among the Pelishtim (Philistines).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 21:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:11"> And HaElohim helped him against the Pelishtim (Philistines), and against the Arabs that dwelt in Gur-Ba'al, and the Me'unim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 17:11; BERESHIS 19:38"> And the Ammonim gave minchah to Uziyahu, and shmo spread abroad even to the approach to Mitzrayim; for he strengthened himself exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 3:13; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:23; MELACHIM BAIS 14:13"> Moreover Uziyahu built migdalim (towers) in Yerushalayim at the Sha'ar HaPinnah, and at the Sha'ar of the Gey, and at the Miktzo'a, and he fortified them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 26:18–21; YESHAYAH 29:17; MELACHIM BAIS 3:4"> Also he built migdalim in the midbar, and dug borot rabbim (many wells), for he had much livestock, both in the Shefelah (Lowlands), and in the Mishor (Plains, Level Country); ikkarim (field workers, farmers) also, and koremim (vineyard workers, vine-keepers) in the hills, and in the Carmel, for he loved adamah (the soil).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 5:2"> Moreover Uziyahu had an army of fighting men that went out to tzava (battle) in troops, according to the number of their mustering by the yad Ye'iel the Sofer and Ma'aseiyahu the Shoter (Officer), under the yad Chananyahu, one of the officials of HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The whole mispar (number) of the Rashei HaAvot over the mighty warriors were two thousand and six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:1"> And under their yad was a battle group, three hundred thousand and seven thousand and five hundred, that made milchamah with mighty ko'ach, to help HaMelech against the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHOFETIM 20:16; SHMUEL ALEF 17:49"> And Uziyahu prepared for them throughout all the army moginim (shields), and spears, and helmets, and armor, and bows, and slingstones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 31:4; MATTITYAHU 4:24; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:7"> And he made in Yerushalayim machines designed by inventive engineers, for use on the migdalim and upon the corners of the city wall, to shoot khitzim (arrows) and avanim gedolot. And shmo spread far abroad, for he was marvellously helped, until when chazak (he became powerful).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:25; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:19; MISHLE 16:18"> But when he became powerful, his lev was lifted up to his downfall, for he was unfaithful against Hashem Elohav, and he entered into the Heikhal Hashem to burn ketoret upon the Mizbe'ach HaKetoret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:28"> And Azaryahu HaKohen went in after him, and with him shemonim Kohanim of Hashem, that were men of courage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 30:7–8; BAMIDBAR 18:7; YIRMEYAH 13:18"> And they stood up against Uziyahu HaMelech, and said unto him, It is not for you, Uziyahu, to burn ketoret unto Hashem, but to the Kohanim the Bnei Aharon, that are hamekudashim (consecrated and ritually pure) to burn ketoret. Go out from the Mikdash, for thou hast been treacherous; neither shall it be for thine kavod (honor) from Hashem Elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 12:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:16; MELACHIM BAIS 5:25–27"> Then Uziyahu became angry, and had a mikteret (censer) in his yad to burn ketoret, and while he was raging with the Kohanim, the tzara'at even broke out on his metzach (forehead) before the Kohanim in the Beis Hashem, near the Mizbe'ach HaKetoret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:22; ESTHER 6:12; VAYIKRA 14:34"> And Azaryahu the Kohen HaRosh, and all the Kohanim, looked upon him, and, hinei, he was leprous on his metzach, and they thrust him out from there; yea, he himself hasted also to go out, because Hashem nun-gimmel-ayin-vav.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="had smitten him, afflicted him, see Isa 53:8,10where Hashem has the “nega” fall on not on this Ben Dovid Uziyahu, but on Moshiach Ben Dovid">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="VAYIKRA 13:46; BAMIDBAR 12:15; MELACHIM BAIS 7:3"> And Uziyahu HaMelech was metzorah (a leper) unto his yom mot, and dwelt in a leper asylum house, because of his leprosy; for he was nigzar<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Isa 53:8which uses same word nigzar, “excluded,” of Moshiach who was nigzar from the land of the living, meaning Moshiach died">c</a></sup>from the Beis Hashem; and Yotam bno was over the Bais HaMelech, governing the Am HaAretz.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YESHAYAH 1:1; YESHAYAH 6:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:29"> Now the rest of the acts of Uziyah, harishonim and ha'acharonim, did Yeshayahu ben Amotz, HaNavi, record.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:27; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 21:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 26:18"> So Uziyahu slept with Avotav, and they buried him with Avotav in the sadeh hakevurah which belonged to the Melachim; for they said, He is a metzorah. And Yotam bno reigned in his place. Yotam was twenty and five shanah when he began to reign, and he was king sixteen shanah in Yerushalayim.</span>
</div>
