<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:4–6; TEHILLIM 135:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:6"> But I have built Thee a lofty Beis, even a place for Thee to dwell olamim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:29; MELACHIM ALEF 8:55–61"> And HaMelech turned his face, v'yevarech at kol Kehal Yisroel, and all the Kehal Yisroel stood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="TEHILLIM 72:18–19; MATTITYAHU 24:35; TEHILLIM 68:32–35"> And he said, Baruch Hashem Elohei Yisroel, Who hath with His hands fulfilled that which He spoke with His mouth to Dovid Avi, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:24; MELACHIM ALEF 8:16; SHMUEL ALEF 13:13–14"> Since HaYom when I brought forth Ami out of Eretz Mitzrayim I chose no city among all the Shivtei Yisroel to build a Bais in, that Shmi might be there; neither chose I any ish to be a Nagid over Ami Yisroel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:4; TEHILLIM 132:13; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:13"> But I chose Yerushalayim, that Shmi might be there; and chose Dovid to be over Ami Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:1; MELACHIM ALEF 5:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:7"> Now it was in the levav Dovid Avi to build a Bais for the Shem of Hashem Elohei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:12; MARKOS 14:8; MELACHIM ALEF 8:18–21"> But Hashem said to Dovid Avi, Because it was in levavcha to build a Bais for Shmi, thou didst well in that it was in levavcha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 7:12–13; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:11–12"> Nevertheless thou shalt not build HaBeis; but bincha (your Son) which shall come forth out of thy loins, he shall build HaBeis for Shmi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="KOHELET 1:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:11"> Hashem therefore hath performed His Devar that He hath spoken; for I am risen up after Dovid Avi, and I sit on the kisse Yisroel, just as Hashem promised, and I built HaBeis for the Shem of Hashem Elohei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 5:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 5:7; MELACHIM ALEF 8:21"> And in it have I put the Aron, wherein is the Brit Hashem that He cut with the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="TEHILLIM 63:4; YESHAYAH 50:10; IYOV 11:13"> And he stood before the Mizbe'ach Hashem in front kol Kehal Yisroel, and spread forth his hands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:54; NECHEMYAH 8:4; TEHILLIM 95:6"> For Sh'lomo had made a kiyyor nechoshet (bronze platform) five cubits long, and five cubits wide, and three cubits high, and had set it in the midst of the Azarah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="see 2Chr 4:9">a</a></sup>and upon it he stood, and knelt down upon his knees before kol Kehal Yisroel, and spread forth his hands toward Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 15:11; DEVARIM 7:9; NECHEMYAH 1:5"> And said, Hashem Elohei Yisroel, ein kmocha Elohim (there is no G-d like Thee) in Shomayim, nor in Ha'aretz; shomer HaBrit, who showest chesed unto Thy avadim, that walk before Thee with all their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:9–10; SHMUEL BAIS 7:12; MELACHIM ALEF 8:24"> Thou which has been shomer over Thy eved Dovid Avi over that which Thou hast promised him; and spoke with Thy mouth, and hast fulfilled it with Thine hand, as it is this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:4; YOCHANAN 15:14–15; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:18"> Now therefore, Hashem Elohei Yisroel, be shomer over Dovid Avi to keep that which Thou hast promised him, saying, There shall not fail thee an ish in My sight to sit upon the Kisse Yisroel; if only Banecha are shomer over their way to walk in My torah, as thou hast walked before Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 11:5; YESHAYAH 41:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 6:4"> Now then, Hashem Elohei Yisroel, let Thy Davar come true, which Thou hast spoken unto Thy eved Dovid.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:6; YESHAYAH 66:1; TEHILLIM 139:7–10"> But will Elohim really dwell with HaAdam on ha'aretz? Hinei, Shomayim and the Shmei HaShomayim cannot contain Thee; how much less this Beis which I built!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:28; LUKAS 18:1–7; YOCHANAN 17:20"> Yet give attention to the tefillah of Thy eved, and to his techinnah (supplication), O Hashem Elohai, to pay heed unto the cry and the tefillah which Thy eved prayeth before Thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 34:15; DANIEL 6:10; TEHILLIM 121:5"> That Thine eyes may be open toward this Beis yomam valailah, toward the makom (place) whereof Thou hast said that Thou wouldest put Shimcha there; to pay heed unto the tefillah which Thy eved prayeth toward this makom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MICHOH 7:18; DANIEL 9:19; YESHAYAH 43:25"> Pay heed therefore unto the tachanunei Avdecha (suppplications of Thy servant), and of Thy people Yisroel, which they shall daven toward this place; hear Thou from Thy dwelling place, even from Shomayim; and when Thou hearest, salachta (forgive).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:11; VAYIKRA 5:1; BAMIDBAR 5:19–22"> If an ish sin against his neighbor, and he is made to swear under oath and he swears an oath before Thine Mizbe'ach in this Beis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YESHAYAH 3:10–11; MISHLE 17:15; YIRMEYAH 51:56"> Then hear Thou from Shomayim, and act, and judge Thy avadim, by requiting the guilty, by recompensing his way upon his own rosh; and by justifying the tzaddik, by giving him according to his tzedakah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:17; MELACHIM BAIS 17:7–18; YEHOSHUA 7:8"> And if thy people Yisroel be defeated before the oyev (enemy), because they have sinned against Thee; and shall return and confess Shmecha, and pray and make techinnah before Thee in this Beis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 33:6–13; EZRA 1:1–6; BERESHIS 13:15"> Then hear Thou from Shomayim, and forgive the sin of Thy people Yisroel, and bring them back to HaAdamah which Thou gave to them and to Avoteichem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:23; DEVARIM 11:17; VAYIKRA 26:19"> When Shomayim is shut up, and there is no matar (rain), because they have sinned against Thee; yet if they pray toward this place, and confess Shmecha, and turn from their sin, when Thou dost afflict them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:35–36; YIRMEYAH 5:24; ZECHARYAH 10:1"> Then hear Thou from Shomayim, and forgive the sin of Thy avadim, and of Thy people Yisroel, when Thou hast taught them the Derech HaTovah, wherein they should walk; and send matar upon Thy land, which Thou hast given unto Thy people for a nachalah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="HISGALUS 9:3–11; YOEL 2:25; RUTH 1:1"> If there be ra'av (hunger, famine) in the land, if there be dever, if there be blight, or mildew, locust, or grass-hopper; if their enemies besiege them in the land of their cities; whatsoever nega or machalah there be,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="TEHILLIM 50:15; TEHILLIM 32:2–6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 6:12–13"> Then what tefillah or what techinnah soever shall be made of kol ha'adam, or of all Thy people Yisroel, when every one shall know his own nega and his own pain, and shall spread forth his hands in this Beis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:9; SHMUEL ALEF 16:7; HISGALUS 2:23"> Then hear Thou from Shomayim Thy dwelling place, and forgive, and render unto every man according unto all his ways, whose lev Thou knowest; (for Thou only knowest the hearts of the Bnei HaAdam),</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:31; IYOV 28:28; TEHILLIM 128:1"> That they may fear Thee, to walk in Thy ways, so long as they live in HaAdamah which Thou gavest unto Avoteinu.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YESHAYAH 56:3–7; MELACHIM ALEF 8:41–43; MATTITYAHU 8:10–11"> Also concerning the nokhri, who is not of Thy people Yisroel, but is come from a far country for the sake of Shimcha Hagadol, Thy yad hachazakeh, and Thy zero'a stretched out; if they come and pray toward this Beis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:14; MELACHIM ALEF 8:16; HISGALUS 11:15"> Then hear Thou from Shomayim, even from Thy dwelling place, and do according to all that the nokhri calleth for to Thee, that kol Amei Ha'aretz may know Shmecha, and fear Thee, as doth Thy people Yisroel, and may know that this Beis which I have built is called by Shimcha.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:6–13; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 18:31; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:4"> If Thy people go out to milchamah against their enemies by the derech that Thou shalt send them, and they pray unto Thee toward this city which Thou hast chosen, and the Beis which I have built for Shmecha,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DANIEL 9:17–19; TEHILLIM 9:3–4; YIRMEYAH 5:28"> Then hear Thou from Shomayim their tefillah and their techinnah, and uphold their mishpat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="KOHELET 7:20; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:2; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8–10"> If they sin against Thee, (for there is no adam which sinneth not), and Thou be angry with them, and deliver them over before their enemies, and they carry them away captive unto an Eretz rechokah or kerovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:18–20; DANIEL 9:5–11; YESHAYAH 64:6–12"> Yet if they change in their heart in the land where they are carried captive, and repent and supplicate unto Thee in the land of their captivity, saying, Chatanu (we have sinned), he'evnu (we have done wrong), and rashanu (we acted wickedly);</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:11–13; DANIEL 6:10; DANIEL 9:3–4"> If they return to Thee with all their lev and with all their nefesh in the land of their captivity, where they have carried them captive, and pray toward their land, which Thou gavest unto Avotam, and toward HaIr which Thou hast chosen, and toward the Beis which I have built for Thy Shem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MICHOH 7:18–20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 6:35; TEHILLIM 25:18"> Then hear Thou from Shomayim, even from Thy dwelling place, their tefillah and their techinnah, and uphold their mishpat, and forgive Thy people which have sinned against Thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:15; TEHILLIM 116:2; NECHEMYAH 1:6"> Now, Elohai, may Thine eyes be open, and let Thine ears be attentive unto the tefillah that is davened in this place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YESHAYAH 61:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:2; TEHILLIM 132:8–10"> Now therefore arise, Hashem Elohim, into Thy resting place, Thou, and the Aron of Thy might; let Thy Kohanim, Hashem Elohim, be clothed with Teshu'ah, and let Thy Chasidim rejoice in goodness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="YESHAYAH 55:3; TEHILLIM 132:1; YESHAYAH 61:1"> Hashem Elohim, turn not away the face of Thine Moshiach. Remember the Chasdei Dovid Avdecha.</span>
</div>
