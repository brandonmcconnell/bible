<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 21:21; VAYIKRA 18:21; VAYIKRA 20:27"> Again, thou shalt say to the Bnei Yisroel, Whosoever he be of the Bnei Yisroel, or of the ger that sojourn in Yisroel, that giveth any of his zera unto Molech; he shall surely be put to death; the Am HaAretz shall stone him with stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:21; YECHEZKEL 5:11; YECHEZKEL 23:38–39"> And I will set My face against that ish, and will cut him off from among his people; because he hath given of his zera unto Molech, to make tamei My Mikdash, and to commit chillul Hashem against Shem Kodshi (My Holy Name).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEVARIM 17:2–5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:30; MELACHIM ALEF 20:42"> And if the Am HaAretz close their eyes when that man giveth of his zera unto Molech, and kill him not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 106:39; HOSHEA 2:13; HOSHEA 2:5"> Then I will set My face against that ish, and against his mishpochah, and will cut him off, and all that go a-whoring after him, to commit whoredom with Molech, from among their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:31; YESHAYAH 8:19; VAYIKRA 20:27"> And the nefesh that turneth to Ovos (Mediums) and Yidonim (Spiritists), to go a-whoring after them, I will even set My face against that nefesh, and will cut him off from among his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="VAYIKRA 11:44; VAYIKRA 19:2; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:15–16"> Consecrate yourselves as kodesh, therefore, and be ye kedoshim: for I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 31:13; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:22; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:23"> And ye shall be shomer over My chukkot, and do them: I am Hashem who sets you apart as kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 21:17; MARKOS 7:10; DEVARIM 27:16"> For every one that curseth his av or his em shall be surely put to death; he hath cursed his av or his em; his dahm shall be upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:4–5; DEVARIM 22:22–24; DEVARIM 5:18"> And the ish that committeth adultery with the wife of another ish, even he that committeth adultery with his neighborʼs wife, the no'ef (adulterer) and the no'efet (adulteress) shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEVARIM 27:20; DEVARIM 27:23; AMOS 2:7"> And the ish that lieth with the wife of his av hath uncovered his fatherʼs nakedness; both of them shall surely be put to death; their dahm shall be upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:15; VAYIKRA 18:23; BERESHIS 38:18"> And if an ish lie with his kallah (daughter-in-law), both of them shall surely be put to death; they have committed tevel (perversion); their dahm shall be upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:9; VAYIKRA 18:22; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7"> If an ish also lie with zachar, as he lieth with an isha, both of them have committed to'evah; they shall surely be put to death; their dahm shall be upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:17; DEVARIM 27:23; YEHOSHUA 7:15"> And if an ish take a wife and her em, it is zimmah; they shall be burned with eish, both he and they; that there be no zimmah among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:23; DEVARIM 27:21; SHEMOT 22:19"> And if an ish give his shikhvat to a behemah, he shall surely be put to death: and ye shall slay the behemah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 21:32; SHEMOT 19:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:20"> And if an isha approach unto any behemah, and lie down thereto, thou shalt kill the isha, and the beast; they shall surely be put to death; their dahm shall be upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:9; DEVARIM 27:22; YECHEZKEL 22:11"> And if an ish shall take his achot, the bat of his av (father), or the bat of his em (mother), and see her nakedness, and she see his nakedness; it is a disgrace; and they shall be cut off in the sight of their Bnei Am; he hath uncovered the nakedness of his achot (sister); he shall bear his iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="VAYIKRA 15:24; VAYIKRA 18:19; YECHEZKEL 18:6"> And if an ish shall lie with an isha during her davah (menstruous unwellness), and shall uncover her nakedness, he hath made bare her mekor (source), and she hath uncovered the source of her dahm; and both of them shall be cut off from among their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:6; VAYIKRA 18:12–30; SHEMOT 6:20"> And thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy motherʼs achot (sister), nor of thy fatherʼs achot (sister); for he uncovereth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., incestuously dishonored">a</a></sup>his near kin; they shall bear their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:14; IYOV 18:19; LUKAS 23:29"> And if an ish shall lie with the wife of his dod, he hath uncovered his uncleʼs nakedness; they shall bear their chet; they shall die childless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:16; MATTITYAHU 14:3–4"> And if an ish shall take his brotherʼs wife, it is niddah; he hath uncovered his brotherʼs nakedness; they shall be childless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:25–28; YECHEZKEL 36:27; VAYIKRA 18:4–5"> Ye shall therefore be shomer over all My chukkot, and all My mishpatim, and do them; that HaAretz, whither I bring you to dwell therein, vomit you not out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEVARIM 9:5; VAYIKRA 18:24; VAYIKRA 18:30"> And ye shall not walk in the chukkot of HaGoy, which I cast out before you; for they committed all these things; therefore I abhorred them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEVARIM 14:2; SHEMOT 6:8; DEVARIM 7:6"> But I have said unto you, Ye shall inherit their land, and I will give it unto you to inherit it, an Eretz zavat cholov udevash: I am Hashem Eloheichem, which have separated you from the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEVARIM 14:3–21; VAYIKRA 11:1–47; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:11–15"> Ye shall therefore distinguish habehemah hatehorah from teme'ah, and between fowls tamei and tahor: and ye shall not make your nefashot sheketz (abomination) by beast, or by fowl, or by any manner of living thing that creepeth on the ground, which I have separated from you as tamei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:15–16; VAYIKRA 20:7; VAYIKRA 20:24"> And ye shall be kedoshim unto Me: for I Hashem am kadosh, and have separated you from the nations, that ye should be Mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:31; VAYIKRA 20:6; SHMUEL ALEF 28:7–9"> An ish also or isha who is an Ov (Medium) or a Yidoni (Spiritist), shall surely be put to death; they shall stone them with stones; their dahm shall be upon them.</span>
