<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe and Aharon, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 24:8; VAYIKRA 14:56; VAYIKRA 14:3"> When a man shall have in the skin of his basar a swelling, a scab, or bright spot, and it be in the skin of his basar like the nega tzara'at; then he shall be brought unto Aharon the kohen, or unto one of his Banim the kohanim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:19–20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:28; MALACHI 2:7"> And the kohen shall examine the nega in the skin of the basar; and when hair in the nega is turned white, and the nega in appearance be deeper than the skin of his basar, it is a nega tzara'at; and the kohen shall examine him, and pronounce him tamei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 12:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:5; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:24"> If the bright spot be white in the skin of his basar, and in appearance be not deeper than the skin, and the hair thereof be not turned white, then the kohen shall quarantine him that hath the nega shivat yamim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the kohen shall examine him on the yom hashevi'i; and, hinei, if the nega in his sight be unchanged, and the nega spread not in the skin; then the kohen shall quarantine him another shivat yamim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="VAYIKRA 11:25; VAYIKRA 14:8; DEVARIM 32:5"> And the kohen shall examine him again on the yom hashevi'i; and, hinei, if the nega be somewhat faded, and the nega spread not in the skin, the kohen shall pronounce him tahor; it is but a scab; and he shall wash his clothes, and be tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 38:3; VAYIKRA 13:35–36; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:16–17"> But if the scab spread much abroad in the skin, after that he hath shown himself to the kohen for his tohorah, he shall appear before the kohen again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:7–8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:18–19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:21"> And if the kohen see that, hinei, the scab spreadeth in the skin, then the kohen shall pronounce him tamei; it is a tzara'at.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> When the nega tzara'at is in a man, then he shall be brought unto the kohen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 5:27; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 26:19–20; VAYIKRA 13:14–15"> And the kohen shall examine him; and, hinei, if the swelling be white in the skin, and it have turned the hair white, and there be raw basar in the swelling;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> It is an old tzara'at in the skin of his basar, and the kohen shall pronounce him tamei, and shall not quarantine him; for he is tamei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8–10; IYOV 42:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:14"> And if a tzara'at break out abroad in the skin, and the tzara'at cover all the skin of him that hath the nega from his head even to his foot, wheresoever the kohen looketh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:41; YESHAYAH 64:6"> Then the kohen shall consider; and, hinei, if the tzara'at have covered all his basar, he shall pronounce him tahor that hath the nega; it is all turned white; he is tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But on the day when raw basar appeareth in him, he shall be tamei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the kohen shall see the raw basar, and pronounce him to be tamei; for the raw basar is tamei; it is a tzara'at.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:14–16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:14–24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:6–8"> Or if the raw basar turn again, and be changed unto white, he shall come unto the kohen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the kohen shall see him; and, hinei, if the nega be turned into white, then the kohen shall pronounce him tahor that hath the nega; he is tahor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 9:9; YESHAYAH 38:21; TEHILLIM 38:3–7"> The basar also, in which, even in the skin thereof, was a boil, and is healed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="VAYIKRA 13:24"> And in the place of the boil there be a white swelling, or a bright spot, white, and somewhat reddish, and it be shown to the kohen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:14; VAYIKRA 13:3; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20"> And if, when the kohen examine it, hinei, it be in appearance deeper than the skin, and the hair thereof be turned white; the kohen shall pronounce him tamei; it is a nega tzara'at broken out of the boil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:5"> But if the kohen examine it, and, hinei, there be no white hair therein, and if it be not deeper than the skin, but be somewhat faded, then the kohen shall quarantine him shivat yamim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And if it spread much abroad in the skin, then the kohen shall pronounce him tamei; it is a nega.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:1; IYOV 34:31–32; MATTITYAHU 26:75"> But if the bright spot stay in its place, and spread not, it is a burning boil; and the kohen shall pronounce him tahor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YESHAYAH 3:24"> Or if there be any basar, in the skin whereof there is a hot burning, and the raw basar that burneth have a white bright spot, somewhat reddish, or white,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="VAYIKRA 13:18–20; VAYIKRA 13:4"> Then the kohen shall examine it; and, hinei, if the hair in the bright spot be turned white, and it be in appearance deeper than the skin, it is a tzara'at broken out of the burning; therefore the kohen shall pronounce him tamei; it is the nega tzara'at.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="VAYIKRA 13:23; VAYIKRA 13:4–5"> But if the kohen examine it, and, hinei, there be no white hair in the bright spot, and it be no deeper than the other skin, but be dim, then the kohen shall quarantine him shivat yamim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And the kohen shall examine him on the yom hashevi'i; and if it be spread much abroad in the skin, then the kohen shall pronounce him tamei; it is the nega tzara'at.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And if the bright spot stay in its place, and spread not in the skin, but it be dim, it is a swelling of the burn, and the kohen shall pronounce him tahor; for it is an inflammation of the burning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YESHAYAH 9:15; MATTITYAHU 13:14–15; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:3–4"> If an ish or isha have a nega upon the head or the chin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="VAYIKRA 13:34–37; VAYIKRA 14:54"> Then the kohen shall examine the nega, and, hinei, if it be in appearance deeper than the skin, and there be in it a yellow thin hair, then the kohen shall pronounce him tamei; it is a scale, even a tzara'at upon the head or chin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And if the kohen examine the nega of the scale, and, hinei, it be not in appearance deeper than the skin, and that there is no black hair in it, then the kohen shall quarantine him that hath the nega of the scale shivat yamim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="VAYIKRA 13:30; MATTITYAHU 23:5; LUKAS 18:9–12"> And in the yom hashevi'i the kohen shall examine the nega; and, hinei, if the scale spread not, and there be in it no yellow hair, and the scale be not in appearance deeper than the skin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> He shall be shaven, but the scale shall he not shave; and the kohen shall quarantine him that hath the scale another shivat yamim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:22; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1; HISGALUS 2:2"> And in the yom hashevi'i the kohen shall examine the scale; and, hinei, if the scale be not spread in the skin, nor be in appearance deeper than the skin, then the kohen shall pronounce him tahor; and he shall wash his clothes, and be tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="VAYIKRA 13:27; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:16–17; VAYIKRA 13:7"> But if the scale spread much in the skin after his tohorah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Then the kohen shall examine him; and, hinei, if the scale be spread in the skin, the kohen need not seek for yellow hair; he is tamei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> But if the scale be in his sight unchanged, and that there is black hair grown up therein; the scale is healed, he is tahor; and the kohen shall pronounce him tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> If an ish also or an isha have in the skin of their basar bright spots, even white bright spots,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:22–25; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:2; KOHELET 7:20"> Then the kohen shall examine; and, hinei, if the bright spots in the skin of their basar be faint white, it is a bohak spot that groweth in the skin; he is tahor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:10; SHIR HASHIRIM 5:11"> And the man whose hair is fallen off his head, he is bald; yet is he tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And he that hath his hair fallen off from the front of his scalp, he is bald at the forehead; yet is he tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And if there be in the bald head, or bald forehead, a white reddish nega, it is a tzara'at breaking out in his bald head, or his bald forehead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Then the kohen shall examine it; and, hinei, if the swelling of the nega be white reddish in his bald head, or in his bald forehead, as the tzara'at appeareth in the skin of the basar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1–2; MATTITYAHU 6:23; YESHAYAH 1:5"> He is an ish tzaru'a, he is tamei; the kohen shall pronounce him utterly tamei; his nega is in his head.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MICHOH 3:7; VAYIKRA 10:6; YECHEZKEL 24:17"> And the tzaru'a in whom the nega is, his clothes shall be torn, and his head bare, and he shall put a covering upon his lower face, and shall cry, Tamei! Tamei!</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 7:3; MELACHIM BAIS 15:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 26:21"> All the days wherein the nega shall be in him he shall be tamei; he is tamei; he shall live alone; outside the machaneh shall his moshav be.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:12; YECHEZKEL 16:16"> When a garment has the nega tzara'at in it, whether it be a wool garment, or a linen garment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="VAYIKRA 13:51; HISGALUS 3:4; DEVARIM 8:11"> Whether it be in the warp, or woof; of linen, or of wool; whether in a leather, or in anything made of leather;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> And if the nega be greenish or reddish in the garment, or in the leather, either in the warp, or in the woof, or in any thing of leather, it is a nega tzara'at, and shall be shown unto the kohen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 44:23"> And the kohen shall examine the nega, and quarantine it that hath the nega shivat yamim;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="VAYIKRA 14:44"> And he shall examine the nega on the yom hashevi'i; if the nega be spread in the garment, either in the warp, or in the woof, or in a leather, or in any work that is made of leather, the nega is a malignant tzara'at; it is tamei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="VAYIKRA 11:35; VAYIKRA 11:33; VAYIKRA 14:44–45"> He shall therefore burn that garment, whether warp or woof, in wool or in linen, or any thing of leather, wherein the nega is; for it is a malignant tzara'at; it shall be burned in the eish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> And if the kohen shall examine, and, hinei, the nega be not spread in the garment, either in the warp, or in the woof, or in anything of leather,</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> Then the kohen shall command that they wash the thing wherein the nega is, and he shall quarantine it another shivat yamim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 24:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:4–8; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20–22"> And the kohen shall examine the nega, after that it is washed; and, hinei, if the nega has not changed its appearance, and the nega be not spread, it is tamei; thou shalt burn it in the eish; whether the eating away hath brought bareness in the front or back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And if the kohen examine, and, hinei, the nega be somewhat faded after the washing of it, then he shall tear it out of the garment, or out of the leather, or out of the warp, or out of the woof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 22:7; HISGALUS 21:8; MATTITYAHU 3:12"> And if it appear still in the garment, either in the warp, or in the woof, or in any thing of leather, it is a spreading nega; thou shalt burn that wherein the nega is with eish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 5:14; HISGALUS 1:5; TEHILLIM 51:2"> And the garment, either warp, or woof, or whatsoever thing of leather it be, which thou shalt wash, if the nega be departed from them, then it shall be washed the second time, and shall be tahor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> This is the torah of the nega tzara'at in a garment of wool or linen, either in the warp, or woof, or any thing of leather, to pronounce it tahor, or to pronounce it tamei.</span>
</div>
