## &#91;METZORA&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKAS 17:14; LUKAS 5:12–14; MARKOS 1:40–44"> This shall be the torat hametzora in the yom of his tohorah: he shall be brought unto the kohen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="VAYIKRA 13:46; LUKAS 7:22; LUKAS 17:15–19"> And the kohen shall go forth outside the machaneh; and the kohen shall examine, and, hinei, if the nega tzara'at be healed in the afflicted person,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 19:6; VAYIKRA 14:6; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:19"> Then shall the kohen command to take for him that is to be cleansed two birds alive and tehorot (clean ones), and cedar wood, and scarlet thread, and hyssop;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="VAYIKRA 14:50; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:14; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:1"> And the kohen shall command that one of the birds be shachat in a clay vessel over mayim chayyim (running water);</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:5; ZECHARYAH 13:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:9–11"> As for the living bird, he shall take it, and the cedar wood, and the scarlet thread, and the hyssop, and shall dip them and the living bird in the dahm of the bird that was killed (shachat) over the mayim chayyim (running water);</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 5:14; YECHEZKEL 36:25; YESHAYAH 52:15"> And he shall sprinkle upon him that is to be made tahor from the tzara'at seven times, and shall pronounce him tahor, and shall release the living bird into the open field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="VAYIKRA 11:25; BAMIDBAR 8:7; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:21"> And he that is to be made tahor shall wash his clothes, and shave off all his hair, and wash himself in mayim, that he may be tahor; and after that he shall come into the machaneh, and shall wait outside of his ohel seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 6:9; VAYIKRA 14:8; BAMIDBAR 8:7"> But it shall be on the yom hashevi'i, that he shall shave all his hair off his head and his beard and his eyebrows, even all his hair he shall shave off; and he shall wash his clothes, also he shall wash his basar in mayim, and he shall be tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="VAYIKRA 2:1; MATTITYAHU 8:4; LUKAS 5:14"> And on the yom hashemini he shall take two kevasim temimim and one ewe lamb of the first year temimah, and three tenths of an ephah of fine flour for a minchah, mixed with shemen, and one log of shemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:26–27; SHEMOT 29:1–4; BAMIDBAR 8:21"> And the kohen who purifies shall present the ish that is to be made tahor, and those things, before Hashem, at the entrance of the Ohel Mo'ed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:24; VAYIKRA 6:6–7; VAYIKRA 8:27–29"> And the kohen shall take one keves (lamb), and sacrifice him for an asham (trespass offering), and the log of shemen, and wave them for a tenufah (wave offering) before Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:11; VAYIKRA 1:11; VAYIKRA 4:24"> And he shall slaughter (shachat) the keves in the place where he shall slaughter (shachat) the chattat (sin offering) and the olah (burnt offering), in the mekom hakodesh; for as the chattat (sin offering) belongs to the kohen, so does the asham (trespass offering); it is kodesh kodashim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:20; VAYIKRA 8:23–24; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:1"> And the kohen shall take some of the dahm of the asham (trespass offering), and the kohen shall put it upon the lobe of the right ear of him that is to be made tahor, and upon the thumb of his right hand, and upon the bohen of his right foot;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 45:7; YOCHANAN 3:34; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20"> And the kohen shall take some of the log of shemen, and pour it into the palm of his own left hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:31; VAYIKRA 4:6"> And the kohen shall dip his right forefinger in the shemen that is in his left hand, and shall sprinkle of the shemen with his forefinger seven times before Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:2; YECHEZKEL 36:27; VAYIKRA 14:14"> And of the rest of the shemen that is in his hand shall the kohen put upon the lobe of the right ear of him that is to be made tahor, and upon the thumb of his right hand, and upon the bohen of his right foot, upon the dahm of the asham (trespass offering);</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:26; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:21–22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:17–18"> And the remnant of the shemen that is in the kohenʼs hand he shall pour upon the head of him that is to be made tahor; and the kohen shall make kapporah for him before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="VAYIKRA 14:12; VAYIKRA 5:1; VAYIKRA 12:6–8"> And the kohen shall offer the chattat (sin offering), and make kapporah for him that is to be made tahor from his tumah (uncleanness); and afterward he shall slaughter (shachat) the olah (burnt offering);</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="VAYIKRA 14:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:2; VAYIKRA 14:8"> And the kohen shall offer the olah (burnt offering) and the minchah upon the Mizbe'ach; and the kohen shall make kapporah for him, and he shall be tahor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="VAYIKRA 5:7; VAYIKRA 12:8; MISHLE 17:5"> And if he be poor, and cannot afford otherwise, then he shall take one keves for an asham (trespass offering) to be waved, to make kapporah for him, and one tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with shemen for a minchah, and a log of shemen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 7:16; YESHAYAH 59:11; YIRMEYAH 48:28"> And two turtledoves, or two young pigeons, such as he is able to afford; and the one shall be a chattat (sin offering), and the other an olah (burnt offering).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="VAYIKRA 14:10–11"> And he shall bring them on the yom hashemini for his tohorah unto the kohen, unto the entrance of the Ohel Mo'ed before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="VAYIKRA 14:12; VAYIKRA 14:10"> And the kohen shall take the keves (lamb) of the asham (trespass offering), and the log of shemen, and the kohen shall wave them for a tenufah (wave offering) before Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="KOHELET 5:1; TEHILLIM 40:6; VAYIKRA 14:14–20"> And he shall slaughter (shachat) the keves of the asham (trespass offering), and the kohen shall take some of the dahm of the asham (trespass offering), and put it upon the lobe of the right ear of him that is to be made tahor, and upon the thumb of his right hand, and upon the bohen of his right foot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the kohen shall pour of the shemen into the palm of his own left hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And the kohen shall sprinkle with his right forefinger some of the shemen that is in his left hand seven times before Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the kohen shall put of the shemen that is in his hand upon the lobe of the right ear of him that is to made tahor, and upon the thumb of his right hand, and upon the bohen of his right foot, upon the mekom (place) of the dahm of the asham (trespass offering);</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="VAYIKRA 14:18; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1–2; SHEMOT 30:15–16"> And the rest of the shemen that is in the kohenʼs hand he shall put upon the head of him that is to be made tahor, to make kapporah for him before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="VAYIKRA 14:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:3; VAYIKRA 12:8"> And he shall sacrifice the one of the torim (turtledoves), or of the young pigeons, such as he can afford;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="VAYIKRA 14:18; VAYIKRA 5:7"> Even such as he is able to afford, the one for a chattat (sin offering), and the other for an olah, with the minchah; and the kohen shall make kapporah for him that is to be made tahor before Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="VAYIKRA 14:10; VAYIKRA 14:21; MATTITYAHU 11:5"> This is the torah of him in whom is the nega tzara'at, who cannot afford that which pertaineth to his tohorah (cleansing).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe and unto Aharon, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:49; BERESHIS 17:8; BAMIDBAR 32:22"> When ye be come into Eretz Kena'an, which I give to you for a possession, and I put the nega tzara'at in a bais of the eretz of your possession;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="TEHILLIM 91:10; DEVARIM 7:26; ZECHARYAH 5:4"> And he that owneth the bais shall come and tell the kohen, saying, It seemeth to me there is as it were a nega (plague) in the bais;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="HISGALUS 18:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:15; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:17–18"> Then the kohen shall command that they empty the bais, before the kohen go into it to examine the nega, that all that is in the bais be not made tamei; and afterward the kohen shall go in to examine the bais,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="VAYIKRA 13:42; VAYIKRA 13:3; VAYIKRA 13:19–20"> And he shall examine the nega, and, hinei, if the nega be in the walls of the bais with depressions, greenish or reddish, which in appearance are deeper than the surface of the wall,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="VAYIKRA 13:50"> Then the kohen shall go out of the bais to the entrance of the bais, and quarantine the bais seven days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="VAYIKRA 13:36; VAYIKRA 13:27; VAYIKRA 13:22"> And the kohen shall come again the yom hashevi'i, and shall inspect; and, hinei, if the nega be spread in the walls of the bais,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:20; LETTER TO TITOS 3:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:5–6"> Then the kohen shall command that they tear down the stones in which the nega is, and they shall throw them into a makom tamei outside the town;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YESHAYAH 65:4; HISGALUS 22:15; MATTITYAHU 24:51"> And he shall cause the bais to be scraped within around, and they shall pour out the afar (dust) that they scrape off outside the town into a makom tamei;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:2; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:21–22; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 17:7–9"> And they shall take other stones, and make them replace those stones; and he shall take other mortar, and shall plaster the bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 6:28–30; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20"> And if the nega come again, and break out in the bais, after that he hath taken away the stones, and after he hath scraped the bais, and after it is plastered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="VAYIKRA 13:51–52; ZECHARYAH 5:4"> Then the kohen shall come and inspect, and, hinei, if the nega be spread in the bais, it is a malignant tzara'at in the bais; it is tamei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 10:27; MATTITYAHU 24:2; MELACHIM ALEF 9:6–9"> And he shall tear down the bais, the stones of it, and the timber thereof, and all the mortar of the bais; and he shall carry them forth out of the town into a makom tamei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 19:21–22; VAYIKRA 22:6; VAYIKRA 11:28"> Moreover he that goeth into the bais all the while that it is quarantined shall be tamei until erev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="VAYIKRA 14:8–9; VAYIKRA 11:25"> And he that reclineth in the bais shall wash his clothes; and he that eateth in the bais shall wash his clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:11; IYOV 5:18; MARKOS 5:29"> And if the kohen shall come in, and examine it, and, hinei, the nega hath not spread in the bais, after the bais was plastered, then the kohen shall pronounce the bais tahor (clean), because the nega is healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="VAYIKRA 14:4"> And he shall take to purify the bais two birds, and cedar wood, and scarlet thread, and hyssop;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> And he shall kill (shachat) the one of the birds in a clay vessel over mayim chayyim (running water);</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="TEHILLIM 51:7"> And he shall take the cedar wood, and the hyssop, and the scarlet thread, and the living bird, and dip them in the dahm of the slain bird, and in the mayim chayyim (running water), and sprinkle the bais seven times;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And he shall cleanse the bais with the dahm of the bird, and with the mayim hachayyim (running water), and with the living bird, and with the cedar wood, and with the hyssop, and with the scarlet thread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="VAYIKRA 14:20; VAYIKRA 14:7"> But he shall release the living bird outside the town into the open field, and make kapporah for the bais; and it shall be tahor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="VAYIKRA 15:32; VAYIKRA 14:32; VAYIKRA 6:9"> This is the torah for all manner of nega tzara'at, and nesek (scale, netek),</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="VAYIKRA 13:47–59"> And for the tzara'at of a garment, and of a bais,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="VAYIKRA 13:2"> And for a swelling, and for a scab, and for a bright spot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="VAYIKRA 10:10; YECHEZKEL 44:23; YIRMEYAH 15:19"> To teach when it is tamei, and when it is tahor: this is the torat tzara'at.</span>
</div>
