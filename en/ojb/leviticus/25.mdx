## &#91;BEHAR&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:24–25; SHEMOT 19:1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe in Mt. Sinai, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:21; VAYIKRA 26:34–35; SHEMOT 23:10"> Speak unto the Bnei Yisroel, and say unto them, When ye come into HaAretz which I give you, then shall HaAretz observe a Shabbos rest unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:10"> Six years thou shalt sow thy sadeh, and six years thou shalt prune thy kerem, and gather in the fruit thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:21; VAYIKRA 25:20–23; SHEMOT 23:10–11"> But in the Shanah HaShevi'it shall be a Shabbos Shabbaton unto HaAretz, a Shabbos unto Hashem; thou shalt neither sow thy sadeh, nor prune thy kerem (vineyard).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 19:29; YESHAYAH 37:30"> That which groweth of its own accord of thy harvest thou shalt not reap, neither gather the grapes of thy untended vine; for it is Shnat Shabbaton unto HaAretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:44; SHEMOT 23:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:34–35"> And the Shabbos of HaAretz shall be food for you; for thee, and for thy eved, and for thy maid, and for thy hired servant, and for thy toshav (guest) that sojourneth with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And for thy cattle, and for the wild animals that are in thy land, shall all the tevuah (increase) thereof be to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:15; BERESHIS 2:2"> And thou shalt count seven Shabbatot of shanim unto thee, seven times seven shanim; and the period of the sheva Shabbatot of shanim shall be unto thee forty and nine shanim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:24; VAYIKRA 23:27; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:8"> Then shalt thou cause the shofar to sound a broken blast on the tenth day of the seventh month, in the Yom HaKippurim shall ye make the shofar sound throughout all your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 34:8; YESHAYAH 63:4; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:16"> And ye shall treat as kodesh the fiftieth year, and proclaim deror (freedom) throughout all the land unto all the inhabitants thereof; it shall be a Yovel (Jubilee) unto you; and ye shall return every man unto the ancestral heritage of his possession, and ye shall return every man unto his mishpochah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="VAYIKRA 27:17; VAYIKRA 25:4–5"> A Yovel shall that fiftieth year be unto you; ye shall not sow, neither reap that which groweth of itself in it, nor gather the grapes in it of thy untended vine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:6–7"> For it is the Yovel; it shall be kodesh unto you; ye shall eat the tevuah (increase) thereof out of the sadeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:10; VAYIKRA 27:17–24; BAMIDBAR 36:4"> In this Shnat Yovel ye shall return every man unto his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:13; VAYIKRA 25:17; SHMUEL ALEF 12:3–4"> And if thou sell ought unto thy neighbor, or buyest ought of thy neighborʼs hand, ye shall not take advantage of one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="VAYIKRA 27:18–23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:5"> According to the number of shanim after the Yovel thou shalt buy of thy neighbor, and according unto the number of shanim of the tevuot (harvests, increases) he shall sell unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:27; VAYIKRA 25:51"> According to the multitude of shanim thou shalt increase the price thereof, and according to the fewness of shanim thou shalt diminish the price of it; for according to the number of the shanim of the tevuot doth he sell unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:14; VAYIKRA 19:32; VAYIKRA 25:43"> Ye shall not therefore take advantage of one another; but thou shalt fear Eloheicha: for I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 23:6; DEVARIM 12:10; MISHLE 1:33"> Wherefore ye shall do My chukkot, and be shomer over My mishpatim, and do them; and ye shall dwell in HaAretz in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YOEL 2:26; TEHILLIM 85:12; VAYIKRA 26:5"> And HaAretz shall yield her pri, and ye shall eat your fill, and dwell therein in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:4; LUKAS 12:29; BAMIDBAR 11:4"> And if ye shall say, What shall we eat in the shanah hashevi'it? Behold, we shall not sow, nor gather in our tevuah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:8; MISHLE 10:22; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:10"> Then I will command Birkhati (My Blessing) upon you in the sixth year, and it shall bring forth the tevuah for shalosh hashanim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 19:29; VAYIKRA 26:10; YESHAYAH 37:30"> And ye shall sow the shanah hasheminit, and eat yet of tevuah yashan (old increase) until the ninth year; until her harvest come in, ye shall eat of the yashan (old).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:11; TEHILLIM 39:12; YECHEZKEL 48:14"> HaAretz shall not be sold permanently: for HaAretz is Mine, for ye are gerim and toshavim with Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:7; VAYIKRA 25:51–53; VAYIKRA 25:27"> And in all the eretz of your possession ye shall grant a Geulah for HaAretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="RUTH 2:20; YIRMEYAH 32:7–8; RUTH 3:12"> If thy brother become poor, and hath sold away some of his possession, and his Go'el HaKarov (Near Kinsman Redeemer) come to redeem it, then shall he redeem that which his brother sold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="VAYIKRA 5:7"> And if the man does not have for him a Go'el, and himself be able to effect the Geulah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Then let him count the shanim from the sale thereof, and refund the balance unto the man to whom he sold it; that he may return unto his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YESHAYAH 35:9–10; VAYIKRA 25:10; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:4–5"> But if he be not able to restore it to him, then that which is sold shall remain in the hand of him that hath bought it until the Shnat HaYovel; and in the Yovel it shall be returned, and he shall return unto his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And if a man sell a bais moshav in a walled city, then he retains the right of Geulah a full year after its sale; within a full year may he redeem it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And if it be not redeemed within the space of a full year, then the bais that is in the walled city shall be established forever to him that bought it throughout his dorot; it shall not return in the Yovel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="TEHILLIM 49:7–8"> But the batim (houses) of the villages which have no chomah (wall) round about them shall be considered as the fields of the country; Geulah shall be for it, and in the Yovel it must return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 21:1–45; BAMIDBAR 35:1–8"> Notwithstanding, the towns of the Levi'im, and the batim (houses) of the towns of their possession, there is a Geulat Olam for the Levi'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 18:20–24; DEVARIM 18:1–2"> And what one will buy from the Levi'im, the bais that was sold, and the town of his possession, shall be released in the year of Yovel; for the batim of the towns of the Levi'im are their possession among the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:36–37; BAMIDBAR 35:2–5"> But the sadeh of the open land of their towns may not be sold; for it is their achuzzat olam (perpetual heritage).</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEVARIM 15:7–8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:29; VAYIKRA 25:25"> And if thy brother become poor, and his hand fails with thee; then thou shalt strengthen him; yea, though he be a ger, or a toshav; that he may live among thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:25; DEVARIM 23:19–20; YECHEZKEL 18:8"> Take thou no neshekh of him, or increase; but fear Eloheicha; that thy brother may live among thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Thou shalt not lend him thy kesef at neshekh, nor give him thy food for increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="VAYIKRA 11:45; BAMIDBAR 15:41; SHEMOT 20:2"> I am Hashem Eloheichem, which brought you forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim, to give you Eretz Kena'an, and to be Elohim unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="SHEMOT 21:2; MELACHIM ALEF 9:22; MELACHIM BAIS 4:1"> And if thy brother that dwelleth by thee become poor, and be sold unto thee, thou shalt not compel him to do avodat eved;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="SHEMOT 21:2–3"> But as a sakhir, and as a toshav, he shall be among thee, and shall serve thee unto the Shnat HaYovel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:28; SHEMOT 21:3; YOCHANAN 8:32"> And then shall he depart from thee, both he and his banim with him, and shall return unto his own mishpochah, and unto the Achuzzat of his Avot shall he return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:55; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:22; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:21–23"> For they are My Eved, which I brought forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim; they shall not be sold in the manner of an eved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:1; VAYIKRA 25:46; SHEMOT 1:13–14"> Thou shalt not rule over him with rigor; but shalt fear Eloheicha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:44; TEHILLIM 2:8–9; YESHAYAH 14:1–2"> Both thy bondmen, and thy bondmaids, which thou shalt have, shall be of the Goyim that are round about you; of them shall ye buy eved and amah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="YESHAYAH 56:3–6"> Moreover of the bnei hatoshavim that do sojourn among you, of them shall ye buy, and of their mishpachot that are among you, which they fathered in your land; and they shall be your possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="YESHAYAH 14:2; VAYIKRA 25:43"> And ye shall take them as an inheritance for your banim after you, to inherit them for a possession; they shall be your bondmen forever; but over your brethren, the Bnei Yisroel, ye shall not rule one over another with rigor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5; VAYIKRA 25:26; SHMUEL ALEF 2:7–8"> And if a ger or toshav grow rich by thee, and thy brother that dwelleth by him grow poor, and sell himself unto the foreigner among thee, or to the eker (member) mishpachat of the ger;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 5:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:11–13; VAYIKRA 25:35"> After that he is sold, his Geulah remains; one of his brethren may redeem him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:26"> Either his dod, or ben dod of him, may redeem him, or any that is near of kin unto him of his mishpachat may redeem him; or if he be able, he may redeem himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="YESHAYAH 16:14; YESHAYAH 21:16; VAYIKRA 25:53"> And he shall reckon with him that bought him from the year that he was sold to him unto the Shnat HaYovel; and the price of his release shall be based on the number of shanim, according to the value of a sakhirʼs pay shall it be with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> If there be yet many shanim remaining, according unto them he shall give for his Geulah the kesef that he was bought for.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And if there remain but few shanim unto the Shnat HaYovel, then he shall count that, and according unto his shanim shall he pay the price of his Geulah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:46; VAYIKRA 25:43"> And as a sakhir hired shanah b'shanah shall he be with him; and the other shall not rule with rigor over him in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="SHEMOT 21:2–3; VAYIKRA 25:40–41; YESHAYAH 49:9"> And if he be not redeemed in these shanim, then he shall be released in the Shnat HaYovel, both he, and his banim with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:42; YESHAYAH 43:3; SHEMOT 13:3"> For unto Me the Bnei Yisroel are avadim; they are My avadim whom I brought forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim: I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
