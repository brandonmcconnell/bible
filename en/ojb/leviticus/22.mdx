<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:21; DEVARIM 15:19; VAYIKRA 22:32"> Speak unto Aharon and to his Banim, that they separate themselves from the kodashim of the Bnei Yisroel which they set apart as kodesh unto Me, so that they will not commit Chillul ha-shem of Shem Kodshi: I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="VAYIKRA 7:20–21; BAMIDBAR 19:13; TEHILLIM 16:11"> Say unto them, Whosoever he be of all your zera among your dorot, that goeth unto the kodashim, which the Bnei Yisroel treat as kodesh unto Hashem, having his tumah upon him, that nefesh shall be cut off from My presence: I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="VAYIKRA 11:39; VAYIKRA 15:2–3; VAYIKRA 15:13–16"> Whatsoever man of the zera Aharon is a metzora, or hath a discharge; he shall not eat of the kodashim, until he be tahor. And whosoever toucheth any thing that is tamei by the dead, or a man whose shikhvat zera goeth from him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="VAYIKRA 11:43–44; VAYIKRA 15:7; VAYIKRA 15:19"> Or whosoever toucheth any creeping thing, whereby he may be made tamei, or a man who makes him tamei, whatsoever tumah he hath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="VAYIKRA 15:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:22; CHAGGAI 2:13"> The nefesh which hath touched any such shall be tamei until erev, and shall not eat of the kodashim, unless he immerse his basar with mayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="VAYIKRA 21:22; DEVARIM 18:3–4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:13–14"> And when the shemesh is down, he shall be tahor, and shall afterward eat of the kodashim; because it is his lechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="VAYIKRA 17:15; SHEMOT 22:31; YECHEZKEL 44:31"> Nevelah (that which dieth of itself), or tereifah (what is torn by beasts), he shall not eat to make himself tamei therewith; I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:43; BAMIDBAR 18:22; BAMIDBAR 18:32"> They shall therefore be shomer over My charge, lest they bear chet for it, and die therefore, if they profane it: I Hashem do set them apart as kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:4; SHMUEL ALEF 21:6; SHEMOT 29:33"> There shall no zar eat of the kodesh: a toshav (guest) of the kohen, or a sakhir (hired servant), shall not eat of the kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:13; SHEMOT 12:44; BAMIDBAR 18:11–13"> But if the kohen buy any nefesh with his kesef, he shall eat of it, or he that is born in his bais; they shall eat of his lechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:13; VAYIKRA 21:3"> If the bat kohen also be married unto a man who is a zar, she may not eat of a terumah (offering) of the kodashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="VAYIKRA 10:14; BERESHIS 38:11; BAMIDBAR 18:11–19"> But if the bat kohen becomes an almanah, or gerusha, and have no zera, and is returned unto bais aviha (house of her father), as in her youth, she shall eat of the lechem aviha; but there shall be no zar eat thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="VAYIKRA 27:15; VAYIKRA 27:13; VAYIKRA 5:15–19"> And if an ish eat of the kodesh bishgagah (unintentionally), then he shall add as restitution the fifth part thereof unto it, and shall give it unto the kohen with the kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 18:32; VAYIKRA 19:8; YECHEZKEL 22:26"> And they shall not profane the kodshei Bnei Yisroel, which they offer unto Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="VAYIKRA 22:9; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:24; YESHAYAH 53:11–12"> And they will cause themselves to bear the avon of guilt when they eat their kodashim; for I Hashem do set them apart as kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="VAYIKRA 1:2; DEVARIM 12:6; BAMIDBAR 15:3"> Speak unto Aharon, and to his Banim, and unto kol Bnei Yisroel, and say unto them, Whatsoever he be of the Bais Yisroel, or of the Ger B'Yisroel, that will offer his korban for any of his nederim, and for all his nedavot, which they will offer unto Hashem for an olah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="VAYIKRA 1:10; VAYIKRA 1:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:27"> That it may be accepted on your behalf, ye shall offer a zachar tamim (male without defect), of the cattle and of the sheep, or of the goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEVARIM 17:1; MALACHI 1:8; DEVARIM 15:21"> But whatsoever hath a mum, that shall ye not offer, for it shall not be for you leratzon (acceptable).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="before Hashem">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 15:8; BAMIDBAR 15:3; VAYIKRA 3:6"> And whosoever offereth a zevach shelamim unto Hashem to fulfill his neder, or as a nedavah in cattle or sheep, it shall be tamim (perfect, without defect) to be accepted; there shall be no mum therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="VAYIKRA 22:20; MALACHI 1:8; VAYIKRA 1:9"> Avveret (blind), or injured, or charutz (maimed), or having an abnormal growth, or dry skin eruption, or running sore, ye shall not offer these unto Hashem, nor make an offering by eish of them upon the Mizbe'ach unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="VAYIKRA 21:18"> Either a bull or a seh that hath a limb too long or too short thou mayest offer for a nedavah; but for a neder it shall not be accepted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="VAYIKRA 22:20; DEVARIM 23:1; VAYIKRA 21:20"> Ye shall not offer unto Hashem that which is bruised, or crushed, or torn, or cut; neither shall ye make any offering thereof in your eretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="VAYIKRA 21:8; EZRA 6:8–10; BAMIDBAR 15:14–16"> Neither from the hand of a ben nakhar shall ye offer the lechem Eloheichem of any of these; because their corruption is in them, and mum be in them: they shall not be accepted for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:30; VAYIKRA 19:23–24; VAYIKRA 22:25"> When a bull, or a sheep, or a goat, is brought forth, then it shall be seven days with its mother; and from the yom hashemini and thenceforth it shall be accepted for a korban isheh (offering made by eish) unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEVARIM 22:6–7; DEVARIM 14:21; SHEMOT 34:26"> And whether it be an ox or seh, ye shall not slaughter (shachat) it and her young both in yom echad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="TEHILLIM 116:17; TEHILLIM 107:22; AMOS 4:5"> And when ye will offer a zevach todah unto Hashem, offer it liretzonechem (for your acceptance).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="before Hashem">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="VAYIKRA 7:15–18; SHEMOT 16:19–20; VAYIKRA 19:7"> On the same day it shall be eaten; ye shall leave none of it until the boker: I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:40; VAYIKRA 19:37; BAMIDBAR 15:40"> Therefore shall ye be shomer mitzvot over My commands, and do them: I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:21; LUKAS 11:2; MATTITYAHU 6:9"> Neither shall ye profane Shem Kodshi; but I will be treated as kodesh among the Bnei Yisroel: I am Hashem, the One making you Kedoshim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="VAYIKRA 11:45; SHEMOT 6:7; VAYIKRA 25:38"> That brought you out of Eretz Mitzrayim, to be for you Elohim (your G-d): I am Hashem.</span>
