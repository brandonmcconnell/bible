<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:8–14; SHEMOT 27:20–21"> Command the Bnei Yisroel, that they bring unto thee pure shemen zayit beaten for the light, to kindle the Ner Tamid (Perpetual Lamp).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Outside the Parochet (Curtain) of the Edut, in the Ohel Mo'ed shall Aharon set it in order from erev unto boker before Hashem tamid (continually); it shall be a chukkat olam in your dorot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 31:8; SHEMOT 37:17–24; SHEMOT 39:37"> He shall set in order the nerot (lamps) upon the Menorah Hatehorah before Hashem tamid (continually).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:30; MELACHIM ALEF 18:31; SHEMOT 40:23"> And thou shalt take fine flour, and bake Sheteym Esreh Challot thereof; two tenths of an ephah shall be in Challah Echat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:48; SHEMOT 25:23–24; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:19"> And thou shalt set them in two rows, six on a row, upon the Shulchan HaTahor before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="VAYIKRA 2:2; HISGALUS 8:3–4; BERESHIS 9:16"> And thou shalt put levonah zakkah (pure incense) upon each row, that it may be on the lechem for a memorial portion, even an offering made by eish unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:4; BAMIDBAR 4:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 9:32"> Shabbos after Shabbos he shall set it in order before Hashem tamid, from the Bnei Yisroel as a Brit Olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="VAYIKRA 8:31; LUKAS 6:4; MARKOS 2:26"> And it shall be for Aharon and for his Banim; and they shall eat it in the Makom Kadosh; for it is kodesh kodashim unto him of the offerings of Hashem made by eish, a chok olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 11:4; SHEMOT 12:38"> And the ben isha Yisroelit, whose father was an Egyptian, went out among the Bnei Yisroel; and this ben isha Yisroeli and a man of Yisroel fought together in the machaneh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:7; VAYIKRA 24:15–16; SHEMOT 3:15"> And the ben haisha Yisroelit pronounced HaShem and blasphemed in a curse. And they brought him unto Moshe; (and shem immo was Shelomit bat Divri, of the tribe of Dan;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHEMOT 18:15–16; BAMIDBAR 15:34; BAMIDBAR 27:5"> And they put him in custody, that the mind of Hashem might be showed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEVARIM 17:7; VAYIKRA 20:27; DEVARIM 21:21"> Bring forth him that hath blasphemed outside the machaneh; and let all that heard him lay their hands upon his head, and let kol HaEdah stone him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 9:13; SHEMOT 22:28; VAYIKRA 5:1"> And thou shalt speak unto the Bnei Yisroel, saying, Whosoever curseth Elohav shall bear his chet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:31; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:7; YOCHANAN 10:33–36"> And he that blasphemeth the Shem of Hashem, he shall surely be put to death, and kol HaEdah shall certainly stone him; as well the ger, as the ezrach, when he blasphemeth the Shem of Hashem, shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 9:5–6; DEVARIM 19:11–12; DEVARIM 27:24"> And he that taketh the nefesh of any man shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="VAYIKRA 24:21; SHEMOT 21:34–36"> And he that taketh the nefesh of a beast shall make it good; nefesh takhat nefesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:38; MATTITYAHU 7:2; DEVARIM 19:21"> And if a man cause a mum in his neighbor; as he hath done, so shall it be done to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEVARIM 19:21; MATTITYAHU 5:38; SHEMOT 21:23–25"> Fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth; as he hath caused a mum in an adam, so shall it be done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="VAYIKRA 24:17–18; SHEMOT 21:33"> And he that killeth a beast, he shall restitute it; and he that killeth a man, he shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:49; BAMIDBAR 15:15–16; BAMIDBAR 9:14"> Ye shall have mishpat echad, as well for the ger, as for the ezrach: for I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:28–29; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:2–3; BAMIDBAR 15:35–36"> And Moshe spoke to the Bnei Yisroel, that they should bring forth him that had blasphemed out of the machaneh, and stone him with stones. And the Bnei Yisroel did as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
