<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 6:7; VAYIKRA 11:44; YECHEZKEL 20:5"> Speak unto the Bnei Yisroel, and say unto them, I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:23; SHEMOT 23:24; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:2–4"> After what is done in Eretz Mitzrayim, wherein ye dwelt, shall ye not do; and after what is done in Eretz Kena'an, whither I bring you, shall ye not do; neither shall ye walk in their chukkot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:2; VAYIKRA 18:26; YECHEZKEL 20:19"> Ye shall do My mishpatim, and be shomer over My chukkot, to walk therein: I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:5; YECHEZKEL 20:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:12"> Ye shall therefore be shomer over My chukkot, and My mishpatim; which if an ish do, he shall live by them: I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:17–21; VAYIKRA 18:7–19; VAYIKRA 20:11–12"> None of you shall approach to any that is near of kin to legallot ervah (to uncover nakedness, i.e., incestuously dishonor); I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:11; YECHEZKEL 22:10; VAYIKRA 18:8–16"> The nakedness of thy av, or the nakedness of thy em, shalt thou not uncover: she is thy em; thou shalt not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:1; DEVARIM 27:20"> The nakedness of the wife of thy av shalt thou not uncover; it is the nakedness of thy av.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:17; DEVARIM 27:22; YECHEZKEL 22:11"> The nakedness of thy achot (sister), the bat of thy av, or bat of thy em, whether she be moledet bais (born at home), or moledet chutz (born outside), even their nakedness thou shalt not uncover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The nakedness of the bat of thy ben, or the bat of thy bat, even their nakedness thou shalt not uncover; for theirs is thine own nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The nakedness of the bat of the wife of thy av, begotten of thy av, she is thy achot (sister), thou shalt not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:19; SHEMOT 6:20"> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of the achot of thy av; she is thy fatherʼs near kinswoman.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., close relative">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of the achot (sister) of thy em: for she is thy motherʼs near kinswoman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:20"> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of the achi avicha (the brother of thy father), thou shalt not approach to his wife; she is thine dodah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:12; BERESHIS 38:26; YECHEZKEL 22:11"> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy kallah (daughter-in-law); she is the wife of thy ben; thou shalt not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEVARIM 25:5; VAYIKRA 20:21; MATTITYAHU 22:24"> Thou shalt not uncover the ervah (nakedness) of thy brotherʼs wife; it is thy brotherʼs nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:14; AMOS 2:7; DEVARIM 27:23"> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of an isha and her bat, neither shalt thou take the bat of her ben, or the bat of her bat, to uncover her nakedness; for they are her near kinswomen: it is zimmah (wickedness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MALACHI 2:15; BERESHIS 30:15; BERESHIS 29:28"> Neither shalt thou take your wifeʼs achot (sister), to be a rival wife, to uncover her nakedness, beside the other in her lifetime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:18; VAYIKRA 15:24; YECHEZKEL 22:10"> Also thou shalt not approach unto an isha to uncover her nakedness, as long as she has her tumah from her period of niddah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:9; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:4"> Moreover thou shalt not lie carnally with thy neighborʼs wife. You shall not give your shikhvat zera to make tamei with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:31; VAYIKRA 20:2–5; DEVARIM 18:10"> And thou shalt not let any of thy zera pass through &#91;the eish&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., be sacrificed and burned or cremated">b</a></sup>to Molech, neither shalt thou profane the Shem Eloheicha; I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:26–27; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:9"> Thou shalt not lie with zachar, as with isha: it is to'evah (abomination, detestable).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:19; VAYIKRA 20:15–16; VAYIKRA 20:12"> Neither shalt thou lie with any behemah. You shall not give your shikhvat to make tamei with it; neither shall any isha present herself to a behemah to have relations with it: it is tevel (perversion).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEVARIM 18:12; VAYIKRA 18:30; VAYIKRA 20:22–23"> Make not yourselves tamei in any of these things; for in all these the Goyim which I cast out before you became tamei;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:28; VAYIKRA 20:22–23; YIRMEYAH 2:7"> And HaAretz became tamei; therefore I do visit the iniquity thereof upon it, and HaAretz itself vomiteth out her inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:5; VAYIKRA 17:10; VAYIKRA 18:30"> Ye shall therefore be shomer over My chukkot and My mishpatim, and shall not commit any of these to'evot; no, neither any of your ezrach, nor any ger that sojourneth among you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:14; VAYIKRA 18:24; DEVARIM 23:18"> (For all these to'evot have the anshei HaAretz done, which were before you, and HaAretz is tamei;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:16; VAYIKRA 20:22; VAYIKRA 18:25"> That HaAretz vomit not you out also, when ye defile it, as it vomited out the Goyim that were before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:6; SHEMOT 12:15; VAYIKRA 17:10"> For whosoever shall commit any of these to'evot, even the nefashot that commit them shall be cut off<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e., suffer the death penalty — see Dan 9:26and Moshiachʼs not for himself death penalty">c</a></sup>from among their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:23; DEVARIM 18:9–12; VAYIKRA 22:9"> Therefore shall ye be shomer over My charge, that ye commit not any one of these chukkot hato'evot, which were committed before you, and that ye make not yourselves tamei therein: I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
