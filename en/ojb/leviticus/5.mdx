<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="VAYIKRA 5:17; MISHLE 29:24; VAYIKRA 19:8"> And if a nefesh sin, after he hears a public adjuration to give testimony, and he is an ed (witness), whether he hath seen or known of it; if he does not utter it, then he shall bear his avon (iniquity).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="VAYIKRA 5:17; DEVARIM 14:8; CHAGGAI 2:13"> Or if a nefesh touch anything tamei (unclean), whether it be a nivlat chayyah teme'ah (carcass of an unclean beast), or a nivlat behemah teme'ah (carcass of unclean livestock), or the nivlat sheretz tamei (carcass of unclean creeping things), and if it be hidden from him; he also shall be tamei, and guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 19:11–16; VAYIKRA 15:1–33; VAYIKRA 12:1–8"> Or if he touch the tum'at adam (uncleanness of man), whatsoever tumah it be that a man shall be made unclean therewith, and it be hid from him; when he knoweth of it, then he shall be guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="KOHELET 5:2–6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:12; SHOFETIM 11:31"> Or if a nefesh swear, rashly pronouncing with his lips to do harah, or to do hatov, whatsoever it be that a man shall pronounce with a shevuah (oath), and it be hid from him; when he knoweth of it, then he shall be guilty in one of these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MISHLE 28:13; BAMIDBAR 5:7; VAYIKRA 26:40"> And it shall be, when he shall be guilty in one of these things, that v'hitvaddah (he shall confess) that he hath sinned in that thing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:28; VAYIKRA 4:32; VAYIKRA 4:20"> And he shall bring his asham (penalty, trespass offering) unto Hashem for his chattat (sin) which he hath sinned, a nekevah from the flock, a lamb or a female goat, for a chattat (sin offering); and the kohen shall make kapporah for him concerning his chattat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="VAYIKRA 12:8; VAYIKRA 14:21–22; MATTITYAHU 3:16"> And if he be not able to afford a seh (lamb), then he shall bring for his asham (guilt offering),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Sanhedrin 98b on Isa 53; see Isa 53:10which says that Moshiachʼs self-offering will be an asham for his people, cf Isa 53:8">a</a></sup>which he hath sinned, two turtledoves, or two young pigeons, unto Hashem; one for a chattat (sin offering), and the other for an olah (burnt offering).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="VAYIKRA 1:15; VAYIKRA 1:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:25"> And he shall bring them unto the kohen, who shall offer that which is for the chattat (sin offering) first, and wring off his head from his neck, but shall not separate it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:30; VAYIKRA 4:34; VAYIKRA 4:18"> And he shall sprinkle of the dahm of the chattat (sin offering) upon the side of the Mizbe'ach; and the rest of the dahm shall be pressed out at the base of the Mizbe'ach; it is a chattat (sin offering).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="VAYIKRA 1:14–17; VAYIKRA 4:26; VAYIKRA 4:20"> And he shall offer the second for an olah (burnt offering), according to the mishpat (prescribed manner); and the kohen shall make kapporah for him for his chattat (sin) which he hath sinned, and it shall be forgiven him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 5:15; VAYIKRA 2:1–2; SHEMOT 16:36"> But if he be not able to afford two turtledoves, or two young pigeons, then he that sinned shall bring for his korban the tenth part of an ephah of fine flour for a chattat (sin offering); he shall put no shemen upon it, neither shall he put any incense thereon; for it is a chattat (sin offering).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:35; VAYIKRA 2:9; VAYIKRA 2:2"> Then shall he bring it to the kohen, and the kohen shall take his handful of it, even a memorial portion thereof, and burn it on the Mizbe'ach, according to the offerings made by eish unto Hashem; it is a chattat (sin offering).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="VAYIKRA 2:3; VAYIKRA 4:26; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:13"> And the kohen shall make kapporah for him as touching his chattat (sin) that he hath sinned in one of these, and it shall be forgiven him; and the remnant shall be the kohenʼs, like the minchah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 30:13; VAYIKRA 4:2; VAYIKRA 6:6"> If a nefesh commit a ma'al (trespass), and sin through ignorance (unintentionally), in the holy things of Hashem; then he shall bring for his asham unto Hashem a ram tamim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Gn 22:13on the vicarious aspect of the substitutionary atonement and compare Isa 53:5,8">b</a></sup>out of the flocks, with thy estimation by shekels of kesef, after the Shekel HaKodesh (the Shekel of the Sanctuary), for an asham (trespass or guilt offering).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="VAYIKRA 22:14; BAMIDBAR 5:7; VAYIKRA 27:27"> And he shall make restitution in regard to the holy thing in which he was remiss, and shall add the fifth part thereto, and give it unto the kohen; and the kohen shall make kapporah for him with the ram of the asham (trespass offering), and it shall be forgiven him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:27; VAYIKRA 4:13; VAYIKRA 4:2–4"> And if a nefesh sin, and commit any of these things which are forbidden to be done by the mitzvot Hashem; though he know it not, yet he is guilty, and shall bear his avon (iniquity).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="VAYIKRA 5:15–16; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:5–6; VAYIKRA 1:4"> And he shall bring a ram tamim out of the flock, with thy estimation, for an asham (trespass offering) unto the kohen; and the kohen shall make kapporah for him concerning his ignorance wherein he erred and knew it not and it shall be forgiven him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="TEHILLIM 51:4; MALACHI 3:8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:19–21"> It is an asham (trespass offering); he hath certainly trespassed against Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Isa 53:10on Moshiachʼs asham offering for Israel — Isa 53:8">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> If a nefesh sin, and commit a ma'al (trespass) against Hashem, and deceives his neighbor in that which was delivered him to keep, or entrusted to his care, or in a thing stolen, or hath cheated his neighbor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Or have found that which was lost, and lieth concerning it, and sweareth with sheker (falsely); in any of all these that a man doeth, sinning therein;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Then it shall be, because he hath sinned, and is ashem (guilty), that he shall return that which he had stolen, or the thing which he hath extorted, or that which was delivered unto his trust, or the lost thing which he found,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Or all that about which he hath sworn lasheker (falsely); he shall even restore it in the principal, and shall add the fifth part more thereto, and give it unto him to whom it appertaineth, in the day of his asham (trespass offering).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And he shall bring his asham (trespass offering) unto Hashem, a ram tamim (without blemish) out of the flock, with thy estimation, for an asham (trespass offering), unto the kohen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the kohen shall make kapporah for him before Hashem; and it shall be forgiven him for anything of all that he hath done that made him guilty.</span>
