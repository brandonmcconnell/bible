## &#91;SHEMINI&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:1; YECHEZKEL 43:26–27; VAYIKRA 15:14"> And it come to pass on yom hashemini, that Moshe called to Aharon and his Banim, and the Ziknei Yisroel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:10–14; VAYIKRA 8:14"> And he said unto Aharon, Take thee a young calf for a chattat (sin offering), and a ram for an olah (burnt offering), temimim (without blemish), and offer them before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZRA 6:17; HISGALUS 5:9; LETTER TO TITOS 2:14"> And unto the Bnei Yisroel thou shalt speak, saying, Take ye a sa'ir izzim (male goat) for a chattat (sin offering); and a calf and a keves (lamb), both of the first year, temimim (without blemish), for an olah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:43; YECHEZKEL 43:2; VAYIKRA 9:23"> Also a bull and a ram for shelamim (peace offerings), to sacrifice before Hashem; and a minchah mixed with shemen; for today Hashem will appear unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 5:2–3; SHEMOT 19:17; NECHEMYAH 8:1"> And they brought that which Moshe commanded before the Ohel Mo'ed; and kol HaEdah drew near and stood before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="VAYIKRA 9:23; YECHEZKEL 43:2; SHEMOT 24:16"> And Moshe said, This is the thing which Hashem commanded that ye should do; and the kavod Hashem shall appear unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:1; VAYIKRA 4:3"> And Moshe said unto Aharon, Go unto the Mizbe'ach, and offer thy chattat (sin offering), and thy olah (burnt offering), and make kapporah for thyself, and for the people; and offer the korban of the people, and make kapporah for them; just as Hashem commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:1–12; VAYIKRA 1:4–5; VAYIKRA 4:29"> Aharon therefore went unto the Mizbe'ach, and slaughtered (shachat) the calf of the chattat (sin offering), which was for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:6–7; VAYIKRA 8:15; VAYIKRA 4:25"> And the Bnei Aharon brought the dahm unto him; and he dipped his forefinger in the dahm, and put it upon the horns of the Mizbe'ach, and poured out the dahm at the foundation of the Mizbe'ach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="VAYIKRA 8:16–17; MISHLE 23:26; VAYIKRA 3:9–11"> But the chelev (fat), and the kidneys, and the diaphragm with the liver of the chattat (sin offering), he burned upon the Mizbe'ach; just as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="VAYIKRA 8:17; VAYIKRA 4:11–12; VAYIKRA 16:27–28"> And the basar and the hide he burned with eish outside the machaneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:25–27; VAYIKRA 1:1–17"> And he slaughtered (shachat) the olah (burnt offering); and the Bnei Aharon presented unto him the dahm, which he sprinkled around upon the Mizbe'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And they presented the olah (burnt offering) unto him, with the pieces thereof, and the head; and he burned them upon the Mizbe'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="VAYIKRA 8:21"> And he did wash the innards and the legs, and burned them upon the olah (burnt offering) on the Mizbe'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:27–31; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:3; VAYIKRA 9:3"> And he brought the korban HaAm (the offering of the people), and took the goat of the chattat (sin offering) for the people, and slaughtered (shachat) it, and offered it for sin, as before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="VAYIKRA 9:12–14; VAYIKRA 8:18–21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:1–22"> And he brought the olah (burnt offering), and offered it in the prescribed way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="VAYIKRA 2:1–2; YOCHANAN 6:53; VAYIKRA 9:1"> And he brought the minchah, and took a handful thereof, and burned it upon the Mizbe'ach, in addition to the olah of the boker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:14–17"> He slaughtered (shachat) also the bull and the ram for a zevach which was for the people; and the Bnei Aharon presented unto him the dahm, which he sprinkled upon the Mizbe'ach around,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="VAYIKRA 3:16; VAYIKRA 9:10; VAYIKRA 3:9"> And the chalavim (fat portions) of the bull and of the ram, the tail, and that which covereth the innards, and the kidneys, and the diaphragm above the liver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="VAYIKRA 3:14–17; VAYIKRA 7:29–34"> And they put the chalavim (fat) upon the breasts, and he burned the chalavim upon the Mizbe'ach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="VAYIKRA 7:30–34; VAYIKRA 7:24; SHEMOT 29:24"> And the breasts and the right thigh Aharon waved for a tenufah (wave offering) before Hashem; just as Moshe commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEVARIM 21:5; LUKAS 24:50; BAMIDBAR 6:23–27"> And Aharon lifted up his hand toward the people, and pronounced a bracha upon them, and came down from offering of the chattat (sin offering), and the olah (burnt offering), and shelamim (peace offerings).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="VAYIKRA 9:6; BAMIDBAR 16:19; LUKAS 1:21–22"> And Moshe and Aharon went into the Ohel Mo'ed, and came out, and pronounced a bracha over the people; and the kavod Hashem appeared unto kol HaAm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHOFETIM 6:21; MELACHIM ALEF 18:38–39; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:26"> And there came an eish out from before Hashem, and consumed upon the Mizbe'ach the olah (burnt offering) and the chalavim (fat portions); which when all the people saw, they shouted, and fell on their faces.</span>
