<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:61; DEVARIM 12:32; VAYIKRA 16:12"> And Nadav and Avihu, the bnei Aharon, each took his censer, and put eish therein, and put ketoret (incense) thereon, and offered eish zarah (strange, unauthorized fire) before Hashem, which He commanded them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:61; BAMIDBAR 16:35; VAYIKRA 9:24"> And there went out eish (fire) from the presence of Hashem, and devoured them, and they died before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:22; VAYIKRA 21:6; YESHAYAH 49:3"> Then Moshe said unto Aharon, This is it that Hashem spoke, saying, I will show Myself kadosh among them that come near Me, and before kol HaAm (all the People) I will be honored. And Aharon was silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 6:22; SHEMOT 6:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:6"> And Moshe called Mishael and Eltzaphan, the bnei Uzziel the dod (uncle) of Aharon, and said unto them, Come near, carry your brethren from before the Kodesh to the outside of the machaneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="VAYIKRA 8:13"> So they went near, and carried them in the kuttanot of them out of the machaneh; as Moshe had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 22:18; YEHOSHUA 7:1; BAMIDBAR 16:22"> And Moshe said unto Aharon, and unto El'azar and unto Itamar, his banim, Don't uncover your head, neither rend your clothes; lest ye die, and lest wrath come upon kol HaEdah; but let your brethren, the kol Bais Yisroel, bewail the burning which Hashem hath kindled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="VAYIKRA 21:12; VAYIKRA 8:30; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:38"> And ye shall not go out from the entrance of the Ohel Mo'ed, lest ye die; for the shemen mishchat (anointing oil) of Hashem is upon you. And they did according to the word of Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Hashem spoke unto Aharon, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 1:15; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:18"> Do not drink yayin nor strong drink, thou, nor thy banim with thee, when ye go into the Ohel Mo'ed, lest ye die; it shall be chukkat olam throughout your generations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 22:26; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:14–16; VAYIKRA 11:47"> And that ye may put difference between kodesh and chol (common, profane), and between tameh (unclean) and tahor (clean);</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:20; MALACHI 2:7; NECHEMYAH 8:8"> And that ye may teach the Bnei Yisroel all the chukkim which Hashem hath spoken unto them by the hand of Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="VAYIKRA 21:22; BAMIDBAR 18:9–10; VAYIKRA 7:9"> And Moshe spoke unto Aharon, and unto El'azar and unto Itamar, his banim that were left, Take the minchah that remaineth of the offerings of Hashem made by eish, and eat it unleavened etzel &#91;Mishlei 8:30 beside&#93; the Mizbe'ach; for it is kodesh kodashim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="VAYIKRA 2:3; VAYIKRA 6:16; BAMIDBAR 18:10"> And ye shall eat it in the makom kadosh, because it is the chok (share, portion) of thee and thy banim, of the sacrifices of Hashem made by eish; for so I am commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 18:11; VAYIKRA 7:29–34; SHEMOT 29:24–28"> And the breast of the waving and raised up thigh shall ye eat in a makom tahor; thou, and thy banim, and thy banot with thee; for they are thy chok, and the chok of thy banim, which are given out of the zevakhim of shelamim (peace offerings) of the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="VAYIKRA 7:34; BERESHIS 17:13; VAYIKRA 7:29–30"> The raised-up thigh and the breast of the waving shall they bring with the offerings made by eish of the chalavim (fat portions), to wave it for a wave offering before Hashem; and it shall be thine, and unto thy banim with thee, by a chok olam (portion forever); as Hashem hath commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="VAYIKRA 9:3; VAYIKRA 9:15; VAYIKRA 6:26"> And Moshe diligently inquired about the se'ir of the chattat, and, hinei, it was burned up; and he was angry with El'azar and Itamar, the bnei Aharon which were left alive, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="VAYIKRA 7:6–7; BAMIDBAR 18:1; VAYIKRA 6:24–30"> Why have ye not eaten the chattat in the makom hakodesh, seeing it is kodesh kodashim, and G-d hath given it you to bear away the avon HaEdah, to make kapporah for them before Hashem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="VAYIKRA 6:30; VAYIKRA 6:26"> Since the dahm of it was not brought in within HaKodesh, ye should indeed have eaten it in the Kodesh, as I commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="VAYIKRA 9:12; VAYIKRA 9:8; YIRMEYAH 14:12"> And Aharon said unto Moshe, See, today have they offered their chattat (sin offering) and their olah (burnt offering) before Hashem; and such things have befallen me; and if I had eaten the chattat today, should it have been accepted in the sight of Hashem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 7:8–9; MATTITYAHU 12:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:18–20"> And when Moshe heard that, he was satisfied.</span>
