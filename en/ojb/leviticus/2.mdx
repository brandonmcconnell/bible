<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="VAYIKRA 6:14–18; VAYIKRA 9:17; YESHAYAH 66:20"> And when a nefesh will offer a korban minchah unto Hashem, his korban shall be of fine flour; and he shall pour shemen upon it, and put incense thereon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="VAYIKRA 5:12; VAYIKRA 6:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:4"> And he shall bring it to the Bnei Aharon the kohanim; and he shall take thereout his handful of the flour thereof, and of the shemen thereof, with all the incense thereof; and the kohen shall burn the memorial portion of it upon the Mizbe'ach, to be an offering made by eish, of a re'ach nicho'ach unto Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="VAYIKRA 10:12–13; BAMIDBAR 18:9; VAYIKRA 21:22"> And the remnant of the minchah shall belong to Aharon and his Banim; it is a kodesh kodashim of the offerings of Hashem made by eish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:7–8; TEHILLIM 22:14"> And if thou bring a korban minchah baked in an oven, it shall be matzot cakes of fine flour mixed with shemen, or matzot wafers anointed with shemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="VAYIKRA 6:21; VAYIKRA 7:9"> And if thy korban minchah be on a pan, it shall be of fine flour matzot, mixed with shemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:1–19; TEHILLIM 22:1–21; MARKOS 14:1–15"> Thou shalt break it in pieces, and pour shemen thereon; it is a minchah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="VAYIKRA 2:1–2; VAYIKRA 7:9"> And if thy korban minchah be in a deep pan, it shall be made of fine flour with shemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And thou shalt bring the minchah that is made of these things unto Hashem; and when it is presented unto the kohen, he shall bring it unto the Mizbe'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="VAYIKRA 2:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:18; VAYIKRA 6:15"> And the kohen shall take from the minchah a memorial portion thereof, and shall burn it upon the Mizbe'ach; it is an offering made by eish, of a re'ach nicho'ach unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="VAYIKRA 2:3"> And that which is left of the minchah shall belong to Aharon and his Banim; it is a kodesh kodashim of the offerings of Hashem made by eish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:9; LUKAS 12:1; SHEMOT 23:18"> No minchah, which ye shall bring unto Hashem, shall be made with chametz; for ye shall burn no se'or, nor any devash, in any offering of Hashem made by eish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:19; BERESHIS 23:10–11; SHEMOT 22:29"> As for the korban reshit (firstfruit offering), ye shall offer them unto Hashem; but they shall not be burned on the Mizbe'ach for a re'ach nicho'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 43:24; MATTITYAHU 5:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:6"> And every korban of thy minchah shalt thou season with melach; neither shalt thou suffer the melach Brit Eloheicha to be lacking from thy minchah; with every minchah of thine thou shalt offer melach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:10; MALACHI 1:11; BAMIDBAR 28:2"> And if thou offer a minchah of thy bikkurim unto Hashem, thou shalt offer for the minchah of thy bikkurim ripe ears of grain roasted by the eish, even meal ground from new grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And thou shalt put shemen upon it, and lay incense thereon; it is a minchah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:26–27; YESHAYAH 61:1"> And the kohen shall burn the memorial portion of it, part of the ground grain thereof, and part of the shemen thereof, with all the incense thereof; it is an offering made by eish unto Hashem.</span>
