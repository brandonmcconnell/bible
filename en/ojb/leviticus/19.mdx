## &#91;KEDOSHIM&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="VAYIKRA 11:44–45; VAYIKRA 20:7; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:15–16"> Speak unto kol Adat Bnei Yisroel, and say unto them, Ye shall be kedoshim (holy ones): for I Hashem Eloheichem am kadosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:12; SHEMOT 20:8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:9"> Ye shall fear every man his em, and his av, and be shomer over My Shabbatot: I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:1; SHEMOT 20:23; TEHILLIM 115:4–7"> Turn ye not unto elilim, nor make to yourselves elohei massekhah (cast metal g-ds); I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="VAYIKRA 22:19; VAYIKRA 22:29; VAYIKRA 7:16"> And if ye offer a zevach shelamim unto Hashem, ye shall offer it for your acceptance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="VAYIKRA 7:11–17"> It shall be eaten the same day ye offer it, and on the next day; and what remains until the yom hashelishi, it shall be burned in the eish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="VAYIKRA 7:18–21; VAYIKRA 22:25; YIRMEYAH 16:18"> And if it be eaten at all on the yom hashelishi, it is piggul (unclean sacrificial flesh); it shall not be accepted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="VAYIKRA 22:15; VAYIKRA 5:1"> Therefore every one that eateth it shall bear his avon, because he hath desecrated as chillul the set-apart-as-kodesh thing of Hashem: and that nefesh shall be cut off from among his people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:22; DEVARIM 24:19–22; RUTH 2:2"> And when ye reap the katzir of your land, thou shalt not wholly reap the corners of thy sadeh, neither shalt thou gather the gleanings of thy katzir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YESHAYAH 17:6; VAYIKRA 25:6; YESHAYAH 24:13"> And thou shalt not glean thy kerem (vineyard), neither shalt thou gather every grape of thy kerem; thou shalt leave them for the oni (poor) and ger (stranger); I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TEHILLIM 101:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:9"> Ye shall not steal, neither deal falsely, neither speak sheker (falsehood, deception, lie) to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:7; DEVARIM 5:11; VAYIKRA 18:21"> And ye shall not swear by My Shem lasheker (falsely), neither shalt thou commit Chillul ha-shem Eloheicha; I am Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:4; MALACHI 3:5; DEVARIM 24:14–15"> Thou shalt not defraud thy re'a (neighbor), neither rob him; the wages of him that is a sakhir (hired man) shall not abide with thee ad boker (until morning).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEVARIM 27:18; VAYIKRA 19:32; VAYIKRA 25:17"> Thou shalt not curse the cheresh (deaf), nor put a michshol (stumbling block) before the ivver (blind), but shalt fear Eloheicha: I am Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:17; MISHLE 24:23; DEVARIM 27:19"> Ye shall do no avel bamishpat (perversion of justice); thou shalt not respect the person of the poor, nor favor the person of the gadol; but in tzedek shalt thou judge thy neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:1; SHEMOT 23:7; MISHLE 20:19"> Thou shalt not go up and down as a rakhil (talebearer, slanderer) among thy people; neither shalt thou stand aside while thy neighborʼs dahm is shed; I am Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:11; LUKAS 17:3"> Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine lev; thou shalt in any wise rebuke thy neighbor, and not bear chet because of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:9; MATTITYAHU 22:39–40; MARKOS 12:31–34"> Thou shalt not avenge, nor bear any grudge against the bnei ammecha (children of thy people), v'ahavta l'reacha kamocha (but thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself): I am Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:14–17; DEVARIM 22:9–11; MATTITYAHU 9:16–17"> Ye shall be shomer over My chukkot. Thou shalt not mate different kinds of animals; thou shalt not sow thy sadeh with mingled zera; neither shall a garment with shaatnez (mixed fibers) come upon thee.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Dt 22:11">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEVARIM 22:23–24; SHEMOT 21:20–21"> And whosoever lieth carnally with an isha, that is a shifcha betrothed to a husband, and not at all redeemed, nor chufshah (freedom) given her; she shall be scourged; they shall not be put to death, because she has not been chuppashah (freed).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="VAYIKRA 6:6–7; VAYIKRA 5:1–6; VAYIKRA 5:15"> And he shall bring his asham (trespass offering, guilt offering) unto Hashem, unto the entrance of the Ohel Mo'ed, even a ram of asham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:26; VAYIKRA 4:20"> And the kohen shall make kapporah for him with the ram of the asham before Hashem for his chattat which he hath sinned; and the chattat which he hath sinned shall be forgiven him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="VAYIKRA 22:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:51; YIRMEYAH 9:25–26"> And when ye shall come into ha'aretz, and shall have planted all manner of food trees, then ye shall treat the fruit thereof as forbidden; shalosh shanim shall it be as forbidden unto you; it shall not be eaten of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MISHLE 3:9; DEVARIM 18:4; BAMIDBAR 18:12–13"> But in the shanah harev'i'it all the fruit thereof shall be kodesh to praise Hashem withal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="CHAGGAI 1:9–11; MALACHI 3:8–10; VAYIKRA 26:3–4"> And in the shanah hachamishit shall ye eat of the fruit thereof, that it may yield unto you the increase thereof: I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEVARIM 18:10–14; MELACHIM BAIS 17:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:6"> Ye shall not eat any thing with the dahm; neither shall ye practice divination, nor practice sorcery.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="VAYIKRA 21:5; DEVARIM 14:1; YECHEZKEL 44:20"> Ye shall not cut the edge of your scalp, neither shalt thou mar the corners of thy beard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEVARIM 14:1; VAYIKRA 21:5; MELACHIM ALEF 18:28"> Ye shall not make any cuttings in your basar for the dead, nor tattoo any marks upon you: I am Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEVARIM 23:17–18; VAYIKRA 21:7; HOSHEA 4:12–14"> Do not prostitute thy bat, to cause her to be a harlot; lest ha'aretz fall to whoredom, and ha'aretz become full of depravity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:2; VAYIKRA 19:3; KOHELET 5:1"> Ye shall be shomer over My Shabbatot, and reverence My Mikdash; I am Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:19; VAYIKRA 20:27; VAYIKRA 20:6–7"> Regard not them that have familiar spirits, neither seek after Ovos (Mediums) and Yidonim (Spiritists), to be defiled by them: I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:1; EKHAH 5:12; MISHLE 16:31"> Thou shalt rise up before the old, and honor the face of the zaken, and fear Eloheicha: I am Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:9; SHEMOT 22:21; MALACHI 3:5"> And if a ger sojourn with thee in your land, ye shall not mistreat him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:19; VAYIKRA 19:18; SHEMOT 12:48–49"> But the ger that dwelleth with you shall be unto you as one born among you, and thou shalt love him as thyself; for ye were gerim in Eretz Mitzrayim: I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEVARIM 25:15; DEVARIM 25:13; MISHLE 16:11"> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in scales, in weights, and dry measures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MISHLE 11:1; SHEMOT 20:2; DEVARIM 25:13–15"> Tzedek scales, tzedek weights, a tzedek ephah, and a tzedek hin, shall ye have: I am Hashem Eloheichem, which brought you out of Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DEVARIM 8:1; VAYIKRA 18:4–5; DEVARIM 5:1"> Therefore shall ye be shomer (on guard to keep) over all My chukkot (statutes), and all My mishpatim (judgments), and do them: I am Hashem.</span>
</div>
</div>
