<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 6:14; VAYIKRA 1:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:1–2"> And if his korban be a zevach of shelamim, if he offer it of the herd; whether it be a zachar or nekevah, he shall offer it tamim (without blemish) before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:10; VAYIKRA 1:4–5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:36–38"> And he shall lay his hand upon the head of his korban, and slaughter (shachat) it at the entrance of the Ohel Mo'ed; and Aharonʼs Banim the kohanim shall sprinkle the dahm upon the Mizbe'ach around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:13; VAYIKRA 4:8–9; SHEMOT 29:22"> And he shall offer of the zevach of the shelamim an offering made by eish unto Hashem; the chelev (fat) that covereth the innards, and all the chelev (fat) that is upon the innards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="VAYIKRA 3:10; VAYIKRA 8:25; SHEMOT 29:22"> And the two kidneys, and the chelev (fat) that is around them, which is by the loins, and the diaphragm with the liver, with the kidneys, it shall he remove.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="VAYIKRA 6:12; SHEMOT 29:13; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5"> And Aharonʼs Banim shall burn it on the Mizbe'ach upon the olah which is upon the wood that is on the eish; it is an offering made by eish, of a re'ach nicho'ach unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 2:11–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:28; VAYIKRA 1:2"> And if his korban for a zevach shelamim unto Hashem be of the flock; zachar or nekevah, he shall offer it tamim (without blemish).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:62; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:12; VAYIKRA 17:8–9"> If he offer a keves (lamb) for his korban, then shall he offer it before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="VAYIKRA 1:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:12; VAYIKRA 3:13"> And he shall lay his hand upon the head of his korban, and slaughter (shachat) it before the Ohel Mo'ed; and Aharonʼs Banim shall sprinkle the dahm thereof around upon the Mizbe'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:22; VAYIKRA 9:19; VAYIKRA 8:25"> And he shall offer of the zevach hashelamim an offering made by eish unto Hashem; the chelev thereof, and the whole fat tail, it shall he remove by the backbone; and the chelev that covereth the innards, and all the chelev that is upon the innards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="VAYIKRA 3:4"> And the two kidneys, and the chelev (fat) that is upon them, which is by the loins, and the diaphragm with the liver, with the kidneys, it shall he remove.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="VAYIKRA 21:17; VAYIKRA 21:6; VAYIKRA 3:16"> And the kohen shall burn it upon the Mizbe'ach; it is the lechem of the offering made by eish unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="VAYIKRA 9:15; YESHAYAH 53:2; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:21"> And if his korban be a goat, then he shall offer it before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="VAYIKRA 3:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:15–21; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:21"> And he shall lay his hand upon the head of it, and slaughter (shachat) it before the Ohel Mo'ed; and the Bnei Aharon shall sprinkle the dahm thereof upon the Mizbe'ach round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:38; MATTITYAHU 22:37; VAYIKRA 3:9–11"> And he shall offer thereof his korban, even an offering made by eish unto Hashem; the chelev (fat) that covereth the innards, and all the chelev (fat) that is upon the innards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the two kidneys, and the chelev (fat) that is upon them, which is by the loins, and the diaphragm above the liver, with the kidneys, it shall he remove.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="VAYIKRA 7:23–25; MATTITYAHU 22:37; YESHAYAH 53:10"> And the kohen shall burn them upon the Mizbe'ach; it is the lechem of the offering made by eish for a re'ach nicho'ach; all the chelev (fat) is Hashemʼs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 9:4; MATTITYAHU 26:28; DEVARIM 15:23"> It shall be a chukkat olam for your generations throughout all your dwellings, that ye eat neither chelev (fat) nor dahm (blood).</span>
