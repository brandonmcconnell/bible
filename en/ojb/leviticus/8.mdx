<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:2–4; SHEMOT 39:41; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:27"> Take Aharon and his Banim with him, and the garments, and the shemen hamishchah (anointing oil), and a bull for the chattat (sin offering), and two rams, and a basket of matzot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:1; NECHEMYAH 8:1"> And gather thou Kol HaEdah together unto the entrance of the Ohel Mo'ed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 39:26; SHEMOT 39:7; SHEMOT 39:5"> And Moshe did as Hashem commanded him; and HaEdah (the Assembly) was gathered together unto the entrance of the Ohel Mo'ed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:4–37"> And Moshe said unto HaEdah, This is the thing which Hashem commanded to be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:26; SHEMOT 29:4"> And Moshe brought Aharon and his Banim, and immersed them with mayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:4; YESHAYAH 61:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:27"> And he put upon him the Kesones,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Yn 19:23">a</a></sup>and girded him with the Avnet (Sash), and clothed him with the Me'il (Robe), and put the Ephod upon him, and he girded him with the Cheishev (Belt) of the Ephod, and fastened it unto him therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZRA 2:63; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:8; YESHAYAH 59:17"> And he put the Choshen (Breastplate) upon him; also he put in the Choshen the Urim and the Tummim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:9–11; ZECHARYAH 6:11–14; SHEMOT 28:36–38"> And he put the Mitznefet (Turban) upon his head; also upon the Mitznefet (Turban) toward his face did he put the Tzitz HaZahav, the Nezer Hakodesh (Holy Diadem); as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 40:9–11; VAYIKRA 8:2; SHEMOT 30:23–29"> And Moshe took the Shemen HaMishchah, and anointed the Mishkan (Tabernacle) and all that was therein and he set them apart as kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:6; SHEMOT 30:29; SHEMOT 16:19"> And he sprinkled thereof upon the Mizbe'ach seven times, and anointed the Mizbe'ach and all its utensils, both the Kiyor (Laver) and its stand, to set them apart as kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHEMOT 30:30; TEHILLIM 133:2; SHEMOT 29:7"> And he poured of the Shemen HaMishchah upon Aharonʼs head, and anointed him, to set him apart as kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:8–9; HISGALUS 5:10; HISGALUS 1:6"> And Moshe brought the Bnei Aharon, and put Kuttonot upon them, and girded them with Sashes, and put Migba'ot (headbands) upon them; as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 43:19; TEHILLIM 66:15; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18"> And he brought the bull for the chattat (sin offering); and Aharon and his Banim laid their hands upon the head of the bull for the chattat (sin offering).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:18–23; YECHEZKEL 43:19–27"> And he slaughtered (shachat) it; and Moshe took the dahm, and put it upon the horns of the Mizbe'ach around with his forefinger, and purified the Mizbe'ach, and poured the dahm at the base of the Mizbe'ach, and set it apart as kodesh, to make kapporah for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:13; VAYIKRA 3:3–5; VAYIKRA 4:8–9"> And he took all the chelev (fat) that was upon the innards, and the diaphragm above the liver, and the two kidneys, and their chelev (fat), and Moshe burned it upon the Mizbe'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:11–12; SHEMOT 29:14; VAYIKRA 6:30"> But the bull, and its hide, its basar, and its dung, he burned with eish outside the machaneh; as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="VAYIKRA 8:2; VAYIKRA 1:4–13; SHEMOT 29:15–18"> And he brought the ram for the olah (burnt offering); and Aharon and his Banim laid their hands upon the head of the ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And he slaughtered (shachat) it; and Moshe sprinkled the dahm upon the Mizbe'ach around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="VAYIKRA 1:8"> And he cut the ram into pieces; and Moshe burned the head, and the pieces, and the chelev (fat).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:2; SHEMOT 29:18; VAYIKRA 1:17"> And he washed the innards and the legs in mayim; and Moshe burned the whole ram upon the Mizbe'ach; it was an olah for a re'ach nicho'ach, and an offering made by eish unto Hashem; just as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="VAYIKRA 8:2; VAYIKRA 8:29; SHEMOT 29:19–31"> And he brought the other ram, the ram of ordination; and Aharon and his Banim laid their hands upon the head of the ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:20; VAYIKRA 14:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:1"> And he slaughtered (shachat) it; and Moshe took of the dahm of it, and put it upon the lobe of Aharonʼs right ear, and upon the thumb of his right hand, and upon the bohen of his right foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:18–22"> And he brought Aharonʼs Banim, and Moshe put of the dahm upon the lobe of their right ear, and upon the thumbs of their right hands, and upon the bohen of their right feet: and Moshe sprinkled the dahm upon the Mizbe'ach around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="VAYIKRA 3:9; VAYIKRA 3:3–5; YESHAYAH 53:10"> And he took the chelev (fat), and the tail, and all the chelev (fat) that was upon the innards, and the diaphragm above the liver, and the two kidneys, and their chelev (fat), and the right thigh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:23; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:12"> And out of the basket of matzot, that was before Hashem, he took one challat matzot, and a loaf of oiled lechem, and one wafer, and put them on the chalavim, and upon the right thigh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 30:21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:14; SHEMOT 29:24–37"> And he put all upon Aharonʼs hands, and upon the hands of his Banim, and waved them for a tenufah (wave offering) before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:25; TEHILLIM 22:13–14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:14–22"> And Moshe took them from off their hands, and burned them on the Mizbe'ach upon the olah (burnt offering); they were ordination offerings for a re'ach nicho'ach; it is an offering made by eish unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:31; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:11; VAYIKRA 7:30–34"> And Moshe took the breast, and waved it for a tenufah before Hashem: it was Mosheʼs share of the ram of ordination; just as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:21; BAMIDBAR 3:3; SHEMOT 30:30"> And Moshe took of the shemen hamishchah, and of the dahm which was upon the Mizbe'ach, and sprinkled it upon Aharon, and upon his garments, and upon his Banim, and upon the garments of his Banim with him; and he set apart as kodesh Aharon, and his garments, and his Banim, and the garments of his Banim with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:31–32; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:20; SHMUEL ALEF 2:13–17"> And Moshe said unto Aharon and to his Banim, Cook the basar at the entrance of the Ohel Mo'ed: and there eat it with the lechem that is in the basket of ordination offerings, as I commanded, saying, Aharon and his Banim shall eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:34; SHEMOT 12:10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:13–14"> And that which remaineth of the basar and of the lechem shall ye burn with eish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:35; BAMIDBAR 19:12; YECHEZKEL 43:25–27"> And ye shall not go out of the entrance of the Ohel Mo'ed in seven days, until the days of your ordination be at an end: for seven days shall he fill your hand.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="shall he fill your hand consecrate you in ordination as kohanim">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:27; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:16; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:11–12"> As he hath done this day, so Hashem hath commanded to do, to make kapporah for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:7; DEVARIM 11:1; MELACHIM ALEF 2:3"> Therefore shall ye abide at the entrance of the Ohel Mo'ed yomam valailah seven days and be shomer over the mishmeret (charge) of Hashem, that ye die not: for so I have been commanded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:32; DEVARIM 4:2; SHEMOT 40:16"> So Aharon and his Banim did all things which Hashem commanded by the hand of Moshe.</span>
