## &#91;BECHUKOTAI&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YESHAYAH 1:19; DEVARIM 11:13–15; DEVARIM 28:1–14"> If ye walk in My chukkot, and be shomer over My mitzvot, and do them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 8:12; TEHILLIM 67:6; VAYIKRA 25:21"> Then I will give you geshem in due season, and HaAretz shall yield her increase, and the etz hasadeh shall yield his pri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="AMOS 9:13; YOEL 2:26; YOEL 2:19"> And your threshing shall reach unto the vintage, and the vintage shall reach unto the sowing time: and ye shall eat your lechem to the full, and dwell in your land safely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YESHAYAH 35:9; TZEFANYAH 3:13; TEHILLIM 147:14"> And I will give shalom in HaAretz, and ye shall lie down, and none shall make you afraid: and I will rid the savage beast out of HaAretz, neither shall the cherev pass through your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And ye shall chase your enemies, and they shall fall before you by the cherev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 23:10; DEVARIM 28:7; DEVARIM 32:30"> And five of you shall chase a hundred, and a hundred of you shall put ten thousand to flight: and your enemies shall fall before you by the cherev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:6–7; NECHEMYAH 9:23; YECHEZKEL 16:62"> For I will look upon you, and make you fruitful, and multiply you, and confirm My Brit (Covenant) with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:22; MELACHIM BAIS 19:29; LUKAS 12:17"> And ye shall be eating the old harvest when you move out the yashan because of the chadash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:8; HISGALUS 21:3; SHEMOT 29:45"> And I set My Mishkan among you: and My Nefesh shall not abhor you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:16; YIRMEYAH 30:22; YIRMEYAH 7:23"> And I will walk among you, and will be Elohim to you, and ye shall be My people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 34:27; VAYIKRA 25:38; TEHILLIM 116:16"> I am Hashem Eloheichem, which brought you forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim, that ye should not be their avadim; and I have broken the bars of your yoke, and made you walk head held high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:15–68; VAYIKRA 26:18; YIRMEYAH 17:27"> But if ye will not pay heed unto Me, and will not do all these mitzvot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 17:15; DEVARIM 31:16; ZECHARYAH 7:11–13"> And if ye shall despise My chukkot, or if your nefesh abhor My mishpatim, so that ye will not do all My mitzvot, but that ye violate My Brit (Covenant),</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="IYOV 31:8; YIRMEYAH 5:17; MICHOH 6:15"> I also will do this unto you: I will even visit upon you terror, wasting disease, and the burning fever, that shall consume the eyes, and drain the nefesh: and ye shall sow your zera in vain, for your enemies shall eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="VAYIKRA 17:10; MISHLE 28:1; TEHILLIM 53:5"> And I will set My Face against you, and ye shall be struck down before your enemies. They that hate you shall reign over you; and ye shall flee when none rodef (pursue, persecute) you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:21; VAYIKRA 26:28; VAYIKRA 26:24"> And if ye will not yet for all this pay heed unto Me, then I will punish you seven times more for your chattot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:23; MELACHIM ALEF 17:1; DANIEL 4:37"> And I will break the ga'on (pride) of your stubbornness; and I will make your skies as barzel, and your soil as bronze;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 127:1; DEVARIM 11:17; YESHAYAH 49:4"> And your ko'ach shall be spent in vain; for your land shall not yield her increase, neither shall the etz HaAretz yield their pri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:18; VAYIKRA 26:27"> And if ye remain walking contrary unto Me, and will not pay heed unto Me, I will bring seven times more makkah (plagues) upon you according to your chattot (sins).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 14:15; DEVARIM 32:24; SHOFETIM 5:6"> I will also send wild beasts among you, which shall rob you of your children, and destroy your cattle, and make you few in number; and darkheichem (your roads, ways) shall be deserted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 5:3; YIRMEYAH 2:30; AMOS 4:6–12"> And if ye will not be corrected by Me, but will walk keri (contrary, hostile) unto Me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="TEHILLIM 18:26; SHMUEL BAIS 22:27; IYOV 9:4"> Then will I also walk b'keri (contrary, hostile) unto you, and will punish you yet seven times for your chattot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:12; YECHEZKEL 5:17; YIRMEYAH 29:17–18"> And I will bring a cherev upon you, that shall avenge Nekam Brit (Vengeance of My Covenant); and when ye are gathered together within your towns, I will send the dever (pestilence) among you; and ye shall be delivered into the hand of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MICHOH 6:14; YESHAYAH 3:1; TEHILLIM 105:16"> And when I have broken the matteh (staff, supply) of your lechem, eser nashim shall bake your lechem in one oven, and they shall dole out your lechem by weight; and ye shall eat, and not be satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:24; VAYIKRA 26:21"> And if ye will not for all this pay heed unto Me, but walk b'keri (contrary, hostile) unto Me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YESHAYAH 59:18; YIRMEYAH 21:5; YESHAYAH 66:15"> Then I will walk keri unto you also in chamat (fury); and I, even I, will chastise you seven times for your chattot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 5:10; EKHAH 4:10; MATTITYAHU 24:19"> And ye shall eat the basar of your banim, and the basar of your banot shall ye eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 23:20; YESHAYAH 27:9; YECHEZKEL 6:13"> And I will destroy your high places, and cut down chammaneichem (your pillars for worshiping the sun), and cast your carcasses upon the carcasses of your idols, and My Nefesh shall abhor you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 2:3; EKHAH 1:10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:26"> And I will turn your cities into ruins, and bring mikdesheichem (your sanctuaries) unto desolation, and I will not take delight in your re'ach nicho'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 9:11; YIRMEYAH 19:8; YIRMEYAH 18:16"> And I will bring HaAretz into desolation; and your enemies which dwell therein shall be astonished at her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 7:14; YECHEZKEL 20:23; DEVARIM 28:64–66"> And I will scatter you among the Goyim, and will draw out a cherev after you; and your land shall be desolate, and your cities ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:21; VAYIKRA 26:43; VAYIKRA 25:2–4"> Then shall HaAretz enjoy her Shabbatot, as long as she lieth desolate, and ye be in your enemiesʼ land; even then shall HaAretz rest, and enjoy her Shabbatot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="YESHAYAH 24:5–6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:22"> As long as she lieth desolate she shall rest, because she did not rest in your Shabbatot, when ye dwelt upon her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 21:7; YESHAYAH 30:17; VAYIKRA 26:17"> And upon them that are left alive of you I will send a fearful faintness into their hearts in the lands of their enemies; and the sound of a shaken leaf shall chase them; and they shall flee, as fleeing from a cherev; and they shall fall v'ein rodef (when none pursueth, persecutes).</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 7:12–13; SHOFETIM 2:14; YIRMEYAH 37:10"> And they shall fall one upon another, as it were before a cherev, when none pursueth; and ye shall have no standing place before your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:48; YESHAYAH 27:13; YIRMEYAH 42:17–18"> And ye shall perish among the Goyim, and the eretz of your enemies shall eat you up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 4:17; YECHEZKEL 24:23; YECHEZKEL 33:10"> And they that are left of you shall waste away in their avon in your enemiesʼ lands; and also in the iniquities of their avot shall they waste away with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MISHLE 28:13; NECHEMYAH 9:2–5; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8–10"> V'hitvadu (if they shall confess) their avon, and the avon of their avot, with their treachery in which they were treacherous against Me, and that also they have walked b'keri (contrary, hostile) unto Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 44:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:26; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:6–7"> And that I also have walked b'keri unto them, and have brought them into the eretz of their enemies; if then their levav he'arel (uncircumcised hearts) be humbled, and they then accept their avon guilt,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., accept the punishment of their iniquity">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="TEHILLIM 106:45; SHEMOT 6:5; YECHEZKEL 16:60"> Then will I remember My Brit (covenant) with Ya'akov, and also My Brit (covenant) with Yitzchak, and also My Brit (covenant) with Avraham will I remember; and I will remember HaAretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:7; VAYIKRA 26:15; YOCHANAN 7:7"> HaAretz also shall be deserted by them, and shall enjoy her Shabbatot, while she lieth desolate without them; and they shall accept their avon:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="accept the punishment of their iniquity">b</a></sup>because, even because they despised My mishpatim, and because their nefesh abhorred My chukkot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:2; NECHEMYAH 9:31; DEVARIM 4:29–31"> And yet for all that, when they be in the eretz of their enemies, I will not cast them away,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e., reject them">c</a></sup>neither will I abhor them, to destroy them utterly, and to break My Brit (covenant) with them: for I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:38; YECHEZKEL 20:22; YECHEZKEL 20:9"> But I will for their sakes remember the Brit Rishonim (covenant of their ancestors) whom I brought forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim in the sight of the Goyim, that I might be Elohim to them: I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="VAYIKRA 27:34; DEVARIM 12:1; DEVARIM 6:1"> These are the chukkim and mishpatim and torot, which Hashem made between Him and the Bnei Yisroel in Mt. Sinai by the hand of Moshe.</span>
</div>
