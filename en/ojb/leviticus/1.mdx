## &#91;VAYIKRA&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:3; SHEMOT 25:22; SHEMOT 29:42"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, and spoke unto him out of the Ohel Mo'ed (Tent of Meeting)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., Tabernacle">a</a></sup>saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="VAYIKRA 22:18–19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 16:29"> Speak unto the Bnei Yisroel, and say unto them, If any man of you bring a korban unto Hashem, ye shall bring your korban of the cattle, even of the herd, and of the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEVARIM 15:21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:14; VAYIKRA 22:19–24"> If his korban be an olah (burnt sacrifice) of the herd, let him offer a zachar tamim (male without blemish); he shall offer it that he may be accepted at the entrance of the Ohel Mo'ed before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:10; SHEMOT 29:15; VAYIKRA 4:20"> And he shall lay his hand upon the head of the olah (burnt offering); and it shall be accepted for him to make kapporah for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="VAYIKRA 1:11; VAYIKRA 3:8; VAYIKRA 3:2"> And he shall slaughter (shachat) the young bull before Hashem; and the kohanim, Aharonʼs banim, shall bring the dahm, and sprinkle the dahm around upon the Mizbe'ach that is by the entrance of the Ohel Mo'ed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="VAYIKRA 7:8; BERESHIS 3:21"> And he shall skin the olah, and cut it into pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="VAYIKRA 6:12–13; BERESHIS 22:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:26"> And the Bnei Aharon the kohen shall put eish upon the Mizbe'ach, and lay the wood in order upon the eish;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="VAYIKRA 9:13–14; VAYIKRA 1:12; MELACHIM ALEF 18:33"> And the kohanim, Aharonʼs banim, shall lay the parts, the head, and the fat, in order upon the wood that is on the eish which is upon the Mizbe'ach:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 8:21; VAYIKRA 1:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:2"> But his innards and his legs shall he wash in mayim; and the kohen shall burn the whole on the Mizbe'ach, to be an olah, an offering made by eish, of a re'ach nicho'ach (sweet savour) unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:6–7; VAYIKRA 1:2–3; BERESHIS 8:20"> And if his korban be of the flocks, namely, of the sheep, or of the goats, for an olah (burnt offering); he shall bring it a zachar tamim (male without blemish).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="VAYIKRA 1:5; SHEMOT 40:22; VAYIKRA 7:2"> And he shall slaughter (shachat) it on the side of the Mizbe'ach northward before Hashem: and the kohanim, Aharonʼs banim, shall sprinkle his dahm around upon the Mizbe'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he shall cut it into pieces, with his head and his fat; and the kohen shall lay them in order on the wood that is on the eish which is upon the Mizbe'ach:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="VAYIKRA 1:9"> But he shall wash the innards and the legs with mayim; and the kohen shall bring the whole, and burn it upon the Mizbe'ach: it is an olah, an offering made by eish, of a re'ach nicho'ach (sweet savour) unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="VAYIKRA 5:7; LUKAS 2:24; VAYIKRA 12:8"> And if the olah for his korban to Hashem be of fowls, then he shall bring his korban of turtledoves, or of young pigeons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="VAYIKRA 5:8–9; TEHILLIM 22:21; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:27"> And the kohen shall bring it unto the Mizbe'ach, and wring off his head, and burn it on the Mizbe'ach; and the dahm thereof shall be pressed out at the side of the Mizbe'ach:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:2; LUKAS 1:35; VAYIKRA 16:27"> And he shall pluck away his crop with his feathers, and cast it beside the Mizbe'ach on the east side, by the place of the ashes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:10; YOCHANAN 19:30; VAYIKRA 1:9–10"> And he shall tear it open with the wings thereof, but shall not tear it in half; and the kohen shall burn it upon the Mizbe'ach, upon the wood that is upon the eish; it is an olah, an offering made by eish, of a re'ach nicho'ach (sweet savour) unto Hashem.</span>
</div>
