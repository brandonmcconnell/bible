<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:4; VAYIKRA 23:37; BAMIDBAR 29:39"> Speak unto the Bnei Yisroel, and say unto them, Concerning the Mo'adim of Hashem, which ye shall proclaim to be mikra'ei kodesh, even these are My Mo'adim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:3; SHEMOT 23:12; SHEMOT 35:2–3"> Sheshet yamim shall work be done; but the yom hashevi'i is the Shabbos of Shabbaton, a mikra kodesh; ye shall do no work therein: it is the Shabbos to Hashem in all your dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:2; SHEMOT 23:14; VAYIKRA 23:37"> These are the Moa'dim of Hashem, even mikra'ei kodesh, which ye shall proclaim in their mo'adim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 5:10; DEVARIM 16:1–8; BAMIDBAR 28:16–17"> In the fourteenth day of the first month at twilight is Hashemʼs Pesach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:18; DEVARIM 16:8; SHEMOT 12:15–16"> And on the fifteenth day of the same month is the Chag HaMatzot unto Hashem; shivat yamim ye must eat matzot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 28:18–25; VAYIKRA 23:8; VAYIKRA 23:21"> In the Yom HaRishon ye shall have a mikra kodesh; ye shall do no melekhet avodah therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> But ye shall offer an offering made by eish unto Hashem shivat yamim; in the seventh day is a mikra kodesh; ye shall do no melekhet avodah therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:26; SHEMOT 23:19; DEVARIM 16:9"> Speak unto the Bnei Yisroel, and say unto them, When ye be come into HaAretz which I am giving unto you, and shall reap the harvest thereof, then ye shall bring an omer (sheaf &#91;of barley&#93;) of the reshit of your harvest unto the kohen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:24; VAYIKRA 10:14; VAYIKRA 9:21"> And he shall wave the omer before Hashem, for your acceptance; on the day after the Shabbos the kohen shall wave it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:10–12; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19; VAYIKRA 1:10"> And ye shall offer that day when ye wave the omer a keves tamim of the first year for an olah unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="VAYIKRA 2:14–16; YOEL 1:9; BAMIDBAR 28:10"> And the minchah thereof shall be two tenths-ephah of fine flour mingled with shemen, an offering made by eish unto Hashem for a re'ach nicho'ach; and the nesekh thereof shall be of yayin, the fourth part of a hin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:26; BERESHIS 4:4–5; VAYIKRA 3:17"> And ye shall eat neither lechem, nor roasted grain, nor plump kernels until the same day that ye have brought a korban unto Eloheichem; it shall be a chukkat olam throughout your dorot in all your dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:10–11; VAYIKRA 25:8; DEVARIM 16:9–10"> And ye shall count unto you from the day after the Shabbos, from the day that ye brought the omer of the wave offering; shevah shabbatot shall be complete,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:1; BAMIDBAR 28:26"> Even unto the day after the seventh Shabbos shall ye count fifty days; and ye shall offer a minchah chadashah unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:10; SHEMOT 34:26; SHEMOT 34:22"> Ye shall bring out of your dwellings two lechem wave-offerings of two tenth ephah; they shall be of fine flour; they shall be baked with chametz; they are the bikkurim unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 28:27–31; VAYIKRA 23:12–13; MALACHI 1:13–14"> And ye shall offer with the lechem shivat kevasim temimim of the first year, and one young bull, and two rams; they shall be for an olah unto Hashem, with their minchot, and their nesakhim, even an offering made by eish, of re'ach nicho'ach unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 28:30; VAYIKRA 4:23–28; VAYIKRA 7:11–18"> Then ye shall sacrifice one kid of the goats for a chattat and two kevasim of the first year for a zevach of shelamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEVARIM 18:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:14; BAMIDBAR 18:8–12"> And the kohen shall wave them with the lechem of the bikkurim for a wave offering before Hashem, with the two kevasim; they shall be kodesh to Hashem for the kohen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:2; VAYIKRA 23:4; BAMIDBAR 18:23"> And ye shall proclaim on the same day, that it may be a mikra kodesh unto you; ye shall do no melekhet avodah therein; it shall be a chukkat olam in all your dwellings throughout your dorot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEVARIM 24:19–21; VAYIKRA 19:9–10; YESHAYAH 58:10"> And when ye reap the harvest of your land, thou shalt not reap to the edges of thy sadeh when thou reapest, neither shalt thou gather the gleanings of thy harvest; thou shalt leave them unto the oni (poor), and to the ger: I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:52; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:16; VAYIKRA 25:9"> Speak unto the Bnei Yisroel, saying, In the seventh month, in the first day of the month, shall ye have a Shabbaton, a zikhron teru'ah mikra kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:21"> Ye shall do no melekhet avodah therein; but ye shall offer an offering made by eish unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="VAYIKRA 16:29–31; SHEMOT 30:10; VAYIKRA 16:24"> Also on the tenth day of this seventh month there shall be Yom Kippurim; it shall be a mikra kodesh unto you; and ye shall afflict your nefashot, and offer an offering made by eish unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 3:9; VAYIKRA 16:34; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:10"> And ye shall do no work in that same day; for it is Yom Kippurim, to make kapporah for you before Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:14; BAMIDBAR 5:2; VAYIKRA 23:27"> For whatsoever nefesh it be that shall not be afflicted in that same day, he shall be cut off from among his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:3; TZEFANYAH 2:5; YIRMEYAH 15:7"> And whatsoever nefesh it be that doeth any work in that same day, that nefesh will I destroy from among his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Ye shall do no manner of work; it shall be a chukkat olam throughout your dorot in all your dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="VAYIKRA 16:31; MATTITYAHU 11:28–30; TEHILLIM 69:10–11"> It shall be unto you a Shabbos Shabbaton, and ye shall afflict your nefashot; in the ninth day of the month at erev, from erev unto erev, shall ye observe your Shabbos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:2; NECHEMYAH 8:14; EZRA 3:4"> Speak unto the Bnei Yisroel, saying, The fifteenth day of this seventh month shall be the Chag HaSukkot for shivat yamim unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:24–25; VAYIKRA 23:7–8"> On the Yom HaRishon shall be a mikra kodesh; ye shall do no melekhet avodah (laborious work) therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 8:18; YOCHANAN 7:37; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:8–11"> Shivat yamim ye shall offer an offering made by eish unto Hashem; on the eighth day shall be a mikra kodesh unto you; and ye shall offer an offering made by eish unto Hashem; it is an atzeret (assembly); and ye shall do no melekhet avodah therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:2; VAYIKRA 23:4; KOHELET 3:1"> These are the Mo'adim of Hashem, which ye shall proclaim to be mikra'ei kodesh, to offer an offering made by eish unto Hashem — an olah (burnt offering), and a minchah, a zevach, and nesakhim, every required thing upon its day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 29:39; VAYIKRA 19:3; EZRA 2:68–69"> Beside the Shabbatot of Hashem, and beside your mattenot, and beside all your nederim, and beside all your nedavot (freewill offerings), which ye give unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:16; DEVARIM 16:13; VAYIKRA 23:34"> Also in the fifteenth day of the seventh month, when ye have gathered in the crop of HaAretz, ye shall keep a Chag (Feast) unto Hashem shivat yamim; on the first day shall be a Shabbaton, and on the eighth day shall be a Shabbaton.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:4; HISGALUS 7:9; DEVARIM 16:14–15"> And ye shall take you on the Yom HaRishon the fruit of the citron tree, branches of temarim (date palms), and twigs of plaited trees, and willows of the brook; and ye shall rejoice before Hashem Eloheichem shivat yamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 29:12; NECHEMYAH 8:18"> And ye shall keep it a Chag (Feast) unto Hashem shivat yamim in the shanah. It shall be a chukkat olam in your dorot; ye shall celebrate it in the seventh month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 8:14–17; BERESHIS 33:17; BAMIDBAR 24:5"> Ye shall dwell in sukkot shivat yamim; all that are the Ezrach Yisroel shall dwell in sukkot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="DEVARIM 31:10–13; TEHILLIM 78:5–6; SHEMOT 13:14"> That your dorot may know that I made the Bnei Yisroel to dwell in sukkot, when I brought them out of Eretz Mitzrayim: I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:1–2; MATTITYAHU 18:20; VAYIKRA 21:24"> And Moshe declared unto the Bnei Yisroel the Mo'adim of Hashem.</span>
