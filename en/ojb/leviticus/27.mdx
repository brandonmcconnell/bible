<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 6:2; SHMUEL ALEF 1:28; SHOFETIM 11:30–31"> Speak unto the Bnei Yisroel, and say unto them, When a man shall articulate a neder, the nefashot shall be for Hashem by thy evaluation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 30:13; VAYIKRA 27:25; BAMIDBAR 18:16"> And thy evaluation shall be of the zachar from esrim shanim even unto shishim shanah, even thy evaluation shall be fifty shekels kesef, after the shekel HaKodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:9–10; ZECHARYAH 11:12–13; MATTITYAHU 26:15"> And if it be a nekevah, then thy evaluation shall be shloshim shekel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And if it be from chamesh shanim even unto esrim shanah, then thy evaluation shall be of the zachar esrim shekel, and for the nekevah aseret shekalim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:40–43; BAMIDBAR 18:14–16"> And if it be from a month old even unto chamesh shanim, then thy evaluation shall be of the zachar chamishah shekalim kesef, and for the nekevah thy evaluation shall be shloshet shekalim kesef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 90:10"> And if it be from shishim shanah and above, if it be a zachar, then thy evaluation shall be chamishah asar shekel, and for the nekevah asarah shekalim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MARKOS 14:7; VAYIKRA 14:21–22; LUKAS 21:1–4"> But if he be poorer than thy evaluation, then he shall present himself before the kohen, and the kohen shall value him; according to the ability of the one who vowed the neder shall the kohen value him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And if it be a behemah, whereof men bring a korban unto Hashem, all that any man giveth of such unto Hashem shall be kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="VAYIKRA 27:15–33; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8"> He shall not exchange it, nor substitute it, a tov for a rah, or rah for tov: and if he shall at all substitute behemah for behemah, then it and its temurah (substitute)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1K 2:24">a</a></sup>shall be kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEVARIM 23:18; MALACHI 1:14"> And if it be any behemah temeiah, of which they do not offer a korban unto Hashem, then he shall present the behemah before the kohen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the kohen shall value it, whether it be tov or rah; as thou valuest it, who art the kohen, so shall it be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="VAYIKRA 27:19; VAYIKRA 27:15; VAYIKRA 22:14"> But if he will at all redeem it, then he shall add chamishito (a fifth part thereof) unto thy evaluation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 18:14; VAYIKRA 25:29–31; VAYIKRA 27:21"> And when a man shall set apart as kodesh his bais to be kodesh unto Hashem, then the kohen shall evaluate it, whether it be tov or rah; as the kohen shall set the value of it, so shall it remain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="VAYIKRA 27:13"> And if he that set it apart as kodesh will redeem his bais, then he shall add the fifth part of the kesef of thy evaluation unto it, and it shall be his.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 45:11–14; HOSHEA 3:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:34–37"> And if a man shall set apart as kodesh unto Hashem some part of a sadeh of his possession, then thy evaluation shall be according to its seeding; an area seeded by a homer of barley shall be valued at chamishim shekel kesef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> If he set apart as kodesh his sadeh from the Shnat HaYovel, according to thy evaluation it shall remain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:15–16; VAYIKRA 25:51–52; VAYIKRA 25:27"> But if he set apart as kodesh his sadeh after the Yovel, then the kohen shall reckon unto him the kesef according to the shanim that remain, even unto the Shnat HaYovel, and it shall be deducted from thy evaluation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="VAYIKRA 27:13"> And if he that set apart as kodesh the sadeh will in any wise redeem it, then he shall add the fifth part of the kesef of thy evaluation unto it, and it shall be his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And if he will not redeem the sadeh, or if he have sold the sadeh to ish acher (another man), it shall not be redeemed any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 44:29; BAMIDBAR 18:14; VAYIKRA 25:31"> But the sadeh, when it is released in the Yovel, shall be kodesh unto Hashem, as a sadeh hacherem (devoted); the possession thereof shall be the kohenʼs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:10; VAYIKRA 25:25"> And if a man set apart as kodesh unto Hashem a sadeh which he hath bought, which is not of the fields of his possession,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., ancestral heritage">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="VAYIKRA 27:18; VAYIKRA 27:12"> Then the kohen shall reckon unto him the worth of thy evaluation, even unto the Shnat HaYovel: and he shall give thine evaluation in that day, as kodesh unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:28; VAYIKRA 27:20"> In the Shnat HaYovel the sadeh shall return and revert unto him of whom it was bought, even to him to whom the Achuzzat HaAretz (Ancestral Heritage of the Land) did belong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHEMOT 30:13; BAMIDBAR 18:16; BAMIDBAR 3:47"> And all thy evaluations shall be according to the shekel HaKodesh; twenty gerah shall be the shekel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHEMOT 13:2; SHEMOT 13:12–13; BAMIDBAR 18:17"> Only the bechor of the behemah, which should be Hashemʼs Bechor, no man shall set it apart as kodesh; whether it be shor (ox), or seh (sheep): it is Hashemʼs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="VAYIKRA 27:11"> And if it be of a behemah hatemeiah, then he shall redeem it according to thine evaluation, and shall add a fifth part of it thereto; or if it be not redeemed, then it shall be sold according to thy evaluation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 6:17–19; VAYIKRA 27:21; SHEMOT 22:20"> Nevertheless, no cherem (devoted thing), that a man shall devote unto Hashem of all that he hath, both of adam and behemah, and of the sadeh of his possession, shall be sold or redeemed; every cherem is kodesh kodashim unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:18–23; BAMIDBAR 21:2–3"> No cherem, which shall be devoted of men, shall be redeemed; but shall surely be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:23; NECHEMYAH 13:12; BERESHIS 28:22"> And all the ma'aser HaAretz (tithe of the Land), whether of the zera HaAretz, or of the pri HaEtz, is Hashemʼs; it is kodesh unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And if a man will at all redeem ought of his ma'aserot (tithes), he shall add thereto the fifth part thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 33:13; YECHEZKEL 20:37; MICHOH 7:14"> And concerning the ma'aser of the herd, or of the flock, even of whatsoever passeth under the rod, the tenth shall be kodesh unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="VAYIKRA 27:10"> He shall not search whether it be tov or rah, neither shall he change it; and if he substitute it at all, then both it and the temurah (exchange, substitute) shall be kodesh; it shall not be redeemed. (Isaiah 53: Moshiach our temurah!)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:46; BAMIDBAR 1:1; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:18–25"> These are the mitzvot, which Hashem commanded Moshe for the Bnei Yisroel in Mt. Sinai.</span>
</div>
