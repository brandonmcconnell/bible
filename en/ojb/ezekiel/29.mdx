<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 29:17; YECHEZKEL 26:1; YECHEZKEL 20:1"> In the tenth year, in the tenth month, in the twelfth day of the month, the Devar Hashem came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 6:2; YIRMEYAH 44:30; YIRMEYAH 46:2–16"> Ben adam, set thy face against Pharaoh Melech Mitzrayim, and prophesy against him, and against all Mitzrayim:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 32:2; YESHAYAH 27:1; YIRMEYAH 44:30"> Speak, and say, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Hineni, I am against thee,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Pharaoh Melech Mitzrayim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the great monster that lieth in the midst of his channels,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">which hath said: My Nile is mine own,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I have made it for myself.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 19:28; YECHEZKEL 38:4; AMOS 4:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I will put hooks in thy jaws,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will cause the dag (fish) of thy channels to stick unto thy scales,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will bring thee up out of the midst of thy river channels,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and all the dag (fish) of thy channels</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shall stick unto thy scales.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 34:20; YECHEZKEL 32:4–6; YIRMEYAH 8:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will throw thee out into the midbar,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">thee and all the dag (fish) of thy river channels;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">thou shalt fall upon the open sadeh;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">thou shalt not be picked up together, nor gathered;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have given thee for food</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to the beasts of ha'aretz</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and to the oph HaShomayim.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:21; YESHAYAH 36:6; SHEMOT 9:14"> And all the inhabitants of Mitzrayim shall know that I am Hashem. Because they have been mishenet kaneh (a staff of reed) to Bais Yisroel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YESHAYAH 36:6; YECHEZKEL 17:15–17; YIRMEYAH 17:5–6"> When they took hold of thee by thy palm, thou didst splinter, and tear all their katef (shoulder); and when they leaned upon thee, thou broke, and madest all their loins unsteady.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 14:17; YECHEZKEL 30:10; YECHEZKEL 29:19–20"> Therefore thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Hineni, I will bring a cherev upon thee, and cut off adam and behemah from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 29:3; MISHLE 16:18; YECHEZKEL 29:10–12"> And Eretz Mitzrayim shall be desolate and in ruins; and they shall know that I am Hashem. Because he hath said: The Nile is Mine, and I have made it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 30:12; YECHEZKEL 30:6–9; SHEMOT 14:2"> Hineni, therefore I am against thee, and against thy river channels, and I will make Eretz Mitzrayim utterly ruins and desolate, from Migdol to Seveneh even unto the border of Kush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 32:13; YIRMEYAH 43:11–12; YECHEZKEL 30:10–13"> No regel of adam shall pass through it, nor regel of behemah shall pass through it, neither shall it be inhabited arba'im shanah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 46:19; YECHEZKEL 30:7; YECHEZKEL 30:23"> And I will make Eretz Mitzrayim desolate in the midst of the countries that are desolate, and her towns among the towns that are laid waste shall be desolate arba'im shanah; and I will scatter the Mitzrayim among the Goyim, and will disperse them through the countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 46:26; YESHAYAH 19:22"> Yet thus saith Adonoi Hashem: At the end of arba'im shanah will I gather Mitzrayim from the peoples where they were scattered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 30:14; YESHAYAH 11:11; YIRMEYAH 44:1"> And I will bring back the captives of Mitzrayim, and will cause them to return into Eretz Patros, into the land of their ancestry; and they shall be there a lowly mamlachah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 10:11; YECHEZKEL 30:13; YECHEZKEL 17:14"> It shall be the shefalah (lowliest) of the mamlechot; neither shall it exalt itself any more above the Goyim; for I will diminish them, that they shall no more rule over the Goyim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HOSHEA 8:13; YESHAYAH 20:5; YECHEZKEL 29:6–7"> And it shall be no more the confidence of Bais Yisroel, but shall bring their avon to remembrance, when they turned toward them; but they shall know that I am Adonoi Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 29:1; YECHEZKEL 1:2; YECHEZKEL 24:1"> And it came to pass in the seven and twentieth year, in the first month, in the first day of the month, the Davar Hashem came unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 27:6; YIRMEYAH 25:9; YECHEZKEL 26:7–12"> Ben adam, Nevukhadretzar Melech Bavel caused his army to avodah gedolah (labor strenuously) against Tzor (Tyre); every rosh was rubbed bare, and every katef (shoulder) was rubbed raw; yet had he no sachar (pay), nor his army, from Tzor (Tyre), for the avodah that he had served against it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 30:24–25; YIRMEYAH 43:10–13; YECHEZKEL 32:11"> Therefore thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Hineni, I will give Eretz Mitzrayim unto Nevukhadretzar Melech Bavel; and he shall take her wealth, and take her plunder, and pillage her booty; and it shall be the sachar (wages) for his army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 25:9; YESHAYAH 10:6–7; YESHAYAH 45:1–3"> I have given him Eretz Mitzrayim as his pay for which he worked, because they worked for Me, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 2:10; LUKAS 21:15; TEHILLIM 132:17"> In that day will I cause the keren (horn, strength) of Bais Yisroel to atzmiach (branch, sprout), and I will give thee the opening of the mouth in the midst of them; and they shall know that I am Hashem.</span>
