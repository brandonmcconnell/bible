<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Devar Hashem came again unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 49:1–6; AMOS 1:13–15; YECHEZKEL 6:2"> Ben adam, set thy face against the Bnei Ammon, and prophesy against them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 36:2; TEHILLIM 70:2–3; MISHLE 17:5"> And say unto the Bnei Ammon, Hear the Devar Adonoi Hashem: thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Because thou saidst, Aha, against my &#91;Beis Ha&#93;Mikdash, when it was desecrated; and against Admat Yisroel, when it was laid waste; and against the Bais Yehudah, when they went into Golus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:33; SHOFETIM 6:33; MELACHIM ALEF 4:30"> Hineni, therefore I will deliver thee to the Bnei Kedem for a morashah (possession, inheritance), and they shall set up their camps in thee, and make their mishkanim in thee; they shall eat thy pri, and they shall drink thy cholov (milk).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 21:20; SHMUEL BAIS 12:26; YESHAYAH 17:2"> And I will make Rabbah a pasture for gemalim (camels), and the Bnei Ammon a resting place for tzon; and ye shall have da'as that I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TZEFANYAH 2:8; OVADYAH 1:12; TZEFANYAH 2:10"> For thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Because thou hast clapped thine hands, and stamped with the regel, and rejoiced with all thy malice against Admat Yisroel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TZEFANYAH 1:4; YECHEZKEL 25:16; YECHEZKEL 6:14"> Hineni, therefore I will stretch out mine yad upon thee, and will hand thee over for plunder to the Goyim; and I will cut thee off from the nations, and I will cause thee to perish from the countries; I will destroy thee; and thou shalt know that I am Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YESHAYAH 15:1–9; YIRMEYAH 25:21; YIRMEYAH 48:1–47"> Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Because Moav and Se'ir do say, Hinei, the Bais Yehudah is like all the Goyim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 48:23; YEHOSHUA 13:17; YEHOSHUA 13:19–20"> Therefore, hineni, I will open the flank of Moav of its towns, of its towns which are on its frontier, the glory of the Eretz Beit-HaYeshimot, Ba'al-Me'on, and Kiryatayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 21:32; TEHILLIM 83:3–6; YECHEZKEL 25:4"> Unto the Bnei Kedem along with the Bnei Ammon will I give them as a morashah, so that the Bnei Ammon will not be remembered among the Goyim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 35:15; YECHEZKEL 30:19; TEHILLIM 149:7"> And I will execute shfatim (punishments) upon Moav; and they shall know that I am Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 49:7–22; YECHEZKEL 25:8; TEHILLIM 137:7"> Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Because Edom hath dealt against the Bais Yehudah by taking vengeance, and with great asham (guilt) offended, and revenged himself upon them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 29:8; MALACHI 1:3–4; YIRMEYAH 25:23"> Therefore thus saith Adonoi Hashem: I will also stretch out Mine yad upon Edom, and will cut off adam and behemah from it; and I will make it desolate; from Teman even to Dedan shall they fall by the cherev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YESHAYAH 11:14; BERESHIS 27:29; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:30–31"> And I will take My vengeance upon Edom by the yad of My people Yisroel; and they shall do in Edom according to Mine anger and according to My fury; and they shall know My vengeance, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 25:6; YESHAYAH 14:29–31; YECHEZKEL 25:12"> Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Because the Pelishtim (Philistines) have dealt by revenge, and have taken vengeance with malice in nefesh, to destroy it for the eyvat olam (ancient enmity);</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 30:14; TZEFANYAH 2:4–15; YECHEZKEL 25:13"> Therefore thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Hineni, I will stretch out Mine yad upon the Pelishtim (Philistines), and I will cut off the Keretim, and destroy the she'erit (remnant) of the seacoast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 25:11; YECHEZKEL 25:14; YECHEZKEL 5:15"> And I will execute great vengeance upon them with tokhechot chemah (punishments of wrath); and they shall know that I am Hashem, when I shall take my vengeance upon them.</span>
</div>
