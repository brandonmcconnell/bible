<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 3:24; BAMIDBAR 11:25–26; YECHEZKEL 3:12"> And the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hakodesh">a</a></sup>entered into me just as He spoke unto me, and stood me upon my raglayim, that I heard Him that spoke unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 3:25; SHMUEL ALEF 8:7–8; DEVARIM 9:24"> And He said unto me, Ben Adam, I send thee to the Bnei Yisroel, to Goyim HaMoredim (Rebellious Nations)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Tribes">b</a></sup>that hath rebelled against Me; they and their Avot have rebelled against Me, even unto this very day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 3:7; YESHAYAH 48:4; YIRMEYAH 5:3"> For they are stubborn banim and chizkei lev (stiff-hearted). I do send thee unto them; and thou shalt say unto them, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 33:33; YECHEZKEL 3:27; MATTITYAHU 10:12–15"> And they, whether they will hear, or whether they will refrain from heeding — for they are a bais meri (house of rebellion), yet shall they know that there hath been a navi among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 1:8; YESHAYAH 51:12; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:14"> And thou, Ben Adam, be not afraid of them, neither be afraid of their devarim, though briers and thorns be with thee, and thou dost dwell among akrabim (scorpions); be not afraid of their devarim, nor be afraid at their looks, for they are a bais meri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 23:28; YECHEZKEL 3:17; YIRMEYAH 1:7"> And thou shalt speak My devarim unto them, whether they will hear, or whether they will refrain from heeding; for they are meri (rebellious).</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 8:3; HISGALUS 5:1–5; DANIEL 10:10"> And when I looked, hinei, a yad was sent unto me; and, hinei, a megillat sefer was therein;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HISGALUS 8:13; YESHAYAH 3:11; HISGALUS 9:12"> And He unrolled the megillah before me; and it was written on front and back: and there was written therein lamentations, and mourning, and woe.</span>
