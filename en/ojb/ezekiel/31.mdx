<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 29:19; YECHEZKEL 30:10; YECHEZKEL 31:18"> Ben adam, speak unto Pharaoh Melech Mitzrayim, and to his multitude:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Whom art thou like in thy greatness?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YESHAYAH 10:33–34; DANIEL 4:10; DANIEL 4:20–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hinei, Ashur (the Assyrian) was a cedar in Levanon</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with yafeh branches, and with a forest shade,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and of a high stature;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and his top was above the thick boughs.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HISGALUS 17:15; MISHLE 14:28; YECHEZKEL 17:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The mayim made it grow,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the tehom (underground water) set him up in height</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">by her streams flowing</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all around its planting,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and sent her rivulets</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">unto all the trees of the sadeh.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DANIEL 4:11; YECHEZKEL 17:5; TEHILLIM 1:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore his height was exalted</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">above kol atzei hasadeh (all the trees of the field),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and his boughs were multiplied,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and his branches became long</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because of the mayim rabbim, as it sent them out.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 17:23; MATTITYAHU 13:32; DANIEL 4:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the oph HaShomayim</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">made their nests in his boughs,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and under his branches did all the beasts of the sadeh</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">give birth to their young,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and under his tzel (shadow)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">dwelt kol Goyim rabbim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thus was he beautiful in his gadol (greatness),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the length of his branches;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for his shoresh (root) reached</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to mayim rabbim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 28:13; BERESHIS 13:10; YESHAYAH 51:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The cedars in the Gan Elohim</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">could not compare with him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the pine trees were not</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like his boughs,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the plane trees were not</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like his branches;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor any etz (tree) in the Gan Elohim</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">was like unto him in his beauty.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 28:13; DANIEL 2:37–38; BERESHIS 13:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have made him beautiful</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">by the multitude of his branches:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so that all the trees of Eden, that were in the Gan Elohim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">envied him.</span></span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 28:17; DANIEL 5:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:25"> Therefore thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Because thou hast increased thyself in height, and he hath shot up his top above the thick boughs, and his lev is proudly lifted up in his height,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 32:11–12; NACHUM 3:18; DANIEL 5:18–19"> I have therefore delivered him into the yad of the mighty one of the Goyim; he shall surely deal with him; I have driven him out according to his wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 28:7; YECHEZKEL 35:8; YECHEZKEL 30:11"> And zarim, the most terrible of the Goyim, have cut him down, and have left him; upon the harim and in all the ge'ayot (valleys) his branches are fallen, and his boughs are broken by all the ravines of the earth; and kol Amei HaAretz (all the nations of the earth) departed from his tzel (shade), and forsook him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 32:4; YECHEZKEL 29:5; YESHAYAH 18:6"> Upon his ruin shall all the oph Shomayim remain, and all the beasts of the sadeh shall come to his branches;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 63:9–10; NECHEMYAH 13:18; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:6"> To the end that none of all the trees by the mayim exalt themselves for their height, neither shoot up their top above the thick boughs, and that no trees that drink mayim may reach up to them in height; for they are all delivered unto mavet, to the depths of ha'aretz, in the midst of the bnei adam, with them that go down to the bor (pit).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MALACHI 3:4; NACHUM 2:8–10; HISGALUS 18:9–11"> Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: In the day when he went down to Sheol I caused a mourning; I covered the tehom (deep) for him, and I restrained the rivers thereof, and the mayim rabbim were held back; and I caused Levanon to mourn for him, and all the trees of the sadeh wilted and fainted for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YESHAYAH 14:15; YESHAYAH 14:8; YECHEZKEL 26:15"> I made the Goyim to shake at the sound of his fall, when I cast him down to Sheol with them that descend into the bor (pit); and all the trees of Eden, the choice and best of Levanon, all that drink mayim, shall be consoled in the depths of ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TEHILLIM 9:17; YECHEZKEL 31:6; YECHEZKEL 31:3"> They also went down into Sheol with him unto them that be slain with the cherev; and they that were his zero'a, that dwelt under his tzel (shade) in the midst of the Goyim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 32:19; YECHEZKEL 32:21; YECHEZKEL 28:10"> To whom art thou thus like in kavod and in greatness among the trees of Eden? Yet shalt thou be brought down with the trees of Eden unto the depths of ha'aretz; thou shalt lie in the midst of the arelim (uncircumcised) with them that be slain by the cherev. This is Pharaoh and all his multitude, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
