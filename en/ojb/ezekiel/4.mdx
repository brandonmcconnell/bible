<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HOSHEA 1:2–9; AMOS 3:2; YECHEZKEL 12:3–16"> Thou also, Ben Adam, take thee a levenah (tile, brick), and lay it before thee, and engrave upon it the Ir, even Yerushalayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 21:22; YIRMEYAH 6:6; LUKAS 19:42–44"> And lay matzor (siege) against it, and erect against it siege works, and build a ramp against it; set the machanot also against it, and set battering rams against it all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 12:6; YESHAYAH 8:18; YESHAYAH 20:3"> Moreover take thou unto thee a machavat barzel (iron griddle), and set it for a kir barzel (wall of iron) between thee and the Ir; and set thy face against it, and it shall be besieged, and thou shalt lay siege against it. This shall be an ot (sign) to Bais Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="VAYIKRA 16:22; VAYIKRA 10:17; BAMIDBAR 18:1"> Lie thou also upon thy left side, and lay the avon Bais Yisroel upon it; according to the mispar of the yamim that thou shalt lie upon it thou shalt bear their avon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:34; YESHAYAH 53:6"> For I have laid upon thee the shanim of their avon (iniquity), according to the mispar (number) of the yamim, three hundred and ninety days; so shalt thou bear the avon Bais Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:34; DANIEL 12:11–12; DANIEL 9:24–26"> And when thou hast accomplished them, lie again on thy right side, and thou shalt bear the avon Bais Yehudah forty days; I have appointed thee a day for each year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YESHAYAH 52:10; YECHEZKEL 21:2; YECHEZKEL 6:2"> Therefore thou shalt set thy face toward the Matzor Yerushalayim (Siege of Jerusalem), and thine zero'a shall be bared, and thou shalt prophesy against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 3:25"> And, hinei, I will tie ropes upon thee, and thou shalt not turn thee from one side to another, till thou hast ended the days of thy matzor (siege).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 4:13; YESHAYAH 28:25; YECHEZKEL 4:16"> Take thou also unto thee chittin (wheat), and se'orim (barley), and fol (beans), and adosim (lentils), and dochan (millet), and kussemim (spelt), and put them in keli echad, and make thee lechem thereof, according to the mispar of the yamim that thou shalt lie upon thy side, three hundred and ninety days shalt thou eat thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:26; YECHEZKEL 45:12; DEVARIM 28:51–68"> And thy okhel (food) which thou shalt eat shall be by weight, twenty shekels a day; from time to time shalt thou eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 5:13; YOCHANAN 3:34; YECHEZKEL 4:16"> Thou shalt drink also mayim by measure, the sixth part of a hin: from time to time shalt thou drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 36:12; BERESHIS 18:6"> And thou shalt eat it as cakes of se'orim, and thou shalt bake it with dung that cometh out of ha'adam, in their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DANIEL 1:8; HOSHEA 9:3–4"> And Hashem said, Even thus shall the Bnei Yisroel eat their lechem tameh among the Goyim, whither I will drive them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:14; YECHEZKEL 20:49; YECHEZKEL 9:8"> Then said I, Not so Adonoi Hashem! Hinei, my nefesh hath not been made tuma'ah for from my youth up even till now have I not eaten nevelah or trefah; neither came there basar piggul into my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then He said unto me, Behold, I have given thee cattle dung instead of dung of adam, and thou shalt prepare thy lechem therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 5:16; YESHAYAH 3:1; VAYIKRA 26:26"> Moreover He said unto me, Ben Adam, hinei, I will break the mateh lechem (staff of bread, food supply) in Yerushalayim: and they shall eat lechem weighted out in rations, and with worry; and they shall drink mayim by measure, and with amazed alarm:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 24:23; VAYIKRA 26:39; YECHEZKEL 33:10"> In order that lechem and mayim be scarce, and that every ish and achiv be confounded with shock and rot away because of their avon.</span>
