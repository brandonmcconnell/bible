<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Davar Hashem also came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MARKOS 4:12; YIRMEYAH 5:21; TEHILLIM 78:40"> Ben adam, thou dwellest in the midst of a bais hameri (rebellious house) which have eynayim to see, and see not; they have oznayim to hear, and hear not; for they are a bais meri (house of rebellion).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 36:3; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:25; YIRMEYAH 26:3"> Therefore, thou ben adam, pack for the Golus thy belongings and go into captivity by day before their eyes; and thou shalt remove from thy makom (place, home) to another makom in their sight; it may be they will consider, though they be bais meri (a rebellious house).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 39:4; YECHEZKEL 12:12; MELACHIM BAIS 25:4"> Then shalt thou bring forth thy gear by day in their sight, as gear for the Golus; and thou shalt go forth at erev in their sight, as they that go forth into the Golus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 25:4; YIRMEYAH 39:2–4"> Dig thou through the kir (wall) in their sight, and carry out &#91;everything&#93; thereby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 4:3; YESHAYAH 8:18; YECHEZKEL 24:24"> In their sight shalt thou bear it upon thy katef (shoulder), and carry it forth in the twilight; thou shalt cover thy face, that thou see not ha'aretz; for I have set thee for a mofet (sign, symbol, portent) unto Bais Yisroel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See 2Kgs 25:7">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 24:18; YECHEZKEL 37:10; YECHEZKEL 37:7"> And I did so as I was commanded. I brought forth my gear by day, as gear for the Golus, and at erev I dug through the kir (wall) with mine yad; I carried it forth in the twilight, and I bore it upon my katef (shoulder) in their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And in the boker came the Devar Hashem unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 24:19; YECHEZKEL 17:12; YECHEZKEL 20:49"> Ben adam, hath not Bais Yisroel, the bais hameri (the rebellious house), said unto thee, What doest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 9:25; YESHAYAH 13:1; YIRMEYAH 24:8"> Say thou unto them, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem; This massa (burden) concerneth the nasi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e. Zedekiah">b</a></sup>in Yerushalayim, and kol Bais Yisroel that are among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 52:15; YIRMEYAH 15:2; YIRMEYAH 52:28–30"> Say, I am your mofet; just as I have done, so shall it be done unto them: they shall be carried away into the Golus and go into captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 25:4; YIRMEYAH 39:4; YECHEZKEL 12:6"> And the nasi that is among them shall bear &#91;his gear&#93; upon his katef (shoulder) in the twilight, and shall go forth; they shall dig through the kir (wall) to carry out thereby; he shall cover his face, that he see not ha'aretz with the ayin (eye).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See 2Kgs 25:7">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 39:7; HOSHEA 7:12; YECHEZKEL 17:20"> My net also will I spread over him, and he shall be caught in My snare; and I will bring him to Bavel to Eretz Kasdim (Chaldeans); yet shall he not see it, though he shall die there.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See 2Kgs 25:7">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 5:2; MELACHIM BAIS 25:4–5; YECHEZKEL 17:21"> And I will scatter toward every ruach (wind) all that are about him to help him, and all his troops; and I will draw out the cherev after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 6:7; YECHEZKEL 12:16; YECHEZKEL 12:20"> And they shall know that I am Hashem, when I shall scatter them among the Goyim, and disperse them throughout the countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 22:8–9; YECHEZKEL 14:22–23; YECHEZKEL 6:8–10"> But I will leave a few anashim of them from the cherev, from the ra'av (famine), and from the dever; that they may declare all their to'avot among the Goyim to where they come; and they shall know that I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Moreover the Devar Hashem came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EKHAH 5:9; TEHILLIM 80:5; IYOV 3:24"> Ben adam, eat thy lechem with fearful shaking, and drink thy mayim with trembling and with anxiety;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 7:14; YECHEZKEL 6:6–7; YECHEZKEL 6:14"> And say unto the am ha'aretz: Thus saith Adonoi Hashem regarding the inhabitants of Yerushalayim, and of Admat Yisroel (Land of Israel); they shall eat their lechem with anxiety, and, as desolate, drink their mayim, because her land shall be desolate from all that is therein, because of the chamas of all them that dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 4:7; YESHAYAH 7:23–24; YIRMEYAH 25:9"> And the towns that are inhabited shall be laid waste, and ha'aretz shall be desolate; and ye shall know that I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the Devar Hashem came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:2–3; YECHEZKEL 11:3; YECHEZKEL 12:27"> Ben adam, what is that mashal that ye have in Admat Yisroel, saying, The days drag on, and every chazon (vision) faileth &#91;to come to anything&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YOEL 2:1; YECHEZKEL 7:5–7; TZEFANYAH 1:14"> Tell them therefore, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: I will make this mashal to cease, and they shall no more use it as a mashal in Yisroel; but say unto them, The days are at hand, even the fulfillment of kol chazon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 14:13–16; YECHEZKEL 13:23; ZECHARYAH 13:2–4"> For there shall be no more any chazon shav (vain vision) nor smooth divination within Bais Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="CHABAKUK 1:5; YECHEZKEL 12:28; YESHAYAH 55:11"> For I am Hashem: I will speak, and the davar that I shall speak shall come to pass; it shall be no more delayed; for in your days, O Bais Hameri (O Rebellious House), will I say the Davar (Word), and will fulfill it, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Again the Devar Hashem came to me, saying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DANIEL 10:14; YECHEZKEL 12:22; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:4"> Ben adam, hinei, they of Bais Yisroel say, The chazon that he seeth is for yamim rabbim to come, and he prophesieth of the times that are far off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 12:23–25; MARKOS 13:32–37; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:2–3"> Therefore say unto them, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem; There shall none of My Devarim be delayed any more, but the Davar which I have spoken shall be done, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
