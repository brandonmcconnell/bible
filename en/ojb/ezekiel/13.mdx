<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Devar Hashem came unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 13:17; YIRMEYAH 37:19; YECHEZKEL 22:28"> Ben adam, prophesy against the nevi'im of Yisroel that prophesy, and say thou unto them that prophesy out of their own levavot, Hear ye the Devar Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EKHAH 2:14; YIRMEYAH 23:28–32; HOSHEA 9:7"> Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Hoy (woe, doom) unto the foolish nevi'im, that follow their own ruach, and have seen nothing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:20; MICHOH 2:11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:9–10"> O Yisroel, thy nevi'im are like the jackals among ruins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 22:30; TEHILLIM 106:23; YESHAYAH 58:12"> Ye have not gone up into the peratzot (breaches), neither repaired the wall for Bais Yisroel to stand firm in the milchamah (battle) in the Yom Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 29:8; YECHEZKEL 22:28; YIRMEYAH 28:15"> They have seen shav (vanity) and kesem kazav (lying divination), saying, Hashem saith; and Hashem hath not sent them; yet they hope that their davar will be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 13:6; MATTITYAHU 24:23–24; YECHEZKEL 13:2–3"> Have ye not seen a vain vision, and have ye not spoken a kesem kazav (lying divination), whereas ye say, Hashem saith it; albeit I have not spoken?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 5:8; YECHEZKEL 21:3; YECHEZKEL 28:22"> Therefore thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Because ye have spoken shav (vanity, futility) and seen kazav (lie), therefore, hineni, I am against you, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 87:6; TEHILLIM 69:28; YECHEZKEL 20:38"> And Mine yad shall be against the nevi'im that see shav (vanity, futility), and that divine kazav (lie): they shall not be in the sod (council) of My people, neither shall they be written in the Ketav (Register) of Bais Yisroel, neither shall they enter into Admat Yisroel; and ye shall know that I am Adonoi Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 22:28; YECHEZKEL 13:16; YIRMEYAH 6:14"> Because, even because they have seduced My people to lead them astray, saying, Shalom; v'ein shalom; and one built up a chayitz (wall, partition, temporary wall), and, hinei, others plastered it with &#91;dissolving&#93; mud-plaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 38:22; YESHAYAH 28:2; NACHUM 1:7–8"> Say unto them that plaster it with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="dissolving, useless">a</a></sup>mud-plaster that it shall fall: there shall be an overflowing geshem; and great hailstones shall fall; and a ruach se'arot (storm wind) shall burst forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 3:13; EKHAH 2:14–15; SHOFETIM 9:38"> Hinei, when the kir (wall) is fallen, shall it not be said unto you, Where is the mud-plaster wherewith ye have daubed it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YESHAYAH 30:30; HISGALUS 16:21; TEHILLIM 148:8"> Therefore thus saith Adonoi Hashem; I will even cause a ruach se'arot (storm wind) in My wrath; and there shall be an overflowing geshem in Mine anger, and great hailstones in My fury to destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MICHOH 1:6; YIRMEYAH 14:15; YIRMEYAH 6:15"> So will I break down the kir (wall) that ye have plastered with mud-plaster, and bring it down to the ground, so that the yesod (foundation) thereof shall be laid bare, and it shall fall, and ye shall be destroyed in the midst thereof; and ye shall know that I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YESHAYAH 30:13; TEHILLIM 62:3; NECHEMYAH 4:3"> Thus will I spend My chemah (wrath) upon the kir (wall), and upon them that have plastered it with mud-plaster, and will say unto you, The kir (wall) is no more, neither they that mud-plastered it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 13:10; YIRMEYAH 6:14; YIRMEYAH 8:11"> Namely, the nevi'im of Yisroel which prophesy concerning Yerushalayim, and which see chazon shalom for her, v'ein shalom, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 13:2; MELACHIM BAIS 22:14; SHOFETIM 4:4"> Likewise, thou ben adam, set thy face against the banot amecha (daughters of your people), which prophesy out of their own lev; and prophesy thou against them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:14; YECHEZKEL 13:20; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:3"> And say, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Hoy (woe, doom) to the seamstresses of magic charms on wrists, who make mispachot (amulets) for the rosh of every size to hunt nefashot! Will ye hunt the nefashot of My people, and yet save your own nefashot alive?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MICHOH 3:5; MISHLE 28:21; YIRMEYAH 23:17"> And will ye profane Me among My people for handfuls of se'orim and for pieces of lechem, to slay the nefashot that should not die, and to save the nefashot alive that should not live, by your kazav (lying) to My people that hear your kazav?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 13:18; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:8–9; YECHEZKEL 13:8–9"> Therefore thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Hineni, I am against your magic charms, wherewith ye there hunt to ensnare the nefashot like birds, and I will tear them from your arms, and will let the nefashot go, even the nefashot that ye hunt like birds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="TEHILLIM 91:3"> Your mispachot (amulets) also will I tear off, and I will save My people out of your yad, and they shall be no more in your yad (hand, power) to be hunted; and ye shall know that I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 23:14; YIRMEYAH 14:13–17; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:18–19"> Because with sheker (falsehood) ye have made the lev tzaddik sad, to whom I have not brought sadness; and strengthened the hands of the rasha, that he should not turn from his derech harah to save his life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 13:21; YECHEZKEL 12:24; MICHOH 3:6"> Therefore ye shall see no longer shav (vanity, futility), nor practice divinations; for I will save My people out of your yad; and ye shall know that I am Hashem.</span>
</div>
