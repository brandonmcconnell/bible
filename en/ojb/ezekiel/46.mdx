<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YESHAYAH 66:23; YECHEZKEL 44:1–2; SHEMOT 20:9"> Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: The Sha'ar HeKhatzer HaPenimit that looketh toward the east shall be shut during the sheshet yemei hama'aseh (the six days of labor); but on Shabbos it shall be opened, and in the yom of the Rosh Chodesh it shall be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 44:3; YECHEZKEL 46:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:10–12"> And the Nasi shall enter by the derech ulam haSha'ar from without, and shall stand by the mezuzat HaSha'ar, and the Kohanim shall prepare his Olah (burnt offering) and his Shelamim, and he shall worship at the threshold of the Sha'ar; then he shall go out; but the Sha'ar shall not be shut until the erev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKAS 1:10; YOCHANAN 10:9; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:19–22"> Likewise the Am HaAretz shall worship at the Petach HaSha'ar before Hashem in the Shabbatot and in Rosh Chodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 45:17; BAMIDBAR 28:9–10"> And the Olah that the Nasi shall offer unto Hashem in the Yom HaShabbos shall be six kevasim temimim, and a ram without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 45:24; YECHEZKEL 46:7; DEVARIM 16:17"> And the minchah shall be an ephah for a ram, and the minchah for the kevasim whatever his hand gives, and a hin of shemen to an ephah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 46:1"> And in the yom of the Rosh Chodesh it shall be a young bull temimim, and six kevasim, and a ram; they shall be without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 46:5"> And he shall prepare a minchah, an ephah for a bull, and an ephah for a ram, and for the kevasim as much as he wishes, and a hin of shemen to an ephah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 46:2; YECHEZKEL 44:1–3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:18"> And when the Nasi shall enter, he shall go in by the derech ulam HaSha'ar and he shall go out by the derech thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:16; SHEMOT 23:14–17; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20–21"> But when the Am HaAretz shall come before Hashem in the Mo'adim, he that entereth in by the derech Sha'ar Tzafon to worship shall exit by the derech Sha'ar Negev; and he that entereth by the derech Sha'ar Negev shall go out by derech Sha'ar Tzafonah; he shall not return by the derech HaSha'ar whereby he came in, but shall go out opposite it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 42:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:22"> And the Nasi in the midst of them, when they go in, shall go in; and when they go out, shall go out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 46:5; YECHEZKEL 46:7; BAMIDBAR 15:1–41"> And in the Chagim and in the Mo'adim the minchah shall be an ephah to a bull, and an ephah to a ram, and to the kevasim as much as one wishes to give, and a hin of shemen to an ephah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 46:1–2; YECHEZKEL 45:17; YECHEZKEL 44:3"> Now when the Nasi shall prepare a nedavah olah or shelamim nedavah unto Hashem, one shall then open for him the Sha'ar that looketh toward the east, and he shall prepare his Olah and his Shelamim, as he did on the Yom HaShabbos; then he shall go out; and after his going out one shall shut the Sha'ar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YESHAYAH 50:4; YOCHANAN 1:29; SHEMOT 12:5"> Thou shalt daily prepare an Olah unto Hashem of a keves of the first year tamim (without blemish); thou shalt prepare it baboker baboker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 28:5"> And thou shalt prepare a Minchah for it baboker baboker, the sixth part of an ephah, and the third part of a hin of shemen, to moisten with the fine flour; a minchah chukkot olam tamid unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:1–10; BAMIDBAR 28:6; SHEMOT 29:42"> Thus shall they prepare the keves, and the minchah, and the shemen, baboker baboker for an olat tamid.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 21:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:7; BERESHIS 25:5–6"> Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: If the Nasi give a mattanah (gift) unto any of his banim, the nachalah thereof shall belong to his banim; it shall be their achuzzah by nachalah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:10; LUKAS 19:25–26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:30–31"> But if he give a mattanah of his nachalah to one of his avadim, then it shall be his to the Shenat HaDeror (Jubilee Year); then it shall return to the Nasi; but his nachalah shall belong only to his banim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Isa 53:10">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 45:8; MELACHIM ALEF 21:19; YECHEZKEL 22:27"> Moreover the Nasi shall not take of the nachalah HaAm by oppression, to thrust them out of their achuzzah; but he shall give his banim nachalah out of his own achuzzah; that My people be not scattered each man from his achuzzah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 42:9; YECHEZKEL 44:4–5; YECHEZKEL 40:44–46"> After he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="the angelic being">b</a></sup>brought me through the entrance, which was at the side of the Sha'ar, into the lishkhot hakodesh of the Kohanim, which looked toward the tzafonah; and, hinei, there was a place at its western sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:13; YECHEZKEL 44:29; YECHEZKEL 44:19"> Then said he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="the angelic being">c</a></sup>unto me, This is the place where the Kohanim shall cook the asham (trespass offering) and the chattat, where they shall bake the minchah; that they bear them not out into the Khatzer HaKhitzonah and so set apart as kodesh HaAm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="the angelic being">d</a></sup>brought me forth into the Khatzer HaKhitzonah, and caused me to pass by the four corners of the Khatzer; and, hinei, in the Khatzer I saw in every corner of the Khatzer there was a Khatzer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> In the four corners of the Khatzer there were enclosed Khatzerot forty cubits long and thirty wide; these four corners were of one measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And there was a row of masonry saviv (around) inside them, round about these four, and it was made with hearths at the bottom of the rows round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 46:20; YOCHANAN 21:15–17; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:2"> Then said he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="the angelic being">e</a></sup>unto me, These are the Bais HaMevashelim, where the Mesharetim of the Bais shall cook the zevach HaAm.</span>
</div>
