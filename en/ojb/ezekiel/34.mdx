<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Devar Hashem came unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 23:1; YIRMEYAH 3:15; YESHAYAH 40:11"> Ben Adam, prophesy against the ro'im (shepherds) of Yisroel, prophesy, and say unto them, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem unto the ro'im (shepherds); Oy to the ro'im (shepherds) of Yisroel who are ro'im tending to themselves! Should not the ro'im (shepherds) be ro'im tending to the tzon (flock)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 11:16; YIRMEYAH 2:30; YECHEZKEL 33:25–26"> Ye eat the chelev, and ye clothe you with the tzemer (wool), ye slaughter the healthy; but ye are not ro'im that tend the tzon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:12–13; MATTITYAHU 9:36; YECHEZKEL 34:16"> The weak have ye not strengthened, neither have ye healed that which was cholah (sick), neither have ye bound up the nishberet (injured ones), neither have ye brought back that which was straying, neither have ye sought haovedet (the lost); but with chazekah (force) and with perek (harshness, severity) have ye ruled them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:36; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 18:16; YIRMEYAH 23:2"> And they were scattered, because there is no ro'eh; and they became okhel (food) to kol chayyat hasadeh, when they were scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 142:4; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:25; YOCHANAN 10:16"> My tzon wandered through all the harim, and upon every high givah (hill); yes, My tzon was scattered upon all the face of ha'aretz, and none did search or seek after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 22:2–3; MICHOH 3:8–9; TEHILLIM 82:1–7"> Therefore, ye ro'im, hear the Devar Hashem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:33; YECHEZKEL 34:5–6"> As I live, saith Adonoi Hashem, surely because My tzon became a prey, and My tzon became okhel to kol chayyat hasadeh, because there was no ro'eh, neither did My ro'im (shepherds) search for My tzon, but the ro'im (shepherds) were ro'im tending to themselves, and tended not My tzon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Therefore, O ye ro'im, hear the Devar Hashem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 34:2; ZECHARYAH 10:3; YECHEZKEL 13:8"> Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Hineni, I am against the ro'im; and I will require an accounting for My tzon at their yad, and cause them to cease from being ro'im to the tzon; neither shall the ro'im (shepherds) be ro'im tending to themselves any more; for I will save My tzon from their mouth, that they may not be okhel for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:16; LUKAS 19:10; YIRMEYAH 23:3"> For thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Hineni, I, even I, will both search for My tzon, and seek after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:11; YOCHANAN 10:11–12; LUKAS 19:10"> As a ro'eh seeketh after his tzon in the day that he is among his tzon that are scattered; so will I seek after My tzon, and will save them out of kol hamekomot (all the places) where they have been scattered there in the yom anan va'arafel (day of clouds and thick darkness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 28:25–26; YECHEZKEL 11:17; YESHAYAH 11:11–16"> And I will bring them out from HaAmim, and gather them from the aratzot (countries), and will bring them to their own adamah, and I will tend them as Ro'eh upon the harim of Yisroel by the ravines, and in all the moshevei HaAretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:9; YIRMEYAH 31:25; TEHILLIM 31:8–10"> I will give them feeding of a roeh in a mireh tov (good pasture) and upon the high harim of Yisroel shall their naveh (fold, abode of sheep) be; there shall they lie in a naveh tov, and in a mireh shamen (fat pasture) shall they feed upon the harim of Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 3:15; TEHILLIM 23:1–2; YOCHANAN 21:15"> As a ro'eh will I tend My tzon, and I will cause them to lie down, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 19:10; YECHEZKEL 34:4; YECHEZKEL 34:11"> I will seek haovedet (that which was lost), and bring back that which was straying, and will bind up the nishberet (that which was injured), and will strengthen the cholah (sick); but I will destroy the shemenah (stout, sleek) and the chazakah; I will tend to them with mishpat (justice).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 34:20–22; MATTITYAHU 25:32–33; ZECHARYAH 10:3"> And as for you, O My tzon, thus saith Adonoi Hashem; Hineni, I judge between seh and seh, between the rams and the goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:13; LUKAS 11:52; BAMIDBAR 16:13"> Seemeth it a small thing unto you to have eaten up the mireh hatov (good pasture), that ye must also trample down with your raglayim what is left of your mirim (pastures)? And to have drunk of the clear mayim, but ye must also foul the rest with your raglayim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And as for My tzon, they feed on that which ye have trampled with your raglayim; and they drink that which ye have fouled with your raglayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:31–46; YECHEZKEL 34:10; TEHILLIM 22:12–16"> Therefore thus saith Adonoi Hashem unto them; Hineni, I, even I, will act as shofet between the seh biryah (fat lamb) and between the seh razah (emaciated lamb).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 11:5; DEVARIM 33:17; LUKAS 13:14–16"> Because ye have thrust with flank and with shoulder, and butted all the weak emaciated ones with your karnayim, till ye have scattered them abroad;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 23:2–3; TEHILLIM 72:12–14; YECHEZKEL 34:10"> Therefore will I save My tzon, and they shall no more be a prey; and I will act as shofet between seh and seh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 37:24–25; YIRMEYAH 30:9; YESHAYAH 40:11"> And I will set up Ro'eh Echad over them, He shall tend them as ro'eh, even Avdi Dovid; He shall feed them. He shall be their Ro'eh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:33; YECHEZKEL 36:28; YIRMEYAH 23:5–6"> And I Hashem will be their Elohim, and Avdi Dovid<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Moshiach">a</a></sup>a Nasi among them; I Hashem have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YESHAYAH 11:6–9; YECHEZKEL 37:26; VAYIKRA 26:6"> And I will make with them a Brit Shalom, and will cause the chayyah ra'ah to cease out of HaAretz; and they shall dwell safely in the midbar, and sleep in the ye'arot (forests).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:12; YESHAYAH 44:3; VAYIKRA 26:4"> And I will make them and the places surrounding My givah (hill) a brocha; and I will cause the geshem to come down in its season; there shall be gishmei brocha (showers of blessing).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 30:8; VAYIKRA 26:4; VAYIKRA 26:13"> And the etz hasadeh shall yield her p'ri, and ha'aretz shall yield her increase, and they shall be safe in their adamah, and shall know that I am Hashem, when I have broken the bars of their ol (yoke), and saved them out of the yad haovedim (hand, power of enslavers) of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 46:27; YIRMEYAH 30:10; YECHEZKEL 39:26"> And they shall no more be a prey to the Goyim, neither shall the chayyat HaAretz devour them; but they shall dwell safely, and none shall make them afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 36:29; YESHAYAH 4:2; YECHEZKEL 34:26–27"> And I will raise up for them a planting of renown, and they shall be no more consumed with ra'av in HaAretz, neither bear the shame of the Goyim any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 37:27; YECHEZKEL 34:24; TEHILLIM 46:11"> Thus shall they know that I Hashem Eloheihem am with them, and that they, even Bais Yisroel, are My people, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="TEHILLIM 100:3; YOCHANAN 10:16; TEHILLIM 78:52"> And ye My tzon, the tzon of My mireh, are adam, and I am Eloheichem, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
