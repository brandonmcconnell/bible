<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 40:2–3; YECHEZKEL 40:17; YECHEZKEL 41:23"> Afterward he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the angelic being">a</a></sup>brought me to the Heikhal, and measured the jambs, six cubits wide on the one side, and six cubits wide on the other side, which was the width of the Ohel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 6:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 3:3; MELACHIM ALEF 6:2"> And the width of the Petach (Entrance) was ten cubits; and the ketefot (shoulders, sidewalls) of the Petach were five cubits on the one side, and five cubits on the other side; and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="the angelic being">b</a></sup>measured the depth thereof, forty cubits; and the width, twenty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 40:16"> Then he went into the Penimah (the Innermost ),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Holy of Holies">c</a></sup>and measured the jamb of the Petach, two cubits; and the Petach, six cubits; and the width of the Petach, sheva (seven) cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 6:20; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:3–8; SHEMOT 26:33–34"> So he measured the depth thereof, twenty cubits; and the width, twenty cubits, along the Heikhal; and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="the angelic being">d</a></sup>said unto me, This is the Kodesh HaKodashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 6:5–6; YECHEZKEL 42:3–14; YECHEZKEL 41:6–9"> After he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="the angelic being">e</a></sup>measured the wall of the Beis,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="HaMikdash">f</a></sup>six cubits; and the width of every tzela (side chamber, cell), four cubits, around the Beis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="HaMikdash">g</a></sup>on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 6:10; MELACHIM ALEF 6:5–6; YECHEZKEL 40:17"> And the tzela'ot (side chambers, cells) were tzela over tzela, thirty-three in number; and there were ledges on the wall which was of the Beis for the tzela'ot round about, that they might take hold there, but they had not hold in the wall of the Beis.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="HaMikdash">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 6:8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:1; MATTITYAHU 13:32"> And tzela'ot (side chambers, cells) were broader at each story; a ramp spiraled upward by stages around the Beis;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="HaMikdash">i</a></sup>therefore the width of the Beis increased as it went upward, so from the lowest to the highest through the middle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 40:5; HISGALUS 21:16"> I saw also the height of the Beis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="HaMikdash">j</a></sup>all around; the yesodot (foundations) of the tzela'ot were a full rod of six cubits long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 41:11; YECHEZKEL 42:4; YECHEZKEL 42:1"> The thickness of the wall, which was for the tzela toward the outside, was five cubits; and that which was open was the space at the end of the tzela'ot of the Beis.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="HaMikdash">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 40:17"> And between the leshakhot was the space the width of twenty cubits around the Beis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="HaMikdash">l</a></sup>all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 41:9; YECHEZKEL 42:4"> And the petach of the tzela were toward the space that was open, one petach toward the north, and another petach toward the south; and the width of the place that was open was five cubits all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 42:1; YECHEZKEL 42:13; YECHEZKEL 41:13–15"> Now the Binyan (building, structure) that was before the Gizrah (separation, separating courtyard, restricted space, behind the Beis HaMikdash) at the end toward the west was seventy cubits wide; and the wall of the Binyan was five cubits thick all around, and the length thereof ninety cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 40:47"> So he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="the angelic being">m</a></sup>measured the Beis,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="HaMikdash">n</a></sup>a hundred cubits long; and the Gizrah, and the Binyan, with the walls thereof, a hundred cubits long;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Also the width of the face of the Beis,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="HaMikdash">o</a></sup>and of the Gizrah toward the east, a hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 42:3; YECHEZKEL 42:1; YECHEZKEL 41:12"> And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="the angelic being">p</a></sup>measured the length of the Binyan along the front of the Gizrah which was behind it, and the atikeha (corner structures) thereof on the one side and on the other side, a hundred cubits, with the Heikhal Hapenimi, and the ulam vestibules of the Khatzer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 6:4; YECHEZKEL 40:25; YECHEZKEL 40:16"> The doorposts, and the narrow chalonim, and the atikim balconies around on their three stories, opposite the saf (threshold), paneled with etz all around, and from the ground up to the chalonot, and the chalonot were covered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 42:15"> To that above the Petach, even unto the Beis HaPenimi, and at the outside, and by the wall all around penimi (inner) and khitzon (outer), by measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:36; YECHEZKEL 40:16; YECHEZKEL 40:22"> And it was carved with keruvim and timorim, so that a palm tree was between a keruv and a keruv; and every keruv had two faces;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 10:14; YECHEZKEL 1:10"> So that the face of an adam was toward the palm tree on the one side, and the face of a young lion toward the palm tree on the other side; it was carved throughout kol HaBeis all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 41:18"> From the ground unto above the Petach were keruvim and timorim carved, and on the wall of the Heikhal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 41:1; MELACHIM ALEF 6:33; YECHEZKEL 40:14"> The mezuzas of the Heikhal were squared; as for the front of the Kodesh &#91;HaKodashim&#93;, the appearance of the one &#91;mezuzah&#93; was like the appearance of the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MALACHI 1:12; YECHEZKEL 23:41; MALACHI 1:7"> The Mizbe'ach was of etz and was three cubits high, and the length thereof two cubits; and the corners thereof, and the length thereof, and the sides thereof, were of etz; and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="the angelic being">q</a></sup>said unto me, This is the shulchan that is before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 6:31–35; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:22; YECHEZKEL 41:1"> And the Heikhal and the Kodesh had two delatot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 6:34; YECHEZKEL 40:48"> And the delatot had two doors apiece, two turning doors; two delatot for the one door, and two delatot for the other door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 41:18"> And there were carved on them, on the delatot of the Heikhal, keruvim and timorim, like those carved upon the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="interior">r</a></sup>walls; and there was a beam upon the face of the Ulam at the outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 41:16; YECHEZKEL 40:16; YECHEZKEL 41:5"> And there were narrow chalonim and timorim on the one side and on the other side, on the sides of the Ulam, and upon the tzela'ot of the Beis,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="HaMikdash">s</a></sup>and thick beams.</span>
