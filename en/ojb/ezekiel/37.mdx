<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 3:22; YECHEZKEL 1:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:39"> The Yad Hashem was upon me, and carried me out in the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hakodesh">a</a></sup>of Hashem, and set me down in the midst of the valley which was full of atzmot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="TEHILLIM 141:7; YECHEZKEL 37:11; DEVARIM 11:30"> And caused me to pass by them round about; and, hinei, there were rabbot me'od in the open valley; and, hinei, they were very dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:25–26; YOCHANAN 5:21; DEVARIM 32:39"> And He said unto me, Ben Adam, can these atzmot live? And I answered, Adonoi Hashem, Thou knowest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:25; YOCHANAN 5:28–29; MATTITYAHU 21:21"> Again He said unto me, Prophesy upon these atzmot, and say unto them, O ye atzmot hayeveshot, hear the Devar Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 37:14; TEHILLIM 104:29–30; BERESHIS 2:7"> Thus saith Adonoi Hashem unto these atzmot: Hinei, I will cause ruach to enter into you, and ye shall live;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOEL 2:27; YESHAYAH 49:23; YOEL 3:17"> And I will lay gidim (sinews, tendons) upon you, and will bring up basar upon you, and cover you with ohr (skin), and put ruach in you, and ye shall live; and ye shall have da'as that I am Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:2; MELACHIM ALEF 19:11–13; YIRMEYAH 13:5–7"> So I prophesied as I was commanded; and as I prophesied, there was a noise, and hinei a rattling, and the atzmot came together, etzem to its etzem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And when I beheld, hinei, the gidim and the basar came up upon them, and the ohr covered them above; but there was no ruach in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:8; SHIR HASHIRIM 4:16; TEHILLIM 104:30"> Then said He unto me, Prophesy unto the ruach; prophesy, Ben Adam, and say to the ruach, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Come from the arba ruchot, O ruach, and breathe upon these slain, that they may live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HISGALUS 11:11; TEHILLIM 104:30; HISGALUS 20:4–5"> So I prophesied as He commanded me, and the ruach came into them, and they lived, and stood up upon their raglayim, a chayil gadol me'od me'od.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:1; TEHILLIM 77:7–9; YECHEZKEL 39:25"> Then He said unto me, Ben Adam, these atzmot are the kol Bais Yisroel. Hinei, they say, Our atzmot are dried up, and tikvateinu (our hope) is gone; nigzarnu lanu (we are cut off).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 26:19; HOSHEA 13:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:16"> Therefore prophesy and say unto them, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Hinei, O My people, I will open your keverot, and cause you to come up out of your keverot, and bring you into Admat Yisroel (the Land of Israel).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 37:6; YECHEZKEL 16:62; TEHILLIM 126:2–3"> And ye shall know that I am Hashem, when I have opened your keverot, O My people, and brought you up out of your keverot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 36:27; YOEL 2:28–29; YECHEZKEL 36:36"> And shall put My Ruach in you, and ye shall live, and I shall place you in your own land; then shall ye know that I Hashem have spoken it, and performed it, saith Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The Devar Hashem came again unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 10:19; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 10:17"> Moreover, thou ben adam, take thee Etz Echad, and write upon it, For Yehudah, and for the Bnei Yisroel his chaverim; then take another Etz Echad, and write upon it, For Yosef, the Etz Ephrayim and for kol Bais Yisroel his chaverim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HOSHEA 1:11; YIRMEYAH 50:4; YESHAYAH 11:13"> And join them one to another into Etz Echad; and they shall become achadim (one) in thine yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 24:19; YECHEZKEL 12:9; YECHEZKEL 17:12"> And when the Bnei Amecha speak unto thee, saying, Wilt thou not show us what thou meanest by these?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 10:6; YECHEZKEL 37:16–17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:13–14"> Say unto them, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Hinei, I will take the Etz Yosef, which is in the Yad Ephrayim, and the Shivtei Yisroel his chaverim, and will put them with him, even with the Etz Yehudah, and make them Etz Echad, and they shall be Echad in Mine Yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 12:3; BAMIDBAR 17:6–9; HOSHEA 12:10"> And the Etzim whereon thou writest shall be in thine yad before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 36:24; YIRMEYAH 29:14; YIRMEYAH 23:8"> And say unto them, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Hinei, I will take the Bnei Yisroel from among the Goyim, whither they be gone, and will gather them on every side, and bring them into their own land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 37:24; YECHEZKEL 34:23–24; YIRMEYAH 3:18"> And I will make them Goy Echad in the land upon the mountains of Yisroel; and Melech Echad shall be Melech to them all; and they shall be no more two Goyim, neither shall they be divided into two Mamlachot any more at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:3–4; YECHEZKEL 36:28–29; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9"> Neither shall they make themselves tameh any more with their gillulim, nor with their shikkutzim, nor with any of their peysha'im; but I will save them out of all their moshavot wherein they have sinned, and will make them tahor; so shall they be My people, and I will be their Elohim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 34:23–24; HOSHEA 3:5; YECHEZKEL 37:25"> And Avdi Dovid<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Moshiach">b</a></sup>shall be Melech over them; and they all shall have Ro'eh Echad; they shall also walk in My mishpatim, and observe My chukkot, and do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="AMOS 9:15; YECHEZKEL 37:24; YECHEZKEL 28:25"> And they shall dwell in HaAretz that I have given unto Avdi Ya'akov, wherein your Avot have dwelt; and they shall dwell therein, even they, and their banim, and their bnei banim ad olam; and Dovid Avdi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Moshiach">c</a></sup>shall be their Nasi l'olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YESHAYAH 55:3; YIRMEYAH 30:19; YECHEZKEL 34:25"> Moreover I will make a Brit Shalom with them; it shall be a Brit Olam with them; and I will establish them, and multiply them, and will set My Mikdash in the midst of them l'olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:3; YOCHANAN 1:14; YECHEZKEL 37:23"> My Mishkan also shall be with them; yes, I will be their Elohim, and they shall be My people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YOCHANAN 17:17–19; YECHEZKEL 20:12; SHEMOT 31:13"> And the Goyim shall know that I Hashem set apart as kodesh Yisroel, when My Mikdash shall be in the midst of them l'olam (forever).</span>
</div>
