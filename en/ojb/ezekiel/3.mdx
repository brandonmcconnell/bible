<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HISGALUS 10:9–10; YECHEZKEL 3:17–21; YECHEZKEL 2:8–9"> Moreover He said unto me, Ben Adam, eat that thou findest; eat this megillah, and go speak unto Bais Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 25:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:19"> So I opened my mouth, and He caused me to eat that megillah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 15:16; HISGALUS 10:9–10; TEHILLIM 119:103"> And He said unto me, Ben Adam, cause thy beten to eat, and fill thy bowels with this megillah that I give thee. Then did I eat it; and it was in my mouth as devash for sweetness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:5–6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:8; YECHEZKEL 2:3"> And He said unto me, Ben Adam, go, get thee unto the Bais Yisroel, and speak My words unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YONAH 1:2; YESHAYAH 33:19; YONAH 3:2–4"> For thou art not sent to an Am Imkei Safah (a people of indecipherable language) and of obscure lashon, but to Bais Yisroel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:20–24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:30–33; YONAH 3:5–10"> Not to amim rabbim of indecipherable language and obscure lashon whose words thou canst not understand. Surely, had I sent thee to them, they would have paid heed unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKAS 10:16; YECHEZKEL 2:4; SHMUEL ALEF 8:7"> But Bais Yisroel will not pay heed unto thee; for they will not pay heed unto Me; for kol Bais Yisroel are khizkei metzach (hard-set of brow) and keshei-lev (hard-hearted).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 1:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:51–56; MICHOH 3:8"> Hinei, I have made thy face chazakim against their faces, and thy metzach chazak (forehead strong) against their metzach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 2:6; YIRMEYAH 1:8; YESHAYAH 50:7"> As a shamir (adamant, hardest stone) more chazak than flint have I made thy metzach (forehead); fear them not, neither be terrified at their looks, though they be a bais meri (house of rebellion).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:11; IYOV 22:22; YECHEZKEL 3:1–3"> Moreover He said unto me, Ben Adam, all My words that I shall speak unto thee receive in thine lev, and hear with thine oznayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 2:7; YECHEZKEL 3:27; YECHEZKEL 33:30"> And go, get thee to them of the Golus, unto the Bnei Amecha, and speak unto them, and tell them, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem; whether they will hear, or whether they will refrain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 8:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:39; YECHEZKEL 11:1"> Then the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hakodesh">a</a></sup>lifted me and took me up, and I heard behind me a kol ra'ash gadol (a voice of a great rustling), saying, Baruch Kavod Hashem from His makom (place, home).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 1:24; YECHEZKEL 10:16–17; YECHEZKEL 10:5"> I heard also the noise of the wings of the Chayos that touched one another, and the noise of the Ofanim (wheels) opposite them, and a voice of ra'ash gadol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 37:1; YIRMEYAH 20:7–9; MELACHIM BAIS 3:15"> So the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hakodesh">b</a></sup>lifted me up, and took me away, and I went in mar (bitterness), in the anger of my ruach; but the yad Hashem was chazah upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 137:1; IYOV 2:13; BERESHIS 50:10"> Then I came to them of the Golus at Tel-Aviv, that dwelt by the Nahar Kevar, and I sat where they sat, and remained there speechlessly astounded among them shivat yamim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 42:7"> And it came to pass at the end of shivat yamim, that the Devar Hashem came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YESHAYAH 52:8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:10"> Ben Adam, I have made thee a tzofeh (watchman) unto the Bais Yisroel; therefore hear the word at My mouth, and give them warning from Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 33:6; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:19–20; YECHEZKEL 3:20"> When I say unto the rasha, Thou shalt surely die; and thou givest him not warning, nor speakest to warn the rasha from his derech harasha'ah, to save his life; the same rasha shall die in his avon; but I will hold accountable thine yad for his dahm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 33:9; YECHEZKEL 14:20; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:16"> Yet if thou warn the rasha, and he turn not from his resha, nor from his derech harasha'ah, he shall die in his avon; but thou hast saved thy nefesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:24; YECHEZKEL 18:26; YECHEZKEL 33:12–13"> Again, When a tzaddik doth turn from his tzedek, and commit evil, and I lay a michshol (stumbling block of temptation) before him, he shall die; because thou hast not given him warning, he shall die in his chattat, and his tzidkot which he hath worked shall not be remembered; but I will hold accountable thine yad for his dahm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:16; MISHLE 9:9; LETTER TO TITOS 2:15"> Nevertheless if thou warn the tzaddik, that the tzaddik sin not, and he doth not sin, he shall surely live, because he is warned; also thou hast saved thy nefesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:6; YECHEZKEL 37:1; YECHEZKEL 8:4"> And the Yad Hashem (Hand, Power of Hashem) was there upon me; and He said unto me, Arise, go forth into the plain, and I will there speak with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 1:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:55; HISGALUS 1:17"> Then I arose, and went forth into the plain; and, hinei, the Kavod Hashem stood there, as the Kavod which I saw by the Nahar Kevar; I fell on my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 2:2; DANIEL 10:19; DANIEL 10:8–10"> Then the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hakodesh">c</a></sup>entered into me, and set me upon my feet, and spoke with me, and said unto me, Go, shut thyself in the confines of thine bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 4:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:16; MARKOS 3:21"> But thou, O Ben Adam, hinei, they tied on thee ropes, and have bound thee with them, that thou canst not go out among them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 24:27; MICHOH 3:6–7; AMOS 8:11–12"> And I will make thy leshon cleave to the roof of thy mouth, that thou shalt be mute, and shalt not be to them an ish mokhi'ach (admonisher); for they are a bais meri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 12:2–3; YECHEZKEL 24:27; YECHEZKEL 3:11"> But when I speak with thee, I will open thy mouth, and thou shalt say unto them, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem; He that heareth, let him hear; and he that refuseth, let him refuse; for they are a bais meri.</span>
</div>
