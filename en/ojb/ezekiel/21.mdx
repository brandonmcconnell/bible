<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Moreover the Devar Hashem came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:46; YECHEZKEL 4:7; YECHEZKEL 25:2"> Ben adam, set thy face toward the south, and preach against the south, and prophesy against the forest land of the Negev;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="IYOV 9:22; YIRMEYAH 21:13; YECHEZKEL 5:8"> And I say to the forest of the Negev, Hear the Devar Hashem: Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Hineni, I will kindle an eish in thee, and it shall devour every green etz (tree) in thee, and every dry etz (tree); the blazing flame shall not be quenched, and all faces from the Negev to the north shall be scorched by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:47; YECHEZKEL 7:2; YECHEZKEL 6:11–14"> And all basar shall see that I Hashem have kindled it; it shall not be quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 21:30; NACHUM 1:9; SHMUEL ALEF 3:12"> Then said I, Ah Adonoi Hashem! They say of me, Doth he not speak meshalim (parables)?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YESHAYAH 22:4; YECHEZKEL 6:11; YIRMEYAH 19:10"> And the Devar Hashem came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 7:17; YECHEZKEL 7:26; YESHAYAH 13:7"> Ben adam, set thy face toward Yerushalayim, and preach against the mikdashim, and prophesy against Admat Yisroel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And say to Admat Yisroel, Thus saith Hashem: Hineni, I am against thee, will draw forth My cherev out of its sheath, will cut off from thee the tzaddik and the rasha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YESHAYAH 34:5–6; YECHEZKEL 21:28; YECHEZKEL 21:3"> Seeing then that I will cut off from thee the tzaddik and the rasha, therefore shall My cherev go forth out of its sheath against kol basar from the Negev to the tzafon (north);</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 110:5–6; YECHEZKEL 20:47; YECHEZKEL 21:25–27"> That kol basar may know that I Hashem have drawn forth My cherev out of its sheath; it shall not return any more.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 21:19; YIRMEYAH 25:33; YIRMEYAH 51:20–23"> Sigh therefore, thou ben adam, with a breaking heart; and with bitterness groan before their eynayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 21:6; YOEL 1:13; YECHEZKEL 21:14"> And it shall be, when they say unto thee, Why sighest thou? That thou shalt answer, For the news; because it cometh; and every lev shall melt, and all hands shall go limp with fear, and every ruach shall faint, and all birkayim (knees) shall be weak as mayim; hinei, it cometh, and shall be brought to pass, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 21:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:2; YECHEZKEL 21:25"> Again the Devar Hashem came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 24:1; BAMIDBAR 24:10; VAYIKRA 26:24"> Ben adam, prophesy, and say:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Thus saith Adonoi: Say, A cherev, a cherev is sharpened,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and also polished;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 21:10; YIRMEYAH 17:27; YECHEZKEL 21:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is sharpened to make a dreadful tevo'ach;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it is polished and lightening-like it flashes.</span><br />Should we then make mirth? The Shevet of Beni<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Moshiach, Gn 49:10; 2Sm 7">a</a></sup>will despise kol etz (every tree).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 21:20; YECHEZKEL 21:4; YECHEZKEL 14:17"> And it is given to be polished, that it may be grasped with the palm; this cherev is sharpened, and it is polished, to deliver it into the yad of the slayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 5:13; YECHEZKEL 21:14; ZECHARYAH 6:8"> Cry and wail, ben adam; for it shall be upon My people, it shall be upon all the nasi'im of Yisroel; including terrors by reason of the cherev shall be upon My people; strike therefore upon thy thigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Because it is a testing, and what? Even if the despising Scepter shall be no more? saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 4:1–3; YIRMEYAH 1:10; YECHEZKEL 5:1–17"> Thou therefore, ben adam, prophesy, and strike thine hands together, and let the cherev do double damage the third time, the cherev of the slain; it is the cherev for great slaughter — it surrounds them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="AMOS 1:14; YIRMEYAH 49:2; YECHEZKEL 25:5"> I have set the point of the cherev against all their she'arim, that their lev may faint, and many may stumble. Ah! It is made for flashing, it is wrapped for slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHOFETIM 18:20; MISHLE 16:33; SHOFETIM 17:5"> Go thee one way or the other, either on right hand, or on left, wherever thy face is appointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 4:2; YECHEZKEL 26:9; YIRMEYAH 51:14"> I will also strike Mine palm together, and I will appease My chemah (wrath). I Hashem have said it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 5:15; YECHEZKEL 21:24; YECHEZKEL 29:16"> The Devar Hashem came unto me again, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="HOSHEA 4:2; MICHOH 3:10–12; YIRMEYAH 3:2"> Also, thou, ben adam, appoint thee two drakhim, that the cherev of Melech Bavel may come; both two shall come forth out of one land; and make a sign, put it at the rosh derech (crossroads) to the Ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 35:5; YECHEZKEL 21:29; YECHEZKEL 17:19"> Appoint a derech, that the cherev may come to Rabbah Bnei Ammon, and to Yehudah in fortified Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 17:24; TEHILLIM 75:7; YIRMEYAH 13:18"> For Melech Bavel stood at the fork in the derech, at the rosh of the two drakhim, to use divination; he shook his khitzim (arrows), he consulted with teraphim, he looked in the liver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MICHOH 5:2; YIRMEYAH 23:5–6; ZECHARYAH 9:9"> At his right hand was the kesem (divination) for Yerushalayim, to set up battering rams, to call for a slaughter, to lift up the voice with teru'ah (shouting), to appoint battering rams against the she'arim, to heap up a siege mound, and to build a siege work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 21:20; YECHEZKEL 21:9–10; TZEFANYAH 2:8–10"> And it shall be unto them as a kesem shav (false divination) in their sight, to them that have sworn shevu'ot (oaths); but he will call to remembrance the avon, that they may be seized.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 21:25; YECHEZKEL 22:28; TEHILLIM 37:13"> Therefore thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Because ye have made your avon to be remembered, in that your peysha'im are uncovered, so that in all your doings your sins do appear; because, that ye are come to remembrance, ye shall be captured with the palm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 16:38; BERESHIS 15:14; YIRMEYAH 47:6–7"> And thou, profane wicked nasi of Yisroel, whose day is come, when avon shall have an end,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="NACHUM 1:6; YIRMEYAH 6:22–23; YECHEZKEL 14:19"> Thus saith Adonoi Hashem; Remove the mitznefet (turban), and take off the ateret; nothing shall be the same; exalt him that is low, and abase him that is exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 25:10; MALACHI 4:1; YECHEZKEL 20:47–48"> I will make it overthrown, overthrown, overthrown: and it shall be no longer, ad bo asher lo hamishpat (until He come to whom the right it is); and I will give it him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gn 49:10">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And thou, ben adam, prophesy and say, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem concerning Bnei Ammon, and concerning their cherpah; even say thou: The cherev, the cherev is drawn; for the slaughter it is polished, to consume like lightning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> While they see chazon shav (vain visions) for thee, while they divine a kazav (lie) unto thee, to bring thee upon the tzavarot (necks) of them that are slain, of the resha'im, whose day is come, when their avon shall have an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Shall I cause it to return into its sheath? I will judge thee in the makom where thou wast created, in thine eretz moledet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And I will pour out Mine za'am (indignation) upon thee, I will blow against thee in the eish of My wrath, and deliver thee into the yad of brutal anashim, skillful in destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Thou shalt be for fuel to the eish; thy dahm shall be in the midst of ha'aretz; thou shalt be no more remembered; for I Hashem have spoken it.</span>
</div>
