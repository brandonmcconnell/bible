<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 40:20; YECHEZKEL 40:17; YECHEZKEL 42:13"> Then he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the angelic being">a</a></sup>brought me forth into the khatzer hakhatzonah, the way toward the north; and he brought me into the lishka that was opposite the Gizrah, and which was opposite the Binyan toward the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 41:13"> Before the length of a hundred cubits was the Petach HaTzafon, and the width was fifty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 41:15–16; YECHEZKEL 41:10; SHIR HASHIRIM 7:5"> Opposite the twenty cubits of the khatzer hapenimah, and opposite the ritzpah which was for the khatzer hakhitzonah, was atik (balcony) against atik in three stories.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 46:19; LUKAS 13:24; MATTITYAHU 7:14"> And before the leshakhot was a mahalach (walkway) ten cubits in width leading inward, a way of one cubit; and their doors toward the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 41:7"> Now the upper leshakhot were narrow; for the atikim took space from these, from the lower, and from the middlemost of the Binyan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 41:6; MELACHIM ALEF 6:8"> For they were in three stories, but had not ammudim like the ammudim of the khatzerot; therefore they were set back from the ground more than the lowest and the middlemost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 42:12; YECHEZKEL 42:10"> And the wall that was outside over against the leshakhot, toward the khatzer hakhitzonah on the forepart of the leshakhot, the length thereof was fifty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 41:13–14"> For the length of the leshakhot that were in the khatzer hakhitzonah was fifty cubits; and, hineh, before the Heikhal were a hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 46:19; YECHEZKEL 44:5"> And from below these leshakhot was the entrance on the east side, as one goeth into them from the khatzer hakhitzonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 42:1; YECHEZKEL 42:13; YECHEZKEL 41:12–15"> In the thickness of the wall of the khatzer toward the east, facing the Gizrah, and over against the Binyan, were leshakhot (side rooms).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 42:4"> And the derech before them was like the appearance of the leshakhot which were toward the north, as long as they, and as wide as they; and all their exits were both according to their arrangements, and according to their doorways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And according to the doorways of the leshakhot that were toward the south was a petach in the rosh of the derech, even the derech directly before the wall toward the east, as one entereth into them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="VAYIKRA 10:17; VAYIKRA 7:6; YECHEZKEL 40:46"> Then said he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="the angelic being">b</a></sup>unto me, The leshakhot hatzafon and the leshakhot hadarom, which are before the Gizrah, they are leshakhot hakodesh, where the Kohanim that approach unto Hashem shall eat the kadshei hakadashim; there shall they put the kadshei hakadashim, and the minchah, and the chattat, and the asham (trespass offering); for the place is kadosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 44:19; VAYIKRA 8:7; SHEMOT 29:4–9"> When the Kohanim enter therein, then shall they not go out of the Kodesh into the khatzer hakhitzonah, but there they shall lay their garments wherein they minister; for they are kodesh; and shall put on begadim acherim (other garments); then shall they approach the precinct of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 43:1; YECHEZKEL 41:15; YECHEZKEL 40:6–16"> Now when he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="the angelic being">c</a></sup>had made an end of measuring the Beis HaPenimi, he brought me forth toward the sha'ar whose view is toward the east, and measured it all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 40:3; HISGALUS 11:1–2; ZECHARYAH 2:1"> He measured the eastern side with the measuring rod, five hundred rods, with the keneh hamiddah (measuring rod) all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He measured the northern side, five hundred rods, with the keneh hamiddah all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He measured the southern side, five hundred rods, with the keneh hamiddah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> He turned about to the western side, and measured five hundred rods with the keneh hamiddah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 45:2; YECHEZKEL 22:26; YECHEZKEL 40:5"> He measured it by the four sides; it had a wall all around, five hundred rods long, and five hundred wide, lehavdil (to make a separation) between the Kodesh (Holy Precinct, area) and the Chol (Common Precinct, area).</span>
