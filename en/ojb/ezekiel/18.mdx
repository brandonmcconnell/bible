<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Devar Hashem came unto me again, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:29–30; EKHAH 5:7; YECHEZKEL 37:11"> What mean ye, that ye use this mashal concerning Admat Yisroel, saying, Avot have eaten sour grapes, and the teeth of the banim are set on edge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:19–20; YECHEZKEL 33:11–20; YECHEZKEL 18:30"> As I live, saith Adonoi Hashem, ye shall not have occasion any more to use this mashal in Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:23; BAMIDBAR 16:22"> Behold, all nefashot are Mine; as the nefesh of the av, so also the nefesh of the ben is Mine; the nefesh that sinneth, it shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 33:14; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:7; MISHLE 21:3"> But if an ish be tzaddik, and do that which is mishpat and tzedakah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:15; DEVARIM 4:19; YECHEZKEL 6:13"> And hath not eaten &#91;sacrifices of gillulim, idols&#93; upon the harim, neither hath lifted up his eyes to the gillulim of Bais Yisroel, neither hath made tameh the isha of his neighbor, neither hath come near to an isha niddah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:16; SHEMOT 22:26; YECHEZKEL 18:12"> And hath not oppressed any ish, but hath restored to the debtor his chavolah (pledge, collateral), hath spoiled none by a robberʼs violence, hath given his lechem to the hungry, and hath covered the eirom (naked) with a garment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:25; ZECHARYAH 8:16; DEVARIM 23:19–20"> He that hath not loaned gelt on neshekh, neither hath taken any tarbit (interest, usury), that hath withdrawn his yad from iniquity, hath executed mishpat emes between ish and ish,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:11; CHABAKUK 2:4; AMOS 5:4"> Hath walked in My chukkot, and hath been shomer over My mishpatim, to do emes; he is tzaddik, he shall surely live, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 21:12; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:12; YOCHANAN 18:40"> If he father a ben-paritz shofech dahm (a violent son who sheds blood), and that doeth the like to his brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HISGALUS 22:14; MATTITYAHU 7:21–27; MELACHIM ALEF 13:8"> Though he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the av">a</a></sup>doeth none of these; he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="the ben">b</a></sup>hath eaten &#91;sacrifices to idols&#93; upon the harim, and made tameh the isha of his neighbor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 8:17; YECHEZKEL 8:6; MELACHIM BAIS 21:11"> Hath oppressed the oni and evyon, hath spoiled by a robberʼs violence, hath not restored the chavol (pledge), and hath lifted up his eyes to the gillulim, hath committed to'evah (abomination),</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:25; VAYIKRA 20:9; YECHEZKEL 3:18"> Hath loaned gelt upon neshekh (usury), and hath taken tarbit (interest); shall he then live? He shall not live; he hath done all these to'evot; he shall surely die; his dahm shall be upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:21; MISHLE 23:24; YIRMEYAH 8:6"> Now, hinei, if he father a ben, that seeth all the chattot aviv which he hath done, and considereth, and doeth not like such,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:6–7; YECHEZKEL 18:11–13"> That hath not eaten &#91;sacrifices to idols&#93; upon the harim, neither hath lifted up his eyes to the gillulim of Bais Yisroel, hath not made tameh the isha of his neighbor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:7; YESHAYAH 58:7–10; IYOV 22:7"> Neither hath oppressed any ish, hath not withheld the chavol (pledge), neither hath spoiled by a robberʼs violence, but hath given his lechem to the hungry, and hath covered the eirom (naked) with a garment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:8–9; MATTITYAHU 18:27–35; YECHEZKEL 18:13"> That hath taken off his yad from the oni, that hath not received neshekh (usury) nor tarbit (interest), hath executed My mishpat, hath walked in My chukkot; he shall not die for the avon aviv, he shall surely live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 3:18; YECHEZKEL 18:26; YECHEZKEL 18:24"> As for aviv, because he cruelly oppressed with extortion, spoiled his brother by a robberʼs violence, and did that which is not tov among his people, hinei, even he shall die in his avon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:5; ZECHARYAH 1:3–6; DEVARIM 5:9"> Yet say ye, Why? Doth not the ben bear the avon (iniquity, guilt) of the av? When the ben hath done that which is mishpat and tzedakah, and hath been shomer over all My chukkot, and hath done them, he shall surely live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEVARIM 24:16; MELACHIM BAIS 14:6; YECHEZKEL 18:4"> The nefesh that sinneth, it shall die. The ben shall not nasah the avon of the av, neither shall the av nasah the avon of the ben; the tzedakat hatzaddik shall be upon him, and the rishat rashah shall be upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:27–28; LETTER TO TITOS 2:11–14; YECHEZKEL 33:19"> But if the rashah will turn from all his chattot that he hath committed, and be shomer over all My chukkot, and do that which is mishpat and tzedakah, he shall surely live, he shall not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MICHOH 7:19; YECHEZKEL 33:16; YESHAYAH 43:25"> All his peysha'im that he hath committed, they shall not be remembered against him: in his tzidkat that he hath done he shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 33:11; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:4; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9"> Have I any pleasure at all in the mot rashah? saith Adonoi Hashem. And not that he should make teshuvah from the darkhei of him, and live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 33:18; MISHLE 21:16; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8"> But when the tzaddik turneth away from his tzedek, and committeth iniquity, and doeth according to all the to'evot that the rashah doeth, shall he live? All his tzedek that he hath done shall not be remembered; in his ma'al (unfaithfulness) that he hath committed treachery, and in his chattat that he hath sinned, in them shall he die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 33:17; MALACHI 3:13–15; MALACHI 2:17"> Yet ye say, The Derech Adonoi is not proper. Hear now, O Bais Yisroel: Is My Derech not proper? Is it not the drakhim of you that are not proper?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> When a tzaddik turneth away from his tzedek, and committeth iniquity, and dieth in them, for his iniquity that he hath done shall he die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:21; YESHAYAH 1:18; YESHAYAH 55:7"> Again, when a rashah turneth away from his wickedness that he hath committed, and doeth that which is mishpat and tzedakah, he shall save his nefesh alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:29; LUKAS 15:17–18; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:10–12"> Because he considereth, and turneth away from all his peysha'im that he hath committed, he shall surely live; he shall not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:25; MISHLE 19:3; YECHEZKEL 18:2"> Yet saith Bais Yisroel, The Derech Adonoi is not proper. O Bais Yisroel, is My Derech not proper? Is it not the drakhim of you that are not proper?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:21; MATTITYAHU 16:27; MATTITYAHU 3:2"> Therefore I will judge you, O Bais Yisroel, every one according to the drakhim of him, saith Adonoi Hashem. Make teshuvah, and turn yourselves from all your peysha'im, so avon shall not be your michshol (stumbling block, downfall).</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="TEHILLIM 51:10; YECHEZKEL 36:26; YECHEZKEL 11:19"> Cast away from you all your peysha'im, whereby ye have committed peysha; and get you a lev chadash and a ruach chadashah;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e., regeneration, new birth see Yn 3:3">c</a></sup>for why will ye die, O Bais Yisroel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; YECHEZKEL 18:23; YECHEZKEL 33:11"> For I have no pleasure in the mot of him that dieth, saith Adonoi Hashem; therefore, make teshuva and live!</span>
