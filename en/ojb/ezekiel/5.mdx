<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YESHAYAH 7:20; YECHEZKEL 44:20; VAYIKRA 21:5"> And thou, Ben Adam, take thee a sharp cherev, take thee a barberʼs ta'ar (razor), and cause it to pass upon thine rosh and upon thy zakan (beard): then take thee scales for weighing and divide the hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 5:12; VAYIKRA 26:33; YECHEZKEL 4:1–8"> Thou shalt burn with flame a third part in the midst of the Ir, when the days of the siege are fulfilled: and thou shalt take a third part, and strike it all around with the cherev: and a third part thou shalt scatter in the ruach; and I will draw out the cherev after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 52:16; YIRMEYAH 40:6; YIRMEYAH 39:10"> Thou shalt also take thereof a few in mispar, and bind them in the folds of thy robes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 4:4; YIRMEYAH 41:1–18; MELACHIM BAIS 25:25"> Then take of them again, and cast them into the midst of the eish, and burn them in the eish; for thereof shall an eish spread unto kol Bais Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 4:1; DEVARIM 4:6; YIRMEYAH 6:6"> Thus saith Adonoi Hashem; This is Yerushalayim: I have set it in the midst of the Goyim and countries that are all around her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 11:10; YECHEZKEL 16:47–48; NECHEMYAH 9:16–17"> And she hath rebelled against My mishpatim with resha'ah more than the Goyim, and My chukkot more than the countries that are all around her: for they have refused My mishpatim and My chukkot, they have not walked in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 2:10–11; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:9; MELACHIM BAIS 21:9–11"> Therefore thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Because more than the Goyim that are all around you, ye have been ungovernable, and have not walked in My chukkot, neither have done My mishpatim, neither have done according to the mishpatim of the Goyim that are all around you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 15:7; YIRMEYAH 24:9; YECHEZKEL 21:3"> Therefore thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Hineni, I, even I, am against thee, and will execute mishpatim in the midst of thee in the sight of the Goyim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:21; DANIEL 9:12; AMOS 3:2"> And I will do to thee that which I have not done, and whereunto I will not do any more the like, because of kol to'avot of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 2:6; DEVARIM 28:64; YECHEZKEL 12:14"> Therefore the avot shall eat banim in the midst of thee, and the banim shall eat their avot; and I will execute shefatim (judgments, punishments) in thee, and kol she'erit of thee will I scatter unto kol ruach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 7:20; YECHEZKEL 7:9; YECHEZKEL 11:18"> Therefore, as I live, saith Adonoi Hashem; Surely, because thou hast made My Mikdash tameh with all thy shikkutzim, and with all thine to'avot, therefore will I also withdraw; neither shall Mine eye pity, neither will I spare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 5:2; YIRMEYAH 15:2; YIRMEYAH 43:10–11"> A third part of thee shall die by dever, and by ra'av shall they be consumed in the midst of thee: and a third part shall fall by the cherev all around thee; and I will scatter a third part into kol ruach, and I will draw out a cherev after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YESHAYAH 1:24; YECHEZKEL 36:5–6; YECHEZKEL 21:17"> Thus shall Mine anger be accomplished, and I will cause My chemah to be satisfied against them, and I will be avenged: and they shall know that I Hashem have spoken in My kinah, when I have accomplished My chemah against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 22:4; NECHEMYAH 2:17; TEHILLIM 79:1–4"> Moreover I will make thee a ruin, and a cherpah among the Goyim that are all around thee, before the eyes of all that pass by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 25:17; YIRMEYAH 22:8–9; MELACHIM ALEF 9:7"> So it shall be a cherpah (reproach) and a gedufah (taunt), a musar and a meshammah (astonishment, object of horror) unto the Goyim that are all around thee, when I shall execute shefatim (judgments) on thee in anger and in chemah and in tokhchot chemah (furious rebukes). I Hashem have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 4:16; DEVARIM 32:23–24; YECHEZKEL 14:13"> When I shall send upon them disastrous khitzim (arrows) of ra'av (famine), which shall be for mashchit (destruction), and which I will send to destroy you: and I will increase the ra'av (famine) upon you, and will break your mateh lechem (staff of bread, food supply):</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 38:22; YECHEZKEL 14:21; VAYIKRA 26:22"> So will I send upon you ra'av (famine) and chayyah ra'ah (wild beasts), and they shall leave thee bereaved as childless: and dever and dahm shall pass through thee; and I will bring the cherev upon thee. I Hashem have spoken.</span>
