<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Again the Devar Hashem came unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 22:2; YECHEZKEL 20:4; YESHAYAH 58:1"> Ben adam, cause Yerushalayim to know her to'avot (abominations),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 16:45; YESHAYAH 51:1–2; DEVARIM 20:17"> And say, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem unto Yerushalayim: Thy origin and thy birth are from Eretz HaKena'ani; avicha was HaEmori (the Amorite) and your em Chittit (a Hittite).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HOSHEA 2:3; BERESHIS 15:13; SHEMOT 5:16–21"> And as for thy moledot (birth), in the day thou wast born thy navel cord was not cut, neither wast thou washed in mayim to cleanse thee; thou wast not salted at all, nor swaddled at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:10; BERESHIS 21:10; SHEMOT 1:22"> None eye pitied thee, to do any of these unto thee, to have compassion upon thee; but thou wast cast out into the open sadeh, when thou thyself were loathed, in your yom huledet (day of birth).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:4–5; YOCHANAN 5:25; LETTER TO TITOS 3:3–7"> And when I passed by thee, and saw thee flailing about in thine own dahm, I said unto thee when thou wast in thy dahm, Chayi! (live!) Indeed, I said unto thee when thou wast in thy dahm, Chayi!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:10; SHEMOT 1:7; YESHAYAH 62:3"> I have caused thee to multiply as the tzemach of the sadeh, and thou hast increased and matured, and thou art come to excellent beauty; thy breasts are fashioned, and thine hair is grown, whereas thou wast erom (naked) and bare.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="RUTH 3:9; YIRMEYAH 2:2–3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:8"> Now when I passed by thee, and looked upon thee, hinei, thy time was the time of dodim (love); and I spread My kanaf (wing, corner of garment) over thee, and covered thy ervah (nakedness); indeed, I swore an oath unto thee, and entered into a brit with thee, saith Adonoi Hashem, and thou becamest Mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 23:5; RUTH 3:3; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:27"> Then washed I thee with mayim; indeed, I thoroughly washed away thy dahm from thee, and I anointed thee with shemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 16:18; YECHEZKEL 16:13; YESHAYAH 61:10"> I clothed thee also with embroidered cloth, and shod thee with tachash leather, and I girded thee about with fine linen, and I covered thee with silk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:22; YESHAYAH 3:19; BERESHIS 41:42"> I decked thee also with jewelry, and I put tzemidim (bracelets) upon thy hands, and a necklace chain on thy neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 3:21; YESHAYAH 28:5; YIRMEYAH 13:18"> And I put a nezem (ring) in thy nose, and earrings in thine oznayim, and an ateret tiferet upon thine rosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:13–14; TEHILLIM 50:2; MELACHIM ALEF 4:21"> Thus wast thou decked with zahav and kesef; and thy raiment was of fine linen, and silk, and embroidered cloth; thou didst eat fine flour, and devash, and shemen; and thou wast exceeding beautiful, and thou didst become fit for royalty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 10:24; EKHAH 2:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:7"> And thy shem (name, renown) went forth among the Goyim on account of thy beauty; for it was perfect, through My hadar (splendor) which I had set upon thee, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YESHAYAH 57:8; YIRMEYAH 2:20; YECHEZKEL 16:25"> But thou didst trust in thine own beauty, and playedst the zonah because of thy renown, and pouredst out thy harlotry on every one that passed by; his it became.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 23:7; YECHEZKEL 7:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:24"> And of thy begadim thou didst take, and adorned thy bamot te'lu'ot (gaudy high places, cult prostitution brilliantly colored harlot “beds”), and playedst the zonah thereupon; such things should not come, neither should they be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 23:14–21; YIRMEYAH 3:9; YECHEZKEL 16:11"> Thou hast also taken thy fair jewels of My zahav and of My kesef, which I had given thee, and madest to thyself tzelamim (images) of zachar (male),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., phallic symbols">a</a></sup>and didst play the zonah with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And tookest thy embroidered garments, and coveredst them;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="the idols">b</a></sup>and thou hast set Mine shemen and Mine ketoret before them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="the idols">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 16:13; HOSHEA 2:8–13; BERESHIS 8:21"> My lechem also which I gave thee, solet (fine flour), and shemen, and devash, wherewith I fed thee, thou hast even set it before them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="the idols">d</a></sup>for a re'ach hannichoach; and it was thus, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 23:37; YECHEZKEL 20:31; YIRMEYAH 7:31"> Moreover thou hast taken thy banim and thy banot, whom thou hast borne unto Me, and these hast thou sacrificed unto them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="the idols">e</a></sup>to be devoured. Was this thy zonah harlotry not enough,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 17:17; VAYIKRA 18:21; DEVARIM 18:10"> That thou hast slaughtered My banim, and offered them to cause them to pass through the eish for them?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="the idols">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 2:2; HOSHEA 11:1; YECHEZKEL 16:43"> And in all thine to'avot and thy zonah harlotry thou hast not remembered the days of thy youth, when thou wast erom (naked) and bare, and wast flailing about in thy dahm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="HISGALUS 12:12; YECHEZKEL 2:10; YECHEZKEL 24:6"> And it came to pass after all thy ra'ah (wickedness) — Oy, oy unto thee! saith Adonoi Hashem —</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:58; YECHEZKEL 16:39; YECHEZKEL 16:31"> That thou hast also built unto thee a gev (eminent place, cult prostitution shrine), and hast made thee a ramah (platform) in every rechov (public square).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 16:31; YIRMEYAH 3:2; BERESHIS 38:21"> Thou hast built thy ramah at rosh derech (crossroad), and hast made thy beauty to be abhorred, and hast spread open thy raglayim to every one that passed by, and multiplied thy zonah harlotry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 23:19–21; YECHEZKEL 20:7–8; YECHEZKEL 8:10"> Thou hast also played the zonah fornicator with the Mitzrayim, thy very fleshy neighbors; and hast increased thy zonah harlotry, to provoke Me to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YESHAYAH 9:12; YECHEZKEL 16:57; YECHEZKEL 16:37"> Hinei, therefore I have stretched out My yad against thee, and have diminished thine food ration, and delivered thee unto the nefesh (passionate desire) of them that hate thee, the banot Pelishtim (daughters of the Philistines), which are ashamed of thy derech zimmah (lewd deportment).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 16:7; HOSHEA 10:6; MELACHIM BAIS 16:10–18"> Thou hast played the zonah also with Ashur, because thou wast insatiable; indeed, thou hast played the zonah with them, and yet couldest not be satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 21:9; SHOFETIM 2:12–19; YECHEZKEL 13:14–23"> Thou hast moreover multiplied thy zenut to eretz Kena'an unto Chaldea; and yet thou wast not satisfied therewith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 3:3; YESHAYAH 1:3; YESHAYAH 3:9"> How degenerate is thine lev, saith Adonoi Hashem, seeing thou doest all these things, the work of a brazen isha zonah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 16:33–34; YESHAYAH 52:3; YECHEZKEL 16:39"> In that thou buildest thine gev in rosh kol derech, and makest thine ramah in every rechov; and hast not been as a zonah, in that thou scornest hire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 2:28; YECHEZKEL 23:37; YIRMEYAH 2:25"> But as the isha (wife) the mena'efet (one that commiteth adultery), which taketh zarim instead of her ish!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YESHAYAH 57:9; HOSHEA 8:9–10; YECHEZKEL 16:41"> They give nedeh (gifts) to all zonot; but thou givest thy nadan (gift) to all thy lovers, and hirest them, that they may come unto thee on every side for thy zenut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And the opposite is in thee from other nashim in thy zonah harlotry, whereas none doth solicit thee to commit zonah harlotry: and in that thou givest an etnan (payment), and no etnan is given unto thee, therefore thou art opposite.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:18; MELACHIM ALEF 22:19; YOCHANAN 4:10"> Therefore, O zonah, hear the Devar Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 23:10; YECHEZKEL 24:13; TZEFANYAH 3:1"> Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Because thy passion was poured out, and thy ervah uncovered through thy zonah harlotry with thy lovers, and with all the gillulim (idols) of thy to'avot, and by the dahm of thy banim, which thou didst give unto them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="HOSHEA 2:10; YIRMEYAH 13:22; HOSHEA 2:3"> Hineni, therefore I will gather all thy lovers, with whom thou hast taken pleasure, and all them that thou hast loved, with all them that thou hast hated; I will even gather them around about against thee, and will uncover thy ervah (nakedness) unto them, that they may see all thy ervah (nakedness, pudenda).</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:10; BERESHIS 9:6; TZEFANYAH 1:17"> And I will judge thee, as nashim that are guilty of no'afot (acts of adulteresses, breaking wedlock) and shefach dahm are judged; and I will bring dahm upon thee in chemah (wrath) and kina (jealousy).</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 23:26; HOSHEA 2:3; YECHEZKEL 16:24–25"> And I will also give thee into their yad, and they shall throw down thine gev, and shall break down thy ramot (high places, cult prostitution sites of pagan worship); they shall strip thee also of thy clothes, and shall take thy fair jewels, and leave thee erom (naked) and bare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 23:47; CHABAKUK 1:6–10; YOCHANAN 8:5–7"> They shall also bring up a kahal against thee, and they shall stone thee with stones, and hack thee to bits with their charavot (swords).</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 23:48; YIRMEYAH 52:13; YIRMEYAH 39:8"> And they shall burn thine batim (houses) with eish, and execute shefatim (judgments) upon thee in the sight of many nashim; and I will cause thee to cease from playing the zonah, and thou also shalt give no etnan (payment) any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 5:13; YECHEZKEL 21:17; YESHAYAH 40:1–2"> So will I make My chemah (wrath) toward thee to rest, and My kina (jealousy) shall depart from thee, and I will be quiet, and will be no more angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 16:22; YECHEZKEL 11:21; TEHILLIM 78:42"> Because thou hast not remembered the days of thy youth, but hast provoked Me in all these things; therefore surely I also will repay thy derech upon thine rosh, saith Adonoi Hashem; and thou shalt not commit this zimmah (lewdness) in addition to all thine to'avot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:2–3; TEHILLIM 106:35–38; SHMUEL ALEF 24:13"> Hinei, every one that useth proverbs shall use this mashal against thee, saying, As is the imma (mother), so is her bat (daughter).</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 11:8; YESHAYAH 1:4; YECHEZKEL 23:37–39"> Thou art bat of thy em, that despised her ish and her banim; and thou art the achot (sister) of thy akhayot, which despised their anashim and their banim; your em was a Chittit (Hittite), and your av an Emori (Amorite).</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 23:4; YECHEZKEL 16:48–49; YECHEZKEL 16:53–56"> And thine elder achot (sister) is Shomron, she and her banot that dwell to thy north; and thy younger achot, that dwelleth to thy south, is Sodom and her banot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 21:9; YECHEZKEL 16:51; YECHEZKEL 5:6–7"> Yet hast thou not walked after their drakhim, nor done after their to'avot; but, as if that were too little a thing, thou wast corrupted more than they in all thy drakhim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:15; LUKAS 10:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:52"> As I live, saith Adonoi Hashem, Sodom thy achot (sister) hath not done, she nor her banot, as thou hast done, thou and thy banot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="TEHILLIM 138:6; YECHEZKEL 28:2; BERESHIS 13:10"> Hinei, this was the avon Sodom thy achot; ga'on (pride), over fullness of lechem, and abundance of idleness was in her and in her banot, neither did she strengthen the yad of the oni (poor) and evyon (needy).</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="BERESHIS 19:24; BERESHIS 18:20; BERESHIS 19:5"> And they were haughty, and committed to'evah before Me; therefore I took them away as I saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 3:8–11; LUKAS 12:47–48; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:9–20"> Neither hath Shomron committed half of thy chattot (sins); but thou hast multiplied thine to'avot more than they, and hast made thy akhayot (sisters) seem righteous in all thine to'avot which thou hast done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:1–5; YECHEZKEL 36:15; YECHEZKEL 16:56"> Thou also, which hast judged thy akhayot (sisters), bear thine own shame for thy chattot (sins) that thou hast committed more abominable than theirs; they are more righteous than thou; indeed, be thou disgraced also, and bear thy shame, in that thou hast made thy akhayot (sisters) seem righteous.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 29:14; YIRMEYAH 12:16; YECHEZKEL 39:25"> When I shall restore their fortunes, the fortunes of Sodom and her banot, and the fortunes of Shomron and her banot, then will I restore the fortunes of thy fortune among them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 14:22–23; YIRMEYAH 2:26; YECHEZKEL 36:31–32"> That thou mayest bear thine own shame, and mayest be disgraced in all that thou hast done, in that thou art a comfort unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 36:11; MALACHI 3:4; YECHEZKEL 16:53"> When thy akhayot (sisters), Sodom and her banot, shall return to their former state, and Shomron and her banot shall return to their former state, then thou and thy banot shall return to your former state.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="YESHAYAH 65:5; LUKAS 15:28–30; TZEFANYAH 3:11"> For thy achot (sister) Sodom was not mentioned by thy mouth in the day of thy ga'on (pride),</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 16:5–7; YECHEZKEL 16:36–37; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:5–6"> Before thy ra'ah (wickedness) was uncovered, as at the time of the cherpah (disgrace) of the banot Aram (Syria), and all that are around her, the banot of the Pelishtim (Philistines), which despise thee all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 23:49; EKHAH 5:7; BERESHIS 4:13"> Thou hast borne &#91;the penalty of&#93; thy zimmah (lewdness) and thine to'avot, saith Hashem.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="HOSHEA 2:19–20; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:24; YECHEZKEL 16:8"> Nevertheless I will remember My Brit (covenant) with thee in the days of thy youth, and I will establish unto thee a Brit Olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:26–31; YESHAYAH 66:7–12"> Then thou shalt remember thy drakhim, and be ashamed, when thou shalt receive thy akhayot (sisters), thine elder and thy younger; and I will give them unto thee for banot, but not because of thy brit (covenant).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="i.e., Sodom and Shomron had no covenant with Hashem">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 24:7; YECHEZKEL 6:7; YECHEZKEL 39:22"> And I will establish My Brit (covenant) with thee; and thou shalt know that I am Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="TEHILLIM 39:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:19; YECHEZKEL 36:31–32"> That thou mayest remember, and be ashamed, and never open thy mouth any more because of thy shame, when I have made kapporah (atonement)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="i.e., when I am pacified, appeased">h</a></sup>toward thee for all that thou hast done, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
