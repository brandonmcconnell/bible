<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 8:1; YECHEZKEL 30:20; YECHEZKEL 24:1"> And it came to pass in the seventh year, in the fifth month, the tenth day of the month, that certain anashim of the zekenim of Yisroel came to inquire of Hashem, and sat before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Then came the Devar Hashem unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MISHLE 28:9; MATTITYAHU 15:8–9; TEHILLIM 50:15–21"> Ben adam, speak unto the zekenim of Yisroel, and say unto them, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Are ye come to inquire of Me? As I live, saith Adonoi Hashem, I will not be inquired of by you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 22:2; YECHEZKEL 23:36; YECHEZKEL 14:20"> Wilt thou judge them, ben adam, wilt thou judge them? Cause them to know the to'avot of their avot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:6; YECHEZKEL 20:15; SHEMOT 3:8"> And say unto them, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: In the day when I chose Yisroel, and lifted up Mine yad<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., took a shevua">a</a></sup>unto the zera Bais Ya'akov, and made Myself known unto them in Eretz Mitzrayim, when I lifted up Mine yad unto them, saying, I am Hashem Eloheichem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:8; YIRMEYAH 32:22; YECHEZKEL 20:15"> In the day that I lifted up Mine yad unto them, to bring them forth from Eretz Mitzrayim into a land that I had searched out for them, zavat cholov udevash, which is the most beautiful of all lands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:3; YECHEZKEL 20:8; VAYIKRA 11:44"> Then said I unto them, Cast ye away every ish the shikkutzim before his eynayim, and make not yourselves tameh with the gillulim (idols) of Mitzrayim; I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:7; YESHAYAH 63:10; YECHEZKEL 20:21"> But they rebelled against Me, and would not pay heed unto Me; they did not every ish cast away the shikkutzim from before their eynayim, neither did they forsake the gillulim (idols) of Mitzrayim. Then I said, I will pour out My chemah (wrath) upon them, to vent My anger against them in the midst of Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 39:7; YECHEZKEL 20:22; SHEMOT 32:12"> But I acted for My Nameʼs sake, that it should not be profaned before the eyes of the Goyim, among whom they were living. In bringing them forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim, in their sight I made Myself &#91;publicly&#93; known unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 15:22; SHEMOT 13:17–18; SHEMOT 14:17–22"> Therefore I caused them to go forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim, and brought them into the midbar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:5; VAYIKRA 18:5; DEVARIM 4:8"> And I gave them My chukkot, and made known to them My mishpatim, which if ha'adam does, he shall even live in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:20; YECHEZKEL 37:28; VAYIKRA 21:23"> Moreover also I gave them My Shabbatot, to be an Ot between Me and them, that they might know that I am Hashem that set them apart as kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:21; YECHEZKEL 20:8; YESHAYAH 56:6"> But Bais Yisroel rebelled against Me in the midbar; they walked not in My chukkot, and they despised My mishpatim, which if ha'adam does, he shall even live in them; and My Shabbatot they greatly defiled. Then I said, I would pour out My chemah (wrath) upon them in the midbar, to consume them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:9; YECHEZKEL 36:22–23; YECHEZKEL 20:22"> But I acted for My Nameʼs sake, that it should not be profaned before the eyes of the Goyim, in whose sight I brought them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 95:11; TEHILLIM 106:26; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:18"> Yet also I lifted up My Yad unto them in the midbar, that I would not bring them into ha'aretz which I had given them, zavat cholov udevash, the most beautiful of all lands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 15:39; YECHEZKEL 20:8; YECHEZKEL 14:3–4"> Because they despised My mishpatim, and walked not in My chukkot, but profaned My Shabbatot; for their lev went after their gillulim (idols).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 11:13; YIRMEYAH 4:27; YIRMEYAH 5:18"> Nevertheless since Mine eye spared them from destroying them, I did not make an end of them in the midbar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 1:2–4; TEHILLIM 78:6–8; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:18"> But I said unto their banim in the midbar, Walk ye not in the chukkot of avoteichem, neither be shomer over their mishpatim, nor make yourselves tameh with their gillulim (idols).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 36:27; DEVARIM 5:32–8; YECHEZKEL 37:24"> I am Hashem Eloheichem; walk in My chukkot, and be shomer over My mishpatim, and do them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YESHAYAH 58:13; YIRMEYAH 17:22; SHEMOT 31:13–17"> And be shomer shabbos over My Shabbatot, keeping them kodesh; and they shall be an Ot between Me and you, that ye may know that I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:13; BAMIDBAR 21:5; YECHEZKEL 20:8"> Notwithstanding, the banim rebelled against Me; they walked not in My chukkot, neither were shomer over My mishpatim to do them, which if ha'adam does, he shall even live in them; they profaned My Shabbatot. Then I said, I would pour out My chemah (wrath) upon them, to accomplish My anger against them in the midbar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:14; YECHEZKEL 20:9; YIRMEYAH 14:21"> Nevertheless, I withdrew Mine yad, and acted for My Nameʼs sake, that it should not be profaned before the eyes of the Goyim, in whose sight I brought them forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:33; DEVARIM 28:64–68; DEVARIM 32:26–27"> I lifted up Mine yad unto them also in the midbar, that I would scatter them among the Goyim, and disperse them through the countries;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:16; YECHEZKEL 6:9; AMOS 2:4"> Because they had not executed My mishpatim, but had despised My chukkot, and had profaned My Shabbatot, and their eynayim were after the gillulim of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="TEHILLIM 81:12; YESHAYAH 66:4; YECHEZKEL 20:39"> Therefore I gave them also chukkot that were not tovim, and mishpatim whereby they could not live;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 6:7; VAYIKRA 18:21; YECHEZKEL 20:31"> And I pronounced them tameh (unclean) because of their own &#91;ritual&#93; gifts, in that they caused to pass through the eish all that openeth the womb, that I might make them desolate, to the end that they might know that I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:24; YECHEZKEL 2:7; YECHEZKEL 3:11"> Therefore, ben adam, speak unto Bais Yisroel, and say unto them, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Yet in this avoteichem have made gidduf (blasphemy) against Me, in that they have been ma'al (unfaithful) to Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 6:13; NECHEMYAH 9:22–26; YIRMEYAH 3:6"> For when I had brought them into ha'aretz, for the which I lifted up Mine yad to give it to them, then they saw every high hill, and all the thick trees, and they offered there their zevakhim, and there they presented the ka'as (provocation) of their korban; there also they made their re'ach hannichoach, and poured out there their nesakhim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 16:24–25; YECHEZKEL 16:31"> Then I said unto them, What is the bamah whereunto ye go? And the shem whereof is called Bamah (High Place) unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 16:12; YIRMEYAH 7:26; SHOFETIM 2:19"> Therefore say unto Bais Yisroel, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Are ye ones making yourself tameh after the manner of avoteichem? And do ye commit zenut after their shikkutzim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:26; TEHILLIM 106:37–39; YIRMEYAH 7:31"> For when ye offer your gifts, when ye make your banim to pass through the eish, ye make yourselves tameh with all your gillulim (idols), even unto this day; and shall I be inquired of by you, O Bais Yisroel? As I live, saith Adonoi Hashem, I will not be inquired of by you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 11:5; YIRMEYAH 44:17; TEHILLIM 139:2"> And that which cometh into your mind shall not be at all — that ye say, We will be as the Goyim, as the mishpekhot of the countries, to serve wood and even (stone).</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 21:5; YECHEZKEL 8:18; YIRMEYAH 44:6"> As I live, saith Adonoi Hashem, surely with a yad chazakah, and with an outstretched zero'a, and with chemah (fury) poured out, will I rule over you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:38; AMOS 9:9–10; YECHEZKEL 34:16"> And I will bring you out from the peoples, and will gather you out of the countries wherein ye are scattered, with a yad chazakah, and with an outstretched zero'a, and with chemah (wrath) poured out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="HOSHEA 2:14; YIRMEYAH 2:35; YECHEZKEL 17:20"> And I will bring you into the midbar of the peoples, and there will I execute judgment with you face to face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:5–10; BAMIDBAR 11:1–35; YECHEZKEL 20:13"> Just as I judged avoteichem in the midbar of the Eretz Mitzrayim, so will I enter into judgment with you, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 33:13; VAYIKRA 27:32; MATTITYAHU 25:32–33"> And I will cause you to pass under the shevet (rod), and I will bring you into the bond of the brit (covenant);</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 13:9; AMOS 9:9–10; TEHILLIM 95:11"> And I will purge out from among you the rebels and the posh'im against Me; I will bring them forth out of the country where they sojourn as gerim, and they shall not enter into Eretz Yisroel; and ye shall know that I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="HOSHEA 4:17; YESHAYAH 1:13–15; YECHEZKEL 43:7"> As for you, O Bais Yisroel, thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Go ye, serve ye every one his gillulim, and hereafter since ye will not pay heed unto Me; and do not profane Shem Kodshi anymore with your gifts, and with your gillulim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="YESHAYAH 56:7; MALACHI 3:4; YESHAYAH 60:7"> For in Har Kodshi, in the mountain of the height of Yisroel, saith Adonoi Hashem, there shall all Bais Yisroel, all of them in ha'aretz, serve Me; there will I accept them, there will I require your terumot, the reshit of your offerings, with all your kodeshim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 28:25; YECHEZKEL 11:17; YECHEZKEL 38:23"> I will accept you as a re'ach hannichoach, when I bring you out from the peoples, and gather you out of the countries wherein ye have been scattered; and I will show Myself Kadosh among you before the eyes of the Goyim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 34:13; YECHEZKEL 38:23; YECHEZKEL 36:23–24"> And ye shall know that I am Hashem, when I shall bring you into Admat Yisroel, into the country for which I lifted up Mine yad to give it to avoteichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 6:9; HOSHEA 5:15; YIRMEYAH 31:18"> And there shall ye remember your drakhim, and all your doings, wherein ye have made yourselves tameh; and ye shall loathe yourselves in your own sight for all your evils that ye have committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 24:24; TEHILLIM 115:1; YECHEZKEL 20:9"> And ye shall know that I am Hashem when I have dealt with you for the sake of Shmi, not according to your wicked drakhim, nor according to your corrupt doings, O ye Bais Yisroel, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
