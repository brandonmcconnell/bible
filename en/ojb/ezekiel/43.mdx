<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 44:1; YECHEZKEL 42:15; YECHEZKEL 46:1"> Afterward he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the angelic being">a</a></sup>brought me to the sha'ar, even the sha'ar that looketh toward the east;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HISGALUS 18:1; YECHEZKEL 11:23; YESHAYAH 6:3"> And, hinei, the Kavod Elohei Yisroel came from the derech of the east; and His voice was like a kol mayim rabbim and ha'aretz was radiant with His Kavod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 9:5; YECHEZKEL 9:1; YIRMEYAH 1:10"> And it was according to the appearance of the vision which I saw, even according to the vision that I saw when He came to destroy the Ir; and the visions were like the vision that I saw by the Nahar Kevar; and I fell upon my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 10:18–19; YECHEZKEL 44:2; YECHEZKEL 43:2"> And the Kavod Hashem came into the Beis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="HaMikdash">b</a></sup>by the derech of the sha'ar whose view is toward the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:2–4; MELACHIM ALEF 8:10–11; YECHEZKEL 8:3"> So the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hakodesh">c</a></sup>lifted me up, and brought me into the khatzer hapenimi and, hinei, the Kavod Hashem filled the Beis.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="HaMikdash">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 40:3; YESHAYAH 66:6; HISGALUS 16:1"> And I heard Him speaking unto me out of the Beis;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="HaMikdash">e</a></sup>and the ish was standing beside me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 43:9; TEHILLIM 47:8; VAYIKRA 26:30"> And He said unto me, Ben Adam, this is the makom (place, home) of My Kisse, and the makom of the soles of My feet, where I will dwell in the midst of the Bnei Yisroel l'olam, and My Shem Kadosh, shall Bais Yisroel no more make tameh, neither they, nor their melachim, by their zenut (whoredom), nor by the pegarim (buried dead bodies) of their melachim in their high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 5:11; YECHEZKEL 8:3–16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:7"> In their setting of their threshold by My threshold, and their mezuzah by My mezuzah, and the wall between Me and them, they have even made tameh My Shem Kodesh by their to'evot that they have committed; therefore I have consumed them in Mine anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 43:7; YECHEZKEL 37:26–28; YECHEZKEL 18:30–31"> Now let them put away their zenut (whoredom), and the pegarim (buried dead bodies) of their melachim, far from Me, and I will dwell in the midst of them l'olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 40:4; YECHEZKEL 16:61; YECHEZKEL 16:63"> Thou Ben Adam, declare the Beis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="HaMikdash">f</a></sup>to Bais Yisroel, that they may be ashamed of their avonot; and let them measure the tachnit (perfection, design).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 36:27; YECHEZKEL 11:20; MATTITYAHU 28:20"> And if they be ashamed of all that they have done, make known to them the tzurah (form, fashion) of the Beis,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="HaMikdash">g</a></sup>and the techunah (arrangement) thereof, and the exits thereof, and the entrances thereof, and all the tzurot thereof, and all the chukkot thereof, and all the tzurot thereof, and all the torot thereof; and write it before their eyes, that they may be shomer over the whole tzurah thereof, and all the chukkot thereof, and do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 40:2; HISGALUS 21:27; TEHILLIM 93:5"> This is the torat HaBeis:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="HaMikdash">h</a></sup>upon the rosh hahar the entire boundary thereof round about shall be kodesh kadashim. Hinei, this is the torat HaBeis.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="HaMikdash">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 40:5; SHEMOT 27:1–8; YECHEZKEL 41:8"> And these are the middot HaMizbe'ach (measurements of the Mizbe'ach) in cubits. The cubit is a cubit and a handbreadth; even the base shall be a cubit (in height), and the width a cubit, and the moulding thereof by the edge thereof round about shall be a span; and this shall be for the gav (height, back) of the Mizbe'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And from the base upon ha'aretz even to the lower enclosure shall be two cubits, and the width one cubit; and from the lesser enclosure even to the greater enclosure shall be four cubits, and the width one cubit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 27:2; TEHILLIM 118:27; VAYIKRA 9:9"> So the ariel (surface of the altar, hearth) shall be four cubits; and from the hearth and upward shall be four keranot (horns).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 27:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:1; SHEMOT 38:1–2"> And the hearth shall be square, twelve cubits long, by twelve wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:26; YECHEZKEL 40:6; SHEMOT 25:25"> And the enclosure shall be square, fourteen cubits long and fourteen wide; and the moulding about it shall be half a cubit; and the base thereof shall be a cubit about; and its ma'alot (steps, stairs) shall look toward the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 40:29; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:4–12; YECHEZKEL 2:1"> And He said unto me, Ben Adam, thus saith Adonoi Hashem: These are the chukkot HaMizbe'ach in the Yom when they shall build it, to offer olah thereon, and to sprinkle dahm thereon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 40:46; YECHEZKEL 44:15; BAMIDBAR 16:40"> And thou shalt give to the Kohanim the Levi'im that are of the Zera Tzadok, which approach unto Me, to minister unto Me, saith Adonoi Hashem, a young bull for a chattat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="VAYIKRA 16:19; VAYIKRA 8:15; SHEMOT 29:12"> And thou shalt take of the dahm thereof, and put it on the four karnot of it, and on the four pinnot of the enclosure, and upon the moulding round about; thus shalt thou cleanse and make kapporah for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:14; VAYIKRA 4:12; VAYIKRA 8:17"> Thou shalt take the bull also of the chattat, and shall burn it in the mifkad (appointed place) of the Beis,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="HaMikdash">j</a></sup>outside the Mikdash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 43:25–26; YECHEZKEL 43:20; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19"> And on the second day thou shalt offer a male of the goats tamim (without blemish) for a chattat; and they shall purify the Mitzbe'ach, as they did purify it with the bull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:1"> When thou hast made an end of purifying it, thou shalt offer a young bull tamim (without blemish), and a ram out of the tzon (flock) tamim (without blemish).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="VAYIKRA 2:13; MARKOS 9:49–50; BAMIDBAR 18:19"> And thou shalt offer them before Hashem, and the Kohanim shall cast melach upon them, and they shall offer them up for an olah unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="VAYIKRA 8:33; SHEMOT 29:35–37; VAYIKRA 8:35"> Shivat yamim shalt thou prepare daily a se'ir (goat) for a chattat; they shall also prepare a young bull, and a ram out of the tzon (flock), temimim (ones without blemish).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:24; VAYIKRA 8:34; SHEMOT 32:29"> Shivat yamim shall they make kapporah for the Mizbe'ach and make it tahor and so consecrate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="VAYIKRA 9:1; YECHEZKEL 20:40–41; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5"> And when these yamim are expired, it shall be, that upon the Yom HaShemini, and so forward, the Kohanim shall make your olot upon the Mizbe'ach, and your Shelamim; and I will accept you, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
