</div>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HISGALUS 16:1; NACHUM 1:6; TEHILLIM 11:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will pour My fury upon Siyn,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the ma'oz (stronghold) of Mitzrayim;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will cut off the multitude of No.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 30:3; YECHEZKEL 29:15; YESHAYAH 10:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">At Tachpanches also the day shall be darkened,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I shall break there the yokes of Mitzrayim;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the ga'on (pride) of her strength shall cease in her;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as for her, an anan (a cloud) shall cover her,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and her banot shall go into captivity.</span><br /></span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="TEHILLIM 37:17; YIRMEYAH 37:7; MELACHIM BAIS 24:7"> Therefore thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Hinei, I am against Pharaoh Melech Mitzrayim, and will break his zero'ot (arms), the chazakah (strong), and that which was broken; and I will cause the cherev to fall out of his yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 30:26; YECHEZKEL 30:17–18; YECHEZKEL 29:12–13"> And I will scatter Mitzrayim among the Goyim, and will disperse them through the countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 30:25; TZEFANYAH 2:12; YESHAYAH 45:5"> And I will strengthen the zero'ot (arms) of Melech Bavel, and put My cherev in his yad; but I will break the zero'ot of Pharaoh, and he shall groan before him with the groanings of the mortally wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 29:21; YECHEZKEL 32:15; YECHEZKEL 29:16"> But I will strengthen the zero'ot (arms) of Melech Bavel, and the zero'ot (arms) of Pharaoh shall fall down; and they shall know that I am Hashem, when I shall put My cherev into the yad of Melech Bavel, and he shall stretch it out against Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
