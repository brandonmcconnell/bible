<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:1; YECHEZKEL 1:2; YIRMEYAH 39:2"> And it came to pass in the eleventh year, in the first day of the month, that the Devar Hashem came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YESHAYAH 23:1–18; YECHEZKEL 36:2; YOEL 3:4"> Ben adam, because that Tzor (Tyre) hath said against Yerushalayim, Aha, she is broken that was the daletot (gates) of the nations; she is turned over to me; I shall be filled, now that she is laid waste;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 51:42; YESHAYAH 5:30; MICHOH 4:11"> Therefore thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Hineni, I am against thee, O Tzor (Tyre), and will cause Goyim rabbim to come up against thee, like the yam (sea) causeth its waves to come up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YESHAYAH 23:11; AMOS 1:10; YECHEZKEL 26:9"> And they shall destroy the chomot (walls) of Tzor (Tyre), and break down her migdalim (towers); I will also scrape her aphar from her, and make her into a barren rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 29:19; YECHEZKEL 27:32; YECHEZKEL 25:7"> It shall be a place for the spreading of nets in the midst of the yam; for I have spoken it, saith Adonoi Hashem; and it shall become a plunder to the Goyim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 16:46; YECHEZKEL 26:8; YIRMEYAH 49:2"> And her banot which are in the sadeh shall be slain by the cherev; and they shall know that I am Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZRA 7:12; DANIEL 2:37; YECHEZKEL 23:24"> For thus saith Adonoi Hashem; Hineni, I will bring upon Tzor (Tyre) Nevukhadretzar Melech Bavel, a melech melachim, from the tzafon (north), with sus, and with chariots, and with parashim, and a kahal with much people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 21:22; YIRMEYAH 52:4; YIRMEYAH 32:24"> He shall slay with the cherev thy banot in the sadeh; and he shall heap up a siege mound against thee, and build a ramp against thee, and lift up the shield against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 26:15"> And he shall set battering rams against thy chomot (walls), and with his weapons he shall break down thy migdalim (towers).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 26:15; YECHEZKEL 27:28; YIRMEYAH 47:3"> By reason of the abundance of his susim their dust shall cover thee; thy chomot (walls) shall shake at the noise of the parash (war-horse), and of the galgal (wheel), and of the chariots, when he shall enter into thy she'arim, as invaders swarm into a breached ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 5:28; YESHAYAH 26:5; CHABAKUK 1:8"> With the hooves of his susim shall he trample down all thy streets; he shall slay thy people by the cherev, and thy strong pillars shall fall to the eretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 23:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:27; ZECHARYAH 9:3–4"> And they shall plunder thy riches, and pillage thy merchandise; and they shall break down thy chomot, and destroy thy batim (houses) of chemdah (desire);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., thy finest dwellings">a</a></sup>and they shall lay thy stones and thy timber and thy aphar in the midst of the mayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 7:34; YESHAYAH 23:16; YESHAYAH 14:11"> And I will cause the sound of thy shirim (songs) to cease; and the sound of thy kinnorot shall be no more heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MALACHI 1:4; IYOV 12:14; BAMIDBAR 23:19"> And I will make thee like the top of a barren rock; thou shalt be for spreading nets upon; thou shalt be rebuilt no more; for I Hashem have spoken it, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 27:35; YIRMEYAH 49:21; YECHEZKEL 26:18"> Thus saith Adonoi Hashem to Tzor (Tyre): Shall not the coastlands tremble fearfully at the sound of thy fall, when the chalal (wounded) cry, when the slaughter is made in the midst of thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 32:10; YONAH 3:6; IYOV 8:22"> Then all the nasi'im of the yam shall come down from their kisse'ot (thrones), and lay aside their robes, and put off their embroidered garments; they shall clothe themselves with trembling; they shall sit upon ha'aretz, and shall tremble at every moment, and be horrified at thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YESHAYAH 14:12; YECHEZKEL 19:1; SHMUEL BAIS 1:25–27"> And they shall take up a kinah (lament, funeral dirge) for thee, and say to thee:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">How thou hast perished,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that wast inhabited of seafaring men,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the renowned ir (city),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">which wast chazakah on the yam,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">she and her inhabitants, which spread their terror —</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all who live there.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="in Tyre">b</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 26:15; YESHAYAH 23:5–7; YECHEZKEL 27:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now shall the coastlands tremble</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the day of thy fall;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">indeed, the coastlands that are in the yam</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shall be traumatized at thy exile.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:7–8; YECHEZKEL 26:3; DANIEL 11:40"> For thus saith Adonoi Hashem: When I shall make thee a desolate ir, like the towns that are not inhabited; when I shall bring up the tehom upon thee, and the mayim harabbim (great waters) shall cover thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 27:13; LUKAS 10:15; ZECHARYAH 2:8"> When I shall bring thee down with them that descend into the bor (pit), with the people of old time, and shall set thee in the low parts of ha'aretz, in places desolate from of old, with them that go down to the bor (pit), so that thou be not inhabited; but I shall bestow splendor on Eretz Chayyim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 27:36; YECHEZKEL 28:19; HISGALUS 18:21"> I will make thee a terror, and thou shalt be no more; though thou be sought for, yet shalt thou never be found again, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
</div>
