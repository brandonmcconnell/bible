<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 3:16; YECHEZKEL 11:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:56"> Now it came to pass in the thirtieth year, in the fourth month, in the fifth day of the month, while I was among those of the Golus by the Nahar Kevar, that Shomayim was opened, and I saw mar'ot Elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 24:12–15; YECHEZKEL 8:1; YECHEZKEL 20:1"> In the fifth day of the month, which was the fifth year of the Golus of HaMelech Yoyakhin,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="593 B.C.E.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 3:14; YECHEZKEL 3:22; MELACHIM BAIS 3:15"> The Devar Hashem came expressly unto Yechezkel the kohen, ben Buzi, in Eretz Kasdim (Chaldeans) by the Nahar Kevar; and the Yad Hashem was there upon him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 1:27; YECHEZKEL 8:2; YIRMEYAH 23:19"> And I looked, and, hinei, a ruach se'arah came out of the tzafon, an anan gadol, and flashing fire, with brilliant light all about it, and out of the midst thereof something gleaming, shining out of the midst of the eish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HISGALUS 4:6–8; YECHEZKEL 1:26; HISGALUS 6:6"> Also out of the midst thereof came the demut (likeness) of four Chayos. And this was their appearance: they had the demut adam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 10:14; HISGALUS 4:7–8; YECHEZKEL 1:15"> And every one had arba'ah panim, and every one had arba kenafayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:15; DANIEL 10:6; TEHILLIM 104:4"> And their regalim were regel yesharah; and the sole of their feet was like the sole of a regel egel; and they gleamed like the shining of burnished nechoshet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 10:21; YECHEZKEL 10:11; YESHAYAH 6:6"> And they had the hands of an adam under their kenafayim on their four sides; and they four had their panim and their kenafayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 10:22; YECHEZKEL 10:11; YECHEZKEL 1:11–12"> Their kenafayim were joined one to another; they turned not as they went; they went every one face forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 10:14; HISGALUS 4:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:9–10"> As for the demut of their panim, they four had the face of an adam, and the face of an aryeh, on the right side; and they four had the face of a shor on the left side; they four also had the face of a nesher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 6:2; YECHEZKEL 1:23; YECHEZKEL 10:19"> Thus were their panim; and their kenafayim were stretched upward; two wings of every one were joined one to another, and two covered their geviyot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 1:9; YECHEZKEL 10:22; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:14"> And they went every one face forward; whither the Ruach was to go, they went; and they turned not when they went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HISGALUS 4:5; TEHILLIM 104:4; MATTITYAHU 28:3"> As for the demut of the Chayos, their appearance was like coals of eish burning, and like the appearance of lapidim (torches); it spread back and forth among the Chayos; and the eish was brilliant, and out of the eish went forth barak (lightning).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:27; ZECHARYAH 4:10; MARKOS 13:27"> And the Chayos ran and returned as the appearance of bazak (a flash of lightning).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DANIEL 7:9; YECHEZKEL 10:9; YECHEZKEL 1:19–21"> Now as I beheld the Chayos, hinei there was one Ofan ba'aretz etzel the Chayos, with its four panim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DANIEL 10:6; YECHEZKEL 10:9–11; SHEMOT 39:13"> The appearance of the Ofanim and their making was like unto the color of tarshish; and they four had one demut; and their appearance and their making was as it were an Ofan within an Ofan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 1:9; YECHEZKEL 1:12; YESHAYAH 55:11"> Arba'at (four) sidedly they went; and they turned not as they went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 10:12; HISGALUS 4:6; HISGALUS 4:8"> As for their rims, they were so high that they were dreadful; and their rims were mele'ot einayim saviv (full of eyes round about) the four of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 10:16; YECHEZKEL 10:19; TEHILLIM 103:20"> And when the Chayos went, the Ofanim went beside them; and when the Chayos were lifted up from ha'aretz, the Ofanim were lifted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 1:12; YECHEZKEL 10:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:32"> Whithersoever the Ruach was to go, they went, thither was the Ruach to go; and the Ofanim were lifted facing them; for Ruach HaChayah was in the Ofanim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 10:17; YECHEZKEL 1:19–20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:2"> When those went, these went; and when those stood still, these stood still; and when those were lifted up from ha'aretz, the Ofanim were lifted up facing them; for the Ruach HaChayah was in the Ofanim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 10:1; YECHEZKEL 1:26; HISGALUS 4:6"> And the demut of the raki'a upon the rashei HaChayah was as the color of terrible ice crystal, spread out upward above their rashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="IYOV 4:18; YECHEZKEL 1:11–12; TEHILLIM 89:7"> And under the raki'a were their kenafayim stretched out, the one toward the other; every one had two, which covered on this side, and every one had two, which covered on that side, their geviyot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 43:2; DANIEL 10:6; HISGALUS 1:15"> And when they went, I heard the kol of their kenafayim, like the kol of mayim rabbim, as the Kol Shaddai, the voice of a tumult, as the sound of a machaneh; when they stood still, they let down their kenafayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 1:22"> And there was a kol (voice) from the raki'a that was over their rosh, when they stood still, and had let down their kenafayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 10:1; SHEMOT 24:10; HISGALUS 1:13"> And above the raki'a that was over their rosh was the demut kisse, as the appearance of a precious stone, a sapphire; and upon the demut hakisse was the demut like the appearance of adam above upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 1:4; YECHEZKEL 8:2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:29"> And I saw something gleaming, shining out like the appearance of eish; inside it all around, from the appearance of his loins even upward, and from the appearance of his loins even downward, I saw something like the appearance of eish, and it had radiance all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="HISGALUS 10:1; HISGALUS 4:3; BERESHIS 17:3"> As the appearance of the keshet (rainbow) that is in the anan in yom hageshem, so was the appearance of the radiance all around.<br />This was the appearance of the demut Kavod Hashem. And when I saw it, I fell upon my face, and I heard a voice of one that spoke.</span>
</div>
