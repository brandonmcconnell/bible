<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Davar Hashem came again unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 19:1; YIRMEYAH 9:10; YECHEZKEL 28:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now, thou ben adam, take up a kinah (lamentation) for Tzor (Tyre);</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 27:4; YESHAYAH 23:11; YESHAYAH 23:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">And say unto Tzor (Tyre),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">O thou that dwell at the entrance of the yam,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">which art a merchant of the peoples for many coastlands,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Thus saith Adonoi Hashem;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">O Tzor (Tyre), thou hast said,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I am perfect in beauty.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 26:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thy borders are in the midst of the seas,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">thy bonim (builders) have perfected thy beauty.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 3:9; YESHAYAH 14:8; SHIR HASHIRIM 4:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have built all thy ship plank boards</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of beroshim (juniper) of Senir;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they have taken cedars from Levanon</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to make masts for thee.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 10:4; YESHAYAH 2:13; ZECHARYAH 11:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Of the oaks of Bashan</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">have they made thine oars;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">thy hull of pines inlaid with ivory,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they made from that brought out of the isles of Kittim (Cyprus).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 10:9; SHEMOT 25:4; BERESHIS 10:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Fine linen with embroidered work from Mitzrayim was that</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">which thou spreadest forth to be thy nes (sail);</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">blue and purple from the coasts of Elishah</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">was that which covered thee.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 10:18; MELACHIM ALEF 9:27; YECHEZKEL 27:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The inhabitants of Tzidon and Arvad</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">were thy oarsmen;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">thy chachamim, O Tzor (Tyre), that were in thee,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">were thy helmsmen.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 5:18; YEHOSHUA 13:5; TEHILLIM 83:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The elders of Geval and the chachamim thereof</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">were in thee thy caulkers to caulk thy seams;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all the oniyyot of the yam with their oarsmen</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">were in thee to market thy merchandise.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 38:5; YECHEZKEL 30:5; YESHAYAH 66:19"> They of Paras (Persia) and of Lud (Lydia) and of Put (Libya) were in thine army, thy anshei hamilchamah; they hanged the mogen and kova (helmet) in thee; they set forth thy splendor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 27:3–4"> The Bnei Arvad with thine army were upon thy chomot (walls) all around, and the men of Gammad were in thy migdalim (towers); they hung their shields upon thy chomot (walls) all around; they have made thy beauty perfect.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 10:4; YESHAYAH 23:10; YECHEZKEL 38:13"> Tarshish was thy merchant by reason of the multitude of all kind of riches; with kesef, barzel (iron), bedil (tin), and oferet (lead), they traded for thy goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 10:2; YESHAYAH 66:19; YECHEZKEL 39:1"> Yavan, Tuval, and Meshech, they were thy merchants; they traded the nefesh adam and kelim (vessels) of nechoshet for thy merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 10:3; YECHEZKEL 38:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 1:6"> They of the Bais Togarmah traded for thy wares with susim and parash and peradim (mules).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 10:7; HISGALUS 18:12; MELACHIM ALEF 10:22"> The Bnei Dedan were thy merchants; many coastlands were the market of thine yad; they brought thee for a payment karnot (tusks) of shen (ivory) and ebony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHOFETIM 10:6; YECHEZKEL 28:13; SHMUEL BAIS 10:6"> Aram (Syria) was thy merchant by reason of the multitude of the goods of thy making; they gave thee for thy wares emeralds, purple, and embroidered work, and fine linen, and coral, and rubies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHOFETIM 11:33; MELACHIM ALEF 5:9; YIRMEYAH 8:22"> Yehudah, and Eretz Yisroel, they were thy merchants; they traded for thy merchandise chittim (wheat) of Minnit, and balsam oil, and devash, and shemen, and balm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YESHAYAH 7:8; BERESHIS 15:2; YECHEZKEL 47:16–18"> Damascus was thy merchant in the multitude of the wares of thy making, for the multitude of all riches; in the yayin of Chelbon, and white wool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 30:23–24; TEHILLIM 45:8; SHIR HASHIRIM 4:13–14"> Vedan also and Yavan (Greece) provided for your wares with fine yarn; wrought iron, cassia, and cane, were among thy merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:3; YECHEZKEL 27:15"> Dedan was thy merchant in saddle blankets for riding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YESHAYAH 60:7; BERESHIS 25:13; MELACHIM ALEF 10:15"> Arabia, and all the nasi'im of Kedar, they traded with thee in lambs, and rams, and goats; in these were they thy merchants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BERESHIS 10:7; YESHAYAH 60:6; TEHILLIM 72:10"> The merchants of Sheva and Ra'amah, they were thy merchants; they traded as peddlers with choicest spices, and with all kinds of even yekarah (precious stones), and zahav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 19:12; YESHAYAH 37:12; AMOS 1:5"> Charan, and Kanneh, and Eden, the merchants of Sheva, Ashur (Assyria), and Kilmad, were thy merchants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 2:8"> These were thy merchants in choice things, in purple clothes, and embroidered work, and in chests of multicolored apparel, bound with chavalim (cords) and cedar boxed in thy marketplace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YESHAYAH 2:16; TEHILLIM 48:7; YESHAYAH 23:14"> The oniyyot of Tarshish were carriers of thy merchandise; and thou wast filled, and made very glorious in the midst of the yamim (seas).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="TEHILLIM 48:7; YECHEZKEL 26:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thy oarsmen have brought thee</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">into mayim rabbim;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the east wind hath broken thee</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the midst of the yamim (seas).</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 26:10; SHEMOT 15:14; YECHEZKEL 31:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The migroshot (open pasturelands) shall shake</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">at the sound of the cry of thy seamen.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 26:16; YECHEZKEL 32:10; HISGALUS 18:17–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">And all that handle the oar,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the mariners, and all the sailors of the yam,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shall come down from their oniyyot,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they shall stand upon the shore;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 6:26; SHMUEL BAIS 1:2; EKHAH 2:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">And shall cause their kol (voice) to be heard because of thee,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and shall cry bitterly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and shall cast up aphar (dust) upon their heads,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they shall roll themselves in the ashes;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 7:18; YESHAYAH 22:12; YESHAYAH 15:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they shall</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="in mourning">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">make themselves utterly bald for thee,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and gird themselves with sackcloth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and shall weep for thee</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">mar nefesh and bitter mourning.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 26:17; HISGALUS 18:18; YECHEZKEL 27:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">And in their wailing they shall take up a kinah (lamentation) for thee,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and lament over thee, saying:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">What city is like Tzor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">destroyed in the midst of the yam?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="HISGALUS 18:19; YECHEZKEL 27:3; YECHEZKEL 28:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">When thy wares went forth by sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">thou filledst many people;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">thou didst enrich the melachim of ha'aretz</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with the multitude of thy riches and of thy merchandise.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 27:26–27; ZECHARYAH 9:3–4; YECHEZKEL 26:19–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the time when thou shalt be broken by the yamim (seas)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the depths of the mayim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">thy merchandise and all thy kahal (company)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the midst of thee shall fall.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 32:10; YESHAYAH 23:6; YECHEZKEL 26:15–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the inhabitants of the coastlands</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shall be astonished at thee,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their melachim shall be very afraid,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they shall be troubled in their countenance.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 18:16; TEHILLIM 37:10; TZEFANYAH 2:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">The socharim (merchants) among the peoples shall hiss at thee;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">thou shalt be a horror,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and ad olam (forever) thou shalt be no more.</span></span>
