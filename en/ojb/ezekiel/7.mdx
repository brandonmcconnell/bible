<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Moreover the Devar Hashem came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="AMOS 8:2; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:7; YECHEZKEL 7:3"> Also, thou ben adam, thus saith Adonoi Hashem unto Eretz Yisroel: Ketz (End), the End is come upon the four corners of ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:30; YECHEZKEL 5:13; YECHEZKEL 33:20"> Now is the Ketz (End, Doom) come upon thee, and I will send Mine anger upon thee, and will judge thee according to thy drakhim, and will repay thee for all thine to'avot (abominations).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 6:7; YECHEZKEL 11:21; YECHEZKEL 5:11"> And Mine eye shall not pity thee, neither will I spare; but I will repay thy drakhim upon thee, and thine to'avot shall be in the midst of thee; and ye shall know that I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NACHUM 1:9; MELACHIM BAIS 21:12–13; YECHEZKEL 5:9"> Thus saith Adonoi Hashem; A ra'ah, a singular ra'ah, hinei, has come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 13:7; YECHEZKEL 7:3; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5"> Ketz is come, the ketz is come; it is ripe for thee; hinei, it is come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 12:28; YECHEZKEL 7:12; YESHAYAH 22:5"> The Tzefirah (the Web) is come upon thee, O thou that dwellest in ha'aretz; the time is come, the Yom Mehumah (Day of Panic) is near, and not of rejoicing in the harim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 9:8; YECHEZKEL 14:19; YECHEZKEL 20:8"> Now will I shortly pour out My fury upon thee, and spend Mine anger upon thee; and I will judge thee according to thy drakhim, and will repay thee for all thine to'avot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HISGALUS 20:13; MICHOH 6:9; YESHAYAH 9:13"> And Mine eye shall not pity, neither will I spare; I will repay thee according to thy drakhim and thine to'avot that are in the midst of thee; and ye shall know that I am Hashem that striketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YESHAYAH 10:5; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:3"> Hinei, the Yom, hinei, it is come; The Tzefirah (Web) is gone forth; the mateh (rod) hath blossomed, zadon (pride) hath budded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TZEFANYAH 1:18; YECHEZKEL 7:23; YESHAYAH 59:6–8"> Chamas is risen up into mateh resha (a rod of wickedness); none of them shall remain, nor of their tumult, nor of any of theirʼs; none of their preeminence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 5:13–14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:29–31; YECHEZKEL 7:5–7"> The time is come, the day draweth near; let not the koneh (buyer) rejoice, nor the mokher (seller) mourn; for charon (wrath) is upon all the multitude thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:24–28; VAYIKRA 25:31; IYOV 15:25"> For the mokher (seller) shall not return to that which is sold, as long as his life is alive; for the chazon (vision) concerns the whole multitude thereof, which shall not be reversed; neither shall any strengthen himself in the avon of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 4:5; YIRMEYAH 7:20; YIRMEYAH 51:27"> They have blown the trumpet, even to make all ready; but none goeth to the milchamah; for My charon (wrath) is upon all the multitude thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 14:18; YECHEZKEL 5:12; EKHAH 1:20"> The cherev is outside, and the dever and the ra'av (famine) inside; he that is in the sadeh shall die with the cherev; and he that is in the Ir, ra'av (famine) and dever shall devour him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YESHAYAH 59:11; YESHAYAH 38:14; EZRA 9:15"> But they that survive of them shall escape, and shall be on the harim like doves of the ge'ayot (valleys), all of them moaning, every one for his avon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 21:7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:12; YIRMEYAH 6:24"> All hands shall go limp, and all birkayim (knees) shall be weak as mayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="AMOS 8:10; YESHAYAH 15:2–3; YESHAYAH 3:24"> They shall also gird themselves with sackcloth, and terror shall cover them; and bushah (shame) shall be upon all faces, and shaved scalps<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the sign of mourning">a</a></sup>upon all their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MISHLE 11:4; TZEFANYAH 1:18; LUKAS 12:19–20"> They shall cast their kesef in the streets, and their zahav shall be like niddah; their kesef and their zahav shall not be able to save them in the Yom Evrat Hashem(Day of the Wrath of Hashem); they shall not satisfy their nefashot, neither fill their stomachs; for it has become the stumbling block of their avon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 24:21; YIRMEYAH 7:30; YECHEZKEL 8:15–16"> As for the beauty of his jewelry, he set it in ga'on (pride); but they made the tzelem of their to'avot and of their shikkutzim therein; therefore I will make it like niddah to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 24:13; TEHILLIM 74:2–8; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:18–19"> And I will give it into the hands of the zarim (strangers) for plunder, and to the resha'im of ha'aretz for loot; and they shall defile it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 18:17; TEHILLIM 74:18–23; TEHILLIM 74:10–11"> My face will I turn also from them, and they shall desecrate My treasure; for the robbers shall enter into it,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., Yerushalayim">b</a></sup>and defile it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 9:9; YIRMEYAH 27:2; YECHEZKEL 11:6"> Make a chain; for ha'aretz is full of mishpat damim, and the ir is full of chamas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 28:7; TEHILLIM 106:41; YECHEZKEL 21:31"> Therefore I will bring the worst of the Goyim, and they shall possess their batim (houses); I will also make the ga'on (pride) of the mighty to cease; and their mikdashim (holy places) shall be defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 8:15–16; YESHAYAH 57:21; EKHAH 4:17–18"> Terror cometh; and they shall seek shalom, and there shall be none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 4:20; YECHEZKEL 14:1; MICHOH 3:6"> Disaster shall come upon disaster, and rumor shall be upon rumor; then shall they seek a chazon (vision) from the navi; but the torah shall perish from the kohen, and etzah from the zekenim (elders).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:30; YECHEZKEL 7:4–8; YECHEZKEL 26:16"> HaMelech shall mourn, and the nasi shall be clothed with despair, and the hands of the am ha'aretz shall fearfully tremble; I will do unto them according to their derech, and according to their mishpatim will I judge them; and they shall know that I am Hashem.</span>
