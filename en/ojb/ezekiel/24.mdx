<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 8:1; YECHEZKEL 20:1; YECHEZKEL 29:17"> Again in the ninth year, in the tenth month, in the tenth day of the month, the Devar Hashem came unto me, saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., note Fast of Tevet">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 39:1; YIRMEYAH 52:4; MELACHIM BAIS 25:1"> Ben adam, write thee the shem of the day, even of this very day; Melech Bavel laid his siege against Yerushalayim this very day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 11:3; YECHEZKEL 17:2; YECHEZKEL 20:49"> And utter a mashal unto the bais hameri (the rebellious house), and say unto them, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Set on the siyr (cooking pot), set it on,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and also pour mayim into it;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MICHOH 3:2–3; YECHEZKEL 22:18–22; MATTITYAHU 7:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Gather the pieces of meat thereof into it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">even every good piece, the thigh, and the shoulder;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">fill it with the choice atzmot (bones).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 52:24–27; YIRMEYAH 52:10; YIRMEYAH 39:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Take the choice of the tzon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and arrange also the atzmot at its bottom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and make it boil well,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and let them cook the atzmot of it therein.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 22:2; NACHUM 3:1; OVADYAH 1:11"> Therefore thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Oy to the ir hadamim (bloody city), to the siyr (pot) whose scum is therein, and whose scum has not been removed from it! Remove it piece by piece; no goral has fallen on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="VAYIKRA 17:13; DEVARIM 12:16; IYOV 16:18"> For her dahm is in the midst of her; she set it upon an exposed rock; she poured it not upon the ground, to cover it with aphar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:21–22; HISGALUS 18:5–10; YECHEZKEL 22:30–31"> That it might cause chemah (fury) to come up to take vengeance; I have set her dahm upon the exposed rock, that it should not be covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 24:6; CHABAKUK 2:12; YESHAYAH 31:9"> Therefore thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Oy to the ir hadamim (bloody city)! I will even make the pyre great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EKHAH 2:16; YIRMEYAH 17:3; EKHAH 1:10"> Heap on wood, kindle the eish, cook the basar, and spice it well, and let the atzmot be burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 21:10; MALACHI 4:1; YESHAYAH 1:25"> Then set it empty upon the coals thereof, that the nechoshet of it may be hot, and may burn, and that the filthiness of it may be melted in it, that the scum of it may be consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 9:5; YIRMEYAH 2:13; YIRMEYAH 5:3"> All efforts are wearied, &#91;scum&#93; is not removed, and her great scum went not forth out of her; in the eish shall be her scum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 22:24; YECHEZKEL 5:13; TZEFANYAH 3:2"> In thy tum'a is zimmah; because I have made thee tahor, and thou wast not tahor, thou shalt not be made tahor from thy tum'a any more, until I have caused My chemah (fury, wrath) to subside upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:27; YECHEZKEL 18:30; YESHAYAH 3:11"> I Hashem have spoken it; it shall come to pass, and I will do it; I will not go back, neither will I spare, neither will I relent: according to thy drakhim, and according to thy doings, shall they judge thee, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Also the Devar Hashem came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 22:10; YIRMEYAH 13:17; SHIR HASHIRIM 7:10"> Ben adam, hineni, I take away from thee the makhmad (darling delight) of thine eynayim with a magefah (stroke); yet neither shalt thou wail nor weep, neither shall thy dema'ot (tears) run down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HOSHEA 9:4; SHMUEL BAIS 15:30; VAYIKRA 10:6"> Forbear to cry, make no evel (mourning) for the mesim (dead ones); bind the turban of thine rosh upon thee, and put thy sandals upon thy feet, and cover not thy safam (mustache), and eat not lechem anashim (bread of sorrow).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:29–30"> So I spoke unto HaAm (the people) in the boker (morning); and at erev (evening) my isha (wife) died; and I did in the boker (morning),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., the next morning after her death the previous evening">b</a></sup>just as I was commanded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 12:9; YECHEZKEL 37:18; MALACHI 3:7–8"> And haam (the people) said unto me, Wilt thou not tell us what these things are to us, that thou art so doing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then I answered them. The Devar Hashem came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 23:47; YECHEZKEL 23:25; TEHILLIM 27:4"> Speak unto Bais Yisroel, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Hineni, I will desecrate My Mikdash, the ga'on (pride) of your power, the makhmad of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="plural">c</a></sup>eynayim, and the very passion of your nefesh; and your banim and your banot whom ye have left behind shall fall by the cherev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:64; YIRMEYAH 16:4–7; YECHEZKEL 24:16–17"> And ye shall do just as I have done: ye shall not cover your safam (mustache), nor eat lechem anashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 33:10; YECHEZKEL 4:17; VAYIKRA 26:39"> And your turbans shall be upon your heads, and your sandals upon your feet; ye shall not wail nor weep; but ye shall waste away for your avonot, and groan one toward another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 4:3; YESHAYAH 20:3; LUKAS 11:29–30"> Thus Yechezkel is unto you as a mofet;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Zech 3:8; Isa 8:18">d</a></sup>according to all that he hath done shall ye do; and when this cometh, ye shall know that I am Adonoi Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 24:21; TEHILLIM 48:2; YIRMEYAH 11:22"> Also, thou ben adam, shall it not be in the day when I take from them their ma'oz (stronghold), the joy of their tiferet (glory), the makhmad (desire) of their eynayim, and that on which their nefesh is uplifted — even their banim and their banot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="IYOV 1:15–19; YECHEZKEL 33:21–22; SHMUEL ALEF 4:12–18"> That he that escapeth in that day shall come unto thee, to cause thee to hear the news<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="of the fall of Jerusalem">e</a></sup>with thine oznayim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 33:22; YECHEZKEL 29:21; LUKAS 21:15"> In that day shall thy mouth be opened to him which is escaped, and thou shalt speak, and be no more mute; and thou shalt be a mofet unto them; and they shall know that I am Hashem.</span>
</div>
