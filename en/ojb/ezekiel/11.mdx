<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 3:14; YECHEZKEL 11:24; YECHEZKEL 11:13"> Moreover the Ruach lifted me up, and brought me unto the Sha'ar Beis Hashem hakadmoni (eastern gate of the Beis HaMikdash of Hashem), and hinei at the petach of the Sha'ar five and twenty ish; among whom I saw Ya'azanyah Ben Azur, and Pelatyahu Ben Benayahu, sarim of HaAm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YESHAYAH 30:1; YIRMEYAH 5:5; TEHILLIM 2:1–2"> Then said He unto me, Ben adam, these are the anashim that plot evil, and give atzat rah (evil counsel) in this ir,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 12:27; YECHEZKEL 12:22; YECHEZKEL 11:7–11"> Which say, The end is not near; let us build batim (houses): this ir is the caldron, and we are the basar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:46–47; YECHEZKEL 21:2; YECHEZKEL 3:2–15"> Therefore prophesy against them, prophesy, O ben adam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 17:10; YIRMEYAH 16:17; HISGALUS 2:23"> And the Ruach Hashem fell upon me, and said unto me, Speak: Thus saith Hashem; Thus have ye said, O Bais Yisroel: for I know the things that come into your ruach, every one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 7:23; YECHEZKEL 22:27; YECHEZKEL 22:12"> Ye have multiplied your slain in this ir, and ye have filled the streets thereof with the slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 24:3–13; MICHOH 3:2–3; MELACHIM BAIS 25:18–22"> Therefore thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Your slain whom ye have placed in the midst of it, they are the basar, and this ir is the caldron; but I will bring you forth out of the midst of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MISHLE 10:24; YESHAYAH 24:17–18; IYOV 3:25"> Ye have feared the cherev; and I will bring a cherev upon you, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 5:8; TEHILLIM 106:41; DEVARIM 28:36"> And I will bring you out of the midst thereof, and deliver you into the yad of zarim, and will execute shefatim (judgments) among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 14:25; YIRMEYAH 52:9–10; MELACHIM BAIS 25:19–21"> Ye shall fall by the cherev; I will judge you at the border of Yisroel; and ye shall know that I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 11:3; YECHEZKEL 11:7–10"> This ir shall not be your caldron, neither shall ye be the basar in the midst thereof; but I will judge you at the border of Yisroel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 8:10; YECHEZKEL 8:16; YECHEZKEL 8:14"> And ye shall know that I am Hashem; for ye have not walked in My chukkot, neither executed My mishpatim, but have done after the mishpatim of the Goyim that are all around you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 11:1; YECHEZKEL 9:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:5"> And it came to pass, when I prophesied, that Pelatyahu Ben Benayah died. Then I fell down upon my face, and cried out with a kol gadol, and said, Ah, Adonoi Hashem! Wilt Thou make a full end of the she'erit (remnant) Yisroel?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Again the Devar Hashem came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 33:24; YESHAYAH 65:5; YOCHANAN 16:2"> Ben adam, thy brethren, even thy relatives, the anashim of thy geulah, and kol Bais Yisroel wholly, are those of whom the inhabitants of Yerushalayim have said, They<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the Exiles">a</a></sup>have gone far from Hashem; unto us is this land given in possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:14; DEVARIM 30:3–4; TEHILLIM 90:1"> Therefore say, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem; Although I have cast them far off among the Goyim, and although I have scattered them among the countries, yet will I be to them as a mikdash me'at (little holy place) in the countries where they went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 28:25; YIRMEYAH 3:18; YECHEZKEL 34:13"> Therefore say, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem; I will even gather you from the nations, and assemble you out of the countries where ye have been scattered, and I will give you Eretz Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 37:23; YECHEZKEL 5:11; YECHEZKEL 42:7–8"> And they shall come there, and they shall remove all the shikkutzim thereof and all the to'avot (abominations) thereof from her.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Eretz Yisroel">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 36:26–27; YIRMEYAH 31:33; TEHILLIM 51:10"> And I will give them a lev echad, and I will put a ruach chadashah (new regenerated spirit) within you; and I will remove the lev haeven (heart of stone) out of their basar, and will give them a lev basar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 14:11; TEHILLIM 105:45; YIRMEYAH 30:22"> That they may walk in My chukkot, and be shomer over Mine mishpatim, and do them; and they shall be My people, and I will be their Elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 9:10; KOHELET 11:9; YIRMEYAH 1:16"> But as for them whose lev walketh after the lev of their shikkutzim and their to'avot, I will repay their derech upon their own rosh, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 10:19; YECHEZKEL 1:19–20"> Then did the Keruvim lift up their wings, and the Ofanim (wheels) beside them; and the Kavod Elohei Yisroel was high above them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 14:4; YECHEZKEL 8:4; YECHEZKEL 10:4"> And the Kavod Hashem went up from the midst of the Ir, and stood upon the har which is on the east side of the ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 3:12; YECHEZKEL 8:3; YECHEZKEL 11:1"> Afterward the Ruach lifted me up, and brought me in a mar'eh (vision) &#91;inspired&#93; by the Ruach Elohim into Kasdim (Chaldea, Babylon), to them of the Golus. So the mareh (vision) that I had seen went up from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 3:4; YECHEZKEL 3:27; YECHEZKEL 3:17"> Then I spoke unto them of the Golus all the things that Hashem had showed me.</span>
</div>
