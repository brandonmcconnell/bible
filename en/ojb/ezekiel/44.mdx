<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 43:1; YECHEZKEL 40:17; YECHEZKEL 42:14"> Then he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the angelic being">a</a></sup>brought me back to the derech sha'ar HaMikdash HaKhitzon, which looketh toward the east; and it was shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 43:2–4; SHEMOT 24:10; YESHAYAH 6:1–5"> Then said Hashem unto me: This sha'ar shall be shut, it shall not be opened, and no ish shall enter in by it; because Hashem Elohei Yisroel, hath entered in by it, therefore it shall be shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 46:2; YECHEZKEL 34:24; YECHEZKEL 37:25"> It is for the Nasi; the Nasi,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="see 34:23-24; 37:24-25, a “Dovid” or Messianic figure">b</a></sup>he only shall sit in it to eat lechem before Hashem; he shall enter by the derech ulam hasha'ar, and shall exit by its derech.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 1:28; HISGALUS 1:17; YECHEZKEL 3:23"> Then he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="the angelic being">c</a></sup>brought me the derech sha'ar hatzafon to the front of the Beis;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="HaMikdash">d</a></sup>and I looked, and, hinei, the Kavod Hashem filled the Beis Hashem; and I fell upon my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 40:4; YECHEZKEL 43:10–11; DEVARIM 12:32"> And Hashem said unto me, Ben Adam, mark well, and behold with thine eyes, and hear with thine ears all that I say unto thee concerning all the chukkot Beis Hashem, and all the torot thereof; and mark well the entrances of the Beis,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="HaMikdash">e</a></sup>with every exit of the Mikdash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 3:9; YECHEZKEL 45:9; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:3"> And thou shalt say to the meri (rebellious), even to the Bais Yisroel, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem; O ye Bais Yisroel, let it suffice you of all your to'avot (abominations),</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="VAYIKRA 22:25; YIRMEYAH 9:26; YECHEZKEL 44:9"> In that ye have admitted into My Mikdash bnei nechor (foreigners), arelim in lev, and arelim in basar, to be in My Mikdash, to defile it, even My Beis, when ye offer My lechem, the chelev and the dahm, and they have broken My Brit (Covenant) because of all your to'avot (abominations).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 44:14; BAMIDBAR 18:3–5; YECHEZKEL 40:45–46"> And ye have not been shomer over the mishmeret of Mine Kadashim; but ye have set as shomrim of My mishmeret in My Mikdash any for yourselves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOEL 3:17; YECHEZKEL 44:7; ZECHARYAH 14:21"> Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: No ben nechor, arel lev, nor arel basar, shall enter into My Mikdash, of any ben nechor that is among the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 22:26; YECHEZKEL 48:11; MELACHIM BAIS 23:8–9"> And the Levi'im that went far away from Me, when Yisroel went astray, which went astray away from Me after their gillulim (idols); they shall even bear their avon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 16:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:34; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:17"> Yet they shall be mesharetim (ministers) in My Mikdash, pekuddot (the ones who have charge) at the Sha'arei HaBeis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="HaMikdash">f</a></sup>and mesharetim in HaBeis;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="HaMikdash">g</a></sup>they shall do the shochet slaughter of the olah (burnt offering) and the zevach for the people, and they shall stand before them to minister unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 16:10–16; TEHILLIM 106:26; YECHEZKEL 14:3–4"> Because they ministered unto them before their gillulim, and caused Bais Yisroel a michshol avon; therefore have I lifted up Mine Yad against them, saith Adonoi Hashem, and nas'u (they shall bear) their avon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 18:3; MELACHIM BAIS 23:9; YECHEZKEL 16:61"> And they shall not come near unto Me, to do the office of a Kohen unto Me, nor to come near to any of My kadashim, in the Kodesh HaKodashim; but nas'u their shame, and their to'avot which they have committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:28–32; BAMIDBAR 18:4"> But I will make them Shomrei Meshmeret HaBeis,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="HaMikdash">h</a></sup>for all the avodah thereof, and for all that shall be done therein.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:8; YECHEZKEL 48:11; YECHEZKEL 44:7"> But the Kohanim the Levi'im, the Bnei Tzadok, that were shomer over the Mishmeret of My Mikdash when the Bnei Yisroel went astray from Me, they shall come near to Me to minister unto Me, and they shall stand before Me to offer unto Me the chelev and the dahm, saith Adonoi Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 41:22; MALACHI 1:12; BAMIDBAR 18:7–8"> They shall enter into My Mikdash, and they shall come near to My Shulchan, to minister unto Me, and they shall be shomer over My mishmeret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:8; SHEMOT 28:43; SHEMOT 39:27–29"> And it shall come to pass, when they enter in at the Sha'arei HeKhatzer HaPenimit, they shall be clothed with linen; and no tzemer shall come upon them, while they minister at the Sha'arei HeKhatzer HaPenimit, within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 39:28; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:4–10; YESHAYAH 3:20"> They shall have linen turbans upon their rosh, and shall have linen breeches upon their loins; they shall not gird themselves with that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="clothing">i</a></sup>with which one perspires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 46:20; VAYIKRA 6:27; VAYIKRA 6:10–11"> And when they go forth into HaKhatzer HaKhitzonah, even into the Khatzer HaKhitzonah to HaAm, they shall take off their garments wherein they ministered, and lay them in the lishkhot hakodesh (holy chambers) and they shall put on begadim acharim; and they shall not set apart as kodesh HaAm with their begadim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:14; BAMIDBAR 6:5; VAYIKRA 21:5–24"> Neither shall they shave their rosh, nor allow their hair to grow long; they shall keep trimmed their rosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="VAYIKRA 10:9; LETTER TO TITOS 1:7–8; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:23"> Neither shall any Kohen drink yayin, when they enter into the Khatzer HaPenimit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="VAYIKRA 21:7; VAYIKRA 21:13–14; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:2"> Neither shall they take for their nashim an almanah, nor a gerushah; but they shall take betulot of the zera Bais Yisroel, or an almanah who was almanah of a Kohen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 22:26; VAYIKRA 10:10–11; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:24–25"> And they shall teach My people the difference between the kodesh and chol, and cause them to discern between the tameh and the tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:4; YECHEZKEL 22:26; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:8–10"> And in a riv they shall stand as shofet; and they shall judge it according to My mishpatim; and they shall adjudicate them in accordance with My torot and My chukkot in all Mine mo'adim; and they shall be shomer to keep My Shabbatot kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:16; VAYIKRA 22:4; MATTITYAHU 8:21–22"> And they shall come near no met adam to make themselves tameh; but for av, or for em, or for ben, or for bat, for ach, or for achot that hath had no ish, they may make themselves tameh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:13–14; BAMIDBAR 19:11–14; BAMIDBAR 6:10–21"> And after he is made tahor, they shall reckon unto him shivat yamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 6:9–11; YECHEZKEL 44:17; VAYIKRA 4:3–35"> And in the yom that he goeth into the Kodesh, unto the Khatzer HaPenimit, to minister in the Kodesh, he shall offer his chattat, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 13:33; BAMIDBAR 18:20; DEVARIM 18:1–2"> And it shall be unto them for a nachalah; I am their nachalah; and ye shall give them no achuzzah in Yisroel; I am their achuzzah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 18:14; VAYIKRA 27:21; VAYIKRA 27:28"> They shall eat the minchah, and the chattat, and the asham; and kol cherem in Yisroel shall be theirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 10:35–37; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 31:10; DEVARIM 18:4"> And the reshit kol bikkurim of all things, and every terumah of all, of every sort of your terumot, shall belong to the Kohanim; ye shall also give unto the Kohen the reshit of your dough, that he may cause the brocha to rest on thine bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="VAYIKRA 22:8; SHEMOT 22:31; DEVARIM 14:21"> The Kohanim shall not eat of any thing nevelah, or terefah, whether it be oph or behemah.</span>
</div>
