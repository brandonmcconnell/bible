<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 22:1; YECHEZKEL 21:1; YECHEZKEL 34:1"> Moreover the Devar Hashem came unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 6:2; BERESHIS 32:3; YECHEZKEL 25:8"> Ben adam, set thy face against Har Se'ir, and prophesy against it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 6:12; YECHEZKEL 35:7; YECHEZKEL 6:14"> And say unto it, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Hineni, O Har Se'ir, I am against thee, and I will stretch out Mine yad against thee, and I will make thee a desolation and a waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 35:9; MALACHI 1:3–4; YOEL 3:19"> I will lay thy towns waste, and thou shalt be desolate, and thou shalt know that I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 137:7; OVADYAH 1:10–16; AMOS 1:11"> Because thou hast had an eivat olam (ancient enmity), and delivered over the Bnei Yisroel to the power of the cherev at the time of their calamity, in the time of their avon ketz (final punishment).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YESHAYAH 63:2–6; HISGALUS 18:24; OVADYAH 1:15"> Therefore, as I live, saith Adonoi Hashem, I will turn thee unto dahm, and dahm shall pursue thee; since thou hast not hated dahm, even dahm shall pursue thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 29:11; YECHEZKEL 35:3; SHOFETIM 5:6–7"> Thus will I make Har Se'ir most desolate, and cut off from it him that goeth and him that cometh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 31:12; YECHEZKEL 32:4–5; YECHEZKEL 39:4–5"> And I will fill his harim with his slain; in thy geva'ot (hills), and in thy ge'ayot (valleys) and in all thy ravines, shall they fall that are slain with the cherev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 25:13; MALACHI 1:3–4; YECHEZKEL 6:7"> I will make thee shimemot olam (eternal desolations), and thy towns shall not be inhabited; and ye shall know that I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 36:5; TEHILLIM 83:4–12; YECHEZKEL 48:35"> Because thou hast said, These two Goyim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Israel and Judah">a</a></sup>and these two countries shall be mine, and we will possess it; although Hashem was there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TEHILLIM 9:16; MATTITYAHU 7:2; TEHILLIM 73:17–18"> Therefore, as I live, saith Adonoi Hashem, I will even do according to thine anger, and according to thine kina (jealousy) which thou hast showed in thy hatred against them; and I will make Myself known among them, when I have judged thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 36:2; TEHILLIM 83:12; YECHEZKEL 6:7"> And thou shalt know that I am Hashem. I have heard all thy insults which thou hast spoken against the harim of Yisroel, saying, They are laid desolate, they are given to us to consume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DANIEL 11:36; YESHAYAH 36:20; YIRMEYAH 29:23"> Thus with your mouth ye have boasted of your greatness against Me, have multiplied your devarim against Me; I have heard them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 51:48; YESHAYAH 14:7–8; YESHAYAH 65:13–15"> Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: When kol ha'aretz (the whole earth) rejoiceth, I will make thee desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 35:3–4; EKHAH 4:21; YESHAYAH 34:5–6"> As thou didst rejoice at the nachalah of the Bais Yisroel, because it was desolate, so will I do unto thee; thou shalt be desolate, O Har Se'ir, and all Edom, even all of it; and they shall know that I am Hashem.</span>
