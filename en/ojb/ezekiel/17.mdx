<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Devar Hashem came unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:49; MATTITYAHU 13:35; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:12"> Ben adam, put forth an allegory, and speak a mashal unto the Bais Yisroel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HOSHEA 8:1; YIRMEYAH 48:40; YECHEZKEL 17:7"> And say, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: A nesher hagadol with great kenafayim (wings), long pinions, full of plumage, which had various colors, came unto Levanon, and took the highest branch of the cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YESHAYAH 43:14; YESHAYAH 47:15; HISGALUS 18:11–19"> He broke off its topmost young twig, and carried it into an eretz kena'an (land of trade); he set it in a city of merchants.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., Babylon">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YESHAYAH 44:4; DEVARIM 8:7–9; YIRMEYAH 37:1"> He took also of the zera of ha'aretz, and planted it in a fertile sadeh; he placed it by abundant mayim, and set it out like a willow tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 17:14; MISHLE 16:18–19"> And it yitzmach (sprouted), and became a spreading gefen of low stature, whose branches turned toward him, and the roots thereof were under him; so it became a gefen (vine), and brought forth branches, and shot forth sprigs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 31:4; YECHEZKEL 17:15; MELACHIM BAIS 24:20"> There was also another nesher hagadol with great kenafayim (wings) and many feathers; and, hinei, this gefen (vine) did bend her roots toward him, and shot forth her branches toward him, that he might water it by the furrows where it had been planted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 17:5–6"> It was planted in a sadeh tov by mayim rabbim, that it might bring forth branches, and that it might bear p'ri, that it might be a majestic gefen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 17:10; YIRMEYAH 32:5; YESHAYAH 30:1–7"> Say thou, Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: Shall it thrive? Shall he not pull up the shorashim (roots) thereof, and cut off the p'ri (fruit) thereof, that it wither? All of its tzemach (sprouted) leaves shall wither, even without great power or many peoples to pluck it up by the shorashim (roots) thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HOSHEA 13:15; YOCHANAN 15:6; MATTITYAHU 21:19"> Indeed, hinei, being transplanted, shall it thrive? Shall it not utterly wither, when the east wind toucheth it? It shall wither in the furrows where it tzemach (sprouted).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Moreover the Devar Hashem came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 24:19; YECHEZKEL 17:3; YECHEZKEL 1:2"> Say now to bais hameri (the rebellious house), Know ye not what these things mean? Tell them: Hinei, Melech Bavel is come to Yerushalayim, and hath taken HaMelech thereof, and the sarim thereof, and led them with him to Bavel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:13; MELACHIM BAIS 24:15–17; YECHEZKEL 17:5"> And hath taken of the royal zera, and cut a Brit with him, and hath taken an oath of him; he hath also taken away the mighty of ha'aretz;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 29:14; DEVARIM 28:43; SHMUEL ALEF 2:30"> That the mamlachah (kingdom) might be abased, that it might not exalt itself, but that by being shomer over his brit it might stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 52:3; YIRMEYAH 38:18; YECHEZKEL 17:7"> But he rebelled against him in sending his malachim into Mitzrayim, that they might give him susim and much people. Shall he prosper? Shall he escape that doeth such things? Or shall he break the brit (covenant), and be delivered?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 12:13; YIRMEYAH 52:11; YECHEZKEL 17:18–19"> As I live, saith Adonoi Hashem, surely in the makom where the melech dwelleth that made him melech, whose oath he despised, whose brit he broke, even with him in the midst of Bavel he shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 37:7; YECHEZKEL 4:2; YECHEZKEL 29:6–7"> Neither shall Pharaoh with his mighty army and great kahal do anything for him in the milchamah, by casting up siege mounds, and building siegework, to cut off many nefashot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:24; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:8; EKHAH 5:6"> Seeing he despised the oath by breaking the brit, when, hinei, he had given his yad,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., promised by oath">b</a></sup>and still did all these things, he shall not escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 5:11; YIRMEYAH 5:9; YIRMEYAH 5:2"> Therefore thus saith Adonoi Hashem; As I live, surely Mine oath that he hath despised, and My Brit (covenant) that he hath broken, even it will I repay upon his own rosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 12:13; YECHEZKEL 32:3; YIRMEYAH 2:35"> And I will spread My net upon him, and he shall be caught in My snare, and I will bring him to Bavel, and will execute justice on him there for his ma'al (unfaithfulness) that he hath been unfaithful against Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 25:5; MELACHIM BAIS 25:11; YECHEZKEL 12:14"> And all his fugitives with all his troops shall fall by the cherev, and they that survive and remain shall be scattered toward kol ruach (all winds); and ye shall know that I Hashem have spoken it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:2; ZECHARYAH 6:12–13; YESHAYAH 11:1–5"> Thus saith Adonoi Hashem: I will also take of the highest branch of the high cedar, and will plant it; I will break off from the top of its young twigs a tender one, and will plant it upon a high mountain and prominent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:32; YECHEZKEL 31:6; YESHAYAH 2:2"> On the high mountain of Yisroel will I plant it; and it shall bring forth boughs, and bear p'ri (fruit), and be a majestic cedar; and under it shall dwell kol tzippor (every bird) of every wing; in the tzel (shadow) of the branches thereof shall they dwell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="AMOS 9:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:27–28; YECHEZKEL 24:14"> And all the trees of the sadeh shall know that I Hashem have brought down the high etz (tree), have exalted the low etz (tree), have dried up the green etz (tree), and have made the etz yavesh (dry tree) to flourish. I Hashem have spoken and have done it.</span>
</div>
