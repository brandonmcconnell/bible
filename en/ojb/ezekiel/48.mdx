<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 47:15–17; YEHOSHUA 19:40–47; HISGALUS 7:4–8"> From the northern frontier near the derech Chetlon, as one goeth to Chamat, to Chatzar Einan, to the northward border of Damascus, near Chamat; for these are his, from the eastern side to the western side, Dan, one portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:24–31; BERESHIS 30:12–13"> And by the border of Dan, from the east side unto the west side, Asher, one portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:32–39; BERESHIS 30:7–8"> And by the border of Asher, from the east side even unto the west side, Naphtali, one portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 17:1–11; YEHOSHUA 13:29–31; BERESHIS 48:14–20"> And by the border of Naphtali, from the east side unto the west side, Menasheh, one portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 17:8–10; YEHOSHUA 17:14–18; YEHOSHUA 16:1–10"> And by the border of Menasheh, from the east side unto the west side, Ephrayim, one portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 13:15–21; BERESHIS 29:32; BERESHIS 49:3–4"> And by the border of Ephrayim, from the east side even unto the west side, Reuven, one portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:1–63; YEHOSHUA 19:9; BERESHIS 29:35"> And by the border of Reuven, from the east side unto the west side, Yehudah, one portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 45:1–6; YESHAYAH 12:6; YESHAYAH 33:20–22"> And by the border of Yehudah, from the east side unto the west side, shall be the terumah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="dedicated reserve of land">a</a></sup>which ye shall offer of five and twenty elef &#91;cubits&#93; wide, and in length equal to one of the other portions, from the east side unto the west side; and the Bais<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="HaMikdash">b</a></sup>shall be within it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The terumah that ye shall offer unto Hashem shall be of five and twenty elef &#91;cubits&#93; in length, and of ten elef &#91;cubits&#93; in width.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 45:4; YECHEZKEL 44:28; MATTITYAHU 10:10"> And for them, even for the kohanim, shall be this terumat hakodesh; toward the north five and twenty elef, and toward the west ten elef in width, and toward the east ten elef in width, and toward the south five and twenty elef in length; and the Mikdash Hashem shall be within thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 40:46; YECHEZKEL 44:10; MATTITYAHU 24:45–46"> It shall be for the kohanim hamekudash (set apart as kodesh) of the Bnei Tzadok; which have been shomer over My mishmeret, which went not astray when the Bnei Yisroel went astray, as the Levi'im went astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="VAYIKRA 27:21; YECHEZKEL 45:4"> And this terumiyyah (separated portion) for the kohanim, out of the terumat ha'aretz, kodesh kodashim, next to the border of the Levi'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 45:3; DEVARIM 12:19; YECHEZKEL 45:5"> And over against the border of the kohanim, the Levi'im shall have five and twenty elef in length, and ten elef in width; all the length shall be five and twenty elef, and the width ten elef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="VAYIKRA 27:28; VAYIKRA 27:9–10; SHEMOT 22:29"> And they shall not sell of it, neither exchange, nor transfer the reshit ha'aretz (prime land); for it is kodesh unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 42:20; YECHEZKEL 45:6; YECHEZKEL 44:23"> And the five alafim, that are left in the width over against the five and twenty elef, shall be chol (secular, unconsecrated) for the Ir, for dwelling, and for migrash (open lands); and the Ir shall be within thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:16"> And these shall be the measurement thereof; the north side four alafim and five hundred, and the south side four alafim and five hundred, and on the east side four alafim and five hundred, and the west side four alafim and five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the migrash (open land) of the Ir shall extend toward the north two hundred and fifty &#91;cubits&#93;, and toward the south two hundred and fifty, and toward the east two hundred and fifty, and toward the west two hundred and fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 9:27; EZRA 2:43–58; YECHEZKEL 45:6"> And the remainder of that area running lengthwise along the terumat hakodesh shall measure ten alafim &#91;cubits&#93; eastward, and ten alafim westward; and the increase thereof shall be for lechem unto them that serve the Ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 45:6; MELACHIM ALEF 4:7–23; NECHEMYAH 11:1–36"> And they that serve the Ir shall serve it out of all the Shivtei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:16; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:17"> All the terumah shall be foursquare, five and twenty elef by five and twenty elef; ye shall set apart as the terumat hakodesh and the property of the Ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 48:22; YECHEZKEL 37:24; YECHEZKEL 48:8"> And the remainder shall be for the Nasi, on the one side and on the other of the terumat hakodesh, and of the property of the Ir, along the five and twenty elef of the terumah toward the east border, and westward along the five and twenty elef toward the west border, along the chalakim (tribal portions) for the Nasi; and it shall be the terumat hakodesh; and the Mikdash HaBayit shall be within thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Moreover from the property of the Levi'im, and from the property of the Ir, being in the midst of that which belongs to the Nasi, between the border of Yehudah and the border of Binyamin, shall be for the Nasi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 18:21–28; YECHEZKEL 48:1–7; BERESHIS 35:16–19"> As for the rest of the Shevatim, from the east side unto the west side, Binyamin, one portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:1–9; BERESHIS 29:33; BERESHIS 49:5–7"> And by the border of Binyamin, from the east side unto the west side, Shim'on, one portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:17–23; BERESHIS 30:14–18"> And by the border of Shim'on, from the east side unto the west side, Yissakhar, one portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:10–16; BERESHIS 30:19–20"> And by the border of Yissakhar, from the east side unto the west side, Zevulun, one portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 13:24–28; BERESHIS 30:10–11"> And by the border of Zevulun, from the east side unto the west side, Gad, one portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 47:15; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:2; YECHEZKEL 47:19–20"> And by the border of Gad, at the south side southward, the border shall be even from Tamar unto the waters of Merivat-Kadesh, along the brook to the Yam HaGadol (the Great Sea, Mediterranean Sea).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e., Mediterranean Sea">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 13:1–21; BAMIDBAR 34:2; BAMIDBAR 34:13"> This is ha'aretz which ye shall allot unto the Shivtei Yisroel for nachalah, and these are their portions, saith Adonoi Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 48:16; HISGALUS 21:16; YECHEZKEL 48:32–35"> And these are the outer limits of the Ir on the north side, four alafim and five hundred measures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:12–13; YESHAYAH 26:1–2; HISGALUS 21:21"> And the she'arim of the Ir shall be after the shmot of the Shivtei Yisroel; three she'arim northward: one sha'ar of Reuven, one sha'ar of Yehudah, one sha'ar of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And at the east side four alafim and five hundred; and three she'arim: one sha'ar of Yosef, one sha'ar of Binyamin, one sha'ar of Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And at the south side four alafim and five hundred measures; and three she'arim: one sha'ar of Shim'on, one sha'ar of Yissakhar, one sha'ar of Zevulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> At the west side four alafim and five hundred, with their three she'arim: one sha'ar of Gad, one sha'ar of Asher, one sha'ar of Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:3; ZECHARYAH 2:10; HISGALUS 22:3"> It was all around eighteen elef in circumference; and the shem of the Ir from that day shall be, Hashem Shammah (Hashem is there).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="T.N.
