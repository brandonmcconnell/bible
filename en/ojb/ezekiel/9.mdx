<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YESHAYAH 6:8; YECHEZKEL 43:6–7; MELACHIM BAIS 10:24"> Then He cried also in mine oznayim with a kol gadol (loud voice), saying, Cause them that have charge over the Ir to draw near, even every ish with his keli mashchet (destroying weapon) in his yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="VAYIKRA 16:4; YECHEZKEL 10:2; YECHEZKEL 10:6–7"> And, hinei, shishah anashim (six men) came from the derech of the Sha'ar HaElyon, which lieth toward the north, and every ish a keli mapatz (slaughter weapon) of his in his yad; and one ish among them was clothed with linen, with a keset hasofer (writing case) at his side; and they went in, and stood beside the Mizbe'ach HaNechoshet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 10:4; YECHEZKEL 11:22–23; YECHEZKEL 43:2–4"> And the Kavod Elohei Yisroel was gone up from the Keruv (Cherub), whereupon it was, to the miftan HaBeis &#91;HaMikdash&#93; (the Threshold of the Beis &#91;HaMikdash&#93;). And He called to the ish clothed with linen, which had the keset hasofer at his side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HISGALUS 9:4; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:22; HISGALUS 14:1"> And Hashem said unto him, Go through the midst of the Ir, through the midst of Yerushalayim, and put a TAV upon the mitzekhot (foreheads) of the anashim that grieve and lament for kol hato'evot that are being done in the midst thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 5:11; YECHEZKEL 8:18; YECHEZKEL 7:9"> And to the others He said in mine hearing, Go ye through the Ir after him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., the man with the scribal kit">a</a></sup>and strike down; let not your eye have pity, and do not spare;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HISGALUS 9:4; SHEMOT 12:23; YIRMEYAH 25:29"> Slay utterly zaken (old man) and bochur (young man), betulah, little children, and nashim; but come not near any ish upon whom is the TAV; and begin at My Mikdash. Then they began with the anashim hazekenim which were before the Beis.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="HaMikdash 1K 4:17">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:17; YECHEZKEL 7:20–22; EKHAH 2:4–7"> And He said unto them, Make the Beis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="HaMikdash">c</a></sup>tameh, and fill the khatzerot (coutyards) with chalalim (slain ones); go ye forth. And they went forth, and slaughtered in the Ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 11:13; BAMIDBAR 16:45; YEHOSHUA 7:6"> And it came to pass, while they were slaying them, and I was left, that I fell upon my face, and cried out, and said, Ah, Adonoi Hashem! Wilt Thou destroy kol She'erit Yisroel in Thy pouring out of Thy chemah (wrath, fury) upon Yerushalayim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="IYOV 22:13; YECHEZKEL 8:12; YECHEZKEL 7:23"> Then He said unto me, The avon Bais Yisroel and Yehudah is exceeding great, and HaAretz is full of damim, and the Ir full of injustice; for they say, Hashem hath forsaken Ha'Aretz, and Hashem seeth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 7:4; YECHEZKEL 8:18; YECHEZKEL 11:21"> And as for Me, Mine eye shall show no pity, neither will I spare, but I will recompense their derech upon their rosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 6:7–8; HISGALUS 16:17; HISGALUS 16:2"> And, hinei, the ish clothed with linen, which had the keset (&#91;writing&#93; case) at his side, reported, saying, I have done as Thou hast commanded me.</span>
