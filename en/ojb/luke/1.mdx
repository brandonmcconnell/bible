<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 7:1; BERESHIS 17:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:15"> And they were both tzaddikim before Hashem, walking a derech tamim in all the mitzvos and chukkim of the Torah of Adoneinu.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gn 6:9; Dt 5:30; 1Kgs 9:4">d</a></sup></span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 40:26–27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:3–4; SHOFETIM 13:3"> And there appeared to Zecharyah a malach Hashem, standing on the right side of the Mizbeach of ketoret.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ex 30:1-10">h</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 1:58; MISHLE 23:24; BERESHIS 21:6"> And he will be a simcha to you and sasson (joy), and there will be sasson rav (great joy) at his huledet (birth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 1:5; BAMIDBAR 6:2–4; LUKAS 7:33"> And he will be gadol before Hashem, and he will not drink yayin or strong drink, and he will be filled with the Ruach Hakodesh from the womb of his Em (mother),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Num 6:3; Lv 10:9; Judg 13:4; Jer 1:5">j</a></sup></span>

<div className="bp">

</div>

<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LUKAS 1:67; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:8; LUKAS 1:44"> And it came about, when Elisheva heard the Birkat Shalom of Miryam, the yeled leaped in Elishevaʼs womb, and she was filled with the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
