<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:6; YOCHANAN 18:24; LUKAS 1:80"> And when, during the same historical period, Anan and Caiapha were Kohanim Gedolim, then it was that the dvar Hashem came to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="the kohen-navi">b</a></sup>Yochanan Ben Zecharyah bamidbar (in the wilderness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKAS 1:77; MATTITYAHU 3:5–6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:24"> And Yochanan went into all the surrounding region of the Yarden darshenen a tevilah of teshuva for the selichat avon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:3–5; YOCHANAN 1:23; LUKAS 1:76–79"> As it has been written in the sefer divrei Yeshayah Hanavi, KOL KOREY BAMIDBAR<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 40:3">c</a></sup>(A voice of one shouting in the wilderness): Prepare the Derech Hashem (the Way of the L-rd). Make his paths straight!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:4; YESHAYAH 42:16; YESHAYAH 45:2"> KOL GEY YINNASE VKHOL HAR VGIVAH YISHPALU (Every valley will be filled in and every mountain and hill will be leveled off), VHAYAH HEAKOV LEMISHOR VHARKHASIM LVIKAH (The crooked will be made straight, the rough paths made into smooth roads);</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YESHAYAH 52:10; YESHAYAH 40:5; TEHILLIM 98:2–3"> VRAU CHOL BASAR ES YESHU'AT ELOHEINU (and all basar will see the salvation of our G-d).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 40:3-5; Ps 98:2; Isa 42:16; 52:10">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:33; MATTITYAHU 12:34; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:10"> Therefore, Yochanan was saying to the multitudes coming out to have the mikveh mayimʼs tevilah supervised by him, You banim of nachashim, who warned you to flee from the charon af<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hashem">e</a></sup>&#91;Hashem&#93; habah (the coming burning wrath &#91;of Hashem&#93;)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:27–31; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:20; YESHAYAH 1:16–18"> Therefore, produce p'ri tov l'teshuva (fruit worthy of repentance), and do not begin to presume within yourselves, saying, We have the zechut Avot (merit of the Fathers) of Avraham Avinu, for, I say to you, that Hashem is able from these avanim (stones) to raise up banim to Avraham Avinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:19; YOCHANAN 15:6; LUKAS 13:9"> And already the ax is laid at the shoresh haetzim (the root of the trees). Therefore, every etz not producing pri tov is cut down and is thrown into the Eish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:37; LUKAS 3:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:30"> And the multitudes were questioning him, saying, What then should we do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 58:7–11; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:18"> And in reply, Yochanan was saying to them, Let the one having two kaftans share with the one having none, and let the one having okhel (food) do likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKAS 7:29; LUKAS 15:1–2; LUKAS 18:13"> Now came also mochesim (tax collectors) to receive the tevilah of teshuva, and they said to him, Rabbi, what should we do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 19:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:28; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:10"> And Yochanan said to them, Collect nothing more than the amount having been commanded you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:5–6; SHEMOT 20:16; SHEMOT 23:1"> And chaiyalim (soldiers) as well were asking him, What should we do also? And Yochanan said to them, Extort kesef from no one, and let there be no lashon hora, and be satisfied with your loin (wages).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:24; YOCHANAN 3:28–29; YOCHANAN 1:19–28"> As the Am &#91;Berit&#93; were filled with expectation, and all were wondering in their levavot (hearts) concerning Yochanan, whether perhaps he might be the Moshiach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 3:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:15–16"> Yochanan answered everyone, saying, I give you a tevilah with a mikveh mayim, but Hu HaBah (He Who Comes)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="i.e., Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">f</a></sup>has more chozek (strength) than me; I am not worthy to untie the strap of his sandals. He will give you a tevilah with the Ruach Hakodesh and with Eish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 15:7; MATTITYAHU 13:30; MICHOH 4:12"> The winnowing fork is in his hand to clean out his threshing floor and to gather the wheat into his storehouse, but the MOTZ (chaff) he will burn up with an EISH LO TIKHBEH (fire not &#91;ever&#93; extinguished).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:29–36; YOCHANAN 1:34; YOCHANAN 1:29"> Therefore, with many other devarim Yochanan was exhorting them, preaching the Besuras HaGeulah to the Am &#91;Berit&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKAS 3:1; MATTITYAHU 11:2; MARKOS 6:17–18"> Now Herod the tetrarch, when he was reproved by Yochanan about Herodias, the wife of his brother, and about all resha'im (evil) which he did,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 24:17–22; YOCHANAN 3:24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:15–16"> Herod added this above all: he locked up Yochanan in the beit hasohar (prison).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:32–34; MATTITYAHU 3:13–17; YOCHANAN 12:27–28"> And it came about while all the Am &#91;Berit&#93; were receiving the tevilah, and when Yehoshua also had been given the tevilah and was davening, Shomayim was opened,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="TEHILLIM 2:7; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:17–18; YOCHANAN 1:32"> And the Ruach Hakodesh descended in demut gashmit as a yonah upon Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach; and then came a bat kol out of Shomayim, saying, ATAH BNI AHUVI ASHER BCHA CHAFATSTI.</span>
</div>
