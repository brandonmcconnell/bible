<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:12; LUKAS 10:19; MATTITYAHU 16:19"> And having called together the Shneym Asar, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach gave to them koach (power) and samchut (authority) over all the shedim and to give refuah (healing) to their illnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:7–8; LUKAS 10:9; MATTITYAHU 3:2"> Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach sent them out as his Shlichim to preach the Malchut Hashem and to give refuah to the cholim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKAS 22:35; TEHILLIM 37:3; LUKAS 10:4–12"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Take nothing for the journey, neither walking stick nor a schnorrerʼs (beggarʼs) sack nor lechem nor kesef, nor an extra kaftan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:11; MARKOS 6:10; LUKAS 10:5–8"> And into whatever bais you may enter, remain there, and from there go out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:51; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:6; MARKOS 6:11"> And as many as do not receive you, going out from that shtetl, shake off the dust from your feet for an edut against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:30; MARKOS 16:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:15"> And they departed and were itinerating throughout the shtetlach, darshenen the Besuras HaGeulah and healing everywhere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MARKOS 6:14–28; MATTITYAHU 14:1–12; LUKAS 3:1"> Now Herod the Tetrarch heard all the things happening and he was perplexed because it was said by some that Yochanan had experienced his Techiyah from HaMesim and come back;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:21; LUKAS 9:19; MARKOS 6:15"> by some also that Eliyahu Hanavi had appeared; but by others that some navi of the ancients arose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 23:8; LUKAS 13:31–32"> But Herod said, Yochanan I beheaded. Who then is this about whom I hear such things? And he was seeking to see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:17; MATTITYAHU 14:13–21"> And having returned, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiachʼs Shlichim told him what things they did. And having taken them for a yechidus, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach withdrew to a shtetl being called Beit-Tzaidah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 9:2; LUKAS 5:31; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:16"> But the multitudes, having realized this, followed him. And having given them a kabbalat panim (welcome), Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach was speaking to them about the Malchut Hashem, and to the ones having need of it, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach was giving refuah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:1; YOCHANAN 6:5–15; HOSHEA 13:5"> Now the day began to decline. And having approached, the Shneym Asar said to Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach, Send away the multitude, so that having gone into the surrounding shtetlach and farms, they may find lodging and may find provisions, for here we are in a desolate place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MISHLE 11:24–25; YOCHANAN 6:5–9; MELACHIM BAIS 4:42–43"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to them, You give them &#91;something&#93; to eat. But they said, There are not to us more than chamesh kikrot (loaves) and dagim, shenayim, unless we go and buy for all this people okhel (food).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MARKOS 8:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:40; MARKOS 6:39–40"> For there were about chamesh elafim anashim (five thousand). But Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to his talmidim, Have them recline,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="as at a farbrengen (inspirational gathering)">a</a></sup>and chamishim (fifty) in a group.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 23:2">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiachʼs talmidim did so, and all reclined.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="as at tish">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 14:19; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:4–5; MATTITYAHU 15:36"> And having taken the chamesh kikrot and the shnei hadagim, and having looked up to Shomayim, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said a bracha over them, and offered the betziat halechem, and was giving to the talmidim to set before the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:44; MATTITYAHU 15:37–38; YOCHANAN 6:11–13"> And they ate and all were satisfied, and they picked up shirayim, shneym asar baskets full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:13–16; LUKAS 11:1; MATTITYAHU 26:36"> And it came about while he was davening alone, with only his talmidim with him, he questioned them, saying, Whom do the multitudes declare me to be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MALACHI 4:5; LUKAS 9:7–8; MATTITYAHU 14:2"> And in reply they said, Yochanan of the tevilah of teshuva; but others, Eliyahu Hanavi; and others, that a certain navi of the ancients has come back to life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:15–17; YOCHANAN 4:29; YOCHANAN 7:41"> And he said to them, And you, who do you declare me to be? And in reply, Kefa said, The Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:20; MATTITYAHU 17:9; MARKOS 8:30–31"> And having warned them, he gave orders to tell no one this,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:1–12; LUKAS 24:7; LUKAS 18:31–34"> Saying, It is necessary for the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">d</a></sup>to suffer much and to be rejected by the Ziknei HaAm and Rashei Hakohanim and Sofrim and to be killed, and after his histalkus (passing), on Yom HaShelishi to undergo the Techiyas HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:38–39; LUKAS 14:26–27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:13"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach was saying to all, If anyone wishes to come after me, let him deny himself and lift up his etz shel mesiros nefesh yom yom and let him follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKAS 17:33; YOCHANAN 12:25; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:23–24"> For whoever wishes to save his nefesh will lose it. But whoever loses his nefesh on my account will save it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MARKOS 8:36; MATTITYAHU 16:26; TEHILLIM 49:6–8"> For what is the revach (profit) to a ben Adam who has gained the whole of the Olam Hazeh, but has lost his own self, forfeited his neshamah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:32–33; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:12; LUKAS 12:8–9"> For whoever has bushah (shame) toward me and my devarim, this one the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">e</a></sup>will be ashamed of, when Moshiach comes in his Kavod and the Kavod of HaAv of him and of the malachim hakedoshim (holy angels).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:28; MARKOS 9:1; YOCHANAN 21:22–23"> But I say to you, Omein, there are some standing here who will by no means taste mavet until they see the Malchut Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 17:1–13; MATTITYAHU 26:37–39; MARKOS 14:33–36"> And it came to pass, about a week after these divrei Moshiach, that when he had taken Kefa and Yochanan and Ya'akov, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach went up to the har to daven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 17:2; MARKOS 9:2–3; SHEMOT 34:29–35"> And it came about that while Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach davens, the appearance of his face became different and his kaftan and all his clothing became dazzling white.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:3–6; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:7–11; LUKAS 1:17"> And two men were conversing with Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach, and these two men were Moshe Rabbeinu and Eliyahu HaNavi,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:21; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:11–12; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:10"> Who appeared in kavod and were speaking of Moshiachʼs Litziat Exodus, which he was about to fulfill in Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DANIEL 8:18; YOCHANAN 1:14; DANIEL 10:9"> But Kefa and the ones with him had been weighed down with sleep. And having awakened fully, they saw his kavod and the two men standing with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MARKOS 9:5–6; MARKOS 10:38; TEHILLIM 4:6–7"> And it came about, just as the men departed from Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach, Kefa said, Adoni, it is good for us to be here; let us make shalosh sukkot, one for you and one for Moshe Rabbeinu and one for Eliyahu HaNavi. (Kefa did not know what he was saying.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="SHEMOT 40:34–38; MATTITYAHU 17:5–7; SHOFETIM 13:22"> And while Kefa was saying these things, an anan (cloud) came and was overshadowing them, and while they entered into the anan they were afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LUKAS 3:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:22–23; MATTITYAHU 3:17"> And a bat kol came from the anan (cloud), saying ZEH BENI BECHIRI, ELAV TISHMAUN (This is my Son the Chosen One, listen to him).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isa 42:1; Ps 2:7">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 17:9; MARKOS 9:9–10; MARKOS 9:6"> When the bat kol became silent, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach was found alone. And they were silent, and reported to no one in those yamim anything of what they had seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 17:14–20; MARKOS 9:14–29"> And it came about on the following day, when they had descended from the har (mountain), a large throng met Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 12:10; YOCHANAN 4:47; LUKAS 7:12"> And an ish from the multitude cried out, saying, Rabbi, I beg you to look at beni (my son), for to me he is a ben yachid (only son),</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MARKOS 9:20; LUKAS 8:29; LUKAS 4:35"> And a shed seizes him and suddenly shrieking and lets out a shrai and throws the bocher into a convulsion with foam at the mouth and it mauls him and will scarcely depart from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LUKAS 9:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:13–16; LUKAS 10:17–19"> And I begged your talmidim that they might cast out the shed, and they were not able.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:4; YOCHANAN 20:27"> And in reply, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said, O perverse dor (generation) without emunah, until when will I be with you, and until when will I put up with you? Bring your ben here.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Dt 32:5">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MARKOS 9:26–27; LUKAS 7:15; HISGALUS 12:12"> Even now as the bocher was approaching Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach, the shed threw him down and convulsed him. But Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach rebuked the shed (the ruach hatumah) and gave refuah to the bocher, and gave him back to his abba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LUKAS 9:43–45; LUKAS 5:26; MATTITYAHU 17:22"> And all were amazed at the gedulat Hashem. And while all were beholding the peledike way he was doing everything, he said to his talmidim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="LUKAS 9:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:23; LUKAS 2:19"> Let these divrei Hashem lodge in your ears: for the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13">h</a></sup>is about to be delivered into the hands of Bnei Adam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="LUKAS 18:34; LUKAS 2:50; YOCHANAN 12:16"> But they had no binah concerning this dvar of Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach, and it had been nistar from them in order that they might not have binah, and they were afraid to ask him about this dvar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:1–5; MARKOS 9:33–37; MATTITYAHU 20:20–22"> Now an argument arose among his talmidim, as to who would be the greatest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="TEHILLIM 139:23; YOCHANAN 16:30; LUKAS 5:22"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach, having perceived the machshavah of their levavot, took a yeled into his arms and stood the yeled next to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:6; LUKAS 14:11; MARKOS 9:37"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Whoever receives this yeled in ha-Shem of me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Moshiach">i</a></sup>is mekabel (receiving, accepting) of me. And whoever is mekabel of me is mekabel of the One<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hashem">j</a></sup>who sent me. For whoever is the least among all of you, this one is the Gadol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="MARKOS 9:38–40; BAMIDBAR 11:27–29; MARKOS 10:13–14"> And in reply Yochanan said, Adoni, we saw someone casting out shedim b'Shem of you and we were trying to stop him, because he is not following as one of the anshei shlomeinu (men of our fraternity).</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:30; LUKAS 11:23; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:3"> But Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Do not stop him. For whoever is not against you, is for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="YESHAYAH 50:5–9; MARKOS 16:19; LUKAS 13:22"> And it came about that as the Yom of Rebbe Melech HaMoshiachʼs aliyah ascent to Shomayim approaches, he set his face bedavka (deliberately) and resolutely to go up to Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:5; LUKAS 17:16; LUKAS 10:1"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach sent messengers ahead of him. And having gone, they entered into a village of Shomron in order to make arrangements for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:9; YOCHANAN 4:40–42; LUKAS 9:48"> And the people of Shomron were not mekabel Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach, because his face was set to go to Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 1:10–14; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:14–18; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19–20"> And when Rebbe Melech HaMoshiachʼs talmidim, Ya'akov and Yochanan, witnessed this, they said, Adoneinu, do you want that we should call eish to come down from Shomayim to consume them?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="2Kgs 1:10,12">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:19; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; YIRMEYAH 17:9"> And, having turned around, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach rebuked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:21; YOCHANAN 3:17"> And they went to another village.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 8:19–22; YOCHANAN 13:37; LUKAS 9:57–60"> And as they were going baderech (on the road), a certain one said to Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach, I will follow you wherever you go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 8:20; LUKAS 14:26–33; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Foxes have dens, and the OPH HASHOMAYIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Job 7:13-14">l</a></sup>(birds of heaven) have nests, but the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">m</a></sup>does not have a place where he may lay down his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:24; MATTITYAHU 6:33; MELACHIM ALEF 19:20"> And he said to another, Follow me. But the ish said, Adoni, allow me to go first and bury the Av of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:3–4; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:6; YOCHANAN 21:15–17"> But Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Leave the mesim (dead ones, spiritually unregenerate ones without hitkhadshut) to bury their mesim (dead ones), but you go and proclaim the Malchut Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="LUKAS 14:26; LUKAS 14:18–20; MELACHIM ALEF 19:20"> And another said also, I will follow you, Adoni. But first allow me to say lhitraot to the ones in my bais.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="1Kgs 19:20">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:38; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20–22"> But Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to him, No one having put his hand upon the plow yet looking back to the things behind is fit for the Malchut Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Gn 19:26">o</a></sup></span>
