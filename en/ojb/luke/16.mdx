<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10; MATTITYAHU 25:14–30; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:3"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying also to the talmidim, A certain oisher (rich man) had a sochen (steward, estate manager or agent), and the charge brought against him was that he was squandering the property of the oisher (rich man).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="KOHELET 11:9–10; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:5; HISGALUS 20:12"> And having called him, the oisher said to the sochen, What is this I hear about you? Submit to a bikoret beshbonot (audit) for that which is under your pekuddah (stewardship care), for you are no longer able to be sochen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 5:31; YESHAYAH 10:3; MISHLE 18:9"> And the sochen said to himself, What may I do, because Adoni takes away the pekuddah from me? I am not strong enough to dig, I am ashamed to beg.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MISHLE 30:9; YIRMEYAH 4:22; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:15"> Oh! I have daas of what I may do, that when I am removed from the work of the sochen, they may receive me into their batim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKAS 7:41–42; MATTITYAHU 18:24"> And having summoned his Adonʼs debtors one by one, he was saying to the first, How much do you owe Adoni?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 2:10; LUKAS 16:12; LUKAS 16:9"> And he said, One hundred jugs of olive oil. And the sochen said to him, Take your bill, sit down, quickly write fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHIR HASHIRIM 8:11–12; LUKAS 20:9; LUKAS 20:12"> Then to another he said, And you, how much do you owe? And he said, One hundred containers of wheat. He says to him, Take your bill and write eighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:8; MISHLE 6:6–8"> And the Baal Bayit praised the unrighteous sochen because he acted with chochmah. Because the Bnei HaOlam HaZeh have more seichel in dealing with their own generation than the Bnei HaOhr.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ps 17:14; 18:26">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:21; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:17–19; DANIEL 4:27"> And I say to you, use the mammon (the wealth of the Olam HaZeh) to make for yourselves yedidim (friends, chaverim), so that when unrighteous mammon fails, they may welcome you into the mishkenot olam (eternal dwellings of the Olam HaBah).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKAS 16:11–12; MATTITYAHU 25:21; LUKAS 19:17"> The one oisgehalten in little, also is neeman in much, and the one who is unrighteous in little, also in much is unrighteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 12:33; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5; MISHLE 8:18–19"> If, then, you were not ne'eman with unrighteous mammon, who will entrust you with hon emes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKAS 19:13–26; MATTITYAHU 25:14–29; IYOV 1:21"> And if you were not neeman with that which belongs to another, who will give you what is your own?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–16; MATTITYAHU 6:24; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:4"> No eved is able to serve two adonim; for either he will have sinah (hatred) toward the one and he will have ahavah (love) toward the other, or one he will be devoted to and the other he will despise. Your avodas service cannot be for both Hashem and Mammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 23:35; LUKAS 20:47; YIRMEYAH 6:13"> And the Perushim, who were ohavei kesef (lovers of money), heard all this and they made leitzonus (fun) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:7; MISHLE 21:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:5"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, You are the ones who in the sight of Bnei Adam credit yourself as being yitzdak im Hashem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Job 25:4">b</a></sup>(justified with G-d), but Hashem has daas (knowledge) of your levavot. Because the thing highly esteemed among Bnei Adam is a to'eva (abomination) before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:9–14; MATTITYAHU 21:32; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:24–25"> The Torah and the Neviim were proclaimed until Yochanan; since then it is the Malchut Hashem that is being preached as Besuras HaGeulah, and anyone entering it must strive to do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:18; LUKAS 21:33; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:25"> But it is easier for HaShomayim and HaAretz to pass away than for one tag (ornamental flourish) of the Torah to fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:9; MARKOS 10:11–12; MATTITYAHU 5:31–32"> Anyone giving the get to his isha and taking another wife commits ni'uf (adultery), and the one marrying a gerusha (divorcee) commits ni'uf (adultery).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 16:49; AMOS 6:4–6; LUKAS 18:24–25"> Now there was a certain oisher (rich man). He was dressed in purple and fine linen, and yom yom (daily) he feasted sumptuosly and every day for him was to make a simcha.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ezek 16:49">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:2; SHMUEL ALEF 2:8; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5"> And there was a certain ish oni (poor man) covered with sores, El'azar by name, who had been laid at the oisherʼs sha'ar (gate).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:27; MARKOS 7:28; YOCHANAN 6:12"> And the ish oni El'azar longed to fill his mogen (stomach) with what fell from the oisherʼs tish. But even the kelevim (dogs) were coming and licking the sores of the ish oni (poor man).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:24; TEHILLIM 49:16–19; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:11"> And it came to pass that El'azar died, and he was carried away by the malachim to the tish at the kheyk (bosom) of Avraham Avinu. And then the oisher (rich man) died also, and he was buried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:4; LUKAS 16:28; MATTITYAHU 18:9"> And lifting up his eynayim in Sheol, where he was in the torments of agony, he sees Avraham Avinu off in the distance and El'azar at tish at his kheyk (bosom).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:10; HISGALUS 22:1; HISGALUS 7:16–17"> And he called, Avraham Avinu! Chaneni na and send El'azar that he may dip the tip of his finger into the mayim and cool my tongue, because I am in torment in this moked (fire).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 66:24">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKAS 6:24; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15; TEHILLIM 17:14"> But Avraham Avinu said, Beni, have zikaron (recollection) that you received your tov in the span of your days, and El'azar likewise received the ra'ah. But now he is given nechamah (comfort) here, but you, yisurim (sufferings, torments).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ps 17:14">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:36; TEHILLIM 49:14; TEHILLIM 50:22"> And, in addition to all these things, there has been fixed between us and you a tehom gedolah (a great abyss, chasm), so that the ones wishing to come over from here to you are not able, neither from there to us may they cross over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And the oisher said, I ask you then, Avraham Avinu, that you may send El'azar to the bais of Avi,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:40; TEHILLIM 49:12–13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:6"> for I have chamesh achim (five brothers) that he may warn them, lest also they may come to this place of yisurim (torments).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:21; LUKAS 24:27; LUKAS 16:16"> But Avraham Avinu says, They have Moshe Rabbeinu and the Neviim. Let them listen to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="HISGALUS 16:9–11; LUKAS 16:24; LUKAS 13:5"> But the oisher said, Lo (no), Avraham Avinu, but if someone from the Mesim should go to them, they will make teshuva.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:8; YOCHANAN 11:43–53"> But Avraham said to him, If Moshe Rabbeinu and the Neviim they do not listen to, neither if someone should make his Techiyah from the Mesim should they be persuaded.</span>
