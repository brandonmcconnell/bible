<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:19"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to his talmidim, It is impossible for the nisayon (temptation) to sin not to come, but oy through whom it comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MARKOS 9:42; MATTITYAHU 18:14; MATTITYAHU 18:3–6"> It is better for him if a millstone is hung around his neck and he had been thrown into the sea than that he should cause a michshol for these little ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:17; MATTITYAHU 18:21; MATTITYAHU 18:15–17"> Pay attention to yourselves. If your ach sins and you rebuke him, and if he makes teshuva, grant him selicha (forgiveness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:12–13; MATTITYAHU 18:21–22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:20"> And if shevah pe'amim during the yom he sins against you and shevah pe'amim he turns around to you saying, I make teshuva, you will grant him selicha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MARKOS 9:24; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:13"> And the Shlichim said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Adoneinu, increase our emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 17:20; MATTITYAHU 21:21; MARKOS 9:23"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu said, If you have emunah like a mustard seed, you would have said to this mulberry etz, be uprooted and be planted in the sea, and it would have given you mishma'at.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:11; LUKAS 13:15; LUKAS 14:5"> But who among you, having an eved plowing or tending kevesim, when the eved comes in from the sadeh, will say to him, Here, come ofen ort (immediately) and recline at tish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKAS 12:37; BERESHIS 43:16; SHMUEL BAIS 12:20"> Rather, would he not say, Prepare something that I may have okhel; gird up your tunic and serve me until I am satisfied, and, after that, you may have okhel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> There is no obligatory effusive todah, when the eved only did his chiyuv (duty).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:16–17; IYOV 22:2–3; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:5–6"> So also you, when you do all these things which are given you as mitzvot, say, We are useless avadim, we only did our chiyuv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 9:51–52; YOCHANAN 4:3–4"> And it came about while Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach goes to Yerushalayim, he was traveling through the middle of Shomron and the Galil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="VAYIKRA 13:45–46; LUKAS 5:12; MELACHIM BAIS 7:3"> And entering into a certain shtetl, some anashim metzoraim (leprous men) met Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, and there were ten of them. Keeping their distance,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lv 13:45,46">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:22; MATTITYAHU 20:30–31; MATTITYAHU 9:27"> They shouted, saying, Yehoshua, Rabbi, chaneinu!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 5:14; VAYIKRA 13:1–46; MELACHIM BAIS 5:14"> And having seen this, he said to them, Go and show yourselves to the kohanim. And it came about while they were going away that they were made metoharim (clean).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lv 14:3">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 30:11–12; TEHILLIM 103:1–4; TEHILLIM 118:18–19"> And one of them, having seen that he had received refuah, returned with a kol gadol (loud voice), saying Baruch Hashem!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:5–25; MATTITYAHU 10:5; HISGALUS 19:10"> And he fell on his face at the feet of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, giving hodayah (thanksgiving). And he was a Shomroni (Samaritan).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:21; TEHILLIM 106:13; BERESHIS 3:9"> And in reply Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said, Were there not ten who were made metoharim? Now the tishah (nine), where are they?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="TEHILLIM 50:23; MATTITYAHU 15:24–28; TEHILLIM 106:13"> Was none of them found to turn around and return and give kavod (glory) to Hashem except this nokhri (foreigner)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKAS 7:50; LUKAS 8:48; MARKOS 10:52"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Get up and go. Your emunah (faith) has delivered you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:36; LUKAS 19:11; DANIEL 2:44"> Now having been asked by the Perushim, Ad mosai? (How much longer?) When comes the Malchut Hashem? Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach replied, The Malchut Hashem is not coming with things observable,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:17; MARKOS 13:21"> nor will they say, Hinei, here it is, or There it is, for hinei, the Malchut Hashem is within you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKAS 5:35; MATTITYAHU 9:15; YOCHANAN 8:21–24"> And he said to the talmidim, Yamim are coming when you will long to see one of the yamim of the Bar Enosh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13">c</a></sup>And you will not see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKAS 21:8; MATTITYAHU 24:23–26; LUKAS 17:21"> They will say to you, Hinei, there it is! Or, hinei, here it is! Do not go out after them nor pursue them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:27; MATTITYAHU 24:30; MALACHI 4:1–2"> For as the lightning flashing out of one part under Shomayim lights up the other part under Shomayim, thus will be the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">d</a></sup>in his Yom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:21; LUKAS 9:22; MARKOS 8:31"> But brishonah it is necessary for him to suffer many things and to be rejected by HaDor HaZeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:37–39; LUKAS 17:24; BERESHIS 7:7–23"> And just as it was in the yamim of Noach, thus will it be also in the yamim of the Bar Enosh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14; Gn 6:5-8; 7:6-24">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKAS 12:19–20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:1–3; LUKAS 16:19–23"> They were eating, drinking, marrying, and being given in marriage, until the Yom when Noach entered into the Tevah (Ark) and HaMabbul (the Flood) came and destroyed everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 16:49–50; BERESHIS 18:20–21; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:1–5"> Likewise, just as it was in the yamim of Lot: they were eating, drinking, buying, selling, planting, and building.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gn 19:1-28">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:6; TZEFANYAH 2:9; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7"> But on the Yom when Lot went out from S'dom, from Shomayim it rained down eish and gofrit and destroyed everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:7; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:7; HISGALUS 1:7"> So it will be on the Yom when the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">g</a></sup>has his hisgalus (revelation).</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LUKAS 21:21; MARKOS 13:14–16; YIRMEYAH 45:5"> On that yom let not anyone on the housetop come back down inside for any of his property in the bais, and the one in a sadeh (field) likewise let him not turn back for things left behind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="BERESHIS 19:26; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:38–39; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:18–22"> Remember eshet Lot (Lotʼs wife).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gn 19:26">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MARKOS 8:35–37; MATTITYAHU 10:39; YOCHANAN 12:25"> Whoever seeks to preserve his nefesh will lose it; but whoever loses it, will preserve it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:40–41; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9; MALACHI 3:16–18"> I say to you, in lailah hazeh there will be two in one bed: one will be taken; and the other will be left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:41; SHEMOT 11:5; SHOFETIM 16:21"> There will be shtayim (two) grinding at the same place: one will be taken, but the other will be left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Shtayim will be in the sadeh (field); one will be taken and the other left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:28; IYOV 39:29–30; HISGALUS 19:17–18"> And in reply they say to him, Where, Adoneinu? But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Where the NEVELAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Dt 21:23">i</a></sup>is, there also the nesharim (vultures, eagles &#91;popularly&#93;) will be gathered together.</span>
