<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MISHLE 29:7; TEHILLIM 8:1–4; LUKAS 18:4"> Saying, An aza (certain) Shofet was in a certain town. Now this Shofet had no fear of Hashem nor any respect for Bnei Adam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YESHAYAH 1:17; YIRMEYAH 5:28; DEVARIM 27:19"> Now an almanah was in that town and she was coming to him saying, Grant me yashrus (justice) against my ish riv (opponent in a lawsuit).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Isa 1:17">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:12–13; LUKAS 16:3; LUKAS 12:17"> And for a zman (time) the Shofet was not willing. But after these things, he said to himself, If indeed Hashem I do not fear nor do Bnei Adam I respect,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKAS 11:8; LUKAS 18:39; SHOFETIM 16:16"> Yet because this almanah (widow) causes me tzoros and is such a nudzh (pest), I will grant yashrus (justice) to her, lest in the end she may wear me out by her constant coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKAS 7:13"> Now Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu said, Listen to what the unrighteous shofet says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:35–37; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; LUKAS 11:13"> Now will not Hashem by all means do mishpat for his Bechirim crying out to him yomam valailah? Will he delay long in bringing them help?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ex 22:23; Ps 88:1">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:23–26; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:8–9; MATTITYAHU 24:9–13"> I say to you that Hashem will bring about their yashrus (justice) swiftly, but when the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">d</a></sup>comes, will he then find emunah (faith) on haaretz?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 16:15; MISHLE 30:12; YESHAYAH 65:5"> And he said this mashal also to some, who have become soimech (confident, putting their trust and being dependent) on themselves that they are tzaddikim and who are despising others.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Isa 65:5">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:31–32; MELACHIM ALEF 8:30; MELACHIM BAIS 20:5"> Shnei anashim (two men) went up to the Beis Hamikdash to daven, one was a Parush and the other, a moches (tax collector).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:5; YESHAYAH 65:5; HISGALUS 3:17"> The Parush was standing by himself, shawkling (ritual swaying while davening), and his tefillah went like this: Adonoi, Modeh Ani that I am not like other men, swindlers, resha'im, menaafim, or even this moches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKAS 11:42; MATTITYAHU 23:23–24; MATTITYAHU 9:14"> I undergo a tzom (fast) twice during the week, I give the ma'aser (tithe) of everything as much as I get.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isa 58:3; Mal 3:8">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZRA 9:6; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8–10; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:15"> But the moches (tax collector) stood at a distance and was not willing even to lift up his eynayim to Shomayim but was beating his chest, saying, Adonoi, rachem na al choteh kamoni! (L-rd, have mercy on a sinner like me!)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 66:2; Jer 31:19">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 14:11; LUKAS 16:15; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10"> I say to you, this one, rather than the other one, went down to his bais having been made yitzdak im Hashem(justified with G-d). Because everyone lifting himself up will be brought low, but the one bringing himself low will be uplifted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MARKOS 10:13–16; MATTITYAHU 19:13–15; SHMUEL ALEF 1:24"> Now also they were bringing to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach yeladim that he might place his hands on them. But having seen this, the talmidim were rebuking them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:3–4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:14"> But Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach called for them saying, Permit the yeladim to come to me and do not hinder them, for of such ones is the Malchut Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:3; MARKOS 10:15; TEHILLIM 131:1–2"> Omein, I say to you, whoever does not welcome the Malchut Hashem as a yeled would, may by no means enter into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MARKOS 10:17–31; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:30; MATTITYAHU 19:16–30"> And a certain one of the manhigim (leaders, one of the wealthier Baale-Battishe Jews) questioned Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, saying, Mori HaTov (Good Teacher), what mitzvah must I do to inherit Chayyei Olam?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="IYOV 15:14–16; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:26"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Why do you call me tov? No one is tov except Hashem alone, nu?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:12–17; DEVARIM 5:16–21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:9"> You have daas of the Mitzvot. LO TINAF, LO TIRTZACH, LO TIGNOV, LO TAANEH VREIACHAH ED SHAKER, KABEID ES AVICHA VES IMMECHA.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ex 20:12-16; Dt 5:16-20">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:2–3; MARKOS 10:20–21"> And he said, In all these things I have been shomer mitzvot and frum from my kinder-yoren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKAS 12:33; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:18–19; MATTITYAHU 19:21"> And having heard, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Still one thing for you is lacking. Everything as much as you have, sell and distribute to the aniyim, and you will have otzar in Himel; and come follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 33:31; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15; IYOV 31:24–25"> But having heard these things, he fell into agmat nefesh (grief, sadness). For he had much kesef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MISHLE 30:9; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5–7; MARKOS 10:23–27"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, when he saw him fall into agmat nefesh, said, How difficult it is for the ones having osher (riches) to enter into the Malchut Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Prov 11:28">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:24"> For it is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for an oisher (rich man) to enter into the Malchut Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LUKAS 13:23"> But the ones, having heard, said, Then who is able to come to Yeshu'at Eloheinu?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:17; MATTITYAHU 19:26; LUKAS 1:37"> And he said, The things impossible with Bnei Adam are possible with Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MARKOS 10:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:7; MATTITYAHU 19:27"> And Kefa said, Hinei, we have left everything to follow you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MARKOS 10:29–31; MATTITYAHU 10:37–39; LUKAS 14:26–28"> And he said to them, Omein, I say to you, that there is no one who left bais or isha or achim or horim (parents) or yeladim because of the Malchut Hashem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:5–6; IYOV 42:10; HISGALUS 2:17"> Who will not receive many times as much in this zman (time) and in the Olam HaBah, Chayyei Olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:17–19; YESHAYAH 53:1–12; TEHILLIM 22:1–31"> And having taken the Shneym Asar (Twelve) aside in a yechidus, he said to them, Hinei, we are making another aliyah leregel (pilgrimage) to Yerushalayim, and all the things having been written by the Nevi'im about the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">j</a></sup>will be fulfilled.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ps 118:22">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LUKAS 23:11; YESHAYAH 50:6; YESHAYAH 53:3"> For he (the Bar Enosh Moshiach) will be delivered up to the Goyim and he will be ridiculed and he will be mistreated and he will be spat upon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:3–4; LUKAS 24:7; MATTITYAHU 27:63"> And, having laid the shot (whip) on him, they will kill him and after his histalkus (passing), on the Yom HaShlishi there will be Techiyas HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LUKAS 9:45; MARKOS 9:32; LUKAS 24:25"> And they had binah (understanding, comprehension) regarding none of these things, and this dvar was nistar (concealed) from them, and they had no understanding of the things being said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:29–34; MARKOS 10:46–52; LUKAS 16:20–21"> And it came about while Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach drew near to Yericho, a certain ivver (blind man) was sitting beside the road begging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:10–11; LUKAS 15:26"> And having heard a multitude traveling through, he was asking what this might be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 2:23; YOCHANAN 19:19; YOCHANAN 12:35–36"> And they reported to him that Yehoshua from Natzeret is passing by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:27; LUKAS 18:39; MATTITYAHU 15:22"> And he cried out saying, Ben Dovid! Chaneni! (Have mercy on me!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="BERESHIS 32:26–28; TEHILLIM 141:1; LUKAS 18:15"> And the ones going past him were rebuking him that he should be silent, but he just so much the more was crying out, Ben Dovid, chaneni!</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:31–34; MARKOS 10:48–52"> And stopping, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach commanded the ivver to be led to him. Having drawn near, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach asked him, What do you wish that I may do for you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 3:5–15; MATTITYAHU 20:21–22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:6"> And he said, Adoni, that I may regain my sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 8:3; LUKAS 8:48; LUKAS 17:19"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Regain your sight; your emunah (faith) has brought you refuah (healing).</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="TEHILLIM 146:8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:10–12; YESHAYAH 43:21"> And at once he saw again and was following Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, shouting, Baruch Hashem! And all the people, who were edei reiyah (eyewitnesses) of this, shouted, Baruch HaShem!</span>
