<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARKOS 3:2; LUKAS 20:20; TEHILLIM 62:4"> And it came to pass when he went into a bais of a certain one of the Rashei HaPerushim on Shabbos for betziat halechem, and they were watching him closely,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And there in front of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was a certain ish suffering from dropsy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:10; MARKOS 3:4; LUKAS 13:14–16"> And in reply, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach spoke to the Baalei Torah and Perushim, saying, Is it mutar (permissible) on Shabbos to give refuah or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 22:46; MATTITYAHU 21:25–27"> But they kept silent. And having taken hold of him, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach granted him refuah and sent him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKAS 13:15; SHEMOT 23:4–5; MATTITYAHU 12:11–12"> And to them Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Who of you having a ben or an ox fall into a well and will not ofen ort (immediately) pull him out on Shabbos?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKAS 20:40; LUKAS 21:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:10"> And they were not able to make a counter argument keneged (against) this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:3; LUKAS 11:43; MATTITYAHU 23:6"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach began speaking a mashal to the seudah (banquet supper) invitees, when he noticed how they had been picking out the rashei hamoshavot (chief seats) at the tish, saying to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MISHLE 25:6–7"> When you are invited by someone to a Chasunoh (wedding), you should not recline at tish in the rashei hamoshavot (chief seats), lest a more distinguished person than you may have been invited by him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MISHLE 16:18; MISHLE 11:2; MISHLE 3:35"> And he who invited you both shall come and say to you, Give place to this one, and then in bushah (shame) you proceed to occupy the seat of humiliation, the low seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MISHLE 25:6–7; MISHLE 15:33; SHMUEL ALEF 15:17"> But when you are invited, go and recline at tish in the moshav hashafel (low seat), so that when the one who has invited you comes, he may say to you, Chaver, move up to a higher place; then you will have kavod in the sight of all with you at the tish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 18:14; MATTITYAHU 23:12; MISHLE 29:23"> For everyone exalting himself shall be humbled, and the one humbling himself shall be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:46; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1–6; LUKAS 6:32–36"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was speaking also to the one who invited him, When you prepare a seudah or a Melave Malkah, do not invite your chaverim or your achim or your krovey mishpochot (relatives) or your shchenim haashirim, lest they also should invite you in return and repayment come to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 14:21; YESHAYAH 58:10; MISHLE 14:31"> But when you prepare a seudah (feast), invite the aniyim (the poor), the baalei hamum (the maimed), the pisechim (the lame), the ivrim (blind),</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MISHLE 19:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:15; YOCHANAN 5:29"> And Birkat Shomayim (the Blessing of Heaven) will befall you, because they do not have the means to repay you; for you will be repaid in the Yom Tekumat HaTzadikkim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Rv 20:5">a</a></sup>(Day of the Resurrection of the Righteous).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:9; LUKAS 22:30; LUKAS 13:29"> And when a certain one of those reclining at tish with Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach heard these things, he said to him, Ashrey is he who will eat lechem in the Malchut Hashem!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Isa 25:6">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 22:2–14; HISGALUS 22:17; YESHAYAH 55:1–7"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to him, A certain man was preparing a big seudah, and he invited many;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:27–29; MATTITYAHU 10:1–4; MATTITYAHU 22:3–4"> And at the dinner hour, he sent his eved to say to the seudah invitees, Come, because everything is ready now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:9–10; LUKAS 17:26–31; LUKAS 18:24"> But each began to come up with a teretz (excuse). The rishon (first) said to him, I bought a sadeh (field) and I am compelled to go out and look at it. Please be mekabel (receive, accept) my teretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And another said, I have bought chamesh pair of oxen, and I am going to try them out. Please be mekabel my teretz (excuse).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEVARIM 24:5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:33; LUKAS 14:26–28"> And another said, I took a wife and therefore I am not able to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKAS 14:13; MATTITYAHU 11:5; LUKAS 14:24"> And the eved returned and reported this to his Adon. Then the Baal Bayit became angry and said to his eved, Go out quickly into the rekhovot (streets) and lanes of the shtetl and bring in here the aniyim (the poor) and the baalei hamum (the maimed) and the ivrim (blind) and the pisechim (the lame).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:8; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:5–6"> And the eved said, Adoni, what you commanded has been done, and still there is room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 22:9–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:47–48"> And the Adon said to the eved, Go out to the rekhovot and along the boundaries and urge them to come in, in order that My Beis may be filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:43; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:46; YOCHANAN 8:24"> For I say to you that no one of those Bnei Adam who were invited will taste my seudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:24–27; LUKAS 12:1"> And large multitudes were accompanying him, and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach turned and said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:37; YOCHANAN 12:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:8"> If someone comes to me, and does not hate<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="hyperbolically, in comparison to Moshiach">c</a></sup>his own Abba and Em (mother) and isha and yeladim and achim and achayot and in addition also his own life,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="in the Olam Hazeh">d</a></sup>he is not able to be my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Moshiachʼs">e</a></sup>talmid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:38; LUKAS 9:23–25; MARKOS 8:34–37"> Whoever does not carry his own etz shel mesiros nefesh following Me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">f</a></sup>is not able to be my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Moshiachʼs">g</a></sup>talmid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MISHLE 24:27; MATTITYAHU 10:22; YEHOSHUA 24:19–24"> For who among you, wanting to build a migdal (tower), will not first sit down and rechen (calculate) the cost, to see if he has enough to complete it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Otherwise, having laid his yesod (foundation) and not being able to finish it, everyone seeing it may begin to make leitzonus (fun, mockery) of him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:11; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:11–14"> Saying, This man began to build and was not able to finish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MISHLE 20:18; MISHLE 25:8; MELACHIM BAIS 18:20–22"> Or what melech, going out to make milchamah (war) against another melech, will not first sit down and consider if he is able with aseret alafim (ten thousand) to meet the one with esrim elef (twenty thousand) coming against him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:20; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:6–10; LUKAS 12:58"> Or else, while the other is noch (yet) far away, he sends an embassy delegation and seeks terms for shalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:7–8; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–16; LUKAS 5:28"> So, then, none of you can become my talmid if you do not renounce all your &#91;idolatrous&#93; holdings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:6; MARKOS 9:49–50"> Therefore, melach (salt) is tov; but if even melach should become tasteless, with what will it be seasoned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:15; LUKAS 9:44; HISGALUS 2:11"> It is useless either for the land or for the dung hill; they throw it away. The one who has ears to hear, shema!</span>
