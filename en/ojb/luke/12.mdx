<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MARKOS 4:22; LUKAS 8:17; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:10"> And nothing that men hide in a cover up is concealed which will not be revealed, and nothing held nistar (hidden) which will not be laid bare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:27; MATTITYAHU 12:36; KOHELET 10:20"> So then, what things you said in the choshech, will be heard in the ohr; and what you whispered in the ear bchadrei chadarim (in a most secret place) will be shouted from the roof tops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:28; YIRMEYAH 1:8; HISGALUS 2:10"> I say to you, my chaverim, do not have pachad (terror) of the killers of the basar, who after that have nothing more they can do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:4; MATTITYAHU 10:28; HISGALUS 14:7"> But I will show you someone of whom you should have yirah; fear the One who after killing the basar has the samchut to throw into Gehinnom. Ken, I say to you, have yirah (fear) of this One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKAS 12:24; TEHILLIM 145:15–16; TEHILLIM 147:9"> Are not chamesh sparrows sold for two assarion? And not even one of them has been overlooked in the eynayim of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:30–31; MATTITYAHU 6:26; YESHAYAH 43:3–4"> But even the hairs of your rosh have all been inventoried. Never fear. You are of more worth than many sparrows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:32–33; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:9–10"> And I say to you, whoever will declare the Ani Maamin public hoda'ah (acknowledgement) of me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="as Moshiach">a</a></sup>before Bnei Adam, the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">b</a></sup>will make public hodaah (acknowledgement) of him before the malachim of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:12; LUKAS 9:26; MARKOS 8:38"> But the one having made hakhchashah (denial) of me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="as the Bar Enosh Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">c</a></sup>before Bnei Adam will be denied before the malachim of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:31–32; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:16; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:13"> And everyone who will say a dvar against the Bar Enosh,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">d</a></sup>he will be given selicha (forgiveness). But the one having committed Chillul Hashem gidduf (blasphemy) against the Ruach Hakodesh will not be given selicha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:17–20; LUKAS 21:12–14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:5–7"> And when they bring you in before the shuls and the rulers and the manhigim, do not have a lev rogez about what you should speak in your own hitstaddekut (defense), or about your legal brief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKAS 21:15; MATTITYAHU 10:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:8"> For the Ruach Hakodesh will be your rabbi teaching you in the same hour what it is necessary to say.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ex 4:12">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 6:45; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:5; YECHEZKEL 33:31"> And someone out of the multitude said to him, Rabbi, speak to my ach to share with me the yerushah (inheritance).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:20"> But he said to him, Ben Adam, who appointed me a shofet or an arbitrator over you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:5; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:6–10; TEHILLIM 119:36–37"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Take care and be shomer against all chamdanut (covetousness), because the Chayyei HaAdam does not consist in the abundance of his possessions.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Job 20:20; 31:24; Ps 62:10">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 26:12–14; HOSHEA 2:8; BERESHIS 41:47–49"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach spoke a mashal (parable) to them saying, An aza (certain) oisher (rich man) had land that produced a good crop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:42; YESHAYAH 58:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:13"> And he was thinking to himself, saying, What should I do? Because I do not have a place where I will store my crops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:15; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:15; LUKAS 12:21"> And he said, This I will do. I will tear down my asim (granaries, storehouses) and I will build larger asim. And there I will gather all my grain and my produce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MISHLE 27:1; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:13–15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:32"> And I will say to my neshamah, Neshamah, you have an ample store of goods for many years to come. Take your ease, LEEKHOL, VLISHTOT, VLISHMOACH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Koh 8:15">g</a></sup>(to eat, and to drink, and to be merry).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="IYOV 27:8; YIRMEYAH 17:11; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:7"> But Hashem said to him, Goilem! Halailah hazeh your nashamah is required of you. Now to whom will be given what you prepared?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Jer 17:11; Job 27:8; Ps 39:6; 49:10">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:19–20; LUKAS 12:33; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5"> Such is the one hoarding up for himself and not having osher toward Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:25–34; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:6; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:5"> And Rebbe, Melech, HaMoshiach said to his talmidim, Therefore, I say to you: do not have a LEV ROGEZ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Dt 28:65">i</a></sup>for your Chayyim, about your okhel (food) or your basar (body), what you might put on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="IYOV 2:6; MISHLE 13:8; IYOV 2:4"> For the neshamah is more than okhel and the basar more than gartel (belt) and shtreimel (expensive hat).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gn 47:18; Ps 16:9-10; Job 19:25,27; Isa 53:11">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:31; TEHILLIM 147:9; LUKAS 12:7"> Consider the ravens! They do not sow nor reap, they have no storeroom or asam (granary), yet Hashem feeds them. Of how much more worth are you than the OPH HASHOMAYIM.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Job 38:41; Ps 147:9">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:27; MATTITYAHU 5:36; LUKAS 19:3"> And can any of you by means of a LEV ROGEZ add one cubit to your span of Chayyim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:7; TEHILLIM 39:6; KOHELET 7:13"> If then you are not able to do even a small thing, why have a LEV ROGEZ about the rest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:28–30; LUKAS 12:24; MELACHIM ALEF 10:1–13"> Consider the lilies! How they grow! A lily does not labor nor spin. But I say to you, not even Sh'lomo HaMelech in all his kavod was arrayed like one of these.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="1Kgs 10:4-7">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 14:31; YESHAYAH 40:6; MATTITYAHU 6:30"> And if Hashem so enrobes the grass of the field, which is here hayom (today) and thrown into the eish makhar (tomorrow), how much more will Hashem enrobe you, you ones of little bitachon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:31; LUKAS 12:22; LUKAS 22:35"> And don't keep striving after okhel (food) and skikuy (drink), and don't have a lev rogez (anxious heart).</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:32; MATTITYAHU 6:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:17"> For all these things the Goyim of the Olam Hazeh strive after, but your Av<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="sh'ba'Shomayim">m</a></sup>has daas (knowledge) that you need these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:33; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:5; TEHILLIM 34:9"> But seek the Malchut Hashem, and these things will be added to you as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:28; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:3–5; HISGALUS 1:6"> Do not have pachad (terror, fear), Eder Katan (Little Flock), because it is the ratzon, the chefetz (desire) of your Av<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="sh'ba'Shomayim">n</a></sup>to give you the Malchut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:34–35; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:45"> Sell your possessions and give tzedakah. Make for yourselves the baitel (wallet) that doesn't wear out, an inexhaustible otzar (treasure) in Shomayim, where no ganav (thief) comes near nor moth destroys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:1–3; MATTITYAHU 6:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:20"> For where your otzar (treasure) is, there also will be your lev (heart).</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:14; MATTITYAHU 25:4–10"> Tighten your gartels for action and have your menorahs lit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:20–21; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:7–8; HISGALUS 3:20"> And you should be like bnei Adam who expectantly khakeh l'vo'o shel (await the arrival of) their Adon when he returns from the Chasunoh, in order that, when he comes and knocks, ofen ort they may open the delet for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:14; MATTITYAHU 24:42; YOCHANAN 13:4–5"> Ashrey are those avadim (servants), whom, having come, the Adon will find keeping shomer. Omein, I say to you, that he will fasten his gartel, and have those avadim (servants) sit down to tish, and he will come and serve them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:4–5; MATTITYAHU 25:6"> And if in the second or if in the third watch he comes and finds it thus, ashrey (happy, blessed) are those.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:10; HISGALUS 3:3; HISGALUS 16:15"> But have daas of this, that if the Baal Bayit had had da'as in what hour the ganav (thief) comes, he would not have allowed his bais to be broken into.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:6; MATTITYAHU 25:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:11"> So you be shomer, for the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">o</a></sup>comes in an hour you do not think.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MARKOS 13:37; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:7; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:8"> And Kefa said, Adoneinu, are you speaking this mashal for us or for all?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:45–46; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:1–4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:17"> And HaAdon said, Who then is the sochen haneeman and navon (faithful and wise steward) whom HaAdon will appoint over his avadim to give them their okhel (food) allowance at the proper time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Ashrey is that eved, whom, having come, his Adon will find doing thus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="LUKAS 22:29–30; MATTITYAHU 24:47; LUKAS 19:17–19"> Omein, I say to you, that he will appoint him over all that he has.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:48–50; HISGALUS 18:7–8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:18–19"> But if that eved says in his lev, Adoni delays to come to me, and if that eved begins to beat the avadim (servants) and the shfakhot (maid servants), and to gluttonize and get down in his schnapps,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LUKAS 12:40; MATTITYAHU 24:51; TEHILLIM 11:5"> HaAdon of that eved will come on a day which he does not expect and at an hour of which he does not have da'as, and will cut him in pieces and assign him the portion of the Apikoros with the koferim (unbelievers).</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:17; YOCHANAN 9:41; YOCHANAN 12:48"> That eved, who had daas of the ratzon of his Adon and did not get prepared or do according to his ratzon, will be beaten with a klap (blow) and not a few.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Dt 25:2">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:1; VAYIKRA 5:17; LUKAS 16:10–12"> But the one not having had daas, but having done things worthy of a klap, will receive but a few. But to everyone to whom much was given, much will be required from him, and to whom was entrusted much, even more achraius (accountability) will be required.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Lv 5:17; Num 15:27-30">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="MALACHI 4:1; LUKAS 12:51–52; MATTITYAHU 3:10–12"> I came to throw Eish on the earth, and how I wish it was already kindled!</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:27–28; YOCHANAN 19:30; YOCHANAN 7:6–8"> And I have a tevilah I have to undergo, and how I am distressed until it is completed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:34–36; LUKAS 12:49; MATTITYAHU 24:7–10"> Do you think that I came to bring Shalom on the earth? No, I tell you, but rather machaloket (division, controversy).</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:1–4; YOCHANAN 15:18–21; MICHOH 7:5–6"> For there will be from now in one bais (household) chamesh (five) having been divided, shalosha against shenayim and shenayim against shalosha,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="MICHOH 7:6; MATTITYAHU 10:21–22; MATTITYAHU 24:10"> AV will be divided against BEN and BEN against AV, EM (mother) against BAT and BAT against EM, KALLAH BACHAMOT (daughter-in-law against mother-in-law) and CHAMOT against KALLAH.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Mic 7:6">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:2–4; MELACHIM ALEF 18:44–45"> And he was saying also to the multitudes, When you see the anan rising over the maarav (west), ofen ort you say, Geshem (rain) is coming, and so it happens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="IYOV 37:17; MATTITYAHU 20:12"> And when there is a south wind blowing, you say, It will be kham (hot), and it happens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:3; MATTITYAHU 24:32–33; MATTITYAHU 11:25"> Tzevu'im! You have daas how to interpret the appearance of the earth and the sky; how is it, then, that you do not have daas of how to interpret HaZman HaZeh (This Time)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:29; YOCHANAN 7:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:26–38"> And why also for yourselves do you not judge what is yashar (straight, right)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="MISHLE 25:8–9; MATTITYAHU 5:23–26; LUKAS 13:24–28"> For as you go with your ish riv (opponent in a lawsuit) to appear before the magistrate, on the derech make an effort to settle with him, lest he drag you to the shofet, and the shofet will hand over you to the shoter, and the shoter will throw you into the beit hasohar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="MARKOS 12:42; MATTITYAHU 18:34; MATTITYAHU 25:46"> I say to you, by no means may you come out, until even the last peruta (small coin) you pay back.</span>
