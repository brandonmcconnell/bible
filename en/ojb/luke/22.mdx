<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:6–23; MATTITYAHU 26:2–5; YOCHANAN 11:55–57"> And the Chag HaMatzot was approaching, the feast called Pesach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:47–53; MATTITYAHU 12:14; LUKAS 20:19"> And the Rashei Hakohanim and the Sofrim were seeking how they might destroy him, for they were afraid of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:2; MARKOS 14:10–11; YOCHANAN 13:26–27"> And HaSatan entered into Yehudah, the one being called Yehudah from K'riot, being one of the Shneym Asar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:24; LUKAS 22:52"> And having departed, Yehudah spoke with the Rashei Hakohanim and Ketzinim (Officers) of the Beis Hamikdash how he might hand him over to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:15–16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:18; MATTITYAHU 27:3–5"> And they got a lot of geshmak out of this, and they agreed to pay him kesef.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Zech 11:12">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MARKOS 14:2; MATTITYAHU 26:5"> And Yehudah consented and he was seeking an opportunity to betray him when there was no multitude with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:18; MARKOS 14:12–16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:7"> Shoin (already) now came the Yom HaMatzot, in which it was necessary to sacrifice the Pesach Korban, the Seh (Lamb).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Isa 53:7; Ex 12:18-20; Dt 16:5-8">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MARKOS 14:13–16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:13"> And he sent Kefa and Yochanan, having said, Go and make our preparations that we may eat and have the Pesach Seder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And they said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Where do you wish that we should prepare it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:4; MATTITYAHU 26:18–19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:26–29"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Hinei. When you enter into HaIr, you will meet a man carrying a jar of mayim. Follow him into the bais (house) into which he enters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:28; HISGALUS 3:20; LUKAS 19:34"> And you will say to the Baal Bayit, The Rebbe says to you, Where is the mekom linah (guest room) where I may conduct the Pesach Seder with my talmidim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:14–15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:13; YOCHANAN 2:25"> And that one will show you a large aliyyah having been furnished: there prepare the Seder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 21:33; LUKAS 19:32; YOCHANAN 2:5"> And having departed, they found things just as he had told them, and they prepared the Seder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:20; MARKOS 14:17; DEVARIM 16:6–7"> And when the hour came, he reclined at tish and the Moshiachʼs Shlichim were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 12:50; YOCHANAN 4:34; YOCHANAN 17:1"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, With great tshuka (deep and sincere desire, longing) I have desired to eat this Pesach with you before I suffer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 14:15; HISGALUS 19:9; LUKAS 22:30"> For I say to you, I may by no means eat it until it is fulfilled in the Malchut Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKAS 22:19; TEHILLIM 116:13; DEVARIM 8:10"> And having taken the Cup of Redemption, having made the bracha, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Take this, and share it among yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:29; LUKAS 22:16; MARKOS 14:25"> For I say to you, from now on by no means shall I drink from the p'ri hagefen until the Malchut Hashem comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:23–29; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:16; MARKOS 14:22–24"> And having taken the Afikoman and having made the hamotzi, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach broke the matzah and gave it to them, saying, This is my BASAR<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ex 12:8">c</a></sup>being given for you: this do in zikaron (remembrance) of me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Lv 5:7; 6:23; Ezek 43:21; Isa 53:8">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:28; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:25; ZECHARYAH 9:11"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach took the kos (cup) similarly after they ate, saying, This kos (cup) is HaBrit HaChadasha in my dahm, being shed for you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ex 24:8; Isa 42:6; Jer 31:31-34; Zech 9:11; Isa 53:10-12">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="TEHILLIM 41:9; YOCHANAN 13:26; YOCHANAN 13:21–22"> Nevertheless, hinei, the hand of the one betraying me is with me on the tish (table).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ps 41:9">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:23; TEHILLIM 109:6–15; TEHILLIM 55:12–15"> Because the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">g</a></sup>indeed goes according to the thing having been determined: but oy to that man through whom he is betrayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:22–25; MARKOS 14:19; MATTITYAHU 26:22"> And they began to discuss among themselves who then it might be among them that would do this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKAS 9:46; MARKOS 9:34; MARKOS 10:37–41"> And there came about also a dispute among them, as to which of them seemed to be the Gadol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:25–28; MARKOS 10:41–45"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, The melachim (kings) of the Goyim domineer them and the ones having shlita (control) over the Goyim are called benefactors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:5; LUKAS 9:48; MATTITYAHU 18:3–5"> But with you it is not so, but he of the Gedolim among you, let him be as the youngest, and he of the Manhigim, as the one serving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:28; LUKAS 12:37; YOCHANAN 13:5–16"> For who is HaGadol? The one reclining at tish or the one serving? Is it not the one reclining at tish? But I am in your midst as one serving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:18; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:25"> But you are the ones having remained with me in my nisyonos (temptations).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:34; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:12; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5"> And I assign shlita (authority) to you, just as Avi assigned shlita to me; a Malchut (Kingdom),</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:28; MATTITYAHU 8:11; HISGALUS 2:26–27"> That you may eat and drink at my tish in my Malchut, and you will sit upon kissot (thrones) judging the Shneym Asar Shivtei Yisroel (Twelve Tribes of Yisroel).</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:8; ZECHARYAH 3:1; IYOV 1:6–12"> Shim'on, Shim'on, hinei, HaSatan asked for you, to sift you like wheat.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Job 1:6-12; Am 9:9">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:8–10; YOCHANAN 21:15–17; YOCHANAN 17:9–11"> But I davened for you, that your emunah may not fail: and when you have made teshuva, give chizzuk to your Achim b'Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MARKOS 14:31; MATTITYAHU 26:33–35; MARKOS 14:29"> But Shim'on Kefa said to him, Adoni, with you I am prepared even to go to the beis hasohar, even to die al kiddush ha-Shem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:38; MATTITYAHU 26:34; MARKOS 14:30"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, I say to you, a tarnegol will not crow hayom until shalosh pe'amim you, Kefa, will make hakhchashah (denial) of your daas of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:9–10; LUKAS 10:4; TEHILLIM 23:1"> He said to them, When I sent you without a money belt and a bag and sandals, did you lack anything? And they said, Nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:22–25; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:14–15"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, But now the one having a money belt, let him take it; likewise also a bag; and the one not having, let him sell his kaftan and let him buy a cherev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:12; LUKAS 23:32; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:21"> For I say to you, that it is necessary that what stands written be fulfilled in me, VES POSHEIM NIMNAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Isa 53:12">i</a></sup>(and with lawless persons he was numbered). For indeed what was written about me is being fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LUKAS 22:49; MATTITYAHU 26:52–54; YOCHANAN 18:36"> And they said, Adoni, hinei, here are shtei charavot (two swords). And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, It is enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LUKAS 21:37; YOCHANAN 18:1–2; MATTITYAHU 21:1"> And having gone out, he went according to his kvius (regimen) to the Har HaZeytim, and his talmidim followed him also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:13; MATTITYAHU 26:36–46; LUKAS 22:46"> And having come to the place, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Daven and offer tefillos, lest you enter lidey nisayon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Mt 6:13">j</a></sup>(into temptation).</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:39; MARKOS 14:35; LUKAS 18:11"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach had withdrawn from them about a stoneʼs throw, and, having fallen down, he was davening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:27–28; MATTITYAHU 26:39; MATTITYAHU 26:42"> Saying, Avi, im yirtzeh Hashem, if You are willing, take this kos (cup) away from me. Nevertheless not the ratzon of me but rtzonecha (Thy will) be done.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Mt 6:10">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:11; MATTITYAHU 26:53; TEHILLIM 91:11–12"> And a malach min HaShomayim appeared to him, strengthening him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="EKHAH 1:12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:7; TEHILLIM 22:12–21"> And having been in agony, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was davening more fervently; and his sweat became like drops of dahm falling down upon the adamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MARKOS 14:40–41; MATTITYAHU 26:43; MATTITYAHU 26:40"> And having got up from davening and having come to the talmidim, he found them sleeping, because of agmat nefesh (grief).</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LUKAS 22:40; YONAH 1:6; LUKAS 21:34–36"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Why are you sleeping? Get up, daven, lest you enter lidey nisayon (into temptation).</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MARKOS 14:41–50; YOCHANAN 18:2–11; LUKAS 22:3–6"> Yet as he was speaking, hinei, a multitude! And going at their head was the one being called Yehudah, one of the Shneym Asar, and he drew near to Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach to give him a neshikah (kiss).</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="MARKOS 14:44–46; SHMUEL BAIS 20:9–10; MISHLE 27:6"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Yehudah, do you with a neshikah the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">l</a></sup>betray?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="LUKAS 22:38"> But having seen what was coming, the ones around Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Adoni, should we strike with the cherev (sword)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:10–11; MARKOS 14:47; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:19"> And a certain one of them struck the eved (servant) of the Rashei Hakohanim and cut off his right ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:21; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:1; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:21–23"> And, in reply, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said, Blaib shtein! (Stop!) No more of this! And having touched the ear, he healed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="LUKAS 22:4; MARKOS 14:48–49; YOCHANAN 17:12"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to the ones having come against him, the Rashei Hakohanim and the Beis Hamikdash shomrim and the Ziknei HaAm, Do you have the chutzpah to come out as against a ganav (thief) with charavot and clubs?</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:18"> Yom Yom (Daily) I was with you in the Beis Hamikdash and you did not stretch out your hands against me, but this is your hour and the shlita of choshech (power of evil, sitra ahra).</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="MARKOS 14:53–54; MATTITYAHU 26:57–58; YOCHANAN 18:12–17"> And having seized Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, they led him away and brought him into the bais of the Kohen Gadol. And Kefa was following from a distance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="TEHILLIM 26:4–5; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:15–17; MARKOS 14:66–72"> And some kindled a hadlakah (bonfire) in the middle of the courtyard, and having sat down together, Kefa was sitting in their midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:17; MARKOS 14:6; MARKOS 14:17"> And a certain shifcha, seeing him sitting in the light of the hadlakah (bonfire), staring intently at Kefa, said, And this one was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9; YOCHANAN 18:25; MATTITYAHU 26:70"> But Kefa denied it, saying, I do not have daas of him, isha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:25; MATTITYAHU 26:71–72; MARKOS 14:69–70"> And after a short while, another, having seen him, said, And you are of them! But Kefa said, Man, I am not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:26–27; MARKOS 14:69–70; MATTITYAHU 26:73–74"> And about one hour had passed, and still another was insisting, saying, Beemes (in actuality) this one was with him also, for he also is a Gelili (inhabitant of the Galil)!</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:74–75; LUKAS 22:34; MARKOS 14:71–72"> But Kefa said, Ben Adam, I do not have daas what you are saying. And immediately, while Kefa was speaking, a tarnegol crowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="LUKAS 22:34; YESHAYAH 57:15–18; YIRMEYAH 31:18–20"> And having turned, Adoneinu looked at Kefa, and Kefa was reminded of the dvar of Moshiach Adoneinu when he said to him, Before the tarnegol crows hayom (today) you will make hakhchashah (denial) of me shalosh pe'amim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:75; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:12; MARKOS 14:72"> And having gone outside Kefa wept bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:22; MARKOS 14:55–65; IYOV 16:9–10"> And the men, the ones having Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach in custody, were ridiculing and beating him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="SHOFETIM 16:25; SHOFETIM 16:21"> And having blindfolded him, they were setting a she'elah in front of him, saying, Give a dvar nevuah, who is the one having given you a klap (blow)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="LUKAS 12:10; MATTITYAHU 12:31–32; MATTITYAHU 27:39"> And with much other Chillul Hashem gadfanut they were speaking against Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:1; MARKOS 15:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:5"> And when boker came, the Ziknei HaAm were gathered together, both the Rashei Hakohanim and the Sofrim, and they led him away to their Sanhedrin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:3–5; YOCHANAN 8:43–45; MATTITYAHU 26:63–68"> Saying, If you are the Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach, tell us. But he said to them, If I say this to you, you will by no means have emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="LUKAS 20:41–44; LUKAS 20:3–7"> And if I set a she'elah before you, you will by no means give answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="DANIEL 7:13–14; TEHILLIM 110:1; MARKOS 16:19"> But from now on the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">m</a></sup>will be YOSHEV LIMIN at the gevurat Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Ps 110:1">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="LUKAS 23:3; MATTITYAHU 27:11; MATTITYAHU 26:64"> And everyone said, Then you are the Ben HaElohim? Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, You are saying that I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:65–66; MARKOS 14:63–64"> And to them they said, Why still do we have need of an eidus (witness)? For we ourselves heard from his peh (mouth)!</span>
