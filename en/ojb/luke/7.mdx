<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 8:5–13; LUKAS 7:1–10; MATTITYAHU 7:28–29"> When he completed all his divrei torah in the oznei haAm (ears of the people), Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach entered into K'far-Nachum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 5:2–3; BERESHIS 24:27; YOCHANAN 4:46–47"> Now a certain eved of a centurion, an eved dear to him, was having a machla (illness) and was near mavet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 8:5; YOCHANAN 4:47; LUKAS 9:38"> And having had daas of Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach, the centurion sent to him Zekenim (Elders) of the Yehudim, asking him to come and to give refuah to his eved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:4; MATTITYAHU 10:11; LUKAS 7:6–7"> And when they had come to Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach, they were earnestly entreating him, saying, He is a worthy man that you grant this for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:11–12; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:3–9; MELACHIM ALEF 5:1"> For he is one of the chasidei ummot haOlam<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e. non-Jews who treat Jews kindly">a</a></sup>who loves our Jewish people and he built for us our shul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MARKOS 5:24; BERESHIS 32:10; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:6"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach was starting out on his derech with them; and when he was already not far from the bais, the centurion sent beloved re'im (friends), saying to him, Adoni, do not trouble yourself further, for I am not worthy for you to come under my roof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 107:20; TEHILLIM 33:9; LUKAS 4:36"> Therefore, I did not consider myself worthy to come to you. But just say the dvar, and my eved will receive refuah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 107:20">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:25–26"> For I too am a man placed under the yad memshalah (the governing authority), having chaiyalim (soldiers) under myself, and I say to this one, Go, and he goes, and to another, Come, and he comes, and to my eved, Do this, and he does it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:1–3; MATTITYAHU 8:10"> And having heard these things, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach was mispoyel (deeply impressed) at him, and, having turned to the multitude following him, he said, I say to you, nowhere even in Yisroel have I found such great emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:50–53; MATTITYAHU 8:13; MATTITYAHU 15:28"> And when the ones having been sent returned to the bais, they found the eved being shalem bguf (healthy).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:38"> And it came about on the next day that Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach proceeded to a shtetl called Naim, and his talmidim were traveling along with him as well as a great company of people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKAS 8:42; LUKAS 8:52; MELACHIM ALEF 17:9"> Now as Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach approached the shaar of the shtetl, hinei, an ish met (dead man) was being carried out for kevurah (burial); he was the ben yachid (only son) of his Em, and she was an almanah (widow), and a considerable crowd from the shtetl was with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TEHILLIM 103:13; YESHAYAH 63:9; TEHILLIM 86:15"> And when Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu saw her, he had rachmei shomayim (heavenly compassion) on her and said, Do not weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:25; YOCHANAN 11:43–44; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:40–41"> And having approached, he touched the aron met (coffin); and the bearers stood still, and he said, Bocher, to you I say, get up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 13:21; MELACHIM BAIS 4:32–37; MELACHIM ALEF 17:23–24"> And the niftar (deceased person) sat up and he began to speak, and Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach gave him to his Em (mother).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:8; LUKAS 7:39; LUKAS 1:68"> And everyone was filled with yirat Shomayim and they were shouting, Baruch Hashem! And they were saying, A navi gadol (great prophet) is among us, and Hashem has visited His people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:24; MARKOS 6:14; MATTITYAHU 9:26"> And this report about him went out into all Yehudah and into all the surrounding countryside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:26; MATTITYAHU 11:2–19"> And Yochananʼs talmidim reported to him about all these things. And when he had summoned a certain two of his talmidim, Yochanan</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 9:6–7; YOCHANAN 4:25; BERESHIS 22:18"> Sent them to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu, saying, Are you Hu HaBah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Moshiach">c</a></sup>or should we be looking for another?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKAS 7:19"> And when the men had come to him, they said, Yochanan of the tevilah of teshuva sent us to you, saying, Are you Hu HaBah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Moshiach">d</a></sup>or should we be looking for another?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MARKOS 3:10; TEHILLIM 90:7–9; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:6"> Genoi at that time Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach gave refuah to many with machlot and afflictions and ruchot raot and to many ivverim he granted sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YESHAYAH 35:5–6; YESHAYAH 61:1–3; LUKAS 4:18"> And in reply he said to them, Go and tell Yochanan what you have seen.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="as edei reiyah, eyewitnesses">e</a></sup>IVRIM TIRENAH, pisechim (lame persons) walk, the metzorim are cleansed, and chereshim hear, the mesim are restored to life, ANIYIM have the Besuras HaGeulah preached to them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isa 29:18,19; 35:5,6; 61:1,2">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:6; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:7–8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:32–33"> And ashrey is whoever does not find a michshol (stumbling block, obstacle)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 57:14">g</a></sup>in me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="as Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:14; YOCHANAN 1:23; LUKAS 1:80"> And when the messengers of Yochanan had departed, he began to say to the multitudes about Yochanan, What did you go out bamidbar to see? A reed shaken by the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YESHAYAH 59:17; ESTHER 8:15; ESTHER 1:3"> But what did you go out to see? A man dressed in a dandyʼs soft clothing? The ones with glorious apparel are living in luxury as courtiers in the palaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LUKAS 1:76; YOCHANAN 5:35; LUKAS 20:6"> But what did you go out to see? A navi? Ken, I say to you, and one more than a navi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MALACHI 3:1; LUKAS 1:76; MARKOS 1:2"> This one is he about whom it has been written, HINENI SHOLEIACH MALAKHI (Behold, I send my messenger) before Your face, who will prepare Your derech in front of You.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Mal 3:1">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:16–17; MATTITYAHU 11:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:25–27"> I say to you, among those born of isha there is no one greater than Yochanan, yet he who is least in the Malchut Hashem is greater than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LUKAS 3:12; LUKAS 7:35; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:25"> (And when kol haAm (all the People) and the mochesim heard this, they acknowledged the Tzidkat Hashem&#91;Righteousness of G-d&#93;, having been submitted to the tevilah of teshuva of Yochanan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LUKAS 13:34; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:1; MATTITYAHU 22:35"> But the Perushim and the Baalei HaTorah rejected the tachlis (purpose, aim) of Hashem for their lives, refusing the tevilah of teshuva of Yochanan.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:16–19; EKHAH 2:13; MARKOS 4:30"> Therefore, to what will I compare the people of hador hazeh (this generation) and what are they like?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:16–19; YIRMEYAH 5:3–5; MISHLE 17:16"> They are like yeladim sitting in the marketplace and calling out to one another; and they say, We played the chalil (flute) for you and you did not dance; we sang a kina (lament, funeral dirge) and you did not weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKAS 1:15; MARKOS 1:6; YOCHANAN 8:52"> For Yochanan of the tevilah of teshuva has come not eating lechem nor drinking yayin, and you say, He has a shed (demon)!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LUKAS 15:2; LUKAS 7:36; YOCHANAN 12:2"> The Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">j</a></sup>has come eating and drinking, and you say, Hinei, a man who is a zolel (glutton) and a shikkor (drunkard), a re'a (friend) of mochesim and chote'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MISHLE 8:32–36; MATTITYAHU 11:19; HOSHEA 14:9"> Yet chochmah is justified by all of her yeladim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MARKOS 14:3–9; MATTITYAHU 26:5–6; LUKAS 14:1"> Now a certain one of the Perushim was requesting Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach at tish, and, he, having entered into the bais of the Parush, reclined to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MARKOS 14:3–9; MATTITYAHU 26:6–13; MATTITYAHU 21:31"> And an isha (woman) was in the shtetl, an isha chotet (a woman of sin), and, when she had daas that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach reclines at tish in the bais of the Parush, she brought an alabaster flask of costly perfume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="TEHILLIM 126:5–6; TEHILLIM 51:17; BERESHIS 18:4"> She stood behind him, and then she began weeping at his feet, and with her tears she began to wash the feet of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and she was drying them with her hair, and she was kissing his feet and was anointing them with perfume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LUKAS 7:16; LUKAS 15:2; MARKOS 7:21"> But when the Parush who had invited Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach saw this, he said to himself, If this one were a navi, he would have had daas who and what sort of isha is touching him, because she is an isha chotet (woman of sin).</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:19; LUKAS 5:31; YECHEZKEL 33:31"> And in reply, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Shim'on, I have something to say to you. And the Parush said, Speak, Rabbi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LUKAS 7:47; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:20; MATTITYAHU 18:28"> Two persons were debtors to a certain creditor; the one owed a choiv (debt) of chamesh meot (five hundred) denarii and the other a choiv (debt) of chamishim (fifty).</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="DANIEL 9:18–19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:24"> Not being able to repay, the creditor graciously forgave both debts. Therefore which of them will have more ahavah for him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="TEHILLIM 116:16–18; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:13–16; LUKAS 7:47"> In reply, Shim'on said, I suppose the one whom the creditor forgave more. And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Your judgment is gantze nachon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:10; SHOFETIM 19:21; BERESHIS 18:4"> And having turned to the isha, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to Shim'on, Do you see this isha? I entered your bais, you did not give me mayim for my feet, but she has washed my feet with her tears, and wiped them with the hairs of her rosh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gn 18:4; Judg 19:21">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:5; SHMUEL BAIS 19:39; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:16"> You did not give me neshikah (kiss), but she, from the time I entered, did not stop kissing my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="TEHILLIM 23:5; KOHELET 9:8; MATTITYAHU 6:17"> With oil my rosh (head) you did not anoint. But she with perfume anointed my feet.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Ps 23:5; Koh 9:8">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:19; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18; YESHAYAH 1:18"> Oib azoi (consequently), I say to you, her chatta'im, which are many, have been granted selicha (forgiven), for with rabbah ahavah she loved. But he who is mekabel selicha (accepting forgiveness) only a little, has ahavah only a little.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:2; MARKOS 2:5; LUKAS 5:20"> And he said to her, Your averos have been granted selicha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="LUKAS 5:20–21; MATTITYAHU 9:3; MARKOS 2:7"> And the ones reclining at tish with Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach began to say to themselves, Who is this who even grants selicha to averos?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="MARKOS 5:34; LUKAS 8:48; LUKAS 18:42"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to the isha, Your emunah has gained you Yeshu'at Eloheinu. L'chi l'shalom (Go in shalom!).</span>
