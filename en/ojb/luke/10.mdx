<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARKOS 6:7–13; LUKAS 9:52; BAMIDBAR 11:24–26"> And after these things, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu gave smichah to shivim (seventy) others and sent them on ahead of him shnayim shnayim (two by two) into every shtetl and place where he was about to arrive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:36–38; YOCHANAN 4:35–38; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:6–9"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach was saying to them, Indeed the Katzir is plentiful, but the poalim of the Katzir are few; therefore, ask the Adon of the Katzir that he might send out poalim into his Katzir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:29; YECHEZKEL 2:3–6"> Go I send you as kevasim (sheep) into the midst of ze'evim (wolves).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKAS 22:35; MELACHIM BAIS 4:29; LUKAS 10:4–12"> Do not carry a bag for kesef, nor a schnorrerʼs sack (beggarʼs bag), nor sandals, and, along the derech, dispense with time-consuming Birkat Shalomʼs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:6; MATTITYAHU 10:12–13; YESHAYAH 57:19"> Into whatever bais you enter, first say, Shalom to this bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:16; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:15–16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:6"> And if there is a ben hashalom there, your shalom will rest upon him. Otherwise, on you it will return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:17–18; MATTITYAHU 10:10–11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:6"> Remain in the same bais eating and drinking the things with them. For worthy is the poel (worker) of his loin (wages). Do not move from bais to bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:27; MATTITYAHU 10:40; LUKAS 10:10"> And into whichever shtetl you enter and they receive you, eat the okhel (food) being set before you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 3:2; YOCHANAN 3:5; LUKAS 10:11"> And give refuah (healing) to the cholim (sick people) and say to them, The Malchut Hashem has come near you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:6; MATTITYAHU 10:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:51"> And into whatever shtetl you enter and they do not receive you, having gone out into the rekhovot (streets) of that shtetl,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 10:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:51; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:26"> Say, Even the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Goyishe unclean">a</a></sup>dust from your shtetl, which clings to us, we shake off from our feet as an omen of din (judgment) against you. But have daas of this, that the Malchut Hashem has come near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:15; MATTITYAHU 11:24; EKHAH 4:6"> I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">b</a></sup>say to you, that for S'dom in Yom HaHu,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Yom HaDin, the Day of Judgment">c</a></sup>it will be more bearable than it will be for that shtetl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YESHAYAH 23:1–18; YOEL 3:4–8; YECHEZKEL 26:1–26"> Woe to you, Korazin, oy to you, Beit-Tzaidah, because if in Tzor and Tzidon had occurred the gevurot that have happened in you, long ago, they, sitting in sackcloth and ashes, would have made teshuva.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:22–25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:1; LUKAS 12:47–48"> But for Tzor and Tzidon it will be more bearable in the Yom HaDin than for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YESHAYAH 14:13–15; MATTITYAHU 4:13; LUKAS 13:28"> And you, K'far-Nachum, surely not up to Shomayim will you be exalted? To Gehinnom you will descend!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:20; MATTITYAHU 10:40; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:8"> The one listening to you listens to me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach">d</a></sup>and the one rejecting you, rejects me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach">e</a></sup>But the one rejecting me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach">f</a></sup>rejects the One<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="the G-d of Yisroel">g</a></sup>who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:20; MARKOS 16:17; LUKAS 9:1"> And the Shivim returned with simcha, saying, Adoneinu, even the shedim submit to us b'Shem of you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, Yeshua">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HISGALUS 12:7–9; YESHAYAH 14:12; HISGALUS 9:1"> And he said to them, I was seeing HaSatan falling like lightning from Shomayim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Isa 14:12">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="TEHILLIM 91:13; MARKOS 16:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:20"> Hinei I have given to you the koach and the samchut to walk on nechashim (snakes) and akrabim (scorpions), and on all the koach of HaOyev, and nothing may by any means injure you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="HISGALUS 20:15; HISGALUS 20:12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:23"> But in this do not have a lev sameach, on account of the ruchot submitting to you, but have lev sameach that your shemot have been inscribed in Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:3; MATTITYAHU 13:11–16; MATTITYAHU 11:25–27"> In the same hour Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach was full of simcha by the Ruach Hakodesh. And he said, Baruch Hashem, Avi, Adon HaShomayim vHaAretz, that You concealed these things from those with chochmah and seichel and You revealed them to yeladim. Ken, Avi, for thus it was well pleasing in Your sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:27; YOCHANAN 1:18; YOCHANAN 6:44–46"> Everything was handed over to me by Avi, and no one has daas of HaBen except HaAv. And no one has daas of HaAv except HaBen, and whomever HaBen wishes to reveal Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:16–17"> And, having turned to the talmidim in a yechidus, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Ashrey &#91;are&#93; the eyes seeing what you see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:56; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:13; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:10–12"> For I say to you that many neviim and melachim wanted to see what you see and they did not see them, and to hear what you hear and they did not hear them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 22:34–39; LUKAS 18:18; MATTITYAHU 19:16–19"> And a certain Talmid Chacham, a Baal Torah, stood up, testing Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach, saying, Rabbi, what mitzvah must I do to inherit Chayyei Olam?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:5; YESHAYAH 8:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:21–22"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to him, In the Torah what has been written? How do you read it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MARKOS 12:30–31; MATTITYAHU 22:37–40; DEVARIM 6:5"> And in reply the Baal Torah said, V'AHAVTA ES ADONOI ELOHEICHA B'CHOL LEVAVCHA UVECHOL NAFSHECHA UVECHOL MODECHA<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Dt 6:4,5">j</a></sup>and L'REACHA KAMOCHA.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Dt 6:5; Lv 19:18">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:11; VAYIKRA 18:5; MATTITYAHU 19:17"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Your answer is frum. Richtik. Do this and you will live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LUKAS 16:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:2; VAYIKRA 19:34"> But because the Baal Torah wanted to justify himself, to be yitzdak im Hashem(justified with G-d)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Job 25:4">l</a></sup>on the basis of his own zokheh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="see Ro 3:20">m</a></sup>(merit), he said to him, And who is my REA (neighbor)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="TEHILLIM 88:4; LUKAS 18:31; EKHAH 2:12"> In reply, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said, A certain ish was coming down from Yerushalayim to Yericho, and he encountered shodedim. They stripped him and inflicted a klap, more than one, and they went away and left him half dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16–18; TEHILLIM 69:20; YIRMEYAH 5:31"> It so happened that a certain kohen was coming down by that derech, and, having seen him, he passed by on the other side.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Lv 21:1-3">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MISHLE 27:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:17; TEHILLIM 109:25"> And likewise also a Levi happened upon the place, but when he came and saw him, he passed by on the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:50; YIRMEYAH 38:7–13; LUKAS 7:13"> But a certain Shomroni, traveling along on the derech, came upon him; and when he saw him, he was filled with rachmei shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:20; TEHILLIM 147:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:15"> And when this Shomroni approached, he bandaged the manʼs wounds, pouring shemen and yayin over them; and when he had placed him upon his own donkey, he brought the man to a malon and cared for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MISHLE 19:17; LUKAS 14:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:23"> And on the next day he produced two denarii and gave them to the inn keeper of the malon and said, Take care of him, and whatever you spend additionally, I will take care of, when I return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LUKAS 7:42; MATTITYAHU 21:28–31; MATTITYAHU 17:25"> Who of these shalosha seems to you to have become a re'a to the one having fallen among the shodedim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16–18; MICHOH 6:8; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:21"> And the Baal Torah answered, The one having shown the man rachamim. And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Go and do likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:19–20; YOCHANAN 11:1–5; YOCHANAN 12:1–3"> And while they were on the derech, he entered into a certain shtetl. And a certain isha by name of Marta received him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MISHLE 8:34; LUKAS 8:35; YOCHANAN 12:3"> And this isha had an achot named Miryam, who sat down at the feet of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu listening to his dvar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:27; LUKAS 12:29; MATTITYAHU 14:15"> But Marta was distracted with much badinen. And having stood by, she said, Adoneinu, is it of no concern to you that my achot has left me alone to serve? Speak, then, to her to help me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:6; LUKAS 21:34; MATTITYAHU 6:25–34"> And in reply Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu said to her, Marta, Marta, you are anxious and worried about many things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="TEHILLIM 27:4; TEHILLIM 16:5–6; YOCHANAN 10:27–28"> But one is necessary. For Miryam chose HaTov which will not be taken away from her.</span>
