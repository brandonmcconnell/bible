<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:17–18; EKHAH 2:20; YECHEZKEL 9:5–7"> Now on the same occasion there were some present reporting to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach about the men of the Galil whose blood Pilate mixed with their zevakhim (sacrifices).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:4; IYOV 22:5–16"> And, in reply, Moshiach said, Do you think that these men of the Galil were greater chote'im (sinners) than all others of the Galil, because they suffered this shud (misfortune)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:19; MATTITYAHU 3:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:38–40"> Lo (no), I say, but unless you make teshuva, you will all likewise perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 3:15; IYOV 1:19; YOCHANAN 9:11"> Or do you think that those shmonah asar (eighteen) upon whom the migdal (tower) in Shiloach fell and killed them, do you think that they were greater chote'im (sinners) than all the Bnei Adam living in Yerushalayim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:30; LUKAS 13:3; YESHAYAH 28:10–13"> Lo (no), I tell you, but unless you make teshuva, you will all likewise perish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:16; YESHAYAH 5:1–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:22"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was speaking this mashal. A certain man had an etz te'enah (fig tree) which had been planted in his kerem, and he came seeking pri (fruit) on it, and he did not find any.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Isa 5:2; Jer 8:13">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:19; LUKAS 3:9; YOCHANAN 15:6"> So he said to the keeper of the kerem, Hinei shalosh shanim (three years) I come seeking pri on this etz te'enah (fig tree) and I do not find any. Therefore, cut it down! Why is it even using up the adamah (ground)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; YOEL 2:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:1"> But in reply he says to him, Adoni, leave it also this year, until I may dig around it and may throw fertilizer on it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:15; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:8"> And if indeed it produces pri in the future, tov me'od (very well); otherwise, you will cut down it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ro 11:23">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:23; LUKAS 4:44; LUKAS 4:15–16"> Now in one of the shuls Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying shiurim on Shabbos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 8:2; LUKAS 8:27; LUKAS 13:16"> And an isha which had a ruach hamachla (a spirit of an infirmity, illness) shmonah asar (eighteen) years was bent double and was not able to straighten up at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="TEHILLIM 107:20; MATTITYAHU 8:16; LUKAS 6:8–10"> And when he saw her, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach called out to her and said, Isha (Woman), you have been set free from your machla (illness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TEHILLIM 107:20–22; MARKOS 16:18; MARKOS 5:23"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach placed his hands upon her. And ofen ort (immediately) she was straightened and she was crying, Baruch Hashem!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:9; MATTITYAHU 12:2; YOCHANAN 9:14–16"> And in reply the Rosh of the Beit HaKnesset, being indignant that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach had given refuah (healing) on Shabbos, was saying to the multitude, There are sheshah yamim (six days) in which melachah (work) should be done; therefore, come during those sheshah yamim and get your refuah; but not on Shabbos!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ex 20:9">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 14:5; YOCHANAN 7:21–24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:9–10"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu answered him and said, Tzevu'im! Does not each of you on Shabbos untie his ox or his donkey from the evus (animal feeding trough) and lead it away to water him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MARKOS 2:27; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:26; LUKAS 19:9"> But ought not this isha, a bat Avraham Avinu as she is, whom HaSatan has bound hinei, nebbach (regrettably) these shmonah asar (eighteen) long years — should she not have been set free from this bond on Shabbos?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16; TEHILLIM 132:18; YESHAYAH 45:24"> And as Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said these things, all his mitnaggedim (opponents) were put to bushah (shame), and all the multitude was having simcha with chedvah (rejoicing) over all the things of kavod being accomplished by him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 66:5">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MARKOS 4:30–34; LUKAS 17:21; MATTITYAHU 13:31–32"> Therefore, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying, What is the Malchut Hashem like? And to what shall I make a tzushtel (comparison) of it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DANIEL 4:12; DANIEL 4:21; DANIEL 2:44–45"> It is like a mustard seed, which a man took and threw into his own garden, and it grew and became an etz (tree), and the OPH HASHOMAYIM nested in its branches.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And again Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, To what shall I compare the Malchut Hashem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:33; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:23–24"> It is like se'or (leaven), which an isha took and hid in shalosh (three) measures of wheat flour until the whole batch was leavened.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKAS 9:51; MATTITYAHU 9:35; MARKOS 10:32–34"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was itinerating throughout the shtetlach (towns) and villages saying shiurim and heading on toward Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:14; MATTITYAHU 22:14; YOCHANAN 21:21–22"> And someone said to him, Adoni, are there only a few coming to Yeshu'at Eloheinu? And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:13–14; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:24–27"> Strive to enter through the sha'ar hatzarut (gate of narrowness), because many, I say to you, will seek to enter and they will not be able to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:10–12; LUKAS 13:27; MATTITYAHU 7:21–23"> After the Baal Bayit gets up and shuts the delet, and you begin to stand outside and to knock on the delet, saying, Adoneinu, open up for us! And in reply, he will say to you, I do not have daas of you; from where are you from?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YESHAYAH 58:2; LETTER TO TITOS 1:16; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:5"> Then you will begin to say, We ate and drank in your presence, and in our rekhovot (streets) you taught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="TEHILLIM 6:8; MATTITYAHU 25:41; MATTITYAHU 7:22–23"> And he will speak, saying to you, I do not know you, from where are you from? Go away from me, kol po'alei resha (all workers of evil)!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 8:11–12; MATTITYAHU 22:13; MATTITYAHU 25:30"> There will be weeping and gnashing of teeth, when you will see Avraham Avinu and Yitzchak and Ya'akov and all the Neviim in the Malchut Hashem, but you yourselves being thrown out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YESHAYAH 43:6; YESHAYAH 66:18–20; YESHAYAH 49:6"> And they will come from mizrach (east) and maarav (west) and from tzafon (north) and darom (south) and they will be the ones bimesibba (reclining at tish) in the Malchut Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:30; MARKOS 10:31; MATTITYAHU 20:16"> And there are some acharonim who will be rishonim and there are some rishonim who will be acharonim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 6:9–11; TEHILLIM 11:1–2; MATTITYAHU 14:1"> In the same hour some Perushim approached Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, saying to him, Depart and go from here, because Herod wants to kill you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:9; YOCHANAN 17:4–5"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Go and tell that fox, Hinei, I cast out shedim and I accomplish refuot (healings) hayom (today) and makhar (tomorrow) and Yom HaShlishi (The Third Day) I reach my goal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKAS 9:53; MATTITYAHU 21:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:27"> Nevertheless, it is necessary for me to travel on hayom (today) and makhar (tomorrow) and the yom following, because it cannot be that a navi should have his violent death and his histalkus (passing) outside Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:37–39; DEVARIM 32:11–12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:52"> O Yerushalayim, Yerushalayim, she who kills the Neviim and stones the ones sent to her! How often I wanted to gather your yeladim just as a hen gathers her chicks under her wings, and you were not willing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="TEHILLIM 118:26; MARKOS 11:9–10; MATTITYAHU 21:9"> Hinei your Beis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="HaMikdash">e</a></sup>is left to you desolate; and I say to you, you shall by no means see me until the zman (time) comes when you say, BARUCH HABAH B'SHEM ADONAI!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Jer 12:17; Ps 118:26; Ro 11:26">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
