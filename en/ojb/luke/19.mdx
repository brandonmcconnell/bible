<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKAS 18:24–27; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 17:5–6"> And, hinei, a man by name Zakkai was there and he was a chief moches (tax collector) and he was an oisher (rich man).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKAS 23:8; LUKAS 9:7–9; YOCHANAN 12:21"> And he was seeking to catch a look at Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach and was not able to do so, because of the multitude, and because he was komah (short of stature).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YESHAYAH 9:10; LUKAS 5:19; MELACHIM ALEF 10:27"> And having run ahead to the front, he climbed up onto an etz (tree), a sycamore, that he might see Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, because by that derech (way) he was about to pass by.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1Kgs 10:27; 1Chr 27:28; Isa 9:10">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:20; YOCHANAN 14:23; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:2"> And as he came to the place, having looked up, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Zakkai, hurry and come down, for hayom (today) in your bais (house) it is necessary for me to remain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKAS 5:29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:34; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:15"> And having hurried, Zakkai came down and welcomed with Kabbalas HaMalchus (accepting his sovereignty as king) Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach with simcha (joy).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKAS 15:2; LUKAS 7:39; LUKAS 7:34"> And all, having seen this, were beginning to kvetch (complain), saying, With a choteh (sinner) he entered to rest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 12:6; BAMIDBAR 5:7; LUKAS 18:22–23"> And Zakkai stood there and said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu: Hinei, half of my possessions, Adoni, I give as tzedakah (contribution to charity) to the aniyim, and if from someone I defrauded of anything, I am paying back arbatayim (four times).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ex 22:1; Lv 6:4,5; Num 5:7; 2Sm 12:6; Ezek 33:14,15">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:7; LUKAS 13:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:16"> And he said to him, Hayom (Today) Yeshu'at Eloheinu has come to this bais, because he also is a Ben Avraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 34:16; MATTITYAHU 1:21; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:13–16"> For the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">c</a></sup>came to seek and to save the lost.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ezek 34:12,16">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 17:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:6; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:1–3"> Now as they were hearing these things, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach added a mashal (parable), because he was near to Yerushalayim and they had the havamina (assumption) that the Malchut Hashem was about to appear immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:14–30; LUKAS 19:12–27; LUKAS 20:9"> Then Moshiach said, A certain man of noble birth traveled to a distant country to get for himself royal power for his malchut and to return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:14–15; YOCHANAN 12:26; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:7–11"> And having summoned ten avadim (servants) of his, he gave them ten minas and he said to them, Conduct business until I come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 8:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:51–52; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:14–15"> But his subjects were hating the man of noble birth and they sent a delegation after him saying, We do not want this one to rule over us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:1–5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:10–12; TEHILLIM 2:4–6"> And it came about when he returned, having received the royal power of his malchut, he summoned to his presence these avadim to whom he had given the kesef, that he might have daas of what they had gained by trading.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:18–26; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:7–8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:10"> And the rishon (first) came, saying, With your mina, Adoni, ten minas more were gained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKAS 16:10; MATTITYAHU 25:21; HISGALUS 2:26–29"> Yafeh! Eved tov! Because you have been ne'eman (faithful) in a very small thing, have shlita (authority) over ten towns.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Prov 27:18">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MARKOS 4:20; MATTITYAHU 13:23; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:12"> And the sheni (second) came saying, Your mina, Adoni, made five minas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:58; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:41–42; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:8"> And the man of noble birth said also to this one, And you have shlita (authority) over five towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:24; MISHLE 26:13–16; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:17"> And the other came saying, Adoni, hinei! Your mina which I had put away in a tichel (kerchief).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:24–25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:7; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:18"> For I was fearing you, because you are a big machmir (stickler), you take what you did not deposit, and you reap what you did not sow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 1:16; MATTITYAHU 12:37; IYOV 15:5–6"> He says to him, From your own mouth I will judge you, eved rasha (bad servant). You had daas that I am a big machmir (stickler), taking what I did not deposit and reaping what I did not sow?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:25–27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:4–5; DEVARIM 23:19–20"> Why then did you not put my kesef on deposit? Then when I came I would have collected ribit (interest).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKAS 16:2; LUKAS 12:20"> And to the ones having stood nearby he said, Take from him the mina and give it to the one having ten minas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKAS 16:2; SHMUEL BAIS 7:19; YESHAYAH 55:8–9"> And they said to him, Adoneinu, he has ten minas!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8; LUKAS 8:18; MATTITYAHU 21:43"> I say to you, that to everyone having, more will be given, but from the one not having, even what he has will be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKAS 19:14; TEHILLIM 21:8–9; MATTITYAHU 22:7"> But these oyevim of mine, the ones refusing me the Kabbalas HaMalchus (refusing my sovereignty as king)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="cf. 70 C.E.">f</a></sup>over them, bring here and execute them before me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKAS 9:51; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:2; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1"> And having said these things, he was traveling ahead, making his aliyah leregel to Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:12; MATTITYAHU 21:17; MATTITYAHU 21:1–11"> And it came about as he came near to Beit-Pagey and Beit-Anyah, by the Mount of Olives, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach sent two of the talmidim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LUKAS 22:8–13; LUKAS 19:32; SHMUEL ALEF 10:2–9"> Saying, Go to the shtetl ahead of you, in which entering you will find an AYIR<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Zech 9:9">g</a></sup>having been tied, upon which none among Bnei Adam ever sat, and having untied it, bring it here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="TEHILLIM 24:1; TEHILLIM 50:10–12; MARKOS 11:3–6"> And if someone asks you, Why are you untying it? — you will say this: HaAdon has need of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LUKAS 22:13"> And having departed, the ones having been sent found it just as Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And while untying the AYIR, its owners said to them, Why are you untying the AYIR?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 9:9; YOCHANAN 10:35; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:9"> And they said, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu has need of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MARKOS 11:7–8; MATTITYAHU 21:7; YOCHANAN 12:14"> And they led it to him and, having thrown their kaftans on the AYIR, they put Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 9:13; MATTITYAHU 21:8"> And as Moshiach rode along, the people were spreading their garments on the road.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="2Kgs 9:13">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:12–13; EZRA 3:10–13; MATTITYAHU 21:1"> And as Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was now approaching the path that makes the descent from the Mount of Olives, all the multitude of the Rebbeʼs talmidim began with simcha (joy) and with a kol gadol (a loud voice) to shout Baruch Hashem for all the nifla'ot (wonders) and gevurot (mighty deeds) of Hashem! For they had been edei reiyah (eyewitnesses) of these,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 9:9; LUKAS 13:35; TEHILLIM 118:22–26"> Saying, BARUCH HABAH, HaMelech B'SHEM ADONOI! Shalom b'Shomayim! And kavod in the Highest!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ps 118:26">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:1–2; MATTITYAHU 23:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:16–18"> And some of the Perushim from the multitude said to him, Rabbi, rebuke your talmidim!</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="CHABAKUK 2:11; YESHAYAH 55:12; MATTITYAHU 3:9"> And in reply he said, I say to you, if these will be silent, the avanim (stones) will cry out.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hab 2:11">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LUKAS 13:34–35; YOCHANAN 11:35; TEHILLIM 119:136"> And as he came near, having seen the Ir (City) &#91;of Yerushalayim&#93;, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach wept over it,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Isa 22:4">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:29; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:26–29; YESHAYAH 48:18"> Saying, If you had only had daas in HaYom HaZeh of the things leading to shalom, but now it was nistar from your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LUKAS 21:20–24; YESHAYAH 29:1–4; YECHEZKEL 4:2"> Because yamim (days) will come upon you when your oyevim (enemies) will construct a siege against you and they will surround you and will hem you in from all directions.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Isa 29:3; Jer 6:6; Ezek 4:2; 28:6">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="LUKAS 21:6; LUKAS 13:34–35; YOCHANAN 3:18–21"> And they will dash you to the ground, you and your yeladim with you. And they will not leave an even (stone) upon an even (stone) within you, because you did not have daas of the zman (time) of your YOM PEKUDDAH (visitation, reckoning).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Isa 10:3">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:12–13; MARKOS 11:15–17; YOCHANAN 2:13–17"> And having entered into the Beis HaMikdash, Moshiach began to throw out the sellers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="YESHAYAH 56:7; YIRMEYAH 7:11; TEHILLIM 93:5"> Saying to them, It has been written, BEITI BEIT TEFILLAH YIKAREI,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Isa 56:7">n</a></sup>but you made it a MEARAT PARITZIM.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Jer 7:11">o</a></sup></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MARKOS 11:18; MATTITYAHU 26:55; MARKOS 11:27–33"> And he was saying shiurim yom yom in the Beis Hamikdash. But the Rashei Hakohanim and the Sofrim were seeking to kill him, and also the Rashei HaAm,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 8:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:14; YOCHANAN 7:46–49"> And they were not finding what they might do, for HaAm were all hanging on his every dvar.</span>
