<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:1–2; MARKOS 16:1–8; MATTITYAHU 28:1–8"> But on Yom HaRishon, the first day of the shavua (week), very early in the boker, they went to the kever (tomb) bringing what spices they prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MARKOS 16:3–4; MATTITYAHU 27:60–66; MARKOS 15:46–47"> But they found the even (stone) having been rolled away from the kever (tomb),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKAS 24:23; YOCHANAN 20:6–7; MATTITYAHU 16:5"> and, having entered, they did not find geviyyato<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Dt 21:23; Gn 47:18">a</a></sup>(his body) of the Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 16:9-10; Job 19:25-27; Isa 26:19; 53:11">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MARKOS 16:5; YOCHANAN 20:11–12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:10"> It came about while they were perplexed about this, indeed, hinei, shnayim (two), who were garmented in dazzling robes, stood by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MARKOS 16:5–6; MATTITYAHU 28:3–5; HISGALUS 1:18"> And having become terrified, the nashim bowed their faces to the ground. They said to the nashim, Why are you seeking HaChai (the One living) among HaMesim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 17:22–23; LUKAS 9:22; MARKOS 8:31"> He is not here, he kam (stood up) litechiyyah (to come to life again). Have zikaron (recollection) how he spoke to you while he was still in the Galil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:21"> Saying, It is necessary for the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">c</a></sup>to be delivered over into the hands of anashim chote'im and to be talui al HaEtz<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Dt 21:23">d</a></sup>(to be hanged on the Tree), and on HaYom HaShlishi to stand up alive again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:16; YOCHANAN 14:26; YOCHANAN 2:19–22"> And they remembered the words of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 24:22–24; MARKOS 16:7–8; MATTITYAHU 28:7–8"> And having returned from the kever (tomb), they reported all these things to the Achad Asar (the Eleven) and to all the others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:56; YOCHANAN 20:11–18; LUKAS 8:2–3"> Now there were Miryam of Magdala and Yochanah and Miryam the Em (mother) of Ya'akov, and the others with them. They were telling the Moshiachʼs Shlichim these things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="IYOV 9:16; MARKOS 16:11; BERESHIS 19:14"> And these words of theirs appeared to the Moshiachʼs Shlichim as utter narrishkait, and the Shlichim had no emunah in what they said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:3–10"> Kefa got up and ran to the kever (tomb). And when he bent over, Kefa sees the tachrichim linen clothes only, and he departed wondering to himself with tzorich iyun (unresolved puzzlement) about the thing that had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 24:18; MARKOS 16:12–13"> And, hinei, shnayim (two) of them that same day were traveling to a shtetl being distant sixty stadia from Yerushalayim, a shtetl the name of which is Ammaus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 6:45; DEVARIM 6:7; MALACHI 3:6"> And they were speaking to one another concerning all these things that had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:20; YOCHANAN 14:18–19; LUKAS 24:36"> And it came about while they were talking and discussing these things that also Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach himself had come near and was traveling along with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:14; YOCHANAN 21:4; LUKAS 24:31"> But the eynayim (eyes) of them were prevented from recognizing Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:20–22; YOCHANAN 16:6; YECHEZKEL 9:4–6"> And he said to them, What devarim (words) are these which you are exchanging with one another as you are walking along? And they stood with a look of agmat nefesh (tzubrochen, broken-hearted).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:25"> And in reply one, Cleopas by name, said to him, Are you the only one visiting Yerushalayim that does not have daas of the things that have happened in it in these days?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:22; YOCHANAN 4:19"> And he said to them, What things? And they said to him, The things about Yehoshua from Natzeret, who was a man, a navi, powerful in ma'aseh and dvar before Hashem and all HaAm,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:13–15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:8–10; LUKAS 23:13"> And how both our Kohanim HaGedolim and minhagim delivered him over to a mishpat mavet and how on HaEtz they hanged him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dt 21:23">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKAS 1:68; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:18–19; LUKAS 2:38"> But we were holding the tikvah that he is the one to bring the Geulah of Yisroel. Ken, and besides all this, it is now HaYom HaShlishi vibahlt (since) these things took place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MARKOS 16:9–10; MATTITYAHU 28:7–8; YOCHANAN 20:1–2"> Also some nashim (women) in our number astounded us. They came to the kever (tomb, Rebbeʼs ohel) early this morning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And not having found geviyyato (his body),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="cf. Gn 47:18; nevelah, corpse Dt 21:23">f</a></sup>the nashim (women) came saying also to have seen a chazon (vision, appearance) of malachim (angels) who say he lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKAS 24:12; YOCHANAN 20:1–10"> And some of the ones with us departed to the kever (tomb), and they found it so, just as also the nashim (women) said, but him they did not see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MARKOS 7:18; MARKOS 9:19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:11–12"> And Moshiach said to them, O foolish ones and slow in lev (heart) to have emunah in all which the Neviim spoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:11; YESHAYAH 53:1–12; ZECHARYAH 13:7"> Was it not necessary for the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach to suffer these things and to enter into his kavod (glory)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YESHAYAH 7:14; LUKAS 24:44; DEVARIM 18:15"> And having begun from Moshe Rabbenu and from all the Neviim, he explained to them in all the Kitvei Hakodesh the things concerning himself.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gn 3:15; Num 21:9; Dt 18:15; Isa 7:14; 9:6; 40:10,11; 53; Ezek 34:23; Dan 9:24; Ps 22; Mal 3:1">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MARKOS 6:48; BERESHIS 19:2; BERESHIS 32:26"> And they drew near to the shtetl where they were traveling and he acted as though to travel vaiter (farther).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:8; LUKAS 14:23; BERESHIS 19:3"> And they strongly urged him saying, Stay with us, because it is towards erev and the yom has declined already. And he entered to stay with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 14:19; LUKAS 24:35; MATTITYAHU 15:36"> And it came about, while he was reclining at tish with them, having taken the matzot, he made the hamotzi, and, with the betziat halechem (the breaking of the bread) he handed it to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LUKAS 24:16; YOCHANAN 8:59; LUKAS 4:30"> And the eynayim of them were opened, and they had daas, recognizing him. And just then he became invisible to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LUKAS 24:45; YIRMEYAH 23:29; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:12"> And they said to one another, Were not our levavot burning within us as he was speaking to us on the derech, as he was opening to us the Kitvei Hakodesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MARKOS 16:13; YOCHANAN 20:19–26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:14"> And getting up that very hour, they returned to Yerushalayim, and they found the Achad Asar (Eleven) gathered together and those with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:5; MARKOS 16:7; LUKAS 22:54–62"> Saying that beemes (really) Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu was mamash (definitely) made to stand up alive and he tahke (actually) appeared to Shim'on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MARKOS 16:12–13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:42; LUKAS 24:30–31"> And they were explaining the things on the derech and how he was made known to them in the hisgalus of the betziat halechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:16; YESHAYAH 57:18; MARKOS 16:14"> And while they were speaking these things, he stood in the midst of them, and he says to them, Shalom Aleichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 28:13; MATTITYAHU 14:26–27; IYOV 4:14–16"> But having been startled and having been terrified, they were thinking they saw a ruach (spirit).</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 4:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:13; DANIEL 4:19"> And he said to them, Why have you been troubled, and why do doubts arise in your levavot?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:27; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:1; KOHELET 12:7"> You see my hands and my feet that I am myself. Touch me and see, because a ruach does not have basar and atzamot (bones) as you see me having.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And having said this, he showed them his hands and his feet.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ps 22:16[17]; TARGUM HASHIVIM">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YOCHANAN 21:5; TEHILLIM 126:1–2; BERESHIS 45:26–28"> And while in their simcha they were still disbelieving and astonished, he said to them, Have you some okhel here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And they gave him part of a broiled dag (fish).</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:41"> And having taken it, in front of them, he ate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 12:10; LUKAS 24:26–27; ZECHARYAH 9:9"> And he said to them, These are my devarim which I spoke to you while still being with you, that it is necessary that all the things having been written in the Torah of Moshe and the Neviim and the Tehillim about me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Moshiach">i</a></sup>to be fulfilled.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ps 2; 16; 22; 69; 72; 89; 110; 118">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:14; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:20; TEHILLIM 119:18"> Then he opened their minds to have binah (understanding) of the Kitvei Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:2–12; YESHAYAH 50:6; LUKAS 24:44"> And he said to them, Thus it has been written, that the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach must suffer his histalkus (passing) and come back to life again from HaMesim on HaYom HaShlishi,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:31; YESHAYAH 49:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:38"> And teshuva for the selicha (forgiveness) of chattaim (sins) is to be preached b'Shem of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua to all the Nations, beginning from Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:32; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:31"> You are to be edim (witnesses) of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:4; YOEL 2:28–32"> And, hinei, I send the havtachah of Avi to you; but you sit in HaIr (the City) until you may be clothed with oz (power) from on High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:12; MATTITYAHU 21:17; MARKOS 11:1"> And he led them outside as far as Beit-Anyah, and having lifted up his hands, he said a bracha over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:9; MARKOS 16:19; MELACHIM BAIS 2:11"> And while he said the bracha over them, he departed from them, being taken up in an aliyah ascent to Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:9; YOCHANAN 16:22; TEHILLIM 30:11"> And they, having reverenced him in worship,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Dan 7:14; cf. Dan 3:18">k</a></sup>returned to Yerushalayim with simcha gedolah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="MARKOS 16:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:46–47; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:41–42"> And they were continually in the Beis Hamikdash praising Hashem.</span>
