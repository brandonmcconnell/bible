<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:15; MATTITYAHU 9:10–13; LUKAS 5:29–32"> Now all the mochesim (tax-collectors) and the chote'im (sinners) were coming near Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach to listen to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:11; LUKAS 19:7; LUKAS 7:39"> And both the Perushim and the Sofrim were grumbling, saying, This one gives a kabbalat panim reception to chote'im (sinners) and eats at tish with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach told them this mashal, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 34:16; YECHEZKEL 34:11–12; MATTITYAHU 18:12–14"> What man of you, if he has meah kevasim (a hundred sheep) and has lost from them one, does not leave the tishim vteshah (ninety-nine) in the open pasture, and go for the one which has been lost until he finds it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:2; LUKAS 19:9; YESHAYAH 46:3–4"> And when he has found it, he puts it on his shoulders with lev same'ach.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ps 23; 119; Jer 31:10; Ezek 34:11-16">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKAS 15:10; LUKAS 15:24; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:25"> And when he returns to his bais, he calls together his chaverim and his shchenim (neighbors), saying to them, Make a simcha with me, because I have found my keves (sheep), the one that was lost!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKAS 5:32; LUKAS 15:10; LUKAS 15:32"> I say to you that in the same way there will be more simcha in Shomayim over one choteh (sinner) who becomes a baal teshuva than over tishim vteshah tzaddikim who have no need of teshuva.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKAS 19:10; YECHEZKEL 34:12; YOCHANAN 10:16"> Or what isha having asaret (ten) drachmas, if she loses one drachma, will not light a licht (light) and sweep the bais and search carefully until she finds it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 15:6–7"> And having found it, she calls together chaverim and shchenim saying, Have simcha with me, for I have found the drachma which I lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKAS 15:7; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:10; YECHEZKEL 33:11"> In the same way, I say to you, there is simcha in the presence of the malachim Hashem over one choteh (sinner) who becomes a baal teshuva.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:23–31"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, A certain ben Adam had shnei banim (two sons).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEVARIM 21:16–17; MARKOS 12:44; LUKAS 15:30"> And the younger of them said to his Abba, Avi, give to me the share of the estate that falls to me. And his Abba divided his wealth between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MISHLE 29:3; YIRMEYAH 2:5; MISHLE 21:17"> And not many yamim later, having gathered together everything, the younger ben went on a journey to a far away country, and there he squandered his osher (riches) with gilui arayot (sexual immorality) and loose living.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Isa 59:2">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:11; AMOS 8:9–12; HOSHEA 2:9–14"> And when he had spent everything, there came a severe ra'av (famine) throughout that aretz, and he began to be nitzrach (needy).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:3; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:25–26; YESHAYAH 1:5"> And he went and became associated with one of the citizens of that aretz, and he sent him into his fields to feed chazirim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Lv 11:7">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:19–21; EKHAH 4:5; HOSHEA 12:1"> And he was longing to fill his mogen (stomach) with the pods which the chazirim were eating, and no one was giving anything to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:4–5; LUKAS 15:18–19"> When he came to his senses, his seichel told him, How many of my Abbaʼs sachirim (hired workers) have more than enough okhel (food) and I am perishing here with hunger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EKHAH 3:40; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8–10; LUKAS 15:21"> I will get up and go to my Abba, and I will say, Avi, I sinned against Shomayim and in your sight.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Lv 26:40; Ps 51:4[6]">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="IYOV 42:6; LUKAS 5:8; TEHILLIM 84:10"> I no longer have the zokheh (merit) worthy to be called a ben of my Abba. Make me as one of your sachirim (hired workers).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 103:10–13; TEHILLIM 86:5; TEHILLIM 86:15"> And when he got up he came home to his own Abba. And while he was still a long way off, his Abba saw him, and was filled with rachmei Shomayim (heavenly mercy, compassion) and tears, and fell upon his neck and kissed him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gn 45:14">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="TEHILLIM 51:4; LUKAS 15:18–19; YIRMEYAH 3:13"> And bno said to the Abba, Avi, I sinned against Shomayim and in your sight. No longer do I have the zokheh (merit) to be worthy to be called your ben.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ps 51:4[6]">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:42; ESTHER 8:2; HISGALUS 6:11"> But his Abba said to his avadim, Shnel! (Quick!) Bring out the best kaftan and clothe him, and put a ring on his hand and sandals for his feet,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Zech 3:4; Gn 41:42">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YESHAYAH 25:6; MATTITYAHU 22:2–14; TEHILLIM 63:5"> And bring the fattened calf, and slaughter it, and let us eat and have a simcha,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:5; YECHEZKEL 34:16; LUKAS 19:10"> Because this ben of mine was dead and now he has returned l'Chayyim! He had been lost and now he is found. And they began to make a simcha.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="TEHILLIM 150:4; YIRMEYAH 31:4; LUKAS 15:11–12"> But the Abbaʼs alterer ben (older son) was in the sadeh (field). And as he was coming, he drew near to the bais, and he heard the zemirot (table songs), and the sound of the klezmer (musician) and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="chasidic">h</a></sup>dancing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And having summoned one of the avadim (servants), the alterer ben (older son) was inquiring what these things might be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:17; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:13"> And the eved said to him, Your ach is present, and your Abba sacrificed the fattened calf, because your Abba received him back bari v'shalem (safe and sound).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YONAH 4:9; SHMUEL ALEF 18:8; YONAH 4:1–4"> And the alterer ben was filled with ka'as (anger), and he did not want to enter. But his Abba came out and was pleading with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LUKAS 18:11–12; ZECHARYAH 7:3; YESHAYAH 65:5"> But in reply the alterer ben said to his Abba, Hinei, so many years I serve you and never a mitzvah of you I disobeyed, and never for me did you give even a young goat that with my chaverim I might make a simcha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MISHLE 29:3; LUKAS 15:12–13; LUKAS 15:32"> But when shows up this ben of yours, the one having devoured your property with zonot (prostitutes), you sacrificed for him the fattened calf.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Prov 29:3">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MARKOS 7:27–28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:1"> And the Abba said to him, Beni, you are always with me, and everything which is mine is yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:1–10; LUKAS 15:24; YESHAYAH 35:10"> But now it was necessary for us to have lev samei'ach and make a simcha, because the ach of you was dead and he has returned l'Chayyim! He has been lost and now is found.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Mal 3:17">j</a></sup></span>
