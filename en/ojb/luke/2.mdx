<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKAS 3:1; MATTITYAHU 4:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:30"> This former mifkad was that taken while Quirinius was ruling in Syria (see Ac 5:37 for latter).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And everyone was traveling to register, each to his own shtetl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MICHOH 5:2; YOCHANAN 7:42; SHMUEL ALEF 16:1"> Now Yosef<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="ben Dovid">a</a></sup>also went up from the Galil, from the shtetl of Natzeret, to Yehudah, to the Ir Dovid (City of David), which is called Beit-Lechem, because he was of the mishpochah and Bais of Dovid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 22:22–27; MATTITYAHU 1:18–19"> And Yosef<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="ben Dovid">b</a></sup>went up to register with Miryam, who had been given to him in erusin (betrothal) and who was with child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 33:11; HISGALUS 12:1–5; MICHOH 5:2"> And it came about, while they were there, the yamim (days) were fulfilled for Miryam to give birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:4; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:9; YESHAYAH 53:2–3"> And she bore her ben, her Bechor (firstborn); and she wrapped him in swaddling clothes, and laid him in an evus (animal feeding trough), because there was no place for them in the malon (inn).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 31:39–40; TEHILLIM 78:70–71; SHMUEL ALEF 17:34–35"> And ro'im (shepherds) were in the same region, living outside in the open air, and keeping shomer over their eder (flock) balailah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YESHAYAH 60:1; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:18; MELACHIM ALEF 8:11"> And, suddenly, a malach Hashem stood before them, and the kavod Hashem shone around them; and they were afraid with a yirah gedolah (great fear).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:9; YESHAYAH 61:1; YESHAYAH 52:7"> And the malach said to them, Do not have pachad (fear, terror); for hinei I announce Besuras HaGeulah to you of great simcha (joy) which will be for kol Am Berit (all the People of the Covenant);</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 9:6; MATTITYAHU 1:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:36"> Because hayom, in Ir Dovid, has been born to you a Moshia (Go'el, Savior, Oisleizer), who is Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach HaAdon.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 9:6[5]; Mal 3:1">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 7:14; YESHAYAH 53:1–2; YESHAYAH 7:11"> And this will be HaOt (The Sign) to you: you will find a small child, an infant, wrapped in swaddling clothes and lying in an evus.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1Sm 2:34; 2Kgs 19:29; Ps 86:17; Isa 7:14">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TEHILLIM 103:20–21; HISGALUS 5:11; TEHILLIM 148:2"> And, suddenly, there was with the malach a multitude of the Tzivos Hashem, the Tzivos HaShomayim (Armies or Hosts of Heaven) praising Hashem, and saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:1; LUKAS 19:38; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:20"> Kavod to Hashem in the Highest; and on haaretz shalom among Bnei Adam kavvanah tovah (of good intention).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Isa 9:5-6; 52:7; 53:5; Mic 5:4-5">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:22; MATTITYAHU 2:1–2; MATTITYAHU 2:9–11"> And it came about, when the malachim withdrew from them to Shomayim, the roim (shepherds) were saying to one another, Let us go now up to Beit-Lechem and let us see this thing that has come about which Hashem has made known to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 19:32; LUKAS 2:12; LUKAS 2:7"> And they came in haste and found both Miryam and Yosef,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="ben Dovid">f</a></sup>and the child was lying in the evus (animal feeding trough).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See Isa 53:7on Moshiach the Seh">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TEHILLIM 66:16; LUKAS 8:39; TEHILLIM 71:17–18"> And when the roim saw this, they made known the dvar concerning this yeled which had been told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKAS 2:47; LUKAS 4:36; LUKAS 1:65–66"> And all who heard it were amazed about the things which were told them by the roim (shepherds).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKAS 2:51; LUKAS 1:66; MISHLE 4:4"> But Miryam was treasuring up all these things, pondering them in her lev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 106:48; YESHAYAH 29:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:18"> And the roim went back, and as they returned, they cried, Baruch Hashem! They gave kavod to G-d for all that they had heard and seen, just as it had been foretold to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKAS 1:31; BERESHIS 17:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:4–5"> And when shemonah yamim were completed for his bris milah, YEHOSHUA was given as SHMO, which he was called by the malach, before he was conceived in the womb.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Zech 6:11-12; 3:8">h</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="VAYIKRA 12:6–8; VAYIKRA 12:2; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:9"> And to offer a korban according to the thing having been said in the Torat Hashem, SHTEI TORIM O SHNEI BENI YONAH (a pair of turtle doves or two young pigeons).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Lv 12:8">k</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:1; LUKAS 2:41; LUKAS 2:51"> And Shim'on came in the Ruach Hakodesh into the courts of the Beis HaMikdash; and when the horim (parents) brought in the yeled, Yehoshua, to do concerning him the required mitzvah of the Torah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Lv 12:6-8">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKAS 1:64; LUKAS 2:20; LUKAS 2:13–14"> Then Shim'on took him into his arms, saying, Baruch Hashem, and the following:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="HISGALUS 14:13; BERESHIS 15:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:23"> And now, Adonoi, dismiss Your eved in shalom, according to Your dvar (word);</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YESHAYAH 49:6; LUKAS 2:10–11; LUKAS 3:6"> Because my eynayim have beheld the Yeshu'at Eloheinu,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Isa 40:5; 52:10">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YESHAYAH 62:1–2; YESHAYAH 42:1–4; TEHILLIM 98:2–3"> Which You prepared in the presence of kol haammim (all the peoples),</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YESHAYAH 60:1–3; YESHAYAH 49:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:23"> An Ohr (Light) to give hisgalus (revelation) to the Nations and the kavod (glory) of Your Am (people), Yisroel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Isa 42:6; 49:6">o</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKAS 1:65–66; YESHAYAH 8:18; LUKAS 2:48"> And the Abba and Em of the yeled were amazed at the things being spoken about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:23; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:7–8; YESHAYAH 8:14–15"> And Shim'on said a bracha over them and said to Miryam his Em, Hinei this one is destined for the michshol (stumbling) and tekumah (revival) of RABBIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Isa 53:11-12">p</a></sup>in Yisroel and for an Ot (Sign) that will be opposed (against which there will be mitnaggedim, opponents).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Isa 8:14; 53:3">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEVARIM 8:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:19; MATTITYAHU 12:24–35"> And a cherev (sword) will pierce the neshamah of you yourself also so that the machshavot of many levavot (hearts) will be revealed.</span>
</div>

This isha (woman) was advanced in age, having lived with her baal (husband) sheva shanim (seven years) from her betulim (virginity),</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:5; TEHILLIM 23:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:23"> And then to the age of eighty-four she had lived as an almanah (widow) who was not departing from the Beis HaMikdash, serving yomam valailah (day and night) with tzomot (fastings) and tefillos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LUKAS 2:25; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:15; MARKOS 15:43"> And at that very moment she came and stood nearby, exclaiming, Baruch Hashem. <span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LUKAS 2:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:4–5; DEVARIM 12:32"> And when they had been shomer mitzvot and completed everything according to the Torat Hashem, they returned to the Galil and to their own shtetl of Natzeret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LUKAS 2:52; LUKAS 1:80; YESHAYAH 11:1–5"> And the yeled continued growing and was given chozek (strength), being filled with chochmah (wisdom), and the Chen vChesed Hashem was upon him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="TEHILLIM 122:1–4; YESHAYAH 2:3; TEHILLIM 42:4"> But supposed him to be in the caravan, and went a dayʼs journey. And they began looking for him among the krovim (relatives) and acquaintances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And, not having found him, they returned to Yerushalayim, looking for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LUKAS 5:17; YESHAYAH 49:1–2; YESHAYAH 50:4"> And it came about, that after shlosha yamim (three days) they found him in the courts of the Beis HaMikdash, sitting in the midst of the rabbis, both listening to them and asking them she'elot (kashes, questions).</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:28; LUKAS 4:32; TEHILLIM 119:99"> And all the ones listening to him where amazed at his binah and at his teshuvot (answers).</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="LUKAS 2:49; MATTITYAHU 12:46; LUKAS 3:23"> And when his horim (parents) saw him, they were astounded, and his Em (mother) said to him, Beni, why did you do thus to us? Hinei, your abba and I were anxiously looking for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:38; YOCHANAN 8:29; MALACHI 3:1"> And he said to them, Why is it that you were looking for me? <span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="LUKAS 2:19; DANIEL 7:28; BERESHIS 37:11"> And he went down with them, and they came to Natzeret; and he continued in mishma'at (obedience) to his horim (parents). And his Em (mother) was treasuring all these things in her lev (heart).</span>
</div>
