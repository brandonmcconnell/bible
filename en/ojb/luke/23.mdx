<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:1–2; MATTITYAHU 27:11–14; LUKAS 22:66"> And the whole multitude of them got up and led Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach before Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:6–7; YOCHANAN 19:12; LUKAS 23:14"> And they began to accuse him saying, This one we found misleading our nation and making it asur to pay taxes to Caesar and calling himself Moshiach and that he is to be a Melech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:49; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:13; LUKAS 22:70"> And Pilate cross-examined him, saying, Are you the Melech HaYehudim? And in reply, he said to him, You say so?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:38; LUKAS 23:14–15; LUKAS 23:22"> And Pilate said to the Rashei Hakohanim and the multitudes, I find no ashmah (guilt) in this man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MARKOS 1:14; YOCHANAN 1:43; YOCHANAN 2:11"> But they were insisting, saying, He incites the people, from where he began with his torah (teaching), throughout all of Yehudah, from the Galil even to this place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:37; LUKAS 13:1"> And when Pilate heard this, he asked whether the man was a Gelili (an inhabitant of the Galil).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKAS 3:1; LUKAS 13:31; MATTITYAHU 14:1"> And having learned that he is under the jurisdiction of Herod, he sent him to Herod, who was also in Yerushalayim during those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKAS 9:7–9; MATTITYAHU 14:1; MARKOS 6:14"> And Herod, having laid eyes on Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach, had great simcha. For he was for a long time wanting to see him, because he keeps hearing things about him and was hoping to watch some ot (miraculous sign) being performed by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:14; MATTITYAHU 7:6; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:23"> And Herod was questioning him with many words, but he answered him nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKAS 11:53; LUKAS 23:14–15; LUKAS 23:5"> And the Rashei Hakohenim and the Sofrim had stood by vehemently accusing Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MARKOS 15:16–20; MATTITYAHU 27:27–30; TEHILLIM 69:19–20"> And having treated him with contempt, Herod with his chaiyalim also ridiculed him, having clothed him in an elegant royal robe. So they thus sent back him to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:27; HISGALUS 17:13–14; TEHILLIM 83:4–6"> And Herod and Pilate became fast friends with one another on that very day, for they were previously at enmity toward each other.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MARKOS 15:14; MATTITYAHU 27:21–23; YOCHANAN 18:38"> And Pilate, when he had assembled the Rashei Hakohanim and the manhigim and the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DANIEL 6:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:26; LUKAS 23:1–2"> Said to them, You brought to me this man causing the people to rise up in mered (revolt), and, hinei, I have cross-examined him before you and I found nothing in this man of the crime of which you make accusations against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And neither did Herod, for he sent him back to us, and, hinei, nothing worthy of mishpat mavet has been done by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:26; LUKAS 23:22; MARKOS 15:15"> Therefore, having disciplined him, I will release him. &#91;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Now he was obliged to release to them one at the Chag (Festival).&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:36; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:22"> But they cried out with one kol, saying, Away with this one! And release to us Bar-Abba!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:14; LUKAS 23:5; LUKAS 23:2"> (Bar-Abba was a man who was thrown into the beis hasohar, because of some insurrection which occurred in HaIr as well as a retzach.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MARKOS 15:15; MATTITYAHU 14:8–9; MATTITYAHU 27:19"> And again Pilate addressed them, wishing to release Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:22–25; MARKOS 15:13; YOCHANAN 19:15"> But they were crying out saying, Let him be talui al HaEtz! Let him be hanged on HaEtz!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Dt 21:23">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKAS 23:14; LUKAS 23:20; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18"> And a paam shlishit (third time), Pilate said to them, But what raah did this one do? No crime worthy of mavet did I find in him. Therefore, having disciplined him, I will release him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 11:8; TEHILLIM 57:4; TEHILLIM 22:12–13"> But they insisted with a kol gadol demanding him to be hanged on HaEtz and their voices were roiv (the larger portion) prevailing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:26; SHEMOT 23:2; MISHLE 17:15"> And Pilate decided that their demand be accomplished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:14; MARKOS 15:6–7; MARKOS 15:15"> And Pilate released the one they were requesting, the one who had been thrown into the beis hasohar on account of an insurrection and retzach, but Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach he delivered over to their ratzon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:32–44; MARKOS 15:21–32; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:9"> And as they led him away, having seized a certain Shim'on, a Cyrenian, coming in from the country, they put upon him Moshiachʼs Etz to carry, following Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKAS 8:52; LUKAS 8:2; MARKOS 15:40"> And a great multitude of HaAm were following him and nashim, who were acting as avelim, lamenting for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHIR HASHIRIM 8:4; SHIR HASHIRIM 5:8; SHIR HASHIRIM 1:5"> And having turned to them, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said, Benot HaYerushalayim (Daughters of Yerushalayim), stop weeping for me; weep for yourselves and for your yeladim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:19; LUKAS 21:23–24; MARKOS 13:17–19"> Because, hinei, yamim are coming in which they will say, Ashrey are the barren and the wombs which did not bear and the breasts which did not nurse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="HISGALUS 6:16; HOSHEA 10:8; YESHAYAH 2:19"> Then they will begin to say to the mountain, Fall on us! And to the hills, Bury us.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hos 10:8; Isa 2:19">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:17–18; YOCHANAN 15:6; MISHLE 11:31"> Because if while an etz (tree) is green they do these things, what may happen when it is dry?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ezek 21:3">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MARKOS 15:27; YESHAYAH 53:12; MATTITYAHU 27:38"> And two POSHA'IM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="POSHA'IM in verses 32, 33, and 39, see Isa 53:9,12">d</a></sup>(criminals, resha'im) were also being led away with Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach to be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MARKOS 15:22–23; YOCHANAN 19:17–18; YOCHANAN 18:32"> And when they came upon the place being called The Skull, there they hanged each on his etz, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and the POSHA'IM (criminals), one on the right, and one on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:44; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:60; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9"> But Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach was saying, Abba, grant selicha to them, for they have no daas of what they are doing. And dividing up his garments, VAPPILU GORAL (they cast lots).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ps 22:19">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="TEHILLIM 22:17; YESHAYAH 42:1; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:4"> And HaAm had stood watching. And the manhigim were mocking him also, saying, Others he saved, let him save himself, if this one is the Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach of Hashem, the Bechir Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ps 22:17; Isa 42:1">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="TEHILLIM 69:21; MATTITYAHU 27:48; MATTITYAHU 27:34"> And the chaiyalim also ridiculed him as they approached, offering CHOMETZ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ps 69:21">g</a></sup>(wine vinegar) to him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ps 22:7">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and saying, If you are HaMelech HaYehudim, save yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MARKOS 15:26; MATTITYAHU 27:37; YOCHANAN 19:19–22"> And there was also an inscription over him, this is hamelech hayehudim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:44; MARKOS 15:32; LUKAS 23:35"> And one of the POSHA'IM (criminals) having been hanged with him was speaking Chillul Hashem gadfanut against him, saying, Are you not the Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:11; LUKAS 12:5; HISGALUS 15:4"> Save yourself and us. And in reply the other, rebuking him, said, Do you not have yirat Shomayim? For you are under the same gezar din (verdict) of harshaah (condemnation as guilty).</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:40–41; EZRA 9:13; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19"> And this is a just gezar din against us, for we deserve what we are getting for our ma'asim (deeds). But this one did nothing rah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:9–14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:31; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:11–13"> And he was saying, Adoni, remember me when you come into your Malchut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:7; TEHILLIM 50:15; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:15–16"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Omein, I say to you, hayom (today) you will be with me in Gan Eden.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:45; MARKOS 15:33–34; AMOS 8:9"> It was already the sixth hour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="noon">i</a></sup>and choshech was over the whole land until the ninth hour.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Am 8:9">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:19–22; MATTITYAHU 27:51; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:3–8"> The shemesh being obscured; and the Parochet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="between the Kodesh HaKodashim and HaKodesh">k</a></sup>in the Beis Hamikdash was torn in two.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Ex 26:31-33">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="TEHILLIM 31:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:59; YOCHANAN 19:30"> And having cried out with a kol gadol, he said, Abba, BYADCHA AFKID RUCHI (into your hands I commit my ruach).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Ps 31:5[6]">m</a></sup>And this having said, he breathed out his last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:54; MARKOS 15:39; LUKAS 23:41"> And the centurion who saw the thing that happened was saying, Baruch Hashem, surely this man was a Tzaddik.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="LUKAS 18:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:37; YIRMEYAH 31:19"> And when all the hamon (crowd) that had assembled at this spectacle had observed the things that had happened, they went away beating their chests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="LUKAS 8:2; TEHILLIM 38:11; LUKAS 23:55"> And all his acquaintances stood at a distance and the nashim, the ones following him from the Galil, observed these things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:38–42; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:22"> And, hinei, a man by name Yosef being a member of the Sanhedrin, and an ish tov and a tzaddik,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="LUKAS 2:25; LUKAS 2:38; YESHAYAH 8:12"> (This one had not consented to their cheshbon and action) came from Ramatayim, a shtetl of Yehudah. He was waiting expectantly for the Malchut Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:38–42"> He approached Pilate, and asked for the gufat Yehoshua.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Ps 16:9-10; Job 19:25-27; Isa 53:11">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:59–60; YESHAYAH 53:9; MARKOS 15:46"> And, having taken down geviyyato (his body), he wrapped it in a linen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="tachrichim">o</a></sup>and placed it in a hewn kever (tomb) where not anyone had yet been laid.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Ps 16:9-10; Job 19:25-27; Isa 53:11">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:62; YOCHANAN 19:31; MARKOS 15:42"> And it was nearly Erev Shabbat, Shabbos was drawing near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="LUKAS 23:49; MATTITYAHU 27:61; MARKOS 15:47"> And following along after, the nashim who had come out of the Galil with him, saw the kever (tomb) and how his NEVELAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Dt 21:23">q</a></sup>(body) was laid.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Gn 47:18; Ps 16:9-10; Job 19:25-27; Isa 53:11">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="LUKAS 24:1; MARKOS 16:1; DEVARIM 5:14"> And having returned, they prepared spices and ointments.<br />And on Shabbos they rested according to the mitzvah (commandment).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Ex 12:16; 20:10">s</a></sup></span>
