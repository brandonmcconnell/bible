<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARKOS 1:16–20; MATTITYAHU 4:18–22; MARKOS 3:9"> Now it came about that while the multitude was listening to the dvar Hashem and pressing in upon Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach, he had been standing beside Lake Kinneret,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MARKOS 1:19; MATTITYAHU 4:21"> And he saw two sirot (boats) having been beside the lake. But the daiyagim (fishermen) had left them and were cleaning the nets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:18; MATTITYAHU 13:1–2; YOCHANAN 1:41–42"> And embarking into one of the sirot which was Shimonʼs, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach asked Shim'on to put out from the land a little; and having sat down, from the sirah (boat) to the multitudes Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach was saying shiurim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YOCHANAN 21:6; MATTITYAHU 17:27"> And when he stopped speaking, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to Shim'on, Launch out into the deep (water) and let down your nets for a catch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 2:5; YOCHANAN 15:14; LUKAS 6:46–48"> And in reply, Shim'on said, Adoni, throughout the whole lailah we have labored and caught nothing. But on account of your dvar I will let down the nets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:58; YOCHANAN 21:6–11; KOHELET 11:6"> And having done this, they enclosed asach (a lot of) dagim, and their nets were being torn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:25"> And they signaled for their shuttafim (partners) in the other sirah to come and help them. And they came and they filled both sirot (boats) so much that they began to sink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YESHAYAH 6:5; IYOV 42:5–6; HISGALUS 1:17"> And having seen this, Shim'on Kefa fell down before Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach, saying, Depart from me, Adoni, for an ish choteh (sinful man) am I.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gn 18:27; Job 42:6; Isa 6:5">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 4:32; TEHILLIM 8:8; MARKOS 9:6"> For astonishment seized Shim'on Kefa and all the ones with him on account of the catch of dagim which they took;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:19; MATTITYAHU 13:47; YECHEZKEL 47:9–10"> And likewise also Ya'akov and Yochanan the banim of Zavdai, who were business shuttafim (partners) with Shim'on. And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to Shim'on, Do not be afraid. From now on you will catch bnei Adam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:20; LUKAS 5:28; MATTITYAHU 19:27"> And having left behind the sirot on the shore, they forsook all, and followed him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="as talmidim">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="TEHILLIM 50:15; LUKAS 17:16; MATTITYAHU 8:2–4"> And it came about, while he was in one of the shtetlach, hinei, there was an ish metzorah full of leprosy. And having seen Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach, and having fallen on his face, he begged him saying, Adoni, if you are willing, you are able to make me tahor (clean).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:29–30; YOCHANAN 4:50–53; HOSHEA 14:4"> And having stretched out his hand, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach touched him, saying, I am willing. Be made tahor. And, ofen ort (immediately), the leprosy departed from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="VAYIKRA 14:2–32; LUKAS 17:14; MARKOS 1:44"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach gave orders to him to tell no one, but go and show yourself to the kohen and make a korban for your tohorah (purification) as Moshe Rabbeinu gave mitzvah; do this for an edut (solemn testimony) to them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Lv 14:2-32">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:30–31; LUKAS 12:1; MARKOS 1:45–2"> But the man was spreading even more the dvar about Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, and many multitudes were assembling to listen and to receive refuah (healing) from their machlot (illnesses).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 6:12; MARKOS 1:35–36; MATTITYAHU 14:23"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was withdrawing in hitbodedut (seclusion, aloneness with G-d) into the wilderness places and was davening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKAS 6:19; LUKAS 8:46; MATTITYAHU 11:5"> And it came about, on one of the yamim when he was teaching Torah, that the Perushim and Sofrim had come from every shtetl of the Galil and Yehudah and Yerushalayim and were sitting by. And the power of Hashem was with Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach to bring refuah (healing).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MARKOS 2:3–12; MATTITYAHU 9:2–8; YOCHANAN 5:5–6"> And hinei, anashim were carrying on a mat a man who had been paralyzed, and they were seeking to carry in the paralytic and to place him before Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARKOS 2:4; MATTITYAHU 10:27; MATTITYAHU 24:17"> And not having found by what way they might carry him, because of the multitude, and having gone up onto the roof, they let the man down with the mat through the tiles so that he was right in the center in front of Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:9; MATTITYAHU 9:2; MARKOS 2:5"> And having seen their emunah, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said, Ben Adam, your chatta'im (sins) have received selicha (forgiveness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YESHAYAH 43:25; MARKOS 2:6–7; LUKAS 7:49"> And the Sofrim began to raise kashes (questions), and also the Perushim, saying, Who is this who is speaking Chillul Hashem gidduf? Who is able to grant selicha to chatta'im but Hashem alone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKAS 24:38; YESHAYAH 66:18; TEHILLIM 139:2"> But he having had daas of their machshavot (thoughts), said to them in reply, Why are you reasoning in your levavot?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MARKOS 2:9; MATTITYAHU 9:5"> Which is easier: to say, Your chatta'im have been granted selicha, or to say, Get up and walk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:13; YESHAYAH 53:11; LUKAS 8:54"> But in order that you may have da'as that the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13">d</a></sup>has samchut on HaAretz to grant selicha to chatta'im, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to the one having been paralyzed, To you I say, Get up, pick up your mat, and go to your bais (house, home).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:24; LUKAS 5:13; TEHILLIM 107:20–22"> And at once the man arose in front of them, picked up the mat upon which he was lying, and departed to his bais, shouting, Baruch Hashem!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:8; MATTITYAHU 12:23; LUKAS 7:16"> And astonishment seized everyone, and they were shouting, Baruch Hashem! And they were filled with yirat Shomayim, saying, Hayom (today) our eyes have beheld niflaot (wonders)!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MARKOS 2:13–22; MATTITYAHU 9:9–17; YOCHANAN 1:43"> And after these things Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach went out and saw a moches (tax collector) named Levi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Mattityahu">e</a></sup>sitting in the tax office, and he said to him, Follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKAS 5:11; MATTITYAHU 19:22–27; MELACHIM ALEF 19:19–21"> And having forsaken all and having got up, Levi was following Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MARKOS 2:15; LUKAS 15:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:27"> And Levi arranged a large seudah in his bais for Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach, and there was a great number of mochesim and others who were with them, reclining at tish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:28–32; LUKAS 5:17; MARKOS 7:3"> And the Perushim and their Sofrim were murmuring against Rebbe Melech HaMoshiachʼs talmidim, saying, Why with the mochesim and chote'im are you eating and drinking?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MARKOS 2:17; MATTITYAHU 9:12–13; YIRMEYAH 8:22"> And in reply, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to them, It is not the bariim who have need of a rofeh, but the cholim (sick persons);</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LUKAS 19:10; LUKAS 15:10; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:15–16"> I have not come to call the tzaddikim but chote'im (sinners) to teshuva (repentance).</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:14–17; MARKOS 2:18–22; YOCHANAN 3:25"> But they said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Yochananʼs talmidim undergo tzomot (fasts) often and offer tefillos; likewise also the ones of the Perushim; but your talmidim eat and drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:29; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:2; YESHAYAH 54:5"> But Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to them, You are not able to make the Bnei haChuppah undergo tzomot while the Choson is with them, are you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LUKAS 17:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:21"> But yamim will come when the Choson is taken away from them; then, in those yamim, they will undergo tzomot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:16–17; MARKOS 2:21–22; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:16"> Now Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was telling also a mashal to them: No one tears a piece from a new garment, and sews it as a patch on an old garment. Otherwise, both the new will be torn, and the patch from the new will not match the old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 9:13; TEHILLIM 119:83; YEHOSHUA 9:4"> And no one puts yayin chadash (new wine) into old wineskins; otherwise, the new wine will burst the wineskins, it will be spilled, and the wineskins will be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:8–13; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:17; YECHEZKEL 36:26"> Rather, yayin chadash must be put into new wineskins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:39; MARKOS 7:7–13; YIRMEYAH 6:16"> And no one having drunk the old desires the chadash (new), for he says, The alter (old) is besere (better).</span>
