<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:23–27; LUKAS 19:47–48; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:12"> And it came about on one of the yamim Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying shiurim to HaAm (the People) in the Beis Hamikdash and was preaching the Besuras HaGeulah, the Rashei Hakohanim and the Sofrim with the Ziknei HaAm stood by;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 2:14; MARKOS 11:28–33; YOCHANAN 2:18"> They spoke, saying to him, Tell us by what shlita (authority) you do these things, or who is the one having given to you this shlita?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:2–3; LUKAS 22:68; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:6"> In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, I will also confront you will a she'elah, and you tell me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKAS 15:18; MATTITYAHU 11:7–19; MATTITYAHU 17:11–12"> The tevilah of teshuva of Yochanan — was it from Shomayim or from Bnei Adam?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:34; YOCHANAN 1:15–18; YOCHANAN 1:30"> And they reasoned to themselves saying, If we say, from Shomayim, he will say, why did you not believe him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:26; MATTITYAHU 11:9; YOCHANAN 10:41"> But if we say, from Bnei Adam, HaAm (the People) all will subject us to sequilah (stoning), for HaAm Yisroel are all convinced that Yochanan is a navi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YESHAYAH 29:14; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:3; YESHAYAH 41:28"> And they answered that they did not have daas of the origin of the tevilah of teshuva of Yochanan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MISHLE 26:4–5; MATTITYAHU 21:27; MARKOS 11:33"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Neither am I telling you by what shlita (authority) I do these things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:33–46; MARKOS 12:1–12; YESHAYAH 5:1–7"> And he began to tell this mashal to HaAm Yisroel. A certain ish planted a kerem and leased it to koremim (vine keepers) and he went away for a long time.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Isa 5:1-7">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:15–16; NECHEMYAH 9:26; MATTITYAHU 21:34–36"> And in season he sent to the koremim (vine keepers) an eved (servant) that from the pri of the kerem they will give to him. But the koremim (vine keepers) sent the eved away empty, having beaten him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HOSHEA 10:1; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:36–37; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:2"> And he proceeded to send another eved. But him also, when they had beaten and dishonored him, they sent him away empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he proceeded to send an eved shlishi (a third servant). And also this one, having wounded, they threw out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 3:17; YOCHANAN 3:16–17; YIRMEYAH 36:7"> And the Adon of the kerem, said, What should I do? I will send Beni Ahuvi. Perhaps this one they will respect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:23"> But having seen him, the koremim (vine keepers) were reasoning with one another saying, This one is the Bechor, the heir. Let us kill him, that the bechorah may become ours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:12; MARKOS 12:6–9; MATTITYAHU 21:37–40"> And having driven him outside the kerem, they killed him. What then will the Baal HaKerem do to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 19:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:46; MATTITYAHU 21:41"> He will come and will destroy these koremim (vine keepers) and will give the kerem to others. And having heard this, HaAm Yisroel said, Chas vShalom!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TEHILLIM 118:22; YESHAYAH 28:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:11"> But having looked at them, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, What then is this which has been written: EVEN MAASU HAYTAH LEROSH PINAH (The Stone they rejected became the capstone)?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 118:22">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:14–15; ZECHARYAH 12:3; DANIEL 2:44–45"> Everyone having fallen upon that EVEN (stone) will be DAKU<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Dan 2:34">c</a></sup>(broken into pieces), and upon whomever it falls, it will crush him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 8:14,15; Dan 2:34f">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKAS 19:47–48; LUKAS 20:14; MATTITYAHU 21:45–46"> And the Sofrim and the Rashei Hakohenim sought to lay their hands upon him in the same hour, but they were afraid of HaAm Yisroel, for they knew that he spoke this mashal (parable) against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKAS 11:54; MATTITYAHU 22:15–22; MATTITYAHU 27:2"> And having watched carefully, they sent spies pretending themselves to be tzaddikim, that they might catch him in his dvar, so as to deliver him to the rulers and the manhigim under the Moshel (Governor).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:4–5; MATTITYAHU 26:49–50; MATTITYAHU 22:16"> And they set a she'elah (question) before him, saying, Rabbi, we have daas that you speak beemes in the shiurim you say and you show no deference toward anyone, but on the basis of HaEmes you give torah about HaDerech Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 9:37; MARKOS 12:14–17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:37"> Is it mutar for us to pay tax to Caesar or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 22:18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:19; LUKAS 5:22"> But having noticed the ORMAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gn 3:1">e</a></sup>(cunning, craftiness) in their manner, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:32"> Show me a denarius. Whose demut (likeness) has it? And whose inscription? And they said, Caesarʼs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:6–7; MARKOS 12:17; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13–17"> And he said to them, Then give the things of Caesar to Caesar and the things of Hashem to Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LUKAS 20:20; MISHLE 26:4–5; LETTER TO TITOS 1:10"> And they were not able to catch him in his dvar before the people and, having been nispoyel (marveled) at his teshuva (answer), they were silent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:1–2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:12; MARKOS 12:18–27"> And some of the Tzedukim — the Tzedukim are the ones speaking against the Techiyas HaMesim, who say there is none — approached Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach. They set a she'elah (question) before him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEVARIM 25:5–10; BERESHIS 38:8; BERESHIS 38:11"> Saying, Rabbi, Moshe Rabbenu laid it down in writing to us, if someoneʼs ach dies and has an isha, and UVEN EIN LO (and there is no son to him), that he should take the almanah of his ach and he should raise up a zera (seed) to his ach.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Dt 25:5">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:20; YIRMEYAH 22:30"> Now there were shiva achim. And the first took an isha, and died UVEN EIN LO (and there is no son to him).</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the second took her to be his isha, and he died childless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the third took her; and likewise also the seventh did not leave behind a ben and they all died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="KOHELET 1:4; KOHELET 9:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:27"> And lemaskana (finally) the isha died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 22:24–28; MARKOS 12:19–23"> The isha, then, in the Techiyas HaMesim, of which of them does she become the wife? For shiva had her as isha (wife).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LUKAS 16:8; LUKAS 17:27; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:4"> And Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach said to them, The banim of the Olam Hazeh marry and are given in marriage:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:5; YOCHANAN 5:29; DANIEL 12:2–3"> But the ones having been considered worthy to attain to the Olam HaBah and the Techiyas HaMesim neither marry nor are given in marriage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:21; MATTITYAHU 22:30"> For neither is it possible any longer for them to die, for they are like malachim and they are bnei haElohim, being bnei haTechiyas HaMesim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:2–6; MARKOS 12:26–27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:30–32"> But that the Mesim are made to stand up alive, even Moshe revealed at the burning bush, as he calls Adonoi ELOHEI AVRAHAM ELOHEI YITZCHAK VELOHEI YA'AKOV.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ex 3:6">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:25–26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:3–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:10–11"> But Hashem is not the G-d of Mesim but is Elohei HaChayyim, for to Hashem all are alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:9; MARKOS 12:28–34; MATTITYAHU 22:34–40"> And, in reply, some of the Sofrim said, Rabbi, you spoke well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 22:46; MARKOS 12:34; MISHLE 26:5"> For no longer were they daring to set a she'elah (question) before him regarding anything.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MARKOS 12:35–37; MATTITYAHU 1:1; MATTITYAHU 22:41–45"> And he said to them, How do they say that the Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach is to be &#91;merely&#93; Dovidʼs ben?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="TEHILLIM 110:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:34–35; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:13"> For Dovid himself says in the book of Tehillim, NEUM HASHEM LADONI: SHEV LIMINI,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LUKAS 19:27; TEHILLIM 110:1; HISGALUS 19:14–21"> AD ASHIT OYVECHA HADOM L'RAGLECHA.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ps 110:1">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="HISGALUS 22:16; LUKAS 1:31–35; MATTITYAHU 1:23"> Therefore, if Dovid calls him Adon, how is he &#91;merely&#93; ben Dovid?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:10; MARKOS 12:38–39; MARKOS 8:34"> In the hearing of all the people, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to his talmidim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LUKAS 11:43; MARKOS 12:38–39; LUKAS 14:7"> Beware of the Sofrim (Rabbonim), the ones wanting to strut around in long kaftans and loving<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="obsequious">i</a></sup>Birkat Shalom greetings in the marketplaces and the rashei hamoshavot (chief seats) in the shuls and the places of kavod at seudos tishen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MARKOS 12:40; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:1; LETTER TO TITOS 1:16"> Which devour the batim (houses) of the almanot (widows) and for the sake of appearance daven lengthy tefillos (prayers): the same shall receive greater damnation.</span>
</div>
