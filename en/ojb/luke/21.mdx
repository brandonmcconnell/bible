<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARKOS 12:41–44; YOCHANAN 8:20; MATTITYAHU 27:6"> And having looked up, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach saw the ashirim (rich people) putting into the Beis Hamikdash Otzar (Treasury) their matanot (gifts).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MARKOS 12:42"> And he saw a certain poor almanah (widow) putting in there two leptas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:34; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:2–3"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Omein, I say to you that this poor almanah (widow) put in more tzedakah than everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:44–45; LUKAS 15:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:34"> For all these put in matanot (gifts) out of their abundance, but this almanah (widow), out of her need, put in her michyah (subsistence).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 2:20; MATTITYAHU 24:1–51; MARKOS 13:1–37"> And as some were speaking about the Beis Hamikdash, that with beautiful avanim (stones) and with matanot (gifts) it has been decorated, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:2; MARKOS 13:2; EKHAH 5:18"> Yamim (Days) will come in which of these things which you see there will not be left an even (stone) upon an even (stone) which will not be thrown down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:6–7; MARKOS 13:14; MARKOS 13:3–4"> And they questioned him, saying, Rebbe, ad mosai? (how much longer?) When will these things be, and what will be the ot when these things are about to take place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1; MARKOS 13:5–6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:6"> And he said, Beware, you should not be deceived. For many will come Bishmi (in my Name)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Moshiach">a</a></sup>saying, “I am he.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="I am the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">b</a></sup>And, the time &#91;of the Geulah&#93; has drawn near.” Do not follow after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 21:28; MATTITYAHU 24:6–8; MARKOS 13:7–8"> But whenever you hear about wars and intifadas, do not be a pachdan (coward). For it is necessary for these things to occur barishonah, but it is not immediately HaKetz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:19–20; MARKOS 13:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:5–6"> Then he was saying to them, Ethnic group will have an intifada against ethnic group, and malchut against malchut,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2Chr 15:6; Isa 19:2">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:29–30; LUKAS 21:25–27"> There will be great earthquakes and in various places, raav (famine) and makkot (plagues), both horrible portents and great moftim (signs) in Shomayim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 29:6; Yoel 3:3">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:12–14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:40; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:30"> But before all these things, they will lay their hands on you and bring redifot (persecutions) on you, handing you over to the shuls and the batei hasohar (prisons), being led away before melachim (kings) and moshelim (governors) because of my Name (Yehoshua, Yeshua).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:28; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:5"> It will turn out for you to be an opportunity for being an eidus (witness) to the edut (testimony).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 12:11–12; MARKOS 13:11; MATTITYAHU 10:19–20"> Therefore, keep in your levavot (hearts) not to prepare to defend yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 1:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:19; LUKAS 12:12"> For I will give to you a peh (mouth) and chochmah which none of your mitnaggedim will be able to resist or to contradict.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 12:53; HISGALUS 6:9; MICHOH 7:5–6"> And you will be handed over also by horim and achim and krovey mishpachot (relatives) and chaverim (friends), and they will have some of you executed and you will die al kiddush ha-Shem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:19; LUKAS 6:22; YOCHANAN 15:21"> And you will be the focus of sinas chinom (baseless hatred) from everyone because of my Name (Yehoshua, Yeshua).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:30; SHMUEL ALEF 14:45; LUKAS 12:7"> And not even a hair of your rosh will by any means perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:36; MATTITYAHU 10:22; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:3"> In your savlanut (patience) and endurance you will gain your nefashot (souls).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKAS 19:43; DANIEL 9:27; MARKOS 13:14"> But when you see Yerushalayim being surrounded by machanot (encamping armies), then have daas that the sho'ah of it has drawn near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 6:1; BERESHIS 19:17; LUKAS 17:31–33"> Then the ones in Yehudah, flee to the mountains, and the ones inside HaIr (the City), let them get out, and the ones in the sadot (fields), let them not enter into HaIr (the City),</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HOSHEA 9:7; YESHAYAH 65:12–16; YESHAYAH 63:4"> Because these are the Yemei Nekamah (Days of Vengeance), as a fulfillment of all the Kitvei Hakodesh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Isa 63:4; Dan 9:24-27; Hos 9:7">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MARKOS 13:17; MATTITYAHU 24:19; LUKAS 23:29"> Oy to the ones with child and to the ones nursing in yamim hahem (those days). For there will be Tzarah Gedolah (Great Tribulation) upon Eretz Yisroel and kaas (anger) against this people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:25; HISGALUS 11:2; YESHAYAH 63:18"> And they will fall by the edge of the cherev (sword) and they will all be led captive into the Golus, and Yerushalayim will be trampled down by the Goyim, until the times of the Goyim are fulfilled.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isa 5:5; 63:18; Dan 8:13">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YESHAYAH 13:10; YOEL 2:30–31; HISGALUS 6:12–14"> And there will be otot (miraculous signs) in the shemesh (sun) and the levanah (moon) and the kochavim (stars), and upon the earth there will be dismay among the Goyim, in perplexity at the sound of the sea and the waves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:29; DEVARIM 28:65–67; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:10–12"> Bnei Adam will be fainting from pachad (terror) and from expectation of the things coming upon the Olam Hazeh, for the kochot (powers) of the heavens will be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:30; HISGALUS 1:7; MARKOS 13:26"> And then you will see the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Moshiach, Dan 7:13-14">g</a></sup>and his Bias HaMoshiach on an Anan (Cloud) with gevurah (miraculous power of Hashem) and kavod rav (great glory).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:23; YESHAYAH 60:1–2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:30"> And when the Reshit (the Beginning) of these things occurs, stand erect and lift up your roshim (heads), because your Geulah (Redemption) draws near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:32–35; MARKOS 13:28–30"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach told a mashal (parable) to them. You see the etz te'enah (fig tree) and all the etz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LUKAS 12:57"> When they sprout leaves already, you see for yourselves and you have daas that Kayits (Summer) is already near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:9; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:37; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:7"> So also you, when you see these things happening, you have daas that the Malchut Hashem is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MARKOS 13:30; MATTITYAHU 24:34; MATTITYAHU 23:36"> Omein, I say to you, that HaDor HaZeh will not pass away until all these things may occur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:8; MARKOS 13:31; MATTITYAHU 24:35"> HaShomayim and HaAretz will pass away, but my Devarim will by no means pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MARKOS 4:19; LUKAS 12:45–46; LUKAS 12:40"> And be shomer regarding yourselves lest your levavot (hearts) may be burdened with dissipation and drunkenness and the cares of the Olam HaZeh and Yom HaHu (That Day) may come upon you PITOM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Mal 3:1">h</a></sup>(suddenly).</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="HISGALUS 16:15; TEHILLIM 11:6; YIRMEYAH 48:43–44"> For as a mokesh (trap) it will come upon all the ones dwelling on the pnei kol haAretz (the face of all the Earth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:28; MATTITYAHU 25:13; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:8"> But be shomer (on guard, alert), always davening that you may be able to escape all these things that are about to happen and that you may be able to stand before the Bar Enosh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Moshiach Dan 7:13-14">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKAS 22:39; MARKOS 11:19; MATTITYAHU 21:17"> Now in those days Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying shiurim in the Beis Hamikdash, and balailah (at night) he was going out into the Har HaZeytim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:1–2"> And all the people were getting up early in the boker to come to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach in the Beis Hamikdash to hear him.</span>
