<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:17"> For the Torah, als (since) it has only a shadow of the tovot ha'atidot (good things to come) and is not the etzem (actual) things, can never by the same yearly zevakhim (sacrifices), which they offer continually, make shleimut (whole, complete) those drawing kiruv (near).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YESHAYAH 43:25; MICHOH 7:19; TEHILLIM 103:12"> Otherwise, would these zevakhim not have stopped being offered, because the worshipers, having experienced tohorah (purification, cleansing) even once, would no longer have had consciousness of averos?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:7; SHEMOT 30:10; VAYIKRA 23:27–28"> But by those zevakhim there is a zikaron (remembrance) and a reminder of averos year after year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:11; YESHAYAH 1:11–15; MICHOH 6:6–8"> For it is impossible for the dahm of parim (young bulls) and se'irim (goats) to take away chatta'im (sins).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 40:6–8; YESHAYAH 1:11; YESHAYAH 7:14"> Therefore, when he comes into the Olam Hazeh, he says ZEVACH UMINCHAH LO CHAFATZTA<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ps 40:6[7]">a</a></sup>(sacrifice and offering You did not desire), but a body you prepared for me;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 39:7in the TARGUM HA-SHIVIM">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MALACHI 1:10; MATTITYAHU 3:17; TEHILLIM 147:11"> OLAH V'CHATA'AH LO SH'ALTA<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 40:6">c</a></sup>(Burnt offering and sin offering you have not desired).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 40:7–8; YOCHANAN 4:34; YOCHANAN 6:38"> Then I said, Look, here I am, I have come — it is written about me in the megillah &#91;scroll&#93; LA'ASOT RETZONECHAH ELOHAI CHAFATZTI<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ps 40:8[9]">d</a></sup>(I desire to do your will, O G-d.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:5; MARKOS 12:33"> When he said above, “ZEVACH and MINCHAH and OLAH and CHATA'AH you have not desired, nor have you taken pleasure in them” — these are offered according to the Torah</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:18–19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:27–28; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:7"> — Then he said, I have come LA'ASOT RETZONECHAH (to do your will.) He takes away HaRishonah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="the way of the zevakhim of the kehunah of Levi">e</a></sup>in order to establish HaSheniyah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="the way of the zevach of the kehunah of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:11"> And it is by the ratzon Hashem that we will have been set apart for kedushah through the korban NEFESH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 53:10cf. ASHAM KORBAN">g</a></sup>of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, once and for all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:1; BAMIDBAR 28:3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:4"> And every kohen stands daily at his avodas kodesh sherut ministering and offering again and again the same korbanot that can never take away chatta'im;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:34"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, having offered up one korban for chatta'im for all time, YASHAV LIMIN HASHEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ps 110:1">h</a></sup>(Sat down at the right hand of G-d),</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:13; TEHILLIM 110:1; LUKAS 20:43"> Waiting from that time onward until OYVAV (His enemies) be made “a footstool for his feet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:25; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:1; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:14"> For by one korban he has perfected forever HaMekudashim (the ones being set apart as Kedoshim).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:7; HISGALUS 2:11; HISGALUS 3:6"> And the Ruach Hakodesh also bears solemn edut to us; for after saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:33–34; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:8–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:27"> ZOT HABRIT ASHER EKHROT (“This is the covenant that I will make) with them after those days,” says the L-rd, “I will put my Torah in the mind of them and I will inscribe it on their heart.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Jer 31:33">i</a></sup>He then says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:34; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:12"> LA'AVONAM U'LECHATTATAM LO EZKAR<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Jer 31:34">j</a></sup>(And their wickedness and their sin I will remember no more).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:14"> Now where there is selicha (forgiveness) for these things, there is no longer a korban for chatta'im.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:18; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:16"> Therefore, Achim b'Moshiach, having confidence for bevitachon (confidently) entering haSha'ar laHashem (gate to approach G-dʼs presence, access of the tzaddikim)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ps 118:20">k</a></sup>into the Kodesh HaKodashim by HaDahm HaYehoshua,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:6; YOCHANAN 10:9; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:3"> Which he opened for us as a Derech Chadasha, a Derech Chayyah, through the parokhet, that is to say, the parokhet opened when was offered the basar of Moshiach.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Ps 16:9-10; Dan 9:26; Isa 53:5-12">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:14–16; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:1"> And als (since) we have a Kohen Gadol over the Beis Hashem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 36:25; YOCHANAN 3:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:26"> Let us approach and draw near to Hashem with a lev shalem, with full assurance and bitachon of Emunah, our levavot having been sprinkled clean (tehorim)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="YAZZEH, “MOSHIACH WILL SPRINKLE,” Isa 52:15">m</a></sup>from an evil matzpun (conscience) and our bodies plunged kluhr (pure) into a tevilah in a mikveh mayim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Ezek 36:25-26">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:3"> Let us, without wavering, hold firmly to the hoda'ah of Tikveteinu, for Ne'eman is the One having given the havtachah (promise).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:11; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:1"> And let us consider how to meorer (stimulate, motivate, shtarken) one another to ahavah and mitzvos,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:42; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:11; MATTITYAHU 18:20"> And let us not turn away and defect from our noiheg (habitually) conducted daily minyan, as some are doing; let us impart chizzuk (strengthening, encouragement) to one another, and by so much the more as you see the Yom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="HaDin (Day of Judgment)">o</a></sup>approaching.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20–22; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:4–6; YOCHANAN 9:41"> For when we intentionally commit chet b'yad ramah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Num 15:30">p</a></sup>(wilful sin with a high hand of defiance) after having received the full da'as of HaEmes, there remains no longer a korban for chattoteinu,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YESHAYAH 26:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:3; TZEFANYAH 1:18"> But only a terrible expectation of Din and Mishpat and of a blazing EISH TZARECHA TOKHLEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Isa 26:11">q</a></sup>(Fire that will consume the enemies of Hashem).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:2; DEVARIM 19:15; DEVARIM 17:2–13"> Anyone who was doiche (rejecting or setting aside) the Torah of Moshe Rabbeinu, upon the dvar of SHNI EDIM O AL PI SHLOSHA EDIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Dt 19:15">r</a></sup>(Testimony of two or three witnesses), dies without rachamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:30; YESHAYAH 63:10"> By how much worse onesh (penalty) do you think the one will be considered worthy who trampled on the Zun fun der Oybershter and also treated as mechallel kodesh (profane) the Dahm HaBrit which set him apart mekudash and also committed Chillul Hashem gidduf against the Ruach Hakodesh of Hashemʼs chesed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:35–36; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:19; NACHUM 1:2"> For we have da'as of the One who said, LI NAKAM V'SHILEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Dt 32:35">s</a></sup>(Vengeance is mine and I will repay) and again YADIN HASHEM AMMO<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Dt 32:36">t</a></sup>(The L-rd will judge his people).</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:28; LUKAS 12:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:29"> It is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the Elohim Chayyim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:4; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:3–13"> But have zikaron of the yamim mikedem (earlier days) in which, when you had received the Ohr Hashem, you endured a great tzoros, a great “Kristallnacht” of yissurim (suffering),</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:36"> sometimes being publicly abused with baleidikung (insult) and fargolgung (persecution) yourselves; other times being oppressed chavrusa partners with the ones so treated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:4; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:16; MATTITYAHU 5:11–12"> For you showed Gemilut Chasadim for the Achim b'Moshiach in the beis hasohar and with simcha you accepted the pogrom-like confiscation of your property because you have da'as that you possess a better and more enduring yerushah (inheritance).</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:58; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:8–10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:6"> Do not discard, then, your bitachon (confident trust) which has gadol sachar (great reward).</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:1; TEHILLIM 37:7; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:7–11"> You are nitzrach (needy) of the kind of savlanut (patience) that has endurance, in order that, having accomplished the ratzon Hashem, you will receive the havtachah (promise).</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:7–9; CHABAKUK 2:3–4; LUKAS 18:8"> For yet a little while and Hu HaBah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach">u</a></sup>YAVO LO YE'ACHER (He will come, he will not delay),</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:11; CHABAKUK 2:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:17"> V'TZADDIK VE'EMUNATO YICHEYEH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hab 2:3-4">v</a></sup>(And my tzaddik will live by Emunah). And, if he shrinks back as a shmad defector, LO RATZTAH NAFSHI BO (My soul has no pleasure in him).</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:5; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5; MARKOS 16:16"> But we are not of those who shrink back as shmad defectors toward churban destruction, but we are of those with Emunah whose neshamah is preserved in Yeshu'at Eloheinu.</span>
</div>
