<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:24–27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:16"> So, therefore, als (since) we have surrounding us so great an Anan Edim (Cloud of Witnesses), let us also lay aside every weighty impediment and easily ensnaring averos, and let us run with endurance and savlanut the race set before us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MICHOH 7:7; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:23–24"> Fixing our gaze on the Mekhonen (Founder) of our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Orthodox Messianic Jewish">a</a></sup>faith, and the One who makes it Shleimut — Yehoshua, who because of the simcha set before him, endured HaEtz HaMoshiach, disregarding its bushah (shame), and “sat down at the right hand of the throne of G-d.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 110:1">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:16; HISGALUS 2:3"> Let your hitbonenut (meditation) be on the one who endured such opposition from chote'im (sinners), that you may not be weary in your neshamot, losing heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:13; HISGALUS 2:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:32–34"> For you have not yet resisted to the point of death al kiddush ha-Shem in your struggle against Chet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MISHLE 3:11–12; IYOV 5:17–18; HISGALUS 3:19"> And you have let slip from zikaron (remembrance) the dvar haChizzuk which he speaks to you as banim, My son, do not despise the musar of Hashem, do not lose heart, resent when rebuked by Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:19; MISHLE 3:12; TEHILLIM 119:75"> KI ES ASHER YE'EHAV HASHEM YOKHI'ACH (for whom Hashem loves he disciplines), and he punishes ES BEN YIRTZEH (every son he receives).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Prov 3:11-12; Job 5:17">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MISHLE 19:18; DEVARIM 8:5; MISHLE 13:24"> For you endure nisyonos for the sake of musar. And Hashem is dealing with you as banim. For what ben is there whom an Abba does not give musar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:6; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:9–10; TEHILLIM 73:1"> But if you are without musar, in which all the yeladim share, then you are not banim but mamzerim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 16:22; BAMIDBAR 27:16; YESHAYAH 42:5"> Furthermore, we had Avot on HaAretz, who were morim that we reverenced and treated with kavod. Should we not even more subject ourselves to the Avi HaRuchot and live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:4; VAYIKRA 11:44–45; LETTER TO TITOS 2:14"> For they disciplined us for a short time, as seemed tov to them, but Hashem disciplines us for tov lanu in order that we might share in his kedushah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:3–5; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:5–6"> All musar for the moment seems not to be na'im (pleasant), but seems to bring agmat nefesh; yet afterwards to those who have been taught by musar, it yields the p'ri haShalom and the p'ri haTzedek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 35:3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:14"> Therefore, “Bring chizzuk to the weak hands and the feeble knees;”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 35:3">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MISHLE 4:26–27; YESHAYAH 35:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:1"> And make the drakhim straight for your feet, so that the ever (limb, member) which is lame may not be dislocated, but rather have refu'ah (healing).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:1; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:15–16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:7"> Pursue shalom with kol Bnei Adam, and the kedushah without which no one will see Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:12; DEVARIM 29:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:4"> See to it that no one fall short of the Chen v'Chesed Hashem; that no SHORESH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Isa 35:3">e</a></sup>of merirut (bitterness) sprouting up may cause tzoros, and by it many be made teme'ot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:31–34; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:15–20; HISGALUS 22:15"> Lest someone guilty of gilui arayot (sexual immorality) or some person who is mitnaged ladat (irreligious, opponent of religion) like Esav, who in exchange for one meal sold HaBechorah belonging to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MISHLE 1:24–31; MATTITYAHU 25:11–12; MATTITYAHU 7:23"> For you have da'as that even afterwards, when he desired to receive the nachalah of the bracha, he was rejected, for he found no place for teshuva, though he sought for it with tears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:11; SHEMOT 24:17; SHEMOT 20:18"> For you have not come to a Har that can be touched and to a blazing Eish and to choshech and gloom and storm,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 18:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:52; DEVARIM 4:12"> And to the blast of a shofar and the sound of devarim, which sound was such that the ones having heard begged that no further dvar be spoken to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:12–13; SHEMOT 19:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:10"> For they could not bear the mitzvah, “If even a BEHEMAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ex 19:13">f</a></sup>(animal) should touch the Har, the punishment will be seqilah (stoning).”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:16; DEVARIM 9:19; YESHAYAH 6:3–5"> And so fearful was the sight that Moshe Rabbeinu said, “YAGORTI”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Dt 9:19">g</a></sup>(I am afraid) with trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:20; HISGALUS 14:1"> But you have come to Mount Tziyon, that is, HaIr HaElohim Chayyim, to the Yerushalayim in Shomayim and to myriads of malachim in a knesset innumerable,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:18; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:40; LUKAS 10:20"> And to the Kehillat HaBechorim who are inscribed in Shomayim and to Hashem, the Shofet HaKol, and to the ruchot (spirits) of the tzaddikim made shleimim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:15; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:28; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:4"> And to Yehoshua, the Metavekh of a Brit Chadasha and to the dahm hahazzayah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isa 52:15">h</a></sup>(blood of sprinkling), which speaks better than the dahm haHevel (blood of Abel).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:5; DEVARIM 30:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:1–3"> See to it that you do not refuse the One speaking. For if those did not escape when they refused Him who gave them warning on ha'aretz, much less shall we escape who turn away from the One whose warning comes from Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="CHAGGAI 2:6–7; YESHAYAH 13:13; SHEMOT 19:18"> And the bat kol of Hashem shook ha'aretz then, but now He has given havtachah (promise), saying, Yet once more V'ANI MARISH ES HASHOMAYIM V'ES HA'ARETZ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hag 2:6">i</a></sup>(And I will shake the heavens and the earth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:31; MATTITYAHU 24:35; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:10–11"> Now the phrase, “Yet once more” denotes the removal of that which can be shaken, that is, created things, in order that the unshakeable may remain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:1–2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:15; DANIEL 2:44"> Therefore, als (since) we are receiving a malchut unshakeable, let us hold on to the Chen v'Chesed Hashem through which we may offer to Hashem, an acceptable avodas kodesh, with yirat Shomayim and chasidus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:24; DEVARIM 9:3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:27"> For, indeed, Eloheinu is a consuming Eish.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Dt 4:24; 9:3; Isa 33:14">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
