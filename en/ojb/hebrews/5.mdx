<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:27"> For every Kohen Gadol taken from among Bnei Adam is ordained for men for the avodas kodesh of Hashem, that he may offer both minchot (gifts) and zevakhim (sacrifices) for chatta'im (sins).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:15; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:18; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:9–10"> The Kohen Gadol is able to deal gently with the Am Ha'Aretz and Wayward, als (since) he himself is subject to helpless human frailty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="VAYIKRA 9:7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:7; VAYIKRA 16:6"> and therefore he is obligated to offer zevakhim not only for the averos of the Am Berit but also for his own averos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:1; BAMIDBAR 16:40; BAMIDBAR 16:5"> And one does not presume to take the kavod of the office of Kohen Gadol upon oneself, but one must have the bechirah (choice, election) of Hashem, just as Aharon did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 2:7; YOCHANAN 8:54; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:33"> So also Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach did not seize kavod for himself to become a Kohen Gadol, but the One having said to him, BENI ATAH, ANI HAYOM YELIDTICHA, (My Son you are; Today I have become your Father.)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ps 2:7">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 110:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:21"> Says also in another passage, ATAH KOHEN L'OLAM AL DIVRATI MALKI-TZEDEK (You are a kohen forever according to the order of Malki-Tzedek).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 110:4">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MARKOS 14:32–39; LUKAS 22:41–44; YOCHANAN 17:1"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, in the days of his earthly life, offered with a raised voice and tears both tefillos and techinnah (prayers of supplication) to the One who was able to deliver him from mavet, and Hashem heard him because of his chasidus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:5–9; YOCHANAN 4:34"> Although he was HaBen, Rebbi, Melech HaMoshiach learned mishma'at from his yissurim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:15"> And having been made shalem (complete), to all those with mishma'at toward Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, he became the source of Yeshu'at Eloheinu Olamim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:5–6; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:20"> Having been designated by Hashem as Kohen Gadol AL DIVRATI MALKI-TZEDEK.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 110:4">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:27; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16"> We have much to say about this for which it is difficult to make a midrash, als (since) you have become spiritually hard-of-hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:1–3; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:2"> For indeed by this time you ought to be morim saying shiurim, but instead you have need again to be taught the orthodox Jewish basic ikarim (principles, essentials) of the Divrei Hashem, and you have become one having need of cholov and not solid okhel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:20; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:2; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:16"> For everyone partaking of cholov is unacquainted with HaDivrei Tzedek, for he is an olel (infant).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:14–15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:18"> But solid okhel is for the mevugarim (mature ones, adults), the ones whose keilim (faculties) have been trained by practice for distinguishing both HaTov and HaRah.</span>
