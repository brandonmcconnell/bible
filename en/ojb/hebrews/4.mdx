<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:15; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:9; MISHLE 28:14"> Therefore, let us walk in yir'at Shomayim, for fear that, while the havtachah (promise) of entering the menuchah of Hashem is still open, anyone of you should seem to have fallen short of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:16–17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:6"> For indeed we have had Besuras HaGeulah preached to us, just as they did also; but the Dvar Hashem preached did not make that generation benefit, because hearing did not form an agudah with emunah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="faith">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TEHILLIM 95:11; MATTITYAHU 11:28–29; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:20"> For we ma'aminim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="in Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach">b</a></sup>enter into that menuchah, just as Hashem has said, ASHER NISHBA'TI V'API IM YEVO'UN EL MENUCHATI<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 95:11">c</a></sup>(As I vowed in My fury, they shall never enter into My rest), although the ma'asim (works) of Hashem were finished from the hivvased tevel (foundation of the world).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:11; SHEMOT 31:17; BERESHIS 2:1–2"> For concerning Shabbos, the Yom HaShevi'i, Hashem has said somewhere, VAYISHBOT ELOHIM BAYOM HASHEVI'I MIKOL MELAKHTO (And He rested on the seventh day from all His work).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gn 2:2">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 95:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:11"> And again in this mekor (passage, citation), IM YEVO'UN EL MENUCHATI<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ps 95:11">e</a></sup>(Never shall they enter into My rest).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:43; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:18–19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:46–47"> Therefore, als (since) it remains for some to enter it, and the ones, who formerly had the Besuras HaGeulah preached to them, did not enter, because they were koferim (unbelievers),</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 95:7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:7–8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:15"> So then again Hashem sets a certain day, “HAYOM”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ps 95:7">f</a></sup>(Today) in one of the Tehillim of Dovid,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="HaMelech">g</a></sup>much later, in that quoted above, “HAYOM IM BEKOLO TISHMAU AL TAKSHU LEVAVCHEM”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ps 95:7f">h</a></sup>(Today, if you hear his voice, do not harden your heart).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 1:15; YEHOSHUA 22:4; DEVARIM 12:9"> For if Yehoshua<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="ben Nun">i</a></sup>had brought them to a place of menuchah (rest), Hashem would not have spoken of another “Yom” after that.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ps 95:7">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:10; HISGALUS 21:4; HISGALUS 7:14–17"> Therefore, there remains a Shabbos menuchah for the Am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HISGALUS 14:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:3–4; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1–2"> For whoever has entered into the menuchah of Hashem has also rested from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="depending on the zokheh (merit) of">k</a></sup>his ma'asim, just as Hashem rested from his.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:28–30; LUKAS 13:24; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:18–1"> Therefore, let us have zerizut (diligence) to enter into that menuchah, for fear that anyone fall through following be'ikvot (in the footsteps) of their same lack of mishma'at (obedience).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="cf. 3:17-18; 4:6">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 23:29; YESHAYAH 55:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:17"> For the Dvar Hashem is chai (living) and chazak (strong), sharper than every double-edged cherev and penetrating as far as the division of nefesh and ruach, of both joints and marrow, and able to judge the machshavot and deliberations of the kavanat halev (the inner directedness of the heart).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:5; TEHILLIM 33:13–15; MISHLE 15:3"> And there is not nivra (anything created) nistar (hidden) from His sight, but all things are laid bare and exposed to the eynayim (eyes) of Him to whom we must render an account.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:24; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:12"> Therefore, als (since) we have a great Kohen Gadol who has made his passing through Shomayim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">m</a></sup>Yehoshua Zun fun der Oybershter, let us hold firmly to the hoda'ah (confession) of the hachrazah (proclamation) of our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Orthodox Jewish">n</a></sup>Emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:17–18; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:21; YESHAYAH 53:4–5"> For we do not have a Kohen Gadol who is unable to sympathize with our weaknesses, but one who has been tempted in every way as we are, yet without chet.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="T.N. tempted in every way as we are, yet without chet, means chet as inborn and immemorial yetzer hara (evil inclination) which is Chet Kadmon (Original Sin) whose power and control is passed down to Bnei Adam from Adam, Ro 3:9; Ro 5:12— see Orthodox Jewish Bible Introduction on Moshiach Immanu-El Ben HaAlmah uncontaminated by Chet Kadmon">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:25; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:19–23"> Therefore, let us approach with bitachon the Kisse of Chesed, that we may receive rachamim and may find chesed for timely ezrah (aid).</span>
</div>
