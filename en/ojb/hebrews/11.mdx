<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:7; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:24–25"> Now Emunah is the substance of things for which we have tikvah. Emunah is the conviction of things not seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:39; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:4"> For by Emunah have our Zekenim been given approval.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TEHILLIM 33:6; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:5; YOCHANAN 1:3"> By Emunah we have binah Shomayim v'ha'Aretz found their “barah” from the Dvar Hashem, so that not from anything visible has what we see come into being.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ps 33:6">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:24; BERESHIS 4:3–5; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:11–12"> By Emunah Hevel (Abel) offered to Hashem a korban that was a mincha tovah than that of Kayin. Through this he was given approval that he was a tzaddik, Hashem bearing solemn eidus (testimony) to his matanot; and by his Emunah, Hevel, though niftar (deceased), still speaks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BERESHIS 5:21–24; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:6"> By Emunah Chanokh was taken up, was translated, not to see mavet, V'EINENNU KI LAKACH OTO ELOHIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gn 5:24">b</a></sup>(and he was not, because G-d took him &#91;up&#93;). Before Chanokh was raptured in his aliyah l'Shomayim, he received solemn eidus (testimony) that he had been pleasing to Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MISHLE 8:17; YIRMEYAH 29:13–14; MATTITYAHU 6:33"> And without Emunah it is impossible to please Hashem. For it is necessary for the one making a kiruv approach to Hashem to have Emunah in the fact that yesh Elohim (G-d is there), and that Elohim gives sachar (reward) to those who seek him with zerizut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 6:13–1; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:20; YECHEZKEL 14:14"> By Emunah Noach, having been warned about the things not yet visible to the eye of flesh, and being an ish of yirat Shomayim, built the Teva (Ark) for the Geulah of the Beis HaNoach. By his Emunah he condemned the Olam Hazeh and he became the yoresh (heir) of the Tzedek Hashem that is credited to Emunah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gn 15:6; Hab 2:4">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:1–4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:2–4; MATTITYAHU 7:24–25"> By Emunah Avraham Avinu, when he was called to go out to a place which he was about to receive as a nachalah (inheritance), responded with mishma'at (obedience), and he went out, not having da'as of where he was going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:8; BERESHIS 13:18; BERESHIS 13:3"> By Emunah he made aliyah to HA'ARETZ ASHER DIBER<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Dt 9:28">d</a></sup>(the land that He promised), Ha'aretz haHavtacha (the Promised Land), as in an eretz zarah (a strange land), living in oholim (tents), as did Yitzchak and Ya'akov, the fellow yorshim (heirs) of the same havtachah (promise);</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:20; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:22"> For Avraham Avinu was looking forward to the Shtetl having a yesod Olam (eternally firm foundation), whose Planner and Builder is Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:20–21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:23; BERESHIS 18:11–14"> By Emunah also Sarah, herself barren, received the ability to found a posterity, and she did so even beyond the normal age, als (since) she considered ne'eman (faithful) the One having given the Havtachah (promise);</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 22:17; BERESHIS 32:12; BERESHIS 15:5"> therefore, also, from one man were born<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dt 26:5">e</a></sup>and indeed this man was kimat (practically, as good as) dead many, as numerous as HAKOKHAVIM in HASHOMAYIM and as innumerable as the sand ASHER AL SEFAT (“that is on the seashore”).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gn 15:5; 22:17">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 23:4; TEHILLIM 39:12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:27"> Yet all of these became niftarim (deceased) in Emunah, not having received the havtachot (promises), but having seen them and, as it were, they gave the havtachot a “Baruch Habah!” welcome from a distance, and they made the Ani Ma'amin hoda'ah (confession) that they were GERIM (strangers) and TOSHAVIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gerim…Toshavim see Ps 39:12[13]">g</a></sup>(sojourners) in the Golus of the Olam Hazeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:18–7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:16; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:14"> For those saying such things make it clear that they are searching for an Eretz Moshav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:6–8; BERESHIS 12:10; BERESHIS 32:9–11"> And if they were remembering ha'aretz from which they made aliyah, they would have had an opportunity to make yerida (to descend back, return).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:20"> But, as it is, they aspire for something better, an Eretz HaAvot b'Shomayim; therefore, Hashem is not ashamed to be called Ehoheihem, for he prepared for them an Ir Kodesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 22:16; BERESHIS 22:1–12; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:21–24"> By Emunah Avraham Avinu, when he underwent nisayon, offered up the Akedas HaYitzchak; and he who had received the Havtachot (Promises) was offering as a korban his Ben Yechid.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Yn 3:16">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BERESHIS 21:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:7; BERESHIS 17:19"> This was Avraham Avinu, about whom it was said KI VEYITZCHAK YIKARE LECHA ZERA<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gn 21:12">i</a></sup>(In Yitzchak will be called your seed).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:17–21; MATTITYAHU 9:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:20"> Avraham Avinu considered that Hashem was able to bring about the Techiyas HaMesim, from which he also received back Yitzchak as a tipus (type, pattern).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 27:27–40; BERESHIS 28:2–3"> By Emunah Yitzchak invoked brachot with respect to future events on Ya'akov and Esav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 47:31–1; BERESHIS 48:5–22"> By Emunah Ya'akov, as he was dying, gave a bracha to each of the banim of Yosef and V'YISHTAKHU YISROEL<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gn 47:31">j</a></sup>(And Yisroel worshiped).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHEMOT 13:19; BERESHIS 50:24–25; YEHOSHUA 24:32"> By Emunah Yosef, as he was dying, dermohn (made mention) of the Yetzi'at Bnei Yisroel (the Going Out, the Exodus of Bnei Yisroel) and he gave instructions concerning his ATZMOT<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gn 50:25">k</a></sup>(bones).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHEMOT 1:22; SHEMOT 1:16; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:6"> By Emunah, Moshe Rabbeinu, when he was born, was hidden SHELOSHA CHODESHIM (three months) because they saw he was a yeled TOV<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Ex 2:2">l</a></sup>and they had no pachad (terror) at the kingʼs decree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHEMOT 2:10–11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:21–24"> By Emunah, Moshe Rabbeinu, when he had grown, refused to be called the son of Pharaohʼs daughter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="TEHILLIM 84:10; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:3–10; IYOV 36:21"> Choosing rather to endure redifah (persecution) with the Am Hashem than to enjoy the ephemeral ta'anugot (pleasures) of averos for a season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:35; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:6; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:14"> For Moshe Rabbeinu considered abuse and tzoros for the sake of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach greater osher (riches) than the otzar (treasure) of Mitzrayim (Egypt), for he had respect unto the recompense of the sachar (reward).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:18; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:1"> By Emunah, Moshe Rabbeinu departed from Mitzrayim (Egypt), not having pachad (fear) of the ka'as (anger) of the king, for he persevered as seeing the One who is unseen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:21–30; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:24"> By Emunah, Moshe Rabbeinu kept Pesach and the sprinkling of the dahm &#91;on the mezuzot&#93;,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Ex 12:22">m</a></sup>for fear that the Destroyer of the Bechorim might destroy them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHEMOT 14:13–21; TEHILLIM 106:9–11; TEHILLIM 78:13"> By Emunah, they went through the Yam Suf as on YABASHAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gn 1:10">n</a></sup>(dry ground); and those of Mitzrayim (Egypt), when they attempted it, were drowned.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Mic 7:19">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 6:3–20; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:4–5"> By Emunah, the walls of Yericho fell down, after they had been encircled for shivat yamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:25; YEHOSHUA 6:22–25; MATTITYAHU 1:5"> By Emunah, Rachav the Zonah did not perish along with the ones without mishma'at (obedience), after she gave the kabbalat panim to the spies b'shalom.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Josh 2:9f">p</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:1; SHMUEL ALEF 1:20; SHMUEL ALEF 16:13"> And what more shall I say? For the time will fail me if I tell of Gid'on, Barak, Shimshon, Yiftach, Dovid, Shmuel and the Nevi'im,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="TEHILLIM 144:1–2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:12–15; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:36"> Who by Emunah conquered mamlechot (kingdoms), worked tzedek (righteousness), obtained havtachot (promises), shut the mouths of arayot (lions),</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 6:16–18; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:9–10; SHMUEL ALEF 17:51–52"> Quenched the power of eish (fire), escaped from the edge of the cherev (sword), out of weakness found strength, became mighty in milchamah (war), put to flight tzive'ot zarim (armies of the aliens).</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:27–37; MELACHIM ALEF 17:22–24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:11"> Nashim received back their mesim restored to Chayyim; and others were tortured to death al kiddush ha-Shem, not accepting their release, in order that they might obtain a better Techiyas HaMesim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 20:2; BERESHIS 39:20; YIRMEYAH 37:15–21"> And others underwent the nisayon of cruel mockings and scourgings, also the sharsherot (chains) and the beis hasohar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 24:21; YIRMEYAH 26:23; MELACHIM ALEF 19:10"> They were killed by seqilah (stoning), they were sawn in two, they were murdered by the cherev, they went about in sheepskins, in goatskins, being nitzrach (needy) and destitute, oppressed, under redifot (persecutions),</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:4; SHMUEL ALEF 22:1; MELACHIM ALEF 19:9"> those of whom the Olam Hazeh was not worthy, wandering in deserts and mountains and caves of the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LUKAS 10:23–24; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:2"> And all these, having Hashemʼs commendation through their Emunah, did not receive the havtachah (promise),</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="HISGALUS 6:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:6; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:11–14"> Hashem having foreseen something better for us, so that, apart from us, they should not be made shleimut.</span>
</div>
