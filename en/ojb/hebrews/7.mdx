<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="ojb" data-translation-id="eng_ojb" data-translation-label="OJB" data-book="HEB" data-book-slug="hebrews" data-book-name="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM" data-chapter="7" data-verses="28">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 14:18–20; TEHILLIM 57:2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:20"> For this MALKI-TZEDEK MELECH SHALEM KOHEN L'EL ELYON, is the one having met Avraham Avinu ACHAREI SHUVO (after returning) from the slaughter of “the kings and having blessed him,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gn 14:17-20">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 18:21; TEHILLIM 85:10–11; SHMUEL ALEF 8:17"> With whom also “AVRAHAM avinu apportioned MA'ASER MIKOL (a tithe of everything).” As for his Name, it is rendered “King of Tzedek (Righteousness)” and then also “Melech of SHALOM.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:23–28; SHEMOT 6:20–27"> Without Av, without Em, without Yichus, having neither a techillah (beginning) leyamim (to days) of him nor a Ketz HaChayyim, but resembling the Zun fun der Oybershter, he remains a “kohen perpetually.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 110:4">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 14:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:8–9"> Now consider how great among the Gedolim this one was to whom also Avraham Avinu gave from his booty a ma'aser (tithe).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="VAYIKRA 27:30–33; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 31:4–6; BAMIDBAR 18:21–32"> And the ones of the Bnei Levi have received the Kehunah (Priesthood) and they have a mitzvah to collect the ma'aser (tithe) from the Am Brit, and they have this mitzvah according to the Torah, that is, to collect from their achim, though these also are descended from the loins of Avraham Avinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:3–4; BERESHIS 13:14–17"> But, this man, though not tracing his descent from them, has received ma'aser (tithe) from Avraham Avinu and has given a bracha to the one having the havtachot (promises).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:27; BERESHIS 48:15–20; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:16"> Now it is beyond all argument that the greater gives a bracha to the lesser.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:20; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:6; HISGALUS 1:18"> Notice, in one case, ma'aser are received by mortal men; in the other case, ma'aser are received by one of whom we have solemn eidus (testimony) that hu Chai (he lives!).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:12; BERESHIS 14:20; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:4"> One could even go so far as to say that even Levi, who receives ma'aser, has paid ma'aser through Avraham Avinu,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 35:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:5; BERESHIS 46:26"> For Levi was still in the loins of his ancestor Avraham when Malki-Tzedek met Avraham Avinu.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:9"> Now if shleimut (completeness) had been attainable through the Kehunah of Levi — for under it came the Mattan Torah (giving of the Torah) to the Am Brit — what further need would there have been to speak of another KOHEN arising “according to the order of Malki-Tzedek”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 110:4">c</a></sup>rather than “al divrati Aharon”?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:31–34; YESHAYAH 66:21; YECHEZKEL 16:61"> For when there is a “changing of the guard” of the Kehunah (Priesthood), this behechrach (necessarily) also affects the Torah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 42:4">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:14; BAMIDBAR 17:5"> For the One about whom these things are said belonged to a different shevet (tribe), from which no one has officiated at the Mizbe'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MICHOH 5:2; YESHAYAH 11:1; LUKAS 3:33"> For it is ugeret (easily seen, evident) that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu was descended from Yehudah, and in connection with that shevet (tribe), Moshe Rabbeinu said nothing about kohanim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 110:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:17–21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:3"> And it is even more evident if another Kohen arises resembling Malki-Tzedek,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:20; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:28"> One who became a kohen, not by means of a mitzvat haTorah concerning yichus (lineage), but according to the gevurah of a Chayyei Ein Sof (Endless Life).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TEHILLIM 110:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:6; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:21"> For of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach comes the solemn eidus, ATAH KOHEN L'OLAM AL DIVRATI MALKI-TZEDEK<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ps 110:4">e</a></sup>(You are a kohen forever according to the order of Malki-Tzedek).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:39; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:9"> For, on the one hand, there is an abrogation of an earlier mitzvah because of its weakness and ineffectuality</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:39; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:16; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:9"> — For the Torah brought nothing to shleimut (perfection); on the other hand, there is the mavo (introduction) of a tikvah tovah yoter (a better hope) through which we draw near to Hashem —</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 18:7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:6; TEHILLIM 110:4"> This was attested with a shevu'ah (oath); for others who became kohanim were installed in the office of kehunah without a shevu'ah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="TEHILLIM 110:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:16–18"> But the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach became a kohen with a shevu'ah (oath) through the One saying to Him, Hashem has made an oath and will not change his mind, You are a kohen forever.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ps 110:4">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:25; BERESHIS 44:32; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:6–12"> According to such a shevu'ah of Hashem, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua has become the orev (surety, guarantee) of a tovah yoter HaBrit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 12:10–11; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:3–14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:8"> Not only this, but it was the fact that the former kohanim were many in number, because mavet prevented them from continuing in the office of kehunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:34; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:28; YESHAYAH 9:6–7"> But because Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach continues l'olam (forever), he has an unchangeable Kehunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:34; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1–2; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:5"> From which also he is able to completely deliver to the Geulah (Redemption) and Yeshu'at Eloheinu the ones approaching Hashem through him, als (since) he has Chayyei Ein Sof (Endless Life) and always lives to intercede in techinnah (supplication) for them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:14–15; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:22"> For such was for us, indeed, a bekavod (suitable) Kohen Gadol, chasid, tamim, tahor, nivdal from chote'im (separated from sinners) and exalted above HaShomayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:28; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:14"> A Kohen Gadol who does not have daily need — as do the other Kohanim Gedolim — on the one hand, to offer up zevakhim for his own averos, and then to offer up zevakhim for the averos of the Am Brit. For this Kohen Gadol offered up himself,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 53:10">g</a></sup>once and for all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:2"> For the Torah of Moshe Rabbeinu appoints Bnei Adam as Kohanim Gedolim, Bnei Adam with frailties, but the Dvar HaShevu'ah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ps 110:4">h</a></sup>which came later than the Torah of Moshe Rabbeinu, appoints HaBen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Zun fun der Oybershter Moshiach">i</a></sup>who came to shleimut (completeness) l'Olam.</span>
</div>
</div>
