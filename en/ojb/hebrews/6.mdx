<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:12–14; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:1; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:14"> Therefore, let us leave behind the stam (elementary) ikarim (essentials) of the devarim hahora'ah (words of teaching) about Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, and let us move ahead to hitbagrut (maturity), not laying again a foundation of teshuva from ma'asim metim (dead works) and Emunah toward Hashem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:13–57; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:31–32; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:3–5"> Of divrei torah on tevilot and tohorah and s'michat yadayim and of the Techiyas HaMesim and of the Mishpat Olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:21; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:19"> And this we shall do, im yirtzeh Hashem(if the L-rd wills).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:26–29; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20–22; MATTITYAHU 5:13"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In this and the following verses, warning is given to the Messianic Jew who re-submits Yehoshua to re-trial and reassigns the original verdict of deceiver: no re-rebirth, no re-repentance for such shmad">a</a></sup>For it is impossible for those who once received the Ohr Hashem, having tasted of the matanah of Shomayim and having become chavrusa partners of the Ruach Hakodesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:3; LUKAS 8:13; MATTITYAHU 13:20–21"> And having tasted the goodness of the Dvar Hashem and the nifla'ot of the Olam Habah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:29; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:4"> and then, having fallen away, and become shmad — it is impossible to renew them again to teshuva, because they have pierced to themselves the Zun fun der Oybershter on HaEtz HaKelalat Hashem and have again held him up to contempt and open bushah (shame).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HOSHEA 10:12; YESHAYAH 44:3; TEHILLIM 65:9–13"> For the Adamah (Mud), which drinks the geshem (rain) that often falls upon it, and brings forth ESEV<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gn 1:11">b</a></sup>(plants) suitable for those for whom it is cultivated, receives a bracha from Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 3:17–18; YOCHANAN 15:6; LUKAS 13:7–9"> But if Adamah (Mud) is producing KOTZ V'DARDAR<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gn 3:18">c</a></sup>(thorns and thistles), it is worthless and near to being arurah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gn 3:17">d</a></sup>(cursed), whose Ketz (End) is for burning in Eish.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="T.N. the allusion is to Eish Gehinnom">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:39; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:4–6"> However, Chaverim, even though we speak in this way, we have been convinced of better things concerning you and of things belonging to Yeshu'at Eloheinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:42; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:10"> For Hashem is not unjust so as to forget your po'al (work) and the ahavah which you have shown toward ha-Shem of him, having rendered avodas kodesh ministry service to the Kedoshim, ken, and you are still serving them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5–8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:2–5"> And we desire that each one of you show the same zerizut (diligence) so as to realize the full bitachon of the tikvah (hope) until HaKetz,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:36; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:25"> That you not become atzlanit (sluggards) but imitators of the ones who through Emunah and savlanut (patience) inherit the havtachot (promises).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 22:15–18; LUKAS 1:73; YIRMEYAH 22:5"> For when Hashem gave the havtachah (promise) to Avraham Avinu, als (since)Hashem had no one greater by which to make a shevu'ah (oath), Hashem made a shevu'ah by Himself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gn 22:16">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 22:17; DEVARIM 1:10; NECHEMYAH 9:23"> Saying, “Surely blessing I will bless you and multiplying I will multiply you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gn 22:17">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:2–3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:17–25; SHEMOT 1:7"> And thus, having waited with savlanut, Avraham Avinu obtained the havtachah (promise).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:13; BERESHIS 31:53"> For Bnei Adam make a shevu'ah by someone greater than themselves, and a shevu'ah given as confirmation, settles every matter decisively.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:18; MISHLE 19:21; YESHAYAH 55:11"> Similarly, when Hashem wanted to demonstrate even more emphatically to the yoreshim (heirs) of the havtachah (promise) the unchangeableness of his willed tachlis (purpose), Hashem guaranteed it with a shevu'ah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 23:19; LETTER TO TITOS 1:2; SHMUEL ALEF 15:29"> In order that by two unchangeable things in which it is impossible for Hashem to speak sheker, we may have chozek (strength) and great encouragement, we who say that “he is my MAKHSEH”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ps 91:2">h</a></sup>(refuge, shelter), and have taken hold of the tikvah (hope) set before us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="TEHILLIM 43:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:5–10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:7"> This tikvah we have as an ogen (anchor) for the neshamah, a tikvah both firm and secure, which enters inside the parokhet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:1; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:6"> Where Yehoshua has entered as a metatron (forerunner) on behalf of us, having become a KOHEN L'OLAM AL DIVRATI MALKI-TZEDEK<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ps 110:4">i</a></sup>(Kohen forever according to the order of Malki-Tzedek).</span>
