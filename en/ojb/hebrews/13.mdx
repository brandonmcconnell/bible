<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:7–8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:13"> We have a Mizbe'ach from which those who serve the Mishkan have no right to eat.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:12–14; YOCHANAN 19:17–18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:58"> Therefore, Yehoshua also, that he might make the Am Brit kedoshim through his own dahm, suffered outside the sha'ar.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:1–3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:12–16"> For we do not have here a lasting Ir HaKodesh, but we seek one to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 107:21–22; TEHILLIM 50:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:1"> By him, therefore, let us offer up a zevach todah to Hashem continually, that is, the fruit of our lips, giving hoda'ah (confession) to SHMO (HIS NAME).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Zech 6:11-12; 3:8">c</a></sup></span>
