<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 8:15; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12–13; MISHLE 7:1–2"> In considering all this, it is necessary for us to pay far greater attention bifraht (particularly) to the things we have heard, lest from it we may drift away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:53; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:28"> For if the Dvar Hashem which was declared through malachim became firmly established and every peysha (transgression) and averah (disobedience to the commandment) received a gemul tzodek (just retribution),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Dt 33:2, TARGUM HASHIVIM">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:28–29; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:25; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:9"> How shall we escape if we neglect so great a Yeshu'at Eloheinu, one that was declared initially through<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">b</a></sup>Adoneinu, and was confirmed and attested to us by those who heard him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:5; MARKOS 16:20"> While Hashem gave solemn eidus (testimony) through otot u'moftim (signs and wonders) and nifla'ot rabbot (many miracles) and matanot (gifts) of the Ruach Hakodesh distributed according to the ratzon Hashem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HISGALUS 11:15; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:5"> For Hashem did not subject the Olam Habah, about which we are speaking, to malachim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 8:4–8; TEHILLIM 144:3; IYOV 7:17–18"> But someone has given solemn eidus (testimony) somewhere, MAH ENOSH KI TIZKERENU UVEN ADAM KI TIFKEDENU? (What is Man that You are mindful of him, or the Son of Man that You care for him?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 8:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:6–7"> You made him a lttle lower than the angels and you crowned him with glory and splendor and you made him ruler over the works of your hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:27; TEHILLIM 8:6"> KOL SHATAH TACHAT RAGLAV (Putting everything under his feet).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 8:5-7">c</a></sup>Now while Hashem subjected all things to him, he left nothing unsubjected to him, though now we do not yet see all things having been subjected to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:7–9; HISGALUS 5:9; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:9–10"> But this is what we do see: Yehoshua, for a short time having been “made lower than the angels,” has, because of the yissurim (suffering) of mavet (death), been “crowned with KAVOD V'HADAR” (glory and splendor)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ps 8:6">d</a></sup>in order that by the Chen v'Chesed Hashem on behalf of all he might taste the histalkus of mavet.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Isa 53:8">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:36; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:31; LUKAS 24:46"> For it was bekavod (fitting, proper) for him, for whom are all things and through whom are all things, in bringing banim rabbim (many sons) to kavod, to bring to shleimut (perfection, completion) the Rosh (Head) and Mekhonen (Founder) of their Yeshu'at Hashem through yissurim (suffering).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:14"> For both HaKadosh who makes holy and Kedoshim who are being made holy all have HaAv Echad. It is for this reason Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach is not ashamed to call them Achim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="TEHILLIM 22:22; TEHILLIM 40:10; YOCHANAN 18:20"> Saying, “I will declare your Name to my brothers, within the congregation I will praise you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ps 22:22[23]">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:17–18; YESHAYAH 12:2; TEHILLIM 91:2"> And again, “I put my bitachon in him”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 12:2">g</a></sup>and again HINEI ANOCHI V'HAYELADIM ASHER NATAN LI HASHEM (Here I am and the yeladim whom Hashem gave to me.)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isa 8:18">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:15"> Therefore, als (since) the yeladim share in the basar vadahm and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach likewise shared in the same things, that through the histalkus of his mavet he might destroy the one having power over mavet, that is, HaSatan,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="See Genesis chapter 3">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:15; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:7; LUKAS 1:74–75"> And he might release these, as many as through eymat haMavet (terror of Death) were subjected to avdut (slavery) all the days of their existence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:16–25"> For surely it was not malachim that concerned him, but the zera Avraham Avinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:14–2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:7–8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:14"> And for this reason, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was obliged to become like the Achim b'Moshiach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="2:11">j</a></sup>in every respect, that he might become a Kohen Gadol rachaman v'ne'eman before Hashem in order to make kapporah for the chatta'im of HaAm.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Isa 53:8; 49:7">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:15–16; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:24"> For, because Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach himself has endured, being tested in the yissurim (sufferings) of his nefesh,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Isa 53:10,11">l</a></sup>he is able to come to the ezrah (aid) of the ones being tested.</span>
