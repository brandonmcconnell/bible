<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:14–15"> For this reason, Achai Hakedoshim b'Moshiach, Chaverim and Chavrusa partners in a Kri'at Marom (High &#91;Himel&#93; Calling), consider carefully the Shliach and Kohen Gadol of the Hachrazah (Proclamation) of our Emunah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Faith, our Orthodox Jewish Ani Ma'amin Body of Emunah, Yd 1:3">a</a></sup>Yehoshua, Yeshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 12:7; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:22"> He being ne'eman (faithful) to the One having given him s'michah as also Moshe Rabbeinu was ne'eman (faithful) in kol Beis Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:9; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5–7; ZECHARYAH 6:12–13"> Yet Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach is considered worthy of more kavod than Moshe Rabbeinu, because more kavod has the Boneh (Builder) of the Beis than the Beis itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:2; ESTHER 2:10"> For every Beis is built by someone, but the One having built everything is Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 12:7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:2; SHEMOT 14:31"> Now Moshe Rabbeinu was ne'eman in kol Beis Hashem as an eved, for a solemn edut of the things which were to be spoken afterward.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., Moshiachʼs torah coming later">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:16; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:35"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was ne'eman as HaBen over the Beis Hashem, whose Beis we are, if indeed the bitachon and the tikvah in which we glory we keep hold of to HaKetz.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 95:7–11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:15; YESHAYAH 55:3"> Therefore, just as the Ruach Hakodesh says, HAYOM IM BEKOLO TISHMAU (Today, if you hear His voice);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 95:7f">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MISHLE 28:14; SHEMOT 17:7; ZECHARYAH 7:11–12"> AL TAKSHU LEVAVCHEM KIMRIVAH K'YOM MASSAH BAMIDBAR (Do not harden your heart as you did at Meribah, as you did that day at Massah in the desert),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:36; DEVARIM 4:9; SHEMOT 19:4"> ASHER NISSUNI AVOTEICHEM BEKHANUNI GAM RA'U PO'OLI ARBA'IM SHANAH (Where your Fathers tested me and tried me though they saw my deeds forty years).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 95:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:10–12; TEHILLIM 67:2"> Therefore AKUT BEDOR VAOMAR AM TO'EY LEVAV HEM V'HEM LO YAD'U DERAKHAI (I was angry with that generation and I said they are a straying-of-heart people and they have not had da'as of my ways);</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:34–35; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:3; BAMIDBAR 32:10–13"> ASHER NISHBA'ETI V'APEY IM YEVO'UN EL MENUKHATI (Therefore I declared on oath in my anger, Never shall they enter into my Rest, Home, Abode, Place of Tranquility.)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gn 49:15; Ps 23; Isa 28:12; 66:1; Ruth 1:9; 1Kgs 8:56">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 17:5; YIRMEYAH 7:24; YIRMEYAH 17:9"> Beware, Achim b'Moshiach, lest there will be in any one of you a lev rah (evil heart) without Emunah, that turns away shmad from Elohim Chayyim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:24–25; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:2"> Instead, give one another chozek (strength) each and every day, as long as it is still called “HAYOM,” (today)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ps 95:7">e</a></sup>lest some of you may fall into KESHI (stubbornness, hardness)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Dt 9:27">f</a></sup>and be stubbornly hardened by the nechalim (deceitfulness) of Chet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:6; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:10"> We have become chavrusa partners of Moshiach if only our bitachon (confidence) we had initially we hold firm until HaKetz;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:7–8; TEHILLIM 95:7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:38"> As it is said, HAYOM IM BEKOLO TISHMAU AL TAKSHU LEVAVCHEM (Today, if you hear his voice, do not harden your heart) as in the Meribah Mered (Rebellion).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="the Ma'al shmad Defection, the Azivah Desertion, see 2Th 2:3">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:2; BAMIDBAR 14:30; BAMIDBAR 26:65"> Now who were they who heard and yet rebelled? Was it not all the ones having had litzi'at Mitzrayim (going out from Egypt) under Moshe Rabbeinu?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:29; BAMIDBAR 26:64–65; BAMIDBAR 14:32–33"> And with whom was Hashem angry ARBA'IM SHANAH? Was it not with the ones having sinned, whose “PEGARIM” (corpses) lay where they had “fallen BAMIDBAR” (in the desert)?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Num 14:29">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:30; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:6; DEVARIM 1:34–35"> And to whom did He swear that they would not enter into the menuchah (resting place) of Him? Was it not to the ones without mishma'at (obedience)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5; YOCHANAN 3:36; MARKOS 16:16"> And so we see that they were not able to enter because of lack of Emunah.</span>
