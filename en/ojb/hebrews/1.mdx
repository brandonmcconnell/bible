<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:20–21; YOEL 2:28; BAMIDBAR 12:6–8"> In many and various drakhim (ways) Hashem in amolike times (olden times) spoke to the Avot by the Nevi'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:16–18"> At the Ketz HaYamim, Hashem spoke to us by HaBen, whom He appointed Bechor of the Bechorah, Yoresh Kol (Heir of All Things), through whom also Hashem BARAH ES HASHOMAYIM V'ES HA'ARETZ;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="see Prov 30:4">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:15–17; HISGALUS 4:11; YOCHANAN 14:9–10"> Who being the Shekhinah zohar (brilliance) of Hashem and the exact impress and demut of Hashemʼs essential nature, being, and reality, and sustaining everything by his Dvar HaKo'ach, after he made tihur (purification) of chatta'im (sins), sat down at LIMIN (the right hand)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 110:1">b</a></sup>of the Majesty on High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:9–11; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:22"> He had become as much superior to the malachim as ha-Shem (the Name) Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach has inherited is more fest (excellent) than theirs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 2:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:33; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:5"> For to which of the malachim did Hashem ever say, BENI ATAH, ANI HAYOM YELIDTICHA (My Son you are; today I have become your Father)?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 2:7">c</a></sup>And again, ANI EH'H'YEH LO L'AV V'HU YIHEYEH LI L'BEN (I will be to Him a Father and He will be to Me as a Son)?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2Sm 7:14">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 97:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:18; DEVARIM 32:43"> And again, when Hashem brings HaBechor into the Olam Hazeh, He says, “Let all Hashemʼs malachim worship him.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dt 32:43TARGUM HA-SHIVIM">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 104:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:14; MELACHIM BAIS 6:17"> And of the malachim, Hashem says OSEH MALAKHAV RUCHOT MESHARTAV EISH L'HET (He makes his angels winds and his servants &#91;i.e., ministering angels&#93; flaming fire).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ps 104:4">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 45:6–7; YESHAYAH 9:6–7; YIRMEYAH 23:5–6"> And Hashem says to HaBen, KIS'AHCHA ELOHIM OLAM VAED SHEVET MISHOR SHEVET MALKHUTECHA (Your throne, O G-d, will endure for ever and ever, and the scepter of justice is the scepter of your kingdom).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ps 45:7">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 45:7; TEHILLIM 23:5; YESHAYAH 61:3"> AHAVTA TZEDEK VATISNA RE'SHA, AL KEN MESHAKHACHA ELOHIM, ELOHEICHA SHEMEN SASSON MECHAVERECHA (You loved righteousness and hated lawlessness; on account of this G-d, your G-d, anointed you with the oil of gladness more than your companions).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ps 45:8">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 102:25–27; BERESHIS 1:1; YOCHANAN 1:1–3"> And, Atah Adonoi LEFANIM HA'ARETZ YASADETA UMA'ASEH YADECHA SHOMAYIM (You L-rd in the beginning founded the earth and the heavens, the work of your hands);</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 51:6; MARKOS 13:31; LUKAS 21:33"> HEMMAH YOVEDU V'ATAH TA'AMOD V'KHULAM KABEGED YIVLU (They will perish but you remain and all of them like the garment they will wear out),</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="TEHILLIM 102:26–27; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:8; YOCHANAN 8:58"> KALVUSH TACHALIFEM V'YACHALOFU V'ATAH HU USHENOTECHA LO YITTAMMU (Like clothing you will change them and they will be discarded, but you are the same and the years of you they never come to an end).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ps 102:25-27; [26-28]">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TEHILLIM 110:1; MARKOS 12:36; MATTITYAHU 22:44"> And to which of the malachim has Hashem ever said, Sit at my right hand until I put your enemies as a footstool for your feet?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ps 110:1">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 34:7; TEHILLIM 103:20–21; TEHILLIM 91:11–12"> Are not all RUCHOT<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ps 104:4">k</a></sup>sharet (ministering spirits, malachey hasharet) sent out with the shlichus (mission) to do avodas kodesh service as Hashemʼs klei kodesh (ministers) on behalf of the ones being about to inherit Yeshu'at Eloheinu?</span>
</div>
