<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="AMOS 8:5; YECHEZKEL 44:10–11"> And I said, What is it? And he said, This is the eifah (measuring basket) going out. <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 23:1–49; HISGALUS 17:1–18; YIRMEYAH 3:1–2"> And, hinei, there was lifted up a lead cover; and there was an isha (woman) yoshevet (sitting) inside the eifah (measuring basket)!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:16; AMOS 9:1–4; MISHLE 5:22"> And he said, This is HaRishah (The Wickedness). And he pushed her inside the eifah; and he pushed the lead cover over the mouth of the eifah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:49; HOSHEA 8:1; DANIEL 9:26–27"> Then lifted I up mine eyes, and looked, and, hinei, there came forth two nashim (women), and the ruach (wind) was in their kenafayim (wings); for they had kenafayim (wings) like the kenafayim of a stork: and they lifted up the eifah between ha'aretz and HaShomayim.</span>
