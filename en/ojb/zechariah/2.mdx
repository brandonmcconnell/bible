<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:39; YECHEZKEL 45:6; HISGALUS 21:15–17"> And I said unto the malach that spoke with me, What are these? And he answered me, These are the keranot which have scattered Yehudah, Yisroel, and Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 1:19; ZECHARYAH 1:8–11; ZECHARYAH 4:5"> And Hashem showed me arba'ah charashim (four craftsmen).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 1:17; YIRMEYAH 33:22; YECHEZKEL 38:11"> Then said I, What come these to do? <span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YESHAYAH 4:5; ZECHARYAH 9:8; TEHILLIM 3:3"> I lifted up mine eyes again, and looked, and, hinei, a man with a measuring line in his hand!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YESHAYAH 48:20; YECHEZKEL 17:21; YESHAYAH 52:11–12"> Then said I: Whither goest thou? And he said unto me: To measure Yerushalayim, to see what is the breadth thereof, and what is the length thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YESHAYAH 48:20; BERESHIS 19:17; YESHAYAH 52:11"> And, hinei, the malach that spoke with me went forth, and another malach went out to meet him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 17:8; DEVARIM 32:10; YOCHANAN 15:21–23"> And said unto him: Run, speak to this na'ar (young man)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e. see 2:5">b</a></sup>saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yerushalayim shall be inhabited like unwalled cities</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because of the multitude of men and cattle therein;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YESHAYAH 19:16; YESHAYAH 14:2; ZECHARYAH 4:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I, saith Hashem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will be unto her a chomat eish (wall of fire) round about,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and will be the kavod in the midst of her.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:20; YOCHANAN 1:14; TZEFANYAH 3:14–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Come, come, and flee</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">out of the Eretz Tzafon, saith Hashem;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for I have scattered you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like the Arbah Ruchot HaShomayim, saith Hashem.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HISGALUS 11:15; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9–10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Come, save thyself, O Tziyon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">thou that dwellest with the Bat Bavel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 1:17; DEVARIM 32:9; YIRMEYAH 10:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">For thus saith Hashem Tzva'os:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">After Kavod (Glory) sent me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">unto the Goyim who plundered you —</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the one that toucheth you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">toucheth the apple of His eye —</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="CHABAKUK 2:20; TEHILLIM 68:5; TEHILLIM 78:65"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hineni, I will surely raise Mine hand against them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they shall be plunder to their avadim;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and ye shall know that Hashem Tzva'os hath sent me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sing and rejoice, O Bat Tziyon;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for, hineni, I will come,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will dwell in the midst of thee, saith Hashem.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">And Goyim rabbim shall be joined to Hashem in Yom HaHu (that day),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and shall be Ami (My People):</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will dwell in the midst of thee,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and thou shalt know that Hashem Tzva'os hath sent me unto thee.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">And Hashem shall inherit Yehudah as His chelek</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the Admat HaKodesh (Holy Land),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and shall choose Yerushalayim again.</span></span>
