<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YESHAYAH 2:12–17; YECHEZKEL 31:17; YESHAYAH 10:33–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wail, O cypress, for the cedar is fallen;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the mighty is despoiled;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">wail, O ye oaks of Bashan;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the dense forest is toppled.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 50:44; YIRMEYAH 25:34–36; ZECHARYAH 11:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear the sound of the wailing of the ro'im (shepherds);</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for their glory is destroyed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the sound of the roaring of young lions;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the ga'on of the Yarden (Jordan) is laid waste.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 11:7; MATTITYAHU 23:37; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:8"> Thus Hashem Elohai said: Shepherd the tzon haharegah (the flock marked for slaughter);</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 50:7; HOSHEA 12:8; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:5–10"> Whose buyers slaughter them, and hold themselves not guilty; and they that sell them say, Baruch Hashem, I have become rich! And their own ro'im (shepherds) spare them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 13:14; MATTITYAHU 22:7; YESHAYAH 9:19–21"> Nor will I any more pity the yoshvei ha'aretz (the inhabitants of the land), saith Hashem; and hinei, I will deliver the men every one into his neighborʼs hand, and into the power of his melech; and they shall strike ha'aretz, and out of their hand I will not deliver them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 11:14; ZECHARYAH 11:4; YESHAYAH 11:4"> So I did shepherd the tzon haharegah (the flock marked for slaughter), even aniyei hatzon (the poor of the flock). And I took unto me two maklot (staffs); the one I called No'am (gracious care), and the other I called Chovelim (bindings);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="of union, cf.11:14">a</a></sup>and I did shepherd the tzon (flock).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YESHAYAH 49:7; MATTITYAHU 23:34–36; YOCHANAN 15:18"> Shloshet haro'im (three of the shepherds) also I disposed of in one month; and my nefesh wearied of them, and their nefesh also abhorred me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 43:11; MATTITYAHU 13:10–11; MATTITYAHU 21:19"> Then said I, I will not shepherd you; that which dieth, let it die; and that which is to be cut off, let it be cut off; and let those that are left eat every one the basar of another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 11:7; TEHILLIM 89:39; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:8–13"> And I took my staff, even No'am, and I broke it asunder, breaking off my Brit (covenant) which I had made with kol ha'amim (all the peoples).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TZEFANYAH 3:12; LUKAS 2:38; DEVARIM 31:21"> And it was broken on that day; and so the aniyei hatzon (poor of the flock), who were the shomrim giving heed to me, perceived that this was the Devar Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:15; SHEMOT 21:32; LUKAS 22:3–6"> And I said unto them, If ye think tov, give me my sachar (price); and if not, refrain. So they weighed out for my sachar (price) shloshim kesef (thirty pieces of silver).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:3–10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:18–19; YESHAYAH 53:2–3"> And Hashem said unto me, Throw it unto the yotzer (potter); the magnificent price that I was priced of them. So I took the shloshim hakesef, and threw them to the yotzer (potter) in the Beis Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ex 21:32; Mt 26:14-15; 27:3-10; Ac 1:18-19">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YESHAYAH 9:21; YESHAYAH 11:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:7–10"> Then I broke my second staff, called Chovelim, breaking off the achavah (brotherhood) between Yehudah and Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YESHAYAH 6:10–12; LUKAS 11:40; YIRMEYAH 2:26–27"> And Hashem said unto me, Take unto thee yet the keli (implements) of a foolish ro'eh (shepherd).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 34:2–6; YIRMEYAH 23:2; MATTITYAHU 23:2–4"> For, hinei, I will raise up a ro'eh in ha'aretz, which shall not care for the dying, nor look for the na'ar (young one), nor heal that which is injured, nor feed the healthy; but he shall eat the basar of the choice, and tear off their hooves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 23:1; YECHEZKEL 13:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hoy (woe) to the worthless ro'eh (shepherd)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that deserteth the tzon!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May the cherev (sword) be upon his zero'a (arm),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and upon his right eye;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">let his zero'a wither away,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and his right eye be utterly extinguished.</span></span>
</div>
