<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:11; YOCHANAN 1:29; TEHILLIM 51:2"> In Yom HaHu there shall be a makor (fountain)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Ps 36:9[10]">a</a></sup>opened to the Bais Dovid and to the inhabitants of Yerushalayim for sin and for niddah (impurity).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., used figuratively but refers especially to untouchability of women during menstruation and other kinds of uncleanness">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:22; YIRMEYAH 23:14–15; HOSHEA 2:17"> And in Yom HaHu it shall come to pass, saith Hashem Tzva'os, that I will cut off the shmot (names) of the atzabim (idols) out of ha'aretz, and they shall no more be remembered; and also I will cause the nevi'im and the ruach hatum'ah (unclean spirit) to pass from ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEVARIM 13:6–11; DEVARIM 18:20; YECHEZKEL 14:9"> And it shall come to pass, that if any shall still prophesy, then his av and his em that bore him shall say unto him, Thou shalt not live; for thou speakest sheker b'Shem Hashem; and his av and his em that bore him shall thrust him through<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="dakar, 12:10">c</a></sup>when he prophesieth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MICHOH 3:6–7; MATTITYAHU 3:4; HISGALUS 11:3"> And in Yom HaHu it shall come to pass, that the nevi'im shall be ashamed, every one of his chizzayon (vision, revelation), when he hath prophesied; neither shall they wear an adderet se'ar (garment of hair, hairy mantle) to deceive;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="AMOS 7:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:17–20"> But he shall say, I am no navi, I am an ish oved adamah (a man who works the soil); for land I owned from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:35; TEHILLIM 22:16; MISHLE 27:5–6"> And if one shall say unto him, What are these makkot (strokes, marks of strokes, scars, wounds) between thine hands? Then he shall answer, Those with which I was wounded in the bais of my friends.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MARKOS 14:27; MATTITYAHU 26:31; YOCHANAN 16:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Awake, O Cherev (sword), against My Ro'eh,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="shepherd, i.e. Moshiach, see Isa 40:11; Ezek 34:23,24; 37:24">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">against the Gever (man) that is My Amit (fellow, intimate companion, associate),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">saith Hashem Tzva'os;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">strike the Ro'eh (shepherd), and the Tzon (sheep, flock) shall be scattered;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will turn Mine hand upon the tzo'arim (little ones);</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Zech 11:7; Mt 26:31,56">e</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 5:12; HISGALUS 16:19; YECHEZKEL 5:2–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">And it shall come to pass, that in kol ha'aretz, saith Hashem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">two-thirds therein shall be cut off and die;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but one-third shall remain therein.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 50:15; YIRMEYAH 29:11–12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will bring the one-third through the eish,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and will refine them as kesef is refined,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and will test them as zahav is tested;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they shall call biShmi (on My Name), and I will hear them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will say: This is My people;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they shall say: Hashem is Elohai.</span></span>
