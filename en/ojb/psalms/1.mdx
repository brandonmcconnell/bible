## TEHILLIM

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="TEHILLIM 26:4–5; MISHLE 4:14–15; MISHLE 13:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessed is the ish that walketh not</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the Etzah (counsel, scheme) of the Resha'im,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor standeth in the Derech Chatta'im,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor sitteth in the Moshav (seat) of the Leitzim (scornful, ones mocking and reviling).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 1:8; TEHILLIM 119:92; TEHILLIM 119:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">But his delight is in the Torat Hashem;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and in His torah doth he meditate yomam v'lailah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 17:8; YECHEZKEL 47:12; TEHILLIM 92:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he shall be like an etz planted by the streams of mayim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that bringeth forth its p'ri in its season;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the leaf thereof also shall not wither;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and whatsoever he doeth shall prosper.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="IYOV 21:18; MATTITYAHU 3:12; TEHILLIM 35:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Resha'im are not so,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but are like the chaff which the ruach driveth away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 5:5; MATTITYAHU 25:46; MALACHI 3:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore the Resha'im shall not stand in the Mishpat,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor Chatta'im (sinners) in the Adat Tzaddikim.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:19; YOCHANAN 10:14; MISHLE 15:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">For Hashem knoweth the Derech Tzaddikim, but the Derech Resha'im shall perish.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The next Psalm says that the whole world, all the earth, is, or will be, the possession of Moshiach, and that Hashem assures Moshiach of this; therefore, the Great Commission (Mt 28:19-20) was given to preach Moshiach to the ends of the earth that the whole world may hear.">a</a></sup></span>
