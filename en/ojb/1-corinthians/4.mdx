<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:21; LUKAS 12:42; LUKAS 16:10–12"> Moreover, it is sought in mefakkechim that one be found that has ne'emanut (faithfulness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:15; SHMUEL ALEF 16:7"> But to me it is a very small thing that I be brought, as it were, before your Bet Din for you to play dayanim (religious judges) judging me, or that I am judged by Bnei Adam on their merely human Yom HaDin; I do not even act as Dayan (Judge of a Rabbinical Court) of myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="TEHILLIM 143:2; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:20–21; TEHILLIM 130:3"> I am aware of nothing against myself, but not in this have I been yitzdak (justified); it is the L-rd who is my Shofet (Judge).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gn 18:25; Dan 7:13-14">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:7; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:13"> Therefore, do not judge anything before the time, until the Bias Adoneinu,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Moshiach">b</a></sup>who both will bring to Ohr (Light) the hidden things of the choshech (darkness) and manifest the motives of the levavot. And then the tehillah (praise) each one will receive will be from Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Job 12:22; Ps 90:8">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:6; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:4; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:19"> Now these things, Achim b'Moshiach, I made a dimyon (comparison) applied with respect to myself and Apollos for your sake, that through us you may learn not to go beyond what things have been written,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2:13">d</a></sup>lest you are puffed up as ba'alei ga'avah (conceited, haughty persons) in favor of one or against the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:27; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:17"> For who makes you so distinguished? And, by the way, what do you have which you did not receive? And if indeed you were given it, why this ga'avah, this boastfulness as if you had not received it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKAS 6:25; MISHLE 13:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:6"> Already you have so much, already you ascended to osher (riches) and without us &#91;Shlichim&#93;! You became melechim (kings); I would that you did indeed become melechim that also we might reign as melechim with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:33; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:36; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:8–12"> For, omein, I believe that Hashem has exhibited us, the Shlichim of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, as last in the program, condemned to death, because, like wretches under a mishpat mavet (death sentence), we became displayed in the arena for the eyes of the Olam Hazeh, malachim as well as Bnei Adam.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ps 71:7">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:18"> We are kesilim (fools) because of Moshiach, but you are chachamim in Moshiach; we are weak, but you are strong; you are treated with honor; we dishonor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:35; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:23–27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:12"> Until the present sha'ah (hour) we both hunger, and thirst, and are naked, and are beaten and homeless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; MATTITYAHU 5:11; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:12–14"> And we have parnasah, toiling with our own hands; being reviled, we make a bracha; being persecuted, we endure it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EKHAH 3:45; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:22"> Being defamed by loshon hora, we conciliate. We have become what the earth wants swept out the door, something considered trash the Olam Hazeh wants removed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Jer 20:18; Lam 3:45">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:15; (III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:4"> The purpose of this iggeret is not to bring you under bushah (shame). I write these things as admonishing my beloved yeladim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:19; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:14"> For, though you may have in Moshiach morei derech numbering ten thousand, you have not many avot, for in Moshiach through the Besuras HaGeulah I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="T.N. Notice 1C 4:6indicates the canon of inerrant Scripture is closed and may not be added to.">g</a></sup>became your abba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:1; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:17"> Therefore, I encourage you, imitate me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:17"> Because of this very thing, I sent Timotiyos to you who is my beni haahuv (beloved son) and ne'eman (faithful), trustworthy in Hashem, who will remind you of my derech baKodesh in Moshiach, even as I give shiurim everywhere in every kehillah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:6–8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:21"> Now as to my coming to you, some were puffed up,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Jer 43:2">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:1–4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:21"> But I will come shortly to you, im yirtzeh Hashem(if the L-rd wills), and I will find out not the speech of the ones having been puffed up but the ko'ach (power).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:5; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:4–5"> For the Malchut Hashem depends not on the talk &#91;of the ish sefatayim (the eloquent speaker)&#93; but on ko'ach (power).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:2; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:7"> What do you want? That I should come to you, so to speak, with an abbaʼs switch or in ahavah and an anavat ruach (a spirit of meekness)?</span>
