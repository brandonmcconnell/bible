<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:15–17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:38; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:33"> Does anyone of you having a dispute with an Ach b'Moshiach dare to be judged before the resha'im (unrighteous, evildoers) and not before the Bet Din of the Kedoshim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:28; DANIEL 7:22; HISGALUS 20:4"> Or do you not have da'as that the Kedoshim will sit in mishpat over the Olam Hazeh? And if the Olam Hazeh is to be judged by you, are you incompetent dayanim to try the smallest cases?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:6; MATTITYAHU 25:41; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:4"> Do you lack da'as that the malachim will come before our Bet Din? Not to mention the things of Olam Hazeh!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:2–4"> If, then, you have cases concerning matters of the Olam Hazeh, how could you appoint as your Bet Din dayanim men who have no standing in the kehillah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:34; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:14; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:13–18"> I speak to your bushah (shame). Is there not among you even one chacham (wise man) who will be able to sit in mishpat between his Achim b'Moshiach?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:14–15; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9–11; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:11–15"> But an Ach b'Moshiach takes another Ach b'Moshiach to court, and this before the courtroom of Apikorosim (Unbelievers)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:17–19; MISHLE 20:22; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:19–23"> Already, therefore, it is a total defeat for you, that you have lawsuits with one another. Why not rather suffer wrong? Why not rather be cheated?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:25; MARKOS 10:19"> But you yourselves do wrong and practice hona'ah (cheating), and this to your Achim b'Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:9–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:19–21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:4–5"> Or do you not have da'as that the resha'im (unrighteous ones) will not inherit the Malchut Hashem? Do not be deceived! Neither zannayim (fornicators), nor ovdei elilim (idolaters), nor mena'afim (adulterers), nor effeminate call boys nor homosexuals,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Job 13:9; Lv 18:20; Dt 22:22; Lv 18:22">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:21; YECHEZKEL 22:29"> Nor ganavim (thieves), nor kamtzanim (misers), nor shikkorim (drunkards), nor megadefim (revilers), nor the ones doing hona'ah (swindling) — none of these will inherit the Malchut Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:30; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:5–7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:2"> And some of you were these things. But you were washed and made tehorim (clean), you were made to be Am Kadosh, you were made to be yitzdak im Hashem in the name of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua Adoneinu and in the Ruach Hakodesh of Eloheinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:23–33; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:27; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:15–16"> “All things to me are proper!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1C 10:23">b</a></sup>But not all things are beneficial. “All things to me are proper!” But I will not be mastered by anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:19; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:12"> “Okhel (food) for the stomach and the stomach for okhel, but Hashem will destroy both one and the other;”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1C 1:8; 3:13; 5:5">c</a></sup>but the body is not for zenut but for Hashem, and Hashem for the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:19–20"> And Hashem brought about the Techiyah from HaMesim for Moshiach Adoneinu and will also bring about the Techiyas HaMesim for us through His gevurah (power).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:30; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:15–16"> Do you not have da'as that your gufot (bodies) are evarim (members, limbs) of Moshiach? Should I then take the evarim (members) of Moshiach and make them evarim (members) of a zonah (prostitute)? Chas v'Shalom! (G-d forbid!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 2:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:31; MARKOS 10:8"> Or do you not have da'as that of the one joining himself to a zonah, that V'HAYU L'VASAR ECHAD (And they will be as one flesh)?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gn 2:24">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 17:21–23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:13"> But the one with deveykus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dt 11:22">e</a></sup>(cleaving, attachment) to Hashem has achdus (union) in the Ruach Hakodesh with Elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:5"> Flee zenut!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Prov 6:23—7:27">f</a></sup>“Every chet is outside the body,” &#91;you say&#93;. But the one guilty of zenut commits chet against the body itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:16; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:16; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5"> Or have you no da'as that your body is a Heikhal Hashem of the Ruach Hakodesh in you, whom you have from Hashem, and you are not your own?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:1; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9"> For &#91;the Geulah redemption of&#93; you &#91;from the Golus of Chet&#93; was purchased with a price; therefore, bring kavod to Hashem with your gufot.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ps 74:2">g</a></sup></span>
