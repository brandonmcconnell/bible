<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:16"> Become imitators of me as I also am an imitator of Moshiach.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="see Ac 21:18-26">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:17; MISHLE 31:28–31"> Now, I commend you that in all things you have remembered me and you hold fast to the masoret torat haShlichim just as I transmitted and handed them over to you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="see Yd 1:3">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 3:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:18"> But I want you to have da'as that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach is the rosh (head) of every one of the Bnei Adam, and the rosh of an isha is the ben Adam (Man, Ba'al),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gn 3:16">c</a></sup>and the rosh of Moshiach is Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gn 3:16">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:30; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:28; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:10"> Every ben Adam davening or speaking forth a nevu'ah (prophecy) having anything hanging down over his rosh brings bushah (shame) upon his rosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 21:12; LUKAS 2:36; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:17"> But every isha davening or speaking forth a nevu'ah (prophecy) in shul, begile rosh (with head uncovered), brings bushah (shame) upon her rosh, for it is one and the same thing to uncover the rosh as it is for the rosh of the isha having been shaved.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dt 21:12">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEVARIM 22:5; BAMIDBAR 5:18"> For, if an isha is not covered, also let her be shorn. But als (since) it is in fact a thing of bushah (shame) for an isha to be shorn or to be shaved, let her be covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:3; BERESHIS 5:1; TEHILLIM 8:6"> For a ben Adam indeed ought not to be covered on the rosh, being the demut HASHEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gn 1:26">f</a></sup>and the kavod Hashem, and the Isha being the kavod (glory), the glorious reflection of Adam.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gn 1:26; 5:1; 9:6">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:13; BERESHIS 2:21–23"> For Adam is not out of the Isha but Isha out of Adam (Man).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gn 2:21-23">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 2:18; BERESHIS 2:23–24; BERESHIS 2:20"> Indeed, Adam was not created because of the Isha, but the Isha because of Adam (Man).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gn 2:18">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:64–65; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:14; MATTITYAHU 18:10"> Because of this, the Isha ought to have a kesut rosh (head covering) of marut (authority, discipline) on her rosh because of the malachim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:28; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:10–14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:12–22"> However, neither is Isha without Adam (Man) nor Adam (Man) without Isha in Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:36; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:6; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:2–3"> For just as the Isha comes out of Adam (Man),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gn 2:21-23">j</a></sup>so also the ben Adam (Man) comes through the Isha<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gn 3:15-16">k</a></sup>but all things are of Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gn 1:1; Ps 24:1; 50:12; 89:11">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 12:57; YOCHANAN 7:24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:15"> You yourselves be the dayan (judge): is it fitting for an isha to offer tefillos to Hashem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="in shul">m</a></sup>begile rosh (with head uncovered)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 14:26; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:35"> Does not teva (nature) itself give you the shiur (lesson) that if a ben Adam wears a long hair-do of a ladyʼs coiffure, it is a dishonor to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> But if an isha wears a long hairdo of a ladyʼs coiffure, it is her kavod?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Song 4:1">n</a></sup>Because the long hair has been given to her instead of the sterntichel (kerchief) or kesut rosh (head covering).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:17; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:3–4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:14"> But if anyone presumes in his thinking to be contentious, we have no such minhag, nor do the kehillot of Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="throughout the world">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:25; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:23; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:22"> But in giving the divrei Torah that follows I give no commendation,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="11:2">p</a></sup>because when you assemble as the shul of Moshiach, it is not for the better that you assemble, but for the worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:10–12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:1"> Ershtins (first of all), indeed when you come together as the kehillah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Ex 12:6">q</a></sup>I hear there exist machlokot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="1C 1:10">r</a></sup>(divisions) and schisms among you, and partly I believe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:19; LETTER TO TITOS 3:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:30"> For it is necessary also for kitot (sects) of minut (heresy), of kefirah (heresy, denial) to be among you that also the approved ones may become manifest among you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Dt 13:3">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Therefore, your farbrengen gatherings in one kahal (community) are not for the purpose of having Moshiachʼs Tish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:23–25"> For each one rushes ahead with his own seudah (meal). One is hungerik (hungry), one has passed out in his schnapps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:28; MISHLE 17:5; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5–6"> Hey, you people, do you not have houses in which to eat and to drink? Or do you despise the Kehillah (congregation) of G-d? And do you bring bushah (shame), even humiliation, on the ones having nothing? What should I say to you? Will I commend you? In this I do not commend you people!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKAS 22:19–20; MATTITYAHU 26:26–28; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:23–25"> For I received from Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu that which also I transmitted and handed on to you, that Adoneinu Yehoshua, on the very lailah (night) in which he was betrayed, took the Pesach matzoh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:53–58; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:27–28; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:3–4"> And, having made the HaMotzi, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach offered the betzi'at halechem (breaking of the bread) and said, ZEH HU VESARI HANNIVTZA BA'ADCHEM; ZOT ASU L'ZIKRONI. (This is my flesh, which is broken on your behalf. Do this in memory of me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKAS 22:20; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:15–20; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:20"> In like manner, after eating the &#91;Pesach&#93; seudah, also &#91;after the bracha over&#93; the &#91;Kiddush&#93; Cup, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, This<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Kiddush">t</a></sup>Kos (Cup) is the BRIT CHADASHA<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Jer 31:31; 32:40">u</a></sup>in my DAHM (blood).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Ex 24:6-8; Zech 9:11">v</a></sup>This do, as often as you drink, in ZIKARON<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Josh 4:7">w</a></sup>(remembrance) of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:28; HISGALUS 1:7; YOCHANAN 14:3"> For as often as you eat this Pesach matzoh and drink from this Pesach Kiddush Cup, you do proclaim the mavet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Isa 53:8-9; Dan 9:26">x</a></sup>of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu until the Bias HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:51; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:29"> Therefore, whoever eats the Pesach matzoh or drinks the Kiddush Cup of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">y</a></sup>Adoneinu unworthily will be guilty and answerable for the basar and the dahm of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">z</a></sup>Adoneinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:4; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:5; EKHAH 3:40"> But let a ben Adam apply cheshbon hanefesh to himself and in that manner let him eat of the Pesach matzoh and let him drink of the Pesach Kiddush Cup.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:27; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:30; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:32–34"> For the one eating and drinking is the one eating and drinking mishpat to himself when not discerning the basar (body).</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:19; BAMIDBAR 20:12; AMOS 3:2"> Because of this, many among you are weak and there are cholim (sick) and a number are sleeping the sleep of the mesim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9; TEHILLIM 32:3–5; HISGALUS 2:5"> But if we were judging ourselves,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="in teshuva">aa</a></sup>we would not be brought into mishpat (judgment).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Ps 32:5">ab</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:5–11; TEHILLIM 118:18; MISHLE 3:11–12"> But, being brought into mishpat (judgment) by Hashem, we are being disciplined, that we may not be condemned<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="to Onesh Gehinnom">ac</a></sup>with the Olam Hazeh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Ps 115:5; 118:18; Prov 3:11,12">ad</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Therefore, Achim b'Moshiach of mine, when you have your farbrengen gatherings and you gather for Tish, wait for one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:21–22; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:19"> If anyone is so hungerik<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="1C 11:21">ae</a></sup>(hungry) &#91;that he cannot wait&#93;, in his home let him eat, lest for mishpat you have kehillah. And as far as the hemshech (remaining part) is concerned, whenever I come I will set b'seder (in order).</span>
