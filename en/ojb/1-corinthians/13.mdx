<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:3; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:20–21"> And if I have nevu'ah and have da'as of all sodot and all da'as, and if I have all emunah so as to remove mountains, but ahavah I do not have, I am nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:14–17; YOCHANAN 15:13; MATTITYAHU 23:5"> And if I'm a marbitz tzedaka and give all I possess in gemilut chasadim and if I give my body al kiddush Hashem for sereifah (death by burning), but ahavah I do not have, I have gained nothing.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Dan 3:28">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:32; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:21–22"> Ahavah suffers long; ahavah is kind; ahavah does not have kinah; ahavah does not brag; ahavah is not puffed up in ga'avah (conceit, pride);</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:3–5; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16–17"> ahavah does not behave shamelessly; ahavah does not in anochiyut insist on its own way; ahavah is not touchy and vindictive, keeping a record of wrongs.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Zech 8:17">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:9; (III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:3–4; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:4"> Ahavah does not find simcha in evil, but rejoices in HaEmes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:4; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:24"> Ahavah covers all things,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Prov 10:12">c</a></sup>believes all things, has tikvah (hope), even savlanut, for all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:1–2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:6"> Ahavah never fails. However, divrei nevu'ah will be abolished; leshonot will cease; da'as will come to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:2–3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:18–19; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:9"> For we have da'as in part, and we have divrei nevu'ah is part.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:12; YESHAYAH 60:19–20; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:7–8"> But when shleimah (completion) comes, the teilvaiz (partial) will disappear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:1–2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:1"> When I was a yeled, I used to speak like one, think like one, reason like one. But when I became mevugar (mature), I put away kinderyohrn.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ps 131:2">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:2; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:9–10"> For still we see through a mirror indistinctly. But then,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="in the Olam Haba">e</a></sup>distinctly, panim el panim. Now I have da'as only in part; then I will have da'as fully, even as also Hashem had full da'as of me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Job 26:14; 36:26; Gn 32:30; Job 19:26">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:14; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:7–18"> But now remain emunah, tikvah, and ahavah, these shalosh (three). And the greatest of these is ahavah.</span>
