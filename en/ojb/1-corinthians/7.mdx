<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:33; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:9"> But, because of the acts of zenut, let each Ben Adam have his own Isha, and let each Isha have her own Ba'al (Husband).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:7; SHEMOT 21:10"> Let the ba'al render the conjugal choiv (debt) to his isha, and likewise also the isha to her ba'al (husband).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HOSHEA 3:3; MATTITYAHU 19:9; MARKOS 10:11–12"> It is not the isha who has samchut (authority) over her own body, but the ba'al (husband); likewise, also it is not the ba'al (husband) who has samchut over his own body, but the isha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:15; YOEL 2:16; ZECHARYAH 12:12–14"> Do not deprive each other, unless by agreement for a set time, that you may renew zerizut (diligence) to tefillah (prayer) and again you may be together, lest HaSatan lead you into nissayon (temptation) because of your lack of shlitah atzmi (self-control).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ex 19:15; 1Sm 21:4,5">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:25; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:12"> But I say this according to concession,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="in view of 5:1-5; 6:12-20">c</a></sup>not according to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiachʼs">d</a></sup>mitzvoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:11–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:5"> But, I wish kol Bnei Adam even to be as I am; however, &#91;this is impossible since&#93; each has his own matanah (gift) from Hashem: one this; and another that.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:32; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:34–35; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:26–27"> But, I say to the bochrim and the almanot (widows), it is beneficial for them if they remain as I am;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:2; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:14; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:11"> But if they do not have shlitah atzmi, let them marry. For better it is to marry than with Eish to be set ablaze.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:32; LUKAS 16:18; MATTITYAHU 19:3–9"> But to the ones having entered bibrit hanissuim (in covenant of marriage), I charge, not I but Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu, an isha is not to separate from her ba'al (husband).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Mal 2:14-16">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 3:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:10; YESHAYAH 50:1"> But, if indeed she is separated, let her remain so, or be reconciled to her basherter; and a ba'al should not leave his isha.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:25"> But, to the rest I — Sha'ul — not Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu, say: if any Ach b'Moshiach has an isha who is an Apikoros and she is willing to live with him, let him not leave her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And if an isha has a ba'al (husband) who is an Apikoros, and he is willing to dwell with her, let her not leave her ba'al (husband).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MALACHI 2:15–16; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:5; LETTER TO TITOS 1:15"> For,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="T.N. following the principle of bikkurim">f</a></sup>the ba'al who is an Apikoros is mekudash b'Ruach Hakodesh (set apart as holy in the Ruach Hakodesh) by the isha, and the isha who is an Apikoros likewise by the Ach b'Moshiach; otherwise, your yeladim are tema'im (unclean); but now they are tehorim (clean).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Mal 2:15">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:19; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:11; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17–18"> But, if the one who is an Apikoros separates and departs, let the separation occur; the Ach b'Moshiach has not been enslaved, or the Achot b'Moshiach in such cases; but Hashem has given you a kri'ah b'shalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:1–2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:14"> For how do you know, isha, if you will not bring your basherter (destined mate), your ba'al, to Yeshu'at Eloheinu?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:3–8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:24"> Only each of you walk the derech<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="T.N. according to Hashemʼs tochnit or etzah Ro 8:28">h</a></sup>to which you were called by Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ps 1:6">i</a></sup>This is my charge in all the kehillot of Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:24"> If as a ben Berit with bris milah anyone received their kri'ah, let him not conceal it; if anyone without bris milah has been called, let him without bris milah not undergo bris milah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:6; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:3–4"> Bris milah is not everything; nor is the lack of it; but being shomer mitzvot Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:12"> Each one walk the derech of his kri'ah (calling, summons), and remain there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:18–24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:11; LUKAS 10:40–41"> If while a bond servant you were called, do not let it consume you, although if you can gain your deror (freedom, liberty),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Lv 25:10">j</a></sup>do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:16; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:22–24"> For, the one in Hashem having been called while a bond servant is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">k</a></sup>Adoneinuʼs ben Chorin (freedman); likewise, the one having been called while a ben Chorin is the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiachʼs bond servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:20; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:18–19; LETTER TO TITOS 2:14"> You were bought with a pidyon nefesh price; do not become avadim haBnei Adam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:23–24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:17"> Each one wherever on the derech of Chayyim he was called, Achim b'Moshiach, there let him remain in deveykus with Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:40"> Next sugya (topic): concerning the betulot (virgins). A mitzvoh of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu I do not have, but a bit of wisdom I offer as one who by the rachamim Hashem is ne'eman (faithful).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:8; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:17"> I consider therefore, it to be beneficial, because of the impending Crisis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="i.e., the Chevlei Moshiach and eschatological woes preceding the Bias Moshiach">l</a></sup>that you remain as you are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:12–14"> Have you entered bibrit hanissuim (in covenant of marriage) with an isha? Do not seek to be free. Are you freed from an isha? Do not seek an isha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:32–36; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:26"> But if indeed you enter bibrit hanissuim (in covenant of marriage), there is no chet; and if the betulah (virgin) marries, there is no averah in that for her. But such will have tzoros in the basar, which I am trying to spare you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Mt 24:19">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:31; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:11–12"> Now this I say, Achim b'Moshiach, the time &#91;until HaKetz&#93; has been shortened. From now on, let those having nashim live as if not having nashim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="TEHILLIM 30:5; LUKAS 6:25; LUKAS 6:21"> And let the ones weeping as not weeping, and let the ones having simcha as not having simcha, and let the ones buying as not possessing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:17; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:7; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:14"> And let the ones using the Olam Hazeh as not fully using it, for the present form of the Olam Hazeh is passing away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:25–34; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:6"> But I would have you free from de'agot (worries). The ben Adam without isha cares for the things of Hashem, how he may please Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:25–33"> But the one having taken an isha cares for the things of the Olam Hazeh, how he may please his isha,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LUKAS 10:40–42; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:20; LUKAS 2:36–37"> And he has been divided.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="1C 1:13">n</a></sup>Both the isha free of a ba'al or the betulah cares for the things of Hashem, that she may be tehorah spiritually and physically. But the isha with a ba'al cares for the things of the Olam Hazeh, how she may please her ba'al.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:33–34; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:36; LUKAS 8:14"> Now, this I say for your own benefit, not that I may throw a noose on your deror (freedom),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Lv 25:10">o</a></sup>but I speak with respect to what is decent, seemly, and sits well with Hashem, without distraction &#91;in avodas kodesh&#93;.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Ps 86:11">p</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:37; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:9; SHMUEL ALEF 2:33"> However, if anyone thinks he does not have proper hitnahagut (conduct) toward the betulah of his eirusin (betrothal, engagement), and if he thinks his basherte (destined mate) is getting along in years, and thus it has to be, what he desires, let him do; there is no chet, let them enter bibrit hanissuim (in covenant of marriage).</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> But he who in his lev has settled the decision, not having the need &#91;of conjugal intimacy&#93;, but having mastery concerning his own desire, and thus he in his lev has decided, not to enter bibrit hanissuim with his betulah (virgin), he does well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:1–2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:8"> So then both the one entering bibrit hanissuim with his betulah does well, and the one not entering bebrit hanissuim with his arusah (betrothed) will do better.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="1C 7:34">q</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:2–3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:14–16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:10"> An isha has been bound (bibrit hanissuim, in covenant of marriage) for so long a time as her ba'al lives, but if her ba'al should sleep the sleep of the mesim, she is free to enter bibrit hanissuim with the ba'al she desires, but only in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:35; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:15–16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:8"> However, happy is she, and even more so, if she remains as she is; and I think in this bit of wisdom I am offering that I have the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
</div>
