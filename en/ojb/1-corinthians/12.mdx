<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:9; TEHILLIM 115:5"> You have da'as that when you WERE Goyim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="T.N. i.e., you are no longer heathen pagans">a</a></sup>somehow you were influenced and led astray to the ELILIM ILLEMIM (Dumb idols, idols incapable of speech).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hab 2:18-19">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:2–3; MATTITYAHU 16:16–17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:9"> Therefore, I make known to you that no one speaking by the Ruach Hakodesh of Hashem says, “Al Yehoshua ki Cherem hu” (a curse of the ban of destruction is on Yehoshua), and no one is able to say, “Yehoshua hu HaAdon” except by the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:4–6"> There are different kinds of matanot (gifts), but the same Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:11–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:6–8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:8–9"> There are different avodot hakodesh (ministries), but the one Adonoi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:11; YOCHANAN 5:17"> And there are a variety of activities &#91;of the Ruach Hakodesh&#93;, but the same G-d working all in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10–11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:6–8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:19"> But to each is given the disclosure of the Ruach Hakodesh for benefit:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MISHLE 2:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:6–10; TEHILLIM 143:10"> To one through the Ruach Hakodesh is given a dvar chochmah (a dvar of wisdom);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ac 16:7">c</a></sup>to another, according to the same Ruach Hakodesh, a dvar da'as (word of knowledge);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ac 5:3">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:14–15; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:8"> To another, by the same Ruach Hakodesh, emunah (faith);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Mt 17:20-21">e</a></sup>to another, by the one Ruach Hakodesh, matanot harippuy (gifts of healing);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="refuah; Ac 3:6-16">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:39; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1; MARKOS 16:17"> to another those of cholel niflaot (accomplishing miracles).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ex 4:21; Yn 11:42-43">g</a></sup>And to another divrei hanevu'ah (words of prophecy),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="1Kgs 17:1">h</a></sup>and to another discernings (being about to make a nafka mina distinction) of ruchot (spirits),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="1Kgs 22:22">i</a></sup>to another kinds of leshonot (tongues),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ac 2:4">j</a></sup>and to another, pitronim (interpretations) of leshonot (tongues).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="1C 14:13">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:4; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:13"> All these things are activated by the one and same Ruach Hakodesh, distributing individually to each one as He determines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:4–5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:27"> For even as the body is echad, and has many evarim (members), and all the evarim (members) of the body, though many, are one body, so is Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:28"> For also in one Ruach Hakodesh we were all given mikveh tevilah into one body, whether Yehudim or Yevanim (Greeks), whether avadim (slaves) or bnei Chorin (freedman), and all were given to drink, as it were, from one Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:27–28; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:25"> For the body is not one, but many evarim (members).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHOFETIM 9:8–15; MELACHIM BAIS 14:9"> If the regel (foot) says, “Because I am not a yad (hand), I am not of the body,” that would not make it any less one of the evarim of the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:3"> And if the ozen (ear) says, “Because I am no ayin (eye), I am not of the body,” that would not make it any less one of the evarim of the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:21; TEHILLIM 139:13–16; MISHLE 20:12"> If the whole body were an ayin (eye), where would be the hearing? If the whole body were hearing, where would be the smelling?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:11"> But now Hashem set the evarim (members), each one of them, in the body according to His ratzon (will), as He wanted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And if all were all one evar (member), where would the body be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:12"> As it is, though there are many evarim (members), there is but one body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZRA 10:1–5; NECHEMYAH 4:16–21; IYOV 29:11"> And the ayin (eye) is not able to say to the yad (hand), “I do not have need of you.” Or, again, the rosh (head), speaking to the raglayim (feet), is not to say, “I have no need of you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MISHLE 14:28; KOHELET 9:14–15; KOHELET 4:9–12"> Just the opposite, the evarim of the body appearing to be weaker are vital.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BERESHIS 3:21; BERESHIS 3:7"> And those evarim of the body which we presume to be dishonorable, on these we clothe with even more honor, and our parts with lesser kibbud (respect, honor) are treated with greater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BERESHIS 3:11; BERESHIS 2:25"> Whereas, our evarim with greater kibbud have no need of more. However, Hashem has so composed the achdus yichudim (unit, harmony of unifications) of the body, giving the superior kibbud to the inferior,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:10–12; YOCHANAN 17:21–26"> lest there be schisms<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="1C 1:10; 11:18">l</a></sup>in the body. But the evarim should have the same gemilut chasadim (deeds of lovingkindness and caring) for one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:3"> And when one evar (member) has tza'ar (pain and suffering), all the evarim (members) have tza'ar with it; or one evar has the aliyah of kibbud, all the evarim has simcha with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:30"> Now you are Moshiachʼs body<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gn 47:18-19">m</a></sup>and individually evarim of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:11–13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:6–8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:18"> Now Hashem placed some in the Adat HaMoshiach (community of Moshiach), in the Kehillah, rishon (first): shlichim, second, nevi'im, third, morim (teachers), then those of cholel nifla'aot (accomplishing miracles),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Ex 4:21">n</a></sup>then matanot harippuy (gifts of healing), then matanot of helps, then manhigut ruchanit (spiritual leadership, administration), kinds of leshonot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:4–11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:14–20"> Surely not all are shlichim? Surely not all are nevi'im? Surely not all are rabbinical morim? Surely not all are those cholel nifa'ot (accomplishing miracles)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:10"> Surely not all have matanot harippuy (gifts of healing)? Surely not all speak in leshonot?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="cf. 1C 14:23,26">o</a></sup>Surely not all have the pitron (interpretation) of leshonot?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:39; MATTITYAHU 5:6"> But earnestly desire the greater matanot (gifts) &#91;of the Ruach Hakodesh&#93;. And yet now I show you a more feste derech.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Rav Sha'ul now turns to the need for balance. Not that he is trying to terminate the miraculous in Moshiachʼs Kehillah [a futile endeavor!], but that he is showing the need for the fruit of the Ruach Hakodesh to balance His gifts, especially in an environment where pride and carnality have quenched ahavah, which is the Yn 3:16center of Moshiachʼs saving histalkus (passing), the death of Moshiach in midst of his Redemptive mission on the analogy of Moses dying before he crosses into the Promised Land.">p</a></sup></span>
