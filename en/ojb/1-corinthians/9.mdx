<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:17; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:19"> Do I not have cherut?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">a</a></sup>Am I not a Shliach? Have I not seen Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu? Are you not my po'al (work) in Adoneinu?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:12; YOCHANAN 6:27; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:1–3"> If to others I am not a Shliach, surely I am to you, for you are the chotam (seal) of my shlichus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ga 2:10">b</a></sup>in Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:16–19; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:10"> My apologetic to the ones cross-examining me is this:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:6; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:8–9; LUKAS 10:7"> Do I not have the privilege of eating and drinking?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:42; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:4; MATTITYAHU 8:14"> Is the privilege not mine to take an achot b'Moshiach as isha in my travels for Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiachʼs avodas kodesh, just as the rest of his Shlichim do and the Achim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Mt 13:55">c</a></sup>of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu and Kefa?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:7–9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:1–2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:36"> Or is it only Bar-Nabba and I who are denied the privilege of not working at a parnasah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MISHLE 27:18; DEVARIM 20:6; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:3–4"> Whoever heard of someone serving as a chaiyal (soldier) but having to pay his own wages for doing so? Who plants a kerem (vineyard) but does not eat the p'ri hakerem? And who serves as a ro'eh (shepherd) over a flock and of the cholov (milk) of the flock does not partake?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Dt 20:6; Prov 27:18">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:31"> Do I say this according to the dvar haBnei Adam? Or does not the Torah say these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEVARIM 25:4; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:18; TEHILLIM 104:27"> For, in the Torah of Moshe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Rabbenu">e</a></sup>it has been written, LO TACHSOM SHOR BEDISHO (You shall not muzzle an ox treading grain).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Dt 25:4">f</a></sup>Surely it is not for oxen that Hashem is concerned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:23–24"> Or does he not speak altogether for our sake, and is it not for us that Hashem says this? Ken, for us, because it was written that the one plowing ought to plow on in tikvah, and the one threshing ought to partake with tikvah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:6; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:15"> If we sowed spiritual things to you, is it too much if we reap in material things from you?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ro 15:27">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:18; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:12"> If others over you can claim this privilege, can we not even more? But we did not make use of this privilege; we endure all things, lest any hindrance we should give to Moshiachʼs Besuras HaGeulah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 18:8–20; BAMIDBAR 5:9–10; VAYIKRA 6:26"> Do you not have da'as that the kohanim serving in the Beis Hamikdash sherut (service in the Temple) eat the things of the Beis Hamikdash; the kohanim attending the Mizbe'ach (altar) have their share with the Mizbe'ach (altar)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:10; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:17–18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:4"> So also Moshiach Adoneinu appointed the ones proclaiming the Besuras HaGeulah to get their parnasah from the Besuras HaGeulah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:12"> But I have not used any of these privileges; I did not write these things that it might be so with me; for itʼs better for me rather to die than that someone deprive me of my kavod &#91;in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Pp 1:21&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:14; YIRMEYAH 20:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:17"> For if I preach the Besuras HaGeulah there is nothing for me to boast about, als (since) necessity is laid on me, for Oy Li (Woe to me!) if I do not preach the Besuras HaGeulah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Jer 20:9">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:9"> For, if I do this willingly, I have a sachar (reward), but if unwillingly, then a ne'emanut (trusteeship), a kehunah for the Besuras HaGeulah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ro 15:16">i</a></sup>has been entrusted to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:7–9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:6"> What then is my sachar (reward)? That in preaching as a maggid of the Besuras HaGeulah, I may make the Besuras HaGeulah free of charge, so as not to make full use of my right in the Besuras HaGeulah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:13; MATTITYAHU 18:15; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:19–20"> For being no indentured servant to any one of the Bnei Adam, I made myself a servant &#91;working for nothing&#93; to kol Bnei Adam, that I might win the more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:20–26"> And I became to the Yehudim as a Yehudi, that I might win Yehudim; to the ones under Gezetz, I became as under Gezetz — not being myself under &#91;the epoch of&#93; Gezetz<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ro 8:2">j</a></sup>— that the ones under Gezetz I might win;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:2"> To the ones without Gezetz, as without Gezetz, though not being without the Torah of Hashem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ac 21:23">k</a></sup>but being under Moshiachʼs Torah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Ro 15:8; Isa 42:4">l</a></sup>that I might win the ones without Gezetz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:33; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:19"> I became weak to the weak ones that I might win the weak ones. I have become all things to kol Bnei Adam, that by all means I might save some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:10; MARKOS 8:35; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:25–27"> And all things I do because of the Besuras HaGeulah that a fellow partaker and deveykus sharer in it I may become.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:1; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:7–8"> Do you not have da'as that the ones running on a race course all indeed run, but it is only one who receives the prize? So run that you may obtain the prize.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:5; HISGALUS 2:10; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:7–8"> And everyone competing in the &#91;Olympic&#93; games in all things exercises shlitah atzmi: those ones, therefore, that they may obtain a perishable wreath; but we, an imperishable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:21; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:5"> Therefore, I run not as one without a goal that is kovua (fixed, set). I box as not beating the air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:5"> But I do more than merely spar with my basar; I pommel it and keep it under strict subjection, so that after I am the maggid to others, I myself will not become declared ineligible.</span>
