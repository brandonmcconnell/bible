<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:39; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:1"> Pursue ahavah (agape), and eagerly desire the things of the Ruach Hakodesh (matanot HaRuach Hakodesh), and especially that you may speak forth a dvar hanevu'ah (word of prophecy).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MARKOS 16:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:46"> For the one speaking in a lashon (tongue) speaks not to Bnei Adam but to Hashem; for no one grasps with their ears, but the speaker by the Ruach Hakodesh speaks sodot (mysteries).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:12; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:13; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:20"> However, the one speaking forth divrei nevu'ah (words of prophecy) speaks to Bnei Adam for chizzuk (strengthening) and musar encouragement and nechamah (comfort).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:22; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:26; MARKOS 16:17"> The one speaking in a lashon (tongue) edifies himself; but, the one speaking forth a dvar hanevu'ah (word of prophecy) edifies kehillah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:28–30; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:10; MARKOS 16:17"> Now I desire all of you to speak in leshonot (tongues), and even more that you may speak forth a dvar hanevu'ah (word of prophecy).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Num 11:29">a</a></sup>Now greater is the one speaking forth a dvar nevu'ah (word of prophecy) than the one speaking in leshonot, unless he gives the pitron (interpretation) of the leshonot (tongues), that the kehillah (congregation) may receive the edification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:17; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:16"> But now, Achim b'Moshiach, if I come to you speaking in leshonot (tongues), what will I benefit you unless I speak to you either with a dvar hisgalus (a word of revelation) or with a dvar da'as or with a dvar nevu'ah or with a dvar hora'ah (word of teaching)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:8; LUKAS 7:32; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:1"> So even lifeless things, like the flute or harp, if they do not articulate a distinction in the notes, how will it be known what is being played on the flute or on the harp?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 10:9; YECHEZKEL 33:3–6; YIRMEYAH 4:19"> Indeed, if a shofar gives an unclear trumpet call, who will prepare himself for krav (battle)?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Num 10:9; Jer 4:19">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:26"> So also unless you by your lashon (tongue) render an intelligible dvar Torah, how will the thing being uttered be known? For you will be merely speaking into the air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> There are doubtless many kinds of foreign languages in the Olam Hazeh, and not one is meaningless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:11"> If, therefore, I do not have da'as of the meaning of the language, I will be to the speaker a foreigner and the speaker will be a foreigner to me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gn 11:7">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:26; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:3–4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:1"> So also you, als (since) you have a zeal for the matanot of the Ruach Hakodesh, endeavor to abound in them for the edification of the Kehillah (Congregation).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MARKOS 11:24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:30; YOCHANAN 14:13–14"> Therefore, the speaker in a lashon (tongue), let him offer tefillos that he may give the pitron (interpretation).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:15–16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:19"> For if I daven (pray) in a lashon, my nashamah davens, but my sikhliyut (rationality) lies shemitah (fallow).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:16; YOCHANAN 4:23–24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:18"> Nu? (Well?) I will daven &#91;in leshonot&#93; with my nashamah, and I will daven also with my seichel; I will sing &#91;in leshonot&#93; with my nashamah, and I will sing niggunim also with my seichel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 16:36; TEHILLIM 106:48; HISGALUS 5:14"> Otherwise, if you make a bracha &#91;in leshonot&#93; with your neshamah, how will the am ha'aretz, who have no idea what you are saying, answer the “Omein”?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Dt 27:15,26; 1Chr 16:36; Neh 8:6; Ps 106:48">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:6"> For indeed you make the bracha well enough, but the other is not being edified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:4–6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:7"> Modeh Ani Hashem that I speak in leshonot more than all of you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:21–22; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:4"> but in kehillah I want to speak five words with my sikhliyut (rationality), that also others I may instruct with a dvar hora'ah (word of teaching), rather than speak ten thousand words in a lashon (tongue).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:19"> Achim b'Moshiach, be not yeladim in your machsh'vot (thoughts); be infantile in kavvanah ra'ah (malice), perhaps, but in your binah, be mature.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Jer 4:22">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YESHAYAH 28:11–12; YIRMEYAH 5:15; DEVARIM 28:49"> In the Torah it stands written, KI BELA'AGEI SAFAH UVELASHON ACHERET YEDABER EL HAAM HAZEH…V'LO AVU SHMO'A (Then with ones of foreign lip and with strange tongue he will speak to this people… but they were not willing to listen), says the L-rd.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isa 28:11-12">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:6–12; MARKOS 16:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:1"> So then the leshonot (tongues) are for an ot (miraculous sign), not to the ones believing, the ma'aminim in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, but an ot (miraculous sign) to the Apikorosim; but divrei hanevu'ah (words of prophecy) are not for the Apikorosim, but for the ones believing, the ma'aminim in Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:18"> If, therefore, the kehillah has a farbrengen gathering and all speak in leshonot (tongues), and then in walks the am ha'aretz or the Apikorosim, will they not say that you are all meshuggah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:37; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:15; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:12–13"> However, if all speak forth divrei hanevu'ah (words of prophecy), and then in walks some Apikorosim or am ha'aretz, such a visitor is brought under conviction by all, he is brought into mishpat (judgment) by all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YESHAYAH 45:14; ZECHARYAH 8:23; TEHILLIM 72:11"> What is hiding in his lev (heart) becomes manifest, and, having fallen on his face, he worships Hashem, declaring that G-d is among you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Zech 8:23; Isa 45:14; Dan 2:47">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:19; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:7–10"> Nu? Well? Achim b'Moshiach, when you come together, each one has a mizmor (hymn, psalm), a musar (teaching with an ethical point), a dvar hisgalus (a dvar of revelation), a lashon (tongue), or a pitron (interpretation) of a lashon (tongue); let all things be for edification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:26"> If anyone speaks in a lashon (tongue), let the speakers be shenayim (two) or at most shloshah (three), and by turn, and let one give the pitron (interpretation).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> But if there is no one to give the pitron, let the one with the lashon (tongue) be silent in the kehillah and let him instead speak to himself and to Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:19–21; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1–3"> And let shenayim or shloshah nevi'im speak and let the other nevi'im be used with discernings of ruchot (spirits).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="1C 12:10">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:19–20; IYOV 33:31–33; IYOV 32:11"> And if a dvar hisgalus (a word of revelation) is given to a navi sitting by, let the first navi become silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:11"> For you all are able one by one to speak forth a dvar hanevu'ah (word of prophecy), in order that all may learn and receive chizzuk (strengthening).</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:19–20; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1; SHMUEL ALEF 10:10–13"> And the neshamot of nevi'im are subject to the nevi'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17–18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:40; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:16"> For Hashem is no Elohei HaMevucha (the G-d of Confusion, Tohu); He is Elohei HaShalom, and this is so in all the kehillot of the Kedoshim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:11–12; BERESHIS 3:16; BAMIDBAR 30:3–13"> Let the nashim in the kehillot be silent, for it is not permitted for them to blurt out, but let them become submissive, as it says in the Torah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gn 3:16">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:12; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:25–27"> And if the nashim wish to inquire about something, let them inquire of their own be'alim b'bayis, for it is a bushah (shame) for an isha to blurt out in the kehillah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gn 3:2">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:1–17; ZECHARYAH 14:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:15"> Or from you did the dvar Hashem go forth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Isa 2:3">k</a></sup>or to you only did it reach?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:6; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:7; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:2"> If anyone thinks himself to be a navi or a man of the Ruach Hakodesh,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="1C 2:13">l</a></sup>let him have full da'as that the things I wrote to you are a mitzvoh of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:3–4; HOSHEA 4:17; HISGALUS 22:11–12"> But if anyone does not recognize this, he is not recognized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:31; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:20"> So then, Achim b'Moshiach of mine, earnestly desire to speak forth a dvar hanevu'ah (word of prophecy), and do not forbid speaking in leshonot (tongues).</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:5; LETTER TO TITOS 1:5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:26–33"> Let all things be done b'seder and without bushah (shame).</span>
