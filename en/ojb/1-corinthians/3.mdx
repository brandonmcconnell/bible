<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:3; TEHILLIM 115:1"> Therefore, neither is the one planting anything nor the one watering, but it is the One giving the increase — Hashem!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:6; MATTITYAHU 16:27; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8"> Now the one planting and the one watering are be'ichud (united), and, each one will receive his own sachar (reward) according to his own amal (toil).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 18:20; 62:12">b</a></sup></span>

But, let each one beware how he builds on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:20; YESHAYAH 28:16; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:19"> For no other yesod other than the one that has been laid can be laid: Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 28:16">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:3; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:7; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:20"> Now if anyone builds on the yesod with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, stubble,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:14–15; HISGALUS 20:12"> The ma'aseh (work) of each bannai will become evident, for, haYom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Yom haDin">e</a></sup>will make it have its hisgalus, because by Eish (Fire) it is revealed; and the Eish (Fire) itself will test the quality of each oneʼs ma'aseh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Num 31:22,23; Jer 23:28,29; Mal 3:3">f</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:12–13; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:23"> If anyoneʼs ma'aseh will be consumed, he will suffer loss, but he himself will be saved,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ep 2:8-9">i</a></sup>yet so as through Eish (fire).</span>

For it has been written, LOCHED CHACHAMIM BE'ARMAM (He catches the wise in their own craftiness).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Job 5:13">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 94:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:21"> And again, HASHEM YODE'A MACHSH'VOT ADAM KI HEMAH HEVEL (The L-rd knows the thoughts of the wise that they are empty vanity).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Ps 94:11">l</a></sup></span>
