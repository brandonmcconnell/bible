<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:4; YESHAYAH 53:1–12"> For I transmitted and handed on to you as authoritative torah, rishon (first), that which was also transmitted and handed on to me as authoritative torah:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ga 1:18">c</a></sup>that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach died on behalf of avoneinu<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 1:4">d</a></sup>(our averos, iniquities, gross wickedness, depravities) according to the Kitvei Hakodesh,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Isa 53:8-9; Dan 9:26">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HOSHEA 6:2; MATTITYAHU 12:40; TEHILLIM 16:10–11"> And that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was buried in a kever<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isa 53:9">f</a></sup>(grave, burial place), and that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was raised in a Techiyah from the mesim on YOM HASHLISHI (Gn 1:11-13<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="T.N. <span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MARKOS 16:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:12; YOCHANAN 1:42"> And that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was seen by Kefa, then by the Sheneym Asar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:10; MARKOS 16:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:36"> Afterward Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was seen by over five hundred Achim b'Moshiach at one time, of whom most remain alive until now, though some sleep the sleep of the mesim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKAS 24:50; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:2–12; LUKAS 24:33"> Afterward, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was seen by Ya'akov,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ga 1:19, also see Mt 13:55">j</a></sup>then by all the Shlichim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:18"> And, last of all, even as if to one born not normally,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="like the rest">k</a></sup>Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was seen by me also.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="cf. 1C 9:1">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:7–8; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:13–15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:3"> For I am the least of Moshiachʼs Shlichim, not qualified to be called a Shliach, because I brought redifah (persecution) upon the Kehillah of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:13; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:13"> But by the Chen v'Chesed Hashem, I am what I am. And the Chen v'Chesed Hashem of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach to me was not in vain, but more abundantly than all the Moshiachʼs Shlichim I labored, yet it was not I, but the Chen v'Chesed Hashem with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:3–4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:2"> Whether it was I or those others, so we preached as Moshiachʼs maggidim, and so you had emunah and became Moshiachʼs ma'aminim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:32; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:18"> And if Moshiach is being preached that from the Mesim (Dead ones) he has had his Techiyah (Resurrection), how is it that some among you say that there is no Techiyas HaMesim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:25–26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:14"> And if there is no Techiyas HaMesim, neither then has Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach been raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:2"> And if Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach has not been raised, then our darshenen to you was in vain and also in vain is your emunah (faith).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:24; IYOV 13:7–10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:32"> Moreover, we are found also to be edei sheker (false witnesses)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Ex 20:16; Dt 19:16-21">m</a></sup>misrepresenting Hashem Himself, because we gave solemn edut<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="1C 1:6">n</a></sup>(testimony) as in the presence of G-d that Hashem raised Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach — whom, of course, He did not raise if, as you say, the Mesim are not bemetzius (in fact) raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For if the Mesim (Dead persons) have not Techiyah, neither has Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach had a Techiyah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:25; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:14"> And if Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach has not already had his Techiyah from the Mesim, your emunah is futile, you are still in your averos (sins),</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:13–14; HISGALUS 14:13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:16"> And even the ones who sleep the sleep of the Mesim in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach have perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:21; HISGALUS 14:13; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:12"> Listen, if for the Olam Hazeh only we have tikvateinu in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, we are to be pitied more than kol Bnei Adam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:23; HISGALUS 1:5"> But in fact Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach has had His Techiyah! Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach is the Bikkurim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Gn 1:11-13; Num 28:26; Lv 23:10-11">o</a></sup>(Firstfruits), the Firstfruits of the ones having fallen asleep &#91;in Moshiach&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:12–17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:22"> For als (since) through an Adam (Man) came mavet,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Gn 2:17">p</a></sup>also through an Adam (Man, &#91;Moshiach&#93;) came the Techiyas HaMesim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:12–21; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:45–49; YOCHANAN 5:21–29"> For as in Adam all die,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Gn 3:19">q</a></sup>so also in Moshiach all will be made alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YESHAYAH 26:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:29; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:52"> But each one in his own order: the Bikkurim, Moshiach; afterward, the ones of Moshiach at the Bias HaMoshiach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DANIEL 7:27; DANIEL 7:14; MATTITYAHU 28:18"> Then HaKetz when Moshiach gives over the Malchut Hashem to G-d, even Elohim Avinu,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Dan 2:44; 7:13-14,27">r</a></sup>after He has abolished all Misrah and Shilton and Gevurah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:12–13; TEHILLIM 110:1; MATTITYAHU 22:44"> For it is necessary for Moshiach to reign until Hashem puts all His OYVIM (enemies) under His RAGLAYIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Ps 110:1">s</a></sup>(feet).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Isa 9:7; 52:7">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:55; HISGALUS 21:4"> The last Oyev (Enemy) to be abolished is Mavet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="TEHILLIM 8:6; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:8; MATTITYAHU 28:18"> For KOL Hashem put TACHAT RAGLAV (All things He subjected under His feet),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Ps 8:6[7]">u</a></sup>but when He says that KOL (all things) have been subjected, it is peshat (plain, literal) that this does not include the One<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hashem">v</a></sup>who put all things in subjection under Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:23"> But, when all things are subjected to Hashem, then also the Zun fun der Oybershter<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Moshiach">w</a></sup>himself will be subjected to the One (Hashem) having subjected all things under him (Moshiach), that in all things G-d may be all.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="i.e., preeminent">x</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:3–4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:16; MATTITYAHU 20:22"> Otherwise, what will they do, the ones being given tevilah on behalf of the dead? If the Mesim really are not raised, why indeed are they given tevilah on behalf of the Mesim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:7–12"> Why also are we putting ourselves at risk and in danger every hour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:10–11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:36; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:9–13"> Daily I die and that is as true a fact, Achim b'Moshiach, as it is that I glory over you in Moshiach Yehoshua Adoneinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YESHAYAH 22:13; YESHAYAH 56:12; LUKAS 12:19"> If it were a mere humanistic matter, my fighting, so to speak, with wild beasts in Ephesus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Ac 19; 2C 1:3-10">y</a></sup>what do I gain? <span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MISHLE 13:20; MATTITYAHU 24:4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:6"> Do not fall under a delusion. “Bad chavrusashaft (association, influence or hashpa'ah) corrupts good midos.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:11–12"> Wake up and come to your senses. Start walking in the derech tzaddikim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Ps 1:6">aa</a></sup>Stop committing averos. Some among you have no saving da'as of Hashem. I say this to your bushah (shame).</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 37:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:38–53; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:19"> But someone will question, “How does this Techiyas HaMesim come about? And in the Techiyas HaMesim, with what kind of body will they come?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Ezek 37:3">ab</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:24; LUKAS 11:40; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:15"> Yold, what you sow is not made alive unless it dies!</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And what is it you sow? Not the body that is to be, but something else: a bare zera (seed), efsher (perhaps) of wheat or some other grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:7; YESHAYAH 61:11; MARKOS 4:26–29"> However, Hashem chooses<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="another">ac</a></sup>body to give to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="“dead” and buried">ad</a></sup>seed, and to each kind of zera is given by Hashem its own body.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Gn 1:11-13; 47:18-19">ae</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="BERESHIS 1:20–26"> Now basar is not all the same: Bnei Adam have one kind of basar; animals, another; birds, another; fish, another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and there are gufot baShomayim (heavenly bodies), and gufot ba'aretz (earthly bodies), but the kavod of the heavenly is of one kind, and the kavod of the earthly of another kind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YESHAYAH 24:23; TEHILLIM 148:3–5; DEVARIM 4:19"> There is one kavod (glory) of the shemesh (sun), and another kavod (glory) of the levanah (moon), and another kavod (glory) of the kochavim (stars), for in kavod (glory), kochav differs from kochav.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Ps 19:4-6; 8:1,3">af</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:43; DANIEL 12:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:21"> So also is the Techiyas HaMesim. <span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:20–21"> What is sown without kavod is raised in kavod. What is sown in weakness is raised in ko'ach (power).</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:50; LUKAS 24:31; YOCHANAN 20:19"> What is sown a natural body, is raised a spiritual body. If there is a natural body, there is also a spiritual one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:21; BERESHIS 2:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:2"> So, also, it has been written, VAY'HI HAADAM L'NEFESH CHAYYAH (And the &#91;first&#93; Man became a living soul);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Gn 2:7">ah</a></sup>but the Adam haacharon (last Adam) became a Ruach mechayyeh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Yn 5:26">ai</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:9–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:22–24"> But the spiritual body is not harishon, but the natural; then afterward the spiritual.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:31; BERESHIS 2:7; BERESHIS 3:19"> The Adam Harishon is AFAR MIN HA'ADAMAH (dust from the earth, ground),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Gn 2:7">aj</a></sup>out of ha'aretz. <span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="BERESHIS 5:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:12–21; YOCHANAN 3:6"> As was the Adam of dust, such also are those of the dust; as was the Adam of Shomayim, such also are those who are of Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:29; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:18; BERESHIS 5:3"> And just as we have borne the demut of the Adam MIN AFAR HA'ADAMAH (from the dust of the earth),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Gn 2:7">al</a></sup>so we will bear also the likeness of the Heavenly Adam (Man).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Gn 5:3">am</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:3–6; MATTITYAHU 16:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:17"> Now this I say, Achim b'Moshiach, that basar vadahm cannot inherit the Malchut Hashem, neither can the perishable inherit the imperishable.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Ps 16:9-10; Job 19:25-26">an</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:14–17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:21; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:20"> Hinei! I speak a sod (mystery) to you: we will not all sleep the sleep of the Mesim, but we will all be changed.</span>

<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:2–4; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:7"> For it is necessary for this perishable nature to put on the imperishable, and this mortal nature to put on the immortal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="YESHAYAH 25:8; HISGALUS 20:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:14–15"> But when this perishable nature puts on the imperishable, and this mortal, the immortal, then the dvar hanevu'ah will come to pass that stands written, “He will swallow up death forever”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Isa 25:8">ao</a></sup>in victory.</span>
