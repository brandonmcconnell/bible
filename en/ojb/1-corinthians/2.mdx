<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:26–27; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:3; MATTITYAHU 11:25"> But we speak the chochmah of Hashem in a hidden sod (mystery),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="1C 2:1">i</a></sup>which was nigzar merosh (determined from the beginning, preordained, predestined, decided beforehand) by Hashem lifnei yemei haOlam (before the days of eternity) for our kavod (glory);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ro 8:29-30">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:27; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:2"> A chochmah which not one of the rulers of the Olam Hazeh has known, for, if they had had da'as, they would not have made talui al HaEtz HaKelalat Hashem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Dt 21:23">k</a></sup>(being hanged on the Tree of the Curse of G-d) the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Moshiach">l</a></sup>Adon HaKavod.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Ps 24:7">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YESHAYAH 64:4; TEHILLIM 31:19; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12"> But even as it has been written, Things which no eye has seen and LO SHAMU (they had not heard) nor did it come up into the heart of Bnei Adam, the things G-d prepared for the ones who have ahavah for him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Isa 64:4[3]; TARGUM HASHIVIM; Isa 52:15">n</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MISHLE 20:27; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:33–34"> For who of Bnei Adam has da'as of the things of Bnei Adam except the ruach of a man in him? So also the things of G-d no one has known except the Ruach Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Jer 17:9; Prov 20:27">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:5–6; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20"> Now we have not received the ruach of the Olam Hazeh but the Ruach Hakodesh from Hashem, that we may have da'as of the things having been freely given to us by Hashem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:17"> Which things also we speak, not in devarim (words) taught by chochmah haBnei Adam, but in devarim taught by the Ruach Hakodesh, making midrash<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="exposition, interpretation">p</a></sup>of the things of the Ruach Hakodesh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="1C 2:12">q</a></sup>by means of the words of the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
</div>
