<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:8–9; SHEMOT 33:17; NACHUM 1:7"> But if a person has Ahavas Hashem, Hashem has da'as of that person.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Jer 1:5">b</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:39; YESHAYAH 41:24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:19–20"> Now concerning the eating of the okhel (food) at the mizbe'ach of avodah zarah which is sacrificed to an elil (idol), we have da'as that an elil is nothing in the world, and that there is no G-d but ECHAD.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Dt 4:35,39; 6:4">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:4; YIRMEYAH 2:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:8"> For even if there are<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="in popular tradition">d</a></sup>so-called “g-ds,” whether in Shomayim or on ha'aretz, even as there are so-called “g-ds” many and “l-rds” many,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:36; YOCHANAN 1:3; MALACHI 2:10"> Yet in fact for us we have da'as that there is ADONOI ECHAD (L-rd is One),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dt 6:4">e</a></sup>Hashem AV ECHAD L'CHULLANU (One Father of us all),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Mal 2:10">f</a></sup>from whom are all things, and we exist for Hashem, and there is Adon Echad,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Mal 3:1">g</a></sup>Moshiach Yehoshua,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Zech 3:8; 6:11-12">h</a></sup>through whom are all things and we through him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:28–29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:22–23"> However, not kol Bnei Adam have this da'as (knowledge). Some are so accustomed to the elil (idol) until now that when they eat, they think of the okhel (food) as being sacrificed to the elil in avodah zarah, and their matzpun (conscience), being weak, is made to be tameh (defiled).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:13"> But okhel (food) will not usher us into the presence of Hashem; neither are we falling short if we do not eat okhel, nor are we better if we eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:20–21; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:16"> But beware lest somehow your cherut (freedom) becomes a michshol (stumbling block) to the weak ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:28–29; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:20–21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:23"> For if anyone sees you, the one having da'as (knowledge), eating in the temple of an elil, will not the matzpun of him be strengthened so as to eat the okhel sacrificed to an elil (idol) at the mizbe'ach of avodah zarah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:1–3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:13"> For the one being weak &#91;in emunah&#93; is being destroyed by your “da'as,” the Ach b'Moshiach for whom Moshiach died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:6; MATTITYAHU 25:45; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:12"> And thus by sinning against the Achim b'Moshiach and wounding their weak matzpunim (consciences), you commit averos against Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:21; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:29"> Therefore, if okhel causes my Ach b'Moshiach to trip on a michshol, I should never eat meat again, lest I cause my Ach b'Moshiach to stumble.</span>
