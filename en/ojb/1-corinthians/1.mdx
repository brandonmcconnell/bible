<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:9–11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:30"> To the Kehillah (congregation) of Hashem existing in Corinth, to the ones having been set apart unto kedushah (holiness) in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, given the kri'ah to be Kedoshim, with all the ones who in every place call on the name of Adoneinu, theirs and ours, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:7; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:2; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:2"> Chen v'Chesed Hashem and Shalom from Elohim Avinu and Adoneinu Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:8; YOCHANAN 14:26; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:14"> Modeh Ani (I give thanks) to my G-d always concerning you for the Chen v'Chesed Hashem having been given to you in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:3"> That in everything you were enriched in Moshiach in all expression and kol da'as (all knowledge),</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:2; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:8; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:6"> Even as the edut (testimony) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was confirmed in you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:20; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:28"> So that you are not lacking in any matnat Elohim, awaiting the hisgalus (revelation) of Adoneinu Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:23–24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:22"> Who also with chizzuk (strengthening) will confirm you ad es Ketz (until the time of the End),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dan 11:35">b</a></sup>unreprovable in the Yom Hashem, the Yom Adoneinu, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Am 5:18">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:23; DEVARIM 7:9"> Ne'eman (faithful) is Hashem through whom you were called into the chavurah (company, fellowship, society) of His Zun fun der Oybershter Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua Adoneinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:1–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:1–7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:25"> Now I exhort you, Achim b'Moshiach, b'Shem Adoneinu Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, that you all speak the same thing and that there not be among you machlokot (divisions of dissension),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ro 11:18">d</a></sup>but that you may have achdus (unity) in the same mind and in the same way of thinking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MISHLE 18:6; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:4; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:23–25"> For it was made clear to me about you, Achim b'Moshiach of mine, by the ones of Chloe, that there is merivah (strife) among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:21–23; YOCHANAN 1:42; MATTITYAHU 23:9–10"> Now I say this, because each of you says, “I am of Sha'ul,” or, “I am of Apollos,” or “I am of Kefa,” or “I am of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:5; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:38"> Has Moshiach been divided? Surely Sha'ul was not for you the one talui al HaEtz (being hanged on the Tree)?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dt 21:23">e</a></sup>Surely it was not in the name of Sha'ul that the Moshiachʼs tevilah in the mikveh mayim was given to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:23; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:4"> Modeh Ani Hashem that to not one of you I gave Moshiachʼs tevilah, except Crispus and Gaius,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ro 16:23">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:2; YOCHANAN 3:28–29; YOCHANAN 7:18"> Lest anyone should say that in my name you were given the Moshiachʼs tevilah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:33"> Now I gave Moshiachʼs tevilah also to Stephanasʼ household; as to the rest, I do not know if I gave Moshiachʼs tevilah to anyone else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:13; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:16"> For Moshiach did not send me to give Moshiachʼs tevilah in the mikveh mayim, but to preach the Besuras HaGeulah, not by means of the lomdes (cleverness, erudition) of the rhetoric of Bnei Adam, lest the gevurah (power) of HaEtz HaKelalat Hashem(the Tree of the Curse of G-d)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Dt 21:23">g</a></sup>of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach be buttel (cancelled out).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:16; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:10"> For the darshenen of HaEtz HaKelalat Hashem(the Tree of the Curse of G-d)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Dt 21:23">h</a></sup>is narrishkait to the ones perishing. But to us who are being delivered in Yeshu'at Eloheinu, it is the gevurat Hashem(the power of G-d).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 29:14; YIRMEYAH 8:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:19"> For it has been written, I will destroy CHOCHMAT CHACHAMAV (the wisdom of the wise ones) U'VINAT NEVONAV (and the intelligence of the intelligent) I will set aside.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Isa 29:14">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:22; IYOV 12:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:19"> Where is the chacham (wise man)? Where is the sofer (scribe) of the yeshiva, where is the talmid chacham? Where is the philosophical debater of the Olam Hazeh? Did not Hashem make the so-called chochmah (wisdom) of the Olam Hazeh to look like narrishkait?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Isa 19:11,12; Job 12:17; Isa 44:25; Jer 8:9">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKAS 10:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:20–22; MATTITYAHU 11:25"> For, als (since) — and this was by the chochmah of Hashem — the Olam Hazeh did not by its chochmah have da'as of Hashem, G-d was pleased through the “sichlut” (foolishness) of the Hachrazah (Proclamation, Kerygma, Preaching) of the Besuras HaGeulah to save the ma'aminim (believers).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:1–4; MATTITYAHU 12:38–39; MARKOS 8:11"> Yehudim ask for otot (signs)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ex 7:3">k</a></sup>and Yevanim (Greeks) seek chochmah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:2; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:8"> But, we proclaim Moshiach and nivlato al haEtz (his body on the Tree):<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Dt 21:23">l</a></sup>to Jews, a michshol;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Isa 8:14">m</a></sup>to Goyim, narrishkait (foolishness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:30; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:18"> Yet, to those whom Hashem has given the kri'ah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="1C 1:1-2">n</a></sup>and summoned, to HaKeru'im (to the Called Ones), both to Yehudim and to Yevanim Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, the Gevurat Hashem and the Chochmat Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:33–36; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:18; ZECHARYAH 4:6–7"> For the so-called “sichlut” (foolishness) of Hashem has more chochmah than Bnei Adam, and the “weakness” of G-d has more koach (power) than Bnei Adam.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="1C 1:18">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:18–20; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9–11"> For you see your kri'ah (call), Achim b'Moshiach, what you were, that not many of you were chachamim (wise ones) by the standards of Bnei Adam, not many ba'alei hashpa'ah (people of influence), not many ba'alei zchus (privileged).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5; MATTITYAHU 11:25; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:20"> But Hashem in His bechirah (selection) chose the things of sichlut (foolishness), that He might bring the chachamim to bushah (shame); and Hashem in His bechirah (selection) chose the things of weakness that He might bring the strong to bushah (shame).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="IYOV 34:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:17; YESHAYAH 2:11"> And those of the Olam Hazeh without mishpochah atzilah (noble birth) and those which are hanivzim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Isa 53:3">p</a></sup>(the despised)Hashem chose, choosing the things that are not, in order to bring to naught the things that are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:27; YIRMEYAH 9:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:9"> His tachlis (purpose) is that no basar (fallen humanity sold under the power of slave master Chet Kadmon, Original Sin)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Ro 7:14">q</a></sup>may boast before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:1"> But you are of Hashem in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua who became to us chochmah (wisdom) from Hashem, our Tzidkanut (Righteousness) and our Kedushah (Holiness) and our Geulah LaOlam (Redemption to the world),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Jer 23:5,6; 33:16">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 9:23–24; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:3"> Al menat (in order that), as it has been written, YITHALLEL HAMITHALLEL B'HASHEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Jer 9:23">s</a></sup>(The one boasting let him boast in the L-rd).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Ps 34:2; 44:8">t</a></sup></span>
