<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKAS 4:8; DEVARIM 13:4; MATTITYAHU 4:10"> And Shmuel spoke unto kol Bais Yisroel, saying, If ye do return unto Hashem with all your hearts, put away the elohei hanekhar and Ashtarot from among you, commit your hearts unto Hashem, serve Him only; and He will deliver you out of the yad Pelishtim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HOSHEA 14:8; HOSHEA 14:3; SHOFETIM 2:11"> Then the Bnei Yisroel did put away Ba'alim and Ashtarot, and served Hashem only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHOFETIM 20:1; SHMUEL ALEF 12:23; YEHOSHUA 15:38"> And Shmuel said, Gather kol Yisroel to Mitzpah, and I will make intercessory prayer and daven for you unto Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 14:14; SHMUEL ALEF 1:15; SHOFETIM 10:10"> And they gathered together at Mitzpah, and drew mayim, and poured it out before Hashem, and did a tzom on that day, and said there, We have sinned against Hashem. And Shmuel judged the Bnei Yisroel at Mitzpah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:11; SHMUEL ALEF 13:6; SHEMOT 14:10"> And when the Pelishtim heard that the Bnei Yisroel were gathered together at Mitzpah, the rulers of the Pelishtim went up against Yisroel. And when the Bnei Yisroel heard it, they were afraid of the Pelishtim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YESHAYAH 37:4; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:16; SHMUEL ALEF 12:19–24"> And the Bnei Yisroel said to Shmuel, Cease not to cry out unto Hashem Eloheinu for us, that He will save us out of the yad Pelishtim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 15:1; TEHILLIM 99:6; SHMUEL ALEF 7:17"> And Shmuel took a suckling lamb, and offered him for an olah unto Hashem; and Shmuel cried out unto Hashem on behalf of Yisroel; and Hashem answered him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 2:10; SHMUEL BAIS 22:14–15; YEHOSHUA 10:10"> And as Shmuel was offering up the olah, the Pelishtim drew near to battle against Yisroel; but Hashem thundered with a kol gadol on that day upon the Pelishtim, and threw them into confusion and panic; and they were struck down before Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the Anshei Yisroel went from Mitzpah, and pursued the Pelishtim, and struck them down, until they came to below Beit-Kar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:10; YEHOSHUA 4:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:22"> Then Shmuel took one even (stone), and set it up between Mitzpah and Shen, and called the shem of it Even-Ezer, saying, Hitherto hath Hashem helped us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHOFETIM 13:1; SHMUEL ALEF 17:49–53; SHMUEL ALEF 14:6–16"> So the Pelishtim were subdued, and they came no more into the territory of Yisroel; and the yad Hashem was against the Pelishtim all the days of Shmuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 106:34; DEVARIM 7:2; DEVARIM 7:16"> And the towns which the Pelishtim had captured from Yisroel were restored to Yisroel, from Ekron even unto Gat; and the territory thereof did Yisroel deliver out of the yad Pelishtim.<br />And there was shalom between Yisroel and HaEmori (the Amorites).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 7:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:20–21; SHOFETIM 2:16"> And Shmuel judged Yisroel all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHOFETIM 10:4; SHOFETIM 12:14; SHOFETIM 5:10"> And he went from shanah b'shanah in circuit to Beit-El, and Gilgal, and Mitzpah, and judged Yisroel in all those places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 1:19; SHMUEL ALEF 8:4; SHMUEL ALEF 1:1"> And his teshuvah (return) was to Ramah; for there was his bais; and there he judged Yisroel; and there he built a Mizbe'ach unto Hashem.</span>
