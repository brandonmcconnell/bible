<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 10:1–12; SHMUEL ALEF 28:4; SHMUEL BAIS 1:21"> Now the Pelishtim fought against Yisroel; and the anshei Yisroel fled from before the Pelishtim, and fell down chalalim (slain ones) on Mt Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 8:33; SHMUEL ALEF 14:49; SHMUEL ALEF 23:17"> And the Pelishtim followed hard upon Sha'ul and upon his banim; and the Pelishtim slaughtered Yehonatan,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Yonatan">a</a></sup>and Avinadav, and Malki-Shu'a, the Bnei Sha'ul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 1:6; BERESHIS 49:23; MELACHIM ALEF 22:34"> And the milchamah became intense against Sha'ul, and the archers overtook him; and he was mortally wounded by the archers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHOFETIM 9:54; SHMUEL BAIS 1:6; SHMUEL BAIS 1:9–10"> Then said Sha'ul unto his noseh keli (armor bearer), Draw thy cherev, and thrust me through therewith; lest these arelim (uncircumcised ones) come and thrust me through, and abuse me. But his noseh keli would not; for he was very afraid. Therefore Sha'ul took a cherev, and fell upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 10:5"> And when his noseh keli saw that Sha'ul was dead, he fell likewise upon his cherev, and died with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 12:17; HOSHEA 13:10–11; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 10:6"> So Sha'ul died, and his shloshet banim, and his noseh keli, and kol anashim of him, that same day together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:33; VAYIKRA 26:32; VAYIKRA 26:36"> And when the anshei Yisroel that were on the other side of the emek (valley), and they that were on the other side of the Yarden, saw that the anshei Yisroel fled, and that Sha'ul and his banim were dead, they abandoned the towns, and fled; and the Pelishtim came and took occupation of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:25; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 10:8"> And it came to pass on the next day, when the Pelishtim came to strip the chalalim, that they found Sha'ul and his shloshet banim fallen on Mt Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 1:20; SHOFETIM 16:23–24; SHMUEL ALEF 17:54"> And they cut off his rosh (head), and stripped off his keli, and sent through eretz Pelishtim all around, to publish it in the bais atzabim, and among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHOFETIM 2:13; YEHOSHUA 17:11; SHMUEL ALEF 21:9"> And they put his keli in the Bais Ashtarot; and they hung up his body on the chomat Beit-Shan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 11:1–11; SHMUEL BAIS 2:4"> And when the inhabitants of Yavesh Gil'ad heard of that which the Pelishtim had done to Sha'ul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 16:14; YIRMEYAH 34:5; SHMUEL BAIS 2:4–7"> kol ish chayil arose, and went kol halailah, and took the geviyyat Sha'ul and the geviyyot of his banim from the chomat Beit-Shan, and came to Yavesh, and cremated them there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 21:12–14; BERESHIS 50:10; SHMUEL ALEF 22:6"> And they took their atzmot, and buried them under a tree at Yavesh, and underwent a tzom shivat yamim.</span>
