<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:61; SHMUEL ALEF 5:3; SHMUEL ALEF 5:1"> And the Aron Hashem was in the territory of the Pelishtim (Philistines) shivah chodashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 7:11; BERESHIS 41:8; DANIEL 5:7"> And the Pelishtim (Philistines) called for the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Dagon">a</a></sup>kohanim and the kosemim (sorcerers), saying, What shall we do with the Aron Hashem? Tell us wherewith we shall send it to its makom (place).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 6:9; SHEMOT 23:15; DEVARIM 16:16"> And they said, If ye send away the Aron Elohei Yisroel, send it not away empty; but ye shall surely return to Him an asham (trespass offering); then ye shall be healed, and it shall be known to you why His Yad is not removed from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHOFETIM 3:3; SHMUEL ALEF 6:17–18; YEHOSHUA 13:3"> Then they said, What shall be the asham (trespass offering) which we shall return to Him? They answered, Five techorim of zahav, and five akhbarim (rats) of zahav, according to the number of rulers of the Pelishtim: for one magefah (plague) was on you all, and on your rulers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 7:19; SHMUEL ALEF 5:6–7; SHMUEL ALEF 5:11"> So ye shall make tzalmei techorim (likenesses of tumors) of yours, and tzalmei akhabarim (likenesses of rats) of yours, of that which mar the land; and ye shall give kavod unto Elohei Yisroel; perhaps He will lighten His Yad from off you, and from off eloheichem (your g-ds), and from off your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 8:15; SHEMOT 7:13; SHEMOT 14:17"> Now then why do ye harden levavchem, as the Mitzrayim and Pharaoh hardened their hearts? When He had wrought wonderfully among them, did they not send them away, so that they departed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 6:3; BAMIDBAR 19:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 13:7"> Now therefore make an agalah chadashah (new cart, wagon), and take two milch<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="milk-yielding">b</a></sup>cows,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e., nursing cows">c</a></sup>on which there hath come no ol (yoke), and hitch the cows to the agalah, and take their calves home, away from them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 6:3–5"> And take the Aron Hashem, and lay it upon the agalah; and put the kelei hazahav, which ye send back to Him for an asham (trespass offering), in a box by the side thereof; and send it away, that it may go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 6:3; YEHOSHUA 15:10; YEHOSHUA 21:16"> And see: if it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="the Aron Hashem">d</a></sup>goeth up by the derech of its own territory<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="i.e., Eretz Yisroel">e</a></sup>toward Beit-Shemesh, then He hath done us this ra'ah hagedolah (great disaster); but if not, then we shall know that it is not His Yad that struck us; the evil came upon us mikreh (by chance, by accident).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the anashim did so; and took two milch cows, and hitched them to the agalah, and penned up their calves babayit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:13–15; SHMUEL BAIS 6:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 13:7"> And they laid the Aron Hashem upon the agalah, and the box with the akhbarim of zahav and the tzalmei techorim (likenesses of tumors) of theirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 20:19"> And the cows took the straight derech to the derech Beit-Shemesh, and went along the path, lowing as they went, and turned not aside to the yamin or to the semol; and the rulers of the Pelishtim went after them as far as the border of Beit-Shemesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And they of Beit-Shemesh were kotzerim (harvesting) their ketzir chittim (wheat harvest) in the valley; and they lifted up their eyes, and saw the Aron, and they rejoiced to see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 24:22; SHOFETIM 21:4; SHMUEL ALEF 11:5"> And the agalah came into the sadeh of Yehoshua, a man of Beit-Shemesh, and stopped there, where there was an even gedolah (a large rock); and they chopped up the wood of the agalah, and offered the cows as an olah (burnt offering) unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 3:3"> And the Levi'im took down the Aron Hashem, and the box that was with it, wherein were the kelei zahav, and put them on the even hagedolah (large rock); and the anashim of Beit-Shemesh offered olot (burnt offerings) and sacrificed zevakhim (sacrifices) on that day unto Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHOFETIM 3:3; SHMUEL ALEF 6:4; YEHOSHUA 13:3"> And when the five rulers of the Pelishtim saw it, they returned to Ekron on that same day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 1:20; YIRMEYAH 25:20; SHMUEL ALEF 5:10"> And these are the techorim (tumors) of zahav which the Pelishtim (Philistines) returned for an asham (trespass offering) unto Hashem; for Ashdod one, for Azah (Gaza) one, for Ashkelon one, for Gat one, for Ekron one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 13:3; DEVARIM 3:5; SHMUEL ALEF 6:14–16"> And the akhbarim of zahav, according to the mispar of all the towns of the Pelishtim (Philistines) belonging to the five rulers, both of fortified cities and of country villages, even unto Avel HaGedolah, whereon they set down the Aron Hashem; it &#91;the even hagedolah&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See verse 15">f</a></sup>remaineth unto this day in the sadeh of Yehoshua, the man of Beit-Shemesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 4:20; SHEMOT 19:21; SHMUEL BAIS 6:7"> And He struck down the anashim of Beit-Shemesh, because they had looked into the Aron Hashem,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Num 4:20">g</a></sup>even He struck down of HaAm shivim ish;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="and chamishim elef ish">h</a></sup>and HaAm mourned, because Hashem had struck down many of HaAm with a makkah gedolah (a great stroke, blow).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 6:9; MALACHI 3:2; HISGALUS 6:17"> And the anashim of Beit-Shemesh said, Who is able to stand before Hashem HaElohim HaKadosh Hazeh (Hashemthis holy G-d)? And to whom shall He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hashem dwelling with the Aron Hashem">i</a></sup>go up from us?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 13:5–6; YEHOSHUA 9:17; YEHOSHUA 18:14"> And they sent malachim to the inhabitants of Kiryat-Ye'arim saying, The Pelishtim (Philistines) have returned the Aron Hashem; come ye down, and take it up to you.</span>
</div>
