<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:1; SHMUEL ALEF 29:1–3; SHMUEL ALEF 7:7"> And it came to pass in those yamim, that the Pelishtim gathered their machanot together for tzava (warfare), to fight with Yisroel. And Akhish said unto Dovid, Know thou assuredly, that thou shalt go out with me to machaneh, thou and thy anashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:9; SHMUEL BAIS 16:16–19; SHMUEL ALEF 27:10"> And Dovid said to Akhish, Surely thou shalt know what thy eved will do. And Akhish said to Dovid, Therefore will I make thee my shomer rosh (head bodyguard) kol hayamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:31; SHMUEL ALEF 25:1; VAYIKRA 20:27"> Now Shmuel was dead, and kol Yisroel had mourned him, and buried him in Ramah, even in his own town. And Sha'ul had deported the ovot (mediums, those with familiar spirits), and the yidonim (spiritists) from HaAretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 31:1; YEHOSHUA 19:18; MELACHIM BAIS 4:8"> And the Pelishtim gathered themselves together, and came and encamped in Shunem; and Sha'ul gathered kol Yisroel together, and they encamped in Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YESHAYAH 57:20–21; DANIEL 5:6; YESHAYAH 21:3–4"> And when Sha'ul saw the machaneh of the Pelishtim, he was afraid, and his lev greatly trembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 12:6; SHMUEL ALEF 14:37; EKHAH 2:9"> And when Sha'ul inquired of Hashem, Hashem answered him not, neither by chalomot (dreams), nor by Urim, nor by nevi'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:16; YEHOSHUA 17:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 10:13"> Then said Sha'ul unto his avadim, Seek me an isha that is a ba'alat ov (medium, a person with a familiar spirit), that I may go to her, and inquire of her. And his avadim said to him, Hinei, there is an eshet ba'alat ov at Ein-Dor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:19; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 10:13; MELACHIM ALEF 22:30"> And Sha'ul disguised himself, and put on begadim acherim, and he went, and two anashim with him, and they came to the isha by lailah; and he said, divine for me by the ov (familiar spirit), and bring him up for me, whom I shall name unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 28:3; MELACHIM BAIS 5:7; SHMUEL BAIS 18:13"> And the isha said unto him, Hinei, thou knowest what Sha'ul hath done, how he hath cut off the ovot (mediums, those with familiar spirits), and the yidoni (spiritist) from HaAretz; why then layest thou a snare for my nefesh, to cause me to die?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 14:11; SHMUEL ALEF 19:6; DEVARIM 18:10–12"> And Sha'ul vowed to her by Hashem, saying, As Hashem liveth, there shall no avon (punishment) happen to thee for this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then said the isha, Whom shall I bring up for thee? And he said, Bring up Shmuel for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 28:3; MELACHIM ALEF 14:5"> And when the isha saw Shmuel, she cried with a kol gadol (loud voice); and the isha spoke to Sha'ul, saying, Why hast thou deceived me? For thou art Sha'ul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TEHILLIM 82:6–7; YOCHANAN 10:34–35; SHEMOT 22:28"> And HaMelech said unto her, Be not afraid: for what sawest thou? And the isha said unto Sha'ul, I see elohim olim (spirits, ones ascending, a spirit ascending) from HaAretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:27; MELACHIM BAIS 2:8; SHMUEL ALEF 24:8"> And he said unto her, Of what form is he? And she said, An ish zaken cometh up; and he is wearing a me'il (robe). And Sha'ul had da'as that it was Shmuel, and he bowed with his face to the ground, and prostrated himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 16:23–26; SHMUEL ALEF 28:6; SHMUEL ALEF 18:12"> And Shmuel said to Sha'ul, Why hast thou disquieted me, to bring me up? And Sha'ul answered, I am greatly distressed; for the Pelishtim make war against me, and Elohim has turned away from me, and answereth me no more, neither by nevi'im, nor by chalomot (dreams): therefore I have called on thee, that thou mayest make known unto me what I should do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 6:27; EKHAH 2:5; TEHILLIM 68:1–3"> Then said Shmuel, Why then dost thou ask of me, seeing Hashem is turned away from thee, and is become thine adversary?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:27–29; SHMUEL ALEF 16:13; SHMUEL ALEF 13:13–14"> And Hashem hath done for Himself just as He spoke by me; for Hashem hath torn the Mamlachah out of thine yad, and given it to thy re'a (neighbor), even to Dovid:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:9; MELACHIM ALEF 20:42; TEHILLIM 50:21–22"> Because thou obeyedst not the kol (voice) of Hashem, nor executedst His charon af upon Amalek, therefore hath Hashem done this thing unto thee this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:5; YIRMEYAH 28:16–17; MELACHIM ALEF 22:20"> Moreover Hashem will also deliver Yisroel with thee into the yad (hand, power) of the Pelishtim; and makhar (tomorrow) shalt thou and thy banim be with me; Hashem also shall deliver the machaneh Yisroel into the yad Pelishtim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 28:5; IYOV 26:2; IYOV 15:20–24"> Then at once Sha'ul fell his full length on the ground, and was greatly afraid, because of the divrei Shmuel; and there was no ko'ach in him; for he had eaten no lechem all the yom, nor all the lailah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHOFETIM 12:3; IYOV 13:14; SHMUEL ALEF 19:5"> And the isha came unto Sha'ul, and saw that he was greatly terrified, and said unto him, Hinei, thine shifcha hath obeyed thy voice, and I have put my nefesh in my palm, and have paid heed unto thy devarim which thou spoke unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Now therefore, shema (pay heed) thou now also unto the voice of thine shifcha, and let me set some lechem before thee; and eat, that thou mayest have ko'ach, when thou goest on thy derech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 21:4; LUKAS 24:29; LUKAS 14:23"> But he refused, and said, I will not eat. But his avadim, together with the isha, urged him; and he paid heed unto their voice. So he arose from ha'aretz, and sat upon the mittah (bed).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKAS 15:23; BERESHIS 18:7–8"> And the isha had a fattened egel in the bais; and she hasted, and slaughtered it, and took kemakh (flour), and kneaded it, and did bake matzot thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And she brought it before Sha'ul, and before his avadim; and they did eat. Then they rose up, and went away that lailah.</span>
