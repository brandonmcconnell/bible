<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="AMOS 8:11–12; TEHILLIM 74:9; SHMUEL ALEF 3:21"> And the na'ar Shmuel ministered unto Hashem before Eli. <span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 4:15; TEHILLIM 90:10; BERESHIS 27:1"> And it came to pass at that time, when Eli was lying down in his makom (his eyes had began to grow dim, that he could not see);</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TEHILLIM 27:4; VAYIKRA 24:2–4; SHEMOT 27:20–21"> And the Ner Elohim had not yet gone out in the Heikhal Hashem, where the Aron Elohim was, and Shmuel was lying down;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YESHAYAH 6:8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:6–11; SHEMOT 3:4"> That Hashem called Shmuel; and he answered, Hineni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he ran unto Eli, and said, Hineni; for thou calledst me. And he said, I called not; lie down again. And he went and lay down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:2; SHMUEL ALEF 4:16; SHMUEL BAIS 18:22"> And Hashem called yet again, Shmuel. And Shmuel got up and went to Eli, and said, Hineni; for thou didst call me. And he answered, I called not, beni; lie down again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:2; YIRMEYAH 9:24"> Now Shmuel did not yet know Hashem; the Devar Hashem was not yet revealed unto him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Yn 1:1,14">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="IYOV 33:14–15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:11–12"> And Hashem called Shmuel again the third time. And he got up and went to Eli, and said, Hineni; for thou didst call me. And Eli perceived that Hashem was calling the na'ar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:19; YESHAYAH 6:8; TEHILLIM 85:8"> Therefore Eli said unto Shmuel, Go, lie down; and it shall be, if He call thee, that thou shalt say, Speak, Hashem; for Thy eved (servant) shomei'a (is listening). So Shmuel went and lay down in his makom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 3:8; SHMUEL ALEF 3:4–6"> And Hashem came, and stood, and called as at other times, Shmuel, Shmuel. Then Shmuel answered, Speak; for Thy eved shomei'a.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="CHABAKUK 1:5; YIRMEYAH 19:3; MELACHIM BAIS 21:12"> And Hashem said to Shmuel, Hinei, I am doing a thing in Yisroel, at which both the oznayim of every one that heareth it shall tingle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 2:27–36; ZECHARYAH 1:6; YEHOSHUA 23:15"> In that day I will perform against Eli all things which I have spoken concerning his Bais; from beginning even to the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MISHLE 19:18; MATTITYAHU 10:37; SHMUEL ALEF 2:12"> For I have told him that I will judge his Bais ad olam for the avon (iniquity) which he knoweth; because his banim brought klalot (curses) upon themselves, and he restrained them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YESHAYAH 22:14; SHMUEL ALEF 2:25; YIRMEYAH 15:1"> And therefore I have sworn unto the Bais Eli, that there shall be no kapporah for the avon Bais Eli by zevach nor minchah ad olam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 1:6–8; MALACHI 1:10; SHMUEL ALEF 1:9"> And Shmuel lay until boker, and opened the dlatot Beis Hashem. And Shmuel feared to show Eli the mareh (vision).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then Eli called Shmuel, and said, Shmuel, beni. And he answered, Hineni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:35; RUTH 1:17; TEHILLIM 141:5"> And he said, What is the davar that Hashem hath said unto thee? Now hide it not from me; Elohim do so to thee, and more also, if thou hide anything from me of all the things that He said unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="IYOV 2:10; YESHAYAH 39:8; SHOFETIM 10:15"> And Shmuel told him everything, and hid nothing from him. And he said, It is Hashem; let Him do what seemeth Him tov.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BERESHIS 39:2; SHMUEL ALEF 9:6; MELACHIM ALEF 8:56"> And Shmuel grew, and Hashem was with him, and did let none of his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Shmuelʼs">c</a></sup>words fall to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHOFETIM 20:1; SHMUEL BAIS 3:10; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:12"> And kol Yisroel from Dan even to Be'er-Sheva knew that Shmuel was established to be a navi of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 3:1; BERESHIS 12:7; SHMUEL ALEF 3:4"> And Hashem appeared again in Shiloh; for Hashem revealed Himself to Shmuel in Shiloh by the Devar Hashem.</span>
