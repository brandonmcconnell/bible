<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHOFETIM 6:27; MICHOH 7:5; SHMUEL BAIS 1:25–26"> Now the day came to pass, that Yonatan Ben Sha'ul said unto the na'ar (young man) that bore his armor, Come, and let us go over to the outpost of the Pelishtim (Philistines), that is on the other side.But he told not aviv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 13:15–16; YESHAYAH 10:28–29"> And Sha'ul tarried in the outskirts of Giv'ah under a pomegranate tree which is in Migron; and HaAm (the people, the army) that were with him were about shesh me'ot ish (six hundred men);</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 22:20; SHMUEL ALEF 2:28; SHMUEL ALEF 4:21"> And Achiyah Ben Achituv, the ach of Ikhavod Ben Pinchas Ben Eli, kohen Hashem in Shiloh, was wearing an ephod. And HaAm knew not that Yonatan was gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 13:23"> And between the passes, by which Yonatan sought to go over unto the outpost of the Pelishtim, there was a rocky cliff on the one side, and a rocky cliff on the other side; and the shem of the one was Botzetz, and the shem of the other Seneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The one steep rock faced north opposite Michmas, and the other southward toward Geva.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:31; ZECHARYAH 4:6"> And Yonatan said to the na'ar that bore his armor, Come, and let us go over unto the outpost of these arelim (uncircumcised); it may be that Hashem will act for us; for nothing restrains Hashem l'hoshi'a (to save), by many or by few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 7:3; ZECHARYAH 8:23; SHMUEL ALEF 10:7"> And his armor-bearer said unto him, Do all that is in thine lev; turn thee; hineni, I am with thee according to thy lev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHOFETIM 7:9–14"> Then said Yonatan, Hinei, we will pass over unto these anashim, and we will show ourselves unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHOFETIM 6:36–40; BERESHIS 24:13–14"> If they say thus unto us, Stand still until we come to you; then we will stand still tachteinu (in our place), and will not go up unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:14; SHMUEL ALEF 10:7; SHOFETIM 7:11"> But if they say thus, Come up unto us; then we will go up; for Hashem hath delivered them into yadenu (our hand, power): and this shall be haot (the sign) unto us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 13:6; SHMUEL ALEF 14:22; SHOFETIM 6:2"> And both of them showed themselves unto the outpost of the Pelishtim (Philistines); and the Pelishtim said, Hinei, the Ivrim (Hebrews) come forth out of the chorim (holes) where they had hid themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:43–44; SHMUEL BAIS 5:24; SHOFETIM 7:15"> And the anashim of the outpost answered Yonatan and his armor-bearer, and said, Come up to us, and we will teach you a thing. And Yonatan said unto his armor-bearer, Come up after me, for Hashem hath delivered them into the yad Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:31; YEHOSHUA 23:10; VAYIKRA 26:7–8"> And Yonatan climbed up upon his hands and upon his feet, and his armor-bearer after him; and they fell before Yonatan; and his armor-bearer slaughtered behind him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And that first attack, which Yonatan and his armor-bearer made, killed about esrim ish (twenty men), within an area of half a yoke.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., the area an oxen yoke could plow in one day, or rather, half of that">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 13:17; BERESHIS 35:5; SHMUEL BAIS 5:24"> And there was fearful panic in the machaneh, in the sadeh, and among kol haAm; the outpost, and the raiders, they also trembled, and ha'aretz quaked; so it was a cheredat Elohim (a panic from Elohim).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:22–25; YESHAYAH 19:2; SHOFETIM 7:22"> And the tzofim (watchmen, lookouts, sentries) of Sha'ul in Giv'at Binyamin looked; and, hinei, the multitude melted away, and they went in all directions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then said Sha'ul unto HaAm that were with him, Number now, and see who is gone from us. And when they had numbered, hinei, Yonatan and his armor-bearer were not there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 30:7–8; SHMUEL ALEF 5:2; SHOFETIM 20:23"> And Sha'ul said unto Achiyah, Bring here the Aron HaElohim. For the Aron HaElohim was at that time with the Bnei Yisroel there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 14:24; BAMIDBAR 27:21; YEHOSHUA 9:14"> And it came to pass, while Sha'ul talked to the kohen,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., Achiyah">b</a></sup>that the tumult that was in the machaneh Pelishtim went on and increased; and Sha'ul said unto the kohen, Withdraw thine yad.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e., letʼs go">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHOFETIM 7:22; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:23; SHMUEL ALEF 14:16"> And Sha'ul and kol HaAm that were with him assembled themselves, and they went to the milchamah; and, hinei, every cherev ish was against his fellow, and there was mehumah gedolah me'od (a very great confusion).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 29:4; SHOFETIM 7:23"> Moreover the Ivrim (Hebrews) that were with the Pelishtim (Philistines) before that time, which went up with them into the machaneh from the country round about, even they also turned to be with them of Yisroel that were with Sha'ul and Yonatan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 13:6; SHMUEL ALEF 31:7"> Likewise all the Ish Yisroel which had hid themselves in Har Ephrayim, when they heard that the Pelishtim fled, even they also followed hard after them in the milchamah (battle).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHEMOT 14:30; SHMUEL ALEF 13:5; TEHILLIM 44:6–8"> So Hashem saved Yisroel that day; and the milchamah continued raging even unto Beit-Aven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 6:26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:2; SHOFETIM 1:28"> And the Ish Yisroel were distressed that day; for Sha'ul had bound under oath HaAm, saying, Arur (cursed) be the ish that eateth lechem before erev, that I may be avenged on mine enemies. So none of HaAm tasted lechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:8; BAMIDBAR 13:27; DEVARIM 9:28"> And all they of HaAretz (the army) came to the wood; and there was devash on the surface of the sadeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="KOHELET 9:2"> And when HaAm were come into the wood, hinei, the devash was oozing; but no one put his yad to his mouth; for HaAm feared the shevuah (oath).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 14:29; SHMUEL ALEF 14:43; SHMUEL ALEF 30:12"> But Yonatan heard not when aviv bound HaAm with the oath; wherefore he put forth the end of the matteh (staff) that was in his yad, and dipped it in a honeycomb of the devash, and put his yad to his mouth; and his eyes brightened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 14:43; SHMUEL ALEF 14:24"> Then answered an ish from HaAm, and said, Avicha strictly bound HaAm with an oath, saying, Arur (cursed) be the ish that eateth lechem this day. And HaAm were faint with famished exhaustion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:18; YEHOSHUA 7:25"> Then said Yonatan, Avi hath troubled HaAretz; see, now, how mine eyes hath brightened, because I tasted a little of this devash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="KOHELET 9:18"> How much more, if only HaAm had eaten freely today of the plunder of their enemies which they found? For would there not have been now a much greater makkah (slaughter) among the Pelishtim (Philistines)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 10:12; YEHOSHUA 19:42"> And they drove back the Pelishtim that day from Michmas to Ayalon; and HaAm were very faint.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:19; VAYIKRA 3:17; VAYIKRA 17:10–14"> And the people flew upon the plunder, and took tzon, and bakar, and calves and butchered them on the ground; and HaAm did eat them with the dahm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:1; MATTITYAHU 7:5"> Then they told Sha'ul, saying, Hinei, HaAm sin against Hashem, in that they eat with the dahm. And he said, Ye have dealt treacherously; today roll an even gedolah (large stone) over here to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Then Sha'ul said, Disperse yourselves among the people, and say unto them, Bring me here every ish his shor (ox), and every ish his seh, and you make shochet slaughter of them here, and eat; and sin not against Hashem in eating with the dahm. And kol HaAm brought every ish his shor with him that lailah, and made shochet slaughter of them there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 7:17; SHMUEL ALEF 7:12; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:5"> And Sha'ul built a Mizbe'ach unto Hashem; it was the first time that he built a Mizbe'ach unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YESHAYAH 48:1–2; TEHILLIM 73:28; MALACHI 2:7"> And Sha'ul said, Let us go down after the Pelishtim by lailah, and plunder them until the ohr haboker, and let us not leave an ish of them. And they said, Do whatsoever seemeth tov in thine eyes. Then said the kohen,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Achiyah">d</a></sup>Let us draw near here unto HaElohim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="to inquire of G-d">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 28:6; SHMUEL ALEF 30:7–8; SHMUEL ALEF 23:9–12"> And Sha'ul asked counsel of Elohim, Shall I go down after the Pelishtim? Wilt Thou deliver them into the yad Yisroel? But He did not answer him on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="SHOFETIM 20:2; YEHOSHUA 7:14–26; BAMIDBAR 24:17"> And Sha'ul said, Draw ye near here, all the pinnot (corners, chiefs of) HaAm; and know and see wherein this chattat hath been this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 14:44; SHMUEL BAIS 12:5; SHMUEL ALEF 14:24"> For, as Hashem the Moshia Yisroel liveth, though it be in Yonatan beni (my son), he shall surely die. But there was none among kol HaAm that answered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:15; SHMUEL ALEF 14:7; SHMUEL ALEF 14:36"> Then said he unto kol Yisroel, Be ye on one side, and I and Yonatan beni will be on the other side. And HaAm said unto Sha'ul, Do what seemeth tov in thine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:24; YEHOSHUA 7:16–18; SHMUEL ALEF 10:20–21"> Therefore Sha'ul said unto Hashem Elohei Yisroel, Give a tamim (perfect lot). And Sha'ul and Yonatan were taken by lot; but HaAm escaped &#91;from being taken by lot&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And Sha'ul said, Cast lots between me and Yonatan beni (my son). And Yonatan was taken by lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 7:19; SHMUEL ALEF 14:27; YONAH 1:7–10"> Then Sha'ul said to Yonatan, Tell me what thou hast done. And Yonatan told him, and said, I did but taste a little devash with the end of the matteh that was in mine yad, and, hineni, I must die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 14:39; RUTH 1:17; SHMUEL ALEF 25:22"> And Sha'ul answered, Elohim do so and more also if thou shalt not surely die, Yonatan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="LUKAS 21:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:34; SHMUEL BAIS 14:11"> And HaAm said unto Sha'ul, Shall Yonatan die, who hath wrought this Yeshu'ah HaGedolah (Great Salvation, Deliverance, Rescue) in Yisroel? Chalilah (far be it!); as Hashem liveth, there shall not one hair of his rosh fall to the ground; for he hath wrought with Elohim this day. So HaAm rescued Yonatan, that he died not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> Then Sha'ul stopped following the Pelishtim; and the Pelishtim withdrew to their own makom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 10:6; SHMUEL ALEF 14:52; SHMUEL BAIS 8:3"> So Sha'ul took the melachah (kingdom, establishment of sovereignty) over Yisroel, and fought against all his oyevim on every side, against Moav, and against the Bnei Ammon, and against Edom, and against the melachim of Tzovah, and against the Pelishtim; and everywhere he turned himself, he terrorized them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="SHEMOT 17:14; SHMUEL ALEF 15:3–7; DEVARIM 25:19"> And he gathered an army, and struck Amalek, and delivered Yisroel out of the hands of them that plundered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 8:33; SHMUEL ALEF 31:2; SHMUEL BAIS 6:20–23"> Now the Bnei Sha'ul were Yonatan, and Yishvi, and Malki-Shu'a; and the shmot of his two banot were these; the shem of the bechirah (first born &#91;daughter&#93;), Merav, and the shem of the younger Michal;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 2:8; SHMUEL ALEF 17:55; SHMUEL BAIS 3:27"> And the shem of eshet Sha'ul was Achino'am Bat Achima'atz; and the shem of the Sar of his Tzeva was Avner Ben Ner and Ner was dod Sha'ul (uncle of Sha'ul).</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 9:1; SHMUEL ALEF 9:21"> And Kish was Avi Sha'ul; Ner Avi Avner was ben Aviel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 8:11; SHMUEL ALEF 8:1"> And there was milchamah chazakah against the Pelishtim all the days of Sha'ul; and when Sha'ul saw any ish gibbor, or any valiant man, he recruited him.</span>
