<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 9:16; SHMUEL ALEF 12:14; TEHILLIM 2:10–11"> Shmuel also said unto Sha'ul, Hashem sent me limeshachacha (to anoint thee) to be Melech over His people, over Yisroel; now therefore shema (pay heed) thou unto the voice of the Divrei Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 25:17–19; SHEMOT 17:8–16; BAMIDBAR 24:20"> Thus saith Hashem Tzva'os, I remember that which Amalek did to Yisroel, how he waylaid him on the derech, when he came up from Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEVARIM 20:16–18; BAMIDBAR 24:20; BAMIDBAR 31:17"> Now go and attack Amalek, and utterly destroy all that they have, and spare them not; but slay both ish and isha, olel, and yonek, shor, seh, gamal, and chamor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:24; SHMUEL ALEF 11:8; SHMUEL ALEF 13:15"> And Sha'ul gathered HaAm together, and numbered them at Tela'im, two hundred elef foot soldiers, and ten elef Ish Yehudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Sha'ul came to a city of Amalek, and lay in wait in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHOFETIM 1:16; BAMIDBAR 10:29–32; SHEMOT 18:19"> And Sha'ul said unto the Keni, Go, depart, get you away from among the Amaleki, lest I destroy you with them; for ye showed chesed to kol Bnei Yisroel, when they came up out of Mitzrayim. So the Keni moved away from among Amalek.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 16:7; BERESHIS 25:18; SHMUEL ALEF 14:48"> And Sha'ul attacked Amalek from Chavilah until where thou comest to Shur, that is alongside Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 30:1; ESTHER 3:1; BAMIDBAR 24:7"> And he took Agag Melech Amalek alive, and destroyed with utter cherem destruction kol haAm with the edge of the cherev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:15; SHMUEL ALEF 15:19; SHMUEL ALEF 15:3"> But Sha'ul and HaAm spared Agag, and the best of the tzon, and of the bakar, and of the fat bulls, and the fat sheep, and all that was tov, and would not utterly destroy them; but everything that was despised and worthless, that they destroyed with utter cherem destruction.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then came the Devar Hashem unto Shmuel, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 13:13; BERESHIS 6:6; SHMUEL BAIS 24:16"> I greatly regret and relent and reconsider that I have set up Sha'ul to be Melech; for he is turned back from following Me, and hath not performed My Devar. And it grieved Shmuel; and he cried out unto Hashem kol halailah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:55; SHMUEL BAIS 18:18; SHMUEL ALEF 25:2"> And when Shmuel rose early to meet Sha'ul in the boker, it was told Shmuel, saying, Sha'ul came to Carmel, and, hinei, he set up a yad (hand, i.e. memorial, monument) for himself. And he turned and went down to Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHOFETIM 17:2; RUTH 3:10; MISHLE 31:31"> And Shmuel came to Sha'ul; and Sha'ul said unto him, Baruch atah l'Hashem; I have carried out the Devar Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MALACHI 3:13–15; YIRMEYAH 2:18–19; YIRMEYAH 2:34–37"> And Shmuel said, What meaneth then this bleating of the tzon (sheep) in mine ears, and the lowing of the bakar (cattle) which I hear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:21; SHMUEL ALEF 15:9; SHEMOT 32:22–23"> And Sha'ul said, They have brought them from Amalek; for HaAm spared the best of the tzon and of the bakar, in order to sacrifice unto Hashem Eloheicha; and the rest we have destroyed in utter cherem desruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 9:27; MELACHIM ALEF 22:16; SHMUEL ALEF 12:7"> Then Shmuel said unto Sha'ul, Stop, and I will tell thee what Hashem hath said to me this night. And he said unto him, Say on.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 10:22; SHMUEL ALEF 9:21; SHMUEL ALEF 15:1–3"> And Shmuel said, When thou wast katon (little) in thine own eyes, wast thou not made the Rosh Shivtei Yisroel, and Hashem anointed thee Melech over Yisroel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MISHLE 10:29; BERESHIS 13:13; BERESHIS 15:16"> And Hashem sent thee baderech (on a mission), and said, Go destroy with utter cherem destruction the chatta'im (sinners), Amalek, and make war against them until they be consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 14:32; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:12"> Why then didst thou not obey the voice of Hashem, but didst pounce upon the plunder, and didst harah (the evil) in the eyes of Hashem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:13; MATTITYAHU 19:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:3"> And Sha'ul said unto Shmuel, Yes, I have obeyed the voice of Hashem, and have gone baderech (on the mission) which Hashem sent me, and have brought back Agag Melech Amalek, and have destroyed Amalek with utter cherem destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:15; SHEMOT 32:22–23; BERESHIS 3:13"> But HaAm took of the plunder the tzon and bakar, the reshit of the cherem to sacrifice unto Hashem Eloheicha at Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HOSHEA 6:6; TEHILLIM 51:16–17; MISHLE 21:3"> And Shmuel said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Hath Hashem as great chefetz (delight) in olot and zevakhim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as in obeying the voice of Hashem?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Hinei, to obey is better than zevach (sacrifice),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and to pay heed than the chelev eilim (fat of rams).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 28:16; SHMUEL ALEF 13:14; VAYIKRA 20:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For meri (rebellion) is as the chattat (sin) of kesem (witchcraft, soothsaying, divination),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and stubbornness is as heathenish iniquity and idolatry.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because thou hast rejected the Devar Hashem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He hath also rejected thee from being Melech.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 12:13; YESHAYAH 51:12–13; BAMIDBAR 22:34"> And Sha'ul said unto Shmuel, Chatati (I have sinned): for I have transgressed the commandment of Hashem, and thy words: because I feared HaAm, and listened to their voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHEMOT 10:17"> Therefore, now, pardon my sin, and turn back with me, that I may worship Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="HOSHEA 4:6; SHMUEL ALEF 13:14; SHMUEL ALEF 16:1"> And Shmuel said unto Sha'ul, I will not go back with thee: for thou hast rejected the Davar Hashem, and Hashem hath rejected thee from being Melech over Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:30–31"> And as Shmuel turned about to go away, he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Sha'ul">a</a></sup>seized the edge of his me'il (robe, mantle), and he tore (it).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 28:17–18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:22; DANIEL 4:32"> And Shmuel said unto him, Hashem hath torn the Mamlechut Yisroel from thee this day, and hath given it to a re'a (neighbor) of thine, that is better than thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 24:14; BAMIDBAR 23:19; LETTER TO TITOS 1:2"> And also the Netzach Yisroel (Eternal One of Israel) will not lie nor relent: for He is not an adam, that He should change His mind.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:44; YOCHANAN 12:43; YESHAYAH 29:13"> Then he said, Chatati (I have sinned): yet honor me now, before the Ziknei Ami, and before Yisroel, and turn back with me, that I may worship Hashem Eloheicha.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> So Shmuel turned back after Sha'ul; and Sha'ul worshiped Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="HISGALUS 18:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:3; YIRMEYAH 48:44"> Then said Shmuel, Bring ye to me Agag Melech Amalek. And Agag came unto him confidently. And Agag thought, Surely the mar hamavet (bitterness of death) is past.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="SHOFETIM 1:7; BERESHIS 9:6; MATTITYAHU 7:2"> And Shmuel said, As thy cherev hath made nashim childless, so shall immecha be childless among nashim. And Shmuel cut Agag in pieces before Hashem at Gilgal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 11:4; SHMUEL ALEF 7:17"> Then Shmuel went to Ramah; and Sha'ul went up to his bais at Giv'at Sha'ul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:11; SHMUEL ALEF 19:24; SHMUEL ALEF 16:1"> Until his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Sha'ulʼs">b</a></sup>yom mot Shmuel came not again to see him: nevertheless Shmuel mourned for Sha'ul: and Hashem relented that He had made Sha'ul Melech over Yisroel.</span>
</div>
</div>
