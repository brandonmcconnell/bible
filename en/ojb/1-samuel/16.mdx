<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 9:1; SHMUEL ALEF 15:23; TEHILLIM 78:68–71"> And Hashem said unto Shmuel, Ad mosai wilt thou mourn for Sha'ul, seeing I have rejected him as Melech al Yisroel? Fill thine keren with shemen, and go, I will send thee to Yishai of Beit-Lechem: for I have provided Me a melech among his banim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 9:12; SHMUEL ALEF 20:29; MATTITYAHU 10:16"> And Shmuel said, How can I go? If Sha'ul hear it, he will kill me. And Hashem said, Take an eglat bakar (heifer of the herd) with thee, and say, I am come to sacrifice to Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 9:16; SHMUEL ALEF 16:12–13; SHMUEL BAIS 15:11"> And call Yishai to the zevach, and I will show thee what thou shalt do; umashachta (and thou shalt anoint) unto Me him whom I say unto thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:13; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:17–18; SHMUEL ALEF 21:1"> And Shmuel did that which Hashem spoke, and came to Beit-Lechem. And the zekenim of the town trembled at his coming, and said, Comest thou in shalom?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 3:5; VAYIKRA 20:7–8; SHEMOT 19:10"> And he said, Shalom; I am come to sacrifice unto Hashem; set yourselves apart as kodesh, and come with me to the zevach. And he set apart as kodesh Yishai and his banim, and called them to the zevach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:13; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:13; SHMUEL ALEF 17:22"> And it came to pass, when they were come, that he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Shmuel">a</a></sup>took one look at Eliav, and said, Surely Hashemʼs Moshiach is before Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:24; YIRMEYAH 17:10; LUKAS 16:15"> But Hashem said unto Shmuel, Look not on his mareh (appearance), or on the height of his stature; because I have rejected him; for Hashem seeth not as HaAdam seeth; for HaAdam looketh at the einayim (eyes, outward form); Hashem looketh at the lev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:13; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:13"> Then Yishai called Avinadav, and made him pass before Shmuel. And he said, Neither hath Hashem chosen this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:13; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:13; SHMUEL BAIS 13:3"> Then Yishai made Shammah to pass by. And he said, Neither hath Hashem chosen this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:13–15"> Again, Yishai made his shivat banim to pass before Shmuel. And Shmuel said unto Yishai, these Hashem hath not bachar (chosen).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:70–71; SHMUEL BAIS 7:8; SHMUEL BAIS 13:3"> And Shmuel said unto Yishai, Are here all thy ne'arim (boys)? And he said, There remaineth yet the katan (small, young), and, hinei, ro'eh batzon (tending the flock) is he. And Shmuel said unto Yishai, Send and get him; for we will not sit down &#91;to eat&#93; till he come here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EKHAH 4:7; TEHILLIM 89:19–20; SHIR HASHIRIM 5:10"> And he sent, and brought him in. Now he was adomoni (ruddy, of healthy red complexion) and withal of a yafeh countenance, and good-looking. And Hashem said, Arise, meshachehu (anoint him); ki zeh hu (for this is he).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 10:6; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:9; YOCHANAN 3:34"> Then Shmuel took the keren of shemen, v'yimshach (and anointed) him in the midst of his achim; and the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hakodesh">b</a></sup>of Hashem came upon Dovid from that day forward. So Shmuel rose up, and went to Ramah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 51:11; SHMUEL ALEF 18:12; SHOFETIM 9:23"> But the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hakodesh">c</a></sup>of Hashem departed from Sha'ul, and a ruach ra'ah from Hashem terrified and overwhelmed him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Sha'ul">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the avdei Sha'ul said unto him, Hinei now, a ruach Elohim ra'ah terrifieth and overwhelmeth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 3:15; MELACHIM ALEF 10:8; SHMUEL ALEF 19:9"> Let adoneinu now command thy avadim, which are before thee, to seek out an ish, who is a menagen (player) on the kinnor (harp); and it shall come to pass, when the ruach Elohim ra'ah is upon thee, that he shall play with his yad, and thou shalt be tov (better, well).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Sha'ul said unto his avadim, Look for me now an ish that can play well, and bring him to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 3:19; BERESHIS 39:23; BERESHIS 39:2"> Then answered one of the ne'arim, and said, Hinei, I have seen a ben of Yishai of Beit-Lechem, that is skillful in playing, and a gibbor chayil and an ish milchamah prudent in matters, and an ish to'ar (handsome man), and Hashem is with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:11; SHMUEL ALEF 17:15; TEHILLIM 78:70–72"> Wherefore Sha'ul sent malachim unto Yishai, and said, Send me Dovid binecha, which is with the tzon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 10:27; MISHLE 18:16; SHMUEL ALEF 17:18"> And Yishai took a chamor laden with lechem, and a skin of yayin, and a gedi (young goat), and sent them by Dovid bno unto Sha'ul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:46; MISHLE 22:29; TEHILLIM 146:3"> And Dovid came to Sha'ul, and stood before him; and he loved him greatly; and he became his no'se kelim (armor-bearer).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Sha'ul sent to Yishai, saying, Let Dovid, now, stand before me; for he hath found chen (favor) in my eyes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:14–16; SHMUEL ALEF 18:10–11; LUKAS 11:24–26"> And it came to pass, when the ruach Elohim was upon Sha'ul, that Dovid took the kinnor, and played with his yad; so Sha'ul was relieved, and was tov, and the ruach hara'ah departed from him.</span>
</div>
