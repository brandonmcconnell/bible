<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 14:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:19; LUKAS 16:8"> And the sarim of the Pelishtim were angry with him; and the sarim of the Pelishtim said unto him, Send the ish back, that he may turn again to his makom (place, home) which thou hast assigned him, and let him not go down with us to milchamah, lest in the milchamah he be as satan (adversary, antagonist) to us; for wherewith should he make himself acceptable unto his adon? Is it not with the heads of these anashim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 21:11; SHMUEL ALEF 18:6–7; MISHLE 27:14"> Is not this Dovid, of whom they sang one to another in mecholot (dances), saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Sha'ul slaughtered his alafim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Dovid his revavot (myriads)?</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 22:34"> Wherefore now shuv (turn, turn back), and go in shalom, that thou displease not the rulers of the Pelishtim.</span>
