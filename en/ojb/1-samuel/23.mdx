<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:44; MICHOH 6:15; NECHEMYAH 3:17–18"> Then they told Dovid, saying, Hinei, the Pelishtim fight against Ke'ilah, and they rob the granot (threshing floors).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 23:4; SHMUEL BAIS 5:19; SHMUEL BAIS 5:23"> Therefore Dovid inquired of Hashem, saying, Shall I go and strike these Pelishtim? And Hashem said unto Dovid, Go, and strike the Pelishtim, and save Ke'ilah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 12:5; TEHILLIM 11:1; SHMUEL ALEF 23:15"> And Anshei Dovid said unto him, Hinei, Anachnu (we) are afraid here in Yehudah; how much more then if we come to Ke'ilah against the maarkhot (military lines) of the Pelishtim?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHOFETIM 7:7; YEHOSHUA 8:7; MELACHIM BAIS 3:18"> Then Dovid inquired of Hashem yet again. And Hashem answered him and said, Arise, go down to Ke'ilah; for I will deliver the Pelishtim into thine yad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So Dovid and his anashim went to Ke'ilah, and fought with the Pelishtim, and brought away their livestock, and attacked them with a makkah gedolah (great defeat). So Dovid saved the inhabitants of Ke'ilah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 22:20; SHMUEL ALEF 14:3; SHMUEL ALEF 14:18"> And it came to pass, when Evyatar (Abiathar) Ben Achimelech fled to Dovid to Ke'ilah, that he came down with the Ephod<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="with oracular lot; see Ex 28:6-30">a</a></sup>in his yad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 24:4–6; IYOV 20:5; SHMUEL ALEF 26:8–9"> And it was told Sha'ul that Dovid was come to Ke'ilah. And Sha'ul said, Elohim hath delivered him into mine yad; for he is shut in, by entering into an ir (town) that hath gates and bar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Sha'ul called kol HaAm together for milchamah, to go down to Ke'ilah, to besiege Dovid and his anashim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 30:7; SHMUEL ALEF 23:6; BAMIDBAR 27:21"> And Dovid knew that Sha'ul secretly plotted ra'ah (evil, harm) against him; and he said to Evyatar (Abiathar) HaKohen, Bring here the Ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:15–16; SHMUEL ALEF 23:8; BERESHIS 18:24"> Then said Dovid, Hashem Elohei Yisroel, Thy eved hath certainly heard that Sha'ul seeketh to come to Ke'ilah, to destroy the ir (town) on my account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 33:3; TEHILLIM 50:15; MATTITYAHU 7:7–8"> Will the Ba'alei Ke'ilah (citizens of Ke'ilah) deliver me up into his yad? Will Sha'ul come down, as Thy eved hath heard? Hashem Elohei Yisroel, tell now Thy eved. And Hashem said, He will come down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 23:20; TEHILLIM 31:8; TEHILLIM 62:1"> Then said Dovid, Will the Baalei Ke'ilah shut me and my anashim up into the yad Sha'ul? And Hashem said, They will deliver thee up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 22:2; SHMUEL ALEF 25:13; SHMUEL ALEF 30:9–10"> Then Dovid and his anashim, which were about shesh me'ot ish, arose and departed out of Ke'ilah, and went wherever they could go. And it was told Sha'ul that Dovid was escaped from Ke'ilah; and he refrained from going forth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 54:3–4; TEHILLIM 32:7; YEHOSHUA 15:24"> And Dovid stayed in the midbar in metzadot (strongholds, masadas) and remained on a har in the midbar Ziph. And Sha'ul searched for him every day, but Elohim delivered him not into his yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Dovid saw that Sha'ul was come out to seek his nefesh: and Dovid was in the midbar Ziph at Chorshah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YESHAYAH 35:3–4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:12–13; IYOV 4:3–4"> And Yonatan Ben Sha'ul arose, and went to Dovid at Chorshah, and strengthened his yad in Elohim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., encouraged him in G-d">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 20:31; SHMUEL ALEF 24:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:16"> And he said unto him, Fear not; for the yad Sha'ul Avi shall not find thee; and thou shalt be Melech over Yisroel, and I shall be mishneh (second) unto thee; and that also Sha'ul Avi knoweth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 21:7; SHMUEL ALEF 20:42; SHMUEL ALEF 18:3"> And they two made a brit (covenant) before Hashem; and Dovid stayed at Chorshah, and Yonatan went to his bais.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 26:1; SHMUEL ALEF 26:3; TEHILLIM 54:1–7"> Then came up the Ziphim to Sha'ul to Giv'ah, saying, Doth not Dovid hide himself among us in metzadot (strongholds, masadas) at Chorshah, in the hill of Chakhilah, which is on the south of Yeshimon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 23:12; MISHLE 29:26; MISHLE 11:23"> Now therefore, O Melech, come down according to all the desire of thy nefesh to come down; and our part shall be to deliver him into the yad HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 22:8; YESHAYAH 66:5; MICHOH 3:11"> And Sha'ul said, Berukhim atem l'Hashem (Blessed be ye of Hashem); for ye have compassion on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="IYOV 5:13"> Now go, prepare further, and know and see his makom where his hideouts are, and who hath seen him there; for it is told me that he dealeth very craftily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:15–16; MARKOS 14:1; BAMIDBAR 10:36"> See therefore, and take knowledge of all the machavo'im (hideouts) where he hideth himself, and come ye again to me with the certainty, and I will go with you; it shall come to pass, if he be in the land, that I will search him out throughout all the thousands of Yehudah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:2; YEHOSHUA 15:55"> And they arose, and went to Ziph ahead of Sha'ul; but Dovid and his anashim were in the midbar Ma'on, in the Aravah on the south of Yeshimon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHOFETIM 15:8; SHMUEL ALEF 23:28"> Sha'ul also and his anashim went to seek him. When Dovid was told, he descended and came down to the Sela (Rock), and stayed in the midbar Ma'on. And when Sha'ul heard that, he pursued Dovid in the midbar Ma'on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="TEHILLIM 17:9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:8; TEHILLIM 140:1–9"> And Sha'ul went on this side of the har, and Dovid and his anashim on that side of the har; and Dovid made haste to get away for fear of Sha'ul; for Sha'ul and his anashim encircled Dovid and his anashim round about to take them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 19:9; TEHILLIM 116:3; DEVARIM 32:36"> But there came a malach unto Sha'ul, saying, Haste thee, and come; for the Pelishtim have invaded HaAretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Wherefore Sha'ul returned from pursuing after Dovid, and went against the Pelishtim; therefore they called that makom Sela HaMachlekot (Rock of Escaping).</span>
</div>
