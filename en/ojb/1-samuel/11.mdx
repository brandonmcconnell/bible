<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 12:12; MELACHIM ALEF 20:34; YECHEZKEL 17:13"> Then Nachash the Ammoni came up, and laid siege to Yavesh Gil'ad; and all the anashim of Yavesh said unto Nachash, Make a brit with us, and we will serve thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:26; BERESHIS 34:14; BAMIDBAR 16:14"> And Nachash the Ammoni answered them, On this condition will I cut a brit with you, that I may gouge out all your ayin yamin (right eyes), and so bring cherpah upon kol Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And the Zekenim of Yavesh said unto him, Give us shivat yamim that we may send malachim unto all the territory of Yisroel; and then, if there be no moshi'a to save us, we will come out and surrender to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHOFETIM 2:4; SHMUEL ALEF 10:26; SHMUEL ALEF 15:34"> Then came the malachim to Giv'at Sha'ul, and told the devarim in the oznayim of HaAm; and kol HaAm lifted up their kol (voices), and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 19:19; SHOFETIM 18:23; BERESHIS 21:17"> And, hinei, Sha'ul came after the bakar out of the sadeh; and Sha'ul said, What aileth HaAm that they weep? And they told him the divrei anshei Yavesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 10:10; SHMUEL ALEF 16:13; SHOFETIM 6:34"> And the Ruach Elohim came upon Sha'ul when he heard those devarim, and his anger was kindled greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHOFETIM 19:29; SHOFETIM 20:1; SHOFETIM 21:5–11"> And he took a tzemed bakar, and cut them in pieces, and sent them throughout all the territory of Yisroel by the hands of malachim, saying, Whosoever cometh not forth after Sha'ul and after Shmuel, so shall it be done unto his bakar. And the pachad Hashem fell on HaAm, and they came out as ish echad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHOFETIM 1:4–5; SHMUEL BAIS 24:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 17:12–19"> And when he mustered them at Bezek, the Bnei Yisroel were three hundred elef, and the Ish Yehudah thirty elef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 18:17"> And they said unto the malachim that came, Thus shall ye say unto the Ish Yavesh Gil'ad, Tomorrow, by the time the shemesh is hot, teshuah will come to you. So the malachim came and reported it to the anshei Yavesh; and they had simchah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 11:2–3"> Therefore the anshei Yavesh said, Tomorrow we will come out to surrender unto you, and ye shall do with us all that seemeth tov in your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHOFETIM 7:16; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13; SHEMOT 14:24"> And it was so on the next day, that Sha'ul set HaAm into shloshah rashim (three companies); and they came into the midst of the machaneh in the ashmoret haboker (morning watch), and slaughtered Ammon until the chom (heat) of the yom; and it came to pass, that they which survived were scattered, so that no two of them were left together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 10:27; LUKAS 19:27; TEHILLIM 21:8"> HaAm said unto Shmuel, Who is he that said, Shall Sha'ul reign as melech over us? Bring the anashim, that we may put them to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 19:22; SHMUEL ALEF 19:5; SHEMOT 14:13"> And Sha'ul said, There shall not an ish be put to death this day; for today Hashem hath wrought Teshuah (Salvation) in Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 10:8; SHMUEL ALEF 7:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:38–39"> Then said Shmuel to HaAm, Come, and let us go to Gilgal, and renew the Meluchah (kingdom) there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 10:8; SHMUEL ALEF 10:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:21–24"> And kol HaAm went to Gilgal; and there they made Sha'ul Melech before Hashem in Gilgal; and there they sacrificed zevakhim of shelamim before Hashem; and there Sha'ul and kol Anshei Yisroel rejoiced with simchah me'od.</span>
