<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:18–19; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:21; NECHEMYAH 7:2"> And it came to pass, when Shmuel was old, that he made his banim shofetim over Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:38; BERESHIS 22:19; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:28"> Now the shem of his ben habechor was Yoel; and the shem of his mishneh (second), Aviyah; they were shofetim at Be'er-Sheva.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:19; TEHILLIM 15:5; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:10"> And his banim walked not in his drakhim, but turned aside after betza (dishonest gain), and took shochad (bribe&#91;s&#93;), and perverted mishpat (justice).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 7:17; SHMUEL BAIS 5:3; SHEMOT 3:16"> Then kol Ziknei Yisroel gathered themselves together, and came to Shmuel unto Ramah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 17:14–15; HOSHEA 13:10–11; SHMUEL ALEF 8:6–8"> And said unto him, Hinei, thou art old, and thy banim walk not in thy drakhim; now appoint for us a Melech to judge us like kol HaGoyim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:11; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5; SHMUEL ALEF 12:17"> But the thing was displeasing in the eyes of Shmuel, when they said, Give us a Melech to judge us. And Shmuel davened unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKAS 10:16; SHMUEL ALEF 10:19; SHEMOT 16:8"> And Hashem said unto Shmuel, Shema unto the voice of HaAm in all that they say unto thee; for they have not rejected thee, but they have rejected Me, that I should not reign as Melech over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 106:34–40; SHEMOT 32:1; SHOFETIM 13:1"> According to all the ma'asim which they have done since the day that I brought them up out of Mitzrayim even unto this day, wherewith they have forsaken Me, and served elohim acherim, so do they also unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 10:25; SHMUEL ALEF 8:11–18; YECHEZKEL 46:18"> Now therefore pay heed unto their voice; howbeit yet protest solemnly unto them, and show them the mishpat hamelech (customary ways of the king, kingly perogative) that shall reign over them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Shmuel told all the Divrei Hashem unto the people that asked of him a Melech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 14:52; DEVARIM 17:14–20; SHMUEL ALEF 10:25"> And he said, This will be the mishpat hamelech that shall reign over you: he will take your banim, and appoint them for himself for his merkavah and his parashim; and some shall run before his merkavah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 22:7; MELACHIM ALEF 4:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 27:1–22"> And he will appoint for him sarei elafim, and sarei chamishim and will set them to plow his ground, and to reap his katzir (harvest), and to make his instruments of war, and his chariot equipment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he will take your banot to be perfumers, and to be tabakhot (cooks) and ofe'ot (bakers).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 46:18; MELACHIM ALEF 21:7; MELACHIM ALEF 21:19"> And he will take your fields, and your kramim (vineyards), and your olive groves, even the best of them, and give them to his avadim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DANIEL 1:3; YESHAYAH 39:7; BERESHIS 37:36"> And he will take the tenth of your zera, and of your kramim (vines), and give to his officers, and to his avadim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And he will take your avadim, and your shifchot, and your finest bochurim, and your donkeys, and put them to his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He will take the tenth of your tzon; and ye shall be his avadim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MICHOH 3:4; YESHAYAH 1:15; MISHLE 1:25–28"> And ye shall cry out in that day because of your melech which ye shall have chosen for you; and Hashem will not hear you in that day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 44:16; YIRMEYAH 7:13; YESHAYAH 66:4"> Nevertheless the people refused to obey the voice of Shmuel; and they said, No; but we will have a Melech over us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 8:5; TEHILLIM 106:35; DEVARIM 7:6"> That we also may be like kol HaGoyim; and that our Melech may judge us, and go out before us, and fight milchamoteinu (our battles).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHOFETIM 11:11"> And Shmuel heard all the words of the people, and he rehearsed them in the ears of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 8:7; HOSHEA 13:11"> And Hashem said to Shmuel, Pay heed unto their voice, and crown them a Melech. And Shmuel said unto the Anshei Yisroel, Go ye back every ish unto his ir (town).</span>
</div>
