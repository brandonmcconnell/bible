<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 18:1; SHMUEL ALEF 18:9"> But Yehonatan Ben Sha'ul liked Dovid me'od.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MISHLE 17:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:24; SHMUEL ALEF 18:1–3"> Yehonatan reported &#91;this&#93; to Dovid, saying, Sha'ul aviv seeketh to kill thee; now therefore be shomer in the boker, and abide baseter (in a secret place) and hide thyself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 20:9; SHMUEL ALEF 20:13"> And I will go out and stand beside avi in the sadeh where thou art, and I will speak of thee with avi; and what I see, that I will tell thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 18:20; MISHLE 31:8–9; BERESHIS 42:22"> And Yehonatan spoke tov of Dovid unto Sha'ul aviv, and said unto him, Let not HaMelech commit chet against his eved, against Dovid; because he hath not committed chet against thee, and because his ma'asim have been toward thee tov me'od:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 11:13; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:14; SHOFETIM 9:17"> For he did put his nefesh in his yad, and slaughtered the Pelishti (Philistine), and Hashem wrought Teshuah Gedolah for kol Yisroel; thou sawest it, and didst rejoice with simcha. Why then wilt thou commit chet against dahm naki (innocent blood), to slay Dovid chinom (without cause)?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MISHLE 26:24–25; YIRMEYAH 5:2; TEHILLIM 15:4"> And Sha'ul paid heed unto the voice of Yehonatan; and Sha'ul swore a shevuah, As Hashem liveth, he shall not be slain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 18:2; SHMUEL ALEF 16:21; SHMUEL ALEF 18:13"> And Yehonatan called Dovid, and Yehonatan reported to him all these devarim. And Yehonatan brought Dovid to Sha'ul, and he was in his presence, as in times past.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 27:3; TEHILLIM 18:32–50"> And there was milchamah again; and Dovid went out, and fought with the Pelishtim (Philistines), and slaughtered them with a makkah gedolah (with a great blow); and they fled from him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:14; SHMUEL ALEF 18:10–11; SHMUEL ALEF 16:16"> And there was a ruach Hashem ra'ah upon Sha'ul, as he sat in his Bais with his khanit (spear) in his yad; while Dovid menaggen (was playing music) with his yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 20:33; SHMUEL ALEF 18:11; MISHLE 21:30"> And Sha'ul sought to pin Dovid to the wall with the khanit; but he slipped away out of Sha'ulʼs presence, and he drove the khanit into the wall; and Dovid fled, and escaped that lailah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TEHILLIM 59:1–17; SHOFETIM 16:2"> Sha'ul also sent malachim unto Bais Dovid, to put him under surveillance, and to slay him in the boker; and Michal his isha warned Dovid, saying, If thou not escape with thy nefesh halailah (tonight), makhar (tomorrow) thou shalt be slain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 2:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:24–25; TEHILLIM 34:19"> So Michal let Dovid down through the chalon; and he went, and fled, and escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 31:19; SHOFETIM 18:17; SHOFETIM 18:14"> And Michal took the terafim, and laid it in the mittah, and put a piece of woven goatʼs hair at its head, and covered it with beged (cloth, garment).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 2:5; SHMUEL BAIS 16:17–19; SHMUEL BAIS 17:20"> And when Sha'ul sent malachim to capture Dovid, she said, He is choleh (ill).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MISHLE 27:3–4; TEHILLIM 37:12; IYOV 31:31"> And Sha'ul sent the malachim back to see Dovid, saying &#91;to them&#93;, Bring him up to me in the mittah (bed), to have him slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And when the malachim were come in, hinei, there was the terafim in the mittah, with a piece of woven goatsʼ hair at its head.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 2:22; SHMUEL ALEF 22:17; SHEMOT 1:17–19"> And Sha'ul said unto Michal, Why hast thou so deceived me, and sent away mine oyev, that he is escaped? And Michal answered Sha'ul, He said unto me, Let me get away; why should I kill thee?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 7:17; TEHILLIM 116:11; SHMUEL ALEF 15:34"> So Dovid fled, and escaped, and came to Shmuel at Ramah, and told him all that Sha'ul had done to him. And he and Shmuel went and dwelt in the dwellings of the nevi'im.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 23:19; SHMUEL ALEF 22:9–10; SHMUEL ALEF 26:1"> And it was told Sha'ul, saying, Hinei, Dovid is at the dwellings of the nevi'im in Ramah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YOEL 2:28; YOCHANAN 7:32; SHMUEL ALEF 10:5–6"> And Sha'ul sent malachim to capture Dovid; and when they saw the kat (sect, group) of the nevi'im prophesying, and Shmuel standing as nitzav (overseeing) them, the Ruach Elohim was upon the malachim of Sha'ul, and they also prophesied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MISHLE 27:22; YIRMEYAH 13:23; YOEL 2:28"> And when it was told Sha'ul, he sent other malachim, and they prophesied likewise. And Sha'ul sent malachim again shelishim (third ones) and they prophesied also.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then went he also to Ramah, and came to a bor hagadol (great pit) at Sekhu; and he asked and said, Where are Shmuel and Dovid? And one said, Hinei, they are at the dwellings of the nevi'im in Ramah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MISHLE 16:9; SHMUEL ALEF 10:10; SHMUEL ALEF 19:20"> And he went there to the dwellings of the nevi'im at Ramah; and the Ruach Elohim was upon him also, and he walked along prophesying, until he came to the dwellings of the nevi'im at Ramah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 6:20; YESHAYAH 20:2; MICHOH 1:8"> And he stripped off his beged also, and prophesied before Shmuel in like manner, and lay down arom (naked) all that day and all that lailah. Thus they say, Is Sha'ul also among the nevi'im?</span>
</div>
