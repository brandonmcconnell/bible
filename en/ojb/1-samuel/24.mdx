<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 23:28–29; YECHEZKEL 22:9; SHMUEL ALEF 23:19"> And Dovid went up from there, and dwelt in metzadot at Ein-Gedi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 26:2; TEHILLIM 104:18; TEHILLIM 37:32"> And it came to pass, when Sha'ul was returned from following the Pelishtim, that it was told him, saying, Hinei, Dovid is in the midbar of Ein-Gedi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHOFETIM 3:24; TEHILLIM 57:1–11; TEHILLIM 142:1–7"> Then Sha'ul took shloshet alafim (three thousand) chosen men out of kol Yisroel, and went to search for Dovid and his anashim upon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the area known as">a</a></sup>Wild Goat Rocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 26:8–11; SHMUEL ALEF 24:10; SHMUEL ALEF 26:23"> And he came to the gidrot hatzon (sheep pens of the flock) on the derech, where was a me'arah (cave); and Sha'ul went in to cover his feet,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., relieve himself">b</a></sup>and Dovid and his anashim remained in the far end of the me'arah (cave).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 24:10; MELACHIM BAIS 22:19; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:20–21"> And the anashim of Dovid said unto him, Hinei, the day of which Hashem said unto thee, Hinei, I will deliver thine oyev (enemy) into thine yad, that thou mayest do to him as it shall seem tov unto thee. Then Dovid arose, and cut off the corner of the me'il of Sha'ul without his notice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 1:14; IYOV 31:29–30; SHMUEL ALEF 26:9–11"> And it came to pass afterward, that the lev Dovid smote him because he had cut off the corner of Sha'ul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:44; TEHILLIM 7:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:17–21"> And he said unto his anashim, Chalilah (far be it) before Hashem that I should do this thing unto adoni, Hashemʼs moshiach, to stretch forth mine yad against him, seeing he is the moshiach of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:23–24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:7; SHEMOT 20:12"> So Dovid restrained his anashim with these devarim, and allowed them not to rise against Sha'ul. But Sha'ul rose up out of the me'arah (cave), and went on his derech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="KOHELET 7:21–22; MISHLE 29:12; MISHLE 16:28"> Dovid also arose afterward, and went out of the me'arah, and called after Sha'ul, saying, Adoni HaMelech. And when Sha'ul looked behind him, Dovid bowed down with his face to the ground, and prostrated himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 105:15; SHMUEL ALEF 24:4; SHMUEL ALEF 26:8–9"> And Dovid said to Sha'ul, Wherefore hearest thou divrei adam, saying, Hinei, Dovid seeketh thy hurt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 26:20; SHMUEL ALEF 23:14; SHMUEL ALEF 23:23"> Hinei, this day thine eyes have seen how that Hashem had delivered thee today into mine yad in the me'arah; and some urged me kill thee; but mine &#91;nefesh&#93; spared thee; and I said, I will not put forth mine yad against adoni; for he is the moshiach of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="TEHILLIM 43:1; SHOFETIM 11:27; TEHILLIM 35:1"> Moreover, avi, see, indeed, see the corner of thy me'il (robe) in my yad; for in that I cut off the corner of thy me'il, and killed thee not, know thou and see that there is neither ra'ah nor peysha (rebellion) in mine yad, and I have not sinned against thee; yet thou huntest my nefesh to take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:20; MATTITYAHU 7:16–18; MATTITYAHU 12:33–34"> Hashem judge between me and thee, and Hashem avenge me on thee; but mine yad shall not be upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 26:20; SHMUEL ALEF 17:43; SHMUEL BAIS 9:8"> As saith the mashal (proverb) of the ancients, Resha (wickedness) proceedeth from the resha'im; but mine yad shall not be upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 43:1; TEHILLIM 35:1; TEHILLIM 119:154"> After whom is the Melech Yisroel come out? After whom dost thou pursue? After a dead kelev (dog), after a parosh (flea).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 26:17; BERESHIS 33:4; LUKAS 21:15"> Hashem therefore be judge, and judge between me and thee, and see, and uphold my cause, and deliver me out of thine yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:44; SHMUEL ALEF 26:21; BERESHIS 38:26"> And it came to pass, when Dovid had made an end of speaking these devarim unto Sha'ul, that Sha'ul said, Is this thy voice, beni Dovid? And Sha'ul lifted up his voice, and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 26:23; TEHILLIM 31:8; SHMUEL ALEF 23:12"> And he said to Dovid, Thou art more tzaddik than I; for thou hast rewarded me tovah, whereas I have rewarded thee ra'ah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="TEHILLIM 18:20; SHMUEL ALEF 23:21; MISHLE 25:21–22"> And thou hast shown this day how that thou hast dealt tovah with me; forasmuch as when Hashem had delivered me into thine yad, thou killedst me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 23:17; MATTITYAHU 2:3–6; SHMUEL BAIS 3:17–18"> For if an ish find his oyev, will he let him go bederech tovah? Wherefore Hashem reward thee tovah for that thou hast done unto me this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 21:23; SHMUEL BAIS 21:6–8; SHMUEL ALEF 20:14–17"> And now, hinei, I know that thou shalt surely be Melech, and that the Mamlechet Yisroel shall be established in thine yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 23:29; YOCHANAN 2:24; MATTITYAHU 10:16–17"> Swear now therefore unto me by Hashem, that thou wilt not cut off my zera after me, and that thou wilt not destroy my shem out of the Bais Avi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Dovid swore unto Sha'ul. And Sha'ul went to his bais; but Dovid and his anashim went up unto the metzudah (stronghold, masada).</span>
