<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 14:51; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 9:36–39"> Now there was an ish of Binyamin, shmo Kish Ben Aviel Ben Tzeror Ben Bechorat Ben Aphiach, an ish of Binyamin, a gibbor chayil (man of valor).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 6:2; BAMIDBAR 13:33; SHMUEL ALEF 16:7"> And he had a ben, shmo Sha'ul, a handsome bochur; and there was not among the Bnei Yisroel one more handsome than he; he was head and shoulders taller than kol HaAm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHOFETIM 5:10; SHMUEL ALEF 10:2; SHOFETIM 10:4"> And the donkeys of Kish Avi Sha'ul were lost. And Kish said to Sha'ul bno, Take now one of the ne'arim with thee, and arise, go look for the donkeys.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:42; YEHOSHUA 24:33; YOCHANAN 3:23"> And he passed through har Ephrayim, and passed through Eretz Shalishah, but they found them not; then they passed through Eretz Sha'alim, and they were not there; and he passed through the territory of Binyamin, but they found them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 10:2; SHMUEL ALEF 1:1; LUKAS 12:11"> And when they were come to Eretz Tzuph, Sha'ul said to his na'ar that was with him, Come, and let us return; lest Avi stop caring for the donkeys, and become concerned for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 13:1; DEVARIM 33:1; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:11"> And he said unto him, Hinei now, there is in this town an Ish Elohim, an ish nikhbad (an honorable man); all that he saith cometh surely to pass; now let us go there; perhaps he can show us darkeinu (our way) that we should go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 14:3; MELACHIM BAIS 8:8; MELACHIM BAIS 4:42"> Then said Sha'ul to his na'ar, But, hinei, if we go, what shall we bring the Ish? For the lechem is all gone in our vessels, and there is not a teshurah (gift) for the Ish HaElohim: what have we?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the na'ar answered Sha'ul again, and said, Hinei, I have here at hand the fourth part of a shekel of kesef; that will I give to the Ish HaElohim, to tell us darkeinu (our way).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YESHAYAH 30:10; MELACHIM BAIS 17:13; AMOS 7:12"> Beforetime in Yisroel, when an ish went to inquire of Elohim, thus he spoke, Come, and let us go to the ro'eh (seer): for he that is now called a navi was beforetime called a ro'eh (seer).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 5:13–14"> Then said Sha'ul to his na'ar, Well said; come, let us go. So they went unto the town where the Ish HaElohim was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 24:11; SHEMOT 2:16; BERESHIS 24:18–20"> And as they went up the hill to the town, they found ne'arot (girls) going out to draw mayim, and said unto them, Is the ro'eh here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 31:54; SHMUEL ALEF 16:2; SHMUEL ALEF 10:5"> And they answered them, and said, He is; hinei, he is ahead of you; make haste now, for he came today to the town; for there is a zevach (sacrifice) for HaAm today in the bamah (high place);</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:11; MATTITYAHU 26:26; LUKAS 24:30"> As soon as ye be come into the town, ye shall straightway find him, before he go up to the high place to eat; for HaAm will not eat until he come, because hu yevarech hazevach (he doth bless the sacrifice); and afterwards hakeru'im (the ones being invited) eat. Now therefore get you up; for about this time ye shall find him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And they went up into the town; and when they were come into the town, hinei, Shmuel came out toward them as he was going up to the bamah (high place).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:21; SHMUEL ALEF 15:1; IYOV 33:16"> Now Hashem galah (had revealed, unveiled) in the ozen (ear) of Shmuel yom echad before Sha'ul came, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 10:1; TEHILLIM 106:44; SHEMOT 3:7–9"> Makhar (tomorrow) about this time I will send thee an ish out of Eretz Binyamin, and umeshachto (thou shalt anoint him) to be Nagid over My people Yisroel, that he may save My people out of the yad Pelishtim; for I have looked upon My people, because their cry has come unto Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HOSHEA 13:11; SHMUEL ALEF 16:6–12; NECHEMYAH 13:25"> And when Shmuel saw Sha'ul, Hashem said unto him, Hinei haIsh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Zech 6:12">a</a></sup>whom I spoke to thee of! This same shall rule over My people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Then Sha'ul drew near to Shmuel in the sha'ar, and said, Tell me, now, where is the Bais HaRo'eh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:25; YOCHANAN 4:29"> And Shmuel answered Sha'ul, and said, I am the Ro'eh (seer): go up before me unto the bamah (high place); for ye shall eat with me hayom, and baboker I will let thee go, and will tell thee all that is in thine levav.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 8:5; SHMUEL ALEF 12:13; SHMUEL ALEF 9:3"> And as for thine donkeys that were lost hayom sheloshet hayamim (now three days), set not thy mind on them; for they are found. And on whom is kol chemdat Yisroel (all the Desire of Yisroel)?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Hag 2:7">b</a></sup>Is it not on thee, and on Kol Bais Avicha?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:17; SHOFETIM 20:46–48; TEHILLIM 68:27"> And Sha'ul answered and said, Am not I of Binyamin, of the smallest of the Shivtei Yisroel? And my mishpakhat the least of all the mishpekhot of the Shivtei Binyamin? Wherefore then speakest thou so to me?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BERESHIS 43:32; LUKAS 14:10"> And Shmuel took Sha'ul and his na'ar, and brought them into the room, and made them sit at the makom berosh (chief place) among hakeru'im (the ones being invited), which were about shloshim ish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 1:5; BERESHIS 43:34"> And Shmuel said unto the tabakh (cook), Bring the portion which I gave thee, of which I said unto thee, Set it aside for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 24:4; VAYIKRA 7:32–33; SHEMOT 29:22"> And the tabakh took up the shok (thigh piece), and that which was upon it, and set it before Sha'ul. And Shmuel said, Hinei that which is set aside! Set it before thee, and eat; for unto this mo'ed hath it been set aside for thee since I said, I have invited HaAm. So Sha'ul did eat with Shmuel that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:9; DEVARIM 22:8; SHMUEL BAIS 11:2"> And when they were come down from the bamah (high place) into the town, Shmuel communed with Sha'ul upon the gag (rooftop).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="BERESHIS 44:4; YEHOSHUA 7:13; SHOFETIM 19:28"> And they arose early; and it came to pass about shachar, that Shmuel called Sha'ul to the gag (rooftop), saying, Up, that I may send thee away. And Sha'ul arose, and they went out both of them, he and Shmuel, outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:14–15; SHMUEL ALEF 20:38–39; MELACHIM BAIS 9:5–6"> And as they were going down to the outskirts of the town, Shmuel said to Sha'ul, Bid the na'ar pass on before us (and he passed on), but stand thou still a while, that I may show thee the Devar Elohim.</span>
</div>
</div>
