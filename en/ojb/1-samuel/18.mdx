<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:15; SHMUEL ALEF 16:21–23"> And Sha'ul drafted him that day, and would let him return no more to the bais aviv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 20:8–17; SHMUEL ALEF 20:42; SHMUEL BAIS 21:7"> Then Yonatan and Dovid made a brit (covenant), because he loved him as his own nefesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:42; LUKAS 15:22; ESTHER 6:8–9"> And Yonatan stripped himself of the me'il (robe) that was upon him, and gave it to Dovid, and his middah (war garments), even to his cherev, and to his keshet, and to his khagor (belt).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 18:30; SHMUEL ALEF 18:14–15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:5"> And Dovid went out whithersoever Sha'ul sent him, and prospered; and Sha'ul set him over the anshei hamilchamah, and he was accepted in the sight of kol HaAm, and also in the sight of the avdei Sha'ul.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 15:20; SHOFETIM 11:34; TEHILLIM 68:25"> And it came to pass as they came, when Dovid was returned from the slaughter of the Pelishti, that the nashim came out of all towns of Yisroel, singing and dancing, to meet Sha'ul HaMelech, with tambourines, with simchah, and with cymbals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 21:11; SHMUEL ALEF 29:5; SHEMOT 15:21"> And the nashim answered one another as they played, and said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Sha'ul hath slain his alafim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Dovid his revavot (myriads).</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:28; MISHLE 13:10; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:5"> And Sha'ul was very angry, and the saying was displeasing in his eyes and he said, They have ascribed unto Dovid revavot, and to me they have ascribed but alafim; and what can he have more but the maluchah (kingship)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MARKOS 7:22; BERESHIS 31:2; MATTITYAHU 20:15"> And there was jealous suspicion in the way Sha'ul eyed Dovid from that day forward.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 19:9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:11; MELACHIM ALEF 18:29"> And it came to pass on the next day, that the ruach Elohim ra'ah came upon Sha'ul, and he prophesied inside the bais; and Dovid made<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="kinnor">a</a></sup>music with his yad, as at other times; and there was a khanit in the yad Sha'ul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 20:33; YESHAYAH 54:17; LUKAS 4:30"> And Sha'ul hurled the khanit; for he said, I will pin Dovid even to the wall with it. And Dovid escaped his presence twice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 18:15; SHMUEL ALEF 18:29; SHMUEL ALEF 16:13–14"> And Sha'ul was afraid of Dovid, because Hashem was with him, and was departed from Sha'ul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 5:2; TEHILLIM 121:8; SHMUEL ALEF 18:16–17"> Therefore Sha'ul removed him from him, and made him his sar elef; and he went out and came in before HaAm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 39:23; MATTITYAHU 28:20; SHMUEL ALEF 16:18"> And Dovid was maskil (successful) in kol drakhim of him; and Hashem was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:5; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5"> Wherefore when Sha'ul saw that he was maskil me'od, he was afraid of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 18:5; BAMIDBAR 27:17; MELACHIM ALEF 3:7"> But kol Yisroel and Yehudah loved Dovid, because he went out and came in before them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:25; SHMUEL ALEF 25:28; SHMUEL ALEF 18:25"> And Sha'ul said to Dovid, Hinei bitti hagedolah Merav, her will I give thee as isha; only be thou ben chayil (valiant) for me, and fight Hashemʼs milchamot. For Sha'ul said, Let not mine yad be upon him, but let the yad Pelishtim be upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 7:18; SHMUEL ALEF 9:21; SHMUEL ALEF 18:23"> And Dovid said unto Sha'ul, Who am I? And what is my life, or the mishpakhat avi in Yisroel, that I should be Choson to HaMelech?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHOFETIM 7:22; SHMUEL BAIS 21:8; SHOFETIM 14:20"> But it came to pass at the time when Merav Bat Sha'ul should have been given to Dovid, that she was given as isha unto Adriel the Mecholati.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 18:28"> And Michal Bat Sha'ul loved Dovid; and they told Sha'ul, and the thing was yashar in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Sha'ulʼs">b</a></sup>eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 18:17; SHEMOT 10:7; YIRMEYAH 9:8"> And Sha'ul said, I will give him her, that she may be a mokesh to him, and that the yad Pelishtim may be against him. Wherefore Sha'ul said to Dovid, Thou shalt this day become Choson to me by one of my two.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="TEHILLIM 36:1–3; TEHILLIM 55:21; SHMUEL BAIS 13:28–29"> And Sha'ul commanded his avadim, saying, Commune with Dovid privately, and say, Hinei, HaMelech hath chafetz (delight) in thee, and all his avadim love thee; now therefore be Choson to HaMelech.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:141; MISHLE 14:20; KOHELET 9:15–16"> And the avadim of Sha'ul spoke those devarim in the ears of Dovid. And Dovid said, Seemeth it a light thing in your eyes to become Choson to HaMelech, seeing that I am a poor man, and lightly esteemed?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And the avadim of Sha'ul told him, saying, According to these words spoke Dovid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BERESHIS 34:12; SHMUEL ALEF 18:17; SHMUEL ALEF 14:24"> And Sha'ul said, Thus shall ye say to Dovid, HaMelech has chafetz (desire) for not any mohar (dowry, bride price) but a hundred arelot (foreskins) of the Pelishtim, to be avenged of the oyevei HaMelech. But Sha'ul schemed to make Dovid fall by the yad Pelishtim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 18:21"> And when his avadim told Dovid these devarim, it pleased Dovid well to be Choson of HaMelech; and the yamim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="leading up to the due-date of the arelot">c</a></sup>were not expired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:14; SHOFETIM 14:19; SHMUEL ALEF 18:13"> Wherefore Dovid arose and went, he and his anashim, and slaughtered of the Pelishtim (Philistines) two hundred ish; and Dovid brought their arelot, and they gave them in full count to HaMelech, that he might be the Choson of HaMelech. And Sha'ul gave him Michal bitto as isha.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 24:20; SHMUEL ALEF 26:25; BERESHIS 37:8–11"> And Sha'ul saw and knew that Hashem was with Dovid, and that Michal Bat Sha'ul loved him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="KOHELET 4:4; YOCHANAN 11:53; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:19"> And Sha'ul was yet the more afraid of Dovid; and Sha'ul became oyev to Dovid kol hayamim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 18:5; SHMUEL BAIS 11:1; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:4"> Then the sarim of the Pelishtim went forth &#91;to fight&#93;; and it came to pass, after they went forth, that Dovid prospered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="in military success">d</a></sup>more than all the avadim (officers) of Sha'ul; so that shmo became esteemed as of good reputation. And Sha'ul spoke to Yonatan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Yehonaton">e</a></sup>bno, and to all his avadim, that they should kill Dovid.</span>
</div>
