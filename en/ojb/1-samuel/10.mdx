<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:13; TEHILLIM 78:71; SHMUEL ALEF 9:16"> Then Shmuel took a flask of shemen, and poured it upon his rosh, and kissed him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ps 2:12">a</a></sup>and said, Hath not Hashem meshachacha (anointed thee) to be Nagid over His nachalah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 9:3–5; BERESHIS 35:19–20; YEHOSHUA 18:28"> When thou art departed from me today, then thou shalt find two anashim near Kevurat Rachel on the border of Binyamin at Tzeltzach; and they will say unto thee, The donkeys which thou wentest to seek are found; and, hinei, Avicha hath taken leave of caring about the donkeys, and careth for you, saying, What shall I do for beni (my son)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 35:3; BERESHIS 35:1; BERESHIS 28:22"> Then shalt thou go on forward from there, and thou shalt come to the plain of Tavor, and there shall meet thee shloshah anashim going up to HaElohim to Beit-El, one carrying shloshet gedayim (three young goats), and another carrying shloshet kikrot (three loaves) lechem, and another carrying a skin of yayin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHOFETIM 18:15"> And they will give thee a Shalom greeting, and give thee two of the lechem; which thou shalt receive of their yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 3:15; SHMUEL ALEF 13:3; SHMUEL ALEF 19:20"> After that thou shalt come to the Giveat HaElohim, where is the outpost of the Pelishtim (Philistines); and it shall come to pass, when thou art come there to the Ir, that thou shalt meet a chevel (company, band) of nevi'im coming down from the high place with a nevel (lyre), tof (tambourine), khalil (flute), and kinnor (harp) before them; and they shall prophesy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 10:10; SHMUEL ALEF 19:23–24; BAMIDBAR 11:25"> And the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hakodesh">b</a></sup>of Hashem will come upon thee, and thou shalt prophesy with them, and shalt be changed into an ish acher (another man, a different man).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHOFETIM 6:12; YEHOSHUA 1:5; YESHAYAH 7:14"> And let it be, when these otot (signs) are come unto thee, that thou do what yadecha (thy hand) finds;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e., what thou seest fit to do">c</a></sup>for HaElohim is with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 11:14–15; SHMUEL ALEF 13:4; SHMUEL ALEF 13:8–15"> And thou shalt go down before me to Gilgal; and, hinei, I will come down unto thee, to offer olot (burnt offerings), and to sacrifice zivkhei shelamim (sacrifices of peace offerings); shivat yamim shalt thou tarry, till I come to thee, and show thee what thou shalt do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YESHAYAH 38:7–8; SHMUEL ALEF 10:2–6; SHOFETIM 7:11"> And it was so, that when he had turned his back to go from Shmuel, Elohim changed for him a lev acher (another heart, a different heart); and all those otot (signs) came to pass that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 10:5–6; SHMUEL ALEF 19:20–24"> And when they came there to the Giv'ah (Hill), hinei, a chevel (company, band) of nevi'im met him; and the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hakodesh">d</a></sup>of Elohim came upon him, and he prophesied among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 19:24; YOCHANAN 7:15; MATTITYAHU 13:54–55"> And it came to pass, when all that knew him beforetime saw that, hinei, he prophesied with the nevi'im, then HaAm said one to another, What is this that hath happened unto the Ben Kish? Is Sha'ul also among the nevi'im?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 54:13; YOCHANAN 6:45; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:17"> And an ish from that place answered and said, And who is avihem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="cf. Am 7:14">e</a></sup>(their father)? Therefore it became a mashal (proverb), Is Sha'ul also among the nevi'im?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And when he had made an end of prophesying, he came to the bamah (high place).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 14:50; MELACHIM BAIS 5:25; SHMUEL ALEF 9:3–10"> And the dod Sha'ul said unto him and to his na'ar, Where went ye? And he said, To search for the donkeys; and when we saw that they were nowhere, we came to Shmuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the dod Sha'ul said, Tell me, now, what Shmuel said unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MISHLE 29:11; SHOFETIM 14:6; SHMUEL ALEF 9:20"> And Sha'ul said unto his dod, He told us plainly that the donkeys were found. But of the matter of the melukhah (kingship), whereof Shmuel spoke, he told him not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHOFETIM 20:1; SHMUEL ALEF 7:5–6"> And Shmuel called HaAm together unto Hashem at Mitzpah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHOFETIM 6:8–9; NECHEMYAH 9:27–28; SHOFETIM 2:1"> And said unto the Bnei Yisroel, Thus saith Hashem Elohei Yisroel, I brought up Yisroel out of Mitzrayim, and delivered you out of the yad Mitzrayim, and out of the yad kol HaMamlachot, and of them that oppressed you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 12:12; SHMUEL ALEF 8:19; YEHOSHUA 24:1"> And ye have this day rejected Eloheichem, who Himself was Moshi'a for you out of all your adversities and your tzoroses; and ye have said unto Him, No, but set a Melech over us. Now therefore present yourselves before Hashem by your Shevatim (Tribes), and by your alefim (thousands, larger kinship units).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 7:16–18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:24–26; SHMUEL ALEF 14:41"> And when Shmuel had caused all the Shivtei Yisroel to come near, the Shevet (Tribe) of Binyamin was lakhad (taken &#91;by lot&#93;, singled out &#91;by lot&#93;).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> When he had caused the Shevet (Tribe) of Binyamin to come near by their mishpekhot, the mishpakhat Matri was lakhad (singled out &#91;by lot&#93;), and Sha'ul Ben Kish was lakhad; and when they searched for him, he could not be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 27:21; SHOFETIM 20:18; SHMUEL ALEF 23:2–4"> Therefore they inquired of Hashem further, Has the ish come here yet? And Hashem answered, Hinei he hath hid himself among the kelim (vessels, baggage, stuff).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 9:2; SHMUEL ALEF 16:7; SHMUEL ALEF 17:4"> And they ran and brought him out from there; and when he stood among HaAm, he was taller than any of HaAm from his shekhamim (shoulders) upward.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:25; MELACHIM ALEF 1:39; SHMUEL BAIS 21:6"> And Shmuel said to kol HaAm, See ye him whom Hashem hath chosen? That there is none like him among kol HaAm? And kol HaAm shouted, and said, May HaMelech live!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 8:11–18; DEVARIM 17:14–20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:1–7"> Then Shmuel told HaAm the mishpat haMelukhah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="see also Dt 17:14-20">f</a></sup>(right of the monarchy), and wrote it in a sefer, and deposited it up before Hashem. And Shmuel sent kol HaAm away, every ish to his bais.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 11:4; YEHOSHUA 18:28; SHMUEL ALEF 15:34"> And Sha'ul also went home to Giv'ah; and there went with him the valiant whose lev Elohim had touched.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEVARIM 13:13; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 17:5; MELACHIM ALEF 10:25"> But the Bnei Beliyaal said, How shall this one yoshi'einu (save us)? And they despised him, and brought no minchah (gift). But he held his peace.</span>
</div>
