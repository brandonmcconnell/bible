<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:12; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:21"> And Dovid fled from Nayot in Ramah, and came and said before Yonatan, What have I done? What is mine avon? And what is my chattat before avicha, that he seeketh my nefesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 9:15; YOCHANAN 17:8; YOCHANAN 15:15"> And he said unto him, Chalilah (may it never be!); thou shalt not die: hinei, avi will do nothing either gadol or katon, but that he will reveal it to me; and why should avi hide this thing from me? It is not so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:26; DEVARIM 6:13; MELACHIM BAIS 2:6"> And Dovid took an oath, moreover, and said, Avicha certainly knoweth that I have found chen in thine eyes; and he saith, Let not Yonatan know this, lest he be grieved; but truly as Hashem liveth, and as thy nefesh liveth, there is hardly a peysah (step) between me and mavet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then said Yonatan unto Dovid, Whatsoever thy nefesh saith, I will even do it for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 10:10; SHMUEL ALEF 19:2; BAMIDBAR 28:11"> And Dovid said unto Yonatan, Hinei, makhar (tomorrow) is Rosh Chodesh, and I should not fail to sit with HaMelech for tish; but let me go, that I may hide myself in the sadeh unto the erev hashlishit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:58; SHMUEL ALEF 9:12; DEVARIM 12:5"> If avicha at all miss me, then say, Dovid earnestly asked leave of me that he might run to Beit-Lechem his town; for there is a zevach hayamim there for kol mishpakhat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:17; ESTHER 7:7; DEVARIM 1:23"> If he say thus, It is tov; thy eved shall have shalom; but if he be very wrathful, then be sure that ra'ah is determined by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 14:32; SHMUEL ALEF 18:3; SHMUEL ALEF 23:18"> Therefore thou shalt deal with chesed unto thy eved; for thou hast brought thy eved into Brit Hashem with thee; notwithstanding, if there be in me avon (iniquity), slay me thyself; for why shouldest thou bring me to avicha?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 20:42; SHMUEL ALEF 19:2; SHMUEL ALEF 20:38"> And Yonatan said, Chalilah (far be it) from thee; for if I had da'as certainly that ra'ah were determined by Avi to come upon thee, then would not I tell it thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:14; SHMUEL ALEF 25:17; BERESHIS 42:30"> Then said Dovid to Yonatan, Who shall tell me? Or what if avicha answer thee roughly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Yonatan said unto Dovid, Come, and let us go out into the sadeh. And they went out both of them into the sadeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 22:22; IYOV 31:4; MISHLE 25:2–3"> And Yonatan said unto Dovid, Hashem Elohei Yisroel, when I have sounded out Avi at this time on Yom HaShishi from now, and, hinei, if there be tov toward Dovid, and I then send not unto thee, and reveal it in thy ozen (ear);</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="RUTH 1:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:11; YEHOSHUA 1:5"> Hashem do so and much more to Yonatan; but if it please Avi to do thee ra'ah, then I will reveal it in thy ozen (ear), and send thee away, that thou mayest go in shalom; and Hashem be with thee, as He hath been with Avi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 9:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:1–2"> And thou shalt not only while yet I live show me the chesed Hashem, that I die not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 21:7; SHMUEL ALEF 24:21; SHMUEL BAIS 9:1–7"> But also thou shalt not cut off thy chesed from my bais ad olam; no, not when Hashem hath cut off the oyevim of Dovid, every one from the pnei ha'adamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:22; SHMUEL ALEF 18:3; SHMUEL BAIS 21:8"> So Yonatan made Brit with the Bais Dovid, saying, Let Hashem even require it at the hands of the oyevim of Dovid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MISHLE 18:24; SHMUEL ALEF 18:1; SHMUEL ALEF 18:3"> And Yonatan caused Dovid to swear a shevua again, because he loved him; for he loved him with the ahavat nafsho (with the love of his own nefesh, self).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 20:25; SHMUEL ALEF 20:5"> Then Yonatan said to Dovid, Makhar (tomorrow) is Rosh Chodesh; and thou shalt be missed, because thy moshav (seat) will be empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 19:2; SHMUEL ALEF 20:5"> And when thou hast stayed three days, then thou shalt go down quickly, and come to the makom (place) where thou didst hide thyself on the day of the ma'aseh (deed),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e. the eventful earlier day, see 20:12">a</a></sup>and shalt remain by the even (stone) Ezel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And I will shoot three khitzim (arrows) on the side thereof, as though I shot at a matarah (target).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 5:2; AMOS 8:14; YIRMEYAH 12:16"> And, hinei, I will send a na'ar, saying, Go, find the khitzim (arrows). If I expressly say unto the na'ar, Hinei, the khitzim (arrows) are on this side of thee, take them; then come thou, for there is shalom to thee, and no davar (problem, concern); as Hashem liveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 20:37"> But if I say thus unto the elem (youth, young man), Hinei, the khitzim (arrows) are beyond thee, go thy way; for Hashem hath sent thee away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 20:14–15; SHMUEL ALEF 20:42; BERESHIS 31:50"> And as touching the matter which thou and I have spoken of, hinei, Hashem be between thee and me ad olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MISHLE 4:17; YESHAYAH 1:11–15; MISHLE 21:3"> So Dovid hid himself in the sadeh; and when Rosh Chodesh was come, HaMelech sat him down to eat lechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 20:18; SHOFETIM 16:20"> And HaMelech sat upon his moshav (seat), as at other times, even upon a moshav by the wall; and Yonatan arose, and Avner sat by Sha'ulʼs side, and Dovidʼs makom (place) was empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="VAYIKRA 15:5; VAYIKRA 11:24; VAYIKRA 11:27"> Nevertheless Sha'ul spoke not anything that day; for he thought, Something hath befallen him, he is not tahor; surely he is not tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:4; SHMUEL ALEF 19:15; SHMUEL ALEF 18:11"> And it came to pass on the day after Rosh Chodesh, which was the second day of the month, that Dovidʼs makom was empty; and Sha'ul said unto Yonatan bno, Wherefore cometh not Ben Yishai to halechem neither yesterday, nor today?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 20:6"> And Yonatan answered Sha'ul, Dovid earnestly asked leave of me to go to Beit-Lechem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:28"> And he said, Let me go, now; for we have mishpakhat zevach in the Ir; and my brother, he hath commanded me to be there; and now, if I have found chen in thine eyes, let me get away, now, and see my brethren. Therefore he cometh not unto the Shulchan HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MISHLE 21:24; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19–20; MISHLE 25:28"> Then af Sha'ul (anger of Sha'ul) was kindled against Yonatan, and he said unto him, Thou ben of the perverse rebellious woman, do not I have da'as that thou hast chosen Ben Yishai to thine own shame, and unto the shame of ervat immecha (thy motherʼs nakedness)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 19:6; SHMUEL ALEF 20:8; SHMUEL ALEF 19:11–15"> For as long as Ben Yishai liveth al ha'adamah (upon the earth) thou shalt not be established, nor malkhutecha (thy kingdom). Wherefore now send and bring him unto me, for he is ben mavet (son of death, he shall surely die).</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:23; SHMUEL ALEF 19:5; LUKAS 23:22"> And Yonatan answered Sha'ul Aviv, and said unto him, Wherefore shall he be slain? What hath he done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 20:7; SHMUEL ALEF 18:11; SHMUEL ALEF 19:10"> And Sha'ul cast the khanit (spear) at him to strike him dead; whereby Yonatan knew that it was determined of Aviv to slay Dovid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:26; MARKOS 3:5; KOHELET 7:20"> So Yonatan arose from the shulchan in chari af (fierce anger), and did eat no lechem the second day of the month; for he was grieved for Dovid, because Aviv had shamefully humiliated him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 20:5; SHMUEL ALEF 20:19"> And it came to pass in the boker, that Yonatan went out into the sadeh at the mo'ed (appointed time) with Dovid, and a na'ar katon with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 20:20–22"> And he said unto his na'ar, Run, find out now the khitzim (arrows) which I shoot. And as the na'ar ran, he shot a khetz beyond him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 20:22"> And when the na'ar was come to the makom of the khetz which Yonatan had shot, Yonatan cried after the na'ar, and said, Is not the khetz beyond thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="TEHILLIM 55:6–9; MISHLE 6:4–5; MARKOS 13:14–16"> And Yonatan cried after the na'ar, Make speed, haste, stay not. And na'ar Yonatan gathered up the khitzim (arrows), and came to his adon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> But the na'ar had no da'as of anything; only Yonatan and Dovid knew the davar (matter).</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And Yonatan gave his weapons unto his na'ar, and said unto him, Go, carry them to the Ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="BERESHIS 29:11; SHMUEL ALEF 18:3; SHMUEL BAIS 9:6"> And as soon as the na'ar was gone, Dovid arose out of a place toward the negev (south), and fell on his face to the ground, and prostrated himself three times: and they kissed one another, and wept one with another, until Dovid wept exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 1:17; SHMUEL ALEF 23:18; SHMUEL ALEF 20:22–23"> And Yonatan said to Dovid, Go in shalom, forasmuch as we have sworn both of us in the Shem of Hashem, saying, Hashem be between me and thee, and between my zera and thy zera ad olam.</span>
