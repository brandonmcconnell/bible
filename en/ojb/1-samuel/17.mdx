<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And the Pelishtim stood on the har on the one side, and Yisroel stood on the har on the other side; and there was a gey between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 11:22; SHMUEL BAIS 21:16–22; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 20:4–8"> And there went out an Ish HaBenayim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., a middle-man champion whose single combat saves the day and decides the victor">a</a></sup>out of the machanot of the Pelishtim, shmo Golyat (Goliath), of Gat, whose height was shesh cubits and a span.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he had a kova (helmet) of nechoshet upon his rosh, and he was wearing a coat of mail armor; and the weight of the coat of armor was five thousand shekels of nechoshet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:45; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:15; MELACHIM ALEF 10:16"> And he had mitzchah (greaves, i.e., leg plate armor) of nechoshet upon his legs, and a kidron (javelin) of nechoshet &#91;slung&#93; between his shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 21:19; SHMUEL ALEF 17:41; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 11:23"> And the khetz (shaft) of his khanit (spear) was like a weaverʼs beam; and his spearʼs blade weighed six hundred barzel (iron) shekels; and the no'se tzinnah (great shield bearer) walked ahead of him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 8:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:3; SHMUEL ALEF 17:26"> And he stood and cried out unto the ma'arkhot Yisroel (ranks of Israel), and said unto them, Why are ye come out to line up for milchamah? Am not I a Pelishti, and ye avadim of Sha'ul? Choose you an ish for you, and let him come down to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 11:1"> If he be able to fight with me, and to kill me, then will we be your avadim; but if I prevail over him, and kill him, then shall ye be to us for avadim, and serve us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:25–26; SHMUEL ALEF 17:45; SHMUEL ALEF 17:36"> And the Pelishti said, I defy the maarkhot Yisroel this day; give me ish, that we may fight together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 1:9; DEVARIM 31:8; MISHLE 28:1"> When Sha'ul and kol Yisroel heard those divrei haPelishti, they were dismayed, and greatly afraid.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:10–11; BERESHIS 35:19; SHMUEL ALEF 16:18"> Now Dovid was the ben ish of that Ephrati from Beit-Lechem Yehudah, shmo Yishai; and he had shmoneh banim; and the ish<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Yishai">b</a></sup>was zaken in the days of Sha'ul, advanced in age among anashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:13; SHMUEL BAIS 21:21; SHMUEL ALEF 17:28"> And the shloshet Bnei Yishai hagedolim went and followed Sha'ul to the milchamah; and the shem of his three banim that went to the milchamah were Eliav the bechor, and mishneh unto him Avinadav, and hashelishi (the third) Shammah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:11; BERESHIS 25:23"> And Dovid was the katan; and the shloshah hagedolim followed Sha'ul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:11; SHMUEL ALEF 16:19–23"> But Dovid went and returned from Sha'ul to tend tzon aviv at Beit-Lechem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:2; LUKAS 4:2"> And the Pelishti drew near morning and evening, and presented himself arba'im yom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:18; SHMUEL BAIS 17:28; RUTH 2:14"> And Yishai said unto Dovid bno, Take now for thy achim an ephah of this roasted grain, and these ten lechem, and run to the machaneh of thy achim;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:14; SHMUEL ALEF 16:20; SHMUEL BAIS 17:29"> And carry these ten charitzei hecholov (cuts of milk, hence cheeses) unto the sar of their elef (unit, thousand), and look how thy achim fare for shalom, and take their arvut (pledge).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Now Sha'ul, and they, and all the Ish Yisroel, were in the Emek (Valley) of Elah (Oak), fighting with the Pelishtim (Philistines).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 26:5; LUKAS 19:43; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:1–2"> And Dovid rose up early in the boker, and left the tzon with a shomer (watchman), and loaded up, and embarked, as Yishai had commanded him; and he came to the camp, as the army was going forth to the fight and shouted for the milchamah (battle).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For Yisroel and the Pelishtim took their positions, ma'arakhah (battle array) facing ma'arakhah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKAS 10:5–6; SHOFETIM 18:15; YESHAYAH 10:28"> And Dovid left his supplies in the yad of the shomer hakelim (supply master), and ran into the ma'arakhah (battle line), and came and gave shalom greeting to his achim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:8–10"> And as he talked with them, hinei, there came up the Ish HaBenayim Golyat HaPelishti shmo, from Gat, out of the lines of the Pelishtim, and spoke according to these words; and Dovid heard them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 13:6–7; VAYIKRA 26:36; YESHAYAH 7:2"> And all the Ish Yisroel, when they saw the ish, fled from before him, and were greatly afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:16; HISGALUS 2:7; HISGALUS 3:12"> And the Ish Yisroel said, Have ye seen this ish that is come up? Surely to defy Yisroel is he come up; and it shall be, that the ish who killeth him, the melech will give ashires gadol and will give him bitto (his daughter) &#91;in marriage&#93;, and bais aviv make khofshi b'Yisroel (free, tax-exempt in Israel).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEVARIM 5:26; SHMUEL ALEF 11:2; SHMUEL ALEF 14:6"> And Dovid spoke to the anashim that stood by him, saying, What shall be done for the ish that killeth this Pelishti, and taketh away the cherpah (reproach) from Yisroel? For who is this Pelishti hearel (uncircumcised Philistine) that he should defy the ma'arkhot Elohim Chayyim (arrayed battle forces of the living G-d)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:25"> And HaAm answered him after this manner, saying, So shall it be done to the ish that killeth him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:36; BERESHIS 37:11; MISHLE 18:19"> And Eliav achiv hagadol heard when he spoke unto the anashim; and af Eliav was kindled against Dovid, and he said, Why camest thou down hither? And with whom hast thou left those few tzon in the midbar? I know thy zadon (presumptuousness), and the evil of thine levav; for thou art come down l'ma'an (in order that) thou mightest watch the milchamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:15; MISHLE 15:1"> And Dovid said, What have I done now? It was only a davar (word) &#91;I said&#93;, wasn't it?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:26–27"> And he turned from him toward another, and spoke after the same manner; and HaAm answered him again according to the davar harishon (the previous word).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MISHLE 22:29"> And when the devarim (words) were heard which Dovid spoke, they rehearsed them before Sha'ul: and he sent for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:18; DEVARIM 20:1–3; TEHILLIM 3:6"> And Dovid said to Sha'ul, Let no lev adam fail because of him; thy eved will go and fight with this Pelishti.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 13:31; DEVARIM 9:2; HISGALUS 13:4"> And Sha'ul said to Dovid, Thou art not able to go against this Pelishti to fight with him: for thou art but a na'ar (boy), and he an ish milchamah (man of war) from his youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And Dovid said unto Sha'ul, Thy eved is ro'eh (shepherd) of tzon of aviv, and there came the ari, and the dov, and carried off seh out of the eder (flock):</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="AMOS 3:12; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:17–18; DANIEL 6:22"> And I went out after him, and struck him, and saved out of his mouth: and when he arose against me, I caught hold by his zakan (beard), and struck him, and killed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:38–39; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:28–29"> Thy eved slaughtered both the ari and the dov: and this Pelishti hearel (uncircumcised Philistine) shall be as one of them, seeing he hath charaf (taunted, defied, disgraced) the maarkhot Elohim Chayyim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:17–18; TEHILLIM 138:7–8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:9–10"> Dovid said moreover, Hashem that saved me out of the paw of the ari, and out of the paw of the dov, He will save me out of the yad of this Pelishti. And Sha'ul said unto Dovid, Go, and Hashem be with thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And Sha'ul dressed Dovid with his armor-clothes, put a kova of nechoshet upon his rosh; also he dressed him with a coat of mail armor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 4:6; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:4–5; HOSHEA 1:7"> And Dovid girded his cherev over his armor-clothes, and he tried to walk; for lo nissah (he had not tested, proved) it. And Dovid said unto Sha'ul, I cannot go with these; for lo nissti (I have not proved them). And Dovid took them off him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:27–29; MATTITYAHU 10:10; SHOFETIM 15:15–16"> And he took his makel (stick, staff) in his yad, and chose him chamishah chalukei avanim (five smooth stones) out of the nachal (brook), and put them in a keli haro'im (shepherdʼs bag) which he had, even in a wayfarerʼs bag; and his kela (slingshot) was in his yad; and he drew near to the Pelishti.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And the Pelishti came on and drew near unto Dovid; and the ish, the no'se hatzinnah, went ahead of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:12; SHMUEL ALEF 17:33; TEHILLIM 123:3–4"> And when the Pelishti squinted and saw Dovid, he despised him: for he was but a na'ar (boy), and admoni (ruddy), and yafeh mareh (good-looking).</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 9:8; SHMUEL BAIS 3:8; SHMUEL ALEF 24:14"> And the Pelishti said unto Dovid, Am I a kelev (dog), that thou comest to me with maklot (sticks)? And the Pelishti cursed Dovid by his g-ds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 28:9–10; MISHLE 18:12; MELACHIM ALEF 20:10–11"> And the Pelishti said to Dovid, Come to me, and I will give thy basar unto the oph hashomayim, and to the behemat hasadeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:5"> Then said Dovid to the Pelishti, Thou comest against me with a cherev (sword), and with a khanit (spear), and with a kidon (javelin); but I come against thee b'Shem Hashem Tzva'os Elohei Ma'arkhot Yisroel, whom thou hast defied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="YESHAYAH 52:10; YEHOSHUA 4:24; MELACHIM BAIS 19:19"> This day will Hashem hand thee over into mine yad; and I will strike thee down, and take thine rosh from thee; and I will give the peger machaneh Pelishtim this day unto the oph hashomayim, and to the chayyat ha'aretz; that kol ha'aretz may know that there is Elohim in Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="TEHILLIM 44:6–7; ZECHARYAH 4:6; HOSHEA 1:7"> And kol hakahal hazeh shall know that Hashem yehoshi'a (saveth) not with cherev or khanit; for the milchamah is Hashemʼs, and He will give you (plural) into yadenu (our hands).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="MISHLE 28:1; TEHILLIM 27:1"> And it came to pass, when the Pelishti arose, and came, and drew nigh to meet Dovid, that Dovid hastened, and ran toward the ma'arakhah (battle line) to meet the Pelishti.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:27–28; MELACHIM BAIS 9:24; MELACHIM ALEF 22:34"> And Dovid put his yad in his keli (bag), and took from there an even (stone), and slung it, and struck the Pelishti in his metzach (forehead), that the even (stone) sunk into his metzach; and he fell upon his face to the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="SHOFETIM 15:15; SHMUEL ALEF 17:39; SHMUEL ALEF 23:21"> So Dovid prevailed chazak over the Pelishti with a sling and with an even (stone), and struck down the Pelishti, and killed him; but there was no cherev in the yad Dovid.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:34; SHMUEL ALEF 21:9; SHMUEL BAIS 23:21"> Therefore Dovid ran, and stood over the Pelishti, and took hold of his cherev, and drew it out of the sheath thereof, and slaughtered him, v'yikhrat (and cut off)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Dan 9:26, Moshiach yikaret (will be cut off) but in Daniel the phrase means “Moshiach will be violently killed”">c</a></sup>his rosh therewith. And when the Pelishtim saw their gibbor was dead, they fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:11; YEHOSHUA 15:33–36; YEHOSHUA 15:45–46"> And the Anshei Yisroel and of Yehudah arose, and shouted, and pursued the Pelishtim, until thou come to the gey (valley), and to the Sha'arei Ekron. And the challei Pelishtim fell by the derech to Sha'arayim, even unto Gat, and unto Ekron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 4:20; MELACHIM BAIS 7:7–16; YIRMEYAH 30:16"> And the Bnei Yisroel returned from chasing after the Pelishtim, and they plundered their machanot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:33; YEHOSHUA 4:7–8; SHMUEL ALEF 21:9"> And Dovid took the rosh of the Pelishti, and brought it to Yerushalayim; but he put his kelim (weapons) in his ohel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:21–22; SHMUEL ALEF 17:58"> And when Sha'ul saw Dovid go forth to meet the Pelishti, he said unto Avner, the sar hatzavah, Avner, whose ben is this na'ar? And Avner said, As thy nefesh liveth, O Melech, I cannot tell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And the Melech said, Inquire thou whose ben this elem (young man) is.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:54"> And as Dovid returned from the slaughter of the Pelishti, Avner took him, and brought him before Sha'ul with the rosh of the Pelishti in his yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:12; SHMUEL ALEF 16:18–19"> And Sha'ul said to him, Whose ben art thou, na'ar? And Dovid answered, I am the ben avdecha Yishai of Beit-Lechem.</span>
