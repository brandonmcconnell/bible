<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:6; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12–15; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:22"> Chaverim, this now is the second iggeret I am writing to you; in these iggrot my tachlis is to arouse your sincere levavot by way of tizkoret (reminder):</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:17; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:21; LUKAS 1:70"> Remember the divrei torah having been previously spoken by the Nevi'im HaKedoshim and the mitzvot of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach">a</a></sup>Adoneinu and Moshieinu spoken by your Shluchim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:18; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:1–2; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:10"> Knowing this first, that during the acharit hayamim there will come leitzim (mockers, scorners) mocking, going after their own ta'avot (lusts),</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 17:15; MALACHI 2:17; YIRMEYAH 5:12–13"> And saying, “Where is the havtachah (promise) of the Bias HaMoshiach? From the time Avoteinu fell asleep, all things continue as they were from the beginning of HaBri'ah (Creation).”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:3; BERESHIS 1:9; TEHILLIM 24:2"> For this is hidden from them (but they want it that way): that the Shomayim existed from long ago and ha'Aretz out of mayim and through mayim BIDVAR Hashem NA'ASU<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 33:6">b</a></sup>(By the word of the L-rd were made).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:38–39; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5; LUKAS 17:27"> Through this very mayim, the tevel (world) of that time perished in the mabbul (flood).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:10; YESHAYAH 51:6; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:12"> But the present Shomayim v'ha'Aretz are being reserved by the same Dvar Hashem, being kept for Eish (Fire) for Yom HaDin and for the destruction of anshei resha (men of wickedness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 90:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:25; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:1"> Chaverim, let not this one thing escape your notice, that “A thousand years in the eyes of Hashem is like one day”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 90:4">c</a></sup>and one day like a thousand years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:4; CHABAKUK 2:3"> But Hashem is not slow concerning his havtachah (promise), as some consider slowness, but is demonstrating savlanut (patience)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3:15">d</a></sup>toward you, not wanting any to perish but all to come to teshuva (repentance).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:1; MATTITYAHU 24:35; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:7"> But the Yom Hashem will come as a ganav (thief), in which the Shomayim with great suddenness will pass away and the elements, burning up, will be destroyed, and the Olam Hazeh and its ma'asim will be found ou.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Num 32:23">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:27; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:12"> Als (Since) all these things will thus be destroyed, what kinds of persons ought you to be in hitnahagut (conduct) that is characterized by kedushah and chasidus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:10; YESHAYAH 34:4; MICHOH 1:4"> Awaiting and hastening the Bias HaMoshiach of the Yom Hashem, because of which the Shomayim will be turned into Eish (Fire) and destroyed, and the elements set ablaze and melted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YESHAYAH 65:17; HISGALUS 21:1; YESHAYAH 66:22"> But SHOMAYIM CHADASHIM VA'ARETZ CHADASHA<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isa 65:17cf. Isa 66:22">f</a></sup>according to the havtachah (promise) of Hashem we await, in which Tzidkanut (Righteousness) dwells.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:23; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:10"> Chaverim, als you await these things, be eager to be found in shalom by him TAMIM U'MUM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ex 12:5; Lv 22:20; Isa 53:7,9">g</a></sup>(unblemished and unspotted).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:4; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:16"> And regard the savlanut (patience) of Adoneinu as Yeshu'at Eloheynu. So also our Chaver and Ach b'Moshiach Sha'ul, according to the chochmah (wisdom) having been given to him, wrote to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:6; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:11; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8"> As also in all his iggrot speaking in them concerning these things, in which are some things lomdish (intricate and complicated to understand), which those unstable and without da'as, twist, as also the other Kitvei Hakodesh, to their own churban (destruction).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:13–15"> Chaverim, you therefore, knowing beforehand, be shomer over yourselves lest with the toyus (error) of the mufkarim (lawless ones) having been led away, you fall from your own stability.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:15; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:2"> But grow in the Chen v'Chesed and Da'as of Adoneinu and Moshieinu, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua. Lo HaKavod Gam HaYom V'Gam L'Yom HaOlam. Omein. (To him be glory both now and to the day of eternity. Omein.)</span>
