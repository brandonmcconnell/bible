<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 2:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:21–26; LETTER TO TITOS 1:4"> Shim'on Kefa, an eved and Shliach of Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua: to the ones having obtained, with us,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Shlichim of Moshiach">a</a></sup>equally precious<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="orthodox Jewish">b</a></sup>emunah (faith)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="the Emunah of the true Dat HaYehudit">c</a></sup>in the Tzedek Hashem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Dan 9:24">d</a></sup>of Eloheinu and Moshieinu Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18; YOCHANAN 17:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:8"> Chesed Hashem and Shalom Hashem to you abundantly in the da'as (personal, saving knowledge) of Hashem and Adoneinu Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:10; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:28–30"> As the gevurat Hashem(the power of G-d) has given us everything for Chayyim (life) and chasidus through the da'as (personal, saving knowledge) of the One Who bestows on us the kri'ah (calling) to His own Kavod (Glory) and Tzidkanut (Righteousness),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See 1:5-7; 2:5,21; 3:13">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:23–24; YECHEZKEL 36:25–27; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:18"> So Hashem has given us precious havtachot gedolot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="3:4,9,13cf. 2:19">f</a></sup>(great promises) that through these promises you might become &#91;regenerate&#93; deveykus participants with the life of the teva haelohi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="cf. Yn 1:13">g</a></sup>(divine nature), having escaped the &#91;degenerate&#93; Olam Hazehʼs lust engendered corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:8; MISHLE 4:23; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18"> For this very reason, you must apply all zerizut (diligence) to supply emunah with midah hatov (the attribute of virtue), and midah hatov with da'as (knowledge),</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:36; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:3–4; LUKAS 21:19"> And da'as with shlitah atzmit (self-control), and shlitah atzmit with chozek (fortitude) and chozek with chasidus (piety),</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:12"> And chassidus with ahavah shel achvah (brotherly love), and ahavah shel achvah with ahavah (agape).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:14; YOCHANAN 15:2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:12"> For if these midos are in you increasingly, they will forestall you from being batlanim (idlers) and lo poreh (unfruitful) in the da'as (personal saving knowledge) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:18–20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:26; LETTER TO TITOS 2:14"> For the one with whom these middos chasidus (qualities of piety) are not present is an ivver (blind man), being shortsighted, having forgotten the tohorah (cleansing) of his past averos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17; LUKAS 6:47–49; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:11"> Therefore, Achim b'Moshiach, be eagerly diligent to make your kri'ah (summons) and bechirah (election) sure, for, if you do these things, you will never fall over a michshol (stumbling block).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HISGALUS 5:10; MATTITYAHU 25:34; HISGALUS 3:21"> For thus will be richly provided for you the entrance into the Malchut Olam of Adoneinu and Moshieinu Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:14–15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:1"> Therefore, it is my tachlis (purpose, final end) always to remind you concerning these things, even though you have da'as of them and have been stabilized in HaEmes that you have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:1; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:3"> But I consider it befitting, as long as I am in this ohel, to arouse you with a tizkoret (reminder),</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:6; YOCHANAN 21:18–19; DEVARIM 31:14"> Als (since) I know that the putting off of my mishkan is imminent, as indeed Adoneinu Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua made clear to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEVARIM 31:19–29; TEHILLIM 71:18; YEHOSHUA 24:24–29"> And also I am eager to cause you to always possess the zichron (memory) of these things after my exodus.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="T.N. Written shortly before his death al kiddush ha-Shem for his Moshiach. See Bodmer papyrus p72 showing early attestation of this authentic last will and testament of Kefa whom Nero caused to be killed.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:14; YOCHANAN 1:14; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:4"> For we had not followed cleverly crafted aggadot or doichek (farfetched) bubbemeises or reid (hearsay) when we made known to you the gevurah (miraculous power) of Adoneinu Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua and the Bi'as HaMoshiach of him; no, we had been edei re'iyah (eyewitnesses) of Moshiachʼs majestic gedulah (greatness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MARKOS 9:7; MATTITYAHU 17:5; MATTITYAHU 3:17"> For having received from Elohim HaAv kavod va'oz, the bat kol was conveyed to Moshiach by the Majestic Glory, ZEH BENI AHUVI ASHER BO CHAFATSTI<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ps 2:7; Gn 22:2; Isa 42:1">i</a></sup>(This is my Son, the beloved, with whom I am well pleased).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 17:6; YEHOSHUA 5:15; SHEMOT 3:5"> And this bat kol we heard out of Shomayim, having been brought with Moshiach upon the Har HaKodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="HISGALUS 22:16; YESHAYAH 9:2; TEHILLIM 119:105"> And we have the Dvar HaNevu'ah (Word of Prophecy) made more sure, to which you do well in giving heed as to a menorah shining in a place of choshech until HaYom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="3:18">j</a></sup>&#91;of Olam Habah&#93; dawns and the Kochav<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Num 24:17">k</a></sup>(Star) haNogah (of Brightness, Venus) rises in your levavot (hearts).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:6"> Knowing this first: that every nevu'ah (prophecy) of the Kitvei Hakodesh is not of the prophetʼs own explanation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:15–17; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:11; SHMUEL BAIS 23:2"> For no nevu'ah was at any time brought by the ratzon HaBnei Adam (will of Man), but men being carried along by the Ruach Hakodesh spoke from Hashem.</span>
