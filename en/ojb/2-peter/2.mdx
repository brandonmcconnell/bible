<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:15; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:4; DEVARIM 13:1–3"> But there were also nevi'ei sheker (false prophets) among Am Yisroel just as also there will be morei sheker among you. These false teachers will secretly bring in heretical doctrines of koferim ba-ikkarim (deniers of fundamentals), producing machshelah (ruin), as these heretics even become meshummad, denying the Ribbono Shel Olam who redeemed them, bringing upon themselves swift destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:24; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:10; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:12"> And many will follow them in walking in darchei zimah (the ways of licentiousness), and because of them the Derech HaEmes will be libeled with lashon hora.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:17; DEVARIM 32:35; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:18"> And in their chomed (covetous) greed, they will exploit you with deceptive words. For them the Mishpat (Judgment) of old is not drei (idle) and their churban (ruin) does not slumber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:6; MATTITYAHU 25:41; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:32"> For if Hashem did not spare malachim that sinned, but cast them into Gehinnom, consigning them to the sharsherot (chains) of choshech to be kept for Mishpat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:7; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:6; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:14–15"> And if Hashem, when he brought a mabbul (flood) upon the world of resha'im (evildoers), did not spare the ancient world, but preserved Noach, the eighth of the nefashot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="in the Teva (Ark)">a</a></sup>and a maggid of Tzidkanut (Righteousness);</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7; LUKAS 17:28–30; DEVARIM 29:23"> And if Hashem judged the cities of S'dom and Amora, reducing them to ashes by a churban catastrophe, making them a mofet (an example) for the ones intending to be resha'im;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 19:16; BERESHIS 19:29; BERESHIS 19:7–8"> And if Hashem delivered Lot, a tzaddik distressed by the hitnahagut (conduct) of the mufkarim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2:8,10; 3:17">b</a></sup>(lawless) walking in darchei zimah (the ways of licentiousness)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:158; YECHEZKEL 9:4; TEHILLIM 119:136"> — For that tzaddik, living among them day after day, was tortured in his nefesh hayesharah (honest soul) by the darchei mufkarim (the ways of lawless ones) that he saw and heard</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 34:15–19; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:13; HISGALUS 3:10"> — Then Hashem knows how to rescue the chasidim from trial and how to preserve the anshei resha (men of wickedness) for punishment for the Yom HaDin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:16; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:10; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:18"> Especially the ones going after the basar (flesh) in ta'avah (lust) producing tum'a (defilement) and the ones despising kol samchut.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Mt 28:18">c</a></sup>Bold and self-willed, these have no yirat Shomayim and therefore commit Chillul Hashem as they slander the glorious ones,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:7; TEHILLIM 104:4"> Whereas malachim, being greater b'oz and koach (in strength and power), al kol panim (nevertheless) do not bring against them a slanderous judgment before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:10; YIRMEYAH 12:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:8"> But these men, like irrational beasts born b'derech hatevah (naturally) to be caught and killed, commit Chillul Hashem in matters where they lack da'as. In the destruction of &#91;the beasts&#93;, they also will be destroyed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:8–9; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:2–3"> Suffering harm as onesh (penalty) for harm done. They actually think there is ta'anug (pleasure) in reveling in broad daylight. They are mumim (defects) and spots reveling in their ta'nugot (evil pleasures) even while they sit at seudot (banquet dinners) with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:3; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:3"> Having eyes full of ni'uf (adultery), ceaselessly committing averos (sins), enticing unstable nefashot (souls), having levavot trained in chomed (covetous) greed — banim mekulalim (accursed children)!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:14; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:11; DEVARIM 23:4–5"> Forsaking the Derech HaYashar (Straight Way), they went astray, having followed the Derech HaBil'am Ben-B'or, who loved the sachar haresha (reward of unrighteousness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 22:21–33; KOHELET 7:25; LUKAS 16:11"> But he had tochachah (reproof) for his own peysha (transgression): a dumb chamor (donkey), having spoken in a manʼs voice, hindered the madness of the meshuga navi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12–13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:14; YIRMEYAH 14:3"> These men are mikva'ot without mayim and mists being driven by storms, for whom has been reserved the gloom of choshech (darkness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:15–16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:13"> Using high sounding words of hevel, they entice, with licentious ta'avot of the basar, the ones barely escaped from those living in toyus (error) and delusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:34; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:16–22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:13"> They offer them the havtachah (promise) of cherut (freedom), but they themselves are avadim (slaves) of shachat (corruption), for whatever defeats a man also enslaves him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:26–27; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:4–8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:19"> For if, having escaped the tumot (defilements) of Olam Hazeh by da'as of Adoneinu and Moshieinu Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, they are again entangled in these, then their last state is worse for them than their first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:24; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:4–6; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:17"> For better it was for them not to have da'as of the Derech HaTzidkanut (the Way of Righteousness) than, having known it, to turn away from the mitzvah hakedosha handed down to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MISHLE 26:11"> The word of the true mashal (proverb) has overtaken them: KECHELEV SHAV AL KE'O<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Prov 26:11">d</a></sup>(As a dog having returned to its own vomit), and a “chazir (swine) is washed only to wallow in the mud.”</span>
