<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:3; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18"> Lemaskana (finally), Achim b'Moshiach, we ask and exhort you in Adoneinu Yehoshua, in order that, just as from us &#91;Moshiachʼs Shlichim&#93; you received &#91;as authoritative halachic tradition, as the Messianic teaching to be followed&#93; how it is necessary for you to be fier zich (comport oneself) in your derech (way of life) and to please Hashem (just as you in fact are doing), so you should abound more and more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:20; YECHEZKEL 3:17; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:10"> For you have da'as what mitzvot (commandments) we gave you through &#91;Moshiach&#93; Adoneinu Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:23"> For this is the ratzon Hashem(the will of G-d), your kedushah (holiness), your consecration, that you abstain from zenut (fornication),</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:1; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:4"> That each one of you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="as Kedoshim">a</a></sup>have da'as of how to control your own k'li (vessel) in kedushah and kavod,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:30–31; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:17–19"> Not in the passion of ta'avah (evil desire, lust) like Goyim who do not have da'as of Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:4; SHEMOT 20:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:19"> That no man commit peysha and take advantage of one of his Achim b'Moshiach in this &#91;sexual&#93; matter, because Hashem is the Nokem (Avenger) concerning all these things, just as also we told you before and warned you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="VAYIKRA 11:44; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:4; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9–12"> For &#91;as far as our bechirah (election) is concerned&#93;, Hashem did not summon us for tu'ma (uncleanness) but in kedushah (holiness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:24; LUKAS 10:16; YOCHANAN 12:48"> Therefore, the one rejecting this mitzvah (commandment) does not reject Bnei Adam but Hashem who gives to you His Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:10; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:23"> Concerning the matter of ahavah shel achvah (brotherly love), you have no need that we write you, for you yourselves are limudei Elohim (taught of G-d) to have ahavah (agape) one for another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:3"> For indeed this you do toward all the Achim b'Moshiach in all of Macedonia. But in this we exhort you, Chaverim, to abound more and more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="KOHELET 4:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:28; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:4"> And have as your ambition to lead a quiet life of shalom bayis, and to mind your own business, and to have a parnasah, working with your own hands, according to the mitzvot (commandments) we gave you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:5; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:17"> The tachlis (purpose) is that the way you fier zich (comport oneself) in your derech (way of life) be conducted properly toward outsiders and that you might not be nitzrach (needy).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DANIEL 12:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:10"> We do not want you to lack da'as, Chaverim, concerning those who have “fallen asleep.” The purpose is that you not have agmat nefesh (grief) like the rest, who do not have tikvah (hope).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:20–21; YESHAYAH 26:19"> For, if we have an Ani Ma'amin belief that Yehoshua had his histalkus and then stood up alive again in the Techiyas HaMoshiach from HaMesim, even so, Hashem will bring with him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Moshiach">b</a></sup>those who have fallen asleep through Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:51–53; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:19"> For this we say to you by the Dvar Hashem, that we who are alive and have been left behind until the Bi'as (Coming, Parousia of) Moshiach Adoneinu, may in no way be kodem (preceding) those who have fallen asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:51–52; HISGALUS 1:7; MATTITYAHU 24:30–31"> For Adoneinu Himself, at the signal, at the bat kol of the Sar HaMalachim (the Archangel), and at the shofar blast of Hashem, shall come down from Shomayim, and the Mesim in Moshiach shall stand up alive in the Techiyas HaMesim first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:52; HISGALUS 11:12; YOCHANAN 14:3"> Then,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="next in sequence">c</a></sup>we who are alive and who have been left behind simultaneously with them shall be snatched up in the ananim (clouds) to meet Adoneinu in the air. And so always with Adoneinu we shall be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:11; LUKAS 21:28; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:14"> Therefore, give chozek (strength) and speak divrei chizzuk (words of encouragement), comforting one another with these words.</span>
