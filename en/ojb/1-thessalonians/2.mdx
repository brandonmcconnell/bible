<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:58"> But you yourselves know, Chaverim b'Moshiach, that our initial reception with you was not empty of result.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:22–24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:2–9"> On the contrary, having suffered before and having been shamefully persecuted (as you know) in Philippi, we had the chozek b'Eloheinu (strength in our G-d) to speak to you the Besuras HaGeulah of Hashem in the face of tzorrim rabbim (great and oppressive enemy opposition).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:17; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:7"> For our appeal, our eidus (witness of testimony), does not spring from toyus (error) or meshuga delusion or from tum'a (uncleanness) or from remiyah (guile, deceit).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:7"> On the contrary, in this way we speak: as persons examined and approved by G-d to be entrusted with the Besuras HaGeulah of Hashem, as striving to please not men but Hashem who tests, who examines, our levavot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:33"> Nor did we ever come with divrei chanuppah (words of flattery), as you know, nor with pretext for chamdanut (greed) — Der Oybershter is mine eidus! (G-d is my witness!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:9; YOCHANAN 5:41"> Nor seeking kavod from men, neither from you nor from others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YESHAYAH 66:13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:11; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:24–25"> Even though we had the samchut<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="authority">a</a></sup>to be able to throw our weight around as Shlichim (Emissaries) of Moshiach, yet we became anav (humble) and eidel (gentle) among you, as a nursing imma (mother) might take care of her own yeladim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:17"> Thus having a tshuka (yearning) for you, it was our good pleasure to share with you not only the Besuras HaGeulah of Hashem, but also our very lives, because you became beloved chaverim to us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:7–9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:3"> For remember, Achim b'Moshiach, our labor and toil yomam valailah working for the purpose of not weighing down any one of you while we maggidim &#91;for Moshiach&#93; made the Hachrazah (Proclamation, Kerygma) to you of the Besuras HaGeulah of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:5; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:12"> You were edim (witnesses) and so is Hashem, with what kedushah and tzedek and tamim manner of shomer masoret we acted toward you ma'aminim b'Moshiach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:14–15; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:22; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:21"> For you know how we treated you, as an abba treats his own yeladim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:1; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:6; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:6–7"> Exhorting you with divrei chizzuk and comforting you, addressing you with solemn edut (testimony) that you let your derech (way of life), your hitnahagut (conduct), be to fier zich (comport oneself) in a manner worthy of Hashem, who summons you to His own Malchut and Kavod.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:17; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:23"> And for this reason also we constantly offer hodayah to Hashem that when you received &#91;as “Ani Maamin” authoritative teaching&#93; the Dvar Hashem, the Word of Hachrazah (Proclamation, Kerygma) from us, you received it not as the dvar HaBnei Adam but, as it actually is, the Dvar Hashem, which also is at work in you who have emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:33–34; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:7–8"> For you became imitators, Chaverim, of the kehillot Hashem in Moshiach Yehoshua which are in Yehudah, because you also were similarly persecuted by your own landslait, just as also they were by the ones in Yehudah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:12; LUKAS 11:48–53; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:52"> Even the ones who caused the histalkus of &#91;Moshiach&#93; Adoneinu Yehoshua and killed the Nevi'im and persecuted us<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Shlichim">b</a></sup>severely. Indeed, they are not pleasing Hashem and are hostile to kol Bnei Adam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:19; MARKOS 16:16"> By hindering us from speaking to the Goyim in order that the Goyim might find Yeshu'at Eloheinu. Thus they always make complete their peysha'im. So the charon af, the burning wrath, the furious retribution of Hashem has overtaken them at last.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:6"> But you, Achim b'Moshiach, while we were separated from you for a short time panim el panim, not b'lev, we made all the more effort in much desire to see your penemer (faces).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:13; HISGALUS 2:10"> For we wanted to come to you. Indeed I, &#91;Rav&#93; Sha'ul, did again and again; but HaSatan hindered us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:16"> For who is our tikvah or simcha or crown to glory in before Adoneinu Yehoshua at His Parousia, at the Bias Moshiach — is it not you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:7; MISHLE 17:6"> For you are our kavod and simcha.</span>
</div>
