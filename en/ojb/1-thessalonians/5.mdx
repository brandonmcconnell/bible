<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:9; MATTITYAHU 24:36"> But concerning the ittim (times) and the zmanim (seasons), Chaverim, you have no need for you to have anything in writing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HISGALUS 16:15; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:10; MATTITYAHU 24:42–44"> For you yourselves know very well that the Yom Hashem comes like a ganav balailah (thief in the night).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKAS 21:34–35; MATTITYAHU 24:37–39; LUKAS 17:26–30"> Whenever they say, “Shalom and Bitachon!” then sudden churban (destruction) will come upon them like the chevlei leydah (birth pangs) of the isha with a baby in her womb. And they can certainly not escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:8; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:10; DEVARIM 19:6"> But you, Achim b'Moshiach, are not in choshech that the Yom should overtake you like a ganav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:18; YOCHANAN 12:36"> For all of you are Bnei Ohr and Bnei Yom; we are not of the Lailah nor of the Choshech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:13; MATTITYAHU 24:42; LUKAS 22:46"> Therefore, let us not “sleep” as the rest, but let us keep awake with zililut da'as (sober-mindedness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:34; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13"> For the ones sleeping sleep balailah, and the ones indulging in shichrut (drunkenness) indulge in shichrut balailah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:12–13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:11; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:13"> But we, being Bnei Yom, let us be bnei zililut da'as (sons of sober-mindedness), having clothed ourselves with the choshen (breastplate) of emunah and ahavah (agape) and as a KOVAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Isa 59:17">a</a></sup>(helmet) the tikvah of Yeshu'at Eloheynu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:10; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:10; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:10"> Because Hashem did not appoint us to charon af (the burning wrath) but to the attainment of Yeshu'at Eloheinu through Adoneinu, Moshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:8–9; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:34"> He is the one who made his histalkus and died on our behalf in order that whether we are &#91;living&#93; and awake or &#91;passed away&#93; and sleeping, we may live together with Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:29; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:2"> Therefore, give chozek (strength) and encouragement, building up one another, as indeed you are doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:7; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:17–18"> Now we ask you, Achim b'Moshiach, to have discerning binah (understanding) and discern the ones laboring among you who are your Mashgichim Ruchaniyim (Spiritual Overseers) in Adoneinu. I'm referring to the ones who admonish and warn you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MARKOS 9:50; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:6"> And esteem them most exceedingly in ahavah (agape), because of their avodas kodesh work. Let there be shalom bayis (family peace) also in the kehillah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:35"> And we exhort you, Achim b'Moshiach, warn the batlanim (unemployed or lazy loafers, idlers), comfort the congregant who is pachdan (fainthearted), those who are weak take an interest in, have savlanut (patience) with all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:10; VAYIKRA 19:18"> See that no one returns ra'ah for ra'ah, but always pursue haTov both for one another and for all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:4; MATTITYAHU 5:12"> Have simcha always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:18"> Daven unceasingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:17"> In everything offer hodayah, for this is the ratzon Hashem in Moshiach Yehoshua for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:30; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:51; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:6"> Do not quench the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:3–6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:1; BAMIDBAR 11:25–29"> Do not reject nevu'ot (prophecies).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:10"> But test kol davar (everything); hold fast to haTov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:31–33"> Abstain from every appearance of ra'ah (evil).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:24; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:8–9"> And now Elohei HaShalom wholly set you apart as Kedoshim and may your whole ruach and nefesh and basar be preserved without blame at the Bi'as HaMoshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:3; BAMIDBAR 23:19; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:9"> Ne'eman (Faithful) is the One who summons you to your kri'ah (calling). He will do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:18–19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:19; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:22"> Achim b'Moshiach, offer tefillos also concerning us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:20"> Say Drishat Shalom to all the Achim b'Moshiach with a neshikat kedoshah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:13; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:3"> By Adoneinu, I solemnly give you the directive that this iggeret hakodesh has to be read to all the Achim b'Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:20; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:23"> The Chen v'Chesed of Adoneinu, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, be with you.</span>
