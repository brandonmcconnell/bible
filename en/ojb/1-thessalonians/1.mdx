<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:13; LETTER TO TITOS 3:4–5; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:10"> Knowing, Achim b'Moshiach ahuvei Hashem(Brothers in Moshiach beloved of G-d), your bechirah (election).</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:52"> And you became imitators of us and of Adoneinu, having received the Dvar Hashem(the Word of the L-rd) in much redifah (persecution) with the simcha of the Ruach HaKodesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 2:7; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:3; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:12"> With the result that you became a mofet (portent), even a role model for all the ma'aminim (believers) in the territory of Macedonia and in Achaia.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Macedonia and Achaia were Roman provinces of southern Greece">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:14–18; YESHAYAH 52:7"> For from you the Dvar Hashem has resounded not only in Macedonia and in Achaia, but also in every place to which your emunah in Hashem has gone forth, with the result that we have no need to say anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:8–9"> For they themselves &#91;the ma'aminim b'Moshiach in Macedonia and Achaia&#93; are reporting about us, what sort of initial reception we had with you, and how you made teshuva, turning to Hashem from elilim (idols), from avodah zarah (idol worship), turning to worship the Elohim Chayyim and Elohei Emes (the Living G-d and the True G-d),</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:9; YESHAYAH 25:8–9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:9–10"> And to expect<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Moshiach">b</a></sup>Zun fun der Oybershter from Shomayim whom Hashem made to stand up in the Techiyas HaMoshiach from the Mesim Yehoshua Moshieinu (our Deliverer) from the Charon Af HaBah (the wrath to come).</span>
