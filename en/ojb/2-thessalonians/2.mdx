<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:14–17; MARKOS 13:27; MATTITYAHU 24:31"> We ask you, Achim b'Moshiach, concerning the Bias HaMoshiach (the Arrival, Coming of Moshiach) Yehoshua Adoneinu and our being gathered together to him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Moshiach">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1–2; MARKOS 13:7; MATTITYAHU 24:4–6"> that you not become too hastily disturbed from your spiritual composure nor frightened either by a ruach or by a dibur (saying, utterance) or by an iggeret (letter), as if from us, saying that the YOM HASHEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Am 5:18">b</a></sup>(Day of the L-rd) has come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:4–6; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:6"> Let no one in any way lead you astray, because, unless the Merid (Rebellion, Revolt, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Anti-Moshiach">c</a></sup>Shmad Betrayal Defection of Apostasy, the Azivah Abandonment) Desertion comes rishonah (first), and the Apocalypse of the Ish HaMufkarut (Man of Lawlessness), the Ish HaChatta'ah Ben HaAvaddon (Man of Sin, Son of Destruction Anti-Moshiach) is unveiled…</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 28:2; DANIEL 7:25; HISGALUS 13:6–7"> I'm referring to the one setting himself against and exalting himself AL KOL EL<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Dan 11:36">d</a></sup>(above every G-d), above all that is given the appellation “G-d” or object of worship, with the result that in the Beis Hamikdash Heikhal he sits down on the MOSHAV ELOHIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ezek 28:2">e</a></sup>(throne of G-d), proclaiming that he himself is Elohim…</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:21; MATTITYAHU 16:9"> Do you not recall that when I was still with you I was telling you these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:8"> Yet you have da'as of what is holding back and restraining<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Anti-Moshiach">f</a></sup>now, so that he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Anti-Moshiach">g</a></sup>may be unveiled and revealed in his own time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:3; HISGALUS 17:7; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:17–18"> For the Sod HaMufkarut (Mystery of Lawlessness) is already working; only he who holds back and restrains just now will do so until he is out of the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:3; HISGALUS 19:20–21; YESHAYAH 11:4"> And then the Ish HaMufkarut (the Man of Lawlessness)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Anti-Moshiach">h</a></sup>will be revealed, whom HaAdon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Moshiach Yehoshua, Mal 3:1; Ps 110:1; Dan 7:13-14">i</a></sup>will destroy by the RUACH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Isa 11:4">j</a></sup>of His mouth and will wipe out at the appearance of His Bi'as (HaMoshiach, His Parousia, Coming).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:24; HISGALUS 19:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:2"> The Bi'as Anti-Moshiach (Coming, Parousia, of Anti-Moshiach) is according to the working of HaSatan with all false ko'ach (power) and otot (signs) and pseudo moftim (wonders),</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:18; MISHLE 1:7; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:2–3"> And with all deception of resha (wickedness) for those who are perishing, because they were not being mekabel (receiving) the Ahavas HaEmes (Love of the Truth) so that they have the Geulah deliverance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:28; YOCHANAN 12:39–43; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:1"> And for this reason Hashem sends to them a powerful madduchei shav (false enticement, delusion) in order that they believe what is sheker.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="1Kgs 22:22">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:32; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:7–8; MARKOS 16:16"> This is for the tachlis (purpose) of condemning to Onesh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gehinnom">l</a></sup>all the ones who have no emunah in HaEmes, but instead have had delight in resha.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:4–5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:4"> But we ought always to offer hodayah (thanksgiving) and make brachot to Hashem concerning you, Achim b'Moshiach, ahuvei Hashem, because Hashem chose you as bikkurim (first fruits) for the Geulah through mekudash b'Ruach HaKodesh (being set apart as holy in the Ruach HaKodesh) and through emunah in HaEmes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:12; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:5"> This is HaEmes to which Hashem called you through our Besuras HaGeulah to the obtaining of the kavod of Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:2; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:6; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:2"> So then, Achim b'Moshiach, stand fast and hold fast to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="orthodox Jewish">m</a></sup>emunah, the Moshiachʼs pnimiyus haTorah that was handed over and transmitted to you, which you were taught, whether by divrei pinu (words of our mouth) or by our iggeret hakodesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:2–5; YOCHANAN 3:16; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:3–8"> Now may HaMelech HaMoshiach Yehoshua Adoneinu Himself and Elohim Avinu, who has regarded us with ahavah (agape), and has granted us nechamat olam (eternal consolation) and tikvah tovah (good hope) by the unmerited Chesed of Hashem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:2"> May He give chozek (strength) and encouragement to you in every mitzvah (good deed) you do and in every dibur (utterance) you speak.</span>
</div>
