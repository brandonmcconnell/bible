<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:3–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:31; DEVARIM 32:20"> And that we may be delivered from anshei resha (men of wickedness), for not all possess the Messianic emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:13"> But Ne'eman (Faithful) is Hashem who gives you chozek (strength) and is shomer over you, guarding you from the Evil One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:6; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:21; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:10"> And we have bitachon in Hashem concerning you, that you are doing and will continue to do what we have directed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:36; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:18; DEVARIM 30:6"> Now may Hashem direct your levavot in the Ahavas Hashem and into the savlanut of Moshiach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:10–11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:17"> Now we charge you, Achim b'Moshiach, b'Shem Adoneinu Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, to shun chavrusashaft (association) with every one of the Achim b'Moshiach who follows the derech habatlanim (way of idlers) and walks not according to Moshiachʼs &#91;pnimiyus&#93; haTorah that was handed over and transmitted to you and which you received from us.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Shlichim">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:6; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:16"> For you yourselves have da'as how it is necessary to imitate us as a mofet, a role model, because we<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Shlichim">b</a></sup>were not batlanim (idlers) among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:28"> Nor did we eat lechem (food) from anyone gratis, but in labor and exertion lailah v'yomam (night and day) we were working for the tachlis (purpose) of not weighing down and burdening any one of you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:4–14"> Not because we do not have the zchus (right), but in order that we impart ourselves as a mofet (role model) to you that you might imitate us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 3:19; MISHLE 13:4; MISHLE 20:4"> For even when we were with you, this we used to direct you: that if anyone will not be a po'el (worker), neither let him be an ochel (eater).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gn 3:19">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:13; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:11"> For we hear that some among you are leading the life of a batlan (loafer), not being busy with a parnasah, but being busybodies with hitarevut (meddlesomeness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:28; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:8"> Now such persons we are directing and exhort in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua Adoneinu to work with quietness that they may eat their own lechem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:9–10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:1"> But you, Achim b'Moshiach, do not lose chozek in well-doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:11"> But if anyone does not obey the gzeira (injunction) in this iggeret hakodesh (holy letter), take special notice of that one; do not have chavrusashaft (association) with him, for the tachlis (purpose) of stirring him with a sense of bushah (shame).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:5"> And do not consider (him) an oyev (enemy) but warn him as an Ach b'Moshiach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="TEHILLIM 29:11; YOCHANAN 16:33; MATTITYAHU 1:23"> Now may Elohei HaShalom Himself grant you shalom in every way. Hashem be with you all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:21; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:18"> I, &#91;Rav&#93; Sha'ul, send Drishat Shalom greetings in my own handwriting, which is the distinctive siman (sign) in every iggeret hakodesh; this is the way I write.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:23"> The unmerited Chen v'Chesed Hashem of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua be with you all.</span>
