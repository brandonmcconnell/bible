<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:20; HISGALUS 1:16; HISGALUS 1:11–13"> To the malach (angel) of the Kehillah (Congregation) in Ephesus, write: These things says the One holding shivat hakokhavim (seven stars) in the yamin (right hand) of him, the One walking in the midst of the sheva menorot hazahav (seven golden menorahs):</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1–3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:21"> I have da'as of your ma'asim (deeds) and your amal (toil) and the savlanut (patient endurance) of you &#91;plural&#93; and that you cannot bear anshei resha (evil men), and have tested the ones making the claim that they are “Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiachʼs shlichim” and are not, and found them kozvim (liars).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:36; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:9; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:7–11"> And you have savlanut (patient endurance), and you have persevered because of ha-Shem of me and have not become weary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:12–13; YIRMEYAH 2:2–5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:10–11"> But I have this against you, that you abandoned your Ahavah HaRishonah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Jer 2:2">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:2–3; HISGALUS 3:19; HISGALUS 2:16"> Therefore, let there be zikaron (remembrance) of from where you have fallen and make teshuva (repentance, turning from sin to G-d) and do the Ma'asim HaRishonim; but, if not, I am coming to you &#91;plural&#93; and I will remove your menorah from its place, unless you make teshuva.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 101:3; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9–10; TEHILLIM 26:5"> But this you have, that you loathe the ma'asim ra'im (evil deeds) of the Nicolaitans, which also I loathe.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 139:21">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:17; HISGALUS 2:11; MATTITYAHU 11:15"> The one having an ear let him hear what the Ruach Hakodesh says to the Kehillot; To the one who wins the nitzachon (victory) I will give to him to eat of the Etz HaChayyim (Tree of Life), which is in the Gan-Eden of Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gn 2:9; 3:22,24; also Gn 2:8; Ezek 28:1; 31:8,9TARGUM HA-SHIVIM">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:17–18; HISGALUS 1:8; HISGALUS 1:11"> And to the malach (angel) of the Kehillah in Smyrna, write: These things says HaRishon and HaAcharon, who became niftar (deceased) and had his histalkus (passing), and came back to Chayyim (Life):<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 44:6; 48:12">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:9; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5–6"> I have da'as of your tzarah &#91;Mt 24:15f&#93; and oni (poverty) — but you are oisher (rich) — and the loshon hora of the ones making the claim and declaring themselves to be Bnei Brit, and are not,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="of the Brit Chadasha">e</a></sup>but are a Shul<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="deluded by">f</a></sup>of the Adversary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; MATTITYAHU 10:22; HISGALUS 3:10–11"> Have no pachad (terror) at all of the things you are about to suffer. Hinei, HaSatan is about to cast some of you into beit hasohar that you may be tested and you will have tzarah aseret yamim (ten days). Be ne'eman ad haMavet (faithful unto death), and I will give you the ateret HaChayyim (the diadem of Life).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Dan 1:12,14">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HISGALUS 20:6; HISGALUS 20:14; HISGALUS 21:8"> The one having &#91;spiritual&#93; ears, let him hear what the Ruach Hakodesh says to the Kehillot. The one who wins the nitzachon (victory) never will be hurt by the Mavet HaSheyni (the Second Death).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:16; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:12; HISGALUS 1:16"> And to the malach (angel) of the Kehillah at Pergamum, write: These things says the one having the sharp two-edged cherev:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isa 49:2">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:9–10; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:8; HISGALUS 3:8–9"> I have da'as of where you make your ma'on (residence), where the kes (throne) of HaSatan is, and you hold fast to ha-Shem of me and did not make hakhchashah (denial) of my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Moshiachʼs">i</a></sup>emunah (faith), even in the days of Antipas my ed hane'eman (my faithful witness) who died al-kiddush ha-Shem, killed among you as a martyr where HaSatan makes his ma'on (residence).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 31:16; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15; HISGALUS 2:20"> But I have a few things against you, because you have there ones holding to “the torah of Bil'am,” who was teaching Balak to put a michshol (stumbling block) before Bnei Yisroel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Num 31:16; 25:1-2">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:6"> So also you have ones eating okhel (food) sacrificed to elilim (idols) and who commit zenut (fornication). You also have ones holding the “torah of the Nicolaitans.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:8; HISGALUS 1:16; HISGALUS 2:5"> Likewise, therefore, make teshuva (repentance, turning from sin to G-d)! But if not, I am coming to you quickly and will war against them by the cherev of my mouth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Isa 49:2">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YESHAYAH 62:2; YESHAYAH 65:15; HISGALUS 19:12–13"> The one having an ear let him hear what the Ruach Hakodesh says to the Kehillot. To the one who wins the nitzachon (victory), I will give him some of the Manna having been nistar (hidden), and I will give him an even levanah (a white stone) and upon it a Shem Chadash (New Name), of which no one has da'as, except the one receiving it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Ps 78:24; Isa 62:2; 65:15; 56:5">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:14–15; HISGALUS 1:11; YOCHANAN 3:35–36"> And to the malach of the Kehillah in Thyatira, write: These things says the Zun fun der Oybershter, the One having his eynayim (eyes) like a flame of eish (fire),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Dan 7:9">m</a></sup>and the feet of him like burnished bronze:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Dan 10:6">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:5; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7; YOCHANAN 15:2"> I have da'as of your ma'asim (deeds) and the ahavah and the emunah and the tzedek<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Dan 9:25">o</a></sup>and the savlanut (patient endurance) of you, and that your ma'asim ha'acharonim are more than harishonim of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:14; MELACHIM ALEF 16:31; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:18–21"> But I have this against you, that you are being permissive toward that isha Izevel (Jezebel), the one calling herself a nevi'ah (prophetess) who teaches and deceives my avadim to commit zenut and to eat okhel (food) sacrificed to elilim (idols).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="1Kgs 16:31; 21:25; 2Kgs 9:7-22; Num 25:1-2">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:4–5; HISGALUS 9:20–21"> And I gave her time that she might make teshuva and she does not wish to make teshuva of her zenut.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="1Kgs 16:3; 2Kgs 9:22; Num 25:1-2">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HISGALUS 17:2; LUKAS 13:5; YECHEZKEL 16:37–41"> Hinei, I am throwing her into a bed of suffering, and the ones committing ni'uf (adultery) with her into Tzarah Gedolah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Mt 24:21">r</a></sup>(Great Tribulation), unless they make teshuva of her ma'asim (deeds).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:27; TEHILLIM 7:9"> And her yeladim (children),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="i.e., those who become like her in her doctrine">s</a></sup>I will kill with Mavet, and all the kehillot will have da'as that I am the One searching their musar klayot valev (pangs of conscience and heart), and I will give to you, each one, according to what you have done.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Ps 7:9; 62:12; 139:1; Prov 21:2; 24:12; Jer 11:20; 17:10; 1Sm 16:7; 1Kgs 8:39">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:11; HISGALUS 12:9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:3"> But I say to you, the rest, to the ones in Thyatira, as many as have not this “torah” (teaching), who did not have da'as of, as they say, “the tiefe (deep, profound) things of HaSatan,” I am putting on you no other burden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:11; HISGALUS 3:3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:23"> Al kol panim (nevertheless), what you have, hold fast until I come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:21; DANIEL 7:27; HISGALUS 2:7"> And the one who wins the nitzachon and the one until HaKets keeping shomer over ma'asei mitzvot of Me, him will I give samchut (authority) over the Goyim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="TEHILLIM 2:8–9; HISGALUS 12:5; HISGALUS 19:15"> To rule them with a SHEVET BARZEL (scepter of iron)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Ps 2:9">u</a></sup>KIKH'LI YOTZER TENAPTZEM (as the vessel of pottery are broken),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Ps 2:8,9; Isa 30:14; Jer 19:11">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="HISGALUS 22:16; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19; LUKAS 1:78–79"> Even as I also have received from HaAv of me and I will give him the Kokhav<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Num 24:17">w</a></sup>(Star) haNogah (of Brightness, Venus, the Morning Star).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:7"> The one having an ear let him hear what the Ruach Hakodesh says to the Kehillot.</span>
