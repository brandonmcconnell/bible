<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:6; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:26; HISGALUS 1:4"> And to the malach (angel) of the Kehillah in Sardis, write: These things says the One having the sheva ruchot of Hashem and the shevat hakokhavim (seven stars): I have da'as of your ma'asim, that nominally you are Chai (Alive) but you are &#91;spiritually&#93; niftar (deceased).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:8; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:1–4; HISGALUS 16:15"> Be shomer and regarding the things remaining and the things on the point of mavet, be chazakim (strong ones). For, I have not found your ma'asim having been shleimim (complete) before Elohai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:5; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:2"> Therefore, let there be zikaron (remembrance) of what has been handed over to you and what you heard, and be shomer and make teshuva. Therefore, if you are not shomer, I will come as a ganav, and never would you have da'as at what sha'ah (hour, time) I will come upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:5; HISGALUS 7:9; KOHELET 9:8"> But you have a few shemot (names) in Sardis which did not soil their kaftans. It is they that will have their halakhah with me in lavan (white), wearing a kittel, because they are walking worthily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:32; LUKAS 12:8; HISGALUS 21:27"> The one who wins the nitzachon (victory), in similar manner, will be clothed in lavan (white), and never will I erase him, the &#91;ba'al&#93; shem &#91;tov&#93;, from the Sefer Chayyim. And I will make hoda'ah (acknowledgement) of the shem of him before Elohim Avi and before the malachim (angels) of Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:7"> The one having an ear let him hear what the Ruach Hakodesh says to the Kehillot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YESHAYAH 22:22; MATTITYAHU 16:19; HISGALUS 1:18"> And to the malach of the Kehillah in Philadelphia, write: These things says HaKadosh, HaNe'eman, the One having the mafte'ach (key) of Dovid, the One opening and no one will shut, the one shutting and no one opens:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Isa 22:22">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:3; HISGALUS 3:7"> I have da'as of your ma'asim. Hinei, I have placed in front of you a delet (door), having been opened, which no one is able to shut: because you have a little ko'ach (power) and have been shomer over my dvar (word),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YESHAYAH 60:14; HISGALUS 2:9; YESHAYAH 49:23"> And did not make hakhchashah (denial) of ha-Shem of me. Hinei, I may make some of the Shul<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="deluded by">b</a></sup>of the Adversary, the ones declaring themselves to be Bnei Brit, and are not &#91;of the Brit Chadasha&#93; but speak sheker; hinei, I will make them come and prostrate themselves before your feet and then they will have da'as that I have ahavah for you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 49:23; 43:4">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9; HISGALUS 14:12; ZECHARYAH 13:9"> Because you were shomer over my dvar of savlanut (patient endurance), I will also be shomer over you, guarding you from the sha'at hanisayon (hour of trial) about to come upon the Olam Hazeh, to try all the ones of the inhabited world, all the ones dwelling upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; HISGALUS 22:12; HISGALUS 2:10"> I am coming quickly; hold fast to what you have, that no one takes your atarah (diadem, crown).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HISGALUS 22:4; HISGALUS 21:2; HISGALUS 14:1"> The one who wins the nitzachon (victory) I will make an ammud (pillar) in the Beis Hamikdash of Elohai and never may he go out of it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ps 23:6">d</a></sup>and I will write upon him ha-Shem of Elohai and ha-Shem of the Ir Hakodesh of Elohai — the Yerushalayim HaChadasha descending down out of Shomayim from Elohai — and ha-Shem HeChadash of me (my New Name).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ezek 48:35">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:7"> The one having an ear, let him hear what the Ruach Hakodesh says to the Kehillot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:15; HISGALUS 1:5; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:20"> And to the malach of the Kehillah in Laodicea, write: These things says the Omein, the Ed HaNe'eman (the Faithful Witness) who is also HaEmes, the Reshit of the Bri'at Hashem,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="i.e., Hashemʼs eternal Chochmah, Prov 8:22; Ps 33:6; Prov 30:4">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:11; MATTITYAHU 6:24; MELACHIM ALEF 18:21"> I have da'as of your ma'asim, that you are neither kar (cold) nor kham (hot). Would that you were kar or kham!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:5; ZECHARYAH 11:8–9; YIRMEYAH 14:19"> But because you are posher (lukewarm) and neither kham nor kar, I am about to spew you out of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MISHLE 13:7; HOSHEA 12:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:24"> Because you say, I am ashir (rich) and have become wealthy and in nothing am I nitzrach (needy), and you do not have da'as that you are the one wretched and pitiful and poor and blind and naked,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hos 12:8">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HISGALUS 16:15; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7; YESHAYAH 55:1"> I counsel you to buy from me zahav (gold) having been purified by eish (fire) — that you may be oisher (rich) — and a kittel, that you may be clothed in lavan (white), and that the bushah (shame) of your nakedness not be made nikar (evident), and eye salve to rub on the eynayim (eyes) of you that you may see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MISHLE 3:11–12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:32; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:5–11"> Those for whom I have ahavah I reprove and discipline. Be kham (hot), therefore, in kanous (zeal) for Hashem and make teshuva.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Dt 8:5; Prov 3:12">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKAS 12:36–37; HISGALUS 19:9; YOCHANAN 14:21–23"> Hinei, I have stood at the delet (door) DOFEK<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Song 5:2">i</a></sup>(knocking); if anyone hears my kol and opens the delet, indeed I will come in to him and we, the two of us, will dine together at the BEIT HAYAYIN.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="“Banquet Hall,” Song 2:4">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:12; MATTITYAHU 19:28; HISGALUS 2:26–27"> The one who wins the nitzachon (victory), I will give to him to sit with me on the Kes (Throne) of me, as I also won the nitzachon and sat with Elohim Avi on the Kes (throne) of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:7; HISGALUS 2:11; HISGALUS 2:17"> The one having an ear let him hear what the Ruach Hakodesh says to the Kehillot.</span>
