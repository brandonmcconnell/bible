<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 21:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:32; LUKAS 21:11"> And an ot gadol (miraculous sign) was seen in Shomayim, an ISHA<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gn 3:15">a</a></sup>having been clothed with the shemesh (sun), and the levanah (moon) underneath the feet of her, and on the rosh of her an atarah (diadem) of kokhavim (stars) numbering Shneym Asar (Twelve),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gn 37:9">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:19; HISGALUS 12:4"> And in her womb having a YELED<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 9:5">c</a></sup>(CHILD, Moshiach), and she cries out, suffering CHEVLEI and being in pain to give birth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 26:17">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HISGALUS 17:12; HISGALUS 12:9; DANIEL 7:24"> And another ot (miraculous sign) was seen in Shomayim. And hinei, a great red Dragon NACHASH,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gn 3:1">e</a></sup>having sheva (seven) heads and eser (ten) horns, and on the heads of it sheva (seven) ketarim (crowns).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Dan 7:7,20">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DANIEL 8:9–12; HISGALUS 12:9; YOCHANAN 8:44"> And the tail of him drags down a third of the kokhavim (stars) of Shomayim and threw them to ha'aretz (the earth), and the Dragon NACHASH was standing before the ISHA (Woman) who was about to give birth, that when she gives birth to the YELED (CHILD, Moshiach)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 9:6[5]">g</a></sup>of her, he might devour the YELED.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Dan 8:10">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:15; HISGALUS 2:26–27; HISGALUS 12:2"> And she gave birth to a BEN (SON, Moshiach),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Isa 9:6[5]">i</a></sup>a zachar (male), who is about to shepherd all the Goyim (Nations) with a shevet barzel (scepter of iron),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ps 2:9">j</a></sup>and the YELED of the ISHA was snatched up to Hashem and to his Kes (Throne).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HISGALUS 13:5; HISGALUS 11:2–3; HISGALUS 12:14"> And the ISHA fled into the wilderness, where she has there a place having been prepared by Hashem, that there she can be nourished a thousand two hundred and sixty yamim (days).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9; DANIEL 12:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:12"> And there was milchamah (war) in Shomayim, Mikha'el and the malachim of him fought against the Dragon NACHASH; and the Dragon NACHASH and his malachim fought back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 37:10; HISGALUS 12:11; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:6"> But the Dragon NACHASH was not strong enough nor was any place found for them any longer in Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 10:18; YOCHANAN 12:31; HISGALUS 20:10"> And the great Dragon, that NACHASH HaKadmoni (Ancient Serpent) was thrown down, the one being called the Malshin (Informer, Accuser) and HaSatan, the one deceiving the whole inhabited world. He was thrown to ha'aretz (the earth) and the malachim of him were thrown down with him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gn 3:1-7">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKAS 22:31; ZECHARYAH 3:1–2; HISGALUS 11:15"> And I heard a kol gadol (loud voice) in Shomayim, saying, “Now has come the Yeshu'at Eloheinu and the Gevurah (Power) and the Malchut of Hashem Eloheinu and the memshalah (government) of his Moshiach, because the Malshin who is the accuser of our Achim b'Moshiach, the one accusing them before Hashem yomam valailah (day and night) was thrown down.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Job 1:9-11; Zech 3:1">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:3–5; YOCHANAN 16:33; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:5"> And they won the nitzachon (victory) over the Dragon NACHASH because of the dahm of the SEH (Lamb),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Ex 12:22-23; Isa 53:7, Moshiach">m</a></sup>and because of the dvar of the eidushaft (testimony) of them and because they did not have ahavah for their neshamot unto Mavet and were willing to die al kiddush ha-Shem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 49:13; HISGALUS 8:13; TEHILLIM 96:11–13"> Therefore be glad, Shomayim, and the ones whose mishkan is there, but oy to ha'aretz (the earth) and to hayam (the sea), because HaSatan came down to you, having great ka'as (anger), having da'as that his time is short.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Ps 96:11; Isa 44:23; 49:13">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 3:15; HISGALUS 12:3–5; YOCHANAN 16:33"> And when the Dragon NACHASH saw that he was thrown down to ha'aretz, he brought redifah (persecution) on the ISHA who gave birth to the zachar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:4; YESHAYAH 40:31; HISGALUS 12:6"> And two wings of the Nesher hagadol (great Eagle) were given to the ISHA that she might fly to her place in the midbar, where she is nourished there for a time and times and half a time, away from the presence of the NACHASH (Serpent).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Ex 19:4; Dan 7:25; 12:7">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:7; YESHAYAH 59:19; TEHILLIM 18:4"> And the NACHASH spewed mayim (water) after the ISHA from its mouth as a river, that he might make her carried away by a river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 17:6; SHEMOT 12:35–36; MELACHIM BAIS 8:9"> And ha'aretz (the earth) aided the ISHA and ha'aretz opened its mouth and swallowed the river which the Dragon NACHASH spewed out of its mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HISGALUS 14:12; BERESHIS 3:15; HISGALUS 11:7"> And the Dragon NACHASH had great ka'as (anger) at the ISHA and went away to make milchamah with the rest of her ZERA<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Gn 3:15; Isa 53:10">p</a></sup>(seed), the ones keeping the mitzvot of Hashem and having the eidus (testimony),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="See eidus, edus, edut in the Introduction to the Book of Iyov. Witness is what these words mean, and they are transliterated variously in the OJB, also eidushaft">q</a></sup>of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And then the Dragon NACHASH took his stand on the seashore sand of the yam (sea).</span>
