<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DANIEL 12:8–9; HISGALUS 4:1"> And I was weeping copiously because no one was found worthy to open the sefer (book) or to look into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YESHAYAH 11:1; YIRMEYAH 23:5–6; HISGALUS 22:16"> And one of the Zekenim says to me, Do not weep, hinei, HaAryeh (The Lion) from the Shevet Yehudah (Tribe of Judah), the Shoresh Dovid (Root of Dovid), has won the nitzachon (victory) and he is able to open the sefer (book) and its sheva chotamot (seven seals).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gn 49:9; Isa 11:1,10">b</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 4:10; YOCHANAN 1:29; HISGALUS 5:12"> And I saw between the Kes (Throne) and the Arbah Chayyot (four living beings) and among the Zekenim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ex 12:21">c</a></sup>(Elders) a SEH (Lamb),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 53:7, Moshiach">d</a></sup>having stood as having been slain, having sheva karnayim (horns),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="omnipotence">e</a></sup>sheva eynayim (eyes),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="omniscience">f</a></sup>which are the sheva ruchot (spirits) of Hashem having been sent into kol ha'aretz (all the earth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HISGALUS 5:1; HISGALUS 4:2–3"> And the SEH (Lamb)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 53:7">g</a></sup>came and has taken the sefer out of the yamin (right hand) of the One sitting on the Kes (Throne).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 141:2; HISGALUS 8:3–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:9–11"> And when the SEH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isa 53:7">h</a></sup>received the sefer, the Arbah Chayyot (four living beings) and the esrim v'arba'ah Zekenim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ex 12:21">i</a></sup>(twenty-four Elders) fell down before the SEH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Isa 53:7">j</a></sup>(Lamb), each one having a nevel (harp) and golden ke'arot (bowls) full of ketoret (incense), which are the tefillos (prayers) of the Kedoshim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ps 141:2; 16:3">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:18–19; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7; MATTITYAHU 26:28"> And they are singing a SHIR CHADASH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Ps 96:1">l</a></sup>saying, Worthy art thou to take the sefer (book) and to open the chotamot (seals) of it, because you were slain and with your dahm &#91;kapporah&#93; you paid the price for the Geulah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Lv 25:50-51">m</a></sup>redemption and purchased ones for Hashem from every mishpochah (family) and lashon (tongue) and am (people) and goy (nation),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Ps 40:3; 98:1; Isa 42:10">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:6; DANIEL 7:27; HISGALUS 20:6"> And made them for Eloheinu a Malchut and kohanim, and they will reign on ha'aretz (the earth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DANIEL 7:10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:22; HISGALUS 4:4"> And I saw and I heard the kol (voice) of many malachim (angels); they numbered myriads of myriads and v'alfei alafim (thousands of thousands), around the Kes (Throne) and the Chayyot (the living beings) and the Zekenim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Ex 12:21">o</a></sup>(Elders),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Dan 7:10">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HISGALUS 5:9; HISGALUS 19:1; HISGALUS 4:11"> Saying with a kol gadol, Worthy is the SEH (Lamb),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Ex 12:3; Isa 53:7Moshiach">q</a></sup>having been slain, to receive the oz (power) and the osher (wealth) and the chochmah (wisdom) and the gevurah (strength) and hod (honor) and kavod (glory) and bracha (blessing).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:11; TEHILLIM 148:2–13"> And every beriyah (creature) which is in Shomayim and on ha'aretz (the earth) and under ha'aretz and on the yam (sea) and all things in them, I heard saying, To the One sitting on the Kes (Throne) and to the SEH (Lamb),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Ex 12:3; Isa 53:7Moshiach">r</a></sup>be the bracha (blessing) and the hod (honor) and the hadar (glory) and the memshalah (dominion) ad Olemei Olamim (forever and ever).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="1Chr 29:11; Mal 1:6; 2:2">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:4; HISGALUS 4:9–11; HISGALUS 5:8"> And the Arbah Chayyot were saying, Omein. And the Zekenim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Ex 12:21">t</a></sup>(Elders) fell down and worshiped.</span>
