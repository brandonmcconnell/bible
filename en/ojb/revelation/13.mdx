<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HISGALUS 12:3; HISGALUS 17:3; DANIEL 7:23–25"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="CHAPTER 13 COULD BE TITLED: THE “NUREMBERG RALLIES” OF THE LITTLE MOUTH OF THE LITTLE HORN (Dan 7:8) WHO IS THE CHAYYAH (BEAST, ANTI-MOSHIACH)">a</a></sup>And I saw out of the yam (sea) a Chayyah (Beast, Anti-Moshiach) coming up, having eser (ten) karnayim (horns) and shiva (seven) heads and on the horns of it asarah (ten) diadems and on the heads of it there were names of Chillul Hashem gidufim (blasphemies).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dan 7:1-6; 11:36">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DANIEL 7:4–7; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:8; HISGALUS 17:12"> And the Chayyah which I saw was like a namer and the feet of it as a dov and the mouth of it as the mouth of an aryeh and the Dragon NACHASH gave to the Chayyah its ko'ach and its Kes and its great samchut (authority).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HISGALUS 17:8; HISGALUS 13:14; HISGALUS 17:17"> And one of its roshim (heads) seemed to have received a mortal wound and to have been slain, and the wound causing its mavet was healed, and kol ha'aretz marveled, following after the Chayyah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HISGALUS 13:2; HISGALUS 18:18; HISGALUS 13:15"> And they worshiped the Dragon NACHASH, because he gave his memshalah (governing authority) to the Chayyah, and they worshiped the Chayyah, saying, “Who is like the Chayyah?” and “Who is able to make war with it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DANIEL 7:8; DANIEL 7:25; DANIEL 11:36"> And a PEH (mouth) was given to the Chayyah saying haughty words and Chillul Hashem gidufim (blasphemies), and was given to it ko'ach to act for shnayim and arba'im (two and forty) chodashim (months).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Dan 7:8,11,20,25; 11:36">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:34; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:24; HISGALUS 12:12"> And it opened the PEH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="PEH in verses 5 and 6, see Dan 7:8,20">d</a></sup>(mouth) of it in Chillul Hashem gidufim (blasphemies) against Hashem to blaspheme ha-Shem (the Name) of him and his Mishkan, that is, the ones tabernacling in Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DANIEL 7:21; HISGALUS 11:7; DANIEL 7:25"> And was given to it to make milchamah (war) with the Kedoshim and to overcome the Kedoshim, and was given to it samchut (authority) over every shevet (tribe) and am (people) and lashon (language) and goy (nation).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dan 7:21">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19–20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:4; MATTITYAHU 25:34"> And all the ones dwelling on ha'aretz (the earth) will worship the Chayyah (Beast, Anti-Moshiach), all the ones whose name has not been written in the Sefer HaChayyim of the SEH (Lamb)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ex 12:3; Isa 53:7, Moshiach">f</a></sup>having been slain from lifnei hivvased tevel (before the foundation of the world).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:7; HISGALUS 2:17; HISGALUS 2:11"> If anyone has an ear let him hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 15:2; YESHAYAH 33:1; HISGALUS 14:12"> If anyone is to go LA'SHVEE (into captivity), into LA'SHVEE (captivity) he goes. If anyone is to be killed by a cherev (sword), he by a cherev (sword) is to be killed. Here is the savlanut (patient endurance) and the emunah (faith) of the Kedoshim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Jer 15:2; 43:11">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DANIEL 7:8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:13–15; MATTITYAHU 7:15"> And I saw another Chayyah (Beast, the Navi Sheker, the False Prophet) coming up out of ha'aretz (the earth), and it had shtey karnayim (two horns) like a SEH, and it was speaking like a Dragon NACHASH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HISGALUS 17:10–11; HISGALUS 14:11; HISGALUS 14:9"> And all the samchut (authority) of the Chayyah harishonah (the first Beast, the Anti-Moshiach) it exercises on &#91;the Anti-Moshiachʼs&#93; behalf, and it causes ha'aretz (the earth) and the ones dwelling on it to worship the Chayyah harishonah (the first Beast) whose wound of death was healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:24; HISGALUS 19:20; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:9–10"> And it (the Navi Sheker, the False Prophet) does otot gedolim (great signs), even making eish (fire) come down out of Shomayim to ha'aretz (the earth) before Bnei Adam.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="1Kgs 18:38; 2Kgs 1:10">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HISGALUS 12:9; HISGALUS 19:20; HISGALUS 13:8"> And it leads kol ha'aretz (all the earth) into hona'ah (deception) because of the otot (signs) which were given to it to perform before the Chayyah (Beast), telling the ones dwelling on ha'aretz (the earth) to make an Atzav<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Atzav in verses 14 and 15, see 1Sm 31:9; Isa 21:9">i</a></sup>(idol, image, PESEL, graven image) to the Chayyah (Anti-Moshiach) who has the wound of the cherev (sword) and yet came alive again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HISGALUS 20:4; HISGALUS 19:20; DANIEL 3:3–6"> And it was given to it to give breath to the Atzav (idol, image, PESEL) of the Chayyah (Anti-Moshiach) that the Atzav (idol, image) of the Chayyah might even speak and might cause that as many as would not worship the Atzav (idol, image) of the Chayyah (Beast, Anti-Moshiach) to be killed and to die al kiddush ha-Shem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Dan 3:3-6">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HISGALUS 20:4; HISGALUS 19:20; YECHEZKEL 9:4"> And it causes all, the Ketanim (small) and the Gedolim (great), both the oishir (rich) and aniyim (poor), the bnei Chorin (freedmen) and avadim (slaves), that to them should be given a tav (mark) on the yamin (right hand) and on the metsakh (forehead) of them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ezek 9:4">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HISGALUS 15:2; HISGALUS 13:16; HISGALUS 14:11"> And that no one should be able to buy or to sell except the one having the tav (mark), the name of the Chayyah (Beast, Anti-Moshiach) or the number of its name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HISGALUS 15:2; HISGALUS 17:9; HISGALUS 21:17"> Here is chochmah: the one having binah, let him do the gematria and get the mispar (number) of the Chayyah (Anti-Moshiach), for it is the mispar (number) of a man, and the mispar of it is SHESH ME'OT V'SHISHIM VASHESH (666).</span>
