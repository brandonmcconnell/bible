<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HISGALUS 7:17; YOCHANAN 4:14; TEHILLIM 36:8–9"> And he showed me a nahar (river) of Mayim Chayyim (Water of Life) bright as crystal, going forth out of the Kisse (Throne) of Hashem and of the SEH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ex 12:3; Isa 53:7Moshiach">a</a></sup>(Lamb).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 36:8; 46:4; Ezek 47:1; Zech 14:8">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HISGALUS 22:1; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:24; HISGALUS 2:7"> In the middle of the rekhov (street) of it, and on either side on the nahar (river) was the Etz HaChayyim, producing Shneym Asar Perot (Twelve Fruits), according to each chodesh (month), yielding the p'ri of it, and the leaves of the Etz (tree) were for the refu'ah (healing) of the Nations.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ezek 47:12">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:21; HISGALUS 21:3–4; YOCHANAN 12:26"> And there shall no longer be any Kelalah (Curse), and the Kisse (Throne) of Hashem and of the SEH (Lamb),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ex 12:3; Isa 53:7Moshiach">d</a></sup>shall be in it, and His avadim shall serve Him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Zech 14:11">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:8; HISGALUS 14:1; HISGALUS 3:12"> And they shall see His face, and ha-Shem of Him will be on the metsakhim (foreheads) of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YESHAYAH 60:19–20; DANIEL 7:18; HISGALUS 20:4"> And there shall no longer be Lailah, and they have no need of the ohr (light) of a menorah and the ohr (light) of the shemesh (sun), because Hashem Adonoi will give forth ohr (light) on them, and they shall reign l'Olemei Olamim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isa 60:19; Dan 7:27">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:1; HISGALUS 21:5; HISGALUS 19:9"> And he said to me, “These devarim are ne'emanim and amittiyim, and Hashem, the Elohei ruchot hanevi'im (G-d of the spirits of the prophets) sent His malach (angel) to show to His avadim (servants) things which are imminent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:3; HISGALUS 3:11; HISGALUS 22:20"> And hinei, I am coming quickly. Ashrey is the one who is shomer, keeping the devarim hanevu'ah (words of the prophecy) of this sefer (book).”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:10; HISGALUS 1:1; HISGALUS 19:19"> And I, Yochanan, am the one hearing and seeing these things. And when I heard and saw, I fell to worship before the feet of the malach (angel) showing me these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:18–19; HISGALUS 14:7"> And he says to me, “See that you do not do it; a fellow eved of yours I am, and of your Achim, the Nevi'im (prophets), and of the ones who are shomer, keeping the devarim of this sefer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DANIEL 8:26; HISGALUS 1:3; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:7"> worship Hashem!” And he says to me, “Do not seal up the devarim hanevu'ah (words of the prophecy) of this sefer (book), for the zman (time) is at hand.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Dan 8:26">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DANIEL 12:10; MISHLE 4:18; YECHEZKEL 3:27"> Let the one being unrighteous, let him be unrighteous noch (still); and let the one who is filthy be filthy noch, and let the tzaddik be a tzaddik noch,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Dan 9:25">h</a></sup>and let the kadosh yitkadesh noch (holy person be sanctified still).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ezek 3:27; Dan 12:10">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:27; YESHAYAH 62:11; YESHAYAH 40:10"> Hinei, I am coming quickly, and My sachar (reward) is with Me, to give to each one according to what he has done.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Isa 40:10; 62:11">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:8; HISGALUS 21:6; YESHAYAH 44:6"> I am the Aleph and the Tav, HaRishon (The First) and HaAcharon (The Last), HaReshit (The Beginning) and HaTachlit (The Ultimate).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:27; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:3; HISGALUS 22:7"> Ashrey are the ones washing their kaftans, that they may have the right to the Etz HaChayyim (the Tree of Life) and they may enter by the she'arim (gates) into the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:19–21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:2"> Outside are the kelevim (dogs) and the mekhashfim (sorcerers, abusers of drugs);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="T.N. the original language “pharmakoi” is where we derive the word for “drugs”; sorcerers often abused drugs to hallucinate or induce a passive state when they worked their magic and witchcraft; see Ga 5:20and Rv 21:8">k</a></sup>and the zonim (sexually immoral) and the rotzkhim (murderers) and the ovdei haelilim (idolaters) and everyone loving and practicing sheker.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Dt 23:18">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HISGALUS 5:5; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19; YESHAYAH 11:1"> I, Yehoshua sent My malach (angel) to give solemn eidus (testimony) to you of these things, for the Kehillot. I am the Shoresh (root) and the tzemach of Dovid, the Kokhav HaShachar.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:14; HISGALUS 21:6; YESHAYAH 55:1–3"> And the Ruach Hakodesh and the Kallah say, “Bo!” And the one hearing let him say, “Bo!” And the one thirsting, let him come, the one desiring let him take the Mayim Chayyim freely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:32; DEVARIM 4:2; MISHLE 30:6"> I give solemn edut to everyone hearing the divrei haNevu'ah of this sefer: if anyone adds to them, Hashem will add to him the makkot (plagues) which are written in this sefer;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Dt 4:2; 13:1; Prov 30:6">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:2; LUKAS 11:52; HISGALUS 22:2"> And if anyone takes away from the devarim of the sefer of this nevu'ah (prophecy), Hashem will take away his share of the Etz HaChayyim and from the Ir HaKodesh, which are written of in this sefer.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Dt 4:2; 13:1; Prov 30:6">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="HISGALUS 22:7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:28; HISGALUS 22:12"> He who gives solemn edut to these things says, “Ken! I am coming bahlt (soon)!” Omein. Come Adoneinu Yehoshua!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:20; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:14"> The Chen v'Chesed Hashem of Adoneinu Yehoshua be with all. Omein.</span>
