<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HISGALUS 11:12; SHEMOT 24:12; HISGALUS 1:19"> After these things I looked, and, hinei, a delet (door) having been opened in Shomayim, the kol harishon (the first voice),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1:10">a</a></sup>the voice like a shofar that I heard speaking to me, said, Come up here! And I will show you MAH DI LEHEVE<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dan 2:28f">b</a></sup>(what will happen) after these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:19; HISGALUS 1:10; YESHAYAH 6:1"> At once I was in the Ruach Hakodesh, and, hinei, there in Shomayim stood a Kes (Throne), and upon the Kes DEMUT KEMAREH ADAM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ezek 1:26-28">c</a></sup>(a figure in appearance like a Man),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1Kgs 22:19; Isa 6:1; Dan 7:9">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 1:28; HISGALUS 21:11; BERESHIS 9:13–16"> And the One sitting there was KEMAREH (in appearance) like jasper stone and carnelian, and a keshet beanan (rainbow)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gn 9:16">e</a></sup>was around the Kes (Throne) that looks like an emerald.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ezek 1:28">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 19:28; HISGALUS 11:16; HISGALUS 4:10"> And around the Kes (Throne) were esrim v'arba'ah kisot (twenty-four thrones) and on the kisot were sitting esrim v'arba'ah Zekenim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ex 12:21">g</a></sup>(twenty-four Elders), each clothed in lavan (white), each wearing a kittel, and on the roshim (heads) of them, golden atarot (crowns).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HISGALUS 11:19; HISGALUS 8:5; HISGALUS 1:4"> And out of the Kes (Throne) comes forth lightning and sounds and thunders and there were sheva lapidei eish (torches of fire) burning before the Kes (Throne), which are the sheva ruchot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Rv 1:4">h</a></sup>(spirits) of Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ex 19:16; Zech 4:2">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HISGALUS 15:2; HISGALUS 5:6; HISGALUS 14:3"> And before the Kes (Throne) there was something like a sea of glass, like crystal. And on each side and around the Kes (Throne) there were Arbah Chayyot (four living beings), being full of eynayim in front and in back.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ezek 1:5">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 10:14; YECHEZKEL 1:10; YECHEZKEL 10:21"> And harishonah (the first) of HaChayyot (the living beings)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ezek 1:10; 1:14">k</a></sup>was like an aryeh (lion), and hasheniyah (the second) of HaChayyot (the living beings) like an egel (calf), and hashlishit (the third) of HaChayyot had the face of a ben Adam (human being), and hareve'it (fourth) of HaChayyot was like a flying nesher (eagle).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YESHAYAH 6:2–13; HISGALUS 1:8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:8"> And the Arba HaChayyot (four living beings), each one of them had six wings and they were full of eynayim (eyes) without and within. And yomam valailah (day and night), they do not cease to rest but continue singing, kadosh, kadosh, kadosh, adonoi tzva'ot, the One who was and is and is to come.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Isa 6:3; Ezek 1:18; Isa 6:3">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HISGALUS 5:13–14; HISGALUS 7:11–12; HISGALUS 10:6"> And whenever the Chayyot will give kavod (glory) and hod (splendor) and hadar (majesty) and shevakh (praise) to the One sitting on the Kes (Throne), to Him that Hu Chai ad olemei olamim (that lives forever and ever),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Ps 47:8">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HISGALUS 5:8; HISGALUS 19:4; TEHILLIM 95:6"> Then the esrim v'arba'ah Zekenim fall prostrate before the One sitting on the Kes (Throne) and worship the One that Hu Chai ad olemei olamim (lives forever and ever) and cast down their atarot (crowns) before the Kes (Throne), saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Dt 33:3">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:16–17; HISGALUS 5:12; YESHAYAH 40:28"> Worthy art Thou, Adoneinu and Eloheinu, to receive hod (honor) and hadar (splendor) and oz (power), because it was your “BARAH” that created all things, and because they existed and came to be by your ratzon (will).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Gn 1:1">o</a></sup></span>
