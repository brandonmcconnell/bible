<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 6:2; MATTITYAHU 10:34; HISGALUS 13:10"> And another sus (horse) went forth, a flame-red one, and to the one sitting on it was given him to take shalom from ha'aretz (the earth) with men slaughtering one another, and was given to him a cherev gedolah (great sword).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Zech 1:8; 6:2">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 6:2; YECHEZKEL 4:16; ZECHARYAH 6:6"> And when he opened the chotam hashlishi (third seal), I heard hashlishit of HaChayyot (the third of the living beings), saying, Come and see! And I saw and hinei, a sus shakhor (black horse), and the one sitting on it having a pair of scales in his yad (hand).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Zech 6:2">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HISGALUS 9:4; HISGALUS 4:6; HISGALUS 7:3"> And I heard, as it were, a kol (voice) in the midst of the Arbah Chayyot (four living beings), saying, “A quart of wheat for a denarius and shloshah quarts of barley for a denarius, but the shemen (oil) and the yayin (wine) you may not harm.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ezek 4:16">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HISGALUS 4:7; HISGALUS 6:3; HISGALUS 6:1"> And when he opened the chotam harevi'i (the fourth seal), I heard the kol (voice) of hareve'it (the fourth) of HaChayyot (the living beings) saying, Come and see!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="HOSHEA 13:14; YIRMEYAH 15:2–3; HISGALUS 1:18"> And I saw, and, hinei, a sus yerakrak (a greenish pale horse), and the one sitting upon it, that riderʼs name was Mavet, and She'ol was following with him. And there was given to them samchut (authority) over a quarter of ha'aretz (the earth), to kill with cherev (sword) and with ra'av (famine) and with mavet (death) and by the chayyot (beasts) of ha'aretz (the earth).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Zech 6:3; Hos 13:14; Jer 15:2,3; 24:10; Ezek 5:12,17">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HISGALUS 20:4; YOCHANAN 16:2; HISGALUS 1:9"> And when he opened the chotam hachamishi (fifth seal), I saw underneath the Mizbe'ach (altar) the nefashot (souls) of the ones having died al kiddush ha-Shem, having been slain as martyrs because of the dvar Hashem (word of G-d) and because of their solemn eidus (testimony) which they had given.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ex 29:12; Lv 4:7">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:2; TEHILLIM 9:12; HISGALUS 16:5–7"> And they cried out, saying, Rabbono shel Olam, HaKadosh and HaNe'eman, ad mosai (how much longer) is it to be until you judge and avenge dahmeinu (our blood) on the inhabitants of ha'aretz (the earth)?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ps 119:84; Zech 1:12; Dt 32:43; 2Kgs 9:7; Ps 79:10">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HISGALUS 14:13; HISGALUS 7:9; HISGALUS 3:4–5"> And they were each given a kittel (white robe) and it was told them that they will rest yet a little while, until the mispar (number) should be complete of their fellow avadim (servants) and their Achim b'Moshiach, the ones about to die al kiddush ha-Shem, being about to be killed as martyrs as they were.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:29; YOEL 2:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:19–20"> And I saw when he opened the chotam hashishi (sixth seal), a great earthquake occurred and the shemesh (sun) became shakhor (black) as sackcloth made of hair and the whole levanah (moon) became like dahm.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ps 97:4; Isa 29:6; Ezek 38:19; Isa 50:3">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YESHAYAH 34:4; MATTITYAHU 24:29; HISGALUS 9:1"> And the kokhavim (stars) of Shomayim fell to ha'aretz (the earth), as an etz te'enah (fig tree) casts off its unripe figs when being shaken by a great wind.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Isa 34:4">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YESHAYAH 34:4; HISGALUS 16:20; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:10"> And Shomayim split apart as a megillah scroll being rolled up; and every mountain and island were moved out of their places.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ps 46:2; Isa 54:10; Jer 4:24; Ezek 38:20; Nah 1:5">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YESHAYAH 2:19; YESHAYAH 2:21; YESHAYAH 24:21–22"> And the melachim (kings) of ha'aretz (the earth) and the gedolim (great ones) and the military leaders and the oishirim (rich men) and the strong men and all avadim (slaves) and Bnei Chorin (freedmen) hid themselves in the caves and in the rocks of the mountains.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Isa 2:10,19-21">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 23:30; HOSHEA 10:8; HISGALUS 4:2"> And they say to the mountains and to the rocks, Fall on us and hide us from the face of the One sitting on the Kes (Throne) and from the za'am<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="16:1f">m</a></sup>(wrath, anger) of the SEH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Ex 12:3; Isa 53:7Moshiach">n</a></sup>(Lamb),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hos 10:8">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MALACHI 3:2; YOEL 2:11; TEHILLIM 76:7"> Because the Yom HaGadol, the Yom HaZa'am, the Great Day of Their Wrath has come, and who is able to stand?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Yoel 1:15; 2:1,2,11; 3:4; Zeph 1:14,15; Nah 1:6; Mal 3:2">p</a></sup></span>
