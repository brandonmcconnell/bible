<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:10; HISGALUS 3:12; HISGALUS 21:10"> And I saw the Ir Hakodesh, the Yerushalayim HaChadasha coming down and descending out of Shomayim from Hashem, having been made ready as a Kallah adorned for her Ba'al (Husband).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Neh 11:18; Isa 52:1">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 37:27; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:16; YOCHANAN 14:23"> And I heard a kol gadol (loud voice), a Bat Kol from the Kisse (Throne) saying, “Hinei, The Mishkan of Hashem is with men, and He shall tabernacle with them, and they shall be His people, and Hashem Himself shall dwell among them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ex 25:8; 2Chr 6:18; Ezek 48:35; Zech 2:10">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YESHAYAH 25:8; HISGALUS 7:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:26"> And He will wipe away every tear from their eyes, and there shall not be any longer Mavet, nor Avelut nor weeping nor pain; the former things have passed away.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 25:8; 35:10">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:17; YESHAYAH 42:9; YESHAYAH 43:19"> And said the One sitting on the Kisse (Throne), “Hinei, I make all things chadash.” And He says, “Write, for these devarim are ne'emanim and amittiyim.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:37–38; YOCHANAN 4:10; HISGALUS 7:17"> And He said to me, “It is finished! I am the Alef and the Tav, the Reshit and the Ketz. To the one thirsting I will give of the Ma'ayan (Fountain) of Mayim Chayyim (Water of Life) freely.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 55:1">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:18; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:10; HISGALUS 2:11"> The one who wins the nitzachon (victory), shall inherit these things and I will be his G-d and he will be to Me beni (my son).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2Sm 7:14">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:9–10; HISGALUS 2:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:5–6"> But for those of morech lev (cowardliness) and those without emunah (faith) and the ones having become vile and ratzchaniyot (murderers) and zonim (fornicators) and mekhashfim (sorcerers) and ovdei haelilim (idolaters) and all the shakranit (liars), their chelek<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Yn 13:8">f</a></sup>will be in the Agam (Lake) burning with Eish (fire) and Gofrit (sulfur), which is HaMavet HaSheyni (the Second Death).”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ps 5:6; Isa 66:24">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:2; HISGALUS 19:7; HISGALUS 16:1–17"> And one of the shivat hamalachim having the sheva ke'arot (bowls) being full of the last sheva makkot (plagues), came and spoke with me, saying “Bo! I will show you the Kallah of the SEH”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ex 12:3; Isa 53:7Moshiach">h</a></sup>(Lamb).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:2; YECHEZKEL 11:24; YECHEZKEL 40:1–49"> And he carried me away in the Ruach Hakodesh onto a great and high mountain, and showed me the Ir Hakodesh, Yerushalayim, descending and coming down out of Shomayim from Hashem,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ezek 40:2">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HISGALUS 4:6; HISGALUS 22:5; HISGALUS 22:1"> Having the Shekhinah kavod (glory) of Hashem. Her brilliance, was like a precious stone, even a jasper, clear as crystal,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Isa 60:1,2; Ezek 43:2">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 48:31–34; HISGALUS 7:4–8; HISGALUS 21:17–21"> Having a great and high wall, having Shneym Asar She'arim (Twelve Gates), and at the She'arim (Gates), Shneym Asar malachim (Twelve angels), and SHEMOT (Names) were inscribed on them, which are the shemot (names) of the Shneym Asar Shivtei HaBnei Yisroel (Twelve Tribes of the Bnei Yisroel).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ezek 48:30-34">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 48:31–34"> There were shloshah she'arim (three gates) on the mizrach (east), there were shloshah she'arim on the tzafon (north), and shloshah she'arim on the darom (south) and shloshah she'arim on the ma'arav (west).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:20; YESHAYAH 54:11"> And the wall of the Ir Hakodesh had Shneym Asar Foundation Stones, and on them were the Shneym Asar shemot (names) of the Shneym Asar Shlichim of the SEH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Ex 12:3; Isa 53:7Moshiach">l</a></sup>(Lamb).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 2:1; HISGALUS 11:1–2; YECHEZKEL 41:1–5"> And the one speaking with me had a gold measuring rod, that he might measure the Ir Hakodesh and its she'arim (gates) and its wall.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Ezek 40:3">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 48:35; YECHEZKEL 48:8–20"> And the city is laid out foursquare, shaped like a cube, and its length is as great as the width; and he measured the Ir Hakodesh with the rod across and it measured Shneym Asar (Twelve) thousand stadia (fifteen hundred miles); the length and the breadth and the height are equal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HISGALUS 7:4; DEVARIM 3:11; HISGALUS 13:18"> And he measured its wall a hundred forty-four cubits (seventy-two yards) by manʼs measurement, which is also that of the malachim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:11; HISGALUS 21:21; HISGALUS 21:19"> And the wall was constructed of jasper and the Ir was pure zahav (gold) like clear glass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 54:11–12; SHEMOT 28:17–21; IYOV 28:16–19"> The foundation stones of the wall of the city were adorned with every kind of precious stone, harishon (the first) foundation stone was jasper, hasheni (the second), sapphire, hashlishi (the third), chalcedony, harevi'i (fourth), emerald;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Ex 28:17-20; Isa 54:11,12; Ezek 28:13">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="HISGALUS 4:3"> The fifth, sardonyx, the sixth, carnelian, the seventh, chrysolite, the eighth, beryl, the ninth, topaz, the tenth, chrysoprase, the eleventh, jacinth, the twelfth, amethyst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:18; HISGALUS 17:4; HISGALUS 22:2"> And the Shneym Asar she'arim were Shneym Asar pearls, respectively each one of the she'arim was a single pearl. And the rekhov (street) of the city was pure zahav (gold), like transparent glass.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Isa 54:12">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:23; MELACHIM ALEF 8:27; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:6"> And I saw no Heikhal in it, for Adonoi Hashem El Shaddai and the SEH (Lamb),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Ex 12:3; Isa 53:7Moshiach">p</a></sup>are its Beis HaMikdash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YESHAYAH 60:19–20; HISGALUS 22:5; HISGALUS 21:11"> And the city has no need of the shemesh (sun) nor of the levanah (moon) that they may shine in it, for the kavod (glory) of Hashem illumined it and its menorah is the SEH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Ex 12:3; Isa 53:7Moshiach">q</a></sup>(Lamb).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Isa 24:23; 60:19">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:26; HISGALUS 22:2; ZECHARYAH 8:22–23"> And the Nations shall walk their derech by its Ohr (Light), and the melachim (kings) of ha'aretz shall bring their glory into it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Isa 60:3,5">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YESHAYAH 60:11; ZECHARYAH 14:7; HISGALUS 22:5"> And b'yom (for there will be no Lailah) the she'arim (gates) of it shall never be shut.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Isa 60:11; Zech 14:7">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:24"> And the melachim will bring the kavod (glory) of the Nations into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="HISGALUS 22:14–15; YESHAYAH 52:1; HISGALUS 20:15"> And never may enter into it any thing tameh and anyone practicing to'evah (abomination) and sheker (falsehood), but only the ones having been written in the Sefer HaChayyim Shel HaSEH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Ex 12:3; Isa 53:7Moshiach">u</a></sup>(the Book of Life of the Lamb).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Isa 52:1; Yoel 4:17">v</a></sup></span>
