<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:27; TEHILLIM 32:2; TZEFANYAH 3:13"> And in the peh of them was not found MIRMAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isa 53:9">h</a></sup>(deceit); they are without mumim (defects).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ps 32:2; Zeph 3:13">i</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:11; HISGALUS 15:4; NECHEMYAH 9:6"> Saying in a kol gadol (loud voice), Have yirat Shomayim toward Hashem and give Him kavod (glory), because the hour of His Yom HaMishpat has come. And worship Him, HaBoreh HaShomayim v'HaAretz (the creator of Heaven and Earth) and Yam (Sea) and ma'ayanot (springs, sources) of mayim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ps 34:9">j</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YESHAYAH 51:17; HISGALUS 16:19; YIRMEYAH 25:15–17"> Such will drink of the yayin of the Charon Af Hashem (burning wrath of Hashem), having been mixed undiluted in the kos (cup) of his Charon Af, and such will be tormented by eish (fire) and gofrit (sulfur) before malachim hakedoshim (the holy angels) and before the SEH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Ex 12:3; Isa 53:7Moshiach">n</a></sup>(Lamb).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Isa 51:17; 66:24; Jer 25:15; 51:7">o</a></sup></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HISGALUS 14:18; HISGALUS 14:14–15; HISGALUS 15:5–6"> And another malach (angel) came out of the Beis Hamikdash in Shomayim, having also with him a sharp MAGGAL (sickle).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Yoel 4:13">x</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YOEL 3:13; HISGALUS 16:8; HISGALUS 14:15–16"> And another malach (angel) came out of the Mizbe'ach (altar), having shilton (authority) over the eish, and he spoke with a kol gadol (loud voice) to the one having the sharp MAGGAL, saying, “Put forth your sharp MAGGAL and gather the clusters of the gefen (vine) of ha'aretz, because the grapes of it are ripe.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Isa 63:1-6">y</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:32–33; HISGALUS 19:15–21"> And the malach (angel) put forth his MAGGAL to ha'aretz (the earth) and gathered the vintage of ha'aretz (the earth) and threw it into the GAT HAGEDOLAH (great winepress)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Yoel 4:13">z</a></sup>of the Charon Af of Hashem (burning wrath of Hashem).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Isa 63:3">aa</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EKHAH 1:15; HISGALUS 19:14–21; YESHAYAH 63:1–3"> And the GAT HAGEDOLAH was trodden on outside the Ir (City), and dahm (blood) came out from the GAT up to the bridles of the susim (horses) for about two hundred miles.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Isa 63:3; Yoel 4:13; Gn 49:11; Dt 32:14">ab</a></sup></span>
