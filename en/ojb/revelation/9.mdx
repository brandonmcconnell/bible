<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HISGALUS 8:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:7; HISGALUS 9:2"> And the malach hachamishi (fifth angel) sounded his shofar; and I saw a kokhav (star) having fallen out of Shomayim to ha'aretz. And was given to it the mafte'ach (key) of the shaft of the Tehom (Abyss).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOEL 2:10; BERESHIS 19:28; YOEL 2:2"> And he opened the shaft of the Tehom (Abyss), and smoke came up out of the shaft as smoke of a great furnace, and the shemesh (sun) was darkened and the air was darkened by the smoke of the shaft.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gn 19:28; Ex 19:18; Yoel 2:2,10">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YOEL 1:4; HISGALUS 9:5; YOEL 2:25"> And out of the smoke came forth arbeh (locusts) to ha'aretz (the earth), and samchut (authority) was given to them like the samchut of the akrabei ha'aretz (scorpions of the earth).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ex 10:12-15">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HISGALUS 7:2–4; YECHEZKEL 9:4; HISGALUS 14:1"> And it was told them that they should not harm the grass of ha'aretz (the earth) nor any greenery nor any etz (tree), except the Bnei Adam, all who do not have the chotam (seal) of Hashem on their metsakhim (foreheads).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ezek 9:4">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HISGALUS 9:10; IYOV 2:6; HISGALUS 9:3"> And it was given to them that they should not kill them, but that they will be tormented chamishah chodashim (five months). Their torment is as the torment of an akrav when it stings a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKAS 23:30; YIRMEYAH 8:3; HISGALUS 6:16"> And in those days Bnei Adam will seek Mavet (Death) and by no means will find it. And they will desire to die and Mavet flees from them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Job 3:21; 7:15; Jer 8:3">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DANIEL 7:8; NACHUM 3:17; YOEL 2:4–5"> And the appearances of the arbeh (locusts) were like susim (horses) having been prepared for milchamah (war), and on the heads of them what looked like atarot (diadems) of zahav (gold) and the faces were like the faces of Bnei Adam.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Yoel 2:4; Dan 7:8">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOEL 1:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:14–15; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:3"> And they had hair that looked like the hair of an isha (a woman), and they had teeth that looked like the teeth of an aryeh (lion),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Yoel 1:6">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOEL 2:5–8; NACHUM 2:4–5; IYOV 39:25"> and they had breastplates like iron breastplates and the sound of the wings of them was as the sound of many merkavot (chariots) with susim (horses) racing into the sadeh krav (battlefield),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Yoel 2:5">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HISGALUS 9:5; HISGALUS 9:3; HISGALUS 9:19"> And they have tails like akrabim (scorpions), with stingers, and the ko'ach (power) of them is in their tails to harm Bnei Adam chamishah chodashim (five months).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="IYOV 26:6; HISGALUS 9:1–2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:2"> They have over them a melech (king), the malach (angel) of the Tehom (Abyss), the name for him in Ivrit, “Abaddon,” (that is, Destruction) and in Elliniki he has the name Apollyon (that is, “Destroyer”).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Job 26:6; 28:22; 31:12; Ps 88:11">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HISGALUS 11:14; HISGALUS 8:13–2; HISGALUS 9:13–21"> The Oy Echad (the First Woe) has passed. Hinei, yet comes two more woes after these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HISGALUS 8:3–5; SHEMOT 30:2–3; HISGALUS 9:1"> And the malach hashishi (the sixth angel) sounded his shofar. And I heard kol echad (one voice) from the four horns of the golden Mizbe'ach (altar) before Hashem,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ex 30:1-3">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HISGALUS 16:12; HISGALUS 9:15; SHMUEL BAIS 8:3"> saying to the malach hashishi, the one having the shofar, Release the arba'at hamalachim (the four angels) having been bound at the great river, Euphrates.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gn 15:18; Dt 1:7; Josh 1:4; Isa 11:15">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HISGALUS 9:18; HISGALUS 8:7; HISGALUS 8:9"> And the arba'at hamalachim (four angels) were released, having been prepared for the sha'ah (hour) and the yom (day) and chodesh (month) and shanah (year), that they should kill a third of Bnei Adam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HISGALUS 7:4; TEHILLIM 68:17; DANIEL 7:10"> And the mispar (number) of tziveot haparashim (troops of cavalry-men) were twice ten thousand times ten thousand. I heard the mispar of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HISGALUS 9:18; HISGALUS 14:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:8"> And thus I saw the susim (horses) in my chazon (vision, revelation, prophecy) and the riders on them, having breastplates fiery red and hyacinth blue and sulfur yellow, and the heads of the susim like heads of arayot (lions), and from the mouths of them goes forth eish (fire) and smoke and gofrit (sulfur).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ps 11:6; Isa 30:33; Ezek 38:22">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HISGALUS 9:17; HISGALUS 9:15"> From these shlosh hamakkot (three plagues)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="this word makkah, makkot [pl.] comes from the Pesach Haggadah “eser makkot” or ten plagues, and is a key word from here on in the book of Revelation, showing the end-time plagues of the Brit Chadasha Exodus of the Geulah Redemption">l</a></sup>were killed a third of Bnei Adam, by the eish (fire) and the smoke and the gofrit (sulfur) coming out of the mouth of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 9:15; HISGALUS 9:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:14"> For the ko'ach (power) of the susim (horses) is in their mouths and in their tails, for their tails are like nechashim (serpents), having heads, and with them they inflict harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DANIEL 5:23; DEVARIM 31:29; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:1"> And the rest of the Bnei Adam, the ones not killed by these makkot (plagues), did not make teshuva and turn from the ma'asim (deeds) of their hands or forsake worship of shedim (demons) and the itztzavim (idols, images, tzelamim), those of gold and of silver and of bronze and of stone and of wood, which neither are able to see nor to hear nor to walk.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Dt 4:28; 31:29; Jer 1:16; Mic 5:13">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:21; HISGALUS 17:5"> And they did not make teshuva (repentance, turning from sin to G-d) of the retzichot (murders) of them nor of the kishufim (sorceries) of them nor of the zenunim (fornication) of them nor of the gneyvot (thefts) of them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Isa 47:9,12">n</a></sup></span>
