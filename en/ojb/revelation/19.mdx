<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HISGALUS 7:10–12; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:11; HISGALUS 11:15"> After these things I heard, as it were, a kol gadol (loud voice) of a great, multititude in Shomayim, saying, “Praise Hashem! The Yeshu'ah (Salvation) and the Kavod (Glory) and the Gevurah (Power) is of Eloheinu!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:43; HISGALUS 6:10; HISGALUS 18:20"> Because emes and tzedek are His judgments because He judged the Zonah Hagedolah who was corrupting ha'aretz (the earth) with the zenut (fornication) of her and He took vengeance on her who has on her hands the dahm of His servants.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YESHAYAH 34:10; HISGALUS 18:18; HISGALUS 14:11"> And again they said, “Praise Hashem! The smoke of her ascends l'olemei olamim!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Isa 34:10">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HISGALUS 5:14; NECHEMYAH 8:6; TEHILLIM 106:48"> And the esrim v'arba'ah Zekenim (twenty-four Elders) fell down and the Arbah Chayyot (four living beings) and they worshiped Hashem who sits on the Kes (Throne), saying, “Omein, Praise Hashem!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 134:1; TEHILLIM 148:11–13; TEHILLIM 150:6"> And a kol (voice) from the Kes (Throne) came forth, saying, “Say ‘Baruch Hashem Eloheinu,’ all you avadim (servants) of him and the ones with yirat Shomayim, ketanim (small ones) and the gedolim (great ones).”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 134:1; 115:13">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 97:1; HISGALUS 14:2; TEHILLIM 93:1"> And I heard, as it were, a sound of a great multitude and a sound like mayim rabbim (many waters) and a sound like mighty thunder saying, “Baruch Hashem! Because Hashem Eloheinu reigns, El Shaddai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:9; HISGALUS 21:2; HISGALUS 19:9"> Let us have simcha and exult in kavod (glory) to him because of the Bedeken of the Kallah of the SEH (Lamb),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ex 12:3; Isa 53:7Moshiach">c</a></sup>and the Chosonʼs Tish and she has made herself ready.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YESHAYAH 61:10; HISGALUS 7:13–14; TEHILLIM 132:9"> And it was given to her that she should be clothed with fine linen, bright and tahor (clean); for the fine linen are the Tzedakot of the Kedoshim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 61:10; Ezek 44:17; Zech 3:4">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HISGALUS 22:6; MATTITYAHU 22:2–4; LUKAS 14:15–16"> And he says to me, Write: “Ashrey are the ones having been summoned to the Seudas Moshiach, the Seudas Yom Nisu'im of the SEH”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ex 12:3; Isa 53:7Moshiach">e</a></sup>(Lamb). And he says to me, “These are divrei Emes of Hashem.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HISGALUS 22:8–9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:25–26; YOCHANAN 5:39"> And I fell before his feet to worship him. And he says to me, “You must abstain from such. For I am a fellow eved with both you and your chaverim who hold on to the edut (testimony) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach; worship Hashem! For the edut (testimony) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua is the Ruach HaNevu'ah (prophecy).”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TEHILLIM 96:13; YESHAYAH 11:3–5; TEHILLIM 98:9"> And I saw Shomayim having been opened, and, hinei, a sus lavan (white horse) and the one riding on it is called Ne'eman and Yashar, and in Tzedek<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Dan 9:25">f</a></sup>He judges and makes milchamah (war).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ex 15:3; Ps 96:13; Isa 11:4">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:16; LUKAS 10:22; HISGALUS 2:17–18"> And the Eynayim of Him are as a flame of eish, and on the head of Him are many atarot, and He has a Name inscribed of which no one has da'as except Himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:1; YESHAYAH 63:1–6; YOCHANAN 1:14"> And He is robed in a kaftan dipped in dahm, and His Name is called, “The dvar hashem.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isa 63:2,3">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:14; HISGALUS 7:9; HISGALUS 19:8"> And the Tzivos Hashem (the Army of Hashem), the Tzivos HaShomayim, were following Him on susim levanim (white horses). They were dressed in fine linen, lavan (white) and tahor (clean).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YESHAYAH 63:2–6; HISGALUS 1:16; HISGALUS 2:27"> And out of the peh (mouth) of Him goes forth a sharp cherev, that with it He may strike the Goyim, and He will shepherd them with a shevet barzel, and He treads the press of the yayin of the fury of Charon Af of Hashem, El Shaddai.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Isa 11:4; Ps 2:9">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HISGALUS 17:14; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:15; DANIEL 2:47"> And He has on His kaftan and on His thigh a name inscribed: melech hamelachim and adon haadonim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:21; HISGALUS 8:13; YECHEZKEL 39:17–20"> And I saw one malach (angel) having taken his stand in the shemesh (sun) and he cried out in a kol gadol (loud voice), saying to all the birds flying in mid-heaven, “Bo! Gather to the Seudah Hagedolah of Hashem;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Jer 12:9; 46:10; Ezek 39:17; Isa 34:6">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 39:18–20; HISGALUS 6:15; SHMUEL ALEF 17:44"> That you may eat the basar of melachim (kings) and the basar of captains and the basar of strong men and the basar of susim and of their riders and the basar of all, both, bnei Chorin and avadim and the ketanim and the gedolim.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ezek 39:18-20">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="HISGALUS 16:16; HISGALUS 16:14; HISGALUS 17:12–14"> And I saw the Chayyah &#91;Anti-Moshiach&#93; and the melachim (kings) of ha'aretz and their armies assembled to make milchamah (war) with the One sitting on the sus (horse) and with the Tzivos Hashem (Army of Hashem).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="HISGALUS 20:10; HISGALUS 21:8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:8–11"> And the Chayyah &#91;Anti-Moshiach&#93; was captured and, with it, the Navi Sheker (the False Prophet), the one having performed the otot (signs) before it, by which he deceived the ones having received the tav (mark) of the Chayyah &#91;Anti-Moshiach&#93; and the ones worshiping its Atzav (idol, image). These two were cast alive into the Agam HaEish (Lake of Fire) burning with gofrit (sulfur).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Dan 7:11">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:11–15; HISGALUS 1:16; HISGALUS 19:17–19"> And the rest were killed with the cherev (sword) of the One sitting on the sus (horse), the cherev (sword) having proceeded out of the PEH of Him, and all the birds were fully fed by their basar.</span>
</div>
