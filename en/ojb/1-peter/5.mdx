<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:3–4; LUKAS 24:48; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:17–18"> Therefore, I give this word of chozek to the Zekenim (Elders)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Ex 12:21">a</a></sup>among you, I, as a fellow Zaken (Elder) and as an Eidus (Witness) of the sevalot (sufferings) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, and also as a partaker of the Kavod about to be unveiled:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 1:7; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:8; LETTER TO TITOS 3:1"> Exercise the avodas hakodesh ministry of Ro'eh (Shepherd) on behalf of the Eder Hashem(Flock of G-d)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Jer 13:17">b</a></sup>among you, serving as Mashgichim Ruchaniyim (Spiritual Overseers), not unwillingly, for the sake of dishonest gelt, but willingly, eagerly, in conformity with Hashem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:17; YECHEZKEL 34:4"> Not as domineering the ones assigned by Hashem to your oversight, but being a mofet (example) for the tzon (flock).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:25; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:2"> After the Sar HaRo'im (Chief of Shepherds) has been manifested,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1:20">c</a></sup>you will receive the unfading Ateret HaKavod (Crown of Glory).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:6; MISHLE 3:34; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:21"> Likewise, bochrim (young men), be submissive to the Zekenim (Elders) and clothe yourselves in the kaftan of anavah (humility) toward one another, because IM LALETZIM HU YALITZ V'LA'ANAYIM YITEN CHEN<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Prov 3:34">d</a></sup>(Indeed Hashem scorns the scorners, but gives grace to the humble).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10; LUKAS 14:11; MISHLE 29:23"> Therefore, be humbled under the mighty hand of Hashem, that you may be exalted in due time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 55:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:6; TEHILLIM 37:5"> HASHLECH AL ADONOI Y'HAVECHA V'HU Y'CHALKELECHA<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ps 55:23">e</a></sup>(Cast your care on Hashem and He will sustain you), because Hashem cares and is concerned about you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:11; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:27"> Be shomer in zililut da'as (sober-mindedness). Your adversary HaSatan &#91;Samma'el&#93;, prowls around like a roaring arye (lion), seeking whom to swallow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:11–13"> Oppose him, firm in emunah, knowing that in the Olam Hazeh, the same Chevlei &#91;Moshiach&#93; are to be laid upon HaAchim b'Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:17; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:3; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:6–7"> Now the Elohei Kol Chen v'Chesed (the G-d of all Grace), the one having bestowed upon you the kri'ah (calling) into His eternal kavod in Moshiach Yehoshua, after you have suffered tzoros for a little while, will Himself restore, confirm, strengthen and establish you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:36; HISGALUS 1:6"> Lo HaKavod V'HaOz L'Olemei Olamim. Omein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:23; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:1"> To you, through Sila HaAch HaNe'eman (the Faithful Brother in Moshiach), as such I regard him, I have written you briefly, a dvar chizzuk, encouraging you and giving edut (testimony) that this is the true Chen v'Chesed Hashem in which you stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:25; TEHILLIM 87:4"> Drishat Shalom to you from your coequal Nivcharah (Chosen One), she in Bavel. Drishat Shalom also from Markos, beni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:1"> Greet one another with a neshikat ahavah (kiss of agape). Drishat Shalom to all of you, the ones in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
