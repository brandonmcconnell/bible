<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:24; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:3–5"> Therefore, als (since) Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach underwent bodily yissurim (sufferings),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4:13">a</a></sup>also you arm yourselves with the same way of thinking as Moshiach, because the one having suffered in the basar (flesh) has finished with chet (sin),</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:22–24; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:14; LETTER TO TITOS 3:3–8"> So as no longer to live the remaining time of the Olam Hazeh in the basar, in the ta'avot Bnei Adam (lusts of men), but in the ratzon Hashem(will of G-d).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:5"> For you have already spent sufficient time (time already gone by) in the Olam Hazeh participating in the ta'avot (lusts) of the Goyim, walking in darchei zimah (the ways of licentiousness), ta'avot (lusts), shichrut (drunkenness), orgiastic drinking mesibot (parties), and unlawful avodas elilim (idolatry).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:12; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:10"> The Goyim think it strange<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4:12">b</a></sup>that you are not running with them into the same mabbul (flood) of dissipation, and so they blaspheme and commit Chillul Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:42; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:1"> But they will give an account to the One being ready to take his moshav (seat) as the Shofet of HaChayyim v'HaMesim (the Living and the Dead).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:2; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:9–11"> For, indeed, this is the reason that the Besuras HaGeulah was preached to the mesim (dead ones),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e., those who have died in the L-rd">c</a></sup>that, though judged in the basar according to men,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="MJ 12:23; Yn 5:25; 1Th 4:13-18">d</a></sup>they might live according to G-d in the spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:8–9; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:13; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:8"> The Ketz (End) of all things now has drawn near. Let your thinking be one of zililut da'as (sober-mindedness), therefore, and exercise kibush hayetzer (self-control), for the sake of your tefillos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MISHLE 10:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:14; MISHLE 17:9"> Above all else, have fervent ahavah among yourselves, for AHAVAH KOL PEYSHA'IM T'CHASSEH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Prov 10:12">e</a></sup>(love covers all wrongs).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:14"> Practice hachnosas orchim (hospitality) with one another without kevetching,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:6–8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:4–11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:1–2"> Each one according as he received a mattanah (gift), as k'lei kodesh ministering to one another, mefakkechim tovim (good stewards, supervisors) of the varied Chen v'Chesed Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:31; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:10; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10"> If anyone speaks, let it be as though it were the oracles of Hashem; if anyone ministers, let it be as by the chozek (strength) which Hashem supplies, that in all things Hashem may be glorified through Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua. Lo HaKavod V'HaOz L'Olemei Olamim. Omein (To whom be the glory and the power into the ages of the ages. Omein.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:12; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:6–7; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:9"> Chaverim, do not be surprised at the masah (trial) of eish (fire) among you coming for you for your testing, as though some strange thing were happening to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:17; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:2–3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:3"> But in so far as you share in the yissurim (sufferings) of Moshiach, in the Chevlei HaMoshiach, be glad that also in the Hisgalus (Revelation, Apocalypse) of his Kavod, you may have simcha and sasson.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:14; LUKAS 6:22; MATTITYAHU 5:11"> If you are reproached for the Shem HaMoshiach, ashrei (happy, blessed) are you, because the Ruach of Kavod and of Hashem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isa 11:2">f</a></sup>rests upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:11; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:13"> Let not any of you suffer as a rotzeach (murderer) or a ganav (thief) or an oseh ra'ah (an evil doer) or as one who is mitarev (meddlesome).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17–18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:29; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:19"> But if any one of you suffers as a ma'amin b'Moshiach (believer in Moshiach), let him not be ashamed, but let him glorify Hashem by this Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 25:29; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:9"> Because the time has come to begin the Mishpat from the Beis Hashem; and if the Mishpat begins with us, what will be the toitzaa (outcome) of the ones without mishma'at (obedience) to the Besuras HaGeulah of Hashem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MISHLE 11:31; YECHEZKEL 18:24; LUKAS 23:31"> And if indeed the Tzaddik is saved with difficulty, where will appear the man without chasidus &#91;in Moshiach&#93;, the rashah and the choteh?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See quoted Prov 11:31">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="TEHILLIM 37:5; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:12; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17"> Therefore, the ones undergoing sevalot (sufferings, yissurim) according to the ratzon Hashem, let them commit their nefashot to a trustworthy Yotzer (Creator) while they continue to do ma'asim tovim.</span>
