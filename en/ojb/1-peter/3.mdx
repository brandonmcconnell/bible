<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:12; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:27"> When they see your yirat Shomayim and your midas chasidus (quality of piety).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:9–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:2; YESHAYAH 3:18–24"> Do not let your adornment be outward, with coiffures and with wearing gold or fine apparel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:12; SHMUEL ALEF 16:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:29"> But let your adornment be nistar (hidden), adornment shebalev (inwardly) by the imperishable<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1:4">a</a></sup>adorning of the quiet ruach anavah (humble spirit), which is before Hashem of great worth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 2:3–4; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:5; LUKAS 2:37"> For so formerly also the nashim hakedoshot (holy women) whose tikvah was in Hashem were adorning themselves, submitting themselves to their own ba'alim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:7–9; YESHAYAH 57:11"> Just so, Sarah submitted in mishma'at (obedience) to Avraham Avinu, calling him ADONI.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gn 18:12">b</a></sup>You nashim became Sarahʼs banot (daughters), if you act as nashei tzedek<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2:15">c</a></sup>and you fear no intimidation.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Prov 3:25">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:19; BERESHIS 2:23–24; MATTITYAHU 19:3–9"> Likewise, Ba'alim (Husbands), dwell with them according to the da'as of the isha as a k'li rach (weaker vessel), showing them kavod as also being yoreshim together<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3:9">e</a></sup>of the mattanah (gift) of the Chen v'Chesed HaChayyim, so that your tefillos will not be hindered.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="3:12; 4:7">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:31–2; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:22"> Now, to say over, all of you be an agudah (bound together union) in your thinking, have achdus, be sympathetic, having ahavah (agape) for the Achim b'Moshiach,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ps 133:1">g</a></sup>being tender-hearted men of mercy v'shiflei ruach (and lowly of spirit),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MISHLE 20:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:14"> Not rendering ra'ah for ra'ah or lashon hora for lashon hora, but, fahkert (on the contrary), rendering a bracha (blessing), because to this tachlis (purpose) you were given your kri'ah (calling), that you may inherit a bracha (blessing).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 34:12–16; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:26; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1"> HAISH HECHAFETZ CHAYYIM OHEV YAMIM LIROT TOV (For the one wanting to love life and to see good days) NETZOR L'SHONECHA MERAH U'SFATECHA MIDABER MIRMAH (Let him stop his tongue from speaking lashon hora and his lips from speaking remiyah).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="guile, fraud, deceit">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:9; TEHILLIM 34:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:14"> “Let him turn away from resha and let him do that which is good, let him seek shalom and pursue it;”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="TEHILLIM 34:15–16; YOCHANAN 9:31; MISHLE 15:29"> EINEI ADONOI EL TZADDIKIM V'AZNAV EL SHAV'ATAM (Because the tzaddikim are before the eyes of Hashem and His ears are open to their tefillos). P'NEI ADONOI B'OSEI RAH L'HACHRIT ME'ARETZ ZICHRAM (But the face of the L-rd is against the ones doing evil to cut off their memory from the earth).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ps 34:13-16">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MISHLE 16:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:28; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:15"> And who is the one harming you if you have kana'ut (zeal) for HaTov (The Good)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:28; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:19–20; MATTITYAHU 5:10–12"> But if indeed you should suffer tzoros because of Tzidkat Hashem, you are blessed and happy. But do not fear their pachad (terror), neither be troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:6; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:25–26; LUKAS 21:14–15"> But reverence in your levavot Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach as Adoneinu, prepared always for a hitstaddekut (an apologetic defense) to everyone coming to you with a she'elah (question), ready with a word concerning the tikvah in you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:18; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15"> But with anavah (meekness) and yirat Shomayim, having a clear matzpun (conscience),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="3:21">j</a></sup>so that, when you are maligned by lashon hora, your abusers may be humiliated by your midas chasidus in Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:14; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:19; MATTITYAHU 26:39"> For im yirtzeh Hashem (if the L-rd wills), it is better to suffer tzoros for doing mitzvot than for doing ra'ah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:28; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:26; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:21"> Because, indeed, Moshiach suffered once on behalf of chatta'im (sins), a Tzaddik (righteous one) on behalf of the chote'im (unrighteous ones, sinners), that he might bring you to Hashem he, having been in the basar<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ps 16:9-10">k</a></sup>put to death, yet, in the Ruach Hakodesh, having been made alive;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="See 1K 4:6; Isa 53:8">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:6; YESHAYAH 61:1; YESHAYAH 42:7"> In which also to the ruchot (spirits) now in mishmar (prison), having gone, Moshiach made the hachrazah (proclamation, kerygma),</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:7; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5; BERESHIS 6:3"> To ones without mishma'at (obedience) back then when the savlanut (patience) of Hashem was waiting, in the days of Noach, while the Teva (Ark) was being prepared, in which a few, that is shemoneh nefashot (eight souls), were delivered through that mabbulʼs mikveh mayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MARKOS 16:16; YECHEZKEL 36:25–26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:38"> And Moshiachʼs tevilah in the mikveh mayim, which this mabbul prefigures, now delivers you, not by means of the removal of filth from the basar, but through the hitchaiy'vut (pledge) of a clean matzpun toward Hashem, through the Techiyas HaMoshiach Yehoshua,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:24; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:12"> Who is at the right hand of Hashem, having gone into Shomayim, with malachim and rashuyot (authorities) and gevurot under his feet.</span>
