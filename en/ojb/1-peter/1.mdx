<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:5–11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:13"> Shim'on Kefa a Shliach of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua to HaBechirim (the Chosen ones),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1K 2:4,6,9">a</a></sup>to the Exiled ones of the Golus, Sojourners living as aliens in the Diaspora, scattered in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:4–5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:24"> To HaBechirim (the Chosen ones) according to the yedi'ah mukdemet (foreknowledge)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1:20">b</a></sup>of Elohim Avinu, the ones set apart as Kedoshim by the Ruach Hakodesh, resulting in mishma'at (obedience)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1:14,22; 3:6; 4:17">c</a></sup>to the Moshiach and resulting in hazzayat HaDahm<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 52:15; Ex 24:7">d</a></sup>(sprinkling of the Blood) of Moshiach Yehoshua. May Chen v'Chesed Hashem and Shalom Hashem abound to all of you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:23; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:13"> Baruch Hashem, HaElohim Avi of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua! By his great rachamim (mercy) we have been born anew to a tikvah chayyah (living hope) through the Techiyas Yehoshua from HaMesim (the dead ones).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:27; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:15; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:4"> We have also been born anew to a nachalah (inheritance)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3:9">e</a></sup>that is without shachat<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ps 16:10">f</a></sup>(corruption, decay), undefiled and unfading, having been preserved in Shomayim for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:13–14"> The ones by the koach of Hashem being guarded through Emunah (Faith) for Yeshu'at Eloheinu (the Salvation of our G-d), which is ready to be revealed at the Ketz Hayamim (End of Days).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:2; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:12"> In this you greatly rejoice, though now for a little while you may have to suffer in various masot (trials, temptations),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="4:12">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="IYOV 23:10; YESHAYAH 48:10; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:12"> That the genuineness of your Emunah, much more valuable than gold which though perishable is tested by Eish (Fire), may be found to the tehillah (praise) and the kavod (glory) and the tiferet (splendor) at the Hisgalus (Revelation, Apocalypse) of Moshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:29; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:13"> He is the one for whom you have ahavah, even though you have not seen him. Though you do not now see him, you have emunah in him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="as Moshiach">h</a></sup>and rejoice with simcha inexpressible and full of kavod,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:22; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:13"> Obtaining the maskana (outcome) of your Emunah, the Yeshu'at Eloheynu of your nefashot (souls).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:17; LUKAS 10:24; LUKAS 24:25–27"> The Nevi'im, who prophesied of the Chen v'Chesed Hashem that was to come to you, searched and inquired about this Yeshu'at Eloheynu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 52:13–14; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:21; LUKAS 24:25–27"> The Nevi'im were searching for what zman (time) or what context of occasion the Ruach of Moshiach in them was pointing to, when predicting the Chevlei Moshiach and HaKavod to follow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:26; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:10"> The hisgalus (revelation) came to the Nevi'im that the avodas hakodesh ministry they were rendering was not to themselves, but to you, when they spoke of the things announced to you through the Mevaserim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="On the word Mevaserim see Isa 40:9.">i</a></sup>(Messianic Darshanim, Maggidim) of the Besuras HaGeulah by the Ruach Hakodesh sent from Shomayim, the very things even malachim desire to look into.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:8; LUKAS 12:35; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:7"> Therefore, tighten the “readiness” gartel of your mind, a mind that is one of kibush hayetzer (self-control). Set your tikvah completely on the Chen v'Chesed Hashem that is being brought to you, at the Hisgalus (Revelation, Apocalypse) of Moshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:2; LETTER TO TITOS 3:3–5; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:2–3"> As Bnei Mishma'at<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="1K 1:2">j</a></sup>(Children of Obedience), not conforming yourselves to your former ta'avot (lusts), when you lacked da'as (personal, saving knowledge),</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:48; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:1; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:14"> But, als (since) the One who bestows on you the kri'ah (calling) is KADOSH HU,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ps 99:5">k</a></sup>so also yourselves become Kedoshim in hitnahagut (conduct),</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="VAYIKRA 11:44; VAYIKRA 19:2; VAYIKRA 20:7"> Because it says in the Torah, KEDOSHIM TIH'YU KI KADOSH ANI<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Lv 19:2">l</a></sup>(You must be holy ones for I am holy).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:34–35; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:7; MATTITYAHU 6:9"> And if you call upon as “Avinu”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Mt 6:9">m</a></sup>the One who is the impartial Shofet (Shofet kol ha'aretz) of each man L'ISH K'MA'ASEIHU<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Ps 62:12[13]">n</a></sup>(To each according to his deeds), then conduct yourself with yirat Shomayim during your time in the Golus of the Olam Hazeh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:20; LETTER TO TITOS 2:14; TEHILLIM 49:7–8"> Having da'as that the padut nafsheynu (redemption ransom of our souls) was not with perishable things such as silver or gold when your Geulah (Redemption) was purchased from the Derech HaHevel (empty way of life) handed down to you from your Avot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:22–24; YOCHANAN 1:29"> No, it was the precious DAHM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Lv 17:11">o</a></sup>as of a SEH TAMIM U'MUM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Ex 12:5; Lv 22:20; Isa 53:7,9">p</a></sup>(lamb unblemished and unspotted), the DAHM of Moshiach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Mt 26:28">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:4; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:9–10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:26"> Who, on the one hand, having been foreknown lifnei hivvased tevel (before the foundation of the world), but, on the other hand, having been manifested<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="5:4">r</a></sup>at the Ketz Hayamim (End of Days) because of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:24; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:9; YOCHANAN 12:44"> Through him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Moshiach">s</a></sup>you have emunah in Hashem, the One who brought forth the Techiyas HaMoshiach from HaMesim (the dead ones) and gave him Kavod. And so your emunah and your tikvah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="1:13">t</a></sup>are in Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:9–10"> Having purified your nefashot (souls) by mishma'at<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="see 1:2">u</a></sup>(obedience) to HaEmes, resulting in ahavah shel achvah (brotherly love) without tzevi'ut (hypocrisy), have fervent ahavah (agape) for one another, from a lev tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:12; YOCHANAN 1:13"> You have been given a new birth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Yn 3:3">v</a></sup>not from perishable zerah (seed), but zerah that is without shachat<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Ps 16:1">w</a></sup>(corruption), through the Dvar Hashem who is Chai v'Nitzav l'Olam (living and remaining firm forever).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:6–8; TEHILLIM 103:15; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:14"> For, All flesh is grass and all of &#91;mankindʼs&#93; glory is like the flower: the grass withers, the flower falls,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:8; MATTITYAHU 5:18; TEHILLIM 119:89"> But the Word of our G-d stands forever.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Isa 40:6-8">x</a></sup>And this is the Dvar proclaimed to you as the Besuras HaGeulah &#91;in Moshiach&#93;.</span>
