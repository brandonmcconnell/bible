<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:4; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18; YOCHANAN 8:32"> From the Zaken<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ex 3:16">a</a></sup>(Elder). To the Gevirah HaBechirah (the Chosen Lady) and her Banim (Sons, Children), for whom I have ahavah (agape) b'Emes, and not only I but also all those who have Da'as HaEmes (Knowledge of the Truth),</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:14; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:17"> Because of HaEmes that dwells inside us, and will be immanu (with us) for Yamim HaOlam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:7; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:14"> Chen v'Chesed Hashem (unmerited Favor and Kindness), Rachamim Hashem (Mercy), and Shalom Hashem (Peace) will be immanu (with us) from Elohim HaAv and from Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, HaBen of HaAv, in Emes and Ahavah (agape).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:3–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:2"> I had simcha gedola (much joy) that I have found some of your Banim with a walk which is Halakhah b'Derech Emes (Conduct in the Way of Truth), just as we received a mitzvah (commandment) to do so from HaAv.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1:3">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:11; YOCHANAN 13:34–35; YOCHANAN 15:12"> And now I beseech you, Gevirah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1:1">c</a></sup>not as a mitzvah chadasha (new commandment) I am writing you, but that which we had meyReshit (from the Beginning): that we should have ahavah (agape) one for the other,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Yn 13:34">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:3; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:24; YOCHANAN 15:10"> And this is ahavah, that we should walk with a halichah according to the Mitzvot of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach; this is the mitzvah just as you have heard it meyReshit. You must walk in it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1:5">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:26; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1–3; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:18–22"> Because mat'im rabbim (many misleaders, deceivers) have gone out into the world, the ones not making the Ani Ma'amin hoda'ah (confession) that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua habah b'basar (has come in bodily flesh); this one is HaMat'eh (the Deceiving One) and the Anti-Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:35; HISGALUS 3:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:14"> Watch out for yourselves, lest you lose what we worked for, but may receive a sachar maleh (full reward).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:16–17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:16; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:22–24"> Everyone who runs ahead and does not remain in the torah of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach does not have Hashem; the one remaining in the torah &#91;of Moshiach&#93;, this one has both HaAv and HaBen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:17–18; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:6"> If anyone comes to you and does not bring this torah, do not receive him into your house,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="kehillah, shtiebel">f</a></sup>and do not give him Drishat Shalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:11; HISGALUS 18:4"> For the one giving Drishat Shalom to him has shuttafut (partnership) with his ma'asim hara'im (evil deeds).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:13–14; YOCHANAN 16:12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:5–7"> I have many things to write to you; I wanted not to do so with parchment and ink, but I have the tikvah (hope) to be with you and to speak panim el panim (directly, in person),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Dt 34:10">g</a></sup>that our simcha (joy) may be shleimah (complete).</span>
</div>

</div>
