<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:1–7; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13–17; KOHELET 10:4"> Remind them to be V'NISHMA (obedient, and we will obey!)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ex 24:7">a</a></sup>to sarim (rulers), to shiltonim (authorities), to be submissive, to be obedient, ready for every ma'aseh tov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:31; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:24–25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:2"> Speak lashon hora of no one, be not ohev riv (quarrelsome). Be eidel (gentle), displaying anavah (meekness) to kol Bnei Adam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:1–3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:21"> For all of us were once without da'as, sorrarim (disobedient), being led astray, avadim (slaves) of ta'avot and to various ta'anugot (pleasures) of the Olam Hazeh, spending our lives in eyvah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gn 3:15">b</a></sup>(enmity) and kina (jealousy), hated, and hating one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:4–10; LETTER TO TITOS 2:10–11; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:10"> But when the Chen v'Chesed and the ahavas Hashem Moshieinu appeared to Bnei Adam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:8–9; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:11"> Not by tzidkateynu (our righteousness) in ma'asim tovim which is to our zchus (merit), but according to His rachamim, He granted us Yeshu'at Eloheynu through the mikveh mayim ruchani of rebirth and hitchadshut of the Ruach Hakodesh,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Yn 3:3">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOEL 2:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:45"> Which Hashem poured out on us richly through Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua Moshieinu,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:17; LETTER TO TITOS 2:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:11"> That, having been made YITZDAK IM HASHEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Job 25:4">d</a></sup>by the Chen v'Chesed of Hashem, we might become yoreshim (heirs) in the tikvah HaChayyei Olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:14; LETTER TO TITOS 1:9; TEHILLIM 16:2–3"> This dvar Emes is trustworthy. And I counsel you to strongly affirm the divrei torah I am handing on to you, that those who have become ma'aminim in Hashem may be shomer for ma'asim tovim. These things to Bnei Adam are good, tov me'od.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:14; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:23; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:7"> But pilpul minutiae controversies and toldot and quarrels and fights about the Torah, avoid, for they are unprofitable hevel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:17; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:10; MATTITYAHU 18:15–17"> An ish hacholek (a man of division, a divisive man) after one or two warnings, avoid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:26; LUKAS 19:22; MATTITYAHU 25:26–28"> Having da'as that such a man is perverted and sinful, bringing harsha'ah (condemnation) upon himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:9; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:8–9"> When I send Artemas or Tychicus to you, try to come to me at Nicopolis, for there I have decided to spend the choref (winter).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:24; MATTITYAHU 22:35; (III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:6–8"> Do your utmost to speed Zenas, the Ben Torah (scholar), and Apollos on their way, that nothing for them may be lacking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:10; LETTER TO TITOS 3:8; MATTITYAHU 7:19"> Let anshei adateynu (the men of our community) learn to be concerned about ma'asim tovim so as to supply urgent needs, that they may not be lo poreh (unfruitful).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:23–24; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:1–2"> Drishat Shalom from all the ones with me. Drishat Shalom to those who have ahavah for us in the emunah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">e</a></sup>Chen v'Chesed Hashem to all of you.</span>
