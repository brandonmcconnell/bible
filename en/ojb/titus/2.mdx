<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:13; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:3; LETTER TO TITOS 2:11–14"> But you, speak the things which are suitable to Moshiachʼs orthodox Jewish doctrine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:23; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:8"> Zekenim need to be temperate, respectable, men of seichel, being orthodox<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">a</a></sup>in the emunah, in ahavah, in savlanut (longsuffering).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 2:4; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:11; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:3–5"> Similarly the senior women, the Zekanot, in hitnahagut (conduct) as befits kedushah, not yentas of lashon hora nor slaves of wine, but melamedot es haTov (teachers of the Good),</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:14; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:2; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:11"> That they may encourage the young nashim to have ahavah for their ba'alim and their banim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MISHLE 31:10–31; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:18; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:10"> To have seichel, tehorot in tznius,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Aishes Chayil">b</a></sup>managers of the Bayit, being tovot, being submissive to their ba'alim, lest Chillul Hashem come to the Dvar HaElohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="KOHELET 12:1; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:5; YOEL 2:28"> The bochrim, similarly, you exhort to have seichel</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:3; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:2"> About all things, showing yourself a mofet of ma'asim tovim. In the hora'ah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">c</a></sup>show integrity, seriousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:12; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16"> Along with dibur (speech) that is orthodox<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">d</a></sup>and beyond reproach, that the mitnagged may be brought to bushah (shame), having no lashon hora to say against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:18–25; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:1–2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:22–25"> Avadim (slaves) need to be submissive to their own adonim in everything, to be acceptable, not talking back,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:16; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:12; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9"> Not pilfering, but showing all good reliability, that they may adorn the hora'ah (teaching) of Hashem Moshieinu in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:8; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:4; YESHAYAH 45:22"> For the Chen v'Chesed of Hashem has appeared, bringing Yeshu'at Eloheynu to kol Bnei Adam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 36:27; MATTITYAHU 16:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:19"> Instructing us to deny all that is frai (irreligious) and not of chasidus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">e</a></sup>and all that is ta'avanut, and to live with seichel and tzidkat Hashem and yirat Shomayim in the Olam Hazeh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:7; MATTITYAHU 16:27"> Awaiting the tikvah hameashsheret (the blessed hope), the appearing of the kavod HaEloheinu HaGadol and Moshieinu Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:10; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9"> Who gave his nefesh on behalf of us, that for us he might bring in the Geulah, redeeming us MI KOL AVONOTAV (from all Israelʼs sins),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ps 130:8; Isa 53:8">f</a></sup>making tahor AM LI SEGULAH (a people for My possession),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ex 19:5">g</a></sup>zealous for ma'asim tovim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:2; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:12–13; LETTER TO TITOS 1:13"> Preach these things, with words that give chizzuk (strengthening) and reprove with all authority. Let no one “write you off.”</span>
