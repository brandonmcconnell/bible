<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 23:19; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:25; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:9"> The mekor (basis) of this is a tikvah of Chayyei Olam which Hashem Who cannot speak sheker<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Num 24:19; 1Sm 15:29">c</a></sup>promised before the Yamim HaOlam (days of eternity),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:11; LETTER TO TITOS 3:4–6; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:1"> But revealed at the proper time Hashemʼs dvar<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Besuras HaGeulah">d</a></sup>in the hachrazah (proclamation, kerygma), with which I was entrusted, according to the mitzvat Hashem Moshieinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:2; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:3; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:1"> To Titos, beni ha'amitti baEmunah (my true son in the faith), the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="orthodox Jewish">e</a></sup>emunah shared in common. Chen v'Chesed Hashem and Shalom from Elohim HaAv and Moshiach Yehoshua Moshieinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:23; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:3; KOHELET 12:9"> For this reason I left you in Crete that you should set beseder (according to acceptable order) what remains and give messianic s'michah to Zekenim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ex 12:21">f</a></sup>city by city, as I directed you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:2–7; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:12; LETTER TO TITOS 1:6–8"> If anyone is without reproach, a ba'al isha echat (a one woman man/master),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="1Ti 3:2; 5:9">g</a></sup>his banim being ma'aminim in Moshiach, and not under accusation of debauchery and zenut or sorrut (rebelliousness, insubordination),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="1Sm 2:22-25">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:18; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:10; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10"> For it is necessary for the congregational Mashgiach Ruchani to be without reproach as Hashemʼs mefake'ach al Beis Hashem(steward or supervisor of the House of G-d), not a ba'al gaavah (a haughty person), not quick in ka'as (anger), not a shikkor (drunkard) given to much wine, not violent, not a gelt-loving kamtzan (miser).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:2; LETTER TO TITOS 2:7; MELACHIM ALEF 5:7"> Rather, the Mashgiach Ruchani must be one who practices hachnosas orchim (hospitality), an ohev es haTov (a lover of the good), having seichel, a tzaddik, kadosh (holy), with shlitah atzmi (self-control),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:15; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:13; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:3"> Devoted to the faithful hatafah (preaching) of the Besuras HaGeulah, adequate for hora'ah (teaching) that exhorts to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Moshiachʼs">i</a></sup>orthodox Jewish doctrine and for refuting and exposing with conviction the ones speaking against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:6; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:4"> For there are indeed many mitnaggedim (opponents), idle talkers and deceivers, especially the ones of the party of the Mohalim HaGoyim (false teacher Circumcisers of Gentiles),</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:6; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:19"> Whose mouths it is necessary to stop, who are subverting entire mishpochot (families) by teaching what they ought not, for the sake of dishonest gelt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:18; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:28"> A certain one of them, a “navi” of their own number, said, “Cretans are always meshakkrim (liars), wicked beasts, lazy trombeniks (gluttons).”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 2:2; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:20; LETTER TO TITOS 2:15"> This eidus (testimony) is true, for which cause rebuke them sharply, that they may be orthodox Jewish in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Moshiachʼs">j</a></sup>emunah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:9"> Not paying attention to Jewish aggadot and to mitzvot d'Rabbanan (precepts imposed by the rabbis),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="i.e., which contradict the Kitvei Hakodesh — 1C 4:6; Dt 4:2">k</a></sup>becoming meshummad (apostate) from HaEmes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:23"> All things are tahor to hatehorim; but, to the ones having been defiled and taking the side of the Apikoros, nothing is tahor, but has been made tameh, both lev and matzpun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:4; YESHAYAH 29:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:6"> Hashem they profess to know, but by their ma'asim they deny him, being disqualified<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="for the rabbanut or ministry of Moshiach">l</a></sup>and being without mishma'at (obedience), and, as to every one of the ma'asim tovim, unpalatably unkosher (unfit).</span>
