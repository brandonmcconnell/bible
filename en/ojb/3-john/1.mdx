<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:4; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:2; TEHILLIM 119:11"> For I had simcha gedola (much joy) when the Achim b'Moshiach arrived &#91;here&#93; and gave eidus (testimony) of you being in HaEmes (the Truth), just as your walk is halakhah b'Derech Emes (conduct in the Way of Truth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:19; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:2; MISHLE 23:24"> I have no greater simcha than this, to hear of my bnei b'Moshiach, that their walk is halakhah b'Derech Emes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:10; LUKAS 12:42; LUKAS 16:10–12"> Chaver, you do a thing of emunah (faith) when it is done for the Achim b'Moshiach,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="engaged in the Shlichus (Divine Mission) of Messianic Kiruv Rechokim itineration in the kehillot">b</a></sup>especially when they are people you don't know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:12; LETTER TO TITOS 3:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:3"> They gave eidushaft (witness of testimony) to your ahavah (agape) before the kehillah (assembly) &#91;here&#93;; you will do well to send them on their way in a manner worthy of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:5; HISGALUS 2:3; MELACHIM BAIS 5:15–16"> For, on behalf of Hashem they went forth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="in the Shlichus of Moshiachʼs Kiruv Rechokim">c</a></sup>taking nothing from the Goyim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:5–9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:10–11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:2–3"> Therefore, we ought to give hachnosas orchim (hospitality) to such, in order that we may be ozrim (helpers) with HaEmes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 1:7–16; LUKAS 22:24–27; MATTITYAHU 23:4–8"> I wrote something to the kehillah &#91;there&#93;; but Diotrephes, who has fallen in love with being “Rosh of the Kehillah,” does not receive us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YESHAYAH 66:5; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:2; LUKAS 6:22"> Oib azoi (consequently), if I come, I will remember his “ma'asim” (works) which he does, his bringing unjustified charges against us with lashon horah (evil speech). And not being satisfied with performing these “mitzvot”, he even refuses to receive the Achim b'Moshiach, and, those intending to do so, he hinders and expels out of the kehillah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 1:16–17; TEHILLIM 37:27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:1"> Chaver, do not imitate HaRah (Evil); rather, imitate HaTov (Good). The one doing HaTov is from Hashem. The one doing HaRah has not regarded Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:7; YOCHANAN 21:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:12"> To Demetrius, eidus (witness of testimony, commendation) has been given by all and, personally, by no less than HaEmes (the Truth); we also give eidus, and you have da'as that our eidus is emes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12"> I had many things to write to you, but I do not want to write to you with pen and ink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 43:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:1–16"> But I have the tikvah (hope) to see you imminently, and we will speak panim el panim (directly, in person).</span>
</div>
