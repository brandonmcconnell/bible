<div className="bp">

</div>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:19; YIRMEYAH 23:14–17; YECHEZKEL 13:7–16"> Then the Melech Yisroel gathered the nevi'im together, about 400 men, and said unto them, Shall I go against Ramot-Gil'ad to battle, or shall I refrain? And they said, Go up; for Hashem shall deliver it into the hand of HaMelech.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 3:11–13; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 18:6–7"> And Yehoshaphat said, Is there not here a navi of Hashem besides, that we might inquire of him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="AMOS 5:10; YOCHANAN 3:19–21; YOCHANAN 7:7"> And the Melech Yisroel said unto Yehoshaphat, There is yet one man, Mikhay'hu ben Yimlach, by whom we may inquire of Hashem: but I hate him; for he doth not prophesy tov concerning me, but rah. And Yehoshaphat said, Let not HaMelech say such.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 9:32; YESHAYAH 39:7; MELACHIM ALEF 22:26–27"> Then HaMelech Yisroel called an officer, and said, Hasten here Mikhay'hu ben Yimlach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ESTHER 5:1; ESTHER 6:8–9; MELACHIM ALEF 18:29"> And the Melech Yisroel and Yehoshaphat Melech Yehudah sat each on his kisse, having put on their robes, at the threshing floor in the entrance of sha'ar Shomron; all the nevi'im prophesied before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 1:18–21; DEVARIM 33:17; YECHEZKEL 22:27–28"> Tzidkiyah ben Kena'anah made him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="goring">a</a></sup>horns of barzel: and said, Thus saith Hashem, With these shalt thou push Aram until thou have consumed them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:22; MELACHIM ALEF 22:6–15; MELACHIM ALEF 22:32–36"> And all the nevi'im prophesied so, saying, Go up to Ramot-Gil'ad, and succeed: for Hashem shall deliver it into the yad HaMelech.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MICHOH 2:6–7; TEHILLIM 11:1; YESHAYAH 30:10–11"> And the malach that was gone to summon Mikhay'hu spoke unto him, saying, Hinei now, the words of the nevi'im declare tov unto HaMelech with one mouth: let thy word be like the word of one of them, speak that which is tov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 24:13; BAMIDBAR 22:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 18:12–13"> And Mikhay'hu said, As Hashem liveth, what Hashem saith unto me, that will I speak.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:27; SHOFETIM 10:14; MATTITYAHU 26:45"> So he came to HaMelech. <span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:13; MATTITYAHU 26:63; YEHOSHUA 6:26"> And HaMelech said unto him, How many times shall I adjure thee<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., make thee swear">b</a></sup>that thou tell me nothing but that which is true in the Shem of Hashem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 27:17; MATTITYAHU 9:36; MELACHIM ALEF 22:34–36"> And he said, I saw kol Yisroel scattered upon the hills, as Tzon that have not a Ro'eh: and Hashem said, These have no adonim: let them return every man to his bais in shalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:8; MISHLE 10:24; LUKAS 11:45"> And the Melech Yisroel said unto Yehoshaphat, Did I not tell thee that he would prophesy no tov concerning me, but rah?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="IYOV 2:1; IYOV 1:6; DANIEL 7:9–10"> And he said, Shema Devar Hashem: I saw Hashem sitting on his kisse, and all the Tzva HaShomayim standing by Him on His right hand and on His left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 14:9; IYOV 12:16; YIRMEYAH 4:10"> And Hashem said, Who shall persuade Ach'av, that he may go up and fall at Ramot-Gil'ad? And one said one thing, and one said another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="IYOV 2:1; MELACHIM ALEF 22:23; IYOV 1:6–7"> And there came forth the ruach, and stood before Hashem, and said, I will persuade him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 14:9; MATTITYAHU 24:24–25; YESHAYAH 44:20"> Now therefore, hinei, Hashem hath put a ruach sheker in the mouth of all these thy nevi'im, and Hashem hath spoken ra'ah concerning thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:2; EKHAH 3:30; MICHOH 5:1"> But Tzidkiyahu ben Kena'anah went near, and struck Mikhay'hu on the cheek, and said, Which way did the Ruach Hashem go when he went from me to speak to you?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 16:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 18:25–27; HISGALUS 2:10"> And say, Thus saith HaMelech, Put this fellow in the bais hakeleh, and feed him with lechem lachatz (bread of affliction) and with mayim lachatz (water of affliction), until I come in shalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 16:29; MICHOH 1:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:10–11"> And Mikhay'hu said, If thou return at all in shalom, Hashem hath not spoken by me. And he said, Pay heed, O people, every one of you.</span>

And the Melech Yisroel disguised himself, and went into the milchamah.</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:23; MICHOH 6:13; SHMUEL ALEF 17:49"> And a certain man drew a keshet (bow) at random, and struck Melech Yisroel between the joints of his armor; wherefore he said unto the driver of his merkavah, Turn around thine hand, and carry me out of the battle; for I am wounded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:28; MELACHIM ALEF 20:42"> And the milchamah increased that yom; and HaMelech was propped up in his merkavah against the Syrians, and died at erev; and the dahm ran out of the wound onto the floor of the merkavah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 14:12; MELACHIM ALEF 22:31; SHOFETIM 21:24"> And there went a proclamation throughout the host about the going down of the shemesh, saying, Every man to his city, and every man to his own country.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> So Melech<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ahab">c</a></sup>died, and was brought to Shomron; and they buried HaMelech in Shomron.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 21:19; MATTITYAHU 24:35; YIRMEYAH 44:21–23"> And one washed the merkavah in the pool of Shomron; and the kelavim licked up his dahm while the zonot bathed; according unto the Devar Hashem which he spoke.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="AMOS 3:15; YECHEZKEL 27:6; TEHILLIM 45:8"> Now the rest of the acts of Ach'av (Ahab), and all that he did, and the Bais HaShen (House of Ivory) which he made, and all the cities that he built, are they not written in the Sefer Divrei HaYamim l'Malkhei Yisroel?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 7:12; MELACHIM BAIS 1:17; MELACHIM BAIS 1:2"> So Ach'av slept with his avot; and Achazyahu beno (his son) reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:31; MELACHIM ALEF 22:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 17:1"> And Yehoshaphat ben Asa began to reign over Yehudah in the fourth year of Ach'av Melech Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 1:17; MELACHIM ALEF 15:2; MELACHIM ALEF 15:10"> Yehoshaphat was 35 shanah when he began to reign; and he reigned 25 shanah in Yerushalayim. And the shem immo was Azuvah bat Shilchi.</span>

<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 14:29; MELACHIM ALEF 11:41; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:34"> And Yehoshaphat made peace with Melech Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 15:12; MELACHIM ALEF 14:24; DEVARIM 23:17"> Now the rest of the acts of Yehoshaphat, and his gevurah that he showed, and how he warred, are they not written in the Sefer Divrei HaYamim l'Malkhei Yehudah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:14; MELACHIM BAIS 3:9; MELACHIM BAIS 8:20"> And the remnant of the kadesh (male and female cult prostitutes), which remained in the days of Asa aviv, he took out of HaAretz.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:26; MELACHIM ALEF 10:22; MELACHIM ALEF 9:28"> There was then no melech in Edom; a deputy was melech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> Yehoshaphat made &#91;merchant&#93; oniyot (ships) of Tarshish to go to Ophir for zahav; but they went not; for the oniyot were shipwrecked at Etzyon-Gever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 21:1; MELACHIM ALEF 22:40; MELACHIM ALEF 15:24"> Then said Achazyahu ben Ach'av unto Yehoshaphat, Let my avadim go with thy avadim in the oniyot. But Yehoshaphat would not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 1:17; MELACHIM ALEF 22:40; MELACHIM ALEF 15:25"> And Yehoshaphat slept with his avot, and was buried with his avot in the Ir Dovid aviv; and Yehoram bno reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 15:26; MELACHIM ALEF 21:25; HISGALUS 3:20"> Achazyahu ben Ach'av began to reign over Yisroel in Shomron the seventeenth year of Yehoshaphat Melech Yehudah, and reigned two years over Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 16:30–32; YESHAYAH 65:3; MELACHIM BAIS 3:2"> And he did rah in the sight of Hashem, walked in the derech aviv, and in the derech immo, and in the derech Yarov'am ben Nevat, who made Yisroel to sin;</span>
