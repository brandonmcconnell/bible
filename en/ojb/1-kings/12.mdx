<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHOFETIM 9:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 10:1–19; SHOFETIM 9:1"> And Rechav'am went to Shechem; for kol Yisroel were come to Shechem to make him Melech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:40; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 10:2–3; MELACHIM ALEF 11:26–31"> And it came to pass, when Yarov'am Ben Nevat, who was yet in Mitzrayim, heard of it, (for he had fled from the presence of HaMelech Sh'lomo, and Yarov'am dwelt in Mitzrayim);</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> That they sent and called him. And Yarov'am and kol Kehal Yisroel came, and spoke unto Rechav'am, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 8:11–18; MELACHIM ALEF 4:7; MATTITYAHU 11:29–30"> Avicha made our ol (yoke) difficult; now therefore make thou the grievous avodat Avicha, and his heavy ol (yoke) which he put upon us, lighter, and we will serve thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:12"> And he said unto them, Depart yet for shloshah yamim, then come back to me. And HaAm departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="IYOV 12:12; IYOV 32:7; SHMUEL BAIS 16:20"> And HaMelech Rechav'am consulted with the Zekenim, that stood before Sh'lomo Aviv while he yet lived, and said, How do ye advise that I may return answer to HaAm Hazeh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MISHLE 15:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 10:6–7; MELACHIM ALEF 12:13"> And they spoke unto him, saying, If thou wilt be an eved unto this people today, and wilt serve them, and answer them, and speak devarim tovim to them, then they will be thy avadim kol hayamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MISHLE 19:20; KOHELET 10:2–3; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 10:8"> But he forsook the atzat HaZekenim (counsel of the Elders), which they had given him, and consulted with the yeladim (young people) that were grown up with him, and which stood before him (i.e., were in his court);</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:6–8; SHMUEL BAIS 17:5–6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 10:9"> And he said unto them, What counsel give ye that we may return answer to HaAm Hazeh, who have spoken to me, saying, Make the ol (yoke) which Avicha did put upon us lighter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 10:10–11; MISHLE 10:14; YESHAYAH 47:6"> And the yeladim that were grown up with him spoke unto him, saying, Thus shalt thou speak unto HaAm hazeh that spoke unto thee, saying, Avicha made our ol (yoke) heavy, but make thou it lighter unto us; thus shalt thou say unto them, My little finger shall be thicker than the loins of Avi!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 27:11; YECHEZKEL 2:6; YIRMEYAH 28:13–14"> And now whereas Avi did lay on you an ol kaved (heavy yoke), I will add to your ol (yoke); Avi hath scourged you with shotim (whips), but I will scourge you with akrabim (scorpions).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 10:12–14"> So Yarov'am and kol HaAm came to Rechav'am the Yom HaShelishi, just as HaMelech had appointed, saying, Come to me again the Yom HaShelishi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17; MISHLE 13:20; KOHELET 10:12"> And HaMelech answered HaAm roughly, and forsook the atzat HaZekenim (counsel of the Elders) that they gave him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 5:16–18; MELACHIM ALEF 12:10–11; SHEMOT 5:5–9"> And spoke to them according to atzat HaYeladim, saying, Avi made your ol (yoke) heavy, and I will add to your ol (yoke); Avi also scourged you with shotim (whips), but I will scourge you with akrabim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:24; DEVARIM 2:30; SHOFETIM 14:4"> So HaMelech paid heed not unto HaAm; for the sibbah (turn of events) was from Hashem, in order that He might perform His davar, which Hashem spoke by Achiyah the Shiloni unto Yarov'am Ben Nevat.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See 1Kgs 11:29-39">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 20:1; YIRMEYAH 23:5–6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 10:16"> So when kol Yisroel saw that HaMelech paid heed not unto them, HaAm answered HaMelech, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">What chelek have we in Dovid?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Neither have we nachalah in Ben Yishai;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to your ohalim, O Yisroel!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Now see to thine own Bais, Dovid!</span><br />So Yisroel departed unto their ohalim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:36; MELACHIM ALEF 11:13; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:13–17"> But as for the Bnei Yisroel which dwelt in the towns of Yehudah, Rechav'am reigned over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 4:6; SHMUEL BAIS 20:24; MELACHIM ALEF 5:14"> Then HaMelech Rechav'am sent Adoram, who was over the mas (forced labor); and kol Yisroel stoned him, that he died. Therefore HaMelech Rechav'am made speed to get himself up to his merkavah, to flee to Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 17:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 10:19; YEHOSHUA 4:9"> So Yisroel has been in rebellion against the Bais Dovid unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:13; MELACHIM ALEF 11:32; HOSHEA 8:4"> And it came to pass, when kol Yisroel heard that Yarov'am was come again, that they sent and called him unto the Edah, and made him melech over kol Yisroel; there was none that followed the Bais Dovid, but the Shevet Yehudah only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:1–3; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:8"> And when Rechav'am was come to Yerushalayim he assembled kol Bais Yehudah, with the Shevet Binyamin, a hundred and fourscore thousand bachur oseh milchamah (chosen fighting men), to fight against Bais Yisroel, to regain the Kingdom for Rechav'am Ben Sh'lomo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:5–7; MELACHIM ALEF 13:1"> But the Devar HaElohim came unto Shema'yah the Ish HaElohim, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:17"> Speak unto Rechav'am Ben Sh'lomo Melech Yehudah, and unto kol Bais Yehudah and Binyamin, and to the remnant of the people, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:15; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:7–8; HOSHEA 8:4"> Thus saith Hashem, Ye shall not go up, nor fight against your brethren the Bnei Yisroel; Shuvu (go back! return!) every ish to his bais; for this thing is from Me. They paid heed therefore to the Devar Hashem, and turned around to depart, according to the Devar Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHOFETIM 8:17; SHOFETIM 8:8; SHOFETIM 9:45–49"> Then Yarov'am built Shechem in har Ephrayim, and dwelt therein; and went out from there, and built Penuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 38:18–21; YESHAYAH 7:9; MARKOS 2:6–8"> And Yarov'am said in his lev, Now shall the mamlachah return to the Bais Dovid:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:5–7; MISHLE 29:25; DEVARIM 12:14"> If HaAm Hazeh go up to offer zevakhim (sacrifices) at the Beis Hashem at Yerushalayim, then shall lev HaAm Hazeh turn back unto adoneihem, even unto Rechav'am Melech Yehudah, and they shall kill me, and return to Rechav'am Melech Yehudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 10:29; SHEMOT 32:8; MELACHIM BAIS 17:16"> Whereupon HaMelech took counsel, and made two eglei zahav (calves of gold), and said unto them, It is too much for you to go up to Yerushalayim; hinei, eloheicha, O Yisroel, which brought thee up out of Eretz Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:19; SHOFETIM 18:27–31; HOSHEA 4:15"> He set the one in Beit-El, the other put he in Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 17:21; MELACHIM ALEF 13:34; MELACHIM BAIS 10:31"> And this thing became chattat (sin); for the people went to worship before the one, even as far as Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 13:9; MELACHIM BAIS 17:32; MELACHIM ALEF 13:32–33"> And he made a bais for the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="idolatrous">b</a></sup>high places, and made kohanim of the lowest of the people, which were not of the Bnei Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:5; MELACHIM ALEF 8:2; AMOS 7:10–13"> And Yarov'am ordained a chag in the eighth month, on the fifteenth day of the month, like unto the chag that is in Yehudah, and he offered upon the mizbe'ach. So did he in Beit-El, sacrificing unto the agalim (calves) that he had made; and he installed in Beit-El the kohanim of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="idolatrous">c</a></sup>high places which he had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 13:1; BAMIDBAR 15:39; MATTITYAHU 15:6"> So he offered upon the mizbe'ach which he had made in Beit-El the fifteenth day of the eighth month, even in the month which he had devised of his own lev; and ordained a chag unto the Bnei Yisroel; and he offered upon the mizbe'ach, and burned ketoret.</span>
