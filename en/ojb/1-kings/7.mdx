<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:1; KOHELET 2:4–5"> But Sh'lomo was building his own Bais (palace complex) thirteen years, and he finished all his Bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 10:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:16; MELACHIM ALEF 10:21"> He built also the Bais Ya'ar HaLevanon; the length thereof was a hundred cubits, and the width thereof fifty cubits, and the height thereof thirty cubits, upon four rows of cedar pillars, with cedar beams upon the pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 6:5"> And it was roofed in erez (cedar) above the tzela'ot (chambers, side-rooms) upon the ammudim; there were forty-five, fifteen per row.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 40:22; YECHEZKEL 40:16; MELACHIM ALEF 6:4"> And there were shekufim (windows) in three rows, and outlook was opposite outlook, shalosh pe'amim (three times).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And all the petachim and mezuzot were framed four-sided by beams; and outlook opposite outlook, shalosh pe'amim (three times).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:12; YECHEZKEL 41:25–26"> And he made Ulam HaAmmudim (Hall of Pillars); the length thereof was fifty cubits, and the width thereof thirty cubits: and the ulam (portico) was in front of them;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., the petachim">a</a></sup>and the other ammudim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="for the portico roof">b</a></sup>with the thick beam were before them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MISHLE 20:8; TEHILLIM 122:5; YESHAYAH 9:7"> Then he made Ulam HaKisse where he would judge, even the Ulam HaMishpat; and it was covered with erez (cedar) from floor to floor.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e., covering the entire floor">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:11; MELACHIM ALEF 3:1; MELACHIM ALEF 9:24"> And his Bais (palace) where he dwelt which was in another khatzer (courtyard) inwards from the Ulam,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="HaKisse">d</a></sup>was similar in workmanship. Sh'lomo made also a Bais for Bat Pharaoh, whom he had taken as isha, and the Bais (palace) he made was like this Ulam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:10–11; MELACHIM ALEF 5:17"> All these were of expensive stone, according to the measure of hewed stones, filed with files, outside and inside, even from the foundation unto the ceiling, and from without to the Khatzer HaGedolah (the Great Court).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HISGALUS 21:19–20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:10–11; YESHAYAH 54:11"> And the foundation was laid with expensive stones, even avanim gedolot, stones of 10 cubits, and stones of 8 cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:20–22; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5"> And above were expensive avanim, after the measure of hewed stones, and cedars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 6:36; MELACHIM ALEF 7:6; YOCHANAN 10:23"> And the Khatzer HaGedolah (Great Court) was formed of shloshah — three courses of hewn stones, and a course of hewn cedar beams, as in the Beis Hashemʼs Khatzer HaPenimit (Innermost Court) and for the Ulam HaBeis.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="HaMikdash">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:13–14; MELACHIM ALEF 7:40"> And HaMelech Sh'lomo sent and brought back Chiram from Tzor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:16; SHEMOT 31:2–6"> He was ben isha almanah of the tribe of Naphtali, and aviv was an ish of Tzor, a khoresh nechoshet and he was filled with chochmah and tevanah, and da'as to work all works in nechoshet. And he came to HaMelech Sh'lomo, and wrought all his work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 52:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 3:15–17; MELACHIM ALEF 7:21"> For he cast two ammudim of nechoshet, of 18 cubits high apiece; and a line of 12 cubits did compass either of them about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 38:19; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:12–13; SHEMOT 38:17"> And he made two capitals of mutzak nechoshet (cast bronze), to set upon the tops of the ammudim; the height of the one capital was 5 cubits, and the height of the other capital was 5 cubits:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:14; SHEMOT 28:24–25; SHEMOT 28:22"> And plaiting of net work, and ropes of chain work, for the capitals which were upon the top of the ammudim, seven for the one capital, and seven for the other capital.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:14; SHEMOT 39:15–18; SHEMOT 28:22"> And he made the ammudim, and two rows around the one network, to cover the capitals that were upon the rosh (top) with pomegranates; and the same did he for the second capital.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:22; MELACHIM ALEF 6:32–35; MELACHIM ALEF 6:18"> And the capitals that were upon the rosh of the ammudim were of shushan (lily) work in the Ulam, four cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 3:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:13; YIRMEYAH 52:22–23"> And the capitals upon the two ammudim had pomegranates also above, over against the bulge which was next to the network; and the pomegranates were two hundred in rows encircling the second capital.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 3:17; MELACHIM ALEF 6:3; MELACHIM ALEF 7:12"> And he set up the ammudim in the Ulam HaHeikhal; and he set up the right ammud, and called shmo thereof Yachin (Establish): and he set up the left ammud, and called shmo thereof Boaz (Strength is in it).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And upon the top of the ammudim was shoshan (lily) work; so was the work of the ammudim completed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:2; MELACHIM BAIS 25:13; YIRMEYAH 52:17"> And he made a Yam Mutzak (Cast Metal Sea), ten cubits from brim to brim; it was completely round and its height was 5 cubits; and a line of 30 cubits did measure around about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 6:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:3; SHEMOT 37:17–22"> And under the brim of it all around about there were knobs surrounding it, ten in a cubit, encompassing the Yam all around about; the knobs were cast in two rows, when it was cast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 52:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:4–5; MATTITYAHU 28:19"> It stood upon twelve bakar (oxen), shalosh looking toward tzafonah (north), and shalosh looking toward the yammah (west), and shalosh looking toward the negbah (south), and shalosh looking toward the mizrachah (east); and the Yam was set above upon them, and all their haunches were facing toward the center.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:5; MELACHIM ALEF 7:38; MELACHIM ALEF 6:18"> And it was a hand-breadth thick, and the brim thereof was wrought like the brim of a kos, with shoshan blossoms; it could hold two thousand bath measures.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:14; MELACHIM BAIS 25:13; YIRMEYAH 52:17"> And he made ten Mekhonot (Stands)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="serving as water carts">f</a></sup>of nechoshet; four cubits was the length of one Stand, and four cubits the width thereof, and three cubits the height of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the constuction of the Mekhonot was on this manner: they had misgerot (panels, flat sides), and the misgerot were between the shelabim (rods, upright braces);</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="HOSHEA 5:14; YECHEZKEL 10:14; SHEMOT 37:7"> And on the misgerot that were between the shelabim were &#91;engraved&#93; arayot (lions) and bakar (oxen), and keruvim; and upon the shelabim there was a base above; and below the arayot and bakar were wreaths of plaited work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 10:10–13; YECHEZKEL 3:13; YECHEZKEL 1:15–21"> And every Mekhonah (Stand) had four nechoshet ofanim (wheels), and axles of nechoshet; and the four corners thereof had supporting posts; under the Kiyor (Basin) were supports cast with wreaths on each side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the mouth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="of the Mekhonah (Stand)">g</a></sup>within the capital and above was a cubit; but the opening thereof was circular like the construction of the base, a cubit and an half; and also upon the rim of it were engravings. The frames were foursquare, not circular.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And under the frames were four ofanim (wheels); and the axles of the ofanim (wheels) were joined to the Mekhonah; and the diameter of each wheel was a cubit and half a cubit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 1:18; YECHEZKEL 1:16"> And the construction of the ofanim (wheels) was like the construction of a merkavah ofan (wheel): their axles, and their hubs, and their rims, and their spokes, were all cast metal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And there were four supports to the four pinnot (corners) of each stand; and the supports were of the stand itself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And at the top of the Mekhonah (Stand) was there a round rim of half a cubit high; and on the top of the Mekhonah (Stand) the rods thereof and the frames thereof were cast as one unit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:29; MELACHIM ALEF 6:29; YECHEZKEL 41:18–20"> For on the luchot of the rods thereof, and on the misgerot (panels) thereof, he engraved keruvim, arayot, and timorot (palms), according to the proportion of every one, and wreaths all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> After this manner he made the ten Mekhonot; all of them had one casting, one middah (size), and one shape.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="SHEMOT 40:11–12; ZECHARYAH 13:1; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7"> Then made he ten kiyyorot nechoshet (basins of bronze); one kiyor could hold forty baths; and every kiyor was four cubits; and upon each of the ten Mekhonot there was one kiyor (basin).</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:6"> And he put five Mekhonot (Stands) on the right side of the Beis,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="HaMikdash">h</a></sup>and five on the left side of the Beis; and he set the Yam on the right side of the Beis eastward opposite the south.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:11–16; SHEMOT 24:6; SHEMOT 39:32–43"> And Chiram made the kiyyrot, and the ya'im (shovels), and the mizrakot (bowls for sprinkling blood). So Chiram completed all the melachah (work) that he made for Sh'lomo HaMelech for the Beis Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:15–22; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:12"> The two ammudim, and the two bowls of the capitals that were on the top of the two ammudim; and the two networks, decorating the two bowls of the capitals which were upon the top of the ammudim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:20"> And four hundred rimonim (pomegranates) for the two networks, even two courses of rimonim (pomegranates) for one network, to decorate the two bowls of the capitals that were upon the ammudim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And the ten Mekhonot (stands), and ten Kiyyorot (basins) on the Mekhonot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:23; MELACHIM ALEF 7:25"> And one Yam, and twelve Bakar under the Yam;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="SHEMOT 27:3; SHEMOT 38:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:16"> And the sirot (pots), and the ya'im (shovels), and the mizrakot (bowls for sprinkling blood); and all these kelim (vessels), which Chiram made for Sh'lomo HaMelech for the Beis Hashem were of burnished nechoshet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 3:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:17; BERESHIS 33:17"> In the plain of the Yarden did HaMelech cast them, in the clay of the adamah (ground) between Sukkot and Tzartan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:18"> And Sh'lomo left all the kelim (vessels) unweighed, because they were exceeding many; neither was the weight of the nechoshet found out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="SHEMOT 37:10–16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:8; SHEMOT 39:36"> And Sh'lomo made all the kelim (vessels) that pertained unto the Beis Hashem: the Mizbe'ach HaZahav, and the Shulchan HaZahav, whereupon was the Lechem HaPanim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:20; ZECHARYAH 4:11–14"> And the Menorot of pure zahav, five on the right side, and five on the left, in front of the Devir (Most Holy Place), with floral designs, and the nerot (lamps), and the melkachayyim (tongs) of zahav,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:29; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:21–22; SHEMOT 27:3"> And the sippot (bowls), and the mezammerot (lamp snuffers), and the mizrakot (bowls for sprinkling blood), and the kappot (ladles), and the machtot zahav sagur (hot coal pans of pure gold), and the gold potot (hinge-sockets) for the daletot (doors) of the Beis HaPenimi, the Kodesh HaKodashim, and also those for the daletot of the Beis,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hamikdash">i</a></sup>that is, the Heikhal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 5:1; SHMUEL BAIS 8:7–11; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:11–18"> So was completed all the work that HaMelech Sh'lomo made for the Beis Hashem. And Sh'lomo brought in the things which Dovid Aviv had set apart as kodesh; even the kesef, and the zahav, and the kelim (vessels), did he put among the otzarot (treasures) of the Beis Hashem.</span>
</div>
