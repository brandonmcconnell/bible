<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:42; LUKAS 11:31; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:1–12"> And when the Malkah (Queen) of Sheva heard of the fame of Sh'lomo, that it was for the Shem Hashem, then she came to test him with chidot (hard questions, riddles).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:23; TEHILLIM 4:4; IYOV 4:2"> And she came to Yerushalayim with a very great caravan, with gemalim that bore spices, and very much zahav, and precious gems; and when she was come to Sh'lomo, she spoke intimately with him of all that was on her lev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MISHLE 1:5–6; MATTITYAHU 13:11; MELACHIM ALEF 10:1"> And Sh'lomo answered her regarding all her questions; there was not anything hid from HaMelech, which he told her not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:42; MELACHIM ALEF 3:28; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:3–4"> And when the Malkah of Sheva had seen kol chochmat Sh'lomo, and the Bais (Palace) that he had built,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:16; MELACHIM ALEF 4:22–23; MELACHIM BAIS 16:18"> And the ma'akhal (food) of his shulchan, and the moshav (seating) of his avadim (officials), and the ma'amad (station) of his attendants, and their robes, and his cupbearers, and his ascent by which he went up unto the Beis Hashem; there was no more ruach (breath) in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:5–6"> And she said to HaMelech, It was emes the report that I heard in mine own eretz of thy devarim and of thy chochmah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:9; YOCHANAN 20:25–29; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:2"> Howbeit I believed not the devarim, until I came, and mine eyes had seen it; and, hinei, the half was not told me; thy chochmah and tov (prosperity) exceedeth the fame which I heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MISHLE 8:34; MISHLE 10:21; LUKAS 11:31"> Ashrei are thy anashim; ashrei are these thy avadim, which stand continually before thee, and that hear thy chochmah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 72:2; MELACHIM ALEF 5:7; SHMUEL BAIS 8:15"> Yehi Hashem Eloheicha baruch Who delighted in thee, to set thee on the Kisse Yisroel; because Hashem loved Yisroel l'olam, therefore made He thee Melech, to do mishpat and tzedakah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 10:2; MATTITYAHU 2:11; MELACHIM ALEF 9:14"> And she gave HaMelech a hundred and twenty talents of zahav, and a very large store of spices and precious gems; there came no more such abundance of spices as these which the Malkah of Sheva gave to Melech Sh'lomo.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:27–28; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:18; TEHILLIM 45:9"> And the fleet of oniyyot also of Chiram, that brought zahav from Ophir, brought in from Ophir a vast supply of almug wood, and precious gems.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 25:1–31; TEHILLIM 150:3–5; HISGALUS 14:2–3"> And HaMelech made of the almug wood misad (steps, support) for the Beis Hashem, and for the Bais HaMelech, kinnorot (harps) also and nevalim (lyres) for the singers; there came no such almug wood, nor were seen unto this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TEHILLIM 37:4; MATTITYAHU 15:28; TEHILLIM 20:4"> And HaMelech Sh'lomo gave unto the Malkah of Sheva all her desire, whatsoever she asked, besides that which Sh'lomo gave her of his royal bounty. So she turned and went to her own country, she and her avadim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:13–28; MELACHIM ALEF 9:28"> Now the weight of zahav that came to Sh'lomo in one year was 666 talents of zahav,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 72:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 9:24; YESHAYAH 21:13"> This was in addition to what he had of the anashei hatarim (merchants), and of the income of traders, and of all the Melachim of Arabia, and of the Governors of the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 14:26–28; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:9–10; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:15–16"> And HaMelech Sh'lomo made two hundred large shields of hammered zahav; six hundred shekels of zahav went to one shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:2; MELACHIM ALEF 14:26"> And he made three hundred moginim of hammered zahav; three mina of zahav went to one mogen (shield); and HaMelech put them in the Bais Ya'ar HaLevanon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="TEHILLIM 45:8; MELACHIM ALEF 10:22; HISGALUS 20:11"> Moreover HaMelech made a kisse shen gadol (great throne of ivory), and overlaid it with the finest, brightest zahav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The kisse had shesh ma'alot (six steps), and the top of the kisse was rounded; and there were armrests on either side on the makom (place) of the seat, and two arayot (lions) stood beside the armrests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 24:9; BERESHIS 49:9; BAMIDBAR 23:24"> And twelve arayim (lions) stood there on the one side and on the other upon the six steps; there was not the like made in any mamlakhot (kingdoms).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 10:17; MELACHIM ALEF 7:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:20–22"> And all HaMelech Sh'lomoʼs kelei mashkeh (drinking vessels) were of zahav, and all the vessels of the Bais Ya'ar HaLevanon were of pure zahav; none were of kesef; it was nothing accounted of in the days of Sh'lomo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:48; BERESHIS 10:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:36–37"> For HaMelech had at sea a fleet of oniyyot Tarshish with the fleet of oniyyot of Chiram; once in shalosh shanim came the fleet of oniyyot Tarshish, bringing zahav, and kesef, ivory, and monkeys, and peacocks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 3:12–13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:2–3; MELACHIM ALEF 4:29–34"> So HaMelech Sh'lomo exceeded all the Melachim of Ha'Aretz in osher and in chochmah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 3:12; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5; MELACHIM ALEF 3:28"> And kol Ha'Aretz sought the face of Sh'lomo, to hear his chochmah, which Elohim had put in his lev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="IYOV 42:11; TEHILLIM 72:10; TEHILLIM 72:15"> They brought every ish his minchah, vessels of kesef, vessels of zahav, and robes, and weapons, and spices, susim, and peradim (mules), yearly at each yearʼs due.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:25; MELACHIM ALEF 4:26; DEVARIM 17:16"> And Sh'lomo gathered together merkavot and parashim; and he had a thousand and four hundred merkavot, and twelve thousand parashim, whom he kept in his chariot cities and with HaMelech in Yerushalayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 1:15–17; DEVARIM 17:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:27"> And HaMelech made kesef to be in Yerushalayim like avanim (rocks), and cedars made he to be like sycamores in the Shefelah, for abundance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEVARIM 17:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:28; YESHAYAH 31:1–3"> And Sh'lomo had susim brought out of Mitzrayim, and from Keveh; HaMelechʼs agents acquired from Kevah at a price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 1:4; MELACHIM BAIS 7:6–7; MALACHI 1:1"> And a merkavah (chariot) went out of Mitzrayim for six hundred pieces of kesef, and a sus for a hundred and fifty; and so for all the melachim of the Chittim (Hittites), and for the melachim of Aram, did they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="HaMelechʼs agents">a</a></sup>export them by the same means.</span>
</div>
