<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YESHAYAH 36:2–22; MELACHIM BAIS 19:9; YESHAYAH 37:9–10"> And he sent malachim to Ach'av Melech Yisroel into the Ir, and said unto him, Thus saith Ben-Hadad,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 15:9; YESHAYAH 10:13–14"> Thy kesef and thy zahav is mine; thy nashim also and thy banim, even the tovim (best) are mine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHOFETIM 15:11–13; DEVARIM 28:48; SHMUEL ALEF 13:6–7"> And the Melech Yisroel answered and said, Adoni, O melech, according to thy word, I am thine, and all that I have.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the malachim came again, and said, Thus speaketh Ben-Hadad, saying, Although I have sent unto thee, saying, Thou shalt deliver me thy kesef, thy zahav, thy nashim, thy banim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 25:34; YOEL 3:5; MELACHIM BAIS 18:31–32"> Yet I will send my avadim unto thee tomorrow about this time, and they shall search thine Bais (Palace), and the batim (houses) of thy avadim; and it shall be, that whatsoever is makhmad (precious) in thine eyes, they shall put it in their yad, and take it away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 5:7; MISHLE 6:14; MELACHIM ALEF 8:1"> Then the Melech Yisroel summoned kol Ziknei HaAretz, and said, Mark, now, and see how this man seeketh ra'ah; for he sent unto me for my nashim, for my banim, for my kesef, for my zahav; and I denied him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> All the zekenim and kol HaAm said unto him, Pay not heed unto him, nor consent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Wherefore he said unto the malachim of Ben-Hadad, Tell adoni HaMelech, All that thou didst send for to thy eved at the first I will do; but this thing I may not do. And the malachim departed, and took him back davar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 19:2; SHEMOT 11:8; MELACHIM BAIS 19:23–24"> And Ben-Hadad sent unto him, and said, The elohim (g-ds) do so unto me, and more also, if the aphar Shomron shall provide even a handful for kol haAm (each of the people) who are at my raglayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MISHLE 27:1; YESHAYAH 10:15–16; SHMUEL ALEF 17:44–47"> And the Melech Yisroel answered and said, Tell him, Let not khoger (one girding on)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="his battle armor">a</a></sup>boast himself as he that taketh off.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 16:9; MELACHIM ALEF 20:16; MISHLE 31:4–5"> And it came to pass, when Ben-Hadad heard this davar, as he was drinking, he and the melachim in the sukkot, that he said unto his avadim, Get ready to attack. And they set themselves in array against the Ir.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 20:28; YECHEZKEL 20:22; YESHAYAH 7:1–9"> And, hinei, there came one navi unto Ach'av Melech Yisroel, saying, Thus saith Hashem, Hast thou seen all this hamon hagadol (vast multitude)? Hineni, I will deliver it into thine yad this day; and thou shalt know that I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:50; BERESHIS 14:14–16; MELACHIM ALEF 18:44"> Ach'av said, By whom? And he said, Thus saith Hashem, Even by the na'arei sarei hamedinot (the servants of the provincial commanders). Then he said, Who shall lead out in the milchamah? He answered, Thou.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHOFETIM 7:16; SHMUEL ALEF 14:2; SHOFETIM 7:7"> Then he numbered the na'arei sarei hamedinot, and they were two hundred and thirty-two; and after them he numbered kol HaAm, even all the Bnei Yisroel, being shivat alafim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="KOHELET 10:16–17; HOSHEA 4:11; MELACHIM ALEF 16:9"> And they set forth at tzohorayim. But Ben-Hadad was drinking himself shikkor in the sukkot, he and the melachim, the thirty and two melachim ozer (allied with) him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 20:14–15; MELACHIM ALEF 20:19"> And the na'arei sarei hamedinot went out first; and Ben-Hadad sent out, and they reported to him, saying, There are anashim come out from Shomron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 14:8–12; SHMUEL ALEF 14:11–12; MISHLE 18:12"> And he said, Whether they be come out for shalom, take them chayyim (alive); or whether they be come out for milchamah, take them chayyim (alive).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> So these na'arei sarei hamedinot came out of the Ir, and the army which followed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 30:16–17; MELACHIM BAIS 19:36; KOHELET 9:11"> And they struck down every ish his ish; and Aram (the Syrians) fled; and Yisroel pursued them; and Ben-Hadad Melech Aram escaped al-sus (on horse) and the parashim (cavalry).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 3:18; SHOFETIM 3:28; MELACHIM BAIS 3:24"> And the Melech Yisroel went out, and struck down sus and chariot, and slaughtered Aram with a makkah gedolah (great slaughter).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 11:1; MELACHIM ALEF 20:26; MELACHIM ALEF 20:13"> And the navi came to the Melech Yisroel, and said unto him, Go, strengthen thyself, and mark, and see what thou doest; for at the teshuvat hashanah (the turn of the year, springtime) Melech Aram will come up against thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 14:23; SHMUEL ALEF 4:8; TEHILLIM 50:21–22"> The avadim of Melech Aram said unto him, Their elohim are elohei harim (g-ds of the hills); therefore they were stronger than we; but let us fight against them on the plain; then surely we shall be stronger than they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:31; MISHLE 21:30; MELACHIM ALEF 20:16"> And do this thing: Remove the melachim, every ish from his makom (place, post), and put officers in their places;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="TEHILLIM 10:3"> And muster thee an army, like the army that thou hast lost, sus for sus, and merkavah for merkavah; and we will fight against them on the plain, and surely we shall be stronger than they. And he paid heed unto their voice, and did so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 13:17; MELACHIM ALEF 20:22; SHMUEL ALEF 4:1"> And it came to pass at the teshuvat hashanah, that Ben-Hadad mustered Aram, and went up to Aphek, to war against Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHOFETIM 6:5; SHMUEL ALEF 13:5–8; SHMUEL ALEF 14:2"> And the Bnei Yisroel were mustered, and were all outfitted as battle-ready, and marched out to meet them; and the Bnei Yisroel encamped opposite them like two little flocks of izzim (goats); but Aram (the Syrians) filled ha'aretz.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 20:13; MELACHIM ALEF 17:18; YECHEZKEL 12:16"> And there came an Ish HaElohim, and spoke unto Melech Yisroel, and said, Thus saith Hashem, Because Aram have said, Hashem is Elohei Harim, but not Elohei Amakim (G-d of Valleys) therefore will I deliver all this hamon hagadol (vast multitude) into thine yad, and ye shall know that I am Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 13:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:6; TEHILLIM 10:16"> And they encamped these opposite those shivat yamim. And so it was, that in the yom hashevi'i the milchamah was joined; and the Bnei Yisroel slaughtered of Aram a hundred thousand foot soldiers in yom echad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:25; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 18:24; MELACHIM ALEF 20:26"> But the rest fled to Aphek, into the Ir; and there a chomah (wall) fell upon twenty and seven elef ish that were left. And Ben-Hadad fled, and went into the Ir, into cheder b'cheder (a room within a room, an inner room).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:31; BERESHIS 37:34; MELACHIM ALEF 20:23"> And his avadim said unto him, Hinei now, we have heard that the melachim of Bais Yisroel are malkhei chesed; let us, now, put sackcloth on our loins, and ropes upon our heads, and go out to the Melech Yisroel; perhaps he will save thy nefesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 20:3–6; IYOV 12:17–18; DANIEL 5:20–23"> So they girded sackcloth on their loins, and put ropes on their heads, and came to the Melech Yisroel, and said, Thy eved Ben-Hadad saith, Let now my nefesh live. And he said, Is he yet alive? He is achi (my brother).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKAS 16:8; MELACHIM BAIS 10:15; MISHLE 25:13"> Now the anashim did interpret this as a sign of mercy from him, and did hastily seize on it; and they said, Thy brother Ben-Hadad! Then he said, Go ye, bring him. Then Ben-Hadad came forth to him; and he brought him up onto the merkavah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 15:20; YESHAYAH 26:10; MELACHIM ALEF 20:42"> And Ben-hadad said unto him, The cities, which Avi took from Avicha, I will return; and thou shalt set up chutzot (market areas) for thee in Damascus, just as Avi set up in Shomron. Then said Ach'av, I will send thee<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="to let thee go">b</a></sup>away with this brit (covenant). So he made a brit (covenant) with him, and sent him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="to let him go">c</a></sup>away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 2:15; MELACHIM ALEF 13:17–18; MELACHIM BAIS 4:1"> And a certain man of the Bnei HaNevi'im said unto his re'a (neighbor) in the Devar Hashem, Strike me, now. The ish refused to strike him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 13:21–24; MELACHIM ALEF 13:26; SHMUEL ALEF 15:22–23"> Then said he unto him, Because thou hast not obeyed the kol Hashem, behold, thou, as soon as thou art departed from me, an aryeh shall kill thee. And as soon as he was departed from him, an aryeh found him, and killed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="SHEMOT 21:12; MELACHIM ALEF 20:35"> Then he found another ish, and said, strike me, now. And the ish struck him, so that in striking he wounded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 14:2; MATTITYAHU 6:16; MELACHIM ALEF 22:30"> So the navi departed, and stood waiting for HaMelech by the derech, and disguised himself with bandage over his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 10:24; IYOV 36:18; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:18–19"> And as HaMelech passed by, he cried out unto HaMelech; and he said, Thy eved went out into the thick of the milchamah; and, hinei, an ish turned aside, and brought an ish unto me, and said, Be shomer over (guard) this ish: if by any means he be missing, then shall thy nefesh be for his nefesh, or else thou must pay a talent of kesef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 12:5–7; MATTITYAHU 25:24–27; IYOV 15:6"> And while thy eved was busy here and there, he was gone. And the Melech Yisroel said unto him, So shall thy mishpat be; thyself hast decided it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 6:26; SHMUEL BAIS 13:19; IYOV 2:8"> And he hasted, and removed the bandage from over his eyes; and Melech Yisroel recognized him that he was of the Nevi'im.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:31–37; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 18:33–34; MELACHIM ALEF 20:39"> And he said unto him, Thus saith Hashem, Because thou hast let go out of thy yad an ish whom I appointed to cherem (utter destruction), therefore thy nefesh shall be for his nefesh, and thy people instead of his people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 21:4; ESTHER 5:13; ESTHER 6:12–13"> And Melech Yisroel came to his bais sar veza'ef (sullen and dejected), and came to Shomron.</span>
</div>
