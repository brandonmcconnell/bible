<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 23:1–2; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:27–28; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:1"> Now HaMelech Dovid was zaken and stricken in yamim; and they covered him with begadim, but he could get no warmth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="KOHELET 4:11; BERESHIS 16:5; MICHOH 7:5"> Wherefore his avadim said unto him, Let there be sought for adoni HaMelech a na'arah betulah; and let her stand before HaMelech, and let her care for him, and let her lie at thy kheyk (bosom), that adoni HaMelech may be warm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:18; ESTHER 2:4; ESTHER 2:2"> So they sought for a na'arah yafeh throughout all the territory of Yisroel, and found Avishag HaShunammit, and brought her to HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 1:25"> And the na'arah was yafeh ad me'od, and cared for HaMelech, and waited on him; but HaMelech knew her not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:1; SHMUEL BAIS 3:4; DEVARIM 17:15–16"> Then Adoniyah Ben Chaggit put himself forward, saying, I will be Melech; and he prepared him merkavot and parashim, and chamishim ish to run before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:3–4; SHMUEL ALEF 3:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:5–6"> And Aviv had not rebuked him at any time in saying, Why hast thou done so? And he also was tov to'ar (good looking); and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="immo">a</a></sup>bore him after Avshalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 20:25; MELACHIM ALEF 2:22; SHMUEL ALEF 22:20–23"> And he conferred with Yoav Ben Tzeruyah, and with Evyatar (Abiathar) HaKohen; and they lent ezer (help), following after Adoniyah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 4:18; SHMUEL BAIS 23:8–39; SHMUEL BAIS 20:25"> But Tzadok HaKohen, and Benayah Ben Yehoyada, and Natan HaNavi, and Shime'i, and Re'i, and the Gibborim which belonged to Dovid, were not with Adoniyah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 17:17; SHMUEL BAIS 13:23–27; SHMUEL BAIS 15:11–12"> And Adoniyah sacrificed tzon and bakar and fatted bull at the Even Zochelet (Stone of Zoheleth), which is Ein-Rogel, and invited all his achim, the Bnei HaMelech, and all the anshei Yehudah who were avadim of HaMelech;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 12:24; MELACHIM ALEF 1:19; SHMUEL BAIS 12:1–15"> But Natan HaNavi, and Benayah, and the Gibborim, and Sh'lomo his brother, he invited not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 3:4; SHMUEL BAIS 7:12–17; SHMUEL BAIS 12:24–25"> Wherefore Natan spoke unto Bat-Sheva em Sh'lomo, saying, Hast thou not heard that Adoniyah Ben Chaggit became Melech, and Dovid adoneinu knoweth it not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MISHLE 11:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:31; BERESHIS 19:17"> Therefore come, let me, now, give thee counsel, that thou mayest save thine own nefesh, and the nefesh of thy ben Sh'lomo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:30; MELACHIM ALEF 1:17; TEHILLIM 132:11–12"> Go and get thee in unto HaMelech Dovid, and say unto him, Didst not thou, adoni, O HaMelech, swear unto thine amah (maid servant, handmaid) saying, Assuredly Sh'lomo thy ben shall reign as Melech after me, and he shall sit upon my kisse? Why then doth Adoniyah reign as Melech?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:1; MELACHIM ALEF 1:17–27"> Hinei, while thou yet speakest there with HaMelech, I also will come in after thee, and confirm thy devarim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:1"> And Bat-Sheva went in unto HaMelech into the cheder; and HaMelech was zaken me'od; and Avishag HaShunammit attended unto HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 25:23; SHMUEL ALEF 24:8; SHMUEL ALEF 20:41"> And Bat-Sheva bowed, and did obeisance unto HaMelech. And HaMelech said, What wouldest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:13; MELACHIM ALEF 1:30; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:6"> And she said unto him, Adoni, thou didst swear by Hashem Eloheicha unto thine amah, saying, Assuredly Sh'lomo thy ben shall reign as Melech after me, and he shall sit upon my kisse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:24; MELACHIM ALEF 1:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:17"> And now, hinei, Adoniyah reigneth as Melech; and now, adoni HaMelech, thou knowest it not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:25; MELACHIM ALEF 1:7–10"> And he hath sacrificed shor and fatted bull and tzon in abundance, and hath invited all the Bnei HaMelech, and Evyatar (Abiathar) HaKohen, and Yoav the Sar HaTzava; but Sh'lomo thy eved hath he not invited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:1; TEHILLIM 123:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:8–10"> And thou, adoni HaMelech, the eyes of kol Yisroel are upon thee, that thou shouldest tell them who shall sit on the kisse adoni HaMelech after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:10; DEVARIM 31:16; BERESHIS 15:15"> Otherwise it shall come to pass, when adoni HaMelech shall lie with his avot, that I and beni (my son) Sh'lomo shall be counted chatta'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="IYOV 1:16–18; BERESHIS 24:15; DANIEL 9:20"> And, hinei, while she yet was speaking with HaMelech, Natan HaNavi also came in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:16; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:7"> And they told HaMelech, saying, Hinei Natan HaNavi! And when he was come in before HaMelech, he bowed himself before HaMelech with his face to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:17–18; MELACHIM ALEF 1:5; MELACHIM ALEF 1:13–14"> And Natan said, Adoni HaMelech, hast thou said, Adoniyah shall reign as Melech after me, and he shall sit upon my kisse?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 10:24; MELACHIM ALEF 1:19; MELACHIM ALEF 1:9"> For he has gone down this day, and hath sacrificed shor and fatted bull and tzon in abundance, and hath invited all the Bnei HaMelech, and the sarei hatzava, and Evyatar (Abiathar) HaKohen; and, behold, they eat and drink before him, and say, Yechi HaMelech Adoniyahu (long live HaMelech Adoniyah)!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:8; MELACHIM ALEF 1:10; SHMUEL BAIS 7:2"> But me, even me thy eved, and Tzadok HaKohen, and Benayah Ben Yehoyada, and thy eved Sh'lomo, hath he not invited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:15; MELACHIM ALEF 1:24; MELACHIM BAIS 4:27"> Is this thing done by adoni HaMelech, and thou hast not showed it unto thy eved, even who should sit on the kisse adoni HaMelech after him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Then HaMelech Dovid answered and said, Call me Bat-Sheva. And she came into the presence of HaMelech, and stood before HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 4:9; TEHILLIM 136:24; SHMUEL ALEF 14:45"> And HaMelech swore a shevu'a (oath), and said, As Hashem liveth, that hath redeemed my nefesh out of kol tzarah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:13; MELACHIM ALEF 1:17"> Even as I swore a shevu'a unto thee by Hashem Elohei Yisroel, saying, Assuredly Sh'lomo thy ben shall reign as Melech after me, he shall sit upon my kisse in my place; so will I certainly do this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DANIEL 2:4; DANIEL 5:10; DANIEL 3:9"> Then Bat-Sheva bowed with her face to eretz, and did reverence to HaMelech, and said, Yechi adoni HaMelech Dovid l'olam (long live adoni HaMelech forever)!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:8; MELACHIM ALEF 1:38; MELACHIM ALEF 1:26"> And HaMelech Dovid said, Call me Tzadok HaKohen, and Natan HaNavi, and Benayah Ben Yehoyada. And they came before HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:30; MELACHIM ALEF 1:38; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:14"> HaMelech also said unto them, Take with you the avadim of adoneichem, and have Sh'lomo beni (my son) to ride upon mine own mule, and bring him down to Gichon;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Yn 12:14">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:25; SHMUEL BAIS 15:10; SHMUEL ALEF 10:1"> And let Tzadok HaKohen and Natan HaNavi mashach (anoint) him there Melech over Yisroel; and blow ye with the shofar, and say, Yechi HaMelech Sh'lomo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:17; TEHILLIM 72:1–2; MELACHIM ALEF 1:13"> Then ye shall come up after him, that he may come and sit upon my kisse; for he shall be Melech in my place; and I have appointed him to be Nagid (ruler) over Yisroel and over Yehudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 28:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:16; TEHILLIM 72:19"> And Benayah Ben Yehoyada answered HaMelech, and said, Omein; And so may Hashem Elohei adoni HaMelech say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 20:13; MELACHIM ALEF 1:47; YEHOSHUA 1:17"> As Hashem hath been with adoni HaMelech, even so may He be with Sh'lomo, and make his kisse greater than the kisse of adoni HaMelech Dovid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:18; MELACHIM ALEF 1:8; MELACHIM ALEF 1:33"> So Tzadok HaKohen, and Natan HaNavi, and Benayah Ben Yehoyada, and the Kereti, and the Peleti, went down, and had Sh'lomo ride upon the mule of HaMelech Dovid, and brought him to Gichon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="TEHILLIM 89:20; MELACHIM ALEF 1:34; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:22"> And Tzadok HaKohen took a keren of shemen out of the ohel, and mashach (anointed) Sh'lomo. And they blew the shofar; and kol HaAm shouted, Yechi HaMelech Sh'lomo!</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 11:14; ZECHARYAH 9:9; MELACHIM BAIS 11:20"> And kol HaAm came up after him, and the people played chalilim (flutes), and rejoiced with simchah gedolah, so that ha'aretz split with the sound of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="IYOV 15:21–22; LUKAS 17:26–29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:31"> And Adoniyah and kol hakeru'im (all the invited ones) that were with him heard it as they had made an end of eating. And when Yoav heard the kol hashofar, he said, Why is the sound of the Ir in an uproar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:36; SHMUEL BAIS 17:17; SHMUEL BAIS 18:27"> And while he yet spoke, hinei, Yonatan Ben Evyatar (Abiathar) HaKohen came; and Adoniyah said unto him, Come, for thou art an ish chayil, and tov tevaser (bringest good tidings).</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And Yonatan answered and said to Adoniyah, Just the opposite! Verily adoneinu HaMelech Dovid hath made Sh'lomo Melech!</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And HaMelech hath sent with him Tzadok HaKohen, and Natan HaNavi, and Benayah Ben Yehoyada, and the Kereti, and the Peleti, and they have had him ride upon pirdah (mule) of HaMelech:</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:40; DANIEL 5:26–28; SHMUEL ALEF 4:5"> And Tzadok HaKohen and Natan HaNavi have anointed him Melech at Gichon; and they are come up from there rejoicing, so that the city rang out. This is the noise that ye have heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:23; TEHILLIM 132:11; MELACHIM ALEF 1:13"> And also Sh'lomo sitteth on the kisse hameluchah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="BERESHIS 47:31; MELACHIM ALEF 1:37; EZRA 6:10"> And moreover the avadim of HaMelech came to make a brocha on adoneinu HaMelech Dovid, saying, May your G-d make the shem of Sh'lomo more famous than thy shem, and make his kisse greater than thy kisse. And HaMelech bowed himself upon the mishkav (bed).</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 3:6; TEHILLIM 132:11–12; TEHILLIM 145:2"> Also so said HaMelech, Baruch Hashem Elohei Yisroel, Who hath given one to sit on my kisse this day, mine eyes even seeing it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="YESHAYAH 21:4–5; MISHLE 28:1; DANIEL 5:4–6"> And kol hakeruvim (all the invited ones) that were with Adoniyah were terrified, and rose up, and went every ish his own derech.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Isa 53:6">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:28; SHEMOT 21:14; SHEMOT 27:2"> And Adoniyah feared because of Sh'lomo, and arose, and went, and caught hold of the karnayim of the Mizbe'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And it was told Sh'lomo, saying, Hinei, Adoniyah feareth HaMelech Sh'lomo; for, hinei, he hath caught hold of the karnayim of the Mizbe'ach, saying, Let Melech Sh'lomo swear a shevua (oath) unto me today that he will not slay his eved with the cherev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 14:45; SHMUEL BAIS 14:11; MISHLE 13:6"> And Sh'lomo said, If he will show himself a ben chayil (a son of worthiness), there shall not a hair of him fall to ha'aretz; but if ra'ah (wickedness) shall be found in him, he shall die.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ro 6:23">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:16; SHMUEL BAIS 14:24; SHMUEL BAIS 1:2"> So HaMelech Sh'lomo sent, and they brought him down from the Mizbe'ach. And he came and bowed himself to Melech Sh'lomo; and Sh'lomo said unto him, Go to thine bais.</span>
