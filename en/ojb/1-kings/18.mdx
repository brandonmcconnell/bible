</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 6:25; MISHLE 28:1; VAYIKRA 26:26"> And Eliyahu went to show himself unto Ach'av. And there was a ra'av chazak (strong famine) in Shomron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 7:2; MELACHIM ALEF 16:9; MATTITYAHU 10:28"> And Ach'av summoned Ovadyah, who was in charge of the Bayit (Palace) — now Ovadyah feared Hashem me'od (greatly);</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:13; MATTITYAHU 10:40–42; HISGALUS 17:4–6"> For it was so, when Izevel cut off the Nevi'im of Hashem, that Ovadyah took a hundred Nevi'im, and hid them by fifty in a me'arah (cave), and fed them with lechem and mayim —</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 14:5–6; YOEL 1:18; YOEL 2:22"> And Ach'av said unto Ovadyah, Go into HaAretz unto all springs of mayim, and unto all brooks; perhaps we may find khatzir (grass) to save the sus (horse) and pered (mules) alive, that we not be dispossessed of all the behemah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 14:3"> So they divided ha'aretz between them to pass throughout it; Ach'av went derech echad by himself, and Ovadyah went derech echad by himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 1:6–8; BERESHIS 44:20; YESHAYAH 60:14"> And as Ovadyah was baderech (on the way, road), hinei, Eliyahu met him; and he recognized him, and fell on his face, and said, Is it you, adoni Eliyahu?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:7; MELACHIM ALEF 18:3; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:17–18"> And he answered him, I am; go, tell adoneicha, Hinei, Eliyahu!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 5:21; MELACHIM ALEF 18:12; MELACHIM ALEF 17:18"> And he said, What have I sinned, that thou wouldest deliver thy eved into the yad Ach'av to slay me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 17:1; MELACHIM ALEF 1:29; MELACHIM ALEF 18:15"> As Hashem Eloheicha liveth, there is no goy (nation) or mamlachah (kingdom), to where adoni hath not sent to seek thee there: and when they said, He is not there, he had the mamlachah and goy swear, that they found thee not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:8; MELACHIM ALEF 18:14"> And now thou sayest, Go, tell adoneicha, Hinei, Eliyahu!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 2:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:39; YECHEZKEL 8:3"> And it shall come to pass, as soon as I am gone from thee, that the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hakodesh">a</a></sup>of Hashem shall carry thee to where I know not; and so when I come and tell Ach'av, and he cannot find thee, he shall slay me; but I thy eved fear Hashem from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:4; MATTITYAHU 25:35; TEHILLIM 18:21–24"> Was it not told to adoni what I did when Izevel slaughtered the Nevi'im of Hashem, how I hid a hundred ish of Hashemʼs Nevi'im by fifty in a me'arah, and fed them with lechem and mayim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:28"> And now thou sayest, Go, tell adoneicha, Hinei, Eliyahu! And he shall slay me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 17:1; MELACHIM ALEF 18:10; LUKAS 2:13–14"> And Eliyahu said, As Hashem Tzva'os liveth, before whom I stand, I will surely show myself unto him today.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So Ovadyah went to meet Ach'av, and told him; and Ach'av went to meet Eliyahu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 7:25; MELACHIM ALEF 21:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:20"> And it came to pass, when Ach'av saw Eliyahu, that Ach'av said unto him, Art thou he that troubleth Yisroel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:2; MELACHIM ALEF 9:9; YIRMEYAH 2:19"> And he answered, I have not troubled Yisroel; but thou, and the Bais Avicha, in that ye have forsaken the Mitzvot Hashem, and thou hast followed after the Ba'alim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:26; MELACHIM ALEF 16:33; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1"> Now therefore summon, and gather to me kol Yisroel unto Har HaCarmel and the nevi'im of Ba'al four hundred and fifty, and the nevi'im haAsherah four hundred, which eat at shulchan Izevel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:9"> So Ach'av sent unto kol Bnei Yisroel, and gathered the Nevi'im together unto Har HaCarmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:24; YEHOSHUA 24:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:21–22"> And Eliyahu came unto kol haAm, and said, Ad Mosai (how long) ye be polekhim (limping ones, dancing ones) between two se'ipim (opinions)? If Hashem be HaElohim, follow after him; but if HaBa'al, then follow after him. And HaAm answered him not a word.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 19:10; MELACHIM ALEF 19:14; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:3–4"> Then said Eliyahu unto HaAm, I, even I only, am left a navi of Hashem; but Ba'alʼs nevi'im are four hundred and fifty ish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Let them therefore give us two parim (bulls); and let them choose one bull for themselves, and cut it in pieces, and lay it on wood, and put no eish under it; and I will dress the other bull, and lay it on wood, and put no eish under it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:38; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:26; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:1"> And call ye on the shem eloheichem, and I will call on the Shem of Hashem; and haelohim that answereth by eish, let him be HaElohim. And kol haAm answered and said, Tov hadavar!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Eliyahu said unto the nevi'im of Ba'al, Choose you one bull for yourselves, and dress it first; for ye are rabbim (many); and call on the shem eloheichem, but put no eish under it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 10:5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:4; MATTITYAHU 6:7"> And they took the bull which was given them, and they dressed it, and called on the shem of Ba'al from boker even until tzohorayim (noon), saying, O Ba'al, answer us. But there was no voice, and no one answered. And they danced<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="same word used in verse 18:21">b</a></sup>around the mizbe'ach which had been made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:65–66; YESHAYAH 41:23; KOHELET 11:9"> And it came to pass at tzohorayim, that Eliyahu mocked them, taunting, saying, Shout with a kol gadol (loud voice); for he is a g-d; either he is meditating, or he is pursuing,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="something">c</a></sup>or ki derech lo (perhaps he had to travel, heʼs relieving himself), or perhaps he sleepeth and must be awakened.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:28; DEVARIM 14:1; MARKOS 5:5"> And they shouted with a kol gadol, and cut themselves after their mishpat (custom) with charavot (swords) and lances, till the dahm gushed out upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:26; SHMUEL ALEF 18:10; SHEMOT 29:39"> And it came to pass, when tzohorayim was past, and they prophesied until the time of the offering of the minchah (evening sacrifice), that there was neither kol (voice), nor any oneh (responder) nor any attender.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:16; MELACHIM ALEF 19:14; MELACHIM ALEF 19:10"> And Eliyahu said unto kol HaAm, Come near unto me. And kol HaAm came near unto him. And he repaired the Mizbe'ach Hashem that was in ruins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 17:34; BERESHIS 35:10; BERESHIS 32:28"> And Eliyahu took twelve avanim (stones), according to the mispar (number) of the Shivtei Bnei Ya'akov, unto whom the Devar Hashem came, saying, Yisroel shall be Shemecha;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:17; SHEMOT 20:24–25; SHOFETIM 21:4"> And with the avanim (stones) he built a Mizbe'ach in the Shem of Hashem; and he made a te'alah (trench) around the Mizbe'ach, as the space of two seahs of zera (seed).</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="BERESHIS 22:9; SHOFETIM 6:20; VAYIKRA 1:6–8"> And he laid the wood in order, and cut up the bull, and laid it on the wood, and said, Fill four jugs with mayim, and pour it on the olah and on the wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:21; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:2"> And Eliyahu said, Do it the second time. And they did it the second time. And he said, Do it the third time. And they did it the third time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:32; MELACHIM ALEF 18:38"> And the mayim ran around about the Mizbe'ach; and even filled the te'alah (trench) also with mayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:29; MELACHIM BAIS 19:19; SHEMOT 3:6"> And it came to pass at the time of the offering of the minchah (evening sacrifice), that Eliyahu HaNavi came near, and said, Hashem Elohei Avraham, Yitzchak and of Yisroel, let it be known this day that Thou art Elohim in Yisroel, and that I am Thy Eved, and that I have done all these things at Thy Davar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DANIEL 9:17–19; LUKAS 1:16–17; YECHEZKEL 36:25–27"> Answer me, Hashem, answer me, that HaAm Hazeh may know that Thou art Hashem HaElohim, and that Thou hast turned back their lev.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:26; VAYIKRA 9:24; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:1"> Then the Eish of Hashem fell, and consumed the olah and the wood, and the avanim, and the aphar, and licked up the mayim that was in the te'alah (trench).</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:24; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:3; MELACHIM ALEF 18:21"> And when kol HaAm saw it, they fell on their faces; and they said, Hashem hu HaElohim; Hashem hu HaElohim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="DEVARIM 18:20; SHOFETIM 4:7; DEVARIM 13:5"> And Eliyahu said unto them, Seize the nevi'im of Ba'al; let not one of them escape. And they took them; and Eliyahu brought them down to the Brook Kishon, and slaughtered them there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:1; KOHELET 9:7; MELACHIM ALEF 17:1"> And Eliyahu said unto Ach'av, Get thee up, eat and drink; for there is a kol hamon hageshem (sound of rushing rain, sound of rain coming).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="LUKAS 6:12; DANIEL 9:7; YEHOSHUA 7:6"> So Ach'av went up to eat and to drink. But Eliyahu climbed up to Rosh HaCarmel (top of Carmel); and he cast himself down upon the ground, and put his face between his birkayim (knees),</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="CHABAKUK 2:3; LUKAS 18:7; LUKAS 18:1"> And said to his eved, Go up now, look toward the yam. And he went up, and looked, and said, There is nothing. And he said, Go again sheva pe'amim (seven times).</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="LUKAS 12:54; IYOV 8:7; ZECHARYAH 4:10"> And it came to pass the seventh time, that he said, Hinei, there ariseth a little cloud out of the yam, as small as kaf ish (a manʼs palm). And he said, Go, say unto Ach'av, Harness up &#91;thy merkavah&#93; and get thee down that the geshem stop thee not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:39–40; YEHOSHUA 17:16; MELACHIM ALEF 21:1"> And it came to pass in the meanwhile, that the Shomayim grew black with clouds and ruach, and there was a geshem gadol. And Ach'av rode, and went to Yizre'el.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Jezreel">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 3:14; YECHEZKEL 1:3; MELACHIM BAIS 9:1"> And the Yad Hashem was on Eliyahu; and he girded up his loins, and ran ahead of Ach'av to the entrance of Yizre'el.</span>
</div>
