<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 36:1; MELACHIM BAIS 23:15–18; MELACHIM BAIS 22:1–2"> And he cried against the mizbe'ach by the Devar Hashem, and said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">O mizbe'ach, mizbe'ach, thus saith Hashem;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Hinei, ben nolad (a son shall be born) unto the Bais Dovid,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yoshiyah shmo;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and upon thee shall he offer</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the kohanim of the</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="idolatrous">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">high places that burn ketoret upon thee,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and atzmot adam shall be burned upon thee.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2Kgs 23:15-16">b</a></sup></span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:22; SHEMOT 4:3–5; SHOFETIM 6:17"> And he gave a sign the same day, saying, This is the mofet (sign) that Hashem hath spoken: Hinei, the mizbe'ach shall be split apart, and the deshen (fat ashes) that are upon it shall be poured out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HISGALUS 11:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 18:25–27; LUKAS 6:10"> And it came to pass, when HaMelech Yarov'am heard the davar of the Ish HaElohim, which had cried against the mizbe'ach in Beit-El, that he put forth his yad from the mizbe'ach, saying, Lay hold on him. And his yad, which he put forth against him, shriveled up, so that he could not draw it back to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 13:3; YIRMEYAH 28:16–17; DEVARIM 18:22"> The mizbe'ach also was split apart, and the deshen poured out from the mizbe'ach, according to the mofet which the Ish HaElohim had given by the Devar Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 9:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:24; SHEMOT 10:17"> And HaMelech answered and said unto the Ish HaElohim, Intercede now before the face of Hashem Eloheicha, and daven for me, that my yad may be restored to me. And the Ish HaElohim made intercessory prayer before Hashem, and the yad HaMelech was restored to him, and became as it was before.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 5:15; SHMUEL ALEF 9:7–8; SHOFETIM 13:15"> And HaMelech said unto the Ish HaElohim, Come with me habaytah (home), and have seudah, and I will give thee a mattat (gift).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 24:13; BAMIDBAR 22:18; MARKOS 6:23"> And the Ish HaElohim said unto HaMelech, If thou wilt give me half thine bais, I will not go in with thee, neither will I eat lechem nor drink mayim in this makom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:17; DEVARIM 13:13–18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:17"> For so was it charged me by the Devar Hashem, saying, Eat no lechem, nor drink mayim, nor return again by the same derech that thou camest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So he went another derech, and returned not by the derech that he came to Beit-El.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 23:18; MELACHIM ALEF 13:20–21; BAMIDBAR 24:2"> Now there dwelt a navi zaken (old prophet) in Beit-El; and his banim came and told him kol hama'aseh that the Ish HaElohim had done that day in Beit-El: the devarim which he had spoken unto HaMelech, them they told also to their av.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And their av said unto them, What derech went he? For his banim had seen on what derech the Ish HaElohim, who came from Yehudah, went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHOFETIM 10:4; SHMUEL BAIS 19:26; SHOFETIM 5:10"> And he said unto his banim, Saddle me the chamor. So they saddled him the chamor; and he rode thereon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:12–13; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:27; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:11–12"> And went after the Ish HaElohim, found him sitting under the elah (the oak tree); and he said unto him, Art thou the Ish HaElohim that camest from Yehudah? And he said, I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then he said unto him, Come with me home, and eat lechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 13:8–9; MATTITYAHU 16:23; BAMIDBAR 22:19"> And he said, I may not return with thee, nor go in with thee; neither will I eat lechem nor drink mayim with thee in this makom;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 20:35; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:15; MELACHIM ALEF 13:1"> For it was told to me by the Devar Hashem, Thou shalt eat no lechem nor drink mayim there, nor turn again to go by the derech on which thou camest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1; MATTITYAHU 7:15; BAMIDBAR 22:35"> He said unto him, I am a navi also as thou art; and a malach spoke unto me by the Devar Hashem, saying, Bring him back with thee into thine bais, that he may eat lechem and drink mayim. But he lied unto him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 13:1; BERESHIS 3:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:19"> So he went back with him, did eat lechem in his bais and drank mayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:22; BAMIDBAR 24:4; BAMIDBAR 24:16–24"> And it came to pass, as they sat at shulchan, that the Devar Hashem came unto the navi that brought him back;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 13:17; SHMUEL ALEF 13:13–14; SHMUEL ALEF 4:18"> And he cried unto the Ish HaElohim that came from Yehudah, saying, Thus saith Hashem, Forasmuch as thou hast defied the mouth of Hashem, and hast not been shomer over the mitzvah which Hashem Eloheicha commanded thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YESHAYAH 14:18–20; MELACHIM ALEF 14:13; YIRMEYAH 22:18–19"> But camest back, and hast eaten lechem and drunk mayim in the makom, of the which Hashem did say to thee, Eat no lechem, drink no mayim; thy nevelah shall not come unto kever avoteicha.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And it came to pass, after he had eaten lechem, and after he had drunk, that he saddled the chamor for him, for the navi whom he had brought back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 20:36; MELACHIM BAIS 2:24; MISHLE 26:13"> And when he was gone, an aryeh met him by the derech, and killed him; and his nevelah was cast down in the derech, and the chamor stood beside her,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="the lion">c</a></sup>and the aryeh also stood beside the nevelah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 13:11"> And, hinei, anashim passed by, and saw the nevelah cast down in the derech, and the aryeh standing beside the nevelah; and they came and told it in the Ir where the navi hazaken dwelt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 9:6; MISHLE 11:31; TEHILLIM 119:120"> And when the navi that brought him back from the derech heard thereof, he said, It is the Ish HaElohim, who defied the mouth of Hashem; therefore Hashem hath delivered him unto the aryeh, which hath mauled him, and slain him, according to the Devar Hashem, which He spoke unto him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And he spoke to his banim, saying, Saddle me the chamor. And they saddled it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 17:6; TEHILLIM 148:7–8; DANIEL 6:22–24"> And he went and found his nevelah cast down in the derech, and the chamor and the aryeh standing beside the nevelah; the aryeh had not eaten the nevelah, nor mauled the chamor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The navi took up the nevelah of the Ish HaElohim, laid it upon the chamor, brought it back; and came to the Ir of the navi hazaken to mourn and to bury him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 22:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:2; MELACHIM ALEF 14:13"> And he laid his nevelah in his own kever; and they mourned over him, saying, Hoy (alas), achi (my brother)!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 23:17–18; BAMIDBAR 23:10; LUKAS 16:22–23"> And it came to pass, after he had buried him, that he spoke to his banim, saying, When I am dead, then bury me in the kever wherein the Ish HaElohim is buried; lay my atzmot beside his atzmot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 16:24; MELACHIM ALEF 13:2; MELACHIM ALEF 12:31"> For the davar which he cried by the Devar Hashem against the mizbe'ach in Beit-El, and against all the batim of the high places which are in the cities of Shomron, shall surely come to pass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:15; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 13:9; BAMIDBAR 17:12–13"> After this thing Yarov'am returned not from his derech har'ah, but installed again of the lowest of the people as kohanim of the high places; whosoever desired, he filled hands of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="i.e., ordained">d</a></sup>him, and he became one of the kohanim of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="idolatrous">e</a></sup>high places.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Lv 7:37and 8:25-36">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 14:10; MELACHIM ALEF 15:29–30; MELACHIM ALEF 12:30"> And this thing became chattat (sin) unto the Bais Yarov'am, even to annihilate it, and to destroy it from off the face of ha'adamah.</span>
</div>
