<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:11–6; MELACHIM ALEF 9:19; MELACHIM ALEF 7:51"> And it came to pass, when Sh'lomo had completed the building of the Beis Hashem, and the Bais HaMelech, and all Sh'lomoʼs desire which he was pleased to do,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 3:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:12; MELACHIM ALEF 11:9"> That Hashem appeared to Sh'lomo the second time, as He had appeared unto him at Giv'on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TEHILLIM 10:17; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:14; MELACHIM BAIS 20:5"> And Hashem said unto him, I have heard thy tefillah and thy techinnah, that thou hast made before Me; I have set apart as kodesh this Beis,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="HaMikdash">a</a></sup>which thou hast built, to put Shemi there ad olam; and Mine eyes and Mine lev shall be there kol hayamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 15:5; MELACHIM ALEF 3:14; MELACHIM ALEF 11:6"> And if thou wilt walk before Me, just as Dovid Avicha walked, in tohm (integrity, guilelessness) of lev, and in yashar, to do according to all that I have commanded thee, and will be shomer over My chukkot and My mishpatim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:4; MELACHIM ALEF 6:12; SHMUEL BAIS 7:16"> Then I will establish the kisse (throne) of thy kingdom over Yisroel l'olam, as I promised to Dovid Avicha, saying, There shall not yikaret (be cut off, fail) thee an ish upon the Kisse Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 7:14–16; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:2"> But if ye shall at all turn from following Me, ye or your banim, and will not be shomer over My mitzvot and My chukkot which I have set before you, but go and serve elohim acherim, and hishtachaveh (worship) them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 44:14; YIRMEYAH 24:9; DEVARIM 4:26"> Then will I cut off Yisroel out of the adamah which I have given them; and this Beis,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="HaMikdash">b</a></sup>which I have set apart as kodesh for Shemi, will I cast out of My sight; and Yisroel shall be a mashal (proverb) and a sheninah (taunt, byword) among kol ha'ammim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEVARIM 29:24–26; YIRMEYAH 22:8–9; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:21"> And at this Beis, which is elyon, every one that passeth by it shall be appalled, and shall hiss; and they shall say, Why hath Hashem done thus unto HaAretz Hazot, and to this Beis?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 2:19; YIRMEYAH 5:19; DEVARIM 29:25–28"> And they shall answer, Because they forsook Hashem Eloheichem who brought forth their avot out of Eretz Mitzrayim, and have taken hold upon elohim acherim, and have worshiped them, and served them; therefore hath Hashem brought upon them all this ra'ah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 6:37–1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:1–18; MELACHIM ALEF 9:1"> And it came to pass at the end of esrim shanim, when Sh'lomo had built the two batim, the Beis Hashem, and the Bais HaMelech,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:8–10"> (Now Chiram Melech Tzor had furnished Sh'lomo with cedar trees and cypress trees, and with zahav, according to all his desire), that then HaMelech Sh'lomo gave Chiram esrim ir (twenty towns) in Eretz HaGalil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 22:34; SHOFETIM 14:3"> And Chiram came out from Tzor (Tyre) to see the towns which Sh'lomo had given him; and they pleased him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:27; AMOS 1:9; MELACHIM ALEF 5:1–2"> And he said, What towns are these which thou hast given me, achi (my brother)? And he called them Eretz Kavul unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:11; MELACHIM ALEF 10:10; MELACHIM ALEF 9:28"> And Chiram sent to HaMelech 120 talents of zahav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 17:11; MELACHIM ALEF 9:24; SHMUEL BAIS 5:9"> And this is the account of the mas (forced labor) which HaMelech Sh'lomo conscripted; to build the Beis Hashem, and his own Bais, and Millo, and the Chomat Yerushalayim, and Chatzor, and Megiddo, and Gezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 3:1; MELACHIM ALEF 9:24; MELACHIM ALEF 7:8"> For Pharaoh Melech Mitzrayim had gone up, and captured Gezer, and burned it with eish, and slain the Kena'ani that dwelt in the ir, and given it for shilluchim (going-away presents) unto his bat eshet Sh'lomo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 16:3; YEHOSHUA 21:22; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:4–18"> And Sh'lomo rebuilt Gezer, and Lower Beit-Choron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:44; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:4"> And Ba'alat, and Tadmor in the midbar, in the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:1; KOHELET 6:9; SHEMOT 1:11"> And all the storage cities that Sh'lomo had, and cities for his merkavot, and cities for his parashim (calvary), and that which Sh'lomo desired to build in Yerushalayim, and in Levanon, and in all the eretz of his memshalet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:23; BERESHIS 15:19–21; DEVARIM 7:1–3"> And kol haAm that were left of HaEmori (the Amorites), HaChitti (Hittites), HaPerizzi (Perizzites), HaChivi (Hivites), and Yevusi (Jebusites), which were not of the Bnei Yisroel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 17:12; SHOFETIM 1:21; YEHOSHUA 15:63"> Their banim that were left after them in HaAretz, whom the Bnei Yisroel also were not able utterly to destroy, upon those did Sh'lomo conscript mas of oved (bondservice) unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:39; MELACHIM ALEF 4:1–27; SHMUEL ALEF 8:11–12"> But of the Bnei Yisroel did Sh'lomo make no eved; but they were anshei hamilchamah, and his avadim, and his sarim, and his shalishim (captains), and commanders of his merkavot, and his parashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 5:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:18"> These were the chief of the officials that were over the melachah (work) of Sh'lomo five hundred and fifty, harodim (the ones supervising) the people that wrought in the melachah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:8; SHMUEL BAIS 5:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:5"> But Bat Pharaoh came up out of the Ir Dovid unto her Bais (Palace) which Sh'lomo had built for her; then did he build the Millo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:12–13; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:16; SHEMOT 23:14–17"> And three times in shanah did Sh'lomo offer olot and shelamim upon the Mizbe'ach which he built unto Hashem and he burned ketoret upon the Mizbe'ach that was before Hashem. So he completed the Beis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:48; BAMIDBAR 33:35; DEVARIM 2:8"> And HaMelech Sh'lomo built a fleet of oniyyot in Etzyon-Gever, which is near Eilot, on the shore of the Yam Suf, in Eretz Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 10:11; MELACHIM ALEF 5:6; MELACHIM ALEF 5:9"> And Chiram sent to the fleet of oniyyot his avadim, anshei oniyyot (shipmen, sailors) that had da'as hayam (knowledge of the sea), with the avadim of Sh'lomo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 8:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:4; MELACHIM ALEF 10:11"> And they came to Ophir, and brought back from there zahav, four hundred and twenty talents, and brought it to HaMelech Sh'lomo.</span>
