<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:46; MELACHIM ALEF 14:23–24; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:2–5"> And he removed kedeshim (the male and female cult prostitutes) out of HaAretz, and got rid of kol hagillulim (all the idols) that his avot had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 32:20; DEVARIM 7:5; ZECHARYAH 13:3"> And also Ma'achah immo, even her he deposed from being Gevirah (Queen Mother), because she had made a mifletzet (idol) to Asherah; and Asa cut down her mifletzet, and burned it in the Kidron Valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:43; MELACHIM ALEF 8:61; MELACHIM ALEF 15:3"> But the high places were not removed; nevertheless levav Asa was shalem with Hashem all his days.</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 15:32; MELACHIM ALEF 14:30; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 16:1–6"> And there was milchamah between Asa and Ba'asha Melech Yisroel all their days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:27; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 16:1–6; YEHOSHUA 18:25"> And Ba'asha Melech Yisroel went up against Yehudah, and built up Ramah, that he might not suffer any to go out or come in to Asa Melech Yehudah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 14:26; MELACHIM BAIS 12:18; MELACHIM ALEF 11:23–24"> Then Asa took all the kesef and the zahav that were left in the otzarot of the Beis Hashem, and the otzarot of the Bais HaMelech, and delivered them into the yad of his avadim; and HaMelech Asa sent them to Ben-Hadad, Ben Tavrimmon Ben Chezyon Melech Aram, that dwelt at Damascus, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:31; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 16:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:8"> There is a brit between me and thee, and between Avi and Avicha; hinei, I send unto thee a shochad (incentive kind of gift) of kesef and zahav; come, break thy brit with Ba'asha Melech Yisroel, that he may withdraw from me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 14:17; MELACHIM ALEF 16:15–18; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 16:5"> And it came to pass, when Ba'asha heard this, he stopped building Ramah, and stayed in Tirtzah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 21:17; YEHOSHUA 18:24; YEHOSHUA 18:26"> Then HaMelech Asa made a proclamation throughout all Yehudah; none was exempted; and they carried away the stones of Ramah, and the timber thereof, wherewith Ba'asha had built; and HaMelech Asa built up with them Geva of Binyamin, and Mitzpah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

Nevertheless in the time of his ziknah (old age) he was diseased in his raglayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 14:20; MELACHIM ALEF 14:12"> And Nadav Ben Yarov'am began to reign as Melech over Yisroel in the second year of Asa Melech Yehudah, and reigned over Yisroel two years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 14:16; MELACHIM ALEF 15:34; MELACHIM ALEF 15:30"> And he did rah in the eyes of Hashem, and walked in the derech Aviv, and in his chattat wherewith he made Yisroel to commit sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:44; YEHOSHUA 21:23; MELACHIM ALEF 14:14"> And Ba'asha Ben Achiyah, of the Bais of Yissakhar, plotted a keshar against him; and Ba'asha struck him down at Gibeton, which belonged to the Pelishtim; for Nadav and kol Yisroel were laying siege to Gibeton.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:35"> Even in the third year of Asa Melech Yehudah did Ba'asha slay him, and reigned in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 14:9–16; MELACHIM BAIS 19:25; MELACHIM BAIS 10:10–11"> And it came to pass, when he reigned, that he put to death kol Bais Yarov'am; he left not to Yarov'am kol neshamah, until he had destroyed him, according unto the Devar Hashem, which He spoke by His eved Achiyah the Shiloni;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1Kgs 14:9-16">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 15:26; MELACHIM ALEF 14:22; MELACHIM ALEF 14:9–16"> Because of the chattot Yarov'am which he sinned, and which he made Yisroel sin, by his provocation wherewith he provoked Hashem Elohei Yisroel to anger.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 14:19; MELACHIM ALEF 16:20; MELACHIM ALEF 16:14"> Now the rest of the acts of Nadav, and all that he did, are they not written in the Sefer Divrei HaYamim L'Malkhei Yisroel?</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 15:26; MELACHIM ALEF 14:16; MELACHIM ALEF 12:28–29"> And he did rah (evil) in the eyes of Hashem, and walked in the derech Yarov'am, and in his chattat wherewith he made Yisroel to commit sin.</span>
