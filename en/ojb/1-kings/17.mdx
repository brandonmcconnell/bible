<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 17:9; TEHILLIM 147:9; IYOV 38:41"> And it shall be, that thou shalt drink of the brook; and I have commanded the orevvim (ravens) to feed thee there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 19:9; MATTITYAHU 16:24; YOCHANAN 15:14"> So he went and did according to the Devar Hashem; for he went and dwelt in the Wadi Kerit, that is near the Yarden.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:23–24; MATTITYAHU 6:31–33; YIRMEYAH 37:21"> And the orevvim brought him lechem and basar in the boker, and lechem and basar in the erev; and he drank of the brook.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YESHAYAH 54:10; YESHAYAH 40:30–31"> And it came to pass at the ketz yamim (end of days), that the brook dried up, because there had been no geshem in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 22:14; MELACHIM ALEF 17:2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:6"> And the Devar Hashem came unto him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 4:26; OVADYAH 1:20; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:7"> Arise, get thee to Tzarphat, which belongeth to Tzidon, and dwell there; hinei, I have commanded an isha almanah there to sustain thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:7; BERESHIS 24:17; BERESHIS 21:15"> So he arose and went to Tzarphat. And when he came to the gate of the ir, hinei, the isha almanah was there gathering sticks; and he called to her, and said, Bring me a little mayim in a vessel, that I may drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:35–40; MELACHIM ALEF 18:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:2"> And as she was going to get it, he called to her, and said, Bring me a morsel of lechem in thine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:33–34; MELACHIM ALEF 17:1; BERESHIS 21:16"> And she said, As Hashem Eloheicha liveth, I have nothing baked, but an handful of meal in a pot, and a little shemen in a jug; and, see, I am gathering two sticks, that I may go in, cook it for me and beni, that we may eat it, and die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:33; MALACHI 3:10; BERESHIS 22:1–2"> And Eliyahu said unto her, Fear not; go and do as thou hast said; but make me thereof a little loaf first, and bring it unto me, and after that make for thee and for thy ben.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:36–38; MATTITYAHU 14:17–20; MELACHIM BAIS 4:42–44"> For thus saith Hashem Elohei Yisroel, The pot of meal shall not be used up, neither shall the jug of shemen fail, until the Yom that Hashem sendeth geshem upon ha'adamah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:17; MARKOS 12:43; MATTITYAHU 15:28"> And she went and did according to the Devar of Eliyahu; and she, and he, and her bais, did eat &#91;many&#93; yamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:28–30; YOCHANAN 4:50–51; LUKAS 1:37"> And the pot of meal was not used up, neither did the jug of shemen fail, according to the Devar Hashem, which he spoke by Eliyahu.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7; DANIEL 5:23; YOCHANAN 11:14"> And it came to pass after these things, that the ben haisha the ba'alat habais, became choleh; and his sickness was so severe, that there was no nashamah left in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKAS 5:8; YOCHANAN 2:4; MELACHIM BAIS 3:13"> And she said unto Eliyahu, Mah li valach (what have I to do with thee), Ish HaElohim? Art thou come unto me to remind me of my avon, and to slay beni?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:32; MELACHIM BAIS 4:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:37"> And he said unto her, Give me thy ben. And he took him out of her kheyk, and carried him up into the aliyyah (upper room), where he abode, and laid him upon his own mittah (bed).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:15–18; MATTITYAHU 21:22; YEHOSHUA 7:8–9"> And he cried out unto Hashem, and said, Hashem Elohai, hast thou even brought evil upon the almanah with whom I sojourn, by slaying her ben?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:40; MELACHIM BAIS 4:33–35"> And he stretched himself upon the yeled shalosh pe'amim, and cried out unto Hashem, and said, Hashem Elohai, let this yeledʼs nefesh return to him again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:39; HISGALUS 11:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:35"> And Hashem heard the voice of Eliyahu; and the nefesh of the yeled returned to him again, and he came alive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:36–37; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:41; LUKAS 7:15"> And Eliyahu took the yeled, and brought him down out of the aliyyah into the bais, and delivered him unto his em; and Eliyahu said, Look! Thy ben liveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:30; YOCHANAN 2:11; YOCHANAN 3:2"> And the isha said to Eliyahu, Now by this I have da'as that thou art an Ish Elohim, and that the Devar Hashem in thy mouth is emes.</span>
</div>
