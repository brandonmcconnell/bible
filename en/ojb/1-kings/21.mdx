<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHOFETIM 6:33; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:22; YEHOSHUA 19:18"> And it came to pass after these things, that Navot the Yizre'eli had a kerem (vineyard), which was in Yizre'el (Jezreel), beside the heikhal (palace) Ach'av Melech Shomron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 8:14; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:14–15; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:9"> And Ach'av spoke unto Navot, saying, Give me thy kerem (vineyard), that I may have it for an herb gan (garden), because it is karov (near) unto my Bais; and I will give thee in exchange for it a tov kerem (better vineyard) than it; or, if it seem tov in thine eyes, I will give thee the worth of it in kesef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 36:7; VAYIKRA 25:23; YECHEZKEL 46:18"> And Navot said to Ach'av, Chalilah li me'Hashem (far be it from me before Hashem), that I should give the nachalat Avoti unto thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 20:43; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:14; YONAH 4:9"> And Ach'av came into his Bais sar veza'ef (sullen and dejected) because of the davar which Navot the Yizre'eli had spoken to him; for he had said, I will not give thee the nachalat avoti. And he lay down upon his mittah, and turned away his face, and would eat no lechem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 16:31; BERESHIS 3:6; MELACHIM ALEF 19:2"> But Izevel his isha came to him, and said unto him, Mah zeh (What &#91;is&#93; this?) Thy ruach is sarah (sullen), that thou eatest no lechem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ESTHER 5:9–14; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:2–7; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:9–10"> And he said unto her, Because I spoke unto Navot the Yizre'eli and said unto him, Give me thy kerem (vineyard) for kesef; or else, if it please thee, I will give thee another kerem (vineyard) in its place; but he answered, I will not give thee my kerem (vineyard).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 8:14; MICHOH 7:3; SHMUEL BAIS 13:4"> And Izevel his isha said unto him, What a grand sovereign over Yisroel you are! Arise, and eat lechem, and let thine lev be cheerful; I will give thee the kerem (vineyard) of Navot the Yizre'eli.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 6:5; MELACHIM BAIS 10:11; EZRA 4:7–8"> So she wrote sefarim (letters) b'shem Ach'av, and sealed them with his chotam (seal), and sent the sefarim unto the zekenim and to the chorim (nobles, rulers) that were in his city, dwelling with Navot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YESHAYAH 58:4; MATTITYAHU 23:13; LUKAS 20:47"> And she wrote in the sefarim, saying, Proclaim a tzom, and set Navot on high among the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:11; VAYIKRA 24:15–16"> And seat two men, bnei beliyaal, opposite him, to bear witness against him, saying, Thou didst upon Elohim and Melech make a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="blasphemous">a</a></sup>brocha! And then take him out, and stone him, that he may die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:19; VAYIKRA 19:15; MELACHIM BAIS 10:6–7"> And the anashim of his city, even the zekenim and the chorim (nobles) who were the inhabitants in his city, did just as Izevel had sent unto them, and just as it was written in the sefarim which she had sent unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 58:4; MELACHIM ALEF 21:8–10"> They proclaimed a tzom, and seated Navot on high among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 9:26; DEVARIM 22:24; KOHELET 10:20"> And there came in two anashim, bnei beliyaal, and sat opposite him; and the anashim of beliyaal witnessed against him, even against Navot, in the presence of the people, saying, Navot blasphemed Elohim vaMelech! Then they took him forth outside of the Ir, and stoned him with stones, that he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="KOHELET 8:14; SHMUEL BAIS 11:14–24; KOHELET 5:8"> Then they sent to Izevel, saying, Navot is stoned, and is dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MISHLE 1:10–16; MISHLE 4:17; MELACHIM ALEF 21:7"> And it came to pass, when Izevel heard that Navot was stoned, and was dead, that Izevel said to Ach'av, Arise, take possession of the kerem of Navot the Yizre'eli, which he refused to give thee for kesef; for Navot is not chai (alive), but met (dead).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="OVADYAH 1:12–14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:32; SHMUEL BAIS 11:25–27"> And it came to pass, when Ach'av heard that Navot was dead, that Ach'av rose up to go down to the kerem of Navot the Yizre'eli, to take possession of it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TEHILLIM 9:12; YESHAYAH 26:21; MELACHIM BAIS 5:26"> And the Devar Hashem came to Eliyahu the Tishbi, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 13:32; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 22:9"> Arise, go down to meet Ach'av Melech Yisroel, which is in Shomron; hinei, he is in the kerem of Navot, where he has gone down to take<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="inheritance">b</a></sup>possession of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:38; SHMUEL BAIS 12:9; CHABAKUK 2:9"> And thou shalt speak unto him, saying, Thus saith Hashem, Hast thou committed murder, then also hast thou inherited? And thou shalt speak unto him, saying, Thus saith Hashem, In the makom (place) where kelavim licked the dahm Navot shall kelavim lick thy dahm also, even thine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 17:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:14; MELACHIM ALEF 21:25"> And Ach'av said to Eliyahu, Hast thou found me, O mine oyev? And he answered, I have found thee; because thou hast sold thyself to work rah in front of the eyes of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 14:10; SHMUEL ALEF 25:34; MELACHIM BAIS 10:1–7"> Hineni, I will bring ra'ah upon thee, and will utterly sweep thee away, and will cut off from Ach'av mashtin b'kir (him that urinates against a wall),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i. e., all males">c</a></sup>atzur (bond) and azuv (free) in Yisroel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 16:3; MELACHIM ALEF 14:16; MELACHIM ALEF 16:11"> And will make thine Bais like the Bais Yarov'am Ben Nevat, and like the Bais Ba'asha Ben Achiyah, for the provocation wherewith thou hast provoked Me to anger, and caused Yisroel to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 9:10; MELACHIM BAIS 9:30–37; MELACHIM ALEF 21:25"> And of Izevel also spoke Hashem, saying, The kelavim shall devour Izevel by the wall of Yizre'el.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 16:4; MELACHIM ALEF 14:11; YIRMEYAH 15:3"> Him of Ach'av that dieth in the Ir the kelavim shall devour; and him that dieth in the sadeh shall the oph haShomayim devour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MISHLE 22:14; MELACHIM ALEF 21:20; MELACHIM ALEF 16:30–33"> But there was none like unto Ach'av, which did sell himself to work rah before the eyes of Hashem, whom Izevel his isha urged on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 21:11; BERESHIS 15:16; VAYIKRA 18:25–30"> And he acted monstrously in going after gillulim (idols), according to all things as did HaEmori, whom Hashem drove out before the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 6:30; BERESHIS 37:34; SHMUEL BAIS 3:31"> And it came to pass, when Ach'av heard those words, that he tore his begadim, put sackcloth upon his basar, did a tzom, lay in sackcloth, and walked about subdued.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the Devar Hashem came to Eliyahu the Tishbi, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; MICHOH 7:18; TEHILLIM 86:15"> Seest thou how Ach'av humbleth himself before Me? because he humbleth himself before Me, I will not bring hara'ah in his days: but in the yamim bno will I bring ra'ah (disaster) upon his Bais.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See 2Kgs 9:23-26">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
