<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:24; MELACHIM ALEF 11:1; MELACHIM ALEF 6:1–15"> And Sh'lomo made an alliance with Pharaoh Melech Mitzrayim, and took Bat Pharaoh as isha, and brought her to Ir Dovid, until he had completed building his Bais (palace), and also the Beis Hashem, and the surrounding chomat Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:43; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:17; DEVARIM 12:2–5"> However HaAm were sacrificing in the high places, because there was no Bayit built unto the Shem of Hashem in those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:3; TEHILLIM 31:23; DEVARIM 30:16"> And Sh'lomo loved Hashem, walking in the chukkot Dovid Aviv; however he was sacrificing and burning ketoret at the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 16:39; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 1:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:5"> And HaMelech went to Giv'on to sacrifice there; for that was the bamah hagedolah (the great high place); a thousand olot did Sh'lomo offer upon that mizbe'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:2; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5–6; BAMIDBAR 12:6"> In Giv'on Hashem appeared to Sh'lomo in a chalom halailah; Elohim said, Ask what I should give thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:4; MELACHIM ALEF 1:48; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:11"> And Sh'lomo said, Thou hast showed unto Thy eved Dovid Avi chesed gadol (great mercy), according as he walked before Thee in emes, and in tzedakah, and in yesharah levav (uprightness of heart) with Thee; and Thou hast been shomer to preserve this chesed hagadol for him, that Thou hast given him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Dovid">a</a></sup>ben to sit on his kisse, as is this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:1; BAMIDBAR 27:17; YIRMEYAH 1:6"> And now, Hashem Elohai, Thou hast made Thy eved Melech in the place of Dovid Avi; and I am but a na'ar katon (little child); I know not how to go out or come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:5; BERESHIS 13:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:2"> And Thy eved is in the midst of Thy people which Thou hast chosen, an Am Rav, that cannot be numbered nor counted for multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5; MISHLE 16:16; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:14"> Give therefore Thy eved a lev shome'a (discerning heart, mind) to govern Thy people, that I may discern between tov and rav; for who is sufficient to judge this Thy so great a people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MISHLE 15:8"> And the davar was pleasing in the eyes of Adonoi, that Sh'lomo had made request of this davar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:26; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:2–3; MISHLE 16:31"> And Elohim said unto him, Because thou hast made request of this davar, and hast not requested for thyself yamim rabbim; neither hast requested osher for thyself, nor hast requested the nefesh of thine oyevim; but hast requested for thyself binah to comprehend mishpat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:14–15; KOHELET 1:16; MATTITYAHU 12:42"> Hinei, I have done according to thy devarim; hinei, I have given thee a discerning lev chacham; so that there was none like thee before thee, neither after thee shall any arise like unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:20; MATTITYAHU 6:33; MISHLE 3:16"> And I have also given thee that which thou hast not requested, both osher, and kavod; so that there shall not be any among the Melachim like unto thee all thy yamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MISHLE 3:2; TEHILLIM 91:16; MELACHIM ALEF 15:5"> And if thou wilt walk in My derakhim, to be shomer over My chukkot and My mitzvot, as Dovid Avicha did walk, then I will lengthen thy yamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:7; MELACHIM ALEF 8:65; ESTHER 1:3"> And Sh'lomo awoke; and, hinei, it was a chalom! And he returned to Yerushalayim, and stood before the Aron Brit Adonoi, and offered up olot, and offered shelamim, and made a mishteh (feast) for all his avadim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 18:16; SHOFETIM 11:1; BAMIDBAR 27:2"> Then came there two nashim, that were zonot (harlots, prostitutes) unto HaMelech, and stood before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:7; BERESHIS 43:20"> And the one isha said, O adoni, I and this isha dwell in one bayit; and I was delivered of child with her babayit (in the house).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And it came to pass the Yom HaShelishi after that I was delivered, that this isha was delivered also: and we were together; there was no zar (stranger, outsider) with us babayit, except we two babayit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And this ben haisha died in the lailah; because she had lain upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:20; MATTITYAHU 13:25; TEHILLIM 139:11"> And she arose in the middle of the lailah, and took beni (my son) from beside me, while thine amah (handmaid) slept, and laid it in her kheyk (bosom), and laid her ben hamet (dead son) in my kheyk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 21:7; SHMUEL ALEF 1:23; EKHAH 4:3–4"> And when I rose in the boker to nurse beni, hinei, met! (dead!) But when I had examined him in the boker, hinei, he was not beni I bore!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 3:23–24"> And the isha ha'acheret (other woman) said, Lo (no); but hachai (the living) is beni (my son), and hamet (the dead) is the ben of yours. But this one said, Lo (no), the ben of yours is hamet (the dead &#91;one&#93;), and beni is hachai! Thus they went on arguing before HaMelech!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Then said HaMelech, The one saith, This is beni (my son) hachai (the living &#91;one&#93;), and thy ben is hamet (the dead &#91;one&#93;); and the other saith, Lo; but the ben of yours is hamet, and beni is hachai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And HaMelech said, Bring me a cherev. And they brought the cherev before HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MISHLE 25:8"> And HaMelech said, Cut the yeled hachai in two, and give half to the one, and half to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YESHAYAH 49:15; YIRMEYAH 31:20; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17"> Then spoke unto HaMelech the isha whose was the ben hachai, for her rachamim yearned for her ben, and she said, O adoni, give her the yeled hachai, and in no wise put him to death. But the other said, Let it be neither mine nor thine, but cut him in two!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Then HaMelech answered and said, Give her the yeled hachai, and in no wise put him to death; she is immo (his mother).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DANIEL 2:47; MELACHIM ALEF 3:9–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:3"> And kol Yisroel heard of the mishpat (judgment) in which HaMelech had given verdict; and they feared HaMelech; for they saw that the chochmat Elohim was within him, to administer mishpat (justice).</span>
