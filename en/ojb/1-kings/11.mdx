<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEVARIM 17:17; MISHLE 6:24; NECHEMYAH 13:23–27"> But Sh'lomo Hamelech loved nashim nokhriyyot rabbot (many foreign women), besides Bat Pharaoh: Moaviyyot, Ammoniyyot, Adomiyyot, Tzedeniyyot, Chittiyyot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:16; DEVARIM 7:3–4; YEHOSHUA 23:12–13"> Of the Goyim concerning which Hashem said unto the Bnei Yisroel, Ye shall not go in to them, neither shall they come in unto you; for surely they will turn away your levav after their elohim; Sh'lomo made his deveykus unto these in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="KOHELET 7:28; SHMUEL BAIS 5:13–16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:21"> And he had nashim sarot sheva me'ot (seven hundred royal wives), and three hundred pilagshim; and his nashim inclined libo (his heart).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:4; MELACHIM ALEF 8:61; MELACHIM ALEF 11:6"> For it came to pass, when Sh'lomo was old, that his nashim inclined libo (his heart) after elohim acherim and levavo (his heart) was not shalem (undivided, fully devoted, complete) with Hashem Elohav, as was the levav Dovid Aviv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHOFETIM 2:13; MELACHIM ALEF 11:7; MELACHIM BAIS 23:13"> For Sh'lomo went after Ashtoret elohei Tzidonim, and after Milkom shikkutz (the abomination of) Ammonim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:24; YEHOSHUA 14:8; YEHOSHUA 14:14"> And Sh'lomo did the rah in the sight of Hashem, and went not fully after Hashem, as did Dovid Aviv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:29; SHOFETIM 11:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:43"> Then did Sh'lomo build a high place for Kemosh, the shikkutz (abomination of) Moav, on the har (hill),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., Mount of Olives">a</a></sup>that is facing Yerushalayim, and for Molech, the shikkutz (abomination of) the Bnei Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:11–12; MELACHIM ALEF 11:1; YECHEZKEL 16:22–29"> And likewise did he for all his nashim nokhriyyot, which burned ketoret and offered zevakhim unto their elohim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:2; MELACHIM ALEF 3:5; DEVARIM 7:4"> And Hashem was angry with Sh'lomo, because levavo was turned from Hashem Elohei Yisroel, Who had appeared unto him twice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 6:12–13; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:17–22; MELACHIM ALEF 9:4–7"> And had commanded him concerning this thing, that he should not go after elohim acherim; but he was not shomer over that which Hashem commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:15–16; MELACHIM ALEF 11:31; MELACHIM BAIS 17:21"> Wherefore Hashem said unto Sh'lomo, Forasmuch as this is done of thee, and thou hast not been shomer over My Brit (Covenant, Sinai Covenant) and My chukkot, which I have commanded thee, I will surely tear away the Mamlachah from thee, and will give it to thy eved (servant).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="that is, Yarov'am ben Nevat, see v. 26">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 20:19; SHMUEL ALEF 9:4–5; MELACHIM BAIS 22:19–20"> Notwithstanding in thy yamim I will not do it lema'an (for the sake of) Dovid Avicha; but I will tear it out of the yad binecha (power of thy son).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:20; DEVARIM 12:11; MELACHIM ALEF 11:32"> Howbeit I will not tear away kol hamamlachah; but will give shevet echad (one tribe) to binecha le'ma'an Dovid Avdi and lema'an Yerushalayim which I have chosen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:26; TEHILLIM 89:30–34; YESHAYAH 13:17"> And Hashem raised up satan (adversary) unto Sh'lomo, Hadad HaAdomi; he was from the zera HaMelech in Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 18:12–13; DEVARIM 20:13"> For it came to pass, when Dovid was in Edom, and Yoav Sar HaTzava was gone up to bury the slain, after he had struck down every zachar in Edom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> (For six months did Yoav remain there with kol Yisroel, until he had cut off every zachar in Edom);</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 2:13–14; SHEMOT 2:1–10; MELACHIM BAIS 11:2"> That Hadad fled, he and anashim Adomiyyim (certain men of Edom) of his fatherʼs avadim with him, to go into Mitzrayim; Hadad being yet a na'ar katan (small child).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 10:12; DEVARIM 33:2; DEVARIM 1:1"> And they arose out of Midyan, and came to Paran; and they took anashim with them out of Paran, and they came to Mitzrayim, unto Pharaoh Melech Mitzrayim; who gave him a bais, and assigned him lechem, and gave him eretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BERESHIS 39:4; YIRMEYAH 43:7–9; BERESHIS 39:21"> And Hadad found chen me'od in the sight of Pharaoh, so that he gave him for an isha the achot of his own isha, the achot Tachpeneis the gevirah (royal lady, queen).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 21:7; SHMUEL ALEF 1:24"> And the achot of Tachpeneis bore him Genuvat bno, whom Tachpeneis weaned in Bais Pharaoh; and Genuvat was in Bais Pharaoh among the Bnei Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:10; SHEMOT 4:19; MATTITYAHU 2:20"> And when Hadad heard in Mitzrayim that Dovid slept with Avotav, and that Yoav Sar HaTzava was dead, Hadad said to Pharaoh, Let me depart, that I may go to mine own eretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 18:22–23; LUKAS 22:35; MARKOS 14:31"> Then Pharaoh said unto him, But what hast thou lacked with me, that, hinei, thou seekest to go to thine own eretz? And he answered, Nothing; howbeit let me go anyway.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:14; SHMUEL BAIS 8:3; SHMUEL BAIS 10:15–18"> And Elohim raised up against him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Sh'lomo">c</a></sup>another satan, Rezon ben Elyada, which fled from adonav Hadadezer Melech Tzovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 10:8; SHMUEL BAIS 10:18; MELACHIM ALEF 20:34"> And he gathered anashim unto him, and became sar gedood (leader of a band of marauders), when Dovid slaughtered them of Tzovah; they went to Damascus, dwelt therein, and reigned he there as king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:2; TEHILLIM 106:40; MELACHIM ALEF 5:4"> And he was a satan to Yisroel all the days of Sh'lomo, in addition to ra'ah that Hadad did; and he despised Yisroel, and he ruled in Aram.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 13:6; MELACHIM ALEF 12:2; MELACHIM ALEF 11:28"> Also the eved Sh'lomo Yarov'am ben Nevat, an Ephrati of the Tzeredah (Zeredah), whose shem immo was Tzeru'ah, an almanah, even he lifted up his yad against HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 9:24; MELACHIM ALEF 9:15; MISHLE 30:32"> And this is the account of his lifting up his yad against HaMelech; Sh'lomo built the Millo, and closed up the peretz of Ir Dovid Aviv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MISHLE 22:29; SHMUEL BAIS 19:20; SHOFETIM 1:22–23"> And the ish Yarov'am was a gibbor chayil; and Sh'lomo seeing the na'ar that he was industrious, he put him over all the sevel (load, burden, enforced labor) of Bais Yosef.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:15; MELACHIM ALEF 14:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:29"> And it came to pass at that time when Yarov'am went out of Yerushalayim, that HaNavi Achiyah the Shiloni found him on the derech; and he had clothed himself with a salmah chadasha (new garment, cloak); and they two were alone in the sadeh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:27–28; SHMUEL ALEF 24:4–5"> And Achiyah took hold of the salmah chadasha that was on him, and tore it into twelve pieces;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:11–12"> And he said to Yarov'am, Take for thee ten pieces; for thus saith Hashem Elohei Yisroel, Hineni, I will tear the mamlachah out of the yad Sh'lomo, and will give to thee asarah hashevatim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:13; MELACHIM ALEF 12:20–21; MELACHIM ALEF 14:21"> (But he shall have hashevet haechad l'ma'an Dovid Avdi, and l'ma'an Yerushalayim, the Ir which I have chosen out of kol shivtei Yisroel;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:9; MELACHIM ALEF 11:5–7; MELACHIM ALEF 9:5–7"> Because that they have forsaken Me, and have worshiped Ashtoret elohei Tzidonim, Kemosh elohei Moav, and Milkom elohei Bnei Ammon, and have not walked in My ways, to do that which is yashar in Mine eyes, and My chukkot and My mishpatim, as did Dovid Aviv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:12–13; MELACHIM ALEF 11:31; IYOV 11:6"> Howbeit I will not take the whole mamlachah out of his yad; but I will make him nasi all the days of his life l'ma'an Dovid Avdi, whom I chose, because he was shomer over My mitzvot and My chukkot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:20; MELACHIM ALEF 11:12; SHEMOT 20:5–6"> But I will take the mamlachah out of yad beno, and will give it unto thee, even aseret hashevatim (ten tribes).</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 8:19; MELACHIM ALEF 11:13; MELACHIM ALEF 15:4"> And unto bno will I give shevet echad (one tribe), l'ma'an Dovid Avdi may have a ner (lamp) always before Me in Yerushalayim, the Ir which I have chosen for Me to put My Shem there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DEVARIM 14:26; MELACHIM ALEF 11:26; SHMUEL BAIS 3:21"> And I will take thee, and thou shalt reign according to all that thy nefesh desireth, and shalt be Melech over Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 7:11; YEHOSHUA 1:5; DEVARIM 31:8"> And it shall be, if thou wilt pay heed unto all that I command thee, and wilt walk in My ways, and do that is yashar in My sight, to be shomer over My chukkot and My mitzvot, as Dovid Avdi did; that I will be with thee, and build thee a bais ne'eman (enduring dynasty) as I built for Dovid, and will give Yisroel unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LUKAS 1:32–33; LUKAS 2:11; YESHAYAH 11:1–10"> And I will for this afflict the zera Dovid,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Isa 53:10">d</a></sup>but not forever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 12:2–9; YESHAYAH 14:24–27; EKHAH 3:37"> Sh'lomo sought therefore to kill Yarov'am. And Yarov'am arose, and fled into Mitzrayim, unto Shishak Melech Mitzrayim, and was in Mitzrayim until the Mot Sh'lomo.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 9:29–31"> And the rest of the acts of Sh'lomo, and all that he did, and his chochmah, are they not written in the Sefer Divrei Sh'lomo?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:11"> And the time that Sh'lomo reigned in Yerushalayim over kol Yisroel was arba'im shanah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 21:18; MELACHIM ALEF 2:10; MATTITYAHU 1:7"> And Sh'lomo slept with Avotav, and was buried in the Ir Dovid Aviv: and Rechav'am bno reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
