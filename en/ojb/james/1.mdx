</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:6–8; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:9–10"> Consider it all simcha, my Achim b'Moshiach, whenever you fall into various nisayonos (tests, trials),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:3–4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:36; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:11"> Because you have da'as that the emunah you have, when it is tested, produces savlanut (patient endurance).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKAS 21:19; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:10; MATTITYAHU 5:48"> And let savlanut be shleimah in its po'al (work) in order that you may be mevugarim (mature, grown up) and complete, lacking in nothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MISHLE 2:3–6; YOCHANAN 15:7; MISHLE 3:5–7"> But if any one of you is lacking chochmah (wisdom), let him direct tefillah (prayer) and techinot (petitions) to Hashem, the One whose matanot (gifts) are given generously and without grudging, and chochmah will be given to him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1Kgs 3:9,10; Prov 2:3-6; Ps 51:6; Dan 1:17; 2:21">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:21–22; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:6; MARKOS 11:22–24"> But let the tefillah be offered with much bitachon in emunah (faith), in no way doubting. For the doubtful man, wavering in emunah, is like a wave of the yam (sea), being tossed by the wind.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1Kgs 18:21">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:3; YESHAYAH 58:3–4; MISHLE 21:27"> Let not such a one presume that he will receive anything from Adoneinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:21; MATTITYAHU 6:24; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:8"> He is an ish (man) of double mind, in all his drakhim (ways), mesupak (uncertain, having doubts) and unstable.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ps 119:113">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 9:23–24; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5–6; MISHLE 19:1"> But let the Ach b'Moshiach of shiflut (lowliness) glory in the da'as that Hashem will exalt him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:24; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:17; IYOV 14:2"> And let the Ach b'Moshiach who has osher (riches) glory in his bizyoinos (humiliation), in the da'as that Hashem will bring him low, because KOL HABASAR KHATZIR<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isa 40:6,7">f</a></sup>(All flesh is grass) and so he likewise will vanish.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Job 14:2; Ps 103:15,16">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:7–8; TEHILLIM 37:35–36; TEHILLIM 49:6–14"> For the shemesh (sun) rose with its burning heat and dried the grass and its TZITZ NAVEL<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isa 40:6-8">h</a></sup>(flower blossom fell). And the beauty of its appearance perished, so also the “oisher” (rich man) in his goings will fade away.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ps 102:4,11">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:10; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:6–8; HISGALUS 2:10"> Ashrey is the one who stands up under nisayon (trial), because, having become approved, that one will be given the Ateret HaChayyim (Crown of Life), which Hashem gave as a havtachah (promise) to those having Ahavas Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="CHABAKUK 2:12–13; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:19–20"> However, let no one say, when he is tempted, “From Hashem I am being tempted,” for Hashem cannot be tempted to crave ra'ah (evil), and He Himself trips up no one with nisayon (temptation).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MARKOS 7:21–22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:22; MATTITYAHU 15:18"> But each one is tempted by his own ta'avah (lust, yetzer hara), being dragged off by it and being allured.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Prov 19:3">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 7:14; IYOV 15:35; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:12–21"> Then after her conception Ta'avah gives birth to Averah (Transgression) and Averah, once she has fully developed, gives birth to Mavet.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gn 3:6; Job 15:35; Ps 7:14; Isa 59:4">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:7; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:12"> Do not fall under a delusion, my beloved Achim b'Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:27; MATTITYAHU 7:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:8"> Every good endowment and every matanah shleimah (complete gift) is from above, coming down from Avi HaOhrot (the Father of Lights) with whom there is no variation or shadow of turning.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Ps 85:12; Gn 1:16; Ps 136:7; Dan 2:22; Num 23:19; Ps 102:27; Mal 3:6">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:29–31; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:23; YOCHANAN 3:3–5"> Birtzon Hashem (by the will of G-d), he gave birth to us by the Dvar HaEmes, that we might be a kind of bikkurim (firstfruits) of all he created.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Jer 2:3">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MISHLE 17:27; MISHLE 21:23; MISHLE 14:29"> Have da'as of this, my beloved Achim b'Moshiach. Let every man be quick to hear, slow to speak, slow to ka'as (anger).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Prov 10:19">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:24–25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:26; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17–18"> For the ka'as of Bnei Adam does not accomplish the Tzikat Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:13; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:11"> Therefore, having put away all filthiness and what remains of resha (wickedness) in shiflut (lowliness) and meekness receive the implanted Dvar Hashem which is able to save your nefashot.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Rv 3:20">o</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:13; LUKAS 6:46–48; YOCHANAN 13:17"> Now be Shomrei HaDvar Hashem and not Shomei HaDvar only, thereby causing yourselves to fall under remiyah (deceit, deception).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:26–27; YECHEZKEL 33:31–32; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:14–26"> Because if anyone is Shomei HaDavar and not Shomrei HaDavar, this one is like a man looking at his ponum in a mirror,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:11; LUKAS 1:66; LUKAS 7:39"> For he observed himself and has gone away and immediately forgot what he looked like.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKAS 11:28; YOCHANAN 13:17; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:17–18"> But the one having peered into the Torah HaShleimah (the Perfect Torah), the Torah HaCherut (the Torah of Freedom), and there remaining, not as a forgetful listener but one who is shomer mitzvot and goes into action, this one will have a bracha on his head in all his acts.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Ps 19:7">p</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:29; TEHILLIM 141:3; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:10"> If anyone considers himself to be one of the Charedim &#91;Orthodox, G-d-fearing Jewish religious ones&#93;, yet has lashon hora and does not bridle his tongue but instead causes his lev to fall under remiyah (deceit), this oneʼs chasidus (piety) is worthless.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Ps 34:13; 39:1; 141:3">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YESHAYAH 1:16–17; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17–19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:9–10"> Avodas Kodesh that is tehorah (pure) and tamimah (unblemished) before Elohim HaAv is this: to visit yetomim (orphans) and almanot (widows) in their tzoros and to be shomer against the defilement of the Olam Hazeh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Dt 14:29; Job 31:16,17,21; Ps 146:9; Isa 1:17,23">r</a></sup></span>
