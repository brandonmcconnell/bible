<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 12:47–48; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:7; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:3"> Not many of you, Achim b'Moshiach, should be Morim for Moshiach, als (since), as far as concerns Mishpat Hashem, you have da'as that we Messianic morim will have the chomer haDin (rigor of the Law) fall on us more severely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:26; MISHLE 13:3; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:10"> For all of us stumble variously. If anyone as far as lashon hora is concerned, does not stumble, this one is an ish tamim able to bridle also the entire guf (body).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1Kgs 8:46; Ps 39:1; Prov 10:19">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TEHILLIM 32:9; TEHILLIM 39:1; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:26"> And if we put bits into the mouths of susim (horses) to bring them into mishma'at (obedience), in just this way we direct their whole gufot (bodies).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="TEHILLIM 107:25–27; MATTITYAHU 8:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:14–38"> Hinei, also the oniyot (ships), even though gedolot and driven by gales, are guided by a very small rudder wherever the impulse of the one steering directs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MISHLE 18:21; YIRMEYAH 9:3–8; MISHLE 12:18"> So also the lashon (tongue), an evar katon (small member) speaks of RAVREVAN<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dan 7:8,20">b</a></sup>(boastful things). Hinei, an eish ketanah (small fire) and yet how great a forest it can set ablaze!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 12:3,4; 73:8,9">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 120:2–3; MISHLE 16:27; MARKOS 7:15"> And the lashon is an Eish, the lashon is made an Olam HaAvel (World of Iniquity) among our evarim (members), defiling with a stain kol haGuf (whole body), and setting ablaze the course of life, and is itself set by eish in Gehinnom.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Prov 16:27">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MARKOS 5:4"> For every species both of wild animals and birds, reptiles and marine creatures is tamed and has been tamed by humankind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:13; TEHILLIM 140:3; TEHILLIM 55:21"> But the Lashon no one of Bnei Adam is able to tame, an uncontrollable ra'ah (evil), full of deadly zuhamah (contamination).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 10:7; BERESHIS 1:26–27; TEHILLIM 62:4"> With this we say a bracha to Hashem, Adoneinu and Avoteinu, and with this we put a kelalah (curse) on Bnei Adam, who have been created according to the demut Elohim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gn 1:26,27f">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:14; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:3"> Out of the same PEH comes forth bracha and also kelalah. My Achim b'Moshiach, these things ought not to be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Surely not out of the same makor (fountain) pours forth mayim both sweet and bitter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKAS 6:43–44; MATTITYAHU 12:33; MATTITYAHU 7:16–20"> Surely an etz te'enah (fig tree) cannot yield olives, my Achim b'Moshiach, or a grape vine figs? Neither can salt water yield sweet water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:23; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17; MATTITYAHU 5:5"> Who has chochmah and binah among you? Let him show by his hitnahagut hatovah (good conduct) that the ma'asim of him are of the shiflut (lowliness) of chochmah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:3"> But if bitter kina (jealousy) you have and anochiyut (selfishness) in your levavot, do not boast and speak sheker against HaEmes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:19"> This is not the Chochmah coming down and descending from above, but is of the Olam Hazeh, of this world and of shedim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:33"> For where kina and anochiyut are, there is tohu vavohu (disorder, chaos) and every ra'ah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:22–23; MISHLE 2:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:4–7"> But the chochmah from above is berishonah (in the first place) tehorah (pure), then ohevet shalom (peace-loving), then eidel (gentle) and considerate, then full of rachamim and p'ri tov, and without maso panim and tzevi'ut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:9; HOSHEA 10:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:11"> And the p'ri haTzedek is shalom sown by the ones making shalom.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Prov 11:18; Isa 32:17; Hos 10:12">f</a></sup></span>
