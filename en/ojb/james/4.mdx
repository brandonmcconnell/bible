<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:23"> From where do milchamot (wars) come? What is their goirem (driving force)? And from where come fights among you? Is it not from here, from the ta'avot (lusts) of you warring in your evarim (members)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:24; MATTITYAHU 7:7–8; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5"> You lust for something and you do not have it; you kill and you envy and you are not able to obtain. You get involved in machalokot (divisions of dissensions) and fights. You do not have because you fail to daven with your request.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:14; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:22; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:6–7"> Or you make techinot (petitions) and you do not receive, because you ask wrongly, that on your ta'avot (lusts) you may spend what you receive.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ps 18:41; 66:18">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:7; YOCHANAN 15:19"> No'efot (adulterers)! Do you not have da'as that to have shaichus (closeness, friendship, intimacy) with the Olam Hazeh is eyvah im Hashem(enmity with G-d)? Therefore, whoever chooses to make the Olam Hazeh his Oihev is made an Oyev (Enemy) of Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Isa 54:4; Jer 3:20; Hos 2:2-5; 3:1; 9:1">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:19; BAMIDBAR 11:29"> Or do you think that in vain the Kitvei Hakodesh attests that Hashem yearns jealously over the Ruach Hakodesh He causes to dwell in us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:12; MISHLE 3:34; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:5"> But He gives all the more Chen v'Chesed! Therefore it says, Hashem LALETZIM HU YALITZ V'LA'ANAYIM YITEN CHEN<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Prov 3:34">c</a></sup>(Hashem opposes the proud mocker but gives grace to the humble).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:8–9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:11–12"> Submit yourselves in mishma'at (obedience) to Hashem. Resist HaSatan, and he will flee from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 145:18; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:22; MALACHI 3:7"> Draw near to Hashem and Hashem will draw near to you. Cleanse your yadayim (hands), you chote'im (sinners)! And purify your levavot, you anashim of double mind!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ps 73:28; Zech 1:3; Mal 3:7; Isa 1:16; Ps 24:4; 119:113; Jer 4:14">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:10–11; MATTITYAHU 5:4; LUKAS 6:25"> Lament and mourn and weep. Let the tzechok (laughter) of you be changed to avelut (mourning), and the simcha of you be turned to tugah (sadness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 147:6; MATTITYAHU 23:12; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:6"> Be humbled before Hashem and He will exalt you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Job 5:11">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:1; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:9"> Do not speak lashon hora against an Ach b'Moshiach. The one speaking against an Ach b'Moshiach or setting himself up as a shofet (judge) of his Ach b'Moshiach speaks against the Torah and sets himself up as shofet of the Torah. Now if the Torah you judge, you are not Shomrei HaTorah but a shofet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:1; MATTITYAHU 10:28"> One is the Mekhokek (Law-Giver) and HaShofet (The Judge), the One who is able to save and to destroy. But who are you, the one who has set yourself up as the shofet of your re'a (neighbor)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MISHLE 27:1; LUKAS 12:17–20; BERESHIS 11:3–4"> Come now, you who say, “Hayom (today) or makhar (tomorrow) we will go into this or that city and we will do business there a year and will sell and make a revach (profit).”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:24; TEHILLIM 39:5; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:17"> Yet you do not even have da'as of what tomorrowʼs “yom” may bring. Look at your life! Are you not an ed (mist), appearing a short time, then indeed disappearing?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Job 7:7; Ps 39:5; 102:3; 144:4; Isa 2:22">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MISHLE 19:21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:3; EKHAH 3:37"> Instead of this, you ought to say “Im yirtzeh Hashem” (if the L-rd wills) “we will live, also we will do this or that.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MISHLE 27:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:6; TEHILLIM 52:7"> But now you boast in your pretensions. All such ravrevanut (boastfulness) is ra'ah (evil, wickedness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:41; YOCHANAN 13:17; YOCHANAN 15:22"> To the one having da'as, therefore, knowing to do tov and not doing it, to him it is chet.</span>
