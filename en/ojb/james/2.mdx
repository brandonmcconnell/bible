<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MISHLE 24:23; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9; VAYIKRA 19:15"> My Achim b'Moshiach, you do not with your acts of maso panim (favoritism) hold to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="orthodox Jewish">a</a></sup>emunah of the glorious Adoneinu Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dt 1:17; Lv 19:15; Prov 24:23">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKAS 15:22; ESTHER 3:10; ESTHER 8:2"> For if there enter into your Beit HaKnesset (House of Assembly, shul, synagogue, shtibel) a man with gold rings on his fingers in expensive bekeshe (kaftan) and shtreimel, and there enters also an underpriviledged nebach, a kabtzen (poor person) in shmattes (rags),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:9; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:6; LUKAS 7:44–46"> and you pay special attention to the takif &#91;influential man&#93; wearing the bekeshe and shtreimel and say, “You sit here in the seat of kibbud” (respect, honor), and to the kabtzen (pauper) you say, “You stand there.” Or “You sit at my feet,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:24; IYOV 34:19; MATTITYAHU 7:1–5"> did you not among yourselves differentiate with prejudice and became shofetim (judges) with machshavot re'sha (evil thoughts)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:3; LUKAS 6:20; HISGALUS 2:9"> Hinei! My beloved Achim b'Moshiach, did not Der Oybershter make the Aniyim of the Olam Hazeh in fact Bechirim of Hashem to be rich in emunah and also yoreshim of the Malchut Hashem, which Adoshem gave as a havtachah (promise) to those with Ahavas Hashem?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Job 34:19">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MISHLE 17:5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:22; MISHLE 14:31"> But you dishonored the ish evyon (poor man, pauper). Do not the oishirim (rich ones) oppress you and they drag you into the Batei Din (Bet Din courts)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:9–11; HISGALUS 13:5–6; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:13"> Do they not commit Chillul Hashem gidduf (blasphemy) against the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiachʼs Shem Tov that has been named upon you?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:14; MATTITYAHU 22:39"> If indeed you are shomer regarding the Dat HaMalkhut (Royal Decree), as it is written in the Kitvei Hakodesh, V'AHAVTAH L'REI'ACHA KAMOCHA<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Lv 19:18">d</a></sup>(And thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself) you do well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:15; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1–4; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:4"> But if you show maso panim (favoritism), you are chote'im (sinners) committing averos (transgressions) against the Torah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dt 1:17">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:10; MATTITYAHU 5:18–19; DEVARIM 27:26"> For whoever is shomer over kol haTorah but stumbles in one mitzvah, such is condemned as ashem (guilty) of averoh (transgression) of kol mitzvot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:13–14; MATTITYAHU 5:21–28; DEVARIM 5:17–18"> For the One having said, LO TINAF (You shall not commit adultery) said also LO TIRTZACH (You shall not murder). Now if you do not commit adultery but you do murder, you have become a Poshei'a al mitzvot HaTorah (Transgressor of the Torah).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ex 20:13,14; Dt 5:17,18">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:25; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:8; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:4–8"> So let your devarim (words) be and so let your ma'asim (deeds) be as those who are about to come under the judgment of the Torah HaCherut.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="1:25">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:7; MATTITYAHU 6:15; MISHLE 21:13"> For the Din (Judgment) will be without rachamim (mercy) to the one not having shown rachamim. Rachamim wins the nitzachon (victory) over HaDin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:26–27; MATTITYAHU 7:21–23; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:22–25"> What is the revach (gain, profit), my Achim b'Moshiach, if anyone claims to have emunah but does not have ma'asim (deeds)? Surely not such “emunah” is able to bring him to Yeshu'at Eloheinu?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 3:11; MATTITYAHU 25:35–40; YESHAYAH 58:7"> If an Ach b'Moshiach or an Achot b'Moshiach is dressed in shmattes (tatters) and lacking “lechem chukeinu”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Mt 6:11">h</a></sup>(our daily bread),</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MISHLE 3:27–28; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16–18; MATTITYAHU 25:42–45"> and anyone of you says to them, “Go in shalom! Be warmed and fed!” but you do not give to them the physical necessities, what is the revach (profit)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:26; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5–9; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:19–20"> So also Emunah, if alongside it there is not in its company Ma'asim, is by itself niftar (deceased, dead).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:13; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:6"> But someone will say, “You have emunah and I have ma'asim.” You make known to me the Hisgalus haSod (the revelation of the mystery) of your emunah without your ma'asim, and I'll show you, Chaver, from my ma'asim, the Emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKAS 4:34; MARKOS 1:24; MATTITYAHU 8:29"> So you're impressed with yourselves that with your emunah you can recite the kri'at Shema, nu? O you do so well… why, even the shedim have your da'as and emunah! But they shudder!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Dt 6:4">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:17; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:26; MISHLE 12:11"> Are you willing to have da'as, O hollow man, that “Emunah”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="of dead orthodoxy">j</a></sup>unharnessed to Ma'asim, stands idle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 22:16–18; BERESHIS 22:9–12; YOCHANAN 8:39"> Avraham Avinu, was he not YITZDAK IM HASHEM (justified with G-d) by his ma'asim when he performed the akedah (binding) and offered up Yitzchak Bno (Isaac his son) upon the mizbe'ach?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gn 22:9,12">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:17–19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:6"> Hinei! While Avraham Avinuʼs Emunah was working, working right alongside was Avraham Avinuʼs Ma'asim, and by Ma'asim the emunah was made shleimah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:6; YESHAYAH 41:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:7"> And the Kitvei Hakodesh was fulfilled, Avraham Avinu V'HE'EMIN B'HASHEM V'YACHSHEVEHA LO TZEDAKAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gn 15:6">l</a></sup>(believed Hashem and it was accounted to him for righteousness). He was even called “Ohev Hashem”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gn 15:6; Isa 41:8; 2Chr 20:7">m</a></sup>(“Friend of G-d”).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:21–22; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–18; TEHILLIM 60:12"> You see that from Ma'asim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="of Emunah">n</a></sup>a man is YITZDAK IM HASHEM and not from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="sterilely unpartnered">o</a></sup>“Emunah” alone.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="i.e., mere intellectual assent">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:31; YEHOSHUA 6:22–25; YEHOSHUA 2:19–21"> And likewise also Rachav the Zonah — was she not made YITZDAK IM HASHEM from Ma'asim, having received the messengers and having sent them out a different way?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:17; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:14"> For just as the guf (body) without the neshamah is niftar (deceased, dead), so also is Emunah without Ma'asim.</span>
</div>
