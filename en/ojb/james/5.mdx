<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:19–20; IYOV 13:28; LUKAS 12:33"> The osher of you has rotted and your malbush has become moth-eaten.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Job 13:28; Ps 39:11; Isa 50:9">b</a></sup></span>

You stored up otzar (treasure) in the Acharit Hayamim!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:13; YIRMEYAH 22:13; DEVARIM 24:14–15"> Hinei, the wages of the po'alim (workers) who cut your fields, the wages you fraudulently withheld, those wages cry out, and the cries of the harvesting po'alim have reached the ears of Adonoi Tz'vaot.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Lv 19:13; Jer 22:13; Mal 3:5; Dt 24:15">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:6; LUKAS 16:25; LUKAS 16:19"> You lived in indulgence upon ha'aretz and lolled in a life of luxury, you fattened your levavot as in a Yom Tivchah (Day of Slaughter).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Jer 12:3; 25:34; Isa 53:7">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:22–23; YOCHANAN 19:9–11; YESHAYAH 53:7"> You condemned, you killed the tzaddik, who does not resist you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:1–3; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:8–9; YOEL 2:23"> Have savlanut (patience), therefore, Achim b'Moshiach, until the Bias HaMoshiach, the Coming of Moshiach Adoneinu. Hinei, the ikar (farmer) awaits the precious p'ri haAdamah (fruit of the earth), having savlanut (patience) for it until it receives the Yoreh (first autumn rain) and the Malkosh (spring rain).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dt 11:14; Jer 5:24; Yoel 2:23">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:22; TEHILLIM 37:7"> You must also have savlanut. Strengthen your levavot, because the Bias HaMoshiach, the Coming of Moshiach Adoneinu, has drawn near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:5; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:11"> Do not murmur, Achim b'Moshiach, against one another, lest you be judged. Hinei, haShofet is standing before the delet!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ex 15:24; 16:2; 17:3; Num 14:2,29; 17:6; Ps 94:2">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:32–38; MATTITYAHU 5:11–12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:7"> Achim b'Moshiach, take as an example, of yissurim (suffering) and of savlanut (patience) the Nevi'im who spoke b'Shem Adonoi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; SHEMOT 34:6; IYOV 1:21–22"> Hinei, we call me'ashirim the ones having endured: the enduring orech ruach (patience) of Iyov you heard of, and the toitzaa (outcome) from Hashem you saw, that Eloheinu is full of rachamim and channun Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Job 1:21,22; 2:10; 42:10,12-17; Ex 34:6; Num 14:18; Ps 103:8">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:33–37; MATTITYAHU 23:16–22; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:17–20"> But, above all, my Achim b'Moshiach, do not swear shevuot (oaths) neither by Shomayim nor by ha'aretz nor any other shevu'ah, but let your “ken” be “ken,” and your “lo” be “lo,” for fear that you fall under HaDin (the Judgment).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TEHILLIM 50:15; YONAH 2:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:24–25"> If anyone is suffering among you, let him daven. If anyone has simcha, let him sing niggunim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ps 50:15">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MARKOS 6:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:8; MARKOS 16:18"> Are there any cholim (sick ones) among you? Let the choleh (sick person) summon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="for Bikkur Cholim (Visiting the Sick)">i</a></sup>the Ziknei HaKehillah (Elders of the Congregation) and let them daven tefillos over him, having applied the shemen mishchah (anointing oil), b'Shem Adoneinu.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ps 23:5; Isa 1:6">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:14–16; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:13; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:16"> And the tefillah of emunah will deliver the choleh (sick person), and Hashem will raise him up. And if he may have been committing peysha'im, he will be given selicha (forgiveness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 29:12–13; MISHLE 15:29; MATTITYAHU 21:22"> Therefore, make vidduy (confession of sin) to one another, and daven tefillos on behalf of one another, so that you may have refuah sheleimah (complete healing). The tefillah of a tzaddik is powerful and effective.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 17:1; LUKAS 4:25; HISGALUS 11:6"> Eliyahu &#91;HaNavi&#93; was a man of like nature to us, and with tefillah he davened for it not to rain, and it did not rain upon ha'aretz for shalosh shanim and shishah chodashim (three years and six months).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="1Kgs 17:1">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:42–45; YIRMEYAH 14:22; MELACHIM ALEF 18:18"> And again Eliyahu &#91;HaNavi&#93; davened, and Shomayim gave GESHEM (rain) and ha'aretz caused its p'ri to sprout.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="1Kgs 18:45">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:1; MATTITYAHU 18:15; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:20"> My Achim b'Moshiach, if anyone among you wanders vait (astray) from HaEmes and someone turns a choteh (sinner) to become a ba'al teshuva,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:8; MISHLE 10:12; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:16"> You should have da'as that the one having helped a choteh (sinner) to become a ba'al teshuva and to turn from the toyus (error) of his derech, and from setiyah HaDerech Hashem(turning aside or deviating from the Way of Hashem), will save the neshamah of him from mavet and will cover a multitude of chatta'im.</span>
