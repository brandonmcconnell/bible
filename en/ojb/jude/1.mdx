<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:23; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9"> From Yehuda, eved (servant) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua/Yeshua and brother of Ya'akov;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Mt 13:55">a</a></sup>To HaKeru'im (the Called Ones), beloved in Elokim HaAv and HaShemurim (the Guarded ones) for Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:2; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:2; HISGALUS 1:4–6"> Rachamim Hashem and Shalom Hashem and Ahavas Hashem to you abundantly!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:27; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:20"> Chaverim, although I was very eager to write to you an iggeret hakodesh concerning the Geulah (Redemption) we share, it suddenly became necessary to write you an urgent appeal to fight for the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="correct Orthodox Jewish">b</a></sup>Emunah (Faith, the Emunah of the true Dat HaYehudit), which was once for all time handed over and transmitted to the Kedoshim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1–3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:4; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:10"> For certain men, anshei resha (men of wickedness), written down long ago<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="in the sfarim of Hashem">c</a></sup>for this harsha'ah (condemnation),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Dan 7:10; Rv 20:12; 1K 2:8">d</a></sup>have infiltrated our ranks, men twisting the Chen v'Chesed HaElokeinu into a license for sensual self-indulgence and denying our only Ribboneinu and Adoneinu Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:64–65; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:16–2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:1–12"> Now I wish to remind you, though you are fully informed, that Hashem, Who once saved a people from Eretz Mitzrayim (Egypt), afterwards destroyed every Apikoros not having Emunah (Faith).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:4; MATTITYAHU 25:41; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:12"> And you have da'as about the malachim (angels), not being content to keep their positions of Memshalah (Rule, Dominion), having deserted their own station, these malachim Hashem has kept under sharsherot (chains) oif eibik (forever), under choshech<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See 2K 2:4">e</a></sup>for the Mishpat HaYom HaGadol, the Judgment of the Great Day.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="the Yom HaDin">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 29:23; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:26–27"> As S'dom and Amora and the cities around them, in a doime (similar) manner to these, indulging in gilui arayot (sexual immorality) against the course of nature and turning aside and going after alien flesh, are set forth to lie in public view as a mofet (example), undergoing the onesh of Eish Olam<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 66:24; Mt 25:46; Rv 20:10">g</a></sup>(Eternal Fire).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:17; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:17; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:10–12"> Likewise, indeed, these &#91;“certain men,” 1:4&#93;, these dreamers, on the one hand, pollute the basar (flesh); on the other hand, they do not recognize HaMemshalah (rule) of glorious heavenly beings; instead, they speak lashon hora with Chillul Hashem railing against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 3:2; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:11; DEVARIM 34:6"> By contrast, Mikha'el the Sar HaMalachim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Dan 10:13">h</a></sup>when he took issue with HaSatan and disputed about the geviyyat Moshe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Rabbeinu">i</a></sup>(body of Moses), did not presume to pronounce a slanderous judgment &#91;against HaSatan&#93;, but said, “Hashem rebuke you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:21–22"> But, on the one hand, these &#91;men&#93; slander whatever they do not understand; on the other hand, what they do have binah of instinctively, like unreasoning animals, by these things they are destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:12; HISGALUS 2:14"> Oy to them! Because they walked in the Derech Kayin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gn 4:3-8">j</a></sup>and to the Toyus Bil'am<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ex 31:16">k</a></sup>(Error of Balaam) they surrendered for revach (gain), and in the mered (rebellion) of Korach against HaMemshalah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Ex 16:1-35">l</a></sup>they were destroyed!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:17–20; MATTITYAHU 15:13; YECHEZKEL 34:8"> These &#91;men&#93; are hidden reefs &#91;moored at&#93; your seudas (agape-feasts), feasting with you without yires-hakoved, shepherding themselves, waterless clouds being carried away by winds, autumn trees without fruit, twice dead, torn up from their shorashim (roots),</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:19; YESHAYAH 57:20"> Wild waves of the sea, foaming up their ma'asim hara'im (evil deeds), wandering kokhavim (stars) for whom the shvartz gloom of choshech has been reserved oif eibik (forever).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEVARIM 33:2; TEHILLIM 50:3–5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:5–6"> But also it was about these &#91;men&#93; that Chanokh, in the seventh &#91;generation&#93; from Adam, prophesied, saying, “Hinei, Adonoi comes with His ten thousands of malachim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:16; KOHELET 11:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:5"> For the purpose of bringing all to Mishpat and for the purpose of proving guilty every neshamah of all of its deeds done without yires-hakoved and of all the harsh things chote'im (sinners) without yires-hakoved spoke against Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:18; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:14"> These &#91;men&#93; are malcontents and fault-finders, walking according to their ta'avot (lusts); their mouth speaks arrogant things, flattering for the tzil (purpose) of financial advantage.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:2; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:11"> But you, Chaverim, keep in mind the predictions<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="2K 3:3">m</a></sup>of the Shlichim of Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:3; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:3; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:1–5"> Because they were telling you that at Acharit HaYamim (the End of Days), there will be leitzim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Ps 1:1">n</a></sup>(scoffers) walking according to their ta'avot (lusts) without yires-hakoved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:14; MISHLE 18:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:9"> These &#91;men&#93; are the ones causing division, worldly men, not having the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:26–27; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:15"> But you, Chaverim, build yourselves up on what is kodesh kodeshim (most holy) of yours, your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="correct, Orthodox Jewish">o</a></sup>Emunah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="1:3, the Emunah of the true Dat HaYehudit">p</a></sup>(Faith), davening in the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:16; EKHAH 3:25–26; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:28"> Keep yourselves in the Ahavas Hashem, awaiting the rachamim of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua unto Chayyei Olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:19–20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:1; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:16–18"> To those who are wavering, show rachamim; others, deliver, snatching them out of the Eish.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Olam, Isa 66:24">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:33; HISGALUS 3:4"> On others, have rachamim mixed with yires-hakoved, hating even the undergarment defiled by corrupt human nature.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:22"> Now to the One who is able to stand shomer over you, to make you moineia (prevented) from falling, and to set you unblemished in the presence of his Shekhinah, with sasson rav (much rejoicing),</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:17; HISGALUS 5:13–14; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:10–11"> To HaElohim HaYachid Moshieinu (to the only G-d our Savior), through Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua, be HaKavod (Glory) and HaGedulah (Greatness) and HaMemshalah (Rule) and HaGevurah (Might) Mei-Olam V'ad Atah V'ad Olemei Olamim (before all time, now, and forever.) Omein.</span>
</div>
