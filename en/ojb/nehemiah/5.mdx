<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YESHAYAH 5:7; SHEMOT 22:25–27; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:6–8"> And there was a great outcry of HaAm and of their nashim against their achim the Yehudim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MALACHI 2:2; TEHILLIM 128:2–4; TEHILLIM 127:3–5"> For there were that said, We, our banim, and our banot, are rabbim (many); therefore we must get dagan (grain) for them, that we may eat, and live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:35–39; MALACHI 3:8–11; BERESHIS 47:15–25"> Some also there were that said, We have mortgaged our sadot (fields), kramim (vineyards), and batim (houses), that we might buy dagan, because of the ra'av (famine).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZRA 4:13; EZRA 4:20; DEVARIM 28:47–48"> There were also that said, We have borrowed kesef for the tax of HaMelech, and that upon our sadot and kramim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:1; VAYIKRA 25:39–43; YESHAYAH 58:7"> Yet now our basar is the same as the basar of acheinu (our brethren, countrymen), our banot as their banot; and, hinei, we bring into the bondage of slavery our banim and our banot, and some of our banot are brought unto bondage already, neither is it in our power to redeem them, for other men have our sadot and kramim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 11:8; NECHEMYAH 13:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:26"> And I was very angry when I heard their outcry and these devarim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 23:19–20; VAYIKRA 25:36; SHEMOT 22:25"> Then I consulted my own thoughts, and I rebuked the Nobles,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See 3:5">a</a></sup>and the officials, and said unto them, Ye exact massa (usury),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., seizing what is pledged against debts, whether people, land, or property">b</a></sup>every one from his brother. And I called a kehillah gedolah against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:47–49; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:11; MATTITYAHU 25:29"> And I said unto them, We as far as we have ability have redeemed acheinu (bought back our brethren) the Yehudim, which were sold unto the Goyim; and will ye even sell your brethren? Or shall they be sold back unto us? Then they were speechless, and found nothing to answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="VAYIKRA 25:36; SHMUEL BAIS 12:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:24"> Also I said, It is not tov this thing ye are doing. Ought ye not to walk in the fear of Eloheinu and end the derision of the Goyim oyveinu (our enemies)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:1; SHEMOT 22:25–27; YECHEZKEL 18:13"> I likewise, and my brethren, and my men, have allowed them to borrow kesef and dagan; let us stop now this massa (burden of debt).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 58:6; VAYIKRA 6:4–5; LUKAS 3:8"> Restore, now, to them, even today, their sadot, their kramim, their zayit groves, and their batim (houses), also the usury of the hundredth part of the kesef, and of the dagan, the tirosh (new wine), and the yitzhar (olive oil), that ye exact of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZRA 10:5; YIRMEYAH 34:8–10; MATTITYAHU 19:21–22"> Then said they, We will restore them, and will require nothing of them; so will we do as thou sayest. Then I summoned the Kohanim, and took an oath of them, that they should do according to this promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 16:36; MATTITYAHU 10:14"> Also I shook out the fold of my robe, and said, So may HaElohim shake out every man from his bais, and from that which belongs to him, that keepeth not this promise, even thus be he shaken out, and emptied. And Kol HaKahal said, Omein, and praised Hashem. And HaAm did according to this promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 13:6; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:8–9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:4–15"> Moreover, from the yom that I was appointed to be their governor in Eretz Yehudah, from the twentieth year even unto the two and thirtieth year of Artachshasta HaMelech, that is, twelve shanim, I and my brethren have not eaten the Lechem HaPechah (Allowance of the Governor).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 5:9; YESHAYAH 50:10; MISHLE 16:6"> But the former governors that had been before me placed a heavy burden on HaAm, and had taken of them lechem and yayin, in addition to the forty shekels of kesef; yea, even their assistants domineered HaAm; but so did not I, because of yirat Elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:5–6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:58"> But instead I continued in the melekhet HaChomah (work of this Wall), neither acquired we any sadeh; and all my men were gathered there unto the melachah (work).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:19; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:9–10; SHMUEL BAIS 9:7"> Moreover there were at my shulchan a hundred and fifty of the Yehudim and officials, besides those that came unto us from among the Goyim around us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 4:22–23; TEHILLIM 37:21; TEHILLIM 37:26"> Now that which was prepared daily was one shor (ox) and six choice sheep; also poultry were prepared at my expense, and every ten days skins of yayin in quantity of all kinds; yet for all this required not I the Lechem HaPechah, because the avodah was heavy upon this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 13:31; TEHILLIM 106:4; NECHEMYAH 13:14"> Remember me, Elohai, for tovah, according to all that I have done for this people.</span>
