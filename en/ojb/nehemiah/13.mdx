<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEVARIM 23:3–5; NECHEMYAH 9:3; NECHEMYAH 13:23"> On that day they read from the Sefer Moshe in the ears of HaAm; and therein was found written, that the Ammoni and the Moavi should not come into the Kehal HaElohim forever,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 23:5; YEHOSHUA 24:9–10; BAMIDBAR 23:8–11"> Because they met not the Bnei Yisroel with lechem and with mayim, but hired Balaam against them, that he should curse them; howbeit Eloheinu turned the kelalah into a brocha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 9:2; SHEMOT 12:38; BAMIDBAR 11:4"> Now it came to pass, when they had heard the Torah, that they separated from Yisroel all the foreign descent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 12:44; NECHEMYAH 6:17–18; NECHEMYAH 13:28"> And before this, Elyashiv the Kohen, having the oversight of the storeroom of the Beis Eloheinu, was allied unto Toviyah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 12:44; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:11; BAMIDBAR 18:21–24"> And he had prepared for him a great chamber, where aforetime they laid the minchah, the levonah, and the vessels, and the ma'aser of the grain, the tirosh, and the oil, which was commanded to be given to the Levi'im, and the singers, and the gatekeepers; and the terumat hakohanim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 5:14; NECHEMYAH 2:5–6; MATTITYAHU 13:25"> But in all this time was not I at Yerushalayim; for in the two and thirtieth year of Artachshasta Melech Bavel came I unto HaMelech, and after certain days obtained I leave of HaMelech;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 13:5; MATTITYAHU 21:12–13; EKHAH 1:10"> And I came to Yerushalayim, and understood of the evil that Elyashiv did for Toviyah, in preparing him a chamber in the courts of the Beis HaElohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MARKOS 11:15–17; EZRA 10:1; TEHILLIM 69:9"> And it grieved me much; therefore I cast forth all the household stuff of Toviyah out of the chamber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:15–19; NECHEMYAH 12:45"> Then I commanded, and they made tahor the chambers, and there brought I again the vessels of the Beis HaElohim, with the minchah and the levonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 10:37; MALACHI 3:8; MALACHI 1:6–14"> And I perceived that the portions of the Levi'im had not been given them, for the Levi'im and the singers, that did the work, had gone back every one to his sadeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 10:39; NECHEMYAH 13:17; NECHEMYAH 13:25"> Then contended I with the officials, and said, Why is the Beis HaElohim forsaken? And I gathered them together, and set them in their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 10:37–39; MALACHI 3:10; NECHEMYAH 12:44"> Then brought all Yehudah the ma'asar of the grain and the tirosh and the oil unto the Otzarot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 22:7; NECHEMYAH 7:2; NECHEMYAH 12:44"> And I made over the Otzarot Shelemyah the Kohen, and Tzadok the Sofer, and of the Levi'im, Pedayah; and next to them was Chanan ben Zakkur, ben Matanyah; for they were considered ne'emanim, and their office was to distribute unto their brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 122:6–9; NECHEMYAH 13:22; NECHEMYAH 13:31"> Remember me, O Elohai, concerning this, and wipe not out my faithful deeds that I have done for the Beis Elohai, and for the offices thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 10:31; NECHEMYAH 13:21; SHEMOT 34:21"> In those days saw I in Yehudah some treading winepresses on Shabbos, and bringing in sheaves, and loading donkeys; as also yayin, grapes, and figs, and all manner of massa (burdens), which they brought into Yerushalayim on Shabbos; and I warned them against selling food on Shabbos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:12; DEVARIM 5:14"> There dwelt men of Tzor also therein, which brought dag (fish), and all manner of merchandise, and sold on Shabbos unto the Bnei Yehudah, and in Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 13:11; NECHEMYAH 13:25; YESHAYAH 1:10"> Then I contended with the nobles of Yehudah, and said unto them, What evil thing is this that ye do, being Mechallel Shabbos (desecrator of Shabbos)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 17:21–23; YIRMEYAH 44:9; BAMIDBAR 32:14"> Did not your avot thus, and did not Eloheinu bring all this evil upon us, and upon this city? Yet ye bring more wrath upon Yisroel by committing Chillul Shabbos (desecration of Shabbos).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:32; SHEMOT 31:14–17; YIRMEYAH 17:19–22"> And it came to pass, that when the gates of Yerushalayim began to be dark before Shabbos, I commanded that the gates should be shut, and charged that they should not be opened till after Shabbos, and some of my own men set I at the gates, that there should no massa (burden) be brought in on Shabbos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Once or twice the merchants and sellers of all kinds of merchandise lodged outside Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:3–4; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:14; EZRA 7:26"> Then I warned them, and said unto them, Why lodge ye about the wall? If ye do so again, I will lay hands on you. From that time forth came they no more on Shabbos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 12:30; NECHEMYAH 13:14; NECHEMYAH 13:31"> And I commanded the Levi'im that they should make themselves tahor, and that they should come and be shomer over the gates, to set apart as kodesh Shabbos. Remember me, O Elohai, concerning this also, and spare me according to the greatness of Thy mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZRA 9:1–2; EZRA 10:10; NECHEMYAH 10:30"> In those days also saw I Yehudim that had married wives of Ashdod, of Ammon, and of Moav,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="TZEFANYAH 3:9"> And their children spoke half in the speech of Ashdod, and could not speak in the language of the Yehudim, but according to the language of one people or another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:3; NECHEMYAH 13:17; NECHEMYAH 10:29–30"> And I contended with them, and cursed them, and smote certain of them, and plucked off their hair, and made them swear by Elohim, saying, Ye shall not give your banot unto their banim, nor take their banot unto your banim, or for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:1–8; MELACHIM ALEF 3:13; KOHELET 7:26"> Did not Sh'lomo Melech Yisroel sin by these things? Yet among many Goyim was there no melech like him, who was beloved of Elohav, and Elohim made him melech over kol Yisroel. Nevertheless even him did foreign women cause to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EZRA 10:2; SHMUEL ALEF 30:24"> Shall we then pay heed unto you to do all this great evil, to transgress against Eloheinu in marrying strange wives?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 2:19; NECHEMYAH 3:1; NECHEMYAH 12:22"> And one of the Bnei Yoyada ben Elyashiv, the Kohen HaGadol, was son-in-law to Sanvalat the Choroni; therefore I drove him away from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 6:14; BAMIDBAR 25:12–13; MALACHI 2:4–8"> Remember them, O Elohai, because they have defiled the kehunah, and the brit (covenant) of the kehunah, and of the Levi'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 10:30; NECHEMYAH 12:1–26; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:1–26"> Thus I cleansed them from all that is foreign, and had them attend again to the duties of the Kohanim and the Levi'im, every one in his appointed work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 13:14; NECHEMYAH 13:22; TEHILLIM 106:4"> And for the korban of wood, at times appointed, and for the bikkurim. Remember me, O Elohai, for good.</span>
