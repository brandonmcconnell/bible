<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 2:19; NECHEMYAH 2:10; NECHEMYAH 4:1"> Now it came to pass when Sanvalat, and Toviyah, and Geshem the Arab, and the rest of oyveinu (our enemies), heard that I had rebuilt HaChomah, and that there was no peretz (gap) left therein (though at that time I had not set up the dalatot in the she'arim),</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="TEHILLIM 37:12; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 8:12; NECHEMYAH 11:35"> That Sanvalat and Geshem sent unto me, saying, Come, let us meet together in one of the villages in the plain of Ono. But they were choshevim (the ones scheming) to do me ra'ah (harm).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:4; MATTITYAHU 10:16; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:15–16"> And I sent malachim unto them, saying, I am doing a melachah gedolah, so that I cannot come down. Why should the melachah cease, whilst I leave it, and come down to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHOFETIM 16:15–20; LUKAS 18:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:5"> Yet they sent unto me this message arbah pe'amim; and I answered them after the same manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:13–15"> Then sent Sanvalat his aid unto me in like manner the fifth time with an unsealed iggeret in his hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 2:19; EZRA 4:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:8"> Wherein was written, It is reported among the Goyim, and Gashmu saith it, that thou and the Yehudim are choshevim (the ones scheming) revolt, for which cause thou buildest the Chomah, that thou mayest be their Melech, according to these reports.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MISHLE 26:24–26; MELACHIM ALEF 1:7; MELACHIM ALEF 1:18"> And thou hast also appointed nevi'im to make this proclamation about thee at Yerushalayim, saying, There is a Melech in Yehudah! Now this report will get back to HaMelech. Come now therefore, and let us take counsel together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 52:2; IYOV 13:4; MATTITYAHU 12:34"> Then I sent unto him, saying, There are no such devarim (things) done as thou sayest, but thou inventest them out of thine own lev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 138:3; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:16"> For they all were trying to make us afraid, saying, Their hands shall get too weakened for the melachah, that it be not completed. Now therefore, strengthen my hands!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 36:5; MATTITYAHU 7:15; TEHILLIM 37:12"> Afterward I came unto the bais Shema'yah ben Delayah ben Mehetave'el, who was confined; and he said, Let us meet together in the Bais HaElohim, within the Heikhal, and let us close the dalatot of the Heikhal, for they will come to slay thee; yea, tonight they come to slay thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TEHILLIM 11:1–2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:13; MISHLE 28:1"> And I said, Should such a man as I flee? And who is there, that, being as I am, would go into the Heikhal to save his life? I will not go in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 13:22; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:3; YECHEZKEL 13:7"> And, hinei, I perceived that Elohim had not sent him; but that he pronounced this nevu'ah (prophecy) against me, for Toviyah and Sanvalat had hired him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 20:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:12; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:7"> Therefore was he hired, that I should be afraid, and do so, and sin, and that they might have given me a shem rah (bad name), that they might discredit me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 13:29; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:14–15; YESHAYAH 9:14–15"> Remember, Elohai, Toviyah and Sanvalat according to these their ma'asim, and also the neviah (prophetess) Noadyah, and the rest of the nevi'im, that would have intimidated me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 1:3; DANIEL 9:25; NECHEMYAH 4:1–2"> So the Chomah was finished in the twenty and fifth day of the month Elul, in fifty and two days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="TEHILLIM 126:2; YEHOSHUA 5:1; NECHEMYAH 4:7"> And it came to pass, that when all oyveinu (our enemies) heard thereof, and all the Goyim around us saw these things, they were much cast down in their own eyes, for they perceived that this melachah was accomplished by Eloheinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MICHOH 7:1–6; MATTITYAHU 24:10–12; NECHEMYAH 3:5"> Moreover in those days the nobles of Yehudah sent many iggerot unto Toviyah, and the iggrot of Toviyah were making reply unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZRA 2:5; NECHEMYAH 7:10; NECHEMYAH 3:4"> For there were many ba'alei shevuah in Yehudah unto him, because he was the choson (son-in-law) of Shechanyah ben Arach, and his son Yehochanan had taken the bat Meshullam ben Berekhyah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:18–21; NECHEMYAH 6:9; YOCHANAN 7:7"> Also they reported his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Toviyahʼs supposed">a</a></sup>merits before me, and uttered my words to him. And Toviyah sent letters with the intent of intimidating me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="T.N. We see that the work of G-d had to be accomplished, not in a vacuum of peace and tranquility, but against the backdrop of manipulation and intimidation. Nehemiah gives G-d the credit and the glory for his escaping every snare.">b</a></sup></span>
