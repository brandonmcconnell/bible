<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 7:36; EZRA 2:34; NECHEMYAH 10:12"> And next unto him the Anshei Yericho built. And next to them built Zakkur ben Imri.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZRA 8:33; NECHEMYAH 3:21; NECHEMYAH 10:7"> And next unto them Meremot ben Uriyah ben Hakotz made repairs. And next unto them Meshullam ben Berekhyah ben Meshezav'el made repairs. And next unto them Tzadok ben Ba'ana did so.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 21:2; YEHOSHUA 9:3–27; NECHEMYAH 3:19"> And next unto them repaired Melatyah the Giv'oni, and Yadon the Meronoti, the men of Giv'on, and of Mitzpah, upto the seat of the governor of Beyond the River.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Euphrates">a</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 3:12; NECHEMYAH 3:17"> And next unto them repaired Refayah ben Chur, the ruler of half the district of Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 3:28–30; NECHEMYAH 10:4; NECHEMYAH 3:23"> And next unto them repaired Yedayah ben Charumaph, opposite his bais. And next unto him repaired Chattush ben Chashavneyah.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 3:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:8–9; SHEMOT 35:25"> And next unto him repaired Shallum ben Halochesh, the ruler of half the district of Yerushalayim, he and his banot.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 6:1; NECHEMYAH 2:13; NECHEMYAH 3:12–13"> But the Dung Gate did Malkiyah ben Rechav, the ruler of the district of Beit-Hakerem repair; he rebuilt it, and set up the daletot thereof, the locks thereof, and the bars thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:7; YESHAYAH 8:6; NECHEMYAH 12:37"> But the Fountain Gate did Shallun ben Kol-Chozeh, ruler of Mitzpah district repair; he rebuilt it, covered it, set up the daletot thereof, the locks thereof, and the bars thereof, and the chomah of the Pool of Shelach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Shiloah">b</a></sup>by the Gan HaMelech, and upto the stairs that go down from the Ir Dovid.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> After him repaired their achim, Bavai ben Chenadad, ruler half the Ke'ilah district.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 3:1; KOHELET 9:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:11"> After him Baruch ben Zakai zealously repaired the other section, from the Corner unto the petach Bais Elyashiv the Kohen HaGadol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 7:63; EZRA 2:61"> After him repaired Meremot ben Uriyah ben Hakotz another section, from the entrance of the Bais Elyashiv even to the end of the Bais Elyashiv.</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 10:2; NECHEMYAH 8:4; NECHEMYAH 3:29–30"> After him repaired Binyamin and Chashuv opposite their bais. <span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 3:19; NECHEMYAH 3:27; NECHEMYAH 10:9"> After him repaired Binnui ben Chenadad another section, from the bais Azaryah unto the Corner, even unto the Pinnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:2; YIRMEYAH 37:21; NECHEMYAH 12:39"> Palal ben Uzai, opposite the Corner, and the Migdal which projects from upper part of the Bais HaMelech, that was by the khatzer (court) of the Guard. After him Pedayah ben Parosh,</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 3:5"> After them the Tekoim repaired another section, opposite the great migdal that projects out, even unto the Chomat Ophel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 23:15; YIRMEYAH 31:40; MELACHIM BAIS 11:16"> From above the Horse Gate repaired the Kohanim, every one opposite his bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 19:2; EZRA 2:37; NECHEMYAH 7:40"> After them repaired Tzadok ben Immer opposite his bais. After him repaired also Shema'yah ben Shechanyah, the Shomer of the East Gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> After him repaired Chananyah ben Shelemyah, and Chanun the sixth son of Tzalaph, another section. After him repaired Meshullam ben Berekhyah opposite his living quarters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 3:8; NECHEMYAH 3:32"> After him repaired Malkiyah one of the goldsmiths unto the place of the Netenim, and of the merchants, opposite the Sha'ar HaMifkad, and to the aliyyat hapinnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 3:1; YOCHANAN 5:2; NECHEMYAH 12:39"> And between the aliyyat hapinnah unto the Sheep Gate repaired the goldsmiths and the merchants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> But it came to pass, that when Sanvalat heard that we were rebuilding the Chomah, he was angered, took great indignation, mocked the Yehudim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And he spoke before his brethren and the army of Shomron, and said, What do these feeble Yehudim? Will they fortify themselves? Will they sacrifice? Will they finish in a day? Will they revive the avanim out of the heaps of the burned rubble?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Now Toviyah the Ammoni was by him, and he said, Even that which they build, if a fox climb up on it, even he shall break down their chomat avanim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Shema, Eloheinu, for we are despised; turn their insults upon their own head, and give them as plunder in a land of captivity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And cover not their avon, and let not their chattat be blotted out from before thee, for they threw insults in the face of HaBonim.</span>
