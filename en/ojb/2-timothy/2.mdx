<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:9–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:10; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:7"> You, therefore, beni, be empowered in the Chen v'Chesed which is in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:13–14; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:6; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:12"> And what things you heard from me through edim rabbim, these things commit to anashim ne'emanim (faithful men) of zrizus (reliability) who will be qualified rabbinic morim to teach others also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:2–3; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:5"> Take your place in suffering as a chaiyal tov (good soldier) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:4; LUKAS 8:14; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20"> No one on duty as a chaiyal is entangled with the everyday chiloni (secular) affairs of inactive duty, in order that he may please the One who has enlisted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:24–27; HISGALUS 3:11; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:7–8"> And no participant in an athletic tacharut (competition) is crowned unless he competes according to the rules.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:36; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:7–11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:6–9"> It is the hard working ikkar (farmer) who ought to have the rishonah mipri ha'adamah (the first share from the harvest of the earth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:20; LUKAS 24:45"> Let your hitbonenut be on what I say, for Hashem will give to you binah in all things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:11–20"> Remember Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, of the zera Dovid, and remember the Techiyas HaMoshiach, according to my Besuras HaGeulah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:31; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:8"> For which I suffer as if I were an evil-doer, even to the point of the bais hasoharʼs bonds, but the Dvar Hashem has no bonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:24; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:3"> Therefore, I endure all things for the sake of the Bechirim (the Chosen ones), in order that they also may obtain the Yeshu'at HaEloheinu in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua with kavod olamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:3–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:5"> Trustworthy is Hashem, for if we died<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="to the Olam Hazeh">a</a></sup>with Him, also we will live<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="in the Olam Habah">b</a></sup>with Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:17; MATTITYAHU 10:33; HISGALUS 20:4"> If we endure, we also will reign with Him; if we will deny Him, He also will deny us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 23:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:3"> If we are not ne'emanim (faithful), He remains ne'eman (faithful), for to deny Himself, He is not able.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:9–11; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:23; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:1"> Remind them of these things, solemnly warning them in the presence of Hashem not to be engaged in disputes over devarim, which is not beneficial, but only ruins the hearers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:4; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:10; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:6"> Do your best to shtel zich (apply yourself), to present yourself to Hashem as one approved, a po'el (workman) without bushah (shame), keeping on a derech yashar the Dvar HaEmes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:9; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:20; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:14"> But profane chatter bereft of kedushah, avoid, for such will advance that which is frai (irreligious).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:20; NACHUM 3:15; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:3"> Their lashon hora will spread like gangrene, as in the case of Hymenaeus and Philetus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:12; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:10"> Who concerning HaEmes missed the mark, teaching that the Techiyas HaMesim has already occurred. They are overthrowing the emunah of some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:3; NACHUM 1:7"> Al kol panim (nevertheless), the solid yesod of Hashem stands firm and zicher (certain), having this seal: v'yoda' Hashem es asher lo (Hashem knows the ones who are his);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Num 16:5">c</a></sup>and let everyone who names the name of Hashem depart from avel (iniquity, gross injustice).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:21–23; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:15"> In a bais gadol, there are not only k'lei (vessels) of gold and silver but also those wooden ones and earthen ones: some, for honorable use; others, for dishonorable use.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:1"> If anyone makes himself tahor from these things he will be a k'li vessel for honorable use, having been set aside as kodesh, useful to HaAdon, ready for every ma'aseh tov.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:11; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:18"> But flee from the ta'avot hane'urim (lusts of youth)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ps 25:7">d</a></sup>and pursue tzedek, emunah, ahavah, and shalom with the ones calling on Adoneinu out of a lev tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:14; LETTER TO TITOS 3:9; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:7"> But speculations characterized by narrishkait and lacking da'as — such refuse, knowing that they produce fights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:2; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19–20; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:2–3"> And an eved Hashem ought not be a Ba'al Machlokes (quarrelsome person), but ought to be eidel (gentle, courteous) to all, a skilled rabbinic moreh, savlan (patient),</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:1; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:4"> Correcting the mitnaggedim (opponents) in anavat ruach (a spirit of meekness), in the tikvah that Hashem may efsher (perhaps) grant them teshuva, resulting in da'as HaEmes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:7; YESHAYAH 49:25–26; TEHILLIM 124:7"> And that they may come to their senses, escaping the pakh (trap) of HaSatan, after having been captured by him to do his will.</span>
</div>
