<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:29–31; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:16"> For Bnei Adam will be in love with self, ohavei kesef (lovers of money), ga'avtanim (proud boasters), speakers of lashon hora, disobedient to horim (parents), without hakarat todah (gratitude), without kedushah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:31; MATTITYAHU 10:21"> Without ahavah, unforgiving and irreconcilable, without shlitah atzmi (self-control), bestial, son'ei HaTov (haters of the Good),</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:18–19; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:6; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:4"> Treacherous, reckless, conceited, ohavei ta'anugot (lovers of pleasures) rather than ohavei Hashem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YESHAYAH 29:13; LETTER TO TITOS 1:16; MATTITYAHU 23:27–28"> Having an outward form of chasidus but the ko'ach having denied. Turn away from these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:4; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17; LETTER TO TITOS 3:3"> For of such are the morim entering into homes and capturing weak-willed nashim laden with chatta'im, led away by various ta'avot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:3–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:14; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:4"> Always learning but never being able to come to da'as HaEmes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:8–11; HISGALUS 2:20; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:5"> As Jannes and Jambres opposed Moshe Rabbeinu, so also these oppose HaEmes. These men have been corrupted in their mind and are failures with respect to the emunah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="of Moshiach, Yd 1:3">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:8; SHEMOT 7:12; YIRMEYAH 28:15–17"> But they will not proceed very far, for the sichlut (folly, stupidity) of them will likewise become conspicuous to all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:11; LETTER TO TITOS 2:7; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5–7"> But you closely followed my torah, my halichah, my tachlis, my emunah, my savlanut (patience), my ahavah, my endurance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TEHILLIM 34:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:5–6; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:23–28"> The redifot (persecutions) and Messianic yissurim (sufferings) which happened to me in Antioch, in Iconium, in Lystra, what kind of redifot I endured. And out of all Adoneinu rescued me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:14; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:12–16; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20–21"> Also all the ones wanting to live as chasidim in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua will be persecuted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:1; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20; LETTER TO TITOS 3:3"> But anashim ra'im and impostors will progress to their abysmal worst, misleading and being misled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:13; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:2; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:13"> But, you, remain in the shiurim you learned, and the things you were convinced of, knowing under which rabbi you sat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:98–99; LUKAS 24:45; MISHLE 22:6"> And that from infancy you have known the Kitvei HaKodesh, which are able to make you chacham with a view to Yeshu'at Eloheynu through emunah in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:12; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19–21"> The entire Kitvei HaKodesh is Hashem-breathed and useful for hora'ah (teaching), for reproof, for correction, for training in tzedek,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:8"> That the ish haElohim may be proficient, having been equipped for every one of the ma'asim tovim.</span>
