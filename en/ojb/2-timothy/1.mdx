</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:1; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:1"> To Timotiyos, beni haahuv (my beloved son). Chesed Hashem, Rachamim Hashem and Shalom Hashem from Elohim Avinu and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua Adoneinu.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:8–9; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:1"> Modeh Ani (I give thanks) to Hashem whom I offer avodas hakodesh service with a clear matzpun — as did the Avot of me — when I remember you constantly in my tefillos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:17–1"> As I remember your weeping, I want to see you, that I may be filled with simcha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:1; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:15"> I remember your genuine emunah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="in Moshiach">a</a></sup>which dwelt first in Lois your savtah (grandmother, bobe) and in your Em, Eunice, and I have been persuaded dwells also in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:14; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10–11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:6–8"> For which reason I remind you to meorer (rekindle) the matnat Elohim, (the gift of Hashem) which is in you through my conferring of s'michah (ordination).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1Ti 4:14">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:15; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:22"> For Hashem did not bestow upon us a ruach of pachad (terror), but of gevurah (miraculous power) and of ahavah (love) and of sound havchanah (judgment).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:16; MARKOS 8:38; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:13–15"> Do not, therefore, be ashamed of the eidus of Adoneinu nor of me, his asir (prisoner), but suffer together with me for the Besuras HaGeulah in the ko'ach of Hashem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:8–9; LETTER TO TITOS 3:4–5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:3–4"> The one having granted us Yeshu'at Eloheinu and having called us with a kri'ah kedoshah (holy calling), not according to the ma'asim of us but according to his own tachlis (purpose) and chesed having been given to us in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua before Yamim HaOlam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:26; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18"> But having been manifested now through the appearing of Moshieinu Moshiach Yehoshua, who nullified death<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e., his own histalkus and Mavet itself">c</a></sup>and also brought Chayyim and alkillayon (incorruptibility) to light through the Besuras HaGeulah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:7–8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:15"> For which I was appointed a karoz (herald), a maggid (darshan, preacher), a Shliach and a rabbi (teacher).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:8; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:24; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:19"> It is because of these things also that I suffer. But I am not ashamed, for I know whom I have believed and I have been persuaded that He is able to stand shomer, guarding<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="preserving">d</a></sup>until HaYom HaHu the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="orthodox Jewish, see 1Ti 6:20">e</a></sup>pikkadon (deposit) entrusted to Him by me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 1:9; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:9"> Follow the pattern of sound orthodox Jewish devarim which you heard from me, in emunah and ahavah in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:13; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:20"> Stand shomer over the orthodox Jewish pikkadon entrusted to you through the Ruach Hakodesh dwelling in us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:10"> Of this you have da'as: all the ones in Asia, of whom are Phygelus and Hermogenes, turned away from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:19; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:20; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:7"> May Hashem grant rachamim to the bais of Onesiphorus, because often he refreshed me and he was not ashamed of my sharsherot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:30–31"> For, when he was in Rome, he sought me with zerizut and found me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:10; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:12; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:3"> May Adoneinu grant to him to find rachamim from Hashem in HaYom HaHu.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="2Ti 1:12">f</a></sup>Also, while in Ephesus in how many ways he did avodas hakodesh you know very well.</span>
</div>
