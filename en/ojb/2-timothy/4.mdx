<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:42; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:8; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:21"> I solemnly charge you before Hashem and Moshiach Yehoshua, the imminent Shofet of the living and dead, and by the Bi'as HaMoshiach and His Malchut:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:14; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:15–16; LETTER TO TITOS 2:15"> Attend to the hatafah (preaching) of the Dvar Hashem. Be ready in season, out of season, expose, rebuke, encourage, with all long suffering and hora'ah (teaching).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1–3; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:1–6; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:1–3"> For there will be a time when sound charedi (orthodox) hora'ah they will not tolerate, but according to their ta'avot they will accumulate morim to tickle their ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 7:11; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:57"> And from HaEmes of Hashem they will turn their ear away shmad and to aggadah they will be turned aside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:8; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:13; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:3"> But, you, exercise shlitah atzmi (self-control) in all things, suffer hardship, do the work of a mevaser of the Besuras HaGeulah, fully carry out your avodas kodesh ministry of kiruv rechokim (bringing near the far away ones).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:17; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:14–15"> For already I am being poured out, and the time of my avekfor (departure),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Pp 1:23">a</a></sup>has come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:24–27; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:1–2"> The milchemet tzedek (war of righteousness) I have fought, the course I have finished, the emunah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Yd 1:3">b</a></sup>I have been shomer over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; HISGALUS 2:10; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:4"> Henceforth, there is laid up for me the keter hatzedakah (crown of righteousness) which Adoneinu, the Shofet Tzedek, will give to me in HaYom HaHu, and not only to me, but also to all the ones who are ohavei Bi'as HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:4; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:21"> Have zerizut (diligence) to come to me quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–16; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:14–15"> For Demas deserted me. Having loved the Olam Hazeh, he departed for Thessalonika; Crescens, to Galatia; Titos, to Dalmatia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:12; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:25"> Lukas alone is with me. Get Markos and bring him with you, for he is useful to me for avodas hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:4; LETTER TO TITOS 3:12"> Now Tychicus I sent to Ephesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:11"> When you come, bring the cloak which I left behind in Troas with Carpus, and the megillos, especially the parchments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:20; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:6; TEHILLIM 28:4"> Alexander the coppersmith did ra'ot rabbot (much evil) to me; Adonoi will repay him L'ISH K'MA'ASEIHU (to each as his deeds).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 62:12">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:2; MATTITYAHU 10:16–17"> You also watch out for him, because he greatly opposed our message.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="about Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:60; YOCHANAN 16:32; MARKOS 14:50"> At my first hitstaddekut (defense), no one came to be with me, but all forsook me. May it not be counted against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:12–14; SHMUEL ALEF 17:37"> But Adoneinu stood with me, and empowered me, that through me the hachrazah (proclamation, kerygma) of the Besuras HaGeulah might be fully preached and all the Nations might hear it. And I was rescued out of the mouth of the lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="TEHILLIM 121:7; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:3; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:24–25"> Hashem will rescue me from every evil work and thus will bring me safely into his Malchut HaShomayim. Lo haKavod l'olam va'ed. Omein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:3–4"> Drishat Shalom to Prisca and Aquila and the bais of Onesiphorus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:26–27"> Erastus remained in Corinth, but Trophimus, ailing, I left behind in Miletus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:22; (III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:14"> Make haste to come before winter. Drishat Shalom to you from Eubulus and Pudens and Linus, Claudia, and all the Achim b'Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:18"> Hashem be with your neshamah. Chen v'Chesed Hashem be with you.</span>
