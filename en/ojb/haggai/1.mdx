<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZRA 2:2; EZRA 3:8; EZRA 3:2"> In the second year of Daryavesh HaMelech, in the sixth month, in yom echad of the month, came the Devar Hashem by Chaggai HaNavi unto Zerubavel ben Sh'altiel, Governor of Yehudah, and to Yehoshua ben Yehotzadak,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., the namesake of Moshiach, see Zech 6:11-12">a</a></sup>the Kohen HaGadol, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZRA 4:23–2; NECHEMYAH 4:10; KOHELET 11:4"> Thus saith Hashem Tzva'os: This people say, The time is not come, the time that Beis Hashem should be built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZRA 5:1; ZECHARYAH 1:1"> Then came the Devar Hashem by Chaggai HaNavi, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 7:2; TEHILLIM 132:3–5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:21"> Is it time for you, O ye, to dwell in your paneled batim, and this Beis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="HaMikdash">b</a></sup>lie in ruins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EKHAH 3:40; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:5; CHAGGAI 1:7"> Now therefore thus saith Hashem Tzva'os; Consider your ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="CHAGGAI 1:9; DEVARIM 28:38–40; HOSHEA 4:10"> Ye have sown harbeh (much), and bring in me'at (little); ye eat, but ye have not enough; ye drink, but ye are not filled with drink; ye clothe yourselves, but there is no warming; and he that earneth wages earneth wages to put them into a purse of holes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:59–60; CHAGGAI 1:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:1"> Thus saith Hashem Tzva'os: Consider your ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 132:13–14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:16; CHAGGAI 2:7"> Go up to the har (mountain), and bring etz, and build HaBeis;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="HaMikdash">c</a></sup>and I will take pleasure in it, and I will be glorified, saith Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="CHAGGAI 1:4; CHAGGAI 2:16–17; CHAGGAI 1:6"> Ye looked for harbeh (much), and, hinei, it came to me'at (little); and when ye brought it home, I blew it away. Why? saith Hashem Tzva'os. Because of Mine Beis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="HaMikdash">d</a></sup>that is in ruins, and ye run every man for his own bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:35; VAYIKRA 26:19; DEVARIM 28:23–24"> Therefore the Shomayim over you is stayed from dew, and Ha'Aretz is stayed from her fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="CHAGGAI 2:17; DEVARIM 28:22; MELACHIM BAIS 8:1"> And I called for a drought upon ha'aretz, and upon the harim (mountains), and upon the grain, and upon the new wine, and upon the oil, and upon that which ha'adamah bringeth forth, and upon ha'adam, and upon livestock, and upon all the labor of the hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 50:10; EZRA 5:2; CHAGGAI 1:14"> Then Zerubavel ben Sh'altiel, and Yehoshua ben Yehotzadak, the Kohen HaGadol, with kol she'erit HaAm, obeyed the voice of Hashem Eloheichem, and the words of Chaggai HaNavi, as Hashem Eloheichem had sent him, and the people did fear before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YESHAYAH 43:2; MATTITYAHU 28:20; MALACHI 3:1"> Then spoke Chaggai malach Hashem with the message of Hashem unto the people, saying, I am with you, saith Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:22; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:21; CHAGGAI 2:21"> And Hashem stirred up the ruach of Zerubavel ben Sh'altiel, Governor of Yehudah, and the ruach of Yehoshua ben Yehotzadak, the Kohen HaGadol, and the ruach of kol she'erit HaAm; and they came and did work in the Beis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="HaMikdash">e</a></sup>of Hashem Tzva'os, Eloheichem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="CHAGGAI 1:1; CHAGGAI 2:20; CHAGGAI 2:1"> In the four and twentieth yom of the sixth month, in the second year of Daryavesh HaMelech.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="520 B.C.E.">f</a></sup></span>
