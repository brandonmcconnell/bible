<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="CHAGGAI 1:1; CHAGGAI 2:20; CHAGGAI 2:10"> In the seventh month, in the one and twentieth yom of the month, came the Devar Hashem by Chaggai HaNavi, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="CHAGGAI 1:1; NECHEMYAH 8:9; EZRA 2:63"> Speak now to Zerubavel ben Sh'altiel, Governor of Yehudah, and to Yehoshua ben Yehotzadak, the Kohen HaGadol, and to the she'erit HaAm, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZRA 3:12; CHAGGAI 2:9; YECHEZKEL 7:20"> Who is left among you that saw this Beis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hamikdash">a</a></sup>in her kavod harishon? And how do ye see it now? In comparison to it, is it not in your eyes as nothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:20; YEHOSHUA 1:9; ZECHARYAH 8:9"> Yet now chazak (be strong), O Zerubavel, saith Hashem; and chazak (be strong), O Yehoshua ben Yehotzadak, Kohen HaGadol; and chazak (be strong), kol Am Ha'Aretz, saith Hashem, and work; for I am with you, saith Hashem Tzva'os.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:10; SHEMOT 29:45–46; YESHAYAH 41:13"> According to the word that I covenanted with you when ye came out of Mitzrayim, so My Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hakodesh">b</a></sup>remaineth among you; fear ye not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:26–28; YESHAYAH 10:25; MARKOS 13:24–26"> For thus saith Hashem Tzva'os: Once more, it is a little while, and I will shake HaShomayim and Ha'Aretz and the yam (sea) and the kharavah (arid land);</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MALACHI 3:1; MELACHIM ALEF 8:11; YOCHANAN 7:37–39"> And I will shake kol Goyim, and the Chemdat kol HaGoyim (desired of all nations) shall come: and I will fill this Beis with kavod, saith Hashem Tzva'os.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 50:10–12; TEHILLIM 24:1; YESHAYAH 60:17"> The kesef is Mine, and the zahav is Mine, saith Hashem Tzva'os.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 85:8–9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:9–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:19–21"> The kavod of this latter Beis shall be greater than of the former, saith Hashem Tzva'os: and in this place will I give shalom, saith Hashem Tzva'os.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="CHAGGAI 2:20; CHAGGAI 1:1; CHAGGAI 1:15–1"> In the four and twentieth yom of the ninth month, in the second year of Daryavesh, came the Devar Hashem by Chaggai HaNavi, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="VAYIKRA 10:10–11; MALACHI 2:7; DEVARIM 33:10"> Thus saith Hashem Tzva'os; Ask now the kohanim concerning the torah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="VAYIKRA 6:27; MATTITYAHU 23:19; VAYIKRA 7:6"> If one carry basar kodesh in the fold of his garment, and with his fold do touch lechem, or that which is boiled, or yayin, or shemen (olive oil), or any food, shall it thus be made kodesh? And the kohanim answered and said, Lo (No).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 5:2–3; BAMIDBAR 19:11–22; VAYIKRA 22:4–6"> Then said Chaggai, If one that is tamei (unclean) by a nefesh (dead body) touch any of these, shall it be tamei? And the kohanim answered and said, It shall be tamei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MISHLE 28:9; LETTER TO TITOS 1:15; YESHAYAH 1:11–15"> Then answered Chaggai, and said, So is this people, and so is this nation before Me, saith Hashem; and so is every work of their hands; and that which they offer there is tamei (unclean).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="CHAGGAI 1:5; EZRA 3:10; EZRA 4:24"> And now, I pray you, consider from this yom and onward, from before an even (stone) was laid upon an even (stone) in the Heikhal Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="CHAGGAI 1:6; MALACHI 2:2; MISHLE 3:9–10"> Since those yamim were, when one came to a heap of grain of twenty measures, there were but ten; when one came to the wine vat for to draw out fifty vessels, there were but twenty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:22; CHAGGAI 1:11; CHAGGAI 1:9"> I struck you with blight and with mildew and with hail in all the labors of your hands; yet ye made not teshuvah to Me, saith Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 8:9; CHAGGAI 2:15; EZRA 5:1–2"> Consider now from this yom and onward, from the four and twentieth yom of the ninth month, even from the yom that the Yesod (Foundation) of the Heikhal Hashem was laid, consider it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MALACHI 3:10; CHABAKUK 3:17–18; MISHLE 3:9–10"> Is the seed still in the granary? As yet the gefen, and the &#91;etz&#93; te'enah, and the pomegranate, and etz hazayit (olive tree), hath not brought forth. From this yom forth will I bless you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And again the Devar Hashem came unto Chaggai in the four and twentieth yom of the month, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 4:6–10; CHAGGAI 2:6–7; CHAGGAI 1:1"> Speak to Zerubavel, Governor of Yehudah, saying, I will shake HaShomayim and Ha'Aretz;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MICHOH 5:10; SHOFETIM 7:22; TZEFANYAH 3:8"> And I will overthrow the throne of mamlachot (kingdoms), and I will destroy the chozek (strength) of the mamlachot HaGoyim; and I will overthrow the merkavot, and those that ride in them; and the susim and their riders shall fall, every one by the cherev (sword) of achiv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YESHAYAH 43:10; YESHAYAH 42:1; SHIR HASHIRIM 8:6"> In that yom, saith Hashem Tzva'os, will I take thee, O Zerubavel ben Sh'altiel, Avdi (My Servant),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Isa 52:13for this Avdi — My Servant — as a reference which is ultimately related to Moshiach">c</a></sup>saith Hashem, and will make thee as a chotam (signet ring); for I have chosen thee, saith Hashem Tzva'os.</span>
</div>
