<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MISHLE 6:15; SHMUEL ALEF 2:25; MISHLE 1:24–31"> <span className="pl pl-1">He, that being often reproved, stiffeneth his neck,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shall suddenly be destroyed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and that without marpeh (remedy).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MISHLE 28:12; MISHLE 11:10; MISHLE 28:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the tzaddikim are increased, the people rejoice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but when the rasha beareth rule, the people groan.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MISHLE 27:11; MISHLE 10:1; MISHLE 15:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who loveth chochmah rejoiceth aviv,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that keepeth company with zonot squanders his hon (substance, wealth).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MISHLE 29:14; MISHLE 20:8; DANIEL 11:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Melech by mishpat establisheth eretz,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but an ish terumot (a man of bribes) bringeth it down.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HOSHEA 5:1; IYOV 17:5; TEHILLIM 5:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">A gever that flattereth his neighbor</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">spreadeth a reshet (net) for his feet.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="KOHELET 9:12; SHEMOT 15:1; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">By peysha (transgression) an ish rah is snared,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the tzaddik doth sing and have simchah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="IYOV 29:16; TEHILLIM 41:1; MISHLE 21:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The tzaddik considereth the cause of the dalim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the rasha (wicked) doeth not understand such da'as (knowledge).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MISHLE 11:11; YIRMEYAH 15:1; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:15–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Scornful men enflame a city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but chachamim turn away af (wrath).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MISHLE 26:4; KOHELET 10:13; MATTITYAHU 7:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">If an ish chacham has to contend with a fool,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the fool">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">rageth or laugheth, and hath no rest.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:18–19; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:12–13; BERESHIS 4:5–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">The anshei damim hate the blameless,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the yesharim seek his nefesh (soul, i.e., well-being).</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., his soulʼs well-being">b</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MISHLE 19:11; MISHLE 12:16; MICHOH 7:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">A kesil (fool) uttereth all his wrath,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but a chacham keepeth it back quietly.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MISHLE 20:8; MELACHIM BAIS 10:6–7; TEHILLIM 52:2–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">If a moshel pays heed to davar sheker,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all his mesharetim (officials, ministers) are resha'im.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:45; MISHLE 22:2; TEHILLIM 13:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The rahsh (pauper) and the oppressor have this in common:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Hashem enlighteneth both their eynayim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MISHLE 16:12; YESHAYAH 11:4; TEHILLIM 72:2–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">If Melech shofet be'emes (judges in truth) the dalim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">his kisse shall be established forever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MISHLE 10:1; MISHLE 17:25; MISHLE 29:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">The shevet (rod) and tokhakhat (reproof) give chochmah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but a na'ar (child) left to himself bringeth immo to shame.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="TEHILLIM 37:36; TEHILLIM 91:8; TEHILLIM 58:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the resha'im are multiplied, peysha (transgression) increaseth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the tzaddikim shall see their downfall.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MISHLE 10:1; MISHLE 29:15; MISHLE 23:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Correct binecha, and he shall give thee rest;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">indeed, he shall give delight unto thy nefesh.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:25; LUKAS 11:28; YOCHANAN 13:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Where there is no chazon (prophetic vision), the people cast off restaint,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e., perish ungovernable">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that is shomer over the torah, happy is he.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MISHLE 26:3; IYOV 19:16; MISHLE 30:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">An eved will not be corrected by devarim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for though he understand he will not answer.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19; MISHLE 26:12; MISHLE 14:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">Seest thou an ish that is hasty in his devarim?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There is more tikvah for a kesil (fool) than for him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that pampers his eved from youth up</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shall have him as his ben at the acharit (end).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MISHLE 15:18; MISHLE 17:19; MISHLE 26:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">An ish af (angry man) stirreth up strife,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and a ba'al chemah (ill-tempered man) aboundeth in peysha (transgression).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:12; LUKAS 14:11; MISHLE 11:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ga'avat adam (pride of man) shall bring him low,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the humble in ruach shall attain kavod.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="VAYIKRA 5:1; MISHLE 1:11–19; MISHLE 8:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who is partner with a ganav hateth his own nefesh;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he heareth the curse of the victim but discloses nothing.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="TEHILLIM 118:8; LUKAS 12:4; MISHLE 18:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">The fear of adam bringeth a mokesh (snare),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he who putteth his trust in Hashem shall be secure.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MISHLE 19:6; MISHLE 19:21; TEHILLIM 20:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Many seek the moshelʼs favor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but mishpat ish cometh from Hashem.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:13; YOCHANAN 15:17–19; TEHILLIM 139:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">An unjust man is a to'evah to the tzaddikim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he that is yeshar derech (upright in the way) is to'evah to the rasha.</span></span>
