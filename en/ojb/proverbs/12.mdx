<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MISHLE 15:10; MISHLE 9:7–8; TEHILLIM 119:97–100"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who loveth musar loveth da'as,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that hateth tokhakhat (reproof, correction) is ba'ar (a boorish, ignorant person).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MISHLE 8:35; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:24; TEHILLIM 112:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">A tov (good man) obtaineth ratzon (favor) from Hashem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but an ish mezimor (a man of crafty devices) will He condemn.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MISHLE 10:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:17; MISHLE 12:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">An adam shall not be made secure by resha,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the shoresh tzaddikim (root of the righteous ones) shall not be moved.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="T.N. Cf 12:4and 31:10.">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MISHLE 14:30; MISHLE 31:10–25; MISHLE 14:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">An aishes chayil (a woman of valor, an excellent wife) is an ateret to her ba'al,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but she that bringeth shame is like rottenness in his atzmot.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 139:23; YESHAYAH 55:7; TEHILLIM 140:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The thoughts of the tzaddikim are right,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the tachbulot (guidance) of the resha'im are mirmah (deceitful).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MISHLE 14:3; YIRMEYAH 5:26; MISHLE 1:11–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">The devarim of the resha'im are to lie in wait for dahm,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the peh (mouth) of the yesharim (upright ones) shall deliver them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:24–27; MISHLE 11:21; MISHLE 10:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">The resha'im are overthrown, and are no more,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the bais tzaddikim shall stand.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:10–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">An ish shall be commended according to his seichel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that is of a perverse mind shall be despised.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 14:11; MISHLE 13:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that is lightly esteemed, and hath an eved,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">is better than he that honoreth himself, and lacketh lechem.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEVARIM 25:4; BERESHIS 33:13–14; BAMIDBAR 22:28–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">A tzaddik regardeth the nefesh (life) of his behemah (beast),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the compassion of the resha'im is cruel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MISHLE 28:19; MISHLE 14:23; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that worketh his adamah shall be satisfied with lechem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that chaseth after worthless persons is devoid of lev.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="of understanding">b</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 17:7–8; YOCHANAN 15:5; LUKAS 8:13–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">The resha coveteth the catch of ra'im (evil men),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the shoresh (root) of the tzaddikim yieldeth fruit.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MISHLE 21:23; MISHLE 11:8; TEHILLIM 64:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">The rah is ensnared by the peysha (transgression) of his sfatayim (lips),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the tzaddik shall escape from tzoros.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MISHLE 18:20–21; YESHAYAH 3:10–11; MISHLE 13:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">An ish shall be filled with tov by the fruit of his peh (mouth),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the recompence of the hands of adam shall be rendered unto him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MISHLE 3:7; MISHLE 26:12; MISHLE 14:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The derech of a fool is yashar (right) in his own eynayim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that payeth heed unto etzah is chacham (wise).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MISHLE 29:11; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19; MISHLE 10:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">A foolʼs wrath is known immediately,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but a prudent man covereth kalon (shame, disgrace, shameful nakedness).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MISHLE 14:5; MISHLE 14:25; MISHLE 24:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that speaketh emunah declareth tzedek,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but an ed shekarim, mirmah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MISHLE 16:24; MISHLE 15:4; TEHILLIM 57:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is one who speaketh rash words like the wounding of a cherev,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the lashon of the chachamim promotes marpeh (healing).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MISHLE 19:9; TEHILLIM 52:4–5; IYOV 20:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The lip of emes shall be established forever,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but a lashon sheker is but for a moment.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:9; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:8–13; MISHLE 12:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Mirmah (deceit) is in the lev of them that plot rah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but to the counsellors of shalom is simchah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:13; TEHILLIM 91:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">There shall no harm befall the tzaddik,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the resha'im shall be filled with rah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="TEHILLIM 5:6; MISHLE 11:20; HISGALUS 22:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sfatayim (lips) of sheker are to'evah to Hashem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but they that deal emunah (faithfully, truthfully) are His delight.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MISHLE 13:16; MISHLE 11:13; MISHLE 10:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">A prudent man concealeth da'as,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the lev kesilim (mind of fools) preaches folly.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MISHLE 12:27; MISHLE 10:4; MISHLE 21:25–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">The yad of the kharutzim (diligent ones) shall rule,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the remiyyah (deceitfully lazy man) shall be put to forced labor.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MISHLE 16:24; MISHLE 15:13; MISHLE 17:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Anxiety in the lev ish causeth depression,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but a davar tov cheers it up.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:26; MATTITYAHU 5:46–48; MISHLE 17:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">The tzaddik prevaileth advantageously over his neighbor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the derech resha'im leads them astray.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MISHLE 13:4; MISHLE 26:15; MISHLE 15:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The remiyyah (deceitfully lazy man) roasteth not that which he took in hunting,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but diligence is the precious possession of man.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="(III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:21; YECHEZKEL 18:20–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the orach tzedakah (way of righteousness) is chayyim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and in the derech netivot (pathway) thereof there is no mavet.</span></span>
