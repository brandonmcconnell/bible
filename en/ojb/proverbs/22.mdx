<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="KOHELET 7:1; LUKAS 10:20; MELACHIM ALEF 1:47"> <span className="pl pl-1">A shem tov is rather to be desired than osher rav,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and chen (grace) rather than kesef and zahav.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="IYOV 31:15; MISHLE 14:31; SHMUEL ALEF 2:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The oisher (rich man) and the rahsh (pauper) have this in common:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Hashem is the Oseh (Maker) of them all.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MISHLE 27:12; MISHLE 14:16; MISHLE 29:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">A prudent man foreseeth the ra'ah, and hideth himself,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the naïve trudge on, and are punished.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:33; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10; TEHILLIM 34:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">By anavah (humility) and the Yirat Hashem</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">are osher, and kavod, and chayyim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MISHLE 15:19; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:18; MISHLE 16:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thorns and pachim (snares) are in the derech of the crooked;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he that is shomer over his nefesh shall be far from them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:4; DEVARIM 6:7; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Train up a na'ar in the derech (road) he should go,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and when he is old, he will not depart from it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:1; MISHLE 22:16; MATTITYAHU 18:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">The oisher ruleth over the rahshim (paupers),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the loveh (borrower, debtor) is eved (slave) to the ish malveh (lender).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:7–8; IYOV 4:8; YESHAYAH 30:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that soweth iniquity shall reap aven (iniquity),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the shevet (rod) of his wrath shall disappear.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MISHLE 19:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:10; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that hath a tov ayin (generous eye) shall have a bracha;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for he giveth of his lechem to the dal (poor).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MISHLE 26:20–21; BERESHIS 21:9–10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cast out the scoffer, and contention shall leave;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">indeed, strife and kalon (shame) shall cease.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MISHLE 16:13; MATTITYAHU 5:8; TEHILLIM 101:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that loveth tahor lev (a pure heart),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the chen (grace) on his sfatayim (lips) the Melech shall be his re'a (friend).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 16:9; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:8–9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">The eynayim of Hashem preserve da'as,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and He overthroweth the devarim of the boged (unfaithful, treacherous, traitor).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MISHLE 15:19; MISHLE 26:13–16; BAMIDBAR 13:32–33"> <span className="pl pl-1">The atzel (sluggard, lazy one) saith, There is an ari (lion) outside;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I shall be murdered in the streets.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="KOHELET 7:26; MISHLE 23:27; MISHLE 6:24–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">The peh (mouth) of zarot (strange women) is a deep pit;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he that is under the wrath of Hashem shall fall therein.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MISHLE 13:24; MISHLE 29:15; MISHLE 19:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Folly is bound in the lev na'ar,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the shevet musar (rod of correction) shall drive it far from him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MISHLE 22:22–23; TEHILLIM 12:5; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that oppresseth the dal (poor) in order to increase,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he that giveth to the oisher, shall surely come to machsor (poverty, want).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MISHLE 23:12; TEHILLIM 90:12; MISHLE 3:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Bow down thine ozen, and hear the devarim of chachamim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and apply thine lev (mind, heart) unto my da'as.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 15:16; MISHLE 2:10; TEHILLIM 119:103"> <span className="pl pl-1">For it is a pleasant thing if thou be shomer over them within thee;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they shall therewith abide ready upon thy sfatayim (lips).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="TEHILLIM 62:8; YIRMEYAH 17:7; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">That thy trust may be in Hashem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have made thee know this yom, even thee!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MISHLE 8:6; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:15–17; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Have not I written to thee shloshim (thirty sayings)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in mo'etzot (counsels) and da'as,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKAS 1:3–4; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:15; YOCHANAN 20:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">That I might make thee know the certain truth of the words of emes;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that thou mightest bring back words of emes to them that send thee?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 7:10; MALACHI 3:5; SHEMOT 23:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Exploit not the dal (poor), because he is dal (poor);</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">neither oppress the oni (afflicted) in the sha'ar,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="TEHILLIM 12:5; TEHILLIM 140:12; MISHLE 23:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">For Hashem will plead their cause,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and plunder the nefesh of those that plundered them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MISHLE 29:22; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:14–17; MISHLE 21:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Make no friendship with a ba'al af (angry man),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and with an ish chemot (man of wrath) thou shalt not go,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:33; MISHLE 13:20; TEHILLIM 106:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lest thou learn his orkhot (ways),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and take a mokesh (snare) for thy nefesh.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MISHLE 11:15; MISHLE 17:18; MISHLE 6:1–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be not thou one of them that shake hands in a pledge,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or of them that are collateral for masha'ot (debts, loans).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MISHLE 20:16; MELACHIM BAIS 4:1; SHEMOT 22:26–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">If thou hast nothing with which to pay,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">why should he take away thy mishkav (bed) from under thee?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEVARIM 27:17; DEVARIM 19:14; MISHLE 23:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Remove not the gevul olam (ancient landmark),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">which avoteicha have set.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:28; MATTITYAHU 25:21; MISHLE 12:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Seest thou an ish mahir (diligent, one who excells) in his work?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He shall stand before melachim;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he shall not stand before obscure men.</span></span>
