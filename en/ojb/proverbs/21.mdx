<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:22; HOSHEA 6:6; MICHOH 6:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">To do tzedakah and mishpat</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">is more acceptable to Hashem than zevach.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MISHLE 6:17; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:5; YESHAYAH 2:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">A high and haughty eye,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and a proud levner resha'im (the lamp of the wicked ones) — are chattat (sin).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MISHLE 10:4; MISHLE 13:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">The plans of the kharutz (diligent) lead surely to profit;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but of every one that is hasty surely to machsor (poverty, want).</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MISHLE 2:15; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:29; TEHILLIM 14:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The derech of the guilty ish is hafachpach (crooked, tortuous),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but as for the pure, his conduct is yashar (right).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MISHLE 19:13; MISHLE 21:19; MISHLE 27:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is better to dwell in a corner of the gag (roof),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">than with a quarrelsome isha in a shared bais.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MICHOH 3:2–3; MISHLE 3:29; TEHILLIM 36:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">The nefesh of the rashah desireth rah;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">his re'a findeth no mercy in his eynayim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MISHLE 19:25; MISHLE 18:15; MISHLE 9:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the scoffer is punished, the simple is made wise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and when insight is disclosed to the chacham, he receiveth da'as.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MISHLE 14:11; TEHILLIM 37:35–36; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The tzaddik (righteous one) wisely considereth the bais of the rashah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and hurleth the resha'im to rah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MISHLE 28:27; MATTITYAHU 6:14; MISHLE 1:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who stoppeth his ozen at the cry of the dal (poor),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he also shall cry out himself, but shall not be heard.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MISHLE 18:16; MISHLE 19:6; MISHLE 17:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">A mattan (gift) in secret pacifieth af (anger),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and a shochad (bribe) in the kheyk (bosom),</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., a concealed bribe">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">strong wrath.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MISHLE 10:29; MATTITYAHU 7:23; MATTITYAHU 13:41–42"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is simchah to the tzaddik to do mishpat,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but terror for the po'alei aven (workers of iniquity).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:38; TEHILLIM 125:5; TEHILLIM 49:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">The adam that wandereth astray from the derech haseichel (road of understanding)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shall rest in the kahal of the refa'im (congregation of the dead).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MISHLE 23:21; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:6; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">The ohev simchah (lover of pleasure) shall be an ish machsor (man of poverty, want, shortage);</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he that loveth yayin and shemen shall not be rich.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MISHLE 11:8; YESHAYAH 43:3–4; YESHAYAH 53:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The reshah shall be a kofer (ransom) for the tzaddik,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the boged (the unfaithful) for the yesharim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MISHLE 21:9; TEHILLIM 120:5–6; TEHILLIM 55:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is better to dwell in eretz midbar,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">than with a quarrelsome and an angry isha.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:3–4; TEHILLIM 112:3; KOHELET 7:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is otzar to be desired and shemen in the naveh (dwelling) of the chacham;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but a kesil adam squanders it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:58; MISHLE 22:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that pursueth tzedakah and chesed</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">findeth chayyim, tzedakah, and kavod.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="KOHELET 7:19; MISHLE 24:5; KOHELET 9:13–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">A chacham scaleth the city of the gibborim (mighty ones),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and casteth down the trusted stronghold thereof.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MISHLE 13:3; MISHLE 18:21; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who is shomer over his peh (mouth) and his lashon (tongue)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">is shomer over his nefesh from tzoros.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="TEHILLIM 1:1; MISHLE 18:12; MISHLE 1:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Proud and haughty scoffer is shmo,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who acteth in superfluity of zadon (pride).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MISHLE 13:4; MISHLE 12:24; MATTITYAHU 25:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">The ta'avah (desire, craving) of the atzel (sluggard, lazy one) killeth him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for his yadayim refuse to work.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="TEHILLIM 37:26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:33–35; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">He craveth greedily kol hayom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the tzaddik giveth and spareth not.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YESHAYAH 66:3; MISHLE 15:8; YIRMEYAH 6:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">The zevach of the resha'im is to'evah (detestable, abomination);</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">how much more, when he bringeth it with zimmah (evil intent).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:13; MISHLE 19:5; MISHLE 19:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">An ed kezavim (lying witness) shall perish,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the ish that heareth &#91;emes&#93; speaketh lanetzach (unto eternity).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:11; MISHLE 11:5; CHAGGAI 2:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">An ish rasha defiantly hardeneth his face,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but as for the yashar (upright) he maketh his derech (road) sure.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MISHLE 19:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:39; YIRMEYAH 9:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is no chochmah nor tevunah (understanding)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor etzah against Hashem.</span><br /></span>
