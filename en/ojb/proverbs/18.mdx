<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:15–17; MISHLE 2:1–6; MISHLE 20:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">A man who separates himself seeks his own ta'avah (desire);</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he defies all tushiyyah (wisdom, sound judgment).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MISHLE 1:7; MISHLE 12:23; TEHILLIM 1:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">A kesil (fool) hath no delight in tevunah (understanding),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but only in airing out his lev (mind, opinions).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MISHLE 22:10; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:14; MISHLE 11:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the rashah cometh, then cometh also contempt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and with dishonor, cherpah (reproach, disgrace).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MISHLE 20:5; MISHLE 10:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The devarim of a manʼs mouth are deep mayim;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the makor chochmah is a flowing stream.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:15; DEVARIM 16:19; MISHLE 28:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is not tov to show partiality to the rashah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to subvert the tzaddik in mishpat.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MISHLE 27:3; MISHLE 16:27–28; MISHLE 22:24–25"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sfatayim (lips) of a kesil enter into contention,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and his peh (mouth) inviteth blows.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MISHLE 13:3; MISHLE 12:13; MISHLE 10:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">The peh (mouth) of a kesil is his undoing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and his sfatayim (lips) are the mokesh (snare) of his nefesh.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MISHLE 12:18; VAYIKRA 19:16; MISHLE 16:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">The devarim of a nirgan (gossip, talebearer, slanderer) are like mitlachamim (delicious tidbits),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they go down into the innermost parts of the beten (belly).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MISHLE 10:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:12; MISHLE 24:30–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">He also that is slack in his work</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">is brother to him that is ba'al mashchit (master of destruction).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 22:3; TEHILLIM 91:2; YESHAYAH 26:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Shem of Hashem is a strong migdal (tower),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the tzaddik runneth into it, and is safe.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MISHLE 10:15; TEHILLIM 52:5–7; KOHELET 7:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The oisherʼs wealth is his strong city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and is a high chomah in his own conceit.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MISHLE 15:33; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:5; YECHEZKEL 28:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Before shever (destruction) the lev ish is haughty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and before kavod is anavah (humility).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:51; DEVARIM 13:14; MISHLE 20:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that answereth a matter before he heareth it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it is folly and kelimmah (reproach, shame) unto him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MISHLE 15:13; MISHLE 17:22; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">The ruach ish will sustain him in sickness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but a crushed ruach who can bear?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5; MISHLE 15:14; MISHLE 1:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The lev of the prudent acquireth da'as,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the ozen of the chachamim seeketh da'as.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MISHLE 19:6; BERESHIS 43:11; MISHLE 17:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">The mattan adam (gift of a man) openeth the way for him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and bringeth him before gedolim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MISHLE 18:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:12–13; SHMUEL BAIS 19:24–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that is harishon (the first) to make his case seemeth tzaddik,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but his re'a cometh and doth cross-examine him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MISHLE 16:33; NECHEMYAH 11:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 24:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">The goral (lot) causeth contentions to cease,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and keepeth the mighty apart.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MISHLE 16:32; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:39; MISHLE 6:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">A brother offended is harder to be won</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">than a strong city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their contentious quarrelings are like the bars of an armon (citadel).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MISHLE 12:13–14; MISHLE 25:11–12; MISHLE 13:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">An ishʼs beten shall be filled with the fruit of his peh (mouth),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and with the produce of his sfatayim (lips) shall he be satisfied.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:29; MATTITYAHU 12:35–37; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Mavet and chayyim are in the power of the lashon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they that love it shall eat the p'ri (fruit) thereof.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MISHLE 19:14; MISHLE 31:10–31; MISHLE 12:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who findeth an isha (wife) findeth tov,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and obtaineth ratzon (favor) from Hashem.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:3; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9–11; RUTH 2:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The poor useth entreaties,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the oisher answereth roughly.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MISHLE 17:17; YOCHANAN 15:13–15; MISHLE 27:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">An ish of many companions may come to ruin,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but there is an ohev (friend) that sticketh closer than a brother.</span></span>
