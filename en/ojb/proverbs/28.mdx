<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:7; TEHILLIM 53:5; YESHAYAH 26:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">The rasha flee when none pursueth;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the tzaddikim are bold as a lion.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 15:28; MELACHIM ALEF 16:8–29; MELACHIM BAIS 15:8–31"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the peysha eretz (land in rebellion) many are the sarim (rulers) thereof,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but by an adam meiven yodei'a (a man of wisdom and knowledge)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shall be lasting order.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:28–30"> <span className="pl pl-1">A gever rahsh (poor man) that oppresseth the dalim (poor ones)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">is like a sweeping matar (rain) which leaveth no lechem (food, crop).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:32; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:11; MATTITYAHU 14:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">They that forsake the torah praise the rasha,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but such as be shomer over the torah contend against them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 92:6; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Anshei rah understand not mishpat,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but they that seek Hashem understand all things.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MISHLE 19:1; MISHLE 28:18; LUKAS 16:19–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Better is the rahsh (poor) that walketh in his integrity,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">than he that is perverse in his drakhim (ways), though he be rich.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:3–4; MISHLE 29:3; MISHLE 3:1–35"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who keepeth torah is a ben meiven,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that is a companion of zolelim (gluttons) shameth aviv.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="IYOV 27:16–17; MISHLE 13:22; KOHELET 2:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that by neshekh (interest) and increase increaseth his substance,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he shall gather it for him that will pity the dalim (poor ones).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 66:18; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:3–4; ZECHARYAH 7:11–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that turneth away his ozen from mishmo'a torah (heeding torah),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">even his tefillah (prayer) shall be to'evah (abomination).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MISHLE 26:27; TEHILLIM 37:25–26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who causeth the yesharim (upright ones) to go astray in a derech rah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he shall fall himself into his own trap,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the blameless ones shall inherit tov.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MISHLE 18:17; KOHELET 9:15–17; MISHLE 26:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The ish oisher is chacham in his own eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the dal meiven (poor man with understanding) searcheth him out.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MISHLE 29:2; MISHLE 11:10; MISHLE 28:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">When tzaddikim do triumph, there is rabbah tiferet (great glory),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but when the resha'im rise, the people go into hiding.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8–10; TEHILLIM 32:3–5; IYOV 31:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that covereth his peysha'im (sins) shall not prosper,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he who confesseth and forsaketh them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shall find mercy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MISHLE 23:17; TEHILLIM 2:11; YESHAYAH 66:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ashrei adam that is reverent always,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that hardeneth his lev shall fall into ra'ah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:8; MATTITYAHU 2:16; MISHLE 19:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">As a roaring ari, and a charging dov (bear),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so is a wicked moshel (ruler) over the am dal (poor people).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 18:21; YIRMEYAH 22:15–17; KOHELET 4:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">The nagid (ruler) lacking tevunot (understanding) is also a great oppressor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that hateth betza (ill-gotten gain) shall prolong his yamim (days).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 9:6; MELACHIM ALEF 21:19; SHEMOT 21:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">If an adam is burdened with dahm (blood, murder) of any nefesh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">let him be a fugitive ad bor (till the pit, death);</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">let no ish help him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MISHLE 10:9; MISHLE 28:6; HISGALUS 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who walketh tamim (blamelessly) shall be saved,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that is perverse in his drakhim shall fall suddenly.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MISHLE 12:11; SHOFETIM 9:4; MISHLE 27:23–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that tilleth his adamah shall have plenty of lechem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that chaseth after empty things shall have poverty enough.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKAS 16:10–12; MISHLE 13:11; HISGALUS 2:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">An ish emunot (faithful man, loyal man) shall abound with birkhot,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that maketh haste to be rich shall not go unpunished.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MISHLE 24:23; MISHLE 18:5; YECHEZKEL 13:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">To show partiality is not tov;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for a piece of lechem that gever will transgress.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MISHLE 23:6; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:9; MISHLE 28:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that hasteth to be rich hath a rah ayin,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and considereth not that want shall come upon him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MISHLE 27:5–6; MATTITYAHU 18:15; TEHILLIM 141:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that rebuketh an adam afterwards shall find more chen</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">(favor) than he that flattereth with the lashon.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:4–6; MISHLE 18:9; MISHLE 19:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who robbeth aviv or immo,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and saith, It is no peysha (transgression),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the same is the chaver (fellow, companion) of an ish mashchit (man of destruction).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MISHLE 11:25; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:6; MISHLE 13:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">The covetous stirreth up strife,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that putteth his trust in Hashem shall be prospered.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MISHLE 3:5; YIRMEYAH 17:9; IYOV 28:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that trusteth in his own lev is a kesil (fool),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he who walketh in chochmah, he shall be delivered.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MISHLE 19:17; DEVARIM 15:7; TEHILLIM 41:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that giveth unto the rahsh (poor) shall not lack,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that hideth his eynayim meeteth many curses.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MISHLE 29:2; IYOV 24:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:23–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the resha'im rise, adam hide themselves,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but when they perish, the tzaddikim increase.</span></span>
