<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEVARIM 25:13–16; MISHLE 16:11; MISHLE 20:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">A balance of mirmah (dishonesty, deception) is abomination to Hashem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but an even shelemah (perfect stone, just weight) is His ratzon.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MISHLE 29:23; MISHLE 15:33; MISHLE 16:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">When zadon (pride) cometh, then cometh kalon (shame, disgrace),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but with the humble is chochmah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MISHLE 13:6; MISHLE 28:18; TEHILLIM 26:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">The integrity of the yesharim (upright ones) shall guide them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the deceit of the bogedim (treacherous ones) shall destroy them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="TZEFANYAH 1:18; MISHLE 10:2; BERESHIS 7:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hon (riches) profit not in the yom evrah (day of wrath),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but tzedakah (righteousness) delivereth from mavet.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MISHLE 5:22; TEHILLIM 9:15–16; MISHLE 1:31–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">The tzidkat tamim (righteousness of the blameless) shall make his derech straight,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the rasha shall fall by his own evil.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="KOHELET 10:8; MELACHIM ALEF 2:44; TEHILLIM 7:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The tzidkat yesharim shall deliver them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but bogedim (treacherous ones) shall be caught in their own evil desire.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MISHLE 10:28; IYOV 8:13–14; IYOV 11:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">At the mot adam rasha, his tikveh shall perish,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the expectation of his</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="surviving">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">strong ones perisheth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DANIEL 6:23–24; MISHLE 21:18; ESTHER 7:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">The tzaddik is delivered out of tzoros,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the rashah cometh on to take his place.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MISHLE 2:10–16; MISHLE 4:5–6; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">A chanef (hypocrite, man without G-d) with his peh (mouth) destroyeth his re'a,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but through da'as (knowledge) shall the tzaddikim be delivered.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MISHLE 28:12; ESTHER 8:15–16; TEHILLIM 58:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">When it goeth well with the tzaddikim, the city rejoiceth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and when the resha'im perish, there is rinnah (joyful shouting).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MISHLE 14:34; MISHLE 29:8; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">By the birkat yesharim the city is exalted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but it is overthrown by the peh (mouth) of the resha'im.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MISHLE 10:19; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:23; LUKAS 18:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that is devoid of lev</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="of understanding">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">despiseth his re'a,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but an ish tevunot holdeth his peace.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MISHLE 20:19; MISHLE 25:9; VAYIKRA 19:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">A bringer of rekhil (gossip),</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="rekhilut">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">revealeth sod (secrets, confidences),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that is of a ne'eman ruach concealeth the matter.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MISHLE 24:6; MISHLE 15:22; MISHLE 20:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Where no strategic policy is, the nation falls,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but in the multitude of yoetz (counsellors, advisors) there is teshu'ah (victory, safety).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MISHLE 17:18; MISHLE 6:1–5; MISHLE 22:26–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that is guaranty for a zar shall suffer for it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he that hateth pledging collateral is secure.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MISHLE 31:30–31; LUKAS 10:42; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:14–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">An eshet chen retaineth kavod (honor, respect),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but ruthless men retain osher.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:7; MATTITYAHU 25:34–40; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The ish chesed (merciful man) doeth good to his own nefesh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that is cruel harmeth his own self.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:8–9; KOHELET 10:8; HOSHEA 10:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The reshah worketh a wage of sheker,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but to him that soweth tzedakah (righteousness) shall be a sachar emes (sure reward).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MISHLE 10:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:8–9; MISHLE 12:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">As tzedakah (righteousness) leadeth to Chayyim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so he that pursueth ra'ah pursueth it to his own mot (death).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:1; MISHLE 21:29; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">They that are of a perverse lev are an abomination to Hashem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but such as are upright in their derech are His delight.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="TEHILLIM 112:1–2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:39; YIRMEYAH 32:39"> <span className="pl pl-1">Though yad join in yad,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">rah shall not go unpunished,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the zera of the tzaddikim shall be delivered.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:3–4; MISHLE 31:30; NACHUM 3:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">As a ring of zahav in the snout of a chazir,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so is an isha yafah which is without ta'am (judgment, discretion).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="TEHILLIM 37:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:8–9; MATTITYAHU 5:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The ta'avat tzaddikim is only tov,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the tikveh of the resha'im is wrath.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MISHLE 19:17; LUKAS 6:38; DEVARIM 15:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is that which scattereth, and yet increaseth more;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and there is that which withholdeth more than is yosher, but it leadeth to machsor (want, need, poverty).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:7; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:6–9; YESHAYAH 58:7–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The nefesh berakhah (generous person) shall be made to prosper,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he that watereth shall be refreshed also himself.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="IYOV 29:13; BERESHIS 42:6; AMOS 8:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that withholdeth grain, the people shall curse him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but brocha shall be upon the head of him that selleth it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="TEHILLIM 7:15–16; MISHLE 17:11; TEHILLIM 9:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that diligently seeketh tov procureth ratzon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that seeketh ra'ah, it shall come unto him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:17; YIRMEYAH 17:8; TEHILLIM 52:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that trusteth in his riches shall fall;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the tzaddik shall flourish like the green leaf.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="KOHELET 5:16; MISHLE 14:19; MISHLE 15:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that troubleth his own bais shall inherit the ruach (wind),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the fool shall be eved to the chacham lev.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DANIEL 12:3; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:19–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">The p'ri tzedek is Etz Chayyim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he that winneth nefashot is chacham (wise).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:17–18; YIRMEYAH 25:29; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:30–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">If the tzaddik are recompensed on ha'aretz (the earth),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">much more the rashah and the choteh (sinner).</span></span>
