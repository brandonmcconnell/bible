<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MISHLE 26:5; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Answer not a kesil (fool) according to his folly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">lest thou also be like unto him.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MISHLE 10:26; MISHLE 25:13; MISHLE 13:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that sendeth a message by the yad of a kesil (fool)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">cutteth off the raglayim, and drinketh chamas.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MISHLE 26:9; TEHILLIM 64:8; MISHLE 17:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The legs of the pisei'ach (lame man) hang limp;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so is a mashal in the peh (mouth) of kesilim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MISHLE 26:1; MISHLE 30:22; MISHLE 19:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">As he that bindeth an even (stone) in a sling,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so is he that giveth kavod to a kesil (fool).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MISHLE 23:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">As a thornbush goeth up into the yad of a shikkor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so is a mashal in the peh (mouth) of kesilim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:6; MISHLE 11:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Like an archer wounding all,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so is he that hireth the kesil (fool), or hireth the passerby.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:22; MATTITYAHU 12:45; SHEMOT 8:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">As a kelev returneth to his vomit,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so a kesil (fool) returneth to his folly.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MISHLE 29:20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:18–19; MISHLE 26:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Seest thou an ish chacham in his own eyes?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There is more tikvah (hope) for a kesil (fool) than for him.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MISHLE 19:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The atzel (sluggard, lazy one) hideth his yad in his dish;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">itʼs too tiring to bring it back to his peh (mouth) again.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MISHLE 25:18; YESHAYAH 50:11; MISHLE 7:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">As a mad man who shooteth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">firebrands, khitzim (arrows), and mavet,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:4; MISHLE 10:23; MISHLE 15:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">So is the ish that deceiveth his re'a,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and saith, Am I not just having a laugh?</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MISHLE 15:18; MISHLE 29:22; SHMUEL BAIS 20:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">As charcoal is to burning coals, and wood to eish;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so is a contentious ish to kindle strife.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKAS 22:47–48; LUKAS 11:39; MISHLE 10:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Fervent sfatayim (lips) and a lev rah</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">are like earthenware covered with silver dross.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MISHLE 12:20; MISHLE 10:18; TEHILLIM 41:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that hateth disguises it with his sfatayim (lips),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and within harboreth mirmah (deceit);</span><br /></span>
