<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MISHLE 15:17; TEHILLIM 37:16; MISHLE 21:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Better is a dry crust, and quietness therewith,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">than a bais full of zevakhim (sacrificial religious meals) with strife.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MISHLE 10:5; KOHELET 4:13; MISHLE 14:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">An eved maskil (wise servant) shall have rule over a ben that causeth shame,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and shall have a share of the nachalah among the achim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YESHAYAH 48:10; YIRMEYAH 17:10; TEHILLIM 26:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">The crucible is for kesef, and the furnace for zahav,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but Hashem is the testor of hearts.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:3–4; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:5; MISHLE 28:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">An evildoer giveth heed to false sfatayim (lips),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and a sheker (liar) giveth ear to a spiteful lashon.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MISHLE 14:31; IYOV 31:29; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who mocketh the poor showeth contempt for his Oseh (Maker),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he that gloats at calamities shall not go unpunished.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 127:3–5; MISHLE 13:22; MISHLE 16:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Bnei banim are the ateret of zekenim;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the tiferet of banim are their avot.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MISHLE 29:12; MISHLE 12:19; TEHILLIM 50:16–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Eloquent speech becometh not a naval (fool),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">much less do lying sfatayim (lips) a ruler.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:8; MICHOH 7:3; SHMUEL ALEF 25:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">A shochad (gift, bribe) is like a charm in the eynayim of him that hath it:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">wherever he turneth, he prospereth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MISHLE 10:12; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:8; MISHLE 16:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that covereth a peysha (transgression) seeketh love;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that repeateth a matter separateth the best of friends.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:19; MISHLE 9:8–9; MISHLE 15:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ge'arah (scolding) goeth deeper into a meiven</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">than a hundred blows into a kesil (fool).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 2:46; SHMUEL BAIS 16:5–9; SHMUEL BAIS 18:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">A rah seeketh only rebellion;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">therefore a merciless malach shall be sent to him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HOSHEA 13:8; SHMUEL BAIS 17:8; MISHLE 28:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let an ish meet a dov robbed of her cubs</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">rather than a kesil in his folly.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TEHILLIM 109:4–13; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who rewardeth ra'ah for tovah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">ra'ah shall not depart from his bais.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MISHLE 20:3; MISHLE 25:8; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:23–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The beginning of madon (strife) is like releasing mayim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">therefore stop contention before a quarrel begins.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:7; MISHLE 18:5; YESHAYAH 5:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that justifieth the rashah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he that condemneth the tzaddik,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">even they both are to'evah to Hashem.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MISHLE 21:25–26; MISHLE 23:23; MISHLE 18:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why is there the purchase price in the yad of a kesil (fool) to acquire chochmah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">seeing he hath no lev for it?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MISHLE 18:24; YOCHANAN 15:13–14; SHMUEL ALEF 20:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">The re'a (friend) loveth at all times,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and a brother is born for tzarah (adversity).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MISHLE 11:15; MISHLE 6:1–5; MISHLE 22:26–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Adam devoid of lev</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="of understanding">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shaketh hands in pledge,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and becometh the security deposit for his neighbor.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MISHLE 16:18; MISHLE 18:12; SHMUEL ALEF 25:36–38"> <span className="pl pl-1">He loveth peysha (transgression, sin) that loveth strife,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he that maketh his threshold high seeketh shever (fracture).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:6–8; MISHLE 10:31; MISHLE 6:12–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that hath a crooked mind findeth no tov,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he that hath a deceitful lashon falleth into ra'ah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MISHLE 10:1; MISHLE 19:13; MISHLE 17:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that begetteth a kesil doeth it to his sorrow,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the avi naval hath no simchah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MISHLE 15:13; MISHLE 12:25; MISHLE 18:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">A lev same'ach doeth tov like a medicine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but a downcast ruach drieth up the bones.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MISHLE 17:8; DEVARIM 16:19; SHEMOT 23:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">A rasha accepts a shochad (bribe) out of the kheyk (bosom),</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., secretly">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to pervert the orkhot mishpat (paths of justice).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="KOHELET 2:14; KOHELET 8:1; MISHLE 14:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Chochmah is before the meiven (discerning one);</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the eynayim of a kesil are on the ends of ha'aretz.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MISHLE 10:1; SHMUEL BAIS 13:1–22; MISHLE 19:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">A ben kesil is a ka'as (grief) to aviv (his father),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and bitterness to her that bore him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MISHLE 17:15; MISHLE 18:5; YOCHANAN 18:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Also to punish the tzaddik is not tov,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor to flog officials for their yashar (uprightness, integrity).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19; MISHLE 10:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that hath da'as spareth his words,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and an ish tevunah (man of understanding) is of a calm ruach.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="IYOV 13:5; MISHLE 15:2; KOHELET 10:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even a fool, when he holdeth his peace, is counted chacham,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he that shutteth his sfatayim (lips) is esteemed a man of understanding.</span></span>
