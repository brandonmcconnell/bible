<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MISHLE 15:5; MISHLE 9:7–8; MISHLE 15:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">A ben chacham heareth his musar av,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but a scoffer heareth not ge'arah (scolding, rebuke).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MISHLE 12:14; MISHLE 18:20; MISHLE 10:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">An ish shall eat tov by the fruit of his peh (mouth),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the nefesh of the bogedim (unfaithful ones, traitors) shall eat chamas.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MISHLE 21:23; MISHLE 18:21; MISHLE 18:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that is shomer over his peh (mouth) guards his nefesh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that openeth wide his sfatayim (lips) shall have ruin.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MISHLE 12:24; MISHLE 12:11; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The nefesh of the atzel (sluggard, lazy one) desireth, and hath nothing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the nefesh of the kharutzim (diligent ones) shall have ample provision.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:25; TEHILLIM 119:163"> <span className="pl pl-1">A tzaddik hateth sheker,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but a wicked man is shamefully loathsome, and bringeth disgrace.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MISHLE 11:3; MISHLE 11:5–6; TEHILLIM 140:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Tzedakah is guard over him whose derech is blameless,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but wickedness overthroweth the chattat (sinner).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKAS 18:11–14; HISGALUS 3:17; MISHLE 11:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is one who maketh himself out to be rich, yet hath nothing;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there is one that maketh himself out to be poor, yet hath hon rav (great wealth).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:26; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:18–19; TEHILLIM 49:6–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">The riches of an ish is kofer (ransom) for his nefesh (life),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the poor heareth no threat.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MISHLE 4:18; MISHLE 24:20; MISHLE 20:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">The ohr (light) of the tzaddik rejoiceth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the ner (lamp) of the resha'im (wicked) shall be put out.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MISHLE 19:20; MISHLE 12:15–16; MISHLE 17:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">By zadon (pride) cometh only contention,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but with the teachable is chochmah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MISHLE 20:21; MISHLE 10:2; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:1–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hon (wealth) gotten by dishonesty shall be diminished,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that gathereth incrementally shall have an increment.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:22; MISHLE 13:19; TEHILLIM 119:81–83"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hope deferred maketh the lev sick,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but when the ta'avah (desire) cometh, it is an Etz Chayyim (Tree of Life).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 15:31; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:16; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who despiseth the davar shall be destroyed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that feareth the mitzvah shall be rewarded.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 18:5; MISHLE 10:11; MISHLE 14:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">The torat chacham (teaching of the wise) is a makor chayyim (fountain of life),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to turn one away from the snares of mavet.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 2:19; MISHLE 4:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Seichel tov giveth favor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the derech bogedim (way of traitors) is hard.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MISHLE 15:2; MISHLE 12:22–23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Every prudent man acts with da'as,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but a kesil (fool) displays his folly.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MISHLE 25:13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:2; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">A malach rasha falleth into rah (trouble),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but a tzir emunim (trustworthy envoy) is marpeh (healing).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MISHLE 15:5; MISHLE 12:1; MISHLE 15:31–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Poverty and shame shall be to him that disdains musar,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that is shomer over tokhakhat (reproof) shall be honored.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="HISGALUS 7:14–17; MISHLE 13:12; MISHLE 29:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">The ta'avah (desire, longing) fulfilled is sweet to the nefesh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but it is to'evat kesilim (the abomination of fools) to depart from rah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:33–34; MISHLE 15:31; MISHLE 9:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that walketh with chachamim shall be wise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but a companion of kesilim (fools) shall come to harm.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="TEHILLIM 32:10; YESHAYAH 3:10–11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:7–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ra'ah (disaster) pursueth chatta'im (sinners),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but tov is the reward of the tzaddikim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="KOHELET 2:26; IYOV 27:16–17; MISHLE 28:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">A tov (good man) leaveth an inheritance to his bnei banim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the wealth of the choteh (sinner) is laid up for the tzaddik.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MISHLE 12:11; MISHLE 28:19; KOHELET 5:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Rav ochel (much food) is in the untilled ground of the poor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but for want of mishpat (justice) it is swept away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MISHLE 29:17; MISHLE 23:13–14; MISHLE 29:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that spareth his shevet (rod) hateth bno (his son),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that loveth him is diligent in his musar (discipline).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="TEHILLIM 34:10; MISHLE 10:3; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The tzaddik (righteous) eateth to the satisfying of his nefesh (soul),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the beten (stomach) of the resha'im shall want.</span></span>
