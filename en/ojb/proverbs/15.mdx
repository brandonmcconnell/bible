<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:13; YIRMEYAH 16:17; YIRMEYAH 23:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The eynayim of Hashem are in kol makom (every place),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">watching the ra'im and the tovim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MISHLE 12:18; MISHLE 16:24; MISHLE 18:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">A marpeh lashon (a tongue of healing) is an Etz Chayyim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but selef (perversity) therein is crushing to the ruach (spirit).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MISHLE 15:31–32; MISHLE 13:1; MISHLE 13:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">A fool spurns his musar av,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that is shomer over tokhakhat (reproof) is prudent.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MISHLE 8:21; MISHLE 21:20; MISHLE 10:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the bais of the tzaddik is much treasure,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but in the revenues of the rashah is trouble.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:34; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sfatayim (lips) of chachamim disperse da'as,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the lev of kesilim doeth not so.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MISHLE 15:29; MISHLE 21:27; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">The zevach of the resha'im is a to'evah to Hashem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the tefillah (prayer) of the yesharim is His delight.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:11; MISHLE 21:21; MATTITYAHU 7:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The derech rashah is a to'evah unto Hashem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but He loveth him that pursueth tzedakah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MISHLE 12:1; YOCHANAN 3:20; MISHLE 13:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Musar is grievous unto him that forsaketh the orach (way),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he that hateth tokhakhat (reproof) shall die.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TEHILLIM 44:21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:13; TEHILLIM 139:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sheol and Avaddon are before Hashem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">how much more then the hearts of Bnei Adam?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="AMOS 5:10; IYOV 21:14; MISHLE 9:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">A scoffer loveth not one that correcteth him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">neither will he go unto chachamim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MISHLE 17:22; YOCHANAN 14:1; MISHLE 18:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">A lev same'ach maketh a cheerful countenance,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but by sorrow of the lev the ruach is broken.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MISHLE 9:9; YESHAYAH 30:10; MISHLE 18:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">The lev of him that hath understanding seeketh da'as,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the peh (mouth) of kesilim feedeth on folly.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:12; MISHLE 15:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the days of the oni are ra'im,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that is of a tov lev hath a mishteh tamid (a continual feast).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MISHLE 16:8; TEHILLIM 37:16; KOHELET 5:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Better is little with the Yirat Hashem</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">than otzar rav (great treasure) and trouble therewith.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MISHLE 17:1; MISHLE 21:19; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Better is a meal of yarak (vegetables) where love is,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">than a fatted calf and sinah (hatred) therewith.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MISHLE 15:1; MISHLE 26:21; MISHLE 29:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">An ish chemah (angry man) stirreth up madon (strife),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that is slow to anger pacifies a quarrel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 35:8; MISHLE 22:5; YESHAYAH 30:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">The derech (road) of the atzel (sluggard, lazy one) is a thorny hedge,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the orach yesharim (way of the upright) is a highway.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MISHLE 29:3; MISHLE 10:1; MISHLE 23:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">A ben chacham maketh glad an av,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but a kesil adam despiseth immo.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:15; MISHLE 10:23; MISHLE 14:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Folly is simchah to him that is destitute of lev,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="of discernment">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but an ish tevunah walketh uprightly.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MISHLE 11:14; MISHLE 20:18; KOHELET 8:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Without counsel, plans go amiss,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but in the multitude of yo'atzim (counsellors) they are established.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YESHAYAH 50:4; MISHLE 25:11–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">An ish hath simchah in the apt reply of his peh (mouth),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and a timely davar, mah tov (how good!)</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:1–2; TEHILLIM 16:11; MATTITYAHU 7:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Orach Chayyim leads upward to the wise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in order that he may depart from Sheol beneath.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="TEHILLIM 146:9; TEHILLIM 68:5–6; MISHLE 14:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hashem will destroy the bais of the proud,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he will maintain the boundary of the almanah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MISHLE 6:16–19; YIRMEYAH 4:14; MISHLE 15:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">The thoughts of the rah are a to'evah to Hashem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the words of the tehorim (pure ones) are pleasant.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:8; MISHLE 1:19; YIRMEYAH 17:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that is greedy of unjust gain troubleth his own bais,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that hateth mattanot (bribes) shall live.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:15; MISHLE 29:11; KOHELET 5:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">The lev tzaddik studieth how to answer,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the peh (mouth) of the resha'im poureth out evil things.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:31; TEHILLIM 145:18–19; MISHLE 15:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hashem is far from the resha'im,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but He heareth the tefillah of the tzaddikim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YESHAYAH 58:11; HISGALUS 22:5; HISGALUS 21:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">The ohr (light) of the eynayim rejoiceth the lev,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and a good report giveth health to the etzem.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MISHLE 15:5; MISHLE 25:12; YOCHANAN 15:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">The ozen that heareth the tokhakhat chayyim (reproof of life)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">lodgeth among chachamim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MISHLE 1:24–33; HISGALUS 3:19; MISHLE 1:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that refuseth musar despiseth his own nefesh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he that heareth tokhakhat (reproof) getteth lev.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="of understanding">b</a></sup><br /></span>
