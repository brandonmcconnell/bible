<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 5:24; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:5; MELACHIM ALEF 19:16–21"> And it came to pass, when Hashem would take up Eliyahu into Shomayim in a se'arah (storm wind, whirlwind), that Eliyahu went with Elishah from Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 2:6; RUTH 1:15–18; MELACHIM BAIS 4:30"> And Eliyahu said unto Elishah, Tarry here, now; for Hashem hath sent me to Beit-El. And Elishah said unto him, As Hashem liveth, and as thy nefesh liveth, I will not leave thee. So they went down to Beit-El.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:1; MELACHIM BAIS 4:38; MELACHIM BAIS 9:1"> And the Bnei HaNevi'im that were at Beit-El came forth to Elishah, and said unto him, Doest thou have da'as that Hashem will take away thy adon from over thy rosh hayom? And he said, Yes, I have da'as; be silent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 2:2; YEHOSHUA 6:26; MELACHIM ALEF 16:34"> And Eliyahu said unto him, Elishah, tarry here, now; for Hashem hath sent me to Yericho. And he said, As Hashem liveth, and as thy nefesh liveth, I will not leave thee. So they came to Yericho.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 2:3; YEHOSHUA 1:1–2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:11"> And the Bnei HaNevi'im that were at Yericho came to Elishah, and said unto him, Doest thou have da'as that hayom (today) Hashem will take away thy adon from over thy rosh? And he answered, Yes, I have da'as; be silent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 2:2; YEHOSHUA 3:15"> And Eliyahu said unto him, Tarry, now, here; for Hashem hath sent me to the Yarden. And he said, As Hashem liveth, and as thy nefesh liveth, I will not leave thee. And they two walked on.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:13; MELACHIM BAIS 2:17; MELACHIM ALEF 18:4"> And chamishim ish (fifty men) of the Bnei HaNevi'im went, standing opposite them at a distance; and they two stood by the Yarden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 19:19; SHEMOT 14:21–22; YEHOSHUA 3:14–17"> And Eliyahu took his aderet (mantle, cloak), and rolled it up, and struck the mayim, and they were split from here and from there, so that they two crossed over on charavah (dry ground).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Josh 3:17">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 11:17; DEVARIM 21:17; YOCHANAN 16:7"> And it came to pass, when they were crossed over, that Eliyahu said unto Elishah, Request what I shall do for thee, before I am taken away from thee. And Elishah said, Let, now, a double portion of thy ruach be upon me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MARKOS 11:22–24; MELACHIM BAIS 2:12; YOCHANAN 16:24"> And he said, Thou hast asked a hard thing; nevertheless, if thou see me when lukach (I am taken away) from thee, it shall be so unto thee; but if not, it shall not be so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 6:17; TEHILLIM 104:3–4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:14"> And it came to pass, as they still walked on, and talked, that, hinei, there appeared a merkavah of eish, and susim of eish, and separated the two of them between them; and Eliyahu went up in a se'arah (storm wind, whirlwind) into Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 13:14; YESHAYAH 37:21; MARKOS 16:19"> And Elishah saw, and he cried, Avi, Avi, the merkavah (war chariot) of Yisroel, and the parash (horsemen, charioteers) thereof. And he saw him no more; and he took hold of his own robe, and tore his apparel in two pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 19:19; MELACHIM BAIS 2:8; MELACHIM ALEF 9:26"> He took up also the aderet Eliyahu that fell from him, and went back, and stood by the bank of the Yarden;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:12; TEHILLIM 42:10; TEHILLIM 115:2"> And he took the aderet Eliyahu that fell from him, and struck the mayim, and said, Where is Hashem Elohei Eliyahu? And when he also had struck the mayim, they split from here and from there; and Elishah crossed over.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:8; YEHOSHUA 3:7"> And when the Bnei HaNevi'im which were watching at Yericho saw him from a distance, they said, The ruach Eliyahu doth rest on Elishah. And they came to meet him, and bowed themselves to the ground before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:39; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:2–3"> And they said unto him, Hinei now, there be among thy avadim chamishim anashim bnei chayil (fifty able-bodied men); let them go, now, and look for thy adon; perhaps the Ruach Hashem hath taken him up, and set him down<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Ac 8:39-40">b</a></sup>upon one of the harim, or in one of the geya'ot (valleys). And he said, Ye shall not send.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 8:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:2"> And when they urged him until he was ashamed, he said, Send. They sent therefore chamishim ish; and they searched shloshah yamim, but found him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And when they returned to him (for he tarried at Yericho), he said unto them, Did I not say unto you, Go not?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:26; DEVARIM 28:2–4; DEVARIM 28:11"> And the anshei HaIr said unto Elishah, Hinei, now, the situation of this city is tov, as adoni seeth; but the mayim is ra'im, and ha'aretz (the land) meshakkalet (barren, unfruitful).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHOFETIM 9:45; TZEFANYAH 2:9; YECHEZKEL 47:11"> And he said, Bring me a tzelokhit chadasha (new bowl), and put melach therein. And they brought it to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 15:25–26; MELACHIM BAIS 4:41; VAYIKRA 2:13"> And he went forth unto the motza hamayim (spring of water), and cast the melach in there, and said, Thus saith Hashem, I have healed these mayim; there shall not be from there any more mavet or meshakkalet (unfruitfulness, barrenness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> So the mayim were healed unto hayom hazeh (this day), according to the devar Elishah which he spoke.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:16; IYOV 19:18; IYOV 30:1"> And he went up from there unto Beit-El; and as he was going up along the derech, there came forth ne'arim ketannim out of the Ir, and jeered in mockery at him, and said unto him, Go on up, thou kere'ach (bald head); go on up, thou kere'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:9–11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:5; HOSHEA 13:8"> And he turned around, and looked on them, and cursed them in the Shem Hashem. And there came forth two dubim (female bears) out of the woods, and mauled 42 yeladim of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:25; MELACHIM ALEF 18:19–20; MELACHIM ALEF 18:42"> And he went from there to Mt Carmel, and from there he returned to Shomron.</span>
</div>
