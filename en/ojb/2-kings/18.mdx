<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:27–1; MATTITYAHU 1:9–10; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:13"> Now it came to pass in the third year of Hoshea Ben Elah Melech Yisroel, that Chizkiyah Ben Achaz Melech Yehudah began to reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:1–2"> Twenty and five years old was he when he began to reign; and he reigned twenty and nine shanah in Yerushalayim. Shem immo also was Avi Bat Zecharyah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 20:3; MELACHIM ALEF 11:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 31:20–21"> And he did that which was yashar in the sight of Hashem, according to all that Dovid Aviv did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 31:1; BAMIDBAR 21:8–9; MELACHIM BAIS 18:22"> He removed the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="idolatrous">a</a></sup>high places and smashed the matzevot, and cut down the Asherah, and broke in pieces the nachash nechoshet that Moshe had made; for up to those yamim the Bnei Yisroel did burn ketoret to it; and it was called Nechushtan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 23:25; MELACHIM BAIS 19:10; MATTITYAHU 27:43"> He trusted in Hashem Elohei Yisroel; so that after him was none like him among all the Melachim of Yehudah, nor any that were before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 23:8; DEVARIM 10:20; YOCHANAN 14:15"> For his deveykus was to Hashem, he departed not from following Him, but was shomer over His mitzvot Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:31; BERESHIS 39:2–3; MELACHIM BAIS 16:7"> And Hashem was with him; he prospered whithersoever he went forth; he rebelled against Melech Ashur, and served him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YESHAYAH 14:29; MELACHIM BAIS 17:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:18"> He struck down the Pelishtim, even unto Azah (Gaza), and the territories thereof, from the migdal notzrim to the fortified city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HOSHEA 10:14; MELACHIM BAIS 17:3–23; MELACHIM BAIS 18:1"> And it came to pass in the fourth year of Melech Chizkiyah, which was the seventh year of Hoshea Ben Elah Melech Yisroel, that Shalmaneser Melech Ashur came up against Shomron, and besieged it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="AMOS 6:7; AMOS 9:1–4; MICHOH 1:6–9"> And at the end of three years they captured it; even in the sixth year of Chizkiyah, that is, in the ninth year of Hoshea Melech Yisroel, Shomron was captured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:26; MELACHIM BAIS 17:6; YESHAYAH 37:12"> And Melech Ashur did carry away Yisroel into Golus in Assyria, and put them in Chalach and in Chavor by the of Gozan River, and in the cities of the Medes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:17; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:8; MICHOH 3:4"> Because they obeyed not the voice of Hashem Eloheihem, but transgressed His Brit, and all that Moshe Eved Hashem commanded, and would not hear them, nor do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YESHAYAH 36:1–22; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:1–23; YESHAYAH 7:17–25"> Now in the fourteenth year of Melech Chizkiyah did Sancheriv Melech Ashur come up against all the fortified cities of Yehudah, and captured them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:7; LUKAS 14:32; MELACHIM ALEF 20:4"> And Chizkiyah Melech Yehudah sent to Melech Ashur at Lakhish, saying, I have offended; withdraw from me; that which thou demandest me will I bear. And Melech Ashur exacted from Chizkiyah Melech Yehudah three hundred talents of kesef and thirty talents of zahav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 16:8; MELACHIM ALEF 15:18–19; MELACHIM BAIS 12:18"> And Chizkiyah gave him all the kesef that was found in the Beis Hashem, and in the otzarot of the Bais HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:3; MELACHIM ALEF 6:31–35"> At that time did Chizkiyah strip off the zahav from the daletot Heikhal Hashem, and from the omenot (doorposts) which Chizkiyah Melech Yehudah had overlaid, and gave it to Melech Ashur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YESHAYAH 20:1; MELACHIM BAIS 20:20; YESHAYAH 7:3"> And Melech Ashur sent Tartan and Rav Saris and Rav Shakeh from Lakhish to Melech Chizkiyah with a large army against Yerushalayim. And they went up and came to Yerushalayim. And when they were come up, they came and stood by the te'alat haberekhah haelyonah (aqueduct of the Upper Pool), on the highway of the Sadeh of the Launderer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 19:2; YESHAYAH 36:3; SHMUEL BAIS 8:16–17"> And when they had called to HaMelech, there came out to them Elyakim Ben Chilkiyah, which was over the Bais (palace), and Shevna the Sofer, and Yoach Ben Asaph the Mazkir (secretary).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 36:4; MELACHIM BAIS 19:10; YESHAYAH 10:8–14"> And Rav Shakeh said unto them, Speak ye now to Chizkiyah, Thus saith HaMelech Hagadol, Melech Ashur; What bitachon (confidence) is this wherein thou trustest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MISHLE 21:30–31"> Thou sayest — but they are mere davar sfatayim (words of lips, vain words) — I have etzah (counsel) and gevurah (strength) for the milchamah. Now on whom dost thou trust, that thou rebellest against me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YESHAYAH 30:7; YECHEZKEL 29:6–7; YESHAYAH 30:2–3"> Now, hinei, thou trustest upon the mishenet (staff) of this bruised reed, even upon Mitzrayim, on which if a man lean, it will go into his yad, and pierce it; so is Pharaoh Melech Mitzrayim unto all that depend on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 31:1; MELACHIM BAIS 18:4–5; MATTITYAHU 27:43"> But if ye say unto me, We trust in Hashem Eloheinu; is not that He, whose high places and whose mizbechot Chizkiyah hath taken away, and hath said to Yehudah and Yerushalayim, Ye shall worship before this Mizbe'ach in Yerushalayim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:42; MELACHIM ALEF 20:10; NECHEMYAH 4:2–5"> Therefore, now, give pledges to adoni Melech Ashur, and I will deliver thee two thousand susim, if thou be able on thy part to set riders upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YESHAYAH 10:8; DEVARIM 17:16; YESHAYAH 36:6"> How then wilt thou turn away the face of one officer of the least of the avadim of adoni, and put thy trust in Mitzrayim for chariots and for parashim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 19:6; YESHAYAH 10:5–6; MELACHIM BAIS 19:22–37"> Am I now come up without Hashem against this makom to destroy it? Hashem said to me, Go up against this land, and destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EZRA 4:7; DANIEL 2:4; YESHAYAH 36:11–12"> Then said Elyakim Ben Chilkiyah, and Shevna, and Yoach, unto Rav Shakeh, Speak, now, to thy avadim in Aramit (Aramaic); for we understand it; and speak not to us in Yehudit (Hebrew) in the ears of the people that are on the chomah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="TEHILLIM 73:8; EKHAH 4:5; YECHEZKEL 4:15"> But Rav Shakeh said unto them, Hath adoni sent me to adoneicha, and to thee, to speak these words? Hath he not sent me to the anashim which sit on the chomah, that may eat their own dung, and drink their own urine with you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YESHAYAH 36:13–18; YESHAYAH 10:8–13; YECHEZKEL 29:3"> Then Rav Shakeh stood and cried with a kol gadol in Yehudit, and spoke, saying, Hear the word of HaMelech Hagadol, Melech Ashur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:15; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:11"> Thus saith HaMelech, Let not Chizkiyah deceive you; for he shall not be able to save you out of his yad;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="TEHILLIM 125:1–2; LUKAS 23:35; MELACHIM BAIS 19:32–34"> Neither let Chizkiyah make you trust in Hashem, saying, Hashem will surely save us, and this city shall not be given into the yad Melech Ashur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 4:25; MELACHIM ALEF 4:20; ZECHARYAH 3:10"> Pay heed not to Chizkiyah; for thus saith HaMelech Ashur, Make a brocha with me, and come out to me, and then eat ye every man of his own gefen, every one of his te'enah, and drink ye every one the waters of his bor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEVARIM 8:7–9; SHEMOT 3:8; DEVARIM 11:12"> Until I come and take you away to an eretz like your own eretz, an eretz dagan and tirosh, an eretz lechem and kramim, a land of zayit yitzhar and of devash, that ye may live, and not die; and pay heed not unto Chizkiyah, when he persuadeth you, saying, Hashem will save us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YESHAYAH 10:10–11; MELACHIM BAIS 19:12–13; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:19"> Hath any of the elohei HaGoyim delivered at all his land out of the yad Melech Ashur?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YESHAYAH 10:9; MELACHIM BAIS 17:23–33; YIRMEYAH 49:23"> Where are elohei Chamat, and of Arpad? Where are elohei Sepharvayim, Hena, and Ivah? Have they saved Shomron out of mine yad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DANIEL 3:15; YESHAYAH 10:15; SHEMOT 5:2"> Who are they among all the elohim of the lands, that have saved their land out of mine yad, that Hashem should save Yerushalayim out of mine yad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="AMOS 5:13; MISHLE 9:7; MATTITYAHU 7:6"> But the people held their peace, and answered him not a davar; for the mitzvat HaMelech was, saying, Answer him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 22:11; BERESHIS 37:29; MELACHIM BAIS 5:7"> Then came Elyakim Ben Chilkiyah, which was over the Bais (palace), and Shevna the Sofer, and Yoach Ben Asaph the Mazkir (secretary), to Chizkiyah with their begadim torn, and told him the words of Rav Shakeh.</span>
