<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 41:1; MELACHIM BAIS 25:25; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 22:10–12"> And when Atalyah em Achazyahu saw that her ben was dead, she arose and destroyed kol zera hamamlachah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 33:26; YIRMEYAH 35:2; YESHAYAH 7:6–7"> But Yehosheva Bat HaMelech Yoram, achot Achazyahu, took Yoash ben Achazyah, and stole him from among the Bnei HaMelech which were being slain; and they hid him, even him and his nurse, in the cheder hamittot (bedroom) from Atalyah, so that he was not slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TEHILLIM 12:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 22:12; MALACHI 3:15"> And he was with her hid in the Beis Hashem shesh shanim. And Atalyah did reign over HaAretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 11:19; MELACHIM BAIS 11:9; MELACHIM BAIS 11:17"> And the seventh year Yehoyada sent and got the commanders of hundreds, with the Kereti and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Peleti">a</a></sup>bodyguard &#91;of HaMelech&#93; and brought them to him into the Beis Hashem, and made a Brit with them, and put them under oath in the Beis Hashem, and showed them the Ben HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 9:25; YECHEZKEL 44:2–3; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:32"> And he commanded them, saying, This is the thing that ye shall do; a third part of you that come on duty on Shabbos shall even be Shomrei Mishmeret Bais HaMelech (Guards on duty at the Royal Palace);</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 23:4–5; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:13–19"> And a third part shall be at the Sha'ar Sur; and a third part at the Sha'ar behind the ones guarding: so shall ye keep shomer the mishmeret HaBais for defense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 23:6; MELACHIM BAIS 11:5"> And two companies of all you that go off duty on Shabbos, even they shall keep shomer the mishmeret Beis Hashem for HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 27:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 23:7; MELACHIM ALEF 2:28–31"> And ye shall surround HaMelech round about, every ish with his weapons in his yad; and he that cometh within the sederot (ranks), let him be slain; and be ye with HaMelech when he goeth out and when he cometh in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 23:8; MELACHIM BAIS 11:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:26"> And the commanders of hundreds did according to all things that Yehoyada HaKohen commanded; and they took every ish his anashim that were on duty on Shabbos, with them that should go off duty on Shabbos, and came to Yehoyada HaKohen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 18:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 5:1"> And to the commanders of hundreds did the Kohen give the spears and shields of Dovid HaMelech, that were in the Beis Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 11:8; YECHEZKEL 8:16; MELACHIM BAIS 11:10"> And the ones guarding stood, every ish with his weapons in his yad, round about HaMelech, from the right side of the Beis to the left side of the Beis, near the Mizbe'ach and the Beis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:16; SHMUEL ALEF 10:24; MELACHIM ALEF 1:39"> And he brought forth the Ben HaMelech, and put the nezer (crown) upon him, and gave him the Edut; and they proclaimed him Melech, and anointed him; and they clapped their hands, and said, Yechi HaMelech!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 23:12–15"> And when Atalyah heard the noise of the ones guarding HaAm, she came to HaAm in the Beis Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:31; MELACHIM BAIS 23:3; MELACHIM BAIS 9:23"> And when she looked, hinei, HaMelech stood at HaAmmud (the Pillar) according to mishpat, and the sarim and the khatzotzerot (trumpets) beside HaMelech, and kol Am HaAretz were samei'ach (rejoicing) and blowing khatzotzerot; and Atalyah tore her robes, and cried, Kesher, kesher! (conspiracy, treason!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 9:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 23:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 23:9"> But Yehoyada HaKohen ordered the commanders of hundreds, the officers of the force, and said unto them, Bring her between the sedorot (ranks); and him that followeth her kill with the cherev. For the Kohen had said, Let her not be slain in the Beis Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 9:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 23:15; MATTITYAHU 7:2"> And they laid hands on her; and she was come to the derech of the entrance HaSusim &#91;by&#93; the Bais HaMelech, and there was she slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 5:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:12–14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:31"> And Yehoyada made a Brit between Hashem and HaMelech and HaAm, that they should be the Am Hashem; and between HaMelech and HaAm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:3; MELACHIM BAIS 10:26; MELACHIM ALEF 18:40"> And kol HaAm HaAretz went into the bais Ba'al, and tore it down; its mizbechot and its tzelem they thoroughly broke in pieces, and slaughtered Mattan kohen HaBaal in front of the mizbechot. And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Yehoyada">b</a></sup>HaKohen assigned the Pekudot (Oversight) over the Beis Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 17:25; YIRMEYAH 22:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 23:19"> And he took the commanders of hundreds, and the Kereti and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Peleti">c</a></sup>bodyguard &#91;of HaMelech&#93;, and kol Am HaAretz; and they brought down HaMelech from the Beis Hashem, and came by the derech Sha'ar HaRatzim to the Bais HaMelech. And he sat on the kisse hamelachim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MISHLE 11:10; MISHLE 29:2; MELACHIM BAIS 11:14"> And kol Am HaAretz rejoiced, and the Ir was sheket; and they slaughtered Atalyah with the cherev in the Bais HaMelech.</span>
