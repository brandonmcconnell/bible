<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 11:21; MELACHIM BAIS 8:26; MELACHIM BAIS 11:4"> In the three and twentieth shanah (year) of Yoash Ben Achazyahu Melech Yehudah, Yehoachaz Ben Yehu began to reign as melech over Yisroel in Shomron, and reigned seventeen shanah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 12:26–33; MELACHIM ALEF 14:16; MELACHIM BAIS 10:29"> And he did that which was rah before the eyes of Hashem, and followed after the chattot of Yarov'am Ben Nevat, which he caused Yisroel to sin; he departed not therefrom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 19:17; MELACHIM BAIS 13:24–25; SHOFETIM 2:14"> And the af Hashem (anger of Hashem) was kindled against Yisroel, and He delivered them into the yad Chazael Melech Aram (Syria), and into the yad Ben-Hadad Ben Chazael, all their yamim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 14:26; SHEMOT 3:7; TEHILLIM 78:34"> And Yehoachaz besought Hashem, and Hashem paid heed unto him; for He saw the lakhatz (oppression) of Yisroel, because Melech Aram (Syria) oppressed them —</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 9:27; MELACHIM BAIS 14:25; MELACHIM BAIS 14:27"> And Hashem gave Yisroel a moshia (savior), so that they went out from under the yad Aram; and the Bnei Yisroel dwelt in their ohalim, as formerly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 16:33; MELACHIM BAIS 13:2; DEVARIM 7:5"> Nevertheless they departed not from the chattot of the Bais Yarov'am, who made Yisroel sin, but walked therein; and also there remained standing the Asherah in Shomron —</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="AMOS 1:3; MELACHIM BAIS 10:32; MELACHIM ALEF 20:15"> For he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Melech Aram">a</a></sup>did leave of the army to Yehoachaz but 50 parashim, and 10 merkavot, and 10,000 foot soldiers; for Melech Aram had destroyed them, and had made them like the aphar for trampling.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:4; MELACHIM ALEF 14:29; MELACHIM ALEF 14:31"> Now the rest of the acts of Yehoachaz, and all that he did, and his gevurah, are they not written in the Sefer Divrei HaYamim L'Malkhei Yisroel?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 10:35; MELACHIM BAIS 13:13; MELACHIM BAIS 14:8"> And Yehoachaz slept with his avot; and they buried him in Shomron; and Yoash bno reigned as Melech in his place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> In the 37th shanah of Yoash Melech Yehudah began Yehoash Ben Yehoachaz to reign as Melech over Yisroel in Shomron, and reigned as Melech 16 shanah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 13:2; MELACHIM BAIS 10:29; MELACHIM BAIS 3:3"> And he did that which was rah before the eyes of Hashem; he departed not from all the chattot Yarov'am Ben Nevat, who caused Yisroel to commit sin; but he walked therein.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 13:14–25; MELACHIM BAIS 14:8–16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 25:17–24"> And the rest of the acts of Yoash, and all that he did, and his gevurah wherewith he warred against Amatzyah (Amaziah) Melech Yehudah, are they not written in the Sefer Divrei HaYamim L'Malkhei Yisroel?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:21; MELACHIM BAIS 14:28–29; MELACHIM ALEF 2:10"> And Yoash slept with his avot; and Yarov'am sat upon his kisse: and Yoash was buried in Shomron with the Malkhei Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 2:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:26; BERESHIS 48:1"> Now Elishah was fallen ill of his illness whereof he would die. And Yoash Melech Yisroel came down unto him, and wept over his face, and said, O Avi, Avi, the merkavah of Yisroel, and the parashim thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Elishah said unto him, Take keshet and khitzim. And he took unto him keshet and khitzim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:34; BERESHIS 49:24; TEHILLIM 144:1"> And he said to Melech Yisroel, Place thine yad upon the keshet. And he put his yad upon it; and Elishah put his hands upon the hands of HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 20:26; MELACHIM BAIS 5:10–14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:18"> And he said, Open the east chalon. And he opened it. Then Elishah said, Shoot. And he shot. And he said, The khetz (arrow) of the teshuah (salvation) unto Hashem, and the khetz of teshuah against Aram; for thou shalt strike down Aram at Aphek, unto complete destruction.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:6; SHEMOT 17:11; YESHAYAH 20:2–4"> And he said, Take the khitzim (arrows). And he took them. And he said unto Melech Yisroel, Strike the ground. And he struck shalosh pe'amim (three times) and stopped.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 13:25; BAMIDBAR 16:15; MELACHIM BAIS 4:40"> And the Ish HaElohim was in wrath with him, and said, Thou shouldest have struck chamesh or shesh pe'amim; then hadst thou struck down Aram till thou hadst completely destroyed it; whereas now thou shalt defeat Aram but 3 pe'amim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 24:2; MELACHIM BAIS 3:7; MELACHIM BAIS 6:23"> And Elishah died, and they buried him. And the raiding parties of Moav would invade the land at the beginning of the shanah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 37:1–10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:12; YOCHANAN 5:28–29"> And it came to pass, as they were burying an ish, that, hinei, they spotted a raiding party; and they cast the ish into the kever of Elishah; and when the ish was let down, and touched the atzmot of Elishah, he revived, and stood up alive on his raglayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 8:12; MELACHIM BAIS 13:3–7; TEHILLIM 106:40–42"> But Chazael Melech Aram oppressed Yisroel all days of Yehoachaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="TEHILLIM 105:8; MELACHIM BAIS 14:27; MICHOH 7:18–20"> And Hashem was gracious unto them, and had rachamim on them, and showed regard unto them, because of His brit (covenant) with Avraham, Yitzchak, and Ya'akov, and would not destroy them, neither cast He them from His presence up to now.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="TEHILLIM 125:3; LUKAS 18:7"> So Chazael Melech Aram died; and Ben-Hadad bno reigned as melech in his place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 13:18–19; MELACHIM BAIS 10:32; MELACHIM BAIS 14:25"> And Yehoash Ben Yehoachaz returned and recaptured out of the yad of Ben-Hadad Ben Chazael the towns, which he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ben-Hadad">b</a></sup>had taken out of the yad of Yehoachaz aviv by milchamah (war). Shalosh times did Yoash defeat him, and recovered the towns of Yisroel.</span>
</div>
