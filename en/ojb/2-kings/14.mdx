</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 26:16; DEVARIM 8:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:25"> Thou hast indeed struck down Edom, and thine lev hath lifted thee up; let thy honor save face, and tarry at home; for why shouldest thou ask for ra'ah, that thou shouldest fall, even thou, and Yehudah with thee?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 18:17; SHMUEL ALEF 4:10; MELACHIM ALEF 22:36"> And Yehudah went down in defeat before Yisroel; and they fled every ish to his ohel.</span>

</div>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 13:6; MELACHIM BAIS 13:11; BERESHIS 38:7"> And he did that which was rah in the eyes of Hashem; he departed not from all the chattot Yarov'am Ben Nevat, who caused Yisroel to commit sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEVARIM 3:17; MATTITYAHU 12:39–40; YEHOSHUA 19:13"> He restored the boundary of Yisroel from the entrance of Chamat unto the yam of the Aravah, according to the Devar Hashem Elohei Yisroel, which He spoke by the yad of His Eved HaNavi Yonah Ben Amittai, who was of Gat HaChefer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:36; SHEMOT 3:7; MELACHIM BAIS 13:4"> For Hashem saw the oni Yisroel, that it was mar me'od (very bitter) for there was neither atzur (slave) nor azuv (free), no ozer (helper) at all for Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 13:23; MELACHIM BAIS 13:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:2–12"> And Hashem had not said that He would blot out the shem of Yisroel from under Shomayim; but He saved them by the yad Yarov'am Ben Yoash.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:24; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 18:5–6; SHMUEL BAIS 8:6"> Now the rest of the acts of Yarov'am, and all that he did, and his gevurah, how he warred, and how he won back Damascus and Chamat from Yehudah for Yisroel, are they not written in the Sefer Divrei HaYamim L'Malkhei Yisroel?</span>
