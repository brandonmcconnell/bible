<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YESHAYAH 62:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:33–9; MATTITYAHU 1:10"> Menasheh was twelve years old when he began to reign as melech, and reigned fifty and five years in Yerushalayim. And shem immo was Cheftzi-Vah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:31; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:2–4"> And he did that which was rah in the eyes of Hashem, after the to'avot of the Goyim, whom Hashem drove out before the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:4; MELACHIM BAIS 17:16; DEVARIM 17:3"> For he built up again the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="idolatrous">a</a></sup>high places which Chizkiyah aviv had destroyed; and he reared up mizbechot for Ba'al, and made Asherah, as did Ach'av Melech Yisroel; and bowed down in worship to kol Tzava HaShomayim, and served them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:29; SHMUEL BAIS 7:13; YIRMEYAH 32:34"> And he built mizbechot in the Beis Hashem, of which Hashem said, In Yerushalayim will I put Shmi (My Name).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:12; MELACHIM BAIS 23:4; MELACHIM BAIS 23:12"> And he built mizbechot for kol Tzava HaShomayim in the two khatzerot (courtyards) of the Beis Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 17:17; VAYIKRA 19:31; VAYIKRA 18:21"> And he made bno pass through the eish, and practiced sorcery, and witchcraft, and dealt with familiar spirits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., he consulted mediums">b</a></sup>and spiritists; he wrought much rah in the eyes of Hashem, to provoke Him to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:16; MELACHIM ALEF 9:3; SHMUEL BAIS 7:13"> And he set a pesel HaAsherah he had made in the Beis Hashem of which Hashem said to Dovid, and to Sh'lomo bno, In this Beis, and in Yerushalayim, which I have chosen out of all Shivtei Yisroel, will I put Shmi (My Name) l'olam;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 7:10; DEVARIM 5:28–29; TEHILLIM 81:11–16"> Neither will I make the regel Yisroel move any more out of ha'adamah (the land) which I gave their avot; only if they will be shomer to do according to all that I have commanded them, and according to all the torah that avdi Moshe commanded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MISHLE 29:12; NECHEMYAH 9:26; MELACHIM ALEF 14:16"> But they paid heed not; and Menasheh seduced them to do rah more than did the Goyim whom Hashem destroyed before the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:34–37; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:15"> And Hashem spoke by His avadim the Nevi'im, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 24:3–4; MELACHIM ALEF 21:26; MELACHIM BAIS 21:9"> Because Menasheh Melech Yehudah hath done these to'avot, hath done wickedly more than all that HaEmori did, which were before him, hath made Yehudah also to sin with his gillulim (idols);</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 19:3; SHMUEL ALEF 3:11; DANIEL 9:12"> Therefore thus saith Hashem Elohei Yisroel, Hineni, I am bringing such ra'ah upon Yerushalayim and Yehudah, that whosoever heareth of it, both his oznayim shall tingle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="AMOS 7:7–8; EKHAH 2:8; YESHAYAH 34:11"> And I will stretch over Yerushalayim the measuring line of Shomron, and the plumbline of the Bais Ach'av; and I will wipe Yerushalayim as a man wipeth a dish, wiping it, and turning it upside down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 23:33; MELACHIM BAIS 19:4; TEHILLIM 89:38–45"> And I will forsake the She'erit of Mine nachalah, and deliver them into the yad of their oyevim; and they shall become a prey and a plunder to all their oyevim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 20:30; DEVARIM 31:27; YECHEZKEL 20:21"> Because they have done that which was rah (evil) in My eyes, and have provoked Me to anger, since the day their avot came forth out of Mitzrayim, even unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 35:33; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:9; MELACHIM BAIS 21:11"> Moreover Menasheh was guilty of much shefach dahm of the innocent, till he had filled Yerushalayim from one end to another; besides his chattot wherewith he caused Yehudah to commit sin, in doing that which was rah in the eyes of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 20:20–21; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:1–20"> Now the rest of the acts of Menasheh, and all that he did, and his chattot that he sinned, are they not written in the Sefer Divrei HaYamim L'Malkhei Yehudah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:20; MELACHIM BAIS 21:26; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:33"> And Menasheh slept with his avot, and was buried in the gan of his own Bais, in the gan of Uzza; and Amon bno reigned as melech in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:21–23; MELACHIM ALEF 22:51; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:14"> Amon was 22 years old when he began to reign as melech, and he reigned 2 shanim in Yerushalayim. And shem immo was Meshulemet Bat Charutz of Yotbah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:32; MELACHIM BAIS 21:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:51"> And he did that which was rah in the eyes of Hashem, just as Menasheh aviv did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And he walked in all the derech that aviv walked in, and served the gillulim that aviv served, and bowed down in worship to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 22:17; MELACHIM ALEF 11:33; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:9"> And he forsook Hashem Elohei Avotav, and walked not in the Derech Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 12:20; MELACHIM BAIS 14:19; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:24–25"> And the avadim of Amon plotted a kesher against him, and assassinated HaMelech in his own Bais (palace).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 14:5; MELACHIM BAIS 11:17; MELACHIM BAIS 14:21"> And the Am HaAretz slaughtered all them that had plotted a kesher against HaMelech Amon; and the Am HaAretz made Yoshiyah bno Melech in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 20:20; MELACHIM BAIS 21:17"> Now the rest of the acts of Amon which he did, are they not written in the Sefer Divrei HaYamim L'Malkhei Yehudah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 21:18; MATTITYAHU 1:10; MELACHIM ALEF 13:5"> And he was buried in his kever in the gan (garden) of Uzza; and Yoshiyah bno reigned as melech in his place.</span>
