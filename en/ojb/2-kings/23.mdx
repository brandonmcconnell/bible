<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 22:8; DEVARIM 31:10–13; HISGALUS 20:12"> And HaMelech went up into the Beis Hashem, and kol ish Yehudah and all the inhabitants of Yerushalayim with him, and the Kohanim, and the Nevi'im, and kol HaAm, both katon and gadol; and he read in their ears all the Divrei Sefer HaBrit which was found in the Beis Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 11:14; MELACHIM BAIS 11:17; DEVARIM 13:4"> And HaMelech stood by HaAmmud, and cut HaBrit before Hashem, to walk after Hashem, and to be shomer mitzvot over His commandments and His edot and His chukkot with all their lev and all their nefesh, to carry out these Divrei HaBrit that were written in this Sefer. And kol HaAm stood &#91;under oath&#93; to HaBrit.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 21:3; MELACHIM BAIS 21:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:3"> And HaMelech commanded Chilkiyahu HaKohen HaGadol, and the Kohanei HaMishneh, and the Shomrei HaSaf, to bring forth out of the Heikhal Hashem all the kelim that were made for Ba'al, and for Asherah, and for all the Tzeva HaShomayim; and he burned them outside Yerushalayim in the fields of the Kidron, and carried the ashes of them unto Beit-El.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 44:17–19; MELACHIM BAIS 21:3–4; HOSHEA 10:5"> And he did away with hakemarim (the idol priests), whom the Melachim of Yehudah had ordained to burn ketoret in the high places in the towns of Yehudah, and in the places around Yerushalayim; them also that burned ketoret unto Ba'al, to the shemesh, and to the yarei'ach, and to the mazalot (constellations) and to all the Tzeva HaShomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:4; MELACHIM BAIS 23:15; MELACHIM ALEF 14:23"> And he took out the Asherah from the Beis Hashem, outside Yerushalayim, unto the Kidron Valley, and burned it in the Kidron Valley, and ground it to powder, and cast its aphar upon the keverim of the Bnei HaAm.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., them that sacrificed unto them">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 15:12; YECHEZKEL 16:16; MELACHIM ALEF 14:24"> And he tore down the batim (houses) of hakedeshim (the male and female cult prostitutes) that were in the Beis Hashem, where the nashim were weaving hangings for Asherah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 15:22; YEHOSHUA 21:17; MELACHIM ALEF 19:3"> And he brought all the Kohanim out of the towns of Yehudah, and made tameh the high places where the kohanim had burned ketoret, from Geva to Be'er-Sheva, and broke down the high places at the she'arim (gates) that were at the entrance of the Sha'ar Yehoshua sar Ir, which were on oneʼs left at the Sha'ar HaIr.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 44:10–14; SHMUEL ALEF 2:36; MALACHI 2:8–9"> Nevertheless the kohanim of the high places came not up to the Mizbe'ach Hashem in Yerushalayim, only they did eat of the matzot among their achim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YESHAYAH 30:33; YIRMEYAH 19:6; VAYIKRA 18:21"> And he made tameh Tophet, which is in the Gey of the ben Hinnom, that no ish might make his ben or his bat to pass through the eish to Molech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 8:16; DEVARIM 4:19; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 14:5"> And he took away the susim that the Melachim of Yehudah had dedicated to the shemesh, at the entrance of the Beis Hashem, by the chamber of Natan-Melech the saris, which was in the colonnades, and set eish to merkevot hashemesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 21:5; TZEFANYAH 1:5; YIRMEYAH 19:13"> And the mizbechot that were on the roof of the upper chamber of Achaz, which the Melachim of Yehudah had made, and the mizbechot which Menasheh had made in the two khatzerot of the Beis Hashem, did HaMelech pull down, and remove them from there, and cast the aphar of them into the Kidron Valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 11:7; MELACHIM ALEF 11:5; BAMIDBAR 21:29"> And the high places that were before Yerushalayim, which were on the right of the Har HaMashchit, which Sh'lomo Melech Yisroel had built for Ashtoret shikkutz Tzidonim, and for Kemosh shikkutz Moav, and for Milkom to'avat Bnei Ammon, did HaMelech make tameh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:5; SHEMOT 23:24; MELACHIM BAIS 23:16"> And he broke in pieces the matzevot, and cut down the Asherim, and filled their makom with the atzmot adam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 23:6; MELACHIM ALEF 14:16; MELACHIM ALEF 21:22"> Moreover the mizbe'ach that was at Beit-El, and the high place which Yarov'am ben Nevat, who caused Yisroel to sin, had made, both that mizbe'ach and the high place did he break down, burn the high place, did grind it to powder, did burn the Asherah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 13:1–2; MELACHIM ALEF 13:32; YOCHANAN 10:35"> And as Yoshiyah turned, he saw the keverim that were there in the har, and sent, and took the atzmot out of the keverim, and burned them upon the mizbe'ach, and made it tameh, according to the Devar Hashem which the Ish HaElohim preached, who proclaimed these words.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See 1Kgs 13:1-2">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 13:1; MELACHIM ALEF 13:30–31"> Then he said, What tziyyun (monument, tombtone) is that that I see? And the anshei HaIr told him, It is the kever of the Ish HaElohim, which came from Yehudah, and proclaimed these things that thou hast done against the mizbe'ach of Beit-El.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See 1Kgs 13:1-3">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 13:31; MELACHIM ALEF 13:1–22"> And he said, Let him alone; let no ish disturb his atzmot. So they left his atzmot undisturbed, with the atzmot of the navi that came out of Shomron.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See 1Kgs 13:11,31">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:6–7; MELACHIM BAIS 17:9; YECHEZKEL 8:17–18"> And all the batim (houses, shrines) also of the high places that were in the towns of Shomron, which the Melachim of Yisroel had made to provoke Hashem to anger, Yoshiyah removed, and did to them according to all the ma'asim that he had done in Beit-El.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 11:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:5; SHEMOT 22:20"> And he slaughtered all the kohanim of the high places that were there upon the mizbechot, and burned atzmot adam upon them, and returned to Yerushalayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:1–8; BAMIDBAR 9:2–5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:1–19"> And HaMelech commanded kol HaAm, saying, Make Pesach unto Hashem Eloheichem, as it is written in the Sefer HaBrit HaZeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:1–3; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:13–20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:3–19"> Surely there was not kept such a Pesach from the days of the Shofetim that judged Yisroel, nor in all the days of the Melachim of Yisroel, nor of the Melachim of Yehudah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> But in the eighteenth year of Melech Yoshiyah, wherein this Pesach was kept to Hashem in Yerushalayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:5; YIRMEYAH 29:13; DEVARIM 6:5"> And like him was there no Melech before him, that turned to Hashem with all his lev, and with all his nefesh, and with all his me'od (strength), according to all the Torat Moshe; neither after him arose there any like him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 21:11–13; MELACHIM BAIS 24:4; YIRMEYAH 3:7–10"> Notwithstanding Hashem turned not from the fierceness of His af hagadol (great wrath), wherewith His anger was kindled against Yehudah, because of all the provocations with which Menasheh had provoked him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:11; MELACHIM BAIS 21:13; MELACHIM BAIS 21:4"> And Hashem said, I will remove Yehudah also out of My presence, as I have removed Yisroel, and will cast off this ir Yerushalayim which I have chosen, and the Beis of which I said, Shmi sham.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 20:20"> Now the rest of the acts of Yoshiyah, and all that he did, are they not written in the Sefer Divrei HaYamim of the Melachim of Yehudah?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 46:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:20–24; SHOFETIM 5:19"> In his days Pharaoh Nekho Melech Mitzrayim went up against Melech Ashur to the river Euphrates; and Melech Yoshiyah went out against him; and he slaughtered him at Megiddo, when he saw him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 9:28; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:1–4; MELACHIM BAIS 21:24"> And his avadim carried him in a merkavah dead from Megiddo, and brought him to Yerushalayim, and buried him in his own kever. And the Am HaAretz took Yehoachaz Ben Yoshiyah, and anointed him, and made him Melech in place of Aviv.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:15; MELACHIM BAIS 24:18; YIRMEYAH 22:11"> Yehoachaz was twenty and three years old when he began to reign; and he reigned shloshah chodashim in Yerushalayim. And shem immo was Chamutal Bat Yirmeyahu of Livnah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 21:2–7; MELACHIM BAIS 21:21–22"> And he did that which was rah in the eyes of Hashem, according to all that his Avot had done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:65; MELACHIM BAIS 25:6; YIRMEYAH 39:5–6"> And Pharaoh Nekho put him in chains at Rivlah in Eretz Chamat, that he might not reign in Yerushalayim; and put HaAretz to an ogesh (tribute, fine) of 100 talents of kesef, and a talent of zahav.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 19:3–4; MELACHIM BAIS 24:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:15"> And Pharaoh Nekho made Elyakim Ben Yoshiyah Melech in the place of Yoshiyah Aviv, and changed shmo to Y'hoyakim, and took Yehoachaz away; and he went to Mitzrayim, and died there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 23:33; MELACHIM BAIS 15:19–20"> And Y'hoyakim gave the kesef and the zahav to Pharaoh; but he taxed HaAretz to give the kesef according to the order of Pharaoh; he exacted the kesef and the zahav of the Am HaAretz of every one according to his tax assessment, to give it unto Pharaoh Nekho.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:5; YIRMEYAH 26:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 3:15"> Y'hoyakim was 25 years old when he began to reign; and he reigned 11 shanah in Yerushalayim. And shem immo was Zevudah Bat Pedayah of Rumah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:22–23; YECHEZKEL 19:5–9; YIRMEYAH 36:31"> And he did that which was rah in the eyes of Hashem, according to all that his Avot had done.</span>
