<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:39; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:1–33; YIRMEYAH 1:2"> Yoshiyah was 8 years old when he began to reign, and he reigned 31 shanah in Yerushalayim. And shem immo was Yedidah Bat Adayah of Botzkat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 5:32; YEHOSHUA 1:7; MELACHIM BAIS 18:3"> And he did that which was yashar in the eyes of Hashem, and walked in kol derech Dovid Aviv, and turned not aside to the yamin or smol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:3–33"> And it came to pass in the 18th year of Melech Yoshiyah, that HaMelech sent Shaphan Ben Atzalyahu Ben Meshullam, the Sofer, to the Beis Hashem, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 12:4; MARKOS 12:41–42; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:9–18"> Go up to Chilkiyahu the Kohen HaGadol, that he may count the kesef which is brought into the Beis Hashem, which the Shomrei HaSaf (the Doorkeepers) have collected of the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 12:11–14; MELACHIM BAIS 12:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 24:12–13"> And let them entrust it into the yad oseh hamelachah (hand of the master workmen), that have the mufkadim (oversight) of the Beis Hashem; and let them pay it to the workmen that wrought in the Beis Hashem, to repair and restore HaBeis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Unto kharashim (craftsmen, artisans) and bonim (builders) and goderim (masons), and to purchase etzim (timber) and quarried stone to repair HaBeis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 12:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:2; NECHEMYAH 7:2"> Howbeit there was no accounting made with them of the kesef that was entrusted into their yad, because they dealt be'emunah (faithfully).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEVARIM 31:24–26; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:14–28"> And Chilkiyahu the Kohen HaGadol said unto Shaphan the Sofer, I have found the Sefer HaTorah in the Beis Hashem. And Chilkiyahu gave the Sefer to Shaphan, and he read it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 8:11; MELACHIM BAIS 25:22; MELACHIM BAIS 18:18"> And Shaphan the Sofer came to HaMelech, brought HaMelech word again, and said, Thy avadim have emptied out the kesef that was found in HaBeis,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="HaMikdash">a</a></sup>and have entrusted it into the yad osei hamelachah, that have the mufkadim of the Beis Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 36:21; YIRMEYAH 13:18; DEVARIM 17:18–20"> And Shaphan the Sofer showed HaMelech, saying, Chilkiyah the Kohen hath delivered unto me a Sefer (Scroll). And Shaphan read it before HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:19; YOEL 2:13; MELACHIM BAIS 22:19"> And it came to pass, when HaMelech had heard the Divrei Sefer HaTorah, that he tore his garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 26:24; MELACHIM BAIS 25:22; MELACHIM BAIS 19:2–3"> And HaMelech commanded Chilkiyah HaKohen, and Achikam Ben Shaphan, and Achbor Ben Michayah, and Shaphan the Sofer, and Asayah Eved HaMelech, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEVARIM 29:23–28; DEVARIM 31:17–18; YIRMEYAH 37:17"> Go ye, inquire of Hashem for me, and for HaAm, and for kol Yehudah, concerning the Divrei HaSefer that is found; for gedolah is chamat Hashem that is kindled against us, because Avoteinu have not paid heed unto the Divrei HaSefer Hazeh, to do according unto all that which is written concerning us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHOFETIM 4:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:22; MICHOH 6:4"> So Chilkiyahu HaKohen, and Achikam, and Achbor, and Shaphan, and Asayah, went unto Chuldah the neviah, the wife of Shallum ben Tikvah, ben Charchas, Shomer HaBegadim (Keeper of the Wardrobe); (now she dwelt in Yerushalayim in the Mishneh); and they spoke with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 1:6; MELACHIM BAIS 1:16; YIRMEYAH 23:28"> And she said unto them, Thus saith Hashem Elohei Yisroel, Tell the ish that sent you to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DANIEL 9:11–14; YEHOSHUA 23:15; DEVARIM 29:27"> Thus saith Hashem, Hineni, I will bring ra'ah upon this makom (place), and upon the inhabitants thereof, even kol Divrei HaSefer which Melech Yehudah hath read;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 21:22; YIRMEYAH 7:20; DEVARIM 29:24–28"> Because they have forsaken Me, and have burned ketoret unto elohim acherim, that they might provoke Me to anger with all the works of their hands; therefore My wrath shall be kindled against this makom (place), and shall not be quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 34:26–28; MALACHI 3:16–17; YESHAYAH 3:10"> But to Melech Yehudah which sent you to inquire of Hashem, thus shall ye say to him, Thus saith Hashem Elohei Yisroel, As touching the words which thou hast heard;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="TEHILLIM 51:17; MELACHIM ALEF 21:29; YESHAYAH 57:15"> Because thine lev was tender, and thou hast humbled thyself before Hashem, when thou heardest what I spoke against this place, and against the inhabitants thereof, that they should become a desolation and a klalah (curse), and hast torn thy garments, and wept before Me; I also have heard thee, saith Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 37:37; YESHAYAH 57:1–2; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 17:11"> Hineni, therefore, I will gather thee unto Avoteicha, and thou shalt be gathered into thy kevarot in shalom; and thine eyes shall not see kol hara'ah which I will bring upon this place. And they brought back word to HaMelech.</span>
