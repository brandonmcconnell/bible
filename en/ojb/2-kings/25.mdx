<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 52:4–11; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:15; YIRMEYAH 34:1–6"> And it came to pass in the ninth year of his reign, in the tenth month, in the tenth day of the month,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Zech 8:19">a</a></sup>that Nevukhadnetzar Melech Bavel came, he, and all his army, against Yerushalayim, and encamped against it; and built siege works against it round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And HaIr remained under siege unto the eleventh year of Melech Tzidkiyah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 52:6; YECHEZKEL 5:12; YIRMEYAH 38:2"> And on the ninth day of the fourth month the ra'av (famine) was severe in the Ir, and there was no lechem for the Am HaAretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 33:21; MELACHIM BAIS 25:5; YIRMEYAH 39:2–7"> And the Ir was broken through, and all the anshei hamilchamah fled by lailah by the way of the gate between two walls, which was near the Gan HaMelech; (now the Kasdim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Chaldeans">b</a></sup>were surrounding the Ir); and HaMelech fled by the derech HaAravah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YESHAYAH 30:16; YIRMEYAH 39:5; AMOS 2:14–16"> And the army of the Kasdim pursued after HaMelech, and overtook him in the plains of Yericho; and all his army was separated from him and scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 23:33; YIRMEYAH 34:21–22; YIRMEYAH 21:7"> So they captured HaMelech, and carried him up to Melech Bavel to Rivlah; and they pronounced mishpat upon him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:4–5; SHOFETIM 16:21; YIRMEYAH 39:6–7"> And they slaughtered the Bnei Tzidkiyah before his eyes, and put out the eyes of Tzidkiyah, and bound him with shackles of nechoshet, and carried him to Babylon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 52:12–16; YIRMEYAH 40:1–4; EKHAH 4:12"> And in the fifth month, on the seventh day of the month, which is the nineteenth year of Melech Nevukhadnetzar Melech Bavel, came Nevuzaradan, captain of the guard, an eved of Melech Bavel, unto Yerushalayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="AMOS 2:5; TEHILLIM 74:3–7; MELACHIM ALEF 9:8"> And he set fire to the Beis Hashem, and the Bais HaMelech (the palace), and all the batim (houses) of Yerushalayim, and every bais hagadol he burned with eish.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Tisha B'Av, see Jer 52:12">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 1:3; YIRMEYAH 39:8; YIRMEYAH 52:14–23"> And all the army of the Kasdim (Chaldeans), that were with the captain of the guard, broke down the chomot (walls) around Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 5:2; YIRMEYAH 15:1–2; YIRMEYAH 39:9"> Now the remainder of the people that were left in the Ir, and the deserters that fell away to Melech Bavel, with the remnant of the multitude, did Nevuzaradan the captain of the guard carry away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 40:7; MELACHIM BAIS 24:14; YECHEZKEL 33:24"> But the captain of the guard left certain of the poor of the land to be koremim and farmers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:18; EKHAH 1:10; MELACHIM BAIS 20:17"> And the pillars of bronze that were in the Beis Hashem, and the moveable stands, and the Yam Hanechoshet that was in the Beis Hashem, the Kasdim (Chaldeans) broke in pieces, and carried all the bronze to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 27:3; MELACHIM ALEF 7:47–50; SHEMOT 38:3"> And the caldrons, and the shovels, and the snuffers, and the basins and all the klei hanechoshet wherewith they ministered, they took away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DANIEL 5:2–3; BAMIDBAR 7:13–14; MELACHIM ALEF 7:48–51"> And the firepans &#91;for incense&#93;, and the bowls, and such things as were made of zahav, and of kesef, the captain of the guard carried away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:47"> The two Ammudim, one Yam, and the Stands which Sh'lomo had made for the Beis Hashem; the bronze of all these vessels was beyond weight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:15–22; YIRMEYAH 52:21–23"> The height of the one ammud was eighteen cubits, and the capital upon it was nechoshet; and the height of the capital three cubits; and the netting, and pomegranates upon the capital round about, all of nechoshet; and similar unto these had the second pillar with netting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 29:25; EZRA 7:1; YIRMEYAH 21:1"> And the captain of the guard took Serayah the Kohen HaRosh, and Tzephanyah the second kohen, and the three keepers of the door;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ESTHER 1:14"> And out of the Ir he took an officer that was set over the anshei hamilchamah, and five men of them that were royal advisers, which were found in the Ir, and the Sofer who was the chief officer in charge of conscripting the Am HaAretz, and threescore men of the Am HaAretz, that were found in the Ir;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EKHAH 4:16; YIRMEYAH 52:26–27; MELACHIM BAIS 23:33"> And Nevuzaradan, captain of the guard, took these, and brought them to Melech Bavel to Rivlah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:64; MELACHIM BAIS 23:27; DEVARIM 4:26"> And Melech Bavel struck them, and slaughtered them at Rivlah in Eretz Chamat. So Yehudah was carried away captive out of their land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 39:14; MELACHIM BAIS 22:12; YIRMEYAH 41:2"> And as for the people that remained in Eretz Yehudah, whom Nevukhadnetzar Melech Bavel had left behind, even over them he made Gedalyahu ben Achikam ben Shaphan ruler.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 40:7–9; YIRMEYAH 40:11–12; YEHOSHUA 18:26"> And when all the officers of the armies, they and their anashim, heard that Melech Bavel had made Gedalyahu governor, there came to Gedalyahu to Mitzpah, even Yishmael ben Netanyah, and Yochanan ben Kareach, and Serayah ben Tanchumet the Netophati, and Ya'azanyahu the ben of a Ma'akhati, they and their men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 40:9–10; YIRMEYAH 43:6; SHMUEL BAIS 19:23"> And Gedalyahu took an oath to them, and to their men, and said unto them, Fear not to be the avadim of the Kasdim; dwell in the land, and serve Melech Bavel; and it shall be well with you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 7:5; ZECHARYAH 8:19; YIRMEYAH 40:15"> But it came to pass in the seventh month, that Yishmael ben Netanyah, ben Elishama, of royal blood, came, and ten men with him, and assassinated Gedalyahu, that he died, along with the Yehudim and the Kasdim that were with him at Mitzpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 43:4–7; YIRMEYAH 41:16–18; YIRMEYAH 42:14–22"> And kol haAm, both katon and gadol, and the officers of the armies, arose, and went to Mitzrayim; for they were afraid of the Kasdim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 52:31–34; BERESHIS 40:20; BERESHIS 40:13"> And it came to pass in the seven and thirtieth year of the Golus of Y'hoyakhin Melech Yehudah, in the twelfth month, on the seven and twentieth day of the month, that Eveel-Merodach Melech Bavel in the year that he began to reign<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="562 B.C.E.">d</a></sup>did lift up the head of Y'hoyakhin Melech Yehudah out of the bais hakeleh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DANIEL 2:37; YIRMEYAH 27:6–11; DANIEL 5:18–19"> And he spoke kindly to him, and set his throne above the kisse of the melachim that were with him in Babylon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 9:7; YESHAYAH 61:3; BERESHIS 41:42"> And changed his prison garments; and he did eat lechem continually before him all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LUKAS 11:3; MATTITYAHU 6:11; NECHEMYAH 11:23"> And his allowance was a continual allowance given him of the Melech, a daily portion for every day, all the days of his life.</span>
</div>
</div>
