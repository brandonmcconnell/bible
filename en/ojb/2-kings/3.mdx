<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 1:17; MELACHIM ALEF 22:51; MELACHIM BAIS 8:16"> Now Yehoram ben Ach'av began to reign over Yisroel in Shomron the eighteenth year of Yehoshaphat Melech Yehudah, and reigned twelve shanah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 10:18; MELACHIM BAIS 10:26–28; MELACHIM ALEF 21:25"> And he wrought rah in the eyes of Hashem; but not like Aviv, nor like Immo; for he put away matzvat HaBa'al (the image of Ba'al) that Aviv had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 14:16; MELACHIM ALEF 15:34; MELACHIM BAIS 15:18"> Nevertheless his deveykus was unto the chattot Yarov'am Ben Nevat, which caused Yisroel to sin; therefrom he did not turn away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 8:2; BERESHIS 26:13–14; IYOV 1:3"> And Mesha Melech Moav was a sheep breeder, and rendered unto the Melech Yisroel 100,000 lambs, and 100,000 rams, with the tzemer (wool).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 1:1; MELACHIM BAIS 8:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 21:8–10"> But it came to pass, when Ach'av was mot, that Melech Moav rebelled against Melech Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 24:1–25; SHMUEL ALEF 11:8; SHMUEL ALEF 15:4"> And Melech Yehoram went out of Shomron on that day, and mustered kol Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 22:10–12; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 18:3"> And he went and sent to Yehoshaphat Melech Yehudah, saying, Melech Moav hath rebelled against me; wilt thou go with me against Moav for milchamah? And he said, I will go up: I am as thou art, my Am as thy Am, and my susim as thy susim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MALACHI 1:2–3; BAMIDBAR 21:4"> And he said, Which derech shall we go up &#91;to attack&#93;? And he answered, The derech through the Midbar Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:47; BAMIDBAR 20:2; SHEMOT 17:1"> So Melech Yisroel, Melech Yehudah, Melech Edom went, made a circuit of shivat yamim in their derech; and there was no mayim for the machaneh, and for the behemah that followed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:34–36; YESHAYAH 51:20; YESHAYAH 8:21"> And Melech Yisroel said, Alas that Hashem hath called these three melachim together, to deliver them into the yad Moav!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:7; MELACHIM ALEF 19:21; MELACHIM BAIS 3:3"> But Yehoshaphat said, Is there not here a navi of Hashem, that we may inquire of Hashem through him? And one of the avadim of Melech Yisroel answered and said, Here is Elishah Ben Shaphat, which poured mayim on the hands of Eliyahu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 5:15; MELACHIM BAIS 2:21; YESHAYAH 60:14"> And Yehoshaphat said, The Devar Hashem is with him. So Melech Yisroel and Yehoshaphat and Melech Edom went down to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 22:6; MELACHIM ALEF 18:19; MELACHIM ALEF 22:10–11"> And Elishah said unto Melech Yisroel, What have I to do with thee? Get thee to the nevi'im of Avicha,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., of Ba'al">a</a></sup>and to the nevi'im of Immecha. And Melech Yisroel said unto him, No; for Hashem hath called these three melachim, to deliver them into the yad Moav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 5:16; MELACHIM ALEF 17:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:3–4"> And Elishah said, As Hashem Tzva'os liveth, before whom I stand, surely, were it not that I regard the presence of Yehoshaphat Melech Yehudah, I would not look toward thee, nor see thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:18–19; YECHEZKEL 1:3"> But now bring me menagen. And it came to pass, when the menagen played, that the Yad Hashem came upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 2:16–18; MELACHIM BAIS 4:3"> And he said, Thus saith Hashem, Make this valley full of ditches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TEHILLIM 107:35; YESHAYAH 41:17–18; YESHAYAH 48:21"> For thus saith Hashem, Ye shall not see ruach, neither shall ye see geshem; yet this valley shall be filled with mayim, that ye may drink, both ye, and your cattle, and your behemah (livestock).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 32:27; LUKAS 1:37; YIRMEYAH 32:17"> And this is but a light thing in the eyes of Hashem; He will deliver Moav also into your yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 13:17; DEVARIM 20:19–20; SHOFETIM 6:16"> And ye shall strike every fortified city, every ir mivchor, and shall fell every etz tov, stop up kol ma'yan mayim, mar every good allotment of land with avanim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:39–40; DANIEL 9:21; MELACHIM ALEF 18:36"> And it came to pass in the boker, when the minchah was offered, that, hinei, there came mayim by the derech of Edom, and HaAretz was filled with mayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:14; MELACHIM ALEF 20:11"> And when kol Moav heard that the melachim were come up to fight against them, they gathered all that could bear arms, and older, and stood at the border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And they rose up early in the boker, and the shemesh shone upon the mayim, and Moav saw the mayim on the other side, and to them it looked as red as dahm;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHOFETIM 5:30; SHEMOT 15:9; YESHAYAH 10:14"> And they said, This is dahm; the melachim are surely slain, and they have struck one another; now therefore, Moav, to the plunder!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:3–4; YEHOSHUA 8:20–22; SHOFETIM 20:40–46"> And when they came to the machaneh Yisroel, Yisroel rose up and struck Moav, so that they fled before them; but they went forward striking Moav, even invading their country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YESHAYAH 16:7; YIRMEYAH 48:36; YIRMEYAH 48:31"> And they beat down the towns, and on every good allotment of land every ish cast his even (stone), and filled it; and they stopped up kol ma'yan mayim, and felled kol etz tov; only in Kir Charaset left they the avanim thereof; howbeit the slingers surrounded it, and struck it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 3:9; AMOS 2:1"> And when Melech Moav saw that the milchamah was chazak against him, he took with him 700 ish that drew cherev, to break through even unto Melech Edom; but they could not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MICHOH 6:7; DEVARIM 12:31; SHOFETIM 11:31"> Then he took bno habechor that should have reigned in his stead, and offered him for an olah upon the chomah. And there was ketzef gadol (great fury) against Yisroel; and they withdrew, and returned to their own land.</span>
