<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:1"> Now even if I should glory a little too much<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="2C 12:6">h</a></sup>in our samchut (authority)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="2C 13:10">i</a></sup>&#91;as Shlichim of Moshiach&#93; which Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu gave for building you up and not for tearing you down, I will make good my claim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Jer 1:10">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:19–21; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:5"> I did not intend to seem as if my iggrot were meant to frighten you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:17; SHEMOT 4:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:13–14"> For they say, “His iggrot indeed are weighty and strong but his physical presence is weak and his shprach (diction), as far as rhetoric is concerned, is despicable.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:20; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:2–3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:10"> Let such a one consider this: that the dvar we speak through iggrot when absent will become action we will very well do when present!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="2C 13:2,10">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MISHLE 27:2; MISHLE 26:12; LUKAS 18:11"> For we dare not to make a geder (classification) for ourselves or to compare ourselves with some of the ones commending themselves,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="2C 3:1; 5:12">l</a></sup>but when they measure themselves by the standard of one another or compare themselves with one another, they do not show seichel.</span>
</div>

We were the chalutzim (pioneers) who came with the Besuras HaGeulah of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:20; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:13"> We do not boast out of our depth, that is, in the labor of others; but tikvateinu is that, as your emunah grows, our sphere of Shlichus (Divine mission) among you will also be enlarged,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:24–28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:21"> So that we may abundantly proclaim the Besuras HaGeulah to the regions beyond you, without boasting in someone elseʼs Messianic chalutz accomplishments.</span>
