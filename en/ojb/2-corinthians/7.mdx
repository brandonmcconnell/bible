</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:33; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:12–13; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:9"> In your levavot make a cheder for us; we wronged no one, we ruined no one, we exploited no one.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2C 12:17">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:11–12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:11"> I do not say this to bring you under harsha'ah, for I have said before that you are in our levavot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2C 6:11-12; Pp 1:7">b</a></sup>whether to die with you or to live with you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2Sm 15:21">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:4; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:12; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:2"> I have much ometz lev (boldness) and confidence toward you. I glory much on your behalf. I have been filled with chozek (strength). I am filled to overflowing with simcha at all of our tzoros.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:13; DEVARIM 32:25; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:1"> For indeed when we had come into Macedonia<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ac 20:1-2; 2C 2:13">d</a></sup>we had no physical mano'ach (rest) but we had tzoros everywhere: battles on the outside, fears inside.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dt 32:25">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:3–4; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:16–17; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:13"> But Hashem who encourages the lowly, encouraged us<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isa 49:13; 2C 1:3-4">f</a></sup>by the coming of Titos;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:2; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:16"> And not only by the coming of him, but also by the encouragement by which he was encouraged over you, reporting to us your longing, your mourning, your kanous (zeal) for me, so that it caused me to have even more simcha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:19; YIRMEYAH 20:7–9; MATTITYAHU 26:21–22"> Because if indeed I caused you agmat nefesh (grief) by the iggeret, I do not regret it, though I did regret it, but I see that that iggeret grieved you only briefly.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="2C 2:4">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 15:7; YIRMEYAH 31:18–20; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:10–11"> Now I have simcha, not that you had agmat nefesh, but that your grief brought you to teshuva, for your agmat nefesh was in Hashem, so that by us you suffered in nothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:18; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:25–26"> For the agmat nefesh that is according to Hashem produces teshuva to Yeshu'at Eloheinu, not to be regretted, but the agmat nefesh of the Olam Hazeh produces mavet.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Mt 27:3-5; MJ 12:17">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:18; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:21–22; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:15"> For, hinei, see what zerizut (diligence) this agmat nefesh of Hashem has produced in you, what defense, what indignation, what yirat Shomayim, what longing, what kanous (zeal), what onesh (punishment)! At every point you proved yourselves to be without ashma (guilt) in the matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:9; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:5; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:17"> Then though I wrote an iggeret to you, it was not for the sake of the one having done hara'ah (the evil), nor for the sake of the one who was beleidikt (offended), but for the sake of your kanous (zeal) for us becoming manifest to you before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:26; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:13"> In this we have chozek (strength).<br />But in addition to our encouragement, we had still more simcha at the simcha of Titos, because his neshamah has been set at rest by all of you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:4; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:4; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:18–20"> Because if I have been somewhat boastful about you Corinthians to Titos, I was in this not put to shame, but just as everything we said to you is HaEmes, so also our boasting to Titos has proven true as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:9; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17"> And his lev (heart) goes out to you, all the more as he has zikaron of the mishma'at (obedience) of all of you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="2C 2:9">i</a></sup>as you received him B'YIRAH (with fear) and BIRA'DAH (trembling).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ps 2:11">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:3; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:8; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:21"> I have simcha that in everything I have confidence in you.</span>
</div>
