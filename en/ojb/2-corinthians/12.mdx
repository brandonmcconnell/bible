<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:7; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:20"> It is necessary for me to boast, nothing gained by it, yet I now come to marot (visions) and chazonot (revelations) of Adoneinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:17; YECHEZKEL 8:1–3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:17"> I have da'as of a man in Moshiach arba esreh shanah (fourteen years) before, whether in or out of the basar I do not have da'as, Hashem knows — such a man was snatched up and raptured to the raki'a haShlishi of Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And I have da'as that such a man, whether in or out of the basar I do not have da'as, Hashem knows,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKAS 23:43; HISGALUS 2:7; YECHEZKEL 31:9"> That he was caught away into Gan-Eden and heard inexpressible devarim which to utter such words Bnei Adam have no heter (permit).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:30; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:9–10"> On behalf of such a one I will boast, but on behalf of myself I will not boast, except in my weaknesses.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2C 11:30">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:16; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:31; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:11"> But if I desire to boast, I will not be a yold (fool)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2C 10:8; 11:16">b</a></sup>for I will speak HaEmes; but I spare you, lest anyone gives me credit beyond what he sees in me or hears in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 33:55; SHOFETIM 2:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:25–26"> Therefore, lest I should be too exalted especially by the excess of my chazonot, there was given to me a kotz (thorn, splinter) in my basar, a malach of HaSatan, for the purpose of using his fists on me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Job 2:6">c</a></sup>lest I should be too exalted.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Num 33:55">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:7; MATTITYAHU 26:39–44; SHMUEL ALEF 15:11"> I davened shalosh pe'amim, calling upon Adoneinu that it might depart from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:13; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:10; YESHAYAH 40:29–31"> And HaAdon said to me, “My Chesed is ad kahn (sufficient for the purpose, enough) for you, for My ko'ach (power) is perfected in weakness.” With lev samei'ach therefore will I boast in my weaknesses that the gevurah of Moshiach might be a shelter over me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1Kgs 19:12">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:3"> Therefore, I take pleasure in weaknesses, in insults, in hardships, in persecutions, in tzoros on behalf of Moshiach; for whenever I am weak, then the gibbor (strong man) am I!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Pp 4:11,13">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:5; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:8–10"> I have been a yold! You forced me. For I ought to be commended by you, for in nothing I was behind the most groise (eminent) Shlichim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="2C 11:5">g</a></sup>even if I am nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:18–19; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:4; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:2"> Indeed the otot (signs) of the Shliach of Moshiach were brought about among you in all savlanut (patience), both by otot and moftim and gevurot.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ro 15:19">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:6; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:7–9"> For in what have you been short-changed compared to the rest of Moshiachʼs Kehillot, except that I myself was not a burden on you?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="2C 11:9">i</a></sup>Do pardon me this slight!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MISHLE 19:14; BERESHIS 24:35–36; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:33"> Hinei, this is the pa'am shlishit I am ready to come to you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="2C 13:1">j</a></sup>and I will not be a burden; for I seek not yours but you, for yeladim ought not to lay up for their horim (parents), but horim for their yeladim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Prov 19:14">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:17"> But with simcha I will expend and be utterly expended for the nefashot (souls) of you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Pp 2:17">l</a></sup>If more abundantly I have ahavah for you, are you to have less ahavah for me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:5; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:2–3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:13"> But let the matter of my being a burden on you be as it may, some say, “Crafty jack that I am, I took you by ormah (cunning).”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gn 3:1">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:17; MELACHIM BAIS 5:20–27"> Now I didn't take advantage of any of you through anyone I sent to you, did I?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:33–35"> I urged Titos to go, and I sent with him the Ach b'Moshiach;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="2C 8:6,16-18">n</a></sup>Titos didn't take advantage of you, did he? Did we not walk our derech by the same Ruach Hakodesh? We did not march out of step, did Titos and I?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:26; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:8"> All along have you been thinking that we are making a hitstaddekut (defense, apology) before you? No, before Hashem in Moshiach we speak! Everything we do, Chaverim, is for the sake of your chozek (strength) and edification!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:19–21; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:29"> For I fear lest efsher (perhaps) when I come I may not find you as I wish<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="2C 2:1-4">o</a></sup>and that you may not find me as you wish; I fear lest efsher there be merivah (strife), kinah (jealousy), ka'as (anger), machalokot (divisions), lashon hora (evil speech), ga'avah (conceit), commotions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:2; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:2–3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:5"> Lest when I come again Hashem Elohai may make me anav (humble) before you, and I should have agmat nefesh over many of the ones who committed averos before,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="2C 13:2">p</a></sup>and have not made teshuva from the tumah (uncleanness) and zenut (fornication) and debauchery which they practiced.</span>
</div>
