<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:1–2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:8"> You are our iggeret, written on our levavot, being known and being read by kol Bnei Adam.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1C 9:2">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 11:19; YIRMEYAH 31:33; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:10"> And you show that you are an iggeret from Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, prepared by us, having been written not with ink but with the Ruach Hakodesh of the Elohim Chayim, not on luchot of stone<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ex 24:12; 31:18; 32:15,16; 34:1; Dt 9:10,11">d</a></sup>but on luchot of lev basar.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Jer 31:33">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:14"> Such is the bitachon (confidence) that we have through Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach toward Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:10"> Not that we are competent from ourselves to claim anything as of ourselves, but our competence is from Hashem,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="2C 2:16">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:63; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:6; YIRMEYAH 31:31"> Who also made us competent as klei kodesh mesharetim (ministers) of a Brit Chadasha<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Jer 31:31">g</a></sup>— not b'khol chomer hadin (not by strict letter of the law, legalism), but of the Ruach Hakodesh: for kol chomer hadin (strict letter of the law) kills,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Dt 27:26; Ro 8:1">h</a></sup>but the Ruach Hakodesh gives Chayim —</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:9–11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:4; SHEMOT 31:18"> Now if the Sherut avodas kodesh (ministry) of mavet chiseled in letters engraved on luchot of stone came with kavod, so that the Bnei Yisroel were not able to gaze into the face of Moshe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Rabbeinu">i</a></sup>because of P'NI MOSHE KI KARON<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ex 34:35">j</a></sup>(face of Moshe Rabbeinu that he was radiant) because of the kavod of his face, the kavod which is fading,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ex 34:29-34">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:14"> Then how much more will the avodas kodesh ministry of the Ruach Hakodesh come in kavod (glory)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:21–22; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:18–21; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:21"> For if there was kavod in the Sherut avodas kodesh of harsha'ah (condemnation),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Dt 27:26; Ro 8:1">l</a></sup>how much more abounds in kavod the ministry of tzedek,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Dan 9:24">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:17; CHAGGAI 2:7–9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:13"> For indeed what once had kavod has lost its kavod<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Ex 34:29-30">n</a></sup>on account of the surpassing kavod (glory).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:20–21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 8:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:21–25"> For if the thing which now is fading away came with kavod, much more has that which remains come in kavod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:20"> Since, then, we have such a tikvah (hope), we act with much boldness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:33–35; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:1–9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:14–15"> And we are not as Moshe,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Rabbeinu">o</a></sup>who was putting HAMASVEH AL PANAV<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Ex 34:33,35">p</a></sup>(the veil over his face) so that the Bnei Yisroel might not see the end of the fading kavod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:3–4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:25"> But their minds were hardened;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Ro 11:25">q</a></sup>for until the present day the same veil remains at the Kri'at HaSefer Torah (the reading of Torah in shul), because the veil is taken away in Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:27–29"> But even today, whenever Moshe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Rabbeinu">r</a></sup>is being read, a veil lies on their levavot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:34; YESHAYAH 29:18; YESHAYAH 25:7"> But whenever one turns to Adoneinu HAMASVEH is taken away.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Ex 34:34; Isa 25:7; Ro 11:23-26">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:13; YOCHANAN 8:32"> Now Adoneinu is HaRuach and where HaRuach Adoneinu is, there is cherut (freedom).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Isa 61:1,2; Yn 7:39; 8:32,36; Ro 8:2; Ga 5:1,13">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:22–24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:2"> Now all of us, with unveiled faces, seeing the kavod of Adoneinu<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Ex 16:7; 24:17">u</a></sup>as if reflected in a mirror, are being transformed into the same demut from kavod to kavod, even as from HaAdon, HaRuach.</span>
