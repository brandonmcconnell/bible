<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:7; HISGALUS 2:2; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1"> For such ones are shlichei sheker, deceitful po'alim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Pp 3:2">k</a></sup>transforming themselves in a masquerade as shlichim of Moshiach.</span>
