<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:19; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:7"> But we ourselves have had the gezar din (verdict) of mishpat mavet (a death sentence) in ourselves so that we should not have emunah (faith) in ourselves but in Hashem, Mechayyei Mesim (Who Revivest the Dead).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Shemoneh Esreh, Jer 17:5,7">g</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:5–7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:21–22"> It was my cheshbon (plan) to visit you by way of Macedonia and again from Macedonia to come to you, and by you to be sent on to Yehudah (Judea).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Ac 19:21; 1C 16:5,6">p</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:8; HISGALUS 1:17; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9"> For the Zun fun der Oybershter, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, the One having been proclaimed among you by us, by Silvanus (Sila) and Timotiyos and me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Ac 18:5">r</a></sup>was not a “Ken and Lo”; but in Him it has always been “Ken.”</span>
