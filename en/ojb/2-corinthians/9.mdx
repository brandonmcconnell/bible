<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:4–15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:9–10"> Concerning your Messianic avodas kodesh sherut for the Kedoshim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2C 8:4,20">a</a></sup>it is superfluous for me to send you this iggeret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:24"> For I have da'as of your readiness to involve yourself in Messianic Jewish ministry and this is the subject of my glorying to the Macedonians, saying that Achaia has been prepared since last shanah and your kanous has been mezarez (a spur into action) to most of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:6; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:14; LETTER TO TITOS 3:1"> And I am sending the Achim b'Moshiach lest our glorying on behalf of you should be made empty in this respect, that you be ready, as I was saying you would be,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:26; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:17; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:24"> Lest perhaps if some Macedonians should come with me and they find you unprepared, we should have bushah, to say nothing of you, in this situation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BERESHIS 33:11; SHMUEL ALEF 25:27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:17"> Therefore, I considered it necessary to encourage the Achim b'Moshiach, that they should go on ahead to you and, having arranged in advance the bountiful terumah (contribution) of the havtacha you made previously so that this matanah would be ready so as to be a bracha and not an exaction.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MISHLE 11:24–25; LUKAS 6:38; MISHLE 22:9"> Note this: the one sowing sparingly will also reap sparingly, and the one sowing for a bracha of bounty will also reap a bracha of bounty.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Prov 11:24,25; 22:9">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:35; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:17"> Each of you should give as he has decided previously in his lev (heart), not giving bedieved (begrudgingly) nor out of necessity; for Hashem loves a cheerful giver.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ex 25:2; Dt 15:10; Prov 22:8TARGUM HASHIVIM">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:18–19; MISHLE 10:22"> And Hashem is able to cause to abound to you all Chen v'Chesed Hashem that in everything, always, having all sufficiency, you may abound to every mitzvah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 112:9; TEHILLIM 112:3; MISHLE 21:21"> As it has been written, PIZAR NATAN LAEVYONIM TZIDKATO OMEDET LA'AD (He scattered, he gave to the poor, His Righteousness endures forever).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ps 112:9; Mal 3:10">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YESHAYAH 55:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:6; HOSHEA 10:12"> Now the one supplying ZERA (seed) to the sower and LECHEM for okhel (food)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Isa 55:10">e</a></sup>will supply and will multiply your zera and will increase the p'ri of the Tzedek of you;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hos 10:12TARGUM HASHIVIM">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:15; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:12–14; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:17–18"> You will be enriched in every ma'aseh chesed to all generosity, which produces through us hodayah (thanksgiving) to Hashem;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="2C 1:11; 4:15">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:14–15; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:4–7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:25"> Because the avodas kodesh of this tzedakah is not only filling up the things lacking of the Kedoshim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="2C 8:14">h</a></sup>but is also abounding through hodayah rabbah (much thanksgiving) to Hashem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:16; MATTITYAHU 5:16; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:12"> Through the proof of this Messianic Jewish sherut you bring kavod to Hashem on the basis of your submission to your Ani Ma'amin Hoda'a confession of the Besuras HaGeulah of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and on the basis of the generosity of your terumah (contribution) to them and to all kol Bnei Adam;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:18–19"> And, reciprocally, the Messianic Jews in Yerushalayim will daven in intercession for you, having great ahavah in Moshiach for you, because of the surpassing Chen v'Chesed Hashem upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:20; YOCHANAN 3:16"> Baruch Hashem for his indescribable matanah!</span>
</div>
