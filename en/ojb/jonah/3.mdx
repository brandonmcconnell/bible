<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 2:7; YIRMEYAH 1:17; YONAH 3:3"> Arise, go unto Nineveh, that ir hagedolah, and preach unto it the preaching that I give thee.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:28–29; TEHILLIM 80:10; BERESHIS 22:3"> So Yonah arose, and went unto Nineveh, according to the Devar Hashem. Now Nineveh was an exceeding ir gedolah of a journey of shloshet yamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEVARIM 18:22; YIRMEYAH 18:7–10; MELACHIM BAIS 20:1"> And Yonah began to enter into the city one dayʼs journey, and he cried out and preached, and said, Yet arba'im yamim (40 days), and Nineveh shall be overthrown.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKAS 11:32; MATTITYAHU 12:41; YOEL 2:12–17"> So the people of Nineveh had emunah in Elohim, and proclaimed a tzom, and put on sackcloth, from their gedolim even to their ketanim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:6–10; ESTHER 4:1–4; DANIEL 9:3"> For word came unto HaMelech of Nineveh, and he arose from his kisse, and he laid aside his royal robe, and covered him with sackcloth, and sat in ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:3; YOEL 1:18; YOEL 2:15–16"> And he caused it to be proclaimed and published throughout Nineveh by the decree of HaMelech and his gedolim, saying, Let neither man nor beast, herd nor flock, taste any thing; let them not eat, nor drink mayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YONAH 1:6; YESHAYAH 55:6–7; YECHEZKEL 18:21–24"> But let man and beast be covered with sackcloth, and cry mightily unto Elohim; yea, let them turn every one from his evil way, and from the chamas that is in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 12:22; YOEL 2:13–14; TEHILLIM 106:45"> Who can tell if HaElohim will turn and relent, and turn away from His fierce anger, that we perish not?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 18:8; MELACHIM ALEF 21:27–29; LUKAS 15:20"> And HaElohim saw their ma'asim, that they shavu (turned) from their derech hara'ah; and HaElohim relented of hara'ah, that He had said that He would do unto them; and He did it not.</span>
