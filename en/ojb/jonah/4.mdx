<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 103:10–14; YESHAYAH 39:2; ESTHER 5:9"> And Hashem Elohim prepared a climbing gourd, and made it to come up over Yonah, that it might be a tzel (shade) over his rosh, to deliver him from his displeasure. So Yonah had simchah gedolah about the climbing gourd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YOEL 1:12; IYOV 1:21; TEHILLIM 102:10"> But HaElohim prepared a tola'at (worm) when the shachar (dawn) came the next day, and it chewed the climbing gourd that it withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YONAH 4:3; TEHILLIM 121:6; YECHEZKEL 19:12"> And it came to pass, when the shemesh did arise, that Elohim prepared a vehement scorching east ruach (wind); and the shemesh beat upon the rosh Yonah, that he grew faint, and wanted to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Moshiach is the Navi like Moshe, the Navi like Yonah, whom Mavet swallows and then vomits up so that he can divide the spoil with those whose chet he bears away to death like the Yom Kippur scapegoat (see Isa 53:12).">b</a></sup>die, and said, It is better for me to die than to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="IYOV 5:2; IYOV 18:4; SHOFETIM 16:16"> And Elohim said to Yonah: Doest thou well to be angry for the climbing gourd? And he said: I do well to be angry, even unto mot (death).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 20:31; BERESHIS 17:12"> Then said Hashem: Thou hast had pity on the climbing gourd, though thou hast not labored for it, neither madest it grow; which came up a ben lailah, and perished a ben lailah;</span>
