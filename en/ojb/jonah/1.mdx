<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 11:32; LUKAS 11:29–30; MELACHIM BAIS 14:25"> Now the Devar Hashem came unto Yonah ben Amittai, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:20–21; BERESHIS 10:11; YESHAYAH 58:1"> Arise, go to Nineveh, that ha'ir hagedolah (great city), and preach against it; for their wickedness is come up before Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TEHILLIM 139:7–12; BERESHIS 4:16; YEHOSHUA 19:46"> But Yonah rose up to run away unto Tarshish from the presence of Hashem, and went down to Yafo; and he found an oniyah going to Tarshish; so he paid the fare thereof, and went down into it, to go with them unto Tarshish from the presence of Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="TEHILLIM 107:23–31; MATTITYAHU 8:24–27; SHEMOT 10:19"> But Hashem sent out a ruach gedolah (great wind) into the yam (sea), and there was a sa'ar gadol (mighty tempest) in the yam, so that the oniyah was like to be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 2:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:18–19; MELACHIM ALEF 18:26"> Then the mariners were afraid, and cried every man unto his g-d, and cast forth the cargoes that were in the oniyah into the yam, to lighten it of them. But Yonah was gone down into the hold of the ship; and he lay, and was fast asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YONAH 3:9; TEHILLIM 107:28–29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:14"> So the shipmaster came to him, and said unto him, What meanest thou, O sleeper? Arise, call upon Eloheicha, if so be that HaElohim will think upon us, that we perish not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MISHLE 16:33; YEHOSHUA 7:13–18; SHMUEL ALEF 10:20–21"> And they said every one to his fellow, Come, and let us cast goralot (lots), that we may have da'as for whose cause this ra'ah is upon us. So they cast goralot (lots), and the goral (lot) fell upon Yonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 47:3; SHMUEL ALEF 30:13; YEHOSHUA 7:19"> Then said they unto him, Tell us, we pray thee, for whose cause ra'ah hazot (this evil) is upon us; What is thine occupation? And whence comest thou? What is thy country? And of what people art thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 146:5–6; TEHILLIM 95:5–6; NECHEMYAH 9:6"> And he said unto them, Ivri anochi (I am a Hebrew); and I fear Hashem, Elohei HaShomayim, which hath made the yam and the yabashah (dry land).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gn 1:9">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YONAH 1:3; IYOV 27:22; YEHOSHUA 7:25"> Then were the men of yirah gedolah (great terror), and said unto him: Why hast thou done this? For the men knew that he was running away from the presence of Hashem, because he had told them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 21:1–6; MICHOH 6:6–7; SHMUEL ALEF 6:2–3"> Then said they unto him, What shall we do unto thee, that the yam against us may be calm?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Isa 53:5,8; Mt 12:39-40; 16:4">b</a></sup>For the yam did rage and was tempestuous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 24:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 21:17; YEHOSHUA 7:12"> And he said unto them, Take me up, and cast me forth into the yam; so shall the yam be calm unto you: for I know that for my sake this sa'ar hagadol (great tempest, storm) is upon you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MISHLE 21:30; IYOV 34:29"> Nevertheless the men rowed hard to bring it to the yabashah; but they could not: for the yam did rage, and was tempestuous against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 115:3; TEHILLIM 135:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:11"> Wherefore they cried unto Hashem, and said, We beseech Thee, Hashem, we beseech Thee, let us not perish for this manʼs nefesh, and lay not upon us dahm naki (innocent blood); for Thou, Hashem, hast done just as it pleased Thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 8:24; TEHILLIM 93:3–4; TEHILLIM 107:29"> So they took up Yonah, and cast him forth into the yam;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Isa 53:5,8; Mt 12:39-40; 16:4">c</a></sup>and the yam ceased from her raging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="TEHILLIM 50:14; BERESHIS 8:20; DANIEL 6:26"> Then the men feared Hashem with a yirah gedolah, and offered a sacrifice unto Hashem, and vowed nedarim.</span>
</div>
</div>
