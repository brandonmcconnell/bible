<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHEMOT 18:21–22; DANIEL 5:31; ESTHER 1:1"> And Daryavesh the Mede took the malchut, being about 62 years old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DANIEL 2:48–49; DANIEL 5:29; ESTHER 7:4"> It pleased Daryavesh to set over the malchut an hundred and twenty satraps, which should be over the whole malchut;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DANIEL 5:14; MISHLE 22:29; BERESHIS 41:38–41"> And over these three overseers, of whom Daniel was one, that the satraps might give accounts unto them, and that the king should suffer no loss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:15; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:12"> Then this Daniel distinguished himself above the overseers and satraps, because an excellent ruach was in him; and the king thought to set him over the whole malchut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:13–16; ESTHER 3:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:20–21"> Then the overseers and satraps sought to find occasion against Daniel concerning the malchut; but they could find none occasion nor fault; forasmuch as he was trustworthy, neither was there any negligence or corrupt thing found in him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DANIEL 2:4; NECHEMYAH 2:3; DANIEL 6:21"> Then said these anashim, We shall not find any occasion against this Daniel, except we find it against him concerning the dat (law, religion) of his G-d.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 59:3; TEHILLIM 62:4; DANIEL 3:27"> Then these overseers and satraps assembled together to the king, and said thus unto him, King Daryavesh, L'olam yicheyeh (live for ever!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DANIEL 6:15; ESTHER 1:19; DANIEL 6:12"> All the overseers of the malchut, the administrators, and the satraps, the counselors, and the governors, have consulted together to establish a royal statute, and to make a firm decree, that whosoever shall make a petition of any g-d or man for shloshim yom (thirty days), except of thee, O king, he shall be cast into the den of lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 146:3; TEHILLIM 118:9; TEHILLIM 62:9–10"> Now, O king, establish the decree and sign the ketav (writing, document), that it cannot be changed, according to the law of the Medes and Persians, which altereth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:29; TEHILLIM 55:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:17–18"> Wherefore king Daryavesh signed the written decree.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TEHILLIM 37:32–33; TEHILLIM 10:9; DANIEL 6:6"> Now when Daniel knew that the ketav was signed, he went into his bais; and his windows being open in his chamber toward Yerushalayim, he kneeled down three times that day, and davened, and gave thanks before his G-d, as he had been doing before this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DANIEL 6:8; DANIEL 3:8–12; ESTHER 1:19"> Then these anashim assembled, and found Daniel davening and making supplication before his G-d.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DANIEL 3:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:29; DANIEL 2:25"> Then they came near, and spoke before the king concerning the kingʼs decree: Hast thou not signed a decree, that every man that shall make a petition of any g-d or man within shloshim yom, except of thee, O king, shall be cast into the den of lions? The king answered and said, The thing is true, according to the law of the Medes and Persians, which cannot be changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MARKOS 6:26; MATTITYAHU 27:17–24; SHMUEL BAIS 3:28–29"> Then answered they and said before the king, That Daniel, which is of the Bnei Golus of Yehudah, shows no regard for thee, O king, nor the decree that thou hast signed, but maketh his petition three times a day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DANIEL 6:12; TEHILLIM 94:20–21; DANIEL 6:8"> Then the king, when he heard these words, was greatly displeased with himself, and set his heart on Daniel to deliver him, and he did strive till the going down of the shemesh to deliver him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="TEHILLIM 37:39–40; IYOV 5:19; TEHILLIM 91:14–16"> Then these anashim assembled unto the king, and said unto the king, Have da'as, O king, that the law of the Medes and Persians is, That no decree nor statute which the king establisheth may be changed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EKHAH 3:53; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:4; MATTITYAHU 27:60–66"> Then the king commanded, and they brought Daniel, and cast him into the den of lions. Now the king spoke and said unto Daniel, Eloheicha (Thy G-d) whom thou servest continually, He will deliver thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ESTHER 6:1; DANIEL 2:1; SHMUEL BAIS 12:16–17"> And an even (stone) was brought, and laid upon the mouth of the den; and the king sealed it with his own signet ring, and with the signet ring of his nobles, that the purpose might not be changed concerning Daniel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:5; MARKOS 16:2; MATTITYAHU 28:1"> Then the king went to his heikhal, and spent the night fasting, neither were entertainments brought before him, and his sheynah fled from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 16:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:25; DANIEL 6:27"> Then the king arose very early in the boker, and went in haste unto the den of lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DANIEL 2:4; DANIEL 6:6; NECHEMYAH 2:3"> And when he came to the den, he cried with an anguished voice unto Daniel; and the king spoke and said to Daniel, O Daniel, eved of HaElohim HaChai, is thy G-d, whom thou servest continually, able to deliver thee from the lions?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:33; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:11"> Then said Daniel unto the king, O king, L'olam yicheyeh (live for ever).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:20; MARKOS 9:23; DANIEL 3:27–28"> My G-d hath sent his malach, and hath shut the lionsʼ mouths that they have not hurt me; forasmuch as before Him innocence was found in me; and also before thee, O king, have I done no rah (wrong).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YESHAYAH 38:13; ESTHER 7:10; DEVARIM 24:16"> Then was the king exceedingly glad for him, and commanded that they should take Daniel up out of the den. So Daniel was taken up out of the den, and no manner of hurt was found upon him, because he had emunah in his G-d.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DANIEL 4:1; EZRA 1:1–2; ESTHER 3:12"> And the king commanded, and they brought those anashim which had accused Daniel, and they cast them into the den of lions, them, their children, and their wives; and the lions overpowered them, and broke all their bones in pieces before they ever came to the bottom of the den.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DANIEL 4:34; LUKAS 1:33; DANIEL 3:29"> Then king Daryavesh wrote unto all people, nations, and languages, that dwell in kol ha'aretz; Shalom aleichem l'machbir (Peace be multiplied unto you).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DANIEL 4:2–3; TEHILLIM 18:50; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:30"> I make a decree, That in every dominion of my malchut men must tremble and fear before the G-d of Daniel,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">for He is HaElohim HaChai,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and stedfast l'olamim (forever),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and His malchut that which shall not be destroyed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and His dominion shall be even ad hasof (unto the end).</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DANIEL 1:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:22–2; YESHAYAH 44:28–1"> <span className="pl pl-1">He delivereth and rescueth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and He worketh otot and mofetim</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in Shomayim and in ha'aretz,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Who hath delivered Daniel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from the power of the lions.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> So this Daniel prospered in the reign of Daryavesh, and in the reign of Koresh (Cyrus) the Persian.</span>
</div>
