<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="CHABAKUK 2:19; YIRMEYAH 16:20; YESHAYAH 46:6"> Nevukhadnetzar the king made a tzelem of gold, whose height was threescore cubits, and the breadth thereof six cubits; he set it up in the plain of Dura, in the province of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DANIEL 3:27; MISHLE 29:12; MELACHIM ALEF 12:32"> Then Nevukhadnetzar the king sent to gather together the satraps, the prefects, and the governors, the judges, the treasurers, the counselors, the magistrates, and all the rulers of the provinces, to come to the dedication of the tzelem which Nevukhadnetzar the king had set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HISGALUS 17:17; HISGALUS 17:13; HISGALUS 13:13–16"> Then the satraps, the prefects, and governors, the judges, the treasurers, the counselors, the magistrates, and all the rulers of the provinces, were gathered together unto the dedication of the tzelem that Nevukhadnetzar the king had set up; and they stood before the tzelem that Nevukhadnetzar had set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DANIEL 4:1; DANIEL 6:25; MICHOH 6:16"> Then a karoz (herald) cried aloud, To you it is commanded, O people, nations, and languages,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DANIEL 3:15; DANIEL 3:10; DANIEL 3:7"> That at what time ye hear the sound of the horn, flute, harp, lyre, psaltery, pipes, and all kinds of music, ye fall down and worship the golden tzelem that Nevukhadnetzar the king hath set up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:50; MATTITYAHU 13:42; YIRMEYAH 29:22"> And whoso falleth not down and worshipeth shall the same hour be cast into the midst of a burning fiery attun (furnace).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HISGALUS 17:8; HISGALUS 13:3; HISGALUS 13:8"> Therefore at that time, when all the people heard the sound of the horn, flute, harp, lyre, psaltery, and all kinds of music, all the people, the nations, and the languages, fell down and worshiped the golden tzelem that Nevukhadnetzar the king had set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZRA 4:12–16; DANIEL 6:12–13; ESTHER 3:8–9"> Wherefore at that time certain Kasdim (Chaldeans) came near, and accused the Yehudim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DANIEL 5:10; DANIEL 6:6; DANIEL 6:21"> They spoke and said to the king Nevukhadnetzar, O king, live for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DANIEL 3:4–7; DANIEL 6:12; ESTHER 3:12–14"> Thou, O king, hast made a decree, that every man that shall hear the sound of the horn, flute, harp, lyre, psaltery, and pipes, and all kinds of music, shall fall down and worship the golden tzelem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And whoso falleth not down and worshipeth, that he should be cast into the midst of a burning fiery attun (furnace).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DANIEL 6:13; DANIEL 2:49; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:7"> There are certain Yehudim whom thou hast set over the affairs of the province of Babylon, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego; these men, O king, have disregarded thee; they serve<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="pay-lammed-chet, serve as deity">a</a></sup>not thy g-ds, nor worship the golden tzelem which thou hast set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DANIEL 3:19; DANIEL 2:12; ESTHER 3:5–6"> Then Nevukhadnetzar in his rage and fury commanded to bring Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. Then they brought these men before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 50:2; DANIEL 3:1; YESHAYAH 46:1"> Nevukhadnetzar spoke and said unto them, Is it true, O Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, do ye not serve my g-ds, nor worship the golden tzelem which I have set up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:29; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:15–17; SHEMOT 5:2"> Now if ye be ready that at what time ye hear the sound of the horn, flute, harp, lyre, psaltery, and pipes, and all kinds of music, ye fall down and worship the tzelem which I have made, well; but if ye worship not, ye shall be cast the same hour into the midst of a burning fiery attun; and who is that G-d that shall deliver you out of my hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DANIEL 1:7; DANIEL 3:12"> Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, answered and said to the king, O Nevukhadnetzar, we are not needful to answer thee in this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:31; LUKAS 1:37; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:25"> If it be so, Eloheinu whom we serve is able to deliver us from the burning fiery furnace, and He will deliver us out of thine hand, O king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:29–32; MATTITYAHU 10:39; HISGALUS 2:10–11"> But if not, be it known unto thee, O king, that we will not be the ones serving as deity<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="pey-lammed-chet">b</a></sup>thy g-ds, nor worship the golden tzelem which thou hast set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DANIEL 3:13; VAYIKRA 26:24; MISHLE 21:24"> Then was Nevukhadnetzar full of fury, and the expression on his visage was changed against Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego; therefore he spoke, and commanded that they should heat the attun seven times hotter than usual.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:25; DANIEL 3:15"> And he commanded certain gibborim (mighty men) of valor that were in his army to bind Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, and to cast them into the burning fiery attun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then these anashim (men) were bound in their mantles, their tunics, and their turbans, and their other garments, and were cast into the midst of the burning fiery furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:33; MISHLE 21:18; MISHLE 11:8"> Therefore because the kingʼs commandment was severe, and the furnace exceedingly hot, the flames of the eish (fire) violently killed those men that took up Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DANIEL 6:16–17; YIRMEYAH 38:6; TEHILLIM 34:19"> And these three men, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, fell down bound into the midst of the burning fiery furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DANIEL 6:7; DANIEL 6:22; DANIEL 3:9–10"> Then Nevukhadnetzar the king was astonished, and rose up in haste, and spoke, and said unto his counselors, Did not we cast shloshah anashim (three men) bound into the midst of the eish (fire)? They answered and said unto the king, True, O king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YESHAYAH 43:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:5; MARKOS 16:18"> He answered and said, Lo, I see anashim arba'ah (four men) free (not bound, loose), walking in the midst of the eish (fire), and they have no hurt; and the form of the fourth is like the Bar Elohin (Ben Elohim, Hebrew).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DANIEL 3:17; YESHAYAH 52:12; HISGALUS 19:5"> Then Nevukhadnetzar came near to the door of the burning fiery furnace, and spoke, and said, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, ye servants of El Elyon (G-d Most High), come forth, and come hither. Then Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, came forth of the midst of the eish (fire).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YESHAYAH 43:2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:34; YESHAYAH 26:11"> And the satraps, governors, and administrators, and the kingʼs counselors, being gathered together, saw these men, upon whose bodies the eish (fire) had no power, nor was a hair of their head singed, neither were their garments affected, nor the smell of eish (fire) had passed on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:7–8; TEHILLIM 34:7–8; TEHILLIM 84:11–12"> Then Nevukhadnetzar spoke, and said, Baruch Elohim shel Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, Who hath sent His malach, and delivered His servants that trusted in Him, and frustrated the kingʼs word, and yielded their bodies, that they might not serve,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="pey-lammed-chet, serve as deity">c</a></sup>nor worship any g-d, except their own G-d.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DANIEL 2:5; TEHILLIM 76:10; DEVARIM 32:31"> Therefore I make a decree, that every people, nation, and language, which speak any thing amiss against the G-d of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, shall be cut in pieces, and their houses shall be made a pile of rubble, because there is no other g-d that can deliver like this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DANIEL 2:49; SHMUEL ALEF 2:30; DANIEL 3:12"> Then the king promoted Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, in the province of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Nevukhadnetzar the king, unto all people, nations and languages, that dwell in all the earth; Shalom aleichem l'machbir (Peace be multiplied unto you).</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> I thought it good to show the otot (signs) and mofetim (wonders) that El Elyon hath wrought toward me.</span>
</div>
