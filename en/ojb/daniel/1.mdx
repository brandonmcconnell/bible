<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:5–7; MELACHIM BAIS 24:1–2; MELACHIM BAIS 24:13"> In the third year of the reign of Y'hoyakim Melech Yehudah came Nevukhadnetzar king of Babylon unto Yerushalayim, and besieged it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:7; ZECHARYAH 5:11; YIRMEYAH 27:19–20"> And Hashem gave Y'hoyakim Melech Yehudah into his hand, with part of the vessels of the Beis HaElohim; which he carried into the land of Shinar to the house of his g-d; and he brought the vessels into the treasure house of his g-d.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YESHAYAH 39:7; MELACHIM BAIS 20:17–18; MELACHIM BAIS 24:15"> And the king spoke unto Ashpenaz the master of his eunuchs, that he should bring certain of the Bnei Yisroel, and of the kingʼs zera (seed), and of the princes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DANIEL 5:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:22; MISHLE 22:29"> Yeladim in whom was no blemish, but well-favored, and skillful in all chochmah (wisdom), and cunning in da'as (knowledge), and understanding science, and such as had ability in them to stand in the heikhal hamelech (kingʼs palace), and whom they might teach the sefer and lashon of the Kasdim (Chaldeans).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DANIEL 1:19; MELACHIM ALEF 10:8; DANIEL 1:8"> And the king appointed them a daily provision of the kingʼs food, and of the yayin which he drank, so nourishing them shanim shalosh (three years), that at the end thereof they might stand before the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 14:14; MATTITYAHU 24:15; DANIEL 2:17"> Now among these were of the Bnei Yehudah, Daniel, Chananyah, Mishael, and Azaryah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DANIEL 5:12; DANIEL 4:8; DANIEL 2:49"> Unto whom the chief of the eunuchs gave names; for he gave unto Daniel the name of Belteshatzar; and to Chananyah, of Shadrakh; and to Mishael, of Meshach; and to Azaryah, of Abednego.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 141:4; YECHEZKEL 4:13–14; VAYIKRA 11:45–47"> But Daniel purposed in his heart that he would not defile himself with the portion of the kingʼs delicacies, nor with the yayin which he drank; therefore he requested of the chief of the eunuchs that he might not defile himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 39:21; MISHLE 16:7; TEHILLIM 106:46"> Now G-d had brought Daniel into chesed and sympathy with the chief of the eunuchs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:16–18; YOCHANAN 12:42–43; MISHLE 29:25"> And the chief of the eunuchs said unto Daniel, I fear adoni hamelech, who hath appointed your meat and your drink; for why should he see your faces worse looking than the yeladim which are of your age? Then shall ye make me endanger my head to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then said Daniel to the steward, whom the chief of the eunuchs had set over Daniel, Chananyah, Mishael, and Azaryah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:2; DANIEL 1:16; BERESHIS 1:29–30"> Prove thy servants, I beseech thee, yamim asarim (ten days); and let them give us vegetables to eat, and mayim (water) to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then let our countenances be looked upon before thee, and the countenance of the yeladim that eat of the portion of the kingʼs meat, and as thou seest, deal with thy servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So he consented to them in this matter, and tested them yamim asarim (ten days).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:25; MISHLE 10:22; MELACHIM BAIS 4:42–44"> And at the end of ten days their countenances appeared fairer and fatter in flesh than all the yeladim which did eat the portion of the kingʼs meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DANIEL 1:12"> Thus the steward took away the portion of their meat, and the yayin that they should drink; and gave them vegetables.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 3:12; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5; DANIEL 2:21"> As for these four yeladim, G-d gave them knowledge and skill in all sefer and chochmah, and Daniel had understanding in all chazon (visions) and chalomot (dreams).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DANIEL 1:5"> Now at the end of the days that the king had said he would bring them in, then the chief of the eunuchs brought them in before Nevukhadnetzar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MISHLE 22:29; BERESHIS 41:46; DANIEL 1:5"> And the king communed with them; and among them all was found none like Daniel, Chananyah, Mishael, and Azaryah; therefore stood they before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 8:19; YESHAYAH 47:12–14; MELACHIM ALEF 4:29–34"> And in all matters of chochmah and binah that the king questioned them, he found them ten times better than all the magicians and astrologers that were in all his realm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DANIEL 6:28; DANIEL 10:1"> And Daniel continued even unto the first year of King Koresh (Cyrus).</span>
</div>
</div>
