<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DANIEL 11:1; DANIEL 1:21; DANIEL 5:31–1"> In the shnat achat (first year) of Daryavesh ben Achashverosh, by zera (descent) a Mede, who was made melech over the realm of the Kasdim (Chaldeans);</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 29:10; YIRMEYAH 25:11–12; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:21"> In the shnat achat of his reign, I, Daniel, received binah (understanding) from the Sfarim (books, Kitvei Hakodesh) of the number of the shanim, that the Devar Hashem was unto Yirmeyah HaNavi, with respect to the desolations of Yerushalayim, SHIVIM SHANAH must be completed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Jer 25:11; 29:10">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 33:3; YIRMEYAH 29:10–13; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:16–18"> And I set my face unto Adonoi HaElohim, to plead by tefillah and tachanunim (supplications), with a tzom, and sackcloth, and ashes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:28; SHEMOT 20:6; DEVARIM 5:10"> And I davened unto Hashem Elohai, and I made vidduy (confession of sin), and said, O Adonoi, HaEl HaGadol V'HaNorah (the great and dreadful G-d), Shomer HaBrit V'HaChesed (Who keepeth covenant and lovingkindness) to them that love Him, and to them that are shomrei mitzvot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MALACHI 3:7; DANIEL 9:11; DANIEL 9:15"> We have sinned, and we have committed iniquity, and we have done wickedly, and we have rebelled, even by departing from Thy mitzvot and from Thy mishpatim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DANIEL 9:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:15–16; LUKAS 20:10–12"> Neither have we heeded or listened unto Thy avadim the nevi'im, which spoke in Thy Name unto our Melachim, our Sarim (princes), and our Avot, and to kol Am HaAretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 44:15; YIRMEYAH 3:25; YIRMEYAH 2:26–27"> To Thee, Adonoi, is the tzedakah, but unto us is boshet hapanim (shame of face), as at yom hazeh; to the men of Yehudah, and to the yoshvei Yerushalayim, and unto kol Yisroel, that are near, and that are far off, through all the countries whither Thou hast driven them, because of their ma'al (betrayal, treachery) wherein they have dealt unfaithfully against Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DANIEL 9:6–7; EKHAH 1:7–8; EKHAH 1:18"> O Hashem, to us belongeth boshet hapanim, to our Melachim, to our Sarim (princes), and to our Avot, because we have sinned against Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:6–8; DANIEL 9:7; DANIEL 9:5"> To Adonoi Eloheinu belong the rachamim and the selichot (forgiveness), for we have rebelled against Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DANIEL 9:6; MELACHIM BAIS 18:12; NECHEMYAH 9:13–17"> Neither have we obeyed the voice of Hashem Eloheinu, to walk in His torot, which He has set before us by the hand of His avadim the Nevi'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 8:5–10; YESHAYAH 1:4–6; DEVARIM 28:15–68"> Yea, kol Yisroel has transgressed Thy Torah, even by departing, refusing to obey Thy kol (voice); therefore ha'alah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dt 29:18">b</a></sup>(the curse) is poured upon us, and hashevu'ah (the oath) that is written in the Torat Moshe the Eved HaElohim, because we have sinned against Him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Lv 26:14f; Dt 28:15f">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 5:9; EKHAH 1:12; IYOV 12:17"> And He hath confirmed His words, which He spoke concerning us, and concerning our shofetim who judged us, that He would bring upon us a ra'ah gedolah, so that there has not been done under kol HaShomayim what has been done to Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:15–68; YESHAYAH 9:13; DANIEL 9:11"> Even as it is written in the Torat Moshe, all hara'ah hazot (this evil) is come upon us, yet we have not mollified the face of Hashem Eloheinu, by turning away from avoneinu (our iniquities), and by having seichel (discernment) in Thy Emes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DANIEL 9:7; NECHEMYAH 9:33; TEHILLIM 51:14"> Therefore hath Hashem watched upon the rah, and brought it upon us; for Hashem Eloheinu is tzaddik in all His ma'asim which He doeth, for we obeyed not His kol (voice).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 9:10; SHEMOT 32:11; NECHEMYAH 1:10"> And now, Adonoi Eloheinu, Who hast brought Thy people forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim with a yad chazakah (mighty hand), and hast gotten Thee renown, as at yom hazeh, we have sinned, we have done wickedly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 8:3; YOEL 3:17; TEHILLIM 87:1–3"> O Adonoi, according to all Thy tzidkot, I beseech Thee, let Thine anger and Thy fury be turned away from Thy Ir (City) Yerushalayim, Thy Har Kodesh, because for chatta'einu, and for the avonot Avoteinu, Yerushalayim and Thy people are become a reproach, an object of scorn to all around about us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TEHILLIM 80:19; EKHAH 5:18; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:20"> Now therefore, shema, Eloheinu, hear the tefillah of Thy eved, and his tachanunim (supplications), and cause Thy face to shine upon Thy Mikdash<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Beis HaMikdash">d</a></sup>that is desolate, lema'an (for the sake of) Adonoi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YESHAYAH 37:17; YIRMEYAH 36:7; MELACHIM BAIS 19:16"> O Elohai, incline Thine ear, and shema; open Thine eyes, and behold our desolations, and HaIr which is called by Thy Name; for we do not present tachanuneinu (our supplications) before Thee on account of tzidkoteinu, but for the sake of Thy rachamim harabbim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DANIEL 9:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:12; AMOS 7:2"> Adonoi, hear; Adonoi, forgive; Adonoi, hearken and act; delay not, for Thine own sake, O Elohai; for Thy city and Thy people are called by Thy Name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 145:18; DANIEL 9:3–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:23"> And while I was speaking, and davening tefillos, and making vidduy (confession of sin) for me and for Ami Yisroel, and laying my techinnah (petition) before Hashem Elohai for the sake of the Har Kodesh of Elohai;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKAS 1:19; DANIEL 8:16; MELACHIM ALEF 18:36"> Yea, while I was davening in tefillah, even haish<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Dan 8:15">e</a></sup>Gavriel, whom I had seen in the chazon in the beginning, being caused to come in full flight, reached me about the time of the minchat erev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DANIEL 9:24–27; DANIEL 10:21; DANIEL 8:16"> And he instructed me, and spoke with me, and said, O Daniel, I am now come forth to make thee have seichel in binah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DANIEL 10:19; MATTITYAHU 24:15; DANIEL 10:11–12"> At the beginning of thy tachanunim (supplications) a commandment went forth, and I am come to make the word known; for thou art greatly valued; therefore understand the devar, and consider the vision.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:11–14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:10; YIRMEYAH 23:5–6"> Shivi'im heptads is decreed upon thy people and upon thy Ir Kodesh (Holy City), to restrain the peysha (transgression), and to make an end of chattat (sin), and to make kapporah for avon, and to bring in Tzedek Olamim (Everlasting Righteousness), and to seal up the chazon and navi, and to anoint the Kodesh HaKodashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EZRA 4:24; YESHAYAH 9:6; YESHAYAH 55:4"> Have da'as, therefore, and get seichel, that from the going forth of the decree to restore and to rebuild Yerushalayim unto Moshiach Nagid shall be shivah heptads, and threescore and two heptads; the rechov shall be built again, and the charutz, even in troublous times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:8; NACHUM 1:8; MARKOS 13:2"> And after threescore and two heptads, yikaret (will be cut off) Moshiach,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isa 53:8">f</a></sup>but not for himself;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 53:4-6,8">g</a></sup>and the troops of the coming nagid shall destroy the Ir and the Kodesh &#91;Beis Hamikdash&#93;;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="i.e., 70.C.E">h</a></sup>and the end thereof shall come with a flood, and unto the end there shall be war. Desolations are determined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:15; LUKAS 21:20; DANIEL 12:11"> And he shall confirm brit (covenant) with rabbim for one heptad; and in the midst of the heptad he shall cause the zevach and the minchah to cease, and on the kenaf (wing) of the abominations is one making desolate, even until the complete destruction, a destruction that is decreed, shall be poured out upon the Shomem (Desolator, Destroyer).</span>
</div>
