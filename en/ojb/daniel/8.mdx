<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DANIEL 11:4; DANIEL 9:2; DANIEL 7:28"> In the shnat shalosh (third year) of the reign of Belshatzar HaMelech, a chazon (vision) appeared unto me, Daniel, after that which appeared unto me in the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ESTHER 1:2; NECHEMYAH 1:1; BERESHIS 10:22"> And I beheld in a chazon; and it came to pass, while I was seeing, that I was in Shushan (Susa) the fortress, which is in the province of Eilam; and I saw in a chazon, and I was by the stream Ulai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DANIEL 8:20; DANIEL 10:5; ZECHARYAH 6:1"> Then I lifted up mine eyes, and saw, and, hinei, there stood before the stream a ram which had two karnayim (horns), and the two karnayim were high; but one was higher than the other, and the higher was coming up last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DANIEL 11:16; DANIEL 11:36; DANIEL 8:7"> I saw the ram pushing westward, and northward, and southward; so that no chayyot (beasts) might stand before him, neither was there any that could deliver out of his hand; but he did according to his will, and made a great display of strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DANIEL 8:21; DANIEL 11:3; DANIEL 8:8"> And as I was considering, hinei, a he-goat was coming from the west on the face of kol ha'aretz, and it was not touching the earth; and as to the goat it had a conspicuous keren (horn) between its eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he came to the ram that had two karnayim, which I had seen standing before the stream, and he ran against him with the wrath of his koach (strength).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DANIEL 7:7; DANIEL 11:11; YEHOSHUA 8:20"> And I saw him approaching the ram, and he was enraged against him, and he smote the ram, broke his two karnayim, and there was no koach in the ram to stand before him, and he cast him down to the ground, and trampled him; there was none to deliver the ram from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DANIEL 7:2; HISGALUS 7:1; DANIEL 5:20"> Therefore the he-goat was acting very presumptuously; and when he was powerful, the keren hagedolah (great horn),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., Alexanderʼs Empire">a</a></sup>was broken; and in the place of it came up conspicuously four, toward the arbah ruchot HaShomayim (the four winds of Heaven).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DANIEL 11:16; YECHEZKEL 20:6; YECHEZKEL 20:15"> And out of one of them came forth a little horn,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Antiochus Epiphanes">b</a></sup>which became exceeding great, toward the south, and toward the east, and toward the &#91;eretz&#93; hatzevi (the glorious &#91;land&#93;).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e., Eretz Israel">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YESHAYAH 14:13; HISGALUS 12:4; DANIEL 8:7"> And it became great, even unto the tzva HaShomayim; and it caused to fall down to the earth some of the host and some of the kokhavim it trampled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DANIEL 12:11; HISGALUS 13:5–7; DANIEL 8:25"> Yea, he magnified himself even unto the Sar HaTzava, and the daily sacrifice was taken away from Him, and the place of His Mikdash was cast down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 12:1; YESHAYAH 59:14; DANIEL 11:31–36"> And a tzva (army) was given over to oppose the daily sacrifice on account of peysha (transgression, rebellion), and it cast down emes to the ground; and it acted, and prospered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HISGALUS 11:2; HISGALUS 6:10; LUKAS 21:24"> Then I heard one kadosh (angel) speaking, and another kadosh said unto that certain kadosh which spoke, How long shall be the chazon concerning the daily sacrifice, and the desolating peysha (transgression), to give both the Kodesh (Sanctuary) and the tzva (host) to be trodden under foot?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DANIEL 12:7; DANIEL 7:25; HISGALUS 11:2–3"> And he said unto me, Unto erev-boker two thousand and three hundred; then shall the Kodesh (Sanctuary) be vindicated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DANIEL 10:16; HISGALUS 1:13; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:10–11"> And it came to pass, when I, even I Daniel, was beholding the chazon, that I sought the meaning; then, hinei, there stood before me as the appearance of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 1:19; LUKAS 1:26; DANIEL 8:2"> And I heard a kol adam (manʼs voice) amidst the Ulai, and he called, and said, Gavriel, cause this one to understand the vision.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Dan 9:21">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DANIEL 8:19; DANIEL 12:4; CHABAKUK 2:3"> So he came near where I stood; and as he came, I was filled with fear, and I fell down upon my face, but he said unto me, Understand, O ben adam, the chazon (vision) points to the time of the ketz (end).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DANIEL 10:18; YECHEZKEL 2:2; DANIEL 10:16"> Now while he was speaking with me, I swooned upon my face toward the ground, but he touched me, and made me stand upright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="CHABAKUK 2:3; DANIEL 11:27; HISGALUS 17:17"> And he said, Hineni, I will cause thee to know that which shall take place in the latter part of HaZa'am (the Wrath period), since at a mo'ed (an appointed time) the Ketz (End) shall be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DANIEL 8:3; DANIEL 11:1–2"> The ram which thou sawest having two karnayim are the melachim of Media and Paras (Persia).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DANIEL 10:20; DANIEL 11:3; DANIEL 8:5–8"> And the shaggy he-goat is melech Yavan (the king of Greece), and the keren hagedolah (great horn) that is between his eyes is the melech harishon (the first king).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="i.e., Alexander">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DANIEL 11:4; DANIEL 8:3; DANIEL 8:8"> Now one being broken and four stood up in its place, four kingdoms shall stand up out of the nation, but not in its koach (strength).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="HISGALUS 13:11–14; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:9–11; HISGALUS 19:20"> And in the latter time of their kingdom, when haposhe'im (the transgressors, the ones rebelling) are come to the full, a melech of unyielding countenance, and mastering intrigues, shall stand up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DANIEL 8:12; DANIEL 11:31–36; DANIEL 7:25"> And his koach shall be mighty, but not by his own koach; and he shall cause astounding destruction, and shall prosper, and accomplish, and shall destroy the mighty and Am Kedoshim (the Holy People).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DANIEL 8:11; HISGALUS 17:14; IYOV 34:20"> And by his cunning also he shall cause mirmah (deceit) to prosper through his hand; and he shall magnify himself in his lev, and by peace shall destroy rabbim (many); he shall also stand up against the Sar Sarim (Prince of Princes); but he shall be broken without &#91;human&#93; hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DANIEL 10:1; DANIEL 12:9; DANIEL 12:4"> And the vision of the evening and the morning which was told is emes; and thou! Shut up the chazon; for it shall be for many yamim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="i.e., distant times">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DANIEL 7:28; CHABAKUK 3:16; DANIEL 8:7"> And I Daniel was faint, and lay ill several yamim; afterward I rose up, and did the kingʼs business; and I was astonished at the vision, but without understanding it.</span>
