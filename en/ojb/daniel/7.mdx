<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DANIEL 1:17; AMOS 3:7; DANIEL 5:1"> In the first year of Belshatzar melech Babylon, Daniel saw a dream and visions of his head upon his bed; then he wrote down the dream, and the sum of the matters he related.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HISGALUS 7:1; HISGALUS 17:15"> Daniel answered and said, I saw in my vision that came by night, and, hinei, the arbah ruchot (four winds) of Shomayim burst forth upon the yam hagadol (great sea).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HISGALUS 13:1; DANIEL 7:17; DANIEL 2:32–33"> And four chayyot hagedolot (great beasts) came up from the yam, different one from another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 4:7; YESHAYAH 14:13–17; YECHEZKEL 17:3"> Harishonah (the first) was like an arye (lion), and had eagleʼs wings. I was beholding until its wings were plucked off, and it was lifted up from ha'aretz, and was made to stand upon two feet like a man, and a manʼs lev was given to it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Dan 4:34[4:31]">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DANIEL 2:39; YESHAYAH 13:17–18; MISHLE 17:12"> And hinei another beast, a second, like a bear, and it was raised up on one side, and three ribs were in its mouth between its teeth; and it was said thus unto it, Arise, devour much basar (flesh).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HISGALUS 13:2; DANIEL 8:20–22; DANIEL 8:5–8"> After this I beheld, and hinei another, like a leopard, which had upon its sides four wings of a bird; the beast had also arba'ah rashim (four heads) and dominion was given to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HISGALUS 13:1; HISGALUS 12:3; HISGALUS 17:12"> After this I saw in the night visions, and hinei a fourth Chayyah (Beast), dreadful and terrible, and strong exceedingly; and it had great iron teeth; it was devouring and crushing, and trampling the residue with its feet; and it was different from all the beasts that were before it, and it had eser karnayim (ten horns).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="HISGALUS 13:5–6; HISGALUS 13:1; TEHILLIM 12:3"> I was contemplating the karnayim (horns), and, hinei, there came up among them another keren (horn), a little one, before whom there were three of the first karnayim plucked up by the roots; and, hinei, in this keren were eyes like the eyes of man, and a mouth speaking great things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:14; MARKOS 9:3; DANIEL 7:13"> I was beholding until<br /><span className="pl pl-1">kisot (thrones) were placed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the Atik Yomin (Ancient of Days),</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., Hashem">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">did sit,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">whose raiment was like white snow,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the hair of His rosh like pure wool;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His kes (throne) was like the fiery flames,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and its wheels like burning eish.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YESHAYAH 30:27; DEVARIM 33:2; HISGALUS 20:11–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">A river of eish was flowing</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and came forth from before Him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">thousand thousands ministered unto Him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and ten thousand times ten thousand stood before Him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the court sat,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the sfarim (books) were opened.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:20; HISGALUS 20:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:8"> I was beholding then because of the voice of the arrogant words which the keren<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Anti-Moshiach">c</a></sup>spoke, I was beholding even till the Chayyah (Beast) was slain, and his body destroyed, and given to the burning eish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DANIEL 8:7; DANIEL 7:4–6"> As concerning the rest of the beasts, their dominion was taken away, yet their lives were prolonged for a time and a season.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:64; HISGALUS 1:7; LUKAS 21:27"> I was beholding in visions of the night,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">and, hinei, one like a Bar Enosh (Ben Adam),</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Moshiach">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">came with the clouds of Shomayim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and came to the Atik Yomin (Ancient of Days),</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="i.e., Hashem">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and before Him He was brought.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HISGALUS 11:15; DANIEL 7:27; YOCHANAN 3:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">And there was given Him (Moshiach, see Rashi on Daniel) dominion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and honor, and sovereignty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that all people, Goyim, tongues,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">should pey-lammed-chet him.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Dan 3:12, serve, reverence as deity Him (Moshiach)">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His dominion is an everlasting dominion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">which shall not pass away,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and His (Messianic) Kingdom</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that which shall not be destroyed.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DANIEL 7:28; YIRMEYAH 17:16; BERESHIS 40:7–8"> As for me, Daniel, my ruach was anxious in the midst of my body, and the visions of my head were troubling me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HISGALUS 7:13–14; ZECHARYAH 2:3; HISGALUS 5:5"> I drew near to one of the attendants to ask him the certainty concerning all this; and he said to me that he would make me to have da'as of the pesher (interpretation) of the things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DANIEL 8:19–22; DANIEL 2:37–40; YOCHANAN 18:36"> These chayyot hagedolot (great beasts), which are arbah (four), are four melachim, which shall arise out of ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DANIEL 7:27; YESHAYAH 60:12–14; HISGALUS 5:10"> But the kedoshim Elyon (holy ones of the Most High) shall receive the Kingdom, and shall possess the Kingdom forever, even forever and ever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DANIEL 7:7; DANIEL 2:40–43"> Then I desired to make sure concerning the fourth Chayyah (Beast), which was different from all the others, exceeding dreadful, whose teeth were of iron, and its claws brass; it was devouring, crushing, and stamped the residue with its feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DANIEL 7:23; DANIEL 7:8; DANIEL 7:11"> And concerning the eser karnayim (ten horns) that were in its head, and of the other which came up, and before whom three fell, even of that horn<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Anti-Moshiach">g</a></sup>that had eyes, and a mouth that spoke arrogant things, which seemed greater than the others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DANIEL 12:7; HISGALUS 17:14; DANIEL 8:24"> I was beholding, and the same keren (horn),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Anti-Moshiach">h</a></sup>made war against the kedoshim, and prevailed against them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DANIEL 7:18; HISGALUS 20:4; HISGALUS 5:10"> Until the Atik Yomin (Ancient of Days, Hashem) came, and judgment was given for the kedoshim Elyon; and the time came that the kedoshim possessed the Kingdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DANIEL 2:40; DANIEL 7:7; LUKAS 2:1"> This is what he said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">The fourth Chayyah (Beast) shall be</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a fourth kingdom upon earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">which shall be different from all kingdoms,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and shall devour kol ha'aretz,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and shall tread it down, and break it in pieces.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DANIEL 7:20; DANIEL 11:36; HISGALUS 13:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the eser karnayim (ten horns),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">out of this kingdom are asarah melachim (ten kings) that shall arise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and another shall arise after them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he shall be different from the first,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he shall subdue three melachim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DANIEL 12:7; HISGALUS 12:14; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Anti-Moshiach">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shall speak great words against Elyon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and shall wear out the kedoshim Elyon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and think to change set times and law, and they shall be given into his hand</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">until a time and times and half a time.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="HISGALUS 20:10–11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:8; HISGALUS 11:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">But judgment was given,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and there was taken away its</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Anti-Moshiachʼs">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">power</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to destroy and to annihilate unto the end.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="TEHILLIM 72:11; LUKAS 1:33; HISGALUS 11:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the dominion and sovereignty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and greatness of the kingdom under kol HaShomayim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shall be given to the people of the kedoshim Elyon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">whose kingdom is a malchut olam (an everlasting kingdom),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and all dominions shall serve and obey Him.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKAS 2:19; LUKAS 2:51; DANIEL 10:8"> Unto this point is the end of the matter. As for me, Daniel, my thoughts much troubled me, and the color of my countenance changed upon me, but I kept the matter in my lev.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="T.N. Chanukah remembers the restored and re-dedicated worship in Jerusalem 164 B.C.E. after the Anti-Moshiach-like activities of Antiochus Epiphanes predicted in chapter 8 below.">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
