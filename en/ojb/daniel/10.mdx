<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DANIEL 6:28; DANIEL 1:21; DANIEL 8:26"> In the shnat shlosh of Koresh (Cyrus) melech Paras (Persia) a davar (word) was revealed unto Daniel, shmo (his name) called Belteshatzar; and emes was the davar, and of a tzava gadol (great conflict, affliction); and he understood the davar, and had binah of the vision.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 1:4; DANIEL 9:24–27; EZRA 9:4–5"> In those days I Daniel was mourning a full shloshah shavu'im (three weeks).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:17; DANIEL 11:8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:27"> Choice lechem I did not eat, neither came basar nor yayin into my mouth, neither did I anoint myself at all, until the completing of the full shloshet shavu'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 2:14; DANIEL 8:2; YECHEZKEL 1:3"> And in the four and twentieth yom of the chodesh harishon (first month), as I was on the bank of the nahar hagadol (the great river) which is the Tigris;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:13–15; DANIEL 12:6–7; YECHEZKEL 9:2"> Then I lifted up mine eyes, and looked, and, hinei, there before me was as an ish clothed in linen, around whose waist was a belt of the finest gold of Uphaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:12; HISGALUS 1:13–17; MATTITYAHU 17:2"> His geviyah (body) also was like the chrysolite, and his face like the appearance of lightning, and his eyes like torches of eish, and his zero'ot (arms) and his raglayim (feet) like in color to polished bronze, and the sound of his words like the kol hamon (voice of a multitude).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:9; BERESHIS 3:10"> And I Daniel alone saw the vision, for the anashim that were with me saw not the vision; but a charadah gedolah (great terror) fell upon them, and they fled, for they hid themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DANIEL 7:28; CHABAKUK 3:16; DANIEL 8:27"> Therefore I was left alone, and saw this great vision, and there remained no ko'ach (strength) in me, for my hod (comeliness) was changed upon me into disfigurement, and I retained no ko'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DANIEL 8:18; IYOV 4:13; BERESHIS 15:12"> Then I heard the kol (voice) of his words, and when I heard the kol of his words, then I was in a deep sleep on my face, and my face was on the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:17; YIRMEYAH 1:9; DANIEL 8:18"> And, hinei, a yad (hand) touched me, and raised me, trembling, upon my knees and upon the palms of my hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:16; YECHEZKEL 2:1; DANIEL 8:16–17"> And he said unto me, O Daniel, ish chamudot (man greatly valued, beloved), understand the devarim that I speak unto thee, and stand upright, for unto thee now shulachti (I was sent). And when he had spoken the davar hazeh (this word) unto me, I stood trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:30–31; DANIEL 9:20–23; DANIEL 10:19"> Then said he unto me, Al tirah (fear not), Daniel, for from the yom harishon (first day) that thou didst set thine lev to understand, oolehitannot (and to afflict, humble thyself) before Eloheicha, thy words were heard, and I am come because of thy words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9; DANIEL 12:1; DANIEL 10:20–21"> But the Sar Malchut Paras (Prince of the Kingdom of Persia) was standing before me 21 yamim; but, hinei, Micha'el, one of the Sarim HaRishonim came to help me; and I was detained there with the Melachim Paras.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DANIEL 8:26; DANIEL 2:28; DANIEL 12:4"> Now I am come to give thee binah (understanding) of what shall befall thy People in the acharit hayamim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Messianic">a</a></sup>(latter days); for there is still a chazon (vision) for &#91;those&#93; yamim (days).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 1:20; YECHEZKEL 24:27; DANIEL 10:9"> And while he was speaking such devarim unto me, I bowed my face toward the ground, and I became unable to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YESHAYAH 6:7; DANIEL 8:15; YIRMEYAH 1:9"> And, hinei, one with the likeness of the bnei adam touched my lips; then I opened my mouth, and spoke, and said unto him that stood before me, Adoni, because of the vision my pangs have overcome me, and I have retained no ko'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YESHAYAH 6:1–5; SHEMOT 24:10–11; MARKOS 12:36"> For how is the eved adoni here able to speak with such as adoni? For as for me, now no ko'ach remains in me, neither is there neshamah (breath) left in me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DANIEL 10:16; LUKAS 22:43; DANIEL 8:18"> Then there came again and touched me one like the appearance of a man, v'yechazkeini (and he strengthened me),</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 35:4; YEHOSHUA 1:9; SHOFETIM 6:23"> And said, Al tira, ish chamudot (fear not, O man greatly valued, beloved); Shalom to you; chazak (be strong), yea, chazak. And when he had spoken unto me, I felt myself strengthened, and said, Let adoni speak; for thou hast strengthened me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DANIEL 8:21; DANIEL 11:2–4; DANIEL 8:5–8"> Then said he, Knowest thou why I have come unto thee? And now I shall return to fight against the Sar Paras (Prince of Persia); and when I am gone forth, hinei! The Sar Yavan (Prince of Greece) comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DANIEL 10:13; DANIEL 12:1; HISGALUS 12:7"> But I shall make known to thee that which is inscribed in the Writing of Emes; and there is no one mitchazak (putting forth strength) with me in these things, but Micha'el your Sar.</span>
</div>
</div>
