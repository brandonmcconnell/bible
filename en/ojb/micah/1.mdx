<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="AMOS 4:13; YESHAYAH 26:21; DEVARIM 32:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">For, hinei, Hashem cometh forth out of His dwelling place,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and will come down, and tread upon the high places of ha'aretz.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NACHUM 1:5; TEHILLIM 97:5; SHOFETIM 5:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the mountains shall melt under Him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the valleys shall be split apart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like wax before the eish,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and like mayim that are poured down a steep place.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="AMOS 8:14; YIRMEYAH 2:19; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because of the peysha (transgression) of Ya'akov is all this,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and because of the chattot Bais Yisroel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">What is the peysha of Ya'akov?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Is it not Shomron?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And what are the high places of Yehudah?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Are they not Yerushalayim?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 13:14; MICHOH 3:12; EKHAH 4:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore I will make Shomron like a rubble heap of the sadeh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and like a place for planting a kerem (vineyard);</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will pour down the stones thereof into the valley,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will lay bare her foundations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 23:18; HOSHEA 2:12; HOSHEA 8:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">And all the pesilim thereof shall be beaten to pieces,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and all the etnan (temple proceeds of cult prostitution) shall be burned with the eish,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and all the idols thereof will I lay desolate;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for she</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., Shomron">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">gathered of the etnan (wage) of a zonah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they shall revert to the etnan (wage) of a zonah.</span></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 30:11–15; YESHAYAH 8:7–8; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:1–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">For her wound is incurable;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for it is come unto Yehudah;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it is come unto the sha'ar ami (gate of my people)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">even to Yerushalayim.</span></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 1:20; YIRMEYAH 6:26; YEHOSHUA 18:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Declare ye it not at Gat,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">weep ye not at all;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">at Beit L'aphrah (House of Dust) roll thyself in the aphar (dust).</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 14:19; MICHOH 1:9; AMOS 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the inhabitant of Marot waited anxiously for relief;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but rah (evil) came down from Hashem unto the sha'ar Yerushalayim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:9; YEHOSHUA 10:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">O thou inhabitant of Lakhish,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">harness the merkavah to the swift horses;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it was the reshit chattat (beginning of sin) to Bat Tziyon;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the transgressions of Yisroel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">were found in thee.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:44; MELACHIM BAIS 16:8; SHMUEL BAIS 8:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore shalt thou give shilluchim (parting gifts) to Moreshet-Gat;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the batim (houses) of Achziv shall be a deception</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to the melachim of Yisroel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:44; SHMUEL ALEF 22:1; YEHOSHUA 15:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet will I bring HaYoresh (The Heir, the one who takes possession) unto thee,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">O inhabitant of Mareshah;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the kavod Yisroel shall come unto Adullam.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YESHAYAH 22:12; IYOV 1:20; YIRMEYAH 7:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">Shave your head in mourning</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the children who were your delight;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">make yourselves as bald as the nesher (vulture);</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for they are gone into the golus from thee.</span></span>
