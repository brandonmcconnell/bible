<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:1; SHEMOT 40:2"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe in the Midbar Sinai, in the first month of the second year after they were come out of Eretz Mitzrayim, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:1–2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:7–8; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:1"> Let the Bnei Yisroel also observe the Pesach at its appointed season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:6–11; VAYIKRA 23:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:2"> In the fourteenth day of this month, at twilight, ye shall observe it in its appointed season; according to kol chukkot of it, according to all the mishpatim thereof, shall ye observe it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And Moshe spoke unto the Bnei Yisroel, that they should observe the Pesach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 5:10; MATTITYAHU 28:20; SHEMOT 12:1–13"> And they observed the Pesach on the fourteenth day of the first month at twilight in the Midbar Sinai; according to all that Hashem commanded Moshe, so did the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 27:2; SHEMOT 18:15; BAMIDBAR 5:2"> And there were certain men, who were teme'im by the nefesh (dead body) of an adam, that they could not observe the Pesach on that day; and they came before Moshe and before Aharon on that day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:7–8; SHEMOT 12:27"> And those men said unto him, We are teme'im (unclean) by the nefesh of a man; but why should we be kept back, that we may not offer a korban Hashem in its appointed season among the Bnei Yisroel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 85:8; BAMIDBAR 27:5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:4"> And Moshe said unto them, Stand still, and I will hear what Hashem will command concerning you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:1–2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:12–13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:28"> Speak unto the Bnei Yisroel, saying, If any man of you or of your descendants shall be tamei by reason of a dead body, or be in a journey afar off, yet he shall observe the Pesach unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:2–15; SHEMOT 12:43–49; YOCHANAN 19:36"> The fourteenth day of the second month<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Iyyar">a</a></sup>at twilight they shall observe it, and eat it with matzot and merorim (bitter herbs).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:36; SHEMOT 12:46; SHEMOT 12:10"> They shall leave none of it unto boker, nor break any bone of it; according to all the chukkat of the Pesach they shall observe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:14; SHEMOT 12:15; BAMIDBAR 9:7"> But the man that is tahor, and is not in a journey, and faileth to observe the Pesach, even the same nefesh shall be cut off from among his people; because he brought not the korban Hashem at its appointed time, that man shall bear his sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:48–49; VAYIKRA 24:22; YESHAYAH 56:3–7"> And if a ger shall stay for a while among you, and will observe the Pesach unto Hashem; according to the chukkat of the Pesach, and according to the mishpat thereof, so shall he do; ye shall have one chukkat, both for the ger, and for him that was born in the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 13:21; SHEMOT 40:34; NECHEMYAH 9:19"> And on the day that the Mishkan was set up the Anan covered the Mishkan, namely, the Ohel HaEdut; and at erev there was upon the Mishkan as it were the appearance of eish, until boker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 9:12; SHEMOT 40:38; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:1"> So it was tamid: the Anan covered it by day, and the appearance of eish by lailah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHEMOT 40:36–38; TEHILLIM 32:8; YOCHANAN 10:9"> And when the Anan was lifted up from the Ohel, then after that the Bnei Yisroel set out; and in the place where the Anan abode, there the Bnei Yisroel encamped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:1; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:6; SHEMOT 17:1"> At the mouth of Hashem the Bnei Yisroel set out, and at the mouth of Hashem they encamped; as long as the Anan abode upon the Mishkan they encamped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 3:7; BAMIDBAR 1:52–53; BAMIDBAR 3:8"> And when the Anan tarried long upon the Mishkan many days, then the Bnei Yisroel obeyed the order of Hashem, and did not set out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And so it was, when the Anan was a few days upon the Mishkan; according to the mouth of Hashem they encamped; according to the mouth of Hashem they set out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 9:19; NECHEMYAH 9:12"> And so it was, when the Anan abode from erev unto boker, and that the Anan was lifted up in the boker, then they set out; whether it was by yomam valailah that the Anan was lifted up, they set out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="TEHILLIM 32:8; SHEMOT 40:36–37; SHEMOT 40:16"> Or whether it were two days, or a month, or longer, that the Anan tarried over the Mishkan remaining thereon, the Bnei Yisroel abode in their tents, and did not set out; but when it was lifted up, they set out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 22:3; BAMIDBAR 9:19; BERESHIS 26:5"> At the mouth of Hashem they encamped, and at the mouth of Hashem they set out; they obeyed the order of Hashem, at the mouth of Hashem by the hand of Moshe.</span>
</div>
