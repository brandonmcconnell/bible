<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:10; BAMIDBAR 15:18; VAYIKRA 25:2"> Speak unto the Bnei Yisroel, and say unto them, When ye be enter into Eretz Moshevoteichem (Land of your Homes), which I give unto you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:18; BERESHIS 8:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:18"> And will make an offering by eish unto Hashem, an olah or a zevach in performing a neder, or in a nedavah or in your mo'adim, to make a re'ach nichoach unto Hashem, of the herd or of the flock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:40; VAYIKRA 23:13; VAYIKRA 6:14"> Then shall he that offereth his korban unto Hashem bring a minchah of a tenth ephah of flour mixed with the fourth part of a hin of shemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 28:7; BAMIDBAR 28:14; SHOFETIM 9:13"> And the fourth part of a hin of yayin for a nesekh shalt thou prepare with the olah (burnt offering) or zevach, for one keves (lamb).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 28:12–14; BAMIDBAR 15:4"> Or for a ram, thou shalt prepare for a minchah two-tenths ephah of flour mixed with the third part of a hin of shemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And for a nesekh thou shalt offer the third part of an hin of yayin, for a re'ach nichoach unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="VAYIKRA 3:1; VAYIKRA 7:11–18; VAYIKRA 1:3"> And when thou preparest a bull for an olah (burnt offering), or for a zevach in fulfilling a vow, or shelamim unto Hashem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 28:14; BAMIDBAR 28:12; VAYIKRA 6:14"> Then shall he bring with a bull a minchah of three-tenths ephah of flour mixed with half a hin of shemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 15:5; BAMIDBAR 6:15"> And thou shalt bring for a nesekh half a hin of yayin, for an offering made by eish, a re'ach nichoach unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Thus shall it be done for one bull, or for one ram, or for a seh from the lambs, or from the goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> According to the number that ye shall prepare, so shall ye do to every one according to their mispar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> All that are native-born shall do these things after this manner, in offering an offering made by eish, of a re'ach nichoach unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And if a ger stays temporarily with you, or whosoever be among you in your dorot, and will offer an offering made by eish, of a re'ach nichoach unto Hashem; as ye do, so he shall do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 9:14; BAMIDBAR 15:29; SHEMOT 12:49"> One chukkah shall be both for you of the Kahal, and also for the ger that sojourneth with you, a chukkat olam in your dorot; as ye are, so shall the ger be before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> One torah and one mishpat shall be for you, and for the ger that sojourneth with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 15:2; DEVARIM 26:1–15"> Speak unto the Bnei Yisroel, and say unto them, When ye come into HaAretz whither I bring you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 5:11–12"> Then it shall be, that, when ye eat of the lechem HaAretz, ye shall offer up a terumah unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="VAYIKRA 2:14; SHEMOT 34:26; DEVARIM 26:2–10"> Ye shall offer up a challah (loaf, cake) of the first of your kneading for a terumah; as ye do the terumat goren (gift of the threshing floor), so shall ye set it aside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:28; BAMIDBAR 18:26"> Of the first of your kneading ye shall give unto Hashem a terumah in your dorot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:2; VAYIKRA 4:27; VAYIKRA 5:15–17"> And if ye have sinned unintentionally, and not observed all these mitzvot, which Hashem hath spoken unto Moshe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Even all that Hashem hath commanded you by the hand of Moshe, from the day that Hashem commanded Moshe, and henceforward among your dorot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:23; VAYIKRA 4:13–21; VAYIKRA 5:15"> Then it shall be, if it be committed by ignorance without the knowledge of HaEdah, that Kol HaEdah shall offer one young bull for an olah, for a re'ach nichoach unto Hashem, with its minchah, and its nesekh, according to the mishpat, and one kid of the goats for a chattat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:25; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:17"> And the kohen shall make kapporah for Kol Adat Bnei Yisroel and it shall be forgiven them; for it is shegagah (inadvertence); and they shall bring their korban, a sacrifice made by eish unto Hashem, and their chattat before Hashem, for their shegagah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 15:24"> And it shall be forgiven Kol Adat Bnei Yisroel, and the ger that sojourneth among them; seeing Kol HaAm were in shegagah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:27–28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:30; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:17"> And if any nefesh sin through shegagah, then he shall bring a she-goat of the first year for a chattat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="VAYIKRA 4:35"> And the kohen shall make kapporah for the nefesh that was inadvertant, since he sinneth by shegagah before Hashem, to make kapporah for him; and it shall be forgiven him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 15:15; VAYIKRA 16:29; VAYIKRA 17:15"> Ye shall have one torah for him that sinneth through ignorance, both for him that is born among the Bnei Yisroel, and for the ger that sojourneth among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:26; TEHILLIM 19:13; DEVARIM 1:43"> But the nefesh that sinneth with a yad ramah (high hand, defiantly), whether he be born in the land, or a ger, the same blasphemes Hashem; and that nefesh shall be cut off from among his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 12:9; MISHLE 13:13; YECHEZKEL 18:20"> Because he hath despised the Devar Hashem, and hath broken His mitzvah, that nefesh shall utterly be cut off; his avon shall be upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHEMOT 35:2–3; SHEMOT 31:14–15; SHEMOT 20:8–10"> And while the Bnei Yisroel were in the midbar, they found a man that gathered sticks on Shabbos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:3–20"> And they that found him gathering sticks brought him unto Moshe and Aharon, and unto Kol HaEdah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="VAYIKRA 24:12; BAMIDBAR 9:8"> And they put him under custody, because it was not declared what should be done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:58; SHEMOT 31:14–15; MELACHIM ALEF 21:13"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, The man shall be surely put to death; Kol HaEdah shall stone him with avanim outside the machaneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 7:25"> And Kol HaEdah brought him outside the machaneh, and stoned him with avanim, and he died; as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 23:5; DEVARIM 22:12; MATTITYAHU 9:20"> Speak unto the Bnei Yisroel, and bid them that they make them tzitzis on the corners of their garments throughout their dorot, and that they put upon the tzitzis of the corners a thread of turquoise wool;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="TEHILLIM 106:39; MISHLE 28:26; TEHILLIM 73:27"> And it shall be unto you for a tzitzit, that ye may look upon it, and remember all the mitzvot Hashem, and do them; and that ye follow not after your own lev and your own eyes, after which ye used to go a-whoring:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:7; VAYIKRA 11:44–45"> That ye may remember, and do all My mitzvot, and be kedoshim unto Eloheichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9–10; YECHEZKEL 36:25–27; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:16"> I am Hashem Eloheichem, Who brought you out of Eretz Mitzrayim, to be for you Elohim: I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
