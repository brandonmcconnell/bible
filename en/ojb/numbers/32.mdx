<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:32; BAMIDBAR 32:3; BERESHIS 13:10–11"> Now the Bnei Reuven and the Bnei Gad had a very great multitude of mikneh; and when they saw Eretz Yazer, and Eretz Gil'ad, that, hinei, the makom was a makom for mikneh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The Bnei Gad and the Bnei Reuven came and spoke unto Moshe, and to El'azar HaKohen, and unto the nasiim of the Edah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 48:34; YESHAYAH 16:8–9; BAMIDBAR 32:34–38"> Atarot, and Divon, and Yazer, and Nimrah, and Cheshbon, and Elealeh, and Sevam, and Nevo, and Be'on,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:34; DEVARIM 2:24–35; BAMIDBAR 21:24"> Even HaAretz which Hashem struck to conquer before the Adat Yisroel, is an eretz for mikneh, and thy avadim have mikneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="RUTH 2:10; YEHOSHUA 7:7; YIRMEYAH 31:2"> They said, If we have found chen in thy sight, let this land be given unto thy avadim for an achuzzah, and bring us not over Yarden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:5; SHMUEL BAIS 11:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:4"> And Moshe said unto the Bnei Gad and to the Bnei Reuven, Shall your achim go to milchamah while ye sit here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 13:27–4; BAMIDBAR 32:9; DEVARIM 1:28"> And why discourage ye the lev Bnei Yisroel from going over into HaAretz which Hashem hath given them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:19–25; BAMIDBAR 14:2; BAMIDBAR 13:2–26"> Thus did Avoteichem, when I sent them from Kadesh-Barnea to look over HaAretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 13:23–10; DEVARIM 1:24–28"> For when they went up unto the Eshcol Valley, and saw HaAretz, they discouraged the lev Bnei Yisroel, that they should not go into HaAretz which Hashem had given them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:11; TEHILLIM 95:11; YECHEZKEL 20:15"> And the Af Hashem was kindled on that day, and He swore, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:28–30; BAMIDBAR 14:24; BAMIDBAR 26:2"> Surely none of the anashim that came up out of Mitzrayim, from twenty years old and upward, shall see HaAdamah which I swore unto Avraham, unto Yitzchak, and unto Ya'akov; because they have not fully followed Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:36; BAMIDBAR 14:30; BAMIDBAR 14:24"> Except Kalev Ben Yephunneh the Kenizi, and Yehoshua Ben Nun; for they have fully followed after Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:5; BAMIDBAR 14:33–35; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:16–19"> And the Af Hashem was kindled against Yisroel, and He made them wander in the midbar arba'im shanah, until kol hador that had done harah in the sight of Hashem, were consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 11:48; TEHILLIM 78:57; MATTITYAHU 23:31–33"> And, hinei, ye are risen up in the place of Avoteichem a brood of anashim chatta'im to add even more to charon af Hashem against Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:11–12; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 15:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 7:19–22"> For if ye turn away from after Him, He will yet again leave them in the midbar; and ye shall destroy kol HaAm Hazeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 33:17; BAMIDBAR 34:22"> And they came near unto him, and said, We will build gidrot tzon here for our mikneh, and towns for our little ones;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 4:12–13; BAMIDBAR 32:29–32; DEVARIM 3:18–20"> But we ourselves will go ready armed ahead of the Bnei Yisroel, until we have brought them unto their makom; and our little ones shall dwell in the fortified cities because of the inhabitants of HaAretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 22:1–5"> We will not return unto bateinu, until the Bnei Yisroel have inherited every ish his nachalah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 13:8; YEHOSHUA 12:1–6; BAMIDBAR 34:15"> For we will not inherit with them across the Yarden, or beyond; because our nachalah is fallen to us on this side of the Yarden eastward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 4:12–13; DEVARIM 3:18–20; YEHOSHUA 1:13–15"> And Moshe said unto them, If ye will do this thing, if ye will go armed before Hashem to milchamah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And will go all of you armed over Yarden before Hashem, until He hath driven out His oyevim from before Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 22:9; YEHOSHUA 22:4; DEVARIM 3:20"> And HaAretz be subdued before Hashem; then afterward ye shall return, and be nekiyim before Hashem, and before Yisroel; and HaAretz Hazot shall be your achuzzah before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YESHAYAH 59:12; MISHLE 13:21; BERESHIS 44:16"> But if ye will not do so, hinei, ye have sinned against Hashem and be sure chattatechem (your sin) will find you out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:16; BAMIDBAR 30:2; BAMIDBAR 32:34–42"> Build you towns for your little ones, and your gidrot tzon; and do that which hath proceeded out of your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 1:13–14"> And the Bnei Gad and the Bnei Reuven spoke unto Moshe saying, Thy avadim will do as adoni commandeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 1:14"> Our little ones, our nashim, our mikneh, and all behemeteinu, shall stay there in the cities of Gil'ad;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 4:12; BAMIDBAR 32:17; BAMIDBAR 12:11"> But thy avadim will cross over, every man armed for war, before Hashem to battle, as adoni saith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 1:13"> So concerning them Moshe commanded El'azar HaKohen, and Yehoshua Ben Nun, and the Rashei Avot HaMattot of the Bnei Yisroel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And Moshe said unto them, If the Bnei Gad and the Bnei Reuven will cross with you over Yarden, every man armed for battle, before Hashem, and HaAretz shall be subdued before you, then ye shall give them Eretz Gil'ad for an achuzzah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 22:19"> But if they will not cross over with you armed, they shall have possessions among you in Eretz Kena'an.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the Bnei Gad and the Bnei Reuven answered, saying, As Hashem hath said unto thy avadim, so will we do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> We will cross over armed before Hashem into Eretz Kena'an, that the achuzzat nachalateinu on that side of the Yarden may be our own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 12:6; DEVARIM 29:8; DEVARIM 3:12–17"> And Moshe gave unto them, even to the Bnei Gad, and to the Bnei Reuven, and unto half the shevet of Menasheh Ben Yosef, the kingdom of Sichon Melech HaEmori, and the kingdom of Og Melech HaBashan, the land, with the cities thereof in the territories, even the cities of the surrounding land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:36; BAMIDBAR 32:3; YESHAYAH 17:2"> And the Bnei Gad built Divon, and Atarot, and Aro'er,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:1; BAMIDBAR 32:3"> And Atrot Shophan, Yazer, and Yogbehah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:3"> And Beit-Nimrah and Beit-Haran, fortified cities; and gidrot tzon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:27; BAMIDBAR 32:3; YESHAYAH 15:4"> And the Bnei Reuven built Cheshbon, and Elealeh, and Kiryatayim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YESHAYAH 46:1; BAMIDBAR 32:3; YEHOSHUA 23:7"> And Nevo, and Ba'al-Me'on, (their shem being changed) and Sivmah; and gave other shmot unto the cities which they built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="BERESHIS 50:23; YEHOSHUA 17:1; BAMIDBAR 26:29"> And the Bnei Machir Ben Menasheh went to Gil'ad, and captured it, and dispossessed the Emori (Amorite) which was in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 17:1; YEHOSHUA 13:29–31; DEVARIM 3:13–15"> And Moshe gave Gil'ad unto Machir Ben Menasheh; and he dwelt therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="DEVARIM 3:14; SHOFETIM 10:4; YEHOSHUA 13:30"> And Yair Ben Menasheh went and captured the settlements thereof, and called them Chavot Ya'ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="TEHILLIM 49:11; SHMUEL BAIS 18:18"> And Novach went and captured Kenat, and the villages thereof, and called it Novach, after shmo.</span>
