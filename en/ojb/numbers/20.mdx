<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 13:21; SHEMOT 15:20; BAMIDBAR 33:36"> Then came the Bnei Yisroel, even the kol HaEdah into the Midbar of Tzin in the first month; and the people abode in Kadesh; and Miryam died there, and was buried there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 16:19; BAMIDBAR 16:42; SHEMOT 15:23–24"> And there was no mayim for the Edah; and they gathered themselves together against Moshe and against Aharon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 17:2; BAMIDBAR 14:1–2; BAMIDBAR 16:31–35"> And the people contended with Moshe, and spoke, saying, If only we had died when our brethren died before Hashem!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 17:3; TEHILLIM 106:21; BAMIDBAR 16:13–14"> And why have ye brought up the Kahal Hashem into this midbar, that we and our livestock should die here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 16:14; YIRMEYAH 2:2; YIRMEYAH 2:6"> And why have ye made us to come up out of Mitzrayim, to bring us in unto this evil place? It is no place of zera (grain), or of te'enah (fig), or of gefen (grapevine), or of pomegranates; neither is there any mayim to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:5; BAMIDBAR 16:4; BAMIDBAR 16:19"> And Moshe and Aharon went from the presence of the Kahal unto the entrance of the Ohel Mo'ed, and they fell upon their faces; and the kavod Hashem appeared unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:1–4; HISGALUS 22:17"> Take the rod and gather thou the Edah together, thou and Aharon thy brother, and speak ye unto the rock before their eyes; and it shall give forth its mayim, and thou shalt bring forth to them mayim out of the rock; so thou shalt give the Edah and their beasts drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 17:10"> And Moshe took the rod from before Hashem, just as He commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 106:32–33; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:9–15; LUKAS 9:54–55"> And Moshe and Aharon gathered the Kahal together before the rock, and he said unto them, Hear now, ye morim (rebels, contentious ones); must we fetch you mayim out of this rock?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:4; SHEMOT 17:6; DEVARIM 8:15"> And Moshe lifted up his hand, and with his rod he struck the rock twice; and the mayim came out abundantly, and the Edah drank, and their beasts also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:37; BAMIDBAR 27:14; YECHEZKEL 36:23"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe and Aharon, Because ye believed Me not, to honor Me as kadosh in the eyes of the Bnei Yisroel, therefore ye shall not bring this Kahal into HaAretz which I have given them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 17:7; TEHILLIM 95:8; DEVARIM 33:8"> These were the Waters of Merivah (Quarreling); because the Bnei Yisroel quarreled with Hashem, and He showed Himself kadosh among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHOFETIM 11:16–17; DEVARIM 23:7; OVADYAH 1:10–12"> And Moshe sent messengers from Kadesh unto the melech of Edom, Thus saith thy brother Yisroel, Thou knowest all the hardship that hath befallen us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:40; DEVARIM 26:6; BERESHIS 46:6"> How Avoteinu went down into Mitzrayim, and we have dwelt in Mitzrayim a long time; and the Egyptians did evil to us, and Avoteinu;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 14:19; SHEMOT 23:20; SHEMOT 33:2"> And when we cried unto Hashem, He heard our voice, and sent a malach, and hath brought us forth out of Mitzrayim; and, hinei, we are in Kadesh, a town in the edge of thy border;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:27; DEVARIM 2:29; BAMIDBAR 21:22–24"> Let us pass, please, through thy country; we will not pass through the fields, or through the kerem (vineyards), neither will we drink of the mayim of the wells; we will go by the Derech HaMelech; we will not turn to the right hand nor to the left until we have passed through thy territory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And Edom said unto him, Thou shalt not pass through me, lest I come out against thee with the cherev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:28; DEVARIM 2:6; SHEMOT 12:38"> And the Bnei Yisroel said unto him, We will go by the main road; and if I and my livestock drink of thy mayim, then I will pay for it; I will only, without doing anything else, pass through on my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHOFETIM 11:17; AMOS 1:11; BERESHIS 32:6"> And he said, Thou shalt not go through. And Edom came out against him with a large army, and with a yad chazakah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHOFETIM 11:18; DEVARIM 2:29; DEVARIM 2:4–8"> Thus Edom refused to give Yisroel passage through his border; wherefore Yisroel turned away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 20:1; BAMIDBAR 20:14; BAMIDBAR 21:4"> And the Bnei Yisroel, even Kol HaEdah, journeyed from Kadesh, and came unto Mt Hor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 33:37"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe and Aharon in Mt Hor, by the border of Eretz Edom, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:8; DEVARIM 32:50; BAMIDBAR 31:2"> Aharon shall be gathered unto his people; for he shall not enter into HaAretz which I have given unto the Bnei Yisroel, because ye rebelled against My word at the Waters of Merivah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 33:38–39"> Take Aharon and El'azar bno, and bring them up unto Mt Hor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 20:24; YESHAYAH 22:21–22; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:11"> And strip Aharon of his garments, and put them upon El'azar bno; and Aharon shall be gathered unto his people, and shall die there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And Moshe did as Hashem commanded; and they went up into Mt Hor in the sight of Kol HaEdah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:6; SHEMOT 29:29–30; DEVARIM 34:9"> And Moshe stripped Aharon of his garments, and put them upon El'azar bno; and Aharon died there on the top of the mount; and Moshe and El'azar came down from the mount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEVARIM 34:8; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:24–25; BERESHIS 1:10"> And when Kol HaEdah saw that Aharon was dead, they mourned for Aharon shloshim yom, even Kol Bais Yisroel.</span>
