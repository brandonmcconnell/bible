<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:8; TEHILLIM 105:11; DEVARIM 1:7–8"> Command the Bnei Yisroel, and say unto them, When ye come into Eretz Kena'an (this is HaAretz that shall fall unto you for a nachalah, even Eretz Kena'an with the borders thereof),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 14:3; YECHEZKEL 47:13; YEHOSHUA 3:16"> Then your southern side shall be from the Midbar Tzin along by the border of Edom, and your southern border shall be from the edge of the Yam HaMelach (Salt Sea, Dead Sea) to the east;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:8; YEHOSHUA 15:3–4; BAMIDBAR 13:26"> And your border shall turn from the south of Ma'aleh Akrabbim, and pass on to Tzin; and the going forth thereof shall be from the south of Kadesh-Barnea, and shall go on to Chatzar Addar, and pass on to Atzmon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:4; BERESHIS 15:18; MELACHIM ALEF 8:65"> And the border shall go around from Atzmon unto the Wadi of Mitzrayim, and the goings out of it shall be toward the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Mediterranean">a</a></sup>Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 1:4; YECHEZKEL 47:20; YECHEZKEL 47:15"> And as for the western border, ye shall even have the Yam HaGadol for a border; this shall be your west border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 33:37; BAMIDBAR 34:6; BAMIDBAR 34:3"> And this shall be your northern border: from the Yam HaGadol ye shall run to Mt Hor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 13:21; MELACHIM BAIS 14:25; YEHOSHUA 13:5–6"> From Mt Hor ye shall run your border unto the approach to Chamat; and the goings forth of the border shall be toward Tzedad;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 47:17"> And the border shall go on to Ziphron, and the goings out of it shall be Chatzar Einan; this shall be your northern border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And ye shall draw a line for you for an eastern border from Chatzar Einan to Shepham;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 23:33; DEVARIM 3:17; YEHOSHUA 11:2"> And the border shall go down from Shepham to Rivlah, on the east side of Ayin; the border shall descend, and shall reach unto the bank of the Yam Kinneret to the east;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 34:3; BERESHIS 13:10; BERESHIS 19:24–26"> And the border shall go down to the Yarden, and the goings out of it shall be at the Yam HaMelach; this shall be your land with the borders thereof all around.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 34:1; YEHOSHUA 14:1–5"> And Moshe commanded the Bnei Yisroel, saying, This is the land which ye shall inherit by goral, which Hashem commanded to give unto the nine mattot (tribes), and to the half tribe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:33; BAMIDBAR 32:23; YEHOSHUA 14:2–3"> For the tribe of the Bnei Reuven according to the bais avotam, and the tribe of the Bnei Gad according to the bais avotam and the half tribe of Menasheh have received their nachalah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:32"> The two tribes and the half tribe have received their nachalah on the east side of the Yarden near Yericho toward the sunrise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 14:1; YEHOSHUA 19:51; BAMIDBAR 13:8"> These are the shmot of the anashim which shall apportion HaAretz unto you; El'azar HaKohen, and Yehoshua Ben Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:4–16"> And ye shall take one nasi of every matteh to possess HaAretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:65; BAMIDBAR 13:30; BAMIDBAR 14:24"> And the shmot of the anashim are these: Of the tribe of Yehudah, Kalev Ben Yephunneh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 49:5"> And of the tribe of the Bnei Shim'on, Shmuel Ben Ammihud;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 49:27; TEHILLIM 68:27"> Of the tribe of Binyamin, Elidad Ben Kislon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the nasi of the tribe of the Bnei Dan, Bukki Ben Yogli;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The nasi of the Bnei Yosef, for the tribe of the Bnei Menasheh, Channi'el Ben Ephod;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And the nasi of the tribe of the Bnei Ephrayim, Kemuel Ben Shiphtan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And the nasi of the tribe of the Bnei Zevulun, Elitzaphan Ben Parnakh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the nasi of the tribe of the Bnei Yissakhar, Palt'iel Ben Azzan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And the nasi of the tribe of the Bnei Asher, Achihud Ben Shelomi;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the nasi of the tribe of the Bnei Naphtali, Pedahel Ben Ammihud;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:51; BAMIDBAR 34:18"> These are they whom Hashem commanded to apportion the inheritance unto the Bnei Yisroel in Eretz Kena'an.</span>
</div>
