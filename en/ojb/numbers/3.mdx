<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHEMOT 6:20; BERESHIS 10:1; SHEMOT 6:16"> These also are the toldot of Aharon and Moshe in the yom that Hashem spoke with Moshe in Mt. Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 6:23; BAMIDBAR 26:60; SHEMOT 28:1"> And these are the shmot of the Bnei Aharon: Nadav the bechor, and Avihu, El'azar, and Itamar.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lv 10">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 28:41; VAYIKRA 8:30; VAYIKRA 8:12"> These are the shmot of the Bnei Aharon, the kohanim hameshuchim whose hand he filled<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., ordained">b</a></sup>to be kohen.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ex 29; Lv 8">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="VAYIKRA 10:1–2; BAMIDBAR 26:61"> And Nadav and Avihu died before Hashem, when they offered eish zarah (unauthorized fire) before Hashem, in the midbar of Sinai, and they had no children; and El'azar and Itamar ministered in the kohenʼs office in the sight of Aharon their av.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Lv 10">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:8; BAMIDBAR 2:33; BAMIDBAR 1:49–53"> Bring the tribe of Levi near,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="i.e., present them as offerings are presented">e</a></sup>and present them before Aharon HaKohen, that they may minister unto him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="i.e., help, be of service to him">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:50; BAMIDBAR 8:15; BAMIDBAR 8:11"> And they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="the Levi'im">g</a></sup>shall be shomer mishmeret (watch the watch, keep the guard duty) for him and for the mishmeret kol HaEdah (duty of the whole Congregation) before the Ohel Mo'ed, to do avodat HaMishkan (the work, service of the Mishkan).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 4:33; BAMIDBAR 10:17; YESHAYAH 52:11"> And they shall be shomer mishmeret over kol klei Ohel Mo'ed (all the vessels, furnishings of the Ohel Mo'ed) for the Bnei Yisroel, doing the avodat HaMishkan (work of the Mishkan).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 8:19; BAMIDBAR 18:6–7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:8"> And thou shalt give the Levi'im unto Aharon and to his banim; they are wholly given unto him from among the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:51; BAMIDBAR 3:38; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:7"> And thou shalt appoint Aharon and his banim; they shall be shomer to watch over their kehunah; the zar that encroach shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:41; BAMIDBAR 8:16; BAMIDBAR 18:6"> And I, hinei, I have taken the Levi'im from among the Bnei Yisroel instead of all the bechor that openeth the womb among the Bnei Yisroel; therefore the Levi'im shall be Mine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 2:23; SHEMOT 13:12; SHEMOT 13:2"> Because all the bechor are Mine; for on the yom that I struck down all the bechor in Eretz Mitzrayim I set apart as kodesh unto Me all the bechor in Yisroel, both man and beast: Mine shall they be: I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe in the midbar of Sinai, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:62; MARKOS 10:14; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:15"> Count the Bnei Levi by the bais of their avot, by their mishpekhot; every zachar from a month old and upward shalt thou number them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:39; BAMIDBAR 4:37; BAMIDBAR 4:49"> And Moshe numbered them according to the command of Hashem, as he had been commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:11; SHEMOT 6:16–19; BAMIDBAR 15:5–23"> And these were the Bnei Levi by their shmot: Gershon, Kehat, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:1–32; SHEMOT 6:17–19"> And these are the shmot of the Bnei Gershon by their mishpekhot: Livni, and Shime'i.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHEMOT 6:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:17–21"> And the Bnei Kehat by their mishpekhot: Amram, Yitzhar, Chevron, and Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 6:19; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:19; BAMIDBAR 3:33"> And the Bnei Merari by their mishpekhot: Machli and Mushi. These are the mishpekhot of the Levi'im according to the bais of their avot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> To Gershon belonged the mishpakhat of the Livni and the mishpakhat of the Shime'i: these are the mishpekhot of the Gershoni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 4:38–40"> Those that were numbered of them, according to the number of kol zachar (all the males), from a month old and upward, even those that were numbered of them were seven thousand and five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:53; BAMIDBAR 2:17"> The mishpekhot of the Gershoni shall encamp behind the Mishkan westward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And the Nasi of the bais av of the Gershoni shall be Elyasaph ben Lael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:32; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 9:14–33"> And the mishmeret of the Bnei Gershon in the Ohel Mo'ed is as follows: the Mishkan, and the Ohel, the covering thereof, and the curtain at the entrance of the Ohel Mo'ed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHEMOT 35:18; SHEMOT 38:9–16; SHEMOT 27:9–16"> And the curtains of the khatzer, and the curtain for the entrance of the khatzer, which is around the Mishkan, and around the mizbe'ach; and the cords of it for kol avodah thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:23; BAMIDBAR 3:19; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:12"> And to Kehat belonged the mishpakhat of the Amrami, and the mishpakhat of the Yitzhari, and the mishpakhat of the Chevroni, and the mishpakhat of the Uzzieli: these are the mishpekhot of the Kehati.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:31; BAMIDBAR 4:35–36; BAMIDBAR 3:7"> In the number of all the zachar, from a month old and upward, were eight thousand and six hundred, shomrei mishmeret haKodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:53; BAMIDBAR 3:23; BAMIDBAR 2:10"> The mishpekhot of the Bnei Kehat shall encamp on the southward side of the Mishkan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the Nasi bais av of the mishpekhot of the Kehati shall be Elitzaphan ben Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHEMOT 36:35–36; SHEMOT 27:1–8; SHEMOT 26:31–33"> And their guard duty shall be: the Aron and the Shulchan, and the Menorah, and the Mizbechot, and the vessels of HaKodesh (the Holy Place) wherewith they minister, and the curtain, and all the avodah thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 4:27; BAMIDBAR 4:16; BAMIDBAR 20:25–28"> And El'azar ben Aharon HaKohen shall be Nasi over the Nasi'ei HaLevi'im, and have the oversight of them that do guard duty over HaKodesh (the Holy Place).</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:19; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:21"> To Merari belonged the mishpakhat of the Machli, and the mishpakhat of the Mushi: these are the mishpekhot of Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 2:9; BAMIDBAR 2:11; BAMIDBAR 1:21"> And those that were counted of them, according to the number of all the zachar, from a month old and upward, were six thousand and two hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:53; BAMIDBAR 2:25; BAMIDBAR 3:28–29"> And the Nasi bais av of the mishpekhot of Merari was Tzuriel ben Avichayil; these shall encamp on the side of the Mishkan northward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="SHEMOT 26:32; SHEMOT 26:37; SHEMOT 26:15–29"> And under the guard duty charge of the Bnei Merari shall be: the frames of the Mishkan, and the crossbars thereof, and the pillars thereof, and the bases thereof, and all the vessels thereof, and all that serveth thereto,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And the pillars of the khatzer round about, and their bases, and their pegs, and their cords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:10; BAMIDBAR 1:53; BAMIDBAR 2:3"> And those that encamp in front of the Mishkan toward the east, even in front of the Ohel Mo'ed eastward, shall be Moshe, and Aharon and his banim, shomrim mishmeret HaMikdash (standing guard duty over the holy area) on behalf of the Bnei Yisroel; and the zar that shall encroach shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:62; MATTITYAHU 7:14; BAMIDBAR 4:47–48"> All that were counted of the Levi'im, which Moshe and Aharon numbered at the command of Hashem, by their mishpekhot, all the zachar from a month old and upward, were twenty and two thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:15; LUKAS 10:20; TEHILLIM 87:6"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, Count all the bechor of the zachar of the Bnei Yisroel from a month old and upward, and calculate the total by their shmot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:12; BAMIDBAR 3:45; BAMIDBAR 8:16"> And thou shalt take the Levi'im for Me — I am Hashem — instead of all the bechor among the Bnei Yisroel; and the cattle of the Levi'im instead of all the firstlings among the cattle of the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And Moshe counted, as Hashem commanded him, all the bechor among the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:39"> And it came about that all the bechor zachar by the number of shmot, from a month old and upward, of those that were numbered of them, were twenty and two thousand two hundred and threescore and thirteen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:12; BAMIDBAR 3:40–41"> Take the Levi'im instead of all the bechor among the Bnei Yisroel, and the cattle of the Levi'im instead of their cattle; and the Levi'im shall be Mine: I am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="SHEMOT 13:13; BAMIDBAR 18:15–16"> And for those that are to be redeemed of the two hundred and threescore and thirteen of the bechor of the Bnei Yisroel, which are more than the Levi'im;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="SHEMOT 30:13; VAYIKRA 27:6; BAMIDBAR 18:16"> Thou shalt even take five shekels for each one individually, after the shekel of HaKodesh shalt thou take them (the shekel is twenty gerahs);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Lv 27:6">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And thou shalt give the kesef, wherewith those over and above them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="the Levi'im">i</a></sup>is to be redeemed, unto Aharon and to his banim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> And Moshe took the kesef hapeduyim of them that were over and above them that were redeemed by the Levi'im;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:46–47; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18; MATTITYAHU 20:28"> Of the bechor of the Bnei Yisroel collected he the kesef; a thousand three hundred and threescore and five shekels, after the shekel of HaKodesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:48; BAMIDBAR 16:15; SHMUEL ALEF 12:3–4"> And Moshe gave the kesef hapeduyim unto Aharon and to his banim, according to the command of Hashem, as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
