<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHEMOT 2:21; BERESHIS 26:34–35; BERESHIS 24:37"> And Miryam and Aharon spoke against Moshe because of the Cushite woman whom he had married; for he had married a Kushite woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 35:12–13; BAMIDBAR 16:3; MICHOH 6:4"> And they said, Hath Hashem indeed spoken only by Moshe? Hath He not spoken also by us? And Hashem heard it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:29; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:13; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:5"> (Now the man Moshe was anav me'od, above all the men which were upon the face of ha'adamah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="TEHILLIM 76:9; BAMIDBAR 16:16–21"> And Hashem spoke suddenly unto Moshe, and unto Aharon, and unto Miryam, Come out ye three unto the Ohel Mo'ed. And they three came out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 11:25; SHEMOT 34:5; TEHILLIM 99:7"> And Hashem came down in an Ammud Anan, and stood in the entrance of the Ohel, and He called Aharon and Miryam; and they both came forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 3:5; BERESHIS 31:10–11; BERESHIS 46:2"> And He said, Hear now My words: If there be a navi among you, I Hashem will reveal Myself unto him in a mar'ah (vision), and will speak unto him in a chalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:2–6; YEHOSHUA 1:1; TEHILLIM 105:26"> Avdi Moshe is not so, who is ne'eman (faithful) in all Mine Bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEVARIM 34:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:12; SHEMOT 33:11"> With him will I speak peh el peh, plainly, and not in dark sayings; and the temuna (form) of Hashem shall he behold; why then were ye not afraid to speak against Avdi Moshe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:22; HOSHEA 5:15; BAMIDBAR 11:1"> And the anger of Hashem was kindled against them; and He departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEVARIM 24:9; MELACHIM BAIS 5:27; SHEMOT 4:6"> And the Anan departed from off the Ohel &#91;Moed&#93;; and, hinei, Miryam became metzora'at (leprous), white as snow: and Aharon looked upon Miryam, and, hinei, she was metzora'at.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 19:19; SHMUEL BAIS 24:10; MISHLE 30:32"> And Aharon said unto Moshe, Alas, adoni, I beseech thee, hold not the chattat (sin) against us, wherein we have done foolishly, and wherein we have sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="TEHILLIM 88:4–5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:8; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:6"> Let her not be as one dead, of whom the basar is half consumed when he cometh out of his motherʼs womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:21; LUKAS 6:28; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:15"> And Moshe cried unto Hashem, saying, El (G-d), refah (heal) her now, I beseech thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEVARIM 25:9; IYOV 30:10; YESHAYAH 50:6"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, If her av had but spit in her face, should she not be in disgrace seven days? Let her be exiled from the machaneh seven days, and after that let her be received in again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEVARIM 24:8–9; MICHOH 7:8–9; MICHOH 6:4"> And Miryam was exiled from the machaneh seven days; and HaAm did not set out till Miryam was received in again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 11:35; BAMIDBAR 33:18; BAMIDBAR 10:12"> And afterward HaAm removed from Chatzerot, and encamped in the Midbar Paran.</span>
