<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHOFETIM 1:16; BAMIDBAR 33:40; YEHOSHUA 12:14"> And when melech of Arad the Kena'ani, which dwelt in the Negev, heard that Yisroel came by the Derech HaAtarim; then he fought against Yisroel, and took some of them prisoners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHOFETIM 11:30; BERESHIS 28:20; SHMUEL ALEF 1:11"> And Yisroel vowed a neder unto Hashem, and said, If Thou wilt indeed deliver this people into my hand, then I will utterly destroy their cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:45; DEVARIM 1:44; TEHILLIM 10:17"> And Hashem paid heed to the kol Yisroel, and delivered up the Kena'ani; and they utterly destroyed them and their cities; and the place was named Chormah (Utter Destruction).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 6:9; SHOFETIM 11:18; BAMIDBAR 32:7"> And they journeyed from Mt Hor by the way of the Yam Suf, to go around Eretz Edom; and the nefesh haAm was much impatient because of the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:19; BAMIDBAR 16:13–14; SHEMOT 17:2–3"> And the people spoke against Elohim, and against Moshe, Why have ye brought us up out of Mitzrayim to die in the midbar? For there is no lechem, neither is there any mayim; and our nefesh loatheth this wretched lechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:9; YIRMEYAH 8:17; DEVARIM 8:15"> And Hashem sent fiery nechashim among the people, and they bit the people; and Am rav miYisroel died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:24; BAMIDBAR 11:2; TEHILLIM 78:34"> Therefore HaAm came to Moshe, and said, We have sinned, for we have spoken against Hashem, and against thee; pray unto Hashem that He take away the Nachash from us. And Moshe davened for the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:14; TEHILLIM 106:43–45; TEHILLIM 145:8"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, Make thee a fiery serpent, set it upon a pole; and it shall come to pass, that every one that is bitten, when he looketh upon it, shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:14–15; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:2; YOCHANAN 6:40"> And Moshe made a nachash of nechoshet, put it upon a pole, and it came to pass, that if a nachash had bitten any man, when he beheld the nachash hanechoshet, then he lived.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Yn 3:14-15">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 33:43–45"> And the Bnei Yisroel set out, and encamped in Ovot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And they journeyed from Ovot, and encamped at Iyei-HaAvarim, in the midbar which is before Moav, toward the mizrach hashemesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:13–14"> From thence they removed, and encamped in the Wadi Zered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHOFETIM 11:18; BAMIDBAR 22:36; YESHAYAH 16:2"> From thence they removed, and encamped on the other side of Arnon, which is in the midbar encroaching the Emori; for Arnon is the border of Moav, between Moav and the Emori.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 10:13; SHMUEL BAIS 1:18"> Wherefore it is said in the Sefer Milchamot Hashem,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Vahev b'Sufah</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and in the Vadis Arnon,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:28; DEVARIM 2:18; DEVARIM 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">And at the stream of the brooks</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that goeth down to the site of Ar,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and lieth upon the border of Moav.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 20:8; YESHAYAH 12:3; YESHAYAH 41:17–18"> And from thence they went to Be'er; that is the Well whereof Hashem spoke unto Moshe, Gather the people together, and I will give them mayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:13; SHEMOT 15:1–2; TEHILLIM 106:12"> Then Yisroel sang this shirah (song),<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Spring up, O well; sing ye unto it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YESHAYAH 33:22; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:17–18; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sarim (princes) dug the well,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the nobles of the people dug it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">by the direction of the Mekhokek (Lawgiver), with their rods.</span><br />And from the midbar they went to Mattanah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And from Mattanah to Nachaliel; and from Nachaliel to Bamot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 23:28; BAMIDBAR 23:14; DEVARIM 1:5"> And from Bamot in the valley, that is in the country of Moav, to the top of Pisgah, overlooking the surface of the wasteland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:26–28; BAMIDBAR 20:14–19; SHOFETIM 11:19–21"> And Yisroel sent messengers unto Sichon Melech HaEmori, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 20:17"> Let me pass through thy land; we will not turn into the fields, or into the kerem (vineyards); we will not drink of the waters of the well, but we will go along by the Derech HaMelech until we be past thy borders.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Yn 14:6">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHOFETIM 11:20; DEVARIM 29:7–8; YESHAYAH 15:4"> And Sichon would not suffer Yisroel to pass through his border; but Sichon gathered all his army together, and went out against Yisroel into the midbar; and he came to Yahatz and fought against Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="AMOS 2:9; YEHOSHUA 24:8; BERESHIS 32:22"> And Yisroel struck down him with the edge of the cherev, and possessed his land from Arnon unto Yabbok, even unto the Bnei Ammon; for the border of the Bnei Ammon was fortified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:33–42; YESHAYAH 16:8–9; YIRMEYAH 48:2"> And Yisroel took all these cities; Yisroel dwelt in all the cities of the Emori, in Cheshbon, and in all the villages thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> For Cheshbon was the city of Sichon Melech HaEmori, who had fought against the former king of Moav, and taken all his land out of his hand, even unto Arnon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YESHAYAH 14:4; BAMIDBAR 21:14; CHABAKUK 2:6"> Wherefore they that speak in proverbs say,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Come into Cheshbon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">let the city of Sichon</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">be built and prepared;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:15; YIRMEYAH 48:45–46; YESHAYAH 15:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">For there is a fire gone out of Cheshbon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a flame from the city of Sichon;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it hath consumed Ar of Moav,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and citizens of the heights of Arnon.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHOFETIM 11:24; MELACHIM BAIS 23:13; YIRMEYAH 48:46"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to thee, Moav!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Thou art undone, O people of Kemosh;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he hath given his banim as fugitives,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and his banot, captives</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">unto Sichon Melech Emori.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 48:18; YESHAYAH 15:2; YIRMEYAH 48:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Down we have cast them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Cheshbon is perished even unto Divon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and we have laid them waste even unto Nophach,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">which reacheth unto Medeva.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 13:8–12; BAMIDBAR 32:33–42; DEVARIM 3:16–17"> Thus Yisroel dwelt in Eretz HaEmori.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 48:32; BAMIDBAR 32:1; BAMIDBAR 32:35"> And Moshe sent to spy out Yazer, and they took the villages thereof, and drove out HaEmori that were there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 13:12; DEVARIM 1:4; DEVARIM 29:7"> And they turned and went up by the way of Bashan; and Og Melech HaBashan went out against them, he, and all his army, to the battle at Edre'i.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="TEHILLIM 135:10–11; YEHOSHUA 8:7; DEVARIM 3:2–3"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, Fear him not: for I have delivered him into thy hand, all his army, his land; thou shalt do to him as thou didst unto Sichon Melech HaEmori, which dwelt at Cheshbon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEVARIM 29:7–8; YEHOSHUA 13:12; DEVARIM 3:3–17"> So they struck him down, and his banim, and all his army, until there was none alive left to him: and they possessed his land.</span>
