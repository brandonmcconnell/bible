## &#91;BEHAALOTECHA&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:37; SHEMOT 40:25; VAYIKRA 24:1–2"> Speak unto Aharon and say unto him, When thou set up the Nerot (lamps), the seven Nerot (lamps) shall cast light in front of the Menorah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And Aharon did so; he set up the Nerot (lamps) thereof to give light in front of the Menorah, as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:31–40; SHEMOT 25:9; SHEMOT 25:18"> And the Menorah was made thus of hammered zahav, from its base unto its flower was it hammered out; according unto the mar'eh (pattern) which Hashem had shown Moshe, so he made the Menorah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:1; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:8; SHEMOT 19:15"> Take the Levi'im from among the Bnei Yisroel, and make them tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 19:13; BAMIDBAR 19:7–10; VAYIKRA 8:6"> And thus shalt thou do unto them, to make them tahor: Sprinkle water of purification upon them, and let them cause a razor to pass over their basar, and let them wash their clothes, and so make themselves tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="VAYIKRA 2:1; SHEMOT 29:1; VAYIKRA 16:3"> Then let them take a young bull with its minchah, even fine flour mixed with shemen, and another young bull shalt thou take for a chattat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 40:12; VAYIKRA 8:3; SHEMOT 29:4–37"> And thou shalt bring the Levi'im before the Ohel Mo'ed: and thou shalt gather Kol Adat Bnei Yisroel together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="VAYIKRA 1:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:6; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:14"> And thou shalt bring the Levi'im before Hashem; and the Bnei Yisroel shall lay their hands upon the Levi'im;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="VAYIKRA 7:30; VAYIKRA 8:29; BAMIDBAR 1:49–53"> And Aharon shall offer the Levi'im before Hashem for a tenufah from the Bnei Yisroel, that they may perform the Avodat Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:4–10; SHEMOT 29:10; VAYIKRA 1:4"> And the Levi'im shall lay their hands upon the rosh of the bulls; and thou shalt offer the one for a chattat, and the other for an olah, unto Hashem, to make kapporah for the Levi'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 8:21; BAMIDBAR 8:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:16"> And thou shalt set the Levi'im before Aharon, and before his Banim, and offer them for a tenufah unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:45; MALACHI 3:17; BAMIDBAR 18:6"> Thus shalt thou set apart as separate the Levi'im from among the Bnei Yisroel; and the Levi'im shall be Mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 8:11; BAMIDBAR 8:13; BAMIDBAR 3:23–37"> And after that shall the Levi'im go in to do the service of the Ohel Mo'ed; and thou shalt make them tahor and offer them for a tenufah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:12; BAMIDBAR 3:45; BAMIDBAR 3:9"> For they are wholly given unto Me from among the Bnei Yisroel; instead of such as open every womb, even instead of the Bechor Kol Bnei Yisroel, have I taken them unto Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHEMOT 13:2; LUKAS 2:23; SHEMOT 13:12–15"> For Kol Bechor Bnei Yisroel are Mine, both adam and behemah; on the day that I struck down kol bechor in Eretz Mitzrayim I set them apart as kodesh for Myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And I have taken the Levi'im takhat (instead of) Kol Bechor Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:53; BAMIDBAR 16:46; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 26:16–20"> And I have given the Levi'im as a gift to Aharon and to his Banim from among the Bnei Yisroel, to do the Avodat Bnei Yisroel in the Ohel Mo'ed, and to make kapporah for the Bnei Yisroel; so that there be no plague among the Bnei Yisroel, when the Bnei Yisroel come near unto HaKodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Moshe, and Aharon, and Kol Adat Bnei Yisroel, did with the Levi'im according unto all that Hashem commanded Moshe concerning the Levi'im, so did the Bnei Yisroel unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 8:7; BAMIDBAR 19:19; BAMIDBAR 8:15"> And the Levi'im purified themselves, and they immersed their clothes; and Aharon offered them as a tenufah before Hashem; and Aharon made kapporah for them to make them tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 31:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 35:8–15; BAMIDBAR 8:15"> And after that went the Levi'im in to do their Avodah in the Ohel Mo'ed before Aharon, and before his Banim; just as Hashem had commanded Moshe concerning the Levi'im, so did they with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 4:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:3; BAMIDBAR 4:23"> This is it that belongeth unto the Levi'im: from twenty and five years old and upward they shall enter to perform service in the Avodat Ohel Mo'ed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 4:23; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:7"> And from the age of fifty years they shall retire from the service of the Avodah and shall serve no more;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:53; BAMIDBAR 3:32; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:15"> But shall assist with their brethren in the Ohel Mo'ed, in performing their duties, and shall do no Avodah. Thus shalt thou do with the Levi'im regarding their duties.</span>
</div>
