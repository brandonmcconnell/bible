<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 22:1; BAMIDBAR 31:12; BAMIDBAR 33:50"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe in the plains of Moav by Yarden near Yericho, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 14:3–4; YECHEZKEL 48:8; YEHOSHUA 21:2–42"> Command the Bnei Yisroel, that they give unto the Levi'im from the nachalah of their achuzzah towns to dwell in; and ye shall give also unto the Levi'im open pasturelands around the towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:14; YEHOSHUA 21:11; YECHEZKEL 45:2"> And the towns shall they have to dwell in; and the open pasturelands of them shall be for their behemah, and for their livestock, and for all their animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And the open pasturelands of the towns, which ye shall give unto the Levi'im, shall reach from the wall of the Ir and outward a thousand cubits all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And ye shall measure from outside the Ir on the east side 2,000 cubits, and on the south side 2,000 cubits, and on the west side 2,000 cubits, and on the north side 2,000 cubits; and the Ir shall be in the midst; this shall be to them the open pasturelands of the towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 21:13; YEHOSHUA 21:3; TEHILLIM 9:9"> And among the towns which ye shall give unto the Levi'im there shall be six cities for refuge, which ye shall appoint for the rotze'ach, that he may flee to there; and to them ye shall add 42 ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 21:3–42; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:54–81"> So all the arim (towns) which ye shall give to the Levi'im shall be 48 ir: them shall ye give with their migrashim (open pasturelands).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:54; BAMIDBAR 33:54; YEHOSHUA 21:1–42"> And the arim (towns) which ye shall give shall be of the achuzzat Bnei Yisroel; from them that have many ye shall give more; but from them that have few ye shall give less; every one shall give of his arim unto the Levi'im according to his nachalah which he inheriteth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:9; DEVARIM 19:1–2; VAYIKRA 14:34"> Speak unto the Bnei Yisroel, and say unto them, When ye be come over the Yarden into Eretz Kena'an,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 21:13; YEHOSHUA 20:2; BAMIDBAR 35:6"> Then ye shall appoint you arim to be arei miklat (cities of refuge) for you; that the rotze'ach may flee to there, which killeth any nefesh bishegagah (unintentionally, accidently).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 20:3–6; DEVARIM 19:6; YEHOSHUA 20:9"> And they shall be unto you arei miklat (cities of refuge) from the Go'el; that the rotze'ach die not, until he stand before the Edah in mishpat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And of these arim which ye shall give there shall be shesh arei miklat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEVARIM 19:8–10; DEVARIM 4:41–43; YEHOSHUA 20:7–9"> Ye shall give shlosh he'arim on this side of the Yarden, and shlosh he'arim shall ye give in Eretz Kena'an, which shall be arei miklat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 15:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:29"> These shesh he'arim shall be for miklat (refuge), both for the Bnei Yisroel, and for the ger, and for the toshav (sojourner) among them; that every one that killeth any nefesh bishegagah (unintentionally, accidently) may flee there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="VAYIKRA 24:17; BERESHIS 9:5–6; DEVARIM 19:11–13"> And if he strike him with keli barzel, so that he die, he is a rotze'ach; the rotze'ach shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHEMOT 21:18"> And if he strike him with an even (stone) in his yad that could kill, and he die, he is a rotze'ach; the rotze'ach shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Or if he strike him with a keli in his hand that could kill, and he die, he is a rotze'ach; the rotze'ach shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 20:3; DEVARIM 19:6; DEVARIM 19:12"> The Go'el HaDahm himself shall slay the rotze'ach; when he meeteth him, he shall slay him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="SHEMOT 21:14; DEVARIM 19:11; SHMUEL BAIS 3:27"> And if he push him out of sin'ah (hatred), or hurl upon him bitzediyyah (with intention, by laying in wait) that he die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Or in eivah (enmity) strike him with his yad, that he die; he that struck him shall surely be put to death; for he is a rotze'ach; the Go'el HaDahm shall slay the rotze'ach, when he meeteth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 35:11; SHEMOT 21:13; YEHOSHUA 20:3"> But if he push him suddenly without eivah (enmity), or have thrown upon him any keli with no tzediyyah (intention, laying in wait),</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Or with any even (stone), that could kill, seeing him not, and cast it upon him, that he die, and was not his oyev, neither sought his ra'ah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 20:6; BAMIDBAR 35:12"> Then the Edah shall judge between the striker and the Go'el HaDahm according to these mishpatim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:7; VAYIKRA 4:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:24–26"> And the Edah shall deliver the rotze'ach out of the yad Goel HaDahm, and the Edah shall restore him to his ir miklat, to which he fled; and he shall abide in it unto the mot HaKohen HaGadol which was mashach (anointed) with the shemen hakodesh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Notice there is freedom at the death of the High Priest just as there is peace at the death of the Moshiach Kohen; c.f. Isa 53:5-9; Ps 110:4">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> But if the rotze'ach shall at any time come outside the border of the ir miklat to which he fled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:2; DEVARIM 19:10; DEVARIM 19:6"> And the Go'el HaDahm find him outside the gevul (borders, city limit) of his ir miklat, and the Go'el HaDahm kill the rotze'ach, he shall not be guilty of dahm,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:26–30; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:4–8"> Because he should have remained in his ir miklat until the mot HaKohen HaGadol; but after the mot HaKohen HaGadol the rotze'ach shall return into his eretz achuzzat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 27:11; BAMIDBAR 27:1"> So these things shall be for a chukkat mishpat unto you throughout your dorot in all your dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:28; DEVARIM 19:15; MATTITYAHU 18:16"> Whosoever killeth any nefesh, the rotze'ach shall be put to death by the mouth of edim (witnesses); but ed echad (one witness) shall not testify against any nefesh to cause him to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEVARIM 19:11–13; BERESHIS 9:5–6; TEHILLIM 51:14"> Moreover ye shall take no kofer (ransom payment, satisfaction) for the nefesh of a rotze'ach, which is guilty of death; but he shall be surely put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:10–13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:21"> And ye shall take no kofer for him that is fled to his ir miklat that he should come again to dwell in HaAretz until the mot HaKohen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKAS 11:50–51; TEHILLIM 106:38; HOSHEA 4:2–3"> So ye shall not pollute HaAretz wherein ye are; for dahm pollutes HaAretz; and kapporah cannot be made for HaAretz for the shefach dahm therein, except by the dahm of him that committed shefach dahm (shedding of blood).</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 5:3; TEHILLIM 135:21; HISGALUS 21:27"> Therefore, make not tameh HaAretz which ye shall inhabit, wherein I dwell; for I Hashem dwell among the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
