<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YESHAYAH 1:13; YOEL 1:14; TEHILLIM 81:3"> Make thee two khatzotzerot (trumpets) of silver; of a whole piece shalt thou make them; that thou mayest use them for the calling of the Edah, and for the setting out of the machanot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 4:5; YOEL 2:15–16"> And when they shall sound them, Kol HaEdah shall assemble themselves to thee at the entrance of the Ohel Mo'ed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 7:2; SHEMOT 18:21; BAMIDBAR 1:4–16"> And if they sound but with one, then the Nasi'im, which are heads of Yisroelʼs thousands, shall gather themselves unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 2:3–9; BAMIDBAR 10:14; YESHAYAH 58:1"> When ye sound a teruah, then the machanot that camp on the east shall go forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 10:18; BAMIDBAR 2:10–16"> When ye sound a teruah the second time, then the machanot that camp on the south shall set out; they shall sound a teruah for their settings out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YOEL 2:1; BAMIDBAR 10:3–4"> But when the Kahal is to be gathered together, ye shall sound it, but ye shall not sound a teruah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 6:4–16; BAMIDBAR 31:6; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:24"> And the Bnei Aharon, the kohanim, shall sound the khatzotzerot; and they shall be to you for a chukkat olam throughout your dorot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 10:18; SHOFETIM 2:18; BERESHIS 8:1"> And if ye go into milchamah (battle) in your land against the enemy that oppresseth you, then ye shall sound a blast on the khatzotzerot; and ye shall be remembered before Hashem Eloheichem, and ye shall be saved from your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 81:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:24–26"> Also in the day of your simcha, and in your moadim, and in the Rosh-Chodesh, ye shall sound a blast on the khatzotzerot over your olot, and over the zevakhim of your shelamim; that they may be to you for a memorial reminder before Eloheichem: I am Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 40:17; BAMIDBAR 1:1; BAMIDBAR 9:11"> And it came to pass on the twentieth day of the second month, in the second year, that the Anan was lifted up from off the Mishkan HaEdut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 21:21; BAMIDBAR 12:16; BAMIDBAR 9:5"> And the Bnei Yisroel set out in their travels from the Midbar Sinai; and the Anan rested in the Midbar Paran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:6; BAMIDBAR 9:23"> And they first set out according to the mouth of Hashem by the hand of Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 2:3–9; BAMIDBAR 1:7; BAMIDBAR 7:12"> First in place went the degel of the machaneh of the Bnei Yehudah according to their tzivos; and over his division was Nachshon ben Amminadav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 7:18; BAMIDBAR 1:8"> And over the division of the tribe of the Bnei Yissakhar was Netanel ben Tzuar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 7:24; BAMIDBAR 1:9"> And over the division of the tribe of the Bnei Zevulun was Eliav ben Chelon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 7:6–8; BAMIDBAR 1:51; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:28"> And the Mishkan was taken down; and the Bnei Gershon and the Bnei Merari set out, bearing the Mishkan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 2:10–16; BAMIDBAR 1:5; BAMIDBAR 26:5–18"> And the degel of the machaneh of Reuven set out according to their tzivos (armies); and over his division was Elitzur ben Shede'ur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:6; BAMIDBAR 7:36"> And over the division of the tribe of the Bnei Shim'on was Shelumiel ben Tzurishaddai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:14; BAMIDBAR 2:14; BAMIDBAR 7:42"> And over the division of the tribe of the Bnei Gad was Elyasaph ben De'uel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 10:17; BAMIDBAR 7:9; BAMIDBAR 4:4–16"> And the descendants of Kehat set out, bearing the Mikdash; and they would erect the Mishkan before their arrival.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 2:18–24; BAMIDBAR 1:10; BAMIDBAR 26:23–41"> And the degel of the machaneh of the Bnei Ephrayim set out according to their tzivos (armies); and over his division was Elishama ben Ammihud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:10; BAMIDBAR 7:54"> And over the division of the tribe of the Bnei Menasheh was Gamli'el ben Pedahtzur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:11; BAMIDBAR 7:60"> And over the division of the tribe of the Bnei Binyamin was Avidan ben Gideoni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 6:9; BAMIDBAR 1:12; BERESHIS 49:16–17"> And the degel of the machaneh of the Bnei Dan set out, which formed the rear guard of all the machanot throughout their tzivos; and over his division was Achi'ezer ben Ammishaddai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:13; BAMIDBAR 7:72"> And over the division of the tribe of the Bnei Asher was Pagiel ben Okhran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:15; BAMIDBAR 7:78"> And over the division of the tribe of the Bnei Naphtali was Achira ben Enan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 2:34; BAMIDBAR 24:4–5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:33"> Thus was the order of march of the Bnei Yisroel according to their tzivos (armies), when they set out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:7; SHOFETIM 4:11; SHEMOT 3:1"> And Moshe said unto Chovav ben Reuel the Midyani, Mosheʼs khoten (father-in-law), We are traveling unto the place of which Hashem said, I will give it you: Come thou with us, and we will do thee good; for Hashem hath promised tov concerning Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="RUTH 1:15–17; TEHILLIM 45:10; MATTITYAHU 21:29"> And he said unto him, I will not go; but I will depart to mine own land, and to my moledet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="IYOV 29:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:14–21"> And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Moshe">a</a></sup>said, Leave us not, please; forasmuch as thou hast da'as how we are to encamp in the midbar, and thou mayest be to us as eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SHOFETIM 1:16; DEVARIM 10:18; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:3"> And it shall be, if thou go with us, yea, it shall be, that what tov Hashem shall do unto us, the same will we share with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:33; SHEMOT 3:1; TEHILLIM 95:11"> And they departed from the Mountain of Hashem three daysʼ journey; the Aron Brit Hashem went before them in the three daysʼ derech, to search out a menuchah for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 9:12; NECHEMYAH 9:19; TEHILLIM 105:39"> And the Anan of Hashem was upon them by day, when they set out from the machaneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="TEHILLIM 68:1–2; YESHAYAH 17:12–14; TEHILLIM 132:8"> And it came to pass, when the Aron set out, that Moshe said, Rise up, Hashem, and let Thine enemies be scattered; and let them that hate Thee flee before Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:10; TEHILLIM 90:13–17; YESHAYAH 63:17"> And when it rested, he said, Return, Hashem, unto the countless thousands of Yisroel.</span>
