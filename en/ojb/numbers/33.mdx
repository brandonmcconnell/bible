<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="TEHILLIM 77:20; MICHOH 6:4; SHEMOT 12:51"> These are the journeys of the Bnei Yisroel, which went forth out of Eretz Mitzrayim by their tzveos under yad Moshe and Aharon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 9:17–23; DEVARIM 10:11; BAMIDBAR 10:6"> And Moshe recorded their goings out according to their journeys by the commandment of Hashem; and these are their journeys according to their goings forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 14:8; SHEMOT 12:37; SHEMOT 13:4"> And they departed from Ra'meses in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month; on the day after the Pesach the Bnei Yisroel went out with a yad ramah (upraised hand, i.e., confidently) l'einei kol Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:12; YESHAYAH 19:1; SHEMOT 18:11"> For the Mitzrayim buried all their bechor, which Hashem had struck down among them; upon their elohim also Hashem executed shefatim (judgments).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:37"> And the Bnei Yisroel set out from Ra'meses, and encamped in Sukkot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 13:20"> And they set out from Sukkot, and encamped in Etam, which is on the edge of the midbar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 14:2; SHEMOT 14:9; BAMIDBAR 33:8"> And they set out from Etam, and turned back unto Pi-HaChirot, which is before Ba'al-Tzephon; and they encamped before Migdol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 15:22–26; SHEMOT 14:21–31"> And they set out from before HaChirot, and passed through the midst of the yam into the midbar, and went derech shloshet yamim (a three daysʼ journey) in the midbar of Etam, and encamped in Marah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 15:27"> And they set out from Marah, and came unto Elim; and in Elim were twelve einot mayim (springs of water), and threescore and ten temarim (palm trees); and they encamped there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:1; SHEMOT 17:1"> And they set out from Elim, and encamped by the Yam Suf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:1"> And they set out from the Yam Suf, encamping in the Midbar Sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And they set out from the Midbar Sin, and encamped in Dophkah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And they set out from Dophkah, and encamped in Alush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 17:1–8; SHEMOT 19:2"> And they set out from Alush, and encamped at Rephidim, where was no mayim for HaAm to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:1–2; SHEMOT 16:1"> And they set out from Rephidim, and encamped in the Midbar Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 11:34; BAMIDBAR 10:11–13; BAMIDBAR 10:33"> And they set out from the Midbar Sinai, and encamped at Kivrot-Hata'avah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 11:35"> And they set out from Kivrot-Hata'avah, and encamped at Chatzerot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 12:16"> And they set out from Chatzerot, and encamped in Ritmah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And they set out from Ritmah, and encamped at Rimmon Peretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And they set out from Rimmon Peretz, and encamped in Livnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:1"> And they set out from Livnah, and encamped at Rissah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And they set out from Rissah, and encamped in Kehelatah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And they set out from Kehelatah, and encamped in Har Shepher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And they set out from Har Shepher, and encamped in Charadah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And they set out from Charadah, and encamped in Mak'helot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And they set out from Mak'helot, and encamped at Tachat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And they set out from Tachat, and encamped at Tarach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And they set out from Tarach, and encamped in Mitkah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And they set out from Mitkah, and encamped in Chashmonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:5–6"> And they set out from Chashmonah, and encamped at Moserot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:6; BERESHIS 36:27; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 1:43"> And they set out from Moserot, and encamped in Bnei Ya'akan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:7"> And they set out from Bnei Ya'akan, and encamped at Chor Haggidgad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:7"> And they set out from Chor Haggidgad, and encamped in Yotvatah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And they set out from Yotvatah, and encamped at Avronah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEVARIM 2:8; MELACHIM ALEF 9:26; MELACHIM ALEF 22:48"> And they set out from Avronah, and encamped at Etzyon-Gever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 20:1; BAMIDBAR 27:14; DEVARIM 32:51"> And they set out from Etzyon-Gever, and encamped in the Midbar Tzin, which is Kadesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:4; BAMIDBAR 20:22–23; BAMIDBAR 20:16"> And they set out from Kadesh and encamped at Mt Hor, at the border of Eretz Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:6; DEVARIM 32:50; BAMIDBAR 20:24–28"> And Aharon HaKohen went up into Mt Hor at the command of Hashem, and died there, in the 40th year after the Bnei Yisroel were come out of Eretz Mitzrayim, in the first yom of the fifth month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And Aharon was a hundred and twenty and three years old when he died on Mt Hor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:1–9"> And Melech Arad the Kena'ani, which dwelt in the Negev in Eretz Kena'an, heard of the coming of the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:4"> And they set out from Mt Hor, and encamped in Tzalmonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And they set out from Tzalmonah, and encamped in Punon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:10"> And they set out from Punon, and encamped in Ovot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:11"> And they set out from Ovot, and encamped in Iyei-HaAvarim at border of Moav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And they set out from Iyim, and encamped in Divon Gad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 48:18; YECHEZKEL 6:14; YESHAYAH 15:2"> And they set out from Divon Gad, and encamped in Almon Divlatayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:49; DEVARIM 34:1; BAMIDBAR 27:12"> And they set out from Almon Divlatayim, and encamped in the mountains of Avarim, before Nevo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 22:1"> And they set out from the mountains of Avarim, and encamped in the plains of Moav by Yarden near Yericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 2:1; YEHOSHUA 13:20; BAMIDBAR 25:1–9"> And they set out by Yarden, from Beit-HaYeshimot even unto Avel-Sheetim in the plains of Moav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 33:48–49"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe in the plains of Moav by Yarden near Yericho, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 3:17; DEVARIM 9:1; DEVARIM 7:1"> Speak unto the Bnei Yisroel, and say unto them, When ye are crossing over Yarden into Eretz Kena'an,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:24; VAYIKRA 26:1; DEVARIM 20:16–18"> Then ye shall drive out all the inhabitants of HaAretz from before you, and destroy all their maskiyyot (stone carved statues), and destroy all their tzalmei massekhot and demolish all their &#91;idolatrous&#93; high places;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="DEVARIM 11:31; TEHILLIM 115:16; DEVARIM 32:8"> And ye shall take possession of HaAretz, and dwell therein; for I have given you HaAretz to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:53–56; YEHOSHUA 18:11–48; YEHOSHUA 15:1–12"> And ye shall distribute HaAretz by goral for an inheritance among your mishpekhot; and to the more numerous ye shall give the larger nachalah, and to the fewer in number ye shall give the smaller nachalah; every manʼs nachalah shall be in the place where his goral falleth; according to the mattot (tribes of) Avoteichem ye shall inherit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="SHOFETIM 2:3; TEHILLIM 106:34–36; DEVARIM 7:16"> But if ye will not drive out the yoshvei HaAretz from before you; then it shall come to pass, that those which ye let remain of them shall be barbs in your eyes, and thorns in your sides, and shall harass you in HaAretz wherein ye dwell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="LUKAS 21:23–24; DEVARIM 28:63; VAYIKRA 20:23"> Moreover it shall come to pass, that I shall do unto you, as I meant to do unto them.</span>
