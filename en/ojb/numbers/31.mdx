<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 27:13; BAMIDBAR 25:14–18; HISGALUS 6:10"> Avenge the Bnei Yisroel of the Midyanim; afterward shalt thou be gathered unto thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:25; BAMIDBAR 25:11; MELACHIM BAIS 10:30"> And Moshe spoke unto HaAm, saying, Arm some of your anashim unto war, let them go against Midyan, and avenge Hashem of Midyan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:8; SHMUEL ALEF 14:6; SHOFETIM 7:2"> Of every matteh a thousand, throughout kol mattot Yisroel, shall ye send to war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So there were supplied out of the thousands of Yisroel, an elef (a thousand) of every matteh, sheneym asar elef armed for war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:44; BAMIDBAR 10:8–9; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 13:12–15"> And Moshe sent them to war, a thousand of every tribe, them and Pinchas Ben El'azar HaKohen, to the war, with the kelei hakodesh, and the khatzotzerot hateru'ah in his yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHOFETIM 21:11; MELACHIM ALEF 11:15–16; SHMUEL ALEF 27:9"> And they warred against Midyan, just, as Hashem commanded Moshe; and they slaughtered kol zachar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 13:21–22; BAMIDBAR 25:15; HISGALUS 2:14"> And they slaughtered the Melachim of Midyan, besides the rest of them that were slain; namely, Evi, and Rekem, and Tzur, and Chur, and Reva, five melachim of Midyan; Balaam Ben Be'or also they slaughtered with the cherev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEVARIM 20:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:8–10; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:5"> And the Bnei Yisroel took all the nashim of Midyan captive, and their little ones, and took the plunder of all their behemah, and all their mikneh, and all their goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YESHAYAH 1:7; SHMUEL ALEF 30:1; YEHOSHUA 6:24"> And they burned with eish all their towns wherein were their moshavot, and all their camps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEVARIM 20:14; YEHOSHUA 8:2"> And they took all the plunder, and all the spoil, both of adam and of behemah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 22:1"> And they brought the captives, and the plunder, and the spoil, unto Moshe, and El'azar HaKohen, and unto the Adat Bnei Yisroel, unto the machaneh at the plains of Moav, which are by Yarden near Yericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 5:2; SHMUEL ALEF 30:21; BAMIDBAR 31:12"> And Moshe, and El'azar HaKohen, and all the nasiim of the Edah, went forth to meet them outside the machaneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 13:19; SHMUEL ALEF 15:13–14; BAMIDBAR 31:48"> And Moshe was in wrath with the pekudei hechayil (officers of the army), with the sarim over thousands, and sarim over hundreds, which were returning from the milchamah of the army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:3; YIRMEYAH 48:10; DEVARIM 2:34"> And Moshe said unto them, Have ye saved all the nekevah alive?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 25:1–9; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15; HISGALUS 2:14"> Hinei, these caused the Bnei Yisroel, through the davar Balaam, to turn unfaithful against Hashem in the matter of Pe'or, and there was a magefah (plague) among the Adat Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHOFETIM 21:11–12; DEVARIM 20:16–18; DEVARIM 7:2"> Now therefore slaughter every zachar among the little ones, and kill every isha that hath known ish by lying with zachar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEVARIM 21:10–14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:8–10; DEVARIM 20:14"> But all the girl nashim, that have no da'as of lying with zachar, save alive for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 19:11–22; BAMIDBAR 5:2; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:8"> And do ye abide outside the machaneh shivat yamim; whosoever hath killed nefesh, and whosoever hath touched any chalal, purify both yourselves and your captives on Yom HaShelishi and on Yom HaShevi'i.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 19:22; BERESHIS 35:2; SHEMOT 19:10"> And purify all your beged, and all keli ohr (all that is made of leather), ma'aseh izzim (all that is made of goat hair) and keli etz (all that is make of wood).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 30:16"> And El'azar HaKohen said unto the anshei hatzavah which went into milchamah, This is the chukkat hatorah which Hashem commanded Moshe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Only the zahav, and the kesef, the nechoshet, the barzel, the bedil, and the oferet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 19:9; BAMIDBAR 19:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:13"> Everything that can withstand eish, ye shall make it go through the eish, and it shall be tahor; nevertheless it shall be purified with the mayim of niddah; and all that can not withstand the eish ye shall make go through the mayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="VAYIKRA 11:25; VAYIKRA 15:13; VAYIKRA 14:9"> And ye shall wash your begadim on Yom HaShevi'i, and ye shall be tahor, and afterward ye shall come into the machaneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Take inventory of the spoil that was taken, both of adam and of behemah, thou, and El'azar HaKohen, and the Rashei Avot HaEdah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 22:8; TEHILLIM 68:12; SHMUEL ALEF 30:24–25"> And divide the spoil into two parts: between them that took the milchamah upon them, who went out to battle, and kol HaEdah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 18:26; BAMIDBAR 31:30; BAMIDBAR 31:47"> And levy a mekhes (tribute) unto Hashem of the anshei hamilchamah which went out to battle: one nefesh from five hundred, both of adam, and of bakar, and of chamorim, and of tzon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:27; DEVARIM 12:19; DEVARIM 12:12"> Take it of their half, and give it unto El'azar HaKohen, for a terumat Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:31; BAMIDBAR 3:7–8; BAMIDBAR 3:25"> And of the Bnei Yisroelʼs half, thou shalt take one portion of fifty, of adam, of bakar, of chamorim, and of tzon, of all behemah, and give them unto the Levi'im, who are shomrei mishmeret Mishkan Hashem(who are guardians of the Mishkan Hashem).</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Moshe and El'azar HaKohen did just as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the spoil, being the rest of the plunder which the am hatzava (personnel of the army) had taken, was 675,000 tzon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And 72,000 bakar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And 61,000 chamorim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And 32,000 nefesh adam from the nashim who had no da'as of zachar by lying with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And the half, which was the chelek of them that went out to war, was in number 337,500 tzon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And the mekhes (tribute) for Hashem of the tzon was 675.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And the bakar were 36,000; of which the mekhes for Hashem was 72.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And the chamorim were 30,500; of which the mekhes for Hashem was 61.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And the nefesh adam were 16,000; of which the mekhes for Hashem was 32 nefesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 18:8; BAMIDBAR 18:19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:9–12"> And Moshe gave the mekhes, which was the terumat Hashem, unto El'azar HaKohen, as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And of the Bnei Yisroelʼs half, which Moshe divided from the anashim hatzove'im,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> (Now the half that pertained unto the Edah was 337,500 tzon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And 36,000 bakar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And thirty thousand chamorim and five hundred,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And sixteen thousand nefesh adam);</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 31:30; LUKAS 10:1–8; YESHAYAH 56:10–11"> Even of the Bnei Yisroelʼs half, Moshe took one portion of fifty, both of adam and of behemah, and gave them unto the Levi'im, who were shomrei mishmeret Mishkan Hashem; just as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And the pekudim (officers) which were over thousands of the tzava (army), the sarim of thousands, and sarim of hundreds, came near unto Moshe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:9; SHMUEL ALEF 30:18–19; TEHILLIM 72:14"> And they said unto Moshe, Thy avadim have taken the muster census of the anshei hamilchamah which are under our charge, and there was missing not one ish of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="SHEMOT 30:12; SHEMOT 30:15–16; TEHILLIM 116:12"> We have therefore brought a korban Hashem, what every ish hath gotten, of articles of zahav, armlets, and bracelets, rings, earrings, and necklaces, to make kapporah for our nefashot before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 7:2–6"> And Moshe and El'azar HaKohen took the zahav of them, even all wrought jewels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And all the zahav of the terumah (offering) that they offered up to Hashem, of the sarim of thousands, and of the sarim of hundreds, was sixteen thousand seven hundred and fifty shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="DEVARIM 20:14; BAMIDBAR 31:32"> (For the anshei hatzava had taken plunder, every ish for himself.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="SHEMOT 30:16; ZECHARYAH 6:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:4"> And Moshe and El'azar HaKohen accepted the zahav of the sarim of thousands and of hundreds, and brought it into the Ohel Mo'ed, for a zikaron (memorial) for the Bnei Yisroel before Hashem.</span>
