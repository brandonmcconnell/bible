## &#91;KORACH&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:11; DEVARIM 11:6; SHEMOT 6:21"> Now Korach ben Yitzhar ben Kehat ben Levi, and Datan and Aviram, the Bnei Eliav, and On ben Pelet, Bnei Reuven, became insolent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:9; YECHEZKEL 23:10; BERESHIS 6:4"> And they rose up before Moshe, with certain of the Bnei Yisroel, two hundred and fifty Nasi'ei Edah, chosen from the assembly, men of renown;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TEHILLIM 106:16; BAMIDBAR 14:14; SHEMOT 19:6"> And they gathered themselves together against Moshe and against Aharon, and said unto them, Ye take too much upon you, seeing Kol HaEdah are Kedoshim, every one of them, and Hashem is among them; why then lift ye up yourselves above the Kehal Hashem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:5; BAMIDBAR 20:6; YEHOSHUA 7:6"> And when Moshe heard it, he fell upon his face;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 65:4; BAMIDBAR 17:5; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:19"> And he spoke unto Korach and unto all his edah, saying, In the boker Hashem will show who are His, and who is the kadosh; and will cause him to come near unto Him: even him whom He hath chosen will He cause to come near unto Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="VAYIKRA 10:1; BAMIDBAR 16:46–48; MELACHIM ALEF 18:21–23"> This do: take you fire pans, Korach, and all his edah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 16:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:4; MELACHIM ALEF 18:17–18"> And put eish therein, put ketoret in them before Hashem tomorrow; it shall be that the man whom Hashem doth choose, he shall be haKadosh; ye take too much upon you, ye Bnei Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Moshe said unto Korach, Hear, now, ye Bnei Levi:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YESHAYAH 7:13; DEVARIM 10:8; YECHEZKEL 44:10–11"> Seemeth it but a small thing unto you, that Elohei Yisroel hath separated you from the Adat Yisroel, to bring you near to Himself to do the Avodat Mishkan Hashem, and to stand before the Edah to minister unto them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MISHLE 13:10; (III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9; BAMIDBAR 18:7"> And He hath brought thee near to Him, and all thy brethren the Bnei Levi with thee; and seek ye the Kehunah also?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:7–8; SHEMOT 17:2; BAMIDBAR 16:3"> For which cause both thou and all thy edah are gathered together against Hashem; and what is Aharon, that ye murmur against him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13–14; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8; MISHLE 29:9"> And Moshe sent to call Datan and Aviram, the Bnei Eliav, which said, We will not come up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:35; SHEMOT 2:14; BAMIDBAR 11:5"> Is it a small thing that thou hast brought us up out of an Eretz zavat cholov udevash, to kill us in the midbar, yet thou also keep trying to dominate us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:24; BAMIDBAR 36:8–10; BAMIDBAR 20:5"> Moreover thou hast not brought us into an Eretz zavat cholov udevash, or given us nachalah of sadeh and kerem; wilt thou gouge out the eyes of these men? We will not come up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 4:4–5; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:10"> And Moshe was very angry, and said unto Hashem, Respect not thou their minchah; I have not taken one donkey from them, neither have I wronged one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 12:3; SHMUEL ALEF 12:7; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:14"> And Moshe said unto Korach, Be thou and all thy edah before Hashem, thou, they, and Aharon, tomorrow;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 12:7"> And take every man his fire pan, put ketoret in them, and bring ye before Hashem every man his fire pan, 250 fire pans; thou also, and Aharon, each of you his fire pan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And they took every man his fire pan and put eish in them and laid ketoret thereon, and stood in the entrance of the Ohel Mo'ed with Moshe and Aharon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:10; BAMIDBAR 16:42; SHEMOT 16:7"> And Korach gathered kol haedah against them unto the entrance of the Ohel Mo'ed; and the Kavod Hashem appeared unto Kol HaEdah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe and unto Aharon, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 16:45; SHEMOT 32:10; TEHILLIM 73:19"> Separate yourselves from among this edah, that I may consume them in a moment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 27:16; ZECHARYAH 12:1; IYOV 12:10"> And they fell upon their faces, and said, El Elohei HaRuchot L'Chol Basar, shall one man sin, and wilt Thou be angry with Kol HaEdah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 16:21"> Speak unto HaEdah, saying, Get you up away from the mishkan (dwelling place) of Korach, Datan, and Aviram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 11:16–17; BAMIDBAR 11:25; BAMIDBAR 11:30"> And Moshe rose up and went unto Datan and Aviram; and the Ziknei Yisroel followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YESHAYAH 52:11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:17; HISGALUS 18:4"> And he spoke unto HaEdah, saying, Move back, now, from the tents of these anashim resha'im, and touch nothing of theirs, lest ye be consumed in all their chattot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="IYOV 9:4; YESHAYAH 28:14; IYOV 40:10–11"> So they moved back from the mishkan Korach, Datan, and Aviram, on every side; Datan and Aviram came out, and stood in the entrance of their tents, their wives, their banim, and their little ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:38; SHEMOT 3:12; YOCHANAN 14:11"> And Moshe said, Hereby ye shall know that Hashem hath sent me to do all these ma'asim; for I have not done them of mine own will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EKHAH 4:22; MELACHIM ALEF 22:28; SHEMOT 32:34"> If these men die the common death of kol haAdam, or if they be visited after the visitation of kol haAdam, then Hashem hath not sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="TEHILLIM 55:15; BAMIDBAR 16:33; IYOV 31:3"> But if Hashem creates a new thing, and haadamah open her mouth, and swallow them up, with all that appertain unto them, and they go down chayyim into Sheol; then ye shall have da'as that these men have spurned Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="TEHILLIM 106:17–18; BAMIDBAR 27:3; DEVARIM 11:6"> And it came to pass, as he had made an end of speaking all these devarim, that haadamah split asunder that was under them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="TEHILLIM 106:17; BAMIDBAR 26:11; HISGALUS 12:16"> And ha'aretz opened her mouth, and swallowed them up, their batim (houses), all the men that were of Korach, and all their possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 32:18; YESHAYAH 14:9; TEHILLIM 9:15"> They, and all that were of them, went down chayyim into Sheol, and ha'aretz closed over them; and they perished from the midst of the Kahal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 14:5; YESHAYAH 33:3; HISGALUS 6:15–17"> And Kol Yisroel that were round about them fled at the cry of them, for they said, Lest ha'aretz swallow us up also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:10; VAYIKRA 10:2; TEHILLIM 106:18"> And there came out from Hashem an eish, and consumed the 250 men that offered ketoret.</span>
</div>
