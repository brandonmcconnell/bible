<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="SHEMOT 25:22; SHEMOT 19:1; BAMIDBAR 10:11–12"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe in the midbar of Sinai, in the Ohel Mo'ed, on the first day of the second month, in the second year after they were come out of Eretz Mitzrayim, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 38:26; SHEMOT 30:12; BAMIDBAR 1:18"> Take ye the rosh (top amount, census) of kol Adat Bnei Yisroel after their mishpekhot, by the bais of their avot, with the number of their shmot, every zachar by their head count;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 30:14; BAMIDBAR 14:29; BAMIDBAR 33:1"> From twenty years old and upward, all that are able-bodied to serve in tzava b'Yisroel (army in Yisroel); thou and Aharon shall number them by their tzva'ot (legions).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:16; SHEMOT 18:25; BAMIDBAR 13:2–15"> And with you there shall be a man of every tribe; every one Rosh of the bais of his Avot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 7:30; BERESHIS 30:5–20; BERESHIS 49:1–33"> And these are the shemot haanashim that shall stand with you: of the tribe of Reuven, Elitzur ben Shede'ur;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 7:36; BAMIDBAR 2:12"> Of Shim'on, Shelumiel ben Tzurishaddai;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKAS 3:32; BAMIDBAR 10:14; MATTITYAHU 1:2–5"> Of Yehudah, Nachshon ben Amminadav;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 7:18; BAMIDBAR 10:15; BAMIDBAR 2:5"> Of Yissakhar, Netanel ben Tzuar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 7:24; BAMIDBAR 10:16; BAMIDBAR 2:7"> Of Zevulun, Eliav ben Chelon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 7:54; BAMIDBAR 7:48; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 7:26–27"> Of the bnei Yosef: of Ephrayim, Elishama ben Ammihud; of Menasheh, Gamli'el ben Pedahtzur;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 7:60; BAMIDBAR 10:24; BAMIDBAR 2:22"> Of Binyamin, Avidan ben Gideoni;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 7:66; BAMIDBAR 2:25; BAMIDBAR 10:25"> Of Dan, Achi'ezer ben Ammishaddai;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 7:72; BAMIDBAR 10:26; BAMIDBAR 2:27"> Of Asher, Pagiel ben Okhran;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 2:14; BAMIDBAR 7:42; BAMIDBAR 10:20"> Of Gad, Elyasaph ben De'uel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 7:78; BAMIDBAR 10:27; BAMIDBAR 2:29"> Of Naphtali, Achira ben Enan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 7:2; SHEMOT 18:21; SHEMOT 18:25"> These were the called men of the Edah, nasiim of the tribes of their avot, rashei alfei Yisroel (head-men of the clans of Yisroel).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:3; HISGALUS 7:4–17; BAMIDBAR 1:5–15"> And Moshe and Aharon took these men which had been designated by their names;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZRA 2:59; NECHEMYAH 7:61; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:3"> And they assembled kol HaEdah together on the first yom of the second month, and they declared their family after their mishpekhot, by the bais of their avot, according to the number of the shmot, from twenty years old and upward, one by one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 1:2; BAMIDBAR 26:1–2; SHMUEL BAIS 24:1–10"> As Hashem commanded Moshe, so he counted them in the midbar of Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:5–7; BAMIDBAR 2:10–11; BERESHIS 46:9"> And the bnei Reuven, Yisroelʼs bechor, by their toldot, after their mishpekhot, by the bais of their avot, according to the number of the shmot, one by one, every zachar from twenty years old and upward, all that are able-bodied to serve in tzava;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:7; BAMIDBAR 2:10–11"> Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Reuven, were forty and six thousand and five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:12–14; BERESHIS 34:25–30; BAMIDBAR 2:12–13"> Of the bnei Shim'on, by their toldot, after their mishpekhot, by the bais of their avot, those that were numbered of them, according to the number of the shmot, one by one, every male from twenty years old and upward, all that were able-bodied to serve in tzava (army);</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:14; BAMIDBAR 2:13; BAMIDBAR 25:8–9"> Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Shim'on, were fifty and nine thousand and three hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:15–18; BERESHIS 46:16; BAMIDBAR 2:14–15"> Of the bnei Gad, by their toldot, after their mishpekhot, by the bais of their avot, according to the number of the shmot, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able-bodied to serve in tzava;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:18; BAMIDBAR 2:15"> Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Gad, were forty and five thousand six hundred and fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="BERESHIS 29:35; BAMIDBAR 26:19–22; SHMUEL BAIS 24:9"> Of the bnei Yehudah, by their toldot, after their mishpekhot, by the bais of their avot, according to the number of the shmot, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able-bodied to serve in tzava (army);</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 24:9; BAMIDBAR 26:22; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 17:14–16"> Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Yehudah, were threescore and fourteen thousand and six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:13; BERESHIS 49:14–15; BERESHIS 30:18"> Of the bnei Yissakhar, by their toldot, after their mishpekhot, by the bais of their avot, according to the number of the shmot, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able-bodied to serve in tzava (army);</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:25; BAMIDBAR 2:6"> Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Yissakhar, were fifty and four thousand and four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:26–27; BERESHIS 49:13; BERESHIS 30:20"> Of the bnei Zevulun, by their toldot, after their mishpekhot, by the bais of their avot, according to the number of the shmot, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able-bodied to serve in tzava (army);</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:27; BAMIDBAR 2:8"> Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Zevulun, were fifty and seven thousand and four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:35–37; DEVARIM 33:17; BERESHIS 46:20"> Of the bnei Yosef, namely, of the bnei Ephrayim, by their toldot, after their mishpekhot, by the bais of their avot, according to the number of the shmot, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able-bodied to serve in tzava (army);</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:37; DEVARIM 33:17; BAMIDBAR 2:19"> Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Ephrayim, were forty thousand and five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:28–34"> Of the bnei Menasheh, by their toldot, after their mishpekhot, by the bais of their avot, according to the number of the shmot, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able-bodied to serve in tzava (army);</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:34; BERESHIS 48:19–20; BAMIDBAR 2:21"> Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Menasheh, were thirty and two thousand and two hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="BERESHIS 49:27; BERESHIS 44:20; HISGALUS 7:8"> Of the bnei Binyamin, by their toldot, after their mishpekhot, by the bais of their avot, according to the number of the shmot, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able-bodied to serve in tzava (army);</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:41; SHOFETIM 20:44–46; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 17:17"> Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Binyamin, were thirty and five thousand and four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="BERESHIS 46:23; BERESHIS 30:5–6; BERESHIS 49:16–17"> Of the bnei Dan, by their toldot, after their mishpekhot, by the bais of their avot, according to the number of the shmot, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able-bodied to serve in tzava (army);</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:43; BAMIDBAR 2:26"> Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Dan, were threescore and two thousand and seven hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="BERESHIS 30:12–13; BERESHIS 46:27; BERESHIS 49:20"> Of the bnei Asher, by their toldot, after their mishpekhot, by the bais of their avot, according to the number of the shmot, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able-bodied to serve in tzava (army);</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:47; BAMIDBAR 2:28"> Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Asher, were forty and one thousand and five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="BERESHIS 49:21; BAMIDBAR 26:48–50; BERESHIS 46:24"> Of the bnei Naphtali, throughout their toldot, after their mishpekhot, by the bais of their avot, according to the number of the shmot, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able-bodied to serve in tzava (army);</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:50; BAMIDBAR 2:30"> Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Naphtali, were fifty and three thousand and four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:64; BAMIDBAR 1:2–16"> These are those that were numbered, which Moshe and Aharon numbered, and the nasiim (princes, rulers, exalted) of Yisroel, being shneym asar (twelve) men, each one representing the bais of his avot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> So were all those that were numbered of the Bnei Yisroel, by the bais of their avot, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able-bodied to serve in tzava b'Yisroel (army in Yisroel);</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:37; BAMIDBAR 26:51; SHEMOT 38:26"> Even all they that were numbered were six hundred thousand and three thousand and five hundred and fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 2:33; BAMIDBAR 3:1–49; BAMIDBAR 26:57–62"> But the Levi'im after the tribe of their avot were not numbered among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> For Hashem had spoken unto Moshe saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:62; BAMIDBAR 2:33"> Only thou shalt not number the tribe of Levi, neither take the sum of them among the Bnei Yisroel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="SHEMOT 38:21; SHEMOT 31:18; BAMIDBAR 4:15"> But thou shalt appoint the Levi'im over the Mishkan HaEdut and over all the vessels thereof, and over all things that belong to it; they shall bear the Mishkan, and all the vessels thereof; and they shall take care of it, and shall encamp round about the Mishkan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 3:38; BAMIDBAR 3:10; SHMUEL ALEF 6:19"> And when the Mishkan setteth forward, the Levi'im shall take it down; and when the Mishkan is to be pitched, the Levi'im shall set it up; and the zar (stranger, outsider, unauthorized party) that cometh nigh shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 2:2; BAMIDBAR 2:34; BAMIDBAR 10:1–36"> And the Bnei Yisroel shall pitch their tents, every man by his own machaneh (camp), and every man by his own degel (standard, banner), throughout their tzva'ot (hosts).</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 16:46; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 23:32; VAYIKRA 10:6"> But the Levi'im shall encamp around the Mishkan HaEdut, that there be no ketzef (wrath) fall upon the Adat Bnei Yisroel; and the Levi'im shall be shomer mishmeret (watch the watch, attend to, keep the charge, the reverence) of the Mishkan HaEdut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:32; SHEMOT 40:16; SHEMOT 39:32"> And the Bnei Yisroel did according to all that Hashem commanded Moshe, so did they.</span>
