<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:33; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:28; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 7:15"> Then came the Banot Tzelophechad Ben Chepher Ben Gil'ad Ben Machir Ben Menasheh, of the Mishpekhot Menasheh Ben Yosef: and these are the shmot of his banot: Machlah, No'ah, and Choglah, and Milcah, and Tirtzah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 15:33–34; DEVARIM 17:8–10; SHEMOT 18:19–26"> And they stood before Moshe, and before El'azar HaKohen, and before the Nasiim and kol HaEdah, by the petach Ohel Mo'ed, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:64–65; YECHEZKEL 18:4; BAMIDBAR 14:35"> Avinu died in the midbar, and he was not in the edah hano'adim (assembly of ones banding together) against Hashem in the Adat Korach; but died in his own chet, and had no banim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 17:4; TEHILLIM 109:13; SHEMOT 32:11"> Why should the shem avinu be withdrawn<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Rv 3:5">a</a></sup>from among his Mishpakhat, because he hath no ben? Give unto us therefore achuzzah among the achei avinu.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="VAYIKRA 24:12–13; IYOV 23:4; MISHLE 3:5–6"> And Moshe brought their mishpat before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:28; TEHILLIM 68:5–6"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:28; YIRMEYAH 49:11; YEHOSHUA 17:4"> The Banot Tzelophechad speak right; thou shalt surely give them achuzzat nachalah among achei avihem; and thou shalt cause the nachalat avihen to pass over unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And thou shalt speak unto the Bnei Yisroel, saying, If an ish die, and have no ben, then ye shall cause his nachalah to pass over unto his bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And if he have no bat, then ye shall give his nachalah unto his achim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And if he have no achim, then ye shall give his nachalah unto achei aviv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 35:29; RUTH 4:3–6; YIRMEYAH 32:8"> And if his av have no achim, then ye shall give his nachalah unto his closest relative of his Mishpakhat, and he shall possess it; and it shall be unto the Bnei Yisroel a chukkat mishpat, as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 33:47–48; DEVARIM 34:1–4; DEVARIM 3:23–27"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, Get thee up on this har haAvarim, and see HaAretz which I have given unto the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:6; BAMIDBAR 31:2; BAMIDBAR 20:24–28"> And when thou hast seen it, thou also shalt be gathered unto thy people, as Aharon achicha was gathered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:37; BAMIDBAR 20:8–13; SHEMOT 17:7"> For ye rebelled against My command in the Midbar Tzin, in the merivat HaEdah (strife of the Assembly) to honor Me as Kodesh before their eyes at the mayim: that is, the mayim of Merivah in Kadesh in the Midbar Tzin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Moshe spoke unto Hashem, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 16:22; YIRMEYAH 3:15; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:9"> Let Hashem Elohei HaRuchot L'khol Basar appoint an Ish over HaEdah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 34:5; MARKOS 6:34; MELACHIM ALEF 22:17"> Which will go out before them, and which will come in before them, and which will lead them out, and which will bring them in; that the Adat Hashem be not like tzon which have no ro'eh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEVARIM 34:9; BAMIDBAR 27:23; BERESHIS 41:38"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, Take thee Yehoshua Ben Nun, an ish in whom is the Ruach,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="HaKodesh">b</a></sup>and lay thine yad<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="for s'michah">c</a></sup>upon him;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Zech 6:11-12">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 3:28; DEVARIM 31:7–8; DEVARIM 31:23"> And stand him before El'azar HaKohen, and before kol HaEdah and give him a charge<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="instruct him with regard to his office">e</a></sup>before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 1:16–18; SHMUEL ALEF 10:9; MELACHIM BAIS 2:9–10"> And thou shalt lay of thine hod (dignity, authority, majesty) upon him, so that kol Adat Bnei Yisroel will obey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 9:14; SHMUEL ALEF 28:6; SHEMOT 28:30"> And he shall stand before El'azar HaKohen, who shall ask counsel for him after the mishpat HaUrim before Hashem: at his word shall they go out, and at his word they shall come in, both he, and kol Bnei Yisroel with him, even kol HaEdah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Moshe did just as Hashem commanded him; and he took Yehoshua, and stood him before El'azar HaKohen, and before kol HaEdah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEVARIM 3:28; BAMIDBAR 27:19; DEVARIM 31:7–8"> And he laid his yadayim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="for s'michah">f</a></sup>upon him, and gave him a charge,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="instructed him with regard to his office">g</a></sup>just as Hashem commanded by yad Moshe.</span>
</div>
</div>
