## &#91;SHELACH LEKHA&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:22–25; BAMIDBAR 32:8; BAMIDBAR 34:18"> Send thou anashim, that they may spy out Eretz Kena'an, which I give unto the Bnei Yisroel: of every tribe of their avot shall ye send a man, every one a nasi among them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 12:16; BAMIDBAR 32:8; DEVARIM 1:19"> And Moshe by the mouth of Hashem sent them from the Midbar Paran; all those men were Roshei Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And these were their shmot: of the tribe of Reuven, Shammua ben Zakur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Of the tribe of Shim'on, Shaphat ben Chori.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 34:19; BAMIDBAR 14:6; BAMIDBAR 13:30"> Of the tribe of Yehudah, Kalev ben Yephunneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Of the tribe of Yissakhar, Yigal ben Yosef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 24:13; BAMIDBAR 11:28; BAMIDBAR 13:16"> Of the tribe of Ephrayim, Hoshea ben Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Of the tribe of Binyamin, Palti ben Raphu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Of the tribe of Zevulun, Gaddiel ben Sodi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Of the tribe of Yosef, namely, of the tribe of Menasheh, Gaddi ben Susi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Of the tribe of Dan, Ammi'el ben Gemalli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Of the tribe of Asher, Setur ben Micha'el.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Of the tribe of Naphtali, Nachbi ben Vophsi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Of the tribe of Gad, Geuel ben Machi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 13:8; SHEMOT 17:9; BAMIDBAR 14:6"> These are the shmot of the men which Moshe sent to spy out the land. And Moshe called Hoshea ben Nun Yehoshua.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Zech 6:11-12on the personal name of Moshiach in prophecy">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHOFETIM 1:9; BERESHIS 12:9; SHOFETIM 1:19"> And Moshe sent them to spy out Eretz Kena'an, and said unto them, Get you up to the Negev, and go up into the mountain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 34:14; SHEMOT 3:8"> And see the land, what it is like, and the people that dwelleth therein, whether they be strong or weak, few or many;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And what the land is that they dwell in, whether it be good or bad; and what cities they be that they dwell in, whether in tents, or in strongholds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEVARIM 31:23; DEVARIM 31:6–8; YECHEZKEL 34:14"> And what the land is, whether it be fertile or barren, whether there be wood therein, or not. And be ye of good courage, and bring of the fruit of the land. Now the time was the time of the first ripe grapes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 20:1; BAMIDBAR 33:36; BAMIDBAR 27:14"> So they went up, and searched the land from the midbar of Tzin unto Rechov, as men come to Chamat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:12; TEHILLIM 78:43; YESHAYAH 19:11"> And they ascended by the south, and came unto Chevron; where Achiman, Sheshai, and Talmai, the Anak, were. (Now Chevron was built seven shanim before Tzoan in Mitzrayim.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 13:24; BAMIDBAR 32:9; DEVARIM 1:24–25"> And they came unto the Wadi Eshcol, and cut down from thence a branch with cluster of anavim echad, and they bore it between two upon a staff; and they brought of the pomegranates, and of the figs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The place was called the Wadi Eshcol, because of the cluster of grapes which the Bnei Yisroel cut down from thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHEMOT 34:28; SHEMOT 24:18; BAMIDBAR 14:33–34"> And they returned from searching of the land after arba'im days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:8; BAMIDBAR 20:1; DEVARIM 1:19"> And they went and came to Moshe, and to Aharon, and to all the congregation of the Bnei Yisroel, unto the Midbar Paran, to Kadesh; and brought back word unto them, and unto Kol HaEdah, and showed them the pri ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:8; SHEMOT 3:17; SHEMOT 13:5"> And they told him, and said, We came unto ha'aretz where thou didst send us, and truly it floweth with cholov and devash; and this is the fruit of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEVARIM 9:1–2; DEVARIM 1:28; BAMIDBAR 13:33"> Nevertheless the people are strong that dwell in the land, and the cities are walled, and gedolot me'od; and moreover we saw the Anak there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:43; SHOFETIM 6:3; BERESHIS 15:19–21"> The Amalek dwell in the eretz of the Negev; and the Chitti, and the Yevusi, and the Emori, dwell in the hills; and the Kena'ani dwell by the yam, and by the bank of the Yarden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 14:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:31; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:13"> And Kalev silenced HaAm before Moshe, and said, Let us go up at once, and take possession of it; for we are well able to overcome it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:28; YEHOSHUA 14:8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:19"> But the anashim that went up with him said, We are not able to go up against the people; for they are chazak (stronger) than we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="AMOS 2:9; BAMIDBAR 14:36–37; YECHEZKEL 36:13–14"> And they spread an evil report of HaAretz which they had explored unto the Bnei Yisroel, saying, HaAretz, through which we have gone to explore it, is a land that eateth up the inhabitants thereof; and all the people that we saw in it are men of a great stature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:28; YESHAYAH 40:22; BERESHIS 6:4"> And there we saw the Nephilim, the Bnei Anak, which come of the Nephilim; and we were in our own sight as grasshoppers, and so we were in their sight.</span>
</div>
