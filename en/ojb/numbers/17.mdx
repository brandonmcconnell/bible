<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:17; YECHEZKEL 19:14; BERESHIS 49:10"> Speak unto El'azar ben Aharon HaKohen that he take up the fire pans out of the burning, and scatter thou the eish yonder; for they are set apart as kodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 6:20; BAMIDBAR 18:1; BAMIDBAR 3:2–3"> The fire pans of these chatta'im against their own nefashot, let them make them hammered-out sheets for a covering of the Mizbe'ach: for they offered them before Hashem, therefore they became kadosh; and they shall be an ot unto the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHEMOT 30:36; SHEMOT 29:42–43; SHEMOT 25:16–22"> And El'azar HaKohen took the bronze fire pans, wherewith they that were burned had offered; and they made hammered-out sheets for an overlay of the Mizbe'ach:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 16:5; BAMIDBAR 17:8; BAMIDBAR 16:11"> To be a memorial reminder unto the Bnei Yisroel, that no zar, which is not of the zera Aharon, encroach to offer ketoret before Hashem; that he be not as Korach, and as his edah, as Hashem said to him by the hand of Moshe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But the next day Kol Adat Bnei Yisroel murmured against Moshe and against Aharon, saying, Ye have killed the Am Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:44; SHEMOT 38:21; BAMIDBAR 18:2"> And it came to pass, when HaEdah was gathered against Moshe and against Aharon, that they looked toward the Ohel Mo'ed; and, hinei, the Anan covered it, and the Kavod Hashem appeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 17:24; YOCHANAN 15:1–6; TEHILLIM 132:17–18"> And Moshe and Aharon came before the Ohel Mo'ed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:6; YESHAYAH 1:2"> Get you away from among this edah, that I may consume them as in a moment. And they fell upon their faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Moshe said unto Aharon, Take a fire pan, and put eish therein from off the Mizbe'ach, and put ketoret, and go quickly unto HaEdah, and make kapporah for them; for there is Ketzef (Wrath) gone out from Hashem; the plague is begun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:11; YESHAYAH 57:16; YESHAYAH 6:5"> And Aharon took as Moshe commanded, and ran into the midst of the Kahal; and, hinei, the plague was begun among HaAm: and he offered ketoret, and made kapporah for HaAm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:19–22; SHMUEL BAIS 6:6–12; BERESHIS 3:3"> And he stood between the mesim and the chayyim; and the plague was stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Now they that were the mesim in the maggefah (plague) were fourteen thousand and seven hundred, in addition to those who were mesim by reason of Korach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Aharon returned unto Moshe unto the entrance of the Ohel Mo'ed; and the plague was stayed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Speak unto the Bnei Yisroel, and take of every one of them a rod according to the Bais Av, of all their Nasiim according to the Bais of their Avot twelve rods; write thou every manʼs shem upon his matteh (rod, staff).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And thou shalt write shem Aharon upon the rod of Levi; for one rod shall be for the Rosh of the Bais of their Avot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And thou shalt lay them up in the Ohel Mo'ed before HaEdut, where I will meet with you there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And it shall come to pass, that the manʼs rod, whom I shall choose, shall sprout; and I will rid Myself of the murmurings of the Bnei Yisroel, whereby they murmur against you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Moshe spoke unto the Bnei Yisroel, and every one of their Nasiim gave him a rod apiece, for each nasi one, according to their Bais Avot, even twelve rods; and the rod of Aharon was among their rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Moshe laid up the rods before Hashem in the Ohel HaEdut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And it came to pass, that the next day Moshe entered into the Ohel HaEdut; and, hinei, the rod of Aharon for the Bais Levi was budded, and brought forth buds, and bloomed blossoms, and yielded almonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And Moshe brought out all the rods from before Hashem unto Kol Bnei Yisroel; and they looked, and took every man his rod.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, Bring Aharonʼs rod again before HaEdut, to be kept for an ot (sign) against the rebels; and thou shalt quite put an end to their murmurings against Me, that they die not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Moshe did so; as Hashem commanded him, so did he.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And the Bnei Yisroel spoke unto Moshe, saying, See, we die, we perish, we will all perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Whoever even encroaches upon the Mishkan Hashem shall die. Shall we be consumed with dying?</span>
</div>
</div>
