<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Hashem spoke unto Moshe, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="VAYIKRA 3:11; BERESHIS 8:21; BAMIDBAR 9:2–3"> Command the Bnei Yisroel, and say unto them, My korban, and My lechem by My fires, My re'ach hannichoach, shall ye be shomer to offer unto Me in its mo'ed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="VAYIKRA 6:9; DANIEL 11:31; YOCHANAN 1:29"> And thou shalt say unto them, This is the fire-offering which ye shall offer unto Hashem; two kevasim (male lambs) of the first year temimim, two a day, for an olah tamid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DANIEL 9:21; MELACHIM ALEF 18:36; SHEMOT 12:6"> The one keves shalt thou offer in the boker, and the other keves shalt thou offer towards erev;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="VAYIKRA 2:1; SHEMOT 16:36; SHEMOT 29:38–42"> And a tenth-ephah of fine flour for a minchah, mixed with the fourth-hin of pressed shemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="AMOS 5:25; VAYIKRA 6:9; TEHILLIM 50:8"> It is an olat tamid (continual burnt offering) which was ordained in Mt Sinai for a re'ach hannichoach, a fire-offering unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 29:42; BAMIDBAR 28:14; YESHAYAH 57:6"> And the nesekh (libation offering) thereof shall be the fourth-hin for the one keves: baKodesh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., the Mizbe'ach">a</a></sup>shalt thou cause the nesekh shechar to be poured out unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the other keves (male lamb) shalt thou offer at erev: like the minchah of the boker, and like the nesekh thereof, thou shalt offer it, a fire-offering of re'ach hannicho'ach unto Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YESHAYAH 58:13; TEHILLIM 92:1–4; HISGALUS 1:10"> And on Yom HaShabbos two kevasim of the first year temimim (unblemished ones) and two tenths-ephah of fine flour for a minchah, mixed with shemen, and the nesekh thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 46:4–5; BAMIDBAR 29:22; BAMIDBAR 29:31"> This is the olah (burnt offering) of every Shabbos, in addition to the continual olah, and its nesekh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 10:10; YECHEZKEL 46:6; EZRA 3:5"> And at each Rosh-Chodesh of yours, ye shall offer an olah unto Hashem; two young bulls, and one ram, shivah kevasim (male lambs) of the first year temimim (unblemished ones);</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 15:4–12; YECHEZKEL 46:5–7; BAMIDBAR 29:10"> And three tenths-ephah of fine flour for a minchah, mixed with shemen, for each bull; and two tenths-ephah of fine flour for a minchah, mixed with shemen for the one ram;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And a tenth-ephah of fine flour mixed with shemen for a minchah for each keves; for an olah (burnt offering) of a re'ach hannichoach, a fire-offering unto Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And their nesakhim shall be a half-hin for each bull, and the third-hin for the ram, and a quarter-hin for each keves: this is the olah (burnt offering) for every Rosh Chodesh throughout the months of the shanah (year).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 28:3; BAMIDBAR 28:22; BAMIDBAR 15:24"> And one young male goat for a chattat unto Hashem shall be offered. It shall be in addition to the continual olah (burnt offering), and its nesekh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:1–8; SHEMOT 12:18; VAYIKRA 23:5–8"> And in the fourteenth day of the chodesh harishon (first month i.e., Nisan) is the Pesach to Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:6; SHEMOT 23:15; SHEMOT 13:6"> And in the fifteenth day of this month is a Chag:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., Chag HaMatzot">b</a></sup>shivat yamim shall matzot be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:16; VAYIKRA 23:7–8"> In the yom harishon shall be a mikra kodesh (holy convocation); ye shall do no manner of melekhet avodah therein;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 15:21; YECHEZKEL 45:21–25; BAMIDBAR 29:8"> But ye shall offer a fire-offering for an olah unto Hashem; two young bulls, and one ram, and shivah kevasim of the first year; they shall be unto you temimim (unblemished ones).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And their minchah shall be of fine flour mixed with shemen: three tenths-ephah shall ye offer for each bull, and two tenths-ephah for the ram;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> One tenth-ephah shalt thou offer for each keves, of the shivat hakevasim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:3"> And one goat for a chattat, to make kapporah for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 28:3; BAMIDBAR 28:10"> Ye shall offer these in addition to the olah (burnt offering) of the boker, which is for the olat hatamid (continual burnt offering).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> After this manner ye shall offer daily, throughout the shivat yamim (seven days):<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e., the seven days of the Chag HaMatzot">c</a></sup>lechem, a fire-offering, reiach hannichoach unto Hashem: it shall be offered in addition to the olat hatamid (the continual burnt offering), and its nesekh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:8; BAMIDBAR 28:18; SHEMOT 13:6"> And on the yom hashevi'i ye shall have a mikra kodesh; ye shall do no melekhet avodah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHEMOT 23:16; SHEMOT 34:22; VAYIKRA 23:10"> Also in the Yom HaBikkurim, when ye bring a minchah chadashah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="i.e., meal offering or grain offering from the new grain crop">d</a></sup>unto Hashem, on your Shavu'os, ye shall have a mikra kodesh; ye shall do no melekhet avodah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="VAYIKRA 23:18–19; BAMIDBAR 28:11; BAMIDBAR 28:19"> But ye shall offer the olah (burnt offering) for a reach hannichoach unto Hashem; two young bulls, one ram, shivah kevasim of the first year;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And their minchah of fine flour mixed with shemen, three tenths-ephah for each bull, two tenths-ephah for the ayil haechad (one ram).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> One tenth-ephah unto one keves for each of the shivat hakevasim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 28:15; BAMIDBAR 28:22; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:24"> And one male goat, to make kapporah for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 28:19; BAMIDBAR 28:3; MALACHI 1:13–14"> Ye shall offer them in addition to the olat hatamid and its minchah, (they shall be unto you tamimim, unblemished ones) and their nesakhim.</span>
</div>
