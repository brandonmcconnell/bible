<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:5; BAMIDBAR 16:35; MELACHIM BAIS 1:12"> And when the people complained, it displeased Hashem; Hashem heard it; His anger was kindled; and the Eish Hashem burned among them, and consumed them that were at the edge of the machaneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:7; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:16; BAMIDBAR 16:45–48"> And the people cried unto Moshe; and when Moshe davened unto Hashem, the eish was quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEVARIM 9:22"> He called shem hamakom Taverah because the Eish Hashem burned among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:6; SHEMOT 12:38; TEHILLIM 106:14"> And the rabble that was among them fell a-lusting; and the Bnei Yisroel also wept again, and said, If only we had basar to eat!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:3; TEHILLIM 17:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:19"> We remember the fish, which we did eat in Mitzrayim at no cost; the cucumbers, and the melons, and the leeks, and the onions, and the garlic;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:5; SHMUEL BAIS 13:4"> But now our nefesh is dried up; there is nothing at all, besides this manna, before our eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:31; BERESHIS 2:12; SHEMOT 16:14–15"> And the manna was like coriander seed, and the color thereof like the color of white gum resin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:31; SHEMOT 16:16–18; SHEMOT 16:23"> And the people went about and gathered it, and ground it in handmills, or crushed it in the mortar, and baked it in pans, and made cakes of it; and the taste of it was like the taste of cakes prepared of shemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:13–14; TEHILLIM 78:23–25; DEVARIM 32:2"> And when the dew fell upon the machaneh in the lailah, the manna fell with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:21; BAMIDBAR 16:27; BAMIDBAR 14:1–2"> Then Moshe heard the people wailing throughout their mishpekhot, every man in the entrance of his ohel; and the anger of Hashem was kindled greatly; Moshe also was displeased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:12; SHEMOT 5:22; YIRMEYAH 15:10"> And Moshe said unto Hashem, Why hast Thou afflicted Thy eved? And why have I not found chen (favor) in Thy sight, that Thou layest the massa (burden) of all this people upon me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 49:23; YESHAYAH 40:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:7"> Have I conceived all this people? Have I fathered them, that Thou shouldest say unto me, Carry them in thy bosom, as the one nursing beareth the nursing infant, unto the land which Thou didst swear unto their avot?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:33; MARKOS 8:4; YOCHANAN 6:5–9"> Where should I get basar to give unto all this people? For they wail unto me, saying, Give us basar, that we may eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHEMOT 18:18; DEVARIM 1:9–12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:16"> I am not able to carry all this people alone, because it is too heavy for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:20–24; MELACHIM ALEF 19:4; YONAH 4:3"> And if Thou deal thus with me, kill me, please, at once, if I have found chen in Thy sight; and let me not see my wretchedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHEMOT 24:1; SHEMOT 24:9; DEVARIM 16:18"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, Gather unto Me seventy men of the Zekenim of Yisroel, whom thou knowest to be the Ziknei HaAm, and officers over them; and bring them unto the Ohel Mo'ed, that they may stand there with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 11:25; SHMUEL ALEF 10:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:4–11"> And I will come down and speak with thee there; and I will take of the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hakodesh">a</a></sup>that is upon thee, and will put<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Him">b</a></sup>upon them; and they shall bear the massa HaAm (burden of the People) with thee, that thou bear it not thyself alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:39; SHEMOT 16:3–7"> And say thou unto the people, Set yourselves apart as kodesh against tomorrow, and ye shall eat basar; for ye have wept in the ears of Hashem, saying, Who shall give us basar to eat? For it was well with us in Mitzrayim; therefore Hashem will give you basar, and ye shall eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Lo (not) ye shall eat one day, nor two days, nor five days, neither ten days, nor twenty days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 106:15; BAMIDBAR 21:5; TEHILLIM 78:27–30"> But even a whole month, until it come out at your nostrils, and it be loathsome unto you; because that meastem (ye have rejected)Hashem Who is among you, and have wept before Him, saying, Why came we forth out of Mitzrayim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:37; BAMIDBAR 1:46; BERESHIS 12:2"> And Moshe said, The people, among whom I am, are 600,000 men on foot; and Thou hast said, I will give them basar, that they may eat a whole month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:33; MELACHIM BAIS 7:2; YOCHANAN 6:9"> Shall the flocks and the herds be slain for them, to suffice them? Or shall all the dag (fish) of the yam be gathered together for them, to suffice them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YESHAYAH 59:1; YESHAYAH 50:2; BERESHIS 18:14"> And Hashem said unto Moshe, Is the Yad Hashem cut short?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e., limited in power">c</a></sup>Thou shalt see now whether My Devar shall come to pass unto thee or not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 11:16; BAMIDBAR 11:26"> And Moshe went out, and told HaAm the divrei Hashem, and gathered the seventy men of the Ziknei HaAm, and set them round about the Ohel &#91;Moed&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 11:17; YOEL 2:28–29; SHMUEL ALEF 10:10"> And Hashem came down in an Anan, and spoke unto him, and took of the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hakodesh">d</a></sup>that was upon him, and gave<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Him">e</a></sup>unto the seventy Zekenim; and it came to pass, that, when the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hakodesh">f</a></sup>rested upon them, they prophesied, but did not continue.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See 1Sm 10:6; 19:24; Ac 19:6">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 36:5; SHMUEL ALEF 20:26; SHMUEL ALEF 10:22"> But there remained two of the anashim in the machaneh, the shem of the one was Eldad, and the shem of the other Medad: and the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hakodesh">h</a></sup>rested upon them; and they were of them that were listed, but went not out unto the Ohel &#91;Moed&#93;; and they prophesied in the machaneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And there ran a young man, and told Moshe, and said, Eldad and Medad do prophesy in the machaneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MARKOS 9:38–40; LUKAS 9:49–50; YOCHANAN 3:26"> And Yehoshua Ben Nun, the mesharet Moshe, one of his chosen young men, answered and said, Moshe adoni, forbid them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:5; MATTITYAHU 9:37–38; LUKAS 10:2"> And Moshe said unto him, Are you jealous for my sake? If only kol Am Hashem were neviim, and that Hashem would put His Ruach upon them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="See Yoel 2:27[3:1]">i</a></sup>!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Moshe went back to the machaneh, he and the Ziknei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHEMOT 16:13; TEHILLIM 105:40; TEHILLIM 78:26–29"> And there went forth a wind from Hashem, it drove in quail from the yam, brought them down over the machaneh at a height of two cubits from the ground for the distance of a dayʼs journey all around the machaneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 45:11; SHEMOT 16:36"> And the people stood up all that yom, and kol halailah, and all the next day, and they gathered in the quail; he that gathered least gathered ten chomers; and they spread them all out for themselves round about the machaneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="TEHILLIM 78:30–31; TEHILLIM 106:14–15; BAMIDBAR 25:9"> And while the basar was yet between their teeth, before it was chewed, the wrath of Hashem was kindled against the people, and Hashem struck down the people with a makkah rabbah me'od (a very great plague).</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DEVARIM 9:22; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:6; BAMIDBAR 33:16"> And the shem of that place was called Kivrot-Hata'avah (graves of greed) because there they buried the people that lusted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 33:17; BAMIDBAR 12:16; DEVARIM 1:1"> And the people set out from Kivrot-Hata'avah unto Chatzerot; and abode at Chatzerot.</span>
