## &#91;BALAK&#93;

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="SHOFETIM 11:25; BAMIDBAR 21:20–35; BAMIDBAR 21:3"> And Balak ben Tzippor saw all that Yisroel had done to the Emori.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHEMOT 15:15; YESHAYAH 23:5; YEHOSHUA 9:24"> And Moav was exceedingly afraid of the people, because they were many; and Moav was filled with dread because of the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 25:15–18; BAMIDBAR 31:8; BAMIDBAR 24:17"> And Moav said unto the Ziknei Midyan, Now shall this kahal lick up all that are round about us, as the ox licketh up the grass of the sadeh. And Balak ben Tzippor was Melech of Moav at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEVARIM 23:4; YEHOSHUA 24:9; MICHOH 6:5"> He sent malachim (messengers) therefore unto Balaam ben Be'or to Petor, which is by the River<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Euphrates">a</a></sup>in his native land, to summon him, saying, Hinei, there is a people come out from Mitzrayim; hinei, they cover the face of ha'aretz, and they are settling next to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 24:9; BAMIDBAR 23:7–8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:16"> Come now, therefore, please, curse for me this people; for they are too mighty for me; perhaps I shall prevail, that we may strike them, and that I may drive them out of ha'aretz; for I know that he whom thou blessest is m'vorach (blessed), and he whom thou cursest is cursed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 9:7–8; MICHOH 3:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:18"> And the Ziknei Moav and the Ziknei Midyan departed with the divination remuneration in their hand; and they came unto Balaam, and spoke unto him the words of Balak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 23:12; BAMIDBAR 22:19–20; YIRMEYAH 12:2"> And he said unto them, Lodge here this night, and I will bring you word again, as Hashem shall speak unto me; and the sarim of Moav abode with Balaam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 20:3; BAMIDBAR 22:20; BERESHIS 16:8"> And Elohim came unto Balaam, and said, What men are these with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Balaam said unto HaElohim, Balak ben Tzippor, Melech Moav, hath sent unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Hinei, there is a people come out of Mitzrayim, which covereth the face of ha'aretz; come now, curse for me them; perhaps I shall be able to overcome them, and drive them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEVARIM 23:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:3; BERESHIS 12:2"> And Elohim said unto Balaam, Thou shalt not go with them; thou shalt not curse the people; for baruch hu (it &#91;the people&#93; is blessed).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEVARIM 23:5; BAMIDBAR 22:14"> And Balaam rose up in the boker, and said unto the sarim of Balak, Go back to your land; for Hashem refuseth to give me permission to go with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 22:37; BAMIDBAR 22:13"> And the sarim of Moav rose up, and they went unto Balak, and said, Balaam refuseth to come with us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:7–8; BAMIDBAR 22:7–8"> And Balak sent yet again sarim, more, and more distinguished than they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And they came to Balaam, and said to him, Thus saith Balak ben Tzippor, Let nothing, please, hinder thee from coming unto me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 24:11; ESTHER 5:11; MATTITYAHU 4:8–9"> For I will exceedingly reward thee, and I will do whatsoever thou sayest unto me; come, therefore, please, curse for me this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 24:13; MELACHIM ALEF 22:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 18:13"> And Balaam answered and said unto the avadei Balak, If Balak would give me his palace full of kesef and zahav, I cannot go beyond the Devar Hashem Elohai, to do ketanah or gedolah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:11; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:3; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:9–10"> Now, therefore, please, tarry ye also here this night, that I may have da'as of what more Hashem will say unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 23:12; BAMIDBAR 24:13; BAMIDBAR 23:26"> And it was lailah when Elohim came unto Balaam, and said unto him, Since the men come to summon thee, rise up and go with them; but only the word which I shall say unto thee, that shalt thou do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MISHLE 1:15–16"> And Balaam rose up in the boker, and saddled his donkey, and went with the sarim (princes) of Moav.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:24; EKHAH 2:4; BAMIDBAR 22:32"> But the Af Elohim (G-dʼs anger) was kindled as he was going; and the Malach Hashem stood in the derech (road) l'satan (for an adversary, opposer) against him. Now he was riding upon his donkey, and his two servants were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:11; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:27–29"> And the donkey saw the Malach Hashem standing in the derech, and his cherev drawn in his hand; and the donkey turned off the derech, and went into the sadeh; and Balaam beat the donkey, to turn her back onto the derech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> But the Malach Hashem stood in the closed-in path among the kramim (vineyards), a wall being on this side, a wall on that side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YESHAYAH 47:12; IYOV 5:13–15"> And when the donkey saw the Malach Hashem, she thrust herself unto the wall, and crushed Balaamʼs regel against the wall; and he beat her again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="HOSHEA 2:6; YESHAYAH 26:11"> And the Malach Hashem went further, and stood in a narrow place, where was no derech to turn either to the right or to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MISHLE 14:16; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19; MISHLE 27:3–4"> And when the donkey saw the Malach Hashem, she lay down under Balaam; and af Balaam was kindled, and he beat the donkey with his staff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:16; LUKAS 1:37; SHEMOT 4:11"> And Hashem opened the mouth of the donkey, and she said unto Balaam, What have I done unto thee, that thou hast beaten me these shalosh regalim (three times)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MISHLE 12:10; MATTITYAHU 15:19; MISHLE 12:16"> And Balaam answered the donkey, Because thou hast mocked me; If only there were a cherev in mine hand, would I have killed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:27–28"> And the donkey said unto Balaam, Am not I thine donkey, upon which thou hast ridden ever since I was thine unto this day? Was I ever accustomed to do so unto thee? And he said, Loh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="BERESHIS 21:19; LUKAS 24:31; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:18"> Then Hashem opened the eyes of Balaam, and he saw the Malach Hashem standing in the derech, and his cherev drawn in his hand; and he bowed down his head, and fell facedown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MISHLE 28:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:10; BAMIDBAR 22:22"> And the Malach Hashem said unto him, Why hast thou beaten thine donkey these shalosh regalim? Hinei, I came l'satan (as adversary, opposer) to thee, because thy derech is perverse before me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 16:33–35; BAMIDBAR 14:37; MELACHIM ALEF 13:24–28"> And the donkey saw me, and turned from me these shalosh regalim; had she not turned from me, surely now also I would have slain thee, and saved her alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="IYOV 34:31–32; SHMUEL BAIS 12:13; SHMUEL ALEF 15:24"> And Balaam said unto the Malach Hashem, Chatati (I have sinned); for I was without da'as that thou stoodest in the derech against me; now, therefore, if it displease thee, I will return home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:9–12; YESHAYAH 37:26–29; BAMIDBAR 22:20–21"> And the Malach Hashem said unto Balaam, Go with the anashim; but only the devar (message) that I shall speak unto thee, that thou shalt speak. So Balaam went with the sarim (princes) of Balak.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YESHAYAH 16:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:15; YIRMEYAH 48:20"> And when Balak heard that Balaam was come, he went out to meet him unto the city of Moav at the Arnon border, which is in the farthest point of the boundary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 24:11; YOCHANAN 5:44; MATTITYAHU 4:8–9"> And Balak said unto Balaam, Did I not earnestly send unto thee to summon thee? Why camest thou not unto me? Am I not able indeed to honor thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 22:18; MISHLE 19:21; BAMIDBAR 23:16"> And Balaam said unto Balak, Hinei, I am come unto thee; have I now any power at all to say anything? The devar (word, message) which Elohim putteth in my mouth, that shall I speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And Balaam went with Balak, and they came unto Kiryat Chutzot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 23:14; MISHLE 1:16; BAMIDBAR 23:2"> And Balak sacrificed oxen and sheep, and sent to Balaam, and to the sarim that were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 23:13; BAMIDBAR 25:2–3; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:15"> And it came to pass on the next day, that Balak took Balaam, and brought him up on the high places of Ba'al, that thence he might see a portion of HaAm.</span>
</div>
