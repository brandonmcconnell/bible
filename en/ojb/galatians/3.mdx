<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:7–8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:6"> O senseless Galatians, who bewitched you? It was before your eyes that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua was publicly shown forth as having been TALUI AL HAETZ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Dt 21:23">a</a></sup>(hanging on the tree).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:16–17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:8"> This one thing only I want to learn from you: did you receive the Ruach HaKodesh by means of ma'asim of Gezetz (works of Law) or by means of the hearing of emunah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:4–8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:7–10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:16–19"> You lack seichel; having begun in the Ruach HaKodesh, will you now be perfected in the basar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:32–39"> Did you suffer so many things lashav (in vain)? — if it really was lashav.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:9–10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:11–12"> So then, Hashem who is supplying to you the Ruach HaKodesh and producing nifla'ot (miracles) among you, by what means does He do it, by ma'asim of Gezetz or by the hearing of emunah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:21–22; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:23"> — Just as Avraham Avinu HE'EMIN B'HASHEM V'YACHSHEVEHA LO TZEDAKAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gn 15:6">b</a></sup>(believed Hashem and it &#91;his faith&#93; was accounted to him for righteousness).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:7–8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:26–29"> Know then, that the Bnei HaEmunah, these ones are the Bnei Avraham Avinu!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:3; BERESHIS 22:18; BERESHIS 28:14"> And the Kitvei HaKodesh, having foreseen that Hashem would yatzdik (justify) the Goyim by emunah, preached the Besuras HaGeulah beforehand to Avraham Avinu, saying, V'NIVRECHU VO KOL GOYEI HA'ARETZ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gn 18:18; 12:3">c</a></sup>(All the peoples of the earth will be blessed in you).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:24"> For this reason, the ones of emunah receive the bracha (blessing) with Avraham Avinu hama'amin (the believer).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEVARIM 27:26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:19–20"> For as many as are seeking “YITZDAK IM HASHEM” by ma'asim of Gezetz are under a kelalah (curse); for it has been written, ARUR ASHER LO YAKIM ES DIVREI HATORAH HAZOT LA'ASOT OTAM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Dt 27:26; cf Ro 2:10">d</a></sup>(Cursed is everyone who does not uphold, abide by all the words of this Torah to do them).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="CHABAKUK 2:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:38"> Now it is clear that not one person is YITZDAK IM HASHEM (justified with G-d) by Gezetz, because V'TZADDIK BE'EMUNATO YICHEYEH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hab 2:4">e</a></sup>(the righteous by his faith will live).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:5–6; NECHEMYAH 9:29"> But the Gezetz is not of emunah, but the man YA'ASEH OTAM VACHAI<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Lv 18:5">f</a></sup>(who does these things will live &#91;by them&#93;).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:24; DEVARIM 21:23; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:12"> Moshiach redeemed us from the kelalah (curse) of the Torah, having become a kelalah (curse) on behalf of us, because it has been written, KILELAT HASHEM is on NIVLATO TALUI AL HAETZ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Dt 21:23">g</a></sup>(Curse of G-d is on the NEVELAH (body, corpse) being hanged on the tree),</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:28–29; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:13; YECHEZKEL 36:26–27"> In order that to the Goyim the Bracha of Avraham Avinu might come by Moshiach Yehoshua, that the havtachah (promise) of the Ruach haKodesh we might receive through emunah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:5"> Achim B'Moshiach, I speak according to human dimyon (analogy). Even a brit (covenant) having been confirmed by Bnei Adam no one sets aside or adds to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 17:7–8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:13; LUKAS 1:55"> Now to Avraham Avinu were spoken the havtachot (promises) and to his ZERA<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gn 22:18">h</a></sup>(seed). He does not say V'LIZERAECHAH (and to your seeds), as concerning many, but as concerning one, “and to the ZERA of you,” and that ZERA is Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:39–40; BERESHIS 15:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:13–14"> And this I say: a brit (covenant), which was previously confirmed by Hashem, cannot be annulled so as to abolish the havtachah (promise) by the Mattan Torah — which was given arba me'ot usheloshim shanah (four hundred and thirty years) later.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ex 12:40">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:12–15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:13–16"> For if the nachalah (inheritance) is based on Gezetz, it is no longer based on havtachah (promise); but Hashem has given the nachalah to Avraham Avinu by havtachah (promise).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:53; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:7–13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:2"> Why then the Mattan Torah (Giving of the Torah)? The Torah was added because of peysha'im, until the ZERA (Moshiach) should come to whom the havtachah had been made.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gn 22:18">j</a></sup>Now the Torah was administered through malachim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Dt 33:2; Ps 68:18">k</a></sup>by the hand of a metavech.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Lv 26:46; Num 36:13">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:5; DEVARIM 6:4; IYOV 9:33"> Now the metavech is not for only one, but Elohim hu echad.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Dt 6:4">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:31; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:20–22"> Is the Torah, mimeila (consequently, as a result), against the havtachot (promises) of Hashem? Chas v'Shalom (G-d forbid)! For if Gezetz had been given that had the ko'ach (power) to affect hitkhadshut (regeneration), then to be YITZDAK IM HASHEM (justified with G-d) would indeed have been based on ma'asim of Gezetz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:32; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:23"> But the Kitvei HaKodesh consigned all things under HaChet (Sin),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Dt 27:26">n</a></sup>that the havtachah (promise) might be given by emunah in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua to the ma'aminim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:24–25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:19"> But before Emunah came, we were being held in custody, being confined and guarded for the about-to-be-revealed Emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:38–39; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:24–25"> This is the result: the Gezetz functioned as our omenet (governess) unto Moshiach, that by emunah we might be YITZDAK IM HASHEM.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:15–18"> But Emunah having come, we are no longer under an omenet (governess).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:5; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:18; YOCHANAN 1:12–13"> For through emunah in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, you are all yeladim of Elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:3–4"> For as many as have had a tevilah into Moshiach have clothed yourselves with Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:12–13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:29–30"> There is not Yehudi nor Yevani (Greek), there is not eved (servant) nor Ben Chorin (freedman), there is not zachar (male) nor nekevah (female), for you are all echad in Moshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:7–8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:7"> And, if you belong to Moshiach,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Isa 53:10">o</a></sup>then you are of the ZERA of Avraham Avinu, you are yoreshim (heirs) according to the havtachah (promise).</span>
</div>
