<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:29; BERESHIS 24:2–3"> Now I say this: for however much time as the yoresh (heir) has not attained his majority (the state or time of being of full legal age, or his religious majority, his Bar Mitzvah), he differs nothing from an eved, though being Ba'al Bayit of all the nachalah (inheritance).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And he is under shomrim (guardians) and omnot (governesses) until the time previously appointed by the Ba'al Bayit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:9"> So also we, when we were immature, had been enslaved under the yesodot (rudiments) of the Olam Hazeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YESHAYAH 7:14; YOCHANAN 1:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:3"> But when the fullness of time had come, Hashem sent forth his Zun fun der Oybershter,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Moshiach, 2Sm 7:14; Ps 2:7; 89:27f">a</a></sup>born of an isha,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gn 3:15; Isa 7:14; Mic 5:2">b</a></sup>born under Gezetz (Law),</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:5; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:18–20"> That Moshiach might bring the Geulah (Redemption) to the ones under Gezetz (Law), that we might receive the Ma'amad HaBanim (the standing as sons), the bechirah adoption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:15–17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:9"> And because you are banim, Hashem sent forth the Ruach of His Zun fun der Oybershter into your levavot, crying “Abba, Avinu!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:16–17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:29–2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:26"> So you are no longer an eved but a ben; and if a ben, also a yoresh through Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:11–12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:8"> But, formerly, when you did not have da'as (knowledge) of Hashem, you were avadim serving that which is by nature not HaEl Ha'Amiti (the true G-d).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:3"> But, now, having known Hashem, or rather having been known by Hashem, how is it that you are returning to the weak and beggarly yesodot (rudiments) of the Olam Hazeh to which again you want to renew your service as avadim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:16–17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:5; VAYIKRA 23:1–44"> You<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Galatian Goyim">c</a></sup>observe yamim (days) and chodashim (months, new moons) and mo'adim (fixed times, festivals) and shanim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:2"> I fear for you, lest somehow efsher (perhaps) I have labored for you lashav (in vain).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:5; MELACHIM ALEF 22:4; BERESHIS 34:15"> Become as I am, because I also became as you are, Achim B'Moshiach. I implore you. You did me no wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:7–10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:30"> And you know that it was due to chulshat habasar (weakness of the flesh, sickness) that I first preached the Besuras HaGeulah to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:40; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:2"> and your nisayon (trial) in my basar you did not despise nor did you loathe, but as a malach Hashem you received me, as Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:2; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16–18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:13"> Where then is your birkat Shomayim? For I testify to you that if possible, having torn out your eynayeem (eyes), you would have made a mattanah (gift) of them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="AMOS 5:10; MISHLE 9:8; YOCHANAN 8:45"> So, then, have I become your oyev (enemy) by telling you HaEmes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:18; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:13–15; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:3"> They (the mohalim haGoyim) are zealously courting you, but not in a good way; rather, they desire to cut you off and shut you out, in order that you may be zealous for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:12; HISGALUS 3:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:27"> Now it is tov ma'od to be zealous in a good thing all the time, and not only during my presence with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:14"> My yeladim, for whom again I suffer chevlei leydah (birth pains) until Moshiach is formed in you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:17–18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:19–21; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:9"> Would that I were present with you just now and could change my tone, because I am baffled by you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:23–24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:5–6"> Tell me, you Goyim who wish to be under Gezetz, do you not possess “shema” hearing of the Torah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BERESHIS 16:15; BERESHIS 21:10; BERESHIS 21:1–2"> For the Torah says that Avraham Avinu had shnei banim (two sons), one of shifchah (the slave woman) and one of the gevirah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:7–8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:28–29"> But the one of the slave woman has been born according to the basar, and the one of the free woman has been born through the havtachah (promise).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="BERESHIS 21:9–13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:15–18; BERESHIS 16:8"> Now these things can be taken derech mashal (figuratively); for these are two beritot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="see Ga 3:17">d</a></sup>(covenants), one from Mount Sinai bearing banim for avdut (slavery, bondage): this is Hagar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKAS 19:44; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:17"> Now, Hagar is the Mount Sinai in Arabia; and corresponds to the Yerushalayim of the present, for she is in avdut with her banim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="see 2C 3:14">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:20; MICHOH 4:1–2"> But the Yerushalayim above is a Bat Chorin (daughter of freedom), the Imma lechulanu (the Mother of us all).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ps 87:5-6; Ex 25:40; Isa 49:20f; 54:1-13">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YESHAYAH 54:1–5; TEHILLIM 113:9; RUTH 4:14–16"> For it has been written, RANNI AKARAH LO YALADAH PITZCHI RINNAH V'TZAHALI LO CHALAH KI RABBIM BENEI SHOMEMAH MIB'NEI VE'ULAH (Sing, rejoice, O barren, the one not giving birth, break forth into song and shout for joy, the one not suffering birth pains; because more are the children of the desolate woman than the one having the husband).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 54:1">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:8–9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:13–18"> But you, Achim b'Moshiach, are Bnei HaHavtacha (Sons of the Promise), like Yitzchak.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gn 18:10">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BERESHIS 21:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:11"> But just as at that time the one born according to the basar was bringing redifah (persecution) on the one born according to the Ruach HaKodesh, so it is now also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="BERESHIS 21:10–12; YOCHANAN 8:35; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:15–17"> But what does the Kitvei HaKodesh say? “Cast out the slave woman and her son, for never will the son of the slave woman inherit with my son, the son of the free woman.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gn 21:10">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:36; YOCHANAN 1:12–13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:1"> Therefore, Achim B'Moshiach, we are not Bnei “HAAMAH” (Sons of the Slave Woman) but Bnei HaKhofshi'yah (Sons of Lady Freedom, the Freedwoman).</span>
