<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:20"> The one having made a matnat Elohim of himself, on behalf of chattoteynu (our sins), so that he might rescue us out of the Olam Hazeh &#91;Yom Tzarah&#93;, this age, this present evil age, according to the ratzon Hashem (will of G-d), even Avinu,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:36; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:25; TEHILLIM 72:19"> Lo hakavod l'olmei olamim. Omein.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:7–8; YESHAYAH 29:13"> I am shocked that so quickly you are being turned from the One who granted you the kri'ah (calling), summoning you by the Chen v'Chesed Hashem of Moshiach. I am shocked that so quickly you are being turned from this to a different “Besuras HaGeulah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:17–18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:10"> Not that there is another, mind you! Except that there are some individuals disturbing and troubling you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ga 5:12">a</a></sup>desiring to twist and pervert the Besuras HaGeulah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Isa 40:9; 60:6">b</a></sup>of Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:13–14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:9; HISGALUS 22:18–19"> But even if we<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Shlichim, emissaries of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">c</a></sup>or a malach from Shomayim should pose as maggidim &#91;for Moshiach&#93; and make a hachrazah (proclamation) to you of a “Besuras HaGeulah” other than that Besuras HaGeulah which we preached to you, let such a one be ARUR HAISH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Josh 6:26">d</a></sup>(Cursed is the man) and set under cherem (ban of destruction) and onesh Gehinnom (damnation).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:17; MISHLE 30:6; DEVARIM 4:2"> As we have previously said, and now again I say, if any one preaches a “Besuras HaGeulah” to you other than that which you received, let him be ARUR HAISH and be consigned to onesh Gehinnom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:6"> Am I now seeking the ishshur (approval) of Bnei Adam? Or the haskama (approval) of Hashem? Or am I seeking to be a man-pleaser? If &#91;and this is not the case&#93; I were still pleasing Bnei Adam, I would not have been the eved of Moshiach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:9–10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:23"> For I mefarsem (make known) to you, Achim B'Moshiach, the Besuras HaGeulah having been preached by me, that it is not according to Bnei Adam;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:1"> For neither did I receive it from Bnei Adam nor was I taught it, but no, it was through a chazon (revelation) of Moshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:3–5"> For you heard of my derech, my halakhah, my hitnahagut (conduct) in earlier times in Yahadut (Judaism), how I was to an extraordinary degree bringing redifah (persecution) upon the Kehillah of Hashem and was making havoc of it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:4–6"> And I was shtaig (working my way up, advancing, progressing) in Yahadut (Judaism) beyond many of my landsmen, being more abundantly machmir and a kannai (zealot) bekius for the minhagim, the Masorot haAvot, the kabbalah (oral tradition), the Torah Sheb'al Peh, the Halakhah of my Avot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 1:5; YESHAYAH 49:5; YESHAYAH 49:1"> But when Hashem was pleased, when it was the ratzon Hashem, Hashem being the One who separated me as kodesh KERA'ANI MIME'EI IMMI<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Isa 49:1">e</a></sup>(He called me from the womb of my mother) and granted me the kri'ah (calling), summoning me through the Chen v'Chesed of Hashem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:5–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:12"> To reveal His Zun fun der Oybershter in me, that I might preach Him among the Goyim, immediately, then, I did not consult with basar vadahm (flesh and blood),</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:20–25; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:32–33; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:18"> Nor did I go up to Yerushalayim to those who were Moshiachʼs Shlichim before me, but I went away hitbodedut into Arabia and again I returned to Damascus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:22–23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:26–29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:17–18"> Then, after shalosh shanim (three years),I went up to Yerushalayim to get acquainted with Kefa, and I stayed with him chamishah asar yamim (fifteen days).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:55; MARKOS 6:3; MATTITYAHU 12:46"> But other of the Moshiachʼs Shlichim I did not see except Ya'akov achi Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="T.N. Ya'akov and Sha'ul were both unbelievers before the appearance of the Moshiach to them after His histalkus — see 1C 15:7">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:1; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:31; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:10–11"> Now, what things I write to you, hinei, before Hashem I do not speak sheker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:41; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:30; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:9"> Next I went into the regions of Syria and of Cilicia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:7; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:1"> But I was unknown panim el panim by the Kehillot of Moshiach in Yehudah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:8–10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:13; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:13–16"> Only they were hearing that “the one once bringing redifah (persecution) upon us is now preaching the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Messianic Orthodox Jewish">g</a></sup>Emunah which once he was pillaging.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:18; LUKAS 15:10"> And they were glorifying Hashem in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="by means of">h</a></sup>me.</span>
