<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:31–32"> Hinei, I Sha'ul say to you, that if you Goyim undergo the bris milah, Moshiach will profit you nothing.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ga 3:12-14">a</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:7"> I have bitachon, (confidence) in you in Adoneinu that you will think nothing other, but the one troubling you will bear the judgment, whoever he may be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:29"> But if I preach &#91;to Goyim&#93; the bris milah, Achim B'Moshiach, why am I still being persecuted? <span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:19; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:9"> For, Achim B'Moshiach, you were called for “zman Cheruteinu”;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="5:1">g</a></sup>only use not the Cherut for a pretext for the basar, but, through ahavah (agape), minister to one another as avadim (servants).</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:24–25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:12–14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:8"> But I say, let your halakhah be by the Ruach HaKodesh, and by no means will you carry out the ta'avot (lusts) of the basar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:13; YOCHANAN 3:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:5–8"> For the basar desires against the Ruach HaKodesh, and the Ruach HaKodesh desires against the basar — for these oppose each other — with the result that the things you wish you cannot do.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="See Romans chapter 7">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:14–15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:25"> But if by the Ruach HaKodesh you are led, you are not under Gezetz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:9–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:5–8; MATTITYAHU 15:18–19"> Now the ma'asei habasar are manifest, dehainu (being): zenut (fornication), tum'ah (impurity), zimmah (licentiousness),</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:19; HISGALUS 21:8"> Avodah zarah (idolatry), kashefanut (sorcery, witchcraft), eivot (enmities), merivah (strife), kinah (jealousy), rogez (anger), anochiyut (selfishness), machalokot (dissensions), kitot (sects),</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:9–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:13"> Tzarut ayin (envyings), shichrut (drunkenness), holelut (carousing) and things like these, of which I tell you beforehand, as I said previously, that the ones practicing such things will not receive the nachalah (inheritance) of the Malchut Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:12–17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:4–7; YOCHANAN 15:5"> But the p'ri of the Ruach HaKodesh is ahavah (agape), simcha (joy), shalom (peace), savlanut (patience), nedivut (generosity, kindness), chesed (loving-kindness), ne'emanut (faithfulness),</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:25; LETTER TO TITOS 2:2"> Anavah (meekness, shiflut, lowliness), shlitah atzmi (self-control)…would you not agree? — against these things there is no issur (proscription in the Torah).</span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:4–5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:2"> If we live by the Ruach HaKodesh, we should stay in line with the Derech HaYashar (Straight Way) of the Ruach HaKodesh.</span>
