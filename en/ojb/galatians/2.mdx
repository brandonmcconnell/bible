<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:23"> Then, after arbah esrey shanim (fourteen years), again<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ac 11:30">a</a></sup>I went up to Yerushalayim with Bar-Nabba, having taken with me also Titos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:5"> Yet I went up according to a chazon (revelation), and I laid before them the Besuras HaGeulah which I proclaim among the Goyim, but I did this privately to the men of repute,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ga 2:9">b</a></sup>lest I should run, or should prove to have run, L'TOHU (in vain).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 49:4; 65:23">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:2–6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:20–21"> But Titos, the one with me, a Yevani (Greek), was not compelled to undergo bris milah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:3"> But because of the achei sheker (false brothers) b'Moshiach, the ones secretly brought in, the ones who crept in to spy out our DEROR<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Lv 25:10">d</a></sup>(freedom) which we have in Moshiach Yehoshua al menat (in order that) they might enslave us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:5"> To these enslavers not for one hour did we yield in subjection, that HaEmes of the Besuras HaGeulah might continue and remain with you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Galatian Goyim">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:34; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:11"> But from the men of repute whatever they once were matters nothing to me, ki ein masso panim im Hashem (for there is no respect of persons with G-d, no partiality), for to me these men of repute added nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:15; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:16"> But, on the contrary, having seen that I have been entrusted with the Besuras HaGeulah for those without the bris milah, just as Kefa was for those with the bris milah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:14–41"> For the One having worked in Kefa for a Shlichus to those with the bris milah also worked in me for a Shlichus to the Goyim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:3; HISGALUS 3:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:2"> And realizing the Chen v'Chesed Hashem having been given to me, Ya'akov<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Mt 13:55">f</a></sup>and Kefa and Yochanan, the men of repute, the ones seeming to be Ammudei HaKehillah (Pillars of the Kehillah), extended to me and to Bar-Nabba the yad yeminam (right hands) as a sign of Achavah B'Moshiach (Brotherhood in Moshiach), that we should be for those of the Goyim, but they for those of the bris milah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:17; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:16"> Only that we should remember the Aniyim (the Poor), the very thing which I was also eager to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:20; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:1"> But when Kefa came to Antioch, I stood against him to his face, because there was found in him a dvar ashmah (a thing of guilt, condemnation).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:28; LUKAS 15:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:2–3"> For, before certain ones<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Jerusalem visitors">g</a></sup>came from Ya'akov, Kefa was as a matter of course sitting at tish at betzi'at halechem (breaking of bread) at the Seudos Moshiach with the Goyim; but, when they came, Kefa drew back and was separating himself, fearing the ones<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="the Jerusalem party">h</a></sup>of the bris milah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ac 15:5">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:33; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:9"> And the rest of the Yehudim who were ma'aminim b'Moshiach joined with Kefa in this dissembling, so that even Bar-Nabba was carried away with their tzevi'ut (hypocrisy).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:10–11"> But when I saw that their halichah, their hitnahagut (conduct) was not the Derech HaYashar (Straight Way) with respect to HaEmes of HaBesuras HaGeulah, I said to Kefa before all, “If you, being a Yehudi, have a hitnahagut that is according to the Derech Goyim and not the Derech Yehudim, how do you compel the Goyim to live as the Yehudim?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:11–12"> We ourselves are Yehudim by birth and not Goyishe chote'im (sinners).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:9; TEHILLIM 143:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:30"> Yet we have da'as that a man cannot be YITZDAK IM HASHEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Job 25:4">j</a></sup>(be justified with G-d) by &#91;depending on&#93; the ma'asim of Gezetz (Law), but through emunah in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua. And we have come to have bitachon in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, that we can be YITZDAK IM HASHEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Job 25:4">k</a></sup>(be justified with G-d) by emunah in Moshiach and not by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="supposed zokheh merit of">l</a></sup>ma'asim, because by taryag mitzvot KOL CHAI LO YITZDAK<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Ps 143:2">m</a></sup>(all living shall not be justified).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:1–2; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:8–10; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:5"> Now, if, by seeking to be YITZDAK IM HASHEM in Moshiach, we ourselves were found also to be chote'im (sinners),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="2:15; Ro 3:9,23">n</a></sup>then in that case is Moshiach a kohen for iniquity, a minister serving sin? Chas v'Shalom (G-d forbid)!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:9–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:4–5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:12–16"> For if what I destroyed, these things I again rebuild,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Ga 1:23; 5:2">o</a></sup>I display myself to be a poshei'a (transgressor).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:10"> For I, through the Torah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Ga 3:13">p</a></sup>died in relation to the gezetz,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Ro 7:4-6">q</a></sup>so that I might live to Hashem. With Moshiach I have been talui al haetz<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Dt 21:23">r</a></sup>(hanging on the tree).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:24; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:4–6"> But it is no longer Anochi (I) who lives, but Moshiach who lives in me, and the Chayyim I now live in the basar, I live by emunah, emunah in the Zun fun der Oybershter,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Moshiach">s</a></sup>the one having ahavah (agape) for me and having given himself over, on my behalf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:11"> I do not set aside the Chen v'Chesed Hashem; for if the MAH (how) an ENOSH (man) is to be YITZDAK IM HASHEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Job 25:4">t</a></sup>is found through Gezetz chumra, then Moshiach died for nothing and L'TOHU (in vain).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Isa 49:4; 65:23">u</a></sup></span>
</div>
