<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="VAYIKRA 19:32; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:5–6; LETTER TO TITOS 2:2"> To a Zaken do not speak harsh rebuke. Rather entreat him as an abba. Treat the bochrim as achim (brothers).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:12; MATTITYAHU 12:50; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:8"> Elderly nashim treat as imahot (mothers); younger nashim as achayot (sisters) in all hatohar (moral purity).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEVARIM 27:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:1; DEVARIM 10:18"> Honor &#91;with support&#93; almanot (widows) that are really almanot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:1–3; YOCHANAN 19:26–27; MATTITYAHU 15:4–6"> But if any almanah has banim or bnei banim, let the banim or bnei banim learn first to show yirat Shomayim vis-a-vis their own bais and to render recompense to the horim (parents), for this is acceptable in the sight of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:3; LUKAS 2:37; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:32"> Now the true almanah, left alone, has set her tikvah on Hashem and continues in her techinnah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2:1">a</a></sup>and her tefillos yomam valailah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:5; HISGALUS 18:7; LUKAS 15:24"> But the one living in ahavat ta'anugot (hedonism) has died while living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:11; LETTER TO TITOS 2:15; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:1"> And insist on these things, that the almanot be irreproachable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:14; LUKAS 11:11–13"> But if anyone does not get a parnasah to provide for his own mishpochah and especially his own bais, he has denied the emunah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="of Moshiach">b</a></sup>and is worse than an Apikoros (skeptic, unbeliever).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:2; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:11; LUKAS 2:36–37"> Let an almanah be enrolled on the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="kehillah support">c</a></sup>list if she is not less than sixty years old, nesu'ah l'ish echad (a one man woman),</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:18; LETTER TO TITOS 3:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:36"> being commended by ma'asim tovim, having brought up banim, having showed hachnosas orchim, having washed the feet of the Kedoshim, having given nechamah (comfort) to the oppressed, having devoted herself to every ma'aseh tov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:14; HOSHEA 13:6; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:5"> But younger almanot refuse, for, when they have ta'avah (lust), in disregard of Moshiach they want nissu'in (marriage),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="see 1Ti 5:9">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:6; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:34"> Having harsha'ah (condemnation, conviction), because their first havtachah (promise, pledge to ministry) they annulled.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1Ti 5:9">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MISHLE 20:19; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:6–11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:30"> And at the same time also they learn atzlut, going around house to house, and not only idle but also platke machers (gossipy intriguers) and busybodies, speaking things they ought not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 2:5; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:1; LETTER TO TITOS 2:8"> Therefore, I counsel younger almanot to proceed with nissu'in (marriage), to bear banim, to manage the bais, giving no occasion to the mitnagged (opponent, antagonist) to reproach us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:2; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:4–5; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20–22"> For already some almanot have turned aside to follow HaSatan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:3–5; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:8"> If any ma'aminah (believing woman) has in her mishpochah (family) almanot, let her assist her almanot and let not Moshiachʼs Kehillah be burdened, that indeed Moshiachʼs Kehillah may assist the ones who are true almanot.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1Ti 5:9">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:12–13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:6"> Let the Zekenim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="see Ex 12:21">g</a></sup>(elders) who have ruled well be considered worthy of double kavod, especially the Zekenim laboring in Messianic hatafah (preaching) and Messianic hora'ah (teaching).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEVARIM 25:4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:7–10"> For the Kitvei HaKodesh says, LO TACHSOM SHOR BEDISHO (You shall not muzzle the ox while he is treading out the grain),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Dt 25:4">h</a></sup>and “Worthy is the workman of his wages.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="see Lk 10:7">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:16; DEVARIM 19:15; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:1"> Do not receive an accusation against a Zaken (Elder), unless on the PI SHNI EDIM O AL PI SHLOSHA EDIM (testimony of two or three witnesses).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Dt 19:15">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:2; LETTER TO TITOS 1:13; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:20"> The Zekenim that are sinning, expose before all, that the rest also may have yirat Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:4; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:1; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:13"> I charge you before Hashem and Moshiach Yehoshua and the malachim habechirim (chosen angels) that these directives you keep without discrimination, doing nothing on the mekor (basis) of masoh panim (partiality).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:11; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:10; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:6"> Give s'michah quickly to no one nor participate in the chatta'im (sins) of others; keep yourself tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="TEHILLIM 104:15; MISHLE 31:4–7; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:4"> No longer drink only mayim, but use a little yayin because of your stomach and your frequent illnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:19–21"> With some, their chatta'im are obvious, going before them to mishpat, but with others, some chatta'im indeed follow after them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="to the Yom HaDin">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MISHLE 10:9; LUKAS 11:33; TEHILLIM 37:5–6"> Likewise, also ma'asim tovim are borur (obvious), but even when they are not, they cannot be hidden.</span>
