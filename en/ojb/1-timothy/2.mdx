<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:16; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:1; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20"> This is good and acceptable before Hashem Moshieinu,</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:20; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:15"> For Adonoi echad hu (there is one G-d) and there is also metavekh echad<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Job 33:23; Isa 43:27; cf. <span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:28; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1–2"> The one who gave his nefesh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Isa 53:10-12">b</a></sup>as a kofer (ransom), on behalf of all. The edut (testimony) of this was given at the right time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:15; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:1"> And for this edut I was given the s'michah (ordination) of Hashem as a karoz (herald), a maggid,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="darshan (preacher) for Moshiach">c</a></sup>a Shliach (Moshiachʼs emissary) — I speak Emes, I do not speak sheker — and a &#91;rabbinic&#93; moreh (teacher) for the Nations in emunah and Emes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 134:2; TEHILLIM 63:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:22"> I want, therefore, kol bnei Adam to daven in every shul, lifting up yadayim kodesh (holy hands) without ka'as (anger) and madon (strife).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:3–5; LETTER TO TITOS 2:3–5; TEHILLIM 149:4"> Similarly also nashim (women) should adorn themselves with respectable comportment and tznius (modesty, piety) in appearance and with decency and propriety, not with coiffures and gold or pearls or costly clothing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:12; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:3–5; HISGALUS 2:19"> But with what is proper for nashim professing yirat Shomayim, that is, by means of ma'asim tovim (good works).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:34–35; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:22–24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:3"> Let the isha (wife) learn in silence in all submission.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:34"> I do not allow an isha (wife) either to have teaching authority over or to have hishtaltut (domination, taking control) over &#91;her&#93; man, but to be in silence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 2:18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:8–9; BERESHIS 1:27"> For Adam was formed rishonah (first), then Chavah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:3; BERESHIS 3:6; BERESHIS 3:12–13"> And Adam was not deceived, but the isha, having been deceived, has come to be in averah (transgression).</span>
