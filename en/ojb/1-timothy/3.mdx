<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 1:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:28; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:2"> Trustworthy is the dvar Emes: if anyone aspires to the congregational office of Mashgiach Ruchani (Spiritual Overseer) over the Adat Hashem (Congregation of G-d), he desires a good task.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 1:6–9; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:13"> It is necessary, therefore, for the congregational Mashgiach Ruchani to be without reproach, ba'al isha echat (a one woman man/master),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See 1Sm 2:22-25, i.e., kedushah and tahorah characterized by heterosexuality, exclusivity, and fidelity">a</a></sup>drug-free and clear-headed, a man of seichel, practiced in derech eretz and hachnosas orchim (hospitality), skillful as a rabbinic moreh (yeshiva teacher),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 1:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:18; LETTER TO TITOS 3:2"> Not a shikkor (drunkard) indulging in much wine, not violent but forbearing and eidel (gentle, courteous), not a ba'al machlokes (quarrelsome person), not a gelt-loving kamtzan (miser).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 1:6; BERESHIS 18:19; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:12"> He must be a ba'al bayit who can manage his own household well, having his banim (children) in submission with all respect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 3:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:28"> Now if anyone does not have da'as of how to manage his own bais, how will he be a menahel ruchani (spiritual administrator) who can give oversight to the Kehillah of Hashem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:12–13; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:2"> He must not be a neophyte in the emunah &#91;of Moshiach&#93;, lest, having become a ba'al gaavah (a haughty person), he might fall into the din HaSatan (the judgment or verdict of the Adversary, the Accuser).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:21; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:5"> Now it is necessary also for him to have a keter shem tov (good name) with the outsiders, lest he might fall into reproach and a pakh (pitfall, trap) of HaSatan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:1; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:3–6"> Messianic Shammashim similarly must be respectable men of derech eretz, not double-tongued, the Shammash not a shikkor (drunkard) indulging in much wine, not a lover of dishonest gain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:19; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9–10; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:5"> Keeping the sod haemunah (the mystery of the faith) with a clear matzpun<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1:5,19">b</a></sup>(conscience).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:22; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:13; LETTER TO TITOS 1:6–7"> And let these Messianic Shammashim also be tested before holding office and then, if they prove unreprovable, let them have the avodas hakodesh sherut (ministry) of Messianic Shammashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 2:3; LETTER TO TITOS 3:2; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:2"> Nashim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="see Ro 16:1">c</a></sup>(women) serving as Shammashim similarly must be tzidkaniyot (righteous women), respectable, not yentas with mouths of lashon hora and rekhilus (gossip), but nashim who are temperate and faithful in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:2; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:4–5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:1"> Let the Shammashim be ba'alei isha achat (one wife husbands), managing well their banim and their own batim (households).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 25:21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:35"> For the ones having served well in the avodas hakodesh of Messianic Shammashim acquire for themselves a good standing and much bitachon in emunah in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:34; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:22; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:15–17"> I have the tikvah to come to you soon, but I am writing these directives so that,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:21–22; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:9"> If I delay, you may have da'as of the halachah in the Beis Hashem, which is the Adat HaEl Chai (the Community of the Living G-d), the ammud (pillar) and yesod (foundation) of HaEmes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:3; MARKOS 16:19"> And confessedly great is the sod of chasidus:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="see 1Ti 3:9">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Elokim was manifested in basar,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">was vindicated</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="proven just">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">by the Ruach HaKodesh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">was seen by malachim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">was proclaimed among the Nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">was believed on in the world,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">was taken up in kavod.</span></span>
</div>
