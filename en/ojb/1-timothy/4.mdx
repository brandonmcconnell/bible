<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:1–9; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:13–15"> Now the Ruach HaKodesh says befeirush (explicitly) that in the acharit hayamim some will become meshummad (apostate) from the emunah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="of Moshiach">a</a></sup>giving heed to deceitful ruchot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1Y 4:1">b</a></sup>(spirits) and teachings of shedim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:30; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:18"> Through the tzevi'ut (hypocrisy) of ones<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="morei sheker">c</a></sup>speaking sheker, the matzpun of whom is seared as with a branding iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:4; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:6"> Such will forbid nisu'im (marriage), commanding an issur (prohibition) against that ma'akhal which Hashem created for partaking with hodayah by the ma'aminim in Moshiach and by the ones who have da'as of HaEmes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:14"> Because the whole Bri'ah (Creation) that Hashem has created is tov<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gn 1:4,10,12,18,21,25,31">d</a></sup>and nothing is to be rejected that is received with hodayah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:14; LUKAS 4:4"> For it is being set apart as kodesh through the dvar Hashem and tefillos and brachot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 2:7–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:16; MATTITYAHU 13:52"> By presenting these things to the Achim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="in Moshiach">e</a></sup>you will be a good k'li kodesh (minister) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, you will be one nourished with the divrei haemunah and the torah yeshara (good, straight Orthodox Jewish teaching) which you have followed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:16; LETTER TO TITOS 2:12; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:4"> But the worldly aggadot and bubbemeises (old wives tales), refuse. Rather, train yourself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1:4">f</a></sup>for chasidus.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="in Moshiach">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:33; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:6; TEHILLIM 37:3–4"> For hitammelut (bodily exercise or training) is kedai (profitable) a bissle, but chasidus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="in Moshiach">h</a></sup>is profitable in every way, holding havtachah (promise) of Chayyim now in the Olam Hazeh, and, later, of Olam Habah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:15"> This dvar torah is trustworthy and worthy of all acceptance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:4; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:2; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:6"> For to this end we labor and strive at the melachah (task), because we have set tikvateynu in the EL CHAI<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Josh 3:10">i</a></sup>who is Moshi'a (Savior) of kol Bnei Adam, that is, the ma'aminim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="in Moshiach Adoneinu">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:2; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:2; LETTER TO TITOS 2:15"> On these things insist and say shiurim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 2:7; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:22; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:15"> Let no one regard with contempt your tza'irut (youth), but become a mofet (example) for the ma'aminim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="in Moshiach">k</a></sup>in loshon (speech), in hitnahagut (conduct), in ahavah, in emunah, and in lev tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:4; LETTER TO TITOS 2:15; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:2"> Until I come, attend to the kri'ah betzibbur (congregational public reading) of the Kitvei Hakodesh, attend to the Messianic hatafah (preaching) and to the Messianic hora'ah (instruction, teaching).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:6; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:9–11; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:18"> Do not neglect the matanah (gift) in you which was given to you by means of dvar hanevu'ah (prophecy) with the s'michah administered by the Ziknei HaKehillah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:15–16; TEHILLIM 143:5"> Put these things into practice, that your progress in shomer masoret (religious devotion) may be manifest to all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 1:9; LETTER TO TITOS 2:7; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8–9"> Be shomer both toward yourself and toward your Messianic hora'ah (instruction), and be arain getun (engrossed), torud (completely absorbed and involved) in them. For by so doing this, you will come to Yeshu'at Eloheynu, and this means not only you yourself but also those who hear your hatafah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="about Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach">l</a></sup></span>
