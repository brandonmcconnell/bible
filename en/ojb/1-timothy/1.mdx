<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 1:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:15"> From Sha'ul, a Shliach of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua according to the authoritative command of the Dvar Hashem, that is, Hashem Moshieinu and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua tikvateynu.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 1:4; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:14–17"> To Timotiyos,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ac 16:1">a</a></sup>a trueborn ben baEmunah (son in the faith). Chesed Hashem, Rachamim Hashem, and Shalom Hashem from Elohim Avinu and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua Adoneinu.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:6–7; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9–10"> Even as I urged you to remain in Ephesus while I was going into Macedonia so that you might enjoin the gzeira (authoritative command) on certain persons not to teach heterodoxy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:7; LETTER TO TITOS 1:14; LETTER TO TITOS 3:9"> Nor to focus on aggadah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ti 1:14">b</a></sup>and endless toldot (genealogies) which give rise to useless speculations in contrast to Hashemʼs imun (training) in emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:22; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:22"> But the tachlis (purpose) of the gzeira<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1:3">c</a></sup>is ahavah (love) out of a lev tahor (pure heart) and a clear matzpun (conscience) and emunah (faith) without tzevi'ut (hypocrisy).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 1:10; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:23–24; LETTER TO TITOS 3:9"> Some people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1:3">d</a></sup>have missed the mark and deviated from these things to hevel (vanity), to divrei havohu (words of emptiness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:22; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:4; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:12"> Wanting to teach Torah as rabbonim, they have binah neither of what they are talking about nor of the things about which they so confidently make assertions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:12–13; TEHILLIM 19:7–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:16"> But we know that the Torah is beneficial if anyoneʼs use of Torah is Torah-true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:19; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:20"> This means one must have da'as that the Torah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="in reference to the mitzvot lo ta'aseh, the negative commandments, lo tachmod, lo tirtzach, lo tinaf, etc.">e</a></sup>is not intended for the anshei tzedek (men of righteousness) but for the bnei Belial and the poshei'im (the ones transgressing, rebelling), those without yirat Shomayim and the chote'im (sinners), osei to'eva (doers of abomination), profane people, killers of their own Av va'Em (father and mother),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ex 21:15">f</a></sup>and rotzeachim (murderers),</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:9–10; VAYIKRA 18:22; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:3"> Zannayim (fornicators), shochvim es zachar (homosexuals), gonvei nefesh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ex 21:16">g</a></sup>(kidnapper, slave dealers), shakranim (liars), nishba'im lasheker (perjurers) and whatever else is keneged (against) sound orthodox torah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Moshiachʼs Torah in correct Messianic teaching">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:7; LETTER TO TITOS 1:3; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:15"> According to the Besuras HaGeulah kavod haElohim ham'vorach (the Good News of Redemption of the glory of the blessed G-d) with which I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Rav Sha'ul">i</a></sup>was entrusted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:13; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:17; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:9–10"> Modeh ani (I give thanks) to the one having empowered me, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua Adoneinu, because he considered me ne'eman (faithful), having appointed me to the rabbanut of Moshiach.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="i.e., Messianic ministry">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:3; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:4–8"> Previously being one guilty of Chillul Hashem gidduf (blasphemy), redifah (persecution), and &#91;religious&#93; terrorism, fort (nevertheless), I received rachamim (mercy), because I acted in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="unregenerate">k</a></sup>ignorance in the absence of emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:7–8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:10"> And the Chen vaChesed Adoneinu super-abounded with emunah and ahavah in Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MARKOS 2:17; LUKAS 19:10; YOCHANAN 3:16–17"> Trustworthy is the dvar Emes and worthy of all mekabel acceptance, that Rebbe, Melech, HaMoshiach Yehoshua came into the Olam Hazeh to rescue chote'im, of whom I am the foremost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:7; LUKAS 7:47"> But because of this, I received rachamim that in me, the foremost, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua might display all savlanut (longsuffering, patience), making me a mofet (model) to the ones about to have bitachon (trust) and emunah in Moshiach, resulting in Chayyei Olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:20–21; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:25; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:15–16"> Now to HaMelech HaOlamim, the One who is Ein Sof (without end), Whom no eye has seen, Adonoi echad, lo hakavod v'hatiferet mayhaolam v'ad haolam (to him be honor and glory from forever to forever). Omein.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:14; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:20; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:1–3"> This gzeira<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="1:3">l</a></sup>I commit to you, beni Timotiyos, according to hanevu'ot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="4:14">m</a></sup>(the prophecies) made previously about you, that by them you might war the good warfare,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:9; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:15–16; HISGALUS 3:10"> Holding emunah and a clear matzpun (conscience), which some persons,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="1:3">n</a></sup>because they pushed aside a clear matzpun,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="1:5">o</a></sup>have suffered shipwreck in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Orthodox Jewish">p</a></sup>emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:4–5; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:32"> Among these are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I handed over to HaSatan, that they might be taught not to commit Chillul Hashem gidduf.</span>
</div>
