<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 2:5; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:17–20; LETTER TO TITOS 2:8–10"> As many avadim as are under the ol (yoke) of slavery let them consider their own adonim as worthy of all kavod, lest the name of Hashem and torateynu (our teaching) suffer Chillul Hashem gidduf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:1; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:11; BERESHIS 16:4–5"> And let the ma'aminim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="in Moshiach">a</a></sup>that have adonim not disrespect their masters because their adonim are achim, but let them all the more render service to them because the ones receiving benefit from their sherut hatov are ma'aminim in Moshiach and ahuvim.<br />Say shiurim on these things and exhort them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:17; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:10"> If anyone teaches heterodoxy (a doctrine that has a chiluk or difference) and does not agree with the orthodox Jewish teaching of Yehoshua the Moshiach Adoneinu and with torah conforming to chasidus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:23; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:4; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:6"> He has succombed to gaa'vah (conceit), having binah of nothing, but having a morbid craving for controversies and disputes over devarim out of which comes kinah (envy), madon (strife), lashon hora (evil speaking), chashadot merusha'im (evil suspicions),</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:5; LETTER TO TITOS 1:11; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:3"> And constant friction between men corrupted in their minds and having become bereft of HaEmes, thinking chasidus to be a means of financial revach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:5; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:8; MISHLE 16:8"> But chasidus with tzufriedenkait (contentment) is great revach (gain, profit).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="IYOV 1:21; TEHILLIM 49:17; LUKAS 12:20–21"> For we brought nothing into the Olam Hazeh, neither are we able to carry anything out of it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:5–6; MATTITYAHU 6:11; MATTITYAHU 6:25–33"> But having okhel (food) and begadim (clothes), with these we will be satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–17; MISHLE 15:27; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:7"> But the ones desiring to be oishirim (rich men) fall into nisayon (temptation) and into a pakh (trap) and into many foolish and destructive ta'avot (lusts) which plunge men into cherem and Avaddon (destruction).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MISHLE 1:19; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:9; TEHILLIM 32:10"> For the ahavas hakesef (love of money) is the shoresh (root) of kol hara'ot (all evils), which some, craving, were thereby led away from the emunah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">b</a></sup>and pierced themselves with many machovim (sorrows).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:22; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:17; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:12"> But you, ish haElohim (man of G-d), flee these things. Pursue tzedek, chasidus, emunah, ahavah, savlanut, and anavah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:10; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:25–26"> Fight the good fight of emunah; lay hold of the Chayyei Olam to which you were called when you made the hoda'ah yafah (good confession) before edim rabbim (many witnesses).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:36–37; MATTITYAHU 27:11; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:21"> I charge you before Hashem, the one giving Chayyim to all things, and before Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, the one having testified the hoda'ah yafah (good confession) before Pontius Pilate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:14; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:23"> I charge you to be shomer over the mitzvoh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="of Moshiach">c</a></sup>spotlessly, irreproachably, until the appearing of Moshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:16; HISGALUS 17:14; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:17"> Which He will make manifest at the right time — HaMvorach, HaRibbon HaYachid, Melech HaMelachim and Adon HaAdonim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:17; YOCHANAN 1:18; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5"> To the One who is alone haAlmavet (Immortal), dwelling in Ohr unapproachable, whom no man among Bnei Adam saw, neither is able to see, lo hakavod v'hagevurah l'olam va'ed. Omein.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MISHLE 11:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:17; MISHLE 27:24"> Charge the oishirim in the Olam Hazeh not to walk in gaa'vah (pride) and high-mindedness, neither to put their tikvah (hope) in the uncertainty of their osher but in Hashem who richly grants us all things for enjoyment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:16; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17; TEHILLIM 37:3"> Charge the oishirim to do HaTov, to be rich in ma'asim tovim, to demonstrate rochav lev (generosity) and willingness to do gemilus chesed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:19–21; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:12; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:19"> Treasuring up for themselves a yesod tov (good foundation) for Olam Habah that they may lay hold of HaChayyim HaAmittiyim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:8; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:16; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:12–14"> O Timotiyos, the pikkadon (deposit)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="of Moshiachʼs torah, Ac 2:42">d</a></sup>entrusted to you be shomer over, turning away from the profane empty utterances and oppositions of the falsely named Da'as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="proto-Gnostic, see 1Y 1—5">e</a></sup>(Knowledge),</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:18; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:6; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:22"> By which, some, professing, missed the mark concerning the emunah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">f</a></sup>Chen v'Chesed Hashem be with you.</span>
</div>
