<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 22:9–10; EZRA 1:11; EZRA 10:16"> Now when the adversaries<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., the enemies, the Samaritans">a</a></sup>of Yehudah and Binyamin heard that the Banim of the Golus were the Bonim (Builders) of the Heikhal for Hashem Elohei Yisroel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 19:37; MELACHIM BAIS 17:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:4"> Then they came to Zerubavel, and to the chief of the avot, and said unto them, Let us build with you, for we seek Eloheichem, as ye do, and we do sacrifice unto Him since the days Esar-Chaddon Melech Ashur (Assyria), which brought us here.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See 2Kgs 19:37; also 17:33">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 2:20; MATTITYAHU 10:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:22–3"> But Zerubavel, and Yeshua, and the rest of the chief of the avot of Yisroel, said unto them, Ye have nothing to do with us to build a Beis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hamikdash">c</a></sup>unto Eloheinu; but we alone will build unto Hashem Elohei Yisroel, as HaMelech Koresh (Cyrus) Melech Paras (Persia) hath commanded us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZRA 3:3; NECHEMYAH 6:9; YIRMEYAH 38:4"> Then the Am HaAretz weakened the hands of the Am Yehudah, and troubled them from building,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NACHUM 1:11; EZRA 5:5–18; TEHILLIM 2:1–2"> And hired counselors against them, to frustrate their etza (plan), all the days of Koresh (Cyrus) Melech Paras (Persia), even until the reign of Daryavesh (Darius) Melech Paras (Persia).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ESTHER 1:1; DANIEL 9:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:5–9"> And in the reign of Achashverosh,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Xerxes I 486—465 B.C.E., the husband of Queen Esther">d</a></sup>in the beginning of his reign, they wrote unto him a sitnah (accusation),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="related to the word Satan, Accuser">e</a></sup>against the inhabitants of Yehudah and Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:26; DANIEL 2:4; YESHAYAH 36:11"> Then, in the days of Artachshasta,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Artaxerxes I 465-424 B.C.E.">f</a></sup>it was Bishlam, Mitredat, Tavel, and the rest of their companions, who wrote unto Artachshasta Melech Paras (Persia); and the writing of the letter was written in Aramaic script, and set forth in Aramaic.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZRA 4:9; SHMUEL BAIS 8:17; SHMUEL BAIS 20:25"> Rechum the high commissioner and Shimshai the scribe wrote a letter against Yerushalayim to Melech Artachshasta as follows;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZRA 5:6; EZRA 6:6; MELACHIM BAIS 17:24"> Then wrote Rechum the high commissioner, and Shimshai the scribe, and the rest of their companions; the judges, the officials, the Tarplim, the Apharsim, the Arkvim, the Babylonians, and those of Shushan, that is, the Elma'im,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZRA 4:17; EZRA 4:11; EZRA 7:12"> And the rest of the nations whom the great and noble Osnapar deported, and settled in the city of Shomron,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Samaria">g</a></sup>and elsewhere in Beyond-the-River<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Euphrates">h</a></sup>wrote; and now</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> This is the copy of the letter that they sent unto him, even unto Artachshasta HaMelech; From thy avadim, the men Beyond-the-River;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Euphrates">i</a></sup>and now</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZRA 5:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:13; EZRA 5:3"> may it be known unto the Melech, that the Yehudim which came up from thee to us are come unto Yerushalayim, building the rebellious and the wicked city, and finishing its walls, and repairing the foundations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZRA 7:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:6–7; NECHEMYAH 5:4"> Be it known now unto the Melech, that, if this city be built, and the walls set up again, then they will not pay tax, tribute, or duty, and so thou shalt damage the royal revenues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:12–15; YECHEZKEL 33:31; YOCHANAN 12:5–6"> Now because we have covenant maintenance (“share the Kingʼs salt”) from the palace, and it was not proper for us to see the dishonor of the Melech, therefore we have sent and informed the Melech,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZRA 4:12; ESTHER 3:5–8; MELACHIM BAIS 24:20–1"> That search may be made in the sefer of the archives of thy avot; so shalt thou find in the sefer of the archives, and know that this city is a rebellious city, and hurtful unto melachim and provinces, a place of sedition from ancient times, for which cause was this city destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZRA 4:20; MELACHIM ALEF 4:24; SHMUEL BAIS 8:3"> We inform the Melech that, if this city be built again, and the walls thereof set up, by this means thou shalt have no possession in the province Beyond-the-River.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Euphrates">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:7; EZRA 5:7; EZRA 4:9–11"> Then the Melech sent an answer: Unto Rechum the high commissioner, and to Shimshai the scribe, and to the rest of their companions that dwell in Shomron (Samaria), and unto the rest Beyond-the-River,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Euphrates">k</a></sup>Shalom, and now</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 8:8"> The nish'tevan (official document) which ye sent unto us hath been fully read before me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 18:7; MISHLE 25:2; DEVARIM 13:14"> And I commanded, and search hath been made, and it is found that this city for a long time hath made insurrection against melachim, and that rebellion and sedition have been made therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:18; MELACHIM ALEF 4:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 18:3"> There have been mighty melachim also over Yerushalayim, which have ruled over all territory Beyond-the-River,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Euphrates">l</a></sup>to whom tax, tribute, and duty were paid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Give ye now ta'am (command) to cause these men to cease, and that this city be not built, until another ta'am shall be given from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ESTHER 7:3–4; ESTHER 3:8–9; EZRA 4:13"> Take heed now that ye fail not to do this; lamah (why) should damage grow to the hurt of the Melech?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:15; MICHOH 2:1; EZRA 4:17"> Now when the copy of letter of Melech Artachshasta was read before Rechum, and Shimshai the scribe, and their companions, they went up in haste to Yerushalayim unto the Yehudim, and made them to cease by force and compulsion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="CHAGGAI 1:15; NECHEMYAH 6:9; EZRA 5:5"> Then ceased the work of the Beis HaElohim which is at Yerushalayim. So it ceased unto the second year<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="i.e., 520 B.C.E.">m</a></sup>of the reign of Daryavesh (Darius) Melech Paras (Persia).</span>
