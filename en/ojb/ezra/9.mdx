<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:24–30; NECHEMYAH 9:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:17–25"> Now when these things were completed, the sarim came to me, saying, HaAm Yisroel, and the Kohanim, and the Levi'im, have not separated themselves from the Am HaAretz, doing according to their abominations, even of the Kena'ani, the Chitti, the Perizzi, the Yevusi, the Ammoni, the Moavi, the Egyptians, and the Emori.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 13:23–24; EZRA 10:2; SHEMOT 34:16"> For they have taken of their banot for themselves, and for their banim, so that the zera hakodesh have mingled themselves with the Am HaAretz; yea, the yad of the sarim (princes) and officials hath been chief in this trespass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 1:4; YEHOSHUA 7:6; YIRMEYAH 48:37–38"> And when I heard this thing, I made keriah of my garment and my mantle, and plucked off the hair of my head and of my beard, and sat down appalled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZRA 10:3; YESHAYAH 66:2; SHEMOT 29:39"> Then were assembled around me every one that trembled at the Divrei Elohei Yisroel, because of the transgression of those of the Golus; and I sat appalled until the minchat haerev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 8:22; MELACHIM ALEF 8:38; MELACHIM ALEF 8:54"> And at the minchat haerev I arose up from my abasement, and having made the keriah of my garment and my mantle, I fell upon my knees, and spread out my hands unto Hashem Elohai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HISGALUS 18:5; TEHILLIM 38:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 28:9"> And davened, O Elohai, I am ashamed and am disgraced to lift up my face to thee, Elohai, for avonoteinu are increased over our head, and ashmateinu (our guilt) is reached up unto Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:6; DANIEL 9:5–8; DEVARIM 4:25–28"> Since the days of Avoteinu have we been in a great trespass unto this day; and for avonoteinu have we, our melachim, and our Kohanim, been delivered into the hand of the melachim of the lands, to the cherev, to captivity, and to spoil, and to humiliation of face, as it is this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 13:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:5–6; HOSHEA 6:2"> And now for a little space grace hath been shown from Hashem Eloheinu, to leave us to escape a she'ar (remnant), and to give us a peg in his Makom Kodesh, that Eloheinu may lighten our eyes, and give us a little reviving in our bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 136:23–24; EZRA 7:6; TEHILLIM 106:45–46"> For we were avadim; yet Eloheinu hath not forsaken us in our bondage, but hath extended chesed unto us in the sight of the Melachim of Paras (Persia), to give us a reviving, to set up the Beis Eloheinu, and to repair the ruins thereof, and to give us a wall in Yehudah and in Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 7:8; BERESHIS 44:16; DANIEL 9:4–16"> And now, O Eloheinu, what shall we say after this? For we have forsaken Thy mitzvot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:24–30; EZRA 6:21; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:1"> Which Thou hast commanded by Thy avadim the nevi'im, saying, HaAretz, unto which ye go to possess it, is an eretz niddah (unclean land) with the filthiness of the Am HaAretz, with their abominations, which have filled it from one end to another with their tum'a.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:3; DEVARIM 23:6; YEHOSHUA 23:12–13"> Now therefore give not your banot unto their banim, neither take their banot unto your banim, nor further their shalom or their prosperity ad olam, that ye may be strong, and eat the good of the land and leave it for your banim ad olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TEHILLIM 103:10; EKHAH 3:22; EKHAH 3:39–40"> And after all that is come upon us for our evil deeds, and for our great guilt, seeing that Thou Eloheinu hast punished us less than avoneinu deserve, and hast given us such deliverance as this,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEVARIM 9:8; EZRA 9:2; DEVARIM 9:14"> Should we again break Thy mitzvot, and join in affinity with the people of these to'evot (abominations)? Wouldest not Thou be angry with us till Thou hadst consumed us, so that there should be no she'erit (remnant) nor escaping?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 130:3; YOCHANAN 8:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:19"> Hashem Elohei Yisroel, Thou art tzaddik; for we remain yet escaped, as it is this day; hineni, we are before Thee in our guilt, though we cannot stand before Thee because of it.</span>
