<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 29:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:22–23; YIRMEYAH 25:12–14"> Now in the first year<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="539 B.C.E.">a</a></sup>of Koresh Melech Paras (Persia), that the Devar Hashem by the mouth of Yirmeyah might be fulfilled, Hashem stirred up the ruach of Koresh Melech Paras (Persia), so that he made a proclamation throughout all his Malchut, and put it also in writing, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YESHAYAH 45:12–13; YESHAYAH 44:26–1; DANIEL 5:23"> Thus saith Koresh Melech Paras (Persia), Hashem Elohei HaShomayim hath given me all the kingdoms of ha'aretz; and he hath charged me to build him Beis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hamikdash">b</a></sup>at Yerushalayim, which is in Yehudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DANIEL 6:26; YIRMEYAH 10:10; YESHAYAH 45:5"> Who is there among you of all His people, Elohav be with him, let him go up to Yerushalayim, which is in Yehudah, and build the Beis Hashem Elohei Yisroel, (He is HaElohim) which is in Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:3; EZRA 7:16–18; EZRA 2:68–70"> And whosoever remaineth in any place where he sojourneth, let the men of his place help him with kesef, and with zahav, and with goods, and with livestock, beside the nedavah (freewill offering) for the Beis HaElohim that is in Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZRA 1:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:13"> Then rose up the chief of the avot of Yehudah and Binyamin, and the kohanim, and the Levi'im, with all them whose ruach HaElohim had stirred up, to go up to build the Beis Hashem which is in Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:7; EZRA 1:4; EZRA 8:33"> And all they that were about them did strengthen their hands with vessels of kesef, with zahav, with goods, and with livestock, and with precious things, in addition to all their nedavot (freewill offerings).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:7; EZRA 5:14; MELACHIM BAIS 24:13"> Also HaMelech Koresh brought forth the vessels of the Beis Hashem, which Nevukhadnetzar had carried off out of Yerushalayim, and had put them in the bais (temple) of his g-ds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZRA 5:14; ZECHARYAH 4:6–10; EZRA 1:11"> Even those did Koresh Melech Paras (Persia) bring forth by the hand of Mitredat the treasurer, and counted them unto Sheshbatzar, the Nasi of Yehudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 7:50; BAMIDBAR 7:19–89; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 4:8"> And this is the number of them; thirty basins of zahav, a thousand basins of kesef, nine and twenty knives,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Thirty bowls of zahav, kesef basins of a second kind four hundred and ten, and other vessels one thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:19–21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:23; MATTITYAHU 1:11–12"> All the vessels of zahav and of kesef were five thousand and four hundred. All these did Sheshbatzar bring up with them of the Golus that were brought up from Bavel unto Yerushalayim.</span>
