<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:2–3; EZRA 9:4; YEHOSHUA 23:12–13"> So now let us make a Brit with Eloheinu to put away all nashim and that born of them, according to the counsel of Adonoi and the charedim at the mitzvot of Eloheinu; and let it be done according to the Torah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:10; KOHELET 9:10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:12–13"> Arise! For this matter is your responsibility; but we will be with you; chazak and act.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEVARIM 9:18; NECHEMYAH 3:1; NECHEMYAH 12:22"> Then Ezra arose from before the Beis HaElohim and went into the chamber of Yehochanan ben Elyashiv. <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZRA 1:1; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:5"> And they issued a proclamation throughout Yehudah and Yerushalayim to all the Banim of the Golus, that they should assemble at Yerushalayim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZRA 7:26; YOCHANAN 9:22; SHOFETIM 21:5"> And that whoever would not come within three days, according to the counsel of the sarim and the zekenim, all his possessions should be forfeited and he himself excluded from the Kahal of those of the Golus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 12:17–18; YIRMEYAH 10:13; EZRA 7:8–9"> So all the men of Yehudah and Binyamin assembled at Yerushalayim within the three days. <span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZRA 9:6; BAMIDBAR 32:14; MATTITYAHU 23:32"> Then Ezra HaKohen stood up and said to them, You have been unfaithful and have married foreign women adding to Ashmat Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:2; EZRA 10:3; YESHAYAH 1:16–18"> Now, therefore, make confession to Hashem Elohei Avoteichem and do His will; separate yourselves from the am ha'aretz and from the foreign women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 13:23; TEHILLIM 78:57; EZRA 10:3–4"> Then Kol HaKahal answered and said with a kol gadol, Ken! As you have said, so it is our duty to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZRA 10:18–44; MATTITYAHU 7:13–14"> But there are Am rav, it is the rainy season, and we are not able to stand bachotz (outside). Nor can the matter be done in one or two days, for we have transgressed greatly in this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:10; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:8; BAMIDBAR 25:4"> Let our leaders represent the Kol HaKahal and let all those in our towns who have married foreign women come at appointed times, together with the ziknei ir and shofetim of each town until the charon af Eloheinu on account of this matter is turned away from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 11:16; NECHEMYAH 10:20; NECHEMYAH 12:33"> Only Yonatan ben Asahel and Yachzeyah ben Tikvah supported by Meshullam and Shabetai the Levi stood opposed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="IYOV 29:16; DEVARIM 13:14; YOCHANAN 7:51"> But the Banim of the Golus did so. So they convened on the first day of the tenth month to investigate the matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And they finished investigating all men who had married foreign women by the 1st day of the 1st month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZRA 5:2; EZRA 3:2; ZECHARYAH 3:1"> And among the Bnei HaKohanim who had married foreign women were found of the Bnei Yeshua ben Yotzadak, and his brothers; Ma'aseiyah, Eli'ezer, Yariv, and Gedalyah.</span>
