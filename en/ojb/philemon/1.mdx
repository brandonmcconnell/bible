<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:1; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:9"> Sha'ul, a prisoner of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua and Timotiyos acheinu (our brother). To Philemon ha'ahuv (the beloved) and our fellow po'el (worker),</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:5"> And to Apphia achoteinu (our sister) and to Archippus our chaver leneshek (comrade arms) and to the Kehillah in your bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:2; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:14"> Chen v'Chesed Hashem and Shalom Hashem to you from Elohim Avinu and from Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:16; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:3"> I always offer a bracha to my G-d when I mention you in my tefillos (prayers),</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:1"> Because I hear of your ahavah (agape) and emunah, which you have for Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua and for all the Kedoshim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:9–11; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:9"> &#91;I pray&#93; that your emunah (faith) being shared in Moshiachʼs kiruv rechokim (bringing near the far away ones) may become effective in the da'as of every mitzvah we may do for Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:4"> For I had simcha gedolah (much joy) and chizzuk (encouragement) because of your ahavah, for the levavot of the Kedoshim have been refreshed through you, Ach b'Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:6; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:12"> Therefore, though I have much boldness in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach that I could in fact order you to do your chovah musarit (moral duty) of avodas hakodesh (holy service),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:1"> Yet I would rather make an appeal on the mekor (basis) of ahavah (agape) — I, &#91;Rav&#93; Sha'ul, the Zaken (Elder) but also a prisoner of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:15; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:2"> I appeal to you concerning beni, having “fathered” him (to a new birth) while in bais hasohar (prison) — that is, Onesimus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the slave boy Onesimusʼ name means “Useful”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:11; IYOV 30:1–2; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:10"> The one once “Useless” to you, but, now, both to you and to me “Useful” (Onesimus).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:14–15; YIRMEYAH 31:20; MARKOS 11:25"> I am sending him who is my very lev (heart) back to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:17"> I was wanting to detain him with me, in order that he might function as a keli kodesh (minister), ministering to me in your place, as your murshe (proxy), while Iʼm detained in the imprisonment of the Besuras HaGeulah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:17; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:2–3"> But I wanted to do nothing without your haskamah (consent, approval), in order that the mitzvah you do might not be meshabed (caused to be obligated) or forced, but voluntary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 50:20; BERESHIS 45:5–8; YESHAYAH 20:6"> Eppis (for some reason) perhaps he was separated from you for a while in order that you might have him back for Yamim HaOlam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:22; MATTITYAHU 23:8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:22"> No longer as a bond servant, but, more than a bond servant, an ach ahuv (a beloved brother),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="in Moshiach">b</a></sup>especially to me, and how much more to you, both as a man and in Adoneinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:23; MATTITYAHU 10:40; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:12"> So if you consider me partnered to you as a chaver &#91;in our common orthodox Jewish emunah&#93;, receive him as you would me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:4–7"> But if in anything he wronged you or owes you, charge this to my cheshbon (account, bill).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:15"> I, &#91;Rav&#93; Sha'ul, am writing this with my own hand: I will repay. This is not to meorer (point out) the fact that on your cheshbon you owe me your very neshamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:4; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:7; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:12"> Yes, Ach b'Moshiach, I would have some “usefulness” from you in Adoneinu. Refresh my lev in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:4; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:16"> Confident of you as one who is shomer mitzvot, I am writing to you, knowing that you will do even above what I say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:24; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12"> Also, this too, prepare a heimishe mekom linah (guest room, lodging place) for me, for I have the tikvah (hope) that through your tefillos I will be restored to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:12"> Drishat Shalom to you from Epaphras, my co-prisoner in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:10–11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:10"> Drishat Shalom, also, from Markos, Aristarchus, Demas, and Lukas, my fellow po'alim (workers).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:20"> The Chen v'Chesed Hashem of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua be with your neshamah.</span>
