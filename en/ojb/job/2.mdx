<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="IYOV 1:6; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:14; LUKAS 1:19"> Again there was a day when the Bnei HaElohim came to present themselves before Hashem, and HaSatan came also among them to present himself before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:8; IYOV 1:7; BERESHIS 16:8"> And Hashem said unto HaSatan, From where comest thou? And HaSatan answered Hashem, and said, From going to and fro through ha'aretz, and from walking up and down in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; IYOV 1:8; IYOV 27:5–6"> And Hashem said unto HaSatan, Hast thou considered in your meditation Avdi (My Servant) Iyov, that there is none like him in ha'aretz, ish tam and yashar, yire Elohim, and shunning rah? And still he holdeth fast his integrity, although thou inciteth Me against him, to ruin him for no reason.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:8–10; MATTITYAHU 16:26; MATTITYAHU 6:25"> And HaSatan answered Hashem, and said, Skin for skin, yea, all that an ish hath will he give for his nefesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="IYOV 1:11; VAYIKRA 24:15; IYOV 19:20–21"> But stretch forth Thine yad now, and touch his etzem and his basar, and he will curse Thee to Thy face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:13; LUKAS 22:31–32; IYOV 1:12"> And Hashem said unto HaSatan, Hinei, he is in thine yad; but spare his nefesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:35; IYOV 30:30; YESHAYAH 1:6"> So went HaSatan out from the presence of Hashem, and afflicted Iyov with shekhin rah (foul boils) from the sole of his regel unto the top of his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="IYOV 42:6; YECHEZKEL 27:30; YONAH 3:6"> And he took him a cheres (potsherd, piece of broken pottery) to scrape himself withal; and he sat down among the ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="IYOV 2:5; IYOV 2:3; MALACHI 3:14"> Then said his isha unto him, Dost thou still retain thine integrity? Curse Elohim, and die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; EKHAH 3:38–41; IYOV 1:21–22"> But he said unto her, Thou speakest as one of the nevalot (foolish women) speaketh. What? Shall we receive hatov from Elohim, and shall we not accept harah? In all this did not Iyov sin in what he said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:15; IYOV 42:11; YIRMEYAH 49:7"> Now when the three friends of Iyov heard of all this ra'ah that had come upon him, they came every one from his own place; Eliphaz the Temani, and Bildad the Shuchi, and Tzophar the Na'amati; for they had made an appointment together to come to sympathize with him and to comfort him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EKHAH 2:10; YECHEZKEL 27:30; NECHEMYAH 9:1"> And when they lifted up their eyes afar off, and recognized him not, they lifted up their voice, and wept; and they made the keri'ah every one on his mantle, and sprinkled dust upon their heads toward Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 50:10; NECHEMYAH 1:4; YECHEZKEL 3:15"> So they sat &#91;shiva&#93; with him upon the ground shivat yamim and shivat lailah, and none spoke a word unto him; for they saw that his suffering was very great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> After this opened Iyov his mouth, and cursed his yom.</span>
