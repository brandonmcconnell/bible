<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:11; YECHEZKEL 14:14; YECHEZKEL 14:20"> There was an ish in Eretz Utz, shmo Iyov; and that ish was blameless and yashar, and yire Elohim, and shunned evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="TEHILLIM 127:3–5; IYOV 42:13; TEHILLIM 128:3"> And there were born unto him seven banim and shalosh banot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="IYOV 29:25; IYOV 42:12; SHOFETIM 6:3"> His possessions also were seven thousand tzon and three thousand gemalim, and five hundred yoke of oxen, and five hundred she-donkeys, and very many avadim; so that this ish was the greatest of kol bnei kedem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:1; TEHILLIM 133:1"> And his banim went and held a mishteh in their bais, every one in his turn in his bais; and sent and called for their three achayot to eat and to drink with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="IYOV 42:8; BERESHIS 8:20; SHMUEL ALEF 16:5"> And it was so, when the yamim of their mishteh were gone, that Iyov sent and set them apart as kodesh, and rose up early in the boker, and offered olot (burnt offerings) according to the mispar (number) of them all: for Iyov said, It may be that my banim have sinned, and cursed Elohim in their hearts. Thus did Iyov kol hayamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="IYOV 2:1; IYOV 38:7; ZECHARYAH 3:1"> Now there was a yom when the Bnei HaElohim came to present themselves before Hashem, and HaSatan came also among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:8; HISGALUS 12:12–17; MATTITYAHU 12:43"> And Hashem said unto HaSatan, From where comest thou? Then HaSatan answered Hashem, and said, From roaming to and fro in ha'aretz, and from walking up and down in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="IYOV 2:3; IYOV 1:1; BAMIDBAR 12:7–8"> And Hashem said unto HaSatan, Hast thou considered in thy heart avdi Iyov, that there is none like him in ha'aretz, a perfect and a yashar ish, yire Elohim, and shunneth evil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:26; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:8; IYOV 2:10"> Then HaSatan answered Hashem, and said, Is Iyov a yire Elohim (G-d fearer) for nothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 34:7; ZECHARYAH 2:5; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5"> Hast not Thou made a hedge<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="of protection">a</a></sup>about him, and about his bais, and about all that he hath on every side? Thou hast blessed the ma'aseh (work) of his hands, and his possessions are increased in ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="IYOV 2:5; IYOV 19:21; HISGALUS 16:11"> But put forth Thine yad now, and strike all that he hath, and he will curse Thee to Thy face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKAS 22:31–32; YOCHANAN 3:35–36; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:7"> And Hashem said unto HaSatan, Hinei, all that he hath is in thy yad; only upon himself put not forth thine yad. So HaSatan went out from the presence of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 21:34; MISHLE 27:1; IYOV 1:4"> And there was a yom when his banim and his banot were eating and drinking yayin in the bais achihem habechor (the house of their firstborn brother):</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:13; YIRMEYAH 51:31; SHMUEL ALEF 4:17"> And there came a malach (messenger) unto Iyov, and said, The oxen were plowing, and the donkeys feeding beside them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 10:7; IYOV 6:19; YESHAYAH 45:14"> And the Sabeans attacked, and took them away; yea, they have slain the na'arim with the edge of the cherev; and I only am escaped alone to tell thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 1:12; BERESHIS 19:24; MELACHIM ALEF 18:38"> While he was yet speaking, there came also another, and said, The eish Elohim is fallen from Shomayim, and hath burned up the tzon, and the na'arim, and consumed them; and I only am escaped alone to tell thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 11:28; CHABAKUK 1:6; BERESHIS 11:31"> While he was yet speaking, there came also another, and said, The Kasdim (Chaldeans) formed shloshah rashim, and fell upon the gemalim and have carried them away, yea and slain the na'arim with the edge of the cherev; and I only am escaped alone to tell thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="IYOV 1:4; IYOV 1:13; IYOV 27:14"> While he was yet speaking, there came also another, and said, Thy banim and thy banot were eating and drinking yayin in the bais achihem habechor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:36; LUKAS 13:1–5; SHOFETIM 16:30"> And, hinei, there came a ruach gedolah from the midbar, and struck the arbah pinot (four corners) of the bais, and it fell upon the na'arim, and they are dead; and I only am escaped alone to tell thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:6; SHMUEL BAIS 12:16–20; BERESHIS 37:34"> Then Iyov arose, and made the keri'ah on his mantle, and shaved his head, and fell down upon the ground, and worshiped,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="KOHELET 5:15; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:18"> And said, Arom came I out of beten immi, and arom shall I return there; Hashem gave, and Hashem hath taken away; yehi Shem Hashem mevorach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; IYOV 2:10; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7"> In all this Iyov sinned not, nor natan tiflah l'Elohim (charged G-d with folly).</span>
</div>
