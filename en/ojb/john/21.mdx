<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:1; YOCHANAN 6:23; MATTITYAHU 28:16"> After these things Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach manifested himself again to His talmidim at Lake Tiberias. Now Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was manifested thus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:21–22; YOCHANAN 1:45–1; YOCHANAN 4:46"> Together there were Shim'on Kefa and T'oma (his name means Twin) and Natan'el, the one from Kanah in the Galil and the Bnei Zavdai and two others of the talmidim of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKAS 5:5; MATTITYAHU 4:18–20; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:7–9"> Shim'on Kefa says to them, I am going to fish. They say to Shim'on Kefa, We are coming also with you. They went forth and embarked into the sirah. And during that lailah they caught not one thing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:14; LUKAS 24:15–16; LUKAS 24:31"> Now when the beginning of Shacharis had already come, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach stood on the shore. The talmidim had not, however, realized it was he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:11–13; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says, therefore, to them, Yeladim, surely not any dagim (fish) you have? In reply, they said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Lo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKAS 5:4–7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:41; YOCHANAN 2:5"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Throw the reshet (net) to the right side of the sirah (boat), and you will find dagim. Therefore, they threw it. And now they were not strong enough to draw in the reshet (net), because of the multitude of dagim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:23; YOCHANAN 20:2; MATTITYAHU 14:28–29"> Therefore, the talmid ha'ahuv says to Shim'on Kefa, It is Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu! Therefore, Shim'on Kefa, having heard this, strapped his gartel around his kaftan, for he was unclothed, and threw himself into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEVARIM 3:11"> But the other talmidim in the sirah, for they were not far from the land but about two hundred cubits out, came dragging the reshet of the dagim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 21:13; MELACHIM ALEF 19:5–6; YOCHANAN 21:10"> Therefore, when the talmidim disembarked onto the shore, they see a hadlakah (bonfire) there and dagim lying on it and lechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, Bring some from the dagim which you caught now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 5:6–8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:41"> Therefore, Shim'on Kefa went up and dragged the reshet (net) onto the shore, full of large dagim, me'ah vchamishim ushloshah (one hundred and fifty three), and, though being so many, did not split the reshet (net).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:41; LUKAS 9:45; YOCHANAN 16:19"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, Come, eat! Now not one of the talmidim was daring to ask him, Who are you? — having had da'as that it is Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 21:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:41; LUKAS 24:42–43"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach comes and takes the lechem and gives to them, likewise the dagim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:26; YOCHANAN 20:19"> This was now the shlishit (third) time Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, having been made to stand up alive from the mesim, was manifested to the talmidim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:11; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:1"> Then when they ate, he says to Shim'on Kefa, Shim'on Bar-Yochanan, do you have ahavah for me more than these? Shim'on Kefa says to him, Ken, Adoni, You have da'as that I have ahavah for You. He says to him, Feed My lambs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:28; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:2; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:25"> He again a second time says to him, Shim'on Bar-Yochanan, do you have ahavah for me? Shim'on Kefa says to him, Ken, Adoni, You have da'as that I have ahavah for You. He says to him, Take care<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="as a ro'eh (shepherd) would">a</a></sup>of My sheep.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2Sm 5:2; Ezek 34:2; Ps 78:71-72">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:38; YOCHANAN 16:30; MARKOS 14:72"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to him the shlishit time, Shim'on Bar-Yochanan, do you have ahavah for me? Kefa was grieved because Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to him the shlishit time, Do you have ahavah for me? And he says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Adoni, You have da'as of all things. You have da'as that I have ahavah for You. Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to him, Feed My kevasim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:36; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:3–4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:11"> Omein, omein, I say to you, that when you were young, you were girding yourself and were walking where you wanted, but when you grow old you will extend your hands and another will gird you and carry you where you do not wish to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:14; YOCHANAN 21:22; MATTITYAHU 10:38"> And this He said signifying by what mavet Shim'on Kefa will glorify Hashem. After this, He said to him, Follow Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YOCHANAN 21:7; YOCHANAN 20:2; YOCHANAN 21:24"> Having turned, Kefa sees the talmid ha'ahuv following them, who also was reclining at tish during the seudah upon the kheyk of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, and this talmid was the one who had said Adoni, who is the one betraying you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKAS 13:23–24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:6–7; MATTITYAHU 24:3–4"> Then Kefa, having seen this one, says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Adoni, and what about this man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HISGALUS 2:25; HISGALUS 3:11; MARKOS 9:1"> He says to him, If I want him to remain until I come, what is that to you? You follow Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEVARIM 29:29; IYOV 33:13; DANIEL 4:35"> Therefore, a rumor went out saying to the achim B'Moshiach that that talmid would not die. But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach did not tell him that he would not die, but rather, If I want him to remain until I come, what is that to you?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:35; YOCHANAN 15:27; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:6"> This is the talmid, the one giving solemn edut (testimony) about these things, and the one having written these things and we have da'as that his edut is emes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="TEHILLIM 40:5; YOCHANAN 20:30–31; TEHILLIM 71:15"> And there are many other things which Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach did, which if they are written one by one, not the Olam Hazeh itself I suppose would have room enough for the sfarim being written.</span>
</div>
