<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:39; YOCHANAN 7:47–50; YOCHANAN 3:10"> Now there was a man of the Perushim. His name was Rav Nakdimon, a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Sanhedrist">a</a></sup>katzin (leader) of the Yehudim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:36; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:38; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:22"> This one came to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach under cover of lailah and said to him, Rabbi, we have da'as that you are a moreh from Hashem having come, for no one is able these otot (miraculous signs) to do, which you do, unless Hashem is with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:17; YOCHANAN 3:5–6; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:23–25"> In reply, he said to him, Omein, omein, I say to you, unless someone is born anew,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="born again, Yn 1:13; Dt 10:16; 30:6; Jer 4:4; Isa 52:1; Ezek 44:7,9">b</a></sup>he is not able to see the Malchut Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:18; YOCHANAN 3:3"> Rav Nakdimon says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, How is a man, being old, able to be born? Surely he is not able into the womb of immo a second time to enter and to be born?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 36:25–27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:38"> In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Omein, omein, I say to you: unless someone is born of mayim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 36:9[10]">c</a></sup>and Ruach Hakodesh,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ezek 36:25-27; 37:14">d</a></sup>he is not able to enter into the Malchut Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Lk 17:21">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:16–21; YECHEZKEL 36:26–27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:4–9"> That which is born of basar is basar, and that which is born of the Ruach is ruach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:22–24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:1–2"> Do not marvel that I said to you, It is necessary for you to be born again, born anew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="KOHELET 11:4–5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:2"> The ruach (wind, Spirit) blows where it wishes, and the sound of it you hear, but you do not have da'as of where it comes from and where it goes;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Koh 11:5">f</a></sup>so it is with everyone having been born of the Ruach Hakodesh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ezek 37:9">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:60; LUKAS 1:34; YESHAYAH 42:16"> In reply, Rav Nakdimon said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, How is it possible for these things to happen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 22:29; YIRMEYAH 8:8–9; YECHEZKEL 11:19"> In reply, he said to him, You hold the teaching office of rabbi, of moreh b'Yisroel and of these things you do not have da'as?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:38; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:6–12; YOCHANAN 12:49"> Omein, omein, I say to you, that of which we have da'as we speak, and of that which we have seen, we give solemn edut (testimony), and the solemn edut of us you &#91;pl.&#93; do not receive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:1–2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:7–9; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:11"> If I told you &#91;pl.&#93; about things of the Olam Hazeh and you have no emunah, how will you have emunah if I tell you about the things of the Olam Haba?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MISHLE 30:4; YOCHANAN 6:38; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:34"> And no one has ascended into Shomayim except the one having descended out of Shomayim, the Bar Enosh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Dan 7:13-14; Prov 30:4; Dt 30:12">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:7–9; YOCHANAN 8:28; YOCHANAN 12:32–34"> And as Moshe lifted up the nachash in the wilderness,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Num 21:8-9">i</a></sup>so it is necessary for there to be a hagbah (lifting up) of the Bar Enosh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ps 22; Isa 53">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:16; YOCHANAN 6:40; YOCHANAN 12:44–46"> That everyone having emunah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gn 15:1-6; Num 14:11; Ex 14:31">k</a></sup>in him may have Chayyei Olam.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Dan 12:2; Isa 52:13">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:8; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:9–10; YOCHANAN 3:15"> For Hashem so had ahavah (agape) for the Olam Hazeh that Hashem gave the matanah (gift)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Isa 9:6[5]">m</a></sup>of Hashemʼs Ben Yechid,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gn 22:12; Prov 30:4; 8:30">n</a></sup>so that whosoever has emunah in him may not be ne'evad (lost, perish, be ruined with destruction), but find Chayyei Olam.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Dan 12:2">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:14; LUKAS 19:10; YOCHANAN 12:47–48"> For Hashem did not send the Ben HaElohim into the Olam Hazeh that he might judge the Olam Hazeh, but that the Olam Hazeh might be brought to the Geulah (Redemption) of the Olam Haba through him (Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Isa 53:11">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MARKOS 16:16; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:1"> The one of emunah who is mekabel Moshiach (accepting the person of the Bar Enosh, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach) is not judged; but the one without emunah who is not mekabel Moshiach, already has been judged and given the psak din (verdict) of “condemned,” because he has no emunah in the Shem of the Ben Yachid of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:12; YOCHANAN 9:39–41"> And this is the psak din, that the Ohr has come into the Olam Hazeh, and Bnei Adam had ahavah for the choshech rather than for the Ohr, for their ma'asim were ra'im (evil).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Ps 52:3">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:11–13; YOCHANAN 7:7; IYOV 24:13–17"> For everyone walking in the derech resha'im has sin'as chinom (baseless hatred) for the Ohr and does not come to the Ohr, lest his ma'asim be exposed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Ps 1:6; Job 24:13-17">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:6; TEHILLIM 139:23–24; (III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:11"> But the one doing HaEmes comes to the Ohr that his ma'asim hamitzot may be manifested, that they have been wrought in Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:1–3; YOCHANAN 3:26; YOCHANAN 2:13"> After these things Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and his talmidim came into the land of Yehudah, and there he was staying with them and was giving the mikveh mayimʼs tevilah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MARKOS 1:4–5; YECHEZKEL 43:2; MATTITYAHU 3:5–6"> And Yochanan was also giving the tevilah near Shalem at Einayim, because there was plenty of water there for a mikveh, and the Yehudim were coming and submitting to the tevilah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:12; MATTITYAHU 14:3; MARKOS 6:17"> Yochanan had not yet been incarcerated in the beit hasohar (prison).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YOCHANAN 2:6; MATTITYAHU 3:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:2"> There was then a discussion of Yochananʼs talmidim with one of the Yehudim about the subject of tohorah (purification).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:7; YOCHANAN 1:15; YOCHANAN 12:19"> And they came to Yochanan and said to him, Rabbi, he who was with you beyond the Yarden, the one to whom you have given solemn edut, hinei, this one gives the mikveh mayimʼs tevilah and kol Bnei Adam come to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:10"> In reply, Yochanan said, A man is not able to receive anything, unless it has been given to him from Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:20; YOCHANAN 1:23; MATTITYAHU 3:11–12"> You yourselves stand as eidus (witness) to me, that I said I am not the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, but I have been sent gevorn forois before him (forerunner).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Mal 3:1">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:15; HOSHEA 2:19; YESHAYAH 54:5"> The one having the Kallah (bride) is the Choson (Bridegroom),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="i.e., Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">t</a></sup>but the Shoshvin (the Chosonʼs best man), the one having stood and hearing him with simcha, rejoices because of the kol (voice) of the Choson (Bridegroom). Therefore, this simcha of mine has been made sheleimah (complete).</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:18; TEHILLIM 72:17–19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:2–6"> It is necessary for that one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">u</a></sup>to increase, but for me to decrease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:23; MATTITYAHU 28:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:5"> The one coming from above (Moshiach) is over all; the one being from Olam Hazeh is of Olam Hazeh and of the Olam Hazeh he speaks; Hu Habah (He who comes),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="i.e., Moshiach">v</a></sup>from Shomayim is over all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:11; YOCHANAN 15:15; YOCHANAN 1:11"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach gives solemn edut (testimony) to that which he has seen and heard, yet no one receives his eidus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:13; LETTER TO TITOS 1:1–2"> The one who is mekabel Moshiachʼs eidus has thereby set his chotam of attestation that Hashem is Emes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:38; MATTITYAHU 12:18; YOCHANAN 15:26"> For he whom Hashem sent speaks the Divrei Hashem, for He gives the Ruach Hakodesh without measure.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Isa 42:1">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:18; YOCHANAN 5:22; YOCHANAN 5:20"> Elohim HaAv (the Father) has ahavah (love) for HaBen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Prov 30:4; 8:30">x</a></sup>and has given all things into his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:24; YOCHANAN 1:12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:9"> The one with emunah in the Ben &#91;HaElohim&#93; has Chayyei Olam; but the one disobeying the Ben &#91;HaElohim&#93; will not see Chayyim, but the Charon Af Hashem remains on him.</span>
