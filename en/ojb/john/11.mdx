<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 10:38–42; YOCHANAN 12:17; MELACHIM BAIS 20:1–12"> Now there was a certain man, a choleh (sick man), El'azar from Beit-Anyah (Bethany), the shtetl of Miryam and Marta her achot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:3; MARKOS 14:3; MATTITYAHU 26:6–7"> Now this was the same Miryam that anointed Adoneinu with mishcha<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="12:3">a</a></sup>(ointment) and wiped his feet with her hair. It was she whose ach El'azar was choleh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:5; HISGALUS 3:19; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:20"> Therefore, the achayot (sisters) sent to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, saying, Adoni, hinei, the one who is your chaver haahuv is choleh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:40; YOCHANAN 9:3; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:14"> And having heard this, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, This machla (illness, krankeit) is not unto mavet (death), but for the kavod Hashem, that the Ben HaElohim may receive kavod through it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:9–13; YOCHANAN 16:27; YOCHANAN 17:26"> Now Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach had ahavah for Marta, and her achot and El'azar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YESHAYAH 30:18; YESHAYAH 55:8–9; BERESHIS 45:1–5"> When, therefore, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach heard that El'azar was choleh, then he remained in the place where he was yomayim (two days).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:40–42; LUKAS 9:51; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:22–24"> Then after this he says to his talmidim, Let us go into the land of Yehudah again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:31; YOCHANAN 8:59; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:12–13"> His talmidim say to him, Rebbe, just now those of the land of Yehudah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Judeans">b</a></sup>were seeking to stone you, and you go there again?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:4; YOCHANAN 12:35; YIRMEYAH 31:9"> In reply, he said, Are there not Sheneym Asar (Twelve) sha'ot (hours) of HaYom? If the derech of anyone is a derech by Yom, he does not stumble, because the Ohr HaOlam Hazeh he sees.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Jer 13:16; Ps 1:6">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:10–11; MISHLE 4:18–19; YIRMEYAH 13:16"> But if the derech of anyone is a derech baLailah, he stumbles because the Ohr is not in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DANIEL 12:2; YOCHANAN 11:13; YOCHANAN 11:43–44"> These things he said. Then, after this, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, El'azar our chaver haahuv has fallen asleep, but I am setting out to awaken him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Therefore, the talmidim said to him, Adoni, if he has fallen asleep, he will be granted refu'ah (healing).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:24"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach had spoken about the mavet of El'azar. However, those ones supposed that he was speaking about only shlof (sleep).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:25; YOCHANAN 16:29; YOCHANAN 10:24"> Then, therefore, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach told them plainly, El'azar died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:10; YOCHANAN 14:10–11; YESHAYAH 54:15"> And I have lev same'ach for your sake, that I was not there, that you may have emunah. But let us go to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:37; MATTITYAHU 10:3; YOCHANAN 21:2"> Therefore, T'oma — his name means Twin — said to his fellow talmidim, Let us go also, that we may die<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="al kiddush HaShem">d</a></sup>with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:39; HOSHEA 6:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:27–31"> Having arrived, therefore, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach discovered El'azar had already been in the kever (tomb) arba'ah yamim (four days).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HISGALUS 14:20; HISGALUS 21:16; YOCHANAN 6:19"> Now Beit-Anyah (Bethany) was near Yerushalayim, about fifteen stadia away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="IYOV 2:11; YOCHANAN 11:31; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:11"> And many of those of Yehudah had come to Marta and Miryam to give them nechamah (consolation, comfort) regarding the ach of the achayot.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Job 2:11">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKAS 10:38–42; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:17; MATTITYAHU 25:1"> Therefore, Marta when she heard that Yehoshua comes, met him; but Miryam was sitting shivah in the bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:32; MELACHIM ALEF 17:18; MATTITYAHU 9:18"> Therefore, Marta said to Yehoshua, Adoni, if you were here, achi (my brother) would not have passed away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:31; YOCHANAN 11:41–42; MATTITYAHU 28:18"> But even now I have da'as that whatever you ask Hashem, Hashem will give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:43–44"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to her, Achich yakum (Your brother will stand up).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="up from the Mesim">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DANIEL 12:2–3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:15; YOCHANAN 5:28–29"> Marta says to Yehoshua, I have da'as that he will rise in the Techiyas HaMesim on the Yom HaAcharon (Last Day).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Dan 12:2">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:14; YOCHANAN 3:36; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:11–12"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to her, Ani Hu the Tekhiyyah and the Chayyim (Life): the one having emunah in me, even if he should die, will live,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:28; YOCHANAN 5:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:13"> and everyone living and having emunah in me, never dies. Do you have emunah in this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:16; YOCHANAN 6:69; YOCHANAN 6:14"> She says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Ken, Adoni. I have emunah that you are the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, the Ben HaElohim, Hu Habah el HaOlam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:20; YOCHANAN 13:13; MARKOS 10:49"> And having said this, she withdrew and summoned Miryam her achot, telling her privately, Rabbeinu and Moreinu is here, and summons you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MISHLE 15:23; TEHILLIM 27:8; SHIR HASHIRIM 3:1–4"> And that isha, when she heard, got up quickly and was coming to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:20"> Now he had not yet come into the shtetl, but was still in the place where Marta met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:19; BERESHIS 37:35; SHMUEL BAIS 12:16–18"> Therefore, those of Yehudah, the ones sitting shiva with her in the bais and giving her nechamah, when they saw Miryam get up quickly and rush out, followed her, assuming that she is going to the kever (tomb) that she might weep there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:21; LUKAS 17:16; LUKAS 5:8"> Therefore, Miryam, when she came where Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was, and when she saw him, fell down at his feet, saying to Rebbe, Adoni, if you were here, achi (my brother) would not have died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:38; YOCHANAN 12:27; MARKOS 3:5"> Therefore, when he saw her weeping and those of Yehudah coming with her and also weeping, he was deeply moved in his neshamah and was troubled in himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MARKOS 15:47; MATTITYAHU 28:6; YOCHANAN 1:39"> And said, Where have you laid him? They said to him, Adoni, come and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LUKAS 19:41; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:15; YOCHANAN 11:33"> Yehoshua wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:3; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:1; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:9–10"> Therefore, those of Yehudah were saying, Hinei! See how much ahavah he had for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:6–7; YOCHANAN 11:32; LUKAS 23:39"> But some of them said, Could not this man, who was able to open the eyes of the ivver, have also prevented this man from passing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:33; LUKAS 24:2; MATTITYAHU 27:60"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, therefore, again being deeply moved within himself, comes to the kever (tomb). Now it was a me'arah (cave) type of kever and a stone was lying against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:17; MARKOS 16:3; TEHILLIM 49:7"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says, Lift the stone. Marta, the achot of the niftar (deceased), says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Adoni, already he smells, for it is now arba'ah yamim (four days).</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:4; YOCHANAN 11:23–26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:17–25"> He says to her, Did I not tell you that if you have emunah (faith), you will see the kavod Hashem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:25; YOCHANAN 17:1; TEHILLIM 123:1"> Therefore, they lifted the stone and he lifted up his eyes and said, Avi, I thank You that You heard me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:17; YOCHANAN 17:21; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:14"> And I knew that You always hear me, but, because of the multitude standing around, I said it, that they may have emunah (faith) that You sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:33–36; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:40; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:34"> And having said these things, he cried out with a kol gadol, El'azar, come forth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:25; YOCHANAN 20:7; YOCHANAN 11:25–26"> Then the niftar, having been bound feet and hands with the linen clothes of the tachrichin, and with his face covered with a mitznefet (head wrapping), came out. He says to them, Untie him and let him go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="YOCHANAN 2:23; YOCHANAN 11:19; YOCHANAN 10:41"> Therefore, many of those of Yehudah, the ones having come to Miryam and having seen the things which Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach did, put their emunah in him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ex 14:31">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:25; YOCHANAN 12:37"> But some of them went away to the Perushim and reported to them everything Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:3; YOCHANAN 12:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:5–6"> Therefore, the Rashei Hakohanim and the Perushim called a meeting of the Sanhedrin and were saying, What are we doing, for this man is performing many otot (miraculous signs)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:15–16; MATTITYAHU 23:35–38; MATTITYAHU 27:25"> If we leave him thus, kol Bnei Adam will have emunah (faith) in him, and the Romans will come and will take away from us both the Beis Hamikdash and the nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:3; YOCHANAN 11:51; YOCHANAN 18:13–14"> But a certain one of them, Caiapha, being Kohen Gadol that year, said to them, You do not have da'as of anything,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:14; LUKAS 24:46; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:8"> Nor do you consider that it is a bedievedike (expedient) thing for you that one man should die on behalf of HaAm and not that all the nation should perish.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Jon 1:12-15; Isa 53:8">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="DANIEL 9:26; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:21; SHEMOT 28:30"> But this he did not utter from himself, but, being Kohen Gadol that year, he uttered a dvar hanevu'ah that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was about to die on behalf of the nation.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ex 28:30; Num 27:21; Isa 53:8">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:16; YESHAYAH 49:6; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:2"> And not on behalf of Am Yisroel only but also the yeladim of Hashem, the ones having been scattered, that he may gather into echad.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Isa 49:6">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:33; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:23; MARKOS 14:1"> From that Yom on, therefore, they planned that they might put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:1; YOCHANAN 7:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 13:19"> Therefore he no longer was walking openly among those of Yehudah, but went away from there into the region near the wilderness, to a shtetl called Ephrayim, and there Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach remained with the talmidim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="2Sm 13:23">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:17–20; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:13–14; YOCHANAN 6:4"> Now Pesach was near and many went up to Yerushalayim from the country before the Pesach for the purpose of HITTEHARU (they purified themselves).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="2Chr 30:18">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:11; YOCHANAN 11:7–8"> They were seeking, therefore, Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach and were saying to one another while they stood in the Beis Hamikdash, What does it appear to you? Surely he will not come to the Chag (Feast)!</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:22; YOCHANAN 8:59; YOCHANAN 5:16–18"> Now the Rashei Hakohanim and the Perushim had given orders that if anyone had da'as of where he is, he should reveal it so that they might arrest him.</span>
