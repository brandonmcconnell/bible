<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARKOS 16:9; MATTITYAHU 27:60; YOCHANAN 19:25"> Now on the Yom Rishon, Miryam of Magdala comes early, while it was still dark, to the kever (tomb) and sees the stone having been taken away from the kever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:23; YOCHANAN 20:13; YOCHANAN 21:24"> Miryam of Magdala runs therefore and comes to Shim'on Kefa and to the talmid ha'ahuv and says to them, They have taken Adoneinu from the kever (tomb) and we do not have da'as where they laid him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKAS 24:12"> Therefore, Shim'on Kefa went forth and the other talmid, and they were coming to the kever (tomb),</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 18:23; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:24"> And were running, the two together and the other talmid ran ahead faster than Kefa, and came first to the kever,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:40; YOCHANAN 20:11; YOCHANAN 11:44"> And having stooped down, the other talmid<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="talmid ha'ahuv">a</a></sup>sees the tachrichin linen clothes lying there. However, he did not enter the kever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:17; YOCHANAN 21:7; MATTITYAHU 16:15–16"> Then comes also Shim'on Kefa following him and Shim'on Kefa entered into the kever (tomb), and sees the tachrichin linen clothes lying there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:44"> And also the mitznefet (head wrapping), which had been upon the rosh of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, the mitznefet not lying with the tachrichin, but apart, having been folded up in one place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:25; YOCHANAN 20:4; YOCHANAN 1:50"> Then, therefore, entered also the other talmid, the one having come first to the kever (tomb), and he saw and had emunah (faith).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 16:10; YESHAYAH 25:8; YESHAYAH 53:10–12"> For they did not yet have da'as of the Kitvei Hakodesh that it is necessary for Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach to have his Techiyas HaMoshiach.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 16:9; Isa 53:10">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:53; YOCHANAN 16:32"> Then the talmidim went away again to their own homes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:5"> Now Miryam had stood at the kever (tomb) outside weeping. Then, as she was weeping, she bent down into the kever (tomb),</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKAS 24:3–7; HISGALUS 3:4; MARKOS 16:5–6"> And sees two malachim (angels) in white, sitting, one at the head and one at the feet, where the Guf of Yehoshua had previously been lying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:15; YOCHANAN 2:4; SHMUEL ALEF 1:8"> And those ones say to her, Isha, why do you weep? She says to them, They took Adoni, and I don't have da'as of where they placed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 24:16; YOCHANAN 21:4; MATTITYAHU 28:9"> When she had said these things, she turned back around, and she sees Yehoshua standing there, and she did not have da'as that it was Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:13; YOCHANAN 18:7; YOCHANAN 1:38"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to her, Isha, why do you weep? Whom do you seek? Miryam, supposing that he was the shomer hagahn (the keeper of the garden), says to him, Adon, if you carried him away, tell me where you placed him, and I'll take him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:3; YOCHANAN 1:38; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:3"> He says to her, Miryam. She turns and she says to him in Ivrit, Rabboni. (This means Mori &#91;my teacher&#93;.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:8–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:6–7"> He says to her, Do not hold on to me, for I have not yet made the aliyah ascent to HaAv; but go to my Achim and tell them, I make the aliyah ascent to Avi and to Avichem, to Elohai and Eloheichem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 22:23">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKAS 24:10; MATTITYAHU 28:10; YOCHANAN 20:1"> Miryam from Magdala comes announcing to the talmidim, I have seen HaAdon.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Mal 3:1">d</a></sup>And she told them that He had said to her these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:26; YOCHANAN 14:27; YOCHANAN 20:21"> On that Yom Rishon, when it was erev, and the delatot (doors) having been shut where the talmidim were, because of fear of those of Yehudah, then came Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and stood in the midst and says to them, Shalom aleichem!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:22; YOCHANAN 16:20; YOCHANAN 20:27"> And having said this, He showed his hands and his side to them. Therefore, the talmidim were filled with simcha at having seen HaAdon.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Mal 3:1">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YOCHANAN 17:18–19; YOCHANAN 13:20; YOCHANAN 3:17"> Therefore He said to them again, Shalom aleichem! As HaAv has sent Me, so also I send you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gn 2:7; Ezek 37:9">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="IYOV 33:4; BERESHIS 2:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:15"> And having said this, Moshiach breathed on them and says to them, Receive the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 18:18; MATTITYAHU 16:19; MARKOS 2:5–10"> If you grant selicha to the averos of any, they have selicha; if there be any whose averos you retain, they are retained.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Mt 16:19; 18:18; Ac 8:17-24">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:16; YOCHANAN 6:66–67; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:25"> But T'oma, one of the Sheneym Asar, the one being called Didymus, was not with them when He came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:30; MARKOS 16:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:12"> Therefore the other talmidim were saying to him, Ra'i'nu es Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu! (We have seen Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu!) But T'oma said to them, Unless I see in his hands the mark of the nails and I put my finger into the place of the nails and also put my hand into his side, I will never have emunah (faith).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:19; YESHAYAH 54:10; YOCHANAN 20:21"> And after shmonah yamim (eight days) again the talmidim of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach were inside, and T'oma with them. Although the delatot were shut, He comes and stood in the midst and said, Shalom Aleichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:25; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:1–2; YOCHANAN 20:20"> Then He says to T'oma, Bring your finger here and see My hands, and bring your hand and put it into My side, and do not be without emunah but be a ma'amin (Messianic believer).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:1; YESHAYAH 9:6; YESHAYAH 7:14"> In reply, T'oma said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Adoni and Elohai!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ps 35:23">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:1"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to him, Because you have seen Me, you have emunah (faith)? Ashrey (Happy) are the ones not having seen and having emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YOCHANAN 21:25; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:15–17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:4"> Therefore, many other otot (miraculous signs) Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach also did before the talmidim, which have not been written in this sefer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:43; YOCHANAN 3:15–16; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:1"> But these things have been written that you might have emunah that Yehoshua is the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, the Ben HaElohim, and that, believing with emunah, you may have Chayyim (Life) b'Shem of Him.</span>
