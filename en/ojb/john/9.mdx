<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And passing along, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach saw a man ivver (blind) from birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKAS 13:2; YOCHANAN 9:34; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:4"> The talmidim of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach asked him, Rebbe, who committed averos, this man or the horim (parents) of him, that he was born ivver?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ezek 18:20; Ex 20:5; Job 21:19">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:4; YOCHANAN 11:40; KOHELET 9:1–2"> In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Neither this man sinned nor the horim of him, but that the pe'ulot of Hashem may be manifested in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:35; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:16; YOCHANAN 4:34"> It is necessary for us to work the pe'ulot of the One having sent me while it is Yom. Lailah comes when no one is able to work.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Jer 13:16">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:12; YOCHANAN 12:46; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:47"> As long as I am in the Olam Hazeh, I am the Ohr HaOlam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MARKOS 8:23; MARKOS 7:33; HISGALUS 3:18"> Having said these things, he spat on the ground and made clay with the saliva and he anointed the clay upon the manʼs eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YESHAYAH 35:5; YOCHANAN 9:11; YOCHANAN 11:37"> And said to him, Go wash in the pool of Shiloach!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2Kgs 5:10">c</a></sup>— The name means sent — He went therefore and washed and came seeing.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 35:5">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 2:8; SHMUEL ALEF 21:11; LUKAS 18:35"> Therefore, the shchenim and the ones seeing the man who was formerly a betler, came, saying, Is this not the man who was sitting and begging?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Some were saying, This is the one! Others were saying, No, but it is a man like him. But the man was saying, I am the one!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:26; YOCHANAN 9:21; KOHELET 11:5"> Therefore, the people were saying to him, How, then, were your eyes opened?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:6–7; YOCHANAN 9:27; YIRMEYAH 36:17"> In reply, the man said, The one called Yehoshua took clay and anointed my eyes and said to me, Go to Shiloach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2Kgs 5:10">e</a></sup>and wash. Therefore, having gone, and having washed, I saw!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:11–13; SHEMOT 2:18–20; YOCHANAN 7:11"> And they said to him, Where is that one? The man says to them, I do not have da'as of that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:57; YOCHANAN 11:46–47; YOCHANAN 12:42"> The people lead the man to the Perushim…the man who had formerly been ivver (blind).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:9; LUKAS 13:10–17; MATTITYAHU 12:1–14"> Now the time period Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach took the clay and opened his eyes was a Shabbos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:26–27; YOCHANAN 9:10–11"> Then again also the Perushim were asking him how his eyes were opened, and the man said to them, The man placed clay on my eyes and I washed, and I see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:43; YOCHANAN 10:19; MATTITYAHU 12:2"> Therefore some of the Perushim were saying, This man is not from Hashem, because he is Mechallel Shabbos (desecrating Shabbos). But others were saying, How is a man who is a choteh (sinner) able to do such otot? There was a machloket (division of dissension) among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:19; YOCHANAN 6:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:22–26"> Therefore, they say to the ivver (blind man) again, What do you say about him, because he opened your eyes? And the man said, He is a Navi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:15–19; YOCHANAN 5:44; YESHAYAH 26:11"> Therefore, those of Yehudah did not believe that the man had been ivver (blind) until they called the horim of the man whose eyes were opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:8–9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:10"> And they asked the horim, saying, Is this the ben of you, whom you say was born ivver? How, therefore, does he now have sight?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> In reply, therefore, his horim said, We have da'as that this man is the ben of us and that he was born ivver,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But how he sees now we do not have da'as nor do we have da'as of who opened his eyes. Interrogate him. Heʼs a &#91;Bar Mitzvah&#93; bar da'as (he is of age). He will speak for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:13; YOCHANAN 16:2; LUKAS 6:22"> His horim said these things, because they were fearing those of Yehudah, for already those of Yehudah had agreed that if any person made hoda'ah (confession) of him to be the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, that person would be put under cherem ban from the shul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:21"> Therefore, his horim said, He has reached his religious majority and is of age. Interrogate him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 7:19; YOCHANAN 9:16; YOCHANAN 8:49"> They called a second time, therefore, the man who had been ivver (blind) and said to him, V'ten lo todah (Give glory to G-d).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Josh 7:19">f</a></sup>We have da'as that this man is a choteh (sinner).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ps 68:35; Josh 7:19">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:10; YOCHANAN 9:30; YOCHANAN 5:11"> In reply, therefore, he said, If he is a choteh (sinner), of that I don't have da'as. Of one thing I do have da'as, that though I was ivver, now I see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> They said, therefore, to him, What did he do to you? How did he open your eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKAS 22:67; YOCHANAN 9:10–15"> The man answered them, I told you already, and you do not listen. Why again do you want to hear? Surely you do not want to become his talmidim also?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:17; YOCHANAN 7:19; MATTITYAHU 27:39"> And they reviled him and said, You are a talmid of that man, but we are talmidim of Moshe Rabbeinu.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Num 12:2,8">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:14; BAMIDBAR 12:2–7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:35"> We have da'as that Hashem has spoken to Moshe Rabbeinu, but this man, we do not have da'as from where he comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:37; TEHILLIM 119:18; YESHAYAH 29:18"> In reply, the man said to them, Here is a real cheftza! That you do not have da'as from where he comes, and he opened my eyes!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MISHLE 15:29; YESHAYAH 1:15; MISHLE 28:9"> We have da'as that G-d does not listen to chote'im (sinners), but if anyone has chassidus, is a yore-shomayim and does the ratzon (will) of Him, this one Hashem hears.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ps 18:23-32; 34:15,16; 66:18; 145:19-20; 51:5[7]; Prov 15:8,29; Isa 1:15; 59:1-2">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YESHAYAH 64:4; HISGALUS 16:18; IYOV 20:4"> Never vi-bahlt the Bri'at HaOlam (the Creation of the World) it was heard of that anyone opened the eyes of an ivver (blind man) having been born thus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:16; YOCHANAN 3:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:38–39"> If this man was not from Hashem, he would not be able to do anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:22; YOCHANAN 9:2; YESHAYAH 66:5"> They answered and said to him, You were born totally B'AVON (in sin)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ps 51:5[7]">j</a></sup>and you teach us? And they threw him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:31; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:13; YOCHANAN 10:36"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach heard that they threw him out, and, having found him, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Do you have emunah (faith) in the Bar Enosh?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Dan 7:13">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:14; SHIR HASHIRIM 5:9; YOCHANAN 1:38"> In reply, the man said, And who is he, Adoni, that I may have emunah in him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:26; TEHILLIM 25:8–9; YOCHANAN 14:21–23"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, You have seen him, and the one speaking with you is he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 14:33; MATTITYAHU 28:9; LUKAS 24:52"> And the man said, Ani ma'amin, Adoni. And he fell down prostrate before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:19; YOCHANAN 12:46; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:18"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, For the sake of Mishpat, I came into the Olam Hazeh, that the ones who are ivrim (blind ones) may see, and the ones seeing may become ivrim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:17; MATTITYAHU 15:12–14; YOCHANAN 7:47–52"> Some of the Perushim heard these things, the ones being with him, and they said to him, Surely we are not ivrim, are we?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YESHAYAH 5:21; YOCHANAN 15:22–24; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8–10"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, If you were ivrim (blind men), you would not have chet, but now vi-bahlt (since) you say, We see, the avon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Psalm 51">l</a></sup>of you remains.</span>
