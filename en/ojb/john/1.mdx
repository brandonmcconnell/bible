<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BERESHIS 1:1; YOCHANAN 17:5; HISGALUS 19:13"> Bereshis (in the Beginning) was the Dvar Hashem,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Isa 55:11; Gn 1:3">a</a></sup>and the Dvar Hashem was agav (along with, etzel)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Prov 8:30; 30:4">b</a></sup>Hashem, and the Dvar Hashem was nothing less, by nature, than Elohim!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 56:10[11]; Yn 17:5; Rv 19:13i.e., the Ma'amar Memra">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Bereshis (in the Beginning) this Dvar Hashem was with Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Prov 8:30">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:16–17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:6; HISGALUS 4:11"> All things through him came to be, and without him came to be not one thing which came into being.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ps 33:6,9; Prov 30:4">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:12; YOCHANAN 12:46; YOCHANAN 5:26"> In him was Chayyim (Life) and the Chayyim (Life) was the Ohr (Light) of Bnei Adam.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ps 36:9[10]">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:19–20; YOCHANAN 12:36–40; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:14"> And the Ohr shines in the choshech,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ps 18:28">g</a></sup>and the choshech did not grasp it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isa 9:1">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MALACHI 3:1; YOCHANAN 3:28; YESHAYAH 40:3–5"> There came an ish haElohim (a man of G-d), having been sent from Hashem. His name was Yochanan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:4; YOCHANAN 1:36; YOCHANAN 5:33–35"> This Yochanan came for an eidus (witness), that he might give solemn edut (testimony) about the Ohr, that kol Bnei Adam might have emunah through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:20; YOCHANAN 3:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:4"> This ish haElohim was not the Ohr, but he came that he might give solemn edut (testimony) about the Ohr.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:46; YOCHANAN 1:4; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:8"> The Ohr, the Ohr HaAmitti (the True Light), which gives rational haskalah (enlightenment) to kol Bnei Adam (all mankind), was coming into the Olam Hazeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YOCHANAN 17:25; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:8"> He was in the Olam Hazeh, the Olam (world) came to be through him;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ps 33:6,9">i</a></sup>yet the Olam Hazeh did not recognize him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 19:14; YESHAYAH 53:2–3; YOCHANAN 3:32"> He came to his own, and his own were not mekabel (accepting) the Kabbalus HaMalchus of him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Isa 53:3">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:14; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:1"> But as many as him lekabel pnei Moshiach (receive him as Moshiach), to them he gave the tokef (authority) to become in fact yeladim haElohim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Dt 14:1">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:18; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:23"> He gave this tokef to the ones whose being born was not by the agency of natural descent, nor by the ratzon (will) of basar (fallen human nature), nor by the ratzon of a gever (male) — rather, to the ones born of G-d.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Yn 3:3,7">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:9; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:16; YESHAYAH 40:5"> And the Dvar Hashem did mitgashem (become bodily) and made his Sukkah, his Mishkan (Tabernacle) among us,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Isa 7:14">m</a></sup>and we<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Shlichim, 1Y 1:1-2">n</a></sup>gazed upon his Shekhinah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Ex 33:18; 40:34; Isa 60:1-2">o</a></sup>the Shekhinah of the Ben Yachid from Elohim HaAv, full of Hashemʼs Chesed v'Emes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 3:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:17; LUKAS 3:16"> And Yochanan gives solemn edut (testimony) about him and has cried out, This was he about whom I said, Hu HaBah (He who comes)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Gn 49:10; Ezek 21:27">p</a></sup>after me is really before me in priority, because, before I came to be, he was.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Yn 8:58">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:5–10"> For from the kol melo (all the plentitude) of him we all received Chesed upon Chesed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:14; YOCHANAN 14:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:19–26"> Because the matan Torah (giving of the Torah) was graciously bestowed through Moshe,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Rabbeinu; Dt 32:46; Ex 31:18; 34:28">r</a></sup>but Chesed and Emes of Hashem came through &#91;Rebbe&#93;, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Ex 34:6; Ps 25:10; 40:11; 85:11; Yn 1:49">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:15; YOCHANAN 6:46; MATTITYAHU 11:27"> No one has ever seen Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Ex 33:20">t</a></sup>It is Elohim the Ben Yachid,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="who shares the nature of Hashem, the Chochmah Ben Elohim at his side, see very importantly Prov 8:30; 30:4">u</a></sup>it is he, the one being in the kheyk (bosom) of HaAv, this one is Hashemʼs definitive midrash (exegesis).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKAS 3:15–18; YOCHANAN 10:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:4"> And this is the solemn edut (testimony) of Yochanan, when those of Yehudah sent kohanim and L'viim from Yerushalayim to him that they might ask him, Mi atah? (Who are you?).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MARKOS 1:7–8; MATTITYAHU 3:11–12; YOCHANAN 3:28–36"> Yochanan made hoda'a (confession, admission) — he did not fail to make hoda'a — and said clearly, I am not the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Rebbe,">v</a></sup>Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MALACHI 4:5; DEVARIM 18:15–18; MATTITYAHU 11:14"> And they asked Yochanan, What, then? Are you Eliyahu HaNavi? And Yochanan says, I am not. Are you the Navi?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Dt 18:15,18">w</a></sup>And he answered, Lo (No).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 24:13"> They said then to him, Mi atah? That we may give a teshuvah (answer) to the ones who sent us. What do you say about yourself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:3–5; MARKOS 1:3; MATTITYAHU 3:3"> Yochanan said, I am a KOL KOREY BAMIDBAR, make straight the DERECH HASHEM!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Isa 40:3, TARGUM HASHIVIM">x</a></sup>as Yeshayah HaNavi said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKAS 11:53; MATTITYAHU 23:13–15; LUKAS 11:39–44"> And the ones that had been sent were of the Perushim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEVARIM 18:18; DEVARIM 18:15; YOCHANAN 1:20–22"> And the Perushim asked Yochanan, If you are not the &#91;Rebbe&#93;, Melech HaMoshiach nor Eliyahu nor the Navi, then why do you administer the mikveh mayimʼs tevilah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:10–11; LUKAS 3:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:5"> Yochanan answered the Perushim, I give a tevilah in a mikveh mayim; among you is standing one of whom you do not have da'as.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:30; MARKOS 1:7; MATTITYAHU 3:11"> Hu HaBah (he who comes),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="i.e., the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach who is coming into the Olam Hazeh">y</a></sup>that is, He who comes after me, is one that I am not worthy even to untie the thong of his sandal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:40; YOCHANAN 3:23; SHOFETIM 7:24"> These events took place in Beit-Anyah (Bethany), beyond the Yarden River, which Yochanan was using as a mikveh mayim in which to administer the tevilah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:7; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:2; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:5"> On the next day, Yochanan sees Yehoshua coming to him, and Yochanan says, Hinei! The Seh HaElohim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Gn 22:8; Ex 12:5-13; Isa 53:7">z</a></sup>the one carrying away the avonot HaOlam Hazeh (sins of this world).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="i.e., as the sa'ir l'Azazel kapporah; Isa 53:6,7,12; Lv 16:22">aa</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:27; YOCHANAN 1:15; LUKAS 3:16"> This is he about whom I said, After me comes an ISH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Zech 6:12">ab</a></sup>who is really before me in priority, because, before I came to be, he was.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Yn 8:58">ac</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MALACHI 3:1; LUKAS 1:17; MALACHI 4:2–5"> And I did not recognize him, but that he might be manifested to Klal Yisroel, I came, therefore, administering the mikveh mayimʼs tevilah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MARKOS 1:10; LUKAS 3:22; MATTITYAHU 3:16"> And Yochanan gave solemn edut, I have seen the Ruach Hakodesh descending like a yonah out of Shomayim and remaining upon him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Isa 11:2">ad</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:5; MATTITYAHU 3:11; LUKAS 3:16"> And I did not recognize him, but the One who sent me to give the mikveh mayimʼs tevilah said to me, Upon whomever you see the Ruach Hakodesh descending and remaining, this is the One giving the tevilah in the Ruach Hakodesh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Yoel 3:1">ae</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="TEHILLIM 2:7; YOCHANAN 1:49; TEHILLIM 89:26–27"> And I have seen and I have given solemn edut (testimony) that this One is the Ben HaElohim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="2Sm 7:14; Ps 2:7; 1Chr 17:10-14; Prov 8:30; 30:4">af</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:25–26; MALACHI 3:16"> On the next day, Yochanan was standing with two of his talmidim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:29; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19–20; YESHAYAH 65:1–2"> And as Yochanan watched Yehoshua walking by, Yochanan says, Hinei the Seh HaElohim!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Gn 22:8; Ex 12:5-13; Isa 53:7">ag</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:17; YOCHANAN 1:43; ZECHARYAH 8:21"> And the two talmidim heard Yochanan speaking, and they followed after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:7; YOCHANAN 3:2; YOCHANAN 1:49"> When he turned and saw them following, he says to them, Mah tevakkeshun (What do you seek)? And they said to him, Rebbe (which means, being translated, my Master Moreh &#91;teacher&#93;), where is your mekom megurim (dwelling place)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LUKAS 24:29; MATTITYAHU 11:28–30; YOCHANAN 6:37"> And he says to them, Bo'u u're'u! (Come and see!) They went, therefore, and saw his mekom megurim, and remained with him that day. The hour was about the tenth (four o'clock in the afternoon).</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MARKOS 1:16–20; MATTITYAHU 4:18–22; YOCHANAN 1:40–42"> One of the two was the achi Shim'on Kefa. His name was Andrew. He was one of the two who heard Yochanan and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:25; YOCHANAN 1:45; TEHILLIM 89:20"> Andrew first finds his own achi Shim'on Kefa and says to him, We have found the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach! — the word, being translated, means “Messiah”.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Dan 9:25; Ps 2:2; 1Sm 2:10">ah</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="YOCHANAN 21:15–17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:22; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:5"> Andrew led Shim'on to Yehoshua. When Yehoshua gazed at Shim'on, he said, You are Shim'on Bar-Yonah. You will be called Kefa, which is translated Petros.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Rock; Gn 17:5,15; 32:28; 35:10">ai</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:9; YOCHANAN 14:8; YESHAYAH 65:1"> On the next day he wanted to go out into the Galil, and he finds Philippos, and says to Philippos, Follow me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="as my talmid; 1Kgs 19:19">aj</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:21; MATTITYAHU 11:21; MARKOS 8:22"> Now Philippos was from Beit-Tzaidah, the shtetl of Andrew and Kefa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="DEVARIM 18:18–22; LUKAS 24:27; MICHOH 5:2"> Philippos finds Natan'el and says to him, He whom Moshe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Rabbeinu">ak</a></sup>wrote of in the Sefer Torah, he whom the Nevi'im also wrote of — Yehoshua ben Yosef &#91;ben Dovid&#93;, from Natzeret we have found!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Dt 18:18; Isa 7:14; 9:6[5]; Ezek 34:23">al</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:52; YOCHANAN 7:41–42; YOCHANAN 4:29"> And Natan'el said to Philippos, Can anything good come out of Natzeret?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="7:41; Isa 11:1; Zech 6:11-12">am</a></sup>Philippos says to Natan'el, Bo'u u're'u! (Come and see!)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="2Kgs 6:13">an</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="HISGALUS 14:5; TEHILLIM 32:2; TEHILLIM 73:1"> Yehoshua saw Natan'el coming to him, and he says, Hinei a genuine Ben Yisroel, in whom is no MIRMAH (deceit).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Ps 32:2; Zeph 3:13; Isa 53:9">ao</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="YESHAYAH 65:24; YOCHANAN 2:25; TEHILLIM 139:1–2"> Natan'el says to him, How do you have da'as of me? In reply, Yehoshua said to him, Before you received your kri'ah (call) from Philippos, while you were beneath the etz hate'enah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Mic 4:4; Zech 3:10">ap</a></sup>(fig tree), I watched you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="TZEFANYAH 3:15; ZECHARYAH 9:9; MATTITYAHU 27:42"> In reply, Natan'el said to him, Rebbe, you are the Ben HaElohim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="2Sm 7:14; Ps 2:7; 89:26-27">aq</a></sup>the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach of Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:40; YOCHANAN 20:29; LUKAS 1:45"> In reply, Yehoshua said to Natan'el, Because I told you that I watched you beneath the etz hate'enah, do you have emunah (faith)? Greater than these things you will see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:56; LUKAS 22:43"> And he says to Natan'el, Omein, omein, I say to you, you will see Shomayim having been opened and malachim (angels) of Hashem ascending and descending on the Bar Enosh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="i.e, Moshiach Dan 7:13-14; Zeph 3:15; Gn 28:12">ar</a></sup></span>
</div>
