<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 34:11–16; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:19; YOCHANAN 10:4"> To this one the doorkeeper opens, and the tzon hears the Ro'ehʼs voice and the Ro'eh calls b'shem (by name) each of his own tzon and leads them out.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 95:7">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:30; YOCHANAN 10:16; YOCHANAN 12:26"> When the Ro'eh has brought out all his own, he goes ahead of them, and the tzon follow the Ro'eh, because they have da'as of the Ro'ehʼs voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:5–6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:6–10; MARKOS 4:24"> But a zar (stranger, foreigner) they will never follow, but will flee from him, because they do not have da'as of the voice of zarim (strangers, foreigners).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YESHAYAH 56:11; TEHILLIM 82:5; MISHLE 28:5"> This figure of speech Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach told them. But those ones did not have binah (understanding) of what he was saying to them.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 34:2; YIRMEYAH 23:1; YOCHANAN 10:1"> All who came before me are ganavim (thieves) and shodedim (robbers), but the tzon did not listen to them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Jer 23:1,2; Ezek 34:2-3">c</a></sup></span>

Through me, if anyone enters, he will come to yeshu'at Eloheinu and will go in and will go out and will find mir'eh (pasture).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Num 27:17; Ps 118:20; 23:2">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKAS 19:10; YOCHANAN 6:51; YOCHANAN 12:47"> The ganav does not come except in order that he may steal and kill and destroy. <span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:27; YOCHANAN 10:11; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:19"> Ani Hu the Ro'eh HaTov, and I have da'as of my tzon and my tzon has da'as of me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ex 33:12">h</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YESHAYAH 56:8; YOCHANAN 11:52; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:25"> And another tzon I have which is not of this mikhla (fold);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="enclosure">i</a></sup>those also it is necessary for me to bring, and my voice they will hear, and they will become eder echad with Ro'eh echad (One flock with one Shepherd).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Isa 56:8; Ezek 34:23; 37:24">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:18; YOCHANAN 10:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:9"> Therefore, HaAv has ahavah for me, because I lay down my neshamah that I may take it up again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:9; LETTER TO TITOS 2:14; YOCHANAN 2:19–21"> No one takes it from me, but I lay it down of my own accord. I have samchut (authority) to lay it down, and I have samchut to receive it again. This mitzvah I received from HaAv of me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Isa 52:13—53:12">k</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:16; YOCHANAN 10:14; HISGALUS 3:20"> My Tzon hear my voice, and I have da'as of them, and they follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:39–40; YOCHANAN 6:37; YOCHANAN 3:15–16"> And I give to them Chayyei Olam, and they will never perish, and no one will snatch them out of my hand.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Isa 66:22">o</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:1–2; YOCHANAN 14:9; YOCHANAN 17:21–23"> I and HaAv are echad.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Dt 6:4; Ps 33:6; Gn 2:24">p</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:18; VAYIKRA 24:16; YOCHANAN 10:30"> In reply, those of Yehudah said to him, For a ma'aseh tov we do not stone you, but for Chillul Hashem and because you, being a man, make yourself G-d.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Lv 24:16">q</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:35; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:1; LUKAS 16:17"> If those ones he called g-ds, to whom the Dvar Hashem came and LO YUFAR KITVEI HAKODESH (and the Kitvei Hakodesh cannot be broken),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Ps 119:89,142">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:17–18; YOCHANAN 3:17; YIRMEYAH 1:5"> Can you say of the One whom HaAv set apart as HaKadosh and sent into the Olam Hazeh that he commits Chillul Hashem because I said, Ben HaElohim Ani Hu?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Jer 1:5">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:24; YOCHANAN 10:25; YOCHANAN 12:37–40"> If I do not accomplish the pe'ulot of Avi, do not regard me with bitachon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:20; YOCHANAN 10:25; YOCHANAN 10:30"> But if I do, even if you do not have emunah in me, chotsh have emunah in the pe'ulot, so that you may continue to have da'as and binah that HaAv is in me and I am in HaAv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:30; YOCHANAN 8:59; YOCHANAN 7:44"> They were seeking, therefore, again to seize him, and he eluded their hand.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:33–34; YOCHANAN 1:29–30; MARKOS 1:37"> And many came to him and were saying, Yochanan indeed did no ot (miraculous sign), but everything Yochanan said about this man was HaEmes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:41; YOCHANAN 2:23; YOCHANAN 7:31"> And many put their emunah (faith) in him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="as Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">v</a></sup>there.</span>
