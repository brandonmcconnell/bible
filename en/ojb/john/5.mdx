<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEVARIM 16:16; SHEMOT 23:14–17; SHEMOT 34:23"> After these things, there was a Chag (Feast) in Yehudah and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach went up to Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 12:39; NECHEMYAH 3:1; NECHEMYAH 3:32"> Now there is in Yerushalayim at the Sha'ar HaTzon (Sheepgate)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Neh 3:1,32; 12:39">a</a></sup>a pool, the one being called in Aramaic, Beit-Zata, having chamasha colonnades.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MARKOS 3:1–4; LUKAS 7:22; MATTITYAHU 15:30"> In these lay a multitude of invalids — ivrim (blind men), pisechim (lame persons), paralyzed. &#91;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For a malach Hashem from time to time descended into the berekhah (pool) and agitated the mayim; therefore, he who entered first after the agitation of the mayim, became whole, whatever choli (disease, sickness) he was held by.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:2; LUKAS 8:43; MARKOS 9:21"> And there was a certain man there SHELOSHIM U'SHEMONEH SHANAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dt 2:14">b</a></sup>(thirty-eight years) having been in his machla (illness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKAS 18:41; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:15; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:13"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, seeing this man lying there, and having da'as that the man had been in this condition for a long time already, says to him, Do you want to have refu'ah?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 53:5">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 72:12; DEVARIM 32:36; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:24"> In reply, the choleh (sick one) said, Adoni, I have no one, when the mayim is disturbed, to put me into the pool. While I am coming, someone steps into the pool ahead of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKAS 5:24; MATTITYAHU 9:6; MARKOS 2:11"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to him, Rise up, take your mat and walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:7–8; YOCHANAN 9:14; MARKOS 3:2–4"> And immediately the man received his refu'ah (healing) and took his mat and was walking around. And that day was Shabbos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 17:21; LUKAS 6:2; YOCHANAN 9:16"> So those of Yehudah were saying to the one having been granted refu'ah (healing), It is Shabbos, so it is asur (prohibited), it is chillul Shabbos (desecration of Shabbat) for you to carry your mat.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Neh 13:15-22; Jer 17:21">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MARKOS 2:9–11; YOCHANAN 9:16"> But the man in reply said to them, The one having given me refu'ah, that one said to me, Take up your mat and walk!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:23; SHOFETIM 6:29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:2"> They asked him, Who is haIsh (the man) telling you to take up your mat and walk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 4:30; LUKAS 24:31; YOCHANAN 8:59"> But the one having been granted refu'ah (healing) did not have da'as who it is, for Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach slipped away, there being a multitude in the place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:11; VAYIKRA 26:23–24; TEHILLIM 118:18"> After these things Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach finds him in the Beis Hamikdash, and said to him, Hinei, you have received your refu'ah. No longer commit averos, for fear that something worse happen to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:11–12; YOCHANAN 4:29; YOCHANAN 9:25"> The man went away and reported to the Yehudim that Yehoshua is the one having granted him his refu'ah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:39; YOCHANAN 7:19–20; MARKOS 3:6"> And, because of this, those of Yehudah brought redifah (persecution) upon Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, because these things he was doing on Shabbos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:4; YOCHANAN 14:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:16"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach in reply, said to them, Avi until now is working and I am working.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gn 2:3">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:30; YOCHANAN 10:33; YOCHANAN 19:7"> Because of this, therefore, those of Yehudah were seeking all the more to kill Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, because not only was he mechallel Shabbos, but also Rebbe was saying that his own Av was Hashem, thereby making himself equal with Elohim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Yn 1:1">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:49; YOCHANAN 8:28; YOCHANAN 14:10"> In reply, therefore, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying to them, Omein, omein, I say to you, HaBen is not able to do anything from himself except what he sees HaAv doing, for what things that One is doing, these things also HaBen likewise is doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:35; MATTITYAHU 3:17; YOCHANAN 17:26"> For HaAv has ahavah for HaBen and all things He shows to him which He does and ma'asim gedolim (greater works) than these He will show him that you may marvel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:11; DEVARIM 32:39; YOCHANAN 11:25"> For just as HaAv raises the Mesim (dead ones) and makes them alive, so also HaBen makes alive whom he wills.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Dt 32:39; 1Sm 2:6; 2Kgs 5:7; Hos 6:2; Ruth 4:5">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:31; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:10"> For not even HaAv judges anyone, but all Mishpat Hashem has given to HaBen,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gn 18:25; Judg 11:27; Dan 7:10,13-14">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:23; LUKAS 10:16; MATTITYAHU 11:27"> That kol Bnei Adam may honor HaBen as they honor HaAv. The one not honoring HaBen does not honor HaAv who sent him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Dan 7:13-14">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:36; YOCHANAN 6:40; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:11–13"> Omein, omein, I say to you, that the one, hearing my dvar and having emunah in the One who sent me, has Chayyei Olam and does not come into the Mishpat Hashem, but has been transferred out of mavet (death) into Chayyim (Life).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:5"> Omein, omein, I say to you, that a sha'ah (hour) is coming and now is, when the Mesim (dead ones) will hear the kol (voice) of the Ben HaElohim and the ones having heard will live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:26; YOCHANAN 1:4; SHEMOT 3:14"> For just as HaAv has Chayyim (Life) in himself,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Dt 30:20; Job 10:12; 33:4; Ps 36:9[10]">j</a></sup>so also HaBen He gave to have Chayyim (Life) in himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:42; YOCHANAN 5:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:31"> And samchut (authority) He gave to him to make mishpat (judgment) because he is the Bar Enosh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Dan 7:13-14">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="HISGALUS 20:12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:14–17; YESHAYAH 26:19"> Do not marvel at this. For a sha'ah (hour, time) is coming in which all the ones in the kevarim (graves) will hear the kol of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Isa 26:19; Ezek 37:12">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DANIEL 12:2–3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:6–10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:15"> And will come out, the ones having practiced HaTov to a Techiyas HaMesim of Chayyim (Life), the ones having practiced HaRah to a Techiyas HaMesim of Mishpat (Judgment).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Dan 12:2">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:38; YOCHANAN 5:19; YOCHANAN 4:34"> I am not able to do from myself anything. As I hear I judge, and mine is mishpat tzedek,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Isa 28:6">n</a></sup>because I do not seek my own ratzon (will) but the ratzon (will) of the One having sent me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Num 16:28">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MISHLE 27:2; YOCHANAN 8:54; HISGALUS 3:14"> If I give solemn edut (testimony) about myself, the edut of mine is not ne'emanah (reliable).</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:33; YOCHANAN 12:28–30; YOCHANAN 5:36–37"> There is Another giving solemn edut about me, however, and I have da'as that the edut He testifies about me is ne'emanah (reliable).</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:29–34; YOCHANAN 3:26–36; YOCHANAN 1:15–27"> You &#91;pl.&#93; have sent to Yochanan and he has given solemn edut (testimony) to HaEmes (the Truth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LUKAS 19:41–42; YOCHANAN 20:31; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:9"> Not that I accept edut from Bnei Adam, but I say these things that you may come to Yeshu'at Eloheinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19; YOCHANAN 1:7–8; MATTITYAHU 11:11"> He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Yochanan">p</a></sup>was a menorah burning and shining and you chose to exult for a time in his Ohr (Light);<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Ps 132:16; Dan 12:3">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:25; YOCHANAN 10:37–38; YOCHANAN 15:24"> but l have edut (testimony) greater than Yochananʼs. For the ma'asim (works) which HaAv has given to me that I should accomplish, these ma'asim which I do give solemn edut (testimony) about me, that HaAv has sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:18; YOCHANAN 1:18; MATTITYAHU 3:17"> And HaAv who sent me has Himself given solemn edut (testimony) about me. You have neither heard His kol (voice) nor the mareh of Hashem have you seen,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Dt 4:12">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:14; DEVARIM 6:6–9; YOCHANAN 12:44–48"> and the dvar Hashem you do not have abiding and dwelling in you, because you have no bitachon (trust), no emunah (faith) in the one whom Hashem has sent.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Isa 26:10; 53:1; Jer 8:8">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LUKAS 24:27; LUKAS 24:44; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19–21"> You search the Kitvei Hakodesh because you think in them you have Chayyei Olam. And those are the ones giving solemn edut about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:19; YOCHANAN 1:11; TEHILLIM 81:11"> And you do not want to come to me that you may have Chayyim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:18; YOCHANAN 5:34; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:6"> I do not accept kavod (glory) from Bnei Adam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:20; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17; YOCHANAN 2:25"> But I have had da'as of you, that the ahavas Hashem(the love of G-d) you do not have in yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:25; MATTITYAHU 24:5; YOCHANAN 17:4–6"> I have come b'Shem Avi and you do not accept me. If another comes in his own name, him you will accept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:43; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:3"> How are you able to have emunah, when you receive kavod from one another, and the kavod from the only Elohim HaEchad you do not seek?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="2Kgs 19:15,19; Isa 37:20;">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:19–20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:7–11"> Do not think that I will accuse you before HaAv; however, there is one accusing you: Moshe, in whom you have set your tikvah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Dt 31:26-27">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="DEVARIM 18:15; DEVARIM 18:18–19; YOCHANAN 1:45"> For if you were having emunah in Moshe, you would have had emunah in me, for he himself wrote concerning me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Gn 3:15; 45:4-9; Dt 18:15">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="LUKAS 16:31; LUKAS 16:29"> But if in the Kitvei Hakodesh of that one you lack emunah, how will you have emunah in my dvar?</span>
