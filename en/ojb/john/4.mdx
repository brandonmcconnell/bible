<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:48; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:13–17"> Although Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach himself did not personally administer the mikveh mayimʼs tevilah — this was administered by his talmidim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:23; YOCHANAN 10:40; YOCHANAN 2:11"> Then Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach left the land of Yehudah and departed again into the Galil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKAS 17:11; LUKAS 2:49; LUKAS 9:51–52"> And it was necessary for him to pass through Shomron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 24:32; BERESHIS 33:19; BERESHIS 48:22"> Therefore, he comes into a city of Shomron being called Sh'khem near the field which Ya'akov Avinu had given to Yosef Ben Ya'akov.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gn 33:19; 48:22; Josh 24:32">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:15; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:17; MATTITYAHU 8:24"> Now a be'er (well) of Ya'akov was there. Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, having become weary from his journey was sitting there at the be'er. The sha'ah (hour, time) was about hashishit (the sixth, noon).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:42; MELACHIM ALEF 17:10; BERESHIS 24:43"> An isha of Shomron comes to draw mayim. Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to her, Give me a drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKAS 9:13; YOCHANAN 4:39; YOCHANAN 4:5"> For Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiachʼs talmidim had gone away into the city that they might buy okhel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:28; MATTITYAHU 10:5; MELACHIM BAIS 17:24–41"> Therefore, the isha of Shomron says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, How can you, being a Yehudi, ask to be given a drink from me, an isha (woman) from Shomron (Samaria)? (For those who are Yehudim do not associate with those of Shomron).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ezra 4:3-6; 9:1—10:44">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:14; HISGALUS 7:17; YOCHANAN 7:37–39"> In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to her, If you knew the matanah (gift) of Hashem and who it is saying to you, Give me a drink,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gn 26:19">c</a></sup>you would have asked him and he would have given you Mayim Chayyim (Living Water).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 44:3; 55:1; Jer 2:13; 17:13; Zech 14:8">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:14; YOCHANAN 3:4"> The isha (woman) says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Adoni, you have no bucket and the be'er is deep. From where then do you have the Mayim Chayyim?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gn 21:19">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:6; YOCHANAN 8:53; MATTITYAHU 12:42"> Surely you are not greater than Ya'akov Avinu who gave the matanah to us of the be'er (well) and drank from it himself as did his banim (sons) and his tzon (flock)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:27; YESHAYAH 65:13–14; LUKAS 16:24"> In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Everyone drinking from this mayim (water) will thirst again,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:38–39; YOCHANAN 6:35; YESHAYAH 49:10"> But whoever drinks of the mayim (water) which I will give to him will never thirst again, but the mayim (water) which I will give him will become in him a makor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ps 36:9[10]">f</a></sup>(fountain) of mayim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 12:3; 58:11">g</a></sup>(water) springing up unto Chayyei Olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:34; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:5; TEHILLIM 4:6"> The isha (woman) says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Adoni, give me this mayim that I may not thirst nor come here to draw mayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:13; HISGALUS 2:23; YOCHANAN 4:18"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to her, Go, call your ba'al (husband), and come back here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> In reply, the isha (woman) said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, I do not have a ba'al. He says to her, Well you spoke, I do not have a ba'al.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 3:20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:10–11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:3"> For beetzem (in fact) chamisha be'alim (five husbands) you had, and the one you have now is not your ba'al (husband). What you have said is emes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="2Kgs 17:24; Hos 2:7">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKAS 7:39; YOCHANAN 6:14; YOCHANAN 9:17"> The isha says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Adoni, I see that you are a navi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:6–7; DEVARIM 27:12; DEVARIM 11:29"> Avoteinu on this mountain<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gerizim">i</a></sup>worshiped<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Dt 11:29; 27:12; Josh 8:33">j</a></sup>and you say that in Yerushalayim is the place where it is necessary to worship.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Dt 12:5-14; Ps 122:1-5">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:8; MALACHI 1:11; YOCHANAN 4:23"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to her, Have emunah (faith), believe me, Isha. A sha'ah (hour, time) comes when neither on this mountain nor in Yerushalayim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Mal 1:11; 1Kgs 8:27; Isa 66:1">l</a></sup>will you worship HaAv.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Isa 63:16">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:23; YESHAYAH 2:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:4–5"> You worship that of which you do not have da'as;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="2Kgs 17:28-41">n</a></sup>we worship that of which we have da'as, because Yeshu'at Eloheinu (salvation) is from the Yehudim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Isa 2:3; Ro 9:3">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:3; YOCHANAN 5:25; MATTITYAHU 15:7–9"> But a sha'ah is coming, and now is, when those of the true avodas kodesh will worship HaAv in the Ruach Hakodesh and in Emes, for indeed &#91;Elohim&#93; HaAv is seeking such to worship Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:17; MATTITYAHU 15:8–9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:3"> Hashem is Ruach (Spirit) and it is necessary for the ones worshiping Him to worship in Ruach and Emes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Ezek 36:26-27; 37:14">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:49; DANIEL 9:24–26; YOCHANAN 1:41–42"> The isha says to him, I have da'as that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach is coming, the one being called the Messiah. When Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach comes, he will proclaim to us everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:24; MARKOS 14:61–62; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:20–21"> Yehoshua says to her, Ani Hu (I am He), the one speaking to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKAS 7:39; YOCHANAN 4:8–9"> And at this very moment, the Moshiachʼs talmidim arrived, and they were marveling that he was speaking with an isha. No one said, however, What are you seeking? Or, Why do you speak with her?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:7; LUKAS 24:33; MARKOS 16:8–10"> Therefore, the isha left her waterpot and went away into the city and says to the bnei Adam of the city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="HISGALUS 22:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 14:24–25; YOCHANAN 4:25"> Come, see an ish who told me everything I have ever done. Surely this one is Moshiach, is he not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:5–8; LUKAS 17:16–18"> They came out of the city and were coming to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:38; BERESHIS 24:33; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:30–34"> Divaile (meanwhile), Moshiachʼs talmidim were asking him, Rebbe, eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="IYOV 23:12; TEHILLIM 119:103; YOCHANAN 4:34"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, I have okhel (food) to eat<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Job 23:12">q</a></sup>of which you have no da'as.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:6–11; LUKAS 9:45"> Therefore, Moshiachʼs talmidim were saying to one another, Surely no one brought him anything to eat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:38; YOCHANAN 5:30; IYOV 23:12"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, My okhel (food) is that I may do the ratzon (will) of the One having sent me and may complete His ma'aseh (work).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Koh 11:5">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LUKAS 10:2–3; MATTITYAHU 9:37–38; YOCHANAN 4:30"> Do not say, Yet arba'ah chodashim (four months) and then comes the katzir (harvest). Hinei, I say to you, Lift up your eyes and see the sadot (fields) that they are white for the katzir (harvest).</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:19–20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:5–9"> The one harvesting receives wages and gathers p'ri (fruit) unto Chayyei Olam, that the one sowing and the other one harvesting may have simcha together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MICHOH 6:15; IYOV 31:8; LUKAS 19:21"> For in this the saying is true, One sows and another reaps.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Job 31:8; Mic 6:15">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:42–43"> I sent you to harvest that upon which you have not labored; others have labored and you have entered into their labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:29; YOCHANAN 4:5; YOCHANAN 10:41–42"> And from that city of Shomron many had emunah in him, because of the solemn dvar of the isha giving edut: He told me everything I have ever done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:15; LUKAS 24:29"> Then when those of Shomron came to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, they were asking him to remain with them, and he remained with them there two yamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:4–5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:12"> And many more put their emunah &#91;in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach&#93;, because of his dvar (word).</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:14; YOCHANAN 1:29; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:20"> And to the isha they were saying, No longer because of what you spoke do we have emunah (faith), for we ourselves have heard and we have da'as that his one is be'emes the Moshi'a HaOlam (the Savior, Go'el HaOlam).</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:8; MARKOS 7:27–28"> And after two yamim (days), he went out from there into the Galil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 13:57; LUKAS 4:24; MARKOS 6:4"> For Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach himself gave solemn edut (testimony) that a Navi does not have kavod (honor) in his own country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="YOCHANAN 2:23; YOCHANAN 2:13–16; DEVARIM 16:16"> Therefore, when he came into the Galil, those of the Galil received Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, vi-bahlt (since) they had seen all which he did in Yerushalayim during the Chag, for they also went to the Chag.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="YOCHANAN 2:1–11; LUKAS 7:2; HOSHEA 5:15"> Then Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach came again into Kanah of the Galil where he made the mayim (water) into yayin (wine). There was a certain royal official whose ben was choleh (ill) in K'far-Nachum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:3; YOCHANAN 11:21; YOCHANAN 11:32"> This man, having heard that Yehoshua comes from the land of Yehudah into the Galil, went to him and was asking him to come down and cure the ben of him. For the yeled was about to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:29; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:22"> Therefore, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Except you see otot (miraculous signs) and moftim (wonders), you will never have emunah (faith).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Dan 3:32-33; 4:34">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="MARKOS 5:23; TEHILLIM 88:10–12; TEHILLIM 40:17"> The royal official says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Adoni, come down before my yeled dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 8:13; MARKOS 7:29–30; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:20–21"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to him, Go! Bincha chai (your son lives)!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="1Kgs 17:23">u</a></sup>The man had emunah (faith) in the dvar that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach had said to him, and the man departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 17:23; YOCHANAN 4:50; YOCHANAN 4:53"> And as the man was going, his avadim met him, saying that the manʼs yeled lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> Therefore, the man inquired from them the sha'ah (hour, time) in which the yeled began to recover. Then they said to him, Etmol (yesterday) at the sha'ah hashevi'it (seventh hour) the fever left him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="TEHILLIM 107:20; LUKAS 19:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:34"> Therefore, the abba knew that it was the same sha'ah (hour, time) in which Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to him, Bincha chai! And the man had emunah and all his bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:45; YOCHANAN 2:1–11"> Now this was ot hasheni (the second miraculous sign) that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach did after having come from the land of Yehudah to the Galil.</span>
