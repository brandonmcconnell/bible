<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARKOS 6:31–44; YOCHANAN 6:23; LUKAS 5:1"> After these things, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach departed beyond Lake Kinneret (Lake Tiberias).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 4:24–25; MATTITYAHU 12:15; MATTITYAHU 15:30–31"> And a riezige (sizable) multitude was following him, because they were seeing the otot (miraculous signs) which he was doing on the cholim (the ones being ill).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:15; LUKAS 9:28; LUKAS 6:12–13"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach went up to the mountain, and there he was sitting with his talmidim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YOCHANAN 2:13; YOCHANAN 11:55; YOCHANAN 5:1"> Pesach was near at hand, the Chag (Jewish Feast).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:33; LUKAS 9:12–13; MATTITYAHU 14:14–15"> Therefore, having lifted up his eyes, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach saw that a great multitude is coming to him, and he says to Philippos, From where may we buy lechem that these ones may eat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 22:1; DEVARIM 13:3; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 32:31"> But he was saying this to test Philippos, for he himself knew what he was about to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 11:21–22; MARKOS 6:37; YOCHANAN 12:5"> In reply, Philippos said, Two hundred denarii worth of lechem would not be enough that each one may take a little okhel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:40–44; MATTITYAHU 4:18"> One of his talmidim, Andrew, ach of Shim'on Kefa, says to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:42–44; MATTITYAHU 14:17; MARKOS 6:38"> There is a na'ar (lad) here who has five barley loaves and two dagim (fish), but what are these for so many?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2Kgs 4:43">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKAS 9:14–16; MATTITYAHU 15:35–36; MARKOS 6:39–41"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Instruct the anashim (men) to recline. Now there was much grass in the area. Therefore the anashim reclined, their number being about chameshet alafim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:23; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:18; MATTITYAHU 15:36"> Therefore, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach took the loaves and, having made the bracha, distributed to the ones bimesibba (reclining); likewise also he distributed of the dagim (fish) as much as they were wanting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKAS 1:53; NECHEMYAH 9:25; NECHEMYAH 8:10"> Now when they were filled, he tells his talmidim, Gather the shirayim (Rebbeʼs leftovers), that nothing may be lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MISHLE 11:24–25; MELACHIM BAIS 4:2–7; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:8–9"> Therefore, what they gathered from the five barley loaves filled Sheneym Asar (Twelve) baskets of shirayim left over by the ones having eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEVARIM 18:15–18; MATTITYAHU 21:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:37"> Therefore, the anashim, having seen what ot (miraculous sign) he did, were saying, This one is beemes the Navi, Hu HaBah, the one coming into the Olam Hazeh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dt 18:15,18">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:36; YOCHANAN 6:15–21; YOCHANAN 7:3–4"> Therefore, Yehoshua, having had da'as that they are about to come and to seize him that they might make him king by force, departed alone again to the mountain, withdrawing by himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And as erev came, his talmidim went down to the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MARKOS 6:45; YOCHANAN 6:24–25; YOCHANAN 2:12"> And having embarked into a sirah, they were going across the sea to K'far-Nachum. And choshech already had come and Yehoshua had not yet come to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="TEHILLIM 107:25; TEHILLIM 135:7; MATTITYAHU 14:24"> And the sea, as a great wind blew, was becoming aroused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARKOS 6:47–49; MATTITYAHU 14:25–26; IYOV 9:8"> Therefore, having rowed about esrim v'chamash or sheloshim stadia, they see Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach walking on the sea and coming near the sirah, and they were gefeirlich (horribly) frightened.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Job 9:8">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 14:27–31; YESHAYAH 43:1–2; YESHAYAH 44:8"> But he says to them, Ani Hu. Do not be afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MARKOS 6:51; MATTITYAHU 14:32–33; HISGALUS 3:20"> Therefore, the talmidim were willing to receive him into the sirah. And immediately the sirah came to the shore to which they were going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MARKOS 6:45; MATTITYAHU 14:22; YOCHANAN 6:2"> On the next day the multitude that had stayed on the other side of Lake Kinneret saw that there had been only one sirah with which to cross the sea. They knew that Yehoshua had not embarked with his talmidim in that sirah, but that his talmidim had departed by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:1; YOCHANAN 6:11–12"> Then some sirot (boats) came ashore from Tiberias and landed near the area where they ate the lechem after Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu had made the bracha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:17; LUKAS 8:40; MARKOS 6:53"> Therefore, when the multitude saw Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach is not there, nor his talmidim, they embarked into the sirot and came to K'far-Nachum, seeking Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:38–39; MATTITYAHU 23:7"> And having found him across the sea, they said to him, Rebbe, when did you come here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:3–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:21"> In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Omein, omein, I say to you, you are seeking me not because you saw otot (miraculous signs), but because you ate of the loaves and were glutted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:2; YESHAYAH 55:2; YOCHANAN 6:54"> Do not work for the okhel that is perishing, but for the okhel remaining to Chayyei Olam, which the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Moshiach">d</a></sup>will give to you, for Elohim HaAv has set His chotam (seal) on this one.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Isa 55:2">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:30; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:6; MATTITYAHU 19:16"> Therefore, they said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, What may we do that we may work the pe'ulot Hashem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:31; YOCHANAN 3:36"> In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, This is the mitzvah of Hashem, that you may believe in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach whom Hashem has sent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:37; YOCHANAN 10:38; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:22"> They said, therefore, to him, What ot (sign) then do you perform that we may see and may have emunah in you? What work do you do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 9:15; TEHILLIM 105:40; TEHILLIM 78:24–25"> Avoteinu ate the manna in the wilderness,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Num 11:7-9; Ex 16:4,15; Neh 9:15">f</a></sup>as it has been written, LECHEM from SHOMAYIM he gave them to eat.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ps 78:24; 105:40">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:1; YOCHANAN 6:58; SHEMOT 16:4"> Therefore, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Omein, omein, I say to you, it was not Moshe who has given you the lechem out of Shomayim, but HaAv of me gives you the lechem ha'amitti (the true bread) out of Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:42; YOCHANAN 17:8; YOCHANAN 3:13"> For the lechem of Hashem is that which comes down out of Shomayim and giving Chayyim (Life) to HaOlam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:15; YOCHANAN 6:26; TEHILLIM 4:6"> Therefore, they said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Adoni, always give us this lechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:48–58; HISGALUS 22:17; YOCHANAN 6:41"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Ani Hu<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ex 3:14">h</a></sup>the lechem haChayyim; the one coming to me never hungers, and the one with emunah in me will never thirst again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8–9; LUKAS 16:31; YOCHANAN 12:37"> But I said to you that you have seen me and yet you do not have bitachon, you lack emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 11:28; YOCHANAN 6:39; YOCHANAN 17:24"> All which HaAv gives to me will come to me, and the one coming to me I will never turn away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:30; YOCHANAN 4:34; YOCHANAN 3:31"> For I have come down from Shomayim not that I may do my ratzon, but the ratzon of the One having sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="YOCHANAN 17:12; YOCHANAN 6:40; YOCHANAN 18:9"> Now this is the ratzon of the One Who sent me, that I should lose nothing of all which He has given me, but I will raise him up on the Yom HaAcharon (Last Day).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Isa 27:3; Jer 23:4">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:54; YOCHANAN 5:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:23"> For this is the ratzon of Avi: that everyone seeing the Ben<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="HaElohim Moshiach">j</a></sup>and believing in him may have Chayyei Olam, and on Yom HaAcharon I will raise him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:51–52; YOCHANAN 6:48; YOCHANAN 6:33"> Therefore, those of Yehudah were murmuring about Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, because he said, Ani Hu<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ex 3:14">k</a></sup>the lechem having come down out of Shomayim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Ex 16:4">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="LUKAS 4:22; MARKOS 6:3; MATTITYAHU 13:55–56"> And they were saying, Is this man not Yehoshua Ben Yosef,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Ben Dovid">m</a></sup>and do we not know his Av and his Em? Now how does he say, Out of Shomayim I have come down.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Yn 1:1,14">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:13; YOCHANAN 6:64; MARKOS 9:33"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach answered and said to them, Do not grumble among yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:65; YOCHANAN 6:39–40; MATTITYAHU 11:25–27"> No one is able to come to me unless HaAv Who sent me should draw him, and I will raise him up on the Yom HaAcharon.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Jer 31:3; Hos 11:4">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="YESHAYAH 54:13; YIRMEYAH 31:33–34; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:16"> It has been written in the Nevi'im, V'CHOL they LIMUDI HASHEM (They shall all be taught of G-d).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Isa 54:13; see Jer 31:33,34">p</a></sup>Everyone having heard from HaAv and having learned comes to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:18; YOCHANAN 7:29; MATTITYAHU 11:27"> Not that anyone has seen HaAv except the one being sent from Hashem; this one has seen HaAv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:36; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:3–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:9–10"> Omein, omein, I say to you, the one having emunah has Chayyei Olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:51; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:24–25; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:16–17"> Ani Hu the lechem haChayyim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Num 14:23; Dt 1:35">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:3–5; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5; ZECHARYAH 1:5"> Your Avot ate in the wilderness the manna and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:33; YOCHANAN 6:51; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:10"> This is the lechem coming down out of Shomayim, so that anyone may eat of it and not die.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Gn 3:22">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="LUKAS 22:19; YOCHANAN 6:33; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:5–12"> Ani hu the lechem haChai (the living bread) having come down out of Shomayim. If anyone eats of this lechem he will live l'olam v'ed. And, indeed, the lechem which I will give on behalf of the Chayyim (life) of HaOlam is the basar of me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Ex 12:8">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:16; YOCHANAN 10:19; YOCHANAN 6:41"> Therefore, those of Yehudah were arguing with one another, saying, How is this man able to give us the basar of him to eat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:12; MATTITYAHU 26:26–28; YOCHANAN 6:55"> Therefore, he said to them, Omein, omein, I say to you, unless you eat the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Seudas Moshiach Seh">t</a></sup>basar of the Bar Enosh and drink<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="the Kiddush Cup of the Brit Chadasha in">u</a></sup>his dahm, you do not have Chayyim (Life) in yourselves.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Ex 12:3; Isa 53:7">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:39–40; YOCHANAN 6:27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:7–10"> The one feeding on my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Seh Elohim">w</a></sup>basar and drinking<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Cup of Redemption in">x</a></sup>dahmi has Chayyei Olam and I will raise him up on the Yom HaAcharon.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Ruth 4:5,10">y</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:1; YOCHANAN 8:31; YOCHANAN 6:32"> For my basar is okhel emes (true food) and dahmi is shikuy emes (true drink).</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:24; YOCHANAN 15:4–5; YOCHANAN 14:20"> The one feeding on my basar and drinking dahmi abides in,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Isa 53:7; Ex 12:3">z</a></sup>dwells in me and I in him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Ex 12:22">aa</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:20; YOCHANAN 5:26; YOCHANAN 17:21"> As HaAv HaChai sent me and I live because of HaAv, so also whoever is feeding on me even that one will live because of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:36; YOCHANAN 6:47–51; YOCHANAN 6:41"> This is the lechem out of Shomayim having come down, not as the Avot ate and died; the one feeding on this lechem will live l'olam v'ed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:24; MISHLE 8:1–3; LUKAS 4:31"> These things he said in a shul while teaching in K'far-Nachum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:66; YOCHANAN 8:43; YOCHANAN 8:31"> Therefore, many of Moshiachʼs talmidim, having heard, said, Hard is this dvar torah. Who is able to hear it?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Isa 53:1">ab</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="YOCHANAN 2:24–25; YOCHANAN 6:64; YOCHANAN 21:17"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, having da'as in himself that his talmidim are grumbling about this, said to them, Does this cause a michshol for you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="MARKOS 16:19; YOCHANAN 3:13; YOCHANAN 17:4–5"> What if you behold the Bar Enosh ascending where he was Bereshis?</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:25; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:12; TEHILLIM 119:93"> The Ruach Hakodesh is that which is making alive, the basar does not profit anything. The devarim which I have spoken to you are as Ruach and they are as Chayyim (Life).</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:26; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:13"> But there are of you some who do not have emunah. For from Bereshis, he had da'as of whom are the ones not having emunah and who is the one betraying him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:44–45; YOCHANAN 6:37; YOCHANAN 3:27"> And he was saying, Because of this, I have told you that no one is able to come to me unless it has been bashert to him from HaAv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="LUKAS 9:62; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:38"> From this time, many of his talmidim fell away and no longer followed him as talmidim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="RUTH 1:11–18; YOCHANAN 6:70; LUKAS 14:25–33"> Therefore, he said to the Sheneym Asar, Surely you do not want to depart also?</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="TEHILLIM 73:25; YOCHANAN 5:24; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:11–13"> In reply, Shim'on Kefa said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Adoni, to whom will we go? You have the divrei haChayyei Olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:27; LUKAS 9:20; MARKOS 8:29"> And we have bitachon and have trusted with emunah and we have da'as that you are HaKadosh of Hashem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Isa 49:7">ac</a></sup>(the Holy One of G-d).</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:27; YOCHANAN 13:2; YOCHANAN 17:12"> In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Did I not choose you Sheneym Asar (Twelve)? And is one of you not a Satan?</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:14–16; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:16–20"> Now he was speaking of Yehudah Ben Shim'on from K'riot, for, though he was one of the Sheneym Asar, this one was about to betray Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
