<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:8; MATTITYAHU 13:21; MATTITYAHU 26:31–33"> I have spoken these things to you that no cause of michshol (stumbling, falling) may trip you up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:9–11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:2–3"> They will put you under the cherem ban in the shuls. A sha'ah (hour, time) is coming when everyone who kills you will suppose they are offering avodas kodesh to Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Isa 66:5">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:21; YOCHANAN 17:25; YOCHANAN 8:55"> And these things they will do because they did not have da'as of HaAv or of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:29; MARKOS 13:23; YOCHANAN 17:12–13"> But these things I have spoken to you, that, when the sha'ah of these things comes, you might have zikaron (remembrance) of these things, that I told you. But I did not say these things to you from the beginning, because I was with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:36; YOCHANAN 7:33; YOCHANAN 16:28"> But now I am going to the One who sent me. And none of you asks me, Where are you going?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:1; YOCHANAN 16:20–22; LUKAS 24:17"> But because I have spoken these things to you, agmat nefesh (grief) has filled your levavot (heart).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:26; YOCHANAN 14:16–17; YOCHANAN 15:26"> But I tell HaEmes (the truth), it is better for you that I go away. For if I do not go away, the Melitz Yosher (Praklit, Advocate, Counselor, Helper in Court) will not come to you. But if go, I will send Him (the Ruach Hakodesh) to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:37; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:15; ZECHARYAH 12:10"> And having come, that one will expose and convict the Olam Hazeh concerning Chet and concerning Tzedek and concerning Mishpat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:22–25; YOCHANAN 8:23–24; YOCHANAN 12:47–48"> Concerning Chet (Sin), because they do not have emunah in me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:21; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:3–4"> Concerning Tzedek,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dan 9:24">b</a></sup>because I go to HaAv and no longer do you see me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:31; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:14"> And concerning Mishpat (Judgment), because the Sar HaOlam Hazeh has been judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MARKOS 4:33; YOCHANAN 15:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:1–2"> Yet many things I have to tell you, but you are not able to bear them now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:26; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:10–13; YOCHANAN 14:17"> But when that One has come, the Ruach Hakodesh, the Ruach HaEmes, He will guide you in all truth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 25:5">c</a></sup>For He will not speak on His own authority, but what things He will hear, He will speak, and the things that are to come He will announce to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:13–14; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:14–18; YOCHANAN 15:26"> That One will give kavod to me, because He will receive of what is mine, and will announce it to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YOCHANAN 17:10; MATTITYAHU 11:27; YOCHANAN 3:35"> All things which HaAv has are mine; therefore, I said that of mine He receives and will announce it to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:33; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:3; YOCHANAN 14:18–24"> A little time and you no longer see me. And again a little time, and you will see me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:16; MARKOS 9:32; YOCHANAN 16:5"> Therefore, some of the talmidim of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to one another, What is this which he says to us? A little time and you do not see me. And, again, A little time and you will see me? And, Because I go to HaAv?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKAS 24:25; MATTITYAHU 16:9–11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:12"> Therefore, they were saying, What is this, which he says, A little time? We do not have da'as of what he says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:30; YOCHANAN 16:16; YOCHANAN 14:19"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach knew that they were wanting to ask him. And he said to them, About this you inquire of one another that I said, A little time and you do not see me. And, again, a little time and you will see me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:2; MATTITYAHU 5:4; TEHILLIM 126:5–6"> Omein, omein, I say to you, that there will be bechi (weeping) and avelut (mourning), but the Olam Hazeh will have simcha. You will have agmat nefesh, but your agmat nefesh (grief) will become simcha.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ps 30:6">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="TEHILLIM 113:9; YESHAYAH 13:8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:3"> The isha (woman) when she gives birth, has agmat nefesh (grief),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Isa 13:8; 21:3; 26:17; Mic 4:9; Hos 13:13">e</a></sup>because the sha'ah (hour, time) of her has come. But when she gives birth to the yeled, she no longer has zikaron of the Tribulation because of the naches that an ish was born into the Olam Hazeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:52; YOCHANAN 16:6"> Therefore, you have agmat nefesh (grief) now,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Jer 31:12">f</a></sup>but again I will see you, and your levavot will have rejoicing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 66:14TARGUM HASHIVIM">g</a></sup>and the simcha of you no one takes from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:7; YOCHANAN 14:13–14; MATTITYAHU 21:22"> And on that day you will not ask me anything. Omein, omein, I say to you, whatever you ask HaAv b'Shem of me, He will give it to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:7–8; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:2–3; YOCHANAN 15:11"> Until now you did not ask anything b'Shem of me. Ask and you will receive that the simcha of you may be shleimah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:12; MARKOS 4:13; MATTITYAHU 13:34–35"> Regarding these things I have spoken figuratively.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ps 78:2; Ezek 21:5">h</a></sup>A sha'ah comes when no longer I will speak figuratively to you, but will announce plainly to you concerning HaAv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:34; YOCHANAN 17:24; YOCHANAN 17:19"> On that day b'Shem of me you will ask, and I do not say to you that I will ask HaAv concerning you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:21; YOCHANAN 14:23; YOCHANAN 17:23"> For HaAv Himself has ahavah for you, because you have ahavah for me and have emunah that I came forth from HaAv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:1; YOCHANAN 13:3; YOCHANAN 8:14"> I came from HaAv and I have come into the Olam Hazeh. Again I leave the Olam Hazeh and go to HaAv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:25"> The talmidim of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach say, Hinei, see now you speak plainly and no longer figuratively do you speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YOCHANAN 21:17; YOCHANAN 17:8; YOCHANAN 16:17"> Now we know that you have da'as of all things, and you have no need that any test you with any she'elah (question). By this we have emunah that you came forth from Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="1Kgs 17:24">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LUKAS 9:44–45; YOCHANAN 13:38"> He answered them, Do you have emunah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 13:7; MATTITYAHU 26:31; YOCHANAN 8:29"> Hinei a sha'ah (hour, time) is coming and has come, that you are SCATTERED<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Zech 13:7">j</a></sup>each one to his own home, and you leave me alone, but I am not alone, because HaAv is with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:27; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:4; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:16"> These things I have spoken to you that in me you may have shalom. In the Olam Hazeh you have tribulation; but have lev same'ach. I have conquered the Olam Hazeh.</span>
