<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:23; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:19; YOCHANAN 13:34"> Now before the Chag (Feast) of the Pesach, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, having da'as that his sha'ah (hour, time) had come, his sha'ah when he should pass from the Olam Hazeh to HaAv, having had ahavah for his own in the Olam Hazeh, he had ahavah for them to HaKetz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:13–17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:3; YOCHANAN 13:26–27"> And seudah taking place, HaSatan already having put into the lev of Yehudah Ben Shim'on from K'riot that he should betray him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:35; MATTITYAHU 11:27; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:2"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach having had da'as that HaAv had given into his hands all things, and that from Hashem Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach came forth and to Hashem he is going,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKAS 22:27; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:9; LUKAS 12:37"> He rises from the seudah and puts aside his me'il and kesones, girding himself with a towel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKAS 7:44; TEHILLIM 51:2; BERESHIS 18:4"> He puts mayim (water) into the basin and began to wash the raglei hatalmidim and to wipe them with the towel with which he had been girded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 3:11–14; YOCHANAN 1:27; LUKAS 5:8"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach comes then to Shim'on Kefa. Kefa says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Adoni, you wash my feet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DANIEL 12:8; YOCHANAN 12:16; YOCHANAN 14:26"> In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to him, Of what I am doing you do not have da'as now, but you will receive binah after these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 36:25; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:16; LETTER TO TITOS 3:5"> Kefa says to him, Never will you wash my feet l'Olam! Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach answered him, Unless I wash you, you do not have a chelek (alloted portion of inheritance) with me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Dt 12:12; Isa 53:10; Lv 5:15-16">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 51:7; TEHILLIM 51:2; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:21"> Shim'on Kefa says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Adoni, then not my feet only but also my hands and my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:1; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:17"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to him, The one having been bathed does not have need except his feet to wash, but is wholly tahor, and you are tehorim, though not all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:26; YOCHANAN 13:2; YOCHANAN 13:21"> For Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach had da'as of the one betraying him. Therefore, he said, Not all are tehorim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:7; YOCHANAN 13:4; YECHEZKEL 24:24"> Therefore, when he washed their feet and resuited himself into his kaftan, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach reclined at tish again and said to them, Do you have da'as of what I have done for you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 6:46; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:3; YOCHANAN 11:28"> You call me Rabbeinu and Adoneinu, and you say well, for Ani Hu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:35; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:16"> If, therefore, I, being Rebbe and Adon, washed your feet, you ought to wash the feet of one another also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:6; MATTITYAHU 11:29; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17–18"> For I gave you a mofet that as I did to you, you may do also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 6:40; YOCHANAN 15:20; MATTITYAHU 10:24–25"> Omein, omein, I say to you, an eved is not greater than his Adon, nor is a shliach (one sent) greater than his Meshalle'ach (Sender).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKAS 11:28; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:17; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:25"> If you have da'as of these things, happy are you, if you put them into practice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="TEHILLIM 41:9; MATTITYAHU 26:23; YOCHANAN 17:12"> I do not speak about all of you — I have da'as of whom I chose — But it is to fulfill the Kitvei Hakodesh, OKHEL LAKHEMI HIGDIL ALAI AKEV (the one eating my bread he lifted up his heel against me).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 41:9[10]">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 43:10; YOCHANAN 14:29; YOCHANAN 16:4"> From now on I tell you before the occurrence that you may have emunah (faith) when it occurs, that Ani Hu.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 41:4; 46:10; 43:10">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKAS 10:16; LUKAS 9:48; MATTITYAHU 25:40"> Omein, omein, I say to you, the one receiving whomever I may send receives me, and the one receiving me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Moshiach">d</a></sup>receives the One having sent me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hashem">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:18; MATTITYAHU 26:21; MARKOS 14:18"> Having said these things, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was spiritually troubled and he gave solemn edut (testimony), and said, Omein, omein, I say to you, that one of you will betray me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKAS 22:23; MATTITYAHU 26:22; MARKOS 14:19"> Moshiachʼs talmidim were looking at one another, being uncertain about whom he speaks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:26; YOCHANAN 21:7; YOCHANAN 21:20"> One of his talmidim was reclining at tish near the kheyk (bosom) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach — the talmid haahuv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:17; LUKAS 1:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:16"> Therefore Shim'on Kefa nods to this one to inquire who it may be about whom Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach speaks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YOCHANAN 21:20; BERESHIS 44:4–12; ESTHER 7:5"> Having leaned back, then, that one thus near the kheyk Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, says to him, Adoni, who is it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:23; YOCHANAN 13:30; MARKOS 14:19–20"> He answers, That one it is to whom I will dip the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="maror into the charoset">f</a></sup>morsel and will give to him. Having dipped, therefore, he takes the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="maror, charoset, matzah">g</a></sup>morsel and gives to Yehudah Ben Shim'on from K'riot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKAS 22:3; YOCHANAN 13:2; KOHELET 9:3"> And after he received the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="maror, charoset, matzah">h</a></sup>morsel, then HaSatan entered into that man; therefore, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to him, What you are bent on doing, do quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> But no one reclining at tish had da'as of why he said this to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:5–6; YOCHANAN 13:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:28"> For some were thinking vi-bahlt (since) Yehudah from K'riot had the aron otzaram (chest of treasury), that he says to him, Buy the things which we need for the Chag (Feast) or that he should give ma'ot chittim (money for wheat financial contributions) for haAniyim (the poor).</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YESHAYAH 59:7; IYOV 24:13–15; MISHLE 4:16"> Therefore, having taken the morsel, that man went out immediately. And it was lailah (night).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:13; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:11; YOCHANAN 7:39"> When, therefore that man went out, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says, Now the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Dan 7:13-14">i</a></sup>receives kavod and Hashem is given kavod in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="HISGALUS 22:1; YESHAYAH 53:10–12; YOCHANAN 17:4–6"> If Hashem receives kavod in him, Hashem will also give him kavod in himself, and Hashem will give him kavod immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:33–34; YOCHANAN 8:21–24; YOCHANAN 16:16–22"> Yeladim, yet a little while I am with you; you will seek me, and as I said to those of Yehudah, Where I go away, you are not able to come. Also to you I say this now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:2; VAYIKRA 19:18; YOCHANAN 15:17"> A mitzvah chadasha I give to you, that you have ahavah (agape) one for the other, as I have had ahavah (agape) for you, so also you have ahavah (agape) one for the other.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Lv 19:18">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:20–21; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:10–14; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:10"> By this will kol Bnei Adam have da'as that my talmidim you are, if ahavah you have one for the other.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:14; YOCHANAN 13:33; YOCHANAN 21:21–22"> Shim'on Kefa says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Adoni, where are you going? In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach answered him, Where I go, you are not able now to follow me, but you will follow later.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:24; MARKOS 14:27–31"> Kefa says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Adoni, why am I not able to follow you now? I will lay down my neshamah for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LUKAS 22:34; MATTITYAHU 26:34; MARKOS 14:30"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach answers, Your neshamah for me will you lay down? Omein, omein, I say to you, a tarnegol will not crow until you make hakhchashah (denial) of me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="as Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">k</a></sup>shloshah times.</span>
</div>
