<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:1; MARKOS 11:1; LUKAS 19:37"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach went to the Har HaZeytim (Mount of Olives).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:55; LUKAS 4:20; LUKAS 21:37"> And at the beginning of Shacharis, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach came again into the Beis Hamikdash and kol haAm (all the people) were coming to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, and having sat, for them he was saying a shiur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And the Sofrim and the Perushim led an isha (woman) having been caught in ni'uf (adultery) and, having stood her in the midst,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lv 20:10">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> They say to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Rabbi, this isha (woman) has been caught in the act of committing ni'uf (adultery).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:10; DEVARIM 22:21–24; YECHEZKEL 16:38–40"> Now in our Torah Moshe Rabbeinu gave us a mitzvah to stone such nashim. You, therefore, what do you say?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lv 20:10; Dt 22:22-24; Job 31:11">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 17:13; LUKAS 10:25; KOHELET 3:7"> But this they were saying to put a nissayon before Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach that they might have something about which to accuse him, but he, having stooped down with his finger, was writing on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:1–5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:21–25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:1–3"> But as they were remaining, questioning him, he stood up and said to them, The one without chet (sin) among you, at her let him be first to throw a stone.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Dt 17:7; Ezek 16:40">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And again, having stooped down, he was writing on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="KOHELET 7:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:15; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:20"> And the ones having heard were departing, one by one, beginning with the zekenim, and he was left alone with the isha standing before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YESHAYAH 41:11–12"> And having stood up, he said to her, Isha, where are they? Does no one condemn you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:17; YOCHANAN 5:14; YESHAYAH 1:16–18"> And she said, No one, Adoni. And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Neither do I condemn you. Go and practice chet no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:46; YOCHANAN 9:5; YOCHANAN 12:35"> Then again he spoke to them, saying, Ani Hu HaOhr HaOlam Hazeh. The one following me will never walk in choshech, but will have the Ohr HaChayyim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 9:1; 42:8; 49:6; 60:1,3; Prov 4:18">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:31–47"> Therefore, the Perushim said to him, You give solemn edut (testimony) about yourself. But your edut is not ne'emanah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:28; YOCHANAN 13:3; YOCHANAN 8:42"> In reply, he said to them, Even if I give solemn edut about myself, my edut is ne'emanah, because I have da'as of where I came from and where I go, but you have no da'as of where I come from or where I go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:7; YOCHANAN 7:24; YOCHANAN 12:47"> You judge according to the basar; I do not judge anyone.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1Sm 16:7">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:31; YOCHANAN 8:29; YOCHANAN 16:32"> But even if I judge, my psak din, my mishpat, is emes, because it is not I alone but the One having sent me, HaAv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEVARIM 19:15; DEVARIM 17:6; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:28"> And it has been written even in your Torah, that the edut (testimony) of two is ne'emanah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Dt 17:6; 19:15">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:38; YOCHANAN 8:58; YOCHANAN 8:25"> Ani Hu the one giving solemn edut (testimony) about myself and the One having sent me, HaAv, gives solemn edut about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:3; YOCHANAN 8:54–55; YOCHANAN 14:6–9"> Therefore, they were saying to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Where is HaAv of you? In reply, he said, You have da'as of neither me nor HaAv of me. If you had had da'as of me, you would have had da'as of HaAv of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:30; YOCHANAN 7:8; MARKOS 12:41"> These devarim, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach spoke in the Beis HaOtzar while saying shiurim in the Beis Hamikdash, and no one arrested him, because his sha'ah had not yet come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:34; YOCHANAN 8:24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:17–18"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, therefore, again to them, I go away (Rebbe's Histalkus) and you will seek me, and in your chet you will go to your mavet. Where I go you are not able to come.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Dt 24:16; Ezek 3:18">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:52; TEHILLIM 22:6; YOCHANAN 8:48"> Therefore, those of Yehudah were saying, Does he mean he will commit suicide, because he says, Where I go you are not able to come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:31; YOCHANAN 17:14; YOCHANAN 17:16"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying to them, You are from mattah (below); Ani Hu from ma'lah (above). You are of the Olam Hazeh; I am not of the Olam Hazeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:36; MARKOS 16:16; YOCHANAN 3:18"> I said, therefore, to you that you will go to your mavet in your averos (sins), for if you do not have emunah (faith) that Ani Hu,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isa 41:4; Ex 3:14-16">h</a></sup>you will die in your chatta'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:17–29; LUKAS 22:67; YOCHANAN 1:22"> They were saying, therefore, to him, Who are you? Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, What I have told you from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:15; YOCHANAN 12:47–50; YOCHANAN 8:40"> I have much to speak regarding you, much about you worthy of harsha'ah (condemnation), but the One having sent me is ne'eman and what I heard from Him these things I speak in the Olam Hazeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YESHAYAH 6:9; YOCHANAN 8:43; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:3–4"> They did not have da'as that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was speaking to them about Elohim HaAv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:19; YOCHANAN 5:30; YOCHANAN 6:38"> Therefore, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, When you perform the hagbah (lifting up) of the Bar Enosh, you will have da'as that Ani Hu,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Isa 41:4; Ex 3:14-16">i</a></sup>and from myself I do nothing, but as HaAv of me taught me, these things I speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:38; YOCHANAN 16:32; YOCHANAN 4:34"> And the One having sent me is with me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Prov 8:30">j</a></sup>He did not leave me alone, because I always do the things that please Him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Prov 8:30; Isa 50:5; 43:10TARGUM HASHIVIM">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:31; YOCHANAN 2:23; YOCHANAN 11:45"> As Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying these things, many put their emunah in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9; YOCHANAN 15:4–9; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:14"> Therefore, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying to the Yehudim who had emunah in him, If you remain in the dvar of me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach">l</a></sup>then you are my talmidim indeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:2; YOCHANAN 8:36; YOCHANAN 17:17"> And you will have da'as of HaEmes (the Truth) and HaEmes will give you cherut (freedom).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Lv 25:10; Isa 61:1-3; 53:4-12">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 3:9; YOCHANAN 8:39; BERESHIS 15:13"> They answered Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, We are of the zera Avraham and to no one have we ever been enslaved. How do you say, You will become Bnei Chorin (freedmen)?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Neh 9:36">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:16; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:19; MISHLE 5:22"> In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Omein, omein, I say to you that everyone practicing Chet is an eved HaChet.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Gn 4:7">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:4–7; LUKAS 15:31; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:15–17"> But the eved does not remain in the Bais ad Olam. HaBen remains ad Olam.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Gn 21:10; Ex 21:2; Dt 15:12">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:2"> If therefore HaBen makes you Bnei Chorin, you shall be Bnei Chorin indeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:14; YOCHANAN 8:45–47; YOCHANAN 7:1"> I have da'as that you are zera Avraham, but you seek to kill me, because my dvar has no place in you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Jer 31:31-34">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:19; YOCHANAN 8:44; YOCHANAN 8:41"> What I have seen in the presence of HaAv of me, of that, I make solemn declaration. Therefore, the things you have heard from your father you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:7; YOCHANAN 8:37; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:7"> They answered and said unto him, Avinu hu Avraham. Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, If you were yeladim of Avraham, you would have been doing the ma'asei Avraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:16"> But now you are seeking to kill me, a man who has told you HaEmes, which I heard from Hashem; this was not a ma'aseh that Avraham did.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Ps 15:2">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YESHAYAH 64:8; YOCHANAN 8:38; YESHAYAH 63:16"> You are doing the ma'asim of your Av. They said, therefore, to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, We are not mamzerim who have been born of zenunim (fornication). We have Av Echad, Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Gn 38:24; Isa 63:16; 64:8; Mal 2:10; Dt 32:6">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:49; YOCHANAN 14:10; YOCHANAN 17:8"> He said to them, If Hashem were the Av of you, you would have had ahavah for me, for I came forth from Hashem, and now I am present here, for I have not come on my own, but HaAv sent me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Isa 55:11">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:39–40; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:7–8; YOCHANAN 7:17"> Why do you not understand my devarim? Because you are not able to give obedient shemah (hearing) to my dvar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:10; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:12; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:8–10"> You are from the Av of you, HaSatan,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Gn 3:4-5; Ps 58:3[4]">u</a></sup>and the ta'avot of your Av you want to do, for that one was a rotze'ach from Bereshis, and he has not taken a stand in HaEmes, because HaEmes is not in him. When he speaks the sheker, he speaks naturally, because he is a shakran (liar) and the av of it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Gn 3:4; 2Chr 18:21; Ps 5:6; 12:2">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:3–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:16; YOCHANAN 18:37"> But because I speak HaEmes, you do not have bitachon (trust) in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:15; YOCHANAN 16:8; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:22"> Who among you convicts me of chet (sin)? If I speak HaEmes, why do you not have emunah in me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:26–27; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9; YOCHANAN 18:37"> The one who is of Hashem hears the divrei Hashem. Therefore, you do not hear because you are not of Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:20; YOCHANAN 8:52; MATTITYAHU 10:5"> In reply, those of Yehudah said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Did we not rightly say that you are a Shomroni (Samaritan) and that you have a shed (demon)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:23; YESHAYAH 49:3; YOCHANAN 13:31–32"> In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, I do not have a shed (demon). But I honor HaAv of me and you dishonor me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:41; YOCHANAN 8:54; YOCHANAN 7:18"> But I do not seek my own kavod; there is One seeking and judging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:25–26; YOCHANAN 5:24; YOCHANAN 3:15–16"> Omein, omein, I say to you, regarding the dvar of me, if anyone is shomer, he will never see mavet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:24; YOCHANAN 8:48; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:9"> Therefore, those of Yehudah said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, Now we have da'as that you have a shed, Avraham died and the Nevi'im; yet you say if anyone keeps the dvar of me he will never taste mavet (death).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Rv 21:8">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:1–7"> Surely you are not greater than Avraham Avinu, who died? And the Nevi'im died. Whom do you make yourself to be?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Zech 1:5">x</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:50; MISHLE 25:27; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:17"> In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, If I give myself kavod, the kavod of me is nothing. It is HaAv of me Who is giving me kavod, Whom you say, He is Eloheinu.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Isa 63:16">y</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:19; YOCHANAN 8:44; YOCHANAN 7:28–29"> And you have not had da'as of Him. But I have had da'as of Him. If I would say that I do not have da'as of Him, I will be like you, a shakran (liar); but I do have da'as of Him, and I keep His dvar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:13; BERESHIS 22:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:7–9"> Avraham your Av had lev same'ach to see the Yom of me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="the Yom HaMoshiach">z</a></sup>and he saw it and had simcha.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Gn 18:18">aa</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> Therefore those of Yehudah said to him, You do not yet possess fifty years and yet you have seen Avraham Avinu?</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:17; YOCHANAN 17:5"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Omein, omein, I say to you, before Avraham came into being, Ani Hu.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Ex 3:4; 6:3; Isa 41:4; 43:10,13">ab</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:8; YOCHANAN 10:30–33; YOCHANAN 10:39–40"> Then they took up stones that they might stone him, but Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was hidden and departed out of the Beis Hamikdash.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Ex 17:4; Lv 24:16; 1Sm 30:6">ac</a></sup></span>
