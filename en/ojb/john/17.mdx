<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:9; MISHLE 18:10; YOCHANAN 17:12–18"> And I am no longer in the Olam Hazeh, but they are in the Olam Hazeh, and I am coming to You, Avi HaKadosh. Be shomer over them in Shmecha, which You have given me, that they may be Echad as we are Echad.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 133:1; Dt 6:4; Gn 2:24">c</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 17:14"> They are not of the Olam Hazeh, just as I am not of the Olam Hazeh.</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 2:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:11; YOCHANAN 17:17"> And for their sake, I set myself apart as kodesh that they also may be set apart for use that is kodesh in HaEmes.</span>
