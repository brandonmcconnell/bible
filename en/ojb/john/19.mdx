<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:5; YESHAYAH 50:6; MATTITYAHU 20:19"> Therefore, then, Pilate took Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and had him scourged.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Dt 25:3; Isa 50:6; 53:5">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MARKOS 15:16–20; LUKAS 23:11; MATTITYAHU 27:27–31"> And the &#91;Roman&#93; chaiyalim (soldiers), having woven a wreath out of thorns, put it on his rosh and a purple &#91;royal&#93; robe they threw around Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:29; MATTITYAHU 26:49; LUKAS 1:28"> And they were coming up to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, and they were saying, Hail! You Melech HaYehudim! And they were repeatedly striking him in the face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:38; YOCHANAN 19:6; LUKAS 23:4"> And Pilate went outside again and says to them, Hinei, I bring him outside to you, in order that you may have da'as that I find no avon (offense), no cause for punishment, in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:2; YESHAYAH 7:14"> Therefore, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach came forth outside, wearing the wreath of thorns and the purple robe. And Pilate says to them, Hinei HaIsh!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Zech 6:12">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:31; YOCHANAN 19:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:23"> Therefore, when the Rashei Hakohanim and the mesharetim saw Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, they cried out, saying, Hang him on HaEtz! Hang him on HaEtz! Pilate says to them, You take him and you hang him on HaEtz! I find no avon in him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Dt 21:22">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="VAYIKRA 24:16; YOCHANAN 5:18; MATTITYAHU 26:63–66"> In reply, those of Yehudah said, We have a Torah and, according to the Torah, he must die,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Lv 24:16">d</a></sup>because he made himself to be the Ben HaElohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:11–19"> Therefore, when Pilate heard this dvar he was afraid even more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:33; TEHILLIM 38:13–15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:28"> And Pilate entered into the Praetorium again and says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, From where are you? But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach did not give an answer.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Isa 53:7">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DANIEL 5:19; YOCHANAN 18:39; DANIEL 3:14–15"> Therefore, Pilate says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, To me you do not speak? Do you not have da'as that I have samchut (authority) to free you and I have samchut (authority) to hang you on HaEtz?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:1; YOCHANAN 3:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:23"> In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach answered him, You do not have samchut (authority) against me at all, except it had been given to you from above; therefore, the avon is gadol, is even greater, the avon of the one having handed me over to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:6–7; YOCHANAN 18:33–36; MARKOS 6:16–26"> From this point, Pilate began seeking to free Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach; but those of Yehudah cried out, saying, If this man you free, you are no friend of Caesarʼs; everyone making himself a Melech speaks against Caesar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TEHILLIM 82:5–7; MATTITYAHU 27:19; AMOS 4:7"> Therefore, Pilate, having heard these devarim, led Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach out, and Pilate sat down upon a tribunal (seat of judgment) in a place being called in Aramaic Gabta, The Pavement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:62; YOCHANAN 19:5; MARKOS 15:25"> Now Erev Pesach was fast approaching, the sha'ah (hour, time) being about the shishit (sixth, the sixth hour, about noon),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="i.e., with Erev Pesach coming at sundown">f</a></sup>and Pilate says to those of Yehudah, Hinei your Melech!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 23:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:36; YOCHANAN 19:6"> Therefore, these cried out, Away, away, hang him on HaEtz! Pilate says to them, Shall I hang on HaEtz your Melech? In reply, the Kohen Gadol said, We do not have a Melech except Caesar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 23:24–25; MATTITYAHU 27:26–31; MARKOS 15:15–20"> Then, therefore, Pilate delivered Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach to them that he should be hanged on HaEtz.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Dt 21:22">g</a></sup>Therefore, they took Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKAS 23:33; LUKAS 14:27; LUKAS 23:26"> And carrying by himself HaEtz (The Tree),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gn 22:6; Dt 21:23">h</a></sup>he went out to the place being called Mekom HaGulgolet (Place of the Skull), which is called in Aramaic Gulgolta.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:12; MARKOS 15:24–27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:13"> There they hanged Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach on HaEtz and with him two others on this side and on that side, and, in the middle, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:37; LUKAS 23:38; MARKOS 15:26"> And, also, Pilate wrote out an inscription and had it placarded on top of HaEtz (The Tree). And what it said was, yehoshua, the one from natzeret, melech hayehudim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:14; HISGALUS 16:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:2"> This inscription, therefore, many of those of Yehudah read, because the place where Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was hanged on HaEtz was near the Ir (City). And it had been written in Ivrit, in Latin, and in Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:14; YOCHANAN 19:19"> Therefore, the Judean Rashei Hakohanim were saying to Pilate, Do not write Melech HaYehudim. Rather write, That one said I am Melech HaYehudim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="TEHILLIM 76:10; TEHILLIM 65:7; MISHLE 8:29"> In reply, Pilate said, What I have written, I have written.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="1Chr 21:17; Isa 53:8">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKAS 23:34; MARKOS 15:24; MATTITYAHU 27:35"> Therefore the chaiyalim (soldiers), when they hanged Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach on HaEtz, took his garments and divided them into arba'ah (four) parts, to each chaiyal (soldier) a part. They also took his kesones.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Lv 16:4; Ps 110:4">j</a></sup>Now the kesones was seamless, woven from the top in one piece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="TEHILLIM 22:18; YOCHANAN 19:28; YOCHANAN 19:36–37"> Therefore, they said to one another, Let us not tear it but let us cast lots for it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Esth 3:7">k</a></sup>to see whose it will be. They did this in order that the Kitvei Hakodesh might be fulfilled, that which says, YECHALKU VEGADAI LAHEM V'AL LEVUSHI YAPILU GORAL (They divide my garments among them and for my clothing they cast lots).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Ps 22:18[19], Ex 28:32">l</a></sup>Therefore the chaiyalim did these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKAS 23:49; MATTITYAHU 27:55–56; YOCHANAN 20:1"> But there had stood beside HaEtz (Tree) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach his Em (Mother) and the achot (sister) of his Em, and Miryam the wife of Klofah, and Miryam from Magdala.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YOCHANAN 21:24; YOCHANAN 20:2; YOCHANAN 2:4"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, therefore, having seen his Em (Mother) and the talmid haahuv having stood by, says to his Em, Isha, hinei your ben!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:48–50; YOCHANAN 16:32; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:2–4"> Then Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to the talmid haahuv, Hinei, Imecha (your Mother)! And from that sha'ah (hour, time) the talmid took her into his own bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="TEHILLIM 69:21; TEHILLIM 22:15; YOCHANAN 19:30"> After this, having had da'as that already everything has become shleimah, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, that the Kitvei Hakodesh may be fulfilled, says, Ani tzameh (I thirst).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Ps 22:15[16]">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 19:18; LUKAS 23:36; MARKOS 15:36"> A k'li (vessel) full of sour wine chometz vinegar was set there.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Ps 69:21[22]">n</a></sup>The sponge full of vinegar having been wrapped around a hyssop branch<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Ex 12:22">o</a></sup>they brought to his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YOCHANAN 17:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:14–17"> Therefore, when Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach received the vinegar, he said, Nishlam! (It is finished!)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Job 19:26-27TARGUM HASHIVIM">p</a></sup>And having bowed his rosh, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach gave up his neshamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:14; YOCHANAN 19:42; MARKOS 15:42"> Therefore, those of Yehudah, vi-bahlt (since) it was Preparation Day,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="with Chag and Shabbos fast approaching">q</a></sup>they did not want the geviyot to be left on HaEtz (Tree)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Ex 12:16; Dt 21:22-23; Josh 8:29; 10:26-27">r</a></sup>during Shabbos, for it was Shabbat HaGadol,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Lv 23:11">s</a></sup>requested Pilate to have the legs broken and the geviyot taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:18; LUKAS 23:39–43"> Therefore the chaiyalim (soldiers) came and broke the first manʼs legs and then the other one hanging on HaEtz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> But having come to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, when they saw that he was already niftar (deceased), they did not break his legs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:6; ZECHARYAH 12:10; ZECHARYAH 13:1"> But one of the chaiyalim pierced<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Zech 12:10">t</a></sup>his side with a romakh (spear), and immediately out came dahm and mayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="YOCHANAN 21:24; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:3–4; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:1–3"> And the ed re'iyah (eyewitness) of this has given solemn edut (testimony). And his edut is ne'emanah (trustworthy). And that one has da'as that he speaks Emes that you might have emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="TEHILLIM 34:20; BAMIDBAR 9:12; SHEMOT 12:46"> For these things happened that the Kitvei Hakodesh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="19:24,28,37">u</a></sup>might be fulfilled, V'ETZEM LO TISHBERU VO (And not a bone of him shall be broken).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Ex 12:46; Num 9:12; Ps 34:20-21">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 12:10; HISGALUS 1:7; TEHILLIM 22:16–17"> And again the Kitvei Hakodesh says, V'HIBITU on him ES ASHER DAKARU (And they will look on him whom they pierced.)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Zech 12:10">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MARKOS 15:42–47; MATTITYAHU 27:57–61; MISHLE 29:25"> Now after these things, Yosef from Ramatayim, being a talmid of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, but besod (in secret) because of fear of those of Yehudah, requested Pilate that he might take the gufat Yehoshua (Ps 16:9-10; Job 19:25-27; Isa 53:11), and Pilate allowed it. Yosef from Ramatayim came, therefore, and took the gufat Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:50–52; SHIR HASHIRIM 4:14; YOCHANAN 3:1–21"> And also Rav Nakdimon came, the one having come first to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach balailah, bearing a mixture of myrrh and aloes, about one hundred pounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:44; YOCHANAN 20:5–7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:6"> Therefore, they took the Guf HaYeshua (Body of Yehoshua) and bound it in linen clothes with spices as is the burial minhag with the Yehudim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LUKAS 23:53; MATTITYAHU 27:60; YOCHANAN 20:15"> Now there was in the place where Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was pierced on HaEtz a gahn (garden) and in the gahn a kever chadash (new tomb) in which never yet anyone had been placed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:31; YOCHANAN 19:14; YESHAYAH 53:9"> And so, because it was Preparation Day for those of Yehudah and because the kever was nearby, they laid Moshiach there.</span>
