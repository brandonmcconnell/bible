<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:46; MISHLE 31:10–12; YOCHANAN 21:2"> On Yom HaShelishi, there was a Chasunoh (Wedding Feast) in Kanah in the Galil; and the Em (Mother) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:40–49; MATTITYAHU 10:40–42; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:39"> He and his talmidim were also invited to the Chasunoh (Wedding Feast).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TEHILLIM 104:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:6; YESHAYAH 24:11"> And when yayin (wine) was lacking, the Em of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to him, They do not have yayin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:6; YOCHANAN 13:1; YOCHANAN 7:30"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to her, Mah lanu valach, Isha?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gn 3:15">a</a></sup>My sha'ah (hour, time) has not yet come.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Mt 26:18,27-28">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:14; BERESHIS 6:22; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:8"> The Em of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to the mesharetim, Asher yomar lakhem ta'asu (Do whatever he tells you).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gn 41:55">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:25; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:26"> Now there were shesh (six) stone water jars lying there. These were for the Jewish tohorot, each holding twenty to thirty gallons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 4:2–6; YOCHANAN 2:5; YOCHANAN 2:3"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, Fill with mayim. And they filled them up to the top.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 2:9; MISHLE 3:5–6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:7"> And he says to them, Draw now and bring to the Rosh HaMesibba (Head of the Reception, Feast). And they brought it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:46; YOCHANAN 7:17; TEHILLIM 119:100"> And when the Rosh HaMesibba tasted the mayim having become yayin, and when he did not have da'as of where it came from — but the mesharetim had da'as, the ones having drawn the water — the Rosh HaMesibba summoned the Choson (Bridegroom).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HISGALUS 7:16–17; MISHLE 9:1–6; LUKAS 16:25"> And the Rosh HaMesibba says to him, Everyone sets out the yayin hatov first, and when they have become drunk, he sets out the inferior; you have kept the yayin hatov until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:30–31; YOCHANAN 1:14; YOCHANAN 3:2"> This was the reshit (beginning) of the otot (miraculous signs) Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach accomplished in Kanah of the Galil, and he manifested the kavod (glory) of him, v'ya'aminu bo (and they put their faith in him).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ex 14:31">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:46; MATTITYAHU 13:55–56; MARKOS 6:3"> After this he went down to K'far-Nachum and the Em of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and the achim and his talmidim also, and there they remained not many yamim (days).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 2:41; YOCHANAN 11:55; YOCHANAN 2:23"> And Pesach was fast approaching, and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach went up to Yerushalayim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dt 16:1-6">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 19:45–46; MARKOS 11:15; MARKOS 11:17"> And he found in the Beis Hamikdash the ones selling oxen and sheep and doves<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Lv 1:14; Dt 14:26">f</a></sup>and the coin-dealers sitting.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Dt 14:25">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:6; ZECHARYAH 4:6; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:4"> And having made a shot (whip) out of ropes Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach drove all of them out of the Beis Hamikdash, both the sheep and the cattle. He also poured out the coins of the machalifei hakesafim (money-changers) and overturned their tishen (tables).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:13; YIRMEYAH 7:11; MARKOS 11:17"> And to the ones selling doves, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Take these things from here. Do not make the Bais Avi a bais hasokharim (house of merchants).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Zech 14:21">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TEHILLIM 69:9; TEHILLIM 119:139"> The talmidim of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach remembered that it had been written, KINAT BETECHA AKHALATNI (Zeal for Your bais hath consumed me).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ps 69:9">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:23; MARKOS 8:11; MARKOS 11:27–28"> In reply, those of Yehudah then said to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, What ot (miraculous sign) do you show us for these things you do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARKOS 14:58; MARKOS 15:29; MATTITYAHU 27:40"> In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Bring churban to this heikhal and in shloshah yamim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Jon 2:1; Hos 6:2">j</a></sup>I will raise it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Those of Yehudah then said, In forty and six years this Heikhal was built and you in shloshah yamim will raise it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:19; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:20–22"> But that one was speaking about the heikhal of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiachʼs basar.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gn 47:18; Ps 16:9-10; Job 19:25-27; Isa 53:10-11">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:16; YOCHANAN 16:4; LUKAS 24:7–8"> Therefore, when he underwent the Techiyas HaMoshiach (Resurrection of the Moshiach), his talmidim remembered that this he was saying and v'ya'aminu<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Ex 14:31">l</a></sup>(and they put their faith) in the Kitvei Hakodesh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Ps 16:9-10">m</a></sup>and the dvar which Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:31; LUKAS 8:13; YOCHANAN 6:14"> And when Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was in Yerushalayim during Pesach at the Chag (Feast), many from Yehudah had emunah (faith) in ha-Shem of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, seeing his otot (miraculous signs) which he was doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:64; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:24"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was not entrusting himself to them, because he had da'as of kol Bnei Adam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:11; MATTITYAHU 9:4; YOCHANAN 6:64"> And he had no need that anyone should give solemn edut (testimony) about them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Isa 11:3">n</a></sup>for he knew what was in Bnei Adam.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Dt 31:21; 1Kgs 8:39">o</a></sup></span>
</div>
