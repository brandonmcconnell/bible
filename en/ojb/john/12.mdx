<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARKOS 14:3–8; YOCHANAN 11:1; LUKAS 24:50"> Then Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach came into Beit-Anyah (Bethany) shesh yamim before Pesach. This was the place where El'azar was, whom Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach made to stand up alive from the mesim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKAS 22:27; LUKAS 10:38–42; HISGALUS 3:20"> Then they made Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach a seudah there. And Marta was serving, and El'azar was one of the ones reclining at tish with Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MARKOS 14:3–9; YOCHANAN 11:2; LUKAS 10:38–39"> Then Miryam, having taken a pound of mishcha (ointment) of expensive genuine spikenard, anointed the feet of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and wiped off with her hair his feet. And the bais was filled with the aroma of the mishcha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:4; SHMUEL ALEF 17:28–29; YOCHANAN 13:2"> And Yehudah from K'riot, one of the talmidim of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, the talmid being about to betray him, says</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKAS 18:22; LUKAS 12:33; MARKOS 14:4–5"> Why was this mishcha not sold for three hundred denarii and given to haAniyim (the Poor)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:29; TEHILLIM 50:16–20; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:6"> But he said this not because it mattered to him about haAniyim, but because he was a ganav and he was removing what was put in the aron otzaram (chest of treasury).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:12; MARKOS 14:6; MATTITYAHU 27:57–60"> Therefore Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Let her alone. She kept this for the Yom HaKevurah (Day of Burial) of me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Isa 53:9">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEVARIM 15:11; MARKOS 14:7; MATTITYAHU 26:11"> For haAniyim you have always with you, but you do not always have me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dt 15:11">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:14; YOCHANAN 11:43–45; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:10–11"> When the great multitude of the Yehudim had da'as of where Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was, they came not only because of him, but also because of El'azar, that they may see the one whom he made to stand up alive from the mesim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKAS 16:31; MATTITYAHU 2:16; YOCHANAN 11:47–53"> But the Rashei Hakohanim took counsel that they also might kill El'azar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:45; YOCHANAN 12:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:45"> Because many of the Yehudim were leaving and believing in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach on account of El'azar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MARKOS 11:7–10; MATTITYAHU 21:4–9; LUKAS 19:35–38"> On the next day the great multitude, the one having come to the Chag (Feast), having heard that Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach is coming to Yerushalayim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TEHILLIM 118:25–26; HISGALUS 15:3; MATTITYAHU 21:9–11"> Took the branches of the temarim (palms)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Lv 23:40">c</a></sup>and went out to a meeting with him and were crying out: HOSHAN NAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ps 118:25-26">d</a></sup>BARUCH HABAH B'SHEM ADONOI!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Zeph 3:15TARGUM HASHIVIM">e</a></sup>Melech Yisroel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 9:9; MARKOS 11:1–7; MATTITYAHU 21:1–7"> And he found an ayir (young donkey), and he sat on it, just as it has been written,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 9:9; YESHAYAH 62:11; TZEFANYAH 3:16–17"> Do not fear BAT TZIYON, HINEI MALKECH YAVO LACH ROKHEV AL AYIR BEN ATONOT (Do not fear, Daughter of Zion. Look! Your King is coming, sitting on a foal of donkeys.)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Zech 9:9; Isa 35:4; Zeph 3:14fTARGUM HASHIVIM">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 2:22; YOCHANAN 14:26; YOCHANAN 7:39"> Regarding these things his talmidim did not have binah in the beginning but when he received kavod, then they remembered that these things had been written with respect to him and that they did these things to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:2; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:9–12; YOCHANAN 19:35"> So the crowd that had been with Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach when he called El'azar out of the kever and made him stand up alive from the mesim were giving solemn edut (testimony).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:11"> Therefore, the multitude met Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach because they heard him to have done this ot (miraculous sign).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:6; TEHILLIM 22:27; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:2"> Therefore the Perushim said to themselves, You see, you can do nothing! Kol Bnei Adam have gone after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:35; YESHAYAH 11:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:4"> Now there were some Yevanim among the ones going up to worship at the Chag (Feast).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 2:2; LUKAS 19:2–4; YOCHANAN 6:40"> These ones, therefore, approached Philippos from Beit-Tzaidah of the Galil, and were asking him, Sir, we want to see Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MARKOS 10:13–14; LUKAS 9:49–50; YOCHANAN 1:40–41"> Philippos comes and tells Andrew, and Andrew and Philippos come and tell Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:31–32; YOCHANAN 17:1–5; YESHAYAH 60:9"> And Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach answers them, saying, The sha'ah (hour, time) has come that the Bar Enosh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Dan 7:13">g</a></sup>may receive kavod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:36–38; YESHAYAH 53:10–12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:9–10"> Omein, omein, I say to you, unless the gargeer hakhitah (grain of wheat) having fallen into the ground, dies, it remains alone; but if it dies, much p'ri it bears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:39; LUKAS 9:23–24; MARKOS 8:35"> The Ohev (lover) of his neshamah will lose it, but the Soneh (hater) of his neshamah in the Olam Hazeh will keep it unto Chayyei Olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:10; SHMUEL ALEF 2:30"> If anyone serves me, let him follow me, and where I am, there also my eved will be. If anyone serves me, HaAv will honor him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:23; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:7; MATTITYAHU 26:42"> Now my neshamah has been troubled, and what may I say? Save me from this sha'ah? No, it is for this tachlis that I came to this sha'ah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ps 6:3,4f; 42:5,11">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:31–32; MATTITYAHU 26:42; HISGALUS 5:9–14"> Avi, bring kavod to Your Name. A bat kol came out of Shomayim, saying, I have both brought kavod to it and I will bring kavod to it again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="IYOV 37:2–5; HISGALUS 6:1; HISGALUS 14:2"> Therefore, the multitude that was standing there and heard, said, That was ra'am (thunder)! Others were saying, A malach (angel) has spoken to him!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Job 37:5; Gn 21:17">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:42; YOCHANAN 11:15; YOCHANAN 5:34"> In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, Not for my sake has this bat kol come, but for yours.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ex 19:9">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:4; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:19; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:8"> Now is the Mishpat HaOlam Hazeh. Now the Sar HaOlam Hazeh will be cast out!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:14; YOCHANAN 8:28; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:24"> And if I receive the hagbah (lifting up) from ha'aretz, I will draw kol Bnei Adam to myself.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Isa 11:10">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:32; YOCHANAN 21:19"> And this Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach was saying, signifying by what kind of mavet he was about to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 37:24–25; YESHAYAH 9:7; DANIEL 7:14"> Then the crowd answered him, We heard from the Torah that the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach remains l'Olam (forever) and how do you say that it is necessary for the Bar Enosh to be lifted up? Who is this Bar Enosh?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Ps 89:4,36-37; 110:4; Isa 9:7; Ezek 37:25; Dan 7:14">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:46; YOCHANAN 16:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:8"> Therefore, he said to them, Yet a little time is the Ohr among you. Walk while you have the Ohr lest choshech overtake you; the one walking in the choshech does not have da'as of where he is going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:8; YOCHANAN 8:59; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:47–48"> While you have the Ohr, walk in the Ohr that you may become Bnei HaOhr. These things spoke Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach. And, having gone away, he was hidden from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKAS 16:31; YOCHANAN 1:11; YOCHANAN 15:24"> Though so many otot he had done before them, they lacked emunah in him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:20"> That the dvar of Yeshayah HaNavi may be fulfilled, which said, Adonoi, MI HE'EMIN LISHMU'ATEINU UZERO'A HASHEM AL-MI NIGLATAH?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Isa 53:1">m</a></sup>(Who has believed our report and to whom is the arm of Hashem revealed)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:38; YOCHANAN 5:44; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:14"> Therefore, they were not able to have emunah, because, again Yeshayah said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="YESHAYAH 6:10; MATTITYAHU 13:13–15; LUKAS 8:10"> HE HAS BLINDED THEIR EYES, HASHMIN LEV HAAM HAZEH (Harden the heart of this people), THAT THEY MIGHT NOT SEE WITH THEIR EYES AND HAVE BINAH WITH THEIR LEV AND MIGHT TURN AND I WILL HEAL THEM.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Isa 6:10">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YESHAYAH 6:1–5; YESHAYAH 6:9–10; YOCHANAN 1:14"> These things Yeshayah spoke because he saw the kavod (glory) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and he spoke about him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Isa 6:1">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:22; YOCHANAN 7:13; YESHAYAH 51:7"> Nevertheless, however, even among the manhigim (leaders) many had emunah (faith) in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach. But because of the Perushim, they were not making hoda'ah (confession) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, lest under the cherem ban they should be put away from the shul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:44; MATTITYAHU 23:5–7; YOCHANAN 5:41"> For their ahavah was for the kavod Bnei Adam rather than the kavod Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="1Sm 15:30">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:40; YOCHANAN 13:20; YOCHANAN 5:24"> But Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach cried out, The ma'amin in me does not have emunah in me, but in the One having sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:9–10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:15"> And the one seeing me sees the One having sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:12; TEHILLIM 36:9; YOCHANAN 9:5"> I have come as an Ohr (Light) into the Olam Hazeh that everyone believing in me may not remain in the choshech (darkness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:17; YOCHANAN 8:15–16; YOCHANAN 12:48"> And if anyone hears my devarim and of my devarim is not shomer, I do not judge him, for I did not come to bring the Olam Hazeh into mishpat, but that I may save the Olam Hazeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="LUKAS 10:16; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:27–28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:16"> The one setting me aside and not receiving the devarim has his Shofet: the dvar which I spoke will judge him on the Yom HaAcharon.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="i.e., the Yom HaDin">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:10; YOCHANAN 8:26; YOCHANAN 5:30"> Because I do not speak on my own, but the one having sent me &#91;Elohim&#93; HaAv He has given me a mitzvah of what I may say and what I may speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="YOCHANAN 17:3; YOCHANAN 6:68; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:20"> And I have da'as that the mitzvah of Him is Chayyei Olam. Whatsoever things I speak, I speak, therefore, just as HaAv has told me.</span>
