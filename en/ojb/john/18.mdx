<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARKOS 14:32; MATTITYAHU 26:36; SHMUEL BAIS 15:23"> These things having said, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach went out with his talmidim across the Kidron Valley,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2Sm 15:23">a</a></sup>where there was a Gahn (Garden) into which Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach entered with his talmidim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKAS 21:37; LUKAS 22:39; MARKOS 11:11–12"> Now also Yehudah from K'riot, the one betraying Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, had da'as of the place, because often Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach gathered there with his talmidim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:12; LUKAS 22:47–53; TEHILLIM 22:12"> Then, Yehudah from K'riot, having taken the band of chaiyalim (soldiers) and avadim of the Rashei Hakohanim and of the Perushim, comes there with torches and lamps and weapons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:1; YOCHANAN 18:7; TEHILLIM 27:3"> Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, therefore, having had da'as of everything coming upon him, went out and says to them, Whom do you seek?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 21:11; YOCHANAN 19:19; MATTITYAHU 2:23"> They said in reply, Yehoshua, the one from Natzeret. Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, Ani Hu. Now also Yehudah, the one betraying him, had stood with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 27:2; TEHILLIM 70:2–3; MELACHIM BAIS 1:9–15"> When therefore Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach told them, Ani Hu, they recoiled and fell to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:4"> Therefore, again he questioned them, Whom do you seek? And they said, Yehoshua, the one from Natzeret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:28; MATTITYAHU 26:56; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:9"> In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, I told you that Ani Hu. If therefore you seek me, let these Bnei Adam go away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 17:12"> That may be fulfilled the dvar which said, Those whom You have given me, I did not lose any one of them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Yn 17:12; 10:11">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MARKOS 14:47; MATTITYAHU 26:51–54; LUKAS 22:49–51"> Then Shim'on Kefa, having a cherev (sword), drew it and struck the eved of the Kohen Gadol and cut off his right ear. The name of the eved was Malchus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 20:22; MATTITYAHU 26:39; YOCHANAN 20:17"> Therefore, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to Kefa, Put the cherev into the sheath. The kos which HaAv of me has given me, should I not drink it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:57; YOCHANAN 18:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:37"> Then the band of chaiyalim, their commander, and the avadim (officials) of Yehudah took him and performed the akedah (binding) on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:24; YOCHANAN 11:51; MATTITYAHU 26:3"> First they led him to Anan (Annas), for he was khoten (father-in-law) of Caiapha, who was Kohen Gadol that year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:49–52"> Now Caiapha was the one having given counsel to those of Yehudah that it is a bedievedike thing (expedient) for one man to die for HaAm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 22:54; MARKOS 14:54; MATTITYAHU 26:58–68"> Now Shim'on Kefa and another talmid were following Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach. And that other talmid was known to the Kohen Gadol, and that other talmid entered with Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach into the court of the Kohen Gadol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:69; MARKOS 14:66–68; LUKAS 22:55–57"> But Kefa had stood at the sha'ar outside. Therefore, the other talmid went out, the one known to the Kohen Gadol, and spoke to the gatekeeper and brought in Kefa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:69–70; YOCHANAN 21:15; LUKAS 22:56–57"> Therefore, the maid says to Kefa, Surely you are not also of this manʼs talmidim? He said, I am not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MARKOS 14:54; YOCHANAN 18:25; MARKOS 14:67"> And the avadim and the mesharetim had made a hadlakah (bonfire), because it was cold, and they were standing around the hadlakah warming themselves. And also Kefa was with them, standing and warming himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARKOS 14:55–65; LUKAS 20:20; LUKAS 22:63–71"> Then the Kohen Gadol interrogated Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach about his talmidim and his torah (teaching).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:26; YESHAYAH 48:16; MATTITYAHU 4:23"> In reply, he said, In public I have spoken to the Olam Hazeh; I always taught in the Beit Haknesset and in the Beis Hamikdash, where all the Yehudim come together. Besod (in secret) I spoke nothing.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 45:19">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:59–60; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:12–13; LUKAS 22:67"> Why do you put a she'elah (question) to me? Put a she'elah to the ones having heard what I spoke to them. Hinei, these ones have da'as of what things I said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:3; MARKOS 14:65; LUKAS 22:63–64"> But these things having said, one of the mesharetim, one standing nearby, struck Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, saying, Is this the way to answer the Kohen Gadol?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ex 22:27">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20–23; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:1; MATTITYAHU 5:39"> In reply, he spoke, If I spoke wrongly, give edut (testimony) about the wrong; but if tov, why do you strike me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:13; MATTITYAHU 26:57"> Then Anan sent him, still in the akedah, to Caiapha the Kohen Gadol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MARKOS 14:67–72; YOCHANAN 18:17–18; BERESHIS 18:15"> Now with Shim'on Kefa still standing and warming himself, they said, therefore, to him, Surely you are not also of his talmidim, are you? In reply, he said, I am not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:1; YOCHANAN 18:10; MATTITYAHU 26:73"> One of the avadim of the Kohen Gadol, one who was a relative of him, one whose ear Kefa cut off, says, Did I not see you in the Gahn (Garden) with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:38; MARKOS 14:71–72; LUKAS 22:60–62"> Therefore, again Kefa made hakhchashah (denial). And immediately a tarnegol (cock) crowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:33; YOCHANAN 19:9; MATTITYAHU 27:27"> Therefore, they led Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach from Caiapha to the Praetorium. And it was early. They themselves did not enter into the Praetorium so as not to become tema'im (unclean) and so as to be able to eat the Pesach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:23; MARKOS 15:2–5; MATTITYAHU 27:11–14"> Pilate went forth outside to them and says, What sitnah (accusation, indictment) do you bring against this man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:13; LUKAS 20:19–26; MARKOS 10:33"> In reply, they said to him, If this man were not an evil-doer, we would not have handed him over to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:6–7; YOCHANAN 19:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:18–20"> Therefore, Pilate said to them, Take him and judge him according to your Torah. Those of Yehudah said to him, It is not lawful for us to kill anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:14; MATTITYAHU 20:19; MATTITYAHU 26:2"> (This was to fulfill the dvar of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach which he said, signifying by what mavet he was about to die).</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKAS 23:3–4; YOCHANAN 18:37; YOCHANAN 19:3"> Therefore, Pilate again entered into the Praetorium and called Yehoshua, and said to him, Are you HaMelech HaYehudim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach asked, From yourself you say this or another told you about me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:14–16; NECHEMYAH 4:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:1–2"> In reply, Pilate said, Am I a Yehudi? Your nation and your Kohen Gadol handed you over to me. What did you do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LUKAS 17:20–21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:12–14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:17"> In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, My Malchut is not of the Olam Hazeh. If my Malchut were of the Olam Hazeh, my mesharetim would have fought, that I should not be delivered to those of Yehudah; but now my Malchut is not from here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:6; YOCHANAN 8:47; YOCHANAN 8:14"> Therefore, Pilate said to him, So you are a Melech (King)? In reply, he said, You say that I am a Melech. For this I have been born and for this I have come into the Olam Hazeh, that I might bear solemn edut (testimony) to HaEmes. Everyone who is of HaEmes hears my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:4; LUKAS 23:4; YOCHANAN 19:6"> Pilate says to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, What is HaEmes? And having said this, again Pilate went out to those of Yehudah, and says to them, I find no avon (offense), no cause for punishment, in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:15–18; LUKAS 23:16–23; MARKOS 15:6–14"> But there is a minhag (custom) for you, that I may release one to you during the Pesach. Do you want, therefore, that I release to you the Melech HaYehudim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LUKAS 23:25; MARKOS 15:15; MATTITYAHU 27:16"> Therefore, they cried out again, saying, Not this man but Bar-Abba. Now Bar-Abba was an insurrectionist shoded (robber).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Yn 10:1; Mk 15:7">e</a></sup></span>
