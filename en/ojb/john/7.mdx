<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:20; MISHLE 18:1–2; MATTITYAHU 23:5"> For no one who seeks public notice does anything besod (secretly). If these things you do, then manifest yourself to HaOlam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MARKOS 3:21; YOCHANAN 1:11–13; MICHOH 7:5–6"> For not even the achim of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach were believing in him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 69:8">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:8; YOCHANAN 2:4; YOCHANAN 7:30"> Therefore, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach says to them, My sha'ah has not yet come, but your time is always ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:18–19; YOCHANAN 3:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:16"> The Olam Hazeh is not capable of sin'ah (hatred) toward you, but the Olam Hazeh hates me, and for this reason: I give solemn edut (testimony) about it because the ma'asim of the Olam Hazeh are ra'im (evil).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:6; YOCHANAN 8:20; YOCHANAN 7:30"> You make aliyah leregel up to the Chag (Feast). I am not going up to this Chag (Feast), because my time has not yet been fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And having said these things, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach remained in the Galil.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YOCHANAN 11:56"> Then those of Yehudah were seeking Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach in the Chag (Feast) and were saying, Where is that man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:40–43; YOCHANAN 9:16; YOCHANAN 7:32"> And there was much telunnah (murmuring) about Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach among the multitudes. Some were saying, He is tov (good), but others were saying, Lo (no), he deceives the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:22; YOCHANAN 19:38; YOCHANAN 20:19"> No one, however, was speaking openly about Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach because of the fear of those of Yehudah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:28; LUKAS 19:47; YOCHANAN 7:37"> But around the middle period of the Chag (Feast), Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach went up to the Beis Hamikdash and was saying shiurim (teaching torah).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 4:22; MATTITYAHU 7:28–29; MATTITYAHU 13:54"> Therefore, those of Yehudah were marveling, saying, How has this man binah (understanding) of a Yeshiva yode'a sefer (scholar), not having learned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:10; YOCHANAN 14:24; YOCHANAN 17:8"> Therefore, in reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said, My Torah (teaching) is not mine but of the One having sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:31–32; YOCHANAN 8:43; YIRMEYAH 31:33–34"> If anyone wants to do the ratzon Hashem, he will have da'as about my Torah, whether it is of Hashem or I speak only from myself.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ps 25:14; 92:15; Num 16:28">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:41; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:31–33; YOCHANAN 13:31–32"> The one speaking from himself seeks his own kavod (glory), but he who is seeking the kavod of the One having sent him, this one is ne'eman and there is no avlah (injustice) in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:17; YOCHANAN 11:53; MATTITYAHU 12:14"> Has not Moshe given you the Torah? None of you mekyyaim (fulfills) es &#91;kol&#93; toras Moshe. Why are you seeking to kill me?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dt 32:46; Prov 20:9; Koh 7:20; Isa 53:6; Ro 7:19">e</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:23; YOCHANAN 5:2–11"> In reply, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach said to them, Of the Pe'ulot of Hashem, I accomplished echad, one pe'ulah, and everyone marvels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="VAYIKRA 12:3; BERESHIS 17:10–14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:9–11"> Moshe has given you bris milah not that bris milah is of Moshe, but it is of the Avot, and on a Shabbos you perpetually perform bris milah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gn 17:10-14; 21:4; Lv 12:3">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:5; YOCHANAN 5:14–16; MATTITYAHU 12:2"> If a man receives bris milah on Shabbos and lo tufar Torat Moshe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gn 17:14">g</a></sup>(the Torah of Moses may not be broken) are you angry with me because I gave a man refu'ah shleimah on Shabbos?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YESHAYAH 11:3–4; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9; YOCHANAN 8:15"> Do not judge according to appearance but judge with mishpat tzedek.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Zech 7:9; 1Sm 16:7; Isa 11:3-4; Lv 19:15">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:7–8; YOCHANAN 7:48; YOCHANAN 12:42"> And, hinei, he speaks publicly and they say nothing to him. Perhaps the manhigim (the gedolim of Judaism) have da'as that this man is the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MICHOH 5:2; YOCHANAN 7:41–42; YOCHANAN 6:42"> But this man, we have da'as where he is from; but the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, whenever he comes, no one has da'as where he is from.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:42; YOCHANAN 8:19; YOCHANAN 8:26"> Therefore, in the Beis Hamikdash while teaching, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach cried out, saying, You have da'as of me, and you have da'as of where I am from. I have not come on my own, but the One Who sent me is ne'eman. He is the One of Whom you do not have da'as.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:55; MATTITYAHU 11:27; YOCHANAN 17:18"> I have da'as of Him, because from Him Ani Hu, and He is the One Who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:20; YOCHANAN 10:39; YOCHANAN 7:6"> Therefore, they were seeking to arrest him, and no one laid a hand on him, because his sha'ah (hour, time) had not yet come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:42; MATTITYAHU 12:23; YOCHANAN 10:41–42"> From the multitude, however, many put their emunah, their bitachon, in him, and were saying, The Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, whenever he comes, surely he will not do more otot (miraculous signs) than the things which this man did?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:5; YOCHANAN 13:33; MARKOS 16:19"> Therefore, he said, Yet a little time I am with you and I go away to the One having sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MISHLE 1:24–31; YOCHANAN 13:33–36; YOCHANAN 14:6"> You will seek me and not find me and where Ani Hu you are not able to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:1; YESHAYAH 11:12; TZEFANYAH 3:10"> Therefore, those of Yehudah said to themselves, Where is this man about to journey that we will not find him? Surely he is not about to sojourn to the Golus of the Yevanim (Greeks) to teach the Yevanim?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Prov 1:28">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:9; YOCHANAN 7:34; YOCHANAN 12:34"> What is this dvar which he said, You will seek me and will not find me and where Ani Hu, you are not able to come?</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:52; YOCHANAN 1:46; YOCHANAN 1:49"> Others were saying, This man is the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach. But some were saying, Surely the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach comes not from the Galil, does he?</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:32; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:21–27"> Then the avadim of the Rashei Hakohanim and Perushim came and the Rashei Hakohanim and Perushim said to them, Why did you not bring him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:28–29; LUKAS 4:22; YOCHANAN 7:26"> In reply, the avadim said, Never Ish spoke like this Ish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:8; MELACHIM BAIS 18:32"> In reply, therefore, the Perushim said, Surely not you also have been deceived?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:42; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:8"> Has any of the manhigim put their emunah (faith) in him, or any of the Perushim?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="T.N. but cf.
