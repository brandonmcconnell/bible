<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 2:21; YESHAYAH 27:2–3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:9"> I am HaGefen HaAmittit (the true vine)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ps 80:7-19; Isa 5:1-7; Ezek 19:10">a</a></sup>and HaAv of me is the Korem (vineyard proprietor).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 3:10; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:10–11; MATTITYAHU 15:13"> Every sarig (shoot, branch) in me not bearing p'ri<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 92:14">b</a></sup>He takes it away, and every sarig bearing p'ri He prunes that it may bear much p'ri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YOCHANAN 17:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:26; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:22"> You are already metoharim (clean) because of the dvar which I have spoken to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:20; YOCHANAN 8:31"> Remain in me and I in you. As the sarig is not able to bear p'ri from itself, unless it remains on the gefen (vine), so neither are you, unless you remain in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:10; YOCHANAN 15:16"> Ani Hu (I am) HaGefen; you are the sarigim (branches). The one remaining in me and I in him, this one bears much p'ri, because apart from me you are not able to do anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:7–8; MATTITYAHU 13:41"> Unless someone remains in me, he is cast out as the sarig and is dried up and they gather them, and into the Eish they throw them, and they are burned.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ezek 15:1-8; 19:10">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 37:4; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:14; YOCHANAN 14:13"> If you remain in me and the devarim of me remain in you, whatever you want, ask, and it will be done for you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:31; MATTITYAHU 5:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:11"> By this HaAv of me was given kavod, that much p'ri you bear, and so be my talmidim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YOCHANAN 17:23–24; YOCHANAN 17:26; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:28"> As HaAv has ahavah for me, so I have ahavah for you. Remain in my ahavah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Yd 1:21">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:15; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:3; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:21–24"> If you are shomer of my mitzvot, you will remain in my ahavah, as I of the mitzvot of HaAv of me have been shomer, and I remain in His ahavah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YOCHANAN 17:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:13; YOCHANAN 16:24"> These things I have spoken to you that the simcha of me may be in you and the simcha of you may be made shleimah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:21; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:8; YOCHANAN 13:34"> This is my mitzvah for you, that you have ahavah (agape) one for the other, as I have ahavah (agape) for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:6–8"> Ahavah (agape) gedolah than this no one has, that someone lay down his neshamah for his chaverim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gn 44:33">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:50; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:3; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:23"> You are my chaverim, if you are shomer oner my mitzvot.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Job 16:20; Prov 18:24">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 25:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:9–12; MATTITYAHU 13:11"> No longer do I call you avadim, because the eved does not have da'as of what his Adon is doing. But you I have called chaverim, because all things which I heard from HaAv of me, of all these things I gave you da'as.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ex 33:11">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:19; YIRMEYAH 1:5–7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:10"> It was not that you chose me, but I chose you, and have given you s'michah that you go and bear p'ri and the p'ri of you remain, that whatever you ask HaAv b'Shem of me (Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach), HaAv may give it to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:12; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:14–17; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:17"> I am giving you these mitzvot, that you have ahavah (agape) one for the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:13; YOCHANAN 7:7; MARKOS 13:13"> If the Olam Hazeh has sin'as chinom (hatred without cause) for you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isa 66:5">h</a></sup>be aware it has had sin'as chinom for me before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:4–5; YOCHANAN 17:14–16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:4–11"> If you were of the Olam Hazeh, the Olam Hazeh would have had ahavah for its own. But because from the Olam Hazeh you are not, but I chose you out of the Olam Hazeh, therefore the Olam Hazeh has sin'as chinom for you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="1Y 4:5; Yn 17:14">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:12; YOCHANAN 13:16; MATTITYAHU 10:24"> Put in zikaron (remembrance) the dvar which I told you, An eved is not gadol (greater) than his Adon. If they brought redifah (persecution) on me, they will also bring redifah on you. If over my dvar they were shomer, also over yours they will be shomer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:22; YOCHANAN 16:3; MATTITYAHU 10:18"> But all these things they will do to you on account of the Shem of me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Isa 66:5">j</a></sup>because they do not have da'as of the One having sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YOCHANAN 9:41; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:30"> If I did not come and speak to them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ezek 2:5">k</a></sup>they would not have chet; but now they have no ptur (excuse) for their chet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:40–42; (II) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:23"> The one having sin'as chinom (hatred without cause) for me (Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach) has sin'as chinom for HaAv of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YOCHANAN 5:36; YOCHANAN 14:9; YOCHANAN 7:31"> If I did not do pe'ulot among them which no other man did, they would not have chet; but now they have beheld and they have had sin'as chinom for both me and HaAv of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="TEHILLIM 109:3; TEHILLIM 69:4; TEHILLIM 35:19"> But this was that the dvar having been written in their Torah might be fulfilled, sone'ai chinom atzmu(They are many hating me without cause).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Ps 69:4[5]; Ps 35:19; 109:3">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:26; YOCHANAN 14:16–17; YOCHANAN 16:13–15"> But when the Melitz Yosher (Praklit, Advocate, Counselor, Helper in Court) comes, whom I will send to you from HaAv, the Ruach Hakodesh, the Ruach HaEmes, who proceeds from HaAv, that one will gives solemn edut (testimony) about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:14; YOCHANAN 21:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:39–42"> And you &#91;Shlichim&#93; will give solemn edut (testimony) also, because from the beginning you are with me.</span>
