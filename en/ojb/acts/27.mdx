<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:25; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:1"> And when it was decided that we set sail to Italy, they were handing over both Rav Sha'ul and some other prisoners to a centurion, Julius by name, of the Imperial Cohort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:29; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:10"> And having embarked in an oniyah of Adramyttium about to sail to the ports along the coast of Asia, we set sail, Aristarchus a Macedonian of Thessalonika being with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:43"> The next day, we put in at a harbor in Tzidon; and Julius treated Rav Sha'ul with kindness, permitting him to be cared for by his chaverim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:36; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:39; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:4"> And from there, having put out to sea, we sailed under the lee of Cyprus because the winds were against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:41"> Then having sailed across the open sea along the coast of Cilicia and Pamphylia, we came down to Myra of Lycia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:9"> And there the centurion found an Alexandrian oniyah sailing to Italy and put us on board.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:21; LETTER TO TITOS 1:5; LETTER TO TITOS 1:12"> But for many yamim we sailed slowly and with difficulty along the coast of Cnidus, and as the wind was not permitting us to go vaiter (farther), we sailed under the lee of Crete off Salmone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And with difficulty sailing past it, we came to a certain place called Fair Havens, near the city of Lasea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 29:7; VAYIKRA 23:27–29; VAYIKRA 16:29–31"> And since considerable time had been lost and it was already unsafe for a voyage, because Yom Kippur had already come and gone; therefore, Rav Sha'ul gave them this eitza (suggestion),</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DANIEL 2:30; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:20–26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:41–44"> Saying to them, Anashim, I see that the voyage will be hardship and much peril, not only of the cargo and the oniyah, but also of our lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 33:4; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:7; YECHEZKEL 3:17–18"> But the centurion was persuaded by the pilot and the owner rather than by the devarim of Rav Sha'ul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="TEHILLIM 107:30; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:8"> But the port being unfavorably situated for spending the winter, the majority decided to set sail from there, if somehow they could reach Phoenix to spend the winter. It was a harbor of Crete, facing southwest and northwest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKAS 12:55; SHIR HASHIRIM 4:16; IYOV 37:17"> And a gentle south wind began to blow, and they thought they could attain their matarah (objective), so they weighed anchor and they were sailing past Crete, close by the shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MARKOS 4:37; TEHILLIM 107:25–27; SHEMOT 14:21–27"> After not much time a violent, typhoon force wind rushed down from Crete, the so called Euraquilo, the Northeaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:27"> And the oniyah, having been caught in it, and not being able to directly face the wind, we gave way to it and were driven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> By running under the lee of a small island called Clauda, we were able only with difficulty to get the lifeboat secured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:41"> After hoisting it up, they were using frapping cables, passing them underneath the oniyah; then, fearing lest on the shallows of Syrtis they might run aground, they lowered the sea anchor, and so they were being driven along.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YONAH 1:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:38; MATTITYAHU 16:26"> And we were being violently tossed by the storm, so much so that the next day they were throwing the cargo overboard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="IYOV 2:4; LUKAS 9:24–25; MARKOS 8:35–37"> And on the Yom Shlishi, with their own hands, they threw out the tackle of the oniyah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:29; MATTITYAHU 8:24–25; YIRMEYAH 2:25"> And neither shemesh (sun) nor kochavim appeared for many yamim and no small tempest assailed us. Lemaskana (finally) all tikvateinu for yeshu'ah was being abandoned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BERESHIS 42:22; TEHILLIM 107:5–6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:7"> And after having much loss of appetite, then Rav Sha'ul stood up in the midst of them, and said, Anashim, you should have obeyed me and not put out to sea from Crete and thereby spared yourselves this hardship and this loss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:25; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:36; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:8–9"> And now I advise you to have ometz lev (courage), for there will be no loss of life among you, except the oniyah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:9; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:17"> For a malach Hashem stood by me during lailah hazeh, of the G-d whom I serve,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:11; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:16–17; MATTITYAHU 10:28"> Saying, Do not be afraid, Sha'ul. It is necessary for you to stand before Caesar; and, hinei, Hashem has given to you all the ones sailing with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:20–21; LUKAS 1:45; BAMIDBAR 23:19"> Therefore have lev same'ach, Anashim. For I have emunah in Hashem that it will be exactly as he has told me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:29"> But it is necessary for us to run aground on some island.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YONAH 1:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:30; HISGALUS 18:17"> Now when the fourteenth night had come, while we being driven about in the Adriatic Sea, toward chatzot halailah (midnight), the sailors were suspecting that we were getting close to land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And having taken soundings, they found twenty fathoms and, having sailed a little vaiter (farther), again they took soundings, and they found fifteen fathoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:19; DEVARIM 28:67"> And fearing lest somehow against the rough places we might run aground, they threw four anchors off the stern, and they were praying for the dawn to break.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:32"> Now when the sailors sought to flee from the oniyah and to let down the lifeboat into the sea on the pretext of casting out anchors,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:13–14; TEHILLIM 91:11–12; YIRMEYAH 29:11–13"> Rav Sha'ul said to the centurion and to the chaiyalim, Unless these remain in the oniyah, you cannot be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:7–9; LUKAS 16:8"> Then the chaiyalim cut away the ropes of the lifeboat, and let it fall away, setting it adrift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Just before boker, Rav Sha'ul was urging everyone to take okhel (food), saying, Today is the 14th day you have been held in suspense and are continuing without eating, having taken nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:30; MELACHIM ALEF 1:52; LUKAS 21:18"> Therefore, I encourage you to take okhel, for it is for your deliverance, for none of you will lose a hair from your heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:36; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:30–31; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:3–4"> And having said these things, and having taken lechem, Rav Sha'ul said the HaMotzi before all, and, after the Betzi'at HaLechem, he began to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:4–6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:22; TEHILLIM 27:14"> And receiving ometz lev, they all took okhel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:41; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:1; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:20"> Now there were in all two hundred and seventy-six nefashot in the oniyah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YONAH 1:5; MATTITYAHU 6:25; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:18–19"> And having eaten enough okhel (food), they were lightening the oniyah by throwing the wheat overboard into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:1"> And when it became day, they were not recognizing the land, but a certain bay they were noticing, having a shore onto which they were wanting, if possible, to run aground the oniyah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="YESHAYAH 33:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:29–30"> And the anchors they cast off and they left them in the sea. At the same time they loosened the ropes of the rudders and raised the sail to the wind and were steering toward the shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:25–26; YECHEZKEL 27:26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:26–29"> But having fallen into a channel, a place between two seas, they ran the oniyah aground, and, while the bow had stuck and remained immovable, the stern was being destroyed by the force of the waves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MARKOS 15:15–20; KOHELET 9:3; TEHILLIM 74:20"> Now the kesher (plan, plot) of the chaiyalim was that they should kill the prisoners, lest anyone, having swum away, should escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:3; MISHLE 16:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:11"> But the centurion, desiring to save Rav Sha'ul, kept them from carrying out the kesher, and he ordered the ones able to swim to throw themselves overboard first and to make for the shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:22; TEHILLIM 107:28–30; YOCHANAN 6:39–40"> As for the rest, some were on planks, others on pieces from the oniyah. And so everyone was brought safely onto the land.</span>
