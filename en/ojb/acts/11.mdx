<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="TEHILLIM 22:27; MALACHI 1:11; YESHAYAH 32:15"> And the Moshiachʼs Shlichim and the Achim b'Moshiach, the ones throughout Yehudah, heard that also the non-Jews received the dvar Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:45; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:1"> But when Kefa went up to Yerushalayim, those of the faction of the Bris Milah were taking issue with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:12; LUKAS 15:2"> Saying, You entered into a bais of anashim arelim (uncircumcised men) and you sat at tish with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKAS 1:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:27; YEHOSHUA 22:21–31"> But Kefa began explaining to them beseder (in order), saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:9–32; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:17"> I was davening in the shtetl of Yafo and in a trance I saw a chazon (vision) of a certain object descending like a large linen cloth lowered by four corners from Shomayim, and it came up to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKAS 4:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:4"> And when I had gazed upon it, I saw fourfooted animals of ha'aretz and chayyat hasadeh and remasim haAdamah and oph HaShomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And I heard also a bat kol saying to me, Get up, Kefa. Kill and eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:14; HOSHEA 9:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:14"> But I said, By no means, Adoni, because nothing common or tameh (unclean) has ever entered into my stomach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:9; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:13–14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:34–35"> And the bat kol answered for a second time from Shomayim, What Hashem made tahor (clean) do not declare tameh (unclean).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YOCHANAN 21:17; BAMIDBAR 24:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:8"> This happened shlosh pe'amim, and everything was pulled up again into Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="SHEMOT 4:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:10–12; SHEMOT 4:14"> Hinei, at once shloshah anashim stood at the bais in which I was, having been sent from Caesarea to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:22; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:2"> And the Ruach Hakodesh said to me to accompany them without hesitating and these sheshet haAchim b'Moshiach came with me also, and we entered into the bais of the ish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:30–32; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:3–6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:43"> He reported to us how he saw the malach in his bais having stood and having said, Send to Yafo and summon Shim'on, the one being called Kefa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:31–34"> Who will speak words to you by which you and all your bais will find Yeshu'at Eloheinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:34–46; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:2–12"> As I began to speak, the Ruach Hakodesh fell upon them, just as also upon us in the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:5; MARKOS 1:8; MISHLE 1:23"> And I remembered the dvar of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu, how he used to say, Yochanan gave a tevilah of teshuva with a mikveh mayim, but you will be given a tevilah with the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:47; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:20–26; DANIEL 4:35"> If then Hashem gave the same matanah to them as also to us after believing in Moshiach Adoneinu, who then was I to stand in the way of Hashem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:12–13; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:10; YECHEZKEL 36:26"> And having heard these things, they were silenced and glorified Hashem, saying Then also to the Nations Hashem has given teshuva unto Chayyim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:36; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:3"> Then the ones, that is, those having been scattered because of the tzoros that happened in connection with Stefanos, came to Phoenicia and Cyprus and Antioch, darshenen to no one the dvar Hashem except only to Yehudim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:32; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:23–24"> And there were some of them, anashim of Cyprus and Cyrene, who having come to Antioch, were speaking also to the Yevanim, darshenen the Besuras HaGeulah of Adoneinu Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKAS 1:66; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:47; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:14"> The hand of Adonoi was on them and a large number, having had emunah, turned to Moshiach Adoneinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:1–3"> The report about them was heard in the ears of the Kehillah of Moshiach in Yerushalayim, and they sent out Bar-Nabba to go to Antioch;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:58; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:43"> Who, having come, and having seen the Chen v'Chesed Hashem, rejoiced and was encouraging everyone with deveykus (devotion) to HaAdon to remain true to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:31"> For Bar-Nabba was a mentsh, a gavra, an ish tov full of the Ruach Hakodesh and of emunah. And a great multitude was added to Adoneinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:30"> And Bar-Nabba left for Tarsus to look for Sha'ul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:28; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:14"> And, having found him, he brought him to Antioch. And it came about that for an entire year, they met with Moshiachʼs Kehillah there, and taught a large multitude. And it was in Antioch that the talmidim were first called Ma'aminim HaMeshichiyim (Messianic Believers).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:11"> Now in these yamim, nevi'im came down from Yerushalayim to Antioch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:2; LUKAS 3:1"> One of them, by name of Agav, having got up, indicated through the Ruach Hakodesh a great famine was about to come all over the world. And this famine actually occurred during the time of Claudius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:12–14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:2–4"> Now the talmidim decided that according to their means, each of them would send support to the Achim b'Moshiach dwelling in Yehudah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:25; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:4"> Which also they did, having sent to the Ziknei HaKehillot by the hand of Bar-Nabba and Sha'ul.</span>
</div>
