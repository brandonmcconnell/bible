<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="VAYIKRA 10:1–3; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:20; MATTITYAHU 13:47–48"> And a certain man by the name Chananyah, with his wife Shappira, sold property,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:37; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:14–15; MATTITYAHU 23:5"> And he, with his wife in collusion as to da'as, misappropriated from the price, and having brought a certain part, laid it at the feet of the Moshiachʼs Shlichim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Josh 7:11">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 23:24; YESHAYAH 29:15; YOCHANAN 13:2"> But Kefa said, Chananyah, why has HaSatan filled your lev (heart) that you lied to the Ruach Hakodesh and misappropriated from the price of the land?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dt 23:21">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:3; YEHOSHUA 7:25–26; MELACHIM BAIS 5:25–27"> While it remained with you, did it not remain yours? And after it was sold, were the proceeds not under your samchut (authority)? How is it that you have hatched this deed in your lev (heart)? Your sheker (lie) was not to Bnei Adam but to Elohim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Dt 23:22; Lv 6:2">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:10–11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:43; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:13"> And hearing these devarim, having fallen down, Chananyah died; and there came great yirat Shomayim upon all the ones listening.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ps 5:6">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:40; SHMUEL BAIS 18:17; DEVARIM 21:23"> And having got up, the bochrim threw the tachrichin shroud over him and, having carried him out, they buried him in a kever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And there was an interval of lav davka (approximately) shloshah sha'ot and then his isha, not having da'as of what had happened, entered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:2"> And in reply to her, Kefa said, Tell me, you sold the sadeh for such and such a price, did you? And she said, Ken, for such and such a price, that was the amount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:3–4; TEHILLIM 95:8–11"> And Kefa said to her, Why was it agreed by the two of you to put the Ruach Hakodesh of Hashem to the test? Hinei! The feet of the ones having buried your ba'al are at the petach (doorway), and they will carry you out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:5"> And she fell immediately at his feet and died. And, the bochrim, having entered, found her dead; and, having carried her out, they buried her with her ba'al.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:11–12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:17"> And there came great yirat Shomayim upon the whole Messianic kehillah and upon all the ones hearing these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:4"> Now by the hands of the Moshiachʼs Shlichim were being effected many otot and moftim among the people and with a sense of achdus they were all in the Ulam Sh'lomo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:47; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:21; YOCHANAN 12:42"> But none of the rest was daring to be mishtatef in a chavura (becoming involved, joining a fellowship) with them; however, the Am Yisroel held the Messianic Jews in high esteem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:47; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:4; YESHAYAH 55:11–13"> And more and more ma'aminim hameshichiyim in Adoneinu were being added, multitudes of anashim and nashim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 14:36; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:11–12; YOCHANAN 14:12"> So much so that even out into the rekhovot they would carry the cholim and put them on pallets and mats, that when Kefa came by, at least his shadow might fall upon some of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MARKOS 16:17–18; MARKOS 2:3–4; MATTITYAHU 15:30–31"> And also the multitudes from the shtetlach surrounding Yerushalayim were coming together, carrying the cholim and the ones being tormented by ruchot teme'ot (unclean spirits); and they were all receiving refu'ah shleimah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:45; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:5"> But there was an uprising of the Kohen Gadol and all the ones with him of the Tzedukim kat, and they were filled with kinah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:3; LUKAS 21:12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:23"> And they laid their hands upon the Moshiachʼs Shlichim and they put them in a public beis hasohar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:26; TEHILLIM 34:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:7–11"> And a malach Adonoi (an angel of Hashem) opened the doors of the beis hasohar balailah, and having led them out, said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gn 16:7; Ex 3:2; Ps 34:7">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YOCHANAN 6:63; YOCHANAN 6:68; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:11–12"> Go and stand and speak in the Beis Hamikdash to the people kol divrei HaChayyim haelleh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:18–19"> And having heard, the Moshiachʼs Shlichim entered the Beis Hamikdash at Shachrit and were saying shiurim. And when the Kohen Gadol and the ones with him had come, they called together the Sanhedrin and all the assembly of Zekenim of the Bnei Yisroel, and they sent to the beis hasohar for the prisoners to be brought to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But their mesharetim, having come, did not find them in the beis hasohar. And having returned, they reported these things</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="TEHILLIM 2:4; TEHILLIM 33:10; DANIEL 6:22–24"> Saying, We found the beis hasohar locked tight and the shomrim standing at the doors; but when we opened up, we found no one inside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:26; YOCHANAN 12:19"> And when the Sar Tzeva HaHeikhal of the Beis Hamikdash and the Rashei Hakohanim heard these words, they were perplexed about them, as far as what would come of all this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> But someone came and reported to them, Hinei, the men whom you put in the beis hasohar are in the Beis Hamikdash standing and saying shiurim to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:21; MATTITYAHU 26:5"> Then the Sar Tzeva HaHeikhal went along with the mesharetim and brought the Moshiachʼs Shlichim back, but not with force — for they feared the people, lest they should be stoned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 5:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:7; LUKAS 22:66"> And when they had brought them, they stood the Moshiachʼs Shlichim before the Sanhedrin, and the Kohen Gadol questioned them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:25; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:52; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:10–11"> Saying, Did we not with a strict command charge you not to say shiurim b'shem hazeh and, hinei, you have filled Yerushalayim with your torah and are determined to bring upon us the dahm of this man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:19; SHMUEL ALEF 15:24; MARKOS 7:7–9"> And in reply Kefa and the Moshiachʼs Shlichim said, It is necessary to obey Hashem rather than Bnei Adam.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ex 1:17">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:13; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:39"> Elohei Avoteinu made Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua to stand up alive again, the very one whom you killed, having made him talui al haEtz (being hanged on the tree).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Dt 21:23">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LUKAS 24:47; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:33; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:10"> This one Hashem exalted as Sar and Moshi'a (Oisleizer) to His right hand to grant teshuva to Yisroel and selicha (forgiveness) of chatta'im (averos, sins).</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:26–27; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:3–4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:8"> And we are the eidus (witness) of these matters and so is the Ruach Hakodesh whom Hashem gave to the ones obeying Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:54; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:37; LUKAS 4:28–29"> And the ones having heard were cut to the quick and in fury were deciding to do away with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:3; LUKAS 2:46; LUKAS 5:17"> But a certain Parush got up in the Sanhedrin, a moreh of Torah by name Rabban Gamli'el, respected by kol Am Yisroel, and he gave orders to put the men outside for a little while.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:26; YIRMEYAH 26:19; MATTITYAHU 27:19"> And Rabban Gamli'el said to them, Anshei Yisroel, pay attention to yourselves what you are about to do with these anashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:9; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:3"> For at an earlier tekufa (era, period), there was the uprising of Theudas, who claimed to be somebody himself, with whom were associated a number of anashim lav davka (approximately) four hundred; who was done away with and as many as were obeying him were all dispersed and it came to nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKAS 2:1–2; TEHILLIM 7:14–15; MATTITYAHU 26:52"> After this there was Yehudah HaGalili in the yamim of the census; who misled the people to follow after him and that man perished and, as many as were obeying him, were all scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:13; EKHAH 3:37; YESHAYAH 8:9–10"> And now I say to you, stay away from these anashim, and leave them alone, because if this cheshbon (plan) or this matter is of Bnei Adam, it will be overthrown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MISHLE 21:30; YESHAYAH 46:10; MATTITYAHU 16:18"> But if it is from Hashem, you are not able to overthrow them — in that case you may even be found to be fighting keneged (against, opposing)Hashem. And they were persuaded by Rabban Gamli'el.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="2C 13:12; Prov 21:30; Isa 46:10">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:17–21"> And having called together the Moshiachʼs Shluchim and having flogged them, they warned them not to speak in the shem of Yehoshua, and they released them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:2; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:13–16; LUKAS 6:22"> Therefore Moshiachʼs Shluchim were going rejoicing from the presence of the Sanhedrin, that they were considered worthy to suffer shame for ha-Shem (the Name).</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:46; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:20; LUKAS 21:37"> And every day in the Beis Hamikdash and from bais to bais they did not stop saying shiurim and drashot about Yehoshua as Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
