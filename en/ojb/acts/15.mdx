<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:22–23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:6–7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:1–2"> Now this brought them into a dispute and a debate of no little proportion with Rav Sha'ul and Bar-Nabba, who were appointed to go up with some others from them to bring this she'elah (question) before the Moshiachʼs Shlichim and Zekenim in Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:11"> Then having been sent on their way by the Kehillah, they were passing through both Phoenicia and Shomron telling in detail how the Nations were turning to Hashem and they were bringing great simcha to all the Achim b'Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:3"> And having come to Yerushalayim, they were there received by Moshiachʼs Kehillah and the Moshiachʼs Shlichim and the Zekenim, and they reported what things Hashem had done with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:1–3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:5–8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:1"> But some of the ma'aminim hameshichiyim from the kat of the Perushim rose up, saying, It is necessary to give the Goyim the bris milah and to command them to be shomer mitzvot, keeping the Torah of Moshe Rabbenu.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MISHLE 15:22; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:17"> And the Moshiachʼs Shlichim and the Zekenim were gathered together as a council to consider this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:17–18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:7–9; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:4–5"> And after much deliberation had taken place, Moshiachʼs Shliach Shim'on Kefa got up. Kefa said to them, Anashim, Achim b'Moshiach, you have da'as that in the early days Hashem chose that among you I would be the one and through my mouth the Goyim would hear the dvar Hashem of the Besuras HaGeulah and would come to emunah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="in the Orthodox Jewish faith">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:47; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:3"> And the One who has da'as of levavot, Hashem, bore solemn edut and stood as their witness, having given the non-Jews the Ruach Hakodesh just as Hashem did also to us Jews.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ac 2:4; 10:46; 11:15-16">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:28"> And in making tehorot their levavot by emunah, in this respect Hashem did not differentiate between us Jews and those non-Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:1; MATTITYAHU 23:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:9"> Therefore, now why are you testing Hashem by laying an ol (yoke) upon the neck of the talmidim which neither Avoteinu nor we were able to bear?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Dt 9:5-6; Jer 31:31-34">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:24; LETTER TO TITOS 3:4–7; LETTER TO TITOS 2:11"> On the contrary, Anu Ma'aminim (We believe) that we Jews come to Yeshu'at Eloheinu by the Chen v'Chesed Hashem of the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu in the same Derech Hashem as those non-Jews do also.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ro 3:9,30; Ga 3:12-13; Isa 53:9-12; Pp 3:9; Dt 9:4-6; Lk 18:11-12">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:19"> And then all the multitude was silent and they were listening to Bar-Nabba and Rav Sha'ul describing what otot and moftim Hashem did among the Nations through them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:12; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:1"> And after Rav Sha'ul and Bar-Nabba fell silent, Moshiachʼs Shliach Ya'akov<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Mt 13:55">e</a></sup>responded, saying, Anashim, Achim b'Moshiach, listen to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:7–9; LUKAS 2:31–32"> Shim'on explained how Hashem first concerned Himself to receive from the Nations a people for SHMO.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Zech 6:12">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:8–12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:47"> And this is in agreement with the devarim of the Nevi'im, just as it has been written,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="AMOS 9:11–12; TEHILLIM 89:35–49; LUKAS 1:31–33"> After these things I will return and AKIM ES SUKKAT DOVID HANOFELET VHARISOTAV AKIM U'VENITIHA (I will restore the tent of Dovid which has fallen and the things having been torn down of it I will rebuild and I will restore it),</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 2:11; ZECHARYAH 8:20–23; YESHAYAH 65:1"> So that the she'erit (remnant) of anashim might seek Hashem and all the GOYIM who are called by my Name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:9; YESHAYAH 46:9–10"> Says the L-rd, who makes these things known from long ago.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Am 9:11fTargum HaShivim, 12; Jer 12:15; Isa 45:21">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:7–10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:24"> Therefore, it is my judgment not to trouble the ones from the Goyim turning in teshuva to Hashem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:29; HISGALUS 2:20; VAYIKRA 3:17"> But to write to them to keep away from the pollution of elilim and of zenut and of the thing strangled and of dahm.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Lv 3:17; 7:26; 17:10-13; 19:26; Dt 12:16,23">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:27; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:14–15"> For Moshe Rabbenu from ancient dorot in every shtetl has his maggidim and in the shuls every Shabbos he has been read.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:40; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:1"> Then it was mekabel (received) by the Moshiachʼs Shlichim and the Zekenim with the entire Kehillah to choose anashim from them to send to Syrian Antioch with Rav Sha'ul and Bar-Nabba, also Yehudah called Bar-Sabba, and Sila, all manhigim among the Achim b'Moshiach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:26; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:21"> With the following iggeret: From the Achim b'Moshiach, Moshiachʼs Shlichim and the Zekenim: to the Achim b'Moshiach throughout Antioch and Syria and Cilicia, to those from among the Nations, Shalom!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:1"> Vi-bahlt (since) we heard that certain men had gone out from us, though not having any directives from us, and have given you mitzvot with devarim unsettling your nefashot (souls),</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:2"> It was mekabel to us, having come to achdus in mind and having chosen anashim to send to you, along with our beloved chaverim Bar-Nabba and Sha'ul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:23–25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:29–30"> Anashim who have imperiled their lives for haShem of Moshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:32; (III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:13"> Therefore we have sent Yehudah and Sila, and they too will confirm us in person by word of mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:13; HISGALUS 2:24; MATTITYAHU 11:30"> For it was mekabel to the Ruach Hakodesh and to us to lay no heavier burden upon you who are from the Nations except these necessary things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:25; HISGALUS 2:14"> That you non-Jewish ma'aminim hameshichiyim keep away from meat sacrificed to elilim, and dahm, and what is strangled, and zenut. If you are shomer to avoid these, you will do well. Shalom!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:33"> Therefore, having been dismissed, they went down to Syrian Antioch, and having gathered together the multitude, they delivered the iggeret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:4–5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:1"> And having read it, there was a response of simcha gedolah at the exhortation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:1"> Both Yehudah and Sila, being themselves nevi'im hameshichiyim also, with many devarim comforted the Achim b'Moshiach and gave them chizzuk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:36; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:31"> And after they had spent considerable time, they were dismissed with shalom from the Achim b'Moshiach and sent back to the ones having dispatched them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="1Sm 1:17">i</a></sup>&#91;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Notwithstanding it seemed tov to Sila to remain there.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:1"> And Rav Sha'ul and Bar-Nabba were staying in Syrian Antioch saying shiurim and drashot with also many others, the content of these being the dvar Hashem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:51"> And after some yamim Rav Sha'ul said to Bar-Nabba, Letʼs return, then, and visit the Achim b'Moshiach throughout every city in which we proclaimed the dvar Hashem to see how they are doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:12; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:5"> And Bar-Nabba was deciding to take along also Yochanan called Markos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:13; MISHLE 25:19; LUKAS 14:27–34"> But Rav Sha'ul was insisting not to take along this one, the one having withdrawn from them from Pamphylia, not having gone with them to the avodas kodesh ministry of the Moshiachʼs Shlichus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:10; KOHELET 7:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:36"> And there was a sharp disagreement so that they were separated from one another, and Bar-Nabba took Markos and sailed away to Cyprus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:23"> And Rav Sha'ul chose Sila and departed, having been commended to the Chen v'Chesed Hashem by the Achim b'Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:32"> And they were traveling through Syria and Cilicia strengthening the Moshiachʼs Kehillot.</span>
