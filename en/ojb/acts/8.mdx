<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:20; DANIEL 3:16–18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:40"> And there came about in that day a great pogrom keneged (against) the Brit Chadasha Kehillah in Yerushalayim, and everyone was scattered throughout the regions of Yehudah and Shomron except the Moshiachʼs Shlichim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="BERESHIS 50:10–11; BERESHIS 23:2; YIRMEYAH 22:18"> And chasidim came and gave kevurah (burial) to Stefanos and they made loud lamentation over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:9"> But Rav Sha'ul was making havoc of the Moshiachʼs Kehillah, barging in bais by bais, dragging off both anashim and nashim, and delivering them over to the beis hasohar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:1"> The ones therefore having been scattered went about darshenen the dvar Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:35–36"> Now Philippos,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ac 6:5">a</a></sup>having gone down to the city of Shomron, was preaching to them the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:41–42; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:12; MATTITYAHU 20:15–16"> And the multitudes with one accord were paying attention to the things being said by Philippos, as they heard him and saw the otot which he was accomplishing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:8–10; MARKOS 16:17–18"> For many had ruchot teme'ot coming out, crying out with a kol gadol, and many having been paralyzed and many pisechim (lame ones) were given refu'ah shleimah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:52; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:48; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:39"> And there was great simchah in that city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:36; YOCHANAN 7:18"> Now a certain ish by name Shim'on was previously in the city practicing magic and astonishing the people of Shomron, saying that he himself was an ish of gadlus (with remarkable superiority) and gadol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:34; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:2; HISGALUS 13:3"> And to this certain Shim'on everyone from katon to gadol was paying attention, saying, Hinei! This man is the ko'ach (power) of Hashem, the ko'ach hagadol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:1; YESHAYAH 44:25"> And they were giving heed to him, because he for a long time had astonished them by magic tricks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:38; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:3; MATTITYAHU 28:19"> But with emunah in the darshenen of Philippos about the Malchut Hashem and the Shem of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, they, both anashim and nashim, were submitting to Moshiachʼs mikveh tevilah of teshuva.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:6–7; YOCHANAN 5:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:11"> And Shim'on himself also had emunah, and, having submitted to Moshiachʼs tevilah of teshuva, was following Philippos, and, seeing otot and nifla'ot and moftim hagedolim taking place, he was constantly nispoyel (standing in awe).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:9"> And when Moshiachʼs Shlichim heard in Yerushalayim that Shomron has accepted the dvar Hashem, they sent Kefa and Yochanan to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:38; YOCHANAN 16:23–24; YOCHANAN 14:13–14"> Who, having come down, davened for them that they might receive the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:38; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:2; MATTITYAHU 28:19"> For the Ruach Hakodesh had not yet fallen upon any one of them, but only they had received a tevilah of teshuva in a mikveh mayim b'Shem Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua Adoneinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:6; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:6"> Then Kefa and Yochanan were laying their hands upon them, and they were receiving the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:5; MATTITYAHU 10:8; MELACHIM BAIS 8:9"> And when Shim'on saw that through the laying on of hands of Moshiachʼs Shlichim that the Ruach Hakodesh is given, he brought to them kesef,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKAS 14:7–11; (III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9; YOCHANAN 5:44"> Saying, Give me also this samchut that on whomever I may lay my hands such may receive the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DANIEL 5:17; MATTITYAHU 10:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:38"> But Kefa said to him, May your kesef perish with you because the matnat Hashem you thought by kesef to acquire.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2Kgs 5:16; Dt 5:17">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:13; TEHILLIM 36:1; MATTITYAHU 6:22–24"> There is not to you a chelek (allotted portion) nor share in this matter, for your lev (heart) is krum (deviant) and not upright before Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Neh 2:20; Ps 78:37">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:25–26; DANIEL 4:27; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:12"> Therefore make teshuva from your wickedness and daven (pray) to Hashem if efsher (perhaps) you will receive selicha (forgiveness) for the intent of your lev,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YESHAYAH 58:6; MISHLE 5:22; YOCHANAN 8:34"> For I see you are in bitter gall and the bond of haresha.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Dt 29:18TARGUM HASHIVIM; Jer 4:18; Isa 58:6">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHEMOT 8:8; BAMIDBAR 21:7; IYOV 42:8"> And in reply Shim'on said, Daven for me to Hashem that nothing may come upon me of which you have spoken.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ex 8:8; Num 21:7; 1Kgs 13:6; Jer 42:2">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:22–23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:8"> The ones then having testified and spoken the dvar Adonoi were returning to Yerushalayim and to many villages of the Shomronim preaching the Besuras HaGeulah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:19; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 1:14; MELACHIM BAIS 1:3"> And a malach Adonoi spoke to Philippos, saying, Get up and go south on the derech going down from Yerushalayim to Gaza. This is desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="TZEFANYAH 3:10; TEHILLIM 68:31; YESHAYAH 56:3–8"> And having got up, Philippos went. And, hinei, an Ethiopian man, a eunuch, a court official of the Kandake of the Ethiopians! He was over all of her Otzrot (treasury) and had come worshipping to Yerushalayim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ps 68:31; 87:4; Zeph 3:10; Isa 56:3-5; 1Kgs 8:41-43">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:16; YESHAYAH 1:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:25"> And he was returning and sitting in his chariot and he was reading Yeshayah HaNavi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:22–23"> And the Ruach Hakodesh said to Philippos, Approach and join this chariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LUKAS 24:44–45; YOCHANAN 5:39; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:17"> And having run, Philippos heard him reading Yeshayah HaNavi, and Philippos said, So do you have binah of what you are reading?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:14; TEHILLIM 73:16–17; YESHAYAH 29:18–19"> And he said, How then might I be able, unless someone will guide me? And he invited Philippos to come up and sit with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:7–8; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:21–24; YIRMEYAH 51:40"> Now the keta (section) of the Tanakh which he was reading was this: KASEH LATEVACH YUVAL (He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Moshiach">g</a></sup>was led as a lamb to the slaughter) and as a Seh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isa 53:7">h</a></sup>(Lamb) before the one having sheared it is silent, LO YIFTACH PIV (He does not open his mouth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:12; YESHAYAH 53:8; ZECHARYAH 13:7"> In humiliation his justice was taken away. The descendants of him who will tell? Because he is taken away from HA'ARETZ CHAYYIM.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Isa 53:7,8TARGUM HASHIVIM">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 15:15; MATTITYAHU 13:36; MATTITYAHU 2:2–4"> And in reply the eunuch said to Philippos, I ask you about whom the Navi (prophet) says this? About himself or about some other person?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LUKAS 24:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:23"> And having opened his mouth, and beginning from this keta (section) of the Kitvei Hakodesh, Philippos began to preach and fier ois (elucidate) Yehoshua/Yeshua to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:47; YOCHANAN 3:5; LETTER TO TITOS 3:5–6"> And as they were going along the derech, they came upon some mayim and the eunuch says, Hinei, mayim! What prevents me from being given Moshiachʼs tevilah of teshuva in the mikveh mayim? &#91;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And Philippos said, If you have emunah b'chol levavcha, it is mutar. And he answered, saying, Ani ma'amin ki Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua Ben HaElohim hu.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:1; YOCHANAN 3:22–23"> And he commanded the chariot to stop and both went down into the mikveh mayim, both Philippos and the eunuch, and Philippos gave him Moshiachʼs tevilah of teshuva in the mikveh mayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:12; MELACHIM BAIS 2:16; YECHEZKEL 8:3"> And when they came up from the mikveh mayim, the Ruach Hakodesh of Hashem took Philippos away, and the eunuch did not see him any longer, but was going on his way with lev same'ach.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="1Kgs 18:12; 2Kgs 2:16; Ezek 3:12,14; 8:3; 11:1,24; 43:5">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:33"> But Philippos was found in Ashdod, and passing through, he was preaching the Besuras HaGeulah to all the towns until he came to Caesarea.</span>
