<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:15; MISHLE 1:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:2"> And Agrippa said to Rav Sha'ul, It is permitted for you to speak concerning yourself. Then Rav Sha'ul, having stretched out his hand, was making his hitstaddekut (defense), saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> I have considered myself fortunate that it is before you, Agrippa HaMelech, that I am able to make my hitstaddekut today concerning everything of which I am accused by Yehudim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:7"> Most of all, you are a bukki (expert, meivin) in all the minhagim and issues of the Yehudim; therefore, I beg you to listen patiently to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:13; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:5"> All the Yehudim have da'as of my Yiddishkeit from my earliest youth, a life spent from the beginning among my own people and in Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:5"> They have had da'as of this for a long time, if they are willing to bear edut, that according to the most machmir kat of our Orthodox Jewish faith my life has been lived out as a Parush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 2:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:20"> And now I stand here on trial on account of my tikvah in the havtacha made to Avoteinu,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:11; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:5"> A havtacha Sheneym Asar Shevateinu have tikvah to attain, as they with earnestness worship Hashem yomam valailah. It if for this tikvah, your Excellency, that I am accused by Yehudim!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:12–20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:31–32"> Why is it considered incredible by any of you that Hashem makes the Mesim to stand up alive?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:6"> Indeed, I myself thought it necessary to do many opposing things against ha-Shem of Yehoshua from Natzeret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:13–14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:21"> And these things I also did in Yerushalayim; and armed with samchut received from the Rashei Hakohanim, many of the kedoshim I locked up in the beis hasohar; and I cast my vote keneged (against) them when they received the death penalty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:19; MATTITYAHU 10:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:45"> And throughout all the shuls I often punished them, forcing them to commit Chillul Hashem gidduf (blasphemy), and since I was so enraged against them, I was bringing redifah on them even as far as foreign cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:5–11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:1–8"> And while doing these very things, as I was traveling to Damascus with authorization and permission from the Rashei Hakohanim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 17:2; HISGALUS 21:23; HISGALUS 1:16"> At noon on the derech, O Melech, I saw from Shomayim an ohr, more brilliant than the shemesh, shining around me and around the ones traveling with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:40; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:7"> And we all fell down to the ground. Then I heard a kol saying to me in the language of the Hebrews, Sha'ul, Sha'ul, why are you bringing redifah upon me? It is hard for you to kick keneged (against) the prod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:20–21; MATTITYAHU 25:45; SHEMOT 16:8"> And I said, Who are you Adoni? And HaAdon said, I am Yehoshua upon whom you are bringing redifah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:14–15; YECHEZKEL 2:1; DANIEL 10:11"> But get up and stand on your feet. For this tachlis, then, I appeared to you, to give you s'michah as a mesharet, an eved, and an eidus (witness), both of what things you have seen and the things which I will reveal to you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ezek 2:1; Dan 10:11">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:15; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:7; YIRMEYAH 1:19"> I will deliver you from your own people and from the Goyim to whom I am sending you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Jer 1:8,19">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:8"> To open their eyes, to turn them from choshech to ohr and from the samchut of HaSatan to Hashem, that they receive selichat avon and nachalah among the ones having been set apart in kedushah by emunah in me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 35:5; Ps 18:28; Isa 42:7,16">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 50:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:16; YECHEZKEL 2:7–8"> O Agrippa HaMelech, I was not disobedient to the chazon from Shomayim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isa 50:5">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 3:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:38"> But beginning with the ones in Damascus and also with those in Yerushalayim, and with all the region of Yehudah and with the Goyim, I was preaching they should make teshuva and turn to Hashem, doing ma'asim worthy of teshuva.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Jer 18:11; 35:15">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:30–31; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:3"> Because of these things, the unbelieving Yehudim seized me while I was in the Beis Hamikdash, and were trying to kill me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:14; YOCHANAN 5:46; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:21"> To this day I have obtained ezer (help) from Hashem, to stand and give solemn edut (testimony) before both ketanim and Gedolim, testifying about nothing beyond the Orthodox Jewish faith based on what the Nevi'im and Moshe Rabbenu said would happen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:1–12; LUKAS 24:26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:18"> That is, the yissurim (sufferings) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, and that by being rishon to stand up alive in the Techiyas HaMoshiach, he would proclaim ohr both to K'lal Yisroel and the Nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:13–14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:23; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:10"> And while he was saying these things in his hitstaddekut, Festus in a kol gadol shouts, Rav Sha'ul, all your yeshiva learning has made you meshuggah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:26; LETTER TO TITOS 1:9; LETTER TO TITOS 2:7–8"> But Rav Sha'ul says, I am not meshuggah, most excellent Festus; I am speaking devarim of Emes and Ta'am (Reason).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:2–3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:1–12; YESHAYAH 30:20"> For HaMelech has da'as of these matters, to whom also I am speaking freely, for I am sure that absolutely none of these things escape his notice, for this has not been done in a corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:22–23"> Agrippa HaMelech, do you believe in the Nevi'im? I have da'as that you do believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:26; MATTITYAHU 10:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:29"> And Agrippa said to Rav Sha'ul, You think, do you, that as quick as that you can make me mekabel Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:1"> But Rav Sha'ul said, I would daven that Hashem, quickly or not, might make not only you but all listening to me today as I am, apart from these sharsherot (chains).</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:23"> HaMelech and the Moshel and Bernice and the ones sitting with them got up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:18"> And having withdrawn, they were saying to one another, This man does nothing worthy of the death penalty or imprisonment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:11–12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:25"> And Agrippa said to Festus, This man could have been released, if he had not appealed to Caesar.</span>
