<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:23–28; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:12"> And it came about while Rav Apollos was in Corinth that Rav Sha'ul traveled through the upper regions and arrived at Ephesus, finding some of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Moshiachʼs">a</a></sup>talmidim there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:39; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:15–17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:5"> And Rav Sha'ul said to them, Did you receive the Ruach Hakodesh when you came to emunah? But they said to Rav Sha'ul, But we never heard that there is a Ruach Hakodesh!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:25; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:16"> And Rav Sha'ul said, Into what then were you given tevilah? And they said, Into the tevilah of Yochanan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:29–34; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:5"> And Rav Sha'ul said, Yochanan gave a tevilah of teshuva to the people, saying that they should have emunah in Hu HaBah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Moshiach">b</a></sup>the one coming after him, that is, in Yehoshua.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Lk 3:16">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:3–4"> And having heard this, they were given Moshiachʼs tevilah of teshuva in haShem of the Moshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:4; MARKOS 16:17"> And when Rav Sha'ul placed his hands upon them, the Ruach Hakodesh came upon them, and they were speaking in leshonot and they were speaking devarim hanevu'ah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And the anashim were about sheneym asar in number.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:20–22"> And having joined the minyan at the shul, Rav Sha'ul was speaking with ometz lev during the course of shloshah chodashim, debating and persuading concerning the things of the Malchut Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:6–8"> But when some unbelievers in the shul were being hardened and were disobeying, speaking Chillul Hashem of the Derech Hashem before the multitude, Rav Sha'ul withdrew from them. Rav Sha'ul took the Moshiachʼs talmidim and yom yom was saying shiurim in the &#91;Messianic&#93; yeshiva of Tyrannus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:31; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:6"> And this happened over a period of two years with the intended result that all the ones inhabiting<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="the Roman Province of">d</a></sup>Asia heard the dvar Hashem, both Yehudim and Yevanim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MARKOS 16:17–20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:18–19"> And gevaltike moftim and niflaot Hashem effected at the hands of Rav Sha'ul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:15; MELACHIM BAIS 13:20–21; MARKOS 16:17"> so that when a handkerchief or an apron touched the skin of Rav Sha'ul and was brought to the cholim, the machlot (sicknesses) and the shedim (demons) left them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:27; MARKOS 9:38; LUKAS 11:19"> And some traveling Jewish exorcists attempted to use haShem of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua over the ones possessed of ruchot ra'ot, saying, I exorcize and cast you out by the Yehoshua whom Rav Sha'ul preaches!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And there were doing this very thing shivat banim of a certain Skeva, a Jewish “Kohen Gadol.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:17–18; LUKAS 4:33–35; MARKOS 1:34"> And in reply the ruach hara'ah (the shed, demon) said to them, I am acquainted with Yehoshua, I have da'as of Sha'ul, but, you, who are you?!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKAS 8:29; MARKOS 5:3–4; MARKOS 5:15"> And the ish in whom was the ruach hara'ah leaped upon them and subdued all &#91;shivah&#93; of them, and overpowered them, so that they fled, naked and wounded, from the bais.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:9–11"> And this became known to all the Yehudim and also the Yevanim inhabiting Ephesus, and pachad (terror) fell upon all of them, and they were exalting haShem of Adoneinu Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:10; TEHILLIM 32:5; MATTITYAHU 3:6"> And many of the ones having come to emunah were making vidduy of their ma'asim, disclosing them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:8; SHEMOT 32:20; BERESHIS 35:4"> And a number of the ones who had practiced kishshuf (magic, sorcery) brought together their occult sfarim and were burning them publicly; and they added up the price of these occult sfarim, and they found it to be fifty thousand silver pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:7; YESHAYAH 55:11"> So the dvar Hashem, the Besuras HaGeulah, was growing with ko'ach and was prevailing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:11"> And when these things were fulfilled, Rav Sha'ul resolved in his ruach to travel through Macedonia and Achaia and go to Yerushalayim, saying After I have gone there, it is necessary for me also to see Rome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:23; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:3"> So Rav Sha'ul sent to Macedonia two of the mesharetim (ministers, servants) with him — Timotiyos and Erastus, while he stayed a little while longer in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="the Province of">e</a></sup>Asia.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:22; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:8–10"> Now during that time there was no small disturbance concerning the Derech Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:9–10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:19"> For a certain one, Demetrius by name, a silversmith and maker of silver idol shrines for Artemis, was providing no small revach (profit) for the craftsmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:19; HOSHEA 12:7–8; HISGALUS 18:11–19"> Demetrius also assembled the craftsmen occupied with that trade and he said, Anashim, you have da'as that our prosperity is from this trade,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:29; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:4; YESHAYAH 44:10–20"> And you see and hear that not only here in Ephesus but in almost all of Asia this Rav Sha'ul has persuaded and drawn away a large multitude saying, Elilim made with human hands are not Elohim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Dt 4:28; Ps 115:4; Isa 44:10-20; Jer 10:3-5">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="TZEFANYAH 2:11; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:5; MATTITYAHU 23:13"> And this is not only a danger to us, that our part, our trade, will fall into disrepute, but also the temple of the elah hagedolah Artemis may be considered as nothing, and also may be about to suffer the loss of her majesty, which all Asia and the Olam Hazeh worship.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="HISGALUS 13:4; HISGALUS 17:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:19–24"> And having listened and having become full of ka'as, the idol makers were crying out, saying, Gedolah is Artemis of the Ephesians!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:2"> And the city was filled with tohu, and they rushed with one impulse into the theater, having seized Gaius and Aristarchus, who were Macedonians and traveling fellow po'alim of Rav Sha'ul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:39; SHMUEL BAIS 18:2–3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:22–31"> And Rav Sha'ul desired to enter into the crowd in the theater, but the talmidim were not allowing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:6"> And also some of the Asiarchs who were his chaverim, sent to him, begging him not to venture into the theater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:34; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:40; MATTITYAHU 11:7–9"> Then some began shouting one thing, some another, in the theater, for the kahal was in confusion, and most had no da'as of the purpose of the kahal (assembly).</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:17; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:14; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:20"> And from the multitude Alexander was pushed forward by Yehudim and some prompted him with shouts, while Alexander waved his hand, wanting to defend himself to the kahal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:28; MATTITYAHU 6:7; HISGALUS 13:4"> But having known what the man was, that he was a Yehudi, all in the theater shouted in unison for lav davka two hours crying out, Gedolah is Artemis of the Ephesians.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:10–11; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:12"> And the town clerk, when he had restrained the crowd, said, Anashim, citizens of Ephesus, who indeed is there among Bnei Adam who does not have da'as that the city of Ephesus is the guardian of the temple of the great Artemis and of the tzelem (image) that fell from Shomayim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MISHLE 14:29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:35–39; MISHLE 25:8"> Since these things are undeniable, it is necessary for you to calm down and to do nothing reckless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:8"> For you brought these anashim here who are neither temple robbers nor speakers of evil against our religion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:14"> Therefore, if Demetrius and the craftsmen with him have a tainah (grievance) against anyone, the civil courts are in session and there are proconsuls. Let it be there that they bring charges against one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And if you seek anything further, let your settlement be obtained in the lawful assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:5–8; MATTITYAHU 26:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:1"> For indeed we are in danger of being indicted for an intifadah (uprising) today, for there is no justification we can render to excuse this disorder and commotion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="KOHELET 9:17; TEHILLIM 65:7; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:8–10"> And having said these things, the town clerk dismissed the kahal (assembly).</span>
</div>
