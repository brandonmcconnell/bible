And on the next day we got to Rhodes, and from there to Patara.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YONAH 1:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:3"> And having found an oniyah crossing over to Phoenicia, we went on board and set sail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:36; MATTITYAHU 4:24"> And having come within sight of Cyprus, and leaving Cyprus behind on our left, we were sailing to Syria and we arrived in Tzor, for there the oniyah was unloading its cargo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:22–23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:10–12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:6–7"> And after having searched for Moshiachʼs talmidim there, we stayed in Tzor shivah yamim. And Moshiachʼs talmidim were telling Rav Sha'ul by the Ruach Hakodesh not to make any aliyah (ascent) to Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:36; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:40; TEHILLIM 95:6"> But when our yamim there were ended, we departed and everyone, including nashim and yeladim, were accompanying us as far as the outskirts of the city; and falling down on the beach, we all were davening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:27; YOCHANAN 1:11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:13"> And having bid them Shalom, we embarked in the oniyah, and they returned to their own batim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:24; SHMUEL ALEF 10:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:12"> And when we had completed the voyage from Tzor, we arrived in Ptolemais, and, having given a Shalom greeting to the Achim b'Moshiach there, we stayed yom echad with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:26–40; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:11"> And on the next day, having left, we came to Caesarea; and entered the bais of Philippos, the maggid of the Besuras HaGeulah who was one of HaShivah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="see Ac 6:3">a</a></sup>And we stayed with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:17; HISGALUS 2:20; YOEL 2:28"> And Philippos was the abba of arbah banot, betulot, who were gifted in giving a dvar nevuah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ex 15:20; Judg 4:4; Neh 6:14">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:16"> And remaining there many yamim, a certain one came down from Yehudah, a navi, Agav by name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:33; YIRMEYAH 13:1–11; SHMUEL ALEF 15:27–28"> And when he came to us, he took Rav Sha'ulʼs gartel and, having bound his own feet and hands with it, Agav said, So says the Ruach Hakodesh: this is the way the Yehudim in Yerushalayim will bind the man who owns this gartel, and they will hand him over to the Goyim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1Kgs 22:11; Isa 20:2-4; Jer 13:1-11">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 16:21–23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:4"> And when we heard these things, we were begging Rav Sha'ul, both we and the locals, not to make any aliyah (ascent) to Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:20–21"> Then Rav Sha'ul answered, What are you doing weeping and breaking my lev (heart)? For I am prepared not only to be bound but to die al kiddush ha-Shem in Yerushalayim, for haShem of the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKAS 22:42; MATTITYAHU 26:42; MATTITYAHU 26:39"> And not persuading him, we remained silent, having said, Let the ratzon Hashem be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:9"> And after these yamim, having made preparations, we were making an aliyah to Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MISHLE 16:31; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:8; TEHILLIM 92:14"> And also some of Moshiachʼs talmidim from Caesarea traveled with us, bringing us for hachnosas orchim to the bais of the Cyprian Mnason, one of Moshiachʼs talmidim of long standing.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="an early disciple">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:1–2"> And when we arrived in Yerushalayim, the Achim b'Moshiach there gave us a kabbalat panim welcome with lev same'ach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:13"> And on the following day Rav Sha'ul was going in with us to Ya'akov,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Ya'akov's letter which precedes the two Igrot Kodesh of Kefa.">e</a></sup>and all the Ziknei HaKehillah came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:18–19"> And having given them a Shalom greeting, Rav Sha'ul was explaining one by one the things Hashem did through his avodas kodesh as Moshiachʼs Shliach to the Umot ha-Olam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:3"> And the ones who heard were saying, Baruch Hashem! And they said to him, You see, Ach b'Moshiach, how many thousands there are among the Yehudim who have emunah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="in our Moshiach">f</a></sup>and they all have kanous (zealousness), are shomer mitzvot for the Torah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Sinai Covenant and its mitzvot">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:12–15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:19–21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:1–6"> And these Orthodox Jewish ma'aminim hameshichiyim have heard a rumor informing them about you, that you teach the Yehudim living among the Goyim in the Golus to be shmad and to commit apostasy from Moshe,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Rabbeinu, i.e., Orthodox Judaism">h</a></sup>telling them to abandon bris milah for Bnei Yisroel and not to be shomer regarding the darkhei haTorah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="i.e. the Sinai Covenant and its mitzvot">i</a></sup>(ways of the Torah).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Num 6:2,5">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:32"> What then is to be done? Certainly these Orthodox Jewish ma'aminim hameshichiyim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="i.e., who have emunah in our Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua, Ac 21:20">k</a></sup>will get word that you have come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:18; BAMIDBAR 6:2–7"> This, therefore, do what we tell you. There are with us arba'ah anashim who are under a neder Nezirim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:18"> Go! Take these for hitkaddeshut (purification) and purify yourself with them and pay expenses for them, that they may cut their hair; then all shall have da'as that there is no emes to the rumor; then it shall be clear that you yourself are in agreement and frum regarding the darkhei haTorah and that you are charedi and shomer mitzvot, in the ranks of those who practice Orthodox Judaism.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Num 6:1-21, Mishnah tractate Nazir">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:19–20"> And concerning the Goyim coming to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Orthodox Jewish">m</a></sup>emunah, we have sent an iggeret with our decision that they avoid what is offered to elilim and dahm and what is strangled and zenut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:24; BAMIDBAR 6:13–20"> Then Rav Sha'ul took the anashim on the following day, and he went with them for tohorah (purification) and was entering the Beis Hamikdash, giving notice of the completion of the yamim of the tohorah until was offered for each one of them the korban.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Num 6:13-20">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:50"> Now when the shivat yamim were about to be completed, unbelieving Yehudim from the Province of Asia &#91;Ephesus&#93;, having seen Rav Sha'ul in the Beis Hamikdash, were stirring up all the multitude, and they laid their hands on him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Jer 26:8">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:5–6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:20–21"> Crying out, Anashim, Bnei Yisroel, help us! This one is the ish who is giving shiurim to everyone everywhere keneged (against, in opposition to) HaAm<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Yisroel">p</a></sup>and the Torah of Moshe Rabbenu and this Makom HaKadosh, and in addition also he brought Yevanim into the Beis Hamikdash and has defiled Makom HaKadosh HaZeh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="the Beis Hamikdash">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:4; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:19"> — For they had previously seen Trophimus the Ephesian in the city with Rav Sha'ul and they had jumped to the conclusion and made the unwarranted assumption that Rav Sha'ul had brought Trophimus past the Soreg<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="barrier of the holy precinct">r</a></sup>in the Beis Hamikdash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:21; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:19–22"> — And the whole city was aroused and the people were rushing together, and they seized Rav Sha'ul, and they were dragging him outside the Beit Hamikdash; and immediately the delatot were shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:23–33; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:9–10"> And while they were trying to kill him, a report went up to a tribune of the Roman cohort that all Yerushalayim was in an oifshtand (uprising) uproar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:27; YESHAYAH 3:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:40"> At once he took his chaiyalim and centurions and ran down to them, and when the rioters saw the Roman tribune and the chaiyalim, they stopped beating Rav Sha'ul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:23"> Then the Roman tribune came near and took hold of Rav Sha'ul and ordered him to be bound with two sharsherot (chains), and the tribune was inquiring who he might be and what he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:32; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:24"> Some in the crowd were shouting one thing, some another, and the tribune was not able to have da'as of anything definite because of the noise, so he ordered him to be brought into the barracks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="CHABAKUK 1:2–3; TEHILLIM 55:9; TEHILLIM 58:2"> And when Rav Sha'ul was on the steps, it came about that he was carried by the chaiyalim, because of the violence of the multitude,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="YOCHANAN 19:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:22; LUKAS 23:18"> For the crowd kept following and shouting, Away with him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:34; LUKAS 21:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:30"> And just when he was about to be brought into the barracks, Rav Sha'ul says to the Roman tribune, Is it permissible for me to say something to you? And the tribune said, Ellinisti Ginoskeis? (Do you know Greek?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:36–37; MATTITYAHU 5:11"> Then you are not the Egyptian who recently stirred up an intifada and led out four thousand assassins into the wilderness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:27"> And Rav Sha'ul said, I gufa (myself) am an ish Yehudi from Tarsus of Cilicia, a citizen of no insignificant city. And I ask you to allow me to speak to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:2; YOCHANAN 5:2"> And when the tribune had given permission, Rav Sha'ul stood on the steps and motioned with his hand to the people. And a great silence fell upon the crowd. Then Rav Sha'ul addressed the multitude in the language of the Hebrews.</span>
