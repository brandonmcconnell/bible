<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:34; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:40"> Therefore Festus, having arrived in the Province, after shloshah yamim went up from Caesarea to Yerushalayim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:15; MISHLE 4:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:1"> Where the Rashei Hakohanim and manhigim of the Yehudim explained to Festus the charges keneged Rav Sha'ul, and they were begging him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:12–15; SHMUEL ALEF 23:19–21"> Asking a favor from him, that Festus might summon Rav Sha'ul to Yerushalayim, while at the same time they were forming a kesher to kill him along the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:23"> Festus answered that Rav Sha'ul was to be kept in Caesarea and that Festus himself intended quickly to go there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:25; YOCHANAN 18:29–30; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:18–19"> Therefore, those in authority among you, Festus says, should come down with me, and if there is anything in the wrong about Rav Sha'ul, then let them bring charges against him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:10; MATTITYAHU 27:19"> And having stayed with them no more than shmonah or asarah yamim, Festus went down to Caesarea; the next day he sat on the Kes HaMishpat and ordered Rav Sha'ul to be brought in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:13; LUKAS 23:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:24"> When Rav Sha'ul came in, the Judeans who had come down from Yerushalayim stood around him and brought serious charges keneged (against) him, which they were not able to prove.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:17; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:12"> Rav Sha'ul defended himself, saying, Neither keneged the Torah nor keneged the Beis Hamikdash nor keneged Caesar have I done anything wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:20"> But Festus, wishing to grant the Judeans a favor, said in reply to Rav Sha'ul, Do you want to go up to Yerushalayim to be judged by me there concerning these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:18; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:18"> And Rav Sha'ul said, I am standing before the Kes HaMishpat of Caesar, where it is necessary for me to be tried. I have done no wrong to my Jewish people, as you also have da'as very well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:32; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:25"> Now if I have done wrong and am worthy of mavet, I am not trying to escape the penalty. But if there is nothing to the charges these bring against me, no one is able to hand me over to them. Therefore, I appeal to Caesar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:12–14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:20"> Then Festus, having talked with his council, answered, To Caesar you have appealed, to Caesar you will go.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:40; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:27–28"> After several yamim had passed, Agrippa HaMelech and Bernice arrived in Caesarea, having paid their respects to Festus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:27"> And while they were spending many yamim there, Festus laid out to the Melech the things with respect to Rav Sha'ul, saying, There is a man here who was left behind in the beis hasohar by Felix.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:1–3; ESTHER 3:9; LUKAS 18:3–5"> When I went to Yerushalayim, the Rashei Hakohanim and the Zekenim of the Yehudim informed me about this man, requesting keneged him a sentence of condemnation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:51; DEVARIM 19:17–18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:30"> I answered them that it violated Roman law to hand over any man before the accused met face to face with his accusers and had an opportunity for a hitstaddekut (defense) concerning the accusation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:10"> Therefore, they were assembled here; I allowed no delay, and on the next day, I sat down on the Kes HaMishpat and ordered the man brought in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But when the accusers stood up, no charge were they bringing of any crimes I was expecting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:3–4"> Instead it was an internal matter having to do with questions regarding Orthodox Judaism, and certain disagreements they had with Rav Sha'ul, and regarding a certain Yehoshua who was deceased, but whom Rav Sha'ul asserted was alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:9"> Since I was not qualified to investigate these religious questions, I was saying he might wish to go to Yerushalayim and there to be judged concerning these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:16; LUKAS 2:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:10–11"> But Rav Sha'ul appealed that he be kept in custody for the decision of Caesar, so I ordered him to be kept until I send him to Caesar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:15; MATTITYAHU 10:18; YESHAYAH 52:15"> And Agrippa said to Festus, I was desiring also myself to hear Rav Sha'ul. Then Festus says, Tomorrow you will hear him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:30; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:13; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:16"> Then on the next day Agrippa and Bernice came with great pomp and entered into the auditorium along with both the military tribunes and the chashuve anashim of Caesarea. And Festus the Moshel gave orders to have Rav Sha'ul brought in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:2–3"> And Festus says, Agrippa HaMelech and all here present with us, you see this man about whom K'lal Yisroel has petitioned me, both in Yerushalayim and here, shouting that he is no longer fit to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:11–12; LUKAS 23:4"> But I found he has done nothing worthy of mavet, and when he appealed to Caesar, I decided to send him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:2–3"> But I have nothing definite to write to our sovereign; therefore, I especially brought him before you, O Agrippa HaMelech, so that, after you have examined him, I may have something I may write.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YOCHANAN 7:51; MISHLE 18:13"> For it seems unreasonable to me sending a prisoner and not having charges to report against him.</span>
</div>
