<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:16; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:12"> And as he gazed at the Sanhedrin, Rav Sha'ul said, Anashim, Achim, I have lived before Hashem until this day with a lev tahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YOCHANAN 18:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:1; MICHOH 5:1"> And the Kohen Gadol Chananyah ordered the ones standing by Rav Sha'ul to strike his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEVARIM 25:1–2; YOCHANAN 7:51; MATTITYAHU 23:27–28"> Then Rav Sha'ul said to him, Hashem is about to strike you, you white-washed wall! You sit judging me according to Torah and yet in violation of the Torah you command me to be struck?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lv 19:15; Dt 25:1,2">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And the ones standing by said, Do you revile the Kohen Gadol of Hashem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 22:28; KOHELET 10:20; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8–9"> And Rav Sha'ul said, I was without da'as, Achim, that he is the Kohen Gadol, for it has been written, NASI V'AMMECHA LO TA'OR (A ruler of your people you will not speak evil of, curse).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ex 22:28[27]">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:5–8"> And Rav Sha'ul, having da'as that one kat is of Tzedukim (Sadducees) and the other of Perushim (Pharisees), was crying out in the Sanhedrin, Anashim, Achim, I am a Parush ben Parush and it is for the tikvah of the Techiyas HaMesim that I am being judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 55:9; YOCHANAN 7:40–43; MATTITYAHU 10:34"> Now when Rav Sha'ul said this, there came about a machlochet (controversy) between the Perushim and Tzedukim, and the multitude was divided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKAS 20:27; MATTITYAHU 22:23; MARKOS 12:18"> Loit (according to) the Tzedukim, there is no Techiyas HaMesim nor a malach nor ruchot, but Perushim acknowledge all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:17–18; YOCHANAN 12:29"> And there was a kol gadol and some of the Sofrim of the kat of the Perushim were arguing vigorously, saying, Nothing rah do we find keneged this ish, and what if a ruach did speak to him or a malach?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:14–2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:24"> And fearing much more machloket was coming, the Roman tribune ordered the troops to go down to take Rav Sha'ul away from the midst of them and to bring him into the barracks lest he be torn to pieces by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:21; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:17"> And on the following lailah, HaAdon stood by Rav Sha'ul and said, Chazak! For as you gave solemn edut about me in Yerushalayim, thus it is necessary for you also to bear solemn eidus in Rome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:30; YESHAYAH 8:9–10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:21"> In the boker, the Judeans joined in a kesher and bound themselves with a shevu'ah that none of them would eat or drink until they had carried out their plot of retzichah b'seter of Rav Sha'ul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:2; SHMUEL BAIS 15:31; SHMUEL BAIS 15:12"> Now there were more than arba'im anashim (forty men) who joined in this kesher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:12; YIRMEYAH 8:12; YESHAYAH 3:9"> After they approached the Rashei HaKohanim (Chief Priests) and the Zekenim (Elders), they said, We have bound ourselves with a shevu'ah (oath) to eat nothing until we have killed Rav Sha'ul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YESHAYAH 59:7; TEHILLIM 37:32–33; MISHLE 1:16"> Now, therefore, you with the Sanhedrin notify the tribune so that he may bring Rav Sha'ul down to you as if intending to determine more accurately the things concerning him, and, before he draws near, we are ready to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:32"> Now when Rav Sha'ulʼs achyon (nephew) heard about the ma'arav (ambush), he went and gained entrance into the barracks and reported this to Rav Sha'ul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MISHLE 22:3; MATTITYAHU 8:8–9; MATTITYAHU 10:16"> And Rav Sha'ul summoned one of the centurions and said, Bring this bochur to the tribune for he has something to report to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:17"> So the centurion took the bochur and brought him to the tribune and says, The prisoner Rav Sha'ul has summoned me and asked me to bring this bochur to you; he has something to say to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:32; ESTHER 9:12; ESTHER 7:2"> The tribune grasped the bochurʼs hand and drew him aside privately, inquiring, What is it you have to report to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:14–15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:1; TEHILLIM 12:2"> And the bochur said, The Judeans agreed to ask you to bring Rav Sha'ul down machar (tomorrow) to the Sanhedrin as if intending to inquire something more accurate concerning him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:12–14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:5–6; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:26"> Therefore, you should not be persuaded by them, for more than arba'im anashim are lying in wait for him and these took a shevu'ah upon themselves neither to eat nor to drink until they have assassinated him, and now they are ready, waiting for your consent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MARKOS 1:44; YEHOSHUA 2:14"> Therefore, the tribune dismissed the bochur, ordering him, Tell no one that you reported these things to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 14:25; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:33; LUKAS 12:38"> And having summoned two of the centurions, the tribune said, Prepare to leave for Caesarea by nine o'clock tonight the following: two hundred chaiyalim, seventy horsemen, and two hundred bowmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:10"> And also provide mounts for Rav Sha'ul to ride, that he may be given safe passage to Felix the Moshel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Then the tribune wrote an iggeret to this effect:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:25; LUKAS 1:3"> Claudius Lysias to the most excellent Moshel, Felix. Greetings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:25–29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:31–33; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:6"> This man was seized by the Judeans and was about to be killed by them, but when I learned that he is a Roman citizen, I came with the chaiyalim and rescued him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:30"> Desiring to have da'as of the charge for which they were accusing him, I brought him down to their Sanhedrin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:31; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:25; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:15"> I found he was being accused about issues of their Torah and the charge was nothing worthy of mavet or of imprisonment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:35; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:5–6"> And when I received information of a kesher (plot) keneged (against) the man, I sent him immediately to you, having given orders also to his accusers to state their case against Rav Sha'ul before your excellency.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:3–4; LUKAS 7:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:23–24"> Therefore, as they were ordered, the chaiyalim took Rav Sha'ul along and brought him under cover of lailah to Antipatris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:10"> And on the next day, having allowed the horsemen to go on with Rav Sha'ul, they returned to the barracks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:23–24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:16"> When the horsemen entered into Caesarea, they delivered the iggeret to the Moshel and also presented Rav Sha'ul to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:39; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:1; DANIEL 2:49"> And having read it, and having asked from what province Rav Sha'ul hailed, the Moshel learned that he was from Cilicia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:30; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:16; MATTITYAHU 27:27"> I will give you a hearing, Felix said, when also your accusers arrive. Then the Moshel commanded Rav Sha'ul to be watched by shomrim in the Praetorium of Herod.</span>
