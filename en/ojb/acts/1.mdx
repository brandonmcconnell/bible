<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKAS 1:3; LUKAS 24:19; MATTITYAHU 4:23–24"> In the sefer harishon I wrote about everything, O Theophilus, about Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, both what he began to do and the shiurim he began to say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:9; YOCHANAN 20:21; YESHAYAH 59:20–21"> Until HaYom, when he made aliyah ascent to Shomayim, having given Moshiachʼs mitzvot through the Ruach Hakodesh to the Shlichim whom he chose,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:5–7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:31; YOCHANAN 20:26"> To whom also he presented himself chai (alive), after his Messianic yissurim (sufferings), by many convincing proofs, during arba'im yamim appearing to his Shlichim and speaking concerning the Malchut Hashem:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKAS 24:49; YOCHANAN 14:16; YOCHANAN 15:26"> And, while gathering them at a yechidus, Moshiach gave instructions to them not to depart from Yerushalayim, but to wait for the havtachah (promise) of &#91;Elohim&#93; HaAv which he said, You heard of from Me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ps 27:14">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 3:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:15–16; LUKAS 3:16"> For Yochanan gave a tevilah of teshuva with a mikveh mayim, but you will receive a tevilah in the Ruach Hakodesh not many yamim from now.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Yoel 3:1">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKAS 19:11; LUKAS 17:20; DANIEL 7:27"> And at yechidus, having come together, they were asking him saying, Adoneinu, is it at this time that You are restoring the Malchut Hashem to Am Yisroel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 24:36; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:1–2; MARKOS 13:32"> But he said to them, It is not for you to have da'as of the itim (times) or moadim (seasons) which &#91;Elohim&#93; HaAv has set by His own samchut (authority).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Dt 29:28; Ps 107:13">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:19; MARKOS 16:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:19"> But you will receive ko'ach when the Ruach Hakodesh has come upon you, and you all will be the Eidus (the Witness) of me, in Yerushalayim, and in all Yehudah, and Shomron and as far as ad ketzeh ha'aretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DANIEL 7:13; LUKAS 24:50–51; MARKOS 16:19"> And having said these things, while they were looking on, in an aliyah ascent to Shomayim, Moshiach was taken up; and an anan (cloud) took him away from their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKAS 24:4; YOCHANAN 20:12; HISGALUS 7:14"> And as they were gazing intently into Shomayim, while Moshiach was going — hinei — two beings, enrobed in white, had been present with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:16; MARKOS 13:26"> Also, these said, Men of the Galil, why do you stand looking into Shomayim? This Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, the one having been taken up from you into Shomayim, will also come again in like manner as you saw him going into Shomayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKAS 24:52; MATTITYAHU 21:1; LUKAS 24:50"> Then they returned to Yerushalayim from Har HaZeytim, which from Yerushalayim is a Shabbos walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:8; LUKAS 22:12; MARKOS 14:15"> And when they entered, they went up to the aliyyah where they were staying, that is, Kefa and Yochanan and Ya'akov and Andrew and Philippos and T'oma and Bar-Talmai and Mattityahu and Ya'akov Ben-Chalfai and Shim'on the Zealot and Yehudah Ben Ya'akov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:2"> These all with one mind were continually devoting themselves to tefillah, with the nashim and with Miryam the Em of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach and with his achim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:20; HISGALUS 3:4"> And at this time, having stood up, Kefa, in the midst of the Achim b'Moshiach — there were 120 persons in the place</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="TEHILLIM 41:9; YOCHANAN 13:18; TEHILLIM 55:12–15"> — Said, Achim b'Moshiach, the Kitvei Hakodesh had to be fulfilled, which the Ruach Hakodesh foretold through the peh of Dovid Hamelech concerning Yehudah, who became a guide to the ones arresting Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:25; MARKOS 3:19; YOCHANAN 17:12"> For he had been numbered among us, and he received his ministry in the Messianic avodas kodesh of the Moshiachʼs Shlichim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:3–10; MATTITYAHU 26:14–15; TEHILLIM 55:23"> (Now this man, therefore, acquired a sadeh out of the sachar (reward) of his peysha and, having fallen headlong, he plotst (burst) open in the middle and all the inward parts of him were poured out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:22; MATTITYAHU 28:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:40"> And this became known to all the ones inhabiting Yerushalayim, so that the sadeh (field) became known in their language as “Akeldama” — that is “Sadeh of Dahm.”)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 69:25; TEHILLIM 109:8–15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:25"> For it has been written in the Sefer Tehillim, TEHI the place of him NESHAMMAH (one being deserted)… V'AL YEHI YOSHEV (May his place be deserted and let him not be the one dwelling in it),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ps 69:26">d</a></sup>and PEKUDATO YIKACH ACHER (his place of leadership may another take.)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ps 109:8">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:27; LUKAS 10:1–2; BAMIDBAR 27:17"> It is necessary, therefore, that one of the anashim who accompanied us during all the time in which Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua went in and went out among us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:33; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:8–9"> Beginning from the tevilah of teshuva of Yochanan until the day when Moshiach was taken up from us — one of these should become Eidus (Witness) with us to the Techiyas HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:26"> And they put forward two anashim, Yosef Bar-Sabba, also called Justus, and Mattityahu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 16:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:8; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:13"> And having davened, they said, Adonoi, you have da'as of the levavot of Kol Bnei Adam. Therefore, show which of these two is your bechirah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1Sm 14:41">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:2; YOCHANAN 6:70–71; YOCHANAN 17:12"> to take the place of this avodas kodesh ministry and Shlichus from which Yehudah turned aside to go to his own place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="VAYIKRA 16:8; YEHOSHUA 18:10; SHMUEL ALEF 14:41–42"> And they drew lots, and the lot fell to Mattityahu, and he was numbered with the Achad Asar of Moshiachʼs Shlichim.</span>
</div>
