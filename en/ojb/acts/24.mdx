<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:2"> And after chamash yamim, the Kohen Gadol Chananyah came down with some Zekenim and an orator, a certain Tertullus, who explained to the Moshel the charges keneged (against) Rav Sha'ul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MISHLE 29:5; MISHLE 26:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:26–27"> And when Rav Sha'ul had been summoned, Tertullus began to accuse him, saying, Your Excellency, much shalom has been attained through you, and reforms have come to this nation by your foresight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:25; LUKAS 1:3"> This in every way and everywhere, we acknowledge, most excellent Felix, with todah rabbah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:32"> But in order that I may not detain you any longer, I beg you in your kindness to hear us briefly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:5"> For having found this man a troublemaker and an inciter of riots among all the Yehudim throughout kol ha'aretz, a manhig of the kat,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="of Judaism">a</a></sup>the Natzrati Kat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:27–32; YOCHANAN 18:31"> He even tried to desecrate the Beis Hamikdash! And so we apprehended him, &#91;and would have tried him according to our own law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> But the sar haelef Lysias came, and with great ko'ach took him away out of our hands, and ordered his adversaries to come unto you.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:35; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:15–16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:5"> When you examine him, you will be able to find out yourself regarding all these things of which we accuse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:16; MICHOH 7:2–3; TEHILLIM 4:2"> And the rest of the Judeans joined in the attack, saying that these things were so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:15; SHMUEL ALEF 2:25; LUKAS 18:2"> And when the Moshel nodded to him to speak, Rav Sha'ul answered, Knowing that for many years you have been a shofet (judge) to this nation, I cheerfully make my hitstaddekut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:11"> You will be able to learn that not more than twelve days have elapsed since I made an aliyah to worship in Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:18"> And neither in the Beis Hamikdash did they find me conversing with anyone nor did they find me stirring up the multitude either in the shuls or anywhere in Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:7; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16"> Nor are they able to prove to you concerning the things of which now they are accusing me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:13"> For Ani modeh (I confess) this to you, that according to the Derech Hashem, which they call a “kat”,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="of Judaism">b</a></sup>I serve the Elohei Avoteinu, having emunah in everything written according to the Torah and the Nevi'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DANIEL 12:2; YOCHANAN 5:28–29; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:14–16"> I have a tikvah (hope) in Hashem, which also these ones themselves anticipate, the coming Techiyas HaMesim of both the Tzaddikim and the Resha'im.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:1; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:5; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:2"> In view of this, I always do my best to keep a matzpun naki (clear conscience) before Hashem and before anashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:25–28; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:1–4"> Now after many years, I came bringing nedavot (donations) to my people and also to make korbanot (sacrifices).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:26–30; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:12"> And this is how they found me, having undergone purification in the Beis Hamikdash, not with rioters nor with any disturbance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:30; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:16"> But there were some Yehudim from the Province of Asia who ought to be present before you to make accusation, if they might have something against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Or let these themselves say what crime they found in me when they stood me before the Sanhedrin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:6–8"> unless it is about this one statement, which I stood and cried out among them, and that statement was concerning the Techiyas HaMesim, and it is for this that I am being judged today by you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:26"> But Felix, who was already rather well informed about the Derech Hashem, adjourned the meeting, saying, When Lysias the tribune comes down, I will decide your case.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:3; MISHLE 16:7"> Then Felix gave orders to the centurion to guard Rav Sha'ul, but to grant him considerable liberty and not to prevent his chaverim from ministering to his needs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:16; MARKOS 6:20"> And after some yamim, Felix came with Drusilla, his wife, who was Jewish. He summoned Rav Sha'ul and listened to him concerning his emunah (faith) in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YOCHANAN 16:8; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:42"> And as Rav Sha'ul was conversing about tzedek<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Dan 9:24">c</a></sup>and kibush hayetzer (self-control) and about the Yom HaDin coming, Felix suddenly became afraid, and said, That will do for the moment. Go now. When I find time, I'll send for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:9–10; MISHLE 17:23; YESHAYAH 56:11"> At the same time Felix was also hoping that bribe money would be given to him by Rav Sha'ul. Therefore, Felix used to frequently send for Rav Sha'ul to converse with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:3"> When two years passed, Felix received a successor, Porcius Festus, and, wanting to grant a favor to the Judeans, Felix left Rav Sha'ul in custody.</span>
</div>
