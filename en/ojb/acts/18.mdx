<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:2; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:20; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:1"> After these things Rav Sha'ul left Athens and went to Corinth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:3–4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:19; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:19"> And in Corinth Rav Sha'ul found some Messianic Jews, namely Aquila hailing from Pontus, having recently come from Italy, where Claudius ordered a decree of Jewish expulsion from Rome, and Priscilla, his isha. Rav Sha'ul went to see them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:9"> And because he had the same parnasah (livelihood), Rav Sha'ul was staying with them, and he was working with them, for they were tentmakers by trade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:23"> And Rav Sha'ul was debating in the shul every Shabbos, and he was convincing Yehudim and Yevanim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:28; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:21"> Now when both Sila and Timotiyos came down from Macedonia, Rav Sha'ul was farnumen (preoccupied) and totally absorbed with the dvar Hashem, bearing solemn edut to the Yehudim that Yehoshua is the Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 33:4; SHMUEL BAIS 1:16; YECHEZKEL 33:8–9"> But when some began opposing Rav Sha'ul, and when they began committing Chillul Hashem, Rav Sha'ul shook out his kaftan and said to them, The responsibility of your lot be upon your own head! For I am tahor (clean); from now on, I go to the Nations.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2Sm 1:16; Ezek 33:4; 3:17-19; Neh 5:13">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:2"> And so Rav Sha'ul left them, and entered into the bais of a certain ish by the name Titius Justus, a yire Elohim whose bais was right next door to the shul!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:13–17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:14–17; MARKOS 16:15–16"> And Crispus, the Rosh Beit HaKnesset, came to emunah in Moshiach Adoneinu along with his entire bais; also many of the Corinthians who heard came to emunah, and were being given Moshiachʼs tevilah of teshuva.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:19–20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:23–25"> Then in the lailah, through a chazon, Adonoi said to Rav Sha'ul, Do not fear, but speak out, and do not keep silent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:9; SHEMOT 4:12; YESHAYAH 41:10"> For I am with you, and no one will attack you to harm you; I have many people in this city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:31"> And Rav Sha'ul sat shanah and shishah chodashim saying shiurim to them on the dvar Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:50; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:27; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:7"> And when Gallio was proconsul of Achaia, the &#91;disobedient&#93; Yehudim rose up with one accord keneged (against, in opposition to) Rav Sha'ul, and they took him to court,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:5–6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:13"> Saying, This one persuades anashim to worship Hashem in a manner hepech der Torah (contrary to the Torah).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:14–15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:11; MARKOS 9:19"> Rav Sha'ul was about to open his mouth when Gallio spoke to the Yehudim, If this matter concerned some wrong or heinous crime, O Yehudim, I might reasonably put up with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:19; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:4"> But if it is kashes (questions) about a dvar and shemot (names) and your Torah, you will see to it for yourselves. I do not intend to be a shofet (judge) of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HISGALUS 12:16; TEHILLIM 76:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:3–4"> And he had them ejected from the courtroom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:1; AMOS 6:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:32"> They seized Sosthenes, the Rosh HaKnesset, and were beating him in full view of the Kes HaMishpat (Judgment Seat), and none of these things created a concern for Gallio.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:24; BAMIDBAR 6:18; BAMIDBAR 6:5–9"> But Rav Sha'ul still remained a number of yamim and then took leave of the Achim b'Moshiach, and was sailing away to Syria; and with him were Priscilla and Aquila. In Cenchrea he cut his hair, for he had taken a neder (vow).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Num 6:2,5,18">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:1; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:3; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:18"> Then they arrived in Ephesus, and Rav Sha'ul left them there, but he joined the minyan in the shul and debated with the Yehudim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MARKOS 1:37–38; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:16"> And when they were asking Rav Sha'ul to stay for a longer time, he did not give his consent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:19; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:32"> But taking leave of them, he said, I will return again im yirtzeh Hashem(G-d willing). Then Rav Sha'ul set sail from Ephesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:40; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 23:23"> And having come down to Caesarea and having gone up and greeted the &#91;Yerushalayim&#93; Kehillah of Moshiach, then Rav Sha'ul went down to Antioch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:41"> And having spent some time, he embarked, passing through the area of Galatia and Phrygia, strengthening all the Moshiachʼs talmidim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:6; LETTER TO TITOS 3:13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:5–6"> Now a certain man, a Yehudi, arrived in Ephesus, Apollos by name, hailing from Alexandria, a lamdan (a Torah scholar with prodigious knowledge), being tief (deep and profound, erudite, keen) in the Kitvei HaKodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:11; HOSHEA 14:9"> This one had been taught the Derech Hashem and was on fire in the Ruach Hakodesh and saying shiurim and drashot accurate and true to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach. But Rav Apollos was acquainted only with the tevilah of teshuva of Yochanan.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Lk 3:16">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:25; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:21"> And this rabbi began to speak with ometz lev (boldness) in the shul. And having heard him, Priscilla and Aquila took him and more accurately instructed him in the Derech Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:13–14; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:16–18; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:2–3"> When Rav Apollos desired to go to Achaia, the Achim b'Moshiach encouraged him and wrote iggrot to Moshiachʼs talmidim there to welcome him. When Rav Apollos arrived, he greatly helped the ones who through the Chen v'Chesed Hashem had come to emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:3–4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:22"> For Rav Apollos was publicly and powerfully refuting the &#91;unbelieving&#93; Yehudim, showing through the Kitvei HaKodesh that Yehoshua is the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
