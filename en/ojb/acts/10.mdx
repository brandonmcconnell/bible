<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:1; MATTITYAHU 8:5–13; MATTITYAHU 27:27"> Now a certain ish in Caesarea by name Cornelius, a centurion from what was called the Italian cohort,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:7–8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:22"> A devout man, a yire Elohim, as was all his bais, who practiced much tzedakah for the benefit of Am Yisroel, and davened to Hashem continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DANIEL 9:20–21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:19"> Cornelius clearly saw in a chazon (vision) lav davka (approximately) at the ninth hour of the day (three o'clock in the afternoon) a malach Hashem having come to him and having said to him, Cornelius!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ps 55:17">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:6; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:31"> And having looked intently at him and having become afraid, Cornelius said, What is it, Adon? The malach said to him, Your tefillos and your giving tzedakah have ascended as a zikaron (memorial) before Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 20:3">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:38; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:7"> Now dispatch some anashim to Yafo and send for a certain Shim'on who is also called Kefa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:43; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:13–14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:14–17"> This one is staying with a certain Shim'on, a tanner, whose bais is by the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:16; SHMUEL ALEF 14:6–7; MATTITYAHU 8:9–10"> And, when the malach speaking to him had departed, Cornelius summoned shnayim of his household avadim and a devout chaiyal (soldier) from among the ones in his service,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:36; TEHILLIM 119:59–60; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:33"> Having explained everything, sent them to Yafo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TZEFANYAH 1:5; TEHILLIM 55:17; SHMUEL ALEF 9:25"> Now on the next day, as these were traveling and drawing near to Yafo, around the sixth hour (noon), Kefa went up on to the roof to daven (pray).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:17; HISGALUS 4:2–3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:2–4"> And Kefa became hungry, and was wanting to have a meal; but while they were preparing it, Kefa fell into a trance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:6; YOCHANAN 1:51; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:25–26"> And he sees Shomayim having been opened, and a certain object descending like a large linen cloth lowered by four corners upon haAretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 7:8–9; YOCHANAN 7:37; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:9–11"> In this were all the fourfooted animals and remasim haAdamah (creepers of the earth) and oph haShomayim (birds of heaven, birds of the air).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:31–34; YIRMEYAH 35:2–5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:10"> And there came a bat kol (voice) to him, Get up, Kefa, kill and eat!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:25; YECHEZKEL 4:14; DANIEL 1:8"> But Kefa said, Chalilah li, Adonoi! For I have never eaten basar piggul (unclean meat) or shikkutz tameh (abominable unclean thing).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Lv 11:4-20,25; Dt 14:3-20; Ezek 4:14">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:25; MATTITYAHU 15:11; LETTER TO TITOS 1:15"> And the bat kol came to Kefa again for a second time, What Hashem made tahor (clean), you should no longer regard as tameh (unclean).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gn 9:3">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:32; YOCHANAN 21:17; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:1"> And this happened shloshah pe'amim; and immediately the object was taken up into Shomayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YOCHANAN 13:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:7–19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:24"> Now, while Kefa was greatly perplexed within himself as to what the chazon which he saw might be, hinei, the anashim, who had been sent by Cornelius, and who had, by inquiring, found the bais of Shim'on, stood at the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:5–6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:11"> And, calling out, they were asking if Shim'on, the one called Kefa, is staying here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:4"> And while Kefa was reflecting on the chazon, the Ruach Hakodesh said to him, Hinei, shloshah anashim are looking for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:7–9; ZECHARYAH 2:9–11; YESHAYAH 48:16"> But get up and go downstairs and accompany them without apprehensions, for I myself have sent them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:38–39; MARKOS 10:51; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:29"> And Kefa went downstairs to the anashim and said, Hinei, I am the one whom you are seeking; for what reason did you come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:12"> And they said, Cornelius, a centurion, an ish tzaddik, and a yire Elohim with a shem tov with all the Am HaYehudim, was directed by a malach kadosh to summon you to his bais and to hear a message from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:45; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:12; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:9"> Therefore, having invited them in, Kefa gave them hachnosas orchim (hospitality, lodging). And on the next day Kefa got up and went away with them, and some of the Achim b'Moshiach from Yafo accompanied him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:40; ZECHARYAH 8:20–23; ZECHARYAH 3:10"> And on the following day, Moshiachʼs Shliach Shim'on Kefa entered into Caesarea, and Cornelius was expecting them, having called together his krovey mishpokhot (relatives) and close friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:10; HISGALUS 22:8–9; DANIEL 2:46"> Now when it came about that Kefa entered, Cornelius met him, falling at his feet to pay him reverence.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dan 7:14; 3:18">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="HISGALUS 19:10; HISGALUS 22:8–9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:14–15"> Kefa made him stand up, saying, Get up. I myself am only a ben Adam like everyone else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:12; YOCHANAN 4:35; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:24"> And as he conversed with Cornelius, Kefa entered and finds many having assembled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YOCHANAN 4:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:6–7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:12–14"> And he said to them, You have da'as that it is asur (prohibited) for an ish Yehudi to associate with or to approach a nokhri (foreigner). And yet to me Hashem showed to call no one shikkutz (abomination) or tameh (unclean).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:60; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:19–20"> Therefore, also, when summoned, I came, raising no objections. And so I ask for what reason you summoned me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:3; DANIEL 9:20–21; NECHEMYAH 9:1–3"> Cornelius said, Four days ago to this hour, I was davening in my bais at the ninth hour, and, hinei, a being stood before me enrobed in shining radiance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:4; DANIEL 9:23; LUKAS 1:13"> And he said, Cornelius, your tefillah was heard and your tzedakah is remembered before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Therefore, send to Yafo and summon Shim'on who is called Kefa. He is staying in the bais of Shim'on the tanner by the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:14; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:12"> At once I sent for you and you did well having come. Now, therefore, we all are present before Hashem to hear all the things that have been commanded to you by Adonoi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:7; DEVARIM 10:17"> And opening his mouth, Kefa said, Omein, I have binah that Hashem is not one to show masso panim (partiality),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Dt 10:17; 2Chr 19:7; Job 34:19">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:12–13"> But in every nation the ones who have yirat Shomayim and work Tzidkat Hashem are acceptable to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 28:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:36; HISGALUS 17:14"> The dvar which he sent to the Bnei Yisroel, preaching shalom through Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua (Hu Adon Kol!)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 52:7; Ps 107:20; 147:18TARGUM HASHIVIM">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKAS 4:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:22"> That word, I say, you know the thing which took place throughout all Yehudah, having begun from the Galil after the tevilah of teshuva which Yochanan preached;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LUKAS 4:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:22; YESHAYAH 61:1–3"> You know Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua from Natzeret, how Hashem anointed him with the Ruach Hakodesh and with ko'ach (power), and how he went about doing mitzvot and giving refu'ah shlemah to all the ones being oppressed by Samael (the devil) because Hashem was with him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isa 61:1">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LUKAS 24:48; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:41"> We are edim (witnesses) of all things which he did both in the countryside of Yehudah and in Yerushalayim. And they also put him to death, having hanged nivlato al haEtz<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Dt 21:23">i</a></sup>(his body on the Tree).</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:30–31; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:3–4"> This one Hashem made to stand up alive again on the Yom HaShelishi and granted to him to be visible,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:22; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:39; YOCHANAN 21:13"> Not to Klal Yisroel, but to edim which had been chosen beforehand by Hashem, that is, to us who ate and drank together with him at Tish after the Techiyas HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:10; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:1; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:5"> Moshiach gave mitzvah to us to preach to the people and to bear solemn edut that this one is the one having been appointed by Hashem as Shofet HaChayyim v'HaMesim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 13:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:38; YESHAYAH 53:11"> To this one all the Nevi'im bear witness that through ha-Shem of him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Moshiach Yehoshua, Yeshua">j</a></sup>everyone who has emunah in him has selicha (forgiveness) of chatta'im (averos, sins).</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:8"> While Kefa was still speaking these words, the Ruach Hakodesh fell upon all the ones hearing the dvar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:38; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:13–14"> And the Messianic Jews who had come with Kefa were nispoyel (standing in awe) that also upon the Goyim the matnat HaRuach Hakodesh has been poured out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:4; MARKOS 16:17"> For they were hearing them speaking in leshonot and exalting Hashem. Then Kefa answered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:36; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:15–17"> Surely no one can refuse the mikveh mayim for these to be given Moshiachʼs tevilah of teshuva who have received the Ruach Hakodesh just as we did, can he?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ac 2:4; cf. Lk 3:16; Ac 11:16">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:38; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:27"> And Kefa directed for them to be given Moshiachʼs tevilah of teshuva in the Shem of Moshiach Yehoshua (Yeshua). Then they asked him to remain some yamim.</span>
