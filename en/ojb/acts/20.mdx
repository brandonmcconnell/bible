<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:21; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:3"> And after the uproar had ended, Rav Sha'ul summoned the Moshiachʼs talmidim and, having exhorted them, and having taken his leave, he departed to go to Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:28"> And having traveled through those regions and having exhorted the Moshiachʼs talmidim there with many words, Rav Sha'ul came to Greece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:19; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:23–24"> And after he had spent shloshah chodashim there, Rav Sha'ul was about to set sail for Syria when a kesher (conspiracy) was plotted against him by the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="unbelieving">a</a></sup>Yehudim, and so Rav Sha'ul decided to return through Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:21; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:20"> And accompanying Rav Sha'ul from Berea was Sopater the son of Pyrrhus; from Thessalonika was Aristarchus and Secundus; from Derbe, Gaius; and from Asia, Timotiyos, Tychicus and Trophimus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:10–11; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:13"> And these, having gone ahead, were waiting for us in Troas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:12; SHEMOT 23:15"> And we sailed away after the Yamim HaMatzot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., Pesach">b</a></sup>from Philippi, and within chamash yamim we came to them in Troas, where we stayed shivah yamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:42; YOCHANAN 20:19"> And on Yom Rishon, when we met for tish,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="it was Motzoei Shabbos when there was a Melaveh Malkeh communal meal">c</a></sup>Rav Sha'ul was saying a shiur to them, since he would have to depart early the next day and was having to extend the message until chatzot halailah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:13; LUKAS 22:12"> And there were a number of menorot in the upper story, where we had assembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MARKOS 13:36; MATTITYAHU 26:40–41; YONAH 1:5–6"> And a certain bocher, by the name of Eutychus, was sitting on the window sill and began to succumb to a deep sleep while Rav Sha'ul was saying a shiur that went on and on. Overcome by this sleep, Eutychus fell from the third story downwards, and was picked up dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MARKOS 5:39; MATTITYAHU 9:23–24; MELACHIM ALEF 17:21–22"> And having come down, Rav Sha'ul fell upon him, and, having embraced him, Rav Sha'ul said, Do not be troubled, for his neshamah is in him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1Kgs 17:21; 2Kgs 4:34">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:9"> And having gone up and, at the Betzi'at HaLechem, having partaken of it, and having spoken until Shacharis, thus Rav Sha'ul departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:14; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:4"> And they led away the bochur chai! And they were not a little comforted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MARKOS 6:46; MARKOS 1:35; MARKOS 6:31–33"> And we went ahead to the oniyah (sailing vessel) and set sail for Assos, where we were intending to take Rav Sha'ul on board, for he had arranged it so, intending to travel himself there by land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When Rav Sha'ul met us in Assos, we took him on board and came to Mitylene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:17"> And from there, having sailed away, we arrived on the next day opposite Chios. And then the day following we crossed over to Samos, and, on the following day, we came to Miletus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:21"> For Rav Sha'ul had decided to sail past Ephesus, so he would not have to spend time in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="the Province of">e</a></sup>Asia, for he was hurrying in order that, if possible, he might by Shavu'os go up to Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 1:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:30; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:17"> And from Miletus, having sent to Ephesus, Rav Sha'ul summoned the Ziknei HaKehillah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:5–6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:1"> And when they came to him, he said to them, You have da'as from the yom harishon in which I set foot in Asia how I was with you the entire time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:24; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:11"> My avodas kodesh service to Hashem with all anavah (humility) and tears and tzoros, and all that happened to me by the kesharim of the &#91;unbelieving&#93; Yehudim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:27; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:42"> How I kept back nothing profitable from you, but kept giving you messianic drashot and shiurim publicly and from bais to bais,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ps 40:10; Jer 26:2; 42:2">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:38; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:18; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:11–13"> Bearing solemn edut both to Yehudim and Yevanim about teshuva toward Hashem and emunah in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:14; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:14; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:14"> And now, hinei, having been bound by the Ruach Hakodesh, I am going to Yerushalayim, not having da'as of the things that are going to happen to me there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:4"> Except that the Ruach Hakodesh, in every city, testifies to me, saying that the beis hasohar and tzoros are awaiting me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:6–8; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:1; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:10"> But I gufa (myself) do not account my existence in the Olam Hazeh of any value to myself, if only I may finish my course and the avodas kodesh sherut which I received from the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua to bear witness to the Besuras HaGeulah of the Chen v'Chesed Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:31; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:38; MATTITYAHU 4:23"> And now, hinei, I have da'as that no longer will you see my face, all of you among whom I went about dar-shenen the Malchut Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:6; YECHEZKEL 3:18–21; YECHEZKEL 33:2–9"> Therefore I bear solemn edut (testimony) to you on this very day today, that I am innocent of the blood of any of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:7–10"> For I did not shrink back from preaching and disclosing to all of you the whole etzat Hashem(counsel, plan of G-d).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:2–3; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:16; YESHAYAH 40:11"> Pay attention to yourselves and to all the Eder Hashem(flock of G-d) in which the Ruach Hakodesh has placed you as mashgichim ruchaniyim (spiritual overseers) to do the work of ro'im (shepherds) over the Kehillah of Hashem which Adoneinu purchased for the Geulah with his own dahm.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="kapporah">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 7:15; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:1; YOCHANAN 10:12"> I have da'as that after my departure savage ze'evim (wolves) will come in among you, not sparing the eder (flock).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ezek 34:5">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:19–20; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:3–4; MISHLE 23:33"> And from you yourselves will rise up anashim speaking perverted doctrines in order to draw away talmidim after themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:10"> Therefore, be shomer, remembering that for shloshah shanim, yomam v'lailah, I did not stop admonishing each one of you with tears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:24"> And now I commend you to Hashem and to the Dvar HaChen v'Chesed Hashem which is able to build you up and give you a nachalah (inheritance) among all the kedoshim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:12; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:18"> I coveted no oneʼs silver or gold or shtreimel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="1Sm 12:3">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:8–9"> You yourselves have da'as that these hands of mine supported all my needs as well as the needs of those with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:16; MISHLE 19:17; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:6–12"> In all this I was showing you a mofet (example) that thus working hard it is necessary to help the weak and to have zikaron of the devarim of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu Yehoshua, that he himself said, “Beser (better) to geben (give) than to nemen (take).”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:5; LUKAS 22:41; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:6"> And having said these things, Rav Sha'ul fell down and, with them all, began davening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:26"> And there was much weeping among them, and, having fallen upon the neck of Rav Sha'ul, they were kissing him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:25"> with agmat nefesh above all at the dvar in which Rav Sha'ul had said that no longer would they see his face again. And they were accompanying him to the oniyah.</span>
