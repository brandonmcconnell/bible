<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:6; BAMIDBAR 8:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:24"> Whom they placed before the Moshiachʼs Shlichim.
