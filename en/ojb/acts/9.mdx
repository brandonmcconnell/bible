<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:14"> Requested from him iggrot of reshus (authorization) to the shuls of Damascus, that if someone he should find being mishtatef (involved) in HaDerech,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hashem">a</a></sup>both anashim and nashim, he may lead them bound to Yerushalayim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Isa 17:1; Jer 49:23">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:12–13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:6"> Now as he goes, it came about that he comes near Damascus, and suddenly shone around him an Ohr (Light) from Shomayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 2:8; BERESHIS 22:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:22"> And he fell on the ground, and heard a kol (voice), saying to him, Sha'ul, Sha'ul, why are you persecuting me?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 6:8">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:13; SHMUEL ALEF 3:4–10; IYOV 9:4"> And he said, Who are you, Adoni? And he said, I am Yehoshua/Yeshua upon whom you bring redifah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:16; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:14–16"> But get up and enter into the city, and it shall be told to you what it is necessary for you to do.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ezek 3:22">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:9; DANIEL 10:7; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:13–14"> And the anashim traveling with Rav Sha'ul had stood speechless, hearing the sound but seeing no one.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dan 10:7">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:18; MELACHIM BAIS 6:17–20"> And Rav Sha'ul got up from the ground, and, though his eyes were open, he was seeing nothing. And leading him by the hand, they brought him into Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YONAH 3:6–8; ESTHER 4:16; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 33:12–13"> And he was shloshah yamim not seeing and neither did he eat nor drink.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 22:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:12"> Now there was a certain talmid in Damascus, by the name Chananyah, and Moshiach Adoneinu spoke to him in a chazon (vision), saying, Chananyah. And he said, Hinei, I am here, Adoni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:39; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:30"> And Moshiach Adoneinu said to him, Get up and go to the rekhov (street) being called Yashar (Straight) and seek in the bais of Yehudah an ish by name Sha'ul from Tarsus. For, hinei, he is davening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MARKOS 5:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:17–18"> And Sha'ul has seen in a chazon (vision) an ish by the name Chananyah come in and place his hands upon him that he may see again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:13–15; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:19–20"> And Chananyah answered, Adoni, I heard from many about this man, how many ra'ot (evil things) he did to your Kedoshim in Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:59; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:22"> And here he has samchut (authority) from the Rashei Kohanim to bind all the ones davening b'Shem of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:15–16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:7–8; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:16–17"> But Moshiach Adoneinu said to him, Go, for this one is a keli nivchar (chosen vessel) of mine to carry Shmi before both Goyim and melachim (kings) and the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:4"> For I will show him how much it is necessary for him to undergo yissurim (sufferings) on behalf of Shmi (my Name).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:4"> And Chananyah departed and entered into the bais, and having placed his hands upon Rav Sha'ul, he said, Ach b'Moshiach Sha'ul, &#91;Moshiach&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Mal 3:1">f</a></sup>HaAdon has sent me. I'm referring to Yehoshua, the one who appeared to you on the derech by which you were coming. He sent me that you may see again and may be filled with the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 22:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:38"> And immediately there fell from his eyes something like scales, and Rav Sha'ul saw again, and, having got up, he submitted to Moshiachʼs tevilah of teshuva.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:20; SHMUEL ALEF 30:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:17"> And having received okhel (food), Rav Sha'ul regained strength.<br />Now he was with the talmidim in Damascus several yamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YOCHANAN 20:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:20; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:14–15"> And immediately in the shuls Rav Sha'ul was preaching Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, saying, He is the Ben HaElohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:13–14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:3; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:10"> And all the ones listening were astonished, and they were saying, Is this not the one making havoc in Yerushalayim among the ones invoking this shem and was not his tachlis (purpose) in coming here to bind them over before the Rashei Hakohanim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:5; YESHAYAH 40:29; LUKAS 21:15"> But Rav Sha'ul even more was being strengthened and was confounding the unbelieving Yehudim dwelling in Damascus, by proving that this Yehoshua is the Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:17–18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:15–16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:19"> But when yamim rabbim were completed, the ones of the Yehudim who were unbelieving plotted to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:32; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 25:3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:3"> But the mezimma (evil design, intrigue) of their kesher (plot) became known to Rav Sha'ul. And they were also watching the she'arim (gates) both yomam valailah that they might kill him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 2:15; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:33; SHMUEL ALEF 19:11–12"> but Moshiachʼs talmidim had taken him b'lailah and they let him down through an opening in the wall, having lowered him in a large basket.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="1Sm 19:12">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 1:17–19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:19"> And having arrived in Yerushalayim, Rav Sha'ul was trying to associate with the Moshiachʼs talmidim, and they were all afraid of him, not believing that he is a talmid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:36; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:29; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:13"> But Bar-Nabba, having taken hold of him, brought him to Moshiachʼs Shluchim and told them how on the derech he saw Moshiach Adoneinu, who had spoken to Rav Sha'ul, and how in Damascus Rav Sha'ul spoke with ometz lev (boldness) b'Shem Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:21; BAMIDBAR 27:16–17; TEHILLIM 121:8"> And Rav Sha'ul was with them, going in and coming out in Yerushalayim, speaking boldly b'Shem Moshiach Adoneinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:1; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:20"> And Rav Sha'ul was speaking and debating keneged (against) the Greek-speaking Yehudim; but they were attempting to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:25; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:40; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:11"> But having learned of this, the Achim b'Moshiach brought Rav Sha'ul down to Caesarea and sent him away to Tarsus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="TEHILLIM 111:10; YOCHANAN 14:16–18; YESHAYAH 33:6"> Then Moshiachʼs Kehillah throughout all of Yehudah and the Galil and Shomron had shalom and was increasing, being built up, and going on in the yir'at Shomayim and in the yir'at Moshiach Adoneinu and beNechamat HaRuach Hakodesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:25; MISHLE 2:8"> Now it came about that Kefa, passing through all the parts of Eretz Yisrael, came down also to Moshiachʼs kedoshim dwelling in Lud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKAS 13:16; YOCHANAN 5:5; MARKOS 2:3–11"> And there Kefa found a certain ish by the name Aeneas who had been bedridden shmoneh shanim, for he was paralyzed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:6"> And Kefa said to him, Aeneas, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua make you whole. Get up and make your bed! And immediately he got up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:42; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 5:16"> And everyone dwelling in Lud and Sharon saw him, and they turned to Adoneinu.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="1Kgs 5:16; 2:1; Isa 33:9; 35:2; 65:10">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:8; LETTER TO TITOS 2:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 2:16"> Now in Yafo there was a certain talmidah by the name Tavitha, which, being translated, means gazelle. This one was abounding in ma'asim tovim and in giving tzedakah, which she was always doing.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Josh 19:46; 2Kgs 2:16; Ezra 3:7">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 1:13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:8; YOCHANAN 11:36–37"> And it came about in yamim hahem that this one, having become ill, passed away. When the women had washed her body, they laid it in the aliyyah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:36; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:32"> And since Lud was near to Yafo, Moshiachʼs talmidim, having heard that Kefa was there, sent shnei anashim to him, summoning Kefa that he not delay in coming to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MISHLE 10:7; MISHLE 31:30–31; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:3"> Kefa, getting up, went with them. And when he arrived, they brought him up into the aliyyah (upper story room), and all the almanot stood beside him, weeping and showing the tunics and garments that Tavitha made while she was with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 9:25; MARKOS 5:40–42; LUKAS 8:54"> But Kefa, having put everyone outside, and having fallen down, davened. And when he turned to the nifteret (deceased), he said, Tavitha, get up! And she opened her eynayim and, having seen Kefa, she sat up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MARKOS 1:31; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:1; BERESHIS 45:26"> And having given his hand to her, he made her to stand up alive again; and, having called the Moshiachʼs Kedoshim and the almanot, Kefa presented her lebedik (alive) before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="YOCHANAN 12:44; YOCHANAN 12:11; YOCHANAN 11:45"> It became known throughout all Yafo, and rabbim (many) had emunah in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Adoneinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:32"> And it came about that Kefa remained in Yafo yamim rabbim with a certain Shim'on, a tanner.</span>
