<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:51; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:46"> Now it came about in Iconium that together they entered into the shul and the same thing transpired: they spoke with the result that both Yehudim and a great multitude of Yevanim became ma'aminim hameshichiyim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:50; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:27–30; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:19"> And the Yehudim who disobeyed were the ones who aroused and stirred up in anger the nefashot (souls) of the Goyim keneged (against) the Achim b'Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:18–20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:32"> Therefore, they continued for a long zman (time) speaking with ometz lev (boldness) for Hashem, who gave edut to the dvar of Chen v'Chesed Hashem by granting otot and moftim to take place by the hands of Moshiachʼs Shluchim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:14; LUKAS 11:21–23"> And the multitude of the city was divided, and some were with the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ac 14:2">a</a></sup>&#91;disobedient&#93; Yehudim but, others, with the Moshiachʼs Shluchim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:19; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:5"> And when an attempt came by both the Goyim and the Yehudim with their rashim (heads, leaders) to mistreat and to stone them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:20–21; MATTITYAHU 10:23; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:11"> when the Moshiachʼs Shluchim became aware of this, they fled to the cities of Lycaonia, Lystra, and Derbe, and the surrounding countryside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:2"> And there the Moshiachʼs Shluchim were preaching the Besuras HaGeulah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:2; YOCHANAN 9:1–2; YOCHANAN 5:3–5"> And a certain ish in Lystra, powerless in his feet, was sitting, pise'ach from womb of his Em, a man who had never walked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:4; MATTITYAHU 15:28; MATTITYAHU 9:28–29"> This one heard Rav Sha'ul speaking, and when he gazed at the man and saw that he has emunah (faith) to be restored to health,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YESHAYAH 35:6; YOCHANAN 5:8–9; YOCHANAN 14:12"> Rav Sha'ul said in a kol gadol, Stand upright on your feet. And the man jumped up and was walking around.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ezek 2:1">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 8:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 12:22"> And the multitudes, when they saw what Rav Sha'ul did, shouted in the Lycaonian language, saying, The g-ds, having been made like anashim, have come down to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:35"> And they were calling Bar-Nabba “Zeus” and Rav Sha'ul “Hermes,” vi-bahlt (since) Rav Sha'ul was the main speaker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DANIEL 2:46; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:25"> And the priest of Zeus was outside the city and brought bulls and flower wreaths to the gates; he and the multitudes wanted to offer pagan korbanot (sacrifices).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 26:65; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:5–6; MELACHIM BAIS 5:7"> But having heard this, the Moshiachʼs Shlichim Bar-Nabba and Rav Sha'ul tore their kaftans and rushed out into the crowd, crying out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:26; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:4; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:17"> Anashim, why are you doing these things? We also are of the same nature as you, mere men, preaching to you to turn in teshuva from these worthless things to the Elohim Chayyim who made HASHOMAYIM V'ES HAARETZ V'ES HAYAM and all the things in them;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1Sm 12:21; Gn 1:1; Ps 146:6; Ex 20:11">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:30; TEHILLIM 81:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:28"> In the dorot having passed, Hashem allowed all the Goyim to go their own way.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ps 81:12; Mic 4:5">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:19–20; YOEL 2:23; IYOV 5:10"> And yet Hashem did not leave himself without an eidus (witness) in doing hatovim to you, giving geshem from Shomayim and seasons of bearing p'ri, filling you with okhel and your levavot with simcha.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dt 11:14; Job 5:10; Ps 65:10; 4:7; 147:20">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 44:16–17; BERESHIS 19:9; BERESHIS 11:6"> And even saying these things, it was with difficulty that Moshiachʼs Shlichim restrained the multitudes from offering pagan korbanot to them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Am 9:11TARGUM HASHIVIM; Jer 12:15; Isa 45:21">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:25; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:50–51"> Then &#91;disobedient&#93; Yehudim came from Pisidian Antioch and Iconium and, having won over the crowds and having stoned Rav Sha'ul, they were dragging him outside the city, thinking he was dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:9–10; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:6"> And after the Moshiachʼs talmidim had surrounded Rav Sha'ul, he got up and entered the city. And on the next day he went with Bar-Nabba to Derbe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:19; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:51"> And having preached the Besuras HaGeulah in that city and having made many talmidim, they returned to Lystra and to Iconium and to Pisidian Antioch,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:10; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:12; YOCHANAN 16:33"> Strengthening the nefashot (souls) of Moshiachʼs talmidim, encouraging them to remain in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Orthodox Jewish">g</a></sup>emunah (faith), and exhorting them that through tzarot rabbot it is necessary for us to enter into the Malchut Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 1:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:30; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 5:14"> And having chosen for messianic s'michah Zekenim to be installed in every one of Moshiachʼs Kehillot, and having davened with tzomot, the Moshiachʼs Shlichim commended them to Adoneinu in whom they had emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:13–14; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:38"> And having gone through Pisidia, they came to Pamphylia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And having spoken the dvar Hashem in Perga, they went down to Attalia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:40; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:19; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:12"> And from there, they sailed away to Syrian Antioch, their point of origin from which they had been commended to the Chen v'Chesed Hashem to the Messianic avodas kodesh which they had completed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:3"> Upon their arrival in Syrian Antioch, they assembled the Moshiachʼs Kehillah there, and were reporting what Hashem had done with them and how he had opened for the Nations a sha'ar of emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:35"> And they were spending not a little zman with the talmidim.</span>
