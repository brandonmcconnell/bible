<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:12–13; YOEL 2:32; TEHILLIM 86:5"> And it will be that everyone whoever calls upon the Name of Adonoi will be saved.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gn 4:26; 26:25; Ps 105:1; Yoel 3:1-5">d</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 4:32; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16–18; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:14–15"> And all the &#91;Jewish&#93; Ma'aminim HaMeshichiyim were together and they were having all things in common.</span>
