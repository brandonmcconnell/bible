<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DANIEL 9:3; YECHEZKEL 27:30–31; SHMUEL BAIS 13:19"> When Mordechai perceived all that was done, Mordechai rent his garments, and put on sackcloth with ashes, and went out into the midst of the city, and cried with a zeakah gedolah umarah (loud and a bitter wailing);</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And came even before the Sha'ar HaMelech; for none might enter into the Sha'ar HaMelech clothed with sackcloth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YESHAYAH 22:12; DANIEL 9:3; YESHAYAH 58:5"> And in every province, whithersoever the Devar HaMelech and his dat (decree) came, there was evel gadol (great mourning) among the Yehudim, and a tzom, and weeping, and wailing; and many lay on sackcloth and ashes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="TEHILLIM 77:2; YIRMEYAH 31:15; ESTHER 1:12"> So Esterʼs na'arot and her sarisim came and told it her. Then was HaMalkah exceedingly distressed; and she sent begadim (raiment) to clothe Mordechai, and to take away his sackcloth from him, but he received it not.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Am 6:6">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:15; ESTHER 1:10"> Then called Ester for Hatach, one of the sarisim of HaMelech, whom he had appointed to attend upon her, and gave him a commandment about Mordechai, to have da'as of mah zeh (what is this?), and al mah zeh (why is this?)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ESTHER 4:3; ESTHER 7:2; ESTHER 9:12"> So Hatach went forth to Mordechai unto the rechov haIr (open square of the city), which was before the Sha'ar HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ESTHER 3:2–15"> And Mordechai told him of all that had happened unto him, and of the parashat hakesef (exact amount of the money) that Haman had promised to pay to the genazim of HaMelech for the Yehudim, to destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="KOHELET 10:4; IYOV 9:15; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:13"> Also he gave him the copy of the edict of the dat (decree, Final Solution) that was published at Shushan to make them shmad, to show it unto Ester, and to declare it unto her, and to charge her that she should go in unto HaMelech, to make supplication unto him, and to make bakash (request, pleading) before him for her Am (People, the Jewish People).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Hatach came and told Ester the divrei Mordechai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Again Ester spoke unto Hatach, and gave him commandment unto Mordechai:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ESTHER 5:1–2; ESTHER 8:4; DANIEL 2:9"> All the avadim of HaMelech, and the people of the provinces of HaMelech have da'as that whosoever, whether ish or isha, shall come unto HaMelech into the pnimiyus khatzer (innermost courtyard), who is not called, there is echat dat (one law) of his to put him to death, except such to whom HaMelech shall hold out the sharvit hazahav (golden sceptre), that he may live. But I have not been called to come in unto HaMelech these shloshim yom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And they told to Mordechai Esterʼs words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MISHLE 24:10–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:30; YOCHANAN 12:25"> Then Mordechai commanded to answer Ester, Think not with thyself that thou shalt escape in the Beis HaMelech, more than kol HaYehudim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="BERESHIS 45:4–8; SHMUEL ALEF 12:22; DEVARIM 32:36"> For if thou altogether holdest thy peace at this time, then shall there revach (relief) and hatzalah (deliverance) arise to the Yehudim from another place; but thou and thy bais Avicha (house of thy father) shall be destroyed; and who knoweth whether thou art come to the Malkhut for such a time as this?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1C 9:16; Ro 1:16">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then Ester bade them return Mordechai this answer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:24; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:3; YOEL 2:12–17"> Go, gather together kol HaYehudim that are found in Shushan, and declare ye a tzom for me, and neither eat nor drink shloshet yamim, lailah vayom. I also and my na'arot will undergo a tzom likewise; and so will I go in unto HaMelech, though it is not according to the dat; and if I perish, I perish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> So Mordechai went his way, and did according to all that Ester had commanded him.</span>
</div>
</div>
