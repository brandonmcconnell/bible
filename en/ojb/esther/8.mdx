<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ESTHER 2:7; ESTHER 2:15; MISHLE 13:22"> On that day did HaMelech Achashverosh give the Bais Haman Tzorer HaYehudim (the estate of Haman the Vexer, Harasser, Enemy of the Jews) unto Ester HaMalkah. And Mordechai came before HaMelech; for Ester had told what he was unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ESTHER 3:10; BERESHIS 41:42; DANIEL 2:48"> And HaMelech took off his taba'at (signet ring), which he had reclaimed from Haman, and gave it unto Mordechai. And Ester set Mordechai over the Bais Haman.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HOSHEA 12:4; YESHAYAH 38:2; MELACHIM BAIS 4:27"> And Ester spoke yet again before HaMelech, and fell down at his feet, and she wept and made supplication to him to avert the rah of Haman HaAgagi and the machshevet of him (his scheme),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., final solution plan">a</a></sup>that he had devised against the Yehudim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ESTHER 5:2; ESTHER 4:11"> Then HaMelech held out the sharvit hazahav (golden sceptre) toward Ester. So Ester arose, and stood before HaMelech,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ESTHER 7:3; ESTHER 5:8; ESTHER 3:12–13"> And said, If it is tov to HaMelech, and if I have found chen (favor, grace) in his sight, and the thing seem kosher (fit, right, proper) before HaMelech, and I be tovah (pleasing) in his eyes, let it be written to revoke the seferim machashevet Haman (letters devised by Haman) ben Hamdata the Agagi, which he wrote to destroy the Yehudim which are in all the provinces of HaMelech;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ESTHER 7:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:2–3"> For how can I endure to see the ra'ah that shall come unto Ami (my people)? Or how can I endure to see the destruction of my moledet (kindred, Jewish kinsmen)?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ESTHER 7:10–1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:13; MISHLE 13:22"> Then HaMelech Achashverosh said unto Ester HaMalkah and to Mordechai HaYehudi, Hinei, I have given Ester the Bais Haman, and him they have hanged upon the etz, because he laid his hand against HaYehudim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ESTHER 1:19; ESTHER 3:12; ESTHER 8:5"> Write ye also in behalf of HaYehudim, as you please, b'shem HaMelech, and seal it with the taba'at (signet ring) of HaMelech; for the writing which is written b'shem HaMelech, and sealed with the taba'at (signet ring) of HaMelech may no man reverse.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ESTHER 1:22; ESTHER 1:1; MELACHIM BAIS 18:26"> Then were the soferim of HaMelech summoned at that time in the chodesh hashelishi, that is, the month Sivan, on the three and twentieth day thereof; and according to all that Mordechai ordered it was written unto HaYehudim, and to the satraps, and the governors and nobles of the provinces which are from India unto Ethiopia, an hundred twenty and seven provinces, unto every province according to the script thereof, and unto every people after their leshon, and to the Yehudim according to their script, and according to their leshon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YESHAYAH 60:6; YIRMEYAH 2:23; YESHAYAH 66:20"> And he wrote b'shem HaMelech Achashverosh, and sealed it with the taba'at of HaMelech, and sent seferim by couriers on horseback riding thoroughbred royal steeds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ESTHER 3:13; TEHILLIM 37:14–15; TEHILLIM 146:6–9"> Wherein HaMelech granted the Yehudim which were in every city to assemble themselves together, and to engage in self-defense, to make shmad, to slay and to annihilate, any chayil (armed force) of any people or province that would attack them, both little ones and nashim, and to plunder their property,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ESTHER 9:1; ESTHER 3:13–15; SHEMOT 15:9–10"> On yom echad in all the provinces of HaMelech Achashverosh, namely, upon the thirteenth day of the twelfth month, which is the month Adar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HISGALUS 6:10; ESTHER 3:14; SHOFETIM 16:28"> A copy of the edict was to be issued as dat (law) in every province and was galui (made known) unto kol ha'amim (all nationalities), and that the Yehudim should be atidim (ready, prepared),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See 3:14">b</a></sup>for yom hazeh to avenge themselves on their oyvim (enemies).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ESTHER 3:15; DANIEL 8:2; ESTHER 2:3"> So the couriers that rode upon royal steeds went out, being hastened and pressed on by the devar HaMelech. And the dat (decree) was given at Shushan the capital.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKAS 16:19; MATTITYAHU 6:29; ESTHER 3:15"> And Mordechai went out from the presence of HaMelech in levush malkhut (royal robe, clothing) of blue and white, and with an ateret zahav gedolah (a great crown of gold), and with a tachrich (garment, robe),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Mt 27:59">c</a></sup>of fine linen and purple; and the city of Shushan celebrated and rejoiced.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MISHLE 11:10; TEHILLIM 97:11; TEHILLIM 18:28"> The Yehudim had orah (light), and simcha, and sasson, and yekar (honor).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ESTHER 9:19; ZECHARYAH 8:20–23; SHMUEL ALEF 25:8"> And in every province, and in every city, whithersoever the devar HaMelech and his dat came, the Yehudim had simcha and sasson, a mishteh (feast) and a yontef. And many of the people of the land mityahadim (became Yehudim); for the pachad HaYehudim fell upon them.</span>
</div>
</div>
