<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DANIEL 6:18; DANIEL 2:1; ESTHER 2:23"> On that night HaMelechʼs sleep fled away, and he commanded to bring the sefer of records of the chronicles; and they were read before HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ESTHER 2:21–22"> And it was found written, that Mordechai had told of Bigtan and Teresh, two of the sarisim of HaMelech, the Mishomrei HaSaf (Keepers of the Door, the Doorkeepers) who conspired to assassinate HaMelech Achashverosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="KOHELET 9:15; BERESHIS 40:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 28:8–10"> And HaMelech said, What yekar (honor)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See 1:4,20">a</a></sup>and what recognition hath been done to Mordechai for this? Then said the servants of HaMelech that ministered unto him, Nothing has been done for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ESTHER 5:14; ESTHER 4:11; ESTHER 5:1"> And HaMelech said, Who is in the khatzer (court)? Now Haman was come into the Khatzer HaKhitzonah (Outer Court) of the Beis HaMelech, to speak unto HaMelech lit'lot (to hang)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Esth 2:23">b</a></sup>Mordechai on HaEtz that he had erected for him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="T.N. See Moshiach Ben Dovid hanging on a tree 2Sm 18:10as well as the specter of the same fate for this descendant of the first royal family of Israel">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the servants of HaMelech said unto him, Hinei, Haman standeth in the khatzer. And HaMelech said, Let him come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MISHLE 16:18; OVADYAH 1:3; YESHAYAH 62:4–5"> So Haman came in. And HaMelech said unto him, What shall be done unto the ish whom HaMelech delighteth to show yekar?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See 1:4,20; 6:3">d</a></sup>Now Haman thought in his lev, To whom would HaMelech delight to do yekar more than to myself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ESTHER 6:11; ESTHER 6:9"> And Haman answered HaMelech, For the ish HaMelech delighteth to show yekar (honor),</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:33; LUKAS 15:22; ESTHER 1:11"> Let the levush malkhut (royal robe, clothing) be brought which HaMelech has worn, and the sus (horse) that HaMelech rideth upon, and the keter malkhut (royal crown, crest) which is set upon its head,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 41:43; MELACHIM ALEF 1:33–34; ZECHARYAH 9:9"> And let this levush and sus be delivered to the hand of one of the most noble sarim (princes) of HaMelech, that they may array the ish therewith whom HaMelech delighteth to show yekar,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See 1:4,20; 6:3,6">e</a></sup>and bring him on horseback through the rechov of the ir, and proclaim before him, Thus shall it be done to the ish whom HaMelech delighteth to show yekar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HISGALUS 18:7; DANIEL 4:37; MELACHIM BAIS 10:10"> Then HaMelech said to Haman, Make haste, and take the levush and the sus, as thou hast said, and do even so to Mordechai HaYehudi, that sitteth at the Sha'ar HaMelech; leave nothing undone of all that thou hast spoken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKAS 1:52; ESTHER 9:3; HISGALUS 3:9"> Then took Haman the levush and the sus, and arrayed Mordechai, and brought him on horseback through the rechov of the ir, and proclaimed before him, Thus shall it be done unto the ish whom HaMelech delighteth to show yekar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 15:30; YIRMEYAH 14:3–4; IYOV 20:5"> And Mordechai came again to the Sha'ar HaMelech. But Haman hasted to his bais, having his head covered in evel (mourning).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See 4:3">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MISHLE 28:18; HOSHEA 14:9; BERESHIS 40:19"> And Haman told Zeresh his isha and all his ohavim everything that had befallen him. Then said his chachamim and Zeresh his isha unto him, If Mordechai be of the zera of the Yehudim, before whom thy downfall hast begun, thou shalt not prevail against him, but shalt nafol tipol (surely fall) before him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See Mt 27:19">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ESTHER 5:8; ESTHER 5:14; DEVARIM 32:35–36"> And while they were yet talking with him, came the sarisim of HaMelech, and hasted to bring Haman unto the mishteh (banquet) that Ester had prepared.</span>
</div>
