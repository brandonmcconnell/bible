<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DANIEL 6:14–18; ESTHER 7:10; ESTHER 1:12–21"> After these things, when the wrath of HaMelech Achashverosh was appeased, he remembered Vashti, and what she had done, and what was decreed against her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 1:2; BERESHIS 12:14; ESTHER 6:14"> Then said the attendants of HaMelech that ministered unto him, Let there be beautiful ne'arot betulot (young virgins) sought for HaMelech;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ESTHER 2:12–15; ESTHER 2:8–9; ESTHER 1:1–2"> And let HaMelech appoint pekidim (overseers) in all the provinces of his malkhut (kingdom, realm), that they may gather together all the beautiful na'arah betulah (young virgins) unto Shushan the capital, to the Bais HaNashim (House of the Women, Harem), unto the custody of Hegai the Saris HaMelech (the Eunuch of the King), the Shomer HaNashim (Custodian of the Women); and let their (bodily) rubbings be given them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 14:6; MATTITYAHU 20:16; ESTHER 1:21"> And let the na'arah which pleaseth HaMelech be Malkah (Queen) instead of Vashti. And the thing pleased HaMelech; and he did so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 16:5; SHMUEL ALEF 9:1; ESTHER 10:3"> Now in Shushan the capital there was an ish Yehudi, shmo Mordechai ben Yair ben Shime'i ben Kish, a Binyamini,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 24:14–15; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 36:20; YIRMEYAH 24:1"> which (ones) had been carried away from Yerushalayim in the Golus among those taken captive with Yechanyah Melech Yehudah, whom Nevukhadnetzar Melech Bavel had carried away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ESTHER 2:15; ESTHER 1:11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:18"> And he brought up Hadassah, that is, Ester, the bat of his dod (uncle); for she had neither av nor em, and the na'arah was lovely in form and beautiful in appearance, whom Mordechai, when her av and em were dead, took for the bat of his own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ESTHER 2:3; ESTHER 2:15"> So it came to pass, when the commandment of HaMelech and his dat (decree, law) was heard, and when ne'arot rabbot (many maidens) were gathered together unto Shushan the capital, to the custody of Hegai, that Ester was brought also unto the Beis HaMelech, to the custody of Hegai, Shomer HaNashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 39:21; MISHLE 16:7; ESTHER 2:12"> And the na'arah pleased him, and she took up chesed before him; and he speedily had her be given her (bodily) rubbings, with her portion of special food; and also he selected sheva hane'arot (seven maids) for her from the Beis HaMelech; and he transferred her and her ne'arot to the preferred quarters in the Bais HaNashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ESTHER 2:20; MATTITYAHU 10:16; ESTHER 3:8"> Ester had not revealed her Am (People),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., the Jewish people">a</a></sup>nor her moledet,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="kindred, i.e., her Jewishness">b</a></sup>for Mordechai had charged her she should not show it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:14; SHMUEL ALEF 17:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:36"> And Mordechai walked kol yom vayom before the khatzer Bais HaNashim (courtyard of the House of the Women, Harem) to have da'as of the shalom Ester (welfare of Ester), and what would become of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:4–5; MISHLE 7:17; YESHAYAH 57:9"> Now before the turn of every na'arah came to go in to HaMelech Achashverosh, she had to complete the yemei merukeihen (the days of her &#91;bodily&#93; rubbings) which were prescribed for the nashim and which involved shneym asar chodesh (twelve months), shisha chodashim (six months) of shemen (oil) of myrrh and shisha chodashim (six months) of perfumes and beauty treatments for nashim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then thus came every na'arah unto HaMelech; whatsoever she desired was given her to go with her out of the Bais HaNashim unto the Beis HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ESTHER 4:11; BERESHIS 34:19; YESHAYAH 62:4–5"> Ba'erev (in the evening) she went, and ba'boker (in the morning) she returned to a Bais HaNashim Sheni (a second House of the Women, Harem) to the custody of Sha'ashgaz, Saris HaMelech, Shomer HaPilagshim (Eunuch of the King, Custodian of the Concubines). She came in unto HaMelech no more, except HaMelech delighted in her, and she were summoned b'shem (by name).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHIR HASHIRIM 6:9; ESTHER 2:3; ESTHER 9:29"> Now when the turn of Ester bat Avichayil, dod Mordechai, who had adopted her as his bat, was come to go in unto HaMelech, she required nothing but what Hegai, Saris HaMelech, the Shomer HaNashim, suggested. And Ester took up chen (favor, grace) in the sight of all them that looked upon her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ESTHER 8:9; ESTHER 2:3; ESTHER 2:1"> So Ester was taken unto HaMelech Achashverosh into his Bais Malkhut (royal residence) in the chodesh ha'asiri (tenth month), which is the month Tevet, in the shnat sheva (seventh year) of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TEHILLIM 113:7–8; LUKAS 1:48–52; YECHEZKEL 17:24"> And HaMelech loved Ester mikol hanashim (more than all the women), and she took up chen vachesed (favor and grace) in his sight more than kol habetulot (all the virgins), so that he set the keter malkhut (royal crown) upon her head, and made her Malkah instead of Vashti.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="SHIR HASHIRIM 3:11; LUKAS 14:8; SHOFETIM 14:10–17"> Then HaMelech gave a mishteh gadol (great feast) unto all his sarim (nobles) and his avadim (servants, officials), even Mishteh Ester; and he decreed a holiday throughout the provinces and distributed gifts according to the &#91;bountiful&#93; yad HaMelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ESTHER 2:21; ESTHER 2:3–4; ESTHER 5:13"> And when the betulot (virgins) were gathered together again, then Mordechai yoshev b'Sha'ar HaMelech (sat in the Kingʼs Gate).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:1–3; ESTHER 2:10; ESTHER 2:7"> Ester had not yet revealed her moledet (kindred) nor her Am (people),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e., the Jewish people">c</a></sup>as Mordechai had charged her; for Ester did the commandment of Mordechai, like as when she was brought up with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ESTHER 6:2; MELACHIM ALEF 16:9; MELACHIM BAIS 21:23"> In those yamim, while Mordechai yoshev b'Sha'ar HaMelech, two of the Sarisim of HaMelech, Bigtan and Teresh, of the Mishomrei HaSaf (Keepers of the Door, Doorkeepers), were angry, and conspired to assassinate HaMelech Achashverosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ESTHER 6:1–2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:33; KOHELET 10:20"> And Mordechai came to da'as of the plot, and told it unto Ester HaMalkah; and Ester informed HaMelech thereof b'shem Mordechai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="BERESHIS 40:22; BERESHIS 40:19; ESTHER 10:2"> And when inquiry was made of the matter, it was found true; therefore the two of them were made talui al etz (hanged on a tree), and it was written in the sefer of the divrei hayamim before HaMelech.</span>
