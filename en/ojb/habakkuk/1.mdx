<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 39:5–9; YIRMEYAH 52:9–11; DEVARIM 5:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">They are terrible and dreadful;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their mishpat and their dignity shall proceed from themselves.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 4:13; TZEFANYAH 3:3; HOSHEA 8:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their susim also are swifter than the leopards,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">keener than the evening wolves;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their parash (cavalry) shall charge ahead,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their parash shall come from afar;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they shall fly as the nesher (eagle), swooping to devour.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 25:9; HOSHEA 13:15; HOSHEA 1:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">They shall come all for chamas;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the swarm of their faces is directed forward,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they shall gather captives as the sand.</span><br /></span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MISHLE 6:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">And makest adam as the dagim of the yam,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as the creeping things, that have no moshel over them?</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="CHABAKUK 1:11; DEVARIM 8:17; DANIEL 4:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore they make zevakhim (sacrifices) unto their net,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and burn incense unto their dragnet;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because by them their portion is sumptuous,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their food plenteous.</span><br /></span>
