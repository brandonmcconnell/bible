<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:53–56; BAMIDBAR 33:54; YECHEZKEL 48:23–29"> Now therefore divide HaAretz Hazot for a nachalah unto the nine Shevatim, and the half tribe of Menasheh,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 13:16; BAMIDBAR 21:30; YEHOSHUA 12:2"> From Aro'er, that is upon the rim of the Arnon Valley, and the town that is in the middle of the valley, and all the plain of Medeva unto Divon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:24–26"> And all the towns of Sichon Melech HaEmori, which reigned in Cheshbon, unto the border of the Bnei Ammon;</span>

</div>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 22:41; BAMIDBAR 32:38; BAMIDBAR 21:19"> Cheshbon, and all its towns that are in the plain; Divon, and Bamot-Ba'al, and Beit-Ba'al Me'on,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:23; YEHOSHUA 21:36–37; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:78–79"> And Yahtzah, and Kedemot, and Mepha'at,</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 12:3; DEVARIM 4:46; YECHEZKEL 25:9"> And Beit-Pe'or, and slopes of Pisgah, and Beit-HaYeshimot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 31:8; DEVARIM 3:10; DEVARIM 2:30–36"> And all the towns of the plain, and kol mamlechot Shichon Melech HaEmori, who reigned in Cheshbon, whom Moshe conquered with the nasiim of Midyan, Evi, and Rekem, and Tzur, and Chur, and Reva, which were vassal princes under Sichon, dwelling in HaAretz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 31:8; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:11; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15"> Also Balaam ben Be'or, the kosem (soothsayer, practicer of divination), did the Bnei Yisroel put to death with the cherev among them that were slain by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the boundary of the Bnei Reuven was the Yarden, and the bank thereof. This was the nachalat Bnei Reuven after their mishpekhot, the towns and villages thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 12:26; SHMUEL BAIS 11:1; BAMIDBAR 32:35"> Territory to them, Yazer, and all the towns of Gil'ad, and half the Eretz Bnei Ammon, as far as Aro'er that is near Rabbah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 17:27; SHMUEL BAIS 2:8; SHOFETIM 10:17"> And from Cheshbon unto Ramat HaMitzpeh, and Betonim; and from Machanayim unto the territory of Devir;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:36; BAMIDBAR 34:11; BERESHIS 33:17"> And in the valley, Beit-Haram, and Beit-Nimrah, and Sukkot, and Tzaphon, the rest of the Mamlechot Sichon Melech Cheshbon, the Yarden and its territory, even unto the end of the Yam Kinneret on the other side of the Yarden eastward.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> This is the nachalah of the Bnei Gad after their mishpekhot, the towns, and their villages.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 13:26; DEVARIM 3:13–15; BAMIDBAR 32:39–41"> Their territory was from Machanayim, all Bashan, kol Mamlechot Og Melech HaBashan, and all the towns of Yair, which are in Bashan, shishim ir;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 12:4; BAMIDBAR 32:39–40"> And half Gil'ad, and Ashtarot, and Edre'i, towns of the Mamlechot Og in Bashan, were pertaining unto the Bnei Machir ben Menasheh, even to the one half of the Bnei Machir by their mishpekhot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> These which Moshe did distribute for nachalah in the plains of Moav, on the other side of the Yarden, by Yericho, eastward.</span>
