<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 22:20; KOHELET 9:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:6–7"> But the Bnei Yisroel broke<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="covenant">a</a></sup>faith in regard to the Cherem; for Achan ben Carmi ben Zavdi ben Zerach of the tribe of Yehudah, took of the Cherem (that which was devoted to destruction); and the Af Hashem was kindled against the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 18:12; HOSHEA 4:15; SHMUEL ALEF 14:23"> And Yehoshua sent anashim from Yericho to Ai, which is near Beit-Aven, on the east of Beit-El, speaking unto them, saying, Go up and spy out HaAretz. And the anashim went up and spied out Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:11–12; LUKAS 13:24; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5"> And they returned to Yehoshua, and said unto him, Let not Kol HaAm go up; but let about two or three thousand Ish go up and attack Ai; and weary not kol HaAm there; for they are but few.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEVARIM 28:25; VAYIKRA 26:17; DEVARIM 32:30"> So there went up there of HaAm about three thousand Ish; and they fled before the anashim of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 2:11; YEHOSHUA 2:9; VAYIKRA 26:36"> And the anashim of Ai struck down of them about thirty and six ish; for they chased them from before the sha'ar even unto Shevarim, and struck them down on the descent; therefore the levav HaAm melted, and became like mayim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 37:29; BERESHIS 37:34; HISGALUS 18:19"> And Yehoshua tore his clothes, and fell to the ground upon his face before the Aron Hashem until erev, he and the Ziknei Yisroel, and they put dust upon their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:5; MELACHIM BAIS 3:10; MATTITYAHU 17:20"> And Yehoshua said, Alas, Adonoi Hashem, why hast Thou at all brought HaAm Hazeh over the Yarden, to deliver us into the yad HaEmori, to destroy us? If only we had been content, and dwelt on the other side of the Yarden!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:5–6; EZRA 9:10; CHABAKUK 2:1"> O Adonoi, what shall I say, when Yisroel turneth their backs before their enemies!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 32:12; TEHILLIM 83:4; YECHEZKEL 36:22–23"> For the Kena'ani and all the inhabitants of HaAretz shall hear of it, and shall surround us, and cut off Shemeinu (our name) from HaAretz; and what wilt Thou do unto Thy Shem Hagadol?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:22; SHEMOT 14:15; SHMUEL ALEF 16:1"> And Hashem said unto Yehoshua, Stand up; why liest thou thus upon thy face?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:1–2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:13; YEHOSHUA 6:17–19"> Yisroel hath sinned, and they have also violated My Brit which I commanded them; for they have even taken of the Cherem, and have also played the ganav, and lied also, and they have put it even among their own possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="TEHILLIM 5:4–5; YESHAYAH 59:2; YEHOSHUA 6:18"> Therefore the Bnei Yisroel could not stand before their enemies, but turned their backs before their enemies, because they are for Cherem; neither will I be with you any more, except ye destroy the Cherem from among you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EKHAH 3:40–41; YEHOSHUA 3:5; MATTITYAHU 7:5"> Up, set HaAm apart as kodesh, and say, Set yourselves apart as kodesh against tomorrow: for thus saith Hashem Elohei Yisroel, There is Cherem among you, O Yisroel; thou canst not stand before thine enemies, until ye take away the Cherem from among you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MISHLE 16:33; YONAH 1:7; YEHOSHUA 7:17–18"> In the boker therefore ye shall be brought according to your tribes; and it shall be, that the shevet (tribe) which Hashem taketh shall come near according to the mishpekhot thereof; and the mishpakhah which Hashem shall take shall come near by batim; and the bais which Hashem shall take shall come near man by man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="BERESHIS 34:7; SHMUEL ALEF 14:38–39; SHOFETIM 20:6"> And it shall be, that he that is taken with the Cherem shall be burned with eish, he and all that he hath; because he hath violated the Brit Hashem, and because he hath wrought shame in Yisroel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:60; YEHOSHUA 3:1; BERESHIS 22:3"> So Yehoshua rose up early in the boker, and brought Yisroel near by their tribes; and the Shevet Yehudah was taken;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 26:20; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:4–7; BERESHIS 38:30"> And he brought the Mishpakhat Yehudah; and the Mishpakhat Zarchi was taken; and he brought the Mishpakhat HaZarchi man by man; and Zavdi was taken;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 5:1–10; SHMUEL ALEF 14:42"> And he brought near his Bais man by man; and Achan ben Carmi ben Zavdi ben Zerach, of the tribe of Yehudah, was taken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 6:5; YIRMEYAH 13:16; YOCHANAN 9:24"> And Yehoshua said unto Achan, Beni (my son), give, now, kavod to Hashem Elohei Yisroel, and give to Him todah; and tell me now what thou hast done; hide it not from me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TEHILLIM 38:18; IYOV 33:27; BERESHIS 42:21"> And Achan answered Yehoshua, and said, Indeed I have sinned against Hashem Elohei Yisroel, and thus and thus have I done;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:15; TEHILLIM 119:37; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–16"> When I saw among the plunder a beautiful robe of Shinar (Babylon), and two hundred shekels of kesef, and a wedge of zahav of fifty shekels weight, then I coveted them, and took them; and, hinei, they are hid in ha'aretz in the midst of my ohel, and the kesef under it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> So Yehoshua sent malachim, and they ran unto the ohel; and, hinei, it was hid in his ohel, and the kesef under it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And they took them out of the midst of the ohel, and brought them unto Yehoshua, and unto all the Bnei Yisroel, and laid them out before Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MISHLE 15:27; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:9–10; YEHOSHUA 7:26"> And Yehoshua, and kol Yisroel with him, took Achan ben Zerach, and the kesef, and the robe, and the wedge of zahav, and his banim, and his banot, and his oxen, and his donkeys, and his tzon, and his ohel, and all that he had; and they brought them unto the Emek Achor (Valley of Trouble).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 6:18; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 2:7; VAYIKRA 24:14"> And Yehoshua said, Why hast thou troubled us? Hashem shall trouble thee yom hazeh. And kol Yisroel stoned him with even (stones), and burned them with eish, after they had stoned them with avanim (stones).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YESHAYAH 65:10; HOSHEA 2:15; DEVARIM 13:17"> And they raised over him a heap of avanim gadol unto this day. So Hashem turned from the fierceness of His Af (Anger). Therefore the shem of that place was called, Emek Achor, unto this day.</span>
</div>
