<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 6:21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:1; YEHOSHUA 8:2"> Now it came to pass, when Adoni-Tzedek Melech Yerushalayim had heard how Yehoshua had taken Ai, and had utterly destroyed it; just as he had done to Yericho and its melech, so he had done to Ai and its melech; and how the inhabitants of Giv'on had made terms of shalom with Yisroel, and were near them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 11:25; YEHOSHUA 2:24; SHEMOT 15:14–16"> That they feared greatly, because Giv'on was an ir gedolah, like one of the royal cities, and because it was larger than Ai, and all the men thereof were gibborim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 11:9; MICHOH 1:13; YEHOSHUA 10:1"> Therefore Adoni-Tzedek Melech Yerushalayim, sent unto Hoham Melech Chevron, and unto Piram Melech Yarmut, and unto Yaphia Melech Lakhish, and unto Devir Melech Eglon, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 9:15; YEHOSHUA 10:1; YESHAYAH 8:9–10"> Come up unto me, and help me, that we may attack Giv'on; for it hath made terms of shalom with Yehoshua and with the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YESHAYAH 8:9–10; BERESHIS 15:16; YEHOSHUA 10:6"> Therefore the five kings of HaEmori, the Melech Yerushalayim, the Melech Chevron, the Melech Yarmut, the Melech Lakhish, the Melech Eglon, gathered themselves together, and went up, they and all their machanot, and encamped before Giv'on, and made war against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 9:6; YEHOSHUA 5:10; DEVARIM 1:15"> And the anshei Giv'on sent unto Yehoshua to the machaneh at Gilgal, saying, Abandon not thy yad from thy avadim; come up to us quickly, save us, help us; for all the melachim of HaEmori that dwell in the har are gathered together against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 8:1; YESHAYAH 8:12; YESHAYAH 8:14"> So Yehoshua ascended from Gilgal, he, and kol Am HaMilchamah with him, and kol gibborei hachayil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:31; DEVARIM 3:2; TEHILLIM 27:1–2"> And Hashem said unto Yehoshua, Fear them not; for I have delivered them into thine yad; there shall not an ish of them stand before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="KOHELET 9:10; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:3; MISHLE 24:11–12"> Yehoshua therefore came unto them pitom, and went up from Gilgal kol halailah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 18:14; YEHOSHUA 16:3; YEHOSHUA 16:5"> And Hashem threw them into confusion before Yisroel, and struck them with a makkah gedolah at Giv'on, and pursued them along the derech that goeth up to Beit-Choron, and cut them down to Azekah, and to Makkedah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 28:2; SHOFETIM 5:20; TEHILLIM 18:12–14"> And it came to pass, as they fled from before Yisroel, and were in the going down to Beit-Choron, that Hashem cast down avanim gedolot from Shomayim upon them unto Azekah, and they died; they were more which died with avnei habarad (hail-stones) than they whom the Bnei Yisroel slaughtered with the cherev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="CHABAKUK 3:11; YESHAYAH 38:8; YESHAYAH 60:20"> Then spoke Yehoshua to Hashem in the day when Hashem delivered up HaEmori before the Bnei Yisroel, and he said in the sight of Yisroel,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Shemesh, stand thou still upon Giv'on;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and thou, Yarei'ach, in the Emek Ayalon.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="CHABAKUK 3:11; YESHAYAH 38:8; YEHOSHUA 10:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the shemesh stood still,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the yarei'ach stopped,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">until the Goy had avenged themselves upon their enemies.</span>Is not this written in the Sefer HaYasher? So the shemesh stood still in the middle of Shomayim, and hastened not to go down about a yom tamim (whole day).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEVARIM 1:30; SHEMOT 14:14; YEHOSHUA 10:42"> And there was no day like that before it or after it, that Hashem paid heed unto the voice of an ish; for Hashem fought for Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 10:43; YEHOSHUA 10:6"> And Yehoshua returned, and kol Yisroel with him, unto the machaneh at Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 24:8; AMOS 9:2; TEHILLIM 139:7–10"> But these five melachim fled, and hid themselves in a me'arah (cave) at Makkedah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And it was told Yehoshua, saying, The five melachim are found hiding in a me'arah at Makkedah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 27:66; IYOV 21:30; AMOS 9:1"> And Yehoshua said, Roll avanim gedolot upon the mouth of the me'arah, and post anashim by it to be shomer over them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="TEHILLIM 18:37–41; YIRMEYAH 8:14; YESHAYAH 9:14–15"> And stop ye not, but pursue after your enemies, and attack them; allow them not to enter into their towns; for Hashem Eloheichem hath delivered them into your yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 8:24; SHMUEL BAIS 20:6; YEHOSHUA 10:10"> And it came to pass, when Yehoshua and the Bnei Yisroel had made an end of striking them with a makkah gedolah me'od, until they were consumed, that the rest which remained of them entered into fortified cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 11:7; YESHAYAH 54:17; YESHAYAH 57:4"> And kol HaAm returned to the machaneh to Yehoshua at Makkedah in shalom; none moved his tongue against any of the Bnei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 15:32"> Then said Yehoshua, Open the mouth of the me'arah, and bring out those five melachim unto me out of the me'arah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 10:1; YEHOSHUA 10:5; YEHOSHUA 10:3"> And they did so, and brought forth those five melachim unto him out of the me'arah, Melech Yerushalayim, Melech Chevron, Melech Yarmut, Melech Lakhish, and Melech Eglon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MALACHI 4:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:20; HISGALUS 2:26–27"> And it came to pass, when they brought out those melachim unto Yehoshua, that Yehoshua called for kol Ish Yisroel, and said unto the Ketzinei Anshei HaMilchamah which went with him, Come near, put your feet upon the necks of these melachim. And they came near, and put their feet upon the necks of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:10; YEHOSHUA 1:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:10"> And Yehoshua said unto them, Fear not, nor be discouraged, be strong and of good courage; for thus shall Hashem do to all your enemies against whom ye fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 8:29; SHMUEL BAIS 21:9; MATTITYAHU 27:25"> And afterward Yehoshua struck them down, and slaughtered them, and hanged them on chamishah etzim; and they were hanging upon the etzim until the erev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 8:29; DEVARIM 21:22–23; YEHOSHUA 7:26"> And it came to pass at the time of the going down of the shemesh, that Yehoshua commanded, and they took them down off the etzim, cast them into the me'arah wherein they had been hiding, and laid avanim gedolot on the mouth of the me'arah, which remain until this very day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 6:21; DEVARIM 7:16; DEVARIM 20:16–17"> And that day Yehoshua took Makkedah, and struck it down with the edge of the cherev, and the Melech thereof he utterly destroyed, them, and every nefesh that was therein; he let none remain; and he did to the Melech of Makkedah as he did unto the Melech of Yericho.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Josh 6:21">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 21:13; YEHOSHUA 15:42; YIRMEYAH 52:1"> Then Yehoshua passed from Makkedah, and kol Yisroel with him, unto Livnah, and fought against Livnah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Hashem delivered it also, and the melech thereof, into the yad Yisroel; and he struck it down with the edge of the cherev, and kol hanefesh therein; he let none remain in it; but did unto the melech thereof as he did unto the melech of Yericho.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Josh 6:21">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 10:3; YEHOSHUA 12:11; YEHOSHUA 10:5"> And Yehoshua passed from Livnah, and kol Yisroel with him, unto Lakhish, and encamped against it, and fought against it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And Hashem delivered Lakhish into the yad Yisroel, which took it on the yom hasheni, and struck it down with the edge of the cherev, and kol hanefesh therein, according to all that he had done to Livnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 16:10; SHOFETIM 1:29; YEHOSHUA 16:3"> Then Horam Melech Gezer came up to help Lakhish; and Yehoshua struck him down and his army, until he had left him none remaining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 10:3; YEHOSHUA 12:12; YEHOSHUA 15:39"> And from Lakhish, Yehoshua passed unto Eglon, and kol Yisroel with him; and they encamped against it, and fought against it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="VAYIKRA 26:44; YEHOSHUA 10:37; IYOV 19:10"> And they took it on that day, and struck it down with the edge of the cherev, and kol hanefesh therein he utterly destroyed that day, according to all that he had done to Lakhish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="SHOFETIM 1:10; YEHOSHUA 15:13; YEHOSHUA 14:13–14"> And Yehoshua went up from Eglon, and kol Yisroel with him, unto Chevron; and they fought against it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And they took it, and struck it down with the edge of the cherev, and the melech thereof, and all the towns thereof, and kol hanefesh therein; he left none remaining, according to all that he had done to Eglon; but destroyed it utterly, and kol hanefesh therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:15; YEHOSHUA 12:13; YEHOSHUA 15:49"> And Yehoshua returned, and kol Yisroel with him, to Devir; and fought against it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="OVADYAH 1:18; YEHOSHUA 11:8; DEVARIM 3:3"> And he took it, and the melech thereof, and all the towns thereof; and they struck them down with the edge of the cherev, and utterly destroyed kol nefesh therein; he left none remaining; as he had done to Chevron, so he did to Devir, and to the melech thereof; as he had done also to Livnah, and to its melech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:2–16; SHEMOT 23:31–33; YEHOSHUA 8:27"> So Yehoshua subdued kol HaAretz HaHar, and of the Negev, and of the Shefelah, and of the asedot (slopes), and all their melachim; he left none remaining, but utterly destroyed kol haneshamah, as Hashem Elohei Yisroel commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 11:16; YEHOSHUA 15:51; DEVARIM 9:23"> And Yehoshua struck them down from Kadesh-Barnea even unto Azah (Gaza), and all the country of Goshen, even unto Giv'on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 10:14; TEHILLIM 46:7; TEHILLIM 80:3"> And all these melachim and their land did Yehoshua take at one time, because Hashem Elohei Yisroel fought for Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 10:15; YEHOSHUA 4:19; SHMUEL ALEF 11:14"> And Yehoshua returned, and kol Yisroel with him, unto the machaneh at Gilgal.</span>
