<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 11:10; TEHILLIM 2:1–4; SHOFETIM 4:2"> And it came to pass, when Yavin Melech Chatzor had heard those things, that he sent to Yovav Melech Madon, to the Melech Shimron, and to the Melech Akhshaph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 12:3; SHOFETIM 1:27; YEHOSHUA 17:11"> And to the melachim to the north, in the har, and the Aravah south of Kinnerot, and in the Shefelah, and in the regions of Dor on the west,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHOFETIM 3:3; YEHOSHUA 18:26; BERESHIS 31:49"> And to the Kena'ani on the east and on the west, and to Emori, and the Chitti, and the Perizzi, and the Yevusi in the har, and to the Chivi at the base of Chermon in Eretz HaMitzpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SHOFETIM 7:12; SHMUEL ALEF 13:5; BERESHIS 32:12"> And they went out, they and all their machanot with them, am rav, even as the sand that is upon the sea in multitude, with susim and chariots rav me'od.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TEHILLIM 118:10–12; HISGALUS 16:14; YESHAYAH 8:9"> And when all these melachim were met together, they came to encamp together at the waters of Merom, to fight against Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 10:8; SHMUEL BAIS 8:4; SHOFETIM 20:28"> And Hashem said unto Yehoshua, Be not afraid before them; for machar about this time will I deliver them up all slain before Yisroel; thou shalt lame their susim, and burn their merkevot with eish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 10:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:2–3"> So Yehoshua, kol Am HaMilchamah with him, came against them by the waters of Merom pitom (suddenly); and they fell upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 13:6; YEHOSHUA 19:28; ZECHARYAH 9:2"> And Hashem delivered them into the yad Yisroel, who struck them down, and pursued them unto Tzidon Rabbah, and unto Misrephot-Mayim, and unto the Valley of Mitzpeh eastward; and they struck them down, until they left them none sarid (remaining).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 11:6; YECHEZKEL 39:9–10"> And Yehoshua did unto them just as Hashem directed him; he lamed their susim, and burned their merkevot with eish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 11:1; SHOFETIM 4:2"> And Yehoshua at that time turned back, and took Chatzor, and struck down the melech thereof with the cherev; for Chatzor formerly had been the rosh kol hamamlachot haelleh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 10:40; DEVARIM 20:16"> And they struck down kol hanefesh that were therein with the edge of the cherev, utterly destroying them; there was not left kol neshamah; and with eish he burned Chatzor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEVARIM 20:16–17; DEVARIM 7:2; BAMIDBAR 33:50–53"> And all the towns of those melachim, and all the melachim of them, did Yehoshua take, and struck them down with the edge of the cherev, and he utterly destroyed them, just as Moshe Eved Hashem commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 30:18"> But as for the towns that stood on their tel (mound, old site), Yisroel burned none of them, except Chatzor only; that did Yehoshua burn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEVARIM 20:14; BAMIDBAR 31:9; YEHOSHUA 8:27"> And all the plunder of these towns, and the behemah, the Bnei Yisroel carried off for themselves; but kol haAdam they struck down with the edge of the cherev, until they had destroyed them, neither left they kol neshamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 1:7; SHEMOT 34:11–13; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:20"> Just as Hashem commanded Moshe His Eved, so did Moshe command Yehoshua, and so did Yehoshua; he left nothing undone of all that Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 10:41; YEHOSHUA 11:21; YEHOSHUA 12:8"> So Yehoshua took kol HaAretz hazot, the har, and kol haNegev, and kol Eretz HaGoshen, and the Shefelah, and the Aravah, and the har Yisroel, and Shefelah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:24; YEHOSHUA 11:3; YEHOSHUA 13:5"> Even from the Har HeChalak, that goeth up to Se'ir, even unto Ba'al-Gad in the valley of the Levanon under Mt Chermon; and all their melachim he took, and struck them down, and slaughtered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 14:7–10; YEHOSHUA 11:23"> Yehoshua made milchamah yamim rabbim with all those melachim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 9:3–27"> There was not an ir that made terms of shalom with the Bnei Yisroel, except Chivi the inhabitants of Giv'on; all others they took in milchamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:18; DEVARIM 20:16–17; SHEMOT 4:21"> For it was of Hashem to harden their hearts, that they should come against Yisroel in milchamah, that he might destroy them utterly, that there might be to them no techinnah (mercy plea), but that he might destroy them, just as Hashem commanded Moshe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEVARIM 9:2; BAMIDBAR 13:22–23; YEHOSHUA 15:13–14"> And at that time came Yehoshua, and cut off the Anakim from the har, from Chevron, from Devir, from Anav, from kol har Yehudah, from kol har Yisroel; Yehoshua destroyed them utterly with their towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 17:4; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 18:1; YEHOSHUA 15:46"> There were none of the Anakim left in the Eretz Bnei Yisroel; only in Azah (Gaza), in Gat, and in Ashdod, there remained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 21:44–45; BAMIDBAR 26:52–55; YEHOSHUA 14:1–15"> So Yehoshua took Kol HaAretz, according to all that Hashem said unto Moshe; and Yehoshua gave it for a nachalah unto Yisroel according to their divisions by their shevatim. And HaAretz rested from milchamah.</span>
