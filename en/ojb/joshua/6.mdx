<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 2:24; YEHOSHUA 2:9–14; TEHILLIM 127:1"> Now Yericho was closely shut up because of the Bnei Yisroel; none went out, and none came in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:24; YEHOSHUA 8:1; YEHOSHUA 2:24"> And Hashem said unto Yehoshua, See, I have given into thine yad Yericho, and the melech thereof, and the gibborei hechayil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:7; BAMIDBAR 14:9; YEHOSHUA 6:7"> And ye shall march around the Ir, all ye anshei hamilchamah, and go around the Ir once. Thus shalt thou do sheshet yamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:17; HISGALUS 16:1; MELACHIM ALEF 18:43"> And shivah Kohanim shall bear before the Aron shivah shoferot hayovelim; and the Yom HaShevi'i ye shall march around the Ir sheva times, and the Kohanim shall sound the shoferot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:4–5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:30; SHEMOT 19:19"> And it shall come to pass, that when they make a long blast with the keren hayovel, when ye hear the sound of the shofar, Kol HaAm shall shout with a teruah gedolah; and the Chomat HaIr shall fall down, and HaAm shall go up every ish charging straight in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:1; DEVARIM 20:2–4; YEHOSHUA 3:3"> And Yehoshua ben Nun called the Kohanim, and said unto them, Take up the Aron HaBrit, and let shivah Kohanim bear shivah shoferot yovelim (seven ram-shofars) before the Aron Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 4:13; YEHOSHUA 6:3; YEHOSHUA 1:14"> And he ordered HaAm, Pass on, and march on the Ir, and let him that is armed pass on ahead of the Aron Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:20; YEHOSHUA 6:3–4"> And it came to pass, when Yehoshua had spoken unto HaAm, that the shivah Kohanim bearing the shivah shoferot hayovelim passed on before Hashem, and sounded the shoferot; and the Aron Brit Hashem followed after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 6:13; BAMIDBAR 10:25; YESHAYAH 58:8"> And the armed men marched ahead of the Kohanim that blew with the shoferot, and the rear guard came after the Aron, with the shoferot of the Kohanim going on sounding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKAS 24:49; MATTITYAHU 12:19; YESHAYAH 42:2"> Yehoshua had commanded HaAm, saying, Ye shall not shout, nor make any noise with your voice, neither shall any davar proceed out of your mouth, until the Yom I tell you to shout; then shall ye shout.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> So the Aron Hashem circled the Ir, going about it once; and they returned to the machaneh, and spent the night in the machaneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 3:1; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:7–8; BERESHIS 22:3"> And Yehoshua rose early in the boker, and the Kohanim took up the Aron Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:9; MATTITYAHU 24:13; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 15:26"> And shivah kohanim bearing shivah shoferot hayovelim before the Aron Hashem went on continually, and sounded the shoferot; and the armed men went before them; with the rearguard after the Aron Hashem, the Kohanim going on, and sounding the shoferot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 6:15; YEHOSHUA 6:11; YEHOSHUA 6:3"> And the Yom HaSheni they circled the Ir once, and returned to the machaneh; so they did sheshet yamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:147; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:19; MATTITYAHU 28:1"> And it came to pass on the Yom HaShevi'i, that they got up early about shachar, circled the Ir after the same manner sheva times; only on that Yom they marched around the Ir sheva pe'amim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 13:15; YEHOSHUA 6:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:22–23"> And it came to pass on the Shevi'it, when the Kohanim sounded the shoferot, Yehoshua said unto HaAm, Shout; for Hashem hath given you the Ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEVARIM 20:17; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:25; MICHOH 4:13"> And the Ir shall be cherem, even it, and all that are therein, to Hashem; only Rachav the zonah shall live, she and all that are with her in the bais, because she hid the malachim that we sent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:11; YEHOSHUA 7:1"> And ye, be shomer to keep yourselves from the cherem, lest ye make yourselves cherem, when ye take of the cherem, and make the Machaneh Yisroel cherem, and trouble it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 26:20; MATTITYAHU 27:6; MARKOS 12:41"> But all the kesef, zahav, and vessels of nechoshet and barzel, are kodesh unto Hashem; they shall come into the Otzar Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:30; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:4–5; YEHOSHUA 6:5"> So HaAm shouted when the Kohanim sounded the shoferot; and it came to pass, when HaAm heard the kol (sound) of the shofar, and HaAm shouted with a teruah gedolah, that the chomah fell down, so that HaAm went up into the Ir, every ish straight before him, and they took the Ir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="TEHILLIM 137:8–9; YIRMEYAH 48:18; YEHOSHUA 11:14"> And they utterly destroyed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="devoted as cherem">a</a></sup>all that was in the Ir, both ish and isha, na'ar and zaken, and shor, and seh, and chamor, with the edge of the cherev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:31; YEHOSHUA 6:17; YECHEZKEL 17:18–19"> But Yehoshua had said unto the two anashim that had spied out HaAretz, Go into the bais haisha hazonah, and bring out from there the isha, and all that she hath, just as ye swore unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:7; BERESHIS 18:24; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:24"> And hane'arim (the young men) that were spies went in, and brought out Rachav, and her av, and her em, and her achim, and all that she had; and they brought out all her mishpechot, and put them outside the Machaneh Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 8:28; HISGALUS 17:16; MELACHIM BAIS 25:9"> And they burned the Ir with eish, and all that was therein; only the kesef, and the zahav, and the vessels of nechoshet and of barzel (iron), they put into the Otzar Bais Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:31; MATTITYAHU 1:5; SHOFETIM 1:24–25"> And Yehoshua saved Rachav the zonah alive, and her Bais Avi, and all that she had; and she dwelleth in Yisroel even unto this day; because she hid the malachim, which Yehoshua sent to spy out Yericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 16:34; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:13; BAMIDBAR 5:19–21"> And Yehoshua made them take this shevu'ah, saying, Arur (cursed) be the ish before Hashem, that riseth up and rebuildeth this Ir Yericho; he shall lay the foundation thereof with his bechor, and with his tza'ir (youngest son) shall he set up the delatot (gates) thereof.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See fulfillment 1Kgs 16:34">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 9:3; YEHOSHUA 1:5; MATTITYAHU 28:20"> So Hashem was with Yehoshua; and his fame was noised throughout kol HaAretz.</span>
