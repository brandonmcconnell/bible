<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 2:1; MARKOS 1:35; BAMIDBAR 25:1"> And Yehoshua rose early in the boker; and they set out from Sheetim, and came to the Yarden, he and Kol Bnei Yisroel, and camped there before they crossed over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 1:10–11"> And it came to pass after shloshet yamim, that the Shoterim went through the Machaneh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEVARIM 31:9; SHMUEL BAIS 6:13; DEVARIM 31:25"> And they commanded HaAm, saying, When ye see the Aron Brit Hashem Eloheichem, and the Kohanim who are Levi'im bearing it, then ye shall move out from your makom, and follow after it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="TEHILLIM 89:7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:28–29; SHEMOT 3:5"> Yet there shall be a rachok (distance, space) between you and it, about two thousand cubits by measure; come not near unto it, that ye have da'as of the Derech by which ye must go; for ye have not passed this way heretofore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 7:13; VAYIKRA 20:7; YOEL 2:16"> And Yehoshua said unto HaAm, Set yourselves apart as kodesh; for tomorrow Hashem will do nifla'ot among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 14:2–3; BAMIDBAR 14:15; MICHOH 2:13"> And Yehoshua spoke unto the Kohanim, saying, Take up the Aron HaBrit, and cross over before HaAm. And they took up the Aron HaBrit, and went before HaAm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 4:14; YEHOSHUA 1:5; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 29:25"> And Hashem said unto Yehoshua, This day will I begin to magnify thee in the sight of kol Yisroel, that they may know that, as I was with Moshe, so I will be with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 3:3; YEHOSHUA 3:17; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 29:15"> And thou shalt command the Kohanim that bear the Aron HaBrit, saying, When ye are come to the brink of the water of the Yarden, ye shall stand still in the Yarden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:1; DEVARIM 12:8"> And Yehoshua said unto the Bnei Yisroel, Come here, and hear the Divrei Hashem Eloheichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:1; SHEMOT 33:2; TEHILLIM 44:2–3"> And Yehoshua said, Hereby ye shall have da'as that the El Chai is among you, and that He will without fail drive out from before you the Kena'ani, and the Chitti, and the Chivi, and the Perizzi, and the Girgashi, and HaEmori, and the Yevusi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ZECHARYAH 6:5; ZECHARYAH 4:14; TEHILLIM 24:1"> Hinei, the Aron HaBrit Adon Kol HaAretz passeth over before you into the Yarden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 4:2; YEHOSHUA 4:9; YEHOSHUA 4:4"> Now therefore take you Shnei Asar Ish out of the Shivtei Yisroel, out of every tribe an ish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SHEMOT 15:8; TEHILLIM 78:13; YEHOSHUA 3:15–16"> And it shall come to pass, as soon as the soles of the feet of the Kohanim bearing the Aron Hashem, Adon Kol HaAretz, shall rest in the waters of the Yarden, that the waters of the Yarden shall be cut off from the waters that flow down from above; and they shall stand in a heap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:44–45; YEHOSHUA 6:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:24–25"> So it came to pass, when HaAm moved out from their ohalim, to cross over the Yarden, and the Kohanim bearing the Aron HaBrit ahead of HaAm;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:15; YEHOSHUA 4:18; YEHOSHUA 3:13"> And as they bearing the Aron were come unto the Yarden, and the feet of the Kohanim bearing the Aron touched the edge of the mayim, (for the Yarden overfloweth all its banks all the days of katzir &#91;harvest&#93;),</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 3:13; MELACHIM ALEF 4:12; MELACHIM ALEF 7:46"> That the waters which flowed down from above stood and rose up in a heap very far away at a town called Adam in the vicinity of Tzartan; and that which flowed down toward Yam HaAravah, the Yam HaMelach was completely cut off; and HaAm crossed over right by Yericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHEMOT 14:22; SHEMOT 14:29; TEHILLIM 66:6"> And the Kohanim bearing the Aron Brit Hashem stood firm on dry ground in the middle of the Yarden, and Kol Yisroel passed over on dry ground, until Kol HaGoy were passed completely over the Yarden.</span>
