<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 18:13; BERESHIS 28:19; SHMUEL BAIS 16:16"> And goeth out from Beit-El to Luz, and passeth along unto the territory of Arki to Atarot,</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 17:7; YEHOSHUA 18:1"> And the boundary ran toward the Yam to Michmetat on the north side; and the boundary ran eastward unto Ta'anat Shiloh, and passed by it on the east to Yanoach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 7:28; YEHOSHUA 6:26; YEHOSHUA 3:16"> And it ran down from Yanoach to Atarot, and to Na'arah, and came to Yericho, and ran to an end at the Yarden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 17:8–9; YEHOSHUA 16:3–6; YEHOSHUA 12:17"> The boundary ran from Tapuach westward unto the Wadi Kanah; and the end thereof ran to the Yam. This is the nachalah of the tribe of the Bnei Ephrayim by their mishpekhot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 17:9"> And the towns set aside for the Bnei Ephrayim were inside the Bnei Menasheh, all the towns with their villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SHOFETIM 1:29; DEVARIM 7:1–2; MELACHIM ALEF 9:16"> And they drove not out the Kena'ani that dwelt in Gezer: but the Kena'ani dwell among the Ephrayim unto yom hazeh, and serve under forced labor.</span>
