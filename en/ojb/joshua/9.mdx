<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 34:6; SHEMOT 3:17; SHEMOT 23:23"> And it came to pass, when kol hamelachim which were beyond the Yarden, in the har, and in the Shefelah, and in all the coasts of the Yam Hagadol<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., the Mediterranean">a</a></sup>over against the Levanon, the Chitti, and HaEmori, the Kena'ani, the Perizzi, the Chivi, and the Yevusi, heard thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HISGALUS 16:14; YOEL 3:9–13; TEHILLIM 2:1–2"> That they gathered themselves together, to make war with Yehoshua and with Yisroel, with one peh (mouth).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., one accord">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 10:2; YEHOSHUA 9:17; SHMUEL BAIS 21:1–2"> And when the inhabitants of Giv'on heard what Yehoshua had done unto Yericho and to Ai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKAS 16:8; MATTITYAHU 10:16; MARKOS 2:22"> They did work in a cunning, crafty manner,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Gn 3:1">c</a></sup>and went and made as if they had been ambassadors, and took old sakkim (sacks) upon their donkeys, and skins of yayin, old, and cracked, and mended;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKAS 15:22; DEVARIM 33:25; DEVARIM 29:5"> And sandals old and patched upon their feet, and selamot ballot (old garments) upon them; and all the lechem of their food supply was dry and moldy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 5:10; MELACHIM BAIS 20:14; YEHOSHUA 9:9"> And they went to Yehoshua unto the machaneh at Gilgal, and said unto him, and to the Ish Yisroel, We are come from an eretz rechokah (far country); now therefore cut ye a brit with us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 11:19; SHOFETIM 2:2; SHEMOT 34:12"> And the Ish Yisroel said unto the Chivi (Hivites), Perhaps ye dwell among us; how then shall we cut a brit with you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEVARIM 20:11; MELACHIM BAIS 10:5; YEHOSHUA 9:11"> And they said unto Yehoshua, We are thy avadim. And Yehoshua said unto them, Who are ye? And from where do ye come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEVARIM 20:15; YEHOSHUA 9:24; YESHAYAH 55:5"> And they said unto him, From an eretz rechokah me'od thy avadim are come because of the Shem of Hashem Eloheicha; for we have heard the report of Him, and all that He did in Mitzrayim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:24–35; DEVARIM 1:4; YEHOSHUA 12:4"> And all that He did to the two melachim of HaEmori, that were beyond the Yarden, to Sichon Melech Cheshbon, and to Og Melech HaBashan, which was at Ashtarot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 9:8; ESTHER 8:17; YEHOSHUA 1:11"> Therefore Zekeinenu and all the inhabitants of Artzeinu spoke to us, saying, Take food supplies with you for the derech, and go to meet them, and say unto them, We are your avadim; therefore now cut ye a brit with us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 9:4–5"> This our lechem we packed cham (hot) for our provision out of our batim (houses) on the day we came forth to go unto you; but now, hinei, it is dry, and it is moldy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And these skins of yayin, which we filled, were chadashim; and, hinei, they are cracked; and these our garments and our sandals are become worn out by reason of the derech me'od.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MISHLE 3:5–6; BAMIDBAR 27:21; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 10:13–14"> And the anashim took of their food supplies, and asked not counsel at the pi Hashem (mouth of Hashem).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 21:2; DEVARIM 20:10–11; YEHOSHUA 11:19"> And Yehoshua made shalom with them, and cut a brit with them, to let them live; and the Nasi'im of the Edah so ratified by oath unto them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MISHLE 12:19"> And it came to pass at the end of shloshet yamim after they had cut a brit with them, that they heard that they were their kerovim (neighbors), and that they dwelt near them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZRA 2:25; YEHOSHUA 18:25–28; YEHOSHUA 18:14"> And the Bnei Yisroel journeyed, and came unto their towns on the yom hashelishi. Now their towns were Giv'on, and Kephirah, and Be'erot, and Kiryat-Ye'arim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="TEHILLIM 15:4; SHMUEL BAIS 21:7; KOHELET 5:6"> And the Bnei Yisroel did not strike them down, because the Nasi'im of the Edah had sworn unto them by Hashem Elohei Yisroel. And Kol HaEdah murmured against the Nasi'im.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="KOHELET 8:2; YEHOSHUA 9:20; KOHELET 9:2"> But all the Nasi'im said unto Kol HaEdah, We have sworn unto them by Hashem Elohei Yisroel; now therefore we may not touch them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MALACHI 3:5; MISHLE 20:25; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:10"> This we will do to them; we will even let them live, lest ketzef (wrath) be upon us, because of the shevu'ah (oath) which we swore unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEVARIM 29:11; YEHOSHUA 9:27; YEHOSHUA 9:23"> And the Nasi'im said unto them, Let them live; but let them be woodcutters and water carriers unto Kol HaEdah; as the Nasi'im had promised them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 9:6; YEHOSHUA 9:16; BERESHIS 3:13–14"> And Yehoshua called for them, and he spoke unto them, saying, Why have ye beguiled us, saying, Rechokim anachnu from you when ye dwell near us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 9:21; BERESHIS 9:25–26; YEHOSHUA 9:27"> Now therefore ye are arurim, and there shall none of you be yikaret (be cut off) from being eved, and woodcutters and water carriers for the Bais Elohai.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEVARIM 20:15–17; DEVARIM 7:1–2; SHEMOT 23:31–33"> And they answered Yehoshua, and said, Because it was certainly told thy avadim, how that Hashem Eloheicha commanded His Eved Moshe to give you kol HaAretz, and to destroy all the inhabitants of HaAretz from before you, therefore we were greatly afraid for nafshoteinu because of you, so we have done this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="BERESHIS 16:6; SHOFETIM 10:15; YIRMEYAH 26:14"> And now, behold, we are in thine yad; as it seemeth tov and yashar unto thee to do unto us, do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And so did he unto them, and saved them out of the yad of the Bnei Yisroel, that they slaughtered them not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEVARIM 12:5"> And Yehoshua made them that day woodcutters and water carriers for the Edah, and for the Mizbe'ach of Hashem, even unto this day, in the makom which He would choose.</span>
</div>
