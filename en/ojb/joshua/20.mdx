<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 7:10; YEHOSHUA 13:1–7; YEHOSHUA 6:2"> Hashem also spoke unto Yehoshua, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEVARIM 19:2–13; DEVARIM 4:41–43; BAMIDBAR 35:11–24"> Speak to the Bnei Yisroel, saying, Designate for you cities of refuge, whereof I spoke unto you by the yad Moshe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> That the slayer that killeth any nefesh unawares and unwittingly may flee there; and they shall be your refuge from the Go'el HaDahm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 38:7; RUTH 4:1–2; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:18"> And when he that doth flee unto one of those cities shall stand at the petach (entrance) of the sha'ar ha'ir, and shall declare his cause in the ears of the zekenim of that city, they shall admit him into the Ir unto them, and give him a makom, that he may dwell among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 35:12; BAMIDBAR 35:25"> And if the Go'el HaDahm pursue after him, then they shall not surrender the slayer up into his yad; because he struck his neighbor unwittingly, and not with malice against him beforetime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 35:12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:26; BAMIDBAR 35:24–25"> And he shall dwell in that city, until he stand before HaEdah L'Mishpat, and until the Mot of the HaKohen HaGadol that shall be in those yamim; then shall the slayer return, and come unto his own city, and unto his own bais, unto the Ir from whence he fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 21:11; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:76; LUKAS 1:39"> And they appointed Kedesh in the Galil in Har Naphtali and Shechem in Har Ephrayim, and Kiryat-Arba, which is Chevron, in Har Yehudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 6:78; YEHOSHUA 21:36; YEHOSHUA 21:27"> And on the other side of the Yarden by Yericho eastward, they assigned Betzer in the midbar upon the plain out of the tribe of Reuven, and Ramot in Gil'ad out of the tribe of Gad, and Golan in Bashan out of the tribe of Menasheh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 35:15; YEHOSHUA 20:4; YEHOSHUA 20:6"> These were the cities designated for kol Bnei Yisroel, and for the stranger that sojourneth among them, that whosoever killeth any nefesh unawares might flee there, and not die by the yad of the Go'el HaDahm, until he stood before HaEdah.</span>
