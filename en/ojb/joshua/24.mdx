<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 23:2; SHMUEL ALEF 10:19; BERESHIS 33:18–19"> And Yehoshua gathered Kol Shivtei Yisroel to Shechem, and called for the Ziknei Yisroel, and for their Rashim, and for their shofetim, and for their shoterim; and they presented themselves before HaElohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 24:15; YECHEZKEL 16:3; BERESHIS 31:30"> And Yehoshua said unto Kol HaAm, Thus saith Hashem Elohei Yisroel, Avoteichem dwelt on the other side of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Euphrates">a</a></sup>River long ago, even Terach, Avi Avraham, and Avi Nachor: and they served elohim acherim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 12:1–4; BERESHIS 21:2–3; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:2–3"> But I took Avichem Avraham from the other side of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Euphrates">b</a></sup>River, and led him through Kol Eretz Kena'an, and multiplied his zera, and gave him Yitzchak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="BERESHIS 36:8; DEVARIM 2:5; BERESHIS 25:24–26"> And I gave unto Yitzchak Ya'akov and Esav; and I gave unto Esav Mt Se'ir, to possess it; but Ya'akov and his banim went down into Mitzrayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SHEMOT 3:10; SHEMOT 7:1–12; TEHILLIM 105:26–36"> I sent Moshe also and Aharon, and I plagued Mitzrayim, according to that which I did among them; and afterward I brought you out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:36; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:29; YESHAYAH 63:12–13"> And I brought Avoteichem out of Mitzrayim; and ye came unto the Yam &#91;Suf&#93;; the Mitzrayim pursued after Avoteichem with chariots and parashim unto the Yam Suf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEVARIM 4:34; SHEMOT 14:10; SHEMOT 14:20"> And when they cried unto Hashem, He put darkness between you and the Mitzrayim, and brought the yam upon them, and covered them; and your eyes have seen what I have done in Mitzrayim; and ye dwelt in the midbar yamim rabbim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 21:21–35; TEHILLIM 135:10–11; NECHEMYAH 9:22"> And I brought you into the Eretz HaEmori, the ones dwelling on the other side of the Yarden; they fought against you; I gave them into your yad, that ye might possess their land; I destroyed them from before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHOFETIM 11:25; MICHOH 6:5; BAMIDBAR 22:2"> Then Balak ben Tzippor Melech Moav arose and warred against Yisroel, and sent and summoned Balaam ben Be'or to curse you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEVARIM 23:5; BAMIDBAR 24:5–10; YESHAYAH 54:17"> But I would not pay heed unto Balaam; therefore he made a brocha over you; so I saved you out of his yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 4:10–12; YEHOSHUA 3:14–17; TEHILLIM 114:5"> And you went over the Yarden, and came unto Yericho; and the Ba'alei Yericho fought against you, and HaEmori, and the Perizzi, and the Kena'ani, and the Chitti, and the Girgashi, the Chivi, and the Yevusi; and I gave them into your yad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEVARIM 7:20; SHEMOT 23:28; TEHILLIM 44:3–6"> And I sent the tzirah (hornet) before you, which drove them out ahead of you, even the two melachim of the Emori; but not with thy cherev, nor with thy keshet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEVARIM 6:10–12; DEVARIM 8:7; YEHOSHUA 21:45"> So I have given you an Eretz for which ye did not toil, and towns which ye built not, and ye dwell in them; kramim and zetim (olive groves) which ye eat but did not plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 12:24; DEVARIM 10:12; BERESHIS 35:2"> Now therefore fear Hashem, serve Him batamim (completely) and in emes; put away the elohim which Avoteichem served on the other side of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Euphrates">c</a></sup>River, and in Mitzrayim; and serve ye Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 18:21; YOCHANAN 6:67–68; RUTH 1:15–16"> And if it seem rah unto you to serve Hashem, choose you this day whom ye will serve; whether the elohim which Avoteichem served that were on the other side of the River, or the elohei HaEmori, in whose land ye dwell; but as for me and my bais, we will serve Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:38–39; SHMUEL ALEF 12:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:2"> And the people answered and said, Chalilah lanu (far be it from us) that we should forsake Hashem, to serve elohim acharim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHEMOT 19:4; YESHAYAH 63:7–14; YESHAYAH 46:4"> For Hashem Eloheinu, He it is that brought us up and Avoteinu out of Eretz Mitzrayim, from the bais avadim, and which did those haotot hagedolot in our sight, and was shomer over us in kol HaDerech in which we went, and among Kol HaAmim through whom we passed;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MICHOH 4:2; SHEMOT 10:2; TEHILLIM 116:16"> And Hashem drove out from before us Kol HaAmim, even HaEmori which dwelt in HaAretz; therefore will we also serve Hashem; for He is Eloheinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YESHAYAH 5:16; SHEMOT 23:21; TEHILLIM 99:9"> And Yehoshua said unto HaAm, Ye are not able to serve Hashem; for He is an Elohim Kedoshim; He is an El Kanno (jealous G-d); He will not forgive your paysha'im nor your chattot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:26–27; YECHEZKEL 18:24; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 28:9"> If ye forsake Hashem, and serve elohei nekhar (foreign g-ds), then He will turn and bring you rah, and consume you, after that He hath done you tov.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:19; YESHAYAH 44:5; DEVARIM 5:27–28"> And HaAm said unto Yehoshua, No; but we will serve Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="TEHILLIM 119:173; TEHILLIM 119:11; IYOV 15:6"> And Yehoshua said unto HaAm, Ye are edim against yourselves that ye have chosen for you Hashem, to serve Him. And they said, We are edim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:16–18; YEHOSHUA 24:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:28–29"> Now therefore put away, said he, the elohei hanechar which are among you, and yield your levav unto Hashem Elohei Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="SHEMOT 24:7; SHEMOT 24:3; DEVARIM 5:27–29"> And HaAm said unto Yehoshua, Hashem Eloheinu will we serve, and His voice will we obey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="NECHEMYAH 9:38; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 23:16; SHEMOT 15:25"> So Yehoshua made a Brit with HaAm that day, and set them a chok and a mishpat at Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHOFETIM 9:6; BERESHIS 35:4; YEHOSHUA 4:20–24"> And Yehoshua wrote these words in the Sefer Torah Elohim, and took an Even Gedolah, and set it up there under an oak, that was near the Mikdash Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKAS 19:40; YEHOSHUA 22:34; YEHOSHUA 22:27–28"> And Yehoshua said unto kol HaAm, Hinei, this Even (Stone) shall be as an edah (witness) unto us; for it hath heard all the words of Hashem which He spoke unto us; it shall be therefore as an edah (witness) unto you, lest ye are untrue to Eloheichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHOFETIM 2:6"> So Yehoshua sent HaAm away, every man unto his nachalah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SHOFETIM 2:8; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:7–8; DEVARIM 34:5"> And it came to pass after these things, that Yehoshua ben Nun Eved Hashem, died, being an hundred and ten years old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:50; SHOFETIM 2:9; SHMUEL BAIS 23:30"> And they buried him in the territory of his nachalah in Timnat-Serach, which is in har Ephrayim, north of Mt Ga'ash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="SHOFETIM 2:7; DEVARIM 31:13; DEVARIM 11:2"> And Yisroel served Hashem all the days of Yehoshua, and all the days of the Zekenim that outlived Yehoshua, and which had da'as of kol ma'asei Hashem, that He had done for Yisroel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="BERESHIS 50:25; BERESHIS 33:19; SHEMOT 13:19"> And the atzmot Yosef, which the Bnei Yisroel brought up out of Mitzrayim, they buried at Shechem, in a parcel of ground which Ya'akov bought of the Bnei Chamor Av Shechem for a hundred pieces of silver: and it became the nachalah of the Bnei Yosef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 22:13; SHEMOT 6:25; YESHAYAH 57:1–2"> And El'azar ben Aharon died; and they buried him on a hill that belonged to Pinchas his son, which was given him in har Ephrayim.</span>
