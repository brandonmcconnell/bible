<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 4:19–20; YEHOSHUA 3:13; YEHOSHUA 4:8"> And command ye them, saying, Take you from here out of the middle of the Yarden, out of the place where the feet of the Kohanim stood firm, twelve avanim (stones), and ye shall carry them over with you, and leave them in the malon (lodging place), where ye shall lodge halailah (tonight).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 4:2; MARKOS 3:14–19"> Then Yehoshua called the Shneym Asar Ish, whom he had prepared of the Bnei Yisroel, out of every tribe an Ish;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Yehoshua said unto them, Pass over before the Aron Hashem Eloheichem into the middle of the Yarden, and take you up every ish of you an even (stone) upon his shoulder, according unto the number of the Shivtei Bnei Yisroel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SHEMOT 13:14; YEHOSHUA 4:21; TEHILLIM 44:1"> That this may be an ot among you, that when your banim ask in time to come, saying, What mean ye by these avanim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SHEMOT 12:14; BAMIDBAR 16:40; SHEMOT 28:12"> Then ye shall answer them, That the waters of the Yarden were cut off from before the Aron Brit Hashem; when it passed over the Yarden, the waters of the Yarden were cut off; and these avanim shall be for a zikaron (memorial) unto the Bnei Yisroel ad olam (forever).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 4:2–5; YEHOSHUA 1:16–18; YEHOSHUA 4:20"> And the Bnei Yisroel did so just as Yehoshua commanded, and took up Shtei Esreh Avanim out of the middle of the Yarden, just as Hashem spoke unto Yehoshua, according to the number of the Shivtei Bnei Yisroel, and carried them over with them unto the malon where they lodged, and laid them down there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 7:12; SHOFETIM 1:26; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 5:9"> And Yehoshua set up Shteym Esreh Avanim in the middle of the Yarden, in the place where the feet of the Kohanim bearing the Aron HaBrit stood; and they are there unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 3:13; TEHILLIM 119:60; MISHLE 27:1"> For the Kohanim bearing the Aron stood in the middle of the Yarden, until everything was finished that Hashem commanded Yehoshua to speak unto HaAm, according to all that Moshe commanded Yehoshua; and HaAm hurried and crossed over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 3:8; YEHOSHUA 4:18; YEHOSHUA 3:17"> And it came to pass, when Kol HaAm had completely crossed over, that the Aron Hashem crossed over, and the Kohanim, ahead of HaAm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BAMIDBAR 32:20–32; YEHOSHUA 1:14"> And the Bnei Reuven, and the Bnei Gad, and the half-tribe of Menasheh, crossed over armed ahead of the Bnei Yisroel, just as Moshe directed them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:11; YIRMEYAH 39:5; YIRMEYAH 52:8"> About forty thousand, battle-ready, crossed over before Hashem for milchamah, to the plains of Yericho.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 3:7; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 30:12; MELACHIM ALEF 3:28"> On Yom Hahu Hashem magnified Yehoshua in the eyes of Kol Yisroel; and they feared him, just as they feared Moshe, all the days of his life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Hashem spoke unto Yehoshua, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HISGALUS 11:19; SHEMOT 25:16–22; YEHOSHUA 3:3–6"> Command the Kohanim bearing the Aron HaEdut, so that they come up out of the Yarden.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="BERESHIS 8:16–18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:35–39; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:23"> Yehoshua therefore commanded the Kohanim, saying, Come ye up out of the Yarden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 3:15; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 12:15; YEHOSHUA 3:13"> And it came to pass, when the Kohanim bearing the Aron Brit Hashem were come up out of the middle of the Yarden, and the soles of the feet of the Kohanim were set on dry land, that the waters of the Yarden returned unto their place, and overflowed all its banks, as they did before.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 5:9; MICHOH 6:5; SHEMOT 12:2–3"> And HaAm came up out of the Yarden on the tenth day of the chodesh harishon, and camped at Gilgal, by the eastern border of Yericho.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 4:3; YEHOSHUA 4:8"> And those twelve avanim, which they took out of the Yarden, did Yehoshua set in a pile at Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 4:6; TEHILLIM 145:4–7; TEHILLIM 105:2–5"> And he spoke unto the Bnei Yisroel, saying, When your banim shall ask their avot in time to come, saying, What mean these avanim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 3:17; YESHAYAH 51:10; YESHAYAH 44:27"> Then ye shall let your banim know, saying, Yisroel crossed over this Yarden on yabashah (dry land).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gn 1:9">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHEMOT 14:21; YESHAYAH 43:16; YESHAYAH 63:12–14"> For Hashem Eloheichem dried up the waters of the Yarden from before you, until ye were passed over, just as Hashem Eloheichem did to Yam Suf, which He dried up from before us, until we were crossed over;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="TEHILLIM 106:8; TEHILLIM 89:13; MELACHIM ALEF 8:42–43"> So that Kol Ammei HaAretz (all the peoples of the earth) might know the Yad Hashem, that it is chazakah; so that ye might fear Hashem Eloheichem kol hayamim (all the days, forever).</span>
</div>
