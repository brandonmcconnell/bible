<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 19:51; SHMUEL ALEF 1:3; YEHOSHUA 21:2"> And the kol Adat Bnei Yisroel assembled together at Shiloh, and set up the Ohel Mo'ed there. And HaAretz was subdued before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And there remained among the Bnei Yisroel shivah shevatim (seven tribes), which had not yet received their nachalah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SHOFETIM 18:9; MISHLE 13:4; MISHLE 10:4"> And Yehoshua said unto the Bnei Yisroel, How long are ye mitrapim (slack ones, cowardly) going to take to go in and possess HaAretz, which Hashem Elohei Avoteichem hath given you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 18:6; YEHOSHUA 4:2; YEHOSHUA 18:3"> Appoint from among you shloshah anashim for each tribe; and I will send them, and they shall rise, and go through and survey the land, and write down a description of it, according to the nachalah of each; and they shall come again to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 16:1–10; YEHOSHUA 19:1–9; YEHOSHUA 15:1–12"> And they shall divide it into shivah chalakim (seven portions); Yehudah shall abide in their territory on the south, and Bais Yosef shall abide in their territory on the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 14:2; YEHOSHUA 18:10; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:19"> Ye shall therefore write a description of HaAretz in shivah chalakim, and bring the description here to me, that I may cast goral for you here before Hashem Eloheinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 13:33; YEHOSHUA 13:8–31; BAMIDBAR 18:20"> But the Levi'im have no chelek among you; for the Kehunah Hashem is their nachalah; and Gad, and Reuven, and the half tribe of Menasheh, have received their nachalah beyond the Yarden on the east, which Moshe Eved Hashem gave them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 18:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:19; SHMUEL ALEF 14:41"> And the men arose, and went away; and Yehoshua charged them that went to map HaAretz, saying, Go and survey the land, and write a description of it, and come again to me, that I may here cast goral for you before Hashem in Shiloh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:19"> And the men went and passed through HaAretz, and described it by towns in shivah chalakim in a sefer, and came again to Yehoshua to the machaneh at Shiloh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:19; YEHOSHUA 18:6"> And Yehoshua cast goral for them in Shiloh before Hashem; and there Yehoshua divided HaAretz unto the Bnei Yisroel according to their portions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 16:1–10; YEHOSHUA 15:1–8; DEVARIM 10:1–22"> And the goral of the tribe of the Bnei Binyamin came up according to their mishpekhot; and the territory of their goral (allotment) came forth between the Bnei Yehudah and the Bnei Yosef.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 16:1; YEHOSHUA 7:2; YEHOSHUA 2:1"> And their boundary on the north side was from the Yarden; and the boundary went up along the side of Yericho on the north, and went up through the har westward; and ended at the midbar of Beit-Aven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="BERESHIS 28:19; YEHOSHUA 16:2–3; YEHOSHUA 10:11"> And the boundary went over from there toward Luz, to the side of Luz (that is, Beit-El) southward; and the boundary descended to Atrot Addar, near the har that lieth on the south side of the Lower Beit-Choron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:9; DIVREY HAYAMIM ALEF 13:5–6; SHMUEL ALEF 7:1–2"> And the boundary ran from there and extended around the west side southward, from the har that lieth before Beit-Choron southward; and ends at Kiryat Ba'al, which is Kiryat-Ye'arim, a town of the Bnei Yehudah; this was the west side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:9"> And the southern side was from the end of Kiryat-Ye'arim, and the boundary went out on the west, and went out to the ma'ayan (spring) of the waters of Nephtoach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MELACHIM BAIS 23:10; YEHOSHUA 15:7–8; YESHAYAH 30:33"> And the boundary came down to the foot of the har that lieth before the Ben Hinnom Valley, and which is in the Emek Repha'im on the north, and descended to the Hinnom Valley, to the south of the Yevusi, and descended to Ein-Rogel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:6"> And ran from the north, and went to Ein Shemesh, and went toward Gelilot, which is opposite the ascent of Adummim, and descended to the Even Bohan ben Reuven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:6; YEHOSHUA 15:61"> And passed along toward the slope opposite Aravah northward, and went down unto the Aravah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="BERESHIS 14:3; YEHOSHUA 15:2; BAMIDBAR 34:3"> And the boundary passed along to the slope of Beit-Choglah northward; and the end of the boundary was at the north bay of the Yam HaMelach (the Salt Sea, the Dead Sea) at the south end of the Yarden; this was the southern boundary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the Yarden was the boundary of it on the east side. This was the nachalah of the Bnei Binyamin, to its boundaries all around, according to their mishpekhot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 18:12; YEHOSHUA 6:1; YEHOSHUA 18:19"> Now the towns of the tribe of the Bnei Binyamin according to their mishpekhot were Yericho, and Beit-Choglah, and the valley of Ketzitz,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 15:6; MELACHIM ALEF 12:29–32; YEHOSHUA 18:18"> And Beit HaAravah, and Tzemarayim, and Beit-El,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="SHMUEL ALEF 13:17"> And Avim, and Parah, and Ophrah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZRA 2:26; YEHOSHUA 21:17; YESHAYAH 10:29"> And Kephar Haammonah, and Ophni, and Gaba; 12 towns with their villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 9:17; MELACHIM ALEF 3:4–5; YEHOSHUA 10:2"> Giv'on, and Ramah, and Be'erot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="SHOFETIM 10:17; EZRA 2:25; YEHOSHUA 9:17"> And Mitzpeh, and Kephirah, and Motzah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And Rekem, and Yirpe'el, and Taralah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 21:14; YEHOSHUA 15:8; SHOFETIM 20:4–5"> And Tzelah, Eleph, and Yevusi, which is Yerushalayim, Givat, and Kiryat; 14 towns with their villages. This is the nachalah of the Bnei Binyamin according to their mishpekhot.</span>
