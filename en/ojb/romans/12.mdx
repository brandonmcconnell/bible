<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:15–16"> I appeal to you, therefore, Achim b'Moshiach, through the rachamei Hashem(mercies of G-d), to present your geviyah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gn 47:18">a</a></sup>all of your being, as a korban chai (living sacrifice), kadosh (holy) and acceptable to Hashem,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gn 8:21">b</a></sup>which is your spiritual avodas kodesh service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:22–24; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:17"> Stop allowing yourself to be conformed to the Olam Hazeh, but be transformed by your hitkhadshut (regeneration, renewal) of your mind, so that you may ascertain what is the ratzon Hashem(the will of G-d), what is the good, acceptable, and perfect will of G-d.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:3–8; MISHLE 26:12"> For I say to all who are among you through the chesed (unmerited favor, grace) given to me, that you should avoid a false sense of superiority in your thinking; rather exercise shlitah atzmi (self-control), thinking with seichel, as G-d has measured to each a measure of emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:27; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:12–14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:15–16"> For just as in one body we have many members (natural capacities), and all the members do not have the same function,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:27–28"> So we all are one body in Moshiach, and are individually members one of another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10–11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:28–31"> Having matanot (gifts) which differ in accordance with the chesed (unmerited favor, grace) given to us. If we speak for G-d as nevi'im (prophets), it should be in proportion to the emunah given to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:11; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:1"> If we offer particular acts of avodas kodesh service, it should be used in serving. One who functions as a rabbinic moreh should be active in teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:7; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 5:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:28"> One who imparts chizzuk (strengthening, encouragement) should be active in that avodas kodesh ministry. One who makes tzedakah (contributions) should do so with nedivut (generosity). The one in manhigut (leadership), with diligence. The one in bikkur cholim (visiting the sick) and gemilut chasadim (benevolence), with simcha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 34:14; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:8; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:5"> Let ahavah (agape) be without tzevi'ut (hypocrisy). Hate what is haRah, be devoted to what is tov.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:3; YOCHANAN 13:34–35; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:1"> Show mishpochah (family) affection to one another in ahavah shel achvah (brotherly love). Be first in esteeming one another, in showing mutual respect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 6:10–11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:25; HISGALUS 3:15–16"> Be zealous without negligence; with bren (fervor, hitlahavut) be burning with the Ruach Hakodesh. With avodas kodesh serve Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:36; TEHILLIM 62:8"> Have simcha in tikvah. Have savlanut in tzoros. Keep davening tefillos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:16; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:12; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:17"> With a spirit of koinonia, keep the pushke full for the needs of the Kedoshim. Aspire to hachnosas orchim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; MATTITYAHU 5:44; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:15"> Say a bracha on those who bring redifah (persecution) on you, let it be a bracha and not a kelalah (curse).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:26; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:3; TEHILLIM 35:13–14"> Join in simcha with those who rejoice, weep with those who weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:8; MISHLE 3:7; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:13–17"> Think with a (spiritual) consensus in achdus harmony among yourselves. Do not cherish the thoughts of the ba'al gaavah (haughty person), but associate with the anaviyim (humble),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isa 29:19">c</a></sup>with the lowly am ha'aretz; do not be chachamim (wise ones) in your own estimation.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ro 1:22">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MISHLE 20:22; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:15; MATTITYAHU 5:39"> Repay no one ra'a (evil) for ra'a (evil). Take into consideration what is haTov in the sight of everyone and do that.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Prov 3:4TARGUM HASHIVIM">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:14; MATTITYAHU 5:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:19"> If possible, so far as it depends on you, live in shalom with everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:35; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:30; MISHLE 24:29"> Do not take your own revenge, beloved, but give opportunity for G-dʼs Charon Af (burning wrath), for it is written, LI NAKAM V'SHILEM (Vengeance is mine and recompense, repayment) — I am He who will repay, says Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Dt 32:35">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MISHLE 25:21–22; MATTITYAHU 5:44; LUKAS 6:27"> “But if your enemy is hungry, feed him; if he is thirsty, give him drink; for in so doing you will heap coals of fire on his head.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Prov 25:21-22">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; LUKAS 6:27–30; MISHLE 16:32"> Do not be overcome by what is ra'a, but overcome haRah with haTov.</span>
