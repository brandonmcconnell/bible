<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:1; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:7–13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:22"> Welcome the one who is weak in emunah (faith). But not for the purpose of setting him straight in arguments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:22–23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:14"> For example, one person has emunah (faith) to eat every potential food; but the weak practice vegetarianism.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:16–17; LUKAS 18:9"> Let the one who eats not hold in contempt or despise the one who does not eat, and let not the one who does not eat pass judgment on the one who eats, for Hashem treats him as an oreach ratzuy (welcome guest).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:11–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:31–39"> Who are you to condemn the eved (house slave) of someone else? In relation to Ribbono (shel Olam) he stands or falls. And he shall stand, for Ribbono (shel Olam) is able to make him stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:16–17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:9–10"> One person judges one day to be more important than another; another person judges every day to be alike. Let each be fully convinced in his own mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:30–31; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:3–5; MATTITYAHU 14:19"> The one who holds an opinion on the day does so to Hashem. And the one who eats does so to Hashem, for he does the bentshen (custom of saying grace after meals) of the Birkat Hamazon to Hashem. And the one who does not eat does so to Hashem and gives the hodayah (thanksgiving) to Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:2; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:19–20"> For no one of us lives for himself and no one dies for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:20; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:23"> For if we live, we live for Hashem; and if we die, we die for Hashem. So whether we live or we die, we belong to Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HISGALUS 1:18; LUKAS 24:26; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 12:2"> For it was for this tachlis (purpose) that Moshiach had his histalkus and came to live again, in order that he might have charge as Moshiach Adoneinu over both the Mesim (dead ones) and the Chayyim (living ones).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:10; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:5; KOHELET 12:14"> So you, why do you judge your Ach b'Moshiach? Or you, why do you despise your Ach b'Moshiach? For we shall all stand in the Bet Din (Court of Law) of Hashem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="see 2C 5:10">a</a></sup>before his Kisse Din (judgment seat), his Kisse Mishpat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 45:22–25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:10–11; TEHILLIM 72:11"> For it is written, “As I live, says Hashem, before Me KOL BERECH (every knee) will bow and KOL LASHON (every tongue) shall give praise to Hashem.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Isa 45:23">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:5; MATTITYAHU 12:36; MATTITYAHU 16:27"> So then each of us will give account of himself to Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:3; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:9–13"> Let us therefore no longer pass judgment on one another, but decide this rather: not to put an occasion for michshol (stumbling, offense, downfall)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ro 9:32-33">c</a></sup>in the way of the Ach b'Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 1:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:2; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:4"> I have da'as and am convinced in Adoneinu Yehoshua that nothing is tamei beetzem (intrinsically), except that to the one who reckons something profane, to that person it is profane.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:11–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:2–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:13"> For if your Ach B'Moshiach is deeply upset on account of &#91;your&#93; okhel (food), you are no longer conducting yourself in terms of ahavah. Do not by your okhel destroy that one for whom Moshiach died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:22; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:29–30"> Therefore, do not let HaTov of you be brought into contempt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 6:33; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:20"> For the Malchut Hashem is not a matter of eating and drinking, but of tzedek (righteousness),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Dan 9:24">d</a></sup>shalom (peace) and simcha b'Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:21; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:11; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:20"> For he who serves Moshiach in this is pleasing to Hashem and approved by people in general.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:18; TEHILLIM 34:14"> So then we pursue what makes for shalom and for the building up of one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:14–15; MATTITYAHU 18:6; LETTER TO TITOS 1:15"> Do not for the sake of okhel bring churban to the work of Hashem. All okhel is tahor but it is wrong to eat anything that causes nisyonot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:1–2"> It is a fine thing not to eat meat nor drink wine nor anything by which your Ach b'Moshiach stumbles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:5; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 3:13"> The emunah that you have, keep beshita (as a matter of conviction or principle) to yourself before G-d. Ashrey is the man who does not condemn himself by the things he approves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:6; LETTER TO TITOS 1:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:29–31"> But in the man who doubts, there is found in him a dvar ashmah (a thing of guilt, condemnation) if he eats, because it is not of emunah. And whatever is not of emunah is averah (sin).</span>
