<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:19; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:4–5"> Let each of us please his re'a with a view to what is beneficial, for upbuilding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TEHILLIM 69:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:8; YOCHANAN 4:34"> For even Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach did not please himself; but, as it is written, V'CHERPOT CHORPECHA NAF'LU ALAI (The reproaches of those who reproach You have fallen on me).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 69:9[10]">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:11; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:16–17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:23–24"> For as much as was written beforehand was written for our limudei kodesh, in order that through savlanut and through the nechamah of the Kitvei Hakodesh we might hold fast tikvah (hope).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:4–5; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:16"> May the G-d of savlanut and of nechamah give you to live in harmony among yourselves in accordance with Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:29–30; HISGALUS 1:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:3"> In order that with achdus of mind and voice you might give kavod to the Elohim and Avi of Adoneinu Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:40; YOCHANAN 13:34; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:2"> Therefore, treat each other as orchim ratzuy (welcome guests), as Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach welcomed you, to the kavod of Hashem (glory of G-d).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:4–5; MATTITYAHU 15:24; YOCHANAN 1:11"> For I declare that Moshiach has become Mesharet Bnei HaMilah (Servant, Minister of the Circumcised) for the sake of the Emes Hashem (the truth of G-d), to confirm the havtachot given to the Avot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHMUEL BAIS 22:50; TEHILLIM 18:49; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:29"> And in order that the Goyim might give praise to Hashem for His chaninah (mercy). As it is written, For this reason I will confess You among the Goyim and sing praises unto Your Name.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 18:49[50]">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 67:3–4; DEVARIM 32:43; YESHAYAH 42:10–12"> Furthermore it says, HARNINU GOYIM AMMO (Rejoice, Goyim with His people).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Dt 32:43">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TEHILLIM 117:1"> And again, HALELU ES ADONOI KOL GOYIM SHABBECHUHU KOL HAUMMIM (Baruch Hashem, Praise the L-rd, all you Goyim, and let all the peoples praise Him).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ps 117:1">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YESHAYAH 11:10; YESHAYAH 11:1; MATTITYAHU 12:21"> And again Yeshayah says, The SHORESH OF YISHAI (the Root of Jesse),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="i.e. Moshiach">f</a></sup>shall come forth, even the one who arises to rule the GOYIM; in him (the Gentiles, the nations) shall put their TIKVAH (hope).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 11:10; 42:4">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:16–17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:8"> May the Elohei HaTikvah (the G-d of hope) fill you with simcha and shalom in believing, that you may overflow in tikvah (hope), in the ko'ach (power) of the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:16; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:21"> Achim b'Moshiach of mine, I myself am convinced concerning you, that you yourselves too are full of yosher (rectitude), full of da'as, able also to admonish one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:3; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:5"> But I wrote to you rather bluntly in this iggeret hakodesh in part as a way of reminding you, by virtue of the chesed (unmerited favor, gift of grace) given me from Hashem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:1; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:15"> To be a mesharet (minister, servant) of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua to the Goyim, serving the Besuras HaGeulah of Hashem, administering with a kohenʼs avodas kodesh service the minchah offering to Hashem of the Goyim, that this offering might be acceptable, mekudash (set apart as holy) in the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:14–16; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:11–21; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:1"> Therefore I have this glorying in Moshiach Yehoshua in reference to what concerns G-d.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:19"> For I will not presume to say anything, except of what Moshiach has accomplished through me for the mishma'at of the peoples, by word and deed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:11–12"> By the ko'ach of otot u'moftim (signs and wonders), by the power of the Ruach Hakodesh; so that from Yerushalayim in a sweep round to Illyricum,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="todayʼs Yugoslavia and Albania">h</a></sup>I have preached the Besuras HaGeulah Hashem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:13–16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:9–15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:20–22"> Thus making it my hasagos (aspiration) to preach the Besuras HaGeulah where Moshiach has not been named, lest I build on anotherʼs yesod (foundation).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YESHAYAH 52:15; YESHAYAH 65:1"> But, as it is written, “Those who had not been told about Him will see, and those who had not heard shall understand.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Isa 52:15">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:17–18"> For this reason I have also regularly been prevented from coming to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:32; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:10–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:29"> But now, als (since) I no longer have scope in these regions and have had a tshuka (longing) to come to you for many years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 15:3"> When I travel to Spain…For I hope to see you as I pass through and to be sent on my way there by you, once I have had the full pleasure of being with you for a time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 20:22"> But now I am traveling to Yerushalayim in avodas kodesh service to the Kedoshim (Messianic Jews in Jerusalem).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:1–9; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 11:27–30; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:2"> For the kehillot of Moshiach (Messianic congregations) in Macedonia and Achaia (Greece) chose to make some tzedakah (contribution) for the aniyim (poor) among the Kedoshim in Yerushalayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:6; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:19"> For they chose to do so and owe them a choiv (debt), for if the non-Jews have received a share in their spiritual affairs, they ought to minister to the Messianic Jews in material affairs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:6"> When, therefore, I have completed this (collection journey avodas kodesh service) and sealed this p'ri (fruit) to them, I will go by way of you to Spain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:10–12; TEHILLIM 16:11"> And I have da'as that when I come to you, I will come in the fullness of the Birkat HaMoshiach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 4:12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:11"> I appeal to you, Achim b'Moshiach, through Adoneinu Moshiach Yehoshua and the ahavas HaRuach Hakodesh, to contend with me in your tefillos to Hashem on my behalf,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:2; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:4; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:1"> that I might be delivered from those without mishma'at in Yehudah and my avodas kodesh service to Yerushalayim might be acceptable to the Kedoshim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:18; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:20; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:7"> That I might come to you in simcha (joy) birtzon Hashem (in the will of G-d) and be mutually refreshed by your hitkhabrut (fellowship) in the Messianic Chavurah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:23"> V'Elohei HaShalom im kulechem. Omein. (May the G-d of peace be with you all. Amen.)</span>
