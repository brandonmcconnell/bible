<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:25–29; BERESHIS 25:32; KOHELET 6:11"> What then is the advantage of the Yehudi? Or what is the value of the milah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:4; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:38; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:17"> Much in every way! For, koidem kol (in the first place), they are entrusted with the Divrei Hashem (the oracles, the words of G-d).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:13; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 4:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:6"> So, where does that leave us? If some have disbelieved, has their lack of emunah annulled the ne'emanut (faithfulness, trustworthiness, reliability) of Hashem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="TEHILLIM 51:4; TEHILLIM 116:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:17"> Chas v'shalom (G-d forbid!) Let G-d be true and KOL HAADAM KOZEV (every man a liar).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ps 116:11">a</a></sup>Even as it is written, L'MA'AN TITZDAK B'DAVRECHA (“in order that you might be vindicated when you speak” and shall overcome when you judge).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 51:4[6]">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:18–20"> But if our unrighteousness brings out and highlights the Tzedek Olamim, the Tzidkat Hashem (the righteousness of G-d), what shall we say? Rhetorically speaking, is G-d unjust in inflicting Charon Af Hashem?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1:18">c</a></sup>(I speak from a human standpoint.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:25; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:31; IYOV 8:3"> Chas v'shalom! (G-d forbid!) For then how could Hashem be HaShofet kol ha'Aretz?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gn 18:25">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:19–20; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:27–29"> “But if the Emes Hashem (truth of G-d) has by my sheker overflowed to His kavod (glory), why am I still judged as a rashah (an evildoer)?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:20"> Why do we not say, as some speakers of lashon hora slanderously report us to say, “Let us do rah that tov might come of it”? The gezar din (verdict) of ashem (guilty) on them is well deserved.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:15; YESHAYAH 65:5"> What then? Are we (Yehudim) better off? Not altogether. For we have now charged both Yehudim and non-Jews as all alike under HaChet (sin),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="i.e., the power of Chet Kadmon Ro 7:23">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 53:1–3; TEHILLIM 14:1–3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:23"> As it is written, EIN TZADDIK BA'ARETZ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Koh 7:20">f</a></sup>(There is none on earth righteous), not even one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TEHILLIM 14:2–4; TEHILLIM 53:2; LETTER TO TITOS 3:3"> There is none who has binah, there is none who seeks out G-d.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="TEHILLIM 14:3; KOHELET 7:20; YESHAYAH 64:6"> All have turned aside, they have become altogether paskudneh, worthless; there is none who does good, there is none, not so much as one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TEHILLIM 5:9; TEHILLIM 140:3; MATTITYAHU 23:27–28"> Their throat is an open grave. They use their tongues to deceive. The venom of asps is under their lips,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TEHILLIM 10:7; TEHILLIM 109:17–18; TEHILLIM 59:12"> Whose mouth is full of curses and bitterness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YESHAYAH 59:7–8; MISHLE 1:16; MISHLE 6:18"> Their feet are swift when it comes to shefach dahm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Ruin and wretchedness are in their ways,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="YESHAYAH 59:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:1; LUKAS 1:79"> And the derech Shalom (the way of peace) they have not known.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="see Ro 5:1">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="TEHILLIM 36:1; MISHLE 23:17; MISHLE 16:6"> There is no yirat Shomayim (fear of G-d) before their eyes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="See Ps 13:1-3; 14:1-3; 5:9,10; 139:4; 140:3; 10:7; Isa 59:7-8; Ps 36:1; Prov 1:16; Ps 35:2">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:22–23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:12–18"> Now we know that whatever the Gezetz (Law) says, it says to those under the Gezetz, in order that every mouth might be stopped and kol HaOlam Hazeh become ashem (guilty) and liable to the Mishpat Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ps 1:5">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:39; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:28"> For by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ro 2:17">j</a></sup>&#91;depending on&#93; ma'asim of gezetz<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ro 2:17; 4:2">k</a></sup>shall KOL CHAI LO YITZDAK<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Ps 143:2">l</a></sup>(all living not be justified), for through the gezetz<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gn 2:16-17">m</a></sup>(law) comes Da'as HaChet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gn 3:7">n</a></sup>(knowledge of sin).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:43; YIRMEYAH 33:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:9"> But now, apart from gezetz, the Tzidkat Hashem has been manifested, being attested to by the Torah and the Neviim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:1"> That is, the Tzidkat Hashem through emunah in Moshiach Yehoshua (Yeshua) to all the ma'aminim (believers). For there is no distinction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="KOHELET 7:20; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:8–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:9"> For all have sinned and suffered want of the kavod Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:18–19; LETTER TO TITOS 3:5–7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:14"> They are acquitted and accounted to be YITZDAK IM HASHEM as a matnat Hashem (gift of G-d) by the unmerited Chen v'Chesed Hashem (grace of G-d) through HaPedut<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="the payment of ransom for the Geulah redemption — 2Sm 7:23that comes about through the Go'el Moshiach Tzidkeinu">o</a></sup>(the ransom),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="T.N. See Ro 11:26-27">p</a></sup>which is in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:23–24; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:18–20; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:10"> Whom G-d set forth as a kapporah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="that which propitiates G-dʼs wrath; c.f. the sa'ir l'azazel in Lv 16:22and Isa 53:12paying the onesh for sin">q</a></sup>through emunah (faith) in the DAHM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Gn 22:7; Ex 12:3,6; Isa 53:7,10">r</a></sup>(blood) of Moshiach, to demonstrate the Tzedek Olamim, the Tzidkat Hashem (righteousness of G-d) in pasach (passing over, letting go the penalty of) the averos (sins) committed in former times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:30"> In the forbearance of G-d, to vindicate his Tzidkat Hashem (righteousness of G-d) in the present time, that HASHEM TZADDIK<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="2Chr 12:6">s</a></sup>(G-d is righteous) Himself and the One who counts to be YITZDAK IM HASHEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Job 25:4">t</a></sup>the person who has Torah of Emunah (the Law of Faith).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="that is, the Law understood in terms of emunah">u</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:23; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:29–31"> Where then is boasting?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="see Ro 4:2">v</a></sup>It has been memayet (precluded, excluded). By what kind of gezetz? Of depending on &#91;zokheh-earning&#93; ma'asim? No, on the contrary,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="T.N. Ro 4:2">w</a></sup>by the (debt). Torah of Emunah (the Law of Faith, that is, the Law understood in terms of emunah).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:8; LETTER TO TITOS 3:7"> For we reckon that a man is acquitted and pronounced to be YITZDAK IM HASHEM by emunah (personal faith, trust), apart from depending on<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="(supposed) zokheh-earning">x</a></sup>ma'asim of gezetz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:6; ZECHARYAH 2:11"> Or is Hashem G-d of the Yehudim only? Is Hashem not also G-d of the Nations? Ken, of non-Jews, too,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:3"> Vi-bahlt (since), after all, Adonai echad<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Dt 6:4">y</a></sup>(G-d is one). Therefore, He will consider to be YITZDAK IM HASHEM and acquit those of the milah on the ground of emunah and the “arelim” (uncircumcised ones) through that same emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:21; MATTITYAHU 5:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:8–10"> Does it follow that we abolish Torah and make it invalid through emunah? Chas v'shalom! Aderaba (to the contrary), we uphold the Torah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="See Ro 4:3; 8:4; Ac 21:26">z</a></sup></span>
</div>
