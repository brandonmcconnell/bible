<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:18; TEHILLIM 119:136; YIRMEYAH 13:17"> That there is great agmat nefesh to me and unceasing anguish in my heart.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:12; TEHILLIM 147:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:6"> In as much as they are Bnei Yisroel: theirs is the Mishpat HaBanim Adoption, the Ma'amad HaBanim Standing as Sons, and the Kavod (glory) and the Shekhinah (glorious presence of G-d) and the Beritot (covenants), the Matan Torah, the Avodas Kodesh (worship) and the Havtachot (promises);</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:30; YOCHANAN 1:1–3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:16–19"> Theirs are the Avot (the Patriarchs), and from them came, in so far as his humanity is concerned, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, al hakol hu HaElohim mam'vorach l'Olam va'ed. Omein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:28–29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:1–2"> But it is not as though the Dvar Hashem has failed. For not all those descended from Yisroel are truly redeemed Yisroel &#91;of the eschatological Geulah Redemption&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="BERESHIS 21:12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:18; YOCHANAN 8:37–39"> Nor is it as though all the banim of K'lal Yisroel are the ZERA of Avraham Avinu, but &#91;as it is written&#93;, BEYITZCHAK YIKARE L'CHA ZERA (In Yitzchak shall your seed be called, named, summoned).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gn 21:12">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 1:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:11–16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:26–29"> That is, it is not the Bnei habasar (old humanity without hitkhadshut) who are the Bnei HaElohim (children of G-d) but the Bnei HaHavtachah (children of the promise) who are reckoned as ZERA (seed, children, including the right of the heir in relation to the father).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="BERESHIS 18:10; BERESHIS 18:14; BERESHIS 17:21"> For this word is one of havtachah (promise): KA'ET SHOV ASHUV UL'SARAH BEN (About this time I will come and Sarah shall have a son).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gn 18:10,14">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:21–23; LUKAS 16:26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:11"> Not only so, but also in the case of Rivkah (Isaacʼs wife) who conceived by the one act of sexual intercourse with Yitzchak Avinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:4–5; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:10"> For when they were not yet born nor had they done any mitzvah or averah, in order that the etzah (wisdom) of the tochnit Hashem(purposeful and willed plan of G-d)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ro 8:28">c</a></sup>should stand in terms of bechirah (divine election, selection, choosing),</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="BERESHIS 25:22–23; SHMUEL BAIS 8:14; MELACHIM ALEF 22:47"> Not from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="zokheh-earning">d</a></sup>mitzvos but from the One who makes the kri'ah (divine summons),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ro 8:30">e</a></sup>it was said to her, RAV YA'AVOD TZA'IR (the elder will serve the younger),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gn 25:23">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MALACHI 1:2–3; DEVARIM 21:15; BERESHIS 29:33"> As it is written, VA'OHAV ES YA'AKOV V'ES ESAV SANEITI (Ya'akov have I loved, but Esau have I hated).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Mal 1:2-3">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:7; TEHILLIM 145:17; DEVARIM 32:4"> What then shall we say? There is no avla (injustice) with G-d, is there? Chas v'shalom!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="SHEMOT 33:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:16; MICHOH 7:18"> For to Moshe Rabbeinu Hashem says, V'CHANNOTI ES ASHER ACHON V'RICHAMETTI ES ASHER ARACHEM (I will have mercy on whom I will have mercy and I will have compassion on whom I will have compassion).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ex 33:19">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:8; MATTITYAHU 11:25–26; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9–10"> So then, it is not a matter of the one who wills or the one who runs. It is a matter of the YAD HASHEM HACHANINAH (the hand of the G-d of gracious, free mercy).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SHEMOT 9:16; MISHLE 16:4; SHEMOT 14:17–18"> For the Kitvei Hakodesh says to Pharaoh, BA'AVUR ZOT HE'EMADTICHA BA'AVUR HAROTECHA ES KOCHI ULEMA'AN SAPER SHMI BECHOL HA'ARETZ (For this purpose I raised you up, in order that I might demonstrate in you my power and in order that my Name might be proclaimed in all the earth).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ex 9:16">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="YEHOSHUA 11:20; SHEMOT 4:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:15–16"> So then, to whom Hashem wills Hashem shows chaninah (mercy, free grace), but whom Hashem wills he hardens.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="that is, makes unresponsive or more mired down in KESHI (stubbornness, hardness); Dt 9:27">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DANIEL 4:35; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 20:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:12"> You will say to me, Then why does Hashem still find fault? For who has resisted His will?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YESHAYAH 29:16; YESHAYAH 45:9–11; YESHAYAH 64:8"> On the contrary, who are you, a human being, to answer back to G-d? VEYETZER AMAR LEYOTZRO (Can the pot say to the potter),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Isa 29:16">k</a></sup>Why have you made me thus?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YESHAYAH 64:8; MISHLE 16:4; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:20–21"> Or does the potter not have the right over the clay<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Jer 18:6">l</a></sup>to make from the same lump one vessel for honorable use and another for dishonorable use?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MISHLE 16:4; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:21"> But what if naniach (supposing)Hashem, willing to demonstrate His Charon Af Hashem(burning anger of G-d) and to make known His ko'ach (power),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="1:16,18">m</a></sup>put up with and endured with savlanut (patience) vessels which are objects of G-dʼs Charon Af (burning anger), objects made ready for Churban,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Ro 9:3">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:4; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:2–5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:27"> And in order that He might make known the wealth of His kavod (glory) on vessels which are objects of Hashemʼs chaninah (mercy, free grace) which He prepared beforehand for kavod?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Ro 8:29-30">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:47–48; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:6–8"> By which I mean us, whom also He called, not only from the Yehudim but also from the non-Jews,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="HOSHEA 2:23; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:10; YOCHANAN 16:27"> As it says in Hoshea, V'AMARTI L'LO AMMI AMI ATAH (And I will call the “not my people” my people),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hos 2:23[2:25]">p</a></sup>and the “not loved” loved;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="HOSHEA 1:9–10; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:1–3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:26"> And it shall be in the place where it was said to them, “You are not my people,” there they shall be called Bnei EL CHAI<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hos 2:1">q</a></sup>(sons of the living G-d).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YESHAYAH 10:20–23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:4–6; YESHAYAH 1:9"> Yeshayah proclaims concerning Yisroel, Even if the number of the Bnei Yisroel are as the sand of the sea, only the She'erit (Remnant) will return (be saved),</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="YESHAYAH 10:23; HISGALUS 19:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:31"> For Hashem will complete and cut short and will perform His Word on the earth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Isa 10:22-23">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="YESHAYAH 1:9; YESHAYAH 13:19; AMOS 4:11"> And as Yeshayah said beforehand, Except Adonoi Tz'vaot had left us SARID KIM'AT<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Ro 9:7">s</a></sup>(some survivors), we would have become like S'dom and we would have been the same as Amora.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Isa 1:9">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:9; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:16"> What then shall we say? That Goyim who do not pursue Tzidkot (righteousness) have attained Tzidkot which is Tzidkot through emunah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:20; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:10–11"> Whereas Yisroel pursuing a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="supposed zokheh-earning">u</a></sup>Tzidkat Torah did not arrive at that Torah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:23; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:8; LUKAS 7:23"> Why so? Because it was not on the mekor (basis) of emunah but on the mekor (basis) of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="zokheh-earning">v</a></sup>ma'asim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Ro 3:20,28; 4:2,6; 9:11-12">w</a></sup>(works). They have stumbled over the EVEN NEGEF (Stone of Stumbling),</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="YESHAYAH 28:16; YESHAYAH 8:14–15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:11"> As it is written, Hinei, I place in Tziyon a stone of stumbling and a rock of offense; and he who believes in Me shall not be put to shame.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Isa 8:14; 28:16">x</a></sup></span>
