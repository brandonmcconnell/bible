<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="TEHILLIM 9:7–8; TZEFANYAH 3:5; TEHILLIM 145:17"> And we have da'as that the judgment of Hashem HaShofet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ro 1:32">a</a></sup>against those who practice such things is in accordance with HaEmes Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ro 1:25">b</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; YESHAYAH 30:18; SHEMOT 34:6"> Or do you think lightly of the wealth of his nedivut (generosity) and of his chesed and of his being ERECH APAYIM (slow of anger, forbearing)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ex 34:6">c</a></sup>and of his savlanut (patience), disregarding the fact that the Chesed Hashem (the kindness of G-d) is to lead you to teshuva?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ps 19:7[8]">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="KOHELET 12:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:2–3"> As a result of your KESHI<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dt 9:27">e</a></sup>(stubbornness, hardness) and your levavot without teshuva, you are storing up for yourself Charon Af Hashem (the burning anger of G-d) in the Yom Af (the Day of Wrath),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="i.e., the Yom HaDin, the Day of Judgment. <span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TEHILLIM 62:12; YIRMEYAH 32:19; MATTITYAHU 16:27"> Who will render L'ISH K'MA'A'SEI HU<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Ps 62:12[13]">g</a></sup>(to each according to his works).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:7–8; LUKAS 8:15; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:10"> To those who, by savlanut (patience), persevere in doing hatov (the good), seek for kavod (glory) and honor and incorruptibility,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ps 16:10">h</a></sup>He will give Chayyei Olam (Eternal Life).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:18–21; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:8; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 6:3–4"> But to those who are self-seeking and who have no mishma'at (obedience) to HaEmes Hashem,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ro 1:25">i</a></sup>but instead have mishma'at to resha, there will be Charon Af Hashem and fury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:17; YECHEZKEL 18:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:16"> There will be affliction and distress on every living neshamah who brings about what is rah (evil), Yehudi above all and Yevani (Greek) as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="YESHAYAH 32:17; YESHAYAH 26:12; BAMIDBAR 6:26"> But tiferet and kavod and shalom to everyone who brings about what is tov (good), Yehudi above all and Yevani as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:17; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 10:34; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:7"> For ki ein masso panim im Hashem (there is no partiality with Hashem).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:16–19"> For as many as have committed averos and sinned lacking the Torah shall also perish lacking the Torah; and as many as have committed averos (sin) under the Torah shall be condemned under the Torah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:22–25; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:7; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:21–25"> For it is not the Shomei HaTorah (hearers of the Law of Moshe Rabbeinu) who are the tzaddikim who are accounted to be YITZDAK IM HASHEM (justified with G-d).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Job 25:4">j</a></sup>It is the Shomrei HaTorah (the keepers of the Torah) who will be counted to be YITZDAK IM HASHEM.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:19–20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:27"> For when non-Jews<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ro 11:13">k</a></sup>who have not a Torah, may do by nature<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="See 2Kgs 1:4">l</a></sup>what the Torah requires,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="see Ro 8:4">m</a></sup>they not having a Torah are a gezetz (law) for themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:18–19"> In that they demonstrate the Torah at work,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Jer 31:33; Ro 8:4; 2C 5:17">n</a></sup>the Torah written in their levavot, their matzpun<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Ac 24:16">o</a></sup>(conscience) also bearing witness,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Ro 9:1">p</a></sup>while their thoughts bring accusation<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Ga 2:11">q</a></sup>or even make defense<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="2C 7:11">r</a></sup>among themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="KOHELET 12:14; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:31"> In the Yom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="HaDin (Day of Judgment)">s</a></sup>when, according to my Besuras HaGeulah, Hashem, through Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, is to judge the secrets of kol Bnei Adam (all men).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

Contra Ro 3:20; 4:2">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:21; DEVARIM 4:8; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:17"> And have da'as of His will and approve the things that matter, being instructed from the Torah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="HISGALUS 3:17–18; MATTITYAHU 15:14; YESHAYAH 49:6"> And being confident that you are a moreh derech (guide) of the ivrim (blind ones), an ohr for those in choshech,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:5; LETTER TO TITOS 1:16; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:13"> A rabbinic moreh (teacher) of the foolish, a melammed (instructor) of the young, having the embodiment of da'as (knowledge) and Emes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Ro 1:25">u</a></sup>in the Torah…</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:27; MATTITYAHU 23:3–28; LUKAS 12:47"> You, then, who teach another, do you not teach yourself? <span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MALACHI 3:8; MATTITYAHU 12:39; MARKOS 11:17"> You who say, LO TINAF<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Ex 20:14">w</a></sup>(Do not commit adultery!), do you commit adultery? You who abhor elilim (idols), do you rob pagan temples?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:16–17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:2"> You who boast<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Ro 4:2; Lk 17:10">x</a></sup>in Torah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Ro 2:29; 3:20; Ga 2:15-21">y</a></sup>through deviating from the Torah, you commit Chillul Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 36:20–23; YESHAYAH 52:5; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:2"> As it is written, Among the Goyim KOL HAYOM HASHEM HAELOHIM MINNO'ATZ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Isa 52:5">z</a></sup>(All the day long the Name of G-d is being blasphemed) because of you.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 4:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:3–6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:51"> For the milah (circumcision) is an asset<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="T.N. contra Ac 21:21">aa</a></sup>if you stand in mishma'at (obedience) to the Torah. But if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="See Ga 5:2-4; Dt 27:26">ab</a></sup>you are mefir (violator) of the Torah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="2:23">ac</a></sup>your milah (circumcision) has become “orlah” (uncircumcision).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:18–19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:4"> If then the ben Adam without milah who is shomer fulfilling the just requirements of the Torah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="T.N. see Ro 8:4">ad</a></sup>will not his orlah (uncircumcision) be counted as “milah”?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 12:41–42; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:29; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:6"> And the naturally uncircumcised ben Adam who has mishma'at (obedience) to the Torah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="T.N. Ro 2:15">ae</a></sup>will arise as a judgment on you. You! The very one who through chumra (stringency, strict adherence to the letter of the law) and circumcision<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="i.e., uncircumcision, see Ro 2:25">af</a></sup>are the transgressor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Ro 7:22-23; Yn 7:19">ag</a></sup>of Torah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:15; MATTITYAHU 3:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:6–8"> For the true Yehudi (Jew) is not the one &#91;humanly&#93; perceived as such, nor true milah that which is performed visibly in the flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEVARIM 30:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:3; DEVARIM 10:16"> The true Yehudi is so in &#91;Hashemʼs&#93; hidden way, and true milah is of the lev, in the &#91;hitkhadshut (renewal) of the&#93; Ruach Hakodesh,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Yn 3:3">ah</a></sup>not in &#91;chumra (stringency, boasting of zokheh in machmir meritorious strict)&#93; letter. He has y'hodeh (praise)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Yehudah, yadah praise…see Gn 29:35">ai</a></sup>that comes not from Bnei Adam but from Hashem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="T.N. Jer 31:31-34">aj</a></sup></span>
