<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:15"> What then shall we say? Are we to persist in Chet (sin) in order that the unmerited Chen v'Chesed Hashem might increase?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:4"> Chas v'shalom! Vi-bahlt (since) we have died to Chet, how can we still live in it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:20–21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:8"> Or do you lack da'as that all we who were given a mikveh mayim tevilah into Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua were given a tevilah into Moshiachʼs histalkus, into his mavet (death)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:22–24"> So then we were co-buried, buried together with KIVRO<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Isa 53:9">a</a></sup>(Moshiachʼs kever) through a tevilah into mavet, in order that, just as Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach was given the Techiyah (Resurrection) from HaMesim (the Dead ones) through the Kavod HaAv, so we also should have a halakhah (walk) &#91;lifnei Hashem&#93; in hitkhadshut (renewal, regeneration), in Chayyim Chadashim (New Life).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:10–11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:12"> For if we have become grown together with the very likeness of his histalkus, his mavet (death), we shall certainly also be grown together with the very likeness of his Techiyah from HaMesim (Resurrection).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:24"> Having da'as of this, that, with Moshiach, our old humanity (in Adam) has been put to death on Moshiachʼs Etz<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dt 21:23">b</a></sup>(Tree, &#91;the Etz HaKelelat Hashem&#93;, the Tree of the Curse of G-d) in order that the etsem HaAdam HaChet (the essence of the sinful human condition) might be done away with, so that we might no longer serve Chet (sin).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="cf. Ro 6:23">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:1–3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:4"> For he who has died is declared niftar (freed, deceased) from Chet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:11–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:3–4; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:10–14"> But if we have died with Moshiach, we believe that we shall also live with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:14; HISGALUS 1:18; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:24–28"> Having da'as that Moshiach, having been given the Techiyah (Resurrection) from the Mesim (Dead ones), no longer dies, Mavet (death) and Histalkus no longer exercise control over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:21; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:6; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:18"> For the Mavet Moshiach died, he died to Chet (sin) once and for all; but the Chayyim Moshiach lives, he lives to Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:3–5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:2"> So also you must reckon yourselves mesim (dead ones) to Chet (Sin) but Chayyim l'Hashem b'Moshiach Yehoshua (alive to G-d in Messiah Yehoshua).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:13"> Therefore, do not let Chet (Sin) reign in your mortal body to obey its ta'avot,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ro 5:17,21">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:5"> And do not give Chet (sin) control of your natural capacities as neshek (weapons) of peysha (unrighteousness, transgression), but present yourselves to Hashem as ones alive from the Mesim and present to Hashem your natural capacities as neshek (weapons) of Tzedek Olamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:4–11"> For Chet (sin) shall not exercise bailus (sovereignty, ownership, dominion) over you; for you are not under Gezetz<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ro 8:2">e</a></sup>but under Chesed (Grace).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 2:11–14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:8–10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:1–2"> What then? Should we commit averah, because we are not under Gezetz<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="see Ro 8:2">f</a></sup>(Law) but under Chesed (Grace)? Chas v'shalom!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YOCHANAN 8:34; MATTITYAHU 6:24; YEHOSHUA 24:15"> Do you not know that when you give control of yourselves as someoneʼs avadim (slaves) to obey him, you are the avadim (slaves) of the one you obey, whether of Chet (Sin) resulting in mavet (death), or of Lishmo'a b'kol Hashem (Listening to the voice of Hashem, mishma'at, obedience) resulting in Tzedek Olamim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:8"> But Baruch Hashem (Blessed be G-d) that you used to be avadim (slaves) of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="slave master">g</a></sup>Chet, but you gave your mishma'at shebalev (obedience from the heart) to the pattern of Torah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isa 42:4">h</a></sup>(the pnimiyus Torah of Moshiach) to which you were handed over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:1"> Having been set free from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="slave master">i</a></sup>Chet (sin), you became an eved of the Tzidkat Hashem (the righteousness of G-d).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:13; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:2–4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:5–7"> I speak in human terms on account of the weakness of your frail fallen humanity. For just as you handed over your natural capacities as avadim (slaves) to tum'a (uncleanness) and to lawlessness which results in lawlessness, so now hand over your natural capacities as servants of Tzidkat Hashem which results in kedushah (holiness).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:16–17; YOCHANAN 8:34"> For when you were avadim (slaves) of Chet, you were free in relation to Tzedek Olamim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:32; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 7:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:23"> What p'ri for Hashem did you produce then? Things for which you now have bushah (shame), for the end result of those things is mavet (death).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:2"> But now, having been set free from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="slave master">j</a></sup>Chet (sin) and having been made an eved Hashem (a servant of G-d), you have your p'ri for Hashem, resulting in kedushah, and the end is Chayyei Olam (Eternal Life).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:36; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:12; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:15"> For the loin (wages) that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="slave master">k</a></sup>Chet (Sin) pays out of its own payroll is mavet (death); however, the gracious matnat hachesed Hashem (the gift of the grace of G-d) is Chayyei Olam b'Moshiach Yehoshua Adoneinu.</span>
</div>
