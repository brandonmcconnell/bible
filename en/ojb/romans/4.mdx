<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YESHAYAH 51:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:16; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:22"> What then shall we say about the one who according to the basar (flesh) is Avraham Avinu? What did he find to be the case?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="T.N. See Ep 2:8-9">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:31; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 11:30; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:9"> For if Avraham Avinu was accounted to be YITZDAK IM HASHEM and acquitted before Hashem on the basis of ma'asim (of gezetz), he has something to boast about. But not before Hashem!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="BERESHIS 15:6; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 2:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:6–8"> For what does the Torah say? Avraham Avinu had emunah (faith) in Hashem V'YACHSHEVEHA LO TZEDAKAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gn 15:6">b</a></sup>(and it was accounted, credited, reckoned, imputed to him for righteousness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:32; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:35"> Now to him who works, the loin (wages, batzalon &#91;remuneration&#93;) is not credited to oneʼs account as a favor or gift of chesed but as a choiv (debt).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:22; LETTER TO TITOS 3:3–7"> But to the man who does not “work” but has emunah and bitachon in the One who takes the impious man lacking chasidus and accounts him to be YITZDAK IM HASHEM (justified with G-d), such a man who does not “work” but has emunah (faith), such emunah is credited to him for TZEDAKAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gn 15:6">c</a></sup>(righteousness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:9; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:30"> As also Dovid HaMelech speaks of the me'ushar (blessedness) of the person whom Hashem reckons to be YITZDAK IM HASHEM (justified with G-d) without dependence on<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="zokheh (merit-earning)">d</a></sup>ma'asim (of gezetz):<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="T.N. with merit viewed as “pay” earned for “work” rendered — see Ro 4:4">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="TEHILLIM 32:1–2; TEHILLIM 130:3–4; LUKAS 7:47–50"> Ashrey (blessed, happy) are those whose lawless deeds have been forgiven, and whose sins have been covered over;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TEHILLIM 32:2; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:24; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:19–20"> ASHREY ADAM LO YAKHSHOV HASHEM LO AVON<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ps 32:1-2">f</a></sup>(Blessed, happy is the one whose sin Hashem will by no means count).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:26–28"> This me'ushar (blessedness), then, does it come on those of the milah (the circumcised) or also on those without the bris milah (the uncircumcised)? For we say, emunah “was credited (counted, reckoned)” to Avraham Avinu for TZEDAKAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gn 15:6">g</a></sup>(righteousness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:6; BERESHIS 17:10"> When then was it “credited”? When Avraham Avinu was bris milah and was in the state of circumcision? Or when Avraham Avinu did not have the bris milah and was in the state of uncircumcision?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:22; LUKAS 19:9; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:22"> Not in circumcision, but in uncircumcision! And he received the ot (sign or distinguishing mark) of the milah (circumcision) as a chotam (seal) of the Tzidkat HaEmunah (the Righteousness of Faith) which he had in his uncircumcision, in order that he might be father of all who believe through uncircumcision, that to be YITZDAK IM HASHEM might be reckoned, counted to them as well,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SHIR HASHIRIM 1:8; YOCHANAN 8:39–40; MISHLE 2:20"> And in order that he might be Av (Father) to the Nimolim (Circumcised ones), to those who are not only HaNimolim but also who follow in the footsteps of the emunah of Avraham Avinu, which he had in &#91;his&#93; uncircumcision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:29; BERESHIS 22:17–18; BERESHIS 28:14"> For the havtachah (promise) to Avraham Avinu and his Zera (seed), that he should be Yoresh HaOlam (Heir of the World), did not come through the context of gezetz but through the Tzidkat HaEmunah (the Righteousness of Faith).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:18–24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:16"> For if those depending on &#91;zokheh of&#93; works are yoreshim (heirs), emunah (faith) is rendered invalid and the havtachah (the promise) is annulled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:19–20"> for gezetz (law) brings about the Charon Af Hashem,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ro 1:18; 3:20; Ex 32:8-10">h</a></sup>and where there is no gezetz there is no averah (transgression, violation of the Law).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="see Ro 9:11-12">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:24–26; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:22"> For this reason the havtachah (promise) is of emunah (faith), in order that it might be in accordance with unmerited Chen v'Chesed Hashem, that the havtachah might be certain to all the zera (seed), not to him who is of the Torah only, but also to bnei emunat Avraham (the sons of the faith of Avraham Avinu, to those who are of the faith of Abraham). Avraham Avinu is the father of us all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:28; BERESHIS 17:4–5; YOCHANAN 5:21"> as it is written, AV HAMON GOYIM N'TATICHA<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gn 17:5">j</a></sup>(I have made you father of many nations). This was in the sight of Hashem in whom “he believed,” G-d who gives Chayyim to the Mesim and calls things which have no existence into existence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:24; BERESHIS 15:5–6; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 27:25"> Against tikvah (hope), in tikvah “he believed,” in order that he might become AV HAMON GOYIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gn 17:5">k</a></sup>(father of many nations) in accordance with what had been said, So shall thy ZERA (seed) be.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gn 15:5">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:11–19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:20–21; BERESHIS 17:17"> Without weakening in emunah (in personal faith, bitachon, trust) he contemplated his own body, now as good as dead vi-bahlt (since) he was about one hundred years old, and also the deadness of Sarahʼs womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKAS 1:45; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 16:13; DANIEL 10:19"> He did not, in disbelief, doubt the havtachah of Hashem(the promise of G-d), but was strengthened in emunah (faith), giving kavod (glory) to Hashem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKAS 1:37; YIRMEYAH 32:27; BERESHIS 18:14"> Being fully convinced that what Hashem had promised He also was able to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:3"> Therefore, V'YACHSHEVEHA LO TZEDAKAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gn 15:6">m</a></sup>(it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="his faith in G-d, see also Ga 2:16">n</a></sup>was credited to him for righteousness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:6"> Nor was it written down for his sake alone that “it was reckoned to him,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:24; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:21; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:30"> But also for us, to whom it is to be reckoned, who believe in Him who raised Yehoshua Adoneinu from the mesim (dead ones),</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:9–10; YESHAYAH 53:5–6; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:21"> Who was handed over for PEYSHA'EINU<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Isa 53:5">o</a></sup>(our transgressions), and made to stand up in his Techiyas HaMoshiach that we be YITZDAK IM HASHEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="see Ro 5:18">p</a></sup>(be justified with G-d, that we have our justification, our acquittal, vindication).</span>
</div>
