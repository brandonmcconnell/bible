<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="TEHILLIM 94:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:29–30; SHEMOT 32:1"> Hashem has not repudiated His people whom He foreknew.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ps 94:14; Ro 8:29">a</a></sup>Or do you not have da'as what the Kitvei Hakodesh says in the section about Eliyahu HaNavi, how he appeals to Hashem against Yisroel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 19:10–18; MELACHIM ALEF 18:13; NECHEMYAH 9:26"> Adonoi, they have killed your Nevi'im, they have torn down your mizbe'achot (altars), and I alone have been left, and they seek my life.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1Kgs 19:10">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MELACHIM ALEF 19:18; BAMIDBAR 25:3; TZEFANYAH 1:4"> But what is Hashemʼs answer to him? “I have kept for Myself seven thousand men, who have not bowed the knee to Ba'al.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1Kgs 19:18">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:5–6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:11"> Thus, therefore, also in the zman hazeh, there has come into being a she'erit (remnant, remainder) in accordance with the bechirah (election) of chesed (free, unmerited favor or grace).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:4–5; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:9"> But if on the mekor (basis) of chen v'chesed (unmerited favor, grace), then not on the mekor (basis) of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="loin (wages), batsolen (pay) for">d</a></sup>ma'asim (works), vi-bahlt (since) otherwise chesed would no longer be chesed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ro 4:4-5">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:5; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:4"> What then? What Yisroel sought for, that is what it did not obtain; but hannivcharim (the elect, the chosen ones) obtained it. And the rest were hardened.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ro 9:17-18">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEVARIM 29:4; YESHAYAH 29:10; YECHEZKEL 12:2"> As it is written, G-d gave to them a ruach tardemah (spirit of deep sleep), eyes that they should not see and ears that they should not hear, until this very day.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 29:10">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TEHILLIM 69:22–23; LUKAS 12:20; TEHILLIM 28:4"> And Dovid said, Let their shulchan (table) become a snare and a net, a trap and a retribution for them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TEHILLIM 69:23; DEVARIM 28:64–68; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:4"> Let their eyes be darkened so that they cannot see, and bend their backs forever.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Ps 69:22,23">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:46–48; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:31; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:19"> I ask, therefore, have they stumbled so as to fall? Chas v'shalom! But by their peysha (transgression), Yeshu'at Eloheinu is coming to the Goyim in order to provoke them to jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:27; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:15"> And if their peysha (transgression) means riches for the world, and their failure means riches for the Goyim, how much more will their fullness mean!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:15; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:11–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:8"> I am speaking to you Goyim. So then, in as much as I am Shliach of the Ethnic Groups, I magnify my avodas kodesh, in the tikvah</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:16; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:3"> that I might provoke my kinsmen to jealousy and might save some of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:10–11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:20–21"> For if their rejection means ritztzuy (reconciliation),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="cessation of enmity, hostility between a wrathful holy G-d and sinful men">i</a></sup>for the gontzer velt (whole world), what shall their acceptance mean other than Chayyim min haMesim (Life from the dead ones)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 44:30; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:18; MISHLE 3:9"> If the terumah haissa (portion, offering of the dough) that is reshit (first) is kodesh (holy), so is the whole; and if the shoresh (root) is kodesh (holy), so also are the ana'fim (the branches).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:11–13; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:6; MATTITYAHU 21:43"> But if some of the ana'fim have been broken off, and you, a wild olive, have been grafted among them and have become sharer in the richness of the olive treeʼs root,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:19–20"> Do not boast<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ro 4:2">j</a></sup>over the ana'fim. If you do boast, it is not the case that you sustain the shoresh, but the shoresh sustains you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:11–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:23–24"> You will say, then, “Ana'fim were broken off in order that I might be grafted in.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:16; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:24; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 10:12"> Quite so: they were broken off on the mekor (basis) of no emunah, but you stand only by emunah. Do not cherish proud thoughts, but fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:4–9; YIRMEYAH 25:29; YIRMEYAH 49:12"> For if G-d did not spare the natural anafim, neither will He spare you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 3:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:2"> Consider then the nedivut (generosity), the chesed of Hashem, and also the fearful judgment of Hashem: to those who fell,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Ro 11:15">k</a></sup>severity; but to you the goodness of Hashem, provided that you continue in that goodness; otherwise, you too will be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:16; ZECHARYAH 12:10; MATTITYAHU 23:39"> Whereas, they also, if they do not continue in a condition of no emunah, shall be grafted in; for Hashem is able to graft them in again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:17–18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:30"> For if you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Goyim">l</a></sup>were cut off from the wild olive tree and grafted unnaturally into the cultivated olive tree, how much more shall those who belong to it naturally be grafted into their own olive tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKAS 21:24; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:14–16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:25"> For I do not want you to be unaware, Achim b'Moshiach, of this raz (mystery), lest you be wise in your own estimation, that a hardening in part has come over Yisroel, until the full number of the Goyim has come in;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="YESHAYAH 59:20; YIRMEYAH 31:31–37; TEHILLIM 14:7"> And so Klal Yisroel shall be delivered, as it is written: Out of Tziyon shall come the Go'el (Deliverer, Redeemer); He will turn away, remove peysha from Ya'akov (Jacob),</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 31:31–34; YESHAYAH 59:21; YECHEZKEL 36:25–29"> And this will be My Berit (covenant) with them, when I take away their sins.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Isa 59:20-21; 27:9">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEVARIM 10:15; DEVARIM 7:7–8; MATTITYAHU 21:43"> With regard to the Besuras HaGeulah they are oyevim (enemies) for your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="you Goyim">n</a></sup>sake, whereas with regard to the bechirah (election) they are beloved for the sake of the Avot (Fathers).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:21; BAMIDBAR 23:19; MALACHI 3:6"> For the matnat Hashem and the kri'at Hashem are irrevocable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:1–2; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:25"> For just as you Goyim were once without mishma'at to Hashem, but now have received chaninah (mercy, pardon) by their lack of mishma'at,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:25"> So also they have now been without mishma'at for your chaninah (mercy, pardon) in order that they also might receive chaninah (mercy).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Ro 9:15-16">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:9; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 2:4–6"> For G-d has confined all in disobedience in order that He might have chaninah (mercy) on all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="IYOV 5:9; IYOV 11:7–9; TEHILLIM 92:5"> O the depth of the riches and the chochmah (wisdom) and da'as (knowledge) of Hashem. How unfathomable are His mishpatim and unsearchable His ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="YESHAYAH 40:13; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:16; IYOV 15:8"> For who has known the Ruach of Hashem? Or who has been ISH ATZATO (His Counselor)?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Isa 40:13">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="IYOV 41:11; IYOV 35:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:7"> Or who has given in advance to Him so that His presents come only as a (choiv) debt repaid?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Job 41:11[41:3]; Ro 4:4">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:15–17; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 8:6; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 2:10"> Because from Him and through Him and to Him are all things. Lo HaKavod l'Olamim. Omein. (To Him be glory forever. Amen.)</span>
