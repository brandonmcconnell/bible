<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:18–19; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:13"> Therefore, now there is no gezar din (verdict) of ashem (guilty), no harsha'ah (condemnation as guilty) for those in Moshiach Yehoshua.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="cf. Ro 5:18">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:14; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:18"> For the Torah of the Ruach HaKodesh that gives Chayyim in Moshiach Yehoshua<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Jer 31:31-34; Ezek 36:26-27; Gn 2:16-17; 3:7,11,19; 5:12-20">b</a></sup>has set me free from the “Gezetz” of Chet and Mavet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:39; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:4–5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:18–19"> For what the Gezetz was unable to do in that it was weak through the basar,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="fallen human nature under Chet Kadmon and without hitkhadshut renewal and regeneration by the Ruach Hakodesh">c</a></sup>G-d sent his own Zun fun der Oybershter<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Moshiach">d</a></sup>in the very demut (likeness) of the basar of sinful humanity and as a chattat<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2C 5:21">e</a></sup>(sin offering, sin atoning sacrifice), and both pronounced and effected a sentence of death on HaChet baBasar (Sin in the Flesh, in the fallen old humanity),</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:22–25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:22"> In order that the maleh chukat haTorah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="see Lv 18:5">f</a></sup>(the full statute requirement of the Torah) might be fulfilled in us whose halakhah is in the Derech &#91;HaChayyim&#93; (the Way &#91;of Life&#93;) according to the Ruach Hakodesh and not in accordance with the basar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:19–25; YOCHANAN 3:6"> For those who exist in terms of the basar take the side of the basar, whereas those who exist in terms of the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hakodesh">g</a></sup>take the side of the Ruach Hakodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:13"> For the way of thinking of the basar is mavet (death), whereas the way of thinking of the Ruach Hakodesh is Chayyim and Shalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:14; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–16; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 4:4"> Because the way of thinking of the basar is hostility, eyvah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gn 3:15">h</a></sup>(enmity) toward G-d, for it does not submit itself to the Gezetz of G-d; for it cannot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:5–6; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:5–6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:9"> And those who are in the basar are not able to please Hashem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:6; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 3:16; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:14"> However, you are not in the basar<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="i.e., unregenerate">i</a></sup>but in the Ruach Hakodesh, assuming that the Ruach Hakodesh of Hashem does indeed dwell in you — if anyone does not have the Ruach HaMoshiach, that person does not belong to Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:20; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:21"> And if Moshiach is in you, the body (of the basar) is dead because of sin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ro 5:12">j</a></sup>but the Ruach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hakodesh">k</a></sup>is life for you because of Tzedek (righteousness).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="cf. Ro 5:18">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:4–5; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:11"> But if the Ruach Hakodesh of Him who gave Yehoshua Techiyah (Resurrection) from the Mesim dwells in you, He who raised Moshiach from the Mesim will give Chayyim to your mortal bodies as well, through His indwelling Ruach Hakodesh in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:2–15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:19–20; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:2–3"> So then, Achim b'Moshiach, we are under no obligation to the basar to live in accordance with the basar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 6:8; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:11; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:27"> For if you live in accordance with the basar<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="old fallen humanity under slave master Chet Kadmon">m</a></sup>you will certainly die; but if by the Ruach Hakodesh you put to death the &#91;shameful&#93; acts of the body, you will live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:18; YOCHANAN 1:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:26"> For as many as are led by the Ruach HaElohim, they are bnei HaElohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:5–7; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:12"> For you did not receive a spirit of avdut, falling back into pachad (fear); but you received the Ruach of Mishpat HaBanim (Adoption), having Ma'amad HaBanim (the standing as Sons),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Ro 9:4">n</a></sup>by which we cry, “Abba, Avinu!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:13; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:22; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:5"> The Ruach Hakodesh himself bears eidus (witness) with our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="regenerate, Yn 3:6">o</a></sup>ruach that we are bnei HaElohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:6"> And if bnei HaElohim, then also yoreshim (heirs) of G-d and co-heirs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Ro 4:13f">p</a></sup>together with Moshiach, provided that we suffer with him in order that we might also be set in kavod (glory, eschatological glorification) with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:17–18; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:13; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:5–7"> For I reckon that the yisurim (suffering) of zman hazeh (of this present time) are not to be compared with the coming kavod (glory) to be revealed in us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MALACHI 3:17–18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:23; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:2"> For the eager expectation of HaBri'ah (the Creation) awaits the heavenly hisgalus (revelation, unveiling) of the bnei HaElohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="BERESHIS 3:17–19; BERESHIS 5:29; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:22"> For HaBri'ah (the Creation) was subjected to hevel (futility), not willingly, but on account of Him who subjected it, in tikvah (hope),</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HISGALUS 22:3–5; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 3:21; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:13"> Because HaBri'ah also itself will be set free from the avdut (slavery) of corruption into the deror<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Isa 61:1">q</a></sup>(freedom) of the kavod (glory) of the bnei HaElohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 12:4; YIRMEYAH 12:11; YOCHANAN 16:21"> For we have da'as that the whole Bri'ah groans and suffers the chevlei leydah (pangs of childbirth) until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:20–21; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:2–5; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:28"> And not only so, but also we ourselves who have the bikkurim (first fruits) of the Ruach Hakodesh also groan within ourselves, eagerly awaiting the Mishpat HaBanim Adoption, that is, the pedut geviyyateinu<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="ransom for Geulah redemption of our body Gn 47:18">r</a></sup>for the Techiyah from HaMesim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="See Ro 3:24-25; 9:4">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:7; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 11:1; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:18"> For in tikvah (hope) we were delivered in eschatological salvation. But tikvah (hope) which is seen is not tikvah, for who hopes for what he sees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:12; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:36; TEHILLIM 27:14"> But if we have tikvah for what we do not see, we eagerly await it with savlanut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 10:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:6"> In the same way, the Ruach Hakodesh helps us in our weakness.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="as creatures: see Ro 5:6">t</a></sup>For as we daven, we do not know as we should for what to make tefillos (prayers), but the Ruach HaKodesh Himself intercedes on our behalf with labor pang groans not intelligibly uttered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="YIRMEYAH 17:10; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 5:14–15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:18"> And He who searches the levavot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Rv 2:23">u</a></sup>knows what is the way of thinking of the Ruach Hakodesh, because He intercedes as G-d would have it on behalf of the Kedoshim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:10; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; BERESHIS 50:20"> And we have da'as that for those who love Hashem everything co-operates toward HaTov for those who are HaKeru'im (the summoned, called ones) according to the etzah (wisdom) of the tochnit Hashem(G-dʼs purposeful and willed plan or goal).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Ro 9:11">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:4–5; YIRMEYAH 1:5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:11"> For those Hashem had da'as of beterem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Jer 1:5">w</a></sup>(beforehand), Hashem also decided upon from the beginning to be conformed to the demut (likeness) of Hashemʼs Zun fun der Oybershter, that he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Moshiach">x</a></sup>should be HaBechor (Firstborn) among many Achim b'Moshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="HISGALUS 17:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:11"> And those Hashem decided upon from the beginning Hashem also summoned; and those Hashem summoned, Hashem also acquitted and pronounced to be YITZDAK IM HASHEM; and those Hashem acquitted and pronounced to be YITZDAK IM HASHEM, Hashem also set in eternal kavod (glory).</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="TEHILLIM 118:6; YIRMEYAH 1:19; TEHILLIM 56:11"> In view of these things, what therefore shall we say? If Hashem is for us, who is against us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="YOCHANAN 3:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:28; TEHILLIM 84:11"> He who indeed did not spare His own Zun fun der Oybershter, but gave him up for us all, how shall He not also with him give us all things.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="see Ro 8:12-17; 4:13f">y</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="HISGALUS 12:10–11; YESHAYAH 50:8–9; YESHAYAH 54:17"> Who will bring charges against the Bechirei HaElohim (chosen ones of Hashem)? It is Hashem who acquits and pronounces to be YITZDAK IM HASHEM (justified with G-d).</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 7:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:27; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:24"> Who is there to bring a judgment of harsha'ah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="cf. Ro 8:1">z</a></sup>(condemnation, to condemn to Onesh Gehinnom)? It is Moshiach Yehoshua who died, rather was kam litechiyah (raised to resurrection), who also is at LIMIN HASHEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Ps 110:1">aa</a></sup>(the right hand of G-d), who also intercedes on our behalf.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="see Ro 8:26-27">ab</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:28; TEHILLIM 103:17; YOCHANAN 16:33"> Who will separate us from the Ahavas Moshiach (love of Moshiach)? Tzoros (affliction, trouble), or distress, or redifot (persecutions), or hunger, or nakedness, or danger, or cherev<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="13:4">ac</a></sup>(sword)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="TEHILLIM 44:22; YESHAYAH 53:7; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:30"> As it is written, KI ALECHA HORAGNU KOL HAYOM NECHESHAVNU K'TZON TIVCHAH<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Ps 44:22[23]">ad</a></sup>(For Your sake we are being killed all the day; we are reckoned as sheep for slaughter).</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:57; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:4; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 2:14"> But in all these things we prevail bichlal (entirely) through Him who had ahavah for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:28; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 14:8; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:12"> For I am convinced that neither Histalkus nor Chayyim nor Malachim nor Rulers, neither things present nor things to come nor kochot (powers),</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="YOCHANAN 10:28–30; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:35; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:8"> Neither height nor depth nor any other creature will be able to separate us from the ahavas Hashem which is in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua Adoneinu.</span>
