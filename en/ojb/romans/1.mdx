<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:16; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:1"> From Sha'ul, an eved of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua, summoned to be a Shliach, set apart for the Besuras HaGeulah of Hashem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 1:2; LUKAS 1:70; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:21"> Which Hashem promised beforehand through His Neviim in the Kitvei HaKodesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 1:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:4; YIRMEYAH 23:5–6"> The Good News of Redemption of Hashem concerns the Zun fun Der Oybershter (Ben HaElohim of Hashem) born from the zera Dovid, as far as basar is concerned,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:14; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 5:5–6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:19–23"> But, as far as the Ruach Hakodesh is concerned, appointed to be Zun fun der Oybershter in power by means of Moshiachʼs Techiyah (Resurrection) from HaMesim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:26; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 6:7"> Through him and for the kavod of his Name, we have received unmerited Chen v'Chesed Hashem and the Shlichus for the tachlis (purpose) of bringing about the mishma'at of emunah among all Nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:9; HISGALUS 17:14; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:10"> Among whom you also are summoned to belong to Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 1:2; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:1–2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:2"> To all who are in Rome, ahuvei Hashem(beloved ones of G-d), summoned by Hashemʼs kri'ah (calling) to be kedoshim (holy ones) — unmerited Chen v'Chesed Hashem and Shalom Hashem from Elohim Avinu and Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua Adoneinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:19; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:4; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:8–9"> First of all, modeh Ani (I thank) my G-d through Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua for all of you, because your emunah (faith), your bitachon, is being reported abroad b'chol haOlam (all over the world).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 6:18; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:3; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 1:16–19"> For der Oybershter is my eidus! (G-d is my witness!), Whom I serve with my neshamah in the Besuras HaGeulah of the Zun fun der Oybershter of Hashem, how, when I daven, I constantly mention you in my tefillos,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:30–32; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 18:21; LETTER TO PHILEMON 1:22"> Always making techinnah (supplication) and petitioning that I might somehow now at last efsher (perhaps) succeed im yirtzeh Hashem(if the L-rd wills) to make my way to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:25; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:29"> For I am longing to see you, that I may impart to you some mattanah ruchanit (spiritual gift) for the tachlis (purpose) of imparting chizzuk (strengthening, encouragement) to you and be mechazek (be strengthened spiritually);</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 3:7–10; (III) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 1:3–4; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 7:13"> Or rather, so that there may be mutual chizzuk (strengthening, encouragement) among you through each otherʼs emunah (faith), both yours and mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 19:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:17; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:17"> Now I do not want you to lack da'as, Achim b'Moshiach of mine, of how I often made plans to come to you, though I have been prevented thus far, in order that I might have some p'ri for Hashem among you as well, just as among the rest of the ethnic peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:16–23; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 9:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 3:11"> I am meshubad (obligated) morally, under shiebud (obligation), to both cultured Greek-speakers and non-Greek-speaking barbarians, both the learned and the untutored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:20; YESHAYAH 6:8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:17"> Hence my eagerness to preach the Besuras HaGeulah to you who are in Rome as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MARKOS 8:38; (II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:8; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:16"> For I am not ashamed of the Besuras HaGeulah. It is the ko'ach (power) of Hashem for the Geulah deliverance Job 19:25; Isa 43:1Ro 3:24 to all who have emunah, to the Yehudi (the Jew) above all, but also the Yevani (Greek).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="CHABAKUK 2:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:11; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 10:38"> For the Tzidkat Hashem,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2C 5:21; Ro 8:4; Pp 3:9">a</a></sup>(the righteousness of Hashem) is having its hisgalus (revelation) in the Besuras HaGeulah by emunah (faith) from first to last, from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="orthodox Jewish">b</a></sup>Faith to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="orthodox Jewish">c</a></sup>Faith, as it is written, V'TZADDIK BE'EMUNATO YICHE'YEH (The righteous by his faith shall live).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hab 2:4; Ga 3:11; MJ 10:35">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:6; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:1–2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:32"> For the Charon Af Hashem(the burning anger of G-d)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ro 1:18; 2:8; 3:5; 4:15; 5:9; 9:22; 12:19; 13:4">e</a></sup>is being revealed from Shomayim. It is being revealed against all without yirat Shomayim and Tzedek, all bnei Adam who wickedly repress HaEmes,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="of Hashem">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:16–17; TEHILLIM 19:1–6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:20"> All anashim who, even though what is knowable about Hashem lies plainly before their eyes (for G-d has shown them!), they nevertheless cling to their resha and wickedly suppress HaEmes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="of Hashem">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:19; YESHAYAH 40:26; TEHILLIM 19:1–6"> For his invisible characteristics from the Bri'at HaOlam (the creation of the world) are perceived intellectually in the things which have been created; that is, both his eternal ko'ach and Elohut are discernable. So Bnei Adam have no terutz (excuse) and are inexcusably culpable (before an angry G-d),</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:17–18; MELACHIM BAIS 17:15; YIRMEYAH 2:5"> Because, even though they in actual fact knew G-d, they did not ascribe Him kavod (glory) as G-d or give hodayah (thanksgiving) to Him, but became filled with hevel (futility, vanity, emptiness, worthlessness) in their thinking, and their senseless levavot were darkened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 1:19–21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:25; MISHLE 26:12"> Claiming to be chachamim (wise ones), they became kesilim (fools),</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="TEHILLIM 106:20; YIRMEYAH 2:11; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 17:29"> And traded in the kavod (glory) of the incorruptible G-d for the mere likeness of the demut (image, icon) of corruptible man, birds, beasts, and reptiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="TEHILLIM 81:11–12; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 7:42; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 14:16"> Therefore, G-d (in wrath) delivered them over in the ta'avot of their levavot to tum'a (uncleanness) to the dishonoring and perverting of their bodies among themselves:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="CHABAKUK 2:18; YIRMEYAH 13:25; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 2:15–16"> They traded in HaEmes Hashem(the Truth of G-d) for sheker, for a lie, and worshiped and served HaBri'ah (the Creature, the Creation) rather than HaBo're (the Beshefer), hamvorach l'olamim. Omein (who is blessed forever. Amen).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:7; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 1:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:24"> For this reason, Hashem(in wrath) delivered them over to paskudneh (contemptible) sexual desires. For their females traded off natural sexual intercourse for unnatural.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="VAYIKRA 20:13; VAYIKRA 18:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:23–24"> Likewise also the males abandoned natural sexual intercourse with the female counterpart and were inflamed with craving for one another, males with males committing what is indecent and receiving back (in exchange) in themselves the appropriate gemu<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isa 3:11">h</a></sup>l (retribution) for their toyus (error).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:10–12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:21; LETTER TO TITOS 1:16"> And as far as G-dʼs worthiness to be recognized by them was concerned, vi-bahlt (since) they marked G-d down as failing the test, therefore G-d (in wrath) delivered them over to a failure of a brain, one that has a mind bent on doing what is perversely unworthy</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 12:20; MISHLE 16:28; TEHILLIM 41:7"> — filled with all resha, wickedness, chamdanut (greediness), and what is damagingly evil — full of kin'a (jealousy), retzach (murder), rivalry, mirmah (deceit), merivah (strife), remiyah (guile, deceit), all kinds of lashon hora, malicious,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:2; LETTER FROM YEHUDA TO THE KEHILLAH 1:16; (II) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:18"> Backbiters, slanderers, haters of G-d, insolent, arrogant, braggarts,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="see Ro 4:2">i</a></sup>contrivers of evil, disobedient to horim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 3:3; MISHLE 18:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:11"> Without seichel, without ne'emanut (faithfulness), without ahavah (love), without rachamanut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 2:1–5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:21"> Although they have known full well the just requirements of Hashem,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="His just gezetz (law)">j</a></sup>that is, that those who practice such things are Bnei mavet,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="See Gn 2:16-17; Ro 5:12">k</a></sup>they not only do the very same, but even give their perverted bracha (blessing) on those who practice such. For this reason, you are without terutz (excuse) for yourself (before an angry G-d), you, sir, each one of you who passes judgment.</span>
