<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 3:1; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13–17; DANIEL 2:21"> Let kol nefesh (every soul, person) be subject to the official governing authorities. For there is no memshalah (government) except given by Hashem, and the powers that be have been established by G-d.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13; LETTER TO TITOS 3:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:5"> So then, he who opposes the authority has resisted the ordinance, official decree of G-d; and those who resist shall receive mishpat (judgment) on themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="KOHELET 10:4–6; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:13–14; MISHLE 20:2"> For rulers are not a cause of pachad (terror) to hitnahagut (conduct) that is of HaTov but HaRah. Do you want to be without pachad of memshalah (government)? Do HaTov, and you will have the commendation of the representative of the memshalah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:19; DIVREY HAYAMIM BAIS 19:6; MISHLE 16:14"> For he is the mesharet (minister, servant) of Hashem to you for HaTov. But if you do HaRah, be afraid. For he does not bear the cherev<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ro 8:35">a</a></sup>to no purpose. For he is the mesharet of Hashem, an avenger for Charon Af<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ro 1:18; 2:8; 3:5; 4:15; 5:9; 9:22; 12:19; 13:4">b</a></sup>against the evildoer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="KOHELET 8:2; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 24:16; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:19"> Wherefore it is necessary to be subject, not only on account of the Charon Af of Hashem, but also on account of matzpun (conscience).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 22:17–21; MARKOS 12:14–17; MATTITYAHU 17:24–27"> For that is why you also pay tribute (taxes). For they are mesharetim of G-d engaged in this very task.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MISHLE 24:21; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:17–18; LUKAS 20:25"> Render to everyone their due: tribute to whom tribute is due, tax to whom tax; fear to whom fear is due, respect to whom respect.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:10; MATTITYAHU 22:39–40; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:14"> Owe (be indebted, obligated) nothing to anyone except a choiv (debt) of ahavah (love); for he who has ahavah has fulfilled the Torah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SHEMOT 20:12–17; MATTITYAHU 22:39; LUKAS 10:27"> For the mitzvoh, LO TIN'AF, LO TIRTZACH, LO TIGNOV, LO TACHMOD,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ex 20:13-15,17; Dt 5:17-19,21">c</a></sup>and any other of the mitzvot (commandments) is akitzur (restated, in summary) in this dvar Torah, V'AHAVTA L'RE'ACHA KAMOCHA (You shall love your neighbor as yourself).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Lv 19:18">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTITYAHU 22:39–40; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 13:8; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 13:4–7"> Ahavah (agape) does no wrong to the re'a (neighbor); therefore the fulfillment of the Torah is ahavah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 5:5–8; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 4:7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:14"> Besides this, you have da'as of the zman, that it is already the hour for you to wake up from sheynah (sleep), for now is Yeshu'at Eloheinu nearer than when we became ma'aminim (believers).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="YECHEZKEL 18:31–32; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:11; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 6:7"> The Lailah (Night, of the old epoch) is far advanced, and HaYom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Yom HaDin, the Day of Judgment">e</a></sup>is imminent, at hand. Let us therefore take off the dark cloak of the deeds of choshech, ridding ourselves of it, and let us put on the neshek (weapons)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Ro 6:13">f</a></sup>of Ohr (light).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 5:18; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 4:3–5"> Let us conduct ourselves decently as in HaYom (The Day), not in carousing and shichrut (drunkenness), not in zenut (fornication) and debauchery and zimmah (licentiousness), not in merivah (strife) and quarreling and anochiyut (selfishness) and kinah (jealousy).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 5:16–17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:27"> But put on Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua Adoneinu and make no provision for the basar (old fallen nature), to satisfy its ta'avot (lusts).</span>
</div>
