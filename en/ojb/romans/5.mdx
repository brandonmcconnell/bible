<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="YESHAYAH 32:17; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 15:13; YOCHANAN 16:33"> Therefore, having been acquitted and declared not guilty, declared to be YITZDAK IM HASHEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Job 25:4">a</a></sup>on the yesod (basis) of our emunah (faith), we have shalom (peace) in relation to Hashem through Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua Adoneinu,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 12:12"> Through whom also we have HaSha'ar laHashem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ps 118:20">b</a></sup>(gate to appoach G-dʼs presence, access of the Tzaddikim) by emunah into this unmerited Chen v'Chesed in which we stand and glory in tikvah of the kavod Eloheinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:2–3; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 3:14"> Not only so, but we also glory in tzoros (troubles, afflictions), knowing that tzarah (trouble) produces savlanut (patience),</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 5:10; LETTER FROM YA'AKOV TO THE KEHILLAH 1:12; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:8–12"> Savlanut produces tested character and midos, and tested character and midos produce tikvah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 4:6; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 3:16–19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 1:20"> And tikvah does not in the end lead to our being meyayesh (despairing) in disillusionment and bushah (shame),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ps 25:3">c</a></sup>because the Ahavas Hashem (G-dʼs love) has been poured out in our levavot through the Ruach Hakodesh given to us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:1–5; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN COLOSSAE 2:13"> For while we were still helpless, Moshiach died for the resha'im (the unrighteous persons, the wicked), doing so at the appointed time!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Dan 9:24-26">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:13; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 16:4"> For only rarely will someone die for a tzaddik (righteous man); though efsher (perhaps) it is conceivable that someone will dare to die for the tzaddik.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="YOCHANAN 15:13; YOCHANAN 3:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:6"> But Hashem demonstrates His ahavah for us in that while we were still chote'im, Moshiach died for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 1:10; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:13"> How much more then, having now been acquitted and pronounced to be YITZDAK IM HASHEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Job 25:4">e</a></sup>on the basis of the Moshiachʼs DAHM (blood) and sacrificial death,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isa 53:11-12">f</a></sup>how much more then shall we be delivered through him from the eschatological Charon Af Hashem (burning anger of G-d)!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:18–19; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 4:10–11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:32"> For if when we were &#91;G-dʼs&#93; oyevim (enemies), we were reconciled to Hashem through the histalkus (passing), the mavet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 53:12; Dan 9:26">g</a></sup>(death) of the Zun fun der Oybershter,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Moshiach">h</a></sup>how much more, having been reconciled and no longer oyevim, shall we be delivered by his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Techiyah (Resurrection)">i</a></sup>Chayyim!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="(II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:18–19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:10; YESHAYAH 61:10"> Not only so, but we also glory in Hashem though Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua Adoneinu, through whom we have now received the ritztzuy (reconciliation, cessation of enmity, hostility between a wrathful holy G-d and sinful men).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:21–22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:19; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:23"> Therefore, just as through one Adam (one man, humanity, Adam), Chet (Sin) entered into the Olam Hazeh and, through Chet (Sin), entered Mavet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ro 1:32; Gn 2:16-17">j</a></sup>(Death), and so Mavet passed through to kol Bnei Adam (all Mankind, all the sons of Adam), because all sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:15; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:56; (I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 3:4"> For before &#91;the epoch of&#93; the Torah (Law), Chet (Sin, Chet Kadmon, original sin) was in HaOlam (HaZeh, the world). But Chet (Sin) is not accounted, recorded<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="to make charges for death penalties">k</a></sup>in the absence of Gezetz.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="i.e., no Law, no violation, death penalty">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:45; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:17; LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 9:27"> Nevertheless, Mavet (Death) reigned supreme from (the epoch of) Adam until (the epoch of) Moshe Rabbeinu, even over those who did not sin in the very same manner of Adamʼs averah (transgression, disobedience, commandment rebelled against and recorded for death penalty) — that is, Adam who is a tipus (pattern, prophetic type), a demut he'atid (a future figure) of Hu HaBah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Isa 59:20; Job 19:25">m</a></sup>(“He who comes,” Moshiach the Coming One, the Coming Go'el Redeemer).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:23; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:8; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:12"> But the averah (transgression) was not like the effect of unmerited chesed (grace). For if by the averah of the one, the rabbim (many) died, how much more the unmerited Chen v'Chesed Hashem (grace of G-d) and the matnat hachesed (free gift of grace) of the Adam HaEchad (one Man) Rebbe Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua have overflowed LARABBIM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Isa 53:11">n</a></sup>(to the many).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 13:38–39; YESHAYAH 1:18; YESHAYAH 44:22"> And the mattanah (free gift) is not like the effect of that one Adamʼs averah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Gn 3:6">o</a></sup>For the mishpat (judgment) is from one averah (transgression) to the gezar din (verdict) of ashem (guilty), to harsha'ah (condemnation as guilty); but the effect of the unmerited Chen v'Chesed Hashem is from averot rabbot (many transgressions) to zikkuy (acquittal), to that of being YITZDAK IM HASHEM<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Job 25:4">p</a></sup>(“justified with G-d,” i.e., acquittal, justification with Hashem of the Many).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Isa 53:11">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:21–22; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:49; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:12"> For if by the averah (transgression) of the one, Mavet (Death) reigned supreme through the one Adam, how much more those, who receive the abundance of unmerited Chen v'Chesed Hashem (grace) and of the Matnat HaTzedakah (the gift of righteousness), shall reign in life through the one Adam, Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 15:22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:12; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:15"> So, then, as through one Averah (transgression) &#91;of Adam&#93; to kol Bnei Adam to harsha'ah (condemnation as guilty), so also through one Mitzvah (righteous or worthy deed) &#91;of Moshiach&#93; to kol Bnei Adam to justification unto Chayei &#91;Olam&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:18; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:8"> For as through the disobedience of the one Adam, the many were made chote'im (sinners), so also through the mishma'at (obedience) of the one Adam,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Moshiach">r</a></sup>the many will be made tzaddikim (righteous ones).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Isa 53:11">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:1; LETTER TO TITOS 3:3–7; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 6:14"> The &#91;epoch of the&#93; Gezetz came to increase the averah (transgression); but where Chet (Sin, Chet Kadmon) increased, unmerited Chen v'Chesed Hashem overflowed in abundance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LETTER TO TITOS 2:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 5:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:10"> In order that as Chet (Sin) reigned in Mavet (Death), so also Chesed might reign through Tzedek Olamim to Chayyei Olam through Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua Adoneinu.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Dan 9:24">t</a></sup></span>
</div>
