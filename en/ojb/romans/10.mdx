<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="(I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 9:20–22; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:1–3; YOCHANAN 5:34"> Achim b'Moshiach, the great tshuka (desire, longing) of my lev and my tefillah to Hashem is for the Yeshu'at Yisroel (salvation of Israel).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 21:20; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:31–32; MISHLE 19:2"> For I can be meid (provide testimony, attest) regarding them that they have a kinat Hashem(zeal for G-d), but not in accordance with saving binah and da'as.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3:9; LUKAS 16:15; (II) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN CORINTH 5:21"> For, having no saving da'as of the Tzidkat Hashem(the righteousness of G-d), and seeking to establish their own,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="that is, self-attained">a</a></sup>they have not subjected themselves to the Tzidkat Hashem(righteousness of G-d).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1:17; 3:5,21,25-26; 6:18">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:24; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 8:3–4; MATTITYAHU 5:17–18"> For Moshiach is the goal of the Torah as a means to being YITZDAK IM HASHEM, for all who have emunah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="VAYIKRA 18:5; YECHEZKEL 20:11; NECHEMYAH 9:29"> For Moshe Rabbeinu writes with reference to the (accessibility of) Tzidkat Torah (righteousness which is from the Torah), The man YA'ASEH OTAM VACHAI<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Lv 18:5">c</a></sup>(who does these things will live) by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEVARIM 30:11–14; MISHLE 30:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 4:8–10"> Whereas the Tzidkat Emunah (righteousness which is from faith) speaks thus: Do not say<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="looking for Messianic salvation being merited by superhuman attainments in works">d</a></sup>in your lev, Who will go up into Shomayim? (that is, to bring Moshiach down)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LETTER TO MA'AMINIM YEHUDIM 13:20; HISGALUS 1:18; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 4:25"> Or, Who will go down into the abyss? (that is, to bring Moshiach up from the Mesim).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEVARIM 30:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:16–17; (I) LETTER TO TIMOTIYOS 4:6"> But what does it say? “The Dvar is near you, in your MOUTH and in your HEART.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dt 30:14">e</a></sup>That is, the Dvar of Emunah which we proclaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKAS 12:8; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 16:31; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 2:11"> Because if you make hoda'ah (confession) “with your PEH” of Adoneinu Yehoshua, and have emunah “in your LEV” that G-d raised him from the Mesim, you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Mt 16:15-18">f</a></sup>will be delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="(I) LETTER OF YOCHANAN TO THE KEHILLAH 4:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 10:9; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 2:16"> For with the “heart” one has emunah unto being YITZDAK IM HASHEM and with the “mouth” hoda'ah is made unto Yeshu'at Eloheinu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="YESHAYAH 28:16; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:33; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:6"> For the Kitvei Hakodesh says, Everyone who has emunah in Him shall not be put to shame.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isa 28:16">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 4:19; HISGALUS 17:14; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:28"> For there is no distinction between Yehudi and Yevani,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="cf. Ro 3:9">h</a></sup>for the same one is Adon Echad l'chulam (one L-rd over all), rich to all who call upon Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 2:21; YOEL 2:32"> For V'HAYAH KOL ASHER YIKRA B'SHEM ADONOI (Everyone whoever calls upon the Name of the L-rd — Yoel 3:5 &#91;2:32&#93;) shall be delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MARKOS 16:15–16; GEVUROT MEYRUACH HAKODESH 26:17–18; LETTER TO TITOS 1:3"> How therefore shall they call on him in whom they have not believed? And how shall they believe in him of whom they have not heard? And how shall they hear without someone doing the hatafah (preaching)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="YESHAYAH 52:7; NACHUM 1:15; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN EPHESUS 2:17"> And how shall they do the hatafah (preaching) unless they have been sent? As it is written, “How beautiful are the feet of those who preach Besuras HaGeulah” (Isa 52:7).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="YESHAYAH 53:1; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 3:3; YOCHANAN 12:38–40"> But not all have mishma'at (obedience) to the Besuras HaGeulah. For Yeshayah says: MI HE'EMIN LISHMU'ATEINU<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Isa 53:1">i</a></sup>(Who has believed that which is heard, our report)?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="cf. Dt 9:4">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN GALATIA 3:5; (I) LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN THESSALONIKA 2:13"> So, then, emunah comes from hearing, and hearing comes through the Dvar HaMoshiach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="TEHILLIM 19:4; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 1:8; MATTITYAHU 24:14"> But I say, is it the case that they have not heard? On the contrary: Their sound has gone out into all the earth and their words to the ends of the inhabited world (Ps 19:4).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEVARIM 32:21; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:11; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 11:14"> But I say, is it the case that Yisroel has not known? First, Moshe Rabbeinu says: I will provoke you to jealousy by those who are not a nation; by a senseless nation I will make you angry.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Dt 32:21">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="YESHAYAH 65:1–2; LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN ROME 9:30; YESHAYAH 55:4–5"> And Yeshayah HaNavi is bold as to say: I have been found by those who do not seek Me; I have revealed Myself to those who do not ask for Me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Isa 65:1">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="YESHAYAH 65:2–5; (I) LETTER OF SHIM'ON KEFA TO THE KEHILLAH 2:8; YIRMEYAH 35:15"> But concerning Yisroel he says: All the day I stretched out My hands to a disobedient and obstinate people.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Isa 65:2">m</a></sup></span>
