<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 8:4–15; 1 Thessalonians 4:9–10"> For concerning the ministration which &#91;is&#93; for the saints, it is superfluous my writing to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:10; 2 Corinthians 8:8; 2 Corinthians 8:24"> For I know your readiness, which I boast of as respects you to Macedonians, that Achaia is prepared since a year ago, and the zeal &#91;reported&#93; of you has stimulated the mass &#91;of the brethren&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; 2 Corinthians 7:14; Titus 3:1"> But I have sent the brethren, in order that our boasting about you may not be made void in this respect, in order that, as I have said, ye may be prepared;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 15:26; 2 Corinthians 11:17; 2 Corinthians 8:24"> lest haply, if Macedonians come with me and find you unprepared, we, that we say not ye, may be put to shame in this confidence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:11; 1 Samuel 25:27; Philippians 4:17"> I thought it necessary therefore to beg the brethren that they would come to you, and complete beforehand your fore-announced blessing, that this may be ready thus as blessing, and not as got out of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; Luke 6:38; Proverbs 22:9"> But this &#91;is true&#93;, he that sows sparingly shall reap also sparingly; and he that sows in &#91;the spirit of&#93; blessing shall reap also in blessing:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 20:35; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> each according as he is purposed in his heart; not grievingly, or of necessity; for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.7 Elohim">a</a></sup>God loves a cheerful giver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20; Philippians 4:18–19; Proverbs 10:22"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="9.8 Elohim">b</a></sup>God is able to make every gracious gift abound towards you, that, having in every way always all-sufficiency, ye may abound to every good work:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 112:9; Psalms 112:3; Proverbs 21:21"> according as it is written, He has scattered abroad, he has given to the poor, his righteousness remains for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 55:10; 2 Corinthians 9:6; Hosea 10:12"> Now he that supplies seed to the sower and bread for eating shall supply and make abundant your sowing, and increase the fruits of your righteousness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Chronicles 29:12–14; 1 Timothy 6:17–18"> enriched in every way unto all free-hearted liberality, which works through us thanksgiving to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="9.11 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14–15; Philemon 1:4–7; Philippians 2:25"> Because the ministration of this service is not only filling up the measure of what is lacking to the saints, but also abounding by many thanksgivings to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="9.12 Elohim">d</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Matthew 5:16; 2 Corinthians 2:12"> they glorifying<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="9.13 Elohim">e</a></sup>God through the proof of this ministration, by reason of your subjection, by profession, to the glad tidings of the Christ, and your free-hearted liberality in communicating towards them and towards all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:14; Philippians 4:1; Philippians 4:18–19"> and in their supplication for you, full of ardent desire for you, on account of the exceeding grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="9.14 Elohim">f</a></sup>God &#91;which is&#93; upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; Ephesians 5:20; John 3:16"> Thanks &#91;be&#93; to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="9.15 Elohim">g</a></sup>God for his unspeakable free gift.</span>
