<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 16:9; 2 Corinthians 9:2; 2 Corinthians 9:4"> But we make known to you, brethren, the grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="8.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God bestowed in the assemblies of Macedonia;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 2:5; Proverbs 11:25; 2 Corinthians 6:10"> that in a great trial of affliction the abundance of their joy and their deep poverty has abounded to the riches of their &#91;free-hearted&#93; liberality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:5; Exodus 35:21–22"> For according to &#91;their&#93; power, I bear witness, and beyond &#91;their&#93; power, &#91;they were&#93; willing of their own accord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> begging of us with much entreaty &#91;to give effect to&#93; the grace and fellowship of the service which &#91;was to be rendered&#93; to the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 12:1; Romans 14:7–9; 2 Chronicles 30:12"> And not according as we hoped, but they gave themselves first to the Lord, and to us by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="8.5 Elohim">b</a></sup>God's will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 2 Corinthians 8:4"> So that we begged Titus that, according as he had before begun, so he would also complete as to you this grace also;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8; 2 Peter 1:5–8; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> but even as ye abound in every way, in faith, and word, and knowledge, and all diligence, and in love from you to us, that ye may abound in this grace also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:6; 1 John 3:17–19; James 2:14–16"> I do not speak as commanding &#91;it&#93;, but through the zeal of others, and proving the genuineness of your love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Philippians 2:6–8; Matthew 20:28"> For ye know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that for your sakes he, being rich, became poor, in order that ye by his poverty might be enriched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18–19; Hebrews 13:16; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> And I give &#91;my&#93; opinion in this, for this is profitable for you who began before, not only to do, but also to be willing, a year ago.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:2"> But now also complete the doing of it; so that as &#91;there was&#93; the readiness to be willing, so also to complete out of what ye have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 12:42–44; 2 Corinthians 9:7; Luke 21:1–4"> For if the readiness be there, &#91;a man is&#93; accepted according to what he may have, not according to what he has not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 4:34; Romans 15:26–27"> For &#91;it is&#93; not in order that there may be ease for others, and for you distress,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:12; Acts 4:34"> but &#91;on the principle&#93; of equality; in the present time your abundance for their lack, that their abundance may be for your lack, so that there should be equality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:18; Luke 22:35"> According as it is written, He who &#91;gathered&#93; much had no excess, and he who &#91;gathered&#93; little was nothing short.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Jeremiah 32:40; 2 Corinthians 2:13–14"> But thanks &#91;be&#93; to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="8.16 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, who gives the same diligent zeal for you in the heart of Titus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 13:22; 2 Corinthians 8:10"> For he received indeed the entreaty, but, being full of zeal, he went of his own accord to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> but we have sent with him the brother whose praise &#91;is&#93; in the glad tidings through all the assemblies;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:3–4; 2 Corinthians 4:15; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> and not only &#91;so&#93;, but &#91;is&#93; also chosen by the assemblies as our fellow-traveller with this grace, ministered by us to the glory of the Lord himself, and &#91;a witness of&#93; our readiness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:22; Romans 14:16; Ephesians 5:15"> avoiding this, that any one should blame us in this abundance &#91;which is&#93; administered by us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Peter 2:12; Philippians 4:8"> for we provide for things honest, not only before &#91;the&#93; Lord, but also before men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 2:20–22"> And we have sent with them our brother whom we have often proved to be of diligent zeal in many things, and now more diligently zealous through the great confidence &#91;he has&#93; as to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Philemon 1:17; 2 Corinthians 8:6"> Whether as regards Titus, &#91;he is&#93; my companion and fellow-labourer in your behalf; or our brethren, &#91;they are&#93; deputed messengers of assemblies, Christ's glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:14; 2 Corinthians 7:4; 2 Corinthians 9:2–4"> Shew therefore to them, before the assemblies, the proof of your love, and of our boasting about you.</span>
