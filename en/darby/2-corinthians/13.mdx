<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:20; 2 Corinthians 12:12; Luke 21:15"> Since ye seek a proof of Christ speaking in me, (who is not weak towards you, but is powerful among you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:18; Romans 6:4; 2 Corinthians 10:3–4"> for if indeed he has been crucified in weakness, yet he lives by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="13.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God's power; for indeed we are weak in him, but we shall live with him by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="13.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>God's power towards you,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 Corinthians 11:28; Psalms 139:23–24"> examine your own selves if ye be in the faith; prove your own selves: do ye not recognise yourselves, that Jesus Christ is in you, unless indeed ye be reprobates?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:20; 2 Corinthians 13:10; 2 Corinthians 13:3–4"> Now I hope that ye will know that we are not reprobates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:12; 1 Timothy 2:2; 2 Corinthians 6:8–9"> But we pray to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="13.7 Elohim">c</a></sup>God that ye may do nothing evil; not that we may appear approved, but that ye may do what is right, and we be as reprobates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; Mark 9:39; Acts 4:28–30"> For we can do nothing against the truth, but for the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; 1 Corinthians 4:10; 1 Thessalonians 3:10"> For we rejoice when we may be weak and ye may be powerful. But this also we pray for, your perfecting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:8; 2 Corinthians 2:3; Titus 1:13"> On this account I write these things being absent, that being present I may not use severity according to the authority which the Lord has given me for building up, and not for overthrowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; Romans 12:16; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> For the rest, brethren, rejoice; be perfected; be encouraged; be of one mind; be at peace; and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="13.11 Elohim">d</a></sup>God of love and peace shall be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 16:16; 1 Peter 5:14; 1 Thessalonians 5:26"> Salute one another with a holy kiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 16:16; Romans 16:21–23; Hebrews 13:24"> All the saints salute you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 2:22; Romans 5:5; Romans 16:20"> The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="13.14 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, and the communion of the Holy Spirit, &#91;be&#93; with you all.</span>
