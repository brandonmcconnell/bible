<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9; Hebrews 12:15; 1 Peter 4:10"> But &#91;as&#93; fellow-workmen, we also beseech that ye receive not the grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="6.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God in vain:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 49:8; Hebrews 4:7; Hebrews 3:13"> (for he says, I have listened to thee in an accepted time, and I have helped thee in a day of salvation: behold, now &#91;is the&#93; well-accepted time; behold, now &#91;the&#93; day of salvation:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:13; 1 Corinthians 9:12; Matthew 17:27"> giving no manner of offence in anything, that the ministry be not blamed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:10; 2 Corinthians 4:8; 2 Corinthians 11:27"> but in everything commending ourselves as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="6.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>God's ministers, in much endurance, in afflictions, in necessities, in straits,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:23–25; 2 Corinthians 11:27; Acts 19:23–34"> in stripes, in prisons, in riots, in labours, in watchings, in fastings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Colossians 1:27; Galatians 5:22"> in pureness, in knowledge, in longsuffering, in kindness, in &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit, in love unfeigned,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Timothy 2:15; Ephesians 6:11–20"> in &#91;the&#93; word of truth, in &#91;the&#93; power of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="6.7 Elohim">c</a></sup>God; through the arms of righteousness on the right hand and left,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 27:63; 1 Peter 4:14; 1 Timothy 4:10"> through glory and dishonour, through evil report and good report: as deceivers, and true;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:36; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> as unknown, and well known; as dying, and behold, we live; as disciplined, and not put to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 16:22; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Revelation 2:9"> as grieved, but always rejoicing; as poor, but enriching many; as having nothing, and possessing all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:3–4; Philippians 1:8; Psalms 119:32"> Our mouth is opened to you, Corinthians, our heart is expanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:2; Micah 2:7; Philippians 1:8"> Ye are not straitened in us, but ye are straitened in your affections;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="3 John 1:4; 1 John 3:18; 1 Thessalonians 2:11"> but for an answering recompense, (I speak as to children,) let your heart also expand itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 10:21; James 4:4"> Be not diversely yoked with unbelievers; for what participation &#91;is there&#93; between righteousness and lawlessness? or what fellowship of light with darkness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; 1 Corinthians 10:20–21; 1 John 5:11–13"> and what consent of Christ with Beliar, or what part for a believer along with an unbeliever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 21:3; Exodus 29:45; Hebrews 8:10"> and what agreement of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="6.16 Elohim">d</a></sup>God's temple with idols? for ye are &#91;the&#93; living<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="6.16 Elohim">e</a></sup>God's temple; according as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="6.16 Elohim">f</a></sup>God has said, I will dwell among them, and walk among &#91;them&#93;; and I will be their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="6.16 Elohim">g</a></sup>God, and they shall be to me a people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Revelation 18:4; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> Wherefore come out from the midst of them, and be separated, saith &#91;the&#93; Lord, and touch not &#91;what is&#93; unclean, and I will receive you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:7; John 1:12; 2 Samuel 7:14"> and I will be to you for a Father, and ye shall be to me for sons and daughters, saith &#91;the&#93; Lord Almighty.</span>
