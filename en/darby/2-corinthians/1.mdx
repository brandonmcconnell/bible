<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:1; Philippians 1:1; Ephesians 1:1"> Paul, apostle of Jesus Christ by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God's will, and the brother Timotheus, to the assembly of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.1 Elohim">b</a></sup>God which is in Corinth, with all the saints who are in the whole of Achaia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Ephesians 6:23; Philippians 1:2"> Grace to you, and peace from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.2 Elohim">c</a></sup>God our Father, and &#91;the&#93; Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 1:3; 1 Peter 1:3; Romans 15:5–6"> Blessed &#91;be&#93; the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.3 Elohim">d</a></sup>God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of compassions, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.3 Elohim">e</a></sup>God of all encouragement;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Isaiah 51:12; 2 Thessalonians 2:16–17"> who encourages us in all our tribulation, that we may be able to encourage those who are in any tribulation whatever, through the encouragement with which we ourselves are encouraged of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.4 Elohim">f</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 1:24; Philippians 3:10; 2 Corinthians 4:10–11"> Because, even as the sufferings of the Christ abound towards us, so through the Christ does our encouragement also abound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; 2 Corinthians 1:4; 2 Corinthians 4:15–18"> But whether we are in tribulation, &#91;it is&#93; for your encouragement and salvation, wrought in the endurance of the same sufferings which we also suffer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:17–18; 2 Thessalonians 1:4–7; James 1:12"> (and our hope for you &#91;is&#93; sure;) or whether we are encouraged, &#91;it is&#93; for your encouragement and salvation: knowing that as ye are partakers of the sufferings, so also of the encouragement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:7–12; 1 Corinthians 15:32; 1 Corinthians 4:8"> For we do not wish you to be ignorant, brethren, as to our tribulation which happened &#91;to us&#93; in Asia, that we were excessively pressed beyond &#91;our&#93; power, so as to despair even of living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; Hebrews 11:19; 2 Corinthians 4:7"> But we ourselves had the sentence of death in ourselves, that we should not have our trust in ourselves, but in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="1.9 Elohim">g</a></sup>God who raises the dead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:10; Psalms 34:19; 2 Peter 2:9"> who has delivered us from so great a death, and does deliver; in whom we confide that he will also yet deliver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Philippians 1:19; 2 Corinthians 4:15; Acts 12:5"> ye also labouring together by supplication for us that the gift towards us, through means of many persons, may be the subject of the thanksgiving of many for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 1 Corinthians 2:13; James 3:13–18"> For our boasting is this, the testimony of our conscience, that in simplicity and sincerity before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="1.12 Elohim">h</a></sup>God, (not in fleshly wisdom but in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="1.12 Elohim">i</a></sup>God's grace,) we have had our conversation in the world, and more abundantly towards you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; Philemon 1:6; 2 Corinthians 13:6"> For we do not write other things to you but what ye well know and recognise; and I hope that ye will recognise to the end,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 2:16; 1 Corinthians 1:8; 1 Thessalonians 2:19–20"> even as also ye have recognised us in part, that we are your boast, even as ye &#91;are&#93; ours in the day of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:19; Romans 1:11; Romans 15:29"> And with this confidence I purposed to come to you previously, that ye might have a second favour;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:5–7; Acts 21:5; Acts 19:21–22"> and to pass through to Macedonia by you, and again from Macedonia to come to you, and to be set forward by you to Judaea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:37; James 5:12; 2 Corinthians 10:2–3"> Having therefore this purpose, did I then use lightness? Or what I purpose, do I purpose according to flesh, that there should be with me yea yea, and nay nay?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:9; Revelation 3:7; John 7:28"> Now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="1.18 Elohim">j</a></sup>God &#91;is&#93; faithful, that our word to you is not yea and nay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 13:8; Revelation 1:17; 2 John 1:9"> For the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="1.19 Elohim">k</a></sup>God, Jesus Christ, he who has been preached by us among you (by me and Silvanus and Timotheus), did not become yea and nay, but yea is in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 John 2:24–25; Galatians 3:22; Revelation 7:12"> For whatever promises of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="1.20 Elohim">l</a></sup>God &#91;there are&#93;, in him is the yea, and in him the amen, for glory to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="1.20 Elohim">m</a></sup>God by us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:27; 1 Peter 5:10; 1 John 2:20"> Now he that establishes us with you in Christ, and has anointed us, &#91;is&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="1.21 Elohim">n</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; 2 Corinthians 5:5; Ephesians 1:13–14"> who also has sealed us, and given the earnest of the Spirit in our hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 1:20; 1 Corinthians 4:21; Romans 1:9"> But I call<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="1.23 Elohim">o</a></sup>God to witness upon my soul that to spare you I have not yet come to Corinth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; 1 Corinthians 15:1; Philippians 1:25–26"> Not that we rule over your faith, but are fellow-workmen of your joy: for by faith ye stand.</span>
