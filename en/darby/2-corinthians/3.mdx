<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:11; 2 Corinthians 10:12; 2 Corinthians 5:12"> Do we begin again to commend ourselves? or do we need, as some, commendatory letters to you, or &#91;commendatory&#93; from you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:1–2; 1 Thessalonians 1:8; Romans 1:8"> Ye are our letter, written in our hearts, known and read of all men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Jeremiah 31:33; Hebrews 8:10"> being manifested to be Christ's epistle ministered by us, written, not with ink, but &#91;the&#93; Spirit of &#91;the&#93; living<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God; not on stone tables, but on fleshy tables of &#91;the&#93; heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Ephesians 3:12; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> And such confidence have we through the Christ towards<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9; Philippians 4:13; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> not that we are competent of ourselves to think anything as of ourselves, but our competency &#91;is&#93; of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.5 Elohim">c</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 6:63; Romans 7:6; Jeremiah 31:31"> who has also made us competent, &#91;as&#93; ministers of &#91;the&#93; new covenant; not of letter, but of spirit. For the letter kills, but the Spirit quickens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:9–11; Romans 10:4; Exodus 31:18"> (But if the ministry of death, in letters, graven in stones, began with glory, so that the children of Israel could not fix their eyes on the face of Moses, on account of the glory of his face, &#91;a glory&#93; which is annulled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:18; Galatians 3:14"> how shall not rather the ministry of the Spirit subsist in glory?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 3:21–22; Hebrews 12:18–21; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For if the ministry of condemnation &#91;be&#93; glory, much rather the ministry of righteousness abounds in glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 1:17; Haggai 2:7–9; Acts 26:13"> For also that &#91;which was&#93; glorified is not glorified in this respect, on account of the surpassing glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 5:20–21; Hebrews 8:13; Hebrews 7:21–25"> For if that annulled &#91;was introduced&#93; with glory, much rather that which abides &#91;subsists&#93; in glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:4; Acts 4:13; Philippians 1:20"> Having therefore such hope, we use much boldness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 34:33–35; Hebrews 10:1–9; Ephesians 2:14–15"> and not according as Moses put a veil on his own face, so that the children of Israel should not fix their eyes on the end of that annulled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3–4; Acts 26:18; Romans 11:25"> But their thoughts have been darkened, for unto this day the same veil remains in reading the old covenant, unremoved, which in Christ is annulled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 13:27–29"> But unto this day, when Moses is read, the veil lies upon their heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 34:34; Isaiah 29:18; Isaiah 25:7"> But when it shall turn to &#91;the&#93; Lord, the veil is taken away.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:2; Galatians 5:13; John 8:32"> Now the Lord is the Spirit, but where the Spirit of &#91;the&#93; Lord &#91;is, there is&#93; liberty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:6; Ephesians 4:22–24; Romans 12:2"> But we all, looking on the glory of the Lord, with unveiled face, are transformed according to the same image from glory to glory, even as by &#91;the&#93; Lord &#91;the&#93; Spirit.</span>
