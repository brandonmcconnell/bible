<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:12; 2 Corinthians 12:7; 1 John 5:20"> Well, it is not of profit to me to boast, for I will come to visions and revelations of &#91;the&#93; Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 22:17; Ezekiel 8:1–3; 1 Thessalonians 4:17"> I know a man in Christ, fourteen years ago, (whether in &#91;the&#93; body I know not, or out of the body I know not,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="12.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God knows;) such &#91;a one&#93; caught up to &#91;the&#93; third heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And I know such a man, (whether in &#91;the&#93; body or out of the body I know not,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="12.3 Elohim">b</a></sup>God knows;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 23:43; Revelation 2:7; Ezekiel 31:9"> that he was caught up into paradise, and heard unspeakable things said which it is not allowed to man to utter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:30; 1 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Of such &#91;a one&#93; I will boast, but of myself I will not boast, unless in my weaknesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:16; 2 Corinthians 11:31; 2 Corinthians 12:11"> For if I shall desire to boast, I shall not be a fool; for I will say &#91;the&#93; truth; but I forbear, lest any one should think as to me above what he sees me &#91;to be&#93;, or whatever he may hear of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:55; Judges 2:3; 2 Chronicles 32:25–26"> And that I might not be exalted by the exceeding greatness of the revelations, there was given to me a thorn for the flesh, a messenger of Satan that he might buffet me, that I might not be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Matthew 26:39–44; 1 Samuel 15:11"> For this I thrice besought the Lord that it might depart from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 12:10; Isaiah 40:29–31"> And he said to me, My grace suffices thee; for &#91;my&#93; power is perfected in weakness. Most gladly therefore will I rather boast in my weaknesses, that the power of the Christ may dwell upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9; Ephesians 6:10; Romans 5:3"> Wherefore I take pleasure in weaknesses, in insults, in necessities, in persecutions, in straits, for Christ: for when I am weak, then I am powerful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:5; 2 Corinthians 11:1; 1 Corinthians 15:8–10"> I have become a fool; ye have compelled me; for I ought to have been commended by you; for I have been nothing behind those who were in surpassing degree apostles, if also I am nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:18–19; 2 Corinthians 11:4; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> The signs indeed of the apostle were wrought among you in all endurance, signs, and wonders, and works of power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Corinthians 9:6; 2 Corinthians 11:7–9"> For in what is it that ye have been inferior to the other assemblies, unless that I myself have not been in laziness a charge upon you? Forgive me this injury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 19:14; Genesis 24:35–36; 1 Corinthians 10:33"> Behold, this third time I am ready to come to you, and I will not be in laziness a charge; for I do not seek yours, but you; for the children ought not to lay up for the parents, but the parents for the children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:8; 2 Corinthians 1:6; Philippians 2:17"> Now I shall most gladly spend and be utterly spent for your souls, if even in abundantly loving you I should be less loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:5; 2 Corinthians 10:2–3; 2 Corinthians 12:13"> But be it so. I did not burden you, but being crafty I took you by guile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:5; 1 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Kings 5:20–27"> Did I make gain of you by any of those whom I have sent to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; Romans 4:12; Acts 20:33–35"> I begged Titus, and sent the brother with &#91;him&#93;: did Titus at all make gain of you? have we not walked in the same spirit? &#91;have we&#93; not in the same steps?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 9:1; 1 Corinthians 14:26; 2 Corinthians 10:8"> Ye have long been supposing that we excuse ourselves to you: we speak before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="12.19 Elohim">c</a></sup>God in Christ; and all things, beloved, for your building up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 5:19–21; 1 Peter 2:1; Romans 1:29"> For I fear lest perhaps coming I find you not such as I wish, and that I be found by you such as ye do not wish: lest &#91;there might be&#93; strifes, jealousies, angers, contentions, evil speakings, whisperings, puffings up, disturbances;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:2; 1 Peter 4:2–3; Colossians 3:5"> lest my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="12.21 Elohim">d</a></sup>God should humble me as to you when I come again, and that I shall grieve over many of those who have sinned before, and have not repented as to the uncleanness and fornication and licentiousness which they have practised.</span>
