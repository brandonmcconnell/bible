<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:4; 1 Corinthians 1:21; 2 Corinthians 11:19"> Would that ye would bear with me &#91;in&#93; a little folly; but indeed bear with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 2:19–20; Ephesians 5:26–27; Isaiah 54:5"> For I am jealous as to you with a jealousy &#91;which is&#93; of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="11.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God; for I have espoused you unto one man, to present &#91;you&#93; a chaste virgin to Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Galatians 1:6; Ephesians 4:14"> But I fear lest by any means, as the serpent deceived Eve by his craft, &#91;so&#93; your thoughts should be corrupted from simplicity as to the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 1:6–8; 1 Corinthians 3:11; 1 Timothy 2:5"> For if indeed he that comes preaches another Jesus, whom we have not preached, or ye get a different Spirit, which ye have not got, or a different glad tidings, which ye have not received, ye might well bear with &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:11–12; 1 Corinthians 15:10; Galatians 2:6–9"> For I reckon that in nothing I am behind those who are in surpassing degree apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 3:4; 1 Corinthians 1:17; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> But if &#91;I am&#93; a simple person in speech, yet not in knowledge, but in everything making &#91;the truth&#93; manifest in all things to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; 2 Corinthians 10:1"> Have I committed sin, abasing myself in order that ye might be exalted, because I gratuitously announced to you the glad tidings of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="11.7 Elohim">b</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:18; 2 Corinthians 11:9; Philippians 4:14–16"> I spoiled other assemblies, receiving hire for ministry towards you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:8–9; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> And being present with you and lacking, I did not lazily burden any one, (for the brethren who came from Macedonia supplied what I lacked,) and in everything I kept myself from being a burden to you, and will keep myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:1; Romans 1:9; Acts 18:12"> &#91;The&#93; truth of Christ is in me that this boasting shall not be stopped as to me in the regions of Achaia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:15; 2 Corinthians 6:11–12; 2 Corinthians 7:3"> Why? because I do not love you?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="11.11 Elohim">c</a></sup>God knows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Corinthians 5:6; 2 Corinthians 5:12"> But what I do, I will also do, that I may cut off the opportunity of those wishing &#91;for&#93; an opportunity, that wherein they boast they may be found even as we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 1:7; Revelation 2:2; 1 John 4:1"> For such &#91;are&#93; false apostles, deceitful workers, transforming themselves into apostles of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 1:8; 2 Corinthians 2:11; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> And &#91;it is&#93; not wonderful, for Satan himself transforms himself into an angel of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Revelation 9:11; Isaiah 9:14–15"> It is no great thing therefore if his ministers also transform themselves as ministers of righteousness; whose end shall be according to their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Corinthians 12:6; 2 Corinthians 12:11"> Again I say, Let not any one think me to be a fool; but if otherwise, receive me then even as a fool, that I also may boast myself some little.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:12; Philippians 3:3–6; 2 Corinthians 9:4"> What I speak I do not speak according to &#91;the&#93; Lord, but as in folly, in this confidence of boasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:10; 2 Corinthians 11:21–23; 2 Corinthians 10:12–18"> Since many boast according to flesh, I also will boast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:10; 1 Corinthians 10:15; 1 Corinthians 8:1"> For ye bear fools readily, being wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 2:4; Galatians 4:3; Galatians 5:1"> For ye bear if any one bring you into bondage, if any one devour &#91;you&#93;, if any one get &#91;your money&#93;, if any one exalt himself, if any one beat you on the face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:10; 2 Corinthians 11:17; 2 Corinthians 10:1–2"> I speak as to dishonour, as though we had been weak; but wherein any one is daring, (I speak in folly,) I also am daring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 3:5; Romans 11:1; Romans 9:4"> Are they Hebrews? I also. Are they Israelites? I also. Are they seed of Abraham? I also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:4–5; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10; 2 Corinthians 6:9"> Are they ministers of Christ? (I speak as being beside myself) I above measure &#91;so&#93;; in labours exceedingly abundant, in stripes to excess, in prisons exceedingly abundant, in deaths oft.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 13:9; Deuteronomy 25:2–3; Matthew 10:17"> From the Jews five times have I received forty &#91;stripes&#93;, save one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 16:37; Hebrews 11:37"> Thrice have I been scourged, once I have been stoned, three times I have suffered shipwreck, a night and day I passed in the deep:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 14:5; Acts 17:5; Acts 13:50"> in journeyings often, in perils of rivers, in perils of robbers, in perils from &#91;my own&#93; race, in perils from &#91;the&#93; nations, in perils in &#91;the&#93; city, in perils in &#91;the&#93; desert, in perils on &#91;the&#93; sea, in perils among false brethren;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Philippians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 6:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:9"> in labour and toil, in watchings often, in hunger and thirst, in fastings often, in cold and nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; Romans 15:16; Acts 20:18–35"> Besides those things that are without, the crowd &#91;of cares&#93; pressing on me daily, the burden of all the assemblies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:13; 1 Corinthians 9:22; Galatians 4:8–20"> Who is weak, and I am not weak? Who is stumbled, and I burn not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:5–11; Proverbs 25:27; 1 Corinthians 2:3"> If it is needful to boast, I will boast in the things which concern my infirmity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 9:5; Ephesians 1:3; John 10:30"> The<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="11.31 Elohim">d</a></sup>God and Father of the Lord Jesus knows — he who is blessed for ever — that I do not lie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 9:24–25; 2 Corinthians 11:26"> In Damascus the ethnarch of Aretas the king kept the city of the Damascenes shut up, wishing to take me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 9:25; 1 Samuel 19:12; Joshua 2:18"> and through a window in a basket I was let down by the wall, and escaped his hands.</span>
