<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 19:25–26; 1 John 3:2; 2 Peter 1:13–14"> For we know that if our earthly tabernacle house be destroyed, we have a building from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, a house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 8:23; 1 Corinthians 15:53–54; 2 Corinthians 5:3–4"> For indeed in this we groan, ardently desiring to have put on our house which &#91;is&#93; from heaven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:7–11; Revelation 16:15; Revelation 3:18"> if indeed being also clothed we shall not be found naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:53–54; 2 Corinthians 5:2; Isaiah 25:8"> For indeed we who are in the tabernacle groan, being burdened; while yet we do not wish to be unclothed, but clothed, that &#91;what is&#93; mortal may be swallowed up by life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:22; Ephesians 1:13–14; Romans 8:23"> Now he that has wrought us for this very thing &#91;is&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.5 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, who also has given to us the earnest of the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:1; Philippians 3:20–21; Hebrews 11:13"> Therefore &#91;we are&#93; always confident, and know that while present in the body we are absent from the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:1–27; 2 Corinthians 4:18; Romans 8:24–25"> (for we walk by faith, not by sight;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:20–24; 2 Corinthians 5:6; John 14:3"> we are confident, I say, and pleased rather to be absent from the body and present with the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; 2 Peter 3:14; Romans 14:18"> Wherefore also we are zealous, whether present or absent, to be agreeable to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Revelation 22:12; Ephesians 6:8"> For we must all be manifested before the judgment-seat of the Christ, that each may receive the things &#91;done&#93; in the body, according to those he has done, whether &#91;it be&#93; good or evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jude 1:23; Hebrews 10:31; 2 Timothy 2:24–26"> Knowing therefore the terror of the Lord we persuade men, but have been manifested to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.11 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, and I hope also that we have been manifested in your consciences.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:1; 2 Corinthians 1:14; Galatians 6:12–14"> &#91;For&#93; we do not again commend ourselves to you, but &#91;we are&#93; giving to you occasion of boast in our behalf, that ye may have &#91;such&#93; with those boasting in countenance, and not in heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:16–17; 2 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Corinthians 12:6"> For whether we are beside ourselves, &#91;it is&#93; to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="5.13 Elohim">d</a></sup>God; or are sober, &#91;it is&#93; for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; Romans 14:7–9; Matthew 10:37–38"> For the love of the Christ constrains us, having judged this: that one died for all, then all have died;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 14:7–9; 1 Peter 4:2–4; 1 Peter 1:14–15"> and he died for all, that they who live should no longer live to themselves, but to him who died for them and has been raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Philippians 3:7–8; John 6:63"> So that we henceforth know no one according to flesh; but if even we have known Christ according to flesh, yet now we know &#91;him thus&#93; no longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26; Ephesians 4:22–24; Isaiah 43:18–19"> So if any one &#91;be&#93; in Christ, &#91;there is&#93; a new creation; the old things have passed away; behold all things have become new:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:20–21; 2 Corinthians 5:19–20; Romans 5:10–11"> and all things &#91;are&#93; of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="5.18 Elohim">e</a></sup>God who has reconciled us to himself by &#91;Jesus&#93; Christ, and given to us the ministry of that reconciliation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 44:22; 1 John 2:1–2"> how that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="5.19 Elohim">f</a></sup>God was in Christ, reconciling the world to himself, not reckoning to them their offences; and putting in us the word of that reconciliation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; Luke 14:23; Malachi 2:7"> We are ambassadors therefore for Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="5.20 Elohim">g</a></sup>God as &#91;it were&#93; beseeching by us, we entreat for Christ, Be reconciled to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="5.20 Elohim">h</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 2:22–24; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 5:19"> Him who knew not sin he has made sin for us, that we might become<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="5.21 Elohim">i</a></sup>God's righteousness in him.</span>
</div>
