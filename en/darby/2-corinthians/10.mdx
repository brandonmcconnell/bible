<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:10; Matthew 11:29; Galatians 5:2"> But I myself, Paul, entreat you by the meekness and gentleness of the Christ, who, as to appearance, &#91;when present&#93; &#91;am&#93; mean among you, but absent am bold towards you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:10; 2 Corinthians 13:2; Galatians 5:16–25"> but I beseech that present I may not be bold with the confidence with which I think to be daring towards some who think of us as walking according to flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; Galatians 2:20; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> For walking in flesh, we do not war according to flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:7; Ephesians 6:13–18; Jeremiah 1:10"> For the arms of our warfare &#91;are&#93; not fleshly, but powerful according to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="10.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God to &#91;the&#93; overthrow of strongholds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Isaiah 55:7; 1 Peter 1:14–15"> overthrowing reasonings and every high thing that lifts itself up against the knowledge of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="10.5 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, and leading captive every thought into the obedience of the Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:9; Numbers 16:26–30; 2 Corinthians 7:15"> and having in readiness to avenge all disobedience when your obedience shall have been fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:12; John 7:24; 1 Corinthians 14:37"> Do ye look at what concerns appearance? If any one has confidence in himself that he is of Christ, let him think this again in himself, that even as he &#91;is&#93; of Christ, so also &#91;are&#93; we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:10; 2 Corinthians 12:6; Galatians 1:1"> For and if I should boast even somewhat more abundantly of our authority, which the Lord has given &#91;to us&#93; for building up and not for your overthrowing, I shall not be put to shame;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:10; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> that I may not seem as if I was frightening you by letters:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:17; Exodus 4:10; Galatians 4:13–14"> because his letters, he says, &#91;are&#93; weighty and strong, but his presence in the body weak, and his speech naught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:20; 2 Corinthians 13:2–3; 2 Corinthians 13:10"> Let such a one think this, that such as we are in word by letters &#91;when&#93; absent, such also present in deed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; Proverbs 26:12; Luke 18:11"> For we dare not class ourselves or compare ourselves with some who commend themselves; but these, measuring themselves by themselves, and comparing themselves with themselves, are not intelligent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 12:6; Romans 15:20; 1 Peter 4:10"> Now we will not boast out of measure, but according to the measure of the rule which the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="10.13 Elohim">c</a></sup>God of measure has apportioned to us, to reach to you also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Timothy 1:11; Romans 1:16"> For we do not, as not reaching to you, overstretch ourselves, (for we have come to you also in the glad tidings of the Christ;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:20; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 2 Corinthians 10:13"> not boasting out of measure in other people's labours, but having hope, your faith increasing, to be enlarged amongst you, according to our rule, yet more abundantly</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 15:24–28; Acts 19:21"> to announce the glad tidings to that &#91;which is&#93; beyond you, not to be boasting in another's rule of things made ready to hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; 1 Corinthians 1:31; James 1:9–10"> But he that boasts, let him boast in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Proverbs 21:2; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> For not he that commends himself is approved, but whom the Lord commends.</span>
