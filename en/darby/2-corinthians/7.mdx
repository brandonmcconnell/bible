<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:7; 1 Peter 2:11; Proverbs 8:13"> Having therefore these promises, beloved, let us purify ourselves from every pollution of flesh and spirit, perfecting holiness in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="7.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God's fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 20:33; 2 Corinthians 6:12–13; 2 Corinthians 11:9"> Receive us: we have injured no one, we have ruined no one, we have made gain of no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:11–12; 1 Thessalonians 2:8; 2 Corinthians 11:11"> I do not speak for condemnation, for I have already said that ye are in our hearts, to die together, and live together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; 2 Corinthians 3:12; James 1:2"> Great &#91;is&#93; my boldness towards you, great my exulting in respect of you; I am filled with encouragement; I overabound in joy under all our affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:13; Deuteronomy 32:25; Acts 20:1"> For indeed, when we came into Macedonia, our flesh had no rest, but &#91;we were&#93; afflicted in every way; without combats, within fears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 2:16–17; 2 Corinthians 7:13"> But he who encourages those that are &#91;brought&#93; low, &#91;even&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="7.6 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, encouraged us by the coming of Titus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:2; Jude 1:3; 2 Corinthians 8:16"> and not by his coming only, but also through the encouragement with which he was encouraged as to you; relating to us your ardent desire, your mourning, your zeal for me; so that I the more rejoiced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Jeremiah 20:7–9; Matthew 26:21–22"> For if also I grieved you in the letter, I do not regret &#91;it&#93;, if even I have regretted it; for I see that that letter, if even &#91;it were&#93; only for a time, grieved you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 15:7; Jeremiah 31:18–20; 2 Corinthians 7:10–11"> Now I rejoice, not that ye have been grieved, but that ye have been grieved to repentance; for ye have been grieved according to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="7.9 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, that in nothing ye might be injured by us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Acts 11:18; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> For grief according to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="7.10 Elohim">d</a></sup>God works repentance to salvation, never to be regretted; but the grief of the world works death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 14:18; 1 Timothy 5:21–22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> For, behold, this same thing, your being grieved according to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="7.11 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, how much diligence it wrought in you, but &#91;what&#93; excusing &#91;of yourselves&#93;, but &#91;what&#93; indignation, but &#91;what&#93; fear, but &#91;what&#93; ardent desire, but &#91;what&#93; zeal, but &#91;what&#93; vengeance: in every way ye have proved yourselves to be pure in the matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:9; 1 Timothy 3:5; 2 Corinthians 2:17"> So then, if also I wrote to you, &#91;it was&#93; not for the sake of him that injured, nor for the sake of him that was injured, but for the sake of our diligent zeal for you being manifested to you before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="7.12 Elohim">f</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:6; 1 Corinthians 12:26; 1 Corinthians 16:13"> For this reason we have been encouraged. And we the rather rejoiced in our encouragement more abundantly by reason of the joy of Titus, because his spirit has been refreshed by you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:4; 2 Thessalonians 1:4; 2 Corinthians 1:18–20"> Because if I boasted to him anything about you, I have not been put to shame; but as we have spoken to you all things in truth, so also our boasting to Titus has been &#91;the&#93; truth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; 2 Corinthians 2:9; 1 John 3:17"> and his affections are more abundantly towards you, calling to mind the obedience of you all, how with fear and trembling ye received him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:3; Philemon 1:8; Philemon 1:21"> I rejoice that in everything I am confident as to you.</span>
