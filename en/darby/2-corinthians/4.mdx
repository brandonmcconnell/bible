<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:13; Galatians 6:9; Ephesians 3:13"> Therefore, having this ministry, as we have had mercy shewn us, we faint not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 1 Thessalonians 2:3–5; 2 Corinthians 5:11"> But we have rejected the hidden things of shame, not walking in deceit, nor falsifying the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, but by manifestation of the truth commending ourselves to every conscience of men before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:14; 1 Corinthians 1:18; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> But if also our gospel is veiled, it is veiled in those that are lost;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:40; 2 Corinthians 3:14; Acts 26:18"> in whom the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.4 Elohim">c</a></sup>god of this world has blinded the thoughts of the unbelieving, so that the radiancy of the glad tidings of the glory of the Christ, who is &#91;the&#93; image of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.4 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, should not shine forth &#91;for them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:24; 1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Thessalonians 2:5–6"> For we do not preach ourselves, but Christ Jesus Lord, and ourselves your bondmen for Jesus' sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 60:2; Genesis 1:3; 1 Peter 2:9"> Because &#91;it is&#93; the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="4.6 Elohim">e</a></sup>God who spoke that out of darkness light should shine who has shone in our hearts for the shining forth of the knowledge of the glory of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="4.6 Elohim">f</a></sup>God in &#91;the&#93; face of &#91;Jesus&#93; Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5–6; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Ephesians 2:8–9"> But we have this treasure in earthen vessels, that the surpassingness of the power may be of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="4.7 Elohim">g</a></sup>God, and not from us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 1:2–4; Romans 8:35–37; 1 Peter 4:12–14"> every way afflicted, but not straitened; seeing no apparent issue, but our way not entirely shut up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Micah 7:8; Psalms 37:24–25"> persecuted, but not abandoned; cast down, but not destroyed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 6:5; 2 Timothy 2:11; 1 Peter 4:13"> always bearing about in the body the dying of Jesus, that the life also of Jesus may be manifested in our body;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:49; Romans 8:36; Psalms 44:22"> for we who live are always delivered unto death on account of Jesus, that the life also of Jesus may be manifested in our mortal flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:9; 1 Corinthians 4:10; Acts 20:24"> so that death works in us, but life in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 116:10; Hebrews 11:1–40; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> And having the same spirit of faith, according to what is written, I have believed, therefore have I spoken; we also believe, therefore also we speak;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14; Romans 8:11; Jude 1:24"> knowing that he who has raised the Lord Jesus shall raise us also with Jesus, and shall present &#91;us&#93; with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Ephesians 3:20–21; 2 Corinthians 9:11–12"> For all things &#91;are&#93; for your sakes, that the grace abounding through the many may cause thanksgiving to abound to the glory of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="4.15 Elohim">h</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Colossians 3:10; Isaiah 40:29"> Wherefore we faint not; but if indeed our outward man is consumed, yet the inward is renewed day by day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:18; 1 Peter 5:10; Romans 5:3–5"> For our momentary &#91;and&#93; light affliction works for us in surpassing measure an eternal weight of glory;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:7; Romans 8:24–25; Hebrews 11:1"> while we look not at the things that are seen, but at the things that are not seen; for the things that are seen &#91;are&#93; for a time, but those that are not seen eternal.</span>
