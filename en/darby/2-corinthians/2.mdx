<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:23; 2 Corinthians 12:20–21; 2 Corinthians 13:10"> But I have judged this with myself, not to come back to you in grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:8; 2 Corinthians 1:14; 1 Corinthians 12:26"> For if I grieve you, who also &#91;is&#93; it that gladdens me, if not he that is grieved through me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 5:10; 2 Corinthians 8:22; Philemon 1:21"> And I have written this very &#91;letter&#93; &#91;to you&#93;, that coming I may not have grief from those from whom I ought to have joy; trusting in you all that my joy is &#91;that&#93; of you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:12; Proverbs 27:5–6; Leviticus 19:17–18"> For out of much tribulation and distress of heart I wrote to you, with many tears; not that ye may be grieved, but that ye may know the love which I have very abundantly towards you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:12–13; Galatians 4:12; Proverbs 17:25"> But if any one has grieved, he has grieved, not me, but in part (that I may not overcharge &#91;you&#93;) all of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:4–5; 1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Corinthians 7:11"> Sufficient to such a one &#91;is&#93; this rebuke which &#91;has been inflicted&#93; by the many;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Galatians 6:1–2; Colossians 3:13"> so that on the contrary ye should rather shew grace and encourage, lest perhaps such a one should be swallowed up with excessive grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:22–23; Galatians 6:1–2; Galatians 6:10"> Wherefore I exhort you to assure him of &#91;your&#93; love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:6; Philemon 1:21; Philippians 2:22"> For to this end also I have written, that I might know, by putting you to the test, if as to everything ye are obedient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 20:23; Matthew 18:18; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> But to whom ye forgive anything, I also; for I also, what I have forgiven, if I have forgiven anything, &#91;it is&#93; for your sakes in &#91;the&#93; person of Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Ephesians 6:11–12; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> that we might not have Satan get an advantage against us, for we are not ignorant of his thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 16:8; Acts 14:27; Colossians 4:3"> Now when I came to Troas for the &#91;publication of the&#93; glad tidings of the Christ, a door also being opened to me in &#91;the&#93; Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:5–6; 2 Corinthians 12:18; Titus 1:4"> I had no rest in my spirit at not finding Titus my brother; but bidding them adieu, I came away to Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Romans 8:37; 2 Corinthians 2:15–16"> But thanks &#91;be&#93; to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.14 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, who always leads us in triumph in the Christ, and makes manifest the odour of his knowledge through us in every place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 1:18; Ezekiel 20:41"> For we are a sweet odour of Christ to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.15 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, in the saved and in those that perish:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5–6; John 9:39; 1 Peter 2:7–8"> to the one an odour from death unto death, but to the others an odour from life unto life; and who &#91;is&#93; sufficient for these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Corinthians 1:12; 2 Peter 2:1–3"> For we do not, as the many, make a trade of the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.17 Elohim">c</a></sup>God; but as of sincerity, but as of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.17 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2.17 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, we speak in Christ.</span>
