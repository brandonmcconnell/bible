<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 26:12; 1 Corinthians 8:2; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> For if any man reputes himself to be something, being nothing, he deceives himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:5; Psalms 26:2; 1 Corinthians 11:28"> but let each prove his own work, and then he will have his boast in what belongs to himself alone, and not in what belongs to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:19; Romans 14:10–12; 2 Corinthians 5:10–11"> For each shall bear his own burden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:17–18; Romans 15:27; Deuteronomy 12:19"> Let him that is taught in the word communicate to him that teaches in all good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6; Hosea 10:12; Job 4:8"> Be not deceived:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="6.7 Elohim">a</a></sup>God is not mocked; for whatever a man shall sow, that also shall he reap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:13; James 3:18; 2 Peter 2:19"> For he that sows to his own flesh, shall reap corruption from the flesh; but he that sows to the Spirit, from the Spirit shall reap eternal life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 2 Thessalonians 3:13; Isaiah 40:30–31"> but let us not lose heart in doing good; for in due time, if we do not faint, we shall reap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 3:27; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> So then, as we have occasion, let us do good towards all, and specially towards those of the household of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:21–23; Romans 16:22"> See how long a letter I have written to you with my own hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:11; Colossians 2:23; Acts 15:1"> As many as desire to have a fair appearance in &#91;the&#93; flesh, these compel you to be circumcised, only that they may not be persecuted because of the cross of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 5:6; Romans 2:17–25"> For neither do they that are circumcised themselves keep the law; but they wish you to be circumcised, that they may boast in your flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Jeremiah 9:23–24; Galatians 2:20"> But far be it from me to boast save in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom &#91;the&#93; world is crucified to me, and I to the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Colossians 3:10–11; Ephesians 2:10"> For &#91;in Christ Jesus&#93; neither is circumcision anything, nor uncircumcision; but new creation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Romans 9:6–8; Philippians 3:3"> And as many as shall walk by this rule, peace upon them and mercy, and upon the Israel of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="6.16 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:5; Colossians 1:24; 2 Corinthians 11:23–25"> For the rest let no one trouble me, for I bear in my body the brands of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 16:20; 2 Timothy 4:22; Philemon 1:25"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ &#91;be&#93; with your spirit, brethren. Amen.</span>
