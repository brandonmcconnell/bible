<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 5:7–8; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Galatians 1:6"> O senseless Galatians, who has bewitched you; to whom, as before your very eyes, Jesus Christ has been portrayed, crucified &#91;among you&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:16–17; Galatians 3:14; Acts 15:8"> This only I wish to learn of you, Have ye received the Spirit on the principle of works of law, or of &#91;the&#93; report of faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 5:4–8; Galatians 4:7–10; Hebrews 7:16–19"> Are ye so senseless? having begun in Spirit, are ye going to be made perfect in flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 15:2; Hebrews 10:32–39"> Have ye suffered so many things in vain, if indeed also in vain?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 3:2; Acts 14:9–10; Acts 19:11–12"> He therefore who ministers to you the Spirit, and works miracles among you, &#91;is it&#93; on the principle of works of law, or of &#91;the&#93; report of faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Romans 4:21–22; James 2:23"> Even as Abraham believed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.6 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, and it was reckoned to him as righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:9; Galatians 3:26–29"> Know then that they that are on the principle of faith, these are Abraham's sons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 28:14"> and the scripture, foreseeing that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.8 Elohim">b</a></sup>God would justify the nations on the principle of faith, announced beforehand the glad tidings to Abraham: In thee all the nations shall be blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 4:16; Galatians 3:29; Romans 4:24"> So that they who are on the principle of faith are blessed with believing Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:26; Romans 8:7; Romans 3:19–20"> For as many as are on the principle of works of law are under curse. For it is written, Cursed is every one who does not continue in all things which &#91;are&#93; written in the book of the law to do them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:4; Romans 1:17; Hebrews 10:38"> but that by law no one is justified with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.11 Elohim">c</a></sup>God &#91;is&#93; evident, because The just shall live on the principle of faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Romans 10:5–6; Nehemiah 9:29"> but the law is not on the principle of faith; but, He that shall have done these things shall live by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Deuteronomy 21:23; Hebrews 9:12"> Christ has redeemed us out of the curse of the law, having become a curse for us, (for it is written, Cursed &#91;is&#93; every one hanged upon a tree,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 3:28–29; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Ezekiel 36:26–27"> that the blessing of Abraham might come to the nations in Christ Jesus, that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 9:17; Romans 6:19; Romans 3:5"> Brethren, (I speak according to man,) even man's confirmed covenant no one sets aside, or adds other dispositions to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 4:13; Luke 1:55"> But to Abraham were the promises addressed, and to his seed: he does not say, And to seeds, as of many; but as of one, And to thy seed; which is Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39–40; Genesis 15:13; Romans 4:13–14"> Now I say this, A covenant confirmed beforehand by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.17 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, the law, which took place four hundred and thirty years after, does not annul, so as to make the promise of no effect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12–15; Galatians 2:21; Romans 4:13–16"> For if the inheritance &#91;be&#93; on the principle of law, &#91;it is&#93; no longer on the principle of promise; but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.18 Elohim">e</a></sup>God gave it in grace to Abraham by promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:53; Romans 7:7–13; Hebrews 2:2"> Why then the law? It was added for the sake of transgressions, until the seed came to whom the promise was made, ordained through angels in &#91;the&#93; hand of a mediator.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:5; Deuteronomy 6:4; Job 9:33"> But a mediator is not of one, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="3.20 Elohim">f</a></sup>God is one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Romans 9:31; Romans 3:20–22"> &#91;Is&#93; then the law against the promises of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="3.21 Elohim">g</a></sup>God? Far be the thought. For if a law had been given able to quicken, then indeed righteousness were on the principle of law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 11:32; Hebrews 9:15; Galatians 3:23"> but the scripture has shut up all things under sin, that the promise, on the principle of faith of Jesus Christ, should be given to those that believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 5:18; Galatians 3:24–25; Galatians 3:19"> But before faith came, we were guarded under law, shut up to faith &#91;which was&#93; about to be revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 10:4; Acts 13:38–39; Romans 7:24–25"> So that the law has been our tutor up to Christ, that we might be justified on the principle of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:4; Hebrews 10:15–18"> But, faith having come, we are no longer under a tutor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 6:18; John 1:12–13"> for ye are all<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="3.26 Elohim">h</a></sup>God's sons by faith in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 13:14; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Romans 6:3–4"> For ye, as many as have been baptised unto Christ, have put on Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:12–13; Colossians 3:11; Romans 3:29–30"> There is no Jew nor Greek; there is no bondman nor freeman; there is no male and female; for ye are all one in Christ Jesus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:7"> but if ye &#91;are&#93; of Christ, then ye are Abraham's seed, heirs according to promise.</span>
