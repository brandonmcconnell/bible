<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 2:3; Acts 14:12; 2 Corinthians 8:23"> Then after a lapse of fourteen years I went up again to Jerusalem with Barnabas, taking Titus also with &#91;me&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 2:6; Philippians 2:16; 1 Thessalonians 3:5"> and I went up according to revelation, and I laid before them the glad tidings which I preach among the nations, but privately to those conspicuous &#91;among them&#93;, lest in any way I run or had run in vain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 5:2–6; Acts 16:3; 1 Corinthians 9:20–21"> (but neither was Titus, who was with me, being a Greek, compelled to be circumcised;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jude 1:4; Galatians 5:1; Galatians 4:3"> and &#91;it was&#93; on account of the false brethren brought in surreptitiously, who came in surreptitiously to spy out our liberty which we have in Christ Jesus, that they might bring us into bondage;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 2:14; Galatians 4:16; Colossians 1:5"> to whom we yielded in subjection not even for an hour, that the truth of the glad tidings might remain with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 2:2; Acts 10:34; 2 Corinthians 12:11"> But from those who were conspicuous as being somewhat — whatsoever they were, it makes no difference to me:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.6 Elohim">a</a></sup>God does not accept man's person; for to me those who were conspicuous communicated nothing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Timothy 2:7; Galatians 1:16"> but, on the contrary, seeing that the glad tidings of the uncircumcision were confided to me, even as to Peter that of the circumcision,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Acts 22:21; Acts 2:14–41"> (for he that wrought in Peter for &#91;the&#93; apostleship of the circumcision wrought also in me towards the Gentiles,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 12:3; Revelation 3:12; Galatians 2:2"> and recognising the grace given to me, James and Cephas and John, who were conspicuous as being pillars, gave to me and Barnabas &#91;the&#93; right hands of fellowship, that we &#91;should go&#93; to the nations, and they to the circumcision;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 24:17; 1 John 3:17; Hebrews 13:16"> only that we should remember the poor, which same thing also I was diligent to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Corinthians 5:16; Acts 15:1"> But when Peter came to Antioch, I withstood him to &#91;the&#93; face, because he was to be condemned:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Luke 15:2; Acts 11:2–3"> for before that certain came from James, he ate with &#91;those of&#93; the nations; but when they came, he drew back and separated himself, fearing those of &#91;the&#93; circumcision;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Ephesians 4:14; Hebrews 13:9"> and the rest of the Jews also played the same dissembling part with him; so that even Barnabas was carried away too by their dissimulation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 2:5; Acts 10:28; Acts 15:10–11"> But when I saw that they do not walk straightforwardly, according to the truth of the glad tidings, I said to Peter before all, If thou, being a Jew, livest as the nations and not as the Jews, how dost thou compel the nations to Judaize?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Ephesians 2:3; Ephesians 2:11–12"> We, Jews by nature, and not sinners of &#91;the&#93; nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Psalms 143:2; Romans 9:30"> but knowing that a man is not justified on the principle of works of law &#91;nor&#93; but by the faith of Jesus Christ, we also have believed on Christ Jesus, that we might be justified on the principle of &#91;the&#93; faith of Christ; and not of works of law; because on the principle of works of law no flesh shall be justified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:1–2; 1 John 3:8–10; 1 John 3:5"> Now if in seeking to be justified in Christ we also have been found sinners, then &#91;is&#93; Christ minister of sin? Far be the thought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 4:9–12; Galatians 2:4–5; Galatians 2:12–16"> For if the things I have thrown down, these I build again, I constitute myself a transgressor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 7:4; Romans 6:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:10"> For I, through law, have died to law, that I may live to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.19 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 5:24; 2 Corinthians 5:15; Romans 6:4–6"> I am crucified with Christ, and no longer live, I, but Christ lives in me; but &#91;in&#93; that I now live in flesh, I live by faith, the &#91;faith&#93; of the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.20 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, who has loved me and given himself for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 11:6; Galatians 3:21; Hebrews 7:11"> I do not set aside the grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.21 Elohim">d</a></sup>God; for if righteousness &#91;is&#93; by law, then Christ has died for nothing.</span>
