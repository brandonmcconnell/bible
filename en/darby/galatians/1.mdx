<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:11–12; 1 Corinthians 1:1; Romans 1:1"> Paul, apostle, not from men nor through man, but through Jesus Christ, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God &#91;the&#93; Father who raised him from among &#91;the&#93; dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:1; Philippians 4:21; Acts 16:5–6"> and all the brethren with me, to the assemblies of Galatia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 John 1:3; 2 Corinthians 13:14; Romans 1:7–15"> Grace to you, and peace, from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.3 Elohim">b</a></sup>God &#91;the&#93; Father, and our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Romans 4:25; Galatians 2:20"> who gave himself for our sins, so that he should deliver us out of the present evil world, according to the will of our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.4 Elohim">c</a></sup>God and Father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Jude 1:25; Psalms 72:19"> to whom &#91;be&#93; glory to the ages of ages. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:4; Galatians 5:7–8; Isaiah 29:13"> I wonder that ye thus quickly change, from him that called you in Christ's grace, to a different gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 15:24; Romans 16:17–18; Galatians 5:10"> which is not another &#91;one&#93;; but there are some that trouble you, and desire to pervert the glad tidings of the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:13–14; Galatians 1:9; Revelation 22:18–19"> But if even we or an angel out of heaven announce as glad tidings to you &#91;anything&#93; besides what we have announced as glad tidings to you, let him be accursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 16:17; Proverbs 30:6; Deuteronomy 4:2"> As we have said before, now also again I say, If any one announce to you as glad tidings &#91;anything&#93; besides what ye have received, let him be accursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Acts 5:29; Ephesians 6:6"> For do I now seek to satisfy men or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.10 Elohim">d</a></sup>God? or do I seek to please men? If I were yet pleasing men, I were not Christ's bondman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 1:1; 1 Corinthians 2:9–10; 1 Corinthians 11:23"> But I let you know, brethren, &#91;as to&#93; the glad tidings which were announced by me, that they are not according to man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 1:16; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Galatians 1:1"> For neither did I receive them from man, neither was I taught &#91;them&#93;, but by revelation of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Corinthians 15:9; Acts 22:3–5"> For ye have heard &#91;what was&#93; my conversation formerly in Judaism, that I excessively persecuted the assembly of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.13 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, and ravaged it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Acts 26:5; Philippians 3:4–6"> and advanced in Judaism beyond many &#91;my&#93; contemporaries in my nation, being exceedingly zealous of the doctrines of my fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 49:5; Isaiah 49:1"> But when<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.15 Elohim">f</a></sup>God, who set me apart &#91;even&#93; from my mother's womb, and called &#91;me&#93; by his grace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:17; Ephesians 3:5–10; Ephesians 6:12"> was pleased to reveal his Son in me, that I may announce him as glad tidings among the nations, immediately I took not counsel with flesh and blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 9:20–25; 2 Corinthians 11:32–33; Galatians 1:18"> nor went I up to Jerusalem to those &#91;who were&#93; apostles before me; but I went to Arabia, and again returned to Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 9:22–23; Acts 9:26–29; Acts 22:17–18"> Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to make acquaintance with Peter, and I remained with him fifteen days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:55; Mark 6:3; Matthew 12:46"> but I saw none other of the apostles, but James the brother of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:1; 2 Corinthians 11:31; 2 Corinthians 11:10–11"> Now what I write to you, behold, before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="1.20 Elohim">g</a></sup>God, I do not lie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 15:41; Acts 9:30; Acts 6:9"> Then I came into the regions of Syria and Cilicia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:14; Romans 16:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:1"> But I was unknown personally to the assemblies of Judaea which &#91;are&#93; in Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:8–10; Acts 9:13; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> only they were hearing that he who persecuted us formerly now announces the glad tidings of the faith which formerly he ravaged:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Acts 11:18; Luke 15:10"> and they glorified<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="1.24 Elohim">h</a></sup>God in me.</span>
</div>
