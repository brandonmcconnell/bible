<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Galatians 5:13; 1 Peter 2:16"> Christ has set us free in freedom; stand fast therefore, and be not held again in a yoke of bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; Acts 15:1; Romans 9:31–32"> Behold, I, Paul, say to you, that if ye are circumcised, Christ shall profit you nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Romans 2:25; James 2:10–11"> And I witness again to every man &#91;who is&#93; circumcised, that he is debtor to do the whole law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:31–32; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Romans 11:6"> Ye are deprived of all profit from the Christ as separated &#91;from him&#93;, as many as are justified by law; ye have fallen from grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 130:5; 1 Thessalonians 1:10; Psalms 62:5"> For we, by &#91;the&#93; Spirit, on the principle of faith, await the hope of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 7:19; James 2:14–26"> For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision has any force, nor uncircumcision; but faith working through love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24; Galatians 3:1; Hebrews 12:1"> Ye ran well; who has stopped you that ye should not obey the truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 1:6; Romans 8:28"> The persuasibleness &#91;is&#93; not of him that calls you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 5:6–7; Mark 8:15"> A little leaven leavens the whole lump.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 5:12; 2 Corinthians 2:3; Galatians 1:7"> I have confidence as to you in &#91;the&#93; Lord, that ye will have no other mind; and he that is troubling you shall bear the guilt &#91;of it&#93;, whosoever he may be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; Galatians 6:12; Galatians 4:29"> But I, brethren, if I yet preach circumcision, why am I yet persecuted? Then the scandal of the cross has been done away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 5:13; Titus 3:10"> I would that they would even cut themselves off who throw you into confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 9:19; 1 Corinthians 8:9"> For ye have been called to liberty, brethren; only &#91;do&#93; not &#91;turn&#93; liberty into an opportunity to the flesh, but by love serve one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Leviticus 19:18; Galatians 6:2"> For the whole law is fulfilled in one word, in Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:3; James 3:14–3; Galatians 5:26"> but if ye bite and devour one another, see that ye are not consumed one of another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 5:24–25; Romans 8:12–14; Galatians 6:8"> But I say, Walk in &#91;the&#93; Spirit, and ye shall no way fulfil flesh's lust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:13; John 3:6; Romans 8:5–8"> For the flesh lusts against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh: and these things are opposed one to the other, that ye should not do those things which ye desire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:14; Romans 6:14–15; Galatians 5:25"> but if ye are led by the Spirit, ye are not under law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Colossians 3:5–8; Matthew 15:18–19"> Now the works of the flesh are manifest, which are fornication, uncleanness, licentiousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Revelation 21:8"> idolatry, sorcery, hatred, strifes, jealousies, angers, contentions, disputes, schools of opinion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Romans 8:13; Romans 13:13"> envyings, murders, drunkennesses, revels, and things like these; as to which I tell you beforehand, even as I also have said before, that they who do such things shall not inherit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.21 Elohim">a</a></sup>God's kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–17; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7; John 15:5"> But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, long-suffering, kindness, goodness, fidelity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:9; Acts 24:25; Titus 2:2"> meekness, self-control: against such things there is no law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Romans 13:14; Romans 8:13"> But they that &#91;are&#93; of the Christ have crucified the flesh with the passions and the lusts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Galatians 5:16; Romans 8:4–5; Romans 8:2"> If we live by the Spirit, let us walk also by the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 3:14–16; Philippians 2:1–3; James 4:16"> Let us not become vain-glorious, provoking one another, envying one another.</span>
