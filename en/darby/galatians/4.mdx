<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 4:23; Galatians 4:29; Genesis 24:2–3"> Now I say, As long as the heir is a child, he differs nothing from a bondman, though he be lord of all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> but he is under guardians and stewards until the period fixed by the father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:20; Colossians 2:8; Galatians 4:9"> So we also, when we were children, were held in bondage under the principles of the world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; John 1:14; Romans 1:3"> but when the fulness of the time was come,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God sent forth his Son, come of woman, come under law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 9:15; Ephesians 1:5; 1 Peter 1:18–20"> that he might redeem those under law, that we might receive sonship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 8:15–17; Romans 5:5; Romans 8:9"> But because ye are sons,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.6 Elohim">b</a></sup>God has sent out the Spirit of his Son into our hearts, crying, Abba, Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:16–17; Galatians 3:29–2; Galatians 3:26"> So thou art no longer bondman, but son; but if son, heir also through<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.7 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:4; Ephesians 2:11–12; 2 Thessalonians 1:8"> But then indeed, not knowing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.8 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, ye were in bondage to those who by nature are not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="4.8 Elohim">e</a></sup>gods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; Galatians 3:3; Romans 8:3"> but now, knowing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="4.9 Elohim">f</a></sup>God, but rather being known by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="4.9 Elohim">g</a></sup>God, how do ye turn again to the weak and beggarly principles to which ye desire to be again anew in bondage?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:5; Leviticus 23:1–44"> Ye observe days and months and times and years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:5; Philippians 2:16; Galatians 2:2"> I am afraid of you, lest indeed I have laboured in vain as to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:5; 1 Kings 22:4; Genesis 34:15"> Be as I &#91;am&#93;, for I also &#91;am&#93; as ye, brethren, I beseech you: ye have not at all wronged me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 12:7–10; 2 Corinthians 11:30"> But ye know that in weakness of the flesh I announced the glad tidings to you at the first;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; 1 Thessalonians 2:13; Hebrews 13:2"> and my temptation, which &#91;was&#93; in my flesh, ye did not slight nor reject with contempt; but ye received me as an angel of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="4.14 Elohim">h</a></sup>God, as Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 5:2; 1 John 3:16–18; Colossians 4:13"> What then &#91;was&#93; your blessedness? for I bear you witness that, if possible, plucking out your own eyes ye would have given &#91;them&#93; to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Proverbs 9:8; John 8:45"> So I have become your enemy in speaking the truth to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 16:18; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; 2 Peter 2:3"> They are not rightly zealous after you, but desire to shut you out &#91;from us&#93;, that ye may be zealous after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; Revelation 3:19; Philippians 1:27"> But &#91;it is&#93; right to be zealous at all times in what is right, and not only when I am present with you —</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 8:29; Romans 13:14"> my children, of whom I again travail in birth until Christ shall have been formed in you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:17–18; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21; 1 Thessalonians 3:9"> and I should wish to be present with you now, and change my voice, for I am perplexed as to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Galatians 3:23–24; Romans 7:5–6"> Tell me, ye who are desirous of being under law, do ye not listen to the law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 16:15; Genesis 21:10; Genesis 21:1–2"> For it is written that Abraham had two sons; one of the maid servant, and one of the free woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 4:28–29"> But he &#91;that was&#93; of the maid servant was born according to flesh, and he &#91;that was&#93; of the free woman through the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 21:9–13; Hebrews 10:15–18; Genesis 16:8"> Which things have an allegorical sense; for these are two covenants: one from mount Sinai, gendering to bondage, which is Hagar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 19:44; Acts 1:11; Galatians 1:17"> For Hagar is mount Sinai in Arabia, and corresponds to Jerusalem which &#91;is&#93; now, for she is in bondage with her children;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 12:22; Philippians 3:20; Micah 4:1–2"> but the Jerusalem above is free, which is our mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 54:1–5; Psalms 113:9; Ruth 4:14–16"> For it is written, Rejoice, thou barren that bearest not; break out and cry, thou that travailest not; because the children of the desolate are more numerous than &#91;those&#93; of her that has a husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 4:23; Romans 9:8–9; Romans 4:13–18"> But ye, brethren, after the pattern of Isaac, are children of promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 21:9; Romans 8:13; Galatians 5:11"> But as then he that was born according to flesh persecuted him &#91;that was born&#93; according to Spirit, so also &#91;it is&#93; now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 21:10–12; John 8:35; Romans 8:15–17"> But what says the scripture? Cast out the maid servant and her son; for the son of the maid servant shall not inherit with the son of the free woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 8:36; John 1:12–13; Galatians 5:1"> So then, brethren, we are not maid servant's children, but &#91;children&#93; of the free woman.</span>
