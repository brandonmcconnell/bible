<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word that came to Jeremiah from Jehovah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:2; Revelation 3:22; Amos 7:16"> Stand in the gate of Jehovah's house, and proclaim there this word, and say, Hear ye the word of Jehovah, all Judah, that enter in at these gates to worship Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:11; Jeremiah 26:13; Isaiah 1:16–19"> Thus saith Jehovah of hosts, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="7.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Israel: Amend your ways and your doings, and I will cause you to dwell in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Matthew 3:9–10; Zephaniah 3:11"> Confide ye not in words of falsehood, saying, Jehovah's temple, Jehovah's temple, Jehovah's temple is this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:12–13; Isaiah 1:19; Jeremiah 7:3"> But if ye thoroughly amend your ways and your doings, if ye really do justice between a man and his neighbour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:21–24; Zechariah 7:9–12; Jeremiah 13:10"> &#91;if&#93; ye oppress not the stranger, the fatherless, and the widow, and shed no innocent blood in this place, neither walk after other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="7.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>gods to your hurt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Jeremiah 25:5; Jeremiah 3:18"> then will I cause you to dwell in this place, in the land that I gave to your fathers from of old even for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 28:15; Ezekiel 13:6–16; Jeremiah 23:14–16"> Behold, ye confide in words of falsehood that cannot profit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:6; Exodus 20:3; James 4:1–4"> What? steal, murder, and commit adultery, and swear falsely, and burn incense unto Baal, and walk after other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="7.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>gods whom ye know not...</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:34; Ezekiel 23:37; Ezekiel 23:39"> then ye come and stand before me, in this house which is called by my name, and say, We are delivered, — in order to do all these abominations!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:45–46; Isaiah 56:7; Matthew 21:13"> Is this house, which is called by my name, a den of robbers in your eyes? Even I, behold, I have seen it, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Jeremiah 26:6; 1 Samuel 4:10–11"> For go now unto my place which was in Shiloh, where I caused my name to dwell at the first, and see what I did to it, for the wickedness of my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Jeremiah 7:25"> And now, because ye have done all these works, saith Jehovah, and I spoke unto you, rising up early and speaking, and ye heard not, and I called you, and ye answered not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 78:60; 2 Kings 25:9; Jeremiah 7:4"> I will even do unto the house which is called by my name, wherein ye trust, and unto the place which I gave to you and to your fathers, as I have done to Shiloh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:1; 2 Kings 17:23; Jeremiah 52:3"> and I will cast you out of my sight, as I have cast out all your brethren, all the seed of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:14; Jeremiah 14:11–12; Jeremiah 15:1"> And thou, pray not for this people, neither lift up cry nor prayer for them, and make not intercession to me; for I will not hear thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:6–18; Ezekiel 14:23; Jeremiah 6:27"> Seest thou not what they do in the cities of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:13; Jeremiah 44:17–19; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> The children gather wood, and the fathers kindle the fire, and the women knead dough, to make cakes to the queen of heaven, and to pour out drink-offerings unto other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="7.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>gods, that they may provoke me to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:11; Jeremiah 2:19; Daniel 9:7–8"> Is it I whom they provoke to anger? saith Jehovah; is it not themselves, to the shame of their own face?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:18; Lamentations 2:3–5; Isaiah 42:25"> Therefore thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Behold, mine anger and my fury shall be poured out upon this place; upon man, and upon beast, and upon the trees of the field, and upon the fruit of the ground; and it shall burn, and shall not be quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Jeremiah 6:20; Amos 5:21–23"> Thus saith Jehovah of hosts, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="7.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God of Israel: Add your burnt-offerings to your sacrifices, and eat the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; 1 Samuel 15:22; Psalms 51:16–17"> For I spoke not unto your fathers, nor commanded them concerning burnt-offerings and sacrifices, in the day that I brought them out of the land of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Deuteronomy 5:33; Exodus 19:5–6"> but I commanded them this thing, saying, Hearken unto my voice, and I will be your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="7.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God, and ye shall be my people; and walk in all the way that I command you, that it may be well with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 81:11–12; Jeremiah 8:5; Jeremiah 7:26"> But they hearkened not, nor inclined their ear, but walked in the counsels, in the stubbornness of their evil heart, and went backward and not forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15; Jeremiah 25:4; Nehemiah 9:26"> Since the day that your fathers came forth out of the land of Egypt, unto this day, have I sent unto you all my servants the prophets, daily rising up early and sending them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Jeremiah 7:24"> but they have not hearkened unto me, nor inclined their ear; and they have hardened their neck: they have done worse than their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:2; Jeremiah 1:7; Isaiah 65:12"> And thou shalt speak all these words unto them, but they will not hearken unto thee; and thou shalt call unto them, but they will not answer thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 59:14–15; Proverbs 1:7; Isaiah 1:4–5"> And thou shalt say unto them, This is the nation which hath not hearkened unto the voice of Jehovah their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="7.28 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God, nor received correction; fidelity is perished, and is cut off from their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 1:20; Micah 1:16; Jeremiah 6:30"> Cut off thy hair, and cast it away, and take up a lamentation on the heights; for Jehovah hath rejected and forsaken the generation of his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:34; Ezekiel 7:20; Daniel 9:27"> For the children of Judah have done evil in my sight, saith Jehovah; they have set their abominations in the house which is called by my name, to defile it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 23:10; Jeremiah 32:35; Leviticus 18:21"> And they have built the high places of Topheth, which is in the valley of the son of Hinnom, to burn their sons and their daughters in the fire; which I commanded not, neither did it come up into my mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:11; Jeremiah 19:6; 2 Kings 23:10"> Therefore, behold, days are coming, saith Jehovah, when it shall no more be said, Topheth, and Valley of the son of Hinnom, but the valley of slaughter; for they shall bury in Topheth, till there be no place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:9; Deuteronomy 28:26; Revelation 19:17–18"> And the carcases of this people shall be food for the fowl of the heavens, and for the beasts of the earth; and none shall scare &#91;them&#93; away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Hosea 2:11; Ezekiel 26:13"> And I will cause to cease from the cities of Judah, and from the streets of Jerusalem, the voice of mirth and the voice of joy, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride; for the land shall become a waste.</span>
