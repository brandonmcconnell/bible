<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:11; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 27:2"> Thus said Jehovah unto me: Go and buy thee a linen girdle, and put it upon thy loins; but dip it not in water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:8; John 15:14; Proverbs 3:5"> And I bought a girdle according to the word of Jehovah, and put it upon my loins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:8"> And the word of Jehovah came unto me the second time, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 4:10; Psalms 137:1; Jeremiah 51:63–64"> Take the girdle that thou hast bought, which is upon thy loins, and arise, go to the Euphrates, and hide it there in a hole of the rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; Exodus 39:42–43; 2 Timothy 2:3"> So I went and hid it by the Euphrates, as Jehovah had commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And it came to pass at the end of many days, that Jehovah said unto me, Arise, go to the Euphrates, and take the girdle from thence which I commanded thee to hide there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:12; Isaiah 64:6; Jeremiah 13:10"> And I went to the Euphrates, and digged, and took the girdle from the place where I had hid it; and behold, the girdle was spoiled, it was good for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the word of Jehovah came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremiah 13:15–17; Zephaniah 3:11"> Thus saith Jehovah: After this manner will I spoil the pride of Judah, and the great pride of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; Jeremiah 9:14; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16"> This evil people, who refuse to hear my words, who walk in the stubbornness of their heart, and go after other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="13.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>gods, to serve them and to worship them, shall even be as this girdle which is good for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Jeremiah 33:9; Psalms 81:11"> For as a girdle cleaveth to the loins of a man, so have I caused to cleave unto me the whole house of Israel and the whole house of Judah, saith Jehovah; that they might be unto me for a people, and for a name, and for a praise, and for a glory: but they would not hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19"> And thou shalt speak unto them this word: Thus saith Jehovah, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="13.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God of Israel, Every skin shall be filled with wine. And they will say unto thee, Do we not very well know that every skin shall be filled with wine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 63:6; Psalms 75:8; Psalms 60:3"> And thou shalt say unto them, Thus saith Jehovah: Behold, I will fill all the inhabitants of this land, and the kings that sit for David upon his throne, and the priests and the prophets, and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem, with drunkenness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Psalms 2:9; Jeremiah 19:9–11"> And I will dash them one against another, both the fathers and the sons together, saith Jehovah; I will not pity, nor spare, nor have mercy so as not to destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 28:14–22; Revelation 2:29; Acts 4:19–20"> Hear ye, and give ear, be not lifted up; for Jehovah hath spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:9; Isaiah 8:22; Isaiah 5:30"> Give glory to Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="13.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God, before he cause darkness, and before your feet stumble upon the mountains of twilight; and ye shall look for light, but he will turn it into the shadow of death, and make &#91;it&#93; gross darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 2:2; Jeremiah 9:1; Jeremiah 14:17"> And if ye will not hear it, my soul shall weep in secret places for &#91;your&#93; pride; and mine eye shall weep sore, and run down with tears, because Jehovah's flock is gone into captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:26; 2 Kings 24:12; 2 Kings 24:15"> Say unto the king and to the queen: Humble yourselves, sit down low; for from your heads shall come down the crown of your magnificence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:27; Jeremiah 39:9; Deuteronomy 28:52"> The cities of the south are shut up, and there is none to open &#91;them&#93;; all Judah is carried away captive: it is wholly carried away captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:22; Jeremiah 1:14; Habakkuk 1:6"> Lift up your eyes, and behold them that come from the north. Where is the flock that was given thee, thy beautiful flock?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 13:8; Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 5:31"> What wilt thou say when he shall visit thee, since thou thyself hast trained them to be princes in chief over thee? Shall not sorrows take thee, as a woman in travail?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nahum 3:5; Jeremiah 16:10–11; Jeremiah 5:19"> And if thou say in thy heart, Wherefore come these things upon me? For the greatness of thine iniquity are thy skirts uncovered, &#91;and&#93; thy heels have suffered violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; Jeremiah 9:5; Matthew 19:24–28"> Can an Ethiopian change his skin, or a leopard his spots? &#91;Then&#93; may ye also do good, who are accustomed to do evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 1:4; Leviticus 26:33; Ezekiel 5:12"> And I will scatter them as stubble that passeth away by the wind of the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 20:29; Jeremiah 2:32; Psalms 9:17"> This shall be thy lot, thy measured portion from me, saith Jehovah; because thou hast forgotten me, and confided in falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Lamentations 1:8; Hosea 2:10; Ezekiel 23:29"> Therefore will I also turn thy skirts over thy face, and thy shame shall be seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hosea 8:5; Ezekiel 6:13; Ezekiel 24:13"> Thine adulteries, and thy neighings, the lewdness of thy fornication, on the hills, in the fields, — thine abominations, have I seen. Woe unto thee, Jerusalem! Wilt thou not be made clean? after how long a time yet?</span>
