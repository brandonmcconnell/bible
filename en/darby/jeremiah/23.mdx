<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:21; Zechariah 11:15–17; Ezekiel 34:2–10"> Woe unto the shepherds that destroy and scatter the sheep of my pasture! saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 32:34; Jeremiah 21:12; Micah 7:4"> Therefore thus saith Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="23.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Israel concerning the shepherds that feed my people: Ye have scattered my flock, and driven them away, and have not visited them: behold, I will visit upon you the evil of your doings, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 9:14–15; Jeremiah 32:37; Isaiah 43:5–6"> And I will gather the remnant of my flock out of all countries whither I have driven them, and will bring them again to their pastures; and they shall be fruitful and shall multiply.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:14–15; 1 Peter 1:5; Ezekiel 34:23–31"> And I will raise up shepherds over them, who shall feed them; and they shall fear no more, nor be dismayed, neither shall any be missing, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Isaiah 4:2; Isaiah 9:7"> Behold, the days come, saith Jehovah, when I will raise unto David a righteous Branch, who shall reign as king, and act wisely, and shall execute judgment and righteousness in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Jeremiah 33:16; Philippians 3:9"> In his days Judah shall be saved, and Israel shall dwell in safety; and this is his name whereby he shall be called, Jehovah our Righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:14–15; Jeremiah 23:3; Isaiah 43:18–19"> Therefore behold, days are coming, saith Jehovah, that they shall no more say, &#91;As&#93; Jehovah liveth, who brought up the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5–6; Amos 9:14–15; Ezekiel 34:13"> but, &#91;As&#93; Jehovah liveth, who brought up and who led back the seed of the house of Israel out of the north country, and from all countries whither I had driven them. And they shall dwell in their own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Lamentations 3:15; Psalms 60:3"> Concerning the prophets: My heart within me is broken; all my bones shake; I am like a drunken man, and like a man whom wine hath overcome; because of Jehovah, and because of the words of his holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 4:2–3; Psalms 107:34; Jeremiah 9:2"> For the land is full of adulterers; for because of execration the land mourneth. The pastures of the wilderness are dried up; for their course is evil, and their force is not right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:4; Jeremiah 6:13; Matthew 21:12–13"> For both prophet and priest are profane: even in my house have I found their wickedness, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:16; Jeremiah 11:23; Psalms 35:6"> Therefore their way shall be unto them as slippery places in the darkness; they shall be driven on, and fall therein: for I will bring evil upon them in the year of their visitation, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:8; Isaiah 9:16; 1 Kings 18:18–21"> And I have seen folly in the prophets of Samaria: they prophesied by Baal, and caused my people Israel to err.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9–10; Jeremiah 29:23; Ezekiel 22:25"> And in the prophets of Jerusalem have I seen a horrible thing: they commit adultery, and walk in falsehood, and strengthen the hands of evildoers, so that none doth return from his wickedness. They are all become unto me as Sodom, and the inhabitants thereof as Gomorrah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:14; Jeremiah 9:15; Lamentations 3:5"> Therefore thus saith Jehovah of hosts concerning the prophets: Behold, I will feed them with wormwood, and make them drink water of gall; for from the prophets of Jerusalem is profaneness gone forth into all the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14; Ezekiel 13:6; Galatians 1:8–9"> Thus saith Jehovah of hosts: Hearken not unto the words of the prophets that prophesy unto you: they lead you to vanity; they speak a vision of their own heart, not out of the mouth of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Jeremiah 13:10; Amos 9:10"> They say constantly unto them that despise me, Jehovah hath said, Ye shall have peace. And they say unto every one that walketh in the stubbornness of his heart, No evil shall come upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:22; Isaiah 40:13–14; Amos 3:7"> For who hath stood in the council of Jehovah, so that he hath perceived and heard his word? who hath hearkened to his word and listened?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:23; Jeremiah 25:32; Amos 1:14"> Behold, a tempest of Jehovah, fury is gone forth, yea, a whirling storm: it shall whirl down upon the head of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:24; Isaiah 55:11; Zechariah 1:6"> The anger of Jehovah shall not return, until he have executed, and until he have performed the purposes of his heart: at the end of the days ye shall understand it clearly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14; Jeremiah 27:15; Jeremiah 23:32"> I did not send the prophets, yet they ran; I have not spoken to them, yet they prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:18; Zechariah 1:4; Jeremiah 25:5"> But if they had stood in my council, and had caused my people to hear my words, then would they have turned them from their evil way and from the wickedness of their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 139:1–10; 1 Kings 20:23; 1 Kings 20:28"> Am I a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="23.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God at hand, saith Jehovah, and not a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="23.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God afar off?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Psalms 139:7; Psalms 90:8"> Can any hide himself in secret places, that I shall not see him? saith Jehovah. Do not I fill the heavens and the earth? saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:32; Jeremiah 23:28; Jeremiah 29:8"> I have heard what the prophets say, who prophesy falsehood in my name, saying, I have dreamed, I have dreamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:1–2; Jeremiah 14:14; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> How long shall &#91;this&#93; be in the heart of the prophets who prophesy falsehood, and who are prophets of the deceit of their own heart?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 8:33–34; Judges 3:7; Acts 13:8"> who think to cause my people to forget my name by their dreams which they tell every man to his neighbour: as their fathers have forgotten my name for Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 1 Timothy 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:12–13"> The prophet that hath a dream, let him tell the dream; and he that hath my word, let him speak my word faithfully. What is the chaff to the wheat? saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Jeremiah 5:14; Luke 24:32"> Is not my word like a fire, saith Jehovah; and like a hammer &#91;that&#93; breaketh the rock in pieces?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; Ezekiel 13:8; Jeremiah 14:14–15"> Therefore, behold, I am against the prophets, saith Jehovah, that steal my words every one from his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:17; 2 Chronicles 18:10–12; 2 Chronicles 18:5"> Behold, I am against the prophets, saith Jehovah, that use their tongues, and say, He hath said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:7–18; Zephaniah 3:4; Lamentations 2:14"> Behold, I am against them that prophesy false dreams, saith Jehovah, and that tell them, and cause my people to err by their lies and by their boasting; and I have not sent them, nor commanded them; and they profit not this people at all, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Malachi 1:1; Jeremiah 12:7; Habakkuk 1:1"> And when this people, or a prophet, or a priest, ask thee, saying, What is the burden of Jehovah? thou shalt then say unto them, What burden? I will even cast you off, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 13:3; Lamentations 2:14"> And as for the prophet, and the priest, and the people, that shall say, The burden of Jehovah, I will even punish that man and his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Jeremiah 42:4; Jeremiah 33:3"> Thus shall ye say every one to his neighbour, and every one to his brother: What hath Jehovah answered? and, What hath Jehovah spoken?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:10; 2 Peter 3:16; Isaiah 3:8"> And the burden of Jehovah shall ye mention no more; for every man's own word shall be his burden: for ye have perverted the words of the living<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="23.36 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God, of Jehovah of hosts, our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="23.36 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Thus shalt thou say to the prophet: What hath Jehovah answered thee? and, What hath Jehovah spoken?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:13–14"> But since ye say, The burden of Jehovah, therefore thus saith Jehovah: Because ye say this word, The burden of Jehovah, and I have sent unto you, saying, Ye shall not say, The burden of Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:33; Ezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 13:13"> therefore behold, I will utterly forget you, and I will cast you off, far from my face, and the city that I gave to you and to your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:11; Ezekiel 5:14–15; Jeremiah 42:18"> And I will bring everlasting reproach upon you, and everlasting shame, that shall not be forgotten.</span>
