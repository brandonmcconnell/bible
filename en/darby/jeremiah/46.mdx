<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Zechariah 2:8; Jeremiah 4:7"> The word of Jehovah that came to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 23:29; Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 25:19"> Of Egypt: concerning the army of Pharaoh-Necho king of Egypt, which was by the river Euphrates at Carchemish, which Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon smote in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, the king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 21:5; Joel 3:9; Nahum 2:1"> Make ready buckler and shield, and draw near to battle!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:9–11; Jeremiah 51:3; 1 Samuel 17:5"> Harness the horses, and mount ye horsemen, and stand forth with helmets; polish the spears, put on the coats of mail!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:29; Jeremiah 6:25; Jeremiah 46:21"> Why do I see them dismayed, turned away back? And their mighty ones are beaten down, and take to flight, and look not back? Terror &#91;is&#93; on every side, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 11:19; Jeremiah 46:12; Psalms 27:2"> Let not the swift flee away, neither let the mighty man escape! — Toward the north, hard by the river Euphrates, they have stumbled and fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:2; Isaiah 8:7–8; Daniel 11:22"> Who is this &#91;that&#93; riseth up as the Nile, whose waters toss themselves like the rivers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 10:13–16; Ezekiel 32:2; Isaiah 37:24–26"> It is Egypt that riseth up as the Nile, and &#91;his&#93; waters toss themselves like the rivers; and he saith, I will rise up, I will cover the earth; I will destroy the city and the inhabitants thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 66:19; Ezekiel 27:10; Nahum 3:9"> Go up, ye horses, and drive furiously, ye chariots; and let the mighty men go forth: Cush and Phut that handle the shield, and the Ludim that handle the bow &#91;and&#93; bend it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:42; Joel 1:15; Jeremiah 46:2"> For this is the day of the Lord Jehovah of hosts, a day of vengeance, that he may be avenged of his adversaries; and the sword shall devour, and it shall be sated and made drunk with their blood; for the Lord Jehovah of hosts hath a sacrifice in the north country, by the river Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:22; Micah 1:9; Nahum 3:19"> Go up to Gilead, and fetch balm, O virgin-daughter of Egypt! In vain shalt thou multiply remedies: there is no healing for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nahum 3:8–10; Isaiah 19:2; Jeremiah 46:6"> The nations have heard of thy shame, and thy cry hath filled the earth; for the mighty man stumbleth against the mighty, they are both fallen together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:30; Jeremiah 43:10–13; Isaiah 19:1–25"> The word that Jehovah spoke to Jeremiah the prophet, concerning the coming of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon to smite the land of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:10; Jeremiah 44:1; Jeremiah 46:3–4"> Declare in Egypt, and publish in Migdol, and publish in Noph, and in Tahpanhes; say, Stand fast, and prepare thee; for the sword devoureth round about thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 66:15–16; Psalms 68:2; Psalms 18:39"> Why are thy valiants swept away? They stood not, for Jehovah did thrust them down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 26:36–37; Jeremiah 51:9; Jeremiah 46:6"> He made many to stumble, yea, one fell upon another; and they said, Arise, and let us return to our own people and to the land of our nativity, from the oppressing sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11–16; Exodus 15:9; 1 Kings 20:10"> There did they cry, Pharaoh king of Egypt is but a noise; he hath let the time appointed go by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:15; Judges 4:6; Joshua 19:22"> &#91;As&#93; I live, saith the King, whose name is Jehovah of hosts, surely as Tabor among the mountains, and as Carmel by the sea, so shall he come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 48:18; Ezekiel 30:13"> Thou, inhabitress, daughter of Egypt, furnish for thyself a captive's baggage, for Noph shall be a desolation and shall be ruined, so that none shall dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hosea 10:11; Jeremiah 47:2; Jeremiah 1:14"> Egypt is a very fair heifer; the gad-fly cometh, it cometh from the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:5; Obadiah 1:13; 2 Kings 7:6"> Also her hired men in the midst of her are like fatted bullocks; for they also have turned back, they have fled away together, they did not stand; for the day of their calamity is come upon them, the time of their visitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 14:8; Isaiah 29:4; Micah 1:8"> Her voice shall go like a serpent's; for they shall march with an army, and come against her with axes, as hewers of wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 6:5; Joel 2:25"> They shall cut down her forest, saith Jehovah, though it be impenetrable; for they are more than the locusts, and are innumerable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:15; Ezekiel 29:1–21; Jeremiah 46:11"> The daughter of Egypt is put to shame; she is delivered into the hand of the people of the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:11; Jeremiah 43:12–13; Isaiah 20:5–6"> Jehovah of hosts, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="46.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Israel, saith, Behold, I will punish Amon of No, and Pharaoh, and Egypt, and her<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="46.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>gods, and her kings; yea, Pharaoh and them that confide in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:11; Jeremiah 44:30; Ezekiel 29:8–14"> And I will give them into the hand of those that seek their life, and into the hand of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, and into the hand of his servants; but afterwards it shall be inhabited, as in the days of old, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5; Jeremiah 30:10–11; Isaiah 43:1"> But thou, my servant Jacob, fear not, neither be dismayed, Israel: for behold, I will save thee from afar, and thy seed from the land of their captivity; and Jacob shall return, and be in rest and at ease, and none shall make &#91;him&#93; afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:32; Revelation 3:19; Jeremiah 10:24"> Fear thou not, my servant Jacob, saith Jehovah: for I am with thee; for I will make a full end of all the nations whither I have driven thee, but I will not make a full end of thee; but I will correct thee with judgment, and I will not hold thee altogether guiltless.</span>
