<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:14; Isaiah 11:11; Exodus 14:2"> The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the Jews who dwelt in the land of Egypt, who dwelt at Migdol, and at Tahpanhes, and at Noph, and in the country of Pathros, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 6:11; Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 34:22"> Thus saith Jehovah of hosts, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="44.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Israel: Ye have seen all the evil that I have brought upon Jerusalem, and upon all the cities of Judah; and behold they are, this day, a waste, and no man dwelleth therein,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:26; Deuteronomy 13:6; Deuteronomy 32:17"> because of their wickedness which they have committed to provoke me to anger, in that they went to burn incense to serve other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="44.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>gods which they knew not, they, &#91;nor&#93; ye, nor your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:5; Jeremiah 7:25; Jeremiah 7:13"> And I sent unto you all my servants the prophets, rising early and sending, saying, Oh, do not this abominable thing which I hate!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:10; Jeremiah 19:13; 2 Chronicles 36:16"> But they hearkened not, nor inclined their ear to turn from their wickedness, to burn no incense unto other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="44.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 21:5; Isaiah 51:17"> And my fury and mine anger was poured forth, and was kindled in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem; and they are become a waste, a desolation, as at this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:10; Jeremiah 51:22; Ezekiel 33:11"> And now thus saith Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="44.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God of hosts, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="44.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God of Israel: Wherefore commit ye great evil against your souls, to cut off from you man and woman, infant and suckling, out of the midst of Judah, to leave you no remnant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:18; Jeremiah 25:6–7; Jeremiah 44:3"> provoking me to anger with the works of your hands, burning incense unto other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="44.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>gods in the land of Egypt, whither ye are come to sojourn, that ye should be cut off, and that ye should be a curse and a reproach among all the nations of the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:17–18; Joshua 22:17–20; Jeremiah 44:15–19"> Have ye forgotten the wickedness of your fathers, and the wickedness of the kings of Judah, and the wickedness of their wives, and your own wickedness, and the wickedness of your wives, which they have committed in the land of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; 1 Peter 5:6; Revelation 15:4"> They are not humbled unto this day, neither have they feared, nor walked in my law, nor in my statutes which I set before you and before your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:10; Leviticus 26:17; Leviticus 20:5–6"> Therefore thus saith Jehovah of hosts, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="44.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God of Israel: Behold, I will set my face against you for evil, and to cut off all Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:15–18; Jeremiah 42:22; Isaiah 65:15"> And I will take the remnant of Judah, that have set their faces to enter into the land of Egypt to sojourn there, and they shall all be consumed: in the land of Egypt shall they fall; they shall be consumed by the sword &#91;and&#93; by the famine, from the least even unto the greatest; they shall die by the sword and by the famine, and they shall be an execration, an astonishment, and a curse, and a reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:22; Jeremiah 43:11; Jeremiah 44:27–28"> And I will punish them that dwell in the land of Egypt as I have punished Jerusalem, by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 9:27; Jeremiah 22:26–27; Jeremiah 42:17"> and none of the remnant of Judah, that have come into the land of Egypt to sojourn there, shall escape or remain, so as to return into the land of Judah, whither they have a desire to return to dwell there; for none shall return but such as shall escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 11:21; Jeremiah 5:1–5; Genesis 19:4"> Then all the men who knew that their wives burned incense to other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="44.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>gods, and all the women who stood by, a great assemblage, even all the people that dwelt in the land of Egypt, in Pathros, answered Jeremiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 21:14–15; Luke 19:27; Jeremiah 16:15–17"> &#91;As for&#93; the word that thou hast spoken unto us in the name of Jehovah, we will not hearken unto thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:18; Nehemiah 9:34; Jeremiah 44:21"> but we will certainly do every word that is gone forth out of our mouth, to burn incense to the queen of the heavens, and to pour out drink-offerings to her, as we have done, we and our fathers, our kings and our princes, in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem; and we had plenty of bread, and were well, and saw no evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachi 3:13–15; Numbers 11:5–6; Job 21:14–15"> But since we left off burning incense to the queen of the heavens, and pouring out drink-offerings to her, we have wanted everything, and have been consumed by the sword and by the famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:18; Jeremiah 44:15; 1 Kings 21:25"> And when we burned incense to the queen of the heavens and poured out drink-offerings to her, did we make for her cakes to portray her, and pour out drink-offerings to her, without our husbands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Jeremiah said unto all the people, to the men, and to the women, and to all the people that had given him that answer, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:17; Jeremiah 44:9; Jeremiah 11:13"> Is it not the incense that ye burned in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem, ye and your fathers, your kings and your princes and the people of the land, that Jehovah remembered, and that came into his mind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:18; Isaiah 7:13; Malachi 2:17"> And Jehovah could no longer bear, because of the evil of your doings, &#91;and&#93; because of the abominations that ye had committed; and your land is become a waste, and an astonishment, and a curse, without inhabitant, as at this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 9:9; Daniel 9:11–12; Nehemiah 13:18"> Because ye have burned incense, and because ye have sinned against Jehovah, and have not hearkened unto the voice of Jehovah, nor walked in his law, nor in his statutes, nor in his testimonies; therefore this evil hath come upon you, as at this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:7; Jeremiah 44:15–16; Jeremiah 42:15"> And Jeremiah said unto all the people, and to all the women, Hear ye the word of Jehovah, all Judah that are in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 14:9; Acts 23:12–15; Isaiah 28:15"> Thus saith Jehovah of hosts, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="44.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God of Israel, saying, Ye and your wives have both spoken with your mouths, and fulfilled with your hands, saying, We will certainly perform our vows which we have vowed, to burn incense to the queen of the heavens, and to pour out drink-offerings unto her. Ye will certainly establish your vows, and entirely perform your vows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 50:16; Ezekiel 20:39; Hebrews 6:13"> Therefore hear ye the word of Jehovah, all Judah that dwell in the land of Egypt: Behold, I have sworn by my great name, saith Jehovah, that my name shall no more be named in the mouth of any man of Judah in all the land of Egypt, saying, &#91;As&#93; the Lord Jehovah liveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; 2 Kings 21:14; Jeremiah 1:10"> Behold, I will watch over them for evil, and not for good; and all the men of Judah that are in the land of Egypt shall be consumed by the sword and by the famine, until there be an end of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:14; Isaiah 27:12–13; Isaiah 10:19"> And they that escape the sword shall return out of the land of Egypt into the land of Judah, a very small company; and all the remnant of Judah, that have come into the land of Egypt to sojourn there, shall know whose word shall stand, mine or theirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Matthew 24:15–16; Jeremiah 44:30"> And this shall be the sign unto you, saith Jehovah, that I will punish you in this place, that ye may know that my words shall certainly stand against you for evil:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 25:4–7; Jeremiah 43:9–13; Jeremiah 34:21"> thus saith Jehovah: Behold, I will give Pharaoh-Hophra king of Egypt into the hand of his enemies, and into the hand of them that seek his life; as I gave Zedekiah king of Judah into the hand of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, his enemy, and that sought his life.</span>
