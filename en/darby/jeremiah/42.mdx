<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:11; Jeremiah 40:13; Jeremiah 6:13"> And all the captains of the forces, and Johanan the son of Kareah, and Jezaniah the son of Hoshaiah, and all the people from the least even to the greatest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:7; Leviticus 26:22; Acts 8:24"> came near and said unto Jeremiah the prophet, Let, we beseech thee, our supplication come before thee, and pray for us unto Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="42.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, for all this remnant (for we are left a few of many, as thine eyes do behold us);</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 86:11; Proverbs 3:6; Psalms 25:4–5"> that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="42.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God may shew us the way wherein we should walk, and the thing that we should do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:23; 1 Samuel 3:17–18; Exodus 8:29"> And Jeremiah the prophet said unto them, I have heard; behold, I will pray unto Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="42.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God according to your words; and it shall come to pass &#91;that&#93; whatsoever thing Jehovah shall answer you, I will declare it unto you: I will keep nothing back from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 31:50; Judges 11:10; Malachi 3:5"> And they said to Jeremiah, Jehovah be a true and faithful witness amongst us, if we do not even according to all the word for which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="42.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God shall send thee to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:23; Deuteronomy 5:29; Romans 8:7"> Whether it be good or whether it be evil, we will hearken unto the voice of Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="42.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God, to whom we send thee; that it may be well with us when we hearken unto the voice of Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="42.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Isaiah 28:16; Habakkuk 2:3"> And it came to pass at the end of ten days, that the word of Jehovah came unto Jeremiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:1; Jeremiah 41:11–16; Jeremiah 43:2–5"> And he called Johanan the son of Kareah, and all the captains of the forces that were with him, and all the people from the least even unto the greatest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 19:4; Jeremiah 42:2; 2 Kings 19:6"> and said unto them, Thus saith Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="42.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God of Israel, to whom ye sent me to present your supplication before him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Ezekiel 36:36; Jeremiah 24:6"> If ye will still abide in this land, then will I build you, and not overthrow &#91;you&#93;, and I will plant you, and not pluck &#91;you&#93; up; for I repent me of the evil that I have done unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Jeremiah 1:19; Jeremiah 1:8"> Be not afraid of the king of Babylon, of whom ye are afraid: be not afraid of him, saith Jehovah; for I will be with you to save you, and to deliver you from his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11; Psalms 106:45–46; Proverbs 16:7"> And I will grant mercies to you, that he may have mercy upon you, and cause you to return to your own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16; Exodus 5:2; Jeremiah 42:10"> But if ye say, We will not dwell in this land; so as not to hearken unto the voice of Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="42.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:17; Jeremiah 4:19; Isaiah 31:1"> saying, No; but we will go into the land of Egypt, where we shall see no war, nor hear the sound of the trumpet, nor have hunger for bread; and there will we dwell;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16; Jeremiah 44:12–14; Jeremiah 42:17"> — and now, therefore, hear the word of Jehovah, ye remnant of Judah: thus saith Jehovah of hosts, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="42.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God of Israel: If ye really set your faces to enter into Egypt, and go to sojourn there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:27; Ezekiel 11:8; Amos 9:1–4"> then it shall come to pass, that the sword which ye fear shall overtake you there in the land of Egypt, and the famine, whereof ye are afraid, shall follow hard after you there in Egypt; and there ye shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:22; Jeremiah 44:28; Jeremiah 44:13–14"> And it shall be that all the men that have set their faces to go into Egypt to sojourn there shall die by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence; and none of them shall remain or escape from the evil that I will bring upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 18:16; Jeremiah 29:18"> For thus saith Jehovah of hosts, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="42.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God of Israel: As mine anger and my fury have been poured forth upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so shall my fury be poured forth upon you, when ye shall enter into Egypt; and ye shall be an execration, and an astonishment, and a curse, and a reproach, and ye shall see this place no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16; Isaiah 30:1–7; Nehemiah 9:26"> Jehovah hath said concerning you, the remnant of Judah, Go ye not into Egypt. Know certainly that I have admonished you this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:3–4; Ezekiel 33:31; Matthew 22:15–18"> For ye deceived yourselves in your own souls, when ye sent me unto Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="42.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God, saying, Pray for us unto Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="42.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God; and according to all that Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="42.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>God shall say, so declare unto us, and we will do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:7; Zechariah 7:11–12; Acts 20:26–27"> And I have this day declared &#91;it&#93; to you; but ye have not obeyed the voice of Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="42.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">n</a></sup>God, nor anything for which he hath sent me unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:17; Ezekiel 6:11; Hosea 9:6"> And now know certainly that ye shall die by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence, in the place whither ye desire to go to sojourn.</span>
