<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Malachi 3:15; Psalms 92:7; Psalms 37:1"> Righteous art thou, Jehovah, when I plead with thee; yet will I speak with thee of &#91;thy&#93; judgments. Wherefore doth the way of the wicked prosper? &#91;wherefore&#93; are all they at ease that deal very treacherously?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 15:8"> Thou hast planted them, they also have taken root: they advance, yea, they bring forth fruit: thou art near in their mouth, but far from their reins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:18; 2 Kings 20:3; Psalms 17:3"> But thou, Jehovah, knowest me; thou hast seen me, and proved my heart toward thee. Drag them out like sheep for the slaughter, and set them apart for the day of slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:10; Psalms 107:34; Jeremiah 4:25"> How long shall the land mourn, and the herbs of all the fields wither? Because of the wickedness of them that dwell therein, the beasts and the birds perish; for they say, He will not see our end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12; Proverbs 24:10; Hebrews 12:3–4"> If thou hast run with footmen, and they have wearied thee, how wilt thou then contend with horses? And if in a land of peace thou thinkest thyself in security, how wilt thou then do in the swelling of the Jordan?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 26:25; Jeremiah 9:4; Micah 7:5–6"> For even thy brethren, and the house of thy father, even they have dealt treacherously with thee, even they have cried aloud after thee. Believe them not, though they speak good &#91;words&#93; unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:6; Jeremiah 11:15; Psalms 78:59–60"> I have forsaken my house, I have cast off my heritage, I have given the beloved of my soul into the hand of her enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Hosea 9:15; Jeremiah 2:15"> My heritage is become unto me as a lion in the forest; it hath raised its voice against me: therefore have I hated it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 56:9; Jeremiah 7:33; 2 Kings 24:2"> My heritage is unto me &#91;as&#93; a speckled bird of prey; the birds of prey round about are against her. Go, assemble all the beasts of the field, bring them to devour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–7; Jeremiah 23:1; Isaiah 63:18"> Many shepherds have destroyed my vineyard, they have trodden my portion under foot, they have made my pleasant portion a desolate wilderness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:10; Isaiah 42:25; Jeremiah 14:2"> they have made it a desolation; desolate, it mourneth unto me: the whole land is made desolate, for no man layeth it to heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:6; Amos 9:4; Ezekiel 14:17"> Spoilers are come upon all heights in the wilderness; for the sword of Jehovah devoureth from one end of the land even to the &#91;other&#93; end of the land: no flesh hath peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:38; Haggai 1:6"> They have sown wheat, and they reap thorns; they have put themselves to pain, &#91;and&#93; do not profit. Be ye therefore ashamed of your revenues, because of the fierce anger of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:18; Zechariah 2:8; Zechariah 10:6–12"> Thus saith Jehovah against all mine evil neighbours, that touch the inheritance which I have caused my people Israel to inherit: Behold, I will pluck them up out of their land, and pluck out the house of Judah from among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:6; Jeremiah 48:47; Jeremiah 49:39"> And it shall come to pass, after I have plucked them up, I will return, and have compassion on them, and will bring them back, each one to his inheritance, and each one to his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:2; Joshua 23:7; Zephaniah 1:5"> And it shall come to pass, if they will diligently learn the ways of my people, to swear by my name, &#91;As&#93; Jehovah liveth — even as they taught my people to swear by Baal — they shall be built up in the midst of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 60:12; Psalms 2:8–12; Daniel 11:4"> And if they will not obey, I will utterly pluck up and destroy that nation, saith Jehovah.</span>
