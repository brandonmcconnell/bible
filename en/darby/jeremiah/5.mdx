<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 18:23–32; 2 Chronicles 16:9; Ezekiel 22:30"> Run ye to and fro through the streets of Jerusalem, and see now, and know, and seek in the broadways thereof, if ye can find a man, if there be &#91;any&#93; that doeth justice, that seeketh fidelity; and I will pardon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Jeremiah 4:2; Hosea 4:1–2"> And if they say, &#91;As&#93; Jehovah liveth! surely they swear falsely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:30; Jeremiah 7:28; Isaiah 9:13"> Jehovah, are not thine eyes upon fidelity? Thou hast smitten them, but they are not sore; thou hast consumed them, they have refused to receive correction: they have made their faces harder than a rock; they have refused to return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Hosea 4:6; Isaiah 28:9–13"> And I said, Surely these are the wretched ones, they are foolish; for they know not the way of Jehovah, the judgment of their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:20; James 2:5–7; Matthew 19:23–26"> I will go unto the great men, and will speak unto them; for they know the way of Jehovah, the judgment of their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God; but these have altogether broken the yoke, have burst the bonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Habakkuk 1:8; Zephaniah 3:3"> Therefore a lion out of the forest shall slay them, a wolf of the evenings shall waste them; the leopard lurketh against their cities, every one that goeth out thence is torn in pieces: for their transgressions are multiplied, their backslidings are increased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Joshua 23:7; Jeremiah 2:11"> Wherefore should I pardon thee? Thy children have forsaken me, and swear by them that are not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God. I have satiated them, and they have committed adultery, and they troop to the harlots' house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:27; Deuteronomy 5:18; 2 Samuel 11:2–4"> &#91;As&#93; well fed horses, they roam about, every one neigheth after his neighbour's wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:9; Jeremiah 5:29; Isaiah 1:24"> Shall I not visit for these things? saith Jehovah, and shall not my soul be avenged on such a nation as this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:27; Matthew 22:7; Amos 9:8"> Go up upon her walls, and destroy; but make not a full end; take away her battlements, for they are not Jehovah's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:20; Isaiah 48:8; Hosea 6:7"> For the house of Israel and the house of Judah have dealt very treacherously against me, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Ezekiel 12:22–28; Jeremiah 23:14–17"> They have denied Jehovah, and say, He is not; and evil shall not come upon us, nor shall we see sword nor famine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:15; Job 8:2; Jeremiah 14:13"> and the prophets shall become wind, and the word is not in them: thus shall it be done unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:29; Jeremiah 1:9; Hosea 6:5"> Therefore thus saith Jehovah, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="5.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God of hosts: Because ye speak this word, behold, I will make my words in thy mouth fire, and this people wood, and it shall devour them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:49; Jeremiah 4:16; Isaiah 33:19"> Behold, I bring a nation upon you from afar, house of Israel, saith Jehovah: it is a mighty nation, it is an ancient nation, a nation whose language thou knowest not, neither understandest thou what they say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 5:28; Psalms 5:9; Romans 3:13"> Their quiver is as an open sepulchre; they are all mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 26:16; Deuteronomy 28:33; Hosea 8:14"> And they shall eat up thy harvest and thy bread, they shall eat up thy sons and thy daughters, they shall eat up thy flocks and thy herds, they shall eat up thy vines and thy fig-trees; they shall destroy with the sword thy strong cities, wherein thou trustedst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:10; Romans 11:1–5; Jeremiah 4:27"> Nevertheless in those days, saith Jehovah, I will not make a full end with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 9:8–9; Deuteronomy 28:47–48; Jeremiah 22:8–9"> And it shall come to pass, when ye shall say, Wherefore hath Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="5.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God done all these things unto us? then shalt thou say to them, As ye have forsaken me, and served strange<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="5.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>gods in your land, so shall ye serve strangers in a land that is not yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Declare this in the house of Jacob, and publish it in Judah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 28:26; Ezekiel 12:2; Isaiah 6:9–10"> Hear now this, O foolish and heartless people, who have eyes and see not; who have ears, and hear not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Psalms 104:9; Job 26:10"> Will ye not fear me? saith Jehovah. Will ye not tremble at my presence, who have set the sand a bound for the sea by a perpetual decree, and it shall not pass it? and its waves toss themselves, but they do not prevail; and they roar, yet can they not pass over it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:28; Deuteronomy 21:18; Psalms 95:10"> But this people hath a stubborn and a rebellious heart; they have turned aside and are gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Joel 2:23; Genesis 8:22"> And they say not in their heart, Let us now fear Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="5.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God, that giveth rain, both the early and the latter, in its season; who preserveth unto us the appointed weeks of harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 59:2; Psalms 107:17; Psalms 107:34"> Your iniquities have turned away these things, and your sins have withholden from you what is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 1:11; Psalms 64:5; Ezekiel 22:2–12"> For among my people are found wicked &#91;men&#93;: they lay wait, as fowlers stoop down; they set a trap, they catch men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 1:12; Jeremiah 9:6; Hosea 12:7–8"> As a cage full of birds, so are their houses full of deceit: therefore they are become great, and have enriched themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 1:23; Deuteronomy 32:15; Jeremiah 7:6"> They are become fat, they shine, yea, they surpass in deeds of wickedness; they judge not the cause, the cause of the fatherless, and they prosper; and the right of the needy do they not adjudge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:9; Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 9:9"> Shall I not visit for these things? saith Jehovah; shall not my soul be avenged on such a nation as this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hosea 6:10; Jeremiah 23:14; Isaiah 1:2"> An appalling and horrible thing is committed in the land:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6; Jeremiah 14:14; Micah 2:11"> the prophets prophesy falsehood, and the priests rule by their means; and my people love &#91;to have it&#93; so. But what will ye do in the end thereof?</span>
