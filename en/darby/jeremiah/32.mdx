<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:1–2; 2 Kings 25:1–2; Jeremiah 25:1"> The word that came to Jeremiah from Jehovah in the tenth year of Zedekiah king of Judah: that year was the eighteenth year of Nebuchadrezzar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:21; Nehemiah 3:25; Jeremiah 33:1"> And the king of Babylon's army was then besieging Jerusalem; and the prophet Jeremiah was shut up in the court of the guard, which was in the king of Judah's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:2–3; Jeremiah 21:4–7; Jeremiah 26:8–9"> For Zedekiah king of Judah had shut him up, saying, Why dost thou prophesy and say, Thus saith Jehovah: Behold, I give this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he shall take it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:4–7; 2 Kings 25:4–7; Jeremiah 37:17"> and Zedekiah king of Judah shall not escape out of the hand of the Chaldeans; for he shall certainly be given into the hand of the king of Babylon, and shall speak with him mouth to mouth, and his eyes shall behold his eyes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:7; Ezekiel 17:9–10; Ezekiel 17:15"> and he shall lead Zedekiah to Babylon, and there shall he be until I visit him, saith Jehovah: though ye fight with the Chaldeans, ye shall not prosper?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Jeremiah said, The word of Jehovah came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:1; Jeremiah 11:21; Leviticus 25:25"> Behold, Hanameel, the son of Shallum thine uncle, shall come unto thee, saying, Buy for thyself my field which is in Anathoth; for thine is the right of redemption, to buy &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:7; Jeremiah 32:2; Jeremiah 33:1"> And Hanameel, mine uncle's son, came to me in the court of the guard according to the word of Jehovah, and said unto me, Buy my field, I pray thee, that is in Anathoth, which is in the land of Benjamin; for the right of inheritance is thine, and the redemption is thine: buy &#91;it&#93; for thyself. Then I knew that this was the word of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Esther 3:9; 1 Kings 20:39"> And I bought of Hanameel, mine uncle's son, the field which is in Anathoth, and weighed him the money, seventeen shekels of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:44; Jeremiah 32:12; Jeremiah 32:25"> And I subscribed the writing, and sealed it, and took witnesses, and weighed the money in the balances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 2:27; 1 Corinthians 11:16; Acts 26:3"> And I took the writing of the purchase, that which was sealed &#91;according to&#93; the law and the statutes, and that which was open;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:16; Jeremiah 51:59; Jeremiah 36:4–5"> and I gave the writing of the purchase unto Baruch the son of Nerijah, the son of Maaseiah, in the sight of Hanameel mine uncle's &#91;son&#93;, and in the presence of the witnesses that had subscribed the writing of purchase, before all the Jews that were sitting in the court of the guard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And I charged Baruch in their presence, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Thus saith Jehovah of hosts, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="32.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Israel: Take these writings, this writing of the purchase, both that which is sealed and this writing which is open; and put them in an earthen vessel, that they may remain many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 3:10; Amos 9:14–15; Jeremiah 30:18"> For thus saith Jehovah of hosts, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="32.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God of Israel: Houses and fields and vineyards shall again be purchased in this land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:1; Genesis 32:9–12; Philippians 4:6–7"> And after I had given the writing of the purchase unto Baruch the son of Nerijah, I prayed unto Jehovah saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 1:37; Jeremiah 32:27; Genesis 18:14"> Alas, Lord Jehovah! Behold, thou hast made the heavens and the earth by thy great power and stretched-out arm; there is nothing too hard for thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Deuteronomy 5:9–10; Exodus 34:7"> who shewest mercy unto thousands, and recompensest the iniquity of the fathers into the bosom of their children after them, thou, the great, the mighty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="32.18 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">c</a></sup>God, — Jehovah of hosts is his name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Matthew 16:27; Psalms 62:12"> great in counsel and mighty in work, whose eyes are open upon all the ways of the children of men, to give every one according to his ways, and according to the fruit of his doings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:10; 2 Samuel 7:23; Exodus 10:2"> who hast displayed signs and wonders unto this day, in the land of Egypt and in Israel and among &#91;other&#93; men; and hast made thee a name, as at this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 26:8; Psalms 105:43"> And thou broughtest forth thy people Israel out of the land of Egypt by signs, and by wonders, and by a powerful hand, and by a stretched-out arm, and by great terror;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Exodus 13:5; Jeremiah 11:5"> and didst give them this land, which thou hadst sworn unto their fathers to give them, a land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:7; Psalms 78:54–55; Daniel 9:10–14"> And they came in and possessed it: but they hearkened not unto thy voice, neither walked in thy law; they have done nothing of all that thou commandedst them to do; so that thou hast caused all this evil to come upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:36; Deuteronomy 4:26; Joshua 23:15–16"> Behold the mounds, they are come unto the city for taking it; and the city is given over into the hand of the Chaldeans, that fight against it, by the sword, and the famine, and the pestilence: and what thou hast spoken is come to pass; and behold, thou seest &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:24; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 77:19"> And thou, Lord Jehovah, thou hast said unto me, Buy for thyself the field for money, and take witnesses; — and the city is given into the hand of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the word of Jehovah came unto Jeremiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Jeremiah 32:17; Isaiah 64:8"> Behold, I am Jehovah, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="32.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God of all flesh: is there anything too hard for me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:3; Jeremiah 32:24; Jeremiah 19:7–12"> Therefore thus saith Jehovah: Behold, I give this city into the hand of the Chaldeans, and into the hand of Nebuchadrezzar the king of Babylon, and he shall take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 19:13; Jeremiah 52:13"> And the Chaldeans, that fight against this city, shall come in and set fire to this city, and shall burn it, and the houses upon whose roofs they have offered incense unto Baal, and poured out drink-offerings unto other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="32.29 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>gods, to provoke me to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:22–26; Deuteronomy 9:7–12; Jeremiah 25:7"> For the children of Israel and the children of Judah have been doing only evil in my sight from their youth; for the children of Israel have only provoked me to anger with the work of their hands, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 23:27; Jeremiah 27:10; 2 Kings 21:4–7"> For this city hath been to me &#91;a provocation&#93; of mine anger and of my fury from the day that they built it even unto this day; that I should remove it from before my face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 9:8; Jeremiah 2:26; Isaiah 1:4–6"> because of all the evil of the children of Israel and of the children of Judah, which they have done to provoke me to anger, they, their kings, their princes, their priests, and their prophets, and the men of Judah, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:13; Jeremiah 2:27; Ezekiel 8:16"> And they have turned unto me the back, and not the face; and though I taught them, rising early and teaching, they hearkened not to receive instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:30; Ezekiel 8:5–16; 2 Kings 21:4–7"> And they have set their abominations in the house which is called by my name, to defile it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:31; 2 Chronicles 33:6; Leviticus 18:21"> and they have built the high places of Baal, which are in the valley of the son of Hinnom, to cause to pass through &#91;the fire&#93; their sons and their daughters unto Molech: which I commanded them not, neither came it into my mind, that they should do this abomination, to cause Judah to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:24; Jeremiah 32:3; Jeremiah 32:28"> And now therefore Jehovah, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="32.36 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God of Israel, saith thus concerning this city, whereof ye say, It hath been given over into the hand of the king of Babylon by the sword, and by the famine, and by the pestilence:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:3; Jeremiah 23:6; Ezekiel 34:12–14"> Behold, I will gather them out of all the countries whither I have driven them, in mine anger, and in my fury, and in great wrath; and I will bring them again unto this place, and I will cause them to dwell safely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Ezekiel 11:19–20; Hebrews 8:10"> And they shall be my people, and I will be their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="32.38 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19–20; Acts 4:32; John 17:21"> And I will give them one heart, and one way, that they may fear me all &#91;their&#93; days, for the good of them, and of their children after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–33; Ezekiel 36:26; Jeremiah 24:7"> And I will make an everlasting covenant with them, that I will not draw back from them, to do them good; and I will put my fear in their heart, that they may not turn aside from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Zephaniah 3:17; Isaiah 62:5"> And I will rejoice over them to do them good, and I will assuredly plant them in this land with my whole heart and with my whole soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Joshua 23:14–15; Jeremiah 33:10–11"> For thus saith Jehovah: Like as I have brought all this great evil upon this people, so will I bring upon them all the good that I have spoken concerning them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:11–14; Jeremiah 32:15; Jeremiah 32:36"> And fields shall be bought in this land, whereof ye say, It is desolate without man or beast; it is given into the hand of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:26; Jeremiah 33:26; Jeremiah 33:11"> &#91;Men&#93; shall buy fields for money, and subscribe the writings, and seal them, and take witnesses, in the land of Benjamin, and in the environs of Jerusalem, and in the cities of Judah, and in the cities of the hill-country, and in the cities of the lowland, and in the cities of the south: for I will turn their captivity, saith Jehovah.</span>
