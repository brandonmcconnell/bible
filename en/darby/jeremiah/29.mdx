<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esther 9:20; Acts 15:23; Hebrews 13:22"> And these are the words of the letter that the prophet Jeremiah sent from Jerusalem to the residue of the elders of the captivity, and to the priests, and to the prophets, and to all the people whom Nebuchadnezzar had carried away captive from Jerusalem to Babylon</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:24–28; Jeremiah 27:20; 2 Chronicles 36:9–10"> (after that Jeconiah the king, and the queen, and the eunuchs, the princes of Judah and Jerusalem, and the craftsmen, and the smiths, had departed from Jerusalem);</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:11; 2 Chronicles 34:20; Jeremiah 39:14"> by the hand of Elasah the son of Shaphan, and Gemariah the son of Hilkijah (whom Zedekiah king of Judah sent to Babylon, unto Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon), saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:5; Isaiah 10:5–6; Isaiah 45:7"> Thus saith Jehovah of hosts, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="29.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Israel, unto all the captivity, whom I have caused to be carried away from Jerusalem unto Babylon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:28; Jeremiah 29:10; Ezekiel 28:26"> Build houses, and dwell in them, and plant gardens, and eat the fruit of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:3–4; Genesis 24:51; Genesis 28:1–4"> Take wives, and beget sons and daughters; and take wives for your sons, and give your daughters to husbands, that they may bear sons and daughters; and multiply there, and be not diminished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:1–2; Ezra 6:10; 1 Peter 2:13–17"> And seek the peace of the city whither I have caused you to be carried away captive, and pray unto Jehovah for it: for in the peace thereof shall ye have peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:6; Jeremiah 5:31; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> For thus saith Jehovah of hosts, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="29.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God of Israel: Let not your prophets that are in your midst, nor your diviners deceive you, neither hearken to your dreams, which ye like to dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:15; Jeremiah 29:31; Jeremiah 29:23"> For they prophesy falsely unto you in my name: I have not sent them, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 9:2; Zechariah 7:5; Jeremiah 24:6–7"> For thus saith Jehovah: When seventy years shall be accomplished for Babylon I will visit you, and perform my good word toward you, in bringing you back to this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 55:8–12; Psalms 33:11; Micah 4:12"> For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith Jehovah, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you in your latter end a hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; Jeremiah 33:3; Psalms 50:15"> And ye shall call upon me, and ye shall go and pray unto me, and I will hearken unto you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:29–31; Psalms 119:2; Matthew 7:7"> and ye shall seek me and find me, for ye shall search for me with all your heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:1–38; Jeremiah 30:3; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> and I will be found of you, saith Jehovah. And I will turn your captivity, and I will gather you from all the nations, and from all the places whither I have driven you, saith Jehovah; and I will bring you again into the place whence I have caused you to be carried away captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:8–9; Jeremiah 28:1–17; Ezekiel 1:3"> If ye say, Jehovah hath raised us up prophets in Babylon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:2–3; Jeremiah 38:17–23; Ezekiel 17:12–21"> yea, thus saith Jehovah concerning the king that sitteth upon the throne of David, and concerning all the people that dwell in this city, your brethren that are not gone forth with you into captivity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:8–10; Jeremiah 29:18; Jeremiah 24:1–3"> thus saith Jehovah of hosts: Behold, I will send against them the sword, the famine, and the pestilence, and will make them like the vile figs, that cannot be eaten for badness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:8; Jeremiah 15:4; Jeremiah 42:18"> And I will pursue them with the sword, with the famine, and with the pestilence, and will give them over to be driven hither and thither into all the kingdoms of the earth, to be an execration, and an astonishment, and a hissing, and a reproach, among all the nations whither I will drive them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:19; Jeremiah 26:5; Jeremiah 7:24–26"> because they have not hearkened to my words, saith Jehovah, wherewith I sent unto them my servants the prophets, rising early and sending; but ye have not hearkened, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:5; Micah 4:10; Ezekiel 3:15"> But ye, all ye of the captivity, whom I have sent from Jerusalem to Babylon, hear the word of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14–15; Lamentations 2:14; Jeremiah 29:8–9"> Thus saith Jehovah of hosts, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="29.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God of Israel, concerning Ahab the son of Kolaiah, and concerning Zedekiah the son of Maaseiah, who prophesy falsehood unto you in my name: Behold, I will give them into the hand of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, and he shall smite them before your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 65:15; Daniel 3:6; Daniel 3:21"> And of them shall be taken up a curse by all the captivity of Judah that are in Babylon, saying, Jehovah make thee like Zedekiah and like Ahab, whom the king of Babylon roasted in the fire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Hebrews 4:13; Malachi 3:5"> because they have committed infamy in Israel, and have committed adultery with their neighbours' wives, and have spoken words of falsehood in my name, which I had not commanded them: and I &#91;am&#93; he that knoweth, and &#91;am&#93; witness, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:31–32"> And thou shalt speak to Shemaiah the Nehelamite, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:3; Jeremiah 29:29; Jeremiah 52:24"> Thus speaketh Jehovah of hosts, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="29.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God of Israel, saying, Because thou hast sent letters in thy name unto all the people that are at Jerusalem, and to Zephaniah the son of Maaseiah the priest, and to all the priests, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:1–2; 2 Kings 9:11; John 10:20"> Jehovah hath made thee priest in the stead of Jehoiada the priest, that there should be officers &#91;in&#93; the house of Jehovah, over every madman and self-made prophet, that thou shouldest put him in the stocks and in the shackles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:16; Jeremiah 43:2–3; Jeremiah 29:26"> And now, why hast thou not reproved Jeremiah of Anathoth, who maketh himself a prophet to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:5; Jeremiah 29:1; Jeremiah 29:10"> Forasmuch as he hath sent unto us in Babylon, saying, It will be long; build houses, and dwell &#91;in them&#93;, and plant gardens, and eat the fruit of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:25"> — And Zephaniah the priest read this letter in the ears of the prophet Jeremiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the word of Jehovah came unto Jeremiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:9; Jeremiah 29:23–24; Jeremiah 14:14–15"> Send to all them of the captivity, saying, Thus saith Jehovah concerning Shemaiah the Nehelamite: Because that Shemaiah hath prophesied unto you, and I sent him not, and he hath caused you to trust in falsehood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:16; 2 Kings 7:19–20; Jeremiah 17:6"> therefore thus saith Jehovah: Behold, I will punish Shemaiah the Nehelamite, and his seed: he shall not have a man to dwell among this people; neither shall he see the good that I will do for my people, saith Jehovah; because he hath spoken revolt against Jehovah.</span>
