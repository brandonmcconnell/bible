<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:12; 1 Kings 20:34; Ezekiel 17:13"> And Nahash the Ammonite came up and encamped against Jabesh-Gilead. And all the men of Jabesh said to Nahash, Make a covenant with us, and we will serve thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:26; Genesis 34:14; Numbers 16:14"> And Nahash the Ammonite said to them, On this &#91;condition&#93; will I treat with you, that I thrust out all your right eyes, and lay it for a reproach upon all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And the elders of Jabesh said to him, Allow us seven days, and we will send messengers into all the districts of Israel; and if there be no man to deliver us, we will come out to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 2:4; 1 Samuel 10:26; 1 Samuel 15:34"> And the messengers came to Gibeah of Saul and told these words in the ears of the people. And all the people lifted up their voice and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 19:19; Judges 18:23; Genesis 21:17"> And behold, Saul came after the oxen from the field; and Saul said, What &#91;aileth&#93; the people that they weep? And they related to him the words of the men of Jabesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:10; 1 Samuel 16:13; Judges 6:34"> And the Spirit of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="11.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God came upon Saul when he heard those words, and his anger was kindled greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:29; Judges 20:1; Judges 21:5–11"> And he took a yoke of oxen and cut them in pieces, and sent throughout the territory of Israel by the hand of messengers, saying, Whoever comes not forth after Saul and after Samuel, so shall it be done to his oxen! And the fear of Jehovah fell on the people, and they came out as one man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 1:4–5; 2 Samuel 24:9; 2 Chronicles 17:12–19"> And he numbered them in Bezek, and the children of Israel were three hundred thousand, and the men of Judah thirty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 18:17"> And they said to the messengers that had come, Thus shall ye say to the men of Jabesh-Gilead: To-morrow ye shall have deliverance when the sun is hot. And the messengers came and informed the men of Jabesh-Gilead; and they were glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:2–3"> And the men of Jabesh said, To-morrow we will come out to you, and ye may do with us according to all that is good in your sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 7:16; James 2:13; Exodus 14:24"> And it came to pass the next day that Saul set the people in three companies; and they came into the midst of the camp in the morning watch, and smote Ammon until the heat of the day: and it came to pass that they who remained were scattered, and not two of them were left together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:27; Luke 19:27; Psalms 21:8"> And the people said to Samuel, Who is he that said, Shall Saul reign over us? bring the men, that we may put them to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:22; 1 Samuel 19:5; Exodus 14:13"> But Saul said, There shall not a man be put to death this day; for to-day Jehovah has wrought deliverance in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:8; 1 Samuel 7:16; 1 Chronicles 12:38–39"> And Samuel said to the people, Come and let us go to Gilgal, and renew the kingdom there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:8; 1 Samuel 10:17; 1 Chronicles 29:21–24"> And all the people went to Gilgal; and there they made Saul king before Jehovah in Gilgal; and there they sacrificed peace-offerings before Jehovah. And there Saul and all the men of Israel rejoiced exceedingly.</span>
