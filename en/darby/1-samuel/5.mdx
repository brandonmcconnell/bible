<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:12; 1 Samuel 4:1; Joshua 13:3"> And the Philistines took the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, and brought it from Eben-ezer to Ashdod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 16:23; Daniel 5:2; 1 Chronicles 10:10"> And the Philistines took the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God and brought it into the house of Dagon, and set it by Dagon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 19:1; Isaiah 46:1–2; Isaiah 46:7"> And when they of Ashdod arose early the next day, behold, Dagon had fallen on his face to the ground before the ark of Jehovah. And they took Dagon, and set him in his place again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 1:7; Isaiah 2:18–19; Jeremiah 10:11"> And when they arose early the next morning, behold, Dagon had fallen on his face to the ground before the ark of Jehovah; and the head of Dagon and both the palms of his hands were cut off upon the threshold; only the fish-stump was left to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:9; Psalms 115:4–7; Joshua 5:15"> Therefore neither the priests of Dagon nor any that come into Dagon's house tread on the threshold of Dagon in Ashdod to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:11; Acts 13:11; Exodus 9:3"> And the hand of Jehovah was heavy upon them of Ashdod, and he laid them waste, and smote them with hemorrhoids, — Ashdod and its borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:33; Jeremiah 48:7; 1 Chronicles 15:13"> And when the men of Ashdod saw that it was so, they said, The ark of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God of Israel shall not abide with us; for his hand is severe upon us, and upon Dagon our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="5.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:11; 1 Samuel 17:4; Zechariah 12:3"> And they sent and gathered all the lords of the Philistines unto them, and said, What shall we do with the ark of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="5.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God of Israel? And they said, Let the ark of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="5.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God of Israel be carried about to Gath. And they carried the ark of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="5.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God of Israel about &#91;thither&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:6; 1 Samuel 7:13; Deuteronomy 2:15"> And it came to pass that, after they had carried it about, the hand of Jehovah was against the city with very great panic; and he smote the men of the city, both small and great, and hemorrhoids broke out upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 1:8; Judges 1:18; Joshua 15:45"> And they sent the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="5.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God to Ekron. And it came to pass, when the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="5.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God came to Ekron, that the Ekronites cried out, saying, They have brought about the ark of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="5.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God of Israel to us, to kill us and our people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:6; Jeremiah 48:42–44; Isaiah 13:7–9"> And they sent and gathered all the lords of the Philistines, and said, Send away the ark of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="5.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God of Israel, and let it go again to its own place, that it kill us not, and our people. For there was deadly alarm throughout the city: the hand of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="5.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God was very heavy there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 12:30; Amos 5:19; 1 Kings 19:17"> and the men that died not were smitten with the hemorrhoids; and the cry of the city went up to heaven.</span>
