<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:5–7; 1 Samuel 6:21; Isaiah 52:11"> And the men of Kirjath-jearim came, and fetched up the ark of Jehovah, and brought it into the house of Abinadab on the hill, and hallowed Eleazar his son to keep the ark of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10–11; Jeremiah 3:22–25; Matthew 5:4"> And it came to pass, from the day that the ark abode in Kirjath-jearim, that the time was long; for it was twenty years. And all the house of Israel lamented after Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 4:8; Deuteronomy 13:4; Matthew 4:10"> And Samuel spoke to all the house of Israel, saying, If ye return to Jehovah with all your heart, put away the strange<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="7.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>gods and the Ashtoreths from among you, and apply your hearts unto Jehovah, and serve him only; and he will deliver you out of the hand of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 14:8; Hosea 14:3; Judges 2:11"> And the children of Israel put away the Baals and the Ashtoreths and served Jehovah only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 1 Samuel 12:23; Joshua 15:38"> And Samuel said, Gather all Israel to Mizpah, and I will pray Jehovah for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:14; 1 Samuel 1:15; Judges 10:10"> And they gathered together to Mizpah, and drew water, and poured it out before Jehovah, and fasted on that day, and said there, We have sinned against Jehovah. And Samuel judged the children of Israel in Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:11; 1 Samuel 13:6; Exodus 14:10"> And the Philistines heard that the children of Israel were gathered together at Mizpah; and the lords of the Philistines went up against Israel; and the children of Israel heard &#91;it&#93;, and were afraid of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 37:4; James 5:16; 1 Samuel 12:19–24"> And the children of Israel said to Samuel, Cease not to cry to Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="7.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God for us, that he will save us out of the hand of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:1; Psalms 99:6; 1 Samuel 7:17"> And Samuel took a sucking-lamb, and offered it as a whole burnt-offering to Jehovah; and Samuel cried to Jehovah for Israel, and Jehovah answered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:10; 2 Samuel 22:14–15; Joshua 10:10"> And as Samuel was offering up the burnt-offering, the Philistines advanced to battle against Israel. And Jehovah thundered with a great thunder on that day upon the Philistines, and discomfited them; and they were routed before Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the men of Israel went out of Mizpah, and pursued the Philistines, and smote them, as far as below Beth-car.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Joshua 4:9; Acts 26:22"> And Samuel took a stone and set it between Mizpah and Shen, and called the name of it Eben-ezer, and said, Hitherto Jehovah has helped us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 13:1; 1 Samuel 17:49–53; 1 Samuel 14:6–16"> And the Philistines were subdued, and came no more into the borders of Israel; and the hand of Jehovah was against the Philistines all the days of Samuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 106:34; Deuteronomy 7:2; Deuteronomy 7:16"> And the cities that the Philistines had taken from Israel were restored to Israel, from Ekron even to Gath; and their territory did Israel deliver out of the hand of the Philistines. And there was peace between Israel and the Amorite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:6; Acts 13:20–21; Judges 2:16"> And Samuel judged Israel all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 10:4; Judges 12:14; Judges 5:10"> And he went from year to year in circuit to Bethel, and Gilgal, and Mizpah, and judged Israel in all those places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:19; 1 Samuel 8:4; 1 Samuel 1:1"> And his return was to Ramah; for there was his house, and there he judged Israel; and there he built an altar to Jehovah.</span>
