<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Samuel 11:14–15; 1 Samuel 10:1"> And Samuel said to all Israel, Behold, I have hearkened to your voice in all that ye said to me, and have made a king over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:5; 1 Samuel 8:1; 1 Samuel 8:20"> And now behold, the king walks before you; and I am old and grey-headed; and behold, my sons are with you; and I have walked before you from my youth up to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 20:33; Numbers 16:15; 1 Peter 5:2"> Here I am: testify against me before Jehovah, and before his anointed. Whose ox have I taken? or whose ass have I taken? or whom have I defrauded? whom have I injured? or of whose hand have I received any ransom and blinded mine eyes therewith? and I will restore it to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 6:4; Psalms 37:5–6; 3 John 1:12"> And they said, Thou hast not defrauded us, and thou hast not injured us, neither hast thou taken aught of any man's hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 22:4; Acts 24:20; Acts 23:9"> And he said to them, Jehovah is witness against you, and his anointed is witness this day, that ye have not found aught in my hand! And &#91;the people&#93; said, &#91;He is&#93; witness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:4; Exodus 6:26; Psalms 99:6"> And Samuel said to the people, &#91;It is&#93; Jehovah who appointed Moses and Aaron, and who brought your fathers up out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Micah 6:1–5; Acts 17:3"> And now stand still, that I may plead with you before Jehovah of all the righteous acts of Jehovah which he did to you and to your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:14–16; Exodus 2:23–24; Psalms 78:54–55"> When Jacob had come into Egypt, and your fathers cried to Jehovah, then Jehovah sent Moses and Aaron, and they brought your fathers forth out of Egypt, and made them dwell in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 13:1; Judges 10:7; Judges 3:12"> And they forgot Jehovah their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="12.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, and he sold them into the hand of Sisera, captain of the host of Hazor, and into the hand of the Philistines, and into the hand of the king of Moab, and they fought against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 10:10; Judges 2:13; Judges 3:7"> And they cried to Jehovah and said, We have sinned, because we have forsaken Jehovah, and have served the Baals and the Ashtoreths; and now deliver us out of the hand of our enemies, and we will serve thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 6:32; Judges 4:6; Judges 8:35"> And Jehovah sent Jerubbaal and Bedan and Jephthah and Samuel, and delivered you out of the hand of your enemies round about, so that ye dwelt in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 8:23; 1 Samuel 8:5–7; Genesis 17:7"> But when ye saw that Nahash the king of the children of Ammon came against you, ye said to me, Nay, but a king shall reign over us; when Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="12.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God was your king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 13:11; 1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Samuel 8:5"> And now behold, the king whom ye have chosen, whom ye have asked for! and behold, Jehovah has set a king over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 24:14; Deuteronomy 28:1–14; Psalms 81:12–15"> If ye fear Jehovah, and serve him, and hearken to his voice, and rebel not against the commandment of Jehovah, then both ye and the king also that reigns over you shall continue following Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="12.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 24:20; Isaiah 1:20; Isaiah 3:11"> But if ye will not hearken to the voice of Jehovah, and if ye rebel against the commandment of Jehovah, then will the hand of Jehovah be against you, as against your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:13; Exodus 14:31; 1 Samuel 12:7"> Now therefore stand and see this great thing which Jehovah will do before your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Proverbs 26:1; 1 Samuel 7:9–10"> Is it not wheat-harvest to-day? I will call unto Jehovah, and he will send thunder and rain; and ye shall perceive and see that your wickedness is great which ye have done in the sight of Jehovah in asking for yourselves a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 14:31; Ezra 10:9; Psalms 106:12–13"> And Samuel called to Jehovah; and Jehovah sent thunder and rain that day. And all the people greatly feared Jehovah and Samuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 9:28; 1 John 5:16; 1 Samuel 12:23"> And all the people said to Samuel, Pray to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="12.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God for thy servants, that we die not; for we have added to all our sins the wickedness to ask for ourselves a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:16; Joshua 23:6; 1 Peter 3:16"> And Samuel said to the people, Fear not: ye have done all this wickedness; yet turn not aside from following Jehovah, and serve Jehovah with all your heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18; Jeremiah 10:15; Jeremiah 16:19"> and turn ye not aside; for &#91;it would be&#93; after vain things which cannot profit nor deliver; for they are vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Deuteronomy 14:2; Psalms 106:8"> For Jehovah will not cast away his people for his great name's sake; because it has pleased Jehovah to make you his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:9; Romans 1:9; 2 Timothy 1:3"> Moreover, as for me, far be it from me that I should sin against Jehovah in ceasing to pray for you; and I will teach you the good and right way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:21; Proverbs 1:7; Isaiah 5:12"> Only, fear Jehovah, and serve him in truth, with all your heart; for see how great things he has done for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 24:20; Hosea 10:3; 1 Samuel 31:1–5"> But if ye do wickedly, ye shall perish, both ye and your king.</span>
