<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:1; 1 Samuel 7:12; 1 Samuel 5:1"> And what Samuel had said happened to all Israel. And Israel went out against the Philistines to battle, and encamped beside Eben-ezer; and the Philistines encamped in Aphek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 Samuel 17:8; Joshua 7:5–8"> And the Philistines put themselves in array against Israel; and the battle spread, and Israel was routed before the Philistines; and they slew in battle array in the field about four thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Numbers 10:35; 1 Corinthians 10:1–5"> And the people came into the camp; and the elders of Israel said, Why has Jehovah smitten us to-day before the Philistines? Let us fetch ourselves the ark of the covenant of Jehovah out of Shiloh, that it may come among us, and save us out of the hand of our enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:2; Numbers 7:89; Psalms 80:1"> So the people sent to Shiloh, and they brought from thence the ark of the covenant of Jehovah of hosts, who sitteth between the cherubim; and the two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, were there by the ark of the covenant of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 6:5; Job 20:5; Amos 6:3"> And it came to pass when the ark of the covenant of Jehovah came into the camp, that all Israel shouted with a great shout, so that the earth shook.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 32:17–18"> And the Philistines heard the noise of the shout, and said, What is the noise of this great shout in the camp of the Hebrews? And they understood that the ark of Jehovah had come into the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:30; Exodus 14:25; Exodus 15:14–16"> And the Philistines were afraid, for they said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God is come into the camp. And they said, Woe unto us! for there has not been such a thing heretofore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 9:14; Exodus 7:5; Psalms 78:43–51"> Woe unto us! who shall deliver us out of the hand of these mighty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>gods? these are the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>gods that smote the Egyptians with every plague in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:13; Judges 13:1; 2 Samuel 10:12"> Shew yourselves valiant and be men, ye Philistines, that ye may not have to be servants to the Hebrews, as they have been servants to you: be men, and fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 26:17; 2 Kings 14:12; Deuteronomy 28:25"> And the Philistines fought, and Israel was routed, and they fled every man to his tent; and there was a very great slaughter, and there fell of Israel thirty thousand footmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:34; Psalms 78:64; 1 Samuel 2:32"> And the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="4.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God was taken; and the two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 7:6; Nehemiah 9:1; 2 Samuel 15:32"> And there ran a man of Benjamin out of the battle, and came to Shiloh the same day, with his clothes rent and with earth upon his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:9; 1 Samuel 4:18; Joshua 7:9"> And when he came, behold, Eli was sitting upon the seat by the way-side watching; for his heart trembled for the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="4.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God. And the man came to tell it in the city, and all the city cried out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Eli heard the noise of the crying, and said, What is the noise of this tumult? And the man came hastily, and told Eli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:2; Psalms 90:10; 1 Kings 14:4"> Now Eli was ninety-eight years old; and his eyes were set, that he could not see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:4; 1 Samuel 3:6; Joshua 7:19"> And the man said to Eli, I am he that came out of the battle, and I have fled to-day out of the battle. And he said, What has taken place, my son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:11; 1 Samuel 4:10–11"> And the messenger answered and said, Israel has fled before the Philistines, and there has been also a great slaughter among the people, and thy two sons also, Hophni and Phinehas, are dead, and the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="4.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God is taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:21–22; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Psalms 69:9"> And it came to pass, when he mentioned the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="4.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God, that he fell from off the seat backward by the side of the gate, and his neck broke, and he died; for the man was old, and heavy. And he had judged Israel forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And his daughter-in-law, Phinehas' wife, was with child, near to be delivered; and when she heard the tidings that the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="4.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God was taken, and that her father-in-law and her husband were dead, she bowed herself and travailed; for her pains came upon her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:17–18; Psalms 77:2; John 16:21"> And as she was dying, the women that stood by her said, Fear not; for thou hast borne a son. But she did not answer, neither did she take it to heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; Jeremiah 2:11; Psalms 78:61"> And she named the child Ichabod, saying, The glory is departed from Israel; because the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="4.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God was taken, and because of her father-in-law and her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 137:5–6; John 2:17"> And she said, The glory is departed from Israel, for the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="4.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God is taken.</span>
