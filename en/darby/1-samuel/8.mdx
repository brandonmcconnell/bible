<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:18–19; 1 Timothy 5:21; Nehemiah 7:2"> And it came to pass when Samuel was old, that he made his sons judges over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:38; Genesis 22:19; 1 Chronicles 6:28"> And the name of his firstborn was Joel; and the name of his second, Abijah; they judged in Beer-sheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Psalms 15:5; 1 Timothy 6:10"> And his sons walked not in his ways, but turned aside after lucre, and took bribes, and perverted justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:17; 2 Samuel 5:3; Exodus 3:16"> Then all the elders of Israel gathered themselves together, and came to Samuel to Ramah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:14–15; Hosea 13:10–11; 1 Samuel 8:6–8"> and said to him, Behold, thou art become old, and thy sons walk not in thy ways: now appoint us a king to judge us, like all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:11; James 1:5; 1 Samuel 12:17"> And the thing displeased Samuel, when they said, Give us a king to judge us. And Samuel prayed to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:16; 1 Samuel 10:19; Exodus 16:8"> And Jehovah said to Samuel, Hearken unto the voice of the people in all that they say unto thee: for they have not rejected thee, but they have rejected me, that I should not reign over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:34–40; Exodus 32:1; Judges 13:1"> According to all the deeds that they have done since the day that I brought them up out of Egypt even unto this day, in that they have forsaken me and served other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="8.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>gods, so do they also unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:25; 1 Samuel 8:11–18; Ezekiel 46:18"> And now hearken unto their voice; only, testify solemnly unto them, and declare unto them the manner of the king that shall reign over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Samuel spoke all the words of Jehovah to the people that asked of him a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:52; Deuteronomy 17:14–20; 1 Samuel 10:25"> And he said, This will be the manner of the king that shall reign over you: he will take your sons, and appoint them for himself, on his chariot and among his horsemen, and they shall run before his chariots;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:7; 1 Kings 4:7; 1 Chronicles 27:1–22"> and &#91;he will take them&#93; that he may appoint for himself captains over thousands, and captains over fifties, and that they may plough his ground, and reap his harvest, and make his instruments of war and instruments of his chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he will take your daughters for perfumers, and cooks, and bakers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:18; 1 Kings 21:7; 1 Kings 21:19"> And your fields, and your vineyards, and your oliveyards, the best, will he take and give to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 1:3; Isaiah 39:7; Genesis 37:36"> And he will take the tenth of your seed and of your vineyards, and give to his chamberlains and to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And he will take your bondmen, and your bondwomen, and your comeliest young men, and your asses, and use them for his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He will take the tenth of your sheep. And ye shall be his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:4; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 1:25–28"> And ye shall cry out in that day because of your king whom ye have chosen; and Jehovah will not answer you in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16; Jeremiah 7:13; Isaiah 66:4"> And the people refused to hearken to the voice of Samuel; and they said, No, but there shall be a king over us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:5; Psalms 106:35; Deuteronomy 7:6"> that we also may be like all the nations; and our king shall judge us, and go out before us, and conduct our wars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:11"> And Samuel heard all the words of the people, and he repeated them in the ears of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Hosea 13:11"> And Jehovah said to Samuel, Hearken unto their voice, and make them a king. And Samuel said to the men of Israel, Go ye every man to his city.</span>
