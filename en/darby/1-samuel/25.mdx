<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:3; Numbers 20:29; Deuteronomy 34:8"> And Samuel died; and all Israel were gathered together, and lamented him; and they buried him in his house at Ramah. And David arose and went down to the wilderness of Paran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 38:13; 1 Samuel 23:24; Joshua 15:55"> And there was a man at Maon, whose business was at Carmel; and the man was very great, and he had three thousand sheep and a thousand goats; and he was shearing his sheep at Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; Proverbs 31:10; Isaiah 32:5–7"> And the name of the man was Nabal, and the name of his wife Abigail; and the woman was of good understanding, and of a beautiful countenance; but the man was churlish and evil in his doings; and he was a Calebite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 38:13; 2 Samuel 13:23"> And David heard in the wilderness that Nabal was shearing his sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 43:23; 1 Samuel 17:22"> Then David sent out ten young men; and David said to the young men, Go up to Carmel, and go to Nabal, and greet him in my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 10:5; 1 Chronicles 12:18; Psalms 122:7"> And thus shall ye say: Long life &#91;to thee&#93;! and peace be to thee, and peace be to thy house, and peace be to all that thou hast!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:21; 1 Samuel 25:15–16; 1 Samuel 22:2"> And now I have heard that thou hast shearers; now thy shepherds who were with us, we hurt them not, neither was there aught missed by them, all the while they were in Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esther 9:19; Nehemiah 8:10–12; Luke 14:12–14"> Ask thy young men, and they will tell thee. Therefore let the young men find favour in thine eyes; for we come in a good day: give, I pray thee, what thy hand may find to thy servants, and to thy son David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 2:15; 2 Chronicles 14:7; Genesis 8:4"> And David's young men came, and spoke to Nabal according to all those words in the name of David, and ceased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 9:28; Isaiah 32:7; 1 Samuel 22:7–8"> And Nabal answered David's servants and said, Who is David? and who is the son of Jesse? there are many servants now-a-days that break away every man from his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 8:6; Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 6:9"> And shall I take my bread, and my water, and my flesh which I have killed for my shearers, and give &#91;it&#93; to men whom I know not whence they are?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 36:21–22; 2 Samuel 24:13; Hebrews 13:17"> And David's young men turned their way, and went back, and came and reported to him according to all those words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:13; Proverbs 25:8; 1 Samuel 24:5–6"> And David said to his men, Gird ye on every man his sword. And they girded on every man his sword; and David also girded on his sword; and there went up after David about four hundred men; and two hundred abode by the baggage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:10; Mark 15:29"> And one of &#91;Nabal's&#93; young men told Abigail, Nabal's wife, saying, Behold, David sent messengers out of the wilderness to bless our master; and he has insulted them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:7; 1 Samuel 25:21; Philippians 2:15"> And the men were very good to us, and we were not hurt, neither missed we anything, as long as we companied with them, when we were in the fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 1:10; Exodus 14:22; Zechariah 2:5"> They were a wall to us both by night and day, all the while we were with them feeding the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:7; Deuteronomy 13:13; 1 Samuel 20:9"> And now know and consider what thou wilt do, for evil is determined against our master, and against all his household; and he is such a son of Belial, that one cannot speak to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:1; Matthew 5:25; Proverbs 6:4–5"> And Abigail made haste, and took two hundred loaves, and two skin-bottles of wine, and five sheep ready dressed, and five measures of parched &#91;corn&#93;, and a hundred raisin-cakes, and two hundred fig-cakes, and laid them on asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 32:20; Genesis 32:16; Proverbs 31:11–12"> And she said to her young men, Go on before me; behold, I come after you. But she did not tell her husband Nabal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 4:24"> And as she was riding on the ass, and coming down by the covert of the hill, behold, David and his men came down opposite to her; and she met them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 17:13; Ephesians 4:31; Romans 12:21"> Now David had said, Surely, in vain have I kept all that this &#91;man&#93; had in the wilderness, so that nothing was missed of all that was his; and he has requited me evil for good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 14:10; 1 Samuel 20:13; 1 Samuel 3:17"> So and more also do<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="25.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God to the enemies of David, if I leave of all that is his by the morning light any male.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:41; Judges 1:14; Joshua 15:18"> And when Abigail saw David, she hasted and lighted off the ass, and fell before David on her face, and bowed herself to the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esther 8:3; 1 Samuel 25:28; 2 Samuel 14:9"> and fell at his feet, and said, Upon me, my lord, &#91;upon&#93; me let the iniquity be; but let thy handmaid, I pray thee, speak in thine ears, and hear the words of thy handmaid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:17; Malachi 2:2; 2 Samuel 13:33"> Let not my lord, I pray thee, regard this man of Belial, Nabal; for as his name is, so is he: Nabal is his name, and folly is with him; and I thy handmaid did not see the young men of my lord, whom thou didst send.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:32; Romans 12:19–20; Genesis 20:6"> And now, my lord, &#91;as&#93; Jehovah liveth, and &#91;as&#93; thy soul liveth, seeing Jehovah has restrained thee from coming with bloodshed, and from avenging thyself with thine own hand, now let thine enemies, and they that seek evil to my lord, be as Nabal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 33:11; 1 Samuel 30:26; 2 Kings 5:15"> And now this blessing which thy bondmaid has brought to my lord, let it be given to the young men that follow my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:17; 2 Samuel 7:11; 1 Samuel 24:11"> I pray thee, forgive the transgression of thy handmaid: for Jehovah will certainly make my lord a lasting house; because my lord fights the battles of Jehovah, and evil has not been found in thee all thy days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 17:21; Matthew 10:29–30; Malachi 3:17"> And if a man is risen up to pursue thee and to seek thy life, the soul of my lord shall be bound in the bundle of the living with Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="25.29 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God; and the souls of thine enemies, them shall he sling out from the hollow of the sling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:14; Psalms 89:20; 1 Samuel 23:17"> And it shall come to pass, when Jehovah shall do to my lord according to all the good that he has spoken concerning thee, and shall appoint thee ruler over Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 40:14; Romans 12:19; Luke 23:42"> that this shall be no stumbling-block to thee, nor offence of heart for my lord, either that thou hast shed blood without cause, or that my lord has avenged himself. And when Jehovah shall deal well with my lord, then remember thy handmaid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 1:68; Exodus 18:10; Psalms 72:18"> And David said to Abigail, Blessed be Jehovah, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="25.32 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God of Israel, who sent thee this day to meet me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:26; Proverbs 28:23; Proverbs 9:9"> And blessed be thy discernment, and blessed be thou, who hast kept me this day from coming with bloodshed, and from avenging myself with mine own hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:11; 1 Samuel 25:26; Joshua 10:6"> But indeed, as Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="25.34 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God of Israel liveth, who has restrained me from hurting thee, except thou hadst hasted and come to meet me, there had not been left to Nabal by the morning light any male.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 19:21; 1 Samuel 20:42; 2 Kings 5:19"> So David received of her hand what she had brought him, and said to her, Go up in peace to thy house; see, I have hearkened to thy voice, and have accepted thy person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:19; 2 Samuel 13:23; 2 Samuel 13:28"> And Abigail came to Nabal; and behold, he held a feast in his house, like the feast of a king; and Nabal's heart was merry within him, for he was drunken to excess; so she told him nothing, less or more, until the morning light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:28; 1 Samuel 25:34; 1 Samuel 25:22"> And it came to pass in the morning, when the wine was gone out of Nabal, that his wife told him these things; and his heart died within him, and he became &#91;as&#93; a stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:10; 2 Kings 15:5; 1 Samuel 6:9"> And it came to pass in about ten days that Jehovah smote Nabal, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:26; 1 Samuel 25:34; Proverbs 22:23"> And when David heard that Nabal was dead, he said, Blessed be Jehovah, who has pleaded the cause of my reproach from the hand of Nabal, and has kept back his servant from evil; but Jehovah has returned Nabal's evil upon his own head. And David sent and communed with Abigail, to take her as his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 24:51; Genesis 24:37–38"> And the servants of David came to Abigail to Carmel, and spoke to her, saying, David has sent us to thee, to take thee as his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; John 13:3–5; Ruth 2:10"> And she arose and bowed herself on her face to the earth, and said, Behold, let thy handmaid be a bondwoman to wash the feet of the servants of my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 24:61–67; Psalms 45:10–11"> And Abigail hasted, and arose, and rode upon an ass, with five damsels of hers that followed her; and she went after the messengers of David, and became his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:5; Joshua 15:56; 1 Samuel 27:3"> David had also taken Ahinoam of Jizreel; and they became, even both of them, his wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:14; 1 Samuel 18:27; 1 Samuel 18:20"> But Saul had given Michal his daughter, David's wife, to Phalti the son of Laish, who was of Gallim.</span>
