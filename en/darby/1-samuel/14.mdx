<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 6:27; Micah 7:5; 2 Samuel 1:25–26"> Now it came to pass one day that Jonathan the son of Saul said to the young man that bore his armour, Come and let us go over to the Philistines' garrison which is on the other side. But he did not tell his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:15–16; Isaiah 10:28–29"> And Saul abode at the extreme end of Gibeah under the pomegranate-tree which &#91;was&#93; in Migron; and the people that were with him were about six hundred men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:20; 1 Samuel 2:28; 1 Samuel 4:21"> (And Ahijah the son of Ahitub, Ichabod's brother, the son of Phinehas, the son of Eli, Jehovah's priest in Shiloh, wore the ephod.) And the people did not know that Jonathan was gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:23"> Now between the passes by which Jonathan sought to go over to the Philistines' garrison there was a sharp rock on the one side and a sharp rock on the other side; and the name of the one &#91;was&#93; Bozez, and the name of the other Seneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The one crag &#91;formed&#93; a pillar on the north opposite to Michmash, and the other on the south opposite to Geba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Romans 8:31; Zechariah 4:6"> And Jonathan said to the young man that bore his armour, Come, and let us go over to the garrison of these uncircumcised: perhaps Jehovah will work for us; for there is no restraint to Jehovah to save by many or by few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:3; Zechariah 8:23; 1 Samuel 10:7"> And his armour-bearer said to him, Do all that is in thy heart; turn thee; behold, I am with thee according to thy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 7:9–14"> Then said Jonathan, Behold, we will pass over to the men, and we will shew ourselves to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 6:36–40; Genesis 24:13–14"> If they say thus to us, Stand still until we come to you, then we will stay in our place, and will not go up to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 24:14; 1 Samuel 10:7; Judges 7:11"> And if they say thus, Come up to us, then we will go up; for Jehovah has given them into our hand; and this shall be the sign to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:6; 1 Samuel 14:22; Judges 6:2"> And both of them shewed themselves to the garrison of the Philistines; and the Philistines said, Behold, the Hebrews come forth out of the holes where they had hid themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:43–44; 2 Samuel 5:24; Judges 7:15"> And the men of the garrison answered Jonathan and his armour-bearer and said, Come up to us, and we will shew you something. And Jonathan said to his armour-bearer, Come up after me; for Jehovah has delivered them into the hand of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Joshua 23:10; Leviticus 26:7–8"> And Jonathan climbed up upon his hands and upon his feet, and his armour-bearer after him; and they fell before Jonathan; and his armour-bearer slew after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And that first slaughter which Jonathan and his armour-bearer wrought was about twenty men, as it were on the half-furrow of an acre of land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:17; Genesis 35:5; 2 Samuel 5:24"> And there was trembling in the camp, in the field, and among all the people; the garrison, and the ravagers, they also trembled, and the earth quaked; for it was a trembling &#91;from&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="14.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:22–25; Isaiah 19:2; Judges 7:22"> And the watchmen of Saul in Gibeah of Benjamin looked, and behold, the multitude melted away, and they went on slaying one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then said Saul to the people that were with him, Muster now, and see who is gone from us. And they mustered, and behold, Jonathan and his armour-bearer were not there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:7–8; 1 Samuel 5:2; Judges 20:23"> And Saul said to Ahijah, Bring hither the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="14.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God. For the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="14.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God was at that time with the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:24; Numbers 27:21; Joshua 9:14"> And it came to pass while Saul talked to the priest, that the noise which was in the camp of the Philistines went on and increased; and Saul said to the priest, Withdraw thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 2 Chronicles 20:23; 1 Samuel 14:16"> And Saul and all the people that were with him were called together, and they came to the battle; and behold, every man's sword was against his fellow, a very great confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:4; Judges 7:23"> And there were Hebrews with the Philistines before that time, who had gone up with them into the camp round about; and they also &#91;turned&#93; to be with the Israelites who were with Saul and Jonathan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:6; 1 Samuel 31:7"> And all the men of Israel who had hid themselves in mount Ephraim heard that the Philistines fled, and they also followed hard after them in the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 14:30; 1 Samuel 13:5; Psalms 44:6–8"> And Jehovah saved Israel that day; and the battle passed over beyond Beth-Aven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 6:26; Romans 10:2; Judges 1:28"> But the men of Israel were distressed that day. Now Saul had adjured the people, saying, Cursed be the man that eateth food until evening, and &#91;until&#93; I am avenged on mine enemies. So none of the people tasted food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Numbers 13:27; Deuteronomy 9:28"> And all they of the land came to a wood; and there was honey on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:2"> And the people had come into the wood, and behold, the honey flowed; but no man put his hand to his mouth, for the people feared the oath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:29; 1 Samuel 14:43; 1 Samuel 30:12"> But Jonathan had not heard when his father adjured the people; and he put forth the end of his staff which was in his hand, and dipped it in the honeycomb, and put his hand to his mouth, and his eyes became bright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:43; 1 Samuel 14:24"> Then answered one of the people and said, Thy father strictly adjured the people, saying, Cursed be the man that eateth food this day; and the people are faint.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 18:18; Joshua 7:25"> And Jonathan said, My father has troubled the land: see, I pray you, that mine eyes are bright, because I tasted a little of this honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:18"> How much more, if the people had eaten freely to-day of the spoil of their enemies which they found? for would there not now have been a much greater slaughter among the Philistines?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 10:12; Joshua 19:42"> And they smote the Philistines that day from Michmash to Ajalon; and the people were very faint.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:19; Leviticus 3:17; Leviticus 17:10–14"> And the people fell on the spoil, and took sheep, and oxen, and calves, and slaughtered them on the ground; and the people ate &#91;them&#93; with the blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Romans 2:1; Matthew 7:5"> And they told Saul, saying, Behold, the people sin against Jehovah, in that they eat with the blood. And he said, Ye have acted perversely: roll me now a great stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And Saul said, Disperse yourselves among the people, and say to them, Bring near to me every man his ox, and every man his sheep, and slaughter them here, and eat; and sin not against Jehovah in eating with the blood. And all the people brought every man his ox with him that night, and slaughtered &#91;them&#93; there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:17; 1 Samuel 7:12; 2 Timothy 3:5"> And Saul built an altar to Jehovah: this was the first altar he built to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 48:1–2; Psalms 73:28; Malachi 2:7"> And Saul said, Let us go down after the Philistines by night, and plunder them until the morning light, and let us not leave a man of them. And they said, Do whatsoever is good in thy sight. Then said the priest, Let us come near hither to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="14.36 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:6; 1 Samuel 30:7–8; 1 Samuel 23:9–12"> And Saul inquired of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="14.37 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God, Shall I go down after the Philistines? wilt thou give them into the hand of Israel? But he did not answer him that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Judges 20:2; Joshua 7:14–26; Numbers 24:17"> And Saul said, Draw ye near hither, all the heads of the people; and know and see wherein this sin has been this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:44; 2 Samuel 12:5; 1 Samuel 14:24"> For, &#91;as&#93; Jehovah liveth, who has saved Israel, though it be in Jonathan my son, he shall certainly die. And no one answered him among all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:15; 1 Samuel 14:7; 1 Samuel 14:36"> Then said he to all Israel, Be ye on one side, and I and Jonathan my son will be on the other side. And the people said to Saul, Do what is good in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 1:24; Joshua 7:16–18; 1 Samuel 10:20–21"> And Saul said to Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="14.41 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God of Israel, Give a perfect &#91;testimony&#93;! And Jonathan and Saul were taken, and the people escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And Saul said, Cast &#91;lots&#93; between me and Jonathan my son. And Jonathan was taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 7:19; 1 Samuel 14:27; Jonah 1:7–10"> And Saul said to Jonathan, Tell me what thou hast done. And Jonathan told him and said, With the end of the staff which is in my hand I tasted a little honey, &#91;and&#93; behold, I must die!</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:39; Ruth 1:17; 1 Samuel 25:22"> And Saul said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="14.44 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God do so &#91;to me&#93; and more also; thou shalt certainly die, Jonathan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 21:18; Acts 27:34; 2 Samuel 14:11"> And the people said to Saul, Shall Jonathan die, who has wrought this great salvation in Israel? Far be it! &#91;as&#93; Jehovah liveth, there shall not a hair of his head fall to the ground; for he has wrought with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="14.45 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God this day. So the people delivered Jonathan, that he died not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And Saul went up from following the Philistines; and the Philistines went to their own place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:6; 1 Samuel 14:52; 2 Samuel 8:3"> And Saul took the kingdom over Israel, and fought against all his enemies round about, against Moab, and against the children of Ammon, and against Edom, and against the kings of Zobah, and against the Philistines; and whithersoever he turned himself, he discomfited &#91;them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Exodus 17:14; 1 Samuel 15:3–7; Deuteronomy 25:19"> And he did valiantly, and smote the Amalekites, and delivered Israel out of the hands of their spoilers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:33; 1 Samuel 31:2; 2 Samuel 6:20–23"> And the sons of Saul were Jonathan, and Jishvi, and Malchi-shua. And the names of his two daughters: the name of the firstborn Merab, and the name of the younger Michal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8; 1 Samuel 17:55; 2 Samuel 3:27"> And the name of Saul's wife was Ahinoam, the daughter of Ahimaaz; and the name of the captain of his host was Abner, the son of Ner, Saul's uncle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:1; 1 Samuel 9:21"> And Kish the father of Saul, and Ner the father of Abner were sons of Abiel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11; 1 Samuel 8:1"> And there was sore war against the Philistines all the days of Saul; and when Saul saw any mighty man, or any valiant man, he took him to himself.</span>
