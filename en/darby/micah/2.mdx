<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 36:4; Isaiah 32:7; Psalms 7:14–16"> Woe to them that devise iniquity and work evil upon their beds! When the morning is light they practise it, because it is in the power of their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:8; Jeremiah 22:17; Amos 8:4"> And they covet fields, and take them by violence; and houses, and take them away; and they oppress a man and his house, even a man and his heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:3; Amos 5:13; Isaiah 2:11–12"> Therefore thus saith Jehovah: Behold, against this family do I devise an evil, from which ye shall not remove your necks; neither shall ye walk haughtily: for it is an evil time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:6; Jeremiah 9:10; Jeremiah 9:17–21"> In that day shall they take up a proverb concerning you, and lament with a doleful lamentation, &#91;and&#93; say, We are utterly spoiled: he hath changed the portion of my people: how hath he removed it from me! He hath distributed our fields to the rebellious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:8; Joshua 18:4; Joshua 18:10"> Therefore thou shalt have none that shall cast the measuring line upon a lot, in the congregation of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 2:12; Isaiah 30:10; Acts 5:40"> Prophesy ye not, they prophesy. If they do not prophesy to these, the ignominy will not depart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 84:11; Psalms 15:2; Jeremiah 15:16"> O thou &#91;that art&#93; named the house of Jacob, Is Jehovah impatient? are these his doings? Do not my words do good to him that walketh uprightly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 120:6–7; 2 Samuel 20:19; Isaiah 9:21"> But of late my people is risen up as an enemy: ye strip off the mantle with the garment from them that pass by securely, that are averse from war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Ezekiel 39:21; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> The women of my people do ye cast out from their pleasant houses; from their young children do ye take away my magnificence for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:9; Psalms 106:38; 2 Kings 17:6"> Arise ye, and depart; for this is not the resting-place, because of defilement that bringeth destruction, even a grievous destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Isaiah 30:10–11; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> If a man walking in wind and falsehood do lie, &#91;saying,&#93; I will prophesy unto thee of wine and of strong drink, he shall be the prophet of this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 4:6–7; Micah 7:18; Hosea 1:11"> I will surely assemble, O Jacob, the whole of thee; I will surely gather the remnant of Israel; I will put them together as sheep of Bozrah, as a flock in the midst of their pasture: they shall make great noise by reason of &#91;the multitude of&#93; men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 52:12; Zechariah 9:14–15; Isaiah 45:1–2"> One that breaketh through is gone up before them: they have broken forth, and have passed on to the gate, and are gone out by it; and their king passeth on before them, and Jehovah at the head of them.</span>
